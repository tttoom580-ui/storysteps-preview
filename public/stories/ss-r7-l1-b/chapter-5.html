月亮路的農場

The Farm on the Moon Road

On the next safe day, the friends visited a small farm near a bend called Moon Road. A bank office had helped the farmer buy a new water tank, and he was proud of caring for his animals without waste. He had to feed many hungry mouths before sunrise. A letter from his sister lay on the table, beside a cup of milk. Outside, the moon was still bright. Tavi had come to ask about the birds that once sang in the apple trees. The farmer did not know where they were now, but he offered to walk with them to the end of his land.

下一個安全的日子，朋友們來到月亮路轉彎處的小農場。銀行曾協助農夫買新水箱，他很自豪能不浪費地照顧動物。日出前，他得餵飽許多張嘴。桌上牛奶旁放著妹妹寄來的信。外頭月亮仍很亮。塔薇是來問以前在蘋果樹上唱歌的鳥。農夫不知道牠們現在在哪裡，但願意陪三人走到農地盡頭。

The farmer's daughter, a teenager, showed them an old movie about the farm. In it, a wooden wheel turned beside the well. She said the family did not sell the wheel after buying a pump, because it taught young animals how water moved. The metal parts needed oil, but the wheel was safe to watch from behind the fence when it turned at a slow speed. Today a yellow string kept visitors behind a straight line. Tavi liked the clear rule. The girl asked them to think about the sound of the wheel. Was its low turning sound one of the spring sounds, or was it a sound made by work?

農夫的十幾歲女兒拿出農場舊影片。影片裡，一個木輪在井邊轉動。她說家人買水泵後沒有賣掉木輪，因為它能教年輕動物了解水怎樣移動。金屬零件需要上油，但當它緩慢轉動時，站在圍欄後觀察是安全的。今天一條黃色繩線讓訪客待在筆直界線後。塔薇喜歡這項清楚規則。女孩請他們想想木輪的聲音：緩慢轉動是春天的聲音，還是工作的聲音？

Nell heard a strange tapping from under the empty water tank. She did not step through the fence. Instead, she asked the farmer to check it. He found a loose wooden board and fixed it with a tool. The tapping stopped. Tavi wrote that this sound was not a bird or a stream. She was learning to tell sounds apart without taking risks. Ivo bent down and hurt his toe against a stone, but it was only a small knock. He sat, checked his foot, and rested before walking again.

奈兒聽見空水箱下傳來奇怪的敲擊聲，沒有跨過圍欄，而是請農夫檢查。農夫發現一塊鬆掉的木板，便用工具固定。敲擊聲停了。塔薇寫下這不是鳥或溪流的聲音。她逐漸學會不冒險也能分辨聲音。伊沃彎腰時腳趾撞上石頭，有點痛，所幸只是輕輕碰傷。他坐下檢查腳，休息後才再走。

The family shared bread and sweet apples. Their talk moved from water to the topic of birds. The farmer said that birds often stopped where food was easy to find, but he did not want visitors to bring them meat or other food. Wild animals needed to find their own meals. “Do not decide where they must live,” he said. “Watch where they choose to go.” Tavi agreed. The friends looked through the fence toward the far trees. The branches were empty, but the ground under them was no longer completely white.

農場一家分享麵包和甜蘋果。他們的談話由水轉到鳥。農夫說鳥常停在容易找到食物的地方，但他不希望訪客帶肉或其他食物去餵。野生動物需要自己找餐食。「不要替牠們決定住哪裡，」他說，「觀察牠們自己去哪裡。」塔薇同意。朋友們隔著圍欄望向遠樹。枝上還沒有鳥，但樹下的地面已不是全白。

On the way home, the farmer's daughter walked beside them for a while. She wanted to learn more about listening and asked if she could join on a later day. Tavi said yes, once her family knew the plan. The friends now understood that a quiet place might hold several sounds that seemed alike to a new listener. They needed patience and help from people who knew each place well. As Moon Road disappeared behind the hill, Tavi kept the farm on her map, not as a place to collect birds but as a place to compare what she heard.

回家路上，農夫的女兒陪他們走了一段。她想多學聆聽，也問以後能否參加。塔薇說可以，但要先讓家人知道計畫。朋友們現在明白，安靜的地方可能藏著幾種對新手聽來很相似的聲音。他們需要耐心，也需要熟悉當地的人協助。月亮路消失在山後時，塔薇仍把農場留在地圖上，不是為了抓鳥，而是方便比較聽見的聲音。

The next morning, the farmer's daughter gave them a copy of her notes about the water tank. She had written down how it sounded before the board was fixed and after the work was done. Tavi could now compare two clear examples from the same place. She knew that a change in sound did not always mean a change in season. Sometimes a person had changed a machine. They added a line to their method: ask what changed around the sound. Ivo remembered his sore toe and laughed when he stepped around the same stone on their next visit. The group thanked the family for showing them real work and real care. Tavi also promised not to bring visitors into the farm without asking first. It was a home and a place of work, not an open field for everyone to enter.

隔天早晨，農夫的女兒交給他們一份水箱紀錄副本。她寫下木板修好前後的聲音。塔薇現在可以比較同一地點的兩個清楚例子。她知道聲音改變不一定是季節改變，有時只是有人修好了機器。他們在方法中增加一項：詢問聲音周圍發生了什麼變化。伊沃想起疼過的腳趾，再次造訪時繞開同一塊石頭，忍不住笑了。團隊感謝這家人展示真實工作和照顧。塔薇也承諾，沒得到允許不會帶訪客進農場。這是住家和工作場所，不是人人可隨意進入的空地。
