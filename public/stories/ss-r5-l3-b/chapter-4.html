The Corridor Behind the Hall

兩把鑰匙，同一座舞台 · 第 4 / 25 章 · 2000–3000 · 英中對照

用互動版查詞、朗讀與收藏

During the second inspection, a delivery cart struck a hanging cable in the narrow passage. Nothing serious broke, but a worker lost his balance. Evan closed the route while Ruo wrote down the location and time. They did not treat a near accident as proof that someone was careless; they wanted a safer route first.

第二次檢查時，搬運車碰到狹窄通道裡垂下的電纜。沒有嚴重損壞，但一名工人失去平衡。亦辰封閉通道，若晴記下地點和時間。他們沒有立刻責怪誰，而是先找安全的替代路線。

Simon arrived with a printed plan that placed his food trucks in front of the only wide doorway. He called it a harmless adjustment. Evan showed how little space would remain for evacuation. Ruo asked Simon to submit the change through the same public review process as every volunteer.

賽門帶來一張印刷平面圖，把他的餐車排在唯一的寬門前。他說只是無傷大雅的調整。亦辰算給他看，疏散空間會剩下多少。若晴要求賽門像每位志工一樣依公開程序送審變更。

When the worker returned, Ruo asked him what he remembered about the fallen box. He said he had heard a wheel scrape before the crash, but had not seen the cable until afterward. Evan checked the distance between the wheels and the wall. Neither answer alone identified the cause. Together they supported moving the delivery route, even before a formal report was finished. Ruo thanked the worker for speaking without pretending certainty. She knew that if the public called every accident a crime, witnesses might become afraid to describe ordinary mistakes. Evan promised that the new plan would include the time needed for deliveries, so people would not rush through the narrow passage again.

工人回來後，若晴問起箱子摔落時的記憶。他說先聽到輪子摩擦聲，事故後才看見電纜。亦辰檢查車輪與牆的距離。兩份說法都無法單獨找出原因，卻足以支持先改送貨動線。若晴感謝工人如實回答而沒有假裝確定。她明白，若大眾把每場意外都當犯罪，證人就會害怕說明普通錯誤。亦辰答應把送貨需要的時間寫入新規畫，避免人再次匆忙穿越狹道。

The inspection team entered through an alley behind the hall. A cherry tree stood beside an old tower, and a van carried the replacement cable. A sudden crash from a dropped box made Ruo dizzy; nobody was injured. Evan showed bravery by asking everyone to stop rather than pretend the work could continue. They agreed on a clear definition of an incident and began to investigate the damaged route. A wool club was teaching visitors to knit scarves, while a pottery student set a cup on a saucer. One bright label advertised a 'sexy' murder mystery performance; Ruo said neither word described the real safety problem. She took a brief nap after the inspection because exhaustion made poor decisions easier. The floor was solid, but the hanging wire was not. Volunteers were thankful for the heater's warmth. Nobody should starve for attention at a public meeting; quiet voices mattered too.

檢查小隊從大廳後巷進入。櫻桃樹在舊塔旁，貨車運來替換電纜。箱子摔落的撞擊聲讓若晴一陣暈眩，幸好無人受傷。亦辰勇敢要求全員停工，不假裝能繼續。他們先定義何謂事件，再調查損壞路線。編織社正教訪客織圍巾，陶藝學生把杯子放在茶托上。一張鮮豔標籤宣傳「性感謀殺推理秀」，若晴說這些字都與真正的安全問題無關。檢查後她短暫小睡，因為疲倦容易讓人做錯決定。地板堅固，垂下的電線卻不安全。志工感謝暖爐的溫暖。公開會議不該只有大聲者得到注意，安靜的意見也重要。

A local reporter asked whether the clubs were accusing the sponsor of a crime. Ruo said no. They had found a problem in a proposed layout, not evidence of a crime. Evan was surprised by how clearly she separated facts from conclusions. Later, he admitted that he had been too quick to dismiss her concern about public records.

在地記者問兩社是否指控贊助商犯罪。若晴回答沒有。他們發現的是規畫問題，不是犯罪證據。亦辰驚訝她能如此清楚區分事實與結論。後來他承認，自己先前太快忽視她對公開紀錄的要求。

The clubs agreed to test a marked walking route with actual visitors before choosing the final floor plan. Ruo invited Helen, a retired librarian who used a wheelchair, and promised she could stop the test whenever she wished. Helen accepted only after Evan showed her that the test would have an accessible exit.

兩社同意讓真正的訪客試走標線路線，再決定最後平面圖。若晴邀請使用輪椅的退休館員海倫，並承諾她隨時可以中止。亦辰向海倫說明無障礙出口後，她才答應參與。

The hall manager gave both presidents a copy of the route inspection sheet. A dangerous corner had been noted three months earlier, but the repair was delayed because the building was not regularly used. Ruo was tempted to put that failure into a dramatic speech about public neglect. Evan asked her to wait until they knew whether a repair order had been issued. He was not protecting the office; he wanted the council to receive a correct account. She agreed reluctantly. The next day the order appeared, dated before their arrival. It showed a delay, not a secret attempt to hide danger. Ruo rewrote her statement, keeping the concern but dropping the accusation. Evan thanked her. She replied that he should thank the clerk who had found the document. She was beginning to understand that fairness could mean making a less exciting story more accurate.

場館管理員給兩位社長各一份動線檢查表。三個月前已有危險角落紀錄，但因建築不常使用，修理延遲。若晴想把這件事寫進關於公共忽視的激烈演說，亦辰請她先確認是否早已下達維修單。他不是在保護辦公室，而是希望議會得到正確紀錄。她不情願地同意。隔天找到的維修單，日期早於他們進場。這顯示延誤，不是祕密掩蓋危險。若晴改寫發言，保留疑慮、刪去指控。亦辰道謝，她說應該謝找到文件的職員。若晴開始明白，公平有時意味著讓刺激的故事變得較不精彩、卻更準確。

That evening the hanging cable was removed and the unsafe path remained closed. Ruo wrote the incident date beside the corrected diagram. Evan thanked the worker who had spoken up. A good safety record, they agreed, did not need a villain; it needed a useful next action.

當晚垂下的電纜已移除，危險通道仍封閉。若晴把事件日期寫在修訂圖旁。亦辰感謝願意出面說明的工人。兩人同意，好的安全紀錄不需要反派，而需要可執行的下一步。
