The Rules Have Edges

兩把鑰匙，同一座舞台 · 第 2 / 25 章 · 2000–3000 · 英中對照

The next morning the college noticeboard was surrounded by students. A rumor claimed Evan had already received the hall keys. Ruo felt angry, but Connie, the volunteer secretary, showed her a receipt: the keys were temporary copies for a safety inspection. Ruo corrected the rumor publicly before Evan could hear it from someone else.

隔天早上，公告欄前擠滿學生。傳言說亦辰已經拿到活動廳鑰匙。若晴生氣了，但志工祕書可妮拿出收據，證明那只是安全檢查用的暫時鑰匙。若晴在亦辰從旁人聽見之前，先公開更正傳言。

Evan did not thank her immediately. He was still upset that she had assumed the rumor might be true. Ruo said he was right to expect a question before an accusation. Then she asked to attend the inspection, not as a guest he could dismiss, but as another applicant with a legal interest in the same public building.

亦辰沒有立刻道謝。他仍介意若晴曾認為謠言可能是真的。若晴承認，指控前本來就該先查問。接著她要求參加檢查，不是作為可以被趕走的客人，而是另一位對同棟公共建築有正當權益的申請者。

Connie asked Ruo to record the correction in the same place where the rumor had first spread. Ruo hesitated because the noticeboard was crowded. She had hoped a quiet conversation with Evan would be enough. Then she remembered the people who had already formed opinions from the false story. Correcting it privately would leave them with the wrong idea. She wrote a short note explaining what the receipt did and did not prove, and signed her own name. At the bottom she invited readers to ask the college office for the original record. Evan read the note later and left it in place. Trust, both realized, would be built through visible choices rather than grand promises.

可妮請若晴在謠言原本傳播的地方公布更正。若晴猶豫，因為公告欄前人很多；她本想私下向亦辰說清楚就好。接著她想到已被錯誤消息影響的人，若只私下更正，其他人仍會誤會。她寫下短短聲明，說明收據能證明和不能證明的事，並親自署名，底下邀大家向辦公室查核原件。亦辰後來讀了，沒有撕下。兩人明白，信任必須從可見的選擇建立，而非華麗承諾。

On the office wall, a poster of a painted beast hung beside an old bowling photograph. A decade had passed since the first community event. The budget was small, and nobody wanted a greedy plan that promised too much. A drunk visitor had once frightened volunteers, so the new safety kit included a clear incident sheet. A retired geography professor wearing a necktie explained the building's position beside the bus stop. Every passenger, he said, should find an entrance without asking for a favor. An expressive student made a map with wide lines and visible arrows. Connie tied the spare keys with a piece of lace. The office would not permit anyone to borrow them without signing. Ruo felt regret about the rumor, but took the risk of correcting it. The college semester was half over; each voter still deserved accurate information.

辦公室牆上的野獸彩繪海報旁掛著一張舊保齡球合照。距離第一屆社區活動已過十年。預算有限，誰都不想做出貪心的承諾。以前有醉酒訪客嚇壞志工，新的安全工具包便加上清楚的事件表。戴領帶的退休地理教授說明建築與公車站的位置。他說，每位乘客都應找得到入口，不必求人幫忙。表情豐富的學生畫了粗線條和明顯箭頭的地圖。可妮用一條蕾絲帶綁住備用鑰匙，辦公室不允許任何人不簽名就借走。若晴為謠言感到後悔，但仍冒著尷尬風險公開更正。學期已過一半，每位投票者仍應得到正確資訊。

Ruo wrote beside the map, "A rumor can frighten a voter more than an honest disagreement." Connie asked her to keep the warning short so nobody would mistake it for an accusation.

若晴在地圖旁寫：「謠言比誠實分歧更可能嚇壞投票者。」可妮請她把警語寫短，免得被誤認為指控。

Inside the station they discovered that a narrow stair separated the exhibition wall from the repair area. A visitor using a wheelchair could enter the hall but not reach every table. Ruo's proposal had shown a beautiful drawing without this problem. She marked her own plan as incomplete.

兩人在舊車站發現一道狹窄階梯，隔開展覽牆和修理區。坐輪椅的訪客雖能進廳，卻無法到達所有攤位。若晴原本的企畫畫得很漂亮，卻漏掉了這個問題。她在自己的圖上標示「尚未完成」。

At the club meeting, members asked whether a joint proposal would erase their separate identities. Ruo proposed a safeguard: both clubs would keep their own budget records and could refuse unsafe tasks. Evan agreed to discuss it, but said his members deserved to decide for themselves. The question was left for a vote.

社團會議上，成員擔心聯合企畫會抹去兩社特色。若晴提出保障：各社保留自己的預算紀錄，也能拒絕不安全的工作。亦辰願意討論，但強調自己的社員應自行決定。問題交由投票處理。

During lunch, one of Ruo's members suggested printing a list of people who had spread the key rumor. Ruo refused. The office receipt did not show who had believed or repeated the story, and naming people would make the club's anger look more certain than its evidence. She asked the members instead to help write a short explanation of the inspection process. They added the dates when temporary keys were issued and returned. Evan's vice president read the explanation and found one confusing sentence. Ruo rewrote it. That small correction became the first document the two clubs created together. It carried both club names, not because either president had won, but because each had checked part of it. Later Evan apologized for assuming Ruo wanted to use the rumor to damage his chances. She accepted, and they agreed to ask before guessing what the other meant. The rule was easy to write and hard to practise.

午餐時，若晴的社員提議印出傳播鑰匙謠言者的名單。若晴拒絕。辦公室收據無法證明誰相信或轉述故事，點名只會讓社團的憤怒看起來比證據更肯定。她改請社員寫簡短的檢查流程說明，補上臨時鑰匙發出與歸還日期。亦辰的副社長讀完後指出一句不清楚的話，若晴重新改寫。這份小小更正成了兩社第一份共同製作的文件，上頭同時有兩社名字，不是因為哪位社長勝利，而是雙方都檢查過。後來亦辰為自己曾猜測若晴想利用謠言打擊他而道歉。她接受，兩人約定不懂對方意思時先問再猜。規則寫來容易，實踐卻不簡單。

When the office closed, Connie placed the returned keys in a drawer and signed the log. Ruo noticed that a small habit could stop a much larger misunderstanding. She would remember the receipt, not as proof that people never lied, but as proof that checking was possible.

辦公室關門時，可妮把歸還的鑰匙收進抽屜，簽好紀錄。若晴看見小習慣能阻止更大誤會。她會記得那張收據，不是因為人永遠不說謊，而是因為事情可以核查。
