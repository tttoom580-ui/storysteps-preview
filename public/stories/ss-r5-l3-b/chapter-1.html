Two Proposals on One Table

兩把鑰匙，同一座舞台 · 第 1 / 25 章 · 2000–3000 · 英中對照

用互動版查詞、朗讀與收藏

Ruo Lin, twenty-seven, returned to Harbor Adult College carrying a proposal for a neighborhood story festival. Across the table sat Evan Zhou, twenty-nine, president of the Repair Club. Each had applied to use the empty railway hall for six months. The council could support only one project. Neither of them wanted to withdraw.

二十七歲的林若晴帶著社區故事節企畫回到海港成人學院。桌子另一端坐著二十九歲的修理社社長周亦辰。兩人都申請使用閒置的鐵路活動廳六個月，但市議會只能支持一個企畫。他們誰也不願退出。

Dean Yang explained the published rules: access would count for thirty points, safety for twenty-five, public benefit for twenty-five, and practical planning for twenty. A joint application was allowed before the fourteenth day, but it needed the approval of both clubs. Ruo wrote the deadline down. An invitation to cooperate was not the same as a promise to surrender.

楊主任說明已公布的規則：無障礙占三十分、安全占二十五分、公共效益占二十五分、執行規畫占二十分。第十四天以前可提出聯合申請，但必須得到兩社同意。若晴記下期限。邀請合作不等於承諾退讓。

Ruo had chosen adult college after working several years at a local archive. She knew how easily a beautiful exhibit could hide an unfinished question. Evan had chosen to study because fixing machines at work had taught him that practical skill also needed clear explanation. They were both older than many students in the courtyard, but neither felt finished learning. When Ruo saw Evan check the exit before arranging his tools, she noticed care beneath his impatience. When Evan saw her write down an unanswered question rather than make up an answer, he noticed the same quality. They still competed for the hall. That evening each returned to a separate room, yet both crossed out the same word in their proposal: 'exclusive.'

若晴在地方檔案館工作幾年後才回成人學院，知道漂亮展覽多麼容易掩蓋未完成的問題。亦辰選擇進修，是因為職場修機器的經驗讓他懂得實作也需要清楚解釋。兩人比庭院裡許多學生年長，卻都不認為學習已結束。若晴看到亦辰先檢查出口再擺工具，發現急躁底下的用心；亦辰看她把無解的問題記下，而不是亂填答案，也看見相同的特質。他們仍在競標。當晚各自回到房間，卻都從企畫書劃掉了同一個字：「獨家」。

The first street trial placed various groups beside the station: a circus class, a jazz trio, and a magician in a fancy fairy costume. A child had built a small hut from cardboard; a heater protected the volunteers from the cold. Ruo bought berry tea in a container marked one pint while a jet passed overhead. A spinning display made the loose screw in Evan's model almost fall. He stopped the spin and raised a red signal. No miracle could replace an honest safety check. A political speaker wanted to use the stage, but the clubs had no permit for campaigning. Ruo refused to rob any group of its place in the line. She would not quit the trial, either. The station deed said the hall belonged to the city. They could tame the confusion by reading that document together.

第一次街頭試辦把不同團體聚在車站旁：馬戲課、爵士三重奏、戴華麗仙子帽的魔術師。孩子用紙板搭小屋，暖爐讓志工在冷天保持溫暖。若晴買了一杯莓果茶，容器標示一品脫，一架噴射機從頭頂飛過。旋轉展示讓亦辰模型裡的鬆動螺絲差點掉出，他立刻停止旋轉，舉起紅色訊號。安全檢查不能指望奇蹟。政治演說者想借舞台，但社團沒有選舉造勢許可。若晴不想奪走任何團體依序使用場地的權利，也不願放棄試辦。車站契據寫明活動廳屬於市府。他們可以一起讀懂文件，讓混亂平靜下來。

At a trial event in the square, Ruo found that the entrance to Evan's workshop blocked the route to her reading corner. Evan argued that the corner would hide a clear exit. They measured the path together and moved both tables. Their first useful decision came from a disagreement neither had won.

在廣場試辦活動時，若晴發現亦辰的工作桌擋住通往閱讀角的路。亦辰則認為閱讀角遮住了出口。兩人一起測量通道、移動桌子。第一次有用的決定，來自一場誰也沒贏的爭論。

A sponsor named Simon offered to fund a polished opening show if the clubs chose him as their only supplier. Ruo asked for the full contract. Evan asked about electrical safety. Simon laughed and called them slow. They agreed on one thing before leaving: neither would sign what the members had not seen.

贊助代表賽門提議資助華麗開幕式，條件是兩社只能用他的供應商。若晴索取完整合約，亦辰詢問用電安全。賽門笑他們動作太慢。兩人離開前至少同意一件事：沒有讓社員看過的文件，誰都不簽。

Before the volunteers went home, Ruo pinned two drawings to the noticeboard. One showed the station as a place where old stories could be heard. The other showed people learning to repair objects that might otherwise be thrown away. Between them was a broad blank space. She wrote a question there: 'Who is missing from these pictures?' Evan returned from packing his tools and studied the board. 'The person who needs to rest,' he said. Ruo drew a chair. 'And the person cleaning after the event,' she added. He drew a small broom. They were still strangers in many ways. She knew little about electricity, and he knew almost nothing about archives. Yet the empty space made them ask better questions than either picture had done. At the door, Evan said he would show his members the revised drawing. Ruo promised to show hers as well. The competition had not ended, but the pictures had changed.

志工回家前，若晴在公告欄貼上兩張圖。一張畫舊故事能被聽見的車站，另一張畫大家學習修好原本可能被丟掉的物品。兩張圖中間留著大片空白，她寫下：「這些圖片少了誰？」亦辰收好工具回來，看著板子說：「需要休息的人。」若晴畫上椅子。「還有活動後清掃的人。」她補充。亦辰畫了一把小掃帚。他們仍有許多不了解彼此的地方：她不懂太多電力知識，他幾乎不懂檔案。可是那片空白讓兩人提出比原圖更好的問題。到門口時，亦辰說會把修訂圖給社員看，若晴答應也會。競爭沒有結束，但圖畫已經改變。

Ruo looked once more at the public rules on the wall. A deadline could encourage careless promises, but it could also force the group to ask which decisions belonged to whom. She kept a copy for her club. Evan did the same. They left with separate folders and a newly shared problem.

若晴又看一次牆上的公開規則。期限可能逼人草率承諾，也可能迫使大家問清楚誰有權決定什麼。她替社團留一份，亦辰也一樣。兩人帶著不同資料夾離開，卻有了共同難題。
