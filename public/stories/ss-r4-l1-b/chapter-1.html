河上的紅繩

The Red Rope

河流兩岸的紅繩 · 第 1 / 25 章 · 1000以下 · 英中對照

用互動版查詞、朗讀與收藏

A small village stood beside a river, north of the hills. Nia, a young otter woman, wanted to cross the old wooden bridge with her pet bird, Dot. In the middle of the bridge, she noticed a red mark on one board. A little bug went into the crack. Nia did not laugh. She took a camera from her bag and made a picture. This was not just an old board. It moved when she put one foot near it. The other animals were coming for a picnic, and Nia wanted them to be safe.

山丘北邊的河旁有一個小村子。年輕的水獺女子妮亞帶著寵物小鳥點點，想走過舊木橋。她注意到橋中央一塊木板上有紅色的記號。一隻小蟲鑽進裂縫。妮亞沒有笑，她從袋子裡拿出相機拍了照片。那不只是一塊舊木板；她的腳靠近時，木板竟然移動了。其他動物正要來野餐，她希望大家平安。

Bo the beaver reached the river first. He was friendly and very strong, but he did not step onto the bridge. “Tell me the fact, not a guess,” he said. Nia showed him the picture. “I will not lie,” she replied. “I heard a sound, and then I saw the board move.” Bo put a small star of red cloth on a post. “We can use this as a warning,” he said. They closed both ends of the bridge. A cup of apple juice waited on the grass, but nobody was ready to drink it yet.

河狸波波第一個來到河邊。他很友善，也很強壯，卻沒有踩上橋。「告訴我事實，不要只猜。」他說。妮亞拿照片給他看。「我不會說謊，」她回答，「我先聽到聲音，然後看見木板移動。」波波把一片紅布星星掛在柱上。「我們可以用它提醒大家。」他說。兩人封住橋的兩端。草地上放著一杯蘋果汁，但此刻沒人有心情喝。

Before they left, Nia asked Bo how they could reach the houses across the river without the bridge. Bo pointed to the hill road. It was longer, but it was open. They took a second picture from the grass so no one had to step on the weak wood. Nia felt sorry for the travelers, yet she knew one safe road was better than two roads when one could break. Her small bird Dot flew home above the trees, while the friends carried the red rope to the far end.

離開前，妮亞問波波，大家不用橋要怎麼抵達對岸的住家。波波指向山丘道路。那條路比較長，但可以通行。他們從草地上拍了第二張照片，不必踩上脆弱木板。妮亞很替旅人難過，卻知道一條安全道路勝過兩條路中有一條可能斷掉。她的小鳥點點從樹上飛回家，朋友們則把紅繩拿到橋的另一端。

Fern also asked them to draw the warning sign with large letters. The sign had to be clear to an old animal and to a young child. Bo wanted to write a long message about wood and water. Nia said, “We can tell the whole story tomorrow. Right now people only need to know that they must stop.” They wrote three short words and placed the sign where the path turned. Nobody came near the loose board again that evening.

芬也請他們用大字寫警告牌，讓年老的動物和小孩都看得懂。波波想寫長長一段有關木頭和河水的說明。妮亞說：「完整故事明天可以再說。現在大家只需要知道必須停下。」於是他們寫了三個短字，把牌子放在小路轉彎處。那天傍晚，再也沒有人靠近鬆動的木板。

Luma the rabbit came with a basket of food. She stopped at the rope and looked across the water. “In the future we need a good bridge,” she said. “But today we need a safe way home.” Nia agreed. She wrote a short notice and put it where every traveler could see it. The friends went to the village leader, Fern the owl, to tell her what had happened. Fern did not promise a quick fix. She asked Nia to bring the camera tomorrow. This little picture was the first step in a much larger job.

兔子露瑪提著食物籃子走來。她停在繩子前，看向河的另一岸。「未來我們需要一座好橋，」她說，「但今天先要安全回家。」妮亞同意。她寫了簡短公告，放在每個旅人都看得見的地方。朋友們去找村長貓頭鷹芬，說明事情經過。芬沒有承諾立刻修好，只請妮亞明天帶相機來。這張小照片，成了一項大工程的第一步。

That night Nia looked at the photograph again. In it, the crack was small and the river looked calm. She understood why someone might say there was no problem. But a calm river could become strong after rain, and a small crack could become larger under heavy feet. She put the photograph beside the red cloth star. Tomorrow she would show both to the village. She did not need to be the strongest animal, or the loudest one. She needed to notice what was there, tell the truth, and ask her friends to help.

當晚妮亞又看了一次照片。照片裡裂縫很小，河水看起來也平靜。她明白為什麼有人可能說沒有問題。但平靜的河在雨後會變急，小裂縫在重腳踩踏下也會擴大。她把照片放在紅布星星旁。明天要把兩樣東西都給村民看。她不必成為最強壯、最吵鬧的動物。她需要做的是注意眼前的事實、說真話，並請朋友一起幫忙。

In the morning the old bridge was still there, but it no longer looked like an easy way across. Nia saw the red rope first. She was glad that it had stayed in place. A child came with a small bag and stopped before the sign. “Can I go to the river?” he asked. “You can look from the high path,” Nia answered, “but you cannot go onto the bridge.” He read the sign slowly and walked back. Nia felt hopeful. The rope had done its job, and the village could begin talking about a safer future. The hard part was still ahead, but nobody had to face it alone.

隔天早上舊橋還在，卻不再像一條容易通過的路。妮亞先看見紅繩，很高興它仍在原處。一個孩子背著小袋子走來，在標誌前停下。「我能去河邊嗎？」他問。「可以從高路上看，」妮亞回答，「但不能上橋。」孩子慢慢讀完牌子，走了回去。妮亞感到有希望。紅繩已經完成任務，村子可以開始討論更安全的未來。困難仍在前方，但沒有人需要獨自面對。
