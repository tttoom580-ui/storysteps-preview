兩岸的一個選擇

Two Banks, One Choice

河流兩岸的紅繩 · 第 4 / 25 章 · 1000以下 · 英中對照

In the east part of the village lived Mrs. Pine, a green turtle who knew the river well. She gave both Nia and Bo a bowl of warm water after a cold bath in the rain. “You must not cross the old bridge,” she said. “Look at the water before you look at the wood.” Bo wanted a short bridge near her house, but a large fallen tree could block that place during a flood. Nia made a choice: they would study a second place, even if it meant more work.

村子東邊住著綠色烏龜松太太，她很了解河流。妮亞和波波淋雨後洗了冷水澡，她端上兩碗溫水給他們。「你們絕不能走舊橋，」她說，「要先看水，再看木頭。」波波想在她家附近蓋一座短橋，但洪水時大樹可能堵住那個位置。妮亞作了決定：即使工作更多，也要研究第二個地點。

They walked quietly to the second bank. Luma gave a little knock on a wooden post, then drew its shape. A cute bird sat above her head. The river was a medium width here, and the ground felt firm. Suddenly a taxi came along the road and made everyone jump. The driver stopped and asked why so many animals were looking down at the river. Nia explained the plan. The driver offered to pass the news to people on the hill. It was a surprise to find another helper so soon.

他們安靜地走到第二個河岸。露瑪輕敲木柱，然後畫下它的形狀。一隻可愛的鳥停在她頭頂。這裡的河面寬度中等，土地踩起來穩固。突然一輛計程車沿路開來，大家嚇了一跳。司機停下，問一群動物為何看著河面。妮亞解釋計畫。司機說可以幫忙向山丘住戶傳話。沒想到這麼快又多一位幫手，真是意外的驚喜。

Nia sat by the water and listened without moving. In the quiet she heard small stones roll near the first place. They were being carried by the river. She made a mark in her book. Bo listened too and saw that the water did not move the same way at the second place. He did not call this proof that the second place was safe. He only said it was worth a closer look. They agreed to show both places to the engineer before making their final choice.

妮亞坐在水邊，不動聲色地傾聽。安靜之中，她聽見第一個地點附近的小石頭滾動，是河水在搬動它們。她在本子上做了記號。波波也聽著，發現第二個地點的水流方式不同。他沒有因此宣稱第二個地方已經安全，只說值得再仔細看看。他們約定先讓工程師看過兩個位置，再作最後選擇。

Luma drew two little pictures for children who might not read every word on the signs. One showed an animal stopping at a rope. The other showed a child using the open road. She added no frightening pictures. “I want them to understand,” she said, “not to be scared all day.” Mrs. Pine liked the idea and offered to put a picture near her door. The group had not made a final bridge choice yet, but they had made the warning signs easier to understand.

露瑪畫了兩張小圖給可能還看不懂每個字的孩子。一張是動物停在繩子前，另一張是孩子走開放道路。她沒有畫可怕的畫面。「我希望孩子懂，」她說，「不是整天害怕。」松太太喜歡這個主意，願意在門旁貼一張。團隊還沒選定最後的橋址，卻已讓警示標誌更容易理解。

Mrs. Pine watched their marks from the path. “A bridge is not a race,” she said. “A good choice gives us time to think.” Bo nodded. He had wanted to work fast, but he did not want to make anyone afraid. The group went back before the ground became too wet. Nia wrote that the second place looked better, but it was not yet approved. They would need a person who knew how to measure wood and water before they could build anything.

松太太站在路上，看著他們留下的記號。「造橋不是比賽，」她說，「好選擇要給大家思考的時間。」波波點頭。他原本想趕快做，卻也不想讓人害怕。泥地變得太濕之前，他們便回去了。妮亞記下第二個地點看起來比較好，但還沒有獲得批准。在開始建造前，他們需要一位懂得測量木材和水位的人。

After dinner, Mrs. Pine told Nia a story from her childhood. A great rain had once put a tree across the narrow part of the river. The water had risen behind it and run over a nearby path. “The river can change its own road,” she said. “We cannot tell it where to go.” Nia wrote this down but marked it as an old story, not a new measurement. The difference mattered. Tomorrow they would compare the story with the old marks on the bank, then ask the engineer what the ground could hold.

晚飯後，松太太向妮亞說起童年往事。一場大雨曾把樹沖到河道狹窄處，水在樹後升高，漫過旁邊小路。「河會改變自己的路，」她說，「我們不能命令它往哪裡走。」妮亞記下來，也標明這是舊故事，不是新測量。這個差別很重要。明天他們會把故事與河岸舊記號比較，再問工程師土地能承受什麼。

Bo walked home with his tools and thought about the two possible places for the bridge. He had once believed the shortest place must be the best place. Now he was not so sure. The old turtle's story had shown him a different way to think. At the turn in the road, he met a child carrying a little bowl of water. The child asked, “Will you finish the bridge tomorrow?” Bo knelt and answered, “Not tomorrow. First we must make sure the place is right.” The child looked disappointed, but then asked if the path would be safe. “That is exactly what we are trying to learn,” Bo said.

波波帶工具回家，想著兩個可能的橋址。他曾以為最短的地方一定最好，現在不再那麼確定。老烏龜的故事讓他換個角度思考。在路口，他遇見一個端著小水碗的孩子。孩子問：「你們明天會把橋造好嗎？」波波蹲下回答：「不會。得先確定地方選對。」孩子有些失望，接著又問路會不會安全。「這正是我們努力想知道的事。」波波說。
