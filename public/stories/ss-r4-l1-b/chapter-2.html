繞遠路回家

The Long Way Home

河流兩岸的紅繩 · 第 2 / 25 章 · 1000以下 · 英中對照

The old bridge was closed, so everyone had to take the hill road. A small apartment above the church was home to an old mouse. She could not walk far. Luma went to ask if anybody could bring her lunch. “I can do it,” said Bo. “But we must be clear about the route.” They drew a line around the deep pond and marked the third turn with a white stone. They did not want anyone to forget the way. In this quiet part of the country, one closed bridge could change many ordinary days.

舊橋封閉後，大家只能走山丘上的道路。教堂上方一間小公寓住著一隻年老的老鼠，她走不了太遠。露瑪去問有沒有人能送午餐給她。「我可以，」波波說，「不過路線一定要清楚。」他們畫出繞過深池塘的路，並在第三個轉彎放上白石頭，不希望任何人忘記方向。在這個安靜的鄉村，一座橋封閉就能改變許多平凡日子。

A reporter named Kit came from the next village. He held a pen and a telephone, not a camera. “Is the bridge falling?” he asked. “We do not know that,” Nia answered. “One piece moved. That is serious enough to stop using it.” Kit wrote only what she said. Later he sat on a sofa at the meeting hall and listened to the people. Someone guessed that the bridge could be fixed in one afternoon. Fern shook her head. “That is only a guess. We must look under it from the safe bank first.”

名叫奇特的記者從鄰村來。他帶著筆與電話，沒有帶相機。「橋快塌了嗎？」他問。「我們不知道，」妮亞回答，「我們只知道一塊木板會動，這就已經嚴重到需要停用。」奇特如實記下她的話。稍後他坐在集會所的沙發上，聽村民說話。有人猜只要一個下午就能修好。芬搖頭：「那只是猜測。我們先從安全的河岸看橋底。」

Nia and Luma walked the hill road together with a basket of warm bread. Their feet were tired long before they reached the old mouse's door. Nia began to understand how hard this new walk would be for someone with short legs or a heavy bag. They stopped once beside a flat stone, drank water, and continued at an easy pace. When they arrived, the old mouse did not ask for a quick bridge. She only asked them to make sure everyone knew the safe road.

妮亞與露瑪一起提著熱麵包走山丘路。還沒到老老鼠的門口，她們的腳就累了。妮亞開始理解，對腿短或提重物的人來說，新路有多難走。她們在一塊平石旁休息、喝水，再以舒服的速度繼續。抵達後，老老鼠沒有催著造快橋，只希望她們確定每個人都知道安全的道路。

The reporter waited until the old mouse had finished eating before asking questions. He wanted a picture of her walking, but she said no. Kit put his pen away at once. “Your story is yours,” he told her. Luma thanked him for listening. Later they made a plan for a small rest place halfway along the hill. The rest place would cost little and would help people before any new bridge was ready. Nia added it to the village book.

記者等老老鼠吃完才提問。他想拍她走路的照片，但她拒絕了。奇特立刻收起筆。「故事由妳決定要不要說。」他告訴她。露瑪謝謝他肯傾聽。後來她們決定在山路中段設一個小休息處。那裡花費不多，卻能在新橋完成前幫上忙。妮亞把它寫進村莊紀錄本。

“I am here to help,” said Luma. She brought a bag for the old mouse and offered to pay for the food with her own coins. Fern said the village could pay for shared help, so no single family had to carry the cost. The group wrote down names and times. One neighbor was late, yet no one was angry. He had used the long road to take warm soup to a sick friend. Nia learned that fixing a bridge started with caring for people who could not cross it.

「我來幫忙。」露瑪說。她提了一袋食物給老老鼠，本來想自掏腰包付款。芬說村子可以支付共同照護的費用，不必由一個家庭獨自負擔。大家寫下姓名和時間。一位鄰居遲到了，卻沒有人生氣，因為他繞遠路送熱湯給生病的朋友。妮亞明白，修橋的第一步，是先照顧無法過橋的人。

In the evening Luma put a small chair by the door of the old mouse's apartment. She asked whether a traveler could rest there, then carry food on to the next home. The old mouse liked the idea and offered drinking water. Nia drew a mark on the hill map. Small places to rest would make the road easier for children and older animals. This did not solve the bridge problem, but it solved a part of the problem people were facing today. Luma said, “We should not wait for a perfect bridge before we help someone who is tired now.”

傍晚露瑪把小椅子放在老老鼠公寓門旁，問旅人是否能先在此休息，再把食物送往下一家。老老鼠喜歡這想法，願意提供飲水。妮亞在山路地圖上畫了記號。小休息點能讓孩子和老動物走路更容易。這並沒有解決橋的問題，卻解決了人們今天面對的一小部分困難。露瑪說：「我們不該等完美的橋造好，才幫現在就累了的人。」

The next day the long road was busy. A mother pulled a little cart, and two children walked beside her. They rested at the new chair for a few minutes, then continued. Nia watched from the safe corner and saw that the cart could turn there without trouble. She made a note to keep that part of the path clear. She also asked Luma to check whether the old mouse needed food tomorrow. Luma was happy to help, but they agreed to ask others to share the next trip. Caring for a village meant making a plan that helpers could keep, not one that left them too tired to return.

隔天長路很忙。一位母親拉著小推車，兩個孩子走在旁邊。他們在新椅子旁休息幾分鐘，然後繼續。妮亞從安全轉角觀察，發現推車能順利轉彎，便記下要保持那裡通暢。她也請露瑪問老老鼠明天是否需要食物。露瑪很樂意，但兩人同意下一趟要請其他人分擔。照顧村子意味著安排幫手能持續做下去的工作，而不是把人累到無法再來。
