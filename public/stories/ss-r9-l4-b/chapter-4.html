為雨而畫的地圖

A Map for Rain

1994，榕灣週報的最後一頁 · 第 4 / 25 章 · 3000–4000 · 英中對照

用互動版查詞、朗讀與收藏

The school lunch story brought letters from readers who had never written to the weekly before. Among them was a request from the town librarian to accompany an inspection of the old hillside road. It was the main attraction for visitors who came to see the magnificent sea from the ridge, but its narrow bridge flooded each summer. The town office had prepared a new evacuation diagram for a storm drill. Caiping arrived with Xiaoyu and found the diagram displayed beside a bright poster about recreation trails. A retired diplomat who lived nearby studied both sheets. "The same road cannot be a safe exit and a sightseeing route during a storm," he said. Caiping asked the official who had drawn the maps. Nobody in the room knew, and nobody had kept the original draft.

學校午餐報導引來不少過去從未寫信給週報的讀者。其中一封是圖書館員的邀請，希望采蘋陪同勘查山坡舊路。這條路因山脊上壯麗的海景而成為遊客景點，窄橋卻每年夏天都會淹水。鎮公所為風雨演練畫了新的疏散示意圖。采蘋和小雨抵達時，圖就貼在鮮豔的休閒步道海報旁。住附近的一名退休外交官看著兩張紙說：「同一條路不能在暴風雨中又當安全出口、又當觀光動線。」采蘋詢問地圖的製作者，室內竟沒人知道，也沒有人保留原始草稿。

At the bridge a mechanic loosened a metal cover and showed them where leaves blocked the drain. He had repaired buses for twenty years but did not pretend to be an engineer. A thick maintenance manual explained the cover's design, yet the new map treated the bridge as if it never flooded. The official called the drawing experimental and said it could be corrected later. The mechanic answered that an experiment with people fleeing danger required more care than a drawing contest. An essential warning had to be understood by someone who was cold, frightened, or carrying a child. Caiping wrote down the exchange without choosing a winner. She asked for actual flood measurements and for photographs of the bridge under different conditions.

到了橋邊，修車師傅鬆開金屬蓋，指出落葉堵住排水口的位置。他修了二十年公車，卻不假裝自己是工程師。厚厚的維修手冊介紹了蓋子的設計，新的疏散圖卻彷彿橋從來不會淹水。公所人員稱這只是實驗性地圖，日後還能修正。師傅回答，讓人們在危險時逃離的試驗，必須比畫圖比賽更謹慎。重要警告應該讓寒冷、恐懼或抱著孩子的人也能理解。采蘋把對話記下來，不急著決定誰勝誰負。她要求查看實際淹水測量值，以及不同狀況下橋樑的照片。

The next morning a school nurse attended the planning meeting. Her first greeting was practical: "Where will people wash their hands?" If the storm shelter had no safe water, a small germ could turn a crowded night into a second emergency. The official hesitated before answering. The plan described roads and buses but not toilets, drinking water, or the needs of older residents. Caiping sensed his hesitation was not dishonest; he had never been assigned those questions. She suggested recording the limits of his responsibility rather than pretending the plan covered everything. The retired diplomat offered to contact two neighborhood groups whose members could test the instructions. It was a modest proposal, but one that might strengthen the map through ordinary experience.

隔天早上，學校護理師來參加規劃會議。她一開口便問了個實際問題：「人們要在哪裡洗手？」如果避難所沒有乾淨的水，微小病菌就可能讓擁擠的一夜變成第二場危機。公所人員猶豫了一下才回答。計畫描述道路與公車，卻沒列出廁所、飲水，或長者需求。采蘋覺得他的遲疑不是說謊，而是根本沒人分派他負責這些問題。她建議明白記下他的責任界線，不要假裝計畫已涵蓋一切。退休外交官願意聯絡兩個里民團體試讀指引。這提案不大，卻可能靠一般人的經驗強化地圖。

Xiaoyu found a weather chart copied from a satellite bulletin sent by fax to the harbor office. It showed a storm approaching from beyond the horizon, although it could not tell them which neighborhood would flood first. The town's outstanding young science teacher helped explain the difference between a forecast and a street-level decision. She drew a simple line around the low areas and asked volunteers to walk the planned route at a normal pace. Nobody was asked to enter a dangerous road. During the safe section of the test, the signs were too high for a child and too small for someone with weak eyesight. The group stopped, measured the problem, and took notes. The result was not a scandal, but it was news worth printing.

小雨找到一張從港務處傳真氣象公告複印的衛星雲圖。它顯示暴風正在地平線之外接近，卻不能指出哪個街區會先淹水。鎮上傑出的年輕科學老師協助解釋「預報」與「街道上的決定」有何不同。她沿著低窪地區畫線，請志工以正常速度試走規劃路線，沒人需要踏進危險路段。安全區段的測試顯示，標誌對孩子而言掛得太高，對視力較差的人又太小。大家停下來量測、記錄。這並非驚天醜聞，卻是值得登報的消息。

By Friday the office had published a corrected map with the bridge marked as closed in heavy rain. The revised plan included contact numbers and a blank space for neighborhood updates. Caiping printed the change alongside the reason for it, with credit to everyone who had contributed. Her father worried that the story would offend the same office that purchased public notices. She understood the risk and promised to publish the office's explanation in full, provided it fit on the same page. "We need to criticize what failed and show what improved," she said. When the weekly came off the press, its map was plain rather than beautiful. But for a reader trying to find a safe route at night, she hoped plain truth would be more useful than magnificent color.

到了星期五，公所公布更正版地圖，註明大雨時橋樑封閉。新的計畫附上聯絡電話，並保留空欄供各里更新。采蘋把改動內容與原因並列印出，也向提供建議的人致謝。父親擔心報導得罪向週報購買公告版面的公所。她理解風險，仍答應完整刊登公所的說明，只要它能放在相同版面。「我們需要批評失敗的部分，也要呈現改好的部分。」她說。新一期印出時，地圖樸素而不華麗。但采蘋希望，對夜間找路的讀者而言，樸實的真相比漂亮的顏色更有用。
