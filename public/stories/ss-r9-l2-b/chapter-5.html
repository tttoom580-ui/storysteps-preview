修繕桌旁的雷聲

Thunder at the Table

雨季借你一把傘 · 第 5 / 25 章 · 1000–2000 · 英中對照

用互動版查詞、朗讀與收藏

A week of rain left the repair team tired and hungry. Iris lost her appetite after finding three umbrellas with the same loose joint. Jun asked everyone to pay attention to the pattern rather than blame a volunteer. Mei made a brilliant suggestion: place the damaged items beside a drawing that showed the joint from three angles. The design group and the repair group had been in conflict over who should inspect the work. Iris wanted the makers to test their own parts; Jun wanted a second person to check every repair. They finally discovered that both kinds of checks were useful, because a maker might know the mechanism while a new user noticed a different risk. They hoped to discover whether all three failed joints came from the same batch.

連續一週下雨，修繕小組又累又餓。艾莉絲發現三把傘的同一個接頭都鬆了，頓時沒了胃口。俊請大家注意共同的問題，不要怪某位志工。美提出一個很棒的建議：把損壞的雨傘放在一張從三個角度畫出接頭的圖旁。設計組和維修組一直為誰該檢驗成果起衝突。艾莉絲主張製作者自己測試，俊則希望每項修理再由第二人檢查。最後他們發現兩種檢查都重要：製作者了解機關，新使用者卻可能發現不同的危險。 他們希望查明三個壞接頭是否都出自同一批產品。

The connection between the loose joints appeared when they looked at the order sheets. All three umbrellas had been bought from a domestic supplier during the same month. A student proposed an electrical tool to tighten them faster, but the metal table was wet and thunder was already close. Iris refused. She moved the work indoors and showed a safe hand method instead. The failure was not a mass disaster, only three damaged parts, yet she took it seriously. They could replace the joints and ask Mrs. Lan to examine the results before the umbrellas went back into service. Jun wrote the decision in the shared log where anyone could read it.

他們查看採購單時，才發現鬆動接頭之間的關聯。三把傘都是同一個月向國內供應商購買的。一名學生提議用電動工具加速鎖緊，但金屬桌面很濕，雷聲也愈來愈近。艾莉絲拒絕，將工作移到室內，改示範安全的手動方法。這不是大規模事故，只是三個零件損壞，但她仍認真看待。他們可以更換接頭，再請蘭女士檢查，確認安全才讓傘重新上架。俊把決議寫進人人可查的共同紀錄。

At dinner, the team shared hot noodle soup in the student kitchen. Someone praised Iris for the clear drawing, and she pointed out that Mei had thought of it. Outside, a rooster painted on a food truck sign looked almost angry under the rain. Jun noticed Iris rubbing the skin on her cold fingers and offered a dry towel. She accepted it but asked him not to treat her like a child. He apologized without making a joke. Iris softened her voice. “I like help,” she said. “I just want to choose when I need it.” Jun promised to ask first. That small exchange did more for their growing trust than the praise at the table.

晚餐時，大家在學生廚房分吃熱麵湯。有人稱讚艾莉絲的圖畫得清楚，她指出那其實是美想出的點子。外面餐車招牌上的公雞在雨中看起來幾乎像在生氣。俊注意到艾莉絲在搓揉凍紅的手指，遞給她一條乾毛巾。她接過，卻請他別把她當小孩。他道歉，沒有開玩笑。艾莉絲語氣放軟：「我喜歡別人幫忙，只是想自己選擇什麼時候需要。」俊答應以後先問。這段小對話，比餐桌上的稱讚更能增加他們的信任。

A visitor from a snowy northern town asked whether the borrowing system could spread beyond the university. Iris said they should not promise more than they could support. The visitor agreed, saying a simple wool cover on a handle had helped her grandmother grip an umbrella in winter. Jun asked if the cover became slippery whenever it got wet. They tested a sample and found that it did. This mattered because winter rain could be as dangerous as summer storms. Iris kept the idea in her notebook but marked it “not approved.” It was a useful reminder that a kind idea still needed a safe material.

一名來自北方雪鄉的訪客問，借傘制度能否推廣到校外。艾莉絲說，他們不該承諾超出能力的事。訪客表示理解，又提起一種簡單的羊毛傘柄套，曾讓祖母冬天比較好握傘。俊問，那種套子淋濕後會不會變滑。他們測試了樣品，發現確實會。這很重要，因為冬雨和夏季暴風雨同樣可能危險。艾莉絲把點子留在筆記本裡，卻標上「尚未核可」。好意仍需安全材料，這是重要的提醒。

That night, the thunder grew louder and the office ordered the outdoor repair area closed. Iris and Jun took the remaining tools inside, checked the power switches, and left a note for the next shift. The tenth umbrella from the first day had finally returned, along with a thank-you card. The card said that its owner had reached the late bus without falling on the dark stairs. Iris read it twice. Jun suggested placing it on the board, but she asked permission from the writer first because even a kind message belonged to someone. He smiled at how firmly she now defended the privacy rule they had argued over.

當晚雷聲更大，事務處下令關閉戶外修繕區。艾莉絲和俊把工具搬進室內，檢查電源開關，並留字條給下一班。第一天尚未歸還的第十把傘終於回來，還附上一張感謝卡。卡片說，借用者因此順利趕上末班公車，也沒在昏暗的樓梯跌倒。艾莉絲讀了兩次。俊建議把卡片貼到展示板上，但她先要求徵得寫信人的同意，因為即使是善意的訊息，也屬於某個人。他看著她堅定維護兩人曾爭論過的隱私規則，微笑了。

When the lights in the hall dimmed for the night, the students sat on the steps for a minute before going home. They could hear water running through the newly cleaned drain. Iris said she used to think that a finished object was the proof of good design. Now she saw that careful repair, clear signs, and honest reports mattered just as much. Jun admitted that he had once believed a good rule could fix almost anything. He had learned that people also needed objects they could actually use. Their views had not become identical. They had simply learned how to leave room for each other inside a shared plan.

走廊的燈調暗準備夜間關閉時，學生們先在臺階坐了一會兒才回家。他們聽見水流過剛清好的排水孔。艾莉絲說，過去她以為完成一件作品就是好設計的證明；現在才明白，仔細修理、清楚標示與誠實回報同樣重要。俊承認，自己以前相信好規則幾乎能解決所有問題，如今學到人們也需要真正能用的物品。他們的看法並未變得完全相同，只是學會在共同計畫裡為彼此留下空間。
