被人拿走的台詞

The Lines Someone Took Away

被改掉的那句話 · 第 1 / 25 章 · 2000–3000 · 英中對照

用互動版查詞、朗讀與收藏

Mara Chen, twenty-six, returned to Harbor Lantern Theatre carrying the eighth draft of her play, When the River Rose. At rehearsal, the actress Nia raised an empty sheet. Every line spoken by the mother character had vanished from the new copies. Mara had not approved the changes. The room was not as simple as it looked. Each chair had a person who needed to trust the people onstage, and every actor brought a different reason for taking part. Mara watched those reasons appear in small movements: a page turned slowly, a hand stayed raised, or someone waited before answering. She was learning that listening could be visible, even during silence. They looked again at the paper on the table. The important part was not how many people agreed immediately but whether anyone could point to what had actually happened. Mara wrote one more question under the date and kept the answer space open.

二十六歲的陳瑪拉帶著《河水漲起時》第八版劇本回到港燈劇院。排練時，女演員妮雅舉起一張幾乎空白的紙：母親角色的所有台詞都從新印本裡消失了。瑪拉從未同意這些修改。排練室沒有表面上那麼簡單。每張椅子上都有人需要信任舞台上的工作者，每位演員也有不同的參與理由。瑪拉從細小動作中看見那些理由：有人緩緩翻頁、有人持續舉手，也有人在回答前先等待。她開始明白，傾聽即使沒有聲音，也能讓人看見。他們再看桌上的紙。重要的不是有多少人立刻同意，而是能否指出實際發生了什麼事。瑪拉在日期下多寫一個問題，留下尚未填寫的答案欄。

Mara had written the mother as a woman who stood in a doorway during a flood and refused to leave a neighbor behind. The missing speech was not a clever ending. It was the moment when she asked why the official warning had come so late. Without it, the character became a face in the crowd.

瑪拉筆下的母親在洪水時站在門口，拒絕丟下鄰居。被刪的台詞不是華麗結局，而是她問出官方警告為何來得太晚的時刻。少了那句話，這個角色只剩人群裡的一張臉。

Theo Peng, the twenty-nine-year-old lighting designer, found two signs on a discarded page: a small blue pencil dot and a black square printed beside a deleted sentence. Mara photographed both before anyone moved the papers. She decided to investigate instead of accusing the nearest person. Theo studied the marks made by the stage lights on the wooden floor. The smallest change in position could move a shadow across a speaker's face. He liked machines because their actions could often be tested. People were harder. A person might change a story out of care, shame, fear, or ambition, and the same result could hide very different reasons. He told Mara not to skip that difference.

二十九歲的燈光設計師彭提歐在廢紙上發現兩種記號：藍色鉛筆小點，以及刪除句子旁列印的黑色方塊。瑪拉先拍照留存，沒有立刻指責身邊的人。提歐研究舞台燈在木地板上形成的痕跡。位置只要有小小變動，陰影就可能移到說話者臉上。他喜歡機器，因為許多動作可以測試；人卻更難理解。同一項改動，可能來自關心、羞愧、恐懼或野心，結果相同，理由卻很不同。他提醒瑪拉不要忽略這點。

Mara wrote down everyday scenes from the neighbourhood, hoping to make each part of the play feel lived in. The square outside was busy with various acts. A circus group was setting up beside a wooden hut, while a jazz band tested its speakers. Someone wore a fairy costume; another waved a fancy magician’s hat. Mara held a cup of berry tea, a pint marked on the paper container, and tried to tame her shaking hands. A heater hummed under a stall. Overhead, a jet crossed the dark sky. The singer onstage made the chorus spin into a brighter tune, and the crowd called it a miracle. Mara did not want to rob anyone of joy, but the printed program felt like a deed giving away her own work. She found a loose screw near the sound desk and set it safely aside. When a red light gave the signal to begin, she could no longer pretend the problem was small. A political speech began between songs, and the host asked whether anyone wanted to quit the noisy square.

瑪拉記下街區生活裡的日常場景，希望每一幕都更真實。廣場上的表演十分多樣。馬戲團在木屋旁架設器材，爵士樂團試音。有人穿著仙子服裝，有人揮舞華麗的魔術師帽。瑪拉握著一杯莓果茶，紙杯上標著一品脫的容量，努力讓顫抖的手平靜下來。攤位下的暖爐嗡嗡作響，一架噴射機飛過夜空。台上歌手把副歌唱得旋轉般明亮，觀眾稱讚像奇蹟。瑪拉不想奪走大家的快樂，可印刷節目單就像把她的作品轉讓出去的契據。她看到音控桌旁有顆鬆動螺絲，先放到安全的地方。紅燈發出開演訊號時，她不能再假裝問題不重要。歌曲之間開始了一場政治演說，主持人還問有沒有人想離開吵鬧的廣場。

Nia placed the empty page beside the complete one. She told Mara that an actress could suggest changes, but she needed to know which words she was being asked to defend. Mara promised not to demand loyalty before offering an explanation. The promise left both of them more exposed and more willing to continue.

妮雅把空白頁放在完整稿旁，說演員可以提出改動，但必須知道自己被要求捍衛哪些文字。瑪拉答應不會在說明之前就要求忠誠。這項承諾讓兩人都更無法躲避問題，卻也更願意繼續。

Director Celeste said the new version would be easier for the sponsor to support. Mara answered that easy did not mean honest. She asked the cast to read her original scene once, without making any public claim about who had edited it. A page could look official simply because it was clean and neatly printed. Mara had learned not to confuse appearance with permission. Before using any document, she wanted to know who made it, when it was prepared, and whether the person it described had agreed to its purpose. Some of those answers were missing. Leaving a space blank felt uncomfortable, but inventing an answer would be worse. In her notebook Mara drew a small star beside the fact that had been checked, and a question mark beside the claim that still needed work. She did not erase the question mark when it made her uncomfortable. A good script had to survive contact with facts.

導演席莉絲說，新版本比較容易獲得贊助商支持。瑪拉回答，容易不代表誠實。她請演員先按原稿讀過一次，在弄清改稿者前不公開指控。一張紙只要印得整齊乾淨，就容易顯得很正式。瑪拉已學會不把外觀和授權混為一談。使用任何文件前，她都想確認由誰製作、何時製作，以及其中被描述的人是否同意這種用途。有些答案仍缺失。留下空白很不舒服，但自行編造會更糟。瑪拉在筆記本裡，已查核的事實旁畫小星星，仍待處理的說法旁畫問號。即使問號令她不自在，她也沒有擦掉。好的劇本必須經得起事實檢驗。

Nia spoke the mother's final question, and the room went quiet. For the first time, Mara heard how much that missing voice mattered. On the back of the deleted page somebody had written, ASK HER FIRST. The handwriting was careful, not threatening. No one agreed on every detail. The disagreement did not mean the company was failing. Mara set down the question in plain language and asked each person to explain which part mattered to them. One worried about money, another about safety, and another about the right to be heard. Once those interests were named, the argument became a little easier to face without turning it into a personal attack.

妮雅念出母親角色最後一句問話，室內頓時安靜。瑪拉第一次真正聽出那段失去的聲音有多重要。被刪頁背面有人寫著「先問她本人」，字跡工整，並沒有威脅意味。沒有人在每個細節都意見一致，但分歧不代表劇團失敗。瑪拉用簡單的話寫下問題，請每個人說明自己重視的部分。有人擔心錢，有人擔心安全，有人重視被傾聽的權利。當各種關切被說出來，爭論就比較容易面對，也比較不會變成人身攻擊。
