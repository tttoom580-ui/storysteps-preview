第一次誠實的讀劇

The First Honest Reading

被改掉的那句話 · 第 5 / 25 章 · 2000–3000 · 英中對照

用互動版查詞、朗讀與收藏

Mara met Ada, now sixty-three, at the history centre with a staff witness present. She apologized for writing the family conversation into the first draft. Ada accepted the apology but declined to let that scene be performed. She did agree to speak about the night at the river gate. They stopped to share water and check on the workers behind the set. A performance could not exist through the actors alone. Someone repaired a wheel, somebody brought clean cups, and somebody else counted exits. Mara had once thought these tasks were separate from writing. Now she understood how the words on a page depended on quiet decisions by people whose names might never be shouted after the curtain. A quiet conversation followed near the stage door. Mara let the other person finish before giving her own view. She had sometimes been quick to defend her first idea. Today she wanted to understand why the second idea existed at all.

瑪拉在歷史中心、由職員在場見證下，見到了六十三歲的艾達。她為把家人的對話寫進初稿道歉。艾達接受道歉，但拒絕讓那場戲上演；她願意談那晚水閘前發生的事。大家停下來喝水，並關心布景後方的工作者。一場演出不可能只靠演員完成：有人修理輪子，有人送來乾淨杯子，也有人清點出口。瑪拉以前覺得這些工作與寫作無關，如今明白紙上的文字仰賴許多安靜的決定，而決定者的名字未必會在謝幕後被高喊。舞台門旁接著發生了一段安靜對話。瑪拉先讓對方說完，再表達自己的看法。她以前有時太快維護最初想法，今天卻想先弄懂第二種意見為何出現。

Ada did not rush to answer the questions. She folded the form once, opened it again, and asked Mara to read the choices aloud. Mara did so without giving advice about which boxes to mark. When Ada finally selected the stage option, her hand was steady. That choice mattered more than a generous speech from the writer.

艾達沒有急著回答。她把表格摺起又攤開，請瑪拉把各選項念出來。瑪拉照做，沒有暗示該勾哪一格。艾達最後選擇可在舞台使用時，手十分平穩。這個選擇比作家熱情的演說更重要。

Rather than asking for one broad signature, Mara separated the new form into choices: public name or no name, exact words or a summary, live stage or printed booklet. Ada chose to use her first name, keep the gate account, and remove her daughter's words. The theatre had a rule that any performer could ask for a break without having to describe a private reason. Mara had once considered it a detail on a long notice board. She now saw why it mattered. People did better work when they were not afraid of punishment for speaking. She reminded the group that care needed a real procedure, not merely a kind tone.

瑪拉沒有要求一個概括的簽名，而是把新表格分成不同選項：公開全名或匿名、原話或摘要、現場演出或印刷手冊。艾達選擇只用名字、保留水閘描述，移除女兒的話。劇院有條規定：演員可以要求休息，不必公開私人理由。瑪拉以前只把它當成公告板上的小細節，如今明白它的重要。當人不怕因開口受到懲罰，就更能做好工作。她提醒大家，關心需要真實程序，不只是一種溫柔語氣。

An ordinary scene outside the theatre sometimes helped an actor understand an extraordinary moment onstage. The breakfast table held bacon and ripe fruit. Someone spread a carpet to stop the chairs from moving. A father began to complain about his considerable travel cost, then stopped to cough and laugh at himself. He agreed to engage with the listening exercise after Mara explained its purpose. A fake festival ticket lay beside a flashlight in the garage, where a small motor kept the fan running. June held her jaw when the old microphone failed, but Theo quietly fixed the cable. A child with a peanut allergy sat near the exit so the snack could be moved away. Mara asked each singer to pronounce the last word clearly and take responsibility for the sound. Outside, a sack of flour slipped from a delivery bike. The fall was harmless, yet it reminded her of the sorrow she had carried for days. Steam rose from tea in the corner, and the group chose a calm discussion instead of the violent argument some visitors had expected.

舞台外的普通場景，有時能幫演員理解台上不尋常的時刻。早餐桌上有培根和成熟水果。有人鋪了地毯，避免椅子滑動。一名父親抱怨自己花了相當多的交通費，隨即咳了兩聲，笑自己太激動。瑪拉解釋活動目的後，他願意參與試聽。車庫裡，手電筒旁放著一張假音樂節票券，小馬達讓風扇持續轉動。舊麥克風故障時，君緊張得托住下巴，提歐則默默修好連接線。一名對花生過敏的孩子坐在出口附近，方便移開零食。瑪拉請每位歌手清楚發出最後一個字的音，也對自己的聲音負責。外頭，一袋麵粉從送貨腳踏車滑落。沒有人受傷，卻讓她想起幾天來的悲傷。角落茶杯冒出蒸氣，大家選擇冷靜討論，而不是某些訪客預期的激烈爭吵。

After the reading, Mara returned to the seat where Ada had sat. She imagined how strange it must be to hear an actress speak a memory from long ago. The theatre could give that memory a different shape, but it could not become its owner. Mara underlined that thought on a new page.

讀劇後，瑪拉回到艾達坐過的位置。她想像一個人聽見演員重述自己多年以前的記憶會多麼奇妙。劇院可以賦予記憶不同形狀，卻不能因此成為它的主人。瑪拉在新頁上劃線記下這個想法。

At rehearsal Nia read the revised scene. The mother's questions were still present, but now they came from a person who had chosen to share them. Celeste called the scene less dramatic. The cast disagreed; a quiet answer could be more powerful than a shouted one. Mara was learning to separate a question about responsibility from a wish for revenge. A careful account might show that several people had made poor choices for different reasons. If the play pretended that one person caused every loss, it would be easy to follow and hard to trust. She wanted an audience to leave with questions they could use outside the building. Someone passed Mara a warm cup of tea after the meeting. She accepted it and stood quietly by the window. For a few minutes she let herself be tired without deciding that the work was hopeless. Tomorrow's questions could wait until the team had rested.

讀劇時，妮雅念出修訂後的場景。母親的提問仍在，卻是由願意分享的人授權而來。席莉絲說情緒不夠戲劇化，演員們卻不同意：平靜的回答可能比喊叫更有力。瑪拉慢慢學會把責任問題和報復慾望分開。謹慎的記述可能指出幾個人出於不同理由做了不好的決定。如果劇本假裝所有損失都來自同一個人，雖然容易理解，卻很難令人信任。她希望觀眾離開劇院後，仍能帶走有用的問題。會議後有人遞給瑪拉一杯熱茶。她接過，安靜站在窗邊。幾分鐘裡，她容許自己疲倦，而不因此認定工作無望。明天的問題可以等團隊休息後再處理。

After rehearsal, Mara thanked Nia for refusing the earlier version. Nia said, 'You listened, so I came back.' Their first five days ended without a solution to the anonymous edits, but they had repaired a wrong decision of their own. The question of credit remained on Mara's mind. A name in a program could honor genuine work, but it could also hide the labor of someone who had not been asked. She proposed a simple record of contributions that people could check before publication. June liked the idea because it treated the people behind the curtain as collaborators instead of invisible helpers.

排練後，瑪拉感謝妮雅拒演早期版本。妮雅說：「因為妳願意聽，我才回來。」前五天仍沒找出匿名改稿者，卻先修正了自己造成的不當決定。瑪拉心裡一直掛念署名問題。節目單上的名字可以尊重真正的工作，也可能掩蓋未被徵詢的付出。她提議公開前先讓相關人士核對貢獻紀錄。君喜歡這項提議，因為它把幕後人員視為合作者，而不是看不見的幫手。
