Boxes of Memory

1998，海禾戲院不打烊 · 第 2 / 25 章 · 3000–4000 · 英中對照

用互動版查詞、朗讀與收藏

The town clerk spread old maps across a table. Haihe had begun as a fishing village, then grown around a rice mill and a small fort on the headland. A museum display described an ancient fossil found near the cliffs. Another poster announced a lecture about the atom and the atomic age, when people had believed new machines would solve every problem. Yulan listened because these scattered histories explained why the cinema's collection was unusual. People had brought school films, newsreels, and travel pictures there for decades. The combination of entertainment and local memory was delicate; a single careless removal could separate a film from the story that made it valuable. She began making a list rather than taking anything home.

鎮公所職員把舊地圖攤在桌上。海禾原本是漁村，後來圍繞碾米廠與岬角上的小堡壘發展。博物館展覽介紹過海崖附近發現的古代化石，另一張海報則預告「原子與原子時代」講座，談人們曾相信新機器能解決所有問題的年代。雨蘭認真聽，因為這些零散的歷史說明了戲院收藏何以特殊。數十年來，大家把學校影片、新聞短片和旅行影像帶來此地。娛樂與地方記憶的結合很脆弱，只要隨意搬走一卷底片，就可能讓影片失去它原本的故事。她開始造冊，而不是急著把東西帶回家。

Back at the cinema, she measured the extent of water damage with Qin. Rain had crossed the storage-room wall, though the film cans on the highest shelf remained dry. Qin's gaze stopped on a box labeled IMMIGRATION STORIES. It contained recorded interviews with families who had arrived from other countries or from distant parts of the island. "Some of them never told these stories at home," Qin said. Her voice became an intimate murmur. Yulan understood that a photograph of the landscape could not replace a person's account of reaching it. She promised to ask families for permission before showing any interview. "I can guarantee care," she said, "not that everybody will agree with me."

回到戲院後，雨蘭和秦姨一起測量漏水的範圍。雨水滲過儲藏室牆壁，但高架上的片盒仍是乾的。秦姨的目光停在一箱標著「移居故事」的錄影帶上，裡面記錄了從其他國家或島上遠方搬來的家庭訪談。「有些人回家後，從來不談這些事，」秦姨低聲說，語氣十分私密。雨蘭明白，風景照片不能取代一個人抵達此地的故事。她承諾放映訪談前會先徵得家屬同意。「我能保證謹慎處理，」她說，「但無法保證大家都同意我的做法。」

A bicycle messenger arrived from the neighboring village with a brown envelope. Inside was a letter from Shen Wen, a former classmate now working as an accountant. He had seen the theater notice and offered to review the books, but asked to be paid only if a proper budget could be agreed on. Yulan liked the honesty. She wrote back that she needed someone who would retain the original receipts and let the committee inspect copies. She would also revise any wrong figure in public. In the evening they talked beside the pier, watching a ferry begin its short voyage across the bay. Wen said that being productive did not always mean making money; sometimes it meant recording exactly what money had been spent.

一名從鄰村騎腳踏車來的送信員帶來牛皮紙信封，裡面是舊同學沈文的信。他現在做會計，看見戲院公告後願意幫忙檢查帳冊，但希望先談妥合理預算再決定報酬。雨蘭欣賞這份坦白。她回信說，需要有人保留原始收據、讓委員會查看副本；若有錯誤數字，她也願意公開修正。傍晚兩人在碼頭談話，看著渡輪展開橫越海灣的短航程。沈文說，有生產力不一定等於賺錢，有時只是把錢確實花在哪裡記錄清楚。

The next morning, they met a retired school counselor who warned about the psychological effect of inviting neighbors to remember painful events. "You cannot turn every private memory into a public show," she said. Yulan agreed. They created three labels for the collection: public, permission required, and private. Qin added a fourth mark for films with uncertain owners. Nobody would decide alone which tape belonged in which box. The arrangement took longer than a simple cleanup, but it prevented an argument that might have destroyed the project before the first screening. Yulan then opened a new page in her notebook and asked the practical question everyone had avoided: how much would it cost to make the building safe?

隔天早上，他們見了一位退休的學校輔導老師。她提醒大家，邀請鄰居回想痛苦往事可能造成心理影響。「不能把每段私人記憶都變成公開表演，」她說。雨蘭同意，於是為收藏設立三種標籤：可公開、須徵求同意、私人資料。秦姨又加上第四種：所有權不明。沒有人可以獨自決定一卷影片該進哪一箱。這比單純打掃更費時，卻避免了可能在首次放映前就摧毀計畫的爭執。雨蘭接著在筆記本翻開新頁，問出大家一直迴避的實際問題：讓建築安全究竟要花多少錢？

In the old ticket office, the women discovered a box of programs from the cinema's opening week. Each ticket had the name of a family member on the back, a custom meant to help the owner thank visitors personally. Yulan considered bringing the box to the hearing, then stopped. The names proved affection, not structural safety or financial strength. She divided her evidence into three folders: memories, condition reports, and costs. She had returned expecting the past to answer her questions, but the past was making the questions more precise. Outside, the afternoon bell rang from the school. Inside, Qin began arranging dry shelves for the film boxes. Their first useful achievement was not a repaired screen. It was a clear place for everything they did not yet understand.

在舊售票間，兩人發現一箱戲院開幕週的節目單。每張票背面都有某個家庭成員的姓名，這是當年老闆用來親自感謝觀眾的習慣。雨蘭原本想把箱子帶到公聽會，卻停了手。這些名字能證明人們的感情，卻不能證明建築安全或財務健全。她把證據分成三個資料夾：回憶、屋況報告、成本。她以為過去會回答問題，沒想到過去反而讓問題變得更精確。學校的午後鐘聲從外頭傳來，秦姨則開始騰出乾燥的架子。她們第一項真正有用的成果，不是修好的銀幕，而是替所有尚未明白的事情找到適當的位置。
