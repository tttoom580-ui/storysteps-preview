不能跨越的線

Lines We Cannot Cross

1998，海禾戲院不打烊 · 第 4 / 25 章 · 3000–4000 · 英中對照

用互動版查詞、朗讀與收藏

The mechanic who agreed to inspect the roof brought his daughter to accompany him, because she wanted to learn technical drawing. He had once repaired fishing boats and was the town's most trusted specialist in old metal joints. Yulan made a diagram of the theater's narrow passages while he checked the beams. She hoped to turn the damaged building into an attraction for visitors, but he warned her that attractive drawings were not engineering reports. A retired diplomat who lived nearby offered to read the draft proposal and point out language that sounded like a promise. "A public document must tell people what you know," he said, "and what you still need to find out."

答應檢查屋頂的機械師帶著女兒同行，因為她想學技術製圖。這位師傅以前修漁船，是鎮上最熟悉老式金屬接頭的人。雨蘭畫出戲院狹窄通道的圖解，他則檢查梁柱。雨蘭希望讓受損的老建築變成遊客景點，但師傅提醒她，漂亮圖面不等於工程報告。一位住在附近的退休外交官願意閱讀提案草稿，並指出哪些措辭太像保證。「公開文件必須告訴大家妳知道什麼，」他說，「也要說出還有哪些事沒查清楚。」

Ventilation was essential. The storage room smelled damp, and one experimental cleaning spray left a sharp odor in the air. Qin told the workers to flee the room until the windows had been opened. "A germ on a dusty seat is not the only danger," she said. "A strong chemical can hurt people too." When neighbors arrived, Yulan welcomed them with cheerful greetings, then asked them not to enter the marked section. One man complained that the work was moving slowly. She explained the safety plan, including the rule that only trained workers could touch wiring or climb damaged ladders. Her answer did not satisfy everyone, but the visible boundary remained in place.

通風非常重要。儲藏室潮濕，一種試用中的清潔噴劑又讓空氣充滿刺鼻味道。秦姨要求工人先逃離房間，等窗戶打開後再回來。「灰椅子上的病菌不是唯一危險，」她說，「強烈的化學品也會傷人。」鄰居到來時，雨蘭熱情問候，隨後請他們不要走進標記區域。有位先生抱怨工程太慢。雨蘭說明安全計畫，包括只有受過訓練的人才能碰電線或爬上受損的梯子。不是人人都滿意，但明確的警戒線始終保留。

At the top of the stairs, the crew hesitated. A magnificent sunset stretched across the horizon, but the light could not hide a loose support near the exit door. The mechanic opened his manual and showed Yulan how the old bolt should fit. "Do not loosen that nut until the beam is supported," he said. Yulan wrote the instruction down exactly. Her hesitation was not a failure of courage; she had nearly touched the wrong part. When the crew finished measuring, the mechanic refused to give a safety certificate. The roof needed work that his small team could not complete alone. Yulan thanked him for an answer she had hoped not to hear.

到了樓梯頂端，工班猶豫了。壯麗的夕陽橫跨地平線，卻遮不住逃生門旁鬆動的支撐件。機械師打開手冊，讓雨蘭看舊螺栓原本該如何接合。「樑柱還沒撐穩前，不准鬆開那顆螺帽，」他說。雨蘭逐字記下指示。她的遲疑不是缺乏勇氣，她剛才差一點就碰錯了地方。測量結束後，機械師拒絕開安全證明。屋頂需要比他的小工班更大的修繕工程。雨蘭感謝他說出她最不想聽的答案。

An outstanding local teacher proposed using the courtyard for recreation while the building stayed closed. Her science club could show children photographs taken by a weather satellite, then discuss how storms formed over the sea. Qin liked the idea because it required neither the stage nor the dangerous upper floor. Yulan wrote a schedule and added one condition: every gathering must strengthen the repair fund and pay for cleaning rather than spend money they did not have. She asked the mechanic's daughter to draw a clear route around the closed areas. The girl did it neatly and added a hand-painted sign: "We are not open yet. We are learning how to open safely."

一位傑出的在地老師建議，建築仍關閉時可以在庭院辦休閒教育活動。她的科學社能展示氣象衛星拍下的圖片，讓孩子討論海上風暴如何形成。秦姨喜歡這個點子，因為不必使用舞台或危險的樓上空間。雨蘭排出時間表，並加上一項條件：每場聚會都必須補助修繕基金並支付清潔費，不可花掉根本沒有的錢。她請機械師的女兒畫出避開封閉區域的清楚動線。女孩整齊畫好，還加了一塊手繪牌子：「我們還沒開門；我們正在學習如何安全開門。」

The inspection had turned a romantic plan into a collection of difficult tasks. That evening Yulan laid three pages on the kitchen table: the roof repair estimate, the safe courtyard plan, and the public meeting notice. Her mother studied them without speaking. At last Mei said that helping did not mean becoming responsible for every cost. She could provide noodles at the first gathering, but she wanted payment for ingredients and an honest count of portions. Yulan accepted. She had expected homecoming to make everything familiar; instead, it was teaching her to ask permission, name limits, and pay attention to labor. Outside the window, someone had lit the theater sign for a moment. It flickered twice and went dark, like a question she was not ready to answer.

這場檢查讓浪漫的計畫變成一串艱難的工作。當晚，雨蘭把三張紙擺在廚房桌上：屋頂修繕估價、安全的庭院活動計畫，以及公開會議通知。母親默默看了一會兒，終於說，幫忙不等於要負擔所有成本。她能在第一次聚會提供麵食，但希望食材有報酬、份數也要確實計算。雨蘭接受了。她本以為返鄉會讓一切熟悉，沒想到學到的是徵求同意、說明限制與尊重勞動。窗外，有人讓戲院招牌亮了一瞬。燈閃了兩次又暗下去，像一個她暫時答不出的問題。

The mechanic left a second note explaining what he had not examined. He could describe the joints and bolts, but he was not qualified to approve the building's overall safety. Yulan appreciated the honesty. She pinned the note beside the inspection diagram instead of hiding it. The difference between a useful opinion and an official certificate had become a central lesson. If the theater reopened, she wanted its walls to be supported by people who knew exactly where their knowledge ended.

機械師留下第二張便條，說明還有哪些部分不是他檢查的範圍。他能解釋接頭和螺栓，卻沒有資格核准整棟建築的安全。雨蘭欣賞這份誠實，於是把便條貼在檢查圖旁，而不是藏起來。有用意見與正式證明的差別，已成為重要的一課。如果戲院重新開門，她希望支撐它的是那些明白自己專業界線的人。
