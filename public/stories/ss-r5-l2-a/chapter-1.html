雲層下的一封信

A Letter Below the Clouds

不會飛的信使，替群山點燈 · 第 1 / 25 章 · 1000–2000 · 英中對照

用互動版查詞、朗讀與收藏

Lan stood on the balcony of the small post house on a windy morning. Below her, the children played on a seesaw beside an empty field. A boy waved a letter at her. His sister lived at a school beyond the northern hills, but the last messenger had refused to carry it. Winter had made the mountain road dangerous, and a flying messenger asked for more money than the family could pay. Lan was twenty, and she had never learned to fly. She held the letter carefully. It was not heavy, yet the sadness inside it felt larger than the whole valley. She promised the boy that she would find a safe approach to the distant school.

一個多風的早晨，嵐站在小郵屋的陽臺上。下方的孩子在空田旁的翹翹板上玩耍。一個男孩朝她揮著信。男孩的姊姊住在北方山丘後的學校，但上一位信使拒絕送信。冬天讓山路變得危險，而飛行信使索取的費用超過這家人的能力。嵐二十歲，始終沒有學會飛行。她小心拿著信。信不重，裡面的思念卻彷彿比整座山谷還大。她答應男孩，要找一條安全的方法，把信送到遠方學校。

Behind the post house was an old stone base. It once held a signal mirror, a tool that could send a flash of sunlight to another hill. Lan's grandfather had been its operator. Her mother called the mirror a dead tradition, but Lan had a different belief: useful work did not become useless merely because it was old. She could not deny that the mirror had failed in the past. The glass had broken, and the houses beyond the ridge could not see it. Yet a small metal plate was still fixed to the stone. Its bright surface sent a moving mark of light across the wall whenever Lan turned it.

郵屋後面有一座舊石座，從前放著訊號鏡，可以把陽光閃到另一座山頭。嵐的祖父曾是它的操作員。母親說那面鏡子屬於死去的傳統，但嵐有不同的信念：一項有用的工作，不會只因年代久遠就失去價值。她不能否認鏡子過去失敗過。玻璃曾經碎裂，山脊後的房屋也看不見它。然而，石座上仍固定著一片小金屬板。只要嵐轉動它，明亮的表面就會在牆上送出移動的光點。

Bo, her male friend from the repair shop, came to discuss her idea. He said a mirror could send a warning much faster than a person could walk. Lan asked him to measure the speed of the light mark across the wall. “That is not the real challenge,” Bo replied. “A person on the next hill must know what the flash means.” They agreed to develop a simple code. For instance, three short flashes would mean HELP, while two long flashes would mean RECEIVED. Every message would also need a sender and a date. Without those details, anyone could cause trouble by sending a false warning.

修理店的男性朋友博來和她討論想法。他說鏡子送出警訊，比人走路快得多。嵐請他量量光點掃過牆面的速度。「真正的挑戰不在這裡，」博回答。「下一座山上的人必須知道閃光代表什麼。」他們決定制定一套簡單的訊號。譬如，三次短閃代表「求救」，兩次長閃代表「收到」。每則訊息還要附上發送者和日期。少了這些細節，任何人都能發出假警報，造成混亂。

They visited the town office to ask for a map. A clerk showed them a large picture of the nation and a narrow underground path marked as a subway for old mining carts. The clerk said that route was secondary and could not be opened during winter. Lan did not ask to open it. She wanted the positions of the hills above it. A woman at the desk laughed and said a person without flying skill should choose a better job. Bo wanted to answer sharply, but Lan put down her pencil and said that a messenger was judged by whether a message arrived, not by how high the messenger could rise.

他們到鎮公所索取地圖。職員拿出一幅國家大圖，上面還畫了一條供舊礦車使用的地下路線，標示為地下鐵。職員說那是次要路線，冬季不能開放。嵐並沒有要求開路；她要的是路線上方山丘的位置。桌旁一名女子笑說，不會飛的人最好換個工作。博想反駁，嵐卻放下鉛筆，說信使的本事應該看訊息有沒有送到，而不是飛得有多高。

At sunset, Lan carried the boy's letter up the short path behind the post house. The air was cold, and the stones were wet. Bo placed the metal plate on a wooden stand while she waited by the opposite window. A flash crossed the yard. Then another. It was not a real mountain message, but it proved the light could reach a chosen place. They wrote down the distance and the time. The child watched from the gate with his hands around a cup of warm water. Lan told him they had not yet reached his sister. “But you have started,” he said, as if that were already a gift.

日落時，嵐帶著男孩的信走上郵屋後的小路。空氣寒冷，石頭濕滑。博把金屬板放在木架上，嵐則站到對面的窗邊等候。一道閃光掠過院子，接著又來一道。這還不是真正的山間訊息，卻證明光可以抵達指定的地方。他們記下距離和時間。男孩站在大門旁，雙手捧著一杯溫水。嵐告訴他，訊息尚未送到姊姊那裡。「可是妳們已經開始了，」男孩說，彷彿這就已是一份禮物。

That night, Lan made a promise to herself. She would not replace a real letter with a few flashes and pretend the job was done. A light signal could tell a school that someone was coming, or tell a doctor that help was needed. It could not carry every word a family wanted to say. The boy's letter would still need a human courier when the road became safe. First, she had to build a line of stations where people could speak across the hills without danger. On the first page of her notebook, she wrote a question: who would keep the lights burning when the first excited volunteers went home?

當晚，嵐對自己許下一個承諾。她不會用幾道閃光代替真正的信件，然後假裝工作已經完成。光訊號能通知學校有人正在前往，也能告訴醫生有人需要幫忙，但無法傳完一家人想說的每句話。等山路安全了，男孩的信還是要由真人送達。首先，她必須建立一串安全的山間訊號站。在筆記本首頁，她寫下一個問題：當最初熱情的志工回家後，誰會繼續照看那些燈？
