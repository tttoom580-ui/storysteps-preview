The Compass That Returned Alone

On the eleventh morning after the survey team vanished, Ren Hai stood beneath the cracked bell of a coastal chapel. A cluster of families had assembled for a public forum, yet the officials offered certainty where they had no evidence. Their report declared that seven people had abandoned the mountain-sea border before the storm. Ren, a thirty-year-old cartographer, knew the claim was false: his younger sister Ren Lin had sent a measured tide reading only six hours earlier. Beside the altar, a generator coughed into silence. The reeds outside were beginning to wither, although this habitat had survived centuries of salt winds. A visiting metropolitan supervisor called the loss an unfortunate transaction between progress and nature. Ren thought of his sister laughing at maps whose straight lines pretended the coast would stay still. He was not ready to let someone reduce her life to a footnote.

測量隊失蹤後的第十一個早晨，任海站在海邊小禮拜堂龜裂的鐘下。一群家屬聚集參加公開論壇，官員卻在毫無證據時給出確定的說法。報告聲稱七人在暴風雨前已離開山海邊境。三十歲的繪圖師任海知道那是謊言：妹妹任霖在官方所稱撤離時間後六小時，還傳回一筆實測潮位。祭壇旁的發電機咳了幾聲後熄火。外頭的蘆葦開始枯萎，儘管這片棲地已挺過幾百年的鹽風。來自都城的督察把損失稱為進步與自然之間令人遺憾的交易。任海想起妹妹曾笑那些筆直的地圖線條，彷彿海岸永遠不會變動。他不願別人把她的一生縮成一條註腳。

Gu Yuan entered with a brass compass wrapped in cloth. At thirty-one, the former spirit-engineer walked with a slight limp, the result of rescuing trapped workers when an unstable talisman collapsed. He now repaired ordinary instruments because that accident had ended his ability to channel spiritual force. His old colleagues were envious of his technical insight but relieved that he could no longer compete for prestigious appointments. He had received the compass from a fisherman who found it lodged in a net. Its dial carried an immense scratch, and beneath the glass somebody had written GATE SEVEN. Gu did not disclose the fisherman's name; the man feared losing his boat. Instead he placed the object on the table and pointed to a record engraved inside its lid. The gate had apparently opened two days after the supposed evacuation. Ren's first impulse was to seize it and run to the northern cliffs.

顧遠帶著一枚用布包住的黃銅羅盤走進來。他三十一歲，是前靈械師，走路微跛；那是在一枚不穩定符器崩毀時救出受困工人留下的傷。事故也使他無法再導引靈力，因此如今只修理普通儀器。舊同事嫉妒他的技術洞察，卻也慶幸他不能再爭取顯赫職位。羅盤是一名漁夫從漁網裡找到後交給他的。刻度盤上有一道巨大的刮痕，玻璃下有人寫著「第七閘」。顧遠沒有揭露漁夫的姓名，因為對方怕失去漁船。他只是把物件放到桌上，指向蓋內刻下的紀錄：那座閘門似乎在所謂撤離的兩天後仍被開啟。任海第一個衝動，就是抓起羅盤奔向北方海崖。

Gu caught Ren's sleeve without pulling him back. “I will go if you choose to,” he said, “but a timestamp is not proof that your sister stood beside the gate.” Ren hated the delay until he noticed a tiny provision printed on the report's final page: independent verification was optional, not prohibited. The council had offered compensation to the families on condition that they waive further searches. For many households, the money meant food, and Ren would not judge them for taking it. Their shared heritage taught them that a promise to the missing should never become another burden on the living. Ren asked the forum to preserve the compass while he checked its origin. A child stumbled over a bench, and Gu helped her upright with his uninjured hand. The simple movement made Ren recognize a man who understood when helping mattered more than winning an argument.

顧遠輕抓任海的袖子，沒有硬把他拉回來。「如果你決定去，我會同行，」他說，「但一個時間戳記不能證明你妹妹當時就在閘門旁。」任海不喜歡拖延，直到他注意到報告最後一頁印著一條細小條款：獨立驗證屬於可選事項，並非禁止。議會願意支付家屬補償金，條件是放棄後續搜尋。對許多家庭而言，這筆錢代表食物，任海不會責怪接受的人。他們共同傳承的觀念是，對失蹤者的承諾不能反過來壓垮活著的人。任海請論壇暫時保管羅盤，讓他先查證來源。一名孩子被長椅絆倒，顧遠用沒受傷的手扶她站穩。這個簡單動作讓任海看出，眼前的人懂得何時幫助別人比贏得爭論更重要。

At dusk the two men examined the back of the dial under a lantern. There were seven small marks, one for each observation station, but the seventh was cut deeper and faced inland. Gu explained three limits of their equipment: tide-stones stored a single pressure echo, they could not identify a person, and opening one without a witness destroyed its value as evidence. Ren repeated the rules, then drew a route that avoided the flooded eastern stair. He had been looking for an answer he could hold, not a heroic companion, yet the quiet engineer made space for his anger without asking him to surrender it. They signed a record stating that neither would conceal findings that affected the safety of nearby villages. Beyond the chapel, the sea retreated from a line of black stones. For the first time in eleven days, Ren had a destination more precise than the word missing: the inspection bridge above Gate Seven.

黃昏時，兩人在燈下檢查刻度盤背面。那裡有七道小記號，分別代表七個觀測站；第七道較深，而且指向內陸。顧遠解釋設備的三項限制：潮石只能保存單次壓力回聲、無法辨識個人，若在沒有見證人的情況下拆開，便會失去證據價值。任海重述規則，接著畫出避開東側淹水階梯的路線。他原本只想抓住一個有形答案，而不是尋找英雄同伴，但沉靜的工程師容許他憤怒，沒有要求他放下。他們共同簽署紀錄，承諾不會隱匿影響附近村莊安全的發現。禮拜堂外，海水從一排黑石間退去。十一天以來第一次，任海終於有了比「失蹤」更明確的目的地：第七閘上方的檢修橋。
