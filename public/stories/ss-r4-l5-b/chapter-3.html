渡口的三個名字

Three Names at the Pier

山海邊境，失蹤者仍在等待 · 第 3 / 25 章 · 4000–5000 · 英中對照

The old pier at Black Creek was built on wooden piles above a narrow creek that smelled of wet iron. Its keeper, Sera, refused to surrender her books to the deputy from the provincial office. Years of abuse by customs inspectors had taught her what happened when a village lost the only copy of its own history. When Ren requested a consultation, she studied his hands rather than his face. They were marked by ink, not weapons. Gu suggested that they record her testimony in the presence of a neutral correspondent who reported for several fishing settlements. The deputy arrived carrying a polished trophy, an absurd prize from a coastal safety contest, and spoke with open contempt about fishermen who questioned official decisions. Sera answered with deliberate calm: the pier's records existed to keep travelers alive, not to decorate a politician's shelf. She offered the men a seat beside a leaking window.

黑溪舊渡口用木樁架在一道狹窄溪流上，水裡飄著濕鐵味。守渡人瑟拉拒絕把帳簿交給省府派來的副官。多年來海關人員的濫權讓她明白，一個村莊失去自己歷史的唯一副本會有什麼後果。任海請求諮詢時，她先看他的手，而不是臉；那雙手沾的是墨水，不是武器。顧遠建議請一位為多個漁村報導的中立記者在場，記下她的證詞。副官拿著一座從海岸安全競賽得來的光亮獎盃到場，對質疑官方決策的漁民表現出毫不掩飾的輕蔑。瑟拉刻意保持平靜，回答道：渡口紀錄是為了讓旅人活下去，不是為了裝飾政客的架子。她請兩人坐在漏水的窗邊。

The book contained three versions of the same journey. First, seven surveyors had crossed the pier together. In the next phase, a smaller group returned to collect equipment, but their permit carried a different seal. Finally, one entry claimed that a diplomatic guest had borrowed the vessel at night. Ren knew his sister's handwriting and could tell she had signed only the first line. Someone had made a swap after the team departed. The deputy called this a harmless clerical correction and told Sera not to notify anyone outside the province. Gu pointed out that a harmless correction should have a named author. The odds of three independent mistakes producing the same new route were small. His gut warned him that the forged permit mattered more than the name on its cover, yet he refused to call a suspicion a fact. With the correspondent watching, they photographed every line, including the errors.

帳簿裡有同一趟航程的三種版本。第一筆記載七名測量員一起過渡；第二階段有較小隊伍回來拿設備，但通行證印章不同；最後一筆則聲稱一名外交貴賓夜裡借了船。任海認得妹妹筆跡，知道她只在第一行簽過名。有人在隊伍離開後調換了證件。副官說這只是無傷大雅的文書修正，還告訴瑟拉不准通知省外人士。顧遠指出，無害的修正也應有具名負責人。三個互不相關的錯誤恰巧指向同一條新路線，機率實在太低。他的直覺警告自己，偽造的通行證比封面上的名字更重要；但他拒絕把懷疑當事實。在記者見證下，他們逐行拍攝，包括那些錯誤。

Behind the pier, a mangrove ecosystem sheltered fish and pale shore crabs. A scientist who studied species once thought extinct had tagged the roots to measure salt damage. Those marks showed a water level far below the official chart during the team's last crossing. She explained that any sustainable rescue route must respect the daily tide, not a single fortunate reading. Ren wanted to sneak into the deputy's office and take his files, but Sera stopped him. “If you steal proof, they will debate your theft instead of their disappearance,” she said. Gu quietly agreed. Sera named her niece as her successor and asked her to prepare a signed copy of the pier records. They left the original where villagers could inspect it. Ren realized that the truth might survive not because one person guarded it perfectly, but because several ordinary people held separate pieces of it.

渡口後方的紅樹林生態系庇護著魚和淡色岸蟹。一名研究曾被認為已經絕種物種的科學家，在樹根上做了標記，測量鹽害。那些刻痕顯示，測量隊最後一次過渡時的水位遠低於官方圖表。她解釋，永續的救援路線必須考慮每天的潮汐，而不是憑一次幸運讀數。任海想偷偷潛進副官辦公室拿檔案，瑟拉阻止了他。「你若偷證據，他們只會爭論你的偷竊，不會談人的失蹤，」她說。顧遠也安靜地同意。瑟拉指定姪女為接任者，請她製作一份由見證人簽章的渡口紀錄副本。原件則留在村裡供人查閱。任海明白，真相也許不是因為有誰完美守護，而是許多平凡人各自保存其中一部分，才能活下來。

On their way out, the deputy offered to make the investigation disappear if they accepted a paid post in the capital. Gu declined without raising his voice. He asked only that the deputy acknowledge the date of the amended permit. The official refused, but the reporter wrote down that refusal too. At sunset, Sera showed Ren a shallow notch in the pier where his sister had once measured an unusually low tide. “She asked whether the sea could be drained from underneath,” Sera remembered. Ren had laughed at that question in an old letter; now he wished he had listened. Gu did not offer comfort he could not guarantee. Instead he sat on the steps and waited while Ren drew the notch accurately into his map. Above them, gulls circled a distant line of cliffs, and the unmarked inland canal began to look less like a legend and more like the route their missing people had been forced to follow.

離開時，副官提出只要他們接受都城的有薪職位，便能讓這場調查消失。顧遠沒提高聲音就拒絕了，只要求副官承認通行證更改的日期。官員不肯，但記者也記下這次拒絕。日落時，瑟拉指給任海看渡口邊一道淺刻痕：妹妹曾在此量到異常低潮。「她問海水會不會從底下被抽走，」瑟拉回憶。任海以前在妹妹的信中讀到這問題，還笑過她，如今只希望當時有認真聽。顧遠沒有給出無法保證的安慰，只坐在階梯上等待，讓任海把刻痕精確畫進地圖。他們頭上，海鷗繞著遠方海崖盤旋，而那條沒被標記的內陸運河，越來越不像傳說，反而更像失蹤者被迫走過的路。
