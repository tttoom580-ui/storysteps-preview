A Trial of Light

燈熄之後，星星仍在 · 第 2 / 25 章 · 1000–2000 · 英中對照

用互動版查詞、朗讀與收藏

The next day, Mira and Kai asked the astronomy club to hold a short meeting. The club had lost much of its membership after the dome closed. Some students said there was no point in another trial; the school might cancel it before anyone saw the stars. Mira placed a drawing on the table. It showed a display of planets in the student hall, with a portable screen that did not depend on the old roof. “We can begin here,” she said. “The beauty of the idea is that children can see the sky even if the dome stays shut.” Kai brought a small speaker and a box of wires. He promised that the sound could be turned off.

隔天米拉與凱請天文社開了一場短會。圓頂關閉後，社團失去了許多會員。有學生說再試辦一次也沒意義，學校可能在大家看見星星前就取消活動。米拉把一張畫放在桌上，圖裡是學生大廳的行星展示，使用可攜式布幕，不依賴舊屋頂。「我們可以從這裡開始，」她說。「這點子美在即使圓頂沒開，孩子也能看星空。」凱帶來小喇叭和一盒電線，保證聲音可以隨時關掉。

Their first problem was an old chain that held the portable screen closed. The lock was bent, and nobody wanted to force it. A student from the chess club offered to help. He studied the shape like a puzzle and said the metal could slide free if two people held the frame steady. Kai checked that it would not fall. Mira followed the student’s guide, and the screen opened without damage. Everyone clapped, but Mira reminded them that a successful opening was not the same as a safe public show. They still needed a current inspection of the frame and clear paths for visitors. Their first small success gave them a reason to continue.

第一個問題是固定可攜式布幕的舊鐵鏈。鎖彎了，沒人敢硬扯。西洋棋社的學生前來幫忙，把金屬形狀當謎題研究，說只要兩人扶穩支架，鎖鏈便能滑出。凱確認布幕不會倒下。米拉照著他的指引操作，布幕毫髮無傷地打開。大家鼓掌，米拉卻提醒：成功打開不代表適合公開表演。他們仍需檢查支架，安排清楚的觀眾通道。這個小小的成功讓大家有理由繼續。

Mira sent a message to Ms. Song with photographs of the frame and its labels. The officer did not respond at once because she was handling another building. Mira nearly wrote a second, angry note, then stopped herself. She did not want to be selfish with another person's time. While they waited, the students tested the sound at low volume. The speaker made a soft hum and then went silent. Kai checked the wires and found a broken connection. “This is the worst part of using old equipment,” he said. “A small problem can hide behind a perfect surface.” He asked if she would help him hunt for a spare cable.

米拉把支架和標籤的照片傳給宋女士。管理員忙著處理別棟校舍，沒有立刻回覆。米拉差點再傳一封生氣的訊息，後來忍住了。她不想自私地占用別人的時間。等待期間，社員用小音量測試。喇叭發出低低的嗡聲後就沒了聲音。凱檢查線材，發現一處接點壞了。「這就是用舊設備最麻煩的地方，」他說。「小問題可能藏在漂亮外表下面。」他問她願不願意一起找備用線。

They found one in the theater store, beside a basket of paper pears from last year's school play. Mira picked up a green pear and said the planet should look like that. Kai disagreed, but he smiled. The argument helped them decide how the show should feel: true facts first, then room for questions and play. A drama student warned that the old club had suffered a loss when someone borrowed equipment without writing down a name. Kai suggested a simple sign-out sheet. Mira added a space for returning items and reporting damage without blame. The students accepted the rule because they had written it themselves, not because a teacher ordered them to obey.

他們在劇場倉庫找到備用線，旁邊還放著去年校園戲劇留下的紙梨子。米拉拿起一顆綠梨，笑說行星應該長這樣。凱不同意，卻也笑了。這個玩笑幫他們決定演出風格：先提供正確知識，再給觀眾提問和遊玩的空間。一名戲劇生提醒，舊社團曾因借用設備沒登記而蒙受損失。凱提議設置借用表，米拉加上歸還與無責通報損壞欄。社員接受這規則，是因為它由大家共同寫成，而不是老師命令他們服從。

During lunch, the club president called the idea “super exciting,” but he worried about its growth. Could the team teach visitors without making a long speech? Could people with different hearing needs enjoy the same room? Kai suggested a written guide beside each picture and a silent version of every scene. Mira made a note to ask the student access office for advice. She also admitted that she sometimes liked praise more than useful criticism. “That makes two of us,” Kai replied. Neither wanted to hide behind a clever self-image. A good team, they agreed, needed members who could speak honestly when a plan was weak.

午餐時，社長稱讚構想「超級令人期待」，卻擔心規模擴大後怎麼辦。團隊能不用長篇講話就教會觀眾嗎？聽力需求不同的人能享受同一個空間嗎？凱建議每張圖旁都有文字指引，每個場景都設無聲版本。米拉記下要向校內無障礙服務單位請教，也承認自己有時比起有用的批評，更喜歡被讚美。「我也是，」凱回答。兩人都不想躲在聰明能幹的自我形象背後。他們同意，好的團隊需要有人能坦白指出計畫的弱點。

At last Ms. Song replied. The hall could host one supervised test if the screen passed inspection, but the dome remained closed. Mira read the message aloud. Some students cheered, while others worried the school might again change its mind. Kai stood beside her and said, “This is a trial, not a promise of a full opening. Let us make the trial worth seeing.” Mira noticed that he did not pretend their problems had vanished. She liked that more than his easy jokes. When she walked home, she passed the old dome and imagined the stars returning one careful step at a time. The locked door no longer looked like the end of the story.

宋女士終於回覆：布幕通過檢查的話，學生大廳可以舉行一次有監督的試映，但圓頂仍不得開放。米拉把訊息念出來。有人歡呼，也有人擔心學校臨時變卦。凱站在她旁邊說：「這是試辦，不是全面開館的保證。我們先把試辦做好。」米拉注意到他沒有假裝問題全都消失了。她開始覺得這比他愛開玩笑更討人喜歡。回家路上，她經過舊圓頂，想像星星一步步謹慎地重回校園。那扇上鎖的門不再像故事的終點。
