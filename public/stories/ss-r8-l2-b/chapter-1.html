上鎖的星空

The Locked Dome

燈熄之後，星星仍在 · 第 1 / 25 章 · 1000–2000 · 英中對照

用互動版查詞、朗讀與收藏

When Mira was twenty-four, she found the old planetarium by following a painted star through the university garden. Its door stood beside a tiny café, and a leaf had grown through a crack in the steps. A gardener with a gray beard was cutting branches nearby. He said the dome had been locked for two years. “People tell such strange stories about this place,” he warned. “Some even say a crime happened inside.” Mira studied astronomy and liked facts more than rumors. She had come because her teacher mentioned a room where students once watched the night sky in daylight. She asked the gardener for a favor: could he show her the person with the key?

二十四歲的米拉沿著大學花園裡畫在地上的星星，找到了舊天文館。入口旁有一家小咖啡館，一片葉子從台階的裂縫長出來。附近有位灰鬍子的園丁正在修剪樹枝。他說圓頂已經上鎖兩年了。「大家對這地方有好多奇怪的傳言，」他提醒她。「有些人甚至說裡面發生過犯罪事件。」米拉念天文學，比起傳言更喜歡事實。她是因為老師提起一間能讓學生在白天看夜空的房間才來的。她請園丁幫忙，帶她去找保管鑰匙的人。

The key belonged to Ms. Song, the buildings officer. She came from another meeting with a plain folder under her arm. A yellow sign mentioned a chemical smell, but it gave no date or report number. Ms. Song would not let Mira enter alone. “My personality is careful, not unfriendly,” she said with a small smile. Then another voice came from behind them. Kai, a twenty-six-year-old theater student, carried a box of rubber gloves and an old sound cable. He had been asked to remove broken equipment. Mira had met him once at a concert; they had argued about whether a dark stage needed music. Neither looked pleased by the surprise.

鑰匙由校舍管理員宋女士保管。她剛開完另一場會，腋下夾著一個樸素的資料夾。黃色告示提到化學氣味，卻沒有日期或報告編號。宋女士不准米拉獨自進去。「我只是個性謹慎，不是不友善，」她微笑著說。這時另一個聲音從背後傳來。二十六歲的戲劇系學生凱拿著一盒橡膠手套和一條舊音訊線。他受託清走損壞的設備。米拉曾在音樂會上遇過他；兩人當時為黑暗舞臺是否需要配樂吵了一架。這次偶遇似乎沒讓誰高興。

Inside, the temperature was lower than in the garden. Dust covered the seats, and a large projector stood in the middle like a sleeping animal. Mira wanted to divide the work: she would study the machine, while Kai would check the sound system. Kai did not agree. “We should inspect the room together before touching anything,” he said. His feeling was that an unsafe building could make even a small mistake serious. Ms. Song nodded. She showed them a record that said the smell came from an old cleaning product, not from a dangerous leak. It was not proof that the room was safe now. They needed a new inspection before any public visit.

館內溫度比花園低。座椅滿是灰塵，一臺大型星象投影機立在中央，像沉睡的動物。米拉想分工：她研究機器，凱檢查音響。凱不同意。「碰任何東西之前，應該先一起查看整個房間，」他說。他感覺不安全的建築會讓一點小失誤變成大問題。宋女士點頭，拿出紀錄說明氣味來自舊清潔用品，並非危險洩漏。但這不代表現在的房間安全。要讓民眾進來，必須先做新的檢查。

While they waited for the inspection form, Mira took out her notebook. Her little cousin had drawn stars in its corners during a family meal, then dropped a watermelon seed between the pages. Kai laughed when the seed fell onto the floor. Mira laughed too, though she did not want him to know she found his laugh pleasant. He asked whether they should weigh the heavy projector before anyone tried to move it. She admitted that was sensible. They could relate the weight to the floor plan and decide where workers might stand. The plan was more useful than their old argument about music, which suddenly felt very small.

等待檢查表時，米拉拿出筆記本。小表妹之前在家庭聚餐時畫了滿頁星星，還把一顆西瓜籽夾在書頁間。籽掉到地上時凱笑了；米拉也笑了，雖然她不想讓他知道自己覺得他的笑聲很好聽。凱問移動沉重投影機之前是否應先秤重。她承認很有道理。他們可以把重量和地板圖對照，決定工人應站在哪裡。這個計畫比兩人從前對配樂的爭論有用得多，那場爭論忽然顯得很小。

At lunch, Mira returned to her shared apartment and found her roommate still wearing pajamas after a night of exam study. Her roommate asked if the old dome might become a café. “There is already one next door,” Mira replied. “Besides, I want the stars back.” The university planned to use the building for storage, because a public show cost money and required staff. A classmate from business school asked who would pay the tax on tickets if they sold them. Mira did not know, so she wrote down the question rather than invent an answer. She also wrote another: Who had decided the dome had no future? She hoped a question could open a door.

午餐時，米拉回到合租公寓，發現室友熬夜準備考試，仍穿著睡衣。室友問舊圓頂能不能改成咖啡館。「隔壁已經有一家了，」米拉回答。「而且我想把星空找回來。」校方打算把建築當倉庫，因為公開演出需要經費和人力。商學院的同學問如果售票，誰要負責票務稅務問題。米拉不知道，便記下問題，而非編造答案。她還寫下另一個問題：是誰決定圓頂沒有未來？她希望問題本身也能打開一扇門。

That evening she returned with a proposal for a free, small educational show. Kai was there, checking the outside doors without going past the safety tape. “I thought you disliked sound,” he said. Mira shook her head. “I dislike music that tells people what to feel before they see the stars.” Kai considered this, then offered to make a quiet sound track that visitors could choose to hear or leave off. He did not ask her to change her science plan, and she did not ask him to work without credit. They agreed to meet the next morning and speak to Ms. Song together. Neither knew whether the dome could reopen. They only knew they wanted to find out.

傍晚她帶著免費小型教育放映的提案回來。凱也在場，正在安全封鎖線外查看出入口。「我以為妳討厭聲音，」他說。米拉搖頭。「我討厭觀眾還沒看見星星，就被音樂決定該有什麼感覺。」凱想了一下，提出製作一段安靜的音景，讓觀眾自行選擇要聽或關掉。他沒要求米拉更改科學內容，她也沒要求他無署名地工作。兩人約好隔天一起找宋女士。他們還不知道圓頂能否重開，但至少願意試著找答案。
