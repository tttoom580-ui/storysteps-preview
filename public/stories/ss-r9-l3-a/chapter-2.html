缺了一條路的地圖

The Map with a Missing Path

The next morning, Shan followed the old mountain avenue from the staff house toward the research wing. A metal cradle once held fragile weather instruments near the door. Volunteers had used colored paper to decorate it for a public exhibition, but the decorations had become wet and heavy. The elevator to the observation deck remained locked, so Noah showed her a safer staircase. A child had left a drawing of a firework in the visitors' book. Shan noticed that the drawing used the same three strokes as the radio log. It might be a coincidence, yet it gave her a reason to examine the old displays before anyone cleared them away.

隔天早上，珊沿著員工宿舍通往研究翼的舊山路前進。門邊有個金屬托架，過去用來承放脆弱的氣象儀器。志工曾用色紙裝飾它，供公開展覽使用，如今裝飾卻被雨浸得又濕又重。通往觀測平台的電梯上了鎖，諾亞便帶她走較安全的樓梯。一本訪客留言簿裡，孩子畫了一朵煙火。珊發現畫中也有和通訊紀錄一樣的三道短線。這可能只是巧合，卻讓她決定在展品被清走前先仔細查看。

A painted sign said one gallon of water was enough for an emergency demonstration. Beside it, an outdated chart carried the name of the regional headquarters. The chart invited children to use their imagination to match cloud shapes with animals: a leopard, a bird, and a fish. Noah smiled at the magical pictures, but Shan noticed an odd blank space where a drainage line should have been. An organic stain had spread over that corner, and somebody had tied a green ribbon around the frame. Under the ribbon she found a date: the day Mei joined the station. The children had not invented the missing channel; the older chart had shown it plainly.

一塊畫過的告示說，緊急示範只需一加侖的水。旁邊的舊圖表印著區域總部名稱，讓孩子運用想像力，把雲形配成豹、鳥和魚。諾亞看著富有魔法感的圖畫笑了，珊卻注意到排水線應有的位置留著一塊古怪空白。那一角有天然污漬蔓延，還有人在框上綁了綠色絲帶。絲帶下方寫著日期：梅加入氣象站的那天。缺失的水道不是孩子想像出來的；舊圖明明畫得很清楚。

At the ridge, a steep section made walking difficult. A visitor had left a survey card reporting a threat of falling stones, although the tourist office's newer brochure called the route easy. Noah bent down to inspect a dark spot on the ground. He warned Shan not to spit into the soil sample or wipe away the marks with her glove. Their purpose was not to collect dramatic pictures but to preserve a measurable difference between the two maps. They photographed each stone, recorded their position, and returned to the safe trail when the wind rose. Shan marked the ridge as unverified rather than open or closed.

山脊有一段陡坡很難走。一位遊客曾在調查卡上記下落石威脅，但新的旅遊手冊卻稱那條路很容易。諾亞蹲下查看地面上的暗點，提醒珊不要朝土壤樣本吐口水，也別用手套擦掉痕跡。他們不是來收集刺激的照片，而是要保存兩張地圖之間可測量的差異。兩人拍下每顆石頭、記錄位置，風變大便退回安全步道。珊在圖上標註「尚未驗證」，沒有直接寫成開放或封閉。

Inside the visitor room, Shan found a printed teaching sheet about the station's communication history. It explained that the three strokes represented a call sign used before digital radios were installed. During public demonstrations, a guide would ring three small bells, then send the matching sound over a loudspeaker. A recording of that lesson might easily be mistaken for a real emergency call. Yet the voices Shan heard at night had not used the sign correctly. This made her less afraid of the sound and more concerned about the person who wanted her to follow it. She placed the page in a numbered evidence sleeve.

訪客室裡，珊找到一張印好的教學紙，介紹氣象站的通訊歷史。紙上說，三道短線代表數位無線電安裝前的呼號。公開示範時，導覽員會先敲三下小鐘，再用擴音器送出相同聲音。那份課程錄音很容易被誤聽成真正的緊急呼叫。然而珊夜裡聽到的聲音並沒有正確使用呼號。她因此不再那麼害怕聲音本身，反而更擔心有人故意誘她走向危險。她把教學紙收進編號證物袋。

Vale arrived before lunch and asked why she had examined the exhibition instead of replacing a sensor. Shan explained that old teaching materials could show what the newer map omitted. He answered that public illustrations were unreliable. She agreed they could be wrong, then asked for the dated engineering drawing behind them. Vale said the document was at headquarters, but he could not name a contact. His hesitation did not prove dishonesty. It did, however, mean the station's official account could not yet be checked. Shan sent a written request and copied it to the independent safety office so the question would not disappear into a private conversation.

午餐前維爾趕到，問她為何不換感測器，反而檢查展覽。珊解釋，舊教學資料可能顯示新版地圖省略了什麼。他說公共插圖不可靠。珊同意圖可能有錯，接著要求查看繪圖所依據、有日期的工程圖。維爾說文件在總部，卻講不出聯絡人。他的遲疑不能證明說謊，卻表示氣象站的官方說法目前仍無法核實。珊提出書面申請，副本送獨立安全辦公室，免得問題消失在私人談話裡。

Before sunset, Noah uncovered a second map under a shelf. It showed the south channel crossing beneath a narrow storage bridge. A red line had been drawn over the bridge after an earlier storm, while the printed route underneath remained visible. Shan could not tell whether the red mark indicated a closure or a proposed repair. She would need the inspection date, the person who made the mark, and proof that the order reached staff members. For the first time she could describe exactly what they did not know. That was not a failure of the investigation; it was a boundary that would keep them from walking blindly into the mountains.

日落前，諾亞在架子下找到第二張地圖。地圖顯示南側水道穿過一座狹窄的儲物橋。某次舊風暴後，有人在橋上畫了一道紅線，底下原有的路線仍看得見。珊還無法判斷紅線是封閉標記還是修繕建議。她需要檢查日期、畫線者身分，以及命令確實傳到工作人員手中的證據。這是她第一次能準確說出「我們不知道什麼」。那不是調查失敗，而是保護他們不盲目走進山裡的界線。
