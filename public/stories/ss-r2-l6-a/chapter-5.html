失蹤箱子的宴會

The Banquet of Missing Boxes

反派的辯護人，決定先替自己翻案 · 第 5 / 25 章 · 5000–6000 · 英中對照

The Royal Academy held its winter banquet under banners praising the chancellor's generosity. Seren arrived with Lysa because the academy's medical division had purchased the disputed supplies. Its dean repeated the public accusation that Seren had arranged a fraudulent shipment to enrich herself. Yet the laboratory report on the table described a different problem: samples from an offshore fishery contained a mineral that could damage drinking water. The report also recommended limiting fertilizer runoff near the coast. Seren saw why the boxes had vanished. They carried evidence of pollution, not weapons for a rebellion. She asked the dean to identify who had ordered the tests and who had been allowed to read the results.

皇家學院的冬季宴會掛滿旗幟，歌頌宰相的慷慨。瑟蓮與萊莎到場，因為學院醫學部曾購買那批有爭議的物資。院長重複公開指控，說瑟蓮安排虛假運輸以中飽私囊。然而桌上的實驗報告指出另一個問題：近海漁場的樣本含有可能污染飲用水的礦物質，報告還建議限制沿海肥料逕流。瑟蓮終於理解箱子為何失蹤：那裡裝的是污染證據，而不是叛亂武器。她要求院長說明，究竟誰下令檢測，又有誰被允許閱讀結果。

A young researcher named Nera stepped forward with her headphone set, a simple acoustic device used to monitor the academy's recording cylinders. Nera had been a prospective witness but feared losing her job. She explained that an unusual rise in patients' chemical intake had been detected before the archive fire. The academy had tried to refine its testing method, only to discover that the official samples were being replaced. She had suffered a wrist fracture while trying to protect a cabinet from a staged accident. Seren did not ask her to perform courage for an audience. She asked whether Nera would testify privately under the Rule of Three, with a written safeguard against retaliation.

年輕研究員妮拉帶著一組耳機走上前，那是學院用來監聽錄音圓筒的簡單聲學裝置。她本可能成為證人，卻害怕失去工作。她解釋，檔案庫大火之前，學院就發現患者攝入的某種化學物質異常增加。研究團隊試圖改良檢驗方法，卻發現官方樣本被人調換。她曾試著保護一座櫃子免遭假意外破壞，因此手腕骨折。瑟蓮沒有要求她當眾表演勇氣，只問她是否願意在有書面防報復措施的前提下，依三證法則私下作證。

The academy treasurer tried to shuffle the documents away. Rowan stopped him, but Seren refused to let the captain seize anything without an order. Even a righteous case could fail if its investigators ignored the law. The dean claimed that a maiden research assistant could not understand a complicated budget, a remark that made Nera's expression harden. She answered calmly that she understood the difference between a medical invoice and a banquet bill. Lysa then presented the payment record for a shipping harness and twelve reinforced crates. The payer was a company owned by the chancellor's brother. No criminal conviction followed that discovery, but the room's serene celebration ended at once.

學院財務官試圖把文件悄悄挪走。羅文阻止了他，但瑟蓮不准隊長在沒有命令的情況下扣押任何物品。即使追求正義的案件，也可能因調查者違法而失敗。院長嘲諷一名年輕未婚女研究助理不可能理解複雜預算，妮拉臉色頓時變冷。她冷靜回答，自己至少分得出醫療發票與宴會帳單。萊莎隨即提出付款紀錄：運貨用具與十二只加固木箱，付款者是宰相兄弟名下的公司。這個發現還不能直接定罪，但宴會祥和的氣氛立刻消失了。

Outside the hall a public sermon called for obedience, while groups of dockworkers demanded a referendum on the polluted harbor. Some wanted to rejoice because they believed Seren had already exposed the chancellor. Others began to resent her for bringing uncertainty into their lives. Seren spoke from the steps and asked them to distinguish suspicion from verdict. She told the workers that the fishery must be tested independently and that no household should lose its water supply while officials argued. A tenant from the harbor shouted that his children had been ill for weeks. His voice changed the discussion more than a dozen speeches could have done.

大廳外有人公開講道，呼籲民眾服從；另一邊，碼頭工人則要求就受污染港灣舉行公投。有人以為瑟蓮已揭露宰相罪行，準備歡欣慶祝，也有人開始怨恨她把不安帶進生活。瑟蓮站在台階上，請群眾區分懷疑與判決。她說漁場必須接受獨立檢驗，官員爭論期間，也不該讓任何家庭失去水源。一位港區租戶高喊，他的孩子已生病數週。那句話比十幾場演說更能改變討論的方向。

After the banquet, Seren visited the temporary water station with Nera. The testing team took samples from three streets, not simply from the homes of the most influential residents. A young assistant asked why they needed separate measurements when everyone seemed to drink from the same pipes. Nera explained that different routes and storage conditions could change the results. Seren was relieved to hear someone teach uncertainty without treating it as weakness. She asked that the report describe the sampling method in language ordinary citizens could understand. People could not make informed choices if experts hid both the evidence and its limitations.

宴會後，瑟蓮與妮拉拜訪臨時供水站。檢測團隊從三條街採樣，而不只檢查最有影響力的住戶。一名年輕助理問，既然大家看似喝同一條管線的水，為何還需要分開測量。妮拉解釋，不同輸送路線與保存條件會影響結果。瑟蓮很欣慰聽見有人教導不確定性，卻不把它當成弱點。她請團隊用一般市民能理解的文字說明採樣方法。若專家同時藏起證據與限制，人民就無法做出知情選擇。

That night Nera provided the name of the doctor who had conducted the first tests. He had vanished after submitting a report to the chancellor's office. The last time she saw him, he carried a small case with a cracked latch and said he would hide the original results where only a patient investigator could find them. Seren added the account to her notes, marking it as testimony awaiting confirmation. Her ten-day stay had now entered its fifth day. She had gained a witness, three partial records, and a motive for suppression, but the central questions remained open. Why frame Seren for a fire, and who had ordered the archive to burn?

那天夜裡，妮拉提供了最早進行檢測的醫師姓名。他把報告提交宰相辦公室後便失蹤。妮拉最後一次見到他時，他帶著一只搭扣裂開的小箱子，說會把原始結果藏在只有耐心的調查者才找得到的地方。瑟蓮把這段話寫進筆記，標記為尚待查證的證詞。十天的延期如今已進入第五日。她得到一名證人、三份不完整紀錄，以及有人想壓下真相的動機，但核心問題仍未解決：為什麼要把火災嫁禍給瑟蓮？又是誰下令焚燒檔案庫？
