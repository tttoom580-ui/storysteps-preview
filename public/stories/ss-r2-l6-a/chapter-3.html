孤兒院的布料帳冊

The Orphanage Ledger

反派的辯護人，決定先替自己翻案 · 第 3 / 25 章 · 5000–6000 · 英中對照

The sealed boxes had not passed the northern gate without a purpose. At dawn, Elen showed the party an advisory notice directing twelve crates to the Haven Orphanage beside the old canal. The notice identified them as recreational supplies for children, yet its weight matched a shipment of medical equipment. Rowan dismissed the discrepancy as a clerical error. Seren disagreed. In the original book, a conspiracy had begun with a forged charitable gift; the author had used the orphanage as scenery and never asked where the money came from. Now the children living there gave the unanswered question a human cost. Seren requested a visit before any witness could be warned.

那十二個封箱不可能毫無目的地穿過北門。清晨，艾蓮拿出一份諮詢通知，指示十二只貨箱送往舊運河旁的港灣孤兒院。通知聲稱那是孩子的休閒用品，重量卻和醫療設備的貨運紀錄一致。羅文起初以為是文書錯誤，瑟蓮卻不認同。原小說裡，一項陰謀始於偽造的慈善捐贈；作者只把孤兒院當作背景，從未追究款項來源。如今住在那裡的孩子，讓這個無人回答的問題有了真實代價。瑟蓮要求在證人被預先告知之前進行探訪。

The orphanage occupied a former textile mill. Its elderly director, Mother Pell, greeted them with sliced cucumber and bread while children argued over a homemade kite. A faded mural celebrated the kingdom's conquest of the northern valleys. Under the mural, an even older inscription used local slang to describe the people once called savage by royal soldiers. Seren noticed how easily a wall could symbolize different histories at once. A boy with a feeble cough pointed at a blue mark on a storage door. “The men said the boxes would make the plague go away,” he explained. “But they took the boxes again at night.”

孤兒院原是一座紡織工廠。年邁的院長佩爾嬤嬤端出切好的小黃瓜與麵包迎接眾人，孩子們在一旁爭論自製風箏該如何飛。褪色的壁畫歌頌王國征服北方山谷的歷史；壁畫下方更古老的碑文，卻用當地方言描述那些曾被王國士兵稱為「野蠻人」的族群。瑟蓮注意到，同一面牆能同時象徵不同的歷史。一位咳嗽無力的小男孩指著儲藏室門上的藍色記號說：「那些人說箱子能讓瘟疫消失，可是晚上又把箱子帶走了。」

Behind a shrub in the courtyard, Ada found a discarded strip of cloth stained with dark grease. The weave was too coarse for children's clothing, yet it matched the liners used to protect valuable paper during transport. Seren asked whether the orphanage had received any vaccine after the winter fever. Mother Pell opened her ledger: the medical donation had indeed arrived on the seventh, but the boxes were collected on the ninth. What remained was ordinary soap and a single broken thermometer. The director had been told not to complain, because complaints might lessen her grant. Her fear was reasonable, and Seren refused to treat silence as guilt.

艾達在庭院一株灌木後找到一截沾著黑色油脂的廢布。它的織法太粗，不適合做童裝，卻與運送珍貴文件時使用的內襯布相同。瑟蓮詢問孤兒院在冬季熱病後是否收到疫苗。佩爾嬤嬤攤開帳冊：醫療捐贈確實在七日抵達，但九日貨箱又被人收走。留下來的只有普通肥皂和一支破掉的溫度計。院長被警告不得抱怨，否則補助可能減少。她害怕得有道理，瑟蓮也拒絕將沉默視為有罪。

A sudden firecracker exploded beyond the gate, and two horses reared. Rowan rushed out, expecting an attack. Instead he found a group of hired youths trying to frighten the visitors away. They smelled of oil and smoke; one still had black grease on his fingers. The incident made the attendants fume, but Seren asked Rowan not to use force. She offered the youths food and a chance to describe who had paid them. Most refused. One girl admitted that a messenger had promised a silver coin if they could make the inspection end before noon. No one knew the messenger's name. That was frustrating evidence, but it was evidence nonetheless.

一聲鞭炮突然在門外炸響，兩匹馬驚得抬起前蹄。羅文衝出去，以為遭到襲擊，卻只找到幾名受雇的年輕人，企圖把訪客嚇走。他們身上帶著油與煙的味道，其中一人的手指還沾著黑油。這讓隨行人員氣得直冒火，但瑟蓮請羅文不要動武。她願意供應食物，換取他們說出誰付了錢。大多數人不肯回答，只有一名少女承認：有人答應給她一枚銀幣，只要她讓調查在中午前結束。沒人知道那個傳話者的姓名。證據令人挫折，卻仍然是證據。

On the journey back, Seren asked Ada how the cloth could be identified without destroying it. Ada suggested a comparison under daylight, followed by a careful record of the stitching pattern. She knew that a courtroom might dismiss her as merely a seamstress. Seren answered that the value of expertise did not depend on an official title. Lysa prepared a separate note describing Ada's method, its limitations, and the fabric samples available for comparison. The evidence would become stronger if another specialist reached the same result independently. It would become weaker if they rushed to make it say more than it could.

回程途中，瑟蓮問艾達如何在不破壞布料的情況下辨識來源。艾達建議先在日光下比較，再仔細記錄縫線模式。她知道法庭可能只把自己當成裁縫。瑟蓮回答，專業的價值不由正式頭銜決定。萊莎另外記下艾達的方法、限制，以及可用來比較的布料樣本。若另一位專家獨立得到相同結果，證據會更強；若她們急著讓它證明超出能力的結論，證據反而會變弱。

In the back office Mother Pell showed Seren a shipping ticket hidden beneath the children's drawings. It bore the crest of a passenger liner that had docked on the ninth, though the canal did not lead to the sea. The ticket could only have come from a separate transfer network. Lysa compared it with the registry and discovered an identical box number. Their findings did not yet surpass suspicion, but they contradicted the official claim that no boxes had left the archive. Seren left a signed copy with the director and took another for court. She also arranged medical help for the boy with the cough. Winning a trial, she was beginning to see, would mean little if the children were still left without protection.

在後方辦公室裡，佩爾嬤嬤拿出一張藏在孩子畫作下面的運貨單。票據上印著一艘九日靠港的客輪徽章，然而這條運河根本不通海，因此它只可能來自另一條轉運網路。萊莎把票據與登記簿相比，發現兩者貨箱號碼完全相同。這些發現還不足以超越懷疑、形成定論，卻足以推翻「沒有箱子離開檔案庫」的官方說法。瑟蓮留給院長一份簽名副本，另一份帶回法庭。她也替那名咳嗽的男孩安排醫療協助。她逐漸明白，如果孩子依然得不到保護，光是打贏官司並沒有太大意義。
