The first public meeting took place in a room above a market. Cheng asked people to describe an actual journey, not simply vote for or against the train. The questions frustrated both sides. One council aide wanted a dramatic petition; a shopkeeper wanted a guarantee that no taxes would rise. The market cashier described losing thirty minutes whenever the last bus departed before her shift ended. Mei-lin's medical checkup was scheduled at dawn, making the night service unexpectedly relevant. Cheng asked whether an accessible curb existed at the proposed replacement bus stop. The market alarm could deafen a nearby worker, and its noise should not be mistaken for a train warning.

第一次公開會議在市場樓上的房間舉行。方澄請大家描述真實的搭車過程，而不是只投贊成或反對票。這種問法讓兩邊都不耐煩：議員助理想要戲劇性的連署，店主則想得到稅金絕不增加的保證。市場收銀員說，末班公車若在她下班前開走，她每次就得多花三十分鐘。美玲的健康檢查排在清晨，因此夜車意外變得重要。方澄詢問替代公車站是否設有無障礙路緣。市場警報聲大得可能震聾附近工人，不能與列車警示聲混淆。

Gu Yu brought attendance sheets from his evening classes. He marked which students would lose an affordable way home and which could safely switch to a bus. Cheng discovered that her first estimate had counted journeys twice and omitted return trips taken with institutional passes. She corrected it in public. An aide called the flawed estimate a disgrace, while Cheng insisted on describing the actual error. The nurse asked how medical waste disposal would work if the clinic lost its evening courier. Cheng recorded every dissent instead of smoothing disagreement into false unanimity. One former resident planned to emigrate next year but still wanted his elderly father to have transport. The habit was slower than guessing, and far less dramatic than a speech. Yet it allowed an ordinary person to correct a professional without first asking permission to be heard.

顧嶼帶來夜校點名表，標出哪些學生會失去負擔得起的返家交通，哪些人可以安全改搭公車。方澄發現第一次估算重複計入某些旅程，卻漏掉機構通行證的返程。她當場更正。一名助理稱錯誤估算是恥辱，方澄則堅持具體說明錯在哪裡。護理師詢問，診所若失去晚間快遞，醫療廢棄物要如何處置。方澄記錄每一份不同意見，而不把分歧粉飾成虛假的一致。一名前居民明年打算移民，仍希望年邁的父親能有交通工具。這種做法比猜測慢，也遠不如演講戲劇化，卻讓一般人不必先取得發言許可，就能糾正專業人士的錯誤。

At home, Mei-lin had invited a community nurse to discuss care options. Cheng expected to be asked for a sacrifice. Instead, her mother asked her to compare fees, travel times, and the nurse's qualifications. The conversation was awkward, but for the first time both women could say no without calling it abandonment. The volunteers hoped to enrich the survey with household journeys absent from official ticket records. A lengthy speech about nostalgia produced less information than one nurse's detailed timetable. A lifelong fisherman explained how the final train connected him to the morning market. The chair addressed Mei-lin as Madam Fang before asking whether she wished to speak. She wrote down what she knew, what she merely suspected, and what a different witness would have to verify. The three columns prevented a persuasive story from turning into an invented fact.

回到家，美玲已請社區護理師來討論照護選項。方澄以為母親會要求她犧牲，沒想到母親只是請她比較費用、交通時間和護理資格。談話有些彆扭，卻是兩人第一次能說不，而不把拒絕叫作拋棄。志工希望用官方票務紀錄缺少的家庭旅程，充實調查內容。一篇冗長的懷舊演說，比不上一名護理師提供的詳細時刻表有用。一位終生以捕魚為業的人說明末班車如何讓他趕上早市。主席稱美玲為方女士，接著詢問她是否願意發言。她把自己確知的事、僅僅懷疑的事，以及仍須由其他證人查證的事分成三欄。這三欄防止一個看似有說服力的故事被當成憑空成立的事實。

The meeting yielded one unexpected ally: a market accountant who was willing to audit the fare data but refused to join any campaign. He asked for written terms and independent access. Cheng agreed. She realized that trust sometimes began not with warmth, but with precise limits. A trace of melancholy entered Mei-lin's voice when she described losing old friendships after surgery. The accountant asked what changes were legally permissible under the ninety-day review order. Gu Yu refused to preach sacrifice to students whose families already struggled. A ticket punch was reminiscent of Cheng's childhood journeys, but she kept nostalgia out of the figures. When the room grew impatient, she returned to the practical question: who could make a choice after receiving the information, and who would carry the cost if the choice was wrong?

會議帶來一位意外盟友：市場的會計師願意查核車票資料，卻拒絕加入任何陣營。他要求書面條款與獨立存取權，方澄答應了。她明白信任有時不是始於熱情，而是始於清楚的界線。美玲說起術後逐漸失去舊友的聯繫時，聲音帶著一絲憂傷。會計師詢問，在九十天檢討命令下，法律允許哪些改動。顧嶼不願向已經辛苦的學生宣講犧牲的大道理。舊式打票鉗讓方澄想起童年旅途，但她沒有把懷舊算進數字。當現場開始不耐煩，她便回到實際問題：誰在充分知情後可以作決定？如果決定錯了，又是誰要承擔代價？

After everyone left, Gu Yu remained to stack chairs. He offered to accompany Cheng to the next station, then added that she was free to refuse. She accepted the ride but not the assumption that they would become a couple. He smiled as though that distinction required no explanation. The rivalry between train supporters and bus operators had turned several meetings into shouting matches. Every corrected estimate would remain open to scrutiny by people outside the agency. The suspense over the closure vote did not justify hiding incomplete evidence. The old railway trademark appeared on tickets sold long before any electronic gate existed.

散會後，顧嶼留下來疊椅子。他提出陪方澄去下一站，隨即補充她完全可以拒絕。她接受同行，卻沒有因此答應交往。他笑了，彷彿兩者的差別根本不必解釋。支持鐵路的人與公車業者之間的競爭，讓幾次會議變成吵架。每份修正後的估算都必須接受機關外部人士檢驗。停駛表決的懸念，不能成為隱瞞未完成證據的藉口。舊鐵路商標印在電子閘門出現前就販售的車票上。
