河床下的市場

The Market Beneath the River

種子帳冊 · 第 3 / 25 章 · 2000–3000 · 英中對照

Mara did not pull the lock. Instead she whispered that the warning had come from an old disagreement, and Iris should hear her background before deciding whom to trust. Years ago she and Iris's mother had worked together to build a democratic water system. Mara had argued that the city needed firm rules before sharing its supplies; Iris's mother believed access should be a civil right. Their friendship began to deepen when the drought grew worse, then broke during a council meeting. 'I voted for the wrong plan,' Mara confessed. Iris read the letter again. Her mother had written it before disappearing, when fear was everywhere. Theo refused to make an immediate judgment. 'We need proof,' he said. The train entered a tunnel lined with bamboo poles, each carrying a dim emergency light. Beyond them was an underground market that looked like a second city.

瑪拉沒有拉下門鎖。她低聲說，那封警告來自多年前的爭執；艾莉絲應該先了解她的背景，再決定信不信她。多年以前，她和艾莉絲的母親合作設計民主的水資源制度。瑪拉認為供水前必須建立明確規則；艾莉絲的母親則相信用水應是公民權利。乾旱惡化時，她們的情誼一度加深，最後卻在市議會會議中破裂。「我投錯了票。」瑪拉承認。艾莉絲又讀了一遍信。母親寫信時尚未失蹤，當時人人惶恐。西奧不願立刻下結論。「我們需要證據。」他說。火車駛進由竹竿支撐的隧道，每根竿子上都有一盞微弱的緊急燈。外頭的地下市場像另一座城市。

The market occupied the dry bed of an ancient river. Traders offered rice, batteries, repaired shoes, and medicine under cloth roofs. One dealer carried a bundle of electronic parts and offered to exchange them for drinking water. A child with a mechanical pigeon on his shoulder invited the travelers to dine in a small shop. He said he could help them find a chip that would open the seed bank. Theo bought him a doughnut with their last food token. While they waited, Iris overheard people chat about the latest council poll. The richest districts had been promised new fountains while families downtown were being sent farther into the dust. Poverty was not merely an unfortunate condition here; it was the result of decisions made far away. Iris looked at the long lines for water and imagined her mother walking among them, refusing to keep quiet.

市場佔據了古老河流乾涸的河床。商人在布棚下販售米、電池、修好的鞋與藥品。有位商人提著一捆電子零件，想換取飲用水。肩上停著機械鴿子的孩子邀請旅人到小店用餐，說自己能幫忙找到打開種子庫的晶片。西奧用最後一枚食物代幣買了甜甜圈給他。等待時，艾莉絲聽到人們閒聊最近的議會民調。最富裕的街區獲承諾建造新噴泉，市中心的家庭卻要被迫搬進更遠的沙塵區。貧窮在這裡不只是倒楣，而是遠方掌權者決策的結果。艾莉絲看著排隊取水的人群，想像母親也曾在其中行走，拒絕沉默。

The boy led them to a hidden workshop where an elderly mechanic tested a strange substance in a clear cup. It turned blue when ordinary water touched it and red when exposed to waste from the city's central plant. 'Your mighty council sends dirty water downhill,' the mechanic said. 'Then it sells us filters for our own safety.' He offered to show Iris the records, but armed men entered the market before he could finish. Theo reacted first, pushing a table between them and the soldiers. Iris caught the falling cup, then made a leap across a narrow drainage channel. The child sent his pigeon after the guards, using it to distract their cameras. Mara stayed behind to explain to the crowd what the chemical test meant. For the sake of the families listening, she finally spoke openly about her own vote. No one applauded, but no one left either.

孩子帶他們進一間隱密工坊。一位老技師正在透明杯裡測試奇怪物質：普通水碰到它會變藍，中央工廠排出的廢水則會令它變紅。「你們偉大的議會把髒水往下游排，」技師說，「再為了我們的安全，向我們賣濾水器。」他本想把紀錄拿給艾莉絲看，武裝人員卻已走進市場。西奧首先反應，推倒桌子擋住士兵。艾莉絲接住跌落的杯子，躍過狹窄排水溝。孩子派出機械鴿子干擾守衛的攝影機。瑪拉留下來，向群眾解釋化學檢測的意義。為了眼前這些家庭，她終於坦承自己當年如何投票。沒有人鼓掌，但也沒有人離開。

They escaped through a tunnel beneath a ruined restaurant. At its end they found a storage room filled with old maps and a large wooden table. The boy introduced himself as Pip and pulled a small device from his sleeve. It contained the chip they needed. 'My father worked on the northern water gates,' he said. 'He vanished after telling me the river was still alive underground.' Iris asked why he was helping strangers. Pip shrugged. 'Because you came back for the cup when everybody else ran. People who save evidence might save people too.' Mara arrived an hour later, bleeding slightly from one arm but otherwise unharmed. She told Iris that speaking the truth had not made her trustworthy; only her next choices could do that. Iris agreed. As the market lights dimmed, they planned a route through the old subway toward the northern tower. Deep below their feet, water moved through stone like a secret being repeated.

他們穿過廢棄餐廳下方的隧道逃走，盡頭是一間堆滿舊地圖與大木桌的儲藏室。孩子自稱皮普，從袖口拿出一個小裝置，裡面正是他們需要的晶片。「我爸爸曾在北方水閘工作，」他說，「他告訴我地下河仍然活著之後，就失蹤了。」艾莉絲問他為什麼幫陌生人。皮普聳肩。「因為大家都跑時，只有妳回頭撿杯子。會救證據的人，也可能救人。」瑪拉一小時後回來，一隻手臂微微流血，其他都無礙。她告訴艾莉絲，說出真相不代表已值得信任，只有往後的選擇能證明。艾莉絲同意。市場燈光暗下時，他們規畫穿過舊地鐵，前往北方高塔。腳底深處，水在岩石間流動，像不斷被重述的祕密。
