收容所挑戰

The Shelter Challenge

死對頭成了我的雙人直播搭檔 · 第 4 / 25 章 · 1000–2000 · 英中對照

用互動版查詞、朗讀與收藏

The next contest task sent the pair to an animal shelter in the eastern part of town. Their department wanted students to attend a public service event and report what help was truly needed. Xia found a little wolf drawing on the shelter gate. A volunteer explained that it was the symbol of an old rescue group, not a real wolf. Inside, several empty cages stood beside a cleaning room. A frightened dog moved slowly in one cage, and the volunteer asked them not to put its face on camera. Ethan said he would follow that standard without question. Xia was glad to hear him defend the animal's private space before anyone reminded him.

下一個競賽任務把兩人送到城東的動物收容所。系上要求學生參加公益活動，報導真正需要什麼協助。夏在大門看到一幅小狼圖案。志工說那是舊救援團體的標誌，不是真的狼。裡面幾個空籠子放在清潔室旁。一隻受驚的狗在籠裡慢慢移動，志工請他們不要拍牠的臉。以森毫不猶豫表示會遵守這項標準。夏很高興，因為不必別人提醒，他就先維護了動物的私人空間。

The shelter's manager showed them a long list of daily work. Volunteers had to comb the dogs, wash bowls, and carry food from a small storehouse. Some animals needed a special drug, which only the clinic could give under a doctor's order. Xia did not want to make their story sound like an easy game. “Our purpose is to encourage help, not to turn an animal's pain into a joke,” she said. Ethan agreed. They planned to film hands washing bowls, people preparing beds, and a safe play area. To make the scene lively, Nora brought a paper bird with one blue wing that could move in the wind.

收容所經理拿出一長串每日工作清單。志工得梳理狗毛、洗碗，還要從小倉庫搬飼料。有些動物需要特殊藥物，只能由診所按照醫囑給藥。夏不想讓報導聽起來像輕鬆的遊戲。「我們的目的，是鼓勵幫忙，不是拿動物的痛苦開玩笑，」她說。以森同意。他們決定拍洗碗的手、準備床鋪的人，以及安全的活動區。為了讓畫面活潑一點，諾拉帶來一隻有藍色翅膀、能隨風動的紙鳥。

Rain began before lunch. Ethan opened an umbrella above the camera, while Xia helped an older volunteer carry boxes. At the kitchen table a cook set a pan on the stove and began to fry vegetables for the workers. He invited the students to eat, but they still had to finish cleaning first. The shelter also worked with a gymnasium nearby, where people could bring donated towels after exercise. Xia found the idea surprisingly good: a minor action repeated by many people could become important support. She asked the manager to explain the connection on camera, and the answer was clearer than any speech they could have written.

午餐前就下起雨。以森撐傘保護攝影機，夏則幫年長志工搬箱子。廚房裡，廚師把平底鍋放上爐子，開始替工作人員炒蔬菜。他邀學生一起吃，但他們得先完成清掃。收容所也與附近的體育館合作，讓運動後的人捐出毛巾。夏覺得這個方法出乎意料地好：許多人重複做一件小事，就可能成為重要支援。她請經理在鏡頭前解釋這種連結，答案比他們自己寫的任何演說都清楚。

The shelter manager also explained that rescue groups across the eastern region shared equipment when one place had too many animals to care for. One group might lend transport boxes, while another could offer clean blankets. Xia asked why the shelter did not simply ask for money. The manager answered that people often wanted to help but did not know what would be useful. A clear list gave them a good beginning. Ethan later added that list beneath the video, with the shelter's permission. He checked the contact information twice. Xia noticed that his quiet care was becoming a normal part of their teamwork.

收容所經理也說明，東部地區的救援團體會在某處動物太多時共享設備。一個團體可以借運輸箱，另一個則提供乾淨毛毯。夏問為何收容所不直接募款。經理回答，很多人想幫忙，只是不知道什麼最有用。清楚的清單能給他們一個好起點。以森在獲得收容所允許後，把清單放在影片下方。他還核對了兩次聯絡資料。夏注意到，他安靜的細心已成為團隊合作的日常。

A dog suddenly jumped at the paper bird. Ethan moved the camera and almost fell. Xia caught his coat, but their motion knocked a bucket across the floor. Both froze. The volunteer laughed and asked them to help clean the water. “We are not brilliant at this,” Ethan said. Xia answered, “But we are learning.” He had always seemed firm and perfect during debates; now he was wet, his hair needed a comb, and his smile looked warm. While they worked, Xia realized that she liked him more when he made a small mistake and admitted it. She decided not to say that aloud.

一隻狗突然撲向紙鳥。以森移動攝影機時差點跌倒。夏抓住他的外套，但兩人的動作把水桶撞翻。兩人都僵住了。志工笑著請他們一起擦水。「我們做這個真不高明，」以森說。夏回答：「但我們在學。」他在辯論時總顯得堅定又完美；現在卻全身濕答答、頭髮需要梳理，笑容也變得溫暖。工作時，夏發現自己更喜歡犯了小錯也肯承認的他。她決定先不說出口。

Back on campus, they edited the report without showing any animal in distress. The shelter approved the final film, and viewers sent towels and food the next day. Professor Lin praised their choices. She said a good team knew when to follow rules and when to ask why a rule existed. Ethan looked across the room at Xia, then moved one chair closer so they could see the same screen. The movement was small, but it made Xia's heart jump. She pretended to study the audio track. It was easier to understand a camera than to understand why this former enemy was beginning to feel familiar.

回到校園後，他們剪好報導，沒有播放動物痛苦的畫面。收容所審核通過影片，隔天觀眾便送來毛巾和食物。林教授稱讚他們的選擇。她說，好團隊懂得何時遵守規則，也懂得何時詢問規則存在的原因。以森隔著房間望向夏，接著把椅子移近，好一起看螢幕。這個動作很小，卻讓夏心跳了一下。她假裝專心研究聲音軌道。理解攝影機，比理解這個昔日死對頭為何漸漸變得熟悉，要容易得多。
