艦隊的判決

The Fleet's Verdict

The island council convened beneath a massive canvas roof because the assembly hall could no longer hold the crowd. It was an unusual episode in local politics: fishermen, shopkeepers, and visiting scholars gathered to challenge a corporation that employed hundreds of their neighbors. One democrat argued that their island charter contained constitutional protections for public waters. A company attorney dismissed the claim as exaggeration. Then a peasant farmer from the inland valley stood and described how polluted irrigation had destroyed his herb garden. His hands were rough, his speech unpolished, and his account far more convincing than the attorney's prepared remarks.

島議會在巨大的帆布棚下開會，因為原本的議事廳已容不下群眾。這成了地方政治上不尋常的一幕：漁民、店主、學者共同質疑雇用數百名鄰居的大企業。一名民主派議員指出，島上自治章程提供公共水域的憲制保障。公司律師斥為誇大。一位內陸農民此時站起，說明污染灌溉如何毀去他的香草園。他的雙手粗糙，口才不佳，證詞卻遠比律師精心準備的說辭有力。

Ocean Reach offered a new incentive: cash for boats that joined its research fleet. During the recession, many families had survived on credit, so the offer was difficult to ignore. A fish seller explained that retail prices had fallen while fuel costs had risen. “We are not greedy for wanting work,” she said. “But greed is asking us to sell the sea that feeds our children.” Her words made the crowd murmur. Yu watched Vale's expression as he calculated the cost of persuasion. He needed just enough people to doubt the evidence and keep working until the next test.

遠洋之境提出新誘因：漁船加入研究船隊即可領取現金。經濟衰退時，許多家庭靠借貸度日，這項提議難以拒絕。一名魚販說明零售魚價下降，燃料卻變貴。「我們想工作，不代表貪婪，」她說，「真正的貪婪，是要我們賣掉養育孩子的海。」人群低聲議論，林雨看見維爾開始盤算說服群眾要花多少代價。他只需要讓足夠多人懷疑證據，就能讓下一場實驗照常進行。

A sudden rattle came from the harbor. Six patrol boats advanced through the mist, their lights blinking in formation. The deputy had ordered them to recruit volunteers to guard the company's vessel. Rae whispered that this was a familiar tactic: create fear, then offer protection from it. The captain claimed he needed to secure the port against outsiders. Yu asked him whether that included fishermen born on the island. His eyes moved toward a row of children holding hand-painted signs. Instead of giving the order to arrest anyone, he stopped his crew at the harbor wall and listened.

港口忽然傳來一陣機械震響。六艘巡邏艇在霧中成隊前進，燈光閃爍。副手命令他們招募志願者守護公司船隻。蕾低聲說，這是熟悉的策略：先製造恐懼，再兜售保護。船長聲稱要防範外來者，林雨問，這是否也包含土生土長的漁民。他望向一排舉著手繪標語的孩子，終究沒有下令抓人，而是讓船員停在港牆邊傾聽。

The vote began after sunset. The council required substantial proof before it could halt the project, but the families had already crossed a moral threshold. They would no longer accept private reassurances in place of public evidence. Just as the clerk prepared to announce the preliminary verdict, a crash shook the pier. A driver had tried to smash the door of the archives, hoping to destroy the dry-dock plans. Two fishermen held him until the police arrived. The attempted break-in changed the meeting: even council members who favored the company now wanted to know what lay inside the tunnel.

日落後開始投票。議會雖要求充分證據才能中止計畫，但居民早已跨越心中的界線：他們不再接受私下安撫來取代公開證明。書記官正準備宣布初步裁決，碼頭卻傳來巨響。一名駕駛企圖撞碎檔案室門，想銷毀乾船塢圖紙。兩位漁民把他制住，直到警方抵達。這場闖入改變會議氣氛；連支持企業的議員也開始追問隧道裡究竟藏了什麼。

The next morning rumors were widespread. Some said the company had built a weapon, others that the missing lighthouse keeper had been paid to disappear. Yu refused to repeat either story. The council voted to freeze new permits and protect the archive, but did not yet order the ship away. It was not the victory the crowd wanted. Bo reminded her that law often advanced more slowly than danger. Still, the fishermen agreed to keep their boats in port until independent inspectors could enter the dry dock. That quiet refusal threatened the project more than any shouted accusation.

隔天上午，謠言已廣泛流傳：有人說公司建造武器，有人說失蹤的守塔人收錢消失。林雨拒絕轉述任何一種。議會決定凍結新許可並保護檔案，卻尚未命令研究船離港。群眾期待的勝利沒有到來。阿博提醒她，法律的腳步往往比危險慢。但漁民同意暫停出海，直到獨立檢查員進入乾船塢。這種安靜的拒絕，比激烈控訴更能威脅公司的計畫。

After the vote, Yu found a page torn from her father's notebook beneath the council table. Someone had drawn the thirteenth light again, this time beside the words “North Gate, low tide.” She wondered whether the person who planted it wanted to help her or lead her into a trap. Rae insisted on coming along. Bo checked the batteries in their lamps and promised to keep a radio open at the lighthouse. They agreed on one rule: if the tide turned before they found a safe entrance, they would retreat. Courage, Yu had learned, was not the same as pretending that danger could be ignored.

散會後，林雨在議會桌下發現一頁從父親筆記本撕下的紙。有人再次畫出第十三道燈光，旁邊寫著「北門，低潮」。她不知道放紙的人是要幫忙，還是引她入陷阱。蕾堅持同行，阿博檢查燈具電池，答應在燈塔守著無線電。他們立下唯一規則：若潮水轉向前仍找不到安全入口，就立刻撤退。林雨已明白，勇敢不等於假裝危險不存在。
