The market hearing drew a large attendance despite the rain. A vendor arrived with a bruise on her arm and alleged that a guard had pushed her away from the council gate. Iona recorded the complaint without guessing what the guard intended. The chair of a contemporary trading corporation argued that the dispute was controversial and might interrupt business. Lera chose to contend with his figures instead of insulting him. She showed that the market could remain open under a shorter, independently measured tide schedule. The corporation could defy the warning, but it could not make the independent waterline disappear.

即使下雨，市場聽證會仍有許多人出席。一名攤販手臂帶著瘀傷，指稱警衛曾在議會門口推她。伊歐娜記下申訴，沒有猜測警衛的動機。一家當代貿易公司的董事長說爭議太大，恐怕影響商業活動。蕾拉選擇用數據反駁，而非出言羞辱。她展示市場如何在縮短後、經獨立測量的潮汐時程下繼續營業。公司可以違抗警告，卻不能讓獨立量出的水線消失。

An engineer proposed an endorsement of the new road plan, but Iona asked the signers to read it first. The essence of the dispute was not whether trade mattered. It was whether one timetable should decide who was allowed to cross the flats. Tomas brought a small offering of bread from the port kitchens; the vendors had prepared it as thanks for the volunteer observers. His soaked outfit drew a few smiles. The break helped facilitate a calmer discussion. From another perspective, the corporation feared losing ships to a neighboring port. Iona added that concern to the minutes without treating it as permission to misstate the tide.

一名工程師提議聯名背書新道路計畫，但伊歐娜請簽署者先讀懂內容。爭議的本質不是商業重不重要，而是是否應讓一份時刻表決定誰能走過灘地。托馬斯帶來港口廚房贈送的麵包，是攤商感謝志工觀測員的心意。他全身濕透的服裝惹得幾人微笑，休息讓討論稍稍平靜。從另一個角度看，公司擔心船隻轉去鄰港。伊歐娜將這份擔憂記入紀錄，卻不認為它能成為謊報潮汐的許可。

A resident named Beren stood and described a ritual his family performed at the northern wells every spring. The revised council map placed those wells inside a planned industrial segment. The irony was difficult to ignore: officials called the land empty while people who lived there could list generations of names. Iona asked Beren whether he wanted the family history published. He declined, though he consented to the location being marked on the public map. The distinction mattered. Statistical records could describe how many people used the wells, but they did not entitle the council to publish every person's story.

居民貝倫站起來，說明家族每年春天在北方水井舉行的儀式。議會新版地圖卻把那些井畫入預定工業區的一部分。這份諷刺令人難以忽略：官員稱土地空無一人，居住在那裡的家庭卻能數出好幾代人的名字。伊歐娜問貝倫是否願公開家史，他拒絕了，但同意在公共地圖上標出井的位置。兩者必須區分。統計資料能記錄多少人使用水井，卻不能讓議會擅自公開每一個人的故事。

Near the close of the hearing, Malrec warned that the city faced a difficult transition. Iona agreed. She wanted a process that did not force frightened merchants to choose between income and truth. A neutral clerk collected the signed witness statements, and two residents received numbered copies. The engineer promised to test the proposed handrail without charging the households. For the first time, the market workers elected their own pair of observers to attend the next tide reading. That overall change was small, but it shifted the balance of the room. Malrec's office could no longer claim to speak for all residents without asking them.

聽證接近尾聲時，馬爾雷克警告城市正面臨艱難轉型。伊歐娜表示同意。她希望程序不會迫使恐懼的商人只能在收入與真相之間選一個。中立書記收取簽名證詞，兩名居民各拿到編號副本。工程師承諾免費為家庭測試新扶手。市場工人也首次選出兩名自己的觀察員，參加下一次潮汐讀數。整體改變雖小，卻影響了整個會議室的權力平衡。馬爾雷克的辦公室今後不能不問居民就自稱代表所有人。

After the meeting, Iona discovered a folded account sheet inside the council's printed budget. It listed payments for a second measuring basin at the north lock, though no basin existed there. Ada confirmed that the proposed site had never been excavated. The line might represent a canceled project or a mistake in filing; either explanation deserved a check. They agreed to request the original purchase order and inspect the ground before claiming fraud. Iona placed the account sheet in a transparent sleeve and wrote down where she found it. A mystery was becoming a pattern, but she refused to skip the steps that made a pattern meaningful.

會後，伊歐娜在議會印製預算中發現一張折起的帳單，列著北閘第二座測量池的付款紀錄，但現場根本沒有那座池。艾達確認預定地從未開挖。這一行可能是取消的工程，也可能是歸檔錯誤；兩種解釋都需要查驗。她們同意先取得原始採購單並實地查看，暫不指控舞弊。伊歐娜把帳單放進透明護套，記下發現位置。謎團逐漸形成規律，但她拒絕跳過能證明規律有意義的步驟。

That night Tomas walked with Iona across the upper road. The warning lamps burned steadily, and the low pathway remained safely closed. From the hill they could see the bell tower, the archive, the market, and the northern flats where Beren's family kept their wells. Each place had a different interest in the timetable, but all of them depended on the same moving water. Iona told Tomas she was afraid that proving the council wrong would not be enough. He answered that the purpose of the evidence was not to win an argument. It was to help people choose what to do tomorrow.

當晚托馬斯陪伊歐娜走過高處道路。警示燈穩定亮著，低窪通道也安全封閉。從山坡可以看見潮鐘樓、檔案館、市場，以及貝倫家族守護水井的北方灘地。各處對時刻表的利益不同，卻都依賴同一片流動的海水。伊歐娜告訴托馬斯，她害怕即使證明議會有錯，也還遠遠不夠。托馬斯回答，證據的目的不是贏得爭論，而是幫助人們決定明天要做什麼。
