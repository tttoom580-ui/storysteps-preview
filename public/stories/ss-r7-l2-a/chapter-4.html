不會輕易碎的瓦

A Tile That Will Not Break

沒有火種的修窯師 · 第 4 / 25 章 · 1000–2000 · 英中對照

用互動版查詞、朗讀與收藏

At the clinic, Lin met the director of winter health services. The director feared disease if the entire seed house stayed cold and its food plants died. She showed Lin a branch of the town network connecting schools, farms, and medicine rooms. A young worker had cut his hand on a tile and lost a little blood. The wound was small, but it was a warning about sharp material. Lin began to combine fine clay with clean sand, hoping to make a stronger tile. She could imagine the finished roof, yet she could also sense that a pretty surface would mean nothing if it cracked under heat.

在診所，林遇見冬季衛生服務的主任。主任擔心整間育苗屋若持續寒冷，糧食作物死亡會影響居民健康。她展示了連結學校、農場和醫護室的城鎮網路。一名年輕工人被瓦片割傷手，流了一點血。傷口雖小，仍提醒大家材料邊緣的危險。林開始把細陶土和乾淨沙粒混合，希望做出更強的瓦。她能想像完工的屋頂，也明白外表再漂亮，只要受熱破裂就毫無用處。

There was a pause while the workers waited for a safe signal. Lin used it to examine the place around the trial tiles. She counted the doors, looked for loose stones, and made sure that no one had left a bag where people needed to walk. The little inspection did not feel like an adventure. A child could have done part of it. But the child would need to know why each check mattered, and an adult would need to take responsibility for any danger found. Lin wrote both ideas in her notes.

工人等待安全訊號時，現場有片刻空檔。林利用時間檢查試驗瓦周圍，數一數門、查看鬆石，並確定沒人把袋子放在通道上。這樣的小檢查不像冒險，有些步驟孩子都能做。但孩子得明白每項檢查的理由，而成人必須對發現的危險負責。林把兩個想法都寫進筆記。

A generous cook brought hamburger buns, a bowl of papaya, and warm tea to the workshop. She said the population of the lower district could not afford repeated failed tests. Lin calculated the score for three trial tiles: each would be judged by weight, cracks, and how long it held warmth. Their target was a tile safe enough to place above seedlings, not one that won a beauty prize. Wen brought pieces of old wood for a simple stand. Lin measured each piece carefully; the wood was worth saving because they had little spare fuel. Therefore, no full fire would begin before a cold test.

一位慷慨的廚師送來漢堡麵包、木瓜和熱茶。她說下城區的人無法負擔一再失敗的試驗。林為三片試驗瓦訂定評分：重量、裂痕與保溫時間都是指標。他們的目標是能安全放在幼苗上方的瓦，而不是外表漂亮的獎品。文找來舊木材搭簡易支架。林仔細量每一片木頭；燃料不足，舊木也值得珍惜。因此正式點火前，他們先做冷態測試。

The clinic director took Lin into the seed house after the day's test. Under thin covers sat trays of tiny green plants. One tray had already turned brown at the edges. The director explained that the winter meals for the shelter depended on these plants, because carts could not always cross the mountain road. Lin touched the dry soil beside a root, careful not to crush the stem. The plants could not tell anyone how many days they had left. She asked the director to mark the weakest beds on the map. Those beds would receive the first safe tiles, even if the wealthy houses complained.

當天測試後，診所主任帶林走進育苗屋。薄布下面是一盤盤細小綠苗，其中一盤邊緣已轉褐色。主任解釋收容所的冬季餐食仰賴這些作物，因為推車未必能穿越山路。林輕碰根旁的乾土，小心不壓斷嫩莖。植物無法告訴任何人還剩幾天。她請主任在地圖標出最脆弱的苗床，那些地方將先獲得安全的保溫瓦，即使富有人家因此抱怨也一樣。

The first tile held together when moved, then broke after a short fall. The second survived the fall but was too heavy for the old frame. The third had no great beauty; its edge was plain and its color uneven. It remained whole. Lin refused to call it perfect, since a cold drop test did not show how it would behave when heated in winter. The director agreed to let them use a small part of the clinic yard for further trials. She also asked for written records so that people who had never met Lin could understand both the progress and the remaining danger. For an escape route during the hot test, the team cleared the door and stored every piece of wood away from it.

第一片瓦搬動時沒有碎，卻在短距離掉落後破裂。第二片挺過了跌落，對舊支架而言卻太重。第三片外表平凡，邊緣不漂亮，顏色也不均勻，但始終完整。林拒絕稱它完美，因為冷態落摔不能代表冬季受熱。主任同意借出診所院子的一小區供後續試驗，也要求留下書面紀錄，讓不認識林的人也能理解進展與仍存在的危險。為了熱測試時的逃生通道，小隊清空門口，並把每塊木頭都移離那裡。

Lin gave the next task a number in the common notebook. Under it she wrote, “weigh all three samples.” She added the place, the date, the worker in charge, and the condition that would require them to stop. Wen checked the words and asked whether someone who had missed the meeting could follow them. Lin changed one long sentence into two short ones. Writing clearly was not a little ceremony before real work. It was part of the work itself. When the page was finished, she left room beneath it for a result, including the possibility that the test would fail.

林替下一項工作在共用筆記本編號，並寫下「替三份樣品秤重」。她加上地點、日期、負責人與必須停止的條件。文檢查文字，問沒參加會議的人能不能照著做。林把一個長句改成兩個短句。清楚寫字不是正式工作前的小儀式，而是工作本身的一部分。寫完後，她在下方留下結果欄位，也保留測試失敗的可能。

On the way out, Lin passed the place where the first cold test had failed. It looked ordinary now, only a short line on a wall that most visitors would never notice. She stopped long enough to remember how much time one wrong guess had cost them. Then she remembered the useful friends, honest questions, and small repairs that had followed. A good ending, she thought, was not a day without mistakes. It was a day when people could learn from mistakes without hiding them, and return to their homes safely afterward. She turned toward the lamps of the lower town.

離開時，林經過第一次冷態測試失敗的位置。如今那裡很平常，只是牆上的一道短線，大多數訪客根本不會注意。她停了一下，想起一次錯誤猜測曾耗費多少時間，也想起之後那些可靠朋友、誠實提問與小小修復。她想，好結局不是沒有錯誤的一天，而是人能不掩蓋錯誤、從中學習，最後平安回家的一天。她轉向下城區一盞盞亮起的燈。
