七張標記

The Seven Signs

星星不見的那一天 · 第 5 / 25 章 · 1000以下 · 英中對照

用互動版查詞、朗讀與收藏

On Thursday, Ms. Lin showed the club a picture of seven signs used by the town hall. Each sign had a number, and the number told workers where to put things. “At least we know why our paper has a seven,” Mia said. “It may be a room sign, not a name.” Ben pointed at a photo of the stage. He could see a box beside a bean plant and a picture of a frog. Another picture showed a hen and three little chicks. The club had made those things for a school play a long time ago. Mia wondered whether the missing star had been moved with old stage things during the last week.

星期四，林老師給社團看鎮公所使用的七張標記照片。每張標記都有一個數字，告訴工人要把物品放到哪裡。「至少我們知道紙上的七可能代表什麼了，」米雅說，「那也許是房間編號，不是人的名字。」班指著一張舞台照片。他看見一個箱子放在豆子盆栽和青蛙圖片旁。另一張照片裡有母雞和三隻小雞。那些道具是社團很久以前為校園戲劇做的。米雅開始想，失蹤星星是否在上週和舊舞台用品一起被搬走。

Jo wanted to dig through all the boxes as soon as they went back. “Wait a minute,” Ms. Lin said. “We must have the right person with us.” Mia looked at the drawing on the wall. She was sorry that it had taken so long to ask a simple question: who had the key to room seven? The answer was not on the picture. The clerk said that a man called Mr. Green had the key during the move. “He has a long list of work,” he explained. “Sometimes he works late at night.” Mia wrote down his name and the time of their next meeting. She did not point at anyone and call them a thief.

喬想下次去就把所有箱子翻開。「等一分鐘，」林老師說，「一定要有適當的負責人在場。」米雅看著牆上的圖畫，有點懊惱自己這麼晚才想到一個簡單問題：誰有七號房的鑰匙？圖片沒有答案。櫃台人員說，搬運期間是格林先生保管鑰匙。「他有很長一張工作清單，」他解釋，「有時候晚上還要加班。」米雅記下他的名字與下次見面的時間。她沒有指著任何人，說對方是小偷。

That afternoon the school held a short race in the yard. The children ran between two lines of white paint. Ben wanted to win, but he was more worried about Mia. “Are you all right?” he asked when she stopped near the gate. Mia rubbed her neck. “I am tired of being wrong,” she said. Ben thought for a moment. “We were wrong about the sports house, but that was still a safe place to check. The workers helped us. Now we know more.” The words made Mia feel better. She was not in a race to find the answer before everyone else. She could take her time, ask again, and keep learning.

當天下午，學校在庭院舉行一場短跑比賽。孩子們沿著兩條白線之間奔跑。班想贏，但他更擔心米雅。「妳還好嗎？」米雅在校門附近停下時，他問。米雅揉揉脖子。「我厭倦一直猜錯了，」她說。班想了想。「我們以為星星在體育館，結果猜錯了，可是那裡本來就值得安全地查看。工人也幫了我們。現在我們知道更多。」這些話讓米雅好受一些。她並不是在跟別人比誰先找到答案。她可以慢慢來，再問一次，繼續學習。

Although the club had a new lead, Mia knew they still had a lot to deal with. They had to get permission, choose a safe time, and ask questions without making people feel bad. Jo wanted to know why grown-ups could not simply move everything back. Ms. Lin explained that old things belonged to different groups and could break if someone moved them too quickly. “A good deal is not always the fastest one,” she said. “It is the one that is fair and safe for everyone.” At the next club meeting, each child took one job. Ana made a list of questions. Ben checked the map. Jo wrote the names of people they should thank. Mia watched the group work and felt the worry in her chest grow smaller. They were not only waiting for answers. They were ready to ask for them in a careful way.

雖然社團有了新線索，米雅知道還有許多事要處理。他們得取得許可、選擇安全時間，也得提問而不讓別人難堪。喬想知道，大人為什麼不能直接把所有東西搬回來。林老師解釋，舊物屬於不同團體，搬得太急可能損壞。「好的處理方式不一定最快，」她說，「而是對所有人都公平且安全。」下一次社團會議上，孩子們各自分工。安娜整理問題；班核對地圖；喬記下應該感謝的人；米雅看著大家工作，感覺胸口的憂慮慢慢變小。他們不只是在等答案，也已經準備好用仔細的方法提出問題。

A cold wind came after the race. It was not snow, but the air felt like winter. Mia put on her coat. “Ms. Lin said we can meet Mr. Green tomorrow,” she told the group. “We need to prepare good questions.” Jo held up her hand. “Was the box heavy? What was its height? Where did he put it?” Ana added, “Did he see a silver star?” Ben smiled. “That last one is the most important.” They put the questions in order and asked Ms. Lin to look at them. A good question, Mia learned, was better than a loud guess. If they were careful and kind, even a slow search could lead them to the right place.

比賽後吹起一陣冷風。雖然沒有下雪，空氣卻像冬天一樣涼。米雅穿上外套。「林老師說我們明天可以見格林先生，」她告訴大家，「要先準備好問題。」喬舉手說：「箱子重嗎？有多高？他把它放去哪裡？」安娜補充：「他有沒有看見銀色星星？」班笑了。「最後那題最重要。」他們把問題排好順序，再請林老師過目。米雅明白，好的問題比大聲猜測更有用。只要小心且友善，即使搜尋進展緩慢，也能帶他們往正確的地方前進。
