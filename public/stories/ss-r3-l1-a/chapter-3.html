路上的腳印

Footprints on the Road

星星不見的那一天 · 第 3 / 25 章 · 1000以下 · 英中對照

用互動版查詞、朗讀與收藏

On Wednesday, Ms. Lin took the club along the road to the sports house. They walked in a line and stayed on one side of the street. A station stood near the bridge. Across the road was a small farm with one brown cow and three white birds. Ben liked the birds, but Mia said they had work to do. “Every step matters,” she said. “Please follow our teacher.” The sports house was not far from a beach where children played in summer. Mia had been born in that town, but she did not know every building. She wondered what could happen in a place she had passed many times without looking inside.

星期三，林老師帶社團沿著道路走到舊體育館。大家排成一列，走在街道同一側。橋邊有一座車站。馬路對面是小農場，裡頭有一頭棕色母牛和三隻白鳥。班很喜歡那些鳥，但米雅提醒他們還有事情要做。「每一步都很重要，」她說，「請跟著老師。」舊體育館離海灘不遠，夏天常有孩子去玩。米雅出生在這座小鎮，卻不是每棟建築都熟悉。她心想，自己走過那麼多次、從未仔細看過的地方，究竟會發生什麼事。

The door was open because two workers were cleaning the floor. Ms. Lin spoke to the man in charge. He asked them to put their wet shoes near the entrance. Mia looked at her shoe and saw a dry yellow mark near the front. It came from school paint, not from the missing star. She did not want to make one more false clue. Inside, an old table stood beside the wall. A bucket of water was under it. One worker used a long stick to pull a box away from the door. The box held balls and ropes. There was nothing silver inside. The worker let them look, but only after he had opened it himself.

大門開著，因為兩名工人正在清潔地板。林老師先和負責人說話。他請大家把沾濕的鞋放在入口旁。米雅低頭看自己的鞋，發現鞋尖有一點乾掉的黃色痕跡。那是學校的顏料，不是失蹤星星留下的線索。她不想又把無關的東西當成證據。裡面有一張靠牆的舊桌子，桌下放著一桶水。一名工人用長棍把箱子從門邊拉開。箱裡只有球和繩子，沒有銀色物品。工人願意讓他們看，但先由他自己把箱子打開。

They looked around the open room for about ten minutes. Ben found a paper with a number seven, but it was blue, not yellow. Jo wanted to run into a small room at the back. Ms. Lin stopped her. “We ask first,” she said, pointing at the club rule in Mia's book. Jo felt bad, then took one step back. A man came from the back room and told them that a truck had arrived on Friday. “The driver left chairs here,” he said. “Then he went to the town hall.” This was new information. It did not tell them where the star was, but it gave the friends a second stop on their map.

他們在開放的房間裡四處查看了大約十分鐘。班找到一張印著七號的紙，但它是藍色，不是黃色。喬想跑進後面的一間小房間，林老師阻止了她。「要先詢問，」老師指著米雅筆記本上的社團規則說。喬有些不好意思，退後一步。一名男子從後面的房間走出來，告訴他們貨車星期五來過。「司機把椅子留在這裡，」他說，「然後開去鎮公所大廳。」這是新資訊。它沒有直接告訴他們星星在哪裡，卻為地圖增加了第二個地點。

Back at school, the children had to clean the art room before they could leave. Mia washed a brush, Ben put paper in a drawer, and Jo swept a little dust from the floor. The work was not exciting, but Ms. Lin said it was part of caring for the room they used. Mia thought about the sports house. The workers there had been cleaning, too, and the club had asked them to stop for a short visit. “We should thank them again,” she said. Ana agreed and wrote a short card. The message did not say the workers had found the star. It said they had helped the club learn where the truck went next. Mia liked that difference. A thank-you card could tell the truth and still feel warm. After the room was ready, Ms. Lin let them put the card in the school mail. They went home tired, but pleased with their work.

回到學校後，孩子們得先把美術教室打掃乾淨才能離開。米雅洗畫筆，班把紙放回抽屜，喬掃掉地上的一些灰塵。工作並不刺激，但林老師說，這也是照顧共用教室的一部分。米雅想到體育館。那裡的工人也正在清潔，而社團還請他們停下工作接受短暫訪問。「我們應該再謝謝他們一次，」她說。安娜同意，寫了一張小卡片。卡片上沒有說工人找到了星星，而是謝謝他們幫助社團知道貨車的下一站。米雅很喜歡這個差別。感謝卡可以既真實又溫暖。教室整理好後，林老師讓他們把卡片交給學校郵件室。大家雖然疲累，卻對今天的工作感到滿意。

On the walk home, Mia put her hand on the map so the wind would not take it. The bridge shook a little as a bus passed. Jo said she was sorry for running ahead. “I was sure we would find the star in that room,” she said. Mia answered, “I wanted to go there, too. But if we forget our rule, people may stop helping us.” They passed the birds again. This time Ben did not stop. He drew a little bird in the corner of the map when they reached school. Under it, Mia wrote one sentence: “The truck went to the town hall after the sports house.” Now the road on their map had a clear next turn.

回程時，米雅用手壓住地圖，免得被風吹走。一輛公車經過時，橋面微微震動。喬為自己剛才跑在前面道歉。「我真的以為那間房裡有星星，」她說。米雅回答：「我也想進去看。但如果我們忘記規則，別人可能就不願再幫忙了。」他們再次經過那幾隻鳥。這回班沒有停下來，直到回到學校，才在地圖角落畫了一隻小鳥。米雅在下方寫下一句：「貨車離開體育館後去了鎮公所大廳。」現在地圖上的道路有了明確的下一個轉彎。
