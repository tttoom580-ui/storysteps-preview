醫務室的時鐘

The Clock at the Infirmary

王庭第七份證詞 · 第 4 / 25 章 · 5000–6000 · 英中對照

用互動版查詞、朗讀與收藏

The infirmary sat behind a row of lush pear trees, close enough to the archive for its staff to hear the alarm. The landlady who rented rooms to the nurses showed Serin into a narrow corridor and warned her that a recent overflow from the washroom had damaged several records. Nurse Orla had saved a comprehensive schedule, however, by storing duplicates inside a wooden dresser. She spoke with the calm dedication of someone accustomed to frightened patients. She had marked the children's appointments with a thermometer reading and the precise minute of each injection. Her familiarity with the daily routine made the entries credible, but Serin still requested evidence from the infirmary superintendent. Orla agreed. A trustworthy witness, she said, should welcome questions that could refute an honest mistake as readily as a deliberate lie.

醫務室坐落在一排茂密的梨樹後，距離檔案庫近到員工能聽見警鐘。出租房間給護理師的女房東帶瑟琳走進狹長走廊，提醒她最近洗衣間溢水，損毀了幾份紀錄。不過護理師奧菈把副本藏在木製梳妝櫃裡，保住了一份完整時程表。她說話沉著，像是早已習慣安撫受驚的病人。孩子的預約旁記著體溫計讀數與每次注射的精確分鐘。她熟悉日常流程，因此紀錄可信，但瑟琳仍要求醫務室主管提出獨立佐證。奧菈同意了。她說，值得信任的證人應當歡迎那些能推翻誠實錯誤的問題，就像歡迎揭穿蓄意謊言的問題一樣。

On a white cloth, Orla laid out the linen that had survived the flood. One corner smelled of bleach, but the handwritten numbers remained visible. “Some people in the palace hierarchy believe a junior nurse cannot correct a judge,” she said. Serin replied that the Witness Code measured observations, not titles. From an open window came the call of a seagull, and faint music from a rehearsal of the royal symphony. The ordinary sounds reminded Serin that the city had kept living while the courts hurried toward a conviction. Orla showed her a record of the exact moment the first bell sounded: she had stopped measuring a fever and written “alarm” in the margin. The physician, entering from another room, had signed beneath it. This independent signature placed the bell nine minutes before the court's official time.

奧菈在白布上攤開從淹水中救出的亞麻布。一角有漂白水的味道，手寫數字卻仍清晰。「王宮權力階序中的某些人認為，低階護理師不可能糾正法官。」她說。瑟琳回答，《證人法》衡量的是觀察，不是頭銜。敞開的窗外傳來海鷗叫聲，遠處還有王室交響樂團彩排的隱約樂聲。平凡聲響提醒瑟琳，當法庭急著定罪時，城市仍照常生活。奧菈指給她看第一次鐘聲響起的確切時刻：她當時暫停測量發燒病人的體溫，在邊欄寫下「警報」。剛從另一個房間進來的醫師在下方簽名。這個獨立署名把鐘聲時間推到法庭官方時間的九分鐘之前。

The superintendent confirmed the clinic clock had been checked against the public clock that morning. He would not promise perfection: the two mechanisms could differ by a minute, perhaps two. Serin appreciated the limitation. A correction of nine minutes could not be explained away by such a small difference. While examining a supply ledger, she overheard two attendants discussing a minister's visit before dawn. They had been ordered to remove an empty medicine case from the courtyard. Serin recorded their names, then invited each to speak separately. She refused to let a rumor grow into a convenient theory. Outside, an older woman had come to mourn a daughter lost in an earlier fire. Orla offered her wholesome soup and a chair, then returned to help Serin without turning the woman's grief into evidence.

主管證實，診所時鐘當天早晨曾與公共時鐘校對。他不保證百分之百準確：兩套機械也許相差一分鐘，甚至兩分鐘。瑟琳感謝他說出限制。九分鐘的差距不可能用如此微小的誤差解釋。檢查用品簿時，她無意間聽見兩名勤務員談論某位大臣黎明前到訪。對方命令他們從院子搬走一只空藥箱。瑟琳記下姓名，再分別邀請兩人單獨陳述，拒絕讓傳聞自行長成方便的理論。室外，一名年長婦人前來悼念多年前在另一場火災中失去的女兒。奧菈端給她營養的熱湯和椅子，隨後回來協助瑟琳，沒有把婦人的悲傷變成證據。

By afternoon the infirmary superintendent had copied the original schedule under two seals, and Serin could compare it with the orphanage ledger. Both institutions agreed on the earlier alarm. The palace might still dispute what caused the fire, but its familiar chronology no longer held. Jori was delighted until Serin reminded him that proving a time wrong was not the same as proving who wrote the false account. The correction did, however, nurture an important possibility: the witnesses had been instructed to describe a common moment rather than their own separate memories. The next question was where they had received those instructions. Serin sent notice to each witness offering a safe opportunity to correct a statement before the hearing. She expected anger, relief, and silence in different measures. All three would have to be handled with care.

午後，醫務室主管已在兩道封印下複製原始時程，瑟琳得以與育幼院簿冊比較。兩個機構都指向較早的警報時間。王宮仍可爭辯火災原因，但熟悉的官方時間線已站不住腳。喬里十分高興，直到瑟琳提醒他，證明時間錯誤不等於證明是誰寫下假供述。這項更正仍孕育出一個重要可能：證人可能接到指示，描述共同指定的時刻，而非各自的記憶。下一個問題是，他們在哪裡收到指示？瑟琳分別通知每名證人，允許他們在聽證前於安全環境下更正供述。她預期會收到不同程度的憤怒、鬆一口氣與沉默，而三者都必須謹慎處理。

That night, unrest spread through the administrative district. A crowd accused the nurses of conspiring with the accused copyist, and another crowd demanded punishment for every palace official. Serin watched the turmoil from a side window without joining either side. The evidence had opened a question, not delivered a verdict. She arranged for extra guards at the infirmary, but insisted they remain outside patient rooms and take no names from medical records. Protecting witnesses did not give investigators permission to invade everyone else's private life. Before leaving, she thanked Orla for preserving an ordinary schedule during a difficult week. Orla answered that the habit had seemed unimportant until today. Serin thought about how many such records a powerful person might have dismissed as too small to matter. She promised herself she would never dismiss them so easily again.

那晚，行政區出現騷動。一群人指控護理師與被告抄寫員勾結，另一群人要求懲罰每一名宮廷官員。瑟琳從側窗看著混亂，沒有加入任何一邊。證據打開的是問題，還不是判決。她安排額外守衛保護醫務室，卻要求他們留在病房外，也不得從醫療紀錄抄走病人姓名。保護證人不代表調查者可以侵犯所有人的私人生活。離開前，她感謝奧菈在艱困的一週仍保留平凡時程。奧菈說，她原本不覺得這個習慣重要，直到今天。瑟琳想起權勢人物可能把多少同類紀錄當作微不足道的東西而忽略。她對自己承諾，往後絕不再如此輕視它們。
