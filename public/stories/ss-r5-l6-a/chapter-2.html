寡居者的日曆

The Widow's Calendar

王庭第七份證詞 · 第 2 / 25 章 · 5000–6000 · 英中對照

The messenger's trail led Serin beyond the eastern crossing, where the capital climbed toward an old royal watch station. At that altitude, terraces held small gardens between walls of dark stone. Families from the aboriginal mountain settlements sold woven baskets beside the road, and accounts of the last uprising still abounded in the taverns. An usher at the watch station remembered a deliveryman with a limp, but his description did not align with the name on the registry. Serin's colleague Jori, a versatile interpreter of local dialects, warned her that a false name was only an initial hurdle. The rider might have delivered a sealed packet without knowing what was inside. Serin disliked his caution, yet she knew that annoyance could not replace evidence. They climbed the final steps in silence.

信差的行蹤把瑟琳帶到東側通道之外。首都的道路在那裡向上延伸，通往古老的王家瞭望站。高海拔的梯田夾在深色石牆之間，原住山地聚落的家庭在路邊出售編織籃子，酒館裡仍充滿關於上次起義的傳聞。瞭望站的引導員記得一名跛腳送件人，但外貌描述與登記簿上的姓名對不起來。瑟琳的同事喬里擅長解讀地方方言，他提醒她，假名字只是一開始的障礙。送件人可能根本不知道密封包裹裝了什麼。瑟琳不喜歡這種謹慎，卻明白煩躁不能取代證據。兩人沉默地走完最後一段階梯。

Lady Elda's widower lived in a plain house near the station bell. He was a retired registrar with stiff hands and a notably blunt manner. He did not ask Serin to sit. Instead, he placed his wife's burial calendar beside the court statement and invited a rigorous comparison of dates. The funeral, witnessed by three clergy members, had taken place thirty-eight months earlier. The latest document claimed Elda had testified only six days ago. The widower had made an endeavor to restore her reputation before, but officials dismissed his letters as grief. Serin studied his posture and the small changes in his voice without pretending to read his mind. Her job was to verify the dates with precision. She requested permission to copy the parish register and the receipt for the funeral cloth.

艾妲夫人的鰥夫住在瞭望站大鐘附近一棟樸素的房子裡。他原是登記官，雙手僵硬，說話十分直率。他沒有請瑟琳坐下，只把妻子的安葬日曆放在法庭證詞旁，要求兩人嚴格比較日期。葬禮在三十八個月前舉行，有三名神職人員見證；最新文件卻聲稱艾妲僅在六天前作證。鰥夫過去曾努力為妻子恢復名譽，官員卻把他的信當作哀傷者的胡言。瑟琳觀察他的姿勢與聲音細微變化，卻不假裝自己能讀心。她的職責是精確核對日期。她請求複印教區登記和葬禮布料的收據。

On the walk back, Jori found an old notice ordering the discharge of two watch guards after the uprising. The commander had tried to veto their appeal, although the law did not grant him that power. One guard had been accused of helping a courier discard a pouch of letters near the border. Serin saw a possible link to their present case, but she refused to adopt it without proof. She asked Jori to track the guard's official address and the date of his dismissal. At a narrow terrace, a cart nearly struck an elderly vendor; Jori stepped forward to usher her out of its path. The vendor thanked him and recalled that delivery riders had recently used a different badge from the regular palace service. That new detail was modest, yet it could be checked.

回程路上，喬里找到一張舊公告，記載兩名瞭望守衛在起義後被解職。指揮官曾試圖否決他們的申訴，雖然法律並未賦予他那項權力。其中一名守衛被控幫信差在邊界附近丟棄一袋信件。瑟琳看見這可能與目前案件有關，卻不願在沒有證據時就接受推論。她請喬里追查守衛的正式住址和解職日期。在一處狹窄梯田旁，一輛馬車差點撞上年長攤販；喬里向前引導她避開。攤販道謝，並想起最近送件騎士使用的徽章與一般王宮勤務不同。這個新細節雖小，卻能查證。

The widower had one more request. His wife's final private letter, written shortly before her death, remained inside a parish box. She had feared that officials would use her name after she could no longer defend it. Serin arranged for two witnesses to open the box together, a procedure that would protect both the document and its owners. The letter mentioned a narrow weakness in the royal registry: some officers could replace an index leaf while leaving the outer seal untouched. It did not accuse any particular person. Serin copied the warning carefully rather than turning it into a dramatic charge. Elda had been dead for years, but she had understood the danger of allowing authority to stand in for truth. The investigator left the station with a calendar, a receipt, and a reason to inspect the index itself.

鰥夫另有一個要求。妻子臨終前寫的一封私人信仍留在教區箱內。她生前擔憂官員會在她無法自辯之後借用她的姓名。瑟琳安排兩名見證人共同開箱，這個程序同時保護文件與所有者。信裡指出王家登記制度的一處狹窄漏洞：部分官員能在不破壞外封印的情況下換掉索引頁。她沒有指控任何特定的人。瑟琳謹慎地抄下警告，沒有把它誇大成戲劇性的罪名。艾妲已經離世多年，卻早就理解讓權力取代真相的危險。調查官帶著日曆、收據以及必須檢查索引的理由離開瞭望站。

Back in the city, the court secretary offered to close the case against Tavi if Serin would simply omit Elda's name from her report. The suggestion tempted her because the young copyist might go home immediately. But a quiet compromise would leave the machine of false testimony intact, ready for its next victim. Serin told the secretary she would submit every document through the same public register, even those that embarrassed her office. When he called her stubborn, she answered that an orderly state did not mean obedient silence. It meant people could check how a decision was made. She also sent the widower a copy of her receipt, so he would know precisely what she had taken and when he could demand it back. Her inquiry now had a witness who could challenge her as well as support her.

回到城裡，法庭祕書提議，只要瑟琳在報告中略去艾妲的名字，就可以結束對塔維的案件。這建議很有吸引力，因為年輕抄寫員或許能立刻回家。但安靜的妥協會讓製造偽證的機器原封不動，準備迎接下一名受害者。瑟琳告訴祕書，她會透過同一份公開登記簿提交每一張文件，包括令自己部門難堪的資料。對方稱她頑固，她回答說，有秩序的國家不等於所有人都服從沉默，而是人們可以檢查決策過程。她也寄收據副本給鰥夫，讓他知道自己取走了什麼、何時能要求歸還。她的調查現在多了一名既能支持也能質疑她的見證人。
