沒有判決的聽證

A Hearing Without a Verdict

王庭第七份證詞 · 第 5 / 25 章 · 5000–6000 · 英中對照

Five days after the archive fire, the Crown Academy offered its large hall for a preliminary hearing. The royal court was too crowded, and Regent Aven wanted the accusation against Tavi examined in public. Outside the academy, workers from the offshore fishery unloaded nets and sacks of fertilizer destined for experimental gardens. Inside, an apprentice technician tested an acoustic recorder fitted with headphones, so even people near the rear doors could hear testimony. Serin disliked the ceremony of it all. A banquet had been prepared for visiting officials, and the polished floor seemed designed to hide the urgency of a young man's detention. She asked the presiding judge to move the meal until after the proceedings. He looked startled, but the Regent approved. For once, the witnesses would not be interrupted by plates and congratulations.

檔案庫火災五天後，王家學院提供大廳舉行初步聽證。王室法庭過於擁擠，攝政王艾雯希望塔維所受的指控能公開審查。學院外，離岸漁場的工人卸下漁網和運往實驗花園的肥料袋；學院內，一名技術學徒測試裝有耳機的收音記錄器，讓靠後門的人也能聽清證詞。瑟琳不喜歡這些儀式。來訪官員有一場宴會，光亮地板彷彿只是要遮掩年輕人遭拘留的迫切處境。她要求主持法官把用餐延到審理結束，對方雖然吃驚，攝政王仍然批准。至少這一次，證人的話不會被餐盤和祝賀聲打斷。

Serin presented the burial register, the infirmary schedule, and the orphanage notes as three different forms of evidence. A palace advocate tried to harness the public fear of fire, warning that disorder could fracture the kingdom. Serin answered that fear did not repair a fracture in the record. The King's Code required a material finding and two independent sources before a conviction. It did not authorize a court to replace a missing source with a loud accusation. The academy's physician described how the clinic's intake notes were written before noon, with time marks that neither he nor the nurses could alter later. A prospective court examiner challenged the physician about an uncertain minute. He acknowledged the possible error. That candor strengthened, rather than weakened, his evidence because it showed the limits of what he claimed to know.

瑟琳提出安葬登記、醫務室時程和育幼院筆記，作為三種不同的證據。王宮代理人試圖利用大眾對火災的恐懼，警告無秩序會使王國分裂。瑟琳回答，恐懼無法修補紀錄上的裂縫。《王法》要求物證和兩個獨立來源才能定罪，不能用高聲指控填補缺失的來源。學院醫師說明，診所的接收紀錄在中午前就已寫下，時間標記不能由他或護理師事後隨意改變。一名準備取得資格的法庭檢驗員質疑其中一分鐘的誤差，醫師坦承可能存在。這份坦白反而強化他的證詞，因為他清楚說出自己能確認的範圍。

At the rear of the hall, a quiet woman in a plain maiden-style collar waited to speak. She called the style a maiden collar, though she was fully grown. She was a tenant in the building where the courier had been seen. The landowner had threatened to evict her if she described the courier's coat. Serin asked the judge for a protective order before putting her name into the open register. The minister's adviser objected, delivering a stern sermon about obedience. Tavi's mother clenched her hands, and Serin heard her resent the delay. Yet rushing the woman into public view might put her in danger. The judge granted a provisional order and took her account privately under independent supervision. She described the same unfamiliar badge that the hill vendor had noticed. Two strangers had seen it in different places. That did not identify the messenger, but it narrowed the search.

大廳後方，一名穿著簡樸高領衣服的女子等待發言。她稱那種樣式為「少女領」，雖然自己早已成年。她租住在有人目擊信差的那棟樓裡。房東威脅她，若描述信差外套，就要將她趕走。瑟琳在把她姓名登錄到公開卷宗前，先向法官申請保護令。大臣顧問反對，發表一篇嚴厲的服從說教。塔維的母親握緊雙手，瑟琳聽出她對延誤的不滿。但貿然把女子暴露於公眾面前可能造成危險。法官核准臨時保護令，讓她在獨立監督下秘密陳述。她描述的陌生徽章與山區攤販所見相同。兩位陌生人在不同地點見過它，這不能直接指認信差，卻縮小了搜尋範圍。

Before the final recess, Serin introduced the case for a referendum on the court's new testimony procedure. She did not ask the crowd to vote on Tavi's guilt; guilt was a legal question, not a popularity contest. She asked whether representatives of the public should be allowed to audit how statements were stored. Regent Aven said the question belonged to parliament and could be considered later. The judge then ruled that the seven statements could not support a conviction without independent review. Tavi would remain under supervised release instead of prison detention. His mother began to rejoice, then stopped herself when she realized the charges had not been dismissed. Serin shared the caution. It was a real achievement, but not a final victory, and the person who manufactured the dead woman's testimony remained hidden.

最後休庭前，瑟琳提出就法庭新證詞管理程序舉辦公民表決的構想。她不是要群眾投票決定塔維有沒有罪；罪責是法律問題，不是人氣競賽。她問的是，社會代表能否參與稽核證詞的保管方式。攝政王艾雯表示，這項問題屬於議會權限，可以稍後討論。法官隨後裁定，七份證詞未經獨立審查，不能支持定罪。塔維獲准在監督下釋放，不必繼續關在牢裡。他的母親正要歡呼，卻想起指控尚未撤銷，硬生生停住。瑟琳也同樣謹慎。這是確實的進展，卻不是最終勝利；製造死者證詞的人仍躲在暗處。

After the hearing, Serin walked alone through the academy garden. An instructor showed students how to refine a sample of soot gathered from the fire, explaining that ash from lamp oil differed from ash from dry paper. The lesson was serene and practical, unlike the shouting in the hall. Serin asked whether such tests could be repeated by another laboratory. “They must be,” the instructor replied, “or they are only demonstrations.” She hired the academy to preserve the remaining samples under separate seals. Jori came to find her with news that several witnesses had requested appointments to correct their accounts. He wanted to shuffle the schedule so the most important one spoke first. Serin refused to decide importance before hearing them. Each witness would be heard on an equal basis. The case had survived its first hearing, but its deepest questions were only beginning.

聽證結束後，瑟琳獨自走過學院花園。一名講師正教學生精製從火場收集的煙灰樣本，說明燈油灰燼與乾紙灰燼有何不同。課程平靜而務實，與大廳的吵鬧截然不同。瑟琳問另一間實驗室能否重複檢驗。講師回答：「必須可以，否則只是示範。」她委託學院以不同封印保存剩餘樣本。喬里帶來消息：幾名證人已請求預約更正供詞。他想調整順序，讓最重要的人先說。瑟琳拒絕在聽完之前決定誰比較重要。每名證人都應得到同等機會。案件已度過第一次聽證，但最深層的問題才正要開始。
