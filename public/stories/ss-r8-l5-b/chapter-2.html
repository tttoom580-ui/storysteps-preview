記得承諾的道路

A Road That Remembers

The northern route would be beneficial to the farms only if its water gates remained public. The government wanted to convert the abandoned watchtowers into corporate storehouses and described the change as a gift to local trade. Yin studied the document while Xu drove the cart toward their destination, a ridge above the marsh. A fever epidemic had once isolated the settlements below, and Master Rui had walked this road to deliver medicines without asking for payment. On an ordinary errand, he had discovered the extraordinary ability of the witness lenses: they could show where a promise was made, but not whether its maker would keep it. As Xu explained this, they passed wagons trying to haul timber through mud. A foreman warned that anyone questioning the tower sale would accept liability for future floods. Yin replied that danger did not make an incomplete contract valid. They stopped beside an old stone marker whose inscription was hidden under fresh paint.

北行道路只有在水閘仍屬公用時，才對農田有益。政府打算把廢棄守望塔改成企業倉庫，稱這項變更能促進地方貿易。殷洛讀著文件，徐岸駕車前往沼澤上方的山脊。昔日一場熱病曾隔離山下村落，芮師傅便沿路無償送藥。他在一次普通跑腿途中發現見證鏡的特殊能力：鏡子能顯示承諾發生的地點，卻無法判斷發誓者是否會履行。徐岸說話時，他們經過陷在泥裡、努力拖運木材的車隊。工頭警告，質疑守望塔出售的人將承擔日後洪災責任。殷洛回答，危險並不會使一份不完整的合約自動有效。他們在古老石碑旁停下，碑文被新漆遮住。

Rain began to plunge down the slope. A reminder bell sounded from the village, where witnesses to the original road agreement had been invited to speak. The first respondent was an old baker with hands that still smelled of flour. She remembered Master Rui standing on this ridge beside her father, who had installed a metal sensor to detect shifts in the embankment. While she offered the travelers warm vegetable stew, she warned them about the temptation to believe any official mark simply because it looked ancient. A young guard carried a torch into the tower and returned with a strip of silver film. The original number had been carefully trimmed away. Yin was unusually vocal about the missing cut: the edge matched the transfer records he had refused to sign three years earlier. Xu had thought the journey would reveal a single dishonest seller. Instead, each witness seemed to point toward a longer transformation of public promises into private possessions.

大雨猛然沿坡而下。村裡的提醒鐘響了，受邀作證的居民陸續聚集。第一位應詢者是雙手仍有麵粉氣味的老麵包師。她記得芮師傅曾和她父親站在這道山脊上；父親當年裝過偵測堤岸位移的金屬感測器。她端出熱蔬菜燉湯，提醒旅人不要因為官印看似古老，就相信它一定正確。一名年輕衛兵舉著火把進塔，帶回一條銀色薄片，原本的編號被小心裁掉了。殷洛罕見地提高聲音指出，切口與三年前他拒簽的移轉紀錄一模一樣。徐岸原以為旅程只會揭露一個不誠實的賣家；如今每名見證人都指向更長久的變化：公共承諾正逐步被轉成私人財產。

The baker had kept another record, a charcoal drawing made when the first stone was placed. It was imperfect: her father's hand had shaken, and the drawing could not reveal whose intention had changed years later. But it showed the original path leading to a community well. Xu set the drawing beside his astrolabe, and the needle stopped moving for the first time since Lantern Square. He understood that the instrument responded to an old silver thread, not to hidden thoughts. This limitation mattered. If the astrolabe were taken into a crowded hall, it could point toward dozens of objects and accuse no one. Yin insisted on keeping the original with the baker and accepting only a signed copy. For a moment Xu disliked his caution. Every hour away from Master Rui felt like a delay. Then he remembered the girl from the planting guild. Her whole neighborhood might depend on whether this one road stayed open, and reckless haste could destroy the evidence she needed.

麵包師還留著另一份紀錄：第一塊石碑落成時畫的木炭草圖。圖並不完美，父親當年手會顫抖，而且圖也無法顯示多年後誰改變了心意；但它畫出通往公共水井的原路線。徐岸把草圖放在星盤旁，指針第一次停止晃動。他明白星盤感應的是舊銀線，而不是人的秘密思想。這項限制很重要：若把星盤帶進人多的廳堂，它可能指向數十件物品，卻不能定任何人的罪。殷洛堅持原件由麵包師保存，兩人只收有簽名的副本。徐岸一時不喜歡他的謹慎；離師父越遠，每小時都像在拖延。但他想起栽種公會的女孩，她整個街區可能都仰賴這條道路保持通行，魯莽反而可能毀掉她需要的證據。

At dusk the travelers climbed to the upper marker, where the road disappeared beneath grass. There they found a second sensor, disconnected but intact, with a small groove shaped like a star. The ridge overlooked three villages and a water channel too narrow for heavy wagons. Yin sketched the view, then gave Xu a copy of the tower's lease. The words permitted repair but did not authorize a sale. Someone had treated one permission as another. Xu wanted to climb farther, even though the path was slick. Yin offered a rope and waited until Xu had tied it properly. Their hands met briefly above the knot, and neither pretended not to notice. The modest kindness unsettled Xu more than the thunder. When they reached the watchtower, a metal shutter displayed a constellation with one line missing. The missing line pointed east toward a river port, and beneath it someone had written a name: Rui, followed by the words, "Do not come alone."

黃昏時兩人爬上較高的石碑，舊路已被草覆蓋。他們找到第二個感測器，雖然斷線卻完好，表面有一條星形淺槽。山脊俯瞰三座村莊與一條不容大車通過的水渠。殷洛畫下景色，交給徐岸一份守望塔租約。條文只允許修繕，並未授權出售；有人把一種許可當成另一種。徐岸仍想往上爬，即使路面濕滑。殷洛遞來繩子，等他綁緊才動身。兩人的手在繩結上短暫碰觸，誰都沒有假裝沒注意。這份小小的善意讓徐岸比雷聲更不安。抵達塔頂時，金屬窗板上刻著缺了一線的星座，斷線指向東方河港。旁邊有人寫著：「芮——不要獨自前來。」
