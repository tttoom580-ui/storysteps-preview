一小時的招牌

The One-Hour Sign

1996，晨光照相館的第二張底片 · 第 1 / 25 章 · 3000–4000 · 英中對照

On the first Monday of June 1996, Zhou Meicen returned to Spring Harbor with one suitcase and a thin cushion for her father's aching chair. Across the narrow street from Morning Light Studio, a chain store had raised a fierce orange sign: PHOTOS IN ONE HOUR. Her parents' cottage was behind the studio, so there was no way to avoid seeing the sign at breakfast. Meicen had spent six years arranging museum exhibitions in Taipei. She had come home for a short visit, but the empty appointment book suggested a problem much larger than a quiet summer.

一九九六年六月第一個星期一，周美岑帶著一只行李箱和一塊給父親舊椅子的薄坐墊回到春港。晨光照相館對街，連鎖店掛起鮮橘色大招牌：「一小時取件」。父母的小屋就在店後，連早餐時也躲不開那面招牌。美岑在臺北策劃博物館展覽已六年，本來只打算短暫返鄉；然而空白的預約簿顯示，這裡遇到的困難遠不止一個冷清夏天。

Her father, Zhou Shulin, tried to flatter her by calling her a genius with money. Meicen knew that was untrue: a genius would not have forgotten how long their film dryer needed to warm up. Her mother, Yuzhen, gave a tired giggle, then pointed toward a torn picture of a parachute team above the counter. The club had once ordered sixty prints. Now its members developed photographs at the chain shop after their morning workout. "We still possess their negatives," Shulin said. "That does not mean we possess their loyalty," Meicen answered.

父親周樹林想討她歡心，稱她是理財天才。美岑知道這不是真的：真正的天才不會連家裡烘片機預熱多久都忘記。母親玉珍疲倦地笑了兩聲，指向櫃檯上方一張破損的跳傘隊照片。那個社團以前一次訂六十張照片，如今隊員晨練後都去連鎖店沖印。「我們還保有他們的底片。」樹林說。「但不表示我們也擁有他們的忠誠。」美岑回答。

At noon, her older brother Mingxuan arrived with a visual chart and a serious presentation. He had calculated a tremendous loss over the previous eight months. His defense of selling the property was simple: the building was old, their parents needed rest, and his construction salary could not support two households. Meicen was pessimistic about saving the old business by nostalgia alone, but she noticed something missing from his chart. The appointment book listed ordinary portraits as well as restored wedding photographs, school records, and carefully copied family letters. Their customers had come for more than speed.

中午，哥哥明軒帶來圖表與一份嚴肅的簡報。過去八個月的虧損十分驚人。他主張賣掉房產，理由很直接：房子老舊、父母需要休息，而他的工程師薪水無法負擔兩個家庭。美岑也不樂觀，認為只靠懷舊救不了老店，但她發現圖表漏了一件事。預約簿不只記著一般人像照，還有婚照修復、學校紀錄以及仔細翻拍的家書。顧客來此並非只求速度。

That afternoon a retired teacher brought in a faded class photograph. She asked the studio to convey the names of every child to a reunion committee, but several pupils had become adults who wanted privacy. Meicen explained that a reference copy could stay in the teacher's own album without becoming a public advertisement. "A photograph is a witness, not permission," she said. The teacher accepted the distinction. At the back door, a neighbor muttered that such careful rules would be the death of the shop. Meicen wrote the complaint down anyway, beside the price of each service.

下午，一位退休教師拿來褪色班級照，希望照相館把所有學生名字交給同學會。但那些孩子早已成年，其中有人不想公開個資。美岑解釋，可以製作供教師私人相簿保存的參考影本，卻不能因此拿來做公開廣告。「照片是見證，不是許可。」她說。教師接受了這個分別。後門鄰居嘟囔說，規矩這麼多，店遲早會倒。美岑仍把抱怨記下，寫在各項服務成本旁邊。

In the evening, Meicen met a young nurse planning a spiritual retreat for exhausted hospital staff. She wanted portraits that would show the diversity of their lives, not identical smiling faces. The nurse had heard rumors that Morning Light kept old customer photographs without asking. Meicen promised to check the shelves before accepting any job. She found only one clear violation: a sample wedding picture displayed in the window after its owner had asked for its removal. She took it down immediately. Her parents watched in silence, surprised that the first thing she repaired was not a machine.

傍晚，一名年輕護理師來談替疲憊醫護舉辦的心靈休養活動。她希望拍出每個人不同的生活面貌，而不是整排相同笑容。她聽說晨光照相館未經同意保存客人的照片。美岑答應先清查底片櫃，再接任何新工作。她立刻發現一件明確違規的事：一張婚紗示範照，照片主人早已要求撤下，卻還擺在櫥窗。美岑當場取下。父母沉默地看著，沒想到她修好的第一件東西不是機器。

After dinner, the family sat beneath the studio's old ceiling fan. Shulin wanted a quick sale because he feared another loan. Mingxuan wanted his parents to stop working nights. Yuzhen wanted to teach the neighborhood children how to keep albums, even if the studio closed. Their wishes did not fit into a single plan. Meicen proposed thirty days, not a promise of rescue: she would compare costs, interview former clients, and test two new services. In return, nobody would sign a property agreement during the trial without showing it to the others.

晚飯後，一家人在照相館老吊扇下坐定。樹林急著賣店，因為他害怕再借錢；明軒希望父母不再熬夜工作；玉珍想教附近孩子整理相簿，就算照相館關閉也無妨。這些願望不可能塞進同一張計畫表。美岑提議先試三十天，不保證救店：她會比較成本、訪問舊客戶，並測試兩項新服務。交換條件是，試辦期間誰也不能不經家人查看，就簽下房產協議。

Her brother asked whether the experiment would include any income, or merely attractive words. Meicen admitted that the answer was uncertain. She would use the shop's own receipts as evidence and invite an outside bookkeeper to review them. Before leaving, Yuzhen placed the retired teacher's photograph on the table. Under its scratched surface, a row of children looked out with solemn faces. "Keep their stories safe," she said, "but let our family have a future too." Meicen switched off the display light. The orange sign across the street continued to shine. It would be there tomorrow, and so would her first difficult decision.

哥哥問，這場試驗到底會帶來收入，還是只有漂亮話。美岑承認目前無法確定。她打算用本店收據作證據，再請外部記帳人查核。離開前，玉珍把退休教師的舊班級照放到桌上。刮痕下，一排孩子神情莊重地望著鏡頭。「妳要守住他們的故事，」她說，「也要讓我們家有未來。」美岑關掉展示燈。對街的橘色招牌依然亮著。明天它還會在，而她的第一個艱難抉擇也一樣。
