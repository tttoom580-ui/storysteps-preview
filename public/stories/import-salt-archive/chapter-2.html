高地的否決權

The Veto on the Hill

灰潮檔案 · 第 2 / 25 章 · 5000–6000 · 英中對照

用互動版查詞、朗讀與收藏

The basement tunnel climbed instead of descending, an architectural trick from the days when people built shelters above unpredictable water. Lena followed Jory through a narrow crossing beneath the old railway. The altitude increased so quickly that their ears began to ache. At its end stood a locked gate, watched by a widow named Sera who repaired respirators for the hill settlements. Her husband had died during the last uprising, and she did not welcome strangers carrying government keys. "Those keys open doors," she said in a blunt voice. "They don't make you trustworthy." Lena explained that the flooded districts had been erased from official records. Sera's posture changed when she saw her grandmother's name among the supposed dead. "She was an aboriginal healer from the upper valley," Sera said. "She died in bed, years after the flood. I was there." The lie had reached further than Lena imagined.

地下通道竟一路往上爬升，而非向下深入。那是過去為躲避難測洪水而把避難所建在高處的建築巧思。莉娜跟著喬里穿過舊鐵路下方一條狹窄的橫向通道。海拔上升得太快，兩人的耳朵開始隱隱作痛。通道盡頭有扇上鎖的大門，由名叫瑟拉的寡婦看守。她替山坡聚落修理呼吸器。丈夫死於上次起義，因此她一點也不歡迎拿著政府鑰匙的陌生人。「鑰匙能開門，」她直截了當地說，「但不能證明你可信。」莉娜解釋，淹水地區的居民曾被人從官方紀錄中抹去。當瑟拉看到祖母的名字也列在死者之中，身姿瞬間變了。「她是上游山谷的原住民療癒者，」瑟拉說，「洪災幾年後，她是在床上過世的。我在場。」這場謊言延伸的範圍，遠超莉娜想像。

Beyond the gate, terraces carved into the mountain held vegetable gardens and rain barrels. Medicinal plants abound here, Sera told them, because families refused to discard the seeds their elders had saved. The hill people had built a versatile network of channels that could catch rain, carry waste, or discharge excess water in a storm. It required precision and a rigorous schedule of repairs; one careless worker could poison an entire terrace. Lena watched a young engineer align a cracked pipe with its replacement while children carried tools between houses. She realized how much practical knowledge the council had dismissed as primitive. "You came to ask for protection," Sera said. "But protection from what?" Lena produced the photograph. Sera studied it without touching it. "From the people who manufacture a version of history," Lena replied, "and then punish anyone who remembers another."

穿過大門，沿山鑿出的梯田種滿蔬菜，四周擺著雨水桶。瑟拉說，這裡藥草繁多，是因為家家戶戶都不肯丟掉長輩珍藏的種子。山居民建起功能多樣的水道網，既能收集雨水、輸送廢水，也能在暴雨時排出多餘積水。整套系統需要精準施工與嚴格的維修時程；一名工人的疏忽就可能污染整片梯田。莉娜看著年輕工程師把裂開的管子對準替換零件，孩子們則在屋舍之間搬運工具。她這才意識到，議會曾把多少實用知識貶為「原始」。「妳來這裡求保護，」瑟拉說，「但妳在躲什麼？」莉娜拿出照片。瑟拉凝視著，沒有伸手碰它。「躲那些製造官方歷史的人，」莉娜回答，「以及他們對記得另一個版本的人所施加的懲罰。」

They were interrupted by three council vehicles at the hillside barrier. A young officer demanded passage under a new emergency directive. Sera stepped forward and pointed to a notice posted beside the gate. "The hill assembly has the right to veto the use of our roads by armed units," she said. The officer's annoyance was visible; he had expected fear rather than legal argument. "An endeavor to obstruct a lawful search has consequences," he warned. Jory whispered that the officer had brought enough fuel to burn the gate, but Sera did not retreat. She asked Lena whether the warrant was valid. Lena examined its seal and found the number had been issued to a hospital, not a court. "It isn't a warrant," she said. "It's a request disguised as an order." That discovery did not make the guns disappear, but it gave the people behind the gate something precise to resist.

三輛議會車輛在山坡關卡外停下，打斷了他們的談話。一名年輕軍官聲稱依最新緊急命令，有權通行。瑟拉走上前，指向門邊的公告。「山區議會有權否決武裝部隊使用我們的道路。」她說。軍官臉上的不悅清晰可見；他原本以為迎接他的會是恐懼，不是法律辯論。「企圖阻礙合法搜索，後果自負。」他警告。喬里悄聲說，那軍官帶的燃料足夠燒掉整扇門，但瑟拉沒有退。她問莉娜拘票是否有效。莉娜仔細查看印章，發現公文編號屬於醫院而非法院。「這不是拘票，」她說，「只是一份假冒命令的請求。」這項發現沒有讓槍枝消失，卻給了門後的人一個明確的反抗依據。

The officer tried a different approach. He offered to usher the refugees into the city's protected shelters if Sera would surrender Lena. Several families hesitated. Food stores were low, and old divisions between hill and port still shaped their loyalties. Lena could not pretend that one document would overcome every hurdle. She asked to speak without a weapon between them, then walked through the inner gate alone. "I'm not asking you to risk your children for a stranger," she said. "I'm asking you to inspect the dates on your own family papers." One father carried out a worn birth certificate. His daughter's birthday had been shifted by four years; on paper she was old enough for compulsory labor. Another woman checked her mother's death record and cried out. Within minutes, more families gathered, bringing evidence from drawers and boxes. The official version of the flood began to break under the weight of ordinary memory.

軍官換了一種手段。他表示，只要瑟拉交出莉娜，就能護送難民進入城內受保護的避難所。有幾戶人家動搖了。糧食不足，而山區與港口之間舊有的隔閡仍左右著彼此的信任。莉娜知道，一紙文件不可能掃除所有阻礙。她請求雙方放下武器說話，接著獨自走過內側柵門。「我不是要你們為一個陌生人賭上孩子的命，」她說，「我只要你們檢查自家的戶籍日期。」一位父親拿出磨損的出生證明，女兒的生日竟被往前改了四年，讓她在文件上達到強制勞動年齡。另一位婦女翻查母親的死亡紀錄，忍不住失聲哭喊。短短幾分鐘，越來越多人拿著抽屜和盒子裡的證據聚攏。官方版本的洪災，開始在尋常人的記憶重壓下碎裂。

The officer lowered his rifle at last. "My own father crossed the eastern line," he admitted. "The register says he never arrived." His confession did not excuse the men who had followed him, but it altered the crossing. Sera allowed the vehicles to turn around without violence on one condition: their drivers must return the next day without weapons and testify before the hill assembly. Lena admired the compromise, though she noticed Sera's hands were shaking. When the engines had gone, Jory examined a metal container left beneath a wheel. It held a map showing another sealed archive, far below the reservoir, and a phrase written in her mother's hand: The names are only the beginning. Lena felt the first true fear of the journey. Her mother had not hidden one fact. She had hidden the entrance to a system powerful enough to make whole communities disappear.

軍官終於放下步槍。「我父親也越過了東部界線，」他承認，「但登記簿說他從未抵達。」他的坦白不能替跟隨他的士兵開脫，卻改變了關卡的局勢。瑟拉同意讓車隊和平撤回，條件是司機們隔天必須不帶武器返來，在山區議會面前作證。莉娜佩服這項折衷，卻注意到瑟拉的雙手正在發抖。引擎聲遠去後，喬里在車輪下找到一個遭人留下的金屬盒。盒內是一張地圖，畫著蓄水庫深處另一座封閉的檔案室；其上還有母親親筆留下的一句話：「姓名只是開始。」莉娜感受到這趟旅程真正的第一股恐懼。母親隱藏的不是一項事實，而是一套足以讓整個社群憑空消失的體系入口。
