第九街區，不接受單人通關 · 第 4 / 25 章 · 3000–4000 · 英中對照

In the former bank, a card drawer scraped against its swollen wooden frame. Tang Yue held the drawer steady while the librarian freed it, and a row of paper requests appeared where the search machine had shown an empty category.

舊銀行裡，一格卡片抽屜刮著受潮木框。唐悅扶穩，館員把它鬆開，露出整排紙申請；搜尋機先前卻把這類顯示為空。

To accomplish the task, they needed written approval before touching the records. A children's game in the entrance was meant to amuse visitors, but a ball continued to bounce against the shelves, producing a continual disturbance.

要完成任務，他們得先取得書面核准才能接觸紀錄。入口的兒童遊戲原本為了逗樂訪客，球卻不停彈向書架，形成持續干擾。

The machine labeled every unreadable card as a failed citizen request.

機器把所有難以辨識的卡都判定成失敗的市民申請。

The librarian demonstrated a paper index that the machine never scanned. The earnest librarian tried not to disappoint anyone, yet she emphasized that even a visiting embassy representative must follow the same record policy. Tang Yue repeated the emphasis in a clear request rather than asking for special treatment.

圖書館員展示一份機器從未掃描的紙本索引。認真的圖書館員不願使人失望，仍強調連來訪的大使館代表也得遵守相同的紀錄政策。唐悅在申請中再次強調公平規則，沒有要求特別待遇。

Tang Yue wanted to preserve the catalog; Fang insisted that people needed an immediate answer. The librarian asked, "Do you want a fast answer or the right request?" Tang Yue replied, "Both, if possible. Evidence first, if not."

唐悅想先保存目錄，方澤則主張人們急需答覆。圖書館員問：「你們要快答案，還是正確申請？」唐悅答：「能兼顧最好；不能的話，證據優先。」

A mystery novel about a murderer lay in the margin of an exhibit, beside a model planet in orbit. Fang called the dramatic cover nonsense as evidence; it showed a story, not actual possession of the missing records, and passive speculation could not solve their problem.

一部寫殺人犯的推理小說放在展覽角落，旁邊是繞行軌道的行星模型。方澤說拿戲劇性封面當證據簡直胡說；那只是故事，不代表誰持有遺失紀錄，消極猜測解決不了問題。

Fang wanted to search every card at once. The librarian showed him how easily their order could be lost: a tiny reference number linked each request to a second drawer. She gave the five a tray with numbered places and allowed only one card out at a time. Mei moved the children's ball game away from the shelves with the attendant's permission. Yu placed a chair beside the tray so the older librarian could check the references without standing throughout the search. Duan copied the layout before they began. The first promising card concerned a street with the same name in another district. Fang nearly wrote down its answer, then noticed the different boundary mark. They returned it to its place and followed the second reference. That card led to the public application filed before the digital index existed. Its paper edges were worn, but the filing number matched the librarian's register. Tang requested a correction that linked the paper source to the digital category instead of declaring the machine useless. The librarian approved a scan with the original position recorded. When the second seal was issued, Fang asked to keep the wrong district reference in their notes as well. It showed why their first quick answer would have sent them to the wrong neighborhood, and why the restored request deserved a closer look.

方澤想一次翻遍，館員示範順序多容易散：每張卡的小索引號連著另一抽屜。她給五人有編號位置的托盤，一次只准取一張。梅嵐經管理員同意移開兒童球戲，俞芊在盤旁放椅，讓年長館員不用全程站著核索引，段芽先畫布局。第一張看似有望，卻屬另一街區的同名街。方澤差點抄下答案，才注意到不同界標。他們放回，追第二索引，找到數位目錄出現前已歸檔的公共申請。紙緣雖磨，號碼與館員簿吻合。唐悅申請把紙本來源連到數位分類，沒有宣稱機器全無用。館員准掃描，並記原位。第二枚章核發時，方澤也保留錯街區索引；它說明第一個快答案為何會把人帶錯社區，以及恢復的申請為何值得細查。

The correction linked the restored application to both paper cross-references, allowing the librarian to trace its place.

更正把恢復的申請連到兩份紙本交叉索引，館員能追它的位置。

A lost request was restored to the stage and the team gained its second seal. A visiting researcher helped a translator read a rhyme written in the old catalog code. Its urgent message was simple: the public application had been filed on paper long before the digital index existed.

遺失的申請恢復到關卡資料，小隊拿到第二枚印章。來訪研究員協助翻譯員讀懂舊目錄代碼裡的一段押韻文。當中急迫的訊息很簡單：那份公共申請早在數位索引存在前，就已寫在紙上歸檔。

The restored request belonged to a neighborhood marked for future closure.

這份申請來自一個被標註未來要關閉的社區。
