The Library of Requests

第九街區，不接受單人通關 · 第 4 / 25 章 · 3000–4000 · 英中對照

The stage lights came up in a silent library staged inside a former bank just as Tang Yue brought her four teammates to the entrance. Their assignment was to locate a public request hidden among thousands of catalog cards. The printed handbook gave them an ordinary responsibility instead: all five names had to appear on the same exit sheet before a stage could count as complete. She read the rule aloud, so no one could later pretend it had been hidden. To accomplish the task, they needed written approval before touching the records. A children's game in the entrance was meant to amuse visitors, but a ball continued to bounce against the shelves, producing a continual disturbance.

唐悅帶著四名隊友走進設在舊銀行內的安靜圖書館時，舞臺燈正好亮起。他們的任務是從數千張目錄卡中找出一份公開申請。印製的手冊反而給了他們平凡的責任：同一關的出口表必須有五個人的名字，才能算完成。唐悅大聲唸出規則，免得日後有人說自己沒看到。要完成任務，他們得先取得書面核准才能接觸紀錄。入口的兒童遊戲原本為了逗樂訪客，球卻不停彈向書架，形成持續干擾。

Beyond the painted street, the instruction board made a dangerous assumption: the machine labels every unreadable card as a failed citizen request. Mei tested the setting with a carpenter’s eye, while Fang used his pencil to separate what they saw from what the organizer merely promised. Duan Ya, an architecture student who preferred written and visual instructions, pointed out that the brightest arrow was not always the most understandable one. Tang Yue asked everyone for a description before allowing anyone to choose a route.

彩繪街道的另一端，指示板藏著危險假設：機器把所有難以辨識的卡都判定成失敗的市民申請。梅嵐以木工的眼光測試場景，方澤則用鉛筆把親眼所見與主辦者的承諾分開。習慣書寫及視覺指示的建築學生段芽指出，最亮的箭頭未必最容易理解。唐悅在任何人選路前，先要求各自描述觀察結果。

Then the team found a detail the public screen had not explained: the librarian demonstrates a paper index that the machine never scanned. Tang Yue told Duan Ya to draw the observation at its real scale. A clue could be useful without being final proof, she reminded them. They also recorded the time, the location, and the person who had first noticed it. Their shared notebook was beginning to matter more than the colorful scoreboard above their heads. The earnest librarian tried not to disappoint anyone, yet she emphasized that even a visiting embassy representative must follow the same record policy. Tang Yue repeated the emphasis in a clear request rather than asking for special treatment.

接著，小隊發現公開螢幕沒解釋的一項細節：圖書館員展示一份機器從未掃描的紙本索引。唐悅請段芽依真實比例畫出觀察結果。她提醒大家，有用線索不等於最終證據。他們還記下時間、地點及最初發現者。頭頂絢麗的計分板越來越不如手中的共同筆記本重要。認真的圖書館員不願使人失望，仍強調連來訪的大使館代表也得遵守相同的紀錄政策。唐悅在申請中再次強調公平規則，沒有要求特別待遇。

The five stopped beside the next checkpoint rather than arguing while walking. This was the problem they had to settle: Tang Yue wants to preserve the catalog; Fang insists that people need an immediate answer. Mei cared about limited working time; Fang cared about an auditable result; Duan Ya wanted the signs to make sense even without spoken instructions. Tang Yue said none of those concerns could simply cancel the others. She asked each person to propose one way to check an assumption at low cost. The librarian asked, "Do you want a fast answer or the right request?" Tang Yue replied, "Both, if possible. Evidence first, if not."

五人停在下一個檢查點旁，沒有邊走邊吵。他們必須處理的問題是：唐悅想先保存目錄，方澤則主張人們急需答覆。梅嵐重視有限的工作時間；方澤重視可稽核的結果；段芽希望即使沒有口頭說明，標示也看得懂。唐悅說這些顧慮不能隨便互相抵消，請每人提出一種低成本核實假設的方法。圖書館員問：「你們要快答案，還是正確申請？」唐悅答：「能兼顧最好；不能的話，證據優先。」

The contest had two ways of rewarding a team. Its bright clock measured speed, but the smaller review desk accepted documented contradictions. Four review seals could activate a public hearing, as the handbook had stated at registration. Tang Yue looked back at the purpose of the stage: locate a public request hidden among thousands of catalog cards. The route would belong to the city one day; none of them had the right to substitute personal confidence for clear evidence. A mystery novel about a murderer lay in the margin of an exhibit, beside a model planet in orbit. Fang called the dramatic cover nonsense as evidence; it showed a story, not actual possession of the missing records, and passive speculation could not solve their problem.

競賽有兩套獎勵方式。耀眼的大鐘測量速度，較小的複核桌則收受附證據的矛盾申報。報名手冊早已載明，四枚複核印章可啟動公開聽證。唐悅再次想起本關目的：從數千張目錄卡中找出一份公開申請。道路有一天屬於全市，他們無權用個人自信取代清楚證據。一部寫殺人犯的推理小說放在展覽角落，旁邊是繞行軌道的行星模型。方澤說拿戲劇性封面當證據簡直胡說；那只是故事，不代表誰持有遺失紀錄，消極猜測解決不了問題。

Once the observers were ready, the team acted on its written plan: the team copies the cross-references and submits a traceable correction. Fang timed the attempt twice instead of reporting a single lucky result. Mei checked the physical route, Yu Qian asked each participant whether the pace was acceptable, and Duan Ya corrected signs that confused new visitors. Tang Yue kept the record open for comment.

觀察員就位後，小隊按書面計畫行動：小隊抄下交叉索引，提交可追溯的更正。方澤計時兩次，不只報告一次僥倖成果。梅嵐檢查實體路線，俞芊詢問每位參與者是否能接受步速，段芽更正讓新訪客困惑的標示。唐悅讓紀錄保持開放供人評論。

The measurement produced a result that neither excitement nor embarrassment could erase: a lost request is restored to the stage and the team gains its second seal. At the review desk, the clerk inspected their original notes instead of accepting a polished summary. Tang Yue could see the contest’s basic contradiction growing clearer with every stage. The organizers asked teams to solve a city problem, yet the scoreboard offered little credit for proving that a slower path was also the one people could actually use. A visiting researcher helped a translator read a rhyme written in the old catalog code. Its urgent message was simple: the public application had been filed on paper long before the digital index existed.

量測得到的結果，不會因興奮或難堪而消失：遺失的申請恢復到關卡資料，小隊拿到第二枚印章。複核桌的職員檢查原始筆記，沒有只接受漂亮摘要。唐悅越來越看清競賽的基本矛盾：主辦者要隊伍解決城市問題，計分板卻幾乎不獎勵那些證明較慢道路反而真能使用的人。來訪研究員協助翻譯員讀懂舊目錄代碼裡的一段押韻文。當中急迫的訊息很簡單：那份公共申請早在數位索引存在前，就已寫在紙上歸檔。

The stage lights dimmed, but a new question remained: the request belongs to a neighborhood marked for future closure. Duan Ya copied the exit sign in her sketchbook in case it changed later. Tang Yue carried the notes out without celebrating a rank they had not earned. Their progress was harder to display than a medal, but it could be checked, challenged, and used by someone who had never met them.

舞臺燈暗下來，新的問題卻留下了：這份申請來自一個被標註未來要關閉的社區。段芽把出口標示抄進速寫本，以防日後改變。唐悅帶著筆記走出去，沒有慶祝自己並未得到的名次。他們的進展不像獎牌那麼醒目，卻能被核對、被質疑，也能幫助從未見過他們的人。
