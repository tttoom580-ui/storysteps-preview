不存在的簽名

The Signature That Wasn't There

第九街區，不接受單人通關 · 第 2 / 25 章 · 3000–4000 · 英中對照

用互動版查詞、朗讀與收藏

The stage lights came up in a square filled with false shopfronts just as Tang Yue brought her four teammates to the entrance. Their assignment was to check a shop-delivery path before the next gate opens. The printed handbook gave them an ordinary responsibility instead: all five names had to appear on the same exit sheet before a stage could count as complete. She read the rule aloud, so no one could later pretend it had been hidden. A collector asked for an autograph on the route card, but the five refused to carve a name into public property. The square advertised convenience for everyone; hiding the inaccessible curb behind painted flowers felt like cruelty disguised as decoration.

唐悅帶著四名隊友走進佈滿假店面的廣場時，舞臺燈正好亮起。他們的任務是在下個出口開放前核對送貨動線。印製的手冊反而給了他們平凡的責任：同一關的出口表必須有五個人的名字，才能算完成。唐悅大聲唸出規則，免得日後有人說自己沒看到。收藏者要求在路線卡上簽名，五人卻拒絕在公共財產上刻字。廣場宣稱提供人人便利；用彩繪花朵遮住難以通行的路緣，實際上是把殘酷偽裝成裝飾。

Beyond the painted street, the instruction board made a dangerous assumption: a printed timetable assumes every delivery rider can jump a high curb. Mei tested the setting with a carpenter’s eye, while Fang used his pencil to separate what they saw from what the organizer merely promised. Duan Ya, an architecture student who preferred written and visual instructions, pointed out that the brightest arrow was not always the most understandable one. Tang Yue asked everyone for a description before allowing anyone to choose a route.

彩繪街道的另一端，指示板藏著危險假設：印製的時刻表假設送貨員都能跳上高路緣。梅嵐以木工的眼光測試場景，方澤則用鉛筆把親眼所見與主辦者的承諾分開。習慣書寫及視覺指示的建築學生段芽指出，最亮的箭頭未必最容易理解。唐悅在任何人選路前，先要求各自描述觀察結果。

Then the team found a detail the public screen had not explained: Duan Ya finds that an earlier route includes a ramp omitted from the digital copy. Tang Yue told Duan Ya to draw the observation at its real scale. A clue could be useful without being final proof, she reminded them. They also recorded the time, the location, and the person who had first noticed it. Their shared notebook was beginning to matter more than the colorful scoreboard above their heads. An elastic strap kept a fake explosive prop from falling onto a display. A recent immigrant who worked as a guide gave useful feedback: the initial map showed the ramp, but the updated one did not.

接著，小隊發現公開螢幕沒解釋的一項細節：段芽發現舊路線有坡道，數位版本卻刪掉了。唐悅請段芽依真實比例畫出觀察結果。她提醒大家，有用線索不等於最終證據。他們還記下時間、地點及最初發現者。頭頂絢麗的計分板越來越不如手中的共同筆記本重要。一條彈性帶固定著展示用的假爆裂物道具。擔任導覽員的新移民提出有用回饋：初版地圖畫了坡道，更新版本卻刪掉了。

The five stopped beside the next checkpoint rather than arguing while walking. This was the problem they had to settle: Fang wants to file a formal complaint, but Mei fears losing their only remaining hour. Mei cared about limited working time; Fang cared about an auditable result; Duan Ya wanted the signs to make sense even without spoken instructions. Tang Yue said none of those concerns could simply cancel the others. She asked each person to propose one way to check an assumption at low cost. "We can trade for a shortcut," Fang suggested. Duan Ya shook her head. "Someone erased a ramp. Trading will not put it back."

五人停在下一個檢查點旁，沒有邊走邊吵。他們必須處理的問題是：方澤主張正式申訴，梅嵐卻怕耗掉僅剩的一小時。梅嵐重視有限的工作時間；方澤重視可稽核的結果；段芽希望即使沒有口頭說明，標示也看得懂。唐悅說這些顧慮不能隨便互相抵消，請每人提出一種低成本核實假設的方法。「我們可以交換捷徑，」方澤提議。段芽搖頭：「有人刪掉了坡道。交易不能把坡道放回來。」

The contest had two ways of rewarding a team. Its bright clock measured speed, but the smaller review desk accepted documented contradictions. Four review seals could activate a public hearing, as the handbook had stated at registration. Tang Yue looked back at the purpose of the stage: check a shop-delivery path before the next gate opens. The route would belong to the city one day; none of them had the right to substitute personal confidence for clear evidence. A little mischief appeared on the floor: a painted paw print led to a locked office. A prevention notice said that only prominent guests enjoyed the privilege of entering, although the passage was marked public on the printed plan.

競賽有兩套獎勵方式。耀眼的大鐘測量速度，較小的複核桌則收受附證據的矛盾申報。報名手冊早已載明，四枚複核印章可啟動公開聽證。唐悅再次想起本關目的：在下個出口開放前核對送貨動線。道路有一天屬於全市，他們無權用個人自信取代清楚證據。地板上有個惡作劇般的爪印圖案，通往上鎖辦公室。預防事故的告示聲稱只有知名貴賓享有進入特權，但紙圖明明把通道標成公共空間。

Once the observers were ready, the team acted on its written plan: they photograph the paper plan, test the ramp, and request the missing sign-off. Fang timed the attempt twice instead of reporting a single lucky result. Mei checked the physical route, Yu Qian asked each participant whether the pace was acceptable, and Duan Ya corrected signs that confused new visitors. Tang Yue kept the record open for comment.

觀察員就位後，小隊按書面計畫行動：眾人拍下紙圖，實測坡道，要求補上漏掉的簽核。方澤計時兩次，不只報告一次僥倖成果。梅嵐檢查實體路線，俞芊詢問每位參與者是否能接受步速，段芽更正讓新訪客困惑的標示。唐悅讓紀錄保持開放供人評論。

The measurement produced a result that neither excitement nor embarrassment could erase: the station accepts the evidence but refuses to alter the ranking immediately. At the review desk, the clerk inspected their original notes instead of accepting a polished summary. Tang Yue could see the contest’s basic contradiction growing clearer with every stage. The organizers asked teams to solve a city problem, yet the scoreboard offered little credit for proving that a slower path was also the one people could actually use. A shopkeeper remarked that businesses could not prosper if every delivery shift ended in a detour. Fang replied that a broken timetable could ruin a business too, and wrote down the actual times instead of guessing.

量測得到的結果，不會因興奮或難堪而消失：關卡收下證據，卻拒絕立即修正排名。複核桌的職員檢查原始筆記，沒有只接受漂亮摘要。唐悅越來越看清競賽的基本矛盾：主辦者要隊伍解決城市問題，計分板卻幾乎不獎勵那些證明較慢道路反而真能使用的人。店主評論說，每一班送貨都繞路，商家便難以興盛。方澤回答，錯誤的時刻表同樣會毀掉生意，於是記下每班實際時間，不再猜測。

The stage lights dimmed, but a new question remained: a rival team offers to trade a signature for a shortcut, raising an ethical question. Duan Ya copied the exit sign in her sketchbook in case it changed later. Tang Yue carried the notes out without celebrating a rank they had not earned. Their progress was harder to display than a medal, but it could be checked, challenged, and used by someone who had never met them.

舞臺燈暗下來，新的問題卻留下了：競爭隊伍提議用簽名交換捷徑，引出新的倫理問題。段芽把出口標示抄進速寫本，以防日後改變。唐悅帶著筆記走出去，沒有慶祝自己並未得到的名次。他們的進展不像獎牌那麼醒目，卻能被核對、被質疑，也能幫助從未見過他們的人。
