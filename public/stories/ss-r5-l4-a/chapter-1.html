Five Names on One Gate

第九街區，不接受單人通關 · 第 1 / 25 章 · 3000–4000 · 英中對照

The city replica brightened around the old exhibition hall beside Ward Nine, and Tang Yue counted the familiar faces before looking at the puzzle. The assignment asked them to register five people together and choose a route through a simulated block. Their five-person entry was legal only while they kept one common log, and the exit required every member to sign. The rule was simple enough to understand; what interested Tang Yue was how easily people might forget it under pressure. Outside the assembly hall, someone had used acid to strip old glue from a notice and left a broom across the walkway. Mei said they should construct a clear entrance, and Tang Yue asked the staff to cooperate rather than blame one another.

城市模型在第九街區旁的舊展覽館四周亮起，唐悅先點清熟悉的隊友才去看謎題。這一關要求他們五人共同報名，選出穿越模擬街區的路線。五人必須共用一本紀錄簿，出口也得由每名成員簽字；規則並不難懂，唐悅在意的是，人們在壓力下多容易忘記它。集會廳外，有人用酸液除去公告上的舊膠，卻把掃帚橫放在人行道上。梅嵐說應先建出暢通入口；唐悅請工作人員合作，而不是互相指責。

The first difficulty was not a locked door but a claim that looked reasonable at first glance: the fastest-looking street ends at a gate built for one person. Fang Ze, the team’s auditor, copied the exact wording rather than changing it to suit their theory. Mei Lan, who built stage scenery for a living, examined the actual surface. Yu Qian, a nursing assistant, waited beside the slowest walker and checked whether there was room to turn.

第一個困難不是上鎖的門，而是一項乍看合理的說法：看似最快的街道，盡頭卻是只容一人通行的門。擔任稽核員的方澤逐字抄下原文，沒有為配合自己的理論擅改內容。以製作舞臺布景為業的梅嵐檢查實際地面；護理助理俞芊陪著走得最慢的人，確認有沒有轉身空間。

Near the next marker, something did not match the official design: a maintenance drawing shows a second gate with five signature boxes. Fang noted the difference in the left column of the evidence sheet, leaving the right column blank for an independent check. Mei wanted to touch the equipment immediately, but Tang Yue asked her to wait for permission. A useful discovery could become useless if the team damaged the very object it intended to measure. The copper ticket counter carried a cartoon of a coward who fled every challenge. A guide tried to entertain the visitors with it, but Tang Yue watched his serious facial expression when he noticed the narrow door.

下一個記號附近，有件事和官方設計不相符：維修圖上畫著第二道門，旁邊有五格簽名欄。方澤把差異寫在證據表左欄，右欄留待獨立複核。梅嵐想立刻碰設備，唐悅請她先等許可。有用的發現若在測量前就把物件弄壞，也會失去效力。銅製售票櫃檯上畫著總是逃避挑戰的懦夫。導覽員想藉此娛樂觀眾；唐悅卻注意到他發現窄門時嚴肅的臉部表情。

It was not an argument between good people and bad people. The disagreement inside the team was practical: Mei wants to test the tempting short street, while Yu Qian asks who would be left behind. Tang Yue let both sides finish. A quick choice could protect their place in the contest, while a careful choice could protect people who would use the real streets after the contest ended. Fang wrote the competing assumptions in different boxes, and Yu Qian insisted that the group state what evidence would change their minds. "One person could pass that gate," Mei said. Yu Qian pointed at the five empty boxes. "Then why are there five spaces for names?"

這不是好人與壞人的爭執。小隊內的分歧很實際：梅嵐想測試誘人的捷徑，俞芊則問誰會被落下。唐悅讓雙方把話說完。快速選擇可能保住競賽名次，謹慎選擇則可能保護比賽結束後真正使用道路的人。方澤把不同假設填進分開的格子，俞芊堅持大家先說清楚，什麼證據會改變自己的看法。「一個人就能過那道門，」梅嵐說。俞芊指著五個空格：「那為什麼要留五個簽名欄？」

Fang drew three columns across a fresh sheet: prediction, observation, and uncertainty. The stage was supposed to help them register five people together and choose a route through a simulated block, yet the published score showed only the fastest number. Duan Ya checked the measurements, and Mei named the practical limits of their tools. They needed enough detail for a stranger to repeat the experiment without relying on the original team. A ministry clerk warned that players must not interfere with the simulated opera performance. Yet the supposedly persuasive instructions failed to describe the strange phenomenon: two identical arrows pointed toward different exits.

方澤在新紙張上畫出三欄：預測、觀察、不確定性。這一關原本要讓他們五人共同報名，選出穿越模擬街區的路線，公布的分數卻只有最快數字。段芽核對量測，梅嵐指出工具的實際限制。他們需要留下足夠細節，讓陌生人不靠原隊伍也能重做實驗。一名主管部門職員警告參賽者不得干擾模擬歌劇演出。然而那份看似有說服力的指示，卻無法解釋奇怪現象：兩支相同箭頭竟指向不同出口。

Their test was deliberately ordinary: they mark both routes on paper and walk at the pace of their slowest teammate. Tang Yue counted the steps without pushing anyone to move faster. When the figures differed, Fang left both values on the page until they understood why. Mei kept the equipment in place; Yu Qian checked that nobody felt forced to continue. The five knew that a public plan built on coercion would still be a bad plan, however handsome its final diagram appeared.

他們刻意採用平凡的測試方法：他們把兩條路畫在紙上，配合隊中最慢的人行走。唐悅計算步數，沒有催促任何人加速。兩組數值不同時，方澤先把兩者都保留在紙上，直到查出原因。梅嵐固定設備，俞芊確認沒有人被迫繼續。五人明白，靠強迫建立的公共方案仍是壞方案，不管最後圖畫得多漂亮。

After a second check, the outcome stood: all five sign the wider exit and earn a review seal instead of a speed bonus. Fang circled the measured facts and drew a line beneath every guess that still needed testing. Tang Yue thought this might be the contest’s unplanned achievement. The ranking could tell them which team was fastest today, but it could not decide by itself what every street should look like tomorrow. They drew the safer route beneath a shady awning, ignoring a slogan that promised victory to the fastest team. Duan Ya checked the map's actual usage with a youthful volunteer, who admitted it had never been tested by a whole group.

第二次核對後，結果依舊：五人簽下較寬出口，取得一枚複核印章，而非速度獎勵。方澤圈起量得的事實，在仍需驗證的猜測下畫線。唐悅認為，這也許是競賽未料到的成果：排名能說明今天誰最快，卻不能獨自決定明天每條街應該長什麼樣子。他們在陰暗遮棚下畫出較安全的路線，不理會宣稱最快者必勝的標語。段芽和年輕志工核對地圖的實際用途，志工承認從未讓完整隊伍測試。

As they prepared to leave, one final detail refused to fit the clean report: the public screen ranks their team last even though nobody was abandoned. Tang Yue entered it beneath a question mark rather than an accusation. She had learned to distrust easy endings, especially ones that arrived before the people most affected had spoken. They had not conquered the city model; they had made one part of it less mysterious, and that would have to be enough for tonight.

準備離開時，最後一項細節仍無法納入整齊的報告：公開螢幕把他們排在最後，儘管沒有人被遺下。唐悅把它寫在問號下，而不是直接當成指控。她已學會懷疑太容易的結局，尤其是受影響最大的人尚未開口時。他們沒有征服城市模型，只讓其中一部分不再神祕；今晚先做到這樣就夠了。
