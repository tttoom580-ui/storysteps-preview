安靜的點子

A Quiet Idea

In Shell Bay, the summer fair was always loud. Sela, a young capybara who liked drawing, saw her small neighbor cover his ears near the music. She asked why everyone had to enjoy the fair in the same way. She wanted to make something gentle, but she did not know where to start. A small bug rested on the wall while Sela watched the fair from a quiet place. She used a camera to remember how the hall looked before their work began. The fact was simple: some neighbors went home when the music became too loud. Sela gave a friendly smile to a child who stood far from the crowd. They took a short break and shared water. A hard job felt easier when everyone could say what they needed. After the break, they went back with a clearer plan.

在貝殼灣，夏季節慶總是很吵。喜歡畫畫的年輕水豚塞拉，看見小鄰居在音樂旁摀住耳朵。她心想：為什麼每個人都得用同一種方式享受節日？她想做點溫柔的事，卻不知道從何開始。塞拉在安靜處看節慶時，一隻小蟲停在牆上。 她用相機記下開工前廳堂的樣子。 事實很簡單：有些鄰居一聽見太大聲的音樂就回家。 塞拉對站在人群外的孩子友善地笑了。大家短暫休息，一起喝水。當每個人都能說出自己的需要時，辛苦的工作也變得比較容易。休息後，他們帶著更清楚的計畫回去做事。

At the edge of town stood a plain hall by the sea. Inside was a white cloth left from an old dance. Sela held a small lamp behind it. Her hand made a large shape on the other side. The shape moved when she moved. Sela laughed with surprise. She pictured a future in which that child could enjoy a festival too. There was a small cup of juice on the old hall table. Daro said they should never lie about whether a test really worked. Sela placed the white cloth in the middle of the room. Everyone had a different job, and each job mattered. Even the quiet work behind the cloth helped the show come alive. Sela promised to name every helper when the day came.

鎮邊有一座臨海的樸素廳堂。裡面留著一塊從前跳舞時用的白布。塞拉拿著小燈站在布後，另一邊立刻出現大大的手影。她一動，影子也動。塞拉驚喜地笑了。她想像未來那個孩子也能享受節慶。 舊廳堂的桌上有一小杯果汁。 達洛說，他們絕不該謊稱測試成功。 塞拉把白布放在房間中央。每個人有不同的工作，而每份工作都很重要。就連白布後面安靜的工作，也能讓表演活起來。塞拉答應在演出當天說出每位幫手的名字。

Daro, a hedgehog who made toys, came to look. He showed Sela how a piece of card could become a fish on the cloth. Uma, a patient turtle, watched them from the open door. She said that a story made of light and shade might be good for people who liked quiet things. The north window let in soft light during the morning. Uma was the first to notice the shadow growing on the cloth. The other two friends moved closer to see what Daro had made. A neighbor's pet dog rested outside instead of coming into the workroom. No one worked alone. When one pair of hands became tired, another friend took a turn. Sela wanted every helper to feel that this new show was theirs, not only hers.

會做玩具的刺蝟達洛走來看看。他示範如何把一片卡紙變成布上的魚影。很有耐心的烏龜烏瑪站在門口看著他們。她說，用光與影子說故事，或許很適合喜歡安靜的人。北邊的窗戶在早晨透進柔和的光。 烏瑪第一個注意到白布上的影子變大。 另外兩個朋友靠近，想看看達洛做了什麼。 鄰居的寵物狗待在外面休息，沒有進工作室。沒有人獨自工作。當一雙手累了，另一位朋友就接手。塞拉希望每位幫手都能覺得這場新表演屬於大家，而不是只屬於她。

The three friends did not plan a large party. They planned one short shadow show for neighbors who wanted a calm evening. Sela drew the hall and a little stage. Daro said he could make moving figures. Uma offered to check the time and ask what the town allowed. One family planned a picnic after the fair, away from the loud music. Sela could reach the lamp easily without stepping across Daro's tools. A red pencil lay on the table, but the friends chose plain black for the fish. One star appeared in the evening sky as they closed the hall. The work was small enough to finish before sunset. That mattered to the group. They did not want to make big promises and leave the hard parts for someone else to fix.

三個朋友沒有打算辦盛大的派對。他們想為喜歡安靜夜晚的鄰居，準備一場短短的影子戲。塞拉畫下廳堂和小舞台；達洛說能製作會動的角色；烏瑪則願意確認時間與場地使用規定。有個家庭打算在節慶後去野餐，遠離吵雜的音樂。 塞拉不用跨過達洛的工具，就能輕鬆拿到燈。 桌上放著紅鉛筆，但朋友們選擇用純黑色畫魚。 他們關廳堂時，晚空出現了一顆星星。這項工作小到可以在日落前完成。這對團隊很重要。他們不想許下很大的承諾，卻把困難的部分留給別人處理。

Before going home, Sela looked at the white cloth again. It was not an exciting object by itself. Yet when friends worked together, it could hold a whole story. She hoped their small idea would give a place to neighbors who often stayed away from the busy fair. They checked the doors, then walked home by the shore. This first day was about asking a question, not finding every answer. They would use only safe materials for the new shadow stage. An older woman smiled when Sela described the quiet plan. A new question came up, and nobody knew the answer at once. The friends did not hide that fact. They chose to try a small test and learn from what really happened.

回家前，塞拉再次看著白布。它本身並不是什麼令人興奮的東西。但只要朋友一起合作，它就能承載整個故事。她希望這個小點子，能讓平常避開熱鬧節慶的鄰居也有地方可去。他們檢查好門，然後沿海岸走回家。 第一天的重點是提出問題，而非找齊所有答案。 新影子舞台只會使用安全材料。 一位年長婦人聽見塞拉描述安靜活動時，露出了笑容。一個新問題出現了，沒有人立刻知道答案。朋友們沒有隱瞞這件事。他們決定先做小測試，再從實際發生的結果學習。
