敞開的門

The Open Door

Before they built more figures, Uma asked everyone to look at the hall entrance. There was a high step near the door, and one chair stood in the way. Some guests might need a wide, clear path. Sela had been thinking about the screen, not about how people would reach it. The side room had a bath, though visitors only needed its sink to wash their hands. One old chair could block the path if they left it near the door. Both Daro and Sela helped Uma measure the entrance. Uma brought a bowl of water to clean the dusty table. Uma looked at the time and called everyone together. They spoke for only a few minutes. Each person said what they would do next, so nobody had to guess about the plan.

在繼續做角色前，烏瑪請大家檢查廳堂入口。門邊有一道高階，還有椅子擋路。有些客人需要寬敞、沒有障礙的通道。塞拉原本只在想布幕，沒想到人們要怎麼進來。側室有浴缸，但訪客只需要那裡的水槽洗手。 一張舊椅子若留在門邊，就會擋住走道。 達洛和塞拉都幫烏瑪測量入口。 烏瑪帶來一碗水清理滿是灰塵的桌子。烏瑪看了看時間，把大家叫來。他們只談了幾分鐘。每個人都說明下一步要做什麼，如此便不必互相猜測計畫。

They asked the town for a safe board that could make the step easier. Oren said an adult worker would set it in place and check it. Daro did not try to make a fast fix by himself. He moved tables and kept the floor free of loose things. Giving visitors a choice of seats made the room feel kinder. Nobody had to cross a busy work area to reach the seats. Daro drew a cute little fish on the safety sign for young readers. Morning light entered through the east door while they moved tables. Uma kept a simple record of the day. She wrote what they had finished and what was still hard. It helped the friends see real progress instead of only the work ahead.

他們向鎮上申請一塊能讓台階較好通行的安全板。歐倫說會請成年工作人員安裝並檢查。達洛沒有自己草率修理，而是搬開桌子，清除地面上鬆散的東西。讓訪客自己選位子，使房間感覺更友善。 沒有人必須穿過忙碌的工作區才能到座位。 達洛在安全告示上畫了一條可愛小魚，給年幼讀者看。 他們搬桌子時，早晨的光從東門照進來。烏瑪用簡單的方式記下當天的工作。她寫下已完成的事，也記下仍然困難的地方。這讓朋友們看到真正的進展，而不只是眼前做不完的工作。

Sela made two kinds of seats. Some friends liked to sit near the front, and others wanted a quiet place by the wall. Both were good choices. She placed the lamp so that its light would not shine into the eyes of people coming through the door. A green line on the floor showed where the free path began. Sela turned her head to check the lamp from the back row. Daro gave a gentle knock on the door before moving the tall shelf. They took another look at the seats before asking guests to come in. The old hall was changing one step at a time. Its walls were still plain, and its floor still showed years of use. Yet the friends could now picture children and parents enjoying a show there.

塞拉安排兩種座位。有些人喜歡坐前排，另一些人想靠牆安靜坐著；兩種選擇都很好。她也把燈放在不會直射入場者眼睛的位置。地板上的綠線標示出通暢走道的起點。 塞拉轉頭，從後排查看燈光。 搬動高架前，達洛輕輕敲了門。 請客人進來前，他們再次查看座位。老廳堂正一步一步改變。牆面仍很樸素，地板也留著多年的痕跡。但朋友們已經能想像孩子和家長在這裡欣賞表演的樣子。

At the test, an older guest asked if he could leave early without being seen by everyone. Uma showed him the open side door. She said no one would ask why a visitor needed to go. Sela felt proud that the room was beginning to care for more kinds of people. A medium chair fit the small space better than the large old sofa. Mrs. Vale, an older neighbor, asked for a chair with a back. Sela said they must make the path clear before practice began. Uma said the side door was not a place to store bags. The day did not go exactly as they had hoped. Still, they had learned one useful thing. They added it to their plan, so tomorrow would begin with a little more knowledge.

測試時，一位年長客人問能不能提早離開而不被大家看見。烏瑪帶他看側門，說不會有人追問離開的原因。塞拉很高興，這個房間開始能照顧不同需求的人。中型椅子比大舊沙發更適合小空間。 年長的鄰居維爾太太想要一張有椅背的椅子。 塞拉說，練習開始前必須清出走道。 烏瑪說，側門不是放袋子的地方。這一天並沒有完全照他們希望的方式進行。不過，他們仍學到一件有用的事。他們把它加進計畫，讓明天能從多一點的理解開始。

When they closed the hall, Daro left the path wide and took a final look around. He said a pretty stage was not enough if someone could not come inside. Sela added a new line to her plan: The first part of the show is the welcome at the door. They would pass the safety plan to Oren for a final check. A quiet corner helped a visitor enjoy the show without extra talk. The surprise was how much better the room felt after a few changes. A taxi stopped outside to bring one older guest to the test. Outside, the sound of the bay came through the open door. It reminded Sela why she wanted a show in this town. It was a place where different voices could meet.

關上廳堂前，達洛把走道留得很寬，再環顧四周。他說，若有人進不來，再漂亮的舞台也不夠。塞拉在計畫上加了一句：「演出的第一部分，是門口的歡迎。」他們會把安全計畫交給歐倫做最後確認。 安靜角落讓訪客不用多說話也能欣賞演出。 令人驚喜的是，做了幾項調整後，房間舒適了許多。 一輛計程車停在外面，載一位年長訪客來測試。門外，海灣的聲音從敞開的門傳進來。這提醒塞拉為何想在這個小鎮辦演出：她希望不同的聲音能在這裡相遇。
