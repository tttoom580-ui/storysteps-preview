有兩個名字的證人

The Witness with Two Names

驛站密信：兩枚印章之下 · 第 3 / 25 章 · 4000–5000 · 英中對照

At the east ferry terminal beside the old border, Aya discovered how much a place could conceal without appearing mysterious. The boards were worn by footsteps, and the air carried the ordinary sounds of a working town. Darien came with her, carrying a notebook rather than a weapon. He had once delivered official messages without questioning their destination, and he now regarded that habit with suspicion. Aya did not ask him to confess everything he remembered at once. She wanted a reliable sequence, beginning with the last person who handled the evidence. 'A good question is smaller than a rumor,' she told him. He answered that small questions sometimes opened very large doors.

在舊邊界旁的東渡輪碼頭，雅發現一個地方不必看起來神祕，也能藏下許多事情。木板被腳步磨舊，空氣裡滿是小鎮平常的聲響。戴倫陪她來，帶的是筆記本，不是武器。他過去送公文時不問目的地，如今對這種習慣產生懷疑。雅沒有要他一次說完所有記憶；她想從最後接觸證據的人開始，建立可信順序。『好問題比謠言小得多，』她說。戴倫回答，小問題有時也能打開很大的門。

They examined a witness statement signed with two aliases on a clean table, with a witness close enough to see every movement. Aya wrote down which pages could be photographed and which were protected by privacy rules. The work was slow. A torn edge, a change of ink, and a number out of sequence all invited dramatic explanations. Darien made a rough diagram, then crossed out the parts he could not justify. 'It looks less impressive now,' he said. Aya disagreed. A map showing only verified information was more useful than a beautiful picture full of invented paths. They sealed the temporary notes separately from the original material, preserving the choice for later examiners to disagree with them.

他們在乾淨桌面上查看使用兩個化名簽署的證詞，讓見證人能看見所有動作。雅記下可拍照頁面與受隱私規則保護的部分。作業很慢；破邊、墨色改變與跳號都容易引出戲劇化解釋。戴倫畫了一張粗圖，又劃掉無法證實的部分。『看起來沒那麼厲害了，』他說。雅不認同：只包含已驗證資訊的地圖，比充滿杜撰路線的漂亮圖更有用。他們把工作筆記與原件分開封存，保留日後審查員不同意他們的權利。

The witness, a beloved boatmaker named Oren, met them beneath a faded sign. He was cautious after decades of aggression along the border. An attorney had once told him that a biological sample could diminish doubt, but no laboratory had kept one. Aya was bound by a narrower scope: confirm what Oren saw, not what people imagined. A security executive had made refugees into easy prey and added layer after layer of rumor. Darien tried to straighten the witness's old map while a ferry shuttle waited. The strap on Oren's bag had broken, and a brass clock gave one final tick. Oren could only shrug when Aya asked whether the courier was a double agent. He preferred realism to heroic stories. Aya respected his viewpoint: she would not specialize in inventing certainty, nor accept a virtual reconstruction as proof of a physical journey. When Oren finally pointed to the clinic's transport code, she understood why the same signature appeared twice: one name could protect a messenger, while another could conceal the person who gave the order.

深受居民敬愛的造船師奧倫在褪色招牌下與他們見面。他歷經邊境數十年的衝突，因此格外謹慎。律師曾告訴他生物樣本可能減少疑點，但實驗室並未留下樣本。雅能確認的範圍更有限：只證實奧倫所見，不把想像當事實。保安高層曾把難民當成獵物，謠言一層層堆上去。渡輪接駁船等待時，戴倫努力攤平老地圖；奧倫提袋的背帶斷了，銅鐘也敲過最後一下。被問到信差是否為雙面間諜，他只能聳肩。他重視現實，不喜歡英雄傳說。雅尊重他的觀點，不以虛擬重建取代實際路程的證據。當奧倫指向診所運輸編碼，她終於理解兩個簽名可能分別保護送信者與掩蓋下令者。

Outside, the immediate danger continued: a security executive had ordered the terminal closed. Aya could see why some residents wished the investigators would simply leave. Public inquiries attracted officials, reporters, and men eager to speak for families who had never elected them. She would not pretend that her search cost the community nothing. Before continuing, she asked local workers what practical help would reduce disruption. They requested safe access to the road and clear notice of any closure. Darien offered to help arrange both. The discussion took time, but it altered the meaning of the visit. They were not collecting painful memories like trophies. They were accountable to people who still had to live there.

外頭的危險還在持續：保安主管下令關閉碼頭。雅看得出部分居民為何希望調查者乾脆離開。公開調查會引來官員、記者，還有許多沒經居民選出的代言人。她不假裝調查完全沒有社區成本。繼續前，她問當地工人什麼幫助能降低干擾。他們要求道路安全通行，以及封路前明確通知。戴倫答應協助安排。討論花了時間，卻改變這趟訪查的意義：他們不是把別人的痛苦記憶當獎盃，而是必須向仍住在那裡的人負責。

Aya finally settled on a concrete course: to ask the witness about the dates before accusing anyone. She explained the decision in front of the witness rather than hiding it in a private exchange. Darien asked what would happen if the decision made the investigation harder. 'Then we have to work harder,' she said. 'The rules should not change merely because we dislike the result.' He found that answer irritating and reassuring at the same time. For twenty-two years he had wondered whether following orders was the same as doing right. Working with Aya offered another possibility: an order deserved respect only when someone could examine its authority, its purpose, and its effect on ordinary lives.

雅最後選擇一條具體做法：先詢問證人日期，不急著指控任何人。她在見證人面前說明，不把決定藏進私下交易。戴倫問，如果因此讓調查更難呢？『那我們就更努力，』她說。『不能只因不喜歡結果就改規則。』這回答既讓他煩躁，也讓他安心。二十二年來他總在想服從命令是否等於做對事。與雅合作讓他看到另一種可能：命令只有在權限、目的與對平凡人的影響都能被檢視時，才值得尊重。

The immediate outcome was real, although incomplete: the witness identified a concealed medical shipment. Aya checked that the people involved understood what would happen next, especially those who had risked speaking. She did not tell them that justice was already guaranteed. Instead she told them what document had been saved, which decision remained open, and whom they could contact if something changed. Darien found that honesty more comforting than a promise of victory. On their way out, a fresh clue entered the record: its transport tag carried the number of a mountain clinic. He looked toward the dark road, prepared to move. Aya remained long enough to copy the source and date. The journey would continue with evidence, not momentum alone.

目前的成果確實存在，雖然還不完整：證人指出一批隱藏的醫療物資。雅確認相關人士，尤其冒險發言的人，都明白下一步。她沒保證正義已經到來，只說明保存了哪些文件、哪些決定未定，以及有變化時應聯繫誰。戴倫覺得這種坦白比必勝承諾更令人安心。離開時，新線索進入紀錄：運送標籤上有山區診所的編號。他望向暗路準備出發，雅則多留一會兒抄下來源與日期。旅程要靠證據前進，不能只靠氣勢。
