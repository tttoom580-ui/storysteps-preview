The Red Thread

最後一幕，不准有人犧牲 · 第 1 / 25 章 · 5000–6000 · 英中對照

用互動版查詞、朗讀與收藏

Iris woke to the sound of a falling counterweight. She was no longer in her editing office but inside the royal theater of her favorite unfinished novel. People called her Lady Liora, the cruel supervisor destined to be executed after a dancer died on coronation night. A young engineer named Nilo was examining a suspended lamp when she saw the red thread tied around its release rope. She remembered that thread from a drawing in the book. Instead of ordering the rehearsal to continue, she shouted for the stage to be cleared and reached the emergency brake herself. A clerk tried to abbreviate the damaged rope’s label before Liora stopped him. The emergency brake became the anchor of the crew’s temporary safety plan. The same inspection rules were applicable to royal and ordinary performances alike. Jun carried the signed log to the independent archive across the square.

伊芮絲在配重墜落聲中醒來。她已不在自己的編輯辦公室，而是在最喜歡的未完小說裡，那座王家劇院。人們稱她為莉奧拉女士——注定在加冕之夜舞者死亡後遭處死的冷酷監督。年輕工程師尼洛正在檢查懸掛的燈具，她看見釋放繩索上綁著一條紅線。她記得書中插圖畫過那條線。她沒有命令排練繼續，而是高聲要求舞台清空，親自伸手拉下緊急煞車。 一名書記想縮寫受損繩索的標籤，莉奧拉制止了他。 緊急煞車成了工班臨時安全計畫的支點。 同一套檢查規則對王室和一般演出都適用。 阿筠把簽署紀錄送到廣場另一端的獨立檔案庫。

The next question was not whether the theater could produce an impressive spectacle. It was whether the company could explain every risk to the people standing beneath the scenery. Jun asked that costume workers receive the same information as engineers. Liora had once considered such meetings an obstacle. Now she understood that a warning which failed to reach its intended listener was no warning at all. Evi held a battered rehearsal notebook against her bosom as she spoke. A misplaced bra in the costume trunk revealed that someone had searched it. The messenger offered a bribe for silence, and Jun recorded his exact words. In the kitchen, cooks continued to broil vegetables for the exhausted performers.

接下來的問題不在於劇院能否製造震撼的表演，而在於劇團能否向站在布景下的人解釋每一項風險。阿筠要求戲服工人與工程師取得相同資訊。過去的莉奧拉曾把這類會議視為阻礙，如今她明白，沒有傳達給應該聽見之人的警告，根本稱不上警告。 艾薇說話時把磨損的排練筆記本緊貼胸前。 戲服箱裡一件放錯位置的胸罩顯示有人曾翻找。 信差提出賄賂要人噤聲，阿筠記下他的原話。 廚房裡，廚師繼續替疲憊的演員炙烤蔬菜。

The lamp stopped a handspan above the floor. Dancer Evi, only twenty-two, stared at the empty spot where she should have been standing. The royal patron, Corvin, demanded to know why the celebrated finale had been interrupted. Liora could not tell him she had read a future that might never happen. She showed him the cut fibers on the release rope, asked the crew to photograph their positions, and sealed the brake handle against further use. She also asked Jun, the costume master, to take the performers to a room away from the machinery. Nilo demanded a comparative test with a second undamaged release rope. Liora blinked one aching eyelid while checking the brake diagram again. Their time was finite, but rushing would turn a repair into another hazard. Only transparent records would let the theater flourish after the festival.

吊燈停在距離地面不到一掌的高度。二十二歲的舞者艾薇凝視本來應該站立的位置。王室贊助人科爾文質問她，為何打斷備受矚目的終幕。莉奧拉不能告訴他，自己讀過一段也許根本不會發生的未來。她指給他看釋放繩索上的割痕，請工班拍下各自位置，並封住煞車把手，禁止再啟動。她又請戲服主管阿筠把表演者帶往遠離機械的房間。 尼洛要求拿另一條完好的釋放繩索進行比較測試。 莉奧拉重新核對煞車圖時，眨了眨痠痛的眼皮。 他們的時間有限，但倉促修理只會製造新的危險。 只有透明紀錄，劇院才能在節慶後持續蓬勃發展。

That night, the workers copied the findings onto a board beside the public entrance. Their notes named dates, witnesses, and limits; they did not turn suspicion into a verdict. Several visitors complained that a festival should offer delight, not difficult questions. Evi answered that the audience could enjoy a performance without being asked to mistake danger for devotion. Liora kept the board where everyone could read it. A hired gangster waited outside the dressing room, frightening the apprentices. The crew marked the junction where two overhead lines crossed dangerously. The injured rigger walked with a limp but insisted on being interviewed. Liora asked him to narrate the accident in his own sequence.

那天晚上，工人把調查結果抄到公共入口旁的告示板。紀錄寫明日期、見證人與證據的限制，沒有把懷疑變成定論。幾名訪客抱怨節慶應該提供歡樂，而不是難題。艾薇回答，觀眾可以享受表演，不必把危險誤認成奉獻。莉奧拉讓告示板繼續留在人人看得見的地方。 一名受雇的黑幫分子守在更衣室外，嚇壞學徒。 工班標出兩條高空線路危險交會的接點。 受傷的吊掛工走路一跛一跛，仍堅持接受訪談。 莉奧拉請他按照自己的順序敘述事故。

By evening the palace announced that Liora had endangered the ceremony through needless alarm. It was the first public sentence in the novel that she had managed to change, and it came with a cost. Nilo admitted that Liora herself had approved the old safety plan months earlier. She did not deny it or blame a subordinate; she signed an order suspending the device and requested an independent inspection. If she intended to protect the people inside this story, she would first have to make herself answerable to them. An orderly placed barriers around the unused staircase until repairs finished. The theater’s ticket teller confirmed that no audience had entered yet. The crew compared old paint with virgin timber that had never been installed. A judge issued a narrow warrant for the sealed maintenance locker.

到了傍晚，王宮發布公告，稱莉奧拉以無端恐慌危及典禮。這是她在小說裡成功改變的第一句公開敘述，卻也付出代價。尼洛指出，數月前批准舊安全計畫的人正是莉奧拉本人。她沒有否認，也沒有把責任推給下屬；她簽令停用裝置，並要求獨立檢查。如果她想保護這個故事裡的人，就得先讓他們有權追究她的責任。 一名勤務員在維修結束前封鎖閒置樓梯。 劇院售票員確認當時尚無觀眾進場。 工班把舊油漆與從未安裝的全新木材比較。 法官核發範圍有限的搜索令，以檢查封存的維修置物櫃。

Before the meeting ended, Liora asked every department to state one thing it would refuse to do. The electricians would not energize untested cables. The actors would not perform beneath uninspected scenery. The custodians would not surrender original records without a signed receipt. These refusals did not stop the production; they gave cooperation a boundary. For the first time, a promise of safety had a specific meaning.

會議結束之前，莉奧拉請每個部門說出一件自己不會做的事。電工不會替未測試的電纜通電；演員不會在未檢查的布景下表演；保管人不會在缺少簽收單的情況下交出原始紀錄。這些拒絕並沒有阻止製作，反而讓合作有了界線。安全承諾第一次有了具體意義。
