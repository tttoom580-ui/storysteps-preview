第一次假約會

The First Practice Date

不必編造的約會 · 第 3 / 25 章 · 1000–2000 · 英中對照

用互動版查詞、朗讀與收藏

Saturday was cloudy, but the small town square was full of people. Lian met Jun beside the south entrance wearing her mother's old silver earrings. She carried a folder of story cards; he arrived on a scooter with a bag of wooden arrows. “Our first attempt at a date,” he said with a careful smile. “Our first attempt at a safe route,” she corrected. Two classmates were waiting to play visitors. Everyone knew the relationship in the script was fictional. That rule helped Lian relax. The opening scene would be easy: two characters meet by chance and decide to find the town's forgotten garden.

星期六天空多雲，小鎮廣場卻擠滿人。蓮戴著母親留下的舊銀耳環，在南側入口等俊。她抱著故事卡片，他則騎機車帶來一袋木箭頭標示。「我們的第一次約會練習，」他小心地笑說。「是第一次安全路線測試，」她糾正。兩位同學已在等著扮演參觀者。大家都知道劇本中的關係是虛構的，這讓蓮放鬆。開場很簡單：兩位角色偶然相遇，決定尋找鎮上被遺忘的花園。

At the first crossing, a delivery runner rushed past and almost hit their paper sign. Jun stepped toward the road to save it. Lian caught his sleeve. “A sign is not worth an injury,” she said. His nod was immediate. They moved the sign away from the entrance and noted that the traffic would cause trouble during the final event. One volunteer wanted to continue without recording the problem. “Then our plan would cheat the next group,” Lian answered. She was not accusing him of being dishonest; she meant that a test without a record was no true test. They made the change together.

到了第一個路口，一名送貨跑腿員匆匆經過，差點撞倒紙標示。俊往車道跨一步想救牌子，蓮拉住他的袖子。「一塊牌子不值得讓人受傷，」她說。他立刻點頭。他們把標示移離入口，記下決賽活動時車流可能造成的問題。一名志工想直接繼續，不記錄麻煩。「那等於欺騙下一組人，」蓮說。她不是指責對方不誠實，而是認為沒記錄的測試根本不算測試。兩人一起修正位置。

Their next stop was a community store. A retired teacher offered bowls of cereal to the volunteers and explained why the old public garden mattered. When the local government built the square, it promised to keep a path for older residents. That promise was still written on a board inside the store. Jun took a photograph, while Lian checked the general history in her notes. The board was a useful link between the written story and a real place. They could operate the exhibition without making up a heroic past. The town had enough ordinary acts of care to tell a strong story.

下一站是社區雜貨店。一位退休老師請志工吃穀片，並說明老花園的重要。當年地方政府興建廣場時，曾答應保留一條讓長者通行的小路；承諾至今還寫在店內一塊板子上。俊拍下照片，蓮則核對筆記裡的一般歷史。這塊板子讓書面故事與真實地點產生連結。他們不必捏造英雄事蹟就能辦展，鎮上普通人的善意已足夠構成好故事。

By noon the clouds had grown dark. Lian looked at the supply box and found that someone had taken the extra maps. She did not use force or shout at the helpers. Instead, she asked each person what they had seen. One student remembered a visitor taking a stack to the wrong table. They found the maps beside a market stall. Jun held an umbrella over her as they counted the pages, but he asked before moving closer. “This is only practical,” he said. “Good,” she replied. “Practical can be kind.” They returned just as rain began to fall across the square.

中午時雲層變得更暗。蓮打開補給箱，發現備用地圖不見了。她沒有強迫任何人，也沒有對志工大叫，只是逐一詢問目擊情況。一位學生想起有人把整疊地圖拿到錯誤的桌子。他們在市場攤位旁找到地圖。俊替她撐傘，走近前仍先問了她一聲。「這只是實際需要，」他說。「很好，」她回答。「實際也可以很溫柔。」他們回到廣場時，雨正好落下。

Back in the club room, Mei showed them the stranger's message about a fixed contest. Jun read it in silence. “I understand why you might suspect me,” he finally said. “But I can give you the dates when our team entered.” Lian checked the documents and found that his team had joined after the contest rules were announced. The claim did not match the timeline. She apologized for believing it for even a minute. He shrugged. “You checked. That is what I would have wanted.” Their easy disagreement felt more useful than a perfect fictional date.

回到社辦，美把陌生帳號指控比賽內定的訊息給他們看。俊沉默讀完。「我懂妳為什麼可能懷疑我，」他終於說。「但我能提供我們報名的日期。」蓮核對文件，發現創客社在規則公布後才報名。指控與時間線不符。她為自己曾短暫相信道歉，他聳聳肩：「妳有查證，這正是我想要的。」這種能好好處理分歧的關係，比完美的虛構約會更有價值。

At sunset they tried the closing scene. In the play, the two characters promised to return to the garden after it was repaired. Lian spoke the line, but she could not make herself sound happy. The garden was still closed, the report still incomplete, and their route still had risks. Jun suggested changing the ending from a promise to a question: “Will you come back and help us find the next page?” The listeners liked it better. Lian did too. She had expected him to demand a grand romance. Instead, he allowed a story to end with honest uncertainty.

日落時他們試演最後一幕。劇中的兩人原本約定，花園修好後一定要回來。蓮念出臺詞，卻怎麼也高興不起來：花園仍關著、報告仍不完整、路線也有風險。俊提議把結尾從承諾改成疑問：「你願意回來，和我們一起找到下一頁嗎？」聽眾更喜歡這版，蓮也一樣。她原以為俊會堅持宏大的浪漫結局，沒想到他容許故事誠實地留下不確定。

At the bus stop, a volunteer called their open discussion a liberal way of working, because people with different views could speak without fear. Lian liked that description. The team could disagree about a line of dialogue and still finish the route together.

公車站旁，一名志工稱他們開放討論的方式很自由包容，因為不同意見的人都能安心發言。蓮喜歡這個描述。大家即使對一行臺詞有分歧，仍能一起完成路線。

That night Jun sent her an updated route with marks beside every unsafe crossing. Lian returned the script with the new ending. Neither added a heart or a joke about their supposed relationship. The practice date had shown them something they could not have learned from posters: when plans failed, they could talk without trying to win. Lian pinned the map above her desk. Below it she wrote three rules for the real contest: ask first, keep records, and leave room for people to change their minds. She had once called Jun her enemy. That word no longer seemed exact.

那晚，俊傳來更新的路線圖，在每個不安全路口旁做上記號。蓮則回傳改好結尾的劇本。兩人都沒有加愛心，也沒拿所謂的交往關係開玩笑。第一次假約會讓他們從海報上學不到的事變得清楚：計畫出錯時，他們可以談論問題，而不是一心想贏。蓮把地圖釘在書桌上，下面寫了三條真正的競賽規則：先問、留紀錄、允許人改變心意。她曾經叫俊「敵人」，如今這個字不再準確。
