沒有人走的側門

The Gate Nobody Used

不必編造的約會 · 第 4 / 25 章 · 1000–2000 · 英中對照

用互動版查詞、朗讀與收藏

On Monday the clubs had to attend a safety meeting at the student department. Their first route had looked lovely in photographs, but an older visitor with a walking stick could not use the eastern gate. A heavy cage for garden tools blocked half the way, and a broken roof sent water onto the steps. “Our purpose is not to make a beautiful path for healthy students,” Lian said. “It must be open to people who move differently.” The officer agreed and asked for a clear list of risks. Jun wrote down measurements instead of blaming the workers who had left the cage there.

星期一，兩個社團都必須參加學生事務部門的安全會議。第一次路線拍照很好看，但一位拿手杖的年長訪客無法通過東側大門。沉重的園藝工具籠擋住半條路，破屋頂的雨水也滴在臺階上。「我們的目的不是替健康學生做一條漂亮小路，」蓮說。「它必須讓行動方式不同的人都能通過。」承辦員同意，要求列出明確風險。俊記下尺寸，沒有責怪把籠子放在那裡的工人。

A medical student from the nearby hospital joined the walk. She explained that some visitors carried a drug for sudden illness, while others needed somewhere quiet to sit. The team should never offer medical advice, but it could show where help was available. In the gymnasium, a volunteer demonstrated how much space a person needed to turn a wheelchair. Mei tied a bright ribbon to an umbrella so they could mark the right corner on the map. Jun suggested that the new path meet a basic safety standard before they added any music or lights. Lian approved that order.

附近醫院的一位醫學生加入步行測試。她說有些訪客會攜帶緊急用藥，另一些人則需要安靜坐下的地方。團隊不該提供醫療建議，但可以標出求助地點。體育館裡，一名志工示範輪椅迴轉需要多大的空間。美把鮮豔緞帶綁在雨傘上，方便在地圖上標示正確轉角。俊建議先讓新路線達到基本安全標準，再增加音樂與燈光。蓮同意這個次序。

At lunch the volunteers took over a corner of the cooking classroom. A student used a pan to fry vegetables, and the smell made everyone hungry. Another student combed a costume wig for their play. These little jobs were not part of the grant, but they made the group feel less divided. Lian was firm about one thing: the costume box was private property, so nobody should borrow it without asking. Jun laughed when Mei placed a paper wing on the wig and called it a bird from the future. Their work was serious, yet their room did not have to be silent.

中午時，志工借用烹飪教室的一角。一位學生用平底鍋炒蔬菜，香味讓大家餓了；另一位則替劇服假髮梳理頭髮。這些小事不是補助計畫的一部分，卻使團隊比較像一個整體。蓮有一點很堅持：服裝箱屬於私人財物，借用前必須先問。美把紙翅膀插上假髮，說這是未來的鳥，俊忍不住笑了。工作很認真，但社辦不必一直安靜。

They found a minor problem in the new route when a bicycle rolled around a blind corner. A fast motion could turn a small mistake into an injury. Jun drew the corner from above, while Lian asked the gardener what people usually did there. “Children run past the wall in summer,” he told them. “In winter, older people avoid the wet stones.” He also remembered a picture of a wolf painted on the old wall. It had been removed after a storm, but its place could still be seen. Lian made a note. The route could tell a local story while warning visitors to slow down.

新路線又有一個小問題：自行車會突然從視線死角的轉角衝出。快速動作可能把小錯誤變成傷害。俊從上方畫出轉角，蓮則問園丁大家平常怎麼經過。「夏天孩子會沿牆奔跑，」他說。「冬天長者會避開濕石頭。」他也記得牆上曾畫著一匹狼，暴風雨後畫被除去，痕跡卻仍看得出來。蓮記下來。這條路可以一邊講地方故事，一邊提醒大家放慢腳步。

Their adviser offered encouragement but no easy money. The university could pay for signs, not for major road work. If the teams wanted a wider gate, they needed permission from the town. “Then we will ask,” Lian said. Jun brought a drawing showing a small change rather than a costly new entrance. He kept the original path as a second choice, so no volunteer would be forced to promise what the club could not deliver. Lian saw how his careful plans protected people from disappointment. That was another kind of kindness she had not noticed during their year as rivals.

指導老師給了鼓勵，卻沒有提供簡單的資金。大學可以支付標示牌費用，無法出錢做大型道路工程。若要拓寬大門，還得向鎮公所申請。「那就去問，」蓮說。俊帶來一張圖，只建議小幅調整，而不是昂貴的新入口。他仍保留原路線當備案，避免志工被迫承諾社團做不到的事。蓮看見他的謹慎如何保護大家免於失望。這是過去競爭時她沒注意到的另一種體貼。

That night, Mei sent Lian an old photograph. It showed the eastern gate before the cage had been placed there. On the wall was the painted wolf, and next to it was a wooden sign pointing to a public reading room. The building was now locked. Nobody on the committee had mentioned it. Lian called Jun and asked whether the room might be included in the exhibition. “We should check who owns it first,” he answered. She was glad he did not simply agree to please her. They had found a question worth following, and the safe path had brought them to it.

當晚，美傳給蓮一張舊照片，拍到工具籠擋住東側大門之前的樣子。牆上有狼的彩繪，旁邊還有一塊木牌指向公共閱覽室。那棟建築如今鎖著，委員會卻沒人提過。蓮打電話問俊能否把閱覽室納入展覽。「先查清楚它屬於誰吧，」他回答。她很高興他沒有只為討好她就答應。這條安全路線，把他們帶到一個值得追查的新問題前。

The town officer explained that the western region had similar paths with the same problem. Lian asked for the contact information of the neighborhood group there. A solution that worked at one gate might help another, but only after local people had a chance to test it.

鎮公所人員解釋，西部區域也有類似步道和相同問題。蓮請對方提供當地社區團體的聯絡方式。一座大門適用的解法，也許能幫上另一處，但必須先讓當地人測試。

Before bed Lian revised their costume notes. The play would no longer end at the garden gate. The fictional couple would find a locked reading room and ask the audience why it had disappeared from public memory. She wanted the visitors to feel curious, not frightened. Jun replied that the garden could become a quiet place for everyone if the school and town made a fair agreement. “No magic,” he wrote. “Just a door people can use.” She looked at those words for a long time. He understood the heart of her story better than she had expected.

睡前，蓮修改了服裝與劇本備註。演出不再結束於花園大門。虛構情侶會發現上鎖的閱覽室，並問觀眾它為何從公共記憶中消失。她希望觀眾好奇，而不是恐懼。俊回覆，只要學校與城鎮達成公平協議，花園就能成為大家安靜停留的地方。「沒有魔法，」他寫。「只有一扇人人能用的門。」蓮看著這些字很久。俊比她預期更理解故事的核心。
