葡萄園裡的機器

The Machine Beneath the Vines

逆鐘旅店 · 第 4 / 25 章 · 5000–6000 · 英中對照

用互動版查詞、朗讀與收藏

The road climbed past a vineyard where frost had killed half the autumn crop. At its center stood an archaeology station, abandoned after its director accused the city of stealing discoveries. Yuna and Miro found a faded badge beside a broken shaver and an old field notebook. The pages contained approximate drawings of a machine with rotating glass disks. Its aesthetic design was beautiful, though the mechanism looked brutal. A patient lay beneath a veil while rings of light appeared to radiate from a narrow opening over the head. The notebook described a harmless method of preserving spoken testimony: an early recording instrument, not a medical device. It had required no human donor at all. Yuna stared at the drawings. If the oldest machine had preserved voices without taking memories, why had the city turned the technology into a procedure that stole parts of people?

道路爬過一座葡萄園，秋季收成已有一半遭霜害。園中央是一處考古研究站，站長指控城市竊取發現後，整座研究站便被棄置。優娜與米羅在破刮鬍刀旁找到褪色徽章及一本田野筆記。頁面上大致畫著一台裝有旋轉玻璃圓盤的機器。造型設計很美，運作結構卻顯得殘酷。病患躺在面紗下，一圈圈光芒似乎從頭頂的狹縫輻射而出。筆記記載的其實是保存口述證詞的無害方法：那是一種早期錄音器，不是醫療裝置，根本不需要任何捐贈者。優娜盯著圖畫出神。倘若最初的機器不必奪走記憶就能保存聲音，為何城市後來竟把它變成偷走人格碎片的手術？

A former disciple of the station's founder appeared from among the vines. His name was Ors, and he had spent years guarding the ruins because the council intended to privatize the entire archive of discoveries. He described the founder's ambition: to enlighten ordinary citizens by letting them hear evidence directly, rather than trust the judgment of powerful families. When the machine became popular, the trustees claimed its inventions as state property. Later they granted exclusive licenses to clinics owned by their relatives. "Prestige can be purchased," Ors said, "but that doesn't make a claim true." Yuna asked him about the memory transplants. He admitted there had been a catastrophic blaze in the original laboratory, destroying the simplest equipment. The council had blamed a stray worker; the report never explained why the fire began. Ors suspected sabotage, but suspicion was not proof.

一名研究站創辦人的昔日門徒從藤蔓間現身。他叫奧斯，多年來一直看守廢墟，因為議會想把整個研究檔案私有化。他說，創辦人的志向是啟發普通公民，讓人民能親耳聽見證據，不必只信權貴的判斷。機器受歡迎後，理事們宣稱所有發明皆屬國有，接著把獨家授權交給自家親戚經營的診所。「威望可以買，」奧斯說，「但買來的主張不一定是真的。」優娜問起記憶移植。奧斯承認，原始實驗室曾發生嚴重大火，最簡單的設備全被燒毀。議會把責任推給一名擅闖的工人，報告卻從未解釋起火原因。奧斯懷疑有人蓄意破壞，但懷疑不是證據。

Inside a buried cellar they found the machine's central drum. Its rotation was controlled by a clockwork gear, and a note on its side warned that the warranty was void if the seals were broken. Miro laughed bitterly at the commercial language. He had seen patients sign away memories under contracts full of similar promises. Yuna was about to open the drum when she noticed an implicit mark in the diagram: a small gap between two disks that suggested a second recording path. "Perhaps the donor was never necessary," she said. "Perhaps someone wanted people to believe that a recovered life always required a sacrifice." Ors cautioned her not to make a fuss before checking the mechanism. He showed her where to turn the crank. The machine shuddered, then played a child's laughter from decades earlier. The sound had the peculiar clarity of something once treasured and almost lost; even Miro, normally eager to argue, stood in silence until the recording ended. Nobody in that room lost a memory. The demonstration was small, but its implications were enormous.

在埋於地下的地窖裡，他們找到機器中央的滾筒。它由鐘錶齒輪控制轉動，側面告示寫著：封條如經拆除，保固即失效。米羅對這種商業用語苦笑。他見過病患在充滿類似保證的契約上簽字，交出自己的記憶。優娜正準備打開滾筒，忽然注意到圖示中的隱含標記：兩片圓盤間留有一道小空隙，暗示第二條錄音路徑。「或許從來不需要捐贈者，」她說，「或許有人希望大家相信，救回一個人就必須犧牲另一人。」奧斯勸她先檢查機構，別急著大驚小怪。他指出搖柄位置。機器顫動了一下，接著播放出幾十年前某個孩子的笑聲。那聲音清澈得奇特，像是一度被珍愛又險些遺失的東西；就連總愛爭論的米羅，都靜靜站著聽到錄音結束。房裡沒有任何人失去記憶。示範很小，意義卻極為重大。

Their discovery drew the attention of a patrol. A magistrate arrived to preside over an emergency inquiry, surrounded by guards wearing the new clinic badges. Ors stepped forward with the notebook and requested a public demonstration. The magistrate refused, claiming that uncontrolled recordings encouraged addiction to other people's pasts. The argument sounded dignified but concealed fear. Yuna realized that the council did not merely want to hide a defective machine. It wanted to hide the existence of a machine that made costly memory donations unnecessary. She packed the drum into a canvas bag, then asked the magistrate to examine the earliest patent. "If there is a lawful reason this technology cannot be shared," she said, "write it where citizens can read it." Rather than answer, the magistrate ordered the patrol to seize the bag. Ors struck the cellar lamp against the floor, plunging them all into darkness. When the guards regained the light, Yuna was gone.

發現很快引來巡邏隊。一名裁判官在佩戴新診所徽章的守衛簇擁下抵達，打算主持緊急調查。奧斯帶著筆記走上前，要求公開展示。裁判官拒絕，聲稱失控的錄音會讓人沉迷於別人的過去。這番話聽來莊嚴，實則藏著恐懼。優娜明白，議會要隱藏的不只是有缺陷的機器，而是一種根本不需要昂貴記憶捐贈就能運作的技術。她把滾筒裝進帆布袋，要求裁判官審查最早的專利。「如果法律有理由禁止共享技術，」她說，「那就寫出來讓市民看。」裁判官沒有回答，反而命令巡邏隊扣押袋子。奧斯把地窖的燈砸向地面，四周頓時漆黑。守衛恢復照明時，優娜已經不見。
