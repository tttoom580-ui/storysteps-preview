沙下水道

The Hidden Channel

第二聲鐘響前，離開白沙灘 · 第 5 / 25 章 · 2000–3000 · 英中對照

用互動版查詞、朗讀與收藏

The fifth morning brought the first clear stretch of weather. Mei compared the current shoreline with the harbor chart found in a chest. The drawing showed an island edge farther west than the present strip of white sand. She walked along the safe rocks and placed markers at each change in ground height. Omar watched from above, holding the rope without pulling it. They discovered a hard ridge beneath what looked like a soft beach. Water rushed along both sides when the tide rose. The place was not a wide shore at all; it was the roof of a narrow channel covered by moving sand.

第五個清晨終於放晴。梅把現在的海岸線和箱中的港灣圖相比，發現舊圖的島緣比現今白沙向西得多。她沿安全岩石行走，每遇地勢變化就放下標記。奧馬在上方看守，握著繩子卻不拉扯。他們在看似柔軟的海灘下找到堅硬岩脊。漲潮時，水從岩脊兩邊急流而過。這裡根本不是寬闊海岸，而是一條被流沙覆蓋的狹窄水道頂部。

The discovery changed their escape plan. They had first hoped to drag the Morning Gull across the sand and repair it near the green tank. Now they knew that movement might break the boat apart and leave them trapped in the tide. Omar was disappointed, but he agreed to stop the attempt. The better route would lead through the old service path above the rock ridge. Mei checked the route twice and marked the point where Jun could rest. A safe decision felt slow compared with a dramatic rescue scene, yet it gave the group something valuable: time and a road they could test.

發現改變了逃生計畫。他們原本想把晨鷗號拖過白沙，移到綠槽附近修復；現在知道這麼做可能讓船破裂，使人困在潮水中。奧馬很失望，但同意停止嘗試。較好的路線會穿過岩脊上方的舊維修道。梅檢查路線兩次，標出君能休息的位置。安全決定看起來比驚險救援慢，卻替三人爭取到寶貴的時間，以及一條可反覆測試的道路。

Mei had once trained as an athlete, but her career was mapping currents, not winning races. The benefit of that training was her ability to drag a heavy chest over rough stone. Inside she found a clue: a scientific harbor chart, a medal shaped like a knight, and a lock that no longer worked. Jun was eager to erase the chalk mark across the chart, but Mei stopped him. Flour spilled from a broken emergency tin and made a horrible white trail across the floor. A note from a trader mentioned the local tribe's old fishing names, although nobody lived on the island now. Another page recorded the volume of sand removed to pave a walkway. The numbers did not match the present beach. Mei realized that the apparent shore might be a fresh deposit covering a dangerous channel.

梅以前受過運動員訓練，但她的職業是測繪海流，不是參加競賽。訓練的好處，是讓她能把沉重箱子拖過粗糙的石頭。箱內有一條線索：科學測繪的港灣圖、騎士形狀的獎牌，以及已經壞掉的鎖。君急著擦掉海圖上的粉筆記號，梅卻阻止他。破掉的應急麵粉罐灑出粉末，在地上留下難看的白痕。一名商人的便條提到當地族群曾使用的漁場名稱，雖然如今島上已無人居住。另一頁記下鋪設步道時移走的沙量。那些數字與現今海灘不符。梅想到，看似穩固的海岸，可能只是新堆積的沙，掩蓋著危險水道。

That night Jun caught a short burst on the radio. It was not a clear voice, only three uneven tones followed by silence. He wrote their lengths and gave the paper to Mei. She saw that the timing almost matched the bell interval they had measured. Almost, but not exactly. Rather than announce that rescue was near, she asked Omar to check whether the island transmitter could repeat a pattern by itself. An automatic machine could create a false promise as easily as a person could. They celebrated with one extra sip of clean water and recorded their first milestone: safe route found; boat escape rejected; radio source still unknown.

當晚君從無線電收到一小段訊號，不是清楚人聲，而是三段長短不齊的聲音，然後沉寂。他寫下每段長度交給梅。梅發現時間幾乎吻合他們測過的鐘聲間隔，但並不完全相同。她沒有宣布救援將至，而是請奧馬查證島上的發報機是否能自行重複模式。自動機器也可能像人一樣，製造虛假的希望。三人多喝了一小口乾淨水慶祝，在筆記中記下第一個階段成果：找到安全道路、放棄拖船逃生、仍不知訊號來源。

By the time the sun touched the western ridge, the group understood why the beach had seemed to change shape. The sand was moving over older stone, pushed by currents that turned around the island. Mei showed Jun where a long strip of seaweed had been trapped between two rock layers. The plants marked an earlier high tide, not a fresh path. Omar checked the damaged boat from a distance and accepted that they could recover nothing more without a proper crew. They stopped planning to make the Morning Gull sail again. That choice hurt him more than he expected. He had maintained the vessel for years, and leaving it felt like leaving part of his work behind. Mei did not tell him to forget it. She offered to record its position carefully, so the rescue center could decide later whether salvage was possible. A map could hold grief without letting grief direct a dangerous decision.

太陽碰到西側岩脊時，三人終於理解沙灘為何像會變形。水流繞島轉向，推著沙在舊岩石上移動。梅讓君看兩層岩石間夾住的一長條海草。那些植物標記的是較早的高潮位，不是新出現的道路。奧馬遠遠檢查破船，接受沒有專業人員就再也拿不回更多物品。他們停止計畫讓晨鷗號重新航行。這個選擇比奧馬預期更痛。他多年保養那艘船，離開它像拋下自己的工作。梅沒有要他忘記，而是提議準確記錄船的位置，讓救援中心日後判斷能否打撈。地圖可以承載失落，卻不讓失落主導危險決策。
