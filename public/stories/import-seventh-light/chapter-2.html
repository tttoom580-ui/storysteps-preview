潮汐下的堡壘

The Fort Beneath the Tide

第七盞燈 · 第 2 / 25 章 · 3000–4000 · 英中對照

用互動版查詞、朗讀與收藏

Tomas brought Lia to an abandoned stone fort before sunrise. Its walls had survived storms, a war, and a long debate over immigration, yet the company now described it as worthless land. Under the floor of an old mill he showed her a delicate fossil embedded in a slab of dark rock. His father had found hundreds, but the official geological report mentioned none. “A fossil is a message from a life that cannot speak,” Tomas said. Lia gazed at the layered stone and imagined a landscape once covered by trees. Beyond the hill lay the proposed site of Helix Tide's atomic research center. Its brochure promised that a combination of new technology and renewable power would guarantee a productive future. It did not explain the extent of the drilling.

天未亮，托瑪斯帶黎雅走進一座廢棄石堡。堡牆熬過暴風、戰爭與多年移民爭論，如今公司卻把這裡稱為毫無價值的荒地。他在舊磨坊地板下指給她看，一塊深色岩石裡嵌著精緻的化石。父親曾發現上百塊，但官方地質報告一塊也沒提。「化石是不能開口的生命寄來的訊息，」托瑪斯說。黎雅凝視岩層，想像這片如今裸露的地景曾遍布樹木。山後是海潮螺旋公司預定的原子研究中心，宣傳冊保證新科技與再生能源能創造高產能未來，卻沒說鑽探規模有多大。

A small device attached to the wall began to murmur at their approach. Tomas called it a messenger left by his father: a recording unit connected to water-level sensors. One message had survived the flood. A tired voice said, “Revise the map. The eastern channels are open to the sea.” The next sentence dissolved into static. Lia played the recording again and recognized an intimate detail in the speaker's breath, the hesitation of someone speaking while in pain. Tomas kept his eyes on the floor. He had spent a year retaining this recording without daring to share it. His fear was not only psychological. If the company controlled the coastline, it could cut off his family home, his boat, and his livelihood.

牆上的小裝置在他們靠近時發出低鳴。托瑪斯說，這是父親留下的信使：連著水位感測器的錄音機。洪水後僅存一則留言，疲憊的聲音說：「修改地圖。東邊水道與外海相通。」下一句便被雜音吞沒。黎雅重播，聽見說話者在痛楚中換氣的細微遲疑。托瑪斯只是低頭看地。他保存這段錄音一年，卻始終不敢公開。他害怕的並非純粹心理作用：如果公司掌控海岸，就能切斷他家的房子、小船與生計。

At noon they met Dr. Mira Sen, a marine physicist who had recently arrived by research vessel. She examined the fossil with almost tender care and explained why the proposed project troubled her. Atomic research was not automatically dangerous, and the word atom should not terrify anyone by itself. But a map that ignored connected water channels made any safety claim unreliable. “Science begins with the parts we admit we do not know,” she said. She had joined the voyage to measure groundwater, not to defend a corporate promise. The three compared the company's diagrams with Tomas's father's notebooks. Each page widened the gap between the official account and the coast they could see.

中午，他們遇見搭研究船來島的海洋物理學家米拉・森博士。她幾乎帶著憐愛檢視化石，解釋計畫為何令她不安。原子研究不必然危險，「原子」一詞也不該令人平白恐懼；但忽略相通水道的地圖，無法支撐任何安全保證。「科學始於我們肯承認自己不知道什麼，」她說。她搭船來是測量地下水，不是替企業背書。三人把公司圖紙與托瑪斯父親的筆記並列，每翻一頁，官方說法與眼前海岸的差距就更大。

They followed a narrow passage beneath the fort. In one corner, shells formed a pattern identical to the one on the back of Lia's photograph. Mira called the mark an early tide symbol, but she could not identify the hand that had drawn it recently. A gust rose through a gap in the wall and set the little recording device humming again. This time they heard a second voice, softer and younger: “If you must choose what to save, save the names.” Tomas froze. The voice belonged to his younger sister, who had left the island after their father's disappearance. She had never told him she was involved. Lia realized the mystery reached farther than a missing keeper; someone had been carrying messages through this underground place for years.

他們沿石堡下方的狹道前進，一角的貝殼排列與照片背面的符號完全相同。米拉說那是早期潮汐記號，卻認不出近期畫記號的人。風從牆縫灌入，讓錄音裝置再度嗡鳴。這次出現另一個更輕、更年輕的聲音：「如果只能選擇一件要救的東西，就救名字。」托瑪斯僵住了，那是妹妹的聲音。父親失蹤後她早已離島，從沒告訴哥哥自己也參與其中。黎雅發現謎團遠不止一位失蹤守燈人；多年來一直有人透過地底傳遞訊息。

On their return, a company boat crossed the harbor and announced a temporary closure of the eastern shore. The captain promised that no private property would be affected. Rosa stood on the pier and asked him to name the properties he had checked. He could not. The exchange seemed small, but the crowd listened. Lia photographed the people instead of the boat, recording their faces as the announcement changed from reassurance to an argument. She knew an image could not prove everything it suggested. Still, a picture of who had been present would matter when memory later became a battlefield. Above the fort, a pale seventh light shone for three steady seconds, then went out in the full daylight.

回程時，公司船橫越港灣，宣布暫時封鎖東岸。船長保證不影響任何私產。蘿莎站在碼頭，請他說出查核過哪些產權，他卻答不上來。這看似小事，群眾卻聽得認真。黎雅沒有拍船，而是拍下公告從安撫變成爭執時每個人的表情。她知道照片無法證明它暗示的一切，但若日後記憶成了戰場，誰曾在場的影像將格外重要。堡壘上方，第七盞淡光在大白天穩穩亮了三秒，才熄滅。
