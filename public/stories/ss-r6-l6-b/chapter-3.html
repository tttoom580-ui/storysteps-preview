有權拒絕的聲音

A Voice That Can Say No

你終於學會告白時，我已經準備離開 · 第 3 / 25 章 · 5000–6000 · 英中對照

用互動版查詞、朗讀與收藏

A visitor might have seen only an elegant hall and a queue of hopeful people. Yu saw the invisible labour beneath it: consent calls, damaged tapes, unpaid overtime, and the care required to return a story when its owner asked. Mrs. Wu, a retired cashier, came to request the removal of an interview about her son's departure. She had agreed to a private research copy, not a public performance. Yu opened a lengthy review despite Han's impatience. Through years of editing oral histories, Lin Yu had learned to distrust sentences that sounded complete without naming the person who had agreed to them. A recording could be technically perfect and still tell a dishonest story about who owned a voice.

遊客眼中可能只有優雅的大廳與排隊的人潮。林予看到的是底下看不見的勞動：授權電話、受損磁帶、未付的加班費，以及主人要求取回故事時必須付出的照顧。退休收銀員吳太太前來要求撤下一段談兒子離家的訪談。她同意的是私人研究副本，不是公開播放。林予即使面對韓館長催促，仍啟動漫長審查。多年來整理口述歷史，林予已不再信任那些聽起來完整、卻沒有寫明誰同意的句子。錄音在技術上可以完美無瑕，卻仍可能對聲音的主人說出不誠實的故事。

In a public collection, confidence is built through traceable actions rather than persuasive speeches. Yu insisted that the team distinguish a suggestion from an instruction and a verified permission from an assumption passed down by a former manager. Han called the objection a disgrace to a respected institution. Yu replied that dissent was permissible and could enrich the archive's credibility. Mei asked both sides to separate reputational fears from the speaker's actual written agreement. At the long table near the listening booths, everyone arrived carrying a different version of what the museum was supposed to protect. Some feared a cancelled event; others feared that a neighbour's story might be taken from them forever.

公共典藏的信任來自能追溯的行動，不是動聽的演說。林予堅持區分建議和指令，也區分經確認的授權，與前任主管口耳相傳的假設。韓館長稱這份異議有損聲譽。林予回答，提出反對是被允許的，也能增進檔案館可信度。梅要求雙方把名聲焦慮和受訪者真正簽署的同意書分開。在試聽室旁的長桌，每個人帶來的都是不同版本的『博物館究竟該保護什麼』。有人怕活動取消，有人怕鄰居的故事一旦被拿走，就再也回不來。

The consent cabinet held a surprising cross-section of lives. A carpenter who planned to emigrate had spoken with lifelong melancholy about leaving his hometown. A nurse described a routine checkup and the danger of noise loud enough to deafen factory workers. Another speaker recalled strict rules for waste disposal beside the curb. In a recording made for children, a woman addressed the narrator as 'madam' or 'ma'am' and laughed at their formal rivalry. The museum had registered a local trademark for its exhibitions, but that mark gave it no ownership of anyone's private grief. Yu asked the archivists to place each statement under scrutiny. No one was allowed to preach that a speaker must accept exposure for the public good. The decision reduced the suspense surrounding Mrs. Wu's request without pretending that every case would be simple.

授權櫃裡記錄著各種人生。計畫移民的木匠終生帶著鄉愁談故鄉；護理師談一般健檢與足以讓工人失聰的噪音。有人回憶路緣旁嚴格的廢棄物處理規範。兒童錄音裡，一名婦人稱旁白為『夫人』，並笑談兩人一本正經的競爭。館方擁有地方展覽商標，卻不能藉此擁有任何人的私密悲傷。林予要求每份陳述都經過檢視，禁止拿公益當說教理由，逼受訪者接受曝光。這項決定消除了吳太太申請的懸念，卻沒有假裝所有案件都容易。

Yan looked as if he wanted to repair the day with one perfect sentence. Yu told him that recordings could be edited, but the missing choice of a living person could not. If he had something difficult to say, he would need to say it without asking her to rescue him. Yan located an old cassette reminiscent of the sound Mrs. Wu remembered from childhood. He refused to duplicate it before she chose what she wanted. For the first time, Yu saw him put someone's choice ahead of the machinery. Zhou Yan, the museum's senior sound engineer, knew the machinery better than almost anyone. He was thirty-eight, careful with fragile tapes, and much less practised at admitting that his silence could wound another person.

周晏看起來想用一句完美的話修復整天。林予告訴他，錄音可以剪輯，活生生的人失去的選擇卻無法倒帶。如果他有難以啟齒的話，就必須說出來，而不是要求她替他解圍。周晏找到一卷聲音讓吳太太想起童年的舊卡帶。她還沒決定前，他拒絕複製。林予第一次看見他把當事人選擇放在機器之前。三十八歲的周晏是館內資深聲音工程師，幾乎沒有人比他更熟悉設備。他對脆弱的磁帶十分細心，卻不太擅長承認自己的沉默也會傷害別人。

Yu did not want a farewell speech in which everyone praised her sacrifice. She wanted the speakers to know where their recordings were, who could use them, and how to ask for a copy or a removal. Then she wanted to board her train. Mrs. Wu signed a limited research permission but declined display. Yu recorded the decision and gave her a copy. The exhibition lost a beautiful recording; the museum gained a chance to practise what its motto claimed. Outside, buses came and went along the river road. Yu had applied for a position at the North Shore Public Archive, two hours away by train. She had not told her colleagues because she wanted the choice to become real before it became gossip.

林予不要一場讚美她犧牲的告別演說。她要受訪者知道錄音在哪裡、誰能使用，以及如何申請副本或撤下。辦妥這些事後，她才想登上火車。吳太太簽下有限度研究授權，卻拒絕展出。林予記錄決定並交給她副本。展覽失去一段漂亮錄音，博物館卻得到實踐館訓的機會。窗外，巴士沿河岸道路來去。林予已申請距離兩小時車程的北岸公共檔案所職缺。她還沒告訴同事，因為她希望這個選擇先成真，再成為眾人口中的八卦。
