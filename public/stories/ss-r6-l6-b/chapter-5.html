董事會前五天

Five Days to the Board

Nobody in the office called the situation a crisis yet. People lowered their voices and asked for another meeting. Yu recognised that delay could be a decision of its own, especially when the disputed recording remained available to the public. Five days remained before the first board review. A breakdown in the museum's telecommunications line delayed permission calls, while Han proposed to bypass the ordinary approval route. Yu refused to let the deadline become an excuse. Through years of editing oral histories, Lin Yu had learned to distrust sentences that sounded complete without naming the person who had agreed to them. A recording could be technically perfect and still tell a dishonest story about who owned a voice.

辦公室裡還沒有人把情況稱為危機。大家壓低聲音，要求再開一次會。林予明白拖延本身也可能是一種決定，尤其當有爭議的錄音仍向公眾開放時。董事會初次審查只剩五天。館內通訊系統故障，延誤授權電話，韓館長又提議繞過正常核准程序。林予拒絕讓期限成為藉口。多年來整理口述歷史，林予已不再信任那些聽起來完整、卻沒有寫明誰同意的句子。錄音在技術上可以完美無瑕，卻仍可能對聲音的主人說出不誠實的故事。

A quiet volunteer waited until the senior people had finished arguing. Yu invited the volunteer to speak first in the second round. The simplest question often came from the person who would have to explain the museum's policy to an anxious visitor. Mei gathered the missing signatures into a separate folder. A monetary contribution from a local sponsor had been recorded twice. Yu asked the accountant to mark the duplicate instead of quietly deleting it, so that the board could see the full audit trail. At the long table near the listening booths, everyone arrived carrying a different version of what the museum was supposed to protect. Some feared a cancelled event; others feared that a neighbour's story might be taken from them forever.

一名安靜的志工等資深同仁吵完才開口。第二輪討論時，林予請志工先說。最單純的問題，往往來自必須向不安訪客解釋館方政策的人。梅把缺少的簽名集中存檔。一筆在地贊助款被重複登錄，林予請會計標出重複項而非悄悄刪去，讓董事會能看見完整稽核軌跡。在試聽室旁的長桌，每個人帶來的都是不同版本的『博物館究竟該保護什麼』。有人怕活動取消，有人怕鄰居的故事一旦被拿走，就再也回不來。

A lively fundraiser filled the side gallery with strange fragments of the city's past. An old chestnut seller wore a silver accessory, while another photograph showed domestic fowl at a roadside market. A display of early rap recordings had a scratched disc with a long slash across its label. A sleeping guard's snore was captured by accident on a historic microphone. Someone offered champagne near the exhibit, and a conservator with a hoarse voice asked guests to keep their drinks away. A medical poster advertised hand lotion; a radio program described how one family's mentality survived a harsh winter. Mei said the nucleus of the public collection was its people, not its glamour. The event could evoke memories and unveil forgotten voices, but only if the organisers had the vigor to resist the cowardly temptation to hide inconvenient facts.

側廊的募款活動擠滿城市往事的奇異碎片。舊栗子攤販戴著銀色配件，另一張照片拍下路邊市場的家禽。早期饒舌唱片標籤有一道長刮痕，老麥克風甚至意外錄到守衛的鼾聲。有人在展品旁端香檳，一名嗓音沙啞的修復師請大家把飲料移遠。醫療海報廣告護手乳；廣播節目談某個家庭在寒冬仍保持的心態。梅說，公共典藏的核心是人，不是光鮮。活動能喚起記憶、揭露被忘記的聲音，前提是主辦者得有勇氣抵抗掩飾不便真相的怯懦。

He asked whether an apology would make their cooperation easier. Yu said it might make the record clearer, but it would not purchase forgiveness. He listened, then stepped aside so she could finish the task she had chosen. Yan had been moody for days. Yu asked him directly whether he intended to finish the cataloguing work. He said yes and stopped trying to guess how much private conversation she owed him for his help. Zhou Yan, the museum's senior sound engineer, knew the machinery better than almost anyone. He was thirty-eight, careful with fragile tapes, and much less practised at admitting that his silence could wound another person.

他問道歉能否讓合作容易些。林予回答，道歉也許能讓事情的紀錄更清楚，卻不能買下原諒。他聽完後退到一旁，讓她完成自己選擇的工作。周晏連日情緒不穩。林予直接問他是否打算完成編目。他回答會，也不再猜測她應該用多少私下談話來回報他的幫忙。三十八歲的周晏是館內資深聲音工程師，幾乎沒有人比他更熟悉設備。他對脆弱的磁帶十分細心，卻不太擅長承認自己的沉默也會傷害別人。

She wrote down the question that mattered most: If no one confessed anything, would she still choose to leave? The answer came quickly. Yes. It hurt, but the clarity was a kind of relief. The board accepted the audit plan and postponed the sponsor's exclusive preview. It was a limited victory: nothing had been finally approved. Yu nevertheless left with the completed checklist and her resignation still intact. Outside, buses came and went along the river road. Yu had applied for a position at the North Shore Public Archive, two hours away by train. She had not told her colleagues because she wanted the choice to become real before it became gossip.

她寫下最重要的一個問題：假如沒有人告白，她還會選擇離開嗎？答案很快浮現。會。這很痛，清晰卻也是一種解脫。董事會接受稽核計畫，延後贊助人的專屬預覽。這只是有限勝利，任何事項都還沒最終核准。林予帶著完成的檢核表離開，辭呈依然有效。窗外，巴士沿河岸道路來去。林予已申請距離兩小時車程的北岸公共檔案所職缺。她還沒告訴同事，因為她希望這個選擇先成真，再成為眾人口中的八卦。
