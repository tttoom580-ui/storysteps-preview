The Notice Beneath the Clock

你終於學會告白時，我已經準備離開 · 第 1 / 25 章 · 5000–6000 · 英中對照

The building smelled of warm paper and wet coats. It was an ordinary workday, which made the coming disagreement feel more serious: no alarm announced the moment when a familiar institution began treating someone else's memory as its own property. Yu filed her resignation before the trustees convened. Director Han had issued a directive to accelerate the annual exhibition, but the permission ledger was missing twenty-seven signatures. She told him that the date on the wall could not outrank the people on the tapes. Through years of editing oral histories, Lin Yu had learned to distrust sentences that sounded complete without naming the person who had agreed to them. A recording could be technically perfect and still tell a dishonest story about who owned a voice.

館內有溫熱紙張與濕外套的味道。這是平凡的工作日，因此即將發生的爭執更顯沉重：沒有警報會宣布，一間熟悉的機構何時開始把別人的記憶視為自己的財產。林予在董事們開會前送交辭呈。韓館長已發出指示，要提早年度展期，但授權簿缺少二十七個簽名。她告訴館長，牆上的日期不能凌駕錄音裡的人。多年來整理口述歷史，林予已不再信任那些聽起來完整、卻沒有寫明誰同意的句子。錄音在技術上可以完美無瑕，卻仍可能對聲音的主人說出不誠實的故事。

The staff wanted a single tidy explanation for the latest complication. Yu asked them to preserve three things instead: the original material, the recorded objection, and a note explaining who had authority to change the display. Han clasped the folder and warned that her departure would heighten public anxiety. Yu answered that withholding a problem would create a worse precedent. Mei, a community trustee, quietly asked for an independent count rather than another emotional argument. At the long table near the listening booths, everyone arrived carrying a different version of what the museum was supposed to protect. Some feared a cancelled event; others feared that a neighbour's story might be taken from them forever.

館員想替最新的麻煩找出一個整齊的說法。林予卻要求保留三樣東西：原始材料、已登記的異議，以及說明誰有權變更展出的註記。韓館長緊抓資料夾，警告她的離職會加深公眾不安。林予回答，掩蓋問題才會建立更糟的先例。社區受託人梅則要求獨立清點，不再進行情緒爭論。在試聽室旁的長桌，每個人帶來的都是不同版本的『博物館究竟該保護什麼』。有人怕活動取消，有人怕鄰居的故事一旦被拿走，就再也回不來。

The lobby catalogue made the problem visible in miniature. One recording described a bear cub and a horse at full gallop; another concerned dental care in an old workers' district. A former dictator had once demanded unconditional loyalty from every janitor in the station. Beside that account stood a pilgrim's description of a roadside shrine. The museum's motto was 'Every voice returns home,' yet a panel called a forced relocation a voluntary transplant. A school group read about farmers who hoped to reap a better harvest, while a label for a glass dwelling misidentified its maker. Mei asked whether an archive that could preserve every detail was also capable of correcting an error. Yu pointed to the unchecked permission box beside the display. That small empty square, she said, mattered more than the grandeur of the exhibit.

大廳展件把問題縮成容易看見的模樣。一段錄音描述小熊與疾馳的馬，另一段談舊工人區的牙科醫療。曾有獨裁者要求車站每位清潔工無條件效忠；旁邊是朝聖旅人對路邊神龕的描述。館訓寫著『讓每個聲音回家』，展板卻把強迫遷居稱作自願移居。學生讀著農民期待收穫的故事；玻璃住屋模型的製作者也被標錯。梅問：保存細節的檔案館，能不能改正錯誤？林予指向展板旁未勾選的授權格。她說，這個小小的空格比整場展覽的氣派更重要。

Yan kept his hands away from the tape deck while they talked. He had once used technical explanations to escape a difficult conversation. Yu could now tell when he was describing a problem and when he was trying to avoid saying what he had done. Yan found Yu beside a stationary listening machine. He wanted to utter something personal, but she asked him first to identify which master files had been copied. His careful answer helped the audit, although it did not erase what he had failed to say years ago. Zhou Yan, the museum's senior sound engineer, knew the machinery better than almost anyone. He was thirty-eight, careful with fragile tapes, and much less practised at admitting that his silence could wound another person.

談話時周晏沒有碰錄音機。他過去常用技術解釋逃避艱難對話。如今林予能分辨，他何時是在說明問題，何時則是在避談自己做過的事。周晏在停住的試聽機旁找到林予。他想說些私事，她卻先問原始檔案被複製了哪些。他謹慎的回答幫上稽核，卻無法抹去多年前他沒說出口的話。三十八歲的周晏是館內資深聲音工程師，幾乎沒有人比他更熟悉設備。他對脆弱的磁帶十分細心，卻不太擅長承認自己的沉默也會傷害別人。

On her way home, Yu watched the station clock turn and imagined a desk at the new archive. The future seemed neither heroic nor safe. It held unanswered questions, ordinary rent, unfamiliar colleagues, and the possibility of work she could name as her own. That evening Yu returned to her small dwelling and submitted an application to North Shore Archive. She had served this museum for eleven years; her resignation was not a synonym for betrayal. It was the first decision she had made without consulting anyone else. Outside, buses came and went along the river road. Yu had applied for a position at the North Shore Public Archive, two hours away by train. She had not told her colleagues because she wanted the choice to become real before it became gossip.

回家途中，林予看著車站時鐘轉動，想像新檔案所的一張書桌。未來既不英雄式，也不保證安全。那裡有未回答的問題、平凡房租、陌生同事，以及她能真正稱作自己工作的可能。當晚林予回到小住處，遞出北岸檔案所的申請。她為館方服務十一年；辭職不是背叛的同義詞。這是她第一次不先詢問別人，就替自己做決定。窗外，巴士沿河岸道路來去。林予已申請距離兩小時車程的北岸公共檔案所職缺。她還沒告訴同事，因為她希望這個選擇先成真，再成為眾人口中的八卦。
