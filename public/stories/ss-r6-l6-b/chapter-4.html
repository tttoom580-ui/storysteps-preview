The Label with Two Dates

你終於學會告白時，我已經準備離開 · 第 4 / 25 章 · 5000–6000 · 英中對照

Every room carried traces of decisions made before the current staff arrived. Yu had learned not to assume that an old label was correct merely because its ink had faded into an attractive shade. Age could preserve an error as easily as a fact. An exhibition designer offered an elegant aesthetic for the opening. Yu noticed that a fire photograph and an archaeological survey had been placed under one date, although the records were decades apart. She asked for a correction before approving the layout. Through years of editing oral histories, Lin Yu had learned to distrust sentences that sounded complete without naming the person who had agreed to them. A recording could be technically perfect and still tell a dishonest story about who owned a voice.

每個房間都留下現任館員到職以前做過的決定。林予早已學會，不能因標籤的墨色褪得好看，就認定舊標示正確。時間既能保存事實，也能保存錯誤。展覽設計師提出優雅的視覺方案。林予發現火災照片與考古測量被標在同一年，實際卻相隔數十年。她要求先更正才核准版面。多年來整理口述歷史，林予已不再信任那些聽起來完整、卻沒有寫明誰同意的句子。錄音在技術上可以完美無瑕，卻仍可能對聲音的主人說出不誠實的故事。

They could have made the issue disappear from the meeting minutes by choosing gentler language. Yu left the uncomfortable words intact. She knew that future colleagues would need to understand not only what had been decided, but also what had been rejected. Han presided over a branding meeting where prestige mattered more than sources. He planned to privatize the listening room for donors. Mei challenged the proposal and demanded a public review of access hours. At the long table near the listening booths, everyone arrived carrying a different version of what the museum was supposed to protect. Some feared a cancelled event; others feared that a neighbour's story might be taken from them forever.

他們本可換上溫和措辭，讓爭議從會議紀錄消失。林予保留了那些令人不快的字句。她知道未來的同事不只需要理解做了什麼決定，也需要知道哪些方案遭到否決。韓館長主持品牌會議，談話裡聲望比來源重要。他打算把試聽室改供捐款人私人使用。梅反對，要求公開審查開放時間。在試聽室旁的長桌，每個人帶來的都是不同版本的『博物館究竟該保護什麼』。有人怕活動取消，有人怕鄰居的故事一旦被拿走，就再也回不來。

The design studio was crowded with objects that resisted easy classification. A badge from a former volunteer lay beside a shaver found after a station blaze. A disciple of a famous architect had donated sketches of a vineyard, but the lines were only approximate. A stray dog appeared beneath a photographer's veil in one negative. A farm record described seasonal rotation, while a device warranty had been stapled to an unrelated invoice. The curator hoped the restored objects would radiate authority. Yu answered that authority should come from evidence, not an implicit assumption that old paper deserved trust. She disliked making a fuss, but she wanted the public to understand how an initial error might grow into a polished myth. The inquiry did not enlighten everyone, yet it gave the volunteers a method for correcting future labels. A visitor admitted an addiction to collecting archaeology pamphlets, but Yu separated fascination from reliable provenance.

設計室擠滿難以分類的物件。舊志工徽章放在車站火災後找到的刮鬍刀旁；知名建築師門徒捐出葡萄園草圖，但線條只是近似。某張底片裡，一隻流浪狗出現在攝影師面紗下。農場紀錄描述作物輪作，設備保固單卻釘在無關發票上。策展人盼修復展品散發權威感。林予說權威應來自證據，而非舊紙必然可信的隱含假設。她不愛小題大作，卻希望觀眾理解最初的錯誤如何長成精緻神話。調查未能啟發每個人，至少提供志工日後更正標籤的方法。一名訪客承認自己沉迷蒐集考古學手冊，林予卻把蒐藏熱情與可靠來源分開。

A machine could reveal where a signal had dropped out. It could not tell them why someone had remained silent when a colleague needed support. Yan understood the distinction, even if he disliked the answer he was beginning to discover. Yan traced the error to a copied design file, not to the photographer. Yu insisted they retain the correction history. He admired her firmness without turning that admiration into another demand for her attention. Zhou Yan, the museum's senior sound engineer, knew the machinery better than almost anyone. He was thirty-eight, careful with fragile tapes, and much less practised at admitting that his silence could wound another person.

機器能指出訊號在哪裡中斷，卻不能解釋同事需要支持時，為何有人選擇沉默。周晏理解這項區別，縱使他並不喜歡自己正在發現的答案。周晏追查發現錯誤來自複製的設計檔，不是攝影師。林予堅持保留更正歷程。他欣賞她的堅定，卻沒把欣賞變成索取她注意力的要求。三十八歲的周晏是館內資深聲音工程師，幾乎沒有人比他更熟悉設備。他對脆弱的磁帶十分細心，卻不太擅長承認自己的沉默也會傷害別人。

In the evening, she put two folders on her table. One held the museum's unfinished work; the other held the plans for her own life. She would care for the first without allowing it to swallow the second. The corrected label went up at closing time. Yu stood beneath it and thought of the North Shore interview scheduled for Friday. An institution could change its label; she was free to change her address. Outside, buses came and went along the river road. Yu had applied for a position at the North Shore Public Archive, two hours away by train. She had not told her colleagues because she wanted the choice to become real before it became gossip.

晚上，她把兩份資料夾放上桌。一份裝著博物館未完成的工作，另一份裝著自己的生活計畫。她會照顧前者，卻不讓它吞沒後者。更正標籤在閉館前掛上。林予站在下面，想著週五安排的北岸面試。機構能更換標示，她也有自由改變住址。窗外，巴士沿河岸道路來去。林予已申請距離兩小時車程的北岸公共檔案所職缺。她還沒告訴同事，因為她希望這個選擇先成真，再成為眾人口中的八卦。
