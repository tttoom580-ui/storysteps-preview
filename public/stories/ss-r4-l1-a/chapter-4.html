小鎮廣場

The Town Square

鈴聲過後的每一封信 · 第 4 / 25 章 · 1000以下 · 英中對照

A boy ran across the town square with a butterfly on his school bag. He nearly dropped a letter when a strong wind began to blow. Ada caught the paper and helped him put it away. A tall worker from the old factory watched them. “Thank you, sir,” the boy said. The worker shook his head and smiled. He had come to ask about mail service for the factory staff. Some workers could only visit after their day ended. “Our office closes at five,” Ada said, “but I can ask whether a safe box outside would help.”

一名男孩背著有蝴蝶圖案的書包跑過小鎮廣場。強風吹來時，他差點把信掉在地上。艾達接住紙張，幫他收好。一位從舊工廠走來的高個子工人看著他們。「謝謝您，先生。」男孩說。工人笑著搖頭。他是來問工廠員工的郵務服務。有些人只能下班後來。「我們五點關門，」艾達說，「但我可以問問，在外面設安全收件箱是否有幫助。」

Dark clouds covered the sun. Mara came to the square carrying tomatoes from the market. She said the cheap ones tasted as good as the costly ones. She offered Ada one for lunch, but Ada had to finish her route first. “Are you healthy enough to walk all afternoon?” Mara asked. “Yes, but I must take water breaks,” Ada answered. The two sat for a minute under the roof while rain began to fall. Mara said she had asked for information at the map office. There was no old house list for Willow Road, only a story about a family garden.

烏雲遮住太陽。瑪拉抱著市場買的番茄走到廣場。她說便宜的番茄和貴的同樣好吃，想送艾達一顆當午餐，但艾達必須先走完路線。「妳身體健康，能走一整個下午嗎？」瑪拉問。「可以，不過我要停下來喝水。」艾達答道。雨開始下時，兩人在屋簷下坐了一分鐘。瑪拉說已向地圖室詢問消息，找不到柳樹路舊房屋名冊，只有一則關於家族花園的故事。

At the post office, Mr. Lee showed them a square of dry wood. It could hold a sign beside the new mail box. “Do not work without a clear service plan,” he told Ada. “A box that no one checks is no help at all.” She wrote down the time and the name of the worker who had asked for it. She would bring the idea to the town office. Mara wanted to paint the box bright red. Ada said they would need permission first. Mara agreed. She was learning that a little patience kept a good idea from making a new problem.

回到郵局後，李先生拿出一片方形的乾木板。它可以用來掛新信箱旁的標誌。「沒有清楚的服務計畫，就不要動工。」他告訴艾達，「沒有人按時收取的信箱，根本幫不上忙。」艾達寫下工人的姓名與提出建議的時間，準備帶去鎮公所。瑪拉想把箱子漆成亮紅色。艾達說要先取得許可，瑪拉同意了。她漸漸學到，耐心能避免好點子變成新麻煩。

In the afternoon a woman came to report a missing card. She was worried that someone had taken it. Mr. Lee asked her to tell them the last place she had seen it. She had put it on a table in the market, then gone to buy fruit. They did not say anyone had stolen it. Ada walked to the market and asked at the service desk. The card was there, safe in a small box. The woman was pleased and thanked the market worker. Ada saw how a question asked in a calm voice could stop a bad story before it grew.

下午，一名女子來通報卡片不見了。她擔心有人拿走。李先生請她說出最後看到卡片的地方。她把卡片放在市場的桌上，後來去買水果。他們沒有說是被偷了。艾達走到市場服務台詢問，卡片就在小盒裡，安然無恙。女子很高興，向市場工作人員道謝。艾達看見，一個冷靜提出的問題，能在不好的傳言變大之前先止住它。

The rain stopped before closing time. Ada saw the boy from the morning on his way home. The butterfly on his bag was wet, but the letter inside was dry. “I kept it safe,” he said. Ada smiled. He had learned to care for a piece of paper because someone wanted to read it. Mara looked toward the old factory and asked what would happen to the town if the post office closed. Ada did not know. Mr. Lee had worked there for many years, but he was getting ready to leave. That question stayed with her.

關門前，雨停了。艾達看見早上的男孩走在回家路上。他書包上的蝴蝶濕了，裡面的信卻是乾的。「我把它保護好了。」他說。艾達笑了。他已懂得珍惜一張紙，因為有人期待讀它。瑪拉望向舊工廠，問如果郵局關門，小鎮會怎麼樣。艾達不知道。李先生已在這裡工作多年，正準備退休。這個問題留在她心裡。

When another cloud crossed the sky, the market worker gave the tall boy a box of tomatoes to carry. One tomato began to roll, and the boy had to stop and hold the box still. His hands began to shake because he was afraid of dropping the food. Ada told him to put it down and take a breath before trying again. He did, and the worker helped him carry half. “It is easier when we ask,” the boy said. Ada thought the same was true of hard work at the post office.

又一朵雲飄過時，市場工人給高個子男孩一箱番茄搬運。一顆番茄開始滾動，男孩只好停下來，穩住箱子。他怕掉落食物，手開始發抖。艾達叫他放下箱子，呼吸一下再試。他照做了，工人幫忙搬一半。「開口求助就容易多了。」男孩說。艾達覺得郵局的艱難工作也是如此。

That evening Ada read the list of requests she had made. The factory wanted a later drop box. The market wanted a clear place for lost cards. Some older people wanted help reading street numbers. None of the ideas was large by itself, but together they showed what the office meant to the town. Ada took off her wet shoes and placed them by the door. She would not promise everyone an answer tomorrow. She would promise to ask, write, and return with what she learned.

那晚，艾達讀著自己記下的需求。工廠希望有晚一點也能投信的箱子；市場希望有清楚的失物放置處；一些長者希望有人幫忙看門牌。每個想法都不大，合在一起卻說明郵局對小鎮的意義。艾達脫下濕鞋放在門邊。她不會保證明天回答所有人，只會答應去問、記錄，再把知道的事帶回來。
