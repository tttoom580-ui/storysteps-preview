花園裡的記號

A Mark in the Garden

Although it was warm in town, the hills were still cold. A small patch of snow lay beside the garden near Willow Road. Ada and Mara went there to look at a public stone marker. They did not dig, because the land belonged to another family. A frog jumped into a pool, and a hen walked past a row of beans. “This is a long way from the river,” Mara said. “Maybe the road moved, but the garden stayed.” Ada wrote that idea down. It was only a guess, yet it gave them a new point to check.

雖然鎮上暖和，山丘上仍冷。柳樹路附近的花園旁，還留著一小片雪。艾達和瑪拉去看一塊公開的石製路標。她們沒有挖土，因為土地屬於別的家庭。一隻青蛙跳進水窪，一隻母雞走過豆子田。「這裡離河好遠。」瑪拉說，「或許路移動了，花園卻留著。」艾達把想法記下。那只是猜測，但值得再查。

Ms. Green, the garden owner, came to the gate. Ada said sorry for standing so close to her flowers. Ms. Green did not look angry. “You may read the marker from the road,” she said, “but please do not go into my garden.” Mara agreed at once. The marker was about the height of Ada's knee. A little arrow pointed south. Under it was a number that no longer matched any house. Ms. Green told them the road had changed during her father's time. “At least the stone is still here,” she said.

花園主人葛林女士來到門口。艾達為站得太靠近花圃道歉。葛林女士沒有生氣。「妳們可以從路邊看路標，」她說，「但請不要進我的花園。」瑪拉立刻答應。路標大約高到艾達的膝蓋，上面一個小箭頭指向南邊，下面的數字已不符合任何門牌。葛林女士說，道路是在她父親的年代改變的。「至少石頭還在。」她說。

They had only a minute before Ada needed to finish her mail race against the closing hour. Mr. Lee always told her it was not really a race. Safe service mattered more than a win. Still, Ada had to be at the office to put the last letters into the right boxes. Mara offered to carry Ada's water bottle, not the mail bag. They walked back and met a man making a deal for garden tools. He did not know the old road, but he remembered that a town map had once hung in the school hall.

艾達只剩一點時間就得完成送信，趕在關門前回到郵局。李先生常提醒她，這其實不是比賽，安全服務比勝利重要。儘管如此，她仍要把最後的信放進正確格子。瑪拉主動幫她拿水瓶，不碰信袋。回程時，她們遇到一名正在談園藝工具交易的男子。他不認識舊路，卻記得學校大廳曾掛著一張老地圖。

Back at the office, Ada put the details into her notes. The marker had a number, an arrow, and a place in the garden. She drew it from memory and placed the picture next to Mara's copy of the envelope. The two drawings were not the same, but both showed lines that might have a meaning. Mara wanted to tell everyone at once. Ada asked her to wait. “We can ask the school for the map tomorrow. We cannot say we have found the house.” Mara agreed, though she looked tired.

回郵局後，艾達把細節寫進筆記。石標上有數字、箭頭，也有花園中的確切位置。她憑記憶畫下，放在瑪拉的信封影本旁。兩張圖不同，但都有可能代表什麼的線條。瑪拉想立刻告訴所有人。艾達請她等等。「明天可以問學校要地圖，但不能說已經找到房子。」瑪拉同意了，雖然看來很累。

That evening, Ms. Green sent a small basket of beans to the post office. She said her daughter had read the old marker many times when she was young. Maybe the school map could help, and maybe not. Ada thanked her without asking for any private family paper. She knew that strangers were not asked to share everything just because another person had a sad question. She could be kind while respecting a closed door. Mara said that was one reason she trusted Ada.

傍晚，葛林女士送來一小籃豆子到郵局。她說女兒小時候常看那塊舊路標。學校地圖也許有幫助，也可能沒有。艾達向她道謝，沒有索取任何私人家庭文件。她明白，別人有難過的疑問，不代表陌生人就必須分享一切。她可以善良，同時尊重關上的門。瑪拉說，這也是她信任艾達的原因。

On the way back, Mara put a hand on the back of her neck and looked at the old trees. “I thought the little stone would tell us everything,” she said. Ada offered her water and showed her a bean plant growing beside the fence. “That plant did not grow in one minute,” she said. “We have to give some questions time too.” Mara smiled at the strange example. They walked past the garden slowly, using the public road. The stone stayed where it was, and neither of them needed to take it home to remember it.

回程時，瑪拉一手摸著後頸，望著老樹。「我原以為小石頭能告訴我們一切。」她說。艾達遞水給她，指著圍欄旁正在生長的豆子。「那棵豆苗也不是一分鐘長成的，」她說，「有些問題也需要時間。」瑪拉被這奇怪的例子逗笑。兩人沿公共道路慢慢走過花園。石標留在原處，她們不需要帶走它才能記得。

Before bed, Ada made a simple plan for the morning. She would finish her first delivery, stop at the school office, and ask whether the old public map could be seen. She would check the date and write down the part that mattered. Then she would tell Mara exactly what she had found, not what she hoped to find. It was a long plan for a small stone, yet Ada felt at peace. She was beginning to understand that care could be quiet and slow, and still be real.

睡前，艾達訂下隔天的簡單計畫：先完成第一趟送信，再到學校辦公室詢問是否能查看公開的老地圖；核對日期，記下重要部分；最後把真正查到的內容告訴瑪拉，而不是自己的期待。為了一塊小石頭，這是個漫長的計畫，但艾達感到平靜。她開始理解，關心可以安靜、緩慢，仍然真實。
