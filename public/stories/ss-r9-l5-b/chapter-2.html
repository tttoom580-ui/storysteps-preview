沒有落點的山脊

A Ridge Without a Landing

雲鶴歸巢：與你修一條天空的路 · 第 2 / 25 章 · 4000–5000 · 英中對照

The next morning the pair crossed to Tin Island in a basket suspended from the service line. Below them, old cloud currents moved like pale rivers. He Yan had repaired the basket's brake twice, yet he still checked it again before inviting Lu Que aboard. Their destination was a narrow ridge where exhausted cranes had recently attempted to land. From the air, the ridge looked safe. Up close, its soil had been stripped by a new company road. A corporate notice claimed the road would be beneficial because it could convert unused ground into a trading station. The map named a profitable destination but failed to mention a nesting colony. Workers had canceled their shifts during a fever epidemic, leaving an errand boy to guard the gate. It took no extraordinary wisdom to see the contradiction: people were promised faster journeys while the creatures that kept their gardens alive lost their resting ground.

隔天早上，兩人坐著懸在勤務索上的吊籃前往錫島。下方，舊雲流像淡色河流緩緩移動。何晏已修過吊籃煞車兩次，仍在邀陸雀上來前再次檢查。目的地是一道狹窄山脊，最近有疲倦的雲鶴試著在那裡降落。從空中看似安全，近看卻發現土壤已被新開的公司道路挖走。 公司公告聲稱道路會帶來好處，因為它能把閒置土地轉變成交易站。地圖標示了有利可圖的目的地，卻沒提到築巢群落。工人因發燒疫情取消班次，只留一名跑腿少年守門。用不著什麼非凡智慧也看得出矛盾：人們被承諾能更快旅行，但讓花園保持生命力的生物卻失去休息地。

He Yan unloaded a box of wooden markers while Lu Que checked the cracked basin near the cliff. A baby crane had fallen there in the night. Its wing was not broken, but the creature was too weak to climb. Lu Que approached slowly with an open palm, allowing it to retreat if frightened. He Yan anchored a canvas shade to a live root rather than the loose stones. Together they made a place for the animal to rest without closing it in. They had to haul water up the ridge, knowing that a sudden plunge into the ravine could kill the young bird. The company's letter denied any liability for injuries outside its fence. He Yan tied a reminder to the rope; Lu Que spoke with a respondent from the nearby village who had seen the first accident. A sensor on the gate had recorded strong gusts, but the manager had never checked its warning light.

何晏卸下一箱木製標記，陸雀則查看懸崖旁裂開的水盆。一隻幼雲鶴夜裡跌進盆中，翅膀沒斷，卻虛弱得爬不出來。陸雀攤開手掌慢慢接近，讓牠害怕時能退開。何晏把帆布遮棚固定在活樹根上，而不是鬆動的石頭上。他們一起做出可以休息、卻不會把鳥關住的空間。 他們必須把水拖上山脊，知道幼鳥若突然墜入深谷可能喪命。公司的信卻否認對圍籬外的傷害負有責任。何晏在繩索上綁了提醒標記；陸雀則和鄰村一位曾目睹第一起意外的受訪居民交談。門上的感測器記錄了強風，經理卻從未查看警示燈。

Rain arrived before noon. In the shelter, Lu Que heated a pot of root soup and insisted that everyone eat, including the guard boy. He Yan made a small measuring kite from scraps. He showed how the road had narrowed the upward air current and explained that the flock would avoid it even if the bells were repaired. The boy asked whether their plan would stop the trading station entirely. He Yan admitted he did not yet know. The stew smelled of ginger, and the temptation to remain under the roof was strong. Yet the guard brought a torch and offered to show them the night entrance. He Yan proposed a transformation of the road design, not its destruction: trim the outer wall, return soil to the ridge, and plant shallow-rooted grass. Lu Que was vocal about one condition: the young crane's path to open sky could never be blocked again.

中午前就下起雨。躲雨時，陸雀煮了一鍋根莖湯，堅持每個人都要吃，包括守門少年。何晏用碎布做出小型測量風箏，展示道路如何縮窄上升氣流，也解釋即使風鐘修好了，雲鶴仍會避開這裡。少年問他們的計畫是否要讓交易站完全停工。何晏承認自己還不知道。 燉湯散發薑味，留在屋頂下避雨的誘惑很大。不過守衛拿來火把，主動帶他們查看夜間入口。何晏提議改造道路設計，而不是摧毀它：修短外牆、把泥土還給山脊，並種植淺根草。陸雀明確表達一項條件：幼雲鶴通往開闊天空的路，不能再次被封住。

By sunset the manager returned. He examined the small kite, the sensor log, and the exhausted bird resting beside the cedar. He did not apologize immediately, but he agreed to suspend night traffic until a safer design could be drawn. The boy carried the first shovel of soil back to the ridge. When the crane lifted its head and answered a call from above, everyone fell quiet. He Yan and Lu Que left with more questions, yet one resting place had been saved. At the lower gate, the group tested the new passage with an empty basket. It could carry supplies without disturbing the birds, although the repair would take days. He Yan refused the manager's offer of a private reward; the design belonged to everyone who helped measure the wind. Lu Que approved not with praise but by placing a clean feather inside He Yan's notebook.

日落時經理回來了。他查看小風箏、感測器紀錄，以及在雪松旁休息的疲倦幼鳥。他沒有立刻道歉，但同意在畫出更安全設計前暫停夜間交通。少年把第一鏟土運回山脊。當幼鳥抬頭回應上空的呼喚，所有人都安靜下來。何晏與陸雀帶著更多疑問離開，但至少救下了一處休息地。 在下方大門旁，眾人用空吊籃測試新通道。它能搬運物資而不干擾鳥群，雖然修復還需好幾天。何晏拒絕經理提供的私人獎賞；設計應屬於每位協助測風的人。陸雀沒有口頭讚美，只把一根乾淨羽毛放進何晏的筆記本。
