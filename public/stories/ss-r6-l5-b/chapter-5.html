溫室的表決

The Greenhouse Vote

折春城的第四季：與你一起等春來 · 第 5 / 25 章 · 4000–5000 · 英中對照

The city charter was constitutional in spirit, promising residents a voice in the use of common land. Yet the mayor, an elected democrat who praised public participation, had treated the transfer of winter as a technical episode beyond discussion. Ji challenged that exaggeration before the neighborhood assembly. He explained how a seven-day emergency had become months of transfers without renewed consent. People were angry, but he refused to promise that a single vote could restore spring overnight. The first decision was smaller and real: no more tiles would leave the lower gardens without a record signed by the growers whose soil supplied them.

城市憲章的精神符合憲政原則，承諾居民對公共土地的使用有發言權。然而，這位常讚揚公民參與的民選民主派市長，卻把轉移冬季說成不必討論的技術事件。季懷在鄰里大會上指出這種誇大。他說明一項原定七日的緊急措施，如何在沒有重新取得同意下持續數月。人們十分憤怒，但他不肯保證一次表決就能讓春天隔夜回來。第一個決定較小卻真實：若沒有提供土壤的農人簽署紀錄，不准再把磚從下坡園圃帶走。

Outside the hall, a fleet of delivery wagons stood beside the market. Some merchants defended the system out of greed, while others feared losing the herb crops that supplied winter medicine. Ji offered an incentive to both sides: volunteers would monitor the return of heat, and merchants could observe whether the revised flow damaged their plants. A young seller admitted that his family depended on the greenhouse wages. Ji thanked him for speaking. He had no wish to drive workers into hunger while saving vegetables. Shen added a practical proposal: move one tile first and publish the readings before deciding whether to move the next.

大會堂外，一整隊運貨車停在市集旁。有些商人出於貪婪捍衛制度，另一些則害怕失去供應冬季藥品的草本作物。季懷提出讓雙方參與的誘因：志工監督熱量回流，商人也能觀察修正水流是否傷害植物。一名年輕攤販坦言家人靠溫室薪資生活。季懷感謝他願意開口。他不想為了救蔬菜而讓工人挨餓。沈舟補上實際方案：先移一塊磚，公布讀值，再決定是否移下一塊。

The greenhouse roofs formed a massive white line above the town. Below it, a peasant family showed the visitors a door that had begun to rattle in the endless freeze. Their harvest had failed while shops reported a local recession. The father blamed himself for accepting temporary help from the company; his daughter pointed out that the agreement never mentioned losing spring for a whole season. Ji copied the contract without taking the family's only document. He recognized that shame could keep victims silent more effectively than an official threat. Shen listened to the daughter's account first, because she had been the person who actually read the terms.

溫室的屋頂在城鎮上方連成一道巨大的白線。下坡一戶農家帶訪客看自從冰封不止便開始嘎嘎作響的門。收成失敗，商店又回報地方經濟衰退。父親責備自己收下公司的暫時援助，女兒卻指出協議從未提及讓春天整季消失。季懷抄下合約，沒有拿走這家人唯一的文件。他明白羞恥有時比官員恐嚇更能使受害者沉默。沈舟先聽女兒說，因為當初真正閱讀條款的人是她。

The assembly voted to recruit twelve observers from different streets. Their task was not to supervise retail prices, but to test the spring flow safely. One furious owner threatened to smash the public measuring board. A group of shopkeepers intervened, arguing that the board contained substantial evidence for everyone's future. Shen noticed something in the dispute: the people who disagreed most had begun defending a shared method of finding answers. Ji asked both sides to appoint monitors. By sunset the first team was ready, equipped with lamps, rope, seed trays, and a clear plan for stopping the experiment if anything went wrong.

大會投票決定從不同街道招募十二名觀察員。他們的任務不是監督零售價格，而是安全測試春季熱流。一名憤怒的業主威脅要砸碎公共測量板，幾名店主上前阻止，認為板上保有與所有人未來攸關的重要證據。沈舟發現了爭論中的變化：意見最不相同的人，開始共同守護一種尋找答案的方法。季懷請雙方各派監督員。日落前，第一隊帶著燈、繩、育苗盤和故障停止方案準備妥當。

A careful tactic at the gate kept the crowd behind a painted threshold. The final verdict of the first inspection was limited: one of five tested tiles lacked the required origin mark, and unauthorized transfers were probably more widespread. It did not yet reveal who had removed that mark. Ji was disappointed, though Shen pointed to the green shoot rising in a warmed test tray. The town had gained both a piece of evidence and one living plant. Ji placed his palm near Shen's on the wooden rail without quite touching it. They had reached the end of their first week together, and neither wanted to turn back.

守門處採用一項謹慎策略，讓群眾留在塗色的界線後方。初次檢查的最終判定很有限：五塊受測磚中有一塊缺少必要的來源標記，未經授權的轉移可能更普遍；但還不知道誰抹掉了標記。季懷有些失望，沈舟卻指向暖過的育苗盤中冒出的一株綠芽。城鎮同時得到一份證據與一株活植物。季懷把手掌放到欄杆上，靠近沈舟的手卻沒有真正碰觸。他們已一起走過第一週，誰也不想回頭。

After the vote, Ji walked to the field where the first test shoot had appeared. It was only the length of his little finger, yet he could already imagine its leaves unfolding in summer. Shen joined him with a lamp and asked whether he would be satisfied if the investigation ended with partial repairs. Ji answered that a partial repair made honestly was better than a perfect promise no one could keep. Still, he wanted to understand who had removed the origin marks. Not to punish a stranger for the pleasure of it, but to prevent someone else from repeating the same mistake when a future winter frightened the city again.

表決之後，季懷走到第一株試驗嫩芽冒出的田邊。它只有小指那麼長，他卻已想像到夏天展葉的模樣。沈舟帶燈加入，問他如果調查只得到部分修復，是否會滿足。季懷回答，誠實完成的部分修復，比誰也無法履行的完美承諾更好。但他仍想知道誰抹掉了來源標記，不是為了懲罰陌生人取樂，而是避免下一次冬天再嚇壞城市時，有人重犯相同錯誤。
