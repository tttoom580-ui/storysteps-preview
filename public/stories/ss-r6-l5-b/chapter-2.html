借來熱度的山路

The Road of Borrowed Heat

折春城的第四季：與你一起等春來 · 第 2 / 25 章 · 4000–5000 · 英中對照

用互動版查詞、朗讀與收藏

At sunrise, the seed keeper explained why returning each tile to its original ground would be beneficial. Borrowed heat could protect a crop temporarily, but no tile could convert frost into summer without drawing warmth from elsewhere. A corporate greenhouse had advertised its hill as the ideal destination for rich travelers. Ji spread two maps across a table and noticed that the private road crossed four public vegetable plots. Shen refused to call the owners thieves until they understood the permits. A paid transfer could be legal in an emergency, he reminded Ji, yet an emergency needed an end date and genuine consent from the people who paid its cost.

日出時，種子保管人說明，把每塊磚送回原地為何對大家有利。借來的熱可以暫時保護作物，但任何一塊磚都不能憑空把霜變成夏天；熱量總得從別處取走。企業溫室把山坡宣傳成富人旅行的理想目的地。季懷在桌上攤開兩張地圖，注意到私人道路橫跨四片公共菜園。沈舟在弄清許可之前不肯把業者叫作賊。他提醒季懷，緊急情況下的付費轉移可能合法，但緊急措施必須有終止日期，也必須取得承擔代價者的真正同意。

A rumor described a spreading plant epidemic, so the gate office sent Ji on an errand to inspect the upper gardens. The sight was extraordinary: a wagon had to haul ice away from one field while children played beneath flowering peaches across the fence. A gardener showed them a notebook from the first night of the freeze. The ink recorded eight ordinary deliveries, then a ninth delivery made after the bell stopped. Ji photographed the page with a memory charm whose power would expire by evening. He asked the gardener to keep the original, because the owner of a record should not lose it merely for helping investigators.

有傳言說植物疫病正在蔓延，城門辦事處便派季懷去檢查上坡園圃。眼前景象異常：一塊田需要用車把冰拖走，圍籬另一側卻有孩子在開花的桃樹下遊玩。園丁讓兩人看冰封首夜的筆記。墨跡記下八趟正常運送，鐘停之後又多了一趟。季懷用記憶符拍下那頁，符力到了傍晚就會消失。他請園丁保留原件，因為協助調查不應讓紀錄的主人失去紀錄。

A company lawyer warned that the explorers would accept liability if they crossed the ridge and damaged a tile. Shen disliked the threat, but a sudden plunge into the drainage cut convinced him that the route was dangerous. He tied a rope before Ji followed. The gardener's written reminder mentioned a survey respondent who had complained about missing heat two months earlier. The complaint was never answered. Ji wanted to shout the person's name, yet Shen asked whether the witness had agreed to become public. They left the name covered until they could ask in person, and used the anonymous date to narrow their search.

公司律師警告說，兩人若越過山脊損壞季節磚，就得承擔法律責任。沈舟不喜歡這種威脅，但一腳突然跌進排水溝也證明路線危險。他先繫上繩索，再讓季懷跟上。園丁留下的書面提醒提到一名兩個月前抱怨熱量失蹤的受訪者，抱怨卻從未得到回覆。季懷想喊出那人的名字，沈舟問他證人是否同意公開。他們把姓名遮住，決定親自詢問，只用匿名日期縮小搜尋範圍。

On the ridge, Shen set a simple sensor beside a stone hollow. It registered heat flowing uphill after sunset, exactly when the poor lane needed it most. Ji cooked a thin stew over a safe flame while Shen copied the measurement. The temptation to smash the nearest tile was strong. Instead, Ji watched a patch of moss return to green when Shen briefly shut a maintenance valve. The change proved that warmth could move back without destroying the greenhouse. It also revealed a limitation: opening the valve too quickly would flood the lower path. Their next move needed careful timing, not a dramatic gesture.

山脊上，沈舟在石洞旁放了一枚簡易感測器。它測到熱量在日落後向山上流動，恰好是窮巷最需要熱量的時候。季懷在安全的小火上煮稀燉湯，沈舟則抄寫讀值。砸碎最近的磚很誘人；季懷卻看到沈舟短暫關上一道維修閥後，一小片苔蘚恢復翠綠。這證明不必摧毀溫室也能讓熱回流，卻同時暴露限制：開閥太快會淹沒下方小徑。他們需要謹慎抓準時間，而不是做出戲劇性的舉動。

At dusk, they followed a torch toward a field station built from old orchard boards. Its walls showed the transformation of the region across many winters: young forests, broken terraces, then expensive glass roofs. Shen used a knife to trim a strip of paper for a rubbing of the valve mark. Ji was unusually vocal when the station chief called the crisis a minor inconvenience. He described the seedlings and the child on the ice, then let the measurements speak. The chief agreed to provide a key for one supervised inspection. This was not permission to change the system, but it was the first door opened without a lie.

黃昏時，他們跟著一支火把走向以舊果園木板建成的野外站。牆上記錄了當地跨越數個冬季的變化：年輕樹林、破損梯田，再到昂貴的玻璃屋頂。沈舟用小刀修齊紙條，以拓下閥門記號。站長稱危機只是小小不便，季懷罕見地提高聲量，說起受凍的幼苗與冰上孩子，隨後讓測量數據自己說話。站長同意提供一把鑰匙，讓他們在監督下檢查。這還不是更動系統的許可，卻是第一扇不用說謊便打開的門。

Ji drew a simple line between the two test sites and wrote down everything they still lacked: a clear account of consent, an independent witness, and a safe plan for returning the borrowed warmth. Shen added one more item, the names of the people who would have to live with the results after the investigators left. He did not say the list to make Ji feel guilty. The list was a map of responsibility. Ji folded it into his coat beside the seedlings, where he could touch it when impatience returned. For the first time in months, he believed that waiting could be a form of honest work.

季懷在兩處測試地點之間畫一條線，寫下尚缺的東西：真正同意的說明、獨立見證人，以及安全送回借來熱量的方案。沈舟又加上一項：調查者離開後仍須承受結果的人名。他不是想讓季懷內疚，而是把責任畫成可閱讀的地圖。季懷把紙折好，與幼苗一起放在外衣裡。當急躁再次湧上來，他便能摸到那張紙。數個月來，他第一次相信，等待也可能是一種誠實工作。
