溫灰山脊

The Ridge of Warm Ash

兩個不能飛升的人，接下了送信任務 · 第 2 / 25 章 · 4000–5000 · 英中對照

The road to Reed Village climbed a narrow ridge where an old corporate mine had once paid villagers to haul crystal from the earth. Its abandoned machines now served as rain collectors. Yu believed that a small transformation could be beneficial: convert a cracked water tank into a shared reservoir, and the village might survive another season. Wen was less certain. An epidemic of fever had been reported nearby, and the route's warning flags fluttered like torches in a long line. Their first errand was simple—deliver a letter to the village healer—but a broken bridge forced them to plunge down a rocky slope. At the bottom, a sensor attached to an old flood gate gave an extraordinary warning. The gate measured heat rather than water, and its metal needle pointed toward danger. Wen looked for a torch in their bag. Yu stopped him. “Fire would turn these dry reeds into a wall,” he said. “Sometimes the loudest signal is the one we should not answer.”

通往蘆葦村的路爬上一道狹窄山脊；從前一家礦業公司付錢請村民把晶石從地下拖出。如今廢棄機械成了接雨水的容器。沈嶼相信小小的轉變就能帶來好處：把破裂水槽改成共用蓄水池，村子或許能多撐一季。李聞卻沒有那麼確定。附近傳出發燒疫情，路旁警示旗像一列火把般晃動。他們第一趟差事本來簡單——把信送給村裡的醫者——但斷橋迫使兩人沿亂石坡往下衝。坡底，一只裝在舊水閘上的感測器發出了異常警告。那閘門測的不是水而是熱，金屬指針正指向危險。李聞想從袋裡找火把，沈嶼攔住他：「火會把乾蘆葦變成一面牆。有時最響的訊號，正是不能回應的訊號。」

A girl stood beside the gate with a pot of vegetable stew. Her mother had sent her to feed the workers who still maintained the road. She was a vocal critic of the mine owners, who had left behind a dangerous liability and called it progress. Yu asked her name and learned that she was A-Qing, the healer's niece. The letter was addressed to her aunt. A-Qing wanted to read it immediately, but Wen showed her the ledger and explained that delivery required the named respondent to sign. “My aunt can hardly hold a pen,” she replied. “Then we can take a thumbprint,” Yu said. The girl studied both men, testing the temptation to distrust them. Finally she offered directions through the orchard. She also gave Wen a blunt reminder: the village was not waiting for heroes to rescue it. It was waiting for travelers who would listen, trim their plans to fit real ground, and leave the paths usable afterward.

閘門旁站著一名提著蔬菜燉湯的女孩。她母親派她來餵仍在維護道路的工人。女孩強烈批評礦主，因為他們留下危險負擔，卻把一切稱作進步。沈嶼問她姓名，得知她叫阿晴，是醫者的外甥女。信是寫給她姨母的。阿晴想立刻讀信，李聞便拿出見證簿，解釋必須由指定收件人簽收。「我姨母連筆都拿不穩。」她說。「那就按指印。」沈嶼回答。女孩打量兩人，仍在抗拒或接受之間掙扎，終於指點他們穿過果園。她也直接提醒李聞：村子等的不是英雄來拯救，而是願意傾聽、依照實際地形修正計畫、離開後仍讓道路可用的旅人。

They reached the healer's house at dusk. A-Qing's aunt lay beside the stove, watching ash settle on the floor. Wen placed the envelope in her hand. She smiled before opening it, as if she recognized the paper. It came from her son, who had crossed the mountains months ago. He had found a destination and work repairing wells, but he could not return until the snow melted. He included instructions for adjusting a damaged pump. Yu copied the public technical steps into the village notebook with the woman's permission; the personal words stayed hers alone. When the healer signed the ledger, a tiny bell stone rang once. Wen felt an unreasonable hope that the sound might carry beyond the house. It did not. Yet people arrived, reading the repair instructions aloud, and within an hour they had made a plan to haul the heavy pump into shelter. Information, Wen discovered, could travel farther than the person who first carried it.

黃昏時，他們抵達醫者的住處。阿晴的姨母躺在爐邊，看灰燼慢慢落到地上。李聞把信放在她掌心，她還沒拆封便微笑，彷彿認得那張紙。信來自數月前越山離家的兒子。他在遠方找到工作，替人修井，但必須等雪融才能回家。信內還附上修理損壞水泵的說明。取得婦人同意後，沈嶼把可公開的技術步驟抄進村簿；私人的話仍只屬於她。醫者在見證簿簽收時，一塊小鐘石響了一聲。李聞忍不住盼望聲音能傳出屋外，但它沒有。不過人們陸續趕來，大聲朗讀修理方法，不到一個時辰便商量好把沉重的水泵搬進棚內。李聞發現，資訊能走得比最初攜帶它的人更遠。

The following morning, the villagers asked whether the couriers could deliver a warning about the fever to the next settlement. Yu examined the message and noticed that it named no patients. The healer had described symptoms, clean water, and the need for rest without spreading private information. “This is the kind of reminder a village can use,” he said. A-Qing offered to travel with them as far as the river crossing. Wen worried that an extra companion meant extra responsibility, but she knew the safe paths better than either man. He did not mistake her offer for an obligation. He asked whether she was free to leave her work, and she answered yes after arranging another helper for her aunt. At dawn their new party set out. Behind them, smoke rose from the kitchen as ordinary villagers repaired an ordinary machine. No extraordinary power had saved Reed Village. A letter, a careful decision, and several willing hands had done something more lasting.

隔天早晨，村民問信差能否把發燒警訊送往下一個聚落。沈嶼查看訊息，注意到裡面沒有任何病人的姓名。醫者只描述症狀、乾淨飲水和休息的重要性，沒有散播個資。「這才是村子用得上的提醒。」他說。阿晴願意帶他們走到河口。李聞擔心多一名同行者就多一份責任，但她比他們更熟悉安全的路。他沒有把她的提議視為理所當然，而是問她能否離開工作；她安排好其他人照顧姨母後，答應可以。天亮時，三人上路。身後廚房升起炊煙，普通村民正修著普通機器。沒有任何超凡力量拯救蘆葦村；一封信、一次審慎決定和幾雙肯動手的手，卻留下了更長久的東西。
