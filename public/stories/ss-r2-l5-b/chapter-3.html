水那邊的名字

The Name Across the Water

兩個不能飛升的人，接下了送信任務 · 第 3 / 25 章 · 4000–5000 · 英中對照

用互動版查詞、朗讀與收藏

At the creek below Reed Village, the party found a pier guarded by a deputy from the Valley Office. He announced that all couriers needed a consultation permit, although the order had been issued only that morning. A-Qing suspected an abuse of authority. The deputy showed contempt for anyone who questioned him, and his deliberate slowness made the waiting line stretch into the trees. Yu did not argue immediately. He asked to read the notice, then found that it applied to cargo boats, not pedestrians carrying letters. The deputy's face reddened. “You think you can sneak past our regulations?” he demanded. “No,” Yu replied, “I think we should follow the regulations you actually wrote.” Wen watched the exchange with mixed admiration and worry. The black envelope beneath his coat had no named recipient, and the odds of explaining that to an official seemed poor. He was relieved when the deputy waved them toward the footbridge instead of searching their bag.

在蘆葦村下方的小溪邊，三人碰到一座有山谷署副官駐守的碼頭。副官宣布所有信差都得申請諮詢許可，儘管命令是當天早上才頒布的。阿晴懷疑這是濫用職權。副官對質疑他的人充滿輕蔑，故意拖慢手續，使隊伍一路排進樹林。沈嶼沒有立刻爭論，而是要求看公告，發現規定只適用貨船，不適用徒步送信者。副官的臉紅了。「你想偷偷繞過規定？」他質問。「不，」沈嶼回答，「我認為我們應該遵守你們真正寫下的規定。」李聞又佩服又擔心。外衣底下的黑信封沒有指定收件人，想向官員解釋清楚恐怕不容易。副官最後指向人行橋，沒有搜包，他終於鬆了口氣。

Across the water, a correspondent from the Mountain Gazette was interviewing fishermen about an extinct silver fish. Its loss had changed the creek's ecosystem: insects multiplied while small birds searched farther for food. The reporter wanted a dramatic story about disaster. A-Qing corrected him. “The fish did not vanish overnight. People noticed each phase and sent warnings. Nobody delivered the warnings.” Wen felt the weight of the mail bag. The reporter asked whether the two couriers were successors to the ancient sky messengers. Yu shook his head. “We are not entitled to their trophy,” he said. “We are trying to carry messages that were ignored.” The reporter offered to swap information: he would describe a safe trail through the marsh if they would tell him about the black envelope. Wen refused. A message could help an entire village and still belong to its sender. The reporter accepted that diplomatic answer and drew a useful map in the margin of his notes.

過橋後，一名《山報》通訊員正在訪問漁夫，調查已滅絕的銀魚。魚的消失改變了溪流生態系：昆蟲大量繁殖，小鳥卻得飛得更遠才能覓食。記者想寫一篇戲劇性的災難報導。阿晴糾正他：「魚不是一夜之間消失的。大家留意到每個階段，也寄過警告，只是沒人把警告送達。」李聞感到肩上郵袋更加沉重。記者問兩位信差是否繼承了古代天使的職位。沈嶼搖頭：「我們沒有資格領他們的獎盃，只是試著把被忽略的訊息帶到。」記者提出交換資訊：他願意告知穿過沼澤的安全小路，條件是他們透露黑信封內容。李聞拒絕。即使一封信能幫助整村，它仍屬於寄件者。記者接受了這個委婉回答，並在筆記邊緣畫了一張實用地圖。

That evening the travelers sheltered in a reed hut, where Yu found a mark carved into an old bridge post. It was the same pattern printed on the black envelope: three small circles surrounding an empty center. Yu had seen the symbol on a map during his training, but the instructor had ordered him not to ask about it. Now he understood only that the mark pointed toward the First Post. Wen wanted to notify the nearest village that the trail might be dangerous; A-Qing suggested they first inspect it in daylight. A sustainable journey, she argued, required more than bravery. It required leaving each crossing safe for those who came later. Wen agreed. Before sleeping, he admitted that he had turned back on the Stair of Clouds because he could not bear to step over a frightened child. Yu sat beside him in silence. “You never told me that,” he said at last. “I thought everyone already knew,” Wen answered.

當晚三人在蘆葦小屋避風，沈嶼在舊橋柱上發現一道刻痕。那圖案和黑信封上的一模一樣：三個小圓圍住空白中心。他受訓時曾在地圖看過符號，但老師命令他不要多問。如今他只知道，記號似乎指向初郵站。李聞想通知附近村落小路可能危險，阿晴建議先等天亮再查。她說，能長久走下去的旅程不能只靠勇氣，還得讓後來的人也能安全通行。李聞同意。睡前他坦承，自己在雲梯上回頭，是因為無法踩過一個受驚的孩子。沈嶼沉默地坐在旁邊，終於說：「你從沒告訴過我。」「我以為大家都知道。」李聞回答。

In the morning, the deputy returned with a different face. He had received a complaint from the reporter and now carried a corrected permit notice. The office would allow couriers to cross the footbridge without paying a fee. He did not apologize, but he accepted the mistake. Wen considered the moment a small victory. At the far end of the trail, they discovered a broken message board filled with unanswered requests: a lost child seeking her father, farmers warning about the creek, and a widow asking for news of a missing boat. The board was not a trophy, nor a promise of success. It was evidence of need. A-Qing stayed behind to rebuild it with local workers, choosing her own duty rather than following the men farther. She promised to write once the crossing was repaired. As the two couriers walked on, Yu said, “We have delivered only one letter.” Wen replied, “And now we know where the next hundred should go.”

翌晨，副官帶著截然不同的神情回來。記者已向署裡投訴，他手上拿著更正後的許可公告。官署允許信差免費通過人行橋。他沒有道歉，卻承認了錯誤。李聞把這看成一場小小的勝利。在小路盡頭，他們找到一面損壞的留言板，掛滿無人回覆的請求：走失女孩尋找父親、農夫警告溪流水情、寡婦打聽失蹤漁船。這面板不是獎盃，也不是成功的保證，而是有人需要幫助的證據。阿晴決定留下來和當地工人重建留言板，選擇自己的責任，而不是繼續跟隨兩位信差。她答應修好渡口就寫信。兩人再度上路時，沈嶼說：「我們才送了一封信。」李聞回答：「但現在知道下一百封該送去哪裡了。」

The next turn of the trail tested Wen's gut feeling that the river carried more answers than the officials' notices. He wrote that feeling in his personal notebook, but left it out of the witness ledger. A hunch might guide a question; it could not become evidence simply because he believed it.

小路轉彎後，李聞的直覺愈來愈強烈：河流可能比官署公告藏著更多答案。他把這份直覺寫進私人筆記，沒有寫進見證簿。猜想可以引導提問，卻不會因為自己相信就變成證據。
