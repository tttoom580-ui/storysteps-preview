The Village Verdict

兩個不能飛升的人，接下了送信任務 · 第 5 / 25 章 · 4000–5000 · 英中對照

Beyond the coast stood a valley where the villagers had written a constitutional promise on the door of their meeting hall: no household would lose its water merely because it could not pay. A retired democrat from the old council read the sentence aloud whenever a dispute began. The district had suffered a recession after a fleet of merchant boats stopped calling at its harbor. An influential retailer offered a massive payment in exchange for the village well. Some farmers considered accepting, but a peasant named Bao had kept the original deed and refused. “One rich buyer can drink only so much,” he said. “The rest of us need the water that remains.” Wen and Yu arrived during the newest episode of this dispute, carrying a letter from Bao's sister in the northern hills. The letter contained no magic plan, only a description of a spring and an offer to share farming methods. Yet it became an incentive for the villagers to examine their own choices.

離開海岸後是一處谷地，村民把一項類似憲法的承諾寫在議事廳門上：任何家庭都不會因付不起錢而失去用水權。舊議會的一位民主派退休議員，每逢爭論便會念出這句話。商船隊不再停靠後，地方經濟陷入衰退。一名有勢力的零售商提出鉅款，想買下村井。有些農人想接受，但一位叫阿保的農夫保留著原始地契，堅決反對。「再有錢的人也只能喝那麼多水，」他說，「剩下的水是大家需要的。」李聞與沈嶼抵達時，這場糾紛正進入新一輪爭論。他們帶來阿保北方山區姊姊的信。信裡沒有神奇解方，只有一處泉水的描述，以及分享耕作方法的提議；卻足以鼓勵村民重新思考選擇。

A wooden rattle called everyone to the square. Bao's supporters carried herbs to show how much the valley still produced; the retailer brought a lawyer who spoke about growth and private investment. The lawyer's exaggeration was easy to hear. He said the well would otherwise dry within a week, even though the village survey showed a substantial supply. A teenager tried to smash the painted sign outside the retailer's shop, but Bao stopped him. “Greed is not an excuse for us to become cruel,” he said. Wen admired the restraint. He and Yu had come as couriers, not judges, and neither intended to recruit people into a conflict. They read Bao's sister's message only after Bao granted permission. It described a practical tactic: distribute water by household need, record measurements in public, and allow an independent review of the figures each season. The idea was not new; the courage to discuss it together was.

木製響板召集村民到廣場。支持阿保的人帶來藥草，證明谷地仍有產出；零售商則帶來律師，談經濟成長與私人投資。律師的誇大之詞很容易聽出：他說井水一週內就會乾涸，然而村裡調查顯示水量仍相當充足。一名少年想砸爛商店外的招牌，阿保攔下他。「別人的貪婪，不代表我們就可以殘忍。」他說。李聞佩服這份克制。他和沈嶼只是信差，不是法官，也不打算招募任何人參與衝突。取得阿保允許後，他們才念出姊姊的訊息。信中提出實用策略：依家庭需要分水、公開測量數字、每季讓獨立人士審查。這不是全新的想法；真正難得的是大家願意一起討論。

The meeting lasted until night. At one point the retailer claimed the council had no legal standing, a statement that made the old democrat laugh. He produced the village charter and explained that it gave every resident a vote on shared property. The threshold for a sale was two thirds of all households. Wen kept silent while the villagers debated. Some wanted to fund repairs through small fees; others worried that any charge would become a barrier for poor families. Bao proposed a gradual plan using extra income from herb gardens. His sister's letter had become a starting point, not an order. The retail offer still stood, but it would now face public rules. When the votes were counted, the verdict rejected the sale and required a public accounting of well repairs. A few supporters of the retailer left angrily. Wen noticed that no one celebrated their defeat. Instead, people began organizing the work they had promised to perform.

會議一直開到深夜。零售商一度說村議會根本沒有法律地位，惹得退休議員大笑。他拿出村約，說明共享財產的決定每戶都有投票權，出售須達全村三分之二門檻。李聞在村民討論時始終不插話。有人希望收取少量費用修井，也有人擔心費用最後會成為窮人的障礙。阿保提出用藥草園額外收入逐步修繕的方案。姊姊的信只是討論的起點，不是命令。那份零售業者的收購提案仍在，但如今必須接受公開規則檢驗。票數統計後，結果否決出售，並要求公開修井帳目。零售商的幾名支持者生氣離開，但李聞注意到沒人慶祝他們失敗，大家反而開始安排自己承諾要做的工作。

Yu found Wen behind the meeting hall, sitting beside a jar of fresh herbs. “You could have told them which vote to choose,” he said. Wen shook his head. “The village doesn't belong to us. We only brought one voice back into the room.” Yu considered that answer longer than Wen expected. Then he opened his old training book and showed a handwritten note about the First Post. In the past, every major delivery had to pass a public witness table; a private ruler could not erase a message without leaving evidence. Wen saw a possible connection between that rule and the missing stations. The men decided to search for the original witness tables, not merely the buildings. Before they left, Bao gave them a small packet of dried leaves as thanks. They accepted it as food rather than payment for the delivery. At the valley gate, Yu asked whether Wen had ever wanted to be famous. “Once,” Wen admitted. “Now I would settle for being useful.”

沈嶼在議事廳後找到李聞，他正坐在一罐新鮮藥草旁。「你本來可以告訴他們該投哪一票。」沈嶼說。李聞搖頭：「村子不是我們的。我們只是把一個人的聲音帶回這個房間。」沈嶼想了很久，接著翻開受訓時的舊本子，指給他看一則關於初郵站的手寫註記。過去每次重要投遞都必須經過公開見證桌；任何私人統治者都不能在不留證據的情況下刪除訊息。李聞看出這項規則或許與消失的驛站有關。兩人決定尋找原本的見證桌，而不只尋找建築。離開前，阿保送了一小包乾葉道謝。他們把它當食物收下，而不是送信報酬。走到谷口時，沈嶼問李聞是否曾想出名。「想過，」李聞承認，「如今只希望自己有用。」

News of the vote spread through the valley, where widespread uncertainty about water had made neighbors suspicious of one another. Bao reminded Wen that the purpose of their tactic was not to defeat every opponent. It was to leave a fair procedure that even those who had lost the vote could use next season.

投票消息傳遍山谷，長期而普遍的用水不安曾讓鄰居彼此猜疑。阿保提醒李聞，他們的策略不是擊敗每個反對者，而是留下一套公平程序，讓這次投票落敗的人下季也能使用。
