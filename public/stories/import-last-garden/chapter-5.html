港口地圖

The Harbor Map

最後的花園 · 第 5 / 25 章 · 2000–3000 · 英中對照

The outer decks contained Aurora's old cargo harbor, a long chamber where small ships once carried materials between the main vessel and supply stations. Most docks were locked, but a trader named Kel still worked beside a rough loading ramp. He had a chest filled with broken tools and a medal earned during the launch ceremony. Lena asked whether he could help them reach the relay station. “I used to make a career of finding unusual routes,” Kel answered, “but I have never taken children into empty space.” Noah showed him the painted dome. Kel's face turned pale. “That station has no right to appear on this map.” An athlete from the old training crew had once used this harbor, and Kel said the shuttle route would benefit from the athlete’s precise flight notes.

外層甲板有「極光號」的舊貨運港口，過去小型飛船在主船與補給站之間運送物資。大多數碼頭已上鎖，但一名叫凱爾的商人仍在粗糙的裝貨坡道旁工作。他的箱子裡堆著壞工具，還有一枚啟航典禮獲得的獎章。莉娜問他能否協助前往中繼站。「我以前靠找特殊航道維生，」凱爾說，「但從沒帶孩子闖進空無一物的宇宙。」諾亞給他看畫中的圓頂。凱爾臉色發白：「那座中繼站不該出現在這張地圖上。」一名舊訓練隊的運動員以前用過這座港口；凱爾說，參考他留下的精確飛行筆記會有幫助。

Kel explained that the station had been built by an independent tribe of engineers who refused the governor's scientific claims about Haven. To keep their work secret, they erased the docking records and covered the old route with harmless transport instructions. The true map was hidden inside a children's game called The Knight's Harbor. Noah opened its dusty box. Wooden figures moved across a board paved with silver squares, while an electronic voice read clues aloud. Each clue described a star in the sky. “This is not a game,” Noah said. “It's navigation training disguised as one.”

凱爾解釋，中繼站由一群獨立工程師建造，他們拒絕相信總督對港灣星的科學說法。為了保密，他們刪除停泊紀錄，並以普通運輸指示遮掩舊航線。真正的地圖藏在兒童遊戲《騎士的港口》裡。諾亞打開積灰的遊戲盒。木製棋子在鋪有銀色方格的棋盤上移動，電子聲音唸出線索，每條線索都描述天空中的一顆星。「這不只是遊戲，」諾亞說，「而是偽裝成遊戲的導航訓練。」

A horrible metallic screech came from the dock. A security vehicle had arrived, and its operator demanded that Kel unlock the cargo gate. The children hurried to solve the puzzle. One knight figure pointed toward a cluster of lights, another toward a dark gap. Lena dragged the pieces into position while Noah calculated the angle. The game produced a narrow strip of paper with a route and a lock code. Kel rubbed his eyes. “I have kept that toy for forty years without knowing it could do this.” The officers began cutting the outer gate with a tool that threw bright sparks.

碼頭傳來可怕的金屬尖叫聲。一輛保全車抵達，操作員命令凱爾打開貨門。兩個孩子急忙解開謎題。一枚騎士棋子指向光群，另一枚指向黑暗間隙。莉娜拖動棋子，諾亞計算角度。遊戲吐出細紙條，上面有航線和鎖碼。凱爾揉著眼睛：「這玩具我留了四十年，居然從不知道它能做到這種事。」警衛開始用冒著火花的工具切開外門。

Kel had a choice between turning them in and risking his own freedom. He studied the medal in his palm. “During launch,” he said, “they called us brave because we trusted the mission. Perhaps real courage begins when we question it.” He gave Lena his small cargo shuttle and offered to fly. They loaded flour, water, spare oxygen cylinders, and a repair kit. Noah was eager to leave, but Lena noticed an untested engine connection. She refused to hurry until Kel checked it, even while the gate shook under repeated blows. The faulty cable would have failed within minutes of launch.

凱爾必須選擇出賣他們，還是冒失去自由的風險。他看著掌中的獎章。「啟航時，大家說我們勇敢，因為我們信任任務，」他說，「也許真正的勇氣是從開始質疑它的那一刻算起。」他把小型貨運接駁艇交給莉娜，並表示願意駕駛。他們裝上麵粉、水、備用氧氣瓶和修理工具。諾亞急著出發，莉娜卻發現一處未測試的引擎接線。即使貨門被撞得不停震動，她也堅持讓凱爾檢查。那條壞線若不修好，起飛後幾分鐘就會故障。

When the shuttle lifted, the harbor's alarm reached its highest volume. A beam of white light swept across their hull. Kel steered through an opening in the ship's protective shield and accelerated into the stars. Behind them Aurora became a silver city drifting across black water. Lena had expected freedom to feel wonderful. Instead she felt a painful weight in her chest as her only home grew smaller. “What if we never return?” she asked. Kel did not answer quickly. “Then we must make the journey worth something,” he said at last, and pointed toward a faint blue star.

接駁艇升空時，港口警報音量升至最高。白色探照光掃過船殼。凱爾駛過主船防護罩的開口，加速衝進群星之間。後方的「極光號」像一座漂浮在黑水上的銀色城市。莉娜原以為自由會令人欣喜，卻只感到胸口沉重，因為唯一的家正逐漸縮小。「如果我們再也回不去呢？」她問。凱爾沉默片刻，才說：「那就得讓這趟旅程值得。」然後，他指向遠處黯淡的藍星。

At the edge of the harbor's sensor range, they saw something unexpected. A second shuttle followed them without navigation lights, keeping exactly the same distance through every turn. Kel switched off the engine and let their vessel drift. The unknown craft stopped too. “A friend might use a secret channel,” Noah said. “An enemy would rather stay silent.” A pulse of light flashed once from the pursuing ship, then twice. It was the whistle pattern Lena had heard in the garden. She raised the painting toward the rear camera. For one second the other vessel displayed the image of a blue ribbon. Someone from Aurora knew their route and had chosen to follow.

離開港口感測範圍前，他們看見意想不到的景象。第二艘接駁艇關閉導航燈，始終以相同距離跟在後方，無論怎麼轉彎都不變。凱爾關掉引擎，任小艇漂浮，對方也停下。「朋友可能用祕密頻道，」諾亞說，「敵人則更可能保持沉默。」追蹤船閃出一次光，接著又兩次，那正是莉娜在花園聽到的哨聲節奏。她把畫舉向後方攝影機，對方船體短暫顯示藍色緞帶的圖案。「極光號」上有人知道他們的航線，而且選擇跟來。
