不是傳染病

Not a Disease

聽不見咒語的靈獸醫師 · 第 4 / 25 章 · 1000–2000 · 英中對照

用互動版查詞、朗讀與收藏

By the fourth morning, three deer showed similar signs of pain. The director ordered a check for disease. A nurse examined their blood and found no sign of a common infection. Yue felt relief, but she did not announce that the entire herd was healthy. A sore shoulder could be serious without being contagious. She followed a branch of the path to the older stable and examined the animals that never wore new straps. They moved freely. That comparison mattered, although it did not prove that every new strap was harmful. Chen recorded the difference before anyone changed the equipment.

第四天早上，三頭鹿出現類似的疼痛反應。主任要求檢查是否有疾病。護理師驗了血，沒有發現共同感染的跡象。月鬆了口氣，卻沒有宣布整群鹿都健康。肩部疼痛即使不會傳染，也可能很嚴重。她沿岔路走到舊獸舍，查看從未穿新鞍具的動物。牠們行動自如。這項對照很重要，雖然還不能證明每副新鞍具都有害。辰在任何人更換裝備前先記下差異。

The director wanted to combine two training groups to save staff time. Yue asked him to wait for the measurements. Their population included young deer, older deer, and animals recovering from injury. A single target weight made no sense for all of them. The director listened, then asked whether her work was worth delaying the spring event. Yue showed him a photograph of Moss leaning away from the strap. "I can imagine the cost of a delay," she said. "Can we also count the cost of making the same mistake every day?" The director granted them two more days. Therefore, the director agreed that the first test should protect the animals before trying to satisfy the visitors waiting for a show.

主任想把兩組訓練隊合併，節省人力。月請他等量測結果。鹿群包含幼鹿、年長鹿及正在復原的動物。對所有個體套用相同目標負重並不合理。主任聽完，問她的工作是否值得延後春季活動。月給他看苔避開鞍帶的照片。「我能想像延後的成本，」她說，「但我們是否也該計算每天重複犯錯的代價？」主任多給了他們兩天。 因此主任同意，第一次測試應優先保護動物，而不是急著滿足等著看表演的遊客。

At the city market, a generous vendor offered them a hamburger and a slice of papaya. He said riders bought food there after flying from distant towns. A delivery network linked the academy to the city, and it depended on healthy animals. He showed Yue a wooden sign beside his stall: emergency carts could escape the crowded street by turning left. The sign was old, but its lesson was clear. Systems needed more than one safe route. Yue carried that thought back to the clinic. She would not claim every animal could be replaced by a cart, but a cart could take some of the burden.

在城市市場，一位慷慨的攤商送他們漢堡和一片木瓜。他說騎士從遠方城鎮飛來後，常在這裡買食物。連接學院與城市的配送網絡仰賴健康動物。他指給月看攤旁的木牌：緊急推車可左轉離開擁擠街道。木牌很舊，道理卻清楚：制度需要不只一條安全路線。月把這個想法帶回診所。她不認為每頭靈獸都能被推車取代，但推車可以分擔一部分負重。

The head keeper called the test unfair because the old stables received lighter work. Yue agreed that the teams had different tasks. She suggested a second comparison in which each deer walked without a load for the same short distance. No animal would be forced to fly. The staff could then observe steps, breathing, and willingness to move. One assistant wrote a score beside each observation, but Yue reminded him that numbers did not explain themselves. A score had meaning only when people understood what was being measured, and why a frightened animal might move differently.

總飼育員說測試不公平，因為舊獸舍的工作較輕。月承認兩組任務確實不同，便建議第二次對照：讓每頭鹿在無負重情況下走相同的短距離，不強迫任何動物飛行。員工可以記錄步伐、呼吸與移動意願。一位助理替每項觀察打分，月提醒他數字不會自己解釋。只有知道測量的是什麼，以及受驚動物為何可能走得不同，分數才有意義。

That evening, Chen built a simple model with strips of leather and soft material. He showed how a ring pressed into a shoulder when the strap tightened. Yue watched the model and felt a sense of anger at the wasted days. Yet she knew that anger alone would not help Moss. They made a second ring from smooth wood and compared the pressure with a weight. The new shape spread the load across a wider area. It looked promising, but the nurse said they still needed a controlled trial with a model before fitting it to a living deer.

那天傍晚，辰用皮條和柔軟材料做了簡單模型，示範鞍帶拉緊時金屬環如何壓入肩膀。月看著模型，想到浪費掉的日子，感到憤怒。但她知道光生氣幫不了苔。他們用光滑木頭做第二個環，放上重物比較壓力。新形狀把負重分散到更大的面積，看起來有希望，但護理師說，在裝到活鹿身上前，仍需先用模型完成受控測試。

The director read the new report without speaking. At last he told the nurse to suspend flights for the three sore deer and to examine the rest. That order was not a final victory. The spring trial continued for the healthy animals, and the equipment maker still expected payment. But for the first time, the academy had treated a refusal to fly as a possible warning rather than a crime. Yue looked through the window at Moss, now resting quietly. A different future had not arrived, but a small space for it had opened.

主任一言不發地讀完新報告。最後，他命令護理師暫停三頭疼痛鹿的飛行，並檢查其餘動物。這不是最後的勝利。健康動物仍要參加春季試煉，裝備製造商也仍在等付款。但學院第一次把拒飛視為可能的警訊，而不是罪過。月透過窗戶看著安靜休息的苔。不同的未來尚未到來，卻已經打開一小道空間。
