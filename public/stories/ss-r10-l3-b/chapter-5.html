試錄帶裡的聲音

The Voice on the Test Tape

把地板留給每一種舞步 · 第 5 / 25 章 · 2000–3000 · 英中對照

用互動版查詞、朗讀與收藏

The small hall smelled of bacon from the market downstairs. A faded carpet covered the entrance where the boards were weakest, and several parents had come to complain about the lack of clear information. Mrs. Wu coughed before speaking. She admitted that repairs would take considerable money, even if volunteers helped. Lian suggested a public meeting with written estimates and an engagement plan for residents who could not attend in person. “We cannot ask people to believe a number just because we put it on a poster,” she said. Ron rolled up the carpet so nobody could mistake it for a repair. What had been hidden beneath it still needed attention. Mrs. Wu tried not to cough again while reading the figures, and Lian asked everyone to give her time.

小舞廳飄著樓下市場的培根香味。入口最脆弱的地板上鋪著褪色地毯，幾位家長前來抱怨資訊不清。吳女士開口前咳了幾聲，承認即使有志工幫忙，維修仍需相當可觀的費用。黎安建議公布書面估價，並安排無法親自到場的居民參與討論。「我們不能只因把數字印上海報，就要求大家相信。」她說。羅恩捲起地毯，避免有人誤以為那就是修繕。被遮住的問題仍需處理。 吳女士讀數字時努力忍住再次咳嗽，黎安請大家讓她慢慢說。

In the garage behind the hall, Ron checked an old motor that once moved the stage curtain. He used a flashlight to inspect its housing and found a fake inspection sticker covering an older warning. Lian felt her jaw tighten when she read the dates. A local supplier had claimed the motor was safe, but the sticker had been printed after the company stopped servicing that model. Ron placed a microphone beside the machine to record its noise during a controlled test. His colleague Mike stood back with the emergency switch. Nobody would use the motor in a public performance until a qualified mechanic cleared it. This was not an argument about artistic taste. It was a question of responsibility.

在舞廳後方的車庫，羅恩檢查一具以前用來拉動舞台布幕的舊馬達。他拿手電筒照著外殼，發現一張假的檢查貼紙蓋住較早的警告。黎安看到日期時，下顎不自覺繃緊。地方供應商聲稱馬達安全，但貼紙是在公司停止維修該機型之後才印的。羅恩把麥克風放在機器旁，在受控測試時錄下噪音。他的同事麥克站在緊急開關旁。合格技師確認前，任何公開演出都不能使用這具馬達。這不是藝術品味的爭議，而是責任問題。

During lunch Cora ate a ripe pear and offered the others a bag of peanut snacks. A child nearby tried to pronounce the name of a visiting dance style and laughed at his own mistake. Lian told him that every dancer started by learning unfamiliar sounds and steps. A volunteer carried a sack of old costumes to the cleaning room, where steam rose from a small iron. Among the costumes was a scarf belonging to the hall's first teacher, who had died the previous winter. Mrs. Wu touched it with sudden sorrow. Lian promised they would remember the teacher, but she would not use that grief to push anyone into supporting a risky building plan.

午餐時，可拉吃著熟透的梨子，還分給大家一包花生零食。附近一個孩子試著發音，念出新來舞蹈風格的名稱，自己念錯又笑了。黎安說，每個舞者都從學陌生的聲音和步子開始。一名志工把一袋舊戲服搬進清洗室，小熨斗冒出蒸氣。戲服裡有條圍巾，屬於去年冬天過世的舞廳第一任老師。吳女士摸著圍巾，突然露出悲傷。黎安答應會記得老師，但不會利用這份傷感，逼任何人支持有風險的建物計畫。

In the afternoon, a violent storm struck the hill, sending water under the garage door. The team stopped testing and moved the tapes to a dry shelf. Ron showed Lian that the temporary cables lay above the floor but were still too close to the entrance. A worker could trip on them while carrying heavy equipment. They wrote down the problem with a photograph and changed the route before the next test. The rain also revealed a leak that had not appeared during the earlier inspection. The inspection report would need an update. Lian was disappointed, yet glad the storm had exposed weakness before an audience arrived.

下午，一場猛烈暴風雨襲擊山城，雨水從車庫門下滲入。團隊停止測試，把錄音帶移到乾燥的架子。羅恩指出臨時電線雖架高離地，仍太靠近入口；工人搬重器材時可能被絆倒。他們拍照記錄，並在下次測試前改動路線。大雨也讓原本檢查時沒出現的漏水處現形。檢查報告必須更新。黎安雖然失望，卻慶幸暴風雨在觀眾到場前就揭露弱點。

That evening Lian sent the trust two versions of the budget: one for essential safety work and one for optional stage effects. She marked the second as possible only after the first passed inspection. Cora reviewed the list and added fees for access advisers and performers. Ron agreed to serve as technical lead, but asked that a second volunteer learn every shutoff procedure. He did not want the entire operation to depend on one person's memory. They had lost a day of rehearsal and gained a page of problems, yet Lian felt the project was growing more honest. A strong group could describe its limits without treating them as shameful secrets. They would also engage the people who worked evenings, not merely those free for daytime meetings.

當晚，黎安寄給信託兩版預算：一份是必要安全工程，另一份是可選的舞台效果。她標明第二份必須等第一份通過檢查才可能執行。可拉檢查清單，補上無障礙顧問與表演者酬勞。羅恩答應擔任技術負責人，但要求再訓練一位志工熟悉所有停機程序。他不想讓全部作業依賴一個人的記憶。他們少了一天排練，卻多得到整頁問題；黎安反而感覺計畫更誠實了。成熟的團隊能說明自己的限制，而不把限制當成丟臉的祕密。 他們也會讓夜間工作者參與，而不只找白天有空開會的人。

The storm ended just before midnight. As Lian passed the hall, she saw one lamp burning behind the curtains. Cora was inside, practicing a short turn on the safe section of floor. She stopped when she noticed Lian and asked whether the class would happen at all. “I don't know yet,” Lian answered. “But I will not ask you to dance on a dangerous floor.” Cora nodded, then pointed to the old clock over the door. “We still have seven weeks,” she said. “Use them well.” Lian left with that sentence in her mind, less afraid of the long work ahead than she had been in the morning.

暴風雨在午夜前停了。黎安經過舞廳，看見布幕後還亮著一盞燈。可拉正在安全區域練習短短的轉身。她發現黎安後停下，問課程到底能不能開成。「我還不知道，」黎安回答，「但我不會請妳在危險的地板上跳舞。」可拉點頭，指向門上的舊鐘。「我們還有七週，」她說，「好好用。」黎安帶著這句話離開。比起早晨，她已不那麼害怕接下來漫長的工作。
