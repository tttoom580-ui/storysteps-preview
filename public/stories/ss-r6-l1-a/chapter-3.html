沙地上的線

Lines on the Sand

第七個位置 · 第 3 / 25 章 · 1000以下 · 英中對照

用互動版查詞、朗讀與收藏

The seven players could not use the town's best field because another team had it every day after work. Tess looked at a map and found an open place near the beach, not far from a bus station. A narrow road led past a small bridge and into a flat area of sand. A bird was standing beside a pool of water. "Can we play here?" Ava asked. Tess said they should first ask the person who looked after the land.

七名球員無法使用小鎮最好的球場，因為另一支隊伍每天下班後都會在那裡練習。泰絲查看地圖，發現海灘附近、離公車站不遠有一片空地。一條狹窄道路經過小橋，通向平坦的沙地。一隻鳥站在水窪旁。「我們能在這裡踢嗎？」艾娃問。泰絲說，他們應該先問管理這片土地的人。

The keeper was an older man called Sam. He had been born in this town and knew every change in the beach. He said they could use the flat part for one hour, but they had to keep away from the water when the sea came in. He showed them a clean line where the wet sand began. The team followed him from one side to the other. Sam did not want them to leave shoes or bottles behind. They agreed to take everything home.

管理員叫山姆，是一位年長的男子。他出生於這個小鎮，知道海灘的每一種變化。他說球隊可以使用平坦地面一小時，但漲潮時必須遠離海水。他指給大家看濕沙開始的清楚界線。隊員們跟著他，從一側走到另一側。山姆不希望他們留下鞋子或瓶子。他們答應把所有東西帶走。

Ben put two stones on the sand as goal marks. The stones were too close, so Rose asked him to pull them farther apart. Kai used a stick to make a line. They tried passing the ball to one another without running. The ball did not move as it did on grass. Sometimes it stopped, and sometimes it went to the wrong side. Ava laughed when she fell on her knees. Tess helped her stand and asked if she was hurt.

班把兩顆石頭放在沙上，當作球門標記。兩顆石頭距離太近，蘿絲請他把它們拉開一點。凱用樹枝畫了一條線。大家試著不跑步、只彼此傳球。球在沙上不像在草地上那樣滾動，有時停住，有時偏向另一邊。艾娃跌到膝蓋上時笑了。泰絲扶她站起來，詢問有沒有受傷。

Near a table by the station, a family was having lunch. A small child pointed at the players and asked if a soccer game was about fighting for a ball. Her mother said, "No. It is about playing by rules." Tess heard them and stopped the practice. She told the group that they had been thinking too much about the ball and not enough about each other. "If a player is alone," she said, "we must look for a safe way to help."

車站旁一張桌子附近，有一家人正在吃午餐。一個小孩指著球員，問足球是不是大家搶同一顆球。她的母親回答：「不是，是按照規則一起比賽。」泰絲聽見後，暫停了練習。她告訴大家，他們太在意球，卻不夠注意彼此。「如果隊友落單，」她說，「我們就要想辦法安全地幫助他。」

Ava tried again. This time she looked up before she kicked. The ball went to June, who passed it to Leo, then back to Tess. No one had to run far. The ball moved in a small circle. "That is better," said Rose. "I can see all of you now." A wave came close to their sand line, and Sam called the end of practice. They picked up the stones and left the beach exactly as they had found it.

艾娃再試了一次。這回她踢球前先抬頭。球傳給瓊，瓊傳給里歐，又回到泰絲腳邊。沒有人需要跑很遠，球就在一個小圈子裡移動。「這樣比較好，」蘿絲說，「我現在看得見你們所有人。」一道浪靠近沙地界線，山姆喊練習結束。他們撿起石頭，讓海灘恢復原樣。

Before they left the sand, Sam asked the group to follow him to a small farm path. A cow was standing behind a fence, and the keeper wanted the players to know why they must not open its gate. "If you open it, anything can happen," he said. "The animal may walk into the road, and a driver may not see it." Tess told the others they would use only the public path. June showed the younger child from the station how to stand back from the fence without touching it. The child asked why the team was learning farm rules on a sports day. Tess answered that a field was part of the world, not a room made only for them. The things around a field mattered too. Sam smiled and said they were welcome to return when the beach was safe.

離開沙地前，山姆請大家跟著他走到一條農場小路。一頭牛站在圍欄後方，管理員想讓球員了解為什麼不能打開牛欄門。「如果開了，什麼事都可能發生，」他說，「動物可能走到路上，駕駛也可能看不見牠。」泰絲告訴隊友，他們只會走公共小路。瓊示範給車站遇到的小孩看，如何不碰圍欄、退到安全的地方。孩子問，為什麼運動日還要學農場規則。泰絲回答，球場是世界的一部分，不是只為他們存在的房間。球場周圍的一切也很重要。山姆微笑說，海灘安全時歡迎他們再來。

On the way back, Tess walked across the bridge with her notebook. She made two lists. One was about the field: where the water came in and how long they could stay. The other was about the team: who spoke, who waited, and who noticed someone else. She had thought the first practice would show the best player. Instead, it showed her that a safe place and clear words were more important than a strong kick.

回程時，泰絲帶著筆記本走過小橋。她列了兩份清單。一份關於場地：海水從哪裡進來、可以停留多久。另一份關於球隊：誰有說話、誰願意等待、誰注意到別人。她原以為第一次練習會找出最好的球員，結果發現，安全的場地和清楚的溝通，比一記強力射門更重要。
