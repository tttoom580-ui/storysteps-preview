展區後方的門

The Door Behind the Exhibition

零號月台的三條規則 · 第 2 / 25 章 · 2000–3000 · 英中對照

用互動版查詞、朗讀與收藏

At first light, Iva walked the avenue above the station and studied the museum's public map. An old photograph showed a mother carrying a child in a cradle down the same stairs. Later volunteers had tried to decorate the entrance, but the new panels hid the original elevator shaft. The current exhibition displayed photographs of early travel, yet its most important caption was missing: a narrow service door had once connected the elevator to the underground shelter. The director, Vale, called it an artistic choice. Iva asked why the route had disappeared from a safety map rather than a tourist guide. He answered that the museum only needed to look welcoming. Iva wrote down his exact words and requested the earlier plans, which he said were stored at headquarters.

天亮時，以薇走到車站上方的大道，研究博物館的公開地圖。一張老照片顯示母親抱著搖籃裡的孩子走下同一座樓梯。後來志工試著裝飾入口，但新展板遮住了原來的電梯井。現在的展覽展示早年旅行照片，卻漏掉最重要的說明：從前有一道狹窄的工作門，連接電梯與地下避難空間。館長維爾稱這是藝術選擇。以薇問他，為什麼消失的是安全地圖上的路線，而不只是觀光導覽上的內容。維爾說博物館只要看起來親切就夠了。以薇記下他的原話，要求調閱舊圖，維爾卻說資料全存放在總部。

The upstairs hall displayed a firework festival from the station's opening year. A painted barrel was labeled "one gallon of lamp oil," and a photograph showed a leopard costume worn during the celebration. The arrangement was rich in imagination, but its charm had an unpleasant purpose: the decorations covered a rectangular opening in the wall. Arun removed one loose panel without damaging it. The dark outline behind the painted leopard matched the width of the wheel mark they had measured below. Vale said the discovery was magical, almost as if the station wanted to tell a story. Iva found that an odd description for a mechanical passage. She marked the opening on a copy of the plan and asked Arun to keep photographs of every attachment screw.

樓上的展廳重現車站開幕年的煙火慶典。一只彩繪桶標著「一加侖燈油」，照片裡則有當年慶典的豹裝表演者。佈置充滿想像力，卻也有令人不快的作用：裝飾品蓋住牆上的長方形洞口。阿倫不破壞展板，只卸下一片鬆動的板材。油畫花豹背後的黑色輪廓，恰好與他們在樓下量得的輪痕同寬。維爾說這發現像魔法一樣，彷彿車站正想講故事。以薇認為，把機械通道稱為魔法實在奇怪。她把洞口標在圖上，並請阿倫將每一顆固定螺絲都拍下來。

The volunteer responsible for the organic garden exhibit had tied a green ribbon around the hidden door handle so visitors would not touch it. She said someone had recently spat on the warning sign, then replaced it with a cheerful postcard. A steep stairway went down from that point. Iva asked permission to survey the steps before anybody used them. She counted twenty-seven, with a broken landing after the seventeenth. The volunteer insisted she had been told to call the stairway a "private attraction," not an emergency route. When Iva asked who gave that order, the woman looked toward Vale. He shook his head but did not deny the instruction. The survey showed a direct connection to the red door below, which meant the missing route could not be dismissed as a decorative feature.

負責有機花園展區的志工曾在隱藏門把上綁了一條綠色緞帶，免得參觀者誤觸。她說最近有人在警告牌上吐口水，接著又把警告牌換成歡樂的明信片。門後的樓梯相當陡峭。以薇先申請測量階梯，才讓任何人使用。她數出二十七階，其中第十七階之後的平台已損壞。志工堅稱有人交代她，要把樓梯稱為「私人景點」，不是逃生通道。以薇問是誰下令，女子便看向維爾。他搖頭，卻沒有否認。這次測量顯示樓梯直通下方紅門，證明失蹤的路線並非單純裝飾。

A threat written in pencil appeared on the back of a tourist ticket: "Leave the wall alone, or the opening will be canceled." Vale called it a joke left by a visitor and tried to wipe the pencil away with his thumb. Iva stopped him before he touched the paper. It had a date, a printed serial number, and a blue mark from the museum's office stamp. She placed it in a clean envelope and asked the volunteer to sign a note describing where she had found it. Arun compared the paper with Dara's missing-worker report; both carried the same unusual stamp. The clue did not prove who wrote the warning, but it proved the threat had passed through an office that controlled visitor records. Iva changed the investigation from a private inspection into a formal missing-person safety review.

一張觀光票背面用鉛筆寫著威脅：「別再碰那面牆，否則開幕將取消。」維爾稱這只是遊客開的玩笑，還想用拇指擦掉字跡。以薇及時阻止他。票上有日期、印好的流水號，以及博物館辦公室蓋的藍色印記。她把票放進乾淨信封，請志工簽下發現位置的紀錄。阿倫把票與達拉的失蹤報告比較，發現兩者帶著同一種少見的印章。線索尚不能證明威脅由誰寫下，卻證明票曾經過管理訪客資料的辦公室。以薇因此把私人檢查提升為正式的失蹤人員安全調查。

The volunteer took Iva aside after Vale left and said she had seen a contractor spit angrily at the warning sign the night before it was replaced. She could not remember his name, only that his helmet had a silver mark. Iva thanked her but did not add the memory to the official account until the volunteer agreed to make a separate statement. A single description can help begin a search, but it should not turn every worker in a silver helmet into a suspect. Arun checked the entrance cameras and found that three crews had used similar helmets. They would compare work schedules before contacting anyone. Meanwhile, the stairway remained closed by a barrier placed at both ends, with a notice explaining the actual missing rail and damaged landing instead of a mysterious prohibition. The museum's hidden door was becoming a documented safety issue rather than an attraction.

維爾離開後，志工把以薇叫到一旁，說警告牌更換前一晚，曾看見承包商憤怒地朝牌子吐口水。她想不起姓名，只記得安全帽有銀色記號。以薇道謝，但在志工同意另作陳述前，沒有把記憶列入正式說法。單一描述可以作為追查起點，不能讓每個戴銀色安全帽的工人都變嫌疑人。阿倫查看入口監視畫面，發現三支工班都用類似安全帽。他們會先比對排班再聯絡任何人。樓梯兩端則都設置護欄，告示明確說明缺少扶手與平台破損，而不是神祕禁令。隱藏的門逐漸成為有文件的安全問題，而非景點。
