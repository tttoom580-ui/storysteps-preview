車輪上的數字

The Number on the Wheel

零號月台的三條規則 · 第 5 / 25 章 · 2000–3000 · 英中對照

用互動版查詞、朗讀與收藏

An athlete from the museum's volunteer group remembered the freight cars better than most visitors. He had trained here for a local benefit race and once considered a career repairing historic vehicles. Iva asked him to stand beside the dark carriage without crossing the safety line. With his hand on his chest, he recalled the sound of an old motor pulling heavy loads. That memory became a clue when Arun compared the present noise with a recording from the volunteer's phone. The car moved with the same slow pull each time, never with a human driver's adjustments. Iva refused to drag the volunteer into the pit for a closer look. Instead, she sent the recording to a rail technician outside the museum and asked for an independent opinion.

博物館志工團裡一名運動員比多數參觀者更熟悉貨車。他曾在這裡為公益賽跑訓練，一度考慮以修復歷史車輛為職業。以薇請他站在黑色車廂旁，但不得跨越安全線。他手按胸口，回想老馬達拖動重物的聲音。當阿倫把現在的聲響與志工手機舊錄音比較時，這份回憶成了線索。車廂每次都以相同的緩慢拉力移動，沒有駕駛會做的細微調整。以薇拒絕把志工拖進檢查坑近看，而是把錄音送給館外的鐵道技師，請對方獨立判斷。

A young cook who delivered food to the station was eager to help. He asked whether he should erase the marks on the floor: his flour sacks had left a white trail the previous evening. Iva asked him to leave the trail untouched until Arun had photographed it. They had been unloaded from a van coming from the harbor. On the delivery receipt, the museum's address had been changed by hand from the public entrance to the sealed freight door. The driver recalled a horrible scraping noise after his last delivery. It had sounded like a knight dragging armor across stone, though he knew that image came from a costume show in the hall. The actual cause was simpler: an unmarked wheel had passed over the rough edge of a newly laid metal plate. Iva traced the white flour to the place where the plate began.

一名替車站送餐的年輕廚師急著幫忙。他問是否該擦掉地上痕跡：前一晚他載來的麵粉袋留下了白色粉路。以薇請他等阿倫拍照後再處理。貨物從港口方向開來的貨車卸下。送貨收據上，博物館地址被人手寫改成封閉的貨運門，而非公開入口。司機記得最後一次送貨後有可怕的刮擦聲，像騎士拖著盔甲走過石頭；他也明白那個比喻只是因為大廳正在辦服裝表演。真正原因更簡單：沒有標示的車輪，輾過新鋪金屬板的粗糙邊緣。以薇沿著白色麵粉痕，追到金屬板的起點。

The metal plate concealed a lock that could be released from a narrow inspection slot. A medal mounted above the slot commemorated workers who had helped pave the old platform. Its plaque described the rough first winter and the scientific improvements that followed. Two names mattered: a trader who donated iron and a member of the local tribe who mapped underground springs. Both had insisted that the route remain public during emergencies. Their signatures appeared in a bound volume beneath the plaque. The present museum plan showed the route as private storage. Iva copied the page with its date and seal. She now had proof that an exit had been turned into something else on paper, even if its physical walls had not moved.

金屬板蓋住一把鎖，得從狹窄的檢查孔才能鬆開。孔上方掛著一枚獎章，紀念當年幫忙鋪設舊月台的工人。獎牌說明提到第一個艱困冬天，以及後來的科學改善。兩個名字特別重要：一名捐鐵的商人，及一名替地下泉水測繪的在地部落成員。兩人都堅持緊急情況下通道必須對公眾開放。他們的簽名收在獎牌下方裝訂的一冊記錄裡。現在的博物館圖面卻把通道標成私人倉庫。以薇拍下有日期與印章的頁面。她終於有證據證明出口在紙面上被改成了其他用途，即使實際的牆壁並未移動。

The independent rail technician called before sunset. A narrow groove on the wheel, visible in Arun's photograph, identified the carriage as a demonstration machine controlled by a fixed cable. It could travel only a short distance and could not carry passengers safely. Vale had described it as a restored train, but its permit covered motion inside a locked exhibit. Iva ordered the public track closed. It was a small achievement, not a complete rescue: Dara remained missing, and the red door was still barred. Nevertheless, the group had learned something important. The moving carriage was not a ghost, the wheel was not new, and someone had recently made the locked exhibit look like an ordinary service. Iva placed her copy of the old volume beside the altered plan and prepared to ask who had authorized that change.

日落前，館外鐵道技師回電。阿倫照片裡車輪上的狹窄凹槽，證明那是由固定鋼索控制的展示機器。它只能移動很短一段距離，完全不適合安全載客。維爾稱它為修復完成的列車，但許可證只允許它在封閉展區內移動。以薇要求立即關閉公共軌道。這是小小的進展，還不是完整救援：達拉仍失蹤，紅門仍被閂住。不過他們確實學到重要的事。移動的車廂不是鬼，車輪也不是新的；最近有人刻意讓封閉展品看起來像普通服務列車。以薇把舊冊子的影本放到遭修改的圖旁，準備追問究竟是誰批准更動。

The day's result gave the search team a new plan. They would no longer wait for the carriage to appear and then chase it like a strange visitor. Instead, they would locate the cable's control room, preserve the operating log, and find the permit that allowed the display to move. The volunteer who recognized the motor wrote his account in his own words rather than copying Iva's summary. That mattered because a witness should not be taught what to remember. Arun covered the flour trail with transparent protective sheets, leaving enough space for workers to move safely around it. The independent technician confirmed that the wheel groove matched an exhibit built long after the original station had closed. A modern machine had been made to look ancient. Somebody had used that appearance to distract people from the safety changes beside the track.

當天成果讓搜索隊改變計畫。他們不再等車廂出現後像追奇怪遊客一樣追它，而要找出鋼索控制室、保存操作紀錄，並取得允許展示車移動的許可證。辨出馬達聲的志工用自己的話寫陳述，沒有照抄以薇摘要。這很重要，因為證人不該被教導該記得什麼。阿倫用透明保護片蓋住麵粉痕，同時留下安全工作空間。獨立技師證實車輪凹槽屬於老車站關閉很久以後才建造的展示設備。一部現代機器被做成古老模樣，有人利用這種外觀分散注意，讓人忽略軌道旁的安全改動。
