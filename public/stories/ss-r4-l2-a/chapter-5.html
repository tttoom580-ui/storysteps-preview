The Missing Inspection

The report about the blocked air line became a burden for Lin. She could not repair it herself, and the town had little money for a second team. A teacher from the nearby college offered to help the workers make a list of each expense. They found that the station had paid double for a small set of metal parts. One feature of the payment record bothered Tao: the seller’s name did not match the company marked on the boxes. The public machine should function for everyone, yet nobody could explain who had approved the purchase. Lin asked for the records without calling anyone a thief.

風道遭堵的報告，成了琳沉重的負擔。她不能自己修好它，而鎮上幾乎沒錢再雇一組人。附近學院的老師提議協助工人列出每筆支出。他們發現車站為少量金屬零件付了兩倍價錢。付款紀錄有個細節讓陶不安：賣家的名字與箱子上標示的公司不同。公共機器理應為所有人服務，卻沒有人能說明誰批准了採購。琳要求查看紀錄，沒有先指控誰是小偷。

A fellow student showed Lin an old film of the station’s production room. In it, a woman in a green sweater stood beside a machine while children played soccer outside. She was the engineer who had built the last safe valve. She seldom appeared in town stories because the mayor’s grandfather had taken credit for the work. Lin did not want a perfect hero to replace another hero. She wanted to learn how the valve had been selected and tested. The engineer’s notes described a selection of springs, each with a different strength. If the workers could find the correct one, the air line might open safely.

一位同學拿給琳看車站生產室的老影片。畫面裡，一名穿綠毛衣的女子站在機器旁，外頭孩子正在踢足球。她是製造最後一只安全閥的工程師。鎮上的故事很少提起她，因為鎮長的祖父把功勞算在自己頭上。琳不想用一位完美英雄取代另一位英雄，她想知道閥門當年如何選擇與測試。女工程師的筆記描述數種強度不同的彈簧。如果工人找到正確的一種，就可能安全開啟風道。

While they searched, a lady from the public kitchen brought soup and a box of tissue for workers with colds. Tao almost swallowed a piece of hard bread too quickly and began to cough. The cook made him sit down. “Your spirit is useful,” she said, “but you must rest.” Lin helped sweep the floor after lunch. By the door someone had placed a printed warning that a terrorist group might attack the station. It had no date, signature, or source. Lin photographed the notice and asked the guard where it came from. He admitted it had been passed from hand to hand without proof.

他們搜尋時，公用廚房的一位女士送來熱湯與面紙，給感冒的工人使用。陶差點把硬麵包吞得太快，咳了起來。廚師叫他坐下。「你的精神很可貴，」她說，「但你需要休息。」午餐後琳幫忙掃地。門邊有人貼出印刷警告，說恐怖組織可能攻擊車站。上頭沒有日期、簽名或來源。琳拍下告示，問衛兵是誰拿來的。他承認那只是人傳人，毫無證據。

The old public computer still worked. Lin decided to upload the inspection dates, the payments, and the unconfirmed warning as separate records, so readers would not mistake a rumor for a finding. The mayor’s clerk arrived and said the files belonged to his office. Lin asked him for the rule that made public payments secret. He could not produce one. The clerk left angry, but a worker told Lin she had not broken any law by making copies of public figures. She wrote down the worker’s advice beside the file. An open record was not a weapon to hurt someone; it was a way to let errors be corrected.

舊公用電腦還能運作。琳決定把檢查日期、付款資料與未經證實的警告分開上傳，讓讀者不會把傳聞當成結論。鎮長的書記走來，說這些檔案屬於他的辦公室。琳請他指出哪條規定使公共支出必須保密，他拿不出來。書記氣沖沖離開，一位工人卻告訴琳，複製公開數字並不違法。她把工人的提醒寫在檔案旁。公開紀錄不是傷人的武器，而是讓錯誤能被修正的方法。

Tao returned to the kitchen to apologize for making a mess. The cook gave him a piece of soft bread and told him to swallow slowly this time. Lin took the chance to ask whether she had seen any workers carrying new metal boxes to the station. The cook remembered a delivery during a storm, but she did not know what was inside. Rather than turn her memory into a claim, Lin added the date to the search list and asked whether the kitchen had kept its receipt. It had. The receipt named the same company as the double payment. Lin thanked the cook and explained that the receipt was another link to check, not proof of anyone’s guilt.

陶回廚房為弄亂環境道歉。廚師給他一塊軟麵包，叫他這次慢慢吞嚥。琳趁機問她是否看過工人把新金屬箱送往車站。廚師記得風暴期間有過一次送貨，卻不知道箱內有什麼。琳沒有把這記憶當成結論，而是把日期加入待查清單，問廚房有沒有留收據。有。收據上是同一家收取雙倍付款的公司。琳道謝，說這是另一條待核對的線索，還不足以證明誰有罪。

That night Lin found the green-sweater engineer’s final note tucked inside a folder. It listed three tests and ended with a plain sentence: “Never replace a valve without checking the air path behind it.” A later hand had crossed out the sentence. The change was small, but it explained why an unsafe repair might have passed inspection. Tao wanted to search the mayor’s office at once. Lin said no. They could request the missing inspection sheet through the public hall. The request might be refused, but a refusal with a date and signature was evidence too. For the first time, the missing page looked less like an accident.

那晚，琳在資料夾裡找到綠毛衣女工程師的最後一張筆記。上面列出三項測試，最後一句寫著：「未檢查閥後風道，不可更換閥門。」後來有人把這句話劃掉。改動很小，卻解釋了不安全的維修為何可能通過檢查。陶想立刻搜查鎮長辦公室，琳說不行。他們可以透過公會堂要求缺失的檢查表。即使被拒，有日期與簽名的拒絕也算證據。第一次，那張失蹤的紙看起來不像意外。
