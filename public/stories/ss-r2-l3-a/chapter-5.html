上鎖的收藏室

The Locked Collection

這座圖書館，禁止替別人作答 · 第 5 / 25 章 · 2000–3000 · 英中對照

Mira followed the old program to the collection room by the harbor. Some materials were delivered from outside the city and recorded by traveling traders. A drawer was marked with a medal no one had earned. Another held records of an athlete whose career ended after a false accusation. The pattern was not random: people who had asked difficult questions were later described as unreliable or dangerous. June had been studying the same collection.

米拉循著舊節目單來到靠港口的收藏室。有些資料由外地運來，交給旅行商人登錄。一個抽屜畫著沒人贏得的獎牌，另一個保存一名運動員遭到不實指控、職涯被毀的資料。這些不是隨機事件：提出棘手問題的人，日後都被形容成不可靠或危險分子。小君一直在研究同一批收藏。

Tomas showed her how to check whether a lock had been changed. The newest screws were clean, while the wood around them was rough and old. Inside, a folded report compared original statements with edited answers. Hale’s office had called the changes a benefit to readers, saying simple stories were easier for the city to accept. But the report included crossed-out names. Mira photographed the pages in place before touching anything.

托瑪斯示範如何判斷鎖是否更換過。新螺絲乾淨，周圍木頭卻又粗又舊。抽屜裡有一份摺好的報告，把原始證詞和編修後的答案並排比較。黑爾辦公室聲稱修改有益讀者，因為城市比較容易接受簡單故事。但報告裡滿是被劃掉的名字。米拉先在原位拍照，才碰觸紙張。

The fifth collection held records of an athlete who had hoped to build a career in the city. Her chest hurt after a race, but the doctor saw no clue of serious illness. A dishonest official tried to drag her name through a scandal. She was eager to explain, yet someone had used an eraser to erase her statement. A cook who worked with flour near the harbor saw the official enter a private office and leave with a sealed letter. The cook called the scene horrible, but could not say what the letter contained. Elsewhere, a costume designer had made a knight's armor for a play about a lost medal. The stage door had a lock that was changed without notice. Workers had been promised that the city would pave the rough road leading to the theater, but the funds had been diverted. A scientific report showed the uneven surface had caused several accidents. A trader who supplied paint said the workers belonged to different communities, not one tribe as the manager had written. A volume of signed receipts supported the trader's claim. Mira placed each fact on a separate slip. To build an argument, she needed a sequence of real events, not a collection of words chosen to make a powerful person look innocent or guilty. She understood why June had been so careful with dates.

第五批收藏記錄一名希望在城裡發展職涯的運動員。她比賽後胸口痛，醫生卻沒找到嚴重疾病的線索。不誠實的官員企圖把她的名字拖進醜聞。她急著解釋，有人卻用橡皮擦掉她的陳述。一名在港口附近用麵粉工作的廚師看見官員走入私人辦公室，出來時拿著密封信。廚師說那一幕很可怕，卻無法說出信裡內容。另一處，服裝設計師為一齣遺失獎牌的戲製作騎士盔甲。舞台門鎖在沒有通知下被更換。工人原本獲承諾城市會鋪平通往劇院的崎嶇道路，資金卻被轉走。科學報告指出不平路面造成數起意外。提供油漆的商人說工人來自不同社群，不是經理報告上寫的同一部落。厚厚一冊簽署收據支持他的說法。米拉把每項事實寫在獨立紙條上。要建立論點，她需要真實事件的順序，而不是挑選能讓權貴顯得無辜或有罪的詞句。她明白小君為何那麼仔細記日期。

The envelope Mira chose was ordinary brown paper, and she wrote its contents on the outside before sealing it. There were photographs of the drawer, copies of the report, and a note listing where each item had been found. She also recorded what she had not touched. Tomas laughed gently and said she was making more work for herself than most official investigators. Mira answered that the extra work was a small price to pay if it kept someone from dismissing June's evidence. They placed one packet in Ada's custody and one with a community lawyer who agreed to store it without claiming it proved more than it did. Mira had learned to distrust the promise of a dramatic solution. The library's wrongs had developed over many years, through forms, payments, and decisions that looked small when viewed alone. Undoing them might require the same kind of patience. The discovery changed her plan: she would no longer search only for June's name. She would trace the whole chain of transfers.

米拉選了普通棕色信封，封口前先在外面記錄內容：抽屜照片、報告副本，以及每項物件的發現地點。她也寫下自己沒有碰過什麼。托瑪斯輕笑，說她替自己製造的工作比多數官方調查員還多。米拉說，只要能讓小君的證據不被輕易否認，多做一點就值得。他們一份交給艾達保管，另一份交給同意存放、不誇大其證明力的社區律師。米拉已不再信任戲劇性解決方案。圖書館的錯誤歷經多年，由看似微小的表格、付款和決策累積而成，要拆解它們也需要耐心。這次發現改變了她的計畫：她不再只找小君的名字，而要追查整條轉移鏈。

This was their first complete set of supporting documents. Mira placed copies in separate envelopes and asked Tomas to keep one outside the library. She refused his offer to carry the originals: the evidence belonged where it had been found until a proper review. Instead, they agreed on two independent records of every discovery. Trust, she thought, could be built through careful work, not blind faith.

這是他們拿到的第一組完整佐證文件。米拉把副本分裝在不同信封，請托瑪斯把一份留在圖書館外。他說可以帶走正本，她拒絕了：正式審查前，證據應待在原位。兩人約定，每一項發現都保留兩份獨立紀錄。信任可以靠仔細做事建立，而不是盲目信仰。
