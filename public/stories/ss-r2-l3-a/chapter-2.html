The Map in the Margin

這座圖書館，禁止替別人作答 · 第 2 / 25 章 · 2000–3000 · 英中對照

The second evening, Mira found the catalogue room open. Tomas, the young caretaker, was replacing a lamp and pretending not to watch her. He had worked at the library for six years and had learned that silence could keep a person safe. Mira showed him the red thread. He touched it only after asking permission, then indicated four marks printed in the margin of June’s record. They looked like mistakes in the paper. Under stronger light, they formed a path from the entrance to an elevator used by staff.

第二晚，米拉發現目錄室開放了。年輕的管理員托瑪斯正在換燈，裝作沒有看她。他在圖書館工作六年，早已學會用沉默保護自己。米拉拿出紅線。托瑪斯先徵得她同意，才碰那條線，接著指出小君紀錄頁邊的四個記號。記號原本像印刷錯誤，放在亮光下卻連成一條路，從入口通往員工專用電梯。

Tomas warned that Hale counted the pages every night. No one was forbidden to read the catalogue, but removing a page would be treated as theft. Mira made a rubbing with a pencil instead. On the lower floor, the elevator stopped before its buttons showed any number. A glass cabinet displayed a city exhibition from thirty years earlier: citizens had asked the library to preserve questions the council refused to hear. One photograph contained June’s red-thread mark, though June had not been born then.

托瑪斯警告她，黑爾每晚都會清點頁數。目錄可以閱讀，但撕走頁面會被當作偷竊。米拉便用鉛筆拓印。到了地下樓層，電梯在樓層燈還未顯示數字時便停下。玻璃櫃展示三十年前的城市展覽：市民曾要求圖書館保存議會拒絕回答的問題。一張照片裡也有小君的紅線記號，可那時小君根本尚未出生。

The next file followed a broad avenue from the library to an old hospital. An exhausted mother rocked a cradle while volunteers tried to decorate the waiting room with paper flowers. A broken elevator delayed medicine, and a charity exhibition was postponed. On the roof, a firework fell into a tank holding a gallon of water, yet the newspaper reported a serious fire. The hospital headquarters had accepted that report without asking any witness. One child's imagination turned the noisy evening into a tale of a leopard escaping from a cage. Another child called the rescue magical, but the nurse wrote that nothing magical had happened: volunteers had simply carried patients downstairs. Mira found the odd difference between those stories important. In a cupboard, organic seeds had been packed in small bags tied with ribbon for patients to plant later. An angry visitor had spit on the floor during a quarrel about a steep bill. A public survey described the visitor as a threat, while a tourist who watched the scene said the visitor was only frightened. Before leaving, the tourist used a cloth to wipe water off the stairs, helping the nurse move safely. The account did not make everyone innocent. It showed why a short official summary could hide actions that changed the meaning of an event. Mira copied the dates from the hospital records and marked the place where the headline first departed from what the witnesses described.

下一份檔案追著一條寬闊大道，從圖書館通往舊醫院。疲憊的母親搖著搖籃，志工用紙花裝飾候診室。壞掉的電梯耽誤藥物運送，慈善展覽也延期。屋頂上，一枚煙火落進裝著一加侖水的水箱，報紙卻寫成嚴重火災。醫院總部沒有詢問證人就接受了報導。一個孩子靠想像力把吵鬧的夜晚說成花豹逃籠的故事。另一個孩子說救援很神奇，但護士寫下：沒有任何魔法發生，只是志工把病人背下樓。米拉認為這些奇怪差異很重要。櫥櫃裡放著有機種子，以緞帶綁成小袋，準備讓病人日後栽種。一名生氣的訪客在爭論高昂帳單時朝地上吐口水。公共調查把訪客形容成威脅，但目擊的觀光客說他只是害怕。離開前，觀光客還用布擦乾樓梯的水，協助護士安全通行。紀錄不是要證明每個人清白，而是說明簡短的官方摘要可能掩蓋改變事件意義的行動。米拉抄下醫院紀錄日期，標出新聞標題第一次偏離證詞的地方。

The photograph troubled Mira for another reason. It showed a crowd standing beside the very desk where Hale worked, but the desk was painted white instead of black. Someone had changed the furniture, the rules, and perhaps the meaning of the place. Tomas told her that a building could be altered without looking new; a fresh coat of paint was often enough to make an old door seem permanent. He did not claim to know who made every change. In fact, he said, his job was easiest when he kept track of what had truly been repaired. Mira admired that modest standard. She asked him for copies of maintenance orders rather than explanations about hidden powers. They checked the numbered photographs against the printed exhibition guide and found one page missing. The guide's contents showed that a section on public signatures ought to occupy that space. Mira copied the empty page number into her notebook. A missing page was not proof of a crime, but it told her where to look next.

照片還有一點讓米拉不安：人群站在黑爾如今工作的桌旁，但桌子當時是白色，不是黑色。有人改了家具、改了規則，也可能改變這地方的意義。托瑪斯說，一棟建築即使外表沒有變新，仍可能被改得面目全非；一層新漆就足以讓舊門看似理所當然。他沒有聲稱知道每一項變更是誰做的。其實他說，當他只記錄真正修過什麼，工作反而最順利。米拉欣賞這種謹慎。她要的是維修命令副本，不是關於神祕力量的解說。他們把編號照片和展覽手冊逐一對照，發現少了一頁。目錄顯示，那個位置應是公開簽名的章節。米拉把缺頁頁碼記進筆記本。少一頁不能證明犯罪，卻能指向下一個調查方向。

Mira realized the sign might be older than her sister. She did not let herself invent a magical explanation. The photograph had a date, a name, and a visible label; those were facts she could test. When she returned upstairs, Tomas had left her a piece of plain paper. Across it he had written, “A copied question is not the same as an owned question.” The words sounded like advice, but also like a warning.

米拉明白那個記號可能比妹妹還古老。她不允許自己隨便編出魔法的解釋。照片有日期、姓名和標籤，那些才是能檢驗的事實。回到樓上時，托瑪斯留給她一張白紙，上面寫著：「抄來的問題，不等於自己擁有的問題。」這句話像建議，也像警告。
