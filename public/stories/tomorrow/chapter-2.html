紅圍巾

The Red Scarf

明天，別來學校 · 第 2 / 25 章 · 1000以下 · 英中對照

用互動版查詞、朗讀與收藏

I went to school. Of course I did. At sixteen, you can be afraid of something and still be more afraid of missing the thing everyone else will see. I told myself I was going because of the science test. I even carried my science book in my hand, as if the book could prove it. The rain had stopped, but the steps were still wet.

我還是去了學校。當然。十六歲的人，可以害怕一件事，卻更害怕錯過所有人都會看到的事。我告訴自己是為了自然考試，甚至把課本拿在手上，好像它能作證。雨停了，階梯卻還是濕的。

Leo waited outside the front gate. His hair stood up on one side, and he held two bags of bread. He gave me the warmer one. “You look terrible,” he said. “Thank you. You look like a broken brush.” We almost smiled. Then the first bell rang. A red scarf fell from the second-floor window and landed on the stone head beside the gate.

Leo 在前門外等我，頭髮一側翹起，手上拿著兩袋麵包。他把較暖的那袋給我。「妳看起來很慘。」「謝謝，你像壞掉的刷子。」我們差點笑了。這時第一聲鐘響，一條紅圍巾從二樓窗口掉下，落在門邊的石像頭上。

Three students ran toward it. Someone had pinned a white card to the scarf. I could see a large black square on it: a code for a website. Phones rose all around us. One girl laughed. Another said, “They started again.” Leo caught my arm before I could walk closer. He did not pull hard. That somehow made me stop faster. “You knew,” he said.

三個學生跑過去。有人在圍巾上別了一張白卡，上面有個大黑方塊：網站的掃描碼。四周的手機都舉起來。一個女孩笑了，另一個說：「又開始了。」我正要靠近，Leo 拉住我的手臂。他沒用力，反而讓我更快停下。「妳早就知道。」

I showed him the second message, but I kept the voice file closed. His face changed. “Who else can use your Later account?” “Nobody.” “Did you ever share your password?” “It was a class project. We all used the same one at first.” He closed his eyes. We both remembered the paper on the classroom wall: Welcome123. No one had told us to change it.

我讓他看第二則訊息，卻沒打開語音。他臉色變了。「還有誰能用妳的 Later 帳號？」「沒人。」「妳分享過密碼嗎？」「那是班級作業，我們一開始都用同一組。」他閉上眼睛。我們都想起教室牆上的紙：Welcome123。沒人叫我們更換。

The code opened Zero, a page that had no school name but somehow belonged completely to our school. The first vote asked: Who should lose their place on Friday's stage? There were four names. June's was at the top, even though she had not been here for weeks. Mine was second. A gray line under the list said, No names. No trouble. Just the truth.

掃描碼連到 Zero。網頁沒有校名，卻完全屬於我們學校。第一題投票問：誰應該失去星期五上台的資格？下面有四個名字。June 排在最上面，儘管她已幾週沒來。我排第二。名單下方的灰字寫著：匿名，沒麻煩，只有真相。

I had seen that line before. Six weeks ago, it had sat above June's video. I remembered watching numbers grow beside her name. I remembered feeling lucky that my name was not there. Today a blue bar grew beside mine. Thirty-one percent. Thirty-four. My chest felt too small. “Close it,” Leo said. “Watching the number won't make it smaller.” But I could not look away.

我看過那句話。六週前，它就在 June 的影片上面。我記得她名字旁不斷增加的數字，也記得我慶幸自己的名字不在上面。今天藍色長條在我的名字旁變長：百分之三十一、三十四。胸口像太小了。「關掉它，」Leo 說，「盯著數字，它也不會變小。」但我移不開眼睛。

Kai came down the steps with his bright stage smile. He was student host for Friday's school show, and teachers trusted him to hold any microphone. “People are bored,” he said, looking at the scarf. “Don't feed it.” He took the card away before a teacher reached us. His hand moved quickly, folding the code into his pocket. “You two should get inside.”

Kai 帶著燦爛的舞台笑容走下階梯。他是星期五校慶表演的學生主持人，老師願意把任何麥克風交給他。「大家只是無聊，」他看著圍巾說，「別理它。」老師走近前，他收走了卡片，動作很快，把掃描碼摺進口袋。「你們兩個該進去了。」

My phone shook in my hand. Another Later message. See? Now you believe me. The next sentence was longer. At lunch, your name will reach fifty percent. Find the blue door before then. I looked up at the second floor. Every window was closed except one. Behind it, for half a second, I saw a sleeve the same yellow as June's old raincoat.

手機在手裡震動，又是 Later。看吧，現在相信了嗎？下一句較長：午餐時，妳的票數會到百分之五十。在那之前找到藍門。我抬頭看二樓，所有窗戶都關著，只有一扇例外。半秒間，我看見一隻袖子，顏色像 June 舊雨衣的黃色。
