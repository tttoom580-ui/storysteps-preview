雲上的道路

The Road Above the Clouds

遊戲降臨後，我只想開一家修理店 · 第 3 / 25 章 · 3000–4000 · 英中對照

On the third morning, a message reached the shop from the mountainous northern district. Its small clinic had lost its air filter, and a gray dust storm was moving toward the building. Lin followed the old road with Niko and Imani, a nurse who had offered to help. The route seemed safe until a wall of blue light rose across the bridge. A sign called it a safety barrier, but there was no guard to ask for permission. Lin watched the sign blink three times and noticed that its words changed with the wind.

第三天早上，北方山區傳來一則求救訊息。那裡的小診所失去了空氣濾網，而一場灰色塵暴正朝建築物移動。林澄和尼可、主動加入的護理師伊曼妮一起沿著舊路前進。路線原本似乎安全，直到橋上升起一面藍色光牆。標誌稱它為「安全屏障」，現場卻沒有守衛可以詢問通行方法。林澄看見標誌閃爍了三次，也注意到上面的文字會隨風改變。

A boy on the other side called to them. "The bridge opens for people carrying useful supplies," he shouted. Lin set down her tool bag, and the barrier stayed closed. Imani showed her medical kit; a narrow opening appeared. "It seems to evaluate what we carry, not who we are," Lin said. The boy gave them a twig from a tree nearby and asked them to hold it against the blue light. Nothing happened. Then Niko lifted the clinic's broken filter frame, which they had come to replace, and the path opened fully.

橋的另一側有個男孩朝他們喊：「橋只讓帶著有用物資的人通過！」林澄放下工具袋，屏障依然關閉。伊曼妮拿出醫療包，牆上才出現一道狹窄的缺口。「它好像是在評估我們攜帶的物品，而不是我們的身分。」林澄說。男孩遞給他們附近樹上的一根小樹枝，要他們放到藍光前試試，卻毫無反應。接著尼可舉起他們準備更換的診所舊濾網框架，道路便完全打開了。

Inside the clinic, patients were coughing, and one old man was beginning to choke on the thick air. The doctor explained that the dust's composition included tiny metal particles. Most people had only mild symptoms, but the elderly could become dangerously ill. The clinic's fan had failed after an accidental power surge. Lin inspected its bearings and found something stranger than a burned wire: the official maintenance panel had been removed weeks earlier. "That was neglect," she said. "The storm did not cause the first problem."

診所裡的病人不停咳嗽，一名老先生甚至開始被濃厚的灰塵嗆得喘不過氣。醫師說明，塵埃的成分含有細小金屬顆粒。大多數人只有輕微症狀，年長者卻可能出現危險。診所風扇是在一次意外電壓突增後故障的。林澄檢查軸承，卻發現比燒壞電線更奇怪的問題：正式的維修面板早在幾週前就被拆走了。「這是疏於維護。」她說，「最初的問題不是塵暴造成的。」

The clinic director, a retired philosopher named Doctor Han, listened without trying to defend himself. "I signed the removal order," he admitted. "The city promised a new machine, and I believed them." Niko looked ready to offend him with an angry reply, but Imani spoke first. "We need the patients to breathe. Blame can wait." Doctor Han lowered his head. His face held more than shame; there was a grave fear that he had helped destroy the place he wanted to protect. Lin asked for the old maintenance books.

診所主任韓醫師曾經研究哲學，退休後又回來管理這間診所。他聽完沒有急著替自己辯護。「拆除命令是我簽的。」他承認，「市府答應會換新機器，我相信了。」尼可正準備憤怒地說些可能冒犯他的話，伊曼妮卻先開口：「我們得先讓病人能呼吸。責備可以等一下。」韓醫師垂下頭。他臉上不只有羞愧，還有一種沉重的恐懼：自己可能親手傷害了最想保護的地方。林澄向他索取舊維修紀錄。

The records showed that the missing panel once measured both air flow and energy use. Lin used the fan's old parts to build a temporary filter and asked Imani to monitor the patients. Niko wanted to work faster, but Lin stopped him. "Haste has a tendency to make small mistakes expensive," she said. "We must protect the people first." The repair cost all four of her daily work credits and earned only seven energy points. Yet the air began to clear. A woman squeezed Lin's hand in gratitude, and the old man could finally speak.

紀錄顯示，失蹤的面板原本能同時測量空氣流量和能源使用量。林澄利用風扇舊零件製作暫時性的濾網，請伊曼妮監測病人的狀況。尼可想加快速度，林澄卻阻止了他。「太著急通常會讓小錯誤付出昂貴代價。」她說，「我們必須先保護人。」這次修理耗掉她一天的四點工作額度，卻只得到七點能量。但空氣開始變得清澈。一位女子感激地握住林澄的手，老先生也終於能說話了。

Outside, Vale's men arrived with an offer to replace the clinic's machines if the director surrendered the land. Their leader claimed that strict private control was the only answer to the city's brutal new rules. Some residents agreed. Others demanded revenge for the missing panel. Lin urged them to avoid fighting. "The missing part and the ownership notice are connected," she said. "If we damage the clinic ourselves, we will lose the records we need." Doctor Han nodded. He chose to keep the clinic public, even though Vale promised that decision would cost him protection.

屋外，維爾的手下帶著條件來了：只要診所主任交出土地，他們就願意替換機器。領頭的人聲稱，面對城市現在殘酷的規則，只有嚴格的私人控制才能解決問題。部分居民贊同，另一些人卻想替失蹤的面板報仇。林澄勸大家不要打起來。「失蹤的零件和產權公告彼此有關。」她說，「如果我們自己破壞診所，就會失去需要的證據。」韓醫師點頭。他選擇讓診所繼續屬於公眾，即使維爾威脅他會因此失去保護。

Doctor Han led the group into a small records room. There, Lin found a map labeled COMMON MAINTENANCE OFFICE, the same name Niko had uncovered beneath the paint. Its branches reached every district in the city, and the central office was marked under the old railway museum. On the back of the map someone had written, "A system without public intelligence cannot serve the public." Han remembered a group of engineers who had argued for reform before the disaster. The city had shut their program down. Lin folded the map carefully. She finally had a destination.

韓醫師帶著三人進入一間小檔案室。林澄在裡面找到一張標示「公共維護辦公室」的地圖，與尼可在油漆底下發現的名字完全相同。辦公室的分部延伸到全城各區，而總部則被標在舊鐵道博物館地下。地圖背面有人寫著：「沒有公共資訊的系統，就無法為公眾服務。」韓醫師想起災變前有一群工程師曾要求改革這套制度，市府卻關閉了他們的計畫。林澄小心摺起地圖。她終於知道下一步該去哪裡。
