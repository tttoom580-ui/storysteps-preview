# 鈴果村的百變遊戲日
# The Many Ways to Play in Bellberry Village

> 拾頁 StorySteps｜Level 1（1000 字以下詞彙級距）｜1B｜第六版全新獨立故事

**題材：** 毛絨動物村莊 × 自創遊戲 × 公平參與 × 友誼成長。

**故事簡介：** 羊駝妮拉和穿山甲米洛發現，村裡的賽跑只能讓跑得快的人展現本領。他們邀請朋友共同設計遊戲日，從不同玩法、清楚標示到安全入口，一步步學會傾聽、測試和修正。活動當天，入口突然需要修理，大家必須做出真正負責任的決定。

**故事規則：** 動物們像人一樣上學、生活及工作；遊戲由參與者自行選擇，任何人可休息或停止；場地安全、重物與緊急事項由負責的大人處理。本故事不使用魔法解決問題。

## CHAPTER 01｜The Race on the North Lawn｜北草地上的賽跑

*英文正文：651 詞；英中段落：6 組；正式新詞：20 詞*

### 正文與翻譯

**EN**

On the north lawn of Bellberry Village, a red ribbon lay beside a painted wooden circle. A small paper star hung above the picnic tables. A friendly woman named Mrs. Lark had brought apple juice and a camera. She wanted a picture of the village's weekend picnic games. Nela, a young alpaca with soft brown hair, stood in the middle of the lawn with her friend Milo, a small pangolin. A bug crossed Milo's pet snail box. Nela moved it gently to the grass. Then the village leader called, “Run to the wooden circle!” Everybody ran except Milo. He reached the last table long after the others.

**繁中**

在鈴果村北邊的草地上，一條紅緞帶放在畫過顏色的木圓環旁。野餐桌上方還掛著一顆小紙星星。友善的雲雀太太帶來蘋果汁和相機，想拍下村子週末的野餐遊戲。年輕的羊駝妮拉頂著柔軟的棕色毛髮，和小穿山甲米洛站在草地中央。一隻小蟲爬過米洛裝寵物蝸牛的盒子，妮拉輕輕把牠移到草地上。接著村長喊：「跑到木圓環那裡！」大家都跑了，只有米洛慢慢走。他比其他人晚很久才抵達最後一張桌子。

**EN**

A boy said that Milo had lost because he was slow. Milo smiled, but his shoulders fell. Nela noticed that he had helped the smallest children find the right path. “That is a fact,” she told the others. “He helped three people. Why does our game only count fast feet?” The leader said that the rules were only for today, and new games could be made for the future. Nela did not want to lie and say the race had been fun for everyone. She asked if they could use the afternoon to design a game in which different skills mattered.

**繁中**

有個男孩說米洛輸了，因為他太慢。米洛雖然笑了，肩膀卻垂了下來。妮拉注意到他曾幫最小的孩子找到正確的路。「這是事實，」她對大家說，「他幫了三個人。為什麼遊戲只算腳跑得快不快？」村長說今天只是試玩，以後可以訂新規則。妮拉不想說謊，假裝每個人都玩得很開心。她提議利用下午設計一種能讓不同本領都派上用場的遊戲。

**EN**

The children made a short test of two different ways to reach the painted wooden circle. One path asked players to hurry, while the other invited them to notice small details hidden along the way. A young guest found a leaf shaped like a heart, and Milo found a line of tiny stones. Neither child knew which answer should count. Nela asked them to explain what they had seen. The guests began talking instead of racing. “This is not a rule yet,” Nela reminded them. “But it is a sign that a good game might ask more than one question.” They kept both paths on the drawing.

**繁中**

孩子們短短測試了兩種抵達畫過顏色的木圓環的方法。一條路要玩家加快腳步，另一條路則邀請玩家注意沿途隱藏的小細節。一位年幼客人找到心形葉子，米洛發現一排小石頭。兩人都不知道哪個答案才算數。妮拉請他們說明自己的觀察，客人們開始交談，不再只是比快。「這還不是正式規則，」妮拉提醒，「但這表示好遊戲也許不只有一種問題。」他們把兩條路都保留在圖上。

**EN**

Mrs. Lark asked Nela why the game needed two circles. Nela explained that helping a younger child and reaching the finish line were different actions. She did not know whether they should earn the same kind of point. Milo suggested they avoid points until they had asked more children. A little visitor said she would like to look for hidden shapes, while her older brother wanted to run. The two argued, then stopped when Nela offered them separate choices. Mrs. Lark took a picture of the new drawing, not of the children, and promised to ask before sharing it.

**繁中**

雲雀太太問妮拉，遊戲為什麼需要兩個圈。妮拉解釋，幫助年幼孩子和到達終點，是兩種不同的行動。她還不知道應不應該算成同一種分數。米洛提議，等詢問更多孩子後再決定是否計分。一位小訪客說想尋找隱藏圖形，哥哥則想跑步。兩人吵了一下，妮拉提供不同選項後才停下。雲雀太太拍下新畫的圖，而不是孩子的照片，並答應分享前會先詢問。

**EN**

Later, the group sat around a small table to talk about the first race. Nela asked every helper to give one clear example instead of simply calling the work good or bad. They remembered that they watched how different children reached the wooden circle. Milo wrote the example on a clean card and put a small question mark beside the parts they still did not know. They talked about what would happen if a stranger arrived without knowing any of their plans. A person might be shy, tired, or ready to ask a question. Nela said that a friendly game needed a clear answer, not a long speech. The group agreed to keep both the fast and the slow path in the plan. Before moving on, they read the card once more and gave each other time to ask for a change.

**繁中**

稍後，小隊圍著小桌子討論第一次賽跑。妮拉請每位助手提出一個清楚例子，而不只是說作品好或不好。他們記得：他們觀察不同孩子怎樣抵達木圓環。米洛把例子寫在乾淨卡片上，還在尚未確定的地方畫個小問號。大家討論，如果陌生訪客什麼計畫都不知道就走進來，會發生什麼事。那個人可能害羞、疲倦，或正準備提問。妮拉說，友善的遊戲需要清楚的回答，而不是長篇演說。小隊同意把快速與慢速兩條路都保留在計畫中。接著大家再次閱讀卡片，也給彼此時間提出修改意見。

**EN**

Milo did not ask to be given first place. He asked for a real part in the game. That was an important difference. Nela drew two circles on a paper plate: one for speed, and another for helping others. The children argued about whether a game could have more than one kind of success. The woman with the camera put her picture away and listened instead. When it was time to go home, the wooden circle was still on the field. Nela wrote a question beside it: “Can this be a day when everybody belongs?”

**繁中**

米洛並沒有要求別人直接把第一名送給他。他只希望自己在遊戲裡有真正能做的事。這兩件事很不一樣。妮拉在紙盤上畫了兩個圈，一個代表速度，另一個代表幫助別人。孩子們爭論一場遊戲是否能有不只一種成功方式。拿著相機的女士收起相片，改為仔細聆聽。回家時，木圓環仍留在草地上。妮拉在圓環旁寫下一個問題：「這能不能成為每個人都屬於其中的一天？」

## CHAPTER 02｜An Invitation for Anyone｜給每個人的邀請

### 正文與翻譯

**EN**

The next morning, Nela carried a piece of paper to the third apartment above the village bakery. Her aunt lived there on a soft sofa and could not walk far. “May anybody come to Game Day?” her aunt asked. Nela was going to guess, but stopped herself. The question was serious. She wrote it with a pen instead of making a promise. At lunch, Milo came late from the pond, where he had helped a young duck. He looked at the question and said, “If we invite anyone, we must make the games easy to reach.”

**繁中**

隔天早上，妮拉拿著一張紙，到村裡麵包店樓上的第三間公寓探望阿姨。阿姨坐在柔軟的沙發上，沒辦法走太遠。「任何人都能參加遊戲日嗎？」阿姨問。妮拉本來想猜個答案，卻停了下來，因為這是嚴肅的問題。她用筆把問題寫下來，而不是隨便答應。午餐時，米洛從池塘那裡晚到了；他剛幫過一隻小鴨。米洛看到問題便說：「如果我們邀請所有人，遊戲就得讓大家容易走到。」

**EN**

They visited the village hall beside the old church. A reporter from the local paper was asking about the coming celebration. He wanted a clear answer about the cost. The village leader said no one would have to pay to enter, and every activity would be free. Nela used the hall telephone to call her aunt and tell her the news. She almost forgot to ask about the seats, but Milo touched her sleeve. They added a new line to the invitation: “Here is a place to sit, and here is a place to rest.”

**繁中**

他們來到舊教堂旁的村民會堂。地方報紙的記者正詢問即將舉辦的活動，想知道是否要收費。村長明確回答：不必付錢就能進場，所有活動都免費。妮拉用會堂的電話打給阿姨，告訴她這個消息。她差點忘記問座位，幸好米洛輕碰她的袖子。他們在邀請函上加了一行：「這裡有座位，也有休息的地方。」

**EN**

Before they finished the invitation, Nela asked her aunt if she might forget a rule after reading it once. Her aunt laughed. “Of course. I forget things like anybody else.” Milo suggested putting the most important information on the front: the place, the time, and the fact that visitors could leave whenever they wished. Nela asked a younger cousin to read it aloud. The cousin found the words easy but wanted a picture of the entrance. They added one without covering the rest of the text. The invitation was getting clearer because the children had stopped writing only for themselves.

**繁中**

完成邀請函前，妮拉問阿姨會不會讀過一次就忘記規則。阿姨笑了。「當然會，我跟任何人一樣都會忘記事情。」米洛建議把最重要的資訊放在正面：地點、時間，以及訪客想離開時可以自行離開。妮拉請年幼的表親大聲讀一遍。表親覺得文字容易懂，卻希望有入口的圖。他們加上一張圖，也沒有遮住其餘文字。邀請函越來越清楚，是因為孩子們不再只替自己寫。

**EN**

The bakery owner offered to leave the invitation on the counter, but Nela first asked whether it would block the place where customers put their bread. They moved the paper to a small board by the door. A parent came in and read the notice twice, then asked if there would be somewhere to wait with a young child. Milo drew a chair beside the invitation. The parent thanked him but pointed out that a picture was not enough; an actual chair had to be there. Nela added that question to the list for the hall keeper.

**繁中**

麵包店主願意把邀請函放在櫃台，妮拉卻先問是否會擋住顧客放麵包的位置。他們把紙移到門邊的小板上。一位家長進來，把通知讀了兩遍，詢問能否帶年幼孩子在某處等待。米洛在邀請函旁畫上椅子。家長謝謝他，也提醒光有圖還不夠，現場真的得有椅子。妮拉把這個問題加入準備向會堂管理員詢問的清單。

**EN**

For another short test, Nela put a blank sheet beside the notes about the public invitation. She asked Milo to describe what a new visitor could see with no help. The group noticed that the older guests read the time but asked about places to sit. Filo was ready to make a quick choice, but Cora asked him to wait until everybody had spoken. **繁中**

為了再做一次簡短測試，妮拉在公開邀請函的筆記旁放了張空白紙，請米洛說明新訪客在沒人幫忙時能看見什麼。小隊注意到：年長訪客讀懂時間後，仍詢問座位在哪裡。費洛急著做決定，可拉請他等每個人都說完。即使答案不是他希望的，他仍仔細聽。雲雀太太提醒孩子，遊戲可以刺激好玩，同時替需要更多時間的人留出空間。最後大家決定在多印邀請函前先向會堂管理員確認真正的椅子，並把這項決定放在下一位助手找得到的地方。規則只有在大家知道並願意遵守時，才能真正發揮作用。

**EN**

The reporter offered to print a notice, but he asked whether the event was a sports race. “Not exactly,” Milo answered. “We are still finding out what it can become.” Nela liked that honest answer. She asked the leader for permission to talk with families across the country road and at the small pond. The leader agreed, provided they stayed with an adult. At the end of the day, Nela found three different needs on her list: a slower path, larger signs, and a quiet corner. None of them could be solved by calling a single child the winner.

**繁中**

記者願意刊登通知，但他問這是否是一場運動賽跑。「不完全是，」米洛回答，「我們還在找出它能變成什麼樣子。」妮拉喜歡這個誠實的回答。她向村長提出，希望在大人陪同下，到鄉間道路另一邊和小池塘旁詢問家庭的意見。村長答應了。一天結束時，妮拉的清單已有三種不同需求：較慢的路線、更大的標示，以及安靜的角落。這些需求都不是選出一個冠軍就能解決的。
