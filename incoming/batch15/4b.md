# 拾頁 StorySteps｜4B《1997，青潭泳池的第六條水道》

**The Sixth Lane at Qingtan Pool**

- 級別：4B，第4級（3000–4000字級）
- 題材：1997–1999年代女性職涯重啟、公共泳池、運動競賽與家庭自主；無CP
- 形式：25章完整英文故事，每段英文後緊接完整繁體中文翻譯；各章附指定詞表和兩道三選一閱讀理解測驗

**故事簡介：**三十六歲的前游泳選手沈若青因肩傷離開賽場，返鄉接手瀕臨轉為私人俱樂部的青潭公共泳池。第六條水道成了菁英訓練與一般居民需求的爭奪焦點。面對漏水、工傷風險、比賽延期和兩次暴雨，她必須學會在支持選手夢想的同時，保障每個人的安全與選擇；母親也準備結束多年無薪工作的生活。

## CHAPTER 01｜The Sixth Lane｜第六條水道

### 正文與翻譯

**EN** On a fierce evening in September 1997, Shen Ruoqing returned to Qingtan Pool carrying one suitcase and a folder of coaching notes. Her mother lived in a small cottage behind the swimming complex, with a blue cushion on the kitchen chair. The city had asked Ruoqing to give a presentation about reopening the pool, but no one had promised her a job. She had once been a promising athlete. A shoulder injury ended her competition years before she could become a champion. Now thirty-six, she possessed experience but little money. Her mother was pessimistic: the pool had lost members, and a private club wanted its valuable sixth lane.

**繁中** 一九九七年九月的一個風雨猛烈的傍晚，沈若青帶著一只行李箱和游泳教學筆記回到青潭泳池。母親住在泳池後方的小屋，廚房椅子上擺著藍色坐墊。市府請若青提出重新開放泳池的簡報，卻沒有人承諾聘用她。她曾是備受期待的運動員，但肩傷在她成為冠軍之前就終結了競賽生涯。如今三十六歲的她有經驗，卻沒有多少錢。母親很悲觀：泳池流失了會員，私人俱樂部還想要占用珍貴的第六條水道。

**EN** At the entrance, veteran lifeguard He Jingyi had assembled a diverse group: an older swimmer, two parents, an anxious teenager, and Du Xiaotang, a young instructor who could not hear every announcement clearly. 'Diversity is not the problem,' Jingyi said. 'A single set of rules for everyone is.' A businessman named Xu Mengzhe arrived with a glossy visual chart. He promised a tremendous increase in income if the sixth lane became an exclusive training area. He tried to flatter Ruoqing by calling her a genius. She did not smile. The chart contained no reference to the people who used the lane for slow rehabilitation.

**繁中** 入口處，資深救生員何靜宜集合了一群不同需求的人：一位長者、兩名家長、一名焦慮的少年，還有無法清楚聽見每次廣播的年輕教練杜小棠。「多樣性不是問題，」靜宜說，「把同一套規則套在每個人身上才是。」商人徐孟哲帶著精美的視覺圖表前來，承諾只要把第六道變成專屬訓練區，收入就能大幅增加。他奉承若青是天才。她沒有笑。那張圖表沒有引用任何使用該水道進行緩慢復健者的資料。

**EN** During a light workout, a child dropped a paper parachute from the viewing stand as part of a school science project. It floated into the shallow end, and two children gave a nervous giggle. Jingyi removed it before anyone slipped. The simple incident revealed a larger issue: the viewing stand was open while the pool was supposedly closed for inspection. Ruoqing wrote 'possible safety violation' in her notebook. Xu said this was an unfair defense against his proposal. Ruoqing replied that protecting his reputation was not the same thing as protecting swimmers. A witness could confirm the open gate, but only an inspection could establish how it had happened.

**繁中** 一次輕度運動訓練時，一名孩子為了學校的科學作業，從看臺丟下一個紙降落傘。它飄到淺水區，兩個孩子緊張地輕笑。靜宜在有人滑倒之前把它拿走。這件小事卻暴露更大的問題：泳池理應封閉檢查，看臺的門卻還開著。若青在筆記本寫下「可能違規」。徐孟哲認為她故意找藉口反對提案。若青回答，保護他的名聲和保護泳客是兩回事。目擊者可以確認門開著，但只有檢查能查明原因。

**EN** The next morning, Ruoqing walked past a spiritual gathering in the park, where neighbors were offering wishes for the pool's future. She respected their hopes but wanted evidence as well. She asked Xiaotang to convey a simple warning on large cards: no one should enter the water until the gates and drains were examined. That afternoon she made her first choice. She would not accept a contract based on projected revenue. She would prepare a public safety plan, including the people the private club had forgotten. Her mother watched from the office doorway. 'You know how to swim fast,' she said. 'Do you know how to persuade people who are tired of waiting?' Ruoqing admitted she did not yet know.

**繁中** 隔天早晨，若青經過公園裡的祈福聚會，鄰居們正為泳池的未來許願。她尊重他們的期盼，但同樣想要證據。她請小棠用大型字卡傳達簡單警告：在閘門與排水口完成檢查前，任何人都不能下水。當天下午，她做了第一個決定。她不會接受只依預估收入制定的合約，而要提出一份包含被私人俱樂部忽略者的公共安全計畫。母親站在辦公室門口看著她。「妳知道怎麼游得快，」她說，「可是妳知道怎麼說服已經等累的人嗎？」若青承認，她還不知道。

**EN** Before going to bed, Ruoqing returned to the office and studied the old timetable. Lane Six had been listed as a rehabilitation lane only on Wednesday mornings, hours when most workers could not attend. She imagined the older woman she had seen at the gate, holding a towel and waiting for permission to enter. Her own shoulder tightened at the memory of the injury that ended her racing career. It had taken her years to accept that a slower body was still a capable body. When she started to write a new schedule, she stopped herself. She had promised to listen before deciding. On a fresh page she wrote three questions: Who uses the lane now? Who wants to use it but cannot? What must change so that more people can use it safely? She placed the page beside the financial proposal and left the rest blank. In the kitchen, her mother had made tea and set out two cups. They talked for a while without trying to solve the future. It was the first quiet evening they had shared since Ruoqing came home, and she wanted to remember it.

**繁中** 睡前，若青回到辦公室研究舊課表。第六水道只在星期三早上被列為復健水道，而那正是大部分上班族不能來的時間。她想起門口那位拿著毛巾、等待獲准入場的老婦人。想到終結競賽生涯的傷，她的肩膀又緊繃起來。她花了多年才接受：速度慢下來的身體仍然有能力。若青剛要寫新課表便停下，她曾承諾先聽再決定。她在新紙上寫下三個問題：現在誰使用這條水道？誰想使用卻不能？要怎樣改變才能讓更多人安全使用？她把紙放在財務提案旁，其他部分留白。廚房裡母親泡好茶，擺出兩個杯子。母女聊了一會兒，沒有急著解決未來。這是若青回家後第一次和母親共度安靜夜晚，她想記住這段時光。

### 理解測驗

**Q1. Why does Ruoqing refuse to sign the private club agreement immediately?**

A. She wants to enter a national race.  
B. The proposal ignores some swimmers and safety checks.  
C. She plans to sell the pool to a hotel.  

**答案：B。** 私人俱樂部的提案忽略復健泳者和必要安全檢查，若青決定先提出公共安全計畫。

**Q2. What does Ruoqing write on a fresh page at night?**

A. Questions about who uses Lane Six and who is excluded.  
B. A list of medals from her old career.  
C. A plan to close every public pool.  

**答案：A。** 她先記下現在誰在用第六水道、誰無法使用，以及如何安全改善，而不是直接決定課表。

## CHAPTER 02｜The Wall Beneath the Water｜水下的牆

### 正文與翻譯

**EN** Ruoqing invited county engineer Lin Zonghe to inspect the pool before any contract meeting. He spread an old landscape map on a table and marked the extent of the original building. Under the modern tiles stood part of a stone fort constructed in an earlier era; a nearby museum had a fossil display describing the layers beneath the hill. The connection was interesting, but Ruoqing asked whether it affected the drains. Zonghe smiled at the practical question. He pointed to a delicate joint where two kinds of pipe met. Their combination had survived many winters, yet the newest repair might have made the joint less flexible.

**繁中** 若青邀請縣府工程師林宗和在任何合約會議前檢查泳池。他在桌上攤開舊地形圖，標出原建築的範圍。現代磁磚底下曾有一部分古老石堡的遺跡；附近博物館的化石展也介紹了山坡底下的地層。這種關聯很有趣，但若青問的是它是否影響排水。宗和對這個務實的問題笑了笑，指出兩種管材相接的一個脆弱接縫。這個組合撐過許多個冬天，但最新一次修理可能讓接縫失去彈性。

**EN** The maintenance room held a poster from an atomic science exhibition. It explained that an atom was far too small to see with the naked eye, while a leak could be measured by ordinary instruments. 'We cannot gaze into concrete and guarantee it is sound,' Zonghe said. They listened to the low murmur of water running behind the wall. Ruoqing asked for a pressure test rather than a dramatic guess. Jingyi worried about the psychological effect of another delayed opening. 'People have heard guarantees for years,' she said. 'Every promise made without a test becomes another reason for them not to trust us.' Ruoqing agreed to revise the public announcement that evening.

**繁中** 維修室裡留著一張原子科學展的海報，說明原子小到肉眼看不見，但漏水卻能用普通儀器測量。「我們不能只是盯著混凝土看，就保證它安全。」宗和說。他們聽著牆後水流低沉的聲音。若青要求進行壓力測試，而不是憑想像猜測。靜宜擔心再次延後開放造成心理影響。「大家已聽過太多保證，」她說，「每個沒經過測試的承諾，都讓人更難相信我們。」若青答應當晚修訂對外公告。

**EN** A messenger from the old rice mill delivered a letter about the site's history. The mill's owner described a voyage that his grandparents had taken to reach Qingtan, along with families who later worked in immigration offices and railway workshops. He wanted the pool to retain a small public history wall. Xu Mengzhe dismissed the request as unproductive. Ruoqing thought differently. A public pool was not merely a place for elite athletes; families had intimate memories of learning to float here, and those memories belonged to the community. She promised no museum that the budget could not support, but she put the old photographs on the agenda.

**繁中** 舊米廠派來一名送信人，帶來一封關於土地歷史的信。米廠主人寫到祖父母當年來到青潭的旅程，以及後來在移民事務處與鐵路工場工作的家庭。他希望泳池保留一面公共歷史牆。徐孟哲認為這項要求沒有生產效益。若青卻不這麼想。公共泳池不只是菁英運動員訓練的地方；許多家庭在這裡第一次學會漂浮，留下親密的記憶。她沒有承諾建造經費負擔不起的博物館，但把老照片列入議程。

**EN** That afternoon Zonghe removed one service panel and found moisture behind the new plaster. He did not call it a disaster. Instead, he photographed the problem, sealed the opening, and explained that an additional test would decide whether the pool could reopen in stages. Ruoqing watched the water line without speaking. She knew the quickest solution would be to let the private club pay for the repair in exchange for the sixth lane. It was tempting, especially when her mother mentioned the electricity bill waiting at home. Yet a productive answer needed more than money; it needed a clear statement of who would be able to swim afterward. She asked Zonghe to retain the original test sheets, even if the result disappointed everyone.

**繁中** 那天下午，宗和拆下維修板，發現新粉刷層後方有水氣。他沒有把它說成災難，而是拍下問題、封住開口，並解釋下一次測試才能決定泳池是否可以分階段開放。若青沉默地看著水線。她知道最快的辦法，是讓私人俱樂部負擔修理費，換取第六水道。尤其母親提到家裡還有電費帳單時，這方法更有誘惑力。但真正有用的答案不能只有金錢，還必須說清楚修好後誰可以游泳。她請宗和保留原始測試表，無論結果多麼令人失望。

**EN** The next day Ruoqing took the old photographs to the library, where a volunteer helped her compare them with building records. A narrow path appeared in several images, but it disappeared from later plans after an extension was built. Nobody knew whether the original passage was still open. Ruoqing considered asking an employee to enter and check, then remembered the cracked pipe. She called Zonghe instead. He arrived with a flashlight, a measuring tape, and a permit for a safe inspection from above. They discovered that the passage had been sealed deliberately during a previous renovation. The seal was not proof that anything improper had happened; earlier workers might have had sound reasons. But the reason should have been recorded. Ruoqing asked the library volunteer to preserve copies of each plan, not to decide which era had been better. The woman said her own grandchildren had learned to swim at the pool and that she wanted them to have a place with a future, not merely a beautiful past. Ruoqing carried that distinction home with her.

**繁中** 隔天，若青把舊照片帶到圖書館，志工協助她與建築紀錄比對。幾張照片出現一條窄路，但後來擴建後的圖面不再有它。沒人知道原通道是否還能通行。若青一度想派員工進去查看，隨即想起裂開的管線，改為打電話給宗和。他帶著手電筒、捲尺及從上方安全檢查的許可到場。大家發現通道在前次改建時被刻意封住。這不代表有人做錯，當年的工人可能有充分理由，只是理由應該留下紀錄。若青請圖書館志工保存每版圖面，而不是裁定哪個年代比較好。志工說，她的孫子也在這座泳池學游泳；她希望他們擁有一個有未來的地方，而不只是漂亮的過去。若青把這項區別放在心裡帶回家。

## CHAPTER 03｜A Lesson in Shallow Water｜淺水區的一堂課

### 正文與翻譯

**EN** The first trial lesson took place on dry ground while repairs continued. Jingyi assigned each volunteer a different task: one would watch the gate, one would explain the emergency signal, and one would record the maximum number of people allowed near the shallow end. Ruoqing asked seventeen-year-old swimmer Xu Yizhen to demonstrate a slow walking pace rather than a racing start. Yizhen looked insulted. 'I came for serious training,' she said.
