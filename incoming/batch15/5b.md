# 不販售的黎明：夜幕劇團

*Dawn Is Not for Sale: The Night Curtain Theatre*

- **書籍代碼**：5B｜第5級 B本｜第六部獨立原創故事
- **詞彙級距**：4000–5000 字級
- **題材**：雙男主成年奇幻冒險 × 巡迴劇場 × 日光鏡陣 × 社區合作
- **主角**：陶岑（Tao Cen，33歲，舞臺機關修復師）；易尋（Yi Xun，35歲，編劇）
- **其他角色**：宋夫人（劇團團長）、薄梅（失明音樂家）、賽爾（人造黎明競技場經營者），以及五鎮居民。
- **簡介**：五座城鎮在一場鏡陣故障後失去真正的早晨。舞臺機關師陶岑與曾參與錯誤宣傳的編劇易尋，乘夜幕劇團的馬車沿山谷尋找失落光路。他們可以展示人們自願提供的記憶，卻不能用幻術製造陽光；真正的黎明必須由工程、安全程序與各鎮共同決定如何分享。

**世界規則**：①記憶燈只能在主人同意下播放其場景一次，紀錄隨後交還主人；②燈不能移動物體或創造陽光，真正的日光只能由有限容量的五面鏡陣改變方向；③鏡陣調整前須確認下游影響，見證人可撤回同意並要求停止測試。

**編排**：以《L5_B本_25章配詞.md》為依據；第1–24章各20個新目標詞，第25章21個；英文正文每段緊接完整繁中翻譯，每章包含正文證據詞表及兩題英文三選一理解測驗。

## CHAPTER 01｜The Chapel Where Morning Stopped｜早晨停在禮拜堂

### 正文與翻譯


**EN**

The last morning had stopped above Glassmere three years earlier, leaving the streets in a violet half-light. Tao Cen arrived with a wagon of stage gears and no intention of staying. He was thirty-three and knew how to make wooden moons rise on cue, but he had never repaired an actual sky. Yi Xun, a thirty-five-year-old playwright with a suitcase of abandoned scripts, caught the wagon before its wheel rolled into a ditch. Neither man recognized the other from the names printed on old advertisements. Both had come because the travelling Night Curtain Theatre had asked for a stage that could face east.

**繁中**

三年前，格拉斯米爾的最後一個早晨停在半空，街道從此籠罩在紫色微光裡。陶岑駕著裝滿舞臺齒輪的車來到這裡，原本沒打算久留。他三十三歲，懂得讓木製月亮準時升起，卻從未修理過真正的天空。三十五歲的編劇易尋提著一箱被放棄的劇本，在車輪滾進溝裡前抓住了馬車。兩人都沒認出對方就是舊海報上的名字。他們來此，只因巡迴夜幕劇團要搭一座朝東的舞臺。


**EN**

A cluster of neighbors waited beside the chapel. Nobody had certainty about what had happened to the sun; the eastern mountains still shone in distant photographs, yet no beam reached the valley. The municipal supervisor offered compensation to anyone willing to leave, but refused to disclose the mirror schematics at the public forum. An envious actor stared at the metropolitan festival posters while a generator rattled beside an immense herb garden. The plants had begun to wither, and the residents worried that both their habitat and their heritage would disappear behind the permanent curtain of dusk.

**繁中**

一群鄰居在禮拜堂旁等著。沒有人確定太陽究竟出了什麼事；遠方相片裡的東山依然明亮，山谷卻照不到任何陽光。市政主管願意補償搬走的人，卻拒絕在公開論壇揭露鏡陣圖紙。一位羨慕大都會慶典的演員望著海報，旁邊的發電機在巨大藥草圃旁嘎嘎作響。植物已逐漸枯萎，居民擔心棲地與文化記憶都會消失在永恆暮色裡。


**EN**

In the travelling theatre, every prop had a history. A cracked bowl had survived a mountain winter; a blue curtain had been patched by people who disagreed about almost everything except the need for shelter. Yi wanted to preserve those marks rather than paint over them. Tao sometimes preferred a neat surface, because a repairer likes to see a job finished. They argued, then found that the scars of use helped newcomers understand what had broken and why. By night, their quarrel had become another practical lesson for the apprentices who carried the lamps.

**繁中**

巡迴劇團裡，每件道具都有歷史。裂了的碗熬過山區冬天；藍色幕布由彼此幾乎什麼都談不攏、卻都認為需要避難處所的人縫補。易尋想保留那些痕跡，不願把它們塗平。陶岑有時偏好整齊表面，因為修復師喜歡看見工作完成。他們爭過之後才發現，使用過的傷痕能讓新來的人明白哪裡壞了、為何會壞。入夜時，他們的爭論已成為提燈學徒的一堂實用課。


**EN**

Madam Song, the company's director, gave the newcomers three rules before handing them a lantern. A memory lantern could replay only a scene freely offered by its owner. It could play that scene once, then the recording belonged to the donor again. Above all, a lantern could show an absent morning but could not make sunlight or move a mirror. Tao tested the shutters instead of arguing about miracles. Yi watched the cracked glass and quietly admitted that he had written the cheerful slogan for the machine that now blocked the eastern light. Tao put down his tool, but did not walk away.

**繁中**

劇團團長宋夫人先向新人說明三條規則，才交出記憶燈。燈只能播放主人自願提供的場景；播放一次後，紀錄就必須交還捐贈者。最重要的是，它能呈現不在場的早晨，卻不能製造陽光或移動反光鏡。陶岑不談奇蹟，先檢查葉片。易尋望著裂玻璃，低聲承認：那臺遮住東方陽光的機器，歡快的宣傳標語正是他寫的。陶岑放下工具，卻沒有離開。


**EN**

Tao felt an impulse to dismiss the playwright, but an unexpected insight stopped him. An optional provision in the theatre lease allowed a witnessed inspection of the original reflector. The supervisor called that inspection an unnecessary transaction, then tried to stumble past a child who stood in the doorway. Yi caught the falling lantern. 'The sunlight belongs to no ticket holder,' he said. 'If a stage can make the danger visible, we should build it.' His words did not excuse his old work; they explained why he had returned to the place that remembered it.

**繁中**

陶岑一度衝動地想趕走編劇，卻突然想到另一個辦法。劇場租約的一項可選條款允許有人見證原始反光鏡的檢查。主管稱這種檢查是多餘的交易，繞門口的孩子時差點踉蹌。易尋接住了快摔落的燈。「陽光不屬於買票的人，」他說，「如果舞臺能讓危險被看見，我們就應該搭起來。」這些話不能原諒他從前的工作，卻解釋了他為何回到記得那段往事的地方。


**EN**

At the shared meal, there was no seat reserved for a hero. The youngest apprentice served soup to the oldest carpenter, and the carpenter passed bread to a visitor who had spoken against their plan. Tao noticed that Yi remembered people's names even when he forgot the exact date of a performance. It made the journey feel less like a competition for praise. What mattered was whether the next town would have enough information to make its own decision after the actors had gone. They prepared instructions that could be followed without them, and tested those instructions on someone who had never seen the equipment.

**繁中**

共餐時沒有保留給英雄的座位。最年輕的學徒替最老的木匠盛湯，木匠又把麵包遞給曾反對計畫的訪客。陶岑注意到，易尋即使忘了演出的確切日期，也記得人們的姓名。這讓旅程不再像爭奪讚美的比賽。真正重要的是：演員離開後，下一座城是否仍有足夠資訊自行決定。他們寫下不需要兩人在場也能操作的說明，並請一位從未見過機器的人實際照著做。


**EN**

By the first performance, Tao had turned the broken reflector into a harmless model. A small lamp stood in for the sun; five paper houses showed where a stolen beam would land if the angle shifted. The children moved the houses themselves, discovering that giving one street a brighter morning could leave another dark. When the audience understood the problem, nobody shouted for a hero. They asked for the real measurements. Yi crossed his old slogan out of the script. That night the company voted to travel east, carrying the model rather than a promise of instant dawn.

**繁中**

第一場演出前，陶岑把壞掉的反光鏡改造成安全模型。一盞小燈代表太陽，五座紙屋標示角度改變後，奪走的光束會落在哪裡。孩子親手移動紙屋，發現讓一條街更亮，可能使另一條街更暗。觀眾理解問題後，沒人喊著要英雄，只問真正的測量數據。易尋把舊標語從劇本劃掉。當晚劇團表決向東出發，帶走模型，而非立刻找回黎明的空話。

### 理解測驗


**1. Why does Tao refuse to promise an instant sunrise?**

- A. The memory lantern can show the past but cannot move real mirrors.
- B. The wagon has no curtains.
- C. Yi has forgotten his own name.

**答案：A。** 記憶燈只能播放經同意的場景，無法改變物理鏡陣。


**2. What does the paper-house demonstration reveal?**

- A. Only actors can see the sun.
- B. Light redirected to one area can deprive another.
- C. The chapel controls the moon.

**答案：B。** 模型顯示光線重新分配可能使其他街區失去照明。

## CHAPTER 02｜The Ridge That Counted Shadows｜計算陰影的山脊

### 正文與翻譯


**EN**

The road to Cedar Ridge climbed above the last trees. Tao carried a brass angle gauge, while Yi walked beside the wagon reading letters from residents who could no longer judge when to open their shops. The company found an old maintenance hut surrounded by instruments. One sensor still flashed green, although its protective cover was cracked. Tao suspected the display had been painted to reassure inspectors. He asked the apprentices to haul the heavy mirror frame onto a level platform, but forbade them to turn the mountain's working mechanism until the villages below had been warned.

**繁中**

通往雪松山脊的路爬過最後一排樹。陶岑攜著黃銅角度規，易尋走在車旁，讀著居民來信：他們如今連何時開店都拿不準。劇團找到一間滿是儀器的舊維修屋。一枚光感器還閃著綠燈，防護罩卻已裂開。陶岑懷疑有人把顯示器塗成讓檢查員安心的顏色。他請學徒把沉重的鏡框搬上平臺，但在通知山下村莊前，嚴禁任何人轉動仍在運作的山機。


**EN**

A corporate survey team arrived with an extraordinary device that could convert reflected light into a printed graph. Its leader claimed their destination was a prosperous ridge resort, not a public repair site. She spoke of a seasonal epidemic of exhaustion among workers who had lived without real daylight. It was a beneficial reason to hurry, but haste would not cancel liability. A local respondent described the temptation to plunge the entire valley into darkness for one spectacular show. Yi wrote the warning down before running an errand to the nearest village with a reminder of the proposed test.

**繁中**

一家企業測量隊帶來一件非凡儀器，能把反射光轉換成印出的圖表。領隊聲稱她們的目的地是興旺的山脊度假地，而非公共修繕場所。她談到長年缺乏真正日照的工人出現一波疲憊問題。這確實是加快行動的有益理由，卻不能因此免除責任。當地一名受訪者說起為了華麗演出而讓整個山谷陷入黑暗的誘惑。易尋先記下警告，才跑腿去最近的村子，送達測試提醒。


**EN**

Yi kept his notebook beside the lantern rather than beneath it. He had once hidden uncomfortable facts behind beautiful lines, believing that an elegant story could make a hard decision easier to bear. Now he placed the plain description first and the poetry second. Tao respected the change but did not pretend that trust had been restored in a single conversation. When Yi offered to hand over the notebook, Tao said, 'Keep it. Read it to me when something changes.' The request sounded modest. It also meant they would continue speaking to one another after the audience went home.

**繁中**

易尋把筆記放在燈旁，而不是藏在燈下。他曾把不舒服的事實藏在漂亮台詞後面，以為優美故事能讓艱難決定比較容易接受。如今他先寫平實描述，再寫詩句。陶岑尊重這份改變，但不假裝一次談話就能恢復信任。易尋想交出筆記，陶岑說：「你留著。事情有變就讀給我聽。」這要求聽來很小，卻代表觀眾回家後，他們仍會繼續對彼此說話。


**EN**

At noon, or what the clock called noon, Tao built a screen from linen and wooden pegs. Every half-hour he compared a real shadow with the false clock on the sensor. Yi drew the results in large letters so that anyone standing farther back could read them. A shepherd noticed a narrow beam arriving three minutes later than the schedule allowed. That was the first piece of evidence that the eastern mirrors were not simply broken: someone had left the system balanced for a different arrangement of towns. The discovery changed their destination from the resort to a disused control tower.

**繁中**

到了鐘面所稱的正午，陶岑用亞麻布與木樁搭起屏幕。每半小時，他都把真實陰影和感器的假時鐘比較。易尋以大字記錄結果，讓站得遠的人也能看清。一名牧人發現一道窄光束比時程晚了三分鐘到達。這是第一項證據，顯示東方鏡陣不只是損壞，而是有人讓系統停留在另一種城鎮分配狀態。於是目的地從度假村改成廢棄控制塔。


**EN**

The guard offered a pot of stew while the workers trimmed the torn canvas. A torch cast uneven lines across the stone floor, and Yi insisted that the team remain vocal about every doubtful reading. 'Call it a transformation if you like,' Tao told the survey leader, 'but the mirror has not transformed the amount of sunlight. It has only changed who receives it.' The leader folded her graph. She had expected a publicity opportunity and instead received a record that could be independently checked. No one was asked to surrender the equipment in order to disagree.

**繁中**

工人修剪破帆布時，守衛端上一鍋燉菜。一支火把在石地上投出不規則線條，易尋堅持每個人都要把可疑讀數大聲說出來。「你可以稱它為轉變，」陶岑對領隊說，「但鏡子並沒有改變陽光總量，只改變誰得到它。」領隊折起圖紙。她原本期待宣傳機會，得到的卻是一份可以獨立核對的紀錄。沒有人因為提出異議就被要求交出儀器。


**EN**

The road carried more than the stage wagon. It carried unfinished apologies, borrowed tools, and promises whose meaning changed with the people who heard them. The actors could pack a scene into a chest, but they could not pack away its consequences. When the wheels stopped, Tao asked what the nearest household needed before asking what the theatre needed. Yi learned to make the same distinction in his scripts: a moving speech was not proof that a plan would work, and a quiet objection deserved as much attention as a cheer.

**繁中**

路上運送的不只是舞臺車，還有未完成的道歉、借來的工具，以及會隨聽者改變意義的承諾。演員能把場景收進箱子，卻不能把後果也收起來。車輪停下時，陶岑先問最近的人家需要什麼，再問劇團需要什麼。易尋也學會在劇本中區分兩者：動人的演說不等於方案可行，輕聲提出的異議和歡呼同樣值得傾聽。


**EN**

When the party descended, a child asked whether the sun had moved farther away. Tao showed him the angle gauge and let him turn its harmless wooden pointer. The child measured an ordinary cloud as it passed the ridge, then understood the difference between a distant light and a local obstruction. Yi watched Tao explain without showing off. He wanted to ask why the carpenter had left his successful theatre career, but the question could wait. For now, the ridge had given them something better than applause: a test anyone could repeat with simple tools.

**繁中**

隊伍下山時，一名孩子問太陽是不是飛得更遠了。陶岑給他看角度規，讓他轉動無危險的木製指針。孩子測量一朵平凡的雲飄過山脊，便明白遙遠的光和當地遮蔽物有何不同。易尋看著陶岑毫不炫耀地解說，很想問他為何離開成功的劇場生涯，但那問題可以稍後再談。如今山脊給他們的比掌聲更好：一套用簡單工具就能重做的測試。


**EN**

A carpenter asked Tao to trim the rough ends of the screen before anyone stood beside it. The small correction prevented cloth from catching on the frame during the next test.

**繁中**

木匠請陶岑先修剪屏幕粗糙邊緣，再讓任何人靠近。小小修正避免下一次測試時布料勾住框架。
