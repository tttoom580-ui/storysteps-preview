# 6A 全新第十一部｜《惡女館長，不肯把歷史賣給王室》

**The Curator Who Would Not Sell the Past**

**級別：** 第6級（5000–6000字彙）　**代碼：** 6A　**作品：** 第十一部全新故事

**題材：** 穿書惡女／王室博物館／文物追索／權謀懸疑／社群自主

**故事簡介：** 現代文物修復師蘇棠穿入奇幻小說，成為被控偷走王室「潮星儀」的惡女館長薇奧拉。她在追查失蹤文物時發現，許多博物館珍藏本是沼地社群出借或在不公平契約下移走的物品。她拒絕用個人認罪換取祕密拍賣，也必須為自己曾批准的失當入藏制度承擔責任。最終，文物能否回家，將由真正擁有它們的人決定。

**架空制度：** 入藏須捐贈方與原所有者同意、並保留雙方印記；更正必須留存舊版；緊急保全有期限與獨立複查；所有權、刑事責任、行政疏失各自調查。主角對原著的記憶不能作為證據。所有人物與制度均屬虛構。

**配詞依據：** 專案附件《L6_A本_25章配詞.md》。第1–24章每章20條新目標詞，第25章21條，保留原始ID、原詞條與詞性；中文義為本稿按語境整理，並非原詞表的官方翻譯。

## CHAPTER 01｜The Empty Cradle｜空置的展座

**英文正文詞數（程式統計）：** 692

### 正文與翻譯

**EN** Su Tang woke behind a glass case with a silver key pressing into her palm. She recognized the Hall of Seven Tides from a novel she had edited, and recognized her new face in the reflection: Viola Aster, the royal curator condemned for stealing the Tide Star astrolabe. A crowd had gathered for the opening exhibition, but the instrument's velvet cradle was empty. In the book, guards arrested Viola before noon and burned the old inventory before her trial. She had one advantage, knowledge of that ending, and one dangerous temptation, to treat it as proof. Instead she locked the doors without trapping visitors and asked the guards to record who had entered the room.

**繁中** 蘇棠在玻璃展櫃後醒來，掌心壓著一把銀鑰匙。她認出眼前是自己編輯過的小說中的七潮館，也在倒影裡認出新身分：因偷走「潮星儀」而被判罪的王室館長薇奧拉・艾斯特。開幕觀眾已經聚集，天文儀器的絨布展座卻空無一物。原著裡，衛兵會在中午前逮捕她，舊清冊則在審判前被燒毀。她雖知道結局，卻不能把記憶當成證據。於是她沒有囚禁訪客，只要求衛兵記錄每個進出展廳的人。

**EN** Mara would not abbreviate the donor's name because two families shared the same initials. A small anchor in the maritime case still carried the marsh community's painted mark. The preservation rule was applicable to royal collectors and village borrowers alike. Viola found the old archive locked, although its door was listed as publicly accessible. Halda held her grandmother's woven tracing against her bosom while the guards questioned her.

**繁中** 瑪拉不肯縮寫捐贈者姓名，因為兩個家族有相同的姓名縮寫。 航海展櫃裡的小船錨仍留著沼地社群畫上的記號。 保全規則同時適用於王室收藏家與村落借用者。 薇奧拉發現舊檔案室上了鎖，儘管清冊寫著可供公眾進入。 衛兵質問哈爾妲時，她把祖母的編織描樣緊貼胸前。

**EN** The registrar Mara showed her a paper inventory bearing two accession seals. One belonged to the palace; the other should have been impressed by the marsh community that had once owned the astrolabe. Under a lamp, the supposed community seal had smooth edges, while the authentic seal displayed in an older drawing had a small nick shaped like a crescent. Jorin, the conservator, refused to identify the forger on appearance alone. The impressions could have been made at different times, or a worn tool could have produced the change. He asked for controlled photographs, not an accusation. Viola agreed, even when a minister demanded a name.

**繁中** 登記員瑪拉拿來一份蓋有兩枚入藏印章的清冊。一枚屬於王宮，另一枚理應由原藏品所屬的沼地社群蓋印。燈光下，所謂社群印章邊緣平整，舊圖裡的真印卻有一道新月形缺口。修復師喬林拒絕只憑外觀指認偽造者；印痕可能來自不同時期，也可能是工具磨損所致。他要求先拍攝可比對照片，而不是先提出指控。即使部長催促她說出嫌疑人，薇奧拉仍同意。

**EN** The confiscated belongings included a bra sewn with the missing worker's name. An auction agent offered a bribe for the nighttime loading schedule, but the porter reported him. Kitchen volunteers began to broil river fish for witnesses who had traveled overnight. Jorin prepared a comparative drawing of the authentic seal and its suspicious replacement. Viola's eyelid twitched when she recognized her signature on the auction permit.

**繁中** 扣押私人物品裡有一件胸罩，縫著失蹤女工的名字。 一名拍賣代理人為取得夜間裝貨表提出賄賂，搬運員卻舉報了他。 廚房志工開始炙烤河魚，供連夜趕來的證人食用。 喬林把真印章和可疑替件畫成比較圖。 薇奧拉認出拍賣許可上的簽名時，眼皮微微抽動。

**EN** Under the hall's charter, an object entered the royal collection only after both the donor and the original owners approved the entry; a correction had to remain visible in the register. The charter also allowed a temporary hold when evidence might disappear, but never a permanent seizure without review. Viola repeated these limits to everyone present. An official who feared inconvenient facts might prefer absolute power, yet that was exactly why the rules needed witnesses, dates, and an appeal. She wrote down what was observed, what was inferred, and what was still unknown in separate columns.

**繁中** 按照七潮館章程，文物必須獲捐贈方與原所有者共同同意才能入藏，任何更正都須保留在登記冊中。若證據可能消失，可以暫時保全，但不能未經複查便永久扣押。薇奧拉向在場者重申界線。害怕事實的官員或許更喜歡絕對權力，這正是規則必須有證人、日期與申訴途徑的原因。她把觀察所得、合理推論和未明事項分成三欄。

**EN** The number of surviving original catalogues was finite, so Mara numbered every handling. Halda hoped local conservation workshops could flourish without royal control over their designs. A hired gangster threatened the glassworker who had repaired the Tide Star's case. The cart disappeared after the junction where the museum road met the quay. Despite his limp, the old porter insisted on identifying the crate he had carried.

**繁中** 存世的原始目錄數量有限，因此瑪拉每次經手都編號。 哈爾妲希望在地保存工坊不受王室掌控設計，仍能蓬勃發展。 受雇的黑幫分子威脅修過潮星儀展櫃的玻璃工。 貨車在博物館道路與碼頭交會的路口之後消失。 老搬運員雖跛行，仍堅持辨認自己搬過的木箱。

**EN** The minister offered to end the crisis if Viola signed a statement admitting careless custody. She found a second page underneath, already prepared to authorize a private auction of the remaining collection. If she signed, the missing object would become her personal crime while every disputed object could be sold. She refused, although the refusal left her under suspicion. The tribunal clerk granted a one-day preservation order only after three named witnesses signed it. At sunset the exhibit remained closed, the star remained missing, and the old accession records were still safe. For the first time, the plot in her memory had failed to dictate the next morning.

**繁中** 部長表示，只要薇奧拉簽署疏於保管的認罪聲明，就能平息風波。她卻發現聲明底下還壓著另一頁，已預先寫好其餘爭議藏品的私人拍賣許可。若她簽名，失竊只會算作個人罪行，其他藏品就能被賣掉。她拒絕了，即使這讓自己持續受到懷疑。三名具名證人簽署後，法庭書記才核發一天的證據保全令。日落時展廳仍關閉，潮星儀仍失蹤，但舊入藏紀錄保住了。原著中的命運第一次沒有照劇本推進。

**EN** The judge asked Halda to narrate what her elders had actually taught her. An orderly guided anxious witnesses into separate rooms before the first hearing. A bank teller confirmed which account had received the auction company's advance payment. The suspect transfer certificate was printed on virgin paper, unlike the stained original register. The court issued a narrow warrant to preserve records, not to arrest every museum employee.

**繁中** 法官請哈爾妲敘述長者真正教過她的內容。 勤務員在首場聽證前引導緊張的證人進入不同房間。 銀行出納確認哪個帳戶收到拍賣公司的預付款。 可疑的轉讓證明印在全新紙上，不同於有污漬的原始登記冊。 法庭核發範圍有限的調閱令，只保全紀錄，不拘捕所有館員。

### 理解測驗

1. Why did Viola refuse to sign the minister's statement?

   - A. She wanted the museum closed forever.

   - B. It would permit a private auction while blaming her alone.

   - C. She had already recovered the Tide Star.

**正解：B。** 繁中解析：聲明不只讓她認下失竊責任，還會授權其餘爭議文物私人拍賣。

   - A. It lacked the crescent nick shown in the older drawing.

   - B. It was made entirely of wood.

   - C. It carried the queen's signature.

**正解：A。** 繁中解析：舊描圖的真印有新月形缺口，可疑印痕卻沒有。

## CHAPTER 02｜The Village That Never Sold｜從未出售的村落

**英文正文詞數（程式統計）：** 674

### 正文與翻譯

**EN** A marsh delegate named Halda arrived before sunrise carrying a box lined with reeds. She had not come to recover an antique for herself. Her village's council had authorized her to challenge the museum's claim that the Tide Star had been sold eighty years earlier. The instrument had guided boats through seasonal channels, and its markings also recorded traditional fishing grounds. Without the original measurements, wealthy traders could pretend the waterways were private. Viola initially expected Halda to rejoice at the discovery of a forged seal. Instead Halda asked why the museum had displayed the object for decades without asking anyone from her community to describe it.

**繁中** 沼地代表哈爾妲在日出前抵達，手裡抱著鋪有蘆葦的盒子。她不是為自己討回古董，而是受村議會授權，質疑博物館宣稱八十年前已買下潮星儀的說法。這件儀器原本用來引導季節性水道上的船隻，刻度也記錄傳統漁場。失去原始測量紀錄，富商便能把公共水域說成私人領地。薇奧拉以為哈爾妲會因發現假印章而歡喜，對方卻問：博物館展出這件物品數十年，為何從未請原社群解說？

**EN** The aboriginal navigation exhibit had been labeled as a royal invention without consulting its makers. Reed boats abound in the marsh drawings, although none appear in the palace map. Halda asked the mapmakers to align their measurements with the older shore markers. The old chart recorded the altitude of a hill used to sight the winter stars. Renn could not hide his annoyance when the villagers requested independent interpreters.

**繁中** 原住民航行展被標成王室發明，卻從未徵詢製作者。 沼地圖畫裡蘆葦船隨處可見，王宮地圖卻一艘也沒有。 哈爾妲請製圖師把測量結果與舊岸邊標記對齊。 舊圖記錄一座冬季觀星用山丘的海拔。 村民要求獨立口譯時，雷恩掩飾不住不悅。

**EN** Halda opened her box and presented a woven tracing rather than a written deed. Its pattern matched the crescent nick in the old seal. Mara checked whether the tracing had been made recently; its fibers were old, but age alone could not establish ownership. The council could provide witnesses who remembered how elders passed the pattern to apprentices. They would testify publicly if their travel and interpretation expenses were covered without conditions. Viola offered transport from the museum budget and insisted that Halda choose the interpreters. Halda accepted travel support but declined the museum's proposal to speak only through a royal scholar.

**繁中** 哈爾妲打開盒子，拿出的不是書面地契，而是一塊編織描樣，圖案與舊印章的新月缺口相符。瑪拉檢查描樣是否近年製作；纖維雖老，年代本身仍不足以證明所有權。村議會能提供記得長者如何傳承圖案的證人，只要旅費與口譯費沒有附加條件，就願意公開作證。薇奧拉提出由館方支付交通費，並堅持口譯由哈爾妲自行選擇。哈爾妲接受資助，卻拒絕只能透過王室學者發言的安排。

**EN** A blunt chisel had left broad marks on the crate, unlike Jorin's fine tools. The disputed water crossing appeared on three village maps but vanished from the sale contract. Mara refused to discard an outdated map while its original annotations remained unexamined. The harbor discharge record showed when the packed cases had left the boat. Recovering the community's history would be a long endeavor rather than a single courtroom victory.

**繁中** 鈍鑿在木箱上留下寬痕，與喬林的精細工具不同。 爭議渡口出現在三份村圖，卻從出售契約消失。 原始註解還沒查清，瑪拉拒絕丟棄舊地圖。 港口卸貨紀錄寫明包裝展箱何時離船。 找回社群歷史是漫長努力，不是一場法庭勝利就能完成。

**EN** The investigating panel began distinguishing possession from lawful title. A museum might hold a delicate object for decades without acquiring the right to sell it. Even a valid purchase of the metal would not automatically transfer a community's shared water knowledge. Viola listed these questions separately on the public board and invited written objections. That procedure irritated donors who wanted a single quick answer, but it also prevented Halda from being treated as a symbol instead of a person capable of refusing the museum's offers.

**繁中** 調查小組開始區分「實際保管」與「合法所有權」。博物館即使收藏脆弱文物數十年，也不一定取得出售權。即使金屬本體曾合法購得，社群共有的水道知識也不會自動轉移。薇奧拉把問題分開列在公告板上，開放書面異議。這個程序讓想要迅速結案的捐贈者不滿，卻也避免哈爾妲被當成象徵，而不是能拒絕館方提議的人。

**EN** The first hurdle was proving that the old seal belonged to an actual village council. Halda changed her posture when Renn described her grandfather as an unreliable storyteller. The metalworker measured the crescent-shaped defect with remarkable precision. The court demanded a rigorous comparison before it would accept the tracing as evidence. An elder identified the stone terrace where apprentices once studied the night sky.

**繁中** 第一道障礙是證明舊印章確實屬於村議會。 雷恩稱她祖父是不可靠的說書人時，哈爾妲坐姿一變。 金工以驚人的精確度測量新月形缺口。 法庭要求嚴謹比對後才願採納描樣為證據。 長者指認學徒從前學觀星的石砌平台。

**EN** That afternoon a courier brought a directive from Councilor Renn ordering all community drawings dismissed as folklore. Viola knew Renn from the novel as the man who testified against her. This time she did not try to expose him by shouting his future actions. She asked him to cite the clause that made an oral tradition inadmissible. He could not find one, because no such clause existed. The clerk recorded the exchange. The evidence was still incomplete, but the hearing could no longer exclude the marsh witnesses merely for lacking palace stationery. Halda promised to return with two elders; Viola promised only that they would be heard.

**繁中** 下午，一名信差送來議員雷恩的命令，要求把社群描樣全部視為民間故事而排除。薇奧拉知道原著中正是雷恩出庭指控她，這次卻沒有喊出他未來的罪行。她只請他指出哪一條法律禁止口述傳統作證。他找不到，因為根本沒有那條規定。書記把問答記錄下來。證據仍不完整，但聽證不再能只因沼地證人沒有王室用紙就把人排除。哈爾妲答應帶兩位長者回來；薇奧拉只承諾他們一定有發言機會。

**EN** The palace catalogue called the village protest an uprising, though witnesses described a peaceful gathering. A volunteer offered to usher the elderly witnesses to the hearing entrance. The Tide Star was a versatile instrument for navigation, weather observation, and seasonal teaching. The marsh council retained a veto over any public use of its sacred recordings. A fisherman's widow brought a receipt proving that her family had only lent the compass.

**繁中** 王宮目錄把村民抗議稱作起義，證人卻說那是和平集會。 志工主動引領年長證人到聽證入口。 潮星儀是兼具導航、觀天氣與季節教學用途的多功能儀器。 沼地議會保留否決公開使用神聖錄音的權利。 漁夫的遺孀帶來收據，證明家人當年只是出借羅盤。

### 正文與翻譯

**EN** The marked crate had passed through a stone quarry before reaching the private yard. Jorin and Halda traveled there with a tribunal observer and discovered an old workers' hostel converted into a temporary packing station. The quarry manager claimed he had never agreed to store museum objects. Yet several workers recognized the crescent mark as a symbol used when marsh boats brought food to their camp. Viola understood that a mark could connect places without proving ownership. She needed the invoices, the workers' accounts, and a reason for the detour before drawing any conclusion about the Tide Star.

**繁中** 標記木箱在抵達私人貨場前，曾經過一處採石場。喬林和哈爾妲在法庭觀察員陪同下前往，發現舊工人宿舍被改成臨時包裝站。採石場經理否認同意儲放博物館文物，幾名工人卻認得新月記號，那原本是沼地船送食物到營地時的標誌。薇奧拉明白，標記能連結地點，不等於證明所有權；在斷定潮星儀去向前，她需要發票、工人說法和繞路原因。

**EN** A quarry worker showed the council the acre of land taken for a royal storage shed. An elderly bachelor kept the only surviving copy of the quarry's first employment agreement. The hostel displayed boxing gloves once used by miners during their days off. A disciplinary notice punished the worker for refusing to move an unsealed crate. An emigrant returned with a letter proving that his family had never sold the instrument.

**繁中** 採石工人向議會指出被徵作王室倉棚的一英畝土地。 一位年長單身漢保存採石場首份僱傭協議的唯一副本。 宿舍展示礦工休假時使用的拳擊手套。 一份紀律處分通知懲罰工人拒搬未封箱子。 一名移民帶信返鄉，證明家人從未出售儀器。

**EN** One worker had been disciplined for refusing to move an unsealed chest. Another had kept notes about a landslide that blocked the direct road, giving a possible innocent explanation for the detour. The notes were dated, but they did not explain why the chest remained at the quarry for eleven days after the road reopened. Halda found a copied list of object numbers on the back of a food invoice. Two numbers belonged to the medical gallery, not the astrolabe. The problem was larger than a single stolen instrument, and the team had to resist making that realization into an exaggerated accusation.

**繁中** 一名工人因拒搬未封箱子而遭處分；另一人記下曾有坍方堵住直路，或許能解釋繞道，但道路重新開放後箱子仍停留十一天，紀錄無法解釋。哈爾妲在食物發票背面找到物件編號抄本，其中兩號屬於醫療展區而非潮星儀。問題比單件失竊更大，但團隊不能因此誇大指控。

**EN** Viola warned the court not to equate a worker's protest with theft. The manager's confident account began to falter when Mara produced the dated gate book. The marsh records linked seasonal navigation with the fertility of riverside fields. Halda refused to forsake the elderly workers merely to recover the Tide Star sooner. A gay couple from the quarry asked that both partners be recognized in the housing register.

**繁中** 薇奧拉提醒法庭，不要把工人抗議等同偷竊。 瑪拉拿出附日期的大門簿後，經理自信的說法開始動搖。 沼地紀錄把季節航行與河岸田地肥沃度連在一起。 哈爾妲拒絕為了早點找回星盤而拋棄年長工人。 採石場的一對男同志伴侶要求住房登記承認雙方身分。

**EN** A witness could be brave and still mistaken about a crate's contents. Viola had the records compared by dates, weights, and handlers instead of praise for the loudest speaker. The panel drew a distinction between the museum's disputed title to objects and the quarry's responsibility for wages. Both problems required action, but neither could be solved by trading away the other. In her notebook Viola wrote a question she had never asked in the old life of the villainess: who would keep watching once she was no longer in charge?

**繁中** 證人可以勇敢，仍可能看錯箱中物。薇奧拉要求用日期、重量與經手人比對，而不是獎勵聲音最大的人。小組區分文物所有權爭議和採石場欠薪責任；兩者都須解決，不能拿其中一項交換另一項。她在筆記寫下原著惡女從未問過的問題：當她不再掌權，還有誰會繼續監督？

**EN** An unnatural gloom filled the hostel after another worker received a dismissal threat. At the road intersection, two witnesses saw the same blue-marked wagon turn west. A landslide had closed the direct quarry road, giving the driver one legitimate reason to detour. The quarry families grew vegetables to nourish children during the wage dispute. An orthodox historian doubted oral testimony, but admitted the charter allowed it.

**繁中** 又一名工人收到解僱威脅後，宿舍瀰漫陰鬱氣氛。 兩名證人在道路交叉口都看見藍色標記貨車向西轉。 山崩曾封閉採石場直路，讓司機有合理繞道原因。 採石場家庭種蔬菜，在欠薪爭議期間滋養孩子。 一名守舊史家懷疑口述證言，卻承認章程容許採納。

**EN** The quarry workers voted unanimously to release copies of their own shift records, on condition that their wages and future jobs were discussed openly. Renn called the vote a rebellion. Viola answered that giving testimony was not an uprising, and asked the panel to protect witnesses from retaliation while allowing the company to respond. The panel ordered the employer to preserve payroll records and set a date for a labor hearing. Halda left without promising loyalty to Viola. She told the curator that trust would depend on what happened after the grand investigation ended.

**繁中** 採石場工人一致投票，願意提供自己的輪班紀錄，條件是公開討論工資與工作保障。雷恩把投票稱為叛亂。薇奧拉回答，提供證詞不是起義，並要求保護證人免受報復，同時保障公司答辯權。小組命令雇主保存薪資資料，另訂勞資聽證日期。哈爾妲沒有宣誓效忠薇奧拉，她說信任要看這場盛大調查結束後會發生什麼。

**EN** The worker cited the prohibition against moving unstable loads without a trained supervisor. The court granted provisional protection to witnesses until the labor hearing concluded. The workers reached a unanimous decision to submit their shift records under supervision. Renn's vanity made him display an enormous portrait above the donors' entrance. A woodpecker tapped the hostel roof while the workers discussed their missing wages.

**繁中** 工人引用禁止在沒有受訓主管時搬運不穩貨物的規定。 法庭給予證人臨時保護，直到勞資聽證結束。 工人一致決定在監督下提交輪班紀錄。 雷恩的虛榮使他在捐助者入口掛上巨幅肖像。 工人討論積欠薪資時，一隻啄木鳥敲著宿舍屋頂。

## CHAPTER 08｜The Hidden Seal｜隱藏的印記

**英文正文詞數（程式統計）：** 643

### 正文與翻譯

**EN** The quarry copies led Mara to the museum's northern storehouse, where rejected display frames and broken transport cases had been stacked for years. Under a layer of dust, Jorin found the missing central pin of the Tide Star. It had been removed with care, not broken by accident. A certificate attached to its box claimed the pin was a modern replacement, but its measurements matched an older community drawing. Viola asked an independent metalworker to test the alloy without damaging it. She wanted to establish whether the pin belonged to the astrolabe before naming anyone who had handled the box.

**繁中** 採石場抄本帶瑪拉來到博物館北倉，裡面多年堆放著淘汰展架與破運輸箱。喬林在灰塵下找到潮星儀失蹤的中央插銷。它是被小心取下，而非意外折斷。盒上的證明寫它是現代替件，尺寸卻與古老社群描樣一致。薇奧拉請獨立金工在不傷害物件的前提下測試合金，先確認插銷是否屬於潮星儀，再談經手者責任。

**EN** The accumulation of dust showed which storage shelves had recently been disturbed. Mara offered a warm beverage to the witness who had waited through the night. A blond assistant remembered delivering the sealed replacement certificates to Renn's office. Jorin would not certify the recovered pin before an independent metal test. The contractor lacked the necessary clearance to enter the northern storehouse after closing.

**繁中** 灰塵堆積情況顯示哪些庫架最近被碰過。 瑪拉替徹夜等候的證人準備熱飲。 一位金髮助理記得曾把密封替件證明送往雷恩辦公室。 獨立金屬檢測完成前，喬林不會正式認證尋回插銷。 承包商沒有閉館後進入北倉所需的通行許可。

**EN** The metalworker found a tiny notch under the decorative ridge, in the same position marked in Halda's tracing. Mara checked the certificate's numbering against the paper register and discovered an administrative gap. Renn's office had authorized three replacement certificates on the day the central pin disappeared. Only two could be found. The third had been issued under the curator's stamp, a fact Viola could neither ignore nor explain away.

**繁中** 金工在裝飾脊線下找到一處小缺口，位置與哈爾妲的描樣相同。合金很常見，因此缺口比化學成分更有價值。他在報告寫「與潮星儀相符」，而非「確定相同」。瑪拉比對證明編號與紙本冊，發現行政空缺：中央插銷消失當天，雷恩辦公室批准三份替件證明，現在只有兩份。第三份使用館長印章簽發，薇奧拉無法忽視或輕易辯解。

**EN** The auditor refused to deduct unexplained transport costs from workers' unpaid wages. The fragile astrolabe was not destined for a private cabinet merely because its label said so. A palace lieutenant signed the gate log but denied seeing the crates' contents.

**繁中** 稽核員拒絕從工人欠薪中扣除無法解釋的運輸費。 脆弱星盤不會只因標籤這樣寫，就注定進入私人收藏櫃。 吊掛團隊沒有向受損青銅外緣施加壓力。 搬運員拒絕那個把他說成單槍匹馬救出全部收藏的英雄故事。 王宮中尉簽過大門簿，卻否認看見箱內物品。

**EN** The metal report gave the investigators a physical comparison, not a final answer. **繁中** 金工報告提供實體比較，不是最終答案。瑪拉標示推論的可信程度，保留兩造都可查閱的原始測量值。新證明程序也設期限，以免臨時緊急規則意外變成永久統治。薇奧拉接受地位受損。即使她記得原著反派的名字，眼前活著的人也值得一套不依賴她個人確信的程序。

**繁中** 識字的學徒協助年長工人閱讀證明，但沒有代替他們發言。 青銅插銷上的蓮花圖案與沼地議會保存的描圖相符。 目錄邊緣註記把兩件文物轉往私人倉庫。 兩趟送貨時間重疊，因此同一司機不可能同時駕兩車。 一所地方博物館願在所有權聽證期間保管爭議框架。

**EN** Viola voluntarily surrendered control of the curator's seal. She told them that a fair investigation could not rely on a suspect's exclusive access to evidence. That night a messenger carried a sealed complaint from the auction contractor. **繁中** 薇奧拉主動交出館長印章管理權。法庭指定瑪拉及社群觀察員監督往後的證明，喬林把插銷移入中立保管櫃。幾名忠心員工指責她削弱自身辯護。她回答，公平調查不能讓嫌疑人獨占證據。當夜，拍賣承包商送來密封申訴，指控哈爾妲捏造村落圖案。哈爾妲要求先親自讀完再讓小組答覆，她要用自己的話維護歷史，而不是透過薇奧拉代言。

## CHAPTER 09｜The Merchant's Map｜商人的地圖

**繁中** 一名商人送來地圖，把潮星儀的舊故鄉畫在無人居住的小島，遠離哈爾妲的村落。若地圖獲採信，雷恩便能主張沒有現存社群擁有文物。瑪拉發現島名墨水與其餘文字不同。製圖師承認標籤是依客戶指示後加，卻拒絕未經法令便透露姓名。薇奧拉向法庭申請範圍有限的揭露命令，沒有公開恐嚇他；沉默尚不等於犯罪。
