# 借一本到明天

## One More Book for Tomorrow

**書籍代碼：** 2B（第六個獨立故事）  
**字級：** 第2級｜1000–2000 英文字級  
**題材：** 成年校園・二手書交換節・公共閱讀・合作成長・慢熱戀愛  
**正式配詞依據：** `L2_B本_25章配詞.md`  
**章數：** 25章（完整結局）

二十三歲的資訊設計學生欣，為即將關閉的舊閱覽館舉辦免費二手書交換節，與重視維修、安全及實際成本的二十五歲工程系學生凱成為搭檔。從一本書的歸屬、訪客的隱私，到經費、颱風及社區的閱讀權利，兩人一同讓「任何人都能來讀書」的承諾延續，也在尊重彼此人生選擇的過程中發展感情。

## CHAPTER 01｜The Locked Annex｜上鎖的舊館

### 正文與翻譯

**EN**

Hsin arrived at the old campus reading annex with a notebook and a key that no longer opened the door. A notice said the building would close at the end of the term. She was twenty-three, studying information design, and she had planned a small book exchange there.

**繁中**

欣帶著筆記本與一把已經打不開門的鑰匙來到校園舊閱覽館。公告寫著館舍將在學期末關閉。她二十三歲，主修資訊設計，原本打算在這裡辦一場小型換書活動。


**EN**

A retired lecturer with a white beard asked whether the shelves would be free for everyone. The café beside the library offered spare tables, but only for the weekend. A sharp chemical smell from fresh paint made them move the books to fresh air. Someone called the building's closure a crime; Hsin said that anger alone was not evidence. They would divide the books into five groups that readers could recognize easily.

**繁中**

一位留著白鬍子的退休講師問，書架是否對所有人免費開放。 圖書館旁的咖啡館願意借出多餘桌子，但只限週末。 新油漆刺鼻的化學氣味使他們把書搬到通風處。 有人把封館稱作犯罪；欣說光有憤怒不能當證據。 他們決定把書分成五個讀者容易辨認的類別。


**EN**

Kai, a twenty-five-year-old engineering student, offered a folding cart. Hsin knew him only as the person who argued that the old room was too expensive to keep. He said the boxes were safe to move, but the building itself needed a professional check.

**繁中**

二十五歲的工程系學生凱推來一台折疊推車。欣只知道他曾主張舊館維護費太高。他說書箱可以安全搬走，但館舍本身必須交由專業人員檢查。


**EN**

Kai asked a favor of the gardener, who knew where the outdoor power outlets were. Hsin had a strange feeling that this little exchange might become something much larger. The gardener brought a bucket to catch drops beneath the old rain pipe. A dry leaf fell between the pages of an old plant guide. A volunteer arrived in pajamas after an overnight study session and made everyone laugh.

**繁中**

凱請園丁幫忙，因為他知道戶外插座的位置。 欣有種奇特的感覺，這場小小換書或許會變得更大。 園丁拿水桶接住舊雨管落下的水滴。 一片乾葉夾在舊植物指南的書頁間。 一名志工熬夜讀書後穿著睡衣出現，逗得大家笑了。


**EN**

They found a worn card inside an old book. It described a free shelf that once welcomed anyone, even people who could not bring a book in return. Hsin wanted to restore that promise, not turn the exchange into a shop.

**繁中**

兩人在舊書裡發現一張磨損的卡片。卡片記載這裡曾有一座免費書架，任何人都能取書，包括沒有書可交換的人。欣想恢復這份承諾，而不是把換書活動變成商店。


**EN**

When Hsin read the closure notice a second time, she saw a line about public access. The university would not decide what to do with the old books until staff had made an inventory. That left the students a narrow chance to help, but it gave them no right to enter a locked building. Hsin called the office, asked which rooms were approved for sorting, and wrote the answer beside the date. She wanted their project to begin with permission rather than a secret trip through a back door.

**繁中**

欣第二次看封館公告時，發現其中一行提到公共使用權。校方在員工清點舊書前，不會決定書的去處。這給了學生一個有限的幫忙機會，但沒有賦予他們進入封鎖建築的權利。欣致電辦公室，詢問哪些教室獲准整理，並把答覆寫在日期旁。她希望計畫從取得許可開始，而不是從偷偷走後門開始。


**EN**

Kai's quiet personality was different from Hsin's quick and open way of speaking. The first sign was plain, with large letters and no expensive decoration. Hsin asked each visitor to relate a book to a real interest rather than its cover. A rubber wheel on the borrowed cart had begun to crack. Such a small crack could become dangerous if a heavy box fell from the cart.

**繁中**

凱安靜的個性與欣迅速又坦率的說話方式不同。 第一張標示很樸素，只有大字，沒有昂貴裝飾。 欣請訪客依照真正的興趣選書，而不是只看封面。 借來推車的一個橡膠輪子開始裂開。 這樣的小裂縫如果使重箱掉落，就可能變得危險。


**EN**

Kai suggested a public test in the safe courtyard. Hsin would organize readers; he would build shelves that could be moved and checked. She accepted his help, but asked that every rule be written clearly so no visitor felt ashamed for having nothing to give.

**繁中**

凱建議先在安全的中庭試辦。欣負責招募讀者；他製作能移動、可檢查的書架。欣接受合作，但要求把每項規則寫清楚，讓沒有東西可捐的人也不會覺得羞愧。


**EN**

Bo said any sales would need clear records for tax and university rules. Kai checked the room temperature before letting them store old paper there. A tiny handwritten star marked the corner of the free-shelf card. A student shared watermelon with the helpers, leaving red juice on a napkin. They had to weigh each box so the shelves would not carry too much.

**繁中**

博說任何銷售都要有清楚紀錄，以符合稅務和校方規定。 凱先檢查室溫，才讓大家在那裡存放舊紙本。 免費書架卡片的角落畫著一顆很小的手繪星星。 一名學生分西瓜給幫手，紅色汁液滴在餐巾紙上。 他們必須秤量每箱書，避免書架承受過重重量。


**EN**

Kai returned with a plan for two shelves made from reused boards. His drawing looked beautiful, but Hsin pointed out that neither shelf had a stated weight limit. He sighed, took the pencil back, and added a space for a test result. "You always find a question," he said. "And you always bring a tool," she answered. Neither sentence sounded like an insult. They moved the first empty cart together and found that it turned much more easily when both people watched the corners.

**繁中**

凱帶回一份以回收木板製作兩座書架的計畫。他的圖畫很好看，欣卻指出書架都沒有標明承重上限。凱嘆氣，拿回鉛筆，加上一欄測試結果。「妳總會找到問題。」他說。「你總會帶來工具。」她回道。兩句話都不像在責備。他們一起推動第一台空推車，發現只要兩人都注意轉角，轉彎就容易多了。


**EN**

That evening the key stayed useless in Hsin's pocket, yet the first page of their plan was full. Before leaving, Kai asked whether she would meet him again tomorrow. She answered, "For the books, certainly." He smiled as if that answer was enough for now.

**繁中**

傍晚，欣口袋裡的鑰匙仍毫無用處，計畫書第一頁卻已寫滿。離開前，凱問她明天是否願意再見面。她回答：「為了書，當然。」他笑了，彷彿暫時有這句回答就夠了。

## CHAPTER 02｜Names on the Cards｜卡片上的名字

### 正文與翻譯

**EN**

On the second day, old borrowing cards appeared inside donated books. Some held names, phone numbers, and notes from students who had graduated years ago. Hsin stopped the volunteers before anyone photographed them. Keeping an interesting story was not worth exposing a stranger's private life.

**繁中**

第二天，捐贈書裡出現舊借閱卡，有些寫著多年以前畢業生的姓名、電話和留言。欣在志工拍照前阻止了大家。保存一個有趣故事，不值得公開陌生人的隱私。


**EN**

The beauty of the old borrowing cards lay in their history, not in private names. Hsin would cancel the photo session until the sensitive cards were removed. Kai found a chain on a storage door and asked who held its key. A chess club donated boards and books, but requested a separate table for games. The current list showed titles and numbers without the donors' personal details.

**繁中**

舊借閱卡的美感在其歷史，而不是私人姓名。 欣決定先取消拍攝，等敏感卡片移走再說。 凱在倉庫門上發現一條鏈子，詢問鑰匙由誰保管。 西洋棋社捐了棋盤和書，卻希望另設一張遊戲桌。 目前的清單只顯示書名與編號，沒有捐贈者的私人資料。


**EN**

**繁中**

**繁中**

**EN**

**繁中**

**繁中**

有位失望的訪客抱怨借閱卡才有趣，凱卻主動支持欣。他解釋大家可以慶祝書本，不必把過去的讀者當成展品。欣沒想到他會先開口。

**繁中**

**EN**

At closing time, two sisters came looking for a book their grandfather had once loved. Hsin could not promise to search private records for their family. Instead, she helped them use the public title list and showed them a similar edition. The sisters thanked her, even though they did not find the exact copy. **繁中**

打烊時，兩姊妹來找祖父曾喜歡的一本書。欣不能答應替她們查私人紀錄，於是教她們使用公開書名清單，還找出相似版本。兩姊妹雖然沒有找到那一本，仍向她道謝。凱從門邊看著，終於明白善意的答覆不一定是把對方要求的東西全給出去。


**EN**

**繁中**

當天留下的照片變少了，信任卻增加了。凱在欣的規則旁畫了一個關著的小盒子，底下寫著：「不是每個故事都屬於我們。」欣留下了那一頁。
