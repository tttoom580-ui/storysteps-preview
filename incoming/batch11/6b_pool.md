# 《水道不為誰停留》
*The Lane I Choose*

**代碼：6B｜全新第八部**  
**級距：第 6 級（5000–6000 字彙）**  
**題材：成年女性重返游泳教育、公共設施改革、職涯自主、平等戀愛**

**故事簡介：** 三十九歲的前競技泳者陳沐禾重返青渠社區泳池，發現即將私營化的館舍缺乏完整的安全與無障礙規劃。她與昔日沉默的建築師賀之庭重新相遇，卻不願讓舊情決定事業。當公眾參與、勞動權益及財務現實接連考驗改建方案，她必須學會：守護眾人的水道，不等於犧牲自己的人生。

## CHAPTER 01｜The Water Beneath the Notice｜公告底下的水聲

### 正文與翻譯


**EN** On the morning Mo Chen returned to Green Channel Pool, she heard water striking tile before she saw the locked entrance. She had spent sixteen years measuring seconds in competition and another seven teaching people to enter water without fear. Now the city intended to lease the public building to a luxury fitness chain. A paper notice promised improvement but said nothing about the older swimmers who came before sunrise or the wheelchair users who needed the shallow ramp. Mo, thirty-nine, had not come home to rescue her childhood. She had come because her former teammate Ying asked her to inspect an unsafe practice schedule. The difference mattered. Mo checked the broken clasp on the changing-room gate before allowing any inspection. Ying asked the committee to convene while witnesses could still attend. A painted otter cub smiled from a faded children's swimming poster. A dental outreach team had used the lobby for a free clinic. One angry resident called Wei a dictator; Mo requested specific complaints instead.

**繁中** 陳沐禾回到青渠泳池的早晨，還沒看見上鎖的大門，便先聽見水拍打磁磚的聲音。她曾有十六年用秒數衡量競賽成績，之後又花了七年教人如何不帶恐懼地入水。現在，市府打算把公共泳池租給豪華健身連鎖企業。公告保證會改善設施，卻沒提及清晨來游泳的長者，或需要淺水坡道的輪椅使用者。三十九歲的沐禾不是為了拯救童年而回來。她是因為昔日隊友宋穎請她查看一份不安全的訓練時程。這個差別很重要。 沐禾先檢查更衣室門上損壞的門扣，才允許進行查驗。 宋穎請委員會趁證人還能到場時召開會議。 褪色的兒童游泳海報上畫著一隻微笑的水獺幼獸。 牙科巡迴團隊曾借用大廳舉辦免費門診。 一名憤怒居民罵魏昊是獨裁者；沐禾則要求提出具體問題。


**EN** The first person she met was Eli Ho, forty-three, an accessibility architect carrying rolled plans under one arm. Once, after Mo injured her shoulder, he had heard a coach call her lazy and had said nothing. They had not spoken properly in eleven years. He offered to show her the building drawings. She refused a private tour and asked that the lifeguards and users be invited as well. Eli did not protest. He placed his plans on the dry steps and said the consultation period ended in thirty days. Inside, an empty lane reflected the fluorescent ceiling, and Mo understood how easy it would be to mistake clean-looking water for a safe public space. The closure directive was posted without a record of the public consultation. An elderly swimmer's dwelling stood opposite the pool, a five-minute walk away. Children used to gallop across the grass after lessons, laughing at one another. Unverified rumors could heighten fear before the actual inspection results arrived. The janitor handed over an old maintenance key and recorded the exchange.

**繁中** 她遇見的第一個人是四十三歲的無障礙建築師賀之庭，手臂下夾著一卷圖紙。十一年前，沐禾肩傷後，教練當著他面說她懶惰，他卻沒有出聲。兩人多年來不曾好好交談。他提出帶她看建築圖，沐禾拒絕私下參觀，要求也邀請救生員和使用者。之庭沒有爭辯，把圖紙攤在乾燥階梯上，告訴她公眾諮詢只剩三十天。館內空蕩的水道映出螢光燈，沐禾忽然明白：看起來乾淨的水，很容易被誤認成安全的公共空間。 停用指令張貼時沒有附上公眾諮詢紀錄。 一名高齡泳客的住所就在泳池對面，步行五分鐘可達。 孩子過去上課後總在草地上奔跑嬉笑。 未經證實的傳言可能在檢驗結果出爐前加劇恐懼。 清潔員交出一把舊維修鑰匙，並登記交接。


**EN** At noon they opened the doors for a short inspection. The ramp railing moved when an adult swimmer leaned against it; the nearest emergency phone had no connection. Ying, now head lifeguard, showed Mo a log with two inspection dates written in the same ink but by different hands. Mo took photographs with the caretaker present and signed a receipt for each copy. She would not accuse anyone on a feeling. In the lobby an eighteen-year-old swimmer named Jia waited with her own list of questions. Jia used a wheelchair on land and was tired of being spoken about rather than spoken to. She asked Mo whether the proposed facility would keep an independent accessible lane. Mo answered, 'We have to make them answer that in writing.' The pool's faded motto promised that everyone belonged in the water. A pilgrim visiting the nearby shrine asked if the public showers would remain open. The old earthquake agreement provided a precedent for an independent safety review. Wei expected to reap membership profits after converting the facility into a private club. The lift platform stayed stationary when Ying pressed its emergency control.

**繁中** 中午，眾人短暫開門檢查。成年泳客靠上坡道扶手時，扶手竟晃動；最近的緊急電話也無法接通。如今擔任救生主管的宋穎拿出紀錄，兩個檢查日期雖用相同墨色，筆跡卻不同。沐禾在管理員見證下拍照，並為每份複本簽收。她不願憑感覺指控任何人。大廳裡，十八歲的游泳者嘉寧帶著自己的問題清單等待。嘉寧在陸地使用輪椅，早已厭倦別人只談論她、不直接和她說話。她問新設施是否會保留獨立的無障礙水道。沐禾回答：「我們得讓他們用白紙黑字回答。」 泳池褪色的座右銘寫著：每個人都能在水中找到位置。 一名到附近神社參拜的朝聖者問公共淋浴間是否仍開放。 過去的地震協議留下了獨立安全審查的先例。 魏昊期待將泳池轉為私人俱樂部後取得會員收益。 宋穎按下緊急控制鍵時，升降平台仍保持靜止。


**EN** That evening Mo considered returning to the coaching job she had temporarily left in another city. Its director had given her two weeks to decide whether she wanted a permanent contract. The pool had no promise to offer in return: only damaged equipment, incomplete records, and people who had learned to lower their expectations. Eli waited by the gate without approaching until she finished a call. 'I owe you an apology,' he said. 'Perhaps,' Mo replied, 'but tonight I need the original maintenance register.' He gave her the document request number, not a confession or a romantic speech. For the first time that day, the work seemed possible without surrendering her boundaries. Mo refused to treat expensive as a synonym for accessible. Gardeners planned to transplant three trees before repairing the outdoor drain. A neighborhood trustee requested the lease terms before any vote. Mo demanded an unconditional stop to the hazardous demonstration, not a permanent ban on swimming. She would not utter an accusation until she had checked the signatures.

**繁中** 當晚，沐禾想起另一座城市暫時離開的教練工作。主管給她兩週時間，決定是否接受正式合約。泳池卻無法給她任何承諾，只有壞掉的設備、不完整的紀錄，以及早已學會降低期待的人。之庭在門旁等她講完電話，才走近說：「我欠妳一個道歉。」沐禾回答：「也許。但今晚我需要原始維護登記簿。」他遞來申請文件的編號，沒有辯解，也沒有浪漫宣言。那天第一次，沐禾覺得自己也許能完成工作，而不必放棄界線。 沐禾拒絕把昂貴視為無障礙的同義詞。 園丁打算在修理戶外排水溝前移植三棵樹。 一名社區受託人要求表決前先公開租賃條款。 沐禾要求無條件停止危險示範，並非永遠禁止游泳。 在核對簽名之前，她不願說出指控。

## CHAPTER 02｜A Line That Cannot Be Crossed｜不能跨越的界線

### 正文與翻譯


**EN** The city granted Mo and Ying a forty-eight-hour examination slot, but only in the dry sections of the building. A private contractor insisted that the water system was operational because a green lamp glowed on its control cabinet. Mo asked whether the lamp had been tested under load. No one could produce the result. Meanwhile, the adult beginners' class stood outside with wet towels in their bags, disappointed at another cancellation. Mo explained that a temporary closure of the deep lane protected them better than a reassuring photograph. Her words earned no applause. They did earn a second inspection, witnessed by a municipal technician who did not work for the future tenant. Every contractor had to abide by the same electrical isolation rules. Eli asked the technician to affirm which outlet had actually been repaired. An alligator mascot on the old lesson chart reminded children to keep their distance. Deep bass notes from a school choir reached the inspection team through the windows. Jia rewrote the warning in colloquial language so more residents could understand it.

**繁中** 市府准許沐禾和宋穎在四十八小時內檢查，但範圍只限乾燥區域。私人承包商堅稱水循環設備正常，理由是控制箱亮著綠燈。沐禾問燈號是否在實際負載下測試過，卻沒人能拿出結果。成人初學班帶著泳巾站在門外，再次停課讓大家失望。沐禾解釋，暫時關閉深水道，比拍一張讓人安心的照片更能保護他們。她沒有得到掌聲，卻爭取到第二次檢查，並由不隸屬未來承租者的市府技師見證。 所有承包商都必須遵守相同的斷電隔離規範。 之庭請技師確認究竟修好了哪個插座。 舊課程圖表上的鱷魚吉祥物提醒孩子保持距離。 學校合唱團的低音透過窗戶傳進檢查隊耳中。 嘉寧將警告改寫成口語，讓更多居民看得懂。


**EN** Eli came with revised diagrams, and the group compared them with the real building. One staircase was labeled as a secondary exit even though its door opened onto an enclosed storage yard. The contractor called it an insignificant discrepancy. Jia asked him how a person using a chair was supposed to negotiate those steps during smoke or a power failure. He looked at Eli rather than answering her. Eli turned the question back: 'Jia is the user. Please answer Jia.' Mo noticed that changed behavior more than his earlier apology. It did not erase the past, but it altered what could happen next. She wrote the discrepancy in the public log and insisted that neither company nor city remove the entry. Minor scheduling disputes were commonplace, but a locked emergency exit was not. The temporary testing permit would expire unless the city received the second report. A burned fuse explained the dark control panel but not the missing maintenance signature. The school offered hospitality to stranded visiting inspectors without requesting favors. Ying's infectious laughter briefly softened the tense discussion about access.

**繁中** 之庭帶來修訂圖面，大家將圖面和實際建築逐項比較。有一道樓梯被標為第二逃生出口，門外卻是封閉的儲物院。承包商稱這只是無關緊要的小差異。嘉寧問他，停電或失火時，輪椅使用者該如何走那些階梯。對方看向之庭，沒有回答嘉寧。之庭把問題轉回去：「使用者是嘉寧，請回答嘉寧。」沐禾比起他的道歉，更注意這個行為上的改變。它無法抹去過去，卻改變了接下來可能發生的事。她把差異記進公開紀錄，要求公司與市府都不得刪除。 小型排班爭議很常見，但鎖住緊急出口絕非小事。 若市府收不到第二份報告，臨時測試許可便會到期。 燒壞的保險絲能解釋控制板為何不亮，卻解釋不了缺失的維修簽名。 學校款待滯留的外地檢查員，沒有要求任何回報。 宋穎富有感染力的笑聲讓緊張的無障礙討論稍微緩和。


**EN** At a meeting in the neighboring school gym, residents argued about whether public money should support a place that some rarely used. Mo let the uncomfortable question stand. She showed the facility's actual annual membership figures, but admitted that the figures excluded free rehabilitation hours and school sessions. A parent asked whether a better way of counting could be invented. Ying offered to bring attendance sheets from three years of programs, provided personal names were removed. Mo accepted the condition. A useful figure would explain need without making vulnerable participants surrender their privacy. The room quieted when Jia announced she would help design the access survey herself. The technician used one liter of clean water in a separate demonstration tank. A novice volunteer was assigned to write labels, never to touch live wiring. Mo said it would be rash to reopen the deep lane after one promising test. The accountant tried to reconcile two invoices bearing different dates. The shabby map had not been updated since the last renovation.

**繁中** 在隔壁學校的體育館會議上，居民爭論公帑是否該支持部分人很少使用的設施。沐禾沒有迴避難題。她展示泳池年度會員數，並承認數字漏掉免費復健時段與學校課程。有家長問能否改用更好的計算方式。宋穎提出可提供三年出席紀錄，條件是移除個人姓名。沐禾同意了。有用的數字應能說明需求，而不迫使處境脆弱的參與者放棄隱私。嘉寧宣布要親自參與無障礙調查設計，會場這才安靜下來。 技師在獨立的示範槽中使用了一公升清水。 新手志工只能負責寫標籤，不得碰觸通電線路。 沐禾說僅憑一次看似成功的測試便重開深水道太草率。 會計試圖核對日期不同的兩張發票。 破舊地圖自上次整修後就沒有更新。


**EN** Before leaving, Eli asked whether Mo could review his accessible changing-room plan. She said yes, as a paid consultant under an agreement that would also let Jia and Ying review it. He smiled at the insistence on a contract. 'I used to think agreement meant everyone was silent,' he said. Mo folded her notes. 'Silence is sometimes just a sign that nobody has been given room to disagree.' They walked to the bus stop together without touching. It was early enough for either to change their mind, and Mo found the simple freedom of that distance unexpectedly comforting. A borrowed spotlight made the presentation easier to see but obscured an exit sign. A volunteer brought ginger syrup for workers who had spent the afternoon in the cold. The handrail began to tilt when the tester leaned against its outer edge. At twilight the team photographed the door before sealing the inspection area. An adult donor offered whiskey at dinner, but the workers refused alcohol during their shift.

**繁中** 離開前，之庭問沐禾能否審閱無障礙更衣室設計。她答應，但必須依付費顧問契約進行，也讓嘉寧和宋穎共同審閱。他對她堅持簽約露出微笑。「我以前以為大家都安靜，就代表同意了。」他說。沐禾收好筆記：「沉默有時只是因為沒人被允許反對。」兩人一起走到公車站，沒有碰觸。彼此都還能改變心意，而這段簡單的距離讓沐禾意外安心。 借來的聚光燈讓簡報更清楚，卻遮住了逃生標誌。 志工帶來薑糖漿，慰勞在寒風中工作一下午的人。 測試者靠在外側時，扶手開始傾斜。 黃昏時，團隊先拍攝門況，再封閉檢查區。 一名成年捐助者在晚餐時提供威士忌，工作人員值班期間婉拒飲酒。
