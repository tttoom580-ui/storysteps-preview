# 十點之後，還有人在等
## Someone Is Still Here After Ten

**書籍代碼：** 2B（重新創作版・第四個不同故事）  
**字級：** 第2級｜1000–2000 英文字級  
**題材：** 成年校園・深夜失物招領・預錄廣播・溫暖戀愛喜劇

二十三歲的聲音設計學生怡思，在大學夜間失物招領室認識了二十五歲的機械修護學生諾亞。兩人想用短短的廣播，幫人找回遺失物，卻從藍色信封裡發現早已被遺忘的夜間服務。面對隱私、預算、工作選擇與逐漸靠近的心，他們學會：真正的關心，不是替別人決定故事，而是留下一扇可以自由出入的門。

## CHAPTER 01｜The Blue Envelope｜藍色信封

### 正文與翻譯

**EN**
The lost-and-found room was smaller than Iris expected. It stood behind the university café, between a storage door and a window facing the garden. Iris, twenty-three, had come to record a short night program for her sound-design class. Instead, she found a table covered with missing things. A gardener with a gray beard was waiting there, holding a dry leaf inside a blue envelope. “I lost the letter that came with this,” he said. “The letter matters more than the leaf.” A student in pajamas hurried past the door. Iris realized that a campus did not really sleep at night.

**繁中**
失物招領室比怡思想像中還小。它位於大學咖啡館後方，夾在儲藏室門與一扇面向花園的窗之間。二十三歲的怡思原本是為了聲音設計課，來錄一段簡短的夜間節目，卻看見桌上堆滿遺失物。一位留著灰鬍子的園丁拿著藍色信封在等候，裡面放著一片乾葉。「我把原本和它放在一起的信弄丟了，」他說，「那封信比葉子重要。」一位穿著睡衣的學生匆匆經過門口。怡思發現，大學的夜晚其實從不真正入睡。

**EN**
Noah arrived with a rubber case for an old microphone. He was twenty-five and studied machine repair, but he helped at the desk because the student office was short of workers. His personality seemed plain at first: he spoke slowly and wrote every number twice. “We divide the work,” he said. “You record a message. I check who may hear it.” Iris asked if the rules were really so serious. Noah showed her three lines on the wall: never say a full name on air, never describe a private paper, and never give an object away without proof. “Such rules protect people,” he said. She put away her recorder and listened.

**繁中**
二十五歲的諾亞帶著裝舊麥克風的橡膠盒走進來。他讀機械修護，因學生事務處人手不足，才來櫃檯幫忙。他起初給人的印象十分平實：說話慢，數字總要寫兩次。「我們分工，」他說，「妳錄訊息，我確認誰可以聽。」怡思問規定真的有那麼嚴格嗎。諾亞指著牆上的三行字：廣播不得說出全名、不得描述私人文件、沒有證明就不能交出物品。「這些規則是為了保護人，」他說。怡思收起錄音機，開始認真聽。

**EN**
The evening became stranger when a woman came to ask about a lost bag. She did not want the bag itself. She wanted the tiny note tied to its handle, a note from someone she had not seen in ten years. Meanwhile, the café owner asked the office for a favor: could they return a box of watermelon slices left in the fridge before the temperature grew too high? Noah agreed to weigh the box and note the time. Another student asked if a chemical stain on a jacket was evidence of a crime. Iris answered carefully, “A stain is a fact. A crime is a claim. They do not mean the same thing.”

**繁中**
傍晚，一名女子前來尋找遺失的袋子。她其實不要袋子，而是想找回綁在提把上的小紙條；那是十年未見的人留給她的。與此同時，咖啡館老闆拜託他們幫個忙：冰箱裡有一盒被忘記的西瓜，能否在溫度升高前送回去？諾亞答應先替盒子秤重並記下時間。另一位學生問，外套上的化學污漬算不算犯罪證據。怡思謹慎回答：「污漬是事實，犯罪是指控，兩者不能畫上等號。」

**EN**
At nine, Iris tested her first recording. “A blue envelope was found near the north gate. Please come to the office and describe what is inside.” She stopped before mentioning the letter. The words felt too cold. Noah did not tell her to sound more friendly; he asked what feeling she wanted the listener to have. “A chance to come back,” she said. They tried again. This time her voice was warmer, but the message was still clear. Iris began to relate the work to her class. A good sound could carry hope without giving away somebody's life. Noah smiled when she noticed that difference.

**繁中**
九點時，怡思試錄第一則廣播：「北門附近拾獲藍色信封。請失主到辦公室說明內裝物品。」她在說出信件內容前停了下來。這段話聽起來有點冰冷。諾亞沒有叫她裝得更親切，而是問她希望聽眾有什麼感覺。「還有機會找回東西，」她說。他們重新錄了一遍。這次聲音更溫暖，資訊卻仍清楚。怡思開始發現這份工作與課堂的關聯：好聲音能帶來希望，又不洩露別人的生活。諾亞看見她理解了這一點，露出微笑。

**EN**
Before closing, an office manager called about a small tax form left in the room. The form belonged in a sealed drawer, not in an open box. Iris carried it there herself. She had expected a cheerful project with funny stories; now she understood that even an ordinary notice could hurt somebody if handled badly. She wrote “A voice can open a door” on her notebook. Noah checked the lock, then returned the gardener's envelope to the safe shelf. Its blue color was easy to see among brown bags. He asked, “Can you come tomorrow?” Iris answered, “Only if we can learn the stories without stealing them.”

**繁中**
關門前，一名辦公室主管打電話來問留在房內的小型稅務表單。那份表單應放進上鎖抽屜，而不是開放式箱子。怡思親自把它收妥。她原以為這會是充滿趣事的輕鬆課堂專案，如今才明白，就連普通公告，處理不好也可能傷人。她在筆記本寫下：「一個聲音可以打開一扇門。」諾亞確認門鎖，把園丁的藍色信封放回安全的架子。它在一排咖啡色袋子之間格外醒目。他問：「明天還來嗎？」怡思回答：「只要我們能了解故事，而不是偷走故事。」

**EN**
Walking home, Iris reread the rules. She wondered why the gardener's letter had been lost while its envelope remained, and why Noah looked away whenever anyone mentioned the old radio room. These were questions, not proof of a secret. She decided not to make them into a dramatic report. Tomorrow she would ask ordinary questions first, and let people choose how much to tell. Her phone lit up with a message from Noah: “I forgot the key to the cabinet. I did remember your recording.” Iris laughed aloud under the streetlight. For the first time that day, she looked forward to another shift.

**繁中**
走回家的路上，怡思重新讀了那三條規定。她不懂為何園丁留下信封卻遺失信件，也不明白為何每當有人提起舊廣播室，諾亞就把視線移開。這些只是疑問，不是祕密的證據。她決定不把它們誇大成戲劇化的報導。明天，她會先問普通問題，再讓每個人自行決定願意說多少。手機亮起，是諾亞傳來的訊息：「我忘了拿櫃子的鑰匙，倒是記得妳的錄音。」怡思站在路燈下笑出聲來。這是她當天第一次期待下一次值班。

### 理解測驗

**1. What rule stops Iris from reading a lost letter on air?**

- A. The price must be known first.
- B. Private papers cannot be described on air.
- C. All letters must be destroyed.

**答案：B。** 牆上的規定禁止在廣播中描述私人文件，因此怡思不能直接公開信件內容。

**2. Why does Iris decide to return the next night?**

- A. She wants to learn stories without taking them from people.
- B. She has been ordered to stay forever.
- C. She needs to sell the envelopes.

**答案：A。** 怡思願意回來，條件是尊重當事人的故事與選擇。

## CHAPTER 02｜Two Labels on One Box｜一個盒子上的兩張標籤

### 正文與翻譯

**EN**
The next night, Iris found two names on the same box. The current label showed a student number; beneath it was an older card with another number. She wanted to display both numbers and ask the campus to hunt for the owner, but Noah covered the card with his hand. “Membership records are not a public game,” he said. The office had suffered a loss of trust after someone posted a student's address the year before. Iris saw why her idea was selfish. She wrote a new message asking the owner to describe the box without naming anyone. She would respond to questions in private, not make a show of her own self. Her first choice had been quick, not wise.

**繁中**
隔天夜裡，怡思發現同一個盒子上有兩個名字。目前的標籤寫著一個學號，底下則是另一張更舊的卡片，數字不同。她本想展示兩個號碼，請全校一起尋找失主，諾亞卻伸手蓋住了卡片。「會員資料不是公開遊戲，」他說。前年有人在網路公開學生住址，失物招領室因此失去部分信任。怡思明白自己的主意有點自私。她改寫公告，請失主描述盒子內容，而不透露姓名。她會私下回應問題，不拿自己的表現當作主角。她第一個念頭雖然快，卻不夠周到。

**EN**
Their club adviser came with a plan for a three-night trial. Students could listen to short recorded notices through a small speaker by the door. The office would count successful returns, but a return would count only after the owner showed proof. A chess player offered to guide guests through the line, and an art student made the display beautiful with blue paper shapes. “Super!” Iris said, then noticed Noah's worried face. He feared that a bright display might promise more than their small team could do. The adviser agreed to write the trial limits in clear words above the speaker. The beauty of the plan, Iris thought, was that people could understand it before they joined.

**繁中**
社團指導老師帶來三晚試辦計畫。學生可以透過門邊的小喇叭聽簡短的預錄公告。辦公室會計算成功領回的案件，但必須等失主提出證明才算數。一名棋社學生自願引導排隊者，美術系學生則用藍色紙片把展示牆布置得很漂亮。「太棒了！」怡思說，隨即看見諾亞擔憂的表情。他怕亮眼的展示讓人期待超過小團隊的能力。指導老師同意把試辦限制清楚寫在喇叭上方。怡思心想，這套計畫的美好之處，在於人們參加之前就能先理解它。

**EN**
At dinner, Iris brought a pear and a list of questions. Noah told her that his grandmother had once run the lost-property desk. She kept an old chain across the back room because the space held private documents. “That chain looked rude,” he remembered. “She said it was there to protect people, not shut them out.” Iris asked why his grandmother had left. Noah answered, “She retired. There is no great secret in every old thing.” He was not angry, but he did not offer more. Iris changed the subject and asked him to try the pear. He accepted half and gave the other half back.

**繁中**
吃晚餐時，怡思帶了一顆梨子和一張問題清單。諾亞說，祖母以前負責失物招領櫃檯。她在後房拉了一條舊鏈子，因為裡面存放私人文件。「那條鏈子看起來很不近人情，」他回憶，「她說不是要把人擋在外面，而是保護裡面的人。」怡思問祖母為何離開。諾亞說：「她退休了。不是每件舊東西都藏著大祕密。」他並沒有生氣，但也不願多說。怡思換了話題，請他吃梨子。他拿走一半，又把另一半留給她。

**EN**
The first return came from a student whose bag contained a guide to a garden club. She could name a small mark on the handle, a mark not mentioned in the public message. Noah checked her answer against the sealed record and returned the bag. The woman smiled with such relief that Iris forgot to be nervous. Later a speaker made a sharp noise, and three students jumped. Iris canceled the next recording while Noah tested the cable. Their best result that night was not the number of bags returned. It was the fact that they stopped a poor system before it hurt anyone.

**繁中**
第一位來領物的學生帶走了一個裝著園藝社指南的袋子。她能說出提把上一個小記號，而那個記號從未出現在公開公告中。諾亞對照封存紀錄，確認後才交還。女生如釋重負地笑了，讓怡思忘了緊張。之後喇叭突然發出尖銳聲，嚇了三位學生一跳。怡思取消下一則錄音，讓諾亞檢查線材。那晚最好的成果不是找回多少袋子，而是他們在系統傷到任何人之前就先停下來修正。

**EN**
Near closing time, a guest asked if the box with two labels belonged to her. Iris gave her the same fair test used for everyone else. The answer was wrong, and the guest looked embarrassed. “I thought it might be my sister's,” she said. Noah did not call her a liar. He invited her to leave a message about the correct box, if she wished. She thanked him. Iris saw that a person could be mistaken without being dishonest. She added one line to their plan: a failed claim should not become a public story. Even the worst moment could teach them a better rule.

**繁中**
快打烊時，一位來客詢問那個貼著雙標籤的盒子是否屬於她。怡思照同樣公平的方式核對資訊。答案不對，對方顯得很窘迫。「我以為可能是姊姊的，」她說。諾亞沒有說她是騙子，而是邀請她如果願意，可以留下真正遺失盒子的描述。她向他道謝。怡思明白，人可能搞錯，卻不一定不誠實。她在計畫上加上一條：未通過的認領不應變成公開故事。即使最糟的時刻，也能教會他們更好的規則。

**EN**
When they finished counting, the adviser praised the team's growth. Four people had visited. Only one object had been returned, yet all four had heard what to do next. “Do you think that is enough?” Iris asked. Noah turned the small box in his hands and said, “We cannot measure trust in one night. We can give it room to grow.” She glanced at the second label again. The dates were six months apart, but the handwriting was the same. She did not tell him it was proof of fraud. She simply wrote both dates in her notebook and decided to check the record tomorrow.

**繁中**
統計結束後，指導老師稱讚團隊的進步。今晚有四人到訪，雖然只領回一件物品，但四人都知道接下來該怎麼做。「這樣就夠了嗎？」怡思問。諾亞捧著小盒子說：「信任不能用一晚量出來，但我們可以給它成長的空間。」她又看了一眼第二張標籤。兩個日期相隔半年，筆跡卻相同。她沒有把它說成舞弊證據，只把日期記進筆記本，決定明天去查紀錄。

### 理解測驗

**1. Why does a box have two labels?**

- A. A student wanted to play a joke.
- B. The box belongs to two owners.
- C. The office changed its numbering method during a move.

**答案：C。** 後來找到的紀錄證實，搬遷時改過編號，部分舊標籤沒有撕除。

## CHAPTER 25｜The Door Stays Open｜那扇門會繼續開著

### 正文與翻譯

On the first morning of the new service, Iris stood outside the station with Noah. The sign had a simple appearance: a blue border, a small chart of hours plus a telephone number, and clear spelling that visitors could read from the path. A classical music student had offered to play at the opening, but Iris asked for the sound to stay soft. The aim was not to win a prize. It was to help ordinary people find a place to return lost things. The company that printed the sign had corrected an error without asking for extra money. Iris thought that small act of justice suited the day better than any grand speech.
