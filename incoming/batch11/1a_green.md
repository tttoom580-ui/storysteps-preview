# 雨後的綠色箱子
# The Green Box After the Rain

> **拾頁 StorySteps｜1A｜第1級（1000以下）｜全新第4版｜獨立25章完整小說**

**題材：** 校園園藝社合作解決失物謎團；無障礙花園、集體照顧與公平查證。

**簡介：** 艾薇與朋友準備學校花園日，卻發現裝著種子與大家筆記的綠箱子，在暴雨與維修後失蹤。她們循著借用紀錄、搬運線索與市場儲藏室逐步查明經過，同時聽見一位同學對安全小徑的需求。孩子們最終找回失物，讓花園真正成為大家能一起使用的空間。

**閱讀說明：** 每章英文正文600–900詞，每段 EN 之後緊接完整繁體中文翻譯；各章附原始配詞 ID 與英文正文證據，以及2題三選一英文理解測驗。原始配詞來源為《L1_A本_25章配詞.md》。詞表、標題與測驗不計入英文正文詞數。

**人物：** 艾薇（Ivy，園藝社學生）、俊（Jun，喜愛修理與繪圖）、凱（Kai，對花園動線提出意見的學生）、溫老師（Ms. Wen），及協助查證的校方、家人和市場工作人員。

## CHAPTER 01｜The Empty Green Shelf｜空掉的綠色架子

*英文正文詞數：637；本章新目標詞：20*

### 正文與翻譯

**EN**

At seven a.m., Ivy came early to the school garden. The sun was just above the roof. She ate fruit and a small cake for breakfast, then looked at the green shelf near the door. Yesterday, a heavy green box had stood there. It held seeds, signs, and a little book for the garden club. Now the shelf was empty. The bell rang for the first class, but Ivy did not move. Their garden day was only four weeks away. Without the box, they could not begin.

**繁中**

上午七點，艾薇提早來到學校花園。太陽剛升到屋頂上方。她早餐吃了水果和一塊小蛋糕，接著望向門邊的綠色架子。昨天，一個沉重的綠箱子還放在上面。裡頭有種子、標示牌和園藝社的小本子。如今架子空了。第一堂課的鐘響了，艾薇卻沒有移動。距離花園開放日只剩四週。沒有箱子，大家便無法開始。

**EN**

There was one more reason Ivy cared about the box. Each student in the club had written one small message about a plant. A boy wanted to grow beans for his grandmother. A girl wanted flowers that would bring bees to the garden. Ivy had written that she wanted a place where a quiet student could ask a question without being laughed at. All those messages were in the notebook inside the box. Losing the box meant losing part of their promise to one another. She did not want to give up that promise simply because the search looked hard.

**繁中**

艾薇在意那個箱子，還有另一個原因。園藝社每位學生都寫過一段關於植物的小心願。一個男孩想替奶奶種豆子，一個女孩想種能吸引蜜蜂的花。艾薇寫的是：希望有個地方，讓安靜的學生能發問，而不會被取笑。所有訊息都在箱子裡的筆記本上。失去箱子，也像失去了大家對彼此的一部分承諾。艾薇不想只因為尋找很困難，就放棄那個承諾。

**EN**

Jun arrived with a cover for the garden table. He was Ivy's friend, and fixing small things was his hobby. A drawing of a monkey with one large tooth was on the side of the box. He had made that funny picture himself, so everyone could know which box belonged to them. “Shall we look behind the couch in the club room?” he asked. They looked, but the box was not there. Ivy asked him to help without moving anything else. Someone might need the room later.

**繁中**

俊帶著花園桌子的罩布來了。他是艾薇的朋友，修理小東西是他的興趣。箱子側面畫著一隻露出一顆大牙齒的猴子。那張有趣的圖是俊親手畫的，好讓大家認出自己的箱子。「我們要不要看看社團教室的沙發後面？」他問。他們找了，箱子卻不在那裡。艾薇請他幫忙時不要移動其他東西，因為稍後可能有人要使用教室。

**EN**

Ms. Wen, their teacher, met them in the hall. She wanted to teach the club how to care for plants and how to care for people. “Searching is not a race,” she said. “First, write down what you know.” Ivy took out a notebook. She wrote: green box; seed cards; monkey picture; last seen on the shelf yesterday. She did not write that anyone had stolen it. Jun nodded. He said that making a fair list was better exercise for his mind than running from room to room.

**繁中**

社團老師溫老師在走廊遇見他們。她想教社團成員照顧植物，也學習照顧人。「找東西不是比賽，」她說，「先寫下你們確定的事。」艾薇拿出筆記本，寫上：綠箱子、種子卡、猴子圖畫、昨天最後一次看見的位置。她沒有寫任何人偷走了箱子。俊點點頭，說列出公平的清單，比在一個個房間之間奔跑更能訓練他的腦袋。

**EN**

That day Ivy made a promise to herself as well. She would tell the truth about what she saw, and she would not make a person feel bad just because a thing was missing. The teacher helped her put a large sheet of paper on the wall. Children could draw what they remembered about the box, but they were not to write another person's name as a guess. When the first class came in, several students added pictures of seeds and small leaves. The wall began to look like a garden before the real garden was ready.

**繁中**

那天艾薇也對自己許下承諾。她會誠實說出看見的事，不會只因東西不見，就讓某個人難受。老師幫她在牆上貼一張大紙。孩子可以畫出記得的箱子模樣，但不能把猜測對象的名字寫上去。第一個班級進來時，好幾位學生畫了種子和小葉子。真正的花園還沒準備好，牆上卻已經有了花園的樣子。

**EN**

At lunch the club shared salad under a tree. One new student came from a foreign country and asked what the green box looked like. Ivy explained slowly, and Jun showed him the monkey picture. The student said he had seen a green shape near the school gate during the rain, but he was not sure it was their box. Ivy thanked him. A guess could be useful, she thought, as long as people did not turn it into a fact too soon. She added a question mark to her notes.

**繁中**

午餐時，社團成員在樹下分吃沙拉。一位來自國外的新同學問綠箱子長什麼樣。艾薇慢慢說明，俊則把猴子圖畫給他看。新同學說，下雨時曾在校門附近看見一個綠色的東西，但不確定是不是他們的箱子。艾薇向他道謝。她想，猜測也許有用，但不能太快把它當成事實。她在筆記上加了一個問號。

**EN**

In the afternoon they found a wet piece of paper under the shelf. Only the words “keep dry” could be read. Ivy put the paper between two clean pages without trying to pull it apart. Jun wanted to ask every student in the school, but Ms. Wen suggested a smaller first step. They would speak to the people who used the garden yesterday. Ivy closed her book. She still wanted the green box back, yet she also wanted everyone to feel safe when they answered her questions.

**繁中**

下午，他們在架子底下找到一小片濕紙。上面只看得清「保持乾燥」幾個字。艾薇把紙片夾在兩張乾淨的紙中間，沒有硬把它攤開。俊想詢問全校每一個學生，溫老師卻建議先從小範圍開始。他們要先問昨天使用花園的人。艾薇闔上筆記本。她仍想找回綠箱子，但也希望每個回答問題的人都能安心。

## CHAPTER 02｜The Wheel Mark｜輪子的痕跡

*英文正文詞數：645；本章新目標詞：20*

### 正文與翻譯

**EN**

The next morning was sunny. Ivy's cousin Leo came to school for a family visit and brought a small science tool that could measure the weather. He was also good at drawing maps. He saw two wheel marks in the soft earth near the gate. “A cart may have passed here,” he said. Jun wanted to follow the marks at once, but Ivy asked Ms. Wen first. The marks might belong to a cart carrying food, not to the green box. Leo drew them carefully before the sun made the ground dry.

**繁中**

隔天早上陽光很好。艾薇的表哥里歐因家庭參訪來到學校，帶了一個能測量天氣的小型科學工具。他也很會畫地圖。他看到校門附近的軟土上有兩道輪痕。「可能有推車經過，」他說。俊想立刻沿著痕跡去找，艾薇卻先詢問溫老師。那些痕跡可能屬於送食物的推車，不一定與綠箱子有關。里歐趁太陽還沒把地面曬乾，仔細畫下輪痕。

**EN**

Leo wanted to add one more mark to his map: the place where the two lines began. He walked back to the gate with Jun and Ms. Wen. The soft ground had a small hole beside the wall. Rainwater could fill it when the next storm came. They took a photograph for the school worker and did not step into the hole. Leo said a good map should show danger as well as possible routes. Ivy agreed. Their search had taught them something useful about the gate, even if the marks did not lead to the green box.

**繁中**

里歐想在地圖上加一個記號：兩道輪痕開始的位置。他和俊、溫老師一起回到校門。牆邊的軟土上有個小洞，下次暴雨時可能會積滿水。他們拍照交給學校工作人員，沒有踏進洞裡。里歐說，好地圖應該標出危險，也標出可能路線。艾薇同意。即使輪痕沒有帶他們找到綠箱子，這場搜尋仍幫他們發現了校門附近的一個問題。

**EN**

The school's helpful garden worker was a tall guy named Mr. Reed. He said one cart had gone toward the river, where the town kept a water station. Another had gone south to the science room. Neither cart needed much power, since people pushed them by hand. A famous garden video showed the same kind of cart, but a video could not tell them which one had moved the box yesterday. Ivy filled her notebook with two possible routes. She left space for a third route they had not seen.

**繁中**

學校熱心的園藝工作人員是一位高個子男子，大家叫他里德先生。他說有一輛推車往河邊的鎮立供水站去，另一輛則往南方的科學教室去。兩輛車都不太需要動力，因為是用手推的。一部有名的園藝影片也出現過這種推車，但影片不能證明昨天是哪輛車搬走了箱子。艾薇在筆記本上畫出兩條可能的路線，還留了一塊空白，給他們尚未發現的第三條路。

**EN**

At lunch, a family brought food for a small school event. There was a sheep picture on a bag of bread. Leo smiled and said his younger brother loved sheep. While they ate, Jun noticed a little cut on Leo's lip and offered him water. They were not in a hurry. A careful search needed people who could think clearly, and hungry people often missed small things. Ivy asked Leo to bring his drawing the next day. He agreed, but said that he still had to ask his own teacher for permission.

**繁中**

午餐時，有一家人帶食物來參加校內的小活動。裝麵包的袋子上畫著綿羊。里歐笑著說他弟弟很喜歡羊。大家吃東西時，俊注意到里歐的嘴唇有一處小傷口，就遞水給他。他們沒有急著出發。仔細尋找需要清楚思考，肚子餓的人常常會忽略小事。艾薇請里歐明天再帶畫來。里歐答應了，但說仍須先徵求自己的老師同意。

**EN**

Before they left, the three children sat near a window. Leo explained that science did not always give an answer on the first try. Sometimes a test showed that an idea was wrong. “That does not make the test a waste,” he said. Ivy thought of the picture of the two carts. It showed a difference they had not noticed at first. Jun folded the map and gave it back to Leo. “You helped us see more clearly,” he said. Leo smiled. Being useful felt better than pretending to know everything already.

**繁中**

離開前，三個孩子坐在窗邊。里歐解釋，科學並不一定在第一次嘗試時就給人答案。有時測試只是證明某個想法錯了。「那不代表測試白費，」他說。艾薇想起兩輛推車的圖，圖上顯示了他們一開始沒注意到的差異。俊把地圖摺好還給里歐。「你幫我們看得更清楚，」他說。里歐笑了。他覺得真正幫上忙，比假裝自己什麼都懂更好。

**EN**

After class the group went to the science room with Ms. Wen. A shelf held clean jars, a small fan, and a metal cart. The cart had four narrow wheels, while the mark outside showed two wide wheels. “It is not the same,” Jun said. Ivy wrote down the difference. She felt glad that they had checked a simple answer before telling the whole school. At the door, Ms. Wen asked whether they had learned something useful. “Yes,” Ivy said. “We have one less guess, and we still have friends willing to help.”

**繁中**

下課後，大家在溫老師陪同下去了科學教室。架上有乾淨的罐子、小電扇與一輛金屬推車。那輛車有四個窄輪子，外頭的痕跡卻只有兩道寬輪痕。「不是同一輛，」俊說。艾薇記下差異。她慶幸自己在告訴全校之前，先查證了這個看似簡單的答案。到了門口，溫老師問他們有沒有學到有用的事。「有，」艾薇說，「我們少了一個猜測，而且還有願意幫忙的朋友。」

**EN**

Before going home, Ivy looked at the two routes again. One line stopped at the science room. The other went toward the water station and the old park. She had never been there with the club. Mr. Reed offered to show the way on Thursday, when the station was open to school groups. “That will be safer,” Ivy said. Jun put away the map and helped carry the empty food bags. He was learning that being useful did not always mean being the first person to run somewhere.

**繁中**

回家前，艾薇又看了那兩條路線。一條線停在科學教室，另一條則通往供水站和舊公園。她從未和社團一起去過那裡。里德先生說，星期四供水站開放學校團體參觀時，可以帶他們去。「這樣比較安全，」艾薇說。俊收好地圖，幫忙搬空的食物袋。他正慢慢明白，有用不一定代表第一個跑到某個地方。

## CHAPTER 03｜A Road by the Water｜水邊的道路

*英文正文詞數：627；本章新目標詞：20*

### 正文與翻譯

**EN**

On Thursday the club walked with Ms. Wen and Mr. Reed to the water station. The road followed the river for a short way. Far away, beyond a low bridge, they could see the beach. A bird sat on the bridge rail. Jun wondered whether the wide wheel marks could still be found, but the rain had washed the road clean. Ivy asked everyone to stay on the safe side of the road. They were there to learn what had happened, not to take a risk for a box.

**繁中**
