# 雲線以上，請聽第三聲
*Above the Fog, Wait for the Third Signal*

**代碼：** 3A（L3-A）  
**級距：** 2000–3000 詞  
**版本：** 全新第六版  
**題材：** 高山氣象站、封閉場景規則推理、設備調查與救援

**故事簡介：** 二十八歲的氣象技術員陳珊受命到北嶺站調查失聯觀測員梅。山上三條奇怪的安全規則、不同步的時鐘和不明無線電聲，逐步指向被遺漏的水道與更動過的警報。珊必須靠測量、舊圖、授權程序與團隊合作救出梅，也查清哪些錯誤讓警告失去作用。

**主要人物：** 陳珊（Shan Chen，28 歲，氣象技術員）；諾亞（Noah，山道嚮導與維修協力者）；梅（Mei，24 歲，年輕觀測員）；山姆（Sam，區域工程師）；維爾（Vale，氣象站站長）；伊沃（Ivo，志工）。

**三條規則（故事開篇即揭示）：**

1. **When the red antenna lamp flashes, leave the metal terrace.** — 紅色天線燈閃爍時，離開金屬平台。
2. **Answer no radio voice until it gives the three-note call sign.** — 來電未發出三音呼號，不要回答。
3. **If the upper water gauge drops while the snow is melting, open the south channel before entering the lower passage.** — 融雪時上方水位計下降，進入下層通道前必須先開南側水道。

**閱讀與配詞說明：** 每個英文正文段落後接一段完整繁體中文翻譯；配詞依《L3_A本_25章配詞.md》指定章節。第1–24章各20個正式新詞條，第25章21個。正文證據只取英文正文，不以章名、翻譯、詞表或測驗充當命中。括號或斜線形式仍按原表一個詞條計算。

## CHAPTER 01｜The Call Above the Clouds｜雲層上的呼叫

### 正文與翻譯

**EN** At twenty-eight, Shan Chen had learned that a broken instrument could tell a more honest story than a confident person. She arrived at North Ridge Weather Station with a repair order marked acceptable, though no one had explained why a junior observer named Mei had disappeared. Her colleague Noah offered to advise her on the mountain path. At the entrance, an old guard rested his chin on his hand beside a carton of coconut milk. Shan tried to connect her radio to the station network, but received only a short tone. The guard looked away when she asked about Mei. That silence was her first reason to doubt the official report.

**繁中** 二十八歲的陳珊早已明白，壞掉的儀器有時比自信滿滿的人更誠實。她拿著一張標示「可以接受」的維修單來到北嶺氣象站，卻沒有人解釋年輕觀測員梅為何失蹤。同事諾亞主動提出要向她說明山路。入口的老警衛托著下巴，桌上放著一盒椰奶。珊試著把無線電連上站內網路，得到的卻只有一聲短音。她問起梅時，警衛移開視線。這份沉默是她第一次懷疑官方報告的原因。

**EN** The director, Vale, called the search a cruel waste of time. He said Mei had probably gone downhill without permission. A first-aid card listed the correct dose of mountain medicine, while a slow drip from the ceiling fell onto its plastic cover. Shan did not envy Vale's calm expression. She wanted to explore the equipment room before deciding anything. There she found a torn note in Mei's handwriting: “Check the upper tank when the snow begins to melt.” The paper was damp, but its ink remained readable. If Mei had simply walked away, why had she hidden a warning inside a broken instrument case?

**繁中** 站長維爾把搜尋稱為殘酷的時間浪費，說梅大概沒獲准就自行下山了。急救卡列著高山藥物的正確劑量，天花板的水卻一滴滴落在卡片的塑膠封面上。珊不羨慕維爾平靜的神情；她想先探索設備室，再做判斷。她在那裡找到一張梅手寫的破紙：「雪開始融時，檢查上方水槽。」紙已受潮，字跡仍可辨識。如果梅只是自行離開，為何要把警告藏在故障儀器盒中？

**EN** Outside, the forecast warned of extreme winds after midnight. Vale suggested that anyone who climbed toward the antenna would gamble with their life. Shan took one glance at the western slope, where old hay had been placed under a covered supply box to keep tools dry. A laundry line rattled in the wind, although no clothes hung from it. Noah noticed that the line pointed toward a disused path. They photographed the ground without following it. Shan insisted that a frightening weather forecast was not permission to stop thinking. They needed to identify what was dangerous, where the danger began, and which paths remained safe.

**繁中** 外頭的預報警告午夜後會有極端強風。維爾說，任何爬向天線的人都是拿生命下注。珊瞥向西坡，看見有人把舊乾草墊在有蓋的補給箱下，以保持工具乾燥。一條晾衣繩被風吹得作響，上面卻沒有衣物。諾亞注意到繩索正指向一條廢棄小徑。兩人先拍照，沒有貿然前進。珊堅持，令人害怕的天氣預報並不是停止思考的藉口。他們需要確認危險是什麼、從哪裡開始，以及哪些路仍可安全通行。

**EN** The guard finally gave a sincere account. He had been sitting on a low stool beside an empty tobacco tin when he heard two people speak over the emergency radio. One voice sounded like Mei's twin sister, who lived far below the mountain; the other ended with a sharp whistle. Shan asked whether he had seen either person. He had not. Instead of guessing, she recorded his exact words and checked the radio log. It listed only one call, made at 2:17 in the morning. In the margin someone had drawn three short strokes. The guard admitted that he had forgotten those marks when he spoke to Vale.

**繁中** 警衛終於坦誠敘述。他當時坐在矮凳上，身旁是一個空菸草罐，忽然聽見緊急無線電裡有兩個人說話。一個聲音像梅住在山下的雙胞胎姊妹，另一個則以尖銳哨聲結尾。珊問他是否看見兩人，他說沒有。珊沒有猜測，而是記下他的原話，再核對通訊紀錄。紀錄只列出凌晨兩點十七分的一通呼叫，旁邊有人畫了三道短線。警衛承認，向維爾報告時忘了提到那些記號。

**EN** On a board near the stairs were three rules printed in black. First: when the red antenna lamp flashes, leave the metal terrace. Second: answer no radio voice until it gives the three-note call sign. Third: if the upper water gauge drops while the snow is melting, open the south channel before entering the lower passage. These sounded like ordinary safety instructions, but the station's newer map showed no south channel. Shan copied the board and the map separately. An explanation that mixed them together too early might hide the very difference she needed to understand. Noah agreed to help her compare the two sources in daylight.

**繁中** 樓梯旁的告示板印著三條黑字規則。第一：紅色天線燈閃爍時，離開金屬平台。第二：無線電裡的聲音沒有先發出三音呼號，就不要回答。第三：雪正在融化且上方水位計下降時，進入下層通道前先打開南側水道。這些聽起來像普通安全規定，但新版地圖上根本沒有南側水道。珊分別抄下告示與地圖。她知道，太早把兩份資料混成一個解釋，可能會遮住最重要的差異。諾亞同意白天幫她核對兩個來源。

**EN** Before sleeping, Shan tested the radio with a simple request for identification. A woman's voice said, “Shan, please come to the lower room.” It did not give the three notes. She pressed the record button but kept the microphone silent. A second later the same sentence returned, with exactly the same small cough before her name. A person could repeat words; identical background noise was harder to explain. Shan wrote down the time and played nothing back over the open channel. She closed the door, looked once more at Mei's warning, and understood that the station's mystery began with a question of trust, not a ghost.

**繁中** 睡前，珊用簡單的身分確認訊息測試無線電。一個女子的聲音說：「珊，請到下層房間來。」卻沒有三音呼號。珊按下錄音鍵，始終沒有打開麥克風。一秒後，同一句話又傳來，連叫她名字前那聲輕咳都一模一樣。人可以重複話語，但完全相同的背景雜音就比較難解釋。珊記下時間，沒有在公開頻道播放錄音。她關上門，再看一眼梅的警告，明白氣象站的謎團始於「該相信誰」，而不是鬼魂。

### 理解測驗

**Q01. Why does Shan question the claim that Mei simply left?**

- A. She finds a hidden warning and no clear explanation of the disappearance.
- B. She sees Mei buy a bus ticket.
- C. The guard says that no radio was ever used.

**正解：A。** 珊找到梅藏起的水槽警告，且官方無法清楚解釋失蹤，因此不接受她自行離去的說法。

**Q02. What must happen before staff answer a radio voice?**

- A. The public clock must show midnight.
- B. The caller must give the three-note call sign.
- C. The antenna lamp must turn green.

**正解：B。** 第二條規則要求來電先發出三音呼號，不能只因聽見名字就回答。

## CHAPTER 02｜The Map with a Missing Path｜缺了一條路的地圖

### 正文與翻譯

**EN** The next morning, Shan followed the old mountain avenue from the staff house toward the research wing. A metal cradle once held fragile weather instruments near the door. Volunteers had used colored paper to decorate it for a public exhibition, but the decorations had become wet and heavy. The elevator to the observation deck remained locked, so Noah showed her a safer staircase. A child had left a drawing of a firework in the visitors' book. Shan noticed that the drawing used the same three strokes as the radio log. It might be a coincidence, yet it gave her a reason to examine the old displays before anyone cleared them away.

**繁中** 隔天早上，珊沿著員工宿舍通往研究翼的舊山路前進。門邊有個金屬托架，過去用來承放脆弱的氣象儀器。志工曾用色紙裝飾它，供公開展覽使用，如今裝飾卻被雨浸得又濕又重。通往觀測平台的電梯上了鎖，諾亞便帶她走較安全的樓梯。一本訪客留言簿裡，孩子畫了一朵煙火。珊發現畫中也有和通訊紀錄一樣的三道短線。這可能只是巧合，卻讓她決定在展品被清走前先仔細查看。

**EN** A painted sign said one gallon of water was enough for an emergency demonstration. Beside it, an outdated chart carried the name of the regional headquarters. The chart invited children to use their imagination to match cloud shapes with animals: a leopard, a bird, and a fish. Noah smiled at the magical pictures, but Shan noticed an odd blank space where a drainage line should have been. An organic stain had spread over that corner, and somebody had tied a green ribbon around the frame. Under the ribbon she found a date: the day Mei joined the station. The children had not invented the missing channel; the older chart had shown it plainly.

**繁中** 一塊畫過的告示說，緊急示範只需一加侖的水。旁邊的舊圖表印著區域總部名稱，讓孩子運用想像力，把雲形配成豹、鳥和魚。諾亞看著富有魔法感的圖畫笑了，珊卻注意到排水線應有的位置留著一塊古怪空白。那一角有天然污漬蔓延，還有人在框上綁了綠色絲帶。絲帶下方寫著日期：梅加入氣象站的那天。缺失的水道不是孩子想像出來的；舊圖明明畫得很清楚。

**EN** At the ridge, a steep section made walking difficult. A visitor had left a survey card reporting a threat of falling stones, although the tourist office's newer brochure called the route easy. Noah bent down to inspect a dark spot on the ground. He warned Shan not to spit into the soil sample or wipe away the marks with her glove. Their purpose was not to collect dramatic pictures but to preserve a measurable difference between the two maps. They photographed each stone, recorded their position, and returned to the safe trail when the wind rose. Shan marked the ridge as unverified rather than open or closed.

**繁中** 山脊有一段陡坡很難走。一位遊客曾在調查卡上記下落石威脅，但新的旅遊手冊卻稱那條路很容易。諾亞蹲下查看地面上的暗點，提醒珊不要朝土壤樣本吐口水，也別用手套擦掉痕跡。他們不是來收集刺激的照片，而是要保存兩張地圖之間可測量的差異。兩人拍下每顆石頭、記錄位置，風變大便退回安全步道。珊在圖上標註「尚未驗證」，沒有直接寫成開放或封閉。

**EN** Inside the visitor room, Shan found a printed teaching sheet about the station's communication history. It explained that the three strokes represented a call sign used before digital radios were installed. During public demonstrations, a guide would ring three small bells, then send the matching sound over a loudspeaker. A recording of that lesson might easily be mistaken for a real emergency call. Yet the voices Shan heard at night had not used the sign correctly. This made her less afraid of the sound and more concerned about the person who wanted her to follow it. She placed the page in a numbered evidence sleeve.

**繁中** 訪客室裡，珊找到一張印好的教學紙，介紹氣象站的通訊歷史。紙上說，三道短線代表數位無線電安裝前的呼號。公開示範時，導覽員會先敲三下小鐘，再用擴音器送出相同聲音。那份課程錄音很容易被誤聽成真正的緊急呼叫。然而珊夜裡聽到的聲音並沒有正確使用呼號。她因此不再那麼害怕聲音本身，反而更擔心有人故意誘她走向危險。她把教學紙收進編號證物袋。

**EN** Vale arrived before lunch and asked why she had examined the exhibition instead of replacing a sensor. Shan explained that old teaching materials could show what the newer map omitted. He answered that public illustrations were unreliable. She agreed they could be wrong, then asked for the dated engineering drawing behind them. Vale said the document was at headquarters, but he could not name a contact. His hesitation did not prove dishonesty. It did, however, mean the station's official account could not yet be checked. Shan sent a written request and copied it to the independent safety office so the question would not disappear into a private conversation.

**繁中** 午餐前維爾趕到，問她為何不換感測器，反而檢查展覽。珊解釋，舊教學資料可能顯示新版地圖省略了什麼。他說公共插圖不可靠。珊同意圖可能有錯，接著要求查看繪圖所依據、有日期的工程圖。維爾說文件在總部，卻講不出聯絡人。他的遲疑不能證明說謊，卻表示氣象站的官方說法目前仍無法核實。珊提出書面申請，副本送獨立安全辦公室，免得問題消失在私人談話裡。

**EN** Before sunset, Noah uncovered a second map under a shelf. It showed the south channel crossing beneath a narrow storage bridge. A red line had been drawn over the bridge after an earlier storm, while the printed route underneath remained visible. Shan could not tell whether the red mark indicated a closure or a proposed repair. She would need the inspection date, the person who made the mark, and proof that the order reached staff members. For the first time she could describe exactly what they did not know. That was not a failure of the investigation; it was a boundary that would keep them from walking blindly into the mountains.

**繁中** 日落前，諾亞在架子下找到第二張地圖。地圖顯示南側水道穿過一座狹窄的儲物橋。某次舊風暴後，有人在橋上畫了一道紅線，底下原有的路線仍看得見。珊還無法判斷紅線是封閉標記還是修繕建議。她需要檢查日期、畫線者身分，以及命令確實傳到工作人員手中的證據。這是她第一次能準確說出「我們不知道什麼」。那不是調查失敗，而是保護他們不盲目走進山裡的界線。

### 理解測驗

**Q03. What does the older education chart show that the new map omits?**

- A. A second public restaurant.
