# 封存試煉：未被允許遺忘的人

## The Sealed Ascension Trial: Names That Refused to Fade

- **代碼**：5B（第5級 B 本；重新生成第三版）
- **級距**：4000–5000 字級
- **題材**：成年雙男主搭檔／封存修仙試煉／制度懸疑與共同修復
- **規格**：25章；英語正文逐段附繁體中文全譯；正式詞表；每章2題英文理解題

**故事簡介**：三十二歲的前試煉考官羅衍與三十歲的拓印修復師江沐，為了各自被掩蓋的家人紀錄，調查十一年前突然封閉的「登天試煉」。試煉留下的鏡子能保留最後一項行動，卻不能恢復被奪走的記憶。當兩人發現當年的同意書遭替換，他們必須在真相、倖存者的隱私與重新開啟試煉的誘惑之間作出選擇。

> 本書為原創架空作品，制度與術法均屬虛構。第三版與此前兩個5B故事（送信旅程、山海救援）互不延續；配詞依專案提供的 `L5_B本_25章配詞.md`。

## CHAPTER 01｜The Roll Beneath the Ash｜灰燼下的名冊

### 正文與翻譯

**EN**

Luo Yan did not come to the mountain chapel for comfort. He came because an announcement promised certainty about the sealed Ascension Trial, eleven years after twenty-seven candidates entered and their names vanished from public rolls. A cluster of families stood outside the door. The bureau offered compensation for lost belongings, never for the memories its examination had taken. Luo recognized the handwriting of his younger brother in the notice's margin. Several observers wanted an immediate answer, but the pair refused to name a culprit before checking who had access. The two men made separate notes, then exchanged them only after each had finished his own account of the scene. They carried the unanswered question into the next room instead of pretending the first answer had been complete.

**繁中**

羅衍來到山上禮拜堂，不是為了尋求安慰。他是因為一份公告聲稱能確定十一年前封存的登天試煉真相：二十七名應試者入場後，姓名便從公開名冊消失。門外聚集一群家屬。官署只願補償遺失物品，卻不提試煉奪走的記憶。羅衍認出公告頁邊是弟弟的字跡。幾名旁觀者想立刻得到答案，兩人卻拒絕在查清誰有權接觸資料前指認犯人。兩人分開記筆記，等各自寫完對現場的描述，才交換比對。他們把未解的問題帶進下一間房，沒有假裝第一個答案已經完整。

**EN**

Jiang Mu, a thirty-year-old restorer of paper rubbings, asked the officials to disclose the inventory before the public forum began. The young clerks were envious of his access to private collections, though that access had never restored his mother's reputation. A generator behind the lectern powered the truth-display screen, but the screen showed only a polished summary. Jiang carried the scorched original from his mother's workshop and refused to surrender it. For the first time, the abandoned examination felt less like a legend and more like an event involving actual people. They measured the gap twice, drew a clear mark, and refused to continue until the observer agreed with the reading. Their route became longer, yet the evidence would survive even if they later changed their minds about what it meant.

**繁中**

三十歲的拓印修復師江沐要求官員在公開論壇開始前揭露庫存清單。年輕書吏羨慕他能接觸私人收藏，卻不知道這種便利始終無法替他母親洗清污名。講臺後的發電機供應真相展示屏電力，螢幕卻只播放經過修飾的摘要。江沐帶著母親工坊留下的燒焦原件，拒絕交出去。那場被遺棄的考核第一次不像傳說，而像一件牽涉真實人物的事件。他們量了兩次間距，畫下清楚的標記，直到觀察者確認讀值才繼續。路線因此變長，但即使日後改變對證據的理解，證據本身仍會保留下來。

**EN**

The sealed hall stood above a protected habitat of silver moss, part of the valley's living heritage. Its immense stone steps had once promised advancement to anyone with enough skill. Luo felt an impulse to climb them immediately and search for his brother. Jiang stopped him with a question: if the stairs consumed a written memory every time someone crossed, whose name was Luo prepared to lose? The question forced him to look at the gate instead of the summit. Even a correct conclusion could do harm if they published private names before the people involved were ready. Luo Yan checked the witness column while Jiang Mu compared the paper with the rubbing he carried in a narrow case. He did not promise that everything would be restored. He promised only that nobody would be erased without being heard.

**繁中**

封閉的殿堂座落在銀苔保護棲地上方，也是山谷活生生的文化遺產。巨大石階過去宣稱凡是有本領的人都能向上晉升。羅衍衝動地想立刻登階尋弟。江沐問他：如果每踏過石階就會耗去一段寫下的記憶，他打算失去誰的名字？這句話迫使羅衍先看向門，而不是峰頂。即使結論正確，若在當事人準備好之前公開私人姓名，也可能造成傷害。羅衍核對見證人欄位，江沐則拿狹長匣中的拓印與紙張比對。他沒有承諾一切都能恢復，只承諾不再有人未獲聆聽就遭抹除。

**EN**

Luo's former training gave him one insight: the door had three fixed rules. Two living witnesses had to approve any entrance; the audit mirror kept the final action of each chamber; the stair erased a named written memory and could not reverse the loss. A metropolitan safety officer admitted that review was optional under an overlooked provision. Jiang asked that those words be copied into the minutes, where they could no longer be hidden beneath ceremony. They checked the wording aloud, because a rule that sounded harmless in a whisper could become cruel when enforced. Before leaving, they repeated the safety condition and confirmed that every participant understood where the exit stood. The next door remained closed, and for once that delay felt like a choice rather than another punishment.

**繁中**

羅衍從過去的訓練想起一項關鍵洞察：這扇門有三條固定規則。任何入場都要兩名活著的見證人同意；稽核鏡會保留每間試煉房最後一個動作；石階會抹去一段有姓名的書面記憶，而且無法逆轉。都會派來的安全官承認，一條被忽略的條款把審查列為可選程序。江沐要求將這段話寫入會議紀錄。他們把條文大聲讀出，因為小聲說來無害的規則，一旦強制執行，可能變得殘酷。離開之前，他們重述安全條件，確認每位參與者都知道出口在哪裡。下一扇門仍緊閉，但這次的延遲終於像一種選擇，而不是另一種懲罰。

**EN**

A boy stumbled over the loose floorboards just as the supervisor reached for Jiang's document. Luo caught the boy rather than the document; Jiang recovered his page himself. The supervisor called the offer a fair transaction between the bureau and families. Outside, silver moss began to wither under the hall's leaking heat. Luo and Jiang exchanged names, not promises, and agreed that the first proof must come from a living witness who could safely say no. Neither man believed courage required a grand speech; sometimes it meant waiting beside a frightened stranger. The older examiner could offer advice, but neither man allowed him to speak on behalf of the surviving candidates. Whatever came next would be judged against the same rules, including those that protected someone they disliked.

**繁中**

監督官伸手要拿江沐文件時，一個男孩被鬆動地板絆倒。羅衍先扶住男孩，而江沐自己收回紙張。監督官稱官署與家屬的安排是一筆公平交易。殿外的銀苔卻因殿內漏出的熱氣開始枯萎。羅衍與江沐交換姓名，沒有輕率許諾，只同意第一份證據必須來自能安全拒絕的活證人。兩人都不認為勇氣需要豪言壯語；有時勇氣只是陪在受驚的陌生人身邊等待。年長考官可以提供建議，兩人卻不允許他替倖存的應試者發言。無論接下來發生什麼，都要遵守同一套規則，包括保護他們不喜歡的人的規則。

### 理解測驗

**1. Why does Luo Yan refuse to climb the old stairs immediately?**

- A. He learns that each crossing consumes a written memory.
- B. The chapel has no visitors.
- C. Jiang says every candidate has returned.

**答案：A。** 石階會奪走一段具名書面記憶，而且損失不可逆，因此羅衍不立刻攀登。

**2. What review rule do the investigators find?**

- A. Only the richest family may enter.
- B. A supervised review is optional under an overlooked provision.
- C. Every observer must abandon their name.

**答案：B。** 一項被忽略的條款容許受監督的審查。

## CHAPTER 02｜The Road That Changes Names｜會改寫姓名的山路

### 正文與翻譯

**EN**

The most beneficial route to the old records crossed a ridge where stone markers could convert a traveler's written name into a gate number. A corporate foundation had purchased the land below, promising a museum as the destination of its redevelopment. Luo found the purchase suspicious, but Jiang warned him that a contract alone proved no crime. Their first errand was to retrieve the land map without stepping on the marked stones. They separated the evidence from their anger, although anger was often the only thing that kept them on their feet. They left a copy in public custody and another with the witnesses, so no single official could silently replace both. Before sleep they compared their notes, corrected one mistake each, and decided not to hide either correction.

**繁中**

前往舊紀錄最有利的路線必須翻過山脊；沿途石標會把旅人的書面姓名轉換成門號。一家企業基金會買下山下土地，聲稱改建後的終點是博物館。羅衍懷疑交易有問題，江沐卻提醒他，單憑合約不能證明犯罪。兩人的第一項差事，是不踩上石標地取回土地圖。他們努力把證據與憤怒分開，儘管憤怒時常是支撐他們站穩的唯一力量。他們把一份副本交公家保管，另一份留給證人，避免任何一名官員悄悄替換兩份紀錄。睡前兩人比對筆記，各自修正一個錯誤，並決定不隱瞞任何修改。

**EN**

At the hill clinic, an epidemic of lost sleep had spread among former candidates, yet one elderly attendant displayed an extraordinary memory for physical routes. She helped them haul a chest of maps from a locked cellar. The foundation denied liability for the clinic's closure, and its lawyer urged them to leave. Luo felt a sudden plunge in confidence when the attendant called him by his brother's name; she had known both men before the trial. A person could be brave and frightened at the same time; the task was to choose with open eyes. They could have concealed their disagreement, but instead stated both views to the witnesses and invited correction. They could not control the entire institution, but they could control whether their own account was faithful.

**繁中**

山間診所裡，昔日應試者正普遍受到失眠症困擾；一名年長侍者卻對路線保有非凡記憶。她協助兩人從鎖著的地窖拖出裝地圖的箱子。基金會否認對診所關閉負有責任，律師也催他們離開。侍者突然喊出羅衍弟弟的名字時，羅衍信心陡然下墜；原來她在試煉前就認識兄弟倆。一個人可以同時勇敢又恐懼；重要的是睜大眼睛作出選擇。他們本可隱藏歧見，卻向證人坦承兩種看法，並邀請對方指正。他們無法掌控整個制度，卻能決定自己的敘述是否忠實。

**EN**

Jiang attached a reminder to the chest: nobody could treat the map as testimony until its source was confirmed. The first respondent at the clinic remembered a bell on the eastern ridge, whereas a broken sensor there recorded three bells. Jiang asked both accounts to remain visible. The temptation to dismiss the respondent was strong, but Luo knew a mistaken witness could still lead them toward a real object that the officials had ignored. The simplest question remained the hardest: who had been allowed to choose, and who had merely been told to obey? A warning bell sounded once. They stepped back, checked the floor, and resumed only when the signal was explained. The question of trust had changed: it was no longer who seemed pure, but who allowed a record to be checked.

**繁中**

江沐在箱子上貼上提醒：來源確認之前，不能把地圖當成證詞。診所的第一位受訪者記得東方山脊上有一座鐘，壞掉的感測器卻記錄過三次鐘響。江沐要求兩種說法都保留。羅衍原想否定受訪者，最後明白即使記憶有誤，也可能帶他們找到官員忽略的實物。最簡單的問題仍然最難：誰曾被允許選擇，誰只是被命令服從？警鐘響了一聲；他們先後退、檢查地面，確認訊號意義後才繼續。信任的問題已經改變：不再是誰看起來純潔，而是誰願意讓紀錄接受檢查。

**EN**

In the kitchen the attendant served a bowl of stew while Luo repaired a torch that used a harmless blue flame. She described the old examination as a transformation of fear into obedience, not the ascent promised on its posters. Jiang carefully trimmed the damaged map's edges and found a second date beneath a pasted label. The woman became vocal when she saw it: that was the day she had been ordered to burn the attendance roll. Every repaired line on the map offered a possibility, not a guarantee, and their next decision had to respect that difference. For a moment their hands met over the same page. Neither pulled away, but the conversation stayed focused on the case. When the bell fell silent, each man asked the other whether to proceed. This time both answers were freely given.

**繁中**

廚房裡，侍者端上燉菜；羅衍則修好一支使用無害藍焰的火把。她說舊考試帶來的轉變不是海報宣稱的飛升，而是讓恐懼變成服從。江沐小心修整地圖破邊，發現貼紙下面另有日期。侍者看到後大聲說，那正是她被命令焚燒簽到名冊的一天。地圖上每條修復的線都只是可能性，不是保證；下一個決定必須尊重這個差別。兩人的手一度在同一頁紙上碰到；誰都沒有退開，談話卻仍聚焦於案件。鐘聲停下後，兩人互問是否繼續；這一次，兩個答案都是自由作出的。

**EN**

The foundation offered a reward for the maps, calling them obsolete property. Jiang chose to photograph every seal first. Luo carried the original back to the clinic instead of risking the ridge after dark. Their small success was not the discovery of a secret master, but evidence that someone had altered an attendance date. The attendant agreed to testify only if her sleeping patients would not be identified; both investigators accepted that limit. A clean copy would have been easier to read, but the crossed-out words revealed more about the decision that mattered. They refused to break the lock in secret; instead, they gathered two witnesses and recorded the reason for opening it. Nothing was settled forever, yet they had made one part of the future less dependent on secrecy.

**繁中**

基金會出價收購地圖，稱它們已是過時財產。江沐選擇先拍下每道印記，羅衍則把原件送回診所，不冒險在天黑後越過山脊。他們的小小進展不是找到神祕主使，而是確認有人修改簽到日期。侍者答應作證，條件是不能揭露失眠病患身分；兩人都接受。乾淨的抄本比較好讀，然而劃掉的文字更能揭露關鍵決定如何形成。兩人拒絕私下撬鎖，而是找來兩名見證人，記錄開鎖的理由。沒有什麼能永遠塵埃落定，但他們已讓未來的一部分不再依賴祕密。
