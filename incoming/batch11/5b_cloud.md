# 雲獸不肯飛：兩個凡人的春天

## The Cloud Herd: A Spring for Two Grounded Cultivators

- **書籍代碼**：5B（第5級 B 本；全新第四部獨立創作）
- **詞彙級距**：4000–5000 字級
- **題材**：成年雙男主／凡人仙俠／雲獸遷徙／生態旅程／互相尊重的成長與關係
- **主要人物**：葉青（Ye Qing，33 歲，動物照護員）；安知（An Zhi，35 歲，風樂器工匠與前能源工程師）
- **故事簡介**：雲獸突然停止遷徙，受傷的幼獸被遺留在蘆葦谷。葉青救下牠，與曾參與建造禁錮雲獸高塔的工匠安知結伴，沿著受損的草甸、城鎮與山路尋找獸群。他們必須在動物自由、城市用電與居民生計之間，修出一條不需強迫任何生命前進的道路。
- **世界規則**：雲獸靠長輩教會的三音呼喚辨識遷徙方向；受困時會在毛皮累積電荷，需在濕地吃草安全散去；每隔一晚需停留在活的開花氣苔草甸，無法靠瞬間法術替代。

> 本書為原創架空奇幻故事。這一版與前三部 5B（送信旅程／山海救援／封存試煉）的人物、故事及結局互不延續。配詞使用專案原始 `L5_B本_25章配詞.md`，保留原詞條、詞性、原始 ID 與原章節分配。

## CHAPTER 01｜The Calf at Reed Chapel｜蘆葦禮拜堂的小獸

### 正文與翻譯

**EN**

Nobody in Reed Valley possessed any certainty about why the cloud beasts had stopped migrating. At dawn, a cluster of villagers gathered outside the stone chapel where the animals had traditionally rested before crossing the eastern mountains. Ye Qing, a thirty-three-year-old animal keeper, had found a calf tangled in a broken fence. Its fur held tiny sparks, and its feet shook whenever the wind changed. A city official promised compensation for damaged fields, provided the villagers allowed his crew to take the calf away. Ye did not accept. He placed a bowl of wet grass beside the frightened creature, then waited until it lowered its head. The calf ate two mouthfuls and moved closer to his hand. It was not tame, but it had made the first choice between them.

**繁中**

蘆葦谷裡沒有人能確定雲獸為何停止遷徙。黎明時，一群村民聚集在石造禮拜堂外；過去，雲獸總會在越過東方群山之前在此歇息。三十三歲的動物照護員葉青發現一隻幼獸被破損的圍欄纏住。牠的毛間閃著細小電火花，每當風向改變，四隻腳便顫抖。城裡來的官員承諾賠償受損農田，條件是村民准許工作隊帶走幼獸。葉青沒有答應。他將一碗濕草放在受驚的幼獸旁，耐心等候，直到牠低頭。幼獸吃了兩口，向他的手靠近。牠並未被馴服，但牠在兩者之間作出了第一個選擇。

**EN**

The official wanted to disclose his plan at an afternoon forum, where a generator would project impressive diagrams onto the chapel wall. An Zhi, a thirty-five-year-old maker of wind instruments, stood at the back of the room and listened. Some villagers were envious of the city's bright towers and the cheap power they supplied. An had once helped design those towers, but he left that job after he learned how their cages held living animals in place. He said nothing about his past yet. Instead, he noticed that the calf's breathing slowed whenever the old bell above the doorway rang three uneven notes. Ye asked him to ring it again. This time the animal raised its head before the final note, as if expecting a voice that had not arrived.

**繁中**

官員打算在下午的公開論壇揭露計畫，並用發電機將精美的圖表投射在禮拜堂牆上。三十五歲的風樂器工匠安知站在人群後方聆聽。有些村民羨慕城市的明亮高塔，以及高塔提供的廉價電力。安知曾參與那些高塔的設計，卻在發現籠舍如何將活生生的動物困在原處後離職。此刻他尚未提起自己的過去。相反地，他注意到門口上方的舊鐘敲出三個不等長的音時，幼獸的呼吸便緩和下來。葉青請他再敲一次。這次幼獸在最後一個音響起之前就抬頭，彷彿在等待一個始終沒有到來的聲音。

**EN**

Behind the chapel lay a narrow habitat of flowering air moss. It was part of the valley's heritage, maintained by families who had followed the herd for generations. The old migratory route was immense, stretching beyond seven mountain districts, but every animal still needed a living patch of moss every second night. Ye felt an impulse to carry the calf directly toward the distant sky. An warned him that cloud beasts navigated by a call learned from their elders; a human imitation might send a young animal toward a cliff. Their coats also stored electrical charge when confined. Only grazing on damp ground let that energy escape safely. The city cages therefore offered shelter in appearance while increasing the danger. Ye stroked the air beside the calf, careful not to touch its sparking neck.

**繁中**

禮拜堂後方是一條狹窄、長滿開花氣苔的棲地。那是山谷代代相傳的自然遺產，由長年跟隨獸群的家族照料。古老遷徙路線極其廣大，橫跨七個以上的山區，但每隻雲獸仍須每隔一晚踏上活著的苔地。葉青一時衝動，想直接抱著幼獸往遠方天空走。安知提醒他，雲獸依靠長輩教會的呼喚聲辨認方向；人類胡亂模仿可能將幼獸引向懸崖。牠們被關住時，毛皮也會累積電荷。唯有在潮濕地面上吃草，電能才能安全散去。城市的籠舍因此表面上像避難所，實際卻增加危險。葉青輕撫幼獸身旁的空氣，刻意不碰牠閃著電光的頸部。

**EN**

An offered one useful insight: the bell did not need to imitate the herd. It could be used to mark silence between genuine calls, giving the calf space to listen. A metropolitan council rule required the town to surrender stray cloud beasts, but its emergency provision allowed a local caregiver to hold an injured animal for three days. The public hearing was optional only if everyone agreed to the surrender. Ye insisted on reading the rule aloud before anyone voted. An had grown used to officials presenting convenient facts as permanent laws; he was surprised when Ye asked the villagers what they wanted rather than instructing them. They chose three days of care and a public count of the remaining moss beds. That small decision kept the animal on its own ground.

**繁中**

安知提出一項重要見解：鐘聲不必模仿獸群。它可以標示真正獸鳴之間的靜默，讓幼獸有時間聽清。都會議會的規定要求小鎮交出走失的雲獸，但緊急條款准許當地照護員暫留受傷動物三天。只有在所有人同意移交時，公開聽證才可省略。葉青堅持在表決前大聲讀出條文。安知早已習慣官員把方便自己的說法當作永恆的法律；當葉青詢問村民意願，而不是直接指示時，他有些意外。村民決定先照護三天，並公開清點剩餘苔地。這個小小決定讓幼獸得以留在自己的土地上。

**EN**

As the official's supervisor approached, a child began to stumble across the loose chapel steps. An caught the child, while Ye held the calf away from the crowd. The supervisor offered An a profitable transaction: build a better fence and forget the migration route. An declined without explaining why the offer made his hands shake. Beyond the courtyard, the moss had begun to wither where hot air from the city machines blew across the valley. If it disappeared, the herd would have no safe resting place even if the calf recovered. That evening Ye invited An to walk the old trail with him. He did not ask for loyalty. He asked whether An could listen carefully enough to discover which direction the calf was already trying to go. An agreed to stay for the first night.

**繁中**

官員的主管走近時，一名孩子在鬆動的禮拜堂臺階上踉蹌。安知扶住孩子，葉青則讓幼獸遠離人群。主管向安知提出一樁有利可圖的交易：替城市建造更好的圍欄，並忘掉遷徙路線。安知拒絕了，卻沒說明為什麼這提議讓他的雙手顫抖。庭院外，城市機械送來的熱風掠過山谷，苔地已開始枯萎。如果苔地消失，即使幼獸康復，獸群也沒有安全的休息場所。傍晚，葉青邀安知一起走古道。他沒有要求忠誠，只問安知能否仔細傾聽，找出幼獸原本就想前往的方向。安知答應留下第一晚。

### 理解測驗

**1. Why does Ye refuse to let the official take the calf immediately?**

- A. The calf needs care and freedom to choose.
- B. The calf belongs to Ye as a pet.
- C. The chapel is too crowded.

**答案：A。** 葉青知道幼獸受傷，需要安全照護和自主選擇，而非立即被關入城市籠舍。

**2. What does An learn about the chapel bell?**

- A. It controls the weather.
- B. Its pauses can help the calf listen for real calls.
- C. It turns moss into electricity.

**答案：B。** 鐘聲可以標示真正獸鳴之間的靜默，不必模仿獸群叫聲。

## CHAPTER 02｜The Red Ridge｜紅色山脊

### 正文與翻譯

**EN**

The next morning the calf made a low call toward the red ridge, beyond which the older cloud beasts had last been seen. Ye believed the journey could be beneficial if they traveled slowly and let the animal choose its own pace. An offered to convert an abandoned handcart into a resting platform, but a corporate contractor claimed the cart as company property. Its painted number had almost vanished, and no one could find a receipt. Rather than argue, An built a lighter frame from the chapel's fallen branches. Their destination was not the city sanctuary advertised on every sign. It was a high meadow where the herd had gathered each spring. The calf stepped onto the platform only after Ye spread fresh moss across its floor. An noticed that permission mattered to the creature more than comfort did.

**繁中**

隔天早晨，幼獸朝紅色山脊低鳴；長大的雲獸最後一次出現，就在山脊另一側。葉青認為，只要慢慢走並讓幼獸自己決定步調，這段旅程可能有益。安知提議把廢棄手推車改成休息平臺，但企業承包商宣稱車子屬於公司。車上的漆字幾乎全褪了色，卻沒人找得到收據。安知沒有爭執，而是利用禮拜堂掉落的樹枝另做一個較輕的架子。他們的目的地不是告示牌上到處宣傳的城市保護所，而是每年春天獸群聚集的高山草甸。葉青在平臺上鋪好新鮮苔草後，幼獸才自行踏上去。安知發現，對牠而言，選擇權比舒適更重要。

**EN**

At the village gate a healer warned them about an epidemic of coughing among the pack animals. She gave Ye an errand: carry clean cloth to the ridge shelter, where travelers had run out of supplies. Her young assistant called the calf extraordinary and begged to come along. Ye explained that excitement could frighten a sick creature as easily as cruelty. The route required them to haul the platform over loose stones, and every sharp turn made its wooden wheels complain. The contractor warned that if they damaged the public path, the village would face legal liability. An quietly recorded the cart's weight and the width of its wheels. He had learned from old mistakes that a good intention did not cancel the responsibility to protect everyone who used the same road.

**繁中**

村口一名醫者警告他們，馱獸之間正流行咳嗽疾病。她交給葉青一件差事：把乾淨布料送到物資耗盡的山脊小屋。年輕助手說幼獸非同尋常，央求同行。葉青解釋，過分興奮和殘忍一樣，都可能驚嚇病中的動物。他們必須拖著平臺越過鬆動石塊，每次急轉彎都讓木輪發出呻吟。承包商警告他們，若損壞公共道路，村莊就得負起法律責任。安知默默記下車子的重量與輪子寬度。他從過去的錯誤學到，出於善意也不能免除保護共同道路使用者的責任。

**EN**

Near the summit a slope suddenly gave way, and a stream of red gravel began to plunge toward the path. An pushed the platform into a hollow while Ye held the calf steady. A reminder from his old apprenticeship came back to An: never trust one reading from an unfamiliar instrument. He questioned a road survey respondent who had seen small cracks there the previous week. Together they examined a cracked stone sensor and discovered that it had been placed on solid rock, far from the sliding soil. The rider's evidence was more useful than the impressive display at the village office. Ye offered the traveler a bowl of warm stew and listened without interrupting. The calf watched from beneath the cart, breathing in rhythm with the slow movement of their hands.

**繁中**

接近山頂時，一處坡面突然鬆脫，紅色碎石朝小路急墜而下。安知把平臺推進凹地，葉青則穩住幼獸。安知想起學徒時的一項提醒：不熟悉的儀器只讀一次，絕不能輕信。他詢問一名道路調查受訪者，對方上週曾看見那裡出現細縫。兩人檢查裂損的石製感測器，發現它被安裝在堅硬岩石上，遠離正在滑動的土壤。旅人的目擊資料，比村公所華麗的展示還有用。葉青請對方喝一碗熱燉湯，安靜聆聽，沒有插嘴。幼獸躲在車底，呼吸逐漸跟上兩人緩慢的手勢。

**EN**

At twilight, smoke from a distant cooking fire formed a silver line above the ridge. An felt the temptation to take a shortcut along an exposed ledge, but Ye pointed to the dark clouds gathering above it. They lit a torch at the shelter and waited for the wind to settle. The keeper there showed them a drawing of the landscape before the energy towers arrived: a wide ribbon of flowers connected every resting meadow. The transformation had been gradual. First workers trim the grass to make room for poles; then heat from the cables dries the soil; finally the moss stops flowering. The keeper was vocal about his anger, yet he allowed the calf to sleep before asking questions. An realized that restoring a route would require more than moving a fence.

**繁中**

黃昏時，遠處炊煙在山脊上方拉成一條銀線。安知很想走曝露在外的窄崖捷徑，但葉青指著上方聚起的烏雲。他們在小屋點起火把，等待風勢平息。小屋管理人拿出能源高塔興建前的風景圖：一整條寬闊花帶串連各處休息草甸。這場轉變是逐步發生的。工人先修剪草地替電桿騰出空間，接著電纜熱氣使土壤乾燥，最後氣苔不再開花。管理人憤怒地直言不諱，卻仍讓幼獸先睡，再來提問。安知意識到，恢復遷徙路線絕不只是移走一道圍籬。

**EN**

During the night the calf woke and pressed one foot into the wet soil at the shelter door. A blue spark escaped harmlessly, leaving a round print that gleamed for an instant. Ye showed An the mark and explained why damp ground must remain part of every resting place. An drew a new route, with a note beside each possible meadow stating when the last flowers had opened. He wanted to see the whole herd, but refused to promise they would find it at once. At first light the coughing animals in the shelter were better, and the healer's cloth had arrived in time. The two men set off again with more supplies, one old map, and a young creature that had begun to recognize their footsteps but still listened for its own kind.

**繁中**

夜裡幼獸醒來，把一隻腳按在小屋門前濕潤的土壤上。一道藍色電光無害地逸散，留下短暫發亮的圓形腳印。葉青指給安知看，解釋為何每個休息地都必須保留潮濕地面。安知重新繪製路線，在每片可能的草甸旁記下最後一次開花時間。他想親眼看到整個獸群，卻不肯保證立刻就能找到。天剛亮，小屋裡咳嗽的馱獸已有好轉，醫者託付的布料也及時送達。兩人帶著更多補給、一張舊地圖，以及一隻開始辨認他們腳步、卻仍傾聽同伴呼喚的幼獸再次出發。
