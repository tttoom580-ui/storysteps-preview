# 星光送不到的第七封信
## The Seventh Letter Beyond the Stars

**代碼：** 2A（全新重寫版）  
**級距：** 第2級｜1000–2000 字級  
**題材：** 凡人信使／觀星塔失聯／山谷路線與燈光通訊／合作成長（無戀愛主線）  
**正式配詞來源：** `L2_A本_25章配詞.md`；25章、501個原表目標詞 ID  

二十歲的山谷信使梅發現觀星塔突然失去與七座村莊的燈光聯繫。她與觀星學徒安循著舊地圖、河道、森林和避難屋逐步恢復訊息傳遞，卻發現最遙遠的第七村早已被認定為「無人居住」。要把最後一封信送到收信人手裡，他們必須找到真正安全的路，也讓各村有權回覆、修正並守護屬於自己的警報。

## CHAPTER 01｜The Tower Without a Star｜沒有星光的高塔

### 正文與翻譯（英文正文 656 詞）

**EN**
Mei took the steep approach to Starwatch Tower before sunset. From its balcony she could see the field below, the stone base of an old seesaw, and the narrow valley beyond. A windy sky made her pull her coat tight. The tower operator had promised to light seven distant lamps for the winter travelers, yet tonight not one shone. Mei was twenty, a messenger rather than a magician. She had no special speed or power. She had only a bag of letters and a belief that a message should reach the person who needed it.

**繁中**
梅在日落前沿著陡路走向觀星塔。從塔的陽臺，她看得到下方的田地、一座舊翹翹板的石製底座，以及更遠處的狹長山谷。多風的天空讓她拉緊外套。高塔操作員原本答應為冬季旅人點亮七座遠方燈塔，今晚卻一盞都沒有亮。二十歲的梅是信使，不是魔法師。她沒有特別快的速度，也沒有神奇力量，只有一袋信，以及相信訊息應送到需要的人手中的信念。

**EN**
Inside the tower, An was trying to fix a glass wheel. He was a young male student of the stars, but he did not pretend to know everything. The challenge was not simply to make the wheel turn. Light had to travel in the correct order, or a traveler might walk toward a closed pass. An could not deny that the old system was failing. He asked Mei to discuss a better way to carry warnings while he examined the glass. Together they decided to develop a written record of every lamp that still worked.

**繁中**
塔內，安正試著修好一面玻璃輪。他是年輕的男性觀星學徒，卻不假裝什麼都懂。他們的挑戰不只是讓輪子轉動。光必須依照正確順序傳遞，否則旅人可能走向封閉的山口。安不能否認舊系統正在失效。他請梅討論更好的警示傳送方式，自己則檢查玻璃。兩人決定共同建立紀錄，寫下每一盞仍能使用的燈。

**EN**
Before opening the old map, Mei asked An to show her how the tower had worked in an ordinary winter. He pointed to a glass wheel and seven small marks around its edge. The wheel did not know whether anyone was safe; it only sent light. Each village had once kept a person near its own lamp, and those people had answered one another. Mei copied the order of the marks into her notebook. She was surprised that the most useful part of the system was not a clever machine but the quiet work of people who stayed awake to receive a message.

**繁中**
打開舊地圖前，梅請安示範高塔在平常冬季如何運作。他指著玻璃輪和邊緣的七個小記號。輪子不知道任何人是否安全，它只會送出光。從前每村都安排人在燈旁值守，彼此回覆。梅把記號順序抄進筆記，驚訝地發現最有用的部分不是巧妙機器，而是那些守夜接訊的人所做的安靜工作。

**EN**
On a secondary shelf Mei found a map of the nation, marked with seven small stars. For instance, the western star stood for a village reached by an underground freight train. Children called the short line a subway, though it carried boxes rather than passengers. Under the map was a note about a tradition: each village must answer the next village before a warning is trusted. The tower had stopped receiving replies three days ago. This was not only a broken machine; it was a broken conversation between places.

**繁中**
梅在第二層書架上找到一張全國地圖，上面標著七顆小星星。例如，西邊的星星代表一座可以搭地下貨運列車抵達的村落。孩子們把那條短線叫作地下鐵，雖然它載的是箱子而不是乘客。地圖下方有張紙，寫著一項傳統：每個村子都必須回答下一個村子，警告才能被相信。三天前高塔就收不到回覆了。壞掉的不只是機器，而是各個地方之間的對話。

**EN**
The tower keeper returned after dark, tired and embarrassed. He told them the last light had gone out while he was looking for a missing message bag. Mei offered to walk to the first village at dawn. An said she might find only empty roads. She answered that even an empty road could tell them something if they observed it carefully. They agreed on one rule before leaving: no one would announce that a path was safe without hearing from a person who had actually crossed it.

**繁中**
天黑後守塔人回來了，疲憊又難堪。他說最後一盞燈是在他尋找失蹤信袋時熄滅的。梅主動提出天亮就走到第一個村落。安說她可能只會遇見空蕩的道路。梅回答，即使道路空無一人，只要仔細觀察，也能帶回線索。出發前，他們同意一條原則：沒有實際走過那條路的人回報，就不能宣稱它是安全的。

**EN**
They carried their notes to the balcony and tried to find all seven hills with their eyes. Two were easy to see; three were hidden behind trees, and the last two lay beyond a high ridge. An explained that a strong light could reach some places directly but never all of them. Mei asked how the last village had received messages in the past. An could not answer. That was important: the person who knew the tower best still had a gap in his knowledge. Mei wrote the question in large letters and left space below it for an answer.

**繁中**
兩人帶著筆記走到陽臺，想用眼睛找出七座山丘。兩座很清楚，三座藏在樹後，最後兩座則在高山脊之後。安解釋，強光可以直接照到某些地方，卻不可能照到全部。梅問最後一村過去如何收訊，安答不上來。這很重要：最熟悉高塔的人也有不知道的事。梅用大字寫下問題，下面留空等答案。

**EN**
At the foot of the tower, a mother waited for news of her daughter across the pass. Mei could not promise a quick answer. She wrote down the daughter’s name and the last day anyone had seen her. The mother thanked her for not pretending. As Mei walked home, the dark tower looked less like a dead building and more like a question standing over the valley. For the first time she understood that delivering letters might also mean helping seven villages listen to one another again.

**繁中**
塔腳下，一位母親等著山口另一端女兒的消息。梅無法保證很快得到答案，只記下女兒的姓名，以及最後有人看見她的日期。母親感謝她沒有假裝知道一切。梅走回家時，漆黑的高塔看起來不像死去的建築，反而像一個立在山谷上方的問題。她第一次明白，送信也可能意味著幫助七個村莊重新聽見彼此。

## CHAPTER 02｜The Map Behind the Mirror｜鏡子後面的地圖

### 正文與翻譯（英文正文 677 詞）

**EN**
The next morning Mei brought An to a barber who lived near the tower road. From a particular angle, his window reflected the western hill perfectly. The barber said his father had once helped the tower workers set their lamps. He still kept a wooden board with their marks. Mei thanked him and said she could appreciate a person who saved old things. He warned them to avoid the deer path after rain, because its stones moved under a traveler’s feet. Outside, a dove sat calmly on the roof.

**繁中**
隔天早晨，梅帶安到塔路附近一位理髮師的店裡。從某個特定角度看，他的窗戶能完整映出西邊山丘。理髮師說父親曾幫守塔人調整燈光，至今仍保留一塊刻著記號的木板。梅道謝，說她很欣賞會保存舊物的人。理髮師提醒他們雨後別走鹿群的小徑，因為腳下的石頭會滑動。屋外，一隻鴿子平靜地停在屋頂。

**EN**
He showed them an old edition of the valley textbook. It said the seven lamps had been built by a workers’ organization, not by the tower alone. Its first president had insisted that even the smallest village receive a light. That village belonged to a minority community on the high slope. On the newest map, its star had become a tiny spot, easy to miss. An looked at the huge blank space around it and said, “A place does not stop existing because someone draws it smaller.”

**繁中**
他拿出一本舊版的山谷教科書。書上記載七座燈塔是工人組織建立的，而不是觀星塔獨自完成。第一任會長堅持連最小的村落也該得到燈光。那座村子位於高坡，是少數族群的聚居地。在新版地圖上，它的星星縮成一個極小、容易被忽略的點。安看著周圍大片空白，說：「不會因為有人把地方畫小，那個地方就不存在了。」

**EN**
The barber’s wife joined them with a box of old photographs. Each picture had a date, a name, and sometimes a note about the weather. One photograph showed a crowded wagon standing below the missing western star. Mei asked whether the wagon had carried people away from a storm. The wife said it had carried medicine in. She remembered because her younger sister was ill that winter. An placed the picture beside the map. The tiny spot on the new page had once been a busy place where people came together when someone needed help.

**繁中**
理髮師的妻子帶著一盒舊照片加入談話。每張照片都有日期、名字，有時還記著天氣。一張照片裡，一輛擠滿物品的馬車停在被省去的西方星號下。梅問馬車是否曾載人躲避風暴。妻子說車是送藥來的，因為那年冬天她妹妹病了。安把照片放到地圖旁。新圖上一個極小的點，從前卻是人們在需要幫助時聚集的熱鬧地方。

**EN**
The barber offered them tea made from a sour mountain fruit. While Mei drank, a painful memory returned: last winter she had carried a letter there and found the road gate locked. No one in the tower had explained why. Now she noticed a line across the old map that was missing from the new one. It led to a lantern station under a hanging rock. The map gave no secret door or wonderful spell, only the place where travelers had once stopped to exchange news.

**繁中**
理髮師端出用酸澀山果泡的茶。梅喝茶時想起一段難受的回憶：去年冬天她曾替那座村莊送信，卻發現道路大門上了鎖。塔裡沒有人解釋原因。如今她注意到舊地圖上有一條線，新地圖卻沒有。線通往懸岩下的一座提燈站。地圖沒有祕門或神奇法術，只標示旅人從前停下交換消息的地方。

**EN**
They copied both maps and asked the barber whether he would speak at a public meeting. He said he needed time to think. Mei accepted his answer. An wanted to announce their discovery immediately, but Mei reminded him that a drawing alone did not prove a road was still open. They would visit it first. The barber put the old book back in its box, as carefully as if it were a living bird. Before they left, he gave them the name of a woman who once carried letters for the western village.

**繁中**
兩人抄下兩張地圖，並問理髮師願不願在公開會議發言。他說需要時間考慮，梅接受了。安想立刻宣布新發現，但梅提醒他，一張圖不足以證明路現在仍可通行。他們得親自走一趟。理髮師把舊書放回盒子，動作小心，彷彿那是活著的鳥。離開前，他給了他們一位曾替西村送信的婦人的名字。

**EN**
Mei offered to return the photographs before sunset. The couple said she could borrow copies but asked that the family names remain private. An took time to draw the wagon and the old road without copying faces. They had learned the difference between evidence about a route and information about a person’s life. Mei liked being trusted with both. On the way to the hanging rock, she put the photographs in a dry case and checked the sky again. There would be no sense in discovering an old route if they made the journey unsafe by rushing.

**繁中**
梅答應日落前歸還照片。夫妻允許她借副本，卻請她保密家人姓名。安花時間畫下馬車與舊路，沒有描摹人臉。他們學到路線證據和私人生活資訊的差別。梅很珍惜這份信任。前往懸岩途中，她把照片放進乾燥盒，再次檢查天空。若因趕路而讓旅程變危險，找到舊路也就沒有意義了。

**EN**
An turned the last page and asked if a path could still exist when nobody used it. The barber answered that a road was more than lines on paper. People had to remember where it led and who might need it again.

**繁中**
安翻到最後一頁，問路若沒人走，是否仍然存在。理髮師回答，道路不只是紙上的線，還必須有人記得它通往何處，以及未來誰可能再需要它。

**EN**
By dusk they reached the hanging rock. Beneath it lay the broken lantern station, covered in leaves. Mei found an iron ring that could still hold a lamp, but no fuel and no sign of recent visitors. An pointed to seven marks on the wall; the seventh had been covered with gray paint. They did not know who had painted it or when. Mei added both questions to her notebook. She had learned that the truth often began with a small mark someone else had stopped looking at.

**繁中**
傍晚兩人來到懸岩。岩下有一座破舊的提燈站，滿是落葉。梅找到一個仍可掛燈的鐵環，卻沒有燃料，也沒有最近有人來過的痕跡。安指著牆上七道記號；第七道被灰漆遮住。他們不知道是誰塗的，也不知道何時塗的。梅把兩個問題寫入筆記。她開始懂得，真相常常從別人不再注意的小記號開始。

## CHAPTER 03｜An Alarm on the Old Road｜舊路上的警報
