# 《山路盡頭，還有未還的書》
*Beyond the Last Library Stop*

**代碼：6B｜全新第九部**  
**級距：第6級（5000–6000字彙）**  
**題材：成年職場戀愛／山區流動圖書車／公共閱讀服務／女性自主**

**簡介：** 四十一歲的流動圖書館主任沈予晴發現，服務偏遠山村十七年的圖書車即將因失真的借閱統計而停駛。她與四十四歲的道路工程師許承岳重逢，兩人曾因一次未被說明的工程責任分道揚鑣。為了保住居民的閱讀權，予晴必須修正數據、檢驗危險道路、建立能在她離開後持續運作的制度，也重新決定愛與工作的界線。

**編輯說明：** 每章英文正文600–900詞，英語段落後緊接繁體中文逐段翻譯；保留《L6_B本_25章配詞.md》原始ID、詞條及詞性。故事、地名及制度為虛構，與先前八部6B無共用情節。

## CHAPTER 01｜The Notice at the Last Stop｜終點站的停駛公告

### 正文與翻譯

**EN** Yuqing reached the mountain square to find a closure notice pasted over the timetable of the traveling library. The notice counted online loans but not the paper books delivered to homes without reliable signal. Yuqing had spent enough years in public service to know how easily a chart could make a difficult decision look inevitable. She carried a pencil and a dated notebook, because a reader might ask tomorrow what had really happened today. The important distinction was not between paper and technology. It was between a service that listened and a service that counted people only when they were easy to count. The metal clasp on the return crate snapped in Yuqing's hand. Readers asked the district office to convene an open meeting. A picture of a bear cub marked the children's shelf. The mobile dental clinic used the same village square. One angry caller called the mayor a dictator, but Yuqing demanded evidence.

**繁中** 予晴抵達山區廣場時，發現流動圖書車時刻表上貼了停駛公告。公告只計算線上借閱，沒把送往訊號不穩住家的紙本圖書列入。 予晴在公共服務工作多年，知道一張圖表很容易把艱難決定偽裝成必然。她隨身帶著鉛筆和註明日期的筆記本，因為明天可能有人會問今天究竟發生什麼。真正的差別不在紙本或科技，而在服務是否願意傾聽，還是只計算那些方便被計算的人。 還書箱的金屬扣環在予晴手中斷裂。 讀者要求區公所召開公開會議。 兒童書架上貼著小熊幼獸的圖片。 巡迴牙科診所也使用同一座村莊廣場。 一名憤怒來電者罵鎮長是獨裁者，予晴則要求提出證據。

**EN** A nine-year-old returned a book with a handmade receipt. Yuqing recognized the number: the same loan appeared as zero in the city report. She photographed the receipt only after the child's grandmother agreed. The volunteers disagreed on the next step, which Yuqing considered healthier than an artificial show of unity. She asked one person to state the practical risk and another to explain the possible benefit. The record would include both. When the same question returned a week later, nobody would need to depend on memory or personal loyalty to understand the decision. The closure directive had no signed explanation for missing paper loans. A remote dwelling could receive books even without an internet connection. In a storybook, horses gallop toward the mountain at dawn. The misleading figures could heighten distrust between neighboring villages. The school janitor offered a dry room for sorting returned books.

**繁中** 一名九歲孩子拿自製借書收據來還書。予晴發現那筆借閱在市府報表竟記為零；徵得祖母同意後，她才拍下收據。 志工對下一步有不同意見，予晴認為這比假裝團結更健康。她請一人說明實際風險，另一人提出可能利益，兩者都記錄。等同一問題一週後再出現，沒有人需要靠回憶或私人忠誠才能理解決定。 停駛指令沒有附上紙本借閱遺漏的簽署說明。 偏遠住所即使沒有網路，也應能收到圖書。 故事書中，馬群在清晨奔向山頭。 誤導性的數字可能加深鄰近村落的不信任。 學校清潔員提供乾燥房間整理還書。

**EN** Chengyue, the road engineer who had remained silent when her old proposal was blamed for a failed route, offered to drive her over the ridge. She accepted a public inspection, not a private favor. Yuqing's career did not fit neatly into the stories neighbors told about love. She enjoyed her work in the city and cared about people in the mountain villages. Neither fact canceled the other. When Chengyue spoke about the future, she asked him to use the word 'I' rather than 'we' until they had separately decided what each person wanted. The old library motto said that every reader deserved a place. A pilgrim asked whether the bus still carried local-history guides. A previous flood agreement offered a precedent for temporary deliveries. The contractor hoped to reap profits from exclusive digital membership. The library bus remained stationary until the driver completed safety checks.

**繁中** 承岳是當年她的舊提案遭指責時保持沉默的道路工程師。他提出載她走山路，她只接受有紀錄的公開勘查，不接受私人恩惠。 予晴的職涯不符合鄰居熟悉的愛情故事。她喜歡城市工作，也關心山村居民；兩者不互相抵銷。承岳談未來時，她請他先說「我」而不是「我們」，直到各自決定想要什麼。 圖書館舊座右銘寫著每位讀者都值得有自己的位置。 一位朝聖者詢問圖書車是否仍備有地方史指南。 過去的洪水協議提供了臨時配送的先例。 承包商希望從獨家數位會員制度獲取利潤。 駕駛完成安全檢查前，圖書車保持靜止。

**EN** The librarians obtained seven days to preserve the paper logs. Yuqing posted a question for every reader: who was missing from the city's numbers? The village lights came on one by one as the volunteers packed the books away. Several people still disagreed, and Yuqing was glad the record showed it. She had seen too many projects fail after being declared a triumph. This time the next inspection had a date, the next review had a public address, and the people who would be affected knew where to speak. Yuqing said a download was not a synonym for a successful reading visit. The school planned to transplant two trees away from the turning area. A community trustee demanded the complete service contract before voting. The children needed unconditional access to their borrowed books during the review. Yuqing refused to utter an accusation before checking the paper register.

**繁中** 圖書員爭取到七天保存紙本紀錄。予晴留下一個問題給所有讀者：市府數字漏掉了誰？ 志工收書時，村燈一盞盞亮起。仍有人不同意，予晴慶幸紀錄寫了下來。她見過太多被宣布勝利後就失敗的計畫。這次下一次檢查有日期，審查有公開地址，受影響的人也知道去哪裡發言。 予晴說下載次數不是成功閱讀服務的同義詞。 學校計畫把兩棵樹移植到迴車區以外。 一位社區受託人要求表決前公開完整服務契約。 審查期間，孩子們仍需要不附帶條件的借書權利。 核對紙本登記冊之前，予晴拒絕說出指控。

**EN** Before the meeting ended, a young courier showed Yuqing the notebook he kept inside his jacket. Whenever a resident returned a book at a doorstep, he wrote the date, the title, and the time the road was passable. His handwriting was uneven, but the sequence of dates was clear. Yuqing asked whether he would allow the notebook to be checked by someone outside the library. He agreed after she promised that the names of children could be concealed. When Chengyue offered to carry the notebooks, she kept them with their owner. Evidence did not become public property simply because it was useful.

**繁中** 會議結束前，一位年輕配送員給予晴看他放在外套裡的筆記本。每當居民在家門口還書，他就寫下日期、書名與當時道路是否可通行。他的字跡不整齊，日期順序卻很清楚。予晴問他是否願意讓圖書館以外的人核對。他在她承諾隱去孩子姓名後才答應。承岳提議幫忙拿筆記本時，她仍把它留在持有人手中。證據即使有用，也不會因此自動成為公共財產。

## CHAPTER 02｜The Bridge with Two Names｜有兩個名字的橋

### 正文與翻譯

**EN** A narrow bridge carried both school buses and delivery vans. The official map called it open, while drivers had given it another name: the bridge that shook after rain. The village square changed character at every hour. Before breakfast it belonged to delivery drivers; after school it filled with children and grandparents. Yuqing listened to both groups rather than treating the loudest voices as a vote. She took down the name of anyone who offered to check a fact, but she never confused a willing witness with a source that required no verification. Every driver must abide by the bridge's posted weight limit. Chengyue asked the inspector to affirm which bolts had been replaced. An alligator in a nature book delighted the younger passengers. The music teacher played a quiet bass line during the waiting period. A volunteer rewrote the warning in colloquial language for local drivers.

**繁中** 一座窄橋同時承載校車與貨車。官方地圖標示正常通行，司機卻私下稱它為雨後會搖晃的橋。 村莊廣場每個時段都有不同面貌。早餐前是配送司機的地盤，放學後則聚滿孩子和祖父母。予晴聽取兩方意見，不把最大聲的意見當成表決。她記下願意查證者的姓名，卻不把願意作證的人視為不需核實的來源。 每位駕駛都必須遵守橋梁公告的載重限制。 承岳請檢查員確認哪些螺栓已經更換。 自然圖鑑中的鱷魚讓年幼乘客興奮不已。 等候時，音樂老師演奏一段低音旋律。 志工把警示改成當地司機熟悉的口語。

**EN** Chengyue checked its load restrictions with an independent inspector. Yuqing refused to put children on a demonstration ride merely to save the library's timetable. The council chamber was built for speeches, but the most useful work took place around the small table in the hallway. Readers pointed at the printed route with hands that trembled from cold or age. A driver corrected the angle of a turn; a student corrected a stop's name. These were not interruptions to expertise. They were the information expertise needed. Minor delays were commonplace, but a shaking bridge required special action. The temporary road permit would expire before the next heavy rainfall. A burned fuse had disabled the inspection trailer's light. Villagers offered hospitality to the inspectors without asking for favors. The school cook's infectious laughter eased the worried meeting.

**繁中** 承岳找獨立技師核對橋梁載重限制。予晴拒絕為了保住圖書車班表，就讓孩子搭乘示範車冒險。 議事廳適合演說，最有用的工作卻在走廊小桌旁發生。讀者用因寒冷或年紀而顫抖的手指著印刷路線；司機更正轉彎角度，學生更正站名。這些不是對專業的干擾，而是專業需要的資訊。 小幅誤點很常見，搖晃的橋卻需要特殊處理。 臨時道路許可會在下一次大雨前到期。 一枚燒壞的保險絲使檢查拖車的燈失效。 村民款待檢查員，並不要求交換好處。 學校廚師富有感染力的笑聲緩和了焦慮的會議。

**EN** At the school she asked Chengyue why he had not defended her eight years earlier. He said fear of losing a contract explained his silence, but did not excuse it. An old hurt still occupied part of their conversations, but it no longer controlled every word. Chengyue offered a factual correction where one was needed and silence where one was not. Yuqing did not promise forgiveness as a reward. She began to imagine meeting him again after the project ended, when neither would have a reason to stand beside the other except wanting to. The crew carried one liter of water for a controlled demonstration. A novice volunteer was told not to approach the closed bridge. It would be rash to send a full bus across before the load test. The accountant tried to reconcile the map dates with the road invoices. The shabby warning sign had become unreadable after years of rain.

**繁中** 在學校裡，她問承岳八年前為何不幫她說話。他坦承害怕失去合約可以解釋沉默，卻不能為沉默開脫。 舊傷仍佔著對話一部分，卻不再控制每一句。需要更正的地方，承岳提出事實；不需開口時，他選擇沉默。予晴不把原諒當獎賞承諾。她開始想像計畫結束後仍與他相見，屆時兩人站在一起，只因為彼此願意。 工作隊帶了一公升水進行受控示範。 新手志工被告知不得靠近封閉橋梁。 載重測試前就讓滿載巴士過橋太草率。 會計試圖核對地圖日期與道路工程發票。 破舊警告標誌多年受雨淋後已無法辨認。

**EN** A safer temporary stop was marked below the bridge. It added walking distance, so the group began arranging assisted deliveries rather than claiming the problem was solved. No one cut a ribbon that afternoon. Instead, the driver checked the tires, a volunteer copied the minutes, and a child waited patiently for the library's next visit. Yuqing felt the quiet satisfaction of an institution doing ordinary things well. There would be another difficult decision soon, but the group had begun to share the right to make it. A handheld spotlight showed the damaged joint under the bridge. The school served pear syrup with warm water to cold volunteers. A loaded truck could make the surface tilt dangerously. At twilight the inspection team sealed the crossing with barriers. An illustrated memoir mentioned whiskey, but the crew remained sober on duty.

**繁中** 眾人在橋下標記較安全的臨時停靠點。雖然步行距離增加，他們開始安排協助送書，而不假裝問題已經解決。 那天下午沒有剪綵。司機檢查輪胎，志工抄寫紀錄，一名孩子耐心等候圖書車下次到訪。予晴感到公共機構做好平凡小事的安靜滿足。不久還會有困難決定，但大家已開始共享決策權。 手持聚光燈照出橋下受損的接頭。 學校用溫水調梨糖漿招待受寒志工。 滿載貨車可能使路面危險地傾斜。 黃昏時，檢查隊以路障封閉通道。 一本插畫回憶錄提到威士忌，工作人員值勤時仍保持清醒。

**EN** The bridge inspector drew two lines on the blackboard at the school: one for the legal weight limit and one for the actual weight of a loaded bus. The lines did not meet. Several parents stared at them without speaking. Yuqing asked the inspector to explain the difference without technical abbreviations, and he did. Outside, a mother said that she could carry her child's books herself if the bus stopped below the hill. Yuqing wrote that offer in the volunteer plan, along with the woman's request for a covered place to wait. The compromise was inconvenient, but it did not ask anyone to gamble with a child's safety.

**繁中** 橋梁檢查員在學校黑板上畫兩條線：一條是法定載重上限，一條是滿載圖書車的實際重量。兩條線沒有交會。好幾位家長盯著黑板，沒有出聲。予晴請檢查員不用技術縮寫再說明一次，他照做。教室外，一位母親說若車停在山下，她願意自己幫孩子拿書。予晴把她的提議寫入志工計畫，也記下她希望有遮雨等候區的要求。折衷方案不方便，卻不用任何人拿孩子的安全下注。

## CHAPTER 03｜The Receipts Nobody Counted｜沒有人計算的收據

### 正文與翻譯

**EN** At a village classroom, four generations of borrowers laid out their handwritten slips. The city database had registered thirty-nine visits; the slips documented more than a hundred deliveries. The first version of any public plan was always tempted to erase its own weaknesses.
