# 玻璃海牆的第三聲
*The Third Knock Beyond the Glass*

**代碼：** 3A（L3-A）  
**級距：** 2000–3000 詞  
**版本：** 全新第七版  
**題材：** 封閉水下觀測中心、三條安全規則、設備調查與救援

**故事簡介：** 29歲的設備調查員戴寧前往已封閉的藍灣水下觀測中心，追查實習觀測員樂雅的失蹤。玻璃海牆的三聲敲擊、無故轉綠的警示燈，以及舊地圖上的西側減壓閥，讓工作人員深信鬧鬼。戴寧必須以壓力紀錄、聲音測試和原始工單分辨真相，在不冒險破壞海牆的情況下找回失蹤者。

**主要人物：** 戴寧（Nina Dai，29歲，設備調查員）；佳恩（Jae，獨立救援潛水員）；樂雅（Leya，失蹤的實習觀測員）；阿卓（Tao，資深導覽員）；方主任（Fang，中心主管）。

**三條規則：**
1. 聽見主海窗外三聲敲擊時，不要回敲；讀取壓力錶並報告時間。 *When three knocks sound beyond the main glass, do not knock back; read the pressure gauge and report the time.*
2. 走廊燈轉綠時，留在白色地線安全側，直到送風機重新啟動。 *When corridor lamps turn green, stay above the white floor line until the air fan has reset.*
3. 低潮時通過下層橋道前，必須開啟並確認西側減壓閥。 *At low tide, open and check the west relief valve before crossing the lower bridge.*

**使用方式：** 每段英文正文後緊接完整繁體中文翻譯。配詞採用專案原始《L3_A本_25章配詞.md》，每章新詞20個（第25章21個），依原ID保留詞條；字面命中限英文正文，不把中文、標題、詞表或測驗算進去。詞表中文語境義是編輯用初稿，並非原始配詞檔附帶的官方譯文；尚待逐條人工校對。

## CHAPTER 01｜Three Knocks at the Glass｜玻璃上的三聲敲擊

### 正文與翻譯

**EN** Nina Dai had been told that her visit to Blue Bay Underwater Center was acceptable only if she stayed beside the guide. She was twenty-nine and had come to advise the safety office after a young observer, Leya, vanished. At the entrance, the guide Tao rested his chin on his hand, staring at an unopened carton of coconut milk. Nina tried to connect her recorder to the building clock. The display showed a date that was two days old. She wrote the difference down before anyone could offer an excuse.

**繁中** 戴寧被告知，只要留在導覽員身邊，她進入藍灣水下觀測中心的申請就可以接受。她二十九歲，是為了年輕觀測員樂雅失蹤一事，前來向安全單位提供建議。入口處，導覽員阿卓用手托著下巴，望著一盒未開封的椰奶。戴寧試著把錄音設備連上建築物的時鐘，螢幕卻顯示兩天前的日期。她在其他人找藉口前先記下這個差異。

**EN** The director, Fang, called the rescue request a cruel way to frighten the staff. A medical cabinet listed the proper dose of emergency medicine; above it, a drip of salt water gathered beneath the ceiling. Nina did not envy Fang's confidence. She wanted to explore the closed corridor and test the instruments separately. Outside the thick window, the sea was calm, which made the warning about extreme pressure seem strange. Tao reminded her that calm water at the surface said little about the force against glass below it.

**繁中** 站長方主任把救援申請稱為殘酷的恐嚇，說它只會讓員工害怕。醫療櫃標著緊急藥物的適當劑量；上方的天花板下方卻凝著一滴鹹水。戴寧不羨慕方主任的自信。她想分開探索封閉走廊，逐一測試儀器。厚玻璃外的海面看起來平靜，讓極端水壓的警告顯得奇怪。阿卓提醒她，水面平靜並不代表水下玻璃承受的力量很小。

**EN** Nina refused to gamble with the crew's safety. She took a quick glance at a supply cupboard where someone had stored hay for an old children's sea-farm display. Beside it hung a bag of laundry, still damp from the evacuation two weeks earlier. The objects looked ordinary, but their position mattered: the cupboard blocked a narrow inspection hatch. Tao said the display had been moved in a hurry, after the building was declared safe. Nina photographed the hatch and asked who had ordered the move. Tao could not remember a name.

**繁中** 戴寧拒絕拿工作人員的安全賭一把。她匆匆瞥向補給櫃，看見裡面存著一把乾草，是舊兒童海岸農場展示用的。旁邊掛著一袋仍有些潮濕的待洗衣物，據說是兩週前撤離時留下的。這些物品看似普通，擺放的位置卻很重要：櫃子擋住了一扇狹窄的檢查孔。阿卓說，建築被宣布安全後，展品曾被匆忙搬動。戴寧拍下檢查孔，追問是誰下令搬的；阿卓想不起名字。

**EN** A sincere night guard finally described what he had heard. He had been sitting on a stool near an empty tobacco tin when three blows sounded from the sea wall. His twin brother had once worked as a diver there, so he knew how a metal tool could sound underwater. Yet the last blow was followed by a whistle from an unused speaker. The guard had reported the whistle, not the knocks, because he feared being laughed at. Nina thanked him for telling the difference. One small distinction could matter more than a dramatic story.

**繁中** 一位坦誠的夜班警衛終於描述了自己聽見的聲音。當時他坐在矮凳上，身邊有個空菸草罐，海牆忽然傳來三次敲擊。他的雙胞胎兄弟曾在此當潛水員，因此他知道金屬工具在水下會發出什麼聲音。然而最後一下敲擊之後，還傳來停用喇叭發出的哨聲。警衛怕被嘲笑，所以只報告哨聲，沒提敲擊。戴寧謝謝他說清楚差別。小小的區別，有時比戲劇化的故事更重要。

**EN** At the corridor entrance, three rules had been printed on a steel plate. First, when three knocks sound beyond the main glass, do not knock back; read the pressure gauge and report the time. Second, when the corridor lamps turn green, stay above the white floor line until the air fan has reset. Third, at low tide, the west relief valve must be opened and checked before the lower bridge is crossed. A handwritten addition said, “No exception for a familiar voice.” Nina copied every word. None of the rules told people that a ghost lived below the sea.

**繁中** 走廊入口的鋼板上印著三條規則。第一，主玻璃外傳來三聲敲擊時，不要回敲；先讀壓力錶，再報告時間。第二，走廊燈轉綠時，待在白色地線以上，直到送風機重啟。第三，低潮時通過下層橋道前，必須先開啟並確認西側減壓閥。有人用手寫補上一句：「即使聽見熟悉的聲音，也不例外。」戴寧把每個字抄下來。規則裡沒有一條說海底住著鬼。

**EN** Jae, the independent rescue diver, arrived with a dry bag of instruments. Nina explained that she would not enter the lower level until they understood the pressure history. Jae wanted to search for Leya immediately, but accepted the limit after seeing a crack in an old bolt. They agreed on a first task: compare the wall clock, pump clock, and handwritten guard log. The three devices could disagree without anyone lying. If someone had changed a record, however, the disagreement might reveal what had been changed and when.

**繁中** 獨立救援潛水員佳恩帶著裝有儀器的防水袋抵達。戴寧說，在弄清楚壓力歷史之前，她不會進入下層。佳恩想立刻尋找樂雅，但看見舊螺栓上的裂痕後，接受了這項限制。兩人同意先比較牆上時鐘、泵浦時鐘及警衛手寫日誌。三種裝置即使不同步，也不表示有人說謊；但若有人改動紀錄，差異也可能揭示改動的內容與時間。

**EN** Just before closing, the sound came again: one knock, a pause, and two more. Nobody moved toward the glass. Nina read the gauge aloud while Jae filmed its needle. The pressure rose for several seconds, then returned to its earlier level. The guard's face turned pale, but his hands remained away from the wall. Nina sealed the recordings in separate envelopes and asked Tao to sign the time sheet. Leya was still missing. Yet for the first time, the frightened team had a measurement instead of another frightening rumor.

**繁中** 臨近關閉時，那個聲音再次出現：一下敲擊，停頓，接著兩下。沒有人走近玻璃。戴寧讀出壓力錶數值，佳恩拍攝指針。壓力上升數秒，隨後回到原來的水平。警衛臉色發白，雙手仍遠離海牆。戴寧把錄音分開封入信封，請阿卓在時間表簽名。樂雅依然失蹤，但這支受驚的團隊第一次擁有了測量數據，而不只是另一則令人害怕的傳言。

### 理解測驗

**Q01. What did Nina do when the knocks returned?**

- A. She read the pressure gauge and recorded the time.
- B. She knocked on the main glass.
- C. She opened the sea-side door.

**正解：A。** 戴寧遵守第一條規則，讀壓力錶並記下時間，沒有回敲玻璃。

**Q02. Which detail made the wall clock unreliable?**

- A. It showed the wrong month on purpose.
- B. Its display showed a date two days old.
- C. It had no hands at all.

**正解：B。** 她發現時鐘顯示兩天前的日期，因此不能直接當作可靠時間來源。

## CHAPTER 02｜Two Maps of the Same Sea｜同一片海的兩張地圖

### 正文與翻譯

**EN** An old avenue along the harbor led to a private service door behind the center. In its former visitor room, a cradle from a family exhibition stood beneath a model submarine. Workers had begun to decorate the room for a reopening that was never announced. The public elevator was locked, but a paper drawing showed another small lift behind the wall. Nina noticed that the exhibition plan and the safety map gave that lift different numbers. She asked Jae to photograph both labels before anyone moved the display case.

**繁中** 港邊一條舊大道通往觀測中心後方的私人維修門。昔日的參觀室裡，一張家庭展覽用的搖籃放在模型潛艇下方。工人曾開始裝飾房間，準備一場從未公告的重新開放。公共電梯上了鎖，但紙本圖面顯示牆後還有一座小型升降機。戴寧發現展覽計畫與安全地圖給那座升降機不同的編號。她請佳恩先拍下兩個標籤，不要急著搬動展示櫃。

**EN** The emergency store contained one firework for a harbor show, a sealed gallon of cleaning fluid, and a binder labeled HEADQUARTERS. At first Nina thought it might be a child's imagination exercise, because a drawing of a leopard was pinned to the cover. But the binder held dated inspection orders. A technician had marked one page with the word magical in red ink, mocking a repair that apparently required no tools. The comment was odd, but the engineer's initials next to it matched an entry in Leya's notebook.

**繁中** 緊急儲藏室裡放著一枚港口表演用的煙火、一加侖密封清潔液，以及寫著「總部」的資料夾。戴寧起初以為那是兒童想像力練習，因為封面釘著一張豹的圖畫。但資料夾內是按日期排列的檢查命令。一位技師用紅筆把某次不用工具的「修理」嘲諷成魔法般的奇蹟。這個註解很奇怪，旁邊的工程師縮寫卻與樂雅筆記中的一筆紀錄相同。

**EN** A guide said the center once kept an organic garden on the roof. Green ribbon marked the safe paths for visitors. Nina saw salt water spit from a damaged joint near the service lift, although the surrounding floor looked dry. Beyond it, a steep ladder descended toward the old bridge. The newest survey had called that passage closed, while an older survey listed it as a temporary escape route. This was no harmless difference of style. A threat had been hidden by replacing a clear warning with a single word: “unused.”

**繁中** 一名導覽員說，中心屋頂以前有一座有機花園，綠絲帶用來標示遊客的安全動線。戴寧看見維修升降機附近的接頭噴出鹹水，周圍地面卻似乎乾燥。再往裡走，一道陡梯通往舊橋道。最新版勘查圖把通道標為封閉，舊版卻把它列為臨時逃生路。這不是無傷大雅的用詞不同，而是有人用「停用」一字取代清楚警示，把威脅藏了起來。

**EN** A tourist photograph lay between the two plans, its corners wet. Jae used a clean cloth to wipe the protective cover, not the photograph itself. The picture showed two layers of glass, separated by an inspection space that the newer plan did not identify. Nina traced the visible steel ribs without touching the panel. If the layers had separate pressure sensors, a knock could travel through both at different times. She was not ready to explain the sound, but at least she knew why a single window on the map might be misleading.

**繁中** 兩張圖之間夾著一張遊客照片，角落受了潮。佳恩用乾淨布擦拭保護套，而沒有直接擦照片。照片裡有兩層玻璃，中間隔著一個新版圖面沒有標出的檢查空間。戴寧隔空沿著可見的鋼肋比對位置，沒有觸碰面板。如果兩層玻璃各有壓力感測器，一次敲擊就可能在不同時間傳過兩層。她還無法解釋聲音，但至少知道地圖上的單片窗戶為何會誤導人。

**EN** Fang insisted that the public map was the only approved map. Nina asked him to show the approval date and the name of the person who checked the replacement. He produced a cover page but not the full signed plan. The difference mattered because moving an emergency route could change how people escaped during a leak. Jae suggested that they postpone the search until the coast office sent an independent copy. Nina agreed, though the delay felt painful. Guessing at a hidden route would not bring Leya home safely.

**繁中** 方主任堅稱公開地圖才是唯一核准的版本。戴寧請他提出核准日期及審核替換圖面的人名，他只拿出封面，卻沒有完整簽核圖。這個差異很重要，因為改變逃生路線可能影響漏水時人們如何撤離。佳恩建議先暫停搜尋，等海岸辦公室送來獨立保存的副本。戴寧雖然覺得拖延難受，仍然同意。靠猜測闖進隱藏通道，無法安全帶樂雅回家。

**EN** That afternoon they measured the distance between two visible bolts. On the old drawing the bolts formed the border of an inspection space; on the new one they appeared to hold a solid wall. The real distance matched the old drawing. Jae placed a harmless paper marker by the service door so the team could return to the same point. Nina called the finding evidence of a missing space, not proof that anyone had trapped Leya there. They needed a second source before taking the next step.

**繁中** 當天下午，他們量了兩顆可見螺栓之間的距離。舊圖上，螺栓圍出一處檢查空間；新圖上，它們卻像是固定整片實心牆。實際距離符合舊圖。佳恩在維修門旁放了一張不影響設備的紙標記，方便團隊返回同一位置。戴寧把發現記為「存在被省略的空間」，而不是「有人把樂雅困在裡面」的證明。下一步還需要第二項來源。

**EN** When the three knocks returned at dusk, Nina stood where both glass layers were visible. The second blow seemed to arrive slightly later at the outer frame. Jae's camera registered the delay, although neither knew its cause. They left the frame untouched. Back at the table, Nina placed the two maps, the photograph, and the new measurement in separate clear sleeves. She drew a question mark beside the missing space. For the first time she could describe exactly what the map failed to show.

**繁中** 傍晚三聲敲擊再次響起，戴寧站在能同時看見兩層玻璃的位置。第二下敲擊似乎在外層框架稍晚傳來，佳恩的攝影機也錄到了這個延遲，雖然兩人還不知道原因。他們沒有碰框架。回到桌旁，戴寧把兩張圖、照片及新測量值分裝進透明護套，在被省略的空間旁畫上問號。她終於能精確說明新版地圖漏掉了什麼。

**EN** On the opening morning, visitors came aboard a small harbor shuttle and walked toward the repaired upper hall. A beetle rested on a rail near the gate. Tao offered to bet that nobody would ask about the old ghost story, but Nina refused the wager. The center would not destroy every trace of its past; it would preserve enough evidence to show how fear and neglected repairs had combined into danger.

**EN** A crab crawled along the shore below the viewing window. The education editor wanted an enjoyable exhibit that was also frank about the failures. **繁中** 一隻螃蟹在觀景窗下的岸邊爬行。教育編輯想把展覽做得有趣，同時坦率呈現失誤。戴寧請導覽員不要打斷提出困難問題的訪客。她記得樂雅家人受過的苦，不願把痛苦變成刺激故事。馬路對面的汽車旅館提供志工優惠房價，新公告旁有松樹遮陰。中心仍是一座真實小鎮的一部分，而不是布景。

**繁中** 出口附近，阿卓告訴戴寧，自己曾相信海牆會回答人的聲音。如今他知道鬆動鋼臂與訓練喇叭的事，卻仍尊重第一條規則。戴寧說，理解規則應讓人更容易遵守，而不是更容易忽視。上層大廳完整開放一天，沒有發生事故。下層走廊仍封閉，測試時程清楚張貼，人人可看。沒有人把不完全的重新開放稱為失敗，那是一條有理由存在的界線。

**繁中** 日落時，玻璃外潮水退去。戴寧沒有聽見敲擊，也不再需要它。樂雅站在身旁，望著曾經隔開她們的海水。兩人談起未來：更好的公開地圖、員工能提問的訓練，以及無法被草率印章抹掉警告的通報制度。海依然深、強大，有時令人畏懼，卻不再需要扮演鬼魂。戴寧闔上筆記本，和樂雅一起走進開放大廳的燈光裡。
