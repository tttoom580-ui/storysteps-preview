# 潮汐劇場的安靜掌聲
# The Quiet Applause at Tide Theater

> 拾頁 StorySteps｜第1級（1000字以下詞彙級距）｜1B｜第四版獨立全新故事

**題材：** 海灣毛絨動物劇團的合作建設與安靜演出。
**故事簡介：** 年輕水獺瑟娜想讓多年封閉的海邊劇場重新開放，卻發現破損地板、漲潮、鳥類休息時間，以及觀眾通道都不能忽視。她與獾布蘭、輪椅使用者琵亞、歌手哈爾等夥伴合作，把原本熱鬧的演出改成有圖畫、動作和大字卡的安靜戲劇；最終讓劇場成為人人都能安心參與的地方。
**世界規則：** ① 海灣動物像人一樣生活、工作與演戲；② 舊舞台未經工程師檢驗或遇到不安全潮汐、天氣時不得進入；③ 岩石邊育雛鳥類需要安靜，表演依照照護員及鎮公所核准的時段進行。故事中的「魔術」是舞台技巧，沒有超自然捷徑。

## CHAPTER 01｜A Stage by the Sea｜海邊的小舞台

*英文正文詞數：629；新目標詞：20*

### 正文與翻譯

**EN**

At the north side of Shell Bay, an old wooden stage stood beside the sea. A red curtain hung over its door, but no one had used it for years. Sena, a young otter woman, went there with a camera to take pictures for the town picnic. Her pet snail, Pebble, sat in a safe glass box at home; she never took him near salt water. A little bug crossed the steps. Sena bent to notice it, and then saw a deep crack in the middle board. A friendly badger named Bram came over with apple juice. “This is not a safe place for a show,” he said. Sena did not lie. “That is a fact,” she answered. “But perhaps we can make it safe.”

**繁中**

在貝殼灣北邊，一座老木舞台靠著海。門口掛著紅布幕，卻已多年沒人使用。年輕的水獺女子瑟娜帶著相機來拍照，準備鎮上的野餐活動。她的寵物蝸牛小石安全地待在家裡的玻璃箱中；她從不帶牠靠近海水。一隻小蟲爬過台階。瑟娜彎腰注意牠，接著發現舞台中央的一塊木板有深深的裂縫。友善的獾布蘭拿著蘋果汁走來。「這裡不適合安全地演出。」他說。瑟娜沒有說謊。「那是事實，」她回答，「但也許我們能把它修好。」

**EN**

On her way back from the picnic, Sena visited the stage once more, staying behind the gate. In the middle of the old curtain, a little hole let the daylight through. She pictured a crab moving across the boards and a fish made from paper dancing above the sea. Then she looked down and saw the chalk line around the crack. The day had given her two different pictures: one of what the stage might become, and another of what it was now. Bram had walked with her because he wanted the same dream, but he was not ready to risk a child's safety for it. They agreed that the most useful first step would be to ask good questions instead of bringing paint and a hammer too soon.

**繁中**

野餐回程，瑟娜再次到舞台，停在門外。老布幕中央有個小洞，日光從中透進來。她想像螃蟹走過木板、紙魚在海上方跳舞。接著她低頭，看見裂縫旁的粉筆線。這一天給了她兩幅不同的畫面：一幅是舞台可能成為的樣子，另一幅是它目前真正的樣子。布蘭陪她走來，因為他也有相同夢想，但他不願為了夢想讓孩子冒險。他們同意第一步應該先好好提問，而不是太早帶著油漆和鎚子來。

**EN**

The town had another stage in a school hall, but it was small and far from the beach. Every summer, children asked why the sea stage stayed closed. Sena had asked the same question when she was little. She wanted a future in which everyone could watch a show while hearing the waves. A star was visible in the blue morning sky only in an old painting on the wall; today the sky was bright and empty. Bram pointed at the cracked board again. “Dreams are good. A broken floor is still a broken floor.” Sena agreed to use chalk to mark the bad part, so that anybody walking near it would stop. They did not step on the board even once.

**繁中**

鎮上在學校禮堂還有另一個舞台，但那裡很小，也離海灘很遠。每年夏天，孩子們都問海邊舞台為什麼一直關閉。瑟娜小時候也問過同樣的問題。她希望將來大家能邊聽海浪邊看表演。牆上一幅舊畫裡，藍色晨空還有一顆星；今天真正的天空明亮而空曠。布蘭再次指著裂開的木板。「夢想很好，但壞掉的地板就是壞掉的地板。」瑟娜同意用粉筆標出危險區域，讓走近的人停下。兩人一次也沒有踩上那塊木板。

**EN**

That evening, Sena told her neighbors why the gate must remain shut. A little child said she had heard a story about a person who could jump over the broken boards. “Maybe that person can,” Sena answered, “but a public stage is for people who walk, run, roll, and rest in many different ways.” She asked the child to imagine a grandparent arriving slowly with a walking stick. The child became thoughtful and moved her toy chair away from the edge of a step. Sena was pleased by that small change. There was no need to frighten anyone. A clear example could show why a rule existed. Before sleeping, she put her camera beside the notes and wondered whether the future would bring a new curtain or a new kind of show.

**繁中**

傍晚，瑟娜向鄰居說明為何大門必須保持關閉。一個孩子說她聽過有人能跳過裂開木板的故事。「也許那個人能，」瑟娜回答，「但公共舞台是給用不同方式走路、奔跑、推輪椅和休息的人使用的。」她請孩子想像祖父母拄著手杖慢慢走來。孩子沉思片刻，把玩具椅從台階邊移開。瑟娜很高興看到這個小變化。不需要嚇唬誰，清楚的例子就能說明規則的原因。睡前她把相機放在筆記旁，想著未來會帶來新布幕，還是全新的表演方式。

**EN**

At the picnic that afternoon, Sena asked the other animals for their ideas. Some wanted a song, some wanted a play, and a child wanted to dance. They all seemed excited, but Mrs. Fern, who looked after the town buildings, spoke carefully. “You may not reach the stage until an engineer checks it,” she said. “You also need a plan for the tide and a quiet time for birds that live by the rocks.” Sena wrote the rules on a large sheet and read them aloud. She did not promise a show yet. Instead, she asked for one week to learn what was possible. Bram offered to help with the first visit. The picnic ended with a question, not a promise.

**繁中**

那天下午的野餐上，瑟娜詢問其他動物的想法。有些想唱歌，有些想演戲，一個孩子則想跳舞。大家都很興奮，但負責管理鎮上建築的芬恩太太謹慎地開口：「工程師檢查之前，你們不能走上舞台。還要有潮水的計畫，以及讓岩石邊的鳥兒安靜休息的時間。」瑟娜把規則寫在大紙上，念給大家聽。她沒有立刻承諾表演，只請求一週時間研究可能的辦法。布蘭願意幫忙第一次勘查。野餐在一個問題中結束，而不是在承諾中結束。

## CHAPTER 02｜The Town's Three Questions｜鎮上的三個問題

*英文正文詞數：614；新目標詞：20*

### 正文與翻譯

**EN**

The next morning, Sena left her apartment early. She passed a church with a bell, then came to the town office. A reporter named Moss was sitting on a sofa with a notebook. “Is anybody going to put on a show?” he asked. Sena did not want to guess. “I am here to ask about safety first,” she said. “We may be late for the summer fair, but we should not forget the people who will sit near the water.” Mrs. Fern showed her three clear questions on a piece of paper: Is the stage strong? Can everyone get in? Can the show stay quiet when the birds need rest? Each answer needed proof, not a pretty picture.

**繁中**

隔天清晨，瑟娜很早就離開公寓。她經過一座有鐘的教堂，來到鎮公所。記者莫斯坐在沙發上，手裡拿著筆記本。「有人要辦表演嗎？」他問。瑟娜不想亂猜。「我是先來問安全問題，」她說，「我們也許趕不上夏季市集，但不該忘記坐在水邊的觀眾。」芬恩太太拿出一張紙，上面有三個清楚的問題：舞台夠堅固嗎？每個人都能進場嗎？鳥兒需要休息時，表演能保持安靜嗎？每個答案都需要證據，不是漂亮的圖片。

**EN**

At the office, Sena also met an older teacher who had taken children to shows for many years. She asked which part of a visit was hardest. The teacher did not talk about the actors at all. “We need to know where to wait, where to sit, and where to go if a child feels tired,” she explained. “A show is not only what happens on a stage.” Sena wrote the words beside the three questions. The teacher pointed out that the road by the church could become busy at lunch. Families in the apartment buildings nearby might prefer to walk rather than use a car. Bram promised to draw more than one safe way to reach the waiting area. Nobody wanted to turn the fun afternoon into a long line of worried visitors.

**繁中**

在公所，瑟娜還遇到一位多年帶孩子看戲的年長老師。她問老師，看戲最困難的是哪個部分。老師完全沒談演員。「我們需要知道在哪裡等、坐哪裡，以及孩子累了時該往哪裡走，」她解釋，「演出不只是舞台上的事。」瑟娜把這些話寫在三個問題旁。老師指出教堂附近的道路午餐時可能很忙。附近公寓住戶也許比較喜歡步行，而不是開車。布蘭答應畫出不只一條通往等候區的安全路線。沒人希望有趣的下午變成一長排憂心等待的訪客。

**EN**

At lunch, Bram met Sena beside the pond behind the office. He brought a pen and drew a third door on the back of the stage. “We could make an easier way for chairs with wheels,” he said. Sena liked the thought, but they would have to ask an engineer before they could build it. A family had offered to pay for some paint, while another neighbor wanted to give wood. Their town was small, but people had come from every part of the country to live there. Sena wrote down names and telephone numbers only after asking permission. She knew that a kind offer did not mean she could share someone's phone number with everyone else.

**繁中**

午餐時，布蘭在公所後面的池塘旁與瑟娜碰面。他帶來一支筆，在舞台背面畫了第三道門。「我們可以做一條方便輪椅通過的入口。」他說。瑟娜喜歡這個想法，但他們得先問工程師才能施工。有個家庭願意付一部分油漆費，另一位鄰居想提供木材。他們的小鎮居民來自全國各地。瑟娜取得允許後才記下大家的姓名和電話號碼。她知道，別人願意幫忙，不代表她可以隨便公開對方的電話。

**EN**

Before going home, Sena asked Mrs. Fern who would be allowed to say that the stage was ready. “The engineer checks the wood,” Mrs. Fern answered. “I check the town rules. The group checks its own show.” That made the work feel larger, but also clearer. Sena put a line under each job in her notebook. If the engineer said no, the play could not be held on the sea stage. If the tide was high, they had to use another place. If a child felt unsafe, an adult would help that child leave. Sena wanted the rules to be easy to remember and fair for anybody who came. She shut the notebook with a calm feeling: asking for help was a sign of care, not weakness.

**繁中**

回家前，瑟娜問芬恩太太，誰可以宣布舞台準備好了。「工程師檢查木頭，」芬恩太太回答，「我檢查鎮上的規定，劇團檢查自己的演出。」這讓工作看起來更大，卻也更清楚。瑟娜在筆記本裡替每項工作畫線。如果工程師說不行，戲就不能在海邊演；如果潮水高，就得改場地；如果孩子感到不安全，成年人要幫孩子離開。瑟娜希望每位訪客都能容易記住這些公平的規則。她平靜地闔上筆記本：尋求幫忙是關心的表現，不是軟弱。

**EN**

Moss came to the pond with his camera. He asked if the show was certain. Bram opened his mouth, but Sena answered first. “No. We are serious about trying, and we are serious about saying what we do not know.” Moss put his camera away. He said that a good reporter should ask again when the facts changed. Sena gave him the public office number, not the list of helpers. On the walk home, she and Bram made a clear plan: find an engineer, watch the tide, and talk to the bird keeper. Sena felt tired, yet she was glad they had not made a promise they could not keep. The three questions would guide their next days.

**繁中**

莫斯拿著相機來到池塘。他問表演是不是確定會舉行。布蘭正要回答，瑟娜先開口：「還沒有。我們認真想嘗試，也認真說出自己不知道的事。」莫斯收起相機，說好記者應該在事實改變時再次詢問。瑟娜給他的是公所的公開電話，不是志工名單。回家路上，她和布蘭訂出清楚計畫：找工程師、觀察潮水、跟照顧鳥類的人談談。瑟娜雖然疲倦，卻很高興他們沒有許下做不到的承諾。這三個問題將指引接下來的日子。

## CHAPTER 03｜The Lines on the Floor｜地板上的線

*英文正文詞數：608；新目標詞：20*

### 正文與翻譯

**EN**

On Monday, an engineer named Toma came to the stage with a lamp, an eraser, and a long ruler. He asked everyone to leave the old boards alone. “We must define the unsafe area before we begin any work,” he said. Sena stood behind a line and took notes. Only a few pieces of wood were bad, but they were in places where a basketball could roll or a child could run. Toma drew an orange square around each weak board. “All these boards need to go,” he said. “The rest may stay if we check them again.” Sena was glad to hear a careful answer. Less wood would need to be replaced than she had feared.

**繁中**

星期一，工程師托馬帶著燈、橡皮擦和長尺來到舞台。他要大家先別碰舊木板。「開工之前，我們必須定義出不安全的區域。」他說。瑟娜站在線後記錄。壞掉的木板只有幾片，卻位在籃球可能滾過、孩子可能跑過的地方。托馬用橘色線框起每一片薄弱木板。「這些都要換，」他說，「其他的如果再次檢查合格，也許可以保留。」瑟娜很高興得到謹慎的答案。要換的木材比她原本擔心的少。

**EN**
