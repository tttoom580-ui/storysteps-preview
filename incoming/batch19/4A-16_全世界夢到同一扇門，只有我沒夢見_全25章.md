# 4A-16｜全世界夢到同一扇門，只有我沒夢見

*Everyone Dreamed of the Red Door Except Me*

第四批・第4級（3000–4000詞級）｜全球怪談｜全25章雙語教學初稿。

主角：孟尋（睡眠研究員）；重要角色：阿璃（社區輔導員）。

**編輯註：**故事事件與結局為本次獨立設計，配詞融入與敘事中間段採規則化擴寫；這是完整章節的教學初稿，尚未經人工文學潤稿。

## CHAPTER 01｜The Door Nobody Could Forget｜沒有人忘得掉的門

### 正文與翻譯

**EN 01**

At eight thirty, every patient at the sleep clinic began drawing the same red door. One used a pencil. Another pressed a fingertip through spilled acid from a cleaning kit, leaving a crooked mark on the paper. By the morning assembly, the sketches covered an entire wall: identical brass knobs, identical black clocks, identical light beneath the threshold. Meng Xun, the researcher responsible for collecting the drawings, had nothing to offer. He had slept seven hours and remembered a dream about missing a train, with no red door at all. His colleagues looked at him as though he were the unusual case. 'I wish I had seen it,' he said. A Li, the counselor, replied, 'Don't wish too quickly. One of the people who opened it has forgotten where he lives.'

**繁中 01**

八點半，睡眠診所每位病人都開始畫同一扇紅門。有人用鉛筆，有人碰翻清潔組的酸性溶液，只在紙上留下歪斜痕跡。晨間集會時，整面牆都貼滿圖：相同的黃銅把手、相同的黑色時鐘、相同的門縫微光。負責收畫的研究員孟尋卻拿不出任何作品。他睡了七小時，只記得夢見趕不上火車，根本沒有紅門。同事看他的眼神彷彿他才是異常個案。「真希望我也夢到了。」他說。輔導員阿璃回答：「別這麼快希望。有一個開門的人連自己住哪都忘了。」

**EN 02**

A cleaner leaned a broom against the clinic door, then asked the researchers not to touch the wall of drawings until a full record had been made. Meng tried to construct a table of the repeated features. He needed the nursing staff to cooperate, but several workers were frightened of being asked to describe a dream aloud. 'You don't have to tell us everything you saw,' A Li said. 'You can draw just the door, or you can refuse.' That simple permission changed the room. One patient crossed out her sketch and went home. Another stayed to help label the times. Meng noticed that he had become the only researcher who could discuss the red door without remembering its dream version.

**繁中 02**

清潔員把掃帚靠在診所門旁，請研究員先完整記錄再碰滿牆圖畫。孟尋試著建立重複特徵的表格，需要護理人員合作，卻發現幾位同事害怕被要求公開說夢。「你們不必把夢全說出來。」阿璃說，「只畫門也行，拒絕也可以。」這句允許改變了房間。一名病人劃掉自己的圖回家，另一人留下幫忙標時間。孟尋注意到，他是唯一能談論紅門、卻不記得它在夢裡長什麼樣的研究者。

**EN 03**

A copper clock in the reception hall stopped at 3:17, the same time printed above every dream drawing. Meng compared the reception counter log with the patients' sleep records. One overnight nurse said she had been too frightened to open the red door and that her friends now called her a coward. A Li asked her to describe what refusal had felt like rather than rewarding bravery or shame. 'I simply woke up,' the nurse said. 'Nothing punished me.' That observation mattered. The dream image might pressure people, but it had not yet proved it could compel an action. Meng wrote the distinction in the first column of his notebook.

**繁中 03**

接待大廳的銅鐘停在三點十七分，與每張夢境圖上的時間相同。孟尋比對接待櫃臺紀錄與病患睡眠資料。一名夜班護理師說，她害怕得不敢打開紅門，朋友因此叫她膽小鬼。阿璃請她說明拒絕的感受，不獎勵勇敢，也不羞辱。「我只是醒了。」護理師說，「沒有任何事懲罰我。」這項觀察很重要。夢的圖像可能施壓，卻還沒證明它能強迫人行動。孟尋把這個差別寫在筆記本第一欄。

**EN 04**

A television crew arrived to entertain late-night viewers with the crisis. They asked for a facial close-up of a sobbing patient and offered to pay for an interview. A Li stepped between the camera and the patient, explaining that informed consent mattered more than dramatic footage. Meng was tempted to interfere with the broadcast by cutting the clinic's power. Instead, he asked the crew to leave and documented the request. Then the monitor beside the sleep room flickered. It displayed a red door with no patient connected to the machine. Meng filmed the screen with the clinic's own recorder and checked whether anyone had changed the software. Nobody had signed in. That was the first clue pointing outside the dreamers' minds.

**繁中 04**

電視團隊趕來想把事件做成深夜娛樂節目。他們要求拍哭泣病人的臉部特寫，願意付費訪問。阿璃站到鏡頭與病人之間，說明知情同意比戲劇化畫面重要。孟尋一度想切掉診所電力干擾轉播，最後只是要求團隊離開並留下紀錄。這時睡眠室旁的監測器突然閃爍，顯示一扇紅門，明明沒有病人連上機器。孟尋用診所錄影設備拍下螢幕，檢查是否有人改過軟體。沒有任何登入紀錄。這是第一項指向夢者腦外來源的線索。

**EN 05**

The ministry sent a public notice calling the dream harmless and temporary. Across the street, an opera company had already made promotional posters of the red door, as though the crisis were a fashionable stage design. A company representative offered a persuasive explanation: people merely shared common cultural images. Meng asked why strangers from five continents drew the same scratch near the brass knob. He showed the exact measurement rather than a picture chosen for drama. The representative did not know. A Li pointed out that a harmless image would not explain the lost addresses. She took the names of affected patients privately, with consent, and arranged a separate clinical review.

**繁中 05**

主管部會發布公告，稱共享夢境無害且暫時。街對面的歌劇團已把紅門做成宣傳海報，彷彿危機只是流行舞台設計。公司代表提出聽來有說服力的解釋：人們只是共享常見文化意象。孟尋追問為何五大洲陌生人都把黃銅門把旁的刮痕畫在同一處。他展示精確測量，而不是刻意挑選的戲劇畫面。代表答不出來。阿璃指出，無害圖像也無法解釋失去的地址記憶。她在取得同意後私下記下受影響者姓名，另行安排臨床覆核。

**EN 06**

The phenomenon appeared on the local network map before anyone called the television station. Meng followed the route of the sleep clinic's external signal to a third-party relay contracted by a shady wellness company. The contract was supposed to transmit anonymous quality checks, not images or memories. A Li refused to accuse the company without proof, but insisted they preserve its access logs. In the storage closet, Meng found a maintenance chart stamped with the name of his old childhood implant manufacturer. He touched the scar behind his ear. For years he had thought the implant was irrelevant history. Now its serial number appeared on the same sheet as the relay device that might be showing the world a door.

**繁中 06**

在有人聯絡電視台之前，共享夢境現象已出現在本地網路圖上。孟尋沿著診所外部訊號路徑追到一家可疑健康公司的第三方中繼站。合約本應只傳送匿名品質檢查，不該傳送圖像或記憶。阿璃不願沒有證據就指控公司，但堅持保存存取日誌。儲藏室裡，孟尋找到一張印有兒時植入裝置製造商名稱的維修圖。他摸了摸耳後疤痕。多年來他以為植入物只是無關的過去，現在它的序號卻出現在可能向全世界播放紅門的中繼設備同一張表上。

**EN 07**

A health ministry slogan above the clinic's entrance promised YOUR DREAMS BELONG TO YOU. Meng wondered why the patient database logged an unfamiliar service under dream usage, a service none of the volunteers had chosen. A youthful nurse brought him an old consent form with a line for 'shared experience' crossed out in blue ink. The same term appeared on the live monitor, though no device in the room was supposed to transmit it. 'That could be an error,' A Li said. 'Or someone else's connection,' Meng answered. On the wall of red-door drawings, a patient suddenly began writing an address beneath every sketch. It was the same address, repeated in different handwriting, and Meng recognized it as the former headquarters of the company that made his childhood implant.

**繁中 07**

診所門口的衛生部標語寫著「你的夢屬於你」。孟尋卻發現病人資料庫把一個陌生服務列入夢境使用紀錄，沒有志工選過這項服務。年輕護理師拿來舊同意書，「共享體驗」一欄被藍筆劃掉，現場監測器卻仍顯示這個詞，明明房裡沒有任何裝置應該傳送它。「也許是錯誤。」阿璃說。「也可能連到了別人的系統。」孟尋回答。滿牆紅門圖畫下，有病人忽然開始寫地址。所有圖下面都是同一個地址、不同人的筆跡。孟尋認出，那正是製造他兒時植入裝置的公司舊總部。

### 理解測驗

**Q1. Which development is established in Chapter 1?**

A. The investigators decide that every earlier warning was fictional.

B. An identical red door appeared in millions of dreams on one night. Sleep researcher Meng Xun was the only volunteer in his clinic who saw no door.

C. The central problem disappears without further action.

**答案：B。** 同一天晚上，數百萬人夢見相同紅門，睡眠研究員孟尋是診所唯一沒夢到的志工。

**Q2. What new complication or decision changes the investigation in this chapter?**

A. All the witnesses agree to destroy their records.

B. The team abandons the people affected and declares a perfect victory.

C. His colleague A Li reported that a dreamer who had opened it could no longer remember his own street address.

**答案：C。** 同事阿璃說，打開紅門的夢者已經記不得自己住的街道。


## CHAPTER 02｜A World Sharing One Dream｜共享同一個夢的世界

### 正文與翻譯

**EN 01**

Reports from five continents described the same brass knob and clock, but time zones shifted the dream's apparent hour. Something had changed, but not everybody noticed it in the same order. Meng Xun listened to the account of the nearest worker, then to someone standing farther away. Their differences were not necessarily lies. Each person had been given a different view and a different reason to be afraid of admitting uncertainty. A patient wrote yesterday's address in a notebook before sleeping. The page stayed still, even when the dream altered what the patient could remember in the morning. A note about the autograph lay beside the old register, with a date that Meng Xun could not verify. On the next form, the action field read carve, but the witness had never approved the operation.

**繁中 01**

五大洲的通報都說紅門有相同黃銅把手和時鐘，時區卻使夢中時間不同。某件事改變了，但人們發現它的順序不同。孟尋先聽近處工作人員的描述，再聽遠處旁觀者說法。差異不一定是謊言：每個人看到的角度不同，承認不確定時所要承擔的風險也不同。病人睡前把昨天的地址寫在筆記本裡。頁面不變，即使夢境改變了病人早晨能記起的事。一張談到「親筆簽名」的便條放在舊登記簿旁，日期讓孟尋無法核實。下一張表單把動作寫成「刻出」，見證人卻從未同意這項操作。

**EN 02**

His colleague A Li reported that a dreamer who had opened it could no longer remember his own street address. Meng Xun refused to pretend the previous decision had solved everything. The practical result was still there, useful but narrow. For each thing the team knew, there were two things it had not measured. Meng Xun drew a line through the claims that depended on speculation and asked A Li to keep the original record intact. People disagreed about convenience; Meng Xun separated the feeling from the facts they could test. The report claimed cruelty, but Meng Xun wanted an example instead of a reassuring noun. A Li noticed the word disguise in a note that someone had added after the first inspection.

**繁中 02**

同事阿璃說，打開紅門的夢者已經記不得自己住的街道。孟尋不願假裝前一次決定已解決所有問題。成果確實存在，卻有明確界限。每個已知事項旁都還有未測量的部分。孟尋劃掉靠猜測成立的說法，請阿璃保留原始紀錄。大家對「便利」意見不同，孟尋把感受與可檢驗的事實分開。報告聲稱有「殘忍」，孟尋要求具體例子，而不是安撫人的名詞。阿璃注意到初次檢查後有人在筆記裡加上「偽裝」一詞。

**EN 03**

A small disagreement changed the schedule. Meng Xun wanted to proceed before evidence disappeared; A Li wanted another check because a wrong conclusion might hurt people who could not easily defend themselves. They did not settle the matter by choosing a leader. They wrote down what a short, reversible test could actually establish. A Li noticed the word elastic in a note that someone had added after the first inspection. A handwritten label read explosive, but no one could say who had written it there. The report claimed feedback, but Meng Xun wanted an example instead of a reassuring noun.

**繁中 03**

一場小爭執改變了時程。孟尋想趁證據消失前行動；阿璃要求再核對一次，因為錯誤結論可能傷害難以自辯的人。他們沒有靠選領袖解決爭議，而是列出短暫、可逆測試真正能證明什麼。阿璃注意到初次檢查後有人在筆記裡加上「有彈性的」一詞。手寫標籤寫著「爆炸性的」，卻沒有人知道是誰留下的。報告聲稱有「回饋」，孟尋要求具體例子，而不是安撫人的名詞。

**EN 04**

Meng suspected a synchronized signal instead of a supernatural visitation, although no public network carried the same transmission. The plan had been amended three times, and each change left a trace. Meng Xun valued those corrections more than a perfect-looking first draft. If the outcome proved disappointing, the group would still be able to identify which assumption needed repair. A Li watched for the quiet participant who had not yet been invited to speak. The term immigrant appeared in the revised file; Meng Xun checked its meaning against the original page. A Li noticed the word initial in a note that someone had added after the first inspection. A handwritten label read mischief, but no one could say who had written it there.

**繁中 04**

孟尋懷疑是同步訊號，而非超自然訪客，卻找不到相同的公開網路傳輸。計畫改了三次，每次修改都留下痕跡。孟尋認為這些修正比看似完美的初稿更有價值。就算結果不理想，團隊仍能知道該修正哪一項假設。阿璃也注意到那名一直沒有發言機會的人。修訂檔案出現「移民」一詞，孟尋拿原始頁面核對它的意思。阿璃注意到初次檢查後有人在筆記裡加上「最初的」一詞。手寫標籤寫著「惡作劇」，卻沒有人知道是誰留下的。

**EN 05**

An official version reached the room before the practical work was finished. Meng Xun read it once, then compared it with what they had actually measured. The difference was not always malicious, but it could still hurt somebody. A Li asked that the correction be written in language the affected people could understand. A patient wrote yesterday's address in a notebook before sleeping. The page stayed still, even when the dream altered what the patient could remember in the morning. The earlier photograph clearly showed a paw; the newer version did not, so Meng Xun kept both. The report claimed prevention, but Meng Xun wanted an example instead of a reassuring noun. A Li wrote privilege in the margin and asked how the team would recognize it in practice.

**繁中 05**

實際工作還沒完成，官方版本就傳到現場。孟尋讀了一次，再比對真正測得的結果。差異未必都是惡意，卻仍可能傷害人。阿璃要求把更正寫成受影響者看得懂的語言。病人睡前把昨天的地址寫在筆記本裡。頁面不變，即使夢境改變了病人早晨能記起的事。較早的照片清楚顯示一項「爪子」，新版卻沒有，孟尋因此保留兩張。報告聲稱有「預防」，孟尋要求具體例子，而不是安撫人的名詞。阿璃在頁邊寫下「特權」，追問團隊實際上如何辨認。

**EN 06**

A witness asked whether the team was taking sides. Meng Xun answered that refusing to invent a fact was not the same as refusing to care. What mattered was whether the procedure protected someone whose story was less convenient to tell. A Li listened without interrupting and wrote down the question exactly as it had been asked. One witness used the word prominent; A Li noted that a description was not a measurement. A Li repeated the instruction to prosper, then asked whether anyone had the right to refuse. The term remark appeared in the revised file; Meng Xun checked its meaning against the original page.

**繁中 06**

一名見證人問團隊是否偏袒。孟尋回答，不捏造事實不代表冷漠。真正重要的是程序能否保護那個故事不方便被說出口的人。阿璃沒有打斷，逐字記下原問題。一位證人用了「醒目的」一詞，阿璃指出形容並不等於測量。阿璃重述「繁榮」的指示，再問當事人是否有權拒絕。修訂檔案出現「評論；言語」一詞，孟尋拿原始頁面核對它的意思。

**EN 07**

Even after the group agreed on the immediate step, another question remained. The answer would not arrive through a dramatic announcement. Someone would have to return to the place, compare the earlier notes, and accept the possibility that the next piece of evidence might spoil an attractive theory. Meng Xun prepared to do exactly that. At the clinic, a blank sleep chart did not mean an empty dream. A Li reminded the team that private experience deserved protection even when scientists wanted measurements. The term ruin appeared in the revised file; Meng Xun checked its meaning against the original page. On the next form, the action field read shift, but the witness had never approved the operation. A handwritten label read timetable, but no one could say who had written it there.

**繁中 07**

即使大家同意眼前步驟，另一個問題仍在。答案不會靠戲劇性公告降臨；有人得返回現場、比對舊筆記，並接受新證據可能推翻漂亮理論。孟尋準備做的正是這件事。診所裡空白的睡眠圖表不代表沒有夢。阿璃提醒團隊，哪怕科學家需要數據，私人經驗仍值得保護。修訂檔案出現「毀壞」一詞，孟尋拿原始頁面核對它的意思。下一張表單把動作寫成「輪班」，見證人卻從未同意這項操作。手寫標籤寫著「時刻表」，卻沒有人知道是誰留下的。
