## CHAPTER 01｜Three Deaths, One Account｜三宗死亡，一個帳戶

### 正文與翻譯

**EN 1**

Three people had died in separate accidents, and their insurance compensation had been sent to the same bank account. Cheng Wei discovered the pattern on a rainy Thursday, while reviewing a cluster of claims for the metropolitan insurance board. The first victim was an engineer, the second a teacher, and the third a nurse. They had lived in different cities and used different insurance agents. There was no certainty that their deaths were connected. But the account number was identical, down to the final digit. Cheng checked the transaction records twice before opening a formal inquiry. She would not call an error a crime without evidence. Still, someone had received money meant for three families bearing immense grief.

**繁中 1**

三個人分別死於不同的意外，他們的保險賠償金卻匯進同一個銀行帳戶。程薇在一個下雨的星期四發現這種規律，當時她正在審閱都會保險監理機構的一組理賠案。第一名死者是工程師，第二名是教師，第三名是護理師。他們住在不同城市，保險代理人也不相同。沒有人能確定三宗死亡彼此有關，但收款帳號從頭到尾完全相同。程薇核對交易紀錄兩次，才開啟正式調查。她不會在沒有證據時，把錯誤直接稱為犯罪。然而確實有人收走了原本應交給三個承受巨大悲痛的家庭的錢。

**EN 2**

She attended the nurse's memorial at a small chapel because the family had invited a representative from the insurer. The chapel stood beside a wetland habitat that the nurse had helped protect. Its wooden roof was part of the town's heritage, repaired by local volunteers. A cluster of white lilies lay near the door, their leaves beginning to wither. Cheng did not introduce herself as an investigator until the family asked why the promised payment had not arrived. The nurse's brother showed her a notice saying the compensation had been completed. “We thought the delay was our mistake,” he said. Cheng felt an impulse to promise immediate repayment, but she did not yet control the funds.

**繁中 2**

程薇出席護理師在小禮拜堂舉行的追思會，因為家屬邀請保險公司派代表參加。禮拜堂旁是一片那名護理師曾協助保護的濕地棲地，木屋頂則是地方文化遺產的一部分，由居民志工修繕。門口放著一簇白百合，葉子正開始枯萎。直到家屬問起承諾的保險金為何沒收到，她才以調查員身分介紹自己。護理師的弟弟拿出通知，上面寫著賠償已完成。他們巨大的失去，彷彿只變成表格上的數字。「我們以為是自己弄錯了，」他說。程薇有股衝動，想保證立刻重新付款，但她當時還無權動用那筆資金。

**EN 3**

Back at the office, Cheng requested the original transfer authorization. During the outage, a generator kept the records servers running. Her supervisor said the documents were probably complete. “Probably is not an audit result,” Cheng replied. He warned her that asking the bank to preserve every related log would be expensive. Cheng asked him to activate the relevant preservation provision anyway. The family could not wait while the company debated its public image. She also refused to disclose the victims' names to reporters who had learned about the matching account. The families deserved privacy before a newspaper headline turned them into clues for strangers.

**繁中 3**

回到辦公室，程薇申請查看原始轉帳授權。停電期間，發電機維持紀錄伺服器運作。她的主管認為文件應該完整。「『應該』不是稽核結果，」程薇回答。主管警告，要求銀行保留所有相關操作紀錄會非常昂貴。程薇仍要求啟動適用的證據保全條款。家屬無法在公司爭論形象時一直等待。她也拒絕向已知道帳號重複的記者揭露死者姓名。家屬應先有隱私保障，而不是在報紙標題裡變成陌生人討論的線索。

**EN 4**

Her colleague Lu Yuan arrived with a printed page from the beneficiary database. “I was envious of your calm,” he admitted. “Then I saw the bank's answer.” The account belonged to a clearing service, not a named individual. Cheng studied the codes. An optional payment route had been selected in all three files, although each policy normally required a direct transfer to the beneficiary. The selection might have been lawful if the families had given consent. None recalled doing so. Cheng made a note to check the consent forms rather than assume they had been forged. The distinction felt small, but it was the first useful insight of the day.

**繁中 4**

同事鹿原帶著受益人資料庫的列印頁來找她。「我原本很羨慕妳的冷靜，」他承認，「直到我看到銀行的回答。」那個帳戶屬於一家清算服務機構，而非具名的個人。程薇研究代碼，發現三份檔案都選用了可選擇的付款路徑，雖然每張保單通常都要求直接匯給受益人。如果家屬同意，這種選擇未必違法；但沒有任何家屬記得曾表示同意。程薇記下要查同意書，而不是立刻假設有人偽造。兩者看似只有一點差別，卻是她當天得到的第一項有用洞見。

**EN 5**

A message arrived from the agency's public forum. Someone had called the bank account “the killer's account,” and the phrase was spreading quickly. Cheng disliked it. The payment destination could be linked to fraud, negligence, or an innocent technical process; it could not by itself identify a murderer. She asked the communications office to publish only verified facts. Her supervisor called this cautious approach bad publicity. “The facts will remain after the headlines disappear,” Cheng said. The third victim's family had requested a meeting, and she did not want them hearing speculative accusations before her own explanation. She prepared a timeline with the payment dates clearly separated from the dates of death.

**繁中 5**

機構的公開討論區傳來訊息。有人把那個銀行帳戶叫做「兇手帳戶」，而且這個說法正快速傳開。程薇不喜歡這個稱呼。收款去向可能牽涉詐欺、疏失，或合法的技術程序；單靠帳號不可能直接認定殺人者。她請公關單位只公布已驗證的事實。主管認為這種謹慎方式對宣傳不利。「標題會消失，但事實仍在，」程薇說。第三名死者的家屬已要求會面，她不希望對方先從其他人口中聽到沒有根據的指控。她整理時間線，清楚區分付款日期與死亡日期。

**EN 6**

When Cheng examined the first paper file, she noticed an unusual signature on the beneficiary form. The witness line had been left blank, yet the digital system showed the change as completed. She asked Lu to verify whether the relevant policy actually required a witness. “Don't turn every gap into a conspiracy,” he said. “I won't,” Cheng answered. “But I won't ignore one either.” Lu laughed quietly and went to check. On the desk, a small cactus that the late nurse's family had sent to the office stood beneath the window. Cheng moved it away from the cold glass. She intended to keep this one alive.

**繁中 6**

程薇檢查第一份紙本檔案時，注意到受益人變更表上的簽名有異。見證人欄位空白，數位系統卻顯示變更已完成。她請鹿原確認相關保單是否真的要求見證人。「別把每個空白都當成陰謀，」鹿原說。「我不會，」程薇回答，「但我也不會忽略它。」鹿原輕聲笑了，便去查證。桌上擺著一盆小仙人掌，是已故護理師的家屬寄給辦公室的。程薇把它移離冰冷的窗玻璃。她打算讓這盆活下去。

**EN 7**

At four, the bank sent a partial reply. The transfers had not gone directly to the clearing service. They had passed through two separate authorizations, and the second approval carried the same employee code in all three cases. The name behind that code had been removed from the company's current staff directory. Cheng asked for the historical directory to be preserved. She also requested an independent review of the insurer's internal controls. The financial irregularity was becoming clearer, but the deaths themselves remained separate investigations. Lu returned and said the missing witness signature violated one policy, but not the other two. Their work had become narrower, and therefore more useful.

**繁中 7**

下午四點，銀行送來部分回覆。三筆款項並非直接匯進清算機構，而是先經過兩層獨立授權；第二層核准在三宗案件中都出現相同的員工代碼。該代碼對應的姓名已從公司現任員工名錄中移除。程薇要求保留歷史名錄，也申請獨立審查保險公司的內部控管。財務異常愈來愈清楚，但三宗死亡本身仍須分開調查。鹿原回來說，缺少見證人簽名違反其中一張保單，卻不違反另外兩張。調查範圍更窄，也因此更有用。

**EN 8**

Just before leaving, Cheng opened the file for the engineer's death and compared the dates once more. She stopped. The second authorization for his insurance payment was dated two days before the accident that killed him. At first she assumed a clock error. Then she checked the paper copy, which showed the same date in a different handwriting. She felt herself stumble over the words as she read them aloud to Lu. He asked her to sit down. They had not found a murderer. They had found a payment approval made before the event that should have triggered it. Somewhere in the records, someone had known more than the families had been told.

**繁中 8**

下班前，程薇打開工程師死亡案的檔案，再次比對日期，突然停住。那筆保險金的第二層授權，竟標示在奪走工程師性命的意外發生前兩天。她起初以為是時鐘錯誤，接著查看紙本副本；上面以不同筆跡寫著相同日期。她把內容念給鹿原聽時，幾乎說不出完整句子。鹿原請她坐下。他們還沒有找到殺人者，卻找到了一筆在理賠事故發生前就完成的付款核准。某個人早在家屬得知消息之前，就知道了更多事情。

   - A. It could indicate several different payment problems and did not establish who caused the deaths.
   - B. She had already identified the criminal.
   - C. The bank refused to discuss any account.

   **Answer: B.** 解析：理賠核准日期早於事故，形成需要獨立查明的時間矛盾。

## CHAPTER 04｜不完整的同意書

### 正文與翻譯

**EN 1**

One beneficiary-change form lacked the required independent witness. Another had been signed by an agent whose license had expired. Cheng compared the paper forms with scanned versions and found that the missing sections had been digitally filled after submission. The dates did not agree. Her manager wanted to classify the differences as paperwork mistakes. Cheng requested a legal hold and an outside review of the signatures. She did not assume the underlying deaths were murders.
