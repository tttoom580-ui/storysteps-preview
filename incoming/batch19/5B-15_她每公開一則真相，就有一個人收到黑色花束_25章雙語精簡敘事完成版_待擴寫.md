## CHAPTER 01｜The First Black Bouquet｜第一束黑色花束

### 正文與翻譯

**EN 1**

The black flowers arrived six hours after Xia Yao published her investigation into the city's largest charity. They were not delivered to the director accused of misusing donations. They went to a volunteer named Wei Min, whose only public statement had been that she did not understand the accounts. Xia learned about the bouquet through the metropolitan news forum, where a photograph was spreading quickly. Beneath it, strangers had written that the flowers were a warning, a confession, or an invitation to revenge. Xia had no certainty about any of those claims. She did know the director had made an immense profit from contracts that the charity could not explain. But she had not accused Wei Min of anything.

**繁中 1**

夏瑤公布對全市最大慈善機構的調查報導六小時後，黑色花束送到了某人手上。收件者不是被控挪用捐款的董事，而是一名叫魏敏的志工。她公開說過的話只有一句：「我看不懂那些帳目。」夏瑤在都會新聞討論區看到花束照片，照片正快速流傳。下面的陌生網友有人說這是警告，有人說是自白，還有人認為這是復仇邀請。夏瑤無法確定哪種說法是真的。她確實知道董事從慈善機構無法解釋的合約中獲得龐大利益，但她從未指控魏敏做過任何事。

**EN 2**

Xia went to the community chapel where Wei Min had volunteered for years. The building was part of the district's heritage, surrounded by a small garden that provided a habitat for bees and moths. Near the entrance lay a cluster of black roses. Their leaves had already begun to wither. A young florist named Li Qing was standing nearby with a notebook. “Those aren't naturally black,” Li said. “Someone dyed white roses.” Xia asked whether the arrangement came from Li's shop. “No,” she replied, “but the wrapping uses our old logo.” There was something useful in the detail, though Xia refused to treat it as proof of who had sent the flowers.

**繁中 2**

夏瑤來到魏敏多年擔任志工的社區禮拜堂。這座建築是地方文化遺產的一部分，旁邊的小花園為蜜蜂與飛蛾提供棲地。入口附近放著一簇黑玫瑰，葉子已開始枯萎。一名叫黎清的年輕花店員拿著筆記本站在旁邊。「那些不是天然黑色的，」黎清說，「有人把白玫瑰染黑了。」夏瑤問花束是不是出自她的店。「不是，」她回答，「但包裝用的是我們以前的商標。」這項細節可能有用，但夏瑤拒絕把它直接當成辨認送花者身分的證明。

**EN 3**

Inside, Wei Min refused a photograph but agreed to speak without her name appearing in the next article. She described the flowers arriving in a plain delivery box, with no sender listed in the transaction record. “People think I stole their money,” she said. “I answered questions because I wanted the charity to pay compensation to families it had failed.” Xia felt her familiar impulse to promise protection. She could not honestly promise safety from every stranger online. Instead, she offered to ask her editor to correct the misleading comments. Wei looked at her with tired eyes. “You told the truth about the director. But your article made it sound as though everyone in that building was guilty.”

**繁中 3**

禮拜堂裡，魏敏拒絕拍照，但同意在下一篇文章不公開姓名的前提下受訪。她說，花束裝在普通的送貨紙箱裡，交易紀錄沒有託運人姓名。「大家以為我偷了他們的錢，」她說，「我回答問題，只是因為希望慈善機構賠償那些被辜負的家庭。」夏瑤又有股熟悉的衝動，想承諾保護她。但她無法誠實地保證魏敏不受任何網路陌生人傷害。於是，她表示會請編輯更正容易造成誤會的留言。魏敏疲倦地看著她。「妳說出了董事的真相，可是妳的文章讓人以為整棟樓裡的每個人都有罪。」

**EN 4**

Back at the newsroom, the supervisor of investigations was watching the traffic figures on a wall screen. The newsroom's backup generator hummed beside a server room after a power failure. “People are interested,” he said. “Don't let a few flowers distract you.” Xia showed him the volunteer's account. He argued that a correction was optional because the article did not name Wei. “Our standards contain a provision on foreseeable harm,” Xia replied. “A person need not be named to be identified.” The supervisor frowned, but allowed her to draft a clarification. She removed an image that revealed the chapel's address and retained the verified financial documents.

**繁中 4**

回到報社，調查組主管正盯著牆上螢幕的流量數字。停電後，新聞機房旁的備援發電機持續嗡嗡作響。「大家很有興趣，」他說，「別讓幾朵花使妳分心。」夏瑤給他看志工的說法。他辯稱，原報導沒有寫出魏敏姓名，是否更正可以選擇。「我們的編輯規範有一條可預見傷害的規定，」夏瑤回答，「一個人即使沒有被寫出姓名，也可能被辨認出來。」主管皺眉，但允許她草擬澄清。她移除了會暴露禮拜堂地址的照片，保留經查核的財務文件。

**EN 5**

Li Qing called while Xia was working. She had found a box of unused wrapping paper in the shop's storage room. The printed logo was from a design the shop had abandoned six years earlier. “I was envious of the larger shops back then,” Li admitted. “I wanted our brand to look expensive.” The admission made Xia smile briefly. Li continued, “Only three employees had keys to that storage room. Two still work here; one left years ago.” Xia asked Li not to send private customer records. “Just preserve the paper and write down where you found it,” she said. The detail offered an insight into the flowers' age, not their sender.

**繁中 5**

夏瑤工作時接到黎清來電。黎清在店裡的倉庫找到一箱沒用完的包裝紙，印著六年前就停用的商標。「那時我很羨慕大型花店，」黎清承認，「我想讓我們的品牌看起來很高級。」夏瑤短暫笑了一下。黎清接著說：「只有三名員工有那間倉庫的鑰匙。兩個還在店裡，另一個多年前離職。」夏瑤請她不要傳送私人客戶紀錄。「把紙留好，記下妳在哪裡找到它就行，」她說。這個細節能幫忙理解包裝紙的年代，卻還不能辨認送花者。

**EN 6**

At six, Xia read the clarification aloud before publishing it. She did not disclose Wei's private statement; she reported only that the volunteer had not been accused of financial misconduct and that the photograph could mislead readers. The comments turned hostile anyway. One reader said she was protecting a friend. Another said she had invented the flowers for attention. Xia seemed to stumble over the final sentence, then stopped and read it again. “The people harmed by the charity still deserve answers,” she wrote, “and so do those harmed by careless reporting.” She knew that a correction could not erase the first article. It was still necessary.

**繁中 6**

六點時，夏瑤在發表澄清前大聲讀了一遍。她沒有揭露魏敏私下的說法，只說明這名志工並未遭指控涉及財務不法，而且照片可能誤導讀者。但留言仍變得尖銳。有人說她在保護朋友，也有人說她為了流量捏造黑花。夏瑤讀到最後一句時似乎結巴，便停下來再讀一次。她寫道：「受到慈善機構傷害的人值得答案；受到草率報導傷害的人同樣值得答案。」她知道澄清無法抹除第一篇文章造成的影響，但仍必須做。

**EN 7**

The newsroom grew quiet as evening settled over the city. Xia sorted the evidence into two files: the charity's unexplained contracts and the black bouquet. Mixing them would make every suspicious flower look like proof of theft. Her colleague Wu Yao examined a screenshot of the package. “Look at this corner,” he said. There was a narrow strip of grey tape beneath the black ribbon. The tape bore a printing mark used by the newspaper's own document department. Xia checked three old packages in the supply cabinet. The marks matched, but the tape could have been obtained elsewhere. Wu agreed. They placed the observation in the second file, marked **SOURCE UNCONFIRMED**.

**繁中 7**

夜色降臨，報社慢慢安靜下來。夏瑤把證據分成兩份：慈善機構說不清楚的合約，以及黑色花束。如果混在一起，任何可疑的花都會被誤認為侵占捐款的證據。同事鄔曜檢視包裹截圖。「看這個角落，」他說。黑緞帶下方有一條窄窄的灰膠帶，上面印著報社文件部門使用的標記。夏瑤從用品櫃找出三個舊包裹，標記確實相同，但膠帶也可能流到外面。鄔曜同意。他們把觀察結果放進第二份檔案，註記：「來源未確認。」

**EN 8**

A new message arrived just as Xia shut her laptop. It contained a photograph of a second bouquet, still wrapped, lying on a desk she recognized. It was her editor's desk. Behind the flowers lay a printed copy of her clarification, including a paragraph she had deleted before publication. Xia opened the article history. The deleted text existed only in the newsroom's private editing system. Someone with access to that system had seen it. For the first time, the black flowers felt less like a public threat and more like a conversation being conducted through stolen drafts. Xia saved the message and called her editor. No one answered.

**繁中 8**

夏瑤正要關上筆電時，收到新訊息。裡面是一張第二束花的照片：花還包著，放在她認得的辦公桌上。那是編輯的桌子。花後面放著她的澄清稿，連她在發表前刪除的一段文字都印在上頭。夏瑤打開文章修訂紀錄；那段被刪掉的文字只存在於報社私有編輯系統。有人能存取系統，而且看過它。黑花第一次不像公開威脅，倒像有人透過偷來的稿件與她對話。夏瑤保存訊息，打給編輯。沒有人接聽。

   - B. The charity director had requested more publicity.
   - C. She discovered the charity's contracts were all lawful.

   **Answer: A.** 解析：魏敏雖未被點名，卻因照片與報導脈絡被誤認為涉案，夏瑤因此澄清。

## CHAPTER 04｜消失的署名
