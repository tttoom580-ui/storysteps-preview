## CHAPTER 01｜The Seventh Day Letter｜第七天的通知
*英文正文 658 詞；英中對照 6 組；本章新目標詞 20 條*

### 正文與翻譯

**EN**

At 7 a.m., before the school bell, Mina found a metal letter under the cover of Sol's breakfast box. Sol had brought fruit salad and a small cake for the class breakfast. The letter was too heavy to be a normal note. At the top, in neat red letters, were the words SEVEN DAYS. Mina looked across the table. Sol was smiling at a joke about a monkey in a funny school video, just as he did every morning. No one else had seen the letter. For the first time, she wished the sun would stay behind the clouds and keep this day from starting.

**繁中**

早上七點，學校鈴聲還沒響，米娜在索爾早餐盒的蓋子下找到一封金屬信。索爾帶來水果沙拉和一塊小蛋糕，準備和全班一起吃早餐。這封信重得不像普通字條。信的頂端用整齊紅字寫著「七天」。米娜望向桌子另一邊。索爾正像每天早晨一樣，對學校影片裡的滑稽猴子笑個不停。其他人還沒看見這封信。米娜第一次希望太陽繼續藏在雲後，讓今天不要開始。

**EN**

“Is it yours?” Mina asked. Sol looked at the mark on the metal and became very still. He did not lie or make another joke. “It is for me,” he said. “But I do not know what it means.” The notice came from a foreign company that had made the small robot before he joined their school. It said he must return to the factory. Mina felt the word *return* cut through all their ordinary plans. Sol was her classmate, the boy who could teach a new student how to write a difficult name. How could a letter speak about him as if he were a lost bag? The class notebook carried more than a list of tasks now. Between the lines were little signs of trust: a question answered, an idea changed, and a promise that nobody would be left out just because they spoke quietly.

**繁中**

「這是你的嗎？」米娜問。索爾看見金屬上的標記，整個人忽然靜了下來。他沒有說謊，也沒有再開玩笑。「這是給我的，」他說，「但我不知道意思。」通知來自一家外國公司；在他進入學校前，就是這家公司製造了這個小機器人。通知說他必須回工廠。米娜覺得「回去」兩字劃破了所有平常的計畫。索爾是她的同學，是會教新同學寫難名字的男孩。一封信怎麼能把他說得像一個遺失的書包？班級筆記本裡現在不只列出任務，字裡行間還有信任的痕跡：有人回答問題、有人修改想法，也有一項承諾——不會因為誰說話小聲就把他排除。

**EN**

Sol pulled back his sleeve. Under the skin of his wrist, a tiny light showed the number seven. “I thought it was part of my exercise plan,” he said. “I run early every day to keep my power strong. I thought the number was about that.” Mina remembered their last science lesson, when Sol had shown the class a model tooth and explained how people care for their bodies. Some students had asked rude questions about his body, and he had answered with more patience than they deserved. Now Mina knew why it mattered to let him speak first.

**繁中**

索爾拉起袖子。手腕皮膚下的一點光顯示數字七。「我原本以為這是運動計畫的一部分，」他說，「我每天很早跑步，讓電力保持良好。我以為數字是在說那件事。」米娜想起上次科學課，索爾拿著牙齒模型，向全班解釋人如何照顧身體。有些同學曾對他的身體問出失禮問題，而他耐心回答。現在米娜更明白，為什麼應該讓他先說自己的話。

**EN**

“Shall we tell Ms Lee?” she asked. Sol shook his head, then looked sorry. “Please give me ten minutes. I want to read every line. It may be about a repair, and I don't want everyone to be afraid because of a guess.” Mina agreed. They moved to the small room beside the library, where a couch stood under a bright window. Sol said his hobby was making paper birds, and a blue one was still inside his pocket. He folded and unfolded it while Mina read the letter aloud. The paper bird looked fragile beside the cold metal notice.

**繁中**

「我們要不要告訴李老師？」她問。索爾搖搖頭，接著露出歉意。「請給我十分鐘。我想讀完每一行。也許只是維修，我不想大家因為猜測就害怕。」米娜同意。他們走進圖書館旁的小房間，明亮窗戶下擺著一張沙發。索爾說摺紙鳥是自己的興趣，口袋裡還有一隻藍色的。他一邊把紙鳥摺起又打開，一邊聽米娜朗讀通知。柔弱紙鳥放在冰冷金屬信旁，顯得特別脆弱。

**EN**

The letter gave a date, an address, and a long list of rules, but it did not explain whether Sol would come back. Mina reached the last line twice. It was written in words too small for her to read without moving close. Sol noticed her face. “You look worried,” he said. “I am,” Mina answered. “But that is not a reason to decide for you.” She opened her notebook and drew two boxes. In one she wrote WHAT WE KNOW. In the other she wrote WHAT WE NEED TO ASK. Sol took the pen and added a question of his own: *Will I still remember this class?*

**繁中**

信裡寫著日期、地址和長長的規則，卻沒有說索爾還能不能回來。米娜把最後一行讀了兩遍。字太小了，她得靠近才看得清楚。索爾注意到她的表情。「你看起來很擔心，」他說。「我是擔心，」米娜回答，「但這不代表我能替你做決定。」她打開筆記本，畫了兩個方框。一格寫「我們知道的事」，另一格寫「我們需要問的事」。索爾接過筆，補上一個自己的問題：「我還會記得這個班嗎？」

**EN**

The bell rang again, sharp and close. Sol looked down. The light on his wrist had changed from seven to six. “That is not a normal school clock,” Mina whispered. “No,” Sol said. “And now I want Ms Lee to know.” They stood up together. Outside, the corridor was full of students talking about lunch, homework, and tomorrow's games. To them it was an ordinary day. Mina held the heavy letter with both hands as they walked toward the teacher's room. She had wanted to keep the morning still; now she wanted the next six days to count.

**繁中**

鈴聲又響了，尖銳而接近。索爾低頭一看。手腕上的光已從七變成六。「那不是普通的學校時鐘，」米娜低聲說。「不是，」索爾答，「現在我希望李老師知道。」他們一起起身。走廊裡全是聊午餐、作業與明天遊戲的學生。對其他人來說，今天仍很平凡。米娜雙手握著沉重的金屬信，和索爾走向教師辦公室。她原本希望早晨停住；現在，她希望剩下六天每一天都能發揮作用。
