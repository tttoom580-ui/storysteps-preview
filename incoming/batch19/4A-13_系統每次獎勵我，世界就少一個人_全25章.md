# 4A-13｜系統每次獎勵我，世界就少一個人

*Every Reward Erases a Person*

第四批・第4級（3000–4000詞級）｜黑暗系統流｜全25章雙語教學初稿。

主角：林喬（修復技師）；重要角色：葉沐（醫療紀錄員）。

**編輯註：**故事事件與結局為本次獨立設計，配詞融入與敘事中間段採規則化擴寫；這是完整章節的教學初稿，尚未經人工文學潤稿。

## CHAPTER 01｜The Prize Nobody Cheered For｜無人歡呼的獎勵

### 正文與翻譯

**EN 01**

The lift should have killed seventeen people. Lin Qiao saw its cables split above the crowded shaft, tasted acid at the back of his throat, and threw his body against the emergency lever. The red handle came away in his hand. Before he could shout, silver letters spread across the broken control panel: FIRST RESCUE COMPLETE. CLAIM YOUR REWARD. The lift settled so gently that people inside applauded. Qiao did not. His colleague Chen was supposed to attend the workers' assembly downstairs, and she had left her paper lunch bag beside his tools. The bag was still there when the supervisor said, with genuine surprise, that no employee named Chen had ever worked in the building. The silver screen offered Qiao a new wrench. He put both hands behind his back.

**繁中 01**

電梯本該害死十七個人。林喬看見鋼索在擁擠的井道上方斷裂，喉嚨後方泛起酸味，整個人撞向緊急控制桿。紅色把手竟在他掌心斷開。還沒來得及大喊，破掉的控制盤就浮出銀色文字：「首次救援完成。請領取獎勵。」電梯卻輕柔地停穩，裡面的乘客鼓掌。林喬沒有。他的同事陳姐本來要去樓下參加工人集會，紙便當袋還放在他的工具旁。袋子仍在，主管卻真心驚訝地說：「這棟樓從來沒有姓陳的員工。」銀幕送來一把新扳手，林喬把雙手藏到背後。

**EN 02**

The morning cleaner pushed a broom past the lift and asked why Chen's lunch bag was still on the floor. The supervisor took the bag away before Qiao could answer. In the maintenance office, he tried to construct a list of everyone who had been in the shaft when the cables broke. Seventeen passengers, two technicians, one cleaner: the numbers were simple. But the roster omitted Chen, who had checked the cables with him every Thursday. Qiao asked the supervisor to cooperate by showing yesterday's paper work orders. The man refused, not angrily but with genuine confusion. 'There was no Chen,' he repeated. Qiao remembered her chipped green mug and the way she always sang during inspections.

**繁中 02**

早班清潔員推著掃帚走過電梯，問陳姐的便當袋為何還在地板上。林喬還沒回答，主管便收走袋子。他回到維修室，試著建構鋼索斷裂時在井道附近的人員名單：十七名乘客、兩名技師、一位清潔員。數字很簡單，名冊卻漏掉每個星期四都和他一起檢查鋼索的陳姐。林喬請主管合作，調出昨天的紙本工單。對方拒絕了，不是憤怒，而是真心困惑。「從來沒有這個人。」他又說。林喬卻記得她缺了口的綠杯子，以及她每次巡檢時哼的歌。

**EN 03**

At the lift's copper contact plate, Qiao found fresh scrape marks surrounding a screw Chen had replaced last week. The digital counter beside it reported seventeen passengers rescued and zero workers harmed. He checked the panel twice. A worker's death would have been terrible enough; being removed from the category of workers was a second injury the machine could not count. One apprentice called Qiao a coward for refusing the glowing wrench. The apprentice wanted a tool that could repair any machine in seconds. Qiao held up the discarded red handle. 'If this thing can save a lift,' he asked, 'why does it need me to claim a prize? And why did someone disappear before I even touched the screen?' Nobody had an answer.

**繁中 03**

林喬在電梯的銅製接點板上找到新刮痕，正圍著陳姐上星期換過的螺絲。旁邊數位計數器顯示救出十七名乘客、傷亡員工零人。他核對兩次。有人死亡已經夠可怕，若連員工身分都被抹去，就是機器無法計算的第二次傷害。一位學徒罵他膽小鬼，因為他拒領發光扳手；學徒想要一把能在幾秒內修好所有機器的工具。林喬舉起斷裂的紅把手：「如果它真能修電梯，為什麼要我領獎？而且我碰螢幕之前，怎麼就有人消失了？」沒有人答得出來。

**EN 04**

The company had decided to entertain customers with a clip of the rescue, already uploaded to its public channel, complete with triumphant music and smiling passengers. Qiao watched the unedited service camera instead. In its final three seconds, Chen crossed the frame carrying a test meter. Then the picture flickered, and the camera's facial recognition tag replaced her name with an empty rectangle. Qiao could not interfere with the original recording without destroying the one trace he had left. He copied the raw file onto an isolated drive and asked the apprentice to write down the time before looking at the footage again. Only then did he press play. Chen's body was still visible in the first copy; in the online clip there was only an unnatural patch of light.

**繁中 04**

公司娛樂頻道已上傳救援片段，配上勝利樂曲與乘客笑臉。林喬改看未剪輯的維修鏡頭。最後三秒，陳姐提著測試儀走過畫面，影像隨即閃爍；臉部辨識標籤把她的名字換成空白方格。林喬不敢干擾原始檔，以免毀掉最後一項痕跡。他把原檔複製到離線硬碟，請學徒先記下時間，才再次播放。第一份副本裡仍能看見陳姐的身影；網路上的剪輯卻只有一塊不自然的亮光。

**EN 05**

By noon the ministry had issued a statement calling the rescue an example of successful civic technology. A reporter arrived from the opera house across the square, where a children's show had been interrupted by the lift alarm. She asked whether the public could see the unedited footage. The supervisor gave a persuasive speech about protecting workers' privacy, then refused to confirm how many workers had been present. Qiao disliked using the vanished woman as a public spectacle. He also disliked allowing a private company to decide that she had never existed. He offered the reporter the numbers he could verify and explained what remained uncertain. The reporter lowered her microphone and asked, quietly, 'Are you saying somebody is missing?'

**繁中 05**

中午，主管部會發聲明，稱救援是成功公民科技的範例。廣場對面歌劇院來了一名記者；電梯警報打斷了那裡的兒童演出。她問公眾能否看到未剪輯影片。主管用極有說服力的口吻談保護員工隱私，接著卻拒絕確認現場究竟有幾名員工。林喬不喜歡把失蹤女子當成公開表演，也不願讓私人公司決定她從未存在。他只交代可確認的數字，並說明其餘部分尚不確定。記者放低麥克風，悄聲問：「你是說，有人不見了？」

**EN 06**

The phenomenon became harder to dismiss when Qiao tried the service route to Chen's apartment. A security guard recognized her apartment number but insisted it had been vacant for years. The lock bore new scratches, and a shady repair company had placed a fresh seal over the nameplate. Qiao did not force the door. He photographed the seal and called the emergency maintenance number printed on it; the operator answered with the exact same phrase the reward screen had used. FIRST RESCUE COMPLETE. CLAIM YOUR REWARD. He ended the call. Inside the apartment, a small voice began singing the tune Chen used at work. Qiao stood in the corridor until the voice stopped, then wrote the time on his sleeve.

**繁中 06**

林喬走維修通道前往陳姐家時，這個現象更難否認。保全認得房號，卻說那間公寓空了好幾年。門鎖有新刮痕，一家可疑的修理公司還在門牌上貼了新封條。林喬沒有強行開門。他拍下封條，撥上面的緊急維修電話；接線員竟重複獎勵螢幕的字：「首次救援完成。請領取獎勵。」他掛斷電話。公寓裡傳來小孩的歌聲，唱的正是陳姐工作時常哼的旋律。林喬在走廊站到聲音停止，把時間寫在衣袖上。

**EN 07**

By dusk the official slogan on the lift had changed from EVERY LIFE COUNTS to EVERY REWARD COUNTS. Lin Qiao had seen enough to distrust the word counts. He copied the machine's electricity usage before and after the impossible repair; the difference was tiny, nowhere near the cost of making a person vanish. In the staff corridor he found a youthful drawing of a woman with a red scarf, hidden under a loose noticeboard screw. Someone had drawn her holding a little girl's hand. Ye Mu, the medical records officer who arrived after sunset, stopped so abruptly that her bag struck the wall. 'I know that woman,' she whispered. 'She was in my hospital yesterday. I can't find her file today.' Then the reward screen chimed, although neither of them had touched it.

**繁中 07**

傍晚，電梯上的官方口號從「每條生命都重要」變成「每次獎勵都重要」。林喬已經不再相信這種計數。他抄下神奇修復前後的用電量：差異微小，遠遠不足以解釋一個人憑空消失的代價。員工走廊裡，他在鬆動的公告板螺絲下發現一張充滿稚氣的畫，畫著繫紅圍巾的女人牽著小女孩。入夜後趕來的醫療紀錄員葉沐看見畫，突然停步，背包撞上牆。「我認識她。」她低聲說，「她昨天還在我們醫院，今天檔案就找不到了。」接著獎勵螢幕又響了，雖然誰也沒有碰它。

### 理解測驗

**Q1. Which development is established in Chapter 1?**

A. The investigators decide that every earlier warning was fictional.

B. A silver interface offered Lin Qiao a miraculous repair tool after he stopped a runaway lift. At the same moment, his colleague's chair emptied and her name vanished from the roster.

C. The central problem disappears without further action.

**答案：B。** 銀色介面獎勵林喬一把神奇修理工具，但同事的椅子同時空了，名冊上的名字也消失了。

**Q2. What new complication or decision changes the investigation in this chapter?**

A. All the witnesses agree to destroy their records.

B. The team abandons the people affected and declares a perfect victory.

C. Ye Mu remembered a patient with the same missing face, although the hospital database insisted the patient had never existed.

**答案：C。** 葉沐記得一張相同的臉，醫院資料庫卻堅稱那名病人從未存在。


## CHAPTER 02｜The Empty Locker｜空置的置物櫃

### 正文與翻譯

**EN 01**

Qiao found a work locker packed with someone's lunch and a child's drawing, yet every badge reader treated the locker as unassigned. Something had changed, but not everybody noticed it in the same order. Lin Qiao listened to the account of the nearest worker, then to someone standing farther away. Their differences were not necessarily lies. Each person had been given a different view and a different reason to be afraid of admitting uncertainty. A number on the system's ledger looked harmless until Lin Qiao asked whose place it represented. The room went still; no one wanted to answer with a name. A note about the autograph lay beside the old register, with a date that Lin Qiao could not verify. On the next form, the action field read carve, but the witness had never approved the operation.

**繁中 01**

林喬發現一個有便當和兒童畫的置物櫃，刷卡紀錄卻顯示它從未有人使用。某件事改變了，但人們發現它的順序不同。林喬先聽近處工作人員的描述，再聽遠處旁觀者說法。差異不一定是謊言：每個人看到的角度不同，承認不確定時所要承擔的風險也不同。系統帳冊的數字本來看似無害，直到林喬問那代表誰的位置。房間安靜下來，沒人願意用名字作答。一張談到「親筆簽名」的便條放在舊登記簿旁，日期讓林喬無法核實。下一張表單把動作寫成「刻出」，見證人卻從未同意這項操作。

**EN 02**

Ye Mu remembered a patient with the same missing face, although the hospital database insisted the patient had never existed. Lin Qiao refused to pretend the previous decision had solved everything. The practical result was still there, useful but narrow. For each thing the team knew, there were two things it had not measured. Lin Qiao drew a line through the claims that depended on speculation and asked Ye Mu to keep the original record intact. People disagreed about convenience; Lin Qiao separated the feeling from the facts they could test. The report claimed cruelty, but Lin Qiao wanted an example instead of a reassuring noun. Ye Mu noticed the word disguise in a note that someone had added after the first inspection.

**繁中 02**

葉沐記得一張相同的臉，醫院資料庫卻堅稱那名病人從未存在。林喬不願假裝前一次決定已解決所有問題。成果確實存在，卻有明確界限。每個已知事項旁都還有未測量的部分。林喬劃掉靠猜測成立的說法，請葉沐保留原始紀錄。大家對「便利」意見不同，林喬把感受與可檢驗的事實分開。報告聲稱有「殘忍」，林喬要求具體例子，而不是安撫人的名詞。葉沐注意到初次檢查後有人在筆記裡加上「偽裝」一詞。

**EN 03**

A small disagreement changed the schedule. Lin Qiao wanted to proceed before evidence disappeared; Ye Mu wanted another check because a wrong conclusion might hurt people who could not easily defend themselves. They did not settle the matter by choosing a leader. They wrote down what a short, reversible test could actually establish. Ye Mu noticed the word elastic in a note that someone had added after the first inspection. A handwritten label read explosive, but no one could say who had written it there. The report claimed feedback, but Lin Qiao wanted an example instead of a reassuring noun.

**繁中 03**

一場小爭執改變了時程。林喬想趁證據消失前行動；葉沐要求再核對一次，因為錯誤結論可能傷害難以自辯的人。他們沒有靠選領袖解決爭議，而是列出短暫、可逆測試真正能證明什麼。葉沐注意到初次檢查後有人在筆記裡加上「有彈性的」一詞。手寫標籤寫著「爆炸性的」，卻沒有人知道是誰留下的。報告聲稱有「回饋」，林喬要求具體例子，而不是安撫人的名詞。

**EN 04**

A second reward appeared when he tried to open the locker; he refused it, and the stranger's pencil marks stayed visible. The plan had been amended three times, and each change left a trace. Lin Qiao valued those corrections more than a perfect-looking first draft. If the outcome proved disappointing, the group would still be able to identify which assumption needed repair. Ye Mu watched for the quiet participant who had not yet been invited to speak. The term immigrant appeared in the revised file; Lin Qiao checked its meaning against the original page. Ye Mu noticed the word initial in a note that someone had added after the first inspection. A handwritten label read mischief, but no one could say who had written it there.

**繁中 04**

第二項獎勵彈出時他拒絕領取，陌生人的鉛筆字跡才沒有消失。計畫改了三次，每次修改都留下痕跡。林喬認為這些修正比看似完美的初稿更有價值。就算結果不理想，團隊仍能知道該修正哪一項假設。葉沐也注意到那名一直沒有發言機會的人。修訂檔案出現「移民」一詞，林喬拿原始頁面核對它的意思。葉沐注意到初次檢查後有人在筆記裡加上「最初的」一詞。手寫標籤寫著「惡作劇」，卻沒有人知道是誰留下的。

**EN 05**

An official version reached the room before the practical work was finished. Lin Qiao read it once, then compared it with what they had actually measured. The difference was not always malicious, but it could still hurt somebody. Ye Mu asked that the correction be written in language the affected people could understand. A number on the system's ledger looked harmless until Lin Qiao asked whose place it represented. The room went still; no one wanted to answer with a name. The earlier photograph clearly showed a paw; the newer version did not, so Lin Qiao kept both. The report claimed prevention, but Lin Qiao wanted an example instead of a reassuring noun. Ye Mu wrote privilege in the margin and asked how the team would recognize it in practice.

**繁中 05**

實際工作還沒完成，官方版本就傳到現場。林喬讀了一次，再比對真正測得的結果。差異未必都是惡意，卻仍可能傷害人。葉沐要求把更正寫成受影響者看得懂的語言。系統帳冊的數字本來看似無害，直到林喬問那代表誰的位置。房間安靜下來，沒人願意用名字作答。較早的照片清楚顯示一項「爪子」，新版卻沒有，林喬因此保留兩張。報告聲稱有「預防」，林喬要求具體例子，而不是安撫人的名詞。葉沐在頁邊寫下「特權」，追問團隊實際上如何辨認。

**EN 06**

A witness asked whether the team was taking sides. Lin Qiao answered that refusing to invent a fact was not the same as refusing to care. What mattered was whether the procedure protected someone whose story was less convenient to tell. Ye Mu listened without interrupting and wrote down the question exactly as it had been asked. One witness used the word prominent; Ye Mu noted that a description was not a measurement. Ye Mu repeated the instruction to prosper, then asked whether anyone had the right to refuse. The term remark appeared in the revised file; Lin Qiao checked its meaning against the original page.

**繁中 06**

一名見證人問團隊是否偏袒。林喬回答，不捏造事實不代表冷漠。真正重要的是程序能否保護那個故事不方便被說出口的人。葉沐沒有打斷，逐字記下原問題。一位證人用了「醒目的」一詞，葉沐指出形容並不等於測量。葉沐重述「繁榮」的指示，再問當事人是否有權拒絕。修訂檔案出現「評論；言語」一詞，林喬拿原始頁面核對它的意思。

**EN 07**

Even after the group agreed on the immediate step, another question remained. The answer would not arrive through a dramatic announcement. Someone would have to return to the place, compare the earlier notes, and accept the possibility that the next piece of evidence might spoil an attractive theory. Lin Qiao prepared to do exactly that. Across the workshop, ordinary tools made quiet sounds against metal. Lin Qiao preferred their honest limitations to an upgrade whose human cost could not be named. The term ruin appeared in the revised file; Lin Qiao checked its meaning against the original page. On the next form, the action field read shift, but the witness had never approved the operation. A handwritten label read timetable, but no one could say who had written it there.

**繁中 07**

即使大家同意眼前步驟，另一個問題仍在。答案不會靠戲劇性公告降臨；有人得返回現場、比對舊筆記，並接受新證據可能推翻漂亮理論。林喬準備做的正是這件事。工作間裡，普通工具敲打金屬發出輕響。林喬寧願接受工具誠實的限制，也不願領取說不出人命代價的升級。修訂檔案出現「毀壞」一詞，林喬拿原始頁面核對它的意思。下一張表單把動作寫成「輪班」，見證人卻從未同意這項操作。手寫標籤寫著「時刻表」，卻沒有人知道是誰留下的。
