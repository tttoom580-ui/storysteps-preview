## CHAPTER 01｜The Captain with a Younger Face｜變年輕的船長

### 正文與翻譯

**EN 1**

The exploration ship *Oriole* arrived thirty years after its disappearance, and the first batch of medical images made no sense. The seven passengers were alive, but their bones and skin belonged to people twenty years younger than they had been at launch. Dr. Lu Sha, head of orbital quarantine, studied the projection above her desk. “A broken scanner?” someone suggested. She checked the medical circuit and ordered a triple examination. The results matched. On the screen, a scar ran along the rim of Captain Arsen's left ear, exactly as the old photographs showed. His face, however, was that of a man in his thirties. The ship had crossed the system's boundary without sending a signal.

**繁中 1**

探索船「金鶯號」在失蹤三十年後突然返航，第一批醫學影像完全令人摸不著頭緒。七名乘員都還活著，但他們的骨骼與皮膚，竟比出發時年輕了二十歲。軌道檢疫主管陸莎醫師研究著桌面上方的投影。「會不會是掃描器壞了？」有人提出疑問。她檢查醫療迴路，要求進行三重檢查，結果卻完全相同。螢幕上，阿森船長左耳邊緣的疤痕和舊照片一模一樣；他的臉卻像三十多歲的男人。這艘船穿越星系邊界時，沒有發出任何訊號。

**EN 2**

The station was operating under an emergency quarantine regime, with food and lodging supplied by a nonprofit medical team. Lu had helped write the rules. She knew what decent care required, especially when frightened people were treated as dangerous objects. Her old mentor had once told her that an inquiry began with questions, not accusations. She remembered that lesson while security officers argued over the placement of armed guards near the airlock. “No weapons where patients can see them,” she ordered. “Regardless of what came home, we have seven human beings in need of examination.” A guard asked whether they should lock the observation window. Lu said no. Fear would not make the evidence stronger.

**繁中 2**

太空站正依緊急檢疫制度運作，飲食與住宿由非營利醫療團隊提供。陸莎曾參與制定這套規則，知道正當照護需要什麼，尤其是在受驚的人容易被當成危險物品時。她過去的導師曾說，調查要從問題開始，而不是從指控開始。當保全人員爭論是否在氣閘旁設置持槍警衛時，她想起了這句話。「不要把武器擺在病人看得到的地方，」她下令，「無論回來的是什麼，我們現在面對的都是七個需要接受檢查的人。」一名警衛問要不要把觀察窗鎖上。陸莎說不要。恐懼無法讓證據更可靠。

**EN 3**

An engineer named Sato climbed onto the saddle of a cargo lift beside the ship. He tried to reinforce a damaged communication pipe, but a transparent coating began to dissolve beneath his glove. The material did not match any known ship component. Under the strain of the lift's weight, the pipe bent like wet paper. Sato called for help before touching it again. “This will terrify the safety board,” he muttered. Lu leaned close to the camera feed. The coating was retreating from the light, not the heat. Something on the ship responded to observation in a way ordinary metal did not. She ordered the engineers to record the change instead of removing the material.

**繁中 3**

工程師佐藤爬上停在太空船旁的貨運升降架鞍座，試圖加固一條損壞的通訊管。但一層透明塗料開始在他的手套下溶解。這種材料不符合任何已知的船體零件規格。升降架的重量造成壓力，管子竟像濕紙一樣彎曲。佐藤沒有再碰它，而是先呼叫支援。「安全委員會肯定會嚇壞，」他嘟囔道。陸莎靠近監視畫面。塗層正在避開光，而不是熱。船上有某種東西會對觀察產生反應，和普通金屬完全不同。她命令工程師先記錄變化，不要立刻移除材料。

**EN 4**

At noon, the station director entered with three officials from the Ministry of Exploration. “We must implement the return ceremony before the news spreads,” he announced. One official called the medical delays an insult to the captain. Another said only an idiot would keep national heroes behind glass. Lu placed both medical reports on the table. “A ceremony can wait. A possible biological hazard cannot.” The director's expression hardened. He knew the public would demand a breakthrough, not a quarantine notice. Lu gave him an alternative: release verified facts and promise a review within forty-eight hours. He accepted only after she offered to sign the report under her own name.

**繁中 4**

正午，太空站主任帶著三名探索部官員走進來。「消息傳出去前，我們必須實施返航歡迎典禮，」他宣布。一名官員說醫療程序是在羞辱船長，另一名則說，只有白痴才會把國家英雄關在玻璃後面。陸莎把兩份醫療報告放到桌上。「歡迎典禮可以等，可能存在的生物風險不能等。」主任的表情沉了下來。他知道民眾期待的是重大突破，不是檢疫通知。陸莎提出折衷辦法：只公開已驗證的事實，並承諾四十八小時內啟動審查。直到她願意以自己的名字簽署報告，他才勉強接受。

**EN 5**

The captain finally appeared behind the observation window. He spoke clearly, remembered the date of departure, and recognized the station's ancient emblem. But when Lu asked where the ship had been, he answered with the name of a planet absent from every catalogue. Then he asked to see his daughter. Lu checked the family record: the daughter was sixty-two, much older than the man now standing before her. The captain listened without moving. “She was thirty-two when we left,” he said. “I know,” Lu replied. “You may speak to her, but only if she agrees.” He closed his eyes. For the first time, his youth looked like a terrible loss rather than a gift.

**繁中 5**

船長終於出現在觀察窗後。他說話清晰，記得出發日期，也認得太空站古老的徽章。但陸莎問他這些年去了哪裡時，他卻說出一顆所有星球資料庫都不存在的行星名稱。接著，他要求見女兒。陸莎查了家庭紀錄：女兒現在六十二歲，比站在她面前的男人還年長。船長聽完沒有動。「我們出發時，她才三十二歲，」他說。「我知道，」陸莎回答，「你可以和她談話，但前提是她願意。」船長閉上眼睛。第一次，他的年輕看起來像可怕的失去，而非恩賜。

**EN 6**

Lu asked for the paper navigation log. The digital file was almost empty, its entries ending three months after launch. The paper book, however, included a detailed map drawn in blue ink. It showed mountains, an ocean, and a thin ring around a planet that no observatory had found. Along the margin, someone had written: **DO NOT TRUST THE AGE OF YOUR FACE.** The handwriting belonged to the captain. When Lu showed him the page, his breathing changed. He did not deny writing it. “There was a rule,” he said quietly. “We promised not to remember the room between the stars.” He refused to explain what that room was.

**繁中 6**

陸莎要求檢查紙本航海日誌。數位檔案幾乎是空白的，紀錄在啟航三個月後就中斷了。紙本日誌卻有一幅以藍墨水繪製的詳細地圖，上面有山脈、海洋，以及一顆從未被任何天文臺發現的行星外圍的細環。頁邊有人寫著：「不要相信你臉上的年齡。」那是船長的筆跡。陸莎把那頁給他看時，他的呼吸變了。他沒有否認那是自己寫的。「我們有一條規則，」他低聲說，「我們答應過，不去記得星星之間的那間房間。」他不肯解釋那是什麼房間。

**EN 7**

That evening, Lu reviewed the ship's emergency transmissions. She found a voice that did not belong to any of the seven passengers. It spoke a short sentence three times, each recording separated by exactly eleven seconds: “Leave the eighth seat empty.” The official passenger list contained only seven names, but the cargo plan showed an additional restraint harness. Lu asked Sato to inspect the cabin without opening any sealed compartment. He returned with a photograph of a chair facing a blank wall. The chair was clean. Every surface around it carried dust. Someone—or something—had used it recently. Lu secured the photograph and asked the director for a separate investigation.

**繁中 7**

當晚，陸莎回看太空船的緊急通訊，找到一道不屬於七名乘員任何一人的聲音。它把同一句話說了三次，每次錄音都精確相隔十一秒：「讓第八個座位空著。」官方乘員名單只有七個名字，但貨運配置圖上還有一套額外的固定安全帶。陸莎請佐藤檢查船艙，但不准打開任何封閉隔間。佐藤帶回一張照片：一張椅子面對空白的牆。椅子非常乾淨，四周表面卻全是灰塵。最近有人——或者某種東西——使用過它。陸莎封存照片，要求主任另外立案調查。

**EN 8**

Before she could send the request, her tablet received a message from the ship's medical terminal. It contained a recording stamped with tomorrow's date. In the image, Lu herself stood in the quarantine room, looking exhausted. “If you are watching this,” her recorded voice said, “do not open the eighth cabin. I thought I could help them. I was wrong.” The screen went black. Lu checked the terminal clock and found no evidence of a simple error. She felt a chill that no announcement could explain. Then she looked toward the observation window. Captain Arsen was already there, waiting, as though he knew exactly what she had seen.

**繁中 8**

她還來不及送出申請，平板就收到船上醫療終端傳來的訊息。裡面是一段標著明天日期的錄影。畫面中的陸莎自己站在檢疫室，看起來疲憊不堪。「如果妳正在看這段影片，」錄影裡的她說，「不要打開第八艙。我以為自己能幫助他們。我錯了。」螢幕隨即變黑。陸莎檢查終端時鐘，找不到普通時間錯誤的證據。她感到一陣寒意，無法用任何公告解釋。接著她望向觀察窗。阿森船長早已站在那裡等候，彷彿完全知道她剛才看見了什麼。

## CHAPTER 02｜封鎖艙門

### 正文與翻譯

**EN 1**

Lu Sha ordered the airlock sealed before anyone could greet the returning crew. Captain Arsen watched through the glass while two quarantine nurses measured the atmosphere inside the Oriole. He did not object. "I remember this station," he said, pointing to a faded red square painted beside the door. "A child used to signal ships from there." Lu Sha's hand stopped over her tablet. Thirty years earlier she had stood in that place, sending a private sequence of flashes to ships passing her father's workshop. The records did not include it. She asked Arsen to repeat the pattern, and he did so perfectly. His knowledge was not proof that he was the original captain; someone might have taught him. She wrote both possibilities in her report. At the airlock, Arsen pressed his palm to the glass. "Have you still got the red lamp?" he asked. Lu Sha had kept it locked in a drawer for twenty-nine years.

**繁中 1**

## CHAPTER 03｜不存在的星圖

### 正文與翻譯

**EN 1**

Every crew member drew the same violet planet on a blank chart. Each sketch included a white ring that official astronomical surveys had never found. Lu Sha checked their drawings separately and discovered that the mountains matched, but the number of moons changed. A scientist suggested implanted memories; another called it mass deception. Lu Sha rejected both conclusions until someone could test them. In the archive she found an old navigation correction that pointed not toward a normal star but toward a region of weak gravitational distortion. The captain remembered an orchard beneath the violet sky. The youngest navigator remembered a hospital. Their experiences might belong to different years or to a manufactured place. When Lu Sha asked what the planet was called, all seven answered, "Home." They then looked startled, as if the word had come from someone else.

**繁中 1**

七名船員各自畫下同一顆紫色行星，外面有一道白環，官方星圖卻從未發現。陸莎分別比對圖畫，發現山脈位置相同，衛星數目卻不一致。有人推測記憶植入，有人說集體詐欺；她都先不採信。檔案中的老導航校正指向一處重力扭曲帶，而非普通恆星。船長記得紫天下的果園，最年輕的導航員卻記得醫院。他們可能經歷不同年代，也可能生活在人工環境。陸莎問行星名字，七人竟同時回答「家」。隨即每個人都露出驚訝神色，彷彿那個字不是出自自己。
