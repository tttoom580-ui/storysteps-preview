# 4B-13｜冰場上的死對頭，是我唯一的雙人搭檔

*My Rival Is My Only Partner on Ice*

第四批・第4級（3000–4000詞級）｜運動戀愛｜全25章雙語教學初稿。

主角：宋梨（花式滑冰選手）；重要角色：顧安（雙人滑選手）。

**編輯註：**故事事件與結局為本次獨立設計，配詞融入與敘事中間段採規則化擴寫；這是完整章節的教學初稿，尚未經人工文學潤稿。

## CHAPTER 01｜Thirty Days to the Final｜決賽倒數三十天

### 正文與翻譯

**EN 01**

Song Li had thirty days left before the national final and one shoulder that still hurt when she pulled on a coat. The club director tried to convey good news with the bright smile of someone delivering a prize: she could return as a pairs skater, provided she partnered with Gu An. Li knew the name before he said it. Gu An was the rival who had corrected her footwork in front of cameras three winters ago and never apologized. Now he stood beside the rink's glass wall, looking toward a cottage on the frozen shore where the team rented rooms during competitions. 'You can say no,' he told her before the director could speak again. That was the first unexpected thing he had ever said to her.

**繁中 01**

距離全國決賽只剩三十天，宋梨的肩膀連穿外套都還會痛。俱樂部主管帶著彷彿送禮的笑容傳來好消息：她可以改當雙人滑選手復出，條件是與顧安搭檔。主管還沒說完，她就知道名字。三年前顧安在鏡頭前糾正她的步法，從沒道歉。現在他站在冰場玻璃牆旁，望著結冰湖岸的小屋，那裡是隊伍比賽期間租住的地方。「妳可以拒絕。」他在主管再次開口前說。這是顧安對她說過的第一句出乎意料的話。

**EN 02**

Li set her bag on the cushion of an empty bench and pulled out the medical report. The director's defense of his plan was simple: pairs skating required diversity of skills, and Gu An had the strength to support her. It was almost convincing until she asked whether he had read the doctor's restrictions. He hadn't. Gu An came over and read the report without reaching for it until Li handed it to him. 'I can't lift my arm above this angle,' she said. He looked at the diagram, then at her. 'Then the program shouldn't ask you to.' For three winters she had rehearsed arguments against him. None of them answered a man who had just agreed with her.

**繁中 02**

宋梨把包放在空長椅的軟墊上，拿出醫療報告。主管替安排辯護的理由很簡單：雙人滑需要多元技能，而顧安有力量支撐她。聽來幾乎合理，直到她問主管讀過醫師限制沒有。主管沒讀。顧安走過來，等宋梨親手交出報告才接過去看。「我不能把手抬過這個角度。」她說。他看過圖示，再望向她：「那節目就不該要求妳那麼做。」三個冬天以來她排練過無數句反駁他的話，卻沒有一句能應付一個剛剛同意她的人。

**EN 03**

The rivalry had always been fierce. At junior nationals Gu An beat Li by less than a point, then described her skating as 'promising' in an interview. She thought he had tried to flatter her because he believed he was superior. He told her now that the reporter had asked him to criticize her. 'I said you were good at the one thing I couldn't do,' he explained. Li did not forgive him at once. 'You're a technical genius,' she said, almost making the words sound like an insult. 'Surely you knew how that sentence would be printed.' His eyes dropped. 'I should have checked.' It was a small admission. After years of dramatic arguments, she found she did not know where to put it.

**繁中 03**

兩人的競爭向來激烈。青年全國賽時，顧安以不到一分的差距贏了宋梨，卻在訪問中說她「很有潛力」。她一直以為他奉承她，是因為自認更強。他現在解釋，記者當時要他批評宋梨。「我說妳最擅長的正是我做不到的事。」宋梨沒有立刻原諒。「你是技術天才，」她說，幾乎把稱讚說成侮辱，「應該知道那句話印出來會變成什麼。」他垂下眼睛：「我該去確認的。」這只是小小承認。多年來習慣戲劇化吵架的她，竟不知道該把這句話放在哪裡。

**EN 04**

The youngest skater nearby tried to hide a giggle when the director announced their first practice would begin with a publicity pose. Li ignored him and asked for the off-ice training plan. It included a resistance parachute workout for speed development, marked safe for athletes with healthy shoulders. Hers was not healthy. Gu An took the sheet to the rehabilitation coach instead of making a pessimistic joke about her chances. The coach crossed out the exercise and replaced it with controlled balance drills. Li tested one with her feet on the floor. The movement was simple and rather dull. Yet it did not hurt. She had almost forgotten how valuable a painless movement could feel.

**繁中 04**

旁邊最年輕的選手聽見主管宣布先拍宣傳姿勢，忍不住偷笑。宋梨沒理他，改要求看陸上訓練計畫。課表包含利用阻力降落傘加速的體能訓練，標示適用於肩膀健康的選手；她的肩膀並不健康。顧安沒有悲觀嘲諷她的機會，而是把表單拿給復健教練。教練刪去那項練習，換成可控的平衡訓練。宋梨雙腳踩地試了一次，動作簡單，甚至有些無聊，卻完全不痛。她幾乎忘記無痛完成一個動作能有多珍貴。

**EN 05**

The director liked to say that the club could possess the world's best training resources. Li was not interested in slogans. At the afternoon presentation she asked for a reference to the actual medical clearance process, the name of the official authorized to stop a lift, and a written procedure for replacing an injured athlete without punishment. The room grew quiet. Gu An raised his hand before the director could dismiss the questions. 'Add the coach's authority too,' he said. 'I won't hold a partner above the ice if the person responsible for safety says no.' Li realized he had just given up a chance to impress a room full of sponsors. He was studying the ice instead of their faces.

**繁中 05**

主管總說俱樂部擁有世界級訓練資源。宋梨不關心口號。下午簡報時，她要求提供實際醫療許可程序的參考資料、誰有權停止托舉的姓名，以及選手受傷時能不受懲罰更換搭檔的書面流程。房間安靜下來。主管還沒敷衍，顧安先舉手：「也要加上教練的權限。如果負責安全的人說不行，我不會把搭檔舉到冰面上方。」宋梨發現他剛放棄了在滿屋贊助商面前表現自己的機會。他看的是冰面，而不是眾人的表情。

**EN 06**

Their old coach wanted the performance to feel spiritual, as though perfect trust could be reached through a beautiful song. Li knew trust could also be a tremendous burden when one body carried another's weight. The real violation would be pretending her shoulder was healed because a marketing story required it. She told Gu An about a bad landing three winters earlier, one she had never described in public because she feared being called weak. He listened to the end. 'I can't promise we'll win,' he replied. 'I can promise I'll hear you when you say stop.' Li took one cautious step onto the ice. For the first time, that sounded like a promise worth testing.

**繁中 06**

舊教練想讓節目帶點精神性的神聖感，彷彿美麗音樂能讓人達到完美信任。宋梨卻知道，當一個人的身體必須承受另一個人的重量時，信任也可能成為巨大的負擔。真正的違規不是輸掉分數，而是為了行銷故事假裝她的肩膀已經痊癒。她對顧安說起三年前那次糟糕落地，那是她一直不敢公開、怕被說軟弱的經歷。他聽完才回答：「我不能保證我們會贏，但可以保證妳說停的時候我會聽見。」宋梨小心踏上冰面。第一次，她覺得這是值得測試的承諾。

**EN 07**

The practice board offered a visual plan of thirty days, each week crowded with lifts, jumps, and a workout schedule that did not acknowledge her injury. Li asked the physiotherapist to witness her refusal to attempt the first overhead throw. Gu An moved the marker to a low, supported lift without pretending it would earn the same points. 'That's not a romantic moment,' Li said when the director raised his phone. 'It's a training correction.' Gu An almost smiled. 'Then let them photograph the correction.' Through the glass Li saw two junior skaters copying the revised movement. She felt the weight of their watching and, for the first time that morning, the possibility that she and her former enemy might create something worth following.

**繁中 07**

訓練板上的視覺時程排滿三十天，托舉、跳躍與體能訓練課表完全沒考慮她的傷勢。宋梨請物理治療師見證自己拒做第一個過頭拋跳。顧安把標記移到有支撐的低托舉，沒有假裝分數不會變。「這不是什麼浪漫瞬間。」主管舉起手機時宋梨說，「這是訓練修正。」顧安差點笑了。「那就讓他們拍修正吧。」玻璃外兩名年輕選手模仿修改後的動作。宋梨感受到被注視的重量，也第一次覺得，也許她能與這位舊對手合作出值得學習的事。

### 理解測驗

**Q1. Which development is established in Chapter 1?**

A. The investigators decide that every earlier warning was fictional.

B. After a shoulder injury ended Song Li's singles season, the club offered her a place in a new pairs program with Gu An, the skater she had argued with for years.

C. The central problem disappears without further action.

**答案：B。** 肩傷讓宋梨的單人賽季提前結束，俱樂部卻提出要她與多年來的死對頭顧安組成雙人滑。

**Q2. What new complication or decision changes the investigation in this chapter?**

A. All the witnesses agree to destroy their records.

B. The team abandons the people affected and declares a perfect victory.

C. Li refused the club's assumption that an athlete must accept any partner and demanded a medical assessment before a trial.

**答案：C。** 宋梨拒絕俱樂部替她決定搭檔，要求先完成醫療評估才試滑。


## CHAPTER 02｜The First Safe Lift｜第一次安全托舉

### 正文與翻譯

**EN 01**

Their first practice stopped before the opening lift because Li's shoulder could not bear the planned angle; Gu An did not try to force it. Something had changed, but not everybody noticed it in the same order. Song Li listened to the account of the nearest worker, then to someone standing farther away. Their differences were not necessarily lies. Each person had been given a different view and a different reason to be afraid of admitting uncertainty. A score sheet could tell the judges what happened in a program, not why two people had chosen to stand on the ice together. Song Li kept those questions separate. The term atom appeared in the revised file; Song Li checked its meaning against the original page. One witness used the word atomic; Gu An noted that a description was not a measurement.

**繁中 01**

第一次練習在托舉前就停下，因為宋梨的肩膀無法承受預定角度，顧安沒有勉強。某件事改變了，但人們發現它的順序不同。宋梨先聽近處工作人員的描述，再聽遠處旁觀者說法。差異不一定是謊言：每個人看到的角度不同，承認不確定時所要承擔的風險也不同。分數表可以告訴裁判節目發生了什麼，卻說不清兩個人為何選擇一起站上冰面。宋梨把兩個問題分開。修訂檔案出現「原子」一詞，宋梨拿原始頁面核對它的意思。一位證人用了「原子能的」一詞，顧安指出形容並不等於測量。

**EN 02**

Li refused the club's assumption that an athlete must accept any partner and demanded a medical assessment before a trial. Song Li refused to pretend the previous decision had solved everything. The practical result was still there, useful but narrow. For each thing the team knew, there were two things it had not measured. Song Li drew a line through the claims that depended on speculation and asked Gu An to keep the original record intact. A handwritten label read combination, but no one could say who had written it there. The summary called the situation delicate; Song Li asked which observation justified that description. Gu An noticed the word extent in a note that someone had added after the first inspection.

**繁中 02**

宋梨拒絕俱樂部替她決定搭檔，要求先完成醫療評估才試滑。宋梨不願假裝前一次決定已解決所有問題。成果確實存在，卻有明確界限。每個已知事項旁都還有未測量的部分。宋梨劃掉靠猜測成立的說法，請顧安保留原始紀錄。手寫標籤寫著「組合」，卻沒有人知道是誰留下的。摘要以「微弱的」形容情況，宋梨追問這種說法有何觀察根據。顧安注意到初次檢查後有人在筆記裡加上「程度」一詞。

**EN 03**

A small disagreement changed the schedule. Song Li wanted to proceed before evidence disappeared; Gu An wanted another check because a wrong conclusion might hurt people who could not easily defend themselves. They did not settle the matter by choosing a leader. They wrote down what a short, reversible test could actually establish. Gu An pointed to the fort in the inventory and asked why its location had changed. The earlier photograph clearly showed a fossil; the newer version did not, so Song Li kept both. The term gaze appeared in the revised file; Song Li checked its meaning against the original page.

**繁中 03**

一場小爭執改變了時程。宋梨想趁證據消失前行動；顧安要求再核對一次，因為錯誤結論可能傷害難以自辯的人。他們沒有靠選領袖解決爭議，而是列出短暫、可逆測試真正能證明什麼。顧安指向清單上的「堡壘」，追問其位置為何改變。較早的照片清楚顯示一項「化石」，新版卻沒有，宋梨因此保留兩張。修訂檔案出現「凝視」一詞，宋梨拿原始頁面核對它的意思。

**EN 04**

They designed a sequence that prioritized footwork and balance, giving their rivalry a technical problem both could solve. The plan had been amended three times, and each change left a trace. Song Li valued those corrections more than a perfect-looking first draft. If the outcome proved disappointing, the group would still be able to identify which assumption needed repair. Gu An watched for the quiet participant who had not yet been invited to speak. The term guarantee appeared in the revised file; Song Li checked its meaning against the original page. Gu An noticed the word immigration in a note that someone had added after the first inspection. A handwritten label read intimate, but no one could say who had written it there.

**繁中 04**

兩人把編排改成重視步法與平衡，讓競爭暫時變成能共同解決的技術題。計畫改了三次，每次修改都留下痕跡。宋梨認為這些修正比看似完美的初稿更有價值。就算結果不理想，團隊仍能知道該修正哪一項假設。顧安也注意到那名一直沒有發言機會的人。修訂檔案出現「保證」一詞，宋梨拿原始頁面核對它的意思。顧安注意到初次檢查後有人在筆記裡加上「入出境」一詞。手寫標籤寫著「私密的」，卻沒有人知道是誰留下的。

**EN 05**

An official version reached the room before the practical work was finished. Song Li read it once, then compared it with what they had actually measured. The difference was not always malicious, but it could still hurt somebody. Gu An asked that the correction be written in language the affected people could understand. A score sheet could tell the judges what happened in a program, not why two people had chosen to stand on the ice together. Song Li kept those questions separate. The earlier photograph clearly showed a landscape; the newer version did not, so Song Li kept both. A note about the messenger lay beside the old register, with a date that Song Li could not verify. Gu An pointed to the mill in the inventory and asked why its location had changed.

**繁中 05**

實際工作還沒完成，官方版本就傳到現場。宋梨讀了一次，再比對真正測得的結果。差異未必都是惡意，卻仍可能傷害人。顧安要求把更正寫成受影響者看得懂的語言。分數表可以告訴裁判節目發生了什麼，卻說不清兩個人為何選擇一起站上冰面。宋梨把兩個問題分開。較早的照片清楚顯示一項「風景布景」，新版卻沒有，宋梨因此保留兩張。一張談到「送件員」的便條放在舊登記簿旁，日期讓宋梨無法核實。顧安指向清單上的「磨坊」，追問其位置為何改變。

**EN 06**

A witness asked whether the team was taking sides. Song Li answered that refusing to invent a fact was not the same as refusing to care. What mattered was whether the procedure protected someone whose story was less convenient to tell. Gu An listened without interrupting and wrote down the question exactly as it had been asked. On the next form, the action field read murmur, but the witness had never approved the operation. An official memo described the plan as productive, but Song Li refused to treat an adjective as evidence. The summary called the situation psychological; Song Li asked which observation justified that description.

**繁中 06**

一名見證人問團隊是否偏袒。宋梨回答，不捏造事實不代表冷漠。真正重要的是程序能否保護那個故事不方便被說出口的人。顧安沒有打斷，逐字記下原問題。下一張表單把動作寫成「低語」，見證人卻從未同意這項操作。官方備忘錄形容計畫「有成果的」，宋梨不願把形容詞當作證據。摘要以「心理上的」形容情況，宋梨追問這種說法有何觀察根據。

**EN 07**

Even after the group agreed on the immediate step, another question remained. The answer would not arrive through a dramatic announcement. Someone would have to return to the place, compare the earlier notes, and accept the possibility that the next piece of evidence might spoil an attractive theory. Song Li prepared to do exactly that. Gu An checked the lift against the rehabilitation plan, not the sponsor's desired photograph. Trust grew from stopping safely as often as from moving together. The instructions used the verb retain without naming who would carry the risk; Song Li circled that gap. On the next form, the action field read revise, but the witness had never approved the operation. A handwritten label read voyage, but no one could say who had written it there.

**繁中 07**

即使大家同意眼前步驟，另一個問題仍在。答案不會靠戲劇性公告降臨；有人得返回現場、比對舊筆記，並接受新證據可能推翻漂亮理論。宋梨準備做的正是這件事。顧安依復健計畫檢查托舉，而非照贊助商想要的照片排動作。信任不只來自同步前進，也來自安全喊停。指示裡用了「保留」這個動詞，卻沒說誰承擔風險；宋梨圈出這項缺漏。下一張表單把動作寫成「修改」，見證人卻從未同意這項操作。手寫標籤寫著「航程」，卻沒有人知道是誰留下的。
