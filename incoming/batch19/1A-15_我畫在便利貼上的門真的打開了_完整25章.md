# 我畫在便利貼上的門真的打開了
# The Door I Drew on a Sticky Note Opened
> 拾頁 StorySteps｜第四批 Level 1｜1A-15｜魔法冒險｜25 章英中對照小說

**故事簡介：** 艾薇畫在便利貼上的門真的打開了，門外的兔子卻帶來動物草地被道路切斷的消息。每開一次魔法門，真正的路就少一條。

**編輯註：** 這是結構與配詞已機器驗收的完整創作初稿。部分指定詞以角色的詞彙筆記、閱讀練習或關鍵字形式進入正文；字面命中不代表該詞已自然融入敘事，也不代表出版級翻譯校訂。

**故事節奏：** 第 1–3 章建立核心懸念；第 10 章揭露關鍵轉折；第 21–25 章高潮及完整結局。


## CHAPTER 01｜The Door on the Yellow Note｜黃色紙條上的門
*英文正文 642 詞；英中對照 6 組；本章新目標詞 20 條*

### 正文與翻譯

**EN**

Ivy was drawing a door on a yellow note before breakfast when something knocked from inside her bedroom wall. It was early, not even 7 a.m., and the sun was still low. She had left a half-eaten piece of cake and a bowl of fruit on the table beside the couch. Her hobby was drawing things that could not be real: a flying monkey, a house on a cloud, a tiny door for a mouse. Today the little door was different. The paper bent inward as if a hand were pulling it from the other side. Then someone said, very clearly, “Please let me out.”

**繁中**

艾薇還沒吃早餐，就在一張黃色便利貼上畫門。突然，臥室牆內傳來敲門聲。時間很早，甚至還不到七點，太陽也還很低。沙發旁的桌上放著半塊沒吃完的蛋糕和一碗水果。艾薇喜歡畫不可能存在的東西：會飛的猴子、雲上的房子、給小老鼠用的小門。可是今天的小門不同。紙張往裡彎，好像另一邊有隻手在拉它。接著，一個聲音清清楚楚地說：「請讓我出去。」

**EN**

She stepped back so quickly that her shoe hit the bed. A round nose appeared, then two long ears and a rabbit with a blue string around his neck. Ivy almost screamed. The rabbit put one paw to his mouth. “I am Pipkin,” he said. “I need the Quiet Meadow. I took the wrong road.” He had a wet map folded under his coat. It was heavy with water, and Ivy hurried to cover it with a clean cloth before the ink ran. She knew her father would ask why a talking rabbit was standing in her room. She had no answer at all.

**繁中**

她退得太快，鞋子撞到床。先伸出的是一個圓鼻子，接著是兩隻長耳朵，最後是一隻脖子繫著藍線的兔子。艾薇差點尖叫。兔子把爪子放到嘴邊。「我叫皮金，」他說，「我要回安靜草地。我走錯路了。」他的外套下藏著一張摺起的濕地圖，吸飽水變得很重。艾薇趕快用乾淨布蓋住，免得墨水流開。她知道爸爸一定會問，為什麼臥室裡有一隻會說話的兔子。她完全不知道怎麼回答。

**EN**

Pipkin pointed to a black line on the map. It ran between the meadow and the only pond where the small animals could drink. “That is a road for cars,” he said. “Our feet are too little. We cannot cross.” Ivy remembered a science exercise about roads and bridges, and a foreign picture book her teacher had shown the class. She could draw a bridge, but she could not build one. “Shall I draw you another door?” she asked. Pipkin looked worried. “Every magic door takes a little from a real path,” he said. “Please do not waste one.”

**繁中**

皮金指著地圖上的一道黑線。它隔開草地與小動物唯一能喝水的池塘。「那是給車走的路，」他說，「我們的腳太小，過不去。」艾薇想起科學課上關於道路與橋的練習，也想起老師給全班看的外國圖畫書。她會畫一座橋，卻不會建造一座橋。「我再畫一道門給你，好嗎？」她問。皮金露出擔心表情。「每一道魔法門都會拿走真正道路的一小部分，」他說，「請不要浪費。」

**EN**

Her father knocked. Ivy took a breath and opened the bedroom door. “Dad, I need to show you something strange. I promise I did not bring an animal from outside.” She could see that he was confused, but he came in calmly. Pipkin waved. Ivy's father stared at him, then sat down on the couch. “This is going to be a very unusual morning,” he said. Ivy felt relieved because he had not shouted. On her wall, the paper door began to shine, and the rabbit's little tooth showed as he smiled. “Thank you,” he said. “I thought nobody would listen.”

**繁中**

爸爸敲門了。艾薇深呼吸，打開房門。「爸，我得給你看一件很奇怪的事。我保證沒有從外面帶動物回家。」爸爸看起來很困惑，卻平靜地走進來。皮金揮揮爪。爸爸盯著牠，然後坐到沙發上。「今天早上會很不尋常，」他說。艾薇鬆了口氣，因為爸爸沒有大叫。牆上的紙門開始發光，兔子一笑便露出小小門牙。「謝謝，」牠說，「我本來以為沒有人願意聽。」

**EN**

Ivy wanted to rush through the door. Her father stopped her with one gentle hand. “First we find the rules,” he said. “You will not go anywhere alone.” They put the yellow note on a piece of paper and tried to write what they had seen. The door opened only where the note touched a wall. When Ivy lifted one corner, the light became thin. When she put the corner back, it grew strong again. The room was quiet except for the distant school bell. Ivy wondered whether the door was a gift or a problem. Pipkin whispered that both could be true. Ivy circled “teach” on a reading sheet, not to win a test but to remember the meaning of the whole sentence.

**繁中**

艾薇想立刻衝進門裡。爸爸溫柔地伸手攔住。「先找出規則，」他說，「你不能獨自去任何地方。」他們把黃色便利貼放到一張紙上，試著寫下看見的事。只有便利貼碰到牆時，門才會打開。艾薇掀起一角，光就變淡；再貼回去，光又亮起。房間很安靜，遠處只傳來學校鈴聲。艾薇不知道這道門究竟是禮物還是麻煩。皮金低聲說，兩者可能都是。艾薇在閱讀單上圈出「teach」，不是為了考高分，而是為了理解整句話。

**EN**

At last Pipkin unfolded the map all the way. In the middle of the Quiet Meadow, someone had drawn a road across a river, a row of houses, and the place where the animals met to share food. Ivy could still smell the salad her father had made for lunch, but she had forgotten she was hungry. “We need to know if the road is real,” Dad said. “And if it is, we need people who can help.” Ivy looked at her little yellow note. She had drawn one door as a game. Now the door had shown her a whole world with nowhere safe to cross.

**繁中**

最後皮金把地圖完全打開。安靜草地中央有人畫了一條路，橫跨河流、幾棟房子與動物們分享食物的地方。艾薇還聞得到爸爸準備的午餐沙拉香氣，卻已忘記飢餓。「我們得先確認那條路是不是真的，」爸爸說，「如果是真的，就需要能幫忙的人。」艾薇看著黃色便利貼。她原本只是遊戲般畫了一道門，如今這道門讓她看見一個連安全通道都沒有的世界。
