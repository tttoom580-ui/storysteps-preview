## CHAPTER 01｜A Bouquet Without Papers｜沒有證件的花束

### 正文與翻譯

**EN 1**

At nine in the morning, a former Demon King entered the Immigration Office carrying a batch of blue flowers and a numbered ticket. His polished horns shone; his tail curled beneath his chair. Mira Sen, the officer behind Window Four, checked his application. The name said Urian of the Nine Ash Kingdoms. Under “occupation,” he had written: **FLORIST.** Under “species,” he had written: **RETIRED DEMON LORD.** Mira studied the neat letters. “You know this form is for a human residence permit?” she asked. “I know,” Urian replied. “I have lived in this city for twenty years.”

**繁中 1**

早上九點，一名前任魔王抱著一批藍色花朵，拿著號碼牌走進移民局。雙角擦得光亮，尾巴則蜷在椅子下。第四號窗口後的移民官米拉・森檢查申請表。姓名欄寫著「九燼王國的烏利安」，職業欄填的是「花店老闆」，物種欄則寫著「退休魔王」。米拉端詳工整的字跡。「你知道這份表格是申請人類居留證的吧？」她問。「我知道，」烏利安回答，「我在這座城市已經住了二十年。」

**EN 2**

The office had recently adopted a new classification regime. Everyone without a recognized species code was considered temporary, regardless of how long they had lived there. Mira thought the rule absurd, but she was required to implement it until a court said otherwise. Her mentor had warned against turning inconvenience into cruelty. She opened an inquiry file instead of pressing the rejection button. “Do you have proof of employment?” Urian produced a market contract, a decent set of tax records, and photographs of a small flower shop. He also produced a receipt from a nonprofit shelter that he had supported for twelve years. None of the documents contained a human species code.

**繁中 2**

移民局最近採用了新的分類制度。凡是沒有認可物種代碼的人，不論住了多久，都被視為臨時居留者。米拉認為這條規定荒謬，卻仍必須依法執行，直到法院作出不同判決。她的導師曾警告，不能把行政不便變成殘酷待遇。於是她開立調查檔案，而不是直接按下拒絕鍵。「你有工作證明嗎？」烏利安拿出市場契約、完整合格的納稅紀錄，以及一間小花店的照片。他還拿出一間非營利收容所的收據，證明自己已支持那裡十二年。但沒有任何一份文件包含人類物種代碼。

**EN 3**

A child pressed her nose against the glass separating the waiting area from the desks. She pointed at the flowers. “Why are they blue?” Urian smiled. “Because the soil was sad. I gave it something cheerful to think about.” A security guard hurried over. The guard gripped the rim of his alarm device, as though Urian might terrify everyone by breathing. Mira asked the child to return to her mother and told the guard to step back. Urian placed one flower on her counter. Its petals began to dissolve into tiny, harmless sparks. “Sorry,” he said. “They don't last away from the garden. I should have brought ordinary roses.”

**繁中 3**

一名孩子把鼻子貼在候位區與辦公桌之間的玻璃上，指著花問：「為什麼它們是藍色的？」烏利安微笑。「因為土壤很難過，我給它一點開心的事情想。」保全急忙走過來，握著警報器邊緣，彷彿烏利安光靠呼吸就能嚇壞所有人。米拉請孩子回到母親身邊，也讓保全退開。烏利安在櫃檯放下一朵花。花瓣漸漸化成無害的小火花。「抱歉，」他說，「離開花園，它們就活不久。我應該帶普通玫瑰來。」

**EN 4**

Mira took the shop photographs from him. A saddle had been fitted to a small delivery bicycle, and two apprentices were arranging pots along the sidewalk. The placement of the shop's sign complied with every street rule. Even the old electrical circuit had been inspected. The only irregularity was its owner's legal identity. “Your business projection is excellent,” she said. “If your customers triple, you may need another delivery worker.” Urian looked surprised. “You read the last page?” “I read applications,” Mira answered. “That's what the office pays me to do.” 

**繁中 4**

米拉接過花店照片。一輛小送貨腳踏車裝著鞍形座，兩名學徒正在人行道旁排列盆栽。店招牌的設置完全符合街道規範，連老舊電力迴路都已通過檢查。唯一的不合規之處，是店主的法律身分。「你的營運預測很不錯，」她說，「如果顧客增加三倍，也許需要再請一名送貨員。」烏利安很驚訝。「妳讀了最後一頁？」「我會讀申請書，」米拉回答，「這就是這個辦公室付薪水請我做的事。」

**EN 5**

The department supervisor approached when he saw the species line. “Don't waste time,” he said. “Only an idiot would approve this.” Mira showed him the documents. “I haven't approved anything. I'm checking whether the residence law actually requires the applicant to be human.” The supervisor said the public would demand a quick removal. He warned her that the case might strain the office's reputation. Mira opened the original legal text. The statute spoke of a safe home, lawful work, and responsibility for past harm; it never defined a person by blood or horns. The new form had added a category that was not in the law. It was not yet a breakthrough, but it was a question worth taking seriously.

**繁中 5**

主管看見物種欄，便走了過來。「別浪費時間，」他說，「只有白痴才會批准這種申請。」米拉把文件拿給他看。「我還沒批准任何事。我正在確認居留法是否真的要求申請人必須是人類。」主管說，民眾會要求盡快驅逐。他警告，這件案子可能讓移民局聲譽承受壓力。米拉打開法律原文。法條談的是安全住所、合法工作，以及對過往傷害的責任，從未用血統或犄角定義一個人。新的申請表卻加上了法律裡不存在的分類。這還算不上突破，但已是值得認真追究的問題。

**EN 6**

Urian watched the exchange silently. Then he reached into his coat and placed a folded paper beside the flowers. It was a list of names, written by his own hand. “People died during my reign,” he said. “Some of their families live here. I pay into the public fund because I owe them more than an apology.” Mira did not promise forgiveness. She could not give it on anyone's behalf. “The court may still ask you about those debts,” she said. “Residence is not a pardon.” Urian nodded once. Outside the office, a crowd had begun to gather, drawn by rumors that the Demon King had returned. He had not returned to a throne. He was trying to keep his flower shop.

**繁中 6**

烏利安一直安靜看著兩人交談。接著，他從外套裡拿出一張摺好的紙，放在花朵旁邊。上面是他親手寫下的人名。「我統治時，有人死去，」他說，「有些人的家屬現在就住在這裡。我向公共基金捐款，因為我欠他們的遠比一句道歉更多。」米拉沒有承諾寬恕，她無權代替任何人寬恕。「法院仍可能要求你對這些責任作出說明，」她說，「居留不是赦免。」烏利安點了一次頭。移民局外已有群眾聚集，因為傳言說魔王回來了。他並沒有回來奪取王座，只是想保住自己的花店。

**EN 7**

Mira began to write a temporary-review request. The supervisor demanded that she delete it. “No one has ever appealed a species code,” he said. “Then we will learn how,” she answered. She attached the legal text, the market records, and the list of names. She also asked a clerk to reinforce the window barrier so that angry visitors could not reach the waiting families. Her decision protected neither Urian from scrutiny nor the office from complaints. It simply gave everyone time to make an informed argument. When she finished, a message appeared on her screen: **APPLICATION HELD FOR REVIEW. DO NOT REMOVE APPLICANT BEFORE HEARING.** The law, for once, had left a door open.

**繁中 7**

米拉開始寫一份暫緩審查申請。主管要求她刪除。「從沒有人申訴過物種代碼，」他說。「那我們就學著辦，」米拉回答。她附上法律原文、市場紀錄與人名清單，也請書記官加固窗口隔板，避免憤怒的訪客接觸候位家庭。這個決定既不讓烏利安免於調查，也不能讓移民局免於投訴；它只是給所有人一點時間，讓大家能在了解事實後提出主張。她送出文件，螢幕上顯示：「申請暫緩審查。聽證前不得遣離申請人。」法律終於留了一道門縫。

**EN 8**

When Urian stood to leave, he hesitated. “May I bring you flowers from the shop after the hearing?” Mira glanced at the fading blue petals. “Only if you bring receipts,” she said. He laughed, an unexpectedly gentle sound. Then the courthouse messenger arrived with another document: a sealed notice from the Ministry of War. It named Urian as the owner of an army that had supposedly been dissolved twenty years earlier. The notice had been signed yesterday. Mira looked up, but the old Demon King had already stopped smiling. “That army,” he said, “should not exist.” Somewhere outside, the crowd began chanting his ancient title. Mira locked the notice in the inquiry file and called the public defender's office.

**繁中 8**

烏利安起身離開時，猶豫了一下。「聽證結束後，我可以從店裡帶花給妳嗎？」米拉看了看逐漸消失的藍花瓣。「可以，但請帶收據，」她說。他笑了，笑聲意外地溫柔。就在這時，法院信差送來另一份文件：戰爭部的密封通知。通知把烏利安列為一支理應在二十年前解散的軍隊之擁有人，簽署日期卻是昨天。米拉抬起頭，老魔王已經不再微笑。「那支軍隊，」他說，「根本不應該存在。」外面傳來群眾呼喊他古代稱號的聲音。米拉把通知鎖進調查卷宗，撥電話給公設辯護辦公室。

1. **Why did Mira open a review file instead of rejecting Urian's application?**
  - A. She believed the application form might impose a species requirement not found in the law.
  - B. Urian promised to give her a royal title.
  - C. The flower shop had paid no taxes.

  **Answer: A.** 解析：米拉查到法條沒有以物種血統決定居留資格，新表格卻新增分類，所以她要求審查。

  - A. The flower shop was closing for repairs.
  - B. A military organization thought to be dissolved might still be active under Urian's name.
  - C. Urian had been appointed the city's mayor.

  **Answer: B.** 解析：通知把烏利安列為舊軍隊的擁有人，但該軍隊理應已解散二十年。

## CHAPTER 03｜不肯說謊的申請人

### 正文與翻譯

**EN 1**
