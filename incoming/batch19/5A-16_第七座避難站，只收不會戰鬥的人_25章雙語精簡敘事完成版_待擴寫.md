## CHAPTER 01｜No Weapons Beyond This Door｜武器不得越過此門

### 正文與翻譯

**EN 1**

The sign above Shelter Seven was painted on an old wooden door: **NO WEAPONS. NO HEROES. EVERYONE MUST WORK.** Dr. Xia He had written the last sentence herself. She was forty, tired, and more frightened of empty medicine shelves than of the creatures beyond the walls. That morning a batch of refugees arrived from Shelter Six. They carried a wounded man, three small children, and a bundle of rifles. Their leader read the sign and laughed. “Only an idiot refuses soldiers in the middle of the end of the world.” Xia looked at the rifles, then at the children. “I am not refusing people,” she said. “I am refusing loaded weapons inside a crowded medical shelter.”

**繁中 1**

第七避難站的舊木門上，漆著一行字：「禁止攜帶武器。這裡沒有英雄。每個人都必須工作。」最後一句是夏禾醫師親手加上的。她四十歲，疲倦不堪，比起城牆外的怪物，更害怕藥架空空如也。那天早上，一批來自第六避難站的難民抵達，帶著一名傷患、三個幼童，以及一捆步槍。領隊看見告示便笑了。「只有白痴才會在世界末日拒絕士兵。」夏禾先看著步槍，再看看孩子。「我不是拒絕人，」她說，「我拒絕的是有人把上膛的武器帶進擁擠的醫療避難站。」

**EN 2**

The settlement had survived for three years under a strict but public safety regime. Its nonprofit clinic served anyone who agreed to disarm, regardless of their past. The food store, a decent wooden building beside the well, could feed six hundred people for twenty days if supplies remained steady. Xia's mentor had helped design the admission rules after a panic at the second shelter killed more people than the creatures had. The rules were not sacred. The residents could amend them through an open inquiry. But until that process was complete, Xia had to implement them fairly. “You may store your weapons outside under guard,” she told the leader. “Then we will treat your friend.”

**繁中 2**

這座聚落靠著嚴格但公開的安全制度，存活了三年。非營利診所服務所有願意卸下武器的人，無論他們過去做過什麼。井旁那棟堅固合格的木造糧倉，只要補給穩定，足以供六百人吃二十天。夏禾的導師曾在第二避難站發生恐慌後協助制定收容規則；那場恐慌奪走的人命，甚至比怪物還多。規則並非神聖不可改，居民可以透過公開調查與討論修訂。但程序完成之前，夏禾仍必須公平地執行。「你們可以把武器交給門外的守衛保管，」她對領隊說，「然後我們就醫治你的同伴。」

**EN 3**

The leader hesitated. A child beside him coughed, holding a tin cup by its rim. Xia offered water before asking another question. The child's mother took the cup with shaking fingers. Through the doorway, Xia could see farmer Yan Tuo working beside a broken pump. He sat on the saddle of a narrow maintenance platform and tried to reinforce the hose connection. The electrical circuit had failed during the night, leaving the settlement without its usual water pressure. In a nearby basin, lime tablets had begun to dissolve, preparing water for safe storage. No one inside had time for a heroic speech. They needed working pipes, clean hands, and honest information.

**繁中 3**

領隊猶豫了一下。旁邊一個孩子抓著錫杯的杯緣咳嗽。夏禾先遞水，才繼續詢問。孩子的母親雙手發抖地接過杯子。透過門口，夏禾看見農學者燕拓正在壞掉的水泵旁忙碌。他坐在狹窄維修平台的鞍座上，試圖加固水管接頭。昨晚電力迴路故障，使聚落失去平常的水壓。旁邊的水盆裡，石灰錠逐漸溶解，準備處理供儲存的水。沒有人有空發表英雄演說。他們需要的是能運作的水管、乾淨的雙手，以及誠實的資訊。

**EN 4**

Teacher Jon came running from the schoolhouse with a weather projection on his tablet. A storm would reach the valley before nightfall, and the strain on the outer doors might triple. “We can move the children to the cellar,” he said, “but first we need to clear the passage.” The armed leader offered to guard the entrance. Jon pointed to the refugees' wounded companion. “Can you carry him instead?” The man stared at him, then put down his rifle. It was a small decision, not a breakthrough, but two other soldiers followed. Xia ordered a stretcher and began checking the wounded man's breathing. He had lost blood, yet his condition was stable enough to move.

**繁中 4**

教師喬恩拿著平板上的天氣預測，從校舍跑來。暴風雨會在入夜前抵達山谷，外門承受的壓力可能增加三倍。「我們能把孩子移到地窖，」他說，「但得先清出通道。」持槍領隊主動提出守住入口。喬恩卻指向難民隊伍裡的傷患。「你可以先幫忙抬他嗎？」男人盯著他看了片刻，最後放下步槍。這只是個小小的決定，不算什麼突破，但另兩名士兵也跟著照做。夏禾叫來擔架，開始檢查傷患的呼吸。他失了血，但生命徵象尚可，足以接受移動。

**EN 5**

A woman inside the station objected to admitting strangers. “They will take our food,” she said. Xia did not dismiss the fear. The pantry report showed exactly what the danger was: every new arrival reduced the time before the next supply run. “Then we must change the plan, not hide the numbers,” Xia answered. She asked Jon to record the names of people who wanted to speak at the evening meeting. Yan called from the pump that he could restore half the water flow with an ordinary valve. Xia approved the repair but insisted on a second inspection. A settlement built on care could still fail if its leaders pretended to know more than they did.

**繁中 5**

站內一名女子反對收容陌生人。「他們會吃掉我們的糧食，」她說。夏禾沒有否定她的恐懼。糧倉報告清楚呈現危險：每增加一名住民，就會縮短下一次補給前所剩的日子。「那我們就該改變計畫，而不是隱瞞數字，」夏禾回答。她請喬恩記下想在晚間會議發言的人名。燕拓從水泵旁喊來，說只要普通閥門就能恢復一半水流。夏禾批准修理，但堅持要再檢查一次。以照護為基礎的聚落，也會因為領導者假裝自己無所不知而失敗。

**EN 6**

By afternoon, the placement of new beds kept exits clear. The arrivals received water and tasks suited to their strength. The armed leader was named Renn. He was a captain whose hands shook tying a bandage. “I spent twenty years learning to fight,” he admitted. “No one taught me how to help when fighting isn't useful.” Xia showed him how to secure the cloth without cutting circulation. She asked whether he wanted to learn more. He nodded. Outside, the creatures began moving along the ridge. Their shapes were distant, and the old alarms could not determine what had attracted them.

**繁中 6**

到了下午，新床位的配置保留了出口通道。新來的人也得到水和符合體力的工作。持槍領隊名叫雷恩，過去是名上尉，綁繃帶時卻雙手發抖。「我花了二十年學習戰鬥，」他承認，「卻沒有人教我，戰鬥派不上用場時該怎麼幫忙。」夏禾教他如何固定布條，又不會妨礙血液循環。她問他要不要再學更多。雷恩點頭。外頭，怪物開始沿山脊移動。牠們的身影仍在遠方，老舊警報器無法判斷究竟是什麼吸引了牠們。

**EN 7**

Jon climbed onto the observation deck. A child had drawn the station, a garden, and six children beneath a sun. Beside it stood a mark no adult had noticed before—three circles near the northern fence. Jon called Yan, who studied the mark against the latest patrol map. The circles matched three fresh dents in the ground. Something heavy had stopped outside the fence last night, then turned away without attacking. Jon wanted to tell the whole station. Yan asked him to wait until they could inspect the marks safely. Evidence must not become rumor.

**繁中 7**

喬恩爬上觀察台。有個孩子畫了避難站、花園，以及陽光下的六名孩子。畫旁還有個大人先前沒注意到的記號——北側圍欄附近的三個圓。喬恩叫來燕拓，兩人把記號和最新的巡邏地圖比對。三個圓正好對應地面上的三處新凹痕。昨晚有某種沉重的東西停在圍欄外，接著轉身離去，沒有攻擊。喬恩想告訴全站居民，燕拓卻請他先等到能安全勘查痕跡。證據不應變成謠言。

**EN 8**

At sunset, Xia stood before the community board and announced the arrivals, the water failure, and the food count. Then she showed everyone the weather warning. The meeting had barely begun when the eastern gate shook. A guard shouted that something large was outside. Renn reached for a weapon that was no longer on his belt. Xia handed him a medical bag instead. “Bring this to the cellar,” she said. The gate shook again. The sound could terrify a child, but no claws came through. A voice called from the other side, faint and human: “Please. We have children.” Xia looked at the gathered residents. The rule about weapons was clear. The decision about opening the door was not.

**繁中 8**

日落時，夏禾站在社區公告板前，向居民說明新到者、水泵故障與糧食存量，接著展示天氣警報。會議才剛開始，東門便突然震動。守衛大喊，有個巨大的東西在外頭。雷恩伸手去摸腰間早已不在的武器。夏禾卻遞給他一個醫療包。「把這個送去地窖，」她說。大門再次搖晃，聲音足以把孩子嚇壞，卻沒有利爪穿進來。另一側傳出微弱的人聲：「拜託，我們有孩子。」夏禾看著聚集的居民。禁止武器的規則很明確；但要不要開門，卻不是。

## CHAPTER 04｜誰保護孩子

### 正文與翻譯
