# 4B-16｜每晚九點，兇案現場會提前直播

*At Nine, Tomorrow's Crime Goes Live*

第四批・第4級（3000–4000詞級）｜網路犯罪｜全25章雙語教學初稿。

主角：聞怡（刑警）；重要角色：韓律（數位鑑識員）。

**編輯註：**故事事件與結局為本次獨立設計，配詞融入與敘事中間段採規則化擴寫；這是完整章節的教學初稿，尚未經人工文學潤稿。

## CHAPTER 01｜Nine O'Clock, Tomorrow's Murder｜九點，明天的謀殺

### 正文與翻譯

**EN 01**

At nine o'clock precisely, a murder happened tomorrow. Detective Wen Yi watched the impossible livestream on an evidence-room computer while the city's emergency calls remained quiet. The image showed a small cottage on the outskirts, a body beside its rear door, and a timestamp dated the next evening. The channel title promised to convey the future before the police could stop it. Wen did not believe titles. She copied the address, asked Han Lu to preserve the stream, and drove to the cottage. Its porch light was on; its owner answered alive, irritated at being disturbed. But on the floor stood the same blue cup from the video. It had a fresh chip along the rim. The owner looked down at it and whispered, 'That wasn't broken an hour ago.'

**繁中 01**

九點整，一樁謀殺在明天發生了。刑警聞怡在證物室電腦上看著不可能的直播，城市緊急電話卻安靜無聲。畫面是一間郊區小屋，後門旁倒著一具身體，時間戳記標著明晚。頻道標題宣稱能在警方阻止前傳達未來。聞怡不相信標題。她抄下地址、請韓律封存直播，開車前往小屋。門廊燈亮著，屋主活生生地來應門，對深夜打擾很不高興。但地板上有影片裡那個藍杯子，杯緣多了一個新缺口。屋主低頭看，喃喃說：「一小時前它還沒破啊。」

**EN 02**

Wen parked beneath the cottage's single porch lamp and found a cushion propping open the rear window. The owner said he had placed it there because the frame stuck in damp weather. He had never heard of the livestream. When Wen explained that a crime scene had appeared online, his first defense was anger; then he began to shake. The diversity of family photographs on the wall was striking, none of them matching the picture on the stream. Wen asked him not to move anything while Han photographed the room. She knew that frightened people sometimes agreed too quickly to whatever an official suggested. If tomorrow was being manufactured, the scene needed to be preserved before the next person came to manufacture it.

**繁中 02**

聞怡把車停在小屋唯一的門廊燈下，發現一塊軟墊卡著後窗。屋主說窗框受潮容易卡住，他才這樣放，從沒聽過直播。聞怡說網路上出現這裡的犯罪現場時，他第一反應是憤怒，接著開始發抖。牆上掛著各種家人照片，沒有一張和直播畫面相符。聞怡請他先別移動物品，讓韓律拍下房間。她知道，受到驚嚇的人有時會過快答應官員的要求。如果明天的現場是被人製造出來的，他們必須在下一個人來製造它之前保護眼前的現場。

**EN 03**

The online discussion was fierce. One account tried to flatter Wen by calling her a genius who would stop the future; another declared she was already too late. She read neither opinion aloud to the owner. Instead, she asked when he had last seen the blue cup intact. His answer was precise: an hour before, while making tea. Han checked the sink, the drying rack, and the kitchen's overhead camera. It had been disconnected during the afternoon by someone wearing a service badge. The cottage owner had assumed the visitor belonged to a maintenance company. Wen wrote the badge color on the first line of her notes. She had stopped thinking about a supernatural prophecy and started thinking about a person who had entered this house.

**繁中 03**

網路討論激烈。有帳號奉承聞怡，說她是能阻止未來的天才；也有人宣稱一切已太遲。她沒有把這些話念給屋主聽，而是問藍杯子最後完整的時間。答案很明確：一小時前泡茶時。韓律檢查洗手槽、瀝水架和廚房天花板攝影機，發現下午有人戴著服務證把它斷線。屋主以為訪客屬於維修公司。聞怡把證件顏色寫在筆記第一行。她不再只想超自然預言，而是開始想有一個人曾進過這棟房子。

**EN 04**

The cottage owner let out a strained giggle when Han produced a bright orange parachute from behind the shed. It was a toy prop, too small to carry anyone, but exactly the sort of theatrical object a production company might leave after filming a fake scene. The owner was pessimistic. 'If they can put pictures of my death online, what can I do?' Wen asked him to identify what belonged to him, not to guess what tomorrow would bring. He recognized the orange cloth as unfamiliar. Han photographed its stitching and recovered a maker's tag. The tag linked to a local supplier of film equipment. The future, it seemed, had purchased its props in advance.

**繁中 04**

韓律從棚後拿出一頂亮橘色降落傘時，屋主勉強笑了一聲。那只是玩具道具，小得載不了人，卻很像製作公司拍假場景後會留下的東西。屋主很悲觀：「如果他們都能把我的死亡畫面放上網，我還能怎麼辦？」聞怡只請他辨認哪些是自己的物品，不要猜明天會怎樣。他不認得橘布。韓律拍下縫線，找到製造標籤，指向本地電影器材供應商。看來未來早就先買好道具了。

**EN 05**

The channel claimed to possess the future, and its presentation included a reference to several police cases that were not public. Wen felt her stomach tighten. The stream's producer might be guessing a murder; someone inside the justice system might also be selling information. Han asked her not to confuse the two questions yet. They preserved the file and extracted its creation date, the software used to edit it, and the sequence in which frames had been exported. The video had been assembled before its supposed broadcast time. A future crime might still occur, but this footage was not a window into tomorrow. It was an artifact made by somebody who wanted people to believe it was.

**繁中 05**

頻道聲稱擁有未來，在簡報畫面裡還引用數件尚未公開的警方案件。聞怡胃部一緊。製作人可能只是猜測謀殺，也可能有人從司法體系內部販賣資訊。韓律提醒她先分清兩個問題。他們封存檔案，擷取建立日期、剪輯軟體及影格匯出順序。影片在宣稱的直播時間之前就被組合完成。明天或許仍可能發生犯罪，但這段畫面不是看向明天的窗，而是有人製造、希望大家誤認為預言的物件。

**EN 06**

The channel's music gave the cottage scene a spiritual, almost ceremonial dread, as though a death were a tremendous event staged for an audience. Wen turned the volume off. She wrote in her notebook that portraying a living person as a corpse without permission was already a serious violation, regardless of whether the prediction came true. Han showed her a second export file. Its background shadows pointed in the wrong direction for the claimed time of day. The owner confirmed that a production van had visited that afternoon, but he had been in the garden and had not seen who entered. Wen arranged protection without announcing it to the viewers. At nine fifty-eight, someone knocked three times on the rear door. The owner looked at her for permission to answer.

**繁中 06**

頻道配樂把小屋畫面變成帶精神儀式感的恐懼，彷彿死亡是專為觀眾安排的巨大場面。聞怡關掉音量，在筆記本寫下：未經同意把活人描繪成屍體，本身已是嚴重侵害，無論預言是否應驗。韓律展示第二份匯出檔，背景陰影方向不符畫面標示的時間。屋主確認下午有製作車來過，但他當時在花園，沒看到誰進門。聞怡安排保護，沒有向觀眾宣布。九點五十八分，後門傳來三下敲門聲。屋主望向她，等她決定是否開門。

**EN 07**

Han Lu drew a visual comparison between the streamed room and the real cottage, leaving uncertain details in a separate column. A witness from the neighboring house described a production van parked behind the property earlier that afternoon. He had thought it was for an exercise video because two people were doing a workout beside a folding camera rig. Wen listened without telling him what the stream showed. When the neighbor left, Han circled the difference that mattered: the footage included a bright red chair, but the living owner's room contained only a pale wooden stool. 'The future has a furniture department,' Wen said. Han did not laugh. Another message had already arrived on the evidence computer: TOMORROW, SAME TIME. THIS TIME YOU WILL BE IN THE FRAME.

**繁中 07**

韓律畫出直播房間與真實小屋的視覺比對表，把不確定細節列在另一欄。鄰居作為見證人說，下午有一輛製作車停在屋後。他以為是拍運動影片，因為有兩個人在折疊攝影架旁做體能訓練。聞怡沒有透露直播內容，只讓他說完。鄰居離開後，韓律圈起真正重要的差異：直播裡有鮮紅椅子，屋主房裡卻只有淺色木凳。「原來未來也有道具部。」聞怡說。韓律沒笑，證物室電腦又跳出新訊息：「明天同一時間。這次你也會入鏡。」

### 理解測驗

**Q1. Which development is established in Chapter 1?**

A. The investigators decide that every earlier warning was fictional.

B. At 9 p.m. detective Wen Yi saw a livestream of a murder scene that did not yet exist, with a timestamp dated tomorrow.

C. The central problem disappears without further action.

**答案：B。** 晚上九點，刑警聞怡看到明天才會發生的命案現場直播，時間戳記竟標著隔天。

**Q2. What new complication or decision changes the investigation in this chapter?**

A. All the witnesses agree to destroy their records.

B. The team abandons the people affected and declares a perfect victory.

C. She sealed the recording before visiting the address, where the supposed victim was still alive and denied knowing the camera.

**答案：C。** 她先封存影片才前往地址，片中死者仍活著，也不認識那支攝影機。


## CHAPTER 02｜The Apartment Before the Crime｜命案前的公寓

### 正文與翻譯

**EN 01**

Wen and digital examiner Han Lu documented the apartment's furniture without telling the residents which detail the forecast video showed. Something had changed, but not everybody noticed it in the same order. Wen Yi listened to the account of the nearest worker, then to someone standing farther away. Their differences were not necessarily lies. Each person had been given a different view and a different reason to be afraid of admitting uncertainty. A label marked TOMORROW on a clip did not make the event inevitable. Wen Yi checked the date on each physical door and each receipt instead. The term atom appeared in the revised file; Wen Yi checked its meaning against the original page. One witness used the word atomic; Han Lu noted that a description was not a measurement.

**繁中 01**

聞怡與數位鑑識員韓律記錄公寓傢俱，沒有告訴住戶預告影片拍到什麼細節。某件事改變了，但人們發現它的順序不同。聞怡先聽近處工作人員的描述，再聽遠處旁觀者說法。差異不一定是謊言：每個人看到的角度不同，承認不確定時所要承擔的風險也不同。影片標上「明天」不代表事情必然發生。聞怡改去核對實體門禁和收據上的每一個日期。修訂檔案出現「原子」一詞，聞怡拿原始頁面核對它的意思。一位證人用了「原子能的」一詞，韓律指出形容並不等於測量。

**EN 02**

She sealed the recording before visiting the address, where the supposed victim was still alive and denied knowing the camera. Wen Yi refused to pretend the previous decision had solved everything. The practical result was still there, useful but narrow. For each thing the team knew, there were two things it had not measured. Wen Yi drew a line through the claims that depended on speculation and asked Han Lu to keep the original record intact. A handwritten label read combination, but no one could say who had written it there. The summary called the situation delicate; Wen Yi asked which observation justified that description. Han Lu noticed the word extent in a note that someone had added after the first inspection.

**繁中 02**

她先封存影片才前往地址，片中死者仍活著，也不認識那支攝影機。聞怡不願假裝前一次決定已解決所有問題。成果確實存在，卻有明確界限。每個已知事項旁都還有未測量的部分。聞怡劃掉靠猜測成立的說法，請韓律保留原始紀錄。手寫標籤寫著「組合」，卻沒有人知道是誰留下的。摘要以「微弱的」形容情況，聞怡追問這種說法有何觀察根據。韓律注意到初次檢查後有人在筆記裡加上「程度」一詞。

**EN 03**

A small disagreement changed the schedule. Wen Yi wanted to proceed before evidence disappeared; Han Lu wanted another check because a wrong conclusion might hurt people who could not easily defend themselves. They did not settle the matter by choosing a leader. They wrote down what a short, reversible test could actually establish. Han Lu pointed to the fort in the inventory and asked why its location had changed. The earlier photograph clearly showed a fossil; the newer version did not, so Wen Yi kept both. The term gaze appeared in the revised file; Wen Yi checked its meaning against the original page.

**繁中 03**

一場小爭執改變了時程。聞怡想趁證據消失前行動；韓律要求再核對一次，因為錯誤結論可能傷害難以自辯的人。他們沒有靠選領袖解決爭議，而是列出短暫、可逆測試真正能證明什麼。韓律指向清單上的「堡壘」，追問其位置為何改變。較早的照片清楚顯示一項「化石」，新版卻沒有，聞怡因此保留兩張。修訂檔案出現「凝視」一詞，聞怡拿原始頁面核對它的意思。

**EN 04**

The real room lacked a distinctive red chair from the livestream, suggesting the footage was staged or altered. The plan had been amended three times, and each change left a trace. Wen Yi valued those corrections more than a perfect-looking first draft. If the outcome proved disappointing, the group would still be able to identify which assumption needed repair. Han Lu watched for the quiet participant who had not yet been invited to speak. The term guarantee appeared in the revised file; Wen Yi checked its meaning against the original page. Han Lu noticed the word immigration in a note that someone had added after the first inspection. A handwritten label read intimate, but no one could say who had written it there.

**繁中 04**

實際房間沒有影片裡醒目的紅椅子，暗示畫面被布置或修改。計畫改了三次，每次修改都留下痕跡。聞怡認為這些修正比看似完美的初稿更有價值。就算結果不理想，團隊仍能知道該修正哪一項假設。韓律也注意到那名一直沒有發言機會的人。修訂檔案出現「保證」一詞，聞怡拿原始頁面核對它的意思。韓律注意到初次檢查後有人在筆記裡加上「入出境」一詞。手寫標籤寫著「私密的」，卻沒有人知道是誰留下的。

**EN 05**

An official version reached the room before the practical work was finished. Wen Yi read it once, then compared it with what they had actually measured. The difference was not always malicious, but it could still hurt somebody. Han Lu asked that the correction be written in language the affected people could understand. A label marked TOMORROW on a clip did not make the event inevitable. Wen Yi checked the date on each physical door and each receipt instead. The earlier photograph clearly showed a landscape; the newer version did not, so Wen Yi kept both. A note about the messenger lay beside the old register, with a date that Wen Yi could not verify. Han Lu pointed to the mill in the inventory and asked why its location had changed.

**繁中 05**

實際工作還沒完成，官方版本就傳到現場。聞怡讀了一次，再比對真正測得的結果。差異未必都是惡意，卻仍可能傷害人。韓律要求把更正寫成受影響者看得懂的語言。影片標上「明天」不代表事情必然發生。聞怡改去核對實體門禁和收據上的每一個日期。較早的照片清楚顯示一項「風景布景」，新版卻沒有，聞怡因此保留兩張。一張談到「送件員」的便條放在舊登記簿旁，日期讓聞怡無法核實。韓律指向清單上的「磨坊」，追問其位置為何改變。

**EN 06**

A witness asked whether the team was taking sides. Wen Yi answered that refusing to invent a fact was not the same as refusing to care. What mattered was whether the procedure protected someone whose story was less convenient to tell. Han Lu listened without interrupting and wrote down the question exactly as it had been asked. On the next form, the action field read murmur, but the witness had never approved the operation. An official memo described the plan as productive, but Wen Yi refused to treat an adjective as evidence. The summary called the situation psychological; Wen Yi asked which observation justified that description.

**繁中 06**

一名見證人問團隊是否偏袒。聞怡回答，不捏造事實不代表冷漠。真正重要的是程序能否保護那個故事不方便被說出口的人。韓律沒有打斷，逐字記下原問題。下一張表單把動作寫成「低語」，見證人卻從未同意這項操作。官方備忘錄形容計畫「有成果的」，聞怡不願把形容詞當作證據。摘要以「心理上的」形容情況，聞怡追問這種說法有何觀察根據。

**EN 07**

Even after the group agreed on the immediate step, another question remained. The answer would not arrive through a dramatic announcement. Someone would have to return to the place, compare the earlier notes, and accept the possibility that the next piece of evidence might spoil an attractive theory. Wen Yi prepared to do exactly that. Han Lu preserved the original file and its metadata before discussing theories. A changed picture could frighten people; it could not change when a cable had been connected. The instructions used the verb retain without naming who would carry the risk; Wen Yi circled that gap. On the next form, the action field read revise, but the witness had never approved the operation. A handwritten label read voyage, but no one could say who had written it there.

**繁中 07**

即使大家同意眼前步驟，另一個問題仍在。答案不會靠戲劇性公告降臨；有人得返回現場、比對舊筆記，並接受新證據可能推翻漂亮理論。聞怡準備做的正是這件事。韓律在討論理論前先封存原始檔與中繼資料。被修改的畫面能嚇人，卻不能改變線纜接通的時間。指示裡用了「保留」這個動詞，卻沒說誰承擔風險；聞怡圈出這項缺漏。下一張表單把動作寫成「修改」，見證人卻從未同意這項操作。手寫標籤寫著「航程」，卻沒有人知道是誰留下的。
