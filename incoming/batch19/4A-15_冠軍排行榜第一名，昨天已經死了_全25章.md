# 4A-15｜冠軍排行榜第一名，昨天已經死了

*The Champion Died Yesterday*

第四批・第4級（3000–4000詞級）｜競技懸疑｜全25章雙語教學初稿。

主角：余程（競技選手）；重要角色：羅琪（體育資料分析員）。

**編輯註：**故事事件與結局為本次獨立設計，配詞融入與敘事中間段採規則化擴寫；這是完整章節的教學初稿，尚未經人工文學潤稿。

## CHAPTER 01｜A Dead Champion at Number One｜死者登上冠軍榜

### 正文與翻譯

**EN 01**

Yu Cheng knew the smell of a racing stadium: wet rubber, disinfectant, and the sharp acid sting of sweat after the finish. This morning he smelled only fresh paint. The national sports assembly had opened with a moment of silence for Han Fei, who had died in an accident the day before. A giant screen showed her face, then switched to the new championship ranking. HAN FEI — FIRST PLACE. The crowd made a confused sound. There had been no overnight competition, no recorded race, and no reason for a score to change. Yet the number beside her name kept climbing. Luo Qi, the data analyst seated behind Yu, quietly took out her phone and photographed three consecutive updates. 'Don't applaud,' she said. 'Count the seconds.'

**繁中 01**

余程熟悉競技場的味道：濕橡膠、消毒劑，以及衝線後汗水刺鼻的酸味。今天早上卻只有新油漆的氣息。全國體育集會先為昨天意外過世的韓霏默哀。巨幕顯示她的照片，接著切換到最新冠軍榜：「韓霏——第一名。」群眾發出困惑的聲音。昨夜沒有比賽，沒有賽事紀錄，更沒有成績改變的理由，但她名字旁的分數還在上升。坐在余程後面的資料分析員羅琪悄悄拿出手機，拍下連續三次更新。「先別鼓掌。」她說，「數秒數。」

**EN 02**

A caretaker swept the empty starting lane with a broom while officials tried to construct an explanation for the impossible ranking. Their first proposal was a delayed score. The second was a ceremonial tribute. Neither fitted the timestamps. Yu asked the data office to cooperate with an independent check, but the federation said the system belonged to its sponsor and the numbers were confidential. Luo Qi held her phone against the glass and showed him the three screenshots. Each new score had arrived exactly thirteen seconds apart. 'That isn't how a dead athlete's results are recorded,' she said. Yu knew this without needing to understand the software: a stopwatch should stop when the race is over.

**繁中 02**

看守員拿掃帚清掃空起跑道，官員則試著建構不可能排名的解釋。第一個說法是延遲上傳成績，第二個說法是紀念性分數；兩者都不符合時間戳記。余程要求資料室配合獨立檢查，協會卻說系統屬於贊助商，數字是機密。羅琪把手機貼向玻璃，讓他看三張截圖。每筆新分數恰好間隔十三秒。「死人不會這樣更新成績。」她說。余程不必懂軟體也知道：比賽結束後，碼表就該停下來。

**EN 03**

A row of copper medals lay behind the registration counter, ready for the photographers. Yu remembered Han Fei arguing that a runner was not a product. The sponsor had called her difficult, and one commentator had called her a coward for refusing an experimental sensor. Now the same people were claiming her rising score proved she had been a genius beyond death. Luo found a technical note stating that performance predictions were calculated before every event. 'So show me where the predictions end and the actual races begin,' Yu said. The official folded the page instead of answering. Yu looked at the unused medals and thought how easily a number could be polished until it no longer resembled anyone's life.

**繁中 03**

報到櫃臺後方排著一列銅色獎牌，等攝影師拍照。余程想起韓霏曾爭辯選手不是商品，贊助商說她難搞，還有評論員罵她膽小鬼，因為她拒絕實驗感測器。現在同一群人又說，死後分數上升證明她天才到超越死亡。羅琪找到技術備註，表示每場比賽前都會先計算預測表現。「那就指出預測在哪裡結束、真正的比賽從哪裡開始。」余程說。官員沒有回答，只把紙摺起。余程看著未用過的獎牌，心想數字多容易被磨亮，直到再也不像任何人的人生。

**EN 04**

The broadcast crew wanted to entertain the crowd while the ranking staff investigated. Their director proposed a facial montage of Han Fei smiling at the camera, with triumphant music and a line saying she had never stopped competing. Yu refused to appear in the montage. He could not interfere with the family's mourning by turning it into a commercial event. Luo instead asked the crew to retain the complete ranking screen from before the moment of silence. In that footage the points began rising thirty seconds before the announcement of Han's death. It was a crucial difference: whatever generated the score had already been running, and the association had been watching it long before the audience noticed.

**繁中 04**

轉播團隊想在排名調查期間娛樂群眾。導演提議剪一段韓霏對鏡頭微笑的臉部特寫，配勝利音樂，說她從未停止競爭。余程拒絕入鏡。他不願把家屬哀悼變成商業節目。羅琪改要求保存默哀前完整的榜單畫面。畫面顯示，宣布韓霏死亡前三十秒，分數就開始上升。這個差異至關重要：產生分數的機制早就在運作，協會也早於觀眾發現之前便看過它。

**EN 05**

The ministry's sports liaison arrived from an opening ceremony at the city's opera hall. He argued that a public dispute would embarrass the country before the international tournament. It was a persuasive argument only if nobody asked whom the silence protected. Yu showed him Han's consent form, which prohibited monitoring after her final race. Luo showed the repeating thirteen-second updates. 'I'm not asking you to believe in ghosts,' she said. 'I'm asking you to separate recorded performance from a model's prediction.' The liaison finally agreed to preserve the server rather than reset it, a small decision that saved the evidence without claiming to solve the whole case.

**繁中 05**

體育主管機關的聯絡官剛從市立歌劇廳開幕典禮趕來。他認為公開爭議會在國際賽前讓國家難堪。只有不追問沉默在保護誰時，這論點才顯得有說服力。余程出示韓霏的同意書，裡面禁止她最後一場比賽後繼續監測。羅琪則展示每十三秒更新一次的分數。「我不是要你相信鬼魂，」她說，「我是要你分清已記錄的表現與模型預測。」聯絡官終於同意保存伺服器，不立刻重設。這個小決定保住證據，卻沒有假裝整件事已被解決。

**EN 06**

Outside the stadium Yu studied the strange phenomenon on a public terminal. The web route to the ranking data led not to yesterday's finish sheets but to a training centre officially closed the previous month. A shady contractor had supplied its software, and the contract classified prediction numbers as marketable content. The word marketable made Luo angry. Han Fei's parents were expected to sit in the front row tomorrow and applaud a memorial built from numbers the association could not verify. Yu asked the staff to turn off the automated cheering sound in the hall. They did. For the first time since the announcement, the silence seemed to belong to the athlete, not the company.

**繁中 06**

體育場外，余程在公共終端檢查奇怪現象。排名資料的網路路徑沒有通往昨天的終點單，而是連到上個月已正式關閉的訓練中心。一家可疑承包商提供軟體，合約竟把預測數字列為可販售內容。「可販售」這三個字讓羅琪憤怒。韓霏的父母明天還得坐在第一排，對著協會無法驗證的數字做成的紀念活動鼓掌。余程請工作人員關掉場館的自動喝采音效。他們照辦了。自宣布以來第一次，沉默彷彿屬於選手，而不是公司。

**EN 07**

The sponsor's slogan was BEYOND HUMAN LIMITS. Yu thought it was a poor way to remember an athlete who had fought for safer training. He requested the system's data usage record while Luo Qi inspected the raw timestamps; the federation sent them a glossy brochure instead. At the exit a youthful competitor asked whether Han Fei's ghost was still running somewhere. Yu could not give her the comforting answer she wanted. 'A score is made by a system,' he said. 'A race is run by a person. We have to find out why the system won't tell the difference.' Above their heads the leaderboard sounded its cheerful chime again, and the dead champion earned another fifty points while an empty starting lane remained perfectly still.

**繁中 07**

贊助商的口號是「超越人類極限」。余程覺得，用這句話紀念曾為安全訓練爭取權益的選手實在不妥。他要求查看系統的資料使用紀錄，羅琪則檢查原始時間戳記；協會回送的卻只是一本光鮮的宣傳冊。出口處，一名稚氣未脫的年輕選手問韓霏的鬼魂是否還在某處奔跑。余程給不了她想聽的安慰。「分數是系統算出來的，」他說，「比賽是人跑出來的。我們得查清為什麼系統分不出來。」頭頂榜單再次歡快響起。空跑道紋絲未動，死去的冠軍卻又多了五十分。

### 理解測驗

**Q1. Which development is established in Chapter 1?**

A. The investigators decide that every earlier warning was fictional.

B. The national ranking screen placed dead athlete Han Fei at number one, a day after the hospital confirmed her death in an accident.

C. The central problem disappears without further action.

**答案：B。** 全國榜單把已過世的韓霏列為冠軍，前一天醫院才確認她意外死亡。

**Q2. What new complication or decision changes the investigation in this chapter?**

A. All the witnesses agree to destroy their records.

B. The team abandons the people affected and declares a perfect victory.

C. Yu Cheng noticed her score kept rising while no event was running, and analyst Luo Qi photographed the timestamps.

**答案：C。** 余程發現沒有賽事時她的分數仍上升，分析員羅琪拍下時間戳記。


## CHAPTER 02｜Points Without a Race｜沒有比賽的分數

### 正文與翻譯

**EN 01**

The federation insisted rankings came from official performance feeds, but the feed's source address led to a closed training centre. Something had changed, but not everybody noticed it in the same order. Yu Cheng listened to the account of the nearest worker, then to someone standing farther away. Their differences were not necessarily lies. Each person had been given a different view and a different reason to be afraid of admitting uncertainty. The federation's seal looked official on the letter, but the attached calculation had no visible source. Yu Cheng asked for a reproducible result rather than a prestigious signature. A note about the autograph lay beside the old register, with a date that Yu Cheng could not verify. On the next form, the action field read carve, but the witness had never approved the operation.

**繁中 01**

協會聲稱排名來自官方賽事資料，來源位址卻連到已關閉的訓練中心。某件事改變了，但人們發現它的順序不同。余程先聽近處工作人員的描述，再聽遠處旁觀者說法。差異不一定是謊言：每個人看到的角度不同，承認不確定時所要承擔的風險也不同。公文上的協會印章看來正式，附上的計算卻沒有來源。余程要的是可重現的結果，不是顯赫的簽名。一張談到「親筆簽名」的便條放在舊登記簿旁，日期讓余程無法核實。下一張表單把動作寫成「刻出」，見證人卻從未同意這項操作。

**EN 02**

Yu Cheng noticed her score kept rising while no event was running, and analyst Luo Qi photographed the timestamps. Yu Cheng refused to pretend the previous decision had solved everything. The practical result was still there, useful but narrow. For each thing the team knew, there were two things it had not measured. Yu Cheng drew a line through the claims that depended on speculation and asked Luo Qi to keep the original record intact. People disagreed about convenience; Yu Cheng separated the feeling from the facts they could test. The report claimed cruelty, but Yu Cheng wanted an example instead of a reassuring noun. Luo Qi noticed the word disguise in a note that someone had added after the first inspection.

**繁中 02**

余程發現沒有賽事時她的分數仍上升，分析員羅琪拍下時間戳記。余程不願假裝前一次決定已解決所有問題。成果確實存在，卻有明確界限。每個已知事項旁都還有未測量的部分。余程劃掉靠猜測成立的說法，請羅琪保留原始紀錄。大家對「便利」意見不同，余程把感受與可檢驗的事實分開。報告聲稱有「殘忍」，余程要求具體例子，而不是安撫人的名詞。羅琪注意到初次檢查後有人在筆記裡加上「偽裝」一詞。

**EN 03**

A small disagreement changed the schedule. Yu Cheng wanted to proceed before evidence disappeared; Luo Qi wanted another check because a wrong conclusion might hurt people who could not easily defend themselves. They did not settle the matter by choosing a leader. They wrote down what a short, reversible test could actually establish. Luo Qi noticed the word elastic in a note that someone had added after the first inspection. A handwritten label read explosive, but no one could say who had written it there. The report claimed feedback, but Yu Cheng wanted an example instead of a reassuring noun.

**繁中 03**

一場小爭執改變了時程。余程想趁證據消失前行動；羅琪要求再核對一次，因為錯誤結論可能傷害難以自辯的人。他們沒有靠選領袖解決爭議，而是列出短暫、可逆測試真正能證明什麼。羅琪注意到初次檢查後有人在筆記裡加上「有彈性的」一詞。手寫標籤寫著「爆炸性的」，卻沒有人知道是誰留下的。報告聲稱有「回饋」，余程要求具體例子，而不是安撫人的名詞。

**EN 04**

A junior athlete reported receiving threats whenever she asked who owned the ranking software. The plan had been amended three times, and each change left a trace. Yu Cheng valued those corrections more than a perfect-looking first draft. If the outcome proved disappointing, the group would still be able to identify which assumption needed repair. Luo Qi watched for the quiet participant who had not yet been invited to speak. The term immigrant appeared in the revised file; Yu Cheng checked its meaning against the original page. Luo Qi noticed the word initial in a note that someone had added after the first inspection. A handwritten label read mischief, but no one could say who had written it there.

**繁中 04**

一位年輕選手表示，每次追問排名軟體的所有權就會收到威脅。計畫改了三次，每次修改都留下痕跡。余程認為這些修正比看似完美的初稿更有價值。就算結果不理想，團隊仍能知道該修正哪一項假設。羅琪也注意到那名一直沒有發言機會的人。修訂檔案出現「移民」一詞，余程拿原始頁面核對它的意思。羅琪注意到初次檢查後有人在筆記裡加上「最初的」一詞。手寫標籤寫著「惡作劇」，卻沒有人知道是誰留下的。

**EN 05**

An official version reached the room before the practical work was finished. Yu Cheng read it once, then compared it with what they had actually measured. The difference was not always malicious, but it could still hurt somebody. Luo Qi asked that the correction be written in language the affected people could understand. The federation's seal looked official on the letter, but the attached calculation had no visible source. Yu Cheng asked for a reproducible result rather than a prestigious signature. The earlier photograph clearly showed a paw; the newer version did not, so Yu Cheng kept both. The report claimed prevention, but Yu Cheng wanted an example instead of a reassuring noun. Luo Qi wrote privilege in the margin and asked how the team would recognize it in practice.

**繁中 05**

實際工作還沒完成，官方版本就傳到現場。余程讀了一次，再比對真正測得的結果。差異未必都是惡意，卻仍可能傷害人。羅琪要求把更正寫成受影響者看得懂的語言。公文上的協會印章看來正式，附上的計算卻沒有來源。余程要的是可重現的結果，不是顯赫的簽名。較早的照片清楚顯示一項「爪子」，新版卻沒有，余程因此保留兩張。報告聲稱有「預防」，余程要求具體例子，而不是安撫人的名詞。羅琪在頁邊寫下「特權」，追問團隊實際上如何辨認。

**EN 06**

A witness asked whether the team was taking sides. Yu Cheng answered that refusing to invent a fact was not the same as refusing to care. What mattered was whether the procedure protected someone whose story was less convenient to tell. Luo Qi listened without interrupting and wrote down the question exactly as it had been asked. One witness used the word prominent; Luo Qi noted that a description was not a measurement. Luo Qi repeated the instruction to prosper, then asked whether anyone had the right to refuse. The term remark appeared in the revised file; Yu Cheng checked its meaning against the original page.

**繁中 06**

一名見證人問團隊是否偏袒。余程回答，不捏造事實不代表冷漠。真正重要的是程序能否保護那個故事不方便被說出口的人。羅琪沒有打斷，逐字記下原問題。一位證人用了「醒目的」一詞，羅琪指出形容並不等於測量。羅琪重述「繁榮」的指示，再問當事人是否有權拒絕。修訂檔案出現「評論；言語」一詞，余程拿原始頁面核對它的意思。

**EN 07**

Even after the group agreed on the immediate step, another question remained. The answer would not arrive through a dramatic announcement. Someone would have to return to the place, compare the earlier notes, and accept the possibility that the next piece of evidence might spoil an attractive theory. Yu Cheng prepared to do exactly that. Luo Qi compared the sensor's raw timestamp with the official results. A predicted number was not a medal, no matter how brightly a sponsor displayed it. The term ruin appeared in the revised file; Yu Cheng checked its meaning against the original page. On the next form, the action field read shift, but the witness had never approved the operation. A handwritten label read timetable, but no one could say who had written it there.

**繁中 07**

即使大家同意眼前步驟，另一個問題仍在。答案不會靠戲劇性公告降臨；有人得返回現場、比對舊筆記，並接受新證據可能推翻漂亮理論。余程準備做的正是這件事。羅琪比對感測器原始時間與官方成績。無論贊助商展示得多亮眼，預測數字都不是獎牌。修訂檔案出現「毀壞」一詞，余程拿原始頁面核對它的意思。下一張表單把動作寫成「輪班」，見證人卻從未同意這項操作。手寫標籤寫著「時刻表」，卻沒有人知道是誰留下的。
