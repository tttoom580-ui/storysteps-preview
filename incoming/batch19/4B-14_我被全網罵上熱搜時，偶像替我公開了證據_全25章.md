# 4B-14｜我被全網罵上熱搜時，偶像替我公開了證據

*When the Idol Shared the Evidence*

第四批・第4級（3000–4000詞級）｜娛樂圈戀愛｜全25章雙語教學初稿。

主角：陳念（實習化妝師）；重要角色：余知（人氣歌手）。

**編輯註：**故事事件與結局為本次獨立設計，配詞融入與敘事中間段採規則化擴寫；這是完整章節的教學初稿，尚未經人工文學潤稿。

## CHAPTER 01｜The Theft on Trending Now｜熱搜上的竊賊

### 正文與翻譯

**EN 01**

By noon the internet had decided Chen Nian was a thief. The photograph was simple enough to convey in a single glance: a trainee makeup artist holding the velvet case of a stolen necklace behind a famous singer's dressing room. But Nian remembered who had put the case in her hand, and she remembered that it was already empty. Across the stage, technicians rolled a painted cottage into position for the evening's music video. Nobody stopped for her question. Her employee badge still opened the side corridor, while her message inbox filled with insults from strangers. Then Yu Zhi appeared at the doorway. He did not tell her to be brave or grateful. 'I want the original recording preserved,' he told security. 'All of it, not the part that makes a good headline.'

**繁中 01**

中午之前，網路已認定陳念是小偷。那張照片一眼就能傳達指控：實習化妝師在知名歌手的化妝間後拿著失竊項鍊的天鵝絨盒。但陳念記得誰把盒子交給她，也記得當時盒子已經空了。舞台另一端，技師正把音樂錄影帶用的小屋布景推到定位。沒有人停下來聽她解釋。她的員工證仍能打開側廊，訊息信箱卻塞滿陌生人的辱罵。這時余知站在門口。他沒有叫她勇敢，也沒要求她感謝。「我要保存原始錄影。」他對保全說，「全部，不是適合寫標題的那一段。」

**EN 02**

Nian sat on the cushion beneath the dressing-room mirror and tried not to touch her phone again. Her first defense had been a sentence typed in panic: I DIDN'T STEAL IT. The agency had refused to post it, claiming that an apology would show more diversity and maturity than a denial. She looked at the original shift schedule instead. At the minute when the missing necklace was supposedly taken, she was helping a dancer cover a bruise with stage makeup; three people had watched. But the camera angle showed only the velvet case in her hands two minutes later. Nian wrote down those two minutes as carefully as she would blend foundation across an uneven patch of skin.

**繁中 02**

陳念坐在化妝鏡下的軟墊椅上，努力不再碰手機。她最早的辯護是慌亂打出的句子：「我沒有偷。」公司拒絕發布，說道歉比否認更能展現多元與成熟。她改去看原始班表。據稱項鍊被拿走的那分鐘，她正在替舞者用舞台妝遮住瘀青，有三個人目睹。但鏡頭只拍到兩分鐘後她手上拿著天鵝絨盒。她仔細記下這兩分鐘，就像她會細心地把粉底推勻在凹凸不平的皮膚上。

**EN 03**

The online attacks were fierce. One account called Nian a genius thief; another tried to flatter the agency by praising its 'decisive protection' of the singer. Nobody asked the people who had worked beside her. A senior stylist sent a private message: Keep the original schedule. Don't let them replace it with a cleaned version. Nian saved the message but did not publish the sender's name. She understood how quickly the crowd would turn on a new target. Across the corridor, Yu Zhi stood listening to the head of publicity describe the scandal as a problem of fan emotion. 'No,' he said at last. 'It is a question of what the recording shows.' The head of publicity stopped smiling.

**繁中 03**

網路攻擊猛烈得可怕。一個帳號稱陳念是天才小偷，另一個奉承公司「果斷保護」歌手的決策。沒有誰去問與她共事的人。資深造型師私訊：「保留原始班表，別讓他們換成整理過的版本。」陳念存下訊息，卻沒有公開對方姓名，因為群眾隨時會轉向下一個目標。走廊另一頭，余知聽公關主管把事件說成粉絲情緒問題。「不。」他終於開口，「問題是錄影究竟拍到了什麼。」公關主管不再微笑。

**EN 04**

A dancer passing the makeup room gave Nian a quick, anxious giggle, then apologized for sounding nervous. She had been rehearsing a stunt using a bright paper parachute, a prop that now lay tangled in the corridor beside the empty necklace display. The dancer was pessimistic about the investigation. 'They already wrote the headline,' she said. Nian asked whether she could describe the moment the box changed hands, without agreeing to go on camera. The dancer nodded. The assistant had brought the case to Nian and said the clasp was damaged. This was the first independent account matching Nian's memory. She wrote it down and asked the dancer to correct anything she had misunderstood.

**繁中 04**

一名路過化妝間的舞者緊張地笑了一聲，立刻為自己的不安道歉。她正在排練使用鮮豔紙降落傘的特技，那個道具此刻纏在走廊裡、空項鍊展示櫃旁。舞者對調查很悲觀。「標題都寫好了。」她說。陳念問她是否願意描述盒子轉手的那一刻，不必上鏡頭。舞者點頭。助理把盒子交給陳念時說的是扣環損壞。這是第一份與陳念記憶相符的獨立說法。她寫下經過，請舞者糾正任何可能記錯的地方。

**EN 05**

Yu Zhi did not possess the authority to order the agency to clear Nian's name, although fans imagined every celebrity could command the people around him. At the afternoon presentation he refused to repeat the theft accusation as fact. Instead, he asked the legal department for a reference to the original security recording and a documented chain of custody. The manager offered him a few selected frames. He asked for the whole file. Nian noticed that he did not describe himself as her rescuer. He described a procedure that any accused worker should be entitled to request. When the meeting ended, he handed Nian the independent lawyer's contact details and left her alone to decide whether to call.

**繁中 05**

余知並沒有命令公司替陳念平反的權力，雖然粉絲總以為明星能指揮周遭所有人。下午簡報上，他拒絕把失竊指控說成事實，而是向法務部門要求原始監視錄影的參考資料與完整保管鏈。經紀主管給他幾張挑過的影格，他要的是整個檔案。陳念注意到，他沒有把自己說成拯救她的人，只說每位被指控員工都應該有權提出這類要求。會議結束後，他把獨立律師聯絡方式交給陳念，讓她自行決定是否致電。

**EN 06**

The singer's public image was built around a spiritual, almost untouchable perfection. Nian had spent months seeing the tremendous work of ordinary people that made that image possible: hairpins, missed dinners, carefully washed brushes, and workers holding their breath before a cue. To accuse one of them without checking the recording was a violation of trust that no beautiful song could erase. She returned to the dressing room to collect her own kit. Under the mirror lay the first test strip she had painted that morning, time-stamped and signed. Beside it was a note: The case was empty when it arrived. She recognized the handwriting as her assistant's, and the writer had not yet spoken to the investigators.

**繁中 06**

歌手公眾形象帶著精神性、幾乎不可觸及的完美感。陳念工作數月，卻看見塑造那形象需要普通人付出多麼巨大的勞動：髮夾、錯過的晚餐、仔細洗淨的刷具，以及等待提示時屏住呼吸的工作者。不核對錄影就指控其中一人，是任何美妙歌曲都無法消除的信任違規。她回化妝間收自己的工具，在鏡下找到早上畫的第一張試色條，標有時間和簽名。旁邊還有一張便條：「送來時盒子就是空的。」她認出字跡屬於助理，而那個人還沒向調查者開口。

**EN 07**

Nian examined the visual timeline the agency had prepared. It showed the moment she touched the box but not the assistant who had brought it; a witness had been cropped out as neatly as a wrinkle from a fashion photograph. Beyond the door, dancers finished their last workout while stage lights warmed the painted walls. Yu Zhi waited as Nian marked the missing twenty-two seconds. 'You don't have to give me the footage,' she said. 'I need an independent copy and the right to decide what happens to my name.' He nodded and asked security to seal the memory card. A new message flashed on Nian's phone before they could leave: APOLOGIZE TONIGHT OR LOSE EVERY JOB YOU WILL EVER HAVE.

**繁中 07**

陳念檢查公司準備的視覺時間線。上面有她碰到盒子的瞬間，卻沒有遞盒子的助理；一名證人被裁掉，就像時尚照片裡的皺紋被修得乾乾淨淨。門外，舞者完成最後一輪體能訓練，舞台燈光烘熱了布景牆。余知等待陳念標出消失的二十二秒。「你不必直接把影片交給我。」她說，「我要獨立副本，也要有權決定我的名字怎麼被使用。」他點頭，請保全封存記憶卡。兩人還沒離開，陳念手機又跳出訊息：「今晚道歉，否則你這輩子別想再有工作。」

### 理解測驗

**Q1. Which development is established in Chapter 1?**

A. The investigators decide that every earlier warning was fictional.

B. Trainee makeup artist Chen Nian was accused online of stealing a rare stage necklace after a backstage photograph showed her holding its case.

C. The central problem disappears without further action.

**答案：B。** 實習化妝師陳念被網友指控偷走珍貴舞台項鍊，因為後台照片拍到她拿著收納盒。

**Q2. What new complication or decision changes the investigation in this chapter?**

A. All the witnesses agree to destroy their records.

B. The team abandons the people affected and declares a perfect victory.

C. Cold-faced singer Yu Zhi asked the agency to preserve the complete camera record instead of posting an emotional defense.

**答案：C。** 向來冷淡的歌手余知要求公司先保存完整監視紀錄，不急著發表情緒性辯護。


## CHAPTER 02｜The Frame That Was Missing｜消失的一格影像

### 正文與翻譯

**EN 01**

The viral photo excluded the assistant who had handed Nian the empty case, changing the apparent sequence of events. Something had changed, but not everybody noticed it in the same order. Chen Nian listened to the account of the nearest worker, then to someone standing farther away. Their differences were not necessarily lies. Each person had been given a different view and a different reason to be afraid of admitting uncertainty. The audience saw a star crossing a stage. Backstage workers saw dozens of precise tasks completed under deadlines. Chen Nian knew that a just account needed both views. The term atom appeared in the revised file; Chen Nian checked its meaning against the original page. One witness used the word atomic; Yu Zhi noted that a description was not a measurement.

**繁中 01**

熱門照片裁掉了把空盒交給陳念的助理，讓事件順序完全變樣。某件事改變了，但人們發現它的順序不同。陳念先聽近處工作人員的描述，再聽遠處旁觀者說法。差異不一定是謊言：每個人看到的角度不同，承認不確定時所要承擔的風險也不同。觀眾看見明星走過舞台，後台員工看見數十項趕在期限前完成的精細工作。陳念知道公正說法需要兩種視角。修訂檔案出現「原子」一詞，陳念拿原始頁面核對它的意思。一位證人用了「原子能的」一詞，余知指出形容並不等於測量。

**EN 02**

Cold-faced singer Yu Zhi asked the agency to preserve the complete camera record instead of posting an emotional defense. Chen Nian refused to pretend the previous decision had solved everything. The practical result was still there, useful but narrow. For each thing the team knew, there were two things it had not measured. Chen Nian drew a line through the claims that depended on speculation and asked Yu Zhi to keep the original record intact. A handwritten label read combination, but no one could say who had written it there. The summary called the situation delicate; Chen Nian asked which observation justified that description. Yu Zhi noticed the word extent in a note that someone had added after the first inspection.

**繁中 02**

向來冷淡的歌手余知要求公司先保存完整監視紀錄，不急著發表情緒性辯護。陳念不願假裝前一次決定已解決所有問題。成果確實存在，卻有明確界限。每個已知事項旁都還有未測量的部分。陳念劃掉靠猜測成立的說法，請余知保留原始紀錄。手寫標籤寫著「組合」，卻沒有人知道是誰留下的。摘要以「微弱的」形容情況，陳念追問這種說法有何觀察根據。余知注意到初次檢查後有人在筆記裡加上「程度」一詞。

**EN 03**

A small disagreement changed the schedule. Chen Nian wanted to proceed before evidence disappeared; Yu Zhi wanted another check because a wrong conclusion might hurt people who could not easily defend themselves. They did not settle the matter by choosing a leader. They wrote down what a short, reversible test could actually establish. Yu Zhi pointed to the fort in the inventory and asked why its location had changed. The earlier photograph clearly showed a fossil; the newer version did not, so Chen Nian kept both. The term gaze appeared in the revised file; Chen Nian checked its meaning against the original page.

**繁中 03**

一場小爭執改變了時程。陳念想趁證據消失前行動；余知要求再核對一次，因為錯誤結論可能傷害難以自辯的人。他們沒有靠選領袖解決爭議，而是列出短暫、可逆測試真正能證明什麼。余知指向清單上的「堡壘」，追問其位置為何改變。較早的照片清楚顯示一項「化石」，新版卻沒有，陳念因此保留兩張。修訂檔案出現「凝視」一詞，陳念拿原始頁面核對它的意思。

**EN 04**

Yu Zhi possessed an unedited backstage recording but warned that publishing it would reveal a coworker's private medical information. The plan had been amended three times, and each change left a trace. Chen Nian valued those corrections more than a perfect-looking first draft. If the outcome proved disappointing, the group would still be able to identify which assumption needed repair. Yu Zhi watched for the quiet participant who had not yet been invited to speak. The term guarantee appeared in the revised file; Chen Nian checked its meaning against the original page. Yu Zhi noticed the word immigration in a note that someone had added after the first inspection. A handwritten label read intimate, but no one could say who had written it there.

**繁中 04**

余知持有未剪輯後台影像，但指出公開它會洩漏同事的私人病情。計畫改了三次，每次修改都留下痕跡。陳念認為這些修正比看似完美的初稿更有價值。就算結果不理想，團隊仍能知道該修正哪一項假設。余知也注意到那名一直沒有發言機會的人。修訂檔案出現「保證」一詞，陳念拿原始頁面核對它的意思。余知注意到初次檢查後有人在筆記裡加上「入出境」一詞。手寫標籤寫著「私密的」，卻沒有人知道是誰留下的。

**EN 05**

An official version reached the room before the practical work was finished. Chen Nian read it once, then compared it with what they had actually measured. The difference was not always malicious, but it could still hurt somebody. Yu Zhi asked that the correction be written in language the affected people could understand. The audience saw a star crossing a stage. Backstage workers saw dozens of precise tasks completed under deadlines. Chen Nian knew that a just account needed both views. The earlier photograph clearly showed a landscape; the newer version did not, so Chen Nian kept both. A note about the messenger lay beside the old register, with a date that Chen Nian could not verify. Yu Zhi pointed to the mill in the inventory and asked why its location had changed.

**繁中 05**

實際工作還沒完成，官方版本就傳到現場。陳念讀了一次，再比對真正測得的結果。差異未必都是惡意，卻仍可能傷害人。余知要求把更正寫成受影響者看得懂的語言。觀眾看見明星走過舞台，後台員工看見數十項趕在期限前完成的精細工作。陳念知道公正說法需要兩種視角。較早的照片清楚顯示一項「風景布景」，新版卻沒有，陳念因此保留兩張。一張談到「送件員」的便條放在舊登記簿旁，日期讓陳念無法核實。余知指向清單上的「磨坊」，追問其位置為何改變。

**EN 06**

A witness asked whether the team was taking sides. Chen Nian answered that refusing to invent a fact was not the same as refusing to care. What mattered was whether the procedure protected someone whose story was less convenient to tell. Yu Zhi listened without interrupting and wrote down the question exactly as it had been asked. On the next form, the action field read murmur, but the witness had never approved the operation. An official memo described the plan as productive, but Chen Nian refused to treat an adjective as evidence. The summary called the situation psychological; Chen Nian asked which observation justified that description.

**繁中 06**

一名見證人問團隊是否偏袒。陳念回答，不捏造事實不代表冷漠。真正重要的是程序能否保護那個故事不方便被說出口的人。余知沒有打斷，逐字記下原問題。下一張表單把動作寫成「低語」，見證人卻從未同意這項操作。官方備忘錄形容計畫「有成果的」，陳念不願把形容詞當作證據。摘要以「心理上的」形容情況，陳念追問這種說法有何觀察根據。

**EN 07**

Even after the group agreed on the immediate step, another question remained. The answer would not arrive through a dramatic announcement. Someone would have to return to the place, compare the earlier notes, and accept the possibility that the next piece of evidence might spoil an attractive theory. Chen Nian prepared to do exactly that. Yu Zhi had learned how quickly an audience could mistake silence for guilt. He chose to protect an original recording before saying anything that might become another headline. The instructions used the verb retain without naming who would carry the risk; Chen Nian circled that gap. On the next form, the action field read revise, but the witness had never approved the operation. A handwritten label read voyage, but no one could say who had written it there.

**繁中 07**

即使大家同意眼前步驟，另一個問題仍在。答案不會靠戲劇性公告降臨；有人得返回現場、比對舊筆記，並接受新證據可能推翻漂亮理論。陳念準備做的正是這件事。余知早知道觀眾多容易把沉默當成有罪。他選擇先保護原始錄影，再說可能變成標題的話。指示裡用了「保留」這個動詞，卻沒說誰承擔風險；陳念圈出這項缺漏。下一張表單把動作寫成「修改」，見證人卻從未同意這項操作。手寫標籤寫著「航程」，卻沒有人知道是誰留下的。
