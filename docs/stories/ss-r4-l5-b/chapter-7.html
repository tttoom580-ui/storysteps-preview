The Atlas in a Backyard

山海邊境，失蹤者仍在等待 · 第 7 / 25 章 · 4000–5000 · 英中對照 · 校訂中

The upper reef led to an old school whose backyard had become a public garden. Its principal, a retired scholar of colonial history, kept maps inside a spacious wooden cabinet. Some were drawn before the border republic replaced the sea governor, when a republican council first promised local communities the right to water. Others documented the acquisition of mineral rights by a private company. Ren could comprehend the coastline changes, but the company's coded transport routes confused him. Gu found a scrap of paper lodged in the cabinet's spine. It listed calcium tests performed beside a sealed maintenance tunnel. The results implied that fresh water had penetrated far under the salt cliffs. A sophomore student helping in the garden showed them a stack of notebooks from the former science club. The notebooks had been rescued from a rubbish fire because the students thought old experiments still deserved a place in the school.

上方礁道通往一所老學校，後院已成了公共花園。校長是退休的殖民史研究者，把地圖收藏在寬敞的木櫃裡。部分畫於邊境共和國取代海督之前，也就是共和議會初次承諾社區享有用水權的年代；另一些記錄私人企業取得礦權的經過。任海能理解海岸線的變化，卻看不懂企業加密的運輸路線。顧遠從櫃子的書脊間找到一張紙片，上面列有密封維修隧道旁的鈣含量測試。結果暗示淡水已深入鹽崖底下。一名在花園幫忙的大二學生帶他們看舊科學社留下的整疊筆記。學生們認為過去的實驗值得保存在學校，因此從垃圾火中搶救了筆記。

Every notebook described the same unusual process: limestone samples dissolved faster near the seventh channel than near the open sea. The principal offered a small bonus to anyone who could restore the missing pages, but warned that the job was risky. A surveillance camera had been installed after someone tried to remove the school archives. Ren disapproved of treating students as suspects; Gu asked instead whether the camera footage could establish when the cabinet was opened. They obtained permission to inspect it with the principal present. The images showed a uniformed worker entering after midnight and leaving with a narrow box. He did not steal every record. He took only the pages naming the tunnel contractor. That selection had a troubling implication: the contractor mattered enough for someone to conceal its name, yet no one had tried to destroy the measurements themselves.

每本筆記都描述同一個異常現象：靠近第七水道的石灰岩樣本，比外海樣本溶解得更快。校長懸賞一小筆獎金給能找回缺頁的人，同時警告這工作有風險。此前有人想拿走學校檔案，學校才裝了監視攝影機。任海不贊成把學生都當成嫌疑人；顧遠則問，影像是否能確認櫃子何時被打開。他們得到校長許可，在校長在場時查看。畫面顯示一名穿制服的工人午夜後進入，帶走一只狹長盒子。他沒有偷走所有紀錄，只取走寫有隧道承包商名字的頁面。這種選擇有令人不安的含意：承包商的名稱值得某人刻意隱藏，但測量結果本身卻沒被銷毀。

The student led them through a corridor lined with faded portraits. One photograph showed Ren Lin receiving a symbolic medal for a science project two years earlier. She stood beside a school team that had built a simple public transit map for families with limited mobility. Ren remembered teasing her about the enormous chunk of stone displayed as her prize. Now he saw that the stone came from the same tunnel described in the notebooks. He asked the principal to keep the photograph safe, resisting the urge to carry it away. Gu watched his face and offered neither advice nor a promise. Instead he placed the recovered paper inside a transparent sleeve and signed the archive record. Ren felt the tension in his shoulders release. His sister had left more than one trail, and people who were not related to her had valued her work before she vanished.

那名學生帶他們穿過掛滿褪色肖像的走廊。一張照片顯示，任霖兩年前因科學專題獲頒象徵性獎章。她站在一支校園隊伍旁，當時他們製作了協助行動不便家庭的簡易大眾運輸地圖。任海想起自己曾笑她把一大塊石頭當獎品；如今才看出石頭來自筆記所述的同一條隧道。他請校長妥善保存照片，忍住直接帶走的衝動。顧遠看著他的表情，沒有給建議，也不作承諾，而是把找到的紙片放進透明保護套並簽署歸檔紀錄。任海感到肩膀放鬆了些。妹妹留下的不只一條線索，甚至在她失蹤以前，就有與她沒有血緣關係的人珍惜她的工作。

Before leaving, they traced the numbered freight routes against the village maps and identified a service road ending at a checkpoint. The route passed an abandoned quarry where the company had stored supplies before the mining license expired. Gu wondered whether the missing names could survive in old purchase orders. The principal agreed to introduce them to the checkpoint librarian, provided that the students' names remained confidential. That afternoon, the two men walked together through the garden. Gu was tired but refused help until Ren asked instead whether they should share the weight of the instruments. He accepted that arrangement. They spoke about the difference between treating someone as helpless and making a practical division of labor. The conversation was modest, yet their growing trust felt almost as important as the road on the map. Both would be needed to cross the next border.

離校前，他們把編號貨運路線與村圖重疊，找出一條通往檢查站的維修道路。道路經過廢礦坑，企業曾在採礦許可到期前把補給品存放在那裡。顧遠想，消失的名字或許仍留在舊採購單上。校長答應介紹他們認識檢查站的圖書管理員，條件是保護學生姓名。那天下午，兩人並肩穿過花園。顧遠疲倦，卻一直拒絕幫忙，直到任海換個方式問，是否該分攤儀器重量。他接受了。他們談起把別人當成無能者，與務實分工究竟有何不同。這番對話很平淡，但逐漸建立的信任，幾乎和地圖上的道路一樣重要。下一道邊界需要兩者兼具才能跨越。

The principal also showed them a letter from Ren Lin to the science club. She had urged the students to test old maps against new measurements, even when the drawings came from famous scholars. Ren read the letter in silence. His sister had not been careless about the world; she had taught others to question it. Gu asked permission to borrow a copy rather than the original, and the principal provided one. As they left the school, Ren saw younger students taking their own measurements of the garden well. The search no longer looked like a road ending at a single locked door. It looked like many people learning to keep doors open.

校長也展示任霖寫給科學社的一封信。她鼓勵學生拿新測值檢驗舊地圖，即使圖出自名家也一樣。任海默默讀完。妹妹不是對世界粗心的人；她教別人質疑它。顧遠請求借用副本而非原件，校長便提供一份。離開學校時，任海看見低年級學生正在量花園水井。搜尋不再像是一條通往單一上鎖門的路，而像許多人正學會讓門保持開放。
