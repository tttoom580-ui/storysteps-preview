The First Witness

山海邊境，失蹤者仍在等待 · 第 5 / 25 章 · 4000–5000 · 英中對照

The southern village of Reedfall had survived a recession by selling dried herbs to travelers and keeping its own emergency grain. Its residents were mostly small farmers, whom wealthy visitors dismissed as peasants. A massive new canal now divided their fields, and a retail company offered cash for the land at prices that sounded generous until the village accountant calculated the long-term loss. The council's democrat representative argued that the community needed a constitutional right to ask where its water went. At the meeting, a merchant accused the fishermen of greed, and a fisherman answered that no incentive could replace a living river. Ren found his sister's pencil mark on a public measuring post just below the threshold where water became dangerous. A witness named Tala remembered that the mark had disappeared the morning after black vehicles crossed the bank. Someone had painted over it in the night.

南方蘆落村靠販售乾燥藥草給旅人，以及自己儲備緊急穀糧，熬過經濟衰退。居民多是小農，富裕訪客輕蔑地稱他們為農民。如今一條龐大新運河切開田地，一家零售企業出價買地，聽來很優渥，直到村會計算出長期損失。議會中的民主派代表主張，社區應享有憲法保障的權利，詢問自己的水去了哪裡。會上商人指責漁民貪婪，漁民回答，沒有任何誘因能取代活著的河。任海在公用測量柱上找到妹妹的鉛筆刻記，位置就在水位開始危險的臨界值下。名叫塔拉的證人記得，黑色車隊過岸後的早晨，那個刻記便消失了。有人在夜裡把它漆掉。

Tala refused to speak until she knew how the testimony would be used. Gu explained that they would not recruit her into a secret faction or force her to accuse anyone. They would record only what she saw, compare it with a dated photograph, and invite an independent observer to check their method. Her first episode was simple: she had heard a metal rattle, watched a worker climb the measuring post, and seen the mark covered with paint. A local teenager then produced an image from the village's annual celebration; the old mark was clearly visible in the background. Ren had to resist exaggeration when he described the discovery. The picture proved that someone had altered the post, not who had ordered the alteration. Gu tapped the image gently. “A modest truth is substantial when it can stand on its own,” he said. Tala finally gave them her signed statement.

塔拉起初拒絕發言，直到弄清證詞將如何使用。顧遠解釋，他們不會招募她加入秘密派系，也不會逼她指控任何人；只記錄她親眼所見，與有日期的照片比對，再邀獨立觀察員檢驗方法。她描述的事件很簡單：她聽見金屬碰撞聲，看見工人爬上測量柱，接著記號被油漆蓋住。當地一名青少年拿出村莊年度慶典的照片，原本的刻記清楚出現在背景中。任海描述這發現時，努力不誇大。照片只能證明有人動過柱子，不能證明誰下令。顧遠輕點圖片說：「一個樸素的真相，只要能獨立站得住腳，就有分量。」塔拉終於簽下證詞。

That evening a storm smashed a temporary gate and sent water rushing toward the lower homes. Gu and Ren joined a volunteer fleet of small boats to move residents to the school on higher ground. One child lost a bag of medicine, and Mara returned through the rain to fetch it while the others held the boats steady. Ren's knowledge of the old bank gave them a quick path, though the last section was nearly washed away. They used a simple tactic: place a rope across the shallowest channel, then guide each family through without allowing anyone to rush. By midnight everybody was accounted for. The rescue did not resolve the missing surveyors' fate, but it showed what the village could accomplish when people shared reliable information. In the morning the council issued a provisional verdict: the crossing was unsafe, and the disputed measuring post had to be preserved for an independent investigation.

當晚暴風雨撞壞一座臨時水閘，水衝向低處住家。顧遠與任海加入由小船組成的志工船隊，將居民送到高處學校。一名孩子遺失藥袋，瑪拉冒雨回去拿，其餘人則穩住船身。任海熟悉舊河岸，找到一條較快的路，雖然最後一段幾乎被水沖走。他們採取簡單策略：把繩子拉過最淺的水道，依序引導各家人通過，不讓任何人爭先。午夜時所有人都點名到齊。救援沒有解開測量隊的命運，卻證明村民分享可靠資訊便能做到什麼。翌日議會作出暫時裁定：渡口不安全，有爭議的測量柱必須保存，以供獨立調查。

The next day, Ren and Gu helped publish a map of the safe ridge path before taking the witness records farther inland. Some residents demanded that the canal office be destroyed at once. Others feared losing jobs if the project stopped. Ren heard both sides without accepting that their only choices were obedience or rebellion. He proposed an open meeting that would publish every measurement, with personal details removed. Tala agreed to attend, and the young photographer offered to keep duplicate records. Their first achievement was small beside the vast mystery, but it was real: a dangerous route had been closed, a safe one marked, and an erased measurement restored to public sight. As dusk settled, Gu told Ren that finding his sister might take longer than any official deadline. Ren looked toward the mountains. “Then we will measure the days honestly,” he replied, “and keep moving.”

隔天，任海與顧遠先協助公布安全山脊路線，才帶著證人紀錄繼續往內陸。部分居民要求立刻摧毀運河辦公室，另一些則擔心工程停工會失去工作。任海聽完兩邊，卻不接受他們只能在服從和反叛之間二選一。他提議召開公開會議，刊出所有測值，但移除個人資料。塔拉同意出席，那位年輕攝影者也願意保存副本。相較於巨大的謎團，這是很小的成就，卻真實：危險路線封閉了、安全路線標好了，被擦掉的測量刻記重回公眾視野。黃昏時顧遠告訴任海，尋找妹妹可能比任何官方期限都久。任海望向山。「那我們就誠實計算每一天，」他回答，「繼續走。」

The village's warning spread farther than the searchers expected. Families from the upper slopes reported widespread changes in their wells, and several supplied dated notes rather than rumors. Gu taught the volunteers to mark a source and time beside each observation. Ren was struck by how quickly people learned a method when it answered a need they already felt. At the next public meeting, he thanked Tala without placing her above the other witnesses. The point of preserving her statement was not to create a lone hero. It was to make it possible for the next frightened person to speak and be taken seriously.

村裡的警示傳得比搜尋者預期更遠。上坡家庭回報井水出現廣泛變化，幾戶還提供有日期的筆記，而非傳聞。顧遠教志工替每項觀察標上來源和時間。任海驚訝地發現，只要方法能回應人們切身需要，大家就學得很快。下一次公開會議，他感謝塔拉，卻沒有把她捧成比其他證人更偉大的人。保存她證詞的意義，不是塑造孤獨英雄，而是讓下一個害怕的人也敢開口，並被認真對待。
