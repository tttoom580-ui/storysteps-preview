The City Beneath the Cliff

山海邊境，失蹤者仍在等待 · 第 9 / 25 章 · 4000–5000 · 英中對照 · 校訂中

The hidden road ended at a city built inside a limestone pyramid of terraces. On the lowest level, an ass pulled a cart full of salt bricks while a bodyguard checked visitors at the boundary. His orders were to discriminate between residents eligible for water coupons and temporary workers who were not. Ren objected that thirst did not care about a person's legal residence. The guard said he was only following the current version of the regulations. Gu asked to see the written rule and found that the clause about temporary workers had been added three weeks ago. A local investigator named Bao, passionate about fair distribution, invited them into her office. She had been collecting complaints from vulnerable families but lacked a map showing where the missing water had gone. Ren unfolded his chart, and for the first time its blank spaces became useful to someone beyond his own search.

隱藏道路止於一座建在階梯狀石灰岩金字塔裡的城市。最下層，一頭驢拉著滿載鹽磚的車，保鑣則在邊界檢查旅客。他奉命區分有資格領取配水券的居民，以及不符合條件的短期工人。任海抗議，乾渴不會在乎一個人的合法居住身分。守衛說自己只是遵守現行版本的規定。顧遠要求看書面條文，發現排除臨時工的條款三週前才加入。一位名叫包晴、熱衷公平分配的地方調查員請他們進辦公室。她一直蒐集弱勢家庭的申訴，卻缺少標示失水去向的地圖。任海攤開圖紙，第一次發現那些空白處也能幫助自己搜尋以外的人。

Bao's documents comprised invoices, a maintenance roster, and a medical log from the lower clinics. The roster named a repair crew that had entered the restricted channel after the official evacuation date. One worker reported a fluid leaking through a crack patched with blue resin. Gu recognized the patch from a photograph taken before his engineering license was withdrawn. It had been designed to incorporate a temporary pressure release into a damaged wall. “Temporary,” he repeated, because the repair should have lasted days, not years. Ren compared the names on the roster with the missing team's list and found an unfamiliar signature beside his sister's initials. Bao explained that a witness could still be alive but afraid to make a public statement. Together they designed a confidential meeting that would protect that person until the testimony could be checked independently.

包晴的文件包括發票、維修排班表及下城診所的醫療紀錄。排班表列出一支在官方所稱撤離日期後仍進入禁區水道的修理隊。某工人回報一種液體從裂縫滲出，裂縫用藍樹脂補過。顧遠認出這種修補方式，他失去工程執照前的一張照片裡就有。那原本是為了把臨時減壓口整合進受損牆面。「臨時，」他重複，因為這種修補應撐幾天，而非好幾年。任海把班表姓名和失蹤隊名單比對，發現妹妹姓名縮寫旁有陌生簽名。包晴解釋，證人可能還活著，只是害怕公開發言。他們一起設計保密會面，在證詞得以獨立檢驗前保護當事人。

Outside the clinic, a therapist treating exhausted mine workers recognized the shape of Gu's old scar and asked whether he needed a seat. He thanked her but declined, then accepted a cup of water. Ren noticed that he rarely allowed himself any kindness, as though losing spiritual ability had made every offer of help a test he might fail. They walked past a stall selling sandals and bright souvenirs to visitors who came to admire the city's unusual walls. Gu bought a cheap leather patch for his bag, not a miracle cure for his leg. “You do not have to undo what happened to you before you are useful again,” Ren said, surprising himself. Gu was quiet a long time before he answered. “I have been useful. I am trying to believe that does not require being unhurt.” The exchange mattered more than the purchase.

診所外，一名照顧疲憊礦工的治療師認出顧遠舊傷的形狀，問他需不需要坐下。他道謝並婉拒，卻接受了一杯水。任海發現他很少容許自己受人照顧，彷彿失去靈力後，每個援手都是可能讓自己不及格的考試。他們走過賣涼鞋與鮮豔紀念品的攤位，那些東西賣給前來欣賞奇特城牆的遊客。顧遠買的是替背包補洞的便宜皮片，不是能治腿的神藥。「你不必先撤銷發生過的事，才能再次有用，」任海說，連自己也吃了一驚。顧遠沉默很久才回答：「我一直都有用。我只是正在學習相信，有用不代表不能受傷。」這番交換比買東西更重要。

At the meeting, Bao produced a valid entry pass used by an unnamed maintenance worker. The permit's serial number matched the code on Ren's sister's corrected transfer record. She might have traveled toward a shelter beneath the laboratory rather than the open sea. Ren wanted to leave immediately, but Bao asked him to wait for a guide who knew the lower tunnels. The city had already lost three workers to sudden currents; nobody wanted a fourth person lost for lack of preparation. Gu made a careful list of ropes, lamps, breathing masks, and witnesses. Ren added a column for people who must be notified if the search failed. It hurt to write those names, but they agreed that courage without accountability could be another form of selfishness. That night they slept above the investigator's office, with the route to the hidden laboratory marked in blue.

會議上，包晴拿出一張由無名維修工使用的有效通行證。證件序號與任霖被更改的轉運紀錄代碼吻合。她可能往實驗室下方的避難所移動，而非到外海。任海想立刻出發，包晴卻請他等熟悉下方隧道的嚮導。城市已因突發水流失去三名工人，沒有人想再因準備不足而失去第四人。顧遠仔細列出繩索、燈、呼吸面罩與見證人。任海加上一欄，寫下若搜尋失敗必須通知的人。寫下那些姓名很痛苦，但他們同意，沒有責任感的勇敢也可能是另一種自私。那晚兩人在調查員辦公室樓上睡下，通往隱藏實驗室的路線已用藍色標好。

Bao warned that the corrected roster might lead to retaliation against low-ranking workers. She offered to introduce the men to an independent archive where the original records could be stored safely. Gu agreed and suggested making two verified copies in different buildings. Ren helped the clerk describe every page without guessing what the missing signatures meant. Their careful work frustrated a reporter seeking an instant accusation, but Bao thanked them. She had seen too many investigations collapse after an exciting claim proved false. By the time the archive closed, the corrected dates were no longer a fragile secret held by one frightened person.

包晴提醒，修正過的班表可能讓基層工人遭到報復。她願意帶兩人去獨立檔案館，安全保管原件。顧遠同意，建議把兩份核驗過的副本分存不同建築。任海協助書記描述每頁內容，沒有猜測失蹤簽名代表什麼。一名追求即時指控的記者對這種小心作業感到不耐，包晴卻向他們道謝。她看過太多調查因刺激的主張被證明錯誤而崩潰。檔案館關門時，修正日期已不再是單一恐懼者握在手裡的脆弱秘密。
