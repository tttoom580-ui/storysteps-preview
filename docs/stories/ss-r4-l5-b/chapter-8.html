A Permit with No Exit

山海邊境，失蹤者仍在等待 · 第 8 / 25 章 · 4000–5000 · 英中對照 · 校訂中

用互動版查詞、朗讀與收藏

The checkpoint sat between bare hills where the last trees had grown ragged from salt. A gathering of travelers waited beneath a sign listing visa rules, and a guard announced that all entry permits were suspended because of a theoretical security threat. Nobody could explain what the threat was. A merchant complained that the delay would create chaos in the city market; a shepherd pointed out that families already faced a shortage of drinking water. Ren asked for a written reason, but the guard said the border office was not required to provide one whatsoever. Gu noticed a second queue leading to a small salon, where a clerk issued visitor passes to customers of the mining company. “So the rule has flexibility for certain people,” he observed. The guard offered no answer. Rather than force their way through, the two men recorded the unequal procedures in the travelers' presence.

檢查站位於光禿山丘之間，僅存的樹木因鹽風而破敗凌亂。旅客聚集在列有簽證規則的告示牌下，守衛宣布因為一項理論上的安全威脅，所有入境許可都已暫停，但沒人說得清威脅是什麼。商人抱怨延誤會讓城裡市場陷入混亂，牧羊人則指出各家已面臨飲水短缺。任海要求書面理由，守衛說邊境辦公室無論如何都沒有提供理由的義務。顧遠注意到另一支隊伍排向小沙龍，礦業公司的顧客能在那裡領到訪客證。「原來規則對某些人還有彈性，」他說。守衛沒有回答。兩人沒有硬闖，而是在旅客見證下記下差別待遇。

They entered the salon as ordinary customers, ordered tea, and asked the owner to explain the passes. She had inherited the business from her mother and was tired of officials pretending its license entitled them to control her guests. A strategic contract required her to report the names of people seeking passage, but it also allowed her to display the approved categories publicly. Gu studied the spectrum of permits, from medical transport to research visits, and found a clause for independent safety inspectors. The owner pointed to a dusty register: the missing survey team had used exactly that category eleven days earlier. Its entries had been crossed out with a dark line. Ren's first reaction was anger, but his years of mapmaking helped him slow down and read what remained. Each erased name had been transferred to a different terminal code, suggesting the people had not simply left the region.

他們以一般顧客身分進入沙龍，點茶並請店主解釋通行證。店主繼承母親的生意，早已厭倦官員假裝營業執照讓他們有權控制店內客人。一份策略性合約要求她通報申請通行者姓名，但也允許公開展示核准類別。顧遠查看從醫療運送到研究訪問的完整許可類別，找到「獨立安全檢查員」一項。店主指向覆滿灰塵的登記簿：失蹤測量隊十一天前用的正是這一類許可。那些紀錄被深色線條劃掉。任海先是憤怒，但多年繪圖經驗讓他放慢速度，辨讀殘存文字。每個被刪除的名字都轉往另一個終端代碼，顯示他們不是單純離開該地區。

Ren asked whether they could copy the pages. The owner agreed only if their work would minimize risk to people whose names remained in the register. They photographed the relevant dates and concealed unrelated travelers' details. A local singer offered a tribute to the missing team that evening, but Gu suggested they first clarify what the witnesses actually knew. Myths could comfort a frightened crowd while quietly replacing the difficult truth. An elderly clerk then showed them a red line on the permit map, shaped like a vein along the cliffs. The line connected the checkpoint to an abandoned laboratory without crossing the regular border. The clerk said it had once been used for medicine, not miners. Ren considered the possibility that the missing people had been moved there by order rather than accident. Still, he wrote a question mark beside the route, not an accusation.

任海問能否複製那些頁面。店主答應，但條件是他們必須盡量降低登記簿裡其他人的風險。他們拍下相關日期並遮住無關旅客資料。當晚一名當地歌手提出要為失蹤隊獻唱致意，但顧遠建議先釐清證人真正知道的事。神話能安慰恐懼的人群，也可能悄悄取代艱難的真相。接著一名老書記在通行圖上指出一條紅線，像血管般沿著海崖延伸。這條線連接檢查站與廢棄實驗室，不經正規邊境。書記說它曾用來運送藥物，不是礦工。任海開始想，失蹤者也許是奉命被移送，而非意外受難。然而，他在路線旁寫的是問號，不是指控。

At dawn they submitted a formal inspection request through the public register instead of buying a special pass. The procedure might stall them for a day, but it produced a document the authorities could not deny receiving. Mara found them a room above the checkpoint kitchen, where a family shared soup with stranded travelers. Ren was frustrated enough to consider turning back to the ridge; Gu reminded him that a safe search could not depend on breaking every gate ahead. “We have more information than yesterday,” he said. “That is progress even when our feet remain still.” Ren looked at the numbered route and realized that somebody had continued to maintain it after its official closure. The mystery was no longer whether the hidden corridor existed. It was who had kept it open, and whether the missing surveyors were still somewhere inside.

天亮時，他們沒有花錢購買特殊通行證，而是透過公開登記簿提出正式檢查申請。程序可能讓他們多等一天，但留下了當局無法否認已收件的文件。瑪拉替他們在檢查站廚房樓上找到房間，一家人正為受困旅客分送熱湯。任海挫折得想折返山脊；顧遠提醒他，安全搜尋不能靠逢閘必破。「我們比昨天知道得多了，」他說，「即使雙腳暫時不動，這也是進展。」任海望著編號路線，明白有人在官方宣布封閉後，仍持續維護它。謎團已不再是隱藏走廊是否存在，而是誰讓它保持開放，以及失蹤測量員是否仍在裡面。

A foreign merchant described the rules as alien to ordinary travelers, and his complaint prompted a useful question about comprehension. Could people understand the permits without hiring a professional adviser? The salon owner admitted that some categories appeared only in technical language. Ren offered to draw a visual guide with pictures and clear dates, while Gu checked that it did not change the meaning of the law. Travelers helped test the guide before it was posted. The exercise did not shorten their delay, but it made a confusing rule easier to challenge. Ren felt less trapped when he saw others using the map to ask their own questions.

一位外地商人說，規則對一般旅客而言陌生得像異世界，這句抱怨引出關於理解能力的重要問題：人們不聘專業顧問，能看懂通行證嗎？沙龍店主承認，部分類別只用技術語言撰寫。任海提議畫一張附圖片與清楚日期的視覺指南，顧遠則確認它不會改變法律原意。貼出前，旅客先協助試讀。這件事沒有縮短等待，卻讓令人困惑的規則更容易受到質疑。看到別人用地圖提出自己的問題，任海覺得不再那麼受困。
