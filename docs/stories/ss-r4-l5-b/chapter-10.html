玻璃下的乾旱

The Drought Under Glass

山海邊境，失蹤者仍在等待 · 第 10 / 25 章 · 4000–5000 · 英中對照 · 校訂中

The laboratory stood at the edge of the wilderness, its glass roofs reflecting a region suffering from drought. Director Sen of the Coastal Institute greeted them with a preliminary report rather than a warm welcome. It described a variable pressure system designed to move water from the mountains to the crowded coast. A rational engineer might admire the design, but the report gave no accounting of how much water had been removed from the upper villages. Ren asked Sen to cite the original measurements. She admitted the institute had received only descriptive summaries, not the raw readings. Gu proposed collaboration: the staff could run fresh tests while his team compared them with independent gauges. Sen hesitated. A corporate sponsor could compel the institute to stop work, and her researchers depended on their salaries. Ren promised no protection he could not provide; he offered to publish the process, including the institute's limits.

實驗室坐落在荒野邊緣，玻璃屋頂映著飽受乾旱的地區。海岸研究所的沈主任迎接他們時，給的是初步報告，不是熱情問候。報告描述一套把山區水轉移到擁擠海岸的可變壓力系統。理性的工程師或許會欣賞設計，但報告沒有核算上游村落究竟失去多少水。任海要求沈主任引用原始測量資料。她承認研究所只收到描述性摘要，沒有原始讀數。顧遠提議合作：研究員做新測試，他們則用獨立量測器交叉比對。沈主任猶豫了，企業贊助者可能迫使研究所停止工作，研究員又需要薪水。任海沒有許下自己無法提供的保護，只提出公開整個程序，包括研究所面臨的限制。

During lunch a technician cooked vegetables on a charcoal grill behind the lab. He worried that a food allergy would force one of the visiting researchers to skip the meal, so the staff labeled every dish. A counselor who had been helping displaced workers joined the table and explained why uncertainty often overwhelmed people more than difficult facts. She described digestion problems that appeared after families were moved into crowded temporary housing. The information did not prove a disease caused by the canal, but it gave the laboratory another careful question to investigate. Someone joked that their expedition sounded like a thriller, complete with secret tunnels and a missing crew. Gu replied that the genre was less entertaining when the fear belonged to real people. Ren found himself grateful for the comment. The work could be exciting, but the people they sought were never a puzzle arranged for their pleasure.

午餐時，一名技術員在實驗室後方的炭烤架上烤蔬菜。他擔心某位來訪研究員有食物過敏，便請大家替每道菜標示成分。一位協助流離失所工人的諮商師加入餐桌，解釋為何不確定感往往比艱難的事實更讓人不堪負荷。她描述家庭搬進擁擠臨時住宅後出現的消化問題。這些資訊不能證明運河引發疾病，卻給實驗室另一個值得謹慎研究的問題。有人開玩笑說，他們的旅程像驚悚小說，有秘密隧道和失蹤隊伍。顧遠回答，當恐懼屬於真實的人時，這種類型便沒那麼有趣了。任海感激這句話。調查也許令人興奮，但他們尋找的人從來不是為了供人解謎而安排的玩具。

The team's main discovery came from a bulk sample of sediment collected at the tunnel terminal. Under a lens, the material contained an unnatural arrangement of blue crystals. Gu recognized it as a pressure-memory compound used in old spirit tools, but its capacity was limited. It could preserve one change in force, not a voice, an image, or a person's thoughts. A radical rumor had claimed the crystals could trap living souls; the evidence did not support that idea. Sen helped them record the actual pattern: a gate had been held partly open during a storm, followed by a second release much later. Ren exclaimed when the timing matched his sister's last reading. They repeated the test three times to avoid treating coincidence as proof. The result did not tell them where the team was, but it confirmed that the official flood timeline omitted a crucial event.

團隊最重要的發現來自隧道終端採集的大量沉積物樣本。鏡片下，材料含有排列不自然的藍色晶體。顧遠認出那是舊式靈械使用的壓力記憶材料，但容量有限，只能保存一次受力變化，不能留下聲音、影像或人的想法。激進傳聞曾宣稱晶體能困住活人的靈魂，證據卻不支持這種說法。沈主任協助記錄實際模式：風暴時水閘被維持在半開狀態，過了很久又發生第二次釋壓。任海驚呼，因為時間與妹妹最後的讀值吻合。他們把測試重做三次，避免把巧合當證據。結果不能告訴他們隊伍在哪裡，卻證實官方洪水時間線漏了一件關鍵事件。

Before the men left, Sen opened a locked cabinet containing the institute's earliest design. It showed that Gate Seven could be shut only if another valve was opened to protect the coastal hospital's water supply. The equations were clear, but the practical route to that second valve was missing from every recent map. A technician remembered a storage passage under a wooden refuge near the sea wall. The document was incomplete, so Gu insisted on checking the structure rather than guessing where the door might be. Ren saw how their questions had grown: no longer only who had vanished, but what pressure had trapped them and who else would be endangered by a careless rescue. Sen signed the test results and gave both men copies. For the first time, a recognized institution had recorded evidence that contradicted the evacuation report, while admitting precisely what it still did not know.

兩人離開前，沈主任打開上鎖櫃子，拿出研究所最早的設計圖。圖上顯示，要關閉第七閘，必須同時打開另一個閥，以保護海岸醫院的供水。方程式很清楚，但通往第二閥的實際道路，在近代地圖上全都消失了。一名技術員記得，海堤旁的木造避難所下面有條儲藏通道。文件並不完整，顧遠堅持實地確認建築，而非猜測門的位置。任海發現他們的問題變多了：不只是誰失蹤，更包括什麼壓力困住他們，以及魯莽救援會危及誰。沈主任在測試結果上簽名，交給兩人各一份。這是第一次，有受認可的機構正式記錄與撤離報告相矛盾的證據，同時誠實承認仍不知道的部分。

At the institute door, Sen asked Ren whether he would return with questions once the missing team was found. He had expected a scientist to care only about the next result, and the invitation surprised him. “Yes,” he answered. “A rescue cannot make the drought disappear.” Gu added that equipment tests should become routine, not a special favor granted to desperate families. Sen wrote down both requests and promised to bring them to the board. The men left carrying results that could be checked, along with a new sense that the investigation might repair more than a single broken gate.

在研究所門口，沈主任問任海，若找到失蹤隊，是否還會帶著問題回來。他原以為科學家只在乎下一個結果，這邀請令他意外。「會，」他回答，「救援不能讓乾旱消失。」顧遠補充，設備測試應成為例行工作，不是給絕望家庭的特殊恩惠。沈主任記下兩項要求，承諾帶到董事會。兩人帶著可供檢驗的結果離開，也逐漸感覺，這場調查可能修補的不只是一扇壞閘。
