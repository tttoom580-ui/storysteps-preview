七名失蹤者的聽證

The Hearing of Seven Names

A lawsuit from the canal company reached the refuge before the rescue permit did. The firm claimed that the searchers intended to steal valuable crystal equipment and demanded a temporary ban on entering its property. Ren was furious that someone could frame the effort as a fraud, but Bao warned him that anger would not reverse a legal order. They attended an emergency session in a small court whose central room resembled an arena. A local celebrity had come to watch, while residents from the adjoining residential district waited outside. The company's lawyer displayed a portfolio of photographs that seemed to show damaged gates, yet none included a date or a clear location. Gu asked to poke holes in the argument with one careful question: what evidence connected any of the damage to their work? The lawyer refused to identify a witness.

運河公司的訴訟文件比救援許可更早送達避難所。公司聲稱搜尋者意圖竊取昂貴晶體設備，要求暫時禁止進入其土地。任海很憤怒，有人竟把救援說成詐騙，但包晴提醒他，憤怒不能撤銷法律命令。他們到一間中央廳形似競技場的小法院參加緊急聽證。一位地方名人前來旁聽，鄰近住宅區居民則等在外面。公司的律師展示一疊照片資料集，看似記錄損壞的水閘，卻沒有日期或清楚位置。顧遠提出一個細緻的問題，試著找出論點的漏洞：有什麼證據能把這些損害與他們的行動連起來？律師拒絕指明證人。

The judge asked both parties to state their intent. Ren said he sought seven missing adults and would accept independent supervision of any hazardous entry. Gu produced the institute's testing record, which showed the danger of opening the wrong valve. A doctor submitted a prescription log proving that the coastal hospital needed stable water pressure for medicine and nutrition services. The company argued that its ultimate duty was to protect property, not travelers. Bao answered that safety and property were not necessarily enemies, provided that nobody was allowed to act without records. The judge postponed a final ruling and authorized a limited external inspection. It was a partial victory, but it kept the route open. Outside, a stranger tried to shove a camera toward Ren's face and demanded to know whether he was prepared to sacrifice patients to save his sister. Ren refused the false choice and repeated the approved safety conditions.

法官要求雙方說明意圖。任海表示要尋找七名失蹤成年人，並願意接受獨立監督，才進入危險區域。顧遠提交研究所測試紀錄，顯示打開錯誤閥門的危險。醫師遞交處方紀錄，證明海岸醫院的藥物與營養服務需要穩定水壓。公司主張它的最終義務是保護財產，不是旅人。包晴回答，只要任何行動都有紀錄，安全與財產未必相互衝突。法官延後最終裁決，允許有限度的外部檢查。這只是部分勝利，卻保住了路線。庭外有陌生人把攝影機硬推向任海臉前，問他是否準備犧牲病人來救妹妹。任海拒絕這種錯誤的二選一，重述獲准的安全條件。

Back at the refuge, the volunteers arranged masks on a rack. The roster deliberately used the plural word 'teams': one for air testing, one for evacuation, and one for medical transfer. A technician selected a random valve number and asked what would happen if the crew opened it without checking the current. Gu explained the calculation carefully, tracing the possible pressure change on a chalkboard. The demonstration seemed to depress the mood until Jian reminded them that recognizing a danger made it easier to prevent. The wheelchair platform passed its final brake test. Ren studied the names on the rescue manifest and added a blank space for each person who might need a separate route home. His sister's name was among them, but not printed larger than the others. A new promise had taken shape: the search would treat every missing life as worthy of rescue.

回到避難所，志工把面罩排上架子。排班表刻意使用「隊伍」的複數形式：一組測空氣、一組負責撤離、一組執行醫療轉送。一名技術員隨機挑一個閥門號碼，問如果不檢查水流就打開，會發生什麼事。顧遠仔細解釋計算，在黑板畫出可能的壓力變化。示範令氣氛一度低落，直到老簡提醒眾人，辨識危險才能更容易預防。輪椅平台通過最後的煞車測試。任海研究救援名單，替每個可能需要不同返家路線的人留空。他妹妹的名字也在其中，字體並沒有比其他人更大。新的承諾逐漸成形：搜尋將把每一條失蹤生命都視為值得救援。

Before nightfall the court officer delivered the written permit. It authorized testing at the upper shaft but prohibited direct entry until two exits were confirmed. Ren folded the paper and read it twice. He had once imagined that finding his sister would depend on a dramatic act of courage, perhaps a single leap into a dark cavern. Instead, their progress rested on careful documents, frightened witnesses, and people willing to check each other's work. Gu noticed his expression and asked whether the restrictions felt like defeat. “No,” Ren said after considering it. “They feel like the difference between searching for someone and making someone else search for us.” The permit offered no ultimate answer, but it bought a lawful chance to ask the next question. At sunrise they would return to the shaft with a trained team and the strength of several independent records behind them.

天黑前法院人員送來書面許可。它准許在上方豎井測試，但在確認兩條出口之前，禁止直接進入。任海摺起文件又讀了兩遍。他曾想像找到妹妹會靠一場戲劇性的英勇行動，也許是躍進黑洞的一瞬。如今進展卻仰賴仔細文件、害怕的證人，以及願意互相檢查工作的人。顧遠注意到他的神情，問限制是否讓他感覺失敗。任海想過後回答：「不。它讓我看清，尋找別人與讓別人反過來找我們有什麼不同。」許可沒有最終答案，卻給他們合法追問下一個問題的機會。日出時，他們將帶著受訓隊伍和多份獨立紀錄，重返豎井。

One of the witnesses leaving court asked whether the rescue would still continue if the legal dispute lasted another month. Bao answered that the emergency permit was valid for a limited period and must be reviewed, but the team's obligation to preserve records did not expire. Ren realized how much had changed since the first meeting at the chapel. He had once sought a person willing to say yes to every plan. Now he valued people who would explain the exact conditions under which the answer had to be no. Gu read the permit once more, folded it carefully, and placed it in a dry case.

一名離庭證人問，如果法律爭議再拖一個月，救援是否還會繼續。包晴回答，緊急許可有效期間有限，需要重新審查，但保存紀錄的義務不會因此失效。任海發現自禮拜堂初次會面後，自己改變很多。他以前想找對每項計畫都說好的同伴，如今更珍惜會解釋何時必須說不的人。顧遠再讀一遍許可，仔細摺好，放進防潮盒。
