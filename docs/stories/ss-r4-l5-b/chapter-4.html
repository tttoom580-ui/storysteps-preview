The Two Parallel Tides

山海邊境，失蹤者仍在等待 · 第 4 / 25 章 · 4000–5000 · 英中對照

Doctor Isha ran a small marine laboratory above the northern bay. Her faculty colleagues in the capital had asked her to remain neutral, but she believed neutrality meant measuring honestly, not refusing to examine evidence. She showed Gu and Ren two parallel lines on a chart: one from the outer sea and one from the hidden channel beneath the cliffs. Their difference was too large to blame on a changing moon. A narrow pipe of unusual diameter had been installed between the readings, according to an old construction list. Gu began to anticipate a new hazard whenever the channel opened. The doctor warned that a pressure wave could travel faster than a person walking on the bridge. Even a fraction of a minute mattered. Ren asked whether she could give them reassurance that his sister was alive; Isha answered that her readings could uncover a mechanism, but could not identify the people inside it.

伊莎醫師在北灣上方經營一間小型海洋實驗室。都城的學院同事曾要她保持中立，但她認為中立是誠實測量，而不是拒絕檢查證據。她向顧遠和任海展示圖上的兩條平行線：一條來自外海，一條來自海崖下的隱藏水道。兩者差距太大，不能只歸因於月相。依舊施工清單，兩個測點間裝有一根直徑特殊的狹管。顧遠開始預期，只要水道開啟便會產生新的危險。醫師提醒，壓力波傳得比人在檢修橋上行走快，連零點幾分鐘都有差別。任海問她能否保證妹妹還活著。伊莎說，她的數據能揭開機制，不能辨認其中的人。

The provincial department sent a clerk whose main talent seemed to be bureaucracy. He insisted that a fiscal review must come before fieldwork and that the team should write a denial of every unauthorized rumor. Ren asked how many people would become ill while papers moved between desks. Isha showed them a study of mortality in fishing communities after the earlier canal was built. The number was troubling, but it did not prove the new gate caused those deaths. She had also tracked a stress hormone in shellfish, which rose near the pipe's outlet. The pattern was distinctive enough to justify more testing. Gu respected her caution. At the first sign of coincidence he had almost declared the matter solved, but scientific work required distinguishing cause from association. They agreed to present the numbers with their limits stated in plain language.

省府派來一名似乎最擅長官僚程序的書記。他堅持必須先做財政審查才能進行野外工作，還要團隊逐項否認所有未經核准的傳聞。任海問，文件在桌與桌之間流轉時，還會有多少人生病。伊莎出示一份舊運河建成後漁村死亡率的研究，數字令人不安，但不能證明新閘門造成那些死亡。她也追蹤貝類的壓力荷爾蒙，發現排水口附近的數值升高。這種獨特模式足以支持進一步檢測。顧遠尊重她的謹慎。他一看到巧合，幾乎就宣布謎底解開，但科學工作必須區分因果和相關。三人同意用一般人能懂的語言公布數字，並清楚說明限制。

A commuter ferry carried them across the inlet, where an elderly practitioner of coastal medicine had set up a table for people suffering from contaminated water. He offered a plea, not a diagnosis: “Do not enter that tunnel until you know how to get out.” He had treated a worker with a strange chemical burn, but without the patient's consent he would not disclose the name. Isha helped him write an anonymous summary. The injury's location was consistent with maintenance work at a low valve; it might also have another explanation. Ren felt his hope and impatience pulling in opposite directions. Gu drew a simple safety plan: first test the air and current from outside, then establish two independent exits, then ask a qualified rescue team to enter. He refused to gamble a second group of lives to prove a theory about the first.

一艘通勤渡輪載他們穿過海灣，岸邊有位年長的海岸醫療從業者為受污染水源影響的人看診。他提出的不是診斷，而是一個請求：「不知道怎麼出來之前，不要進那條隧道。」他曾治療一名有奇怪化學灼傷的工人，但沒有病人同意，便不會透露姓名。伊莎協助他寫匿名摘要。傷口位置符合在低處閥門維修的情況，也可能有別的解釋。任海感到希望與急躁把他往相反方向拉。顧遠畫出簡單安全計畫：先從外部檢測空氣和水流，再確立兩條獨立出口，最後才請合格救援隊進入。他拒絕為了證明第一群人的遭遇，就拿第二群人的命冒險。

At night Isha unrolled an old map of the province and found a line of water structures omitted from modern publications. An engineer had labeled them as a contingency system intended for a catastrophic storm. Yet the recent measurements suggested the pipes were in regular use. Ren recognized his sister's notation beside a crossing point: 'Check where the missing volume goes.' He was suddenly able to see the question beneath every other question. If someone was redirecting water, where did that water end up, and who had ordered it? He drew an arrow but left its endpoint blank. Gu placed the compass beside it and did the same. Their work had no dramatic revelation that evening, only a sharper definition of what they still needed to find. That restraint was becoming a kind of trust, and it gave Ren strength for the dangerous road ahead.

夜裡伊莎攤開省內舊地圖，找到一串被現代出版品略去的水利設施。某位工程師曾把它們標為應對毀滅性風暴的備援系統；然而最近測值顯示管道平時也在運作。任海認出妹妹寫在一處交會點旁的記號：「查查消失的水量去了哪裡。」他突然看清所有問題底下真正的疑問：如果有人轉移水流，水流最後到哪裡？又是誰下令？他畫了一支箭，卻把終點留白。顧遠把羅盤放在旁邊，也沒有搶先寫上答案。那晚沒有戲劇性的重大發現，只有更清楚的待查事項。這種克制漸漸成了信任，也讓任海有力氣走向前方危險的道路。

Before leaving the laboratory, Ren asked Isha to reassure the families without promising more than science could offer. She agreed to explain the readings at a public meeting, including the limits of her equipment. The clerk objected that an open discussion might damage the department's reputation. Isha answered that a reputation built on silence deserved examination. Ren copied her statement carefully. The first rescue plan they had drawn that day was a plan not to rush; it might appear unsatisfying on a poster, but it preserved the possibility of returning with a full team. Gu folded the map and walked outside into the salt wind.

離開實驗室前，任海請伊莎安撫家屬，但不要許下超過科學所能提供的保證。她同意在公開會議解釋讀數，包括設備限制。書記反對公開討論，說可能傷害部門名聲。伊莎回答，建立在沉默上的名聲本來就值得檢驗。任海仔細抄下她的話。他們當天畫出的第一份救援計畫，其實是一份「不要急」的計畫；它放在海報上也許不討喜，卻能保留帶著完整隊伍平安返回的可能。顧遠摺好地圖，走進鹹風裡。
