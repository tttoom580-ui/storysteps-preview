鹽霧山脊

The Ridge of Salt

山海邊境，失蹤者仍在等待 · 第 2 / 25 章 · 4000–5000 · 英中對照

The path to the inspection bridge crossed a narrow ridge where salt winds cut the grass into pale strips. Gu's old field sensor showed a sudden rise in pressure every ninety minutes. He wanted to convert the readings into a chart before they moved; Ren preferred to reach the destination while daylight remained. Their argument ended when a seabird plunged toward a hollow that had seemed solid from above. Below the chalky surface, water raced through an unseen channel. “An extraordinary shortcut,” Gu said, “for anyone eager to disappear.” They set a torch beside the unsafe edge and marked the stones with white chalk. A supply clerk from the corporate research bureau had warned them not to accept liability for damage to official instruments. Ren answered that they were taking responsibility for their own feet, not the bureau's reputation. This small distinction made Gu smile despite the cold.

通往檢修橋的路橫越一條狹窄山脊，鹹風把草割成蒼白細條。顧遠的舊式野外感測器顯示，壓力每九十分鐘就突然上升。他想先把讀數轉換成圖表，任海則希望趁天亮抵達目的地。爭論在一隻海鳥猛然俯衝向看似堅固的凹地時結束。白堊色地表下，水正沿看不見的通道急奔。「真是條非凡的捷徑，」顧遠說，「適合急著失蹤的人。」他們在危險邊緣立起火炬，並用白粉標出石塊。企業研究局的補給員曾警告他們，不可擅自承擔官方設備損壞的責任。任海回答，他們只為自己的腳步負責，不替研究局維護名聲。這點小小的區別讓顧遠在寒風裡笑了。

By noon they met Mara, a vocal guide who carried a wooden box of spare boots up the slope. She asked them to haul water to a temporary camp in exchange for a hot bowl of stew. Several residents there had suffered a fever that officials called an epidemic, although the local doctor said contaminated wells were more likely. The camp was already working on a beneficial transformation: replacing a leaking pipe with a covered rain barrel. Gu helped trim the cracked rubber around the valve, while Ren copied the doctor's dates. He had come to look for one missing sister, but the village's troubles were not an irrelevant errand. The record showed that the water became bitter on the same evening Gate Seven's clock changed. A reminder painted on the clinic wall read, 'Measure before you promise.' He added the sentence beside his sister's name.

中午，他們遇見嗓門洪亮的嚮導瑪拉。她抱著一箱備用靴子上坡，請兩人幫忙搬水到臨時營地，作為交換請他們吃一碗熱燉菜。營地已有幾名居民發燒，官員稱之為流行病，但當地醫師認為更可能是井水污染。居民正進行有益的改造：用加蓋雨水桶取代漏水管。顧遠幫忙修整閥門周圍龜裂的橡膠，任海則抄下醫師的紀錄日期。他此行是為了尋找一個失蹤的妹妹，卻不能說村民的困境只是無關緊要的跑腿差事。紀錄顯示，井水變苦的那一晚，正是第七閘時鐘出現變化的晚上。診所牆上寫著一句提醒：「先測量，再承諾。」他把這句話抄在妹妹名字旁。

Mara was the first respondent willing to sign her account. She described a convoy that passed the ridge carrying sealed containers, then returned empty without using the public road. Ren felt the temptation to treat the vehicles as proof of a kidnapping, but Gu reminded him that trucks could carry repair supplies as easily as prisoners. They compared the testimony with a logbook that the camp kept for its own safety. At the corresponding hour, a second sensor upstream had registered a downward spike, not an increase. The two readings could fit a diversion of water from one branch to another. They would need access to the underground valve before making that claim publicly. Ren had spent years drawing elegant coastlines; now he was learning that an honest map also showed blank spaces and the boundaries of what its maker did not know.

瑪拉是第一位願意簽名留下證詞的受訪者。她描述一支車隊，載著密封容器經過山脊，之後卻沒有走公路便空車返回。任海很想把這當作綁架的證據，但顧遠提醒他，卡車既可能載囚犯，也可能只是載修理材料。他們把證詞與營地為了安全自行保存的工作日誌比對。同一時段，上游第二具感測器記錄到的卻是壓力驟降，而非上升。兩組數據可能符合水從一條支道被引往另一條的情況。但若要公開提出這項推論，他們還得檢查地下閥門。任海多年來擅長繪製優美海岸線，如今正在學習，一張誠實的地圖也應標示空白，以及繪圖者不知道的界限。

At the far end of the camp an old woman displayed a faded sketch of the bridge as it had looked before the last storm. Someone had cut a square opening beneath its central arch. No official revision mentioned such a change. Gu traced the pencil marks and found that the depth measurements matched the compass's seven divisions. Ren photographed the sketch with Mara present, noting the light and angle so nobody could later accuse them of altering it. As they left, he caught the engineer shifting weight away from his injured leg. “We can stop before dusk,” Ren said. Gu looked surprised, then admitted the ridge had been harder than expected. Their cooperation had begun as an exchange of skills; this was the first time either man changed the pace simply because the other needed it. Below them the tide drew a white line toward the hidden bridge, and for once the line was not merely threatening. It pointed to a place they could investigate together.

營地另一端，一位老婦人展示了暴風雨前檢修橋的褪色素描。有人在中央拱橋下切出一個方形開口，官方修訂紀錄卻完全沒提過。顧遠沿著鉛筆線條比對，發現深度標示與羅盤的七個刻度吻合。任海在瑪拉見證下拍下素描，記錄光線與角度，免得日後有人指控他們竄改。離開時，他注意到工程師把重量移離受傷的腿。「我們可以在天黑前停下，」任海說。顧遠有些意外，才承認山路比預期困難。他們起初只是交換各自的專長；這是第一次，有人單純因為另一人的需要而調整步調。下方的潮水畫出一道白線，延伸向藏起來的橋。這回，那道線不只是威脅，也是他們能一起追查的方向。
