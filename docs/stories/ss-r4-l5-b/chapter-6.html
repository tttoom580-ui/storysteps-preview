The Skeleton Reef

The route beyond Reedfall passed an abandoned shrine whose roof was shaped like the rib of a great sea creature. Inside, a faded mural depicted a skeleton of the old coastal wall, its stones held together by channels rather than magic. Gu asked why a municipal engineer had signed the mural. A caretaker explained that, long ago, monks and builders had shared the same agenda: shield coastal homes from storm water. The temple's theology described water as a guest, never property. Later a federal authority had declared the reef a mining district and replaced the flexible stone gates with rigid steel ones. A child standing beside the mural began to sob when he heard that his family's new well had become toxic. Gu knelt to speak at eye level. “Beware the idea that a broken machine is only a machine,” he told Ren afterward. “The people who depend on it are part of its design.”

蘆落村以外的道路經過一座廢棄神殿，屋頂形似巨型海獸的肋骨。殿內褪色壁畫描繪古海堤的骨架，石塊靠水道而非魔法連接。顧遠不解為何壁畫上有市府工程師簽名。看守人說，很久以前僧侶與工匠有共同的議程：替沿岸住家擋住風暴水。寺院的神學將水視為訪客，絕非私人財產。後來聯邦當局把礁區劃成礦區，用僵硬鋼閘取代可彎曲的石門。壁畫旁一名孩子聽到自家新井變得有毒，開始啜泣。顧遠蹲下來與他平視。事後他告訴任海：「當心把壞掉的機器只當機器。仰賴它生活的人，也是設計的一部分。」

The caretaker gave them a map hidden beneath a board used for a village ball game. Each inning, children had scored by tossing shells into holes marked with old tide routes. What seemed like a game was actually a mnemonic for closing emergency gates in sequence. Gu found a reef sample with a pale organism clinging to its underside. If the creatures had survived for generations, they could reveal which channels still carried fresh water. Ren suggested treating the map as a metaphor: the first shell meant the upper valve, the second the escape channel, and the last the return gate. But metaphors could mislead. They copied the physical marks and tested them against the actual channels. A candidate, or nominee, for the local water council watched their work and asked to take credit before the election. Gu declined politely, insisting that the discovery belonged to the village record, not a campaign poster.

看守人拿出一張藏在村童球戲計分板下的地圖。每局孩子把貝殼投進標有舊潮道的洞裡得分。看似遊戲的活動，其實是記憶緊急水閘關閉順序的方法。顧遠找到一塊礁石樣本，底面黏著淡色生物。如果這些生物世代存活，便可能揭示哪些水道仍有淡水。任海提議把遊戲看成隱喻：第一顆貝殼代表上游閥、第二顆代表逃生道、最後一顆代表回流閘。但隱喻可能誤導人。他們抄下實際標記，再與真正水道比對。一位當地水務議會候選人旁觀後，要求在選前取得發現的功勞。顧遠禮貌拒絕，堅持這項發現屬於村莊紀錄，不是競選海報。

At the reef's northern edge, a stubborn current threatened to overtake the footpath. Mara, who had joined them again, tied a rope around an iron hook and told everyone to cling to the dry side of the wall. A rusted gate had sealed a narrow passage that might lead toward Gate Seven. Ren found a bottle that smelled sharply of vinegar, used once to clean marine growth from the hinges. Gu tried a small amount on a detached screw instead of pouring it into the water; the sample showed that the corrosion was too deep for a quick repair. He refused the dramatic gesture of breaking the gate. The caretaker said that years earlier a brief rebellion against the mining office had ended with villagers accused of sabotage. They could not afford another accusation. Instead, they traced a second path along the upper reef, documenting each measurement as they went.

礁區北緣，強硬的水流幾乎追上步道。重新加入他們的瑪拉把繩子繫在鐵鉤上，叫所有人緊貼堤牆乾燥的一側。一扇生鏽水閘封住狹窄通道，可能通往第七閘。任海找到一瓶有刺鼻醋味的液體，過去用來清潔鉸鏈上的海生物。顧遠只在拆下的螺絲上試少量，沒有把液體直接倒進水裡；樣本證明腐蝕太嚴重，無法快速修好。他拒絕以砸開閘門作為戲劇性的解決方式。看守人說，多年前村民曾短暫反抗礦務局，結果被指控破壞設施，他們承擔不起另一場指控。於是眾人改沿上方礁道找第二條路，並一路記下每次測量。

By dusk they reached a dry shelf and looked back at the divided sea. For the first time the hidden channel was visible along its entire length, from the miners' tower to the salt marsh. Ren marked it in red but left the far tunnel unexplored. Gu showed him the map of the shell game beside their modern chart. The old builders had anticipated that a pressure system would fail unless people knew how to interrupt it. That knowledge had survived as children's play long after the officials forgot its purpose. Ren thought of the missing team, who had probably found the same dangerous truth and refused to erase it. They camped among wind-bent pines. Before sleep, Ren said he had once believed Gu's caution was fear. “Sometimes it is,” Gu answered. “Fear can still be useful if it helps us bring everyone home.” The distinction remained with Ren through the night.

黃昏，他們抵達乾燥石棚，回頭望向被分開的海。這是第一次，隱藏水道的全長清楚可見：從礦工塔一路延伸到鹽沼。任海用紅色標出它，卻把最遠的隧道留作未勘查。顧遠把貝殼遊戲地圖放在現代圖表旁。老建造者早就預料，若沒人知道如何中斷壓力系統，系統終將失效。這份知識在官員忘記用途後，仍以孩童遊戲的形式留下。任海想到失蹤隊，大概也找到同樣危險的真相，卻拒絕抹去。他們在被風吹彎的松樹間紮營。睡前任海說，他曾以為顧遠的謹慎是膽小。「有時確實是，」顧遠說，「但如果恐懼能幫我們把大家帶回家，也不見得沒有用。」這份區別陪著任海度過整夜。

That night the caretaker said scholars had dismissed the shrine's strange games as part of the genre of children's folklore, unworthy of technical study. He watched Gu compare the shell marks with the actual positions of the gates and laughed softly. “People keep knowledge in the forms they can carry,” he said. Ren thought about his maps and understood that a drawing, a song, and a child's game might all serve as records if their meaning could be tested. He added the source of the shrine story to his field notes rather than calling it a revelation, and the caretaker nodded in approval.

那晚看守人說，學者過去總把神殿奇特遊戲歸入兒童民俗故事的類型，不認為值得技術研究。他看著顧遠把貝殼記號與真正水閘位置比對，輕輕笑了。「人會用自己能帶走的形式保存知識，」他說。任海想到地圖，明白圖畫、歌曲和童戲都可能成為紀錄，只要其中意義可以檢驗。他在野外筆記記下神殿故事來源，而沒有稱它為天啟，看守人贊許地點頭。
