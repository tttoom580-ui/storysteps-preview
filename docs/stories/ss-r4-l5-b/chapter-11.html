木門避難所

The Refuge of Wooden Doors

山海邊境，失蹤者仍在等待 · 第 11 / 25 章 · 4000–5000 · 英中對照 · 校訂中

The refuge near the sea wall had once been a workers' workshop, then a shelter for families displaced by the canal. Its keeper, Old Jian, moved through the rooms in a wheelchair and knew every hinge by sound. He was apt to call any visitor a thief, not because he enjoyed suspicion but because the last inspection had ended in theft of medicine and maps. When Gu showed his old engineering scar, Jian relaxed slightly. He remembered the failed rescue that had injured Gu, and he refused to characterize the event as cowardice. “You carried three people out with no promise that your strength would return,” he said. Gu felt a familiar shame rise, followed by surprise: another person remembered the rescue rather than its cost. Ren watched a child gasp at the sound of waves howling beneath the floor. A hidden shaft ran under the building, and the doors were still intact.

海堤附近的避難所過去是工人工坊，後來收容因運河失去家園的家庭。看守人老簡坐著輪椅在房間間移動，光聽聲音就知道每個鉸鏈的狀況。他動不動把訪客叫作小偷，不是因為喜歡懷疑，而是上次檢查後藥物與地圖遭竊。顧遠讓他看自己的舊工程傷疤，老簡才稍微放鬆。他記得那場讓顧遠受傷的救援，並拒絕把它描述為懦弱。「你救出三個人，卻沒有保證力量會回來，」他說。顧遠感到熟悉的羞愧湧上，隨後又有些意外：有人記得的是他的救援，不只是代價。任海看見一名孩子聽到地板下海浪咆哮時倒抽一口氣。建築下方確有隱藏豎井，木門仍完好無損。

Jian unlocked a cabinet filled with spare parts and a rifle left behind by a private guard. He refused to let the weapon become part of the search. “A narrow tunnel is no arena for threats,” he said. Instead he offered lamps and a wheeled platform that could support an injured traveler. The repair would require an upgrade to the platform's brakes, and Gu recommended testing it on a safe slope first. A clinical volunteer examined the rescue supplies and recorded which medicines had expired. Ren found a folded brochure from a tourist casino on the coast, apparently used as packing paper. On its back was a hand-drawn route, signed by an heir of the old lighthouse family. The sketch marked an air vent beside the hidden shaft. The owner of the mark had likely never intended to publish it, which made its survival accidental but no less useful.

老簡打開裝滿備件的櫃子，裡頭還有私人守衛留下的一把步槍。他拒絕讓武器成為搜尋工具。「狹窄隧道不是威脅人的競技場，」他說。他改拿出燈與可承載傷者的輪式平台。平台煞車需要升級，顧遠建議先在安全坡道測試。一名臨床志工檢查救援用品，記錄過期藥物。任海找到一張海岸觀光賭場的折頁，似乎被拿來包裝物品。背面畫著手繪路線，署名是舊燈塔家族的繼承人。草圖標出豎井旁的通風口。畫記號的人或許根本沒想公開，這份資料的倖存是偶然，卻不減其用途。

While Gu repaired the platform, Ren organized a small public workshop to show residents how the shaft could be tested without entering it. A woman claiming copyright over the old lighthouse drawing demanded that nobody copy it. Ren explained that they would respect the artist's rights, and asked permission to display only the safety features necessary for rescue. The woman agreed after Jian recognized her family's seal. Outside, a spectator tried to turn the meeting into entertainment by guessing which missing person had died. Ren stopped the game at once. Jian's volunteer team showed the residents that one air opening still carried a steady breeze from deep underground. The discovery fascinated the children, but Gu warned that moving air was not proof of breathable conditions throughout the tunnel. They set a sealed sensor near the entrance and waited for actual measurements.

顧遠修平台時，任海辦了一場公開工坊，向居民展示如何在不進入豎井的情況下測試。某位主張燈塔舊圖著作權的女子要求所有人不得複製。任海解釋他們會尊重作者權利，只請求展示救援所需的安全部分。老簡認出她家族印章後，她同意了。屋外一名看熱鬧的人拿失蹤者是否已死來下注猜測，任海立刻制止。老簡的志工隊向居民展示，一處通風口仍有穩定氣流從地底吹來。孩子們對此著迷，但顧遠提醒，空氣流動不能證明整條隧道都有足夠氧氣。他們把密封感測器放在入口附近，等待實際量測。

That night Ren and Gu stayed at the refuge instead of forcing an entrance. The sound of the sea rose against the planks, and the platform rested ready beside the locked door. Jian asked what they would do if they found the survey team but could not move everyone at once. Gu answered that they would calculate the transport time, call trained helpers, and choose a sequence based on medical need rather than personal attachment. Ren understood the pain hidden in that answer. His sister might be in the tunnel, yet he could not demand that others be abandoned for her. He told Gu he trusted him to say what was safe, even when it was not what he wanted to hear. Gu looked toward the breathing vent. “And I trust you to remind me that a calculation is about people,” he replied. Neither man slept easily, but neither was alone with his fear.

那晚任海與顧遠留在避難所，沒有強行進入豎井。海聲拍打木板，準備好的平台停在上鎖門旁。老簡問，如果找到測量隊，卻無法一次移送所有人，該怎麼辦。顧遠回答，他們會計算運送時間，召集訓練過的助手，依醫療需求而非個人感情決定順序。任海明白這答案裡藏著的痛。他的妹妹可能就在隧道裡，但不能要求為了她拋下別人。他告訴顧遠，即使安全判斷不是他想聽的，也會信任顧遠說實話。顧遠望向通風口。「而我相信你會提醒我，計算的對象是人，」他回答。兩人都睡不好，但不再各自獨自面對恐懼。

When the night tide rose, a sheer cliff of dark stone blocked the view from the refuge to the sea. Jian explained that the missing surveyors had helped him repair the window beside that cliff shortly before they disappeared. They had asked for a reliable list of exits, not a shortcut through the walls. Ren wrote the detail in his notebook and promised to send Jian a copy of the eventual rescue route. The old keeper pretended not to care, but he checked the locks twice before bed. His caution felt like affection expressed in the language of bolts and hinges.

夜潮升起時，一面陡峭黑色石崖擋住避難所往海的視野。老簡解釋，失蹤測量員在消失前不久曾幫他修好崖旁窗戶。他們要的是可靠的出口清單，而不是穿牆捷徑。任海把細節寫進筆記，答應將最後確定的救援路線副本交給老簡。老看守人假裝不在意，睡前卻檢查兩次鎖。他的謹慎像是用螺栓與鉸鏈表達的關心。
