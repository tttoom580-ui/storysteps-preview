A Rival in a Perfect Uniform

月光失物招領社，今天也不接受退貨！ · 第 8 / 25 章 · 2000–3000 · 英中對照 · 校訂中

Yuji arrived at the club wearing a spotless volunteer jacket and carrying a folder thicker than Mochi. He offered to help them file correct forms if they stopped calling the festival committee careless. Kaho felt an ache in her shoulders after moving chairs. An old bookcase held records from the previous festival. Kaho glanced toward Itsuki, expecting the club president to supply an easy answer. He merely offered her a pencil and waited. That was annoying in a surprisingly useful way: she had to decide what she actually believed before repeating somebody else. Mochi considered the silence a suitable moment to request food. Yuji listened to Kaho's complaint without interrupting her. Itsuki remained conscious of the club's limited budget.

裕司穿著乾淨得發亮的志工外套，抱著比麻糬還厚的文件夾來社辦。他說，只要社員別再說委員會粗心，他就幫忙填正確的表格。 搬完椅子後，花穗的肩膀隱隱痠痛。 一座舊書櫃裝著上次祭典的紀錄。 花穗望向樹，以為社長會給她一個簡單答案。他卻只遞來鉛筆，安靜等候。這有點令人懊惱，卻意外地有幫助：她必須先決定自己真正相信什麼，而不是重複別人的話。麻糬認為沉默正適合開口要食物。 裕司沒有插嘴，聽完花穗的抱怨。 樹始終留意社團有限的預算。

Kaho refused the bargain but accepted the forms. She asked for written reasons whenever a request was denied. Yuji seemed startled that anyone wanted an explanation rather than a personal favor. The print shop offered a discount for student posters. A grasshopper jumped onto Mochi's spotless new collar. Itsuki bent down to see what Kaho was pointing at, close enough for her to notice a spot of ink on his cheek. She almost mentioned it, then chose the more urgent question first. A good team, she was learning, did not need to agree immediately; it needed to notice the same facts. Kaho regretted her hasty judgment about Yuji's intentions. Aoi agreed to inform the members about the corrected schedule.

花穗不接受交換條件，卻收下表格。她要求每項遭拒的申請都附書面理由。裕司似乎很驚訝，竟有人要解釋而不是求情。 印刷店替學生海報提供折扣。 一隻蚱蜢跳到麻糬乾淨的新領圈上。 樹彎身看花穗指著什麼，靠近到她能看見他臉頰上的墨點。她差點提醒他，最後仍先問比較緊急的事。她逐漸學到，好團隊不必立刻同意，而是要先看見同樣的事實。 花穗後悔太倉促判斷裕司的用意。 葵同意通知社員更正後的時程。

Mochi secretly entered a poster contest under the name Sir Fluffy Moon. Aoi promised to help only if the fox stopped stealing her lunch; Mochi said that was a cruel demand. Mochi believed that stealing biscuits counted as leisure time. They used the archive index to locate Sora's missing proposal. Kaho was embarrassed by how often her first answer turned out to be incomplete. Itsuki told her that changing a conclusion after learning something new was not defeat. She did not quite believe him yet, but she stopped crossing out her earlier notes. Keeping the mistake visible might help them avoid repeating it. The fox called the filing cabinet a monster that ate reports. Kaho helped organize the documents by date and subject.

麻糬偷偷以「蓬鬆月亮爵士」之名報名海報比賽。葵答應幫忙，條件是牠別再偷午餐；麻糬卻說這要求太殘酷。 麻糬相信偷餅乾也算休閒活動。 他們用檔案索引找到空遺失的提案。 花穗常因第一個答案不完整而尷尬。樹告訴她，知道新事實後改變結論並不是失敗。她還不能完全相信，但不再把舊筆記塗掉。留下錯誤，也許能幫大家避免重犯。 狐狸把檔案櫃叫作吞報告的怪物。 花穗幫忙依日期與主題整理文件。

The group sorted older notices and found that Sora's final proposal had been filed under the wrong club name. The mistake explained why the administration believed the room was vacant. A portrait of an early club founder hung beside the door. Mochi submitted a ridiculous poster with its face in the moon. Itsuki suggested writing the decision in plain language before anyone signed it. Kaho read the sentence aloud and found a part that could be misunderstood. They fixed it together. The extra minutes felt worthwhile, especially after remembering how much trouble one careless line had already caused. Yuji promised to release the corrected meeting notes to the members. A child had folded a paper rocket for the festival display.

大家整理舊公告，發現空的最後一份提案被歸在錯誤社團名下。這個錯誤解釋了校方為何認為教室閒置。 一幅早期社團創辦人的肖像掛在門旁。 麻糬交出一張荒唐海報，把自己的臉放進月亮裡。 樹提議在任何人簽字前，先把決定寫成簡單的文字。花穗朗讀時發現一處可能誤解的地方，大家一起修正。想到一行粗心文字曾造成多少麻煩，這幾分鐘就顯得很值得。 裕司答應向社員公布更正過的會議紀錄。 一個孩子替祭典展示摺了紙火箭。

Yuji promised to request a formal correction. He had not become Kaho's friend overnight, but for the first time he stood on their side of the same table. A white swan appeared in the painting beside the river path. The old box was packed too tight for anyone to open easily. Kaho closed her notebook on a question that would have to wait until tomorrow. Waiting no longer felt like giving up. It meant allowing time for the right person to answer. Outside, the campus lights were turning on one by one, and Itsuki walked beside her without demanding another promise. Itsuki said the club's strange work was what made it unique. The event's safety plan clearly rejected violence and threats. Yuji returned the corrected file with a signature and date. Kaho placed it beside the old version, so nobody would need to rely on a private conversation to understand the change.

裕司答應提出正式更正。他沒有一夜之間變成花穗的朋友，卻第一次站在同一張桌子的同一側。 河邊道路旁的畫裡有一隻白天鵝。 舊箱子塞得太緊，沒人能輕易打開。 花穗闔上筆記本，把一個問題留到明天。等待不再像放棄，而是給真正有權回答的人一些時間。外頭校園燈光逐盞亮起，樹走在她身旁，沒有要求她再許下一個承諾。 樹說社團奇怪的工作正是它的獨特之處。 活動的安全計畫明確拒絕暴力與威脅。 裕司交回有簽名日期的更正檔。花穗把它與舊版放在一起，讓任何人都不必靠私下談話才能理解變更。
