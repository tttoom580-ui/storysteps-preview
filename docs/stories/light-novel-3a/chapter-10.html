The Key under the Broken Bell

月光失物招領社，今天也不接受退貨！ · 第 10 / 25 章 · 2000–3000 · 英中對照 · 校訂中

At the festival storage house, the old bell refused to ring. A caretaker showed the club a rusty key that had been discovered under its wooden stand. A visiting banker offered to support the festival through a public grant. The old bell had once hung beside a sheltered bay. Nobody in the group had perfect information. Kaho reminded herself that uncertainty was not a reason to invent a satisfying story. They could ask one more question, check one more object, and leave the rest open for now. Itsuki looked relieved to hear somebody say that out loud. A thin beam of sunlight lit the rusty key on the table. Mochi named itself champion of finding things under furniture.

祭典倉庫裡的舊鐘怎麼也敲不響。管理員拿出一把在木底座下找到的生鏽鑰匙，請社員幫忙查明用途。 一名來訪銀行家願意透過公開補助支持祭典。 那只舊鐘以前曾掛在一處避風海灣旁。 團隊裡沒有任何人掌握全部資訊。花穗提醒自己，不確定不是編造漂亮故事的理由。大家可以再問一個問題、再檢查一件東西，其餘暫時保留。樹聽見有人把這話說出口，表情放鬆了。 一束細陽光照亮桌上的生鏽鑰匙。 麻糬自封為尋找家具下物品的冠軍。

The key opened a narrow cabinet containing safety sketches, repair requests, and Sora's notes from the year a summer event had ended early. None of the pages mentioned a curse. Kaho proposed a creative way to display the old plans. The number of visitors would decrease if the side route stayed closed. At first Kaho wished someone would give her a simple order. Then she remembered why she disliked being told what to think. She asked the others to slow down and explain their reasons. Even Mochi listened for a moment before returning to the important business of searching for biscuits. The empty storage room returned an echo when Itsuki called out. The caretaker gave Kaho a fearful look at the stuck bell.

鑰匙打開狹窄的櫃子，裡面有安全草圖、修繕申請及那年夏季活動提前結束時空寫的筆記。沒有任何一頁提到詛咒。 花穗提出一種有創意的展示舊圖的方法。 側邊通道若一直關閉，訪客數就會減少。 起初花穗希望有人直接給她一道簡單命令，接著又想起自己為什麼討厭被規定想法。她請大家放慢速度，說明理由。連麻糬都聽了一會兒，才重新投入尋找餅乾的重大工作。 樹呼喊時，空倉庫傳來回音。 管理員看到卡住的鐘，害怕地望了花穗一眼。

Yuji said the committee had always been told that Sora had simply forgotten to plan for rain. Kaho found a record showing Sora had asked for a second exit weeks earlier. The historic sketches showed how the old exits had changed. A metal holder kept the damaged key from falling off the shelf. Itsuki looked ready to take on the entire problem himself. Kaho recognized the expression because she sometimes made the same mistake. She reminded him that a club was not simply a president followed by helpers. Aoi nodded; Mochi objected that a kingdom certainly worked that way, and was ignored. A water leak had damaged the lowest drawer in the cabinet. Kaho refused to call a worried crowd a dangerous mob.

裕司說委員會一直被告知，當年只是空忘了安排雨天備案。花穗卻找到紀錄：空早在幾週前就要求增設第二出口。 歷史草圖顯示舊出口是如何變動的。 金屬架托住損壞的鑰匙，避免它掉下層架。 樹的表情像準備一個人扛下所有問題。花穗認得那神情，因為她有時也犯同樣的錯。她提醒他，社團不是一個社長帶著一群助手。葵點頭；麻糬反對說王國就是如此運作，沒人理牠。 漏水已經毀損櫃子最下層的抽屜。 花穗拒絕把擔心的人群稱作危險暴民。

Aoi copied the dates, and Itsuki called a campus staff adviser to keep the documents safe. Mochi tried to wear the key as a necklace, which nobody considered a good preservation method. Yuji said it was normal to request evidence before changing a plan. A child had drawn a bright palace on an old festival flyer. Kaho listened to a hesitant answer without trying to fill the silence. She had once mistaken confidence for kindness, but the two did not always travel together. Sometimes respect sounded like a quiet question and a willingness to hear no. Itsuki followed her lead, which surprised them both a little. Mochi found a single penny inside a broken wooden frame. The committee had decided to postpone the risky event until repairs.

葵抄下日期，樹聯絡學校職員保存文件。麻糬想把鑰匙當項鍊，沒有人認為那是良好的保存方式。 裕司說變更計畫前要求證據很正常。 一個孩子在舊祭典傳單上畫了明亮宮殿。 花穗聽著遲疑的回答，沒有急著填補沉默。她以前把自信誤認成體貼，但兩者未必同行。有時尊重只是一句安靜的詢問，以及願意接受拒絕。樹跟著她做，兩人都有點意外。 麻糬在壞木框裡找到一枚小硬幣。 委員會已決定把有風險的活動延期到修好之後。

The group left the broken bell untouched and reported its condition. They had gained a real lead: the problem was not just a missing song or a forgotten name, but an unfinished safety decision. A broken receiver lay beside the forgotten public-address system. Sora's notes mentioned a scholarship for young festival artists. A little laughter returned as they cleaned the tables. Kaho noticed that the club had begun to feel less like a place she had entered by accident and more like a place she could choose. There would be another difficulty soon, she was sure. For tonight, they had done enough to go home. Kaho warned the fox not to steal anything from the archive. Mochi got its tail caught in the zipper of a supply bag. The rusty key went into an ordinary labeled envelope. That seemed like a quiet ending for a dramatic discovery, but Kaho had learned that proper records could protect people.

大家沒有再碰壞掉的鐘，而是通報它的狀況。新的線索終於明確：問題不只是消失的歌詞或被遺忘的名字，而是一項未完成的安全決定。 故障的接收器放在被遺忘的廣播系統旁。 空的筆記提到提供給年輕祭典藝術家的獎學金。 大家清理桌面時又有了些笑聲。花穗發現，這社團不再只是她意外走進的地方，而是她能自行選擇的地方。她確信下一個難題很快會來。今晚，他們已經做得夠多，可以回家了。 花穗警告狐狸不要從檔案室偷東西。 麻糬的尾巴卡進物資袋的拉鍊裡。 生鏽鑰匙被裝進普通的標示信封。對戲劇性的發現而言，這結尾很安靜，但花穗已知道正確紀錄能保護人。
