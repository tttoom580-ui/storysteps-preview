消失的最後一句歌詞

A Song That Lost Its Last Line

月光失物招領社，今天也不接受退貨！ · 第 9 / 25 章 · 2000–3000 · 英中對照 · 校訂中

The music club asked the lost-and-found team to restore the missing end of an old festival song. Its composer remembered writing something hopeful, but all surviving copies ended in silence. A brass bell stood beside the faded recording machine. The music club prepared a small buffet after rehearsal. The room grew busy with opinions, and Kaho nearly lost track of the original question. She drew a little square around the most important detail in her notes. Aoi leaned over her shoulder, saw the square, and nodded. Something difficult became manageable once it had a clear place on the page. Replacing the recording system would be costly for the students. The original poster named Mei as the lantern designer.

音樂社想找回祭典舊歌消失的結尾。作曲者記得自己寫過一句充滿希望的詞，但所有保存下來的版本都在那裡突然停止。 褪色的錄音機旁放著一只黃銅鈴。 音樂社在彩排後準備了小型自助餐。 房間裡意見紛紛，花穗差點忘記最初的問題。她在筆記中替最重要的細節畫上小方框。葵從肩後看見那個方框，點了點頭。難題一旦在紙上有了清楚的位置，似乎就比較能處理了。 對學生來說，換掉錄音系統很昂貴。 原始海報把美依列為燈籠設計師。

Aoi played the old recording at half speed. What sounded like a broken note turned out to be applause covering a spoken announcement, and the name Sora appeared again. Aoi used a tiny hammer to repair the loose frame. Mochi declared the silent recording to be music from hell. They moved the loose papers away from the edge of the table and made space to compare what each person had found. Kaho described her observation without decorating it. Aoi added what the equipment could and could not show. Their different skills made the picture less exciting, perhaps, but much more useful. The composer gave a humorous account of her first failed performance. An icy drink made Kaho shiver after the warm rehearsal.

葵把舊錄音放慢，原本像破音的地方其實是掌聲蓋住了一段廣播，而空的名字再次出現。 葵用小鎚子修理鬆掉的框架。 麻糬說這段安靜的錄音簡直是地獄音樂。 大家把鬆散紙張移開桌緣，騰出地方比較各自發現的線索。花穗不加渲染地描述觀察，葵補充設備能與不能顯示的內容。各種能力放在一起，也許讓事情少了刺激，卻更有用。 作曲者幽默地講述第一次失敗的演出。 彩排後，一杯冰冷飲料讓花穗打了寒顫。

Kaho interviewed singers about what they remembered rather than asking the machine to compose a beautiful ending. One singer confessed that she had never liked the original final line. A poster showed a kangaroo dancing under a silver moon. Mist covered the river when they left the rehearsal hall. Aoi placed two sheets side by side, and the differences finally became visible. The club could argue about motives all afternoon, but a change on paper was something they could examine together. Kaho pointed to the line that needed an answer and asked who had the right to provide it. A porcelain cup rattled beside the old speakers. The recorder preserved a spoken announcement beneath the applause.

花穗問歌手記得什麼，沒有要求機器編一個漂亮的結尾。一位歌手坦言，她其實從來不喜歡原本那句歌詞。 一張海報畫著袋鼠在銀月下跳舞。 他們離開彩排廳時，薄霧籠罩河面。 葵把兩張紙並排放好，差異終於清楚。社團大可爭論動機一整個下午，但紙上的修改是大家能共同檢查的。花穗指向需要回答的那一行，問誰有權提供答案。 一只瓷杯在舊喇叭旁發出喀啦聲。 錄音機保存了掌聲下方那段口頭廣播。

Itsuki thought the club should keep the disliked line because it was history. Kaho agreed but suggested adding a new verse clearly marked as the present students' work. Itsuki asked Mochi to scrub ink off the table before leaving. They placed a paper seal over the box after copying the file. The club divided the work into pieces small enough for each person to choose freely. Kaho handled the drawings, Aoi checked practical details, and Itsuki contacted the people who needed to decide. They did not turn disagreement into a contest. Mochi alone complained that no one had appointed a royal snack officer. A singer wanted to skip the difficult final verse. The old stadium was once used for community performances.

樹認為既然是歷史，就應保留那句不受歡迎的詞。花穗同意，並提議再寫一段明確標示由現在學生創作的新歌詞。 樹請麻糬走前把桌上的墨水刷乾淨。 複製檔案後，他們在箱子上貼了紙封條。 社團把工作分成每個人都能自由選擇的小部分。花穗負責畫作，葵檢查實務細節，樹聯絡需要作決定的人。他們沒有把不同意見變成比賽。只有麻糬抱怨沒人任命王室點心官。 一名歌手想跳過困難的最後一段。 舊體育場以前曾用來舉辦社區表演。

They played both versions at rehearsal. The room laughed and sang together; Kaho realized that protecting the past did not mean forbidding people from adding their own voices. Kaho shared a sweet tangerine with the worried singer. Aoi explained the technical reason for the missing audio. The club room looked ordinary again once the last visitor had left. Kaho knew better now than to trust appearances alone. She made a short list of what to check next and left it where everyone could read it. Aoi approved of the practical list; Mochi approved of the space for its name. Nobody was allowed to threaten the singer into changing her answer. Aoi had to tighten one cable before the second recording test. The new verse was not better simply because it was new. It belonged beside the old song because its makers had listened, chosen, and written their names honestly.

彩排時，兩個版本都被唱了出來。大家又笑又唱，花穗明白，保護過去不代表禁止現在的人加入自己的聲音。 花穗和緊張的歌手分享一顆甜橘子。 葵解釋錄音缺失的技術原因。 最後一位訪客離開後，社辦又顯得十分普通。花穗如今知道不能只相信表面。她列下接下來要檢查的事情，放在大家都看得到的地方。葵欣賞這份實用清單；麻糬則欣賞上面留了牠名字的位置。 沒有人可以威脅歌手改變回答。 第二次錄音測試前，葵必須把一條線纜鎖緊。 新歌詞不會因為新就比較好。它能與舊歌並列，是因為創作者曾仔細聆聽、作出選擇，並誠實留下名字。
