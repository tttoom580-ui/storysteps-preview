把我撿回去的社團

The Club That Found Me

月光失物招領社，今天也不接受退貨！ · 第 1 / 25 章 · 2000–3000 · 英中對照 · 校訂中

At Hoshimi University, nineteen-year-old Kaho Fujisawa wanted only a quiet first semester. Instead, a paper fox climbed out of her lunch bag and declared that she belonged to the Moonlight Lost-and-Found Club. Itsuki said a three-hour trial membership was perfectly acceptable. Aoi promised to advise Kaho about the machine's strange buttons. Kaho had promised herself she would not join another troublesome project. Yet an unanswered question always had a way of following her home. She opened her notebook instead of pretending not to care. The decision seemed small, but Itsuki noticed it immediately and moved aside to make room for her. Kaho rested her chin on a stack of unfinished posters. Mochi stole the coconut bread from Kaho's lunch bag.

十九歲的藤澤花穗只想在星見大學安靜度過第一學期。沒想到，一隻紙狐狸從她的午餐袋爬出來，宣布她已經被月光失物招領社錄取。 樹說先試加入三小時完全可以接受。 葵答應教花穗認識機器那些奇怪的按鈕。 花穗曾答應自己不再加入麻煩計畫，但還沒回答的問題總會一路跟著她回家。她打開筆記本，沒有假裝不在乎。這決定看似微小，樹卻立刻注意到，還挪開位置讓她坐下。 花穗把下巴靠在一疊未完成的海報上。 麻糬偷走花穗午餐袋裡的椰子麵包。

Kaho followed the fox to an unused classroom behind the art building. A tall student named Itsuki was labeling empty boxes while his friend Aoi tested a machine made from a secondhand vending cabinet. Aoi tried to connect the old printer to a new laptop. Kaho feared that a cruel prank was hiding behind the fox. Aoi treated every surprising result like an ordinary problem that could be examined. She asked who had seen it, when it had happened, and whether they could check it again. Mochi announced that such questions would make magic terribly unpopular. Kaho wrote them down anyway, one careful line at a time. The first-aid card listed the correct dose of ordinary medicine. A drip from the ceiling landed directly on Mochi's paper nose.

花穗跟著狐狸走進美術館後方的閒置教室。高個子的樹正在替空箱子貼標籤，朋友葵則測試一台由二手販賣機改造的裝置。 葵試著把舊印表機連接到新筆電。 花穗擔心這隻狐狸背後藏著殘酷的惡作劇。 葵把每個驚人的結果都當成可以檢查的普通問題。她問誰看過、何時發生，以及能不能再確認一次。麻糬宣布這些問題會讓魔法變得很不受歡迎。花穗還是一行一行仔細記下。 急救卡列出了常見藥物的正確劑量。 天花板滴下一滴水，正好落在麻糬的紙鼻子上。

Kaho laughed at the idea of finding lost promises, then admitted she had lost the courage to show her drawings. She wrote that wish on a card; the booth printed a line from her private notebook. Kaho could not help feeling envy toward Itsuki's easy confidence. The fox begged them to explore the room behind the curtains. Mochi insisted that every serious meeting needed a royal announcement. Aoi replied that every royal announcement needed an accurate time and a clear purpose. Their argument lasted less than a minute before Kaho interrupted with the question that actually mattered. Itsuki hid a smile behind the nearest folder. Aoi warned that extreme heat would ruin the little printing motor. Mochi offered to gamble its dinner on the next ticket.

花穗原本嘲笑找回約定的想法，接著承認自己失去了展示畫作的勇氣。她把願望寫在卡片上；機器竟印出她私人筆記裡的一行字。 花穗忍不住羨慕樹那份自然的自信。 狐狸央求大家探索窗簾後方的房間。 麻糬堅持嚴肅會議都需要王室宣言。葵回說王室宣言至少也要有準確時間和清楚目的。不到一分鐘，花穗便用真正重要的問題打斷爭論，樹躲在資料夾後偷笑。 葵警告極端高溫會毀掉小小的印表機馬達。 麻糬說願意拿晚餐賭下一張票的內容。

Itsuki explained that the booth could find unfinished promises but could never force an owner to accept them. The paper fox, Mochi, interrupted to demand a snack and called this the club's most important rule. One glance at the ticket made Kaho forget her planned excuse. A bundle of hay waited beside the old festival animal costumes. Kaho asked whether the people affected by the choice had been allowed to speak. The question changed the pace of the conversation. Even those who preferred a quick answer had to admit that the answer would mean little without consent. Mochi was unusually quiet, which Aoi described as a historic achievement. Someone had left a bag of laundry under the club desk. Itsuki gave a sincere apology for letting Mochi inspect her bag.

樹解釋，這台機器能找到未完成的約定，卻不能強迫主人領回。紙狐狸麻糬插嘴要點心，還說那才是社團最重要的規矩。 花穗只瞥了票一眼，就忘了準備好的藉口。 一捆乾草放在舊祭典動物裝旁。 花穗問，受這項選擇影響的人是否有機會發言。這個問題改變了談話速度。就連偏好快速答案的人，也得承認沒有同意的答案意義不大。麻糬難得安靜，葵稱這是歷史性的成就。 有人把一袋待洗衣物留在社團桌下。 樹真誠道歉，因為他放任麻糬翻看花穗的包包。

Kaho agreed to stay for one afternoon, not a lifetime. When she picked up her ticket, the room's blank wall showed a tiny picture of a lantern festival she had never visited. A short stool served as the fox's imaginary royal throne. A faded warning said that tobacco was forbidden inside this room. By the time they put their things away, the day had turned a little different from the one Kaho expected. She had not solved every problem, but she knew one fact she had not known before. Itsuki thanked her without making a grand speech. Mochi demanded that gratitude be served with a biscuit. A photograph showed a festival painter standing beside her twin. The fox gave a loud whistle whenever someone mentioned cake. Kaho put the lantern picture into her notebook, even though she had promised never to draw in front of strangers again. She had already broken one rule of her own making.

花穗只答應待一個下午，沒有承諾一輩子。她拿起票券時，空白牆面竟浮出一場她從未參加過的燈籠祭典。 一張矮凳成了狐狸幻想中的王座。 褪色的告示說這間教室內禁止菸草。 當大家收好東西時，這一天已經和花穗原本想的不一樣。她沒有解決所有問題，卻知道了一件先前不知道的事。樹沒有發表長篇演說，只簡單道謝。麻糬則要求感謝必須附一塊餅乾。 一張照片拍到祭典畫家和她的雙胞胎手足站在一起。 每當有人提到蛋糕，狐狸就會吹出響亮的口哨。 花穗把燈籠圖片放進筆記本，雖然她曾說不再當著陌生人的面畫畫。她已經打破自己訂下的一條規矩。
