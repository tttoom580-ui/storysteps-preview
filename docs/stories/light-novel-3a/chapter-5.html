Mei visited the club to sign a simple permission form. Kaho felt nervous because the return would be their first official case, and she had to speak in front of everyone. The university athlete helped carry the heavy lantern frame. Mei said the correction would benefit future young artists. Kaho had expected magic to solve the difficult parts of student life. Instead, it seemed to hand her forms, questions, and people with different ideas. She caught herself smiling at the absurdity. Aoi raised an eyebrow, as though asking whether Kaho was finally beginning to enjoy the club. Kaho wondered whether drawing could become her career. She pressed the signed permission form against her chest.

美依來社團簽署簡單的同意書。花穗很緊張，因為這是她們第一件正式歸還案件，而她必須站在所有人面前說明。 大學運動員幫忙搬運沉重的燈籠框架。 美依說這次更正能讓未來的年輕藝術家受益。 花穗本以為魔法能替學生生活解決困難，結果它只是不斷交給她表格、問題及想法不同的人。她發現自己竟因這份荒謬而微笑。葵挑了挑眉，彷彿在問花穗是不是終於開始喜歡社團。 花穗心想，畫畫是否能成為她的職業。 她把簽好的同意書緊緊抱在胸前。

The machine accepted a copy of the design and printed a new ticket with both artists' names. Nothing flashed or exploded. The quiet sound of a printer was enough to make Mei cry. A date on the corner offered a clue about the old file. Mochi tried to drag the signed ticket toward its biscuit jar. The problem did not look dramatic from a distance. It was made of small objects, ordinary schedules, and choices somebody had forgotten to discuss. Kaho took a closer look instead of turning away. Itsuki saw her expression change and quietly passed her the page she needed next. The newspaper reporter seemed eager to call the booth a miracle. Kaho refused to erase the first artist's original sketch.

機器收下設計圖副本，印出載有兩位作者名字的新票。沒有閃光，也沒有爆炸。印表機輕輕作響，卻足以讓美依落淚。 角落的日期提供了舊檔案的一條線索。 麻糬想把簽好的票拖往餅乾罐。 遠遠看來，這問題並不戲劇化，只涉及小物件、普通時程及某些人忘了討論的選擇。花穗沒有轉開視線，而是靠近看清楚。樹看見她表情改變，默默遞上下一頁需要的資料。 校報記者急著把機器稱為奇蹟。 花穗拒絕抹去第一位藝術家的原稿。

A school newspaper asked whether the magic had solved the conflict. Kaho answered that people had solved it; the booth had merely shown them a question they could choose to face. Someone had left a bag of flour for the festival baking class. A painting showed the old harbor where the parade began. Nobody cheered when the difficulty became clear. Still, the team had moved from guessing to a question they could answer. Kaho drew a small star beside that line in her notebook. It was not a prize or a promise of success; it meant they finally knew what to try next. The idea of losing the club room felt horrible to Kaho. Mochi declared itself a knight protecting the printer.

校報問魔法是不是解決了衝突。花穗回答，解決問題的是人，機器只是指出大家可以選擇面對的問題。 有人留下祭典烘焙課用的一袋麵粉。 一幅畫描繪遊行起點的舊港口。 困難變清楚時，沒有人歡呼。但團隊已經從猜測走到可以回答的問題。花穗在筆記那行旁畫了一顆小星星。那不是獎品，也不是成功保證，只表示大家終於知道接下來能試什麼。 想到要失去社團教室，花穗覺得太可怕。 麻糬宣稱自己是守護印表機的騎士。

Itsuki revealed that the university might close the club after the summer festival. Its room was listed as unused, and their former captain's report remained incomplete. Itsuki checked the door lock after the visitors left. The club received a small medal from the workshop children. Aoi checked the details one final time while Kaho prepared a short explanation for the others. They agreed on what could happen now and what must wait. Nobody was asked to trade safety or privacy for a beautiful result. The choice looked ordinary on paper, yet it mattered to everyone in the room. A newly paved pavement led from the art building to the park. The rough edge of the frame scratched a volunteer's glove.

樹透露，大學可能在夏季祭典後解散社團。行政紀錄把教室列為閒置，而前任社長的報告仍未完成。 訪客離開後，樹檢查了門鎖。 社團收到工坊孩子送的一枚小獎章。 葵最後核對細節，花穗則準備給其他人的簡短說明。大家決定現在能做什麼、什麼必須等待。沒有人被要求拿安全或隱私交換漂亮成果。紙上的選擇很普通，卻關係到房間裡每一個人。 新鋪好的鋪面人行道從美術館通往公園。 畫框粗糙的邊緣刮到志工的手套。

Kaho could have returned to the quiet life she wanted. Instead, she wrote her own name beneath the club roster and asked what evidence the university needed to let them stay. Aoi asked for scientific evidence before trusting the old machine. A local trader donated ribbons without demanding an advertisement. Itsuki checked the door before leaving, and Kaho checked that the shared notes would be available tomorrow. Their habits were different, but together they protected more than either could alone. Mochi folded itself into a box and announced that even legendary guardians needed sleep. Nobody argued with that. The history display mentioned a coastal tribe's traditional lantern art. Kaho lowered the machine's volume so Mei could speak comfortably. Signing the roster did not turn Kaho into a hero. It made her a member, with work to share and a place where people expected her to return tomorrow.

花穗本可回到她想要的安靜生活。她卻在社員名單下簽名，問學校究竟需要什麼證據，才願意讓社團留下。 葵要求科學證據，才願意相信那部舊機器。 一名當地商人捐了緞帶，沒有要求廣告。 離開前，樹檢查門鎖，花穗確認共用筆記明天仍可查閱。他們習慣不同，合起來卻能保護更多事情。麻糬把自己摺進盒子，宣布傳說守護者也需要睡覺。沒人反對。 歷史展示提到沿海部落傳統燈藝。 花穗調低機器音量，讓美依能自在地說話。 在名單上簽字沒有讓花穗變成英雄，卻讓她成了社員，有工作可以分擔，也有一個明天有人盼她再來的地方。
