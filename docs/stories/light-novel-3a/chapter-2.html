不標價格的販賣機

A Vending Machine with No Price

月光失物招領社，今天也不接受退貨！ · 第 2 / 25 章 · 2000–3000 · 英中對照 · 校訂中

The next morning, Kaho returned with a written list of questions. Aoi said the booth took neither money nor secrets; it only worked when someone freely described what they hoped to recover. The old shopping avenue was quieter than Kaho expected. A wooden cradle held the oldest surviving festival lantern. Kaho glanced toward Itsuki, expecting the club president to supply an easy answer. He merely offered her a pencil and waited. That was annoying in a surprisingly useful way: she had to decide what she actually believed before repeating somebody else. Mochi considered the silence a suitable moment to request food. The organizer asked them to decorate the display only after permission. A tiny elevator carried boxes to the upstairs workshop.

隔天早上，花穗帶著寫好的問題回來。葵說機器不收錢，也不收祕密，只有當事人自願說出想找回什麼時才會運作。 老商店街比花穗預期的還安靜。 一座木製搖籃形支架托著最古老的祭典燈籠。 花穗望向樹，以為社長會給她一個簡單答案。他卻只遞來鉛筆，安靜等候。這有點令人懊惱，卻意外地有幫助：她必須先決定自己真正相信什麼，而不是重複別人的話。麻糬認為沉默正適合開口要食物。 籌辦人請大家先得到同意，再裝飾展示區。 一座小電梯把箱子送往樓上的工坊。

A ticket led them to the old shopping avenue, where a festival organizer had lost the design of a lantern parade. The organizer remembered drawing it, but not who had helped her. The exhibition included sketches from earlier student festivals. A single firework photograph showed the crowded square at night. Itsuki bent down to see what Kaho was pointing at, close enough for her to notice a spot of ink on his cheek. She almost mentioned it, then chose the more urgent question first. A good team, she was learning, did not need to agree immediately; it needed to notice the same facts. The cleaning team ordered one gallon of water for the brushes. A sign called the little office the festival headquarters.

一張票把眾人帶到老商店街。一名祭典籌辦人失去了燈籠遊行的設計圖；她記得自己畫過，卻忘了誰曾幫她完成。 展覽收錄了歷年學生祭典的素描。 一張煙火照片拍下夜晚擁擠的廣場。 樹彎身看花穗指著什麼，靠近到她能看見他臉頰上的墨點。她差點提醒他，最後仍先問比較緊急的事。她逐漸學到，好團隊不必立刻同意，而是要先看見同樣的事實。 清潔隊為畫筆準備了一加侖水。 招牌把那間小辦公室稱為祭典總部。

Kaho proposed interviewing the people who worked there instead of using magic to guess. Itsuki called that an excellent first decision; Mochi called it an inconvenient one because interviews had no free cake. Kaho's imagination supplied three impossible explanations for the missing corner. A painted leopard guarded the door of the costume shop. Kaho was embarrassed by how often her first answer turned out to be incomplete. Itsuki told her that changing a conclusion after learning something new was not defeat. She did not quite believe him yet, but she stopped crossing out her earlier notes. Keeping the mistake visible might help them avoid repeating it. Mochi claimed its magical talent included finding lost desserts. An odd smell came from the box of old paper decorations.

花穗提議訪問當時的工作人員，不用魔法猜答案。樹說這是出色的第一個決定；麻糬卻嫌麻煩，因為採訪不會附送免費蛋糕。 花穗的想像力為缺角提出三種不可能的解釋。 一隻畫出來的豹守著服裝店門口。 花穗常因第一個答案不完整而尷尬。樹告訴她，知道新事實後改變結論並不是失敗。她還不能完全相信，但不再把舊筆記塗掉。留下錯誤，也許能幫大家避免重犯。 麻糬宣稱自己的魔法才能還包括尋找失蹤的甜點。 裝滿舊紙飾品的箱子飄出奇怪氣味。

In a narrow shop, they found a photograph with a missing corner. The woman in it wore a ribbon printed with two names, but one name had been covered by fresh ink. The shop used organic glue to repair fragile lantern frames. A blue ribbon carried the two artists' original names. Itsuki suggested writing the decision in plain language before anyone signed it. Kaho read the sentence aloud and found a part that could be misunderstood. They fixed it together. The extra minutes felt worthwhile, especially after remembering how much trouble one careless line had already caused. Mochi promised not to spit cake crumbs onto the photograph. The stairs were steep, so they carried the boxes slowly.

他們在狹窄的店鋪找到缺角照片。照片中的女子繫著印有兩個名字的緞帶，卻有一個名字被新的墨水塗掉。 店家用有機膠修復脆弱的燈籠骨架。 藍色緞帶印著兩位創作者原本的名字。 樹提議在任何人簽字前，先把決定寫成簡單的文字。花穗朗讀時發現一處可能誤解的地方，大家一起修正。想到一行粗心文字曾造成多少麻煩，這幾分鐘就顯得很值得。 麻糬答應不把蛋糕屑吐在照片上。 樓梯很陡，因此他們慢慢搬運箱子。

The organizer chose to lend them the photograph for one day. Kaho copied the terms in her sketchbook, impressed by how much clearer a mystery became when everyone knew what had been agreed. Kaho prepared a short survey for the remaining workshop workers. The missing record was a threat to a fair public exhibition. Kaho closed her notebook on a question that would have to wait until tomorrow. Waiting no longer felt like giving up. It meant allowing time for the right person to answer. Outside, the campus lights were turning on one by one, and Itsuki walked beside her without demanding another promise. A tourist stopped to ask why the fox seemed to move. Aoi used a dry cloth to wipe dust from the picture frame. At the station crossing, Kaho looked back at the little shop and wondered how many ordinary people had been left out of stories that everybody thought they knew.

籌辦人同意借出照片一天。花穗把條件寫進素描本，驚訝地發現只要大家知道約定內容，謎團就清楚許多。 花穗為還在的工坊員工準備了簡短問卷。 遺失的紀錄會威脅公開展覽的公平性。 花穗闔上筆記本，把一個問題留到明天。等待不再像放棄，而是給真正有權回答的人一些時間。外頭校園燈光逐盞亮起，樹走在她身旁，沒有要求她再許下一個承諾。 一名觀光客停下來，問狐狸為何看起來會動。 葵用乾布擦去相框上的灰塵。 走過路口時，花穗回頭望著小店，心想那些人人自以為熟悉的故事裡，究竟漏掉了多少普通人的名字。
