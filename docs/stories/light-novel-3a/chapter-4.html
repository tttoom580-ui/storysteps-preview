Two Names on a Lantern

月光失物招領社，今天也不接受退貨！ · 第 4 / 25 章 · 2000–3000 · 英中對照 · 校訂中

The club visited the lantern workshop where Sora had once volunteered. Its owner admitted that two students had created the famous silver-lantern pattern, though recent posters credited only one. Mei said her ambition was to teach children to make lanterns. The oldest festival had once taken place inside a red barn. Nobody in the group had perfect information. Kaho reminded herself that uncertainty was not a reason to invent a satisfying story. They could ask one more question, check one more object, and leave the rest open for now. Itsuki looked relieved to hear somebody say that out loud. Mochi wore a paper collar that said Temporary Assistant. A free concert was scheduled outside the workshop that evening.

社員走訪空曾當志工的燈籠工坊。老闆承認著名的銀色燈籠圖案其實由兩名學生共同創作，但新海報只留下其中一人的名字。 美依說她的抱負是教孩子製作燈籠。 最早的祭典曾在一間紅色穀倉裡舉行。 團隊裡沒有任何人掌握全部資訊。花穗提醒自己，不確定不是編造漂亮故事的理由。大家可以再問一個問題、再檢查一件東西，其餘暫時保留。樹聽見有人把這話說出口，表情放鬆了。 麻糬戴著寫有「臨時助理」的紙領圈。 當晚工坊外安排了一場免費演唱會。

Kaho expected a villain. Instead, she met a tired printer who had copied an old file without checking its history. The error still mattered, even if nobody had planned it. Two versions of the poster could confuse visitors. The dim work lamp made the original signature hard to read. At first Kaho wished someone would give her a simple order. Then she remembered why she disliked being told what to think. She asked the others to slow down and explain their reasons. Even Mochi listened for a moment before returning to the important business of searching for biscuits. Aoi asked everyone not to freeze when the printer stopped. Mei appreciated Kaho's humor about the fox's wet feet.

花穗原以為會遇到壞人，結果只看到一位疲倦的印刷師，照著舊檔案複製卻沒查來源。即使沒人故意犯錯，錯誤依然重要。 兩種版本的海報可能讓訪客混淆。 昏暗的工作燈讓原本的簽名難以辨認。 起初花穗希望有人直接給她一道簡單命令，接著又想起自己為什麼討厭被規定想法。她請大家放慢速度，說明理由。連麻糬都聽了一會兒，才重新投入尋找餅乾的重大工作。 葵請大家不要在印表機停住時嚇得僵住。 美依欣賞花穗拿狐狸濕腳印開的玩笑。

Itsuki wanted to call Sora immediately, but Kaho asked him to listen to the second artist first. She had learned that a correct fact could still hurt when delivered without care. A junior volunteer carried the new paper frames. A visiting novelist had described the silver lantern in a story. Itsuki looked ready to take on the entire problem himself. Kaho recognized the expression because she sometimes made the same mistake. She reminded him that a club was not simply a president followed by helpers. Aoi nodded; Mochi objected that a kingdom certainly worked that way, and was ignored. The workshop lunch included an onion soup made by Mei's aunt. The painter wanted both signatures printed beneath the design.

樹想立刻聯絡空，但花穗請他先聽另一位作者的想法。她明白，即使說的是事實，沒有顧慮的公開方式仍可能傷人。 一位低年級志工搬來新的紙框。 一名來訪的小說家曾在故事裡描寫銀色燈籠。 樹的表情像準備一個人扛下所有問題。花穗認得那神情，因為她有時也犯同樣的錯。她提醒他，社團不是一個社長帶著一群助手。葵點頭；麻糬反對說王國就是如此運作，沒人理牠。 工坊午餐有美依阿姨煮的洋蔥湯。 畫家希望設計圖下方印出兩個簽名。

The second artist, Mei, said she wanted her name restored but did not want another public fight. They agreed to make a quiet correction before any new advertisements went out. The old drawing was precious because no second copy existed. Kaho waited for Mei's response before calling the printer. Kaho listened to a hesitant answer without trying to fill the silence. She had once mistaken confidence for kindness, but the two did not always travel together. Sometimes respect sounded like a quiet question and a willingness to hear no. Itsuki followed her lead, which surprised them both a little. A vendor prepared roast sweet potatoes outside the door. Aoi used a water spray to soften the stuck paper.

第二位作者美依說，她希望恢復自己的名字，卻不想再引發公開爭吵。大家同意先安靜更正，再發送新的宣傳品。 這張舊畫很珍貴，因為沒有第二份副本。 花穗等美依回覆後才打給印刷師。 花穗聽著遲疑的回答，沒有急著填補沉默。她以前把自信誤認成體貼，但兩者未必同行。有時尊重只是一句安靜的詢問，以及願意接受拒絕。樹跟著她做，兩人都有點意外。 攤販在門外準備烤地瓜。 葵用噴水器稍微潤濕黏住的紙張。

Mochi stepped on a wet sheet of ink and left fox footprints across the table. While everyone rushed to clean it, Kaho noticed a tiny date on the original design that did not match the festival record. A squirrel ran across the window while they worked. Itsuki gave the table a gentle tap to get everyone's attention. A little laughter returned as they cleaned the tables. Kaho noticed that the club had begun to feel less like a place she had entered by accident and more like a place she could choose. There would be another difficulty soon, she was sure. For tonight, they had done enough to go home. A traveler had donated the colored glass used in the lamps. Mei's vision was a festival where every maker received credit. Before leaving, Kaho copied the strange date twice. She had no idea what it meant yet, but she was finally learning not to throw away an uncomfortable detail.

麻糬踩進未乾的墨水，在桌上留下一排狐狸腳印。眾人忙著清理時，花穗發現原稿角落的日期竟與祭典紀錄不符。 工作時，一隻松鼠跑過窗邊。 樹輕敲桌子，讓所有人注意。 大家清理桌面時又有了些笑聲。花穗發現，這社團不再只是她意外走進的地方，而是她能自行選擇的地方。她確信下一個難題很快會來。今晚，他們已經做得夠多，可以回家了。 一位旅人曾捐贈製燈用的彩色玻璃。 美依的願景是一場每位製作者都得到署名的祭典。 離開前，花穗把那個奇怪日期抄了兩次。她還不知道它代表什麼，卻終於學會不隨手丟掉令人不安的細節。
