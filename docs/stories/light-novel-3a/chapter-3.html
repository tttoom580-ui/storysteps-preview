溫室裡那隻不是貓

The Greenhouse Cat Is Not a Cat

月光失物招領社，今天也不接受退貨！ · 第 3 / 25 章 · 2000–3000 · 英中對照 · 校訂中

用互動版查詞、朗讀與收藏

An unexpected ticket brought the club to the campus greenhouse. A first-year student had asked for help finding a little cat that appeared in every drawing but never in a photograph. The client could not afford a professional animal search team. Kaho wore an apron so the greenhouse soil would not stain her clothes. The room grew busy with opinions, and Kaho nearly lost track of the original question. She drew a little square around the most important detail in her notes. Aoi leaned over her shoulder, saw the square, and nodded. Something difficult became manageable once it had a clear place on the page. A blue bucket stood below a leaking garden pipe. The moving leaves cast a perfect cat-shaped shadow on the wall.

意外的票券把社員帶到校園溫室。某位一年級學生想找一隻總在畫裡出現，卻從未被相機拍到的小貓。 委託人負擔不起專業尋找動物團隊的費用。 花穗穿著圍裙，以免溫室土壤弄髒衣服。 房間裡意見紛紛，花穗差點忘記最初的問題。她在筆記中替最重要的細節畫上小方框。葵從肩後看見那個方框，點了點頭。難題一旦在紙上有了清楚的位置，似乎就比較能處理了。 藍色水桶放在漏水的花園水管下。 晃動的葉片在牆上投下完美的貓形陰影。

Aoi checked the lamps and discovered that the cat was a shadow cast by two moving plants. The discovery disappointed their client until Kaho offered to paint the beautiful shadow honestly. Aoi saved a small dessert for the disappointed client. Mochi dragged a trail of dirt across the clean floor. They moved the loose papers away from the edge of the table and made space to compare what each person had found. Kaho described her observation without decorating it. Aoi added what the equipment could and could not show. Their different skills made the picture less exciting, perhaps, but much more useful. The fox made a dramatic bow before revealing its wrong theory. The energetic greenhouse assistant ran between three tables.

葵檢查燈具後，發現那隻貓是兩盆會晃動的植物投下的影子。委託人很失望，直到花穗提議忠實畫下那片美麗陰影。 葵為失望的委託人留下一份小甜點。 麻糬在乾淨的地板上拖出一道泥土痕跡。 大家把鬆散紙張移開桌緣，騰出地方比較各自發現的線索。花穗不加渲染地描述觀察，葵補充設備能與不能顯示的內容。各種能力放在一起，也許讓事情少了刺激，卻更有用。 狐狸在公布錯誤推理前，戲劇化地鞠了一躬。 活力十足的溫室助理在三張桌子間跑來跑去。

Inside an old cabinet they found a box of festival props and a note signed by Sora, the former club captain. The note warned that a convincing picture was not always a true record. Each visitor wrote an entry in the public greenhouse log. The cat shadow began to fade when the morning sun moved. Aoi placed two sheets side by side, and the differences finally became visible. The club could argue about motives all afternoon, but a change on paper was something they could examine together. Kaho pointed to the line that needed an answer and asked who had the right to provide it. The greenhouse smelled of wet leaves and fresh paint. A safety inspector had recently checked the hanging lamps.

她們在舊櫃子裡找到祭典道具，以及前任社長空留下的便條。便條提醒大家：看起來可信的畫面，不一定是真實紀錄。 每位訪客都在溫室公開紀錄簿寫下紀錄。 晨光移動時，貓的影子開始淡去。 葵把兩張紙並排放好，差異終於清楚。社團大可爭論動機一整個下午，但紙上的修改是大家能共同檢查的。花穗指向需要回答的那一行，問誰有權提供答案。 溫室裡有濕葉與新油漆的味道。 安全檢查員最近才檢查過懸掛的燈具。

Kaho began to understand why Itsuki insisted on keeping both sketches and photographs. Mochi wanted to keep only the ones in which the fox looked handsome; nobody supported that suggestion. Mochi accepted a strawberry lollipop as payment for watching the door. The repair of the garden roof was still ongoing. The club divided the work into pieces small enough for each person to choose freely. Kaho handled the drawings, Aoi checked practical details, and Itsuki contacted the people who needed to decide. They did not turn disagreement into a contest. Mochi alone complained that no one had appointed a royal snack officer. An old festival poster displayed a giant paper ox. A little penguin figure stood beside the student drawings.

花穗逐漸明白樹為何堅持素描與照片都要保留。麻糬只想保存自己看起來英俊的照片，但沒有人支持牠的提議。 麻糬收下一根草莓棒棒糖，當作守門的報酬。 花園屋頂的修繕仍在進行中。 社團把工作分成每個人都能自由選擇的小部分。花穗負責畫作，葵檢查實務細節，樹聯絡需要作決定的人。他們沒有把不同意見變成比賽。只有麻糬抱怨沒人任命王室點心官。 舊祭典海報畫著一隻巨大的紙牛。 一個小企鵝模型立在學生畫作旁。

When the student received the new picture, she thanked them without asking for an impossible real cat. Their machine printed a second image: a paper lantern with its artist's name torn away. Kaho nearly stepped into a shallow pit beside the plants. Aoi pulled the plug before touching the faulty lamp. The club room looked ordinary again once the last visitor had left. Kaho knew better now than to trust appearances alone. She made a short list of what to check next and left it where everyone could read it. Aoi approved of the practical list; Mochi approved of the space for its name. A senior student remembered who had painted the lost lantern. Mochi tried to spy on the artists through a crack in the door. The new picture did not bring a real animal into the greenhouse, but the student carried it home with both hands, as carefully as if it were alive.

學生拿到新畫後向大家道謝，沒有再要求一隻不可能存在的真貓。機器隨後印出第二張畫：一只被撕掉作者姓名的紙燈籠。 花穗差點踩進植物旁的淺坑。 葵碰故障燈具前先拔掉插頭。 最後一位訪客離開後，社辦又顯得十分普通。花穗如今知道不能只相信表面。她列下接下來要檢查的事情，放在大家都看得到的地方。葵欣賞這份實用清單；麻糬則欣賞上面留了牠名字的位置。 一位高年級學生記得是誰畫了遺失的燈籠。 麻糬試圖從門縫偷看藝術家。 新畫沒有讓真貓出現在溫室，但學生用雙手小心捧回家，彷彿那張畫真的有生命。
