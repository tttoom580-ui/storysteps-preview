沒有人到場的那天

The Day Nobody Came

The club advertised a meeting about festival safety. Nobody arrived except a cleaner who wanted to know when she could finish the room. Kaho stared at twenty unused chairs. A child had drawn the alphabet around the edge of an old poster. The unused chairs came from a basement storage room. There were moments when Kaho wanted to disappear behind the nearest door. She stayed because the person in front of her deserved to finish speaking. Itsuki did not rush to rescue her from an awkward pause. The pause gave them a chance to notice a detail they might otherwise have missed. The cleaner pointed to a sign forbidding any cigarette indoors. Aoi used a metal clip to hold the new meeting notices.

社團宣傳了一場祭典安全座談。最後只有一位想知道何時能打掃完的清潔員出現。花穗盯著二十張空椅子發呆。 一個孩子曾在舊海報邊緣寫滿字母表。 沒有用上的椅子來自地下室儲藏間。 有些時候花穗真想躲進最近的門後，但她留下來，因為眼前的人值得把話說完。樹沒有急著替她打破尷尬沉默。這段沉默反而讓大家注意到原本可能錯過的細節。 清潔員指著室內禁菸的告示。 葵用金屬夾固定新的會議公告。

Kaho nearly concluded that nobody cared. The cleaner explained that many volunteers worked evenings and could not attend a meeting announced one day before it happened. A painted cock stood on the corner of a village festival banner. The archive collection included stories told by part-time workers. For a while the group worked without speaking. There was comfort in the sound of paper being turned and pencils moving across the table. Kaho realized that helping did not always require a brilliant speech. Sometimes it required staying present long enough to notice what another person was trying to show. The morning was foggy, so several commuters arrived late. Yuji offered to hire a translator for visitors who needed one.

花穗差點以為根本沒人在意。清潔員卻提醒她，許多志工晚上要工作，臨時前一天通知的會議根本沒辦法參加。 一隻畫出的公雞站在村莊祭典布條角落。 檔案收藏包括兼職工作者說出的故事。 有一段時間大家安靜工作，只有翻紙與鉛筆移動的聲音，反而讓人安心。花穗發現，幫忙不一定要說出驚人的話。有時只要留下來，花足夠時間看懂別人想給自己看的東西。 清晨霧很濃，幾名通勤者因此遲到了。 裕司提議聘請翻譯協助有需要的訪客。

Aoi helped Kaho prepare a short survey and two meeting times. Itsuki wrote an honest notice explaining what they knew, what they did not know, and how people could decline contact. Kaho valued honesty more than an impressive attendance number. The old workshop had once welcomed children from a kindergarten. Kaho caught herself wishing for a bright miracle, then looked at the ordinary people doing careful work around her. That seemed better than a miracle she could not explain. She offered to take one difficult task, and Itsuki accepted without asking whether she was strong enough to manage it. Itsuki admitted that learning to listen was harder than making posters. One volunteer said that liberty included the freedom not to speak.

葵幫花穗準備簡短問卷及兩個會議時段。樹寫出誠實公告，說清楚已知與未知，並告訴大家可以拒絕聯絡。 花穗認為誠實比漂亮的出席人數更重要。 舊工作坊以前曾接待一群幼兒園孩子。 花穗一度希望出現耀眼奇蹟，接著看見身旁那些認真做事的普通人。這似乎比無法解釋的奇蹟更好。她主動接下一件困難的工作，樹沒有先質疑她是否夠強。 樹承認學會傾聽比做海報還難。 一位志工說，自由也包含不發言的權利。

Several volunteers replied privately. One person remembered carrying lanterns while a narrow exit stayed locked; another remembered Sora checking each child before closing the doors. The cleaner's simple advice made the failed meeting meaningful. A packet of colored powder had been left with craft supplies. The decision was smaller than the dream that had brought them there. Kaho felt a moment of disappointment and allowed herself to feel it. Then she noticed that a smaller plan could still keep its promises. Itsuki asked whether she was ready to continue, and she answered only after checking that she truly was. They compared the new schedule with the previous notice. Mochi called a flower-shaped chair queer in an amused voice.

幾位志工私下回信。有人記得搬燈籠時狹窄出口仍鎖著；也有人記得空關門前逐一確認孩子都安全。 清潔員的簡單建議讓失敗的會議變得有意義。 一包彩色粉末被留在手作材料裡。 這項決定比當初的夢想小。花穗失望了一會兒，也允許自己失望。接著她發現，較小的計畫仍可以守住約定。樹問她準備好繼續了嗎，她確定自己真的準備好了才回答。 他們拿新時程與前一份公告對照。 麻糬覺得花形椅子很奇特，開玩笑地說了一句。

They did not treat silence as agreement. Kaho took down the empty chairs and decided the next invitation would fit the people they wanted to hear, not merely the team's timetable. Kaho asked permission before the team could reveal a volunteer's name. The cleaning shelf held a bottle of ordinary shampoo. The final task was not a victory pose but a careful record of what had happened. Kaho added the date and asked the others to check her words. Only then did she put away her pencil. She looked at Itsuki, found him looking back, and felt quietly glad that they had chosen to stay. A grandmother offered to weave ribbons for the next event. Itsuki said the cleaner had shown more wisdom than their posters. The empty room had taught them something a crowded meeting might not have taught: an invitation was useful only when the invited person could actually answer it.

社團沒有把沉默當作同意。花穗收起空椅子，決定下次邀請要配合受訪者的生活，而不是只照社員自己的時間。 花穗要求團隊先取得同意，才能透露志工名字。 清潔架上放著一瓶普通洗髮精。 最後要做的不是擺出勝利姿勢，而是仔細記錄當天的事。花穗寫下日期，請其他人確認用字，才把鉛筆收起來。她抬頭看樹，他也正看著她；她安靜地慶幸兩人都選擇留下。 一位奶奶願意為下次活動編織緞帶。 樹說清潔員展現的智慧勝過他們做的海報。 空蕩教室教給她們的，或許是滿場會議教不到的事：邀請只有在受邀的人真能回應時才有用。
