會說謊的祭典地圖

The Festival Map That Lied

月光失物招領社，今天也不接受退貨！ · 第 6 / 25 章 · 2000–3000 · 英中對照 · 校訂中

用互動版查詞、朗讀與收藏

The club obtained a public festival map and found a lane missing from the new version. It was the only easy route for people carrying heavy lantern frames toward the park. Kaho said they could achieve a safe route only by testing it. The organizer served a fruit cocktail to volunteers over twenty after work. There were moments when Kaho wanted to disappear behind the nearest door. She stayed because the person in front of her deserved to finish speaking. Itsuki did not rush to rescue her from an awkward pause. The pause gave them a chance to notice a detail they might otherwise have missed. A shorter walk would be desirable for people carrying heavy equipment. Mochi blamed a little devil in the map for every wrong turn.

社團取得公開祭典地圖，發現新版少了一條巷子。那原本是搬運沉重燈籠框架到公園最容易走的路。 花穗說只有實地測試才能達成安全路線的目標。 籌辦人在工作後提供水果雞尾酒給二十歲以上的志工。 有些時候花穗真想躲進最近的門後，但她留下來，因為眼前的人值得把話說完。樹沒有急著替她打破尷尬沉默。這段沉默反而讓大家注意到原本可能錯過的細節。 對搬運重物的人來說，較短的路線會比較理想。 麻糬把每次迷路都怪罪於地圖裡的小惡魔。

Itsuki suspected deliberate sabotage; Kaho suggested they walk the lane before accusing anyone. They discovered a damaged wall and a locked gate, both recorded in a repair request that had not been answered. Itsuki looked doubtful when the gate refused to open. Aoi warned them not to dump unused paint near the drain. For a while the group worked without speaking. There was comfort in the sound of paper being turned and pencils moving across the table. Kaho realized that helping did not always require a brilliant speech. Sometimes it required staying present long enough to notice what another person was trying to show. The local governor had once opened the festival with a short speech. An old inn provided a resting place along the longer route.

樹懷疑有人蓄意破壞；花穗建議先走過那條巷子再指責誰。眾人找到受損牆面與上鎖鐵門，兩者早已列在未處理的修繕申請中。 鐵門打不開時，樹露出懷疑的神色。 葵警告大家不要把剩餘油漆倒在排水口旁。 有一段時間大家安靜工作，只有翻紙與鉛筆移動的聲音，反而讓人安心。花穗發現，幫忙不一定要說出驚人的話。有時只要留下來，花足夠時間看懂別人想給自己看的東西。 地方首長以前曾用簡短演說為祭典揭幕。 一家老旅館在較長的路線上提供休息空間。

Aoi drew a safer route on clear paper and placed it over the old map. The difference was obvious enough that even Mochi stopped pretending not to understand human roads. Mochi found a cup of grape jelly in the volunteers' cooler. Aoi called Kaho her pal when they finished the new route. Kaho caught herself wishing for a bright miracle, then looked at the ordinary people doing careful work around her. That seemed better than a miracle she could not explain. She offered to take one difficult task, and Itsuki accepted without asking whether she was strong enough to manage it. A pile of broken wooden frames blocked the original path. A painted pole marked the corner where they had to turn.

葵把安全路線畫在透明紙上，再覆到舊地圖。差異如此清楚，連一直假裝看不懂人類道路的麻糬都不裝了。 麻糬在志工冰箱裡找到一杯葡萄果凍。 完成新路線後，葵稱花穗是自己的好夥伴。 花穗一度希望出現耀眼奇蹟，接著看見身旁那些認真做事的普通人。這似乎比無法解釋的奇蹟更好。她主動接下一件困難的工作，樹沒有先質疑她是否夠強。 一堆壞掉的木框擋住原本道路。 一根彩繪柱標出必須轉彎的路口。

The festival committee chair, Yuji, refused to publish an untested route. Kaho disliked his stiff manner but agreed that a good idea was not the same as an inspected path. Yuji refused to rank volunteers by how fast they could walk. A discarded razor lay in the grass, and a worker removed it safely. The decision was smaller than the dream that had brought them there. Kaho felt a moment of disappointment and allowed herself to feel it. Then she noticed that a smaller plan could still keep its promises. Itsuki asked whether she was ready to continue, and she answered only after checking that she truly was. The group needed a reliable map more than a dramatic story. The revised path passed through a quiet suburb near the river.

祭典委員長裕司拒絕公布未測試的路線。花穗不喜歡他僵硬的態度，卻承認好的主意不代表已受檢驗的道路。 裕司拒絕依步行速度替志工排名。 草地上有被丟棄的刮鬍刀，工作人員安全地收走了。 這項決定比當初的夢想小。花穗失望了一會兒，也允許自己失望。接著她發現，較小的計畫仍可以守住約定。樹問她準備好繼續了嗎，她確定自己真的準備好了才回答。 團隊需要可靠的地圖，勝過一個戲劇性的故事。 修訂後的路線穿過河邊寧靜的郊區。

They arranged a supervised walk with volunteers. That evening, the booth printed a ticket marked with Sora's initials and a note saying the missing lane had caused trouble before. Itsuki said their idea was not superior to an official inspection. They followed a narrow trail until it reached the main road. The final task was not a victory pose but a careful record of what had happened. Kaho added the date and asked the others to check her words. Only then did she put away her pencil. She looked at Itsuki, found him looking back, and felt quietly glad that they had chosen to stay. Kaho hoped the project would build unity among different clubs. A festival story mentioned two friends who chose to wed by the sea. They decided to keep both maps: the familiar old one and the safer revised one. The difference between them would become part of their report, not an embarrassment to hide.

大家安排志工監督實地試走。當晚，機器印出一張有空姓名縮寫的票，還寫著這條消失的巷子以前就出過問題。 樹說他們的主意不能凌駕正式檢查。 他們沿著窄小步道走到主要道路。 最後要做的不是擺出勝利姿勢，而是仔細記錄當天的事。花穗寫下日期，請其他人確認用字，才把鉛筆收起來。她抬頭看樹，他也正看著她；她安靜地慶幸兩人都選擇留下。 花穗希望企畫能讓不同社團更團結。 祭典故事提到兩位朋友選擇在海邊結婚。 大家決定留下兩張地圖：熟悉的舊版與較安全的新版。兩者差異會寫進報告，而不是當成羞恥掩蓋。
