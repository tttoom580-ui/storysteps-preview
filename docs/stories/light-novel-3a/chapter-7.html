雨天兌換券

The Coupon for a Rainy Day

月光失物招領社，今天也不接受退貨！ · 第 7 / 25 章 · 2000–3000 · 英中對照 · 校訂中

Rain spoiled the club's first outdoor rehearsal. A visitor brought an old coupon promising a sunny day, and Mochi immediately offered to redeem it with dramatic confidence. The booth's automatic timer stopped the moment rain hit the sensor. A small automobile carried supplies to the community hall. Kaho had promised herself she would not join another troublesome project. Yet an unanswered question always had a way of following her home. She opened her notebook instead of pretending not to care. The decision seemed small, but Itsuki noticed it immediately and moved aside to make room for her. The charity had raised money to clothe children in winter. A volunteer committee approved the indoor drawing lesson.

大雨毀了社團第一次戶外彩排。一位訪客帶來寫著能換得晴天的舊券，麻糬立刻滿懷自信地說要替他兌換。 雨水碰到感測器時，機器的自動計時器就停住了。 一輛小汽車把物資運往活動中心。 花穗曾答應自己不再加入麻煩計畫，但還沒回答的問題總會一路跟著她回家。她打開筆記本，沒有假裝不在乎。這決定看似微小，樹卻立刻注意到，還挪開位置讓她坐下。 那個慈善活動曾募款替孩子準備冬衣。 志工委員會同意舉辦室內繪畫課。

Kaho discovered that the coupon came from a children's charity event, not a real weather service. Its promise was to offer an indoor activity whenever bad weather canceled a picnic. Mochi warned that the wet paper would drown in the puddle. Each child received a warm dumpling after the activity. Aoi treated every surprising result like an ordinary problem that could be examined. She asked who had seen it, when it had happened, and whether they could check it again. Mochi announced that such questions would make magic terribly unpopular. Kaho wrote them down anyway, one careful line at a time. The hall caretaker repaired a dripping faucet before class. A low fence kept visitors away from the flooded garden.

花穗發現這是兒童公益活動的兌換券，不是氣象服務。它真正的承諾是：下雨取消野餐時，主辦方會提供室內活動。 麻糬警告濕掉的紙會在水窪裡淹沒。 活動後每個孩子都拿到一顆熱餃子。 葵把每個驚人的結果都當成可以檢查的普通問題。她問誰看過、何時發生，以及能不能再確認一次。麻糬宣布這些問題會讓魔法變得很不受歡迎。花穗還是一行一行仔細記下。 活動中心管理員上課前修好了滴水的水龍頭。 矮圍籬讓訪客遠離淹水花園。

Instead of inventing impossible sunshine, the club opened a free drawing class under the community hall roof. Several children painted rain as silver strings rather than a mistake. A flock of birds took shelter beneath the sports hall roof. The cafeteria gave children juicy orange slices as a snack. Mochi insisted that every serious meeting needed a royal announcement. Aoi replied that every royal announcement needed an accurate time and a clear purpose. Their argument lasted less than a minute before Kaho interrupted with the question that actually mattered. Itsuki hid a smile behind the nearest folder. A flash of lightning made everyone move farther from the windows. Kaho felt the mental weight of keeping so many children calm.

社團沒有憑空造出晴天，而是在活動中心屋簷下開設免費繪畫課。幾個孩子把雨畫成銀線，不再覺得下雨是錯誤。 一群鳥躲到體育館屋簷下避雨。 餐廳提供多汁的柳橙片當孩子的點心。 麻糬堅持嚴肅會議都需要王室宣言。葵回說王室宣言至少也要有準確時間和清楚目的。不到一分鐘，花穗便用真正重要的問題打斷爭論，樹躲在資料夾後偷笑。 一道閃電讓大家離窗戶更遠一些。 花穗感受到安撫這麼多孩子的心理壓力。

Itsuki held the paper while Kaho guided small hands across the page. They worked together so easily that Aoi accused them of practicing a romance scene. Both denied it much too quickly. Aoi warmed the soup in a microwave after the class ended. Without its wet paper cape, Mochi looked almost naked. Kaho asked whether the people affected by the choice had been allowed to speak. The question changed the pace of the conversation. Even those who preferred a quick answer had to admit that the answer would mean little without consent. Mochi was unusually quiet, which Aoi described as a historic achievement. A bird had built its nest under the covered walkway. Itsuki reminded the children not to pollute the stream with paint.

樹替花穗扶住畫紙，她則引導孩子的小手移動。兩人配合得太自然，葵忍不住說他們在排戀愛戲。兩人否認得太快了。 課程結束後，葵用微波爐加熱湯。 少了濕掉的紙披風，麻糬看起來幾乎光溜溜的。 花穗問，受這項選擇影響的人是否有機會發言。這個問題改變了談話速度。就連偏好快速答案的人，也得承認沒有同意的答案意義不大。麻糬難得安靜，葵稱這是歷史性的成就。 有隻鳥把巢築在有遮棚的走道下。 樹提醒孩子不要用顏料污染溪水。

The visitor collected his ticket with a smile, then mentioned that Sora had organized the same indoor workshop years ago. That detail made the old festival records worth another look. The children designed a poster about river protection. A worker brought a shovel to clear mud from the entrance. By the time they put their things away, the day had turned a little different from the one Kaho expected. She had not solved every problem, but she knew one fact she had not known before. Itsuki thanked her without making a grand speech. Mochi demanded that gratitude be served with a biscuit. Mochi called stealing a lunch biscuit its greatest sin. The cook saved one egg yolk for the festival cake. On the way home, Kaho noticed that the rain had never actually stopped. Somehow that no longer felt like a problem the club had failed to solve.

訪客笑著領回票，順口提到空多年前也辦過同樣的室內課。這個細節讓大家重新注意舊祭典紀錄。 孩子們設計了一張河川保護海報。 工人拿來一把鏟子清除入口的泥土。 當大家收好東西時，這一天已經和花穗原本想的不一樣。她沒有解決所有問題，卻知道了一件先前不知道的事。樹沒有發表長篇演說，只簡單道謝。麻糬則要求感謝必須附一塊餅乾。 麻糬說偷午餐餅乾是牠最大的罪過。 廚師留下一顆蛋黃準備做祭典蛋糕。 回家路上，花穗發現雨其實一直沒停。但這次，她不再覺得那是社團沒能解決的失敗。
