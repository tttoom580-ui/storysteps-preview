The distinction was familiar, and that was precisely why it worried the people who had to act. Familiar arrangements could hide old mistakes remarkably well. Her voice was not flawless; it had simply become unmistakably her own. It might help to assign a qualified observer, but An He refused to mistake possibility for proof. 安禾在洗衣店樓上的小錄音室見到製作人林鏡。尚未有人開口，房間便分成被承諾安全的人，以及被期待提供安全的人。這種分別太熟悉，反而令必須行動的人不安。看似理所當然的安排，很容易藏住 오래以前的錯誤。她的聲音不完美，卻終於毫無疑問是自己的。「指派」或許有幫助，安禾卻拒絕把可能當成證明。舊標日期的試唱帶出現了「卡路里」，安禾追問新版為何漏掉。在能夠「專注」之前，團隊需要一位能自由提出異議的見證人。

The room had noisy pipes, but its files included every separate track of the new song. The records did not tell a single story. One column described an intention; another showed what happened after real people entered the space. The difference was inconvenient, especially for those who had already spent money defending the original plan. Inconvenience was not a reason to erase it. Listeners loved the rough breathing left between the notes. The first result was neither victory nor defeat. After the argument, a long silence settled around the group. It was not agreement. People were calculating what the next attempt might take from them, and whether anyone would notice the loss. Their caution was an ordinary human response, not a flaw in their courage.
