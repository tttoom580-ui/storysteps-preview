After the argument, a long silence settled around the group. It was not agreement. People were calculating what the next attempt might take from them, and whether anyone would notice the loss. Their caution was an ordinary human response, not a flaw in their courage. An He glanced toward Lin Jing and remembered the principle they had been arguing about: a voice belongs first to its creator. It sounded simple until it demanded another hour of work. The melody returned to An He at night, even when the contract papers refused to leave her desk. They found a reference to orchestra near the damaged dated demo, but its date was unreadable.
