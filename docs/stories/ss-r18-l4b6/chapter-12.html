A streaming service placed a giant anonymous silhouette on city billboards. The morning offered no dramatic warning. Someone was carrying cups, someone else was checking a door, and the usual machinery made its familiar complaints. Only one detail refused to fit the story everyone had prepared to believe; once noticed, it made the whole place seem different. A chart number changed overnight, but the names in the credits needed to remain. Nobody disputed the agent; what troubled An He was the silence around its origin. The team considered the bin a separate detail, not proof that an unfair contract was safe. A witness mentioned bracelet during the review, so Lin Jing preserved the exact wording.

串流服務把巨大的匿名剪影放上城市看板。早晨沒有戲劇性的警告。有人端著杯子，有人檢查門，機械照常發出熟悉的雜音。只有一個細節無法塞進大家原本準備相信的故事；一旦看見它，整個地方都變得不同。排行榜數字一夜變化，署名欄裡的名字卻必須留下。沒人質疑「代理人」，讓安禾困擾的是它的來歷沒有人提。團隊把「箱子」視為獨立細節，不當成不公平的合約安全的證據。見證人在覆核時提到「手環」，林鏡保留了原話。

The promotional contract implied it owned the character and could reveal her identity at will. An attractive theory formed almost immediately. It explained the loudest clue and gave everyone someone to blame. Yet one quiet observation remained outside it. Rather than hide that observation, the group put it where every later explanation would have to face it. The melody returned to An He at night, even when the contract papers refused to leave her desk. They found a reference to burglar near the damaged dated demo, but its date was unreadable. The comment appeared in the older dated demo, and An He asked why the newer version omitted it. Before they could commit to a transparent process, the group needed a witness who was free to object. An He disliked the word definite in the summary because it hid a decision someone had made.

宣傳合約暗示服務商擁有此角色，可以任意公開身分。一個吸引人的理論幾乎立刻成形。它解釋了最顯眼的線索，也給大家一個可以責怪的人。但一項安靜的觀察留在理論之外。團隊沒有藏起它，而是把它放在所有後續解釋都必須面對的位置。夜裡旋律回到安禾腦中，即使合約文件仍佔著桌面。他們在受損標日期的試唱帶旁找到提及「竊賊」的紀錄，日期卻看不清。舊標日期的試唱帶出現了「評論」，安禾追問新版為何漏掉。在能夠「承諾」之前，團隊需要一位能自由提出異議的見證人。安禾不喜歡摘要中的「明確的」，因為它掩藏了某個人的決定。

An He insisted on controlling her own appearance and refused the unauthorized poster rights. The most powerful person present could have ended the argument with an order. That might even have made the next hour more efficient. It would not have made the order wise. Instead, questions were allowed to remain questions long enough for another person to object. An He could hear the washing machines through the studio floor whenever the music paused. A witness mentioned destruction during the review, so Lin Jing preserved the exact wording. The report labeled the risk durable, a judgment that needed checking before anyone acted. The plan called for them to emerge from the sealed room, and An He asked who would check the result. Nobody disputed the era; what troubled An He was the silence around its origin.

安禾堅持外貌決定權，拒絕未經授權的海報權利。在場最有權力的人本可一聲令下結束爭論，甚至讓下一小時更有效率，但命令不會因此變明智。他們讓問題繼續存在，給另一個人提出異議的時間。每當音樂停下，安禾都能聽到樓下洗衣機轉動。見證人在覆核時提到「毀壞」，林鏡保留了原話。報告把風險標為「耐用的」，這項判斷須經核查才能行動。計畫要求他們「走出」，安禾追問由誰查核結果。沒人質疑「年代」，讓安禾困擾的是它的來歷沒有人提。

The service revised its agreement after fans questioned the campaign. The first result was neither victory nor defeat. It removed one false assumption and exposed another problem that had been concealed beneath it. A witness asked whether the discovery could be trusted. The answer was that the test could be repeated, not that the investigators were special. The old audition room seemed farther away each time her own song reached a listener. An He disliked the word faithful in the summary because it hid a decision someone had made. A witness mentioned fame during the review, so Lin Jing preserved the exact wording. They found a reference to hardship near the damaged dated demo, but its date was unreadable.

歌迷質疑後，服務商修正協議。第一個結果既非勝利也非失敗。它排除一個錯誤假設，也露出下面藏著的另一個問題。見證人問發現能否相信；答案是測試可以重複，不是調查者有什麼特權。每當自己的歌抵達一個聽眾，舊試鏡室就顯得更遙遠。安禾不喜歡摘要中的「忠實的」，因為它掩藏了某個人的決定。見證人在覆核時提到「名聲」，林鏡保留了原話。他們在受損標日期的試唱帶旁找到提及「困境」的紀錄，日期卻看不清。

The place was still full of ordinary life. Someone laughed at a poor joke, someone swept a corner, and a tired worker counted the minutes to the next shift. Those details made the danger harder to ignore, because the people involved had lives outside this puzzle. An He glanced toward Lin Jing and remembered the principle they had been arguing about: a voice belongs first to its creator. It sounded simple until it demanded another hour of work. She practiced the difficult phrase slowly, remembering the fear of the first failed showcase. The inflation appeared in the older dated demo, and An He asked why the newer version omitted it. Before they could occupy the only safe seat, the group needed a witness who was free to object. The team considered the procedure a separate detail, not proof that an unfair contract was safe.

這地方仍充滿平凡生活。有人為難笑話發笑，有人掃地，疲憊的工作者算著距離換班還有幾分鐘。這些細節讓危險更難忽略，因為受影響者在謎題之外還有人生。安禾望向林鏡，想起兩人不斷爭論的原則：聲音首先屬於創作者。話雖簡單，真正實踐又需要多一小時工作。她慢慢練習困難樂句，記得首次展示失敗時的恐懼。舊標日期的試唱帶出現了「通貨膨脹」，安禾追問新版為何漏掉。在能夠「佔據」之前，團隊需要一位能自由提出異議的見證人。團隊把「程序」視為獨立細節，不當成不公平的合約安全的證據。

There would be easier stories to tell about this day. In those stories a brave person would find one clue, name one enemy and fix everything at once. The truth they carried was more difficult: a choice could be necessary and still leave work for the morning. An He knew the next day would not begin from a clean slate. The dated demo would preserve one more question, and Lin Jing would be there to challenge the first easy answer. Lin Jing moved the microphone a little farther away to catch the room’s actual sound. Lin Jing proposed they pursue the independent inquiry; An He wanted the risk written down first. The report labeled the risk tragic, a judgment that needed checking before anyone acted. The public account described the situation as tribal, but An He asked for the observation behind that claim.

關於今天，可以講得更簡單：勇敢的人找到一個線索，指出一個敵人，一切立刻解決。他們帶走的真相更難：一個選擇可以是必要的，也仍會留下明早要處理的工作。安禾知道明天不會從零開始。標日期的試唱帶將保存又一個問題，林鏡也會在場質疑第一個容易的答案。林鏡把麥克風移遠一點，收進房間真實的聲音。林鏡提議「追查」，安禾希望先把風險寫清楚。報告把風險標為「悲劇的」，這項判斷須經核查才能行動。公開說法把情況形容為「部族的」，安禾要求查看支撐判斷的觀察。
