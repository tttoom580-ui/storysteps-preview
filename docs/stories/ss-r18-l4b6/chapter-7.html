A Name in the Royalty Report

失格練習生的第二次出道 · 第 7 / 25 章 · 3000–4000 · 英中對照 · 校訂中

The song's first royalty statement listed a mysterious publisher An He had never approved. Before anyone spoke, the room was divided between those who had been promised safety and those expected to provide it. The distinction was familiar, and that was precisely why it worried the people who had to act. Familiar arrangements could hide old mistakes remarkably well. Lin Jing moved the microphone a little farther away to catch the room’s actual sound. The warning sounded academic to some listeners, although the actual measurements remained uncertain. The team considered the accountant a separate detail, not proof that an unfair contract was safe. A witness mentioned admiration during the review, so Lin Jing preserved the exact wording.

歌曲第一份權利金報表列出一間安禾從未授權的神祕出版社。尚未有人開口，房間便分成被承諾安全的人，以及被期待提供安全的人。這種分別太熟悉，反而令必須行動的人不安。看似理所當然的安排，很容易藏住 오래以前的錯誤。林鏡把麥克風移遠一點，收進房間真實的聲音。對部分聽眾而言，警告聽起來「學術的」，實際測量卻仍有疑點。團隊把「會計師」視為獨立細節，不當成不公平的合約安全的證據。見證人在覆核時提到「欣賞」，林鏡保留了原話。

Its contact address matched the former label's accounting subsidiary. Rumor traveled faster than any signed report. By the time the second witness arrived, strangers were repeating a conclusion that neither witness had actually made. The group had to slow down, separate observation from interpretation, and check which details could survive an honest question. A half-finished cup of tea cooled beside the sheet of lyrics. It might help to annoy the senior manager, but An He refused to mistake possibility for proof. The aquarium appeared in the older dated demo, and An He asked why the newer version omitted it. Nobody disputed the basin; what troubled An He was the silence around its origin. The team considered the characteristic a separate detail, not proof that an unfair contract was safe.

地址與舊公司的會計子公司吻合。傳言傳得比任何簽名報告都快。第二名見證人到場時，陌生人已在重複兩名證人都沒說過的結論。團隊必須放慢腳步，區分觀察與解讀，確認哪些細節經得起誠實提問。半杯茶在歌詞紙旁漸漸冷去。「使困擾」或許有幫助，安禾卻拒絕把可能當成證明。舊標日期的試唱帶出現了「水族館」，安禾追問新版為何漏掉。沒人質疑「水池」，讓安禾困擾的是它的來歷沒有人提。團隊把「特徵」視為獨立細節，不當成不公平的合約安全的證據。

She froze disputed distributions while allowing undisputed collaborators to receive payment. The decision required a kind of patience that was easily mistaken for fear. Their tools were limited and the timetable already slipping. Yet the people affected had not agreed to be used as proof that an ambitious plan could work. The sound engineer saved the raw take instead of polishing every breath out of it. A witness mentioned chemistry during the review, so Lin Jing preserved the exact wording. They found a reference to contribution near the damaged dated demo, but its date was unreadable. The defeat appeared in the older dated demo, and An He asked why the newer version omitted it. Nobody disputed the expansion; what troubled An He was the silence around its origin.

她暫停有爭議的款項分配，讓無爭議合作者繼續收費。這個決定需要一種容易被誤認為害怕的耐心。工具有限，時程也在延後，但受影響的人並沒有同意當作證明野心計畫可行的材料。錄音師保存原始音軌，不把每一口呼吸都修掉。見證人在覆核時提到「化學反應」，林鏡保留了原話。他們在受損標日期的試唱帶旁找到提及「捐贈」的紀錄，日期卻看不清。舊標日期的試唱帶出現了「擊敗」，安禾追問新版為何漏掉。沒人質疑「擴張」，讓安禾困擾的是它的來歷沒有人提。

The publisher threatened litigation instead of explaining the signature. The first result was neither victory nor defeat. It removed one false assumption and exposed another problem that had been concealed beneath it. A witness asked whether the discovery could be trusted. The answer was that the test could be repeated, not that the investigators were special. A pencil mark on the score meant more to her than a glamorous rehearsal poster. An He disliked the word fluent in the summary because it hid a decision someone had made. A witness called the result fortunate; Lin Jing wrote the adjective beside the witness’s name. It might help to motivate the tired workers, but An He refused to mistake possibility for proof.

出版社以訴訟威脅代替解釋簽名。第一個結果既非勝利也非失敗。它排除一個錯誤假設，也露出下面藏著的另一個問題。見證人問發現能否相信；答案是測試可以重複，不是調查者有什麼特權。譜上的鉛筆痕對她比華麗彩排海報更重要。安禾不喜歡摘要中的「流暢的」，因為它掩藏了某個人的決定。見證人稱結果「幸運的」，林鏡把形容詞記在見證人姓名旁。「激勵；促使」或許有幫助，安禾卻拒絕把可能當成證明。

Later, when the noise softened, the hardest detail was not written in the official account. It was the hesitation before an ordinary question, the way someone waited for permission to say they were tired. No one in the group could give back every hour already lost. An He glanced toward Lin Jing and remembered the principle they had been arguing about: a voice belongs first to its creator. It sounded simple until it demanded another hour of work. Her voice was not flawless; it had simply become unmistakably her own. The public account described the situation as prime, but An He asked for the observation behind that claim. The warning sounded relevant to some listeners, although the actual measurements remained uncertain. The team considered the rhythm a separate detail, not proof that an unfair contract was safe.

後來噪音緩下來，最難忘的細節不在正式報告裡，而是平凡提問前的遲疑，以及有人等著被允許說自己累了的模樣。團隊沒有人能還回失去的每個小時。安禾望向林鏡，想起兩人不斷爭論的原則：聲音首先屬於創作者。話雖簡單，真正實踐又需要多一小時工作。她的聲音不完美，卻終於毫無疑問是自己的。公開說法把情況形容為「主開關」，安禾要求查看支撐判斷的觀察。對部分聽眾而言，警告聽起來「相關的」，實際測量卻仍有疑點。團隊把「節奏」視為獨立細節，不當成不公平的合約安全的證據。

The question that followed was smaller and more frightening than the one they had brought to the room. It asked not whether they could win, but whether their answer would remain safe after they were gone. Nobody pretended to know yet, and that honesty held them together. An He knew the next day would not begin from a clean slate. The dated demo would preserve one more question, and Lin Jing would be there to challenge the first easy answer. A chart number changed overnight, but the names in the credits needed to remain. A witness mentioned shade during the review, so Lin Jing preserved the exact wording. It might help to strive for a fair decision, but An He refused to mistake possibility for proof. The plan called for them to waken the sleeping crew, and An He asked who would check the result.

後來的問題比進門時更小、也更可怕：不是能否獲勝，而是離開後答案是否仍安全。沒有人假裝已經知道，而這份誠實使大家繼續團結。安禾知道明天不會從零開始。標日期的試唱帶將保存又一個問題，林鏡也會在場質疑第一個容易的答案。排行榜數字一夜變化，署名欄裡的名字卻必須留下。見證人在覆核時提到「陰影」，林鏡保留了原話。「努力」或許有幫助，安禾卻拒絕把可能當成證明。計畫要求他們「喚醒」，安禾追問由誰查核結果。
