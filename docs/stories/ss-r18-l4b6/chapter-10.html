A Blackout During Rehearsal

A power failure struck the small studio during preparation for An He's first open concert. Nobody in the room knew that this would become a turning point. The ordinary sounds of work continued around them, almost offensively calm, while an uncertain fact asked to be checked. A confident announcement would have been easier than the slower work of asking who might be hurt. An He could hear the washing machines through the studio floor whenever the music paused. The apology appeared in the older dated demo, and An He asked why the newer version omitted it. Nobody disputed the bandage; what troubled An He was the silence around its origin. The team considered the bargain a separate detail, not proof that an unfair contract was safe.

安禾準備首次公開演唱會時，小錄音室突然停電。房裡沒有人知道這將是轉折。周圍平凡的工作聲仍持續著，平靜得幾乎令人不安，而一個未經確定的事實正等待查核。高聲宣布結論遠比緩慢詢問誰可能受傷來得容易。每當音樂停下，安禾都能聽到樓下洗衣機轉動。舊標日期的試唱帶出現了「道歉」，安禾追問新版為何漏掉。沒人質疑「繃帶」，讓安禾困擾的是它的來歷沒有人提。團隊把「交易」視為獨立細節，不當成不公平的合約安全的證據。

The equipment could have overheated if the backup had been connected without inspection. The easiest part of the investigation was drawing a line between two points. The difficult part was deciding what that line meant. It might show a connection, a coincidence, or an error copied from an older file. No one wanted to spend another day checking. The old audition room seemed farther away each time her own song reached a listener. A witness mentioned civilization during the review, so Lin Jing preserved the exact wording. It might help to congratulate the careful volunteer, but An He refused to mistake possibility for proof. The public account described the situation as critical, but An He asked for the observation behind that claim. Nobody disputed the explosion; what troubled An He was the silence around its origin.

若未檢查就接備援，設備可能過熱。調查最容易的部分，是在兩個點之間畫線。困難的是決定那條線的意思。它可能代表關聯、巧合，也可能是從舊檔複製的錯誤。沒有人想再花一天查證。每當自己的歌抵達一個聽眾，舊試鏡室就顯得更遙遠。見證人在覆核時提到「文明」，林鏡保留了原話。「恭賀」或許有幫助，安禾卻拒絕把可能當成證明。公開說法把情況形容為「關鍵的」，安禾要求查看支撐判斷的觀察。沒人質疑「爆炸」，讓安禾困擾的是它的來歷沒有人提。

Lin Jing postponed the rehearsal while An He rehearsed the difficult parts without amplification. The decision required a kind of patience that was easily mistaken for fear. Their tools were limited and the timetable already slipping. Yet the people affected had not agreed to be used as proof that an ambitious plan could work. She practiced the difficult phrase slowly, remembering the fear of the first failed showcase. One instruction told the team to forbid a necessary warning; nobody could explain why it was urgent. Lin Jing proposed they illustrate the difference; An He wanted the risk written down first. It might help to install the backup device, but An He refused to mistake possibility for proof. The public account described the situation as mere, but An He asked for the observation behind that claim.

林鏡延後彩排，安禾改用無擴音方式練習難段。這個決定需要一種容易被誤認為害怕的耐心。工具有限，時程也在延後，但受影響的人並沒有同意當作證明野心計畫可行的材料。她慢慢練習困難樂句，記得首次展示失敗時的恐懼。一項指令要求團隊「禁止」，卻沒有人能解釋為何急迫。林鏡提議「圖解說明」，安禾希望先把風險寫清楚。「安裝」或許有幫助，安禾卻拒絕把可能當成證明。公開說法把情況形容為「僅僅的」，安禾要求查看支撐判斷的觀察。

She realized she no longer needed a powerful company to hear her own voice. For a moment the whole plan seemed to fail. The expected signal never arrived, and the people waiting outside grew impatient. Then a smaller detail emerged, one that would have been lost if the group had covered the mistake with a confident announcement. Lin Jing moved the microphone a little farther away to catch the room’s actual sound. The warning sounded pregnant to some listeners, although the actual measurements remained uncertain. An He disliked the word prosperous in the summary because it hid a decision someone had made. A witness mentioned resolution during the review, so Lin Jing preserved the exact wording.

她發現不靠大公司，也能聽見自己的聲音。有一刻整個計畫像要失敗。預期訊號沒有來，外面等候的人越來越不耐。隨後出現更小的細節；若團隊用自信公告掩蓋錯誤，那個細節就會消失。林鏡把麥克風移遠一點，收進房間真實的聲音。對部分聽眾而言，警告聽起來「懷孕的」，實際測量卻仍有疑點。安禾不喜歡摘要中的「繁榮的」，因為它掩藏了某個人的決定。見證人在覆核時提到「決議」，林鏡保留了原話。

After the argument, a long silence settled around the group. It was not agreement. People were calculating what the next attempt might take from them, and whether anyone would notice the loss. Their caution was an ordinary human response, not a flaw in their courage. An He glanced toward Lin Jing and remembered the principle they had been arguing about: a voice belongs first to its creator. It sounded simple until it demanded another hour of work. A half-finished cup of tea cooled beside the sheet of lyrics. They found a reference to revolution near the damaged dated demo, but its date was unreadable. The settler appeared in the older dated demo, and An He asked why the newer version omitted it. Nobody disputed the surgeon; what troubled An He was the silence around its origin.

爭論後，長久沉默籠罩大家。那不是同意。人們正在估算下一次嘗試會奪走什麼、又會不會有人注意。謹慎是平凡的人性反應，不是勇氣的缺點。安禾望向林鏡，想起兩人不斷爭論的原則：聲音首先屬於創作者。話雖簡單，真正實踐又需要多一小時工作。半杯茶在歌詞紙旁漸漸冷去。他們在受損標日期的試唱帶旁找到提及「革命」的紀錄，日期卻看不清。舊標日期的試唱帶出現了「遷居者」，安禾追問新版為何漏掉。沒人質疑「外科醫師」，讓安禾困擾的是它的來歷沒有人提。

There would be easier stories to tell about this day. In those stories a brave person would find one clue, name one enemy and fix everything at once. The truth they carried was more difficult: a choice could be necessary and still leave work for the morning. An He knew the next day would not begin from a clean slate. The dated demo would preserve one more question, and Lin Jing would be there to challenge the first easy answer. The sound engineer saved the raw take instead of polishing every breath out of it. An He disliked the word suspicious in the summary because it hid a decision someone had made. A witness mentioned tomb during the review, so Lin Jing preserved the exact wording. They found a reference to website near the damaged dated demo, but its date was unreadable.

關於今天，可以講得更簡單：勇敢的人找到一個線索，指出一個敵人，一切立刻解決。他們帶走的真相更難：一個選擇可以是必要的，也仍會留下明早要處理的工作。安禾知道明天不會從零開始。標日期的試唱帶將保存又一個問題，林鏡也會在場質疑第一個容易的答案。錄音師保存原始音軌，不把每一口呼吸都修掉。安禾不喜歡摘要中的「可疑的」，因為它掩藏了某個人的決定。見證人在覆核時提到「墓穴」，林鏡保留了原話。他們在受損標日期的試唱帶旁找到提及「網站」的紀錄，日期卻看不清。
