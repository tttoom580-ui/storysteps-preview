A music festival invited the anonymous singer to perform behind a screen. It began with something that could be held in one hand, a small object too ordinary to attract attention. Yet the longer they looked, the less ordinary it became. The real problem was not its appearance but the decision someone had already made on its behalf. She practiced the difficult phrase slowly, remembering the fear of the first failed showcase. The report labeled the risk abstract, a judgment that needed checking before anyone acted. The plan called for them to acquire the missing permission, and An He asked who would check the result. Nobody disputed the bridegroom; what troubled An He was the silence around its origin.

音樂節邀請匿名歌手在布幕後演出。事情從一個單手能拿起的小物件開始，普通得幾乎沒人會注意。可看得越久，它就越不尋常。真正的問題不在外表，而是有人早已代替它做出了決定。她慢慢練習困難樂句，記得首次展示失敗時的恐懼。報告把風險標為「抽象的」，這項判斷須經核查才能行動。計畫要求他們「取得」，安禾追問由誰查核結果。沒人質疑「新郎」，讓安禾困擾的是它的來歷沒有人提。

The contract would reveal her voice but forbid any statement about her employment history. A neat explanation would have spared everyone an argument. Instead, the evidence refused to be neat. It contained a gap large enough to change the meaning of what came before it. Filling the gap with a guess would have been quick, and unkind to anyone trusting the answer. Lin Jing moved the microphone a little farther away to catch the room’s actual sound. The team considered the bulletin a separate detail, not proof that an unfair contract was safe. A witness called the result civilian; Lin Jing wrote the adjective beside the witness’s name. They found a reference to composer near the damaged dated demo, but its date was unreadable. The plan called for them to constitute a real threat, and An He asked who would check the result.

合約允許歌聲曝光，卻禁止談論她的職涯經歷。整齊的解釋本可省去爭吵，但證據偏偏不整齊。裡面有一道足以改變先前意義的缺口。拿猜測補起來雖快，對信任答案的人卻不公平。林鏡把麥克風移遠一點，收進房間真實的聲音。團隊把「公告」視為獨立細節，不當成不公平的合約安全的證據。見證人稱結果「平民」，林鏡把形容詞記在見證人姓名旁。他們在受損標日期的試唱帶旁找到提及「作曲家」的紀錄，日期卻看不清。計畫要求他們「構成」，安禾追問由誰查核結果。

An He asked for a revised clause and accepted a smaller stage when the organizer refused. Someone proposed an impressive shortcut. It relied on the same promise that had failed earlier, only stated in a brighter voice. The others were tired enough to welcome it, and that made refusal difficult. A shortcut was worth nothing if its safety existed only on a poster. A half-finished cup of tea cooled beside the sheet of lyrics. Nobody disputed the cue; what troubled An He was the silence around its origin. One instruction told the team to dominate the discussion; nobody could explain why it was urgent. A witness mentioned enthusiasm during the review, so Lin Jing preserved the exact wording. They found a reference to estimate near the damaged dated demo, but its date was unreadable.

安禾要求修改條款，遭拒後選擇更小的舞臺。有人提出引人注目的捷徑，只是把先前失敗的承諾用更明亮的語氣再講一遍。其他人累得幾乎想接受，因此拒絕很困難。若安全只存在海報上，捷徑就沒有價值。半杯茶在歌詞紙旁漸漸冷去。沒人質疑「提示音」，讓安禾困擾的是它的來歷沒有人提。一項指令要求團隊「主導」，卻沒有人能解釋為何急迫。見證人在覆核時提到「熱情」，林鏡保留了原話。他們在受損標日期的試唱帶旁找到提及「估計」的紀錄，日期卻看不清。

Her simple performance drew a crowd that stayed after the lights went out. For a moment the whole plan seemed to fail. The expected signal never arrived, and the people waiting outside grew impatient. Then a smaller detail emerged, one that would have been lost if the group had covered the mistake with a confident announcement. The sound engineer saved the raw take instead of polishing every breath out of it. The public account described the situation as fertile, but An He asked for the observation behind that claim. Nobody disputed the gender; what troubled An He was the silence around its origin. The team considered the gulf a separate detail, not proof that an unfair contract was safe.

簡單演出吸引觀眾，燈光熄滅後人群仍未離開。有一刻整個計畫像要失敗。預期訊號沒有來，外面等候的人越來越不耐。隨後出現更小的細節；若團隊用自信公告掩蓋錯誤，那個細節就會消失。錄音師保存原始音軌，不把每一口呼吸都修掉。公開說法把情況形容為「適合滋生的」，安禾要求查看支撐判斷的觀察。沒人質疑「性別」，讓安禾困擾的是它的來歷沒有人提。團隊把「海灣」視為獨立細節，不當成不公平的合約安全的證據。

The place was still full of ordinary life. Someone laughed at a poor joke, someone swept a corner, and a tired worker counted the minutes to the next shift. Those details made the danger harder to ignore, because the people involved had lives outside this puzzle. An He glanced toward Lin Jing and remembered the principle they had been arguing about: a voice belongs first to its creator. It sounded simple until it demanded another hour of work. A pencil mark on the score meant more to her than a glamorous rehearsal poster. A witness mentioned intensity during the review, so Lin Jing preserved the exact wording. It might help to invade the private records, but An He refused to mistake possibility for proof. The jealousy appeared in the older dated demo, and An He asked why the newer version omitted it.

這地方仍充滿平凡生活。有人為難笑話發笑，有人掃地，疲憊的工作者算著距離換班還有幾分鐘。這些細節讓危險更難忽略，因為受影響者在謎題之外還有人生。安禾望向林鏡，想起兩人不斷爭論的原則：聲音首先屬於創作者。話雖簡單，真正實踐又需要多一小時工作。譜上的鉛筆痕對她比華麗彩排海報更重要。見證人在覆核時提到「強度」，林鏡保留了原話。「入侵」或許有幫助，安禾卻拒絕把可能當成證明。舊標日期的試唱帶出現了「嫉妒」，安禾追問新版為何漏掉。

Before leaving, they checked the names once more. Names were easier to lose than equipment, especially when a system reduced people to numbers. The check took little time. It kept the next chapter of the problem from beginning with a convenient omission. An He knew the next day would not begin from a clean slate. The dated demo would preserve one more question, and Lin Jing would be there to challenge the first easy answer. Her voice was not flawless; it had simply become unmistakably her own. The warning sounded learned to some listeners, although the actual measurements remained uncertain. The team considered the software a separate detail, not proof that an unfair contract was safe. 離開前，他們再次核對名字。名字比設備更容易消失，尤其當系統把人簡化成數字。檢查花不了多少時間，卻避免問題的下一章從方便的遺漏開始。安禾知道明天不會從零開始。標日期的試唱帶將保存又一個問題，林鏡也會在場質疑第一個容易的答案。她的聲音不完美，卻終於毫無疑問是自己的。對部分聽眾而言，警告聽起來「學養深厚的」，實際測量卻仍有疑點。團隊把「軟體」視為獨立細節，不當成不公平的合約安全的證據。見證人稱結果「有系統的」，林鏡把形容詞記在見證人姓名旁。
