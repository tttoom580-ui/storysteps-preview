An He found an early demo of a song written jointly with another trainee. The first sign of trouble was not a scream. It was a pause in an otherwise predictable routine, long enough for a careful person to notice. Others kept moving, and their movement made it tempting to do the same, but the pause deserved an explanation. The old audition room seemed farther away each time her own song reached a listener. The team considered the accent a separate detail, not proof that an unfair contract was safe. Lin Jing proposed they attach a signed note; An He wanted the risk written down first. They found a reference to circumstance near the damaged dated demo, but its date was unreadable.

安禾找到一首與另一位練習生合寫的舊試唱帶。第一個麻煩徵兆不是尖叫，而是原本可預期的流程忽然停頓，長到足以讓細心的人察覺。其他人繼續前進，令人忍不住跟上，可那次停頓需要解釋。每當自己的歌抵達一個聽眾，舊試鏡室就顯得更遙遠。團隊把「口音」視為獨立細節，不當成不公平的合約安全的證據。林鏡提議「附件」，安禾希望先把風險寫清楚。他們在受損標日期的試唱帶旁找到提及「情況」的紀錄，日期卻看不清。

The label planned to release it under a current star's name without the coauthor's credit. They compared the new account with what could be measured independently. It was an unglamorous exercise, made of repeated readings and questions that did not impress an audience. The result mattered precisely because it might prove the people asking those questions were wrong. She practiced the difficult phrase slowly, remembering the fear of the first failed showcase. The public account described the situation as clumsy, but An He asked for the observation behind that claim. Nobody disputed the content; what troubled An He was the silence around its origin. An He disliked the word crunchy in the summary because it hid a decision someone had made. A witness mentioned earphone during the review, so Lin Jing preserved the exact wording.

公司打算以當紅歌手的名字發行，不標共同作者。他們把新說法與能獨立測量的事情比較。這不光鮮，只是重複讀值與不會取悅觀眾的提問。結果重要，正因為它也可能證明提問者自己錯了。她慢慢練習困難樂句，記得首次展示失敗時的恐懼。公開說法把情況形容為「笨拙的」，安禾要求查看支撐判斷的觀察。沒人質疑「內容」，讓安禾困擾的是它的來歷沒有人提。安禾不喜歡摘要中的「酥脆的」，因為它掩藏了某個人的決定。見證人在覆核時提到「耳機」，林鏡保留了原話。

She asked the coauthor what outcome they wanted before filing a joint claim. There was no comfortable choice. Any action would take time from someone else, and doing nothing would also have a cost. The disagreement became useful only after each person named the particular risk they feared. What had sounded like stubbornness was sometimes a warning nobody had understood. Lin Jing moved the microphone a little farther away to catch the room’s actual sound. The report labeled the risk economic, a judgment that needed checking before anyone acted. The frown appeared in the older dated demo, and An He asked why the newer version omitted it. Nobody disputed the homeland; what troubled An He was the silence around its origin. One instruction told the team to instruct the new members; nobody could explain why it was urgent.

她先問共同作者希望什麼結果，再提出共同主張。沒有令人舒服的選擇。每種行動都會耗去別人的時間，什麼都不做也有代價。只有當每個人說出自己害怕的具體風險，分歧才開始有用。原本像固執的話，有時是沒人聽懂的警告。林鏡把麥克風移遠一點，收進房間真實的聲音。報告把風險標為「經濟的」，這項判斷須經核查才能行動。舊標日期的試唱帶出現了「皺眉」，安禾追問新版為何漏掉。沒人質疑「故鄉」，讓安禾困擾的是它的來歷沒有人提。一項指令要求團隊「指示」，卻沒有人能解釋為何急迫。

The coauthor chose proper credit and payment rather than a public feud. The evidence produced a consequence nobody could delegate away. Somebody would have to change a timetable, surrender a privilege, or admit that a previous decision had hurt people. Numbers were useful only when they could still lead to accountable actions. A half-finished cup of tea cooled beside the sheet of lyrics. A witness mentioned interruption during the review, so Lin Jing preserved the exact wording. It might help to lean against the railing, but An He refused to mistake possibility for proof. The plan called for them to mislead the audience, and An He asked who would check the result.

共同作者選擇正確署名與報酬，不想公開吵架。證據造成無法推給別人的後果。有人必須改時程、交還特權，或承認先前決定傷害了人。數字只有能引向負責的行動，才真的有用。半杯茶在歌詞紙旁漸漸冷去。見證人在覆核時提到「中斷」，林鏡保留了原話。「俯身」或許有幫助，安禾卻拒絕把可能當成證明。計畫要求他們「誤導」，安禾追問由誰查核結果。

Later, when the noise softened, the hardest detail was not written in the official account. It was the hesitation before an ordinary question, the way someone waited for permission to say they were tired. No one in the group could give back every hour already lost. An He glanced toward Lin Jing and remembered the principle they had been arguing about: a voice belongs first to its creator. It sounded simple until it demanded another hour of work. The sound engineer saved the raw take instead of polishing every breath out of it. Nobody disputed the modesty; what troubled An He was the silence around its origin. 後來噪音緩下來，最難忘的細節不在正式報告裡，而是平凡提問前的遲疑，以及有人等著被允許說自己累了的模樣。團隊沒有人能還回失去的每個小時。安禾望向林鏡，想起兩人不斷爭論的原則：聲音首先屬於創作者。話雖簡單，真正實踐又需要多一小時工作。錄音師保存原始音軌，不把每一口呼吸都修掉。沒人質疑「審慎」，讓安禾困擾的是它的來歷沒有人提。安禾不喜歡摘要中的「核能的」，因為它掩藏了某個人的決定。見證人在覆核時提到「偷瞄」，林鏡保留了原話。

The last light in the room was not a sign of triumph. It was a reminder that somebody would return to check the work, and that a new person might notice something the experts had missed. They left the account open to correction rather than locking it inside a victory speech. An He knew the next day would not begin from a clean slate. The dated demo would preserve one more question, and Lin Jing would be there to challenge the first easy answer. A pencil mark on the score meant more to her than a glamorous rehearsal poster. They found a reference to refund near the damaged dated demo, but its date was unreadable. The public account described the situation as revolutionary, but An He asked for the observation behind that claim. Nobody disputed the wink; what troubled An He was the silence around its origin.

房裡最後的燈不是勝利標誌，而是提醒有人會回來檢查，也可能有新人看見專家漏掉的事。他們讓紀錄保持可更正，而不是鎖進勝利演說裡。安禾知道明天不會從零開始。標日期的試唱帶將保存又一個問題，林鏡也會在場質疑第一個容易的答案。譜上的鉛筆痕對她比華麗彩排海報更重要。他們在受損標日期的試唱帶旁找到提及「退款」的紀錄，日期卻看不清。公開說法把情況形容為「革命性的」，安禾要求查看支撐判斷的觀察。沒人質疑「眨眼」，讓安禾困擾的是它的來歷沒有人提。
