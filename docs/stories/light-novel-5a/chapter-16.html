The Archive Has a Server Room

被召喚後只能改錯字的我，竟要拯救異世界的七座鐘塔？！ · 第 16 / 25 章 · 4000–5000 · 英中對照 · 校訂中

In the capital, the palace offered us accommodation in a hotel with velvet curtains and absolutely no working locks. I asked for a smaller room, and the receptionist seemed insulted by my compassion for the door. Our real destination was the central archive, where a spilled cleaning solution had begun to contaminate the glass memory plates. An exceptional archivist named Lune explained that the official maintenance report contained a contradiction: it called the damage harmless, then ordered workers to clear all debris under armed guard. "Why exclude the technicians who know the system?" I asked. Lune smiled grimly. "Because they keep asking that question."

回到首都，王宮安排我們住進一家有天鵝絨窗簾、門鎖卻完全不能用的旅館。我要求換小房間，櫃檯似乎覺得我對門的同情很失禮。我們真正的目的地是中央檔案館；那裡的清潔液外洩，開始污染玻璃記憶板。一位能力出眾的檔案員露恩解釋，官方維修報告自相矛盾：一方面稱損壞無害，一方面又命令工人在武裝監督下清除所有碎屑。「為何排除最熟悉系統的技師？」我問。露恩苦笑：「因為他們一直問這問題。」

The archive operated like a franchise of smaller record houses, but the capital controlled the server holding the official register. Kael checked a recent installation and found that its integration with older plates had never been completed. "Someone must justify the shortcut," he told the supervisor. A worker raised a trembling hand and said that a failed nomination for chief engineer had left the project without a qualified leader. Every participant had signed a safety pledge, yet the inspection pages had been sealed as sacred and inaccessible. My pencil revealed that the word "private" had been added later. The record was not magically fixed, but the workers could finally ask to see it.

檔案館像連鎖加盟店一樣管理各地紀錄室，但首都控制存放官方名冊的伺服器。凱爾檢查新裝置，發現它和舊玻璃板的系統整合從未完成。「有人必須解釋這條捷徑。」他對主管說。一名工人顫抖舉手，說總工程師提名失敗後，計畫就沒有合格負責人。每位參與者都簽過安全承諾，檢查頁卻被視為神聖機密，不准查閱。我的鉛筆顯露「私人」一詞是後來才加上。紀錄沒有被魔法修好，但工人終於能要求查看。

Lune led us through a hallway where the oldest records were stored in plain wooden boxes. She said paper could survive a hundred years if people protected it, whereas a crystal server required constant power. The palace had replaced too many local copies with central machines, thereby making a single accident more dangerous. I asked why she had stayed when the archive's managers ignored her warnings. "Because somebody should remember where the copies are," she replied. She opened a box containing signatures from the towns that had built the first relay. Their names were not glorious, but they were carefully written, and many of their descendants still lived along the same rivers.

露恩領我們走過一條長廊，最古老的紀錄都放在普通木盒裡。她說，只要有人保護，紙張能存活百年；水晶伺服器卻需要持續供電。王宮用中央機器取代太多地方副本，因此一次事故變得更加危險。我問，管理者不理她的警告，她為何仍留下。「因為總得有人記得副本放哪裡。」她回答。她打開一盒建造第一鐘塔的城鎮簽名。這些名字不顯赫，卻寫得仔細，許多後代至今仍住在相同河岸。

Vale arrived before sunset and called our inquiry insane. "If the server stops, millions of deliveries stop," he said. "Therefore I cannot permit a public experiment." Kael agreed that an uncontrolled shutdown could trigger a vicious cascade across the bells. The technicians proposed a staged copy, with the fourth bell temporarily supported by its local community. Lune offered to supervise the data transfer. Vale refused until she produced a signed precedent from the city's founding year. He looked shaken when he recognized the names. I realized he was not only protecting power; he was frightened of what might happen without it.

日落前維爾到了，說我們的調查瘋狂。「伺服器一停，數百萬份物資都會停送，」他說，「因此我不能准許公開實驗。」凱爾同意，失控關機可能引發跨鐘塔的惡性連鎖。技師們提出分階段複製，暫時由第四座鐘的地方社群支援運作。露恩願意監督資料轉移。維爾仍拒絕，直到她拿出建城那年的簽名先例。他認出那些名字時明顯動搖。我發現他不只在保護權力，也真的害怕失去它之後會發生什麼。

We left the archive without opening the server, but with permission for a controlled test at dawn. Outside, Pip stared at the hotel key and asked whether sleeping in a royal bed made him a prince. "Only if you take responsibility for the plumbing," I told him. Kael laughed so suddenly that I nearly dropped my notebook. Later, on the balcony, he admitted that the memory plates contained a record of his worst engineering mistake. I did not ask him to tell me more before he was ready. Tomorrow we would need facts that could stand in daylight, not a confession made for my comfort.

我們離開時還沒開啟伺服器，但取得黎明受控測試的許可。門外，皮普盯著旅館鑰匙，問睡過王室床鋪會不會變成王子。「只要你負責修水管。」我回答。凱爾突然笑出聲，害我差點掉了筆記本。稍晚在陽台上，他承認記憶板裡藏著自己最嚴重的工程失誤。我沒有在他準備好前逼他多說。明天我們需要的是能在日光下站得住腳的事實，不是為了安慰我而說的自白。

The controlled test that morning lasted only three minutes. Technicians watched the power meter, while representatives from the nearby districts stood beside the stop lever. When the old memory plate began to warm, Lune ended the test exactly as planned. Nobody complained about the short duration because the results showed which connection needed repair. Vale asked to review the readings himself and stayed much longer than his guards expected. I watched him examine the paper copies with a troubled expression. A man could be wrong for a long time and still choose a different action tomorrow. That possibility did not excuse the harm, but it meant change remained imaginable.

那天早上的受控測試只有三分鐘。技師盯著電表，鄰近區域代表站在停機拉桿旁。舊記憶板開始升溫時，露恩依計畫準時停機。沒有人抱怨時間短，因為結果已指出哪條接線需要修。維爾要求親自看讀數，停留時間遠超過守衛預期。我看著他憂心地翻閱紙本副本。一個人可能長期犯錯，卻仍能選擇明天採取不同的行動。這不能免除傷害，但讓改變仍然可以想像。
