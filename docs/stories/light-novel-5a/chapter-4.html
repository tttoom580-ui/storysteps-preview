The Ambulance Runs on Soup

被召喚後只能改錯字的我，竟要拯救異世界的七座鐘塔？！ · 第 4 / 25 章 · 4000–5000 · 英中對照 · 校訂中

Our fourth assignment began with boredom and ended in a spectacular emergency. The civic noticeboard said a medicine wagon was blocking the hospital driveway, while freight carts carrying winter blankets had filled the surrounding streets. A nurse with an eloquent voice explained that families in temporary housing could not obtain their medication. "There is no room," the driver complained. "Then make room without leaving someone behind," I answered. Kael consulted the safety guideline and found an older route marked for medical deliveries. The wagon's windshield was cracked, but its engine was sound enough for a careful journey.

第四項任務從無聊開始，以一場驚人的緊急事件結束。市民公告板說，藥品車堵住醫院車道，載冬被的貨車又塞滿四周道路。一位口才流利的護理師解釋，臨時住房裡的家庭領不到藥。「沒地方走了。」司機抱怨。「那就找一條不把任何人丟下的路。」我回答。凱爾查閱安全指引，找到一條標示給醫療運送用的舊路。車子擋風玻璃雖然裂了，引擎仍足以讓我們小心行駛。

The rain began to intensify just as Pip found a tin box full of outdated route tags. One label was so worn that its writing was barely noticeable. I used a mark to expose the original date, not to invent a better road. The nursing supervisor said the delay could prolong a child's fever. A merchant offered an innovative fuel crystal, promising to stimulate the engine for a faster climb. Kael tested it first and discovered that it overheated the pipes. "Speed is not morality," he told the merchant, refusing to gamble with the patients. The boy in the wagon cheered for him anyway.

皮普找到裝著過期路標的錫盒時，雨勢正開始增強。其中一張標籤磨損得幾乎看不見字。我用一次標記顯露原本日期，而不是變出更好的道路。護理主管說，延誤可能拖長一個孩子的發燒時間。一位商人拿來創新燃料水晶，聲稱能刺激引擎，讓車子更快爬坡。凱爾先測試，發現它會讓管線過熱。「速度不是道德。」他拒絕拿病人冒險。車上的男孩卻照樣替他歡呼。

While the wagons were being rearranged, the hospital cook taught Pip how to keep soup hot inside thick ceramic jars. The simple method had been used for years, although nobody had bothered to include it in the official emergency manual. I helped the volunteers label each jar with its ingredients so patients with allergies would not receive the wrong food. One elderly woman recognized her daughter's handwriting on a faded delivery tag and began to cry. The hospital had declared her daughter absent from the town's registered household list. We could not solve that problem at the roadside, but we preserved the tag and promised to ask the family before using it as evidence.

大家重新安排貨車時，醫院廚師教皮普如何用厚陶罐保溫熱湯。這方法已用了多年，官方緊急手冊卻從未收錄。我幫志工在每罐湯上標示食材，避免過敏病人拿錯食物。一名老太太認出褪色配送標籤上女兒的筆跡，開始哭泣。醫院把她女兒列成不在鎮上的登記戶。我們無法在路邊解決這問題，但保存了標籤，並承諾使用前一定先問家屬。

A young courier began to plead with the driver to take his grandmother first. Nearby, another patient was trying not to vomit from the rough movement of the wagon. I felt close to despair, because there was no clean solution. Dahl had told me that my strongest trait was not cleverness but the habit of asking whom a plan excluded. I asked the nurse to list every patient and the time each medicine had to arrive. The answer was practical, not heroic: two smaller trips on the old route, with a heated waiting room at the halfway point.

一名年輕信差懇求司機先載他的祖母。旁邊另一名病人忍著顛簸，努力不讓自己嘔吐。我幾乎絕望，因為沒有漂亮的解答。達爾曾說，我最大的特質不是聰明，而是習慣問一個計畫排除了誰。我請護理師列出每位病人，以及每種藥品最晚該抵達的時間。答案很務實，一點也不英雄：利用舊路分兩次小批運送，並在中途設置有暖氣的等候室。

By midnight both trips were complete. The nurse thanked us, but I asked her to thank the volunteers who had carried soup through the rain. Pip announced that the soup had officially replaced gasoline, and Kael looked so offended that I laughed until my sides hurt. At the hospital door, a woman handed me a list of missing villages. I recognized the same three names erased from the first bell's food register. The problem was spreading from roads to clinics. Kael did not promise an easy rescue; he simply asked what we would check first tomorrow.

午夜前兩趟運送都完成了。護理師感謝我們，我卻請她也感謝那些冒雨送湯的志工。皮普宣布熱湯正式取代汽油，凱爾的表情像受到莫大侮辱，害我笑到肚子痛。醫院門口，一名女子交給我一張失蹤村落名單。我認出其中三個名字正是第一鐘塔糧食名冊裡被抹去的村子。問題已從道路延伸到診所。凱爾沒有承諾輕鬆救援，只問我們明天該先查什麼。

At the hospital, I watched the nurse check each medicine against the patient's chart, even though people kept asking her to hurry. Her careful work reminded me of proofreading, except that a mistake here could injure someone rather than embarrass an editor. I told her that, and she shrugged. "Both jobs require the courage to stop a busy person," she said. Kael was outside tightening a wheel, sleeves rolled up despite the rain. Pip asked if I was staring at him. I said I was studying mechanical efficiency. The courier laughed so hard he dropped his empty soup bowl. I hoped the floor would open and swallow me before anyone noticed.

在醫院，我看著護理師逐一對照藥物與病歷，儘管大家一直催她快點。這份仔細工作讓我想到校對，只是這裡出錯會傷人，而不只是讓編輯丟臉。我把這想法告訴她，她聳肩：「兩份工作都需要勇氣，叫忙碌的人停下來。」外面的凱爾捲起袖子冒雨鎖緊輪子。皮普問我是不是在看他。我說我正在研究機械效率。信差笑得把空湯碗掉在地上。我真希望地板開洞把我吞下去。
