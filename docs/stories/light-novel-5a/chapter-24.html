The Final Exam Has No Perfect Score

The palace's final examination promised every village autonomy, yet the written rules still let one official overrule local choices. A powerful bully in the council mocked our capability to maintain the system without royal control. The hearing turned uncomfortable when a customs officer presented evidence of cocaine hidden in a smuggler's baggage near the bell station. "A criminal investigation is necessary," Tessa said, "but it cannot condemn every traveler who uses the same road." The queen asked what action could compensate people who had lost access to supplies. I answered that the core problem was an unreliable registration process, not the existence of travelers.

王宮最終審查承諾各村自治，可書面規則仍允許一名官員推翻地方選擇。議會裡一位霸道權貴嘲笑我們沒有王室控制就無法維持系統的能力。一名海關官員提出證據，說鐘塔站附近有走私者行李藏著古柯鹼，聽證會氣氛變得沉重。「刑事調查有其必要，」泰莎說，「但不能因此定罪使用同一道路的每名旅人。」女王問，如何補償失去物資使用權的人。我回答，核心問題是不可靠的登記流程，而不是旅人的存在。

The chairwoman remained courteous while opposing speakers grew louder. A broken escalator at the courthouse delayed older witnesses, and Kael arranged a safe alternate route. "The inherent risk of a system is that it can fail," he told the council, "so we must plan for interference rather than pretend it will never happen." The technical report included photographic evidence of each relay valve and a profile of the soil near the volcano. A farmer from a highland ranch testified that the city had taken water without warning. I asked the clerk to record his statement in full, lest it be summarized into something easier to ignore.

主席始終保持禮貌，反對發言卻越來越大聲。法院的手扶梯故障，耽誤年長證人，凱爾安排安全替代通道。「系統本來就有失敗風險，」他告訴議會，「我們必須預想外來干擾，而不是假裝永遠不會發生。」技術報告附上每個轉運閥的照片證據，以及火山附近土壤的剖面資料。一名高地牧場農民作證，城市未經通知就取走水源。我請書記完整記錄證詞，以免它被濃縮成更容易忽略的說法。

During the council break, Vale asked me whether I despised him. He had spent years believing that a centralized register was the only way to prevent another disaster. I told him I despised the secrecy, not the fear that had begun it. Fear could explain a decision, but it did not make the damage disappear. He nodded and said the next record keeper should have fewer powers than he had held. We added a rule requiring local copies of all critical instructions. It felt strange to accept help from the man who had opposed us for weeks. I decided that a good ending could include disagreement that did not vanish.

議會休息時，維爾問我是否憎恨他。他多年相信，中央名冊是防止再次災難的唯一方法。我說自己痛恨的是祕密，而不是催生它的恐懼。恐懼可以解釋決策，卻不能抹去傷害。他點頭，說下一任紀錄管理者應比自己擁有更少權力。我們加入規則，要求所有重要指令都保留地方副本。接受一個反對我們好幾週的人幫忙，感覺很奇怪。我決定，好結局可以包含沒有消失的分歧。

A student brought a pitcher of clean water to demonstrate how the final relay could share its supply. The engineers proposed to sow hardy plants around the pipes, using roots to reduce erosion. The council asked whether a substitute administrator could run the network if Vale resigned. Lune answered yes, provided the role rotated and no one could hide the logs. A scholar from the suburban college presented a thesis comparing the proposed rules with the last twenty years of outages. Its conclusion was modest: the new process was not perfect, but it was easier for ordinary people to question and correct.

一名學生拿來一壺乾淨的水，展示最後轉運鐘如何共享水量。工程師提議在管線旁播種耐寒植物，利用根系減少侵蝕。議會問，若維爾辭職，是否有替代管理者能運作網路。露恩說可以，條件是職務輪替，任何人都不能隱藏紀錄。一位郊區學院學者提出論文，比較新規則與過去二十年的停電。結論很謹慎：新程序不完美，卻更容易讓普通人質疑與修正。

The vote ended with a conditional approval: the seventh bell could be tested only after the volcano villages selected witnesses and received independent safety instructions. Vale handed over the network keys in front of the council. He did not apologize for every decision, but he admitted that fear had made him treat people as entries instead of neighbors. Kael looked pale and determined. I held his hand, not because I believed tomorrow would be easy, but because our choices belonged to us. We climbed the hill toward the final bell as the first edge of the eclipse darkened the moon.

表決結果是有條件通過：火山村落選出見證人並取得獨立安全說明後，才能測試第七鐘。維爾在議會面前交出網路鑰匙。他沒有為每項決定道歉，卻承認恐懼讓他把人當成條目，而非鄰居。凱爾臉色蒼白，眼神堅定。我握住他的手，不是因為相信明天很容易，而是因為我們的選擇屬於自己。當日蝕的第一道陰影遮住月亮，我們爬上通往最後鐘塔的山丘。

The night before the eclipse, Kael checked every piece of equipment and let the junior engineers challenge his calculations. I watched him answer calmly, even when a young apprentice found a mistake. Pip made a list of food, lamps, first-aid supplies, and places to shelter if the volcano road closed. Nobody treated preparation as a lack of faith. I sharpened my correction pencil and counted the marks available for the next day. There were three, the same as always. I would not become stronger by receiving a mysterious final ability. I would have to use the limited skill I already understood, alongside people who knew things I did not.

日蝕前夜，凱爾檢查每件設備，也允許年輕工程師質疑他的計算。我看著他平靜回答，連學徒發現一個錯誤時也一樣。皮普列出食物、燈具、急救用品，以及火山道路關閉時可避難的地方。沒人把準備當成缺乏信心。我削好校對鉛筆，數了隔天可用的標記。仍只有三次，和往常一樣。我不會靠神祕最終能力突然變強，必須用自己已理解的有限本領，和知道我所不知道事情的人一起做事。
