銀河祭典有鮪魚味

The Galaxy Festival Smells of Tuna

被召喚後只能改錯字的我，竟要拯救異世界的七座鐘塔？！ · 第 9 / 25 章 · 4000–5000 · 英中對照 · 校訂中

To reach the third bell we joined an expedition across the night harbor, where the annual galaxy festival had turned every street into a glowing river. Tessa became our ally and warned that an anonymous bidder had offered a huge bid for the island's old cemetery. The mayor claimed the purchase would fund a new citizenship office, but attendance at the ceremony was compulsory for residents who wanted permits. "A festival should not feel like an assault," I said. Pip tried to distract me with grilled tuna. Unfortunately, the wonderful smell only made the argument more difficult to ignore.

為了抵達第三座鐘，我們加入橫越夜港的探險隊。年度銀河祭讓每條街都像發光河流。泰莎成了盟友，並警告一名匿名投標者提出高價，想買下島上的舊墓園。市長宣稱收購能資助新的公民身分辦公室，可想取得許可證的居民卻被強制參加典禮。「祭典不該像一種攻擊。」我說。皮普想用烤鮪魚轉移我的注意力。不幸的是，誘人的香味只讓這場爭論更難忽略。

A young mother stood at the exterior door of the permit hall with a crying child in her arms. She wanted to embrace her father at the cemetery before the gates closed. The official manifest listed the family as absent despite their long residence. Beside the harbor lounge, a new mint had begun producing silver passes carrying the premier's portrait. Their rear side contained a code that only the mayor's clerks could read. An impatient guard tried to snatch the mother's papers. Kael stepped between them and asked him to squat beside the child so they could speak without frightening her.

一名年輕母親抱著哭泣的孩子站在許可大廳外門。她想在墓園關閉前擁抱父親。官方旅客名冊卻把一家人列成缺席，儘管他們早已在島上生活多年。港口休息室旁的新鑄幣廠開始製作印有首相肖像的銀色通行證，背面藏著只有市長書記讀得懂的編碼。一名沒耐性的守衛想搶走母親文件。凱爾擋在兩人之間，請守衛蹲到孩子旁邊，好讓大家不必嚇著孩子就能說話。

The astronomer who guided us across the harbor was an older woman with a telescope so large it had its own wheels. She explained that the stars did not command the bells; they merely offered a reliable pattern against which engineers could check timing errors. I was relieved to hear that, because arguing with the sky sounded exhausting. She had recorded several occasions when the third bell sent a signal before the council claimed it had been activated. Tessa wanted to publish the discovery immediately, but we first asked the island keeper to verify the clock. He agreed, and the evidence became stronger because we waited.

帶我們穿越港口的天文學家是位年長女性，望遠鏡大得有自己的輪子。她解釋，星星不會命令鐘塔，只提供可靠的參照圖案，讓工程師檢查時間誤差。聽到這句我鬆了口氣，畢竟跟天空吵架應該很累。她記錄過好幾次第三鐘塔在議會宣稱啟動前就送出訊號的情況。泰莎想立即報導，我們先請島上守鐘人核對時鐘。他答應了，因為我們願意等，證據反而變得更強。

The premier arrived under a curtain of falling stars, smiling as if the island belonged to him. I noticed a stink rising from the drainage canal below the temporary stage. Pip discovered that the new mint had diverted water away from the family district, flooding the lowest street and threatening the cemetery wall. The mayor insisted that the festival lights were too important to interrupt. Kael asked the engineers for their actual limits, and the islanders chose to switch off half the lamps until the canal was safe. A smaller festival remained, with people singing instead of machines.

首相在流星布幕下抵達，微笑得像整座島都屬於他。我聞到臨時舞臺下排水溝傳出臭味。皮普發現，新鑄幣廠改變了排水方向，使居民區最低的街道淹水，還威脅墓園圍牆。市長堅稱祭典燈光太重要，不能中斷。凱爾向工程師詢問真正的安全上限，島民決定先關掉一半燈具，直到排水溝安全。規模較小的祭典仍繼續，人們用歌聲代替機器。

That night the astronomers showed us how the third bell aligned with the stars. Its signal had been hidden inside the silver passes, not because the moon demanded it, but because a private supplier wanted to control admission. We collected the earliest permits and statements from the families who used them. The island keeper agreed to call a public meeting after the festival. As we sailed away, the young mother waved beside her father's grave. I wondered how many small reunions a city could steal before people stopped calling the theft ordinary.

那夜天文學家告訴我們第三座鐘如何和星辰對準。它的訊號被藏進銀色通行證，並非月亮要求，而是私人供應商想控制進入資格。我們收集最早的許可證，以及使用它們的家庭證詞。島上守鐘人答應祭典後召開公開會議。離開時，那位年輕母親站在父親墓旁向我們揮手。我忍不住想，一座城市能偷走多少次小小重逢，大家才不會再把這種偷竊視為平常。

When the festival ended, the city collected lanterns from the water instead of letting them drift into the fishing grounds. Pip volunteered enthusiastically, then discovered that wet paper was much heavier than he expected. Kael joined him without complaint, and soon half the island was helping. I sat beside the young mother from the permit hall and listened as she described the way her father had taught her to read the tide. The evening reminded me of home in Tokyo, where crowded stations were also made livable by thousands of small, unpaid acts. I was beginning to miss my old life without wishing this new one away.

祭典結束後，城市把水上的燈籠收回，沒有任其漂入漁場。皮普熱心自願，才發現濕紙比想像重得多。凱爾不抱怨地加入，很快半座島都來幫忙。我坐在許可大廳那位年輕母親身旁，聽她說父親如何教她讀潮汐。這夜讓我想到東京的家，那裡擁擠車站也靠千百件不起眼、沒有報酬的小事才變得可以生活。我開始思念舊日子，卻沒有希望新日子消失。
