The Market Where Coins Remember

The eastern market had abundant grain but almost no small currency, a problem that made the traders furious. A seller offered a crude explanation about the poor, while Tessa read a calmer commentary on the city's tax system. The school psychologist said that fear could change cognitive judgments; people saw empty purses and imagined empty fields. I asked the merchants to write a declaration of what goods they actually held. A young artist drew a map to depict the grain warehouses, and Pip bought enough sweet dough to feed everyone at the table. The crisis already looked different when we counted real supplies.

東方市場穀物充足，小額貨幣卻幾乎不夠，讓商人十分生氣。一名攤販粗魯地怪罪窮人，泰莎則朗讀一篇較冷靜的城市稅制評論。學校心理師說，恐懼會改變認知判斷；人們看見空錢袋，就想像田地也空了。我請商人寫下手中真正擁有的貨物聲明。一名年輕畫家畫出穀倉位置，皮普則買了夠整桌人吃的甜麵糰。當我們開始清點實際物資，危機就已看起來不同。

The council promised equity in distribution, but its old rule treated one silver coin as equivalent to a week's food allowance, regardless of changing prices. "That is not how an economy works," Kael said. I suggested testing the notion that a simple food voucher could bridge the shortage until new coins arrived. A merchant objected that the measure violated business ethics. We invited shopkeepers and residents to compare the prices openly, rather than prohibit disagreement. Under the awning, a child asked whether a mammal could pay with shells. Pip immediately nominated the neighborhood goat as treasurer.

議會承諾公平分配，舊規則卻不管物價如何變化，都把一枚銀幣當成一週食品配給的等值。「經濟不是這樣運作的。」凱爾說。我提議測試一種想法：新硬幣抵達前，以簡單食物券暫時應付短缺。一名商人反對，說這違反商業倫理。我們邀店家與居民公開比價，而不是禁止異議。棚子下，一名孩子問哺乳動物能不能用貝殼付錢。皮普立刻提名街坊山羊當財務長。

The argument over vouchers took place in a noisy market setting, where every seller could see the crowd and be seen in return. We asked volunteers to record prices at several stalls without forcing anyone to sell at a particular rate. The first comparison showed that one cart had raised prices after its bridge route closed, while another had kept prices low by using the repaired river crossing. The difference mattered. Blaming every merchant would have ignored the actual costs. Tessa wrote the figures on a public board and invited corrections from anyone who had a receipt. People began arguing about evidence instead of about one another's character.

食物券的爭論發生在吵雜的市場環境，每名攤販都看得見人群，也會被人群看見。我們請志工記錄數個攤位的價格，卻不強迫任何人以指定費率販售。第一次比較顯示，一輛貨車因橋路關閉而漲價，另一輛則利用修好的河橋維持低價。差異很重要。若把所有商人都怪罪一遍，就忽略了真正成本。泰莎把數字寫在公共板上，歡迎任何持有收據的人更正。人們開始爭論證據，而不是互相攻擊人格。

The premier's aide carried a presidential-style proclamation with a royal seal, although the capital had no elected president. That was the first warning. We used a portable lens to scan the actual warehouse receipts and found that the proclamation referred to goods stored at a different address. The official supposedly responsible for the shipment was away on leave. My pencil did not shatter the document or expose a secret villain; it simply revealed a later change to the address line. The merchants agreed to keep selling essential food while a neutral panel checked the records.

首相的助理拿來一份仿總統式措辭的公告，蓋著王室印章，儘管首都根本沒有民選總統。這是第一個警訊。我們用攜帶式透鏡掃描真正的倉庫收據，發現公告指向另一個地址存放的貨物。據稱負責運送的官員其實正在休假。我的鉛筆沒有粉碎文件，也沒有找出祕密反派，只顯露地址欄後來遭改動。商人答應在中立小組核對紀錄期間，仍繼續販售必需食物。

By evening the market had a temporary voucher system chosen by its users. Small coins returned from neighboring towns, and the goat's career in finance ended before it began. Kael looked at me over his tea. "If you can correct numbers, can you correct your own future?" he asked. I told him I still did not know whether the summoning circuit could send me home. He said he would help me find the answer, even if he disliked it. We made no grand promise. That honesty was worth more than a coin that remembered every hand it had passed through.

傍晚前市場由使用者自行選定臨時食物券制度。鄰近城鎮送來零錢，山羊的財務生涯還沒開始就結束了。凱爾隔著茶杯望向我。「妳能訂正數字，能訂正自己的未來嗎？」他問。我說仍不知道召喚電路能否送我回家。他答應幫我找答案，即使答案讓他難過。我們沒有許下宏大承諾。這份誠實，比一枚記得每位持有者的硬幣還珍貴。

During the evening meal, Pip asked if the new vouchers would let him buy unlimited noodles. I reminded him that unlimited food was generally unavailable even in an isekai adventure. He looked disappointed, then offered to help the market's elderly customers read the small print. Kael watched him guide a woman through the form and said the courier had changed more than he realized. I thought we all had. None of us possessed a powerful weapon or a perfect answer, but we were learning how to share responsibility without surrendering our own judgment. Tomorrow would bring another kind of problem, and we would meet it together.

晚餐時皮普問，新食物券能不能讓他買無限量麵條。我提醒他，就算在異世界冒險裡，無限食物通常也不存在。他很失望，接著卻自願幫年長顧客閱讀細字。凱爾看著他引導一名婦女填表，說這位信差改變得比自己想像多。我想我們都一樣。沒有人有強大神器或完美答案，但我們正在學習分擔責任，同時不放棄自己的判斷。明天會有另一種問題，而我們會一起面對。
