The last bell stood beside a quiet volcano, where an old geothermal pipeline carried heat to the valley. A choir from the villages greeted us with songs, and I felt awe at how many people had chosen to come. Some families cared for relatives with chronic illness; one father held a sleeping baby with a spare diaper tucked into his coat. Their commitment was not to a heroic speech but to a safe water and food supply. Kael explained the correlation between rising pressure and earlier relay failures, making clear that correlation alone was not proof of cause. The public coverage of the test would include every warning, not merely the successful moments.

最後一座鐘立在安靜火山旁，古老的地熱管線把暖氣送向山谷。各村組成的合唱團以歌聲迎接我們，我對這麼多人願意到場感到敬畏。有些家庭照顧慢性病親屬，一位父親抱著熟睡嬰兒，外套裡還塞著備用尿布。他們承諾的不是英雄演說，而是安全的水和食物供應。凱爾解釋壓力上升與過去轉運故障的相關性，也說明相關不等於因果證明。這次測試的公開報導將包含每項警告，而不只呈現成功時刻。

The village jury asked us to identify a clear criterion for stopping the experiment. Vale, now serving as a technical witness rather than commander, suggested that no one presume the old pipeline could bear unlimited heat. The engineers showed a structural map, including an obscure joint near the mountain's western side. "A siege by lava would be dramatic," Pip whispered, "but our real enemy is a poorly maintained valve." I almost laughed. The first symptom of trouble was a low vibration, not an explosion. Kael ordered a pause so the workers could tackle the loose joint before increasing pressure.

村民陪審團要求我們提出明確的停止測試標準。如今以技術見證人而非指揮官身分出席的維爾提醒，沒有人能假設舊管線承受得了無限熱量。工程師展示結構圖，其中有山西側一處不顯眼的接頭。「被熔岩圍攻很有戲劇性，」皮普小聲說，「但真正敵人是維護不良的閥門。」我差點笑出來。第一個故障徵兆是低沉震動，而非爆炸。凱爾命令暫停，讓工人在加壓前處理鬆動接頭。

Before the final vote, the mountain's gloomy morning sky made the waiting crowd nervous. A landlord from a nearby village admitted he had once opposed the shared relay because he feared losing rental income from emergency shelters. He had since watched families move safely into public rooms without anyone being forced to surrender property. His testimony did not settle every concern, but it showed that a person could change a position after seeing evidence. Lune read the proposed rules slowly, allowing questions after each section. The villagers did not need to trust us forever. They needed a system that would still let them ask questions when we were gone.

最後投票前，山間陰沉的晨空讓等待人群十分緊張。附近村落的一名房東承認，自己曾反對共享鐘塔，因為害怕緊急避難所影響租金收入。後來他親眼看見家庭安全住進公共房間，沒有人被迫交出產權。他的證詞不能解決所有擔憂，卻證明人在看見證據後能改變立場。露恩慢慢朗讀新規則，每段都讓大家發問。村民不需要永遠信任我們，他們需要的是我們離開後，仍有權繼續提問的制度。

Some delegates wanted to merge all seven records into one central list, while others argued for separate village control. The discussion became a choice of cooperation versus independence, though we knew the two did not have to be enemies. Lune proposed a shared index with local copies, so every community could check changes and refuse unsafe instructions. The witnesses examined the original records, compared the duplicates, and approved the final correction. I used my third mark to expose the last erased village name. The bell did not ring because I pointed at it. It rang only when the people whose lives depended on it agreed.

有些代表想把七份紀錄合併成中央名單，另一些則主張由村落個別控制。討論成了合作對抗獨立的選擇，儘管我們知道兩者不必互為敵人。露恩提議建立共享索引、保留地方副本，讓每個社群都能檢查改動，並拒絕不安全指令。見證人核對原件、比較副本，通過最後修正。我用第三次標記顯露最後一個被抹去的村名。鐘聲不是因為我指向它就響起；只有真正仰賴它生活的人同意，鐘才肯響。

When all seven bells sounded, wagons began moving on routes that the villages themselves had approved. The eclipse passed without a perfect miracle; one pipe remained closed for repairs, and the council posted the delay honestly. I still had the sealed diagram of the return circuit. After the celebrations, Kael offered to help me test it, then said he would respect whatever I chose. I decided to stay for another season, finish teaching at the Night School, and later visit Tokyo when the gate was proven safe. Pip demanded a holiday and two extra buns. This was not an ending handed to me by a god. It was the next page I had written with my own hands.

七座鐘同時響起時，貨車沿著各村親自同意的路線開始移動。日蝕過去，沒有發生完美奇蹟；有一段管線仍因修理保持關閉，議會誠實公告延誤。我仍保有封存的返程電路圖。慶祝結束後，凱爾願意幫我測試，並說無論我怎麼選都會尊重。我決定再留下來一季，在夜間學校完成教學，等傳送門通過安全檢驗後再去東京探望。皮普要求放假，還要多吃兩個麵包。這不是神明替我安排好的結局，而是我用雙手寫出的下一頁。

After the bells rang, Dahl gave me a certificate for completing my temporary apprenticeship. It was badly decorated, contained two spelling mistakes, and described me as "Hero of Official Paperwork." I laughed until I cried. Pip had organized a celebration at the Night School, where the young inventor displayed her paper cranes and the forest spirit sent a formal congratulations through its mushroom attorney. Kael stood beside me and waited for my answer about the return gate. I told him I had chosen another season here, not a lifetime promised in advance. He said that was enough. The next morning I opened the classroom door and wrote the first lesson on the board: "A correction begins with listening."

七鐘響起後，達爾發給我完成臨時學徒課程的證書。裝飾很糟，有兩個拼字錯誤，還把我稱為「官方文書英雄」。我笑到流淚。皮普在夜間學校安排慶祝，年輕發明家展示紙鶴，森林精靈也透過蘑菇律師正式送上祝賀。凱爾站在我身邊，等我回答返程傳送門的事。我告訴他，我選擇在這裡再待一季，不是預先許諾一輩子。他說這樣就夠了。隔天早上，我打開教室門，在黑板寫下第一堂課：「訂正，從傾聽開始。」
