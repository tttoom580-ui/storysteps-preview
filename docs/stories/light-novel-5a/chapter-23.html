女王的花園不是農田

The Queen's Garden Is Not a Farm

The final relay needed a stable supply of food for workers stationed near the volcano. An agricultural architect proposed expanding the palace garden, claiming that its beauty would boost harvests. A farmer stood up to assert that flowers and crops had different needs. The royal bureau ordered everyone to comply with a new planting mandate, but the plan ignored the villages that actually grew food. "A pretty drawing is not a diagnosis," Kael said, studying the water pipes. The queen listened from her throne and asked us to explain what the farms would lose if the garden took their water.

最後一座轉運鐘需要穩定糧食，供給駐在火山附近的工人。一名農業建築師提議擴大王宮花園，聲稱美景能提升收成。一位農夫站起來主張，花卉與農作物需求不同。王室機關命令所有人遵守新種植規定，計畫卻忽略真正生產食物的村落。「漂亮圖畫不是診斷。」凱爾研究水管時說。女王在王座上聽著，請我們解釋花園若取走農田的水，農家會失去什麼。

A genetic specialist described seed varieties selected for the valley's thin soil. Their research made drought losses easier to predict, but not inevitable. In the midst of the argument, Pip knocked over a model and tried to mount the table to catch it. The wooden tower fell with a spectacular slam, leaving a brown stain on the queen's immaculate carpet. I was certain we would be executed for crimes against interior decoration. The queen merely laughed and asked us to rebuild the model with real water measurements. Her calm response made the entire room breathe again.

一名遺傳學專家介紹適合山谷薄土的種子品種。研究使乾旱損失更容易預測，卻不代表損失不可避免。爭論中皮普撞倒模型，還想爬上桌子接住它。木塔砰然倒下，在女王潔淨的地毯留下棕色污漬。我確信我們要因危害室內裝潢被處決。女王卻只是笑，叫我們用真實水量重做模型。她冷靜的反應讓全場重新能夠呼吸。

The farmer who had challenged the palace map invited us to his home before the final journey. His family kept a small notebook recording how long each well had supplied water during dry seasons. The figures were imperfect, but they provided useful starting points for the official measurements. Kael helped the farmer compare the notebook with the new engineering plan. A gap appeared where the palace gardeners had never counted household water. The queen's adviser accepted the correction, and the village signed the revised schedule. I wished every argument ended with such quiet cooperation, but the sky reminded us that the eclipse would arrive regardless of our wishes.

挑戰王宮地圖的農夫在最終旅程前邀我們到家中。他家有一本小筆記，記下乾季時每口水井供水的天數。數字不完美，卻提供正式量測很有用的起點。凱爾幫他把筆記和新工程圖比較，發現王宮園丁從未把家戶用水算進去。女王顧問接受修正，村落也簽署新時程。我真希望每場爭論都能以這樣安靜的合作收尾，但天空提醒我們，日蝕不會因願望就改期。

The queen's adviser called the proposed channel a masterpiece of progressive engineering. A village elder compared it with an old map painted beside a stone saint, showing the natural streams before the palace was built. We tested the new route via a temporary ditch and watched the lower fields dry faster than the upper garden. Kael stopped the trial immediately. The engineers revised the plan to protect household wells first, and the queen agreed to let farmers vote on water schedules. The solution was less grand than a shining canal, but the first seedlings survived the afternoon heat.

女王顧問稱新水道是進步工程的傑作。村長拿它與刻在石聖像旁的舊地圖比較，地圖畫出王宮興建前的天然溪流。我們透過臨時水溝測試新路線，發現下方農田比上方花園更快變乾。凱爾立即停止實驗。工程師修訂方案，優先保護家戶水井，女王也答應讓農民投票決定用水時段。這解答不如閃亮水道宏偉，但第一批幼苗撐過了下午的酷熱。

As we left the palace, the queen called my name and handed me a sealed envelope recovered from the old summoning office. It contained a diagram of the return circuit and a note stating that travel required the same independent safety test as any relay. "You may go home after the eclipse," she said, "if you still wish it." I thanked her without answering. The sky above the city had begun to darken, and we had one bell left to prepare. For the first time, I was not afraid of the question of where I belonged. I was afraid of losing the people who might help me choose.

離開王宮時，女王叫住我，交給我一封從舊召喚辦公室取回的封信。裡面有返程電路圖，以及說明旅行必須像任何鐘塔一樣通過獨立安全測試的備註。「日蝕後，妳可以回家，」她說，「如果妳仍想回去。」我道謝，卻沒有回答。城市上方的天空已開始變暗，而我們只剩最後一座鐘要準備。我第一次不害怕自己屬於哪裡這個問題，反而害怕失去那些能陪我做選擇的人。

On our last night in the city, Pip prepared a farewell feast even though nobody had yet decided to leave. He set out bread, sweet bean soup, and a cake shaped like a tiny bell. Kael checked the return-circuit diagram at the table, then closed it without asking what I planned to do. I appreciated the restraint. I had already lost one familiar world through a machine's mistake; I did not want another person to choose my future for me. We promised to talk after the seventh bell, when the immediate danger had passed and a real decision could be made.

在城中的最後一晚，皮普準備了告別宴，儘管還沒有人決定離開。他擺出麵包、甜紅豆湯，還有一個小鐘形狀的蛋糕。凱爾在桌上檢查返程電路圖，接著合起來，沒問我打算怎麼辦。我很感謝他的克制。我已因機器錯誤失去過一個熟悉世界，不想再讓別人替我選未來。我們答應第七鐘響後再談，等迫在眉睫的危險過去，才做真正的決定。
