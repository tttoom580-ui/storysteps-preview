凱爾不再假裝的那一天

The Day Kael Stopped Pretending

My assumption had been that Vale alone designed the failed relay, but an old engineering chart showed Kael's signature beside the original calculation. Years earlier a pressure blast had injured several workers, and the government had demanded immediate repairs. The crisis began with an external change in population density near the river, not with a single villain's plan. During an inspection cruise, the engineers had warned that opening the fourth bell too early might cause a second failure. Kael admitted he had accepted a compromise without obtaining consensus from the families who would bear the risk.

我一直假設只有維爾設計了出問題的轉運鐘，但舊工程圖上，原始計算旁也有凱爾的簽名。多年前一次壓力爆炸造成幾名工人受傷，政府要求立即修復。危機起因是河岸人口密度從外部急遽改變，而非某個反派的單一陰謀。工程師搭巡檢船時警告，太早開啟第四鐘可能導致第二次故障。凱爾承認，當年他接受了折衷方案，卻沒有取得將承擔風險的家庭共識。

"I did not forge the inspection report," Kael said, "but I stayed silent when others made the damage look smaller." Vale had promised that the repairs were temporary. His engineers turned the warning screens into an illusion of safety while the workers kept operating the machines. In the meantime the palace reassigned most experienced personnel. A premature restart harmed the surrounding houses, and the clinic had to prescribe treatment for smoke exposure. "You should have told me," I whispered. Kael nodded. "I was wary that you would leave." I wanted to say I would never do that, but love built on an impossible vow would be another kind of lie.

「我沒有偽造檢查報告，」凱爾說，「可是別人把損壞說得比較輕微時，我保持沉默。」維爾曾保證修復只是暫時措施。他的工程師把警示螢幕變成安全幻象，工人卻繼續操作機器。與此同時，王宮把多數資深人員調走。過早重啟傷害周圍住宅，診所不得不為吸入煙霧的人開藥。「你應該告訴我。」我低聲說。凱爾點頭：「我怕妳離開。」我很想說自己絕不會，但建立在不可能誓言上的愛，也只是另一種謊言。

One of the injured workers walked with a cane and was prone to exhaustion after long meetings. We moved the interview to a room near the entrance so she would not have to climb stairs. She asked Kael why he had not returned to speak with the families after the accident. He answered that he had been ashamed and frightened of making things worse. "Silence made it worse," she said. Then she showed him the list of repairs her neighbors had requested for years. He accepted the list and asked which items should be inspected first. She did not forgive him, but she agreed to help check the results.

一名受傷工人拄著手杖，長時間開會容易疲累。我們把訪談移到入口附近房間，讓她不必爬樓梯。她問凱爾為何事故後沒有回來和家屬說話。他回答自己羞愧，也害怕讓事情更糟。「沉默才讓事情更糟。」她說，接著拿出鄰居多年要求的維修清單。他接下清單，問哪些項目該先檢查。她沒有原諒他，但同意一起核對結果。

Tessa located the workers' whereabouts through old wage records. One former supervisor said the presidency of the engineering council had changed hands just before the report disappeared. Another witness described how outer-wall families, especially those treated badly because of racism, had been dismissed as unreliable. The testimony did not make every conclusion certain, but it showed why some voices were missing. Kael apologized to the families in person, presumably knowing that many would not forgive him. His apology did not erase the damage. It made him willing to answer the next question without hiding behind a title.

泰莎透過舊工資紀錄找到工人的下落。一位前主管說，工程委員會主席職位在報告消失前剛換人。另一名證人描述城外家庭如何因種族歧視受到不公待遇，被當成不可信任。證詞不能保證每個結論都正確，卻能解釋為何有些聲音消失了。凱爾親自向這些家庭道歉，想必也知道許多人不會原諒。道歉沒有抹去損害，只讓他願意不再躲在頭銜後面，正面回答下一個問題。

That evening the lamps began to sparkle above the river. Kael offered to return to the capital alone, leaving me free to continue without him. I told him I was hurt by his silence, but I would decide my own next step. We could still work together if the villagers agreed and he disclosed his role openly. He accepted the conditions without argument. "I want to earn your trust," he said. "Then keep telling the truth when it costs you something," I replied. Pip, listening from the doorway, quietly set out three cups instead of two.

當晚河面上方的燈開始閃爍。凱爾提議獨自返回首都，讓我可以不帶他繼續行動。我告訴他，我因他的沉默而受傷，但下一步會由我自己決定。只要村民同意、他公開自己的角色，我們仍可合作。他沒有爭辯，接受條件。「我想重新贏得妳的信任。」他說。「那就在說實話會讓你付出代價時，仍繼續說。」我回答。在門口偷聽的皮普安靜放好三只杯子，而不是兩只。

I spent that evening alone beside the river, thinking about what forgiveness meant. I cared for Kael, but caring did not give me the right to settle a debt owed to other people. He found me after sunset and sat at a respectful distance. We watched a small ferry cross the water, its lantern moving steadily despite the current. "I don't want you to make me a better person," he said. "I want to do the work myself." I told him that was the first honest romantic sentence he had ever offered. He laughed, and the sound felt like an opening rather than an ending.

當晚我獨自坐在河邊，思考原諒到底意味著什麼。我在乎凱爾，但在乎不等於有權替其他人結清債務。日落後他找到我，在有禮的距離坐下。我們看一艘小渡輪橫過河面，燈籠在水流中仍穩穩前進。「我不想讓妳把我變成好人，」他說，「我想自己做該做的事。」我說，那是他說過第一句真正誠實的浪漫話。他笑了，笑聲像一扇開啟的門，而不是結局。
