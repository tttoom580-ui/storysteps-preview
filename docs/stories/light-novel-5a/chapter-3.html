The Forest Has Its Own Lawyer

The next morning, a forest spirit served me a lawsuit with breakfast. Its attorney was a mushroom wearing a miniature tie, and the accusation was that the capital's road showed aggression toward the roots of a beloved tree. "Is this a biological complaint?" I asked. "All complaints are biological when you breathe," answered the mushroom. Kael Ardent, a twenty-nine-year-old engineer assigned to escort us, was cautious about the spirit's demands. He was bound by a work order to inspect the route, not to promise repairs. I liked that he understood a limit, though his expression suggested he disliked everyone before coffee.

隔天早上，森林精靈連同早餐送來一張訴狀。牠的律師是戴迷你領帶的蘑菇，指控首都道路對一棵受愛戴的大樹根部施加侵害。「這算生物方面的申訴嗎？」我問。「只要你會呼吸，所有申訴都跟生物有關。」蘑菇回答。奉命護送我們的二十九歲工程師凱爾・亞登，對精靈的要求相當謹慎。他的工作命令只要求檢查道路，不准任意承諾修理。我欣賞他明白界線，儘管他的表情像是喝咖啡前討厭每個人。

The executive who approved the road had placed a thin layer of stone above the roots, calling it harmless. But a forest shuttle that carried seed collectors now shook badly enough to frighten its passengers. Kael showed us the scope of the original repair contract. If we could reduce the weight, the pressure might diminish before the rains. The spirit watched us as if we were prey, ready to swallow our tools. "Your realism is refreshing," I told Kael after he calculated the cost. He gave a small shrug. "A bridge is not made stronger by wishing it so," he replied.

核准道路的高階主管曾在樹根上鋪薄薄一層石板，宣稱不會有害。但載運採種者的森林接駁車如今搖晃得讓乘客害怕。凱爾展示原始修繕合約的工作範圍。若能減輕重量，或許能在雨季前降低壓力。精靈像盯著獵物一樣看我們，彷彿隨時會把工具吞下。「你的現實主義真令人耳目一新。」他算完費用，我這樣說。他聳聳肩：「希望不會讓橋自己變結實。」

The mushroom attorney insisted on showing us the tree's oldest roots before any inspection began. We crouched beneath the road and saw that one support stone had been set directly above a hollow where rainwater collected. The danger was real, but removing the whole road could cut the village off from its market. Kael drew two possible repairs on a slate and asked the spirit to choose what should be tested. To my surprise, it chose the slower option because young birds nested nearby. I had expected a fantasy world to operate through dramatic spells. It was teaching me the less dramatic skill of asking questions before using any power.

蘑菇律師堅持先讓我們看樹最老的根，才准開始檢查。我們蹲在道路下，發現一塊支撐石直接壓在會積雨水的空洞上。危險是真的，但整條拆路可能使村落無法去市場。凱爾在板上畫兩種修法，請精靈選擇先測試哪一個。出乎意料，牠挑了較慢的方案，因為附近有幼鳥築巢。我原以為奇幻世界靠戲劇化咒語運作，它卻在教我較不戲劇化的技能：動用權力前先問問題。

Kael had learned to specialize in systems that failed at inconvenient moments. He asked Pip to straighten the measuring rod and fasten its loose strap. Every tick from the pressure gauge made the mushroom lawyer twitch. I offered my own viewpoint: the forest needed a working route, and the capital needed deliveries, so neither side should accept an invisible risk. Kael opened a virtual model of the bridge on his crystal slate. Instead of granting us a miracle, the model showed exactly which parts could be removed safely. The spirit agreed to one supervised test, provided its own witnesses could stop the work.

凱爾專門處理那些總在最不方便時故障的系統。他請皮普扶正量測桿，繫緊鬆掉的帶子。壓力計每滴答一聲，蘑菇律師就抖一下。我提出自己的觀點：森林需要可用的道路，首都需要物資，雙方都不該接受看不見的風險。凱爾在水晶板上開啟橋梁虛擬模型。它沒有賜給我們奇蹟，而是精確顯示哪些部分能安全拆除。精靈答應接受一次監督測試，條件是牠的見證人也能叫停作業。

By dusk, the shuttle crossed the repaired section without shaking. The mushroom declared a temporary truce, then charged me one acorn for legal services. I paid, because arguing with a tiny attorney sounded like an excellent way to lose another afternoon. Kael stayed behind to check the bolts while Pip collected the witness signatures. When he returned, there was mud on his sleeve and relief in his eyes. "You did not need to stay," I said. "Neither did you," he replied. I noticed he had saved the broken measuring rod as evidence instead of throwing it away.

傍晚時，接駁車平穩通過修復路段。蘑菇宣布暫時休戰，然後向我收取一顆橡實的律師費。我付了，因為和迷你律師爭論顯然會耗掉整個下午。凱爾留下檢查螺栓，皮普則去收集見證人簽名。他回來時袖子沾泥，眼神卻鬆了一口氣。「你不必留下。」我說。「妳也不必。」他回答。我注意到他把壞掉的量測桿留作證據，沒有隨手丟棄。

After the repair, Kael handed the mushroom a copy of the stress measurements. The attorney placed an acorn on top and declared it officially received. Pip claimed this made the acorn a government employee, a possibility nobody had the strength to debate. We walked away as lantern bugs woke in the trees, and Kael told me why he trusted numbers: unlike powerful people, measurements could be repeated by someone else. I answered that numbers also depended on what was measured and who was allowed to look. He considered that for a long time. When he finally said I was right, I nearly slipped on a root from sheer surprise.

修復後，凱爾把應力測量副本交給蘑菇。律師在上面放一顆橡實，宣布正式收件。皮普說這讓橡實變成公務員，而大家已經累得沒力氣辯論。我們離開時，樹間燈蟲剛醒。凱爾說他為何信任數字：不像權貴，量測可以由別人重做。我回答，數字也取決於量測什麼，以及誰有權查看。他想了很久，最後說我說得對。我驚訝得差點在樹根上滑倒。
