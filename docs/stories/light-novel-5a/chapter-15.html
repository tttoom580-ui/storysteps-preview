玻璃海下的投票

A Vote Under the Glass Sea

The coastal resort had advertised a grand ceremony for the third bell, complete with a chef, a brass band, and free sweets for every voter. The actual ballot asked a complicated question about who would control the bell's water supply. People were told to mark their choice in consecutive rows, which confused residents with limited reading ability. A boom from the harbor interrupted the announcement; an old pump had failed. The mayor suggested continuing the festival and ignoring the noise, but a fisherwoman refused. "A broken pump can cripple our boats," she said. "We need to diagnose it before we vote."

海岸度假村宣傳要為第三鐘塔舉辦盛大典禮，有主廚、銅管樂隊，還有送給每位投票者的免費甜點。真正的選票卻問了一個複雜問題：誰來控制鐘塔用水。居民被要求在連續幾排格子中填選，讓閱讀能力較弱的人很困惑。港口忽然傳來巨響，老水泵故障了。市長建議繼續祭典，別管噪音，一位女漁民卻拒絕。「壞水泵會讓我們的船不能動，」她說，「投票前得先診斷。」

Kael examined the machinery and found a foul-smelling filter packed with seaweed. The resort manager was indifferent to the damage because most tourists would leave before high tide. A marine biologist explained how the local ecology depended on the same water channel used by the fishing fleet. "Likewise, the hotel depends on the people who keep that channel open," I told the manager. He began to moan about lost revenue. Pip suggested a temporary bypass, but the fisherwoman insisted that any plan include an automatic stop if the water level dropped. We wrote the limits where everyone could see them.

凱爾檢查機器，找到塞滿海藻、臭氣撲鼻的濾網。度假村經理對損壞漠不關心，因為多數觀光客在滿潮前就會離開。海洋生物學家解釋，當地生態仰賴的正是漁船也使用的水道。「同樣地，飯店也依賴維持水道暢通的人。」我對經理說。他開始哀嘆收入損失。皮普提議暫時旁通，女漁民卻堅持，任何方案都必須在水位下降時自動停機。我們把安全上限寫在人人看得見的地方。

Before the vote, the fisherwoman asked everyone to take their belongings off the pier. If an emergency required evacuation, she did not want bags and crates blocking the route. Her practical request exposed the complexity of the harbor plan better than any speech. The hotel manager offered new sponsorship for a lifeboat, but the fisherwoman insisted that ownership and maintenance remain public. He finally agreed, perhaps because he understood his own employees needed that boat too. I checked the meeting notice one last time. It described who could stop the trial, where the results would be posted, and how residents could appeal a decision.

投票前，女漁民請大家把隨身物品從碼頭移走。如果緊急撤離，她不希望袋子和木箱堵路。這項務實要求，比任何演講都更清楚暴露港口計畫的複雜性。飯店經理提出新的救生船贊助，女漁民卻堅持所有權與維護都得保持公共管理。他終於同意，也許因為明白自己的員工同樣需要那艘船。我最後檢查會議公告，上面寫明誰能叫停測試、結果公布地點，以及居民如何申訴決定。

The debate turned to sovereignty: did the capital or the islanders own the right to control local water? A sponsor offered to pay for repairs if the island gave up its independent vote. I said the sentiment sounded generous but hid a poor bargain. Our solution was more straightforward: a public maintenance fund, technical supervision chosen by the residents, and a separate decision about the relay register. Some villagers feared being stranded without supply boats. We showed how the temporary bypass could operate safely for a limited time, and promised to stop it if the measurements changed.

辯論轉向主權：控制地方水源的權利究竟屬於首都還是島民？一名贊助者願意支付維修費，條件是島嶼放棄獨立投票。我說這種情感看似慷慨，實際卻藏著不公平交易。我們的方案更直接：公共維修基金、由居民選任的技術監督，以及與轉運鐘名冊分開處理的投票。某些村民害怕船隻停運而受困。我們展示臨時旁通系統如何在有限時間內安全運作，並承諾數據改變就立即停機。

The vote passed only after the clerk read the choices aloud and permitted everyone to ask questions. A new witness list was signed, and the third relay bell rang beneath the glass sea. The water market lit up as if a second sunrise had risen underwater. Pip nearly cried, then said he had something in his eye. Kael touched my hand beneath the table and waited until I squeezed back. I could hear the distant bell through the floorboards. Three bells were now working, yet the remaining four depended on the capital's most secret records. Vale was waiting for us there.

書記逐項朗讀選項，讓所有人提問後，投票才順利通過。新的見證人名單完成簽署，第三座轉運鐘在玻璃海下響起。水上市場亮了，彷彿海底升起第二次日出。皮普差點哭了，卻說眼睛進了東西。凱爾在桌下輕碰我的手，等我回握才沒有移開。我聽見遠方鐘聲穿透木地板。三座鐘已正常運作，剩下四座卻依賴首都最祕密的紀錄。維爾正在那裡等我們。

After the bell rang, the young inventor from the water market sent a new paper crane floating across the tide. Its light blinked twice, then settled into a steady glow. "Does that mean the whole world is fixed?" Pip asked. "It means one test worked," Kael said. I realized how much I had grown to appreciate that answer. We sat together beside the water and listened to the marketplace return to its ordinary noise. There were still four bells to repair, and the capital had more hidden records than I wanted to imagine. For one evening, however, ordinary life felt like an achievement rather than an interruption.

鐘響後，水上市場的年輕發明者把新紙鶴放到潮水上。它的光閃了兩下，才穩定亮起。「這代表世界全部修好了嗎？」皮普問。「代表一次測試成功。」凱爾說。我發現自己越來越欣賞這種回答。我們坐在水邊，聽市場恢復平常的喧鬧。還有四座鐘要修，首都藏的紀錄多得讓我不敢想像。然而至少這個晚上，日常生活感覺像一種成就，而不是干擾。
