A Festival for Unedited Names

The city planned a grand ceremony to celebrate a revised bell register, but the directory of invited guests contained only royal officials. A newspaper columnist asked whether the public could alter the schedule. The council clerk said every participant needed a certificate, which made the supposed collective event rather exclusive. I took the document to the city square and posted a simple question: "Who is missing?" For once I kept my ego out of the answer. Workers, parents, and traveling performers wrote their names on the board, and the list grew longer than the palace had expected.

城市準備舉辦盛大典禮，慶祝修訂鐘塔名冊，賓客目錄卻只有王室官員。一位報紙專欄作家問，公眾能否更改流程。議會書記說每名參加者都得有證書，使這場號稱集體的活動變得非常排他。我把文件拿到市民廣場，貼上一個簡單問題：「還漏了誰？」這回我沒有把自己的自尊放進答案。工人、父母、巡迴表演者紛紛把名字寫上板子，名單遠比王宮預期得長。

A lawmaker warned that the opposition would use the event to attack him. "If you would rather disappear than hear a complaint, you can opt out," Pip told him with perfect courtesy. The remark was petty but effective. Tessa reminded the crowd that prejudice had kept many refugees away from earlier meetings. Lune insisted on protecting the integrity of the original records; new names could be added only when people consented, not because the crowd applauded. I searched the square for Kael and found him nowhere near the stage. He was helping a child read the registration instructions.

一名議員警告，反對派會利用活動攻擊他。「如果你寧願消失也不想聽抱怨，可以選擇退出。」皮普以完美禮貌回敬。這話有點小心眼，卻很有效。泰莎提醒人群，偏見使許多難民避開過去的會議。露恩堅持保護原始紀錄的完整性；新名字只有在當事人同意時才能加入，不能因人群鼓掌就隨意增列。我在人群中找凱爾，發現他不在舞臺附近，而是在幫孩子讀登記說明。

During the afternoon, a woman asked whether she could submit her family's name without telling the whole crowd why it had been removed. Lune said yes and pointed her toward a private review table staffed by two independent clerks. I realized that public accountability did not require turning private pain into entertainment. The committee could publish its method and findings while protecting details that families wanted to keep. Tessa prepared a report explaining this difference, and the newspaper agreed to print it in full. The article was less dramatic than a list of accusations, but its usefulness might last longer than a single day's excitement.

下午，一名婦女問能否提交家人的名字，卻不必向群眾說明過去為何被刪除。露恩說可以，並指向由兩名獨立書記負責的私人審查桌。我明白，公共問責不需要把私人痛苦變成娛樂。委員會可以公布方法與結果，同時保護家庭想保留的細節。泰莎寫報導解釋這項差異，報社答應全文刊登。文章不如一長串指控戲劇化，但它的實用性或許比一天的興奮持久。

The sponsor provided food stalls, including a spicy noodle cart that Pip declared the festival's true reason for existing. A local counselor offered quiet rooms for people whose earlier experiences had left trauma, and nobody was required to share a story publicly. At the center of the square, the mayor asked whether we would undertake a full review of the city's emergency names. Lune said yes, provided the review was independent and the original records remained protected. It was a difficult promise, but not a theatrical one. Even the mayor seemed relieved that no one was asking him to solve every problem alone.

贊助者提供小吃攤，其中一輛辣麵車被皮普宣布為祭典存在的真正理由。地方輔導員設置安靜房間，讓過往經驗造成創傷的人休息，沒人被要求公開講自己的故事。廣場中央，市長問我們是否願意承擔全市緊急名冊的完整複查。露恩說可以，條件是審查必須獨立，原始紀錄也要受保護。這是一份艱難、卻不戲劇化的承諾。就連市長也像鬆了口氣，因為沒人要求他一個人解決所有問題。

The final performance was a song with no named hero. Each district sang one verse, and the chorus changed as more voices joined. I stood beside Kael and asked whether he was comfortable staying. "I want to hear what people say," he answered. It was not an apology, and it was better than one. After the song, the council agreed to preserve the witness list and schedule an open inspection of the fourth and fifth bells. Vale watched from the archive tower. He was still holding the keys, but he no longer seemed certain that silence was the only safe choice.

壓軸表演是一首沒有指定英雄的歌。每個區域唱一段，越多人加入，合唱就越不同。我站在凱爾身旁，問他留在這裡是否自在。「我想聽聽大家怎麼說。」他回答。這不是道歉，卻比道歉更好。歌唱完，議會同意保存見證人名單，安排公開檢查第四與第五鐘塔。維爾站在檔案塔上觀看。他仍握著鑰匙，但似乎不再確信沉默是唯一安全選擇。

At the edge of the festival, Pip discovered a message from the night school asking whether I would teach a course on reading contracts. I stared at it for a long time. Back in Tokyo, I had corrected other people's words for a living; here, I might help people read words that shaped their own lives. Kael said he could build a simple practice model for the students, provided I agreed to mark every mechanical mistake. I accused him of trying to turn our friendship into unpaid labor. He offered half the profits from his nonexistent repair shop. We laughed, then began planning the lessons anyway.

祭典邊緣，皮普發現夜間學校寄來訊息，問我願不願意教契約閱讀課。我盯著它很久。在東京，我靠訂正別人的文字維生；在這裡，我或許能協助人們讀懂影響自己生活的文字。凱爾說他能替學生做簡單練習模型，條件是我得替每個機械錯誤做標記。我指控他想把友情變成無薪勞動。他提出分享那間根本不存在的修理店一半利潤。我們笑了，卻也開始認真規劃課程。
