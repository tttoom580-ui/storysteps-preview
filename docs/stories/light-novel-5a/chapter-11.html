差一點成為英雄的研究所

The Institute of Almost Heroes

We arrived at the Bell Institute to accelerate the investigation, but its entrance sign told visitors not to compliment the director's pet dragon. Pip did so anyway. The dragon sneezed on his shoes. Inside, Professor Dahl showed us a damaged component from the fourth relay and asked Kael whether the engineers could deploy a safe replacement. The answer was grim: repair crews suffered fatigue after working six days without rest. "Then we need more people, not longer shifts," I said. The institution's director looked offended, as if overtime were an ancient form of magic that only outsiders failed to appreciate.

我們抵達鐘塔研究所，想加速調查，但入口招牌警告訪客不要稱讚所長的寵物龍。皮普偏偏稱讚了，龍對著他的鞋打了個噴嚏。裡面達爾教授展示第四轉運鐘損壞的零件，問凱爾工程師能否部署安全替換件。答案很沉重：維修隊已連續工作六天，累得筋疲力盡。「那就增加人手，不是增加工時。」我說。所長的表情像受到冒犯，彷彿加班是古代魔法，只有外人才不懂珍惜。

The laboratory kept a specimen of each relay crystal in a locked room. Its minimal safety instructions had been signed by a veteran technician, yet the signature now appeared beneath a recommendation to skip inspections. Dahl said the veteran would never accept such a trivial shortcut when lives were at stake. I watched the crystal quiver as the fourth bell sent a weak signal from the northern realm. "Can we ask for a neutral examination?" I asked. The director agreed, although the institute had to undergo a public review of its own record keeping. For once, the dragon seemed to approve of a sensible plan.

實驗室把每座轉運鐘的水晶樣本保存在上鎖房間。最簡略的安全指示由一名資深技師簽名，但他的簽名如今竟出現在跳過檢查的建議下。達爾說，那名老技師不可能在攸關性命時接受這種微不足道的捷徑。我看見水晶隨著第四座鐘從北方領域傳來的微弱訊號顫動。「可以要求中立檢驗嗎？」我問。所長答應了，儘管研究所也得接受公開的紀錄管理審查。這回連寵物龍都像是贊同理智的計畫。

The institute employed a night guard who had lost his hearing in an old workshop accident. He communicated through written cards and showed us the marks left on a repaired safety panel. Kael recognized a number from his former engineering unit and went very still. I offered to take the next question, but the guard pointed to Kael and waited. After a long pause, Kael admitted that he had approved a similar panel years earlier. The guard did not accuse him. Instead, he asked whether the new design would fail in the same way. Kael promised to provide a calculation that anyone trained to read it could check.

研究所雇用一名在舊工坊事故中失去聽力的夜間守衛。他用寫字卡溝通，帶我們看安全板修理後留下的痕跡。凱爾認出前工程隊的編號，整個人僵住。我提議替他問下一題，守衛卻指向凱爾，耐心等著。沉默很久後，凱爾承認自己幾年前核准過類似面板。守衛沒有指責，只問新設計會不會以同樣方式失敗。凱爾答應提供任何受過訓練的人都能核對的計算。

The repair guild submitted a petition asking workers to choose their own rest schedule. Kael studied its technical specialty clauses and found that the proposed plan would protect the crystals as well as the people. I used a correction mark to reveal an omitted sentence in the maintenance contract; the institute's copy and the veteran's archived notebook matched. We gathered witnesses rather than announce a verdict. The technicians cheered when the director finally agreed to hire a second crew. A system could thrive only when its workers were allowed to say they were tired, I decided.

維修公會提交請願書，要求工人自行參與排定休息時程。凱爾研究其中的專業技術條款，發現新計畫既能保護水晶，也能保護人。我用修正標記顯露維護契約遺失的一句話；研究所副本與老技師存檔筆記內容一致。我們蒐集見證人，而非急著宣布裁決。所長終於答應聘請第二組人員時，技師們歡呼起來。我想，一套制度只有在工人有權說自己累了時，才能真正興盛。

That evening we ate dinner beside the institute greenhouse, where the dragon curled around a warm pipe. Kael told me he had once designed the fourth bell's cooling system, then stopped speaking. I wanted to ask why he had left the royal engineering corps, but his hands were shaking as he poured tea. Instead I asked whether he trusted our new measurements. "More than my old predictions," he said. I let the silence stand. A good editor knows that a blank space is not always an error, and a good friend knows when to wait for the missing sentence.

當晚我們在研究所溫室旁吃飯，寵物龍蜷在暖管邊。凱爾說他曾設計第四鐘塔的冷卻系統，然後就沉默了。我想問他為何離開王室工程隊，可他倒茶的手微微發抖。我改問他信不信新的量測結果。「比我以前的預測更可信。」他說。我讓沉默留著。好校對員知道空白不一定是錯誤；好朋友也知道何時該等對方說完。

I spent the next hour helping Dahl sort old repair notes by date. Several pages described successful tests, but the names of the workers who had carried them out were missing. Dahl refused to guess who deserved the credit. We asked the technicians, checked the time records, and marked the uncertain entries for later review. By the time the greenhouse lights went out, we had a much smaller list of genuine questions. Pip said this was disappointing because no dramatic monster had appeared. I told him the dragon had already attacked his shoes. The creature lifted one eyelid, clearly considering a second strike.

接著一小時，我幫達爾依日期整理舊維修筆記。幾頁寫著測試成功，實際執行工人的名字卻消失了。達爾拒絕猜誰該得到功勞。我們詢問技師、核對工時，並標記仍不確定的條目，留待後查。溫室熄燈時，真正的問題名單已縮短許多。皮普說這很掃興，因為沒有戲劇化怪物出現。我提醒他，龍早就攻擊過他的鞋。那隻生物睜開一隻眼，顯然正在考慮第二次出手。
