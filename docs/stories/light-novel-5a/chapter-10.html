法庭不是魔王關卡

The Courtroom Is Not a Boss Arena

The island inquiry led us to the capital's medical court beneath a bronze dome. A former butcher had become a respected entrepreneur by selling cheap food to rural clinics. He now faced a ridiculous charge: that his charity shipments contained stolen medical samples. The case began with an anonymous letter from an arrogant official who had once promised him protection. "Someone may have chosen to betray him," Tessa said, "but our task is to check credibility, not guess at motives." I agreed, even when the prosecutor tried to make the courtroom feel like a tournament arena.

島上調查把我們帶到首都青銅圓頂下的醫療法庭。一名昔日屠夫轉行成受尊敬的企業家，向鄉村診所販售便宜食品。如今他面對荒唐指控：慈善貨物裡藏有被偷走的醫療樣本。案件始於一封匿名信，寄信者是曾答應保護他的傲慢官員。「可能有人選擇背叛他，」泰莎說，「但我們得查證可信度，而不是猜動機。」我同意，即使檢察官努力讓法庭像競技場。

A physician explained that the samples came from a genetics program designed to identify inherited illnesses. Each molecule of material required written consent and careful storage. The clinic had also provided confidential counseling on pregnancy and abortion, services that patients were legally entitled to discuss without public humiliation. A witness testified that the shipment labels belonged to the food sector, not the research laboratory. My pencil revealed that a page had been inserted after the original packing list. The judge ordered the two versions examined by an independent specialist before anyone announced guilt.

一位醫師解釋，樣本來自尋找遺傳疾病的基因研究計畫。每一份分子材料都需要書面同意和妥善保存。診所也提供關於懷孕與人工流產的保密諮詢，病人依法有權討論這些服務，而不必公開受辱。一名證人作證說，貨物標籤屬於食品部門，不是研究實驗室。我的鉛筆顯露原始裝箱單後來被插入一頁。法官命令由獨立專家檢查兩個版本，任何人都不得先宣布有罪。

Before the medical hearing, I asked the physician whether she was comfortable speaking about the clinic's work. She said she would testify about the shipping records, but not about individual patients without their permission. The judge accepted that limit. For the first time, I realized how much strength it took to refuse a dramatic answer when the room wanted one. The butcher offered to open his books to independent auditors, even though doing so might reveal embarrassing mistakes from earlier years. "I would rather correct my accounts than have strangers invent my motives," he said. Tessa wrote his words down without turning them into a headline.

醫療聽證前，我問醫師是否願意談診所工作。她說會就運送紀錄作證，但沒有病人許可就不會談個別病史。法官接受這條界線。我首次明白，當整間房都渴望戲劇化答案，拒絕給出答案需要多大勇氣。那位屠夫願意開放帳冊接受獨立稽核，儘管可能揭露早年令人尷尬的錯誤。「我寧可自己改帳，也不願陌生人替我編造動機。」他說。泰莎如實記下，沒有把這句話扭成聳動標題。

Kael proposed an initiative to integrate the food and health delivery routes without merging private patient records. The butcher worried that losing his shop's mortgage would end the program. We showed the court a vertical diagram of the warehouse shelves and explained why the disputed boxes could not have occupied the same space. The prosecutor challenged our calculations, which was fair; evidence ought to survive hard questions. Meanwhile, a wildlife ranger confirmed that the refrigerated cart had crossed the forest reserve without stopping. Several stories could now be tested instead of merely believed.

凱爾提出一項倡議：整合食物和醫療配送路線，但不合併病人的私人資料。那位屠夫擔心若付不起店鋪貸款，就得關閉計畫。我們向法庭展示倉庫貨架的垂直剖面圖，說明爭議箱子為何不可能同時占用相同空間。檢察官質疑我們的計算，這很公平；證據本就該承受嚴格提問。同時野生動物巡護員證實，冷藏車經過森林保護區時並未停車。現在有好幾種說法能被測試，而不是只靠相信。

The judge postponed the accusation and ordered the shipment route preserved. It was not a dramatic acquittal, but the butcher's customers could still obtain food while the case continued. Afterward Kael bought me a warm sweet bun and asked why I looked disappointed. "Nobody shouted that justice had won," I admitted. "You want a final battle," he said. "I want people to get dinner," I corrected. He looked pleased by the answer. We were learning to sustain difficult work without treating every hearing as a victory parade, and that felt strangely more heroic than shouting spells.

法官暫緩判定，命令保存配送路線的證據。這不是戲劇性的無罪宣判，但案件審理期間，屠夫的顧客仍能買到食物。離開後凱爾買給我一個熱甜麵包，問我為何失望。「沒有人大喊正義獲勝。」我承認。「妳想要最終決戰。」他說。「我想讓大家吃上晚餐。」我糾正。他似乎很滿意這答案。我們正在學會持續艱難的工作，而不是把每場聽證都當成勝利遊行；這意外比高喊咒語還像英雄。

We passed the afternoon in a cafeteria where nobody recognized me as a summoned heroine. A cook handed us trays, a child complained about bitter vegetables, and someone argued about the price of rice. The ordinary noise was a relief after the court. Kael asked what job I would have chosen here if the bells had never failed. I said I might still have become an editor, because I liked stories that allowed people to speak in their own words. He said he would probably have built small bridges. Neither answer sounded grand enough for a prophecy, but together they felt like a place where we could stand. Before leaving, we listed the statements the court still needed and invited each witness to check the wording before it was submitted.

下午我們待在一間沒人認出我是召喚女英雄的餐廳。廚師遞來餐盤，孩子抱怨蔬菜太苦，有人爭論米價。經過法庭後，平凡吵雜聲反倒讓人安心。凱爾問，如果鐘塔從未故障，我在這裡會選什麼工作。我說可能還是編輯，因為喜歡讓人物用自己的話說故事。他說自己大概會去蓋小橋。兩種答案都不夠宏偉，當不了預言，可放在一起卻像是一個能讓我們站穩的地方。離開前，我們列出法庭仍需的證詞，也邀每位證人確認措辭後才提交。
