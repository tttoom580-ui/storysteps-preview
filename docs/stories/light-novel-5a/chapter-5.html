A Bell Rings for the People Who Stay

被召喚後只能改錯字的我，竟要拯救異世界的七座鐘塔？！ · 第 5 / 25 章 · 4000–5000 · 英中對照 · 校訂中

Attendance at the first-bell hearing was larger than anyone predicted. Residents arrived in contemporary work clothes, carrying a bruise here and a broken boot there from the flooded route. The mayor tried to contend that every delay was a minor inconvenience, but a corporation seeking exclusive transport rights had already described the debate as controversial. A grandmother stood up to defy both sides. "The essence of this matter is simple," she said. "Our names were on the old list. We are still here." Her words did more to facilitate the hearing than the mayor's entire opening speech.

第一鐘塔聽證會的出席人數超過所有人的預期。居民穿著現代工作服，帶著淹水道路留下的瘀傷與破靴子來到現場。市長主張每次延誤都只是小麻煩，可一家想取得獨家運輸權的公司早已把這場辯論說成有爭議。祖母站起來反對雙方。「事情的本質很簡單，」她說，「舊名單上有我們的名字，而我們現在還在這裡。」這句話比市長整場開幕演說更能促成討論。

The corporation brought a generous offering of gold and asked the council to endorse a private shortcut. The irony was that its proposed route passed directly through a village it claimed did not exist. Pip wore a ridiculous formal outfit and whispered the costs to me while I checked the overall map. Kael presented a different perspective: repairing the public bridge would take longer, but every resident could inspect the results. "It is not about whom you trust today," he said. "It is about a route that works after all of us have gone." I found his serious voice unfairly attractive.

那家公司送來豐厚的金幣，要求議會背書一條私人捷徑。諷刺的是，它建議的道路直接穿過它宣稱不存在的村莊。皮普穿著可笑的正式套裝，低聲向我報出費用，我則核對整體地圖。凱爾提出不同的觀點：修復公共橋梁要花較長時間，但每一位居民都能檢查成果。「重點不是今天相信誰，」他說，「而是我們都離開後，這條路仍能使用。」我發現他嚴肅的聲音有點過分迷人。

Before the public vote, Dahl gathered everyone who had helped us during the week. The bridge captain explained why he had initially refused to change the toll; a village elder explained why that hesitation had hurt families waiting for food. They disagreed without shouting, and their words became part of the hearing record. I was nervous because the crowd expected a summoned heroine to make a dazzling announcement. Instead I read aloud the three rules of my pencil and told them I would accept whatever they decided after seeing the evidence. The room grew quiet. The promise cost me more courage than I expected.

公開投票前，達爾召集本週協助過我們的人。橋隊長解釋自己最初為何拒絕改費率；村長則說明那份遲疑如何傷害等糧的家庭。雙方不同意對方，卻沒有吼叫，發言都進入聽證紀錄。我很緊張，因為人群期待被召喚的女英雄發表燦爛宣言。我卻只朗讀鉛筆三條規則，並說在證據看清後會尊重大家的決定。房間安靜下來。這承諾比我想像中更需要勇氣。

The council's ritual required every affected village to choose a witness, not merely accept the capital's signature. We divided the record into one segment for each settlement and attached the statistical count of households that would use the road. Dahl called this a difficult transition away from comfortable shortcuts. The clerk asked whether my red pencil could force the bell to accept the new list. I shook my head. "It can show that an old entry was erased. It cannot force anyone to approve a replacement." Each elder then examined the original ledger and signed without pressure.

議會的儀式規定，每個受影響村落必須自行選出見證人，而不是只接受首都簽名。我們將紀錄分成各村一段，附上會使用道路的家戶統計數字。達爾說，這是離開舒適捷徑的困難轉型。書記問我的紅鉛筆能不能強迫鐘塔接受新名單。我搖頭：「它能顯示舊條目被刪掉，卻不能逼任何人同意替換。」各村長老於是自行核對原始帳冊，在沒有壓力下簽名。

At sunset the first relay bell rang, and carts of bread rolled across the repaired bridge. The cheering was loud enough to make the stone pigeons on the roof scatter. In the crowd, I spotted a man in white gloves watching the signatures instead of the food. Dahl called him Archivist Vale, custodian of the seven-bell network. "He believes the city can survive only if every village obeys one central record," Dahl murmured. Vale bowed politely and disappeared before I could speak. Our first victory was real. So was the warning that somebody powerful considered it a mistake.

日落時第一座轉運鐘響起，載麵包的車隊通過修好的橋。歡呼聲大得讓屋頂的石鴿四散。我在人群裡看到一名戴白手套的男子，他盯著簽名而非食物。達爾稱他為檔案總管維爾，是七鐘網路的保管人。「他認為只有讓所有村莊服從同一份中央紀錄，城市才能活下去。」達爾低語。維爾禮貌鞠躬，在我開口前就離開了。我們的第一場勝利是真的；有人握有權勢，卻把勝利視為錯誤的警告，也是真的。

The food carts that crossed the bridge carried supplies to three settlements, but they also carried a message for the rest of the kingdom: lost names could be restored without replacing one ruler with another. Dahl warned us that the next bells controlled heat, transport, and the archive itself, making each correction more complex. Pip celebrated by decorating the courier bicycle with paper stars until Kael complained that they blocked his view. I felt the familiar pleasure of fixing a line of text and the unfamiliar fear of knowing that someone important disliked the result. On the distant tower, a single white window remained lit long after midnight.

穿過橋的糧車把物資送往三個村落，也替王國帶去一則訊息：遺失姓名可以恢復，不必用另一名統治者取代舊統治者。達爾提醒，接下來幾座鐘控制熱能、運輸與檔案本身，修正將更複雜。皮普為慶祝把紙星星貼滿信差腳踏車，直到凱爾抱怨擋住視線。我感到訂正一行文字的熟悉喜悅，也第一次因某位重要人物不喜歡結果而害怕。遠方高塔有一扇白色窗戶，午夜後仍亮著。
