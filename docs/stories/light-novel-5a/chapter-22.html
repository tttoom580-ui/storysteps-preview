The Apartment That Kept Every Voice

被召喚後只能改錯字的我，竟要拯救異世界的七座鐘塔？！ · 第 22 / 25 章 · 4000–5000 · 英中對照 · 校訂中

The old riverside block had ample empty rooms, yet families slept outside because a contractor charged impossible rental fees. A landlord announced the policy at a colorful booth beneath a gorgeous banner showing exotic gardens. People gave polite applause, which he mistook for approval. I checked the agreement and found a clear distinction between ordinary rent and emergency shelter. "You cannot confine a family to the street because you like the prettier option," I said. Lune warned me that the building's legacy included both generous public gifts and years of badly kept records, so neither praise nor blame would be simple.

河邊老公寓空房很多，家庭卻睡在戶外，因為承包商收取根本付不起的租金。房東在一個色彩鮮艷的攤位宣布政策，頭頂是印著異國花園的美麗布條。人們禮貌鼓掌，他卻誤以為大家贊成。我查閱協議，發現普通租賃與緊急避難明確不同。「不能因為你喜歡漂亮方案，就把家庭困在街上。」我說。露恩提醒我，這棟樓的歷史既有慷慨的公共捐助，也有多年管理不善的紀錄，功過不能輕易定論。

One resident spoke in a regional dialect the officials struggled to understand. Rather than dismiss her, a local teacher offered to interpret and recite the relevant clause aloud. The building's old heating appliance used a different operating mode from modern machines, so Kael asked to inspect its utility connections before moving families inside. A patient with a benign tumor needed a quiet room near the clinic; the staff arranged one without announcing his medical history to the crowd. Each family chose the information it wanted shared. The work was slower than a magical command and much kinder.

一名居民使用官員不太聽得懂的地方方言發言。地方教師沒有忽視她，而是自願口譯並朗讀相關條款。建築物老暖氣的運轉模式不同於現代機器，凱爾要求檢查公共設施接線後再讓家庭入住。一名患有良性腫瘤的病人需要靠近診所的安靜房間，工作人員為他安排，卻不向群眾公開他的病史。每個家庭自行選擇願意分享哪些資訊。工作比魔法命令慢，卻也溫柔得多。

The landlord's assistant initially took a firm stance against opening the empty rooms. She had seen tenants refuse to leave after past emergencies and feared losing her job if the new arrangement failed. I listened instead of calling her cruel. Lune found the original agreement describing a temporary review process with clear dates and an appeal procedure. The assistant read it slowly and admitted that no one had ever explained those protections to her. She agreed to help the families move in once the safety checks were complete. I wondered how often fear turned an ordinary worker into the face of an unfair rule.

房東助理最初堅決反對開放空房。過去緊急事件後，有些房客拒絕離開，她害怕新安排失敗就會丟工作。我沒有罵她殘忍，而是先聽。露恩找到原始協議，裡面說明有明確日期和申訴程序的臨時審查。助理慢慢讀完，承認從來沒有人向她解釋這些保障。她同意在安全檢查完成後協助家庭入住。我想，不公平規則有多常讓普通工人變成它的代言人。

The mayor demanded a verbal guarantee that every apartment was safe, whereas Kael insisted on written inspection results with dates. We discovered a storage door that would not unlock because its key had been filed under the wrong building number. My pencil exposed the error, but a licensed locksmith opened the door. Inside were blankets, lamps, and a letter from the original donor explaining that the rooms were meant for emergencies. The landlord read it twice. He still had legal questions, but he agreed to stop charging the families while independent advisers examined the old trust.

市長要求口頭保證每間公寓都安全，凱爾卻堅持提供附日期的書面檢查結果。我們發現一扇倉庫門打不開，因為鑰匙被歸到錯誤的樓號。我用鉛筆顯露錯誤，但真正開門的是合格鎖匠。裡面有毛毯、燈具和最初捐贈人的信，說明這些房間本就為緊急時期準備。房東把信讀了兩遍。他仍有法律疑問，但同意在獨立顧問查核舊信託期間，暫停向家庭收費。

That night the hallways filled with cooking smells and quiet conversation. A little girl asked whether the building would remember her voice. "Only if you want it to," I answered, showing her how the guest book worked. Kael stood by an open window and explained that the summoning circuit was likely a reversible transport gate, though he had not tested it. He offered to study the plans with me after the eclipse. I felt grateful, frightened, and strangely hopeful at once. I could choose to return to Tokyo someday without making the friends I had found here less real.

那夜走廊充滿飯菜香和輕聲談話。一名小女孩問，這棟樓會不會記得她的聲音。「只有妳願意才會。」我讓她看訪客簿怎麼使用。凱爾站在敞開的窗邊說，召喚電路很可能是可逆的運輸門，儘管他還沒實際測試。他願意在日蝕後陪我研究圖紙。我同時感激、害怕，又莫名懷著希望。有一天我可以選擇回東京，而不必因此讓在這裡找到的朋友變得不真實。

The girl who asked whether the house would remember her voice brought me a drawing of the new guest book. She had included a space where residents could say which parts of their stories they wanted to share. I told her she had invented a better form than many royal clerks. Her mother laughed and asked if I could teach the children how to read official notices. The invitation made my future feel less like an open wound and more like an open door. I could miss Tokyo, care for Kael, and choose an uncertain life without pretending that one choice erased the others.

問公寓會不會記住聲音的小女孩，帶來一張新訪客簿的設計圖。她留出一個欄位，讓居民寫下願意分享故事的哪些部分。我說她發明的表格比許多王室書記還好。她母親笑著問，我能不能教孩子讀官方公告。這份邀請讓我的未來不再像張開的傷口，更像敞開的門。我可以思念東京、在乎凱爾，並選擇不確定的生活，而不假裝某個選擇會抹掉其他一切。
