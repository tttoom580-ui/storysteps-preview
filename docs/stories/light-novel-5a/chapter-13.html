Three Signatures and a Broken Heater

被召喚後只能改錯字的我，竟要拯救異世界的七座鐘塔？！ · 第 13 / 25 章 · 4000–5000 · 英中對照 · 校訂中

In the northern clinic, a small boy showed me a rash that was clearly an allergic reaction, then asked whether magic could cure it. I had to acknowledge that my pencil was useless for medicine. The physician did not share his astonishment at my answer; she was busy getting consent for a treatment plan. Kael checked the heater's power conversion unit while Pip delivered meals to patients. A considerate nurse explained that the hospital had a budget deficit, so the planned extension of its ward had been delayed. "Hence the blankets," she said, handing me one before I could complain about the cold.

北方診所裡，一名小男孩讓我看明顯的過敏疹子，問魔法能不能治好。我不得不承認，自己的鉛筆對醫療毫無用處。醫師沒有和他一起對我的回答感到驚訝；她正忙著取得治療方案的同意。凱爾檢查暖氣的電力轉換器，皮普幫病人送餐。一名體貼的護理師解釋，醫院預算赤字讓擴建病房的計畫延後。「所以有這些毯子。」她說，在我抱怨寒冷前遞給我一條。

The hospital's legal office had changed the format of patient records to make them easier to store. Unfortunately, the new system put a legitimate diagnosis beside an unrelated payment demand, as if refusing the fee meant refusing treatment. A frightened parent clutched a lump of crumpled paper and sat on a mattress near the door. She worried that her daughter's chronic condition would be ignored. The head physician told me their preference was to separate medical consent from billing, but someone had added a compulsory stamp. We copied both versions and asked patients which changes they had actually accepted.

醫院法務室為了方便儲存，改變病人紀錄的格式。不幸的是，新系統把合法診斷和無關的付款要求放在一起，彷彿拒付就等於拒絕治療。一名害怕的家長捏著皺成一團的紙，坐在門邊床墊上，擔心女兒的慢性病會被忽視。主治醫師說，他們偏好把醫療同意與收費分開，卻有人加上強制印章。我們複製兩個版本，詢問病人實際接受哪些改動。

The doctor confessed that she had begun to feel pessimism about the clinic's future. Every winter she saw the same failures arrive under different names, and every spring the capital promised a fresh solution. I asked what would astonish her more: a perfect speech from the queen or a heating bill anyone could read. She laughed and chose the bill. Together we made a page that separated emergency costs, ordinary maintenance, and voluntary gifts. The clinic posted it where patients could ask questions without standing in front of an official desk. It was a small improvement, but nobody needed permission to understand it.

醫師坦承，她開始對診所的未來感到悲觀。每年冬天她都看見相同故障換個名稱重來，每年春天首都又承諾新的解答。我問哪個更能讓她驚訝：女王完美演說，還是一張人人都讀得懂的暖氣帳單。她笑著選帳單。我們一起做一頁清單，把緊急費用、例行維護與自願捐款分開。診所貼在病人能提問、不必站在官員桌前的位置。進步很小，但沒有人需要取得許可才看得懂。

A traveling performer who had lost his voice volunteered to read the public notice with hand signs. His friend, recently sober after a difficult year, helped interpret for older residents. I took the precaution of asking every family whether the meeting could mention their private circumstances. Some said no, and that was the end of the question. Kael suggested we utilize the clinic's spare generator for a short trial, with nurses able to stop it immediately. He found that the power loss came from a loose connector, not a mysterious syndrome. Pip pretended to faint from relief.

一名失去聲音的巡迴表演者自願用手勢宣讀公告。他那位經歷艱難一年、最近恢復清醒生活的朋友，幫年長居民解讀。我謹慎地逐一問家屬，會議能否提及他們的私人狀況。有人說不行，事情就到此為止。凱爾建議利用診所的備用發電機短暫測試，護理師可隨時停機。他發現電力損失來自鬆掉的接頭，而非神祕症候群。皮普高興得假裝昏倒。

By afternoon the heater worked and the clinic had posted a clearer consent form. Nobody called me a healer, which was comforting because I had been terrified they might. The doctor thanked Kael for explaining his calculations without hiding the limits. He shrugged, then asked whether I had eaten. I suddenly realized we had been working together for almost two weeks, and his first question after every crisis was no longer about the machinery. "Only if you agree to eat too," I said. Pip announced that the two of us were engaged to the soup kettle. Neither of us corrected him.

下午暖氣恢復運作，診所也張貼更清楚的同意書。沒有人稱我為治療師，這讓我鬆口氣，因為我最怕的就是這種誤會。醫師感謝凱爾清楚說明計算和限制。他聳肩，接著問我吃了沒。我突然發現，我們已經一起工作將近兩週，而每場危機後，他最先問的已不再是機器。「你也吃，我才吃。」我說。皮普宣布我倆已和湯鍋訂婚。我們誰也沒有糾正。

A young apprentice found the spare connector in a box that had been labeled as broken for years. Kael asked her to explain how she had noticed it. She said she compared the old diagram with the shape of the new socket, then checked the part number twice. Rather than calling her a genius, he asked whether she wanted credit in the repair report. She nodded so hard her cap fell off. Watching them, I thought about how many capable people remained invisible because nobody asked who had done the work. Later I wrote her name carefully into the public log, with her permission and the precise time of the repair.

一名年輕學徒在標示為故障品多年的盒子裡找到備用接頭。凱爾請她解釋如何發現。她說先比較舊圖和新插座形狀，再核對零件編號兩次。他沒有叫她天才，而是問她要不要在維修報告署名。她用力點頭，帽子都掉了。我看著他們，想到多少有能力的人只是因為沒人問工作是誰做的，就一直隱形。稍後我徵得她同意，仔細把她的名字和修復時間記進公開日誌。
