The Midnight Raid on the School Roof

被召喚後只能改錯字的我，竟要拯救異世界的七座鐘塔？！ · 第 19 / 25 章 · 4000–5000 · 英中對照 · 校訂中

A rumor that the archive would be raided drew half the city to the old school. An adolescent courier ran through the main aisle carrying a note, while a retired boxer kept the doors clear for children. Tessa, now acting as a radio commentator, warned listeners not to confuse a frightening headline with a confirmed emergency. People who usually commute across the river were trapped by a closed bridge. Kael called a competent safety officer and asked what the guards had actually ordered. The officer had to concede that nobody had requested an arrest. Someone simply wanted to remove the school's original witness book before morning.

檔案館可能遭突襲的傳言，把半座城的人都引到舊學校。一名少年信差穿過主走道送來紙條，退休拳擊手則替孩子維持出入口暢通。如今擔任電臺評論員的泰莎提醒聽眾，別把可怕標題和已確認的緊急事件混為一談。平常通勤過河的人因橋關閉而受困。凱爾致電稱職的安全官，問守衛到底接到什麼命令。安全官不得不承認，沒人要求逮捕任何人，只是有人想在天亮前移走學校原始見證簿。

The roof held a horizontal track used by the school's astronomy class. A small pedal-powered cart could move records across the gap between two buildings, but its load limit was low. "We need the right proportion of books to equipment," the teacher said. Her students had designed an innovation: a braking loop that prevented the cart from running away on its own. We tested it without passengers, then assigned a clear slot for each box. Pip tried to make a joke about racing the moon, but the smell of smoke from the river made everyone serious. We still had time to stop if the wind changed.

學校屋頂有一條天文課用的水平軌道。一輛腳踏驅動的小推車能把文件送過兩棟樓間的空隙，可載重很低。「書與設備的比例必須正確。」老師說。學生們設計了一項創新：煞車迴路能防止車子自己滑走。我們先在無乘客狀態下測試，再替每箱文件安排明確位置。皮普想開玩笑說要跟月亮賽跑，但河邊飄來的煙味讓所有人都嚴肅起來。只要風向改變，我們還有時間停下。

The school's head teacher insisted that every student could decide whether to help with the transfer. Some chose to carry boxes, some offered food, and others went home. Nobody was shamed for being afraid. The head teacher told me that protecting young people sometimes meant allowing them to say no even when a task was important. I thought of my own arrival in Bellhaven, when the palace had almost treated my ability as public property. The older I became in this strange world, the more I valued an ordinary choice made freely. It was a lesson no status screen could award as an experience point.

學校校長堅持每位學生都能自行決定是否幫忙移送。有些搬箱，有些供應食物，另一些直接回家。沒有人因害怕而被羞辱。校長說，保護年輕人有時意味著任務再重要，也要准許他們說不。我想到初到鈴港時，王宮幾乎把我的能力當公共財產。這個奇怪世界待得越久，我就越珍惜自由做出的平凡選擇。這道理不是任何狀態視窗能獎勵的經驗值。

Under the eaves, a scandal pamphlet accused the school of hiding stolen goods. The paper had the scent of fresh glue, and its ink began to soak through my gloves. I compared it with an official update on the evacuation plan. The pamphlet claimed an armed raid was under way, but the dated log proved it had been printed before any guards arrived. "That is undoubtedly misleading," Tessa said, careful not to call its author a criminal without further evidence. A witty student pointed out that the pamphlet's map put the river on the wrong side of the building. We copied the page and kept the original dry.

屋簷下，一張醜聞傳單指控學校藏匿贓物。紙上有新膠水的氣味，墨水甚至滲進我的手套。我將它與官方最新撤離方案比對。傳單說武裝突襲正在進行，但有日期的紀錄證明它在守衛抵達前就已印好。「這毫無疑問具有誤導性。」泰莎說，仍小心避免在沒有更多證據前把作者稱為罪犯。一名機智學生指出，傳單地圖竟把河畫在建築物另一邊。我們複製該頁，讓原件保持乾燥。

At midnight the safety officer returned with permission for a supervised transfer to the public archive. The school children carried lanterns, not weapons, and each box traveled with a signed inventory. I used no correction mark that night. The truth was visible enough when people agreed to look. Afterward Kael sat beside me on the roof and asked whether I had been afraid. "Very," I said. "You?" "Of course." He rested his shoulder against mine. We watched the river lights turn back on, one small house at a time.

午夜時安全官帶著許可回來，准許在監督下將文件移交公共檔案館。孩子們拿的是燈籠，不是武器；每箱文件都附著簽名清單。那夜我沒有使用任何修正標記。只要人們願意看，真相其實已足夠清晰。後來凱爾坐在我身旁的屋頂，問我剛才怕不怕。「很怕。」我說，「你呢？」「當然怕。」他把肩靠向我。我們看著河岸燈火一戶一戶重新亮起。

When the transfer ended, Tessa invited the safety officer to speak on the radio. He explained why the first rumor had sounded believable and why an official correction was necessary. He did not blame the crowd for being frightened; he described what information people should check during the next emergency. Kael helped the school repair a broken rooftop rail before leaving. The students painted the repaired section bright yellow and wrote the safe load beside it. Pip wanted to sign his name as the hero of the night, but the children told him the rail belonged to their school. He accepted that verdict with theatrical suffering.

移送結束後，泰莎邀安全官上電臺說明。他解釋第一則謠言為何令人信服，以及官方更正為何必要。他沒有責怪人群害怕，而是說明下次緊急事件應先查什麼資訊。離開前凱爾幫學校修好屋頂壞掉的欄杆。學生把修復段漆成亮黃色，旁邊寫上安全載重。皮普想簽名當那夜的英雄，孩子們卻告訴他欄杆屬於學校。他以戲劇性的痛苦表情接受判決。
