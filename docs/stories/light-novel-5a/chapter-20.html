The Six-Bell Agreement

The city finally agreed to accommodate the outer districts at the main negotiating table. An administrative analyst explained that the relay network was an asset belonging to all seven regions, not the exclusive estate of the palace. Vale arrived with a fabulous collection of old keys and looked ready to declare the meeting over. Instead, he sat down and asked for counsel from the district representatives. I felt myself blush when Kael quietly offered me the chair beside him. A moment later the mood darkened: several families were still trying to grieve loved ones lost during the old relay accident.

城市終於同意讓城外區域坐上主要談判桌。一名行政分析師解釋，轉運鐘網路是七個區域共同擁有的資產，不是王宮的獨家產業。維爾帶著一大串華麗的舊鑰匙現身，表情像準備宣布散會。他卻坐下，向各區代表徵求意見。凱爾悄悄把旁邊椅子留給我，我忍不住臉紅。下一刻氣氛沉重起來：仍有幾個家庭為舊鐘塔事故失去的親人悲傷。

A technician placed a fiber cable on the table beside the previous narrative of the accident. The cable showed that an overhead relay had been connected to a backup line without authorization. The prior report described the change as harmless, but the measurement logs disagreed. "Are you trying to provoke me?" Vale asked. "No," Kael said. "We want a negotiation that begins with the actual system." Vale allowed an independent examination and agreed that the city could purchase new parts through a public process. The technicians would publish every tested limit and stop the work whenever an alarm sounded.

技師把一段光纖纜線放在桌上，旁邊是事故的舊版敘述。纜線顯示，頭頂的轉運裝置曾在未授權狀態下連接備援線。先前報告稱改動無害，量測紀錄卻不一致。「你想激怒我嗎？」維爾問。「不是，」凱爾說，「我們想以真正的系統現況開始談判。」維爾准許獨立檢查，同意城市透過公開程序購買新零件。技師會公布每項測試上限，警報一響就停止作業。

The negotiations almost collapsed when two districts argued about which one should receive the first shipment after the repair. A hospital representative said patients needed urgent medicine; a farmer explained that without seed deliveries there would be no harvest next month. Instead of declaring one side more important, the council asked both to list their deadlines and safe alternatives. The resulting schedule reserved a small medical route and a separate farm route, with public checks on actual deliveries. I expected Vale to object, but he quietly pointed out a missing bridge inspection. We added it to the plan. A difficult opponent could still contribute a useful correction.

兩區為修復後誰先領物資爭論時，談判差點破裂。醫院代表說病人急需藥品，農民說沒有種子，下個月就沒有收成。議會沒有宣判哪邊更重要，而是請雙方列出期限與安全替代方案。最後時程保留一條小型醫療線和另一條農用線，實際配送接受公開核對。我原以為維爾會反對，他卻安靜指出橋梁少了一次檢查。我們把它加進計畫。困難的對手仍可能提出有用的修正。

The delegates insisted that emotional sensitivity mattered as much as engineering accuracy. A transparent account would record which town received power, why a change was made, and who could reverse it. An old boatbuilder offered his yacht as a temporary medical ferry, but only if the river authority confirmed it was safe. Another delegate asked for a new rail connection to prevent food deliveries from depending on the bells alone. We put the proposals to a vote, and the representatives signed a six-bell agreement with witnesses from every affected district. Nobody used my pencil; the text was clear before anyone touched it.

代表們堅持，情緒敏感度和工程精確度同樣重要。透明帳冊要記下哪座城獲得電力、為何改變，以及誰有權撤回。一位老造船匠願意提供自己的遊艇作臨時醫療渡輪，條件是河川管理局確認安全。另一名代表要求新建鐵道，避免糧食配送只依靠鐘塔。我們將提案交付投票，各區受影響者派出見證人，簽下六鐘協議。沒有人用到我的鉛筆；文字在任何人動手前就已清楚。

At sunset the fourth, fifth, and sixth bells answered one another across the city. Vale stayed to watch the lights stabilize instead of leaving in anger. Kael asked whether I wanted to go walking after the meeting. I said yes, but only after we delivered the final signed copies. On the bridge he admitted that he liked me, not because I had saved him, but because I challenged him when he wanted an easy answer. "I like you too," I said, surprised by how calm the words felt. He asked if he could kiss me. I said yes. Somewhere below us Pip pretended not to cheer.

日落時，第四、第五、第六座鐘在城中彼此應答。維爾留下看燈光穩定，沒有氣沖沖離開。凱爾問我會議後要不要散步。我說好，但要先把最後簽署副本送到指定地方。橋上他承認喜歡我，不是因為我救了他，而是因為他想找簡單答案時，我會質疑他。「我也喜歡你。」我說，意外發現這句話說得很平靜。他問能不能吻我。我說可以。橋下某個地方，皮普假裝沒有歡呼。

After our walk, Kael did not suddenly become a different man. He still spoke too seriously about bolts, and I still corrected the signs on doors that nobody else noticed. The difference was that we could speak about an uncomfortable truth without immediately trying to escape it. Pip announced that he would charge us a consultation fee for every moment of romantic awkwardness he had endured. I said he owed us a fee for every time he had interrupted a serious conversation. We agreed to cancel both accounts. The seventh bell was still waiting, but the next morning no longer felt like an impossible final boss.

散步後，凱爾沒有突然變成另一個人。他仍會太認真地談螺栓，我仍會訂正別人沒注意的門牌。不同的是，我們能談令人不舒服的事實，而不急著逃開。皮普宣布，要為自己承受的每次戀愛尷尬向我們收諮詢費。我說，他每次打斷嚴肅對話也該付費。我們同意兩邊帳款互相抵銷。第七鐘仍在等待，可隔天早晨已不再像無法打敗的最終魔王。
