The Script That Held a City Hostage

A traveling theater arrived with a story about an abnormal kingdom where nobody could refuse the king. Its actors were trying to acquaint young viewers with the history of slavery, but the capital's censor called the script dangerous. A bolt slammed across the theater door, trapping the troupe inside. "That is taking people hostage," I told the guard. He claimed an emergency order made the action compatible with public safety. Kael asked to see the order before anyone tried to confront him. The guard produced a page written in an unfamiliar hand, and the paper smelled of fresh ink.

一個巡迴劇團帶來一齣故事，說某個異常王國裡沒有人能拒絕國王。演員想讓年輕觀眾認識奴隸制度的歷史，首都審查員卻把劇本稱為危險作品。一根門閂突然落下，把整個劇團關在戲院裡。「這是在挾持人質。」我對守衛說。他聲稱緊急命令讓這行動符合公共安全。凱爾要他先出示命令，才准任何人上前對質。守衛拿出陌生筆跡寫成的紙張，聞起來還有新墨水味。

The director described a continental tour that had already performed this play without trouble. At one stop the audience had discussed discrimination against people born outside the walls. Here, however, the censor called the actors traitors for questioning the official norm. A frightened apprentice began to mumble that someone might kidnap the lead performer if the show continued. Tessa asked an independent observer to record the guards' positions and the time the lock was placed. "We need to protect everyone," she said, "including the officials who may be following a false instruction." Her calm voice steadied the room.

導演說，劇團已在整片大陸巡演這齣戲，從未出事。有一站的觀眾甚至討論出生在城牆外的人遭受的歧視。可在這裡，審查員卻因演員質疑官方常規，就把他們叫做叛徒。一名受驚學徒喃喃說，如果演出繼續，也許有人會綁架主演。泰莎請獨立觀察員記錄守衛位置和上鎖時間。「我們得保護所有人，」她說，「包括可能奉行假命令的官員。」她冷靜的聲音穩住現場。

When the performers stepped into the square, they felt a sudden sensation of freedom that made several of them cry. The lead actor admitted he had thought about leaving the city after the order arrived. Instead he stayed because younger members of the troupe had nowhere else to go. The director asked me to check the new playbill for mistakes. I found a missing accent, two incorrect dates, and a line that named the sponsor as the author of the play. I crossed out the last claim only after the real writer confirmed it was false. She smiled when her own name appeared in the corrected program.

演員走進廣場時，忽然有一股自由感湧上心頭，好幾人忍不住哭泣。主演承認命令送達後，他曾想離開城市，卻因劇團年輕成員無處可去而留下。導演請我檢查新節目單。我找到一個漏掉的重音符號、兩個錯誤日期，以及一行把贊助者寫成劇作者的文字。真正作者確認那是假的，我才劃掉最後一項說法。修正後的節目單印出她自己的名字時，她笑了。

An old missionary who had helped establish the city's shelters recognized the seal on the order. She brought a stock of earlier documents from the district archive. Their formats matched, but the new page included a viewer restriction nobody remembered approving. I showed the originals to the theater workers and asked whether they would permit my mark. They agreed; the missing signature line appeared, proving that the order had never been authorized. The guard opened the door, relieved to have a reason he could explain to his superior. No one needed to be treated as a villain merely for admitting a mistake.

一名曾幫忙建立城市避難所的老傳教士認得命令上的印章。她從區域檔案館帶來一批舊文件。格式相符，但新頁多了一項沒人記得核准的觀眾限制。我向劇團工作者展示原件，詢問他們是否同意我使用標記。大家答應後，消失的簽署欄便顯現，證明該命令從未正式授權。守衛如釋重負地打開門，因為他終於有理由向上司解釋。沒有人需要只因承認犯錯就被當成惡人。

We helped the troupe arrange a new performance in the public square. The story contained a viable hope: people could learn from the past without accepting its cruelty. Children watched the actors bow and asked questions that made the adults uncomfortable in useful ways. When the lead performer thanked me, I said the actors had been brave enough to remain on stage. He laughed. "You are an editor who refuses to take credit for an ending," he observed. I wished that were always true. Sometimes I still wanted the kind of applause a summoned heroine was supposed to receive.

我們協助劇團在公共廣場安排新演出。劇情帶著可行的希望：人們能從過去學習，而不必接受過去的殘酷。孩子們看演員謝幕，提出使大人不自在卻很有意義的問題。主演向我道謝時，我說真正勇敢的是願意留在舞臺上的演員。他笑了：「妳是個不肯把結局功勞攬走的編輯。」我希望自己總能如此。有時我仍想要被召喚女主角理應得到的掌聲。

Tessa suggested recording the performance for anyone who could not attend in person. The actors agreed to be recorded but asked that the children in the crowd not be shown without family permission. We arranged the cameras accordingly. Kael helped build a narrow ramp to the stage, and the mushroom attorney from the forest unexpectedly appeared among the viewers. It claimed to represent the rights of potted plants used as scenery. Pip bought it a seat with a good view and requested that no further lawsuits be filed before dinner. The mushroom accepted the seat but refused to make promises about dinner.

泰莎提議錄下演出，讓無法到場的人也能觀看。演員同意錄影，但要求沒有家庭許可就不要拍到觀眾席的孩子。我們據此安排攝影機。凱爾協助搭建通往舞臺的緩坡，森林的蘑菇律師竟意外出現在觀眾中。牠聲稱代表作為布景的盆栽權利。皮普替牠買了視野好的座位，請求晚餐前不要再提訴訟。蘑菇接受座位，卻拒絕就晚餐做任何承諾。
