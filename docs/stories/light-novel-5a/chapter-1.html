My Summoning Error Has a Receipt

被召喚後只能改錯字的我，竟要拯救異世界的七座鐘塔？！ · 第 1 / 25 章 · 4000–5000 · 英中對照 · 校訂中

The first thing I learned about another world was that its bakery sold a batch of moon-shaped buns before sunrise. The second was that a talking stamp could insult me. I was Kisaragi Mio, twenty-six, a Tokyo proofreader with a decent record and no heroic ambitions. A faulty summoning circuit had dropped me into Bellhaven's central kitchen. When the floor monster refused to dissolve, I circled a missing safety clause in the spell hanging above its head. The monster became an ordinary sack of flour. My unexpected breakthrough earned me applause and a bill for the broken oven.

我對異世界的第一個認識，是這裡的麵包店在日出前出售一批月亮形狀的麵包。第二個認識，是會說話的印章居然能罵人。我叫如月澪，二十六歲，是東京的校對員，工作紀錄還算體面，從沒想當英雄。失靈的召喚電路把我丟進鈴港的中央廚房。地板怪物怎樣都不肯消失時，我在牠頭上的咒文圈出遺漏的安全條款。怪物立刻變回一袋普通麵粉。這個意外突破讓我得到掌聲，也收到一張烤箱維修帳單。

"An idiot with a red pencil defeated my guardian?" demanded the royal clerk. "It was a correction pencil," I said, which apparently made matters worse. Professor Dahl, a retired mentor to the city's bell engineers, prevented my arrest by requesting an inquiry into the failed summons. He explained that the nonprofit Night School would implement a temporary apprenticeship rather than let the palace claim me as property. "You can mark errors," he warned, "but you cannot write new facts. You get three marks each day. A correction becomes binding only when the original record and affected witnesses agree." I had finally found a boss who gave clear instructions.

「拿紅鉛筆的笨蛋打敗了我的守衛？」王宮書記怒吼。「是校對鉛筆。」我糾正他，結果似乎更糟。退休的鐘塔工程導師達爾教授要求調查召喚失敗一事，阻止我被逮捕。他說，非營利的夜間學校將實施臨時學徒制度，而不是讓王宮把我當財產。「妳能標出錯誤，卻不能寫出新的事實。每天只有三次標記。只有原始紀錄與受影響證人一致同意，修正才會生效。」他警告。我終於遇到一位會把工作規則說清楚的主管。

Dahl also explained why the city had summoned a proofreader instead of a warrior. The summoning office had requested someone who could recognize alterations in official language, but the instruction had been translated into "person who wins battles with a red weapon." That was apparently close enough for the royal machine. I demanded a copy of the request, and he gave it to me without hesitation. I found five spelling mistakes and one terrifying omission: the form contained no return date. "Will I ever go home?" I asked. Dahl admitted he did not know. At least he did not answer with a heroic prophecy. I decided uncertainty was better than a false promise.

達爾還解釋，城市為何召喚的是校對員，而不是戰士。召喚辦公室原本要求一個能辨識官方文字竄改的人，但指令被翻成「使用紅色武器贏得戰鬥的人」。這對王室機器而言居然夠接近。我要求看申請副本，他毫不猶豫交給我。我找出五個拼字錯誤和一項可怕的遺漏：表格裡沒有返程日期。「我還能回家嗎？」我問。達爾坦言不知道。至少他沒有拿英雄預言搪塞。我決定，不確定也比虛假的承諾好。

A courier with cat ears named Pip showed me the placement list for families stranded outside the city walls. Above it, a projection of the coming eclipse flashed the number twenty-five: days until the seven relay bells would renew the capital's supply lines. Under the current regime, homes without registered names would receive nothing. "We help them regardless of their papers," Pip said. I asked whether the old bridge could be reinforced before the eclipse. Dahl admitted that the first bell controlled food wagons, and its register had recently lost three village names. Suddenly my tiny talent looked less like a joke and more like a dangerous job.

一名叫皮普的貓耳信差給我看被困城牆外家庭的安置名單。上方投影出即將到來的日蝕，倒數數字是二十五：七座轉運鐘塔重新核發首都物資線的剩餘天數。依照現行制度，未登記姓名的住戶什麼都領不到。「不管他們有沒有文件，我們都會幫忙。」皮普說。我問能不能在日蝕前加固舊橋。達爾承認第一座鐘塔控制糧車，而它的名冊最近少了三個村名。突然間，我這點微不足道的本領不再像笑話，而像一份危險工作。

I rode behind Pip on a bicycle whose saddle tried to throw me into every puddle. Near the bridge, a silver creature balanced on the rim of a cracked bell, watching us with too many eyes. The sight was meant to terrify travelers, but its tired wings made me feel sorry for it. My wrist ached with the strain of carrying Dahl's enormous rulebook. "The toll has gone up to triple the old price," Pip whispered. I used my second mark to reveal a crossed-out village exemption. It did not open the gate. We still needed the village elders to testify, and the guards to listen.

我坐在皮普腳踏車後座，鬆動的坐墊幾乎把我甩進每一個水坑。橋邊一隻銀色生物停在裂鐘的邊緣，用多得不正常的眼睛看著我們。牠顯然是用來嚇唬旅人的，可疲倦的翅膀反讓我同情。達爾那本厚重規則書讓我的手腕不堪負荷。「過橋費變成原來三倍了。」皮普低語。我用了第二次標記，顯露出被劃掉的村民免稅條文。但大門並未因此開啟。我們還需要村長作證，也需要守衛願意聽。

At the bridge, the guards allowed us to photograph the disputed notice, but they refused to alter the toll on my word alone. Their captain said the village elders would return the next day, if the road remained open. I respected his caution, although I wished he had shown the same concern before the charge increased. Pip shared the last moon bun with me and pointed toward the distant hills. Seven dark bells stood against the sunrise, each connected to homes, roads, and the ordinary work of staying alive. I had hoped my first night in another world would include a warm bed. Instead it ended with flour in my hair and a very important appointment.

橋邊守衛准許我們拍攝爭議公告，卻拒絕只憑我的話修改過路費。隊長說，只要道路仍開放，村長隔天就會回來。我尊重他的謹慎，雖然希望收費漲價前他也能如此小心。皮普把最後一個月亮麵包分給我，指向遠方山丘。七座黑色鐘塔映在晨光裡，每一座都連著住家、道路和維持日常生活的工作。我原以為異世界第一晚會有溫暖的床。結果頭髮沾滿麵粉，還多了一個非常重要的約會。
