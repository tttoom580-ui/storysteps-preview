下街的水

Water for the Lower Street

By the fourth morning, Cloudstep had another problem. Several children on the lower street had stomach pain, and a nurse found a little blood in one child’s mouth from dry, cracked lips. It was not a new disease; families had been drinking water from a muddy branch of the stream. The town health director asked everyone to boil water. Lin visited the entire street with Tao and wrote down which houses had clean supplies. A generous shopkeeper handed them bottles and two hamburgers for lunch. “You cannot help people while you forget to eat,” she said. The work was tiring, but the answers were clear.

第四天早上，雲階又出了問題。下街幾名孩子肚子不舒服，護理師還看見一個孩子因乾裂嘴唇而嘴角流了一點血。這不是新疾病；大家一直在喝支流水道的混濁水。鎮衛生主管請大家把水煮沸。琳和陶走遍整條街，記下哪些住戶還有乾淨的水。一位慷慨的店主送來瓶裝水和兩個漢堡當午餐。「忘記吃飯就幫不了人，」她說。工作辛苦，但答案很明確。

The old station was built from stone and wood, with a network of tubes running under the market. Lin began to imagine a short-term way to bring water from the upper tank while the lift stayed closed. The workers would combine a clean pipe with the old public tap. They had enough material for twenty homes, but the lower population was much larger. Tao suggested choosing houses by distance. Lin said that a fair plan should also consider young children and sick people. They asked the nurse to help make a clear list, without writing anyone’s private illness on a public board.

舊車站用石頭和木材建成，市集底下有一整套管路網。琳開始設想，在升降機仍關閉時，如何暫時把上方水槽的水送下來。工人可以把乾淨水管接上舊公用水龍頭。他們的材料只夠二十戶使用，可下街人口遠多於此。陶提議按住家距離分配，琳認為公平計畫也要考慮年幼孩子與病人。他們請護理師協助列出清楚名單，但不在公開板上寫出任何人的私人病情。

At the market a fruit seller gave them papaya and said the upper district had already set a target for its own repair work. He did not think the lower street was worth the trouble. Lin answered that people in both parts of town needed water, and nobody could escape that simple fact. She could sense anger rising in the crowd, so she asked Tao to show the supply figures instead of arguing about whose house mattered more. The figures showed that one narrow pipe could serve a public tap for six hours a day. Therefore, the first step was not to promise every house a private connection, but to open a shared point safely.

在市集，水果攤老闆送他們木瓜，並說高區早已為自己的維修訂好目標，他認為下街不值得費心。琳回答，鎮上兩區的人都需要水，誰也逃不開這個事實。她感到人群的怒氣升高，便請陶展示供水數字，而不是爭論誰家比較重要。數據指出，一根細水管每天可以供應公用水龍頭六小時。因此第一步不是承諾每家都有私人管線，而是安全地開設共同取水點。

They built a small frame to hold the pipe. Tao measured each board, and the nurse checked that the water came from the clean tank. Lin wrote the hours on a sign large enough to read from the road. At first the pipe shook and one joint leaked. A worker wanted to push more water through it, but the manual warned that extra pressure could break a weak link. Lin asked him to stop. They changed the joint and tried again at half the flow. This time the water ran clear. A child filled a cup, watched it closely, and smiled. It was a modest score for the day, yet one the street could see.

他們搭起小支架固定管子。陶量好每塊木板，護理師確認水確實來自乾淨水槽。琳把取水時間寫在路上就看得清楚的牌子上。起初管子震動，一個接頭漏水。工人想加大水流，但手冊警告額外壓力可能使脆弱接頭破裂。琳請他停下，換過接頭後用一半流量再試。這次流出的水很清澈。一個孩子裝滿杯子，仔細看了看，露出笑容。那是小小的當日成果，卻是整條街都能見到的成果。

Near sunset the shopkeeper returned with one last hamburger for the nurse, who had not eaten since morning. A little boy asked why the water was free when the shop sold food for money. Lin explained that the town paid for the public pipe together, just as it paid for the road. The temporary tap had a limited supply, so everyone still needed to take only what they could use. The child nodded and filled half his jar. Watching him, Lin understood that fair rules had to be simple enough for the people who lived with them. It was not enough for a director to sign a page; the rule also had to make sense on the street.

黃昏前，店主又帶來最後一個漢堡，送給從早上就沒吃東西的護理師。一個小男孩問，為何店裡食物要錢，水卻免費。琳解釋，公共水管是大家共同出錢維持的，就像道路一樣。臨時水龍頭供應有限，所以每人仍應只取所需。孩子點頭，只裝了半罐。看著他，琳明白公平規則必須讓實際生活其中的人也能懂。主管簽一張紙還不夠，規則也要能在街上說得通。

That evening the health director examined the new tap and signed its temporary safety notice. The town still needed its full water system, and the hidden passage remained a question. But the lower street had gained a safe place to draw water without waiting for the mayor. Lin showed Tao the list of people who had helped: the nurse, the fruit seller, the shopkeeper, and the workers. She wanted the names on the public record because a town was more than its leader. As they closed the archive, an older worker brought one more fact. The hidden passage had once supplied air to the same tank. Someone had blocked that air line on purpose.

傍晚，衛生主管檢查新水龍頭，簽下臨時安全許可。鎮上仍需要完整水系，隱藏通道的疑問也還在。但下街已經有安全取水點，不必只能等鎮長。琳把協助者名單給陶看：護理師、水果攤老闆、店主與工人。她希望把名字寫進公開紀錄，因為一座城鎮不只屬於領導者。關上檔案室前，一名老工人帶來新事實：隱藏通道從前替同一座水槽供應空氣，而且有人故意堵住那條風道。
