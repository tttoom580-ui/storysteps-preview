假的警報

The False Alarm

The next day brought an alarm before breakfast. A sharp bell rang from the empty goods yard, and people ran toward the square. Tao kept calm and asked them not to crowd the station gate. He had seen the old drawing of the control cell, a little room that held the warning switch. Lin studied the classic design of its door. It had two handles, one for a direct test and one for a full stop. Someone had painted a cowboy on the wall long ago, perhaps to make the room seem friendly to children on a school tour. Now the cheerful picture looked strange beside the red warning light.

隔天早餐前，警報又響了。貨場空空的，卻傳出尖銳鐘聲，人們奔向廣場。陶保持冷靜，請大家別擠在車站門口。他見過控制小室的舊圖，那是裝著警示開關的房間。琳研究這扇門的經典設計：上面有兩個把手，一個用來直接測試，另一個用來完全停止。很久以前，有人把牛仔畫在牆上，也許是想讓參觀的孩子覺得親切。如今那張開朗的圖像在紅色警示燈旁顯得古怪。

The station guard said it was everyone’s duty to leave. He wanted to press the full-stop handle, but Lin pointed to a line in the manual. The first handle could test the alarm without moving the lift. The second would close every air door for an hour. That could punish the people waiting for clean air below. Lin showed the guard the page and asked him to read it aloud. He agreed to a short test under his watch. Tao held a clock and Lin pressed the small button. The alarm rang once, then stopped. Its sound came from the old test line, not from the main engine.

站衛說大家都有離開的責任。他想按下完全停止把手，琳卻指出手冊的一行說明。第一個把手可測試警報而不移動升降機；第二個則會關閉所有通風門一小時，讓下面等待新鮮空氣的人受苦。琳讓站衛看那頁，請他朗讀。站衛同意在他監督下短暫測試。陶拿著鐘計時，琳按下小按鈕。警報響了一聲便停了。聲音來自舊測試線，而非主引擎。

A farmer arrived with a lamb in his arms. His flock had been frightened by the noise. A young visitor asked whether the red light meant a strike had damaged the train. Another raised a phone for a quick shot of the control room. Lin asked both to stand back. “A picture can help,” she said, “but a false story spreads faster.” She did not object to questions. She objected to people opening doors before they understood what was behind them. A town worker offered a dryer to clear water from the test box. Lin checked that its power was off before anyone touched the wet wire.

一名農夫抱著小羊走來，羊群被聲響嚇壞了。年輕訪客問是不是有人罷工破壞列車才亮紅燈，另一人則拿起手機想拍控制室的照片。琳請他們退後。「照片有用，」她說，「但錯誤故事傳得更快。」她並不反對提問，反對的是還不懂門後有什麼就急著打開門。一名鎮工人提議用吹乾機清理測試箱積水。琳先確認電源已關，才讓大家碰觸濕電線。

By afternoon the workers found a broken spring inside the test box. The main bell had never sounded that morning. Tao took a drawing to the public notice board and explained the difference. Some people were disappointed; a dangerous mystery was more exciting than a small spring. Lin said it mattered precisely because it was small. They could repair one piece without closing the whole station. The guard agreed to let them record the test. He still would not open the hidden passage, but he gave them the date of its last official inspection. That date came before the newest manual had been printed.

下午，工人在測試箱裡找到一根斷彈簧。當天早上主警鐘根本沒響。陶把圖帶到公告板，向大家解釋差別。有些人很失望，危險謎團比小彈簧刺激。琳說，正因為它小，才值得注意。他們可以修好一個零件，而不必關閉整座車站。站衛同意讓他們記錄測試。他仍不肯打開隱藏通道，但告訴他們最後一次正式檢查的日期；那日期早於新版手冊印製。

At the evening market, the barber complained that he had missed an appointment for a haircut because the alarm had closed the street. Tao offered to pay for the lost time, but the barber refused. He wanted the station to work, not a gift from two young people. They talked about the trade between safety and speed: closing everything might feel safe, yet it could leave important public services useless. Lin wrote that a test button must not be mistaken for an emergency stop. She asked the guard to place clear words beside both handles. The next visitor should not need to know the history of the entire station to choose the correct one. A readable sign could prevent the next false alarm before it began.

傍晚市集裡，理髮師抱怨警報封街，使他錯過一個理髮預約。陶想賠他的損失，理髮師拒絕了。他要的是能運作的車站，不是兩個年輕人的禮物。他們談到安全與速度之間的取捨：把所有東西關掉看似安全，也可能讓重要公共服務失效。琳記下測試按鈕不能被誤當緊急停止鈕，請衛兵在兩個把手旁貼上清楚文字。下一位訪客不該得讀完整座車站的歷史，才知道該選哪個。清楚易讀的標示，也許能在下次假警報發生前就預防它。

Tao found an old sail from a river boat in the yard and used it to cover the open test box from rain. It belonged to a trader who had once crossed the lake below Cloudstep. The trader offered the cloth for free after hearing why they needed it. Lin gave him a written note, so the workers would know whom to thank. She also wrote the words “test alarm” beside the morning event. No one had been trapped, and no machine had exploded. Yet the false alarm had shown how easily a crowd could be moved by fear. Before going home, Lin drew a circle around the inspection date. Their next task was to discover who had changed the manual after that day.

陶在貨場找到一片舊帆布，是從前河船的船帆，便拿來遮住打開的測試箱，不讓雨淋進去。那帆屬於曾橫渡雲階下方湖泊的商人。商人聽明白用途，免費把布借給他們。琳留下書面紀錄，好讓工人知道該感謝誰。她還在早晨事件旁寫上「測試警報」。沒有人受困，機器也沒爆炸。但這次假警報顯示，人們很容易被恐懼帶動。回家前，琳圈出檢查日期。他們下一步要找出，是誰在那天以後改了手冊。
