管中的液體

The Liquid in the Tube

鐘聲之外，還有一行字 · 第 9 / 25 章 · 1000–2000 · 英中對照 · 校訂中

The test team entered the outer yard with a guard and two experienced workers. Every person was alive and ready, but Lin could feel their fear. She read the safety page aloud so no one needed to guess what would happen next. They opened a small lid on the outside of the lantern wall. A clear liquid filled a narrow tube behind it. Tao thought it was water. The engineer said it was colored test oil, used to show the position of an air valve. A sudden burst of wind shook the cover, and everyone stepped back. Nothing broke. The tube had simply shown them that air was moving where the manual said it should not.

測試小隊在衛兵與兩名資深工人陪同下進入外院。人人都平安、準備就緒，琳卻感覺得到他們的恐懼。她朗讀安全頁，讓大家不用猜下一步。工人打開提燈牆外側一個小蓋子，後方細管裡裝著透明液體。陶以為是水，工程師說那是帶色的測試油，用來顯示風閥位置。一陣突然的風猛地震動蓋子，大家都後退。沒有東西壞掉，細管只是顯示：有空氣在不該流動的地方流動。

Lin drew an image of the tube on her page and gave it a clear title. The guard wanted a quick solution, but she said the result of a test could only answer the question it was made for. It showed pressure, not the full cause. Tao made a silly face to ease the tension. One worker laughed and said he could forgive a small joke, but not a careless hand near a moving valve. Lin agreed. She asked the group to explain the next step before anyone moved. The team checked the manual, placed a mark beside the lid, and left the machine alone for the day.

琳畫下管子的圖，並給圖一個清楚標題。衛兵想立刻找到解方，但琳說測試結果只能回答原本設計要問的問題：它顯示壓力，不代表已知全部原因。陶扮了個傻臉想緩和氣氛。一位工人笑著說，他能原諒小玩笑，卻不能原諒亂碰移動閥門的手。琳同意，請全組先解釋下一步才准動作。大家核對手冊，在蓋子旁做記號，當天便不再碰機器。

In the archive, Lin compared the new drawing with the original plan. The old valve had a symbol shaped like a moon, while the newer page used a sun. That change was not explained. The improvement claimed in the margin had no test data beside it. Tao wanted to put the pictures on the public board. Lin agreed only after they added the words “not yet checked.” A useful report must show the limit of what people know. The next question was whether the new valve had been installed upside down or whether the tube had been connected to a different air path.

在檔案室，琳把新圖與原始計畫對照。舊閥上是月亮形的符號，新頁卻改成太陽，沒有任何解釋。頁邊聲稱的改良也沒有附測試數據。陶想把照片貼上公告板，琳同意，但先加上「尚未核實」。有用的報告必須顯示知識的界線。下一個問題是新閥是否裝反，或細管被接到不同的風路。

The team gathered by the river for supper, where a cook served delicious rice and vegetables. The guard had been quiet all day. At last he said his father had helped write the newer manual, and the family had always been proud of it. Lin did not say the father had lied. She asked whether the guard would help search for the notes behind the revision. He nodded slowly. “If the change was good, the notes will show why. If it was bad, we should learn that too.” His words mattered because he was willing to question something he loved.

小隊到河邊吃晚餐，廚師準備了好吃的米飯與蔬菜。衛兵整天很安靜，終於說父親曾協助編寫新版手冊，全家一直以此為榮。琳沒有說他父親撒謊，而是問他願不願意協助找出修訂的背景筆記。衛兵慢慢點頭：「如果改得好，筆記會說明原因；如果不好，我們也該知道。」他的話重要，因為他願意質疑自己珍視的事物。

Near the lake, a guide told them about an old shark drawing painted on a boat after a fishing trip. The animal had never lived in this mountain water; it was a joke from a distant sea journey. Lin used the story to explain why the character of a symbol depended on its place and history. A moon on a valve could be a model number, not a sign of magic. She tried to express the idea in clear language for the guard: “Read the mark with the page that names it.” The guard repeated the sentence and wrote it beside his keys. He said he would check the model before trusting a label again. Their next test would need the same care.

湖邊一位嚮導說起，從前有人出海捕魚後在船上畫了鯊魚，那種動物從未住在山湖裡，圖只是遠航歸來的玩笑。琳借此解釋，符號的性質取決於出現的位置與歷史。閥上的月亮可能是型號，不是魔法標誌。她試著用簡明的話向衛兵表達：「讀記號時，要連同命名它的頁面一起看。」衛兵複述一次，寫在鑰匙旁。他說日後再相信標籤前，一定先核對機型。下一次測試也需要同樣細心。

Before leaving the yard, Tao found a second tube connected to an unused exit vent. He did not open it. He drew its position and asked the guard to seal the outer door. Lin wrote that they had found a possible path for moving air, not a path for moving people. The distinction felt small, but she knew that errors began with words. That night the bell rang only once. Some neighbors called it proof that the machine was fixed. Lin told them the test had changed nothing inside the engine. Still, she slept with a little more hope. They had seen the problem rather than merely heard about it.

離開外院前，陶發現第二根管子接到一個停用的出口通風孔。他沒有打開，只畫下位置，請衛兵封好外門。琳寫下：他們找到的是可能的空氣通路，不是人可通行的道路。這區別很小，她卻知道錯誤常始於用詞。當晚鐘只響一次。有些鄰居說這證明機器修好了。琳告訴他們測試並未改變引擎內部；但她仍帶著更多希望入睡。至少他們已親眼看見問題，而非只聽傳說。
