The Drawing at an Angle

鐘聲之外，還有一行字 · 第 2 / 25 章 · 1000–2000 · 英中對照

On the second morning, Tao found a thin board behind the station desk. It carried a drawing of the old line, but its north arrow pointed toward the floor. Lin tilted the board to the proper angle and saw a narrow path beside the main tunnel. The path did not appear on the town map. A barber from the lower street had once cut the hair of the old station chief. He remembered a lamp being carried down that path every winter. “I appreciate your question,” he told Lin, “but please do not repeat my name until you know what is safe.” Lin promised to avoid making him a public witness without his consent.

第二天早晨，陶在車站桌子後找到一塊薄板，上面畫著舊線路，北方箭頭卻指向地板。琳將板子轉到正確角度，才看見主隧道旁的一條窄道。鎮上的地圖沒有那條路。下街的理髮師以前替老站長剪過頭髮，記得每到冬天就有人沿那條路搬燈。「謝謝妳問起，」他對琳說，「但在確認安全以前，請別說出我的名字。」琳答應，未經他的同意，不會讓他成為公開證人。

The route led past the forest, where a deer sometimes crossed and a white dove nested above the gate. Their first search ended at a huge stone wall with a single lantern hanging from a hook. The wall was rough, but one spot near the floor was smooth from years of hands touching it. Tao felt for a door and found nothing. Lin noticed a number carved below the lantern. It matched a number in the newest edition of the manual but not in the older one. She wondered why a small change had been made to a part of the station that officials claimed did not exist.

那條路經過森林，偶爾有鹿走過，白鴿也在門上築巢。他們第一次搜索停在一面巨大的石牆前，牆上鉤子掛著一盞提燈。石牆粗糙，但靠近地板的一小塊因長年被手觸摸而磨得光滑。陶摸索門板，卻一無所獲。琳注意到提燈下刻著一個號碼，與新版手冊相同，卻不見於舊版。她不懂，既然官員說這地方不存在，為何有人要修改這裡的編號。

Back in town, an organization for mountain workers held its weekly meeting. Its president, Mrs. Vale, invited Lin to show the two pages. The workers included a minority group from the west slope whose homes had lost water first. One member described a painful walk uphill with heavy jars. He had brought sour apples for lunch, but he pushed them aside to listen. Mrs. Vale asked whether Lin knew the truth about the closed wall. “Not yet,” Lin answered. “I know there are two different drawings. I also know someone changed the number. That is all the evidence we have today.”

回到鎮上，山地工人協會正舉行每週會議。會長維爾太太邀請琳展示兩頁不同的圖。工人中有來自西坡的少數族群，他們家最早停水。一名成員描述背著沉重水罐爬坡的痛苦。他帶了酸蘋果當午餐，卻先放到一旁聽講。維爾太太問琳是否知道封牆後面的真相。「還不知道，」琳回答。「我只知道兩份圖不一樣，也知道有人改了號碼。今天的證據只有這些。」

A school teacher offered a textbook about old mountain machines. It showed how tunnels carried both goods and air. The picture was simple enough for any child to understand. Lin used it to explain that a small passage might be a safe route for workers, not a secret road for thieves. Tao wanted to break the wall at once. Lin refused. There might be pressure behind it, and the manual warned against opening unknown doors. They needed to learn why the passage had been closed before entering it. The promise was hard to keep while the town waited for water, but a fast guess could hurt people.

一名學校老師拿來一本介紹舊式山地機械的教科書，裡面畫著隧道如何同時運送貨物與空氣。圖簡單得連孩子都能理解。琳用它說明，小通道可能是工人的安全道路，不一定是竊賊的祕道。陶想立刻敲破石牆，琳拒絕了。牆後可能有壓力，手冊也警告不可隨意開啟未知的門。在進去之前，他們需要弄懂封閉的原因。鎮民等水等得焦急，遵守承諾並不容易，但魯莽的猜測可能傷人。

When they returned to the wall, the particular scratch beside the number looked different in the afternoon light. Lin asked Tao not to touch it. She held the old photograph next to the real stone and noticed that the painted cover sat a little higher than it had before. Someone had moved it during a repair. The detail was too small to tell them why, but it gave them a place to begin. She drew it twice, once from the front and once from the side, so that an expert could check the angle later. The barber had trusted them with an old memory. Lin wanted to return that trust with work that was careful enough to last.

他們回到石牆時，號碼旁那道特殊刮痕在午後光線下看起來不同。琳請陶不要碰。她把老照片放到真實石牆旁，發現漆過的蓋子比以前高了一點，顯然曾在某次維修被移動。這細節還不能解釋原因，卻給了他們調查起點。她從正面、側面各畫一次，好讓專家日後檢查角度。理髮師把珍貴回憶交給他們，琳想用經得起時間考驗的謹慎工作回報這份信任。

That evening the barber sent a message. His grandfather had worked at the station and left a box of papers, including a picture of the lantern wall. Lin went to his shop with Tao and opened the box only after asking permission. The picture showed a small metal cover beside the number. Someone had painted over it. The cover did not show in the latest manual. Lin asked whether she could copy the picture for the workers’ meeting. The barber agreed on the condition that his grandfather’s name would stay in the archive, not on a town notice. Lin thanked him for helping while keeping his own choice.

傍晚，理髮師傳來訊息。他祖父曾在車站工作，留下了一箱文件，其中有提燈石牆的照片。琳與陶到店裡，徵得同意才打開箱子。照片上，號碼旁有一小片金屬蓋，後來被人塗漆遮住。最新版手冊也沒畫出它。琳詢問可否把照片複製給工人會議使用。理髮師同意，條件是祖父姓名只留在檔案中，不貼上鎮公告。琳感謝他的幫助，也尊重他的選擇。
