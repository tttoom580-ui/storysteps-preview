The Bell That Rang Twice

鐘聲之外，還有一行字 · 第 1 / 25 章 · 1000–2000 · 英中對照

用互動版查詞、朗讀與收藏

At the edge of Cloudstep, the mountain town, a bell rang twice before sunrise. It was meant to ring only once. Lin, a twenty-year-old map keeper, woke to a windy morning and watched a red light move across the old station from her balcony. Below, a small field lay beside the stone base of a public seesaw. Children had stopped playing. The town operator hurried past them at unusual speed, carrying a sealed case. Lin knew the eastern lift had broken three nights before. Now the upper houses had no clean water, and the mayor had ordered everyone to keep away from the station.

山城雲階的邊緣，天亮前鐘聲響了兩次。照理它只該響一次。二十歲的地圖保管員琳在多風的清晨醒來，從陽臺看見一盞紅燈沿著舊車站移動。下方的小田地旁，石頭底座上的翹翹板已沒有人玩。孩子們停下遊戲。一名操作員提著封好的箱子，以異常速度從他們身邊跑過。琳知道東側升降機三晚前壞了，如今高處住戶沒有乾淨的水，鎮長也命令所有人遠離車站。

Lin went to the archive room, where she helped her aunt keep maps and letters. Her aunt had a strong belief in careful work. “A claim is not better because a powerful person says it,” she often told Lin. On the desk lay an old manual for the underground goods line. Travelers called it a subway, although it had never carried regular city passengers. The first page showed how to approach a broken gate without touching its main wheel. At the bottom, in small writing, someone had added: “If the bell rings twice, check the secondary line before starting the engine.” Lin read the sentence again.

琳走進檔案室，她平常在此幫阿姨保存地圖與書信。阿姨深信做事要仔細。「有權的人說了，不會讓主張變得更正確，」她常這樣告訴琳。桌上放著舊地下貨運線的操作手冊。旅人稱它為地下鐵，雖然它從未定期載送市區乘客。第一頁畫著如何靠近故障閘門而不碰主輪。底下的小字寫著：「若鐘響兩次，啟動引擎前先查第二線路。」琳又讀了一遍。

Her friend Tao arrived with bread and a worried face. He was a male apprentice at the station, not a trained operator yet. “The mayor says the problem is the weather,” he said. Lin did not deny that storms could damage machines. But their challenge was to find which part had failed. For instance, a loose signal wire could turn on a warning even when the main lift still worked. They began to discuss a simple test and planned to develop a record of each noise, light, and movement. The station workers called such notes a waste of time. Lin thought a record might save them from guessing.

朋友陶帶著麵包和憂心的神情來了。他是車站的男性學徒，還不是合格操作員。「鎮長說是天氣造成的，」他說。琳並不否認風暴可能損壞機器，但他們眼前的挑戰，是找出哪個部分真正出了問題。例如，訊號線鬆脫就可能打開警示燈，即使主升降機仍可運作。他們開始討論簡單的測試，打算建立聲音、燈光與動作的紀錄。車站工人說這只是在浪費時間；琳卻認為，紀錄能讓大家不用猜。

At noon they visited the public hall. Above its door hung a picture of the nation’s first mountain railway. The picture honored a tradition of sharing transport between rich and poor areas. But the current rules gave all repair time to the upper district, where the mayor lived. A teacher told Lin that younger people should not question old decisions. Lin answered that the town was not asking her to rule the nation. It was asking her to read one page. Tao held up the manual so others could see the line about the second bell. A few people leaned forward, curious rather than angry.

中午，他們到了公會堂。門上掛著一幅國家第一條山地鐵道的畫，紀念富區和窮區共用交通工具的傳統。但現行規則把所有維修時間都留給鎮長所住的高區。一名老師告訴琳，年輕人不該質疑舊決定。琳回答，鎮民不是要她治理國家，而是要她讀懂一頁紙。陶舉起手冊，讓大家看見關於第二次鐘聲的那一行。一些人向前靠近，神情好奇而非憤怒。

The mayor agreed to one small inspection, but only if the old lock remained closed. Lin accepted the limit. She and Tao stood outside the eastern gate as the bell rang again. A green light appeared first. Then a red light shone from the other side of the hill. It was not the pattern shown on the warning board. Lin wrote the two colors in order and drew the distance between them. She could not yet say what the signal meant. She could say that it was not random. By evening she had a question worth more than a rumor: who had moved the second signal lamp?

鎮長同意進行一次小規模檢查，但條件是舊鎖不得打開。琳接受了限制。她與陶站在東門外，鐘聲再次響起。先亮起的是綠燈，接著山丘另一側發出紅光。那與警示板上的圖樣不同。琳依序記下兩種顏色，也畫出它們的距離。她還不知道訊號的意義，但可以確定它不是隨機的。到了傍晚，她已找到一個比傳言更有價值的問題：究竟是誰移動了第二盞訊號燈？

Before leaving, Lin placed a copy of the page inside a dry box in the archive. Tao wanted to carry the whole book to the station. She refused, gently but firmly. If they lost the only manual, no one could repeat their work. They would bring copies and keep the original safe. From the top of the hill, the town looked peaceful under the night lamps. Yet half its windows were dark because the water pumps had stopped. Lin promised neither a quick repair nor a wonderful discovery. She promised to return the next day, ask the next question, and let anyone check her notes.

離開前，琳把那一頁的副本放進檔案室的乾燥盒。陶想把整本手冊帶到車站，她溫和卻堅定地拒絕。要是弄丟唯一的手冊，就沒人能重做他們的工作。他們會攜帶副本，保管好原件。從山頂望去，鎮上夜燈下十分寧靜，卻有半數窗戶因抽水機停擺而暗著。琳沒有保證能快速修復，也沒承諾奇蹟般的發現。她只答應隔天再來，問下一個問題，並讓所有人都能檢查她的筆記。
