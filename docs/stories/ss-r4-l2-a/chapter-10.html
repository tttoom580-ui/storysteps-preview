谷地的紅旗

The Red Flag in the Valley

A red flag appeared over the valley on the tenth day. Its place on the warning track meant the upper tank was almost full. Lin knew the distance between the tank and the lower street was great, so any sudden flow could be dangerous. The workers stayed active, checking one small part at a time. Tao rode his cycle to the lower gate and found a notebook tied to the post. It belonged to a former station worker who had written down every signal. One line indicated that the red flag was not a call to turn the main wheel. It was a warning to stop water entering the tank.

第十天，谷地上方升起紅旗。依警示軌道上的位置，它表示上方水槽幾乎已滿。琳知道水槽與下街距離很遠，任何突然的大水流都可能危險。工人們持續活動，一次只檢查一小部分。陶騎腳踏車到下門，發現一冊筆記本綁在柱上，是前站員記錄每個訊號的本子。其中一行指出，紅旗不是要求轉動主輪，而是警告停止讓水流進水槽。

The mayor’s clerk arrived in a flat cart and asked why the group had not gone forward with the repair. Lin showed him the note. He said the law required a trained leader to be present. She agreed, then asked him to name one. The clerk was silent. “Our concern is safety,” she said, “not a chance to gain power.” The clerk looked at the figures and admitted the tank might be under pressure. He promised to contact an engineer but would not sign anything yet. Lin wrote down his words and the time. A useful promise needed a date when it could be checked.

鎮長書記坐著平板車來，質問小隊為何不繼續修。琳讓他看那段筆記。他說法律要求合格主管在場，琳同意，接著請他指定一位。書記沉默了。「我們關心的是安全，」她說，「不是爭取權力的機會。」書記看了數字，承認水槽可能承受壓力。他答應聯絡工程師，但暫時不願簽字。琳記下他的話與時間。真正有用的承諾，必須有可以核對的期限。

In the valley, a farmer found a wallet near the warning post. It held a single photograph and the address of an old worker who had left town. There was no money. Tao handed it to the guard rather than opening the private letter inside. The guard read only the address needed to return it. Lin wondered whether the worker might know why the signals had changed. She would send a request through the post rather than follow him without permission. The guard thanked them for respecting the owner. A small act of care strengthened the trust they would need for more difficult questions.

谷地裡，一名農夫在警示柱旁找到錢包，裡面有一張照片與離鎮老工人的地址，沒有現金。陶把錢包交給衛兵，而不是打開裡面的私人信件。衛兵只讀取寄回所需的地址。琳想，也許那位老工人知道訊號為何改變。她決定寫信詢問，不會未經同意就追訪。衛兵感謝他們尊重失主。這小小的細心，增加了他們日後面對難題時需要的信任。

That evening Lin and Tao watched workers place stones along the track to keep the path clear. They did not have the riches of the upper district, nor did they have expensive tools. They had a notebook, permission to test, and people willing to help. Lin asked the guard to explain why the old manual described two kinds of warning flag. He said one was for the tank and one for the engine. The same red color could indicate different risks depending on its location. Lin added this to the public notice so nobody would confuse the two again.

傍晚，琳與陶看工人沿軌道放石塊，保持道路暢通。他們沒有高區的財富，也沒有昂貴工具，只有筆記本、測試許可與願意幫忙的人。琳請衛兵解釋，為什麼舊手冊說有兩種警示旗。他說一種對應水槽，一種對應引擎。同樣的紅色，因位置不同而代表不同風險。琳把這點加上公告，避免大家再次混淆。

At the station desk, an old form asked for each worker’s name, age, and sex. Lin asked whether the last item was needed for a pressure test. Mara said it was not, so they chose a new form that recorded training and assigned duties instead. The change suited the work better and protected private details. There was a short silence as the team compared the forms. Then a young clerk asked whether an emergency could occur without anyone hearing the bell. Mara said yes: a lamp or a pressure mark could also warn them. Lin added those signs to the new sheet. The team had been learning that safety came from several clear checks, not from a single loud sound.

車站桌上的舊表格要求填姓名、年齡與性別。琳問壓力測試是否真的需要最後一項。瑪拉說不需要，所以他們改用記錄訓練和分工的新表。這改動更適合工作，也保護私人資訊。小隊比較兩份表時短暫沉默。接著年輕書記問，若沒人聽見鐘聲，也會發生緊急情況嗎？瑪拉說會，燈光或壓力刻線也可能示警。琳把這些訊號寫進新表。大家逐漸學到，安全來自多項清楚檢查，不是只靠一聲大響。

The new form also had to suit the workers who had little time to read. An suggested a picture beside each safety step. Lin tested the design with the guard, who could follow it quickly without skipping a question.

新表格也得適合閱讀時間不多的工人。安提議在每個安全步驟旁加圖。琳請衛兵試讀，他能迅速照做，也沒有漏掉任何問題。

By nightfall the mayor sent an engineer to review the tank. She confirmed that no water should be released until a pressure test was complete. Lin felt both relief and worry. Their work had prevented a dangerous action, but the full system still stood still. Tao asked what they had gained after ten days of questions. Lin showed him the clean tap on the lower street, the signed survey permit, and the new warning chart. They had not won a grand prize. They had given people a safer week and a clearer reason for the next step. The bell rang once across the quiet valley.

天黑前，鎮長派工程師檢查水槽。她確認，在壓力測試完成前不能放水。琳同時鬆了口氣又憂心：他們阻止了危險動作，整個系統卻仍停擺。陶問，十天來問這麼多問題究竟得到什麼。琳指給他看下街的乾淨水龍頭、簽好的調查許可與新警示圖。他們沒贏到大獎，卻讓居民過了更安全的一週，也讓下一步更有根據。安靜谷地上，鐘聲響了一次。
