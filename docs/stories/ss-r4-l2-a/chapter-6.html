湖岸會議

The Meeting by the Shore

Lin received a letter inviting her to a public meeting about the station affair. It would be held beside the shore, where the town usually played badminton on weekends. The mayor wanted to end the argument quickly. He called the broken lift an unexpected crisis and said private experts should decide everything. Lin brought the manual, her journal, and the dates of the missing inspections. Mrs. Vale brought cabbage soup for the workers. “A meeting needs human voices as well as numbers,” she said. The words reminded Lin that people who carried water every day understood the problem in ways no office could measure.

琳收到一封邀請函，要她參加關於車站事件的公開會議。地點在湖岸，鎮民週末常到那裡打羽球。鎮長想盡快終止爭論，稱升降機故障是意外危機，應由私人專家全權決定。琳帶著手冊、自己的日誌與缺失檢查的日期。維爾太太為工人帶來高麗菜湯。「會議需要人聲，也需要數字，」她說。琳想起，每天親自提水的人理解問題的方式，是辦公室裡的數字無法完全衡量的。

The mayor opened with a long speech about the culture of Cloudstep. He said the council had governed the town for many years. Lin respected that history, but she asked one question: who had decided to remove the air test from the instruction page? The clerk looked at his papers and then stared ahead. The mayor said the change was a clever way to save time. Tao pointed out that the old engine could not work without a clear air path. A fireman who had helped in an earlier flood agreed. He had seen how pressure built inside a closed tube. Nobody in the meeting could name the person who had approved the change.

鎮長以長篇演說開場，談雲階的文化與議會多年治理的歷史。琳尊重那段過去，但她只問一個問題：誰決定刪掉說明頁上的風道測試？書記看了看文件，又直視前方。鎮長說那是節省時間的聰明辦法。陶指出舊引擎沒有通暢風道就無法運作。一位曾參加救洪的消防員同意，因為他看過封閉管道內壓力如何升高。會場上卻無人能說出核准修改的人是誰。

A neighbor from the west slope asked whether ordinary people could participate in the repair decisions. The mayor said individual voices would make the process slow. Mrs. Vale answered that a town was made of individuals, not just a single desk. Lin put forth a simple plan: publish the test steps, allow trained workers to check them, and let residents report what they saw. She did not ask everyone to touch the machine. Some tasks required special knowledge, and safety came first. She asked only that explanations be open and that nobody be punished for asking a fair question.

一位西坡鄰居問，一般居民能否參與維修決策。鎮長說個人意見只會拖慢流程。維爾太太回答，城鎮由一個個人組成，不是一張桌子。琳提出簡單方案：公布測試步驟，讓受訓工人檢查，居民則回報觀察結果。她並沒有要求人人碰機器。有些工作需要專業知識，安全最重要。她只要求說明公開，任何提出合理問題的人都不該受罰。

Near the end, a child in clean socks sat on a pillow and listened while her mother spoke. The woman described carrying water up the steps after night work. She did not want the mayor to resign or the station to close. She wanted a clean tap near home and a safe journey to school for her daughter. The room grew quiet. Lin understood that her search was no longer about winning an argument. If the town opened its records and did nothing else, the child would still carry a heavy bucket. They needed a repair plan that worked in daily life.

會議快結束時，一個穿乾淨襪子的孩子坐在枕頭上，聽媽媽發言。母親描述自己上完夜班還得沿石階提水。她不要求鎮長下台，也不想車站關閉；她只想住家附近有乾淨水龍頭，女兒上學能平安。全場安靜下來。琳懂了，這不再只是一場爭辯。即使鎮民拿到公開紀錄，卻沒有其他行動，孩子仍得提重桶。他們需要真正能融入日常生活的修復方案。

The meeting gave Tao real excitement, but it also raised a difficult question about who had the right to govern the station during an emergency. Mrs. Vale explained that the council could set public goals while trained workers made the dangerous technical choices. She drew two circles on the board and showed where they overlapped. The mayor had often treated both jobs as his alone. Lin liked the drawing because it made room for responsibility without giving away safety. She asked that the meeting record the difference clearly. When someone later said the workers wanted to take over the government, the minutes would show exactly what they had requested: shared oversight, not a new ruler.

會議讓陶非常興奮，也提出一個難題：緊急時誰有權管理車站？維爾太太說，議會可制定公共目標，危險的技術判斷則交由受訓工人。她在板上畫兩個圈，標出重疊的地方。鎮長過去常把兩份職責都視為自己所有。琳喜歡這張圖，因為它既讓責任分明，又不犧牲安全。她要求把區別清楚記入會議紀錄。日後若有人說工人想奪權，紀錄就能顯示真正的要求是共同監督，而不是換一個統治者。

The council voted to allow a limited survey of the old passage with the safety guard present. The mayor warned that breaking the rules could lead to prison. Lin accepted that warning as another reason to work carefully. When the meeting ended, Tao felt excited but uneasy. He wished they could move faster. Lin handed him their signed permission and said, “This is a real step. We did not take it from anyone. They gave it after hearing the people who must live with the result.” Over the lake the evening light became gold. Tomorrow they could inspect the covered wall without entering it.

議會投票同意，在安全衛兵陪同下有限度調查舊通道。鎮長警告，違規可能坐牢。琳把這句警告當成必須更加小心的理由。散會時陶既興奮又不安，想更快推進。琳把簽好的許可交給他說：「這是真正的一步。我們沒有從誰手上搶來，是大家聽過直接受影響的居民後給我們的。」湖上晚光變成金色。明天他們就能在不進入通道的情況下檢查被蓋住的牆面。
