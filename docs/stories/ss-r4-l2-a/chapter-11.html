工程師的第一堂課

The Engineer’s First Lesson

The engineer sent from the capital was named Mara. She had once been a beginner at the very station she was asked to inspect. She told Lin and Tao that the first goal was not to turn the engine on but to learn where the pressure could go. A charge of stored air might look harmless until a valve opened. She used a mug of water, an iron spoon, and three grapes to make a small model on the table. Tao wanted to chase an answer through every tunnel at once. Mara asked him to slow down. “The importance of a test is that you can say what it proved,” she said.

從首都派來的工程師名叫瑪拉。她當年也曾是這座車站的新手。她告訴琳和陶，第一個目標不是啟動引擎，而是弄清壓力能往哪裡走。儲存的空氣壓力在閥門打開前看似無害。她拿杯水、鐵湯匙和三顆葡萄，在桌上做出小模型。陶想一次追查所有隧道，瑪拉請他慢下來。「測試重要，是因為你能說清楚它證明了什麼，」她說。

Mara asked the team to emphasize the old rule about opening the air path first. Then she showed them a valve that had become stuck with jam from a market wagon. A child had once spilled sweet food through an open service window. That tiny accident did not explain the large failure, but it proved that ordinary care mattered. Lin cleaned the outer cover with permission and found a gold mark beneath the dirt. The mark matched the original moon symbol. “The metal part is old,” Mara said. “The printed page was changed later.” Lin wrote both observations without adding an answer they could not yet support.

瑪拉請小隊特別強調先開風路的舊規則，接著讓他們看一個被果醬卡住的閥門。從前有孩子把甜食打翻，從打開的維修窗口流了進去。這小意外不能解釋大故障，卻證明日常照料很重要。琳得到許可後清潔外蓋，發現污垢下有金色記號，正好與原本的月亮符號相同。「金屬零件是舊的，」瑪拉說，「印刷頁是後來改的。」琳把兩項觀察都寫下，沒加上無法證實的答案。

The station had once been a social meeting place for people of many different beliefs. In a quiet room, workers had kept objects connected with different forms of religion. Mara asked them not to disturb those things. The room now held a wooden trap for mice and a damaged signal board. On the board, a button was marked “shoot test light,” meaning that a beam would travel to a mirror across the room. Tao wondered if it was safe. Mara simply covered the beam with a screen and tested it at the lowest setting. A small spot appeared where the manual said it should.

車站從前是不同信仰居民的社交場所。安靜小室裡保存著與多種宗教有關的物件，瑪拉請大家不要隨意碰觸。房內如今放了捕鼠器與損壞的訊號板。按鈕寫著「發射測試光」，意思是讓光束射到房間另一側鏡子。陶問是否安全。瑪拉只用遮板擋住光線，以最低設定測試。小光點出現在手冊預期的位置。

The test was a small success. It showed that the warning lamps could work when powered from the correct board. Lin felt trust growing between the guard and the engineers. Trust did not mean never checking a friend; it meant giving a person a fair chance to explain their work. Mara wrote a true account on the public board: the test passed, but the main valve had not been opened. She said this was the usual way a repair should be recorded. If the next crew arrived tomorrow, it would not need to guess what had been done today.

這次測試得到小小成功：警示燈只要接上正確控制板，就能運作。琳感到衛兵與工程師之間的信任正在增加。信任不是永遠不檢查朋友，而是公平地讓人說明工作。瑪拉在公開板寫下真實紀錄：燈光測試通過，但主閥尚未打開。她說維修通常就該這樣記載。下一班工人隔天到來時，便不必猜前一天做過什麼。

Mara asked the team to mark the test objects with their proper names. The grape on the table was not a valve, the mug was not a real tank, and the spoon was not a power switch. Those differences seemed obvious until Tao tried to explain the model to a child without using a picture. He found himself saying “this thing” again and again. Lin laughed kindly and helped him write a short guide. By the end, the child could point to each part and say what it stood for. Mara called that a real achievement. Knowing something yourself was useful; helping another person understand it made the knowledge last.

瑪拉請小隊為測試物件標上正確名稱。桌上的葡萄不是閥門，杯子不是真水槽，湯匙也不是電源開關。這些差別看似明顯，直到陶試著不看圖向孩子說明模型時，才發現自己一直講「這個東西」。琳善意地笑了，幫他寫一份短指引。最後孩子能指著每個部分，說出它代表什麼。瑪拉稱讚這是真成就。自己懂一件事很有用，能讓另一個人也懂，知識才能留得更久。

On the way home Tao asked why the mayor had left the lights broken for so long when the test was so simple. Lin reminded him that a passing light test and a full repair were different things. Someone might have hidden a mistake, but someone else might simply have believed an unclear page. They needed the old revision notes before drawing that line. Mara promised to help find them. Lin had gained an expert who could explain the machine, yet the question of who changed the book was still her responsibility to ask. She slept with the manual closed and her questions clearly marked.

回家的路上，陶問：既然燈光測試這麼簡單，鎮長為什麼讓它壞這麼久？琳提醒他，燈光測試通過與整台機器修好是兩回事。有人可能藏了錯誤，也可能只是相信一頁不清楚的說明。在劃下結論前，他們需要舊修訂筆記。瑪拉答應幫忙找。琳得到一位能解釋機器的專家，但究竟誰改了手冊，仍需要她自己追問。那晚她闔上手冊，將疑問清清楚楚標記好。
