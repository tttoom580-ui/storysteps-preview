空白的一行

The Blank Line

鐘聲之外，還有一行字 · 第 12 / 25 章 · 1000–2000 · 英中對照 · 校訂中

Mara helped Lin send a formal request to the capital for the missing revision file. The office replied with a blank page and a note saying no copy was available. Tao wanted to blame the mayor at once. Lin said they should first find out why the page was empty. A customer at the post office offered a clue: an old clerk had moved from the capital to a farm in the countryside. He used to write every change in blue ink. If a page had been removed, he might remember what had stood there. Lin asked for his public work address, not his private home.

瑪拉幫琳向首都正式申請失蹤的修訂檔。辦公室回來一張空白頁，註明無副本可提供。陶立刻想責怪鎮長。琳說應先查清那頁為何空白。郵局的一位顧客提供線索：有名老書記從首都搬到鄉下農場。他以前用藍墨水記錄每次改動。若頁面被抽掉，他或許記得內容。琳索取的是公開工作地址，而非私人住址。

The journey took them past a dentist’s office and a small roadside shop. They bought fries and pudding because the train would not stop for lunch. Tao wore old slippers until Lin reminded him that rocky roads required proper shoes. A sudden rain made the path difficult. They had to choose the right direction at every fork, and they hardly spoke until the clouds lifted. Lin was scared of losing the folder to the water, so she wrapped it in cloth. She was more afraid of making a public claim on weak evidence than of arriving late. Every careful mile kept their notes safe.

旅程經過牙醫診所與路邊小店。他們買了薯條和布丁，因為火車不會停下吃午餐。陶原本穿舊拖鞋，琳提醒山路得穿牢固鞋子。突然一場雨讓路更難走。每到岔路口都要選對方向，雲散之前兩人幾乎沒說話。琳怕資料夾被水弄濕，便用布包好。她更怕的是以薄弱證據公開指控，而非遲到。每一段謹慎走過的路，都讓筆記安全無虞。

They found the retired clerk in a small workshop. He measured the length of wooden boards for neighbors and kept a teapot warm by the window. At first he regarded Lin with suspicion. He had been asked to hide old papers before, and the memory still hurt. Lin showed him the official request and explained exactly what she hoped to learn. She promised to respect his choice if he did not wish to speak. After a long pause he brought out a box and unfolded a receipt for the missing page. It had been sent back to Cloudstep for a settlement of printing costs.

他們在一間小工坊找到退休書記。他正替鄰居量木板長度，窗邊暖著一只茶壺。起初他懷疑地看著琳。他曾被要求藏起舊文件，那段記憶仍令他難受。琳出示正式請求，明確說出想知道什麼，並承諾若他不願說也會尊重。他沉默很久，才拿出箱子，展開那張失蹤頁的收據：文件已寄回雲階，用於結清印刷費用。

The old clerk offered to show them how he checked a measurement on a shipping form. He placed two rulers beside the paper and pointed out that a small difference could become a real danger when people built a large machine. Lin was surprised by the care in his hands. She had thought of him only as a man who carried old files, not as someone who understood the work behind them. He asked her to regard each line as a promise to the next reader. Then he opened a drawer and showed them a copy of a request for a correction. It carried the name of the printing company but had been returned without a decision. Tao asked whether the old clerk had kept it out of anger. The man said he had kept it because he did not want to forget what had happened. Lin understood. A record could honor a person’s memory without deciding blame before the facts were checked. They left the workshop with a new copy and a clearer question for the company.

老書記提議示範，自己從前如何核對貨運表上的測量數字。他把兩把尺放在紙旁，指出小小差異在建造大機器時可能成為真正危險。琳驚訝於他手上的細心。她原本只把他當成搬運舊文件的人，沒想到他懂文件背後的工作。他請琳把每一行視為對下一位讀者的承諾。接著他拉開抽屜，拿出一份要求更正的申請副本。上面寫著印刷公司名稱，卻被退回、未作決定。陶問他是否因為生氣才保存。老人說，他只是不要忘記發生過的事。琳懂了：紀錄可以尊重人的記憶，而不必在事實核對前先決定誰有錯。他們帶著新副本離開工坊，也帶著更清楚的問題回去找公司。

Before leaving, Lin checked the clerk’s copy against the receipt one last time. The dates agreed. She asked him to keep the original dry and safe, so the same question could be answered again if needed.

離開前，琳最後一次把老書記的副本與收據核對，日期相符。她請他妥善保持原件乾燥、安全，日後若有人再問，同樣的問題便能重新核查。

The receipt did not name the person who changed the page. It did name the company that paid for the revised manual. Lin recognized the name from the double payment records. The old clerk let her copy the receipt and signed the date. He asked that they explain the limits of what it showed. Lin agreed. He had not seen the final print, and he could not swear which words had been taken out. But he could prove where the missing file had been sent. Outside, Tao smiled for the first time that day. Their search had found a direction without taking away anyone’s dignity.

收據沒有寫出改頁者，但寫了支付新版手冊費用的公司。琳認出那名字與雙倍付款紀錄相同。老書記准她複製收據並簽上日期，也請她說清這張收據的證明範圍。琳同意。他沒看過最後的印刷版本，不能保證哪個字被刪；但他能證明失蹤文件送到何處。走出工坊時，陶那天第一次笑了。他們找到調查方向，也沒有傷害任何人的尊嚴。
