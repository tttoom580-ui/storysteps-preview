寒年的日曆

The Calendar of Cold Years

鐘聲之外，還有一行字 · 第 7 / 25 章 · 1000–2000 · 英中對照 · 校訂中

用互動版查詞、朗讀與收藏

The workers began their survey with a calendar that marked every winter storm for thirty years. It helped Lin see that the climate had changed, but not as the mayor described. The two-bell warning appeared even in a dry period, when no major storm crossed the mountain. Tao wanted to advance straight toward the locked passage. Lin asked him to step backward and look at the dates again. If the warning had been a natural event, its pattern should match the weather. Instead it followed old service days. They decided to introduce the calendar to the workers before making a new guess.

工人們開始調查，先查看一本記錄三十年冬季風暴的日曆。琳看出氣候確實變了，但與鎮長所說不同。雙響警報甚至出現在乾燥時期，那時根本沒有大風暴經過山區。陶想立刻往上鎖通道前進，琳請他退一步重新看日期。如果警報是自然現象，規律應與天氣相符；然而它反而跟舊維修日重合。他們決定先把日曆介紹給工人，再做下一步推測。

The path rose above a rocky desert-like slope, where little grass could grow. An older guide carried a mop, because the inspection floor was always wet near the lower door. He said keeping the place clean was a humble job but an important one. Without a dry floor, workers might slip before reaching the machines. Lin thanked him and recorded his advice as a principle of safety. Tao found a turtle in a shallow pool and moved his foot so he would not disturb it. The guide smiled. “The ideal repair does not leave harm behind just because it makes people happy,” he said.

小路沿著多岩、近似荒漠的山坡往上爬，幾乎長不出草。一位老嚮導帶著拖把，因為下方門口的檢查地板總是濕的。他說清潔工作雖卑微卻重要；地板不乾，工人還沒摸到機器就可能滑倒。琳道謝，將提醒記為安全原則。陶看見淺池裡一隻烏龜，便移開腳步不打擾牠。嚮導笑道：「理想的維修不會只因能讓人高興，就把傷害留給其他生命。」

At the lower door, a metal box contained a strip of paper with the number twelve printed on it. The guide had never noticed it. Lin compared the number with the calendar and found that the light system was checked every twelve days in winter, not every twelve months. The latest manual had left out the word “days.” Tao was surprised that one small word could change the schedule so much. Lin said that progress often depended on details. When people copied a page, they needed to check not only numbers but also the words that explained what those numbers meant.

在下方門口，一個金屬盒裡放著印有「十二」的紙條，嚮導從未留意。琳對照日曆，發現冬天燈光系統應每十二天檢查一次，不是每十二個月；新版手冊漏掉了「天」字。陶很驚訝，一個小字就能大幅改變時程。琳說進步往往取決於細節。抄錄頁面時，人們不只要核對數字，也要檢查說明數字意義的文字。

After the survey, Tao went for a short jog to clear his head. Lin stayed in the archive and read the old records for another period of service. She discovered that the last complete test had happened one day before the mayor’s father took office. That did not prove the family had caused the problem, but it gave them a useful line of inquiry. She drew two columns: facts and possible explanations. Under facts she wrote the old schedule and missing word. Under explanations she wrote only a question mark. It was less exciting than declaring victory, but she knew why it mattered.

調查後，陶去慢跑，讓頭腦清醒。琳留在檔案室，讀另一段維修時期的紀錄。她發現最後一次完整測試發生在鎮長父親上任前一天。這不能證明家族造成故障，卻提供一條有用的調查方向。她畫了兩欄：事實與可能解釋。事實欄寫下舊時程與遺漏的字；解釋欄只寫問號。這沒有宣布勝利那麼刺激，但她明白其重要。

A young worker said the mountain itself was a living being and that no machine should disturb its natural paths. Lin listened with respect. She did not share every part of his belief, but she agreed that a repair should contain the water rather than send it across bare soil. The guide showed a shallow ditch made long ago for that purpose. It was still open, though the newer map did not mark it. The team added it to the survey. If the main tank ever overflowed, the ditch could carry water away from homes. The discovery did not solve the warning problem, but it gave the workers another reason to preserve old knowledge instead of throwing it away.

一名年輕工人說山本身就是有生命的存在，不該讓機器打擾自然的路徑。琳尊重地聽著。她未必同意他的全部信念，卻認為修復應把水收在適當渠道內，而不是任水流過裸露土壤。嚮導指給她看一條很久以前為此挖的淺溝。溝仍暢通，新地圖卻沒標出。小隊把它加入調查。如果主水槽有一天溢出，溝能引水離開住家。這發現沒有解決警報問題，卻讓工人更有理由保存舊知識，而不是丟棄。

That night Lin brushed her teeth with the last of her clean water and put her toothbrush beside the lamp. She had spent the day reading about machines, but now she thought about how the cold days would affect ordinary homes. The town could not wait forever. She wrote to the council asking for safe access to the outer air door under guard. The request included the old schedule and a promise not to move the main wheel. Thus the next survey would test one clear idea, not every possible danger at once. She was not sure the council would agree, but she had given it a fair question to answer.

當晚，琳用剩下的一點乾淨水刷牙，把牙刷放在燈旁。她整天讀機械文件，此刻卻想到寒冷日子會怎樣影響一般家庭。鎮民不能一直等待。她寫信請議會允許在衛兵監督下安全接近外側風門，附上舊時程，並承諾不轉動主輪。因此下一次調查會只測試一個清楚的想法，不會一次冒進所有危險。她不知道議會是否同意，但至少提出了值得認真回答的問題。
