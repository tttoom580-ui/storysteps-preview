Ivy had been thinking about simple things in the art room when a detail refused to fit the old picture. For a moment, everyone around seemed busy with something else. Ivy tried not to rush, because a wrong guess could make the problem harder for someone nearby. The yellow sketchbook was close at hand, ready for the facts that would be easy to forget once people began talking. A little note used “different” as its key word; Ivy kept the note because the rest of the line was still useful. Ivy circled “heart” on a reading sheet, not to win a test but to remember the meaning of the whole sentence.
