A city sign announced that workers would finish the new fence on Friday. The art room had its own familiar rhythm. Ivy knew the sound of footsteps there, yet something about this moment called for a closer look. It was tempting to make a quick story out of a small surprise. Instead, Ivy looked for one thing that could be checked and one person who deserved to be heard. There was room in the yellow sketchbook for questions as well as answers, and today the questions were beginning to matter. In the corner of the yellow sketchbook, Ivy found the word “close” beside an old drawing and stopped to ask what it meant. “laugh,” Ben read from the practice page, then tried to connect the word to something they could actually see. A little note used “yard” as its key word; Ivy kept the note because the rest of the line was still useful.

市府告示宣布工人會在星期五完成新圍欄。美術教室有自己熟悉的節奏。艾薇熟悉那裡的腳步聲，但此刻某件事值得更仔細地看。一個小驚喜很容易讓人立刻編出解釋。艾薇卻先尋找一件可核對的事，以及一位需要被傾聽的人。黃色素描本裡不只可以寫答案，也能記下問題；今天，那些問題開始變得重要。在黃色素描本的角落，艾薇看見一幅舊畫旁寫著「close」，於是停下來詢問意思。「laugh。」班讀出練習頁上的字，接著試著把它和眼前真正看得到的東西連起來。一張小筆記以「yard」為關鍵字；艾薇留下它，因為剩下的句子仍然有用。

The first reaction in the art room was a mixture of surprise and hope. Ivy felt both, and did not pretend otherwise. Pipkin worried the fence would block the last animal path to the pond. The trouble was real, even if the danger had not yet become clear to everyone in the art room. Ivy took a step back to let someone with more experience speak. Help did not always mean being the first person to act. That gave Ben time to notice a detail that had been lost in the noise. Dad joined the conversation after hearing both sides. The questions were simple, but the answers were not. Who would be affected? What could be checked today? A drawn door could open in a second, but a safe path took people, plans, and time. Ivy was beginning to understand the difference. When Ivy returned to the art room, the place was almost the same. The difference was in what Ivy now understood. Ben had helped without taking away the choice, and that made the help easier to accept. For now, they could rest. Tomorrow would ask its own question.
