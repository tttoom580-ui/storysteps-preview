Ben compared old and new maps and found that a small stream had been moved under the road. The art room had its own familiar rhythm. Ivy knew the sound of footsteps there, yet something about this moment called for a closer look. It was tempting to make a quick story out of a small surprise. Instead, Ivy looked for one thing that could be checked and one person who deserved to be heard. There was room in the yellow sketchbook for questions as well as answers, and today the questions were beginning to matter. A little note used “call” as its key word; Ivy kept the note because the rest of the line was still useful. Ivy circled “maybe” on a reading sheet, not to win a test but to remember the meaning of the whole sentence. The word “sentence” appeared in an example someone had written earlier, and Ben asked if the example fit their situation.
