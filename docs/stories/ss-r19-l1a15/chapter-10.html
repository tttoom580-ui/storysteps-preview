Before anyone called the day strange, Ivy had noticed how quiet the art room could become when people were worried. The smallest sounds seemed clear, and the dry smell of paper and paint made the place feel both familiar and new. Rather than act as if every answer was obvious, Ivy opened the yellow sketchbook and began with what had really happened. Ben drew a straight line on the map, then stopped. Real animals could not always follow the neat lines that people loved to draw. In the corner of the yellow sketchbook, Ivy found the word “apple” beside an old drawing and stopped to ask what it meant. “hit,” Ben read from the practice page, then tried to connect the word to something they could actually see. A little note used “pencil” as its key word; Ivy kept the note because the rest of the line was still useful.
