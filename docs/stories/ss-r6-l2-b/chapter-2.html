不見的梨子卡

A Missing Pear Card

寄給明天的兩張明信片 · 第 2 / 25 章 · 1000–2000 · 英中對照

用互動版查詞、朗讀與收藏

The first meeting of the new Slow Post team was held in the library's small room. An wanted a bright display of postcards; Chen wanted a locked box and a clear guide for volunteers. A chain on the old cupboard was broken, so they borrowed a new lock instead of hiding the damage. A student from the chess club said rules could make an activity less friendly. “Only if we forget the people,” An answered. She asked each member to write one message about what made the campus beautiful. Some described flowers, others mentioned quiet corners. Chen wrote, “A place where someone can say no.”

慢遞小組第一次會議在圖書館的小房間舉行。安想設置鮮明的明信片展示牆；澄則要求上鎖的收納箱，以及明確的志工指引。舊櫃子的鎖鏈壞了，他們借來新鎖，沒有掩飾損壞。一位西洋棋社的學生說，規則會讓活動失去親切感。「除非我們忘了規則是為誰而訂，」安回答。她請每位社員寫一句話，描述校園美麗之處。有人寫花，有人寫安靜的角落。澄寫的是：「這是一個可以說不的地方。」

During the count, they noticed a loss. A postcard painted with a green pear was missing. It belonged to a former student who had joined the project years ago. Her membership number was on the envelope, but her address was not. An began to hunt through every shelf. Chen asked her to respond to the problem with a record rather than a guess. Together they checked the current list, then the notes from yesterday. The card had been placed on a table before lunch. Nobody could say who moved it. An feared the worst, but there was no reason yet to accuse anyone.

清點時，他們發現少了一樣東西：一張畫著綠梨子的明信片。卡片屬於多年前參加過活動的一位畢業生。信封上有她的會員編號，卻沒有地址。安開始翻找每一層書架。澄請她用紀錄而不是猜測來處理。他們一起核對現行清單，再查看昨天的筆記。午餐前卡片還放在桌上，但沒人能說出是誰移走了它。安擔心最壞的情況發生，然而現在沒有理由指責任何人。

While they searched for the pear card, An nearly opened a personal drawer belonging to a student worker. Her hand stopped above the handle. The card could be there, but the drawer was not hers. She called the worker and asked permission instead. He checked the drawer himself and found nothing. An felt foolish for losing time, yet Chen said the extra minute had saved them from another problem. A quick search would have been easy. A careful search required them to respect a person even when no one else was watching. An decided she wanted the second kind of team.

找梨子卡時，安差點打開一名工讀生的私人抽屜。她的手停在把手上方。卡片可能就在裡面，但那不是她的抽屜。她改打電話徵詢許可，工讀生自己檢查後，確認裡頭沒有。安覺得浪費時間很傻，澄卻說多花的一分鐘讓他們避免另一個問題。迅速搜查很容易；謹慎搜查則要求他們即使沒人看見，也要尊重當事人。安決定自己要帶領的是後一種團隊。

At the student center, a speaker announced a super busy week of club events. Two volunteers wanted to cancel their shifts. An felt that they were selfish, but Chen reminded her that volunteering was a choice. They could try a short trial period with fewer hours. A guide from the library offered to lend them a cart. An saw the cart's lower drawer was stuck. When she pulled it open, the pear card lay under a folded cloth. The card had not been stolen. It had slipped down when someone cleaned the table. For a long moment An could only laugh at herself.

學生中心的廣播喇叭宣布，接下來一週社團活動特別繁忙。兩名志工想取消排班。安一時覺得他們自私，澄卻提醒她志願服務本來就能自由選擇。他們可以先試行較短的排班。圖書館的導覽員借給他們一台推車。安發現推車最下方的抽屜卡住了，拉開一看，畫著梨子的明信片就躺在一塊摺好的布下。卡片並未遭竊，只是有人整理桌子時滑了下去。安忍不住笑了自己好一陣子。

“The beauty of a good system,” Chen said, “is that it gives us time to find the truth.” An pointed out that a system could also make people feel small. “Then we should change it when it does,” he replied. They moved the display to a public wall but kept all names and private writing out of sight. Their first visitors could read stories about the purpose of slow mail and leave their own blank cards. At the end of the day, the room looked less grand than An's dream. She liked it more. Its quiet growth had begun with a mistake.

「好制度最美的地方，」澄說，「是它讓我們有時間查明真相。」安指出制度也可能讓人覺得渺小。「那就等它有這種問題時改掉，」他回答。他們把展示移到公共牆面，但姓名和私人文字都不公開。第一批訪客能閱讀慢遞的宗旨，也可以留下空白卡片。一天結束時，房間比安夢想的樸素許多，她反而更喜歡。這項計畫的成長，從一個錯誤開始。

When the missing card was safely back, An gave a small talk about her own error. She had first thought of her self-image as an able leader and worried more about looking clever than doing the right thing. Saying that aloud was uncomfortable. A younger volunteer raised a hand and admitted she had made the same mistake in another club. Chen wrote a new sentence on the guide: “Look for the card before you look for a person to blame.” The members discussed it, changed two words, and approved it together. An liked that the guide was becoming theirs, not just hers.

遺失卡片安全回來後，安簡短談起自己的錯誤。她一開始在意自己作為能幹領導人的形象，比起做對的事，更怕看起來不聰明。把這些話說出口並不自在。一位年紀較輕的志工舉手承認，自己也曾在其他社團犯同樣的錯。澄在指引上寫下新句子：「先找卡片，再找該責怪誰。」社員討論、改了兩個字，一起通過。安喜歡指引逐漸屬於大家，而不只是她。

Before closing, An placed the pear card into a marked envelope and wrote its movement in the log. She had learned that looking for a lost object and looking for someone to blame were different tasks. Chen offered a piece of fruit from his lunch bag. It was a pear, of course. She took it and told him that his jokes were getting worse. “But you are smiling,” he answered. “That is not proof,” she said, still smiling. They locked the cupboard together and left the room with a new plan for the next day.

關門前，安把梨子卡放進標明編號的信封，並在紀錄本上登記移動情況。她明白了找回遺失物品與尋找責怪對象是兩回事。澄從午餐袋拿出一顆水果給她，當然又是梨子。安接過來，說他的笑話愈來愈糟。「可是妳在笑，」他回答。「那不算證據，」她仍笑著說。兩人一起鎖上櫃子，帶著隔天的新計畫離開房間。
