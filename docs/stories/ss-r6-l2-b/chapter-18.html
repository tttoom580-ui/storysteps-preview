抽屜裡的蠟燭

A Candle in the Drawer

寄給明天的兩張明信片 · 第 18 / 25 章 · 1000–2000 · 英中對照 · 校訂中

用互動版查詞、朗讀與收藏

A visiting artist arrived with a recent picture of a whale painted on a sheet of thick paper. He wanted to receive an older letter from a folk art group. An checked the names and dates. The artist claimed that the letter belonged to him, but the record pointed to a different person. He took a step backward, surprised. “Then it was my fault to assume,” he said. He had a mature way of accepting the mistake, and An appreciated it. Rather than open the envelope, they called the group to ask how it wished to handle the letter.

一位來訪畫家帶著近來畫在厚紙上的鯨魚圖，想領取民俗藝術團體寄來的舊信。安查了姓名與日期。畫家聲稱信屬於自己，但紀錄指向另一人。他吃驚地退後一步。「那是我擅自以為，錯在我，」他說。他成熟地接受誤會，令安欣賞。兩人沒有拆信，而是聯絡團體，詢問對方希望如何處理。

At lunch, a cockroach ran across the storage room floor. One student shouted and dropped a snack near the door. Another turned on a strong lamp, hoping to drive it away. Chen asked everyone to avoid burning anything in the room. He found an old candle hidden in a drawer and set it aside for safe disposal. The fire rules were clear: no open flames near paper. A worker checked the electric switch and placed a cover over an unused socket. An wrote a short statement of what had happened, because even small problems deserved an honest record.

午餐時，一隻蟑螂跑過儲藏室地板。一名學生驚叫，掉了點心在門邊；另一人打開強光燈想趕走牠。澄請所有人不要在室內燃燒任何東西。他在抽屜裡找到一根舊蠟燭，取出交由人員安全處理。防火規定很清楚：紙張附近不得使用明火。工作人員檢查電源開關，也替閒置插座加裝保護蓋。安簡短記錄了發生的事，因為小問題也值得誠實對待。

The artist's arrival was recorded as a visit, not as a delivery. An wanted the difference to remain clear. She explained that an independent art group had kept its own letters, and the university team was only helping with a question. When someone asked whether the old candle could be used for a stage scene, she said no. It would be unsafe to burn anything near the stored paper. Chen added a short notice to state the safety rules on the wall, making sure each point was easy to read. The artist thanked them for treating a quiet storage room as a place deserving care.

畫家的到來被記成拜訪，而非送達。安希望保留這項區別。她解釋，獨立藝術團體自行保存信件，大學團隊只是協助處理疑問。有人問能否把舊蠟燭拿去舞台上使用，她說不行，在儲存紙張的地方燃燒物品很危險。澄把簡短安全規則寫上牆，確認每一點都清楚易讀。畫家謝謝他們把安靜的儲藏室也當成值得照顧的地方。

Before closing the storage room, Chen asked an authorized worker to check the old socket again. The worker confirmed that it should remain unused until repairs were completed. An added a simple mark beside it and explained the rule to the volunteers. The artist helped move his picture to a safe table, then took it home. Nobody tried to make the small repair into a public event. The room was safe, the letter was protected, and the right people knew what still needed work.

關閉儲藏室前，澄請獲授權的工作人員再檢查舊插座。對方確認完成維修之前都不能使用。安在旁邊加上簡單標記，向志工說明規則。畫家協助把作品移到安全桌面，隨後帶回家。沒有人試圖把小小修繕變成公開活動。房間安全了，信件也受到保護，該負責的人知道還有什麼需要完成。

The folk art group answered that the missing receiver had moved to another country. They asked the team to keep the letter unopened until they could contact her. An agreed. Later she met the artist at a small bar where a student band was playing. Neither of them drank alcohol; they were there to hear the music. He said art often asked people to tell a story, but sometimes the kindest choice was to let a story stay unfinished. An thought of the letter and felt that he understood. She thanked him for not asking to see it.

民俗藝術團體回覆，真正的收件人已移居國外。他們請團隊保留未拆封的信，等聯絡上她再說。安答應了。後來她在一間有學生樂團演奏的小酒吧遇見畫家；兩人都沒喝酒，只是去聽音樂。他說，藝術常要求人說出故事，可有時最善良的選擇，是讓故事暫時沒有結局。安想到那封信，覺得他理解了這點。她謝謝他沒要求看信。

Later, a student asked An why she had not tried harder to find the artist's missing friend. She said the search belonged to the art group and the person being sought. The Slow Post team had no right to turn private questions into its own adventure. A wrong message could follow someone across many years. The student looked thoughtful. He said he often wanted a story to finish with every mystery solved. An admitted that she felt the same. Yet a real person was not a character who existed only to complete somebody else's plot. The answer remained with its owner.

稍後，一名學生問安為什麼不更努力尋找畫家失聯的朋友。安說尋找的權利屬於藝術團體和被尋找的人；慢遞無權把私人問題變成自己的冒險。錯誤訊息可能影響一個人很多年。學生想了一會，說自己總希望每個故事的謎都能解開。安承認她也一樣。然而，真實的人不是為了替別人的情節收尾才存在的角色。答案應該留在主人手裡。

That evening Chen waited outside the hall. An told him about the artist and the candle, then said she had learned two different lessons in one day: not every claim was true, and not every small risk was harmless. He offered her a paper bag of warm bread. She told him she would rather walk than ride the bus. They took the longer street and talked about painting, old songs, and whether a person could change while keeping the same favorite food. By the time they reached her door, neither was in a hurry to say goodbye.

當晚澄在大廳外等她。安說起畫家與蠟燭，說自己一天學到兩件不同的事：不是每項主張都是真的，也不是每個小風險都無害。他遞給她一袋溫熱的麵包。安說想走路，不想搭公車。他們選了較長的街道，談繪畫、老歌，以及一個人是否能在保留最愛食物的同時改變自己。走到她家門前時，兩人都不急著道別。
