名單的價格

The Price of a List

寄給明天的兩張明信片 · 第 20 / 25 章 · 1000–2000 · 英中對照 · 校訂中

用互動版查詞、朗讀與收藏

A businessman offered the university a major gift. He said his company could send a billion printed cards if the Slow Post team shared its address list. An listened without showing anger. A large number did not change the central question: had the writers agreed? They had not. Chen noticed a tiny dot beside the offer and asked what it meant. The businessman said the company would also use the data to send other advertisements. “Then our decision is clear,” An said. “We refuse to trade private trust for free printing.” The man seemed surprised by her calm expression.

一位商人向大學提出一筆大額捐贈。他說公司能印製十億張卡片，只要慢遞分享地址名單。安沒有發怒，平靜聽完。龐大的數字無法改變核心問題：寄件人同意了嗎？沒有。澄注意到提案旁一個小點，問代表什麼。商人承認公司也會用資料寄送其他廣告。「那我們的決定很清楚，」安說。「我們拒絕用私人信任換免費印刷。」商人對她平靜的表情顯得意外。

The offer started a new argument among students. Some worried about the balance between keeping a free service and paying for paper. A science student compared the plan to studying an organ without permission, which made everyone uncomfortable. Another student joked that one cup of cocoa had never solved a budget problem. An did not treat their fear as foolish. She asked volunteers to list each cost and find a smaller way to fix the shortage. They could print fewer cards, reuse clean paper, and invite people to bring their own. None of those options required selling a secret.

提案讓學生之間起了新的爭論。有人擔心免費服務和紙張成本如何平衡。一位理科學生把這比作未經同意就研究別人的器官，讓全場不太自在。另一位學生開玩笑說，一杯可可從來沒能解決預算問題。安沒有把他們的恐懼當成傻話。她請志工列出每項開支，尋找更小但可行的方法改善短缺。他們可以少印卡、重用乾淨紙張，也可以請寄件人自備。這些方法都不需要出售秘密。

A student whose family lived by the ocean said the company offer reminded her of advertisements that followed her online for years. She had once searched for a family gift and then received the same message every day. It had been annoying, but sharing someone's private address could cause far more harm. An invited her to explain this in the public budget meeting without naming any family member. The student's example helped volunteers understand the problem. They were not refusing money because they disliked business. They were refusing a deal that asked for information they had promised to protect.

一名家住海邊的學生說，那份公司提案讓她想起網路廣告多年追著她跑的經驗。她以前搜尋過家人的禮物，之後天天收到相同廣告。那很煩，但分享他人私人地址可能造成更嚴重的傷害。安邀請她在公開預算會議說明這件事，不提家人姓名。她的例子幫助志工理解問題。他們拒絕金錢不是討厭商業，而是交易要求他們交出已承諾保護的資訊。

An prepared a simple reply to the businessman. It thanked him for the offer but explained why the project could not share its address list. She showed the reply to the librarian, not to ask permission for her personal opinion, but to make sure the message accurately described the team's rules. The librarian approved the wording and thanked the students for protecting the visitors' trust. Chen posted the public budget after checking that it contained no private names. Their small service could now plan another term without a hidden bargain.

安準備給商人的簡短回覆，感謝提案，並說明計畫為何不能分享地址名單。她把回覆給圖書館員看，不是請求對個人意見的許可，而是確認文字準確描述團隊規則。圖書館員同意措辭，謝謝學生守護訪客信任。澄核對公開預算不含私人姓名後才張貼。這項小服務現在可以規劃下一學期，不必背負隱藏交易。

At lunch, a young parent came to write about the birth of her daughter. She was sleepy but smiling, and she had a small pain in her shoulder from carrying a heavy bag. Her child looked around with wonder and tried to touch the paper with her tongue. An laughed and moved the card away. The mother said she hoped her daughter would grow up knowing that her own words mattered. An thought of the company offer. If they accepted it, a child might one day receive advertisements from a story her parent had trusted them to keep safe.

午餐時，一位年輕家長來寫下女兒出生的故事。她雖然睡眼惺忪，仍面帶笑容，肩膀也因為背重包而有點痛。孩子好奇地張望，還想用舌頭舔紙。安笑著把卡片移開。母親說希望女兒長大後知道，自己的話值得被重視。安想到那份企業提案：如果接受，這孩子未來也許會因為家長託付的故事而收到廣告。那會背離所有人的信任。

After the vote, the library offered a smaller form of help: paper storage and access to a room twice a week. An checked the conditions. There would be no demand to publish private letters and no hidden use of the address list. She accepted with thanks. Chen suggested a public note explaining how much help had been given. An agreed, because she did not want kindness to become a secret debt either. They ended the meeting with enough resources for the next term. The plan was smaller than the company had offered, but it belonged to the people who used it.

投票後，圖書館提出較小的支援：紙張儲存空間，以及每週兩次使用教室的權利。安核對條件，沒有強制公開私信，也沒有暗中使用地址名單，便道謝接受。澄建議發布公開說明，列出支援內容。安同意，因為她也不想讓善意變成秘密欠款。會議結束時，團隊已有足夠資源運作下一學期。這個方案不如大公司提出的龐大，卻真正屬於使用它的人。

The team voted to reject the offer and publish a simple budget. Chen helped An prepare the numbers, but she delivered the explanation herself. Afterward he told her that he was proud of her. She answered that she was proud of him too, especially because he could disagree without trying to win. They bought two hot drinks and sat near the blue box. There was no exciting surprise waiting inside. For once, An was grateful for that. She rested her hand beside his on the bench, and he waited until she moved it closer before taking it.

小組投票拒絕提案，並公開簡易預算。澄協助安整理數字，但由她自己對外說明。事後他說為她驕傲，她也回應自己以他為榮，尤其欣賞他能提出不同意見，卻不一定要分勝負。他們買了兩杯熱飲，坐在藍色郵筒附近。郵筒裡沒有什麼驚人的驚喜。這一次，安反而很感激。她把手放在長椅上，澄等她主動移近，才輕輕握住。
