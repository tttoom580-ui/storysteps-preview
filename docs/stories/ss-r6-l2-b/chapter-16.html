演習日

The Practice Earthquake

寄給明天的兩張明信片 · 第 16 / 25 章 · 1000–2000 · 英中對照 · 校訂中

A practice earthquake alarm sounded while the Slow Post team was preparing the next delivery. The manager of the student hall pointed to an arrow on the wall and reminded everyone of the basics: leave bags behind, stay calm, and use the stairs. One volunteer tried to recover a rare postcard from the upper shelf. An told him to stop. No piece of paper, however valuable, was worth a broken leg. They walked outside and counted people before opening any boxes. Chen checked the list twice. Indeed, one member was missing, but she had gone on a hike and was never inside the building.

慢遞小組準備下一批投遞時，防震演習警報響了。學生中心管理員指著牆上的箭頭，提醒大家基本原則：留下行李、保持冷靜、走樓梯。一名志工想從上層書架搶救一張稀有明信片，安叫他停下。無論卡片多珍貴，都不值得讓人摔斷腿。他們走到戶外，先清點人數，再決定是否打開箱子。澄核對兩次名單。的確少了一名社員，但她去健行了，原本就不在樓內。

The drill left some people in a bad mood. A small child who had come with his aunt began to beg for his toy hippopotamus, still inside the hall. An promised they would look for it after the manager said it was safe. Chen tried to help the child stretch his arms and breathe slowly. Nearby, a student showed a picture of a worm from her biology class, hoping to make him laugh. It worked. The child forgot the toy for a moment and asked whether worms could feel earthquakes. “That is a question for your teacher,” An said with a smile.

演習讓一些人心情不好。一名跟阿姨前來的小孩哭著求大家拿回留在館內的河馬玩偶。安答應等管理員確認安全後再找。澄教孩子伸展手臂、慢慢呼吸。旁邊一名學生拿出她生物課拍的蟲子照片，想逗孩子笑，果然奏效。孩子暫時忘了玩偶，改問蟲子能不能感覺地震。「這要問你的老師，」安笑著回答。

The building manager invited the volunteers to discuss what had gone well during the drill. An said the group had left the room quickly, but the count outside had been confusing. One member was away, yet the list still showed her as present. Chen suggested a simple sign-in system for each shift. It would record who entered the room without keeping personal letters or private details. The manager agreed. An wrote the change on the board and asked everyone to test it at the next meeting. She wanted the students to learn from the alarm, not merely to forget it once the noise stopped.

大樓管理員邀志工討論演習的優缺點。安說大家離開房間很迅速，但在外頭清點人數時有點混亂。一名社員不在場，名單卻仍顯示她已到。澄建議每次排班使用簡單簽到制度，只記錄誰進入房間，不保存私人信件或其他私密資訊。管理員同意了。安把修改寫上看板，請大家下次開會時測試。她希望學生從警報中學習，而不是噪音停了就把一切忘掉。

Once the doors opened again, Chen checked the quality of the storage shelves and noticed a loose screw. He asked the hall workers to fix it rather than doing an unsafe repair himself. An used the office printer to create a new safety sheet. It described the relation between people and equipment in simple words: protect people first, objects second. An drew a small royal crown above a cartoon box to make the rule easier to remember. Chen looked at it and said, “The box is not the king.” She laughed. “That is exactly the point.”

館門重新開放後，澄檢查儲物架的品質，發現一顆鬆動的螺絲。他請館舍人員維修，沒有自行冒險。安用辦公室印表機製作新的安全單，簡單說明人與設備的關係：人優先，物品其次。她在卡通箱子上方畫了一頂王冠，讓人更容易記住規則。澄看著圖說：「箱子不是國王。」安笑了。「這正是重點。」

At the end of the day, An and Chen sat on a low wall outside the student center. They spoke about what it meant to care for someone. Chen said he had once thought care meant solving a problem before the other person noticed it. An said she used to think care meant never making someone wait. Now both ideas seemed incomplete. Sometimes the right act was to ask, listen, and leave room for a decision. An looked at him and said she was glad they had learned that together. He answered with a quiet smile and offered his hand without moving it closer until she chose to take it.

一天結束時，安與澄坐在學生中心外的矮牆上，談起關心一個人究竟是什麼意思。澄說自己以前以為關心就是在對方發現前解決問題。安則曾認為關心就是絕不讓人等待。如今兩種想法都顯得不完整。有時正確的行動是詢問、傾聽，並留下決定空間。安看著他，說很高興兩人一起學會這件事。澄安靜地笑了，伸出手，卻等她主動握住才靠近。

That evening the child came back for his toy. An handed it over after checking his aunt's name. The boy hugged the hippopotamus and thanked everyone. On the way home, An told Chen that today's work had felt very different from romance. “I hope so,” he answered. “I would never use danger to make a date exciting.” She took his hand for the first time, because she wanted to. He looked surprised but did not hold tighter than she did. Their steps stayed even as they passed the quiet old post box.

傍晚，孩子回來領玩偶。安確認阿姨的姓名後交還。男孩抱著河馬向大家道謝。回家路上，安對澄說今天的工作和浪漫差得很遠。「希望如此，」他回答。「我絕不會靠危險讓約會刺激。」安第一次主動牽起他的手，因為她願意。澄有些驚訝，卻沒有握得比她更緊。兩人步伐一致地走過安靜的舊郵筒。
