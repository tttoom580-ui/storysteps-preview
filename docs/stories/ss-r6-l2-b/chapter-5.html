雷雨中的路線

Thunder Over the Route

寄給明天的兩張明信片 · 第 5 / 25 章 · 1000–2000 · 英中對照

用互動版查詞、朗讀與收藏

A storm reached the campus on Friday, just as the Slow Post team prepared its first weekly report. Thunder shook the windows, and an electrical problem stopped the elevator. The volunteers had to carry boxes by hand, but Chen refused to let anyone lift a mass of wet paper alone. An listened to his method: move the dry cards first, place the heavy boxes on the low shelf, and check each name twice. A brilliant plan meant nothing if people were hurt using it. When the lights went out, they waited in the hall until the building manager said it was safe.

星期五，慢遞小組準備第一份週報時，暴風雨襲來。雷聲震動窗戶，電力故障使電梯停擺。志工們只能徒手搬箱子，但澄不准任何人獨自搬起一大堆濕紙張。安聽從他的方法：先搬乾燥卡片，把重箱放在低層書架，每個名字都核對兩次。再出色的計畫，若執行時會讓人受傷也沒有意義。停電後，他們在走廊等待，直到大樓管理員確認安全。

The rain brought unexpected guests. A farm student who missed his bus came in with a story about a rooster that woke his family before dawn. His grandmother had made him a warm wool hat for a snowy winter, and he had sent her a card to say thanks. A nursing student spoke about her domestic duties at home and how hard it was to find time to eat. An offered a bowl of noodle soup from the student kitchen. Their appetite returned as they talked. For the first time, the blue box felt less like equipment and more like a place where strangers could rest.

雨帶來意外的客人。一名錯過公車的農學院學生走進來，說起家裡有隻公雞總在天亮前叫醒全家。祖母曾在下雪的冬天織一頂溫暖的羊毛帽給他，他寫卡片向祖母道謝。一位護理系學生談到家務責任，以及自己多難抽空吃飯。安從學生廚房端來一碗麵湯。大家邊聊邊恢復了食慾。藍色郵筒第一次不像一件設備，而像讓陌生人歇腳的地方。

After the power came back, An helped the volunteers discover which boxes were still dry. She used a simple list rather than memory, asking one person to read a number while another checked it. When someone found a missing sleeve, the room grew noisy with relief. An did not offer empty praise. She thanked the student for noticing the error and said exactly how the careful check had saved the card. Chen stood nearby with clean cloths. He never tried to take credit for the plan, even though he had suggested the method that morning.

電力恢復後，安協助志工查出哪些箱子仍是乾的。她用簡單清單，不靠記憶，一個人念編號，另一個人核對。有人找回失蹤的保護套時，整間房間都鬆了口氣。安沒有空泛稱讚，而是具體感謝那位學生注意到錯誤，說明細心查核如何保住卡片。澄拿著乾淨布巾站在附近。雖然早上是他提出這個方法，他也沒有試圖搶功。

A conflict began when one volunteer wanted to spread photographs of the crowded room online. The pictures showed people's faces without their permission. An asked him to stop. He said public attention would help their project, and he did not want to lose the chance. Chen supported An, but he let her explain the problem herself. “A connection is not something we can take,” she said. “We need to ask before we share.” The volunteer looked down at his phone, then deleted the pictures. He promised to ask next time. Nobody praised him for doing the minimum; they simply returned to work.

一名志工想把擠滿人的房間照片分享到網路，引發了衝突。照片裡有未經同意入鏡的面孔。安請他停下。他說公開關注能幫助計畫，不想錯過機會。澄支持安，卻讓她自己解釋。「關係不是我們可以直接拿走的東西，」她說。「分享之前必須先問。」志工低頭看手機，刪除了照片，答應下次先徵詢同意。沒有人因為他做到最基本的事就大力稱讚；大家只是回去繼續工作。

When the rain eased, An discovered that one card had become damp near the corner. It carried a note about a child with sensitive skin who could not wear a certain scarf. The sender wanted to thank a neighbor for finding a soft one. An held the card under a lamp, careful not to touch the ink. Chen helped her place it between clean sheets. He told her that whenever a card was damaged, they should record the damage instead of pretending nothing happened. By evening the ink was dry enough to read, and the words had survived.

雨勢稍緩時，安發現角落有張卡片受潮。上面寫著一個皮膚敏感的孩子不能戴某種圍巾，寄件人想感謝鄰居替孩子找到柔軟的那條。安把卡片拿到檯燈下，盡量不碰觸墨水。澄幫她夾進乾淨紙張之間，提醒她每當信件受損，都應如實登記，不能假裝無事。傍晚時，墨跡已乾到可以辨認，字句也留了下來。

In the next meeting, An asked what support the volunteers needed. One wanted stronger shelves; another asked for clear shifts during exams. A third hoped they could close early when a storm warning appeared. Instead of trying to solve everything on her own, An wrote the requests on the board and invited ideas. Chen watched her listen. He saw that she was no longer afraid to share control of a plan she cared about. When they walked to the bus stop, he told her so. She laughed and said, “I still get impatient.” “I know,” he answered. “You also notice it now.”

下一場會議中，安詢問志工需要什麼支持。有人希望書架更牢固，有人想在考試期間有明確排班，也有人希望發布風暴警報時能提早關閉。安沒有試圖獨自解決一切，而是把需求寫上白板，邀請大家提出方法。澄看著她傾聽。他發現安已不再害怕分享自己珍惜的計畫控制權。走向公車站時，他把這個觀察告訴她。安笑說：「我還是會不耐煩。」「我知道，」他答。「但妳現在會察覺了。」

The team finished its weekly report after dark. Five cards had been delivered, three had been returned, and nine were still waiting for permission. An did not hide the delays. She wrote that the project needed better storage and a clear rule for bad weather. Chen read the report, then set a cup of warm tea beside her. “You noticed what went wrong,” he said. “That is how a good plan grows.” An smiled. She was beginning to notice how often he made room for her choices, even when they disagreed. It was a quiet form of care, and she wanted to understand it.

天黑後，小組完成第一份週報。五張卡片已送達、三張已退回，另有九張還在等待收件同意。安沒有隱瞞延誤，而是寫下計畫需要更好的收納方式，以及惡劣天候時的明確規則。澄讀完報告，把一杯熱茶放在她身邊。「妳看見了哪裡出錯，」他說。「好計畫就是這樣長大的。」安笑了。她逐漸注意到，即使兩人意見不同，他也經常替她保留選擇空間。那是一種安靜的關心，她想更了解它。
