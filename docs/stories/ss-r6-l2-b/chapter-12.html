雨中的屋頂

A Roof in the Rain

寄給明天的兩張明信片 · 第 12 / 25 章 · 1000–2000 · 英中對照 · 校訂中

用互動版查詞、朗讀與收藏

A loose tile fell from the library roof after another night of rain. The dog in the yard began to bark, and a student slipped on the wet steps, hurting her ankle. Chen called the campus office with one click on his phone, then helped move the card boxes away from the water. The repair team warned of a delay, so Slow Post would need a new room. An selected the music practice hall, which had a wide door and a dry floor. A few volunteers were upset because the new space was farther away, but nobody argued that safety could wait.

又一夜大雨過後，圖書館屋頂掉落一塊鬆動的瓦片。院子裡的狗開始吠叫，一名學生在濕階梯上滑倒，扭傷腳踝。澄按一下手機便聯絡了校園辦公室，接著幫忙把卡片箱搬離積水。維修團隊表示需要延後開放，慢遞必須找新房間。安選了有寬敞入口與乾燥地板的音樂練習廳。幾位志工因為路程變遠而不高興，但沒有人認為安全可以等。

The move revealed a box of cards from a cultural exchange program. One card had a description of a guava tree; another mentioned a novel that the writer had carried while traveling. A third showed children playing on a swing. An placed these cards in separate sleeves, following the exact numbers on the old list. She did not assume that every cheerful picture meant a happy memory. Some writers might have changed their minds. Chen said a protective rule could be gentle without being weak. He wrote that line on the new board.

搬遷時，大家找到一箱文化交流計畫留下的卡片。一張描寫芭樂樹，一張提到寄件人在旅行時帶著的小說，第三張畫了孩子們玩盪鞦韆。安照舊清單的確切編號，把卡片分別放進保護套。她沒有認為每幅快樂的圖畫都代表愉快回憶；有些人可能早已改變心意。澄說，保護人的規則可以溫和，卻不必軟弱。他把這句話寫在新看板上。

When the new room opened, An asked the volunteers to select routes that were safe for people using walking aids. A student who loved travel suggested a scenic path near the river, but the path was too steep after rain. Chen put a clear warning upon the route map and offered a flatter way. A group from the youth center helped test the directions without carrying any private mail. An liked that they were involved in planning rather than just following orders. The revised route took longer, but more people could use it without asking for special help.

新房間開放後，安請志工挑選對使用助行器的人也安全的路線。一位熱愛旅行的學生建議走河邊風景優美的小徑，但雨後坡度太陡。澄在路線圖上加上明確警告，另提供一條較平坦的路。青少年中心的小組協助測試指示，不接觸任何私人郵件。安喜歡大家能參與規劃，而非只能聽命行事。新路線雖較長，卻讓更多人不必特別求助也能使用。

While arranging the practice hall, An noticed that the new tables were too high for one visitor. She asked the room manager for a lower table rather than expecting the person to manage alone. The manager found a folding table in storage, and Chen checked that it was stable. The visitor later thanked the team. She could now write with her arms resting comfortably on the surface. An saw that the smallest change to a room could make the greatest difference to someone entering it.

整理練習廳時，安發現新桌子對一位訪客太高。她請房間管理員找矮一點的桌子，而不是要對方自行克服。管理員從倉庫找來折疊桌，澄確認它穩固。訪客稍後向團隊道謝，如今她能舒適地把手臂放在桌面書寫。安明白，房間裡最小的改動，對進門的某個人也許有最大的意義。

At lunch, a volunteer told a story about an evil character in a book who opened every secret letter. An laughed and said real trouble was often less dramatic. “Sometimes people mean well and still hurt others,” she added. Her friend offered a spoon of sweet soup and asked why the team was so serious. An explained that a single reply could make someone hopeful, angry, or afraid. The friend listened carefully. She had thought the cards were only pretty paper. Now she understood why the team checked every request before sending anything.

午餐時，一位志工說起書裡的邪惡人物，專門拆看所有秘密書信。安笑著說，真實的麻煩通常沒那麼戲劇化。「有時人們明明好意，仍會傷害別人，」她補充。朋友遞來一匙甜湯，問團隊為何如此嚴肅。安解釋，一封回信就可能使人期待、生氣或害怕。朋友認真聽著。她原以為卡片只是漂亮紙張，如今理解為何小組在寄出之前要一再確認。

A librarian asked An to write a short description of the new room for the university website. She listed the dry floor, wide doorway, quiet tables, and rules about photography. Chen checked the details with the maintenance team. An used to find such careful steps boring, but now she saw that a clear description could save people a wasted trip. That afternoon an older visitor arrived with a walking stick and entered without difficulty. She thanked the students for considering her before the room opened. An was pleased by the result, because a good welcome began long before a guest walked through the door.

圖書館員請安替大學網站寫一段新房間介紹。她列出乾燥地板、寬門口、安靜桌位，以及拍照規定。澄向維護團隊確認細節。安以前覺得這些步驟很無聊，現在明白清楚的介紹能免去人們白跑一趟。當天下午，一位拿手杖的長者順利進門，謝謝學生在開放前就替她考慮。安很高興，因為好的歡迎在訪客踏進門前很久就開始了。

When they finished setting up the new room, Chen offered to walk An to the bus stop. She asked if he was helping because she led the project. “No,” he said, “because I enjoy talking to you.” He spoke so plainly that she almost missed the importance of the answer. She told him she enjoyed their conversations too, but she needed time before making a personal promise. “Then take time,” he said. Rain tapped on the roof above them, steady and soft. An had never thought patience could sound like affection.

布置完新房間，澄提出陪安走到公車站。安問他是不是因為自己是專案負責人才這麼做。「不是，」他說，「因為我喜歡和妳說話。」他的回答太坦率，安差點沒察覺其重要。她告訴澄，自己也喜歡兩人的對話，但在做出私人承諾之前需要時間。「那就慢慢來，」他說。雨滴輕柔而規律地敲打頭上的屋頂。安從未想過，耐心也能聽起來像是喜歡。
