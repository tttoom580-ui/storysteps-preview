The Last Practice Day

寄給明天的兩張明信片 · 第 24 / 25 章 · 1000–2000 · 英中對照 · 校訂中

用互動版查詞、朗讀與收藏

A small fair was planned for the next weekend, and the team wanted to complete its final training. At the bakery downstairs, a baker gave them a box of salty bread. Children from a nearby school came with balloons painted like an ape and a camel. One balloon burst, and the children looked upset, but An reminded them that a failure in a game did not have to ruin the whole day. They could make another decoration. The basis of their event was never a perfect scene; it was the chance for people to write and choose freely.

小型活動預定下週舉行，團隊想完成最後一次訓練。樓下麵包店送來一盒鹹麵包。附近學校的孩子帶來畫著猿猴和駱駝的氣球。一顆氣球爆了，孩子們很難過，安提醒他們遊戲裡的失敗不必毀掉整天，可以改做其他裝飾。活動的基礎從來不是完美場面，而是讓人能自由書寫與選擇。

The new volunteers practiced how to behave when a receiver was absent. They should leave a notice without a private name, then take the card back. A former student volunteered to play a difficult visitor. He called Chen handsome and offered to skip the rules if Chen would smile for a photo. Everyone laughed, but Chen answered firmly that appearance changed nothing. He would follow the same process for every guest. An admired how he managed the scene without making the visitor feel foolish. Afterward he told her he had been nervous too.

新志工練習收件人缺席時如何應對：留下不含私人姓名的通知，再把卡片帶回。一位畢業生自願扮演難纏訪客。他誇澄英俊，說只要澄願意對鏡頭微笑，就能跳過規則。全場大笑，澄卻堅定回答外貌改變不了任何事，他對每位訪客都採相同程序。安欣賞他沒有讓對方難堪，便完成了這個場景。事後澄坦承自己其實也緊張。

A volunteer's absence at the last rehearsal forced the group to change two shifts. An did not panic. She asked who had enough energy to take the work and reminded everyone that they could say no. One student admitted she had lost her nerve after the storm and did not want to travel outside campus. An thanked her for being clear and gave her an indoor task instead. Another volunteer began to tire while carrying boxes, so Chen asked him to rest. Their promise to protect people had to matter on an ordinary workday, not only during an emergency.

最後彩排有名志工缺席，小組必須調動兩個班次。安沒有慌張，而是詢問誰仍有體力接手，並提醒大家可以拒絕。一位學生承認風暴後已失去勇氣，不想再到校外工作。安謝謝她說清楚，改安排室內任務。另一名志工搬箱子時開始疲倦，澄請他休息。他們保護人的承諾，必須在普通工作日也有意義，不只在緊急時刻才算數。

A child asked whether the blue box would disappear after the students left. An knelt beside the child and explained that the librarian and new volunteers had agreed to care for it. She showed the handover chart with pictures, not private names. The child smiled and placed a card in the new slot. Chen noticed An watching him with a soft expression. He asked what she was thinking. She said she was glad that endings could also be beginnings when people made room for others.

一個孩子問，大學生離開後藍色郵筒會不會消失。安蹲下來解釋，圖書館員與新志工已答應接手照顧。她展示有圖片、沒有私人姓名的交接圖。孩子笑著把卡片放進新投遞口。澄注意到安溫柔地看著那孩子，便問她在想什麼。她說，只要願意為別人留位置，結束也能成為開始。

A grandmother arrived with a memory card she had kept through several moves. It carried a mixture of joy and sorrow: a holiday meal, a lost home, and a friend who had helped her survive a hard winter. She did not want the card displayed. An said she could keep it and still join the writing table. The woman thanked her, then promised to bring a new blank card for her grandson. Chen watched An help her choose an envelope. He saw how much she had changed since the first time she wanted to open every old postcard.

一位祖母帶著搬過幾次家仍保存的回憶卡片前來。上面混合了歡喜與悲傷：節慶餐點、失去的家，以及幫她度過艱難冬季的朋友。她不想展示卡片。安說她可以自己留著，仍能參與寫信桌。女士道謝，答應帶一張新空白卡給孫子。澄看著安幫她選信封，想起最初的安幾乎想打開每張舊卡。她已改變許多。

When the rehearsal ended, An sat beside the blue box with an empty card. She thought about writing a message to her younger self, the girl who had wanted every problem solved before dinner. She wrote, “You can care deeply and still stop when you need to.” Chen returned with two cups of water and did not ask what she had written. She told him anyway, because she wanted to share it. He smiled and said he hoped she would keep that lesson after graduation. An hoped so too. The future seemed less frightening now that she knew she could choose it one day at a time.

彩排結束後，安帶著空白卡坐在藍色郵筒旁。她想寫信給以前的自己，那個總想在晚餐前解決所有問題的女孩。她寫道：「你可以非常在乎，也可以在需要時停下。」澄端來兩杯水，沒有問她寫了什麼。安仍主動告訴他，因為她想分享。澄笑說，希望她畢業後仍記得這一課。安也這麼希望。如今她知道可以一天一天做選擇，未來便沒有那麼可怕。

The team ended practice with a tired but cheerful group photograph. They checked that everyone in the picture had agreed before posting it. An and Chen stood on opposite sides of the table, laughing with their friends. Later, when the room was empty, she went to him and said she wanted a photograph of just the two of them, for themselves. He asked where she wanted to stand. She chose the blue box. The picture would not go on any public wall. It would be a private memory they had both chosen to keep.

彩排最後，團隊拍了一張疲憊卻開心的合照。他們先確認照片裡每個人都同意，才決定發布。安與澄站在桌子兩側，和朋友們笑著。稍後房間清空，安走向澄，說她想拍一張只有兩個人的照片，自己留著。他問她想站在哪裡。她選了藍色郵筒旁。照片不會貼上公開牆，而是兩人共同選擇保存的私人回憶。
