先詢問的英雄

The Hero Who Asked First

寄給明天的兩張明信片 · 第 23 / 25 章 · 1000–2000 · 英中對照 · 校訂中

用互動版查詞、朗讀與收藏

When the roads reopened, a school invited Slow Post to speak about responsible volunteering. An did not want to be called a heroine. She told the students that the real work was often quiet: keeping a record, carrying water to someone thirsty, or waiting for an answer. A girl brought a drawing of a ladybug on a leaf. Another child had built a little steel bridge from blocks, with a triangle under each side for strength. Chen measured its width with a ruler and praised the careful design. Neither child needed a dramatic rescue story to have done something worth sharing.

道路重新開放後，一所學校邀慢遞分享負責任的志願工作。安不願被稱為女英雄。她告訴學生，真正的工作常常很安靜：留下紀錄、替口渴的人送水，或等待對方回答。一名女孩拿來瓢蟲停在葉上的圖畫；另一個孩子用積木搭小鋼橋，兩側各放一個三角形增加穩定。澄用尺量了橋的寬度，稱讚設計很仔細。兩個孩子都不需要驚天動地的救援故事，便已做出值得分享的事。

A student's employer had offered to pay for a formal poster about the project. An said any poster must mention the limits of the service, not only its successes. She also wanted a line about friendship: help should not become a debt that people were forced to repay. The teacher liked the idea, but a boy asked whether a good friend could ever say no. “Anytime,” An answered. “A no can protect a friendship, because it lets both people tell the truth.” Chen saw her smile at the answer. It was the lesson she had learned from the old gardener months before.

一位學生的雇主願意出錢製作正式宣傳海報。安說海報必須提到服務界線，不只是成功案例。她還想加入一段談友情的話：幫助不應變成逼人償還的債。老師喜歡這個主意，一名男孩卻問好朋友是否也能拒絕。「任何時候都可以，」安回答。「拒絕也能保護友情，因為它讓雙方都能說實話。」澄看見她回答時的笑容。這正是幾個月前那位園丁教她的事。

One student asked whether the team should advise visitors about their diet when a letter mentioned food. An said that would not be their job. They could point people toward qualified help, but they should not make personal health decisions for strangers. At the writing table, a boy rested his hand on his hip and stayed silent while his grandmother thought about what to say. Nobody hurried her. After several minutes she wrote only one sentence: “I am glad you came today.” The boy read it and smiled. An saw that a little space for silence could be more useful than a long speech.

一名學生問，若信件提到食物，團隊是否該建議訪客的飲食。安說那不是他們的工作。他們可以引導人尋求合格協助，卻不該替陌生人做個人健康決定。寫信桌旁，一個男孩把手放在腰側，安靜等待祖母思考要寫什麼。沒有人催她。幾分鐘後，她只寫下一句：「我很高興你今天來了。」男孩讀完便笑了。安發現，留一點沉默空間，有時比長篇演講更有用。

After lunch, a girl asked Chen whether he and An were friends or something more. He smiled but did not answer for An. He said their private lives were not part of the class lesson. An heard the exchange and appreciated the answer. Later she told him she would be happy to introduce him as someone she was dating when they both wanted that. He said they could decide together. She liked that their relationship could be real without becoming a public show.

午餐後，一名女孩問澄和安是朋友，還是更進一步的關係。他笑了，卻沒有替安回答，只說私人生活不是這堂課的內容。安聽見後很欣賞。稍後她告訴澄，等兩人都願意，她很樂意介紹他是正在交往的人。澄說他們可以共同決定。她喜歡這段關係可以真實存在，卻不必變成公開表演。

At the school lunch table, students discussed healthy diets and the stress of exams. An listened without pretending to be a doctor. When a girl asked for personal health advice, An suggested speaking with a qualified adult who could help. She showed the same careful manner when a parent asked about private letters. The team could offer information, not control another person's life. Chen realized that this was why he had grown close to An. She cared about people without making herself the center of their stories.

學校午餐桌上，學生討論健康飲食與考試壓力。安認真聽，沒有假裝自己是醫師。一名女孩詢問個人健康建議，她提議找合格且能提供協助的成年人談談。當家長問到私人書信時，她也以同樣謹慎的態度回覆。團隊可以提供資訊，不能控制別人的生活。澄明白自己為何漸漸親近安：她關心人，卻不把自己放進每個人的故事中心。

The school teacher thanked An for visiting and offered to put the volunteer guide in the student office. An agreed after checking that the guide contained no private examples. Chen asked the children what they had learned. A girl said helping meant asking before doing something for someone. Another said a good friend could still say no. An wanted to remember both answers. She had spent months learning similar lessons, yet hearing them in the children's voices made them fresh again. On the way home she asked Chen whether he thought their work had changed them. He said yes, in ways neither had planned.

老師感謝安前來，提議把志工指引放在學生辦公室。安確認內容沒有私人案例後同意了。澄問孩子們學到什麼。一名女孩說幫忙以前要先問，另一名說好朋友仍然可以拒絕。安想記住兩個回答。她花了好幾個月才學會相似道理，如今從孩子口中聽到，卻又有新的感受。回家路上，她問澄工作是否改變了他們。他說有，而且改變的方式兩人都未曾預料。

After the visit, An told Chen she had begun to realize what kind of future she wanted. She still hoped to work in design, but she wanted her work to be useful for people who were often overlooked. Chen said he wanted to teach simple computer tools after graduation. Their plans were different, yet there was room for both. They walked in silence for a while, letting the choices settle. Then An asked whether he would like another date on Sunday. His answer came at once: yes.

參訪結束後，安告訴澄，自己開始明白想要怎樣的未來。她仍想從事設計工作，但希望作品能幫到經常被忽略的人。澄說自己畢業後想教人使用簡單電腦工具。兩人的計畫不同，卻能同時存在。他們安靜走了一段路，讓這些選擇慢慢沉澱。接著安問他星期天願不願意再約會。他立刻回答：願意。
