The Animal Shelter Card

寄給明天的兩張明信片 · 第 4 / 25 章 · 1000–2000 · 英中對照

用互動版查詞、朗讀與收藏

An had promised to attend a student meeting in the eastern part of town. The post office had received a card from a young volunteer at an animal shelter. Its front showed a wolf and a bird with one injured wing, drawn beside an empty cage. The writer wanted to thank the shelter workers, but the note also included private details about a patient and a drug. An took the card to the health department for advice without revealing the patient's name. The officer explained that even a kind purpose did not allow them to share someone's medical information.

安答應出席城東一場學生會議。慢遞小組收到一張收容所年輕志工寄出的卡片。正面畫著狼和一隻翅膀受傷的鳥，旁邊是一個空籠子。寄件人想感謝收容所員工，但文字也提到病患與藥物等私人資料。安帶著不含病患姓名的問題向衛生部門請教。承辦人解釋，即使出發點是善意，也不能任意公開他人的醫療資訊。

Chen suggested a firm standard: each receiver should see only what the sender had chosen to send, and volunteers should never copy the text. An encouraged the shelter student to write a new, safer message. The student was a little upset, but she agreed when An explained the reason. They sat under an umbrella outside the campus gymnasium and talked about how small details could hurt someone. A girl passed by carrying a comb and a cooking pan for a drama class. She stopped to ask whether the postcard project needed more hands.

澄提出明確標準：收件人只能看見寄件人願意寄出的內容，志工不能複製信件文字。安鼓勵那位收容所學生改寫一張較安全的卡片。對方有點難過，但聽完原因後還是同意了。他們坐在校園體育館外的傘下，討論小細節如何可能傷害他人。一名女孩經過，手上拿著戲劇課用的梳子和平底鍋。她停下來問慢遞計畫是否需要人手。

Before the shelter visit, An read a note about the whole region and its lack of animal doctors. She wanted to help, but she knew the mail project could not become a medical service. She asked the department officer for a safe way to encourage volunteers without inviting them to do jobs beyond their training. The officer suggested that they share the shelter's public volunteer information, not private records. An wrote down that advice. It let the group be useful while remaining honest about what they could and could not do.

前往收容所前，安讀到一份關於整個地區動物醫師不足的報告。她想幫忙，但知道慢遞不能變成醫療服務。她詢問部門承辦人，如何鼓勵志工又不讓他們承擔超出訓練範圍的工作。對方建議分享收容所公開的志工資訊，而不是私密紀錄。安記下這個建議。小組因此仍能有所貢獻，也能誠實面對自身能力界線。

During the meeting, the drama student offered to prepare food for a small event. She could fry vegetables on an electric stove, but the room rules did not permit cooking there. An told her not to worry; the group could bring cold sandwiches instead. They also learned that a minor member would need a guardian's permission to take part in off-campus deliveries. The new rule slowed their plans, yet the students understood its purpose. Chen drew a little motion arrow on the planning board to show which volunteers would move cards and which would answer questions.

會議上，戲劇課的女孩提議為小活動準備食物。她會用電爐炒蔬菜，但活動室規定不准在裡面烹煮。安要她別擔心，大家可以改帶冷三明治。他們也得知未成年社員若要參加校外投遞，需取得監護人許可。新規則讓行程慢了些，學生們仍理解其目的。澄在計畫板上畫了一個移動箭頭，說明哪些志工負責搬運卡片，哪些負責回答問題。

In the afternoon the animal shelter invited them to visit. A tired dog rested quietly behind a gate. The staff member pointed out that the drawing of a wolf was only a story; the real animals needed careful daily work. An thought of the difference between an exciting picture and a life that needed protection. The student wrote a second card, thanking the people who cleaned the rooms and fed the birds. The personal medical details were gone. The card could now express care without using another person's pain as a story.

下午，收容所邀請他們參觀。一隻疲倦的狗安靜地趴在門後。工作人員說明，卡片上的狼只是故事，真實的動物需要每天細心照顧。安想到刺激的圖畫與需要保護的生命之間有多大差別。那名學生重寫了一張卡，感謝清潔房間、餵食鳥類的人。私人醫療細節已被刪去。這張卡現在能表達關心，而不把別人的痛苦當作故事材料。

The shelter worker showed them a table where visitors could write new thank-you cards. No one had to take a card home immediately. A boy who loved birds drew a nest with three eggs, then asked whether he could put his full name on the public board. An suggested using only a first name or leaving the picture unsigned. The boy chose a small bird mark instead. Chen looked at the drawing and said the decision was a good one. The boy smiled and placed the card in the safe box without anyone telling him what he had to feel.

收容所員工帶他們到一張桌前，訪客可以在那裡寫新的感謝卡。沒有人必須立刻把卡帶回家。一個喜歡鳥的男孩畫了裝著三顆蛋的鳥巢，接著問能否把全名放在公開看板。安建議只寫名字，或讓畫保持無署名。男孩改選一個小鳥符號。澄看著畫說這個決定很好。男孩笑著把卡片放進安全箱，沒有人指示他必須有什麼感覺。

On the ride back, Chen asked whether An regretted being so careful. She said no. “At first I thought sending a message was simply about moving paper. Now I see that a person travels with the message, even when we cannot see them.” He looked at her for a moment and then turned back to the road. An knew that he had heard more than an answer about the project. She did not ask him to explain the smile on his face. For now, she was happy to notice it.

回程時，澄問安是否後悔如此小心。她說不會。「一開始我以為送信只是移動紙張。現在我知道，一個人也隨著訊息一起前往，即使我們看不到那個人。」澄看了她片刻，又轉頭望向道路。安知道，他聽見的不只是對計畫的回答。她沒有問他臉上的笑意代表什麼。此刻能注意到那個笑容，她已經很開心。
