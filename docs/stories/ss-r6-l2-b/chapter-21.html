敞開的教室

The Class With the Open Door

寄給明天的兩張明信片 · 第 21 / 25 章 · 1000–2000 · 英中對照 · 校訂中

用互動版查詞、朗讀與收藏

Slow Post invited new volunteers to a short class. An stood beside a blackboard and wrote the three most important words: ask, record, and return. Chen brought a blanket because the room was cold, while a student offered to lend them a portable instrument for a welcome song. The introduction was meant to be friendly, not grand. A lone student sat at the back with a magazine in her hands. She said she was shy and did not like talking in front of groups. An told her that listening was also useful work. Neither confidence nor a loud voice was required to help.

慢遞替新志工開了一堂短課。安站在黑板旁，寫下三個最重要的詞：詢問、記錄、退回。澄因為教室寒冷帶來毯子，一名學生則願意借出可攜式樂器，供歡迎歌曲使用。開場介紹要親切，不必盛大。後排有個獨自坐著的學生，手裡拿著雜誌。她說自己害羞，不喜歡在人前說話。安告訴她，傾聽也是有用的工作。幫忙不需要自信滿滿或聲音宏亮。

An handed each volunteer a sample card. One told a story about a goat on a distant farm. Another mentioned a salesperson who had returned a lost purse. A third described a child washing a table with soap before dinner. The stories were simple, perhaps even dull to someone seeking adventure. Yet An asked the class to review each one with care. A card might seem unimportant to a stranger while holding someone's pride or grief. The students practiced asking for consent before moving any paper, and they learned how to handle a refusal without making excuses.

安發給每位志工一張樣本卡。有的寫遠方農場的一隻山羊，有的提到幫忙歸還失物的售貨員，還有一張描述孩子晚餐前用肥皂擦桌子。對追求冒險的人來說，故事也許平淡甚至無趣，但安請大家仔細檢視。卡片在陌生人眼中可能不重要，在當事人心中卻承載驕傲或悲傷。學生練習搬動紙張前先徵求同意，也學會不找藉口地接受拒絕。

A new volunteer arrived late and immediately offered an excuse about traffic. An told her that the team needed accurate records, not perfect people. The student admitted she had simply forgotten the time. An thanked her for telling the truth and helped her find the next available shift. Chen watched the exchange from the back of the room. He remembered how afraid An had been of mistakes during the first weeks. Now she could ask for honesty without making anyone feel small. The student returned the following day early, bringing a set of blank cards she had prepared at home.

一名新志工遲到，立刻用交通當藉口。安告訴她，團隊需要準確紀錄，而不是完美的人。學生承認自己只是忘了時間。安謝謝她說實話，協助安排下一個有空的班次。澄從教室後方看著，想起最初幾週安多麼害怕犯錯。如今她能要求誠實，卻不讓任何人覺得渺小。學生隔天提早到，還帶來在家準備好的空白卡。

After class, a volunteer asked An for a text she could send to her family explaining the project. An wrote a short message but made sure it did not promise free professional mail service. The volunteer thanked her and invited the group to dinner. There would be steak for some guests and vegetables for others. An said she was somewhat tired and would join another time. Chen did not make a face when she declined. He simply said he was tired too. They packed away the blackboard and left together, pleased that the new class had learned more than a set of rules.

下課後，一名志工想要一段文字，好向家人解釋計畫。安寫了簡短訊息，特別避免承諾免費專業郵務。志工謝過她，邀大家吃晚餐，有人吃牛排，有人吃蔬菜。安說自己有點累，下次再參加。澄沒有因她拒絕而失望，只說他也累了。他們收起黑板一起離開，為新志工學到的不只是一套規則而感到高興。

At the end of the training, An invited every new volunteer to ask one question. Some asked about broken envelopes; others asked how to support people who had changed their minds. One wanted to know what happened if the team leaders graduated. An showed them the handover plan and introduced the librarian who would supervise the next term. She could see relief on several faces. Chen said a project was strongest when its rules did not depend on one person's memory. An agreed. It was satisfying to build something that could continue without turning its founders into heroes who could never leave.

訓練最後，安邀每名新志工提出一個問題。有人問信封破損怎麼辦，也有人問如何支持改變心意的人。一名學生想知道負責人畢業後怎麼辦。安展示交接計畫，介紹下學期負責督導的圖書館員。她看見幾張臉露出安心神情。澄說，計畫最穩固的時候，是規則不再只靠一個人的記憶。安同意了。能建立一件繼續運作的事很令人滿足，而創辦人也不必變成永遠不能離開的英雄。

On the walk home, An told Chen she had worried about becoming a teacher. She feared making a mistake in front of people. Chen answered that showing how to correct a mistake was part of good teaching. She thought about that and laughed. “So even my bad handwriting might have a use?” “Especially if you let people ask what it says,” he replied. The sky was clear above them. There were no urgent cards to carry and no meeting to win. An felt peaceful in the quiet space they had worked so hard to make.

回家路上，安告訴澄自己原本擔心扮演老師，害怕在人前犯錯。澄回答，示範如何改正錯誤，也是好的教學。她想了想便笑了。「所以我難看的字也可能有用？」他說：「特別是妳願意讓人問妳寫了什麼。」頭頂天空晴朗。沒有急件要送，也沒有會議要贏。安在兩人辛苦創造的安靜空間裡，感到平和。
