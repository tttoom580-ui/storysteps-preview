紫色緞帶

A Purple Ribbon

寄給明天的兩張明信片 · 第 13 / 25 章 · 1000–2000 · 英中對照 · 校訂中

用互動版查詞、朗讀與收藏

A purple ribbon tied around a metal box brought the next question. A student asked the team to send it to her old teacher, but the box also held a small treasure from her childhood. The student had worked part-time for a clothing brand and wanted to show she had earned her degree. An asked how much the package weighed, then realized Slow Post handled cards, not heavy gifts. “We can send a note,” she said, “and you can carry the gift yourself.” The student seemed against the idea at first, then agreed it would make the meeting more personal.

綁著紫色緞帶的金屬盒帶來下一個問題。一位學生想請小組把盒子送給舊老師，裡面還有她童年的小寶物。她曾在服飾品牌打工，如今想讓老師知道自己取得學位。安問包裹多重，隨即想起慢遞只處理卡片，不送重物。「我們可以寄一張便條，」她說，「禮物由妳親自帶去。」學生起初反對，後來同意親自拜訪會更有意義。

At the same table, a gentleman from the science department explained why even a gram of extra weight mattered in an aircraft. He had once spent a night asleep at an airport during a canceled flight. His story made Chen laugh, because their small project had no planes and no large machines. Another visitor described a prince in a storybook who had rejected a letter from a princess. A child nearby was more interested in a spider moving along the wall. An listened to them all. She was learning that people brought their whole lives to a simple writing table.

同桌一位科學院的紳士解釋，即使飛機上多一公克重量也很重要。他曾因航班取消而在機場睡過一夜。澄聽了笑起來，因為他們的小計畫既沒有飛機，也沒有大型機器。另一名訪客說起童話裡的王子拒收公主來信。附近一個孩子則只對牆上爬行的蜘蛛感興趣。安一一聽著。她漸漸明白，人們會把整個人生帶到這張簡單的寫字桌前。

The student weighed the purple box and said it held only a small amount of metal. She was disappointed when the team still refused to carry it. Chen showed her the written rule and explained that the limit applied to everyone, not just heavy packages. “You can reject my plan,” An told her, “but we cannot break the rule for one person.” After a pause the student offered a better idea: she would send a small note asking the teacher to meet. When they compared the names, the contact details were a match. The plan would keep both people free to choose.

學生秤了紫色盒子，說裡面只有少量金屬。即使如此，團隊仍拒絕代送，她很失望。澄展示書面規則，解釋限制適用於所有人，而不只重量大的包裹。「妳可以拒絕我的方案，」安說，「但我們不能為一個人破例。」停頓片刻後，學生提出更好的辦法：先寄簡短便條，詢問老師願不願意見面。核對姓名後，聯絡資料確實相符。這個方式讓雙方都能自由選擇。

Chen showed the student how to seal the note without hiding a gift inside. The teacher would be able to decide whether to meet before knowing about the box. An also offered to prepare a blank reply card, so the teacher would not feel forced to answer in person. The student agreed and placed the card in the correct tray. She said the process was less exciting than she expected, but she felt calmer. An thought calm might be a better beginning for an important meeting than surprise.

澄示範如何封好便條，不把禮物偷偷塞進去。老師能在知道盒子內容前，自行決定是否見面。安也準備一張空白回覆卡，讓老師不必被迫當面回答。學生同意，把卡放進正確托盤。她說過程比預期不刺激，卻覺得更安心。安認為對重要會面而言，安心也許比驚喜更適合作為開端。

The teacher replied that she was happy to meet her former student but did not want anyone to make an event out of it. An respected the answer. She explained the strict limit to the student and offered a quiet meeting room. The student agreed. Before the visit, she bathed, chose her best jacket, and practiced what she wanted to say. Chen watched her check a list and smiled at An. “Good leadership,” he whispered, “sometimes means arranging a room and then leaving people alone.” An wrote the sentence down because it felt true.

老師回覆，自己樂意見以前的學生，但不希望有人把見面辦成活動。安尊重她的回答，向學生說明嚴格界線，並提供安靜的會議室。學生同意了。見面前，她洗了澡、挑好最喜歡的外套，也練習要說的話。澄看著她核對清單，朝安笑了笑。「好的領導，」他低聲說，「有時就是安排好房間，然後讓人們獨處。」安把這句話記下，因為她覺得很對。

Before the meeting, the student said she needed to bathe and change after work. An agreed to move the time. Waiting did not make the visit less important. Outside, a natural breeze moved the trees, and a child drew a purple kite on the pavement. Chen helped An set out two chairs but left the door open for the teacher to decide whether to enter. An thought about how much they had learned. At first, she had wanted to carry the most interesting things herself. Now she was glad when people could carry their own stories and choose where to take them.

見面前，學生說她工作後需要洗澡換衣服。安同意改時間，等待不會使會面變得不重要。外頭自然的微風吹動樹木，孩子在地上畫紫色風箏。澄幫安擺好兩張椅子，門仍保持打開，讓老師自己決定要不要進來。安想到他們學會了多少事。一開始，她想親手搬運最有意思的東西。如今，看見人們能自己帶著故事、選擇走向何處，她反而更高興。

After the teacher and student met, both came out smiling. Neither told the team what they had talked about. That was fine. An and Chen cleaned the table together. He found a piece of ribbon caught beneath a chair, handed it to her, and asked whether she was free on Sunday afternoon. She looked at him, surprised. “For project work?” she asked. “No. For a walk, if you want one.” She took a breath. “I want one,” she said, “but I have an assignment first.” They agreed on a time that suited them both.

老師與學生見面後，兩人都帶著笑容走出來，誰也沒告訴團隊談了什麼。這樣就很好。安與澄一起收拾桌子。他在椅子下發現一段緞帶，遞給安，接著問她星期天下午有沒有空。安驚訝地看他。「做專案嗎？」她問。「不是。散步，妳願意的話。」她吸了口氣。「我願意，」她說，「不過我要先完成作業。」他們約好了雙方都方便的時間。
