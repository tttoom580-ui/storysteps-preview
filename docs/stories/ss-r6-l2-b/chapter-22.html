不與颱風賽跑

The Storm We Did Not Race

寄給明天的兩張明信片 · 第 22 / 25 章 · 1000–2000 · 英中對照 · 校訂中

用互動版查詞、朗讀與收藏

The city warned of a typhoon two days before the autumn card event. Some volunteers thought the team could meet anywhere indoors, but the building manager said no room was safe until it had been checked. A fisherman from the coast called to say the sea was already wild. Chen studied the weather notice and asked everyone to stop planned deliveries. A student who loved adventure said he could move faster than the storm. An replied that being brave was not the same as pretending risk did not exist. They canceled the event and informed every registered visitor.

秋季卡片活動前兩天，城市發布颱風警報。有志工認為換到任何室內場地都可以，但大樓管理員表示，未經檢查不能認定安全。一位海岸漁夫打電話說，海面已十分狂暴。澄查看天氣通知，請大家停止預定投遞。一名愛冒險的學生說自己跑得比風暴快。安回答，勇敢不等於假裝風險不存在。團隊取消活動，通知所有已登記的訪客。

A volunteer slipped on a wet stair and damaged a small box. He looked frightened and offered to pay the entire bill. Chen examined the cards first. Only the outer cover was torn, so they could solve the problem with a new sleeve. An told the volunteer to sit down and check for pain. She did not doubt his good intentions, but she wanted him to understand why stopping was necessary. A golden sticker had fallen from the box; a silver one was still on it. Neither sticker was worth a person getting hurt.

一名志工在濕樓梯上滑倒，摔壞一個小盒子。他嚇壞了，主動表示要支付全部費用。澄先檢查卡片，發現只有外層封套破損，可以換新套解決。安請志工坐下，確認有沒有受傷。她不懷疑他的好意，但希望他理解為什麼必須停止工作。盒子上的金色貼紙掉了，銀色那張還在。沒有任何貼紙值得讓人受傷。

A student in part-time employment at an outdoor center called to ask whether the canceled day could be moved. Her mate from a hiking group had taken a hard slip near a fallen tree, and she knew the danger of rushing. An thanked her for the opinion and explained that the team would wait for a safety report. A volunteer who had once worked as a wildlife hunter offered to check the path, but Chen said only authorized staff should decide whether it was open. They could not solve a weather problem by sending more students into the forest. Even the smallest mistake could damage a person's life.

一名在戶外中心兼職受僱的學生來電，詢問取消的活動能否改期。她的健行夥伴曾在倒樹附近滑倒，因此了解倉促行動的危險。安謝謝她的意見，說團隊會等待安全報告。一名曾從事野外追蹤的志工提出去檢查路況，澄卻表示只有獲授權的工作人員可以決定是否開放道路。他們不能靠派更多學生進森林來解決天氣問題。再小的錯誤都可能傷害一個人的生活。

The following morning, the student who had fallen called to say he was fine. An thanked him for the news but still asked him not to return until the building reopened. She checked whether every volunteer had received the cancellation notice. One message had gone to an old address, so Chen corrected it and sent a new notice without revealing any personal story. The team had lost a day of activity, but no one was missing and no card had been abandoned. An considered that a result worth recording.

隔天早上，跌倒的學生來電說他沒事。安謝謝他報平安，仍請他等大樓重新開放再來。她確認每位志工都收到取消通知，發現有一則寄往舊地址。澄修正後重發通知，沒有揭露任何私人故事。團隊失去一天活動，卻沒有人失聯，也沒有卡片被遺棄。安認為這是值得記錄的成果。

During the storm the team worked by phone, not on the road. A volunteer in temporary employment at the city office helped check which messages had been received. Another student drank cola while writing notices. He was the former hunting club's best runner, but even he agreed to stay home. In an instant, an emergency call came from a friend whose partner was trapped by flooding. An directed him to the proper rescue service rather than sending students into danger. The rescue workers took over. An felt grateful that the team knew the limits of its work.

風雨期間，小組只透過電話工作，不上路。一名在市府短期受僱的志工協助確認通知是否送達；另一個學生喝著可樂寫公告。他曾是野外追蹤社最好的跑者，這次也同意留在家裡。忽然，朋友來了緊急電話，說伴侶受洪水圍困。安引導他聯絡正式救援單位，而不是派學生冒險。救援人員接手了。安慶幸團隊知道自己的工作界線。

When the city finally announced that public rooms were safe, An did not set a new date at once. She first asked the volunteers whether they still had time. Some were studying for exams, and another had accepted extra work. She made a smaller schedule that respected their choices. The people who could not join received a simple thank-you note, not a complaint. Chen helped her move the remaining cards to a dry cupboard. They checked the list and saw that not a single private letter had been left in the damaged hall. It was the kind of quiet success that never appeared in photographs.

市府終於宣布公共房間安全後，安沒有立刻訂新日期，而是先問志工是否仍有時間。有些人正在準備考試，另一人接了額外工作。她重新安排較小規模的班表，尊重每個選擇。無法參加的人收到的是簡單感謝，不是抱怨。澄幫她把剩下的卡片移到乾燥櫃子裡。核對清單後，他們確認沒有一封私信留在受損大廳。這種安靜的成功，往往不會出現在照片裡。

When the latter part of the storm passed, people sent their opinions about the canceled day. Most thanked the team; some felt disappointed. An answered each message without arguing. She wrote that the possibility of a safe event would be reviewed after the city reopened public buildings. Chen called that evening and asked if she had eaten. She said yes, then asked the same question. They laughed at how ordinary their care had become. The storm was not a test of love, and neither needed to prove affection by standing in danger.

風暴後段過去後，人們陸續送來對取消活動的意見。大部分表示感謝，也有人失望。安一一回覆，沒有爭辯。她說等市府重新開放公共建築後，才會評估安全復辦的可能。當晚澄打電話問她吃過飯沒，她說吃了，也反問同樣的問題。兩人笑自己關心彼此的方式已變得這麼日常。風暴不是愛情考驗，誰也不必站在危險裡證明喜歡。
