草地郵卡日

The Outdoor Post Fair

寄給明天的兩張明信片 · 第 10 / 25 章 · 1000–2000 · 英中對照 · 校訂中

用互動版查詞、朗讀與收藏

The first outdoor Slow Post fair began during the university's short vacation. There were biscuits, fruit, and a musical group on the grass. A teacher brought a toy fox and a wooden goose for children to draw. An wanted to gather everyone beneath a large tree, but Chen measured the available space with a meter stick. Each table needed enough room for a wheelchair to pass. His proper planning made the fair look less crowded than other events. A snail crawled across the grass while the children watched. An laughed that the snail might be the project's best symbol.

大學短假期間，第一場戶外慢遞日開始了。草地上有餅乾、水果和音樂表演團體。一位老師帶來狐狸玩偶與木製鵝，讓孩子們照著畫。安想把所有人聚到大樹下，澄卻先用公尺尺量出可用空間。每張桌子之間都必須留出輪椅可以通行的距離。他周全的規劃讓活動看起來不那麼擁擠。孩子們看著一隻蝸牛爬過草地，安笑說牠可能才是計畫最好的象徵。

A baker offered pieces of yam cake at a low price per unit. Some volunteers ignored his sign and thought the cakes were free, so An placed a clear label on the basket. Nearby, a student tried to repair a paper kite with glue. Chen helped her, but he warned that wet glue could harm a postcard. Another table invited people to write congratulations to someone finishing a hard year. An wrote one for her sister, who had shown courage while changing jobs. She did not put her sister's address on the public board. There were many ways to celebrate without sharing everything.

一位烘焙師以低價、按份販售山藥糕。幾名志工沒注意他的標示，以為蛋糕免費，安便在籃子上放了清楚的價格牌。附近一名學生用膠水修紙風箏，澄幫了忙，卻提醒濕膠水可能傷害明信片。另一張桌子請人寫下給辛苦一年之人的祝賀。安寫給剛勇敢轉職的姊姊，沒有把姊姊的地址放上公開看板。慶祝的方法很多，不必把一切都分享出去。

An almost forgot the biscuit boxes when the wind picked up. A child ran after a paper flag, and his mother called him back before he reached the road. The volunteers did not ignore the warning. They moved the tables farther from traffic and checked the ropes again. A teacher wrote a note of congratulations for the children who had finished their school play. She asked whether the note could be posted in public, and An helped her remove the children's family names first. The children would still see the kind words without losing their privacy.

風起時，安差點忘了收餅乾盒。一個孩子追著紙旗跑，母親在他接近馬路前把他叫回。志工們沒有忽視警告，立即把桌子移得更遠離車流，並再度檢查繩子。一位老師寫下祝賀便條，送給剛完成學校戲劇的孩子。她問能否公開張貼，安先協助去除孩子們的姓氏。這樣他們仍能看見善意的話語，而不失去隱私。

The fair's style was simple. There were no large prizes and no loud advertisement. A gradual line of visitors came to the table to ask questions. One elderly woman wanted to write to her childhood friend but had lost the address. An explained that the team could help write a card but could not search for a private address without permission. The woman thanked her for the answer and decided to keep the card until she could contact the friend herself. Chen admired the choice. It showed that a service could be useful even when it did not deliver something immediately.

活動風格很簡單，沒有大獎，也沒有吵鬧的廣告。訪客陸續排隊到桌前詢問。一位年長女士想寫信給童年好友，卻弄丟了地址。安解釋，小組可以協助寫卡，但不能未經同意查找私人地址。女士謝過回答，決定先保留卡片，等自己聯絡到朋友再寄。澄欣賞這個選擇。這說明服務即使沒有立即完成投遞，也能有所幫助。

Near the closing hour, the fox toy fell beneath a bench. Chen knelt to pick it up, but An reached it first and held it out to the little girl who owned it. The girl thanked her and offered a drawing of the fox in return. An accepted only after asking whether the girl wanted to give it away. The child's mother smiled and said that was the most important part of the day's lesson. Later Chen told An he had enjoyed watching her work. She said she had enjoyed seeing him laugh with children. Neither said anything romantic, yet the quiet warmth stayed with them.

接近收攤時，狐狸玩偶掉到長椅下。澄跪下想撿，安先碰到，便把它交回小主人。女孩道謝，想送一張狐狸畫作。安先問她是否真的願意送出，才接受。女孩的母親笑說，這正是今天最重要的一課。稍後澄告訴安，他很喜歡看她工作的樣子。安說自己也喜歡看他和孩子笑鬧。兩人沒有說任何浪漫的話，溫暖卻留了下來。

After the last visitor left, An found a card addressed to Chen in the new box. The paper was small and the handwriting careful. She could have told him it was there, but she could not read it for him. Instead, she placed it in his private tray with the other items. He noticed her careful hands and said thank you. “I used to think a good project needed great speed,” she admitted. “Now I think it needs the right length of time.” He nodded. Their work had become a place where each could learn from the other, without being pushed into a story neither had chosen.

最後一位訪客離開後，安在新郵箱找到一張寄給澄的卡片。紙張很小，字跡整齊。她本可以立刻告訴他，卻不能替他讀信。她把卡片和其他物品一起放進他的私人托盤。他注意到她小心的動作，道了謝。「我以前以為好計畫要跑得很快，」安坦承。「現在我覺得要給它剛好的時間。」澄點頭。他們的工作已成為彼此學習的地方，無須被推進任何未經選擇的故事。
