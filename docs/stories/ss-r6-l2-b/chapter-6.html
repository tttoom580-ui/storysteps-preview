音樂家的回信

The Musician's Return

寄給明天的兩張明信片 · 第 6 / 25 章 · 1000–2000 · 英中對照 · 校訂中

用互動版查詞、朗讀與收藏

The storm left puddles along the sidewalk, but the team had to examine a new question. An received an official request from the university office. Could Slow Post accept letters from people who did not study there? A crowd of neighbors had gathered near the building to ask. One held a raincoat over a sleeping child. Another carried a picture of a giraffe drawn by his granddaughter. The office wanted a clear term for the service: a student project, not a city post office. An agreed. She could welcome visitors without making promises the team had no power to keep.

暴雨過後，人行道上留下水窪，但團隊又遇到新問題。安收到大學辦公室的正式請求：慢遞可以接受校外人士寄信嗎？一群鄰居已聚在大樓旁詢問。有人用雨衣替睡著的孩子遮雨，也有人拿著孫女畫的長頸鹿。校方希望明確界定服務性質：這是學生計畫，不是市立郵局。安同意了。她可以歡迎訪客，卻不能做出團隊無權履行的承諾。

Their favorite visitor that afternoon was an elderly musician named Mr. Wu. He had once played in a small band in the western part of town. On the eve of his retirement, he had written a page of poetry to his younger self, but he never sent it. Now he wanted to know whether Slow Post could help him deliver it home. “I am not at war with my past,” he said. “I only wish to hear it once more.” The words made An think about the soul of the project. It was not mail alone; it was the chance to meet one's own unfinished thoughts.

那天下午，最受大家喜愛的訪客是年長音樂家吳先生。他曾在城西的小樂團演奏。退休前夕，他寫過一頁詩給年輕時的自己，卻一直沒寄出去。如今他想知道慢遞能否幫他把信送回家。「我不是在和過去作戰，」他說。「只是想再聽一次自己的聲音。」這句話讓安想到計畫的靈魂。它不只是傳遞郵件，也是讓人重新遇見尚未完成的心事。

At the library desk, the official from campus services asked to see the new visitor form. Chen showed him that it contained no space for writing personal messages. The officer suggested a larger heading so the rules would be clear even to people who had never visited the university. An added one, then asked for a larger-print version. Her first design had looked beautiful on her laptop, but the words were too small for an elderly reader. She listened without defending herself. The visitors were helping the team make its rules easier to use. That was worth more than a perfect first design.

圖書館櫃檯前，校園事務承辦人要求看看新的訪客表。澄讓他確認表單沒有填寫私人信件內容的欄位。承辦人建議放大標題，讓從未到過大學的人也看得懂。安加上標題，也製作大字版。她原先的設計在電腦上很好看，但字體對年長讀者太小。她沒有辯解，只是聽取意見。訪客正在協助團隊讓規則更容易使用，這比第一次設計就完美更加重要。

Chen offered to repair the old envelope with a paper sleeve, but he did not touch the letter inside. Mr. Wu was sensitive about his handwriting. An asked whether he wanted to read the poem in public. He replied that he would rather read it alone first. That was enough. They prepared a quiet table by the window, away from the busiest hall. Outside, another storm cloud appeared. The old man looked toward the window, then accepted the envelope. A few minutes later he smiled with wet eyes. Nobody asked what the poem said.

澄提議用紙套修補舊信封，但沒有碰裡面的信。吳先生對自己的筆跡相當敏感。安問他是否想公開朗讀詩作，他說希望先獨自讀一遍。這個回答已經足夠。他們在窗邊安排一張安靜的桌子，遠離繁忙的走道。窗外又飄來一片烏雲。老人朝窗外望了望，接著接過信封。幾分鐘後，他眼中帶淚地笑了。沒有人追問詩裡寫了什麼。

Before Mr. Wu left, he showed An a concert program from his younger days. The paper was worn at the edges, and the names had grown faint. He said he had once been afraid that growing old would make his music disappear. Now he saw that a song could be remembered by one person and still matter. An did not promise to save every memory. She offered him a fresh sleeve for the program and asked whether he would like to keep it. He nodded. Chen wrote down only the request for storage help, not the old man's private story.

吳先生離開前，拿出年輕時的音樂會節目單給安看。紙邊已磨損，名字也逐漸褪色。他說自己曾害怕變老後音樂就會消失，現在卻明白，即使只有一個人記得一首歌，它仍然有意義。安沒有承諾保存每一段回憶，只拿出新的保護套，問他要不要使用。他點頭。澄只記下提供保護套的要求，沒有記錄老人家的私人故事。

At closing time, An and Chen wrote a new rule for outside visitors. Each person would receive the same explanation, no matter where they lived. The university could help with space, but the team would not promise professional delivery. A neighbor who made maps offered to draw safe walking routes. Another volunteer checked whether the small door could open wide enough for a wheelchair. An was tired, yet she felt a new kind of strength. When Chen asked if she wanted to walk home together, she said yes because she liked his company, not because the project needed one more helper.

打烊時，安與澄替校外訪客訂下新規則。無論住在哪裡，每個人都會收到同樣的說明。大學可以提供場地，但小組不能承諾專業郵務配送。一位會畫地圖的鄰居主動提供安全步行路線，另一名志工則確認小門能否讓輪椅通行。安很疲倦，卻感受到新的力量。澄問她要不要一起走回家，她答應了，理由是喜歡和他相處，而不是計畫又需要多一名幫手。
