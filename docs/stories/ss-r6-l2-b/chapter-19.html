不能下載的信

The Letter That Could Not Be Downloaded

寄給明天的兩張明信片 · 第 19 / 25 章 · 1000–2000 · 英中對照 · 校訂中

用互動版查詞、朗讀與收藏

The computer club offered a new delivery system. It would let an employee download a list of addresses and send automatic notices. An asked for a discussion before the team accepted it. “An effective tool is not always a necessary tool,” she said. The latest version looked clever, but it could store private details longer than people wished. Chen described the difficulty of removing data once copies had been made. A guest from the law school agreed. Their policy needed to protect people even when a fast solution seemed easier.

電腦社提供一套新投遞系統，能讓員工下載地址清單並自動傳送通知。安要求團隊討論後再決定是否採用。「有效的工具不一定就是必要的工具，」她說。最新版看起來很聰明，卻可能把私人資料保留得比寄件人期望更久。澄說明資料一旦被複製，就很難徹底移除。法律系來賓表示同意。即使快速方法看起來更容易，制度也必須保護當事人。

The meeting took place over pizza, and someone laid a mat by the door for wet shoes. An tried to describe the choice clearly. They could keep a small record of delivery dates without saving the words inside each letter. A student spoke about a traditional prayer card his grandmother had written. Another remembered struggling alone through a long illness. Both wanted to control their own stories. No software could promise that for them unless the team designed its work around consent. Chen wrote a smaller plan on the board: store only what is truly needed.

會議邊吃披薩邊進行，有人在門口放了地墊讓濕鞋踩著。安努力把選擇說清楚：他們可以只保存投遞日期，不儲存每封信內的文字。一名學生提起祖母寫的傳統祈禱卡，另一人想起自己獨自與長期疾病搏鬥的日子。兩人都想掌握自己的故事。除非團隊以同意為核心設計工作，否則軟體無法替他們保證。澄在白板上寫出較小的方案：只保存真正必要的資料。

The students tested the new computer form with made-up addresses and names. A simple mistake showed how one field could have an unexpected effect: when a letter was marked returned, the program still sent a reminder to its old receiver. Chen stopped the test and asked the club to fix the rule. The programmers agreed that a return request must end further contact. An watched them correct the problem and felt grateful they had found it before using real information. She wrote the result into the weekly record, including the mistake and the change that solved it.

學生使用虛構姓名與地址測試新電腦表單。一個簡單錯誤讓大家看到欄位可能產生意外效果：信件標示退回後，程式仍向舊收件人發提醒。澄中止測試，請社團修改規則。程式設計者同意，退回要求必須終止後續聯絡。安看著問題被修好，慶幸大家在使用真實資料前就發現它。她把結果寫進週紀錄，包含錯誤本身與解決方式。

The programmers gave An a short report listing each change to the test system. She asked them to keep it with the guide, so the next group would know why the rules existed. Chen explained that useful records were not the same as storing every private detail. A good record could show that a mistake had been found and fixed without revealing who wrote a letter. An saw the distinction clearly. Technology could support careful human choices, but it could not replace the need to make those choices.

程式設計學生交給安一份短報告，列出測試系統的每項修改。她請他們把報告和指南放在一起，讓下一組知道規則的由來。澄解釋，有用的紀錄不等於保存每項私人細節。好的紀錄可以證明某個錯誤曾被發現與修正，卻不必揭露寫信者身分。安更清楚地看見這個差別。科技能支持謹慎的人類選擇，卻不能代替人做選擇。

During the break, a volunteer complained of a toothache and went home early. An told him not to struggle through his shift for the sake of the team. She felt a small sense of relief when he accepted the offer. The computer club's leader then agreed to change the system. It would not download entire private messages, and users could request the removal of their contact details. The club would test each feature with made-up data. An thanked him for being willing to change his design, and he said he wanted to build something people could truly trust.

休息時，一名志工因牙痛提早回家。安請他別為團隊硬撐班表。聽見他願意離開休息，她也鬆了口氣。電腦社社長隨後同意修改系統：它不會下載完整私信，使用者也能要求移除聯絡資料。社團會使用虛構資料測試每項功能。安感謝他願意調整設計，他說自己也希望做出人們真正能信任的東西。

That afternoon An helped a student write to her future self. The student said she feared changing her mind about school and career. An asked whether she wanted the letter kept for a month or a year. The student chose a year. Chen showed her how to write a date on the outside without making the message public. An liked the way this small service gave people time. A person did not need to decide their entire future in one afternoon. Sometimes the best gift was a safe place to leave a question until one was ready to meet it again.

當天下午，安協助一名學生寫信給未來的自己。她說害怕自己改變對學業與職涯的想法。安問她希望保存一個月還是一年，學生選了一年。澄教她如何把日期寫在外面，不公開信件內容。安喜歡這項小服務給人的時間。一個人不必在某個下午就決定全部未來。有時最好的禮物，就是替一個問題找到安全地方，直到主人準備好再與它相遇。

That night Chen asked An whether she thought they were becoming too serious. She laughed. “I do not think making careful choices means we cannot be happy.” They bought warm buns from a small street shop and sat on a bench. She told him she liked working with him, but she also liked the quiet moments when they were simply two people. He said he felt the same. They did not make a big speech. Instead, they agreed to keep one evening a week free of project talk. It was a small promise they could both keep.

那天夜裡，澄問安是否覺得兩人變得太嚴肅。她笑了。「謹慎做選擇，不代表不能快樂。」他們在街邊買了熱包子，坐到長椅上。安說自己喜歡和他一起工作，也喜歡兩人只是普通人的安靜時光。他說他也一樣。他們沒有發表長篇宣言，而是約好每週留一個晚上完全不談專案。那是一個雙方都能做到的小承諾。
