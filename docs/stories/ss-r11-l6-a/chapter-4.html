The Charity Ledger

王室療養院，不准替病人說謊 · 第 4 / 25 章 · 5000–6000 · 英中對照

As the inquiry continued, the question of who could speak grew more complicated. The trust's name appeared on the ward's public donation board, yet its private ledger charged patients for care advertised as free. Adela asked the accountant for every supporting receipt. The first question was not whether the palace was embarrassed, but whose voice the institution had ignored. Adela had spent years auditing records in another life; that knowledge taught her how easily a neat form could conceal a human decision. She asked every visitor to distinguish what they had actually seen from what someone had merely told them. The cleaner used bleach on ordinary floors but kept it away from sealed samples. The attorney demanded a comprehensive inventory of pigments and purchases. A credible account distinguished what the witness saw from what she guessed. The workers showed dedication by carrying neighbors' belongings above the floodwater. A narrow dresser concealed an older supply book behind its drawers. The note was placed in the public inspection folder, where the time, author, and reason for every correction could be checked. Adela did not let a private conversation replace that record; personal trust mattered, but verifiable decisions mattered more.

隨著調查持續，誰有權說話的問題變得更加複雜。信託名稱出現在病房公開捐款板上，私人帳冊卻向病人收取宣稱免費的照護費。艾黛拉要求會計提出所有佐證收據。 第一個問題不是王宮會不會難堪，而是制度忽視了誰的聲音。艾黛拉在另一段人生裡多年稽核紀錄，知道整齊表單多麼容易藏住人的選擇。她要求每位來訪者分清親眼所見與道聽塗說。 清潔工以漂白水清理一般地板，卻避免讓它接觸封存樣本。 律師要求完整盤點顏料與採購紀錄。 可信的證詞會區分親眼所見與推測。 工人把鄰居物品搬離積水，展現了投入與責任心。 狹窄的五斗櫃抽屜後藏著一本舊進貨簿。 這份記錄放入公開檢查資料夾，每次更正的時間、作者和原因都可以查核。艾黛拉不讓私下對話取代這份紀錄；私人信任重要，可驗證的決定更重要。

The investigators separated their observations from the explanations offered by interested officials. The ward cleaner recognized an altered stamp because she used the same cupboard for supplies. Her memory was useful, but Nia sought dated photographs before presenting it as fact. Sera drew a line through the center of her notebook. On the left she copied verified dates; on the right she wrote the questions raised by those dates. The difference seemed small until two accounts contradicted one another. With the columns separated, neither witness had to be called a liar before the records were examined. Sera's familiarity with older nursing records helped her identify the unusual marking fluid. The old hierarchy had encouraged apprentices to hide errors from supervisors. Their landlady insisted that her receipt remain in her own hands. The abandoned riverside garden looked lush after the rain. Several residents paused to mourn a clerk lost in an earlier flood.

調查員將親眼觀察與利害關係官員提出的解釋分開記錄。病房清潔工認出被改過的章戳，因她平常用同一個櫥櫃取用品。她的記憶有用，但妮雅先找有日期的照片才把它列為事實。 賽拉在筆記本中間畫線，左邊抄已核實日期，右邊寫日期引發的疑問。這個差別起初很小，直到兩份說法互相矛盾。欄位分開後，便不用在檢查紀錄前先把任何證人稱作說謊者。 賽拉熟悉舊護理紀錄，因此認出那種不尋常的標記液。 舊有階級制度讓學徒不敢向主管報告錯誤。 房東太太堅持收據仍由自己保管。 雨後荒廢的河畔花園仍顯得草木茂盛。 幾名居民停下來悼念早年洪災中過世的護理員。

The next decision mattered because it could change an ordinary patient’s life before the case reached a verdict. Corven accused Adela of creating panic and cited her earlier signature on the payment system. She acknowledged that signature openly rather than rewriting the history of her position. Vale's representatives offered an attractive shortcut: a private agreement, a generous payment, and silence from everyone who accepted. The proposal divided the room. Adela reminded the families that no one had to choose that day, and independent advice would be available before any document was signed. The clinic's new policy sought to nurture trust by giving every patient a voice. An overflow of canal water reached the warehouse entrance. Evin said she had happened to overhear an argument about a missing receipt. Adela would not refute an allegation until its source was identified. A seagull landed on the flooded stone step outside the clinic.

下一項決策之所以重要，是因為它可能在判決前就改變普通病人的生活。柯文指責艾黛拉製造恐慌，並提出她過去在收費制度上的簽名。她公開承認簽過字，沒有試圖改寫自己職務的歷史。 維爾的代表提出誘人的捷徑：私下協議、豐厚付款，以及接受者的沉默承諾。提案讓眾人意見分裂。艾黛拉提醒家屬不必當天決定，在任何簽名前都能取得獨立意見。 診所的新政策希望讓每位病人都能發聲，藉此培養信任。 運河溢流的水漫到倉庫入口。 艾文說她碰巧聽見有人為失蹤收據爭吵。 艾黛拉在找出指控來源前，不會急著反駁。 一隻海鷗落在記錄店外被水淹過的石階上。

Before closing the daily record, Adela asked what remained uncertain and whose consent had not yet been heard. A judge ordered an independent account review. That night, the cleaner found a missing patient's name written beneath the final total. The final lamp remained lit as the staff exchanged shifts. Outside, the city kept moving: carts crossed bridges, children hurried home, and vendors closed their stalls. The investigation affected them all, whether or not they had heard Adela’s name. That thought made careful work feel larger than the palace. The warehouse superintendent unlocked the pigment accounts for inspection. A distant symphony rehearsal continued in the shelter above the canal. The clinic lent a thermometer to check a feverish worker. The city's turmoil did not justify seizing private records without a warrant. Residents requested wholesome meals for children waiting in the shelter.

結束當日紀錄前，艾黛拉詢問哪些地方仍不確定，以及誰的同意還沒有被聽見。法官下令獨立查帳。當晚，清潔工在總數下方發現一名失蹤病人的姓名。 最後一盞燈在交班時仍亮著。城裡照常運轉：貨車過橋、孩子匆忙回家、攤販收起貨物。調查影響所有人，不管他們是否聽過艾黛拉的名字。這讓謹慎工作顯得比王宮更重要。 倉庫管理員打開顏料帳冊供人查驗。 運河上方避難所仍傳來遠處交響樂排練聲。 診所借出一支溫度計，檢查發燒的工人。 城市動亂也不能合理化沒有搜索令就扣押私人紀錄。 居民要求為在避難所等候的孩子提供健康餐食。
