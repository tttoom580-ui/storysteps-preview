As the inquiry continued, the question of who could speak grew more complicated. A porter admitted that certain patients had been taken to a room with no number during night shifts. He feared losing his wages if the palace discovered he had spoken. The ward had learned to call uncertainty a weakness. Adela now used it as a safeguard: a doubtful claim went into a separate column until another person checked it. Sera saw how much slower that made the work. She also saw the first frightened witness return voluntarily the following morning. The manager continued to allege that no night deliveries had occurred. The town hoped to beautify the abandoned bank with a public reading garden. A clerk corrected the caption beneath the mill's old photograph. The argument reached its climax when Dalia produced the shift register. Sera used a clean tool to extract a fiber from the form's edge.

隨著調查持續，誰有權說話的問題變得更加複雜。搬運員坦承，夜班曾將部分病人送往沒有門牌的房間。他害怕王宮知道自己開口後就失去薪水。 病房過去總把不確定視為弱點。艾黛拉如今將它當成保護措施：存疑的說法先放入獨立欄位，等另一個人查核。賽拉發現工作因此慢了許多，也看見第一名害怕的證人隔天自願回來。 經理仍宣稱夜間沒有任何貨運。 小鎮希望把荒廢河岸美化成公共閱讀花園。 一名護理員更正了物資廠舊照片下的說明文字。 達莉亞拿出排班表時，爭論達到高潮。 賽拉用乾淨工具從紙邊抽取纖維。

The investigators separated their observations from the explanations offered by interested officials. Sera found a worn floor plan showing an extra storage chamber. When the team entered with an observer, they found adjustable beds and a locked cabinet of blank consent forms. The team tested each claim against a second record whenever possible. When no second record existed, they wrote that limitation plainly. Adela found the discipline exhausting and indispensable in equal measure. The mill sponsored extracurricular classes for apprentices who wished to learn accounting. The riverside households were hospitable despite their distrust of the palace. Fen's usually jolly manner faded when the missing wages were mentioned. A gardener offered to mow the path leading to the waste channel. The driver with the curled mustache refused to sign an invented statement.

調查員將親眼觀察與利害關係官員提出的解釋分開記錄。賽拉找到磨損的樓層圖，顯示多一間儲藏室。團隊在觀察員陪同下進入，看見可調式病床與裝滿空白同意書的上鎖櫃子。團隊盡可能以第二份紀錄檢驗每項說法。沒有第二份時，就如實寫下限制。艾黛拉覺得這種紀律令人疲憊，卻又不可或缺。 物資廠贊助想學會計的學徒參加課外課程。 河邊住戶雖不信宮廷，仍熱情款待訪客。 提到欠薪時，向來快活的芬恩不再說笑。 園丁主動提出修剪通往廢料水道的小路。 留捲鬍子的車夫拒絕在捏造的證詞上簽名。

The next decision mattered because it could change an ordinary patient’s life before the case reached a verdict. Corven said the room was a temporary recovery area. Adela did not dismiss that explanation; she requested dates, patient names and independent medical assessments to test it. A crowd gathered outside, angry enough to accept almost any answer. Dalia asked Adela not to nag workers who were already answering carefully. The mill garden was a small oasis between noisy workshops. Every ounce of imported violet pigment appeared in a separate account. The missing carriage reportedly stopped at the outskirts of the capital. Parliament had granted mills the right to elect safety representatives.

下一項決策之所以重要，是因為它可能在判決前就改變普通病人的生活。柯文稱那是臨時恢復室。艾黛拉沒有立刻否定，而是要求日期、病人姓名及獨立醫療評估加以檢驗。 外面聚起憤怒的人群，幾乎任何答案都願意接受。 達莉亞請艾黛拉別再嘮叨已仔細作答的工人。 物資廠花園是喧鬧工坊間的一小片綠洲。 每盎司進口紫色顏料都列在獨立帳目裡。 據報失蹤馬車曾停在首都郊區。 議會曾賦予物資廠選舉安全代表的權利。

Before closing the daily record, Adela asked what remained uncertain and whose consent had not yet been heard. A page torn from the night log bore the initials of the royal contractor, Vale. Adela sealed the page and requested its original binding. By evening the hospital looked much the same as it had that morning. The corridors were still narrow, the work still hard, and most questions still open. Yet one person had been allowed to choose where to stand and what to say. Adela treated that small change as a duty, not a victory speech. Adela showed him the sample held against the evening sun. 'A sheet may lie about its author,' she said, 'but the people who made it leave traces worth listening to.' A ruthless buyer had threatened to cancel all local orders. The village sculptor made small clay seals for school demonstrations. Adela valued the simplicity of the fiber test over a grand accusation. Two simultaneous orders used identical quantities but different customer names. A slump in public bulletin sales had left several workers unpaid.

結束當日紀錄前，艾黛拉詢問哪些地方仍不確定，以及誰的同意還沒有被聽見。夜班紀錄被撕下的一頁留下王室承包商維爾的姓名縮寫。艾黛拉封存頁面，並要求取回原始裝訂冊。 傍晚時醫院看來與早上一樣，走廊仍窄、工作仍難、問題大多未解。但至少一個人得以決定自己站在哪裡、說什麼話。艾黛拉把這小改變看成責任，不是值得慶功的演說。 」 一名無情買主威脅取消全部當地訂單。 村中雕塑家製作小陶印，供學校示範。 艾黛拉重視纖維測試的簡明，而不是宏大的指控。 兩筆同時下達的訂單數量相同，客戶姓名卻不同。 醫療通報銷售下滑使數名工人領不到工資。
