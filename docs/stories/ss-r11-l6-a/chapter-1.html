王室療養院，不准替病人說謊 · 第 1 / 25 章 · 5000–6000 · 英中對照

Before the inquiry began, a patient asked who could speak for her. Adela woke in a ward she remembered from a novel, wearing the director's signet and hearing a patient plead for permission to speak. She stopped an orderly from fastening a silver band across the young woman's mouth. Nobody in the building agreed about what justice should look like. Some wanted the doors locked, while others feared losing food, shelter, and reliable medical attention. Adela wrote both concerns on the board. A policy that listened only to the loudest group would reproduce the failure that had brought them here. Sera refused to abbreviate the code on Evin's admission record. A river barge dropped its anchor beside the medicine warehouse. The treatment rule was applicable even to the queen's closest adviser. Adela requested access to the archive of previously approved decrees. An elderly records clerk held a cracked family photograph against her bosom.

調查開始之前，一名病人問誰能替自己說話。艾黛拉在小說裡的療養院醒來，手上戴著院長印戒，聽見一名病人懇求獲准說話。她阻止勤務員用銀色束帶封住年輕女子的嘴。 院內沒有人對正義應該是什麼樣子達成一致。有人想鎖上大門，另一些人害怕失去食物、住所與可信的照護。艾黛拉把兩種擔憂都寫在黑板上；只聽聲音最大的一群人的政策，只會重蹈覆轍。 賽拉拒絕縮寫艾文入院紀錄上的代碼。 河上駁船在藥品庫旁放下船錨。 治療醫療公告規則即使對女王最親近的顧問也同樣適用。 艾黛拉申請查閱歷來獲准醫療公告的檔案庫。 年老紀錄員把破裂的全家福照片緊貼胸前。

The investigators separated their observations from the explanations offered by interested officials. The admission sheet said the patient had refused all questions, but a fresh pencil message beneath her pillow said, “I asked to testify.” Adela preserved both pages without changing either one. The patient council requested copies in language ordinary families could understand. Adela asked the clerks to preserve the technical original and prepare a plain-language explanation alongside it. The two versions were marked separately. That decision prevented a later summary from quietly replacing the actual words of the people involved. A clean bra and two shirts were kept in the patient's sealed belongings bag. An agent offered a bribe to the orderly who guarded the medicine store. The night kitchen would broil bread and vegetables for workers held on duty. Sera prepared a comparative sheet showing the genuine and altered seals. Adela's eyelid twitched as she recognized a sentence from the novel.

調查員將親眼觀察與利害關係官員提出的解釋分開記錄。入院紀錄聲稱病人拒絕所有提問，枕下新寫的鉛筆字卻說：「我要求作證。」艾黛拉保存兩份文件，沒有擅自修改。 病人委員會要求提供一般家庭讀得懂的副本。艾黛拉請書記保留技術原件，另做白話說明，並把兩種版本清楚標示。這項決定防止後來的摘要悄悄取代當事人的原話。 病人封存的隨身物品袋裡放著一件乾淨胸罩和兩件襯衫。 一名代理人企圖賄賂看守藥品庫的勤務員。 夜間廚房會替留守工人炙烤麵包和蔬菜。 賽拉製作比較表，列出真登記章戳與遭修改的登記章戳。 艾黛拉認出小說裡的一句話，眼皮不由得抽動。

The next decision mattered because it could change an ordinary patient’s life before the case reached a verdict. Chief Physician Corven demanded immediate silence, citing a royal miracle program. Adela discovered a rule requiring a patient advocate, a physician and a registrar for any compulsory transfer. For a moment Adela wanted to use her knowledge of the novel as proof. The urge frightened her. A remembered ending did not establish the truth about living people, and the plot was already changing around her. She put the memory aside and returned to what the witnesses could support. The supply of unused consent forms was finite, so every copy was numbered. The nurses hoped independent medical bulletins could flourish after the crisis. A hired gangster blocked the alley where the second delivery wagon had passed. Fen saw the suspicious wagon turn at the eastern junction. The injured technician walked with a limp but still described the night shift.

下一項決策之所以重要，是因為它可能在判決前就改變普通病人的生活。首席醫師柯文以王室奇蹟療法為由要求立即噤聲。艾黛拉查到強制轉送必須經病人代理人、醫師及登記員共同審核。 有一瞬間艾黛拉想把自己對小說的記憶當作證據。這衝動讓她害怕。記得結局不能證明眼前活人的真相，況且情節正因她而改變。她放下記憶，回頭檢查證人能支持的內容。 未使用的同意書數量有限，因此每張副本都編了號。 護理員希望危機過後，獨立醫療通報能蓬勃發展。 一名受雇的黑幫分子擋住第二輛貨車走過的巷道。 芬恩看見可疑貨車在東邊路口轉彎。 受傷的護理員雖然跛行，仍描述了夜班情況。

Before closing the daily record, Adela asked what remained uncertain and whose consent had not yet been heard. She filed an emergency objection before sunrise, knowing the original Adela had once signed the dangerous rule. The clerk accepted the objection but warned that the crown could dismiss her. When the witnesses left, Adela stayed to check the record against the questions they had actually been asked. She corrected a spelling mistake but would not alter an uncertain answer. The distinction seemed almost invisible on the page. To someone whose life might depend on that page, it was everything. Fen agreed to narrate the route without guessing what the driver intended. An orderly led frightened visitors away from the heavy machinery. A bank teller confirmed the orderly's unusual early-morning payment. The altered treatment order was recorded on virgin form without any earlier stamp. The magistrate granted a limited warrant to inspect the sealed records room.

結束當日紀錄前，艾黛拉詢問哪些地方仍不確定，以及誰的同意還沒有被聽見。她在日出前提出緊急異議，明知原本的艾黛拉曾簽下那條危險規定。書記收件後警告她，王室可能解除她的職務。 證人離開後，艾黛拉留下，依照實際提問核對紀錄。她更正拼字錯誤，卻不修改尚不確定的答案。紙面上這差別幾乎看不出來；對生命可能取決於這一頁的人，卻是全部。 芬恩同意只敘述路線，不猜測車夫的意圖。 一名勤務員引導受驚訪客遠離重型機械。 一名銀行出納證實勤務員清晨收到異常款項。 偽造醫療公告印在全新紙上，沒有先前的登記章戳。 法官核發有限搜索令，准許檢查封存的病歷室。
