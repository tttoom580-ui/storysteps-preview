A Laboratory with Two Doors

As the inquiry continued, the question of who could speak grew more complicated. At the riverside laboratory, chemist Daro opened a public testing room and a private archive only after seeing the court order. He worried that the crown could withdraw his professional license. The ward had learned to call uncertainty a weakness. Adela now used it as a safeguard: a doubtful claim went into a separate column until another person checked it. Sera saw how much slower that made the work. She also saw the first frightened witness return voluntarily the following morning. Vale demanded a concession from nurses before offering emergency funds. A cynical headline suggested that every witness could be bought. The emergency provision did not apply by default to an unverified rumor. Stockpiles of form began to deplete as the hearings continued. A faint fragrance of wet cedar lingered on the sealed evidence boxes. The old system had rewarded people who never admitted a mistake. The new inquiry required them to state errors in permanent ink, even when doing so made an admired official look careless. Adela signed that rule herself.

隨著調查持續，誰有權說話的問題變得更加複雜。河岸實驗室的化學師達洛見到法庭命令後，才開放公開檢驗室與私人檔案室。他擔心王室撤銷自己的專業執照。 病房過去總把不確定視為弱點。艾黛拉如今將它當成保護措施：存疑的說法先放入獨立欄位，等另一個人查核。賽拉發現工作因此慢了許多，也看見第一名害怕的證人隔天自願回來。 維爾要求診所讓步後才願提供緊急經費。 一則憤世嫉俗的標題暗示所有證人都能收買。 緊急條款不會因未查證流言就自動適用。 聽證持續時，表單庫存開始耗盡。 封存的證物箱留有淡淡濕雪松香味。 舊制度獎勵從不承認錯誤的人。新的調查要求用不可擦除的墨水記下失誤，即使這會顯得受人敬重的官員粗心。艾黛拉親自簽署了這項規則。

The investigators separated their observations from the explanations offered by interested officials. Two samples carried the same batch label but different ingredient records. Daro arranged separate tests and would not call either substance a poison before the results were reviewed. The team tested each claim against a second record whenever possible. When no second record existed, they wrote that limitation plainly. Adela found the discipline exhausting and indispensable in equal measure. Guards could not intrude into private homes without proper authority. A jockey from the royal stables remembered the carriage changing horses. The court demanded the literal wording of the emergency clause. The nurses' morale improved when the court protected ordinary distribution. No editor could prosecute a suspect through headlines alone.

調查員將親眼觀察與利害關係官員提出的解釋分開記錄。兩個樣本有相同批號，成分紀錄卻不同。達洛安排分開檢測，不願在審核結果前把任何物質稱為毒物。團隊盡可能以第二份紀錄檢驗每項說法。沒有第二份時，就如實寫下限制。艾黛拉覺得這種紀律令人疲憊，卻又不可或缺。 衛兵沒有適當授權就不能闖入私人住宅。 王室馬廄的賽馬騎師記得那輛馬車曾換馬。 法庭要求提出緊急條款的逐字文字。 法庭保障正常配送後，護理員的士氣提升。 任何編輯都不能只靠標題就起訴嫌疑人。

The next decision mattered because it could change an ordinary patient’s life before the case reached a verdict. Vale's attorney claimed the discrepancy came from translation. Adela requested the source language, the person who translated it, and a dated revision history. A crowd gathered outside, angry enough to accept almost any answer. The mill laboratory denied any use of radioactive material in form testing. Evin did not retort angrily; she answered with delivery figures. A bent screwdriver lay beside the cabinet where the missing medicine label had been stored. A serving of warm stew was distributed to witnesses after dusk. The splendor of the courtroom could not make a weak document genuine.

下一項決策之所以重要，是因為它可能在判決前就改變普通病人的生活。維爾的律師聲稱差異來自翻譯。艾黛拉要求原文、譯者姓名及有日期的修訂紀錄。 外面聚起憤怒的人群，幾乎任何答案都願意接受。 物資廠實驗室否認表單測試使用過放射性材料。 艾文沒有怒氣沖沖地反駁，而是用配送數字回答。 存放失蹤藥品標籤的櫥櫃旁，放著一把彎曲的螺絲起子。 黃昏後，證人每人分到一份熱燉菜。 法庭的華麗氣派不能使薄弱文件變真。

Before closing the daily record, Adela asked what remained uncertain and whose consent had not yet been heard. Daro identified one unauthorized substitution, while the effect on patients remained uncertain. The team placed the entire batch under temporary restriction. By evening the hospital looked much the same as it had that morning. The corridors were still narrow, the work still hard, and most questions still open. Yet one person had been allowed to choose where to stand and what to say. Adela treated that small change as a duty, not a victory speech. Spontaneous applause stopped when Nia reminded listeners that evidence remained incomplete. Fen's stepfather offered to guide the independent inspectors through the old district. A frightened clerk began to stutter while describing the private workshop. The treasury agreed to subsidize independently verified emergency bulletins. Sera rejected a synthetic form sample that lacked the original fiber pattern.

結束當日紀錄前，艾黛拉詢問哪些地方仍不確定，以及誰的同意還沒有被聽見。達洛確認其中一次替換未經授權，但對病人的影響仍不確定。團隊對整批貨品採取暫時限制。 傍晚時醫院看來與早上一樣，走廊仍窄、工作仍難、問題大多未解。但至少一個人得以決定自己站在哪裡、說什麼話。艾黛拉把這小改變看成責任，不是值得慶功的演說。 妮雅提醒證據尚不完整時，自發掌聲便停了。 芬恩的繼父願意帶領獨立稽查員穿過舊城區。 一名受驚書記描述私人工坊時開始口吃。 國庫同意補助經獨立查核的緊急通報。 賽拉排除一張缺少原始纖維紋路的合成紙樣本。
