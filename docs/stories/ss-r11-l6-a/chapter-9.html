Across the Northern River

As the inquiry continued, the question of who could speak grew more complicated. Adela and Nia followed an authorized shipment inquiry to the northern river. A ferryman confirmed that sealed crates had traveled against the current under an exemption signed by Vale. Nobody in the building agreed about what justice should look like. Some wanted the doors locked, while others feared losing food, shelter, and reliable medical attention. Adela wrote both concerns on the board. A policy that listened only to the loudest group would reproduce the failure that had brought them here. Publishing unverified patient names could alienate the families they needed to protect. A dark blot covered part of the medicine batch number. The emergency kitchen served chili to workers waiting by the river. The surviving receipts helped restore continuity to the delivery timeline. The cumulative weight of three lost shipments exceeded a normal wagon load. One visitor asked for a simple answer. Sera explained what had been confirmed, which facts were disputed, and what the next review could actually decide. The visitor thanked her for the limits as much as for the information.

隨著調查持續，誰有權說話的問題變得更加複雜。艾黛拉與妮雅循合法貨運調查抵達北方河流。船夫證實曾有封箱逆流運送，使用維爾簽署的豁免文件。 院內沒有人對正義應該是什麼樣子達成一致。有人想鎖上大門，另一些人害怕失去食物、住所與可信的照護。艾黛拉把兩種擔憂都寫在黑板上；只聽聲音最大的一群人的政策，只會重蹈覆轍。 公布未核實的病人姓名，可能使需要保護的家庭疏遠調查員。 一大塊深色污漬遮住了部分藥品批號。 緊急廚房替河邊等候的工人提供辣味燉菜。 倖存收據協助恢復配送時間線的連續性。 三趟遺失貨運的累計重量超過一般馬車載重。 一名訪客要求簡單答案。賽拉說明哪些事實已確認、哪些仍有爭議，以及下次審查實際能裁定什麼。訪客對她清楚交代限制和提供資訊一樣感激。

The investigators separated their observations from the explanations offered by interested officials. The crates' weight matched medicine containers but their marked destination was a textile warehouse. Adela wrote down this inconsistency without guessing what the cargo contained. The patient council requested copies in language ordinary families could understand. Adela asked the clerks to preserve the technical original and prepare a plain-language explanation alongside it. The two versions were marked separately. That decision prevented a later summary from quietly replacing the actual words of the people involved. The magistrate demanded decisive action to protect people, not to silence questions. Even a damaged carbon copy was not dispensable before review. A jasmine plant outside the dispatch house survived the heat. The fire crew allowed witnesses to lodge in the school overnight. A maple tree near the loading dock bore fresh marks from a cart.

調查員將親眼觀察與利害關係官員提出的解釋分開記錄。箱子重量與藥品容器相符，標示目的地卻是紡織倉庫。艾黛拉記錄矛盾，沒有猜測內裝物。 病人委員會要求提供一般家庭讀得懂的副本。艾黛拉請書記保留技術原件，另做白話說明，並把兩種版本清楚標示。這項決定防止後來的摘要悄悄取代當事人的原話。 法官要求果斷保護民眾，而不是壓制提問。 在審查前，即使受損的複寫本也不能隨意丟棄。 發報站外一株茉莉花撐過高溫。 消防隊安排證人在學校過夜寄宿。 裝貨碼頭旁的楓樹上有貨車留下的新痕。

The next decision mattered because it could change an ordinary patient’s life before the case reached a verdict. Local merchants warned that accusing the warehouse might close the only winter supply route. Nia promised to inspect a specific shipment, not to punish an entire town. The urge frightened her. The boardinghouse mistress distributed blankets to displaced families. Volunteers worked to mobilize safe transport for elderly residents. The editor paused to muse on how quickly rumors became headlines. Evin's persistence kept the missing-hour question in the public report. A socialist form and a merchant form both demanded a neutral fire inquiry.

下一項決策之所以重要，是因為它可能在判決前就改變普通病人的生活。地方商人警告，指控倉庫可能讓唯一的冬季補給路線關閉。妮雅承諾只檢查特定貨批，不懲罰整座城鎮。這衝動讓她害怕。 寄宿屋女主人分發毯子給流離失所的家庭。 志工動員安全車輛接送年長居民。 編輯停下思索，流言竟能如此快變成標題。 艾文的堅持讓缺少一小時的問題留在公開報告中。 社會主義醫療通報與商業醫療通報都要求中立火災調查。

Before closing the daily record, Adela asked what remained uncertain and whose consent had not yet been heard. A customs copy revealed a second cargo number beneath the first. It belonged to a shipment marked for the queen's private laboratory. When the witnesses left, Adela stayed to check the record against the questions they had actually been asked. She corrected a spelling mistake but would not alter an uncertain answer. The distinction seemed almost invisible on the page. To someone whose life might depend on that page, it was everything. The shelter gave residents a place to socialize after the shock. The disputed trips seemed to span the hour before sunrise. Fen began a trek to collect counterpart receipts from inland depots. Dalia organized a vigorous but peaceful meeting about warehouse safety. The river shipment ledger remained legible because its cover was waterproof.

結束當日紀錄前，艾黛拉詢問哪些地方仍不確定，以及誰的同意還沒有被聽見。海關副本露出第一個貨號底下的第二個編號，竟屬於標示送往女王私人實驗室的貨批。 證人離開後，艾黛拉留下，依照實際提問核對紀錄。她更正拼字錯誤，卻不修改尚不確定的答案。紙面上這差別幾乎看不出來；對生命可能取決於這一頁的人，卻是全部。 避難所讓居民受驚後有地方互相交流。 爭議貨運似乎橫跨日出前的一小時。 芬恩徒步前往內陸站點蒐集對應收據。 達莉亞召開熱烈但和平的倉庫安全會議。 河運貨單的封面具有防水功能，因此字跡仍可辨識。
