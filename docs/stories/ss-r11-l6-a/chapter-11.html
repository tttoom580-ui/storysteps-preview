女王的御醫

The Queen's Physician

As the inquiry continued, the question of who could speak grew more complicated. The queen's personal physician asked to meet Adela without palace guards. He said his office had been given selected summaries rather than the full ward records. In the novel, a speech by the director would have solved the scene within a page. The actual people before Adela did not owe her an easy ending. They wanted receipts, safe passage, and the right to say no. She learned to remain in the room after the dramatic part was over. A Celsius scale marked the temperatures recorded in the medicine storage room. Excessive friction damaged one of the demonstration machines. A guard's growl of irritation did not stop the independent test. False certainty could induce the public to accept an unjust accusation. Craftworkers continued to inhabit rooms above the academy workshop. Afterward the staff compared the written order with what had happened in the ward. When they found a difference, they recorded it without inventing an excuse. The patient council would receive the same report as the palace.

隨著調查持續，誰有權說話的問題變得更加複雜。女王的御醫要求在沒有宮廷衛兵的情況下見艾黛拉，說自己的辦公室只收到挑選過的摘要，沒有完整病歷。 小說裡院長的一段演說，一頁內就能解決場景。但現實中站在艾黛拉面前的人，沒有義務給她輕易的結局。他們要的是收據、安全通道與說不的權利。她學會在戲劇化場面結束後，仍留在現場做事。 藥品儲藏室的溫度紀錄以攝氏刻度標示。 過度摩擦使一部示範機受損。 衛兵惱怒地低吼，也沒有讓獨立測試中止。 錯誤的確定感可能誘使公眾接受不公指控。 工匠仍住在學院工坊樓上的房間。 之後員工比對書面命令與病房實際情況。若發現差異，他們如實記錄，不編造藉口。病人委員會將收到與王宮相同的報告。

The investigators separated their observations from the explanations offered by interested officials. He showed her a letter requesting the independent tests that Corven had publicly described as unnecessary. The date preceded the announcement of the miracle cure. Before any object became evidence, Nia wrote down who had handled it, when it moved, and which questions remained unanswered. This did not make the investigators popular. Several people begged them to announce a culprit immediately. Nia refused, saying that certainty purchased with an invented detail was just another form of deception. It was ironic that a damaged letter could help defend honest recording. A mound of broken glass stood behind the sealed laboratory door. A peacock wandered across the academy garden during the hearing. The academy's pianist agreed to perform at the workers' relief concert. The investigators needed to probe the medicine label's origin before naming a suspect.

調查員將親眼觀察與利害關係官員提出的解釋分開記錄。他拿出一封要求獨立檢測的信，柯文公開稱這些檢測毫無必要；信件日期早於奇蹟療法公布。 每件物品成為證物前，妮雅都記下誰碰過它、何時移動，以及哪些問題還沒答案。這不會使調查員受歡迎，因為許多人希望立刻公布犯人。妮雅拒絕，說靠捏造細節換來的確定感，本身就是另一種欺騙。 受損字模竟能幫助維護誠實記錄，頗具諷刺。 封閉實驗室的門後堆著一堆碎玻璃。 一隻孔雀在聽證時走過學院花園。 學院鋼琴師答應參加工人救助音樂會。 調查員必須深入探查藥品標籤的來源，才能指認嫌疑人。

The next decision mattered because it could change an ordinary patient’s life before the case reached a verdict. Adela wondered whether the queen herself knew the deception. Nia insisted that no one should infer royal involvement from a physician's letter alone. Corven spoke as though the success of the hospital belonged to its managers alone. Evin disagreed. A ward existed because people needed help, she said, and their consent was not a decorative signature. Adela allowed that answer to stand without turning it into a speech about her own courage. Each examiner signed a report describing their respective methods. Sera called it shortsighted to hide the failed trials. The teacher tried to simplify a complex demonstration for ordinary readers. The shipment passed a narrow strait before reaching the inland clinic. A superficial resemblance between letters could mislead an untrained observer.

下一項決策之所以重要，是因為它可能在判決前就改變普通病人的生活。艾黛拉懷疑女王是否知情。妮雅堅持不能只憑御醫的一封信就推斷王室涉案。 柯文說得彷彿醫院的成就只屬於管理者。艾文不同意：病房存在，是因為人們需要幫助，而同意書不是裝飾簽名。艾黛拉讓這句話自己站得住腳，沒有拿來演說自己的勇氣。 各檢驗員分別簽署說明自身方法的報告。 賽拉說掩蓋失敗測試是短視的。 教師試著為一般讀者簡化複雜示範。 貨物穿過一條狹窄海峽，才抵達內陸診所。 字母表面的相似可能誤導未受訓者。

Before closing the daily record, Adela asked what remained uncertain and whose consent had not yet been heard. The physician agreed to testify, on condition that patients' names be withheld from the public copy. The hearing would now include the palace's internal reports. Sera asked whether the investigation would ever feel finished. No official could suppress a qualified physician's dissenting findings. The pattern had near-perfect symmetry except for the crescent-shaped damage. A wooden token marked each worker's paid shift at the relief station. Dalia tried to unify the workers without demanding that they praise Adela. The urgency of the hearing did not erase the need for repeated tests.

結束當日紀錄前，艾黛拉詢問哪些地方仍不確定，以及誰的同意還沒有被聽見。御醫同意作證，條件是公開副本隱去病人姓名。聽證將首次納入王宮內部報告。 賽拉問調查是否有一天會真的結束。 任何官員都不能壓下合格醫師提出的不同檢驗結果。 除了新月形損傷，圖案幾乎完全對稱。 救助站用木製代幣標示每名工人的受薪班次。 達莉亞努力團結工人，卻不要求他們讚美艾黛拉。 聽證再緊急，也不能省略反覆測試。
