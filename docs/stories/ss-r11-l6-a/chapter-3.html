禮拜堂下的走廊

The Corridor Beneath the Chapel

As the inquiry continued, the question of who could speak grew more complicated. Behind the chapel pantry, Sera found a locked passage connecting the ward to a disused school. Adela refused to force the door without a recorded warrant. In the novel, a speech by the director would have solved the scene within a page. The actual people before Adela did not owe her an easy ending. They wanted receipts, safe passage, and the right to say no. She learned to remain in the room after the dramatic part was over. A local advisory board checked the proposed text before distribution. The history lesson discussed conquest without praising violence. Tomas feared a conspiracy to make the hospital's witnesses appear unreliable. The children's lunch included cucumber cut beside warm bread. One witness had a feeble voice after a sleepless night. Afterward the staff compared the written order with what had happened in the ward. When they found a difference, they recorded it without inventing an excuse. The patient council would receive the same report as the palace.

隨著調查持續，誰有權說話的問題變得更加複雜。賽拉在禮拜堂餐具室後找到上鎖通道，連往廢棄學校。艾黛拉拒絕在沒有書面令狀的情況下破門。 小說裡院長的一段演說，一頁內就能解決場景。但現實中站在艾黛拉面前的人，沒有義務給她輕易的結局。他們要的是收據、安全通道與說不的權利。她學會在戲劇化場面結束後，仍留在現場做事。 地方諮詢委員會在發行前檢查了擬議文字。 歷史課討論征服，卻沒有歌頌暴力。 湯瑪斯擔心有人密謀使醫院證人看起來不可信。 孩子們午餐吃了配麵包的切片小黃瓜。 一名證人徹夜未眠，聲音微弱。 之後員工比對書面命令與病房實際情況。若發現差異，他們如實記錄，不編造藉口。病人委員會將收到與王宮相同的報告。

The investigators separated their observations from the explanations offered by interested officials. A retired porter recalled carrying medicine crates through the passage at night. His account matched a receipt but contradicted the official claim that the corridor had been sealed for years. Before any object became evidence, Nia wrote down who had handled it, when it moved, and which questions remained unanswered. This did not make the investigators popular. Several people begged them to announce a culprit immediately. Nia refused, saying that certainty purchased with an invented detail was just another form of deception. A discarded firecracker lay outside the sealed side entrance. The old sterilizing unit began to fume when its motor overheated. A small stain of grease identified the orderly's tool case. Clear explanations might lessen the panic spreading through the market. A passenger liner on the river carried form toward the southern depots.

調查員將親眼觀察與利害關係官員提出的解釋分開記錄。退休搬運員記得夜裡經由通道搬運藥箱。他的說法與收據相符，卻和官方聲稱通道已封閉多年矛盾。 每件物品成為證物前，妮雅都記下誰碰過它、何時移動，以及哪些問題還沒答案。這不會使調查員受歡迎，因為許多人希望立刻公布犯人。妮雅拒絕，說靠捏造細節換來的確定感，本身就是另一種欺騙。 封閉的側門外有一枚被丟棄的鞭炮。 老舊消毒機的馬達過熱時開始冒煙。 一小塊油脂污漬幫助辨認勤務員的工具箱。 清楚解釋或能減輕市場蔓延的恐慌。 一艘河上客輪載著表單前往南方轉運站。

The next decision mattered because it could change an ordinary patient’s life before the case reached a verdict. The chapel keeper feared blame and asked for protection before speaking. Nia arranged a separate interview, allowing him to correct his statement without a royal official present. Corven spoke as though the success of the hospital belonged to its managers alone. Evin disagreed. A ward existed because people needed help, she said, and their consent was not a decorative signature. Adela allowed that answer to stand without turning it into a speech about her own courage. The orphanage asked that its pupils remain outside the public inquiry. The school recorded a warning that false health claims could spread like plague. Its recreational garden remained open to children during the hearing. A savage editorial urged punishment before anyone had seen the evidence. Beren found a broken metal letter beneath a rose shrub.

下一項決策之所以重要，是因為它可能在判決前就改變普通病人的生活。禮拜堂管理員害怕被追究，要求先獲保障才肯說話。妮雅安排獨立訪談，讓他能在王室官員不在場時更正供述。 柯文說得彷彿醫院的成就只屬於管理者。艾文不同意：病房存在，是因為人們需要幫助，而同意書不是裝飾簽名。艾黛拉讓這句話自己站得住腳，沒有拿來演說自己的勇氣。 孤兒院要求學生不要被捲入公開調查。 學校製作警告，提醒虛假健康資訊也可能像瘟疫般擴散。 聽證期間，學校的休閒花園仍對孩子開放。 一篇猛烈的社論在證據公開前便鼓吹懲罰。 貝倫在玫瑰灌木下找到一枚破損字模。

Before closing the daily record, Adela asked what remained uncertain and whose consent had not yet been heard. Inside, the team discovered numbered empty shelves, not proof of poison. A small ledger listed shipments paid by a charitable trust. Sera asked whether the investigation would ever feel finished. Adela looked at the documents waiting on the table and admitted that she did not know. The promise she could make was narrower: no missing page would be replaced with a convenient guess, and no patient would be turned into a symbol without consent. Fen explained the orderly's slang to an unfamiliar judge. The little crescent mark could surpass a noble signature in evidential value. The school badge was meant to symbolize learning rather than royal obedience. A textile worker donated fresh cloth for patients who had lost their belongings. The health leaflet accurately explained when a vaccine required professional advice.

結束當日紀錄前，艾黛拉詢問哪些地方仍不確定，以及誰的同意還沒有被聽見。眾人在通道裡只找到編號的空架子，還稱不上毒物證據；一本小帳冊記載款項來自慈善信託。 賽拉問調查是否有一天會真的結束。艾黛拉看著桌上待查文件，承認自己不知道。她能許下的承諾更有限：不會用方便的猜測補上失頁，也不會未經同意便把病人當作象徵。 芬恩向不熟悉行話的法官解釋勤務員用的俚語。 小小的新月缺口的證據價值，可能超過貴族簽名。 校徽象徵學習，而非對王室盲從。 一名紡織工人捐出新布料，給遺失隨身物品的病人使用。 健康傳單正確說明何時須就疫苗諮詢專業人員。
