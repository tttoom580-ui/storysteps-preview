櫃中的聲音

The Voices in the Cabinet

王室療養院，不准替病人說謊 · 第 8 / 25 章 · 5000–6000 · 英中對照 · 校訂中

As the inquiry continued, the question of who could speak grew more complicated. Sera recognized the cabinet of wax recordings: it contained interviews made before patients received silver bands. Some voices belonged to people later described as incapable of speech. The first question was not whether the palace was embarrassed, but whose voice the institution had ignored. Adela had spent years auditing records in another life; that knowledge taught her how easily a neat form could conceal a human decision. She asked every visitor to distinguish what they had actually seen from what someone had merely told them. The museum recorded the accumulation of form samples over fifty years. Ora offered a warm beverage before the delicate examination began. A blond visitor had asked about the seven-pointed wheel watermark. An expert could certify the fiber pattern but not identify the original buyer. The clerk required clearance before entering the restricted record room. The note was placed in the public inspection folder, where the time, author, and reason for every correction could be checked. Adela did not let a private conversation replace that record; personal trust mattered, but verifiable decisions mattered more.

隨著調查持續，誰有權說話的問題變得更加複雜。賽拉認出裝有蠟筒錄音的櫃子，那些訪談錄於病人佩戴銀色束帶以前，其中一些聲音的主人後來被描述成無法說話。 第一個問題不是王宮會不會難堪，而是制度忽視了誰的聲音。艾黛拉在另一段人生裡多年稽核紀錄，知道整齊表單多麼容易藏住人的選擇。她要求每位來訪者分清親眼所見與道聽塗說。 博物館記錄了五十年累積的表單樣本。 精細檢查開始前，奧拉端來熱飲。 一名金髮訪客曾詢問七角輪水印。 專家可證明纖維圖樣，卻不能指認原買主。 書記進入限制紀錄室前須取得許可。 這份記錄放入公開檢查資料夾，每次更正的時間、作者和原因都可以查核。艾黛拉不讓私下對話取代這份紀錄；私人信任重要，可驗證的決定更重要。

The investigators separated their observations from the explanations offered by interested officials. A trained listener checked dates and recording speed before making transcripts. Adela insisted that the speakers themselves should approve public quotation wherever possible. Sera drew a line through the center of her notebook. On the left she copied verified dates; on the right she wrote the questions raised by those dates. The difference seemed small until two accounts contradicted one another. With the columns separated, neither witness had to be called a liar before the records were examined. The museum could not deduct an unrelated repair bill from witness expenses. Adela refused to call the outcome destined merely because she knew the novel. The technician had to exert gentle pressure to reveal the imprint beneath the seal. Ora's heroic act was preserving an ordinary sample when others threw theirs away. A retired lieutenant volunteered to protect the museum's front entrance.

調查員將親眼觀察與利害關係官員提出的解釋分開記錄。受過訓練的聽寫員先檢查日期及播放速度才謄寫。艾黛拉堅持只要可行，公開引述前就要先取得說話者同意。 賽拉在筆記本中間畫線，左邊抄已核實日期，右邊寫日期引發的疑問。這個差別起初很小，直到兩份說法互相矛盾。欄位分開後，便不用在檢查紀錄前先把任何證人稱作說謊者。 博物館不能從證人費用中扣除無關修繕帳。 艾黛拉拒絕因讀過小說就說結局命中注定。 技師必須輕輕施壓，才能顯出印章下方的壓痕。 奧拉的英勇之舉，是在他人丟棄時保存平凡樣本。 一名退役中尉自願守護博物館正門。

The next decision mattered because it could change an ordinary patient’s life before the case reached a verdict. The crown's agent wanted to broadcast every recording to embarrass Vale. Adela refused: exposing another person's private pain was not the same as delivering justice. The proposal divided the room. The museum's guide was literate in several regional scripts. A lotus design decorated the old mill's form mold. The extra line was marginal, but the team preserved it in the photographs. The second sample showed an overlap between two fiber threads. A provincial clerk wrote to confirm the manufacturer of the form.

下一項決策之所以重要，是因為它可能在判決前就改變普通病人的生活。王室代理人想播放所有錄音使維爾難堪。艾黛拉拒絕，因公開他人的隱私痛苦並不等於伸張正義。提案讓眾人意見分裂。 博物館導覽員能讀懂數種地方文字。 古老紙模上刻有蓮花圖案。 額外線條雖細微，團隊仍在照片中保存。 第二張樣本顯示兩條纖維彼此重疊。 一名地方記錄師來信確認表單製造者。

Before closing the daily record, Adela asked what remained uncertain and whose consent had not yet been heard. Evin recognized a voice and whispered the name of the missing man, Tomas. His final message referred to a river shipment. The final lamp remained lit as the staff exchanged shifts. That thought made careful work feel larger than the palace. The museum listed each examiner's qualification beside the test results. Simple refreshments were provided to witnesses during the long inspection. Sera promised to retrieve the original memorial-booklet invoice. The storage room stood on reclaimed ground beside an old swamp. Ora saw the preservation of fragile records as her lifelong vocation.

結束當日紀錄前，艾黛拉詢問哪些地方仍不確定，以及誰的同意還沒有被聽見。艾文認出其中一段聲音，低聲說出失蹤男子湯瑪斯的名字。他最後的留言提到一批河運貨物。 最後一盞燈在交班時仍亮著。這讓謹慎工作顯得比王宮更重要。 博物館把每位檢驗者的資格列在結果旁。 漫長檢查期間，證人獲得簡單茶點。 賽拉答應取回紀念冊的原始發票。 儲藏室建在一處舊沼澤旁的填土地。 奧拉把保存脆弱文獻視為終身志業。
