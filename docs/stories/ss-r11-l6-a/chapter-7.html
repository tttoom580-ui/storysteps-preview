As the inquiry continued, the question of who could speak grew more complicated. The royal contractor Vale introduced himself as the financier who had saved the ward during a previous crisis. He insisted that the new cure should remain a commercial secret. In the novel, a speech by the director would have solved the scene within a page. The actual people before Adela did not owe her an easy ending. They wanted receipts, safe passage, and the right to say no. She learned to remain in the room after the dramatic part was over. The survey measured an acre of disputed farmland before drawing a new boundary. A bachelor who ran the village inn offered rooms for the witnesses. A boxing club lent its hall for a neutral community meeting. A disciplinary hearing would examine the official who altered the map. The emigrant carried his unexpired passport inside a leather case. Afterward the staff compared the written order with what had happened in the ward. When they found a difference, they recorded it without inventing an excuse. The patient council would receive the same report as the palace.

隨著調查持續，誰有權說話的問題變得更加複雜。王室承包商維爾自稱曾在危機中出資拯救療養院，堅稱新療法應維持商業機密。 小說裡院長的一段演說，一頁內就能解決場景。但現實中站在艾黛拉面前的人，沒有義務給她輕易的結局。他們要的是收據、安全通道與說不的權利。她學會在戲劇化場面結束後，仍留在現場做事。 測量工作先量出一英畝爭議農地，再重畫邊界。 經營村中旅店的單身男子提供證人住宿。 拳擊俱樂部借出會館，供社區舉辦中立會議。 紀律聽證將調查更改地圖的官員。 準備移居他國的男子把有效護照放在皮套裡。 之後員工比對書面命令與病房實際情況。若發現差異，他們如實記錄，不編造藉口。病人委員會將收到與王宮相同的報告。

The investigators separated their observations from the explanations offered by interested officials. Adela compared the public advertisement with the contract and noticed that patients had signed for an ordinary nutritional program, not a permanent experiment. Before any object became evidence, Nia wrote down who had handled it, when it moved, and which questions remained unanswered. This did not make the investigators popular. Several people begged them to announce a culprit immediately. Nia refused, saying that certainty purchased with an invented detail was just another form of deception. Adela refused to equate the carter's fear with dishonesty. His voice began to falter when he described the threatened tenants. Farmers worried that the new road would damage soil fertility. Evin urged tenants not to forsake their right to appeal. The form defended gay residents against unequal housing rules.

調查員將親眼觀察與利害關係官員提出的解釋分開記錄。艾黛拉比對公開廣告與合約，發現病人簽署的是普通營養計畫，並非永久實驗。 每件物品成為證物前，妮雅都記下誰碰過它、何時移動，以及哪些問題還沒答案。這不會使調查員受歡迎，因為許多人希望立刻公布犯人。妮雅拒絕，說靠捏造細節換來的確定感，本身就是另一種欺騙。 艾黛拉拒絕把運貨員的恐懼等同於不誠實。 說到受威脅的佃農時，他的聲音開始顫抖。 農民擔心新道路會傷害土壤肥沃度。 艾文勸佃農不要放棄申訴權。 這份醫療通報反對在住房規則上歧視同性戀居民。

The next decision mattered because it could change an ordinary patient’s life before the case reached a verdict. Vale offered money for a quiet settlement and argued that questioning his business would frighten investors. Nia explained that funding could not replace informed permission from patients. Corven spoke as though the success of the hospital belonged to its managers alone. Evin disagreed. A ward existed because people needed help, she said, and their consent was not a decorative signature. Adela allowed that answer to stand without turning it into a speech about her own courage. Gloom settled over the village when another eviction letter arrived. The cart had stopped at an intersection marked by an old stone well. A landslide forced travelers to use the longer northern path. Farm families sought funds to nourish damaged fields after the storm. The orthodox procedure demanded a registered order before restricting a patient's movement.

下一項決策之所以重要，是因為它可能在判決前就改變普通病人的生活。維爾提出私下和解金，聲稱質疑企業會嚇跑投資人。妮雅解釋，資金不能取代病人的知情同意。 柯文說得彷彿醫院的成就只屬於管理者。艾文不同意：病房存在，是因為人們需要幫助，而同意書不是裝飾簽名。艾黛拉讓這句話自己站得住腳，沒有拿來演說自己的勇氣。 另一封驅離信到來時，村中籠罩陰鬱氣氛。 貨車曾停在有舊石井的十字路口。 一場土石崩落迫使旅人改走較長的北路。 農家尋求經費滋養暴風雨後受損的田地。 傳統法定程序要求先有登記命令，才能限制病人行動。

Before closing the daily record, Adela asked what remained uncertain and whose consent had not yet been heard. The inspector ordered the contract disclosed to the tribunal. A handwritten addition granted Vale control over every patient's voice recording. Sera asked whether the investigation would ever feel finished. Adela looked at the documents waiting on the table and admitted that she did not know. The promise she could make was narrower: no missing page would be replaced with a convenient guess, and no patient would be turned into a symbol without consent. A legal prohibition prevented eviction during the review period. The judge issued a provisional order to preserve the map. The tenant council reached a unanimous decision to request public records. Adela recognized that protecting her vanity would endanger the inquiry. A woodpecker tapped the orchard fence during the witness interviews.

結束當日紀錄前，艾黛拉詢問哪些地方仍不確定，以及誰的同意還沒有被聽見。稽查官命合約向法庭公開。一條手寫追加條款竟將所有病人的聲音錄音交給維爾控制。 賽拉問調查是否有一天會真的結束。艾黛拉看著桌上待查文件，承認自己不知道。她能許下的承諾更有限：不會用方便的猜測補上失頁，也不會未經同意便把病人當作象徵。 一項法律禁令禁止在審查期間驅離。 法官核發暫時命令保存地圖。 佃農議會一致決定申請公開紀錄。 艾黛拉明白，維護虛榮只會危害調查。 證人訪談時，一隻啄木鳥敲著果園籬笆。
