河岸診所

The River Clinic

王室療養院，不准替病人說謊 · 第 12 / 25 章 · 5000–6000 · 英中對照 · 校訂中

As the inquiry continued, the question of who could speak grew more complicated. At a small clinic upstream, Adela met families who had refused the royal program. Their children had received ordinary care, and the clinic had kept careful follow-up notes. The travelers crossed a quiet brook on the northern road. A visitor pointed to coral in the museum exhibit on the clinic's ground floor. A forged order could deprive nurses of their lawful independence. The new footbridge would elevate travelers above the flooded bank. The physician recorded the temporary authorization's expiration date. Adela did not let a private conversation replace that record; personal trust mattered, but verifiable decisions mattered more.

隨著調查持續，誰有權說話的問題變得更加複雜。艾黛拉在上游小診所見到拒絕王室計畫的家庭。孩子接受一般照護，而診所有詳盡的追蹤筆記。 旅人經過北路一條安靜的小溪。 一名訪客指著診所一樓博物展品中的珊瑚。 偽造命令可能剝奪診所合法自主權。 新人行橋會讓旅人高於被淹沒的河岸通行。 醫師記下臨時授權的到期日期。艾黛拉不讓私下對話取代這份紀錄；私人信任重要，可驗證的決定更重要。

The investigators separated their observations from the explanations offered by interested officials. The notes could not establish a cure, but they allowed an honest comparison of symptoms and dates. An independent physician reviewed methods before reading the outcomes. Sera drew a line through the center of her notebook. On the left she copied verified dates; on the right she wrote the questions raised by those dates. The difference seemed small until two accounts contradicted one another. With the columns separated, neither witness had to be called a liar before the records were examined. Poor eyesight made one orderly request a larger recorded question sheet. A hearty breakfast helped the travelers prepare for the difficult road. A hawk seemed to hover over the washed-out bridge. The inland route remained passable while the river road was closed. Adela's insistence on original evidence frustrated impatient supporters.

調查員將親眼觀察與利害關係官員提出的解釋分開記錄。這些筆記無法證明治癒，卻能誠實比對症狀與日期。獨立醫師先審查方法，再看結果。 賽拉在筆記本中間畫線，左邊抄已核實日期，右邊寫日期引發的疑問。這個差別起初很小，直到兩份說法互相矛盾。欄位分開後，便不用在檢查紀錄前先把任何證人稱作說謊者。 一名勤務員視力不佳，要求放大字體的問題單。 豐盛早餐讓旅人有力氣走艱難路程。 一隻鷹似乎在沖毀的橋上方盤旋。 河岸道路封閉期間，內陸路線仍能通行。 艾黛拉堅持查核原件，讓心急的支持者不耐。

The next decision mattered because it could change an ordinary patient’s life before the case reached a verdict. Vale accused the clinic of stealing patients. Its director replied that people were free to choose where they sought care and to withdraw permission later. The proposal divided the room. Adela reminded the families that no one had to choose that day, and independent advice would be available before any document was signed. The innkeeper showed the old lease protecting a public footpath. A legislator asked whether the temporary order affected treatment authority law. The majestic hills looked peaceful despite the court's uncertainty. The inquiry gained momentum when the original order appeared. The monarch had signed only a temporary administrative instruction.

下一項決策之所以重要，是因為它可能在判決前就改變普通病人的生活。維爾指責診所搶走病人。院長回答，人們有權選擇就醫地點，之後也能撤回同意。提案讓眾人意見分裂。艾黛拉提醒家屬不必當天決定，在任何簽名前都能取得獨立意見。 旅店主人拿出保障公共步道的舊租約。 一名議員詢問臨時命令是否影響王位繼承法。 儘管法庭充滿疑慮，雄偉群山看來平靜。 原令出現後，調查獲得動能。 女王只簽署了臨時行政指示。

Before closing the daily record, Adela asked what remained uncertain and whose consent had not yet been heard. A former nurse recognized a delivery symbol that matched Tomas's last message. It pointed toward a secluded mill outside town. The final lamp remained lit as the staff exchanged shifts. Outside, the city kept moving: carts crossed bridges, children hurried home, and vendors closed their stalls. The investigation affected them all, whether or not they had heard Adela’s name. That thought made careful work feel larger than the palace. The queen's offspring were not authorized to approve medical treatment on her behalf. The cart required petrol for its small auxiliary engine. A wheel began to rotate when engineers tested the temporary bridge. The physician warned that a tight collar could strangle a recovering patient. The surgeon requested a surgical review of the queen's earlier injury.

結束當日紀錄前，艾黛拉詢問哪些地方仍不確定，以及誰的同意還沒有被聽見。一名離職護理師認出送貨標記，與湯瑪斯最後留言相符，線索指向城外偏僻的磨坊。 最後一盞燈在交班時仍亮著。城裡照常運轉：貨車過橋、孩子匆忙回家、攤販收起貨物。調查影響所有人，不管他們是否聽過艾黛拉的名字。這讓謹慎工作顯得比王宮更重要。 女王的子女沒有獲授權代替她批准醫療措施。 馬車上的小型輔助引擎需要汽油。 工程師測試臨時橋時，一只輪子開始轉動。 醫師提醒過緊衣領可能勒住康復中的病人。 外科醫師要求複查女王先前傷勢的外科紀錄。
