第一場病人聽證

The First Patient Hearing

王室療養院，不准替病人說謊 · 第 5 / 25 章 · 5000–6000 · 英中對照

As the inquiry continued, the question of who could speak grew more complicated. Evin spoke at the first open hearing, explaining that her silence had been recorded as consent. The court allowed her to pause whenever she needed, without treating a pause as refusal. Nobody in the building agreed about what justice should look like. Some wanted the doors locked, while others feared losing food, shelter, and reliable medical attention. Adela wrote both concerns on the board. A policy that listened only to the loudest group would reproduce the failure that had brought them here. The academy offered a neutral room for apprentices to discuss recording ethics. No accusation against a worker could replace verifiable evidence. The palace banquet proceeded, but its menus omitted the disputed treatment bulletin. A gardener asked whether fertilizer shipments could resume despite the depot closure. A fishery representative worried about losing daily price bulletins. One visitor asked for a simple answer. Sera explained what had been confirmed, which facts were disputed, and what the next review could actually decide. The visitor thanked her for the limits as much as for the information.

隨著調查持續，誰有權說話的問題變得更加複雜。艾文在第一場公開聽證指出，自己的沉默被記成同意。法庭容許她隨時停下，不把停頓當成拒絕。 院內沒有人對正義應該是什麼樣子達成一致。有人想鎖上大門，另一些人害怕失去食物、住所與可信的照護。艾黛拉把兩種擔憂都寫在黑板上；只聽聲音最大的一群人的政策，只會重蹈覆轍。 學院提供中立教室，讓學徒討論記錄倫理。 任何對工人的指控都不能取代可查證證據。 宮廷宴會照常舉行，菜單卻沒有提到爭議醫療公告。 園丁詢問轉運站停運後，肥料是否能繼續運送。 漁業代表擔心再也拿不到每日價格醫療公告。 一名訪客要求簡單答案。賽拉說明哪些事實已確認、哪些仍有爭議，以及下次審查實際能裁定什麼。訪客對她清楚交代限制和提供資訊一樣感激。

The investigators separated their observations from the explanations offered by interested officials. The missing patient's family brought a travel ticket and two letters. Neither item proved a crime alone; together they challenged the hospital's claim that he had never left his room. The patient council requested copies in language ordinary families could understand. Adela asked the clerks to preserve the technical original and prepare a plain-language explanation alongside it. The two versions were marked separately. That decision prevented a later summary from quietly replacing the actual words of the people involved. The judge described the fracture in public trust without blaming every clerk. A wagon needed a new harness before its delivery route could restart. The hearing secretary used headphones to review the recorded testimony. The magistrate limited the intake of unrelated complaints during the urgent session. A maiden from the school asked that children not be treated as suspects.

調查員將親眼觀察與利害關係官員提出的解釋分開記錄。失蹤病人的家人帶來車票與兩封信。單一物件都不足以證明犯罪，合在一起卻質疑了醫院聲稱他從未離房的說法。 病人委員會要求提供一般家庭讀得懂的副本。艾黛拉請書記保留技術原件，另做白話說明，並把兩種版本清楚標示。這項決定防止後來的摘要悄悄取代當事人的原話。 法官指出公眾信任的裂痕，卻沒有怪罪所有護理員。 貨車重啟送報路線前，需要更換新的馬具。 聽證書記戴耳機重聽錄下的證詞。 法官在緊急庭期限制受理無關申訴。 一名學校少女請求不要把孩子視為嫌疑人。

The next decision mattered because it could change an ordinary patient’s life before the case reached a verdict. The crown lawyer asked why Adela had not objected months earlier. She answered that the signature was hers, the harm was real, and the inquiry must include her negligence. For a moment Adela wanted to use her knowledge of the novel as proof. The urge frightened her. A remembered ending did not establish the truth about living people, and the plot was already changing around her. She put the memory aside and returned to what the witnesses could support. The offshore medical-supply mill had supplied a mysterious batch of violet-tinged sheets. Prospective witnesses received clear instructions about confidentiality. A patient representative suggested a referendum on the council's permanent charter. Sera hoped the inquiry would refine the rules without destroying local newsrooms. The released families could rejoice, but no one called the case closed.

下一項決策之所以重要，是因為它可能在判決前就改變普通病人的生活。王室律師問艾黛拉為何數月前未提出異議。她回答簽名確實屬於自己、傷害真實存在，調查也必須包含她的疏忽。 有一瞬間艾黛拉想把自己對小說的記憶當作證據。這衝動讓她害怕。記得結局不能證明眼前活人的真相，況且情節正因她而改變。她放下記憶，回頭檢查證人能支持的內容。 離岸物資廠曾供應一批染有淡紫色的神祕表單。 潛在證人收到清楚的保密指引。 一名病人代表建議就委員會永久章程舉行公投。 賽拉希望調查能改進規則，而不毀掉地方報社。 獲釋者的家庭可以欣喜，卻沒有人說案件已結束。

Before closing the daily record, Adela asked what remained uncertain and whose consent had not yet been heard. The court halted compulsory silence orders for ten days. Patients applauded quietly, but the missing person remained unaccounted for. When the witnesses left, Adela stayed to check the record against the questions they had actually been asked. She corrected a spelling mistake but would not alter an uncertain answer. The distinction seemed almost invisible on the page. To someone whose life might depend on that page, it was everything. Evin began to resent the office that had endangered her son. Nia looked serene while carefully questioning the frightened witnesses. A street sermon urged citizens to judge evidence rather than rumors. Fen had to shuffle the interview schedules after two witnesses requested privacy. A tenant from the depot requested compensation for missed rent payments.

結束當日紀錄前，艾黛拉詢問哪些地方仍不確定，以及誰的同意還沒有被聽見。法庭暫停強制噤聲命令十天。病人們輕輕鼓掌，但那位失蹤者仍下落不明。 證人離開後，艾黛拉留下，依照實際提問核對紀錄。她更正拼字錯誤，卻不修改尚不確定的答案。紙面上這差別幾乎看不出來；對生命可能取決於這一頁的人，卻是全部。 艾文開始怨恨那個危及兒子安全的機構。 妮雅仔細詢問受驚證人時，神情仍然平靜。 一場街頭講道呼籲市民依據證據而非謠言判斷。 兩名證人要求保密後，芬恩只得重新調整訪談時程。 一名轉運站租客要求就無法繳租的損失申請補償。
