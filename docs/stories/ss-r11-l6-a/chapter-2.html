王室療養院，不准替病人說謊 · 第 2 / 25 章 · 5000–6000 · 英中對照

As the inquiry continued, the question of who could speak grew more complicated. Independent inspector Nia arrived to read the patient-rights code aloud. A patient named Evin said she had been awake during the supposed examination but no one asked her a question. The ward had learned to call uncertainty a weakness. Adela now used it as a safeguard: a doubtful claim went into a separate column until another person checked it. Sera saw how much slower that made the work. She also saw the first frightened witness return voluntarily the following morning. An aboriginal elder requested a correction in her community's written language. Rumors abound when officials refuse to explain why a patient has disappeared. Sera tried to align the three identical signatures beneath a sheet of glass. The hillside depot stood at an altitude that slowed overloaded carts. Fen could not hide his annoyance at the guard's careless questions. The old system had rewarded people who never admitted a mistake. The new inquiry required them to state errors in permanent ink, even when doing so made an admired official look careless. Adela signed that rule herself.

隨著調查持續，誰有權說話的問題變得更加複雜。獨立稽查官妮雅到場宣讀病人權利規程。病人艾文表示，所謂檢查期間她一直清醒，卻沒有人問過她任何問題。 病房過去總把不確定視為弱點。艾黛拉如今將它當成保護措施：存疑的說法先放入獨立欄位，等另一個人查核。賽拉發現工作因此慢了許多，也看見第一名害怕的證人隔天自願回來。 一名原住民族長者要求用社區書面語發布更正。 官員拒絕解釋病人失蹤原因時，流言便四處蔓延。 賽拉嘗試將三個完全相同的簽名隔著玻璃對齊。 山丘轉運站位於較高海拔，讓超載貨車行駛緩慢。 芬恩無法掩飾對衛兵草率提問的不悅。 舊制度獎勵從不承認錯誤的人。新的調查要求用不可擦除的墨水記下失誤，即使這會顯得受人敬重的官員粗心。艾黛拉親自簽署了這項規則。

The investigators separated their observations from the explanations offered by interested officials. Three transfer forms carried identical clock marks and the same slanting pen stroke. Sera, a young nurse, volunteered her own shift notes for comparison, although doing so endangered her position. An order from the palace could open a door, but it could not explain what had happened behind it. The team tested each claim against a second record whenever possible. When no second record existed, they wrote that limitation plainly. Adela found the discipline exhausting and indispensable in equal measure. Evin gave a blunt account of how her son had been taken away. The eastern crossing remained open to ordinary travelers during the inquiry. Adela forbade anyone to discard the torn label before it was photographed. The injured driver carried a medical discharge note from the town clinic. Their endeavor required patient witnesses and records, not dramatic accusations.

調查員將親眼觀察與利害關係官員提出的解釋分開記錄。三張轉送表單有完全相同的時間戳及傾斜筆畫。年輕護理師賽拉自願提供值班筆記比對，儘管這可能危及她的職位。 王宮命令能打開一道門，卻不能解釋門後發生過什麼。團隊盡可能以第二份紀錄檢驗每項說法。沒有第二份時，就如實寫下限制。艾黛拉覺得這種紀律令人疲憊，卻又不可或缺。 艾文直截了當敘述兒子被帶走的經過。 調查期間，東側渡口仍對一般旅客開放。 艾黛拉禁止任何人在拍照存證前丟掉破標籤。 受傷車夫帶著鎮上診所開立的出院證明。 這項努力需要願意耐心作證的人與紀錄，而不是戲劇化指控。

The next decision mattered because it could change an ordinary patient’s life before the case reached a verdict. Corven called the repeated marks a harmless convenience. Nia replied that identical forms did not prove who forged them, but they did justify a temporary hold on further transfers. A crowd gathered outside, angry enough to accept almost any answer. Adela knew that a quick accusation might calm them for an hour and destroy a fair trial for years. She asked the guards to protect the doorway without stopping peaceful questions. The response was imperfect, but it left room for witnesses to approach. The false supplier code became their first major hurdle. The driver changed his posture when shown the unfamiliar depot name. The comparison demanded precision down to the width of a metal letter. An independent clerk promised a rigorous examination of the spacing. Evin dried the recovered sheet on her sheltered terrace.

下一項決策之所以重要，是因為它可能在判決前就改變普通病人的生活。柯文稱重複記號只是便宜行事。妮雅指出，相同表單不能證明誰偽造，卻足以支持暫停進一步轉送。 外面聚起憤怒的人群，幾乎任何答案都願意接受。艾黛拉知道倉促指控也許能安撫一小時，卻會毀掉多年的公平審判。她請衛兵保護入口，但不要阻止和平提問。做法並不完美，至少留了證人靠近的空間。 虛假的供應商代碼成為第一個重大障礙。 車夫看到陌生的轉運站名稱時，姿勢突然改變。 比對必須精確到金屬字模的寬度。 獨立記錄師承諾嚴謹檢查字間距。 艾文把尋回的表單放在有遮蔽的露臺上晾乾。

Before closing the daily record, Adela asked what remained uncertain and whose consent had not yet been heard. The tribunal issued a forty-eight-hour preservation order. As the doors opened, Evin named a corridor that did not appear on the official plan. By evening the hospital looked much the same as it had that morning. The corridors were still narrow, the work still hard, and most questions still open. Yet one person had been allowed to choose where to stand and what to say. Adela treated that small change as a duty, not a victory speech. The treatment bulletin threatened to provoke an uprising if its claims went unchallenged. A station volunteer offered to usher worried readers toward the correction board. The versatile ward cart could carry medicines, blankets, or emergency water. The steward declined to veto every public bulletin merely because one was suspect. Evin was a widow who relied on the depot's evening wages.

結束當日紀錄前，艾黛拉詢問哪些地方仍不確定，以及誰的同意還沒有被聽見。法庭發出四十八小時保存令。大門打開時，艾文說出一條官方平面圖上不存在的走廊。 傍晚時醫院看來與早上一樣，走廊仍窄、工作仍難、問題大多未解。但至少一個人得以決定自己站在哪裡、說什麼話。艾黛拉把這小改變看成責任，不是值得慶功的演說。 若醫療公告內容無人質疑，可能引發起義。 車站志工主動引導憂心的讀者前往更正醫療公告欄。 多功能病房推車可以運送藥品、毛毯或緊急飲水。 管理員沒有因一份醫療通報可疑就否決所有醫療通報的發行。 艾文是一名寡婦，靠轉運站晚班工資生活。
