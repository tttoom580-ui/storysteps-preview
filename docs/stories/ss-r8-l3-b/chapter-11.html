箭頭背後的數字

The Number Behind the Arrow

把夜路畫成星圖 · 第 11 / 25 章 · 2000–3000 · 英中對照 · 校訂中

用互動版查詞、朗讀與收藏

The council asked for accurate travel times rather than estimates. Nora measured each route again, this time with volunteers who chose different walking speeds. They used a phone only to record time, not to decide what a person's body should do. An older carpenter explained that a ditch beside the road had grown deeper after the last storm. Eli inspected the loose cover near it and reported the location to the county office. Nora discovered that a simple arrow on a map could carry a dangerous amount of confidence when it failed to explain the conditions beneath it.

市議會要求提供準確的步行時間，而不是估計。知禾再次測量每條路，這次請不同步速的志工自行選擇速度。手機只用來記錄時間，不用來決定人的身體應該怎麼走。年長木匠說，路邊排水溝在上次風暴後變得更深。莊岳檢查旁邊鬆動的蓋板，向縣府回報位置。知禾發現，地圖上一支簡單箭頭，若沒有說明底下的實際條件，可能帶著危險的自信。

At the recreation center a diver gave a safety talk near the freezer room. The center stored gasoline for an emergency generator behind a locked gate, far from the main hall. A volunteer proposed carrying a plastic model of the route for visitors who found printed maps difficult. Eli said the design was intelligent, but it needed an operation plan and real tests. Another student wanted to perform a dramatic demonstration, making the audience guess which path was safe. Nora rejected the idea: safety was not a game in which a wrong answer deserved a punishment. They would explain the theory plainly and show the evidence.

休閒中心裡，潛水員在冷凍庫旁進行安全說明。中心在遠離主廳的上鎖區域存放緊急發電機的汽油。一名志工提議做塑膠立體路線模型，讓不方便閱讀平面地圖的訪客也能使用。莊岳說設計很聰明，但必須有操作計畫和實測。另一名學生想做戲劇化示範，讓觀眾猜哪條路安全。知禾否決了：安全不是答錯就該受罰的遊戲。他們應清楚解釋原理，並展示證據。

Workers moved timber across the yard while violet flowers grew beside a low wall. Nora watched the muscle of a person's arm as he raised a heavy plank; even a strong worker began to sweat after a few trips. Eli said the structure was roughly safe for a handcart but not yet checked for wheelchair access. A rushed test could weaken everyone's trust, so he asked the center to approve a proper inspection. Nora used local media notices to invite volunteers without revealing anyone's personal journey. The team decided that one clear warning was more useful than ten confident guesses.

工人在院子搬運木材，低牆旁長著紫羅蘭。知禾看著有人用手臂肌肉抬起厚木板；即使強壯，來回幾趟也汗流浹背。莊岳說那個結構對手推車而言大致安全，但尚未檢查輪椅是否能通行。草率測試可能削弱大家的信任，因此他請中心核准正式檢查。知禾透過地方媒體公告招募志工，卻不公開任何人的私人路線。團隊認為，一句清楚的警告比十句自信猜測更有用。

After the test, Nora placed two travel times beside each route, both clearly labeled by conditions. One number reflected an unobstructed walk; the other recorded a slower journey with a rest. She knew some readers would call this complicated. Eli said the complexity was already there in the street; the map was finally honest enough to show it. They sat on the hall steps until the cleaning staff asked them to leave. On the way out, Nora caught herself looking forward to discussing another difficult question with him. She did not need agreement to enjoy his attention.

測試後，知禾在每條路旁放了兩種步行時間，並清楚標示測試條件。一個數字是在沒有障礙下行走，另一個記錄中途休息的較慢行程。她知道有讀者會嫌複雜。莊岳說，複雜原本就在街上，現在只是地圖終於誠實呈現。他們坐在會館階梯上，直到清潔人員請他們離開。走出去時，知禾發現自己期待再次與他討論難題。她不需要每件事都達成一致，也能享受他的陪伴。

At the county library, a teacher asked the students to bless the opening of the atlas with a speech. Nora said a kind wish was welcome, but it could not replace checking the route. A retired swimmer explained how to dive safely only after inspecting depth and water conditions. The comparison helped the class: a person should not step into an unknown path merely because the map looked inviting. Eli showed a photograph of the loose cover and explained who had authority to repair it. The students added a note saying that the location was under review, rather than silently moving the marker to a nicer place.

在縣立圖書館，一位老師請學生用祝福詞為地圖集揭幕。知禾說善意的祝福很受歡迎，但不能代替路線查核。退休游泳教練解釋，潛水前必須檢查水深與環境，才能安全下潛。這個比喻幫助學生理解：不能只因地圖看起來誘人，就走進不明的道路。莊岳展示鬆動蓋板的照片，說明誰有權維修。學生加上「待審查」註記，而不是默默把標記移到比較好看的位置。

The new time chart raised another question. Should the map show the fastest possible journey or the time a person could expect on an ordinary night? Nora proposed showing a range and explaining how it had been measured. Eli insisted that the upper number should include pauses for crossings and rest. They asked several volunteers to test the wording before publication. A young reader said the explanation helped her feel less embarrassed about walking slowly. Nora remembered her mother and felt a small, steady hope. It was not important that everyone moved at the same speed. It was important that nobody had to disappear from the map.

新的時間表又帶出問題：地圖應標最快可達時間，還是普通夜晚實際可能需要的時間？知禾建議列出範圍，解釋如何測量。莊岳堅持較長時間必須包含過馬路與休息的停頓。他們請幾位志工試讀再發行。年輕讀者說，這樣的說明讓她不再因走得慢而難為情。知禾想起母親，感到一點穩定的希望。重點不是人人速度相同，而是任何人都不該從地圖消失。
