診所與警告

The Clinic and the Warning

把夜路畫成星圖 · 第 6 / 25 章 · 2000–3000 · 英中對照 · 校訂中

用互動版查詞、朗讀與收藏

Monday's route passed a night clinic, a food stall, and a low apartment passage. Nora had assumed that medical workers wanted the fastest road home. A nurse corrected her: after twelve hours on her feet, she needed a well-lit place where she could stop without blocking anyone. Eli measured the pavement and watched where carts bumped the curb. The group recorded the width of the inner walkway and the height of each step. Nora began to understand that the word “safe” was not one single promise. It described a series of choices made by people with different bodies, schedules, and needs.

星期一的路線經過夜間診所、小吃攤和低矮的公寓通道。知禾原以為醫護人員只想走最快的路。一名護理師糾正她：連續站了十二小時後，她需要明亮、可以停下歇腳又不擋路的地方。莊岳測量人行道，也觀察推車在哪裡撞上路緣。團隊記下內側走道的寬度及每階高度。知禾逐漸明白，「安全」不是單一承諾，而是一連串為不同身體、作息與需要所做的選擇。

A biology teacher explained that bacteria could multiply in standing water near the clinic. The project creator of an old drainage plan had designed the ditch as a necessity, but no one had cleared it recently. A local politician arrived to praise community excellence while asking the students not to mention missing public resources. Nora thanked him for visiting, then wrote his request in the meeting summary. Her stomach tightened when he called the stale water a small problem. Eli said that a suspect claim was not proof of wrongdoing; they needed a direct inspection. The teacher demonstrated a simple technique for taking clean samples and warned them not to touch the water.

生物老師解釋，診所附近的積水可能讓細菌繁殖。舊排水計畫的設計者本來把那條溝當作必要設施，卻已經很久沒有人清理。地方政治人物到場稱讚社區的優秀表現，並要求學生不要提到公共資源不足。知禾感謝他來訪，卻把這項要求如實寫入會議摘要。他把發臭的死水說成小問題時，知禾感到胃部一緊。莊岳說，可疑的說法不能直接當成不法證據；他們需要實際檢查。老師示範乾淨採樣的簡單方法，並警告大家別碰水。

“Anyhow, we can mark the place as needing inspection,” Nora said. A teenage volunteer worried that this language sounded weak. Eli answered that honest limits were stronger than invented certainty. They had seen a tropical storm turn a shallow ditch into a fast stream; water could change the meaning of a street overnight. Across the lane, a shopkeeper covered fruit with waxed paper and asked whether the group could recommend his stall. Nora explained that the map was not an advertisement. The owner looked disappointed, then offered to tell them when the clinic lights usually failed. His information was useful because it could be checked.

「總之，我們可以先標示這裡需要檢查。」知禾說。一位青少年志工擔心這樣聽來太軟弱。莊岳回答，誠實承認限制，比憑空捏造確定性更有力量。他們看過熱帶風暴讓淺溝變成急流；水可能在一夜之間改變街道的意義。對街店主用上蠟紙包水果，問團隊能不能推薦他的攤位。知禾解釋地圖不是廣告。老闆有些失望，後來卻主動告知診所的燈通常何時故障。這資訊有用，因為可以查證。

They made two records at the clinic: one for the public map and one for the officials responsible for maintenance. The public version showed a warning and a tested detour. The private report included photographs, dates, and measurements that could be verified. Nora asked Eli to sign the technical sheet; he asked her to sign the statements about resident interviews. For once, they were not arguing over which club deserved more credit. Their names stood side by side because the task truly required both kinds of work. As they left, the nurse pointed out a third possible path neither had noticed.

他們在診所留下兩份紀錄：一份供公開地圖使用，另一份交給負責維護的公務單位。公開版標示警告及已測試的繞道路線；內部報告則包括可核對的照片、日期與尺寸。知禾請莊岳在技術表上簽名，莊岳則請她在居民訪談的說明上簽名。這次他們沒有爭誰比較值得得到功勞，兩人的名字並列，是因為工作真的需要兩種能力。離開時，護理師指出第三條他們都沒注意到的小路。

The next clinic visit began with a mistaken assumption. Nora had written “well lit” on her draft, but a nurse explained that a bright lamp outside the entrance shone directly into the eyes of people leaving the building. A small bump in the pavement was easy to miss after a long shift. Eli showed how a light could be aimed downward without reducing visibility. Across the street, a display for a children's science fair showed a toy missile, but the nurse laughed when someone compared its movement to the water running through the ditch. The actual problem was less exciting and more urgent: the blocked drain needed official maintenance.

下一次去診所，從一個錯誤假設開始。知禾在草稿寫上「照明良好」，護理師卻解釋入口外的強燈會直射下班者眼睛，讓人看不清路面的一個小凸起。莊岳示範燈光可往下照，不必降低可見度。對街兒童科學展展示玩具飛彈，有人把它的移動比喻成溝裡的水流，護理師聽了笑起來。但真正的問題沒那麼刺激，卻更迫切：堵住的排水口需要官方維護。

Nora added a column for the resource each repair would require: time, tools, trained people, or public approval. She also wrote down which statements were still guesses. A shop assistant brought wax crayons so children could draw what a safe crossing looked like to them. One child put a bench beside every corner; another drew a giant umbrella over the whole road. The pictures were impossible as construction plans, but useful as evidence of what the children valued. Eli said their ideas could become test questions. Nora agreed, and for once their notebooks carried the same heading without either club losing its identity. She refused to assume the brightest streetlamp was necessarily the safest one.

知禾在表上增加一欄，列出每項修復需要的資源：時間、工具、受訓人員或公共核准。她也標出哪些說法仍只是猜測。店員拿來蠟筆，讓孩子畫出心目中安全的路口。一個孩子每個角落都畫長椅，另一個則在整條街畫了大雨傘。這些畫不能直接當工程圖，卻能讓人看見孩子重視什麼。莊岳說，這些想法可以轉成測試問題。知禾同意了。兩本筆記第一次寫下相同的標題，卻沒有讓任何一社失去自己的身分。 她拒絕假設最亮的路燈必然就是最安全的。
