安全一步的代價

The Price of a Safe Step

把夜路畫成星圖 · 第 2 / 25 章 · 2000–3000 · 英中對照

用互動版查詞、朗讀與收藏

Eli brought a careful budget to the station, with prices for tools, reflectors, and a replacement lamp. Nora arrived carrying colored pencils, a measuring tape, and a folded invitation from the neighborhood committee. She wanted to know what a safe crossing felt like to someone carrying heavy shopping. He wanted to know what parts would fail after a decade of rain. Their questions seemed different until a woman with a cane stopped before the stairs. The old map drew a straight line through the station yard. The woman could see the destination, but there was no level path to reach it. Nora crossed out her own attractive arrow before Eli could say a word.

莊岳帶著仔細列好的預算來到車站，裡面有工具、反光片與更換燈具的價格。知禾則帶來色鉛筆、捲尺，以及里民委員會的摺疊邀請函。她想知道提著重物的人走過路口時有什麼感受；他想知道哪些零件會在十年風雨後壞掉。兩人的問題原本不同，直到一位拄著手杖的女士停在階梯前。舊地圖畫了一條筆直穿越站前廣場的線，女士看得見目的地，卻沒有平坦的路能走到那裡。莊岳還沒開口，知禾就劃掉自己畫得很好看的箭頭。

The community hall hosted a bowling club next to a class that drew animals. A child had painted a beast on the door, and a professor of geography explained how hills changed walking time. His necktie was crooked, but his map was precise. An expressive passenger described a bus stop hidden behind a wall. She said the view could frighten someone traveling alone. A drunk visitor had once broken a light there, and the replacement kit had never arrived. Eli tied a bright lace around a temporary marker so people could find the detour. Nora wrote that neither fear nor comfort could be measured by distance alone.

社區會館裡，保齡球社旁邊有一堂動物繪畫課。孩子在門上畫了野獸，地理學教授則解釋坡度如何改變步行時間。他的領帶歪歪的，地圖卻畫得很精確。一位表情豐富的乘客描述一個藏在牆後的公車站，她說獨自出行的人可能會因此受到驚嚇。曾有一名酒醉訪客打壞那裡的燈，但替換工具組始終沒送達。莊岳把亮色鞋帶綁在臨時標記上，讓人能找到繞道處。知禾寫下：恐懼與安心都不能只用距離衡量。

A greedy contractor offered to paint every wall if the clubs would permit his company to control the final map. Eli refused. The permit for public signs did not give a private seller control over people's routes. Nora suggested a small poll among night workers, with clear space for people to disagree. An older voter said she regretted trusting the painted shortcut for years. The risk was not limited to one semester of classes, she explained; residents used these streets every day. The meeting made one thing visible: a map could be accurate on paper and still exclude a person in practice.

一名貪心的承包商表示，只要兩社允許他的公司掌控最終地圖，就願意替所有牆面上漆。莊岳拒絕了。公共標誌的許可不代表私人商家可以決定大家的路線。知禾提議對夜班工作者做小型民調，並留下明確的反對選項。一位年長選民說，她很後悔多年來相信地上畫出的捷徑。她解釋，風險不是只持續一個學期，而是居民每天都得經過。會議讓大家看清一件事：地圖在紙上可能正確，實際上卻仍排除某些人。

They walked the accessible route together, not at the pace of the fastest volunteer but at the pace chosen by the woman with the cane. It took nine minutes longer than the painted shortcut. At the end, she asked whether the repair club could fix the low rail that made turning difficult. Eli measured it and promised only to investigate, because he did not have permission to alter public property. Nora liked that he could say no without dismissing the request. She drew two route lines and a question mark where the rail stood. A good map, she realized, should show what was unknown as carefully as what was certain.

他們一起走無障礙路線，不照最快志工的步伐，而是讓拄杖女士自己決定速度。這條路比地上畫的捷徑多花九分鐘。走到終點，女士問修理社能不能修好那段妨礙轉彎的低欄杆。莊岳量了尺寸，只承諾先調查，因為他沒有權限擅自修改公有設施。知禾欣賞他能夠拒絕，卻不忽視別人的要求。她畫出兩條路線，在欄杆旁加了問號。她漸漸明白，好地圖對尚未確認的事，也應該像對確定的事一樣仔細標示。

On the way back, the woman with the cane asked to examine the club's first draft. She was not angry, but she did not hide her regret at seeing the same inaccessible line repeated in so many brochures. Nora allowed her to cross out the route and write a comment directly on the paper. It was difficult to watch a stranger change something she had drawn for hours. Yet the correction came from a person who would actually use the path. Eli asked whether the woman wanted her name attached to the comment. She declined, and they respected that choice without making it an obstacle to using the information.

回程時，拄杖女士想看社團的第一版草稿。她沒有生氣，卻坦白說，看到許多手冊一再畫同一條無法通行的線，讓她非常後悔過去曾相信那些圖。知禾讓她直接在紙上劃掉路線並寫意見。看著陌生人修改自己畫了好幾小時的作品很難受，但更正來自真正需要走那條路的人。莊岳問女士想不想署名；她拒絕了。兩人尊重選擇，沒有因此排除她提供的資訊。

The test also changed the clubs' budgets. Eli moved money from decorative banners to measuring equipment and temporary safety markers. Nora reduced the printing cost by proposing one small trial edition instead of a thousand finished copies. The changes made neither proposal more exciting on a poster, but they made both easier to check. Their teacher asked them to explain the decisions in writing so later volunteers could understand them. Nora noticed that Eli never called her drawings useless. He asked what each picture helped a reader decide. She began asking him the same kind of question about his tools. Their work became sharper without becoming less kind.

測試也改變兩社的預算。莊岳把裝飾布條的費用移到測量器材和臨時安全標記；知禾則提議先少量試印，不一次印一千本。這些更動沒有讓海報更吸引人，卻讓兩份提案更容易檢查。老師請他們寫下決策原因，讓後來的志工能理解。知禾發現莊岳從不說她的圖沒用，而是問每張圖能幫讀者做什麼決定。她也開始用同樣方式問他的工具。工作變得更精準，態度卻沒有因此冷漠。
