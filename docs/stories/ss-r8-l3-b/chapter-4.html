The Alley with No Arrow

把夜路畫成星圖 · 第 4 / 25 章 · 2000–3000 · 英中對照

用互動版查詞、朗讀與收藏

The narrow alley behind the market was missing from both grant proposals. Nora had walked past it for years because the walls were ugly and the light was poor. When a resident named Mrs. Sun invited them inside, she discovered a useful passage between the clinic and the evening bus stop. A step had cracked near the entrance. People moved around it by instinct, but a visitor might not see the danger. Nora asked Eli to examine the step while she recorded the route from three different heights. She would not publish a line that looked simple from above but became confusing on the ground.

市場後方的窄巷在兩份補助提案裡都不見蹤影。知禾多年來經過這裡都沒有走進去，因為牆面難看、照明也差。一位姓孫的居民邀請兩人進巷，她才發現那裡有條連接診所與晚間公車站的便道。入口附近有一階樓梯裂了，熟客會直覺繞開，外來者卻可能看不出危險。知禾請莊岳檢查台階，自己則從三種不同高度記錄路線。她不願公布一條俯視看來簡單、走到地面卻令人困惑的路。

Mrs. Sun called her son's decision to guide neighbors an act of bravery. He had installed a small cable light after a minor crash, but the wire was no longer safe. A cherry tree grew beside a wall, and under it someone had left a knitted scarf with a handwritten label. Nora felt dizzy when she saw the word “shortcut” painted above the broken stairs. A news report had used the word murder in a different case, and rumors had made some residents afraid of every dark corner. Eli said a frightening story was not a definition of danger. They would investigate the physical path first and separate rumors from evidence.

孫女士說，兒子主動帶鄰居走路是種勇敢的表現。一次小碰撞之後，他裝過一盞接著電纜的小燈，但電線如今已不安全。牆旁種著櫻桃樹，樹下有人留了條編織圍巾，上面貼著手寫標籤。知禾看見破階梯上方漆著「捷徑」兩字，感到一陣暈眩。新聞曾在另一樁案件使用「謀殺」一詞，流言卻讓部分居民害怕所有黑暗角落。莊岳說，嚇人的故事不等於對危險的定義。他們應先調查實際路況，讓傳聞與證據分開。

The owner of a small shop offered them tea on a saucer and said that his new sign was “sexy enough to attract tourists.” Nora smiled, then asked about the solid handrail beneath it. The owner admitted it was too low. A stray dog had once nearly starved in that alley; neighbors had fed it and built a shelter near the old tower. A parked van now blocked the shelter entrance. Mrs. Sun took a nap while they worked, and afterward returned with sandwiches. She was thankful that the visitors listened instead of treating the place as an empty problem. Her welcome brought warmth to a cold afternoon.

小店老闆用茶碟端來茶，還說自己的新招牌「夠性感，能吸引觀光客」。知禾笑了笑，接著詢問招牌下方那段堅固欄杆。老闆承認欄杆太低。以前有隻流浪狗差點在巷裡餓死，鄰居輪流餵牠，還在老塔旁蓋了遮蔽處；如今一輛廂型車卻擋住入口。眾人工作時，孫女士小睡了一會，醒來後帶來三明治。她很感謝訪客願意傾聽，而不是把巷子當成一個空洞的問題。寒冷的午後因此多了溫暖。

Instead of putting a bright star on the alley, Nora drew an open circle. A star meant a place they had tested; the circle meant more work remained. Eli photographed the unsafe cable and submitted a maintenance request to the city, with Mrs. Sun's permission. He refused to repair the electrical line himself because the public utility had to inspect it first. Their decision disappointed a student who wanted a quick success photograph. Nora understood the student's frustration, but she had seen how easily a beautiful image could become a false promise. The final map would have to admit its limits in plain language.

知禾沒有在巷子上畫亮星，而是畫了一個空心圓。星星代表路線已測試，圓圈表示還有工作未完成。莊岳取得孫女士同意後，拍下不安全的電纜，向市府提出維修申請。他拒絕自行修理，因為公用電線必須先由主管單位檢查。想拍快速成果照的一名學生很失望。知禾理解那份挫折，卻也見識過美麗影像如何輕易變成虛假的承諾。最終地圖必須用直白的話承認自己的限制。

Later the scarf's owner returned. She said her grandmother had taught her to knit while they waited for the evening bus. The scarf had slipped from her bag, not been abandoned. Nora gave it back and asked permission to sketch the story of the bus stop. The young woman said yes, but asked that her grandmother's name remain private. Their conversation reminded Nora that objects on a street were not always clues to a crime; they often belonged to ordinary lives. The van owner came back, moved his vehicle, and apologized for blocking the dog's shelter. Someone brought food for the dog so it would not starve again.

後來，圍巾主人回來了。她說祖母以前在等晚班公車時教她編織，圍巾只是從袋子裡滑出，並非被遺棄。知禾把圍巾交還，徵求同意描畫這個公車站故事。年輕女子同意，但希望祖母姓名保密。談話提醒知禾，街上的物品不一定是犯罪線索，更常屬於平凡生活。廂型車車主回來挪車，也為堵住狗屋道歉。有人替狗送來食物，讓牠不會再挨餓。

Nora and Eli stayed until the shop owners finished their evening deliveries. They learned that the alley was busiest just after six, when workers rushed from the clinic toward the buses. They also learned that the shaded corner was quiet enough for people to rest. Nora proposed marking both details, because one map could show an area's rhythm without calling it wholly good or bad. Eli promised to submit the damaged-rail report on Monday. Before they parted, Mrs. Sun asked them to return after the repairs. Nora said they would, but only when the city could confirm that work was complete. A star would have to be earned.

知禾和莊岳留到店家完成晚間送貨，才知道這條巷子在六點過後最擁擠，診所下班的工人會急著趕公車。他們也發現陰涼轉角足夠安靜，適合短暫休息。知禾提議把兩種情況都標出來，因為地圖可以呈現地方的節奏，而不是簡單說整區好或壞。莊岳承諾星期一提交欄杆損壞報告。分開前，孫女士請他們修好後再回來。知禾答應，但要等市府確認工程完成，才會把這裡畫上星星。星星必須值得。
