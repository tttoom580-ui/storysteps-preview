The Photograph Nobody Wanted

把夜路畫成星圖 · 第 8 / 25 章 · 2000–3000 · 英中對照 · 校訂中

用互動版查詞、朗讀與收藏

The third week began with a disagreement about photographs. Nora planned to show the route beside the riverside warehouses. Eli wanted to publish the location of a dangerous crossing immediately. A resident pointed out that one picture also showed the window of a person who had asked not to appear in any report. Nora froze. The picture was useful evidence, but publishing it without permission would break their promise. She asked the person what kind of record would be acceptable. The answer was simple: show the road, not my home. Nora changed the angle and kept the original file in restricted storage.

第三週一開始，團隊就為照片起了爭執。知禾想展示河岸倉庫旁的路線；莊岳則想立刻公開危險路口的位置。一位居民指出，其中一張照片也拍到某個不願在報告出現的人的窗戶。知禾愣住了。照片是重要證據，但未經允許公開就違背了承諾。她詢問當事人願意接受哪種紀錄，得到的回答很簡單：拍道路，不要拍我的家。知禾換了角度，將原檔留在限制存取的空間裡。

Near the storehouse, a buffalo painted on a wall appeared to charge at a passing bicycle. An old bullet-shaped sign hung beside the gate. A citizen who operated the traffic controller explained why drivers could not see pedestrians from one corner. Behind a small cooker, workers sold crispy rice snacks. A porter carrying baggage asked them to grab his trolley before it rolled away. He had seen litter collect in that gutter lately and believed the city still owed the area a cleanup. Nora recorded his request, but not his face. Every useful observation came with a question about who had the right to share it.

倉庫附近，牆上的水牛彩繪看起來像要衝向路過的腳踏車。大門旁掛著一塊形似子彈的舊標誌。一位負責交通控制器的市民解釋，駕駛為什麼看不到從某個轉角走出的行人。小型炊具後面，工人賣著酥脆米點心。一名搬運行李的腳夫請他們抓住快滑走的推車。他說最近那條排水溝常積垃圾，市府仍欠這一區一次清理。知禾記下他的請求，卻沒拍他的臉。每一項有用觀察，都伴隨著誰有權分享它的問題。

A talkative shop owner suggested putting the whole project on the web to attract wealthy sponsors. Another visitor began to yell when the team refused to identify an apartment resident. Eli stepped between them without raising his voice. He said that requesting a safer street did not mean giving up personal privacy. Nora noticed how the man's shadow stretched across the loading dock. She also noticed that her own first idea had been too broad. The process required consent at every step, especially when a photograph looked harmless to the person taking it. They asked an independent adviser to review their draft rules.

一位健談的店主建議把整個計畫放上網，吸引富有的贊助者。團隊拒絕透露公寓住戶身分，另一名訪客便大喊大叫。莊岳站到兩人之間，卻沒有提高音量。他說，要求街道更安全，不代表必須放棄個人隱私。知禾看見男子的影子延伸到裝卸碼頭，也發現自己最初的想法太粗糙。整個過程每一步都需要同意，尤其當照片對拍攝者看起來毫無問題時。他們請獨立顧問檢查草擬的規則。

That evening Nora erased a favorite drawing from the public presentation. The lines had been beautiful, but they revealed too much about someone else's life. Eli sat across from her and said he knew what it felt like to lose hours of work. She replied that protecting a person was not losing the work; it was choosing what the work was for. He agreed without trying to make her feel better too quickly. Together they developed a small symbol showing when a photograph had been changed to protect privacy. The symbol did not hide the change. It explained it.

當晚，知禾從公開簡報刪掉一張自己很喜歡的畫。線條很美，卻透露了太多別人的生活。莊岳坐在她對面，說自己懂得失去數小時心血的滋味。她回答，保護一個人不是失去作品，而是選擇作品為何而存在。他沒有急著安慰她，只是表示同意。兩人一起設計了一個小符號，標示照片因保護隱私而調整過。這符號不會掩飾變更，而是解釋變更。

The privacy debate continued at the next club meeting. A resident said the city owed her a truthful warning about the crossing, yet she did not want strangers to study her home's windows. Nora agreed that both wishes were reasonable. Another student brought up sexual harassment on dark streets and asked whether the atlas could help without exposing people who had experienced it. Nora said the guide could mark lighting, available assistance, and verified reporting contacts. It would not publish personal stories without clear consent. Eli supported the distinction. He cared about safety, but he would not ask someone to surrender privacy as the price of being heard.

下一次社團會議延續隱私爭論。一位居民說市府欠她對路口的真實警示，但她不希望陌生人研究自己家的窗戶。知禾同意兩項願望都合理。另一名學生提到暗巷性騷擾，問地圖能否提供幫助，又不暴露經歷者。知禾說，指南可以標示照明、可求助場所及已核實的通報聯絡資訊，但未經明確同意，不會公布個人故事。莊岳支持這個區別。他在意安全，也不會要求人們以交出隱私作為被傾聽的代價。

They revised the photo policy together. The public copy would show only the section of road needed to explain the crossing. An internal image might preserve more detail for approved staff, with a record of why it was kept. Residents could ask for a review of either version. Nora knew that this work would be slower than posting pictures immediately, but she preferred a careful map to a popular mistake. Eli walked her to the train after the meeting. She told him that the hardest part of leading a club was realizing that a good intention did not excuse a careless action. He said he was still learning that lesson too. The officials still owe the residents a clear account of the crossing's condition.

他們一起修訂照片規則。公開版只顯示說明路口必須看見的道路部分；內部圖片可讓獲准人員保留較多細節，同時記錄保存理由。居民有權要求審查兩種版本。知禾知道這比立刻上傳照片慢很多，但她寧願做仔細的地圖，也不要受歡迎的錯誤。會後莊岳陪她走到車站。她說，帶領社團最難的是發現好意不能成為粗心的藉口。他說自己也還在學這一課。 主管單位仍欠居民一份對路口現況的清楚說明。
