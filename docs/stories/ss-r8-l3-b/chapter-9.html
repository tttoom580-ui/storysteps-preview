消失的渡口小路

The Missing Ferry Path

把夜路畫成星圖 · 第 9 / 25 章 · 2000–3000 · 英中對照 · 校訂中

用互動版查詞、朗讀與收藏

Nora was asked to inspect the ferry cabin and the steps beside it. The public timetable showed a level passage, but it had been closed for months. A weekly repair report described a missing handrail, and nobody could tell her why the closure was absent from the city database. Eli believed it might be a simple delay in updating records. Nora was less certain. Instead of guessing who was to blame, they agreed to ask the ferry office for the last approved plan and compare it with the path people actually used. Their decision disappointed a student who wanted a dramatic mystery. Nora preferred an ordinary answer that could be proved.

知禾受託檢查渡輪船艙及旁邊的階梯。公開時刻表畫著一段平坦通道，那裡卻已封閉好幾個月。每週維修報告提到扶手遺失，沒人能解釋為何市府資料庫沒更新封閉資訊。莊岳認為可能只是登錄延遲，知禾卻不確定。兩人沒有急著猜誰該負責，而是向渡輪辦公室索取最後核准的圖面，和人們實際使用的路徑比對。一位想看到戲劇性謎團的學生很失望，知禾卻更喜歡可以證明的平凡答案。

On the pier a horn made a child jump in fright. Water began to splash against the lower stairs, and a worker warned that the passage would sink beneath the high tide shortly. A skilled sailor showed them the safer upper walkway. A visitor joked that the roar of the engines could bore anyone waiting in line, but another passenger said the noise caused her to faint. Someone asked if a weapon had damaged the rail; the sailor answered that he had seen no such evidence. Nora recorded the laughter that followed, then wrote that jokes and guesses could never replace measurements.

碼頭上的喇叭聲讓孩子嚇了一跳。浪花拍打下層階梯，工人警告漲潮後那段通路很快就會被水淹沒。熟練的船員帶他們走較安全的上方步道。有訪客開玩笑說，引擎轟鳴會讓排隊的人無聊到發呆；另一位乘客卻說噪音令她幾乎昏倒。有人問欄杆是否被武器破壞，船員回答自己沒看過那種證據。眾人的笑聲過後，知禾寫下：笑話與猜測永遠不能代替測量。

A teenager had drawn a tune across the waiting-room wall in colored notes. Her lover, an amateur musician, had planned to play it for travelers, but the event was canceled. The two began to wander along the water to look for another place. Nora asked them whether a map could show quiet areas without treating quiet as an empty space. They welcomed the idea. Nearby, a mechanic had cut his hand; it began to bleed slightly, and Eli directed him to the first-aid station. Their route work had become a series of small decisions about care, not an adventure whose worth depended on danger.

一名少女在候船室牆上畫了一段彩色音符。她的戀人是業餘音樂家，原想演奏給旅客聽，活動卻取消了。兩人於是沿水邊散步，找其他演出地點。知禾問他們，地圖是否能標示安靜區，卻不把安靜當作什麼都沒有的空白。他們很贊成。附近有機械工割傷手，傷口稍微流血，莊岳引導他去急救站。路線工作已變成一連串關於照顧的小小決策，而不是必須靠危險才顯得有價值的冒險。

By evening the ferry office found two different plans. One had been approved before the rail broke; the other described a temporary closure but had never reached the public website. Nora wrote “missing update,” not “deliberate lie.” Eli checked the date of each document and photographed the notice at the pier. They recommended an upper path and placed the lower stairs under review. On the ride home, Nora asked Eli why he had wanted a repair station so badly. He told her that his father had once missed work because one damaged wheel had cost more than he could afford. She listened without trying to turn the story into a campaign slogan.

傍晚，渡輪辦公室找出兩份不同圖面。一份在扶手損壞前核准，另一份記錄臨時封閉，卻從未更新到公開網站。知禾寫的是「更新遺漏」，而不是「蓄意說謊」。莊岳核對每份文件的日期，並拍下碼頭公告。兩人建議使用上方通路，下層樓梯則待重新審查。回程時，知禾問莊岳為何那麼想成立維修站。他說父親曾因一個壞掉的車輪修不起而無法上班。知禾只是聽著，沒有急著把故事變成宣傳口號。

A lesson in democracy appeared in an unlikely place: the ferry waiting room. The office had posted a suggestion box but never told passengers which requests were accepted or rejected. Nora asked for a record of past decisions and whether a passenger could appeal a closure. A skillful clerk explained the process and admitted that the public notices were difficult to understand. Eli suggested a chart showing which office handled each kind of problem. The clerk agreed to test the chart with actual passengers before posting it. Nora realized that being consulted meant little if no one ever learned what happened to the answer.

一堂關於民主的課竟出現在渡輪候船室。辦公室設了意見箱，卻從不告訴旅客哪些請求被接受、哪些被拒絕。知禾詢問過去的決策紀錄，以及乘客能否對封閉措施申訴。熟練的職員解釋程序，也承認公告難懂。莊岳建議畫出各類問題應由哪個單位處理的流程圖。職員同意先讓真正乘客試讀再公布。知禾明白了：人們若永遠不知道回覆的結果，「已諮詢」就沒有太大意義。

The ferry line moved slowly as the sky darkened. Nora noticed an elderly rider waiting apart from the crowd, unsure which gate had changed. Instead of pointing from a distance, she approached and asked whether he wanted help reading the notice. He did. Afterward she added a large-print version to her draft and asked the office to check its accuracy. Eli stood by the railing, checking the safe distance between passengers and moving equipment. Neither action was dramatic. Together, they made the ferry terminal less confusing for someone who had to use it on an ordinary night.

天色漸暗，渡輪隊伍緩慢移動。知禾注意到一位年長乘客獨自站在人群旁，不確定哪個閘門改了。她沒有遠遠指方向，而是走近問他需不需要幫忙讀公告。他表示需要。事後，知禾在草稿加入大字版本，請辦公室確認正確性。莊岳站在欄杆旁，檢查乘客與移動設備之間的安全距離。兩件事都不戲劇化，卻一起讓某個普通夜晚必須搭船的人少一點困惑。
