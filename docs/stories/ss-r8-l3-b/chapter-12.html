藍色資料夾

The Blue Folder

把夜路畫成星圖 · 第 12 / 25 章 · 2000–3000 · 英中對照 · 校訂中

用互動版查詞、朗讀與收藏

The group needed reliable information about the tunnel beneath the station. Nora found a blue folder in the public archive, but several pages described a road that no longer existed. She took the folder aside and asked an assistant at the service desk whether a newer version was available. The assistant explained that the land was partly public property and partly leased to a private client. A careful edit had to separate the two. Nora thanked her and prepared a list of questions rather than copying the old map into her new one. Eli meanwhile checked the electricity records for the lamps along the upper road.

團隊需要可靠資料，確認車站地下通道的情況。知禾在公開檔案室找到藍色資料夾，幾頁卻描述早已不存在的道路。她把資料夾放到一旁，詢問服務台助理有沒有新版。助理解釋，土地有部分是公有財產，另一部分租給私人承租方；編修時必須清楚分開。知禾道謝，先列出問題，而不是直接把舊圖抄進新圖。莊岳則去查上方道路的路燈用電紀錄。

In the waiting room, an elderly sailor used a tablet to show a photograph of the tunnel's sealed entrance. Its hatch had been painted blue before the closure. A sudden flash from a camera startled him, and a child blew a bubble near the window. He spoke of a narrow canyon where he once sailed, then smiled at the comparison: the old tunnel also seemed to trap voices between its walls. Nora did not treat his memory as proof of the tunnel's present condition. She asked for the photograph's date and received a clear answer. The sailor was pleased that his experience could help without becoming a legend.

候診室裡，一位年長船員用平板電腦展示封閉隧道入口的照片。入口活門在關閉前漆成藍色。相機突然閃光使他嚇了一跳，窗邊孩子則吹出泡泡。他說起自己曾經航行的狹窄峽谷，接著笑說老隧道也像峽谷一樣，會把聲音困在牆壁間。知禾沒有把記憶當成隧道現況的證據，而是詢問照片日期，得到清楚回答。船員很高興自己的經驗能派上用場，而不必被編成傳說。

A capable engineer explained the monthly inspection schedule. She represented a local maintenance company but had no authority to approve public access. The situation made some students less hopeful, yet Nora insisted that limits were useful information. A cook selling spice bread at the entrance asked whether a sword-shaped metal sign was safe. Eli inspected it from a distance and asked the building manager to examine the attachment. A woman began to weep when she heard that the entrance might remain closed. She used to walk that road with her father. Nora offered to record her memory for the historical section, only if she wished.

一位能幹的工程師解釋每月檢查時程。她代表地方維護公司，但無權核准公共通行。這讓部分學生失去希望，知禾卻堅持限制本身就是有用資訊。入口賣香料麵包的廚師問，一塊劍形金屬招牌是否安全。莊岳保持距離檢查，請建物管理員查看固定處。一名女士聽到入口可能繼續封閉，開始流淚；她以前常與父親走那條路。知禾提出可以把這段回憶放進歷史區，但完全尊重她是否願意。

Their map began to contain two kinds of blue: one for tested routes and another for memories of paths that had closed. Nora liked that the colors looked related but did not pretend to mean the same thing. Eli asked why she had chosen the second shade. She answered that a city was made of stories as well as surfaces, but the two must be labeled differently. He understood. They agreed that the public atlas should not be a promise to reopen the tunnel. It could instead explain the closure honestly and help residents find a path that worked today.

他們的地圖開始使用兩種藍色：一種代表測試過的路線，另一種代表已封閉道路的記憶。知禾喜歡兩種顏色彼此呼應，卻不假裝意義相同。莊岳問她為何選第二種藍，她回答，城市由故事和實際路面共同組成，但兩者需要不同標籤。他懂了。兩人同意，公開地圖集不能承諾重開隧道，應該誠實解釋封閉原因，幫居民找到今天真正能走的路。

The retired captain who had supplied the tunnel photograph visited again. He brought a notebook with dates, names of vessels, and a hand-drawn entrance plan. Nora asked him to confirm which information he had seen himself and which stories he had heard from others. He smiled and said that a captain's memory was not a legal record. The careful distinction made his evidence more useful. Eli compared the plan with the newer archive, finding that a wall had moved during later construction. Nora wrote down the difference and asked the property officer to represent the city in the next review rather than leave the question unresolved.

提供隧道照片的退休船長再次來訪，帶了記有日期、船名與手畫入口圖的筆記本。知禾請他確認哪些資訊是親眼所見，哪些只是聽人說的。他笑說船長的記憶不是法律紀錄。這種仔細區分反而讓證據更有用。莊岳拿圖與較新的檔案比對，發現後來施工曾移動一道牆。知禾記下差異，請財產管理員代表市府參加下一次審查，不再讓問題懸而未決。

That afternoon Nora prepared a history page beside the current route page. She placed the older photograph in a small frame with its date clearly visible. The safe upper path appeared on another page with a warning about the closed lower entrance. Her teacher asked whether readers might confuse the two, so she tried a different title and tested it with three people. All three understood the distinction. Eli brought a simple sketch of the sealed gate and showed where the official notice should stand. The work was slow, but for the first time Nora felt that careful editing was part of the story, not a dull task after the drawing was finished.

那天下午，知禾把歷史頁排在現行路線頁旁。舊照片放在小框內，日期清楚可見；另一頁則標出安全的上方路線，並警告下方入口已封閉。老師擔心讀者混淆，知禾於是換標題，請三個人測試，三人都理解差別。莊岳帶來封閉大門的簡圖，指出官方公告應設在哪裡。過程緩慢，但知禾第一次覺得，細心編輯也是故事的一部分，不是畫完後才要做的無聊工作。
