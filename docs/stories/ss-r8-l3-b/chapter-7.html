市場路線的改變

The Market Switch

把夜路畫成星圖 · 第 7 / 25 章 · 2000–3000 · 英中對照 · 校訂完成

用互動版查詞、朗讀與收藏

A sudden market closure made the team's careful schedule useless. A delivery entrance remained open, but the public gate was locked during repairs. People carrying bags moved toward a narrow brick passage. Nora saw how awful the confusion felt to first-time visitors; even experienced workers looked awkward as they stepped aside for carts. Eli checked the emergency instructions and found that they assumed everyone could use the same set of stairs. They decided to test a different route with a volunteer who used a walking frame. She chose to join because she wanted better signs, not because anyone needed a dramatic photograph.

市場突然關閉，使團隊仔細排好的行程失去用處。送貨入口仍開著，公共大門卻因維修上鎖。提著袋子的人群湧向一道窄窄的磚砌通道。知禾看出這種混亂讓初次來訪者多麼難受；連熟客都因為得閃避推車而顯得笨拙。莊岳檢查緊急指示，發現內容假設所有人都能走同一段樓梯。他們決定請使用助行架的志工一起測試其他路線。她願意參加是因為想要更好的標示，而不是誰需要一張戲劇化照片。

At the corner shop, a cupboard held glass jars of homemade flavor syrup. The owner said he had invented a simple cover that stopped rain from falling into the jars. Nora noticed a lily in a vase, a box of junk, and a faded note about a dime once paid for an old bus ticket. A cook showed them a route behind the building, but warned that hot oil could explode if water reached the stove. Eli reminded the group not to treat every open door as a public passage. A worker's kindness did not create a permanent right to cross private property.

轉角店的櫃子裡放著一排裝了自製調味糖漿的玻璃罐。店主說，他發明了一個簡單罩子，能防止雨滴落進罐中。知禾看見花瓶裡的百合、一箱雜物，以及昔日車票只要一角錢的褪色便條。廚師帶他們看建築後面的通道，卻警告熱油遇水可能爆開。莊岳提醒大家，不能把每扇打開的門都當公共通路。工人的善意，不等於行人取得永久穿越私人土地的權利。

A complaint about low wage rates arrived while they were measuring a ramp. Nora listened but did not pretend that her map could fix every injustice. A worker showed them a cut on his flesh from an exposed edge. Eli asked him to seek medical advice rather than accept an untrained student's guess. They kept a steady pace despite the pressure. Nora promised to remind the market office about the sharp surface and to return sometime after it was repaired. The woman with the walking frame called the revised route terrific, but added that the turning space was still too tight. That detail mattered more than praise.

他們測量坡道時，收到一項關於低工資的抱怨。知禾認真聽，卻沒有假裝一張地圖能修好所有不公。工人展示自己被外露邊緣割傷的皮肉，莊岳請他尋求醫療建議，而不是聽沒有受訓的學生猜測。團隊在壓力下仍維持穩定速度。知禾答應提醒市場管理處注意尖銳表面，並在修好後找時間再來。使用助行架的女士稱讚新路線很棒，卻補充轉彎空間仍然太窄。對團隊來說，那個細節比讚美更重要。

When the gate finally opened, Nora was tempted to mark the original road safe again. Eli asked if the gate would always open at that hour. No one could answer. They left the temporary route on the map, clearly dated, with a note saying it depended on the market manager's permission. Nora had begun the month hoping to draw one beautiful line through the city. Now she was learning to show alternatives, exceptions, and reasons. The woman with the walking frame thanked them for waiting while she spoke. Nora felt proud that they had not mistaken speed for progress.

大門終於打開時，知禾差點又把原路畫成安全路線。莊岳問，這扇門以後每天都會在那個時間開嗎？沒有人能回答。他們於是留下有日期的臨時路線，並清楚註明必須取得市場管理員許可。知禾月初還期待畫出一條穿越城市的美麗線條，如今正學會標出替代方案、例外與原因。使用助行架的女士感謝他們願意等她把話說完。知禾很高興自己沒有把速度誤認為進步。

When market workers heard about the changed gate, several asked the council to appeal the closure. Nora explained that the formal appeal belonged to tenants and business owners, not the student clubs. The clubs could help record the walking problems clearly. A retired inventor joined them and described a movable wooden sign he had built years ago. Its design was simple enough for volunteers to understand, but the new version still needed approval before installation. Nora watched Eli examine the old drawing with care. He never mocked the inventor for using materials different from the ones in his own workshop.

市場工人得知大門改動後，幾人想向市議會申訴封閉措施。知禾解釋，正式申訴權屬於承租人和店主，學生社團只能協助清楚記錄通行問題。一位退休發明家加入他們，介紹多年前製作的可移動木牌。設計簡單，志工容易理解，但新版仍須獲准才能裝設。知禾看著莊岳仔細研究舊圖。他沒有因材料和自己工坊常用的不同，就嘲笑發明者。

Near the food stand, an assistant placed every used jar in a box for washing. She said the market's daily changes were often invisible to visitors who stayed only an hour. In the morning, delivery workers needed access; after dark, parents wanted clear space near the crossing. Nora added time labels beside the symbols on her map. Eli tested whether those labels would be readable in poor light. The group agreed to return after the market reopened, rather than claim that a temporary detour was permanent. Their report became more useful because they were willing to describe the conditions under which it might stop being true. He did not invent the original arrow, but he offered to explain why it had failed.

食物攤旁，助手把用過的玻璃罐放進箱子準備清洗。她說，市場每天的變動對只停留一小時的訪客常常看不見。清晨送貨工人需要通行，入夜後家長則希望路口有清楚的空間。知禾在地圖符號旁加上時段，莊岳測試昏暗時能否讀得清楚。大家同意等市場重新開放再回來，而不是把臨時繞道說成永久道路。正因願意說明何時不再適用，報告才更有價值。 他沒有發明原本的箭頭設計，卻願意解釋它為什麼失效。
