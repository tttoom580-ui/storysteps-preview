空桌上的地圖

The Map on the Empty Table

把夜路畫成星圖 · 第 1 / 25 章 · 2000–3000 · 英中對照

用互動版查詞、朗讀與收藏

Nora Lin arrived at the adult learning center with a folded map and a question she could not answer: why was the shortest route home also the one people feared after dark? Her mother had worked late shifts for twenty years. Last winter she had fallen beside a broken streetlamp, yet the city's public map still called that street safe. Nora, twenty-seven, led the Urban Sketch Club. She wanted to draw a map based on what people actually experienced, not merely on the bright lines printed by an office. Across the room stood Eli Chuang, thirty, head of the Repair Club. His proposal was a mobile bicycle repair station. The council offered one small grant, and each club believed its project deserved it.

二十七歲的林知禾帶著折好的地圖走進成人學習中心，心裡有個無法回答的問題：為什麼回家最短的路，也是人們天黑後最害怕的路？她的母親做了二十年夜班，去年冬天曾在壞掉的路燈旁跌倒；市府地圖卻仍把那條街標成安全路線。知禾是城市速寫社社長，想畫一張根據人們實際經驗製作的地圖，而不只是照抄公家單位印出的亮麗線條。房間另一邊站著三十歲的修理社社長莊岳。他想設置流動單車維修站。市議會只提供一筆小型補助，兩個社團都認為自己的計畫值得獲選。

Their first meeting took place beside a winter fair. A vendor sold berry pies near an old circus poster. Someone had painted a fairy on a wooden hut, and a magician invited children to guess which card hid a picture of the moon. A jazz trio played under a heater while a jet crossed the evening sky. The square looked fancy from far away. Up close, Nora noticed a loose screw beneath a sign and a ramp blocked by boxes. A helper brought a pint of water to a tired dancer. It was no miracle that the fair ran smoothly; many people were quietly fixing what visitors never noticed. Nora marked those repairs with little stars.

他們第一次碰面就在冬季市集旁。攤販在舊馬戲團海報附近賣莓果派，有人在木棚上畫了仙子，魔術師則邀請孩子猜哪張牌底下藏著月亮。爵士三重奏在暖爐下演奏，噴射機飛過傍晚天空。廣場遠看十分華麗，走近後，知禾卻看見指示牌底下鬆動的螺絲，還有被箱子堵住的坡道。志工替疲倦的舞者送來一品脫的水。市集順利進行並不是奇蹟，而是許多人默默修好遊客看不見的問題。知禾用小星星在筆記上標出那些修復工作。

The city clerk pointed to the deed for the old station building, which the council owned, and explained that the grant had to benefit various residents. A political argument had already begun online. Some people wanted better lights; others thought that bicycles would rob walkers of space. Eli told Nora he would not quit simply because her drawings were popular. Nora answered that a picture could signal danger more clearly than a long report. To test her point, she gave a signboard a gentle spin: its arrow pointed toward a locked gate. Eli helped her tame the wobbling board, then admitted that a map without repairs would solve only half the problem. The clerk proposed a six-week field test instead of an immediate winner.

市府承辦員指出老車站建物的所有權契據屬於市議會，並解釋補助必須讓各種居民受益。網路上已經吵起政治口水戰：有人想要更亮的燈，有人擔心單車會奪走行人的空間。莊岳表示，他不會因為知禾的畫受歡迎就退出。知禾則說，一張圖有時比長篇報告更能清楚發出危險訊號。她輕輕轉動一塊指示牌，箭頭竟指向上鎖的門。莊岳幫她穩住搖晃的牌子，也承認沒有修復工作的地圖只解決了一半問題。承辦員於是提議先進行六週實地測試，而不是立刻選出贏家。

Nora opened her notebook to the first clean page. She wrote three promises: ask before recording anyone, test a route before recommending it, and never erase an uncomfortable answer merely because it might cost a prize. Eli read them without smiling. Then he added a fourth in careful letters: name the person responsible for every repair. Nora thought the addition was practical, perhaps annoyingly practical. Still, the night was cold, and the old station was waiting. They agreed to start there at seven the following evening. Neither offered to surrender the grant. For the first time, however, each understood why the other cared.

知禾翻開筆記本的第一張空白頁，寫下三個承諾：記錄任何人之前先徵得同意、推薦路線之前親自測試、不能因為不舒服的答案可能害自己失去獎金就把它擦掉。莊岳讀完沒有笑，只用端正的字加上第四條：每一項修理都要寫明負責人。知禾覺得這一條很實際，甚至實際得有點煩人。但夜已深，老車站還在等他們。兩人約好隔晚七點從那裡開始，誰也沒有讓出補助的意思。然而，他們第一次明白彼此為何那樣在乎。

Before leaving the fair, Nora turned back to the locked gate. She asked the caretaker why the arrow had never been corrected. He explained that the sign was installed for a festival ten years ago and no one had been assigned to maintain it. Each office thought another office owned the work. Nora sketched the arrow and wrote the caretaker's exact explanation beneath it, with his permission. For the first time she saw how an innocent mistake could survive for years when nobody was clearly responsible. Eli watched her draw, then took a measurement of the gate. Neither could fix it that night, but they could make the question impossible to ignore.

離開市集前，知禾回頭看了上鎖的大門，問管理員為何那支箭頭從未被更正。管理員說，標誌是十年前某次節慶裝設的，之後沒有單位被指定維護。每個辦公室都以為工作屬於其他單位。知禾畫下箭頭，取得許可後在下方寫上管理員的原話。她第一次看見，一個無心的錯誤若責任不清，竟能存在多年。莊岳看著她作畫，接著測量大門。他們當晚修不了，但能讓這個問題不再被忽視。

At the center's exit, the clerk gave each club a separate copy of the rules. The competition was still real: after six weeks the council might select only one proposal. Nora folded her copy carefully because she wanted to remember that agreement did not erase her own aim. Eli carried the measuring tools, and they walked toward different bus stops. Just before turning away, he asked how her mother was recovering from the winter fall. Nora was startled that he had remembered. She answered that her mother was well enough to work, but still avoided the unlit street. He did not offer an impossible promise to repair it tomorrow. He offered to look at the records with her.

在中心出口，承辦員分別給兩社一份規則。競爭仍然真實存在：六週後市議會可能只選一案。知禾小心折好自己的那份，提醒自己合作不會抹去原有目標。莊岳帶著測量工具，兩人走向不同的公車站。分開前，他問起知禾母親去年冬天跌倒後恢復得如何。知禾很意外他記得，回答母親已能工作，卻仍不走那條沒燈的街。他沒有做出明天就修好的不可能承諾，只提議一起查相關紀錄。
