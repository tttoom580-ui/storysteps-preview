鬆動馬達的聲音

The Sound of a Loose Motor

把夜路畫成星圖 · 第 5 / 25 章 · 2000–3000 · 英中對照

用互動版查詞、朗讀與收藏

The Repair Club opened its shared garage on Sunday morning. Nora expected piles of broken bicycles, but the place had a clear floor plan, labeled shelves, and a small recording corner. Eli wanted to teach visitors how to notice a problem before an accident happened. Nora brought a drawing of the alley entrance. He asked whether a simple sound cue could help people identify the correct turn without looking down at a phone. She thought the idea was promising, but reminded him that a loud signal might disturb residents who were trying to sleep. They agreed to invite night workers to test any proposed sound before treating it as useful.

星期天早上，修理社開放共用車庫。知禾原以為裡面堆滿壞掉的腳踏車，卻看見清楚的地面配置、標示整齊的架子，以及小小的錄音角落。莊岳想教訪客在事故發生前注意異常。知禾帶來巷口速寫，他問能不能用簡單聲響，讓行人不必低頭看手機就能認出轉彎位置。她覺得這想法有希望，但也提醒大聲提示可能吵到需要睡覺的住戶。兩人決定請夜班工作者先試用任何候選音效，再判斷是否真的有用。

A neighbor brought bacon rolls and ripe fruit for breakfast. A worker complained about the cold carpet in the hall and gave a considerable list of practical suggestions. Someone began to cough when dust rose from a sack of old parts. Eli handed out masks and opened the garage door. A flashlight revealed a fake safety sticker on a battery box. Nora felt her jaw tighten: the sticker looked official enough to mislead a beginner. With a microphone, Eli recorded the motor before and after tightening its screws. He asked each visitor to pronounce the warning aloud, so its meaning could be checked, not merely admired.

鄰居帶來培根麵包與熟水果當早餐。一名工人抱怨會館的地毯太冷，又提出相當長的一串實用建議。舊零件袋揚起灰塵，有人開始咳嗽；莊岳發給大家口罩，並打開車庫門。手電筒照出電池盒上一張假的安全貼紙。知禾咬緊下顎：那貼紙看起來太像官方標示，足以誤導新手。莊岳用麥克風錄下鎖緊螺絲前後的馬達聲，又請每位訪客把警語唸出來，好確認意思是否清楚，而不只是欣賞字體。

When the group discussed engagement with residents, Nora insisted that participation must be voluntary. She would not turn every person into content for her map. Eli said their responsibility also included children who could not read small notices. A peanut seller suggested a raised symbol on a post. An older cyclist told them that his sorrow came from a crash no one had believed; he disliked public attention but wanted the place repaired. Warm steam rose from the tea kettle as he spoke. The violent noise of a passing truck briefly covered his words, so Nora stopped writing and asked him to repeat only what he wanted recorded.

談到如何與居民互動時，知禾堅持參與必須出於自願。她不會把每個人都變成地圖內容。莊岳說，他們的責任也包括看不懂小字公告的孩子。賣花生的小販建議在柱子上加凸起符號。一位年長騎士提起不被相信的事故，語氣滿是悲傷；他不喜歡公開受訪，只希望那個地方有人修好。熱茶壺升起蒸氣，他正說話時，一輛卡車發出猛烈噪音，蓋過他的聲音。知禾停筆，只請他重述願意被記下的部分。

Eli returned to the workbench after the visitors left. His hands moved with calm confidence, yet he admitted that he had once made a dangerous repair by guessing instead of checking a manual. Nora was surprised to hear him speak so openly about failure. She told him that her first published drawing had erased the steps that made a path impossible for her mother. They sat beside the silent motor and compared the two mistakes. Neither mistake was proof that they should stop working. Both were reasons to let people challenge them. Nora drew a raised symbol on the trial map, leaving its final shape for residents to decide.

訪客離開後，莊岳回到工作桌前。他的雙手沉穩熟練，卻坦言自己曾經不查手冊、只靠猜測做出危險的修理。知禾意外他能如此坦白說失敗，於是也告訴他：自己最早公開的一張畫，把母親無法跨越的階梯整段省略了。兩人坐在安靜的馬達旁比較錯誤。犯錯不代表必須停止工作，反而提醒他們應允許別人質疑。知禾在試驗地圖畫上凸起符號，把最後形狀留給居民決定。

During cleanup, one visitor began to complain about how slowly the demonstration had moved. Nora invited her to explain what she had hoped to learn. The visitor answered that she was a new rider and wanted to know how to check a bicycle before work. Eli changed the next lesson plan to begin with five simple questions. He refused to shorten the safety check itself, but he made the order clearer. Nora drew a small sequence of pictures to accompany the lesson. Watching new riders use the drawings gave her a reason to keep drawing for people rather than for judges.

清理場地時，一位訪客開始抱怨示範進行得太慢。知禾請她說明原本希望學到什麼。對方回答，自己是新手騎士，想知道上班前怎麼檢查腳踏車。莊岳調整下次課程，改從五個簡單問題開始。他沒有縮短安全檢查本身，而是讓順序更清楚。知禾畫了一小組連續圖陪襯課程。看見新手真的用圖理解，她更有理由繼續為使用者畫圖，而不是為評審畫圖。

That evening Eli gave Nora a short tour of the garage's storage room. Each shelf had a record showing when its tools were last checked. He said the system was boring but had prevented many accidents. Nora compared it with her sketch archive and realized that she had never kept versions clearly separated. They agreed to teach one another: he would show her how to make a simple change record, and she would help him illustrate the instructions for first-time visitors. No one would own the other's project. They would exchange skills and keep separate responsibility for what each club published.

當晚，莊岳帶知禾看車庫的儲藏室。每層架子都有工具上次檢查日期紀錄。他說制度無聊，卻已預防很多意外。知禾想起自己的速寫檔案，發現自己從未清楚區分不同版本。兩人約定互相教學：他教她建立簡單的修改紀錄，她幫他畫初學者看得懂的使用說明。誰也不擁有對方的計畫。兩人只交換技能，各社仍對自己出版的內容負責。
