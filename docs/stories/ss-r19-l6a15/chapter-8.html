A messenger arrived before the bells had finished ringing. The news sounded impossible only because everyone had been trained not to question the institution that delivered it. Sera publicly predicts a market fire, and stall owners move their oil before the spark appears. Sera asked the messenger to repeat the part that had been omitted from the written notice. It was there, in the omission, that the day's real story began.

鐘聲尚未結束，信使已經抵達。消息之所以聽來不可能，只因每個人都習慣不質疑傳遞消息的制度。瑟拉公開預告市集失火，攤商在火花出現前把油料移開。瑟拉請信使重述書面公告遺漏的部分。這一天真正的故事，就從那處缺漏開始。

The prophecy lens showed Sera a collapse in a fraction of a second. She wrote down the conditions before the image faded, because a disaster without its causes could be used to justify almost anything. Tarin made her add the words this may be prevented beneath every prediction.

預言透鏡在剎那間讓瑟拉看見崩塌。她趁畫面消失前寫下條件，因為沒有原因的災難影像幾乎可以拿來合理化任何事。塔林要求她在每份預言下加上「此事可能被避免」。

Beyond the meeting room, people were already finding ways to survive. They traded tools, shared the location of safe passages, and argued over fair turns without waiting for permission. Sera listened longer than the schedule allowed. Tarin reminded the officials that no policy was complete until it made sense to the people expected to live with it. Their notes began with those ordinary acts of cooperation.

會議室外，人們早已開始尋求生存方法：交換工具、分享安全路線的位置，無須等候許可便爭論公平輪班。瑟拉聽得比議程允許的更久。塔林提醒官員，政策若不能讓實際生活其中的人理解，就不算完整。他們從這些平凡合作開始記錄。

The overlooked details in the witness file made the question harder, not easier: The storage log showed an accumulation of sealed bundles beneath the classroom floor over several years. A blond clerk named Ruen carried a beverage to a retired teacher who would testify about missing children. A blond teacher carefully unfolded the school copy without touching its inked surface. Two appointed experts had to certify the age of the paper before the court could rely on it. Nessa requested written clearance to photograph the school copy and leave the original in place. Taken alone, no detail settled the case; together they showed why a public answer mattered.

證人檔案裡被忽視的細節使問題更加複雜，而不是更簡單： 儲藏簿顯示，數年間教室地板下累積了多捆封存文件。 一名金髮書記魯恩端飲料給退休教師，她將為失蹤的孩子作證。 一名金髮老師小心展開學校副本，沒有碰觸墨跡表面。 法院採信前，必須有兩名指定專家證明紙張年代。 妮莎申請書面許可拍攝學校副本，原件則留在原處。 單獨看來沒有一項細節能結案，合在一起卻說明了公開答覆為何重要。

Then the next piece of evidence changed the shape of the case. The resulting non-event earns no official praise, exposing how the court rewards disasters more than prevention. Sera read the statement twice, searching for the sentence that would make the contradiction disappear. There was none. Tarin did not reach for a dramatic accusation; instead, they listed three people who could verify the finding separately. The delay would be unpopular, but a dramatic discovery deserved more care, not less.

接著出現的新證據改變了整起案件的輪廓。火災沒有發生，宮廷卻不予讚賞，暴露朝廷獎勵災難勝過預防。瑟拉把陳述讀了兩遍，試圖找出能消除矛盾的句子，卻沒有找到。塔林沒有立刻提出轟動指控，而是列出三位可分別驗證發現的人。延遲會不受歡迎，但重大發現更需要謹慎。

A second bundle of observations brought the consequences closer to ordinary life: The clerk could deduct transport expenses from the budget only with receipts signed by both custodians. Maelin realized the copied warning was destined to matter more than the elegant ceremonial title. The survey team had to exert care when lifting the old storage chest from damp floorboards. The villagers called the teacher's quiet refusal to surrender the original heroic, though she asked for no praise. A retired lieutenant helped protect the school without interfering with the investigators' authority. Sera asked the clerk to preserve the original wording rather than improve the story.

第二疊觀察紀錄使後果貼近普通人的生活： 書記只有拿到雙方保管者簽署的收據，才能從預算扣除運費。 瑟拉發現副本裡的警告注定比華麗的儀式標題更重要。 測量團隊從潮濕地板抬起舊儲藏箱時必須格外用力且小心。 村民稱老師拒絕交出原件的沉默堅持很英勇，她卻不求讚美。 一名退役中尉協助保護學校，沒有干涉調查權限。 瑟拉要求書記保存原本措辭，而不是把故事修飾得更好聽。

Sera had expected the hardest part to be exposing the secret. Instead, it was choosing what to do after a partial truth became public. The people who had been ignored did not all want the same remedy. Tarin proposed that each affected group could submit a written objection and receive a specific answer. The method would take time, but it recognized them as participants rather than scenery.

瑟拉原以為最難的是揭露祕密，後來才發現，部分真相公開後如何行動才是難題。曾被忽略的人並不全想要相同補救。塔林提議每個受影響群體都能提出書面異議並取得具體答覆。方法需要時間，但終於把他們當成參與者，而不是背景。

The team kept the concrete observations alongside the disputed theory: Every literate child in the school could read the disputed river sentence more accurately than the official pamphlet. A lotus flower had been pressed between the pages as a marker for the river passage. The marginal note explained why the river verb described custody rather than ownership. The seals showed an overlap of ink from two different periods, prompting further laboratory tests. A provincial inspector confirmed that the classroom register had not left the district for ten years. Tarin marked what could be verified and left the rest open to challenge.

團隊把具體觀察與有爭議的理論並列保存： 學校裡每個識字的孩子都能比官方小冊子更準確讀懂爭議河語句。 一朵蓮花被壓在書頁間，作為河流條款的書籤。 頁邊註記解釋為何河語動詞指保管而非所有權。 印章顯示兩個年代的墨跡重疊，因此需要進一步化驗。 一名地方督察確認，教室登記冊十年間未離開該區。 塔林標記能查證的部分，其餘保留給人質疑。

The prophecy lens showed Sera a collapse in a fraction of a second. She wrote down the conditions before the image faded, because a disaster without its causes could be used to justify almost anything. Tarin made her add the words this may be prevented beneath every prediction.

預言透鏡在剎那間讓瑟拉看見崩塌。她趁畫面消失前寫下條件，因為沒有原因的災難影像幾乎可以拿來合理化任何事。塔林要求她在每份預言下加上「此事可能被避免」。

The record continued with small facts that would be lost in a confident speech: The interpreter's professional qualification did not excuse her from explaining an omitted warning. Parents offered refreshments after the experts completed their examination of the preserved copy. Nessa promised to retrieve a second copy only after receiving permission from the Hill Council. The provincial court stood beside a swamp where lotus flowers opened at dawn. The children learned that a translator's vocation involved responsibility to readers, not blind loyalty to rulers. Nothing in these records excused the people who had made the dangerous decision.

紀錄接著列出那些在自信演說中容易消失的小事實： 翻譯官的專業資格不能免除她解釋警語為何被省略的責任。 專家完成保存副本的檢查後，家長提供茶點。 妮莎承諾取得山地議會許可後才取回第二份副本。 省級法庭位於沼澤旁，黎明時蓮花盛開。 孩子們學到翻譯家的職志是對讀者負責，而非盲目效忠統治者。 紀錄中沒有任何一項能替做出危險決定的人開脫。

When the lamps were lowered, Sera returned to a cracked prophecy lens. It was neither proof of innocence nor a shortcut to justice. It was a reminder that the next decision would be witnessed by people who no longer trusted easy promises. Tarin remained at the door while the final record was sealed. Outside, the imperial observatory waited for news that had to be more precise than a victory speech.

燈光調暗時，瑟拉再次看向一枚裂開的預言透鏡。它既不是清白證明，也不是通往正義的捷徑，而是在提醒眾人：下一個決定會由不再相信輕易承諾的人見證。塔林守在門邊，最後一份紀錄被封存。外頭的帝國觀星臺等待的消息，必須比勝利演說更精確。
