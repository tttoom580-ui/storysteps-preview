Nobody had called it a crisis at first. A clerk had used a harmless word, a guard had followed an ordinary instruction, and by evening somebody else was carrying the cost. Sera refused to begin with the official conclusion. Tarin asks Sera to describe what she did not see, and they identify a dozen peaceful possibilities excluded from the archive. In the imperial observatory, even an accurate number could become a lie when it left out the person whose life it counted.

起初沒有人稱這是危機。書記使用看似無害的詞，守衛照普通命令行事，到了晚上代價卻落在別人身上。瑟拉拒絕從官方結論開始。塔林請瑟拉說明自己沒看見什麼，兩人找出檔案刻意排除的十幾種和平可能。在帝國觀星臺，精確的數字若遺漏真正承受後果的人，也可能成為謊言。

The prophecy lens showed Sera a collapse in a fraction of a second. She wrote down the conditions before the image faded, because a disaster without its causes could be used to justify almost anything. Tarin made her add the words this may be prevented beneath every prediction.

預言透鏡在剎那間讓瑟拉看見崩塌。她趁畫面消失前寫下條件，因為沒有原因的災難影像幾乎可以拿來合理化任何事。塔林要求她在每份預言下加上「此事可能被避免」。

The evidence did not arrive as a confession. It came through a date that did not fit, a measurement that had been repeated, and a witness who knew exactly what the official account had left out. Sera asked Tarin not to describe suspicion as proof. If the accusation was wrong, an innocent person would suffer. If it was right, careless language could still give the guilty a way to escape.

證據不是以自白形式出現，而是一個對不上的日期、一項重複測量的結果，以及一位知道官方說法遺漏何事的證人。瑟拉請塔林不要把懷疑說成證明。若指控錯了，無辜者會受害；若指控正確，草率措辭仍可能讓有罪者脫身。

The overlooked details in the witness file made the question harder, not easier: The survey covered an acre of wet meadow that the palace pamphlet called newly acquired crown land. A bachelor who had lived beside the meadow all his life showed Keir the oldest boundary stone. The village boxing club had stored its equipment in a barn now claimed by royal collectors. The council announced a disciplinary review for the officer who had ignored the suspension order. An emigrant returning home discovered that the revised map no longer showed her family's grazing route. Nothing in these records excused the people who had made the dangerous decision.

證人檔案裡被忽視的細節使問題更加複雜，而不是更簡單： 測量涵蓋一英畝潮濕草地，王宮小冊子稱之為新取得的王室土地。 一名終身住在草地旁的單身漢向凱爾指出最古老的界石。 村裡的拳擊社曾把器材存放在一間如今遭王室徵收員主張所有權的穀倉。 議會宣布對無視暫停令的官員進行紀律審查。 一名返鄉移民發現新版地圖不再顯示家族放牧路線。 紀錄中沒有任何一項能替做出危險決定的人開脫。

A small act of honesty unsettled the room more than a shouted threat could have done. The archivist refuses to enter the omissions, fearing they will be treated as treason. Sera watched a witness decide not to change a difficult detail merely because it complicated the preferred story. Tarin recorded the correction openly. Their inquiry would be slower now, but its shape belonged less to the powerful and more to the people who had lived through the event.

一個小小的誠實舉動，帶來比喊叫威脅更強的震動。檔案官拒絕登錄這些缺漏，擔心被當成叛國。瑟拉看見證人沒有因為細節讓主流說法變複雜就改口。塔林公開記錄修正。調查會因此變慢，卻能較少受權貴支配，也更貼近親歷者。

A second bundle of observations brought the consequences closer to ordinary life: Maelin refused to equate a temporary lease with permanent ownership simply because one English verb sounded convenient. The young witness began to falter when Vask's lawyer asked three questions at once. Farmers argued that the fertility of the floodplain depended on seasonal access to both banks. Iva would not forsake her community's claim merely to secure a private appointment with the governor. A gay teacher asked the committee to use the names families chose, not those assigned in outdated registers. Taken alone, no detail settled the case; together they showed why a public answer mattered.

第二疊觀察紀錄使後果貼近普通人的生活： 瑟拉拒絕只因一個英語動詞方便，就把暫時租賃等同永久所有權。 瓦斯克律師一次提出三個問題時，年輕證人開始結巴猶豫。 農民指出，沖積平原的土壤肥力仰賴兩岸季節性通行。 伊娃不願只為得到總督私下接見就放棄社區權利主張。 一位男同志教師要求委員會使用家庭自己選的名字，而非過時登記所指定的名字。 單獨看來沒有一項細節能結案，合在一起卻說明了公開答覆為何重要。

The debate sharpened when Marshal Dorian demanded a decision before the evidence could be copied. Sera replied that urgency and secrecy were not the same thing. They established a temporary limit, published the reason for it, and scheduled an independent review. The result satisfied nobody completely. That was acceptable; the purpose of the process was to make abuse harder, not to produce applause.

當多里安元帥要求在證據複製前就做決定，辯論變得尖銳。瑟拉回應說緊急和保密不是同一回事。團隊訂立暫時限制、公開理由，並排定獨立審查。結果沒有讓任何人完全滿意，但程序的目的本來就不是得到掌聲，而是使濫權更困難。

The team kept the concrete observations alongside the disputed theory: Gloom spread through the room when the court announced that compensation would require another hearing. The old intersection of farm roads appeared on all three authenticated survey maps. A landslide delayed the inspection, so Keir refused to send inexperienced workers uphill. The local school proposed a food program to nourish children while grain ownership remained unresolved. The orthodox court scholars disliked the river dialect but admitted its grammar was legally relevant. Sera asked the clerk to preserve the original wording rather than improve the story.

團隊把具體觀察與有爭議的理論並列保存： 法庭宣布補償還需另開聽證時，陰鬱氣氛籠罩全場。 那處古老農路交叉口在三份經認證的測量圖上都存在。 山崩延誤檢查，凱爾因此拒絕派缺乏經驗的工人上山。 當糧食所有權仍未解決時，地方學校提出供餐計畫滋養孩童。 正統宮廷學者不喜歡河語方言，卻承認其文法具有法律相關性。 瑟拉要求書記保存原本措辭，而不是把故事修飾得更好聽。

The prophecy lens showed Sera a collapse in a fraction of a second. She wrote down the conditions before the image faded, because a disaster without its causes could be used to justify almost anything. Tarin made her add the words this may be prevented beneath every prediction.

預言透鏡在剎那間讓瑟拉看見崩塌。她趁畫面消失前寫下條件，因為沒有原因的災難影像幾乎可以拿來合理化任何事。塔林要求她在每份預言下加上「此事可能被避免」。

The record continued with small facts that would be lost in a confident speech: A prohibition against night crossings remained in effect only where bridge damage had been independently confirmed. The provisional route notice would expire after seven days unless a new safety review justified renewal. The vote was unanimous, yet the council could not erase an independent court order. The governor's vanity had led him to display a larger map than anyone could read closely. A woodpecker struck a hollow tree beside the stone that marked the meadow's western edge. Tarin marked what could be verified and left the rest open to challenge.

紀錄接著列出那些在自信演說中容易消失的小事實： 夜間禁渡只在橋梁損傷經獨立確認的地方繼續生效。 暫行路線公告七天後失效，除非新的安全審查證明需要延長。 表決結果一致，議會卻無法抹除獨立法庭的命令。 總督的虛榮使他展示了一張大得無法近看細讀的地圖。 一隻啄木鳥敲擊草地西緣界石旁的空心樹。 塔林標記能查證的部分，其餘保留給人質疑。

A message arrived after the public doors had closed. It carried no grand declaration, just a name and a time that did not match the official schedule. Sera resisted the urge to call it proof. Tarin opened a fresh page in the journal and recorded the discrepancy exactly. They had won time, not certainty, and there were still people who might lose everything if they hurried.

公共大門關閉後，一則訊息送到。它沒有豪言壯語，只有一個名字和一個對不上官方時程的時間。瑟拉克制住把它當成證明的衝動。塔林翻開日誌新的一頁，逐字記錄差異。他們贏得的是時間，不是確定性；若急於求成，仍有人可能失去一切。
