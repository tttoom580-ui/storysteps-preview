The Price of Looking

帝國預言官只能看見失敗的未來 · 第 4 / 25 章 · 5000–6000 · 英中對照 · 校訂中

Morning brought a decision that had already been made for everyone. There was a signature at the bottom of the order, but no space for those most affected to answer. Sera reveals that every vision removes one ordinary memory of her own, leaving her unsure which childhood stories are real. Sera knew that refusing a bad choice was not the same as knowing the correct one. Before making a promise, the team needed something they could test.

清晨帶來一項替所有人做好的決定。命令底下有簽名，卻沒有讓受影響最深者回答的地方。瑟拉坦白每次預言都會奪走一段平凡記憶，使她逐漸分不清童年的真偽。瑟拉知道拒絕壞選項不等於已經知道正確答案。作出承諾前，團隊需要能夠驗證的事物。

The prophecy lens showed Sera a collapse in a fraction of a second. She wrote down the conditions before the image faded, because a disaster without its causes could be used to justify almost anything. Tarin made her add the words this may be prevented beneath every prediction.

預言透鏡在剎那間讓瑟拉看見崩塌。她趁畫面消失前寫下條件，因為沒有原因的災難影像幾乎可以拿來合理化任何事。塔林要求她在每份預言下加上「此事可能被避免」。

Tarin arrived with a second account that neither confirmed nor dismissed the first. They compared the routes by which each record had reached the room, then placed the uncertainties beside the facts. Sera could feel the pressure to announce a solution before anyone became frightened. That pressure was precisely why a second witness mattered: a conclusion that could not survive an independent question would be dangerous, even if it happened to be right.

塔林帶來第二份說法，既未證實也未推翻第一份。兩人比對紀錄抵達房間的途徑，再把不確定之處和事實並列。瑟拉感受到眾人希望立即宣布解方，以免恐懼擴大的壓力。但也正因這種壓力，第二位證人才重要：無法通過獨立質問的結論，即使碰巧正確，也可能很危險。

The overlooked details in the witness file made the question harder, not easier: Workers used bleach to clean the boardinghouse corridor, but Nessa forbade cleaning the evidence cabinet. The judges demanded a comprehensive visitor list rather than a collection of rumors about the witnesses. The landlady appeared credible because her account matched the dated kitchen purchase book. Iva thanked the guards for their dedication to keeping witnesses safe without controlling what they said. A torn ribbon lay inside the dresser beside the room where the visiting scribe had slept. Taken alone, no detail settled the case; together they showed why a public answer mattered.

證人檔案裡被忽視的細節使問題更加複雜，而不是更簡單： 工人用漂白劑清潔寄宿屋走廊，妮莎卻禁止清洗證物櫃。 法官要求完整訪客名單，而非收集關於證人的流言。 房東太太的說法與附日期的廚房採購簿相符，因此顯得可信。 伊娃感謝衛兵盡心保護證人安全，而不干涉證言內容。 那名訪客書記睡過的房間旁，梳妝檯內放著一段破緞帶。 單獨看來沒有一項細節能結案，合在一起卻說明了公開答覆為何重要。

The contradiction reached the table just as the meeting was about to end. Tarin promises to record what she chooses to remember, not to force her to keep seeing disasters. For a long moment no one spoke. Sera understood why silence felt comfortable; each answer threatened someone's settled account of how the world worked. Tarin broke the stillness by asking who would be harmed if they simply filed the discovery away. No one could answer without naming a real person.

會議即將結束時，矛盾證據送上桌面。塔林答應記下她自願保留的記憶，不會逼她持續看見災難。一時之間無人開口。瑟拉明白沉默為何讓人舒服：任何答案都可能動搖某人對世界的既定理解。塔林打破沉默，問若把發現歸檔，究竟誰會受害。沒人能在不指名真實人物的情況下回答。

A second bundle of observations brought the consequences closer to ordinary life: Maelin's familiarity with the novel made her suspicious, but she refused to present it as proof. The palace hierarchy placed interpreters below ministers, although their errors could affect entire villages. The landlady recorded the visitor's time of departure without inventing a reason for the late hour. A lush garden behind the house gave frightened children somewhere quiet to sit. The villagers still mourn the ferryman lost during last year's unsafe winter crossing. Sera asked the clerk to preserve the original wording rather than improve the story.

第二疊觀察紀錄使後果貼近普通人的生活： 瑟拉熟悉原著而起疑，卻拒絕把這種熟悉當作證據。 王宮階層把譯員置於大臣之下，他們的錯誤卻可能影響整個村莊。 房東太太只記下訪客離開的時間，沒有猜造他深夜離去的理由。 屋後繁茂的花園讓受驚的孩子有安靜的地方可坐。 村民仍哀悼去年冬季危險渡口事故中失去的渡船夫。 瑟拉要求書記保存原本措辭，而不是把故事修飾得更好聽。

Sera had expected the hardest part to be exposing the secret. Instead, it was choosing what to do after a partial truth became public. The people who had been ignored did not all want the same remedy. Tarin proposed that each affected group could submit a written objection and receive a specific answer. The method would take time, but it recognized them as participants rather than scenery.

瑟拉原以為最難的是揭露祕密，後來才發現，部分真相公開後如何行動才是難題。曾被忽略的人並不全想要相同補救。塔林提議每個受影響群體都能提出書面異議並取得具體答覆。方法需要時間，但終於把他們當成參與者，而不是背景。

The team kept the concrete observations alongside the disputed theory: The teacher hoped a fair treaty would nurture trust among children speaking different languages. After heavy rain, water began to overflow the stone basin outside the witness house. Maelin happened to overhear a clerk argue about an order to remove margin notes. An engineer's measurements could refute the claim that the wall had never leaked. A seagull circled above the harbor where the archive chest had been unloaded. Tarin marked what could be verified and left the rest open to challenge.

團隊把具體觀察與有爭議的理論並列保存： 老師希望公平條約能培養使用不同語言的孩子間的信任。 大雨後，水開始漫出證人屋外的石盆。 瑟拉偶然聽見一名書記爭論刪除頁邊註記的命令。 工程師的測量可以反駁牆壁從未漏水的說法。 一隻海鷗在卸下檔案箱的港口上方盤旋。 塔林標記能查證的部分，其餘保留給人質疑。

The prophecy lens showed Sera a collapse in a fraction of a second. She wrote down the conditions before the image faded, because a disaster without its causes could be used to justify almost anything. Tarin made her add the words this may be prevented beneath every prediction.

預言透鏡在剎那間讓瑟拉看見崩塌。她趁畫面消失前寫下條件，因為沒有原因的災難影像幾乎可以拿來合理化任何事。塔林要求她在每份預言下加上「此事可能被避免」。

The record continued with small facts that would be lost in a confident speech: The school superintendent offered a neutral classroom for the next comparison meeting. A distant symphony drifted from the town hall while the witnesses checked their statements. The infirmary nurse placed a thermometer beside an ill courier and offered him rest before questioning. Public turmoil increased when officials announced that the border had already been decided. A wholesome supper helped the children settle before the adults returned to their difficult discussions. Nothing in these records excused the people who had made the dangerous decision.

紀錄接著列出那些在自信演說中容易消失的小事實： 學校督學提供一間中立教室供下次比對會議使用。 證人核對證詞時，遠處市政廳傳來交響樂聲。 醫務室護士把溫度計放在生病信差旁，讓他先休息再接受詢問。 官員宣稱邊界已經決定後，公共動盪加劇。 健康而營養的晚餐讓孩子安定下來，大人才繼續艱難討論。 紀錄中沒有任何一項能替做出危險決定的人開脫。

When the lamps were lowered, Sera returned to a cracked prophecy lens. It was neither proof of innocence nor a shortcut to justice. It was a reminder that the next decision would be witnessed by people who no longer trusted easy promises. Tarin remained at the door while the final record was sealed. Outside, the imperial observatory waited for news that had to be more precise than a victory speech.

燈光調暗時，瑟拉再次看向一枚裂開的預言透鏡。它既不是清白證明，也不是通往正義的捷徑，而是在提醒眾人：下一個決定會由不再相信輕易承諾的人見證。塔林守在門邊，最後一份紀錄被封存。外頭的帝國觀星臺等待的消息，必須比勝利演說更精確。
