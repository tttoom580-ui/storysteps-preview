The Historian Who Asked Why

帝國預言官只能看見失敗的未來 · 第 3 / 25 章 · 5000–6000 · 英中對照 · 校訂中

The first sign of trouble was not an explosion or a scream. It was a familiar procedure producing a result nobody could defend. Court historian Tarin compares three of Sera’s old visions with what actually occurred in the affected villages. Sera checked the time, the witnesses, and the rule that authorized the action. The three did not agree. Outside, ordinary life continued, which made the error more frightening rather than less.

麻煩的第一個徵兆不是爆炸或尖叫，而是熟悉程序得出了誰也無法辯護的結果。宮廷史官塔林比較瑟拉過去三次預言與受影響村莊的實況。瑟拉核對時間、證人與授權規則，三者卻互相矛盾。外頭的平凡生活照常進行，反而讓這個錯誤更加可怕。

Sera could witness the worst plausible branch only under the conditions shown to her. A vision could change when those conditions changed, and every look ahead took one ordinary memory she could never recover by magic.

瑟拉只能看見特定條件下最糟但可能發生的分支。條件改變，預言也會改變，而每次窺視未來都會奪走一段無法靠魔法取回的普通記憶。

The prophecy lens showed Sera a collapse in a fraction of a second. She wrote down the conditions before the image faded, because a disaster without its causes could be used to justify almost anything. Tarin made her add the words this may be prevented beneath every prediction.

預言透鏡在剎那間讓瑟拉看見崩塌。她趁畫面消失前寫下條件，因為沒有原因的災難影像幾乎可以拿來合理化任何事。塔林要求她在每份預言下加上「此事可能被避免」。

Beyond the meeting room, people were already finding ways to survive. They traded tools, shared the location of safe passages, and argued over fair turns without waiting for permission. Sera listened longer than the schedule allowed. Tarin reminded the officials that no policy was complete until it made sense to the people expected to live with it. Their notes began with those ordinary acts of cooperation.

會議室外，人們早已開始尋求生存方法：交換工具、分享安全路線的位置，無須等候許可便爭論公平輪班。瑟拉聽得比議程允許的更久。塔林提醒官員，政策若不能讓實際生活其中的人理解，就不算完整。他們從這些平凡合作開始記錄。

The overlooked details in the witness file made the question harder, not easier: An advisory plaque still praised the Empire's conquest of the eastern frontier, but most houses were empty. The hill envoy rejected the word conquest because the agreement described cooperation rather than military victory. Vask called the missing binding a conspiracy invented by people who wanted to embarrass the crown. The innkeeper served cucumber salad while both delegations waited for the witness room to open. An elderly scribe looked feeble, yet he remembered exactly where the archive chest had stood. Nothing in these records excused the people who had made the dangerous decision.

證人檔案裡被忽視的細節使問題更加複雜，而不是更簡單： 告示牌仍讚揚帝國征服東部邊境，但大多數房屋已空無一人。 山地使者反對使用「征服」，因為協議寫的是合作而非軍事勝利。 瓦斯克把遺失裝訂線稱為企圖羞辱王室的人編造的陰謀。 兩方代表等待證人室開門時，旅店主人端上小黃瓜沙拉。 一名年老書記看似虛弱，卻清楚記得檔案箱原本的位置。 紀錄中沒有任何一項能替做出危險決定的人開脫。

Then the next piece of evidence changed the shape of the case. In each case, a simple repair prevented the catastrophe without a single military order. Sera read the statement twice, searching for the sentence that would make the contradiction disappear. There was none. Tarin did not reach for a dramatic accusation; instead, they listed three people who could verify the finding separately. The delay would be unpopular, but a dramatic discovery deserved more care, not less.

接著出現的新證據改變了整起案件的輪廓。每一次都是簡單修繕阻止災難，根本不需要軍令。瑟拉把陳述讀了兩遍，試圖找出能消除矛盾的句子，卻沒有找到。塔林沒有立刻提出轟動指控，而是列出三位可分別驗證發現的人。延遲會不受歡迎，但重大發現更需要謹慎。

A second bundle of observations brought the consequences closer to ordinary life: A firecracker exploded outside the gate, and the officers checked whether it was a signal or prank. The printer began to fume when investigators asked for the invoice he had already surrendered. Keir wiped grease from a cargo wheel to read the scratched route number beneath it. The preservation order could lessen the risk of destruction but could not determine who had altered the text. A river liner had carried the chest to the island station before the last treaty ceremony. Taken alone, no detail settled the case; together they showed why a public answer mattered.

第二疊觀察紀錄使後果貼近普通人的生活： 門外一枚爆竹炸響，官員檢查究竟是暗號還是惡作劇。 調查員再次索取已上交的發票時，印刷工開始氣憤。 凱爾擦去貨輪上的油脂，讀取下面刻出的路線編號。 保全令能降低文件遭毀的風險，卻不能判定誰改過文字。 一艘河上客輪在最後一次條約儀式前把箱子送往島站。 單獨看來沒有一項細節能結案，合在一起卻說明了公開答覆為何重要。

The debate sharpened when Marshal Dorian demanded a decision before the evidence could be copied. Sera replied that urgency and secrecy were not the same thing. They established a temporary limit, published the reason for it, and scheduled an independent review. The result satisfied nobody completely. That was acceptable; the purpose of the process was to make abuse harder, not to produce applause.

當多里安元帥要求在證據複製前就做決定，辯論變得尖銳。瑟拉回應說緊急和保密不是同一回事。團隊訂立暫時限制、公開理由，並排定獨立審查。結果沒有讓任何人完全滿意，但程序的目的本來就不是得到掌聲，而是使濫權更困難。

The team kept the concrete observations alongside the disputed theory: The orphanage kept a teaching copy of the river alphabet in its small reading room. An old plague notice proved that the village had used the crossing long before the disputed decree. The recreational sailing club had logged the unusual midnight delivery in its harbor notebook. An angry official called the river protesters savage, and Maelin demanded that the insult be removed from the record. Nessa found a small shrub growing beside the wall where the binding thread had been dropped. Sera asked the clerk to preserve the original wording rather than improve the story.

團隊把具體觀察與有爭議的理論並列保存： 孤兒院在小閱覽室保存了一份河語字母教學本。 一張舊瘟疫公告證明該村在爭議命令之前很久便使用渡口。 休閒帆船社在港口筆記本記下異常的午夜送貨。 一名憤怒官員稱河民抗議者野蠻，瑟拉要求從紀錄中刪除侮辱用語。 妮莎在裝訂線掉落的牆邊發現一叢小灌木。 瑟拉要求書記保存原本措辭，而不是把故事修飾得更好聽。

The prophecy lens showed Sera a collapse in a fraction of a second. She wrote down the conditions before the image faded, because a disaster without its causes could be used to justify almost anything. Tarin made her add the words this may be prevented beneath every prediction.

預言透鏡在剎那間讓瑟拉看見崩塌。她趁畫面消失前寫下條件，因為沒有原因的災難影像幾乎可以拿來合理化任何事。塔林要求她在每份預言下加上「此事可能被避免」。

The record continued with small facts that would be lost in a confident speech: The interpreter explained that local slang could not be substituted for a defined legal term. The hill witnesses hoped the evidence would surpass the palace's confident but unsupported account. Three carved stones symbolize the agreement to share the river rather than divide it by force. A textile merchant produced a dated receipt for cloth delivered to the treaty ceremony. A vaccine shipment had passed the same checkpoint, showing that the route served ordinary medical needs. Tarin marked what could be verified and left the rest open to challenge.

紀錄接著列出那些在自信演說中容易消失的小事實： 譯員解釋，地方俚語不能取代定義明確的法律用語。 山地證人希望證據能勝過王宮自信卻缺乏支持的說法。 三塊雕石象徵共同使用河流，而不是武力瓜分。 一名紡織商拿出條約儀式時交貨的布匹日期收據。 一批疫苗也曾通過同一檢查站，顯示路線服務一般醫療需求。 塔林標記能查證的部分，其餘保留給人質疑。

A message arrived after the public doors had closed. It carried no grand declaration, just a name and a time that did not match the official schedule. Sera resisted the urge to call it proof. Tarin opened a fresh page in the journal and recorded the discrepancy exactly. They had won time, not certainty, and there were still people who might lose everything if they hurried.

公共大門關閉後，一則訊息送到。它沒有豪言壯語，只有一個名字和一個對不上官方時程的時間。瑟拉克制住把它當成證明的衝動。塔林翻開日誌新的一頁，逐字記錄差異。他們贏得的是時間，不是確定性；若急於求成，仍有人可能失去一切。
