The General's Empty Witness

帝國預言官只能看見失敗的未來 · 第 9 / 25 章 · 5000–6000 · 英中對照 · 校訂中

The first sign of trouble was not an explosion or a scream. It was a familiar procedure producing a result nobody could defend. A witness to the border incident is listed as dead in two different years, yet his daughter says he is alive. Sera checked the time, the witnesses, and the rule that authorized the action. The three did not agree. Outside, ordinary life continued, which made the error more frightening rather than less.

麻煩的第一個徵兆不是爆炸或尖叫，而是熟悉程序得出了誰也無法辯護的結果。邊境事件證人被記載在兩個不同年份死亡，女兒卻表示他還活著。瑟拉核對時間、證人與授權規則，三者卻互相矛盾。外頭的平凡生活照常進行，反而讓這個錯誤更加可怕。

The prophecy lens showed Sera a collapse in a fraction of a second. She wrote down the conditions before the image faded, because a disaster without its causes could be used to justify almost anything. Tarin made her add the words this may be prevented beneath every prediction.

預言透鏡在剎那間讓瑟拉看見崩塌。她趁畫面消失前寫下條件，因為沒有原因的災難影像幾乎可以拿來合理化任何事。塔林要求她在每份預言下加上「此事可能被避免」。

Tarin arrived with a second account that neither confirmed nor dismissed the first. They compared the routes by which each record had reached the room, then placed the uncertainties beside the facts. Sera could feel the pressure to announce a solution before anyone became frightened. That pressure was precisely why a second witness mattered: a conclusion that could not survive an independent question would be dangerous, even if it happened to be right.

塔林帶來第二份說法，既未證實也未推翻第一份。兩人比對紀錄抵達房間的途徑，再把不確定之處和事實並列。瑟拉感受到眾人希望立即宣布解方，以免恐懼擴大的壓力。但也正因這種壓力，第二位證人才重要：無法通過獨立質問的結論，即使碰巧正確，也可能很危險。

The overlooked details in the witness file made the question harder, not easier: Maelin feared that careless accusations would alienate the Hill Council before the evidence could be examined. Nessa examined a blot of blue ink beside the disputed verb on the hill copy. The innkeeper added chili to the stew served after the long mountain journey. A witness list supplied continuity between the first treaty meeting and the later public reading. The cumulative weight of small dated records became harder for the palace to dismiss. Sera asked the clerk to preserve the original wording rather than improve the story.

證人檔案裡被忽視的細節使問題更加複雜，而不是更簡單： 瑟拉擔心草率指控會在查驗證據前疏遠山地議會。 妮莎檢查山地副本爭議動詞旁的一滴藍色墨漬。 旅店主人在長途山行後端上的燉菜裡加入辣椒。 證人名單使首次條約會議與後來公開宣讀之間的脈絡得以連續。 許多附日期的小紀錄累積起來的證據分量，使王宮更難忽視。 瑟拉要求書記保存原本措辭，而不是把故事修飾得更好聽。

The contradiction reached the table just as the meeting was about to end. Sera suspects that the court has been editing the events that trigger her visions. For a long moment no one spoke. Sera understood why silence felt comfortable; each answer threatened someone's settled account of how the world worked. Tarin broke the stillness by asking who would be harmed if they simply filed the discovery away. No one could answer without naming a real person.

會議即將結束時，矛盾證據送上桌面。瑟拉懷疑宮廷一直修改引發她預言的事件。一時之間無人開口。瑟拉明白沉默為何讓人舒服：任何答案都可能動搖某人對世界的既定理解。塔林打破沉默，問若把發現歸檔，究竟誰會受害。沒人能在不指名真實人物的情況下回答。

A second bundle of observations brought the consequences closer to ordinary life: Anika demanded a decisive answer about whether local interpreters had reviewed the printed summary. A witness's accent was not dispensable merely because the court clerk found it inconvenient. Jasmine grew near the inn window where the delegates reviewed their copied documents. The hill lodge offered both sides equal rooms and refused to charge the river guests more. A red maple leaf marked the treaty page the old translator had asked his daughter to remember. Tarin marked what could be verified and left the rest open to challenge.

第二疊觀察紀錄使後果貼近普通人的生活： 安妮卡要求明確回答：地方譯員是否審查過印製摘要。 證人的口音不能只因法庭書記覺得麻煩就被當作可有可無。 代表審閱抄件的旅店窗邊長著茉莉花。 山區旅舍提供雙方同樣房間，拒絕向河民多收費。 一片紅楓葉標記著老翻譯員請女兒記住的條約頁面。 塔林標記能查證的部分，其餘保留給人質疑。

There were two tempting shortcuts. One would protect the reputation of Marshal Dorian and leave the public guessing. The other would expose every private detail at once and call that transparency. Sera rejected both. The issue was whether fear of disaster justifies the empire’s wars, not whether the team could win an argument in a single afternoon. A smaller step, supported by independent observers, could protect people while the wider dispute remained open.

有兩種誘人的捷徑：一種保住多里安元帥的名聲，讓大眾自行猜測；另一種把所有私人細節一次揭露，並稱之為透明。瑟拉兩者都拒絕。核心是對災難的恐懼是否足以合理化帝國發動戰爭，不是團隊能否在一個下午贏得爭論。由獨立觀察者支持的一小步，即使爭議未結，也能先保護民眾。

The team kept the concrete observations alongside the disputed theory: The mistress of the house showed a dated guest book recording the translator's last visit. Local volunteers agreed to mobilize carts for a return journey before the mountain snow arrived. Maelin began to muse about how many borders had been changed by ordinary-looking words. Anika's persistence persuaded a reluctant clerk to release the original meeting attendance sheet. A socialist printer joined the public debate, arguing that agreements must be legible to working families. Nothing in these records excused the people who had made the dangerous decision.

團隊把具體觀察與有爭議的理論並列保存： 女主人出示附日期的訪客簿，記錄老譯員最後一次來訪。 當地志工同意在山雪來臨前動員馬車協助返程。 瑟拉開始沉思，究竟有多少邊界是被看似普通的字詞改變。 安妮卡的堅持讓猶豫的書記交出原始會議出席表。 一名社會主義印刷工加入公共辯論，認為協議必須讓勞動家庭讀得懂。 紀錄中沒有任何一項能替做出危險決定的人開脫。

The prophecy lens showed Sera a collapse in a fraction of a second. She wrote down the conditions before the image faded, because a disaster without its causes could be used to justify almost anything. Tarin made her add the words this may be prevented beneath every prediction.

預言透鏡在剎那間讓瑟拉看見崩塌。她趁畫面消失前寫下條件，因為沒有原因的災難影像幾乎可以拿來合理化任何事。塔林要求她在每份預言下加上「此事可能被避免」。

The record continued with small facts that would be lost in a confident speech: Delegates could socialize after the meeting, but they were forbidden to negotiate secret changes to the record. The access log covered a span of seven hours, with one unexplained gap near midnight. The final trek down the slope took longer because the team carried fragile copies in sealed boxes. A vigorous debate followed the discovery of the deceased translator's mark beside the clause. The waterproof wrapping had protected the hill copy through three seasons of heavy rain. Taken alone, no detail settled the case; together they showed why a public answer mattered.

紀錄接著列出那些在自信演說中容易消失的小事實： 代表可在會後交際，卻禁止私下協商更改紀錄。 門禁紀錄涵蓋七小時，其中午夜附近有一段無法解釋的空白。 下坡的最後一段跋涉較久，因為團隊以封箱搬運脆弱副本。 發現已故譯員在條款旁的記號後，現場展開激烈辯論。 防水包裝讓山地副本挺過三季豪雨。 單獨看來沒有一項細節能結案，合在一起卻說明了公開答覆為何重要。

The chapter ended without an easy reassurance. Sera had learned one thing that could be checked and another that still needed an answer. Tarin wrote both on the same page so that relief would not erase doubt. Their decision had moved the crisis forward, but it had also drawn the attention of Marshal Dorian. By morning, the next question would reach people far beyond this room.

這一天沒有簡單的安慰。瑟拉知道了一件能查證的事，也留下另一件尚待回答的事。塔林把兩者寫在同一頁，免得鬆一口氣就抹掉疑問。他們的決定使危機向前，也引起多里安元帥注意。明早下一個問題將傳到房間外更遠的地方。
