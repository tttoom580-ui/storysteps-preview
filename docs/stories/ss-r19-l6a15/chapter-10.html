The Worst Future Was Selected

帝國預言官只能看見失敗的未來 · 第 10 / 25 章 · 5000–6000 · 英中對照 · 校訂中

Morning brought a decision that had already been made for everyone. There was a signature at the bottom of the order, but no space for those most affected to answer. At the war council, Tarin proves the empire publishes only the most frightening of many possible visions. Sera knew that refusing a bad choice was not the same as knowing the correct one. Before making a promise, the team needed something they could test.

清晨帶來一項替所有人做好的決定。命令底下有簽名，卻沒有讓受影響最深者回答的地方。在軍事議會上，塔林證明帝國只公布眾多可能未來中最恐怖的一個。瑟拉知道拒絕壞選項不等於已經知道正確答案。作出承諾前，團隊需要能夠驗證的事物。

Tarin carried a locked box into the war council and set it before the emperor. Inside were twelve versions of the same forecast: in one a bridge collapsed, in another the river changed course, and in seven the war never began. Only the bloodiest vision had reached the public. Sera checked the ink, the dates, and the seal; every version was genuine. Marshal Dorian called the others irrelevant because they did not frighten anyone into action. Sera could suddenly see how her gift had been converted into a weapon. She had never claimed that failure was inevitable. The court had edited possibility into certainty, then ordered thousands to kill in its name. She stood and requested that the entire record be read aloud.

塔林帶著上鎖木盒走進軍事議會，放在皇帝面前。盒內有同一份預報的十二個版本：有的橋倒塌、有的河流改道，另有七個版本裡戰爭根本沒有開始。公開的卻只有最血腥的一種。瑟拉核對墨跡、日期與印璽，所有版本都是真的。多里安元帥稱其他版本毫無意義，因為它們不會嚇得人們採取行動。瑟拉突然明白，自己的天賦如何被變成武器。她從未說失敗不可避免。朝廷卻把可能性剪輯成確定性，再以它之名命令數千人殺戮。她站起身，要求宣讀全部紀錄。

The overlooked details in the witness file made the question harder, not easier: The chancellor offered a concession that would feed the villages only if they accepted his border claim. Maelin heard a cynical laugh when she asked why hungry families had been given such a choice. The treasury threatened to default on the bridge contract unless disputed grain fees were collected. Winter hunger could deplete the villagers' food stores long before the judges finished their arguments. The fragrance of warm bread drifted into the hearing room from the public kitchen outside. Tarin marked what could be verified and left the rest open to challenge.

證人檔案裡被忽視的細節使問題更加複雜，而不是更簡單： 宰相提出讓步：村民只有接受他的邊界主張，才能獲得糧食。 瑟拉詢問為何要逼挨餓家庭作這種選擇時，聽見一聲冷嘲。 國庫威脅，若不收取有爭議的糧食費，就會拖欠橋梁合約款項。 法官辯論結束前，冬季飢餓可能早已耗盡村民糧食儲備。 熱麵包的香氣從外面的公共廚房飄進聽證室。 塔林標記能查證的部分，其餘保留給人質疑。

What changed the discussion was a fact that should have been obvious from the start. Dorian reveals a locked vault of less disastrous alternatives that he has concealed for years. Sera felt the old temptation to turn that fact into an immediate victory. Instead, the team separated what the new evidence established from what it merely suggested. That distinction frustrated the loudest spectators, but it helped the witnesses remain willing to speak again.

改變討論的是一件本來就該看得見的事實。多里安承認自己多年來把災難較小的替代可能封存在密庫。瑟拉一瞬間也想把它當成立刻勝利的證明，團隊卻把新證據真正確立的部分與僅能推測的部分分開。這讓最喧鬧的旁觀者失望，卻使證人願意再次出面。

A second bundle of observations brought the consequences closer to ordinary life: A palace agent tried to intrude on the confidential meeting between Iva and her legal adviser. A young jockey delivered a sealed message after taking the fastest road beside the racetrack. Anika demanded a literal reading of the disputed sentence before the palace offered its interpretation. The temporary food order raised morale among ferry workers who had feared losing their winter wages. The tribunal could prosecute deliberate fraud only after establishing who had authorized the replacement. Nothing in these records excused the people who had made the dangerous decision.

第二疊觀察紀錄使後果貼近普通人的生活： 王宮代理人試圖闖入伊娃與法律顧問的保密會面。 一名年輕騎師沿賽馬場旁最快的路送來封口信件。 安妮卡要求先逐字朗讀爭議句，再聽王宮的解釋。 臨時配糧令提高了渡口工人的士氣，他們原本擔心失去冬季工資。 法庭必須確定誰核准替換文件，才能起訴故意詐欺。 紀錄中沒有任何一項能替做出危險決定的人開脫。

No one in the room was permitted to declare that a good intention canceled a harmful action. Sera insisted on recording the error, identifying who had authorized it, and protecting those affected before assigning wider blame. Tarin drew a simple boundary around what they could do tonight. The line looked disappointingly modest, yet it was the first promise that did not require another person to surrender a right.

室內沒有人能宣稱善意可以抵銷傷害。瑟拉堅持記錄錯誤、確認授權者，並在追究更大責任前保護受害者。塔林畫出今晚能做到的清楚界線。這條線看似微不足道，卻是第一個不要求別人放棄權利的承諾。

The team kept the concrete observations alongside the disputed theory: An old mining report mentioned radioactive stone, but Keir confirmed it had no connection to the treaty case. Vask delivered a sharp retort when Maelin said that a convenient translation was not necessarily a lawful one. A mechanic used a screwdriver to repair the hearing room's broken speaking stand before testimony resumed. Each serving of soup was recorded separately so food distribution could not be confused with land settlement. The splendor of the royal hall could not hide the hunger described by witnesses from the river villages. Taken alone, no detail settled the case; together they showed why a public answer mattered.

團隊把具體觀察與有爭議的理論並列保存： 舊礦業報告提及放射性石料，凱爾確認它與條約案件無關。 瑟拉說方便的翻譯未必合法時，瓦斯克尖刻地反駁。 技師用螺絲起子修好聽證室損壞的發言台，證詞才得以繼續。 每份湯食單獨記錄，避免配糧與土地和解混為一談。 王室大廳的華麗掩蓋不了河岸村民證詞中的飢餓。 單獨看來沒有一項細節能結案，合在一起卻說明了公開答覆為何重要。

The prophecy lens showed Sera a collapse in a fraction of a second. She wrote down the conditions before the image faded, because a disaster without its causes could be used to justify almost anything. Tarin made her add the words this may be prevented beneath every prediction.

預言透鏡在剎那間讓瑟拉看見崩塌。她趁畫面消失前寫下條件，因為沒有原因的災難影像幾乎可以拿來合理化任何事。塔林要求她在每份預言下加上「此事可能被避免」。

The record continued with small facts that would be lost in a confident speech: A spontaneous offer from local bakers filled the temporary shelter with bread before nightfall. The boy began to stutter when he saw his stepfather at the back of the hall. The young clerk began to stutter under pressure, so the judge gave him time to answer slowly. The government agreed to subsidize emergency grain transport without demanding a surrender of property rights. A synthetic ribbon on the false archive seal differed from the natural fiber used in the originals. Sera asked the clerk to preserve the original wording rather than improve the story.

紀錄接著列出那些在自信演說中容易消失的小事實： 當地麵包師自發提供協助，夜幕前把麵包送滿臨時避難所。 少年在庭後看見繼父，說話開始結巴。 年輕書記在壓力下開始口吃，法官讓他慢慢回答。 政府同意補貼緊急運糧，不要求居民放棄財產權。 偽造檔案封印上的合成纖維帶與原件使用的天然纖維不同。 瑟拉要求書記保存原本措辭，而不是把故事修飾得更好聽。

On the way out, Sera passed people waiting for a result that would affect their meals, their homes, or their ability to say no. None of them had the luxury of treating the dispute as an intellectual puzzle. Tarin paused to answer a child's practical question before they left. The answer was incomplete, but unlike the old official notice, it admitted the uncertainty out loud.

離開時，瑟拉經過等待結果的人群，這項結果將影響他們的餐食、家園或說不的能力。他們不能奢侈地把爭論當成智力謎題。塔林停下回答一名孩子實際的問題。答案並不完整，卻與舊公告不同：它坦白承認不確定之處。
