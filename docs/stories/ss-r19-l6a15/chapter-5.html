The Grain Ship That Sank Twice

帝國預言官只能看見失敗的未來 · 第 5 / 25 章 · 5000–6000 · 英中對照 · 校訂中

By noon, rumors had outrun the facts. Some people wanted a villain; others wanted a hero who could end the argument without asking another question. A grain ship appears in Sera’s vision sinking at dawn, but an independent captain changes its course. Sera had worked long enough to distrust both shortcuts. In the imperial observatory, the first task was to protect the evidence before anyone could turn it into a weapon.

到了中午，謠言早已跑得比事實快。有人想找反派，也有人想找英雄，好在不再追問的情況下結束爭論。瑟拉看見糧船黎明沉沒，獨立船長卻自作主張改變航線。瑟拉工作多年，已經不相信這兩種捷徑。在帝國觀星臺，當務之急是先保護證據，免得任何人將它變成武器。

The prophecy lens showed Sera a collapse in a fraction of a second. She wrote down the conditions before the image faded, because a disaster without its causes could be used to justify almost anything. Tarin made her add the words this may be prevented beneath every prediction.

預言透鏡在剎那間讓瑟拉看見崩塌。她趁畫面消失前寫下條件，因為沒有原因的災難影像幾乎可以拿來合理化任何事。塔林要求她在每份預言下加上「此事可能被避免」。

The argument was not merely about who held authority. It was about what happened when an official decision met a household, a workplace, or a body that could not obey it safely. Sera asked Tarin to describe the smallest practical consequence. Only then did they realize that the proposed remedy would reproduce the very harm it claimed to prevent. They changed the plan before presenting it in public.

爭論不只是誰有權威，而是官方決定落到家庭、職場或無法安全服從的身體上時會發生什麼。瑟拉請塔林說明最小、最實際的後果。他們這才發現提議中的補救會重複它聲稱要防止的傷害，因此在公開發表前修改了計畫。

The overlooked details in the witness file made the question harder, not easier: Several students quietly began to rejoice; others feared that the academy would be closed for allowing dissent. Vask rejected the accusation that his office had changed the treaty for private gain. At the banquet, every guest received a copy of the disputed clause in three languages. A farmer explained that fertilizer purchased for spring planting was now locked inside a seized warehouse. The coastal fishery had lost buyers because the new checkpoint charged an unauthorized fee. Sera asked the clerk to preserve the original wording rather than improve the story.

證人檔案裡被忽視的細節使問題更加複雜，而不是更簡單： 幾名學生悄悄歡欣，其他人則害怕學院因容許異議而被關閉。 瓦斯克否認其辦公室為私利修改條約的指控。 宴會上每位賓客都拿到三種語言並列的爭議條款。 農民解釋，為春耕購買的肥料如今被鎖在遭扣押的倉庫。 沿岸漁業因新檢查站收取未授權費用而失去買家。 瑟拉要求書記保存原本措辭，而不是把故事修飾得更好聽。

What changed the discussion was a fact that should have been obvious from the start. The ship survives, proving a failed future is a warning rather than a fixed sentence. Sera felt the old temptation to turn that fact into an immediate victory. Instead, the team separated what the new evidence established from what it merely suggested. That distinction frustrated the loudest spectators, but it helped the witnesses remain willing to speak again.

改變討論的是一件本來就該看得見的事實。糧船成功靠岸，證明失敗的未來是警訊，不是既定判決。瑟拉一瞬間也想把它當成立刻勝利的證明，團隊卻把新證據真正確立的部分與僅能推測的部分分開。這讓最喧鬧的旁觀者失望，卻使證人願意再次出面。

A second bundle of observations brought the consequences closer to ordinary life: Keir reported a fracture in the bridge beam that could endanger the next grain convoy. Dock workers used a safety harness while repairing the warehouse roof before the rain. An archivist wore headphones to review a preserved reading of the treaty ceremony. The emergency grain intake had doubled, but the market had no public record of distribution. The maiden voyage of a new ferry brought delegates who had never met the palace officers. Tarin marked what could be verified and left the rest open to challenge.

第二疊觀察紀錄使後果貼近普通人的生活： 凱爾報告橋梁橫樑有裂縫，可能危及下一支運糧隊。 碼頭工人在雨前修理倉庫屋頂時使用安全吊帶。 檔案員戴著耳機聆聽保存的條約儀式宣讀錄音。 緊急糧食收取量倍增，市場卻沒有公開分配紀錄。 新渡船的首航載來從未見過宮廷官員的代表。 塔林標記能查證的部分，其餘保留給人質疑。

There were two tempting shortcuts. One would protect the reputation of Marshal Dorian and leave the public guessing. The other would expose every private detail at once and call that transparency. Sera rejected both. The issue was whether fear of disaster justifies the empire’s wars, not whether the team could win an argument in a single afternoon. A smaller step, supported by independent observers, could protect people while the wider dispute remained open.

有兩種誘人的捷徑：一種保住多里安元帥的名聲，讓大眾自行猜測；另一種把所有私人細節一次揭露，並稱之為透明。瑟拉兩者都拒絕。核心是對災難的恐懼是否足以合理化帝國發動戰爭，不是團隊能否在一個下午贏得爭論。由獨立觀察者支持的一小步，即使爭議未結，也能先保護民眾。

The team kept the concrete observations alongside the disputed theory: An offshore storm forced the banquet's planned river parade to be cancelled. A prospective buyer was told that the grain's ownership remained under legal review. The Assembly demanded a referendum only after residents had received translations they could actually read. Nessa asked the clerks to refine the public table without concealing the original spelling. Families could rejoice at the temporary food order without pretending the land dispute was over. Nothing in these records excused the people who had made the dangerous decision.

團隊把具體觀察與有爭議的理論並列保存： 近海風暴迫使宴會預定的河上遊行取消。 一名潛在買家被告知，糧食所有權仍在司法審理中。 議會要求先讓居民讀懂譯文，再舉行公民投票。 妮莎請書記改善公開對照表，但不可隱藏原始拼法。 家人可以為臨時配糧令欣喜，無須假裝土地爭議已結束。 紀錄中沒有任何一項能替做出危險決定的人開脫。

The prophecy lens showed Sera a collapse in a fraction of a second. She wrote down the conditions before the image faded, because a disaster without its causes could be used to justify almost anything. Tarin made her add the words this may be prevented beneath every prediction.

預言透鏡在剎那間讓瑟拉看見崩塌。她趁畫面消失前寫下條件，因為沒有原因的災難影像幾乎可以拿來合理化任何事。塔林要求她在每份預言下加上「此事可能被避免」。

The record continued with small facts that would be lost in a confident speech: Some fishers began to resent the palace spokesman for describing their losses as trivial. A serene moment followed when the three delegations read the same sentence aloud together. The temple speaker gave a sermon about promises but refused to decide what the treaty legally meant. The guests had to shuffle between the hall and the archive to inspect the authenticated seals. A tenant farmer testified that the governor had collected payment on land he did not own. Taken alone, no detail settled the case; together they showed why a public answer mattered.

紀錄接著列出那些在自信演說中容易消失的小事實： 部分漁民開始怨恨王宮發言人，因為他把損失說得微不足道。 三方代表一起朗讀同一句話後，現場出現短暫寧靜。 寺院講者發表關於承諾的講道，卻拒絕替條約的法律意思下定論。 賓客必須在宴會廳與檔案室間緩步移動，查看認證印章。 一名佃農作證說，總督在不屬於他的土地上收取費用。 單獨看來沒有一項細節能結案，合在一起卻說明了公開答覆為何重要。

The chapter ended without an easy reassurance. Sera had learned one thing that could be checked and another that still needed an answer. Tarin wrote both on the same page so that relief would not erase doubt. Their decision had moved the crisis forward, but it had also drawn the attention of Marshal Dorian. By morning, the next question would reach people far beyond this room.

這一天沒有簡單的安慰。瑟拉知道了一件能查證的事，也留下另一件尚待回答的事。塔林把兩者寫在同一頁，免得鬆一口氣就抹掉疑問。他們的決定使危機向前，也引起多里安元帥注意。明早下一個問題將傳到房間外更遠的地方。
