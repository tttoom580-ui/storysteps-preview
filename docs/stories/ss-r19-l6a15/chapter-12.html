The Child of the Floodplain

The room smelled of dust, hot metal, and rain carried in on people's coats. Someone had left an ordinary object on the central table, yet no one wanted to touch it. A child from the river plain recounts how her village survived an expected flood through ordinary evacuation drills. Sera saw that the object mattered less for what it was than for the decision it proved had been made. There would be no honest way back to ignorance.

室內有灰塵、熱金屬與人們外套帶進的雨味。有人把平凡物件放在中央桌上，卻沒有人願意碰它。河漫灘的孩子說出村莊如何靠普通撤離演習撐過預言中的洪水。瑟拉明白重要的不是物件本身，而是它證明了何種決定已經發生。如今已不可能誠實地假裝無知。

The prophecy lens showed Sera a collapse in a fraction of a second. She wrote down the conditions before the image faded, because a disaster without its causes could be used to justify almost anything. Tarin made her add the words this may be prevented beneath every prediction.

預言透鏡在剎那間讓瑟拉看見崩塌。她趁畫面消失前寫下條件，因為沒有原因的災難影像幾乎可以拿來合理化任何事。塔林要求她在每份預言下加上「此事可能被避免」。

The evidence did not arrive as a confession. It came through a date that did not fit, a measurement that had been repeated, and a witness who knew exactly what the official account had left out. Sera asked Tarin not to describe suspicion as proof. If the accusation was wrong, an innocent person would suffer. If it was right, careless language could still give the guilty a way to escape.

證據不是以自白形式出現，而是一個對不上的日期、一項重複測量的結果，以及一位知道官方說法遺漏何事的證人。瑟拉請塔林不要把懷疑說成證明。若指控錯了，無辜者會受害；若指控正確，草率措辭仍可能讓有罪者脫身。

The overlooked details in the witness file made the question harder, not easier: A narrow brook behind the island workshop supplied fresh water to the mechanics. Keir found coral fragments beneath a pier that the old treaty listed as shared shelter. The double fee could deprive boat owners of money needed for winter repairs. The harbor council voted to elevate safety inspection above the argument over ownership. The lease carried an expiration date that the palace summary had quietly omitted. Taken alone, no detail settled the case; together they showed why a public answer mattered.

證人檔案裡被忽視的細節使問題更加複雜，而不是更簡單： 島上工坊後的一條小溪替技師提供淡水。 凱爾在一座舊條約列為共用避難所的碼頭下發現珊瑚碎片。 雙重收費可能剝奪船主冬季修船所需的資金。 港務議會投票決定把安全檢查置於所有權爭論之上。 租約有到期日期，王宮摘要卻悄悄省略了它。 單獨看來沒有一項細節能結案，合在一起卻說明了公開答覆為何重要。

A small act of honesty unsettled the room more than a shouted threat could have done. Her testimony makes Sera’s vision useful without requiring anyone to surrender power. Sera watched a witness decide not to change a difficult detail merely because it complicated the preferred story. Tarin recorded the correction openly. Their inquiry would be slower now, but its shape belonged less to the powerful and more to the people who had lived through the event.

一個小小的誠實舉動，帶來比喊叫威脅更強的震動。她的證詞使預言能幫助人，卻不必讓任何人交出權力。瑟拉看見證人沒有因為細節讓主流說法變複雜就改口。塔林公開記錄修正。調查會因此變慢，卻能較少受權貴支配，也更貼近親歷者。

A second bundle of observations brought the consequences closer to ordinary life: The old boat builder's eyesight had weakened, so his apprentice read the dates aloud. A hearty meal was prepared for everyone stranded by the storm, including the visiting officials. A rescue bird seemed to hover above the harbor entrance before settling on the roof. The inland authorities had demanded fees for boats using an island landing they did not own. The witnesses' insistence on keeping the original lease prevented a second undocumented substitution. Sera asked the clerk to preserve the original wording rather than improve the story.

第二疊觀察紀錄使後果貼近普通人的生活： 老船匠視力衰退，因此由學徒朗讀日期。 為所有因暴風雨受困的人準備了豐盛餐點，包括外來官員。 一隻救援隊飼養的鳥似乎在港口入口上方盤旋，然後停在屋頂。 內陸當局曾向使用其無所有權島岸碼頭的船隻收費。 證人堅持保存原租約，防止第二次未記錄的替換。 瑟拉要求書記保存原本措辭，而不是把故事修飾得更好聽。

Sera had expected the hardest part to be exposing the secret. Instead, it was choosing what to do after a partial truth became public. The people who had been ignored did not all want the same remedy. Tarin proposed that each affected group could submit a written objection and receive a specific answer. The method would take time, but it recognized them as participants rather than scenery.

瑟拉原以為最難的是揭露祕密，後來才發現，部分真相公開後如何行動才是難題。曾被忽略的人並不全想要相同補救。塔林提議每個受影響群體都能提出書面異議並取得具體答覆。方法需要時間，但終於把他們當成參與者，而不是背景。

The team kept the concrete observations alongside the disputed theory: Maelin and Keir crossed the strait to inspect the original lease, leaving the legal debate open while dangerous boats were repaired. The legislator asked who had approved the new fee before any storm warning was issued. The majestic cliffs of the island looked unchanged despite the conflict written into the paperwork. Anika used the storm's momentum to persuade rival officers to open a shared shelter. The monarch had signed the original treaty but not the later pamphlet sent to the island. Tarin marked what could be verified and left the rest open to challenge.

團隊把具體觀察與有爭議的理論並列保存： 瑟拉和凱爾跨海檢查原租約，同時讓法律爭議保持開放，並先修好危險船隻。 議員詢問，誰在風暴警報發布前就核准新費用。 島上雄偉峭壁看來未變，紙上的爭議卻已延燒。 安妮卡利用風暴帶來的迫切感，說服對立官員開放共用避難所。 君主簽署過原始條約，卻未簽署送往島上的後來小冊子。 塔林標記能查證的部分，其餘保留給人質疑。

The prophecy lens showed Sera a collapse in a fraction of a second. She wrote down the conditions before the image faded, because a disaster without its causes could be used to justify almost anything. Tarin made her add the words this may be prevented beneath every prediction.

預言透鏡在剎那間讓瑟拉看見崩塌。她趁畫面消失前寫下條件，因為沒有原因的災難影像幾乎可以拿來合理化任何事。塔林要求她在每份預言下加上「此事可能被避免」。

The record continued with small facts that would be lost in a confident speech: A boatman's offspring produced the family receipt that recorded the first legal landing fee. The ferry crew reserved petrol for medical trips instead of using it to carry ceremonial guests. The wind began to rotate the loose harbor sign until Keir tied it securely. Maelin warned that the false fee might strangle the island's small repair trade over time. The nurse carried a surgical kit to the storm shelter in case anyone was injured. Nothing in these records excused the people who had made the dangerous decision.

紀錄接著列出那些在自信演說中容易消失的小事實： 船夫的子女拿出家庭收據，記載最早的合法靠岸費。 渡船員預留汽油給醫療航次，而非運送典禮賓客。 風開始使鬆動的港口標誌旋轉，直到凱爾將它綁牢。 瑟拉警告，不實收費長期可能扼殺島上小型修船業。 護士把外科急救工具帶到風暴避難所，以備有人受傷。 紀錄中沒有任何一項能替做出危險決定的人開脫。

When the lamps were lowered, Sera returned to a cracked prophecy lens. It was neither proof of innocence nor a shortcut to justice. It was a reminder that the next decision would be witnessed by people who no longer trusted easy promises. Tarin remained at the door while the final record was sealed. Outside, the imperial observatory waited for news that had to be more precise than a victory speech.

燈光調暗時，瑟拉再次看向一枚裂開的預言透鏡。它既不是清白證明，也不是通往正義的捷徑，而是在提醒眾人：下一個決定會由不再相信輕易承諾的人見證。塔林守在門邊，最後一份紀錄被封存。外頭的帝國觀星臺等待的消息，必須比勝利演說更精確。
