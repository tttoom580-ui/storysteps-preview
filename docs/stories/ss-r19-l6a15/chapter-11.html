The Trial of a Warning

帝國預言官只能看見失敗的未來 · 第 11 / 25 章 · 5000–6000 · 英中對照 · 校訂中

By noon, rumors had outrun the facts. Some people wanted a villain; others wanted a hero who could end the argument without asking another question. Sera is charged with betraying state secrets after she asks citizens to review the unpublished predictions. Sera had worked long enough to distrust both shortcuts. In the imperial observatory, the first task was to protect the evidence before anyone could turn it into a weapon.

到了中午，謠言早已跑得比事實快。有人想找反派，也有人想找英雄，好在不再追問的情況下結束爭論。瑟拉要求人民檢閱未公開預言，因此遭控洩露國家機密。瑟拉工作多年，已經不相信這兩種捷徑。在帝國觀星臺，當務之急是先保護證據，免得任何人將它變成武器。

The prophecy lens showed Sera a collapse in a fraction of a second. She wrote down the conditions before the image faded, because a disaster without its causes could be used to justify almost anything. Tarin made her add the words this may be prevented beneath every prediction.

預言透鏡在剎那間讓瑟拉看見崩塌。她趁畫面消失前寫下條件，因為沒有原因的災難影像幾乎可以拿來合理化任何事。塔林要求她在每份預言下加上「此事可能被避免」。

A witness asked whether the inquiry would make tomorrow safer. Sera could not promise that. The truth could establish responsibility, but it would not repair a broken machine, feed a hungry child, or return lost time on its own. Tarin suggested a temporary protection that could be checked after sunrise. They wrote down its limits in plain language and invited the people affected to challenge them.

一位證人問調查是否能讓明天更安全，瑟拉無法保證。真相能確立責任，卻不會自行修好機器、餵飽孩子或歸還失去的時間。塔林提出一項日出後可以檢驗的暫時保護措施。他們用白話寫清限制，邀請受影響的人提出異議。

The overlooked details in the witness file made the question harder, not easier: The temperature in the print shop rose above thirty degrees Celsius during the inspection. Friction had worn the old seal plate unevenly, unlike the smooth new impression. A guard dog began to growl when strangers approached the workshop's rear door. Maelin would not induce a confession by promising a pardon the tribunal had never authorized. Several families inhabit the alley beside the workshop, and their accounts of deliveries were recorded separately. Nothing in these records excused the people who had made the dangerous decision.

證人檔案裡被忽視的細節使問題更加複雜，而不是更簡單： 檢查期間，印刷坊的氣溫升到攝氏三十度以上。 摩擦使舊印版磨損不均，與光滑的新印痕不同。 陌生人靠近工坊後門時，警犬開始低吼。 瑟拉不會藉承諾法庭未核准的赦免來誘導供認。 幾戶家庭住在工坊旁的巷子裡，他們對送貨的說法被分別記錄。 紀錄中沒有任何一項能替做出危險決定的人開脫。

For once the numbers did not merely decorate a report; they contradicted the order that had been issued in their name. The charge forces the court to explain why a prediction is treated as military property. Tarin asked whether a mistake could explain the result. Sera agreed to test that possibility too. They checked an independent source, marked every assumption, and made copies before the originals could be transferred to an office beyond public reach.

這一次數字不只是報告裝飾，而是直接反駁以數字名義發布的命令。控罪反而迫使法庭說明，為何預言竟被當成軍事財產。塔林詢問是否可能只是出錯，瑟拉也同意檢查這種可能。他們核對獨立來源、標記每項假設，在原件被移往公眾無法接觸的辦公室前留下副本。

A second bundle of observations brought the consequences closer to ordinary life: It was ironic that the palace demanded perfect language while publishing a summary no one had checked. The workers found a mound of discarded paper behind a wall used for temporary storage. A peacock wandered across the old courtyard as investigators examined the sealed printing plates. The town pianist stopped practicing when the officers began their formal interviews. Nessa used a narrow probe to inspect the edge of the plate without touching the engraved letters. Taken alone, no detail settled the case; together they showed why a public answer mattered.

第二疊觀察紀錄使後果貼近普通人的生活： 王宮要求語言完美，自己卻發行未經核對的摘要，實在諷刺。 工人在臨時儲物牆後發現一堆丟棄的紙張。 調查員檢查封存印版時，一隻孔雀走過舊庭院。 官員開始正式訪談時，鎮上的鋼琴師停止練習。 妮莎用細探針檢查印版邊緣，沒有碰到刻字。 單獨看來沒有一項細節能結案，合在一起卻說明了公開答覆為何重要。

The debate sharpened when Marshal Dorian demanded a decision before the evidence could be copied. Sera replied that urgency and secrecy were not the same thing. They established a temporary limit, published the reason for it, and scheduled an independent review. The result satisfied nobody completely. That was acceptable; the purpose of the process was to make abuse harder, not to produce applause.

當多里安元帥要求在證據複製前就做決定，辯論變得尖銳。瑟拉回應說緊急和保密不是同一回事。團隊訂立暫時限制、公開理由，並排定獨立審查。結果沒有讓任何人完全滿意，但程序的目的本來就不是得到掌聲，而是使濫權更困難。

The team kept the concrete observations alongside the disputed theory: Each witness received a copy of the notes taken during their respective interviews. A shortsighted minister called the missing word unimportant because the map looked almost unchanged. Maelin tried to simplify the public explanation without removing the legal qualification from the original. Ships crossing the strait depended on accurate winter notices from the same office that printed the treaty. Nessa refused a superficial inspection and measured the spacing between engraved letters. Sera asked the clerk to preserve the original wording rather than improve the story.

團隊把具體觀察與有爭議的理論並列保存： 每名證人都收到自己那場訪談的筆記副本。 一名目光短淺的大臣稱遺失的字不重要，因為地圖看來幾乎沒變。 瑟拉試圖簡化公開說明，但不刪去原文的法律限定語。 穿越海峽的船隻仰賴同一辦公室發布的正確冬季公告。 妮莎拒絕膚淺檢查，仔細測量刻字間距。 瑟拉要求書記保存原本措辭，而不是把故事修飾得更好聽。

The prophecy lens showed Sera a collapse in a fraction of a second. She wrote down the conditions before the image faded, because a disaster without its causes could be used to justify almost anything. Tarin made her add the words this may be prevented beneath every prediction.

預言透鏡在剎那間讓瑟拉看見崩塌。她趁畫面消失前寫下條件，因為沒有原因的災難影像幾乎可以拿來合理化任何事。塔林要求她在每份預言下加上「此事可能被避免」。

The record continued with small facts that would be lost in a confident speech: No officer was allowed to suppress a witness statement merely because it contradicted the palace account. The perfect symmetry of the new seal suggested a different tool from the worn official stamp. A brass token bearing the shop number appeared in the carrier's payment purse. The delegates hoped the three authentic versions could unify the public record without erasing their differences. An urgency notice required investigators to preserve the plates before the workshop reopened. Tarin marked what could be verified and left the rest open to challenge.

紀錄接著列出那些在自信演說中容易消失的小事實： 任何官員都不得只因證詞與王宮說法相反就壓下證言。 新印記過於對稱，顯示可能使用不同於磨損官印的工具。 信差的付款袋裡出現一枚帶工坊編號的黃銅代幣。 代表希望三份真實版本能統一公開紀錄，同時不抹去差異。 一份緊急通知要求調查員在工坊重開前保全印版。 塔林標記能查證的部分，其餘保留給人質疑。

A message arrived after the public doors had closed. It carried no grand declaration, just a name and a time that did not match the official schedule. Sera resisted the urge to call it proof. Tarin opened a fresh page in the journal and recorded the discrepancy exactly. They had won time, not certainty, and there were still people who might lose everything if they hurried.

公共大門關閉後，一則訊息送到。它沒有豪言壯語，只有一個名字和一個對不上官方時程的時間。瑟拉克制住把它當成證明的衝動。塔林翻開日誌新的一頁，逐字記錄差異。他們贏得的是時間，不是確定性；若急於求成，仍有人可能失去一切。
