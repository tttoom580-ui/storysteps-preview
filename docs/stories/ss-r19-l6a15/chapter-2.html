A Prophecy Has No Crown

帝國預言官只能看見失敗的未來 · 第 2 / 25 章 · 5000–6000 · 英中對照 · 校訂中

A messenger arrived before the bells had finished ringing. The news sounded impossible only because everyone had been trained not to question the institution that delivered it. Marshal Dorian demands a forecast of the emperor’s next battle, but Sera sees only burning hospitals and abandoned fields. Sera asked the messenger to repeat the part that had been omitted from the written notice. It was there, in the omission, that the day's real story began.

鐘聲尚未結束，信使已經抵達。消息之所以聽來不可能，只因每個人都習慣不質疑傳遞消息的制度。多里安元帥要求預測下一場戰役，瑟拉卻只看見燃燒的醫院和荒廢的田地。瑟拉請信使重述書面公告遺漏的部分。這一天真正的故事，就從那處缺漏開始。

Sera could witness the worst plausible branch only under the conditions shown to her. A vision could change when those conditions changed, and every look ahead took one ordinary memory she could never recover by magic.

瑟拉只能看見特定條件下最糟但可能發生的分支。條件改變，預言也會改變，而每次窺視未來都會奪走一段無法靠魔法取回的普通記憶。

The prophecy lens showed Sera a collapse in a fraction of a second. She wrote down the conditions before the image faded, because a disaster without its causes could be used to justify almost anything. Tarin made her add the words this may be prevented beneath every prediction.

預言透鏡在剎那間讓瑟拉看見崩塌。她趁畫面消失前寫下條件，因為沒有原因的災難影像幾乎可以拿來合理化任何事。塔林要求她在每份預言下加上「此事可能被避免」。

The evidence did not arrive as a confession. It came through a date that did not fit, a measurement that had been repeated, and a witness who knew exactly what the official account had left out. Sera asked Tarin not to describe suspicion as proof. If the accusation was wrong, an innocent person would suffer. If it was right, careless language could still give the guilty a way to escape.

證據不是以自白形式出現，而是一個對不上的日期、一項重複測量的結果，以及一位知道官方說法遺漏何事的證人。瑟拉請塔林不要把懷疑說成證明。若指控錯了，無辜者會受害；若指控正確，草率措辭仍可能讓有罪者脫身。

The overlooked details in the witness file made the question harder, not easier: An aboriginal elder explained the old names of the river bends before any court translator offered an interpretation. Disputes abound when a public notice contains only the palace language and excludes the others. Nessa tried to align the three handwritten headings without altering the irregular spacing beneath them. Keir recorded the altitude of the ridge because spring floods could isolate the crossing below. The magistrate concealed his annoyance when Iva asked him to repeat a legal question slowly. Tarin marked what could be verified and left the rest open to challenge.

證人檔案裡被忽視的細節使問題更加複雜，而不是更簡單： 一位原住民族長者在宮廷譯員解說前，先說明河灣的舊地名。 公告只有宮廷語而排除其他語言時，爭端便大量出現。 妮莎試圖對齊三份手寫標題，但不改動其下不規則的間距。 凱爾記下山脊海拔，因為春洪可能切斷下方渡口。 伊娃請地方法官慢慢重述法律問題時，他掩飾住自己的不耐。 塔林標記能查證的部分，其餘保留給人質疑。

A small act of honesty unsettled the room more than a shouted threat could have done. An official report transforms her warning into a recommendation for an early invasion. Sera watched a witness decide not to change a difficult detail merely because it complicated the preferred story. Tarin recorded the correction openly. Their inquiry would be slower now, but its shape belonged less to the powerful and more to the people who had lived through the event.

一個小小的誠實舉動，帶來比喊叫威脅更強的震動。官方報告竟把她的警告改寫成先發制人的侵略建議。瑟拉看見證人沒有因為細節讓主流說法變複雜就改口。塔林公開記錄修正。調查會因此變慢，卻能較少受權貴支配，也更貼近親歷者。

A second bundle of observations brought the consequences closer to ordinary life: Iva gave a blunt answer: her neighbors had agreed to store grain, never to lose their homes. Sera tried to align their route with the royal charts, but the marked crossing had been destroyed months earlier. Maelin would not discard a damaged draft until both sides had inspected its surviving lines. The river guard received a medical discharge note but still had to answer questions about his shift. Their endeavor was to restore a shared crossing, not to replace one ruler with another. Nothing in these records excused the people who had made the dangerous decision.

第二疊觀察紀錄使後果貼近普通人的生活： 伊娃直截了當地回答：鄰居同意保管糧食，從未同意失去家園。 瑟拉試著讓路線與王室地圖對齊，卻發現標示的渡口早在數月前就毀了。 瑟拉不願在雙方檢查殘存字句前丟棄破損草稿。 河岸衛兵拿到出院證明後，仍須回答有關值班的問題。 他們努力的是恢復共用渡口，而非以另一位統治者取代現任者。 紀錄中沒有任何一項能替做出危險決定的人開脫。

No one in the room was permitted to declare that a good intention canceled a harmful action. Sera insisted on recording the error, identifying who had authorized it, and protecting those affected before assigning wider blame. Tarin drew a simple boundary around what they could do tonight. The line looked disappointingly modest, yet it was the first promise that did not require another person to surrender a right.

室內沒有人能宣稱善意可以抵銷傷害。瑟拉堅持記錄錯誤、確認授權者，並在追究更大責任前保護受害者。塔林畫出今晚能做到的清楚界線。這條線看似微不足道，卻是第一個不要求別人放棄權利的承諾。

The team kept the concrete observations alongside the disputed theory: A missing customs number became the first hurdle for families seeking to recover their grain. Keir changed his posture when the old survey poles no longer matched the new boundary sketch. The interpreter demanded precision in the word for custody because a careless synonym could transfer ownership. Nessa insisted on a rigorous comparison of every stamped page before anyone announced fraud. Families waited on a stone terrace overlooking the water while the magistrate prepared his order. Taken alone, no detail settled the case; together they showed why a public answer mattered.

團隊把具體觀察與有爭議的理論並列保存： 遺失的海關編號成為家庭領回糧食的第一道障礙。 舊測量桿與新邊界圖不符時，凱爾改變站姿仔細查看。 譯員要求精確使用「保管」一詞，因為草率的同義詞可能轉移所有權。 妮莎堅持先嚴格比對每張蓋印頁面，任何人才能宣布有偽造嫌疑。 家人們在俯瞰河水的石露臺等候地方法官起草命令。 單獨看來沒有一項細節能結案，合在一起卻說明了公開答覆為何重要。

The record continued with small facts that would be lost in a confident speech: The commander feared an uprising if hungry villagers were again told that their names did not matter. Volunteers offered to usher elderly witnesses toward the chamber without choosing their answers for them. The versatile survey cart carried measuring poles, winter blankets, and a box of public notices. The local council chose not to veto the temporary food route merely because the palace disliked it. A widow spoke about the fee she had paid twice for the same ferry passage. Sera asked the clerk to preserve the original wording rather than improve the story.

紀錄接著列出那些在自信演說中容易消失的小事實： 指揮官擔心若再次告訴挨餓的村民其姓名無關緊要，將引發起義。 志工引導年長證人走向聽證室，但不替他們決定答案。 多用途測量車載著測量桿、冬毯與一箱公開公告。 地方議會沒有只因王宮不喜歡就否決臨時糧食路線。 一名寡婦談起她為同一趟渡船被收取兩次的費用。 瑟拉要求書記保存原本措辭，而不是把故事修飾得更好聽。

On the way out, Sera passed people waiting for a result that would affect their meals, their homes, or their ability to say no. None of them had the luxury of treating the dispute as an intellectual puzzle. Tarin paused to answer a child's practical question before they left. The answer was incomplete, but unlike the old official notice, it admitted the uncertainty out loud.

離開時，瑟拉經過等待結果的人群，這項結果將影響他們的餐食、家園或說不的能力。他們不能奢侈地把爭論當成智力謎題。塔林停下回答一名孩子實際的問題。答案並不完整，卻與舊公告不同：它坦白承認不確定之處。
