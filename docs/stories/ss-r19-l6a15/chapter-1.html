勝利燃燒的那一天

The Day the Victory Burned

帝國預言官只能看見失敗的未來 · 第 1 / 25 章 · 5000–6000 · 英中對照 · 校訂中

用互動版查詞、朗讀與收藏

Sera saw the bridge collapse while the empire was still cheering. In her vision, soldiers rode home beneath white banners, a little girl dropped a flower, and the central arch folded into the river. By the time the dead reached the surface, the victory songs had not even stopped. Then the prophecy lens went dark. Sera was back inside the imperial observatory, her eyelid twitching with the effort of remembering something she had just lost. The emperor demanded a favorable forecast. She could see only failures. “Close the bridge,” she answered. “Now.”

帝國仍在歡呼時，瑟拉已看見橋梁倒塌。預言裡，士兵舉著白旗騎馬歸來，一名女孩掉落手中的花，中央橋拱隨即折入河水。死者浮上水面時，凱旋歌甚至還沒停。預言透鏡接著暗去。瑟拉回到帝國觀星臺，眼皮因努力回想剛剛失去的記憶而抽動。皇帝要求吉祥預報，她卻只能看見失敗。「封橋，」她回答，「現在。」

The order should have been applicable to every traveler, including officers with royal warrants, but the marshal had already arranged a parade. An archive clerk tried to abbreviate the list of expected guests. Sera refused. She needed to know precisely who would be standing on the bridge and how many people could still be moved. Historian Tarin brought a comparative drawing of the structure made before and after the last repair. The bronze anchor holding the central arch had been replaced with a cheaper copy. No demon had attacked it. No prophecy had broken it. Someone had signed for poor workmanship and called the result safe.

封橋命令本應適用每位旅人，包括持王室令狀的軍官，但元帥已安排好凱旋遊行。檔案書記想縮短預計到場者名單，瑟拉不肯。她需要知道橋上會站哪些人，以及還能撤走多少。史官塔林帶來維修前後的比較圖。固定中央橋拱的銅錨已被廉價替件取代。沒有惡魔攻擊橋梁，也不是預言把它弄壞。有人核准了粗劣施工，卻聲稱結果安全。

By the river, volunteers were broiling fish for the returning families. An injured ferryman walked with a limp as he helped an elderly widow cross the market. She pressed her grandson against her bosom, afraid of another alarm. An orderly helped people waiting in line. At a donation tent, a young soldier awkwardly asked for clean clothes for his sister, who had lost even her bra when the barracks burned. The city wanted the victory to flourish; the people simply wanted to reach home alive. Sera watched the bridge from a distance. A prediction could not move a stone or support a child. Its usefulness depended on what the living chose to do.

河邊志工正炙烤魚，供返鄉家庭食用。一名受傷渡船夫跛著腳，協助年長寡婦穿過市集。寡婦把孫子抱在胸前，害怕警報再次響起。捐衣帳篷旁，年輕士兵尷尬地替妹妹索取乾淨衣物；營房失火後她甚至失去胸罩。城市想讓勝利繁榮，人民只想平安回家。瑟拉遠望橋梁。預言不能搬動石頭，也不能支撐孩子，是否有用取決於活人接下來的選擇。

Tarin found a bank teller who had paid the bridge contractor from the military account. The teller said that a gangster had offered him a bribe to move the payment date back by one month. He refused and had been threatened ever since. Tarin asked whether he would narrate the incident in a safe hearing. The man stared toward the junction where the barracks road met the market; a pair of armed guards waited there. Sera did not claim that fear proved the witness correct. She asked him to describe only what he had seen, then sent the report to an independent engineer.

塔林找到一名出納，他曾從軍方帳戶支付橋梁承包商款項。出納說有黑幫分子向他行賄，要求把付款日期提前一個月。他拒絕後便不斷遭威脅。塔林問他是否願意在安全聽證中敘述經過。男人望向營房道路和市集交會處，一對持武器守衛正站在那裡。瑟拉沒有說恐懼能證明證人正確，只請他描述親眼所見，再把報告交給獨立工程師。

The marshal arrived with a fresh order on virgin parchment. It accused Sera of sabotaging the imperial celebration and demanded her arrest before sundown. She read the charge aloud so that no one could later invent a different one. “My visions have a finite cost,” she told him. “Every time I look, I lose a memory. If I can prevent a disaster without losing another, I will.” Marshal Dorian called her disloyal. Tarin asked why his engineers had ignored the broken anchor. Dorian answered with a threat instead of a measurement, and the crowd began to notice.

元帥帶著全新羊皮紙上的命令抵達，指控瑟拉破壞帝國慶典，要求日落前拘捕她。她大聲讀出罪名，免得日後有人捏造不同說法。「我的預言有有限的代價，」她告訴他，「每次凝視未來，我都會失去一段記憶。若能不再失去記憶就阻止災難，我就會這麼做。」多里安元帥罵她不忠。塔林質問他的工程師為何忽視斷裂銅錨，元帥只用威脅代替測量，群眾也開始注意到。

The bridge closed one hour before the parade. When the returning soldiers arrived, they grumbled but followed the temporary footpath. At the precise moment Sera had seen in her vision, the central arch dropped into the river. No one was standing on it. The crowd went silent; then the same people who had called her a traitor began shouting that she had saved them. Sera wanted neither verdict. She looked at the repair papers, at the missing signature, and at the river carrying away the stone. A failure had happened and a tragedy had not. Somewhere between those facts lay a future the marshal had not wanted her to see.

遊行前一小時，大橋封閉。返城士兵雖抱怨，仍沿臨時步道前進。就在瑟拉預言的精確時刻，中央橋拱落入河中，上面一個人都沒有。群眾沉默片刻，原本罵她叛徒的人接著高喊她救了大家。瑟拉不想接受任一種判決。她看著維修文件、消失的簽名，以及河水帶走的石塊。失敗確實發生，悲劇卻沒有發生。這兩件事之間，藏著元帥不願讓她看見的未來。
