試演那一天

The Audition

The first actor arrived at two o'clock in the afternoon. He was a shy seal who wore glasses and held a little package of paper shells. “I can play the crab,” he said. An actress from the school band asked to read the opening words, and two children wanted to be waves. Sena stood in the corner with a key to the hall. She had thought only five people would come, but more than twenty arrived. They were there to celebrate the chance to perform, not to win a great ticket prize. “I want a play where everyone has a part,” Sena said. “We may have more small roles than big ones. That is all right.”

第一位演員下午兩點到達。他是一隻害羞的海豹，戴著眼鏡，捧著裝了紙貝殼的小包裹。「我可以演小螃蟹。」他說。學校樂團的一位女演員想念開場台詞，兩個孩子想演海浪。瑟娜站在角落，手握大廳鑰匙。她原以為只有五個人來，結果到了二十多位。大家是來慶祝有機會表演，不是為了贏得昂貴的票券獎品。「我希望每個人都有角色，」瑟娜說，「小角色可能比大角色多，但沒關係。」

Sena noticed that the shy seal actor was waiting behind the curtain after everyone else had gone. He said he was afraid of forgetting the crab's last move. “If I make a mistake, will the whole play die?” he asked. Sena smiled gently. “One mistake will not end our story. We can stop, look at the sign, or begin that part again.” She asked him to practice the move twice at three o'clock the next day, with a friend beside him. He agreed and said he would feel more comfortable that way. Sena wrote the time down. A performance should help a young actor grow in confidence, not make him afraid of one wrong step. She wanted the club to keep that promise.

瑟娜發現害羞的海豹演員在其他人離開後還等在布幕後。他說自己害怕忘記螃蟹最後的動作。「如果我出錯，整齣戲會死掉嗎？」他問。瑟娜溫柔微笑：「一次錯誤不會讓故事結束。我們可以停下、看看提示牌，或重新開始那一段。」她約他第二天下午三點和朋友一起把動作練兩次。他答應了，說這樣會比較自在。瑟娜把時間記下。表演應該幫年輕演員建立信心，不是讓他害怕一次走錯。她希望社團守住這項承諾。

At break time, a child said, “I am tired. Can I go home?” Sena answered yes at once. No one had to stay for more practice than they wanted. Bram had picked up fruit at the supermarket and put it by the door. Some families were poor and could not buy special clothes, so Sena told everyone that simple things from home were enough. The media reporter Moss arrived with his camera, but he waited outside until people agreed to be photographed. The actors tried the opening scene once more. A seal dropped a shell, and another actor helped pick it up. The play was not perfect, yet they were beginning to act like a team.

休息時，一個孩子說：「我累了，可以回家嗎？」瑟娜立刻答應。沒有人必須練習超過自己願意的時間。布蘭從超級市場帶來水果，放在門旁。有些家庭經濟不寬裕，買不起特別的服裝，因此瑟娜告訴大家，從家裡帶簡單的東西就夠了。媒體記者莫斯帶著相機來了，但等大家同意拍照才入內。演員又排了一次開場。一隻海豹掉了貝殼，另一位演員幫忙撿起來。戲還不完美，但他們開始像一個團隊。

When Bram took down the old list of names, he found that two children had been given the same role by mistake. They had each worked hard on it, so he did not want to choose one without speaking to them. Sena invited both to the table. One child wanted to carry the paper shell; the other wanted to make the crab's shadow. The role could be divided into two parts, and both would be useful. Bram put their choices in the new plan. “We do not have to make a contest out of every job,” he said. The children smiled and went back to practice. What looked like a problem had become a good scene because the team had asked the people involved instead of deciding for them.

布蘭取下舊名單時，發現兩個孩子被誤排成同一個角色。兩人都努力練習，他不想沒先談就選掉其中一個。瑟娜邀請他們坐下。一個孩子想拿紙貝殼，另一個想製作螃蟹的影子。角色可以分成兩個部分，兩人都很重要。布蘭把選擇寫進新計畫。「不是每件工作都得變成比賽。」他說。孩子們笑著回去練習。原本的問題因為團隊先問當事人，而變成一場更好的戲。

Before the last scene, the band asked whether it could play a quiet opening tune in the school hall. Sena agreed to test it there, but not yet at the beach. She wanted the birds and tide rules to remain clear. One young actor suggested giving every visitor a ticket with a picture of the crab. Bram said printing many tickets would cost money, so they could instead use one simple sign at the door. The children liked that idea. When the last actor left, Sena locked the hall and counted the borrowed items. Nothing was missing. She walked home with the feeling that the little story now belonged to many people, not only to her.

最後一場之前，樂團問能否在學校大廳演奏輕柔的開場曲。瑟娜同意先在那裡測試，但不答應立刻搬去海邊；她要讓鳥類和潮水的規則維持清楚。一位年輕演員提議替每個觀眾印有螃蟹圖案的票。布蘭說印很多票要花錢，不如在入口放一張簡單的大牌子。孩子們喜歡這個辦法。最後一位演員離開時，瑟娜鎖上大廳，清點借來的物品，沒有遺失任何東西。回家路上，她感覺這個小故事已屬於許多人，不再只屬於自己。
