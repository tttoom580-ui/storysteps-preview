The Lines on the Floor

潮汐劇場的安靜掌聲 · 第 3 / 25 章 · 1000以下 · 英中對照 · 校訂完成

On Monday, an engineer named Toma came to the stage with a lamp, an eraser, and a long ruler. He asked everyone to leave the old boards alone. “We must define the unsafe area before we begin any work,” he said. Sena stood behind a line and took notes. Only a few pieces of wood were bad, but they were in places where a basketball could roll or a child could run. Toma drew an orange square around each weak board. “All these boards need to go,” he said. “The rest may stay if we check them again.” Sena was glad to hear a careful answer. Less wood would need to be replaced than she had feared.

星期一，工程師托馬帶著燈、橡皮擦和長尺來到舞台。他要大家先別碰舊木板。「開工之前，我們必須定義出不安全的區域。」他說。瑟娜站在線後記錄。壞掉的木板只有幾片，卻位在籃球可能滾過、孩子可能跑過的地方。托馬用橘色線框起每一片薄弱木板。「這些都要換，」他說，「其他的如果再次檢查合格，也許可以保留。」瑟娜很高興得到謹慎的答案。要換的木材比她原本擔心的少。

That afternoon, the engineer gave the team a lesson using small blocks of wood. He placed one block on a table and asked the children to look at the ends. One was dry and hard; the other was soft after years of sea wind and water. “Two boards can have the same color, but they may not be equally strong,” he said. The class learned to ask what was inside a thing, not only how it looked. Sena did not let the children touch the damaged wood, which could have rough edges. They could study a safe sample instead. Bram measured the spaces between the good boards and drew them on the plan. The work took longer than he wanted, yet the list of unknown parts grew smaller with every careful test.

那天下午，工程師用小木塊替團隊上了一課。他把木塊放在桌上，請孩子們看兩端。一端乾燥堅硬，另一端經年受海風與海水影響而變軟。「兩片木板顏色可能相同，但不一定一樣堅固。」他說。孩子們學到要問物品裡面的情況，不只看外表。瑟娜沒有讓孩子摸可能有粗糙邊緣的壞木板，而是讓他們研究安全樣本。布蘭量出好木板之間的距離，畫在計畫上。工作比他想的久，但每一次仔細測試，都讓未知項目更少。

Toma used a modern tool to measure the floor, then sent an email with the results. Sena printed it at the library. At a quarter past three pm, she called a meeting near the town's largest plant shop. The road had heavy traffic because a truck was moving soil across town. “We can meet inside instead,” Bram said. They chose a quiet table and read every page. The report said the stage could reopen, but only after repairs and another test. It also said that the sea reached the front steps on some nights. Sena drew a second line on the map. There would be no show at night until they understood the water better.

托馬用現代工具測量地板，接著寄出電子郵件報告。瑟娜到圖書館把它印出來。下午三點十五分，她在鎮上最大的植物店附近約了會議。因為卡車正運送土壤穿過鎮上，道路車流很大。「我們改到室內開會吧。」布蘭說。兩人找了安靜的桌子，逐頁讀報告。報告說舞台可以重新開放，但必須先修理，再做一次測試。上面也寫著某些夜晚海水會到達前方台階。瑟娜在地圖上畫出第二條線。在更了解海水之前，夜間不會有表演。

At the plant shop, an older gardener offered space for posters about the project. Sena made one poster with a picture of the damaged floor and another with the team's simple plan. She wanted people to understand why the gate was still shut. A few people asked when the opening would be, and she answered that no date had been chosen. Someone suggested that she call it a modern sea theater already. Sena shook her head. “A name does not repair wood,” she said. “We can call it a theater when visitors can use it safely.” The gardener nodded and gave her a cup of water. It was a quiet answer, but people stopped asking for a quick opening and began asking how they could help.

在植物店，一位年長園丁願意提供地方張貼專案海報。瑟娜做了一張展示損壞地板的照片，另一張是團隊的簡單計畫。她希望人們了解大門為何還關著。幾個人問何時開幕，她回答目前還沒決定日期。有人建議現在就叫它「現代海洋劇場」。瑟娜搖頭：「取名字不能修木板。等訪客能安全使用時，再叫它劇場吧。」園丁點頭，給她一杯水。這個回答雖然安靜，大家卻不再只催著快開幕，而開始問能幫什麼忙。

A boy from the school asked whether he could mail a drawing of his dream show to the team. Sena smiled. “You can bring it to our next meeting, or you can give it to your teacher,” she said. “We will look at every idea.” He drew children holding small paper fish, and a painted sun above them. It was not an expensive picture, but it gave Sena a new thought. The show did not need a large roof or bright lights to feel special. It needed a safe floor and a story people wanted to share. On the way home, Bram said, “We will have work tomorrow.” Sena answered, “Yes, and now we know where to start.”

一個學生問，可不可以把夢想中的表演圖畫寄給團隊。瑟娜微笑說：「你可以帶到下次會議，也可以交給老師。我們會看每一個想法。」男孩畫了拿著紙魚的孩子，頭上是一輪畫出來的太陽。這張畫不昂貴，卻給了瑟娜一個新點子。演出不需要巨大的屋頂或刺眼的燈才會特別。它需要安全的地板，以及大家想分享的故事。回家路上，布蘭說：「明天有工作了。」瑟娜回答：「是啊，而且現在我們知道從哪裡開始。」
