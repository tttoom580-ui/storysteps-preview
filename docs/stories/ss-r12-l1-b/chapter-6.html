岩石上的鳥兒

The Birds Above the Rocks

潮汐劇場的安靜掌聲 · 第 6 / 25 章 · 1000以下 · 英中對照 · 校訂完成

At the small bird shop, an old heron named Uma showed Sena a desk full of pictures. “The history of this beach is written by the birds,” she said. Each spring they came to the deep space between two rocks. When the water began to rise, their young stayed high above it. Sena asked what the theater team could do. Uma put an arm around the back of her seat and pointed through the window. “A quiet afternoon is more convenient for us than a loud evening,” she said. “However, the best answer may be a show without drums.” Sena was surprised. She had imagined music with every scene. But she promised to listen before making a choice.

在小小的鳥類照護店裡，年長的蒼鷺烏瑪讓瑟娜看滿桌照片。「這片海灘的歷史由鳥兒寫成。」她說。每年春天，牠們會回到兩塊岩石間深深的空隙。海水開始上升時，幼鳥留在較高處。瑟娜問劇團能做什麼。烏瑪把手臂放在椅背上，指向窗外。「安靜的下午比吵鬧的傍晚更方便我們照顧鳥兒，」她說，「但最好的答案也許是不打鼓的演出。」瑟娜很驚訝。她原本想像每場戲都配音樂，但她答應先傾聽再選擇。

Uma took the team to a safe place above the rocks to watch the birds from far away. She asked the children to use their eyes and not call out when a bird flew near. Sena saw how a parent bird returned again and again with food. The work reminded her of the actors, who also needed practice and care to grow confident. “If we make the stage noisy now, the birds may not come back to this spot,” Uma explained. Sena asked if there were quiet hours in every season. Uma said the needs changed, so the group would have to ask again before each new show. A rule that worked in summer was not a promise about winter. Sena added that point to the theater plan.

烏瑪帶團隊到岩石上方的安全地點，從遠處觀察鳥兒。她請孩子們用眼睛看，別在鳥兒飛近時大叫。瑟娜看見親鳥一次又一次帶著食物回來，想到演員也需要練習與照顧，才能建立自信。「如果現在把舞台弄得很吵，鳥兒可能不再回到這裡。」烏瑪解釋。瑟娜問每個季節是否都有安靜時段。烏瑪說需求會改變，所以每次新的演出之前都得再次詢問。夏天適用的規則，不代表冬天也一樣。瑟娜把這點加進劇場計畫。

Outside the shop, Bram stepped in mud and nearly lost a shoe. A small fish jumped near the water, and he made a funny face. “I can find the good parts of a bad morning,” he said. Uma joined them and showed them where people could stand without troubling the birds. She did not ask the group to stop forever. Instead, she asked them to stop when the young birds needed peace. Sena drew that area on her plan. She would speak to the actors about softer voices. It was not only about being kind; it was about sharing the same place with animals who could not ask for help in words.

店外，布蘭踩進泥巴，差點掉了一隻鞋。岸邊一條小魚跳出水面，他做出滑稽的表情。「糟糕的早晨也能找到好事。」他說。烏瑪跟著出來，指給他們看哪些地方可以站人而不干擾鳥兒。她沒有要求劇團永遠停演，只希望在幼鳥需要安靜時暫停。瑟娜把那些區域畫進計畫，準備和演員討論較柔和的聲音。這不只是善良的問題，也是在學習與無法用人類語言求助的動物分享同一個地方。

In the shop, Uma showed them a wooden box with pictures from thirty years ago. The old stage had once hosted a quiet puppet show, and people had watched without shouting. “People think a play must be loud to be good,” she said. “That is not a fact. It is only one way of telling a story.” Bram asked how they might begin a quiet show. Uma moved two paper birds across her desk, then let them stop and look at each other. No one spoke, but the children understood that the birds had met. Sena laughed at the simple scene. The group left with a new idea for their crab play: when two characters met, they could show it with a pause and a gentle wave.

在店裡，烏瑪拿出三十年前的木盒照片。老舞台曾演過安靜的布偶戲，觀眾沒有大喊大叫。「大家以為戲一定要吵才好，」她說，「那不是事實，只是一種說故事的方法。」布蘭問安靜的戲要怎麼開始。烏瑪讓兩隻紙鳥在桌上移動，接著停下來互相注視。沒人說話，孩子們卻看懂牠們相遇了。瑟娜被簡單的場景逗笑。團隊帶著新點子離開：螃蟹戲裡兩個角色相遇時，可以用停頓和溫柔揮手表達。

That night, the local news showed a picture of the closed stage. A shop owner had put an envelope of old programs on Sena's table, and she read them for ideas. Some old shows had been quiet stories told with hands and colored paper. “Welcome to the past,” Bram joked when he saw them. Sena smiled, but she did not want to repeat the past without thought. She wanted the present team to make its own show. A new idea was growing: the sound of the sea could be part of the play, while the actors used signs and movement. The next step was to ask the children whether that kind of story would be fun.

那晚，地方新聞播放了關閉舞台的照片。一位店主在瑟娜桌上放了一個裝著舊節目單的信封，她翻閱尋找靈感。過去有些表演是用手勢和彩紙說故事的安靜戲劇。「歡迎回到過去。」布蘭看著節目單開玩笑。瑟娜笑了，但不想不加思考地複製舊作。她希望現在的團隊創作自己的節目。一個新想法正在萌芽：讓海浪聲成為戲的一部分，演員則用標誌與動作表演。下一步是問孩子們，這樣的故事好不好玩。
