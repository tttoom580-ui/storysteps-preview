鎮上的三個問題

The Town's Three Questions

The next morning, Sena left her apartment early. She passed a church with a bell, then came to the town office. A reporter named Moss was sitting on a sofa with a notebook. “Is anybody going to put on a show?” he asked. Sena did not want to guess. “I am here to ask about safety first,” she said. “We may be late for the summer fair, but we should not forget the people who will sit near the water.” Mrs. Fern showed her three clear questions on a piece of paper: Is the stage strong? Can everyone get in? Can the show stay quiet when the birds need rest? Each answer needed proof, not a pretty picture.

隔天清晨，瑟娜很早就離開公寓。她經過一座有鐘的教堂，來到鎮公所。記者莫斯坐在沙發上，手裡拿著筆記本。「有人要辦表演嗎？」他問。瑟娜不想亂猜。「我是先來問安全問題，」她說，「我們也許趕不上夏季市集，但不該忘記坐在水邊的觀眾。」芬恩太太拿出一張紙，上面有三個清楚的問題：舞台夠堅固嗎？每個人都能進場嗎？鳥兒需要休息時，表演能保持安靜嗎？每個答案都需要證據，不是漂亮的圖片。

At the office, Sena also met an older teacher who had taken children to shows for many years. She asked which part of a visit was hardest. The teacher did not talk about the actors at all. “We need to know where to wait, where to sit, and where to go if a child feels tired,” she explained. “A show is not only what happens on a stage.” Sena wrote the words beside the three questions. The teacher pointed out that the road by the church could become busy at lunch. Families in the apartment buildings nearby might prefer to walk rather than use a car. Bram promised to draw more than one safe way to reach the waiting area. Nobody wanted to turn the fun afternoon into a long line of worried visitors.

在公所，瑟娜還遇到一位多年帶孩子看戲的年長老師。她問老師，看戲最困難的是哪個部分。老師完全沒談演員。「我們需要知道在哪裡等、坐哪裡，以及孩子累了時該往哪裡走，」她解釋，「演出不只是舞台上的事。」瑟娜把這些話寫在三個問題旁。老師指出教堂附近的道路午餐時可能很忙。附近公寓住戶也許比較喜歡步行，而不是開車。布蘭答應畫出不只一條通往等候區的安全路線。沒人希望有趣的下午變成一長排憂心等待的訪客。

At lunch, Bram met Sena beside the pond behind the office. He brought a pen and drew a third door on the back of the stage. “We could make an easier way for chairs with wheels,” he said. Sena liked the thought, but they would have to ask an engineer before they could build it. A family had offered to pay for some paint, while another neighbor wanted to give wood. Their town was small, but people had come from every part of the country to live there. Sena wrote down names and telephone numbers only after asking permission. She knew that a kind offer did not mean she could share someone's phone number with everyone else.

午餐時，布蘭在公所後面的池塘旁與瑟娜碰面。他帶來一支筆，在舞台背面畫了第三道門。「我們可以做一條方便輪椅通過的入口。」他說。瑟娜喜歡這個想法，但他們得先問工程師才能施工。有個家庭願意付一部分油漆費，另一位鄰居想提供木材。他們的小鎮居民來自全國各地。瑟娜取得允許後才記下大家的姓名和電話號碼。她知道，別人願意幫忙，不代表她可以隨便公開對方的電話。

Before going home, Sena asked Mrs. Fern who would be allowed to say that the stage was ready. “The engineer checks the wood,” Mrs. Fern answered. “I check the town rules. The group checks its own show.” That made the work feel larger, but also clearer. Sena put a line under each job in her notebook. If the engineer said no, the play could not be held on the sea stage. If the tide was high, they had to use another place. If a child felt unsafe, an adult would help that child leave. Sena wanted the rules to be easy to remember and fair for anybody who came. She shut the notebook with a calm feeling: asking for help was a sign of care, not weakness.

回家前，瑟娜問芬恩太太，誰可以宣布舞台準備好了。「工程師檢查木頭，」芬恩太太回答，「我檢查鎮上的規定，劇團檢查自己的演出。」這讓工作看起來更大，卻也更清楚。瑟娜在筆記本裡替每項工作畫線。如果工程師說不行，戲就不能在海邊演；如果潮水高，就得改場地；如果孩子感到不安全，成年人要幫孩子離開。瑟娜希望每位訪客都能容易記住這些公平的規則。她平靜地闔上筆記本：尋求幫忙是關心的表現，不是軟弱。

Moss came to the pond with his camera. He asked if the show was certain. Bram opened his mouth, but Sena answered first. “No. We are serious about trying, and we are serious about saying what we do not know.” Moss put his camera away. He said that a good reporter should ask again when the facts changed. Sena gave him the public office number, not the list of helpers. On the walk home, she and Bram made a clear plan: find an engineer, watch the tide, and talk to the bird keeper. Sena felt tired, yet she was glad they had not made a promise they could not keep. The three questions would guide their next days.

莫斯拿著相機來到池塘。他問表演是不是確定會舉行。布蘭正要回答，瑟娜先開口：「還沒有。我們認真想嘗試，也認真說出自己不知道的事。」莫斯收起相機，說好記者應該在事實改變時再次詢問。瑟娜給他的是公所的公開電話，不是志工名單。回家路上，她和布蘭訂出清楚計畫：找工程師、觀察潮水、跟照顧鳥類的人談談。瑟娜雖然疲倦，卻很高興他們沒有許下做不到的承諾。這三個問題將指引接下來的日子。
