人人都能走的路

A Path for Everyone

潮汐劇場的安靜掌聲 · 第 11 / 25 章 · 1000以下 · 英中對照 · 校訂中

用互動版查詞、朗讀與收藏

Sena took a basket of colored paper to the library, where a group was making signs for the new path. She wanted to check the color of each sign in good light. A girl named Pia came in wearing a white belt and carrying a kite with a picture of a fish. Pia used a chair with wheels when she went a long way. “I hate it when people decide for me what I can do,” she said. “If the path is thin, please tell me. I can help test a model.” Sena did not make a joke about the chair or ask Pia for health details. She asked whether Pia wished to join the planning team. Pia said yes, as long as her choices counted like those of every other player.

瑟娜帶著一籃彩紙到圖書館，大家正在替新通道做指示牌。她想在明亮的地方檢查每種顏色。女孩琵亞走進來，腰間繫著白色腰帶，手上拿著畫了魚的風箏。她走遠路時會使用輪椅。「我討厭別人替我決定能做什麼，」她說，「如果通道太窄，請告訴我。我能幫忙測試模型。」瑟娜沒有拿輪椅開玩笑，也沒有打聽琵亞的健康隱私，而是問她是否願意加入規劃團隊。琵亞答應了，條件是她的選擇要和其他成員一樣受到重視。

After the meeting, Pia and Sena went to the high road to look at the space where the path would begin. They did not enter the work area. Pia said she often met people who wanted to help by pushing her chair without asking. “Sometimes I want help, and sometimes I do not,” she explained. “The best way is to ask me first.” Sena thanked her for saying so. She had been careful with the old stage, but she saw that she could also be more careful with other people's choices. Bram arrived carrying a folded drawing. He asked whether Pia wished to look at the model from the path side or the sea side. Pia chose the path side. For a few minutes, they tested the picture together and changed one turn.

會議後，琵亞和瑟娜走到高處道路，看看通道將從哪裡開始，但沒有走進施工區。琵亞說，她常遇到有人沒問過就想推她的輪椅。「有時我想要幫忙，有時不需要，」她解釋，「最好的辦法是先問我。」瑟娜謝謝她願意說。她一直小心對待老舞台，現在看見自己也能更用心尊重別人的選擇。布蘭帶著摺好的圖走來，問琵亞想從通道側還是海側看模型。琵亞選了通道側。他們一起測試圖面幾分鐘，改了一個轉角。

They spread several pictures on the table. One showed a path from the road to the high waiting area. Another showed a family room beside the old hospital, which the town sometimes used for public events. “Those are useful ideas,” Bram said, “but the hospital room is far from the sea.” Pia looked at the map. “A good path is not only wide,” she said. “It must be firm when the ground is wet.” Sena wrote that down. She would send the engineer a copy of the drawing, not ask children to test the real shore. Every small line on the paper was part of a real person's trip. That made the drawing feel important.

他們把幾張圖攤在桌上。一張畫的是從道路通向高處等候區的通道，另一張是舊醫院旁的家庭活動室，鎮上偶爾在那裡辦公開活動。「這些點子很有用，」布蘭說，「但醫院活動室離海太遠。」琵亞看著地圖說：「好的通道不只是夠寬，地面濕的時候也必須穩固。」瑟娜把這點寫下來。她要把圖寄給工程師，而不是請孩子去真正的海岸試走。紙上每條細線都關係著真實的人要走的路，圖畫因此顯得重要。

The library keeper offered to put a large sign near the front door so neighbors could read the path plan. Sena wrote a note asking for ideas, but she also made clear which parts had already been checked by the engineer. A child suggested painting the whole road blue. Pia thought a blue line might be useful, but a road could become wet and slippery with the wrong paint. They would have to ask Toma before changing the surface. The child looked disappointed until Sena invited him to design a blue paper sign instead. He liked the new job and began drawing at once. The group learned that listening to an idea did not mean accepting every part of it. It meant taking the person seriously.

圖書館管理員願意在大門旁放一張大牌，讓鄰居閱讀通道計畫。瑟娜寫了徵求意見的短箋，也清楚標明哪些部分已經工程師檢查。一個孩子建議把整條路漆成藍色。琵亞覺得藍色線條或許有用，但若用錯油漆，路面濕時可能滑倒。修改地面前得先問托馬。孩子原本失望，直到瑟娜邀請他改設計藍色紙牌。他很喜歡新工作，立刻動筆。團隊學會傾聽不代表接受每個細節，而是認真看待提出想法的人。

At the end of the day, Pia showed the group how she measured a turn with a toy chair. If the chair hit a wall, she moved the paper wall and tried again. She said, “Dear friends, do not build a path just because it looks pretty. Check whether it works.” Everyone laughed at her serious voice, and Pia laughed too. A younger child wanted to put the kite above the path as a welcome sign. Pia liked it, but asked them to keep it out of the way of tall visitors. Sena made a list of all the tests they still needed. For the first time, the path felt like something people would use, not just something on a drawing.

那天結束前，琵亞示範如何用玩具輪椅測量轉彎空間。如果椅子撞上牆，她就移動紙牆再試一次。她說：「親愛的朋友們，不要只因為通道好看就去蓋。要檢查它能不能用。」大家被她認真的語氣逗笑，琵亞自己也笑了。一個小孩子想把風箏掛在通道上方當迎賓標誌。琵亞喜歡，但提醒要避開高個子的訪客。瑟娜列出尚待完成的測試。這條路第一次像是真有人會走的路，而不只是一幅畫。
