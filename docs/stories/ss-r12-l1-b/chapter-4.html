潮水留下的線

The Water Line

潮汐劇場的安靜掌聲 · 第 4 / 25 章 · 1000以下 · 英中對照 · 基礎校訂完成

Before sunrise, Sena and Bram went east to look at the sea. A green line of wet plants showed where the water had been the night before. On the wall was a small bowl that a bird keeper used for water; she had moved it to a safe place above the steps. “Both the floor and the path matter,” Mrs. Fern said when she came. “You must not cross the low steps when the sea is high.” A cute seal child knocked on the old gate and asked for a surprise show. Sena shook her head. “We cannot pass this gate yet. A safe choice is more important than a fast one.” The child looked sad, but agreed to stay back.

日出前，瑟娜和布蘭往東走，觀察海面。一條綠色的濕植物痕跡顯示昨晚海水到過哪裡。牆邊有個鳥類照顧員使用的小水碗，她已把碗移到台階上方安全處。芬恩太太來時說：「地板和通道都重要。海水高時，你們一定不能走過低台階。」一個可愛的小海豹敲了敲舊門，問能否辦場驚喜演出。瑟娜搖頭：「我們還不能通過這道門。安全的選擇比快速的選擇重要。」孩子有點難過，但同意退後。

A small group of children gathered behind the safe line to watch the water. One asked why the wet green marks were higher than the sand. Toma explained that the tide could come in and then go out again, leaving marks behind. He drew a little wave on his paper and used a bowl of water to show the movement. He made it clear that this was only a model. A real wave could be much stronger, so nobody should use the model as permission to enter the beach. Sena noticed that the children understood the danger better when they could see it. They asked good questions and stayed quiet while the engineer worked. Even the youngest child remembered to knock on the gate instead of trying to climb it.

一小群孩子站在安全線後觀察海水。一個孩子問，為什麼濕綠色的痕跡比沙地還高。托馬解釋潮水會進來，也會退去，留下痕跡。他在紙上畫浪，並用一碗水示範移動方式。他特別說明這只是模型；真實海浪可能強得多，不能因為看過模型就進入海灘。瑟娜發現，孩子親眼看見後更明白危險。他們提出好問題，工程師工作時也保持安靜。連最小的孩子都記得敲門，而不是爬門。

Toma returned after breakfast and put a medium block of wood by the gate. He wanted to see if the water would move it. Then he took a photograph from a place above the beach. “Do not try to make the sea listen to us,” he told the group. “We must look at what it really does.” A taxicab stopped on the high road, and the driver, Mrs. Dot, came down with towels. She had just given a baby a bath at home and was carrying clean towels for the town store. She offered one for the wet table, but she did not step past the safe line. Her small act helped the team keep their notes dry.

托馬吃過早餐後回來，在門邊放了一塊中等大小的木塊，想觀察海水是否會移動它。接著他從海灘上方的安全位置拍照。「別想叫海聽我們的，」他告訴大家，「我們得看它實際怎麼做。」一輛計程車停在高處道路，司機朵特太太帶著毛巾走來。她剛在家替寶寶洗過澡，正順路把乾淨毛巾送去商店。她借出一條擦濕桌子，自己卻沒有跨過安全線。她的小小幫忙讓大家的筆記保持乾燥。

Later, Mrs. Fern led a walk along the high road. The group looked at where a taxi could stop and where a person could cross from the town side without stepping into traffic. Bram drew the places on paper, and Sena asked the adults if they could see the stage gate from the road. They could, but the old sign was too small. The group made a list of better words for a new sign. “The message should be clear in good weather and bad,” Mrs. Fern said. “People should know where to wait even when no one is here to explain.” Sena imagined families arriving after a long ride and felt glad they were thinking about the journey as well as the play.

後來，芬恩太太帶大家沿著高處道路走。他們查看計程車能停在哪裡，以及人們如何從鎮側穿越而不走進車流。布蘭在紙上畫出地點，瑟娜問成年人能否從路上看見舞台大門。大家看得見，但舊指示牌太小。團隊列出新牌子應寫的字。「訊息在好天氣或壞天氣都應該清楚，」芬恩太太說，「即使沒人在旁解釋，人們也要知道在哪裡等待。」瑟娜想像一家人坐了很久車才到，慶幸大家不只想演出，也在思考來訪的路程。

By afternoon, the block had moved. It was clear that the sea could reach farther than people remembered. Some wanted to put the stage higher at once. Others wanted to close it forever. Sena asked everyone to be quiet while Toma explained a third choice: keep the old stage for short daytime shows, and build a dry waiting area on higher land. There was enough room for a gentle path, too. It would not be a perfect answer for every day, but it would let the town try without placing people in danger. Bram wrote down what the plan could do, and what it could not. They left the gate closed and carried the new drawing home.

到了下午，那塊木頭已經移了位置。很明顯，海水能到達的地方比人們記得的更遠。有人想立刻加高舞台，也有人想永遠關閉。瑟娜請大家安靜聽托馬說明第三種選擇：舊舞台只安排短時間的白天演出，等候區則設在較高的乾地，並規劃平緩通道。這不是每一天都適用的完美答案，但可以讓鎮民在不冒險的情況下嘗試。布蘭記下這個計畫能做到和做不到的事。他們讓門保持關閉，把新圖帶回家。
