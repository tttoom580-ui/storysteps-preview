博物館的盒子

The Museum Box

潮汐劇場的安靜掌聲 · 第 8 / 25 章 · 1000以下 · 英中對照 · 校訂中

The town museum had a book about plays from long ago. Sena went there at noon and found the keeper, a deer named Wren. He said some people thought old plays were boring, but he did not agree. “Each show is a new idea when new people tell it,” he said. In a wooden case, he kept a crown from a play about a queen, a small fork from a dinner scene, and a full roll of painted cloth. He would lend the cloth but not the crown. It was dangerous to wear a heavy crown near the sea, and the museum had only one. Sena listened carefully and thanked him for his clear rule.

鎮上的博物館有一本介紹古老戲劇的書。瑟娜中午過去，找到管理員鹿阿文。他說有些人覺得舊戲無聊，但他不同意。「讓新的人重新說故事，每齣戲都會有新想法。」他說。木盒裡放著演女王時用過的皇冠、晚餐場景的小叉子，還有一整卷彩繪布。他願意出借布，卻不借皇冠。沉重的皇冠在海邊佩戴有危險，博物館也只有一頂。瑟娜仔細聽了，感謝他說明清楚的規定。

Wren led the visitors through a small room about old theater workers. There was a box for painted pictures, a shelf for books, and a line on the floor that told people how far to stand from the old cloth. The group had to listen to his instructions, because some things were too fragile to touch. “I like a museum where questions are welcome,” Sena said. Wren answered that questions helped keep the objects alive in people's minds. He told them about children who had played in a traveling theater long ago. The actors carried their props by train and sometimes performed in school rooms instead of large buildings. Sena felt closer to those players than to the famous queen in the picture. They had made stories with what they had.

阿文帶訪客走過一間介紹舊劇場工作者的小房間。裡面有裝彩繪圖的盒子、書架，以及告訴人們要離舊布多遠的地板線。團隊必須聽他的指示，因為有些物品太脆弱，不能碰。「我喜歡可以提問的博物館。」瑟娜說。阿文回答，提問能讓物品在人們心中繼續活著。他說起很久以前參與巡迴劇團的孩子，演員搭火車運道具，有時在學校房間而不是大建築裡演戲。瑟娜覺得自己更像那些演員，而不是畫裡有名的女王。大家都是用手上已有的東西來說故事。

At the desk, Bram paid one dollar for a copy of an old stage drawing. He put the receipt in a file so the team could check the money later. Wren showed them how to roll the cloth without making marks on it. “You must tell everyone else who uses this that it belongs to the museum,” he said. “A mistake is easier to fix when we say so at once.” Sena agreed. She had once tried to move a heavy chair alone and broken a small leg on it. She remembered how much harder the work became when she waited to speak. This time she asked Bram to help with every large object.

在櫃臺，布蘭花一美元影印了一份舊舞台圖，並把收據放進檔案，方便日後查帳。阿文教他們怎樣捲好彩繪布而不留下摺痕。「你們要告訴其他使用者，這是博物館的東西，」他說，「犯錯時立刻說出來，比較容易補救。」瑟娜同意。她曾獨自搬一張沉重椅子，弄斷一隻椅腳；拖延不說只讓事情更難處理。這一次，她請布蘭協助搬每一件大物品。

Back at school, Sena placed the borrowed cloth on a clean table and told the group how it must be handled. They made a practice roll with ordinary paper first. One child pulled too hard, and the paper tore. He looked worried, but Sena said a practice mistake was useful if it taught them what not to do with the real cloth. They tried again, this time with slower hands. Each child had a turn, and the result was smooth. Sena put the museum rules beside the box, where every helper could see them. She did not want a beautiful borrowed thing to become damaged because no one knew its story. Sena felt proud of their careful hands. Good care would allow the next group to enjoy it too.

回到學校，瑟娜把借來的布放在乾淨桌上，向大家說明如何處理。他們先拿普通紙做捲收練習。一個孩子拉得太大力，紙裂開了。他很擔心，瑟娜卻說練習時的錯誤有用，因為能教會大家正式的布該怎麼保護。他們慢慢再試，每個孩子輪流做一次，最後捲得很平整。瑟娜把博物館規則放在盒子旁，讓每個幫手看得見。她不希望漂亮的借用品因為沒人知道它的故事而受損。瑟娜為大家細心的雙手感到驕傲。好好照顧，下一組人才能繼續欣賞。

On the train home, a family asked about the large cloth roll. Sena explained the little crab story. A girl said, “I want to see the sea, but I cannot hear very well.” That was something Sena had not considered. “Then we should make the actions easy to see,” she answered, “and put the words on cards, too.” The girl's mother smiled. Sena wrote the idea in her notebook before she could forget it. A successful show was not simply one where the actors finished every line. It was one where the people watching could follow the story. The train reached Shell Bay, and Sena carried a more useful idea than any old crown.

回程火車上，一家人好奇地問起那卷大布。瑟娜說明小螃蟹的故事。一個女孩說：「我想看海，可是我的聽力不太好。」這是瑟娜之前沒想到的事。「那我們應該讓動作更容易看懂，」她回答，「也把台詞寫在卡片上。」女孩的媽媽微笑。瑟娜趁還沒忘記前把想法記進筆記本。成功的演出，不只是演員講完每句話，而是觀眾能跟上故事。火車抵達貝殼灣，瑟娜帶回的點子比任何舊皇冠都實用。
