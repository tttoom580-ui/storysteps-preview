沒有歌聲的聲音

A Voice Without a Song

潮汐劇場的安靜掌聲 · 第 9 / 25 章 · 1000以下 · 英中對照 · 校訂中

A bear named Hal came to the first acting meeting. He was a singer, and he could ring the school bell with one strong note. But he looked sad. “If we have no loud song, whose part will I play?” he asked. He had spent years learning to sing and did not want to lose that part of himself. Sena did not tell him that his worry was nothing. She asked him to keep the story clear with soft words and signs. Hal was brave enough to try. A farmer from another country, who was new to town and felt lonely, watched the test. She said she could understand the action even when she did not know every word.

熊哈爾來參加第一次表演聚會。他是一名歌手，聲音強而有力，甚至能像鐘一樣響亮。但他看起來很難過。「如果沒有大聲的歌，我可以演什麼角色？」他問。他學唱歌多年，不想失去那一部分的自己。瑟娜沒有說他的擔心沒什麼。她請他試著用輕柔的話和手勢讓故事更清楚。哈爾勇敢地試了。剛從另一個國家搬來的農夫在旁觀看，她在新鎮上有點孤單，卻說即使聽不懂所有字，也能理解動作。

The farmer told Hal that she had been a foreigner when she first came to this country. She could speak some of the local language but often missed jokes and songs. “When people use only words, I can feel outside the group,” she said. “When you move the paper fish slowly, I understand why the crab wants to help.” Hal looked at her in surprise. He had thought his biggest gift was his voice. Now he saw that he could also help with the shape of a story. He asked her which part she liked best. She pointed to the scene where the fish waited for the crab instead of leaving him behind. Hal promised to keep that pause in the play. It was a small choice, but it mattered to someone.

那位農夫告訴哈爾，自己剛到這個國家時曾是外國人。她會說一些當地語言，卻常常聽不懂笑話與歌。「如果人們只用話語，我會覺得被排除，」她說，「但你慢慢移動紙魚時，我懂螃蟹為什麼要幫忙。」哈爾很驚訝。他原以為自己的最大禮物是歌聲，現在才發現他也能用故事的形狀幫助別人。他問農夫最喜歡哪一段。農夫指向紙魚等著螃蟹、不把牠丟下的場景。哈爾答應保留那個停頓。那只是小小選擇，卻對某個人很重要。

During the break, everyone drank milk and ate warm bread. The farmer did not eat meat, so Bram had prepared food she could enjoy. Hal asked whether he looked foolish in the photograph they had taken. Sena showed him the picture and asked if he wished to keep it. “Only for our group, please,” he said. “I do not want it on a public wall yet.” Sena respected his answer. Beside the hall was a clean toilet and a warm waiting room, which made it easier for families to stay. She began to see that a theater included much more than the part with the lights. It also needed a place where people felt welcome and safe.

休息時，大家喝牛奶、吃溫麵包。那位農夫不吃肉，布蘭準備了她能吃的食物。哈爾問，剛拍的照片裡自己是不是看起來很笨。瑟娜拿給他看，問他願不願意留下。「先只給我們團隊看就好，」他說，「我還不想把它貼在公共牆上。」瑟娜尊重他的回答。大廳旁有乾淨廁所和溫暖的等候室，讓家庭可以待得舒服些。她開始明白，劇場不只是打了燈的舞台，還需要讓人安心、願意留下的空間。

Later, a young person brought a drawing of the fish and asked if the team could put it on the public wall. Sena asked the artist whether that was what she wanted. She said yes but wanted her name below it. “Then we will write your name,” Sena said. Hal remembered his own photograph and how glad he had been when Sena asked before sharing it. He told the artist that asking was a good habit for everyone. The wall soon held pictures by many different people, each marked the way its maker wanted. Sena looked at them and felt that the show was already bringing neighbors together. Even before the first performance, the town had begun to share its stories with more care.

後來，一位年輕人帶來魚的圖畫，問團隊能不能貼在公共牆上。瑟娜先問畫者本人是否願意。畫者說願意，但希望在下方寫上自己的名字。「那我們就署上妳的名字。」瑟娜說。哈爾想起自己的照片，當時瑟娜先問他的意願，他很感激。他告訴畫者，先詢問是每個人的好習慣。牆上很快貼滿不同人的作品，每張都依作者的意願標示。瑟娜望著它們，覺得這齣戲早已開始讓鄰居靠近。即使正式演出還沒開始，鎮上已學會更用心分享彼此的故事。

After practice, Hal asked to try a different role. He held up a blue paper fish and moved it slowly, while the others made waves with their hands. The farmer laughed and said she could feel the sea without hearing a loud song. “Well, that is a start,” Hal said. He still wanted to sing sometimes, and Sena told him he could sing at the school hall when it was right to do so. One quiet show was not the end of his music. Hal smiled for the first time that day. Sena noticed that giving someone a choice could be better than asking them to forget what they loved.

練習後，哈爾想試另一個角色。他拿起藍色紙魚慢慢移動，其他人用手比出海浪。那位農夫笑說，即使沒有大聲歌唱，也感覺得到海洋。「嗯，這算是個開始。」哈爾說。他仍想偶爾唱歌，瑟娜告訴他，適合的時候仍能在學校禮堂唱。這場安靜的戲不代表他的音樂結束。那天哈爾第一次露出笑容。瑟娜發現，給別人選擇，往往比要求他們忘記熱愛的事更好。
