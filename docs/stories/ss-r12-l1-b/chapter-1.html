海邊的小舞台

A Stage by the Sea

At the north side of Shell Bay, an old wooden stage stood beside the sea. A red curtain hung over its door, but no one had used it for years. Sena, a young otter woman, went there with a camera to take pictures for the town picnic. Her pet snail, Pebble, sat in a safe glass box at home; she never took him near salt water. A little bug crossed the steps. Sena bent to notice it, and then saw a deep crack in the middle board. A friendly badger named Bram came over with apple juice. “This is not a safe place for a show,” he said. Sena did not lie. “That is a fact,” she answered. “But perhaps we can make it safe.”

在貝殼灣北邊，一座老木舞台靠著海。門口掛著紅布幕，卻已多年沒人使用。年輕的水獺女子瑟娜帶著相機來拍照，準備鎮上的野餐活動。她的寵物蝸牛小石安全地待在家裡的玻璃箱中；她從不帶牠靠近海水。一隻小蟲爬過台階。瑟娜彎腰注意牠，接著發現舞台中央的一塊木板有深深的裂縫。友善的獾布蘭拿著蘋果汁走來。「這裡不適合安全地演出。」他說。瑟娜沒有說謊。「那是事實，」她回答，「但也許我們能把它修好。」

On her way back from the picnic, Sena visited the stage once more, staying behind the gate. In the middle of the old curtain, a little hole let the daylight through. She pictured a crab moving across the boards and a fish made from paper dancing above the sea. Then she looked down and saw the chalk line around the crack. The day had given her two different pictures: one of what the stage might become, and another of what it was now. Bram had walked with her because he wanted the same dream, but he was not ready to risk a child's safety for it. They agreed that the most useful first step would be to ask good questions instead of bringing paint and a hammer too soon.

野餐回程，瑟娜再次到舞台，停在門外。老布幕中央有個小洞，日光從中透進來。她想像螃蟹走過木板、紙魚在海上方跳舞。接著她低頭，看見裂縫旁的粉筆線。這一天給了她兩幅不同的畫面：一幅是舞台可能成為的樣子，另一幅是它目前真正的樣子。布蘭陪她走來，因為他也有相同夢想，但他不願為了夢想讓孩子冒險。他們同意第一步應該先好好提問，而不是太早帶著油漆和鎚子來。

The town had another stage in a school hall, but it was small and far from the beach. Every summer, children asked why the sea stage stayed closed. Sena had asked the same question when she was little. She wanted a future in which everyone could watch a show while hearing the waves. A star was visible in the blue morning sky only in an old painting on the wall; today the sky was bright and empty. Bram pointed at the cracked board again. “Dreams are good. A broken floor is still a broken floor.” Sena agreed to use chalk to mark the bad part, so that anybody walking near it would stop. They did not step on the board even once.

鎮上在學校禮堂還有另一個舞台，但那裡很小，也離海灘很遠。每年夏天，孩子們都問海邊舞台為什麼一直關閉。瑟娜小時候也問過同樣的問題。她希望將來大家能邊聽海浪邊看表演。牆上一幅舊畫裡，藍色晨空還有一顆星；今天真正的天空明亮而空曠。布蘭再次指著裂開的木板。「夢想很好，但壞掉的地板就是壞掉的地板。」瑟娜同意用粉筆標出危險區域，讓走近的人停下。兩人一次也沒有踩上那塊木板。

That evening, Sena told her neighbors why the gate must remain shut. A little child said she had heard a story about a person who could jump over the broken boards. “Maybe that person can,” Sena answered, “but a public stage is for people who walk, run, roll, and rest in many different ways.” She asked the child to imagine a grandparent arriving slowly with a walking stick. The child became thoughtful and moved her toy chair away from the edge of a step. Sena was pleased by that small change. There was no need to frighten anyone. A clear example could show why a rule existed. Before sleeping, she put her camera beside the notes and wondered whether the future would bring a new curtain or a new kind of show.

傍晚，瑟娜向鄰居說明為何大門必須保持關閉。一個孩子說她聽過有人能跳過裂開木板的故事。「也許那個人能，」瑟娜回答，「但公共舞台是給用不同方式走路、奔跑、推輪椅和休息的人使用的。」她請孩子想像祖父母拄著手杖慢慢走來。孩子沉思片刻，把玩具椅從台階邊移開。瑟娜很高興看到這個小變化。不需要嚇唬誰，清楚的例子就能說明規則的原因。睡前她把相機放在筆記旁，想著未來會帶來新布幕，還是全新的表演方式。

At the picnic that afternoon, Sena asked the other animals for their ideas. Some wanted a song, some wanted a play, and a child wanted to dance. They all seemed excited, but Mrs. Fern, who looked after the town buildings, spoke carefully. “You may not reach the stage until an engineer checks it,” she said. “You also need a plan for the tide and a quiet time for birds that live by the rocks.” Sena wrote the rules on a large sheet and read them aloud. She did not promise a show yet. Instead, she asked for one week to learn what was possible. Bram offered to help with the first visit. The picnic ended with a question, not a promise.

那天下午的野餐上，瑟娜詢問其他動物的想法。有些想唱歌，有些想演戲，一個孩子則想跳舞。大家都很興奮，但負責管理鎮上建築的芬恩太太謹慎地開口：「工程師檢查之前，你們不能走上舞台。還要有潮水的計畫，以及讓岩石邊的鳥兒安靜休息的時間。」瑟娜把規則寫在大紙上，念給大家聽。她沒有立刻承諾表演，只請求一週時間研究可能的辦法。布蘭願意幫忙第一次勘查。野餐在一個問題中結束，而不是在承諾中結束。
