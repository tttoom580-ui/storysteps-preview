第一堂課

The First Class

潮汐劇場的安靜掌聲 · 第 7 / 25 章 · 1000以下 · 英中對照 · 校訂中

用互動版查詞、朗讀與收藏

The school opened a class by the west garden on Saturday. Sena put a large list of tasks on the wall. The main job was to collect ideas for a quiet play, but she also wanted to know who liked painting, acting, or moving chairs. A rabbit named Lila brought chocolate for everyone and a note from her mother, a housewife who could sew simple cloth animals. “We can start with flowers,” Lila said. “There is a flower by the stage that opens every morning.” Sena let the children talk for one hour, then asked them to choose one real story. They chose a small crab who wanted to find a safe home without pushing any other animal away.

星期六，學校在西邊花園開了一堂課。瑟娜在牆上貼出大大的工作清單。主要工作是收集安靜戲劇的點子，但她也想知道誰喜歡畫畫、演戲或搬椅子。兔子莉拉帶了巧克力給大家，還有媽媽的紙條；她媽媽是家庭主婦，會縫簡單的布動物。「我們可以從花開始，」莉拉說，「舞台旁有一朵花每天早上都會開。」瑟娜讓孩子們談了一小時，再請他們選一個真實可理解的故事。他們選了小螃蟹尋找安全住處，又不想把其他動物趕走的故事。

After the class, Sena asked each child what part had been most fun. One liked the paper seats, another liked the painted crab, and a third liked helping someone who had hidden a model. The answers were different, but the children all wanted to return. Bram made a simple list of supplies for the next class. He would bring paper, tape, and a box to keep the models dry. Lila suggested that the club collect flowers for a pretty table, but Sena explained that they should not pick flowers from the birds' quiet area. They could draw flowers instead. The children agreed and began making bright paper plants. A rule did not need to end an idea. It could help them find a kinder version of it.

下課後，瑟娜問每個孩子哪個部分最好玩。有人喜歡紙座椅，有人喜歡彩繪螃蟹，也有人喜歡幫助藏起模型的同學。答案各不相同，但大家都想再來。布蘭列出下堂課的簡單用品表，會帶紙張、膠帶和防潮盒。莉拉提議採花裝飾桌子，瑟娜解釋不能去鳥兒的安靜區摘花，可以改畫花。孩子們同意，開始做明亮的紙植物。規則不一定要終止點子，也可以幫忙找到更體貼的做法。

Bram brought wood samples from the farm and set them on a table. “Of course we cannot work on the stage until the test is done,” he reminded the class. “But we can make paper models here.” The children stood around him, and each built a tiny seat. Some seats were too narrow for a toy with wheels, so they made wider ones. Lila asked if a popular song would bring more people. Sena said a song could be fun, but it would need to fit the quiet hours near the birds. “Let us make a show that works for the place we have,” she said. “There is no need to copy every big theater.”

布蘭從農場帶來木材樣本放在桌上。「當然，測試完成前我們不能上台施工，」他提醒班上，「但可以在這裡做紙模型。」孩子們圍著他，每個人做了一張小座椅。有些座椅太窄，帶輪子的玩具坐不進去，他們便改做更寬的。莉拉問，流行歌曲能否吸引更多觀眾。瑟娜說歌曲可以很好玩，但必須符合鳥兒附近的安靜時段。「讓我們為現有的地方創作適合的戲吧，」她說，「不需要模仿每一座大劇院。」

That night, Lila's mother called to say the cloth animals were ready. She had sewn a crab and a fish from clean pieces of old fabric. Sena asked if she could bring them to school for the children to see. “Yes, but let us wait until after my work tomorrow,” the mother said. “I need the morning for my family.” Sena wrote down the time and thanked her. It had become easy to see the helpers only as people who could give more time. She wanted to remember that they had homes and plans of their own. At the next class, she would begin by asking what help was possible, not by telling everyone what they must do. That would be a better start for a true club.

那晚，莉拉的媽媽打電話說布動物已經縫好。她用乾淨的舊布縫了一隻螃蟹和一條魚。瑟娜問能不能帶去學校給孩子看。「可以，但等我明天工作結束後，」媽媽說，「早上我要照顧家人。」瑟娜記下時間並道謝。她發現自己很容易只把幫手看成能再付出時間的人，卻忘記大家也有各自的家與計畫。下次上課，她要先問別人能幫什麼，而不是告訴每個人必須做什麼。真正的社團應該這樣開始。

Near the end of class, a small boy hid his model under the table. He thought it was not good enough. Sena asked him to bring it out only if he wished. After a while he showed a simple ramp made of folded paper. It was the best path in the room. Bram tested it with a toy chair and made a mark on the plan. The boy was proud, and the class began to cheer. Sena asked them to use soft hands instead of loud voices, so they waved in the air. It was their first quiet cheer. The children went home knowing that a good idea could come from any member, not only the loudest one.

下課前，一個小男孩把模型藏在桌下，覺得自己做得不夠好。瑟娜說，只要他願意再拿出來就好。過了一會兒，他拿出用摺紙做的簡單斜坡。那竟是教室裡最好走的通道。布蘭用玩具輪椅測試，在計畫上做了記號。男孩很自豪，全班開始歡呼。瑟娜請大家用雙手代替大聲叫喊，於是孩子們在空中揮手。這是他們第一次安靜的歡呼。大家回家時都知道，好點子可能來自任何成員，不只是聲音最大的人。
