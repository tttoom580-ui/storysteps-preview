小小的預算

A Small Budget

潮汐劇場的安靜掌聲 · 第 5 / 25 章 · 1000以下 · 英中對照 · 校訂中

The town bank could not give the team all the money they wanted. Sena and Bram made a list of simple things they could sell at a school fair: painted stones, paper hats, and small cards. A teenager named Yuki brought a yellow box to hold the money. Her family lived on a farm and would feed the helpers when they worked. “A warm meal is better than a long speech,” she said. Bram smiled, but asked her to check the plan with her family first. That night, the moon rose over the sea, and the group watched a short movie about safe wooden floors. A line in the film said that every strong thing begins with careful work. Sena wrote it down as a topic for their next talk.

鎮上的銀行無法提供團隊想要的全部經費。瑟娜和布蘭列出幾樣能在學校市集販售的小東西：彩繪石頭、紙帽和小卡片。青少年悠希帶來一個黃色盒子裝錢。她家住在農場，願意在大家施工時提供食物。「一頓熱餐比長篇演說更有用。」她說。布蘭笑了，但請她先和家人確認。那晚，月亮升上海面，大家看了一部談安全木地板的短片。片中說，每件堅固的事都從仔細的工作開始。瑟娜把這句話記下，當成下次討論的題目。

On the following day, the school set out a small table for the fund. Every family could see how much money had come in and what the team planned to buy. Yuki counted the coins twice and wrote the number in a large book. “Many small gifts can make one useful thing,” she said. Bram agreed, but reminded her that a gift of time was also important. The farmer who lent tools had saved them from buying new ones, and the teacher who opened a classroom had given them a free place to practice. Those things did not appear as money in the yellow box. Sena added a second list of help beside the budget. She wanted the team to remember the people, not just the coins.

隔天，學校擺出一張募款小桌。每個家庭都能看見收了多少錢，以及團隊打算買什麼。悠希把硬幣數了兩遍，再把金額寫進大本子。「很多小禮物能合成一件有用的事。」她說。布蘭同意，但提醒她時間的付出也很重要。借工具的農夫讓大家不用買新工具，開放教室的老師也提供免費排練場所。這些幫助不會以錢的形式出現在黃色盒子裡。瑟娜在預算旁加了第二份幫助清單。她希望團隊記得的是人，不只是硬幣。

At the farm, a goat showed Sena how to put a thin string across two sticks to make a straight line. She tried to walk along it, but a stone touched her toe, so she moved slowly. “Where does this line go?” she asked. “Through the middle of the model path,” the goat answered. “Think of it as the place where wheels will move.” A strange sound came from the barn. It was only a door moving in the wind, yet it made everyone laugh. Bram checked the bottle of oil for the old tools and said it should stay in a locked box, away from children. The team could borrow tools, but only an adult worker would use them.

在農場裡，一隻山羊教瑟娜把細繩拉在兩根木棒之間，做成筆直的線。她試著沿線走，但石頭碰到腳趾，所以放慢腳步。「這條線通去哪裡？」她問。「穿過模型通道的中央，」山羊回答，「把它想成輪子要走的地方。」穀倉傳來奇怪的聲音，原來只是門被風吹動，大家都笑了。布蘭查看舊工具用的油瓶，說要鎖進箱子，遠離孩子。團隊可以借工具，但只能由成年工人操作。

That evening, Sena walked slowly past the market with Bram. They saw a family choosing food and a child holding a little paper fish from the class. The child asked whether the play would be free. Sena said the team hoped to welcome everyone without selling tickets, but the plan still needed approval. She did not want to promise something before the numbers were final. Bram pointed to the farm road, which went straight toward the hill. It would be easy for adult helpers to bring safe materials along that way. “We know where the work starts,” he said. “We know who is helping. Now we need patience.” Sena nodded. They chose a slow walk home so the budget pages would stay safe. The idea had changed from a wish into a plan that could be checked.

那晚，瑟娜和布蘭慢慢走過市集。他們看見一家人在挑食物，一個孩子拿著課堂上做的小紙魚。孩子問演出是不是免費。瑟娜說團隊希望不賣票、歡迎每個人，但計畫還需要核准。她不想在數字確定前隨便答應。布蘭指向筆直通往山坡的農場道路，那條路方便成年幫手運來安全材料。「我們知道工作從哪裡開始，」他說，「也知道誰在幫忙，接下來需要耐心。」瑟娜點頭。他們慢慢走回家，保護好預算紙張。這個想法已經從願望變成可以檢查的計畫。

When Sena reached home, a letter from the council was waiting. It said the town could offer a little wood if the team showed a safe design and kept a clear record of every sale. The team could not take money from the bank without a plan, but it could work toward a small goal. Sena read the letter again under the yellow kitchen light. Bram called to say that four families had offered food, and the school had agreed to lend tables. The work still looked large. Yet it now had a budget, a place to start, and people who wished to help. Sena folded the letter and put it beside the stage drawing, ready for the next morning.

瑟娜回家時，議會寄來的信已經到了。信上說，只要團隊提出安全設計，並清楚記錄每筆販售，鎮上就能提供一小部分木材。沒有計畫，團隊不能隨便向銀行借錢；但他們可以朝一個小目標努力。瑟娜在廚房黃燈下又讀了一遍信。布蘭打電話來說，四個家庭願意提供食物，學校也同意借桌子。工作依然龐大，但現在已有預算、起點和願意幫忙的人。瑟娜把信摺好，放在舞台圖旁，準備迎接隔天。
