日落時的測試

The Test at Sunset

The team stood around the school stage in the evening. Outside, the light grew dark and a cool wind came from the left side of the road. Lila pressed a button to start the sound machine. A soft wave sound filled the room. “That is interesting,” Bram said, “but we usually do not have machines on the sea stage.” Sena asked everyone to watch the actors without any sound at all. Hal held a painted rose, and the others showed the little crab looking for a home. It was sweet and simple. When a paper wall began to fall, Hal caught it and stayed still until Bram could help. No one was hurt, but the trouble showed that the wall needed a stronger base.

傍晚，團隊圍在學校舞台旁。外面漸漸變暗，涼風吹向道路左側。莉拉按下按鈕，啟動音效機，輕柔的海浪聲充滿室內。「很有意思，」布蘭說，「但海邊舞台通常沒有這些機器。」瑟娜請大家完全不放聲音，只看演員表演。哈爾拿著畫好的玫瑰，其他人表演小螃蟹尋找家的故事。場景甜美而簡單。一面紙牆快倒時，哈爾接住它，站著不動直到布蘭來幫忙。沒人受傷，但這次小麻煩顯示牆的底座需要加強。

When the paper wall was fixed, the actors tried the scene three more times. The first time, the crab moved too quickly and the fish could not follow. The second time, a sign fell behind a chair. The third time, the scene was clear from every corner of the hall. Sena asked the children how they knew it worked. They said they could tell who wanted help and who was giving it, even without a word. That answer pleased her more than loud praise. Bram wrote down the good changes so that the actors would not forget them. “We should stay with the version that works,” he said. “We can try new ideas later, but we need one clear story for opening day.” Everyone agreed. The actors shared a small cheer.

紙牆修好後，演員把那場戲又排了三遍。第一次，螃蟹動作太快，魚跟不上；第二次，字卡掉到椅子後面；第三次，從大廳每個角落都能看懂。瑟娜問孩子們怎麼知道成功了。孩子們說，即使沒有一句話，也看得出誰需要幫忙、誰在伸出援手。這回答比大聲稱讚更讓她高興。布蘭把有效的修改記下，免得演員忘記。「我們先留著這個有效的版本，」他說，「新點子以後還能試，但開幕日需要一個清楚的故事。」大家同意。演員們輕輕歡呼。

During the break, a student opened a box of potato cakes and showed the team a menu for the fair. Sena thanked her, but asked people not to eat near the painted cloth. A few drops could spoil the museum's gift. Hal gave a small smile when he saw how carefully everyone moved. “We are learning to listen with our eyes,” he said. Bram asked if the dark evening would bring a better crowd. Sena reminded him of the birds and the tide. “A large crowd is not worth worry or danger,” she answered. “We have one safe time in the afternoon. That is the time we should work toward.”

休息時，一位學生打開裝著馬鈴薯餅的盒子，給大家看市集的菜單。瑟娜感謝她，但請大家不要在彩繪布旁吃東西。只要滴上幾滴食物，就可能毀了博物館借出的布。哈爾看見大家小心移動，露出微笑。「我們正在學用眼睛聽。」他說。布蘭問，傍晚是否能吸引更多人。瑟娜提醒他鳥兒和潮水的事。「人多不值得冒險，」她回答，「我們有一個安全的下午時段，就朝那個時間準備吧。」

Before leaving, Hal asked if the cast could sit in the audience seats and watch the model together. They did so, moving from the front row to the back. A sign that looked large from the stage seemed small from the last chair. A slow hand movement seemed beautiful from one side and confusing from the other. The children began to make small changes without waiting for Sena to tell them what to do. Some lifted their signs, while others changed the place where they stood. Sena noticed the care they took. The rehearsal had become more than learning lines. It was learning how to see the play through someone else's eyes. That was an important part of making room for everyone.

離開前，哈爾問全體能否坐到觀眾席，一起看模型。他們從前排坐到後排。舞台上看來很大的字卡，從最後一張椅子看就很小；一邊看來優美的慢動作，另一邊卻可能不清楚。孩子們不再等瑟娜指示，就開始做小修改。有人把牌子舉高，有人調整站位。瑟娜注意到他們的用心。排練不只是背台詞，也是學會從別人的眼睛看戲。這是為每個人留出空間的重要部分。

At home, Sena wrote down every sound the group had tested. She drew a small rose beside the scene where the crab helped a lost fish. She had to admit that a quiet play needed more practice than she expected. A hand moved too fast could make a sentence hard to read, and a picture held too low could disappear behind a chair. But no one wanted to quit. Bram sent a message: “The paper wall is fixed, and Hal wants to try again.” Sena read it twice. The stage was not ready, but the group was learning how to work together. She closed her notebook, listened to the real waves, and rested before the next day's test.

回到家後，瑟娜記下團隊測試過的每一種聲音。在小螃蟹幫助迷路小魚的那場戲旁，她畫了一朵小玫瑰。她不得不承認，安靜的戲需要比想像中更多練習。手勢太快會讓句子難懂，圖片舉得太低則會被椅子擋住。但沒有人想放棄。布蘭傳來訊息：「紙牆修好了，哈爾還想再試一次。」瑟娜讀了兩遍。舞台還沒準備好，但團隊正在學習如何合作。她闔上筆記本，聽著真正的浪聲，為明天的測試休息。
