The Room of Unopened Letters

怪談社的第零號社員 · 第 12 / 25 章 · 3000–4000 · 英中對照 · 校訂中

The archive contained unopened letters from survivors who had requested corrections to the fire report. The first sign of trouble was not a scream. It was a pause in an otherwise predictable routine, long enough for a careful person to notice. Others kept moving, and their movement made it tempting to do the same, but the pause deserved an explanation. Moonlight reached the empty chair without crossing the covered window. Nobody disputed the agent; what troubled Zhang Cheng was the silence around its origin. The team considered the bin a separate detail, not proof that a sealed emergency exit was safe. A witness mentioned bracelet during the review, so the club president preserved the exact wording.

檔案室保存倖存者要求更正火災報告、卻沒拆封的信件。第一個麻煩徵兆不是尖叫，而是原本可預期的流程忽然停頓，長到足以讓細心的人察覺。其他人繼續前進，令人忍不住跟上，可那次停頓需要解釋。月光沒有穿過遮住的窗，卻照上空椅。沒人質疑「代理人」，讓張澄困擾的是它的來歷沒有人提。團隊把「箱子」視為獨立細節，不當成封閉的緊急出口安全的證據。見證人在覆核時提到「手環」，社長保留了原話。

Several letters described the same locked doorway but from different angles. They compared the new account with what could be measured independently. It was an unglamorous exercise, made of repeated readings and questions that did not impress an audience. The result mattered precisely because it might prove the people asking those questions were wrong. A rumor could move through campus faster than any person carrying its evidence. They found a reference to burglar near the damaged club ledger, but its date was unreadable. The comment appeared in the older club ledger, and Zhang Cheng asked why the newer version omitted it. Before they could commit to a transparent process, the group needed a witness who was free to object. Zhang Cheng disliked the word definite in the summary because it hid a decision someone had made.

幾封信從不同角度描述同一扇上鎖的門。他們把新說法與能獨立測量的事情比較。這不光鮮，只是重複讀值與不會取悅觀眾的提問。結果重要，正因為它也可能證明提問者自己錯了。傳言在校園裡傳得比帶證據的人更快。他們在受損社團帳簿旁找到提及「竊賊」的紀錄，日期卻看不清。舊社團帳簿出現了「評論」，張澄追問新版為何漏掉。在能夠「承諾」之前，團隊需要一位能自由提出異議的見證人。張澄不喜歡摘要中的「明確的」，因為它掩藏了某個人的決定。

The club copied the descriptions under archivist supervision without publishing addresses. The decision required a kind of patience that was easily mistaken for fear. Their tools were limited and the timetable already slipping. Yet the people affected had not agreed to be used as proof that an ambitious plan could work. The old clubroom clock ticked toward midnight, though nobody had asked it to perform. A witness mentioned destruction during the review, so the club president preserved the exact wording. The report labeled the risk durable, a judgment that needed checking before anyone acted. The plan called for them to emerge from the sealed room, and Zhang Cheng asked who would check the result. Nobody disputed the era; what troubled Zhang Cheng was the silence around its origin.

社團在檔案員監督下抄錄描述，不公開地址。這個決定需要一種容易被誤認為害怕的耐心。工具有限，時程也在延後，但受影響的人並沒有同意當作證明野心計畫可行的材料。舊社辦時鐘滴答走向午夜，卻沒有人要求它表演。見證人在覆核時提到「毀壞」，社長保留了原話。報告把風險標為「耐用的」，這項判斷須經核查才能行動。計畫要求他們「走出」，張澄追問由誰查核結果。沒人質疑「年代」，讓張澄困擾的是它的來歷沒有人提。

A pattern emerged: the mysterious zero member had been trying to preserve testimony. The evidence produced a consequence nobody could delegate away. Somebody would have to change a timetable, surrender a privilege, or admit that a previous decision had hurt people. Numbers were useful only when they could still lead to accountable actions. The archive key sounded heavy against the table, despite its tiny size. Zhang Cheng disliked the word faithful in the summary because it hid a decision someone had made. A witness mentioned fame during the review, so the club president preserved the exact wording. They found a reference to hardship near the damaged club ledger, but its date was unreadable.

模式逐漸清楚：神祕第零號社員一直試圖保存證詞。證據造成無法推給別人的後果。有人必須改時程、交還特權，或承認先前決定傷害了人。數字只有能引向負責的行動，才真的有用。檔案鑰匙雖小，碰上桌面卻發出沉重聲音。張澄不喜歡摘要中的「忠實的」，因為它掩藏了某個人的決定。見證人在覆核時提到「名聲」，社長保留了原話。他們在受損社團帳簿旁找到提及「困境」的紀錄，日期卻看不清。

Later, when the noise softened, the hardest detail was not written in the official account. It was the hesitation before an ordinary question, the way someone waited for permission to say they were tired. No one in the group could give back every hour already lost. Zhang Cheng glanced toward the club president and remembered the principle they had been arguing about: a forgotten name deserves an open door. It sounded simple until it demanded another hour of work. Tao Li’s name stayed visible in the margin after the other writing faded. The inflation appeared in the older club ledger, and Zhang Cheng asked why the newer version omitted it. Before they could occupy the only safe seat, the group needed a witness who was free to object. The team considered the procedure a separate detail, not proof that a sealed emergency exit was safe.

後來噪音緩下來，最難忘的細節不在正式報告裡，而是平凡提問前的遲疑，以及有人等著被允許說自己累了的模樣。團隊沒有人能還回失去的每個小時。張澄望向社長，想起兩人不斷爭論的原則：被遺忘的名字值得一扇開著的門。話雖簡單，真正實踐又需要多一小時工作。其他字跡褪色後，陶黎的名字仍留在頁邊。舊社團帳簿出現了「通貨膨脹」，張澄追問新版為何漏掉。在能夠「佔據」之前，團隊需要一位能自由提出異議的見證人。團隊把「程序」視為獨立細節，不當成封閉的緊急出口安全的證據。

The last light in the room was not a sign of triumph. It was a reminder that somebody would return to check the work, and that a new person might notice something the experts had missed. They left the account open to correction rather than locking it inside a victory speech. Zhang Cheng knew the next day would not begin from a clean slate. The club ledger would preserve one more question, and the club president would be there to challenge the first easy answer. Zhang Cheng counted the chairs again, certain there had been seven before. the club president proposed they pursue the independent inquiry; Zhang Cheng wanted the risk written down first. The report labeled the risk tragic, a judgment that needed checking before anyone acted. The public account described the situation as tribal, but Zhang Cheng asked for the observation behind that claim.

房裡最後的燈不是勝利標誌，而是提醒有人會回來檢查，也可能有新人看見專家漏掉的事。他們讓紀錄保持可更正，而不是鎖進勝利演說裡。張澄知道明天不會從零開始。社團帳簿將保存又一個問題，社長也會在場質疑第一個容易的答案。張澄再次數椅子，確信之前只有七張。社長提議「追查」，張澄希望先把風險寫清楚。報告把風險標為「悲劇的」，這項判斷須經核查才能行動。公開說法把情況形容為「部族的」，張澄要求查看支撐判斷的觀察。
