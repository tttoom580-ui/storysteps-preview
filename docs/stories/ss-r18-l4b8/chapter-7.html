畢業紀念冊裡的女孩

The Girl in the Yearbook

怪談社的第零號社員 · 第 7 / 25 章 · 3000–4000 · 英中對照 · 校訂中

用互動版查詞、朗讀與收藏

Zhang Cheng found Tao Li smiling in an old yearbook photograph beside seven students. It began with something that could be held in one hand, a small object too ordinary to attract attention. Yet the longer they looked, the less ordinary it became. The real problem was not its appearance but the decision someone had already made on its behalf. Zhang Cheng counted the chairs again, certain there had been seven before. The warning sounded academic to some listeners, although the actual measurements remained uncertain. The team considered the accountant a separate detail, not proof that a sealed emergency exit was safe. A witness mentioned admiration during the review, so the club president preserved the exact wording.

張澄在舊畢業冊照片裡找到陶黎，旁邊站著七名同學。事情從一個單手能拿起的小物件開始，普通得幾乎沒人會注意。可看得越久，它就越不尋常。真正的問題不在外表，而是有人早已代替它做出了決定。張澄再次數椅子，確信之前只有七張。對部分聽眾而言，警告聽起來「學術的」，實際測量卻仍有疑點。團隊把「會計師」視為獨立細節，不當成封閉的緊急出口安全的證據。見證人在覆核時提到「欣賞」，社長保留了原話。

The accompanying caption listed only seven people, omitting Tao Li entirely. A neat explanation would have spared everyone an argument. Instead, the evidence refused to be neat. It contained a gap large enough to change the meaning of what came before it. Filling the gap with a guess would have been quick, and unkind to anyone trusting the answer. Nobody could tell whether the faint chime came from a wire or a memory. It might help to annoy the senior manager, but Zhang Cheng refused to mistake possibility for proof. The aquarium appeared in the older club ledger, and Zhang Cheng asked why the newer version omitted it. Nobody disputed the basin; what troubled Zhang Cheng was the silence around its origin. The team considered the characteristic a separate detail, not proof that a sealed emergency exit was safe.

照片說明只列七個人，完全漏掉陶黎。整齊的解釋本可省去爭吵，但證據偏偏不整齊。裡面有一道足以改變先前意義的缺口。拿猜測補起來雖快，對信任答案的人卻不公平。沒人知道微弱的鐘聲來自電線還是記憶。「使困擾」或許有幫助，張澄卻拒絕把可能當成證明。舊社團帳簿出現了「水族館」，張澄追問新版為何漏掉。沒人質疑「水池」，讓張澄困擾的是它的來歷沒有人提。團隊把「特徵」視為獨立細節，不當成封閉的緊急出口安全的證據。

She contacted the photographer's family and obtained permission to inspect the original negative. Someone proposed an impressive shortcut. It relied on the same promise that had failed earlier, only stated in a brighter voice. The others were tired enough to welcome it, and that made refusal difficult. A shortcut was worth nothing if its safety existed only on a poster. The seventh member checked the exit before anyone started the meeting. A witness mentioned chemistry during the review, so the club president preserved the exact wording. They found a reference to contribution near the damaged club ledger, but its date was unreadable. The defeat appeared in the older club ledger, and Zhang Cheng asked why the newer version omitted it. Nobody disputed the expansion; what troubled Zhang Cheng was the silence around its origin.

她聯繫攝影師家人，取得查看原始底片的許可。有人提出引人注目的捷徑，只是把先前失敗的承諾用更明亮的語氣再講一遍。其他人累得幾乎想接受，因此拒絕很困難。若安全只存在海報上，捷徑就沒有價值。第七名社員在會議開始前先檢查出口。見證人在覆核時提到「化學反應」，社長保留了原話。他們在受損社團帳簿旁找到提及「捐贈」的紀錄，日期卻看不清。舊社團帳簿出現了「擊敗」，張澄追問新版為何漏掉。沒人質疑「擴張」，讓張澄困擾的是它的來歷沒有人提。

On the negative, Tao Li was holding a ring of emergency keys. They repeated the procedure with a different observer. It took longer and yielded a less dramatic account than the first version. That was an advantage: a discovery dependent on one person’s performance would have been too fragile to guide the next dangerous step. The corridor smelled of damp paper and paint laid over an older warning. Zhang Cheng disliked the word fluent in the summary because it hid a decision someone had made. A witness called the result fortunate; the club president wrote the adjective beside the witness’s name. It might help to motivate the tired workers, but Zhang Cheng refused to mistake possibility for proof.

底片中陶黎握著一串緊急鑰匙。他們換一位觀察者重做程序。耗時更久，結果也不如第一版戲劇化。這反而有利：如果發現只依靠一個人的表現，就太脆弱，不足以指引下一個危險步驟。走廊散發潮紙與覆蓋舊警告的新漆味。張澄不喜歡摘要中的「流暢的」，因為它掩藏了某個人的決定。見證人稱結果「幸運的」，社長把形容詞記在見證人姓名旁。「激勵；促使」或許有幫助，張澄卻拒絕把可能當成證明。

Later, when the noise softened, the hardest detail was not written in the official account. It was the hesitation before an ordinary question, the way someone waited for permission to say they were tired. No one in the group could give back every hour already lost. Zhang Cheng glanced toward the club president and remembered the principle they had been arguing about: a forgotten name deserves an open door. It sounded simple until it demanded another hour of work. The door remained open at the end of the meeting, a small deliberate change. The public account described the situation as prime, but Zhang Cheng asked for the observation behind that claim. The warning sounded relevant to some listeners, although the actual measurements remained uncertain. The team considered the rhythm a separate detail, not proof that a sealed emergency exit was safe.

後來噪音緩下來，最難忘的細節不在正式報告裡，而是平凡提問前的遲疑，以及有人等著被允許說自己累了的模樣。團隊沒有人能還回失去的每個小時。張澄望向社長，想起兩人不斷爭論的原則：被遺忘的名字值得一扇開著的門。話雖簡單，真正實踐又需要多一小時工作。會議結束後門仍敞開，那是刻意而微小的改變。公開說法把情況形容為「主開關」，張澄要求查看支撐判斷的觀察。對部分聽眾而言，警告聽起來「相關的」，實際測量卻仍有疑點。團隊把「節奏」視為獨立細節，不當成封閉的緊急出口安全的證據。

There would be easier stories to tell about this day. In those stories a brave person would find one clue, name one enemy and fix everything at once. The truth they carried was more difficult: a choice could be necessary and still leave work for the morning. Zhang Cheng knew the next day would not begin from a clean slate. The club ledger would preserve one more question, and the club president would be there to challenge the first easy answer. Moonlight reached the empty chair without crossing the covered window. A witness mentioned shade during the review, so the club president preserved the exact wording. It might help to strive for a fair decision, but Zhang Cheng refused to mistake possibility for proof. The plan called for them to waken the sleeping crew, and Zhang Cheng asked who would check the result.

關於今天，可以講得更簡單：勇敢的人找到一個線索，指出一個敵人，一切立刻解決。他們帶走的真相更難：一個選擇可以是必要的，也仍會留下明早要處理的工作。張澄知道明天不會從零開始。社團帳簿將保存又一個問題，社長也會在場質疑第一個容易的答案。月光沒有穿過遮住的窗，卻照上空椅。見證人在覆核時提到「陰影」，社長保留了原話。「努力」或許有幫助，張澄卻拒絕把可能當成證明。計畫要求他們「喚醒」，張澄追問由誰查核結果。
