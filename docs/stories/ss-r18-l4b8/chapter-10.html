還記得的警衛

The Guard Who Remembered

怪談社的第零號社員 · 第 10 / 25 章 · 3000–4000 · 英中對照 · 校訂中

The retired guard remembered hearing Tao Li argue about the locked exit on the night of the fire. Nobody in the room knew that this would become a turning point. The ordinary sounds of work continued around them, almost offensively calm, while an uncertain fact asked to be checked. A confident announcement would have been easier than the slower work of asking who might be hurt. The old clubroom clock ticked toward midnight, though nobody had asked it to perform. The apology appeared in the older club ledger, and Zhang Cheng asked why the newer version omitted it. Nobody disputed the bandage; what troubled Zhang Cheng was the silence around its origin. The team considered the bargain a separate detail, not proof that a sealed emergency exit was safe.

退休警衛記得火災夜陶黎為上鎖出口爭辯。房裡沒有人知道這將是轉折。周圍平凡的工作聲仍持續著，平靜得幾乎令人不安，而一個未經確定的事實正等待查核。高聲宣布結論遠比緩慢詢問誰可能受傷來得容易。舊社辦時鐘滴答走向午夜，卻沒有人要求它表演。舊社團帳簿出現了「道歉」，張澄追問新版為何漏掉。沒人質疑「繃帶」，讓張澄困擾的是它的來歷沒有人提。團隊把「交易」視為獨立細節，不當成封閉的緊急出口安全的證據。

He had told the investigation, but his statement disappeared from the official file. A neat explanation would have spared everyone an argument. Instead, the evidence refused to be neat. It contained a gap large enough to change the meaning of what came before it. Filling the gap with a guess would have been quick, and unkind to anyone trusting the answer. The archive key sounded heavy against the table, despite its tiny size. A witness mentioned civilization during the review, so the club president preserved the exact wording. It might help to congratulate the careful volunteer, but Zhang Cheng refused to mistake possibility for proof. The public account described the situation as critical, but Zhang Cheng asked for the observation behind that claim. Nobody disputed the explosion; what troubled Zhang Cheng was the silence around its origin.

他曾向調查小組說明，證詞卻從官方檔案消失。整齊的解釋本可省去爭吵，但證據偏偏不整齊。裡面有一道足以改變先前意義的缺口。拿猜測補起來雖快，對信任答案的人卻不公平。檔案鑰匙雖小，碰上桌面卻發出沉重聲音。見證人在覆核時提到「文明」，社長保留了原話。「恭賀」或許有幫助，張澄卻拒絕把可能當成證明。公開說法把情況形容為「關鍵的」，張澄要求查看支撐判斷的觀察。沒人質疑「爆炸」，讓張澄困擾的是它的來歷沒有人提。

Zhang Cheng recorded his recollection with permission and sought independent corroboration. There was no comfortable choice. Any action would take time from someone else, and doing nothing would also have a cost. The disagreement became useful only after each person named the particular risk they feared. What had sounded like stubbornness was sometimes a warning nobody had understood. Tao Li’s name stayed visible in the margin after the other writing faded. One instruction told the team to forbid a necessary warning; nobody could explain why it was urgent. the club president proposed they illustrate the difference; Zhang Cheng wanted the risk written down first. It might help to install the backup device, but Zhang Cheng refused to mistake possibility for proof. The public account described the situation as mere, but Zhang Cheng asked for the observation behind that claim.

張澄經同意錄下回憶，並尋求獨立佐證。沒有令人舒服的選擇。每種行動都會耗去別人的時間，什麼都不做也有代價。只有當每個人說出自己害怕的具體風險，分歧才開始有用。原本像固執的話，有時是沒人聽懂的警告。其他字跡褪色後，陶黎的名字仍留在頁邊。一項指令要求團隊「禁止」，卻沒有人能解釋為何急迫。社長提議「圖解說明」，張澄希望先把風險寫清楚。「安裝」或許有幫助，張澄卻拒絕把可能當成證明。公開說法把情況形容為「僅僅的」，張澄要求查看支撐判斷的觀察。

The guard showed them a copy of the missing statement kept in his own drawer. A surprising number of people had been waiting for that small result. Some had hoped to hear reassuring words; others needed permission to act. The result offered neither comfort nor permission by itself. It gave them a narrower, more honest problem to solve. Zhang Cheng counted the chairs again, certain there had been seven before. The warning sounded pregnant to some listeners, although the actual measurements remained uncertain. Zhang Cheng disliked the word prosperous in the summary because it hid a decision someone had made. A witness mentioned resolution during the review, so the club president preserved the exact wording.

警衛拿出自己抽屜裡保存的失蹤證詞副本。有令人意外的多人一直等著這個小結果。有人想聽安慰，有人需要行動許可。但結果本身既不提供安慰也不提供許可，只讓問題縮小、也更誠實。張澄再次數椅子，確信之前只有七張。對部分聽眾而言，警告聽起來「懷孕的」，實際測量卻仍有疑點。張澄不喜歡摘要中的「繁榮的」，因為它掩藏了某個人的決定。見證人在覆核時提到「決議」，社長保留了原話。

Later, when the noise softened, the hardest detail was not written in the official account. It was the hesitation before an ordinary question, the way someone waited for permission to say they were tired. No one in the group could give back every hour already lost. Zhang Cheng glanced toward the club president and remembered the principle they had been arguing about: a forgotten name deserves an open door. It sounded simple until it demanded another hour of work. Nobody could tell whether the faint chime came from a wire or a memory. They found a reference to revolution near the damaged club ledger, but its date was unreadable. The settler appeared in the older club ledger, and Zhang Cheng asked why the newer version omitted it. Nobody disputed the surgeon; what troubled Zhang Cheng was the silence around its origin.

後來噪音緩下來，最難忘的細節不在正式報告裡，而是平凡提問前的遲疑，以及有人等著被允許說自己累了的模樣。團隊沒有人能還回失去的每個小時。張澄望向社長，想起兩人不斷爭論的原則：被遺忘的名字值得一扇開著的門。話雖簡單，真正實踐又需要多一小時工作。沒人知道微弱的鐘聲來自電線還是記憶。他們在受損社團帳簿旁找到提及「革命」的紀錄，日期卻看不清。舊社團帳簿出現了「遷居者」，張澄追問新版為何漏掉。沒人質疑「外科醫師」，讓張澄困擾的是它的來歷沒有人提。

They filed the result beside the earlier mistakes, not above them. Tomorrow would require a different question and perhaps a different kind of courage. For now, the important thing was that the people most affected could read what had happened and challenge the account if it was wrong. Zhang Cheng knew the next day would not begin from a clean slate. The club ledger would preserve one more question, and the club president would be there to challenge the first easy answer. The seventh member checked the exit before anyone started the meeting. Zhang Cheng disliked the word suspicious in the summary because it hid a decision someone had made. A witness mentioned tomb during the review, so the club president preserved the exact wording. They found a reference to website near the damaged club ledger, but its date was unreadable.

他們把結果放在先前的錯誤旁邊，而不是蓋過錯誤。明天需要不同的問題，也許需要不同的勇氣。眼下最重要的是受影響者能讀到經過，也能在紀錄錯誤時提出挑戰。張澄知道明天不會從零開始。社團帳簿將保存又一個問題，社長也會在場質疑第一個容易的答案。第七名社員在會議開始前先檢查出口。張澄不喜歡摘要中的「可疑的」，因為它掩藏了某個人的決定。見證人在覆核時提到「墓穴」，社長保留了原話。他們在受損社團帳簿旁找到提及「網站」的紀錄，日期卻看不清。
