七名社員八則訊息

Seven Members, Eight Messages

怪談社的第零號社員 · 第 6 / 25 章 · 3000–4000 · 英中對照 · 校訂中

The club received eight anonymous messages about the old fire, one for each chair. It began with something that could be held in one hand, a small object too ordinary to attract attention. Yet the longer they looked, the less ordinary it became. The real problem was not its appearance but the decision someone had already made on its behalf. Nobody could tell whether the faint chime came from a wire or a memory. A witness called the result conservative; the club president wrote the adjective beside the witness’s name. They found a reference to consultant near the damaged club ledger, but its date was unreadable. The consumer appeared in the older club ledger, and Zhang Cheng asked why the newer version omitted it.

怪談社收到八則匿名的舊火災訊息，每張椅子一則。事情從一個單手能拿起的小物件開始，普通得幾乎沒人會注意。可看得越久，它就越不尋常。真正的問題不在外表，而是有人早已代替它做出了決定。沒人知道微弱的鐘聲來自電線還是記憶。見證人稱結果「保守的」，社長把形容詞記在見證人姓名旁。他們在受損社團帳簿旁找到提及「顧問」的紀錄，日期卻看不清。舊社團帳簿出現了「消費者」，張澄追問新版為何漏掉。

Seven messages repeated details already public; the eighth named a hidden stairwell. The easiest part of the investigation was drawing a line between two points. The difficult part was deciding what that line meant. It might show a connection, a coincidence, or an error copied from an older file. No one wanted to spend another day checking. The seventh member checked the exit before anyone started the meeting. Nobody disputed the dye; what troubled Zhang Cheng was the silence around its origin. One instruction told the team to enclose the original document; nobody could explain why it was urgent. A witness mentioned goods during the review, so the club president preserved the exact wording. They found a reference to grace near the damaged club ledger, but its date was unreadable.

七則重述公開資料，第八則指出隱藏樓梯。調查最容易的部分，是在兩個點之間畫線。困難的是決定那條線的意思。它可能代表關聯、巧合，也可能是從舊檔複製的錯誤。沒有人想再花一天查證。第七名社員在會議開始前先檢查出口。沒人質疑「染料」，讓張澄困擾的是它的來歷沒有人提。一項指令要求團隊「封入」，卻沒有人能解釋為何急迫。見證人在覆核時提到「貨品」，社長保留了原話。他們在受損社團帳簿旁找到提及「從容」的紀錄，日期卻看不清。

The members compared the messages with floor plans and called an inspector before approaching the stairs. They argued quietly because shouting would only give the nearby cameras and strangers another story to tell. Beneath the disagreement lay a practical question: who would bear the consequences if the confident person turned out to be mistaken? No one volunteered to answer for somebody else. The corridor smelled of damp paper and paint laid over an older warning. The public account described the situation as habitual, but Zhang Cheng asked for the observation behind that claim. The warning sounded ignorant to some listeners, although the actual measurements remained uncertain. One instruction told the team to invest in safer equipment; nobody could explain why it was urgent. the club president proposed they isolate the damaged circuit; Zhang Cheng wanted the risk written down first.

社員先比對平面圖，請來檢查員才接近樓梯。他們壓低聲音爭辯，因為大喊只會讓旁邊的鏡頭與陌生人又多一個故事。爭論底下是實際問題：自信的人若弄錯，誰承擔後果？沒有人願意代替別人回答。走廊散發潮紙與覆蓋舊警告的新漆味。公開說法把情況形容為「習慣性的」，張澄要求查看支撐判斷的觀察。對部分聽眾而言，警告聽起來「不了解的」，實際測量卻仍有疑點。一項指令要求團隊「投資」，卻沒有人能解釋為何急迫。社長提議「隔離」，張澄希望先把風險寫清楚。

The stairwell existed, but its door had been sealed by an unauthorized lock. The first result was neither victory nor defeat. It removed one false assumption and exposed another problem that had been concealed beneath it. A witness asked whether the discovery could be trusted. The answer was that the test could be repeated, not that the investigators were special. The door remained open at the end of the meeting, a small deliberate change. They found a reference to linen near the damaged club ledger, but its date was unreadable. The luxury appeared in the older club ledger, and Zhang Cheng asked why the newer version omitted it. Nobody disputed the microscope; what troubled Zhang Cheng was the silence around its origin.

樓梯真的存在，門上卻有未核准的鎖。第一個結果既非勝利也非失敗。它排除一個錯誤假設，也露出下面藏著的另一個問題。見證人問發現能否相信；答案是測試可以重複，不是調查者有什麼特權。會議結束後門仍敞開，那是刻意而微小的改變。他們在受損社團帳簿旁找到提及「亞麻布」的紀錄，日期卻看不清。舊社團帳簿出現了「奢華」，張澄追問新版為何漏掉。沒人質疑「顯微鏡」，讓張澄困擾的是它的來歷沒有人提。

After the argument, a long silence settled around the group. It was not agreement. People were calculating what the next attempt might take from them, and whether anyone would notice the loss. Their caution was an ordinary human response, not a flaw in their courage. Zhang Cheng glanced toward the club president and remembered the principle they had been arguing about: a forgotten name deserves an open door. It sounded simple until it demanded another hour of work. Moonlight reached the empty chair without crossing the covered window. The team considered the occupation a separate detail, not proof that a sealed emergency exit was safe. A witness mentioned orientation during the review, so the club president preserved the exact wording. They found a reference to rage near the damaged club ledger, but its date was unreadable.

爭論後，長久沉默籠罩大家。那不是同意。人們正在估算下一次嘗試會奪走什麼、又會不會有人注意。謹慎是平凡的人性反應，不是勇氣的缺點。張澄望向社長，想起兩人不斷爭論的原則：被遺忘的名字值得一扇開著的門。話雖簡單，真正實踐又需要多一小時工作。月光沒有穿過遮住的窗，卻照上空椅。團隊把「職業」視為獨立細節，不當成封閉的緊急出口安全的證據。見證人在覆核時提到「職前說明」，社長保留了原話。他們在受損社團帳簿旁找到提及「盛怒」的紀錄，日期卻看不清。

The last light in the room was not a sign of triumph. It was a reminder that somebody would return to check the work, and that a new person might notice something the experts had missed. They left the account open to correction rather than locking it inside a victory speech. Zhang Cheng knew the next day would not begin from a clean slate. The club ledger would preserve one more question, and the club president would be there to challenge the first easy answer. A rumor could move through campus faster than any person carrying its evidence. The stereo appeared in the older club ledger, and Zhang Cheng asked why the newer version omitted it. The warning sounded timid to some listeners, although the actual measurements remained uncertain. One instruction told the team to tremble as the light changed; nobody could explain why it was urgent.

房裡最後的燈不是勝利標誌，而是提醒有人會回來檢查，也可能有新人看見專家漏掉的事。他們讓紀錄保持可更正，而不是鎖進勝利演說裡。張澄知道明天不會從零開始。社團帳簿將保存又一個問題，社長也會在場質疑第一個容易的答案。傳言在校園裡傳得比帶證據的人更快。舊社團帳簿出現了「立體聲」，張澄追問新版為何漏掉。對部分聽眾而言，警告聽起來「膽小的」，實際測量卻仍有疑點。一項指令要求團隊「顫抖」，卻沒有人能解釋為何急迫。
