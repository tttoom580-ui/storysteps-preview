The Corridor That Repeats Names

Members heard their names whispered from the abandoned east wing. Nobody in the room knew that this would become a turning point. The ordinary sounds of work continued around them, almost offensively calm, while an uncertain fact asked to be checked. A confident announcement would have been easier than the slower work of asking who might be hurt. The corridor smelled of damp paper and paint laid over an older warning. One instruction told the team to accompany the returning survivor; nobody could explain why it was urgent. A witness mentioned attraction during the review, so the club president preserved the exact wording. They found a reference to diagram near the damaged club ledger, but its date was unreadable.

社員聽見廢棄東翼傳來自己的名字。房裡沒有人知道這將是轉折。周圍平凡的工作聲仍持續著，平靜得幾乎令人不安，而一個未經確定的事實正等待查核。高聲宣布結論遠比緩慢詢問誰可能受傷來得容易。走廊散發潮紙與覆蓋舊警告的新漆味。一項指令要求團隊「陪同」，卻沒有人能解釋為何急迫。見證人在覆核時提到「賣點」，社長保留了原話。他們在受損社團帳簿旁找到提及「圖表」的紀錄，日期卻看不清。

A recorder captured voices only when the corridor's emergency bell flashed. The easiest part of the investigation was drawing a line between two points. The difficult part was deciding what that line meant. It might show a connection, a coincidence, or an error copied from an older file. No one wanted to spend another day checking. The door remained open at the end of the meeting, a small deliberate change. The diplomat appeared in the older club ledger, and Zhang Cheng asked why the newer version omitted it. Nobody disputed the draft; what troubled Zhang Cheng was the silence around its origin. Zhang Cheng disliked the word essential in the summary because it hid a decision someone had made. A witness called the result experimental; the club president wrote the adjective beside the witness’s name.

錄音機只有在走廊緊急鈴閃爍時才錄到聲音。調查最容易的部分，是在兩個點之間畫線。困難的是決定那條線的意思。它可能代表關聯、巧合，也可能是從舊檔複製的錯誤。沒有人想再花一天查證。會議結束後門仍敞開，那是刻意而微小的改變。舊社團帳簿出現了「外交官」，張澄追問新版為何漏掉。沒人質疑「草案」，讓張澄困擾的是它的來歷沒有人提。張澄不喜歡摘要中的「必要的」，因為它掩藏了某個人的決定。見證人稱結果「實驗性的」，社長把形容詞記在見證人姓名旁。

They stayed outside the unsafe wing and asked the facilities team to inspect the bell. They argued quietly because shouting would only give the nearby cameras and strangers another story to tell. Beneath the disagreement lay a practical question: who would bear the consequences if the confident person turned out to be mistaken? No one volunteered to answer for somebody else. Moonlight reached the empty chair without crossing the covered window. It might help to flee from the unsafe corridor, but Zhang Cheng refused to mistake possibility for proof. The germ appeared in the older club ledger, and Zhang Cheng asked why the newer version omitted it. Nobody disputed the greeting; what troubled Zhang Cheng was the silence around its origin. The team considered the hesitation a separate detail, not proof that a sealed emergency exit was safe.

他們留在危險東翼外，請設施組檢查警鈴。他們壓低聲音爭辯，因為大喊只會讓旁邊的鏡頭與陌生人又多一個故事。爭論底下是實際問題：自信的人若弄錯，誰承擔後果？沒有人願意代替別人回答。月光沒有穿過遮住的窗，卻照上空椅。「逃離」或許有幫助，張澄卻拒絕把可能當成證明。舊社團帳簿出現了「病菌」，張澄追問新版為何漏掉。沒人質疑「問候」，讓張澄困擾的是它的來歷沒有人提。團隊把「猶豫」視為獨立細節，不當成封閉的緊急出口安全的證據。

The bell contained a decade-old cassette sealed behind its cover. The evidence produced a consequence nobody could delegate away. Somebody would have to change a timetable, surrender a privilege, or admit that a previous decision had hurt people. Numbers were useful only when they could still lead to accountable actions. A rumor could move through campus faster than any person carrying its evidence. A witness mentioned horizon during the review, so the club president preserved the exact wording. It might help to loosen the damaged fastener, but Zhang Cheng refused to mistake possibility for proof. The public account described the situation as magnificent, but Zhang Cheng asked for the observation behind that claim.

警鈴外殼後封著一卷十年前的錄音帶。證據造成無法推給別人的後果。有人必須改時程、交還特權，或承認先前決定傷害了人。數字只有能引向負責的行動，才真的有用。傳言在校園裡傳得比帶證據的人更快。見證人在覆核時提到「地平線」，社長保留了原話。「鬆開」或許有幫助，張澄卻拒絕把可能當成證明。公開說法把情況形容為「壯麗的」，張澄要求查看支撐判斷的觀察。

After the argument, a long silence settled around the group. It was not agreement. People were calculating what the next attempt might take from them, and whether anyone would notice the loss. Their caution was an ordinary human response, not a flaw in their courage. Zhang Cheng glanced toward the club president and remembered the principle they had been arguing about: a forgotten name deserves an open door. It sounded simple until it demanded another hour of work. The old clubroom clock ticked toward midnight, though nobody had asked it to perform. Nobody disputed the manual; what troubled Zhang Cheng was the silence around its origin. The team considered the mechanic a separate detail, not proof that a sealed emergency exit was safe. A witness called the result outstanding; the club president wrote the adjective beside the witness’s name.

爭論後，長久沉默籠罩大家。那不是同意。人們正在估算下一次嘗試會奪走什麼、又會不會有人注意。謹慎是平凡的人性反應，不是勇氣的缺點。張澄望向社長，想起兩人不斷爭論的原則：被遺忘的名字值得一扇開著的門。話雖簡單，真正實踐又需要多一小時工作。舊社辦時鐘滴答走向午夜，卻沒有人要求它表演。沒人質疑「操作手冊」，讓張澄困擾的是它的來歷沒有人提。團隊把「機械技師」視為獨立細節，不當成封閉的緊急出口安全的證據。見證人稱結果「傑出的」，社長把形容詞記在見證人姓名旁。

The last light in the room was not a sign of triumph. It was a reminder that somebody would return to check the work, and that a new person might notice something the experts had missed. They left the account open to correction rather than locking it inside a victory speech. Zhang Cheng knew the next day would not begin from a clean slate. The club ledger would preserve one more question, and the club president would be there to challenge the first easy answer. The archive key sounded heavy against the table, despite its tiny size. They found a reference to recreation near the damaged club ledger, but its date was unreadable. The satellite appeared in the older club ledger, and Zhang Cheng asked why the newer version omitted it. Before they could strengthen the protective railing, the group needed a witness who was free to object.

房裡最後的燈不是勝利標誌，而是提醒有人會回來檢查，也可能有新人看見專家漏掉的事。他們讓紀錄保持可更正，而不是鎖進勝利演說裡。張澄知道明天不會從零開始。社團帳簿將保存又一個問題，社長也會在場質疑第一個容易的答案。檔案鑰匙雖小，碰上桌面卻發出沉重聲音。他們在受損社團帳簿旁找到提及「休閒活動」的紀錄，日期卻看不清。舊社團帳簿出現了「衛星」，張澄追問新版為何漏掉。在能夠「加強」之前，團隊需要一位能自由提出異議的見證人。
