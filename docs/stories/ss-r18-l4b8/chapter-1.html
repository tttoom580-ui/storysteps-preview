The Eighth Chair

怪談社的第零號社員 · 第 1 / 25 章 · 3000–4000 · 英中對照 · 校訂中

University student Zhang Cheng joined a folklore club that officially listed only seven members. Nobody in the room knew that this would become a turning point. The ordinary sounds of work continued around them, almost offensively calm, while an uncertain fact asked to be checked. A confident announcement would have been easier than the slower work of asking who might be hurt. A rumor could move through campus faster than any person carrying its evidence. the club president proposed they convey the urgent warning; Zhang Cheng wanted the risk written down first. They found a reference to cottage near the damaged club ledger, but its date was unreadable. The cushion appeared in the older club ledger, and Zhang Cheng asked why the newer version omitted it.

大學生張澄加入正式只有七名社員的怪談社。房裡沒有人知道這將是轉折。周圍平凡的工作聲仍持續著，平靜得幾乎令人不安，而一個未經確定的事實正等待查核。高聲宣布結論遠比緩慢詢問誰可能受傷來得容易。傳言在校園裡傳得比帶證據的人更快。社長提議「傳達」，張澄希望先把風險寫清楚。他們在受損社團帳簿旁找到提及「山間小屋」的紀錄，日期卻看不清。舊社團帳簿出現了「軟墊」，張澄追問新版為何漏掉。

At their first meeting an eighth chair stood beneath a covered window. An attractive theory formed almost immediately. It explained the loudest clue and gave everyone someone to blame. Yet one quiet observation remained outside it. Rather than hide that observation, the group put it where every later explanation would have to face it. The old clubroom clock ticked toward midnight, though nobody had asked it to perform. Nobody disputed the defense; what troubled Zhang Cheng was the silence around its origin. The team considered the diversity a separate detail, not proof that a sealed emergency exit was safe. A witness called the result fierce; the club president wrote the adjective beside the witness’s name. It might help to flatter a powerful sponsor, but Zhang Cheng refused to mistake possibility for proof.

第一次社課時，遮住的窗下卻多出第八張椅子。一個吸引人的理論幾乎立刻成形。它解釋了最顯眼的線索，也給大家一個可以責怪的人。但一項安靜的觀察留在理論之外。團隊沒有藏起它，而是把它放在所有後續解釋都必須面對的位置。舊社辦時鐘滴答走向午夜，卻沒有人要求它表演。沒人質疑「防禦」，讓張澄困擾的是它的來歷沒有人提。團隊把「多元性」視為獨立細節，不當成封閉的緊急出口安全的證據。見證人稱結果「兇悍的」，社長把形容詞記在見證人姓名旁。「奉承」或許有幫助，張澄卻拒絕把可能當成證明。

She asked who had placed it there and wrote down the different answers rather than choosing one. The decision required a kind of patience that was easily mistaken for fear. Their tools were limited and the timetable already slipping. Yet the people affected had not agreed to be used as proof that an ambitious plan could work. The archive key sounded heavy against the table, despite its tiny size. The genius appeared in the older club ledger, and Zhang Cheng asked why the newer version omitted it. Nobody disputed the giggle; what troubled Zhang Cheng was the silence around its origin. The team considered the parachute a separate detail, not proof that a sealed emergency exit was safe. A witness called the result pessimistic; the club president wrote the adjective beside the witness’s name.

她詢問誰放的椅子，把互相矛盾的答案都記下。這個決定需要一種容易被誤認為害怕的耐心。工具有限，時程也在延後，但受影響的人並沒有同意當作證明野心計畫可行的材料。檔案鑰匙雖小，碰上桌面卻發出沉重聲音。舊社團帳簿出現了「天才」，張澄追問新版為何漏掉。沒人質疑「輕笑」，讓張澄困擾的是它的來歷沒有人提。團隊把「降落傘」視為獨立細節，不當成封閉的緊急出口安全的證據。見證人稱結果「悲觀的」，社長把形容詞記在見證人姓名旁。

At midnight the empty chair scraped the floor while every member was watching. For a moment the whole plan seemed to fail. The expected signal never arrived, and the people waiting outside grew impatient. Then a smaller detail emerged, one that would have been lost if the group had covered the mistake with a confident announcement. Tao Li’s name stayed visible in the margin after the other writing faded. It might help to possess the original document, but Zhang Cheng refused to mistake possibility for proof. The presentation appeared in the older club ledger, and Zhang Cheng asked why the newer version omitted it. Nobody disputed the reference; what troubled Zhang Cheng was the silence around its origin.

午夜時，眾人看著空椅子自行刮過地板。有一刻整個計畫像要失敗。預期訊號沒有來，外面等候的人越來越不耐。隨後出現更小的細節；若團隊用自信公告掩蓋錯誤，那個細節就會消失。其他字跡褪色後，陶黎的名字仍留在頁邊。「擁有」或許有幫助，張澄卻拒絕把可能當成證明。舊社團帳簿出現了「發表會」，張澄追問新版為何漏掉。沒人質疑「參考」，讓張澄困擾的是它的來歷沒有人提。

The place was still full of ordinary life. Someone laughed at a poor joke, someone swept a corner, and a tired worker counted the minutes to the next shift. Those details made the danger harder to ignore, because the people involved had lives outside this puzzle. Zhang Cheng glanced toward the club president and remembered the principle they had been arguing about: a forgotten name deserves an open door. It sounded simple until it demanded another hour of work. Zhang Cheng counted the chairs again, certain there had been seven before. Zhang Cheng disliked the word spiritual in the summary because it hid a decision someone had made. A witness called the result tremendous; the club president wrote the adjective beside the witness’s name. They found a reference to violation near the damaged club ledger, but its date was unreadable.

這地方仍充滿平凡生活。有人為難笑話發笑，有人掃地，疲憊的工作者算著距離換班還有幾分鐘。這些細節讓危險更難忽略，因為受影響者在謎題之外還有人生。張澄望向社長，想起兩人不斷爭論的原則：被遺忘的名字值得一扇開著的門。話雖簡單，真正實踐又需要多一小時工作。張澄再次數椅子，確信之前只有七張。張澄不喜歡摘要中的「精神上的」，因為它掩藏了某個人的決定。見證人稱結果「巨大的」，社長把形容詞記在見證人姓名旁。他們在受損社團帳簿旁找到提及「違規」的紀錄，日期卻看不清。

They filed the result beside the earlier mistakes, not above them. Tomorrow would require a different question and perhaps a different kind of courage. For now, the important thing was that the people most affected could read what had happened and challenge the account if it was wrong. Zhang Cheng knew the next day would not begin from a clean slate. The club ledger would preserve one more question, and the club president would be there to challenge the first easy answer. Nobody could tell whether the faint chime came from a wire or a memory. The public account described the situation as visual, but Zhang Cheng asked for the observation behind that claim. Nobody disputed the witness; what troubled Zhang Cheng was the silence around its origin. The team considered the workout a separate detail, not proof that a sealed emergency exit was safe.

他們把結果放在先前的錯誤旁邊，而不是蓋過錯誤。明天需要不同的問題，也許需要不同的勇氣。眼下最重要的是受影響者能讀到經過，也能在紀錄錯誤時提出挑戰。張澄知道明天不會從零開始。社團帳簿將保存又一個問題，社長也會在場質疑第一個容易的答案。沒人知道微弱的鐘聲來自電線還是記憶。公開說法把情況形容為「視覺的」，張澄要求查看支撐判斷的觀察。沒人質疑「見證者」，讓張澄困擾的是它的來歷沒有人提。團隊把「體能訓練」視為獨立細節，不當成封閉的緊急出口安全的證據。
