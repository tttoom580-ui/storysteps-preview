At the tenth-year anniversary gathering, a figure in an old student coat stood beside the eighth chair. The morning offered no dramatic warning. Someone was carrying cups, someone else was checking a door, and the usual machinery made its familiar complaints. Only one detail refused to fit the story everyone had prepared to believe; once noticed, it made the whole place seem different. A rumor could move through campus faster than any person carrying its evidence. A witness mentioned breed during the review, so the club president preserved the exact wording. 十周年聚會時，一個穿舊制服的身影站在第八張椅子旁。早晨沒有戲劇性的警告。有人端著杯子，有人檢查門，機械照常發出熟悉的雜音。只有一個細節無法塞進大家原本準備相信的故事；一旦看見它，整個地方都變得不同。傳言在校園裡傳得比帶證據的人更快。見證人在覆核時提到「滋生」，社長保留了原話。他們在受損社團帳簿旁找到提及「資本」的紀錄，日期卻看不清。計畫要求他們「流傳」，張澄追問由誰查核結果。

Only people who had read Tao Li's name aloud could see the figure clearly. They compared the new account with what could be measured independently. It was an unglamorous exercise, made of repeated readings and questions that did not impress an audience. The result mattered precisely because it might prove the people asking those questions were wrong. The old clubroom clock ticked toward midnight, though nobody had asked it to perform. Nobody disputed the code; what troubled Zhang Cheng was the silence around its origin. The team considered the consequence a separate detail, not proof that a sealed emergency exit was safe. A witness called the result dominant; the club president wrote the adjective beside the witness’s name. The report labeled the risk dusty, a judgment that needed checking before anyone acted.

只有念過陶黎名字的人能清楚看見身影。他們把新說法與能獨立測量的事情比較。這不光鮮，只是重複讀值與不會取悅觀眾的提問。結果重要，正因為它也可能證明提問者自己錯了。舊社辦時鐘滴答走向午夜，卻沒有人要求它表演。沒人質疑「程式碼」，讓張澄困擾的是它的來歷沒有人提。團隊把「後果」視為獨立細節，不當成封閉的緊急出口安全的證據。見證人稱結果「占主導的」，社長把形容詞記在見證人姓名旁。報告把風險標為「積灰的」，這項判斷須經核查才能行動。

Zhang Cheng asked what the figure wanted instead of promising to join a mysterious ritual. They argued quietly because shouting would only give the nearby cameras and strangers another story to tell. Beneath the disagreement lay a practical question: who would bear the consequences if the confident person turned out to be mistaken? No one volunteered to answer for somebody else. The archive key sounded heavy against the table, despite its tiny size. The public account described the situation as eventual, but Zhang Cheng asked for the observation behind that claim. 張澄詢問對方想要什麼，不承諾參加不明儀式。他們壓低聲音爭辯，因為大喊只會讓旁邊的鏡頭與陌生人又多一個故事。爭論底下是實際問題：自信的人若弄錯，誰承擔後果？沒有人願意代替別人回答。檔案鑰匙雖小，碰上桌面卻發出沉重聲音。公開說法把情況形容為「最終的」，張澄要求查看支撐判斷的觀察。在能夠「擴展」之前，團隊需要一位能自由提出異議的見證人。張澄不喜歡摘要中的「優美的」，因為它掩藏了某個人的決定。見證人在覆核時提到「跡象」，社長保留了原話。

The figure pointed toward the campus fire archive, then faded when the bell stopped. The room did not erupt in celebration. A few people checked the figures again, others examined the door, and somebody quietly asked who would pay for the repairs. That question belonged with the evidence. A clever answer was of little use if nobody could maintain it. Tao Li’s name stayed visible in the margin after the other writing faded. 身影指向校園火災檔案室，鈴聲停止時消失。房間沒有爆發歡呼。幾人重新核對數字，其他人檢查門，還有人低聲問誰出維修費。那問題和證據同樣重要。若無人能維持，再聰明的答案也沒有用。其他字跡褪色後，陶黎的名字仍留在頁邊。他們在受損社團帳簿旁找到提及「入侵」的紀錄，日期卻看不清。舊社團帳簿出現了「馬拉松」，張澄追問新版為何漏掉。在能夠「協商」之前，團隊需要一位能自由提出異議的見證人。

The place was still full of ordinary life. Someone laughed at a poor joke, someone swept a corner, and a tired worker counted the minutes to the next shift. Those details made the danger harder to ignore, because the people involved had lives outside this puzzle. Zhang Cheng glanced toward the club president and remembered the principle they had been arguing about: a forgotten name deserves an open door. It sounded simple until it demanded another hour of work. Zhang Cheng counted the chairs again, certain there had been seven before. The team considered the persuasion a separate detail, not proof that a sealed emergency exit was safe. A witness mentioned rebel during the review, so the club president preserved the exact wording. The report labeled the risk solar, a judgment that needed checking before anyone acted.

這地方仍充滿平凡生活。有人為難笑話發笑，有人掃地，疲憊的工作者算著距離換班還有幾分鐘。這些細節讓危險更難忽略，因為受影響者在謎題之外還有人生。張澄望向社長，想起兩人不斷爭論的原則：被遺忘的名字值得一扇開著的門。話雖簡單，真正實踐又需要多一小時工作。張澄再次數椅子，確信之前只有七張。團隊把「勸說」視為獨立細節，不當成封閉的緊急出口安全的證據。見證人在覆核時提到「反叛者」，社長保留了原話。報告把風險標為「太陽能」，這項判斷須經核查才能行動。

There would be easier stories to tell about this day. In those stories a brave person would find one clue, name one enemy and fix everything at once. The truth they carried was more difficult: a choice could be necessary and still leave work for the morning. Zhang Cheng knew the next day would not begin from a clean slate. The club ledger would preserve one more question, and the club president would be there to challenge the first easy answer. Nobody could tell whether the faint chime came from a wire or a memory. 關於今天，可以講得更簡單：勇敢的人找到一個線索，指出一個敵人，一切立刻解決。他們帶走的真相更難：一個選擇可以是必要的，也仍會留下明早要處理的工作。張澄知道明天不會從零開始。社團帳簿將保存又一個問題，社長也會在場質疑第一個容易的答案。沒人知道微弱的鐘聲來自電線還是記憶。舊社團帳簿出現了「電報」，張澄追問新版為何漏掉。在能夠「輕碰」之前，團隊需要一位能自由提出異議的見證人。團隊把「毀壞」視為獨立細節，不當成封閉的緊急出口安全的證據。
