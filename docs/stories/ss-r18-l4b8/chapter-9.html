封閉圖書館的聲音

A Voice from the Closed Library

怪談社的第零號社員 · 第 9 / 25 章 · 3000–4000 · 英中對照 · 校訂中

A recorded voice guided Zhang Cheng to a locked library room after midnight. Before anyone spoke, the room was divided between those who had been promised safety and those expected to provide it. The distinction was familiar, and that was precisely why it worried the people who had to act. Familiar arrangements could hide old mistakes remarkably well. The archive key sounded heavy against the table, despite its tiny size. The team considered the accent a separate detail, not proof that a sealed emergency exit was safe. the club president proposed they attach a signed note; Zhang Cheng wanted the risk written down first. They found a reference to circumstance near the damaged club ledger, but its date was unreadable.

一道錄製的聲音在午夜後引導張澄到上鎖的圖書館房間。尚未有人開口，房間便分成被承諾安全的人，以及被期待提供安全的人。這種分別太熟悉，反而令必須行動的人不安。看似理所當然的安排，很容易藏住 오래以前的錯誤。檔案鑰匙雖小，碰上桌面卻發出沉重聲音。團隊把「口音」視為獨立細節，不當成封閉的緊急出口安全的證據。社長提議「附件」，張澄希望先把風險寫清楚。他們在受損社團帳簿旁找到提及「情況」的紀錄，日期卻看不清。

The room's catalog had been altered to omit books borrowed by Tao Li. An attractive theory formed almost immediately. It explained the loudest clue and gave everyone someone to blame. Yet one quiet observation remained outside it. Rather than hide that observation, the group put it where every later explanation would have to face it. Tao Li’s name stayed visible in the margin after the other writing faded. The public account described the situation as clumsy, but Zhang Cheng asked for the observation behind that claim. Nobody disputed the content; what troubled Zhang Cheng was the silence around its origin. Zhang Cheng disliked the word crunchy in the summary because it hid a decision someone had made. A witness mentioned earphone during the review, so the club president preserved the exact wording.

房間目錄被修改，刪去陶黎借過的書。一個吸引人的理論幾乎立刻成形。它解釋了最顯眼的線索，也給大家一個可以責怪的人。但一項安靜的觀察留在理論之外。團隊沒有藏起它，而是把它放在所有後續解釋都必須面對的位置。其他字跡褪色後，陶黎的名字仍留在頁邊。公開說法把情況形容為「笨拙的」，張澄要求查看支撐判斷的觀察。沒人質疑「內容」，讓張澄困擾的是它的來歷沒有人提。張澄不喜歡摘要中的「酥脆的」，因為它掩藏了某個人的決定。見證人在覆核時提到「耳機」，社長保留了原話。

She requested access with a librarian present and photographed the checkout cards. They argued quietly because shouting would only give the nearby cameras and strangers another story to tell. Beneath the disagreement lay a practical question: who would bear the consequences if the confident person turned out to be mistaken? No one volunteered to answer for somebody else. Zhang Cheng counted the chairs again, certain there had been seven before. The report labeled the risk economic, a judgment that needed checking before anyone acted. The frown appeared in the older club ledger, and Zhang Cheng asked why the newer version omitted it. Nobody disputed the homeland; what troubled Zhang Cheng was the silence around its origin. One instruction told the team to instruct the new members; nobody could explain why it was urgent.

她請圖書館員在場取得閱覽權，拍攝借書卡。他們壓低聲音爭辯，因為大喊只會讓旁邊的鏡頭與陌生人又多一個故事。爭論底下是實際問題：自信的人若弄錯，誰承擔後果？沒有人願意代替別人回答。張澄再次數椅子，確信之前只有七張。報告把風險標為「經濟的」，這項判斷須經核查才能行動。舊社團帳簿出現了「皺眉」，張澄追問新版為何漏掉。沒人質疑「故鄉」，讓張澄困擾的是它的來歷沒有人提。一項指令要求團隊「指示」，卻沒有人能解釋為何急迫。

One card held an address for a former campus security guard. A surprising number of people had been waiting for that small result. Some had hoped to hear reassuring words; others needed permission to act. The result offered neither comfort nor permission by itself. It gave them a narrower, more honest problem to solve. Nobody could tell whether the faint chime came from a wire or a memory. A witness mentioned interruption during the review, so the club president preserved the exact wording. It might help to lean against the railing, but Zhang Cheng refused to mistake possibility for proof. The plan called for them to mislead the audience, and Zhang Cheng asked who would check the result.

其中一張卡寫著前校警的地址。有令人意外的多人一直等著這個小結果。有人想聽安慰，有人需要行動許可。但結果本身既不提供安慰也不提供許可，只讓問題縮小、也更誠實。沒人知道微弱的鐘聲來自電線還是記憶。見證人在覆核時提到「中斷」，社長保留了原話。「俯身」或許有幫助，張澄卻拒絕把可能當成證明。計畫要求他們「誤導」，張澄追問由誰查核結果。

A small act of care interrupted the discussion. It solved no mystery and changed no official ruling. Yet it reminded them that the people named in the records were not pieces on a board; they had bodies, families and the right to decide what came next. Zhang Cheng glanced toward the club president and remembered the principle they had been arguing about: a forgotten name deserves an open door. It sounded simple until it demanded another hour of work. The seventh member checked the exit before anyone started the meeting. Nobody disputed the modesty; what troubled Zhang Cheng was the silence around its origin. Zhang Cheng disliked the word nuclear in the summary because it hid a decision someone had made. A witness mentioned peep during the review, so the club president preserved the exact wording.

一個小小的照顧動作打斷討論。它沒有解開謎，也沒改變裁決，卻提醒大家紀錄裡的人不是棋子；他們有身體、家庭，也有決定下一步的權利。張澄望向社長，想起兩人不斷爭論的原則：被遺忘的名字值得一扇開著的門。話雖簡單，真正實踐又需要多一小時工作。第七名社員在會議開始前先檢查出口。沒人質疑「審慎」，讓張澄困擾的是它的來歷沒有人提。張澄不喜歡摘要中的「核能的」，因為它掩藏了某個人的決定。見證人在覆核時提到「偷瞄」，社長保留了原話。

Before leaving, they checked the names once more. Names were easier to lose than equipment, especially when a system reduced people to numbers. The check took little time. It kept the next chapter of the problem from beginning with a convenient omission. Zhang Cheng knew the next day would not begin from a clean slate. The club ledger would preserve one more question, and the club president would be there to challenge the first easy answer. The corridor smelled of damp paper and paint laid over an older warning. They found a reference to refund near the damaged club ledger, but its date was unreadable. The public account described the situation as revolutionary, but Zhang Cheng asked for the observation behind that claim. Nobody disputed the wink; what troubled Zhang Cheng was the silence around its origin.

離開前，他們再次核對名字。名字比設備更容易消失，尤其當系統把人簡化成數字。檢查花不了多少時間，卻避免問題的下一章從方便的遺漏開始。張澄知道明天不會從零開始。社團帳簿將保存又一個問題，社長也會在場質疑第一個容易的答案。走廊散發潮紙與覆蓋舊警告的新漆味。他們在受損社團帳簿旁找到提及「退款」的紀錄，日期卻看不清。公開說法把情況形容為「革命性的」，張澄要求查看支撐判斷的觀察。沒人質疑「眨眼」，讓張澄困擾的是它的來歷沒有人提。
