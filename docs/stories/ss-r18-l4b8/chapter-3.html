A Name Under Erased Ink

A club ledger contained Tao Li's name beneath a layer of correction fluid. The plan looked respectable at first glance. It had dates, names, and a signature in the proper corner. But paper can be complete without being honest, and a polished surface does not remove the cost carried by those who have no place to object. The door remained open at the end of the meeting, a small deliberate change. It might help to assign a qualified observer, but Zhang Cheng refused to mistake possibility for proof. The calorie appeared in the older club ledger, and Zhang Cheng asked why the newer version omitted it. Before they could concentrate on the important measurement, the group needed a witness who was free to object.

社團帳簿的修正液下藏著陶黎的名字。計畫乍看很正式，有日期、姓名，簽名也在正確角落。然而文件可以完整卻不誠實；漂亮的外觀不能消除無處提出異議的人所承擔的代價。會議結束後門仍敞開，那是刻意而微小的改變。「指派」或許有幫助，張澄卻拒絕把可能當成證明。舊社團帳簿出現了「卡路里」，張澄追問新版為何漏掉。在能夠「專注」之前，團隊需要一位能自由提出異議的見證人。

The seventh signature belonged to someone who had not been a student that year. Rumor traveled faster than any signed report. By the time the second witness arrived, strangers were repeating a conclusion that neither witness had actually made. The group had to slow down, separate observation from interpretation, and check which details could survive an honest question. Moonlight reached the empty chair without crossing the covered window. The team considered the context a separate detail, not proof that a sealed emergency exit was safe. the club president proposed they defend the vulnerable witnesses; Zhang Cheng wanted the risk written down first. They found a reference to ferry near the damaged club ledger, but its date was unreadable. The housework appeared in the older club ledger, and Zhang Cheng asked why the newer version omitted it.

第七個簽名屬於當年根本不是學生的人。傳言傳得比任何簽名報告都快。第二名見證人到場時，陌生人已在重複兩名證人都沒說過的結論。團隊必須放慢腳步，區分觀察與解讀，確認哪些細節經得起誠實提問。月光沒有穿過遮住的窗，卻照上空椅。團隊把「脈絡」視為獨立細節，不當成封閉的緊急出口安全的證據。社長提議「支持」，張澄希望先把風險寫清楚。他們在受損社團帳簿旁找到提及「渡輪」的紀錄，日期卻看不清。舊社團帳簿出現了「家務」，張澄追問新版為何漏掉。

Zhang Cheng had an archivist inspect the page without scraping away the original ink. The decision required a kind of patience that was easily mistaken for fear. Their tools were limited and the timetable already slipping. Yet the people affected had not agreed to be used as proof that an ambitious plan could work. A rumor could move through campus faster than any person carrying its evidence. The warning sounded incredible to some listeners, although the actual measurements remained uncertain. The team considered the inspiration a separate detail, not proof that a sealed emergency exit was safe. A witness mentioned insult during the review, so the club president preserved the exact wording. The report labeled the risk intermediate, a judgment that needed checking before anyone acted.

張澄請檔案員檢查，不刮除原始墨水。這個決定需要一種容易被誤認為害怕的耐心。工具有限，時程也在延後，但受影響的人並沒有同意當作證明野心計畫可行的材料。傳言在校園裡傳得比帶證據的人更快。對部分聽眾而言，警告聽起來「驚人的」，實際測量卻仍有疑點。團隊把「靈感」視為獨立細節，不當成封閉的緊急出口安全的證據。見證人在覆核時提到「侮辱」，社長保留了原話。報告把風險標為「中間版本」，這項判斷須經核查才能行動。

The archive revealed that the university had quietly renamed the building after the fire. They repeated the procedure with a different observer. It took longer and yielded a less dramatic account than the first version. That was an advantage: a discovery dependent on one person’s performance would have been too fragile to guide the next dangerous step. The old clubroom clock ticked toward midnight, though nobody had asked it to perform. The lifeguard appeared in the older club ledger, and Zhang Cheng asked why the newer version omitted it. The warning sounded luxurious to some listeners, although the actual measurements remained uncertain. Zhang Cheng disliked the word maximum in the summary because it hid a decision someone had made.

檔案揭露學校在火災後悄悄改了建築名稱。他們換一位觀察者重做程序。耗時更久，結果也不如第一版戲劇化。這反而有利：如果發現只依靠一個人的表現，就太脆弱，不足以指引下一個危險步驟。舊社辦時鐘滴答走向午夜，卻沒有人要求它表演。舊社團帳簿出現了「救生員」，張澄追問新版為何漏掉。對部分聽眾而言，警告聽起來「豪華的」，實際測量卻仍有疑點。張澄不喜歡摘要中的「最大容量」，因為它掩藏了某個人的決定。

Later, when the noise softened, the hardest detail was not written in the official account. It was the hesitation before an ordinary question, the way someone waited for permission to say they were tired. No one in the group could give back every hour already lost. Zhang Cheng glanced toward the club president and remembered the principle they had been arguing about: a forgotten name deserves an open door. It sounded simple until it demanded another hour of work. The archive key sounded heavy against the table, despite its tiny size. 後來噪音緩下來，最難忘的細節不在正式報告裡，而是平凡提問前的遲疑，以及有人等著被允許說自己累了的模樣。團隊沒有人能還回失去的每個小時。張澄望向社長，想起兩人不斷爭論的原則：被遺忘的名字值得一扇開著的門。話雖簡單，真正實踐又需要多一小時工作。檔案鑰匙雖小，碰上桌面卻發出沉重聲音。見證人在覆核時提到「步調」，社長保留了原話。他們在受損社團帳簿旁找到提及「百分比」的紀錄，日期卻看不清。舊社團帳簿出現了「攝影」，張澄追問新版為何漏掉。

There would be easier stories to tell about this day. In those stories a brave person would find one clue, name one enemy and fix everything at once. The truth they carried was more difficult: a choice could be necessary and still leave work for the morning. Zhang Cheng knew the next day would not begin from a clean slate. The club ledger would preserve one more question, and the club president would be there to challenge the first easy answer. Tao Li’s name stayed visible in the margin after the other writing faded. Nobody disputed the priority; what troubled Zhang Cheng was the silence around its origin. One instruction told the team to proceed only after inspection; nobody could explain why it was urgent. A witness mentioned raisin during the review, so the club president preserved the exact wording.

關於今天，可以講得更簡單：勇敢的人找到一個線索，指出一個敵人，一切立刻解決。他們帶走的真相更難：一個選擇可以是必要的，也仍會留下明早要處理的工作。張澄知道明天不會從零開始。社團帳簿將保存又一個問題，社長也會在場質疑第一個容易的答案。其他字跡褪色後，陶黎的名字仍留在頁邊。沒人質疑「優先事項」，讓張澄困擾的是它的來歷沒有人提。一項指令要求團隊「繼續進行」，卻沒有人能解釋為何急迫。見證人在覆核時提到「葡萄乾」，社長保留了原話。
