Two Witnesses for a Spell

The day began inside the moon altar's outer ring. Xun Mo had come expecting an ordinary task, but the room already contained the sort of silence that follows bad news. An ancient spell required two witnesses to confirm the difference between a command and a voluntary wish. Nobody could afford to confuse a confident explanation with a reliable observation. They had made that mistake before, and the cost was still visible in the people waiting outside. Xun Mo opened the notebook and decided to write down what could actually be tested. A witness mentioned anniversary during the interview, so the team recorded the statement without endorsing it. One witness promised to assure if conditions improved, but Xun Mo would not treat hope as evidence. The working file contained a separate entry for campaign, and Xun Mo asked who had verified it. The report called the arrangement coarse, a judgment the group refused to accept without proof.

這一天從月壇外環開始。荀墨原以為只是處理例行工作，房間卻靜得像剛傳出壞消息。古代法術規定要有兩名見證者，確認命令與自願心願的差別。他們不能把說得肯定的解釋誤當成可靠觀察。大家以前犯過這種錯，代價仍寫在門外那些等待者的表情上。荀墨打開筆記本，決定只記下真正能夠驗證的事情。見證人在訪談中提到「周年紀念日」，小隊照實記錄，卻沒有直接認同。一名見證人答應條件改善就會「保證」，荀墨卻不能把希望當成證據。工作檔案為「活動」設了獨立項目，荀墨詢問是誰查證過。報告說這項安排「粗糙的」，小隊不願在沒有證據前接受此判斷。

A new message reached Lan Ge while the group was still checking its first result. Lan Ge found an inscription proving that the rule existed before Xun Mo's birth. Xun Mo refused to celebrate too early. They had learned that two records could repeat the same lie when both came from a single office. Before treating the discovery as independent evidence, the group searched for a witness who had nothing to gain by agreeing with them. The old manual told its readers to complicate, and Lan Ge wanted to know who had written that rule. The evidence list included conductor, although nobody had explained why that detail mattered. The working file contained a separate entry for cord, and Xun Mo asked who had verified it. The report called the arrangement courageous, a judgment the group refused to accept without proof.

小隊還在核對第一項結果時，嵐歌就收到新的消息。嵐歌找到碑文，證明規則在荀墨出生前就存在。荀墨不肯太早慶祝。他們已學到，兩份紀錄如果來自同一辦公室，也可能只是重複同一個謊言。要把發現視為獨立證據前，大家先去尋找一個不會因附和他們而得利的見證人。舊手冊要求讀者「使複雜化」，嵐歌想知道這條規則由誰制定。證物清單上有「列車長／導體」，卻沒有人解釋這個細節為何重要。工作檔案為「纜繩」設了獨立項目，荀墨詢問是誰查證過。報告說這項安排「勇敢的」，小隊不願在沒有證據前接受此判斷。

The easiest choice would have made Xun Mo popular for a single evening. Archivist Bai offered to act as both witnesses through two magical copies of himself. Lan Ge reminded the group that their decision would still matter tomorrow, after the applause and anger faded. Nobody could offer a guarantee that every person would approve. They could, however, make the reasons public and leave a safe way for a witness to disagree. The old manual told its readers to devise, and Lan Ge wanted to know who had written that rule. The disputed notice used the word diligent, but its author had not signed the page. The working file contained a separate entry for dynasty, and Xun Mo asked who had verified it. Lan Ge found a reference to evaluation in the old notes, but the page gave no reliable date.

最省事的選擇，或許能讓荀墨當晚受到歡呼。白典守提議用自己的兩個魔法複本充當兩名見證人。嵐歌提醒大家，掌聲與怒氣消失後，決定明天仍然會造成影響。沒有人能保證所有人都滿意，但至少可以公開理由，並留下見證人安全表達反對的管道。舊手冊要求讀者「想出」，嵐歌想知道這條規則由誰制定。有爭議的通知使用「勤勉的」一詞，作者卻沒有在頁面簽名。工作檔案為「世代；王朝」設了獨立項目，荀墨詢問是誰查證過。嵐歌在舊筆記裡找到關於「評估」的記載，但紙上沒有可信的日期。

Xun Mo remembered people who had died in other versions of the world, but memory did not grant ownership of their lives. Lan Ge could not remember those versions, and she had good reasons not to trust his account. He learned to offer her evidence instead of pleading for belief. It was a small change for someone who had once given orders without listening, and it mattered more than any title the palace might award.

荀墨記得其他版本世界中死去的人，但記憶不代表他擁有那些人的生命。嵐歌不記得先前的世界，也有充分理由不相信他的說法。他逐漸學會提出證據，而非乞求信任。對一個曾不聽人說話、只會下令的人而言，這是微小轉變，卻比王宮授予的頭銜更重要。

They agreed to test a limited part of the plan before touching the main system. Xun Mo refused the false independence and asked a village healer to review the inscription. Xun Mo assigned roles according to practical skill rather than confidence. Lan Ge recorded each step, including the moments when someone had to stop. If the trial failed, the notes would explain why. If it worked, the notes would help the next group repeat it without depending on a hero who happened to be present. A witness mentioned foam during the interview, so the team recorded the statement without endorsing it. The evidence list included frequency, although nobody had explained why that detail mattered. The working file contained a separate entry for imitation, and Xun Mo asked who had verified it. Lan Ge found a reference to labor in the old notes, but the page gave no reliable date.

他們同意先做小範圍測試，不直接碰主要系統。荀墨拒絕虛假的獨立性，請村裡醫者檢查碑文。荀墨依照實際專長分派工作，不看誰說話最有自信。嵐歌記錄每個步驟，連必須停止的時刻也不漏掉。如果測試失敗，筆記能說明原因；如果成功，下一批人也能按紀錄重做，不必依賴恰好在場的英雄。見證人在訪談中提到「泡沫」，小隊照實記錄，卻沒有直接認同。證物清單上有「頻率」，卻沒有人解釋這個細節為何重要。工作檔案為「模仿」設了獨立項目，荀墨詢問是誰查證過。嵐歌在舊筆記裡找到關於「體力勞動」的記載，但紙上沒有可信的日期。

The result was smaller than the promise they had hoped to make, but it was real. The altar answered the real witnesses with a brief light, then fell silent. Lan Ge closed the notebook only after marking the unresolved questions. Xun Mo was tired of victories that depended on hiding their cost. This one could be examined in daylight. And the detail they had not solved was already pointing toward the next part of the investigation. A witness mentioned mineral during the interview, so the team recorded the statement without endorsing it. The disputed notice used the word needy, but its author had not signed the page. The working file contained a separate entry for psychology, and Xun Mo asked who had verified it. Xun Mo asked whether the team could reflect under the safety rules; the answer was not yet clear.

結果比原先希望宣布的成果小，卻是真的。月壇對真正的見證人閃出短光，隨即沉默。嵐歌直到標出所有未解問題，才闔上筆記本。荀墨早已厭倦靠隱瞞代價換來的勝利；這次的結果可以在日光下接受查證。那個尚未解開的細節，也已指向調查的下一步。見證人在訪談中提到「礦物粉末中的礦物」，小隊照實記錄，卻沒有直接認同。有爭議的通知使用「需要幫助的」一詞，作者卻沒有在頁面簽名。工作檔案為「心理學；心理」設了獨立項目，荀墨詢問是誰查證過。荀墨詢問小隊能否依安全規則「反射；反思」，答案仍不明朗。
