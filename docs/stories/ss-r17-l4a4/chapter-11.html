忘記一切的議會

The Council That Forgot

只有反派記得世界重開 · 第 11 / 25 章 · 3000–4000 · 英中對照 · 校訂中

Rain, dust, and distant voices made the capital high council chamber feel smaller than it was. Xun Mo arrived with a plan, then saw a detail that the plan had not accounted for. Noble families dismissed the reset testimony because every official archive appeared to agree with one history. The difference between guessing and checking suddenly seemed enormous. A person who sounded brave could still be mistaken; a person who admitted uncertainty could still save the group. Xun Mo chose the second kind of courage. One witness promised to accuse if conditions improved, but Xun Mo would not treat hope as evidence. The working file contained a separate entry for assurance, and Xun Mo asked who had verified it. Xun Mo asked whether the team could boast under the safety rules; the answer was not yet clear. A witness mentioned commerce during the interview, so the team recorded the statement without endorsing it.

雨聲、灰塵與遠處的人聲，讓首都高等議會議事廳顯得格外狹窄。荀墨帶著計畫抵達，卻看見計畫沒有考慮到的細節。貴族家族因官方檔案都只承認一種歷史，駁回重開證詞。猜測和查證的差別突然變得很大。聽起來勇敢的人也可能犯錯，敢承認不確定的人反而可能救下整個團隊。荀墨決定選擇後者。一名見證人答應條件改善就會「指控」，荀墨卻不能把希望當成證據。工作檔案為「保證」設了獨立項目，荀墨詢問是誰查證過。荀墨詢問小隊能否依安全規則「吹噓」，答案仍不明朗。見證人在訪談中提到「商業」，小隊照實記錄，卻沒有直接認同。

Lan Ge brought the next piece of information on a sheet that had been folded several times. Lan Ge presented the separate cliff, mine, and border tests as present-world evidence. The team compared its date with what they had witnessed for themselves. One line was persuasive; another contradicted the first. Xun Mo asked for the original source instead of selecting whichever version made their work easier. Even a genuine clue could be dangerous when removed from its context. The disputed notice used the word consistent, but its author had not signed the page. A written instruction told the staff to contribute before the deadline, but nobody had logged the result. Lan Ge found a reference to convention in the old notes, but the page gave no reliable date. The old manual told its readers to expose, and Lan Ge wanted to know who had written that rule.

嵐歌把下一份資料帶來，紙張已被折過好幾次。嵐歌呈上懸崖、礦坑與邊境三項當前世界的獨立測試。小隊把上面的日期與親眼所見逐項比較。一行文字很有說服力，下一行卻與它矛盾。荀墨沒有挑比較方便的版本，而是要求查看原始來源。即使是真的線索，一旦脫離脈絡也可能造成危險。有爭議的通知使用「前後一致的」一詞，作者卻沒有在頁面簽名。書面指示要求工作人員在期限前「貢獻」，卻沒有人記下結果。嵐歌在舊筆記裡找到關於「大會；集會」的記載，但紙上沒有可信的日期。舊手冊要求讀者「揭露」，嵐歌想知道這條規則由誰制定。

An argument broke out before anyone could announce a solution. Archivist Bai tried to move the debate into a closed royal meeting. Xun Mo did not win it with a long speech. Instead, the team drew two columns: what the record established and what remained uncertain. Archivist Bai challenged their interpretation, which was uncomfortable but useful. A plan that collapsed under one honest question was not ready to protect anyone outside the room. The evidence list included hurricane, although nobody had explained why that detail mattered. The working file contained a separate entry for hydrogen, and Xun Mo asked who had verified it. Lan Ge found a reference to Ignorance in the old notes, but the page gave no reliable date. Someone had marked the warning informative; Lan Ge checked whether that description was accurate.

尚未有人宣布解法，爭論便已爆發。白典守試圖把辯論移到不公開的王室會議。荀墨沒有用長篇演說壓倒對方，而是在紙上分成兩欄：紀錄已證實的事，以及仍然不確定的事。白典守質疑他們的解讀，雖然令人不快，卻也有用。禁不起一次誠實提問的計畫，還不能拿去保護門外的人。證物清單上有「颶風」，卻沒有人解釋這個細節為何重要。工作檔案為「氫」設了獨立項目，荀墨詢問是誰查證過。嵐歌在舊筆記裡找到關於「無知」的記載，但紙上沒有可信的日期。有人把警告標成「提供資訊的」，嵐歌則查證這種描述是否準確。

Somewhere beyond the capital high council chamber, families were living a day they believed had never occurred before. Xun Mo felt the weight of remembering similar days that had ended in disaster. But he also knew their present choices were not merely copies of an earlier world. Lan Ge asked him to stop describing people as characters in a script. Each person could answer for themselves, if the machines and rulers would leave them enough time.

在首都高等議會議事廳外，許多家庭正過著他們以為第一次發生的日子。荀墨背負著相似日子曾以災難結束的記憶，但他也明白當下的人不是過往世界的複製品。嵐歌請他別再把人說成劇本角色。只要機器與統治者願意給人時間，每個人都能自己回答。

Nothing in the trial worked exactly as predicted. Xun Mo insisted that villages affected by the altar had a right to observe the hearing. Xun Mo insisted on keeping the unsuccessful measurements instead of replacing them with cleaner numbers. That choice annoyed Archivist Bai, who wanted a quick announcement. But the failure established a boundary they had only guessed at before. A useful boundary was better than a promise built on wishful thinking. The disputed notice used the word loyal, but its author had not signed the page. The working file contained a separate entry for manufacture, and Xun Mo asked who had verified it. Lan Ge found a reference to misfortune in the old notes, but the page gave no reliable date. A witness mentioned recovery during the interview, so the team recorded the statement without endorsing it.

這次測試沒有一項完全照預測發展。荀墨堅持受月壇影響的村落有權旁聽。荀墨堅持留下不成功的測量值，不用好看的數字替換。白典守急著發公告，因此相當不滿；然而失敗讓他們找到了先前只能猜測的限制。清楚的界線，總比建立在一廂情願上的承諾可靠。有爭議的通知使用「忠於」一詞，作者卻沒有在頁面簽名。工作檔案為「製造」設了獨立項目，荀墨詢問是誰查證過。嵐歌在舊筆記裡找到關於「災禍；不幸」的記載，但紙上沒有可信的日期。見證人在訪談中提到「復原」，小隊照實記錄，卻沒有直接認同。

When evening arrived, the immediate danger had changed shape rather than vanished. A council clerk secretly sent them a list of erased votes. Xun Mo gathered the others and repeated only the facts they could support. Some people would need more time. Others had already decided to leave. Neither choice made anyone less worthy of protection. The next step belonged to those who would live with its consequences, not merely to the person speaking loudest. The evidence list included relaxation, although nobody had explained why that detail mattered. The working file contained a separate entry for resistance, and Xun Mo asked who had verified it. Lan Ge found a reference to skyscraper in the old notes, but the page gave no reliable date. Someone had marked the warning troublesome; Lan Ge checked whether that description was accurate.

傍晚到來時，眼前的危險只是改變形態，並未完全消失。議會書記私下交出一份被刪除的投票名單。荀墨召集同伴，只重述有證據支持的事。有人還需要時間，也有人已決定離開；這兩種選擇都不會使任何人失去受保護的資格。下一步應由承擔後果的人決定，而不是交給聲音最大的人。證物清單上有「休息時間」，卻沒有人解釋這個細節為何重要。工作檔案為「阻力」設了獨立項目，荀墨詢問是誰查證過。嵐歌在舊筆記裡找到關於「摩天大樓」的記載，但紙上沒有可信的日期。有人把警告標成「棘手的」，嵐歌則查證這種描述是否準確。
