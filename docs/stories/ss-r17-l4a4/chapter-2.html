A King's Order With No Date

Rain, dust, and distant voices made the eastern palace guardhouse feel smaller than it was. Xun Mo arrived with a plan, then saw a detail that the plan had not accounted for. The king issued an arrest order for a murder Xun Mo remembered committing in an earlier cycle. The difference between guessing and checking suddenly seemed enormous. A person who sounded brave could still be mistaken; a person who admitted uncertainty could still save the group. Xun Mo chose the second kind of courage. A witness mentioned autograph during the interview, so the team recorded the statement without endorsing it. One witness promised to carve if conditions improved, but Xun Mo would not treat hope as evidence. The working file contained a separate entry for convenience, and Xun Mo asked who had verified it. Lan Ge found a reference to cruelty in the old notes, but the page gave no reliable date.

雨聲、灰塵與遠處的人聲，讓東宮守衛所顯得格外狹窄。荀墨帶著計畫抵達，卻看見計畫沒有考慮到的細節。國王為一宗荀墨只在前一輪犯下的殺人案發出逮捕令。猜測和查證的差別突然變得很大。聽起來勇敢的人也可能犯錯，敢承認不確定的人反而可能救下整個團隊。荀墨決定選擇後者。見證人在訪談中提到「親筆簽名」，小隊照實記錄，卻沒有直接認同。一名見證人答應條件改善就會「刻出」，荀墨卻不能把希望當成證據。工作檔案為「便利」設了獨立項目，荀墨詢問是誰查證過。嵐歌在舊筆記裡找到關於「殘忍」的記載，但紙上沒有可信的日期。

Lan Ge brought the next piece of information on a sheet that had been folded several times. Lan Ge found that the current victim was still alive and working in the palace kitchens. The team compared its date with what they had witnessed for themselves. One line was persuasive; another contradicted the first. Xun Mo asked for the original source instead of selecting whichever version made their work easier. Even a genuine clue could be dangerous when removed from its context. The old manual told its readers to disguise, and Lan Ge wanted to know who had written that rule. The disputed notice used the word elastic, but its author had not signed the page. An inspector described the situation as explosive; Xun Mo wanted measurements instead of adjectives. Lan Ge found a reference to feedback in the old notes, but the page gave no reliable date.

嵐歌把下一份資料帶來，紙張已被折過好幾次。嵐歌查到這一輪的受害者仍活著，正在宮廷廚房工作。小隊把上面的日期與親眼所見逐項比較。一行文字很有說服力，下一行卻與它矛盾。荀墨沒有挑比較方便的版本，而是要求查看原始來源。即使是真的線索，一旦脫離脈絡也可能造成危險。舊手冊要求讀者「偽裝」，嵐歌想知道這條規則由誰制定。有爭議的通知使用「有彈性的」一詞，作者卻沒有在頁面簽名。檢查員把情況形容為「爆炸性的」，荀墨卻想要數據，而不是形容詞。嵐歌在舊筆記裡找到關於「回饋」的記載，但紙上沒有可信的日期。

An argument broke out before anyone could announce a solution. Archivist Bai argued that Xun Mo's future guilt justified immediate execution. Xun Mo did not win it with a long speech. Instead, the team drew two columns: what the record established and what remained uncertain. Archivist Bai challenged their interpretation, which was uncomfortable but useful. A plan that collapsed under one honest question was not ready to protect anyone outside the room. A witness mentioned immigrant during the interview, so the team recorded the statement without endorsing it. The disputed notice used the word initial, but its author had not signed the page. The working file contained a separate entry for mischief, and Xun Mo asked who had verified it. Lan Ge found a reference to paw in the old notes, but the page gave no reliable date.

尚未有人宣布解法，爭論便已爆發。白典守認為荀墨未來可能犯罪，就足以立即處決他。荀墨沒有用長篇演說壓倒對方，而是在紙上分成兩欄：紀錄已證實的事，以及仍然不確定的事。白典守質疑他們的解讀，雖然令人不快，卻也有用。禁不起一次誠實提問的計畫，還不能拿去保護門外的人。見證人在訪談中提到「移民」，小隊照實記錄，卻沒有直接認同。有爭議的通知使用「最初的」一詞，作者卻沒有在頁面簽名。工作檔案為「惡作劇」設了獨立項目，荀墨詢問是誰查證過。嵐歌在舊筆記裡找到關於「爪子」的記載，但紙上沒有可信的日期。

Somewhere beyond the eastern palace guardhouse, families were living a day they believed had never occurred before. Xun Mo felt the weight of remembering similar days that had ended in disaster. But he also knew their present choices were not merely copies of an earlier world. Lan Ge asked him to stop describing people as characters in a script. Each person could answer for themselves, if the machines and rulers would leave them enough time.

在東宮守衛所外，許多家庭正過著他們以為第一次發生的日子。荀墨背負著相似日子曾以災難結束的記憶，但他也明白當下的人不是過往世界的複製品。嵐歌請他別再把人說成劇本角色。只要機器與統治者願意給人時間，每個人都能自己回答。

Nothing in the trial worked exactly as predicted. Xun Mo admitted the wrong he had done before without claiming that memory was legal proof now. Xun Mo insisted on keeping the unsuccessful measurements instead of replacing them with cleaner numbers. That choice annoyed Archivist Bai, who wanted a quick announcement. But the failure established a boundary they had only guessed at before. A useful boundary was better than a promise built on wishful thinking. A witness mentioned prevention during the interview, so the team recorded the statement without endorsing it. The evidence list included privileged, although nobody had explained why that detail mattered. An inspector described the situation as prominent; Xun Mo wanted measurements instead of adjectives. Xun Mo asked whether the team could prosper under the safety rules; the answer was not yet clear.

這次測試沒有一項完全照預測發展。荀墨承認自己前一輪做過的錯事，卻不把記憶當成這一輪的法律證據。荀墨堅持留下不成功的測量值，不用好看的數字替換。白典守急著發公告，因此相當不滿；然而失敗讓他們找到了先前只能猜測的限制。清楚的界線，總比建立在一廂情願上的承諾可靠。見證人在訪談中提到「預防」，小隊照實記錄，卻沒有直接認同。證物清單上有「特權」，卻沒有人解釋這個細節為何重要。檢查員把情況形容為「醒目的」，荀墨卻想要數據，而不是形容詞。荀墨詢問小隊能否依安全規則「繁榮」，答案仍不明朗。

When evening arrived, the immediate danger had changed shape rather than vanished. The king postponed the execution but demanded a test of the villain's warning. Xun Mo gathered the others and repeated only the facts they could support. Some people would need more time. Others had already decided to leave. Neither choice made anyone less worthy of protection. The next step belonged to those who would live with its consequences, not merely to the person speaking loudest. A witness mentioned remark during the interview, so the team recorded the statement without endorsing it. One witness promised to ruin if conditions improved, but Xun Mo would not treat hope as evidence. The working file contained a separate entry for shifted, and Xun Mo asked who had verified it. Lan Ge found a reference to timetable in the old notes, but the page gave no reliable date.

傍晚到來時，眼前的危險只是改變形態，並未完全消失。國王延後處決，要求驗證反派的警告。荀墨召集同伴，只重述有證據支持的事。有人還需要時間，也有人已決定離開；這兩種選擇都不會使任何人失去受保護的資格。下一步應由承擔後果的人決定，而不是交給聲音最大的人。見證人在訪談中提到「評論；言語」，小隊照實記錄，卻沒有直接認同。一名見證人答應條件改善就會「毀壞」，荀墨卻不能把希望當成證據。工作檔案為「輪班」設了獨立項目，荀墨詢問是誰查證過。嵐歌在舊筆記裡找到關於「時刻表」的記載，但紙上沒有可信的日期。
