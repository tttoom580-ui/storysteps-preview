鹽礦救援

Rescue at the Salt Mine

只有反派記得世界重開 · 第 8 / 25 章 · 3000–4000 · 英中對照 · 校訂中

Rain, dust, and distant voices made the abandoned salt mine galleries feel smaller than it was. Xun Mo arrived with a plan, then saw a detail that the plan had not accounted for. Miners were trapped when guards sealed a gate to preserve the altar's secret supply route. The difference between guessing and checking suddenly seemed enormous. A person who sounded brave could still be mistaken; a person who admitted uncertainty could still save the group. Xun Mo chose the second kind of courage. The working file contained a separate entry for ancestors, and Xun Mo asked who had verified it. Lan Ge found a reference to Contrary in the old notes, but the page gave no reliable date. A witness mentioned craft during the interview, so the team recorded the statement without endorsing it. The evidence list included crush, although nobody had explained why that detail mattered.

雨聲、灰塵與遠處的人聲，讓廢棄鹽礦坑道顯得格外狹窄。荀墨帶著計畫抵達，卻看見計畫沒有考慮到的細節。守衛為保護月壇祕密補給路而封門，導致礦工受困。猜測和查證的差別突然變得很大。聽起來勇敢的人也可能犯錯，敢承認不確定的人反而可能救下整個團隊。荀墨決定選擇後者。工作檔案為「祖先」設了獨立項目，荀墨詢問是誰查證過。嵐歌在舊筆記裡找到關於「正好相反」的記載，但紙上沒有可信的日期。見證人在訪談中提到「手工技藝」，小隊照實記錄，卻沒有直接認同。證物清單上有「擠壓；造成壓傷」，卻沒有人解釋這個細節為何重要。

Lan Ge brought the next piece of information on a sheet that had been folded several times. Lan Ge checked the mine's old ventilation plan and found a route safe enough for trained rescuers. The team compared its date with what they had witnessed for themselves. One line was persuasive; another contradicted the first. Xun Mo asked for the original source instead of selecting whichever version made their work easier. Even a genuine clue could be dangerous when removed from its context. An inspector described the situation as defensible; Xun Mo wanted measurements instead of adjectives. The report called the arrangement distinguished, a judgment the group refused to accept without proof. The old manual told its readers to equip, and Lan Ge wanted to know who had written that rule. The evidence list included guilt, although nobody had explained why that detail mattered.

嵐歌把下一份資料帶來，紙張已被折過好幾次。嵐歌檢查舊通風圖，找到受訓救援者可以使用的路線。小隊把上面的日期與親眼所見逐項比較。一行文字很有說服力，下一行卻與它矛盾。荀墨沒有挑比較方便的版本，而是要求查看原始來源。即使是真的線索，一旦脫離脈絡也可能造成危險。檢查員把情況形容為「有充分理由的」，荀墨卻想要數據，而不是形容詞。報告說這項安排「傑出的；精湛的」，小隊不願在沒有證據前接受此判斷。舊手冊要求讀者「器材／設備」，嵐歌想知道這條規則由誰制定。證物清單上有「愧疚」，卻沒有人解釋這個細節為何重要。

An argument broke out before anyone could announce a solution. Archivist Bai claimed the miners were enemies of the crown because they had entered a forbidden area. Xun Mo did not win it with a long speech. Instead, the team drew two columns: what the record established and what remained uncertain. Archivist Bai challenged their interpretation, which was uncomfortable but useful. A plan that collapsed under one honest question was not ready to protect anyone outside the room. The working file contained a separate entry for handwriting, and Xun Mo asked who had verified it. Lan Ge found a reference to hose in the old notes, but the page gave no reliable date. Someone had marked the warning identical; Lan Ge checked whether that description was accurate. One witness promised to involve if conditions improved, but Xun Mo would not treat hope as evidence.

尚未有人宣布解法，爭論便已爆發。白典守稱礦工進入禁區就是王室之敵。荀墨沒有用長篇演說壓倒對方，而是在紙上分成兩欄：紀錄已證實的事，以及仍然不確定的事。白典守質疑他們的解讀，雖然令人不快，卻也有用。禁不起一次誠實提問的計畫，還不能拿去保護門外的人。工作檔案為「字跡」設了獨立項目，荀墨詢問是誰查證過。嵐歌在舊筆記裡找到關於「水管」的記載，但紙上沒有可信的日期。有人把警告標成「完全相同的」，嵐歌則查證這種描述是否準確。一名見證人答應條件改善就會「涉及；參與」，荀墨卻不能把希望當成證據。

Somewhere beyond the abandoned salt mine galleries, families were living a day they believed had never occurred before. Xun Mo felt the weight of remembering similar days that had ended in disaster. But he also knew their present choices were not merely copies of an earlier world. Lan Ge asked him to stop describing people as characters in a script. Each person could answer for themselves, if the machines and rulers would leave them enough time.

在廢棄鹽礦坑道外，許多家庭正過著他們以為第一次發生的日子。荀墨背負著相似日子曾以災難結束的記憶，但他也明白當下的人不是過往世界的複製品。嵐歌請他別再把人說成劇本角色。只要機器與統治者願意給人時間，每個人都能自己回答。

Nothing in the trial worked exactly as predicted. Xun Mo helped carry supplies under Lan Ge's command instead of taking control. Xun Mo insisted on keeping the unsuccessful measurements instead of replacing them with cleaner numbers. That choice annoyed Archivist Bai, who wanted a quick announcement. But the failure established a boundary they had only guessed at before. A useful boundary was better than a promise built on wishful thinking. A written instruction told the staff to launch before the deadline, but nobody had logged the result. The report called the arrangement lousy, a judgment the group refused to accept without proof. The old manual told its readers to misunderstand, and Lan Ge wanted to know who had written that rule. The evidence list included pregnancy, although nobody had explained why that detail mattered.

這次測試沒有一項完全照預測發展。荀墨在嵐歌指揮下搬運物資，沒有奪走指揮權。荀墨堅持留下不成功的測量值，不用好看的數字替換。白典守急著發公告，因此相當不滿；然而失敗讓他們找到了先前只能猜測的限制。清楚的界線，總比建立在一廂情願上的承諾可靠。書面指示要求工作人員在期限前「推出；啟動」，卻沒有人記下結果。報告說這項安排「品質差的」，小隊不願在沒有證據前接受此判斷。舊手冊要求讀者「誤會」，嵐歌想知道這條規則由誰制定。證物清單上有「懷孕」，卻沒有人解釋這個細節為何重要。

When evening arrived, the immediate danger had changed shape rather than vanished. A rescued miner recognized the machine's symbol from ordinary taxes paid to the palace. Xun Mo gathered the others and repeated only the facts they could support. Some people would need more time. Others had already decided to leave. Neither choice made anyone less worthy of protection. The next step belonged to those who would live with its consequences, not merely to the person speaking loudest. The working file contained a separate entry for riddle, and Xun Mo asked who had verified it. Lan Ge found a reference to stroke in the old notes, but the page gave no reliable date. A witness mentioned suggestion during the interview, so the team recorded the statement without endorsing it. The evidence list included technician, although nobody had explained why that detail mattered.

傍晚到來時，眼前的危險只是改變形態，並未完全消失。獲救礦工認出機器標誌，原來它還出現在繳給王宮的普通稅單上。荀墨召集同伴，只重述有證據支持的事。有人還需要時間，也有人已決定離開；這兩種選擇都不會使任何人失去受保護的資格。下一步應由承擔後果的人決定，而不是交給聲音最大的人。工作檔案為「謎題」設了獨立項目，荀墨詢問是誰查證過。嵐歌在舊筆記裡找到關於「一筆／中風」的記載，但紙上沒有可信的日期。見證人在訪談中提到「建議」，小隊照實記錄，卻沒有直接認同。證物清單上有「技術員」，卻沒有人解釋這個細節為何重要。
