The day began inside the northern cliff path. Xun Mo had come expecting an ordinary task, but the room already contained the sort of silence that follows bad news. Xun Mo predicted a hidden bell would ring before a rockslide destroyed the supply road. Nobody could afford to confuse a confident explanation with a reliable observation. They had made that mistake before, and the cost was still visible in the people waiting outside. Xun Mo opened the notebook and decided to write down what could actually be tested. The disputed notice used the word accidental, but its author had not signed the page. The working file contained a separate entry for barrier, and Xun Mo asked who had verified it. Xun Mo asked whether the team could blink under the safety rules; the answer was not yet clear. Someone had marked the warning brutal; Lan Ge checked whether that description was accurate.

這一天從北方懸崖小徑開始。荀墨原以為只是處理例行工作，房間卻靜得像剛傳出壞消息。荀墨預告隱藏的鐘聲將在落石摧毀補給路前響起。他們不能把說得肯定的解釋誤當成可靠觀察。大家以前犯過這種錯，代價仍寫在門外那些等待者的表情上。荀墨打開筆記本，決定只記下真正能夠驗證的事情。有爭議的通知使用「意外的」一詞，作者卻沒有在頁面簽名。工作檔案為「柵欄」設了獨立項目，荀墨詢問是誰查證過。荀墨詢問小隊能否依安全規則「眨眼」，答案仍不明朗。有人把警告標成「粗獷而堅固的」，嵐歌則查證這種描述是否準確。

A new message reached Lan Ge while the group was still checking its first result. Lan Ge placed independent observers near the cliff without telling them the prediction. Xun Mo refused to celebrate too early. They had learned that two records could repeat the same lie when both came from a single office. Before treating the discovery as independent evidence, the group searched for a witness who had nothing to gain by agreeing with them. One witness promised to choke if conditions improved, but Xun Mo would not treat hope as evidence. The working file contained a separate entry for composition, and Xun Mo asked who had verified it. Xun Mo asked whether the team could evaluate under the safety rules; the answer was not yet clear. A witness mentioned gratitude during the interview, so the team recorded the statement without endorsing it.

小隊還在核對第一項結果時，嵐歌就收到新的消息。嵐歌安排獨立觀察者守在懸崖旁，沒有告訴他們預測內容。荀墨不肯太早慶祝。他們已學到，兩份紀錄如果來自同一辦公室，也可能只是重複同一個謊言。要把發現視為獨立證據前，大家先去尋找一個不會因附和他們而得利的見證人。一名見證人答應條件改善就會「阻斷／堵住」，荀墨卻不能把希望當成證據。工作檔案為「結構；組成」設了獨立項目，荀墨詢問是誰查證過。荀墨詢問小隊能否依安全規則「評估」，答案仍不明朗。見證人在訪談中提到「感激」，小隊照實記錄，卻沒有直接認同。

The easiest choice would have made Xun Mo popular for a single evening. Archivist Bai warned that helping the villain would damage the honor of the royal guard. Lan Ge reminded the group that their decision would still matter tomorrow, after the applause and anger faded. Nobody could offer a guarantee that every person would approve. They could, however, make the reasons public and leave a safe way for a witness to disagree. The evidence list included grave, although nobody had explained why that detail mattered. The working file contained a separate entry for intelligence, and Xun Mo asked who had verified it. The report called the arrangement mild, a judgment the group refused to accept without proof. Someone had marked the warning mountainous; Lan Ge checked whether that description was accurate.

最省事的選擇，或許能讓荀墨當晚受到歡呼。白典守警告幫助反派會玷污王室守衛榮譽。嵐歌提醒大家，掌聲與怒氣消失後，決定明天仍然會造成影響。沒有人能保證所有人都滿意，但至少可以公開理由，並留下見證人安全表達反對的管道。證物清單上有「嚴重的」，卻沒有人解釋這個細節為何重要。工作檔案為「情報／智能」設了獨立項目，荀墨詢問是誰查證過。報告說這項安排「溫和的」，小隊不願在沒有證據前接受此判斷。有人把警告標成「山區的」，嵐歌則查證這種描述是否準確。

Xun Mo remembered people who had died in other versions of the world, but memory did not grant ownership of their lives. Lan Ge could not remember those versions, and she had good reasons not to trust his account. He learned to offer her evidence instead of pleading for belief. It was a small change for someone who had once given orders without listening, and it mattered more than any title the palace might award.

荀墨記得其他版本世界中死去的人，但記憶不代表他擁有那些人的生命。嵐歌不記得先前的世界，也有充分理由不相信他的說法。他逐漸學會提出證據，而非乞求信任。對一個曾不聽人說話、只會下令的人而言，這是微小轉變，卻比王宮授予的頭銜更重要。

They agreed to test a limited part of the plan before touching the main system. Xun Mo used a public warning horn to stop the convoy before the slide. Xun Mo assigned roles according to practical skill rather than confidence. Lan Ge recorded each step, including the moments when someone had to stop. If the trial failed, the notes would explain why. If it worked, the notes would help the next group repeat it without depending on a hero who happened to be present. The evidence list included neglect, although nobody had explained why that detail mattered. A written instruction told the staff to offend before the deadline, but nobody had logged the result. Lan Ge found a reference to philosopher in the old notes, but the page gave no reliable date. A witness mentioned reform during the interview, so the team recorded the statement without endorsing it.

他們同意先做小範圍測試，不直接碰主要系統。荀墨用公共警示號角，在落石前攔下車隊。荀墨依照實際專長分派工作，不看誰說話最有自信。嵐歌記錄每個步驟，連必須停止的時刻也不漏掉。如果測試失敗，筆記能說明原因；如果成功，下一批人也能按紀錄重做，不必依賴恰好在場的英雄。證物清單上有「疏忽」，卻沒有人解釋這個細節為何重要。書面指示要求工作人員在期限前「冒犯」，卻沒有人記下結果。嵐歌在舊筆記裡找到關於「哲學家」的記載，但紙上沒有可信的日期。見證人在訪談中提到「改革」，小隊照實記錄，卻沒有直接認同。

The result was smaller than the promise they had hoped to make, but it was real. The convoy survived, but the bell's stone frame bore a symbol from a forgotten age. Lan Ge closed the notebook only after marking the unresolved questions. Xun Mo was tired of victories that depended on hiding their cost. This one could be examined in daylight. And the detail they had not solved was already pointing toward the next part of the investigation. The evidence list included revenge, although nobody had explained why that detail mattered. The working file contained a separate entry for tendency, and Xun Mo asked who had verified it. Lan Ge found a reference to twig in the old notes, but the page gave no reliable date. A witness mentioned urged during the interview, so the team recorded the statement without endorsing it.

結果比原先希望宣布的成果小，卻是真的。車隊活了下來，鐘的石框卻刻著失落年代的符號。嵐歌直到標出所有未解問題，才闔上筆記本。荀墨早已厭倦靠隱瞞代價換來的勝利；這次的結果可以在日光下接受查證。那個尚未解開的細節，也已指向調查的下一步。證物清單上有「報復」，卻沒有人解釋這個細節為何重要。工作檔案為「傾向」設了獨立項目，荀墨詢問是誰查證過。嵐歌在舊筆記裡找到關於「樹枝」的記載，但紙上沒有可信的日期。見證人在訪談中提到「催促」，小隊照實記錄，卻沒有直接認同。
