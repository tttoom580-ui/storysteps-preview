Rain, dust, and distant voices made the underground royal library feel smaller than it was. Xun Mo arrived with a plan, then saw a detail that the plan had not accounted for. A hidden shelf contained chronicles describing the same year with different endings. The difference between guessing and checking suddenly seemed enormous. A person who sounded brave could still be mistaken; a person who admitted uncertainty could still save the group. Xun Mo chose the second kind of courage. Xun Mo asked whether the team could adopt under the safety rules; the answer was not yet clear. A witness mentioned agency during the interview, so the team recorded the statement without endorsing it. The evidence list included behavior, although nobody had explained why that detail mattered. A written instruction told the staff to cherish before the deadline, but nobody had logged the result.

雨聲、灰塵與遠處的人聲，讓王室地下圖書館顯得格外狹窄。荀墨帶著計畫抵達，卻看見計畫沒有考慮到的細節。一座暗架保存了同一年卻有不同結局的史書。猜測和查證的差別突然變得很大。聽起來勇敢的人也可能犯錯，敢承認不確定的人反而可能救下整個團隊。荀墨決定選擇後者。荀墨詢問小隊能否依安全規則「採用」，答案仍不明朗。見證人在訪談中提到「機構」，小隊照實記錄，卻沒有直接認同。證物清單上有「行為」，卻沒有人解釋這個細節為何重要。書面指示要求工作人員在期限前「珍惜」，卻沒有人記下結果。

Lan Ge brought the next piece of information on a sheet that had been folded several times. Lan Ge verified the books used paper made during reigns that official history said never existed. The team compared its date with what they had witnessed for themselves. One line was persuasive; another contradicted the first. Xun Mo asked for the original source instead of selecting whichever version made their work easier. Even a genuine clue could be dangerous when removed from its context. Lan Ge found a reference to congress in the old notes, but the page gave no reliable date. A witness mentioned conscience during the interview, so the team recorded the statement without endorsing it. The evidence list included decorations, although nobody had explained why that detail mattered. A written instruction told the staff to discourage before the deadline, but nobody had logged the result.

嵐歌把下一份資料帶來，紙張已被折過好幾次。嵐歌驗證紙張來自官方歷史宣稱不存在的王朝。小隊把上面的日期與親眼所見逐項比較。一行文字很有說服力，下一行卻與它矛盾。荀墨沒有挑比較方便的版本，而是要求查看原始來源。即使是真的線索，一旦脫離脈絡也可能造成危險。嵐歌在舊筆記裡找到關於「議會」的記載，但紙上沒有可信的日期。見證人在訪談中提到「良心」，小隊照實記錄，卻沒有直接認同。證物清單上有「裝飾」，卻沒有人解釋這個細節為何重要。書面指示要求工作人員在期限前「使卻步」，卻沒有人記下結果。

An argument broke out before anyone could announce a solution. Archivist Bai ordered the books burned to protect the single accepted account. Xun Mo did not win it with a long speech. Instead, the team drew two columns: what the record established and what remained uncertain. Archivist Bai challenged their interpretation, which was uncomfortable but useful. A plan that collapsed under one honest question was not ready to protect anyone outside the room. The report called the arrangement fundamental, a judgment the group refused to accept without proof. The old manual told its readers to horrify, and Lan Ge wanted to know who had written that rule. The disputed notice used the word influential, but its author had not signed the page. The working file contained a separate entry for interaction, and Xun Mo asked who had verified it.

尚未有人宣布解法，爭論便已爆發。白典守命人焚書，以保護唯一被承認的歷史版本。荀墨沒有用長篇演說壓倒對方，而是在紙上分成兩欄：紀錄已證實的事，以及仍然不確定的事。白典守質疑他們的解讀，雖然令人不快，卻也有用。禁不起一次誠實提問的計畫，還不能拿去保護門外的人。報告說這項安排「根本的；基本的」，小隊不願在沒有證據前接受此判斷。舊手冊要求讀者「使驚恐」，嵐歌想知道這條規則由誰制定。有爭議的通知使用「有影響力的」一詞，作者卻沒有在頁面簽名。工作檔案為「相互作用」設了獨立項目，荀墨詢問是誰查證過。

Somewhere beyond the underground royal library, families were living a day they believed had never occurred before. Xun Mo felt the weight of remembering similar days that had ended in disaster. But he also knew their present choices were not merely copies of an earlier world. Lan Ge asked him to stop describing people as characters in a script. Each person could answer for themselves, if the machines and rulers would leave them enough time.

在王室地下圖書館外，許多家庭正過著他們以為第一次發生的日子。荀墨背負著相似日子曾以災難結束的記憶，但他也明白當下的人不是過往世界的複製品。嵐歌請他別再把人說成劇本角色。只要機器與統治者願意給人時間，每個人都能自己回答。

Nothing in the trial worked exactly as predicted. Xun Mo carried copies to a neutral monastery rather than claiming the books belonged to him. Xun Mo insisted on keeping the unsuccessful measurements instead of replacing them with cleaner numbers. That choice annoyed Archivist Bai, who wanted a quick announcement. But the failure established a boundary they had only guessed at before. A useful boundary was better than a promise built on wishful thinking. Lan Ge found a reference to outcome in the old notes, but the page gave no reliable date. Someone had marked the warning primitive; Lan Ge checked whether that description was accurate. The evidence list included reception, although nobody had explained why that detail mattered. The working file contained a separate entry for robber, and Xun Mo asked who had verified it.

這次測試沒有一項完全照預測發展。荀墨把影本送往中立修道院，沒有宣稱書應歸自己所有。荀墨堅持留下不成功的測量值，不用好看的數字替換。白典守急著發公告，因此相當不滿；然而失敗讓他們找到了先前只能猜測的限制。清楚的界線，總比建立在一廂情願上的承諾可靠。嵐歌在舊筆記裡找到關於「結果」的記載，但紙上沒有可信的日期。有人把警告標成「古早的／原始的」，嵐歌則查證這種描述是否準確。證物清單上有「接待會」，卻沒有人解釋這個細節為何重要。工作檔案為「強盜」設了獨立項目，荀墨詢問是誰查證過。

When evening arrived, the immediate danger had changed shape rather than vanished. A drawing in the final book showed a machine under the moon altar. Xun Mo gathered the others and repeated only the facts they could support. Some people would need more time. Others had already decided to leave. Neither choice made anyone less worthy of protection. The next step belonged to those who would live with its consequences, not merely to the person speaking loudest. Lan Ge found a reference to scenery in the old notes, but the page gave no reliable date. Someone had marked the warning shameful; Lan Ge checked whether that description was accurate. The evidence list included terror, although nobody had explained why that detail mattered. The working file contained a separate entry for wizard, and Xun Mo asked who had verified it.

傍晚到來時，眼前的危險只是改變形態，並未完全消失。最後一本書畫著月壇下方的巨大機器。荀墨召集同伴，只重述有證據支持的事。有人還需要時間，也有人已決定離開；這兩種選擇都不會使任何人失去受保護的資格。下一步應由承擔後果的人決定，而不是交給聲音最大的人。嵐歌在舊筆記裡找到關於「舞臺布景」的記載，但紙上沒有可信的日期。有人把警告標成「可恥的」，嵐歌則查證這種描述是否準確。證物清單上有「恐懼」，卻沒有人解釋這個細節為何重要。工作檔案為「巫師」設了獨立項目，荀墨詢問是誰查證過。
