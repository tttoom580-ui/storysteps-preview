在山中鎖鏈機房，麻煩不是從尖叫或警報開始，而是從一件大家自認了解的小事產生分歧。每當世界被迫重開，一條龍逐漸衰弱的心跳就會為月壇供能。荀墨先聽周遭人的說法，沒有急著下判斷。群眾想找到簡單的壞人時，謠言總比仔細提問傳得快。即使最喜歡的推測最後被推翻，小隊也需要留下仍然有用的紀錄。證物清單上有「準確度」，卻沒有人解釋這個細節為何重要。書面指示要求工作人員在期限前「調整」，卻沒有人記下結果。荀墨詢問小隊能否依安全規則「分析」，答案仍不明朗。見證人在訪談中提到「競爭者」，小隊照實記錄，卻沒有直接認同。

The oldest document on the table looked less important than the newest screen. That appearance was misleading. Lan Ge found records showing the dragon had never agreed to serve as a power source. Lan Ge read the handwritten notes aloud while Xun Mo checked the sequence of events. Their different skills mattered more than either person's rank. When they found a contradiction, they preserved both versions. Destroying an inconvenient record would only make the next investigation harder. By afternoon, the discovery had reached the people who could lose the most. Archivist Bai said the sacrifice was necessary to protect the kingdom from its enemies. Archivist Bai offered a quick settlement, but it required the group to stop asking questions. Xun Mo considered the frightened faces around the room. It would be easy to sound heroic and demand that everyone take the same risk. Instead, each person was allowed to describe what they were prepared to do and what they would refuse. One witness promised to injure if conditions improved, but Xun Mo would not treat hope as evidence. An inspector described the situation as intense; Xun Mo wanted measurements instead of adjectives. Lan Ge found a reference to mule in the old notes, but the page gave no reliable date. Someone had marked the warning mutual; Lan Ge checked whether that description was accurate.

到了下午，這項發現已傳到最可能蒙受損失的人耳中。白典守說為保護王國免於敵人入侵，犧牲不可避免。白典守提出快速和解，條件卻是所有人不准再追問。荀墨看著房內不安的臉孔。此時說些英雄話、要求大家冒一樣的風險很容易；他們卻讓每個人自己說明願意做什麼，以及拒絕承擔什麼。一名見證人答應條件改善就會「使受傷」，荀墨卻不能把希望當成證據。檢查員把情況形容為「激烈的」，荀墨卻想要數據，而不是形容詞。嵐歌在舊筆記裡找到關於「騾子」的記載，但紙上沒有可信的日期。有人把警告標成「共同的」，嵐歌則查證這種描述是否準確。

The palace called one person a hero and another a villain as though both roles were fixed before birth. Yet the road beneath their feet held evidence of choices neither title could explain. Xun Mo had done cruel things, and acknowledging them was not a spell that erased their cost. Lan Ge watched what he did when the next choice became difficult. She trusted actions enough to check them, not enough to surrender her judgment.

王宮把人分成英雄與反派，彷彿角色出生前就已注定。然而腳下道路留下的選擇證據，無法用任何稱號解釋。荀墨確實做過殘酷的事，承認錯誤也不是抹去代價的魔法。嵐歌觀察他面對下一個難題時怎麼選擇。她願意查核他的行動，卻不會因此交出自己的判斷。

The streets outside the mountain's chained engine room were quieter, but quiet was not proof of safety. The dragon said it remembered pain but not the worlds its pain had created. Xun Mo checked that everyone knew how to ask for help before the group separated. They had gained one answer and inherited two new responsibilities. Tomorrow's work would require patience from people who had every reason to be angry. For the first time that day, the team had a direction worth following. The disputed notice used the word remarkable, but its author had not signed the page. An inspector described the situation as slight; Xun Mo wanted measurements instead of adjectives. Lan Ge found a reference to surroundings in the old notes, but the page gave no reliable date. 山中鎖鏈機房外的街道變安靜了，但安靜不代表安全。龍說自己記得痛，卻不記得痛苦創造的那些世界。荀墨先確認大家知道如何求助，才讓小隊分開。他們得到一個答案，也多了兩份責任。明天的工作需要那些本來就有理由憤怒的人保持耐心。至少在這一天結束時，小隊終於有了一個值得追查的方向。有爭議的通知使用「不尋常的；顯著的」一詞，作者卻沒有在頁面簽名。檢查員把情況形容為「微小的」，荀墨卻想要數據，而不是形容詞。嵐歌在舊筆記裡找到關於「周遭環境」的記載，但紙上沒有可信的日期。見證人在訪談中提到「福利；福祉」，小隊照實記錄，卻沒有直接認同。
