The Soldiers Who Never Hesitated

Morning brought a decision that had already been made for everyone. There was a signature at the bottom of the order, but no space for those most affected to answer. Kellan discovers military recruits whose capacity for empathy was deliberately reduced through the same childhood program. Kellan knew that refusing a bad choice was not the same as knowing the correct one. Before making a promise, the team needed something they could test.

清晨帶來一項替所有人做好的決定。命令底下有簽名，卻沒有讓受影響最深者回答的地方。凱倫發現徵召士兵的同理心曾透過同一套童年計畫遭刻意降低。凱倫知道拒絕壞選項不等於已經知道正確答案。作出承諾前，團隊需要能夠驗證的事物。

Yuna displayed the military trial file on the hearing room wall. The children whose anger had been erased were not represented by scores for peace; they were represented by scores for obedience under simulated combat orders. In another column, reduced empathy was recorded as improved operational efficiency. Kellan felt sick. Every public advertisement had promised that the procedure would prevent violence, yet the state had been preparing people to carry it out without hesitation. Minister Calder insisted that the experiments were a separate program. Yuna showed the matching surgical identifiers and the transfer budget. The same infants had been enrolled in both. Kellan kept his blank consent card on the desk where the cameras could see it.

尤娜把軍事試驗檔投射在聽證室牆上。遭刪除憤怒的孩子沒有被以和平分數衡量，而是按模擬戰鬥命令下的服從程度計分。另一欄把同理心減少記成行動效率提升。凱倫感到噁心。所有公開廣告都承諾手術可以預防暴力，國家卻在培養不會猶豫地執行暴力的人。卡爾德部長堅稱這是獨立計畫。尤娜拿出吻合的手術識別碼與資金轉移紀錄，證明同一批嬰兒被納入兩套試驗。凱倫把自己的空白同意卡留在桌上，讓攝影機清楚拍到。

The overlooked details in the witness file made the question harder, not easier: An old training film used an animate cartoon train to teach children the route beyond the western gate. Simple arithmetic showed that reported train repairs continued long after the official closure date. The students called the corrected graph awesome, then asked how it was made. The railway worker offered celery soup made from vegetables grown in a garden near the sealed gate. Fresh cement around the old gate contradicted claims that no one had maintained it for decades. Yuna marked what could be verified and left the rest open to challenge.

證人檔案裡被忽視的細節使問題更加複雜，而不是更簡單： 一部舊教學影片用栩栩如生的卡通列車教孩子認識西門外路線。 簡單的算術就能看出，列車維修紀錄在官方封閉日期之後仍持續多年。 學生稱更正圖表很棒，接著追問它如何製作。 鐵路維修員拿出芹菜湯，食材種在封閉城門旁的菜園。 舊城門周圍的新水泥，與數十年無人維護的說法矛盾。 尤娜標記能查證的部分，其餘保留給人質疑。

What changed the discussion was a fact that should have been obvious from the start. Minister Calder’s office funded the trial while describing it publicly as crime prevention. Kellan felt the old temptation to turn that fact into an immediate victory. Instead, the team separated what the new evidence established from what it merely suggested. That distinction frustrated the loudest spectators, but it helped the witnesses remain willing to speak again.

改變討論的是一件本來就該看得見的事實。卡爾德部長辦公室資助試驗，卻對外稱是犯罪預防。凱倫一瞬間也想把它當成立刻勝利的證明，團隊卻把新證據真正確立的部分與僅能推測的部分分開。這讓最喧鬧的旁觀者失望，卻使證人願意再次出面。

A second bundle of observations brought the consequences closer to ordinary life: The coastal radio mentioned a confederation of towns that the city's textbooks described as extinct. A public crackdown on unauthorized boats could not substitute for fixing confusing instructions, Yuna argued at the hearing. The gatehouse displayed a crocodile logo identical to the animal on Jun's childhood train poster. The old railway had a dual power system, allowing trains to operate even when city electricity failed. The city could not equalize every skill level, but it could make entry fairer. Nothing in these records excused the people who had made the dangerous decision.

第二疊觀察紀錄使後果貼近普通人的生活： 海岸廣播提到一個城鎮邦聯，城市教科書卻稱其早已消亡。 言秋在聽證時主張，嚴厲取締未經許可的船隻不能取代修正令人困惑的指示。 門衛室裡的鱷魚標誌，與小鈞童年列車海報上的動物相同。 舊鐵路有雙重電力系統，即使城市停電，列車仍能運行。 市府無法讓每個人的技能相同，卻能使入場機會更公平。 紀錄中沒有任何一項能替做出危險決定的人開脫。

No one in the room was permitted to declare that a good intention canceled a harmful action. Kellan insisted on recording the error, identifying who had authorized it, and protecting those affected before assigning wider blame. Yuna drew a simple boundary around what they could do tonight. The line looked disappointingly modest, yet it was the first promise that did not require another person to surrender a right.

室內沒有人能宣稱善意可以抵銷傷害。凱倫堅持記錄錯誤、確認授權者，並在追究更大責任前保護受害者。尤娜畫出今晚能做到的清楚界線。這條線看似微不足道，卻是第一個不要求別人放棄權利的承諾。

The team kept the concrete observations alongside the disputed theory: A council faction argued that opening the gate would endanger the city's fragile social order. The gate engineer feared he would incur criminal penalties merely for preserving an obsolete switch. Kellan said the greatest injustice was forcing people to forget relatives who might still be alive. The unexplained coastal signal deepened the intrigue surrounding the city's false founding history. A false offer of coastal jobs might have been used to lure families onto the evacuation trains. Taken alone, no detail settled the case; together they showed why a public answer mattered.

團隊把具體觀察與有爭議的理論並列保存： 一個議會派系主張開城門會危及城市脆弱的社會秩序。 城門工程師害怕只因保存淘汰轉轍器，就會受到刑事處罰。 凱倫說，最大的冤屈是強迫人們忘記可能仍在世的親人。 無法解釋的海岸訊號，讓城市虛假建城史背後的陰謀更加撲朔迷離。 虛假的海岸工作機會，可能曾被用來引誘家庭登上撤離列車。 單獨看來沒有一項細節能結案，合在一起卻說明了公開答覆為何重要。

In the clinic corridor, parents read the names of emotions as if selecting colors for a room. Kellan hated the neatness of the printed boxes. Yuna reminded him that fear could protect a child and anger could tell a child when a boundary had been crossed. Neither feeling was a defect.

診所走廊裡，家長閱讀情緒名稱的神情，就像挑房間顏色。凱倫痛恨整齊的印刷選項。尤娜提醒他，恐懼可能保護孩子，憤怒也可能告訴孩子界線遭人侵犯。兩者都不是缺陷。

The record continued with small facts that would be lost in a confident speech: Vale called the inspector obstinate for refusing to surrender the old receiver without a warrant. A pickpocket incident near the river prompted extra security at the event. The cleaning crew left on time because the new contracts required punctual shifts. Kellan urged restraint until qualified workers could inspect the old tracks beyond the gate. After twelve hours of testimony, the exhausted witness began to stagger toward the exit, and the chair called a break. Kellan asked the clerk to preserve the original wording rather than improve the story.

紀錄接著列出那些在自信演說中容易消失的小事實： 維爾說檢查員固執，只因他拒絕在沒有令狀時交出舊接收器。 河邊發生扒手事件，活動因此加強保全。 清潔團隊準時下班，因為新合約要求準點輪班。 凱倫呼籲克制，等合格工人檢查城門外舊軌道再行動。 證人作證十二小時後，疲憊得往出口踉蹌，主席因此宣布休息。 凱倫要求書記保存原本措辭，而不是把故事修飾得更好聽。

On the way out, Kellan passed people waiting for a result that would affect their meals, their homes, or their ability to say no. None of them had the luxury of treating the dispute as an intellectual puzzle. Yuna paused to answer a child's practical question before they left. The answer was incomplete, but unlike the old official notice, it admitted the uncertainty out loud.

離開時，凱倫經過等待結果的人群，這項結果將影響他們的餐食、家園或說不的能力。他們不能奢侈地把爭論當成智力謎題。尤娜停下回答一名孩子實際的問題。答案並不完整，卻與舊公告不同：它坦白承認不確定之處。
