不懂恐懼的孩子

The Child Who Could Not Fear

新生兒必須先刪除一種情緒 · 第 4 / 25 章 · 5000–6000 · 英中對照 · 校訂中

Morning brought a decision that had already been made for everyone. There was a signature at the bottom of the order, but no space for those most affected to answer. A six-year-old who had fear removed walks toward an open train platform without recognizing danger. Kellan knew that refusing a bad choice was not the same as knowing the correct one. Before making a promise, the team needed something they could test.

清晨帶來一項替所有人做好的決定。命令底下有簽名，卻沒有讓受影響最深者回答的地方。一名被刪除恐懼的六歲孩子，不懂危險地朝開放式列車月臺走去。凱倫知道拒絕壞選項不等於已經知道正確答案。作出承諾前，團隊需要能夠驗證的事物。

In the clinic corridor, parents read the names of emotions as if selecting colors for a room. Kellan hated the neatness of the printed boxes. Yuna reminded him that fear could protect a child and anger could tell a child when a boundary had been crossed. Neither feeling was a defect.

診所走廊裡，家長閱讀情緒名稱的神情，就像挑房間顏色。凱倫痛恨整齊的印刷選項。尤娜提醒他，恐懼可能保護孩子，憤怒也可能告訴孩子界線遭人侵犯。兩者都不是缺陷。

Yuna arrived with a second account that neither confirmed nor dismissed the first. They compared the routes by which each record had reached the room, then placed the uncertainties beside the facts. Kellan could feel the pressure to announce a solution before anyone became frightened. That pressure was precisely why a second witness mattered: a conclusion that could not survive an independent question would be dangerous, even if it happened to be right.

尤娜帶來第二份說法，既未證實也未推翻第一份。兩人比對紀錄抵達房間的途徑，再把不確定之處和事實並列。凱倫感受到眾人希望立即宣布解方，以免恐懼擴大的壓力。但也正因這種壓力，第二位證人才重要：無法通過獨立質問的結論，即使碰巧正確，也可能很危險。

The overlooked details in the witness file made the question harder, not easier: A museum display falsely described public interest in old photographs as an addiction to dangerous nostalgia. The clean aesthetic of the new galleries concealed the violence of their missing captions. The planner could give only an approximate count of attendees. The curator called his unauthorized work urban archaeology because ordinary history departments refused the materials. Yuna wore a temporary keeper's badge, taking care not to mistake the emblem for unlimited authority. Taken alone, no detail settled the case; together they showed why a public answer mattered.

證人檔案裡被忽視的細節使問題更加複雜，而不是更簡單： 博物館一張展板竟把民眾對舊照片的興趣說成沉迷於危險懷舊。 新展廳整潔的美學風格，掩蓋了遭刪除說明所代表的暴力。 規劃者只能提供來賓人數的大約估計。 館長稱自己的未授權工作是都市考古學，因為正規歷史部門拒收這些資料。 言秋佩戴臨時守護員徽章，謹慎提醒自己這個標誌不等於無限權力。 單獨看來沒有一項細節能結案，合在一起卻說明了公開答覆為何重要。

The contradiction reached the table just as the meeting was about to end. His family is blamed for negligence though the program promised safer children. For a long moment no one spoke. Kellan understood why silence felt comfortable; each answer threatened someone's settled account of how the world worked. Yuna broke the stillness by asking who would be harmed if they simply filed the discovery away. No one could answer without naming a real person.

會議即將結束時，矛盾證據送上桌面。計畫曾承諾孩子會更安全，家屬卻被指控疏忽。一時之間無人開口。凱倫明白沉默為何讓人舒服：任何答案都可能動搖某人對世界的既定理解。尤娜打破沉默，問若把發現歸檔，究竟誰會受害。沒人能在不指名真實人物的情況下回答。

A second bundle of observations brought the consequences closer to ordinary life: The official account mentioned a great blaze, although the station's stone walls showed no fire damage. One young disciple of the state historian copied his claims without checking a single photograph. The public meeting could enlighten residents only if the technical drawings were legible. The guard made a public fuss about camera rules to distract an officer from Yuna's interview. The new museum label carried an implicit threat: remembering the wrong street made someone suspicious. Kellan asked the clerk to preserve the original wording rather than improve the story.

第二疊觀察紀錄使後果貼近普通人的生活： 官方記載提到一場大火，車站石牆卻沒有任何火損。 一名國家史官的年輕門生照抄他的說法，連一張照片都沒核對。 技術圖面必須看得懂，公開會議才能讓居民理解情況。 警衛故意大聲為攝影規定爭論，好分散警員對莎拉訪談的注意。 新館藏標籤暗含威脅：記得錯誤街道的人會遭到懷疑。 凱倫要求書記保存原本措辭，而不是把故事修飾得更好聽。

Kellan had expected the hardest part to be exposing the secret. Instead, it was choosing what to do after a partial truth became public. The people who had been ignored did not all want the same remedy. Yuna proposed that each affected group could submit a written objection and receive a specific answer. The method would take time, but it recognized them as participants rather than scenery.

凱倫原以為最難的是揭露祕密，後來才發現，部分真相公開後如何行動才是難題。曾被忽略的人並不全想要相同補救。尤娜提議每個受影響群體都能提出書面異議並取得具體答覆。方法需要時間，但終於把他們當成參與者，而不是背景。

The team kept the concrete observations alongside the disputed theory: A government historian would preside over the reopening ceremony, though he had never inspected the archive. The museum's prestige depended on public trust, not simply on royal-looking seals and elegant lighting. The council planned to privatize the old station land before anyone could establish its rightful owners. Cold white lights appeared to radiate purity, but their brightness made the erased faces harder to see. The lifeguard rotation had to cover every permitted training hour. Yuna marked what could be verified and left the rest open to challenge.

團隊把具體觀察與有爭議的理論並列保存： 一名從未檢查檔案的政府史官，卻將主持重新開幕典禮。 博物館的聲望來自公眾信任，不是華麗印章與燈光。 議會計畫在確定土地合法所有人之前，把舊車站用地私有化。 冷白燈光似乎散發純淨感，亮度卻讓被擦掉的面孔更難辨認。 救生員輪班必須涵蓋每個核准訓練時段。 尤娜標記能查證的部分，其餘保留給人質疑。

In the clinic corridor, parents read the names of emotions as if selecting colors for a room. Kellan hated the neatness of the printed boxes. Yuna reminded him that fear could protect a child and anger could tell a child when a boundary had been crossed. Neither feeling was a defect.

診所走廊裡，家長閱讀情緒名稱的神情，就像挑房間顏色。凱倫痛恨整齊的印刷選項。尤娜提醒他，恐懼可能保護孩子，憤怒也可能告訴孩子界線遭人侵犯。兩者都不是缺陷。

The record continued with small facts that would be lost in a confident speech: An old electric shaver displayed beside the photographs carried a manufacturer's address from Orchard East. A stray note beneath the glass included the route number of the missing passenger train. Official captions placed a veil of respectable language over the fact that whole families had vanished. A vineyard owner offered land for a safe pullout but required a written agreement. The contractor's warranty still covered the sealed valve, proving the tunnel had been serviced recently. Nothing in these records excused the people who had made the dangerous decision.

紀錄接著列出那些在自信演說中容易消失的小事實： 照片旁展示的老式電鬍刀，標著東果園的製造商地址。 玻璃展示櫃下夾著一張零散紙條，上面寫著失蹤客車的路線編號。 官方說明以體面措辭掩蓋了整個家庭失蹤的事實。 葡萄園主人願提供安全停車用地，但要求書面協議。 承包商的保固仍涵蓋封閉閥門，證明隧道不久前有人維修。 紀錄中沒有任何一項能替做出危險決定的人開脫。

When the lamps were lowered, Kellan returned to a blank emotion consent card. It was neither proof of innocence nor a shortcut to justice. It was a reminder that the next decision would be witnessed by people who no longer trusted easy promises. Yuna remained at the door while the final record was sealed. Outside, the neonatal registration center waited for news that had to be more precise than a victory speech.

燈光調暗時，凱倫再次看向一張空白情緒同意卡。它既不是清白證明，也不是通往正義的捷徑，而是在提醒眾人：下一個決定會由不再相信輕易承諾的人見證。尤娜守在門邊，最後一份紀錄被封存。外頭的新生兒登記中心等待的消息，必須比勝利演說更精確。
