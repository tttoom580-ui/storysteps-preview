By noon, rumors had outrun the facts. Some people wanted a villain; others wanted a hero who could end the argument without asking another question. Kellan studies government happiness scores and finds that missing emotions are counted as proof of successful adjustment. Kellan had worked long enough to distrust both shortcuts. In the neonatal registration center, the first task was to protect the evidence before anyone could turn it into a weapon.

到了中午，謠言早已跑得比事實快。有人想找反派，也有人想找英雄，好在不再追問的情況下結束爭論。凱倫研究政府幸福指標，發現缺乏情緒反應竟被視為調整成功。凱倫工作多年，已經不相信這兩種捷徑。在新生兒登記中心，當務之急是先保護證據，免得任何人將它變成武器。

In the clinic corridor, parents read the names of emotions as if selecting colors for a room. Kellan hated the neatness of the printed boxes. Yuna reminded him that fear could protect a child and anger could tell a child when a boundary had been crossed. Neither feeling was a defect.

診所走廊裡，家長閱讀情緒名稱的神情，就像挑房間顏色。凱倫痛恨整齊的印刷選項。尤娜提醒他，恐懼可能保護孩子，憤怒也可能告訴孩子界線遭人侵犯。兩者都不是缺陷。

The argument was not merely about who held authority. It was about what happened when an official decision met a household, a workplace, or a body that could not obey it safely. Kellan asked Yuna to describe the smallest practical consequence. Only then did they realize that the proposed remedy would reproduce the very harm it claimed to prevent. They changed the plan before presenting it in public.

爭論不只是誰有權威，而是官方決定落到家庭、職場或無法安全服從的身體上時會發生什麼。凱倫請尤娜說明最小、最實際的後果。他們這才發現提議中的補救會重複它聲稱要防止的傷害，因此在公開發表前修改了計畫。

The overlooked details in the witness file made the question harder, not easier: The defective fastener was sold as an accessory, not part of the main coat. The archived report described a breakdown in the relay safeguards during the first citywide memory trial. Ivo could bypass the shutter motor, but he refused to endanger the receptionist without asking. A photograph showed officials raising champagne glasses beside the first experimental sleep relay. The paper archive smelled faintly of chestnut polish, a scent Yuna associated with her mother's workbench. Kellan asked the clerk to preserve the original wording rather than improve the story.

證人檔案裡被忽視的細節使問題更加複雜，而不是更簡單： 瑕疵扣件被當作配件販售，而非斗篷本體。 封存報告描述第一次全城記憶測試時，中繼器的安全機制曾故障。 伊沃能繞過百葉窗馬達，卻拒絕未經詢問就使接待員陷入危險。 一張照片裡，官員在第一座實驗性睡眠中繼器旁舉起香檳杯。 紙本檔案帶著淡淡栗木蠟氣味，讓言秋想起母親的工作桌。 凱倫要求書記保存原本措辭，而不是把故事修飾得更好聽。

What changed the discussion was a fact that should have been obvious from the start. A statistician admits that complaints were excluded from the official dataset. Kellan felt the old temptation to turn that fact into an immediate victory. Instead, the team separated what the new evidence established from what it merely suggested. That distinction frustrated the loudest spectators, but it helped the witnesses remain willing to speak again.

改變討論的是一件本來就該看得見的事實。統計師承認人民抱怨被排除於官方資料集。凱倫一瞬間也想把它當成立刻勝利的證明，團隊卻把新證據真正確立的部分與僅能推測的部分分開。這讓最喧鬧的旁觀者失望，卻使證人願意再次出面。

A second bundle of observations brought the consequences closer to ordinary life: Vale called the safety engineers cowardly because they had refused to approve a dangerous expansion. A blue thread tied to the warning sheet could evoke Kellan's childhood memory of her mother's hands. The contractor's abandoned cafeteria still displayed a menu for roast fowl from the founding banquet. The receptionist's voice grew hoarse as she explained how many records were scheduled for destruction. Kellan recognized the citrus lotion her mother had used from a stain inside the old engineer's glove. Yuna marked what could be verified and left the rest open to challenge.

第二疊觀察紀錄使後果貼近普通人的生活： 維爾說安全工程師膽小，只因他們拒絕批准危險擴建。 警告紙上綁著的藍線，能喚起凱倫對母親雙手的童年記憶。 承包商廢棄的餐廳仍展示建城宴席的烤禽類菜單。 接待員解釋即將銷毀多少紀錄時，聲音變得沙啞。 凱倫從舊工程手套內側的污漬，認出母親常用的柑橘乳液。 尤娜標記能查證的部分，其餘保留給人質疑。

There were two tempting shortcuts. One would protect the reputation of Minister Calder and leave the public guessing. The other would expose every private detail at once and call that transparency. Kellan rejected both. The issue was whether a state may alter a child’s mind before the child can consent, not whether the team could win an argument in a single afternoon. A smaller step, supported by independent observers, could protect people while the wider dispute remained open.

有兩種誘人的捷徑：一種保住卡爾德部長的名聲，讓大眾自行猜測；另一種把所有私人細節一次揭露，並稱之為透明。凱倫兩者都拒絕。核心是國家是否能在孩子尚無同意能力時改造其心智，不是團隊能否在一個下午贏得爭論。由獨立觀察者支持的一小步，即使爭議未結，也能先保護民眾。

The team kept the concrete observations alongside the disputed theory: The records exposed a dangerous mentality: officials valued silence more than the health of residents. The monetary trail linked the contractor's expansion payments to an office outside normal public oversight. The building's moody lighting made every empty corridor seem occupied by someone who had vanished. At the nucleus of the network stood a reservoir relay designed to synchronize an entire district. A sharp rap at the metal door announced the safety inspector's arrival. Nothing in these records excused the people who had made the dangerous decision.

團隊把具體觀察與有爭議的理論並列保存： 紀錄揭露一種危險心態：官員重視安靜勝過居民健康。 金錢流向把承包商的擴建款項連到一個不受一般公共監督的辦公室。 大樓陰沉多變的燈光，使每條空走廊都像住著消失的人。 整套網路的核心是水庫中繼器，設計目的在同步整個行政區。 金屬門上傳來急促敲擊聲，宣告安全檢查員抵達。 紀錄中沒有任何一項能替做出危險決定的人開脫。

In the clinic corridor, parents read the names of emotions as if selecting colors for a room. Kellan hated the neatness of the printed boxes. Yuna reminded him that fear could protect a child and anger could tell a child when a boundary had been crossed. Neither feeling was a defect.

診所走廊裡，家長閱讀情緒名稱的神情，就像挑房間顏色。凱倫痛恨整齊的印刷選項。尤娜提醒他，恐懼可能保護孩子，憤怒也可能告訴孩子界線遭人侵犯。兩者都不是缺陷。

The record continued with small facts that would be lost in a confident speech: A red slash across the engineer's recommendation marked the moment the council ignored the warning. A recording captured a sleeping worker's snore before an illegal correction signal interrupted it. Nari had audited telecommunications contracts before discovering the city's hidden sleep-control equipment. The chair agreed to unveil the contract terms before any final vote. An elderly boat captain defended the public path with surprising vigor, insisting that younger residents deserved safe access too. Taken alone, no detail settled the case; together they showed why a public answer mattered.

紀錄接著列出那些在自信演說中容易消失的小事實： 工程師建議上的紅色劃線，標記了議會無視警告的那一刻。 錄音收進一名熟睡工人的鼾聲，接著被違法修正訊號打斷。 娜莉曾稽核電信契約，因而發現城市祕密睡眠控制設備。 主席同意在最終表決前公開契約條款。 一名年長船長充滿活力地捍衛公共步道，堅稱年輕居民也應享有安全通行。 單獨看來沒有一項細節能結案，合在一起卻說明了公開答覆為何重要。

The chapter ended without an easy reassurance. Kellan had learned one thing that could be checked and another that still needed an answer. Yuna wrote both on the same page so that relief would not erase doubt. Their decision had moved the crisis forward, but it had also drawn the attention of Minister Calder. By morning, the next question would reach people far beyond this room.

這一天沒有簡單的安慰。凱倫知道了一件能查證的事，也留下另一件尚待回答的事。尤娜把兩者寫在同一頁，免得鬆一口氣就抹掉疑問。他們的決定使危機向前，也引起卡爾德部長注意。明早下一個問題將傳到房間外更遠的地方。
