A City Without Anger

A messenger arrived before the bells had finished ringing. The news sounded impossible only because everyone had been trained not to question the institution that delivered it. Public advertisements claim emotional editing has ended crime, but Kellan notices families afraid to report mistreatment. Kellan asked the messenger to repeat the part that had been omitted from the written notice. It was there, in the omission, that the day's real story began.

鐘聲尚未結束，信使已經抵達。消息之所以聽來不可能，只因每個人都習慣不質疑傳遞消息的制度。廣告宣稱情緒編輯已終結犯罪，凱倫卻發現家庭害怕申報虐待。凱倫請信使重述書面公告遺漏的部分。這一天真正的故事，就從那處缺漏開始。

Emotional editing altered developing neural systems and had no proven complete reversal. A child might learn different responses later, but no researcher could promise that a removed feeling would simply grow back.

情緒編輯會改變發育中的神經系統，目前沒有證實可完全逆轉的方法。孩子日後可能學習不同反應，但研究者不能保證被刪除的情緒會自行長回。

In the clinic corridor, parents read the names of emotions as if selecting colors for a room. Kellan hated the neatness of the printed boxes. Yuna reminded him that fear could protect a child and anger could tell a child when a boundary had been crossed. Neither feeling was a defect.

診所走廊裡，家長閱讀情緒名稱的神情，就像挑房間顏色。凱倫痛恨整齊的印刷選項。尤娜提醒他，恐懼可能保護孩子，憤怒也可能告訴孩子界線遭人侵犯。兩者都不是缺陷。

The evidence did not arrive as a confession. It came through a date that did not fit, a measurement that had been repeated, and a witness who knew exactly what the official account had left out. Kellan asked Yuna not to describe suspicion as proof. If the accusation was wrong, an innocent person would suffer. If it was right, careless language could still give the guilty a way to escape.

證據不是以自白形式出現，而是一個對不上的日期、一項重複測量的結果，以及一位知道官方說法遺漏何事的證人。凱倫請尤娜不要把懷疑說成證明。若指控錯了，無辜者會受害；若指控正確，草率措辭仍可能讓有罪者脫身。

The overlooked details in the witness file made the question harder, not easier: Kellan promised to abide by the clinic's consent rules even while helping Kellan preserve the invoice. The director would not affirm the official opening date without seeing the original ledger. The painted alligator on the clinic wall wore a conductor's cap from an erased railway line. Deep bass notes from a school choir reached the inspection team through the windows. The children's colloquial word for the forbidden street survived in a playground counting rhyme. Yuna marked what could be verified and left the rest open to challenge.

證人檔案裡被忽視的細節使問題更加複雜，而不是更簡單： 凱倫承諾遵守診所的同意規則，同時協助凱倫保存發票。 主任沒看過原始帳冊，不願確認官方啟用日期。 診所牆上的彩繪鱷魚戴著一頂來自被抹除鐵路的列車長帽。 學校合唱團的低音透過窗戶傳進檢查隊耳中。 孩子口語中稱呼禁忌街道的詞，仍留在遊戲數數歌裡。 尤娜標記能查證的部分，其餘保留給人質疑。

A small act of honesty unsettled the room more than a shouted threat could have done. A social worker explains that citizens with anger removed may struggle to recognize injustice. Kellan watched a witness decide not to change a difficult detail merely because it complicated the preferred story. Yuna recorded the correction openly. Their inquiry would be slower now, but its shape belonged less to the powerful and more to the people who had lived through the event.

一個小小的誠實舉動，帶來比喊叫威脅更強的震動。社工說明，遭刪除憤怒的人可能較難辨認不義。凱倫看見證人沒有因為細節讓主流說法變複雜就改口。尤娜公開記錄修正。調查會因此變慢，卻能較少受權貴支配，也更貼近親歷者。

A second bundle of observations brought the consequences closer to ordinary life: Treating forgotten dreams as commonplace noise was part of the clinic's training manual. The hospital's exemption would expire at midnight, giving them only hours to examine the records. Ivo discovered that an old fuse had protected the basement archive from the memory relay network. Kellan offered quiet hospitality to worried parents, never asking for their children's forbidden dream details. The authorities called the visions infectious, although the clinic could provide no evidence of transmission. Nothing in these records excused the people who had made the dangerous decision.

第二疊觀察紀錄使後果貼近普通人的生活： 把被遺忘的夢當成尋常雜訊，是診所訓練手冊的一部分。 醫院的特別許可將在午夜到期，留給他們查閱紀錄的時間只剩數小時。 伊沃發現一枚舊保險絲，讓地下檔案室沒有接上記憶中繼網路。 凱倫溫和接待焦慮的家長，從不逼問孩子的禁忌夢境內容。 當局稱那些幻象有傳染性，但診所拿不出任何傳播證據。 紀錄中沒有任何一項能替做出危險決定的人開脫。

No one in the room was permitted to declare that a good intention canceled a harmful action. Kellan insisted on recording the error, identifying who had authorized it, and protecting those affected before assigning wider blame. Yuna drew a simple boundary around what they could do tonight. The line looked disappointingly modest, yet it was the first promise that did not require another person to surrender a right.

室內沒有人能宣稱善意可以抵銷傷害。凱倫堅持記錄錯誤、確認授權者，並在追究更大責任前保護受害者。尤娜畫出今晚能做到的清楚界線。這條線看似微不足道，卻是第一個不要求別人放棄權利的承諾。

The team kept the concrete observations alongside the disputed theory: A liter of cleaning fluid had spilled over the paper archive, obscuring several names but not the stamps. A novice technician had left the storage sensor disconnected, accidentally preserving the original poster. Kellan considered it rash to accuse the clinic staff before she understood who controlled their systems. The old invoice and the official date could not reconcile without an independent explanation. The shabby ledger cabinet looked worthless, which was probably why nobody had removed it. Taken alone, no detail settled the case; together they showed why a public answer mattered.

團隊把具體觀察與有爭議的理論並列保存： 一公升清潔液灑在紙本檔案上，模糊了幾個姓名，印章卻還清楚。 一名新手技師曾讓儲藏室感測器保持斷線，意外保存了原始海報。 凱倫認為尚未查清誰控制系統，就指責診所員工太過魯莽。 沒有獨立說明，舊發票與官方日期便無法相互調和。 破舊的帳冊櫃看起來毫無價值，可能因此沒人搬走。 單獨看來沒有一項細節能結案，合在一起卻說明了公開答覆為何重要。

In the clinic corridor, parents read the names of emotions as if selecting colors for a room. Kellan hated the neatness of the printed boxes. Yuna reminded him that fear could protect a child and anger could tell a child when a boundary had been crossed. Neither feeling was a defect.

診所走廊裡，家長閱讀情緒名稱的神情，就像挑房間顏色。凱倫痛恨整齊的印刷選項。尤娜提醒他，恐懼可能保護孩子，憤怒也可能告訴孩子界線遭人侵犯。兩者都不是缺陷。

The record continued with small facts that would be lost in a confident speech: A borrowed spotlight made the presentation easier to see but obscured an exit sign. A volunteer brought ginger syrup for workers who had spent the afternoon in the cold. The handrail began to tilt when the tester leaned against its outer edge. The artificial twilight outside the clinic concealed the fact that no real sun had reached the street. An old label marked whiskey lay in the storeroom beside the railway invoices, dated before the city's founding. Kellan asked the clerk to preserve the original wording rather than improve the story.

紀錄接著列出那些在自信演說中容易消失的小事實： 借來的聚光燈讓簡報更清楚，卻遮住了逃生標誌。 志工帶來薑糖漿，慰勞在寒風中工作一下午的人。 測試者靠在外側時，扶手開始傾斜。 診所外的人造暮光掩蓋了真實太陽根本照不到街道的事實。 一張印著威士忌的舊標籤躺在儲藏室鐵路發票旁，日期早於官方建城日。 凱倫要求書記保存原本措辭，而不是把故事修飾得更好聽。

On the way out, Kellan passed people waiting for a result that would affect their meals, their homes, or their ability to say no. None of them had the luxury of treating the dispute as an intellectual puzzle. Yuna paused to answer a child's practical question before they left. The answer was incomplete, but unlike the old official notice, it admitted the uncertainty out loud.

離開時，凱倫經過等待結果的人群，這項結果將影響他們的餐食、家園或說不的能力。他們不能奢侈地把爭論當成智力謎題。尤娜停下回答一名孩子實際的問題。答案並不完整，卻與舊公告不同：它坦白承認不確定之處。
