The Laboratory Under the School

Nobody had called it a crisis at first. A clerk had used a harmless word, a guard had followed an ordinary instruction, and by evening somebody else was carrying the cost. Kellan refused to begin with the official conclusion. Yuna finds a research wing beneath a public school, where children are tested on their obedience to artificial commands. In the neonatal registration center, even an accurate number could become a lie when it left out the person whose life it counted.

起初沒有人稱這是危機。書記使用看似無害的詞，守衛照普通命令行事，到了晚上代價卻落在別人身上。凱倫拒絕從官方結論開始。尤娜在公立學校地下找到研究區，孩子被用來測試是否服從人工指令。在新生兒登記中心，精確的數字若遺漏真正承受後果的人，也可能成為謊言。

In the clinic corridor, parents read the names of emotions as if selecting colors for a room. Kellan hated the neatness of the printed boxes. Yuna reminded him that fear could protect a child and anger could tell a child when a boundary had been crossed. Neither feeling was a defect.

診所走廊裡，家長閱讀情緒名稱的神情，就像挑房間顏色。凱倫痛恨整齊的印刷選項。尤娜提醒他，恐懼可能保護孩子，憤怒也可能告訴孩子界線遭人侵犯。兩者都不是缺陷。

The evidence did not arrive as a confession. It came through a date that did not fit, a measurement that had been repeated, and a witness who knew exactly what the official account had left out. Kellan asked Yuna not to describe suspicion as proof. If the accusation was wrong, an innocent person would suffer. If it was right, careless language could still give the guilty a way to escape.

證據不是以自白形式出現，而是一個對不上的日期、一項重複測量的結果，以及一位知道官方說法遺漏何事的證人。凱倫請尤娜不要把懷疑說成證明。若指控錯了，無辜者會受害；若指控正確，草率措辭仍可能讓有罪者脫身。

The overlooked details in the witness file made the question harder, not easier: The bulky old tax ledgers could not fit through the school door without removing its damaged frame. Officials could deem a resident nonexistent by deleting an address, even while collecting that person's taxes. The elder called the city's memory policy a quiet dictatorship built from forms rather than soldiers. An unregistered delivery cart could glide along the lower rails without triggering street cameras. Children sold small handicraft birds to purchase paper that city printers refused to supply them. Nothing in these records excused the people who had made the dangerous decision.

證人檔案裡被忽視的細節使問題更加複雜，而不是更簡單： 厚重的舊稅務帳冊，不拆掉損壞門框就搬不進學校。 官員可以刪除地址就認定居民不存在，卻同時向那人收稅。 長者稱城市的記憶政策是一種靠表格而非士兵建立的無聲獨裁。 一輛未登記的送貨車能沿下層軌道滑行，不觸發街道攝影機。 孩子們販售手工小鳥，換取城市印刷廠拒絕供應的紙張。 紀錄中沒有任何一項能替做出危險決定的人開脫。

A small act of honesty unsettled the room more than a shouted threat could have done. The experiments record the absence of protest as a positive military outcome. Kellan watched a witness decide not to change a difficult detail merely because it complicated the preferred story. Yuna recorded the correction openly. Their inquiry would be slower now, but its shape belonged less to the powerful and more to the people who had lived through the event.

一個小小的誠實舉動，帶來比喊叫威脅更強的震動。實驗竟把沒有抗議記成正面軍事成果。凱倫看見證人沒有因為細節讓主流說法變複雜就改口。尤娜公開記錄修正。調查會因此變慢，卻能較少受權貴支配，也更貼近親歷者。

A second bundle of observations brought the consequences closer to ordinary life: A community interpreter translated the inspectors' questions for elders who still used the old district dialect. The neighborhood's lesbian teacher said she feared losing her students, not the disclosure of her identity. For residents, liberation meant an address and protection from raids, not a speech from aboveground heroes. Kellan rejected the lofty promise that everyone would be saved if they simply trusted her. Ivo used a paper utility map to navigate the tunnels without sending a digital location signal. Taken alone, no detail settled the case; together they showed why a public answer mattered.

第二疊觀察紀錄使後果貼近普通人的生活： 社區口譯員替仍說舊區方言的長者翻譯檢查員的問題。 下層社區的女同志教師說，她害怕的是失去學生，而不是身分被揭露。 對居民而言，解放是擁有地址與免於突襲的保障，不是地面英雄的演說。 凱倫拒絕那種空泛崇高的承諾：只要信她，所有人就會得救。 伊沃用紙本管線圖穿越隧道，沒有發出數位定位訊號。 單獨看來沒有一項細節能結案，合在一起卻說明了公開答覆為何重要。

The debate sharpened when Minister Calder demanded a decision before the evidence could be copied. Kellan replied that urgency and secrecy were not the same thing. They established a temporary limit, published the reason for it, and scheduled an independent review. The result satisfied nobody completely. That was acceptable; the purpose of the process was to make abuse harder, not to produce applause.

當卡爾德部長要求在證據複製前就做決定，辯論變得尖銳。凱倫回應說緊急和保密不是同一回事。團隊訂立暫時限制、公開理由，並排定獨立審查。結果沒有讓任何人完全滿意，但程序的目的本來就不是得到掌聲，而是使濫權更困難。

The team kept the concrete observations alongside the disputed theory: The lower district's volunteer medics warned that overwork would leave them unable to handle another raid. The elder placed a smooth pebble beside every confirmed name on the handwritten community register. The application for registered street addresses remained pending despite decades of tax payments. An abandoned plantation above the tunnels supplied seeds to gardens the city denied existed. The community used a hand plow to cultivate strips of soil beside the old service tracks. Kellan asked the clerk to preserve the original wording rather than improve the story.

團隊把具體觀察與有爭議的理論並列保存： 下層社區志工醫護警告，過勞會使他們無力應付下一次突襲。 長者在手寫社區名冊每個核實姓名旁放一顆光滑小石頭。 儘管居民繳了幾十年稅，街道地址的登記申請仍在待審。 隧道上方一座廢棄種植園提供種子，供給城市否認存在的菜園。 社區用手推犁在舊維修軌道旁耕作狹長土地。 凱倫要求書記保存原本措辭，而不是把故事修飾得更好聽。

In the clinic corridor, parents read the names of emotions as if selecting colors for a room. Kellan hated the neatness of the printed boxes. Yuna reminded him that fear could protect a child and anger could tell a child when a boundary had been crossed. Neither feeling was a defect.

診所走廊裡，家長閱讀情緒名稱的神情，就像挑房間顏色。凱倫痛恨整齊的印刷選項。尤娜提醒他，恐懼可能保護孩子，憤怒也可能告訴孩子界線遭人侵犯。兩者都不是缺陷。

The record continued with small facts that would be lost in a confident speech: No member of any royalty had visited the lower district, though official portraits hung in its old school. The threat of another correction pulse could spur residents to hide rather than testify. Kellan watched an elderly couple stroll down a street that did not appear on any official map. A boy with a deep tan from the garden lamps asked whether the real sun was warmer. The elder warned Kellan not to treat the residents' missing citizenship as a trifle. Yuna marked what could be verified and left the rest open to challenge.

紀錄接著列出那些在自信演說中容易消失的小事實： 沒有任何王室成員來過下層社區，舊學校卻還掛著官方肖像。 新一輪修正脈衝的威脅，可能促使居民躲藏而不是作證。 凱倫看見一對老夫婦漫步在任何官方地圖都沒有的街道。 一名被菜園燈曬黑的男孩問，真正的太陽會不會更暖。 長者警告凱倫，別把居民失去公民身分當成小事。 尤娜標記能查證的部分，其餘保留給人質疑。

A message arrived after the public doors had closed. It carried no grand declaration, just a name and a time that did not match the official schedule. Kellan resisted the urge to call it proof. Yuna opened a fresh page in the journal and recorded the discrepancy exactly. They had won time, not certainty, and there were still people who might lose everything if they hurried.

公共大門關閉後，一則訊息送到。它沒有豪言壯語，只有一個名字和一個對不上官方時程的時間。凱倫克制住把它當成證明的衝動。尤娜翻開日誌新的一頁，逐字記錄差異。他們贏得的是時間，不是確定性；若急於求成，仍有人可能失去一切。
