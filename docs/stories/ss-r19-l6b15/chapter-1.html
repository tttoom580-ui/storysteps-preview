出生表格上的空格

The Empty Box on the Birth Form

新生兒必須先刪除一種情緒 · 第 1 / 25 章 · 5000–6000 · 英中對照 · 校訂中

用互動版查詞、朗讀與收藏

Before his daughter had a heartbeat, the state had already called her dangerous. Kellan stood at the neonatal registration center with an empty consent card in his hand. It instructed him to remove one emotion from his future child: fear, anger, sadness, envy, or grief. A smiling fox cub galloped across the screen while a recorded voice promised a safer generation. Kellan tried to utter the word no, but the clerk pointed to a compulsory box. “It is a directive, not a request,” she said. The clasp on his daughter’s identification bracelet lay stationary in a sterile tray. The child it would identify was not yet born.

女兒還沒有心跳，國家就已經稱她危險。凱倫站在新生兒登記中心，手裡拿著空白同意卡。上面要他從未來孩子身上刪除一種情緒：恐懼、憤怒、悲傷、嫉妒或哀痛。螢幕上一隻微笑的狐狸幼獸奔馳，錄音承諾新一代會更安全。凱倫想說「不」，書記卻指著必填欄。「這是指令，不是請求，」她說。一只嬰兒識別手環的扣環靜止放在無菌托盤裡，等著戴上尚未出生的孩子手腕。

The program’s motto was printed above every booth: “Calm Children, Peaceful Cities.” Minister Calder called violent emotion a disease that society could transplant out of the next generation. A nearby parent asked whether a healthy baby’s fear was comparable to a damaged tooth that needed dental treatment. The clerk described fear and anger as synonyms for disorder. Kellan disagreed. He had felt fear when his partner’s pregnancy grew complicated, and it had pushed him to seek help. Anger had once made him report an abusive supervisor. Those feelings had not made him a criminal. They had told him that something mattered.

計畫座右銘印在每個櫃位上：「平靜的孩子，和平的城市。」卡爾德部長稱暴力情緒是一種疾病，可以從下一代身上移除。一旁的家長問，健康嬰兒的恐懼，是否真像壞牙齒一樣需要牙科治療。書記把恐懼和憤怒當成「失序」的同義詞。凱倫不認同。伴侶懷孕出現併發症時，他會害怕，正是恐懼催促他求助；他也曾因憤怒而舉報虐待員工的主管。那些情緒沒有讓他成為罪犯，反而告訴他有重要的事需要處理。

Neurologist Yuna agreed to convene an independent medical meeting after examining the trial records. A hospital trustee offered unconditional access to the data room, but warned that the documents were under government scrutiny. Yuna found no valid precedent for a routine, irreversible operation on every healthy newborn. The editing method affected developing brain circuits; there was no proven way to restore a feeling once removed. Outside the records room, a janitor was carefully watering a plant from the old children’s ward. Its leaves had survived three attempts to transplant it. “Not every living thing grows the way the form predicts,” he said.

神經科醫師尤娜讀過試驗紀錄後，同意召集獨立醫療會議。醫院受託人允許無條件查看資料室，卻提醒文件正受政府審查。尤娜找不到對每名健康新生兒例行進行不可逆手術的有效法律先例。編輯技術會影響發育中的腦部迴路，被移除的情緒目前沒有證實可以恢復。紀錄室外，清潔員細心替兒童病房留下的植物澆水。它經歷三次移植才活下來。「不是每種生命都會照表格預測的方式成長，」他說。

In the waiting room, families described the pressure they faced. One mother had been told that choosing sadness would help her son reap success at school. Another father had chosen anger because he wanted a child who would never defy him. A young pilgrim from a remote settlement carried a letter explaining why her community refused the program. She had walked hundreds of kilometers to deliver it because the registry excluded her district from electronic petitions. Their stories were different. Kellan understood that merely keeping his own card blank would not protect the families who could not afford to resist.

候診室裡，家長們說起面臨的壓力。一位母親被告知刪除悲傷能幫兒子在學校獲得成功；另一位父親選了憤怒，因為他想要永不頂嘴的孩子。一名偏遠聚落的年輕朝聖者帶來信件，說明社群為何拒絕計畫。登記局不接受她們的電子請願，她只好徒步數百公里送達。每個人的經歷都不同。凱倫明白，只讓自己的表格保持空白，保護不了那些無力反抗的家庭。

The ministry broadcast displayed an image of a dictator and said that only cruel leaders had ever opposed collective safety. Kellan looked around the modest dwelling where families waited for news. A mother comforted a frightened toddler instead of demanding that the fear disappear. A volunteer slowed a noisy toy so it would not heighten the child’s distress. These were ordinary acts of care, not military failures. Yuna showed him a trial page where the number of complaints had been used as a measure of success. It never counted children who could no longer explain that they were hurt.

部長的廣播播放一名獨裁者照片，宣稱只有殘酷領袖才會反對集體安全。凱倫環顧家長等消息的小小候診住所。一名母親安慰受驚幼兒，而不是要求恐懼消失；志工調低吵鬧玩具的聲音，避免加劇孩子的不安。那是平凡照顧，不是軍事上的失敗。尤娜拿出試驗紀錄，其中竟以申訴次數作為成功指標，卻從不計算那些再也無法說出自己受傷的孩子。

When Kellan returned to the clerk, the screen had selected anger on his behalf. Beside the box was a clause stating that a missing answer counted as unconditional approval. He held the blank card against the light and asked for a paper receipt proving he had refused. The clerk hesitated. The printer, however, issued a second receipt before she could stop it. On the bottom of the page, behind the public health seal, was a military project number. Yuna recognized its prefix. It belonged not to childcare but to a program for training soldiers who would never hesitate. Kellan put the receipt in his pocket and walked toward the public court.

凱倫回到櫃台時，螢幕竟已替他選好「憤怒」。旁邊的條款說明，沒有回答便視為無條件同意。他把空白卡片迎向燈光，要求一張證明自己拒絕的紙本收據。書記猶豫片刻，印表機卻在她阻止前吐出第二張收據。頁面最下方、公衛印璽之後，印著一組軍事計畫編號。尤娜認得前綴，那不是托育項目，而是訓練從不猶豫的士兵之計畫。凱倫把收據放進口袋，朝公共法庭走去。
