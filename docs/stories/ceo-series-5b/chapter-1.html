The Man Who Refused a Price

他的帝國靠岸時：總裁與不出售碼頭的男人 · 第 1 / 25 章 · 4000–5000 · 英中對照 · 校訂中

On the morning Cheng Yan arrived at the East Pier, his black car stopped before a chapel that had survived three typhoons. At thirty-six, he was the youngest chief executive in Aster Group's history. He expected the purchase of Tide House Cooperative to be another transaction, completed before lunch. Instead, Xu Linchuan, the thirty-two-year-old director elected by the dockworkers, was waiting with a folding chair and a question. "If you buy the pier," Xu asked, "where will the people who work here go?" Cheng had prepared numbers, not an answer.

程硯抵達東碼頭那天早上，黑色轎車停在一座經歷過三次颱風的禮拜堂前。三十六歲的他是星衡集團歷來最年輕的執行長。他原以為收購潮屋合作社只是另一筆能在午餐前完成的交易。沒想到由碼頭工人選出的三十二歲主任許臨川，早已帶著折疊椅等在那裡。「如果你買下碼頭，在這裡工作的人要去哪裡？」許臨川問。程硯準備了數字，卻沒準備答案。

The morning gathering had started with the usual arrangement: a long table for people in suits and a narrow strip of pavement for everyone else. Xu changed it before Cheng arrived. He brought the chairs into a circle, placed a box of spare pens in the center, and wrote three questions on a board. Who would own the buildings? Who would maintain the storm barriers? Who could refuse the agreement? None of those questions was answered by a beautiful model of the proposed development. When Cheng saw the board, he asked his assistant to stop distributing the glossy brochures. The decision irritated the sales team, but it allowed the first meeting to become an actual conversation. Even the skeptical dockworkers stayed long enough to hear the company's figures.

早晨的集會原本按照慣例安排：穿西裝的人坐長桌，其他人只能站在狹窄人行道上。許臨川在程硯抵達前就改變了布置。他把椅子排成圓圈，中間放一盒備用筆，在板上寫了三個問題：誰擁有建築？誰維護防洪設施？誰有權拒絕協議？華麗的改建模型回答不了任何一題。程硯看見板子後，請助理停止發放光亮的宣傳冊。業務團隊因此不滿，但第一次會議終於變成真正的對話。就連懷疑的碼頭工人也留下來聽公司報數據。

Outside the chapel, a cluster of residents demanded certainty about compensation. They wanted Cheng to disclose whether a new generator would replace the old one, and whether the wetland habitat protected under the harbor's heritage plan would survive. At the public forum, an envious developer called their demands sentimental. Xu replied that metropolitan growth was immense, but so was the cost of allowing a neighborhood to wither. The cooperative supervisor placed an optional provision on the table: no sale without a recorded vote. Cheng's first impulse was to reject it; instead, he listened for an insight that his own advisers had missed.

禮拜堂外，一群居民要求明確的補償承諾。他們想知道程硯是否會揭露新發電機取代舊機的安排，以及受港口歷史保存計畫保護的濕地棲地能否存續。公開論壇上，一名心生嫉妒的開發商指責居民感情用事。許臨川回答，大都會的發展規模固然龐大，讓社區逐漸枯萎的代價也一樣巨大。合作社主管把一項可選條款放上桌：未經記名投票，不得出售。程硯第一個衝動是拒絕，但他反而傾聽顧問們可能忽略的洞見。

A boy carrying bottled water almost caused Cheng to stumble on the broken pavement. Xu caught his sleeve, steadying him without a smile. It was an embarrassingly small kindness after a morning of expensive threats. Later, in the boardroom overlooking the harbor, Cheng learned that the purchase papers listed the cooperative's members as tenants rather than owners. The error had not been created by Xu. It had arrived inside Aster's own files, complete with a signature that nobody at the pier recognized. Cheng shut the folder and asked for the original register before he would sign anything.

一個提著瓶裝水的男孩差點讓程硯在破裂的人行道上絆倒。許臨川扶住他的袖口，臉上沒有笑意。在一整個充滿昂貴威脅的早晨後，這個微小善意反而令人難堪。稍後，程硯在俯瞰港灣的董事會議室發現，收購文件竟把合作社社員列成租戶，而非所有人。錯誤不是許臨川造成的，而是藏在星衡自己的檔案裡，還附有碼頭上無人認得的簽名。程硯合起資料夾，要求在簽署任何文件前先看原始名冊。

Xu knew the danger of treating Cheng as a symbol rather than a person. A villain could be hated easily, and a savior could be thanked too quickly; both roles spared the powerful man from answering ordinary questions. As the crowd began to leave, Xu asked two residents to record exactly what had been promised and what remained undecided. Cheng signed the minutes, then crossed out a phrase that claimed the meeting had reached agreement. "It hasn't," he said. Xu noticed that the correction cost Cheng nothing and yet required him to contradict his own staff. It was a beginning, not redemption. Across the harbor, workers continued unloading the boats as though important conversations were only another part of the day's labor.

許臨川知道，把程硯當成象徵而不是人，會有危險。壞人很容易被恨，救世主也容易太快獲得感謝；兩種角色都能讓有權勢的人免於回答平凡問題。人群開始散去時，許臨川請兩位居民記下哪些承諾已經說出、哪些仍未決定。程硯在會議紀錄上簽名，接著劃掉一句宣稱雙方已達成共識的話。「還沒有，」他說。許臨川注意到，這個更正對程硯沒有金錢成本，卻需要他反駁自己的員工。這是開始，而不是救贖。港灣另一端，工人繼續卸船，彷彿重要談話也只是每日勞動的一部分。

Xu had no interest in being rescued by a wealthy stranger. He had spent seven years rebuilding a business that gave older fishers, students, and people with disabilities a place to work. Yet when Cheng returned the unsigned contract that evening, Xu recognized an unfamiliar restraint in the man's expression. "I can wait for the truth," Cheng said. "I cannot promise the board will." Xu held out his hand, not for a bargain but for the copied register. Their fingers touched above the paper. Neither man mistook that brief warmth for permission to own the other.

許臨川無意讓一個富有的陌生人拯救自己。他花了七年重建這門生意，讓年長漁民、學生及身心障礙者都有工作的地方。然而，當程硯傍晚帶回尚未簽署的合約時，他看見對方神情裡一種不熟悉的克制。「我能等真相，」程硯說，「但我無法保證董事會會等。」許臨川伸出手，不是為了交易，而是接過影印名冊。兩人的指尖在紙上碰到；誰也沒有把那一瞬的溫度誤認為擁有對方的許可。
