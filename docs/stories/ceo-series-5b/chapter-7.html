他的帝國靠岸時：總裁與不出售碼頭的男人 · 第 7 / 25 章 · 4000–5000 · 英中對照 · 校訂中

The first independent inspection opened a second dispute: who owned the abandoned buildings behind the market? Aster planned an acquisition, but the cooperative had used the backyard for training for decades. An executive proposed a bonus for Xu if he gave up the claim. Xu cut a chunk from an old limestone wall and showed the mineral deposits to the lawyer. "Calcium does not tell us who owns this place," he said, "but the marks under it tell us where the old door stood." Cheng asked for permission to examine the original colonial survey rather than dismissing the evidence.

第一次獨立檢驗帶出第二場爭議：市場後面廢棄建築究竟屬於誰？星衡計畫收購，合作社卻已把後院當作訓練場使用數十年。一名主管提出獎金，希望許臨川放棄主張。許臨川從舊石灰牆上取下一塊碎片，向律師展示礦物沉積。「鈣質不會告訴我們誰擁有這裡，」他說，「但底下的痕跡能看出舊門的位置。」程硯要求准許查閱殖民時期的原始測量圖，而不是否定這些線索。

The original survey had been printed on thick cloth paper and folded so many times that its edges had nearly disappeared. An archivist showed the volunteers how to flatten it without pulling at the fragile corners. Cheng offered to pay for a digital copy, but the archivist explained that scanning was only the beginning: the annotations, stamps, and uncertain dates also required careful description. Xu sat beside a young student who was learning to read old measurements. They compared the drawn gate with the building outside and discovered that the official entrance and the practical entrance were different things. It was a small finding with consequences for people who crossed the yard every day.

原始測量圖印在厚布紋紙上，摺過太多次，邊緣幾乎消失。檔案員教志工如何攤平，避免拉扯脆弱角落。程硯提出付錢製作數位副本，檔案員解釋掃描只是開始：附註、印章及不確定日期同樣需要謹慎描述。許臨川坐在一名學讀舊測量數據的學生旁。他們比對圖上的門和外頭建築，發現官方入口與實際入口不是同一回事。這項小發現，影響了每天穿過院子的居民。

The documents were difficult to comprehend. One clause required the building to support public transit; another allowed private warehouses, with an implication that future owners could block the passage. A sophomore volunteer found that the gate was listed as a symbolic entrance, not a legal boundary. The room was spacious enough to stack thousands of files, yet every shelf was crowded. A security officer showed them surveillance photographs, including one of an employee trying to penetrate the locked archive after hours. The attempt was risky, but it was not proof that the cooperative had sent him.

文件難以理解。一條款規定建築必須支持公共交通，另一條允許私人倉庫，暗示未來所有人或可封閉通道。一名大二志工發現，門在資料上被列為象徵性入口，而非法定界線。房間寬敞得足以堆上千份文件，書架卻全滿了。保全主管出示監視照片，其中一張拍到員工下班後試圖闖入鎖住的檔案室。那行為有風險，卻不能證明是合作社派他來的。

A retired accountant who identified himself as a republican disapproved of both parties' legal threats. He offered to sort the scrap papers, insisting on a list of every file removed. Xu watched Cheng kneel on the floor beside the volunteer rather than delegate the dusty work. A long day of lifting boxes left Cheng's spine stiff, and Xu laughed when he tried to stand without complaining. "Don't make a habit of acting heroic," Xu said. "I would rather work with someone who knows when to sit down."

一位自稱共和派的退休會計師不贊成雙方以法律威脅。他自願分類廢紙，堅持每份移出的檔案都要列冊。許臨川看著程硯蹲在志工旁邊整理落滿灰塵的資料，沒有把工作全交給別人。搬了一整天箱子後，程硯的脊椎僵硬，想站起來又忍著不抱怨，讓許臨川忍不住笑。「別把當英雄當成習慣，」許臨川說，「我寧願跟懂得什麼時候該坐下的人合作。」

Xu's team made a list of questions for a neutral legal review. They deliberately avoided asking the lawyer to predict a victory. Instead, they wanted to know which claims could be supported now, which documents had to be authenticated, and which rights might survive even if the building changed owners. Cheng read the list slowly. He understood each word but was unused to a strategy that did not begin with the strongest possible demand. He asked whether the community might be willing to share the passage with his company's trucks. Xu said the answer depended on hours, noise, and safety, not on who had the larger bank account.

許臨川的團隊列出供中立法律審查的問題。他們刻意不要求律師預測勝訴，而是想知道哪些主張已有支持、哪些文件需要認證，以及即使建築換主人，哪些權利仍可能存在。程硯慢慢讀清單。他懂每個字，卻不習慣不從最強硬要求開始的策略。他問社區是否可能同意讓公司卡車共用通道。許臨川說答案取決於時間、噪音與安全，而不是誰銀行帳戶比較大。

The retired accountant said plainly, "I disapprove of secret deals, even when my favorite side proposes them." The sentence made Cheng uncomfortable, which was exactly why he wrote it down.

退休會計師直說：「我不贊成祕密交易，就算是我支持的那一方提出也一樣。」這句話讓程硯不自在，也正因如此，他把話記了下來。

They located a stamped map proving that the public passage had never been formally closed. Xu knew this would not end the dispute; a lawyer could still challenge how the old survey applied to present roads. Nevertheless, the map protected residents from an immediate eviction notice. Cheng handed the original to the archive officer and kept only a certified copy. As they walked out together, Xu said the view from the gate was beautiful. Cheng looked at him instead of the harbor, then quickly pretended to check the sky for rain.

他們找到一張蓋章地圖，證明公共通道從未正式封閉。許臨川知道爭議仍未結束；律師依然可能質疑舊測量圖是否適用於現在的道路。然而，地圖至少暫時保護居民不受立即驅離通知影響。程硯把原件交給檔案官，只留下認證副本。兩人一同走出去時，許臨川說門口景色很美。程硯卻看向許臨川，而不是港灣，接著趕緊假裝在看天空是否會下雨。
