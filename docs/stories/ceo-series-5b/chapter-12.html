The Lawsuit at Noon

他的帝國靠岸時：總裁與不出售碼頭的男人 · 第 12 / 25 章 · 4000–5000 · 英中對照 · 校訂中

At noon a celebrity investor announced a lawsuit accusing Tide House of fraud. The claim appeared on a giant screen outside the sports arena, where a company-sponsored health fair was taking place. Xu read the papers in a quiet room behind a rack of medical supplies. The plaintiff said his ultimate intent was to protect the public, yet the alleged theft involved a random number copied from an unfinished portfolio. Cheng suggested a public denial. Xu instead asked counsel to preserve every draft and to explain the difference between an error and deliberate dishonesty.

正午，一名名人投資者宣布提告，指控潮屋合作社詐欺。消息出現在體育館外的大螢幕上，當時場內正舉辦企業贊助的健康展。許臨川躲進醫療用品架後的安靜房間讀起訴文件。原告聲稱最終意圖是保護大眾，但所謂的竊取行為涉及一個從未完成的作品集複製出的隨機數字。程硯建議公開否認，許臨川則請律師保存每個草稿，並釐清錯誤與故意不誠實的差別。

The first legal review identified three different versions of the disputed invoice. Each showed the same service but a different date. The clerk who had prepared the earliest one was nervous, so Xu arranged for her to speak with independent counsel rather than across a table from corporate executives. Cheng made copies available through the agreed procedure and asked his own team not to contact her privately. It was possible that someone had committed fraud, but it was equally possible that an error had spread through a poorly designed system. The hearing would need to separate those explanations by evidence. Xu appreciated that Cheng was no longer treating uncertainty as a personal insult.

初步法律審查找到同一張爭議發票的三個版本。服務相同，日期卻各異。最早開立發票的職員很緊張，許臨川安排她先與獨立律師談，而不是直接面對公司高層。程硯依約定程序提供副本，並要求自己的團隊不得私下接觸她。可能有人涉及詐欺，也可能只是設計不良的系統讓錯誤層層傳播。聽證需要靠證據區分兩種解釋。許臨川欣賞程硯不再把不確定當成對自己的羞辱。

The health fair itself provided unexpected clues. Its nutrition program had been designed for residential neighborhoods with limited access to fresh food. A doctor showed Xu a prescription form with a plural heading where a singular title belonged, suggesting that several templates had been mixed. A volunteer poked at a sample package made with synthetic crystal and asked why the shipping number had been reversed. Meanwhile, organizers tried to ban reporters from a public session. Cheng insisted that the press could attend if patients' private records remained covered.

健康展本身提供了意外線索。營養計畫原本為難以取得新鮮食物的住宅區設計。醫師向許臨川展示一張處方表單，標題本應用單數卻印成複數，暗示不同版本的範本曾被混用。志工戳了戳以人造水晶製作的樣品包裝，問貨運號碼為何被倒置。另一方面，主辦者試圖禁止記者參加公開場次。程硯堅持只要病人隱私紀錄遮住，媒體就應能旁聽。

When a security officer tried to shove a junior clerk out of the room, Xu stood between them and demanded the officer stop. Cheng did not speak over Xu; he called for the officer's supervisor and requested a written incident record. The clerk then produced an earlier delivery receipt with the correct number. The evidence did not decide the lawsuit, but it made clear that the accusation rested on a document with a troubled history. Lawyers from both sides agreed to a secure exchange of records before the hearing.

保全人員試圖把一名年輕職員推離房間時，許臨川站在兩人中間要求停止。程硯沒有搶著替許臨川發言，而是叫來保全主管，要求留下書面事件紀錄。職員隨後拿出較早的送貨收據，上面有正確號碼。這份證據無法決定訴訟結果，但清楚指出指控仰賴一份來源有疑點的文件。雙方律師同意聽證前透過安全程序交換紀錄。

By evening the news had reached families who depended on the clinic. Several worried that the lawsuit would close the doors immediately. Xu held a short meeting in the waiting room and explained what was and was not affected. The health services would continue under a separate agreement, and no patient's treatment depended on the outcome of the ownership dispute. Cheng attended at the back, letting the clinic manager answer questions. When a child asked whether the doctors would still be there tomorrow, the manager gave a clear answer based on the current staffing plan. Xu watched the child's shoulders relax. He knew that reassurance was meaningful only when it could be checked against reality.

傍晚消息傳到依賴診所的家庭。好幾人擔心訴訟會讓診所立刻關門。許臨川在候診室開短會，說明哪些服務受影響、哪些不受影響。醫療服務依獨立協議持續，病人治療不取決於所有權爭議的結果。程硯在後排旁聽，讓診所經理回答問題。一個孩子問醫師明天還會不會來，經理依照現有班表給出清楚回答。許臨川看見孩子放鬆肩膀。他知道，安慰必須能與現實核對才有意義。

Xu would not let the lawsuit depress the clinic staff. He asked the clerk to poke a finger at the correct invoice line and show everyone how to reverse an incorrect entry without destroying the original record.

許臨川不願讓訴訟使診所員工消沉。他請職員指著正確的發票欄位，示範如何撤銷錯誤登錄，同時保留原始紀錄。

The legal setback depressed Xu more than he wanted to admit. After the hall emptied, Cheng found him standing before the dark arena. "You can be furious," Cheng said. "You don't have to be brave every minute." Xu turned around, searching Cheng's face for the familiar ambition to take charge. He found concern instead. "Can I kiss you?" Cheng asked. Xu nodded, and the kiss was brief, awkward, and real. It did not settle the lawsuit; it only made the long evening easier to face together.

法律挫折讓許臨川比他願意承認的還沮喪。大廳清空後，程硯發現他站在黑暗的體育館前。「你可以生氣，」程硯說，「不用每一分鐘都勇敢。」許臨川轉身，在程硯臉上尋找熟悉的掌控野心，卻只看見關切。「我可以吻你嗎？」程硯問。許臨川點頭，這個吻短暫、笨拙，卻真實。它沒有解決訴訟，只讓漫長夜晚比較容易一起面對。
