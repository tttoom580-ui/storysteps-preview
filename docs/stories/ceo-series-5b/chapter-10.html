沒有觀眾的晚餐

Dinner Without an Audience

他的帝國靠岸時：總裁與不出售碼頭的男人 · 第 10 / 25 章 · 4000–5000 · 英中對照 · 校訂中

Cheng finally kept his promise and invited Xu to dinner in a small restaurant by the bus terminal. Xu checked the menu for an allergy before ordering. Their meal began with grilled vegetables from the drought-struck farms outside the city. "I thought you'd take me somewhere with marble floors," Xu said. "I did consider it," Cheng replied. "Then I realized you would spend the evening trying to escape." It was the closest they had come to an ordinary date. Neither brought a public-relations assistant, a bodyguard, or even a printed agenda.

程硯終於履行承諾，邀許臨川在公車總站旁的小餐館吃飯。許臨川點菜前先確認食物過敏原。他們吃的烤蔬菜來自城外遭乾旱影響的農場。「我以為你會帶我去有大理石地板的地方，」許臨川說。「我確實考慮過，」程硯回答，「後來想到你大概會整晚想逃走。」這是他們最接近普通約會的一次。兩人都沒帶公關助理、保鑣，連印好的議程也沒有。

Xu had expected the date to be another negotiation disguised as dinner. For the first ten minutes he watched Cheng with the caution he normally reserved for complicated contracts. Yet Cheng did not ask whether he had changed his mind about the sale. He asked about the first boat Xu had repaired and the person who had taught him to read weather signs. Xu told a story about his mother, who had insisted that he learn a practical skill before studying finance. Cheng admitted that his own family had expected him to learn only how to give orders. They compared childhood ambitions, laughing at how strange their present lives would have seemed to two lonely boys.

許臨川原以為這場約會會是包裝成晚餐的另一場談判。前十分鐘，他用平常看複雜合約的謹慎態度觀察程硯。然而程硯沒有問他是否改變對收購的立場，而是問他修過的第一艘船，以及誰教他讀天氣訊號。許臨川講起母親的故事：她堅持他在讀金融前，先學一項實用技能。程硯承認自己的家族只期待他學會下命令。兩人比較童年的志願，笑說如今的生活對當年那兩個孤單男孩而言一定很陌生。

At the next table, a counselor was explaining digestion to a child with food sensitivities. The restaurant owner had received a bulk delivery by mistake and could cite the invoice number from memory. Xu turned that detail into an unexpected example of collaboration: ordinary workers remembered facts executives often overlooked. A lecturer from the maritime institute arrived with a descriptive preliminary report about water quality. Its variable measurements did not compel an immediate conclusion, and the temptation to announce something radical would only overwhelm the families waiting for facts.

隔壁桌的輔導員正向一個食物敏感的孩子解釋消化。餐館老闆先前收到一批數量龐大的錯誤貨物，竟能背出發票號碼。許臨川藉此說明合作的重要：普通員工記得主管常忽略的事實。海事研究所講師帶來一份關於水質的描述性初步報告，數值變化尚不能迫使人立刻下結論；若貿然宣布激進說法，只會讓等待真相的家庭不堪負荷。

The chef came out to exclaim that someone had left a thriller novel in the kitchen and a hiking map of the wilderness under the counter. Xu laughed at the absurd discovery. Cheng, usually severe, laughed too. They agreed that it was rational to take the evening off from the investigation, though neither believed the problem had vanished. Outside, the clouds cleared long enough for a little moonlight to touch the pavement. Cheng asked whether he could hold Xu's hand. Xu gave it to him, slowly and without looking away.

廚師跑出來驚呼有人把一本驚悚小說留在廚房，還把荒野健行地圖塞在櫃臺下。許臨川覺得荒唐，忍不住笑；平時嚴肅的程硯也笑了。兩人同意暫時放下調查、享受這個夜晚是理性的選擇，雖然誰也不以為問題已消失。外頭雲散開一會兒，月光落在人行道上。程硯問能不能牽他的手。許臨川慢慢把手交給他，沒有移開目光。

The restaurant owner recognized Xu from the harbor and quietly asked whether the clinic was safe. Xu explained that the repairs continued but the service was operating under a temporary plan. Cheng listened without interrupting. When the owner offered to waive their bill as thanks, Xu declined and explained that the meal was a personal occasion. Cheng paid for his own food, and Xu paid for his. The gesture would not appear in a newspaper, but it marked a line between cooperation in public affairs and the freedom to know another person in private. After dinner, they left the restaurant without cameras and walked slowly through the evening crowd.

餐館老闆認出許臨川，悄悄問診所是否安全。許臨川解釋修繕仍在進行，但服務已依臨時計畫營運。程硯聽著沒有插嘴。老闆想以感謝為由免收餐費，許臨川婉拒，說這是私人聚餐。程硯付自己的錢，許臨川也付自己的。這件事不會登報，卻在公共合作與私下認識一個人的自由之間畫出界線。晚餐後，他們沒有帶著鏡頭離開，只慢慢走進夜晚的人群。

The chef invited them to grill mushrooms themselves over a small outdoor fire. Xu burned the first one; Cheng ate it anyway, and both laughed until the chef brought another plate.

廚師邀他們在戶外小火上親手烤蘑菇。許臨川把第一顆烤焦，程硯仍吃了，兩人笑到廚師又端來一盤。

They walked all the way back to the public ferry rather than calling a car. Cheng stopped before the gate and said, "You should know that I'm not good at being uncertain." Xu answered, "Then practice. I am not a prize at the end of your investigation." Cheng nodded. The city around them was loud with buses and late shoppers, but the space between their joined hands felt private. When Xu let go to board the ferry, Cheng did not follow. He stayed where Xu had asked him to stay, watching the lights move across the water.

他們沒有叫車，而是一路走回公共渡輪站。程硯在閘門前停下說：「你應該知道，我不太擅長面對不確定。」許臨川回答：「那就練習。我不是你調查結束後的獎品。」程硯點頭。周圍充滿公車與夜間購物人潮的聲音，兩人相牽的手之間卻像有一片私密空間。許臨川放開手登船時，程硯沒有跟上。他留在許臨川要求的地方，看燈光在水上移動。
