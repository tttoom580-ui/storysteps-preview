沒有電梯的晚宴

The Gala With No Elevator

他的帝國靠岸時：總裁與不出售碼頭的男人 · 第 11 / 25 章 · 4000–5000 · 英中對照 · 校訂中

At Aster's annual gala, the grand entrance resembled a casino, complete with crystal lights and guests who came to bet on the company's future. Cheng was the only heir present, and the board expected him to characterize the cooperative as a stubborn obstacle. Xu arrived with the wheelchair users from the harbor workshop. The elevator was broken. "This is an apt description of your accessibility policy," Xu said, studying the staircase. Cheng's expression changed. He moved the speeches downstairs and asked the organizers to upgrade the entrance before inviting another group.

星衡年度晚宴的豪華入口像一座賭場，水晶燈下站滿想押注公司未來的賓客。程硯是到場的唯一繼承人，董事會希望他把合作社形容成頑固的障礙。許臨川帶著碼頭工作坊的輪椅使用者抵達，卻發現電梯壞了。「這真是對你們無障礙政策很貼切的描述，」許臨川望著樓梯說。程硯神情一變，將演講移到樓下，並要求主辦單位改善入口才再邀請其他團體。

The gala committee initially argued that moving the speeches would ruin the event's expensive design. Xu asked them to look at the empty staircase instead. People had arrived from across the city, but several guests could not enter the main room at all. Cheng examined the emergency exit plan and discovered the accessible route ended at a locked service door. The committee had signed the safety report without walking the path. He instructed the organizers to pause admissions and reopen the lower hall as a shared space. The musicians moved their stands, the caterers carried trays downstairs, and the evening continued. Its most memorable achievement was not the lighting but the willingness to change the plan.

晚宴委員會起初爭辯，移動講臺會毀掉昂貴的活動設計。許臨川請他們看看空蕩的樓梯。賓客從城裡各處趕來，卻有幾位根本進不了主會場。程硯查看緊急出口圖，發現無障礙路線竟終止於一扇上鎖的服務門。委員會在沒走過路線的情況下就簽了安全報告。他要求主辦者暫停入場，把下層大廳重新開放作共同空間。樂手搬下譜架，餐飲人員把托盤送到樓下，晚宴仍繼續進行。最令人難忘的成就不是燈光，而是願意改變計畫。

An exhibition of clinical equipment drew spectators who gasped at the restored machines. The company claimed copyright over an invention designed at the cooperative, although the original drawings were intact in its archive. A retired sculptor displayed a scar from a childhood accident and recommended that the equipment be offered to places of refuge first. Outside the gallery, a demonstration about the theft of public designs grew noisy enough for someone to howl a protest through a megaphone. An antique rifle mounted behind glass remained only a historical exhibit; no one was permitted to touch it.

臨床設備展吸引了觀眾，大家對修復的機器驚訝得倒抽一口氣。公司宣稱擁有一項合作社設計的發明著作權，儘管原始圖紙仍完好地保存在合作社檔案裡。一位退休雕刻家展示童年事故留下的傷疤，建議先把設備送往避難場所。展廳外，抗議公共設計遭竊取的人聲越來越高，有人甚至透過擴音器大聲呼喊。玻璃櫃中的古董步槍只是歷史展品，任何人都不准碰觸。

Cheng had always been fascinated by the ease with which wealthy guests forgot the workers who served them. Tonight he could no longer ignore it. Xu asked him to stand beside a technician while she explained why a faulty lift endangered patients. He did. The technician spoke to Cheng directly, without fear, because Xu had insisted on that rule before entering. Cheng promised a published repair schedule rather than a vague donation. For once, the press applauded a specific commitment instead of the price of a table.

程硯一向對富有賓客輕易忘記服務者的方式感到著迷，今晚他卻無法再視而不見。許臨川請他站在技師身邊，聽她解釋故障電梯如何危及病人。他照做了。由於許臨川事先堅持這項規則，技師能毫不畏懼地直接對程硯說話。程硯承諾公布修繕時程，而非空泛捐款。這一次媒體稱讚的是具體承諾，而非餐桌價格。

During the reception, an older woman asked Xu whether Cheng had invited him as a fashionable companion. Xu answered that he had come to represent the workshop, and that anything more was nobody else's business. Cheng heard the exchange and did not attempt to improve it with a public romantic declaration. Later he asked Xu whether the question had upset him. "Only because people think they can name a relationship before we do," Xu replied. Cheng considered that answer carefully. He had spent his working life naming projects, assigning values, and putting dates on decisions. Learning to leave a private feeling unnamed until both people were ready felt strange, but also generous.

接待時，一位年長女士問許臨川，程硯是不是把他當成時髦伴侶帶來。許臨川回答自己代表工作坊出席，其他事情與旁人無關。程硯聽見了，卻沒有用公開浪漫宣言試圖把話說得更漂亮。後來他問許臨川是否因此不快。「只是因為大家以為可以比我們更早替關係命名，」許臨川說。程硯仔細想了。他整個職業生涯都在命名計畫、估價、替決策訂日期。學著把私人感情留到兩人都準備好再命名，感覺陌生，卻也寬厚。

The old exhibition could still fascinate a child, and the first spectator let out a gasp at the restored lift. Cheng asked the technician to recommend a schedule, not a miracle. The sheer number of repairs remained daunting, but now each had an assigned owner.

舊展覽仍能使孩子著迷，第一位觀眾看見修復的電梯時倒抽一口氣。程硯請技師建議時程，而不是許下奇蹟。修繕數量之多仍令人望而生畏，但如今每項工作都有明確負責人。

On the balcony afterward, Xu admitted that he had expected a humiliating spectacle. Cheng answered that he had expected Xu not to come at all. They stood close enough to hear each other breathe above the traffic. "I didn't come for your money," Xu said. "I know." Cheng looked unexpectedly shy. "Would you believe me if I said I was glad you came for me?" Xu did not offer a kiss. He touched Cheng's wrist once and replied, "I'll believe you if you keep listening tomorrow."

事後在陽臺上，許臨川承認原以為會碰上羞辱人的場面。程硯則說他根本沒想到許臨川會來。兩人站得很近，越過車流聲還能聽見彼此呼吸。「我不是為你的錢來的，」許臨川說。「我知道。」程硯看起來意外害羞。「如果我說我很高興你是為我而來，你會相信嗎？」許臨川沒有親他，只輕碰一下他的手腕，回答：「如果你明天還願意聽，我就相信。」
