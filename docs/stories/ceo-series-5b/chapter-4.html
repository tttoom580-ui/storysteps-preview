一條生命線的價格

The Price of a Lifeline

他的帝國靠岸時：總裁與不出售碼頭的男人 · 第 4 / 25 章 · 4000–5000 · 英中對照 · 校訂中

The cooperative's small clinic faced closure when the city revised its budget. An elderly commuter told Xu that the northern province had promised a medical boat years ago, but no one could locate the order. Cheng had anticipated an ordinary discussion about fuel. Instead, he confronted a maze of bureaucracy, a denial stamped on every form, and a coincidence that bothered him: the medical boat's recorded diameter was too large to pass beneath the bridge. No one had tested whether the vessel named on the invoice had ever existed.

市府調整預算後，合作社的小診所面臨關閉。一位年長通勤者告訴許臨川，北方省份多年前承諾提供醫療船，但沒人找得到訂單。程硯原以為只是普通的燃料討論，卻碰上錯綜複雜的官僚程序、每張表格上蓋著的否決章，以及一個令他困惑的巧合：醫療船紀錄上的船身直徑大得根本無法通過橋下。沒人測試過發票上的那艘船是否真正存在。

The clinic's doctor refused to let the debate become an excuse for postponing treatment. She divided patients according to urgency and asked every helper to sign for the supplies they carried. Cheng offered to assign private guards, but she requested trained nurses and a dependable boat instead. Xu supported her choice. A service could look impressive in a photograph and still fail the people who needed it most. They inspected the temporary dock together, finding loose boards near the ramp and a missing handrail at the steps. Cheng called the engineer immediately, then stayed until the repairs were checked. This time he did not count the hours as a loss of executive time.

診所醫師拒絕讓爭議成為延後治療的藉口。她依緊急程度分流病人，請每位協助者為搬運的物資簽名。程硯提議派私人保鑣，醫師卻要求受訓護理師與可靠船隻。許臨川支持她的選擇。一項服務可能拍起照來很出色，實際上卻辜負最需要它的人。他們一起檢查臨時碼頭，發現坡道旁木板鬆脫、階梯缺少扶手。程硯立刻叫工程師來，並留下直到修復驗收。這一次，他沒有把這些時間當成執行長的損失。

The clinic had a distinctive blue roof, painted by nursing faculty volunteers. Its fiscal report showed that only a fraction of the promised aid had arrived. A marine hazard notice warned of a toxic algae bloom, while the physician described an acute illness among workers and rising mortality among older patients. Two independent tests ran in parallel. One measured the water; the other examined the payments. A local practitioner entered a plea for keeping treatment open while the accounts were investigated. Xu refused to treat residents as evidence in somebody else's dispute.

診所有一片醒目的藍色屋頂，是護理學院志工漆的。財政報告顯示承諾的補助僅有一小部分到帳。海事危險公告警告有毒藻華，而醫師描述工人出現急性疾病、年長患者死亡率上升。兩項獨立測試同步進行：一項檢測水質，另一項檢查付款。當地醫療工作者請求查帳期間仍保留治療服務。許臨川拒絕讓居民變成別人爭執裡的證據。

The laboratory report could not yet uncover the source of the algae, and Cheng deliberately said so at the press conference. He also told the city that his company would pay for a temporary boat without requiring any vote on the acquisition. Xu asked whether that was charity or negotiation. "It is a bridge for patients," Cheng said. "If you think it buys your signature, tear up the check." Xu took the funding letter, read the conditions twice, and kept a copy for the cooperative's counsel.

實驗室報告還無法揭露藻華的來源，程硯在記者會上刻意說清楚。他也告訴市府，公司願意出資提供臨時醫療船，不要求合作社對收購進行任何投票。許臨川問這究竟是慈善還是談判。「它是給病人的一座橋，」程硯說，「如果你覺得那是拿來買簽名的，就把支票撕掉。」許臨川收下資助函，讀了兩遍條款，並留一份給合作社法律顧問。

Back at the waiting room, an elderly fisherman told Cheng that he had worked through three major changes of government and had never seen a budget that knew the shape of his life. Cheng asked what such a budget would include. The fisherman answered with ordinary details: fuel on rainy days, a safe place for his wife's medicine, and a chair he could reach without climbing stairs. Xu watched Cheng write each item down. Later he reminded Cheng that the list was only one person's experience, not a complete community survey. Cheng agreed and asked the clinic to organize interviews with people who rarely attended public meetings. That was how the next proposal began.

回到候診室，一位年長漁民告訴程硯，自己經歷過三次政府大變動，卻從未見過一份真正理解他生活樣貌的預算。程硯問那樣的預算該包含什麼。漁民回答平凡細節：下雨天的燃油、替妻子存放藥物的安全地方，以及不用爬樓梯也能坐到的椅子。許臨川看著程硯把每項記下。稍後他提醒程硯，這只是個人經驗，不是完整社區調查。程硯同意，請診所安排訪談那些很少出席公開會議的人。下一份提案就從這裡開始。

The nurse explained that a hormone test might help one patient but would not identify the source of the water problem. She asked Cheng to anticipate delays in the laboratory and to reassure patients with clear updates rather than confident guesses.

護理師解釋，荷爾蒙檢測也許能幫助其中一位病人，卻無法找出水質問題的來源。她請程硯預先考量實驗室延遲，並用清楚的進度消息安撫病人，而非充滿自信的猜測。

During the return crossing, the boat rocked and Xu's fingers tightened on the rail. Cheng moved closer, but waited until Xu nodded before offering his hand. He was learning that reassurance meant little when it arrived as an order. They discussed the clinic's night schedule rather than the feelings neither was ready to name. Yet Xu noticed that Cheng remembered every nurse's shift and every patient's route. A man who remembered such details might, with effort, learn to see people beyond a balance sheet.

回程渡船搖晃時，許臨川的手指抓緊欄杆。程硯走近，但等到許臨川點頭才伸出手。他正學會：如果安慰變成命令，就沒有多大意義。兩人討論診所夜間時刻表，而不是尚未準備命名的情緒。然而許臨川注意到，程硯記得每位護理師的班表及每位病人的路線。這樣記得細節的人，也許經過努力，能學著在資產負債表之外看見人。
