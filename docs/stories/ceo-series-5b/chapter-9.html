他的帝國靠岸時：總裁與不出售碼頭的男人 · 第 9 / 25 章 · 4000–5000 · 英中對照 · 校訂中

A scandal began with a photograph taken outside Cheng's residence. His bodyguard had pushed a reporter behind a boundary line, and the picture made it appear that Xu had ordered the action. "I was an ass for allowing that arrangement," Cheng said, surprising his own staff. He dismissed the security contractor until an independent investigator could determine whether workers had been treated unfairly. Xu insisted that the review comprise interviews with ordinary visitors as well as executives. A company cannot claim to protect people while making them afraid to speak.

一張拍於程硯住處外的照片引起風波。保鑣把記者推過警戒線，畫面看來卻像是許臨川下令的。「我竟允許這種安排，真是個笨蛋，」程硯說，連員工都很驚訝。他暫停保全承包商的合約，等獨立調查員查清工人是否遭不公平對待。許臨川堅持調查應包括普通訪客與主管的訪談。一家公司若讓人害怕發言，就不能自稱是在保護人。

The security review was uncomfortable for everyone involved. Guards felt they were being blamed for following instructions, while visitors feared that criticizing guards would put them on a blacklist. Xu suggested separating the questions: who wrote the rule, who trained staff, what happened at the door, and what could prevent a repeat? An outside mediator accepted the proposal. Cheng did not sit at the head of the table. He placed his own copy of the old policy in the evidence box and let the mediator call witnesses in a neutral order. The first guard to speak admitted that he had never received training in assisting people with limited mobility. Nobody shouted at him; the answer itself showed where change was needed.

保全審查讓每個相關的人都不自在。警衛覺得自己只是照指示行事卻被責怪，訪客則怕批評警衛會上黑名單。許臨川建議把問題分開：誰訂規則？誰負責訓練？門口實際發生什麼？如何防止重演？外部調解人接受這項提議。程硯沒有坐在主位，而是把自己的舊政策副本放進證據箱，讓調解人以中立次序傳喚證人。第一名發言警衛承認從沒受過協助行動不便者的訓練。沒人對他大喊；這個答案本身就指出了改革方向。

The investigator learned that the entry rules discriminate against some applicants, although everyone eligible to visit the cooperative should be treated alike. A revised version of the policy would incorporate clear limits on the use of force and offer extra help to vulnerable visitors. One worker described slipping on a fluid patch beside a decorative pyramid; another recalled an old souvenir stand blocking the wheelchair route. A therapist said a safe building required more than expensive cameras. Xu arrived in worn sandals, his passionate arguments far more persuasive than the flawless suit Cheng had offered to buy him.

調查員發現入口規則對部分申請人有歧視，儘管所有符合資格的合作社訪客都該受到平等對待。修訂版本會納入使用武力的明確限制，並為脆弱訪客提供更多協助。一名工人描述曾在裝飾金字塔旁的液體積水區滑倒；另一人記得舊紀念品攤擋住輪椅路線。治療師說，安全的建築不能只靠昂貴監視器。許臨川穿著磨舊的涼鞋到場，他充滿熱情的論點比程硯原先想買給他的完美西裝更有說服力。

Cheng wanted to undo the entire contract at once. Xu said that anger was not a substitute for a valid process. They interviewed staff, preserved the original recording, and published a plan with independent review dates. The incident had embarrassed both men, but Xu was less concerned about headlines than about the driver who feared losing his job for speaking honestly. Cheng offered the driver temporary paid leave. Xu checked that the offer came without a silence condition, then allowed himself a small, grateful smile.

程硯想立刻撤銷整份合約。許臨川說，生氣不能代替有效程序。他們訪談員工、保存原始錄影，並公布附有獨立審查日期的計畫。事件讓兩人都很難堪，但許臨川比起新聞標題，更擔心那位怕因誠實說話而失業的司機。程硯給司機有薪暫時休假。許臨川確認沒有附加禁言條件，才露出一個小小的感謝笑容。

In the afternoon, Xu walked through the new entrance with two volunteers who used different mobility aids. They found that the signs were too high and the waiting space too narrow. Cheng watched the demonstration without touching anyone's chair or offering a solution before it was requested. Later, his assistant asked why he had allowed an ordinary visitor to criticize a company contractor on camera. "Because the visitor had to use the door," Cheng said. The sentence sounded obvious once spoken, yet it challenged years of company habits. Xu heard the answer and allowed himself to imagine that changes in a large institution could begin with small acts of attention.

下午，許臨川陪兩位使用不同輔具的志工走過新入口。他們發現標誌太高、等候區太窄。程硯看著示範，沒有碰任何人的輪椅，也沒有在別人提出需求前先給答案。稍後助理問，他為何容許普通訪客在鏡頭前批評公司承包商。「因為訪客真的要用那扇門，」程硯回答。話一說出來好像理所當然，卻挑戰了公司多年習慣。許臨川聽見後，開始想像大型機構的改變，也許真的能從注意小事開始。

Xu left one sandal beside the library door to dry after the rain. Cheng placed it neatly by the other, resisting the urge to turn a small practical gesture into a declaration.

許臨川把一隻被雨淋濕的涼鞋放在圖書館門邊晾乾。程硯把它整齊放回另一隻旁邊，忍住把這個小動作說成什麼重大宣言的衝動。

Later, at the terrace of the public library, Cheng confessed that his father had taught him to solve fear with guards and money. Xu said he did not want guards standing between them. "I don't need to be guarded from the world," he told Cheng. "I need you to believe me when I set a boundary." Cheng put his phone away and listened. That quiet act offered no dramatic photograph, yet it mattered more to Xu than any speech the chief executive had delivered.

後來在公共圖書館的露臺上，程硯坦言父親教他用保鑣和金錢解決恐懼。許臨川說他不想看見保鑣站在兩人之間。「我不需要別人把世界隔開，」他對程硯說，「我需要你在我劃下界線時相信我。」程硯收起手機，認真聽。這安靜的動作拍不出戲劇性照片，對許臨川而言卻比執行長任何演說都重要。
