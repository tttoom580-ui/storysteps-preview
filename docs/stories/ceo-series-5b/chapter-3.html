消失的名冊

The Register That Disappeared

他的帝國靠岸時：總裁與不出售碼頭的男人 · 第 3 / 25 章 · 4000–5000 · 英中對照 · 校訂中

The original membership register should have been stored at the old pier office, but its shelf was empty. Xu took Cheng to the creek behind the warehouse, where flood marks showed how often official maps had been wrong. A visiting correspondent accused the cooperative of financial abuse; Cheng's deputy answered with an insult that only deepened the tension. Xu demanded a proper consultation rather than a contest of contempt. "If you want to defend me," he told Cheng, "begin by letting me speak." Cheng apologized to the reporter and asked what document supported the allegation.

原始社員名冊本應存放在舊碼頭辦公室，架上卻空空如也。許臨川帶程硯走到倉庫後的小溪，洪水痕跡顯示官方地圖曾多次出錯。一位來訪記者指控合作社濫用資金；程硯的副手以侮辱回應，只讓緊張升高。許臨川要求正式諮詢，而不是比誰更會輕蔑。「如果你想替我辯護，」他對程硯說，「先讓我說話。」程硯向記者道歉，並請他說明指控依據哪份文件。

The reporter had obtained a photograph of the empty shelf and treated it as proof that someone had destroyed evidence. Xu asked her to show the caption and the date. The image was real, but it had been taken after the room was cleared for repairs, not during the alleged theft. Cheng could have used the mistake to ridicule the newspaper. Instead, he asked the reporter to publish a correction and offered the original cleaning schedule for verification. She agreed, though she reserved the right to investigate the missing register. Xu approved of that distinction. The public deserved scrutiny of powerful institutions, but scrutiny needed the same care when the accusation was directed at people without expensive legal teams.

記者拿到空書架的照片，當成有人銷毀證據的證明。許臨川請她出示照片說明與拍攝日期。影像是真的，卻拍於房間清空準備維修之後，而非所謂竊盜當時。程硯本可藉此嘲諷報社，卻請記者刊登更正，並提供原始清潔時程供查證。她同意，但保留繼續調查失蹤名冊的權利。許臨川認同這項區別。大眾有權監督強大機構，但當指控對象沒有昂貴法律團隊時，監督也需要同樣謹慎。

They adopted a deliberate, diplomatic approach. The first phase was to notify the record office and preserve the remaining files; the second was to interview former members separately. The pier's damaged ecosystem made the missing records especially important, because an extinct shorebird had once nested where a private warehouse now stood. The odds of finding a complete register seemed poor. One worker suggested they sneak into the office at night and swap the shelves before inspectors arrived. Xu rejected the plan: a sustainable victory could not begin with a secret exchange of evidence.

他們採取刻意審慎、具外交手腕的方法。第一階段通知登記處並保全剩餘文件；第二階段分別訪談舊社員。碼頭受損的生態系讓失蹤紀錄格外重要，因為如今私人倉庫所在的地方，從前曾有一種已滅絕的岸鳥築巢。找到完整名冊的機率似乎不高。有工人提議夜裡偷偷進辦公室，趁稽查員抵達前調換書架。許臨川否決了：能長久維持的勝利不能從秘密交換證據開始。

Cheng's father called from the capital to announce a successor who would replace him if the acquisition failed. The threat hit Cheng in the gut. He had grown up winning trophies and believing achievement would buy safety, yet the old man could discard him in a single call. Xu heard the silence afterward and did not pretend to know how that felt. Instead, he passed Cheng a cup of tea and said the pier would still need the truth, with or without a powerful surname attached.

程硯的父親從首都打來電話，宣布若收購失敗，就找接班人取代他。威脅像一拳打在程硯腹部。他從小獲得一座座獎盃，以為成就能換來安全，父親卻能用一通電話把他拋棄。許臨川聽見通話後的沉默，沒有假裝懂那種感受，只遞給他一杯茶，說碼頭仍需要真相，不管背後有沒有顯赫的姓氏。

The office was narrow and smelled of damp wood. A rusted fan stood beside the cabinet, and somebody had left a cup of dry tea on the windowsill years before. The retired clerk described how records had been moved during each storm, sometimes into homes and sometimes onto boats. His memory gave the investigators useful directions but could not establish the order of every transfer. Xu asked him to mark the places on a map rather than guess at dates. Cheng brought a lamp close enough to make the faint writing visible without touching the old paper. When the list began to emerge, the two men exchanged a brief glance. Discovery felt less like triumph than like relief.

辦公室狹窄，散發潮濕木頭味。鏽蝕的電扇站在櫃子旁，窗臺上有人多年前留下了一杯乾掉的茶。退休職員描述每次暴風過後文件如何搬移，有時進住家，有時搬上船。他的記憶提供了有用方向，卻無法確定每次轉移的順序。許臨川請他在地圖上標示地點，而不是猜日期。程硯把燈移近，讓淡去的字跡可辨，卻沒有碰觸舊紙。名單逐漸浮現時，兩人短暫交換眼神。發現真相不像勝利，更像終於能鬆一口氣。

On a high shelf, the clerk found a single trophy engraved with the first cooperative director's name. It had been used as a paperweight, never as proof of ownership. Xu asked that it remain with the archived register.

職員在高架上找到一座刻著合作社首任主任名字的獎盃。它一直被當成鎮紙，而不是所有權證明。許臨川要求獎盃與名冊一起保存。

A retired clerk finally remembered a locked cabinet under the floor of the former ferry office. The two men went there together, with an independent witness and a camera recording every step. Inside lay half the register, brittle from moisture but legible. Xu smiled for the first time that day. Cheng wanted to take the folder away and protect it in his private safe; he stopped himself and asked where a neutral archive could store it. Xu's answer was almost a whisper: "Now you are beginning to understand why I stayed."

一位退休職員終於想起舊渡輪辦公室地板下有個上鎖的櫃子。兩人帶著獨立見證人與全程錄影的攝影機一同前往。櫃中有半本名冊，紙張因潮濕而脆弱，字卻仍可辨認。許臨川那天第一次露出笑容。程硯想把資料夾帶走，鎖進自己的私人保險箱；他忍住衝動，改問哪個中立檔案庫能保存它。許臨川幾乎輕聲說：「你現在開始明白我為何留下了。」
