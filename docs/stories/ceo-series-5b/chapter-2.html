山脊公路

The Ridge Road

他的帝國靠岸時：總裁與不出售碼頭的男人 · 第 2 / 25 章 · 4000–5000 · 英中對照 · 校訂中

A storm cut the coastal road before dawn. Xu's destination was the ridge station, where a broken sensor had left fishing boats without a safe weather warning. Cheng arrived in an immaculate coat and announced that Aster could convert its corporate vehicles into an emergency transport fleet. Xu pointed to a narrow bridge. "Those trucks cannot pass," he said. "Your money does not make them smaller." Cheng took off the coat, rolled up his sleeves, and asked the workers which vehicle could haul the equipment instead. The answer was an ordinary farm cart.

黎明前的一場暴風雨切斷了沿海公路。許臨川要前往山脊站，因為故障的感測器讓漁船失去安全的天氣警報。程硯穿著一件一塵不染的外套趕到，宣稱星衡可把公司車輛改成緊急運輸隊。許臨川指著狹窄的橋說：「那些卡車過不去。你的錢不能把它們變小。」程硯脫下外套、捲起袖子，問工人哪種車能拖運設備。答案是一輛普通農車。

They chose the cart because the driver understood the bridge better than the outside consultants. He had crossed it for twenty years and could tell from the boards when the weight was uneven. Cheng listened to the explanation with surprising concentration. He had once approved a transport budget while looking only at the total cost, unaware that the least expensive road was also the safest for small vehicles. Xu drew a new timetable on the back of an envelope. Each stop included a place for rest, a way to turn back, and the name of a person who would answer the radio. The route would take longer than Cheng's original plan, but nobody objected after watching the water rush below.

他們選擇農車，是因為車夫比外來顧問更了解那座橋。他走了二十年，光從橋板聲音就能聽出重量是否分布不均。程硯意外專心地聽著解釋。他曾只看總成本就批准運輸預算，沒想到最便宜的路也最適合小型車輛、安全性最高。許臨川在信封背面畫出新時刻表。每一站都標出休息地點、折返方法，以及負責回答無線電的人名。這條路比程硯原先的計畫慢，但大家看見橋下的急流後，再也沒有人反對。

The repair was beneficial to every village on the coast, but the contract raised a question of liability if a boat sank during the outage. A local respondent to the safety survey recalled an epidemic years earlier, when a messenger doing an errand had carried medicine over the same ridge. Xu called that account extraordinary but insisted it should be checked. Cheng kept a reminder in his notebook: an appealing story was not evidence. When rain made the cart plunge toward a ditch, he held the rope with both hands while Xu directed the driver.

這次修繕對沿岸每個村落都有好處，但合約也引出問題：若警報中斷期間有船沉沒，責任由誰承擔？一位參與安全調查的受訪者回憶，多年前發生流行病時，一名跑腿信使曾越過同一段山脊送藥。許臨川覺得這故事非同尋常，卻堅持仍須查證。程硯在筆記本寫下提醒：動人的故事不等於證據。雨水讓農車猛地朝水溝滑去時，他雙手抓緊繩索，由許臨川指揮車夫。

They sheltered in a kitchen where an old woman served fish stew. A torch hung beside the door, illuminating the documents spread across the table. The emergency plan needed more than a cosmetic trim: it required a transformation in how warnings reached boats. Cheng was vocal about the temptation to approve a fast temporary solution. Xu refused to confuse speed with care. They drew two different routes, one for people and one for supplies, and agreed that neither would run until the bridge engineer confirmed its load limit.

他們躲進一間廚房，老婦人端來魚燉菜。門旁掛著火把，照亮桌上攤開的文件。緊急計畫需要的不只是表面修飾，而是警報傳遞方式的徹底轉變。程硯坦率說出自己想快速批准臨時方案的誘惑。許臨川不願把速度誤當成照顧。他們畫出兩條不同路線，一條供人通行，一條供物資運送，並同意橋梁工程師確認載重上限前，兩條都不啟用。

After the cart reached the station, the repair crew checked the new warning signal against the older mechanical bell. The two systems did not agree at first, so they stopped and tested the wiring again. Cheng was embarrassed to discover that the error came from a subcontractor whose invoice he had approved. Xu did not ask him to resign over a single faulty connection; he asked him to make the correction visible and to prevent the same mistake from being hidden next time. They wrote the adjustment in the public maintenance book. For Cheng, accustomed to presenting finished achievements, leaving a record of failure felt uncomfortable. For the harbor crew, it was simply how responsible maintenance worked.

農車抵達山站後，維修隊把新警報訊號與舊機械鐘相互比對。兩套系統起初不一致，於是他們停下重新檢查線路。程硯難堪地發現錯誤來自一個他曾批准發票的分包商。許臨川沒有因為單一接線錯誤要求他辭職；他要程硯公開修正內容，防止下次同樣錯誤被藏起來。他們把調整寫進公開維護簿。對習慣展示完成成果的程硯而言，留下失敗紀錄很不舒服；對港口工人來說，這只是負責任維護的基本方式。

After midnight, Cheng found Xu asleep at the table, one hand resting on the revised map. He covered Xu with the dry coat he had earlier considered too fine for the pier. When Xu woke, he said nothing about the gesture. Instead, he asked whether Cheng had eaten. The chief executive, who was accustomed to people asking what he wanted, did not know how to answer a question so simple. They shared the last bread, and the first clear warning from the repaired station reached the harbor before sunrise.

午夜過後，程硯發現許臨川在桌邊睡著，一隻手仍放在修正地圖上。他把原先嫌太精緻、不適合碼頭穿的乾外套蓋在許臨川身上。許臨川醒來後，沒有提起這個舉動，只問程硯吃過東西沒有。習慣別人詢問他想要什麼的執行長，竟不知道如何回答這麼簡單的問題。他們分吃最後一塊麵包，修復站的第一道清晰警報也在日出前傳回港口。
