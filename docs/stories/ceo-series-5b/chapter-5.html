潮來之前的一票

A Vote Before the Tide

他的帝國靠岸時：總裁與不出售碼頭的男人 · 第 5 / 25 章 · 4000–5000 · 英中對照 · 校訂中

The city council assembled in a former market hall while the cooperative held its first emergency vote. A constitutional expert explained why the community charter mattered even if a wealthy democrat on the council disliked it. An opposition speaker described the previous flood as an episode of pure incompetence; Xu corrected the exaggeration with a dated maintenance record. Outside, an entire fleet of fishing boats waited on the tide. The owners were not props in a quarrel between rich families. Their work, and sometimes their lives, depended on the decision.

市議會在舊市場大廳開會，合作社同時舉辦第一次緊急表決。一位憲政專家解釋社區章程為何重要，即使有位富有的民主派議員不喜歡它。反對者把上一次水患形容成完全無能造成的事件；許臨川拿出附日期的維修紀錄，糾正這項誇張說法。屋外整支漁船船隊等著潮水。船主不是豪門爭吵中的道具。他們的工作，有時甚至生命，都取決於決定。

The ballots were folded by hand and counted twice. Two residents chosen from opposing groups checked each number before it was entered in the record. Cheng stood outside the counting room because his presence might influence people who were afraid of his company. Xu appreciated the restraint but refused to pretend it had come naturally. The cooperative's charter existed precisely because goodwill could change, especially when money and fear entered a room together. A volunteer emerged with the first count and asked whether a disputed ballot should be included. Xu did not decide alone; he called the observers and opened the rulebook. The process took longer, but the result could withstand questions later.

選票由人手折好，清點兩次。兩名分別由對立團體選出的居民在數字登錄前交叉核對。程硯站在計票室外，因為他的出現可能影響害怕公司勢力的人。許臨川欣賞這份克制，卻不會假裝那是天生的。合作社章程存在，正因為善意可能改變，尤其當金錢與恐懼同時進入房間。志工帶著第一次計票結果出來，問有爭議的選票是否應列入。許臨川沒有獨斷，而是召集觀察員、翻開規則手冊。程序花更久，但結果日後更能接受質疑。

Cheng's director offered an incentive to anyone who signed early, a tactic that looked painfully like greed. An old farmer called himself a peasant and asked why his herb garden would be classified as retail space. The crowd began to rattle the metal doors. News of a recession had already made families nervous, and a massive redevelopment was beyond their threshold of trust. Xu recruited volunteers to count ballots in public. A lawyer said the figures would form a substantial part of the final verdict, but could not replace a safety inspection or a review of the contracts.

程硯的部門主管向提早簽名的人提供獎勵，這種策略看來像令人不安的貪婪。一位老農自稱農民，問他的藥草園為何被列成零售空間。人群開始搖晃金屬門。經濟衰退的消息早已讓家庭不安，而龐大的改建工程超過了他們信任的門檻。許臨川招募志工公開計票。律師表示數字會成為最後判定的重要部分，但不能取代安全檢查與合約審查。

When a stone smashed a window, Xu stopped the count until everyone could move away from the glass. Cheng's security chief demanded that the meeting end. Cheng refused. He asked for the doors to be opened and for volunteers from both sides to watch the resumed count. The result was narrow but clear: the cooperative would not sell, though it would negotiate access to the emergency dock. The resolution spread quickly through the city, not as a declaration of war but as proof that a powerful company could be told no.

有石頭砸碎窗戶時，許臨川暫停計票，先讓大家遠離玻璃。程硯的保全主管要求立刻散會，程硯拒絕。他要求打開大門，並讓雙方志工監督重新計票。結果差距不大，卻很清楚：合作社不出售，但願意協商緊急碼頭的使用。決議很快傳遍全城，不是宣戰，而是證明強大的公司也可以被拒絕。

The town's youngest volunteer had never spoken at such a meeting before. She said the dock was not beautiful in the way a tourist expected; its paint peeled, and its fish stalls smelled strongly in summer. But her grandfather had taught her to mend nets there, and the little workshop had paid for her school books. She wanted better facilities without losing that history. Cheng listened as though hearing a language that his training had somehow excluded. When the vote was complete, he did not offer a speech about generosity. He asked the planning office to remove the unauthorized eviction dates from its timetable immediately.

鎮上最年輕的志工從未在這類會議發言。她說碼頭不像觀光客期待的那麼漂亮：油漆剝落，夏天魚攤氣味強烈。但祖父在那裡教她補網，小工作坊還付過她的教科書費。她想改善設施，不想失去歷史。程硯聽著，彷彿聽見自己受過的訓練竟排除掉的語言。投票結束時，他沒有發表慷慨演說，而是要求規劃處立即刪除未經授權的驅離日期。

The peaceful conclusion did not end the widespread argument over redevelopment. Xu asked volunteers to recruit neighbors for an open inspection, while Cheng warned his staff that no one had permission to smash a locked door in the name of gathering evidence.

平和的結局並沒有終結廣泛的改建爭議。許臨川請志工招募鄰居參與公開檢查，程硯則警告員工，任何人都無權以蒐集證據為名砸開上鎖的門。

Cheng congratulated Xu in front of the cameras, then left without asking for a private reward. Xu followed him to the stairs and found him holding the ruined acquisition plan. "You look angry," Xu said. "I am," Cheng replied. "Mostly at myself. I thought every problem was a locked door, and I had the largest key." Xu laughed despite the long day. "Sometimes the door belongs to someone else." Cheng looked at him with a softness that was almost dangerous. "Then I suppose I should knock."

程硯在鏡頭前向許臨川道賀，隨後離開，沒有索取任何私下回報。許臨川追到階梯口，看見他拿著已失效的收購計畫。「你看起來很生氣，」許臨川說。「是的，」程硯回答，「主要是在氣我自己。我以為每個問題都是上鎖的門，而我手上有最大的鑰匙。」許臨川在漫長的一天後仍忍不住笑。「有時候門是別人的。」程硯望著他，神情柔和得幾乎危險。「那我想，我應該先敲門。」
