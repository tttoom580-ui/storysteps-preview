A Poisoned Dinner Invitation

他的帝國靠岸時：總裁與不出售碼頭的男人 · 第 6 / 25 章 · 4000–5000 · 英中對照 · 校訂中

Cheng invited Xu to a charity dinner, but Xu arrived carrying an agenda instead of a gift. The municipal harbor office had ignored complaints about a chemical smell near the fish market. "Beware of polite invitations made while the pipes are still leaking," Xu said. Cheng, who had spent his youth learning how to host ministers, found that warning more useful than a dozen compliments. At the entrance, a photographer tried to make Xu cling to Cheng's arm for a staged portrait. Xu stepped aside, and Cheng instructed the photographer to take pictures only with permission.

程硯邀許臨川參加慈善晚宴，許臨川卻帶著議程而不是禮物。市港務處一直忽略魚市場附近的化學異味投訴。「管線還在漏的時候，要小心那些客氣的邀請，」許臨川說。從小學著招待部長的程硯，竟覺得這句警告比十幾句奉承更有用。入口處一名攝影師想安排許臨川挽著程硯的手臂拍照。許臨川退開，程硯要求攝影師只有在取得同意後才能拍攝。

The kitchen's oldest cook recognized the smell before the engineers did. It reminded her of a cleaner used many years earlier, and she insisted that the pipes be tested before anyone guessed at the cause. Cheng asked for samples to be stored separately and labeled in front of witnesses. Xu checked that workers who spoke up would not lose their shifts. The process was slower than the public-relations team wanted, but it protected both the evidence and the people who supplied it. A volunteer posted a notice explaining what was known, what was uncertain, and when the next update would appear. Families came to read it rather than rely on rumors.

廚房最年長的廚師比工程師更早認出那股氣味。它讓她想起多年前使用的清潔劑，她堅持先測試管線再猜原因。程硯要求樣本分開保存，並在見證人面前貼標籤。許臨川確認願意說話的員工不會因此失去班次。程序比公關團隊希望的慢，卻保護了證據與提供證據的人。志工張貼公告，清楚寫出已知事項、不確定之處，以及下一次更新時間。家庭開始來看公告，而不是依靠謠言。

Inside, a federal consultant described the incident as a minor problem, using a baseball inning as a metaphor for how the harbor would recover. A young municipal nominee objected: the water contained a living organism whose growth could overtake the cleaning filters. The market workers called their protest a rebellion against rigid rules that shielded wealthy tenants. A cook displayed the skeleton of a dead fish beside two bottles labeled vinegar and toxic solvent. A theologian from the local college spoke not about theology but about the duty to tell the truth when neighbors were at risk.

宴會裡，一位聯邦顧問把事件說成小問題，還以棒球局數比喻港口會如何恢復。一名年輕市政候選人反駁，指出水中有種生物，其繁殖可能超過清潔濾網的負荷。市場工人稱抗議是對保護富裕租戶之僵化規則的反抗。廚師在兩瓶分別標示醋與有毒溶劑的液體旁，展示一條死魚的骨架。當地學院的神學家沒有談神學，而是談鄰居陷入危險時說實話的義務。

A waiter brushed past with a tray and struck Cheng's rib. Xu caught the falling glass before it broke. They moved to a quieter table, where Xu's hands began to shake with anger. "Your company signed the maintenance contract," he said. Cheng opened the digital records and found that the laboratory test had been delayed twice. He stopped the dinner's donation presentation and announced a third-party inspection. The room fell silent. It was not a grand gesture of rescue; it was the first time he had publicly admitted that his own name stood on a document he did not understand.

侍者端盤經過時撞上程硯的肋側。許臨川及時接住掉落的玻璃杯。他們移到安靜的桌邊，許臨川氣得雙手發抖。「維護合約是你們公司簽的，」他說。程硯打開電子紀錄，發現實驗室檢測已延後兩次。他中止晚宴的募款簡報，宣布委託第三方檢驗。全場安靜下來。這不是華麗的英雄救援，而是他首次公開承認自己的名字出現在一份自己沒有真正理解的文件上。

The dinner's sponsors had expected smiling photographs and a neat list of donations. Instead, they found themselves sitting at a table with cooks, cleaners, and volunteers who knew the building better than they did. One sponsor asked whether the event was now a failure. Xu said failure would be pretending the leak did not exist. Cheng added that a business could support a community without deciding what the community should feel. Some guests left early. Others remained to help distribute food. At the end of the night, Xu watched Cheng wash a tray without being asked and recognized something more convincing than a grand speech.

晚宴贊助者原先期待笑臉照片與整齊捐款名單，結果卻坐在更了解建築的廚師、清潔員與志工旁。一名贊助者問活動是不是已失敗。許臨川說，真正的失敗是裝作洩漏不存在。程硯補充，企業可以支持社區，卻不該決定社區應有什麼感受。有些客人提前離開，另一些留下協助分餐。當夜晚結束，許臨川看著程硯沒人要求就清洗托盤，覺得這比華麗演講更有說服力。

During cleanup, a child asked what genre the evening's music had been. Xu answered that no song could shield the workers from a dangerous leak. Behind the kitchen door, a frightened apprentice began to sob; Cheng brought her a chair and called a supervisor she trusted.

收拾時，一個孩子問當晚演奏的是什麼音樂類型。許臨川回答，再好聽的歌也無法保護工人免於危險的洩漏。廚房門後，一名受驚的學徒開始啜泣；程硯搬來椅子，叫來她信任的主管。

Xu waited on the balcony while Cheng faced his directors. Below them, vendors were closing their stalls beside the dark water. When Cheng finally joined him, he looked exhausted. "There is a kind of power," Xu said, "in being willing to hear that you were wrong." Cheng offered a tired smile. "My father would call that weakness." Xu leaned on the railing. "Your father isn't here." For once Cheng did not reply with a clever line. He stood beside Xu, letting the night air cool his anger.

許臨川在陽臺等待程硯與董事們談話。樓下，攤販正沿著漆黑水岸收攤。程硯終於走來時，看起來疲憊極了。「願意聽見自己錯了，也是一種力量，」許臨川說。程硯疲倦地笑了。「我父親會稱那是軟弱。」許臨川靠著欄杆。「你父親現在又不在這裡。」這一次程硯沒有伶俐反駁，只站在他身邊，讓夜風吹散怒意。
