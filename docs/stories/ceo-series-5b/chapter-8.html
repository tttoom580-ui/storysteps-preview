失去電力的夜晚

A Night Without Electricity

他的帝國靠岸時：總裁與不出售碼頭的男人 · 第 8 / 25 章 · 4000–5000 · 英中對照 · 校訂中

The annual waterfront gathering began with music and ended in chaos when an electrical shortage left the district dark. Xu, wearing a ragged volunteer jacket, organized a queue outside the old hair salon. A visiting engineer with an expiring visa was among those unable to reach his hotel. Someone called him an alien, and Xu corrected the insult before giving directions. The emergency manager admitted that the public's comprehension of evacuation signs had been poor. He promised that every resident would be entitled to clear information, regardless of language or income.

每年的水岸聚會以音樂開場，卻因供電不足使整區陷入黑暗，在混亂中結束。許臨川穿著一件破舊志工外套，在舊美髮沙龍外安排隊伍。一位簽證即將到期的外國工程師也無法回到旅館。有人叫他外星人，許臨川先制止侮辱，再提供方向。緊急管理員承認民眾對撤離標誌理解不足，並承諾不論語言或收入，每位居民都應取得清楚資訊。

The shelter volunteers soon discovered that people understood instructions in very different ways. Some needed written directions, some needed someone to explain the route aloud, and others required a ramp that had been missing from the original plan. Xu asked each group what would help rather than guessing from appearances. Cheng assigned his staff to carry drinking water and spare batteries, but he made clear that the shelter coordinator would decide where those supplies went. He had not forgotten the first morning at the pier, when his expensive car could not fit down a street. Tonight the same lesson appeared in the shape of a crowded corridor and a door that opened the wrong way.

避難所志工很快發現，大家理解指示的方法各不相同。有些人需要書面方向，有些需要人出聲說明路線，另一些則需要原計畫缺少的坡道。許臨川詢問各群體真正需要什麼，而不是看外表猜測。程硯安排員工搬飲水與備用電池，但明確表示物資放哪裡由避難所協調員決定。他沒有忘記初到碼頭那天，昂貴的轎車無法通過狹窄街道。今晚同樣的教訓，以擁擠走廊與開錯方向的門再次出現。

Cheng's strategic team wanted to minimize public concern with a theoretical explanation of the grid failure. Xu demanded flexibility instead: practical lanterns, accessible buses, and truthful maps. A myth circulated that the blackout was a tribute to an old harbor saint; another rumor claimed that a hidden mineral vein had melted the cables. Neither claim had evidence whatsoever. Cheng decided to suspend the ceremonial concert rather than risk a crush near the stage. He ordered the staff to distribute printed instructions across the full spectrum of neighborhoods, including buildings outside the wealthy center.

程硯的策略團隊想用理論性的電網說明，盡量減少公眾擔憂。許臨川要求的是彈性：真正可用的燈、無障礙巴士、誠實的地圖。人們流傳一種神話，說停電是在向港口聖人致敬；另一個謠言稱地下礦脈融化了電纜。兩種說法都毫無證據。程硯決定暫停儀式音樂會，避免舞臺附近發生推擠，並命員工把印刷指示送往各類社區，包括富裕市中心以外的建築。

Xu discovered that the backup batteries at the cooperative were working, but the operator had never been told which switch could power the public shelter. Cheng could have given an order and walked away. Instead, he stood beside the electrician while she explained the sequence to a volunteer, then asked Xu whether the shelter had enough drinking water. By midnight the old hall was warm and orderly. The night taught Cheng that emergencies revealed the cost of plans written without asking who would use them.

許臨川發現合作社的備用電池正常，操作員卻從未被告知哪個開關可以供電給公共避難所。程硯本可以下令後離開，卻選擇站在電工旁，看她向志工解說操作次序，再問許臨川避難所有沒有足夠飲水。午夜時分，舊大廳已經溫暖而有秩序。這一夜讓程硯明白，沒有詢問使用者就寫出的計畫，會在緊急時刻暴露代價。

The silence after an emergency can be more difficult than the noise during it. Families sat waiting for information, and children asked whether the power would come back before morning. Xu refused to give a certain time when engineers could not supply one. Cheng brought his phone to the notice board, offering to post updates directly from the repair team with their names attached. The volunteers agreed after checking that private details would remain hidden. When the first lights returned to the street, a little boy cheered, but Xu still asked the staff to inspect the dark stairwells. A bright window did not mean the whole building was safe.

緊急事件後的安靜，有時比過程中的噪音更難熬。家庭坐著等資訊，孩子問早上前會不會復電。工程師無法確認時，許臨川拒絕給出確切時間。程硯把手機拿到公告板旁，提議直接張貼維修隊附姓名的更新。志工確認私人資料會遮住後才同意。街上第一盞燈亮起時，小男孩歡呼，但許臨川仍請員工檢查黑暗樓梯間。一扇明亮的窗戶，不代表整棟樓都安全。

At a food stall, the manager checked that every volunteer was entitled to water and a rest break. The lights were still off, but the people running the stall refused to charge families who had taken shelter there.

在食物攤，一名管理員確認每位志工都有權得到飲水與休息時間。燈仍未亮，經營攤位的人卻拒絕向前來避難的家庭收費。

They stayed awake beside the emergency radio. Xu's shoulder brushed Cheng's whenever another message arrived, but neither moved away. Cheng finally asked whether Xu wanted dinner the following evening, not as a client or a public ally but simply as himself. Xu considered the question. "You can ask again when the lights are on," he said, "and when the people here are safe." Cheng laughed softly. It was not a refusal forever; it was a time and a condition he was willing to respect.

他們守在緊急無線電旁整夜未眠。每當新訊息進來，許臨川的肩膀就碰到程硯，卻誰也沒有移開。程硯終於問他隔天晚上願不願意一起吃飯，不是以客戶或公開盟友的身分，只是以自己本人的身分。許臨川想了想。「等燈亮了、這裡的人安全了，你可以再問一次。」程硯低聲笑了。那不是永遠拒絕，而是一個他願意尊重的時間與條件。
