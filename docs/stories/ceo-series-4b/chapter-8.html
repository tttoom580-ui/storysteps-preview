不存在的新娘

The Bride Who Did Not Exist

1999，總裁的心動不在合約裡 · 第 8 / 25 章 · 3000–4000 · 英中對照 · 校訂中

用互動版查詞、朗讀與收藏

On Monday morning, Zhixia found her face on the society page of a newspaper. The photograph came from the hotel rooftop. Beneath it, a headline claimed that Yaochuan's president had chosen his future bride. The paper even described Chengqian as an eager bridegroom, although no one had asked either person a question. A company bulletin repeated the rumor, and employees began watching her whenever she crossed the lobby. Zhixia felt her enthusiasm for the new contract disappear. Her work was not an abstract symbol of a romantic conquest. It was a serious inquiry involving people who had trusted her with information. She telephoned Chengqian and asked him to meet in the public meeting room, not his private office. 'We need to stop this before it harms the investigation,' she said.

星期一早上，知夏在報紙的社交版看到自己的臉。照片來自飯店屋頂。底下的標題聲稱曜川總裁已選定未來新娘，甚至把承謙寫成興奮的新郎，雖然沒有任何人問過當事人。公司公告板也轉貼了傳言，知夏每次穿過大廳，員工都會看她。她對新合約的熱情一下消失了。她的工作不是浪漫征服的抽象符號，而是牽涉許多信任她的人的嚴肅調查。她打電話請承謙到公共會議室見面，而非他的私人辦公室。「我們得在這傷害調查以前阻止它。」她說。

Chengqian came with the newspaper folded in his hand. 'I did not authorize this,' he said. 'Then say so publicly,' Zhixia answered. He admitted that his uncle had been pressuring him to acquire an overseas partner through a business marriage. The board viewed an unmarried president as a weak point, and some directors treated a woman's presence as evidence that he could be controlled. 'I will not let their jealousy dominate our work,' he said. Zhixia replied that the problem was bigger than jealousy. 'Gender is not a marketing tool. Your family may discuss your future, but it cannot use me as a cue in a financial performance.' He looked ashamed. He had thought denying the rumor privately would be enough. Now he understood the gulf between his influence and her vulnerability.

承謙拿著折起的報紙走來。「我沒授權這則消息。」「那就公開說清楚。」知夏回答。他承認叔叔一直逼他用商業聯姻取得海外夥伴。董事會把未婚總裁視為弱點，有些董事還把女人的出現當成能操控他的證據。「我不會讓他們的嫉妒主導我們的工作。」他說。知夏回答，問題不只是嫉妒。「性別不是行銷工具。你的家人可以討論你的未來，不能把我當成財務表演的提示道具。」他顯得羞愧。他原以為私下否認就夠了，如今才明白自己的影響力與她的脆弱處境之間，有多大的鴻溝。

They agreed on a short statement: no engagement existed, and Zhixia's contract concerned product safety alone. Chengqian would read it himself. Feng asked whether a softer message might preserve the public's curiosity. Zhixia refused. 'A misleading suggestion can invade someone's life long after a headline is forgotten.' The marketing team also wanted to estimate how much the rumor would increase sales. Chengqian ordered that calculation removed from the weekly report. He did not do so with dramatic anger. He simply recognized that his team had learned to turn every personal detail into commercial value. He told Feng that professional boundaries would constitute a rule for future publicity, not an exception made for one woman. Zhixia kept a copy of the statement beside her signed consulting agreement.

兩人同意發一則簡短聲明：兩人並無婚約，知夏的合約只涉及產品安全。承謙會親自宣讀。馮主任問，語氣若更含蓄，是否能保留大眾好奇心。知夏拒絕。「誤導的暗示即使標題被忘了，仍能侵入一個人的生活。」行銷團隊還想估算傳聞會增加多少銷售。承謙命令將這項計算從週報刪除。他沒有戲劇性地發怒，只是認清團隊早已學會把每個私人細節都變成商業價值。他告訴馮主任，尊重專業界線將成為未來宣傳的規則，不是單為一名女性開的例外。知夏把聲明副本放在已簽署的顧問合約旁。

That afternoon, they visited a flower farm outside the city. The farm supplied a fertile valley's delicate blossoms to the perfume laboratory. Its owner kept records on an old computer running simple inventory software. She explained that the intensity of a scent depended on harvest conditions, not merely on the attractive picture printed on a box. A local composer was rehearsing music for the farm's spring festival. His daughter, an ordinary civilian with no connection to the corporation, recognized Zhixia from the newspaper. 'Are you really marrying that rich man?' she asked. Zhixia answered, 'No. I am checking whether his company is telling the truth about its products.' The girl nodded with surprising seriousness. 'That sounds like a harder job.' Chengqian did not contradict her.

下午，兩人前往城外的花田。那片肥沃河谷供應香水實驗室細嫩的花朵。農場主人用一台老電腦和簡單的庫存軟體記錄收成。她說明香氣強度取決於採收條件，不只是盒子上印得多漂亮。當地一位作曲家正在排練春季慶典的音樂。他的女兒只是與集團毫無關係的普通市民，卻從報上認出知夏。「妳真的要嫁給那個有錢人嗎？」她問。知夏回答：「沒有。我在檢查他的公司是否對產品說實話。」女孩出奇認真地點頭。「那聽起來是更難的工作。」承謙沒有反駁。

On the return journey, Chengqian told Zhixia he had admired how she answered the girl. 'You did not try to embarrass me,' he said. 'I did not need to,' she replied. 'The truth was enough.' A systematic review of the farm's records had revealed no problem with the approved flowers, so they could narrow the inquiry to processing and storage. The discovery was useful, but another question occupied the car. 'If I were not your client,' Chengqian began, then stopped. Zhixia looked out at the river. 'You are my client,' she said gently. 'Finish the work properly first.' He accepted the answer. For the first time in years, he could not use money, position, or impatience to shorten the distance between what he wanted and what another person freely chose.

回程時，承謙告訴知夏，他很欣賞她回答女孩的方式。「妳沒有故意讓我難堪。」「我沒有必要。」她回答。「事實就夠了。」花田紀錄經系統性檢查後沒有發現核准花材的問題，因此調查可縮小到加工和儲存。這個發現很有用，車裡卻還有另一個問題。「如果我不是妳的客戶……」承謙開了口，又停住。知夏望向河流。「你現在就是我的客戶。」她溫和地說。「先把工作做好。」他接受答案。多年來，他第一次無法用錢、地位或不耐煩，縮短自己想要的東西與另一人自由選擇之間的距離。
