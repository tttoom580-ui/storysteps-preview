閥門破裂的夜晚

The Night of the Broken Valve

1999，總裁的心動不在合約裡 · 第 10 / 25 章 · 3000–4000 · 英中對照 · 校訂中

用互動版查詞、朗讀與收藏

Just after nine on Thursday evening, a valve broke in the company's blending room. The sound was sharp enough for a guard to mistake it for an explosion, and the alarm sent workers rushing outside. No one had been trapped, but a technician cut her hand on a metal edge and needed a bandage. Zhixia arrived with the emergency coordinator while Chengqian was still driving from a board dinner. The injured technician was pregnant and worried that she would be punished for leaving her station. Zhixia told her that checking her condition came first and that a qualified clinician should decide whether further care was needed. A visiting surgeon from the nearby clinic helped arrange transport without pretending the injury could be assessed from a telephone description. The incident demanded a careful investigation, not panic.

星期四晚上九點過後，公司調香室裡的一只閥門破裂。聲響尖銳得讓警衛誤以為發生爆炸，警報使工人紛紛跑到室外。沒有人受困，但一名技術員被金屬邊緣劃傷手，需要包紮。知夏與緊急應變協調員趕到時，承謙仍從董事晚宴開車過來。受傷技術員懷有身孕，擔心離開崗位會被處罰。知夏告訴她，先確認健康狀況，後續是否需要照護應由合格醫療人員判斷。附近診所一名外科醫師協助安排交通，沒有假裝能靠電話描述判定傷勢。這起事故需要仔細調查，不是恐慌。

The factory supervisor insisted that the damaged valve was a mere inconvenience. Chengqian arrived in time to hear him. 'If the same part fails tomorrow, what will you call it then?' he asked. Zhixia examined the maintenance records and found that the safety notice had been removed during a hurried installation. The crew had been ordered to install the part before inspection. She asked who had approved the work. A suspicious silence followed. The supervisor finally admitted that he had accepted a bargain from an outside contractor: lower cost in exchange for a shorter inspection. Chengqian did not congratulate himself on discovering the problem. He ordered the room closed and asked that every worker involved receive a written explanation of what would happen next. Zhixia reminded him that a company's resolution mattered only if people could verify it later.

工廠主管堅稱壞掉的閥門只是小麻煩。承謙剛好趕到，聽見這句話。「如果同一個零件明天再壞，你又要叫它什麼？」他問。知夏檢查維護紀錄，發現趕工安裝時安全告示被拆掉。工人原來被要求在檢查前先把零件裝好。她問誰核准施工，現場出現可疑的沉默。主管最後承認，他接受外包商的一筆低價交易，以縮短檢查換取成本下降。承謙沒有為發現問題而自我慶賀，他下令封閉房間，要求向每名相關工人發出書面說明。知夏提醒他，公司的決議只有在日後可被查證時才有意義。

On Friday, the company posted a public notice at its stores and on its new website. The page, simple by modern standards, gave customers the batch numbers under review and a telephone number for refunds. An advertising manager argued that such openness would make a prosperous company appear weak. Zhixia used a diagram to illustrate the opposite: a clear procedure reduced confusion and repeated questions. Chengqian added a personal apology, but she made him remove a line promising that no similar error could ever happen again. 'You cannot forbid every accident by writing it into a speech,' she said. 'You can commit to safer systems and independent checks.' He changed the sentence without argument. Employees who had watched him defend his authority for years exchanged surprised glances.

星期五，公司在門市和新設立的網站張貼公開告示。網頁以當時的標準而言很簡單，列出受審查的批號與退款電話。廣告經理認為這種公開會讓繁榮的企業顯得軟弱。知夏用圖解說明相反的道理：清楚的程序能減少混亂與重複詢問。承謙加上一段個人道歉，但知夏請他刪掉「保證永遠不再發生類似錯誤」那一行。「不是寫進演講裡就能禁止所有事故。」她說。「你能承諾的是更安全的制度和獨立檢查。」他毫不爭辯地改了句子。多年來看慣他捍衛權威的員工交換了驚訝眼神。

In the afternoon, Chengqian found Zhixia at a small historical museum beside the city wall. She had come to collect her mother, who was volunteering at an exhibition on early settlers and the region's changing civilization. A stone tomb outside the museum had become a place where families left flowers for ancestors. Zhixia did not want it turned into a luxury advertising scene. Chengqian had come only to deliver a set of repaired laboratory keys, but he remained to listen to her mother describe the neighborhood. The older woman asked whether he understood that money could not purchase a family's memories. 'I am beginning to,' he answered. Zhixia saw that he was not trying to win her mother with gifts, and that simple restraint softened something in her.

下午，承謙在城牆旁的小歷史館找到知夏。她來接母親，母親在介紹早期移居者與地方文明變遷的展覽裡擔任志工。館外一座石墓已成為家族祭祖獻花的地方，知夏不希望它被改成豪華廣告場景。承謙只是送來修好的實驗室鑰匙，卻留下來聽她母親介紹街區。長輩問他是否理解錢買不到一家人的記憶。「我開始懂了。」他回答。知夏看出他沒有用禮物討好母親，這份克制使她心裡某個地方柔軟下來。

When the museum closed, Zhixia and Chengqian walked beneath the old city trees. 'My uncle wants a revolution in sales,' he said. 'He thinks we can solve everything by changing the director.' Zhixia shook her head. 'Replacing one person while keeping the same pressures is not a revolution. It is a new face on an old problem.' They stopped beside the tram rails. He admitted that he had once believed a critical decision needed only his signature. Now he knew he had to let people disagree. 'I am not particularly good at that,' he confessed. 'I have noticed,' she answered. He laughed quietly. Their hands nearly touched as they turned toward the station, but she kept hers around the strap of her bag. Neither pretended that the moment meant nothing; neither tried to make it mean more than it did.

博物館關門後，知夏與承謙走在老樹下。「叔叔想要一場銷售革命。」他說。「他認為換掉一個主管就能解決一切。」知夏搖頭。「只換人、不改壓力，不是革命。只是舊問題換一張新臉。」兩人在電車軌道旁停下。他承認自己過去相信重大決策只要有他的簽名就夠，現在知道必須讓人有不同意見。「我不是很擅長。」他坦白。「我注意到了。」她回答。他輕聲笑了。兩人轉向車站時，手幾乎碰在一起，她卻仍握著背包肩帶。兩人都沒有假裝這一刻毫無意義，也沒有急著賦予它更多意義。
