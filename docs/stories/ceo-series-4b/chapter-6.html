The Silk Lining

On Monday, the laboratory team opened the fourth crate under a camera. Zhixia worked as the independent consultant, while Su Yuan and a quality officer handled the seals. The box contained display bottles wrapped in linen, though the shipping note described ordinary paper goods. One cloth showed a pale blue dye that had stained the glass. Zhixia used a microscope to inspect the particles and asked the team to isolate the material from every approved sample. 'We are not proving a crime,' she reminded them. 'We are identifying what should not have been mixed.' The quality officer, a conservative man who disliked public questions, demanded that she keep the discovery quiet until the board met. She answered that the consumer notice could not wait if unapproved products had already reached shops.

星期一，實驗室團隊在攝影機前拆開第四只貨箱。知夏以獨立顧問身分監督，蘇沅和品質主管處理封條。箱內是用亞麻布包裹的展示瓶，但出貨單卻寫一般紙製商品。其中一塊布上的淡藍染料沾到了玻璃。知夏使用顯微鏡檢查微粒，要求團隊把這些物品與所有核准樣品隔離。「我們不是在證明有人犯罪。」她提醒大家。「我們是在辨認哪些東西不該混在一起。」品質主管思想保守，不喜歡公開提問，要求她等董事會開完才公布發現。她回答，如果未核准商品已流入店鋪，消費者通知就不能等。

Chengqian arrived after a meeting about the group's latest investment. His secretary had enclosed a handwritten note from Chairman Gu in his folder: WE CANNOT AFFORD ANOTHER PUBLIC EMBARRASSMENT. Zhixia watched Chengqian read it. He looked calm, but his habitual gesture of tightening his watch strap betrayed him. 'Are you angry with your uncle?' she asked. 'I am angry with myself for being ignorant of what happened in my own company.' He spoke without his usual polished grace. For a moment he seemed less like a luxury empire's ruler and more like a tired man discovering that his power had limits. Zhixia said, 'Then learn how to listen to people who work where you cannot see.' He folded the note and asked her to draft the consumer warning.

承謙結束集團最新投資案會議後趕來。祕書在他的資料夾裡附了一張顧董事長的親筆字條：「我們承受不起另一場公開難堪。」知夏看著他讀完。他表面平靜，卻習慣性地把手錶錶帶拉緊，洩漏了情緒。「你在生叔叔的氣嗎？」「我氣自己竟然不知道自己公司發生什麼。」他的話失去平日優雅的修飾。那一刻，他不像掌管奢侈品牌帝國的人，更像剛發現權力有極限的疲倦男子。知夏說：「那就學著聽那些在你看不見的地方工作的人。」他把字條折好，請她草擬消費者公告。

In a nearby studio, the marketing department prepared a television advertisement with a powerful stereo soundtrack. A timid model stood beside a scented fan and began to tremble when a director shouted for another take. Zhixia interrupted and asked whether the model had received an orientation about the untested display. She had not. The director's rage was immediate. 'You are destroying a day's work!' Chengqian shut off the fan before anyone could answer. He instructed the crew to use an unscented visual prop and offered the model the choice to continue or leave with pay for the completed hours. She chose to leave. The scene ended without applause. Still, Zhixia noticed how much quieter the room became when the equipment stopped and a frightened person was allowed to speak.

附近攝影棚裡，行銷部正在拍一支配有強烈立體聲配樂的電視廣告。一名膽怯的模特兒站在香霧風扇旁，導演吼著要重拍時，她開始發抖。知夏打斷拍攝，詢問模特兒是否接受過未測試設備的操作說明。答案是沒有。導演立刻暴怒。「妳毀了一整天的工作！」承謙先關掉風扇，再叫攝影團隊改用無香味的視覺道具，並讓模特兒自己選擇留下或離開，已完成的工時照付。她選擇離開。現場沒有掌聲；知夏卻注意到，設備停下、害怕的人獲准開口以後，房間安靜了許多。

That afternoon, Chengqian showed Zhixia an investment plan for a new clean production room. He wanted the laboratory to become a model of good practice, but the budget included no training for the workers. 'Equipment does not replace an occupation's learned skills,' she told him. He asked how much training would cost. She gave him an estimate and a list of people who should help design it. 'You could lead the entire division,' he suggested. 'You would have a larger office and more control.' She shook her head. 'I can help make the division trustworthy. I do not want to belong to it.' He looked disappointed, then asked whether their working arrangement could still include dinner. Her answer came slowly. 'Dinner as colleagues, perhaps. Not while you are negotiating my contract.'

下午，承謙給知夏看新潔淨生產室的投資計畫。他希望實驗室成為優良作業典範，預算裡卻沒有工人訓練費。「設備不能代替工作累積的專業技能。」她說。他問訓練要花多少，她提供估算和應參與設計的人員名單。「妳可以領導整個部門。」他提議。「會有更大的辦公室，更多控制權。」她搖頭。「我可以協助部門變得可信，但不想成為它的所有物。」他露出失望神色，接著問，他們的工作安排是否仍可能包括共進晚餐。她慢慢回答：「也許可以當同事吃飯。但在你還和我談合約的時候，不行。」

Before leaving, Zhixia returned to the fourth crate. Under the linen lining, Su Yuan had found a second label, attached with glue rather than the manufacturer's usual thread. The label named a subcontractor that had never appeared on the company's approved list. Zhixia photographed it and asked Su Yuan to sign a record with her. He hesitated. 'If this becomes public, will the company blame me?' 'I cannot promise what everyone will do,' she said. 'I can promise that the record will show who found it and how.' The new label gave them a direction, not yet an answer. Chengqian stood at the door, holding the draft consumer notice. For once he did not announce a decision. He waited for Zhixia to finish documenting the evidence, then asked whether she needed a ride home. She chose the train.

離開前，知夏回到第四只貨箱旁。蘇沅在亞麻布內襯下找到第二張標籤，上面用膠黏貼，與製造廠慣用的線繩不同。標籤寫著一家從未出現在公司核准清單上的分包商。知夏拍下照片，請蘇沅與她共同簽署發現紀錄。他猶豫了。「如果事情公開，公司會不會怪我？」「我不能保證每個人會怎麼做。」她說。「但我能保證紀錄會寫明是誰、如何找到它。」新標籤提供一個方向，尚不是答案。承謙站在門邊，拿著消費者公告草稿。這次他沒有先宣布決定，而是等她完成記錄，再問是否需要送她回家。她選擇搭火車。
