The Woman Who Stopped the Launch

1999，總裁的心動不在合約裡 · 第 1 / 25 章 · 3000–4000 · 英中對照 · 校訂中

In April 1999, Ye Zhixia stepped out of a taxi in front of the Meridian Hotel, carrying a gray sample case and a folder of laboratory notes. Behind the hotel, the evening sky had turned pink. Inside, Yaochuan Group was presenting its most expensive new fragrance, First Light. Zhixia had been invited as an independent scent evaluator, not as a guest who would flatter the company president. Her mother had called from their cottage that morning, reminding her to bring a cushion for the long train journey. Zhixia had laughed and packed the cushion anyway. Now, as she crossed the grand lobby, she felt pessimistic about the event. The company's printed presentation promised a tremendous breakthrough, but the reference samples sent to her laboratory had arrived with two different sealing dates.

一九九九年四月，葉知夏帶著灰色樣品箱和一疊實驗筆記，在子午線飯店門前下了計程車。飯店後方的晚霞染成粉紅色。飯店裡，曜川集團正要發表旗下價格最高的新香水《初光》。知夏受邀擔任獨立嗅覺評估師，不是來奉承公司總裁的賓客。早上母親還從河邊的小屋打電話，提醒她長途火車要帶軟墊。知夏笑著照做。此刻穿過華麗大廳，她卻對活動不太樂觀。公司印製的簡報承諾一次驚人的突破，但送進她實驗室的參考樣品卻標了兩個不同的封裝日期。

The ballroom was decorated with an enormous visual display of white flowers. A silk parachute hung from the ceiling, meant to suggest that the fragrance would carry dreams across the world. A string quartet played near the doors. Zhixia noticed that the people serving drinks wore name tags in six languages; the diversity pleased her more than the decorations. She also noticed an exhausted assistant rubbing his eyes. 'Have you tried the sample?' she asked quietly. He nodded. 'It stings. Management says the formula is gentle.' She wrote down his words without turning him into a public witness. From the stage, a salesman called the chief chemist a genius. A few directors gave a polite giggle. Zhixia did not join them. She asked to see the batch ledger before anyone opened another bottle.

宴會廳裡用大片白花布置了一面巨大的視覺裝置。一具絲綢降落傘垂在天花板下，象徵香氣能把夢想送向世界。弦樂四重奏在門邊演奏。知夏發現端飲料的服務員佩戴六種語言的名牌；這種多樣性比裝飾更令她喜歡。她也注意到一名助理疲倦地揉著眼睛。「你試過樣品嗎？」她輕聲問。他點頭。「會刺眼。主管說配方很溫和。」她記下他的話，卻不把他推成公開證人。舞臺上的業務員稱首席化學師是天才，幾位董事禮貌地輕笑。知夏沒有跟著笑，而是要求在開啟另一瓶香水以前先看批次紀錄。

At seven, a tall man in a charcoal suit entered without music or announcement. Lu Chengqian, thirty-seven, was the president of Yaochuan Group. His fierce expression made the nearby executives step aside. He seemed to possess the room before he said a word. 'Ms. Ye,' he said, 'your report is due tomorrow.' 'A possible safety violation is due now,' she replied. Her voice did not rise. She laid two sample cards beside the contract, then explained that one bottle had a sharp trace missing from the other. She could not yet prove why. A director demanded that Chengqian defend his staff in public. Instead, he asked Zhixia what evidence would justify a pause. 'The batch history and a controlled comparison,' she said. 'Not your opinion and not mine.'

七點鐘，一名穿深灰西裝的高個男人走進來，沒有音樂，也沒人通報。他是三十七歲的曜川集團總裁陸承謙。他神情嚴峻，附近的主管自動讓出道路。彷彿還沒開口，他就已掌握整個房間。「葉小姐，妳的報告明天才到期。」「可能的安全違規，現在就得處理。」知夏平穩地回答。她把兩張試香卡放在合約旁，說明其中一瓶有另一瓶沒有的尖銳氣味。她還不能證明原因。一名董事要求承謙當眾替員工辯護；承謙卻問知夏，哪些證據足以支持暫停發表。「批次歷史和受控制的比較測試。」她說。「不是你的看法，也不是我的看法。」

Chengqian's secretary gave him a telephone message from an overseas buyer. He folded it once. 'Stop the demonstration. Seal the open bottles and keep the guests away from them.' The command traveled across the room faster than the music. Someone protested that the company would lose a fortune. 'Perhaps,' Chengqian said. 'But selling a product before we know what is inside costs more.' Zhixia helped convey the instruction to the servers and asked that no one name the assistant who had spoken to her. The salesman said she was ruining everyone's evening. She answered, 'I am trying to prevent a worse one.' For the first time, Chengqian looked at her with something other than irritation. It was not admiration yet; it was the recognition that she would not retreat because he was rich.

承謙的祕書遞來一張海外買家的電話留言。他把紙折了一次。「停止示範。封存開過的瓶子，讓賓客遠離樣品。」命令比音樂傳得還快。有人抗議公司會損失一大筆錢。「也許。」承謙說。「但在不知道瓶中究竟是什麼以前就出售，代價更高。」知夏幫忙把指示傳達給服務人員，也要求不要公開那名向她反映情況的助理姓名。業務員說她毀了大家的夜晚。她回答：「我是想避免更糟的夜晚。」承謙第一次用不是惱怒的眼神看她。那還稱不上欣賞，而是看出這女人不會因為他有錢就退讓。

Later, in an empty conference room, Chengqian placed a new document beside her case. It offered her a full-time position, a private car, and a salary beyond anything her small practice had earned. He had heard that she completed a morning workout before every difficult meeting, and he called that discipline a useful asset. Zhixia read the paper carefully. In its final paragraph, the company claimed ownership of every note she produced. 'That is not consulting,' she said. 'That is purchasing my judgment.' She pushed it back. Her independence was not a spiritual idea; it was a practical defense against pressure. 'Tomorrow I will send you a proposal with clear limits.' He watched her close the case. 'Most people ask what I can give them.' 'I am asking what you intend to take.' His telephone rang, but neither of them moved to answer it.

稍晚，在一間空會議室裡，承謙把一份新文件放到她的樣品箱旁。上面提供全職職位、專車和遠超她小型工作室收入的薪資。他聽說知夏每逢重要會議前都會晨間運動，便說這份自律很有價值。知夏仔細讀完合約。最後一段規定她製作的一切筆記都歸公司所有。「這不是顧問工作。」她說。「這是要買下我的判斷。」她把文件推回去。獨立性對她不是空泛的精神理念，而是抵抗壓力的實際防線。「明天我會寄來條件明確的提案。」他看著她闔上樣品箱。「多數人都問我能給什麼。」「我問的是你打算拿走什麼。」電話響了，兩人卻都沒有伸手去接。
