電話裡的聲音

The Voice on the Telephone

1999，總裁的心動不在合約裡 · 第 9 / 25 章 · 3000–4000 · 英中對照 · 校訂中

用互動版查詞、朗讀與收藏

A customer hotline opened before the investigation was complete. Zhixia asked the staff to record complaints in the words customers actually used, without replacing them with comforting phrases. The first caller spoke with a rural accent and said a tester had irritated her eyes. A young operator, embarrassed by the accent, asked her to repeat the complaint. Zhixia took the earphone and apologized for the interruption. 'Could you tell us the store and date?' she asked. The woman answered carefully. She had come to the city from her homeland inland and felt that the elegant shop assistants had treated her as ignorant. Zhixia promised a written refund procedure, not a medical conclusion she was unqualified to make. After the call, she reminded the operators that modesty and patience mattered more than a polished voice.

調查尚未結束，公司先設立了客訴專線。知夏要求客服按照顧客原本的用詞記錄，不要改寫成安撫人的話。第一名來電者帶著鄉間口音，說試用品刺激了眼睛。一名年輕客服因聽不習慣口音，請她重複。知夏接過耳機，為打斷對方而道歉。「可以告訴我們是哪間店、哪一天嗎？」她問。女人仔細回答，說自己從內陸故鄉來城市，覺得高級店員把她當無知的人。知夏承諾提供書面退款程序，卻不會作出她沒有資格提出的醫療結論。電話結束後，她提醒客服，謙遜與耐心比聲音好聽更重要。

Another caller reported a clumsy sales clerk who had spilled a sample on a customer. The content of the written attachment to the complaint described a damaged coat, but the store file called it 'mere dissatisfaction.' Zhixia frowned. 'That description can mislead the person reviewing it,' she said. She asked the store to retain the original note and to offer the customer a choice between a replacement assessment and a refund. A manager warned that too many refunds could harm the company's economic position. Chengqian, listening from the door, asked him whether a false record would improve it. The manager had no answer. Zhixia knew the company could not repair every unhappy circumstance instantly. But it could stop making people fight to be heard. She wrote a clear checklist and instructed the hotline team to use it.

另一名顧客反映，一名笨手笨腳的店員把試用品灑到客人身上。客訴附件的內容寫著外套受損，店內紀錄卻只寫「單純不滿」。知夏皺眉。「這種描述會誤導審查的人。」她說。她要求店方保留原始紀錄，讓顧客在評估損失和退款間選擇。經理警告退款太多會傷害公司的經濟狀況。站在門口旁聽的承謙問他，做假紀錄能改善經濟狀況嗎？經理答不出來。知夏知道公司無法立即修復每件不愉快的事，卻能停止讓人為了被聽見而爭鬥。她寫好清楚的核對表，指示專線人員使用。

The afternoon brought a strange rumor. A shopkeeper had told customers that First Light used a nuclear process and was therefore either perfectly pure or terribly dangerous. Neither claim matched the production records. Zhixia invited the shopkeeper to a public explanation session instead of humiliating him. On the table she placed a small model of a molecule, a bottle of ordinary water, and a printed description of the actual mixing equipment. 'Revolutionary advertising language does not turn a product into a new branch of physics,' she said. Several listeners laughed. Chengqian watched her turn a frightening word into an understandable question without pretending the problem was solved. When he asked how she stayed calm, she offered him a packet of crunchy biscuits. 'I eat lunch,' she said, 'and I read what people actually wrote.'

下午傳來一則奇怪謠言：一名店主告訴顧客，《初光》使用核能製程，因此不是絕對純淨，就是極度危險。兩種說法都不符合製造紀錄。知夏邀請店主參加公開說明會，而不是羞辱他。她在桌上擺放分子模型、一瓶普通清水及實際混合設備的文字說明。「廣告用革命性的語言，不會讓產品變成物理學的新分支。」她說。幾名聽眾笑了。承謙看著她把一個令人害怕的字變成可理解的問題，卻沒有假裝問題已解決。他問她如何保持冷靜，她遞來一包酥脆餅乾。「我會吃午餐。」她說。「也會讀人們真正寫下的話。」

At the end of the session, Chengqian's sister Lu Xinyi arrived from the overseas office. She had a sharp sense of humor and wore simple clothes, unlike the men surrounding her brother. 'I wanted to peep into the room and see this famous consultant,' she said with a wink. Zhixia replied that she would be easier to see from the front. Xinyi laughed and introduced herself as the group's operations director. She wanted to help trace the fourth crate, but her brother warned that the board might treat her involvement as a family war. 'It is already a family business,' Xinyi said. 'That should not make the facts family property.' Zhixia appreciated her straightforward tone. Together they arranged to compare store records with the warehouse logs, without allowing either sibling to control the evidence.

說明會結束時，承謙的妹妹陸心怡從海外辦公室抵達。她很有幽默感，穿著簡單，與哥哥周圍的男人不同。「我本來想偷看一下這位有名的顧問。」她眨眼說。知夏回答，從正面看會更容易。心怡笑著介紹自己是集團營運總監。她想協助追查第四只貨箱，但哥哥警告，董事會可能把她的參與看成家族內戰。「它本來就是家族企業。」心怡說。「但事實不該因此變成家族財產。」知夏欣賞她直接的語氣。兩人安排比對門市紀錄與倉庫日誌，不讓任何一個兄妹控制證據。

That evening, Chengqian waited outside the hotline room until Zhixia finished the last call. He leaned against the wall, holding two paper cups of tea. 'You have worked since dawn,' he said. 'The callers have too,' she replied. He offered her a lift and she declined, but she accepted the tea. They walked together to the tram stop, leaving enough distance that no photographer could turn a casual gesture into a story. 'I am trying not to make your life harder,' he said. 'Then help me make the work easier for everyone,' she answered. She had no reason to hide that she liked his company, but she would not let affection rewrite the investigation. As the tram arrived, he stepped back and allowed her to board first. It was a small courtesy, and she noticed it.

傍晚，承謙在客訴室外等知夏處理完最後一通電話。他靠著牆，拿著兩杯紙杯茶。「妳從天亮忙到現在。」「來電的人也是。」她回答。他提議送她回家，她婉拒，卻收下茶。兩人一起走到電車站，保持足夠距離，不讓攝影師把普通動作編成故事。「我在努力不要讓妳的生活更困難。」他說。「那就幫我讓每個人的工作容易一點。」她回答。她不必隱藏自己喜歡和他相處，但也不會讓好感改寫調查。電車進站時，他退後一步讓她先上車。那只是小小的禮貌，她卻注意到了。
