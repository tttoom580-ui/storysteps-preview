簽名的代價

The Price of a Signature

1999，總裁的心動不在合約裡 · 第 2 / 25 章 · 3000–4000 · 英中對照 · 校訂中

The next morning, Zhixia arrived at Yaochuan's headquarters with a revised agreement. It guaranteed three things: independent access to the sample records, protection for employees who reported problems, and her right to publish a safety conclusion even if it displeased the president. Chengqian read it beside a glass wall overlooking the harbor. From that height, the landscape looked as carefully arranged as a painting. 'You want a guarantee of freedom while using my laboratory,' he said. 'You want my signature without surrendering yours,' she replied. He raised his gaze and almost smiled. 'You negotiate like someone preparing for a voyage.' 'I am preparing to go where your advertising department has refused to look.' He signed the first page, but left a second page blank for the legal team to revise.

隔天早上，知夏帶著修改後的協議來到曜川總部。協議保證三件事：她能獨立查閱樣品紀錄、反映問題的員工受保護，以及即使結論令總裁不悅，她仍有公布安全判斷的權利。承謙在俯瞰港口的玻璃牆旁閱讀。從這個高度看去，城市景觀整齊得像一幅畫。「妳想用我的實驗室，卻要我保證妳自由。」他說。「你想要我的簽名，卻不讓我保住自己的名字。」她回答。他抬起目光，幾乎笑了。「妳談判像準備遠航。」「我是準備走進你們廣告部不肯看的地方。」他簽了第一頁，第二頁卻留給法務重新修訂。

Their first destination was a former textile mill by an old stone fort. The company rented part of the building to store historical formulas and scent samples. A delivery messenger met them outside and warned that the archive room was colder than the office. In the courtyard, a museum displayed a fossil from the nearby cliffs and a model of an atom, left from a school exhibition. Chengqian asked whether an atomic diagram could prove that a perfume was safe. 'No,' Zhixia said. 'It can describe matter, but it cannot replace testing the actual combination in a bottle.' She opened her notebook to show the difference between a chemical description and a real-use complaint. A delicate ingredient could behave differently when mixed or stored. She would not claim more certainty than the evidence allowed.

他們第一站是舊石堡旁的一座紡織廠。公司租下其中幾間房，存放歷史配方與試香樣品。一名送件人員在門外迎接，提醒檔案室比辦公室冷。庭院裡的博物館陳列著附近海崖挖出的化石，以及一座學校展覽留下的原子模型。承謙問，原子結構圖是否能證明香水安全。「不能。」知夏說。「它能描述物質，不能代替測試瓶子裡的實際組合。」她打開筆記本，說明化學描述與使用者反映之間的差異。細緻的原料在混合或儲存後可能有不同表現。她不願宣稱證據尚未支持的確定性。

The mill's caretaker, Mrs. Qiao, spoke softly, almost in a murmur. She had worked there since her parents completed their immigration papers decades before. When Zhixia asked about a missing box, the woman showed her a narrow shelf hidden by a curtain. 'We were told to retain the old files,' she said. 'But last month someone moved the newest ones into the chairman's private room.' Chengqian's jaw tightened. His uncle, Gu Zhengxun, chaired the board and had managed the company before him. Zhixia watched the change in his face without asking an intimate question about the family. 'To what extent can we trust this register?' she asked. Mrs. Qiao pointed out that each transfer should have carried two signatures. Four recent entries carried only one.

紡織廠的管理員喬太太說話很輕，幾乎像低語。數十年前她父母辦好移民文件後，她就一直在此工作。知夏詢問一只失蹤的紙箱，喬太太指給她看一個被布簾遮住的窄架。「他們叫我們保留舊檔案。」她說。「但上個月有人把最新的搬進董事長的私人房間。」承謙的下顎繃緊。他的叔叔顧正勛是董事長，也曾在他之前經營公司。知夏看見他神色改變，卻沒有追問親密的家族問題。「我們能在多大程度上相信這份登記簿？」她問。喬太太指出，每一次移交都應有兩個簽名，最近四筆卻只有一個。

At lunch, Chengqian suggested that a fast, productive inquiry would finish before investors noticed the missing entries. Zhixia refused to make speed the main measure. 'A frightened worker may remember dates slowly. Pressure will not improve memory.' She also warned that the psychological cost of questioning staff could be serious when a powerful employer sat beside them. Chengqian agreed to wait outside during interviews. On the way to his car, they passed a small room where workers had preserved photographs of the mill's first families. Zhixia paused over a portrait of young women seated at machines. 'They made this place valuable long before it became your archive,' she said. He replied, 'I know.' She answered, 'Then show me in your decisions.' The remark stayed with him longer than any compliment from the previous night's banquet.

午餐時，承謙認為只要調查迅速有效，就能在投資人發現缺漏前完成。知夏拒絕把速度當成唯一標準。「害怕的員工可能需要時間回想日期，施壓不會讓記憶更準確。」她也提醒他，當強勢雇主坐在身邊詢問，員工承受的心理壓力可能很大。承謙同意訪談時在外面等。前往停車處時，兩人經過一間保留著工廠創始家庭照片的小室。知夏停在一張年輕女工坐在機器前的照片前。「在這裡成為你的檔案室很久以前，就是她們讓這個地方有價值。」她說。「我知道。」「那就讓你的決定證明。」這句話留在承謙心裡，比前晚宴會上的任何稱讚都久。

By evening, Zhixia had found one decisive detail. A page from the old mill register named three containers sent to the hotel, but the hotel's receipt showed four. The unexplained fourth container had arrived during the week before the launch. Chengqian called his logistics director and asked for a paper trail rather than reassurance. 'If my uncle ordered that container, will you let me inspect it?' Zhixia asked. He looked across the desk at her. 'I cannot promise to like what you find,' he said. 'I can promise not to hide it.' She put the signed agreement into her case. Trust, she thought, was not a sudden attraction between two people. It was the slower work of seeing whether a promise survived an inconvenient fact. Outside, the evening harbor lights came on one by one.

傍晚，知夏找出一項關鍵細節。紡織廠的舊登記簿記載送到飯店的是三箱，飯店收貨單卻寫四箱。無法解釋的第四箱是在發表會前一週抵達的。承謙打電話給物流主管，要求拿出書面紀錄，而不是口頭保證。「如果第四箱是你叔叔訂的，你會讓我檢查嗎？」知夏問。他隔著桌子望向她。「我不能保證喜歡妳找到的東西。」他說。「但我保證不隱藏。」她把簽妥的協議放進箱子。她想，信任不是兩個人突然互相吸引，而是看承諾能否撐過不方便的事實。窗外港燈逐盞亮起。
