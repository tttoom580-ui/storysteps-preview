The Rooftop Photograph

1999，總裁的心動不在合約裡 · 第 4 / 25 章 · 3000–4000 · 英中對照 · 校訂中

用互動版查詞、朗讀與收藏

A week after the suspended launch, Chengqian invited Zhixia to inspect a promotional exhibition planned for a hotel rooftop. She agreed only when her written assignment identified her as an independent reviewer. His public relations director, Ms. Feng, offered elaborate greetings, then asked whether Zhixia would accompany the president for a publicity photograph. 'My presence is not an attraction,' Zhixia replied. The director's smile froze. Chengqian stepped between them, not to speak for Zhixia but to change the subject. 'Show us the experimental display,' he said. Above the bay stood a magnificent glass arch, intended to project a satellite image of the evening horizon. The projection was beautiful. The smell rising from the machines was not. Zhixia opened her notebook before the host could explain it away.

發表會暫停一週後，承謙邀請知夏檢查飯店屋頂預定舉辦的宣傳展。她要求書面任務明列自己是獨立審查人員，才答應前往。公關主任馮女士熱情寒暄後，問知夏能否陪總裁拍一張宣傳照。「我的出席不是觀光景點。」知夏回答。主任的笑容凝住了。承謙走到兩人中間，不是替知夏發言，而是改變話題。「帶我們看看實驗展示。」他說。海灣上方架著一座壯麗的玻璃拱門，準備投影傍晚地平線的衛星影像。投影很美，機器散出的味道卻不好聞。知夏在主人開口解釋前就打開筆記本。

A mechanic named Bao explained that the display had been repaired twice. He showed her a diagram of the ventilation system, but his manual contained a different drawing. 'The newest draft arrived by fax yesterday,' he said. 'It asked us to loosen a seal to increase the scent.' Zhixia asked who had approved that change. No name appeared. She was concerned that the warm spray could carry unwanted particles. Although she could not identify a particular germ without testing, she knew the absence of evidence was not evidence of safety. Chengqian stopped the machine and asked Bao to preserve both versions of the instructions. When the director complained about the loss of recreation for the guests, Bao quietly said that no one had asked whether his crew wanted to breathe the spray all evening.

一名叫阿寶的機械技師說明，展示機已維修過兩次。他拿出通風系統圖，但操作手冊裡的圖卻不同。「最新版草稿昨天才傳真來。」他說。「上面要求我們鬆開一道密封圈，好增加香氣。」知夏問誰核准了改動，紙上沒有名字。她擔心加熱噴霧會攜帶不該出現的微粒。雖然不做檢測就無法確認特定病菌，她仍知道沒有證據不等於安全。承謙關閉機器，請阿寶保留兩個版本。主任抱怨賓客少了娛樂活動，阿寶卻低聲說，從沒有人問過工人願不願意整晚吸入噴霧。

A visiting trade diplomat arrived with representatives from an overseas distributor. Feng hurried forward, eager to protect the company's outstanding public image. She urged Chengqian to speak about future profits rather than damaged equipment. He hesitated. Zhixia watched that hesitation more closely than his face. A powerful man could command a room, she thought, but that did not make him brave. Chengqian finally told the visitors the exhibition had been halted for a technical review. He did not claim the product was harmless. One representative offered to help the company strengthen its records and asked for a schedule. The other looked offended and left. 'There goes half our market,' Feng whispered. 'No,' Zhixia said. 'There goes one buyer who did not want to hear the essential facts.'

一名外國貿易外交官陪同海外經銷商代表抵達。馮主任急忙上前，想保住公司出色的公眾形象。她催促承謙談未來獲利，不要談設備損壞。他猶豫了。知夏注意的不是他的表情，而是這份遲疑。她想，一個能命令人群的男人，不一定就勇敢。承謙最後告訴賓客，展示因技術審查而暫停。他沒有聲稱產品無害。一名代表表示願意協助公司強化紀錄，並要求提出時間表。另一人顯得不滿，轉身離開。「我們失去半個市場了。」馮主任低語。「不。」知夏說。「我們失去的是一個不願聽重要事實的買家。」

A photographer captured the moment Zhixia and Chengqian stood alone near the glass arch. The photograph made them look close, although a thick folder lay between their hands. When Zhixia noticed the camera, she stepped away. 'Do not use that picture without asking,' she told Feng. The director promised to review it. Downstairs, a frightened technician tried to flee the discussion because his supervisor had ordered the change. Chengqian asked him to stay, but Zhixia shook her head. 'Give him a choice. You can request testimony, not trap a person.' The technician agreed to send a written account the next day. He left with Bao and the original manual. Chengqian later thanked Zhixia for correcting him in public. She replied, 'Next time, correct yourself first.' His surprised laugh followed her into the lift.

攝影師拍下知夏與承謙單獨站在玻璃拱門旁的畫面。照片裡兩人似乎很親密，雖然彼此的手中間還隔著厚厚的文件夾。知夏發現鏡頭後立刻退開。「沒有徵求同意，別用這張照片。」她對馮主任說。主任承諾先審核。下樓後，一名害怕的技術員試圖逃離談話，因為改動是主管交代的。承謙要他留下，知夏卻搖頭。「讓他自己選擇。你可以請他作證，不能困住他。」技術員同意隔天寄來書面說明，便和阿寶帶著原版手冊離開。事後承謙謝謝知夏在眾人面前糾正他。她回答：「下次先自己糾正自己。」他驚訝的笑聲一路跟著她進了電梯。

That evening, a draft of Feng's press release arrived by fax. It praised the president's decisive leadership and described Zhixia as his 'special companion.' Zhixia took a pen and crossed out the phrase. She replaced it with her actual professional title, then wrote a note that every photograph needed explicit permission. Chengqian called her before midnight. 'I have rejected the release,' he said. 'You need not thank me.' 'I wasn't going to,' she answered, and heard him laugh again. There was a silence neither rushed to fill. 'The arch was magnificent,' he offered at last. 'Yes,' she said. 'It may become safe enough to use after a real test.' He paused. 'You never let me finish a pretty story.' 'Not when the ending is written before the facts.'

當晚，馮主任傳來新聞稿草稿。裡面稱讚總裁果斷領導，還把知夏寫成他的「特別女伴」。知夏拿筆劃掉這句，改成自己的正式職稱，並註明每張照片都需取得明確同意。午夜前，承謙打電話給她。「我已經退回新聞稿。」他說。「妳不必謝我。」「我本來就沒打算。」她回答，聽見他又笑了。兩人都沒有急著填滿沉默。「那座拱門真的很壯麗。」他最後說。「是啊。」知夏說。「經過真正測試後，也許能安全使用。」他停了停。「妳總不讓我講完漂亮故事。」「只要結局寫在事實之前，我就不讓。」
