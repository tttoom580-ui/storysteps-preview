董事長的紅色資料夾

The Chairman's Red Folder

1999，總裁的心動不在合約裡 · 第 11 / 25 章 · 3000–4000 · 英中對照 · 校訂中

Chairman Gu summoned Zhixia to his office on the top floor. The room contained a dusty telegraph machine, a clock that no longer ran, and a polished red folder. He said the group had grown through bold capitalism and that his nephew had become too cautious. 'A dominant company cannot let every worker negotiate its future,' he argued. 'If we allow doubt to circulate, doubt will breed panic.' Zhixia listened, then asked whether he intended to give her access to the fourth crate's transfer records. He smiled with practiced persuasion. 'Join our board, and you can see everything.' She understood the hidden cost. 'My inquiry would no longer be independent,' she said. He replied that independent people often became lonely. The consequence of refusing was uncertain, but she did not let his warning make her sign.

顧董事長把知夏叫到頂樓辦公室。房裡有一部積灰的電報機、一只停擺的時鐘，以及一個亮面的紅色資料夾。他說集團靠大膽的資本主義經營成長，姪子現在卻太謹慎。「主導市場的企業不能讓每個員工都來談判自己的未來。」他說。「若讓懷疑到處流傳，懷疑就會滋生恐慌。」知夏聽完，詢問能否查閱第四只貨箱的移交紀錄。他帶著練習過的說服笑容。「加入董事會，妳就能看見一切。」她明白隱藏代價。「那我的調查就不獨立了。」她說。他回答，獨立的人往往很孤單。拒絕會帶來什麼後果還不確定，但她沒有因這句警告而簽名。

On the desk lay a file marked with a warehouse code. Zhixia recognized it from the label beneath the linen, but she could not inspect it without permission. Chairman Gu noticed her gaze and closed the folder. 'The eventual result of this inquiry must protect the company,' he said. 'It must protect the truth,' she replied. A bell rang below them as runners prepared for the company's charity marathon. From the window she could see a line of solar lamps beside the course, a project meant to advertise the group's environmental ambitions. One fallen lamp had been left in the road. Zhixia pointed it out and asked the chairman to call the event staff. He seemed annoyed by the small interruption. 'Details matter when people are asked to trust us,' she said. At last he made the call.

桌上放著一份標有倉庫代碼的文件。知夏認出那串代碼曾出現在亞麻布下的標籤上，但沒有許可不能翻閱。顧董事長注意到她的目光，闔上資料夾。「這次調查的最終結果必須保護公司。」「必須保護真相。」她回答。樓下響起鐘聲，參加公司慈善馬拉松的跑者正在集合。窗外跑道旁排著太陽能燈，這是集團宣傳環境理念的專案。其中一盞倒在路面上。知夏指出危險，請董事長通知活動人員。他似乎不耐煩這種小打斷。「當我們要求人們信任，細節就重要。」她說。他終於打了電話。

Downstairs, Xinyi was organizing the volunteers. She had been called a rebel by directors because she questioned her uncle's plans. A runner showed her a damaged shoe and joked that the marathon might wreck his feet. Xinyi laughed and sent him to the first-aid table. When Zhixia joined them, a gentle breeze made the paper flags tickle their arms. 'You look as if you have walked into a storm,' Xinyi said. Zhixia explained the offer without revealing details she had no right to share. Xinyi warned that the chairman would try to expand his control over the investigation. 'He thinks every disagreement is an invasion of his territory,' she said. Zhixia answered that she would not turn a family conflict into public theater. The evidence still needed to stand on its own.

樓下，心怡正在安排志工。她因質疑叔叔的計畫，被董事們稱為叛逆者。一名跑者拿著磨損的鞋開玩笑，說馬拉松可能毀了他的腳。心怡笑著叫他去急救桌。知夏加入時，微風吹動紙旗，旗角輕搔她們的手臂。「妳看起來像走進風暴。」心怡說。知夏說明董事長的提議，卻沒透露自己無權分享的細節。心怡提醒她，董事長會試圖擴大對調查的控制。「他把每種不同意見都當成別人入侵他的領地。」她說。知夏回答，她不會把家族矛盾變成公開演出。證據仍必須能獨立成立。

Later, Chengqian learned about the meeting from his uncle, who accused Zhixia of refusing an extraordinary opportunity. Chengqian called her immediately. 'You should have told me he approached you,' he said. His voice was sharp. 'I had not finished deciding what I was allowed to share,' she replied. He fell silent. 'You are right. I assumed that because I hired you, I could demand every detail.' She appreciated the apology but did not rush to comfort him. They arranged to discuss the matter with both legal advisers present. He added that he would ask the board to separate its audit committee from the executive office. It was a small structural change, but perhaps more useful than another emotional promise. Zhixia put down the telephone feeling both tired and strangely hopeful.

稍晚，承謙從叔叔那裡得知會面。叔叔指責知夏拒絕了一個難得機會。承謙立刻打電話給她。「他找妳談，妳應該告訴我。」他語氣尖銳。「我還沒確認哪些細節有權分享。」她回答。他沉默了。「妳說得對。我以為既然雇用了妳，就能要求知道一切。」她接受道歉，卻沒有急著安慰他。兩人安排在各自法務顧問在場時討論。他補充，會要求董事會把稽核委員會與經營辦公室分開。這只是小小的結構改變，可能比另一個情緒化承諾更有用。知夏放下電話，既疲倦又莫名有點期待。

On Monday, an independent recorder compared the damaged seal with the approved factory pattern. A narrow scratch was the first clear indication that someone had opened the container after its official inspection. The finding did not identify the person, but it explained why the original documents and the bottles disagreed. When Zhixia brought the result to Chengqian, he resisted the urge to announce victory. 'I can see where this leads,' he said. 'You can see one possible road,' she corrected. He gave her a graceful nod, a gesture so formal it almost made her laugh. Then he handed her a photocopy of the red folder's index, obtained with the audit committee's permission. The missing code belonged to a subcontractor controlled by the chairman's old business partner. This was no longer merely a warehouse mistake.

星期一，獨立記錄員比對受損封條與核准工廠的標準式樣。一道窄刮痕是第一個清楚跡象，顯示有人在正式檢查後重新開過貨箱。發現還不能指認那個人，卻解釋了原始文件與瓶子為何對不上。知夏把結果帶給承謙時，他忍住了宣布勝利的衝動。「我看得出這會導向哪裡。」「你只看到一條可能的路。」她糾正。他優雅地點頭，正式得讓她差點笑出來。然後遞來一張紅色資料夾目錄的影本，這是得到稽核委員會許可後取得的。失蹤代碼屬於董事長舊商業夥伴掌控的分包商。事情已不只是倉庫出錯。
