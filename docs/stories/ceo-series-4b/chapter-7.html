The Training Nobody Wanted

1999，總裁的心動不在合約裡 · 第 7 / 25 章 · 3000–4000 · 英中對照 · 校訂中

用互動版查詞、朗讀與收藏

The company began a safety workshop in an old education center beside the municipal aquarium. Zhixia had designed it with two warehouse workers and an academic researcher from the local university. She refused to open with a lecture about obedience. Instead, each group received an unknown sample and a record of its journey. They had to identify the characteristic markings, compare the labels, and explain what information was missing. The chief accountant sat in the back, calculating how the training would affect the expansion budget. Chengqian came without an assistant. 'You could have sent a deputy,' Zhixia said. 'I have sent deputies for too many years,' he replied. His attendance surprised the workers, but it did not win their trust. That, she reminded him, would require consistency beyond a single morning.

公司在市立水族館旁的舊教育中心開始安全訓練。知夏與兩名倉儲員工及當地大學的學術研究人員共同設計課程。她沒有用服從命令的演講開場，而是讓每組拿到一份未知樣品及運送紀錄，辨認特徵標記、比對標籤，再說明缺少哪些資訊。總會計坐在後排，計算訓練如何影響擴廠預算。承謙沒帶助理就來了。「你可以派副手。」「我派副手派了太多年。」他說。他的出席讓工人意外，卻沒有立即贏得信任。知夏提醒他，這需要一個早晨以外的持續行動。

At the first table, Su Yuan demonstrated how to read a safety sheet. He spoke with a nervous rhythm until one senior supervisor began to annoy him with interruptions. Zhixia asked the supervisor to wait his turn and invited Su Yuan to finish. A worker who spoke three languages helped translate unfamiliar terms into fluent everyday speech. The room grew easier to breathe in. They compared a basin of clear water with a dyed sample and discussed why color alone could not establish purity. The researcher explained a simple point about chemistry: a familiar smell was not proof that a formula was unchanged. 'Our contribution is not a miracle,' she said. 'It is a method that anyone trained properly can repeat.' Several workers who had expected punishment began asking practical questions.

在第一桌，蘇沅示範閱讀安全資料表。他緊張地維持節奏，一名資深主管卻不斷插話，令他困擾。知夏請主管等輪到自己再說，讓蘇沅講完。一名會三種語言的員工協助把陌生術語翻成流暢的日常用語，房間裡的氣氛逐漸鬆了。他們比較一盆清水與染色樣品，討論為何單看顏色無法證明純淨。研究人員說明一個簡單化學原理：熟悉的味道不代表配方沒有改變。「我們的貢獻不是奇蹟。」她說。「而是一套受訓後任何人都能重做的方法。」幾名原以為會受責罵的工人開始提出實際問題。

During the break, Chengqian walked beneath the aquarium's blue glass roof. Zhixia found him studying a slow fish moving in the shade of a coral model. 'I thought leadership meant I should waken everyone before dawn,' he said. 'My father used to tell me that a successful man must defeat sleep before he could defeat a competitor.' Zhixia replied that exhaustion was not a prime business strategy. She had seen too many mistakes made by people afraid to rest. Chengqian admitted he was fortunate to have learned the lesson without someone being badly injured. Then he asked what had motivated her to create an independent practice. 'I wanted my name on work I could defend,' she said. Her answer carried no grand speech, only the quiet pride of someone who had chosen a difficult life deliberately.

休息時，承謙走到水族館的藍色玻璃屋頂下。知夏找到他時，他正看著一條魚在珊瑚模型的陰影裡緩緩游動。「我以為領導就是我應該在天還沒亮以前叫醒所有人。」他說。「父親以前說，成功的男人得先打敗睡眠，才能打敗競爭者。」知夏回答，疲勞不是最重要的商業策略。她見過太多人因害怕休息而犯錯。承謙承認，自己很幸運，還沒等到有人嚴重受傷，就學到了教訓。接著他問她為何創立獨立工作室。「我想在自己能夠辯護的工作上署自己的名字。」她說。回答沒有豪言壯語，只有一個刻意選擇艱難道路的人所擁有的安靜自尊。

In the afternoon, the company accountant presented a table showing that safety training had increased costs. He expected Zhixia to apologize. Instead, she asked him to include the cost of rework, returns, and idle equipment. He had omitted them because they belonged to different departments. 'Numbers separated from their relevant causes can make a costly decision appear cheap,' she said. Chengqian looked at the revised total and asked the accountant to present it to the board. Some managers called this an attempt to make the company look weak. Zhixia answered that hiding risk would not make anyone stronger. Workers began to strive for accuracy rather than speed, and Su Yuan received permission to lead the next session. His expression of disbelief turned into a shy smile.

下午，會計主管提出一張表，顯示安全訓練使成本增加。他預料知夏會道歉，她卻請他把返工、退貨和設備閒置的成本也列進去。原來因為這些費用分屬不同部門，他便漏掉了。「數字若脫離相關原因，就可能讓昂貴的決定看起來很便宜。」她說。承謙看過修正總額，請會計把數據呈給董事會。幾名主管認為這是在讓公司示弱，知夏回答，隱藏風險並不會讓誰更強。員工開始追求正確，而不只求速度。蘇沅也獲准主持下一堂課，他難以置信的表情慢慢變成羞澀笑容。

After the last table was cleared, Chengqian returned with a simple bouquet. 'For an outstanding lesson,' he said. Zhixia accepted the flowers, but reminded him that admiration was no substitute for a fair contract. 'I know. The lawyers finally finished it.' He handed her the document, with both exit conditions written clearly and equal notice periods for either party. She read it standing beside the empty classroom. There were no hidden ownership terms and no request for private companionship. 'This is acceptable,' she said, and signed. He looked happier than he had at his own product launch. They shook hands. Neither mistook the gesture for a promise of romance. But when he turned to leave, he glanced back, and she found herself watching the door for one heartbeat longer than she needed to.

最後一張桌子收好後，承謙帶著一小束花回來。「獻給出色的一堂課。」他說。知夏收下花，卻提醒他欣賞不能代替公平合約。「我知道。律師終於改好了。」他遞上文件，雙方終止條件寫得清楚，通知期間也相同。她站在空教室旁讀完，裡面沒有隱藏的所有權條款，也沒要求私人陪伴。「可以接受。」她說，簽了名。他看起來比自己的產品發表成功時還高興。兩人握手，沒有一方把這動作誤當成愛情承諾。但他轉身離開時回頭看了一眼，她發現自己望著門的時間，比必要的長了一次心跳。
