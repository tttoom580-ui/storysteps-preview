第四只貨箱

The Fourth Crate

1999，總裁的心動不在合約裡 · 第 3 / 25 章 · 3000–4000 · 英中對照 · 校訂中

用互動版查詞、朗讀與收藏

Zhixia reached the harbor before the first passenger ferry departed. A cold wind pushed her hair across her face, and she struggled to concentrate on the shipping manifest. Her assignment was simple in writing: trace the fourth crate from the mill to the hotel. In practice, the journey crossed three companies and two storage yards. A clerk offered to proceed only after Chengqian called him personally. Zhixia refused that shortcut. 'Tell me which records anyone with proper permission could see,' she said. The clerk looked embarrassed, then showed her the ordinary visitor procedure. The maximum number of crates on the delivery form was six, but the final page had been replaced. The paper was smoother, and its ink had not aged with the others. She photographed the difference without touching the original.

知夏在第一班渡輪離港前抵達碼頭。冷風把頭髮吹到臉上，她努力集中精神查看貨運清單。她的任務寫起來很簡單：追查第四只貨箱從紡織廠到飯店的路線。實際上，那段路經過三家公司和兩座儲貨場。職員表示，只有承謙親自打電話才肯繼續辦理。知夏拒絕這條捷徑。「請告訴我，有正式許可的一般人可以查看哪些紀錄。」她說。職員有些尷尬，才拿出普通訪客程序。送貨表格最多可列六箱，但最後一頁被換過。紙張更光滑，墨色也不像其他頁那樣舊。她拍下差異，沒有觸碰原件。

A young employee named Su Yuan met her at the waterfront café. He had worked in the sample warehouse for five months and feared losing his job. Over tea and a small raisin bun, he explained that the company had measured productivity by the percentage of boxes moved each hour. His manager insisted they work at an incredible pace even on days when labels were missing. 'I know the priority should be safety,' Su Yuan said, 'but saying that aloud felt like an insult to everyone who worked harder.' Zhixia answered that effort and accuracy were not enemies. She asked him to describe his usual day in context, not to accuse anyone. At home, he shared housework with his sister and studied for an intermediate accounting certificate. He had little time or energy for more unpaid tasks. He joked that he used to count every calorie, but now he counted minutes of sleep.

一名叫蘇沅的年輕員工在海邊咖啡館與她碰面。他在樣品倉庫工作五個月，擔心失去工作。兩人喝茶、吃葡萄乾小麵包時，他解釋公司按每小時搬運箱數的百分比計算生產效率。即使標籤遺失，主管也要求大家以難以置信的速度工作。「我知道安全應該優先。」蘇沅說。「但說出口，好像就在侮辱那些更努力工作的人。」知夏回答，努力與正確並不衝突。她請他根據平常的工作情境說明，而不是指控任何人。回家後，他和姊姊分擔家事，還準備中級會計證照，已經沒有多少時間和精力做更多無薪工作。他開玩笑說，過去計算每一卡路里，如今只計算能睡幾分鐘。

Chengqian arrived in a dark car and offered to take them across the inlet on a luxurious private boat. Zhixia pointed instead to the public ferry. 'Our missing container used this route. If you want to defend your logistics system, look at the journey your workers actually make.' He gave an impatient sigh but bought three tickets. The lifeguard stationed beside the pier warned that the tide would turn in an hour. During the crossing, Zhixia took photography notes of the loading ramps and checked the safety rails. Chengqian noticed a scar on her wrist and asked whether a past accident had inspired her work. 'It taught me to read the labels,' she replied. 'The inspiration came later, from people who were blamed for mistakes that began above their heads.' He did not ask her to tell more.

承謙坐深色轎車趕來，提議搭豪華私人船艇過海灣。知夏卻指向公共渡輪。「失蹤的貨箱走的就是這條路。你若要替物流系統辯護，就該看看工人真正走的路。」他不耐煩地嘆了口氣，仍買了三張船票。碼頭旁的救生員提醒一小時後會轉潮。航程中，知夏用攝影方式記下裝貨坡道，並檢查安全欄杆。承謙注意到她手腕上的疤痕，問她是否因舊傷而開始這份工作。「那件事教我看標籤。」她回答。「真正的啟發是後來看見有人被迫承擔上層決策造成的錯誤。」他沒有繼續追問。

Across the inlet, the warehouse supervisor showed them a small office filled with receipts. He admitted that the company's courier had delivered four crates, but one had been recorded as an empty display case. A display case did not normally require temperature control, while a fragrance ingredient did. Zhixia drew a line beneath the discrepancy. 'Who changed the category?' The supervisor named a director, Fu Manlin, and produced a signed instruction. Chengqian knew Manlin as the person who had built the company's overseas sales. He had called her brilliant for years. Now he felt the uncomfortable distance between admiration and proof. Zhixia suggested an independent review before anyone accused her. 'If we choose the wrong enemy,' she said, 'we may overlook the person who altered the bottle.' He watched her write every question down before offering an answer.

到了海灣另一端，倉庫主管帶他們進一間堆滿收據的小辦公室。他承認快遞送來四箱貨，其中一箱卻被登記為空展示箱。展示箱通常不需要控溫，香料原料卻需要。知夏在差異下畫線。「誰改了類別？」主管說出董事傅曼琳的名字，並拿出簽過名的指示。承謙知道曼琳是公司海外銷售的功臣，多年來都稱她出色。如今他不舒服地感受到，欣賞與證明之間有多遠。知夏提議先獨立審查，不要急著指控。「選錯敵人，就可能忽略真正改動瓶子的人。」她說。承謙看著她先把每個問題寫下，才肯給答案。

That night, the hotel called to ask whether it could reopen the fragrance counter for a charity dinner. Chengqian was tempted; the guests were influential and the hall was already paid for. He asked Zhixia how much risk the new bottles presented. 'We cannot calculate a useful percentage without knowing which batch they came from,' she said. 'A short delay is less costly than a false assurance.' She spoke firmly, without treating his question as foolish. He ordered the counter to remain closed and sent the manager a written explanation. When Zhixia returned to her modest apartment, she found a fax from his office: a corrected contract, preserving her ownership of her notes. Under his signature was one handwritten sentence: I WILL TRY TO LEARN THE RIGHT PACE. She read it twice, then filed it beside the shipping photographs rather than her personal letters.

當晚，飯店來電詢問慈善晚宴能否重新開放香水專櫃。承謙有些動搖；賓客很有影響力，場地費也已付清。他問知夏，新瓶子有多大風險。「不知道它們來自哪個批次，就算不出有意義的風險比例。」她說。「短暫延後比錯誤保證便宜。」她語氣堅定，卻不把他的疑問當成愚蠢。承謙下令專櫃繼續關閉，並向飯店經理提供書面說明。知夏回到樸素的公寓時，發現辦公室傳來一張傳真：修正後的合約保留了她的筆記所有權。他在簽名下親筆寫了一句：「我會試著學會正確的速度。」她讀了兩遍，仍把它歸到貨運照片旁，而不是私人信件裡。
