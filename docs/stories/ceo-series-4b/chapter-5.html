A Contract with an Empty Line

1999，總裁的心動不在合約裡 · 第 5 / 25 章 · 3000–4000 · 英中對照 · 校訂中

用互動版查詞、朗讀與收藏

The board met on Friday in a room overlooking the river. Chengqian's uncle, Chairman Gu, sat beneath a portrait of the company's founder. He called the suspended launch a serious threat to prosperity. A financial officer recommended abandoning the product line and dismissing the warehouse crew. Zhixia was invited to explain why the samples differed, but not to vote. She arrived with a portable measuring device, a stack of receipts, and a distinct proposal: keep the line closed, protect the records, and test the disputed batches before deciding who was responsible. Chairman Gu objected that the inquiry would enlarge the scandal. 'A larger investigation is not the same as enlargement of a scandal,' she replied. 'It can reduce the impact of a real mistake.' Her steady answer made a few directors lower their eyes.

星期五，董事會在俯瞰河流的會議室召開。顧董事長坐在公司創辦人的肖像下方，稱發表會停辦是對繁榮的重大威脅。財務主管建議放棄整條產品線並解雇倉儲人員。知夏受邀說明樣品差異，卻沒有投票權。她帶來一部可攜式量測裝置、一疊收據及一份明確提案：產品線繼續關閉，保存紀錄，先測試有爭議的批次，再判斷責任。顧董事長反對，說調查只會擴大醜聞。「調查範圍變大，不等於醜聞被放大。」她回答。「這樣反而能降低真實錯誤造成的影響。」她平穩的回答讓幾名董事垂下眼睛。

A young director quoted a popular idiom about a captain never leaving his ship. 'That is imaginative,' Zhixia said, 'but a perfume company is not a sinking canoe.' The remark broke the tension. Chengqian almost smiled, though his uncle looked offended. The marketing department had planned an orchestra and a river display for the next launch, with a canoe carrying flower arrangements to the stage. Zhixia asked them to abandon the scented smoke effect but offered to preserve the music. The adjustment would let guests enjoy the performance without breathing an untested mist. Even the finance officer admitted it was a favorable compromise. Chairman Gu warned that one compromise would invite a hundred objections. 'Then record them,' Chengqian said. 'People who raise an objection deserve an answer, not punishment.'

一名年輕董事引用一句流行成語，說船長永遠不會離開自己的船。「很有想像力。」知夏說。「但香水公司不是快沉的獨木舟。」這句話稍微化解緊張。承謙幾乎笑出聲，叔叔卻很不高興。行銷部原本計畫下次發表時安排管弦樂團與河上表演，讓一艘獨木舟載花飾抵達舞臺。知夏要求取消香霧效果，但建議保留音樂。這項調整能讓賓客欣賞表演，又不用吸入未經測試的霧氣。連財務主管都承認這是有利的折衷。顧董事長警告，答應一項就會引來一百項反對。「那就記錄下來。」承謙說。「提出反對的人值得得到回答，而不是懲罰。」

Outside, several employees had gathered to protest the proposed dismissals. Some held signs; others stood in silence. Su Yuan told Zhixia that his team received only a small meal allowance and had worked overtime during the launch preparation. He was worried that the board would seize their time sheets to blame them for missing labels. Zhixia helped the workers request copies under the company's ordinary record policy. She did not promise a victory. Her own anxiety rose as she watched security guards approach the entrance, but Chengqian came out and ordered the guards to remain at a distance. 'The workers have the right to speak,' he said. He then announced that no one would be dismissed before the inquiry concluded. The crowd did not cheer. They asked for the promise in writing.

會議室外，幾名員工聚集抗議裁員提案。有人舉牌，有人沉默站著。蘇沅告訴知夏，團隊只有少量餐費津貼，發表會準備期間卻經常加班。他擔心董事會扣走工時表，再把標籤遺失的責任推到他們身上。知夏協助員工依公司一般紀錄程序申請副本，沒有保證一定成功。看見保全靠近入口時，她自己也感到焦慮；承謙卻走出來，要保全保持距離。「員工有表達意見的權利。」他說。隨後宣布調查結束前不解雇任何人。人群沒有歡呼，而是要求書面承諾。

In the afternoon, Zhixia visited her mother. The cottage kitchen smelled of rice and orange peel, a welcome change from test bottles. Mother had saved an old magazine featuring the company's founder as a business idol. 'He seemed able to buy anything,' she said. Zhixia replied that buying buildings was easier than buying trust. They talked about the pressure of work and why Zhixia had left her previous laboratory. Her manager there had wanted her to change one syllable in a report, turning 'possible' into 'impossible.' It had been enough to make her resign. Mother worried that a powerful company might hurt her again. 'It might,' Zhixia admitted. 'That is why I need a contract that gives me a way to walk away.' Her mother nodded, then pushed a warm bowl toward her.

下午，知夏去看母親。小屋廚房裡有米飯與橘皮香氣，比試香瓶舒服得多。母親留著一本舊雜誌，封面把公司創辦人寫成商界偶像。「他看起來什麼都買得起。」母親說。知夏回答，買房子比買信任容易。兩人談起工作的壓力，以及知夏為何離開前一間實驗室。當時主管要求她把報告裡的一個音節改掉，讓「可能」變成「不可能」。那已足以使她辭職。母親擔心大企業會再次傷害她。「有可能。」知夏承認。「所以我需要一份能讓我自行離開的合約。」母親點頭，推來一碗熱飯。

At dusk, Chengqian brought the revised contract to her office himself. It included a fixed fee, a reasonable adjustment procedure, and a clause preventing the company from taking her independent notes. One line remained empty: the conditions under which either party could end the work. 'My lawyer has not agreed on the wording,' he said. Zhixia handed him a pencil. 'Then we do not sign tonight.' He studied the empty space, then put down his expensive pen. 'I used to believe that a firm signature could settle every argument.' 'A signature is a beginning,' she answered. 'It is not proof that the terms are fair.' He left without pressing her. By the window, she watched his car disappear into traffic and felt an unfamiliar warmth that had nothing to do with the scent samples.

黃昏時，承謙親自把修正合約帶到她的辦公室。裡面列了固定報酬、合理的調整程序，以及公司不能取走她獨立筆記的條款。有一行仍然空白：雙方在什麼條件下可以終止工作。「我的律師還沒同意措辭。」他說。知夏遞給他一支鉛筆。「那今晚就不簽。」他看著空白處，放下昂貴的鋼筆。「我以前相信一個堅定的簽名就能解決爭論。」「簽名是開始。」她回答。「不是條件公平的證明。」他沒有逼她，便離開了。她站在窗前看著他的車消失在車流裡，感到一股陌生的暖意，與試香樣品毫無關係。
