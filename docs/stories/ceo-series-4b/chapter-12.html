失蹤的帳冊

The Missing Ledger

1999，總裁的心動不在合約裡 · 第 12 / 25 章 · 3000–4000 · 英中對照 · 校訂中

用互動版查詞、朗讀與收藏

On Tuesday morning, the warehouse guard found a broken lock and an open filing cabinet. Someone had entered the sample archive after midnight. A bin of blank labels had been overturned, and the transfer ledger was gone. The guard called the police, but an executive immediately announced that a burglar must have stolen company secrets. Zhixia asked him to avoid a definite conclusion before the evidence was examined. Nothing else of obvious value had disappeared. A silver bracelet lay near the door, but it could belong to an employee, a visitor, or someone trying to mislead the inquiry. The police officer agreed to photograph it in place. Chengqian arrived as workers gathered in the corridor, frightened that the company would accuse them. He ordered that no one be questioned alone by a supervisor.

星期二早上，倉庫警衛發現門鎖被破壞，檔案櫃打開了。有人午夜後進入樣品檔案室。一只裝空白標籤的箱子翻倒，移交帳冊失蹤。一名主管立刻宣布一定是竊賊偷走了商業機密，知夏請他在調查前不要下確定結論。其他明顯有價值的東西沒有消失。門邊留著一只銀手鐲，但可能屬於員工、訪客，也可能是有人故意留下來誤導。警察同意先原地拍照。承謙抵達時，工人們害怕公司會怪罪自己，正聚在走廊。他下令主管不得單獨訊問任何員工。

The insurance agent arrived before lunch and demanded a list of damaged property. He described the incident as a tragic destruction of company heritage. Zhixia corrected him: the cabinets were damaged, but most archives remained intact. She asked the staff to separate confirmed losses from assumptions. An older clerk pointed to a durable cardboard box that had protected duplicate receipts through years of humidity. The copies included the missing ledger's final three pages. They had been stored under a label marked 'inflation records,' because the previous accountant had once used the same shelf for price histories. A careless thief might never have looked there. Chengqian praised the clerk, but she waved away his compliment. 'I am faithful to the records,' she said, 'not to any chairman.'

午餐前，保險代理人趕來，要求列出受損財產。他把事件形容為公司遺產遭受悲劇性毀滅。知夏糾正他：櫃子確實受損，但大部分檔案完好。她請員工把確認的損失和猜測分開。一名老職員指著一只耐用的紙箱，多年來它在潮濕環境中保住了備份收據。副本包含失蹤帳冊最後三頁，因為前任會計曾把同一架子拿來放物價膨脹紀錄，所以箱上也貼著那個標籤。粗心的竊賊可能根本不會找那裡。承謙稱讚她，她卻揮手拒絕。「我忠於紀錄。」她說。「不是忠於哪一個董事長。」

Among the papers, one page named a small supplier collective from the eastern hills. It purchased floral oils from several communities, including a tribal cooperative that maintained its own production and payment records. The company's purchasing agent had described the cooperative's goods as 'unreliable' without visiting it. Zhixia refused to accept the comment as fact. 'Different communities deserve the same fair procedure,' she said. Xinyi volunteered to contact the cooperative directly, asking permission before sharing its files. A representative explained that they had never shipped to the unauthorized subcontractor. Someone had borrowed the cooperative's name to make an ordinary invoice look trustworthy. A new explanation began to emerge. The discovery could become a significant part of the case, but Zhixia warned the team not to turn the supplier into another convenient suspect.

紙張裡有一頁提到東部丘陵的供應合作組織。它向數個社區收購花油，其中包含由原住民族成員組成、保有獨立生產及付款紀錄的合作社。公司的採購代理人沒有去訪查，就把合作社的貨寫成「不可靠」。知夏不接受這句評語當事實。「不同社區都應受到同樣公平的程序對待。」她說。心怡自願直接聯繫合作社，在分享其文件前先徵得同意。代表解釋，他們根本沒有向那家未核准分包商出貨。有人借用合作社的名字，讓普通發票看起來可靠。新的解釋開始浮現。這可能成為案件的重要部分，知夏卻提醒團隊，不能再把供應商當成方便懷疑的人。

In the afternoon, journalists gathered outside the building. Some wanted a scandal; others wanted to know if the product was safe. Chengqian's public fame made every answer a headline. He asked Zhixia to speak beside him, but she declined until the audit committee had finished its review. 'I will not occupy the space meant for witnesses,' she said. He gave the statement alone, limiting it to facts the company could verify. When one reporter asked whether he would pursue charges against the warehouse staff, he answered that accusations required evidence. He also promised to commit funds to independent record storage. Zhixia watched from behind a glass door. She knew he was trying, but she also knew a public promise could become another kind of pressure unless the procedure was actually funded and followed.

下午，記者聚集在大樓外。有人想要醜聞，有人只想知道產品是否安全。承謙的知名度讓每句話都可能成為標題。他請知夏站在身邊發言，她卻拒絕，說要等稽核委員會完成審查。「我不會占據原本應留給證人的位置。」她說。他獨自發表聲明，只說公司能確認的事實。當記者問他會不會控告倉儲員工，他回答指控需要證據。他也承諾投入經費建立獨立的紀錄保管制度。知夏從玻璃門後看著。她知道他在努力，也知道公開承諾若沒有真正編列預算和執行程序，仍可能變成另一種壓力。

That night, Chengqian met Zhixia at the company's old records room. He had found a photograph of his father standing with the first workers decades earlier. 'He built everything through hardship,' Chengqian said. 'I keep thinking that if I fail, the whole era will be judged a failure.' Zhixia asked whether that burden belonged to him alone. He took a long breath. 'Probably not.' She studied the photograph, then noticed a figure at the edge: Chairman Gu's business partner holding the same red folder she had seen upstairs. A date written on its back matched the first unauthorized supplier contract. 'This may not prove who took the ledger,' she said, 'but it tells us when the relationship began.' Chengqian looked at the old picture as if it had finally begun to speak.

當晚，承謙與知夏在公司的舊檔案室見面。他找到一張幾十年前父親與第一批工人站在一起的照片。「他是靠艱難的日子建立一切。」承謙說。「我總覺得，如果我失敗，整個時代都會被說成失敗。」知夏問，這個負擔是否只屬於他。他深吸一口氣。「大概不是。」她研究照片，注意到邊緣有個人：顧董事長的商業夥伴，手裡拿著她在頂樓見過的紅色資料夾。照片背後的日期與第一份未核准分包合約相符。「這還不能證明誰偷走帳冊。」她說。「但告訴我們這段關係何時開始。」承謙望著老照片，彷彿它終於開口說話。
