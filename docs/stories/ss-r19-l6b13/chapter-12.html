The Dark Districts March

The room smelled of dust, hot metal, and rain carried in on people's coats. Someone had left an ordinary object on the central table, yet no one wanted to touch it. Residents from twenty dark districts carry mirrors to the public square without attacking company workers. Nara saw that the object mattered less for what it was than for the decision it proved had been made. There would be no honest way back to ignorance.

室內有灰塵、熱金屬與人們外套帶進的雨味。有人把平凡物件放在中央桌上，卻沒有人願意碰它。二十個黑暗街區的居民帶著鏡子走上廣場，沒有攻擊公司員工。奈拉明白重要的不是物件本身，而是它證明了何種決定已經發生。如今已不可能誠實地假裝無知。

Above the rooftops, the mirror array rotated with silent precision. Nara could read its position from a shadow on a water tank. Sunlight was physical, not a promise on a billing screen. Eli wondered aloud how many children had learned to associate dawn with the sound of a payment warning.

屋頂上方，反射鏡陣無聲而精準地旋轉。奈拉可以從水塔陰影讀出它的位置。陽光是真實的物理現象，不是帳單螢幕上的承諾。伊萊低聲問，有多少孩子已把黎明和付款警告的聲音連在一起。

The evidence did not arrive as a confession. It came through a date that did not fit, a measurement that had been repeated, and a witness who knew exactly what the official account had left out. Nara asked Eli not to describe suspicion as proof. If the accusation was wrong, an innocent person would suffer. If it was right, careless language could still give the guilty a way to escape.

證據不是以自白形式出現，而是一個對不上的日期、一項重複測量的結果，以及一位知道官方說法遺漏何事的證人。奈拉請伊萊不要把懷疑說成證明。若指控錯了，無辜者會受害；若指控正確，草率措辭仍可能讓有罪者脫身。

The overlooked details in the witness file made the question harder, not easier: A laboratory attendant identified the storage shelves where the children's original treatment registers had been kept. The cooking class showed how batter could stain a cotton apron. The old census counted hundreds of children absent from the city's approved founding population. A retired chemist explained how old ink could be dated without damaging the children's records. Birds began to chirp outside the hall as the budget workshop ended. Taken alone, no detail settled the case; together they showed why a public answer mattered.

證人檔案裡被忽視的細節使問題更加複雜，而不是更簡單： 一名實驗室服務員指出，兒童原始治療名冊存放在哪些架上。 烹飪課示範麵糊如何弄髒棉圍裙。 舊人口普查列出數百名不在官方建城人口名單上的兒童。 退休化學家解釋如何不損傷兒童紀錄，就能測定舊墨水年代。 預算工作坊結束時，鳥兒開始在禮堂外啁啾。 單獨看來沒有一項細節能結案，合在一起卻說明了公開答覆為何重要。

A small act of honesty unsettled the room more than a shouted threat could have done. Their peaceful light display reveals how much sunlight still reaches the city from the edges of the array. Nara watched a witness decide not to change a difficult detail merely because it complicated the preferred story. Eli recorded the correction openly. Their inquiry would be slower now, but its shape belonged less to the powerful and more to the people who had lived through the event.

一個小小的誠實舉動，帶來比喊叫威脅更強的震動。和平反光展示揭露鏡陣邊緣仍有大量陽光抵達城市。奈拉看見證人沒有因為細節讓主流說法變複雜就改口。伊萊公開記錄修正。調查會因此變慢，卻能較少受權貴支配，也更貼近親歷者。

A second bundle of observations brought the consequences closer to ordinary life: The clinic agreed to dispense basic medical supplies during the night trial but kept emergency medication under professional supervision. The technician let out a groan when he discovered that the latest digital copy lacked entire pages. The missing laboratory files were only the visible tip of an iceberg of altered civic records. The undamaged consent forms proved invaluable for distinguishing voluntary treatment from compulsory correction. Eli had funded a lavish celebration of safe sleep while cutting staff assigned to monitor side effects. Nara asked the clerk to preserve the original wording rather than improve the story.

第二疊觀察紀錄使後果貼近普通人的生活： 第二只時鐘又顯示矛盾時間戳時，疲倦的工程師輕輕呻吟。 技師發現最新數位副本缺了整頁時，發出一聲呻吟。 失蹤的實驗室檔案，只是整批遭修改市政紀錄露出的冰山一角。 完好的同意書對區分自願治療與強制修正極其珍貴。 言秋砸錢舉辦奢華安眠慶典，卻削減追蹤副作用的人力。 奈拉要求書記保存原本措辭，而不是把故事修飾得更好聽。

Nara had expected the hardest part to be exposing the secret. Instead, it was choosing what to do after a partial truth became public. The people who had been ignored did not all want the same remedy. Eli proposed that each affected group could submit a written objection and receive a specific answer. The method would take time, but it recognized them as participants rather than scenery.

奈拉原以為最難的是揭露祕密，後來才發現，部分真相公開後如何行動才是難題。曾被忽略的人並不全想要相同補救。伊萊提議每個受影響群體都能提出書面異議並取得具體答覆。方法需要時間，但終於把他們當成參與者，而不是背景。

The team kept the concrete observations alongside the disputed theory: The original clinic logo showed an open hand, unlike the lifespan exchange's later symbol of a watchful eye. A loudspeaker ordered visitors to leave, but the judge's preservation warrant kept the examination team inside. New martial security regulations threatened to transfer the medical archive to an armed enforcement office. No confirmed outbreak preceded the children's transfers, despite what the founding textbooks claimed. A preserved oyster shell in Nara's childhood box suggested a visit to the coast before the city sealed its gates. Eli marked what could be verified and left the rest open to challenge.

團隊把具體觀察與有爭議的理論並列保存： 原診所標誌是一隻張開的手，不同於壽命交易所後來監視的眼睛符號。 擴音器命令訪客離開，但法官的保全令使檢查小組得以留下。 新的軍事化安全規章威脅要把醫療檔案移交武裝執法辦公室。 儘管建城教科書如此宣稱，兒童轉移之前沒有任何確診疫情爆發。 梅童年盒子裡保存的牡蠣殼，顯示城門封鎖前她可能去過海岸。 伊萊標記能查證的部分，其餘保留給人質疑。

Above the rooftops, the mirror array rotated with silent precision. Nara could read its position from a shadow on a water tank. Sunlight was physical, not a promise on a billing screen. Eli wondered aloud how many children had learned to associate dawn with the sound of a payment warning.

屋頂上方，反射鏡陣無聲而精準地旋轉。奈拉可以從水塔陰影讀出它的位置。陽光是真實的物理現象，不是帳單螢幕上的承諾。伊萊低聲問，有多少孩子已把黎明和付款警告的聲音連在一起。

The record continued with small facts that would be lost in a confident speech: The early engineers never intended the technology to populate every home with compulsory monitors. The fallen tree was a setback, not a reason to abandon the service. Some residents remained skeptical of the proposed licensed detour. Visitors could subscribe to updates without buying the collection. The charity promised not to withhold apprentice wages during an audit. Nothing in these records excused the people who had made the dangerous decision.

紀錄接著列出那些在自信演說中容易消失的小事實： 早期工程師從未打算讓強制監測環遍布每戶家庭。 倒木是挫折，不是放棄服務的理由。 部分居民仍懷疑提議中的合格繞道路線。 來賓不必購買系列，也能訂閱更新資訊。 慈善機構承諾稽核期間不扣留學徒薪水。 紀錄中沒有任何一項能替做出危險決定的人開脫。

When the lamps were lowered, Nara returned to an unpaid sunlight meter. It was neither proof of innocence nor a shortcut to justice. It was a reminder that the next decision would be witnessed by people who no longer trusted easy promises. Eli remained at the door while the final record was sealed. Outside, the ring-shadow city waited for news that had to be more precise than a victory speech.

燈光調暗時，奈拉再次看向一只欠費的陽光計量器。它既不是清白證明，也不是通往正義的捷徑，而是在提醒眾人：下一個決定會由不再相信輕易承諾的人見證。伊萊守在門邊，最後一份紀錄被封存。外頭的環影城等待的消息，必須比勝利演說更精確。
