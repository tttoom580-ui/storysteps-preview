A messenger arrived before the bells had finished ringing. The news sounded impossible only because everyone had been trained not to question the institution that delivered it. Director Solven offers each household seven free minutes of sun per day, calling it a generous safety measure. Nara asked the messenger to repeat the part that had been omitted from the written notice. It was there, in the omission, that the day's real story began.

鐘聲尚未結束，信使已經抵達。消息之所以聽來不可能，只因每個人都習慣不質疑傳遞消息的制度。索爾文總監宣布每戶每日免費七分鐘日照，稱之為慷慨的安全措施。奈拉請信使重述書面公告遺漏的部分。這一天真正的故事，就從那處缺漏開始。

Orbital mirrors could redirect sunlight, never create it. Removing all shade in a heat wave could be as dangerous as withholding light in winter, so a safe solution required transparent control rather than a reckless shutdown.

軌道反射鏡只能改變陽光方向，不能創造陽光。熱浪時移除所有遮光可能和冬天斷光一樣危險，所以安全方案必須是透明控制，而非魯莽停機。

Above the rooftops, the mirror array rotated with silent precision. Nara could read its position from a shadow on a water tank. Sunlight was physical, not a promise on a billing screen. Eli wondered aloud how many children had learned to associate dawn with the sound of a payment warning.

屋頂上方，反射鏡陣無聲而精準地旋轉。奈拉可以從水塔陰影讀出它的位置。陽光是真實的物理現象，不是帳單螢幕上的承諾。伊萊低聲問，有多少孩子已把黎明和付款警告的聲音連在一起。

The evidence did not arrive as a confession. It came through a date that did not fit, a measurement that had been repeated, and a witness who knew exactly what the official account had left out. Nara asked Eli not to describe suspicion as proof. If the accusation was wrong, an innocent person would suffer. If it was right, careless language could still give the guilty a way to escape.

證據不是以自白形式出現，而是一個對不上的日期、一項重複測量的結果，以及一位知道官方說法遺漏何事的證人。奈拉請伊萊不要把懷疑說成證明。若指控錯了，無辜者會受害；若指控正確，草率措辭仍可能讓有罪者脫身。

The overlooked details in the witness file made the question harder, not easier: Nara promised to abide by the clinic's consent rules even while helping Nara preserve the invoice. The director would not affirm the official opening date without seeing the original ledger. An alligator mascot on the old lesson chart reminded children to keep their distance. An old recording played a low bass note that made Jun remember the arrival of a train. The children's colloquial word for the forbidden street survived in a playground counting rhyme. Eli marked what could be verified and left the rest open to challenge.

證人檔案裡被忽視的細節使問題更加複雜，而不是更簡單： 奈拉承諾遵守診所的同意規則，同時協助奈拉保存發票。 主任沒看過原始帳冊，不願確認官方啟用日期。 舊課程圖表上的鱷魚吉祥物提醒孩子保持距離。 舊錄音響起低沉的低音，讓小鈞想起列車進站。 孩子口語中稱呼禁忌街道的詞，仍留在遊戲數數歌裡。 伊萊標記能查證的部分，其餘保留給人質疑。

A small act of honesty unsettled the room more than a shouted threat could have done. Nara measures a school garden that needs longer light cycles to keep its food plants alive. Nara watched a witness decide not to change a difficult detail merely because it complicated the preferred story. Eli recorded the correction openly. Their inquiry would be slower now, but its shape belonged less to the powerful and more to the people who had lived through the event.

一個小小的誠實舉動，帶來比喊叫威脅更強的震動。奈拉測出學校菜園需要更長光週期才能讓作物活下去。奈拉看見證人沒有因為細節讓主流說法變複雜就改口。伊萊公開記錄修正。調查會因此變慢，卻能較少受權貴支配，也更貼近親歷者。

A second bundle of observations brought the consequences closer to ordinary life: Treating forgotten dreams as commonplace noise was part of the clinic's training manual. The hospital's exemption would expire at midnight, giving them only hours to examine the records. Ivo discovered that an old fuse had protected the basement archive from the memory relay network. Nara offered quiet hospitality to worried parents, never asking for their children's forbidden dream details. The authorities called the visions infectious, although the clinic could provide no evidence of transmission. Nothing in these records excused the people who had made the dangerous decision.

第二疊觀察紀錄使後果貼近普通人的生活： 把被遺忘的夢當成尋常雜訊，是診所訓練手冊的一部分。 醫院的特別許可將在午夜到期，留給他們查閱紀錄的時間只剩數小時。 伊沃發現一枚舊保險絲，讓地下檔案室沒有接上記憶中繼網路。 奈拉溫和接待焦慮的家長，從不逼問孩子的禁忌夢境內容。 當局稱那些幻象有傳染性，但診所拿不出任何傳播證據。 紀錄中沒有任何一項能替做出危險決定的人開脫。

No one in the room was permitted to declare that a good intention canceled a harmful action. Nara insisted on recording the error, identifying who had authorized it, and protecting those affected before assigning wider blame. Eli drew a simple boundary around what they could do tonight. The line looked disappointingly modest, yet it was the first promise that did not require another person to surrender a right.

室內沒有人能宣稱善意可以抵銷傷害。奈拉堅持記錄錯誤、確認授權者，並在追究更大責任前保護受害者。伊萊畫出今晚能做到的清楚界線。這條線看似微不足道，卻是第一個不要求別人放棄權利的承諾。

The team kept the concrete observations alongside the disputed theory: A liter of cleaning fluid had spilled over the paper archive, obscuring several names but not the stamps. A novice technician had left the storage sensor disconnected, accidentally preserving the original poster. Nara considered it rash to accuse the clinic staff before she understood who controlled their systems. The old invoice and the official date could not reconcile without an independent explanation. The shabby ledger cabinet looked worthless, which was probably why nobody had removed it. Taken alone, no detail settled the case; together they showed why a public answer mattered.

團隊把具體觀察與有爭議的理論並列保存： 一公升清潔液灑在紙本檔案上，模糊了幾個姓名，印章卻還清楚。 一名新手技師曾讓儲藏室感測器保持斷線，意外保存了原始海報。 奈拉認為尚未查清誰控制系統，就指責診所員工太過魯莽。 沒有獨立說明，舊發票與官方日期便無法相互調和。 破舊的帳冊櫃看起來毫無價值，可能因此沒人搬走。 單獨看來沒有一項細節能結案，合在一起卻說明了公開答覆為何重要。

Above the rooftops, the mirror array rotated with silent precision. Nara could read its position from a shadow on a water tank. Sunlight was physical, not a promise on a billing screen. Eli wondered aloud how many children had learned to associate dawn with the sound of a payment warning.

屋頂上方，反射鏡陣無聲而精準地旋轉。奈拉可以從水塔陰影讀出它的位置。陽光是真實的物理現象，不是帳單螢幕上的承諾。伊萊低聲問，有多少孩子已把黎明和付款警告的聲音連在一起。

The record continued with small facts that would be lost in a confident speech: A borrowed spotlight made the presentation easier to see but obscured an exit sign. A volunteer brought ginger syrup for workers who had spent the afternoon in the cold. The handrail began to tilt when the tester leaned against its outer edge. The artificial twilight outside the clinic concealed the fact that no real sun had reached the street. An old label marked whiskey lay in the storeroom beside the railway invoices, dated before the city's founding. Nara asked the clerk to preserve the original wording rather than improve the story.

紀錄接著列出那些在自信演說中容易消失的小事實： 借來的聚光燈讓簡報更清楚，卻遮住了逃生標誌。 志工帶來薑糖漿，慰勞在寒風中工作一下午的人。 測試者靠在外側時，扶手開始傾斜。 診所外的人造暮光掩蓋了真實太陽根本照不到街道的事實。 一張印著威士忌的舊標籤躺在儲藏室鐵路發票旁，日期早於官方建城日。 奈拉要求書記保存原本措辭，而不是把故事修飾得更好聽。

On the way out, Nara passed people waiting for a result that would affect their meals, their homes, or their ability to say no. None of them had the luxury of treating the dispute as an intellectual puzzle. Eli paused to answer a child's practical question before they left. The answer was incomplete, but unlike the old official notice, it admitted the uncertainty out loud.

離開時，奈拉經過等待結果的人群，這項結果將影響他們的餐食、家園或說不的能力。他們不能奢侈地把爭論當成智力謎題。伊萊停下回答一名孩子實際的問題。答案並不完整，卻與舊公告不同：它坦白承認不確定之處。
