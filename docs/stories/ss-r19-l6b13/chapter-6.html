地下室的花園

A Garden in a Basement

從今天起，太陽需要每月訂閱 · 第 6 / 25 章 · 5000–6000 · 英中對照 · 校訂中

The room smelled of dust, hot metal, and rain carried in on people's coats. Someone had left an ordinary object on the central table, yet no one wanted to touch it. Families cultivate vegetables using reclaimed heat and reflective foil in an abandoned transit station. Nara saw that the object mattered less for what it was than for the decision it proved had been made. There would be no honest way back to ignorance.

室內有灰塵、熱金屬與人們外套帶進的雨味。有人把平凡物件放在中央桌上，卻沒有人願意碰它。家庭在廢棄車站用回收熱能與反光箔種蔬菜。奈拉明白重要的不是物件本身，而是它證明了何種決定已經發生。如今已不可能誠實地假裝無知。

Above the rooftops, the mirror array rotated with silent precision. Nara could read its position from a shadow on a water tank. Sunlight was physical, not a promise on a billing screen. Eli wondered aloud how many children had learned to associate dawn with the sound of a payment warning.

屋頂上方，反射鏡陣無聲而精準地旋轉。奈拉可以從水塔陰影讀出它的位置。陽光是真實的物理現象，不是帳單螢幕上的承諾。伊萊低聲問，有多少孩子已把黎明和付款警告的聲音連在一起。

A witness asked whether the inquiry would make tomorrow safer. Nara could not promise that. The truth could establish responsibility, but it would not repair a broken machine, feed a hungry child, or return lost time on its own. Eli suggested a temporary protection that could be checked after sunrise. They wrote down its limits in plain language and invited the people affected to challenge them.

一位證人問調查是否能讓明天更安全，奈拉無法保證。真相能確立責任，卻不會自行修好機器、餵飽孩子或歸還失去的時間。伊萊提出一項日出後可以檢驗的暫時保護措施。他們用白話寫清限制，邀請受影響的人提出異議。

The overlooked details in the witness file made the question harder, not easier: The witness tapes survived because Eli's mother had stored them in airtight metal cylinders. The engineering team could not attain a safe restoration protocol without testing volunteers' reactions first. An old congresswoman had signed the emergency evacuation review and disappeared from later records. One sealed ration crate still contained curry packets labeled for the train station's night workers. Nara had begun to despise the lie, but she refused to treat every frightened employee as an enemy. Eli marked what could be verified and left the rest open to challenge.

證人檔案裡被忽視的細節使問題更加複雜，而不是更簡單： 證人錄音帶能留存，是因為言秋母親把它們裝在氣密金屬筒裡。 工程團隊若不先測試自願者反應，就無法達成安全的記憶恢復程序。 一名前任女性議員簽過緊急撤離審查，卻從後來紀錄中消失。 一箱密封口糧裡仍有咖哩包，標示是給車站夜班工人的。 奈拉開始厭惡謊言，卻拒絕把每位害怕的員工都視為敵人。 伊萊標記能查證的部分，其餘保留給人質疑。

For once the numbers did not merely decorate a report; they contradicted the order that had been issued in their name. Their harvest proves that company-controlled daylight is not the only source of survival. Eli asked whether a mistake could explain the result. Nara agreed to test that possibility too. They checked an independent source, marked every assumption, and made copies before the originals could be transferred to an office beyond public reach.

這一次數字不只是報告裝飾，而是直接反駁以數字名義發布的命令。他們的收成證明企業控制的日照不是生存的唯一來源。伊萊詢問是否可能只是出錯，奈拉也同意檢查這種可能。他們核對獨立來源、標記每項假設，在原件被移往公眾無法接觸的辦公室前留下副本。

A second bundle of observations brought the consequences closer to ordinary life: The archived sky schedule described an eclipse that public lessons insisted had never occurred. Nari checked whether the old ration packs were still edible before placing them with the archive. Ivo's esteem for Nara grew when she chose to protect the witness names instead of seeking fame. A painted evergreen tree marked the maintenance tunnel that led away from the live network. A child asked whether a fish fin worked like the flap on the book box. Nothing in these records excused the people who had made the dangerous decision.

第二疊觀察紀錄使後果貼近普通人的生活： 封存的天空時刻表記載了一次日食，公共課程卻堅稱那從未發生。 娜莉把舊口糧一起封存前，先確認它們是否仍可食用。 奈拉選擇保護證人姓名而非追求名聲，讓伊沃對她更為尊重。 一棵彩繪常綠樹標記著通往現行網路之外的維修隧道。 孩子問魚鰭是否像書箱的活門那樣運作。 紀錄中沒有任何一項能替做出危險決定的人開脫。

No one in the room was permitted to declare that a good intention canceled a harmful action. Nara insisted on recording the error, identifying who had authorized it, and protecting those affected before assigning wider blame. Eli drew a simple boundary around what they could do tonight. The line looked disappointingly modest, yet it was the first promise that did not require another person to surrender a right.

室內沒有人能宣稱善意可以抵銷傷害。奈拉堅持記錄錯誤、確認授權者，並在追究更大責任前保護受害者。伊萊畫出今晚能做到的清楚界線。這條線看似微不足道，卻是第一個不要求別人放棄權利的承諾。

The team kept the concrete observations alongside the disputed theory: A flake of old paint fell from the ceiling when the evacuation tape began to play. The inventory distinguished emergency supplies from merchandise once sold to the missing district. The monotony of the relay's steady hum made its sudden silence in the chamber feel frightening. Keeping paper travel diaries had once been a common pastime before the Authority discouraged writing. The original design required redundancy: two independent archives in case one system failed or was compromised. Taken alone, no detail settled the case; together they showed why a public answer mattered.

團隊把具體觀察與有爭議的理論並列保存： 撤離錄音帶開始播放時，一片老油漆從天花板剝落。 清冊將緊急物資與昔日出售給失蹤行政區的商品分開。 中繼器單調不變的嗡鳴，讓維修室裡突如其來的寂靜格外可怕。 在管理局不鼓勵書寫以前，紙本旅行日記曾是常見消遣。 原始設計要求備援機制：兩套獨立檔案，防止其中一套故障或遭滲透。 單獨看來沒有一項細節能結案，合在一起卻說明了公開答覆為何重要。

Above the rooftops, the mirror array rotated with silent precision. Nara could read its position from a shadow on a water tank. Sunlight was physical, not a promise on a billing screen. Eli wondered aloud how many children had learned to associate dawn with the sound of a payment warning.

屋頂上方，反射鏡陣無聲而精準地旋轉。奈拉可以從水塔陰影讀出它的位置。陽光是真實的物理現象，不是帳單螢幕上的承諾。伊萊低聲問，有多少孩子已把黎明和付款警告的聲音連在一起。

The record continued with small facts that would be lost in a confident speech: Eli wanted to roam the lower tunnels, but the court order authorized only one chamber. Vale had dismissed the engineers' warnings as rubbish without ever reading the measurements. An official poster promised to slay the 'monster of dreams,' turning ordinary sleep into an imagined enemy. A sly alteration in the tape label hid the passengers' true destination under a routine maintenance code. Cold vapor rose from the old cooling pipes as the preservation team opened the storage cabinet. Nara asked the clerk to preserve the original wording rather than improve the story.

紀錄接著列出那些在自信演說中容易消失的小事實： 言秋想在下層隧道四處探查，但法院命令只允許進入一間維修室。 維爾未曾讀過測量數據，就把工程師警告斥為垃圾話。 官方海報宣稱要殺死「夢的怪物」，把普通睡眠塑造成虛構敵人。 錄音帶標籤上的狡猾修改，把乘客真正目的地藏在例行維護代碼下。 保全小組打開儲存櫃時，舊冷卻管冒出冰冷水霧。 奈拉要求書記保存原本措辭，而不是把故事修飾得更好聽。

On the way out, Nara passed people waiting for a result that would affect their meals, their homes, or their ability to say no. None of them had the luxury of treating the dispute as an intellectual puzzle. Eli paused to answer a child's practical question before they left. The answer was incomplete, but unlike the old official notice, it admitted the uncertainty out loud.

離開時，奈拉經過等待結果的人群，這項結果將影響他們的餐食、家園或說不的能力。他們不能奢侈地把爭論當成智力謎題。伊萊停下回答一名孩子實際的問題。答案並不完整，卻與舊公告不同：它坦白承認不確定之處。
