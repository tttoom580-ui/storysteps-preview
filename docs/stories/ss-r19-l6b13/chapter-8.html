A messenger arrived before the bells had finished ringing. The news sounded impossible only because everyone had been trained not to question the institution that delivered it. Schoolchildren build safe rooftop reflectors to share a small patch of morning light. Nara asked the messenger to repeat the part that had been omitted from the written notice. It was there, in the omission, that the day's real story began.

鐘聲尚未結束，信使已經抵達。消息之所以聽來不可能，只因每個人都習慣不質疑傳遞消息的制度。學童在屋頂安全架設反光板，分享一小塊晨光。奈拉請信使重述書面公告遺漏的部分。這一天真正的故事，就從那處缺漏開始。

Above the rooftops, the mirror array rotated with silent precision. Nara could read its position from a shadow on a water tank. Sunlight was physical, not a promise on a billing screen. Eli wondered aloud how many children had learned to associate dawn with the sound of a payment warning.

屋頂上方，反射鏡陣無聲而精準地旋轉。奈拉可以從水塔陰影讀出它的位置。陽光是真實的物理現象，不是帳單螢幕上的承諾。伊萊低聲問，有多少孩子已把黎明和付款警告的聲音連在一起。

Beyond the meeting room, people were already finding ways to survive. They traded tools, shared the location of safe passages, and argued over fair turns without waiting for permission. Nara listened longer than the schedule allowed. Eli reminded the officials that no policy was complete until it made sense to the people expected to live with it. Their notes began with those ordinary acts of cooperation.

會議室外，人們早已開始尋求生存方法：交換工具、分享安全路線的位置，無須等候許可便爭論公平輪班。奈拉聽得比議程允許的更久。伊萊提醒官員，政策若不能讓實際生活其中的人理解，就不算完整。他們從這些平凡合作開始記錄。

The overlooked details in the witness file made the question harder, not easier: The hospital stocked every necessary antibiotic, but medicines could not reverse an improperly timed neural pulse. Families began to applaud when the court suspended the strongest correction settings in their district. An astronomer explained that the clinic's perfect sky was a projection, not evidence about the world outside. The Authority had tried to computerize every patient statement, leaving almost no independent paper copies. Ivo helped disable the overheating relay before it could injure anyone on the hospital floor. Taken alone, no detail settled the case; together they showed why a public answer mattered.

證人檔案裡被忽視的細節使問題更加複雜，而不是更簡單： 醫院備有必要的抗生素，藥物卻無法逆轉時機錯誤的神經脈衝。 法院暫停該區最強的修正設定時，家屬開始鼓掌。 一名天文學家解釋，診所完美的天空是投影，不能證明外面的世界如何。 管理局試圖將每份病患陳述電腦化，幾乎不留下獨立的紙本副本。 伊沃協助停用過熱中繼器，避免醫院樓層有人受傷。 單獨看來沒有一項細節能結案，合在一起卻說明了公開答覆為何重要。

Then the next piece of evidence changed the shape of the case. A company drone removes the devices, claiming ownership of every reflected beam. Nara read the statement twice, searching for the sentence that would make the contradiction disappear. There was none. Eli did not reach for a dramatic accusation; instead, they listed three people who could verify the finding separately. The delay would be unpopular, but a dramatic discovery deserved more care, not less.

接著出現的新證據改變了整起案件的輪廓。公司無人機拆走裝置，聲稱每一道反射光都屬於企業。奈拉把陳述讀了兩遍，試圖找出能消除矛盾的句子，卻沒有找到。伊萊沒有立刻提出轟動指控，而是列出三位可分別驗證發現的人。延遲會不受歡迎，但重大發現更需要謹慎。

A second bundle of observations brought the consequences closer to ordinary life: Nurses had to evacuate patients according to the written plan rather than follow Vale's sudden instructions. The medical panel asked why expenditure on sleep control had tripled while ordinary clinics lost funding. One patient became irritable after a high-intensity pulse, a side effect hidden in the official statistics. The investigation asked whether the intermediary was Nara Shuo's kin. A teacher offered a quiet meditation room for anxious children, while medical staff kept the treatment area clear. Nara asked the clerk to preserve the original wording rather than improve the story.

第二疊觀察紀錄使後果貼近普通人的生活： 護理師必須依書面計畫疏散病患，而非聽從維爾的臨時指示。 醫療小組詢問，為何睡眠控制支出增加三倍，一般診所預算卻遭削減。 一名病患接受高強度脈衝後變得易怒，這項副作用被官方統計隱藏。 調查要查明中間商是否為林朔親屬。 老師替焦慮的孩子安排安靜的冥想室，同時讓醫護治療區保持暢通。 奈拉要求書記保存原本措辭，而不是把故事修飾得更好聽。

Nara had expected the hardest part to be exposing the secret. Instead, it was choosing what to do after a partial truth became public. The people who had been ignored did not all want the same remedy. Eli proposed that each affected group could submit a written objection and receive a specific answer. The method would take time, but it recognized them as participants rather than scenery.

奈拉原以為最難的是揭露祕密，後來才發現，部分真相公開後如何行動才是難題。曾被忽略的人並不全想要相同補救。伊萊提議每個受影響群體都能提出書面異議並取得具體答覆。方法需要時間，但終於把他們當成參與者，而不是背景。

The team kept the concrete observations alongside the disputed theory: A nickel coin fell from the cracked box of donated objects. The investigator used the floor map to orient new witnesses. The museum used the outdated label 'oriental' on a medical artifact; the curator agreed to replace it with a precise origin. A child treated for pneumonia had been wrongly classified as suffering from forbidden-dream sickness. The neurologist refused to activate the untested prototype until an ethics committee approved the trial. Eli marked what could be verified and left the rest open to challenge.

團隊把具體觀察與有爭議的理論並列保存： 一枚五分硬幣從破裂的捐贈物箱中掉出。 調查員用樓層圖協助新證人辨明方向。 博物館用過時的「東方的」標籤描述醫療文物，館長同意改用精確產地。 一名肺炎孩童被錯誤分類為罹患禁忌夢境疾病。 在倫理委員會批准試驗前，神經科醫師拒絕啟動未測試的原型機。 伊萊標記能查證的部分，其餘保留給人質疑。

Above the rooftops, the mirror array rotated with silent precision. Nara could read its position from a shadow on a water tank. Sunlight was physical, not a promise on a billing screen. Eli wondered aloud how many children had learned to associate dawn with the sound of a payment warning.

屋頂上方，反射鏡陣無聲而精準地旋轉。奈拉可以從水塔陰影讀出它的位置。陽光是真實的物理現象，不是帳單螢幕上的承諾。伊萊低聲問，有多少孩子已把黎明和付款警告的聲音連在一起。

The record continued with small facts that would be lost in a confident speech: The psychiatry department confirmed that fear and loss of sleep could explain some patients' symptoms. The panel demanded a robust study with independent reviewers, not a promotional chart from the Authority. Vale claimed sovereign authority over all dreams, but the court questioned whether any such power existed. A hospital spokesperson said the ruling covered only one district and did not settle the wider case. Behind the children's wardrobe, staff found an unused cable channel leading to the relay maintenance shaft. Nothing in these records excused the people who had made the dangerous decision.

紀錄接著列出那些在自信演說中容易消失的小事實： 精神醫學部確認，恐懼與睡眠不足能解釋部分病患症狀。 小組要求有獨立審查員參與的嚴謹研究，而非管理局的宣傳圖表。 維爾宣稱政府對所有夢境擁有主權，法院卻質疑這種權力是否存在。 醫院發言人表示，裁定只涵蓋一個行政區，尚未解決整體案件。 兒童衣櫃後面，員工發現一條未使用的電纜通道，通往中繼器維修井。 紀錄中沒有任何一項能替做出危險決定的人開脫。

When the lamps were lowered, Nara returned to an unpaid sunlight meter. It was neither proof of innocence nor a shortcut to justice. It was a reminder that the next decision would be witnessed by people who no longer trusted easy promises. Eli remained at the door while the final record was sealed. Outside, the ring-shadow city waited for news that had to be more precise than a victory speech.

燈光調暗時，奈拉再次看向一只欠費的陽光計量器。它既不是清白證明，也不是通往正義的捷徑，而是在提醒眾人：下一個決定會由不再相信輕易承諾的人見證。伊萊守在門邊，最後一份紀錄被封存。外頭的環影城等待的消息，必須比勝利演說更精確。
