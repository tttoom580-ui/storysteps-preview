太陽索取金錢的早晨

The Morning the Sun Asked for Money

從今天起，太陽需要每月訂閱 · 第 1 / 25 章 · 5000–6000 · 英中對照 · 校訂中

用互動版查詞、朗讀與收藏

The sun rose over Nara’s apartment for precisely eight seconds. Then her window turned black and displayed a payment screen. PLEASE RENEW YOUR DAYLIGHT SUBSCRIPTION. Across the street, a mother holding a fox cub toy stood beside the pediatric clinic, begging for a little light. The nursery’s plants were already bending toward an emergency lamp. Nara worked for the municipal light department; she knew that the orbital mirrors were still full of sunshine. The clasp on her service meter was warm from the current. What had been removed was not the sun but permission to receive it.

陽光在奈拉公寓上方升起，精確地照了八秒，接著窗戶轉黑，跳出付款畫面：「請續訂日照服務。」街對面，一位拿著小狐狸幼獸玩偶的母親站在兒科診所旁，乞求一點光。育嬰室的植物早已彎向緊急照明燈。奈拉在市政日照部門工作，知道軌道反射鏡依然照滿陽光。她的公務計量器扣環因電流而發熱。被移除的不是太陽，而是接收陽光的許可。

At nine, the company announced a new directive: every dwelling would receive seven free minutes of daylight and could purchase the rest. A commentator called the old mayor a dictator for having required a universal right to the sky. Director Solven prepared to convene a ceremony celebrating the market reform. Nara read the official motto, “A Fair Share of Every Dawn,” and wondered whether the phrase fair share had become a synonym for what people could afford. A janitor cleaning the transit roof told her that he used to watch sunlight gallop along the metal rails before the new shade panels came down.

九點，公司公布新指令：每處住所每日可得七分鐘免費日照，其餘必須購買。評論員稱前市長是獨裁者，因為他曾要求人人擁有接觸天空的權利。索爾文總監正準備召開市場改革慶典。奈拉讀著官方座右銘「公平分享每一道黎明」，心想「公平分享」是否已成了「有錢買得到」的同義詞。清潔員在交通站屋頂告訴她，以前他會看陽光沿金屬軌道奔馳，直到新遮光板降下。

Nara carried an old optical meter to the clinic’s dental wing, where children were scheduled for treatment that required a stable light cycle. The meter remained stationary on its stand, but the readings above the shadow layer were normal. A hospital trustee offered her unconditional access to the building if she would promise to keep the patients’ identities private. She agreed. There was no legal precedent, she realized, for letting a private firm decide which medical procedure deserved morning light. She photographed the readings instead of making a speech. An accusation without proof would only heighten the parents’ anxiety.

奈拉把舊光度計帶到診所牙科區，孩子們正在接受需要穩定光週期的治療。計量器穩穩放在支架上，遮光層上方的讀數卻完全正常。醫院受託人表示，只要她承諾保護病人身分，就無條件開放建物供查驗。她答應了。她忽然明白，從沒有法律先例允許私人公司決定哪一種醫療程序配得到晨光。她先拍下讀數，沒有發表演說。沒有證據的指控只會加劇家長的焦慮。

Former mirror pilot Eli met her beside a community garden that had begun to die. A gardener was trying to transplant seedlings into movable containers so they could chase stray beams of light. “They tell us we will reap more efficiency,” Eli said, “but the system throws away most of what the sky provides.” He had flown the mirrors when the array was built. The design could redirect sunlight but never create it. If the corporation had truly run out of available light, its reserve meter would have fallen. Instead it was rising. Eli handed Nara a copy of the orbital allocation log and asked her to check the figures independently.

前鏡陣駕駛伊萊在逐漸枯萎的社區花園旁與她碰面。園丁正試圖把幼苗移植進可移動容器，好追逐零星光束。「他們說我們會收穫更高效率，」伊萊說，「系統卻丟棄天空供給的大部分資源。」鏡陣建造時，他曾負責駕駛。他知道設計只能轉向陽光，不能創造陽光。若公司真的沒有可用光量，備用計量應下降，然而數值卻在升高。伊萊把軌道分配日誌交給奈拉，請她獨立核對。

By afternoon, a traveler described herself as a pilgrim of the first dawn. She had walked for days from an unregistered district, carrying a picture of a sun that children there had never seen. Nara listened while Solven’s publicity speakers promised that subscriptions would make the entire city flourish. The traveler did not ask for a free luxury; she asked why an ordinary sunrise had been defined as a luxury at all. Nara sent a copy of the meter reading to the public health board. Her report did not demand that every mirror stop turning. It demanded that the company identify who had ordered the shadows and on what authority.

下午，一名旅人自稱「第一道黎明的朝聖者」。她從未登記的街區徒步數日，帶來一張當地孩子從沒看過的太陽照片。奈拉聆聽她說話，索爾文的宣傳喇叭卻仍保證訂閱會使全城繁榮。旅人不是索取免費奢侈品，而是問平凡日出為何被定義成奢侈。奈拉把讀數送交公共衛生委員會。她的報告並未要求所有反射鏡停轉，而是要求公司說明誰有權下令製造陰影，以及依據何在。

At dusk, a service drone arrived with a notice informing Nara that her technician badge would be revoked unless she uttered a formal apology. She read the notice twice. Beneath the threat was a line of financial data that did not belong on an employee warning: PREPAID MILITARY LIGHT RESERVE—FULL. Eli looked at the number and went pale. Above them, the last narrow stripe of daylight crossed the abandoned school roof. “We are not running out of sunlight,” Nara said. “Somebody is keeping it where the invoices cannot reach.” The drone requested her signature. She left the box blank.

黃昏時，勤務無人機送來通知：奈拉若不正式說出道歉詞，技師證就會遭撤銷。她讀了兩遍。威脅下方竟有一行不該出現在員工警告上的財務資料：「預付軍用日照儲備——滿額。」伊萊看了數字便臉色發白。最後一道狹長陽光越過廢棄學校的屋頂。「我們沒有用完陽光，」奈拉說，「有人把它藏在帳單碰不到的地方。」無人機要求她簽名。她讓欄位保持空白。
