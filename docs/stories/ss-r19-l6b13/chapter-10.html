短缺是被製造的

The Scarcity Was Invented

從今天起，太陽需要每月訂閱 · 第 10 / 25 章 · 5000–6000 · 英中對照 · 校訂中

Morning brought a decision that had already been made for everyone. There was a signature at the bottom of the order, but no space for those most affected to answer. Nara decodes the orbital logs and shows that most light was diverted into an unused military mirror reserve. Nara knew that refusing a bad choice was not the same as knowing the correct one. Before making a promise, the team needed something they could test.

清晨帶來一項替所有人做好的決定。命令底下有簽名，卻沒有讓受影響最深者回答的地方。奈拉解開軌道日誌，證明多數陽光被轉送到閒置的軍用鏡陣儲備。奈拉知道拒絕壞選項不等於已經知道正確答案。作出承諾前，團隊需要能夠驗證的事物。

Nara spread the orbital allocation log across three screens, then covered the company's billing figures with a sheet of paper. The physical light measurements told a simpler story. Every dark district had been denied a share of sunlight that the mirrors were already reflecting into a sealed military reserve. The reserve was almost full. Solven had called the shortage natural and raised prices each time a neighborhood protested. Eli placed the original pilot orders beside the chart. Their signatures were authentic; the justification was not. When the public hearing began, Nara asked the director one question: 'If there is no sunlight left to sell, why are your military mirrors throwing it into empty space?' The broadcast cut to black before he answered.

奈拉把軌道分配日誌鋪滿三面螢幕，再用一張紙遮住公司帳單數字。真實的光量測數據顯示更簡單的故事：每個黑暗街區被拒絕分配的陽光，早已被鏡陣反射進封閉的軍用儲備，幾乎滿載。索爾文稱短缺是自然現象，街區每次抗議便加價。伊萊把原始駕駛命令放在圖表旁。簽名是真的，理由卻不是。公開聽證開始時，奈拉只問總監一件事：「如果沒有陽光可賣，為什麼你們的軍用鏡子正把它拋進空無一物的太空？」他還沒回答，直播便轉成黑畫面。

The overlooked details in the witness file made the question harder, not easier: An old training film used an animate cartoon train to teach children the route beyond the western gate. Simple arithmetic showed that reported train repairs continued long after the official closure date. Ivo called the live coastal signal an awesome discovery, then carefully noted its unverified source. The railway worker offered celery soup made from vegetables grown in a garden near the sealed gate. Fresh cement around the old gate contradicted claims that no one had maintained it for decades. Eli marked what could be verified and left the rest open to challenge.

證人檔案裡被忽視的細節使問題更加複雜，而不是更簡單： 一部舊教學影片用栩栩如生的卡通列車教孩子認識西門外路線。 簡單的算術就能看出，列車維修紀錄在官方封閉日期之後仍持續多年。 伊沃說即時海岸訊號是驚人的發現，隨後仍謹慎註明來源尚未驗證。 鐵路維修員拿出芹菜湯，食材種在封閉城門旁的菜園。 舊城門周圍的新水泥，與數十年無人維護的說法矛盾。 伊萊標記能查證的部分，其餘保留給人質疑。

What changed the discussion was a fact that should have been obvious from the start. Solven admits the subscription price was based on artificial scarcity rather than physical shortage. Nara felt the old temptation to turn that fact into an immediate victory. Instead, the team separated what the new evidence established from what it merely suggested. That distinction frustrated the loudest spectators, but it helped the witnesses remain willing to speak again.

改變討論的是一件本來就該看得見的事實。索爾文承認訂閱價格依據人為短缺，而非真正的物理不足。奈拉一瞬間也想把它當成立刻勝利的證明，團隊卻把新證據真正確立的部分與僅能推測的部分分開。這讓最喧鬧的旁觀者失望，卻使證人願意再次出面。

A second bundle of observations brought the consequences closer to ordinary life: The coastal radio mentioned a confederation of towns that the city's textbooks described as extinct. A public crackdown on unauthorized boats could not substitute for fixing confusing instructions, Eli argued at the hearing. A crocodile drawing on the children's wall kept the safety lesson playful. The old railway had a dual power system, allowing trains to operate even when city electricity failed. The city could not equalize every skill level, but it could make entry fairer. Nothing in these records excused the people who had made the dangerous decision.

第二疊觀察紀錄使後果貼近普通人的生活： 海岸廣播提到一個城鎮邦聯，城市教科書卻稱其早已消亡。 言秋在聽證時主張，嚴厲取締未經許可的船隻不能取代修正令人困惑的指示。 兒童牆上的鱷魚圖讓安全課帶點趣味。 舊鐵路有雙重電力系統，即使城市停電，列車仍能運行。 市府無法讓每個人的技能相同，卻能使入場機會更公平。 紀錄中沒有任何一項能替做出危險決定的人開脫。

No one in the room was permitted to declare that a good intention canceled a harmful action. Nara insisted on recording the error, identifying who had authorized it, and protecting those affected before assigning wider blame. Eli drew a simple boundary around what they could do tonight. The line looked disappointingly modest, yet it was the first promise that did not require another person to surrender a right.

室內沒有人能宣稱善意可以抵銷傷害。奈拉堅持記錄錯誤、確認授權者，並在追究更大責任前保護受害者。伊萊畫出今晚能做到的清楚界線。這條線看似微不足道，卻是第一個不要求別人放棄權利的承諾。

The team kept the concrete observations alongside the disputed theory: A council faction argued that opening the gate would endanger the city's fragile social order. The gate engineer feared he would incur criminal penalties merely for preserving an obsolete switch. Residents called unequal access to safety information an injustice, especially when only wealthy boat owners received private warnings. The unexplained coastal signal deepened the intrigue surrounding the city's false founding history. A flashy app could lure officials away from the needs of paper-card residents. Taken alone, no detail settled the case; together they showed why a public answer mattered.

團隊把具體觀察與有爭議的理論並列保存： 一個議會派系主張開城門會危及城市脆弱的社會秩序。 城門工程師害怕只因保存淘汰轉轍器，就會受到刑事處罰。 居民認為安全資訊分配不均是一種不公，尤其只有富有船主能得到私人警示。 無法解釋的海岸訊號，讓城市虛假建城史背後的陰謀更加撲朔迷離。 華麗應用程式可能引誘官員忽略紙本求援急救手冊證居民。 單獨看來沒有一項細節能結案，合在一起卻說明了公開答覆為何重要。

Above the rooftops, the mirror array rotated with silent precision. Nara could read its position from a shadow on a water tank. Sunlight was physical, not a promise on a billing screen. Eli wondered aloud how many children had learned to associate dawn with the sound of a payment warning.

屋頂上方，反射鏡陣無聲而精準地旋轉。奈拉可以從水塔陰影讀出它的位置。陽光是真實的物理現象，不是帳單螢幕上的承諾。伊萊低聲問，有多少孩子已把黎明和付款警告的聲音連在一起。

The record continued with small facts that would be lost in a confident speech: Vale called the inspector obstinate for refusing to surrender the old receiver without a warrant. A pickpocket had once stolen a train ticket and kept it as a souvenir after the railway disappeared. The cleaning crew left on time because the new contracts required punctual shifts. The chair showed restraint by allowing critics to finish their comments. After twelve hours of testimony, the exhausted witness began to stagger toward the exit, and the chair called a break. Nara asked the clerk to preserve the original wording rather than improve the story.

紀錄接著列出那些在自信演說中容易消失的小事實： 維爾說檢查員固執，只因他拒絕在沒有令狀時交出舊接收器。 一名扒手曾偷過火車票，鐵路消失後卻把票當作紀念品保存。 清潔團隊準時下班，因為新合約要求準點輪班。 主席克制自己，讓批評者把話說完。 證人作證十二小時後，疲憊得往出口踉蹌，主席因此宣布休息。 奈拉要求書記保存原本措辭，而不是把故事修飾得更好聽。

On the way out, Nara passed people waiting for a result that would affect their meals, their homes, or their ability to say no. None of them had the luxury of treating the dispute as an intellectual puzzle. Eli paused to answer a child's practical question before they left. The answer was incomplete, but unlike the old official notice, it admitted the uncertainty out loud.

離開時，奈拉經過等待結果的人群，這項結果將影響他們的餐食、家園或說不的能力。他們不能奢侈地把爭論當成智力謎題。伊萊停下回答一名孩子實際的問題。答案並不完整，卻與舊公告不同：它坦白承認不確定之處。
