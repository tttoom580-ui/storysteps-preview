Nina had been thinking about simple things in the college music room when a detail refused to fit the old picture. For a moment, everyone around seemed busy with something else. Nina tried not to rush, because a wrong guess could make the problem harder for someone nearby. The sound journal was close at hand, ready for the facts that would be easy to forget once people began talking. A little note used “apartment” as its key word; Nina kept the note because the rest of the line was still useful. Nina circled “late” on a reading sheet, not to win a test but to remember the meaning of the whole sentence. The word “reporter” appeared in an example someone had written earlier, and Leo asked if the example fit their situation.
