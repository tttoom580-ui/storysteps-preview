At the school festival, Nina sang the new song while Leo watched the sound levels from the side. The college music room had its own familiar rhythm. Nina knew the sound of footsteps there, yet something about this moment called for a closer look. It was tempting to make a quick story out of a small surprise. Instead, Nina looked for one thing that could be checked and one person who deserved to be heard. There was room in the sound journal for questions as well as answers, and today the questions were beginning to matter. It sounded like a good idea, but they still wanted to check it. “Let us keep ‘month’ in our word notes,” Nina said. “We can check its meaning again if we need it.” In the corner of the sound journal, Nina found the word “young” beside an old drawing and stopped to ask what it meant.

學園祭上，妮娜唱出新歌，里歐在舞台旁留意音量。大學音樂教室有自己熟悉的節奏。妮娜熟悉那裡的腳步聲，但此刻某件事值得更仔細地看。一個小驚喜很容易讓人立刻編出解釋。妮娜卻先尋找一件可核對的事，以及一位需要被傾聽的人。聲音日記裡不只可以寫答案，也能記下問題；今天，那些問題開始變得重要。這聽起來是好主意，但他們仍要確認。「把『month』留在詞彙筆記裡吧，」妮娜說，「有需要時再確認意思。」在聲音日記的角落，妮娜看見一幅舊畫旁寫著「young」，於是停下來詢問意思。

The first reaction in the college music room was a mixture of surprise and hope. Nina felt both, and did not pretend otherwise. Leo pointed out that a helpful choice for one person might still make trouble for someone else. Together they tried to find a step small enough to test, but useful enough to show whether their idea worked. Nina circled “cost” on a reading sheet, not to win a test but to remember the meaning of the whole sentence. The word “miss” appeared in an example someone had written earlier, and Leo asked if the example fit their situation. “Let us keep ‘truck’ in our word notes,” Nina said. “We can check its meaning again if we need it.”

大學音樂教室裡第一時間既有驚訝，也有希望。妮娜兩種感受都有，並不打算假裝沒有。里歐指出，對某人有幫助的選擇，仍可能給另一個人添麻煩。兩人一起尋找一個夠小、能安全測試，同時足以證明想法是否可行的步驟。妮娜在閱讀單上圈出「cost」，不是為了考高分，而是為了理解整句話。「miss」出現在前人留下的例句裡，里歐問這個例子是否適合現在的情況。「把『truck』留在詞彙筆記裡吧，」妮娜說，「有需要時再確認意思。」

A note came out wrong, but the group carried on and the audience stayed with them. The trouble was real, even if the danger had not yet become clear to everyone in the college music room. Nina took a step back to let someone with more experience speak. Help did not always mean being the first person to act. That gave Leo time to notice a detail that had been lost in the noise. Leo loved sound because it could hold a moment. Nina was learning that a moment did not have to hold an answer. A little note used “club” as its key word; Nina kept the note because the rest of the line was still useful. Nina circled “meeting” on a reading sheet, not to win a test but to remember the meaning of the whole sentence. The word “stop” appeared in an example someone had written earlier, and Leo asked if the example fit their situation.

一個音唱錯了，大家仍繼續演出，觀眾也支持他們。麻煩是真實的，即使大學音樂教室裡並非每個人都看清了風險。妮娜退一步，讓更有經驗的人說話。幫忙不一定代表第一個採取行動。這讓里歐有時間注意到先前被吵鬧掩蓋的細節。里歐喜歡聲音，因為聲音能留住瞬間。妮娜開始明白，瞬間不一定包含答案。一張小筆記以「club」為關鍵字；妮娜留下它，因為剩下的句子仍然有用。妮娜在閱讀單上圈出「meeting」，不是為了考高分，而是為了理解整句話。「stop」出現在前人留下的例句裡，里歐問這個例子是否適合現在的情況。

音樂老師聽過雙方意見後加入討論。問題很簡單，答案卻未必簡單。誰會受影響？今天能確認什麼？哪些事要等具備專業能力的人在場？妮娜把答案抄進聲音日記。當計畫是寫給所有人看，而不只是制定者自己時，也就更容易執行。「kind。」里歐讀出練習頁上的字，接著試著把它和眼前真正看得到的東西連起來。一張小筆記以「share」為關鍵字；妮娜留下它，因為剩下的句子仍然有用。

Nina realized the mistake had made the real moment theirs. The new detail made the room seem a little different. Things that had looked unrelated began to form a line Nina could follow. Leo warned that one clear clue did not mean they knew everything. Nina agreed and wrote the date beside the note. There was work still to do, but now they understood why that work mattered. 妮娜明白，那個小錯誤反而讓此刻真正屬於他們。新細節讓房間看起來有些不同。原本互不相關的事物，開始連成妮娜能循著走的一條線。里歐提醒，一條清楚線索不代表知道全部。妮娜同意了，在筆記旁寫下日期。仍有很多事要做，但大家現在知道為什麼這些事重要。在聲音日記的角落，妮娜看見一幅舊畫旁寫著「holiday」，於是停下來詢問意思。「national。」里歐讀出練習頁上的字，接著試著把它和眼前真正看得到的東西連起來。

When Nina returned to the college music room, the place was almost the same. The difference was in what Nina now understood. Leo had helped without taking away the choice, and that made the help easier to accept. For now, they could rest.
