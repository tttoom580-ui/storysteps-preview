Nina had been thinking about simple things in the college music room when a detail refused to fit the old picture. For a moment, everyone around seemed busy with something else. Nina tried not to rush, because a wrong guess could make the problem harder for someone nearby. The sound journal was close at hand, ready for the facts that would be easy to forget once people began talking. In the corner of the sound journal, Nina found the word “draw” beside an old drawing and stopped to ask what it meant. “later,” Leo read from the practice page, then tried to connect the word to something they could actually see. A little note used “sit” as its key word; Nina kept the note because the rest of the line was still useful.
