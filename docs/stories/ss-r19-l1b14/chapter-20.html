Leo's aunt offered to take both old devices back to the lab for storage. Before anyone called the day strange, Nina had noticed how quiet the college music room could become when people were worried. The smallest sounds seemed clear, and the smell of old wood and coffee made the place feel both familiar and new. Rather than act as if every answer was obvious, Nina opened the sound journal and began with what had really happened. A little note used “elephant” as its key word; Nina kept the note because the rest of the line was still useful. Nina circled “million” on a reading sheet, not to win a test but to remember the meaning of the whole sentence. The word “violin” appeared in an example someone had written earlier, and Leo asked if the example fit their situation.

里歐的姑姑提議把兩副舊耳機都帶回實驗室保存。在人們說這一天奇怪之前，妮娜就注意到，當大家擔心時，大學音樂教室會變得多麼安靜。細微的聲音變得清楚，舊木頭與咖啡的氣味也讓這地方既熟悉又陌生。妮娜沒有裝作一切都很明白，而是打開聲音日記，從真正發生的事情開始。一張小筆記以「elephant」為關鍵字；妮娜留下它，因為剩下的句子仍然有用。妮娜在閱讀單上圈出「million」，不是為了考高分，而是為了理解整句話。「violin」出現在前人留下的例句裡，里歐問這個例子是否適合現在的情況。

Nobody could say yet how the story would end. Nina found this uncomfortable, especially with tomorrow’s ten seconds still waiting for an answer. Leo suggested they take turns speaking, because repeating the same fear would not create a new solution. A little order returned to the conversation, and that made the next difficult step possible. Leo loved sound because it could hold a moment. Nina was learning that a moment did not have to hold an answer. “appear,” Leo read from the practice page, then tried to connect the word to something they could actually see. A little note used “lazy” as its key word; Nina kept the note because the rest of the line was still useful. Nina circled “soup” on a reading sheet, not to win a test but to remember the meaning of the whole sentence.

沒有人知道事情會如何結束。妮娜覺得不安，尤其明天的十秒聲音還等著答案。里歐建議輪流發言，因為一再重複恐懼並不會帶來新方法。談話恢復了一些秩序，也讓接下來的難題變得有機會處理。里歐喜歡聲音，因為聲音能留住瞬間。妮娜開始明白，瞬間不一定包含答案。「appear。」里歐讀出練習頁上的字，接著試著把它和眼前真正看得到的東西連起來。一張小筆記以「lazy」為關鍵字；妮娜留下它，因為剩下的句子仍然有用。妮娜在閱讀單上圈出「soup」，不是為了考高分，而是為了理解整句話。

Leo hesitated because he had used them to feel less afraid of making mistakes. This was the point where a simple wish met the world as it really was. Nina wanted to help, but wanting did not make every road safe or every rule fair. Leo asked what the other people involved might lose if the wrong choice were made. The answer took longer than either had expected. They agreed not to build the rest of their plan on a fact they had never checked. In the corner of the sound journal, Nina found the word “knife” beside an old drawing and stopped to ask what it meant. “round,” Leo read from the practice page, then tried to connect the word to something they could actually see.

里歐猶豫，因為耳機曾讓他不那麼害怕犯錯。這正是簡單願望遇上真實世界的時刻。妮娜想幫忙，但只靠想法不會讓所有路變安全，也不會讓所有規則公平。里歐問，如果選錯了，牽涉其中的人可能失去什麼。兩人花了比預期更長的時間才回答。他們同意，不把整個計畫建立在尚未核實的事實上。在聲音日記的角落，妮娜看見一幅舊畫旁寫著「knife」，於是停下來詢問意思。「round。」里歐讀出練習頁上的字，接著試著把它和眼前真正看得到的東西連起來。

The group returned to the sound journal and looked at the first line again. It now seemed to ask a different question. Nina asked Leo to explain the idea in plain words, as if telling it to someone who had just arrived. When the idea sounded confusing, they changed the plan rather than blaming the listener. “Let us keep ‘go’ in our word notes,” Nina said. “We can check its meaning again if we need it.” In the corner of the sound journal, Nina found the word “pool” beside an old drawing and stopped to ask what it meant.

大家重新看著聲音日記的第一行，發現它彷彿提出了另一個問題。妮娜請里歐用簡單的話解釋，就像對剛來的人說明。說明若讓人困惑，他們就修改計畫，而不是責怪聽的人。「把『go』留在詞彙筆記裡吧，」妮娜說，「有需要時再確認意思。」在聲音日記的角落，妮娜看見一幅舊畫旁寫著「pool」，於是停下來詢問意思。

Nina said he was allowed to be unsure without being alone. The answer brought relief, but it also carried a new responsibility. Nina did not look away from that part. Leo asked who should hear the news next, and whether it belonged to them to share it. They chose the careful answer over the exciting one, though excitement was still in the air. The word “fool” appeared in an example someone had written earlier, and Leo asked if the example fit their situation. “Let us keep ‘out’ in our word notes,” Nina said. “We can check its meaning again if we need it.”

妮娜說他可以感到不確定，不必因此孤單。答案帶來安心，也帶來新的責任。妮娜沒有迴避這一部分。里歐問下一個該讓誰知道消息，以及他們是否有權公開。他們選擇審慎的回答，而非最刺激的那個，雖然空氣中仍充滿興奮。「fool」出現在前人留下的例句裡，里歐問這個例子是否適合現在的情況。「把『out』留在詞彙筆記裡吧，」妮娜說，「有需要時再確認意思。」

The clock moved on. Nina felt tired, yet there was a good kind of energy left after honest work. Nobody had to say that the day was perfect. They only had to remember what had worked and what still needed care. Nina closed the sound journal gently, already curious about the next choice. Nina circled “fail” on a reading sheet, not to win a test but to remember the meaning of the whole sentence. The word “mountain” appeared in an example someone had written earlier, and Leo asked if the example fit their situation. “Let us keep ‘wet’ in our word notes,” Nina said. “We can check its meaning again if we need it.”

時鐘繼續走。妮娜雖然疲倦，卻保有完成誠實工作後的好心情。沒有人必須宣稱這天完美；只要記得哪些方法有效、哪些事仍需照顧。妮娜輕輕闔上聲音日記，開始好奇下一個選擇。妮娜在閱讀單上圈出「fail」，不是為了考高分，而是為了理解整句話。「mountain」出現在前人留下的例句裡，里歐問這個例子是否適合現在的情況。「把『wet』留在詞彙筆記裡吧，」妮娜說，「有需要時再確認意思。」
