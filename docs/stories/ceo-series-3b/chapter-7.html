沒有掌聲的廚房

The Kitchen Without Applause

The audit moved to the kitchen after cooks reported that their work schedules changed without notice. Yuqing visited during a quiet hour with the head chef's consent. She found a narrow cupboard full of old forms and a brick wall patched with different colors. A worker said the storage area had been called awful for years. Yaochuan joined the visit wearing an expensive coat, which made everyone awkward. He took it off and asked the chef where to begin. The chef pointed to the time clock, not the elegant dining room.

廚師通報班表在未通知下改動後，稽核轉往廚房。予晴經主廚同意，挑安靜時段到訪。她看到窄櫥櫃塞滿舊表單，磚牆則補上不同顏色的材料。一位員工說，倉庫多年來都被稱作糟糕。曜川穿著昂貴外套加入，讓眾人尷尬。他脫下外套問主廚該從哪裡開始，主廚指向打卡鐘，而不是華麗餐廳。

A chef showed them a jar of lily tea and a vase from the old opening ceremony. Both had been kept after the company replaced most tools. Beside them stood junk that should have been removed long ago. The chef explained how a missing dime in a cash box could lead to an investigation, while hours of unpaid work were treated as normal. Yuqing asked him to show an example, and he produced a wage sheet with two different totals. The discovery gave the staff a concrete reason to appeal the old decision.

主廚拿出一罐百合茶及一個開幕時留下的花瓶。公司更換大部分工具後，兩者仍被保留，旁邊卻堆著早該清掉的雜物。主廚解釋，收銀盒少一角錢會引發調查，幾小時沒領到的工資卻被當成理所當然。予晴請他提出例子，他拿來一張寫著兩種總額的薪資單。這個發現讓員工有具體理由對舊決定提出申訴。

The kitchen manager said he had invented a flexible system to help workers exchange shifts. The schedule showed something different: changes were made after the work had been done. Chen requested all timekeeping records for the same period. Yuqing reminded everyone not to accuse the manager before hearing his explanation. A fair investigation was not an invitation to replace one unfair story with another. Yaochuan agreed to remove the manager's control over the disputed records while the review continued.

廚房經理說自己發明了彈性制度，方便員工交換班次。班表顯示的卻是另一回事：有些修改在工作結束後才發生。陳女士要求取得同一時期的全部工時紀錄。予晴提醒眾人，尚未聽經理解釋前不要直接指控。公平調查不能用另一個不公平故事取代原本的問題。曜川同意在查核期間暫停經理對爭議紀錄的控制權。

When an emergency alarm sounded, the cooks moved steadily to the side door. A pan had produced smoke, but nothing had begun to explode. One junior cook described the flavor of burnt oil while another checked that nobody's flesh had been burned. “A terrific response,” the safety officer said, then reminded them that an alarm could not be ignored sometime just because it was inconvenient. Yuqing noticed how a genuine emergency changed priorities more quickly than any press campaign. She asked staff to record what had worked and what had failed.

緊急警鈴響起時，廚師們穩定地移向側門。一只鍋冒出煙，但沒有東西真的爆炸。一名年輕廚師描述燒焦油的味道，另一人確認沒有人被燙傷。「反應非常好，」安全主管說，並提醒大家不能因為某次警報不方便就忽略它。予晴發現真正的緊急狀況比任何公關活動都更快改變優先順序。她請員工記錄哪些地方做對了，哪些沒有。

After the all-clear signal, the kitchen returned to work. Yuqing stayed to wash a tray rather than take photographs. The chef asked whether the company could still operate if the audit found large unpaid sums. She did not know. “You deserve an answer based on the accounts,” she said, “not one based on my hope.” He appreciated the distinction. Outside, Yaochuan watched a delivery truck leave the gate. He admitted that his own office received polished summaries and almost no honest interruptions from the kitchen.

警報解除後，廚房恢復工作。予晴留下洗托盤，而不是拍照片。主廚問，如果稽核發現公司欠了大筆款項，飯店還能不能繼續營運。她不知道。「你值得一個根據帳目而來的答案，」她說，「不是根據我希望的答案。」主廚很欣賞這個區別。外頭，曜川看著送貨車駛出大門，承認自己的辦公室只收到漂亮摘要，幾乎聽不到廚房真正的打斷聲。

The chef said he could invent a hundred new dishes, but no clever menu would remind management to pay the staff correctly. A good restaurant needed steady hands and steady rules. Yuqing repeated his words to the branch manager and asked him to describe how shift changes would be approved. The manager offered a form that required two signatures; Lu requested that workers receive a copy for their own records. Yaochuan agreed to test the system for one month before expanding it. No one called the proposal terrific until a worker had tried it on a busy evening. Yuqing liked the caution. It was evidence that the chef's practical knowledge had begun to matter as much as the executive plan.

主廚說自己能發明上百道新菜，但再厲害的菜單也無法提醒管理層正確付薪。一間好餐廳需要穩定的雙手，也需要穩定的規則。予晴把他的話轉告分館經理，請他說明換班如何核准。經理提出雙方簽名表單，許露要求員工也能保留副本。曜川同意先測試一個月，再考慮擴大。直到員工在忙碌夜晚真正用過，沒有人急著稱讚方案太棒。予晴喜歡這份謹慎，因為主廚的實際知識開始和高層計畫同樣重要。

On the walk back, he asked if she would design a system that made disagreement visible before it became a scandal. Yuqing answered that she could advise the company, but the staff must help make the rules. “And if they ask for a rule I dislike?” he asked. “Then you explain your reasons and let them challenge those reasons.” He almost smiled. It was not a romantic confession. It was a more difficult kind of attention, and Yuqing began to respect it.

回去路上，他問她能否設計制度，讓不同意見在變成醜聞前就能被看見。予晴說她可以提供建議，但規則必須讓員工參與制定。「如果他們要求我不喜歡的規則呢？」他問。「那你解釋理由，也讓他們挑戰那些理由。」他差點笑了。那不是浪漫告白，而是更困難的傾聽；予晴開始尊重這一點。
