第一個被確認的數字

The First Verified Number

總裁，請先撤回那則聲明 · 第 11 / 25 章 · 2000–3000 · 英中對照 · 校訂中

用互動版查詞、朗讀與收藏

The outside auditors delivered their first verified finding on a rainy Monday. Several workers had been paid for the wrong number of hours, and the errors followed a repeated pattern. The auditors could not yet decide whether the pattern was intentional. Yuqing proposed that the company correct confirmed amounts immediately, then continue examining the remaining accounts. Yaochuan agreed to release funds without waiting for a final publicity plan. It was the first time she had seen him spend money before deciding how the story would sound.

週一雨天，外部稽核員交出第一項確認結果：幾位員工領到的工資時數不對，錯誤呈現重複模式。稽核員尚不能判定是否出於故意。予晴建議先補發已確定的金額，再繼續檢查其他帳目。曜川同意在最終公關計畫出爐前先撥款。這是她第一次看到他還沒決定故事該怎麼說，就先花錢處理問題。

An accurate count required more than a clever theory. In a county branch near a ditch, staff had worked through a flood while the freezer broke and a gasoline delivery was delayed. One worker had to dive into a storage room to rescue a box of records before the water reached it. Another injured a muscle carrying timber across the wet floor. The plastic covers on the logbooks were loose, but most pages had survived. The local supervisor had reported roughly the right hours; a later software operation had changed the totals.

準確計算需要的不只是聰明理論。在一處靠近排水溝的郡區分館，員工曾在洪水中工作，當時冷凍庫故障，汽油運送也延誤。一人為了在水淹到之前搶救紀錄箱，衝進倉庫；另一人搬運木材時拉傷肌肉。日誌外的塑膠套鬆了，多數頁面仍保存。當地主管最初填報的工時大致正確，後來卻被軟體操作改了總額。

The finance director said the difference was too small to threaten the group. Lu replied that it was not small to an employee paying rent. Yuqing asked both sides to avoid using a single total as a substitute for individual accounts. The auditors would notify each affected worker privately and explain how to challenge a calculation. Yaochuan wanted to send an apology with every payment. Yuqing agreed only if the note gave a contact for corrections rather than asking for public gratitude.

財務主管說差額太小，不至於威脅集團。許露回應，對要付房租的員工而言並不小。予晴請雙方不要用一個總數代替個別帳目。稽核員會私下通知受影響員工，並解釋如何對計算提出異議。曜川希望每筆補款都附道歉信。予晴同意的條件是，信中必須提供更正聯絡方式，而不是要求公開感謝。

The medium-sized press room filled with reporters who wanted a dramatic confession. A journalist wore a violet coat and asked whether a single payment could weaken public anger. A manager began to sweat. Yuqing answered that payment was a duty, not a performance. “The intelligent question,” she said, “is whether the new operation can prevent the same error.” She explained the review's scope and refused to claim that all records were already correct. The room quieted when she offered the written method rather than a polished slogan.

中型記者室擠滿想聽戲劇化坦承的記者。一位穿紫羅蘭色外套的記者問，一筆補款能不能削弱公眾怒氣。一名經理開始冒汗。予晴回答，補款是義務，不是表演。「聰明的問題，」她說，「是新作業能否防止相同錯誤。」她說明查核範圍，拒絕宣稱所有紀錄都已正確。她提供的是書面方法而非精緻口號，會場反而安靜下來。

The first employee who received a corrected payment asked not to be named in the company's announcement. Yaochuan authorized the payment without bargaining for an interview. Yuqing saw the decision as a quiet turning point. He was finally willing to lose a public opportunity in order to keep a private promise. Yet the money did not erase the earlier delay. She reminded the board that correction and accountability were separate tasks, and both needed dates and assigned owners.

第一位收到補款的員工要求不要在公司公告中具名。曜川不以接受訪問為條件，直接核准付款。予晴將此視為安靜的轉折：他終於願意失去一個公開曝光機會，來履行私人承諾。但錢無法抹去先前的延誤。她提醒董事會，更正與究責是兩項不同工作，都需要期限和負責人。

An older worker raised his hands as if to bless the new system, then lowered them and asked to see how it would perform during the next busy week. The auditors welcomed the question and scheduled a sample review. Yaochuan said the company would publish the result even if errors remained. Yuqing suggested adding a direct contact for workers who found a mistake. She had learned that a document could be technically accurate on the day it was printed and outdated shortly afterward. The best report made room for correction. As the meeting ended, the worker smiled and said he hoped not to have to use the complaint line again. Nobody promised he never would. They promised that if he needed it, someone would answer.

一位年長員工舉起雙手，彷彿要祝福新制度，接著放下手，要求看看它下個忙碌星期會如何運作。稽核員歡迎問題，安排抽樣複查。曜川說，即使仍有錯誤，公司也會公布結果。予晴建議增加員工發現錯誤時可直接聯絡的管道。她學到，一份文件即使印出當天技術上正確，不久後仍可能過時。最好的報告必須允許更正。會後員工笑說，希望不必再打申訴電話。沒有人保證他絕不需要，只保證需要時有人回答。

At the end of the day he handed her the list of personal actions she had requested at dinner. It included a commitment to attend a worker forum without cameras, to review the authority of the marketing department, and to sign a policy preventing retaliation. She read it slowly. “These are meaningful,” she said. “They will become more meaningful when someone checks them after three months.” He asked whether she could be that person. She explained that independence might require someone else. He accepted the possibility without complaint.

一天結束時，他交給她那晚餐時答應的個人行動清單，內容包括沒有攝影機的員工座談、檢討行銷部權限，以及簽署防報復政策。她慢慢讀完。「這些有意義，」她說，「三個月後有人再檢查，會更有意義。」他問她能不能當那個人。她解釋，為了獨立性，可能需要其他人。他沒有抱怨，接受了這個可能。
