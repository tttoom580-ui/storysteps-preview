被剪掉的聲音

The Voice Removed

總裁，請先撤回那則聲明 · 第 5 / 25 章 · 2000–3000 · 英中對照 · 校訂中

A technician restored the first full version of the viral video from a backup device. It showed the housekeeper, Fang Mei, speaking at a staff meeting. She had asked the company to correct unpaid overtime. At the end, her voice became tired and she said she might quit if no one listened. The public clip had removed the earlier request, leaving only tears. Yuqing wrote a new timeline and asked Mei whether she wanted the full version published. Mei said no. The video contained other workers who had never agreed to appear publicly.

技師從備份裝置找回最初的完整影片。畫面裡，房務員方梅在員工會議上發言，要求公司改正未付的加班費。最後她聲音疲倦，說如果沒人願意聽，她可能會辭職。網路上的短片剪掉前面的要求，只留下眼淚。予晴重新整理時間線，問方梅願不願意公開完整版。方梅說不願意，因為裡面還有其他未同意公開露面的員工。

At a breakfast table a guest complained about cold bacon and a wet carpet. A worker with a cough brought a flashlight to inspect the dark garage, where a delivery motor had failed beside a sack of ripe fruit. The kitchen offered a peanut snack and a cup of tea, but Mei's question about pay received no answer. Another staff member held a fake microphone and tried to pronounce the group's slogan. Yuqing listened to the recording and heard steam from the kitchen interrupt a serious discussion. The small details made the meeting ordinary, not violent.

早餐桌旁有位客人抱怨培根冷了、地毯濕了。一名咳嗽的員工帶著手電筒檢查昏暗車庫，送貨機車的馬達在一袋成熟水果旁故障。廚房端出花生點心與茶，但方梅關於薪資的問題仍沒得到答覆。另一名員工拿假麥克風練習念出集團口號。予晴聽錄音時，聽見廚房蒸氣聲打斷嚴肅討論。這些細節讓會議顯得平凡，而非暴力。

The marketing director wanted to publish a dramatic correction featuring Mei herself. He argued that an emotional apology would attract sympathy. Yuqing answered that Mei had already given the company her time and words; she should not be forced to give it her face as well. Yaochuan asked if he could speak to Mei privately. Yuqing said only if Mei wanted it, and only with a representative she trusted. For once he accepted a condition before sending an invitation.

行銷主管想推出一段由方梅親自出鏡的戲劇化澄清，認為感性的道歉能博得同情。予晴回答，方梅已經把時間和話語交給公司，不該再被迫交出自己的臉。曜川問能不能私下和方梅談。予晴說，只有她願意，而且必須有她信任的代表陪同。他這次在發出邀請前先接受條件。

The team discussed its responsibility to staff before discussing publicity. An engagement party in the next hall continued with cheerful music, but several workers watched with sorrow because a colleague had been dismissed after asking about wages. Someone suggested that a press conference could turn public anger into praise. Yuqing pointed to the recorded question on the screen. “Considerable attention is not the same as a solution,” she said. “An apology can begin a repair, but it cannot calculate the missing hours or send money to an employee.”

團隊先討論對員工的責任，才談宣傳。隔壁廳的訂婚派對仍播放歡樂音樂，幾位員工卻帶著悲傷看著，因為同事詢問薪資後遭到解雇。有人提議靠記者會把大眾憤怒轉成讚美。予晴指著螢幕上被記錄的提問說：「受到大量關注不等於問題解決。道歉可以開始修復，但不能算出少付的時數，也不能把錢匯給員工。」

Yaochuan asked the finance manager to preserve the time sheets without removing personal details from the original files. A separate copy would be prepared for the independent reviewer with careful access controls. Yuqing drafted a notice explaining the scope of the review and how workers could correct their own records. She refused to call every claim true before checking it, just as she had refused to call every claim false. The method was slower than a press release, but it gave people a fair chance to be heard.

曜川要求財務主管保存原始工時表，不得從原始檔刪除個人資料；另製作受到權限控制的副本，交給獨立審查員。予晴起草公告，說明查核範圍及員工如何更正自己的紀錄。她沒有在查證前就宣稱所有指控都是真的，正如她先前拒絕宣稱全部都是假的。這個方法比發新聞稿慢，卻讓每個人有公平被聽見的機會。

Mei said she had tried to complain through the official channel three times. Each time a manager promised to call her back, and each time the promise vanished. When she described the final meeting, Yaochuan's jaw grew tight. Yuqing asked him not to interrupt. Mei was not asking to be comforted by an important man; she wanted the hours on her payslip checked against her own notes. Lu placed the notes on the table and asked for a dated receipt showing that the company had received them. Yaochuan signed the receipt but did not declare the numbers correct. Mei thanked him for accepting the documents. She did not thank him for a solution that had not yet happened, and no one demanded it.

方梅說自己曾三次透過正式管道申訴。每次經理都答應回電，承諾卻每次落空。她描述最後一場會議時，曜川下顎繃緊。予晴請他不要打斷。方梅不是在尋求重要人物安慰，而是要核對薪資單上的時數與自己的筆記。許露把筆記放在桌上，要求一張附日期的收件證明。曜川簽收，卻沒有宣稱數字都正確。方梅感謝他接受文件，沒有感謝尚未發生的解決方案，也沒有人要求她這麼做。

After the meeting Yaochuan stood beside the elevator, still holding the unsigned dramatic apology. “Do you think I care only about the image?” he asked. Yuqing considered her answer. “I think you are accustomed to fixing the image first. I need to know whether you can change what the image is showing.” He folded the paper and put it away. That evening he authorized an outside payroll review. The first payment adjustments would wait for evidence, not for a photographer. Mei sent Yuqing one message: “Thank you for not making me perform my pain.”

會後曜川站在電梯旁，手裡還拿著那份未簽名的戲劇化道歉稿。「妳覺得我只在乎形象？」他問。予晴想過才回答：「我覺得你習慣先修補形象。我需要知道你能不能改變形象背後的事情。」他把紙折起來收好。當晚，他批准外部薪資查核。第一筆調整款將根據證據發放，而不是配合攝影師的時間。方梅傳訊給予晴：「謝謝妳沒有要我表演自己的痛苦。」
