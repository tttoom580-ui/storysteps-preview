A Door in the Alley

總裁，請先撤回那則聲明 · 第 4 / 25 章 · 2000–3000 · 英中對照 · 校訂中

用互動版查詞、朗讀與收藏

An anonymous message led Yuqing to the service alley behind the harbor branch. The note said that employees had once used a door there to leave after midnight, until it was locked without warning. Yuqing arrived with Lu and a building inspector, never alone and never secretly. The door was still there behind stacked boxes. A camera faced it, but the sign describing the route had been painted over. Yuqing took photographs with dates and asked the inspector to record what he could verify.

匿名訊息引導予晴走到海港分館後方的服務巷。便條說，員工過去午夜後會從那扇門離開，後來卻在沒有通知下被鎖住。予晴與許露、建築檢查員一同到場，沒有單獨行動，也沒有偷偷潛入。門還在堆起的箱子後方，攝影機對著它，但路線標示已被油漆蓋住。予晴拍下標明日期的照片，請檢查員記錄能查證的部分。

A cable crossed the alley above a van delivering cherry cakes. The driver said a near crash had made him dizzy the previous week. A woman carrying a knit scarf offered him a saucer of water and thanked the team for bringing warmth to the cold yard. Lu asked whether the driver's bravery would appear in the safety report. The inspector answered that bravery was not a substitute for a solid barrier. An old label on the door called it a fire exit; another document used a different definition. They agreed to investigate both.

一條電纜橫過巷子上方，一輛送櫻桃蛋糕的廂型車從下方經過。司機說上週差點撞車，讓他頭昏眼花。一名圍著針織圍巾的女子遞給他一小碟水，感謝團隊在寒冷院子裡帶來溫暖。許露問司機的勇敢會不會寫進安全報告。檢查員說，勇敢無法取代堅固的護欄。門上舊標籤稱它為逃生出口，另一份文件卻使用不同定義。大家同意兩者都要調查。

A security chief said the door was only a storage entrance and had never served another purpose. Yuqing asked him to show the floor plan that supported his statement. He produced an image with a missing corner. The inspector refused to reach a conclusion on the spot. The team checked whether other routes were open, then asked the branch manager to provide a temporary escort for late workers. Yaochuan approved the escort but complained that every additional question seemed to create another expense.

保全主管說那只是倉庫入口，從未有其他用途。予晴請他拿出能證明此說法的平面圖，他提供的圖片卻少了一角。檢查員拒絕當場下結論。團隊先確認其他路線可通行，再要求分館經理暫時安排人員陪同夜班員工離開。曜川同意，卻抱怨每多問一個問題，就多出一筆成本。

In the waiting room someone had left a magazine featuring a sexy model in front of a hotel tower. Lu pushed it aside to make room for an employee who needed a short nap after an overnight shift. A radio drama described a murder mystery, but nobody mistook that fiction for evidence of a real crime. The employee said she feared she might starve for work if she complained openly. Yuqing thanked her, promised to protect her name, and wrote down the exact statement without making it more dramatic.

等候室有人留下一本雜誌，封面是位性感模特兒站在飯店高樓前。許露把雜誌移開，好讓剛上完大夜班的員工短暫小睡。收音機正播著謀殺推理劇，但沒有人把虛構故事當成真實犯罪的證據。員工說，她害怕公開申訴後會失去收入、挨餓度日。予晴向她致謝，答應保護姓名，並按原話記錄，沒有故意說得更戲劇化。

By sunset the inspection had revealed a practical risk but not a confirmed motive. The building inspector ordered the boxes cleared and requested complete plans. Lu offered to help communicate the temporary route to staff. Yuqing made sure the order came from the qualified inspector, not from a public relations office trying to look useful. Yaochuan arrived just as the last box was moved. He seemed ready to give a speech. Instead he carried a heavy barrier into place with the other workers and said nothing for several minutes.

日落前，檢查確認了實際風險，卻尚未確定動機。檢查員要求清開箱子並提供完整圖面。許露願意協助將臨時路線通知員工。予晴確定命令出自合格檢查員，而不是想表現的公關室。最後一個箱子移開時，曜川剛好到場。他似乎準備發表一番話，卻和員工一起把沉重護欄搬到定位，幾分鐘都沒說話。

The driver was thankful that the team had listened, but he asked for a second kind of help. The delivery route passed the alley every morning, and he wanted to know whom to contact if the boxes returned. The inspector wrote a number on the temporary barrier, and the branch manager agreed to include the route in the weekly checks. Yuqing added the driver's question to the report because it measured whether the fix could survive an ordinary shift. Yaochuan offered to send a gift basket. The driver smiled and said he would rather have a clear road. Yaochuan put away his phone and helped the inspector attach the notice at a height where drivers could actually read it.

司機很感謝團隊傾聽，卻提出另一種協助需求。送貨路線每天早晨都會經過那條巷子，他想知道箱子若再次堆回去該聯絡誰。檢查員在臨時護欄上寫下電話，分館經理答應把路線納入每週檢查。予晴將司機的問題寫進報告，因為它能衡量改善是否經得起平日班次。曜川提出送禮籃，司機笑說更希望道路暢通。曜川收起手機，幫檢查員把公告貼在司機真正看得見的位置。

“Was that enough?” he asked when they returned to the car park. Yuqing shook her head. “It was necessary. Enough would mean the route stays safe after we leave.” He made a note on his phone rather than defending himself. Later he forwarded the inspector's written order to every branch manager. Yuqing copied the document into the investigation folder. The locked door remained a question, but it was no longer hidden behind boxes. That small change mattered more than a polished sentence about responsibility.

回到停車場時，他問：「這樣夠了嗎？」予晴搖頭。「這是必要的。要說夠了，還得我們離開後路線仍然安全。」他沒有辯解，而是在手機上記下。稍後，他把檢查員的書面命令轉寄給所有分館經理。予晴將文件存入調查資料夾。被鎖住的門仍是疑問，卻不再藏在箱子後方。這個小改變比漂亮的責任宣言更重要。
