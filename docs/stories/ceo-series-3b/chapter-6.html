稽核室

The Audit Room

Yuqing chose a neutral conference room for the outside payroll review. The auditors were not guests to entertain; they needed reliable records, quiet desks, and permission to question managers. Yaochuan offered his own office, but she rejected it because staff might feel watched there. He studied her for a moment and then gave the team a smaller room with separate access. “I assume you have more conditions,” he said. “Yes,” she replied, “and you will receive them in writing.”

予晴替外部薪資審查選了一間中立會議室。稽核員不是需要招待的貴客，他們需要可靠紀錄、安靜桌面，以及詢問主管的權限。曜川提供自己的辦公室，她卻拒絕，因為員工可能因此感覺受到監視。他看了她片刻，改給團隊一間獨立出入的小會議室。「我猜你還有更多條件。」他說。「有，」她回答，「你會收到書面版本。」

The audit began with a summary of practical concerns. One teenage worker had reported a tropical rash after cleaning a warm storage area, where bacteria could multiply near old pipes. A box of stale snacks stood beside a heater coated with wax. An employee said the smell hurt her stomach. Another described a bump on the stairs that had never been repaired. “We cannot call the worker the creator of every problem,” Yuqing said. “The question is whether a basic necessity was provided, and whether management responded when warned.”

稽核從一份實務疑慮摘要開始。一位青少年員工曾通報清理溫熱倉庫後出現熱帶型皮疹，因為老水管旁可能滋生細菌。一盒不新鮮的點心放在覆著蠟的暖爐旁。一名員工說氣味讓她胃部不舒服；另一人描述階梯上始終沒修好的凸起處。「不能把每個問題都算成員工造成的，」予晴說，「重點是基本需求有沒有被提供，管理層收到警告後做了什麼。」

The lead auditor, Ms. Chen, explained how she would separate payroll errors from complaints about conditions. A worker might be paid correctly and still feel unsafe. Another might enjoy the job and still be owed overtime. A manager asked if publishing those distinctions would harm the brand. Chen answered that the audit was designed to establish facts, not to write advertising copy. Yaochuan did not interrupt. He took notes in the back row, where he could hear without controlling the meeting.

主稽核員陳女士說明，她會把薪資錯誤與工作環境申訴分開處理。員工即使薪資正確，也可能感到不安全；喜歡工作的人也可能仍被欠加班費。一名主管問，公開區分這些情況會不會傷害品牌。陳女士說，稽核目的是確定事實，不是寫廣告文案。曜川沒有打斷，他坐在後排記筆記，能聽見大家說話，卻不控制會議。

A politician had offered to tour the hotel and praise its record of excellence. The legal team rejected the visit during the investigation. “Anyhow, we need the inner access logs before we invite a camera,” Chen said. The IT officer explained a technique for checking whether files had been altered, and the security adviser made an awkward comparison to a missile launch: once a message spread, recalling it was difficult. Yuqing disliked the dramatic image but agreed with its warning. One suspect action in a log was not yet evidence against a person.

一位政治人物提議參觀飯店，稱讚集團的卓越紀錄。法務團隊拒絕在調查期間安排訪問。「總之，在邀請攝影機以前，我們需要內部存取紀錄。」陳女士說。資訊主管說明檢查檔案是否遭更動的技術，保全顧問則尷尬地把訊息擴散比喻成飛彈發射：一旦送出去就難以收回。予晴不喜歡誇張比喻，但同意其中警告。紀錄裡的一項可疑動作，還不能當成指控個人的證據。

Yuqing asked each department to identify what it could provide and what required another authorization. She refused a plan that would collect every employee's private messages merely to save time. Instead the team sought only records connected to the disputed shifts, with independent oversight. The auditor made sure workers could submit corrections privately. Lu offered to explain the system to colleagues in simple language. For the first time, the company had a process in which a cleaner did not have to face a director alone.

予晴請每個部門列出可提供的資料，以及需要額外授權的項目。她拒絕為省時間而收集所有員工私人訊息的提案。團隊改為只取得與爭議班次有關的紀錄，並由獨立人員監督。稽核員確保員工可私下提出更正，許露則願意用簡單語言向同事解釋流程。公司第一次有了一套制度，讓清潔員不必獨自面對主管。

The auditors also listed the resource limits of the review. They could examine relevant accounts and interview willing workers, but they could not read every private conversation in the company. A director complained that the result would be incomplete. Chen replied that a narrow, lawful inquiry with clear limits was better than an unlimited search that damaged trust. Yuqing asked for the limits to appear on the first page of every public summary. Yaochuan signed the procedure, then asked a practical question: how would the team know whether the new method actually worked? Chen proposed checking a small sample after the first payments. That answer satisfied Yuqing because it treated an improvement as something to measure, not to announce.

稽核員也列出查核資源的限制。他們能檢查相關帳目、訪談願意受訪的員工，卻不能閱讀全公司每段私人談話。一名董事抱怨結果不完整。陳女士回答，範圍清楚、符合法規的查核，比損害信任的無限制搜索更好。予晴要求每份公開摘要第一頁都列出限制。曜川簽署程序後問了實際問題：團隊如何知道新方法真的有效？陳女士建議首批付款後抽樣複查。予晴滿意，因為這把改善當成要測量的事，而不是只要宣布的事。

At dinner Yaochuan asked why Yuqing never accepted an easier solution. She set down her chopsticks. “An easy solution for the person with power may be expensive for everyone else,” she said. He wanted to challenge her, but the day's reports lay between them. He finally asked what he could do without interfering. “Give the auditors access, keep the workers paid, and let the findings surprise you.” He nodded. She paid for her own dinner, and the small gesture mattered to both of them.

晚餐時曜川問，為什麼予晴從不接受比較容易的解法。她放下筷子說：「對有權力的人而言容易的解法，可能讓其他所有人付出昂貴代價。」他本想反駁，但當天的報告就在兩人之間。他最後問，自己能做什麼而不干涉。「給稽核員資料權限、照常付員工薪資，並容許結果出乎你的意料。」他點頭。這餐她自己付錢，這個小舉動對兩人都有意義。
