螢幕上的聲明

The Statement on the Screen

總裁，請先撤回那則聲明 · 第 1 / 25 章 · 2000–3000 · 英中對照 · 校訂中

用互動版查詞、朗讀與收藏

Tang Yuqing stepped into the Starcrest Hotel ballroom ten minutes before the annual ceremony. She had been hired as an independent communications adviser, not as an employee. On a wall of screens the hotel group displayed a perfect picture of its service. At the same moment, a short video of a crying housekeeper appeared on hundreds of phones. The woman seemed to say that she would quit because guests had been treated better than the people who cleaned their rooms.

唐予晴在年度典禮開始前十分鐘走進曜星飯店的宴會廳。她受聘為獨立公關顧問，而非集團員工。牆上的螢幕展示集團完美服務的形象；同一時間，一段女房務員哭泣的短片出現在數百支手機上。影片中的她似乎說，自己要離職，因為飯店對客人的待遇比對打掃房間的人還好。

A berry drink stood beside the program for a circus-themed charity night. The printed invitation promised a fairy tale, a magician, live jazz, and a fancy dinner. A model jet hung above the stage; a small painted hut represented the group's first hotel. Beside the invitation lay an old deed, copied for the history display, describing who had once owned the building. Yuqing noticed that nobody was looking at the decorations now. The guests were watching the video and asking whether it was real.

一杯莓果飲料放在馬戲團主題慈善晚會的節目單旁。印好的邀請函承諾童話、魔術師、現場爵士樂與華麗晚宴。舞台上方懸著噴射機模型，一間彩繪小屋代表集團最初的飯店。旁邊還有為歷史展複製的舊產權契據，記錄建築物過去的主人。予晴發現此刻沒有人再看裝飾，賓客都盯著影片，問那究竟是不是真的。

Shen Yaochuan, the thirty-five-year-old chief executive, approached without wasting a greeting. His dark suit was flawless and his expression gave nothing away. “Write a denial,” he said. “Send it before the guests leave.” Yuqing looked at the text he had prepared. It claimed that the video was false, that every worker was satisfied, and that the group had never ignored a complaint. “Which of those claims can you prove?” she asked. He stared at her, plainly unused to the question.

三十五歲的執行長沈曜川走過來，連寒暄都省了。他的深色西裝一絲不苟，表情卻看不出情緒。「寫一份否認聲明，」他說，「客人離開前發出去。」予晴看了他備好的文字，裡面宣稱影片虛假、所有員工都滿意，而且集團從未忽略任何申訴。「這些話你能證明哪幾句？」她問。他望著她，顯然不習慣被這樣反問。

A technician tried to repair a broken heater behind the stage. One loose screw made the ventilation panel shake, while a red signal on the control board continued to flash. The event manager ordered the dancers to spin across the platform as if nothing had happened. Yuqing stopped the music until the problem was checked. “A miracle would be lovely,” she said, “but safety isn't a miracle.” A director warned that a political rival might use the video to rob them of the contract. Yaochuan said he could tame the crisis with various connections.

技師正在舞台後方修理壞掉的暖爐。一顆鬆動的螺絲讓通風板震動，控制板上的紅色訊號仍持續閃爍。活動經理卻命令舞者照常在台上旋轉，彷彿什麼都沒發生。予晴要求暫停音樂，先檢查問題。「有奇蹟當然很好，」她說，「但安全不能靠奇蹟。」一名董事警告政敵可能利用影片奪走合約。曜川則說，他能靠各種人脈平息危機。

Yuqing moved the discussion into a quiet meeting room and wrote two columns on a whiteboard: what the company knew and what it merely believed. The video was short, its original source unknown. The housekeeper's name was not on the guest list, and the manager who knew her was absent. Yuqing recommended a temporary statement that acknowledged the complaint and promised to preserve evidence. Yaochuan's jaw tightened when he saw how little it said. Yet he did not cross out a single word.

予晴把討論移到安靜的會議室，在白板上寫下兩欄：公司已知的事，以及只是相信的事。影片很短，原始來源不明。房務員的名字不在賓客名單上，認識她的主管也不在場。予晴建議發表暫時聲明，承認收到申訴並承諾保存證據。曜川看到那麼簡短的內容時，下顎繃緊，卻沒有劃掉任何一個字。

When the public relations team prepared the post, Yuqing asked the legal adviser to preserve both drafts with their dates. The first draft was a useful record of what pressure could make people claim without proof. She also asked staff not to identify the housekeeper publicly. “A person is not our advertisement,” she said. In the ballroom the music started again only after the technician signed the inspection sheet. Yaochuan watched her work, then asked whether she always refused orders this quickly. “Only the ones that require me to lie,” she answered.

公關團隊準備發文時，予晴請法務顧問保存兩個版本及日期。第一份草稿能記錄人受壓時會如何在缺乏證據下做出宣稱。她也要求員工不要公開那名房務員的身分。「人不是我們的廣告。」她說。宴會廳裡，技師簽完檢查單後，音樂才重新響起。曜川看著她工作，問她是不是一向這麼快就拒絕命令。「只有當命令要我說謊時。」她回答。

The assistant found a nearly full pint of juice on the meeting table and asked if it should be cleared away. Yuqing nodded, then stopped her to ask whether the servers had been told about the delayed ceremony. They had not. The announcement on the screen had occupied everyone, including the people whose evening work was about to become longer. She added a note about overtime and transport to the event report. When Yaochuan saw it, he first asked why a crisis adviser cared about the staff dinner. She answered that a reputation crisis began with many small choices, not only with a famous headline. He watched a server carry the tray away and said he would arrange a late bus. Yuqing asked him to verify the route with workers before announcing it.

助理發現會議桌上還有將近一品脫果汁，問要不要收掉。予晴點頭，隨即停下來問服務人員是否已經知道典禮延遲。他們不知道。螢幕上的公告佔據了所有人注意力，包括今晚工作會因此延長的人。她在活動報告加上加班和交通的提醒。曜川看見後，先問為何危機顧問要管員工晚餐。她回答，名譽危機起於許多小選擇，不只是知名標題。他看著服務生端走托盤，說會安排晚班車。予晴請他在公告前先向員工確認路線。

The first public message went out at midnight. It did not clear anyone's name, and it did not promise that the investigation would be painless. Yuqing sent a copy to the worker representative and gave her a private contact number. Yaochuan offered to have a driver take Yuqing home. She declined and booked her own ride, keeping the relationship professional. As the car pulled away, she saw a second video arrive on her phone. This one began three seconds before the first, with the housekeeper speaking in a steady voice.

午夜時，第一份公開訊息發出去了。它沒有替任何人洗清責任，也沒有保證調查不會讓人難受。予晴把副本送給員工代表，留下私人聯絡方式。曜川提出派司機送她回家，她婉拒並自行叫車，維持專業關係。車子駛離時，她收到第二段影片。這一段比第一段早了三秒開始，那名房務員的聲音平穩而清楚。
