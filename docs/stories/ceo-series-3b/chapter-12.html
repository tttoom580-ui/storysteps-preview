停電測試

The Blackout Test

A scheduled electricity test at the hill branch revealed another gap between a plan and real life. The hotel's monthly safety report claimed that all guest elevators and service routes had been checked. During the test, an elderly visitor became confused at a dark corridor, and a worker could not find the correct information board. Nobody was harmed, but Yuqing insisted that the event be recorded as a warning. Yaochuan came to observe, not to command the technical team.

山丘分館預定的停電測試，再次暴露計畫與現實的落差。飯店每月安全報告宣稱所有客梯與服務通道都已檢查。測試期間，一名年長訪客在昏暗走廊迷失方向，一位員工也找不到正確的資訊告示。沒有人受傷，但予晴堅持把事件記成警訊。曜川到場觀察，沒有指揮技術團隊。

A captain from the river rescue service had agreed to watch the test as a client adviser. He brought a map of a nearby canyon and described how a flash of light could mislead a traveler in fog. Near a service hatch, a bubble formed beneath a damaged floor covering. The electrician asked everyone to stand aside while she checked the wiring. Yuqing saw that the capable technician did not need a sword or a heroic speech. She needed room to work, reliable property records, and permission to stop the exercise.

河道救援隊的一位隊長受邀以客戶顧問身分觀察測試。他帶來附近峽谷地圖，說明霧中一道閃光可能如何誤導旅人。服務艙口附近，受損地板覆材下鼓起一個氣泡。電工請大家站到一旁，讓她檢查線路。予晴發現這位能幹技師不需要寶劍或英雄演說，只需要工作空間、可靠的產權紀錄，以及停止測試的權限。

The branch manager tried to edit the old report before the review team received it. Chen stopped him, preserved the original version, and documented the attempted change. He said he only wanted the document to represent the improved situation. Yuqing replied that revised conditions belonged in a new entry with a date. A record of the past was not a decoration to change whenever the present became embarrassing. Yaochuan ordered a pause in testing until the written steps matched the actual building.

分館經理想在審查團隊收到舊報告前修改它。陳女士制止，保存原版，並記錄那次修改企圖。經理說自己只希望文件呈現改善後的情況。予晴回答，新的情況應寫在附日期的新紀錄裡。過去的紀錄不是能因現在尷尬就隨意更換的裝飾。曜川要求暫停測試，直到書面步驟符合建築現況。

In the staff room someone cooked a hopeful meal with extra spice, and a worker began to weep when she heard that the test had stopped. She feared that another closure might cost her wages. Yuqing explained the situation privately and brought a payroll officer to answer practical questions. “The building's safety and your income are both real needs,” she said. “We cannot solve one by pretending the other does not exist.” The company arranged paid training while the technicians completed the repairs.

員工休息室有人用額外香料煮了頓充滿希望的餐食，一名員工聽見測試停止時卻哭了，擔心再次關閉會讓她少領薪資。予晴私下解釋情況，帶來薪資主管回答實際問題。「建築安全和妳的收入都是真實需求，」她說，「不能假裝另一個不存在來解決其中一個。」公司安排有薪訓練，讓技師能完成修復。

Yuqing wrote a short briefing for guests, using plain directions rather than technical excuses. It stated which routes were open, where assistance was available, and when the next update would come. She avoided claiming that the entire property was unsafe; that would have gone beyond the evidence. She also avoided claiming it was perfect. Yaochuan watched her choose every word. He said he had once thought strong leadership meant sounding certain. Now he saw how much care was required to tell people exactly what was known.

予晴為客人寫了一份簡短說明，以清楚路線代替技術藉口。內容說明哪些通道開放、去哪裡求助，以及下次更新時間。她沒有宣稱整棟建築都不安全，因為那超出證據；也沒有說它完美。曜川看著她斟酌每個字，說自己以前以為強勢領導就是聽起來很肯定，現在才看見準確告知已知事實需要多少用心。

When the technicians returned, they found that the warning board had been placed behind a decorative screen. The information was accurate but useless to anyone who could not see it. Yuqing asked the team to move the board into the public line of sight and test it with visitors who had never used the building before. One guest said the arrows were confusing; another said the words were too small. The designer listened without defending her first draft. Yaochuan asked why a simple sign required so many revisions. Yuqing told him that the goal was not to prove the designer was clever. It was to help a frightened person find a safe path when the lights failed.

技師回來時發現警示板被裝飾屏風擋住。資訊雖然正確，卻無法幫助看不到的人。予晴請團隊把板子移到公眾視線內，讓從未到過建築的訪客測試。一位客人說箭頭令人困惑，另一人說字太小。設計師沒有替第一版辯解，而是聆聽意見。曜川問簡單標誌為什麼需要這麼多次修改。予晴回答，目標不是證明設計師聰明，而是在停電時幫助害怕的人找到安全路線。

After the test they stood on a balcony overlooking the dark hills. Yaochuan told her that his father had taught him to hide doubts from employees. Yuqing said she understood the fear of looking weak, but hiding a doubt often made everyone weaker. He asked if she had ever wanted to quit this project. “Often,” she answered. “I stay because the people involved deserve a proper ending, not because I need you to admire me.” He thanked her and did not ask for more.

測試後，兩人站在俯瞰暗色山丘的陽台上。曜川說父親教他不要讓員工看見自己的懷疑。予晴理解他害怕顯得軟弱，但隱瞞疑慮常使每個人更脆弱。他問她是否曾想退出這個案子。「常常，」她回答，「我留下是因為相關的人值得一個妥善結局，不是因為需要你欣賞我。」他向她道謝，沒有再追問。
