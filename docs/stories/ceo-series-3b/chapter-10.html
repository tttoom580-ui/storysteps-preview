沒有腳本的晚宴

The Dinner without a Script

The group had planned a grand dinner to reassure investors. Yaochuan wanted to cancel it, afraid that any celebration would look cruel while workers were waiting for answers. Yuqing advised him not to treat every guest as a public relations problem. The dinner could continue if the company stopped presenting it as proof that everything was fine. He agreed to remove a promotional film and replace it with a quiet notice explaining the ongoing review. For once he did not ask her to write a speech praising him.

集團原本計畫一場盛大晚宴安撫投資人。曜川想取消，擔心員工還在等待答案時，任何慶祝都顯得冷酷。予晴建議他不要把每位客人都當成公關問題。如果公司不再把晚宴當作一切正常的證明，活動仍可進行。他同意撤下宣傳影片，改放一則平實公告，說明查核仍在進行。這一次，他沒有要求予晴寫一篇稱讚他的演說。

At one table a bride wore a fashionable silk dress with a pearl pin. A server handed her a handkerchief when an open stove sent smoke near the curtains. The safety team checked for flame and found none. The chef offered melon and warm soup as a small relief. A guest made a bold joke about needing an angel to rescue the evening, then tried to punch a balloon for amusement. Yuqing told him to stop before he frightened the staff. The incident made her stare at the gap between a luxury image and ordinary care.

一桌賓客中有位新娘，穿著時髦絲質禮服，別著珍珠胸針。開放式爐具的煙飄近窗簾時，服務生遞給她手帕。安全團隊檢查是否有火焰，確認沒有。主廚端來甜瓜和熱湯稍作安撫。有位客人大膽開玩笑說需要天使救場，還想打破氣球取樂。予晴制止他，免得嚇到員工。這段插曲讓她盯著奢華形象與日常照顧之間的距離。

Yaochuan sat beside Yuqing after the service team had finished its work. He asked whether she believed he could change. She looked at the table instead of his face. “I can observe decisions,” she said. “I cannot promise to believe a person merely because he wants me to.” He accepted the answer but asked what she had noticed. She listed the preserved records, the independent audit, and the fact that Mei had not been forced on camera. “Those are beginnings,” she added. He nodded, understanding that he had not been given a romantic reward.

服務團隊完成工作後，曜川坐到予晴旁邊，問她相不相信自己能改變。她看著桌面而不是他的臉。「我能觀察決定，」她說，「不能因為一個人想被相信就答應相信他。」他接受答案，又問她注意到了什麼。她列出保存紀錄、獨立查核，以及方梅沒有被迫出鏡。「這些只是開始。」她補充。他點頭，明白這不是浪漫的獎賞。

An optimistic guest suggested that a fine dinner would somehow impress the public. Yuqing explained that a moral duty did not disappear under a beautiful structure or a perfect tablecloth. The tide outside the window was rising, and the building's old sea wall still needed a separate inspection. A manager asked her to determine which warning should appear on the event screen. She chose the one visitors could act on: where to find help if the weather changed. The rest belonged in the maintenance report, not in a fairy tale about the hotel.

一位樂觀賓客說，精緻晚宴總會以某種方式打動大眾。予晴解釋，道德責任不會因美麗建築或完美桌巾而消失。窗外潮水正在上漲，建築的舊海堤還需要另外檢查。一名經理請她決定活動螢幕上該顯示哪項警告。她選擇客人能採取行動的資訊：天氣變化時去哪裡求助。其他內容屬於維修報告，而不是關於飯店的童話故事。

After the meal, Yaochuan walked her to the entrance without touching her or trying to extend the evening. He said he had begun reading the raw complaints at night. “It is uncomfortable,” he admitted. “Good,” she answered, then softened her voice. “Uncomfortable can be useful when it leads to work.” He asked her to send a list of the problems he personally had the power to correct. She promised a list, not forgiveness. They parted under the lobby lights with a new kind of respect between them.

飯後曜川陪她走到門口，沒有碰她，也沒試圖拖長夜晚。他說自己開始在夜裡閱讀原始申訴。「很不舒服。」他承認。「很好。」她回答，然後放柔聲音。「不舒服若能帶來實際工作，就有用。」他請她列出自己有權親自改正的問題。她答應列清單，沒有答應原諒。兩人在大廳燈光下道別，彼此之間多了一種新的尊重。

Later the chef invited Yuqing and Yaochuan to the empty kitchen for a practical demonstration. He showed how a service team prepared a meal when a guest had an allergy, from the first note to the final plate. Every worker needed to know which instruction belonged to them and when to ask for help. Yuqing observed that the sequence resembled a responsible correction notice: identify the concern, check the facts, assign action, and confirm what changed. Yaochuan asked whether any step could be skipped if a guest was impatient. The chef shook his head. “Speed matters,” he said, “but so does the person who must live with the result.” The chief executive carried that sentence into the next morning's meeting.

後來主廚邀予晴與曜川到空廚房，看一場實務示範。他展示當客人有過敏問題時，服務團隊從第一張備註到最後一道餐點如何準備。每位員工都必須知道自己的指令，以及何時求助。予晴發現順序很像負責任的更正通知：指出問題、核對事實、分配行動、確認改變。曜川問客人沒耐心時能否跳過步驟。主廚搖頭。「速度重要，」他說，「但要承受結果的人也重要。」總裁把這句話帶進隔天晨會。

Yuqing arrived home to find her sister waiting with noodles and questions. She told her about the quiet dinner but did not share anyone's confidential testimony. Her sister laughed and asked if the famous chief executive was handsome. “That is the least difficult question about him,” Yuqing said. She slept poorly, thinking of the gap between a man who could learn and the position that allowed him to cause harm without meaning to. By morning she knew what mattered: no private feeling would change her written findings.

予晴回到家，妹妹帶著麵等她，也帶著一堆問題。她說了安靜晚宴的事，沒有透露任何保密證詞。妹妹笑著問那位知名總裁帥不帥。「那是關於他最不難回答的問題。」予晴說。那夜她睡得不好，想著一個願意學習的人，和一個能在無意間因權力傷害他人的職位之間的距離。天亮時她確定最重要的事：私人感情不會改變她的書面結論。
