The Numbers that Moved

On the third day, the records manager opened a copy of the customer-response database. The summary promised that ninety-eight percent of concerns had been resolved within a week. Yuqing asked to see a sample of the original messages. When the manager compared the files, he found that some workers' concerns had been renamed “general suggestions” without their knowledge. The percentage depended on those names. Changing the label had changed the apparent result without changing a single person's experience.

第三天，檔案管理員開啟顧客回應資料庫副本。摘要宣稱九成八的問題都在一週內解決。予晴要求查看原始訊息的樣本。管理員比對後發現，有些員工的申訴未經當事人知情，就被改名為「一般建議」。百分比取決於這些分類名稱。改掉標籤，表面成果就變了，但沒有任何人的實際遭遇因此改變。

One file described a downtown restaurant with bamboo chairs and a pigeon that returned to its window each morning. The owner had asked the hotel to buy meals for workers in poverty, not to advertise its kindness. A dealer offered the hotel cheap food in a bundle, but an unusual chip on the payment card caused concern. In the background of their chat, a civil officer discussed whether the grant rules were democratic. The conversation was ordinary; the database made it sound like a complaint about a single doughnut.

一份檔案提到市中心餐館，裡面有竹椅，窗邊每天早上都會來一隻鴿子。店主請飯店替經濟困難的員工購買餐點，不是要宣傳自己的善意。一位供應商提供便宜的餐食組合，但支付卡上的異常晶片問題令人擔心。聊天背景裡，一名公務員正在討論補助規則是否民主。那本是平常對話，資料庫卻把它變成對單一甜甜圈的抱怨。

Yaochuan insisted that the marketing director had approved the figures in good faith. Yuqing asked for the definition of “resolved.” Nobody in the room could produce it. A junior analyst said the definition had changed during a software update. Yuqing thanked him in front of everyone, then asked that he not be blamed for explaining his work. Yaochuan gave the analyst permission to retain an independent copy. The decision cost him an easy story, but it gave the team something better: a trace they could test.

曜川堅稱行銷主管當初善意核准了數字。予晴要求看「已解決」的定義，房間裡卻沒有人找得出來。一名初階分析師說，軟體更新時定義曾被更動。予晴當眾向他致謝，並要求不要因為他說明工作而責怪他。曜川允許分析師保留獨立副本。這個決定讓他失去容易對外說的故事，卻讓團隊得到更好的東西：可以檢驗的紀錄。

A local poll had shown a mighty leap in guest approval, yet the owner of the meal shop said real conditions had not improved. “For the sake of the staff, don't deepen their trouble with a false chart,” she told Yuqing. The company had invited several executives to dine there, but none had noticed that the substance of the request was still unresolved. Yuqing wondered how the board would react when it learned that a perfect score could be built from incomplete categories. She wrote the question plainly instead of softening it.

一份地方民調顯示賓客滿意度大幅躍升，但餐館老闆說實際情況沒有改善。「為了員工好，別用假的圖表加深他們的麻煩。」她告訴予晴。公司曾邀幾位主管到那裡用餐，卻沒有一人注意請求的實質內容仍未解決。予晴想知道董事會發現完美分數可能來自不完整分類時會如何反應。她沒有委婉修飾，而是直接寫下問題。

By late afternoon the team had counted three separate problems: a changed category, a missing approval record, and a public statement that relied on both. They could not yet say who had ordered the changes. Yuqing asked for an access review and written witness interviews. Yaochuan wanted to announce that the company had found the cause. “We have found a method, not a person,” she corrected. He looked at the clock, then signed the request for outside help. He was beginning to learn the cost of saying less than he wished.

下午稍晚，團隊歸納出三個不同問題：被改動的分類、缺失的核准紀錄，以及建立在兩者之上的公開聲明。他們尚不能說是誰下令修改。予晴要求檢查存取紀錄，並留下書面訪談。曜川想宣布公司已找出原因。「我們找到的是方法，不是人。」她更正。他看了看時鐘，最後簽字同意尋求外部協助。他開始學到：說得比自己想說的少，也是一種代價。

Yuqing asked the analyst to show how a word on a form became a number on a chart. He opened an example that began as a request for safer transport and ended as a note about general satisfaction. The change looked small on one screen, but it carried the request away from the people who could answer it. She asked him to record the transformation step by step. When Yaochuan entered, she did not point to a villain. She pointed to the system and asked who had approved its rules. Several departments shared responsibility, and their answers did not yet agree. Yuqing said the board could not repair a broken measurement by choosing the friendliest explanation. They needed a definition that workers and managers could both examine.

予晴請分析師示範表單上的一個詞如何變成圖表上的數字。他打開一筆例子：原本要求更安全的交通，最後卻變成一般滿意度備註。單看螢幕，變動很小，卻把請求帶離有能力回答的人。她請他逐步記錄轉換過程。曜川進來時，她沒有指著某個反派，而是指向系統，問誰核准規則。幾個部門都有責任，回答卻尚未一致。予晴說，董事會不能選最友善的說法就修好有問題的測量；他們需要員工與經理都能檢視的定義。

That night Yuqing sent Lu a short explanation of the audit, avoiding the names of workers who had not agreed to be identified. Lu responded with a photograph of a handwritten complaint dated two years earlier. Its case number matched one marked “resolved” in the database. The handwritten page ended with the sentence, “I never received an answer.” Yuqing placed both versions side by side. The next chapter of the scandal would not be written by the loudest executive, but by the difference between two quiet documents.

當晚予晴寄給許露一份簡短的稽核說明，避開未同意揭露身分的員工姓名。許露回傳一張兩年前手寫申訴的照片。申訴編號正好對應資料庫裡標為「已解決」的一筆紀錄。手寫頁面的最後一句是：「我從未收到答覆。」予晴把兩個版本並列。這場風波接下來的篇章，不會由聲音最大的主管書寫，而會由兩份安靜文件的差異決定。
