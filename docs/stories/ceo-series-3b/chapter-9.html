消失的一分鐘

The Missing Minute

The technical review found a minute missing from the saved staff meeting video. The IT team had copies of different lengths, but nobody could explain why. Yuqing asked them to preserve every version rather than select the one that seemed most helpful. The gap mattered because it covered a manager's response to Mei. Yaochuan wanted to identify the editor immediately. Yuqing reminded him that a missing minute could reflect several operations, not necessarily a deliberate attack.

技術查核發現員工會議影片少了一分鐘。資訊團隊保存著長短不同的版本，卻沒有人能解釋原因。予晴請他們保留全部版本，而不是挑最有利的一個。缺少的片段恰好涵蓋經理回答方梅的部分。曜川想立刻找出剪輯者，予晴提醒他，少掉一分鐘可能有多種原因，不一定是蓄意攻擊。

In a small cabin used for archived equipment, a horn sounded when a backup machine restarted. The sudden roar caused a technician to faint, but another worker helped him before he could bleed from a fall. A splash from a leaking pipe made the floor wet near the sink. The room held a weekly maintenance log, a database drive, and a box labeled “missing tapes.” The scene gave Yuqing fright, yet she refused to wander through the devices without a skillful technician. The tools were not a weapon to use in a personal fight.

在一間存放舊設備的小機房裡，備份機器重新啟動時喇叭發出聲響。突然的轟鳴讓一名技師昏倒，另一位員工及時扶住他，避免跌倒流血。漏水管濺出的水讓水槽附近地面濕滑。房裡存放每週保養紀錄、資料庫硬碟與一箱標著「遺失影帶」的物品。場面讓予晴害怕，但她拒絕在沒有熟練技師陪同下亂碰設備。這些工具不是私人爭鬥的武器。

The technician recovered a file showing that the cut had been made by an automatic export program. A separate log showed someone later chose the shortened version for the press folder. Both facts were important, and neither proved who had wanted the public clip to spread. Yuqing asked the auditors to question staff who had access. Yaochuan finally said, “I dislike uncertainty.” She replied, “Then help us keep it honest. An uncertain answer can still be responsible.” He wrote that sentence in his notebook without telling her.

技師復原的檔案顯示，最初的剪切由自動匯出程式造成。另一份紀錄顯示，後來有人選了短版放進新聞資料夾。兩項事實都重要，但都不能證明是誰想讓短片傳播。予晴請稽核員詢問有權限的員工。曜川終於說：「我討厭不確定。」她回答：「那就協助我們誠實面對它。不確定的答案，也可以是負責任的答案。」他沒告訴她，卻把這句話寫進筆記本。

At the guest lounge a band played a cheerful tune, and laughter drifted through the hall. A couple introduced each other as lover and partner, joking about how boring travel could be without surprises. A guest asked whether the hotel was practicing democracy by letting cleaners complain. Lu replied that workers needed a way to be heard, not a stage for entertainment. The conversation ended shortly after Yaochuan entered. He did not demand applause or an explanation for the silence.

賓客休息室的樂團演奏輕快曲調，笑聲飄過大廳。一對伴侶互稱戀人，開玩笑說旅行沒有意外就會很無聊。一位客人問，飯店讓清潔員申訴是否算實踐民主。許露回答，員工需要被聽見的管道，不是提供娛樂的舞台。曜川走進來後，談話很快結束。他沒有要求掌聲，也沒有逼大家解釋沉默。

Yuqing met Mei in a private room with Lu present. Mei confirmed that she had approved an internal recording for training, not a public campaign. The difference changed the question of permission. She wanted the company to correct its handling of staff footage and wages, but she still did not want her name used to promote the brand. Yuqing wrote down those wishes separately. When she reported them to Yaochuan, she watched whether he would try to negotiate away the second request. He did not.

予晴在許露陪同下於私人會議室見到方梅。方梅確認自己同意的是內部訓練錄影，不是公開宣傳。這個差異改變了同意範圍的問題。她希望公司改正員工影片使用方式及薪資，但仍不願讓自己的名字替品牌宣傳。予晴把兩項意願分開記下。向曜川報告時，她觀察他是否會試圖討價還價、取消第二項要求。他沒有。

An engineer said the backup procedure was designed to save space, not to bore anyone with duplicate files. Yuqing answered that a complete record could be boring and still be essential. She asked the team to document every automatic cut, including its date, purpose, and effect on the final video. The engineer agreed, relieved that nobody was demanding an impossible reconstruction of unclear sound. Yaochuan asked whether the review had found the person responsible for sharing the public clip. Chen said that question required separate interviews and access evidence. The answer frustrated him, but he accepted it. An inquiry should not name a person merely because the public wanted a name before midnight.

工程師說備份程序的設計是為了節省空間，不是用重複檔案讓大家無聊。予晴回答，完整紀錄即使無聊，也可能很重要。她請團隊記錄每次自動剪切的日期、目的及對最終影片的影響。工程師同意，慶幸沒有人要求他不可能地還原模糊聲音。曜川問審查是否找到了分享公開短片的人。陳女士說那需要另外訪談與存取證據。他雖然沮喪，仍接受答案。不能只因大眾想在午夜前知道名字，就隨便指認一個人。

Near midnight the IT team located the original sound track. It contained the missing manager's reply, but the words were too unclear to settle the dispute. The team saved it without pretending otherwise. Yuqing returned to her office and wrote a careful notice about what the evidence established. A message from Yaochuan arrived: “You were right to keep every version.” She was surprised by how much the simple sentence meant. It was not affection yet, but it was an admission without conditions, and she valued that.

午夜前，資訊團隊找到原始聲軌，裡面有缺失的經理回答，但聲音不清楚，無法解決爭議。團隊保存檔案，沒有假裝聽得懂。予晴回辦公室寫下一份謹慎的證據說明。曜川傳來訊息：「妳堅持保留每個版本是對的。」她意外發現，這句簡單的話對自己很重要。那還不是愛意，卻是沒有條件的承認，而她很珍惜。
