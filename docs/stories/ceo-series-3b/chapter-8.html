客人的私人來信

A Guest's Private Letter

總裁，請先撤回那則聲明 · 第 8 / 25 章 · 2000–3000 · 英中對照 · 校訂中

用互動版查詞、朗讀與收藏

The hotel received a complaint from a guest whose private letter had appeared on an advertising page. The letter described a difficult family visit and included details that could identify the writer. A marketing assistant had thought the note was inspiring and had passed it along without asking. Yuqing asked the company to remove the post immediately, keep a secure record of what had happened, and contact the writer through the original private channel. She would not turn the incident into another public apology without the person's permission.

飯店收到一位客人申訴：他的私人來信出現在廣告頁面上。信裡寫著艱難的家庭探訪，還包含能辨識作者身分的細節。行銷助理認為信很感人，未經詢問便轉交出去。予晴要求立即下架貼文、妥善保存事件紀錄，並透過原本的私人管道聯繫作者。未經本人同意，她不會把事件變成另一場公開道歉。

The advertising team had built a web page showing a wealthy tourist beside a buffalo statue. A child could click a controller and watch a cooker prepare crispy food. Someone had used the image of a bullet to describe a fast booking process; Yuqing called the choice careless. Other guests had lately complained about litter and an employee who would yell at anyone blocking the entrance. “A citizen does not owe us personal stories merely because we offer a room,” she said. The team stopped trying to grab more information than people had agreed to share.

廣告團隊做了一個網頁，畫面是富有遊客站在水牛雕像旁。孩子能按控制器，看炊具製作酥脆食物。有人用子彈圖案比喻快速訂房流程，予晴認為這個選擇很不謹慎。最近還有客人抱怨亂丟垃圾，以及一名員工會對擋住入口的人大吼。「市民不會因為我們提供房間，就欠我們私人故事。」她說。團隊不再試圖抓取超出同意範圍的資料。

The writer, an elderly man called Mr. He, did not want compensation in exchange for silence. He wanted his letter removed and an explanation of how it had been shared. Yuqing arranged a conversation with a privacy specialist, not with photographers. Yaochuan offered to attend, but Mr. He declined. The chief executive accepted the answer and sent a written apology without asking for the right to quote the man's reaction. Yuqing saw that his restraint was now becoming a habit rather than a single performance.

寫信者是一位何姓長者，他不想用沉默換取賠償。他要的是信件下架，以及資料如何外流的解釋。予晴安排隱私專家與他談話，沒有請攝影師。曜川提出參加，但何先生婉拒，總裁接受答覆，寄出書面道歉，也沒有要求引用老人的反應。予晴看見他的克制逐漸成為習慣，而不是單次表演。

A talkative director called the case a small shadow on a successful campaign. Yuqing answered that sexual details or other private information could not be used simply because a story attracted clicks. Every permission had a limit. She asked for a written request process so staff could report unauthorized posts and have them removed quickly. The director said this would slow marketing. Yaochuan asked which mattered more: a fast campaign or a guest who could trust what the hotel promised. No one expected him to ask it so calmly.

一名健談的董事把這件事形容為成功活動上的小陰影。予晴回答，不論涉及性方面或其他私人資訊，都不能因為故事吸引點閱就任意使用。每一份同意都有界線。她要求設立書面申請流程，讓員工能通報未經授權的貼文，並迅速下架。董事說這會拖慢行銷。曜川問，快速活動和客人對飯店承諾的信任，究竟哪個更重要。沒有人料到他會問得如此平靜。

At the end of the day the team reviewed every guest story used in the campaign. Several had valid consent; two did not. The invalid pages came down, and the record of their reach was preserved for the affected writers. Yuqing emphasized that deleting a post did not erase the fact that people had seen it. The company would have to explain the error and offer a path for correction. The work was slow and mostly invisible. That was why it mattered more than a glowing headline.

一天結束時，團隊複查活動用過的每篇客人故事。有幾篇取得有效同意，兩篇沒有。未授權頁面下架，觸及紀錄則保存給受影響作者。予晴強調，刪文不代表人們沒有看過。公司必須解釋錯誤並提供更正途徑。這份工作很慢，大多數人也看不見；正因如此，它比亮眼標題更重要。

At the end of the privacy review, an employee carried the remaining baggage from a storage cabinet to the records office. Some boxes contained handwritten letters; others held consent forms that had never been signed. Yuqing stopped the team from treating all old documents as one category. A message could be retained for a legitimate reason without becoming suitable for advertising. The privacy specialist made a list showing where each collection belonged and when it should be reviewed. Yaochuan studied the list, then asked whether the company could contact affected writers without making them repeat their stories. Yuqing said yes, through careful private notices that offered help rather than demanding attention. He approved the plan without asking whether it would trend online.

隱私檢查結束時，一名員工把儲藏櫃裡剩餘的行李與箱子送到檔案室。有些箱子裝著手寫信，另一些裝著從未簽名的同意書。予晴制止團隊把所有舊文件混為一類。訊息可能因合法理由保存，卻不因此適合廣告。隱私專家列出每組資料應放在哪裡、何時重新檢查。曜川看著清單，問能否不讓受影響作者重述經驗就聯絡他們。予晴說可以，使用謹慎的私人通知，提供協助而非索取關注。他批准計畫，沒有問是否會在網路上爆紅。

Yaochuan found Yuqing alone in the lobby after the meeting. He said he had spent years believing that public attention belonged to whoever could purchase it. “You make attention look like a debt,” he observed. “Sometimes it is,” she said. “If someone gives you trust, you owe them care.” He hesitated, then asked whether she would join him for coffee on Saturday, outside working hours. She answered that she would consider it after the review and after she knew what boundaries their different positions would require.

會後曜川在大廳看見獨自站著的予晴。他說自己多年來都相信，誰有錢購買曝光，注意力就屬於誰。「妳讓注意力看起來像一筆債。」他說。「有時的確如此，」她回答，「人把信任交給你，你就欠他一份照顧。」他猶豫一下，邀她星期六在工作時間外喝咖啡。她說會等審查更清楚，也想明白兩人不同職位需要哪些界線後，再考慮。
