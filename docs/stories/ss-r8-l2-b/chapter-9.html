A Missing Page

燈熄之後，星星仍在 · 第 9 / 25 章 · 1000–2000 · 英中對照 · 校訂中

用互動版查詞、朗讀與收藏

In addition to the safety report, Mira began a new chapter in the team's daily record: the history of the dome. She found an article in the university magazine about its first opening and a historical painting of the night sky above the campus. The article named three people who had built the original show, but it did not say who had written the text. Mira felt fear that the missing author would be forgotten again. “If we use it,” she told Kai, “we must find out whether we have permission.” Kai agreed. They did not need the old script to succeed; their new show was already their own. Yet learning the true story mattered. It would be worse to erase someone's name simply because the old papers were hard to read.

除了安全報告，米拉也在團隊每日紀錄裡開了新章節，介紹圓頂的歷史。她在校刊找到早年開館的文章，也找到一幅描繪校園夜空的歷史畫作。文章列出三位打造原始演出的人，卻沒說旁白是誰寫的。米拉害怕那位被忽略的作者再度被忘記。「如果要用這些素材，」她對凱說，「必須先弄清楚有沒有使用許可。」凱同意。他們不必依賴舊劇本才能成功；新演出已是自己的作品。但知道真實歷史仍重要。不能因為舊資料難讀，就把某個人的名字抹掉。

In the storage room, Jo drew a map with a red crayon and two blue pencils so volunteers could see which shelf held each object. A picture frame hung from a nail near the door, and an old puppy poster covered part of the wall. Beneath the poster they found a list of names and dates. Jo did not pull it away without asking the shopkeeper who managed the small campus supply store next door. The man explained that the poster had been there for years and belonged to the theater department. He helped them lift it carefully. The names on the list matched the magazine article, except for one missing partner. Mira took a photograph for the school archive.

倉庫裡，喬用一支紅色蠟筆和兩支藍色鉛筆畫了地圖，讓志工知道每個架子放了什麼。門邊有個畫框掛在釘子上，牆面的一部分被小狗海報蓋住。海報底下露出一份姓名和日期清單。喬沒有直接扯下，而是先問隔壁小型校園用品店的店主；店主說海報屬於劇場系，已掛了好多年，便幫忙小心抬起。清單上的名字和校刊一致，卻多了文章沒提到的一位合作夥伴。米拉為校史檔案拍下照片。

The missing name belonged to a student illustrator who had made the earliest star maps. Mira could not find a legal notice showing that the images were available for public use. She sent a careful question to the archive office instead of taking the pictures. Kai offered to make a new set of maps with Jo. “That way our show can shine without borrowing another person's work without permission,” he said. They would still name the old artist in the history section, where the archive allowed factual description. Mira found the balance fair. Admiration did not give them ownership, but it could lead them to create something honest.

多出的名字是一位替早期星圖作畫的學生插畫家。米拉找不到能證明圖片可以公開使用的合法授權說明，便向校史室詢問，而不是直接拿去放映。凱提議和喬重新繪製一套星圖。「這樣我們的演出就能發光，不必未經同意借用他人的作品，」他說。至於舊藝術家的名字，仍可依校史室允許的方式，在歷史介紹中說明事實。米拉覺得很公平。欣賞並不代表擁有，但欣賞能帶來誠實的創作。

To keep the new work organized, Mira divided the map into a simple section for children and a detailed section for older visitors. Jo used paper paste on a test model, while Kai checked the drying time. They did not want wet paper near the electrical equipment. A volunteer asked whether the system had too many rules. Mira answered that a rule should solve a real problem. Unless it solved a real problem, the team could change it. She showed the volunteer their list of mistakes and improvements. The record did not make them look perfect. It showed how they had learned. For the first time, Mira felt proud of a messy notebook.

為了讓新作品好整理，米拉把星圖分成適合兒童的簡易區和供年長訪客看的詳細區。喬在測試模型上使用紙漿糊，凱確認乾燥時間。他們不想把潮濕紙張放在電器附近。一位志工問這套制度會不會規則太多。米拉回答，規則應該解決真實問題；除非規則真的能解決問題，否則團隊就能修改。她把錯誤及改進清單給志工看。紀錄沒有讓他們看起來完美，卻顯示一路怎麼學習。米拉第一次為一本亂糟糟的筆記本感到驕傲。

That afternoon, Professor Hsu confirmed that the missing illustrator was still alive and could be contacted through the alumni office. Mira asked whether they should write immediately. He suggested a short, polite note, with no pressure to respond. Kai said he could help with the wording but wanted Mira to decide what to ask. “You're the project lead,” he reminded her. “I'm your partner, not your voice.” She liked that distinction. She wrote that they hoped to tell the dome's history accurately and would not use the pictures without permission. She also asked whether the illustrator wanted to visit, making clear that saying no was welcome.

下午徐教授確認那位插畫家仍在世，校友會可以轉達訊息。米拉問是否該立刻寫信。教授建議寫封簡短禮貌的信，不給對方回覆壓力。凱表示可以幫忙措辭，但問題應由米拉決定。「妳是計畫負責人，」他提醒她，「我是妳的夥伴，不是替妳發聲的人。」她喜歡這個區分，也終於能說出自己好幾週來的恐懼：圓頂舊日的美感可能會消失。信裡寫明他們希望正確介紹圓頂歷史，沒有許可不會使用圖片，也詢問插畫家願不願意到訪，同時明說拒絕完全沒關係。

When they finished, Kai asked Mira about their planned walk by the sea. She had forgotten the date in the middle of the archive work. He did not blame her. Instead he asked if she still wanted to go. She did. They moved it to the next free afternoon and entered the new time on the calendar. Mira knew that a growing project would always demand her attention, but she did not want it to take every hour of her life. A friendship could become important without becoming another task on a list. She told Kai that she was learning this slowly. He said he was learning too.

寫完後凱問起原本約好的海邊散步。米拉忙著處理檔案，竟忘了日期。他沒有責怪，只問她還想不想去。她當然想。他們改約下一個空閒下午，把新時間寫進行事曆。米拉知道計畫愈做愈大，總會占用注意力，但她不希望它奪走人生全部時間。友誼可以變得重要，卻不必成為清單上的另一項任務。她告訴凱自己正在慢慢學，他說他也是。
