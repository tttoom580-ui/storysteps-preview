屋頂測試

The Roof Test

燈熄之後，星星仍在 · 第 12 / 25 章 · 1000–2000 · 英中對照 · 校訂中

用互動版查詞、朗讀與收藏

A month into the project, the old dome still stood behind a closed gate. The building team had to delay its roof inspection because strong winds made climbing unsafe. Mira felt upset but did not argue. A safety officer showed her the exact weather limit on the form and explained why a wet roof could be dangerous. Kai, who had hurt his ankle during a rehearsal years ago, remembered how easily a small step could become a fall. He gave the volunteers a clear description of the new schedule. People could continue preparing the portable show, but nobody would touch the roof until the trained workers returned.

計畫進行一個月後，舊圓頂仍關在大門後。校舍組因風勢太強，不得不延後屋頂檢查，因為攀爬不安全。米拉很失望，卻沒有爭辯。安全員指出表格上精確的風速限制，說明潮濕屋頂的危險。凱多年前在排練時傷過腳踝，記得小小一步也可能變成摔倒。他替志工清楚說明新日程。大家可以繼續準備可攜式演出，但受訓工人回來前，誰都不得碰屋頂。

At the next planning session, Jo brought a guava and cut it into pieces with a plastic spoon. A volunteer from the university youth program asked whether the show could include a novel about an evil wizard who stole the moon. Mira laughed. “We can tell a story,” she replied, “but we have to say which parts are invented.” The volunteer wrote a short description for a cultural storytelling corner. Kai helped select sounds that would not be too loud for children. He also suggested protective covers for the paper models. Mira liked seeing the team build a lesson with questions instead of simply copying her original plan.

下次規畫會，喬帶來番石榴，用塑膠湯匙分著吃。大學青年計畫的一位志工問演出能不能介紹一部邪惡巫師偷走月亮的小說。米拉笑了。「可以說故事，」她回答，「但必須指出哪些部分是虛構的。」志工替文化說故事區寫了簡短說明。凱幫忙選擇不會讓孩子覺得太大聲的音效，也建議替紙模型做保護套。米拉很高興看見大家用問題建立教材，而不是只照抄她的原始計畫。

The team tested an interactive display with a small swing made of string and cardboard. A visitor could pull it gently to see how a shadow changed. With each click of the button, a light moved across the wall. Jo checked that no loose part could reach a child's hand. The access adviser reminded them that some visitors could not use their hands easily, so Mira added a second version where a volunteer moved the model upon request. They agreed that a visitor did not have to explain a personal need to receive help. The goal was a welcoming experience, not a test of who could move the model fastest.

團隊測試一個互動展示，用繩子和紙板做小小的擺動架。觀眾輕拉就能看影子如何變化。每一次按鈕的喀噠聲響起，燈便沿牆移動。喬檢查沒有鬆動部件會碰到孩子的手。無障礙顧問提醒，有些訪客手部不方便使用，米拉便設計另一版，可由志工應請求操作。大家同意，觀眾不必說明個人狀況就能得到協助。目的是讓人自在參與，不是測誰動模型最快。

Wen sent a reply about the old recording. She allowed the team to quote one short line of her own words in the history exhibit, but she preferred that her full voice not be played. Mira thanked her for the clear answer. Kai deleted the voice sample from the show draft and kept only their own new narration. “It is strange,” he said, “how much work we can save by asking a question early.” Mira agreed. They had spent weeks wondering how to recover the past. Now they could travel forward with a new program that respected the people who had made the old one.

溫女士回覆舊錄音的事。她同意團隊在歷史展示中引用自己寫的一句短話，但不希望播放完整聲音。米拉謝謝她回答得明白。凱從節目草稿中刪除舊聲音樣本，只保留他們新錄的旁白。「很奇妙，」他說，「及早問清楚，反而能省掉好多工夫。」米拉同意。他們花了好幾週想找回過去，如今可以帶著尊重舊作者的新節目向前走。

That afternoon, a dog began to bark outside the theater, and Jo rushed to see if someone had been hurt. It was only a playful pet, and its owner quickly called it back. Mira watched the little scene from the doorway. The owner had not meant to disturb anyone, but the sound made several children jump. Kai used the moment to ask whether the show's sound level should be lower. They tested it again and chose a gentler opening. Sometimes the best warning came not from a written rule, but from paying attention to how real people reacted.

下午劇場外有狗叫聲，喬急忙出去看是否有人受傷。那只是一隻愛玩的寵物，主人很快把牠叫回。米拉從門口看著。主人沒有打擾別人的意思，但叫聲仍讓幾個孩子嚇了一跳。凱藉此問節目的音量是否應再低一些。他們重新測試，選了更溫和的開場。有時最好的提醒不是來自書面規定，而是注意真實的人如何反應。

At sunset, Mira called Kai to say she had received the new roof date. He was walking home and stopped under a tree to write it down. “We cannot promise the dome will be open soon,” she said. “But we can promise to tell people what we know.” He said that sounded like the right plan. Then he asked whether their sea walk still mattered to her after so many changes. “It does,” she answered. “I don't want every good thing in my life to become part of the project.” He agreed, and they chose Sunday afternoon. For once, the calendar held an appointment that needed no inspection form.

日落時米拉打電話給凱，告訴他屋頂檢查的新日期。他正走回家，在樹下停住記下。「我們不能保證圓頂很快重開，」她說，「但可以保證把知道的事說清楚。」凱認為這計畫很好。接著他問經過這麼多變化，海邊散步對她是否仍重要。「重要，」她回答。「我不想讓人生所有好事都變成專案的一部分。」他同意，兩人約好星期日下午。這一次行事曆上終於有一項不用安全檢查表的約定。
