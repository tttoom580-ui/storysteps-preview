The Shelf by the Sea

燈熄之後，星星仍在 · 第 8 / 25 章 · 1000–2000 · 英中對照 · 校訂中

The team held its next meeting at a park by the sea. Jo's family had invited them to a small barbecue after a community science day. Mira looked at the open ocean and remembered her childhood, when she thought stars could disappear if clouds covered them. Kai listened with a gentle smile. “I used to believe the moon followed my bus,” he said. Their memories were different, but both had given the sky a special meaning. Mira wanted the new show to begin with that feeling and then explain the reality behind it. She told the students not to command children to stop imagining. A question could move learning forward better than an order.

團隊在海邊公園開下一次會議。喬的家人邀請大家參加社區科學日後的小型烤肉聚餐。米拉望著海洋，想起童年時自己以為雲遮住星星，星星就會消失。凱溫柔地笑著說：「我以前相信月亮會跟著我的公車走。」兩人的記憶不同，天空卻都曾有特別意義。米拉想以這種感覺開場，再解釋背後的現實。她提醒大家，不要命令孩子停止想像。提問比命令更能推動學習。

Jo placed paper models on a shelf beside the picnic tables. A stone held down the pages, and a wooden stick marked the direction of the afternoon sun. Kai brought a box with one chopstick for each group to use as a pointer, but Jo said the sharp ends should be covered. They could use folded paper instead. The average height of the children coming to the event was lower than that of the adults, so the display needed two levels. Mira helped lift the lower board into place. She was glad that a simple change could make a lesson easier to see without making it less serious.

喬把紙模型放在野餐桌旁的架子上，用石頭壓住紙張，再用一根木棒標示下午太陽的方向。凱拿來一盒筷子，打算讓每組用一根筷子當指示棒，但喬認為尖端要包起來。他們最後改用折紙。參加活動的孩子平均身高比成人低，所以展示板需要兩種高度。米拉幫忙把較低的一塊抬到位置上。她很高興一個簡單調整，就能讓教材更容易看見，也不會降低內容的嚴謹度。

During lunch, Jo's grandfather told them a folk story about a star that served a lonely traveler. Mira enjoyed it but asked about its source before putting it into the show. “You can tell the tale as a tale,” he answered. “Do not say it is a fact.” She liked that clear difference. Kai suggested a scene where a friendly voice first told the story, then Mira explained what travelers can truly learn from the stars. The two parts could help visitors see how imagination and knowledge work together. It was a means of making room for wonder without changing the facts. They did not need to turn the storyteller into a servant of the science lesson. His story had value of its own.

午餐時，喬的祖父講了個民間故事：一顆星星為孤獨旅人服務。米拉很喜歡，但在放進節目前先詢問來源。「可以把故事當故事說，」祖父回答，「別把它說成事實。」她喜歡這個明確區分。凱提議先由親切的聲音說故事，再由米拉解釋旅人實際能如何利用星星。兩部分能讓觀眾看見想像與知識如何合作。這是一種保留驚奇、又不改動事實的方法。說故事的人不必成為科學課的僕人，他的故事本來就有價值。

A box of models grew too heavy, and the handle broke when Kai tried to lift it. He set the load down at once instead of carrying it anyway. Mira helped him divide the materials into smaller bags. She noticed how quickly she now trusted his judgment about physical work. When the wind grew strong, the team moved inside the park office. No one argued about fate or bad luck. They simply checked what could be used and what should stay dry. Jo wrote the changes on the plan. “Whatever happens to the weather,” she said, “we can still keep our promises about safety.”

有一箱模型太重，凱抬起時把手斷了。他立刻放下負重，沒有勉強搬運。米拉幫他把材料分裝到小袋裡。她注意到自己如今多麼容易相信他對實務工作的判斷。風勢變強時，大家搬進公園辦公室，沒有把意外怪罪給命運或壞運氣，只是檢查哪些東西能繼續用、哪些必須保持乾燥。喬把變動寫進計畫。「不管天氣怎樣，」她說，「我們仍可以遵守安全的承諾。」

At the end of the day, the community organizer asked to whom the science show belonged. Mira answered that the students owned their own drawings and music, while the university managed the hall and equipment. It was an important difference. The organizer offered a space for another free event and asked whether the team could visit children who could not travel to campus. Mira wanted to say yes at once. Kai touched her notebook and reminded her to check the schedule first. She thanked him later. It was kind to offer help, but kindness also meant being able to do what one had promised.

活動尾聲，社區承辦人問這場科學演出屬於誰。米拉回答，學生保有自己創作的圖片與音樂，而大學管理場地和設備。這個差別很重要。承辦人願意再提供一次免費活動場地，也詢問團隊能否探訪不能到校的孩子。米拉很想立刻答應，凱卻輕碰她的筆記本，提醒她先查日程。之後她向他道謝。願意幫忙是一份善意，但善意也意味著自己做得到所承諾的事。

Back on campus, Mira and Kai carried the models to a dry room. Mira asked why he had looked so happy while listening to her childhood story. He said he liked hearing about the person behind the careful science notes. She felt her face become warm. “You know, you can ask me questions outside work,” she said. “I am not a machine.” Kai answered that he knew, and asked whether she wanted to walk by the sea again without a box of models. Mira said she would like that. They chose a free afternoon after the next inspection. It was a small plan, and it belonged to both of them.

回到校園後，米拉和凱把模型搬到乾燥房間。米拉問他剛才聽自己講童年故事時，為何那麼高興。凱說他喜歡認識嚴謹科學筆記背後的那個人。她臉頰一熱。「你知道可以在工作以外問我問題，」她說。「我不是機器。」凱回答自己知道，接著問下次能不能不帶模型箱，一起再去海邊走走。米拉說很樂意。他們選了下次安全檢查後的一個空閒下午。那是小小的計畫，而且由兩人共同決定。
