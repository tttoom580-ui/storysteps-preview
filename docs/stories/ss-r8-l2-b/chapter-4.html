Behind the Wolf

燈熄之後，星星仍在 · 第 4 / 25 章 · 1000–2000 · 英中對照

用互動版查詞、朗讀與收藏

The university safety department agreed to inspect the portable show. Mira expected one officer, but four people came: an engineer, a fire officer, a student access adviser, and Ms. Song. Everyone who planned to attend the public trial would need a safe way out. Kai stood beside the screen with a comb in his hair because he had rushed from a theater rehearsal. Mira tried not to laugh. He caught her look and said, “My hair is a minor problem. Please look at the exit.” The engineer drew a firm line on the floor plan to show where no chairs could stand. Mira copied it into the team notebook without complaint.

大學安全部門同意檢查可攜式放映。米拉原以為只會來一位人員，卻來了工程師、消防人員、無障礙顧問和宋女士四人。每位準備參加試映的人都必須有安全的出口。凱剛從戲劇排練趕來，頭髮裡還插著梳子。米拉努力忍住笑。他看見她的表情就說：「我的頭髮只是小問題，請看出口。」工程師在平面圖上畫了一條堅定的線，標出不得擺椅子的區域。米拉毫無怨言地抄進團隊筆記。

They tested the motion of the screen and checked the small cage around the cooling fan. It had been made to keep fingers away from moving parts. One screw was loose. Kai found a tool, but the engineer asked him to wait until the equipment owner approved the repair. Mira felt impatient. The purpose of the inspection was to find problems, not to show how quickly a student could fix them. A cleaner brought an umbrella dripping from the rain outside and warned that water sometimes entered through the eastern window. That made the location of every electric cable more important than anyone had expected.

他們測試布幕的動作，也檢查冷卻風扇外的小護罩。護罩是為防止手指碰到轉動部件，其中一顆螺絲鬆了。凱找來工具，工程師卻請他等設備負責人批准再修。米拉有點不耐煩。檢查的目的原本就是找出問題，而不是看學生能多快修好。清潔人員帶著一把還在滴水的傘進門，提醒東側窗戶偶爾會漏雨。這使每條電線的位置比大家想的更重要。

At lunch, the hall served fried rice and vegetables from a large pan. Mira did not want the team to fry food beside expensive equipment, so they ate in the cafeteria. A medical student had left a box marked drug information on a nearby chair, and Jo returned it to its owner without opening it. “Private materials stay private, even when they are in the wrong place,” she said. The access adviser liked that rule. She also suggested a lower display board for visitors who could not stand for long. Kai asked the school gym if they could borrow light chairs with a clear area between rows. The answer was yes, if they returned them each day.

中午大廳供應以大平底鍋做的炒飯與蔬菜。米拉不希望團隊在昂貴設備旁煎炒食物，所以大家到餐廳吃。一名醫學生把標著藥物資訊的盒子放在椅上，喬沒有打開，而是直接交還物主。「私人物品就算放錯地方，仍然是私人的，」她說。無障礙顧問很欣賞這條原則，也建議設置較低的展示板，方便不適合久站的觀眾。凱向體育館詢問能否借輕便椅，並保留座位間的通道。對方說可以，但必須每天歸還。

The afternoon test went wrong in a funny way. A shape that should have looked like a flying bird turned into the shadow of a wolf. “There goes our wing,” Kai said, pointing to the bent paper model. The students laughed, but Mira asked them to stop the machine before reaching for it. She explained how the size of a shadow could change when its source moved. The engineer agreed that the lesson was useful. He also asked for a standard plan showing who would control each scene. Mira wrote that Kai would manage sound and light while she would explain the sky. Each person could stop the show if something looked unsafe.

下午測試出了個好笑的差錯。原本應該像飛鳥的形狀，變成一隻狼的影子。「我們的翅膀不見了，」凱指著彎曲的紙模型說。大家笑了，米拉卻先請他們關機，再伸手調整。她解釋影子的大小會因光源移動而變化。工程師認為這也是很好的教學素材，並要求一份標準作業表，寫明誰負責每一幕。米拉記下由凱控制聲光，自己解說星空；若發現危險，任何人都可以要求停演。

In the evening, the student adviser said the show could welcome children from the whole region if the first test went well. Mira felt encouraged, but Kai reminded her they had not passed yet. He was not trying to discourage her. He wanted a promise they could keep. Mira asked the adviser to encourage the volunteers who were nervous about helping children with different needs. The adviser said clear questions were better than quiet guesses. They asked the officers to mark the remaining work in simple language: repair the fan guard, move the cables, cover the leaking window, and test the doors. Ms. Song signed the list and gave them a date for another inspection. Mira thanked Kai for speaking carefully when she wanted to celebrate. He said he would celebrate with her when the doors were truly ready.

晚上學生事務顧問說，如果首次試映順利，未來可以邀請全區孩子。米拉受到鼓舞，但凱提醒她他們還沒通過檢查。他不是故意潑冷水，而是想做出守得住的承諾。米拉請顧問鼓勵對協助不同需求孩童感到緊張的志工。顧問說，清楚提問比默默猜測好。他們請檢查人員以簡單文字列出待辦事項：修好風扇護罩、移動電線、處理漏水窗戶並測試門。宋女士簽署清單，約好再檢日期。米拉謝謝凱在她想慶祝時仍說得謹慎。他說等門真正準備好，就會和她一起慶祝。

Before leaving, the engineer returned to the shadow of the wolf. He asked Mira to explain why the bird had changed shape. She moved a cardboard model while Kai held the light still. As the model turned, its shadow crossed a mark on the wall and became longer. The children would be able to see the change for themselves. The engineer said that was a strong teaching idea, as long as the machine remained behind a safe line. Mira made one more note: questions from visitors were not interruptions. They were part of the show, and the team should leave time for them.

離開前，工程師再次看向那隻狼的影子，請米拉說明飛鳥為何變形。凱固定燈光，米拉轉動紙板模型。模型一轉，影子就跨過牆上的記號，拉得更長。孩子們可以親自看到變化。工程師說這是很好的教學方法，但機器仍必須留在安全線後方。米拉又記下一點：觀眾提問不是打斷表演，而是表演的一部分，團隊應留時間給大家提問。
