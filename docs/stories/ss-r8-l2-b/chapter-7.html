鏡片裡的聲音

The Voice in the Lens

燈熄之後，星星仍在 · 第 7 / 25 章 · 1000–2000 · 英中對照 · 校訂中

用互動版查詞、朗讀與收藏

After the first show, Mira found an address written on the back of an old picture. It belonged to Wen, a former planetarium guide who had retired ten years earlier. Mira asked Professor Hsu for advice before visiting. “The actual owner of a memory gets to decide how it is shared,” he said. “Do not treat an old recording as free material just because the building is closed.” Mira wrote a polite letter explaining their project and her reason for making contact. Kai added his name and a question about the music. They mailed it without including any promise about using Wen's work. A few days later, Wen invited them to meet at a small tea house.

首演之後，米拉在舊照片背面找到一個地址。那是十年前退休的星象館導覽員溫女士住處。拜訪前米拉先向徐教授請教。「回憶真正的主人有權決定它怎麼被分享，」教授說。「不能因為建築關閉，就把舊錄音當成免費素材。」米拉寫了封禮貌的信，說明計畫和聯繫理由。凱加上自己的名字，並詢問音樂的事。他們寄信時沒有先假設能使用溫女士的作品。幾天後，溫女士邀請他們到一間小茶館見面。

Wen wore a blue necklace shaped like a circle of stars. She was a good listener and let Mira finish her whole story before she spoke. “I am glad someone remembers the dome,” she said. “But please do not make me a prisoner of my old role. I have another life now.” She had become an artist, making small cups from clay and painting birds on them. A piece of blue-and-white china stood beside the window. Mira apologized for assuming that Wen would want to return to the old show. Wen smiled and said she would be happy to share a little history, but only if they asked about each piece before using it.

溫女士戴著一條藍色項鍊，吊墜像一圈星星。她是善於傾聽的人，讓米拉把整個故事說完才開口。「很高興有人記得圓頂，」她說，「但請別讓我被過去的身分困住。我現在也有別的生活。」她後來成了陶藝家，製作小杯子，在上面畫鳥。窗邊擺著一件青花瓷。米拉為自己預設她一定想重返舊演出而道歉。溫女士微笑說願意分享一些歷史，但每一項要用的資料都得先詢問。

Wen brought a tape with a written label. It held a recording of the old star show's opening beat, followed by her voice describing the sky at midnight. Kai had the skill to play it, but he did not take the tape from her hand. First they discussed the origin of the recording. Wen had written the words, but a student musician had made the music. None of them knew whether that student wanted the tune played again. Kai suggested they sort the material into parts and request permission for each one. Wen praised him for being careful. Mira liked how he could respect the past without letting it control their choices.

溫女士帶來一卷貼有標籤的錄音帶，裡面錄著舊星空節目開場的節拍，以及她介紹午夜天空的聲音。凱懂得播放，卻沒有立刻從她手上拿走。三人先談錄音來源。旁白由溫女士撰寫，音樂卻是一名學生作的。沒有人知道那位學生是否願意歌曲再次播出。凱建議把素材分開整理，逐項徵得許可。溫女士稱讚他謹慎。米拉喜歡他能尊重過去，又不讓過去替現在的人做決定。

At tea, Wen offered toast with fruit and asked about their relationship. Mira nearly dropped her cup. “Our relationship is a working one,” she said, perhaps too quickly. Kai looked at the table, then answered gently. “We are becoming friends as well.” Wen laughed, but did not press them. She pointed to a photograph of a glass lens that had once stood inside the main projector. The lens had not disappeared; it had been sent to a university collection for study. An expert there might explain whether it could safely return. Mira noted the information on a form and promised not to enter the locked dome to search for it herself.

喝茶時，溫女士端來水果吐司，問起他們的關係。米拉差點摔了杯子。「我們是工作關係，」她答得也許太快。凱看著桌面，溫柔地補上一句：「也正在成為朋友。」溫女士笑了，卻沒有追問。她指著一張玻璃鏡片照片，說那片鏡片過去裝在主投影機裡。它沒有失蹤，而是送到大學典藏室研究。那裡的專家或許能說明能否安全裝回。米拉在表格上記下資訊，承諾不會擅自走進上鎖的圓頂尋找。

On the way home, Kai told Mira he used to think science students wanted everything to be silent. She told him she had believed theater students cared more about praise than facts. “We were both wrong,” he said. A boy ran past them wearing a naughty smile and carrying a toy telescope. His mother followed, laughing. The street looked pleasant in the late light. Mira understood that a public show had to leave room for many kinds of people: children who asked wild questions, artists who had moved on, and workers who needed clear rules. She asked Kai whether he wanted to help make a new opening, one that did not copy the old tape. He said yes.

回家路上，凱說他以前以為天文系學生希望一切都保持安靜。米拉也承認自己曾以為劇場學生比事實更在乎掌聲。「我們都錯了，」他說。一個男孩帶著淘氣的笑容和玩具望遠鏡跑過，母親在後面笑著追。傍晚街道很舒服。米拉理解公開演出必須容納各式各樣的人：問奇怪問題的孩子、已經轉向新生活的藝術家，以及需要清楚規則的工作者。她問凱願不願意一起創作不照抄舊錄音的新開場，凱說願意。

That night Mira drew stars on the black side of a paper plate while Kai wrote a short rhythm. They agreed to show their draft to Wen, not to ask her to approve every choice, but to thank her for what she had shared. Mira was pleased that Kai could hear a future in an old story. Kai said he was learning to see one too. When they left the studio, the clock was almost at midnight. Neither spoke about the question Wen had asked at tea. Still, when they came to the road where their paths separated, they stood there longer than either needed to. A new kind of question had arrived, and neither had to answer it that night.

那晚米拉在紙盤黑色的背面畫星星，凱則寫了一小段節奏。他們同意讓溫女士看草稿，不是要她批准每個選擇，而是感謝她分享的經驗。米拉很高興凱能在舊故事裡聽見未來。凱說自己也開始學著看見。離開工作室時，時間快到午夜了。兩人都沒提起溫女士喝茶時的問題。但走到各自回家的岔路時，他們站得比平常久。一個新的問題已經到來，而他們不必當晚回答。
