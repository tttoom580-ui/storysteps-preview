A Crack in the Night

燈熄之後，星星仍在 · 第 5 / 25 章 · 1000–2000 · 英中對照

A week before the public trial, Mira lost her appetite. She had spent the morning reading the safety list and giving too much attention to every new problem. Kai noticed that she had not eaten. He brought her a bowl of noodle soup from the student shop and sat beside her without asking questions. “I know we have a conflict about how fast to work,” he said at last. “But my support does not depend on winning the argument.” Mira looked at him, surprised by the simple words. She had expected another lecture about rules. Instead, he waited until she finished the soup before asking whether she wanted help with the plan.

公開試映前一週，米拉忙到失去食慾。整個上午她反覆看安全清單，過度注意每個新問題。凱發現她沒吃飯，從學生商店帶來一碗湯麵，坐在她旁邊，沒有立刻問東問西。「我知道我們對做事速度有衝突，」他終於說。「但我支持妳，不是因為要在爭論中獲勝。」米拉驚訝地看著他。她本以為又要聽一場規則講座。凱卻等她吃完麵，才問她要不要一起整理計畫。

Their next task was an electrical test. A technician found a weak connection behind the screen and said the metal frame should not be used until the part was replaced. That was a brilliant discovery, but it meant another delay. Jo suggested a different method: use the smaller wall screen that already belonged to the classroom. It would carry less weight and need no hanging frame. Mira liked the idea because it solved the problem rather than hiding it. Kai measured the light and checked whether the new screen could spread a clear image across the room. They both signed the revised test plan, then sent it to the safety department for approval.

下一項工作是電力測試。技師發現布幕後有一處接點脆弱，表示零件更換前不能使用金屬支架。這是很重要的發現，卻也意味著延期。喬提出不同方法：改用教室原有的小型牆面布幕。它重量較輕，也不需要吊掛支架。米拉很喜歡，因為這解決了問題，而非掩蓋它。凱量了光線，確認新布幕能否讓影像清楚鋪滿房間。兩人一起在更新的測試表簽名，送交安全部門核准。

While they waited, Mira visited the campus history room. It contained a photograph of the planetarium during a snowy winter many years ago. A bright star had been painted over the door, and a small rooster stood in the garden beside it. The old gardener explained that students once raised birds there as part of a domestic science class. Mira laughed at the thought of a rooster watching stars. Then she went on to discover an older picture showing how the dome's sound system had been built with help from theater students. The link between science and performance was not new. Kai belonged to a tradition the building already remembered.

等待核准時，米拉去了校史室。裡面有張多年以前雪季的天文館照片，門上畫著亮星，旁邊花園還有一隻公雞。老園丁解釋，過去學生曾在那裡上家政與生活科學課，飼養過家禽。米拉想到公雞也在看星星，不禁笑了。接著她繼續尋找，發現更早的照片，顯示圓頂音響系統原本就是戲劇系學生協助建造。科學與表演的連結不是新事。凱正延續著這棟建築記得的傳統。

The team made a new display about weather, light, and the night sky. Jo brought a wool scarf to show how clouds can cover what is already there. Mira used a drawing of human skin to explain why we feel warm in sunlight, though she made it clear that the scarf was only a model. They added photographs of thunder clouds and talked about why people should never stay outside during dangerous weather just to see stars. A parent from the town offered praise for the simple explanations. Mira was pleased, but she asked for criticism too. The parent wanted clearer signs for young visitors, so Jo made them that afternoon.

團隊做了一塊新展示板，介紹天氣、光和夜空。喬帶來一條羊毛圍巾，示範雲如何遮住本來就在那裡的東西。米拉用人體皮膚示意圖說明陽光為何讓人感到暖和，同時強調圍巾只是模型。他們加入雷雲照片，提醒觀眾不能為了看星星而在危險天候下待在戶外。一名鎮上家長稱讚解說簡單清楚。米拉很開心，卻也主動請對方批評。家長希望孩子容易看懂指示牌，喬當天下午便補做了。

At dusk, a heavy mass of clouds moved over the campus. Rain struck the windows, followed by the sound of thunder. Ms. Song closed the student hall until the storm passed. Mira almost cried because the schedule had changed again. Kai pointed to the emergency plan. “Whenever weather makes the space unsafe, we stop. That was our agreement.” She knew he was right. They moved the paper models to a dry shelf and checked the floor for water. After the storm, Mira found a small crack near the east window and marked it on the plan. A cancelled hour had helped them find a danger before children arrived.

黃昏時，一大團雲壓向校園。雨打在窗上，接著傳來雷聲。宋女士宣布學生大廳在暴風雨過去前關閉。行程又變，米拉幾乎要哭。凱指著緊急計畫說：「只要天氣讓場地不安全，我們就停下來。這是之前的約定。」她知道他說得對。他們把紙模型搬到乾燥架上，檢查地板是否積水。雨停後，米拉發現東窗旁有一道細裂縫，立刻標在圖上。取消的一小時，反而讓他們在孩子到來之前找出危險。

That night, Mira wrote two lists: what had gone wrong, and what had gone right. The first was longer, but the second mattered more. No one had been hurt. They had found a safer screen and a better way to tell the weather story. Kai read her lists and added one line: “We did not leave anyone alone with a problem.” Mira smiled at that. The next morning she asked the team to gather for a calm meeting, not an emergency. Their work was still far from finished, but she was beginning to understand that care could be a practical skill. It was not only a feeling, and it was not something she had to do by herself.

當晚米拉寫下兩份清單：出了什麼問題，以及有哪些事情做對了。第一份比較長，但第二份更重要。沒有人受傷；他們找到更安全的布幕，也想出更好的天氣解說方式。凱看過清單，加上一句：「我們沒有讓任何人獨自面對問題。」米拉因此笑了。隔天早上她邀請團隊開一場平靜的例會，而不是緊急會議。工作仍遠未完成，但她開始理解，關懷也可以是一種實際技能。它不只是感覺，也不必由她一個人完成。
