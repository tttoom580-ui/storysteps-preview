雨天備案

The Rain Plan

燈熄之後，星星仍在 · 第 6 / 25 章 · 1000–2000 · 英中對照 · 校訂中

用互動版查詞、朗讀與收藏

On the eve of the first approved show, a storm warning arrived on every student's phone. The university building would remain open for classes, but outdoor paths could become slippery. Mira and Kai were standing under a roof near the sidewalk when the message appeared. A crowd moved toward the main hall to avoid the rain. “We should accept the warning, not fight it,” Kai said. Mira agreed. They had already written a rain plan, so she called the team and asked each person to check the signs, chairs, and entrance. Her favorite part of the project was no longer the picture of Saturn. It was watching people help one another without being ordered.

首次獲准放映的前一晚，全校學生手機收到暴風雨警報。校舍仍會開放上課，但戶外步道可能濕滑。訊息出現時，米拉和凱正站在靠近人行道的屋簷下。一群人朝大廳走去避雨。「我們應該接受警報，而不是和它對抗，」凱說。米拉同意。他們早已寫好雨天備案，便打電話請每位隊員檢查指示牌、椅子和入口。她最喜歡的計畫部分已不再是土星圖片，而是看著大家不用被命令，也願意互相幫忙。

Jo examined the floor and found a wet corner by the western door. She put up a sign and asked facilities staff to repair the seal later. A young musician arrived carrying a violin case. He had been invited to play a short piece before the show, but now he worried about wet equipment. Kai offered him a dry room and said the music could be postponed without harming the science lesson. The violinist smiled. “You know, a good performance has a soul even when the music stops,” he said. Mira thought that sounded like poetry. She wrote it down, then checked that the quiet version of the show was ready.

喬查看地面，在西側門邊發現濕角落。她設置警告牌，請校舍人員稍後修好密封條。一位年輕音樂家帶著小提琴盒到場。他原本受邀在放映前演奏短曲，卻擔心設備受潮。凱提供乾燥房間，說延期演奏不會影響科學課。小提琴手笑著說：「好表演就算音樂停了，仍有自己的靈魂。」米拉覺得像詩，便記下來，然後確認無聲版本準備好了。

An official from the student office came to examine the audience plan. A family had asked whether they could bring a child who used a wheelchair. Mira pointed to the clear entrance and the reserved space beside the seats. The official checked the slope and approved the arrangement. Outside, a little boy in a yellow raincoat watched the signs. He carried a toy giraffe with a very long neck. “Will that animal see more stars than me?” he asked. Mira laughed and explained that height did not change the stars on the screen. What mattered was having a clear view, and she helped him choose a place where he could see.

學生事務處官員來檢查觀眾動線。有家庭詢問能否帶使用輪椅的孩子來。米拉指出寬敞入口及座位旁保留的空間。官員檢查坡度後，同意這項安排。戶外一個穿黃雨衣的小男孩正看著標示，手上抱著長脖子的長頸鹿玩偶。「牠會比我看到更多星星嗎？」他問。米拉笑了，解釋身高不會改變布幕上的星星，重要的是視線清楚。她幫他選了一個看得見的地方。

As the room filled, Kai walked slowly toward the control table. The current term had been busy for him, and he was afraid of making a mistake in front of people. Mira noticed his tense face. She asked whether he wanted to stop for water, and he said yes. When he returned, they checked the list together. The projector was stable, the emergency lights worked, and the rain had not reached the wires. “We can begin,” he said. Mira spoke first, welcoming visitors to a show created by many students. She explained that the old dome itself was still closed, so nobody should try to enter it after the event.

房間漸漸坐滿時，凱慢慢走向控制桌。這學期他很忙，也怕在觀眾面前失誤。米拉注意到他的緊繃，問他要不要先喝水，他說好。回來後，他們一起核對清單。投影機穩定、緊急照明正常，雨水也沒有碰到電線。「可以開始了，」他說。米拉先歡迎來賓，說這場演出由許多學生共同完成，也解釋舊圓頂仍然關閉，請大家活動結束後不要自行入內。

The show began with a simple picture of the night sky. A quiet voice described the moon, then stopped so visitors could look for themselves. The boy with the giraffe pointed toward a bright planet. Another child asked whether a star could be used during war. Mira answered carefully that people had long used stars to find direction, but the sky belonged to everyone, not to one army. Kai changed the picture slowly. Kai knew that some children were sensitive to sudden noise, so no sudden sound frightened the younger children. When the last planet faded, the room stayed still for a moment, then filled with warm applause. Mira turned to Kai, and for once he looked as surprised as she did.

演出以一張簡單夜空圖開始。柔和的聲音介紹月亮後便停下來，讓觀眾自己找。抱著長頸鹿玩偶的男孩指向明亮的行星。另一個孩子問戰爭時能不能靠星星辨路。米拉謹慎回答，人類長久以來會用星星辨方向，但天空屬於每個人，不屬於某支軍隊。凱緩緩切換畫面，凱知道有些孩子對突然的聲響很敏感，因此沒有任何突如其來的聲音嚇到小孩子。最後一顆行星淡去時，房間安靜了一會兒，接著響起溫暖掌聲。米拉看向凱，發現他和自己一樣驚訝。

After visitors left, the team counted chairs and wiped the tables. The rain continued, but there was no need to hurry into it. Mira thanked each volunteer by name. Kai stood beside the screen, holding his notebook close to his chest. “This was better than I imagined,” he said. Mira wanted to tell him that she liked working with him, but her words became tangled. Instead she asked if he would share a late dinner with her when the storm passed. “As colleagues?” he asked, smiling. “As two tired people who earned dinner,” she answered. They both laughed, and she felt that this was a beginning she did not have to explain yet.

觀眾離去後，團隊清點椅子、擦拭桌面。雨還沒停，大家也不必急著冒雨回家。米拉一一叫出志工名字並道謝。凱站在布幕旁，筆記本抱在胸前。「比我想像的還好，」他說。米拉想告訴他自己很喜歡與他一起工作，話卻糾結在嘴邊。於是她改問雨停後要不要一起吃晚餐。「以同事身分？」他笑著問。「以兩個值得吃頓飯的疲累人類身分，」她回答。兩人都笑了；她覺得這個開始還不需要急著解釋。
