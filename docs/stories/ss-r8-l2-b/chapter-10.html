一公尺一公尺

One Meter at a Time

燈熄之後，星星仍在 · 第 10 / 25 章 · 1000–2000 · 英中對照 · 校訂中

Their next challenge was space. The larger hall was available only for two hours, and the library wanted a clear path around every table. Jo measured the length of each row with a meter stick. She wrote down how many visitors could stand safely in each area. Mira checked the unit of every number so nobody would mistake centimeters for meters. Kai made a small fox puppet for the children's section, while Jo used glue to make a paper goose. They both laughed when the goose looked more like a duck. “A good model needs gradual improvement,” Mira said. The word sounded serious for a paper bird, but the team understood what she meant.

下一個挑戰是空間。大廳只能借兩小時，圖書館也要求每張桌子周圍保留通道。喬用公尺尺測量每排長度，記下每個區域可安全容納的人數。米拉核對所有數字的單位，以免把公分誤當公尺。凱替兒童區做了一隻小狐狸手偶，喬則用膠水做了一隻紙鵝。鵝看起來更像鴨子，兩人都笑了。「好模型需要逐步改進，」米拉說。這句話拿來形容紙鳥似乎太嚴肅，但大家懂她的意思。

At the planning table, a volunteer offered biscuits, a little congratulation card, and cooked yam left from her lunch. Mira asked everyone to gather before eating so they could settle the schedule. A large poster said “congratulations” beside a drawing of the moon. Jo wanted to hang it in the entrance, but Kai worried that visitors might mistake the test for a completed opening. Mira changed the words to “Welcome to our next trial.” She did not want to ignore how much work remained. They agreed that each twenty-minute show needed a proper break for questions and cleaning. The team could not serve a new audience every five minutes, no matter how excited everyone felt.

規畫桌上，一位志工拿出餅乾、小小的祝賀卡，以及午餐剩下的蒸地瓜分享。米拉請大家先集合討論時間表。大海報上畫著月亮，旁邊寫著「恭喜」。喬想把它掛在入口，凱卻擔心觀眾以為試映就是全面開館。米拉把文字改成「歡迎參加下一次試映」。她不願忽略尚未完成的工作。大家同意每二十分鐘一場，並安排提問及清潔時間。不論多興奮，都不能每五分鐘接一批新觀眾。

Kai prepared a musical opening with three clear notes. Mira wanted to know what they meant. He said one note suggested a small step, the second a larger view, and the third an open question. She liked the style, but asked him to test it with people who preferred silence. Kai arranged a version with no music and kept the same timing for the pictures. “The picture should do the main work,” he said. Mira thanked him for listening. The sound would be an invitation, not a command. She thought about how much courage it took to let another person change an idea that mattered to you.

凱準備了由三個清楚音符組成的音樂開場。米拉問它們代表什麼。他說第一個音暗示小小一步，第二個是更大的視野，第三個是開放的問題。米拉喜歡這種風格，但請他找偏好安靜的人試聽。凱安排了不放音樂的版本，圖片時間仍完全相同。「主角應該是畫面，」他說。米拉謝謝他願意聽意見。聲音是邀請，不是命令。她想到，讓別人改變自己在意的點子，需要多大的勇氣。

For the outdoor part, they planned a slow walk around the garden when the weather allowed it. Jo drew a snail at the start and a running fox at the end, so visitors could choose their own pace. A child asked whether each person had to finish one page per minute. “No,” Mira said. “These are places to explore, not a race.” The team placed benches along the path and checked that people could return to the hall without crossing the busy road. A local family offered a week of volunteer help during their vacation. Mira thanked them but said they could choose only the hours they were truly free.

戶外活動部分，他們計畫在天候允許時，讓觀眾慢慢走過花園。喬在起點畫蝸牛，終點畫奔跑的狐狸，表示每個人都能選擇速度。一名孩子問是不是每分鐘一定要讀完一頁。「不用，」米拉說。「這些是探索的地方，不是賽跑。」團隊沿路放長椅，確認大家不必穿越繁忙道路就能回到大廳。當地一個家庭願意在假期幫忙一週。米拉道謝，也強調只要選真正有空的時段即可。

On Friday, the inspector visited again. The new screen passed the checks, and the written signs were easy to read. Ms. Song said the team had permission for another public event, but not for the old dome. The students cheered. Kai raised a biscuit like a small prize, and Mira laughed. She congratulated each member and reminded them to keep the equipment records. At dinner, he offered to walk her home. She said yes, and the two talked about books, food, and what they might do after graduation. Their conversation no longer felt like a meeting. That change was as surprising to Mira as the bright planet on their screen.

星期五，檢查員再次到訪。新布幕通過檢查，文字標示也清楚易讀。宋女士說團隊獲准再辦一場公開活動，但舊圓頂仍不能開放。社員歡呼。凱舉起一片餅乾當小獎盃，米拉忍不住笑。她逐一恭喜大家，也提醒記得保存設備紀錄。晚餐後，凱提議送她回家。她答應了，兩人沿路聊書、食物，以及畢業後的打算。這場對話不再像開會。對米拉來說，這變化和布幕上突然亮起的行星一樣意外。

Before sleeping, Mira looked at the plan for the coming month. There were still many empty boxes, and the project could fail if they rushed. But they now had a working show, a safer room, and people who knew what to do. She wrote a quiet note to herself: “Ask for help before the problem grows.” Then she added another line about Kai: “He listens, even when he disagrees.” She did not label this as love. It was enough to know she wanted another walk with him and another day of building something useful. The stars on her desk calendar looked a little brighter that night.

睡前米拉翻看下個月的計畫。仍有許多空白格，若太急，整個專案可能失敗。但他們已有能運作的演出、更安全的房間，以及知道自己該做什麼的團隊。她悄悄給自己寫了句話：「問題擴大前，先求助。」又加上一句關於凱的紀錄：「即使不同意，他仍會聽。」她還不急著把這叫作愛情。知道自己想再跟他散步，也想再一起做點有用的事，就已經足夠。那晚桌上行事曆的星星似乎亮了一些。
