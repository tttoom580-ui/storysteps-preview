The Wrong Entrance

燈熄之後，星星仍在 · 第 3 / 25 章 · 1000–2000 · 英中對照

用互動版查詞、朗讀與收藏

The first test began on a cloudy morning. Mira made an attempt to start the portable projector, but the screen stayed white. The cause was simple: someone had placed the image cable in the wrong entrance on the control box. Kai crouched beside her and pointed at the label. “I do this during rehearsal too,” he admitted. “The trick is to stop before you force anything.” A classmate called Jo arrived with cereal in a paper cup and offered to help. Jo wore one silver earring and had studied stage design for three years. She could read the small labels without touching the wires, so Mira asked her to guide the next check.

第一次試映從多雲的早晨開始。米拉試著啟動可攜式投影機，布幕卻只有一片白。原因很簡單：有人把影像線插進控制盒錯誤的入口。凱蹲在旁邊指著標籤說：「我排練時也犯過這種錯，重點是在硬推之前先停下。」同學喬端著紙杯裝的早餐穀片趕來幫忙。喬戴著一只銀耳環，在舞臺設計系念了三年。她不必碰電線就能讀清小標籤，米拉便請她帶領下一輪檢查。

A rumor spread that the team planned to cheat on its safety report. Mira wanted to answer immediately, but Kai asked where the claim had come from. A student had seen them moving the projector and thought they were hiding it from the general inspection. Jo explained that the portable unit was allowed in the hall, while the old machine remained locked in the dome. They posted a link to the school's written approval, with private contact details removed. Mira learned that an honest reply needed evidence, not just anger. Kai gave her a short nod. It was a small moment, but she felt they were finally working on the same side.

有傳言說團隊打算在安全報告上作弊。米拉很想立刻反駁，凱卻先問謠言從哪裡來。原來有人看到他們搬投影機，以為是在躲避一般檢查。喬解釋可攜式設備獲准在大廳使用，舊機器仍鎖在圓頂內。他們貼出學校書面核准文件的連結，並遮住私人聯絡資料。米拉明白，誠實的答覆需要證據，不只需要怒氣。凱對她輕輕點頭。那只是小小一刻，她卻感到兩人終於站在同一邊。

After lunch, the student government sent a question about access. The building served a public university, so could the display be open to people outside the club? Mira wanted a liberal plan that welcomed anyone, but Ms. Song reminded her that the hall had limited space. A group of runners from the sports team might arrive at the same time as children from the neighborhood. A runner from the sports team offered to supply water and help visitors find their seats. The club decided to operate a free booking list and keep a clear path beside the chairs. That way, the show could continue without asking anyone to stand in a crowded doorway. Nobody needed to join the club or pay to watch. In the final plan, a visitor could also leave early without giving any reason. Mira wrote these choices on the first page.

午餐後，學生自治會送來無障礙與參觀權益方面的問題。這是公立大學的場地，展示是否能讓社團外的人參加？米拉想採自由開放的方案，宋女士卻提醒大廳容納人數有限。運動隊的跑者可能與社區孩童同時到場。運動隊的一名跑者主動提供飲水，並幫觀眾找座位。社團決定採免費預約，椅子旁保留通道。這樣演出就能繼續，無須讓任何人擠在門口。無論是不是社員都能觀看，也不須付費。最終規畫還允許觀眾不必解釋理由就提前離開。米拉把這些選擇寫在第一頁。

That afternoon, Kai rode his scooter to the theater store to collect a replacement lamp. Mira asked whether she could go with him, then changed her mind when she saw the rain. She chose to remain in the hall and check the supplies. There were extra chairs, signs, and water, but not enough large-print guides. Jo volunteered to make them. This was their first lesson about a public show: the science could be perfect while the welcome was poor. Mira did not want that. She called the campus print room and arranged for larger words and clearer pictures. When Kai returned, she showed him the guides before showing him the lamp.

下午凱騎機車到劇場倉庫取替換燈泡。米拉本來想跟去，看到雨勢後改變主意，留下清點物資。椅子、指示牌和飲水都有備用，但大字版導覽單不夠。喬自願幫忙製作。這是他們對公開演出的第一個領悟：科學內容可以完美，接待卻可能很差。米拉不希望這樣，便聯絡校內印製室，安排更大的字和清楚的圖片。凱回來時，她先讓他看導覽單，才看燈泡。

During the second attempt, a pale blue planet appeared on the screen. It turned slowly while the room stayed quiet. Jo smiled. Kai played a gentle sound, then turned it off so the audience could compare the two versions. Mira described the difference between the real sky and a model. “A model is not the sky itself,” she said. “It is a way to ask better questions.” Someone asked whether stars could speak. Kai answered that the music was made by people, not by stars. Mira appreciated his clear words. He had not used a beautiful sound to pretend that something false was true.

第二次嘗試時，淡藍色行星終於出現在布幕上。它緩緩旋轉，房間保持安靜。喬笑了。凱播放柔和音景，再關掉，讓觀眾比較兩種版本。米拉解釋真實星空與模型的差異。「模型不是天空本身，」她說，「它是幫我們提出更好問題的方法。」有人問星星會不會說話。凱回答音樂是人做的，不是星星發出的。米拉很欣賞他說得清楚。他沒有用美麗的聲音假裝錯誤的事是真實的。

At sunset, Mira helped Jo stack the chairs. They talked about how strange it felt to create a show with a person who had once seemed impossible to work with. Jo asked if Mira liked Kai. Mira nearly dropped a chair. “We are making a planetarium show,” she said. “That is a project, not a love story.” Jo only laughed and reminded her that both things could be true without either being forced. Mira did not answer. On her way out she passed the wrong cable entrance and saw a new label, printed in large letters. A mistake had become a lesson for everyone. She hoped the rest of the project could change in the same way.

日落時，米拉與喬一起疊好椅子。她們聊到與過去覺得難以合作的人共同做一場表演，是件多麼奇妙的事。喬問米拉是不是喜歡凱。米拉差點把椅子摔下去。「我們是在做星象館演出，」她說，「這是計畫，不是愛情故事。」喬只是笑，提醒她兩件事可以同時成立，但都不能被勉強。米拉沒回答。離開時，她看見錯誤接線孔旁貼上了大字新標籤。一場失誤成了大家的教材。她希望這個計畫接下來也能這樣成長。
