一張票的代價

The Cost of a Ticket

燈熄之後，星星仍在 · 第 11 / 25 章 · 1000–2000 · 英中對照 · 校訂中

用互動版查詞、朗讀與收藏

The university collection agreed to let Mira and Kai examine the old lens, but moving it required a trained guard and a small service fee. Mira checked the amount in writing before she accepted. The lens was kept in a narrow room beside an archive of science books. A crow sat on the window and tapped the glass, making Kai lift one eyebrow. “Even the birds want tickets,” he whispered. The collection manager was a skilled worker who had studied glass for many years. She said the lens was pale at one edge and could not yet return to the projector. Mira was disappointed, but she thanked the manager for keeping it safe. “We can disagree about the future and still respect your work,” she said.

大學典藏室同意讓米拉和凱查看舊鏡片，但搬動需要受訓管理員，並須支付少量服務費。米拉先把費用書面確認，再答應。鏡片保存在一間狹長的房間，旁邊是科學書庫。一隻烏鴉停在窗邊敲玻璃，讓凱挑起一邊眉毛。「連鳥都想買票，」他輕聲說。典藏室管理員是多年研究玻璃的專業人員。她指出鏡片一側已泛白，現在還不能放回投影機。米拉雖然失望，仍謝謝她好好保管它。「我們對未來的想法可以不同，仍能尊重您的工作，」她說。

The manager gave them a description written by the original maker. The drawing showed a mark only one centimeter wide, but that tiny detail explained why the light had once looked uneven. Mira wanted to include the drawing in their research notes, not in the public show, until permission was clear. Kai suggested asking the author's family about the document. The manager said the author's name was already recorded and offered to send their question through the proper office. “A puzzle is not solved by taking whatever looks useful,” she said. Mira smiled. She was beginning to hear the same lesson from many different people, and each version made it stronger.

管理員拿出原製作者留下的說明。圖上有個只有一公分寬的記號，那小細節卻能解釋早年的光線為何不均勻。米拉想把圖先放進研究筆記，不急著公開，直到確認授權。凱提議詢問作者家人。管理員說作者姓名已有正式紀錄，願意透過正確窗口轉達問題。「解謎不是看見有用的東西就拿走，」她說。米拉笑了。她從許多人那裡聽到同一個道理，而每種說法都讓這道理更清楚。

Back at the student theater, Kai prepared a small stage for the next event. He had a disagreement with the lighting team about where the audience should stand. One student called his plan stupid. Kai looked hurt but did not answer with a rude remark. He asked them to test both layouts and compare the paths. Mira watched from the door. She was glad he had not made the argument about who was smarter. Later he said it had been difficult to stay calm. She admitted she knew that feeling. Their relationship was changing because they could see each other's weak moments, not because either suddenly became perfect.

回到學生劇場，凱替下一場活動安排小舞臺。他和燈光組對觀眾站位有分歧。一名學生說他的計畫很蠢。凱受了傷，卻沒有用粗魯的話回擊，而是請大家測試兩種配置、比較通道。米拉站在門邊看著，很高興他沒有把爭論變成誰比較聰明的競賽。後來凱承認保持冷靜很難。她說自己明白那種感覺。他們的關係之所以改變，不是因為誰突然變完美，而是因為開始看見彼此脆弱的一面。

That evening, Jo invited the team to her sister's wedding reception. A waiter carried cups between tables while a priest spoke with two guests about the old church near campus. Mira and Kai sat beside a window, relieved to talk about something other than equipment. Jo asked whether they were on a date. Mira said they had agreed to walk by the sea next week, but the wedding was not their date. Kai nodded, respecting her answer. Mira was pleased he did not try to turn a public joke into a private promise. Later, as music filled the room, he asked if she wanted to dance. She did, and they chose a quiet corner away from the crowd.

那天傍晚，喬邀請團隊參加姊姊的婚宴。服務生在桌間端著杯子，一名神父正和兩位客人聊校園附近的老教堂。米拉和凱坐在窗邊，終於能談設備以外的事。喬問他們是不是在約會。米拉說兩人約好下週去海邊散步，但今天的婚宴不是約會。凱點頭，尊重她的回答。米拉很高興他沒有把公開玩笑變成私人承諾。後來音樂響起，凱問她要不要跳舞。她願意，兩人選了人少的角落。

On their way home, they passed a line of trees leading toward the campus forest. Kai said he had enjoyed the dance even though he had missed a step. Mira admitted that she had been nervous too. He did not reach for her hand without asking. Instead he said, “May I?” She gave him her hand for the rest of the walk. They talked about the old lens and the new show, but also about the music they wanted to learn and the cities they hoped to visit. Neither promised forever. The night was enough as it was. Above the trees, stars shone between passing clouds, and Mira did not need a projector to notice them.

回家時，他們經過一排通往校園小森林的樹。凱說雖然踩錯一步，還是很喜歡剛才的舞。米拉承認自己也緊張。他沒有擅自牽她的手，只是問：「可以嗎？」她把手交給他，兩人一路牽著走。他們聊舊鏡片和新演出，也聊想學的音樂與想去的城市。誰都沒有許下永遠的承諾。這一夜本身就很好。樹梢上方，星星在流雲間閃耀，米拉不必靠投影機也能發現它們。

On Monday, Mira shared the visit notes with the club. She asked everyone to remember that the collection was neither a shop nor a theater store. Its job was to care for objects so people in the future could study them. A younger student asked why they could not borrow the lens for one night. Mira showed the pale mark in the report and explained the risk of moving old glass. The student listened and suggested making a larger drawing instead. Jo liked that plan and offered to build a model that visitors could touch. The lens would stay safe, and the lesson could still grow.

星期一米拉和社團分享參訪筆記。她提醒大家典藏室既不是商店，也不是劇場倉庫；它的工作是保管物件，讓未來的人仍能研究。較年輕的社員問為何不能只借鏡片一晚。米拉指出報告裡的泛白記號，解釋搬運老玻璃的風險。社員聽懂了，改提議製作更大的圖。喬很喜歡，願意做讓訪客能摸的模型。鏡片可繼續安全保存，教材也能成長。
