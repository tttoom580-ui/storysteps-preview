獸人村的第一間早餐店 · 第 16 / 25 章 · 1000以下 · 英中對照

A bright morning came after the first week of work. Fia sat inside the red house and read the notes in her book. The shop had changed a great deal, but one matter still worried her: the road by the pond became unsafe in heavy rain. A gray pig named Grandfather Oak visited with a folded paper in his hand. He had worked on boats when he was young and knew the land between the village and the sea. "I heard you want to help people during bad weather," he said. "My old map may help you see where the water goes." Fia pulled out a chair for him.

營業第一週後，迎來一個明亮早晨。菲雅坐在紅屋內讀筆記。小店已改變很多，但仍有一件事讓她擔心：大雨時池塘旁的路會不安全。一隻灰色老豬奧克爺爺帶著摺好的紙來訪。他年輕時在船上工作，熟悉村子和大海之間的地形。「我聽說妳想在壞天氣幫忙，」他說，「這張舊地圖或許能讓妳看清水往哪裡流。」菲雅替他拉出椅子。

The map had many lines. Grandfather Oak put one finger on the pond and moved it to the river. "When rain fills the high ground, water comes down this way," he explained. Fia did not understand every mark, so she asked him to tell her again. He drew a new line on a clean page. "This path stays dry longer," he said. "But you must check it each time. A map is not a promise." Fia wrote that sentence in her book. She had begun to make a habit of asking careful questions rather than guessing. It was a habit she wanted to keep. The weather could change very fast in the hills, so a safe road this morning might need another check by evening.

地圖上有許多線。奧克爺爺用手指從池塘畫向河流。「高地被雨水灌滿時，水就沿這條路流下。」他解釋。菲雅看不懂每個記號，便請他再說一次。他在乾淨書頁上畫出新線。「這條路比較晚淹水，」他說，「但每次都要檢查。地圖不是保證。」菲雅把這句話記進本子。她開始養成仔細問問題、不隨意猜測的習慣，並想一直保持下去。 山裡天氣變化非常快，早上安全的道路，到傍晚可能還得再檢查。

Pip came in carrying a pink flower pot. He had heard about the map and offered to mark safe places with little plant signs, but Toma said signs alone could not make a path safe. They all agreed. Toma would report the road's condition after each heavy rain, while Pip would help keep the garden path clear. Moss said he could hold some dry bread for workers who had to stay late. Fia thanked them, but reminded everyone that the shop was not a rescue station. In a real danger they would call the village helpers and follow their advice. They could support that work without pretending to know everything.

皮普抱著粉紅花盆走進來。他聽說地圖，提議在安全位置放植物小牌，但托馬說光靠標誌不能保證道路安全。大家都同意。托馬會在每次大雨後回報道路情況，皮普負責讓花園小路暢通。莫斯說能替必須晚歸的工人留一些乾麵包。菲雅謝過大家，也提醒：早餐店不是救援站。遇到真正危險，應聯絡村裡的救助人員並聽從指示。他們能協助，而不是假裝什麼都懂。

Near noon, Grandfather Oak asked if he could sing a song from his old days at sea. His voice was low, but the tune was gentle. He sang about returning home when the sky was dark. Fia listened while she set cups on the table. "That is a wonderful song," she said. "My grandmother used to sing when she cooked." The old pig smiled. "Then perhaps this house will have two kinds of food," he said. "Bread for the body and songs for the heart." Fia laughed. She knew music did not fix a road, yet it could make a long wait feel less lonely.

接近中午，奧克爺爺問能不能唱一首年輕時在海邊聽的歌。他的聲音低沉，曲調溫柔，唱的是天黑時回家的故事。菲雅邊擺杯子邊聽。「真是美妙的歌，」她說，「祖母做飯時也常唱歌。」老豬微笑。「那這屋子或許會有兩種食物，」他說，「給身體的麵包，以及給心的歌曲。」菲雅笑了。她知道音樂不能修路，卻能讓漫長等待不那麼寂寞。

In the afternoon, Fia made a new paper for the wall. It held a short list of things to do when rain was strong: check the road with Toma, keep food dry, make sure everybody had a way home, and call for help if needed. She showed it to Edda. Edda liked the plan, but asked who would take care of the shop when Fia was away. Fia paused. She had written many things for other people and almost forgotten herself. She added one more line: "No one goes into deep water alone." Edda nodded. "Now the plan looks better," she said.

下午，菲雅做了一張新告示貼牆，上面列出大雨時的工作：和托馬檢查道路、讓食物保持乾燥、確認大家有回家的方法、必要時求助。她給艾達看。艾達喜歡計畫，卻問菲雅不在時誰負責店。菲雅停住了。她替別人寫了許多事，差點忘了自己。她補上一行：「任何人都不能獨自走進深水。」艾達點頭。「這樣計畫才更完整。」她說。

At closing time, Fia looked at the new page beside the menu. She hoped it would never be needed, but it made her feel calmer. Grandfather Oak stood at the door with his map. "Keep a copy," he told her. "The sea and the hills teach us to look ahead." Fia thanked him and promised to return the original paper safely. She shut the door, then stood under the wooden star for a quiet moment. A good shop was not just a place for sunny mornings. It had to be part of the village on hard days, too.

收工時，菲雅看著菜單旁的新告示。她希望永遠用不到，卻也因準備而安心。奧克爺爺拿著地圖站在門邊。「留一份副本吧，」他說，「大海和山教我們往前看。」菲雅謝謝他，答應把原圖妥善歸還。她關門後，靜靜站在木星星下。好店不只在晴天有用，也要能成為村子艱難日子裡的一部分。
