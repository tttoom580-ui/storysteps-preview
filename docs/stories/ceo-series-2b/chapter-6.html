沒有屋頂的月臺

The Platform Without a Roof

總裁，這張車票不賣給你 · 第 6 / 25 章 · 1000–2000 · 英中對照 · 校訂中

用互動版查詞、朗讀與收藏

On the eve of the western line's annual festival, June arrived at a low station building and found a crowd waiting outside. An official asked her to accept a quick repair plan, but she wanted to examine the roof before signing anything. A young musician played a gentle song near the sidewalk while children gathered around a wooden giraffe. The picture made the station look cheerful, yet the broken tiles above the entrance were still dangerous. June remembered that her first teacher had never let students hide a failed test. The teacher would say that failure was a message, not a mark of shame. She repeated that lesson to the younger staff. Adrian listened, thinking about how often his company had praised a good result without asking whether the method was sound. A changed habit, he knew, had to begin with a changed question.

西線年度節日前夕，雨晴抵達一棟低矮站房，發現一群人在外面等候。一位官員要她接受快速維修計畫，但她堅持先檢查屋頂才簽字。年輕音樂家在人行道旁演奏輕柔樂曲，孩子們圍著木雕長頸鹿。這畫面讓車站顯得熱鬧，可是入口上方破裂的屋瓦依舊危險。 雨晴記得啟蒙老師從不讓學生藏起失敗的測試。老師說，失敗是訊息，不是羞恥的印記。她把這課教給年輕員工。承遠想起公司過去常讚揚好結果，卻不問方法是否可靠。他知道習慣要改，首先得改變提問方式。

June's favorite part of the job was speaking with the people who used a place every day. A woman in a yellow raincoat pointed toward a wet patch and described where water fell during each storm. June took the warning seriously. She had learned that safety reports could be sensitive because someone might fear losing work. Adrian waited until the woman had finished, then promised that the report would be kept and the roof would be examined by an outside expert. A worker at the back of the room raised a hand and then slowly lowered it. June invited the question without calling attention to the worker's fear. The answer was not ready, so she promised to return with evidence, not with a guess. Adrian saw how much courage it took to admit that a question was still open. People seemed to trust them more, not less, for it.

雨晴最喜歡的工作，是與每天使用場地的人交談。一名穿黃雨衣的女子指向潮濕地面，描述每次暴風雨時水會從哪裡滴落。雨晴認真記下提醒。她知道安全報告常牽涉敏感問題，因為有人可能怕失去工作。承遠等女子說完，才承諾保留報告，並請外部專家檢查屋頂。 房間後方一名工人舉手又慢慢放下。雨晴邀請他提問，沒有特別指出他的害怕。答案尚未備妥，她承諾帶證據回來，而不是猜測。承遠看見承認問題仍無解也需要勇氣。人們似乎因此更信任他們，而非更少。

Inside the ticket office, a retired teacher kept a book of local poetry. One poem described a long war that had once closed the valley route. June read the words aloud. The railway had carried food and letters for generations; its soul was not the expensive lamps in the first-class cars. Adrian listened without making a speech about his company. For the first time he understood why the western line mattered to people who would never buy a luxury seat. The team put a date beside every promise. June also asked for the name of the person who would do the work, not only the person who approved the money. Adrian wrote both names on the plan. He found that clear responsibility made difficult choices less personal. When something went wrong, people could return to the task instead of arguing about who had looked most confident in a meeting.

售票處裡，一名退休老師保存著地方詩集。一首詩描寫多年前戰爭如何讓山谷路線中斷。雨晴朗讀詩句。鐵道世代運送食物與信件，它的靈魂不是頭等車廂裡昂貴的燈。承遠安靜聆聽，沒有發表公司演說。他第一次明白西線對那些永遠買不起豪華座位的人有何意義。 團隊在每項承諾旁寫下日期。雨晴還要求寫出實際執行者，而不只核准經費者。承遠把兩人的名字都寫上。他發現清楚責任讓困難選擇不再那麼私人化。出問題時，大家能回頭處理任務，而不是爭論會議上誰看起來最有自信。

The district manager proposed a new term for the public station contract: local residents would help choose repair priorities. Adrian agreed to discuss it, but June reminded him that a promise needed a date and a responsible person. They walked toward the damaged platform while workers marked a safe path with bright tape. A child asked if the train would return tomorrow. Adrian answered that he hoped so, but only after the roof and brakes were ready. The day had been full of sharp questions. When the work finally paused, Adrian offered tea without asking for an answer about their private lives. June accepted the cup and looked at the quiet station. She was beginning to enjoy his company, but she would not confuse comfort with consent to anything more. He seemed to understand. Sometimes respect was visible in the questions a person did not ask.

區域經理提出公共車站合約的新條款：讓當地居民協助決定維修優先順序。承遠同意討論，雨晴卻提醒承諾要有日期和負責人。他們朝受損月臺走去，工人用鮮明膠帶標示安全通道。一個孩子問火車明天會不會回來。承遠說他也希望如此，但得等屋頂與煞車都安全。 這一天充滿尖銳問題。工作終於暫停時，承遠端來茶，沒有要求她回答私人關係。雨晴接下杯子，望著安靜車站。她開始享受他的陪伴，但不會把舒服誤認為同意更進一步。他似乎懂了。有時尊重，正表現在一個人沒有追問的問題裡。

At night June sat under the temporary shelter and reviewed photographs. Adrian returned with tea, set it on the table, and asked whether she would like company. She said yes, but she would not discuss the unfinished report off the record. He laughed softly. "Then let's talk about the music," he said. They listened to the festival rehearsal beyond the fence. For a little while, neither of them had to be the person with the final answer.

夜裡，雨晴坐在臨時棚下檢查照片。承遠端茶回來，放在桌上，問她願不願意有人陪。她說可以，但不會私下談未完成的報告。他輕聲笑道：「那就聊音樂吧。」兩人隔著圍欄聽節日彩排。有那麼一會兒，他們都不必扮演擁有最後答案的人。
