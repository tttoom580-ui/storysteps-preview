村莊的試車

The Village Test

總裁，這張車票不賣給你 · 第 10 / 25 章 · 1000–2000 · 英中對照 · 校訂中

The first public test was available only to trained workers, not passengers. June carried a meter to check the length of the brake response, and Leo measured each unit twice. A fox watched them from a hill while a goose crossed the track beside the farm. June asked everyone to gather at a safe point and ignore the animal noise until the test ended. Proper rules mattered especially when the place looked peaceful enough to invite carelessness. The railway looked different when June stood beside a worker instead of looking down from a meeting room. She could hear where the floor shook and see which signs were difficult to read. Adrian joined her without bringing a crowd of assistants. He was beginning to learn why the place where a decision was made could change the decision itself.

第一次公開測試只開放受訓工人，不載旅客。雨晴帶著測量儀檢查煞車反應距離，里歐每個單位都量兩次。山坡上一隻狐狸望著他們，農場旁還有一隻鵝走過鐵軌。雨晴要大家在安全地點集合，測試結束前別受動物叫聲干擾。地方越平靜，越需要遵守正式規則，以免疏忽。 當雨晴站在工人身旁，而不是從會議室往下看，鐵道便顯得不同。她能聽出地板哪裡震動，也看見哪些標示難讀。承遠沒帶一群助理，直接加入她。他開始明白，做決定的地點，也會改變決定本身。

The station café offered biscuit and roasted yam to volunteers. Children made a musical greeting for the workers, and a small sign said congratulations to everyone who had helped with repairs. Adrian thanked them but did not announce a victory. A gradual improvement was good news, yet a successful test was not the same as a license to carry paying customers. He asked June to explain the difference in simple language, and she did so without treating any question as foolish. At the end of the conversation, June asked whether anyone had understood the instruction differently. Two people gave different answers. Instead of blaming them, she rewrote the sentence in shorter words. Adrian copied the new line into his notes. He was surprised that clearer language could save time later. The workers were relieved to know that asking for a clearer rule did not make them look foolish.

車站咖啡館替志工準備餅乾和烤地瓜。孩子們用歌聲向工人致意，一塊小牌子寫著祝賀每位參與修復的人。承遠向他們道謝，卻沒有宣告勝利。逐步改善是好消息，但一次成功測試不等於取得載客許可。他請雨晴用簡單的話解釋差別，她沒有把任何疑問當成愚蠢。 談話結束時，雨晴問有沒有人對指示有不同理解。兩人給出不同答案。她沒有責怪，而是用更短的字重寫句子。承遠把新版抄進筆記。他驚訝於清楚語言能在之後節省時間。員工也鬆了口氣，知道要求說清楚不會被當成愚蠢。

A technician brought glue for a broken sign, then noticed a snail moving across the test board. June laughed and carefully moved the board away. She checked whether the instrument's style of display could confuse a tired operator. The numbers were correct, but their color made a warning hard to read. Adrian approved a clearer design and asked for the cost per station. For the first time his questions about price helped the repair rather than reducing it. When the room grew tired, June asked for a short break. Adrian agreed before anyone complained. They returned with fresh eyes and found a small error they had missed. It was not a dramatic moment, but it changed the outcome. June wrote the correction in the record, and Adrian signed beside it. The most important part was not that they had been right; it was that they could correct themselves.

技師拿膠水修破招牌，卻看見一隻蝸牛正爬過測試板。雨晴笑著小心移開板子。她檢查儀器顯示數字的樣式，是否會讓疲累的操作員看錯。數字正確，顏色卻讓警告不易辨識。承遠同意換成更清楚的設計，並詢問每站成本。第一次，他對價格的提問是在幫助修復，而非削減它。 大家疲倦時，雨晴要求短暫休息。承遠在有人抱怨前就同意。重新開始後，大家用清醒的眼睛發現一處先前漏掉的小錯。這不是戲劇化時刻，卻改變了結果。雨晴記下更正，承遠在旁簽字。最重要的不是一開始正確，而是能夠改正。

At lunch Leo joked that June needed a vacation more than another form. She admitted he was right, but said she would rest after the next independent review. Adrian surprised her by asking if he could join the village walk instead of leaving by private car. She agreed on one condition: he would listen to residents, not give them a sales talk. The walk took longer than planned because people had stories, worries, and the courage to share useful ideas about the line. Adrian had spent years believing that solving a problem meant being the first person to speak. June showed him another way: stay long enough to understand the person who would live with the answer. He did not always find it easy. Yet the quiet effort changed how he saw both the railway and the woman standing beside him. He hoped she could notice that change without feeling asked to reward it.

午餐時里歐開玩笑說，雨晴需要的是休假，不是另一張表。她承認他說得對，但要等下一次獨立審查後再休息。承遠讓她意外地提出想參加村莊步行，而非搭私人車離開。她答應，但有一個條件：他要聽居民說話，不是去推銷。這趟散步比預計久，因為居民有故事、擔憂，也有勇氣分享對鐵道有用的想法。 承遠多年來相信，解決問題就是第一個開口說話。雨晴讓他看到另一條路：留下來，理解必須承受答案的人。他做起來並不總是容易。可是這份安靜的努力，改變了他看鐵道與身旁女子的方式。他希望她能看見改變，卻不覺得有義務因此獎勵他。

As the evening train passed on a different line, June looked at the quiet test carriage. It was not ready for travelers, but it was closer than it had been. Adrian stood a respectful distance away. He asked whether she believed he could change the company. "I don't know," she said. "But today you let people finish their sentences. That is a start." He smiled, as if a small sign of trust meant more than another award on his office wall.

傍晚另一條線的列車經過時，雨晴看著安靜的測試車廂。它還不能載客，但已比從前更接近目標。承遠站在適當距離，問她是否相信他能改變公司。「我不知道，」她說，「但今天你讓每個人把話說完。這是個開始。」他笑了，彷彿這一點信任比辦公室牆上的獎牌更重要。
