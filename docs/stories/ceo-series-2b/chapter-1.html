黎明的紅色停駛牌

The Red Tag at Dawn

總裁，這張車票不賣給你 · 第 1 / 25 章 · 1000–2000 · 英中對照 · 校訂中

用互動版查詞、朗讀與收藏

June Lin reached the station before sunrise, carrying a test case instead of a handbag. A man with a neat beard hurried out of the platform café and nearly stepped on a tiny green leaf. Behind him, a passenger in blue pajamas asked why the famous Starlight train had not opened. June had placed a red safety tag across its door. She was twenty-eight, an independent engineer, and her answer was simple: no one would board until the brakes passed a fair test. June kept a second notebook for questions that nobody could answer yet. She believed an empty space on a page was better than a number invented to look complete. Adrian had once disliked such spaces. Now he asked which question should be checked first. Their new way of working was slower, but it left a path that another person could follow.

林雨晴天亮前便帶著測試箱抵達車站，而不是提著手提包。一名鬍鬚修整整齊的男子匆匆走出月臺咖啡館，差點踩到一片細小的綠葉。他身後有位穿藍色睡衣的旅客，問為什麼著名的星光號還沒開門。雨晴已在車門掛上紅色停駛牌。她二十八歲，是獨立工程師；她的回答很簡單：煞車通過公平測試前，任何人都不能上車。 雨晴另備一本筆記，專門寫下暫時無人能回答的問題。她認為紙上留白，總比捏造數字看似完整更好。承遠以前不喜歡這種空白，如今卻問哪個問題該先查。他們的新工作方式比較慢，卻留下了其他人也能跟著走的清楚路徑。

She showed the station manager the cracked rubber ring that should protect the brake line. A strange chemical smell rose from the part, and her small instrument showed a rising temperature. The gardener who cared for the station flowers said he had noticed the same smell after yesterday's cleaning. June wrote down his name. A worker's memory was not final proof, but it was a reason to examine the equipment before anyone tried to move the train. Someone had prepared a bright sign promising good news. June asked that it stay folded until the workers checked the facts. A young assistant seemed disappointed; everyone wanted something to celebrate. Adrian spoke to the assistant gently and explained that good news was worth sharing only when it was true. The team could still take pride in its effort, even while another test remained unfinished.

她向站長展示本應保護煞車管路、卻已裂開的橡膠圈。零件散發奇怪的化學氣味，她的小儀器也顯示溫度正在上升。照顧車站花草的園丁說，昨天清潔後他就聞過同樣的味道。雨晴記下他的姓名。工人的記憶還不是最終證據，卻足以成為檢查設備的理由，不能貿然開車。 有人備好一張宣告好消息的鮮豔牌子。雨晴要求先收起來，等工人核對事實。年輕助理顯得失望；大家都想慶祝。承遠溫和地解釋，好消息只有真實才值得分享。即使另一項測試尚未完成，團隊仍可為自己的努力感到驕傲。

Adrian Chu arrived in a plain dark coat. At thirty-four, he was the president of Aurora Rail and the man newspapers called the cold king of trains. He watched two porters divide a heavy box of watermelon for the waiting staff. June asked them to weigh the box before moving it; the loading figure on the sheet was wrong. Adrian studied her careful work. He had expected an argument about money. Instead, he found an engineer who treated every small number as a person's safety. June placed the old plan beside the new one so the team could see each change. A number without a reason was not enough. Adrian asked one worker to explain the plan in ordinary language. When the worker became confused, they found another line that needed repair. It was a useful test of the words themselves, before those words guided a real action.

三十四歲的楚承遠穿著樸素深色外套抵達。他是極光鐵道公司的總裁，報紙稱他為「冷面列車王」。他看見兩名搬運工將一箱沉重的西瓜分給候車人員。雨晴先請他們秤重再搬；清單上的載重數字有誤。承遠仔細看著她的工作。他本以為會有一場金錢爭執，沒想到遇見一位把每個小數字都當成人命看待的工程師。 雨晴把舊計畫與新計畫並排，讓團隊看見每項修改。只有數字沒有理由還不夠。承遠請一名工人用日常語言解釋計畫。工人說到一半困惑，大家便發現另一句需要修改。這是在文字真正指導行動前，對文字本身進行的有用測試。

"Can you do me one favor?" Adrian asked. "Remove the tag while my guests are here." June refused. A delayed trip could lead to a tax problem or a public complaint, but ignoring a known danger could become a crime. She could relate to his worry about the company, yet his worry could not replace a test. She handed him a written report and told him the same rules would apply to a cheap train and a luxury train. Adrian's world was full of rooms where people agreed with him too quickly. Beside June, he found no easy praise. She could be warm and still say that his plan was wrong. He did not know whether she would ever love him, and he was learning not to turn that uncertainty into a demand. His care for her would mean little if it cost her independence.

「能幫我一個忙嗎？」承遠問。「我的貴賓還在，先把停駛牌拿掉。」雨晴拒絕了。延誤旅程可能帶來稅務問題或客訴，但明知危險仍置之不理，可能構成犯罪。她理解他對公司的擔心，可是擔心不能代替測試。她交給他書面報告，說明廉價列車和豪華列車都適用同樣的規則。 承遠的世界充滿太快同意他的人。雨晴身旁沒有輕易的讚美。她可以溫柔，同時指出他的方案有錯。他不知道她將來是否會愛他，也在學著不把不確定轉成要求。如果他的關心要以她的獨立為代價，那便沒有多少價值。

Adrian's usual personality was calm and hard to read. This morning he felt something less familiar than anger: respect. June had a strange feeling that the proud CEO was listening, not only waiting for a chance to speak. She disliked such public meetings, where a camera could turn a careful sentence into a sharp headline. Still, she explained each result to the staff, not just to the boss. Her job was to protect the people who had no place at the boardroom table.

承遠平日個性冷靜，情緒很難看透。今天早上，他感受到比憤怒更陌生的東西：尊重。雨晴有種奇妙的感覺，這位驕傲的總裁正在聽，而不是只等著發言。她不喜歡這種公開場合，因為鏡頭可能把審慎的一句話剪成尖銳標題。儘管如此，她仍向全體員工說明結果，而不只對老闆報告。她的工作是保護那些進不了董事會的人。

By eight, the first trip was canceled, and the passengers received clear choices about refunds. Adrian signed the notice himself. When June thanked the tired station workers, he stood beside her without trying to take credit. "Tomorrow," he said, "show me every test we need." She answered that she would show him the evidence, not a way around it. For the first time, the red tag felt less like the end of a journey and more like the beginning of a difficult partnership.

八點時，首班車確定取消，旅客也得到清楚的退費選項。承遠親自簽署通知。雨晴向疲累的站務員致謝時，他站在一旁，沒有搶功。「明天，」他說，「帶我看每一項必要的測試。」她回答，她會展示證據，而不是教他規避規定。第一次，那張紅色停駛牌不像旅程的終點，反而像一段艱難合作的起點。
