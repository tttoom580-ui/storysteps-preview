消失的一頁

The Missing Page

總裁，這張車票不賣給你 · 第 9 / 25 章 · 1000–2000 · 英中對照 · 校訂中

A local shopkeeper gave June an old newspaper article about the railway's first winter. The historical section included a painting of the station, a puppy sleeping beside the clock, and a bright yellow crayon mark around a door. June studied the picture because the mark showed an entrance missing from new plans. Her partner Leo checked the paper against the daily repair book. One chapter of that book had been torn away, leaving only two nails in its cover. There were photographs, numbers, and stories on the table, but they were not the same kind of evidence. June marked what she had measured herself and what another person had reported. Adrian asked for both to be kept. It was easier to make a strong speech from one neat answer. It was harder, and more useful, to show where the answer still had limits.

當地店主給雨晴一篇關於鐵道第一個冬天的舊報紙文章。歷史版面刊著車站畫作、一隻睡在鐘旁的小狗，以及用黃色蠟筆圈出的門。雨晴研究圖片，因為標記指出新版圖上消失的入口。搭檔里歐把報紙與每日維修簿核對。維修簿其中一章被撕走，封面只剩兩顆釘子。 桌上有照片、數字與故事，但它們不是同一種證據。雨晴標明哪些是自己測量的，哪些是他人通報的。承遠要求兩種都保留。從一個整齊答案編出有力演說比較容易；指出答案仍有哪些限制更難，卻更有用。

Adrian wanted the company to succeed, but he had begun to fear what success built on false records would cost. June said a legal review was necessary before anyone made a public claim. In addition, she would not paste guesses into the missing section and call it a record. A worker brought them a small tube of repair paste from the storage room, thinking it might help the torn cover. June thanked him and said the book should remain untouched until an archive expert examined it. The younger workers were careful around Adrian because they knew he could change their jobs with one decision. June did not ask them to forget that power. She asked him to give them a written way to report danger without punishment. He agreed. Trust grew more easily when people had rules to protect them, rather than having to depend on a boss's good mood.

承遠希望公司成功，但他開始害怕建立在假紀錄上的成功會有什麼代價。雨晴說，在公開指控前必須進行法律檢視。此外，她不會把猜測黏進遺失的章節就稱為紀錄。一名工人從倉庫拿來小管修補膠，認為能修好封面。雨晴向他道謝，說檔案專家檢查前應保持原狀。 年輕員工在承遠身邊格外小心，因為知道他一個決定就能改變自己的工作。雨晴沒有要他們忘記這種權力，而是請承遠提供書面通報管道，讓人不必怕遭報復。他同意。當規則能保護人，而不是只能依靠老闆心情，信任才更容易成長。

The painting was brighter than the present hall. Sunlight seemed to shine through a window that was now covered by a wall. Leo found a system of old plans in the city library, filed under a different street name. June traced the changes carefully. Unless she could show who made each change and when, the drawings would be interesting but not enough to prove a safety failure. Adrian asked for copies with dates, not pretty images for a speech. The people checking the work had to be free to say no. June explained this without anger, as a simple fact about a useful inspection. Adrian agreed that a signed contract should protect that freedom. He had the legal office write the rule down and sent it to the workers. Nobody could claim later that an honest warning had broken the team's trust.

畫裡的大廳比現在明亮。陽光似乎從如今被牆封住的窗照進來。里歐在市立圖書館找到一套舊圖，卻歸在不同街名底下。雨晴仔細追查修改。若無法證明每次修改者與日期，這些圖雖有趣，卻不足以證明安全失誤。承遠要求附日期的影本，而不是演講用的漂亮圖片。 檢查工作的人必須有說「不」的自由。雨晴不帶怒氣地解釋，這是有用檢查的基本事實。承遠同意用書面合約保護此自由，請法務室寫明並交給工人。以後沒有人能說誠實警告破壞了團隊信任。

That afternoon a director telephoned Adrian and told him to make the story disappear. He said another delay would be worse than a small mistake. Adrian refused. The call ended abruptly, and June could see that he was frightened. She did not comfort him by saying everything would be fine. Instead she asked what evidence the board had seen and offered to prepare a fair report for all sides. Their partnership grew stronger because neither person needed to pretend the problem was easy. Neither of them mentioned the future while the day's work continued. June had a report to finish, and Adrian had people to answer to. Their different responsibilities did not disappear because they liked one another. If anything, the growing feeling made honest boundaries more important. They walked together for a short distance, then returned to their separate duties without anger or a promise they could not keep.

下午一名董事打電話給承遠，要他讓這件事消失，說再次延誤會比小錯更糟。承遠拒絕。電話突然掛斷，雨晴看得出他害怕。她沒有用「一切會好的」安慰他，而是詢問董事會看過哪些證據，並提議為各方準備公平報告。他們的合作更穩固，因為兩人都不必假裝問題很簡單。 白天工作還在進行，兩人都沒談未來。雨晴有報告要完成，承遠有需要交代的人。即使互有好感，不同責任也不會消失。恰恰相反，感情越深，誠實界線越重要。他們同行一小段路，便各自回到職務上，沒有憤怒，也沒有無法兌現的承諾。

As dusk fell, the shopkeeper returned with a second clipping. It named the designer who had objected to closing the old exit. June placed both papers in a sealed folder with the dates written outside. Adrian looked at the little pile and said, "I used to think a large company was strong because it could move quickly." June answered, "Sometimes strength means stopping long enough to look at one missing page." He nodded, knowing she was talking about more than paper.

黃昏時店主又帶來另一張剪報，上面寫著曾反對封閉舊出口的設計師姓名。雨晴把兩份剪報放進封口資料夾，外面寫上日期。承遠看著那一小疊紙說：「我以前以為大公司強大，是因為動作快。」雨晴回答：「有時強大是願意停下來，看清少掉的一頁。」他點頭，知道她說的不只是紙。
