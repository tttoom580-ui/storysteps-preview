The Map with Two Dates

總裁，這張車票不賣給你 · 第 3 / 25 章 · 1000–2000 · 英中對照 · 校訂中

The next morning was cloudy. June made an attempt to trace the cause of a weak brake signal, beginning at the eastern entrance. Her old classmate Leo, now a repair worker, arrived on a scooter with two boxes of parts. He offered her cereal from a paper cup, but she was too busy reading the records. Someone had tried to cheat a routine check by copying the final number from an earlier form. The copied figure was neat, yet it did not match the machine. There were photographs, numbers, and stories on the table, but they were not the same kind of evidence. June marked what she had measured herself and what another person had reported. Adrian asked for both to be kept. It was easier to make a strong speech from one neat answer. It was harder, and more useful, to show where the answer still had limits.

隔天早晨多雲。雨晴從東側入口開始，試圖追查煞車訊號微弱的原因。她的老同學、如今當維修員的里歐騎機車載來兩箱零件。他拿紙杯裝的麥片請她吃，但她忙著讀紀錄。有人把先前表格的最終數字抄到例行檢查表，企圖作弊。抄寫得很整齊，數字卻與機器不符。 桌上有照片、數字與故事，但它們不是同一種證據。雨晴標明哪些是自己測量的，哪些是他人通報的。承遠要求兩種都保留。從一個整齊答案編出有力演說比較容易；指出答案仍有哪些限制更難，卻更有用。

Leo noticed a silver earring near the test desk and put it in the lost-property box. A runner from the station office brought a second record, this one marked with a government number. It listed a general safety rule: no officer could force an inspector to change a result. Adrian read it twice and gave a quiet nod. He told Leo to continue the repair but not to operate the brake pump until June had checked the supply line herself. The younger workers were careful around Adrian because they knew he could change their jobs with one decision. June did not ask them to forget that power. She asked him to give them a written way to report danger without punishment. He agreed. Trust grew more easily when people had rules to protect them, rather than having to depend on a boss's good mood.

里歐在測試桌附近看見一只銀耳環，便放進失物箱。站務室的跑腿員送來第二份紀錄，上面有政府文件編號，寫著一般安全規則：任何主管不得強迫檢查員更改結果。承遠讀了兩遍，安靜地點頭。他告訴里歐繼續維修，但雨晴親自檢查供應管線之前，不准啟動煞車泵。 年輕員工在承遠身邊格外小心，因為知道他一個決定就能改變自己的工作。雨晴沒有要他們忘記這種權力，而是請承遠提供書面通報管道，讓人不必怕遭報復。他同意。當規則能保護人，而不是只能依靠老闆心情，信任才更容易成長。

The two reports seemed to link to different weeks. June examined the dates and found that the replacement part had been recorded before the company had ordered it. That was not a small mistake. Adrian had always believed a liberal policy of trusting local teams would make work faster. June said trust was useful only when records were clear enough to check. He understood that giving people freedom did not mean leaving them alone with unsafe tools or impossible orders. The people checking the work had to be free to say no. June explained this without anger, as a simple fact about a useful inspection. Adrian agreed that a signed contract should protect that freedom. He had the legal office write the rule down and sent it to the workers. Nobody could claim later that an honest warning had broken the team's trust.

兩份報告似乎指向不同星期。雨晴檢查日期，發現更換零件的紀錄竟早於公司下單的時間。這不是小錯誤。承遠一直相信寬鬆的管理政策能讓地方團隊做事更快。雨晴說，只有紀錄清楚到可以核對，信任才有用。他明白給人自由不等於讓人獨自面對不安全的工具與不可能完成的命令。 檢查工作的人必須有說「不」的自由。雨晴不帶怒氣地解釋，這是有用檢查的基本事實。承遠同意用書面合約保護此自由，請法務室寫明並交給工人。以後沒有人能說誠實警告破壞了團隊信任。

A clerk said the old records might be in the locked archive. Adrian could use his position to order the door opened, but June asked him to make a written request first. The difference mattered: a secret search would let anyone question the evidence later. They drafted the request together. Adrian was surprised to find that June could disagree firmly without insulting anyone. She was equally surprised when he accepted her correction in front of his own staff. Neither of them mentioned the future while the day's work continued. June had a report to finish, and Adrian had people to answer to. Their different responsibilities did not disappear because they liked one another. If anything, the growing feeling made honest boundaries more important. They walked together for a short distance, then returned to their separate duties without anger or a promise they could not keep.

一名職員說舊紀錄可能在上鎖的檔案室。承遠可以憑職權命人開門，但雨晴請他先提出書面申請。這個差別很重要：私下搜查會讓別人日後質疑證據。他們一起擬好申請。承遠驚訝地發現，雨晴能堅定反對卻不羞辱任何人；雨晴也驚訝於他願意在員工面前接受自己的指正。 白天工作還在進行，兩人都沒談未來。雨晴有報告要完成，承遠有需要交代的人。即使互有好感，不同責任也不會消失。恰恰相反，感情越深，誠實界線越重要。他們同行一小段路，便各自回到職務上，沒有憤怒，也沒有無法兌現的承諾。

Outside, the clouds lifted enough to reveal the line of hills beyond the station. June told Adrian that she would follow the facts even if they led to his own office. "Good," he said, although the word cost him something. "And if they lead to a worker with no power, we will listen before we judge." Leo watched them from the doorway and laughed. "You two agree on the most difficult things," he said, "and argue about how to say good morning."

屋外雲層稍稍散開，露出車站外的山線。雨晴告訴承遠，即使線索指向他的辦公室，她也會追到底。「很好。」他說，儘管這個字讓他很難出口。「如果線索指向沒有權力的員工，我們也要先聽再判斷。」里歐從門口看著兩人，笑說：「你們在最難的事情上有共識，卻會為怎麼說早安吵架。」
