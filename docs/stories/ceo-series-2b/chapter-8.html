小箱子的重量

The Weight of a Small Box

總裁，這張車票不賣給你 · 第 8 / 25 章 · 1000–2000 · 英中對照 · 校訂中

用互動版查詞、朗讀與收藏

June's childhood had been full of slow trains and family meals. She remembered eating barbecue with chopsticks while her father told stories about distant stations. Today she stood beside a loading car, checking whether the average weight on a form matched reality. Adrian's team had been given a command to move every box before noon. June stopped that order. A heavy load could disappear from a report, but its real weight would still affect the brakes. The first person to notice trouble was rarely the person with the highest title. June knew this from years spent beside noisy machines. She asked small questions in a calm voice and wrote down the answers without changing their meaning. Adrian watched her and understood that listening was a kind of work. A meeting did not become useful simply because its leader spoke well.

雨晴的童年充滿慢車與家庭聚餐。她記得吃烤肉、用筷子夾菜時，父親常說起遠方車站。今天她站在行李車旁，核對表格上的平均重量與實際是否相符。承遠的團隊接到中午前搬完所有箱子的命令。雨晴叫停。沉重載荷可以從報表上消失，卻不會因此不影響煞車。 最早發現問題的人，通常不是頭銜最高的人。雨晴多年待在吵雜機器旁，深知這點。她平靜提出細小問題，並原意記下答案。承遠看著她，明白傾聽也是工作。會議不會只因領導者口才好，就自動變得有用。

A gentle worker named Sami explained the meaning of a faded mark on an old shelf. It pointed to a source of extra weight: repair stones kept in a wooden box. The box held a walking stick too, left by a former station servant many years ago. Adrian asked whom it belonged to, and Sami explained that the family had donated it to a small local history room. June asked for permission before moving anything. A cleaner stopped by to ask whether the workers could keep a clear path to the door. June thanked her and moved the boxes. Adrian noticed that a useful safety change could start with the person who cleaned the floor, not a director. Later he asked the cleaner to join the next planning meeting. She smiled, surprised that someone had remembered the question.

名叫薩米的溫和工人解釋舊架上褪色記號的意義。那記號指向額外重量的來源：裝在木箱裡的修路石塊。箱中還有一根手杖，是多年前一位前站務僕役留下的。承遠問手杖屬於誰，薩米說那家人已將它捐給地方歷史室。雨晴在搬動任何物品前先徵得同意。 一名清潔員來問能否保持通往門口的道路暢通。雨晴道謝並移開箱子。承遠注意到，安全改善可以從掃地的人開始，不一定要靠董事。後來他邀請清潔員參與下一次規劃會議。她驚訝有人記得自己的問題，露出笑容。

"Whatever the old plan says, we need a safe means of moving this case," June said. She suggested dividing the objects and moving the stone box separately. Adrian agreed, though the delay meant losing a morning of work. The men pushed the cart forward slowly. For June, the small decision represented a larger reality: a powerful order could not change the physical limits of a machine. The railway would be safe only when its plans matched the world outside the office. An assistant wanted to mark the job complete as soon as the first measurement looked good. June asked for the second reading. It was slightly different. They discussed the difference and noted the conditions of both tests. Adrian said they should keep the first result as well. The purpose of a record was to remember what had happened, not only what people wished had happened.

「不管舊計畫怎麼寫，我們都需要安全搬運箱子的方式。」雨晴說。她建議把物件分開，石箱另行搬運。承遠同意，雖然因此耽誤了一上午。工人緩慢地把車推向前方。對雨晴而言，這小決定反映更大的現實：有力的命令無法改變機器的物理限制。鐵道唯有讓計畫符合辦公室外的世界，才可能安全。 一名助理看到第一次量測結果良好，就想把工作標成完成。雨晴要求再量一次，結果略有不同。他們討論差異，記下兩次測試條件。承遠說第一次結果也要保留。紀錄的目的在記住真正發生的事，而不只是人們希望發生的事。

They visited the history room and learned why the stones had been saved. Long ago, local families had repaired the line with their own tools when a flood cut the valley off. Some had called it fate; others had simply worked until a bridge stood again. Adrian noticed that nobody in the story had waited for a wealthy stranger to rescue them. June smiled. "That is why people need the right to help decide what happens to this line," she said. June closed her notebook and noticed how tired everyone looked. In earlier years she might have stayed until the lights went out, afraid a small mistake would be blamed on her. Now she had colleagues who could share the load. Adrian understood that helping did not mean taking her work away. It meant making enough room for her to do it well and to rest afterward.

兩人參觀歷史室，得知那些石頭為何被留下。很久以前，洪水切斷山谷聯外道路，當地家庭便用自己的工具修復鐵道。有人說那是命運，也有人只是一直工作，直到橋重新立起。承遠注意到故事中沒有人等著有錢的陌生人來拯救。雨晴笑說：「所以居民需要有權決定這條線的未來。」 雨晴合上筆記本，看見眾人疲累的模樣。早年她或許會留到熄燈，怕小錯被算到自己頭上。如今有同事能分擔。承遠也明白幫忙不是把她工作搶走，而是給她足夠空間做好工作，做完還能休息。

At dinner Adrian asked June about her father. She told him a little and kept the rest private. He did not press. Instead he spoke about a time when his own family had valued winning contracts more than making friends. The conversation was quiet and real. When he walked her back to the platform, he asked if she might join him for dinner again after the investigation. She answered, "Ask me when neither of us is trying to change a report."

晚餐時承遠問起雨晴的父親。她說了一部分，其他保留。他沒有追問，只談到自己的家庭曾把贏得合約看得比交朋友更重要。談話安靜而真誠。送她回月臺時，他問等調查結束後能否再一起吃飯。她回答：「等我們都不需要更動報告時，再問我一次。」
