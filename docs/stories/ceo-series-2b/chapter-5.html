雪聲之下

The Sound Beneath the Snow

總裁，這張車票不賣給你 · 第 5 / 25 章 · 1000–2000 · 英中對照 · 校訂中

A sudden snowy morning changed the team's plans. June had little appetite, but Adrian insisted they stop for hot noodle soup before inspecting the roof. He did not try to control her day; he simply pointed out that tired people made errors. She laughed and asked him to pay attention to his own breakfast as well. Outside, thunder rolled above the hills, and a strange smell seemed to spread from an electrical box near the station stairs. A mistake on a quiet day could be a warning about danger on a busy one. June tried to treat each warning as a chance to learn, not as a reason to search for an enemy. Adrian asked the team to keep earlier versions of their plans. If a decision changed, readers needed to know what had changed and why. That simple rule protected honest workers.

一個突然下雪的早晨打亂團隊計畫。雨晴沒什麼食慾，但承遠堅持先去喝碗熱麵湯，再檢查屋頂。他不是要控制她的行程，只提醒疲倦的人容易出錯。她笑著要他也留意自己的早餐。外面山上雷聲隆隆，站內樓梯附近的電箱似乎散發奇怪氣味。 安靜日子裡的小錯，可能是在提醒忙碌時的危險。雨晴把每次警告視為學習機會，而非尋找敵人的理由。承遠要求團隊保留計畫的舊版本。若決策改變，讀者需要知道改了什麼、為何而改。這條簡單規則保護了誠實的工人。

Leo examined the electrical connection with June. They discovered wool caught behind the cover, perhaps carried there by birds from a nearby farm. A rooster cried in the distance. Leo wore gloves to protect his skin from the sharp edge of the cover. The tiny nest itself was harmless, but damp wool against a damaged wire could be dangerous. June used a careful method: she cut the power, photographed the scene, and asked two workers to watch each step. Adrian offered support, not an order to work faster. A visitor asked whether the rich passengers would still get special treatment. Adrian answered that comfortable seats could cost more, but the right to a safe journey could not. June nodded. She did not expect everyone to have the same ticket, only the same basic protection. The visitor thanked them for giving a direct answer. That small exchange made the discussion less like an advertisement and more like public work.

里歐和雨晴一起檢查電線接點。他們發現外蓋後卡著羊毛，也許是鳥從附近農場帶來的。遠處傳來公雞啼叫。里歐戴著手套，保護皮膚不被外蓋的尖角割傷。小巢本身無害，但潮濕羊毛碰到破損電線會很危險。雨晴採用謹慎的方法：切斷電源、拍照存證，再請兩名工人見證每一步。承遠給的是支援，而不是催促的命令。 一名訪客問富裕旅客是否仍有特別待遇。承遠回答，舒適座位可以賣得更貴，但安全旅程的權利不能分級。雨晴點頭。她不期待所有人拿同樣車票，只期待享有同樣的基本保護。訪客感謝他們直接回答。這段小交流使討論不像廣告，更像真正的公共工作。

A brilliant young worker suggested that a domestic heating unit might explain the fault. June praised the idea but asked for a test instead of a guess. The machine drew too much power only when many lamps were on at once. That pattern helped the team separate one problem from another. Adrian saw why good engineers welcomed conflict between ideas: when people could question a theory openly, they could find mistakes before a passenger suffered. Before signing, June read the important lines aloud. She asked someone who had not prepared the document to check the numbers. Adrian had once thought such steps showed a lack of confidence. Now he understood they were a way of respecting everyone who would depend on the result. A careful process might feel slow in the moment, but it could prevent a much longer delay.

一位聰明的年輕工人猜測家用暖氣設備可能造成故障。雨晴稱讚他的想法，但要求測試，不接受猜測。機器只有在很多燈同時亮起時才會耗電過量。這個模式協助團隊把不同問題分開。承遠明白好工程師為何容許意見衝突：人們能公開質疑理論，才能在旅客受傷前發現錯誤。 簽字前，雨晴大聲讀出重要條款，請未參與起草的人再核對數字。承遠以前以為這些程序表示缺乏信心，如今明白這是尊重所有將依賴結果的人。當下審慎程序可能顯得慢，卻有機會避免更長的延誤。

At lunch they reviewed a mass of old electrical records. June learned that a repair team had reported the same issue last winter, but no one had followed up. She said the missing connection between reports was as dangerous as the broken wire. Adrian called the manager and requested a written history, including whenever the alarms had sounded. He did not ask for a name to blame. He wanted a system that could remember a warning even after a worker left the company. June knew she could admire a person and still disagree with him. She had worked too hard to win the right to make her own decisions. Adrian seemed to be learning the same lesson from the other side. Their conversations were sometimes awkward, but they were becoming honest. Neither could buy or command the next step. It would happen only if both chose it freely.

午餐時，他們查閱一大疊舊電力紀錄。雨晴發現去年冬天修理隊就通報過相同問題，卻沒人追蹤。她說報告彼此沒連結，跟破損電線一樣危險。承遠打電話請經理提供書面紀錄，包含每次警報響起的時間。他沒要求找人來怪罪，而是想建立一個即使員工離職，仍能記住警告的制度。 雨晴知道自己可以欣賞一個人，同時不同意他的看法。她努力很久，才取得自主決定的權利。承遠似乎正從權力的另一端學習同一課。他們的對話有時笨拙，卻越來越誠實。下一步無法由任何一方購買或命令，只能由雙方自由選擇。

As the storm eased, June opened the workshop window. The snow turned the yard white, and the lamps remained off while the circuit cooled. Adrian stood beside her and asked whether she ever felt lonely doing a job that made people angry. She said sometimes, but a good team made the work worthwhile. "Then let me earn a place on that team," he replied. She did not answer at once. A promise was easy; she preferred to see what he would do next.

暴風雪漸歇，雨晴打開工坊窗戶。雪把院子染白，電路冷卻期間燈也保持關閉。承遠站在她身旁，問她做一份常惹人生氣的工作，是否會感到孤單。她說偶爾會，但好的團隊讓工作值得。他回答：「那就讓我靠行動取得團隊的一席之地。」她沒有立刻回答。承諾很容易，她更想看他接下來怎麼做。
