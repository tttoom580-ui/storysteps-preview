司機藏起的抽屜

The Driver's Hidden Drawer

總裁，這張車票不賣給你 · 第 7 / 25 章 · 1000–2000 · 英中對照 · 校訂中

用互動版查詞、朗讀與收藏

An expert in old trains gave June advice about a strange mark on the brake form. The mark did not match the actual metal plate. They went to the archive to address the difference, and Adrian found a hidden drawer behind a broken desk. Inside lay a china cup, a small lens, and a form naming the engineer who had first designed the system. A necklace hung from the handle. None of the objects seemed valuable, but their origin could explain who had altered the records. June kept a second notebook for questions that nobody could answer yet. She believed an empty space on a page was better than a number invented to look complete. Adrian had once disliked such spaces. Now he asked which question should be checked first. Their new way of working was slower, but it left a path that another person could follow.

一位老列車專家向雨晴解釋煞車表格上的奇怪記號，給了她建議。記號與實際金屬板不符。兩人去檔案室查明差異，承遠在破桌後面找到暗抽屜。裡面有瓷杯、小鏡片，以及寫著原設計工程師姓名的表格。把手上還掛著項鍊。這些物品似乎都不值錢，但它們的來源可能說明誰改了紀錄。 雨晴另備一本筆記，專門寫下暫時無人能回答的問題。她認為紙上留白，總比捏造數字看似完整更好。承遠以前不喜歡這種空白，如今卻問哪個問題該先查。他們的新工作方式比較慢，卻留下了其他人也能跟著走的清楚路徑。

A retired conductor said a naughty child had once played with the drawer and found a letter at midnight. The child was now a grown woman who taught music. She was a careful listener and remembered that the letter mentioned a prisoner who had helped build the railway after a past conflict. June knew the word sounded alarming. She asked for the full story rather than treating an old label as proof of wrongdoing. People could change, and a single sentence did not describe a whole life. Someone had prepared a bright sign promising good news. June asked that it stay folded until the workers checked the facts. A young assistant seemed disappointed; everyone wanted something to celebrate. Adrian spoke to the assistant gently and explained that good news was worth sharing only when it was true. The team could still take pride in its effort, even while another test remained unfinished.

一名退休列車長說，多年前有個調皮孩子玩抽屜時，在半夜找到一封信。那孩子如今是教音樂的成年人。她善於傾聽，記得信裡提到一名過去衝突後參與築路的囚犯。雨晴知道這個字令人不安，因此要求了解完整故事，不把舊稱呼當成犯錯證據。人會改變，單一句子也無法說盡一生。 有人備好一張宣告好消息的鮮豔牌子。雨晴要求先收起來，等工人核對事實。年輕助理顯得失望；大家都想慶祝。承遠溫和地解釋，好消息只有真實才值得分享。即使另一項測試尚未完成，團隊仍可為自己的努力感到驕傲。

They stopped at a café for toast while June sorted copies by date. Adrian wanted to beat the deadline, but he accepted that the work could not be rushed. A pleasant older waiter remembered the engineer's role: she had insisted that the brake records stay open to public review. The waiter did not know whether the policy had ever been followed. That question mattered more than the mysterious necklace. June wrote it at the top of her notebook. June placed the old plan beside the new one so the team could see each change. A number without a reason was not enough. Adrian asked one worker to explain the plan in ordinary language. When the worker became confused, they found another line that needed repair. It was a useful test of the words themselves, before those words guided a real action.

兩人在咖啡館吃吐司時，雨晴按日期整理影本。承遠想趕上期限，但接受工作不能急。一位親切的年長服務生記得原工程師的角色：她曾堅持讓煞車紀錄接受公眾查閱。服務生不知政策後來是否落實。這個問題比神祕項鍊更重要。雨晴把它寫在筆記本最上方。 雨晴把舊計畫與新計畫並排，讓團隊看見每項修改。只有數字沒有理由還不夠。承遠請一名工人用日常語言解釋計畫。工人說到一半困惑，大家便發現另一句需要修改。這是在文字真正指導行動前，對文字本身進行的有用測試。

Adrian proposed a public list of documents. June said the company could publish repair data, but it must protect the private addresses of witnesses. It was not enough to say every name belonged to the public. He nodded and asked the legal team to design a clear rule. For once, he found the careful limits of her plan attractive rather than annoying. She could see a wider problem while still noticing the person standing in front of her. Adrian's world was full of rooms where people agreed with him too quickly. Beside June, he found no easy praise. She could be warm and still say that his plan was wrong. He did not know whether she would ever love him, and he was learning not to turn that uncertainty into a demand. His care for her would mean little if it cost her independence.

承遠提出公開文件清單。雨晴說公司可以發布維修數據，但必須保護證人的私人地址，不能認為所有姓名都屬於公眾。他點頭，請法務團隊設計清楚的規則。這一次，他覺得她謹慎劃出的界線令人欣賞，而不是煩人。她既能看見更大的問題，也不忽略眼前的人。 承遠的世界充滿太快同意他的人。雨晴身旁沒有輕易的讚美。她可以溫柔，同時指出他的方案有錯。他不知道她將來是否會愛他，也在學著不把不確定轉成要求。如果他的關心要以她的獨立為代價，那便沒有多少價值。

At the end of the day, the retired conductor returned with a photograph of the engineer. She looked much like June, not in appearance but in the way she held a tool and faced the camera. Adrian said the old worker had left them a difficult gift: a question that still needed an answer. June replied that the best way to thank her was not to turn her into a legend. It was to use her notes and finish the work correctly.

一天結束時，退休列車長帶回原工程師的照片。她看起來像雨晴，不是長相，而是拿工具、面對鏡頭的姿態。承遠說，老工程師留給他們一份難解的禮物：一個仍需要答案的問題。雨晴回答，感謝她的最好方法不是把她變成傳奇，而是利用她的筆記，正確完成工作。
