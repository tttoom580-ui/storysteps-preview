簽名的代價

The Price of a Signature

總裁，這張車票不賣給你 · 第 11 / 25 章 · 1000–2000 · 英中對照 · 校訂中

用互動版查詞、朗讀與收藏

A local author invited June to read a short history of the forest railway. The book included drawings of a bridge only a few centimeters wider than the present one. A black crow sat on the window while an old guard explained the narrow path. The writer had not been rude about the company, but he had asked hard questions. Adrian's eyebrow rose when he saw a note beside one photograph: the old safety fee had paid for inspections, not private dinners. A mistake on a quiet day could be a warning about danger on a busy one. June tried to treat each warning as a chance to learn, not as a reason to search for an enemy. Adrian asked the team to keep earlier versions of their plans. If a decision changed, readers needed to know what had changed and why. That simple rule protected honest workers.

一位地方作家請雨晴閱讀森林鐵道簡史。書裡畫的橋只比現在的寬幾公分。一隻黑烏鴉停在窗邊，老警衛則解釋狹窄步道。作家並沒有無禮地批評公司，只是提出尖銳問題。承遠看見照片旁的註記時挑起眉毛：舊安全費應用於檢查，而不是私人宴席。 安靜日子裡的小錯，可能是在提醒忙碌時的危險。雨晴把每次警告視為學習機會，而非尋找敵人的理由。承遠要求團隊保留計畫的舊版本。若決策改變，讀者需要知道改了什麼、為何而改。這條簡單規則保護了誠實的工人。

The old station had a small stage where a wedding had once taken place. A retired waiter remembered the event and a priest who had signed the guest book. His memory might help solve a puzzle about the missing date on a repair form. June asked the waiter to describe what he knew without guessing. She wanted to include his words in the evidence file only with permission. Adrian listened and realized that a friendly witness could still be wrong about a day or a name. A visitor asked whether the rich passengers would still get special treatment. Adrian answered that comfortable seats could cost more, but the right to a safe journey could not. June nodded. She did not expect everyone to have the same ticket, only the same basic protection. The visitor thanked them for giving a direct answer. That small exchange made the discussion less like an advertisement and more like public work.

老車站有座小舞臺，多年前曾舉行婚禮。一位退休服務生記得那場活動，以及在賓客簿簽名的神父。他的記憶也許能解開維修表日期缺漏的謎。雨晴請服務生只說確知的事情，不要猜測。她想取得同意後才把他的話納入證據卷。承遠聽著，意識到友善的證人也可能記錯日期或姓名。 一名訪客問富裕旅客是否仍有特別待遇。承遠回答，舒適座位可以賣得更貴，但安全旅程的權利不能分級。雨晴點頭。她不期待所有人拿同樣車票，只期待享有同樣的基本保護。訪客感謝他們直接回答。這段小交流使討論不像廣告，更像真正的公共工作。

A skilled carpenter showed them a box that had held the old guest book. The wood was pale from age, but the date under the lid was clear. June and Adrian had a disagreement about its value. He wanted to announce the find; she said they needed to compare it with official records first. Neither shouted. Their relationship as coworkers was becoming stronger because a disagreement no longer felt like a personal attack. Before signing, June read the important lines aloud. She asked someone who had not prepared the document to check the numbers. Adrian had once thought such steps showed a lack of confidence. Now he understood they were a way of respecting everyone who would depend on the result. A careful process might feel slow in the moment, but it could prevent a much longer delay.

一位技術熟練的木匠帶他們看曾裝過賓客簿的盒子。木頭因年久褪成淡色，蓋子下的日期卻很清楚。雨晴與承遠對它的價值有分歧。他想公布發現，她說必須先與正式紀錄核對。兩人都沒有大喊。他們作為同事的關係變得更堅實，因為意見不同不再像人身攻擊。 簽字前，雨晴大聲讀出重要條款，請未參與起草的人再核對數字。承遠以前以為這些程序表示缺乏信心，如今明白這是尊重所有將依賴結果的人。當下審慎程序可能顯得慢，卻有機會避免更長的延誤。

That evening the board demanded a single name to blame for the delayed opening. Adrian refused to give them June's name, nor would he hide his own decisions. He explained that skilled inspection had found several real faults. Paying an expert was not a waste; paying for silence would be. June heard about the meeting later and thanked him for saying only what was true. She did not need a hero to protect her from every rude remark. She needed fair treatment and the right to speak for herself. June knew she could admire a person and still disagree with him. She had worked too hard to win the right to make her own decisions. Adrian seemed to be learning the same lesson from the other side. Their conversations were sometimes awkward, but they were becoming honest. Neither could buy or command the next step. It would happen only if both chose it freely.

當晚董事會要求找出一個人為延遲開幕負責。承遠拒絕交出雨晴的名字，也不隱藏自己的決策。他說明熟練的檢查確實找出多處故障。付費請專家不是浪費；花錢買沉默才是。雨晴後來聽說會議，感謝他只說真話。她不需要英雄替她擋下每一句無禮批評，她要的是公平待遇與自己發聲的權利。 雨晴知道自己可以欣賞一個人，同時不同意他的看法。她努力很久，才取得自主決定的權利。承遠似乎正從權力的另一端學習同一課。他們的對話有時笨拙，卻越來越誠實。下一步無法由任何一方購買或命令，只能由雙方自由選擇。

They returned the guest book to its owner before leaving. Adrian asked June whether he had made a mistake by staying quiet about the evidence. She said caution was not weakness, provided the truth was not buried. He walked beside her toward the lit road. "You always make the difficult choice sound simple," he said. "No," she answered, "I just say it in short words. It is still difficult." He laughed, and she found herself glad that he could laugh at his own pride.

離開前，他們把賓客簿還給主人。承遠問雨晴，自己暫緩公布證據是否做錯。她說只要真相沒有被埋沒，謹慎不是軟弱。他陪她朝明亮的道路走去。「你總把艱難的選擇說得很簡單。」他說。「不，」她回答，「我只是用短句說。事情還是很難。」他笑了，她發現自己很高興他能笑看自己的驕傲。
