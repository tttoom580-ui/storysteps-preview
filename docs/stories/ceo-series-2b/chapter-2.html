通往真相的車票

A Ticket for the Truth

總裁，這張車票不賣給你 · 第 2 / 25 章 · 1000–2000 · 英中對照 · 校訂中

用互動版查詞、朗讀與收藏

The station hall was famous for its beauty, but June cared more about the floor beneath the bright display. A loose metal chain blocked a narrow door. The current map did not show that exit, and a tour guide said visitors were often confused. Adrian wanted to cancel the public walk, fearing another embarrassing picture. June suggested keeping the tour but showing the blocked door honestly. People could judge a problem better when they could see it with their own eyes. The first person to notice trouble was rarely the person with the highest title. June knew this from years spent beside noisy machines. She asked small questions in a calm voice and wrote down the answers without changing their meaning. Adrian watched her and understood that listening was a kind of work. A meeting did not become useful simply because its leader spoke well.

車站大廳以美麗著名，但雨晴更在意華麗展示櫃下方的地面。一條鬆動的金屬鏈擋住狹窄的門。現行地圖沒有畫出那個出口，導覽員也說旅客經常困惑。承遠想取消公開參觀，怕又拍出難堪照片。雨晴建議保留參觀，誠實展示堵住的門。人們親眼看見問題後，才能作出更好的判斷。 最早發現問題的人，通常不是頭銜最高的人。雨晴多年待在吵雜機器旁，深知這點。她平靜提出細小問題，並原意記下答案。承遠看著她，明白傾聽也是工作。會議不會只因領導者口才好，就自動變得有用。

Inside the old waiting room, retired driver Mr. Ho played chess with a volunteer. They had watched the railway's growth for forty years, and both remembered a side passage that had vanished from new plans. June started a hunt through old maps, while Adrian checked records of past repairs. A missing map was a real loss, not merely a problem for the public image of the firm. A worker brought them sliced pear and asked if they had eaten anything since dawn. A cleaner stopped by to ask whether the workers could keep a clear path to the door. June thanked her and moved the boxes. Adrian noticed that a useful safety change could start with the person who cleaned the floor, not a director. Later he asked the cleaner to join the next planning meeting. She smiled, surprised that someone had remembered the question.

在舊候車室裡，退休司機何先生與志工下棋。兩人看著鐵路成長了四十年，都記得新圖上消失的側通道。雨晴開始翻找舊地圖，承遠則查閱過去維修紀錄。地圖遺失是真正的損失，不只是公司形象的麻煩。一名員工端來切好的梨子，問兩人是否從清晨就沒吃東西。 一名清潔員來問能否保持通往門口的道路暢通。雨晴道謝並移開箱子。承遠注意到，安全改善可以從掃地的人開始，不一定要靠董事。後來他邀請清潔員參與下一次規劃會議。她驚訝有人記得自己的問題，露出笑容。

Adrian's office had introduced a costly membership plan for rich visitors. A speaker on the wall repeated the same cheerful message every ten minutes: "Your journey will be super." June asked who would respond when an ordinary traveler needed help. The manager had no good answer. She felt that safety could not depend on a person's title or sense of self. Adrian heard her question and asked the team to write an answer that named a real worker, not an empty phone number. An assistant wanted to mark the job complete as soon as the first measurement looked good. June asked for the second reading. It was slightly different. They discussed the difference and noted the conditions of both tests. Adrian said they should keep the first result as well. The purpose of a record was to remember what had happened, not only what people wished had happened.

承遠的辦公室曾推出給富裕旅客使用的昂貴會員方案。牆上喇叭每十分鐘便重複歡快訊息：「您的旅程將超棒。」雨晴問，普通旅客需要幫助時，誰會回應？經理答不上來。她認為安全不能取決於人的頭銜或自我感覺。承遠聽見問題，要求團隊寫出實際負責人的名字，而不是空洞的電話號碼。 一名助理看到第一次量測結果良好，就想把工作標成完成。雨晴要求再量一次，結果略有不同。他們討論差異，記下兩次測試條件。承遠說第一次結果也要保留。紀錄的目的在記住真正發生的事，而不只是人們希望發生的事。

One director called June selfish for delaying the service. She replied that a trial run had to come before a public trip, even if it caused a short delay. The worst choice would be to hide danger and call silence success. Adrian did not defend himself by blaming the engineer. Instead, he admitted that the old plan had measured ticket sales better than it measured passenger safety. This admission surprised the directors more than any angry speech could have done. June closed her notebook and noticed how tired everyone looked. In earlier years she might have stayed until the lights went out, afraid a small mistake would be blamed on her. Now she had colleagues who could share the load. Adrian understood that helping did not mean taking her work away. It meant making enough room for her to do it well and to rest afterward.

一名董事指責雨晴自私，因為她延誤了服務。她回答，公開載客前必須先試車，即使會造成短暫延遲。最糟的選擇就是隱藏危險，還把沉默稱為成功。承遠沒有責怪工程師來替自己辯護，反而承認舊計畫比較重視票房，沒有同等重視旅客安全。這項承認比任何憤怒的演說都更令董事驚訝。 雨晴合上筆記本，看見眾人疲累的模樣。早年她或許會留到熄燈，怕小錯被算到自己頭上。如今有同事能分擔。承遠也明白幫忙不是把她工作搶走，而是給她足夠空間做好工作，做完還能休息。

They inspected the passage together after sunset. The chain could be replaced quickly, but the door required a proper test. Adrian offered June a permanent job with an impressive salary. She thanked him and refused. A safety inspector could not become the private employee of the person she was checking in the middle of an investigation. His smile faded, then returned in a smaller form. "Then I will learn how to work with someone I cannot hire," he said. June almost smiled back.

日落後，他們一起檢查側通道。鏈條很快就能更換，但門還需要正式測試。承遠以優渥薪水邀請雨晴成為正式員工。她道謝後拒絕。安全稽核進行到一半時，檢查員不能變成受查公司老闆的私人員工。他的笑容消失了一下，又以更溫和的樣子回來。「那我就學著跟不能雇用的人合作。」他說。雨晴差點也笑出來。
