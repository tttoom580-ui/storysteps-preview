檔案室屋頂的雨

Rain on the Archive Roof

總裁，這張車票不賣給你 · 第 12 / 25 章 · 1000–2000 · 英中對照 · 校訂中

用互動版查詞、朗讀與收藏

Rain struck the archive roof, and a dog began to bark near the door. June slipped on a wet step and hurt her ankle. Adrian offered his arm but waited for her reply before touching her. She accepted help to reach a chair. A computer made a sharp click as Leo opened a file with an exact description of the old bridge. The delay had not been caused by evil strangers, as rumors suggested, but by years of small choices left unchecked. June remembered that her first teacher had never let students hide a failed test. The teacher would say that failure was a message, not a mark of shame. She repeated that lesson to the younger staff. Adrian listened, thinking about how often his company had praised a good result without asking whether the method was sound. A changed habit, he knew, had to begin with a changed question.

大雨打在檔案室屋頂，一隻狗在門邊吠叫。雨晴在濕台階上滑倒，扭傷腳踝。承遠伸出手臂，卻先等她回答才碰她。她接受協助坐到椅上。里歐打開舊橋檔案時，電腦發出清脆的點擊聲，裡面有精確說明。延誤不是謠言裡邪惡陌生人造成的，而是多年未受檢查的小選擇累積而成。 雨晴記得啟蒙老師從不讓學生藏起失敗的測試。老師說，失敗是訊息，不是羞恥的印記。她把這課教給年輕員工。承遠想起公司過去常讚揚好結果，卻不問方法是否可靠。他知道習慣要改，首先得改變提問方式。

A student volunteer brought guava slices and a spoon for tea. She explained that the archive held cultural stories as well as technical plans. One small novel described young people who traveled by train to study in the city. A drawing on the last page showed a swing beside the station garden. June remembered seeing the same swing outside, now covered by protective tape because its frame was weak. A story could preserve a place, but it could not make unsafe wood strong again. A worker at the back of the room raised a hand and then slowly lowered it. June invited the question without calling attention to the worker's fear. The answer was not ready, so she promised to return with evidence, not with a guess. Adrian saw how much courage it took to admit that a question was still open. People seemed to trust them more, not less, for it.

一名學生志工送來芭樂切片和泡茶的小湯匙。她說檔案室除了技術圖也保存文化故事。一本小小說描寫青年搭火車去城裡求學，末頁畫著車站花園旁的鞦韆。雨晴記得外面有同一座鞦韆，如今因支架脆弱而貼上保護膠帶。故事能保存地方的記憶，卻不能讓不安全的木頭恢復強度。 房間後方一名工人舉手又慢慢放下。雨晴邀請他提問，沒有特別指出他的害怕。答案尚未備妥，她承諾帶證據回來，而不是猜測。承遠看見承認問題仍無解也需要勇氣。人們似乎因此更信任他們，而非更少。

When June selected the correct bridge plan, she found a repair note written upon the back. It described two earlier warnings and a request that had never received a reply. Adrian looked upset, but he did not ask anyone to destroy the page. He sent a copy to the independent review office and kept the original in its protective case. June watched him follow the process without asking for special treatment. This was the kind of change that would matter after a romantic feeling faded. The team put a date beside every promise. June also asked for the name of the person who would do the work, not only the person who approved the money. Adrian wrote both names on the plan. He found that clear responsibility made difficult choices less personal. When something went wrong, people could return to the task instead of arguing about who had looked most confident in a meeting.

雨晴選出正確橋梁圖時，在背面發現一則維修註記，寫著兩次舊警告，以及始終未獲回覆的申請。承遠看起來很沮喪，卻沒有叫人銷毀那頁。他把影本送給獨立審查辦公室，原件留在保護盒中。雨晴看見他依程序做事，沒有要求特權。這樣的改變，即使浪漫情感有天退去，仍然有價值。 團隊在每項承諾旁寫下日期。雨晴還要求寫出實際執行者，而不只核准經費者。承遠把兩人的名字都寫上。他發現清楚責任讓困難選擇不再那麼私人化。出問題時，大家能回頭處理任務，而不是爭論會議上誰看起來最有自信。

At the door a group of local youth asked when the line would reopen. June gave an honest answer: after inspection, repair, and official approval. Adrian added that they would publish a weekly progress note. One teenager asked whether the company could offer learning visits before passenger service began. June thought that was possible in a separate, safe area. Their idea gave the team another reason to keep listening to the community rather than making every decision behind closed doors. The day had been full of sharp questions. When the work finally paused, Adrian offered tea without asking for an answer about their private lives. June accepted the cup and looked at the quiet station. She was beginning to enjoy his company, but she would not confuse comfort with consent to anything more. He seemed to understand. Sometimes respect was visible in the questions a person did not ask.

門口一群當地青年問鐵路何時復駛。雨晴誠實回答：必須經過檢查、修復與正式核准。承遠補充，他們會每週發布進度。一名少年問載客服務開始前，能否舉辦參訪課程。雨晴認為若在獨立而安全的區域，應該可行。這點子讓團隊更有理由聽取社區意見，而不是關門決定所有事。 這一天充滿尖銳問題。工作終於暫停時，承遠端來茶，沒有要求她回答私人關係。雨晴接下杯子，望著安靜車站。她開始享受他的陪伴，但不會把舒服誤認為同意更進一步。他似乎懂了。有時尊重，正表現在一個人沒有追問的問題裡。

As the rain slowed, Adrian asked June whether she needed a doctor for her ankle. She had already called a clinic and arranged a visit. He said he was glad, without trying to take charge. They stood by the doorway watching the wet platform shine. June realized that she was beginning to care for him, and that frightened her a little. She promised herself that she would not soften a safety report because of it. The feeling could wait; the work could not.

雨勢減弱時，承遠問雨晴的腳踝是否需要看醫生。她已打電話到診所安排就診。他說很高興她有處理，沒有搶著接管。他們站在門邊，看濕月臺發亮。雨晴察覺自己開始在意他，這讓她有點害怕。她對自己承諾，不會因為這份感情放寬安全報告。感情可以等，工作不行。
