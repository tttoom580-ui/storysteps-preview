向外開的那扇門

A Door That Opens Outward

總裁，這張車票不賣給你 · 第 4 / 25 章 · 1000–2000 · 英中對照 · 校訂中

用互動版查詞、朗讀與收藏

June asked every department to attend a safety meeting at the repair yard. A worker brought a small cage for an injured bird and placed it in the shade. Another used a comb to clear threads from his coat after crawling under a carriage. These little scenes reminded June that railway workers had lives outside their jobs. She explained that a minor drug spill near the cleaning room also needed a report; it was no reason to blame anyone before checking the facts. The railway looked different when June stood beside a worker instead of looking down from a meeting room. She could hear where the floor shook and see which signs were difficult to read. Adrian joined her without bringing a crowd of assistants. He was beginning to learn why the place where a decision was made could change the decision itself.

雨晴要求各部門到修車場參加安全會議。一名工人帶來裝著受傷小鳥的小籠子，放在陰涼處。另一人鑽過車廂底部，拿梳子清掉外套上的線頭。這些小事提醒雨晴，鐵路員工不只有工作生活。她說明清潔室附近少量藥品外漏也須通報，但在查清事實前不能責怪任何人。 當雨晴站在工人身旁，而不是從會議室往下看，鐵道便顯得不同。她能聽出地板哪裡震動，也看見哪些標示難讀。承遠沒帶一群助理，直接加入她。他開始明白，做決定的地點，也會改變決定本身。

The eastern workshop had a small gymnasium used for staff training. In its kitchen, a cook tried to fry eggs in a worn pan for the night team. June watched the motion of the emergency door beside it. The door moved inward, against the flow of people who might need to escape. That was below the required standard. Adrian asked whether they could simply place a private guard there. She answered that no guard could make a badly designed door safe. At the end of the conversation, June asked whether anyone had understood the instruction differently. Two people gave different answers. Instead of blaming them, she rewrote the sentence in shorter words. Adrian copied the new line into his notes. He was surprised that clearer language could save time later. The workers were relieved to know that asking for a clearer rule did not make them look foolish.

東側工坊有一間供員工訓練的小體育館。附設廚房裡，廚師用舊平底鍋替夜班同仁煎蛋。雨晴觀察旁邊緊急門的開啟動作。門向內開，與逃生人潮的方向相反，低於應有標準。承遠問可否只派私人警衛站在門邊。她回答，警衛也無法讓設計錯誤的門變安全。 談話結束時，雨晴問有沒有人對指示有不同理解。兩人給出不同答案。她沒有責怪，而是用更短的字重寫句子。承遠把新版抄進筆記。他驚訝於清楚語言能在之後節省時間。員工也鬆了口氣，知道要求說清楚不會被當成愚蠢。

Their meeting soon became loud. One manager wanted to encourage the young workers to speak, while another feared the discussion would damage the firm's name. June wrote the purpose of the meeting on a board: find danger, agree on repairs, and protect the whole region of stations. Adrian supported a firm schedule rather than a vague promise. He also accepted that several changes would cost more than expected. For once, a budget line did not end an argument about human safety. When the room grew tired, June asked for a short break. Adrian agreed before anyone complained. They returned with fresh eyes and found a small error they had missed. It was not a dramatic moment, but it changed the outcome. June wrote the correction in the record, and Adrian signed beside it. The most important part was not that they had been right; it was that they could correct themselves.

會議很快變得吵雜。一名經理想鼓勵年輕員工發言，另一名卻怕討論傷害公司名聲。雨晴在板上寫出會議目的：找出危險、同意修復方式，並保護整個區域的車站。承遠支持明確而堅定的時程，而不是模糊承諾。他也接受一些改善會超出預算。這一次，預算數字沒有讓安全討論被迫終止。 大家疲倦時，雨晴要求短暫休息。承遠在有人抱怨前就同意。重新開始後，大家用清醒的眼睛發現一處先前漏掉的小錯。這不是戲劇化時刻，卻改變了結果。雨晴記下更正，承遠在旁簽字。最重要的不是一開始正確，而是能夠改正。

A little boy waiting with his mother pointed to a painted wolf on the wall. "Why does it have a wing?" he asked. The mural showed an old story about a wolf traveling under an umbrella through a storm. June used the picture as a simple example. In an emergency, people did not stop to read long rules. They needed exits that were easy to see and open. Adrian thanked the child for asking a question that the adults had missed. Adrian had spent years believing that solving a problem meant being the first person to speak. June showed him another way: stay long enough to understand the person who would live with the answer. He did not always find it easy. Yet the quiet effort changed how he saw both the railway and the woman standing beside him. He hoped she could notice that change without feeling asked to reward it.

一個跟母親等候的小男孩指著牆上的狼壁畫問：「牠為什麼有翅膀？」壁畫講的是一隻狼撐著傘穿過暴風雨的古老故事。雨晴借畫面舉例：緊急時人不會停下來讀冗長規則；他們需要清楚可見、容易打開的出口。承遠感謝孩子提出大人忽略的問題。 承遠多年來相信，解決問題就是第一個開口說話。雨晴讓他看到另一條路：留下來，理解必須承受答案的人。他做起來並不總是容易。可是這份安靜的努力，改變了他看鐵道與身旁女子的方式。他希望她能看見改變，卻不覺得有義務因此獎勵他。

That evening Adrian walked June to the gate, holding his umbrella above the notes rather than above her head. She teased him for caring more about paper than people. He stopped and moved the umbrella, asking permission before stepping closer. She agreed, amused by the care behind the question. At the gate she reminded him that the door test had failed. He answered, "Then it stays closed until we fix it." It was not a romantic promise, but she trusted it more than one.

那天晚上，承遠送雨晴走到大門，手裡的傘竟先替文件遮雨，而不是遮她。她打趣說他比起人更在乎紙張。他停下來移動雨傘，靠近前先徵求她同意。她帶著笑意答應。到門口時，她提醒他門的測試不合格。他回答：「那就維持關閉，直到修好。」這不是浪漫的誓言，卻比誓言更讓她信任。
