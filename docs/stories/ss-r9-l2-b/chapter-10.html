一公尺的雨

A Meter of Rain

雨季借你一把傘 · 第 10 / 25 章 · 1000–2000 · 英中對照 · 校訂中

用互動版查詞、朗讀與收藏

The first open repair workshop was planned for the middle of the month. The student center had one room available, but its tables were too short for some volunteers. Mei brought a biscuit box full of spare handles, and Mrs. Lan offered congratulations on the team's first clean return record. Iris thanked her but said a good record required courage to show the mistakes as well as the successes. The group gathered around a long table and compared samples. A child had drawn a fox and a goose on the wall chart, making the arrows easier to notice. Jun kept the drawings but asked that the safety words remain clear. The students agreed to gather again after lunch to check each unit of parts, while Mrs. Lan changed her teaching style for the younger volunteers.

第一次公開修繕工作坊安排在月中舉行。學生中心有一間空房，但桌子對部分志工來說太低。美拿來一盒餅乾盒裝的備用傘柄，蘭女士恭喜團隊第一次取得完整的歸還紀錄。艾莉絲謝謝她，卻說好的紀錄也要有勇氣展示錯誤，而不只有成果。大家聚在長桌旁比較樣品。一個孩子在牆上的圖表畫了狐狸和鵝，讓箭頭更醒目。俊保留畫作，但請人維持安全文字的清晰度。 學生同意午餐後再集合檢查每一單位的零件，蘭女士則為年輕志工調整了教學風格。

Iris measured the length of each sample with a tape. One folding handle was short by almost a centimeter, which made its button hard to reach. She marked one meter on the floor so that volunteers could see how much room a person needed to open an umbrella without touching another person. A musical club offered background music, but Jun asked them to pause it during spoken instructions. The group would repair no more than four umbrellas per hour, and only approved parts would be used. Their goal was proper work, not a large count. Iris was pleased when the volunteers repeated that rule back to her.

艾莉絲用捲尺量每個樣品的長度。一個折疊傘柄短了將近一公分，讓按鈕變得難按。她在地面標出一公尺，讓志工看見開傘時需要多少空間，才不會碰到旁人。音樂社想播放背景音樂，但俊請他們在口頭講解時暫停。每小時最多只修四把傘，而且只能使用經核可的零件。目標是確實做好工作，不是追求高數量。志工能把這條規則清楚複述出來，讓艾莉絲很高興。

The workshop began slowly. A student compared the speed of the repairs to a snail, and everyone laughed. Mrs. Lan showed why glue could not replace a broken metal joint, no matter how strong the label sounded. The process was gradual: inspect, clean, replace, test, and record. A volunteer wanted to ignore an old scratch because the umbrella still opened, but Iris explained that they needed to check whether the scratch hid a crack. The team took photographs only of the damaged parts. Jun held the camera away from people's faces and asked permission before including any hands in a teaching picture.

工作坊進行得很慢。有學生說修傘速度跟蝸牛一樣，大家都笑了。蘭女士示範，無論膠水標籤寫得多強，都不能拿來代替斷裂的金屬接頭。流程只能循序漸進：檢查、清潔、更換、測試、紀錄。一名志工想忽略舊刮痕，因為傘還能打開，但艾莉絲說必須確認刮痕底下沒有裂縫。團隊只拍受損零件。俊把鏡頭避開人臉，若教學照片要拍到手，也先詢問當事人。

For lunch, the community center served roasted yam and vegetable soup. Iris told Jun that she might spend her vacation at a design lab in another city if she won the fellowship. He congratulated her before asking how long she would be away. “I do not know,” she admitted. She liked that his first response was happiness for her, not worry about losing her help on the project. Jun told her he planned to work with adult learners during the summer. They compared schedules without trying to change them. For the first time, Iris wondered whether liking someone could include being happy when that person chose a different road.

午餐時，社區中心提供烤地瓜與蔬菜湯。艾莉絲告訴俊，如果她拿到研究獎助，假期可能要去另一個城市的設計實驗室。他先恭喜她，再問會離開多久。「我不知道，」她承認。她喜歡他的第一個反應是替她高興，而不是擔心計畫失去她。俊也說自己夏天打算教成人課程。兩人比較時間表，沒有試圖改變對方。艾莉絲第一次想，喜歡一個人，也許包括真心替對方選擇不同道路而高興。

At the end of the day, the team inspected every repaired item again. Six umbrellas had been brought in; four passed the tests, one needed a new part, and one had to be retired. Iris wrote the results in the public log. The workshop had not produced a dramatic mountain of ready umbrellas, but it had taught people what a safe repair looked like. Mrs. Lan said she would come again if the school paid for the special parts and respected her time. Jun wrote that condition into the next proposal. Iris looked at him and thanked him for remembering that a useful idea still depended on real people.

活動結束時，團隊再次檢查所有修好的物品。共收進六把傘，四把通過測試，一把需要新零件，另一把必須淘汰。艾莉絲把結果記入公開紀錄。工作坊沒有堆出驚人的成品，但教會了大家安全修理的標準。蘭女士說，只要學校支付特殊零件費，也尊重她的時間，她願意再來。俊把條件寫入下一份提案。艾莉絲看著他，謝謝他記得：再好的點子，也要靠真實的人來完成。

That evening they closed the room together. Iris stood by the window as rain moved slowly across the glass. She had planned to ask Jun for coffee, but remembered their friend Mei might still need help carrying the records. Jun seemed to read her thought and offered to finish the closing work first. They moved chairs, wiped the floor, and locked the parts cupboard. Only then did he ask if she wanted to walk to the bus stop with him. Iris said yes. They walked under one umbrella with enough space between them to choose whether to move closer. Neither made that choice for the other.

當晚兩人一起關閉教室。艾莉絲站在窗邊，看雨慢慢滑過玻璃。她原想約俊喝咖啡，又想到美可能需要幫忙搬紀錄。俊彷彿猜到她的想法，提議先完成收尾工作。他們搬好椅子、擦乾地板、鎖上零件櫃。直到這時，俊才問她想不想一起走到公車站。艾莉絲答應了。兩人共撐一把傘，肩膀間仍留著足以自主選擇是否靠近的距離。誰也沒有替對方做決定。
