屋頂測試

The Roof Test

雨季借你一把傘 · 第 12 / 25 章 · 1000–2000 · 英中對照 · 校訂中

用互動版查詞、朗讀與收藏

A small dog began to bark when a piece of the community center roof moved in strong wind. A visitor stepped back suddenly and hurt her ankle on the stairs. Jun helped her sit while another volunteer called the center staff. Iris did not click the emergency sign as if a message alone could solve the problem. She marked the area closed and wrote an exact description of what they had seen. The center manager announced a delay in all activities under that roof until a qualified worker inspected it. Some students were upset about losing the weekend workshop, but Iris supported the decision. A damaged roof was not a problem students should try to fix themselves.

風勢變強時，社區中心屋頂有一部分晃動，一隻小狗突然吠叫。一名訪客急忙後退，在樓梯上扭傷了腳踝。俊扶她坐下，另一名志工通知中心人員。艾莉絲沒有以為按下緊急通報鍵就能解決問題，而是把區域關閉，詳細記下親眼看到的情況。中心經理宣布，合格人員檢查屋頂前，所有屋頂下的活動都必須延後。有些學生因週末工作坊取消而不高興，但艾莉絲支持決定。損壞的屋頂不是學生該自行修理的問題。

The next day, Jun brought a short cultural history of the community hall to the team. It had once hosted classes, family meals, and a reading circle. A novel written by a former teacher described children waiting for rain to end there. Iris liked the story, but she kept it separate from the safety report. The workers needed measurements, not memories. Mrs. Lan arrived with protective covers for the dry umbrellas and asked which space remained safe. Iris replied that only the inner room had been cleared for use. The others would stay closed until inspection. They moved their lesson indoors without arguing with the order.

隔天，俊帶來一份社區中心的文化沿革。這裡曾舉辦課程、家庭聚餐與讀書會。一位前任教師寫的小說也描述孩子們在這裡等雨停。艾莉絲喜歡故事，但仍把它和安全報告分開。工作人員需要的是測量數字，不是回憶。蘭女士帶來乾燥雨傘的保護套，詢問哪些空間仍安全。艾莉絲回答，目前只核可內側教室；其他地方在檢查前必須關閉。他們把課程移入室內，沒有違反命令。

At the temporary room, Mei served guava slices with a spoon while students discussed how to select the safest route. One path passed under a children's swing, and another crossed a narrow driveway. Jun asked them to travel both paths on foot before making a choice. Iris wrote the times in her notebook and drew a map. Upon reaching the second path, they found that a low wall blocked the view of people turning into the drive. They rejected it. The longer path had a covered side and clearer sight lines. It was not the quickest route, but it gave people more room to notice danger.

臨時教室裡，美用湯匙分給大家番石榴切片，學生討論如何選出最安全的路線。一條路會經過兒童鞦韆下方，另一條則穿過狹窄車道。俊請他們實際走完兩條路再決定。艾莉絲把時間記在筆記本上，並畫地圖。走到第二條路時，他們發現矮牆擋住了車輛轉入時的視線，因此排除這條路。較長的路有遮棚，也更容易看見周圍。它不是最快，但讓人有更多空間及早注意危險。

When the injured visitor returned, she told the team she had not wanted to become the reason an event was canceled. Jun assured her that the closing decision belonged to the center, not to her. Iris asked whether the route had been clear before the dog barked. The visitor said she had seen a sign but not the loose step. They recorded the answer without blaming her. A youth volunteer offered to make a brighter sign, but Iris said the broken step had to be repaired first. A warning could help people avoid danger, yet it could not replace a safe building. The young volunteer understood and offered to help carry the new signs later.

受傷的訪客回來時說，她不希望自己成為活動取消的原因。俊讓她放心，關閉是中心做的決定，責任不在她。艾莉絲問狗叫之前路線是否清楚。訪客說她看到標示，卻沒發現鬆動的臺階。他們把回答記下，沒有責備她。青年志工提議做更亮的告示，但艾莉絲說必須先修好壞掉的臺階。警告可以幫人避開危險，卻不能取代安全的建築。年輕志工明白後，表示日後願意搬新標示。

The roof inspection found damage to one edge and a weak fixing near a drain. Repair would take longer than a single day. The center manager sent a written reply to their questions, including which rooms were safe and when another check was planned. Iris thanked him for giving a clear answer instead of a hopeful guess. Jun helped move the workshop record boxes to the university. They had lost a convenient location, but not the whole project. The second return point stayed closed; borrowers were directed to the safe hall at the west gate. The students had learned that a good partnership also meant knowing when to pause. No one had planned an evil act; the roof had failed, and the right response was to make it safe.

屋頂檢查發現一側邊緣受損，排水孔旁的固定件也不牢。修理需要超過一天。中心經理以書面回覆問題，包括哪些房間安全，以及下次檢查的預定時間。艾莉絲感謝他提供明確答案，而不是充滿希望的猜測。俊協助把工作坊紀錄箱移回大學。他們失去一處方便場地，卻沒有失去整個計畫。第二歸還點暫時關閉，借用者改到西門安全走廊還傘。學生學到，好的合作也包括知道何時暫停。 沒有人蓄意做壞事；是屋頂出了問題，正確的回應是確保安全。

Friday dinner was postponed because Iris's fellowship interview moved to that evening. She told Jun as soon as she knew. He replied with encouragement and asked whether another day would suit her. His message was so simple that she read it three times. She had expected either disappointment or a show of easy confidence; instead she received space to decide. After the interview she walked under the university lights, tired but hopeful. The clouds had opened, revealing a small patch of clear sky. She called Jun and asked if he was still awake. He was, and they talked until her bus arrived.

艾莉絲的研究獎助面試改到週五晚上，因此晚餐也延後。她一知道就立刻告訴俊。他回覆鼓勵的話，並問換一天是否方便。訊息簡單得讓她讀了三次。她原以為會收到失望的回應，或故作輕鬆的態度，結果得到的是自主決定的空間。面試後，她在校園燈光下走著，又累又有希望。雲層散開一角，露出一小片晴空。她打電話問俊還醒著嗎。他醒著，兩人聊到她的公車抵達。
