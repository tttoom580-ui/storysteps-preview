雨季借你一把傘 · 第 9 / 25 章 · 1000–2000 · 英中對照 · 校訂完成

用互動版查詞、朗讀與收藏

A student reporter wrote an article about the umbrella trial and asked Iris for an interview. The first chapter of the story described the repaired rack as a colorful gift to campus. Iris liked the photographs, but she noticed an important addition was missing: Jun's work on the return rules and the community center's space. The reporter had used a bright crayon sketch of the old rack as a cover image. Iris asked him to explain the daily records instead of writing only about the design. He admitted that he feared readers would find a clear system less exciting than a story about lost objects. Iris said truth did not have to be dramatic to matter.

校園記者寫了一篇雨傘試行報導，並邀請艾莉絲受訪。文章開頭把修好的傘架描寫成送給校園的繽紛禮物。艾莉絲喜歡照片，卻發現遺漏了重要部分：俊制定的歸還規則，以及社區中心提供的空間。記者用舊傘架的鮮豔蠟筆畫作封面。艾莉絲請他也說明每日紀錄，而不是只講設計。記者承認，自己擔心讀者覺得清楚的制度沒有失物故事刺激。艾莉絲說，事實不必戲劇化，也值得被看見。

At the campus archive, Jun found a historical map showing why the old west gate had become a dangerous corner. Years earlier, a service road had been added without moving the walking sign. A legal agreement allowed the university to place equipment there but did not excuse unsafe conditions. The librarian showed them an old painting of students sheltering under a tree. Iris studied the image, then found a rusty nail in the wooden notice board nearby. She asked for the board to be inspected before anyone hung a new sign. The librarian smiled. The students were learning to see both stories and structures.

在校史檔案室，俊找到一張歷史地圖，顯示西門舊路口為何變得危險。多年前增設了服務道路，卻沒有移動行人標示。一份合法協議准許學校在此放置設備，但不能因此忽略不安全的情況。館員拿出一幅舊畫，畫中學生躲在樹下避雨。艾莉絲仔細看著，接著在旁邊的木製公告板發現一根生鏽釘子。她請人先檢查公告板，再掛新標示。館員笑了：學生開始同時看見故事與建築結構。

Mei, Iris's project partner in a design class, offered to paste new labels on the return boxes. A puppy from the campus animal shelter followed her into the hall, then ran away with an empty cardboard tube. The volunteers laughed and retrieved it safely. Mrs. Lan, the shopkeeper, arrived carrying extra thread and showed them a damaged section of a fabric cover. The thread would shine under the hall lights, making the edge easier to see. Iris suggested using a strip of bright cloth instead of a light that required power. Mrs. Lan agreed that simple repair could sometimes succeed where expensive equipment added little value.

艾莉絲設計課的搭檔美，主動幫忙把新標籤貼上歸還箱。一隻來自校園動物收容所的小狗跟著她跑進走廊，還叼走一個空紙筒。志工笑著安全拿回紙筒。店老闆蘭女士帶來額外的線，指給大家看布套上一段損壞的地方。那種線在走廊燈下會反光，讓邊緣比較容易看見。艾莉絲提議改用鮮明布條，而不是需要電力的燈。蘭女士同意，簡單修理有時比昂貴設備更有效。

The reporter returned with a revised draft. He had removed the student's private details and added a clear explanation of how the return system worked. But the last paragraph said the entire project would close unless the school immediately gave Iris more money. Jun pointed out that no such decision had been made. Iris asked the reporter to change it. She did not want fear used as a tool to force an outcome. The reporter apologized and wrote that a review would take place at the end of the trial. That sentence was less exciting, but it was accurate. Iris felt better after reading it. An incorrect warning would make the students' fear worse rather than help them understand the review.

記者帶著修訂稿回來。他刪掉學生的私人資訊，也清楚說明歸還制度。不過最後一段寫著，若學校不立刻給艾莉絲更多經費，整個計畫就會結束。俊指出，根本還沒有這種決定。艾莉絲請記者修改，不願以恐懼逼迫任何結果。記者道歉，改寫成試行期結束時將進行審查。這句話沒那麼刺激，卻正確。艾莉絲讀完後感到安心。 不正確的警告只會讓學生更害怕，無助於理解審查。

Later that day, Jun showed Iris the updated cost sheet. The team had enough parts for three more weeks but not enough for another ten umbrellas. They would need a fair plan before applying for support. Iris suggested asking Mrs. Lan to teach a low-cost repair course. Jun added that a paid professional should not be replaced by unpaid students without consent. They agreed to invite the shopkeeper to a planning meeting instead of writing her role for her. The conversation felt ordinary and practical, yet Iris had begun to trust how Jun handled disagreement. He no longer seemed like a person who only said no.

那天稍晚，俊給艾莉絲看更新後的成本表。團隊的零件還能用三週，但不足以再增加十把傘。他們必須先提出公平計畫，再申請補助。艾莉絲建議請蘭女士教授低成本修繕課。俊補充，不能未經同意就讓無給薪學生取代應該付費的專業人員。他們決定邀蘭女士參加規劃會議，而不是替她指定角色。這段對話普通又實際，但艾莉絲開始信任俊處理歧見的方式。他不再只是那個只會說「不」的人。

On Sunday, Iris and Jun visited the design exhibition. They stood before a small model of a bridge and argued gently about whether its beauty came from shape or use. Neither won. Afterward they shared tea in a quiet corner and talked about their families. Iris said her parents supported her studies but hoped she would choose a stable job. Jun said he wanted to teach adults who had left school early. They did not turn those hopes into promises for each other. When he asked if they could visit another exhibition someday, Iris said yes, and she meant it. The answer felt different from agreeing to a committee meeting.

星期天，艾莉絲和俊去看設計展。他們站在一座小橋模型前，溫和爭論它的美究竟來自形狀還是用途。兩人都沒有贏。之後他們在安靜角落喝茶，聊起家庭。艾莉絲說父母支持她讀書，但希望她找穩定工作。俊說他想教那些早年離開學校的成年人。他們沒有把彼此的希望變成對方的承諾。俊問以後能否再一起看展，艾莉絲答應了，而且是真心的。這個「好」和答應參加委員會會議的感覺不一樣。
