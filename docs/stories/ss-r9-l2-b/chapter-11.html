每個人的舞臺

A Stage for Everyone

雨季借你一把傘 · 第 11 / 25 章 · 1000–2000 · 英中對照 · 校訂中

用互動版查詞、朗讀與收藏

The student union invited the umbrella team to a small fund-raising fair. A local author would read from a book, and the school arts club would perform on the stage. Iris measured the entrance: the step was fifteen centimeters high, enough to block a wheelchair without a ramp. A crow called from a tree as she drew the problem. The organizer raised an eyebrow when she asked to change the plan. “The fee for a ramp will take money from the event,” he said. Jun disagreed. He explained that the cost of access was part of holding a public event, not a special favor. Iris placed her drawing on the table and waited for a serious answer. Every centimeter mattered when a visitor had to move safely through the door.

學生會邀請雨傘小組參加小型募款市集。當地作家將朗讀作品，學校藝術社也會在舞臺表演。艾莉絲量了入口：臺階高十五公分，沒有坡道就足以阻擋輪椅。她畫圖時，樹上一隻烏鴉叫了起來。主辦人聽她要求修改計畫，揚起眉毛。「坡道的費用會吃掉活動經費，」他說。俊不同意。他解釋，讓所有人都能進入是公共活動的基本成本，不是特別恩惠。艾莉絲把圖放到桌上，等待認真的答覆。 訪客要安全通過門口時，每一公分都很重要。

They called a skilled technician to examine the narrow entrance. He said the old temporary ramp was too steep and could not be used. The fair would include a smaller indoor stage until a safe solution was found. A guard at the forest path entrance offered to direct visitors to the level door. Jun insisted that neither the guard nor the signs should tell people they were a problem. Their job was to explain the route clearly. Iris was impressed by how often he thought about the words people heard, not only the place they reached. She had been learning the same lesson through objects. The members did not disagree about the need for access; they disagreed about the timetable.

他們請熟練的技師來檢查狹窄入口。技師說，舊臨時坡道太陡，不能使用。在找到安全辦法前，市集改用較小的室內舞臺。森林步道入口的警衛願意引導訪客走平坦的門。俊堅持，警衛與告示都不該讓人覺得自己是麻煩；任務是清楚說明路線。艾莉絲很佩服他總會想到人們聽見的話，而不只是最後抵達的地點。她正從物品設計學習相同的道理。 委員對無障礙需求並無歧見，爭的是施作時間表。

Before the fair, Mrs. Lan told Iris about a puzzle in her own relationship with the university. The school often asked local shops for free help but rarely invited them to plan events. A rude email had even called her repair lessons “unimportant work.” Iris was angry, but Mrs. Lan asked her not to fight on her behalf. “Ask what I want,” the shopkeeper said. She wanted a fair contract and the chance to teach. Iris brought that request to the committee. The members agreed to pay for two sessions and list the shop as a professional partner, rather than a donor who owed them endless time.

市集前，蘭女士告訴艾莉絲，她與學校的合作關係一直有個難題。學校常請附近商家免費幫忙，卻很少邀請他們參與規劃。一封無禮的電子郵件甚至把她的修繕課稱作「不重要的工作」。艾莉絲很生氣，但蘭女士請她不要替自己吵架。「先問我想要什麼，」她說。她希望有公平合約，也能真正教課。艾莉絲把要求帶進委員會。委員同意支付兩堂課的費用，並將店家列為專業夥伴，而不是欠學生無限時間的捐助者。

On the day of the fair, the event hall filled with visitors. A waiter from the catering team placed cups near the entrance, but moved them when Jun explained that the path needed to stay open. A visitor said she had attended a wedding in the same hall the month before and had faced the same step problem. That fact gave the committee a reason to seek a lasting repair, not only a temporary answer for the fair. Iris asked the visitor whether she could share the comment without her name. The woman agreed. They added it to the facilities request, where it would remain useful after the music ended. A priest who used the hall for community meetings added that the pale old direction signs were hard to read at night.

市集當天，活動廳擠滿了訪客。餐飲組一位服務生把杯子放在入口旁，俊說明通道需要保持暢通後，他便移走。一名訪客說，她上個月在同一個禮堂參加婚禮，也遇到相同臺階問題。這件事讓委員會有理由尋求長期改善，而不只是為市集暫時應付。艾莉絲問那位訪客能否匿名引用意見，對方答應了。他們將意見附在總務申請中，音樂結束後仍會有用。 一位曾在此舉辦社區會議的神職人員補充，舊的淡色方向標示夜裡很難看清。

During the reading, the author spoke about a town that valued quiet workers. Iris looked at Mrs. Lan teaching a child how to test a handle, then at Jun explaining the return map to an older visitor. Neither was on the stage, yet both were making the day possible. The event raised enough money to replace several broken parts, but not enough to open a third station. Iris was disappointed for a moment. Jun reminded her that the team had also found an access problem and gained a proper teaching agreement. Those results would last longer than a single evening of applause.

朗讀時，作家說起一個珍惜默默工作者的小鎮。艾莉絲看向正在教孩子測試傘柄的蘭女士，又看向替長者說明還傘地圖的俊。他們都沒站上舞臺，卻讓活動得以順利進行。市集募到足以更換幾個零件的款項，仍不足以增設第三個站點。艾莉絲一時失望。俊提醒她，團隊也發現了無障礙問題，並建立正式教學合約。這些成果會比一晚的掌聲持續更久。

After the last visitor left, Jun asked Iris whether she wanted to have dinner with him the following Friday. He looked nervous, which made her smile. She asked if it was a planning meeting. “No,” he said. “I mean just dinner, if you would like it.” Iris had been hoping for a clear invitation but had not wanted to push him. She accepted, then added that Friday might change if her fellowship interview was moved. Jun said her interview should come first. She went home thinking about how good it felt to be invited without being expected to put her own life on hold.

最後一位訪客離開後，俊問艾莉絲下週五願不願意和他吃晚餐。他看起來有點緊張，讓她笑了。她問是不是規劃會議。「不是，」他說，「就是單純吃飯，如果你願意。」艾莉絲一直希望他能清楚邀約，卻不想逼他。她答應了，又補充如果研究獎助面試改期，週五可能得調整。俊說面試應該優先。她回家時一直想著，被邀請而不必為此暫停自己的人生，是多麼好的感覺。
