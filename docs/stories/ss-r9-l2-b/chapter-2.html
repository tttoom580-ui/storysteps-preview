公平的試行

A Fair Trial

雨季借你一把傘 · 第 2 / 25 章 · 1000–2000 · 英中對照

用互動版查詞、朗讀與收藏

The next morning, Iris returned with a simple display board. The beauty of the plan was not its color; it was the space left for people to disagree. Jun wanted to cancel the old rule that asked every borrower to leave a student number. Iris agreed that identity checks would keep some visitors away. Mrs. Lan, however, warned them that a free service might reduce her sales. At a table beside the cafe, two retired teachers played chess and listened to the debate. One said a strong chain made a rack look secure but could not teach people to return what they borrowed. The other asked what they would do when the weather was at its worst.

隔天早上，艾莉絲帶著簡單的展示板回來。這份計畫的美不在色彩，而在它留下了讓人表達不同意見的空間。俊想取消舊規定：每個借用者都必須留下學號。艾莉絲同意，身分查核會讓部分訪客不敢使用。但蘭女士擔心免費服務會影響生意。咖啡店旁的桌邊，兩名退休老師一邊下西洋棋，一邊聽他們討論。一人說，粗鎖鏈可以讓傘架看似安全，卻教不會人們歸還借用物；另一人問，天氣最糟時他們打算怎麼辦。

Jun showed the current record: twelve umbrellas missing, but six had been found in other school buildings. The old form had no place to record a return outside the west gate. “That changes the story of our loss,” he said. Iris suggested a map that would guide borrowers toward a second drop point. Their trial would last seven days, with ten umbrellas, not a hundred. A public chart would show returns and repairs without names. Mrs. Lan watched Iris hunt through her bag for a pen. The shopkeeper offered her a pencil and, to everyone's surprise, suggested a simple return box beside the cafe. She did not want a system that pretended local shops did not exist.

俊拿出目前的紀錄：原本說有十二把傘失蹤，但其中六把已在校內其他建築找到。舊表格根本沒有欄位可記錄在西門以外的歸還。「這改變了我們對損失的理解，」他說。艾莉絲建議加上一張地圖，引導借用者到第二個歸還點。試行只維持七天，使用十把傘，而不是一百把。公開圖表只顯示歸還與維修狀況，不顯示姓名。蘭女士看著艾莉絲在包裡翻找筆，便借給她一支鉛筆，還出乎眾人意料地提議把簡單的還傘箱放在咖啡店旁。她不希望新制度假裝附近商家不存在。

At lunch, Mei brought a pear for Iris and read the first message from the volunteer group. A student asked whether membership in a club was required to borrow an umbrella. “No,” Iris wrote back. Jun asked her to respond more clearly: neither fees nor a phone should be necessary. Mei laughed and told Iris to be herself instead of copying Jun's careful words. Iris called him selfish for correcting every line. Then she saw a second message from a student who could not stand for long. That person needed a low shelf and a seat. Iris admitted that Jun had noticed a problem she had missed. The two changed the design together.

午餐時，美帶來一顆梨子給艾莉絲，並讀出志工群組的第一則訊息。有學生問，借傘是否必須先加入社團。艾莉絲回覆「不用」。俊請她回答得更清楚：不需要付費，也不需要手機。美笑著叫艾莉絲做自己，不必照抄俊那套謹慎的措辭。艾莉絲抱怨他太自我，連每一行都想修改。接著她看見另一則訊息：有位不能久站的學生需要低一點的架子和座位。艾莉絲承認俊發現了她漏掉的問題。兩人一起修改設計。

At the afternoon meeting, the dean placed a speaker on the table so remote students could hear. A volunteer described the new plan as “super easy,” but Jun asked for a real test, not a slogan. Each borrowed umbrella would carry a number, and the number alone would appear on the return slip. Nobody would be punished for reporting a broken part. If one umbrella vanished, they would check the record and the nearby buildings before claiming someone had stolen it. Iris watched Jun explain the rules. His voice was firm without being cold. When he looked at her for confirmation, she nodded. Their trial finally had conditions both could defend. The growth of the service would be measured in safe returns, not in the number of photographs. Her sense of self would stay strong even when she accepted advice.

下午開會時，主任把擴音器放在桌上，讓遠端學生也能聽見。一名志工稱讚方案「超級簡單」，但俊要求真正測試，不要只說口號。每把借用傘都有編號，歸還紙條只記編號。主動回報零件損壞的人不會受罰。若有一把傘找不到，他們會先查紀錄和附近建築，不直接宣稱有人偷走。艾莉絲看著俊說明規則。他的語氣堅定，卻不冷漠。他看向她尋求確認，她點頭。這個試行計畫終於有了兩人都願意捍衛的條件。 服務的成長要用安全歸還來衡量，而不是照片數量。即使接受建議，她仍能保有自我認同。

On the way out, Jun found a note from the bus office. The last bus would leave ten minutes earlier next week. Without that information, even a successful umbrella stand might fail the people who needed it most. Iris drew a seat under the return sign, then drew a clear path to the stop. They would measure it the following day. “You are making my job difficult,” Jun said. “Good,” Iris replied. “Your questions make mine better, too.” It was not an apology for their argument, but it was the first time either admitted that the argument had helped.

離開時，俊找到公車單位的通知：下週末班車將提早十分鐘。若沒有這項資訊，就算雨傘站成功，也可能幫不到最需要的人。艾莉絲在歸還標示下畫了一張椅子，又畫出通往站牌的清楚路線。他們隔天要實際測量。「你讓我的工作變難了，」俊說。「很好，」艾莉絲回答，「你的問題也讓我的工作變好了。」這不是為爭執道歉，但卻是他們第一次承認，爭論帶來了幫助。

That evening, Mrs. Lan put their poster beside a small sign for her own shop. Iris had expected her to refuse, yet the woman said more people walking past safely could also help the cafe. Jun sent the updated map to the student office and closed his laptop. He considered asking Iris to walk home together, then noticed that she was still testing a handle. Instead, he offered to carry the box of sample parts. She accepted the help but carried the drawing board herself. Their new partnership looked much like the rack they were making: two people sharing weight without giving up their own hands.

當晚，蘭女士把他們的海報放在自己的店面招牌旁。艾莉絲原以為她會拒絕，但蘭女士說，更多人能安全經過，也可能對咖啡店有幫助。俊把更新的地圖寄給學生事務處，闔上筆電。他想邀艾莉絲一起回家，卻注意到她還在測試傘柄，便改口提出幫忙搬零件箱。她接受協助，但自己拿著畫板。他們新的搭檔關係，就像正在製作的傘架：兩人分擔重量，同時保有各自的雙手。
