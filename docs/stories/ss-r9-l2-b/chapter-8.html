放錯的架子

The Wrong Shelf

雨季借你一把傘 · 第 8 / 25 章 · 1000–2000 · 英中對照 · 校訂中

用互動版查詞、朗讀與收藏

The team's average return time had improved, but two umbrellas seemed to disappear every weekend. Iris and Jun visited the Saturday barbecue held beside the community hall, because many borrowers passed that place on their way home. An older man remembered that in his childhood, neighbors left spare umbrellas near the old bus shelter. A cook offered them chopsticks with their lunch and told them the new boxes were hard to see behind a delivery cart. Nobody had ordered the umbrellas to vanish. Their route simply crossed an area where other people needed space. Iris took the complaint seriously instead of calling it bad luck or fate.

團隊的平均歸還時間改善了，但每到週末似乎仍有兩把傘消失。艾莉絲和俊去社區活動中心旁的星期六烤肉活動，因為許多借用者回家時會經過那裡。一位長者回憶童年時，鄰居會把備用傘放在舊公車候車亭旁。廚師遞給他們筷子與午餐，還說新的還傘箱被送貨推車擋住，很難看見。沒有人命令雨傘消失，只是歸還路線和其他人的活動空間重疊。艾莉絲認真看待這個抱怨，沒有把它當成運氣不好或命運使然。

A volunteer wanted to command everyone to return umbrellas to the west gate. Jun explained why that would not work: the meaning of a shared service was that people could use it without making an unsafe trip. Iris proposed moving the second box a few meters forward, away from the cart path. The cook asked them to keep a gentle turn space for deliveries. Their new design had to carry both concerns. The reality was more complex than the first beautiful drawing. Still, the group found a means of solving it: a shelf on wheels that could be moved for events but locked safely during regular hours. They agreed the moving shelf should carry a load of no more than ten umbrellas.

一名志工想要求所有人都把傘還到西門。俊解釋這不可行：共享服務的意義，在於不用為了歸還而走危險的路。艾莉絲建議把第二個箱子向前移幾公尺，避開推車動線。廚師請他們保留送貨車的緩和轉彎空間。新設計必須顧及雙方。現實比第一張漂亮草圖複雜得多，但大家找到了解法：帶輪子的架子，活動時可移動，平時則能安全固定。 他們同意移動架最多只能承載十把傘的重量。

The older man laughed when someone called the volunteers servants of the university. “A service is not the same as being a servant,” he said. “You are choosing to help.” He showed Iris an old stone marker that had once stood beside the shelter. The mark on it was the source of an earlier road map, although the road had moved long ago. Iris decided to stick a small map beside the new shelf, showing the present path rather than the old one. She asked the group to check whatever signs they used against the actual street. A picture could mislead people even when the maker had good intentions.

有人稱志工是學校的僕人，長者聽了笑了。「提供服務不等於當僕人，」他說，「你們是選擇幫忙。」他帶艾莉絲看候車亭旁一塊舊石碑。石碑上的標記曾是早期道路地圖的來源，但道路很久以前就改了。艾莉絲決定在新架子旁貼一張小地圖，顯示現在的路，而不是舊路。她請大家把所用標示逐一對照現場街道。即使製作者出於好意，圖畫仍可能誤導別人。

At the hall door, a volunteer asked Jun to whom the second return point belonged. He answered that the university would remain responsible for the equipment, while the community center would provide space during agreed hours. Neither place would receive names or travel records. Iris wrote the details on the back of her plan. She had once thought a partnership meant a friendly photograph and a shared sign. Now she saw that it also required clear duties and a way to end the arrangement safely. The center manager signed a temporary permission form, and the students promised to return the site to its original condition after the trial.

在活動中心門口，志工問俊第二個歸還點到底歸誰管理。俊回答，設備仍由大學負責，社區中心則依約定時段提供空間。兩方都不會取得借用者姓名或移動紀錄。艾莉絲把細節寫在圖紙背面。她過去以為合作就是合照和共同招牌，如今才明白，還需要清楚的責任與安全終止安排。中心經理簽下臨時使用許可，學生也承諾試行結束後把場地恢復原狀。

By evening, the two “missing” umbrellas were found behind a cupboard in the center's storage room. A cleaner had moved them to protect the floor from water and had not known about the new system. Iris was embarrassed but grateful. She asked the cleaner to join the next sign test. Jun marked the items as found, not stolen, and made sure the record showed the reason for the error. There was no villain to catch. The real work was to make the next person's reasonable action fit the plan. Iris began to enjoy that challenge, because each answer came from listening to somebody whose day looked different from her own.

傍晚時，兩把「失蹤」的傘在活動中心儲藏室的櫥櫃後找到了。清潔人員為了不讓水滴到地上而移走它們，卻不知道新制度。艾莉絲有些難為情，但很感激，並邀請清潔人員參加下次標示測試。俊把物品記成「已尋獲」，而不是「遭竊」，也在紀錄裡寫明錯誤原因。他們沒有壞人要抓，真正的工作是讓下一個人的合理行動也能融入制度。艾莉絲開始喜歡這種挑戰，因為每個答案都來自傾聽生活與自己不同的人。

On the return bus, Iris sat beside Jun with the empty lunch box on her knees. They talked about childhood memories of rain: Iris remembered making paper boats; Jun remembered waiting at an uncovered stop with his mother. He said that was why he cared so much about small things like seats and dry signs. Iris understood him better than she had during their first argument. When the bus reached campus, she asked if he wanted to visit a design exhibition with her on Sunday. “As a team member?” he asked, trying to hide a smile. “As someone who asks useful questions,” she answered. He said he would like that.

回程公車上，艾莉絲把空餐盒放在膝上，坐在俊身旁。他們聊起童年的雨天：艾莉絲記得摺紙船，俊則記得和母親站在沒有遮棚的站牌下等車。他說，正因如此，自己才格外在意座位和乾燥的標示這些小事。艾莉絲比第一次爭論時更懂他了。公車抵達校園時，她問他週日想不想一起去看設計展。「以隊員身分嗎？」他忍笑問。「以會提出有用問題的人身分，」她回答。俊說他很樂意。
