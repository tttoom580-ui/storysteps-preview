A Place to Stand

雨季借你一把傘 · 第 4 / 25 章 · 1000–2000 · 英中對照

用互動版查詞、朗讀與收藏

The first indoor opening was held in the eastern hall. Iris asked volunteers to attend a short training session instead of simply handing them keys. A rescue group brought a travel cage for a frightened stray cat found behind the gym, and a volunteer carried it away from the crowd. Jun arrived with his hair wet and used a comb from his bag before speaking to the student department. Iris laughed at his serious face. He explained that students with limited movement needed a clear space between the rack and the wall. Even a minor change in the angle of an umbrella handle could affect whether a person could reach it.

第一次室內試行在東側走廊舉行。艾莉絲請志工先參加簡短訓練，而不是直接交給他們鑰匙。一個動物救援團體帶來運送籠，準備把體育館後面一隻受驚的流浪貓帶走，志工把牠移離人群。俊頭髮濕濕地趕到，還從包包拿出梳子整理頭髮，才去向學生事務處說明。艾莉絲笑他表情嚴肅。他解釋，行動不便的學生需要傘架與牆壁間留出清楚空間。即使傘柄角度只有小幅改變，也會影響別人能否伸手拿到。

A medical student checked the cleaning plan and asked them not to use a strong drug or unknown liquid on handles. They would clean parts with approved soap and water, then allow them to dry. Jun encouraged volunteers to report problems, but he did not demand that anyone do work beyond their training. Mrs. Lan ran a small, firm business near campus and offered to teach basic repairs for a fair fee. Iris feared that paying her would use too much of the budget, yet the shopkeeper had tools the students lacked. They agreed to start with a free safety lesson and discuss paid work separately.

一位醫學生檢查清潔計畫，提醒大家不要用強效藥劑或不明液體擦拭傘柄。他們會用經核可的肥皂與清水清理零件，再讓它們晾乾。俊鼓勵志工回報問題，但不要求任何人做超出訓練範圍的工作。蘭女士在校園旁經營一家穩定的小店，願意收取合理費用教授基本修繕。艾莉絲擔心付費會用掉太多預算，但店家有學生缺乏的工具。雙方同意先辦免費安全課，付費工作另外討論。

At lunch, the food club offered to fry eggs in a pan for volunteers. Iris checked that cooking would stay away from the fabric table. A paper model hung over the rack like a small wing; it showed how an open umbrella should face away from the walking path. Jun tested the model with a student using a cane. The student asked for a larger sign, not a louder announcement. Iris changed the design on the spot. Their purpose was to make an umbrella available without making anyone feel watched. The standard had to work for people in the whole region of the campus, not only students who moved quickly. Jun tried to encourage shy volunteers to ask questions. Iris checked the motion of every opening handle and kept all private borrower details off the board.

午餐時，餐飲社說願意在平底鍋裡煎蛋給志工吃。艾莉絲確認烹飪區與布料桌保持距離。傘架上方掛著一個像小翅膀的紙模型，示範打開的雨傘應朝離人行道的方向。俊請一位拿手杖的學生測試模型。那位學生要的是更大的標示，而不是更大聲的廣播。艾莉絲當場修改設計。他們的目的，是讓人能拿到雨傘，又不覺得受到監視。這套標準必須適用於整個校區的人，而不只是走得快的學生。 俊試著鼓勵害羞的志工提問。艾莉絲檢查每個傘柄開啟的動作，也確保看板上沒有借用者的私人資料。

During the test, a child from the nearby family center spotted a wolf painted on a volunteer's bag and asked whether the rack was a forest shelter. Everyone laughed, then Iris saw that the question had value. The rack should look welcoming but still be easy to understand. She replaced her abstract symbol with a simple drawing of an umbrella and an arrow. Jun checked the words on the sign with two visiting parents. They understood it without help. The first ten umbrellas were hung, each one tested for broken ribs and sharp edges. Nobody cheered loudly; they simply watched the first borrower take one and walk safely away.

測試時，附近家庭中心的一個孩子看到志工袋子上的狼圖案，問傘架是不是森林避難所。大家笑了，艾莉絲卻發現這個問題很有價值：傘架應該令人願意靠近，也必須容易理解。她把抽象符號換成簡單的雨傘與箭頭圖。俊請兩位來訪的家長看看標示，他們不用協助就看懂了。第一批十把傘掛上架，每一把都檢查過傘骨斷裂與尖銳邊緣。沒有人大聲歡呼；大家只是看著第一位借用者拿起一把傘，安全地離開。

The first return came an hour later. The borrower placed the wet umbrella in the marked area rather than in the dry rack. The volunteer wrote only its number and checked the handle. Iris felt a rush of happiness so strong that she nearly took a picture of the student. She stopped herself. The rules they had made together applied to her, too. Jun noticed her hand move toward her phone and then lower. He did not tease her. Instead, he said the first return was enough reason to buy tea for the team. Iris asked whether he always celebrated with tea. “Only when there is still work to do,” he replied.

一小時後，第一把傘被還回來。借用者把濕傘放在標示區，而不是放進乾傘架。志工只記下編號，並檢查傘柄。艾莉絲高興得幾乎想拍下那名學生，卻及時停手。兩人一起訂出的規則也適用於她。俊看見她的手伸向手機又放下，沒有取笑她，只說第一次成功歸還很值得請大家喝茶。艾莉絲問他是不是總用喝茶慶祝。「只有事情還沒做完的時候，」他回答。

After closing, they checked the paper box against the umbrellas. Ten had gone out and nine had returned; the tenth borrower still had time before the next day. Iris wanted to stay and wait, but Jun reminded her that the team had written a clear return period and should follow it. He walked with her to the covered steps, carrying a box of clean cloths. When the wind pushed rain through the side of the hall, they shared one of the test umbrellas. Their shoulders touched for a moment. Neither spoke about it. The silence was not awkward; both were busy keeping the paper records dry.

關閉試行點後，他們對照紙條箱與傘數。十把傘借出，九把已歸還；第十位借用者仍有時間在隔天還傘。艾莉絲想留下來等，但俊提醒她，團隊既然寫明歸還期限，就應照規則執行。他抱著一箱乾淨抹布陪她走到有遮棚的臺階。風把雨吹進走廊一側時，兩人一起撐起一把測試用傘。肩膀短暫碰觸，他們都沒說什麼。沉默並不尷尬；兩人正忙著保護紙本紀錄不被淋濕。
