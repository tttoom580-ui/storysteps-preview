上鎖的傘架

The Locked Rack

雨季借你一把傘 · 第 1 / 25 章 · 1000–2000 · 英中對照

用互動版查詞、朗讀與收藏

The rain began just after Iris left her design class. At the west gate of Harbor University, a metal rack held twelve broken umbrellas behind a new lock. A man with a gray beard stood outside the small cafe across the road, watching students run between puddles. Iris asked why the rack was closed. The cafe owner, Mrs. Lan, said the school had called the missing umbrellas a crime. Someone had also reported a strong chemical smell near the repair table. “I sell umbrellas,” Mrs. Lan added, “but I do not want anyone to get hurt.” Iris looked at the empty hooks. The lock had ended a useful service before she had even joined it.

艾莉絲剛離開設計課，雨便下了起來。海港大學西門旁，一座金屬傘架裡有十二把壞傘，外面卻加上了新鎖。街對面的小咖啡店外，一名留著灰鬍子的男子看著學生在水窪間奔跑。艾莉絲問傘架為何關閉。咖啡店老闆蘭女士說，學校把傘的失蹤稱為犯罪，也有人反映修繕桌附近有濃烈的化學藥劑味。「我是賣傘的，」她補充，「但我也不希望任何人受傷。」艾莉絲望著空蕩蕩的掛鉤。她還沒加入這個有用的服務，它就已經被一把鎖終止了。

Jun arrived with a list of night students who had missed the last bus in heavy rain. He asked Iris to divide the problem into three parts: broken equipment, missing items, and unsafe walking paths. She thought he was giving orders. “Could you do me a favor and listen first?” she said. Her sharp words came from a familiar feeling: people often treated design as decoration. Near the gate, the university gardener picked a wet leaf from a blocked drain. He explained that water had covered the path before the umbrellas went missing. Jun listened without speaking. He wanted evidence, not a quick person to blame, and Iris began to see that he did too.

俊帶著一份夜間學生的資料趕到；這些學生曾在大雨中錯過末班公車。他請艾莉絲把問題分成三部分：設備損壞、物品遺失，以及行走路線不安全。她以為他在下命令。「你可以先聽我說完，幫我這個忙嗎？」她問。她說話尖銳，是因為熟悉的感受又冒出來：大家常把設計當成裝飾。校門附近，學校園丁從堵塞的排水孔撿起一片濕葉子。他說傘失蹤前，人行道就已淹水。俊安靜聽著。他想要證據，而不是隨便找個人責怪；艾莉絲開始發現自己也是如此。

The next witness was a student wearing blue pajamas under a long coat. She had come from a late study group and did not want her photograph taken. Jun respected that request. His calm personality surprised Iris, who had expected another loud committee leader. Iris drew a plain paper plan: a rack with a rubber floor pad, clear return signs, and no camera facing borrowers. Jun asked how the design would relate to students who could not carry a heavy umbrella. Iris turned the paper around so they could both see it. The two were not friends yet, but this was the first question that made the drawing better.

下一位目擊者在長外套底下穿著藍色睡衣。她剛結束深夜讀書會，不希望被拍照。俊尊重她的要求。他平靜的個性讓艾莉絲意外；她原以為他是另一個吵鬧的委員會幹部。艾莉絲畫出簡單的紙上方案：傘架底下鋪橡膠墊、設置清楚的歸還標示，也不讓攝影機朝向借傘的人。俊問，這個設計如何顧及無法拿重傘的學生。艾莉絲把紙轉過來，讓兩人都看得見。他們還不是朋友，但這是第一個讓設計變得更好的問題。

“We cannot fix such a system with a pretty sign alone,” Jun said. Mrs. Lan brought a receipt showing how purchase tax raised the cost of every new umbrella. The gardener showed them a small thermometer; the temperature had fallen quickly after sunset, making wet students shake with cold. A tiny child waiting with her mother held a picture of a watermelon she had drawn at school. The child offered to decorate the new rack. Before Iris could agree, Jun asked them to weigh a repaired umbrella and test whether a child could carry it safely. Iris wrote his idea beside her own. She liked the care behind his questions, even when his timing annoyed her.

「這樣的制度不能只靠漂亮的標示來修好，」俊說。蘭女士拿來收據，說明購買稅如何提高每把新傘的成本。園丁拿出小溫度計；日落後氣溫迅速下降，淋濕的學生冷得發抖。一個跟母親等車的小女孩拿著學校畫的西瓜圖，說願意幫新傘架做裝飾。艾莉絲還沒答應，俊就請大家先秤一把修好的傘，測試孩子能否安全拿起。艾莉絲把他的點子寫在自己的旁邊。即使他的提問時機有點惱人，她仍喜歡問題背後的細心。

At the student office, Dean He gave them forty-eight hours to propose a safer pilot. The missing items would still be investigated, and the old rack would stay locked. Iris wanted to demand an immediate reopening, but Jun pointed to the wet steps outside. The problem had started before anyone accused a student of taking an umbrella. They agreed to count the usable umbrellas, walk the bus route, and collect comments without names. “If our plan works,” Iris said, “you must stop calling my sketches decoration.” Jun smiled. “If it works, I will help build them.” For the first time that day, the rain sounded less like a warning and more like a question they could answer together.

在學生事務處，何主任給他們四十八小時提出更安全的試辦計畫。遺失物仍須調查，舊傘架也會繼續上鎖。艾莉絲原想要求立即重新開放，但俊指著外面濕滑的臺階。問題在有人被指控拿走雨傘以前就存在了。他們同意清點可用雨傘、實際走一遍公車路線，並匿名收集意見。「如果我們的方案成功，」艾莉絲說，「你就不能再把我的草圖叫作裝飾。」俊笑了。「如果成功，我還會幫你把它們做出來。」那一天，雨聲第一次不像警告，而像一道可以一起回答的問題。

They separated at the gate with different jobs. Iris would test handles and repair parts in the design room. Jun would ask evening students what happened when the last bus was late. Neither wanted the other's praise yet. Still, Iris noticed that Jun had waited until she reached the covered path before taking his own road home. She almost called after him, then decided against it. A small act of care did not have to become a promise. Tomorrow, she would bring measurements, and he would bring the bus schedule. That was enough for a beginning, even while twelve umbrellas remained locked behind metal bars.

兩人在校門分開，各自有工作。艾莉絲要在設計教室測試傘柄與修繕零件；俊要詢問晚間學生錯過末班公車時的經歷。他們都還不想得到對方的稱讚。不過，艾莉絲注意到俊等她走到有遮雨棚的路段，才轉身走自己的路。她差點喊住他，最後沒有。一個體貼的小舉動不必立刻變成承諾。明天她會帶來測量結果，他會帶來公車時刻表。即使十二把傘仍鎖在鐵欄後，這已足夠作為開始。
