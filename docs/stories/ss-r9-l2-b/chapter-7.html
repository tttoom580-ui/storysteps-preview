牆上的名字

Names on the Wall

雨季借你一把傘 · 第 7 / 25 章 · 1000–2000 · 英中對照 · 校訂中

用互動版查詞、朗讀與收藏

The actual number of missing umbrellas was now two, not twelve. Yet an anonymous note appeared on the wall, giving a student's home address and accusing her of taking one. Jun asked the office for advice about removing it without copying it into a wider report. Someone from the music club had been practicing a steady beat nearby, but stopped when the student began to cry. Iris covered the note with blank paper before anyone could photograph it. A student carrying a box of china cups offered tape. Their repair project had begun with objects, but now a person's safety mattered more than any umbrella.

真正找不到的雨傘現在是兩把，而不是十二把。但牆上忽然出現匿名字條，寫出一位學生的住家地址，還指控她拿走一把傘。俊請教事務處如何移除字條，同時避免把內容複製到更廣泛的報告。附近音樂社原本在練習固定的節拍，當那名學生哭起來時也停了下來。艾莉絲先用白紙蓋住字條，阻止旁人拍照。一位搬著瓷杯箱的學生借給她膠帶。修繕計畫從物品開始，但現在，一個人的安全比任何雨傘都重要。

The university's privacy expert explained that even a well-meant report could spread harm if it repeated personal details. Iris filled in a complaint form using the time and location, not the exposed address. The office checked the hall's camera lens only for the paper being placed, under its normal access rules. It did not show the student borrowing an umbrella. Jun listened to the expert's explanation, then told the team that none of them should guess who had written the note. A pleasant volunteer said she had seen a visitor with a red necklace, but Iris refused to treat that small detail as proof. It could fit many people. Jun was a careful listener, especially when a student was afraid to speak.

學校的隱私專家說明，即使出於好意，報告若重複個人資訊也可能擴大傷害。艾莉絲填寫申訴表，只記下時間與地點，不抄出被公開的地址。事務處依正常權限查看走廊攝影機鏡頭，僅確認紙條被貼上的情況；畫面並沒有拍到那名學生借傘。俊聽完專家的說明，要求團隊不要猜測寫字條的人。熱心志工說看見一位戴紅項鍊的訪客，但艾莉絲拒絕把這個細節當成證據，因為符合描述的人可能很多。 俊是個細心的傾聽者，尤其在學生不敢開口時。

Near midnight, Iris and Jun began to sort the remaining return records. They found that one “missing” umbrella had actually come back with a broken strap and been placed in the wrong box. The other was still outside. A student complained that these rules made every honest person a prisoner of paperwork. Jun replied that the role of the records was to protect people from false claims, not to control them. He asked whether the whole process could be made shorter. Iris removed two unnecessary boxes from the form. It now took less than a minute to return an umbrella without giving a name.

接近午夜時，艾莉絲和俊開始分類剩下的歸還紀錄。他們發現其中一把所謂「失蹤」的傘其實早已歸還，只因背帶斷裂被放進錯誤的箱子；另一把仍在外面。有學生抱怨規則讓老實人都成了文書作業的囚犯。俊回答，紀錄的作用是避免人被錯誤指控，而不是控制大家。他問能否縮短整個流程。艾莉絲從表格刪掉兩個不必要的欄位。現在不用留下姓名，也能在一分鐘內完成歸還。

After the office removed the note, the student who had been accused returned to thank them. She did not want her name in the public story. Iris promised not to use it, even if a dramatic story might help win support for the project. The student said she had taken an umbrella only once, when her bus had broken down. She returned it the next morning. Jun asked whether the route sign had been clear. She showed them that the two return boxes looked almost the same at night. Iris made one bright and one dark, with large drawings that did not depend on color alone. Their mistake had helped someone else unfairly become a suspect. A small child called the wandering dog naughty, but Iris reminded her that the animal was only frightened by the noise.

事務處移除字條後，那位被指控的學生回來道謝。她不想自己的名字出現在公開故事裡。艾莉絲答應，即使戲劇性的故事可能幫計畫爭取支持，也不會使用她的名字。學生說她只借過一次傘，是公車故障那天；隔天早上就還了。俊問她歸還路線的標示是否清楚。她指出夜裡兩個歸還箱看起來幾乎一樣。艾莉絲把一個改成明亮、一個改成深色，又加上不只依賴顏色的大圖案。他們設計上的錯誤，曾讓無辜者不公平地成了嫌疑人。 一個小孩說亂跑的狗很頑皮，但艾莉絲提醒她，動物只是被聲音嚇到了。

In the morning, Mei brought toast and told Iris she looked tired. Jun arrived with a new list of night-bus times. He had found the origin of one misunderstanding: the staff had counted a repaired umbrella as newly borrowed because its old tag had fallen off. Iris fixed the tag system before opening. She did not celebrate by putting the accusation behind them. Instead, she added a rule: any public complaint must describe an event, not expose a person's private information. The dean accepted it for the pilot. Their project had become more than a place to borrow umbrellas; it was also a lesson in how to correct a mistake without destroying trust.

早上，美帶來吐司，說艾莉絲看起來很累。俊帶著新的夜間公車時刻表到來。他找到了其中一項誤會的來源：修好的雨傘因舊標籤掉落，被工作人員當成新借出的傘。艾莉絲開放前先修改標籤制度。她沒有因指控結束就慶祝，而是新增一條規則：公開申訴應描述事件，不得揭露個人隱私。主任同意在試行期採用。這個計畫不再只是借傘的地方，也教大家如何在不摧毀信任的情況下改正錯誤。

As they left the office, Jun gave Iris a careful look. “You did the difficult part,” he said. She shook her head. “The student did. She came back even after we failed her.” Jun did not argue. He walked beside Iris down the long hall, past the place where the note had hung. The wall was empty now. Iris hoped that emptiness would give the student room to return without being watched. She reached for the handle of the shared umbrella, then looked at Jun. “Do you want to carry it this time?” He answered yes, and she let him.

離開辦公室時，俊認真看了艾莉絲一眼。「最難的部分是你做的，」他說。她搖頭：「是那位學生做的。即使我們沒照顧好她，她還是願意回來。」俊沒有爭辯。他陪艾莉絲走過長廊，經過先前貼字條的地方。牆面如今空白。艾莉絲希望這片空白能讓那位學生自在回來，不必受到注視。她伸手要握住共用雨傘的傘柄，又看向俊。「這次你想拿嗎？」俊答應，她便交給了他。
