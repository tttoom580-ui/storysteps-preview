紅色標線

The Red Line

雨季借你一把傘 · 第 3 / 25 章 · 1000–2000 · 英中對照

用互動版查詞、朗讀與收藏

On the third day, Iris made an attempt to walk the return route during the morning rush. The cause of the long line became clear within five minutes: students on scooters crossed the path to reach a parking lot. A cloudy sky made the painted boundary hard to see. Mei gave her a cup of cereal and asked whether she planned to continue without breakfast. At the eastern entrance, a classmate held up a lost earring and asked whether the umbrella team had collected it. Iris placed it in the official lost-property box. She was determined not to turn the new project into a place where objects disappeared without a record.

第三天，艾莉絲試著在早晨尖峰時間走一遍歸還路線。不到五分鐘，她就看出隊伍壅塞的原因：騎機車的學生會穿過人行道，前往停車場。陰天讓地上的標線更加難辨。美拿給她一杯麥片，問她是不是打算不吃早餐就繼續工作。在東側入口，一位同學拿著遺失的耳環，問雨傘小組是否收過。艾莉絲把耳環放進正式失物箱。她決定不讓新計畫成為物品沒有紀錄就消失的地方。

The final route test was interrupted by a loud accusation. A student claimed someone had used a second paper slip to cheat the return count. Others tried to force the volunteer to name the borrower. Jun stopped them. “There is no general rule saying an unsigned slip proves theft,” he said. A government notice about public access was posted near the gate, but it had nothing to do with this dispute. Iris told the group that an open, liberal policy still needed careful records. They would link every return slip to the umbrella number, never to a student's name. The accused volunteer relaxed enough to speak.

最後一次路線測試被一場大聲指控打斷。有學生聲稱某人用第二張紙條作弊，讓歸還數字失真。旁人試圖逼志工說出借用者姓名。俊阻止了他們。「沒有任何一般規定說，沒簽名的紙條就能證明有人偷東西，」他說。校門附近張貼著一份政府公共通行公告，但那與這次爭議無關。艾莉絲告訴大家，開放的制度仍須有仔細的紀錄。他們會把每張歸還紙條連到傘的編號，而不是學生姓名。被指控的志工終於放鬆到可以說話。

The volunteer explained that the first slip had become wet and unreadable. She had written another while standing beside the same rack. Jun gave a slow nod. Iris asked the office to operate a dry box for used slips and a second box for damaged ones. A student runner volunteered to carry the sealed papers to the office each evening. The team could not promise that no mistake would ever happen, but the change meant mistakes could be checked instead of hidden. When Iris thanked the volunteer for speaking up, the woman said she had nearly left the project. Her courage, not the angry accusation, had improved it. They also checked the supply of dry paper boxes before reopening the return desk.

志工說明，第一張紙條被雨淋濕，已經讀不清楚，她才站在同一個傘架旁重寫一張。俊緩緩點頭。艾莉絲請事務處設置乾燥的使用後紙條箱，另設損壞紙條箱。一位跑步社學生自願每晚把密封的紙張送回事務處。團隊不能保證永遠不出錯，但新的做法讓錯誤可以被查核，而不是隱藏。艾莉絲感謝志工願意說出經過，志工說自己差點退出。改善制度的是她的勇氣，不是憤怒的指控。 他們也在重開歸還桌前，確認乾燥紙箱的供應是否足夠。

In the afternoon they examined the red line beside the scooter crossing. The paint ended too early, and a delivery cart blocked the sign. Iris measured the area while Jun asked riders what they could see from the road. One rider admitted that the old direction arrow looked like permission to turn into the walking path. This was not simply a careless person; it was also poor information. Iris moved the planned rack away from the corner and drew a wider space for turning. Jun checked that the new location would not block a wheelchair. They sent the drawing to the facilities office for review instead of painting a line themselves.

下午，他們檢查機車通道旁的紅色標線。油漆太早結束，送貨推車又擋住了告示。艾莉絲測量範圍，俊則詢問騎士從道路上看到了什麼。一位騎士承認，舊箭頭讓他以為可以轉進人行道。問題不只是有人粗心，也包括資訊設計不良。艾莉絲把預定的傘架位置移離轉角，並畫出更寬的轉彎空間。俊確認新位置不會擋住輪椅。他們把圖交給總務單位審查，而不是自己直接畫線。

At sunset, Dean He approved a smaller test area inside the covered hall. The outdoor crossing would remain closed until the facilities office could check it. Iris felt frustrated; she had wanted a quick public success. Jun reminded her that an opening photo would mean little if someone fell the next morning. They sat beside the locked old rack and counted what they had learned in three days. Six umbrellas were not stolen. One angry claim had been corrected. A dangerous crossing had been found before opening. “That is progress,” Jun said. Iris looked at his wet sleeves and believed him, although she still wished progress moved faster.

日落時，何主任批准他們先在有遮棚的走廊內做小規模測試。戶外路口在總務單位檢查前仍須關閉。艾莉絲感到沮喪；她原本希望快點公布成果。俊提醒她，如果隔天有人跌倒，開幕照片就毫無意義。他們坐在舊傘架旁，數著三天學到的事。六把傘並沒有被偷；一項憤怒的指控得到澄清；危險的路口在開放前被發現。「這就是進展，」俊說。艾莉絲看著他濕透的袖子，相信了他，雖然仍希望進展能更快。

Before leaving, Iris added a red warning mark to the map and a note explaining why the route was closed. She did not hide the delay. A first-year student stopped to read it, then thanked her for making the warning easy to understand. Jun had already gone to check the revised bus schedule. Iris realized she no longer watched the gate only for students in need of umbrellas. She also found herself watching for the tall education student with a notebook and a careful voice. She could not yet say what that meant, and she was not ready to ask him.

離開前，艾莉絲在地圖加上紅色警示記號，並寫明路線暫停使用的理由。她沒有隱瞞延誤。一位大一學生停下來閱讀，感謝她把警告寫得容易理解。俊已經去查看更新後的公車時刻表。艾莉絲發現自己不再只注意校門口哪些學生需要雨傘，也會留意那名拿著筆記本、說話謹慎的高個子教育系學生。她還不知道這代表什麼，也暫時不想問他。
