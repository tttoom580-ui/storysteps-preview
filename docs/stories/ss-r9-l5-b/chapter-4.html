中空橋梁的尺度

The Measure of a Hollow Bridge

雲鶴歸巢：與你修一條天空的路 · 第 4 / 25 章 · 4000–5000 · 英中對照

On Salt Island, a hollow stone bridge carried workers across a gap between farms and the school. The wind had begun humming inside its pillars. He Yan inspected each opening and discovered that the bridge had been widened without changing the ropes below it. Lu Que noticed children holding their ears whenever the distant bell rang. The two men asked the school to close the bridge for one afternoon, despite the inconvenience to families who relied on it. They had anticipated a slow inspection, not the mountain of bureaucracy that met them. A commuter demanded to cross at once and called the strange noise a coincidence. The clerk issued a denial because the bridge's official diameter had not changed on paper. Yet the distinctive echo, measured by a teacher from the science faculty, showed that the stone had been hollowed deeper. He Yan asked for the actual measurements.

鹽島上，一座中空石橋供工人往返農田與學校。風開始在橋柱裡嗡鳴。何晏檢查每一個開口，發現橋面曾被加寬，底下的繩索卻沒跟著更換。陸雀注意到，每當遠方風鐘響起，孩子們就摀住耳朵。兩人請學校暫時封橋一個下午，雖然會使依賴它的家庭不便。 他們原先預料檢查會很慢，卻沒想到遇上如此繁複的官僚程序。一名通勤者要求立刻過橋，說怪聲只是巧合。書記以文件上橋的正式直徑未變為理由駁回請求。然而，一位科學學院教師量出的獨特回聲，證明石材被挖得更深。何晏要求查看實際數值。

The teacher brought old plans from the library. The drawings showed a curved channel that carried the sound of the central bell beneath the road. It had been intended to warn travelers during storms. A recent extension had changed the channel's pitch and made the warning sound almost constant. Birds could hear it from a much greater distance than people. Lu Que wondered whether all nine islands might carry similar hidden channels. An old fiscal report showed that only a fraction of the repair budget had reached the bridge. The missing supports were a genuine hazard, not an imaginary complaint. Lu Que visited a clinic where a healer was studying stress hormone changes among schoolchildren. Though the island was far from the sea, its ancient marine shell foundations still carried sound. The teacher warned that a dangerous level of noise could increase animal mortality during nesting season.

教師從圖書館帶來舊圖。圖紙顯示路面下有一條彎曲通道，把中央風鐘的聲音傳過橋。它原本用來在風暴時警告旅人。最近的擴建改變了通道的音高，使警報幾乎不停地響。鳥類能在遠比人更遠的距離聽見它。陸雀開始懷疑九座島上是否都有類似的隱藏通道。 一份舊財政報告顯示，只有一小部分維修預算真正用在橋上。缺少支撐構成真實危險，不是憑空抱怨。陸雀造訪診所，醫者正在研究學生的壓力荷爾蒙變化。島雖然遠離海洋，古老的海貝地基仍會傳遞聲音。教師警告，過高噪音可能增加築巢季動物的死亡率。

He Yan tested a removable wooden baffle rather than drilling more holes. The first board cracked. He began again with a shorter piece, allowing wind to escape through the upper arch. Lu Que stood below and signaled when the note softened. Together they discovered that the bridge could keep its warning function without carrying the harmful pitch. The teachers agreed to supervise a week of tests. The commuters would use the lower path in the meantime. They marked two parallel routes on the map so neither farm would be cut off. A father made an urgent plea for access to the clinic, and the local practitioner confirmed that the lower path was safe for a stretcher. The province inspector arrived expecting an argument, but He Yan reassured her with measurements. She helped uncover a second, older channel behind the stonework, a discovery that changed their understanding of the bell network.

何晏沒有再鑽孔，而是測試可以拆下的木製擋板。第一塊板裂了。他換用較短的木板，讓風從上方拱口逸出。陸雀站在下方，在聲音變柔和時打信號。兩人發現橋仍能保留警示功能，卻不必傳遞有害音高。教師們同意監督一週測試；通勤者暫時改走下方路徑。 他們在地圖上畫出兩條平行路線，避免任何農田與外界隔絕。一位父親急切請求保留通往診所的路，當地醫者確認下方小徑能安全運送擔架。省級督察原本以為會遇到爭吵，何晏卻用測量結果使她安心。她協助揭開石工後面第二條更古老的通道，這個發現改變了他們對整套風鐘網絡的理解。

At sunset, the children crossed the lower path in a quiet line. The bridge above them made a clear note only when a real gust struck its opening. Lu Que watched a crane pass across the sky without flinching. He Yan took a piece of the cracked baffle for later comparison. The pair now suspected that the nine islands had been linked by sound long before the modern shipping guild arrived. The inspector authorized the temporary closure and made the results available at the school. No ceremony followed. Instead, the students painted wind directions on stones along the alternative path. Their drawings would fade in rain and need to be renewed; He Yan found that ordinary maintenance more reassuring than a permanent monument celebrating one clever repair.

日落時，孩子們安靜地排成一列走過下方小路。上面的橋只有在真正的強風吹進孔洞時才發出清楚的聲音。陸雀看見一隻雲鶴掠過天空，沒有驚慌退縮。何晏帶走一小片裂開的擋板，以便日後比對。兩人如今懷疑，在現代航運工會出現前，九座島嶼就已由聲音連在一起。 督察批准暫時封橋，並在學校公開測試結果。沒有任何慶祝儀式。學生們只是在替代小徑的石頭上畫上風向。那些圖案會被雨水沖淡，必須不斷重新繪製；何晏覺得這種平凡維護，比紀念單次聰明修理的永久石碑更令人安心。
