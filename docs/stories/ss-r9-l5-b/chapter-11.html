第一座側塔的守護人

The Keeper of the First Tower

雲鶴歸巢：與你修一條天空的路 · 第 11 / 25 章 · 4000–5000 · 英中對照 · 校訂中

The first side tower stood behind a resort where wealthy pilots spent their holidays. Its owner had covered the old entrance with a painted board and turned the courtyard into an amusement garden. He Yan and Lu Que could hear the tower's faint note beneath the noise of visitors, but the garden's guards refused them entry. He Yan recognized the narrow door from the ancient map. Lu Que noticed that exhausted birds were trying to rest on the roof and being driven away by bright lanterns. He Yan was apt to speak too sharply when faced with a locked workshop. The garden contained a small casino, though the tower itself was much older than the games. A museum label tried to characterize its builders as careless dreamers. Lu Que found a clinical note showing that the lights had injured resting birds. The resort's guide claimed copyright over the tower drawings, but the older plans had been kept in the public school for generations.

第一座側塔位於富裕飛行員度假的旅館後方。旅館主人用彩繪木板遮住舊入口，把庭院改成遊樂花園。何晏與陸雀能從遊客噪音下聽見側塔微弱的音調，花園守衛卻不讓他們進去。何晏從古地圖認出那道窄門。陸雀則發現疲倦的鳥群試圖在屋頂休息，卻被明亮燈籠趕走。 何晏遇到上鎖的工坊時，往往容易說話太衝。花園裡有一間小賭場，但塔遠比遊戲設施古老。博物館標籤把建造者描述成粗心的夢想家。陸雀找到一份臨床紀錄，顯示燈光傷害了休息中的鳥。旅館嚮導聲稱塔的圖紙享有著作權，但更早的圖早已在公立學校保存數代。

A retired tower keeper approached them after hearing He Yan pronounce the old note sequence. She had once maintained the listening bowls and had lost her job when the resort bought the courtyard. Now she lived in a narrow room above the kitchen. The keeper did not ask them to fight the owner for her. She asked them to restore the sound that used to warn children when a storm approached. He Yan promised to examine the mechanism before naming a price. The old mechanism continued to fascinate him, and Lu Que gave a quiet gasp when the keeper opened the panel. A carved seal named her family as the tower's original heir, though ownership had passed to the town. Outside, a wounded crane gave a long howl, but the stone gears remained intact. The keeper recommended beginning with the lowest listening bowl, which could be tested without disturbing the rest of the structure.

一位退休守塔人聽見何晏念出古老的音調順序，主動走近。她以前負責維護聽音碗，後來旅館買下庭院，她便失去工作。如今她住在廚房上方狹窄的房間。守塔人沒有要求兩人替她和旅館主人爭鬥，而是請他們恢復過去用來警告孩子風暴將至的聲音。何晏答應先檢查機械，再談費用。 古老機構仍令何晏著迷；守塔人打開板蓋時，陸雀輕輕倒抽一口氣。雕刻封印稱她家族是塔最初的繼承人，但所有權早已轉交城鎮。外面一隻受傷雲鶴發出長長哀號，石齒輪卻仍完好。守塔人建議先從最低處的聽音碗開始，那裡可以測試而不影響整體結構。

The owner appeared with two guards and accused the travelers of disturbing guests. He Yan stepped away from the panel and asked the keeper whether she wanted to continue. She said yes, but only if the repair could remain public property. Lu Que suggested dimming the outer lanterns for a single evening, allowing birds to rest while the bowl was checked. The owner disliked the proposal because customers enjoyed the lights. The chef then reminded him that half the guests had come to watch the cranes. One guard carried an old rifle for show, but no one needed to use it. Lu Que pointed to a scar on a crane's wing and the sheer number of animals seeking refuge. The guests became spectators at an unexpected repair. When the tower's missing bronze pin was found under a bench, the keeper suspected theft rather than ordinary wear. He Yan recorded where it lay without deciding how it got there, then offered to upgrade the damaged hinge after its original part had been documented.

旅館主人帶著兩名守衛出現，指控旅人打擾客人。何晏離開板蓋旁，問守塔人是否還願意繼續。她說願意，但修復成果必須仍屬公共財產。陸雀建議只在今晚調暗外側燈籠，讓鳥能休息，大家也能檢查音碗。主人不喜歡這提議，因為客人喜歡亮燈。廚師卻提醒他，半數客人就是來看雲鶴的。 一名守衛拿著舊步槍充場面，但沒有人需要使用。陸雀指著雲鶴翅膀上的傷疤，以及尋求庇護的鳥多得驚人的數量。客人變成了一場意外維修的觀眾。塔失蹤的青銅插銷在長椅下被找到，守塔人懷疑是失竊，而非正常磨損。何晏記下發現位置，沒有先斷定它為何在那裡，並提出記錄原零件後再升級損壞的鉸鏈。

By nightfall, the resort lanterns were lowered. He Yan restored the bronze pin, and the first side tower answered the test horn with a soft blue note. The central bell still sent its harmful pulse, but the new note no longer strengthened it. A dozen cranes settled on the dark roof. The owner offered a fee, which the keeper directed toward a maintenance fund for the tower. He Yan accepted only enough to replace the rope he had used. A traveler using a wheelchair asked whether the tower path would also be repaired. The keeper took him into the workshop and showed him plans for a gentler ramp. She wanted the side tower open to everyone, not just people who could climb old stone stairs. Lu Que looked toward the next island, where a red sail moved in the distant wind.

入夜後，旅館燈籠調暗。何晏修回青銅插銷，第一座側塔用柔和的藍色音調回應測試號角。中央風鐘仍送來有害脈動，新音調卻不再使它增強。十幾隻雲鶴停在暗下的屋頂。旅館主人提出酬金，守塔人把錢轉入塔的維護基金。何晏只收下更換用過繩索所需的費用。 一名使用輪椅的旅人問塔旁的路是否也會修好。守塔人帶他進工坊，展示坡度更緩的斜道圖。她希望側塔對所有人開放，而不只是爬得動古石階的人。陸雀望向下一座島，那裡有一面紅帆在遠方風中移動。
