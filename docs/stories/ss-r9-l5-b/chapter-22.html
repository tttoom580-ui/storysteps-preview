The Repair That Had to Be Shared

雲鶴歸巢：與你修一條天空的路 · 第 22 / 25 章 · 4000–5000 · 英中對照 · 校訂中

The day before the still hour, the crew gathered beside the central bell to review every part of the new sleeve. Master Wen showed the young engineers which fastenings had failed in the storm. He Yan asked each station to name one reason it might need to stop. Lu Que reported the condition of the nearby nesting cliffs. They agreed that the repair could begin only if the watch boats remained in place and the side towers were ready to take over the warning. Their risk assessment was signed by a commission of workers from several islands. A carpenter brought a cedar coffin for the crane that had died, so the young helpers could take part in its burial later. He Yan struggled to conceive a schedule that left enough rest between jobs. The director accepted a temporary decline in cargo traffic and said he could envision a better system after the repairs, if everyone learned from the delays.

止風刻前一天，隊員在中央風鐘旁集合，逐一檢查新套環。文師傅向年輕工程師指出風暴中失效的扣件。何晏請每站說出一個可能必須停止的理由。陸雀報告附近築巢懸崖的狀況。大家同意只有瞭望船就位、側塔能接替警報時，修復才可以開始。 風險評估由來自多島工人的委員會簽署。一名木匠帶來雪松棺木，讓年輕幫手之後能參與死去雲鶴的安葬。何晏努力構想一份讓每項工作之間有足夠休息的時程。主管接受貨運量暫時下降，說只要大家從延誤中學習，他能想像修復後有更好的系統。

The following morning, the crews took their assigned places. The retired keeper listened at the blue tower, the mason waited beside the red sail, and the reservoir worker watched the silver-wave pool. At the central bell, He Yan checked the drum's speed with a handheld meter. Lu Que stood near the open gate, ready to signal if the cranes reacted badly. The sun rose, and for a brief moment the wind fell almost completely still. A judicial observer had been invited to make sure the repair rules were followed, not to direct the engineering. The day's milestone was the first safe fitting of the sleeve. A careless spectator tried to mock the cautious pace, but a nurse explained how much nutrient and rest the exhausted workers required. Tiny dust particles drifted through the still air. He Yan reminded everyone that the calm would end soon, and that the next command must be simple.

隔天早晨，各隊到指定位置。退休守塔人在藍塔聽音，石匠守在紅帆旁，水庫工人注視銀浪池。中央風鐘前，何晏用手持儀測量轉鼓速度。陸雀站在開放閘門附近，準備在鳥群反應不良時打信號。太陽升起，風在短短一瞬間幾乎完全平息。 一位司法觀察員受邀確認修復規則被遵守，不是來指揮工程。今天的里程碑是第一次安全安裝套環。一位粗心觀眾嘲笑謹慎速度，護理師卻解釋疲憊工人需要多少營養與休息。微小塵粒飄在靜止空氣裡。何晏提醒大家寧靜很快就會結束，下個命令必須簡短清楚。

He Yan and two workers guided the copper-leaf band around the drum. Wen read the fastening order aloud from the old manual. One bolt resisted, and the team stopped instead of forcing it. Lu Que noticed the air moving across the open gate and raised a yellow flag. He Yan changed the angle of the bolt, fitted it slowly, and then stepped back. When the drum turned again, the band held. The fourth channel remained quiet, while the older warning notes passed safely through the side towers. The retired keeper, who lived on a modest pension, thanked the younger workers for sharing the task. The repair had improved productivity only because it reduced harmful interruptions. For He Yan, the effect was more profound than any faster ship route. The mason's sibling, a young pilot, volunteered to make a solo flight outside the nesting corridor. He refused the stereotype that caution belonged only to people who were afraid.

何晏和兩名工人把銅葉帶套上轉鼓。文師傅照古手冊朗讀扣件次序。一顆螺栓卡住，隊伍停下，沒有硬扭。陸雀注意到空氣正流過開放閘門，舉起黃旗。何晏改變螺栓角度，慢慢裝妥後退開。轉鼓再度旋轉時，帶子牢牢固定。第四聲道保持安靜，舊警告音則安全地穿過側塔。 靠少量退休金過活的老守塔人感謝年輕工人共同完成任務。這次修復之所以提高生產效率，是因為減少有害中斷。對何晏而言，它的意義比任何更快的船線都深刻。石匠的手足是一位年輕飛行員，自願在築巢風廊之外單獨試飛。他拒絕「只有膽小的人才謹慎」的刻板印象。

One clear warning traveled from the central bell to each side tower. The blue note sounded first, then red, then silver. On the cliffs below, the cranes lifted their heads but stayed where they were. The human watch boats received the same warning through their lantern system, offering a second route if the bell failed again. The director declared the repair complete for the day. He Yan wrote down the remaining tests that would be needed before ordinary cargo speeds could return. No one thrust a victory banner into the air. The crews instead signed a treaty of maintenance duties between islands and listed subsequent inspections beside every tower. Lu Que watched a young crane stretch its wings in the clear afternoon light. He Yan met his eyes across the platform. Neither needed a grand speech to know the repair had changed what they could build together.

一聲清楚警報從中央風鐘傳到各側塔。藍音先響、接著紅音、最後銀音。下方懸崖上，雲鶴抬頭，卻留在原處。人工瞭望船也透過燈號收到同一警示，風鐘若再失效仍有第二條通路。主管宣布當日修復完成。何晏記下恢復一般貨運速度前仍必須做的測試。 沒有人猛然舉起勝利旗。隊員只是在群島之間簽下維護責任協議，列出每座塔後續的檢查。陸雀看著一隻幼雲鶴在清朗的午後伸展雙翅。何晏越過平台與他對望。兩人都不需要華麗演說，就明白這項修復改變了他們今後能共同建造的事物。
