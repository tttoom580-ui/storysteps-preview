蕨島上空的雲鶴

The Cranes Above Bracken

雲鶴歸巢：與你修一條天空的路 · 第 1 / 25 章 · 4000–5000 · 英中對照

At sunrise, He Yan climbed the roof of a seed house on Bracken Island and saw a flock of cloud cranes flying in a circle. The birds normally crossed the eastern gap without stopping, carrying pale pollen on their long feathers. Today their leader cried three times and turned back. He Yan was thirty-four, a maker of wind kites who had come to repair a broken delivery bridge, not to study migrating creatures. The sound made him lower his tools. He wanted certainty before he acted, so he went to the old chapel where a cluster of farmers had gathered. The council offered compensation for ruined crops, but refused to disclose the reason the flight route had changed. An envious apprentice muttered that the wind guild always received the best instruments while ordinary growers received excuses. He Yan listened instead of defending his former teachers.

日出時，何晏爬上蕨島種子屋的屋頂，看見一群雲鶴在空中盤旋。這些鳥平常會直接飛越東方的空隙，長羽毛上帶著淡色花粉。今天領頭的雲鶴叫了三聲，卻回頭飛走。三十四歲的何晏是製作風箏的工匠，原本來修理損壞的送貨橋，並不是研究遷徙生物。那聲叫喚使他放下工具。 他希望先確定情況才採取行動，因此走到舊禮拜堂，幾名農民正聚集在那裡。議會願意補償毀壞的作物，卻拒絕揭露飛行路線改變的原因。一名羨慕工匠器材的學徒嘀咕，風工會總能拿到最好的儀器，普通農民卻只得到藉口。何晏沒有為舊師門辯護，而是認真聆聽。

At the noon meeting a tall young man interrupted the officer who blamed the birds. Lu Que, thirty-two, had tended the nesting cliffs for twelve years. He laid a feather beside the wind map, showing soot on the side that should have faced clean air. He Yan placed his compass next to it. The compass needle was steady, but the feather trembled when the bell sounded from the distant central island. They had found something neither could explain alone. The meeting had begun as a public forum about a faulty generator. Farmers feared for the fragile habitat on their slopes and for an agricultural heritage older than the wind guild. An immense shadow swept over the square when the cranes turned. Lu Que resisted the impulse to shout at the officials. Instead, his insight was simple: the birds were not lost; an unfamiliar sound was blocking their usual passage.

中午的集會上，一名高個子的年輕男子打斷了責怪鳥群的官員。三十二歲的陸雀已照顧築巢懸崖十二年。他把一根羽毛放在風圖旁，上面本應朝向乾淨空氣的一側沾了煙灰。何晏把羅盤放在旁邊。羅盤指針很穩，羽毛卻在遠方中央島嶼的鐘聲響起時顫動。他們找到一個單靠任何一方都無法解釋的現象。 集會原本是討論一臺故障發電機的公開論壇。農民擔心山坡上脆弱的棲息地，也擔心比風工會更古老的農耕傳承。雲鶴轉向時，巨大的影子掠過廣場。陸雀克制了對官員怒吼的衝動。他的洞察其實很簡單：鳥不是迷路，而是陌生的聲音擋住了牠們平常的通道。

The metropolitan office proposed catching the entire flock and sending it by freight lift. Lu Que refused. A crane would stop feeding if bound for more than one night, he explained, and no clever machine could replace a season of wild flight. He Yan knew the old wind guild had once used netted cages for emergencies, but he also knew the cages damaged wings. He offered a narrow test using only an empty kite and a listening bowl. An optional vote was offered, but the growers demanded a written provision protecting the flock. When a boy began to stumble on the crowded stairs, Lu Que steadied him; the guild supervisor did not even look up from his ledger. He Yan saw a transaction being arranged for rented cages, and quietly asked that no money change hands before the results of the test. This was the first decision they made together.

都會辦公室提議捕捉整群雲鶴，用貨梯運送。陸雀拒絕了。他解釋，如果雲鶴被綑超過一夜，就會停止進食；再聰明的機器也無法取代一整季的野外飛行。何晏知道舊風工會曾在緊急情況使用網籠，也知道籠子會傷害翅膀。他提議只用空風箏與聽音碗做小範圍測試。 官員提出自願表決，但農民要求先有保護鳥群的書面條款。一名男孩在擁擠樓梯上踉蹌時，陸雀扶住了他；工會主管連頭都沒從帳本抬起來。何晏看見有人正在安排租借籠子的交易，便冷靜要求測試結果出來前不要付款。這是他們第一次共同做決定。

They climbed to the root tower at dusk. A living cedar held the old rope anchors, and the listening bowl gave a low note whenever air passed the southern edge. The note rose each time the central bell rang. He Yan moved the empty kite two steps west and watched its tail settle. Lu Que opened a basket of fresh leaves. The nearest crane came down to rest, freely, on the cedar branch. Neither man tried to touch it. At the base of the tower, seedlings had started to wither where the flock no longer scattered pollen. A farmer brought water and asked whether the two visitors would stay. He Yan said he could repair a bridge but could not promise to command the sky. Lu Que answered that a road for birds must leave them free to fly away. They agreed to inspect the neighboring islands before the next seventh dawn.

黃昏時他們爬上樹根塔。一株活雪松固定著古老的繩索錨點，每當空氣流過南側，聽音碗就會發出低音。中央風鐘每響一次，那聲音就升高。何晏把空風箏向西移兩步，見到尾帶恢復穩定。陸雀打開一籃鮮葉。最近的一隻雲鶴自願降下，停在雪松枝上休息。兩人都沒有試著碰牠。 塔底的幼苗因鳥群不再散播花粉而開始枯萎。一位農民帶來水，問兩名訪客會不會留下。何晏說他能修橋，卻不能承諾命令天空。陸雀回答，為鳥群建造的道路必須容許牠們自由飛走。他們約定在下一個第七日黎明前，查看鄰近各島的情況。
