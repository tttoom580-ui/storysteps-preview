容得下兩個人的天空

A Sky with Room for Two

雲鶴歸巢：與你修一條天空的路 · 第 25 / 25 章 · 4000–5000 · 英中對照 · 校訂中

Spring arrived across the nine floating islands without a single announcement from the central bell. The cranes brought pollen to the northern gardens, and new leaves appeared on trees that had stood empty for years. He Yan was offered a senior post in the restored wind guild, with a workshop near the capital and a steady income. Lu Que was asked to lead the seasonal habitat team on Bracken Island. For the first time, their plans for the future pointed toward different places. He Yan walked alongside Lu Que beside the canal that watered the new nursery. They discussed an alternate route for supply carts and why the city's old argument about communism and shared property had never settled who should maintain a public tree. What mattered was a crucial practical choice: the road would evolve only if the people living beside it could guide its use. He Yan loosened his grip on the capital's offer and asked what Lu Que wanted.

春天來到九座浮島，中央風鐘沒有為它發布任何公告。雲鶴把花粉帶回北方花園，多年光禿的樹開始長新葉。修復後的風工會邀何晏擔任高職，提供都城附近工坊與穩定收入。陸雀則被邀回蕨島領導季節性棲地隊伍。他們的未來計畫第一次指向不同地方。 何晏與陸雀並肩走在灌溉新苗圃的水渠旁。他們談運貨車的替代路線，也談城市過去關於共產主義與共有財產的爭論，從未真正解決誰該維護公共樹木。真正重要的是一個關鍵的實際選擇：只有住在路旁的人能參與引導使用，道路才會持續演變。何晏放鬆對都城職位的執著，問陸雀想要什麼。

Lu Que answered that he wanted to keep the nursery open to wild birds and young apprentices alike. He did not want He Yan to abandon his craft or accept a lesser future merely to stay near him. He Yan explained that he could build a small traveling workshop on Bracken Island and teach repairs across the other islands by season. He did not know whether such a life would be profitable. Lu Que said he could live with uncertainty, but not with a promise made without thought. He Yan presented a hypothesis: a traveling workshop could survive through shared repairs and seasonal teaching. Lu Que answered with cautious optimism and offered to help test the idea for one year. They wanted originality in the program, not a copy of the old guild's rigid orders. A poetic phrase from an ancient prophet was written above the nursery door: listen before the wind turns. Beneath it, a small pulse of sound came from the living root tower.

陸雀回答，他想讓苗圃同時向野鳥與年輕學徒開放，也不希望何晏為了待在自己身邊，便放棄手藝或接受較差的未來。何晏說，他能在蕨島建小型移動工坊，按季節到其他島教授維修。他不知道這樣的生活是否賺錢。陸雀說他可以接受不確定，卻不能接受未經思考便做出的承諾。 何晏提出一項假設：移動工坊可透過共同修繕與季節教學維持。陸雀帶著謹慎樂觀回答，願意一起試行一年。他們希望計畫有原創性，而不是照抄舊工會僵硬命令。苗圃門上寫著古老預言者留下的一句詩意話語：風轉前，先聆聽。門下活根塔傳來一點輕微的聲音脈動。

They opened the workshop together before the summer rains. Its rules were simple: every test had a clear stop signal, every student could ask questions, and no animal could be used to prove a machine's power. The first class included a former cargo pilot, two orchard apprentices, and the boy who had once guarded the road on Tin Island. He Yan taught them knots. Lu Que taught them to read feathers, leaves, and the movement of tiny insects before choosing where to build. There was no fixed quota of repairs to finish each month. A cooperative shareholder from the market offered tools rather than orders. When the first cranes began to soar above the roof, Lu Que asked the students to specify what they observed before celebrating. The sight was a stimulus to work carefully, not an excuse to hurry. He Yan saw a striking resemblance between the open sky above them and the first safe corridor they had built together.

夏雨前，他們一起開了工坊。規則很簡單：每次測試要有清楚停止信號、每位學生都能提問、任何動物都不能被用來證明機器力量。第一班學員有前貨運飛行員、兩名果園學徒，還有以前在錫島守路的少年。何晏教繩結，陸雀教他們在選擇建造地點前，先讀懂羽毛、樹葉與細小昆蟲的移動。 工坊沒有每月必須完成的固定維修配額。一位來自市集合作社的持股成員提供工具，而不是命令。第一批雲鶴在屋頂上方翱翔時，陸雀請學生先明確說出觀察所見，再來慶祝。這景象是謹慎工作的動力，不是催促的藉口。何晏看見頭頂開闊天空與兩人最初修出的安全風廊有驚人的相似。

One evening, after the students had gone home, He Yan asked Lu Que whether he would like to share the small house beside the nursery. He said he was asking because he wanted a life with him, not because the work required it. Lu Que took his hand and answered yes. They left the door open to the garden, where the last cloud cranes of the day were settling among the branches. The future remained changeable, but for the first time that uncertainty felt like a path they could walk together. At the next village gathering, the workshop gave every apprentice a repair voucher for one community task. He Yan returned the final borrowed measuring tool, and Lu Que placed a bowl of water beneath the cedar. Above the tree, the flock moved freely into a sky that belonged to no guild. The two men watched until the white wings became small points in the gold light, then went inside together.

有天傍晚，學生都回家後，何晏問陸雀，願不願意一起住在苗圃旁的小屋。他說自己這樣問，是因為想與陸雀共度生活，不是工作上必須如此。陸雀握住他的手，回答願意。他們讓通往花園的門敞開，當天最後的雲鶴正落在枝間。未來仍會變動，但這一次，不確定性像一條他們能一起走的路。 下一次村中集會，工坊給每位學徒一張社區維修工作的兌換券。何晏歸還最後一件借用的測量工具，陸雀在雪松下放一碗水。樹上方，鳥群自由飛向不屬於任何工會的天空。兩人看著白色翅膀在金光裡變成小點，然後一起走進屋內。
