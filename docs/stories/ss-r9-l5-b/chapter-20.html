沒有主人的風暴

A Storm With No Master

Shortly after midnight, the great spring front struck the central island. The bell began ringing too fast, its fourth channel throwing a harsh note across the migration route. Cargo pilots signaled that they could not see through the clouds. He Yan reached the control room with the copper sleeve, but the drum was turning too quickly to approach. Lu Que ran to the lower observation deck and reported that the resting birds were lifting from their ledges in panic. Their first complication was a strict constraint: touching the drum at full speed could be deadly. The director presented an elaborate emergency chart, but sparks began to erupt from the control panel before he finished speaking. A metal fragment struck the floor. He Yan drew a graphic sketch of the failing mechanism and gave the crew a simple interpretation: the extra channel was feeding vibration back into the bell.

午夜過後不久，春季大風前鋒擊中中央島。風鐘開始敲得太快，第四聲道把刺耳的音調投向遷徙路線。貨運飛行員發信號說看不穿雲層。何晏帶著銅葉套環趕到控制室，轉鼓卻旋得太快，無法接近。陸雀跑到下方觀測台，報告休息中的鳥正驚慌地飛離平台。 第一個難題是嚴格限制：在全速轉動時碰觸轉鼓，可能致命。主管拿出複雜的緊急圖表，還沒說完，控制台就迸出火花。一塊金屬碎片打到地上。何晏迅速畫下故障機械的圖，向工人簡單解釋：多出的聲道正在把震動回送到風鐘裡。

The crew proposed cutting the fourth channel, but doing so would silence the storm warning for cargo pilots. He Yan said they needed to send one final warning first, then switch to the slower human watch boats already stationed along the route. The director hesitated because the company had never attempted that procedure. Lu Que pointed out that the old channels were still functioning. They could use them to carry one clear warning before separating the fourth line. The proposed intervention differed from mainstream practice, so the director asked who could qualify to authorize it. Lu Que explained the risk ratio between one controlled silence and hours of uncontrolled noise. A senator on the emergency call worried about lost shipping revenue. He Yan refused to tempt anyone with the promise of perfect safety. They could reduce one danger only by accepting a slower, carefully marked route for ships.

工人提議切斷第四聲道，卻會讓貨運飛行員失去風暴警報。何晏說必須先送出最後一次警示，再改用已經部署在路上的慢速人工瞭望船。主管猶豫，因為公司從未試過這個程序。陸雀指出舊聲道仍在運作，可以先用它們傳送一次清楚警報，再分開第四條線。 這項介入措施不同於主流做法，主管詢問誰有資格批准。陸雀解釋一次受控停聲與數小時無法控制噪音之間的風險比例。緊急通話中的參議員擔心航運收入損失。何晏拒絕用絕對安全的承諾誘惑任何人。他們只能透過接受較慢、標記清楚的航線，降低其中一種危險。

The signal went out. On the dark water below, human watch boats raised their lanterns one after another. The cargo pilots answered with blue flags and reduced speed. He Yan then pulled the release lever, opening the fourth channel's emergency gate. The drum slowed, but the damaged support began to bend. Instead of rushing forward with the sleeve, he told the crew to retreat and brace the frame from outside. Lu Que held the lower warning line until everyone had stepped clear. An unprecedented blast of wind passed through the chamber, creating a momentary vacuum behind the open gate. The central bell began to unfold its outer panels to release pressure. A seasoned warrior from the island guard helped carry the injured crew to shelter. He Yan checked for hidden heat radiation before allowing anyone near the drum again. The storm had not ended, but the harshest note had stopped crossing the nesting cliffs.

警報送出去了。下方黑暗的水面上，人工瞭望船一艘接一艘舉起燈。貨運飛行員以藍旗回應，並降低速度。何晏這才拉下釋放拉桿，打開第四聲道緊急閘門。轉鼓慢下來，損壞支撐卻開始彎曲。他沒有急著拿套環衝過去，而是叫工人撤退，從外側撐住骨架。陸雀一直守著下方警報線，直到所有人安全離開。 前所未有的強風穿過鐘室，在開啟的閘門後造成短暫真空。中央風鐘的外層板開始展開，釋放壓力。島衛一名有經驗的戰士協助搬運受傷工人到庇護處。何晏檢查是否有隱藏的熱輻射，才允許任何人再次靠近轉鼓。風暴尚未結束，但最刺耳的聲音已不再越過築巢懸崖。

At dawn the storm moved east, leaving the central bell silent for the first time in years. The cargo ships remained safe on the slow marked route, although their deliveries were delayed. Lu Que returned from the observation deck with news that the young cranes had settled again. He Yan sat on the floor beside the unused sleeve. They had not completed the repair. They had chosen a controlled stop over a dangerous victory, and that choice had kept both people and birds alive. Master Wen arrived with the morning inspection crew. He saw the open emergency gate and nodded without claiming that the old design had been right all along. He Yan handed him the broken fragment and asked him to help calculate a safer sequence for the next still hour. Lu Que walked beside them as the first pale light returned to the clouds.

黎明時風暴向東移動，中央風鐘多年來第一次安靜下來。貨船沿緩慢而有標記的航線保持安全，雖然交貨延後。陸雀從觀測台回來，帶來幼雲鶴已再度落下休息的消息。何晏坐在地上，身旁放著還沒用上的套環。他們沒有完成修復，而是選擇受控停止，不追求危險的勝利。這個選擇讓人與鳥都活了下來。 文師傅與早晨檢查隊一起到場。他看見開啟的緊急閘門，點頭，沒有宣稱舊設計從頭到尾都正確。何晏把斷裂碎片交給他，請他協助計算下個止風刻更安全的順序。第一道淡光回到雲層時，陸雀與他們並肩走著。
