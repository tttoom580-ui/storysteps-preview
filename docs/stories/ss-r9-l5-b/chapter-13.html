雲下水庫

The Reservoir Below the Clouds

雲鶴歸巢：與你修一條天空的路 · 第 13 / 25 章 · 4000–5000 · 英中對照 · 校訂中

The silver-wave tower stood on the edge of a deep reservoir beneath the drifting islands. The rock platform was accessible only by a narrow lift that moved with the wind. He Yan refused to take heavy tools until he had tested the lift's cables. Lu Que searched the water's edge for bird tracks and found a place where injured cranes had been resting without shelter. They made a small camp rather than approach the tower in darkness. An acute shortage of medicine troubled the camp. The reservoir administrator blamed travelers, but Lu Que noticed a bias in the ration list. One defendant in an old water dispute had been denied supplies despite a serious wound. The healer confirmed the man was not contagious, yet he still needed treatment. He Yan measured the lift in a different dimension, checking its sideways movement rather than height alone. A documentary team working nearby agreed to film the measurements instead of interrupting the injured people.

銀浪側塔立在漂浮島下方一座深水庫的邊緣。岩台只能搭乘隨風移動的狹窄升降梯抵達。何晏在測試纜線前拒絕搬重工具。陸雀沿水邊搜尋鳥跡，找到一處受傷雲鶴曾休息、卻沒有遮蔽的地方。他們先搭小營地，沒有在黑暗中接近塔。 營地面臨嚴重的藥物短缺。水庫管理人責怪旅客，陸雀卻看出配給表存在偏見。一名舊水權糾紛中的被告即使重傷，也遭拒發物資。醫者確認他並沒有傳染性疾病，卻仍需要治療。何晏從另一個維度測量升降梯，除了高度，也檢查側向晃動。附近的紀錄片團隊同意拍攝測量過程，不打擾傷患。

At dawn they crossed to the platform. The tower was made of pale stone, and its bell was suspended above a circular pool. He Yan discovered that the pool's surface served as the third listening bowl. When wind passed beneath the islands, the water vibrated in wide rings. Yet a new concrete wall divided the pool, causing the notes to reflect back toward the nesting cliffs. Lu Que had seen the cranes turn away from precisely that reflection. The workers had erected the wall to protect the lift, and no one had thought to flip its direction. Gravity pulled the old stone anchors down, but the new wall trapped sound above the water. A senator had introduced legislation requiring stronger flood barriers, yet the rules said nothing about the bell's effect on animals. He Yan asked to oversee a model test before anyone removed a block. Lu Que stood beside him with the listening fork.

黎明時兩人來到岩台。側塔以淡色石頭建造，鐘懸在圓形水池上方。何晏發現池面其實是第三只聽音碗。風從島下通過時，水面會震出寬闊的圓環。但一面新混凝土牆分開池子，使聲音反射回築巢懸崖。陸雀曾看見雲鶴正好避開這股反射。 工人為保護升降梯而豎起牆，沒人想到要翻轉方向。重力把舊石錨向下拉，新牆卻把聲音困在水面上方。一名參議員曾提出要求更強防洪牆的立法，規則卻沒提風鐘對動物的影響。何晏要求在拆除任何石塊前先監督模型測試，陸雀拿著聽音叉站在旁邊。

They tested three small clay walls inside a basin. One reflected the harmful note, one blocked the lift's warning, and the third let both signals pass without strengthening the echo. The third design used a slanted opening that could be closed during exceptional floods. The administrator remained doubtful until a storm pilot confirmed that the warning would still reach the lift. Lu Que pointed out that the cranes would need a quiet resting ledge as well. The administrator tried to overturn the plan on the premise that no repair could serve both people and birds. He Yan placed a small sphere of clay in the pool to demonstrate the new opening. Nearby, a healer offered therapy for workers injured in the previous flood. Each delay took a toll on the camp, but rushing the full repair might have created a worse disaster. At sunset, the administrator allowed a controlled trial.

他們在水盆裡測試三面小泥牆。第一面反射有害音高，第二面阻擋升降梯警示，第三面則能讓兩種信號通過而不增強回音。第三種設計有個傾斜開口，特大洪水時可以關閉。管理人仍抱懷疑，直到一名暴風飛行員確認警報仍能傳到升降梯。陸雀指出雲鶴也需要安靜的休息平台。 管理人想以人與鳥不可能同時受益的前提推翻計畫。何晏把一顆小泥球放進池子，示範新開口。附近的醫者為先前洪水中受傷的工人提供治療。每次拖延都使營地付出代價，但貿然全面施工可能造成更大災難。日落時，管理人終於批准受控試驗。

The workers cut a narrow opening in the wall and supported it with a stone arch. The water found the new passage. For the first time since the rebuilding, the silver-wave tower sounded without driving the cranes away. The blue and red towers answered faintly from above the clouds. He Yan listened carefully, counting the spaces between the three notes. They were not yet in the right order, but the notes could finally reach one another without violence. On the shore, a child offered them an olive-colored ribbon and called the repair weird but beautiful. He Yan tied the ribbon beside the new opening so the crew could spot it in mist. Lu Que watched the water settle and wondered whether a bell could learn patience from the creatures it was built to guide. Their next task would require all three towers to answer at the same still hour.

工人切出牆上的狹窄開口，用石拱加固。水找到了新的通路。自重建以來，銀浪側塔第一次發出沒有驚走雲鶴的音調。藍塔與紅塔從雲上微弱回應。何晏仔細數著三個音之間的空隙。順序還不對，但它們終於能在不製造傷害的情況下彼此傳達。 岸上，一名孩子送他們橄欖色緞帶，說這項修復很奇怪，卻很漂亮。何晏把緞帶繫在新開口旁，讓工人在霧裡也能辨認。陸雀看著水面平靜，心想原本用來引導生物的鐘，是否也能從生物身上學會耐心。下一項任務，要求三座側塔在同一個止風刻回應。
