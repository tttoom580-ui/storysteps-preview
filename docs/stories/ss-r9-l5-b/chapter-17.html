工程師沒有寄出的信

The Engineer's Unsent Letter

In the control room attic, with the custodian's permission, He Yan opened a locked box of letters written by the engineer who had built the extra channel. The engineer, Master Wen, was still alive but had retired to a nearby island. One letter described how the new drum prevented a deadly cargo collision. Another described the first season when cloud cranes stopped coming to the northern cliffs. The two events were separated by only weeks. He Yan asked permission to speak with Wen before changing his design. The accounting sheets were comparable across those years, but they could not describe the lives of the birds. Lu Que said he had come to adore the quiet moments when cranes cleaned their feathers. He Yan offered to conduct an independent test rather than demand a confession from anyone. The director sent a delegate to accompany them and promised not to interfere. They left the dusty attic through a narrow service stair.

控制室閣樓裡，何晏經保管人同意，打開一盒上鎖的信，信是當年設計額外聲道的文師傅所寫。文師傅還活著，卻已退休到鄰島。一封信描述新轉鼓如何避免致命貨船碰撞；另一封描述雲鶴第一次不再飛到北方懸崖的季節。兩件事只差幾週。何晏請求先與文師傅談話，再修改設計。 那些年的會計帳目可以相互比較，卻無法描述鳥群的生命。陸雀說，他已很喜愛看雲鶴安靜梳理羽毛的時刻。何晏提出獨立測試，而不是向任何人強求認罪。主管派一名代表陪同，承諾不會干涉。他們從布滿灰塵的閣樓走下一道狹窄勤務梯。

Master Wen lived in a cottage surrounded by reeds. He greeted the visitors without surprise, saying he had expected the bell to bring someone to his door eventually. He Yan showed the new measurements, and Wen recognized the dangerous pitch immediately. He admitted that the old test had used empty models, not living birds. He had urged the company to repeat the trials after the first migration failed, but his request had been delayed during a series of storms. Wen's devotion to saving travelers had produced an unexpectedly destructive result. He led them through a low doorway and showed a charcoal filter built for the drum. He had hoped to initiate a redesign, but the idea never reached the board. A legendary guild master had once predicted that strong bells would end all accidents. Wen now understood the likelihood of such certainty was very small, and his motive had not been enough to make the design safe.

文師傅住在蘆葦環繞的小屋。他不意外訪客到來，說自己早預料風鐘終究會把某人帶到門口。何晏展示新測量，文師傅立刻認出危險音高。他承認舊測試只用空模型，沒有活鳥。第一次遷徙失敗後，他曾要求公司重做試驗，但一連串風暴使要求被延誤。 文師傅致力拯救旅人的奉獻，卻造成意料外的破壞結果。他帶兩人穿過低矮門口，展示為轉鼓製作的木炭濾音器。他原想啟動重新設計，想法卻從未送到董事會。一位傳奇工會師父曾預言，強力風鐘能終結所有事故。文師傅如今理解這種確定性的可能性很低，善意動機也不足以使設計安全。

Lu Que asked Wen to describe the last day he saw a full flock above his cottage. The old engineer looked toward the reeds and spoke slowly. He remembered the sound of hundreds of wings and the sudden silence that followed when the new drum began to turn. He had chosen to believe the birds would return the next year. When they did not, he lacked the courage to go back to the company. Lu Que did not offer quick forgiveness; he asked Wen to help correct the harm now. A cold draft made Wen shiver, and a small crane outside began to sniff at the cottage doorway. The old man said no supreme authority could undo the lost nesting seasons. He passed He Yan a strip of rough metal, asking him to feel its texture. The drum's interior had the same pattern, a flaw that increased the harmful note. The knowledge had tormented Wen for years, but he had kept the sample rather than destroy it.

陸雀請文師傅描述最後一次看見完整鳥群掠過小屋的那一天。老工程師望著蘆葦，慢慢說起。他記得幾百雙翅膀的聲音，以及新轉鼓開始轉動後突然降臨的寂靜。他曾選擇相信鳥群隔年會回來。等牠們沒有回來時，他已沒有勇氣回公司。陸雀沒有立刻原諒，而是請文師傅現在一起改正傷害。 冷風使文師傅打寒顫，外面一隻小雲鶴在小屋門口嗅聞。老人說，沒有任何至高權威能撤銷失去的築巢季節。他把一片粗金屬交給何晏，請他觸摸質地。轉鼓內部有同樣紋理，那項缺陷會增強有害音高。這個認知折磨文師傅多年，他卻留下樣本，沒有銷毀。

He Yan studied the sample and proposed a narrow sleeve that could soften the drum without destroying its storm-warning function. Wen said the sleeve would have to be fitted during a still hour, when the drum moved most slowly. The next still hour was four mornings away. Lu Que asked what they could do before then. Wen suggested reducing cargo speed and using human signal boats along the most dangerous lanes. The company would not like it, but it could be done immediately. He Yan copied the sleeve design before leaving. Wen asked to come with them, even though his hands were no longer steady enough to fit it. Lu Que said the old engineer could teach the younger workers what he had learned and warn them against repeating the mistake. The journey back to the central island was quiet, but it no longer felt like two strangers carrying an impossible burden alone.

何晏研究樣本後，提出裝上狹窄套環，降低轉鼓噪音，卻不破壞暴風警報功能。文師傅說，套環必須在轉鼓最慢的止風刻安裝。下次止風刻還有四個早晨。陸雀問之前能做什麼。文師傅建議降低貨運速度，並在最危險的航道使用人工信號船。公司不會喜歡，但可以立刻執行。 離開前，何晏抄下套環設計。文師傅請求同行，雖然雙手已不夠穩，不能親自安裝。陸雀說，老工程師可以把學到的事教給年輕工人，也提醒他們別重蹈覆轍。回中央島的路很安靜，卻不再像兩個陌生人獨自背負不可能的重擔。
