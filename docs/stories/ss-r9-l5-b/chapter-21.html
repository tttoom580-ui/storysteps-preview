寂靜之後的早晨

The Morning After the Silence

雲鶴歸巢：與你修一條天空的路 · 第 21 / 25 章 · 4000–5000 · 英中對照 · 校訂中

The islands woke to an unfamiliar quiet. Without the central bell's repeated warning, ship crews moved cautiously along the marked lanes, guided by watch boats and colored flags. The system was slower, but no vessel had been lost during the storm. In the nesting meadows, Lu Que counted the surviving cranes. Most had returned to shelter, though several were exhausted and one had died before reaching the western ledge. He Yan stood beside him, understanding that a safe ending for one night did not erase every loss. He Yan felt quiet affection for Lu Que, who kept caring for the birds when applause had ended. A temporary alliance of pilots, farmers, and healers agreed to authorize a slower shipping route. They carried water to a barren nesting patch and prepared a respectful burial for the dead crane. The town's planned carnival had been postponed, and its cooking tents were turned into relief kitchens instead.

群島在陌生的安靜中醒來。少了中央風鐘反覆警示，船員沿標記航道謹慎前進，由瞭望船和彩旗引導。系統變慢了，但風暴中沒有船失蹤。築巢草地裡，陸雀清點存活雲鶴。大多數已回庇護處，但有幾隻筋疲力竭，一隻在抵達西方平台前死去。何晏站在他身旁，明白某一夜的安全結局無法抹平每項損失。 何晏對陸雀生出安靜的深情；掌聲結束後，他仍在照顧鳥群。飛行員、農民與醫者成立臨時聯盟，同意授權較慢的航運路線。他們把水送到貧瘠的築巢區，為死去的雲鶴準備莊重的埋葬。鎮上原本要舉辦的嘉年華延後，料理帳篷則改成救助廚房。

The company director visited the meadow with Master Wen. He did not ask for forgiveness on behalf of the bell company. He brought a list of ships that had arrived late and the hospitals waiting for their supplies. Lu Que and He Yan studied it with the farmers. Some deliveries were urgent. Others could wait until the damaged drum was made safe. They agreed that every decision about transport needed to consider both those waiting at the docks and those living beneath the air lanes. The town monitored fuel consumption to keep the watch boats running. No one wanted to convict a mechanic simply for following an old order, but the director agreed to donate money for repair. Lu Que carried a jug of water past the stalled machinery. The new mechanism would need a public test before anyone called it an eternal solution. At the kitchen, volunteers served bread with mustard and warm soup to tired pilots.

公司主管和文師傅來到草地。他沒有代表風鐘公司要求原諒，而是帶來延誤船舶名單，以及等待物資的醫院資料。陸雀與何晏和農民一起研究。部分貨物十分緊急，其他的可以等轉鼓安全後再送。他們同意，任何運輸決定都必須同時顧及碼頭等待的人，以及住在航道下方的生命。 鎮上監測燃料消耗，確保瞭望船能繼續工作。沒有人想因機械師服從舊命令就定罪，但主管同意捐款修復。陸雀提著一壺水，走過停下的機器。新機構必須經過公開測試，才有資格被稱作長久解法。廚房裡，志願者把塗芥末的麵包與熱湯端給疲倦飛行員。

By afternoon, the red and blue towers were sending short, clear signals at agreed hours. The silver-wave tower waited until the reservoir crew confirmed stable water levels. He Yan wrote a schedule that named the person responsible at every station. He also marked a space for the person who might need to stop the next test. The director signed the plan, not because he had been forced, but because his workers had explained why the old arrangement could no longer continue. The director declared that the emergency route was operational and asked the crew to proclaim the change honestly. A few posters still carried propaganda promising instant speed without risk. Lu Que's rescue squad removed those posters and replaced them with current times and safety limits. Stability depended on accurate transmission of warnings, not vague claims that the sky would always obey human plans.

下午，紅塔與藍塔開始在約定時刻發出短而清楚的信號。銀浪塔等到水庫人員確認水位穩定才加入。何晏寫下時程表，列出每一站的負責人，也留下誰能停止下次測試的欄位。主管在計畫上簽名，不是因為被逼迫，而是工人已向他說明舊安排為何不能繼續。 主管宣布緊急路線已可運作，並要求人員誠實公告變更。少數海報仍有宣傳口號，承諾毫無風險的即時高速。陸雀的救援小隊把它們撤下，改貼目前時刻與安全限制。穩定取決於警告的準確傳送，而不是模糊宣稱天空永遠服從人類計畫。

At dusk, He Yan found Lu Que beside the youngest surviving crane. They sat for a long time without speaking. Finally He Yan said he had been afraid of disappointing him with another failed machine. Lu Que answered that the failure would have been pretending danger did not exist. He placed one hand over He Yan's, giving him time to move away. He Yan stayed. The next still hour was close; they would work again, but tonight they could rest. The meadow became quiet as the last relief boat returned to the dock. Lu Que covered the young crane with a light shade but left the path to open air unobstructed. He Yan put away the measuring cord and stayed beside him. Their work had brought them to an uncertain place, and neither needed to pretend the next morning would be easy.

黃昏時，何晏在最年幼的存活雲鶴旁找到陸雀。兩人並肩坐了很久，沒有說話。最後何晏坦承，他一直害怕另一臺失敗的機器會令陸雀失望。陸雀回答，真正的失敗是裝作危險不存在。他把一隻手覆在何晏手上，留時間讓對方退開。何晏沒有退。下個止風刻很快會來，他們還要再工作，但今晚可以休息。 最後一艘救助船回到碼頭，草地慢慢安靜。陸雀替幼雲鶴架起輕遮棚，仍讓通往戶外的路暢通。何晏收起測量繩，留在他身邊。他們的工作把彼此帶到充滿不確定性的地方，誰也不必假裝明天就會輕鬆。
