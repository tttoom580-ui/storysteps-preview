銅葉與真實代價

Copper Leaves, Honest Costs

雲鶴歸巢：與你修一條天空的路 · 第 18 / 25 章 · 4000–5000 · 英中對照 · 校訂中

He Yan needed strong, flexible material for the new drum sleeve. A grove of copper-leaf trees on Orchard Island produced fibers that could withstand heat without losing their shape. The trees were protected, and taking too many branches would damage the next season's fruit. Lu Que suggested collecting only fallen leaves and old bark. He Yan calculated how many pieces were required for the sleeve and agreed that the repair could proceed slowly rather than strip the orchard bare. The island administration controlled the old grove under an antique land agreement. A bizarre sign warned visitors not to touch even fallen fabric-like leaves. He Yan wondered whether the rule had been written to conceal poor maintenance. Lu Que asked the keeper to explain the practical framework of conservation rather than argue about corruption. The first inspection found only gross neglect, not proof that anyone had stolen the trees.

何晏需要結實又有彈性的材料製作轉鼓套環。果園島的銅葉樹會產生能耐熱、不易變形的纖維。這些樹受到保護，砍太多枝會傷害下一季果實。陸雀建議只收集落葉與老樹皮。何晏算出套環需要的材料數量，同意讓工程慢慢進行，也不把果園剝光。 島上行政機構依一份古老土地協議管理樹林。一塊古怪告示禁止旅人連像布料般的落葉都碰。何晏懷疑規則是否為了掩蓋維護不善。陸雀請管理人解釋保育的實際架構，不急著爭論貪腐。第一次檢查只證明了嚴重疏忽，並沒有發現有人偷樹的證據。

They walked the grove with the orchard keeper, who showed them where older trees shed enough bark to make the sleeve. Young apprentices carried baskets and separated healthy material from spoiled pieces. He Yan tested each strip by bending it over a heated copper rod. The material did not snap. Lu Que examined the roots after the collection and found no fresh damage. The keeper agreed to lend the harvested fiber as long as the group replanted new copper-leaf seedlings. Some villagers greeted the travelers with hostility because they feared the forest would be taken away. An old icon of the copper tree stood at the entrance, and the keeper proposed that each household nominate a volunteer to watch the harvest. A small orchard rally formed beside the well. Rival farming groups argued, but the keeper persuaded them to measure how much material had fallen naturally. Their shared count helped practical evidence prevail over angry rumor, calming the dispute.

他們與果園管理人走遍樹林，管理人指出哪些老樹會自然脫落足夠的樹皮。年輕學徒帶籃子，把健康材料與腐壞部分分開。何晏把每段纖維放在熱銅桿上彎折，材料沒有斷。陸雀採集後檢查樹根，沒有新傷。管理人同意借出採得的纖維，條件是隊伍重新種下銅葉樹苗。 部分村民帶著敵意迎接旅人，因為害怕樹林被奪走。入口立著銅葉樹的古老圖像，管理人建議每戶提名一位志願者監督採集。井旁出現一場小型果園集會。互相競爭的農團發生爭論，管理人卻說服他們計算自然掉落的材料數量。共同計數讓實際證據勝過憤怒謠言，爭議逐漸平息。

That night He Yan worked beside the keeper's hearth, weaving the copper fibers into a narrow band. Lu Que sat opposite and treated a cut on his hand. He Yan admitted that he once measured a craft only by how quickly it could be completed. He now counted how many trees and hands had made it possible. Lu Que took the unfinished band and held it close to the fire so He Yan could test the color. Their hands touched briefly. Neither moved away. The village offered a small subsidy for replanting trees, and the apprentices felt a thrill when they found fresh shoots on seedlings in the nursery. A schoolteacher said the work could support tuition for future forestry students. The keeper cautioned that careless cutting would undermine the orchard more surely than any argument. He Yan described the repair as a shared venture, not a private contract. Lu Que agreed that its success would have to be measured in living roots as well as sound.

那晚何晏在管理人爐火旁，把銅纖維編成狹長帶子。陸雀坐在對面，替他手上的割傷上藥。何晏承認自己過去只用完成速度衡量手藝，現在則會計算有多少樹和多少雙手讓作品成為可能。陸雀拿起未完成的帶子，靠近火讓何晏測顏色。兩人的手短暫碰到，誰也沒有避開。 村子提供一筆小額補助重新栽樹，學徒在苗圃的樹苗上發現新芽時，十分興奮。一名教師說這項工作也能支持未來林業學生的學費。管理人警告，隨意砍伐比任何爭論都更確實會破壞果園。何晏形容修復是共同的冒險，而不是私人合約。陸雀同意，成功必須同時以活樹根與聲音衡量。

The finished band was light enough for one person to carry but strong enough to circle the drum. He Yan packed it with the silver wire and tested the new fastenings on a small wooden model. Lu Que placed fresh seedlings at the grove's edge before they departed. The keeper asked them to return after the storm and tell the villagers what happened. He Yan promised to report both failure and success, because the orchard deserved the truth either way. Across the valley, the spring wind sounded unusually low. He Yan checked the weather marks and noticed the storm front was moving faster than expected. The two men left before dawn, carrying the sleeve that might soften the bell and a responsibility to the trees that had given them its material. The road ahead now led straight toward the greatest danger of their journey.

完成的帶子輕到一個人就能搬運，卻夠結實，可以繞過轉鼓。何晏把它與銀線一起收好，在小木模型上測試新扣件。離開前，陸雀把新樹苗放到樹林邊。管理人請他們風暴後回來，告訴村民結果。何晏承諾不論成敗都會報告，因為果園無論如何都值得知道真相。 山谷對面，春風的聲音異常低沉。何晏查看天氣標記，發現風暴前鋒比預期快。兩人天未亮就出發，帶著可能使風鐘音調變柔的套環，也帶著對提供材料的樹木的責任。前面的路現在直通旅程中最大的危險。
