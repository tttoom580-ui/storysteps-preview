混亂的玻璃港

Glass Harbor in Disorder

Glass Harbor looked bright from above, with colored roofs and long airship docks shining like mirrors. On the ground, however, travelers waited in silent lines beneath a broken departure board. A storm had delayed every cargo vessel, and the harbor master was asking pilots to carry extra passengers. He Yan and Lu Que came looking for an old tuning fork stored in the lighthouse. Instead, they found families unable to reach the island's medical tents. A customs clerk called the birds alien to the harbor, as if they had no place in its history. The crowd descended into chaos when two lifts stopped together. Lu Que asked for basic comprehension of the migration route before issuing orders. The harbor charter did not entitle anyone to trap wild creatures, but the officials claimed that an emergency allowed greater flexibility. He Yan demanded a clear limit to that claim.

玻璃港從上空看很明亮，彩色屋頂與長長的飛船碼頭像鏡子一樣閃耀。地面上，旅客卻在故障的出發告示板下排著安靜長隊。風暴延誤了所有貨船，港務長正要求飛行員載更多旅客。何晏與陸雀原是來尋找燈塔收藏的舊音叉，卻發現許多家庭無法抵達島上的醫療帳篷。 海關書記把鳥稱作港口的外來者，彷彿牠們與這裡的歷史無關。兩座升降梯同時停止時，群眾陷入混亂。陸雀要求官員先理解基本遷徙路線，再下命令。港口章程沒有賦予任何人捕捉野生生物的權利，官員卻說緊急狀況需要更大彈性。何晏要求說清楚彈性的界線。

At the pier they met a tailor repairing travel coats for stranded passengers. She directed them to the lighthouse keeper, who had hidden a tuning fork inside a locked cupboard after the last storm. The fork was made to identify a note that ordinary ears could not separate from the wind. He Yan tested it beside a window. Lu Que heard the harbor bell answer in a pitch that caused the nearest cranes to rise from their rest. At a gathering in the old salon, the keeper tried to minimize the problem by repeating a comforting myth about cranes that could never tire. Lu Que showed the birds' ragged feathers and the empty food boxes. A shortage of resting places was the real danger. The tuning fork displayed a wide spectrum of notes, only one of which disturbed the birds. The keeper agreed to measure that note before the next storm.

碼頭上，他們遇見一位替受困旅人修補外衣的裁縫。她指引兩人去找燈塔守護人；守護人在上次風暴後，把音叉鎖進櫥櫃。音叉原用來辨認普通耳朵難以從風中分出的音高。何晏在窗邊測試，陸雀聽見港口鐘以一種讓附近雲鶴驚起的音調回應。 舊交誼廳的聚會上，守護人引用一個雲鶴永不疲倦的安慰神話，想淡化問題。陸雀展示鳥群破爛的羽毛與空食箱。真正的危險是休息地不足。音叉呈現廣泛的音頻範圍，只有其中一個音高使雲鶴不安。守護人同意在下場風暴前測出那個音。

The pair built a temporary waiting area at the edge of the harbor garden. He Yan tied a roof to two living roots, leaving the sky open above. Lu Que and the tailor arranged shallow water bowls and a quiet path for families walking to the clinic. Several pilots volunteered to carry supplies rather than passengers. The harbor master finally agreed to separate emergency medical flights from ordinary trade, a small change that made both safer. One delivery lift began to stall as the pressure changed. He Yan made a strategic choice to suspend its operation rather than risk a rescue high above the harbor. A young scholar offered a theoretical plan that required no inspection, but the pilots refused it. They built a small tribute of paper cranes for those who had once maintained the lighthouse and searched its walls for the old safety marks.

兩人在港口花園邊搭建臨時等候區。何晏把屋頂固定在兩處活根上，上方仍保留開闊天空。陸雀與裁縫安排淺水碗，並開出一條讓家庭步行前往診所的安靜小路。幾名飛行員自願運送物資，不再額外載客。港務長終於同意把緊急醫療飛行與一般貿易分開，這個小調整讓雙方都更安全。 氣壓改變時，一座貨運升降梯開始停滯。何晏作出策略性選擇，暫停運轉，而不是冒險在港口上空救援。一名年輕學者提出不必檢查的理論方案，飛行員拒絕採用。他們摺了幾隻紙鶴，向過去維護燈塔的人致意，並搜尋牆上的舊安全標記。

In the lighthouse wall, they found a hidden compartment containing the fork's wooden case and a diagram of the three side towers. One tower was marked with a blue leaf, another with a red sail, and the third with a silver wave. He Yan recognized the leaf as the symbol from Reed Island. Lu Que realized that the wave might refer to a route through the cloud sea, not a body of water. Their map gained a direction, though not yet a safe crossing. A blue vein in the old stone carried sound from the sea-facing wall. The harbor clerk demanded a travel visa before they could leave with the fork, but the keeper pointed out that it was a public instrument and required no permit whatsoever. He Yan signed a promise to return it after the repair. Lu Que kept the wooden case dry against his chest as they departed toward the silver-wave island.

燈塔牆裡有個暗格，存放音叉木盒與三座側塔的圖。第一座塔標有藍葉，第二座是紅帆，第三座是銀浪。何晏認出藍葉是蘆島見過的符號。陸雀想到，銀浪或許指的是雲海路線，不是真正的水域。他們的地圖終於有了方向，卻還沒有安全的渡越方式。 舊石頭裡的一道藍色紋脈把聲音傳向面海的牆。港口書記要求他們帶音叉離開前先辦旅行簽證，但守護人指出那是公共儀器，根本不需要任何許可。何晏簽下修復後歸還的承諾。兩人朝銀浪島出發時，陸雀把木盒貼在胸前，細心保持乾燥。
