When the bell became quiet enough for the cranes, people began preparing to use the skyways again. A school invited He Yan and Lu Que to explain the system to young apprentices before the spring market reopened. He Yan feared the lesson might sound like another proud guild lecture. Lu Que suggested beginning with an ordinary question: why had the birds refused the old route? They carried a wooden model and a basket of fresh leaves into the classroom. He Yan drew a carbon line across the model to show the old cargo channel. A chubby apprentice asked whether every island must conform to the same design. Lu Que welcomed the question and changed the discourse toward ecological differences among the islands. The class wrote a simple equation for weight and wind pressure, then made an explicit rule: no experiment could proceed unless the creatures had room to leave.

風鐘終於安靜到雲鶴能接受時，人們開始準備重新使用天空道路。春市重開前，一所學校邀何晏與陸雀向年輕學徒解釋系統。何晏怕課程又變成自大的工會演講。陸雀建議從普通問題開始：鳥為何拒絕舊路？兩人帶著木模型與一籃鮮葉進教室。 何晏用炭筆在模型上畫線，顯示舊貨運聲道。一位圓潤的學徒問，每座島是否都必須符合相同設計。陸雀欣然接受問題，把討論轉向群島間的生態差異。全班寫出重量與風壓的簡單方程式，接著訂下明確規則：任何實驗都必須讓生物保有離開的空間。

The apprentices tested the model with paper cranes. When the teacher tilted a fan toward the nesting side, the paper cranes blew against a wall. Lu Que did not call this a failure of the students. He asked what their materials had shown. A girl moved the fan to the far side, creating a clear corridor. He Yan measured the result, then let the class make the next change themselves. By noon, the students had drawn three workable routes for different winds. To make the change visible, He Yan offered to inject a little colored water into the glass wind model. The pupils then modified the opening and watched the color spread. A baker arrived with warm pastry for lunch, and the school's patron asked whether the lesson could become a yearly rehearsal. Lu Que agreed, provided the program included a scenario in which the safest decision was to cancel the flight.

學徒用紙鶴測試模型。教師把扇子朝築巢側傾斜時，紙鶴都吹向牆壁。陸雀沒有說學生失敗，而是問材料告訴了他們什麼。一名女孩把扇子移到遠側，形成清楚風廊。何晏測量結果，再讓全班自行作下一項修改。中午前，學生已畫出適合三種風向的路線。 為使變化更可見，何晏提議在玻璃風道模型裡注入少量有色水。學童接著修改開口，觀看顏色擴散。麵包師帶來熱糕點作午餐，學校的贊助人問課程能否成為每年排練。陸雀同意，條件是課程必須包含一個「取消飛行才最安全」的情境。

In the afternoon, the class visited the harbor to watch cargo pilots follow the repaired signals. The traffic moved slowly but steadily. Lu Que showed the students a tree whose roots held one end of the skyway, then asked them to measure its health rather than merely count the ships. One student discovered new leaves near a repaired anchor. He Yan wrote her name beside the observation and thanked her for noticing evidence he had missed. Beyond the harbor, engine smog still covered a narrow channel. He Yan said a sophisticated machine was useless if its waste harmed the nests below. The school garden grew squash for the relief kitchen, and pupils offered to submit a report about protecting the beds. When two ropes began to tangle on a practice frame, they stopped the test rather than pull harder. The youngest helper laughed at the old machine's stubborn whine.

下午，全班到港口看貨運飛行員依修好的信號通行。交通很慢，卻很穩定。陸雀帶學生看一株根系固定風道一端的樹，請他們測量樹的健康，而不只數船隻。一名學生在修好的錨點旁發現新葉。何晏把她的名字記在觀察旁，感謝她看見他漏掉的證據。 港口外，引擎煙霧仍籠罩狹窄通道。何晏說，再精密的機器若排出廢氣傷害下方鳥巢，也沒有用。學校菜園種了南瓜供救助廚房使用，學生提出要提交保護菜畦的報告。練習架上兩條繩開始纏住時，他們停止測試，沒有硬拉。最年輕的助手笑著聽舊機器固執的嗚鳴。

After the lesson, the school director thanked them for teaching both skill and restraint. He Yan remembered how he had once measured his worth by the number of repairs finished. Now he had helped students learn when not to repair something, or when to ask a question first. Lu Que asked whether he regretted leaving the old guild. He Yan said he regretted losing years to pride, but not the craft itself. The two men walked home through the quiet market street. A young graduate worried about unemployment after the old cargo system was replaced. He Yan invited him to join the island maintenance teams, where practical skill would remain necessary. Lu Que added that no new work should be invented merely to keep a machine running. They left the school a model anyone could take apart, study, and improve without seeking their approval.

課程結束後，校長感謝他們既教技能，也教克制。何晏想起自己從前只用完成的維修數量衡量價值。如今他教會學生何時不該動手，何時要先提問。陸雀問他是否後悔離開舊工會。何晏說，他後悔因自負失去的歲月，卻不後悔這門手藝。兩人沿安靜的市集街道走回家。 一位年輕畢業生擔心舊貨運系統更換後會失業。何晏邀他加入島嶼維護隊，因為實用技能仍會被需要。陸雀補充，不應只為讓機器繼續運轉而硬造工作。他們把可以拆解、研究、改良的模型留在學校，任何人都不必先得到兩人的批准。
