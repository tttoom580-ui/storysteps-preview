舊地圖裡的果園

The Orchard in the Old Map

雲鶴歸巢：與你修一條天空的路 · 第 7 / 25 章 · 4000–5000 · 英中對照 · 校訂完成

The three-circle symbol led them to an abandoned map room beneath Reed Island's orchard. At its entrance, a staircase descended through roots thicker than a person's waist. He Yan had copied an old wind atlas as an apprentice; the stairwell matched a drawing he once thought imaginary. Lu Que traced feather marks on the railing. These were not signs of animals trapped underground. They were traces of cranes that had used the passage as shelter during a storm long ago. The guild's acquisition of the orchard had included every building but not the farmers' backyard wells. A newly posted bonus tempted tenants to sell their plots. Lu Que tested the water for calcium and collected a chunk of soil beside a colonial stone marker. The soil was rich, yet the marker described the place as empty land. He Yan began to suspect that old maps had deliberately erased a history of shared cultivation.

三圓標誌把他們帶到蘆島果園下方一間廢棄的地圖室。入口的階梯穿過比人腰還粗的樹根往下走。何晏學徒時抄過一幅古風圖，樓梯間正好符合他曾以為是幻想的圖畫。陸雀沿著扶手摸到羽毛痕跡。這並不是動物被困地下的證據，而是很久以前有雲鶴在風暴中使用此處避難的痕跡。 工會收購果園時，買下全部建築，卻沒有買下農民後院的井。新公告提供獎金，誘惑佃戶出售土地。陸雀檢測井水中的鈣含量，並在殖民時期的石界標旁採集一塊土。土壤肥沃，界標卻稱此地是荒地。何晏開始懷疑舊地圖刻意抹去了共同耕種的歷史。

They found a painted mural showing nine islands joined not by straight roads but by curved strands. At the center stood a bell; three smaller towers carried its tone around the storm season. There were no nets in the mural. Cranes followed openings in the moving clouds, while humans crossed on separate lines. He Yan understood why the first builders had used living root towers: roots could bend with an island instead of breaking when the rock shifted. The carved instructions were difficult to comprehend. Mei Sui's grandfather would disapprove of any plan that harmed the roots, and the implication was clear: repairing the skyway meant caring for the trees first. A long tunnel could penetrate the orchard cliff, but the republican city council had never inspected it. He Yan copied the warnings into his notebook and refused to call the old builders foolish merely because their language was unfamiliar.

兩人在地圖室找到一幅壁畫，九座島嶼不是被直路連起來，而是由彎曲的線條串連。中央是一座風鐘，另外三座小塔在風暴季把音調傳開。壁畫上沒有網籠。雲鶴沿著流雲中的空隙飛行，人類則走另一條線。何晏明白早期建造者為何使用活根塔：岩石移動時，根能跟著彎曲，不像死木容易折斷。 雕刻的指示不容易理解。梅穗的祖父一定不會贊成傷害樹根的計畫，而含意已很清楚：要修天空道路，得先照顧樹。長隧道能穿透果園懸崖，但共和城市議會從未檢查。何晏把警告抄進筆記，不因古人的語言陌生，就說他們愚蠢。

Inside the map room, a student from the island academy was sorting broken tiles. She had spent two summers cleaning the site without pay. She showed them a page describing the order of the three towers. He Yan noticed that the sequence changed with the direction of the seasonal wind. This explained why simply ringing every bell at once would be dangerous. Lu Que asked the student to join them as a paid guide, not an invisible helper. Following the old route was risky, and He Yan refused to hide that fact. He reused scrap wood for a handrail while the academy sophomore measured the spacious chambers. At the stairwell's turn, Lu Que found the spine of an ancient crane in the soil, undisturbed for generations. They did not remove it as a curiosity. Instead, they marked the place, allowing the island's natural history students to study it later.

地圖室裡，一名島上學院的學生正在整理破碎瓷片。她已連續兩個夏天無薪清理遺址。她拿出一頁記載三座側塔次序的文字。何晏注意到這個順序會隨季節風向而改變，因此同時敲響所有鐘反而危險。陸雀邀請學生以有薪嚮導身分同行，不再當看不見的助手。 沿著舊路前進有風險，何晏不肯隱瞞。他用廢木料製作扶手，學院的二年級生則測量寬敞的房間。樓梯轉角處，陸雀在土裡找到一段古雲鶴的脊椎，幾代人都沒有碰過。他們沒有把它拿走當奇物，而是標記位置，留給島上的自然史學生日後研究。

They emerged beside a small clearing that looked west across four islands. From there they could see the central bell for the first time. It hung above the clouds, larger than a ship's hull, with one side darkened by repairs. A narrow bridge to the first side tower had collapsed. He Yan sketched the bridge's former angle and placed the three-circle tile beside his compass. Lu Que watched a flock avoid the empty space where the bridge once stood. They stacked the salvaged tiles into a dry box while the student checked for signs of surveillance at the forgotten entrance. The three circles were symbolic, but the arrows beside them described a practical transit schedule between islands. The student translated the oldest words: first listen, then answer, then let the path open. He Yan copied the sequence without pretending the missing bridge was already safe.

他們從一片小空地走出，往西可以看見四座島。這裡第一次看得見中央風鐘。它懸在雲上，比船身還大，一側因修補而發黑。通往第一座側塔的窄橋已坍塌。何晏畫下橋原本的角度，把三圓瓷片放在羅盤旁。陸雀看見鳥群避開那道橋曾經存在的空白。 他們把救出的瓷片堆放進乾燥箱，學生則檢查被遺忘的入口有沒有監視的痕跡。三圓具有象徵意義，但旁邊的箭頭記錄了島與島之間實際的通行時間表。學生翻譯最古老的文字：先聆聽，再回應，然後讓通道開啟。何晏抄下次序，沒有假裝失去的橋已安全。
