第一條開放風廊

The First Open Corridor

雲鶴歸巢：與你修一條天空的路 · 第 5 / 25 章 · 4000–5000 · 英中對照

By the fifth day, cranes crowded the cliffs of Salt Island. The central bell rang at the wrong hour, and the youngest birds refused to step into the air. He Yan proposed joining the repaired bridge channel to a temporary sail above the gap. Lu Que warned that a visible path was not enough; the flock needed shelter at both ends. They spent the morning inviting farmers to inspect the plan, then removed every net that could close behind a bird. The island had a constitutional rule that protected common nesting ground. A visiting democrat from the lowland council urged everyone to vote, but the argument turned into another episode of exaggeration when the shipping fleet warned of economic ruin. Lu Que challenged the greed behind the warning. He Yan showed that a narrow route for birds would still leave the cargo lane open, provided the ships crossed at separate hours.

第五天，雲鶴擠滿鹽島懸崖。中央風鐘在錯誤的時刻響起，最年輕的鳥不肯展翅。何晏提議把修好的橋梁聲道接到缺口上方的臨時風帆。陸雀警告，可見的道路還不夠；鳥群需要兩端都有休息處。他們整個上午請農民檢查計畫，接著拆掉所有可能在鳥群身後關起的網。 島上有一條保障共同築巢地的憲制規定。一名來自低地議會的民主派代表呼籲眾人投票，但航運船隊警告經濟將毀滅，辯論又變成一場誇大的風波。陸雀質疑這份警告背後的貪婪。何晏證明只要船隻在不同時段通行，鳥類使用狹窄風道時，貨運航線仍能保持開放。

A grower named Mei Sui brought bundles of fresh grass and offered the south end of her farm as a rest station. The apprentices planted the grass in soil that had been carried up by hand. He Yan fixed a light sail between two living root towers and set a red cord to show its safe limit. Lu Que scattered berries nearby but refused to use bait strong enough to drive the flock in a chosen direction. The island offered an herb garden as an incentive to volunteers, but Mei Sui wanted no special reward. A massive cloud rose above the peasant terraces, and the rope began to rattle. The regional recession had made farmers suspicious of every new scheme; still, several agreed to recruit neighbors for the night watch. At a retail stall, He Yan bought spare rope and canvas before the last daylight faded.

名叫梅穗的農民帶來幾捆新鮮草，願意把農田南端當成休息站。學徒們把草種在親手運上的土裡。何晏在兩座活根塔之間固定一面輕風帆，並用紅繩標示安全極限。陸雀在附近撒些漿果，卻拒絕使用會迫使鳥群朝指定方向飛行的強誘餌。 島上以藥草園作為志願者的獎勵，但梅穗不想領取特別報酬。巨大的雲升上農民梯田，繩索開始格格作響。區域經濟衰退讓農民對每個新方案都很懷疑；仍有幾人同意招募鄰居值夜。在一處零售攤位，何晏趁天還亮時買了備用繩索與帆布。

The first test went badly. A gust pushed the empty sail sideways and struck the stone wall. He Yan cut the release cord before it could pull against the tree roots. The failure angered him, but Lu Que pointed to the fleeing insects below the sail: they had warned of the changed wind before the measuring bowl reacted. On the second attempt, the volunteers watched the insects as well as the instruments. The sail held its line. They did not smash the old equipment in anger. Instead, He Yan measured the substantial difference between the two gusts and revised his tactics. The safety threshold was written where every helper could see it. The village would make its own verdict about the route after a real crossing, not a speech from the guild. Meanwhile, a widespread message went to neighboring islands asking them to leave their resting trees untouched.

第一次測試失敗了。一陣強風吹得空帆側轉，撞上石牆。何晏在風帆拉扯樹根之前割斷釋放繩。失敗使他懊惱，陸雀卻指向帆下四散的昆蟲：牠們比測量碗更早察覺風變。第二次測試時，志願者同時觀察昆蟲與儀器。風帆終於穩住方向。 他們沒有憤怒地砸毀舊設備。何晏測出兩次風勢之間顯著的差異，修改了戰術。安全臨界值寫在每位助手都看得見的地方。村子會在真正渡越後自行判斷這條路，而不是只聽工會發言。同時，一則廣泛傳到鄰島的訊息，請大家暫時不要動那些供鳥休息的樹木。

When sunset touched the far cliff, the elder crane stepped into the air. It followed the quiet corridor across the gap, turned once to inspect the open landing, and settled on Mei Sui's grass. Three younger birds followed without being called. Lu Que smiled for the first time that day. He Yan did not take credit for the crossing. He watched the red cord and kept one hand ready to release it, knowing that freedom depended on more than a successful first flight. That evening the farmers brought food instead of speeches. A child counted four cranes beneath the cedar, then asked whether all the others could come tomorrow. Lu Que answered that they might choose another day, or another route. He Yan rolled up the map and marked one green line where the passage had worked. It was the first open corridor, and only the beginning.

夕陽碰到遠方懸崖時，年長的雲鶴踏入空中。牠沿著安靜風廊越過缺口，轉身看了看開放的落點，最後停在梅穗的草地上。三隻幼鳥不必呼喚就跟了過來。陸雀那天第一次露出笑容。何晏沒有把功勞歸給自己。他盯著紅繩，一隻手隨時準備釋放，明白自由不只是一次成功飛行。 當晚農民帶來食物，沒有發表演說。一個孩子數著雪松下的四隻雲鶴，問其他鳥明天是否都能來。陸雀回答，牠們或許會選其他日子、其他路線。何晏捲起地圖，在成功通過的位置畫下一條綠線。那是第一條開放的風廊，卻只是開始。
