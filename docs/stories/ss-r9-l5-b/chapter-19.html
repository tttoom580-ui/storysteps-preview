西方缺口的鳥群

The Flock at the Western Gap

The spring front reached the western gap before the repair crew returned to the central bell. Dark clouds pressed against the islands, and the last large flock of cranes gathered on a narrow ledge. He Yan knew that the birds would try to cross once the first strong gust arrived. Lu Que counted the young birds and saw that several were too tired to follow. The two men called the side-tower volunteers and organized resting places along the repaired corridor. He Yan refused to attribute the flock's distress to any single person before checking the weather. On behalf of the village, Mei Sui offered a compact shelter near the gap. The volunteers showed great competence as they worked to coordinate supplies and signals. A young pilot was making his debut in a rescue crew, but the elder keeper reminded him that saving creatures was not a chance to prove personal destiny.

修復隊還沒回到中央風鐘，春季前鋒就抵達西方缺口。黑雲壓向群島，最後一大群雲鶴聚在狹窄平台上。何晏知道第一股強風吹來時，鳥群就會試圖渡越。陸雀數著幼鳥，發現幾隻太累，無法跟上。兩人召集側塔志願者，沿修好的風廊安排休息處。 何晏拒絕在查看天氣前，把鳥群的困境歸咎於任何單一個人。梅穗代表村子提供缺口附近的緊湊庇護處。志願者熟練地協調物資與信號。一位年輕飛行員首次加入救援隊，老守塔人卻提醒他，拯救生物不是證明個人命運的機會。

A cargo ship offered to carry the weakest birds across, but Lu Que refused to force them into its hold. Instead, he proposed moving food and water to two natural resting branches. He Yan calculated how long the branches would remain sheltered from the rising wind. He tied narrow strips of cloth above the safe ledges so that humans could see them without alarming the cranes. When the first young bird stepped toward a branch, he lowered his voice and waited. The sky above the gap lay outside the shipping guild's domain, so its captain agreed to carry donations instead of passengers. The crew had to execute a careful plan: protect animals that were not immune to exhaustion during migration. Their strongest worker, a muscular sailor, offered to climb the far tower. Lu Que reminded him that the birds were not opponents to be defeated; their needs set the rhythm of the work.

一艘貨船願意把最虛弱的鳥載過去，但陸雀拒絕把牠們強塞進船艙。他提議改把食物與水移到兩處天然休息枝上。何晏計算這些枝條在增強的風中還能遮蔽多久。他在安全平台上方綁上窄布條，使人類看得見，卻不驚擾鳥群。第一隻幼鳥走向枝條時，他放低聲音，耐心等待。 缺口上空不屬於航運工會的管轄區，因此船長同意載運捐贈物資，而不是旅客。船員必須執行細緻計畫：保護在遷徙中並非不會疲勞的動物。最強壯的水手主動提出爬上遠塔。陸雀提醒他，鳥群不是要擊敗的對手，牠們的需要才決定工作的節奏。

As the front approached, the red tower began to shake. He Yan climbed its lower steps and found a fastening loosened by repeated waves of wind. He could tighten it, but only if the next gust allowed a safe pause. Lu Que watched the flock from below and raised a blue flag when the birds moved back to the shelter. He Yan worked quickly, then came down without waiting for applause. The fastening held as the next heavy gust swept past. The side towers increased their output only when the weather required it. When a warning sounded, the volunteers had to scramble to lower the cloth markers. Beyond the gap, a spectacular pattern of sunlight crossed the cloud layers. A child held a sponge to keep rain from collecting in the tuning box, while a vendor brought spare roof tiles to strengthen the shelter. Every small act mattered more than a dramatic rescue pose.

前鋒接近時，紅塔開始搖動。何晏爬上低階，發現一處扣件被反覆風浪震鬆。只有下一波強風之間出現安全間隔，他才能鎖緊。陸雀在下方觀察鳥群，牠們退回庇護處時便舉起藍旗。何晏迅速工作，沒有等人鼓掌便下塔。下一股重風掃過時，扣件仍然穩住。 側塔只在天氣確實需要時提高輸出。警報一響，志願者就得急忙降下布標。缺口之外，陽光在雲層上形成壯觀的紋路。一名孩子用海綿避免雨水積在調音盒裡，小販帶來備用屋瓦，加固庇護處。每件小事都比戲劇化的救援姿勢重要。

By evening, most of the flock had crossed or chosen to rest. The youngest birds stayed on the near ledge under Lu Que's watch. He Yan sent a message to the central bell: the fourth cargo signal must remain low until the front passed. The director answered that the company could not hold the setting without risking cargo traffic. He Yan and Lu Que looked at the darkening sky and realized the temporary corridor would need a stronger protection before midnight. They drew one more map before night. It showed the places where people could travel without crossing the birds' resting ledges, and where human watch boats could replace the loudest bell signals. He Yan signed the plan; Lu Que added the positions of the nests. They sent copies to every island that could still be reached by messenger kite.

傍晚，大部分鳥群已渡過或選擇停下休息。最小的幼鳥留在近側平台，由陸雀看守。何晏向中央風鐘傳訊：第四貨運信號必須保持低檔，直到前鋒通過。主管回答，公司無法在不危及貨運交通的情況下維持這個設定。何晏與陸雀望向變暗的天空，明白午夜前臨時風廊需要更強的保護。 入夜前，他們又畫了一張地圖，標示人們可避開鳥群休息台地的路線，以及人工瞭望船能取代最刺耳鐘聲的位置。何晏簽下計畫，陸雀補上鳥巢座標。他們把副本交給仍能用信使風箏聯絡的每座島。
