燼島市集的風鐘

The Bell of Ember Market

雲鶴歸巢：與你修一條天空的路 · 第 6 / 25 章 · 4000–5000 · 英中對照 · 校訂中

On Ember Island, the air smelled of hot metal and fried roots. The island was built around a shallow bell tower, but its own bell had fallen silent years ago. Every seven days, the central bell sent a low note through the empty tower, turning its walls into an echo chamber. He Yan and Lu Que arrived during the market festival. People were playing games beneath colored flags, apparently unaware that the sound made nesting birds fly toward the cliffs. The council's agenda concerned trade taxes, not migrating cranes. He Yan warned the officials to beware of the tower's damaged supports; a loose iron ring seemed to cling to the inner wall by rust alone. A federal inspector was visiting from the lower islands to hear a new genre of wind ballads. Lu Que asked whether the musicians had noticed the unusual note. Several said their instruments had been impossible to tune all week.

燼島空氣裡有熱金屬與炸根莖的氣味。島城圍繞一座低矮鐘塔建造，但自己的鐘多年前就不再響了。每七天，中央風鐘的低音穿過空塔，使牆壁成了回音室。何晏與陸雀在市集節慶期間抵達。人們在彩旗下玩遊戲，似乎不知道鐘聲會讓築巢的鳥群飛向懸崖。 議會議程關心的是交易稅，不是遷徙雲鶴。何晏警告官員要小心鐘塔損壞的支撐：一個鬆掉的鐵環似乎只靠鏽跡黏在內牆上。來自低地群島的聯邦督察正到訪，要聽一種新類型的風歌。陸雀問樂師是否注意到異常音高，幾人說這一週樂器怎樣都調不準。

They climbed the bell tower after the musicians agreed to stop playing for an hour. Lu Que closed his eyes and listened past the market noise. Behind the iron ring, a faint second tone answered from inside the stone. He Yan opened a panel and discovered a long copper wire attached to a wooden wheel. The wheel had not turned in years, yet the wire still carried the central bell's vibration to the island's nesting ledge. Below them, children were playing a short inning of a ball game. A singer used the bell as a metaphor for promises that traveled farther than their makers intended. The municipal office had nominated a young mason to repair the tower, but the nominee was waiting for permission to touch anything. Lu Que pointed to a pale organism growing beneath the wire; its trembling threads showed exactly where the dangerous note passed.

樂師同意暫停演奏一小時後，兩人登上鐘塔。陸雀閉上眼，越過市集噪音仔細聆聽。鐵環後面，石頭內部傳來微弱的第二個聲音。何晏打開板子，發現一條長銅線連著木輪。木輪已多年不轉，但銅線仍把中央風鐘的震動傳到島上的築巢台地。 下方的孩子們正在打一小局球賽。一位歌手把鐘當成比喻，說承諾往往傳得比立誓的人預想得更遠。市政辦公室已提名一位年輕石匠修塔，但候選人還在等碰任何東西的許可。陸雀指向銅線下方一種淡色生物；它顫動的細絲準確顯示有害音波通過的位置。

He Yan proposed loosening the wire in stages. The inspector objected because the signal also warned cargo pilots of storms. Lu Que offered a compromise: leave the storm warning visible on the tower roof but remove its echo from the nesting ledge. The mason agreed to help if the work could be inspected in daylight. They tried one turn of the wheel. The birds stopped circling, but a row of market lamps dimmed below, proving the system was connected to more than they knew. A delivery kite tried to overtake another near the tower, and the crowd shouted as the two almost collided. Rumors of rebellion spread when the lights dimmed. He Yan found a broken rib on the old kite frame and used it to brace the wire; Lu Que held a rigid wooden shield below in case the iron ring fell. They refused to claim success until the cargo pilots had tested the warning again.

何晏提議分階段放鬆銅線。督察反對，因為這信號也用來警告貨運飛行員風暴將至。陸雀提出折衷辦法：讓屋頂仍能看見暴風警示，但移除築巢台地的回音。石匠同意幫忙，條件是工程要能在白天檢查。他們試著轉動木輪一格。鳥群不再盤旋，下方一排市集燈卻暗了，證明系統的連結比他們想像更廣。 一架送貨風箏試圖在塔旁超越另一架，兩者差點相撞，群眾驚叫。燈光變暗後，反叛的謠言四處流傳。何晏找到舊風箏骨架一根斷肋，用來支撐銅線；陸雀則在下方舉著硬木護盾，以防鐵環落下。他們拒絕在貨運飛行員重新測試警報前宣稱成功。

At dusk, the mason climbed with a new wooden brace. The tower gave one clean warning note when the test horn sounded; no echo reached the nests. Pilots below raised their flags to confirm they had heard it. The inspector signed the test sheet. Then Lu Que noticed an old maker's mark inside the panel: three joined circles, each with a different line through it. He Yan recognized the mark from a guild lesson about the three side towers that must answer the central bell. They found a bird skeleton in a forgotten maintenance room and carried it carefully to the temple caretaker. The mason began to sob, remembering his father's warnings. A priest offered a lecture on theology, but Lu Que asked instead for a basin of clean water. Someone had left toxic cleaning fluid beside the nests and a bottle of vinegar beside the workers' meals. The room needed practical care before any ceremony.

黃昏時，石匠帶著新木撐爬上塔。測試號角響起時，鐘塔只發出一次清楚的警告音，沒有回音傳到巢區。下方飛行員舉旗確認聽見。督察在測試單上簽名。接著陸雀注意到板內古老的工匠標誌：三個相連的圓，每個圓都有不同的線穿過。何晏認出那是工會課堂提過的三座側塔標誌，只有它們一起回應，才能校準中央風鐘。 他們在被遺忘的維修室發現一副鳥骨架，小心交給廟宇管理人。石匠想起父親的警告，忍不住啜泣。一位祭司準備講神學，陸雀卻先要求一盆乾淨的水。有人把有毒清潔液放在鳥巢旁，把一瓶醋放在工人餐食旁。這房間需要的是實際照護，不是儀式。
