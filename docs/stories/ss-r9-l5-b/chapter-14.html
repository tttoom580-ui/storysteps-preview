The Auction of Old Bells

雲鶴歸巢：與你修一條天空的路 · 第 14 / 25 章 · 4000–5000 · 英中對照 · 校訂中

用互動版查詞、朗讀與收藏

To connect the three towers, He Yan needed a length of silver wire made by the original bell makers. No modern alloy held the note through long cloud passages. A merchant had gathered the last pieces from abandoned workshops and planned to sell them at an auction in the capital. The two men traveled there with the retired keeper, who knew how to recognize genuine wire. They could not afford the highest bid, but they hoped to persuade the merchant to lend part of the stock. The capital council was debating whether to abolish an obsolete toll and amend the city's old repair code. He Yan could articulate why the wire mattered, but the auction rules favored buyers with wealth. The sale took place beside a cathedral that had survived three storms. The keeper said the real purpose of the trip was not to combat the merchants; it was to find someone compassionate enough to place living islands above quick profit.

要連起三座側塔，何晏需要原始鐘匠製作的一段銀線。現代合金無法讓音調穿過長距離雲道仍保持穩定。一名商人從廢棄工坊收集最後幾段，準備在都城拍賣。兩人與退休守塔人同行，她知道如何辨認真銀線。他們出不起最高價，但希望說服商人借出部分存貨。 都城議會正討論廢除過時的通行費，並修訂城市舊維修規範。何晏能清楚說出銀線的重要性，但拍賣規則偏向有錢買家。拍賣場就在一座撐過三場風暴的大教堂旁。守塔人說此行真正目的不是與商人開戰，而是找到有同情心、願意把群島生命放在短期利潤前面的人。

In the auction hall, the merchant displayed polished bells, broken tools, and a coil of silver wire. He Yan examined the coil and found three distinct marks at its ends, matching the symbols on the side towers. The merchant admitted that the wire had been removed when the old network was modernized. He Yan offered a contract: loan the wire for one seasonal test, then return it or replace it if damaged. The merchant asked for a guarantee that the test would not destroy his stock. The auction clerk demanded strict compliance with the sale conditions, but He Yan refused to deceive him with a promise of certain success. A factory manager presented an emission report showing that new wire production was expensive. Several enthusiastic apprentices offered to restore the old coil by hand. The merchant agreed to grant a temporary loan if the work was supervised and every change recorded on a public sheet.

拍賣廳裡，商人展示擦亮的鐘、破工具與一捲銀線。何晏查看線捲，發現末端有三種不同的刻記，正好符合側塔符號。商人承認銀線是在舊網絡現代化時拆下的。何晏提出合約：借用一季測試，之後歸還；若損壞便補償。商人要求保證測試不會毀掉庫存。 拍賣書記要求嚴格遵守售賣條件，何晏卻拒絕以必然成功的承諾欺騙他。一位工廠經理提出排放報告，顯示製造新銀線成本很高。幾名熱情學徒提出以手工修復舊線捲。商人同意暫借，條件是全程有人監督，並在公開表單上記下每一項變更。

While the apprentices cleaned the coil, Lu Que visited a small animal shelter behind the cathedral. A young cloud crane had been brought there after a storm. It was frightened by crowds but calmed when the keeper lowered her voice. Lu Que stayed until it could eat on its own. When He Yan found him at dusk, he did not accuse him of abandoning the work. He sat beside the shelter and asked what the bird needed before they continued the journey. A juvenile predator had been seen near the shelter, and the keeper worried about the birds. A legislative inspector visiting the cathedral suggested closing the open porch, but Lu Que feared that would trap the young crane. He found a safer prospect: move food away from the entrance and repair the side fence. Beside a display of an ancient bird skull, visitors had left a theatrical wig and empty tins; the shelter needed careful storage, not dramatic warnings.

學徒清理線捲時，陸雀到大教堂後方的小動物庇護所。一隻幼雲鶴在風暴後被送到那裡，害怕人群，守護人放低聲音後才平靜。陸雀留下照顧，直到牠能自行進食。何晏黃昏找到他時，沒有責怪他離開工作，而是坐在庇護所旁，問這隻鳥還需要什麼，他們才能繼續旅程。 庇護所附近曾出現一隻幼年掠食動物，守護人擔心鳥群。一名到教堂的立法督察建議關閉開放門廊，陸雀卻怕那會困住幼雲鶴。他找到較安全的方案：把食物移離入口並修好側圍籬。古鳥頭骨展示旁，遊客丟了戲劇假髮與空罐；庇護所需要謹慎收納，不是戲劇性的警告。

By morning, the coil was clean and the shelter fence secure. The merchant allowed the borrowed wire to leave the auction under the keeper's supervision. He Yan carried it carefully in a wooden case. Lu Que said the bird would stay with the shelter until it chose to fly, and He Yan accepted that answer. Neither had obtained everything he wanted in the capital, yet both had protected something that could not be measured by the price of silver. Before departure, the apprentices tied three small colored threads beside the silver coil to mark where it must never be bent. The keeper promised to return with the final results, whether they were successful or not. The city bells rang for the morning market, and for once Lu Que heard them as a reminder of cooperation rather than of danger.

隔天早晨，銀線清理完畢，庇護所圍籬也安全了。商人允許在守塔人監督下把借用銀線帶出拍賣場。何晏小心把線放進木盒。陸雀說幼鳥會留在庇護所，直到自行決定飛離，何晏接受這個安排。兩人在都城都沒有得到想要的一切，卻各自守住無法用銀價衡量的事物。 出發前，學徒在銀線捲旁繫上三條彩線，標示絕對不能彎折的位置。守塔人承諾不論測試成功與否，都會帶著結果回來。城市的鐘為早市響起，這一次陸雀聽到的不是危險，而是合作的提醒。
