銀浪邊界

At the Boundary of Silver Wave

雲鶴歸巢：與你修一條天空的路 · 第 9 / 25 章 · 4000–5000 · 英中對照 · 校訂中

Silver Wave Island was smaller than its name suggested. A single village rose beside a stepped hill, where guards watched the border with tall wooden poles. The island had long provided medicine for cranes injured in storms, but recently the guards had begun turning away travelers without wealthy sponsors. Lu Que recognized one of the rejected caretakers from his own village. He Yan offered to repair the old bridge at the entrance in exchange for a place to sleep. A stubborn ass blocked the gate while its owner argued with a bodyguard. The written boundary rules were supposed to comprise protections for safe travel by all caretakers, yet the guards had started to discriminate against people from poorer islands. A clerk said only certain visitors were eligible to cross. Lu Que replied that wild birds recognized no such barrier. He Yan asked the clerk to show where the new rule had been approved.

銀浪島比名字讓人想像的小。村落建在梯狀山丘旁，守衛用高木杆看守邊界。島上長期替風暴中受傷的雲鶴提供藥物，最近卻開始拒絕沒有富裕贊助人的旅客。陸雀認出被拒的一位照護者來自自己的村子。何晏提出修好入口舊橋，交換一處過夜之地。 一頭固執的驢堵在門前，主人與保鑣爭執不休。邊界書面規則原本應包含保障所有照護人安全通行的條款，守衛卻開始歧視來自較窮島嶼的人。書記說只有特定訪客才有資格過境。陸雀回答，野鳥可不承認這種界線。何晏要求書記指出新規則究竟在哪裡通過。

The village healer led them to a hospital garden built around a stone pool. Water there was believed to help injured birds regain their balance. Lu Que tested it with a shallow cup and discovered that the pool's current moved in the wrong direction whenever the distant bell rang. He Yan followed the pipes beneath a low wall. A hidden valve had been diverting water to a private residence, leaving the public pools almost empty. The fluid in the pipes was clean, but the valve had been installed without anyone's permission. He Yan asked how to incorporate a fair water schedule into the repair. An independent investigator arrived with a notebook, and the village healer was passionate about restoring the public pools. Together they patched the leaking joint and left a clear sign explaining which lever could be moved during an emergency.

村裡的療癒師帶他們到一座圍繞石池建造的醫院花園。人們相信池水能幫受傷的鳥恢復平衡。陸雀用淺杯測試，發現每當遠方風鐘響起，池中的水流就會逆向。何晏沿低牆下的管線追查。一個隱藏閥門把水導往私人住所，讓公共水池幾乎見底。 管線裡的液體很乾淨，但閥門安裝時沒有取得任何人的許可。何晏詢問如何把公平分水時段納入修復。一位獨立調查員帶著筆記本到場，村中療癒師非常熱衷讓公共水池恢復。眾人一起補好漏水接縫，留下清楚標誌，說明緊急時能操作哪支拉桿。

By afternoon, the guards invited the travelers to a storehouse to explain the new entry policy. The official responsible was absent, and the clerk admitted that he had copied the rule from an unsigned sheet. He Yan proposed keeping a list of genuine medical needs instead of judging strangers by their clothes. Lu Que promised to help any injured bird, whether its keeper came from the capital or from an island without roads. The guards slowly lowered their poles. A stone pyramid stood in the square beside the village residence, and a young child had left one sandal beneath it. The caretaker offered a carved shell as a souvenir, but He Yan wanted only the location of the old side tower. A therapist from the hospital showed them the path and warned that its stairs were steep. Lu Que carried water for the travelers who had been left waiting outside the gate.

下午，守衛邀旅人到倉庫說明新入境政策。負責官員不在，書記承認規定是從一張未簽名的紙抄下來的。何晏提議記錄真正的醫療需求，而不是依陌生人的衣著判斷。陸雀承諾會幫助每隻受傷的鳥，不論照護者來自都城，還是連道路都沒有的島。守衛慢慢放下木杆。 廣場上的石金字塔形紀念物立在村中住所旁，一名孩子把一隻涼鞋留在底下。照護者送來雕刻貝殼作紀念品，何晏卻只想知道舊側塔的位置。醫院的治療師帶他們認路，警告階梯很陡。陸雀替先前被留在門外等候的旅人帶水。

Near the tower they found an old wind gate with two sets of instructions, one carved in stone and one painted much later. The newer sign directed cranes toward a closed yard. The older sign pointed toward an open ledge above the clouds. He Yan compared the marks and realized that someone had reversed the route after the gate was repaired. Lu Que waited until dusk to observe the birds, then confirmed that they still preferred the open ledge when it was quiet. He Yan wanted to undo the false sign immediately, but the investigator asked him first to confirm which version was valid. They interviewed travelers who had used the gate in earlier years and noted those most vulnerable to the unsafe change. The older route was supported by both the stone carving and living evidence. That night they reopened the ledge, leaving the choice of landing to the birds themselves.

塔旁的舊風門有兩套指示，一套刻在石上，另一套是較晚才畫上去的。新標誌引導雲鶴前往封閉庭院，舊標誌卻指向雲上的開放平台。何晏比對記號，發現有人在修門後把路線顛倒了。陸雀等到黃昏觀察鳥群，確認只要安靜，牠們仍偏好開放平台。 何晏想立刻撤銷錯誤標誌，調查員卻請他先確認哪個版本有效。他們訪問早年使用過風門的旅人，記下最容易受危險改道影響的人。舊路線同時得到石刻與現場生態證據支持。當晚他們重新開放平台，把是否降落的選擇留給鳥群。
