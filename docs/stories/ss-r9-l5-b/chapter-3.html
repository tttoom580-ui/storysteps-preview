向上流的溪水

The Creek That Turned Upward

雲鶴歸巢：與你修一條天空的路 · 第 3 / 25 章 · 4000–5000 · 英中對照

Willow Island lay west of the old flight path. Its creek had once run through a field of flowering moss before falling gently into the clouds. Now the water rose in thin ribbons toward an iron pier. Lu Que saw hundreds of insects trapped beneath a glass canopy. He Yan heard the deep whistle of a hidden pump. Their guide insisted that the device had been installed to improve irrigation, but no one could explain who had ordered it to run through the night. The village elder feared an abuse of power but wanted consultation before removing the machine. A merchant listened with open contempt, while a traveling correspondent sketched the strange creek for his newspaper. He Yan chose a deliberate inspection rather than an argument. The deputy administrator claimed to have a diplomatic solution: both farmers and merchants could share the water, provided the pump stayed where it was. Lu Que asked who would speak for the insects.

柳島位於舊飛行線的西方。島上的溪流原本穿過開花的苔原，再輕輕落入雲海。如今水卻成了細帶，向上流往一座鐵製碼頭。陸雀看見幾百隻昆蟲被困在玻璃棚下。何晏聽見藏起來的抽水機發出低沉哨聲。嚮導堅稱設備是為改善灌溉而安裝，卻沒人能解釋誰命令它整夜運轉。 村長擔心有人濫用權力，卻希望拆機器前先諮詢居民。一名商人帶著輕蔑神情旁聽，旅行通訊員則為報紙畫下奇異溪流。何晏選擇有計畫地檢查，而不是爭吵。副行政員宣稱有外交式的折衷方案：只要抽水機不動，農民與商人就可以共用水。陸雀問，那些昆蟲又由誰發聲？

The canopy belonged to an old botanical station. Its roof had cracked in the last storm, but a fresh lock showed that someone still used it. Under the floor, they found a wheel that redirected the entire creek into the pump. Lu Que counted the trapped insects and identified a species that cloud cranes ate before their long crossing. If the station remained sealed, the birds would arrive at the next island with empty stomachs. He Yan carefully disconnected the wheel. The local ecosystem was losing a creature once thought almost extinct. When Lu Que found the insects' empty shells, his gut tightened, but he did not pretend the odds were hopeless. He asked the elder to notify every household of the first phase of repair. They would open the canopy at dawn, then restore the creek in stages. A sudden release might wash away the nests they meant to protect.

玻璃棚屬於一座舊植物研究站。屋頂在上次風暴中裂開，但新鎖顯示還有人使用。地板下，他們找到一個把整條溪水導向抽水機的輪子。陸雀數了被困的昆蟲，認出一種雲鶴長途飛行前必吃的食物。如果研究站繼續封閉，鳥群抵達下一島時便會挨餓。何晏小心卸開輪子。 當地生態系正在失去一種曾被認為幾乎滅絕的生物。陸雀找到昆蟲空殼時，胃部一緊，卻沒有假裝存活機率已毫無希望。他請村長通知每戶人家第一階段的修復安排。他們會在黎明打開玻璃棚，再逐步恢復溪流。若一下放出全部水量，反而可能沖走想保護的巢。

During the night, someone tried to close the open vents. He Yan heard shoes on the floor above and took the stairs without drawing a weapon. The intruder was the station's apprentice, afraid that the merchants would dismiss her if the expensive water contract failed. She admitted that she had been told to hide the pump's true purpose. Lu Que offered her food and a chair before asking her to describe what she had seen. No one threatened her. She had not planned to sneak inside, but the foreman had taken the key. The machine's listed successor was a man who had died two winters earlier. He Yan proposed a sustainable repair that could supply water without draining the entire stream. The apprentice asked if the traders would swap her wages for silence. Lu Que answered that her safety mattered more than any trophy the guild could award for a successful experiment.

夜裡有人試圖關閉打開的通風口。何晏聽見上層地板的腳步聲，沒有拔出武器就走上樓梯。闖入者是研究站學徒，她害怕高價供水合約失敗後商人會解雇她。她承認有人要她隱瞞抽水機真正的用途。陸雀先拿食物和椅子給她，才請她說明所見，沒有人威脅她。 她本不打算偷偷潛入，但領班把鑰匙拿走了。機器登記的繼任管理者竟是一位兩個冬天前已過世的人。何晏提議可持續的修復方式，既供應用水，也不抽乾整條溪。學徒問商人是否會用薪水換她的沉默。陸雀回答，她的安全比工會為成功實驗頒發的任何獎盃都重要。

By the third morning the creek flowed downhill again. The insects crawled toward sunlight, and the first cranes crossed above the meadow without circling. The villagers kept the smaller pump but moved its intake below the nesting pools. He Yan wrote a new maintenance plan with the apprentice named as a paid technician. Lu Que watched the birds rise and felt the first cautious pleasure of the journey. They had repaired a link, not the entire sky. Before leaving, He Yan drew the safe water level on a wooden post beside the pier. Lu Que planted a strip of moss at its base, where traveling children could see the creek's return. Their names were not carved into the post. Instead, it bore one short instruction: look at the living bank before turning the wheel.

第三天早晨，溪水終於再度向下流。昆蟲爬向陽光，第一批雲鶴飛越草地時不再盤旋。村民保留較小的抽水機，卻把取水口移到築巢水池下游。何晏寫下新維護計畫，讓學徒以有薪技術員身分參與。陸雀看著鳥群升起，第一次對旅程感到謹慎的喜悅。他們修好的是其中一個環節，而不是整片天空。 離開前，何晏在碼頭旁的木柱畫出安全水位。陸雀在柱底種下一道苔蘚，讓路過的孩子看得見溪流回來的痕跡。柱上沒有刻他們的名字，只有一句短短的指示：轉動輪子前，先看河岸還活著什麼。
