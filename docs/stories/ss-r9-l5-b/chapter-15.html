人人都能走的路

A Path Everyone Can Use

雲鶴歸巢：與你修一條天空的路 · 第 15 / 25 章 · 4000–5000 · 英中對照 · 校訂中

He Yan returned to Reed Island with the silver wire and a new plan for the next still hour. The three side towers had been repaired, but the connections between them remained incomplete. He needed volunteers at each tower, a steady timing signal, and a clear way to stop the test if anything failed. Lu Que insisted that injured birds and tired travelers must not be left beneath the wires. The pair spent the morning arranging quiet shelters along the expected route. They made the root-tower path accessible to older residents by building a gentle ramp. A collector offered rare bell parts, while a traveling comedian lifted everyone's spirits during the work. He Yan mixed a strong compound for the wooden joints and explained his conception of a safe route: no single tower could carry the entire strain. Lu Que checked each anchor for decay and replaced pieces that no longer held securely.

何晏帶著銀線與下次止風刻的新計畫回到蘆島。三座側塔都修好了，但彼此連接仍未完成。他需要每座塔的志願者、穩定的時間信號，以及任何環節失敗時可以停止測試的方法。陸雀堅持受傷鳥群與疲憊旅人不能被留在銀線下方。兩人整個上午沿預定路線安排安靜庇護處。 他們修出緩坡，讓年長居民也能使用樹根塔旁的小徑。一位收藏家提供稀有鐘零件，一名巡迴喜劇演員則在工作時逗大家開心。何晏調製堅固的接木混合料，說明他對安全路線的構想：不能讓任何一座塔獨自承受全部拉力。陸雀檢查每個錨點是否腐朽，替換不再牢靠的部分。

On the morning of the test, the old tower keeper stood at the blue station, the young mason at the red station, and He Yan at the silver-wave station. Lu Que traveled between them by the lower garden paths, watching the cranes rather than the bells. They agreed to use colored flags if speaking tubes failed. No one would begin without seeing the next tower's flag. When the sun reached the cloud edge, all three stations were ready. The repair had fostered a new partnership among islands that once competed. The apprentices would inherit the maintenance lessons, not the authority to command every future decision. They measured the interval between signals twice before starting a patrol along the lower road. Lu Que noticed a change in the birds' perception of the sound: they raised their heads but did not flee. He Yan wrote this observation on the same sheet as the mechanical readings.

測試當天早晨，老守塔人站在藍色站、年輕石匠守紅色站，何晏則在銀浪站。陸雀沿下方花園小徑往返，觀察的不是鐘而是雲鶴。他們同意傳聲管故障時改用彩旗。沒看到下一座塔的旗，任何人都不能開始。太陽到達雲緣時，三座站台都準備好了。 這次修復促成了曾互相競爭的島嶼間的新合作。學徒將繼承維護課程，卻不會繼承命令未來所有決定的權力。各站在沿下方道路巡查前，把信號間隔測了兩次。陸雀注意到鳥群對聲音的感知改變了：牠們抬頭，卻沒有逃走。何晏把這項觀察寫在機械數據同一張表上。

The blue note sounded first, followed by the red. The silver-wave station waited the extra pause suggested by Lu Que's bird observations. Then He Yan struck the third bowl. A narrow band of clear air appeared between the islands, visible where drifting pollen began to flow along it. The first crane flew across, rested on a branch, and called to the flock behind it. More birds followed without a human signal. The workers held their positions until the last young bird had landed. The caretaker handed out a short questionnaire to volunteers, asking whether the instructions had been clear. They would resume normal traffic only after the roots were checked. He Yan carried a wooden rod along the bridge while the village sheriff examined the safety ropes. A subtle movement of the cedar leaves showed that the route had begun to yield to the changing wind instead of fighting it.

藍色音調先響，接著是紅色。銀浪站等過陸雀觀鳥後建議的額外停頓，何晏才敲第三只音碗。島與島之間出現一條狹窄的清澈氣流帶，漂浮花粉沿它流動，使道路變得可見。第一隻雲鶴飛過後停在樹枝上，向後方鳥群呼喚。更多鳥不需人類信號就跟了過來。工人留在位置上，直到最後一隻幼鳥落地。 照護者發給志願者短問卷，詢問指示是否清楚。只有在樹根檢查完畢後，才會恢復一般交通。何晏拿著木桿走過橋，村中治安官檢查安全繩索。雪松葉子的一點細微擺動顯示風道已開始順應變化的風，而不是和它對抗。

The test ended when the sun rose fully. No roots had been damaged, and the flight path stayed open long enough for the small flock to cross. Lu Que was relieved but wanted to see how the route behaved during stronger storms. He Yan agreed; a successful demonstration was not permanent safety. They rested beneath the cedar and watched the birds feed in the new meadow. The next challenge was clear: the central bell still rang at the wrong moments, and the great spring front was approaching. The volunteers met in a small suite above the workshop and praised the superb cooperation between islands. He Yan used a pencil to underline every unanswered question on the test report. They had shown that a safe corridor was possible; they had not proved it would survive the coming storm. He and Lu Que decided to approach the central bell before the next still hour.

太陽完全升起時，測試結束。沒有樹根受損，風道開放的時間足夠小鳥群通過。陸雀鬆了一口氣，卻想知道更強風暴時通道會怎樣。何晏同意，成功展示不等於永久安全。兩人在雪松下休息，看鳥群在新草地進食。下一個挑戰已很清楚：中央風鐘仍在錯誤時刻響起，而春季大風前鋒正逐漸逼近。 志願者在工坊上方的小套房裡開會，稱讚各島極出色的合作。何晏拿鉛筆在測試報告中為每個尚未回答的問題畫底線。他們證明了安全風廊有可能存在，卻沒證明它能熬過即將來臨的風暴。他與陸雀決定在下個止風刻前接近中央風鐘。
