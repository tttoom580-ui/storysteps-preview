中央風鐘的核心

The Heart of the Central Bell

雲鶴歸巢：與你修一條天空的路 · 第 16 / 25 章 · 4000–5000 · 英中對照 · 校訂中

The central island floated above the other eight, its cliffs hidden by a belt of spinning cloud. He Yan and Lu Que reached it on a cargo lift after the captain agreed to carry their tools. From below, the bell looked like a silver moon. Up close, its surface showed hundreds of rivets and careful repairs made by generations of workers. Yet a modern control room had been built behind the bell, and the old manual no longer matched the arrangement inside. The bell company had allocated its budget to polished banners and fresh canvas for visiting officials, leaving the root anchors unrepaired. The controller kept confidential records of each sound test. One worker, recovering from an alcoholic father's neglect, spoke with conviction about the danger of silence. His training curriculum had taught him to obey a warning light without asking what the light measured. He agreed to show the visitors the original machinery.

中央島浮在其他八島之上，懸崖隱在旋轉的雲帶後。船長同意運工具，何晏與陸雀才搭貨梯抵達。從下方看，風鐘像一輪銀月；靠近時，表面布滿鉚釘和幾代工匠細心修補的痕跡。但鐘後建了一間現代控制室，舊手冊再也對不上裡面的配置。 風鐘公司把預算分配給迎接官員的漂亮旗幟與新帆布，樹根錨點卻沒有修好。控制員把每次音響測試紀錄保密。一名工人因酗酒父親的忽視而受過苦，堅定談起沉默帶來的危險。他接受的訓練課程只教他服從警示燈，沒教他問燈究竟測量什麼。他同意帶訪客看原始機械。

Inside the bell chamber, they found three long channels connected to the side towers. A fourth, newly installed channel ran directly toward the shipping docks. It carried a stronger signal than any of the others. He Yan traced it to a rotating drum that could make the bell ring more often to protect fast cargo ships. Lu Que noticed the fourth channel pointed across the cranes' nesting route. No one had tested what its repeated note did to living creatures. The enterprise director called the finding a difficult dilemma between commerce and conservation. He Yan thought it honorable to protect workers, but not to manipulate information about animals. A wealthy sponsor had built a mansion beside the control room, displaying a miniature model of the bell. Lu Que used the model to perceive where the fourth channel crossed the oldest path. They marked the dangerous point with a red thread.

風鐘室內，三條長聲道連往側塔；第四條新裝的聲道直接通向貨運碼頭，信號比其他任何一條都強。何晏沿著它找到一面旋轉鼓，可以讓鐘更頻繁地響，以保護快速貨船。陸雀注意到第四聲道正指向雲鶴築巢路線。從來沒有人測試過反覆音調對生物的影響。 企業主管把這項發現稱為商業與保育之間的兩難。何晏認為保護工人是值得尊敬的事，操縱動物資訊卻不是。富裕贊助人在控制室旁建了一座宅邸，展示鐘的微縮模型。陸雀利用模型察覺第四聲道與最古老路線交叉的位置。他們用紅線標出危險點。

The company offered to reduce the extra signal for one night. He Yan refused a temporary silence that might vanish as soon as the visitors left. He requested a physical switch that any trained worker could inspect and operate during a storm. The director said the company would lose shipping revenue. Lu Que asked how much the farms had already lost in food and nesting space. Neither answer was simple, and for once the director listened before speaking again. A cracked pillar stood beneath the drum, and the wrong sequence of signals could startle the whole flock. The old engineer said that earlier generations had treated the bell with near religious worship, afraid to question its design. He Yan laid the repaired side-tower map beside the controls and explained that the mechanism should guide travelers through storms, not command living birds. They agreed to conduct a limited inspection without moving the drum that night.

公司願意把多出的信號降低一晚。何晏拒絕這種訪客一走就可能消失的暫時安靜，要求裝設任何受訓工人都能在風暴中檢查、操作的實體開關。主管說公司會損失航運收入。陸雀問農田已在食物與築巢空間方面損失多少。兩個問題都沒有簡單答案，而主管這次先聽完才再次開口。 旋轉鼓下有根裂開的柱子，錯誤信號順序可能驚動整個鳥群。老工程師說，前幾代人幾乎以宗教崇拜的態度對待風鐘，不敢質疑設計。何晏把修好的側塔地圖放在控制台旁，說明機構本該在風暴中引導旅人，而不是命令活鳥。眾人同意當晚只做有限檢查，不移動轉鼓。

Before midnight, they finished tracing the fourth channel. It had been added only six years earlier, after a shipping accident in the outer cloud lane. The director had intended to save human lives; the engineers had never measured the effect on migration. He Yan wrote a revised design using the existing emergency release lever and adding a backup alarm. Lu Que drew the resting cliffs that would be protected if the signal stopped crossing them. They had found the cause, but not yet a safe way to change it. From the chamber window, the three side towers shone faintly beneath the clouds. On the service balcony, two off-duty workers played hockey with a wooden puck, briefly forgetting the endless repairs. The side towers' separate notes had begun to agree, but the new cargo drum kept interrupting them. He Yan closed his notebook and asked the director for one more day to calculate a replacement warning. Lu Que offered to watch the nests throughout the test. Neither man wanted victory if it came at the expense of people who could not choose the risk.

午夜前，他們追完第四聲道的路徑。它只在六年前增加，起因是外側雲航道發生貨船事故。主管當初想救人，工程師卻從未量測遷徙所受影響。何晏畫出利用既有緊急釋放拉桿、加設備用警報的新設計。陸雀標出停止信號跨越後能受到保護的休息懸崖。他們找到原因，卻還沒找到安全改法。 從鐘室窗外看，三座側塔在雲下微微發亮。勤務陽臺上，兩名下班工人用木製冰球玩曲棍球，短暫忘了無休止的維修。側塔各自的聲音已開始一致，新貨運鼓卻不斷打斷。何晏合上筆記，請主管再給一天計算替代警示。陸雀提出全程觀察巢區。若勝利的代價是讓無法選擇風險的人承受後果，兩人都不想要那種勝利。
