風學院的安靜試驗

The Wind Institute's Quiet Test

The Wind Institute sat on the edge of the cloud wilderness, beyond the last public ferry. Its scholars trained students to read air currents with glass instruments and listening bowls. He Yan had studied there when he was young and had left after a serious disagreement about testing methods. He expected a cold welcome. Lu Que insisted they come anyway, because the fork from Glass Harbor could not be calibrated without the institute's old laboratory. The cook asked about Lu Que's nut allergy before preparing food in bulk for visitors. At breakfast, He Yan had to cite the school rules to request access to the workshop. A younger teacher argued that collaboration would be better than forcing entry. The old master refused to compel anyone to participate, but invited a counselor to help settle the dispute. They agreed that the test would be open to independent observers.

風學院坐落在雲野邊緣，越過最後一班公共渡船才到得了。學者教學生用玻璃儀器與聽音碗讀風流。何晏年輕時曾在這裡學習，後來因測試方法引發嚴重分歧而離開。他預料不會受到歡迎。陸雀仍堅持前來，因為玻璃港帶來的音叉若沒有學院舊實驗室就無法校準。 廚師準備大量訪客餐食前，先詢問陸雀是否對堅果過敏。早餐時，何晏引用校規要求進入工坊。一名年輕教師說合作比強行闖入更好。老院長不願強迫任何人參與，但請來一位輔導員協助化解爭議。最後大家同意讓獨立觀察者見證測試。

Inside the laboratory, they arranged three glass cups around the old tuning fork. Each cup represented one side tower. When the central bell's recorded note played, only the third cup shook enough to spill water. He Yan repeated the experiment with a shorter fork and found the same result. The dangerous pitch did not come from a single damaged tower. It emerged when three different notes reached the same place at the wrong time. A descriptive chart recorded the sound waves, while Lu Que kept track of the birds outside. He had noticed that poor digestion followed several days of unnatural flight. The surrounding valleys had suffered drought, and one student exclaimed that the bell must be responsible for every hardship. He Yan stopped her before the claim spread. A grill beside the laboratory was smoking, but that did not prove the bell had caused the smoke.

實驗室裡，他們把三個玻璃杯排在舊音叉周圍，每只杯代表一座側塔。播放中央風鐘的錄音時，只有第三個杯子震得溢出水。何晏換了較短的音叉重做實驗，結果相同。有害音高並非來自單一損壞的塔，而是三種不同音調在錯誤時刻抵達同一處所產生。 一張描述性圖表記錄音波，陸雀則注意外面的鳥群。他發現鳥群在幾天異常飛行後容易消化不良。附近山谷正經歷乾旱，一名學生驚呼所有苦難一定都是風鐘造成。何晏在說法傳開前阻止她。實驗室旁烤架冒煙，也不能因此證明煙是風鐘造成。

The scholars opened the ancient tower manual and compared it with their measurements. The manual described a still hour that occurred every seventh dawn, when the strongest currents slowed and the three side towers could be tuned in order. He Yan had heard the story as a student but never seen it tested. Lu Que asked whether they could perform a small rehearsal before risking the actual bells. The scholars agreed to build a wooden model the next day. At the institute, some students felt overwhelmed by the prospect of a large repair. Their preliminary report recommended a radical redesign, but He Yan called for rational limits. They could test the sequence, not rebuild every terminal on the islands overnight. A professor who loved mystery thrillers joked about a hidden villain; Lu Que reminded him that wild creatures needed shelter now, regardless of who had made the original mistake.

學者打開古塔手冊，比對測量結果。手冊描述每七日黎明出現的止風刻，強氣流會放慢，三座側塔才能依順序校音。何晏當學生時聽過這故事，卻從沒見過測試。陸雀問能否先用小模型演練，而不是立即冒險敲真正的鐘。學者同意隔天造木製模型。 在學院裡，部分學生被大型修復的前景壓得喘不過氣。初步報告建議徹底重設，但何晏要求保持理性界線。他們能測試順序，卻不可能一夜之間重建群島所有終端站。一位愛讀驚悚小說的教授拿隱藏反派開玩笑；陸雀提醒他，不論當年誰犯錯，野生生物現在就需要庇護。

The wooden model worked on its second attempt. The first had failed because the scholars assumed equal pauses between notes. Lu Que had noticed the cranes shifting their weight differently before each wind change. He suggested measuring the silence after the second note rather than the note itself. When they adjusted the pause, the dangerous vibration disappeared from the glass cups. The students cheered, but He Yan wrote the limits of the experiment at the top of the board. The length of the pause was a variable that earlier models had ignored. Outside, the wilderness remained restless, and the central bell still rang incorrectly. A successful table test was a clue, not a finished road. He Yan thanked Lu Que for noticing what the instruments had missed. Lu Que answered that the tools had noticed things his own ears could not hear. Neither tried to claim the discovery alone.

木製模型第二次測試成功了。第一次失敗，是學者假設每個音之間的停頓都相等。陸雀注意到，雲鶴會在每次風變前以不同方式移動重心。他建議測量第二個音之後的安靜長度，而不是音本身。調整停頓後，玻璃杯中的有害震動消失。學生歡呼，何晏卻先在黑板頂端寫下實驗限制。 停頓長度是一個早期模型忽略的變數。外面的荒野仍不安定，中央風鐘仍在錯誤時刻響起。桌面實驗成功只是線索，不是已修好的路。何晏感謝陸雀注意到儀器錯過的變化。陸雀回答，工具也察覺了他的耳朵聽不到的東西。兩人都沒有獨占發現。
