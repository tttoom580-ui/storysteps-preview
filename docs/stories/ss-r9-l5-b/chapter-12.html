紅帆競技場

The Red Sail Competition

The second side tower had been absorbed into a training arena for airship pilots. The island's best flyers competed there each spring, performing difficult turns around a red sail. This year the organizer planned a grand ceremony just as the cranes were due to pass. He Yan asked for one practice period when the arena could be empty. Lu Que worried that the audience lights and sudden turns would scatter the migrating flock. They entered by the staff gate, carrying the tuning fork in its wooden case. The organizer refused a complete ban on flying because a famous celebrity was scheduled to perform. Yet one crystal trophy had cracked when the bell rang the previous evening. The steward did not want to depress ticket sales, but admitted that the damage might involve fraud by a contractor. His intent was to finish the show; He Yan's was to keep the tower from tearing loose. A threatened lawsuit would not make the loose bolts safe.

第二座側塔被併入飛船駕駛員訓練競技場。島上最好的飛行員每年春天都在此比賽，繞著紅帆做高難度轉彎。今年主辦方計畫舉辦盛大典禮，時間正好撞上雲鶴過境。何晏請求借一段空場的練習時段。陸雀擔心觀眾燈光與突然轉向會使遷徙鳥群四散。他們從員工門進入，木盒裡裝著音叉。 主辦方拒絕全面禁止飛行，因為一位知名明星已預定表演。不過前一晚風鐘響起時，一座水晶獎盃竟然裂開。管事不願使門票銷售低迷，但承認損壞可能涉及承包商詐欺。他的意圖是讓演出完成；何晏的目標則是避免塔鬆脫。就算有人威脅提起訴訟，也無法讓鬆動螺栓變安全。

The tower stood at the arena's center, covered by a stage curtain. Beneath the cloth, its red metal sail showed deep scratches where practice ships had brushed past. He Yan crawled under the platform to inspect its counterweight. Lu Que remained in the open, watching the wind. When the bell rang, the sail turned against its supports instead of with them. A single strong gust could tear the whole frame away. The pilots used a nutrition chart to plan long flights, and Lu Que recognized the same guidance for bird rescue. The sign addressed the teams in the plural: they could share the practice space, but only in separate hours. He Yan used a rod to poke a blocked gear and discovered a hidden portfolio of repair drawings on a high rack. A prescription for an injured pilot had accidentally been mixed among the plans; the repair recommendations appeared random and had never been tested.

塔位於競技場中央，被舞台布幕遮住。布下的紅色金屬帆有深深刮痕，顯示練習船曾擦過。何晏鑽到平台下檢查配重。陸雀留在開闊處觀風。風鐘響起時，風帆逆著支撐轉動，而不是順著它。只要再來一陣強風，整個骨架可能被扯走。 飛行員用營養表規劃長途航行，陸雀認出其中一些原則也適用鳥類救援。告示以複數的「隊伍」為對象：大家可以共享練習場，但必須分不同時段。何晏用金屬桿戳開卡住的齒輪，在高架上發現一套隱藏的修繕圖冊。一張開給受傷飛行員的處方箋不小心混進圖紙；維修建議本身看起來隨意，從未經過測試。

During the rehearsal, He Yan asked the pilots to fly without passengers and keep twice the usual distance from the tower. The first pass confirmed that the sail vibrated when engines approached. On the second, a sudden crosswind bent a support. Lu Que signaled the pilots to turn away, and the staff pulled a release cord. The damaged frame settled safely on the platform. They had prevented an accident, but the red sail still could not answer the central bell. The arena stood beside a residential neighborhood, and its residents demanded a safer plan. He Yan proposed to reverse the direction of the counterweight and schedule a separate test session after dusk. A guard tried to shove the damaged frame into a storage shed, but Lu Que stopped him so the support could be measured first. The ultimate goal was not to win a competition. It was to make the passage safe before the flock arrived.

彩排時，何晏請飛行員不要載客，並與塔保持平常兩倍的距離。第一次通過確認引擎接近時風帆會震動。第二次，一股側風折彎支撐。陸雀打信號讓飛行員轉開，工作人員拉下釋放繩。受損骨架安全落回平台。他們避免了一場意外，但紅帆仍無法回應中央風鐘。 競技場緊鄰住宅區，居民要求更安全的計畫。何晏提議反轉配重方向，並安排黃昏後另一次測試時段。一名守衛想把損壞骨架推進倉庫，陸雀阻止他，好讓大家先測量支撐。最終目標不是贏得比賽，而是在鳥群抵達前確保通道安全。

The organizer canceled the next day's aerial contest and invited the pilots to help repair the sail. Several came without being paid, knowing they would need the route themselves during storm season. The celebrity arrived early and offered to perform in the town square instead. By the following morning, the red sail could turn freely on its restored joint. It sounded one clear note when He Yan struck the fork. The island cheered, but he waited to hear the blue tower answer before smiling. The second note did not erase the danger from the central bell, but it no longer made the danger worse. Lu Que watched a crane circle the arena once and continue toward the western clouds. The two men recorded the result and packed their tools. Only the silver-wave tower remained to be tuned, and its location lay beyond the public routes.

主辦方取消隔日空中競賽，邀飛行員一起修帆。幾人不收薪水就來了，因為風暴季他們自己也需要這條路。明星提前到場，主動改在鎮廣場表演。隔天早晨，紅帆已能靠修好的接點自由轉動。何晏敲響音叉時，它發出一個清楚的音。島上歡呼，他卻等到聽見藍塔回應才露出笑容。 第二個音沒有消除中央風鐘的危險，卻不再使危險惡化。陸雀看著一隻雲鶴繞競技場一圈，繼續飛向西方雲層。兩人記下結果，收好工具。如今只剩銀浪側塔尚未校音，它位於公共路線之外。
