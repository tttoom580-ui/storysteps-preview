新手村村長宣布取消勇者制度 · 第 12 / 25 章 · 2000–3000 · 英中對照 · 校訂中

A nearby settlement asks to join the defense plan without surrendering its independence. Tessa felt the meaning of this new event before the full crowd understood it. In Newleaf Village, each unusual sign could become a rumor by evening, and rumors were hard to pull back once they had spread. Bram stood beside the nearest witness and asked for the sequence of actions, not a dramatic conclusion. Some details contradicted what they had learned the day before. That was disappointing and useful at the same time. Tessa opened a fresh page, wrote the date at the top, and decided the truth would need more than one voice.

鄰近聚落要求加入防禦計畫，但不願交出自治權。 群眾尚未完全理解時，泰莎已感到這件新事的分量。在新葉村，每個異常徵兆到了晚上都可能變成謠言，而謠言一旦傳開就很難收回。布蘭站到最近的證人身邊，詢問行動的先後順序，而非戲劇性的結論。有些細節與昨天得到的資訊矛盾，既令人失望也很有用。泰莎翻開新的一頁，在上方寫下日期，決定真相需要不止一個人的聲音。

News of the plan reached the Royal Hero Office before sunset. The official reply was quick, polished, and strangely certain. Tessa read it twice, looking for the questions it refused to answer. Bram noticed that it described a public problem as a private disagreement, although many witnesses had been affected. They did not accuse anyone merely because the answer sounded unpleasant. Instead, they requested the original documents and the name of the person responsible for storing them. A rival deserved the chance to respond to a clear question, but not the right to hide evidence behind an impressive signature.

日落前，計畫的消息傳到王室勇者署。官方回覆快速、漂亮，而且確定得古怪。泰莎讀了兩次，尋找其中刻意避開的問題。布蘭注意到它把影響多名證人的公共問題描述成私人爭執。他們沒有因回答難聽就隨意指控，而是要求原始文件與保管人的姓名。對手有機會回答清楚的問題，卻沒有權利躲在漂亮簽名後藏匿證據。

The discussion was interrupted by somebody who had not been invited. The visitor carried a story about the Royal Hero Office, but the story mixed known facts with guesses. Tessa asked the visitor to separate what had been seen from what had merely been heard. Bram offered water and a chair, not a promise that every claim would be believed. A useful detail emerged only after the visitor stopped trying to sound certain. The team compared it with the earlier notes and found a new question. They could not solve it that night, so they wrote it down without pretending otherwise.

一位不請自來的訪客打斷討論。此人帶來關於王室勇者署的故事，卻把已知事實和猜測混在一起。泰莎請訪客分開親眼所見與道聽塗說。布蘭遞上水和椅子，卻不承諾全部相信。訪客不再假裝很確定後，才說出有用細節。團隊對照早期筆記，發現新問題。當晚無法解決，他們便把問題寫下，不假裝已經找到答案。

During a pause in the investigation, Bram unfolded an old language ledger. Five marked entries on the first line read "alphabet", "basement", "cigarette", "clip", "cock". The next line held "collection", "foggy", "hire", "honesty", "kindergarten". None was a magic password or an answer by itself; each was a fragment of a larger history. Tessa asked who had written the marks, why they had been kept, and whether they belonged to the same account. It would have been easy to declare the puzzle solved simply because the words were now visible. Instead, they copied the terms, recorded the source, and returned to the unresolved problem outside the room.

調查暫停時，布蘭攤開一本舊語詞紀錄。第一行的五個註記寫著「alphabet」、「basement」、「cigarette」、「clip」、「cock」；下一行則有「collection」、「foggy」、「hire」、「honesty」、「kindergarten」。它們都不是魔法密碼，也無法單獨解答問題；每個詞只是更大段歷史的片段。泰莎詢問誰寫下這些字、為何保存，以及它們是否屬於同一份記錄。只因文字可見就宣稱解開謎題很容易，但他們只抄下詞條、標明來源，便回到室外仍未解決的問題。

A messenger reached Newleaf Village carrying two conflicting reports. Tessa read them slowly rather than asking everyone to trust the louder witness. Bram placed the village bell beside the papers and drew a line under the part they could verify. It was tempting to guess what had happened, especially when people were afraid. Yet one wrong guess could send a friend into danger. The group wrote down the times, the names, and the things nobody had seen clearly. Someone objected to wasting time. Tessa replied that a moment spent checking facts was less expensive than a mistake that could not be repaired.

一名信使帶著兩份互相矛盾的報告抵達新葉村。泰莎慢慢讀完，沒有要大家相信聲音較大的證人。布蘭將村莊銅鐘放在紙張旁，在可以確認的地方畫線。尤其人們害怕時，猜測發生了什麼很容易；但一次猜錯就可能把朋友送入危險。團隊記下時間、姓名和沒有人看清的事。有個人抗議浪費時間。泰莎回答，花一點時間查證，總比犯下無法補救的錯誤便宜。

During a pause in the investigation, Bram unfolded an old language ledger. Five marked entries on the first line read "learning", "liberty", "meaningful", "powder", "previous". The next line held "queer", "reveal", "shampoo", "weave", "wisdom". None was a magic password or an answer by itself; each was a fragment of a larger history. Tessa asked who had written the marks, why they had been kept, and whether they belonged to the same account. It would have been easy to declare the puzzle solved simply because the words were now visible. Instead, they copied the terms, recorded the source, and returned to the unresolved problem outside the room.

調查暫停時，布蘭攤開一本舊語詞紀錄。第一行的五個註記寫著「learning」、「liberty」、「meaningful」、「powder」、「previous」；下一行則有「queer」、「reveal」、「shampoo」、「weave」、「wisdom」。它們都不是魔法密碼，也無法單獨解答問題；每個詞只是更大段歷史的片段。泰莎詢問誰寫下這些字、為何保存，以及它們是否屬於同一份記錄。只因文字可見就宣稱解開謎題很容易，但他們只抄下詞條、標明來源，便回到室外仍未解決的問題。

After the meeting, Bram walked with Tessa toward a quieter road. There was still a great deal to repair, and the group had not agreed on every detail. Tessa admitted being afraid of failing the people who had offered their trust. Bram answered that the aim was not to become impossible to question. It was to make a plan that could be corrected before anybody's pride became everybody's danger. They checked the next appointment and promised to bring another pair of eyes. Friendship did not remove the risk. It made admitting mistakes possible, and that changed how they understood courage.

會議後，布蘭陪泰莎走向較安靜的道路。仍有許多事情待修，團隊也不是每項細節都同意。泰莎坦承害怕辜負那些信任自己的人。布蘭說，目標不是變成不容質疑的人，而是讓計畫能在某人的自尊危害大家之前被改正。他們確認下次約定，答應多找一雙眼睛來檢查。友情不會消除風險，但它讓認錯變得可能，也改變了他們對勇氣的理解。

Before leaving, Tessa wrote one line at the bottom of the page: the day's answer could still be corrected. Bram checked that people affected by the decision could read it and reply. A messenger brought news that the next meeting would concern 'The First False Alarm'. Neither knew precisely what this new matter would demand. They did know what should not be traded away to save a few hours: honest records, a safe path, and the right of someone weaker to disagree. Tessa carried the unfinished notes toward Newleaf Village, listening to the footsteps behind them and thinking of the question that morning would bring.

離開前，泰莎在紙頁底下寫道：今天的答案仍然可以訂正。布蘭確認受決定影響的人能讀到並回應。信使傳來消息，下次會議將處理〈第一次誤報〉。兩人都不確定新事件要求什麼，卻知道哪些東西不能只為節省幾小時就被交換掉：誠實紀錄、安全道路，以及弱者提出異議的權利。泰莎帶著未完成的筆記走向新葉村，聽著背後腳步聲，想著明早會出現的問題。
