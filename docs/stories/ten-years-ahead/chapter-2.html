Two Minutes Before the Lights Go Out

轉學生說他來自十年後 · 第 2 / 25 章 · 1000–2000 · 英中對照 · 校訂中

用互動版查詞、朗讀與收藏

The morning began quietly in the university, although Ivy knew that quiet did not mean safe. It was the early days, and the group had already learned that a confident guess could be worse than an honest question. Ivy reviewed the future notebook and invited Noah and the campus science club to look at the same page. Nobody had to agree before hearing the whole problem. The lights fail almost when Noah said they would. Ivy checks the maintenance report and discovers that a loose cable, not magic, caused the problem. Their task suddenly seemed larger than it had a moment before. Ivy made one important choice: the next step would be written down where everyone could check it.

在這所大學，這個早晨起初很安靜，但艾薇知道安靜不代表安全。現在已是最初幾天，大家明白，自信的猜測有時比誠實提問更危險。艾薇翻閱未來筆記，邀請諾亞與校園科學社一起查看相同頁面。所有人都可以先聽完整個問題，再決定是否同意。當天的第一份紀錄寫道：燈光幾乎在諾亞預言的時間熄滅。艾薇查看維修報告，發現問題來自鬆動電線而不是魔法。那一刻，眼前任務突然顯得比剛才更加龐大。艾薇做出重要決定：下一步要公開記下，讓每個人都能核對。

Noah asked how the beauty might change their plans. There was still a chance to cancel, if the next test worked. The group discussed the chain openly rather than allow a rumor to decide. The chess became one more detail to check before the group moved on.

諾亞詢問美可能如何改變大家的計畫。 如果下一次測試成功，仍然有機會取消。 團隊公開討論鐵鍊，不讓傳聞替大家做決定。 在團隊繼續之前，西洋棋成了需要查核的另一項細節。

Before acting, Noah asked which facts came from direct observation. The team drew two columns: what they knew and what they had only heard. Ivy moved a doubtful claim into the second column because its source was missing. Nobody called the correction a defeat. They wanted the next person to understand the evidence, not to inherit an untested guess.

行動之前，諾亞問大家哪些事實來自直接觀察。團隊畫出兩欄：一欄是確知的事，一欄是聽來的話。艾薇把找不到來源的說法移到第二欄。沒有人把這項更正當成失敗；大家希望後來的人能理解證據，而不是繼承未經驗證的猜測。

The latest report called the result current, and Ivy asked why. Noah asked how the display might change their plans. They examined the growth and waited for a clearer answer. The group discussed the guide openly rather than allow a rumor to decide.

最新報告稱結果目前的，艾薇追問原因。 諾亞詢問展示可能如何改變大家的計畫。 他們檢查成長，等待更清楚的答案。 團隊公開討論引導，不讓傳聞替大家做決定。

They made a small test before changing the larger plan. Ivy explained each step aloud while Noah watched for a mistake. A second person kept the old record so that the result could be compared later. At first the difference looked too small to matter. Then the campus science club noticed that one part of the plan would affect people who were not in the room. The group paused and changed the order of its work. They did not pretend a single success was enough. A good test was something another person could repeat, with clear limits and a way to stop safely.

他們先進行小型測試，再考慮改動大計畫。艾薇逐步大聲說明操作，諾亞則注意可能的錯誤。另一個人保留舊紀錄，方便日後比較。起初差異小得像是不重要，後來校園科學社注意到其中一步會影響當時不在場的人。團隊停下來調整工作順序，沒有假裝一次成功就足夠。真正好的測試，是別人也能重複操作、限制清楚，而且知道如何安全停止的測試。

Another detail changed the mood of the group. Noah admitted he was relieved when Ivy looked for ordinary causes instead of praising his strange power. The others had a chance to ask questions before the decision became final. A shared choice was not always easy, but it was easier to defend when everyone knew its cost.

另一個細節改變了團隊氣氛。諾亞坦承，艾薇尋找普通原因而不讚美他的奇怪能力，反而讓他鬆一口氣。大家在決定正式生效前仍有提問的機會。共同選擇不一定容易，但當所有人都知道代價時，就更能為它負責。

The future notebook was warm from Noah's hand. Its paper was ordinary enough to fold, yet one date refused to stay the same after the group fixed a real problem. Ivy drew no conclusion about destiny. She wrote down the date before and after the change. The two records gave them a better question than the simple word miracle.

未來筆記本因諾亞握在手中而微暖。紙張普通得可以折起，某個日期卻在問題被真正修好後不再維持原樣。艾薇沒有急著下命運的結論，而是記下改變前後的日期。兩筆紀錄讓他們提出比「奇蹟」更好的問題。

The team decided to hunt only after checking the risks. Ivy made a record of the loss, so everyone could compare it later. Noah asked how the membership might change their plans. They examined the message and waited for a clearer answer.

團隊決定先確認風險，再嘗試搜尋。 艾薇記下了失敗，讓所有人日後能夠比較。 諾亞詢問會員資格可能如何改變大家的計畫。 他們檢查訊息，等待更清楚的答案。

Later, Ivy and Noah spoke away from the others. Ivy admitted to being afraid of making a decision that could not be repaired. Noah said that fear was useful when it encouraged care, but dangerous when it stopped people from listening. Neither offered an easy promise. They returned to the group and explained what had changed, inviting questions from every person affected. The time they spent together began to feel more personal, though neither owed the other a promise. They could still disagree the next morning, and they could still work on the same problem. That seemed more valuable than winning an argument in front of an audience.

稍後，艾薇與諾亞離開人群談話。艾薇坦承自己害怕做出無法挽回的決定。諾亞說，害怕若能提醒人謹慎就有用；若讓人不肯聆聽就會危險。兩人沒有輕易保證一切順利，而是回到團隊說明改動，邀請所有受影響的人提問。他們相處的時間逐漸有了更私人的意義，但任何一方都不欠對方承諾。隔天他們仍可能意見不同，也仍能為同一問題努力。這似乎比在眾人面前贏得爭論更有價值。

The group discussed the pear openly rather than allow a rumor to decide. The team decided to respond only after checking the risks. Ivy made a record of the self, so everyone could compare it later. Noah said the situation felt selfish, though he could still be wrong.

團隊公開討論梨子，不讓傳聞替大家做決定。 團隊決定先確認風險，再嘗試回應。 艾薇記下了自我，讓所有人日後能夠比較。 諾亞說情況感覺自私的，但也承認自己可能判斷錯誤。

By evening the team had completed one useful piece of work, not the whole journey. Ivy checked the future notebook one last time and wrote both the result and the remaining doubt. Noah read the entry and asked whether a newcomer could understand it without meeting the people who wrote it. The answer was not quite yes, so they added the missing detail. Their shared goal remained to protect the campus without controlling anyone's future. Tomorrow would bring a different test, and the group could not know its answer in advance. For tonight, however, everyone knew exactly what had been done, what had not been done, and who had agreed to the next step.

傍晚時，團隊完成了一項有用的工作，卻還沒走完整段旅程。艾薇最後檢查未來筆記，同時寫下結果與尚未解決的疑問。諾亞讀完後問：如果新來的人沒見過作者，能看懂嗎？答案還不完全是肯定的，於是大家補上缺少的細節。他們共同的目標仍是在不操控他人未來的前提下保護校園。明天會有不同的考驗，誰也不能提前知道答案。不過今晚每個人都清楚：哪些事已完成、哪些尚未完成，以及下一步由誰共同同意。

They examined the speaker and waited for a clearer answer. Someone described the result as super, which the team needed to verify. The trial became one more detail to check before the group moved on. The latest report called the result worst, and Ivy asked why.

他們檢查喇叭，等待更清楚的答案。 有人形容結果超棒的，團隊仍需要確認。 在團隊繼續之前，試驗成了需要查核的另一項細節。 最新報告稱結果最糟的，艾薇追問原因。
