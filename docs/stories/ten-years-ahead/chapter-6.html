A Plaque with the Wrong Name

The morning began quietly in the university, although Ivy knew that quiet did not mean safe. It was a difficult week, and the group had already learned that a confident guess could be worse than an honest question. Ivy reviewed the future notebook and invited Noah and the campus science club to look at the same page. Nobody had to agree before hearing the whole problem. The notebook gives the name of a student supposed to receive an award. Ivy proves that the future list was only a draft, and the committee corrects its process. Their task suddenly seemed larger than it had a moment before. Ivy made one important choice: the next step would be written down where everyone could check it.

在這所大學，這個早晨起初很安靜，但艾薇知道安靜不代表安全。現在已是困難的一週，大家明白，自信的猜測有時比誠實提問更危險。艾薇翻閱未來筆記，邀請諾亞與校園科學社一起查看相同頁面。所有人都可以先聽完整個問題，再決定是否同意。當天的第一份紀錄寫道：筆記本寫著未來得獎者的名字。艾薇證明那份名單只是草稿，委員會因此修正審查流程。那一刻，眼前任務突然顯得比剛才更加龐大。艾薇做出重要決定：下一步要公開記下，讓每個人都能核對。

Ivy offered to accept, but wanted the others to agree first. Noah asked how the building might change their plans. They examined the crowd and waited for a clearer answer. The group discussed the eve openly rather than allow a rumor to decide.

艾薇願意接受，但希望先取得其他人的同意。 諾亞詢問建築物可能如何改變大家的計畫。 他們檢查人群，等待更清楚的答案。 團隊公開討論前夕，不讓傳聞替大家做決定。

Before acting, Noah asked which facts came from direct observation. The team drew two columns: what they knew and what they had only heard. Ivy moved a doubtful claim into the second column because its source was missing. Nobody called the correction a defeat. They wanted the next person to understand the evidence, not to inherit an untested guess.

行動之前，諾亞問大家哪些事實來自直接觀察。團隊畫出兩欄：一欄是確知的事，一欄是聽來的話。艾薇把找不到來源的說法移到第二欄。沒有人把這項更正當成失敗；大家希望後來的人能理解證據，而不是繼承未經驗證的猜測。

The team decided to examine only after checking the risks. The latest report called the result favorite, and Ivy asked why. Noah asked how the giraffe might change their plans. They examined the musician and waited for a clearer answer.

團隊決定先確認風險，再嘗試檢查。 最新報告稱結果最喜歡的，艾薇追問原因。 諾亞詢問長頸鹿可能如何改變大家的計畫。 他們檢查音樂家，等待更清楚的答案。

They made a small test before changing the larger plan. Ivy explained each step aloud while Noah watched for a mistake. A second person kept the old record so that the result could be compared later. At first the difference looked too small to matter. Then the campus science club noticed that one part of the plan would affect people who were not in the room. The group paused and changed the order of its work. They did not pretend a single success was enough. A good test was something another person could repeat, with clear limits and a way to stop safely.

他們先進行小型測試，再考慮改動大計畫。艾薇逐步大聲說明操作，諾亞則注意可能的錯誤。另一個人保留舊紀錄，方便日後比較。起初差異小得像是不重要，後來校園科學社注意到其中一步會影響當時不在場的人。團隊停下來調整工作順序，沒有假裝一次成功就足夠。真正好的測試，是別人也能重複操作、限制清楚，而且知道如何安全停止的測試。

Another detail changed the mood of the group. The award committee agreed to publish its rules, so future names would not be decided in secret. The others had a chance to ask questions before the decision became final. A shared choice was not always easy, but it was easier to defend when everyone knew its cost.

另一個細節改變了團隊氣氛。得獎委員會同意公開規則，讓未來的名字不再由秘密決定。大家在決定正式生效前仍有提問的機會。共同選擇不一定容易，但當所有人都知道代價時，就更能為它負責。

The clock above the main hall struck the hour, and several students looked up at once. Noah alone kept his eyes on the second hand. Ivy wondered how often he had watched that clock in another version of the day. She did not ask him to prove his loneliness. Instead, she offered him a chair and a chance to explain only what he chose.

大廳上方的時鐘報時，幾名學生同時抬頭。只有諾亞一直看著秒針。艾薇好奇，他在另一個版本的日子裡看過多少次這座鐘。她沒有要求他證明孤獨，只拉出椅子，給他自行決定說多少話的機會。

Someone described the result as official, which the team needed to verify. The poetry became one more detail to check before the group moved on. Ivy made a record of the raincoat, so everyone could compare it later. Noah asked how the repair might change their plans.

有人形容結果正式的，團隊仍需要確認。 在團隊繼續之前，詩成了需要查核的另一項細節。 艾薇記下了雨衣，讓所有人日後能夠比較。 諾亞詢問修理可能如何改變大家的計畫。

Later, Ivy and Noah spoke away from the others. Ivy admitted to being afraid of making a decision that could not be repaired. Noah said that fear was useful when it encouraged care, but dangerous when it stopped people from listening. Neither offered an easy promise. They returned to the group and explained what had changed, inviting questions from every person affected. The time they spent together began to feel more personal, though neither owed the other a promise. They could still disagree the next morning, and they could still work on the same problem. That seemed more valuable than winning an argument in front of an audience.

稍後，艾薇與諾亞離開人群談話。艾薇坦承自己害怕做出無法挽回的決定。諾亞說，害怕若能提醒人謹慎就有用；若讓人不肯聆聽就會危險。兩人沒有輕易保證一切順利，而是回到團隊說明改動，邀請所有受影響的人提問。他們相處的時間逐漸有了更私人的意義，但任何一方都不欠對方承諾。隔天他們仍可能意見不同，也仍能為同一問題努力。這似乎比在眾人面前贏得爭論更有價值。

They did not treat the description sensitive as evidence by itself. The group discussed the sidewalk openly rather than allow a rumor to decide. The soul became one more detail to check before the group moved on. Ivy made a record of the storm, so everyone could compare it later.

他們沒有只憑「敏銳的」這種描述就當成證據。 團隊公開討論人行道，不讓傳聞替大家做決定。 在團隊繼續之前，靈魂成了需要查核的另一項細節。 艾薇記下了暴風雨，讓所有人日後能夠比較。

By evening the team had completed one useful piece of work, not the whole journey. Ivy checked the future notebook one last time and wrote both the result and the remaining doubt. Noah read the entry and asked whether a newcomer could understand it without meeting the people who wrote it. The answer was not quite yes, so they added the missing detail. Their shared goal remained to protect the campus without controlling anyone's future. Tomorrow would bring a different test, and the group could not know its answer in advance. For tonight, however, everyone knew exactly what had been done, what had not been done, and who had agreed to the next step.

傍晚時，團隊完成了一項有用的工作，卻還沒走完整段旅程。艾薇最後檢查未來筆記，同時寫下結果與尚未解決的疑問。諾亞讀完後問：如果新來的人沒見過作者，能看懂嗎？答案還不完全是肯定的，於是大家補上缺少的細節。他們共同的目標仍是在不操控他人未來的前提下保護校園。明天會有不同的考驗，誰也不能提前知道答案。不過今晚每個人都清楚：哪些事已完成、哪些尚未完成，以及下一步由誰共同同意。

Noah asked how the term might change their plans. Noah noted toward in the margin and asked for an example. The group discussed the war openly rather than allow a rumor to decide. At first the answer looked western, but the team checked again.

諾亞詢問術語可能如何改變大家的計畫。 諾亞在頁邊記下「朝向」，並要求舉例。 團隊公開討論戰爭，不讓傳聞替大家做決定。 答案起初看來西邊的，但團隊再次核對。
