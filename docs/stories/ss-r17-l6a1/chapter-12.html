王冠的私人河流

The Crown's Private River

帝國公主與被通緝的魔法師 · 第 12 / 25 章 · 5000–6000 · 英中對照 · 校訂中

The royal summer estate stood where a clear brook met the sea. Coral decorated its old fountains, and a majestic arch rose above the private harbor. Elara arrived to inspect the lease by which the emperor had granted military access to nearby water stations. The document's expiration date had been extended without a public notice, depriving inland communities of a chance to object. A legislator who had questioned the extension was suddenly denied entry to parliament. Elara knew a monarch could hold public property in trust but could not turn every river into private wealth. She placed a copy of the law beside the lease and requested a hearing open to the villages that depended on the water. If such restrictions continued, they could strangle trade between the inland farms and the sea.

王室夏宮位於清澈小溪入海之處，古老噴泉裝飾著珊瑚，私人港口上方豎著雄偉的拱門。艾拉拉來此檢查一份租約：皇帝藉此准許軍隊使用附近的水站。租約到期日被延長，卻沒有公開通知，使內陸社區失去提出異議的機會。一名質疑延長案的議員突然被拒於國會門外。艾拉拉知道君主可以代管公共財產，卻不能把每條河都變成私人財富。她把法律副本放在租約旁，要求舉行對依賴河水的村莊公開的聽證。 這些限制若持續，可能扼殺內陸農田與海岸之間的貿易。

The estate physician examined Cassian before the next investigation. His eyesight remained good, but repeated spells had left a tremor in his fingers and a weakness in his chest. He needed rest, hearty meals, and time away from cold reservoirs. Elara insisted on following the advice, even when Cassian argued that people were in danger. The physician explained that exhausted muscles could fail during a critical spell, just as poor surgical instruments could fail in an emergency. Cassian accepted a day of rest. To keep working, Elara read the transport logs herself. She began to understand how much labor he had hidden beneath his calm, and how often the court had treated his suffering as evidence of guilt.

夏宮醫師在下一次調查前替卡西安檢查。他的視力仍好，但反覆施法已使手指顫抖、胸口虛弱。他需要休息、豐盛的餐食，並暫時遠離低溫儲能庫。即使卡西安說人們仍有危險，艾拉拉也堅持遵從醫囑。醫師解釋，疲憊肌肉可能在關鍵施法時失效，就像不良外科器械在急救時會出問題。卡西安同意休息一天。為了繼續工作，公主親自閱讀運輸紀錄。她開始理解他平靜外表下隱藏了多少辛苦，而法庭又有多少次把他的痛苦當成有罪證據。

At noon, a messenger brought photographs of a damaged pumping station. A guard had tried to rotate an old wheel by force, and the pressure surge nearly crushed a nearby worker. The station's director had responded by ordering an emergency shutdown. Chancellor Varr's office then accused the director of sabotage. Elara asked for a written statement from every operator before deciding whether the order was justified. Her insistence frustrated the royal security chief. “You would elevate a mechanic above the throne?” he demanded. “I would let a mechanic describe a machine,” she replied. “That knowledge does not threaten legitimate authority. It prevents authority from becoming reckless.” The workers also found that a petrol-powered backup pump had been billed twice but delivered only once.

中午，信使送來一座受損抽水站的照片。一名守衛試圖強行旋轉舊輪，壓力暴增，差點壓傷附近工人。站長因此下令緊急停機，瓦爾辦公室卻指控他破壞設備。艾拉拉要求先取得每名操作員的書面說明，再決定命令是否合理。王室安全長對她的堅持很不滿。「妳要把一名技師抬到王座之上嗎？」他質問。「我只是讓技師描述機器，」她回答，「這種知識不會威脅正當權威，卻能防止權威變得魯莽。」 工人還發現一台汽油驅動的備用水泵被請領兩次費用，實際卻只交付一次。

That evening, Cassian told Elara about his mother, who had guided displaced offspring from many families across the frontier. She had survived the war but died after a blocked road delayed medicine delivery. “I hated your father,” Cassian admitted, “because his portrait was hanging in every office that refused to help her.” Elara felt the familiar instinct to defend her family, then let it pass. “You don't owe him tenderness,” she said. “Nor do you owe me a simpler story.” Across the terrace, a lantern hovered in the warm air. Their conversation gained momentum as they spoke about grief without trying to compare whose loss counted more.

當晚，卡西安告訴艾拉拉，母親曾帶著許多家庭的孩子穿越邊境避難。她熬過戰爭，卻在一條道路遭封鎖、藥物無法及時送達後去世。「我恨妳父皇，」卡西安承認，「因為每一間拒絕幫助她的辦公室都掛著他的肖像。」艾拉拉感到熟悉的護家衝動，卻讓那衝動過去。「你不欠他溫柔，」她說，「也不必為了我把故事說得簡單。」露台另一端，一盞燈籠漂浮在暖風中。兩人談論悲傷，不再比較誰的損失更重要，對話因而逐漸深入。

The following morning, Elara found a hidden schedule behind the estate's water ledger. It showed that the crown had rented one reservoir to the army for a secret operation during the week of the first border fire. The signature belonged to the emperor's private treasurer, not Chancellor Varr. This was the first direct link to her father's household, and its meaning frightened her. She asked a court registrar to verify the seal before anyone made a public claim. Cassian offered to stand with her at the inquiry. “I do not need you to defend the emperor,” he said. “I need you to help me find out what he did.” She met his eyes and agreed.

隔天早晨，艾拉拉在夏宮水務帳冊後方找到一份隱藏時程表。表上顯示，第一次邊境大火那一週，王冠曾把一座儲能庫租給軍隊進行祕密行動。簽名屬於皇帝私人司庫，而非瓦爾宰相。這是第一條直接連到父皇內廷的證據，含義讓她恐懼。她請法院登記官先驗證封印，不准任何人立刻公開下結論。卡西安願意在聽證時站在她身旁。「我不需要妳替皇帝辯護，」他說，「我需要妳幫我查清他做了什麼。」她迎向他的目光，點頭同意。

Before leaving the estate, Elara watched fishermen preparing their boats. Their ordinary work had continued under the shadows of royal gardens and military orders, yet neither institution had asked how the river served them. She wrote to the legislator who had been barred from parliament and offered the verified lease documents for a public inquiry. It would not make the emperor innocent or guilty on its own. It would establish a question that could no longer be answered behind closed doors. Cassian joined her near the harbor wall. This time, when he reached for her hand, he waited for her invitation. She took it. The future between them remained uncertain, but its first condition was already clear: neither would own the other.

離開夏宮前，艾拉拉看著漁民整備船隻。他們平凡的工作一直在王室花園與軍令陰影下延續，然而兩種制度都沒詢問河流如何養活他們。她寫信給被拒於國會門外的議員，願意把核驗後的租約文件交給公開調查。單靠這些文件既不能證明皇帝無罪，也不能證明他有罪，卻能建立一個不能再關起門來回答的問題。卡西安走到她身旁的港牆邊。這回，他伸手前等待她邀請。她握住了他的手。兩人的未來仍不確定，但第一個條件已很清楚：誰也不擁有對方。
