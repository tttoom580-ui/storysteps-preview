中尉的帳簿

The Lieutenant's Ledger

帝國公主與被通緝的魔法師 · 第 8 / 25 章 · 5000–6000 · 英中對照 · 校訂中

The provincial court stood beside a swamp where lotus flowers opened at dawn. Lieutenant Mira sought clearance for the witnesses, while Cassian asked a local surveyor to certify the paths through the marsh. An accumulation of sediment had changed the waterways, and the official map was no longer accurate. A blond clerk named Ruen carried a beverage to a retired teacher who would testify about missing children. Elara noticed that many village residents were literate in two languages but had been recorded as illiterate because imperial forms accepted only one script. That error had made their petitions appear marginal, even when hundreds signed them. She ordered no correction herself; the public registry would have to hear each claimant.

省級法庭位於沼澤旁，黎明時蓮花盛開。米拉中尉替證人申請通行許可，卡西安則請地方測量員認證穿越濕地的路線。累積的泥沙改變了水道，官方地圖已不準確。一名金髮書記魯恩端飲料給退休教師，她將為失蹤的孩子作證。艾拉拉注意到許多村民能讀寫兩種文字，卻因帝國表格只接受一種書寫系統，而被登記成不識字。這個錯誤使他們的請願看來無足輕重，即使有數百人簽名也一樣。公主沒有擅自更正，而是要求公共登記處逐一聽取申請人的說明。

The teacher explained that the Sun Engine's transport officers were required to record every shipment by weight. A copy of those figures survived in the harbor office, where a careful official had refused to deduct unexplained losses. Elara asked to retrieve the pages under judicial supervision. The officer who delivered them had once been praised for heroic service during the first border winter; now he feared being branded a traitor for providing facts. “My vocation is to keep people safe,” he said. “It is not to protect a minister's reputation.” Cassian reached across the table to steady a loose page but withdrew when the witness flinched. Trust, Elara was learning, could not be demanded as a military qualification.

退休教師解釋，太陽引擎的運輸軍官必須按重量記錄每批貨物。港務處保留了一份數字副本，一名謹慎的官員拒絕扣除來源不明的損失。艾拉拉要求在司法監督下調取文件。送來資料的軍官曾因第一個邊境寒冬的英勇服務受到嘉獎，如今卻害怕提供事實會被說成叛徒。「我的志業是讓人安全，」他說，「不是保住大臣的名聲。」卡西安伸手想扶住鬆動的紙頁，見證人瑟縮便立即收回。艾拉拉逐漸明白，信任不能像軍事資格一樣被要求。

A chart compared the military shipments with the civilian loss reports. The two sets of dates overlap on seven nights, and each overlap corresponded to a sudden decline in river temperature. To understand the pattern, Elara visited the pumping station herself. Workers were required to exert enormous effort keeping the old equipment running, yet the state called them expendable. Their wages barely covered basic food. Mira introduced a proposal for a public safety board that would include workers and residents rather than only provincial officers. Some nobles objected that such participation was beneath their rank. Elara said knowledge of a machine could matter more than ancestry, and invited the workers to explain the pipes.

一張圖比較軍方運輸與民間損失報告。兩組日期在七個夜晚重疊，每次重疊都對應河水溫度突然下降。為弄懂規律，艾拉拉親自參觀抽水站。工人必須付出巨大努力維持老機器運作，政府卻把他們當成可替換的耗材，工資僅夠買基本食物。米拉提出設立公共安全委員會，讓工人和居民參與，而非只由省級官員決定。有貴族反對，認為這種參與有失身分。艾拉拉說對機器的了解可能比血統更重要，並請工人講解管線。

At noon, a provincial official presented a report declaring that all missing equipment had been destined for hospital repairs. The phrase sounded comforting, but the shipping lists told a different story. A hundred crates labeled medical supplies had moved north to an army depot. Cassian demonstrated how his magic could leave an identifiable cold ring on a copper fitting, then declined to use the trick as a substitute for ordinary inspection. He was becoming weaker each time he cast. Elara insisted that future tests require a medic and a consent form. “You are not a machine,” she told him. He smiled, surprised not by the words but by her willingness to stop a useful spell.

中午，一名省府官員提交報告，宣稱所有失蹤設備都注定要送去修理醫院。這句話聽來令人放心，貨運清單卻顯示另一回事：一百箱標作醫療用品的貨物被運往北方軍營。卡西安示範自己的魔法如何在銅接頭上留下可辨識的冷霜環，卻拒絕把這技巧當成一般檢查的替代品。他每次施法都更虛弱。艾拉拉堅持未來測試必須有醫護人員及同意書。「你不是機器，」她告訴他。他笑了，驚訝的不是這句話，而是她願意停止一項有用的法術。

In the afternoon the court accepted the ledger as evidence, subject to a full audit of its custody. No officer was convicted, and no general order was overturned yet. The decision was modest, but it gave the families a date for the next hearing and a way to demand missing records. Outside, the retired teacher offered the group simple refreshments. Elara ate slowly while listening to stories of children who had crossed the marsh with borrowed shoes. There were no banners or ceremonial salutes. For the first time, she understood why people continued attending hearings that seemed too small to change the Empire. A record created today might protect someone who could not appear until tomorrow.

下午，法庭接受帳簿作為證據，但要求完整稽核其保管過程。尚無軍官被定罪，也沒有全面推翻命令。裁定雖小，卻讓家庭知道下次聽證日期，也取得要求遺漏資料的途徑。法庭外，退休教師為一行人準備簡單茶點。艾拉拉邊吃邊聽孩子們穿著借來鞋子走過沼澤的故事。沒有旗幟，也沒有禮炮。她第一次明白，人們為何願意不斷參加看來微不足道的聽證。今天建立的一筆紀錄，可能保護明天才有能力出席的人。

That evening, Cassian showed Elara a drawing of the underground network. The Sun Engine was not merely a weapon but a chain of pumping stations that reached across the provinces. By itself each station appeared harmless. Combined, the network could freeze an entire border route while sending its stored heat to the army. “If I destroy the central tower, people may lose their homes and hospitals,” Cassian said. “If we leave it intact, Varr can use it again.” Elara answered that the solution would have to change who controlled the system, not merely who possessed the strongest spell. In the dying light, they began drafting conditions for civilian oversight.

傍晚，卡西安給艾拉拉看地下網路圖。太陽引擎不只是一件武器，而是一串橫跨各省的抽水站。每個站點單獨看來都無害，合在一起卻能凍結整條邊境道路，同時把儲存的熱能送往軍隊。「如果我摧毀中央塔，人們可能失去家園和醫院，」卡西安說，「如果保留原樣，瓦爾又能使用它。」艾拉拉回答，解方必須改變控制制度的人，而不只是看誰擁有最強法術。暮色中，他們開始起草民間監督條件。
