墨跡、茶香與叛軍地圖

Ink, Tea, and the Rebel Map

帝國公主與被通緝的魔法師 · 第 9 / 25 章 · 5000–6000 · 英中對照 · 校訂中

用互動版查詞、朗讀與收藏

The next witness was Ysra, an envoy from the eastern federation whom the Empire officially called a rebel mistress of spies. She received Elara at a mountain lodge surrounded by maple trees and fragrant jasmine. A servant served tea with chili biscuits, a local combination that made Mira cough. Ysra studied the princess with guarded interest. “Your presence may alienate my people,” she warned. “We have been told your family cannot distinguish negotiation from surrender.” Elara did not deny the history. She asked only for a chance to hear the eastern account of the first attack. Ysra agreed on the condition that both sides preserve complete minutes rather than convenient extracts.

下一名證人是東方聯邦使者伊絲拉；帝國官方稱她是指揮間諜的叛軍女頭目。她在一座楓樹與茉莉環繞的山間旅舍接見艾拉拉。侍者端上茶與辣椒餅乾，這種本地組合嗆得米拉咳嗽。伊絲拉戒備地打量公主。「妳的出現可能使我的人民疏遠我們，」她警告，「我們一直被教導，妳家族分不清談判與投降。」艾拉拉沒有否認歷史，只請求聽取東方對第一次襲擊的敘述。伊絲拉同意，條件是雙方都保留完整會議紀錄，而非方便的摘錄。

Ysra produced a map marked with twenty years of treaties. Ink had been applied over one route to blot out the former border crossing, but sunlight exposed its outline. The envoy explained that continuity of village access was guaranteed in the oldest agreement. Later amendments had gradually removed references to the crossing, until cumulative omissions made a right disappear without any single dramatic repeal. Elara admired the persistence required to preserve such records. “Our archives call these roads insignificant,” she said. Ysra replied, “They span the distance between a market and a winter without food.” The silence afterward was not hostile. It was the sound of two people measuring the cost of words.

伊絲拉拿出一幅標著二十年條約的地圖。有人用墨塗抹一條路，想掩蓋昔日邊界渡口，但陽光照出它的輪廓。使者解釋，最早的協議保障村民持續使用這條路；後來的修正案逐漸刪除渡口相關文字，累積的省略使權利在沒有任何正式廢止令的情況下消失。艾拉拉佩服保存這些紀錄所需要的堅持。「我們的檔案把這些道路稱為不重要，」她說。伊絲拉回答：「它們跨越的是從市場到飢寒冬季之間的距離。」隨後的沉默並不敵對，而是兩個人衡量文字代價的聲音。

In the next room, people debated how to mobilize villagers for a public meeting without provoking soldiers. One organizer identified herself as a socialist who wanted shared ownership of water stations, while another preferred independent village councils. Elara listened instead of choosing a faction to favor. Their disagreement was vigorous but not violent. Both demanded that families be allowed to socialize freely, travel safely, and repair homes without paying fees to military contractors. “These are not extraordinary privileges,” Elara said. “They are ordinary conditions for peace.” Ysra watched her carefully. “Then you must say so when your own generals are listening.” The princess promised a public statement rather than a private whisper. Both groups insisted that no family should be treated as dispensable simply because it lived far from the capital.

隔壁房裡，人們討論如何動員村民出席公開集會，又不激怒士兵。一名組織者自稱社會主義者，主張水站共有；另一人則偏好獨立村議會。艾拉拉只聽，不挑選自己要支持的派別。他們爭論得激烈，卻沒有暴力。雙方都要求家庭能自由交往、安全旅行，並在不付錢給軍方承包商的情況下修屋。「這不是什麼特殊特權，」公主說，「只是和平生活的一般條件。」伊絲拉謹慎地看著她。「那妳必須在自己的將軍面前說出來。」公主答應公開表態，而不只私下低語。 雙方都堅持，不能只因一家人住得離京城遠，就把他們當成可以犧牲的人。

On a shelf, Cassian found an old field journal written by a technician from the first war. The author had been ordered to trek into the mountains and test the Sun Engine before civilians were evacuated. A waterproof cover protected the pages, and the calculations were still legible. They showed a decisive increase in heat extraction on the night the orphanage burned. The author ended his account with a short question: “Why was the evacuation cancelled after the test began?” Cassian's hands tightened around the book. Elara asked him to leave the journal where it was until witnesses could document its discovery. He obeyed, though every part of him wanted to carry it away.

卡西安在書架上找到一名第一次戰爭技師留下的野外日誌。作者奉命徒步進入山區，在平民撤離前測試太陽引擎。防水封套保存了紙頁，計算仍清晰可讀。數據顯示，孤兒院起火那夜的熱能抽取量有決定性的增加。作者最後寫下一個簡短問題：「為什麼測試開始後，撤離卻被取消？」卡西安握緊書本。艾拉拉請他先留在原處，等證人記錄發現過程再取走。他照做，儘管內心每一部分都想立刻把日誌帶走。

Later, Elara and Cassian walked beneath the maple trees. He had expected the princess to insist that a foreign envoy was lying. She admitted instead that the Empire might have started the war. “Might?” he asked. “I need proof strong enough for people who never met you,” she replied. Cassian watched leaves turn in the wind. “I used to think the truth would speak for itself. Then I learned how easily powerful people can keep others from hearing it.” Elara said they would need more witnesses, not louder accusations. He offered his arm where the path descended. She took it, choosing a brief intimacy without confusing it with a political promise. Elara let herself muse about what a shared border school might look like after peace.

稍後，艾拉拉與卡西安在楓樹下散步。他原本以為公主會堅稱外國使者說謊。她卻承認帝國可能才是開戰的一方。「只是可能？」他問。「我需要強到讓從未見過你的人也能相信的證據，」她回答。卡西安看著葉片在風中旋轉。「我以前以為真相會自己說話，後來才知道權勢之人多容易讓別人聽不見。」艾拉拉說，需要的是更多證人，不是更響亮的指控。下坡時他伸出手臂，她接住了，選擇短暫的親近，卻沒有把它混成政治承諾。 艾拉拉不禁思索，和平之後一所跨境共學的學校會是什麼模樣。

Before leaving, Ysra proposed a joint examination of the field journal under observers from both territories. Elara agreed and sent messages to the civic court. The agreement covered only evidence preservation; it was not a peace treaty, nor a promise of military alliance. That distinction reassured some eastern elders and angered others. The meeting ended without dramatic friendship, but neither side had been arrested. On the road back, Mira handed Elara a sealed royal announcement. Chancellor Varr had declared that the princess was under investigation for aiding the wanted mage. The warrant against Cassian now carried her name as an accomplice. Elara read it twice, then said, “We will answer in public.”

離開前，伊絲拉提議由雙方觀察員共同檢驗野外日誌。艾拉拉同意，並向公民法庭送出通知。協議僅涉及證據保全，不是和平條約，也不是軍事同盟的承諾。這項區別讓某些東方長老安心，也使其他人憤怒。會議沒有戲劇性的友誼，卻也沒有人遭拘捕。回程時，米拉遞給公主一份封印王室公告。瓦爾宰相宣布公主因協助通緝魔法師而接受調查。針對卡西安的通緝令如今也把她列為共犯。艾拉拉讀了兩遍，然後說：「我們公開回應。」
