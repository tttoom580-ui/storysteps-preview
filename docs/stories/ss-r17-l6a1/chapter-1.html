黎明前的通緝令

The Warrant Before Dawn

帝國公主與被通緝的魔法師 · 第 1 / 25 章 · 5000–6000 · 英中對照 · 校訂中

Princess Elara was twenty-eight when her father ordered her to arrest the most dangerous mage in the Empire. The royal clerk tried to abbreviate the fugitive's name, Cassian Vale, but Elara made him write every letter. A warrant authorized capture, not execution; the distinction was applicable even to an enemy of the throne. Beneath the council chamber lay an archive containing military orders, yet the sealed document before her carried tomorrow's date. The minister offered a comparative report instead of the original. Elara had learned to distrust summaries that removed a question. “Our patience is finite,” said the emperor. “Bring him to me before the eastern delegation arrives.”

艾拉拉公主二十八歲那年，父皇命她逮捕帝國最危險的魔法師。王室書記想將逃犯卡西安・維爾的名字縮寫，她卻要求每個字母都寫清楚。逮捕令授權的是拘捕，不是處決；即使對王座的敵人，這項區別仍然適用。議事廳下方有保存軍令的檔案庫，然而眼前封存的文件竟標著明天的日期。大臣拿比較報告代替原件。艾拉拉早已學會懷疑那些刪掉問題的摘要。「我們的耐心有限，」皇帝說，「東方使團抵達前，把他帶到我面前。」

At the river market, war refugees lined up for bread beneath an old anchor displayed outside the sailors' guild. A widow pressed her ration card to her bosom while an orderly from the infirmary checked injured travelers. One young woman requested a clean tunic and a bra from the donation chest; her own clothes had burned in a border raid. Beside the relief tent, a bank teller identified coins paid to a suspected gangster who had threatened witnesses. An officer dismissed the detail as rumor and offered Elara a bribe disguised as a charitable contribution. She refused it. In a bundle of seized papers, she discovered a command copied onto virgin parchment, although its edges had been deliberately stained to look old.

河岸市場裡，戰爭難民在水手公會門外的舊船錨下排隊領麵包。一名寡婦把配給卡貼在胸前，醫務所的勤務員則查看受傷旅人。一位年輕女子向捐衣箱索取乾淨上衣和胸罩，因為她原來的衣服在邊境襲擊中燒毀。救濟帳篷旁，銀行出納辨認出一筆支付給涉嫌威脅證人的黑幫分子的款項。軍官說那只是傳聞，還以慈善捐款為名向艾拉拉行賄。她拒絕了。她在扣押文件中發現，一道命令被抄在全新的羊皮紙上，紙邊卻被刻意弄髒，假裝年代久遠。

Smoke rose from a kitchen where volunteers were broiling fish above coals for the hungry. An old courier with a limp seized Elara's sleeve and pointed toward the junction of two stone roads. “The wanted mage crossed there,” he whispered. His eyelid twitched whenever soldiers passed. He said he could narrate the entire pursuit if she promised the guards would not drag him away. Elara took him behind the kitchen wall. He described silver frost on a wagon wheel after a spell had been cast, but insisted the mage had saved two children rather than attacked them. The courier showed her a child's scarf, still damp from the river. Elara ordered that it be preserved rather than displayed as proof of guilt.

廚房冒出炊煙，志工在炭火上炙烤魚，供飢餓的人食用。一名跛腳老信差抓住艾拉拉的袖口，指向兩條石路交會處。「通緝的魔法師從那裡經過，」他低聲說。每當士兵經過，他的眼皮便會抽動。他表示，只要公主保證守衛不把他拖走，便願意敘述整場追捕經過。艾拉拉帶他到廚房牆後。他說施法之後，馬車輪上留下銀色霜痕，卻堅稱魔法師救了兩個孩子，而非攻擊他們。信差交出一條仍帶河水濕氣的兒童圍巾。公主命人妥善保存，不許拿它直接當作有罪證據。

Magic had three limits every apprentice learned: a spell needed an anchor, it consumed stored heat, and it left a measurable trace. No spell could change the past or rewrite a person's memories. This knowledge had helped the Empire flourish through decades of peaceful engineering, before its rulers transformed the same inventions into weapons. Elara examined the frosted wheel herself. Its marks were consistent with a rescue shield, which absorbed heat from water; the royal accusation described an explosive flame. Her captain, Mira, asked whether the old man might be mistaken. “Certainly,” Elara answered. “So might the minister. We will test both accounts.” She gave the courier a written promise of safe testimony and sent him home.

每個學徒都知道魔法有三項限制：法術需要錨定媒介、消耗儲存的熱能，而且會留下可測量的痕跡。沒有法術能改變過去或改寫人的記憶。這些知識曾幫助帝國靠和平工程繁榮數十年，後來統治者卻把同樣的發明變成武器。艾拉拉親自檢查結霜的車輪。痕跡符合吸收河水熱量的救援護盾，王室指控的卻是爆裂火焰。隊長米拉問老人是否可能弄錯。「當然可能，」公主回答，「大臣也一樣。兩種說法都得驗證。」她給信差一份書面的安全作證保證，讓他回家。

At dusk, the princess followed the courier's route toward a ruined pumping station. Children played between broken pipes while a flower seller tried to keep her plants alive in the dry wind. Once, this district had been called the garden of the river because trade and music passed freely through it. Now soldiers checked identities at every gate. Elara remembered an illustrated lesson from childhood: a ruler's first duty was to protect those who could not command soldiers. She wondered why that lesson had vanished from her father's speeches. A child handed her a paper bird. On its wing someone had written, in careful ink, “Ask who ordered the first fire.” The message was unsigned.

黃昏時，公主沿信差的路線走向廢棄抽水站。孩子們在破水管之間玩耍，一名賣花人試圖讓花朵撐過乾燥的風。這一區從前被稱為河畔花園，因為貿易和音樂能自由通行。現在每座城門都有士兵查驗身分。艾拉拉想起兒時圖畫課本寫的話：統治者的首要責任，是保護無法指揮軍隊的人。她不明白這句教誨為何從父皇的演說裡消失。某個孩子遞給她一隻紙鳥，翅膀上有人用端正的墨跡寫著：「問問是誰下令點燃第一場火。」紙上沒有署名。

A hooded stranger stood across the canal, one hand resting on a copper disk. For a moment the water around him turned to mist, and a frozen ring appeared beneath his boots. Elara raised her sword. The stranger did not flee. “If you came to kill me, Princess, tell me which crime you were promised I committed.” The voice belonged to Cassian Vale. She could have called the guards, collected the reward, and ended the hunt before midnight. Instead she asked for proof about the border fire. Cassian glanced at the child with the paper bird. “The answer is inside your palace,” he said. Then he released the copper disk into the water and disappeared under a shield of harmless fog.

運河對岸站著一名戴兜帽的陌生人，一隻手搭在銅圓盤上。片刻間，他身旁的水化成霧，靴底下出現結冰的圓環。艾拉拉拔劍，陌生人卻沒有逃跑。「如果妳是來殺我的，公主，請先告訴我，他們答應妳我犯了哪一項罪。」那聲音屬於卡西安・維爾。她原本可以立刻召集守衛、領取賞金，在午夜前結束追捕。但她反而要求對方出示邊境縱火案的證據。卡西安看了一眼拿紙鳥的孩子。「答案就在妳的宮殿裡，」他說。接著他把銅盤投入水中，消失在無害的霧盾之後。
