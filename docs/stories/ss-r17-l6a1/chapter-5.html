學院裡沉默的客人

The Academy's Silent Guests

帝國公主與被通緝的魔法師 · 第 5 / 25 章 · 5000–6000 · 英中對照 · 校訂中

Elara's first ally in the capital was Headmistress Sera of the Imperial Academy. Years ago Sera had taught both the princess and Cassian, then refused to approve a military course that hid its injury records. Now she allowed them to enter a sealed lecture hall, but only as prospective witnesses, not celebrated heroes. A banquet was taking place across the courtyard, where donors praised the war effort. A young apprentice described an accusation against Cassian: he had supposedly stolen a design for offshore heat collectors from the academy. Sera produced a letter showing that Cassian had submitted the design publicly, with his name and date recorded. The theft allegation began to fracture under the weight of its own chronology.

艾拉拉回京後找到的第一位盟友，是帝國學院院長瑟拉。多年前，瑟拉曾教過公主與卡西安，後來因一門軍事課程隱瞞受傷紀錄而拒絕批准。如今她允許他們進入封閉講堂，但只能以潛在證人身分入內，不能當成受歡迎的英雄。庭院對面正在舉行宴會，捐助者讚美戰爭。一名年輕學徒說，卡西安據稱偷走學院的離岸熱能採集器設計。瑟拉拿出一封信，證明卡西安早已公開提交設計，姓名與日期清楚登記。竊取指控開始因自身時間線的矛盾而瓦解。

The engineering laboratory had once tested fertilizer for the northern farms and water treatments for coastal fishery villages. Military contractors later turned it into a warehouse for equipment. An inspection officer displayed a heavy harness used to stabilize heat reservoirs; beside it lay confiscated headphones that operators wore to hear warning tones. Intake records showed that the Sun Engine had drawn far more warmth from civilian districts than the official limits allowed. Mira asked for independent measurements. Sera agreed but warned that a court would need the original instruments as well as the copied results. Elara requested preservation rather than immediate punishment. The difference frustrated the younger soldiers, who wanted someone arrested before another supply train could disappear.

工程實驗室從前為北方農田測試肥料，並替海岸漁村研究水處理，後來軍方承包商把它改成設備倉庫。一名檢驗官展示用來穩定熱能儲庫的沉重背帶，旁邊放著操作員被沒收的耳機，原本用於聽警報。能源輸入紀錄顯示，太陽引擎抽取的民用區熱能遠超官方限額。米拉要求獨立測量。瑟拉同意，但提醒法庭需要原始儀器和複製結果。艾拉拉要求先保全證據，而非立刻懲罰。年輕士兵對此很沮喪；他們希望在下一列補給車失蹤前先逮捕某人。

In the courtyard, a troupe rehearsed a ceremonial dance called the Maiden's Promise, though its lyrics had been rewritten to glorify the war. A singer borrowed Elara's scarf and explained that her tenant family had been evicted when army engineers demanded their land. Their home stood near a pier that might soon become a military harbor. At an assembly that afternoon, the singer asked for a referendum on the construction. A minister scoffed that villagers could not understand strategy. Elara answered that a public decision about homes required public information, and that no amount of royal music could substitute for consent. Several students quietly began to rejoice; others feared that the academy would be closed for allowing dissent.

庭院裡，一個劇團排練名為〈少女的承諾〉的儀式舞蹈，但歌詞早被改成頌揚戰爭。一位歌手借了艾拉拉的圍巾，說自己的租戶家庭因軍方工程師徵地而遭驅逐。他們的家靠近一座碼頭，可能很快變成軍港。下午的集會上，歌手要求就工程舉行公民投票。大臣嘲笑村民不懂戰略。公主回答，涉及家園的公共決策必須公開資訊，再多王室音樂也不能取代同意。幾名學生悄悄歡欣，其他人則害怕學院因容許異議而被關閉。

Cassian invited Elara into the old workshop where they had learned to refine copper anchors. “You used to call me arrogant,” he reminded her. She remembered his objections to the academy's militarized experiments, and how she had dismissed them as resentment. “You were stubborn,” she replied. “I was worse. I assumed my father's seal made an argument true.” He sat on a bench beneath a skylight while the afternoon sun shifted across the floor. His hands still trembled from the bridge spell. She offered him the medical supplies stored in her field bag, but did not touch him without asking. “May I check the frost injury?” He extended his hand. This small permission mattered more than the court's grand speeches.

卡西安帶艾拉拉進入舊工坊，他們以前在這裡學習精煉銅製媒介。「妳以前總說我自大，」他提醒她。她記得當年他反對學院把實驗軍事化，自己卻把反對當作怨恨。「你確實固執，」她回答，「但我更糟。我以為父皇的印璽能讓說法自動成真。」他坐在天窗下的長椅上，午後陽光緩緩移過地面。他的手仍因峽谷施法而顫抖。公主拿出行囊中的醫療用品，卻先詢問而未擅自觸碰。「我可以檢查凍傷嗎？」他伸出手。這個小小的許可，比宮廷宏偉的演說更重要。

At sunset, Sera delivered a report about a reservoir test scheduled to occur at midnight. The trial would use water stored beneath the crowded western neighborhood. The official form called the procedure safe, serene, and efficient. Yet the original risk sheet contained a warning that a simultaneous discharge of two chambers might freeze the houses above them. Elara copied the warning and asked the academy's independent examiner to sign the page. Cassian said that if the machine were activated at full capacity, he could not stop it alone. “Then we need people, not a miracle,” she said. Together they planned an appeal to the civic court before the test began, with every precaution documented.

日落時，瑟拉送來一份午夜儲能測試報告。試驗會使用擁擠的西區住宅地下所存的水。官方表格稱程序安全、平穩而有效率，但原始風險表警告：兩個艙室若同時放能，可能凍結上方的房屋。艾拉拉抄下警告，請學院的獨立檢驗員在頁面簽名。卡西安說，若機器以全功率啟動，他不可能獨自阻止。「那我們需要的是人，不是奇蹟，」公主說。兩人決定測試前向公民法庭提出申訴，並把每項防護措施記錄清楚。

Late that evening, a preacher outside the academy gave a sermon about duty and royal gratitude. The audience listened politely, but several families left when the speaker demanded unquestioning obedience. Elara had once believed that loyalty meant silence. Watching them depart, she understood that trust required more than a crown. Before retiring, Sera offered a final warning: powerful patrons could shuffle documents between departments and make a truthful witness appear unreliable. The academy could protect Cassian for one night, not forever. “Tomorrow,” Sera said, “you must decide whether you are defending the throne or the people living beneath it.” Elara answered, “I am beginning to think the throne has forgotten what it is for.”

深夜，學院外一位傳教者談起責任與對王室的感恩。聽眾原本禮貌聆聽，但當講者要求毫無疑問地服從，幾個家庭便離席。艾拉拉以前相信忠誠代表沉默。看著他們離開，她明白信任需要的不只是一頂王冠。休息前，瑟拉給了最後警告：權勢捐助者能把文件在部門之間調換，讓說真話的證人看起來不可信。學院只能保護卡西安一夜，不能保護他一輩子。「明天，」瑟拉說，「妳必須決定，是要保護王座，還是王座下生活的人。」「我開始認為，王座忘了它存在的目的，」艾拉拉回答。
