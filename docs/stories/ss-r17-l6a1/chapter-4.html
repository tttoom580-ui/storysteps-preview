洪水之上的房間

A Room Above the Flood

帝國公主與被通緝的魔法師 · 第 4 / 25 章 · 5000–6000 · 英中對照 · 校訂中

用互動版查詞、朗讀與收藏

The river rose during the night, forcing the travelers to seek rooms in a narrow boarding house. Its landlady, Mistress Orla, recognized Cassian immediately. She had once helped him bleach smoke stains from blankets after the orphanage fire, and she refused to call a rescue worker a murderer. She gave the party a comprehensive list of guests from that winter, including dates that did not match military reports. Her familiarity with the town's old hierarchy made her cautious: a superintendent from the royal transport bureau had inspected her records twice. “He said a widow should be grateful for royal protection,” Orla recalled. Elara found her account credible enough to investigate, but refused to treat affection for Cassian as proof that every detail was true.

河水在夜裡上漲，旅人只好住進一棟狹窄的寄宿屋。房東奧拉夫人一眼就認出卡西安。孤兒院大火後，她曾幫他用漂白劑洗掉毛毯上的煙漬，因此拒絕稱一位救援者為殺人犯。她拿出那年冬天完整的房客名單，許多日期與軍方報告不符。熟悉鎮上舊有權力階層的她相當謹慎：王室運輸局的一名監督員曾兩次檢查她的紀錄。「他說寡婦應該感謝王室保護，」奧拉回憶。艾拉拉認為這份證詞可信到值得調查，但不會因房東喜歡卡西安，就認定每一項細節皆為事實。

The guest room held an old dresser and a thermometer with a cracked glass stem. Cassian repaired neither with magic; he said a spell used to fix trivial things would waste heat needed for emergencies. Elara noticed the devotion with which he cleaned the children's drawings. He had spent years trying to nurture survivors who were taught to mourn in silence. A little girl brought them a bowl of wholesome bean soup, then asked whether soldiers would take her brother next. Elara had heard such questions at banquets only as abstract figures. Hearing one spoken across a kitchen table made her feel the distance between official language and ordinary fear. She could not promise the child safety yet. Elara began to understand the quiet dedication behind years of tending the survivors.

客房裡有一座舊五斗櫃，以及玻璃管裂開的溫度計。卡西安沒有用魔法修理；他說為小事施法會浪費緊急時刻需要的熱能。艾拉拉注意到，他清潔孩子們的畫時十分專注。他多年來努力照顧那些被要求默默哀悼的倖存者。一個小女孩送來一碗營養的豆湯，接著問士兵是否也會帶走她的哥哥。公主過去只在宴會上把這類問題聽成抽象數字。現在有人在廚房桌邊親口說出，讓她感到官方語言與日常恐懼之間的巨大距離。她仍無法向女孩保證安全。 艾拉拉開始理解，多年照顧倖存者背後那份沉默的奉獻。

Rain pressed against the windows, and the river threatened to overflow its banks. A gull circled the quay where a long-abandoned concert hall once hosted a symphony every spring. Beyond it, the marsh looked lush and peaceful, concealing collapsed drainage channels. Mira proposed evacuating the lower houses before midnight. Cassian pointed out a route shown on the local charts but absent from the imperial version. Together they marked shelters on a hand-drawn map and asked residents whether the route would actually work. Elara used no royal decree to force people from their homes. She explained the risk, arranged transport for those who agreed, and secured a separate plan for people who could not move easily.

雨水敲打窗戶，河水幾乎溢出河岸。一隻海鷗盤旋在碼頭上，附近那座廢棄音樂廳從前每年春天都會演奏交響樂。更遠處的沼澤看似草木茂盛而平靜，實際卻藏著崩毀的排水道。米拉提議午夜前撤離低窪民宅。卡西安指出地方地圖上有、帝國版本卻沒有的一條路。兩人一起在手繪地圖上標明避難所，並詢問居民那條路是否真的可行。艾拉拉沒有靠王室命令強迫人們離家；她說明風險、安排願意撤離者的交通，也為行動不便的人另外規劃。

While carrying records upstairs, Elara happened to overhear a pair of officials arguing in the courtyard. One warned that the palace had ordered all copies of the Sun Engine transport logs destroyed. The other insisted the order came from Chancellor Varr, not the emperor himself. Elara wrote the words down without claiming to know their meaning. Orla then produced a receipt bearing the chancellor's mark, showing a heavy machine delivered to the orphanage district two days before the fire. It was enough to refute the claim that no imperial equipment had entered the town. It was not enough to identify who had operated it. Elara remembered the difference, even as anger made her hands shake. The turmoil caused by the rising water had not prevented Orla from keeping her records in order.

艾拉拉搬文件上樓時，無意間聽見兩名官員在院子裡爭執。一人警告，宮廷已下令銷毀太陽引擎運輸紀錄的所有副本；另一人堅稱命令來自瓦爾宰相，而不是皇帝本人。艾拉拉把話記下，沒有假裝自己已明白全部意思。奧拉接著拿出一張有宰相印記的收據，證明大火前兩天，有一部重型機器運進孤兒院區。這足以反駁「帝國設備從未進入小鎮」的說法，卻不足以辨認操作機器的人。即使氣得雙手發抖，公主仍記住了這個區別。 河水上漲引發的混亂，仍沒有阻止奧拉把紀錄保存整齊。

Near dawn, the water reached the first floor. Mira organized boats, Cassian chilled a broken gate just enough to slow the flow, and Orla led the children through an upper passage. Elara remained on the steps until the last household had answered the evacuation call. For the first time, she saw leadership as the patient labor of asking, counting, and returning, rather than the glory of a single speech. The old house suffered damage, but no resident was lost. Orla handed her the original receipt in a waterproof sleeve. “If you take this to the capital, someone will say I invented it.” Elara answered, “Then the court must hear how you kept it, and let others test it.”

接近黎明時，河水淹上了一樓。米拉安排船隻，卡西安讓破損的閘門稍微結冰，減緩水流，奧拉帶孩子們從高處通道離開。艾拉拉留在階梯上，直到每戶人家都回應撤離通知。她第一次看見，領導並非一場漂亮演說，而是不斷詢問、清點與折返的耐心工作。老房子受損，但居民無一失蹤。奧拉把原始收據放進防水袋交給她。「妳帶回京城，一定有人說這是我編的。」公主回答：「那就讓法庭聽妳如何保存，讓別人檢驗。」

By sunrise, the group had left the boarding house. Cassian walked beside Elara for a few minutes without speaking. He told her that when his sister died in the first evacuation, the Empire had counted her among enemy casualties because no local birth register remained. Elara did not offer a convenient apology for a system she had not yet repaired. Instead she asked for her sister's name and wrote it in her own ledger. Cassian watched the pencil move. “You understand that a name is not a reward,” he said. “It belongs to the person.” She nodded. The river behind them carried broken branches toward the sea, and the first patch of blue sky opened above the damaged town.

日出時，隊伍離開寄宿屋。卡西安與艾拉拉沉默並肩走了一段路。他說妹妹在第一次撤離中喪命，因為當地出生登記冊遺失，帝國竟將她算成敵方傷亡。艾拉拉沒有為尚未修復的制度提供輕率道歉，只問了妹妹的姓名，寫進自己的帳冊。卡西安看著鉛筆移動。「妳明白姓名不是獎賞吧，」他說，「它本來就屬於那個人。」她點頭。身後的河流把斷枝帶向大海，受損小鎮上方終於露出第一片藍天。
