A Trial Without a Prisoner

帝國公主與被通緝的魔法師 · 第 10 / 25 章 · 5000–6000 · 英中對照 · 校訂中

The capital woke to the splendor of victory banners, although no battle had been won. Chancellor Varr had staged a celebration to distract from the public inquiry. Soldiers handed out sweet buns, and the fragrance of crushed flowers covered the smell of oil along the river. Elara entered the civic court under protective escort, carrying no weapon. A cynical noble joked that a princess charged with aiding a fugitive deserved a prison rather than a seat. The judge refused to entertain the remark. He asked the prosecutor to state the literal law under which she was accused. The chancellor's office had defaulted to a vague charge of disloyal conduct; it could not identify a prohibited act. The hearing began with that absence.

京城醒來時滿街都是勝利旗幟，儘管沒有任何戰役獲勝。瓦爾宰相安排慶典，想轉移大眾對公開調查的注意。士兵發放甜麵包，壓碎花朵的香氣蓋住河邊油味。艾拉拉在保護人員陪同下走進公民法庭，沒有攜帶武器。一名憤世嫉俗的貴族嘲笑，被控協助逃犯的公主應該進監獄，而非坐在庭上。法官拒絕理會，要求檢察官說明她究竟違反哪一條明確法律。宰相辦公室只籠統指控不忠，卻說不出任何被禁止的具體行為。聽證從這個缺口開始。

Varr offered a concession: if Elara surrendered Cassian, the accusations against her would vanish. She declined, saying that no state should prosecute a person merely to obtain someone else's custody. The chancellor's lawyer made a sharp retort about national morale, claiming that questioning the Sun Engine would deplete public confidence. Elara answered that confidence built on concealed failures was not strong enough to protect anyone. A spontaneous murmur passed through the audience. The judge warned both parties to avoid political speeches, then ordered the prosecution to reveal the documents supporting its complaint. The official file contained a single witness statement, unsigned and dated before Elara's visit to the border. Mira asked that its chain of custody be examined.

瓦爾提出讓步：只要艾拉拉交出卡西安，對她的指控就會消失。她拒絕了，表示國家不能為取得另一個人的拘押權而起訴無辜者。宰相律師尖銳反駁，談起國民士氣，聲稱質疑太陽引擎會耗盡公眾信心。艾拉拉回答，建立在隱瞞失誤上的信任不夠保護任何人。旁聽席傳出自發的低語。法官警告雙方不要作政治演說，接著命檢方提出支撐控訴的文件。官方檔案只有一份沒有簽名的證人陳述，日期甚至在公主出訪邊境之前。米拉請求查驗文件保管鏈。

A young courier came forward with a screwdriver and a box of broken clock parts. He had been a jockey before the war, riding dispatch horses between provinces, and had later repaired office clocks for extra wages. One such clock stood in the chancellor's record room. He testified that its mechanism had been adjusted the day before the suspicious statement appeared. His stepfather, the palace maintenance chief, had told him to keep quiet. The boy began to stutter when he saw his stepfather at the back of the hall. Elara asked the judge to pause and let him speak at his own pace. She would rather lose a dramatic moment than turn a frightened witness into another victim.

一名年輕信差帶著螺絲起子和一箱壞時鐘零件上前。他戰前是騎馬傳遞公文的騎師，後來靠修理辦公室時鐘增加收入。其中一座時鐘就放在宰相的檔案室。他作證說，可疑陳述出現前一天，有人調整過時鐘機械。他的繼父是宮廷維修主管，曾命他保持沉默。少年在庭後看見繼父，說話開始結巴。艾拉拉請法官暫停，讓他按照自己的節奏說話。她寧可失去戲劇性的一刻，也不願再把受驚的證人變成受害者。

Meanwhile, Sera's researchers presented a separate laboratory report. The military had claimed its newest heat reservoirs contained harmless synthetic stone. The academy found traces of a radioactive mineral in some discarded insulation, but the quantity was too small to establish a cause of illness. The finding required controlled handling, not public panic. Cassian warned that the hazard had nothing to do with his spellcasting, which transferred heat rather than emitting such particles. Elara asked for the report to be read in full. An official who expected a sensational accusation looked disappointed. Facts, she reminded herself, were not servants hired to make her side win; they could limit the claims of her allies too.

同時，瑟拉的研究員提出另一份實驗室報告。軍方聲稱最新儲能庫採用無害的合成石材；學院在丟棄的隔熱材料中發現少量放射性礦物痕跡，但含量不足以確認疾病原因。這項發現需要受控處理，不是引發群眾恐慌。卡西安提醒，這種危害與他的施法無關；他的法術轉移熱量，不會放出那類粒子。艾拉拉要求完整宣讀報告。一名原本期待驚人控訴的官員顯得失望。她提醒自己，事實不是雇來幫己方贏的僕人；事實也會限制盟友的主張。

By evening, the court dismissed the vague complaint against Elara, without ruling on Cassian's original warrant. The judge also ordered the chancellor's office to preserve the altered clock, the disputed statement, and the original shipping registers. A legal aid fund would subsidize travel for frontier witnesses who could not afford the journey. Outside, an elderly vendor offered each departing witness a serving of warm soup. Elara had expected applause. Instead she saw people quietly comparing their hearing dates and deciding where to stay. Cassian waited beyond the gate, careful to avoid the officers still authorized to arrest him. “You did not need me in there,” he said. “I needed what you helped uncover,” she replied. No official would be permitted to intrude on protected witness meetings without a court order.

傍晚，法庭撤銷了對艾拉拉的模糊控訴，卻尚未處理卡西安原本的逮捕令。法官也命宰相辦公室保全被調過的時鐘、爭議陳述及原始貨運登記。法律援助基金將補助無力負擔旅費的邊境證人。庭外，一名年長攤販給離開的每位證人一份熱湯。艾拉拉原本預期掌聲，看到的卻是人們安靜核對下次開庭日期，商量住處。卡西安在門外等候，避開仍有權拘捕他的官兵。「裡面妳不需要我，」他說。「我需要的是你幫我找到的證據，」她答。 沒有法院命令，任何官員都不得闖入受保護的證人會面。

They walked apart along the canal until no guards were following. Cassian admitted that, when she first raised a sword against him, he had considered knocking her into the water and escaping. “I might have deserved the cold bath,” she said. He smiled, then grew serious. “If we expose Varr, will your father still rule?” Elara looked at the palace towers across the river. “I don't know. The emperor has the right to defend himself against evidence, not the right to erase it.” Cassian stopped. “That answer cost you something.” She knew he meant her childhood image of a wise father. “It should have cost me questions years ago,” she said. They continued walking without trying to name what was growing between them.

兩人沿運河分開走了一段，確認沒有守衛尾隨。卡西安坦言，公主第一次拔劍指向他時，他曾想把她推進河裡逃走。「或許我當時值得洗個冷水澡，」她說。他笑了，隨即正色。「若我們揭穿瓦爾，妳父皇還能統治嗎？」艾拉拉望著對岸宮殿高塔。「我不知道。皇帝有權對證據提出辯解，沒有權利抹除它。」卡西安停住腳步。「這個答案讓妳付出了代價。」她明白他指的是兒時那位明君父親的形象。「多年前我就該付出提問的代價，」她說。兩人繼續向前，沒有急著為逐漸滋長的感情命名。
