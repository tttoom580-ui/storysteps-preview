衡量細節的法庭

The Court of Small Measures

帝國公主與被通緝的魔法師 · 第 6 / 25 章 · 5000–6000 · 英中對照 · 校訂中

The civic court met beside a public garden on the outskirts of the capital. Its stone entrance was decorated with carved birds and a caption praising impartial justice. People had tried to beautify the hall with flowers for the midsummer festival, but protest notices covered the gates. Chancellor Varr's spokesman began by claiming that Cassian had sabotaged the reservoir. “We allege deliberate treason,” he declared, though no test had occurred yet. Elara asked the judge to extract the precise accusation from the speech and place it in the record. She wanted the court to distinguish plans, suspicions, and actions. The clerk, a cheerful man with a large mustache, agreed to write the words exactly as spoken.

公民法庭位於京城郊區的公共花園旁。石門飾有鳥類雕刻，下方的題字讚美公正司法。人們原本用花朵美化大廳，迎接仲夏節，如今大門卻貼滿抗議公告。瓦爾宰相的發言人開場便指控卡西安破壞儲能庫。「我們指稱這是蓄意叛國，」他說，儘管測試根本還未進行。艾拉拉要求法官從演說中抽取具體指控，寫入紀錄。她想讓法庭區分計畫、懷疑與已發生的行為。留著大鬍子的開朗書記答應逐字記錄。

A delegation of academy students had arrived after an extracurricular debate class. Their teacher was hospitable, offering water and a shady bench to witnesses who had traveled far. A sculptor among them carried a model of the underground chambers, built from plaster so ordinary citizens could understand the design. The trial's complication was simple: one chamber could be tested safely, but two running together might create a dangerous climax of cold pressure. Elara avoided elaborate magical words and asked the sculptor to show the risk in plain language. He moved two small weights on the model, which collided under the audience's eyes. The judge authorized a temporary pause of the dual-chamber test until independent inspection. A simultaneous release of both chambers was therefore forbidden until the second inspection.

一群學院學生參加課外辯論課後來到法庭。他們的老師十分好客，為遠道而來的證人準備飲水與陰涼長椅。其中一位雕塑家帶著石膏製的地下艙室模型，好讓一般市民看懂設計。測試的困難其實很簡單：單一艙室可以安全測試，兩艙同時啟動卻可能造成危險的低溫壓力高峰。艾拉拉避免使用繁複魔法術語，請雕塑家用白話展示風險。他移動模型上的兩個小砝碼，它們在觀眾眼前撞在一起。法官於是核准在獨立檢查前，暫停雙艙測試。 因此，在第二次檢查以前，兩個艙室不得同時放能。

During the recess, a jolly gardener arrived to mow the court's grass, grumbling that officials always chose hearing days to inspect the garden. A grandmother told him to stop nagging and gave him an ounce of sweet tea. These everyday interruptions comforted Elara. To the families gathered outside, the case was not an abstract duel between a princess and a mage. It was about whether children would wake in cold rooms, whether hospitals would lose heat, and whether the elderly could trust a warning. She sat among them without a guard at her elbow. A baker explained that his neighborhood had become a temporary oasis for families driven from the frontier. The court's decision mattered to all of them. The grandmother told the gardener not to nag tired witnesses who needed a quiet bench.

休庭時，一名樂天的園丁來割草，抱怨官員總挑開庭日檢查花園。一位祖母要他別再嘮叨，遞給他一小杯甜茶。這些日常打岔反而讓艾拉拉安心。對庭外的家庭來說，案件不是公主與魔法師之間抽象的決鬥，而是孩子會不會在冰冷房間醒來、醫院會不會失去熱源、老人能不能信任警報。她坐在眾人中間，身旁沒有護衛。一位麵包師說，他的街區如今成了邊境流亡家庭暫時的綠洲。法庭的決定攸關所有人。 祖母告訴園丁，別嘮叨那些需要安靜長椅的疲憊證人。

In the afternoon, a member of parliament appeared with a packet of engineering drawings. She warned that Chancellor Varr had made even cautious critics look disloyal. The nobleman's methods were ruthless but often lawful on paper: he delayed funding, demanded impossible permits, and threatened public employees with dismissal. Elara asked how to challenge that power. The legislator suggested simplicity rather than spectacle. “Publish the original testing conditions, invite independent technicians, and let affected residents submit questions. Then we can compare the findings to the minister's claims.” Cassian listened from the visitors' bench. He had expected the princess to summon soldiers. Instead she was learning to give away control of the evidence.

下午，一名國會議員帶著工程圖出席。她警告瓦爾宰相擅長把再謹慎的批評者都說成不忠。這名貴族的方法冷酷，但在紙面上常常合法：延後經費、索取不可能取得的許可，並以解雇威脅公務員。艾拉拉問如何對抗這股權力。議員建議用簡明程序，而非壯觀場面。「公布原始測試條件、邀請獨立技師，再讓受影響居民提出問題。之後便能把結果和大臣說法比較。」卡西安坐在旁聽席聆聽。他原以為公主會召集士兵，沒想到她正學著把證據控制權交出去。

The judge's ruling did not clear Cassian of every charge, but it prevented the midnight experiment. The minister's supporters called the decision a slump in imperial authority. Elara regarded it as a victory for evidence over hurry. Outside, sunset washed the garden in gold. A messenger from the palace asked her to return alone. He brought a note bearing the emperor's personal seal: “My daughter, you have mistaken defiance for courage. Come before the throne at once.” Elara looked at Cassian. He offered to accompany her, though entering the palace might end his freedom. “No,” she said. “I asked you to trust a court. I will not make you risk your life to attend my family argument.”

法官的裁定沒有洗清卡西安所有罪名，卻阻止了午夜實驗。大臣支持者稱這是帝國權威的衰退。艾拉拉認為，證據戰勝倉促便是勝利。法庭外，夕陽把花園染成金色。宮廷信使要求她獨自回宮，並帶來皇帝的私人印信：「女兒，妳把違抗誤認為勇氣。立刻來到王座前。」艾拉拉看向卡西安。他主動表示願意陪同，儘管入宮可能失去自由。「不，」她說，「我請你相信法庭，就不該讓你為我的家務爭執冒生命危險。」

Back at the academy, the friends arranged a watch for the night. Mira would remain at the civic court with the original models, Sera would shelter the witnesses, and Cassian would compare the heat traces from the abandoned town with the royal machine's specifications. Elara prepared for her father's anger, not knowing whether it came from ignorance or complicity. She changed out of her travel clothes and placed the child's drawing in her satchel. The dark circle on the paper had become familiar, yet its meaning was still uncertain. “Whatever happens,” she told Mira, “do not let the evidence disappear because I wear a crown.” For the first time, the captain answered without a salute: “Then come back to us as yourself.”

回到學院後，朋友們安排夜間值守。米拉留在公民法庭保管原始模型，瑟拉庇護證人，卡西安則比較廢棄小鎮的熱痕與王室機器規格。艾拉拉準備面對父皇的怒氣，卻不知道怒氣源於不知情還是涉案。她換下旅行服，把孩子的畫放進行囊。畫上的黑色圓環已很熟悉，含義仍未明朗。「無論發生什麼，」她對米拉說，「別因為我戴著王冠，就讓證據消失。」隊長第一次沒有行禮，只回答：「那就以妳自己的身分回來。」
