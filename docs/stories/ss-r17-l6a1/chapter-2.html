會記帳的山

The Mountain That Keeps Accounts

帝國公主與被通緝的魔法師 · 第 2 / 25 章 · 5000–6000 · 英中對照 · 校訂中

用互動版查詞、朗讀與收藏

Elara pursued Cassian into the eastern highlands with Mira and six volunteers rather than a regiment. The imperial maps described the people of the upper valley as aboriginal clans; the term was not an excuse to erase their treaties. Small settlements abound along the ridges, where families tended terraced gardens under extreme weather. At that altitude the air was thin enough to make breathing difficult. Mira tried to align their route with the royal charts, but the marked crossing had been destroyed months earlier. The guide's annoyance was visible. “The capital still draws bridges where only wreckage remains,” she said, blunt as a hammer. Elara wrote down the error and asked for a map drawn by people who actually traveled the valley.

艾拉拉帶著米拉和六名志願者，而非整支軍團，追著卡西安進入東部高地。帝國地圖把上游居民稱為原住部族，但這種稱呼並不能成為抹去他們條約的理由。山脊遍布小聚落，家家在嚴酷天候下照顧梯田。這樣的海拔使空氣稀薄，呼吸格外費力。米拉試著讓路線與王室地圖對齊，卻發現標示的渡口早在數月前就毀了。嚮導的惱怒溢於言表。「京城還在畫橋，這裡卻只剩殘骸，」她直言不諱。艾拉拉記下錯誤，要求取得真正走過山路的居民所繪的地圖。

At a wind-battered terrace, the party found an elderly widow repairing her roof. She had lost her partner at the beginning of the war and would not discard the letters he left behind. She showed Elara one letter bearing a military discharge mark dated three days before the alleged border attack. “My husband never entered the army,” she said. “Yet they announced he was discharged for desertion.” The princess maintained a careful posture while examining the seal; her anger could not improve the evidence. The widow refused payment but asked that her testimony be carried to an independent court. Elara promised an endeavor, not a victory. Such a promise, the widow said, sounded more honest than a royal blessing.

在飽受狂風吹襲的梯田邊，隊伍找到一名修屋頂的年長寡婦。她在戰爭初期失去伴侶，始終不肯丟棄他留下的書信。她拿出一封蓋有軍隊退伍章的信，日期竟比據稱的邊境襲擊早三天。「我丈夫從未當過兵，」她說，「他們卻宣布他因逃兵罪遭到除役。」艾拉拉檢查封印時努力保持鎮定，因為怒氣不能改善證據。寡婦拒絕收錢，只求她把證詞交給獨立法庭。公主承諾會盡力，而非保證勝利。寡婦說，這樣的承諾比王室祝福更誠實。

The next hurdle was a military checkpoint above a narrow ravine. Soldiers stood in rigid rows while an officer demanded the princess's authority to pass. Her seal should have been enough, but a new advisory order had reached the mountain first. The officer announced that travelers must undergo rigorous questioning, and that no royal family member could veto the security rules. Elara asked him to show the order. Its signature was genuine, yet the date was wrong; he had received it before the court supposedly approved it. She requested precision instead of confrontation. Mira read the document aloud, marking every time and name, while the mountain guide quietly counted the guards and prepared a safer path.

下一道難關，是深谷上方的軍事檢查站。士兵僵直地列隊站立，一名軍官要求公主提出通行權限。她的印璽原本就足夠，但一份新的諮詢命令竟先抵達山區。軍官宣布旅人都須接受嚴格盤查，任何王室成員也不能否決安全規則。艾拉拉要求查看命令。簽名是真的，日期卻不對；官兵收到文件的時間，早於法庭據稱批准的時間。她選擇精確查證而不是衝突。米拉逐字讀出命令，標記所有時間與姓名，嚮導則悄悄數清守衛，準備另一條安全路線。

Cassian appeared on the opposite rim of the ravine, wearing no uniform and carrying no weapon. He used a versatile copper ring to anchor a bridge of cold air across the gap; it could hold only one traveler at a time, and each crossing drained warmth from his hands. He did not claim to be invincible. When a soldier raised his bow, the mage lowered the bridge rather than risk a panicked stampede. “I came because your pursuit will be used to justify another uprising,” he told Elara. “The minister wants every valley to look rebellious.” She had been trained to answer threats with immediate force. Today she asked what proof he carried. Cassian pointed to a ledger behind the checkpoint wall.

卡西安出現在峽谷的另一側，沒有穿制服，也沒帶武器。他用一枚用途多變的銅環錨定一條冷空氣橋；橋一次只能承受一人，每次通行都會抽走他手上的熱。卡西安並不自稱無敵。士兵舉弓時，他反而撤下橋梁，以免驚慌引發踩踏。「我來是因為妳的追捕會被用來合理化下一場起義，」他對艾拉拉說，「大臣想讓每座山谷都像叛亂地區。」她過去受訓時被要求立即以武力回應威脅。今天她卻詢問他有什麼證據。卡西安指向檢查站牆後的一本帳簿。

The ledger recorded food deliveries that never arrived and weapons shipped under the labels of farming equipment. Someone had ordered soldiers to usher displaced families down the mountain, while sending the food intended for them to private storehouses. The guide said that empty villages were then photographed as proof that their inhabitants supported rebels. Elara copied the entries in front of two witnesses. She would neither seize the entire ledger nor ask the guards to conceal their own involvement; each person deserved a chance to answer a specific allegation. The officer, ashamed, admitted that the orders came from the royal capital. “Then my next journey is home,” the princess said. Cassian warned that the road back would be more dangerous than the hunt outward.

帳簿記載了從未抵達的食糧，以及以農具名義運送的武器。有人下令士兵引導流離失所的家庭下山，卻把原本供應他們的食物送到私人倉庫。嚮導說，空蕩的村莊隨後被拍下，當成居民支持叛軍的證明。艾拉拉在兩名證人面前抄錄條目。她既不打算拿走整本帳，也不會要求守衛隱瞞自身涉案的部分；每個人都應有機會回應具體指控。軍官羞愧地承認命令來自王都。「那麼我下一段路就是回家，」公主說。卡西安警告，歸途會比追捕時更加危險。

That night they sheltered beneath a cliff where lanterns flickered in the wind. The widow's letter, the false map, and the military ledger did not yet prove who had begun the war, but together they defeated the convenient story that Cassian alone had created the chaos. Elara addressed him without the word criminal for the first time. “Travel with us to the river court. If your evidence survives an independent hearing, I will ask that the warrant be suspended.” Cassian studied her face, searching for the familiar arrogance of the palace. “And if the court serves your father?” he asked. “Then I will have to decide what my duty really means.” The two kept watch at separate ends of the camp.

當晚他們躲在懸崖下，燈籠在風中閃爍。寡婦的信、錯誤地圖和軍方帳簿尚不能證明戰爭由誰挑起，但合在一起，已足以擊破「混亂全由卡西安一人造成」的方便說法。艾拉拉第一次不再用罪犯稱呼他。「跟我們到河岸法庭吧。若證據能通過獨立聽證，我會請求暫停通緝令。」卡西安凝視她，像在尋找宮廷熟悉的傲慢。「如果法庭聽命於妳父親呢？」他問。「那我就必須決定，真正的責任是什麼。」兩人在營地兩端分別守夜。
