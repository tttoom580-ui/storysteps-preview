被燒過兩次的小鎮

The Town That Burned Twice

帝國公主與被通緝的魔法師 · 第 3 / 25 章 · 5000–6000 · 英中對照 · 校訂中

The next morning, Cassian led the party through an abandoned riverside town. An advisory plaque still praised the Empire's conquest of the eastern frontier, but most houses were empty. In the square stood a fire-blackened orphanage that the royal papers called a rebel fortress. A broken swing moved in the wind beside a shrub growing through the steps. Elara searched for evidence of a conspiracy rather than assume it. She found a kitchen inventory that listed cucumber, salt, and flour for sixty children on the evening before the fire. Someone had continued to supply the orphanage. The official account claimed it had been deserted for weeks. The contradiction was small, practical, and more disturbing than any dramatic accusation. An abandoned river liner stood at the dock, its passenger deck still painted with cheerful blue stars.

隔天早上，卡西安帶領隊伍穿過一座廢棄的河畔小鎮。告示牌仍讚揚帝國征服東部邊境，但大多數房屋已空無一人。廣場上有一間被火燒黑的孤兒院，王室報紙卻稱它是叛軍要塞。破損的鞦韆在風中搖動，階梯縫裡長出灌木。艾拉拉沒有先認定陰謀存在，而是尋找證據。她找到廚房存貨表，記載起火前一晚給六十名孩子準備的小黃瓜、鹽和麵粉。有人一直在供應孤兒院。官方卻說它早已廢棄數週。這個微小而實際的矛盾，比戲劇化的指控更令人不安。 一艘廢棄的河上客輪停在碼頭，乘客甲板上仍畫著開朗的藍色星星。

A survivor named Lio emerged from a cellar carrying a box of children's drawings. His voice was feeble after months of smoke-related illness, but he remembered a firecracker bursting at the south gate before the flames began. Elara almost dismissed the sound as irrelevant. Cassian explained that military signal powder could imitate festival fireworks; the residue would smell different from normal lamp grease. He asked permission before touching the child's belongings and took only a grain-sized sample from the door hinge. A bitter fume rose when he warmed it with his own body heat. His fingers went numb. “A test, not a verdict,” he said. “Let a second examiner confirm it.” Survivors also remembered a plague of lung illness that followed the smoke for months.

一位名叫里歐的倖存者從地窖走出，抱著一盒孩子的畫。他長期受煙害影響，聲音微弱，卻記得大火前南門曾響起一聲鞭炮爆裂。艾拉拉幾乎認為那聲音不重要。卡西安解釋，軍用信號火藥可以模仿節慶煙火，但殘留物的氣味會和一般燈油不同。他徵求同意後才碰孩子的物品，僅從門鉸鏈取出米粒大小的樣本。他用自己的體溫加熱，冒出刺鼻煙氣，手指也隨之麻木。「這是檢驗，不是判決，」他說，「讓另一名檢驗員確認。」 倖存者還記得，大火煙霧之後曾有肺部疾病流行數月。

The town's clinic had been converted into a recreational hall for occupying soldiers. Its stores contained a vaccine shipment that had never reached the families outside, and boxes of fine textile labeled as medical blankets. Mira grew angry when she saw children's coats missing while officers kept ceremonial uniforms. Elara ordered an inventory under public observation. A soldier used savage slang for the local people, and the princess stopped him. “A uniform does not give you permission to speak about them that way.” The young man looked startled; no royal visitor had ever corrected his captain in front of refugees. Lio quietly took the name of the clerk willing to testify about the missing supplies.

鎮上的診所被占領軍改為休閒廳。倉庫裡有從未送到戶外家庭手中的疫苗，還有標作醫療毛毯的精緻紡織品。米拉看見孩子缺外套、軍官卻留著禮服，十分憤怒。艾拉拉下令在公開見證下清點物資。一名士兵用野蠻的俚語稱呼本地人，公主立即制止。「穿制服不代表你有權這樣談論他們。」年輕士兵愣住了；過去從未有王室訪客在難民面前糾正軍官。里歐悄悄記下願意為失蹤物資作證的書記姓名。

In a teacher's notebook, Elara found a sketch of a silver tower standing beyond the town wall. The tower's shape was identical to a machine drawn in the palace's engineering manuals, except that the drawing showed an extra chamber below ground. Cassian said the chamber could store the heat extracted from river water; its capacity could surpass that of every public reservoir in the province. It might symbolize progress if used to warm homes. If misused, the same machine could drain a whole district before the troops arrived. No mage could summon heat from nothing. Someone had to choose where the energy came from, and someone had to approve that choice. Those signatures were what Elara needed. A careful inspection might lessen the danger to villages still connected to the hidden pipes.

艾拉拉在教師筆記本裡找到一張銀塔素描，塔立在鎮牆外。它的形狀和王宮工程手冊裡的機器完全相同，只有圖上多了一座地下艙室。卡西安說，那艙室能儲存從河水抽取的熱能，容量甚至可能超過全省的公共儲能庫。用來供暖時，它象徵進步；遭到濫用時，同一台機器卻能在軍隊抵達前抽乾整片城區的熱。沒有魔法師能憑空召來熱能。一定有人決定能源從哪裡取得，也一定有人批准。艾拉拉需要找的就是那些簽名。 仔細檢查或許能減輕仍連著暗管的村莊所面臨的危險。

As they prepared to leave, the occupied town's commander rode into the square and demanded Cassian's surrender. Elara stood between them, invoking the law that protected witnesses during an active inquiry. The commander laughed and said that a princess had no right to obstruct imperial justice. “Then let the record show that the imperial army threatened an identified witness without presenting a warrant,” she replied. She did not reach for her sword. Mira recorded the exchange, and two townspeople signed beneath it. A quiet audience gathered behind the commander. He could arrest a fugitive, but not easily carry out a secret execution before dozens of witnesses. He withdrew with a promise that the capital would hear of her disobedience.

他們準備離開時，駐軍指揮官騎馬進入廣場，要求卡西安投降。艾拉拉站在兩人中間，引用調查期間保障證人的法律。指揮官大笑，說公主無權阻撓帝國司法。「那麼就請記錄：帝國軍在未出示逮捕令的情況下威脅一名具名證人，」她回答，沒有拔劍。米拉記錄對話，兩名鎮民在下面簽名。指揮官背後逐漸聚起沉默的人群。他可以逮捕逃犯，卻無法輕易在數十名證人面前祕密處決。最後他撤退，警告會把公主的不服從上報京城。

At sunset, Lio gave Elara the last drawing from the box. It showed the orphanage before the fire, full of children, with a bright star above the roof. On the reverse side was an impression made by a small hand: three circles around a black sun. Cassian recognized the symbol as the mark of the Sun Engine, the Empire's military heat network. He did not claim that the symbol solved the crime. “Children copy what they see,” he said. “Ask who brought that machine here.” Elara folded the drawing with care. She had begun the journey intending to bring a criminal to her father. Now she intended to bring her father a question he could no longer avoid.

日落時，里歐把盒中最後一張畫交給艾拉拉。畫裡是大火前的孤兒院，孩子們在裡面，屋頂上方有一顆明亮的星。背面有小手壓出的印痕：三個圓圈環繞一輪黑日。卡西安認出那是帝國軍用熱能網「太陽引擎」的標記。他沒有聲稱符號就能破案。「孩子會畫下自己看見的東西，」他說，「查出誰把機器送到這裡。」艾拉拉小心折好畫紙。出發時，她原本打算帶一名罪犯去見父皇；如今，她要帶回一個父皇再也無法迴避的問題。
