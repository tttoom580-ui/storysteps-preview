王座前的女兒

A Daughter Before the Throne

帝國公主與被通緝的魔法師 · 第 7 / 25 章 · 5000–6000 · 英中對照 · 校訂中

The emperor received Elara in a gallery lined with portraits of imperial victories. Through its windows she could see a garden of one hundred acre plots, each carefully divided among noble families. Her father stood beside a painting of his own coronation, looking less like a soldier than a tired bachelor who had never expected to inherit an empire. “I taught you that peace is worth sacrifice,” he began. Elara answered that sacrifice could not mean forsake people whose names were absent from reports. His expression did not falter, but the room seemed to grow colder. He announced a disciplinary review of her conduct and asked whether her defense of Cassian came from affection. “It comes from dates and documents,” she said.

皇帝在掛滿帝國勝利肖像的長廊接見艾拉拉。窗外花園分成大片土地，每塊以一英畝計算，整齊分給貴族家族。父皇站在自己的加冕畫旁，看來不像戰士，更像一個疲倦的單身漢，彷彿當年從未料到自己會繼承帝國。「我教過妳，和平值得犧牲，」他開口。艾拉拉回答，犧牲不能意味著拋棄那些連名字都不在報告裡的人。他的表情沒有動搖，房內卻彷彿更冷了。他宣布要審查她的紀律，並問她替卡西安辯護是否出於愛慕。「出於日期與文件，」她回答。

The emperor tried to equate dissent with treason. Elara spread the widow's letter and the transport receipt on the table. She mentioned an emigrant family whose farm had lost fertility after military reservoirs diverted warm water, and a landslide that blocked the last safe road. She had encountered soldiers trained in boxing while village children lacked nourishing meals. “Is this the peace you mean?” she asked. Her father frowned at the implication. “War produces terrible necessities.” “Then necessity should leave a record,” she replied. “Not every inconvenience is an excuse for secrecy.” Outside, a woodpecker struck a tree with stubborn regularity, an oddly precise sound in the gloomy palace grounds. At the intersection of two estate paths, the gloom of approaching rain made the palace garden seem smaller. The children needed warm food to nourish their bodies before another winter arrived.

皇帝試圖把異議等同叛國。艾拉拉把寡婦的信與運輸收據攤在桌上，提起一戶移民家庭：軍用儲能庫改變了溫水流向，使他們的土地失去肥力；一次山崩又堵住最後的安全道路。她見過接受拳擊訓練的士兵，卻也見過缺少營養餐食的村中孩子。「這就是您說的和平？」她問。父皇皺起眉。「戰爭會帶來可怕的必要措施。」「那必要措施就應留下紀錄，」她答，「不是每個不方便都能成為保密藉口。」窗外，一隻啄木鳥以固執而規律的節奏敲樹，在陰鬱的宮苑裡顯得格外清晰。 在莊園兩條小徑交叉處，將至的雨天陰鬱讓宮廷花園顯得更小。 孩子們需要溫暖食物滋養身體，才能迎接下一個冬天。

Chancellor Varr entered without waiting for permission. He argued that the orthodox military doctrine required a prohibition on unauthorized travel in the frontier. Elara asked for the provisional orders issued before the border fire; Varr said they had been destroyed in a flood. She knew the archive beneath the council hall stood above that flood line. The chancellor's confidence irritated her, but she made no accusation she could not support. He proposed that the royal council vote to remove her from the inquiry. His allies voted unanimously in favor. Then Mira stepped from the side door and delivered the civic court's independent preservation order, which protected the investigation regardless of the council's political decision. The vote was unanimous, yet the council could not erase an independent court order.

瓦爾宰相未等通報便走進來。他主張正統軍事教義要求禁止未經授權的邊境旅行。艾拉拉要求查看邊境大火前發布的臨時命令，瓦爾卻說文件在洪水中毀了。她知道議事廳下方的檔案庫位置高於當年的水位。宰相的自信令人惱火，但公主沒有提出無法支持的指控。他建議王室議會投票解除她的調查權，同黨一致贊成。此時米拉從側門進來，交上公民法庭的獨立證據保全令。無論議會作何政治決定，這份裁定都保護調查繼續進行。 表決結果一致，議會卻無法抹除獨立法庭的命令。

When the hearing ended, Elara met her younger brother Dain in the palace conservatory. He was gay and had spent years hiding the man he loved from courtiers who treated every difference as a scandal. He knew something about the cost of the family's vanity. “Father believes silence keeps the house standing,” he said, watching water collect beneath a rose. “But it also traps everyone inside.” Dain offered access to an old register that listed the Sun Engine's earliest sponsors. Elara refused to let him steal it; he could copy the public portions and request judicial permission for the rest. She did not want one secret abuse repaired by another. Her brother smiled sadly and agreed.

聽證結束後，艾拉拉在宮廷溫室見到弟弟戴恩。他是男同志，多年來一直對把每種差異都當醜聞的朝臣隱瞞自己所愛的男子。他深知家族虛榮的代價。「父皇相信沉默能讓家族屹立，」他看著玫瑰下的積水說，「但沉默也把所有人困在裡面。」戴恩願意讓她查看一份記有太陽引擎早期贊助者的舊名冊。公主不准他偷出文件；公開部分可以複製，其餘須向法院申請。她不願以新的祕密濫權修補舊的濫權。弟弟帶著苦笑同意了。

Outside the conservatory, Cassian waited beneath a stone arch, having entered with Sera under the court's witness protection. Elara told him the council had stripped her of her official inquiry title. He offered neither pity nor the false comfort of a heroic speech. “You still know how to ask questions,” he said. They compared the preserved orders and noticed that the empire had charged frontier villages for military supplies delivered before the alleged outbreak of war. A disordered account had become a whole system of profit. They agreed to bring the finding to the provincial hearing, where local witnesses could challenge it. Elara felt the first small freedom of acting without an inherited title behind every decision.

溫室外，卡西安站在石拱門下；他與瑟拉在法院證人保護安排下入宮。艾拉拉告訴他，議會已剝奪她的正式調查頭銜。他沒有憐憫，也沒有空洞的英雄演說。「妳仍知道怎麼提問，」他說。兩人比較保全的命令，發現帝國早在據稱開戰前，就向邊境村莊收取軍需費。混亂帳目背後竟形成整套牟利制度。他們決定將發現帶到省級聽證，讓地方證人提出質疑。艾拉拉第一次感到，做決定時不必讓繼承而來的頭銜站在自己背後，也是一種自由。

That night her father sent a private note, asking her to stop embarrassing the family. Elara wrote a reply but did not send it immediately. She described the dead children, the frozen rivers, and the people who could no longer cross the frontier. She did not claim the emperor had personally ordered every wrong. The evidence did not yet support such certainty. But she told him that refusing to investigate was becoming a choice of its own. Before sealing the letter, she heard Cassian laughing with Mira in the corridor, an ordinary human sound inside a palace built for controlled silence. She wondered whether she had mistaken obedience for love throughout her life.

當晚父皇送來私人字條，要她停止讓家族難堪。艾拉拉寫了回信，卻沒有立即寄出。她描述死去的孩子、結冰的河流，以及再也無法跨越邊境的人們。她沒有宣稱所有錯誤都是皇帝親自下令，因為證據尚不足以如此肯定。但她告訴父皇，拒絕調查本身也正變成一種選擇。封信前，她聽見卡西安和米拉在走廊笑出聲，那是刻意維持寂靜的宮殿裡罕見的日常人聲。她不禁想，自己是否一生都把服從誤認為愛。
