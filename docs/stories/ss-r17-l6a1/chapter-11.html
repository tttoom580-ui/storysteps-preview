海峽下的機器

The Machine Beneath the Strait

帝國公主與被通緝的魔法師 · 第 11 / 25 章 · 5000–6000 · 英中對照 · 校訂中

用互動版查詞、朗讀與收藏

The oldest Sun Engine stood under a narrow strait, its copper pipes concealed in the foundation of an abandoned customs house. Sera brought a thermometer calibrated in Celsius and a probe designed to measure changes in river heat. The machine's upper wheel turned slowly because friction had damaged its bearings. Cassian heard a low growl from a pressure chamber and warned that forcing it open could induce a collapse. A peacock wandered through the courtyard, absurdly elegant among broken walls. Elara wished the visit could be simplified into a story about one evil inventor, but the engineering plans were signed by dozens of officers. Their respective responsibilities would have to be examined, not collapsed into one accusation. She refused to simplify the repair history into a tale with only one villain.

最古老的太陽引擎位於狹窄海峽下方，銅管藏在廢棄海關屋的地基裡。瑟拉帶來以攝氏度校準的溫度計，以及測量河水熱能變化的探針。機器上方的輪子轉得很慢，因為摩擦已損傷軸承。卡西安聽見壓力艙傳出低沉吼聲，警告強行打開可能引發坍塌。一隻孔雀在院中遊走，在破牆之間顯得荒謬地優雅。艾拉拉希望此行能簡化成單一邪惡發明者的故事，但工程圖上有數十名軍官簽名。各人責任必須分別查明，不能都塞進同一項指控。 她拒絕把修繕歷史簡化成只有一個反派的故事。

A retired mechanic lived on a grassy mound near the station. His daughter was a pianist who played at the harbor tavern, and their home had survived the war because the family maintained the pumping system. He remembered being ordered to suppress an alarm after the engine froze a fishing boat. The official report described the event as a superficial interruption with no injuries. The mechanic showed Elara a scar from rescuing a trapped worker. “It is ironic,” he said, “that the system called me careless when I refused to hide what it had done.” He had preserved a token of the damaged brass valve, too small to be useful to a thief but large enough for a laboratory comparison.

一名退休技師住在測站附近的草丘上。他的女兒是鋼琴家，在港邊酒館演奏；一家人因負責維護抽水系統而撐過戰爭。他記得曾被命令壓下引擎凍住漁船後發出的警報。官方報告把事件稱為沒有傷亡的表面中斷。技師向艾拉拉展示救出受困工人時留下的疤。「諷刺的是，」他說，「我拒絕隱瞞機器造成的事，制度卻說我粗心。」他保存了一小塊受損黃銅閥門；對小偷沒什麼價值，卻足以進行實驗室比對。

Mira proposed a public test with technicians from the eastern federation and the imperial academy. Their instruments would be placed symmetrically on opposite banks to reduce disagreement about measurements. Elara sought to unify the different teams around one procedure, not one political conclusion. The eastern delegate asked whether anyone would be allowed to stop the test if a safety threshold was crossed. “Yes,” Elara said, “and that authority belongs to the technicians closest to the danger.” Sera wrote the condition into the protocol. The urgency of the investigation did not justify shortsighted risks. Cassian remained outside the chamber, ready to anchor a cooling shield only if the workers requested it. The symmetry of the instruments mattered because each team had to measure the same change independently.

米拉提議由東方聯邦與帝國學院技師共同進行公開測試。雙方儀器將對稱設於兩岸，減少測量上的爭議。艾拉拉希望不同團隊遵守同一套程序，而非接受同一個政治結論。東方代表問，若跨越安全門檻，是否有人有權停止測試。「有，」公主說，「而且這項權力屬於最接近危險的技師。」瑟拉把條件寫進規程。調查再緊急，也不能合理化目光短淺的風險。卡西安留在艙外，只有工人提出要求時才準備啟動冷卻護盾。 儀器的對稱配置很重要，因為每組人都必須獨立測量同一項變化。

The first measurements revealed a pattern of heat loss running toward a hidden pipe. When the workers opened a small access cover, they found the same black-sun symbol as the one drawn by the orphanage child. An old service book listed the pipe as part of an emergency civilian heating route; a later annotation redirected it toward a military warehouse. The alterations bore the chancellor's departmental seal. Cassian wanted to declare the case solved. Elara shook her head. “A department can act without its head's knowledge, and a seal can be misused. We have direction, not yet a verdict.” She saw his frustration, yet he nodded. The truth would need to survive even the doubts of those who wanted it most.

第一批測量顯示，熱能正沿著某條暗管流失。工人打開一個小檢修蓋，發現與孤兒院孩子所畫相同的黑日標記。舊維修冊把管線列為民用緊急供暖路線，後來的註記卻把它改導向軍用倉庫。修改處蓋有宰相部門印章。卡西安想宣布破案，艾拉拉搖頭。「部門可能在主管不知情時行事，印章也可能被冒用。我們有方向，還沒有判決。」她看見他的挫折，他仍點了頭。真相必須經得起最希望它成立的人提出的質疑。

By late afternoon the river had begun to warm as technicians restored the civilian valve to a safe setting. Fishermen watched the change with cautious hope. A child asked whether the water would now remain warm every winter. Elara answered that a single repair did not guarantee future protection. The communities would need maintenance funding, inspection rights, and a voice in operating the machine. Cassian looked pleased when she spoke of institutions rather than heroes. After the last instrument was packed, the pianist invited them to a small concert. Elara accepted for herself, not as a princess expected to appear at every celebration. It felt strange to have a choice that harmed no one. The fishermen who inhabit these shores would need a permanent place on the safety board.

下午較晚時，技師把民用閥門調回安全設定，河水開始回暖。漁民帶著謹慎希望觀看變化。孩子問冬天的河水今後是否都會保持溫暖。艾拉拉回答，一次修理不能保證未來安全；社區還需要維護經費、檢查權及參與操作的聲音。卡西安聽她談制度而不是英雄時，露出欣慰神色。儀器收拾完後，鋼琴家邀請他們參加小型演奏會。艾拉拉以自己的意願接受，不是因為公主理應出席每場慶典。能作出不傷害任何人的選擇，讓她覺得陌生。 居住在這片海岸的漁民需要在安全委員會中擁有永久席次。

The music hall was a modest room lit by candles, but the melody traveled above the sound of waves. Cassian sat beside Elara without speaking. At the final passage, his hand rested on the bench between them. She placed her own hand there, close enough that their fingers touched, and did not pull away. Neither mistook the gesture for forgiveness of the past or agreement about the future. It meant only that for one moment they felt safe together. After the performance, Mira brought news from the capital: Chancellor Varr had ordered the remaining coastal stations placed under direct military command. The window for ordinary inspection was closing. Elara thanked the pianist and prepared to leave at dawn.

音樂廳只是間燭光照亮的小屋，旋律卻越過海浪聲傳遠。卡西安坐在艾拉拉身旁，沒有說話。最後一段演奏時，他把手放在兩人之間的長椅上。她也把手放過去，兩人的手指輕輕碰觸，她沒有抽回。兩人都沒把這姿態誤認成對往事的原諒，或對未來的承諾。它只表示，那一刻他們在一起感到安全。演奏結束後，米拉帶來京城消息：瓦爾宰相下令將剩下的沿海站點全部交由軍方直接指揮。一般檢查的時間正在縮短。艾拉拉謝過鋼琴家，準備黎明出發。
