A Crown of Leaves

我養的史萊姆偷偷成立了王國 · 第 1 / 25 章 · 2000–3000 · 英中對照 · 校訂中

Milo stopped at the garden gate because his slimes had built a throne from flowerpots. A tiny green creature wore a crown of leaves, while another pointed a carrot like a sword. A hundred small voices sang a strange anthem. Milo had been laughed out of three guilds for raising monsters that could not fight. Now the creatures had elected their own king, announced war, and written his name on their declaration without asking. The neighboring kingdom had an army. Milo had a watering can. He looked at the royal slime, which blinked at him, and wondered how to cancel a war without upsetting his pets.

米洛在花園門口停下，因為他的史萊姆竟用花盆建起王座。一隻小綠球戴著葉冠，另一隻把胡蘿蔔當劍。上百道小聲音唱起奇怪的國歌。米洛養的魔物不會戰鬥，已被三個公會嘲笑並趕走。現在史萊姆選了自己的國王、宣布開戰，還未經同意就把他的名字寫進宣戰書。鄰國擁有軍隊，米洛卻只有一把澆水壺。他看著眨眼的史萊姆國王，想著如何在不傷害寵物感情的情況下取消戰爭。

The discussion was interrupted by somebody who had not been invited. The visitor carried a story about Lord Brass, but the story mixed known facts with guesses. Milo asked the visitor to separate what had been seen from what had merely been heard. Neri offered water and a chair, not a promise that every claim would be believed. A useful detail emerged only after the visitor stopped trying to sound certain. The team compared it with the earlier notes and found a new question. They could not solve it that night, so they wrote it down without pretending otherwise.

一位不請自來的訪客打斷討論。此人帶來關於布拉斯領主的故事，卻把已知事實和猜測混在一起。米洛請訪客分開親眼所見與道聽塗說。奈莉遞上水和椅子，卻不承諾全部相信。訪客不再假裝很確定後，才說出有用細節。團隊對照早期筆記，發現新問題。當晚無法解決，他們便把問題寫下，不假裝已經找到答案。

A messenger reached Moss Hollow carrying two conflicting reports. Milo read them slowly rather than asking everyone to trust the louder witness. Neri placed the slime crown beside the papers and drew a line under the part they could verify. It was tempting to guess what had happened, especially when people were afraid. Yet one wrong guess could send a friend into danger. The group wrote down the times, the names, and the things nobody had seen clearly. Someone objected to wasting time. Milo replied that a moment spent checking facts was less expensive than a mistake that could not be repaired.

一名信使帶著兩份互相矛盾的報告抵達苔谷。米洛慢慢讀完，沒有要大家相信聲音較大的證人。奈莉將史萊姆王冠放在紙張旁，在可以確認的地方畫線。尤其人們害怕時，猜測發生了什麼很容易；但一次猜錯就可能把朋友送入危險。團隊記下時間、姓名和沒有人看清的事。有個人抗議浪費時間。米洛回答，花一點時間查證，總比犯下無法補救的錯誤便宜。

During a pause in the investigation, Neri unfolded an old language ledger. Five marked entries on the first line read "acceptable", "advise", "chin", "coconut", "connect". The next line held "cruel", "dose", "drip", "envy", "explore". None was a magic password or an answer by itself; each was a fragment of a larger history. Milo asked who had written the marks, why they had been kept, and whether they belonged to the same account. It would have been easy to declare the puzzle solved simply because the words were now visible. Instead, they copied the terms, recorded the source, and returned to the unresolved problem outside the room.

調查暫停時，奈莉攤開一本舊語詞紀錄。第一行的五個註記寫著「acceptable」、「advise」、「chin」、「coconut」、「connect」；下一行則有「cruel」、「dose」、「drip」、「envy」、「explore」。它們都不是魔法密碼，也無法單獨解答問題；每個詞只是更大段歷史的片段。米洛詢問誰寫下這些字、為何保存，以及它們是否屬於同一份記錄。只因文字可見就宣稱解開謎題很容易，但他們只抄下詞條、標明來源，便回到室外仍未解決的問題。

The first test gave the team a little hope and one new problem. Milo insisted they record both results. Neri watched the boundary of the working area and told observers to keep their distance. The second test was slower, but it showed exactly where the old method failed. A careless account would have called the effort a complete success. They knew better. Each person signed the record only for the part personally checked, leaving space for another witness to disagree. The honest gaps in the notes were not weaknesses; they were signs that the writers understood the limits of what they knew.

第一次測試帶來一點希望，也帶來一個新問題。米洛堅持同時記下兩項結果。奈莉守著工作區邊界，請旁觀者保持距離。第二次測試更慢，卻準確指出舊方法的失敗位置。草率的紀錄會把行動說成完全成功，但他們知道不能如此。每個人只為親自確認的部分簽名，也留空間讓其他證人提出異議。筆記裡誠實的空白不是弱點，而是執筆者理解自身所知有限的證明。

During a pause in the investigation, Neri unfolded an old language ledger. Five marked entries on the first line read "extreme", "gamble", "glance", "hay", "laundry". The next line held "sincere", "stool", "tobacco", "twin", "whistle". None was a magic password or an answer by itself; each was a fragment of a larger history. Milo asked who had written the marks, why they had been kept, and whether they belonged to the same account. It would have been easy to declare the puzzle solved simply because the words were now visible. Instead, they copied the terms, recorded the source, and returned to the unresolved problem outside the room.

調查暫停時，奈莉攤開一本舊語詞紀錄。第一行的五個註記寫著「extreme」、「gamble」、「glance」、「hay」、「laundry」；下一行則有「sincere」、「stool」、「tobacco」、「twin」、「whistle」。它們都不是魔法密碼，也無法單獨解答問題；每個詞只是更大段歷史的片段。米洛詢問誰寫下這些字、為何保存，以及它們是否屬於同一份記錄。只因文字可見就宣稱解開謎題很容易，但他們只抄下詞條、標明來源，便回到室外仍未解決的問題。

After the meeting, Neri walked with Milo toward a quieter road. There was still a great deal to repair, and the group had not agreed on every detail. Milo admitted being afraid of failing the people who had offered their trust. Neri answered that the aim was not to become impossible to question. It was to make a plan that could be corrected before anybody's pride became everybody's danger. They checked the next appointment and promised to bring another pair of eyes. Friendship did not remove the risk. It made admitting mistakes possible, and that changed how they understood courage.

會議後，奈莉陪米洛走向較安靜的道路。仍有許多事情待修，團隊也不是每項細節都同意。米洛坦承害怕辜負那些信任自己的人。奈莉說，目標不是變成不容質疑的人，而是讓計畫能在某人的自尊危害大家之前被改正。他們確認下次約定，答應多找一雙眼睛來檢查。友情不會消除風險，但它讓認錯變得可能，也改變了他們對勇氣的理解。

Before leaving, Milo wrote one line at the bottom of the page: the day's answer could still be corrected. Neri checked that people affected by the decision could read it and reply. A messenger brought news that the next meeting would concern 'The Wrong Letter'. Neither knew precisely what this new matter would demand. They did know what should not be traded away to save a few hours: honest records, a safe path, and the right of someone weaker to disagree. Milo carried the unfinished notes toward Moss Hollow, listening to the footsteps behind them and thinking of the question that morning would bring.

離開前，米洛在紙頁底下寫道：今天的答案仍然可以訂正。奈莉確認受決定影響的人能讀到並回應。信使傳來消息，下次會議將處理〈送錯的戰書〉。兩人都不確定新事件要求什麼，卻知道哪些東西不能只為節省幾小時就被交換掉：誠實紀錄、安全道路，以及弱者提出異議的權利。米洛帶著未完成的筆記走向苔谷，聽著背後腳步聲，想著明早會出現的問題。
