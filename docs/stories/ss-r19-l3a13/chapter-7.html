我養的史萊姆偷偷成立了王國 · 第 7 / 25 章 · 2000–3000 · 英中對照 · 校訂中

The council chooses freedom of movement, but several slimes reject the new taxes proposed by human advisers. Milo felt the meaning of this new event before the full crowd understood it. In Moss Hollow, each unusual sign could become a rumor by evening, and rumors were hard to pull back once they had spread. Neri stood beside the nearest witness and asked for the sequence of actions, not a dramatic conclusion. Some details contradicted what they had learned the day before. That was disappointing and useful at the same time. Milo opened a fresh page, wrote the date at the top, and decided the truth would need more than one voice.

議會選擇保障遷徙自由，幾隻史萊姆卻拒絕人類顧問提出的新稅。 群眾尚未完全理解時，米洛已感到這件新事的分量。在苔谷，每個異常徵兆到了晚上都可能變成謠言，而謠言一旦傳開就很難收回。奈莉站到最近的證人身邊，詢問行動的先後順序，而非戲劇性的結論。有些細節與昨天得到的資訊矛盾，既令人失望也很有用。米洛翻開新的一頁，在上方寫下日期，決定真相需要不止一個人的聲音。

There was still a distance between understanding a problem and solving it. Milo and Neri walked through Moss Hollow with a fresh drawing, asking the same question in different places. A child noticed a mark that adults had missed. An elder remembered a change made years before. The clues did not fit together at once, but neither did they cancel each other out. Milo marked each source and refused to invent a missing answer. They returned with dirty shoes and a clearer idea of what needed a second visit. Sometimes the small witness was the one who showed the safest way forward.

理解問題與解決問題之間仍有距離。米洛和奈莉拿著新畫的圖走過苔谷，在不同地方問相同問題。一個孩子注意到大人忽略的記號，一位長者想起多年前的變化。線索沒有立即拼合，卻也不互相抵銷。米洛標註每項來源，不替空白捏造答案。他們帶著髒鞋子回來，更清楚哪些地方需要再訪。有時指向安全道路的，正是最不起眼的證人。

When the plan was discussed, Neri did not agree with everything Milo said. Their argument began over a detail that seemed small, but it soon exposed a difference in what each considered fair. A volunteer offered to settle the issue by a quick vote. Milo asked to hear from the people who would bear the greatest risk first. The answer changed the proposal. Nobody enjoyed admitting they had missed something, yet the room became quieter after the correction. It was easier to make a useful choice when disagreement was allowed to stay in the open.

討論計畫時，奈莉並不完全同意米洛的話。爭論始於一個看似微小的細節，卻揭露了兩人對公平的不同理解。有志工提議立刻投票解決，米洛則要求先聽取風險最高者的意見。回答改變了提案。沒有人喜歡承認自己漏看了什麼，但訂正後房間反而安靜。當分歧能公開存在時，人們更容易做出有用的選擇。

During a pause in the investigation, Neri unfolded an old language ledger. Five marked entries on the first line read "automatic", "automobile", "clothe", "committee", "drown". The next line held "dumpling", "faucet", "fence", "flock", "juicy". None was a magic password or an answer by itself; each was a fragment of a larger history. Milo asked who had written the marks, why they had been kept, and whether they belonged to the same account. It would have been easy to declare the puzzle solved simply because the words were now visible. Instead, they copied the terms, recorded the source, and returned to the unresolved problem outside the room.

調查暫停時，奈莉攤開一本舊語詞紀錄。第一行的五個註記寫著「automatic」、「automobile」、「clothe」、「committee」、「drown」；下一行則有「dumpling」、「faucet」、「fence」、「flock」、「juicy」。它們都不是魔法密碼，也無法單獨解答問題；每個詞只是更大段歷史的片段。米洛詢問誰寫下這些字、為何保存，以及它們是否屬於同一份記錄。只因文字可見就宣稱解開謎題很容易，但他們只抄下詞條、標明來源，便回到室外仍未解決的問題。

At the end of the day, Milo sat beside the slime crown and tried to count what had genuinely changed. There was no sudden victory, but several people who had been afraid now knew whom to ask for help. Neri brought a second copy of the records and placed it out of reach of anyone who might remove the first. The plan had become less exciting and more dependable. That did not disappoint Milo. The question was never how to appear fearless. It was how to leave a safer place behind, even if the next person in charge had a different name.

一天結束時，米洛坐在史萊姆王冠旁，試著清點真正改變了什麼。沒有突然的勝利，但原本害怕的幾個人如今知道該向誰求助。奈莉帶來第二份紀錄，放到即使第一份被拿走也不會一起消失的地方。計畫變得不那麼刺激，卻更可靠。米洛並不失望。問題從來不是如何顯得無所畏懼，而是即使下個負責人換了名字，也能留下更安全的地方。

During a pause in the investigation, Neri unfolded an old language ledger. Five marked entries on the first line read "lightning", "mental", "microwave", "naked", "nest". The next line held "pollute", "protection", "shovel", "sin", "yolk". None was a magic password or an answer by itself; each was a fragment of a larger history. Milo asked who had written the marks, why they had been kept, and whether they belonged to the same account. It would have been easy to declare the puzzle solved simply because the words were now visible. Instead, they copied the terms, recorded the source, and returned to the unresolved problem outside the room.

調查暫停時，奈莉攤開一本舊語詞紀錄。第一行的五個註記寫著「lightning」、「mental」、「microwave」、「naked」、「nest」；下一行則有「pollute」、「protection」、「shovel」、「sin」、「yolk」。它們都不是魔法密碼，也無法單獨解答問題；每個詞只是更大段歷史的片段。米洛詢問誰寫下這些字、為何保存，以及它們是否屬於同一份記錄。只因文字可見就宣稱解開謎題很容易，但他們只抄下詞條、標明來源，便回到室外仍未解決的問題。

After the meeting, Neri walked with Milo toward a quieter road. There was still a great deal to repair, and the group had not agreed on every detail. Milo admitted being afraid of failing the people who had offered their trust. Neri answered that the aim was not to become impossible to question. It was to make a plan that could be corrected before anybody's pride became everybody's danger. They checked the next appointment and promised to bring another pair of eyes. Friendship did not remove the risk. It made admitting mistakes possible, and that changed how they understood courage.

會議後，奈莉陪米洛走向較安靜的道路。仍有許多事情待修，團隊也不是每項細節都同意。米洛坦承害怕辜負那些信任自己的人。奈莉說，目標不是變成不容質疑的人，而是讓計畫能在某人的自尊危害大家之前被改正。他們確認下次約定，答應多找一雙眼睛來檢查。友情不會消除風險，但它讓認錯變得可能，也改變了他們對勇氣的理解。

Before leaving, Milo wrote one line at the bottom of the page: the day's answer could still be corrected. Neri checked that people affected by the decision could read it and reply. A messenger brought news that the next meeting would concern 'Missing Messenger'. Neither knew precisely what this new matter would demand. They did know what should not be traded away to save a few hours: honest records, a safe path, and the right of someone weaker to disagree. Milo carried the unfinished notes toward Moss Hollow, listening to the footsteps behind them and thinking of the question that morning would bring.

離開前，米洛在紙頁底下寫道：今天的答案仍然可以訂正。奈莉確認受決定影響的人能讀到並回應。信使傳來消息，下次會議將處理〈失蹤的使者〉。兩人都不確定新事件要求什麼，卻知道哪些東西不能只為節省幾小時就被交換掉：誠實紀錄、安全道路，以及弱者提出異議的權利。米洛帶著未完成的筆記走向苔谷，聽著背後腳步聲，想著明早會出現的問題。
