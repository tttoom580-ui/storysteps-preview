Student Thirty-One sent a new warning that the class should cancel its memory exhibition. Every phone in the room could show the same words, yet each student read them with a different fear. One remembered an old argument; another worried about a private drawing; someone else wondered if a joke had gone too far. Lin did not try to collect those worries. Noah suggested that people speak to a trusted adult in private if they needed help. That made the mystery harder to turn into a game, but it also made the classroom safer. Not every secret belonged to the class simply because a message claimed to know it. Lin walked around the empty chair. A small poster on the wall included the word button. 「第三十一名學生」再次警告，說班級應取消回憶展。 教室裡每支手機都能看到同樣文字，每位學生卻帶著不同的害怕閱讀。有人想到以前的爭執，有人擔心私人的畫，也有人懷疑玩笑是否太過火。琳沒有去收集這些憂慮。諾亞建議需要幫助的人私下找可信任的成人。這樣謎團就不容易被當成遊戲，教室也更安全。不是每個祕密都因一則訊息聲稱知道，就屬於全班。 琳繞過空椅子。牆上的小海報寫著按鈕這個字。昏暗角落需要多一盞燈。有人曾問諾亞關於傍晚的問題。入口附近的告示提到落下。

The exhibition will show old photos and notes that not everyone approved for public use. At first, the discovery seemed to point in only one direction. Then Noah asked a quiet question that made Lin look twice. A useful observation should not depend on everybody liking the same answer. Lin wrote down what was certain and left a blank space for what they did not know. It felt a little strange to leave a page unfinished. Still, that blank space was honest. It meant they could learn something new tomorrow without having to pretend yesterday's guess had always been right. "We have enough time to check once more," Noah said. Lin suggested reviewing permissions while the teacher handles the unknown account. Some people wanted to act immediately. Lin understood their worry, yet the choice would affect more than one person. Noah helped turn scattered suggestions into a plan with a beginning and an end. They wrote what each person could do, and what no one should be pushed to do. That last part mattered. 'A fair answer cannot depend on someone being too afraid to refuse,' Lin said. When they finally moved, it was with a plan that could be checked instead of a guess that only sounded confident. 琳提議先查照片授權，讓老師負責處理陌生帳號。 有些人希望立刻行動。琳理解焦急，但這個選擇會影響不只一人。諾亞把零散建議整理成有開頭和結尾的計畫。他們寫下每人能做什麼，也寫下哪些事不能強迫別人做。後面這一點尤其重要。「公平的答案不能靠別人不敢拒絕。」琳說。最後他們按可以檢查的計畫行動，而不是跟著聽起來很有信心的猜測。 課堂閱讀卡用玫瑰造了一個例句。牆上的小海報寫著微笑這個字。入口附近的告示提到聲音。牆上的小海報寫著留下這個字。有人曾問諾亞關於學生的問題。

The exhibition team pauses the photo wall but kept planning other activities. The classroom door closed softly after the last meeting. Lin remembered how quickly one message could change a person's day. Noah had helped slow down the rush to guess, and the teacher had kept the official investigation where it belonged. Nothing about that was like a dramatic chase. Still, it protected something more important than the thrill of a twist: the idea that a student's private fear was not public property. 展覽組暫停照片牆，但其他活動仍繼續規劃。 最後一次討論後，教室門輕輕關上。琳想起一則訊息能多快改變一個人的一天。諾亞幫忙阻止匆忙猜測，老師也讓正式調查由該負責的人進行。這完全不像戲劇性的追逐，卻保護比反轉情節更重要的事：學生私人的害怕不是公共財產。琳希望即使怪帳號消失，全班也能記住這個道理。 「這張筆記裡的「甜蜜的」是什麼意思？」琳問。琳想起本週稍早看到的困擾圖畫。那個時候房間通常更安靜。課堂閱讀卡用觀看造了一個例句。琳想起本週稍早看到的擔心圖畫。

Before leaving, Lin gave the day one last careful look. It had contained a surprise, a question, a decision, and a result. Those parts did not always arrive in the order people wanted. Noah asked whether they were ready for tomorrow. 'Ready to listen,' Lin answered, 'even if tomorrow changes the plan.' The reply was small, but it was true. They put away the things they had used, thanked the people nearby, and made sure nobody was left expecting a promise that had never been agreed to.

離開前，琳最後仔細回顧這一天：有驚喜、問題、決定，也有結果。事情不一定照人希望的順序發生。諾亞問明天準備好了嗎？「準備好傾聽了，」琳回答，「即使明天又要改計畫。」這句話很簡單，卻是真的。他們收拾用品、謝謝附近的人，也確認沒有讓誰期待一個從未答應的承諾。

The small objects from the day were back in their places. The important words were written down, and the people involved had heard the result. Lin could not know every part of the future, but there was comfort in knowing what to do first: ask, check, and listen. Noah said goodbye with a tired smile. Lin returned the smile. Somewhere beyond the familiar classroom, another ordinary day was waiting. It would bring its own surprises, but they did not have to meet them without friends or without a plan.

今天的小物件都回到了原位。重要的話已寫好，相關的人也知道結果。琳不可能知道未來每件事，卻因知道第一步該怎麼做而安心：發問、查證、傾聽。諾亞疲憊地笑著道別，琳也回以微笑。在熟悉的classroom之外，又有平凡的一天等待著，會帶來新的驚喜，但他們不必獨自、毫無計畫地面對。
