The unknown account felt strange precisely because the room was so familiar. Lin could name every classmate, yet the screen suggested there was one more. Noah looked at the official roster and then at the phone, careful not to treat either as an invitation to search private things. 'We should ask the school,' he said. Lin agreed. Finding the truth would matter less if they frightened innocent students along the way. A drawing of a bear hung on the classroom notice board. "We can be brave and still ask for help," Noah said. It should have been removed when the class website changed last year. This new detail changed the way Lin saw the problem. It was tempting to join it to the first idea and declare the matter solved, but Noah pointed out that a clue could have another explanation. They looked again, thinking about where the information came from and whether anyone else could check it. 'If we are wrong, we should be able to say so,' Lin said. That was not weakness. It was a way of keeping an interesting story from becoming an unfair one. A small pause for checking seemed better than a large mistake. One lonely visitor sat by the window and watched the rain. "We can lose when the others are ready," Lin suggested. Somebody had packed meat for a short break later. Somebody had packed milk for a short break later. Lin said the word nothing in a careful answer.

去年班級網站搬遷時，這個檔案本應移除。 這個新細節改變了琳對問題的看法。把它接上最初的想法、宣布答案似乎很容易，但諾亞提醒線索也可能有其他解釋。他們重新檢視資訊從哪裡來，別人能否核對。「如果我們錯了，就應該說出來。」琳說。這不是軟弱，而是避免有趣故事變成不公平的說法。花一點時間查證，總好過犯下大錯。 一位孤單訪客坐在窗邊看雨。「等其他人準備好，我們就能失去。」琳提議。有人為稍後的休息準備了肉。有人為稍後的休息準備了牛奶。琳仔細回答時說出了「沒有任何東西」。

Ms. Chen closed the access with school support and informs affected families. Before acting, Lin asked one more question: could the same goal be reached without making somebody else carry the cost? Noah thought for a while and suggested a smaller, clearer first step. It was less exciting than the plan in their heads, but it could be explained to anyone watching. Lin accepted the change. They spoke to the people responsible, checked the rule that applied, and agreed to return if they needed more help. Good intentions mattered, yet they were not a reason to skip a careful decision. 陳老師在學校支援下關閉存取，通知受影響家庭。 動手前，琳多問一句：能不能不讓別人承擔代價，也達到同樣目的？諾亞想了一會，提出更小、更清楚的第一步。它沒有想像中刺激，卻能向旁人說清楚。琳接受修改。他們和負責的人談過、確認規則，也約好需要時再求助。好意很重要，卻不是跳過仔細決策的理由。 琳想起本週稍早看到的人圖畫。牆上的小海報寫著照片這個字。琳想起本週稍早看到的環狀光圈圖畫。琳因有人被排除而感到難過。琳想起本週稍早看到的歌手圖畫。

Lin understood that fixing a leak matters more than catching someone quickly. A question remained, but the group was a little less afraid of asking it carefully. Lin wrote down which details were confirmed by the school and which were only words from an unknown account. Noah checked the list once more. They did not need to say every private name aloud to make progress. The strongest evidence was the kind that could be explained without harming somebody who had not chosen to speak. Lin closed the notebook and decided the class deserved both an answer and a chance to feel ordinary again. A notice near the entrance mentioned toilet. They talked about the wall near the entrance and how to keep it safe. The day felt warm to Lin, so Noah asked what was wrong. The class reading card used well in an example sentence. "Whose paper is this?" Lin asked.

琳明白修補外洩問題，比急著抓人更重要。 問題仍在，班上卻比較不怕仔細提問了。琳記下哪些細節已由學校確認，哪些只是陌生帳號說過的話。諾亞再核對一次。他們不必把每個私人姓名說出來才算有進展。最有力的證據，是不傷害尚未願意說話的人也能說清楚的證據。琳合上筆記本，覺得全班既值得真相，也值得重新過平凡生活。 入口附近的告示提到洗手間。他們討論入口附近的牆，以及如何保持安全。琳覺得今天有些溫暖的，諾亞便問怎麼了。課堂閱讀卡用還好造了一個例句。「這是誰的紙？」琳問。

The notebook was becoming thicker with small observations. Lin had once imagined that a good story needed one dramatic moment to make everything clear. Real days did not behave that way. One small choice led to another, and people sometimes changed their minds for good reasons. Noah helped put today's paper in the right place. They could return to it later and see exactly why they had acted as they did. That mattered more than making the day sound exciting to someone who had not been there.

筆記本裡的小觀察越來越厚。琳以前以為好故事需要一個戲劇性瞬間讓一切清楚，真實生活卻不是這樣。一個小選擇連著下一個選擇，人也可能因合理理由改變心意。諾亞幫忙把今天的紙收在正確位置。日後再翻開，就能知道當初為什麼那樣行動。這比向沒到場的人說得刺激更重要。

That evening, Lin tried to describe the day in just two sentences. The first sentence was about the problem. The second was about what people did together. Both mattered, and neither would have made sense alone. Lin left space beneath them for another note tomorrow. Noah sent a short reminder to rest. Lin smiled at it. A person could care deeply about a goal and still eat dinner, talk with family, and sleep. Whatever happened next, those ordinary things were part of the answer too.

那天晚上，琳試著用兩句話描述今天。第一句是問題，第二句是大家一起做的事。兩句都重要，單獨看卻不完整。琳在下面留空，準備明天再記。諾亞傳來簡短提醒，要記得休息。琳笑了。人可以非常在意目標，同時仍要吃晚餐、陪家人說話、好好睡覺。不論接下來如何，這些平常的事也是答案的一部分。
