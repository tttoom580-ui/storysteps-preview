A Message at Midnight

班級群組裡的陌生人 · 第 3 / 25 章 · 1000以下 · 英中對照 · 校訂中

At midnight Student Thirty-One posted a blurred picture of an old class notice. The classroom was full of ordinary signs of school life: bags under desks, notebooks on chairs, and sunlight across a wall. The unknown account felt strange precisely because the room was so familiar. Lin could name every classmate, yet the screen suggested there was one more. Noah looked at the official roster and then at the phone, careful not to treat either as an invitation to search private things. 'We should ask the school,' he said. Lin agreed. Finding the truth would matter less if they frightened innocent students along the way. Lin said the word all in a careful answer. A small poster on the wall included the word area. The class reading card used basketball in an example sentence. "We can begin when the others are ready," Lin suggested. "Can we define what success means here?" Lin asked.

午夜時「第三十一名學生」貼出一張模糊的舊班級公告。 教室裡都是平常的校園景象：書包在桌下、筆記本放在椅上，陽光照到牆面。陌生帳號之所以詭異，正因房間太熟悉。琳認得每位同學，螢幕卻顯示還有一個人。諾亞對照正式名冊與手機，沒有把它們當成可以搜查別人私物的理由。「先問學校吧。」他說。琳同意。若沿路嚇壞無辜同學，查到真相的意義也會變小。 琳仔細回答時說出了「全部」。牆上的小海報寫著區域這個字。課堂閱讀卡用籃球造了一個例句。「等其他人準備好，我們就能開始。」琳提議。「我們能先定義這裡的成功是什麼嗎？」琳問。

The date on the paper came from the previous school year, not this term. A question that had sounded easy in the morning now looked more complicated. Lin did not dislike that change; learning often made a simple picture less simple. Noah compared the new detail with what they had already seen, while Lin checked whether the two could really fit together. They discovered that looking carefully was different from looking for proof of a favorite idea. 'We can change our minds,' Noah said. Lin agreed. A good plan could grow as new facts arrived, and no one had to win an argument first. The class reading card used email in an example sentence. A small poster on the wall included the word eraser. Lin said the word few in a careful answer. A small poster on the wall included the word lamp. Lin remembered a drawing of leave seen earlier that week.

紙上日期來自上一學年，不是本學期。 早上聽來簡單的問題，如今變得複雜。琳並不討厭這種變化；學習常讓簡單畫面變得豐富。諾亞比對新細節和已觀察的事，琳確認兩者能否真的接上。他們發現仔細觀察和只找支持自己想法的證據不同。「我們可以改變想法。」諾亞說。琳同意。好計畫能隨新事實成長，不需要先爭個輸贏。 課堂閱讀卡用電子郵件造了一個例句。牆上的小海報寫著橡皮擦這個字。琳仔細回答時說出了「少數」。牆上的小海報寫著燈這個字。琳想起本週稍早看到的離開圖畫。

Lin asked her friend Noah to compare public notice boards in the daytime. The choice did not come with a promise of success. Lin knew it might take patience and that an honest attempt could still fail. Noah suggested checking the smallest part first, so they could stop safely if it did not work. They considered who should ask permission, who should keep the notes, and who would speak if a misunderstanding appeared. 'We do not have to do everything alone,' Lin said. Sharing the work made the decision less frightening, not because it was easier, but because everyone knew what they had agreed to do. There was less noise once everyone knew the plan. A small poster on the wall included the word mail. The day felt modern to Lin, so Noah asked what was wrong. Lin remembered a drawing of night seen earlier that week. Somebody had packed orange for a short break later.

琳請朋友諾亞白天再比對公開公告欄。 這個選擇沒有保證成功。琳知道它需要耐心，認真嘗試也可能失敗。諾亞建議先測試最小的部分，不合適就能安全停止。他們討論由誰詢問許可、誰保管紀錄、誤會發生時由誰說明。「不必什麼都自己做。」琳說。分工讓決定不再那麼可怕，不是因為事情變簡單，而是每個人都清楚自己答應什麼。 每個人了解計畫後，吵鬧聲少了。牆上的小海報寫著郵件這個字。琳覺得今天有些現代化的，諾亞便問怎麼了。琳想起本週稍早看到的夜晚圖畫。有人為稍後的休息準備了橘色。

They find the same picture in a school archive that many volunteers once used. A question remained, but the group was a little less afraid of asking it carefully. Lin wrote down which details were confirmed by the school and which were only words from an unknown account. Noah checked the list once more. They did not need to say every private name aloud to make progress. The strongest evidence was the kind that could be explained without harming somebody who had not chosen to speak. Lin closed the notebook and decided the class deserved both an answer and a chance to feel ordinary again. A small poster on the wall included the word plant. The meeting was set for 3 p.m. on the notice. Someone had asked Noah a question about quarter. The open book contained a short story about traffic. A notice near the entrance mentioned will.

他們在許多志工曾使用的校方資料夾找到相同照片。 問題仍在，班上卻比較不怕仔細提問了。琳記下哪些細節已由學校確認，哪些只是陌生帳號說過的話。諾亞再核對一次。他們不必把每個私人姓名說出來才算有進展。最有力的證據，是不傷害尚未願意說話的人也能說清楚的證據。琳合上筆記本，覺得全班既值得真相，也值得重新過平凡生活。 牆上的小海報寫著盆栽這個字。告示上寫著會議在下午三點舉行。有人曾問諾亞關於一刻鐘的問題。翻開的書有一則談到往來人流的小故事。入口附近的告示提到將會。

Later, Lin looked back at the four parts of the day: what happened, what they learned, what they decided, and what followed. The list was simple enough to fit on a little page, but it held much more than a score or a rumor. Noah read the page and pointed to a place where the order could be clearer. Together they fixed it. They also left a line for the next person to write a question. That made the record useful even when the people who started it were not there.

稍後，琳回想今天的四部分：發生了什麼、學到了什麼、做了什麼決定，以及結果如何。清單雖然只有小小一頁，卻比一個分數或流言包含更多內容。諾亞讀完後指出一處順序不清楚，兩人一起修改。他們還留下一行，讓之後接手的人能寫下問題。這樣，即使最初參與的人不在場，紀錄也依然有用。

When Lin turned the last page of the notes, one line stood out: people deserve the chance to explain their own choices. It was easy to forget that in a busy day, especially when everyone wanted a quick ending. Noah had reminded Lin more than once to keep an open mind. The lesson was not that people should wait forever. It was that acting with care usually made the next action safer and fairer. Lin closed the book and made a quiet plan to remember that tomorrow.

琳翻到筆記最後一頁，看見一行話特別醒目：每個人都應有機會解釋自己的選擇。忙碌時很容易忘記，尤其人人都想快點結束。諾亞不只一次提醒琳保持開放心態。這不是要大家永遠等待，而是說仔細行動通常會讓下一步更安全、更公平。琳合上本子，默默決定明天也要記住。
