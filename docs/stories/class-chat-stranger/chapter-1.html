第三十一名學生

Student Thirty-One

Lin opened the class chat and saw a new member called Student Thirty-One. Every phone in the room could show the same words, yet each student read them with a different fear. One remembered an old argument; another worried about a private drawing; someone else wondered if a joke had gone too far. Lin did not try to collect those worries. Noah suggested that people speak to a trusted adult in private if they needed help. That made the mystery harder to turn into a game, but it also made the classroom safer. Not every secret belonged to the class simply because a message claimed to know it. A tiny bug crossed the dry edge of the window. The camera on the counter stayed off until permission was given. One fact mattered: the group had to check before judging. Noah offered a friendly smile instead of a quick judgment. Lin wondered what a small choice today might mean for the future.

琳打開班級群組，看見多了一名叫「第三十一名學生」的成員。 教室裡每支手機都能看到同樣文字，每位學生卻帶著不同的害怕閱讀。有人想到以前的爭執，有人擔心私人的畫，也有人懷疑玩笑是否太過火。琳沒有去收集這些憂慮。諾亞建議需要幫助的人私下找可信任的成人。這樣謎團就不容易被當成遊戲，教室也更安全。不是每個祕密都因一則訊息聲稱知道，就屬於全班。 一隻小蟲爬過窗邊乾燥的角落。櫃檯上的相機保持關閉，等待取得拍攝同意。有一件事實很重要：大家必須查證後再判斷。諾亞給了個親切的微笑，沒有急著評斷。琳想知道今天的小選擇會如何影響未來。

Their class had only thirty students, and nobody knew the unfamiliar profile. The information was small enough to overlook. Lin nearly moved on, but Noah wanted to know what it meant for the people involved. They talked through two possibilities, then checked which parts could actually be supported. In the classroom, other things were happening at the same time; it would have been easy to mix them together. 'Let's keep these separate,' Lin said. One careful question led to another, and the situation became clearer without anyone needing to raise a voice or make a dramatic accusation. A glass of juice waited at the side of the table. Nobody wanted a lie to take the place of a clear answer. The middle of the page was left blank for a new idea. A little arrow on the map pointed north. Lin read the notice by the door before making a plan.

班上只有三十人，沒有人認識這個陌生帳號。 這項資訊很小，幾乎會被忽略。琳正想往下走，諾亞卻想知道它對相關的人代表什麼。他們討論兩種可能，再確認哪些有證據支持。classroom同時還發生其他事，很容易混淆。「先把它們分開。」琳說。仔細的一問接著一問，不必大聲說話或戲劇性指控，情況也漸漸清楚。 桌子一側放著一杯果汁。沒有人希望謊言取代清楚的答案。頁面的中間留白，準備寫下新點子。地圖上的小箭頭指向北方。琳先讀過門旁公告，才開始做計畫。

Lin saved a screenshot and asked the teacher to check the member list. The next step sounded small when Lin said it aloud. That was a good sign. A clear action was easier to try than a large promise that no one understood. Noah repeated the plan to make sure both had heard it in the same way. When one part sounded unfair, they changed the wording. The plan left space for a person to ask a question or take a break. Lin noticed a quiet sense of relief. The problem remained real, but it no longer needed to be faced through shouting, rushing, or guessing about another person's feelings. The other chair stayed free for anyone who needed it. A picture of a pet sleeping safely hung near the entrance. Someone mentioned a picnic planned for the next free afternoon. "We can reach that corner if we move carefully," Lin said. A red mark showed the part of the page still unfinished.

琳先截圖保留紀錄，再請老師查核成員名單。 琳把下一步說出口時，聽起來只是一件小事。這反而是好現象。明確行動比大家聽不懂的大承諾更容易嘗試。諾亞重複計畫，確認兩人理解相同。有一部分聽來不公平，他們便改了說法。計畫也讓人能提問和休息。琳感到些許輕鬆。問題依然存在，卻不必靠吵鬧、急躁或猜測別人的心思來面對。 另一張椅子留給有需要的人。入口附近掛著寵物安心睡覺的圖片。有人提到下一個空閒下午的野餐計畫。「只要小心移動，我們就能到那個角落。」琳說。紅色記號標出頁面尚未完成的部分。

The new member sent one line: I remember your secrets. The classroom door closed softly after the last meeting. Lin remembered how quickly one message could change a person's day. Noah had helped slow down the rush to guess, and the teacher had kept the official investigation where it belonged. Nothing about that was like a dramatic chase. Still, it protected something more important than the thrill of a twist: the idea that a student's private fear was not public property. Lin wanted the class to hold onto that lesson long after the strange account was gone. A small star sticker marked the page they wanted to keep. They checked the first step and then moved to the second. "This is what we can check," Lin explained. "Let us use the notes we already have," Noah suggested. A woman near the door asked a quiet question.

新成員只傳了一句：「我記得你們的祕密。」 最後一次討論後，教室門輕輕關上。琳想起一則訊息能多快改變一個人的一天。諾亞幫忙阻止匆忙猜測，老師也讓正式調查由該負責的人進行。這完全不像戲劇性的追逐，卻保護比反轉情節更重要的事：學生私人的害怕不是公共財產。琳希望即使怪帳號消失，全班也能記住這個道理。 他們想保存的那一頁貼著小星星貼紙。他們查好第一步，然後進入第二步。「這就是我們能查證的部分。」琳說明。「先用手邊已有的筆記吧。」諾亞提議。門邊一位女士輕聲提出問題。

After the busy part ended, Lin and Noah found a quiet minute. There were still things they wanted to say, yet neither felt a need to win the conversation. Lin asked what had been most difficult. Noah answered honestly and then asked the same question back. They were surprised to hear two different answers. That did not make either one wrong. The talk helped them see that working together did not mean thinking exactly alike. It meant taking those differences seriously enough to make a plan both could understand.

忙碌告一段落後，琳和諾亞有了片刻安靜。他們還有話想說，卻不必在對話中爭贏。琳問最困難的是哪部分。諾亞誠實回答，也反問同一個問題。兩人的答案竟然不同，卻不代表誰錯了。這場談話讓他們理解，合作不必想法完全一致，而是認真看待差異，做出雙方都能理解的計畫。

As the lights outside grew softer, Lin remembered the feeling at the start of the chapter's trouble. It had seemed larger before anyone could name it. Now it had a shape, a few facts, and a next step. That did not make it harmless, but it made it possible to face. Noah had helped without taking the whole choice away. Lin hoped to remember that balance. It was a kind of strength that did not make a loud sound, and it might be needed again very soon.

窗外的光漸漸柔和，琳想起麻煩剛開始時的感受。那時在還沒說清楚前，事情看起來特別巨大。如今它有了形狀、幾項事實和下一步。問題不一定變得無害，卻能面對了。諾亞提供幫助，卻沒有奪走琳的選擇權。琳想記住這種平衡。這份力量不需要大聲，也許很快又會派上用場。
