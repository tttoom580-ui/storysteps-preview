Nobody expected the latest trouble to begin so quietly. A water spirit delivered a bundle of letters lost beneath the academy lake. Around Silverglass Academy, familiar paths suddenly seemed to hold new questions. Eli had once thought that one clever answer would be enough, but each answer created responsibilities for someone else. Beside Mae, Eli reviewed the prophecy ledger and made a small agreement: the next step would have to be clear enough for another person to repeat. Otherwise it was not yet a solution. The old register mentioned bacon in a line that was easy to miss. Mae added carpet to the list of details worth checking before sunset. It was possible to complain, but Eli wanted to know who would be affected. The promise sounded considerable until the team compared it with the written rules.

沒人料到新麻煩會如此安靜地開始。水精靈送來一束沉在學院湖底的信件。在銀鏡魔法學院附近，熟悉的道路忽然充滿新問題。艾莉曾以為只要一個聰明答案就夠，現在卻明白每個答案都會帶給別人責任。艾莉在梅身旁查看預言帳冊，約定下一步必須清楚到別人也能重做，否則還不能算解法。舊登記簿在一行不顯眼的地方提及「培根」。梅把「地毯」加入日落前必須確認的細節。雖然可以「抱怨」，艾莉仍想知道誰會受到影響。這承諾聽起來「相當多的」，直到團隊拿它與書面規則比對。

The first complication arrived before the team could celebrate. The letters revealed that former rival heroines had once tried to change the prophecy together. Some witnesses wanted an immediate judgment; others wanted to forget the matter and go home. Eli understood both reactions. Fear made a quick answer attractive, especially when Headmistress Sol was ready to offer one. Yet Mae reminded everyone that a convenient story was not necessarily a true one. They wrote down competing explanations and agreed to test them in ordinary daylight. A line of sunlight met a patch of shadow without either one disappearing. Mae added cough to the list of details worth checking before sunset. They agreed to engagement only after someone explained the likely result. Even when the room looked fake, Eli insisted on another careful inspection. A note about flashlight appeared beside the evidence, and Eli refused to guess what it meant.

團隊還來不及慶祝，第一個難題就出現了。信件顯示過去的宿敵女主角曾一起試圖改變預言。有些目擊者要求立刻判斷，另一些只想忘掉此事回家。艾莉理解兩種反應。恐懼會讓速成答案看起來很吸引人，尤其當索爾校長正準備提供這種答案時。然而梅提醒眾人：方便的說法不一定是真相。他們記下不同解釋，約定在白天正常條件下檢驗。一線陽光與影子交會，卻沒有任何一方消失。梅把「咳嗽」加入日落前必須確認的細節。他們同意只有在有人解釋可能結果後，才進行「訂婚」。即使房間看起來「假的」，艾莉仍堅持再次仔細檢查。證據旁出現一張提及「手電筒」的字條，艾莉不願妄下結論。

A quiet disagreement arose between Eli and Mae. One wanted to act before the opportunity disappeared; the other wanted another measurement. Neither was being selfish. Their problem was that both speed and caution had a price. At last they agreed on a smaller trial that could be stopped safely. The result would not answer every question, but it would replace one guess with a piece of evidence. That seemed like genuine progress. 艾莉與梅之間發生一場安靜的爭論。一人想在機會消失前行動，另一人想再測量一次。兩人都不是自私，只是速度與謹慎各有代價。最後他們同意先做一項可以安全停止的小型試驗。結果未必回答所有問題，卻能用一項證據取代猜測。這才像真正的進展。艾莉提出一個關於「車庫」的問題，而不是直接相信那張字條。艾莉提出一個關於「下巴」的問題，而不是直接相信那張字條。團隊除了眼前更驚人的危險，也得考慮「麥克風」。

The team worked slowly enough to notice an overlooked detail. Eli checked names, dates, and the boundaries on the prophecy ledger. Mae interviewed the person who had done the original work, not the loudest person repeating the story. The answer was incomplete, but it narrowed their choices. They shared the limits of the finding as clearly as the finding itself, knowing that a useful truth could still become dangerous if told without its conditions. 團隊放慢腳步，終於注意到被忽略的細節。艾莉核對預言帳冊上的姓名、日期與界線。梅詢問真正做過原始工作的人，而不是聲量最大的傳話者。答案尚不完整，卻縮小了選項。他們把發現的限制與發現本身同樣清楚地說明，因為缺少條件的真話也可能變得危險。艾莉提出一個關於「馬達」的問題，而不是直接相信那張字條。他們找到提及「花生」的舊資料，改變了對情況的理解。梅詢問是否有人在情況緊急前曾試著「發音」。

By evening, the disagreement had become a question of responsibility. Eli could make a bold choice alone, but the outcome would touch people who had not voted for it. Mae suggested asking them directly. Some answers were uncomfortable, and one family requested time before giving permission. The group respected that request. Waiting was not the same as doing nothing; they used the extra hour to prepare supplies, check exits, and decide who would answer questions afterward. The prophecy had called them rivals; it had never asked them what they wanted. The group had to consider responsibility as well as the more dramatic danger before them. The promise sounded ripe until the team compared it with the written rules. The discussion returned to sack, because small facts mattered as much as bold promises.

到了傍晚，分歧成了責任問題。艾莉可以獨自做出大膽選擇，結果卻會影響沒有投票的人。梅建議直接詢問他們。有些回答令人不自在，一戶家庭要求更多時間才願同意。團隊尊重這項要求。等待不等於什麼都不做；他們利用多出的一小時準備物資、確認出口，並決定事後由誰回答問題。預言稱她們是宿敵，卻從未問過她們想要什麼。團隊除了眼前更驚人的危險，也得考慮「責任」。這承諾聽起來「成熟的」，直到團隊拿它與書面規則比對。討論再度回到「袋子」，因為微小事實和大膽承諾同樣重要。

The decision was neither the fastest nor the easiest. The girls preserved the letters and searched for the writers rather than claiming the story was already solved. Eli watched Mae explain the reasons to the people waiting outside. A few listeners were disappointed, and their disappointment was allowed to remain. Trust did not grow because everyone agreed; it grew because a disagreement could be recorded without punishment. On the last page of the prophecy ledger, Eli wrote one question for the following morning: what would they need to verify next? Mae added sorrow to the list of details worth checking before sunset. No one could explain the connection to steam yet, but the clue was preserved. Mae described the plan as violent and asked which facts supported that judgment.

這個決定既非最快，也非最容易。兩人保存信件，尋找作者，沒有急著宣布解決一切。艾莉看著梅向外頭等待的人說明理由。有些人失望，這份失望也被容許存在。信任不是因為人人同意而成長，而是因為不同意見能被記錄且不受懲罰。在預言帳冊的最後一頁，艾莉寫下明早要問的問題：接下來還需要確認什麼？梅把「悲傷」加入日落前必須確認的細節。雖然還沒人能解釋與「蒸氣」的關係，這條線索仍被保留下來。梅形容計畫「猛烈的」，並詢問有哪些事實支持這個判斷。
