A new day began in Silverglass Academy, but nobody could call it ordinary. Eli invited Mae to spend an afternoon in town without using spells or discussing the prophecy. Eli examined the prophecy ledger before trusting the first explanation. There was a difference between being brave and making other people carry the cost of a quick decision. Mae arrived with a practical question: who would check the result if the plan went wrong? Their answer began with careful notes rather than a dramatic speech. The written instruction used aside, and Mae checked exactly what it changed. The discussion returned to bubble, because small facts mattered as much as bold promises. A note about canyon appeared beside the evidence, and Eli refused to guess what it meant. For a moment, the answer seemed capable, but a second check told a more complex story.

銀鏡魔法學院迎來新的一天，卻沒有人覺得平凡。艾莉邀梅到城裡度過一個不用魔法、也不談預言的午後。艾莉先查看預言帳冊，沒有立刻相信第一種解釋。勇敢與讓別人承擔草率決定的代價，是兩件不同的事。梅帶來一個務實的問題：如果計畫出錯，誰來檢查結果？他們從仔細記錄開始，而不是從激昂演說開始。書面指示用了「在一旁」這個詞，梅仔細確認它改變了什麼。討論再度回到「氣泡」，因為微小事實和大膽承諾同樣重要。證據旁出現一張提及「峽谷」的字條，艾莉不願妄下結論。有一瞬間，答案看似「有能力的」，再次檢查卻顯示事情更複雜。

There was an obstacle the cheerful announcement had left out. Mae admitted that she barely knew what she enjoyed beyond meeting other people's expectations. Headmistress Sol insisted that a powerful person should settle the dispute at once. Eli asked a simpler question: what would happen to those without power? Mae drew two columns on a sheet, one for verified facts and one for guesses. The second column was longer. Nobody liked admitting uncertainty, but the list showed exactly where their next hour of work belonged. The prophecy had called them rivals; it had never asked them what they wanted. Eli asked a quiet question about captain rather than assuming the note was correct. The group had to consider client as well as the more dramatic danger before them. They agreed to edit only after someone explained the likely result. For a moment, the answer seemed elderly, but a second check told a more complex story.

歡樂的公告漏掉了一項障礙。梅承認自己幾乎不知道，除了符合他人期待之外還喜歡什麼。索爾校長堅稱應由有權勢者立即解決爭議。艾莉提出更簡單的問題：沒有權力的人會怎樣？梅在紙上畫兩欄，一欄記已驗證事實，一欄記猜測。第二欄更長。沒有人喜歡承認不確定，但清單清楚指出接下來一小時該做什麼。預言稱她們是宿敵，卻從未問過她們想要什麼。艾莉提出一個關於「隊長」的問題，而不是直接相信那張字條。團隊除了眼前更驚人的危險，也得考慮「客戶」。他們同意只有在有人解釋可能結果後，才進行「編輯、從紀錄中刪除」。有一瞬間，答案看似「年長的」，再次檢查卻顯示事情更複雜。

Not everyone agreed with the plan. A younger observer asked why anyone should trust Eli when the story had already gone wrong once. The question stung, but it was fair. Eli described the earlier mistake without blaming a convenient stranger. Mae added what they had changed and which risk remained. By the end of the conversation, nobody promised perfection. They had something better: a way to notice the next problem while there was still time to respond. Eli asked a quiet question about electricity rather than assuming the note was correct. 不是所有人都同意計畫。年輕旁觀者問：事情已經出過錯，憑什麼還相信艾莉？這問題刺耳，卻很公平。艾莉說明早先的錯誤，沒有找方便的陌生人當替罪羊。梅補充已改變的做法與仍存在的風險。談話結束時，沒人保證完美；但大家有了更好的事物：及早發現下一個問題的方法。艾莉提出一個關於「電力」的問題，而不是直接相信那張字條。雖然還沒人能解釋與「閃光」的關係，這條線索仍被保留下來。艾莉提出一個關於「艙口」的問題，而不是直接相信那張字條。

They returned to the evidence instead of arguing about intentions. Eli compared the latest observation with the prophecy ledger, while Mae asked who had recorded each detail. The difference between two accounts was small enough to miss and important enough to change the plan. Headmistress Sol preferred a public announcement, but the team chose a private verification first. They did not have to admire each other to share a correct measurement, and they did not have to agree on everything to prevent harm. For a moment, the answer seemed hopeful, but a second check told a more complex story. They found an old reference to information that changed their understanding of the situation. The promise sounded monthly until the team compared it with the written rules.

他們回到證據，不再爭論動機。艾莉把最新觀察與預言帳冊比對，梅則追問每個細節由誰記錄。兩份說法的差異小得容易漏看，卻重要到足以改變計畫。索爾校長偏好公開宣告，團隊卻選擇先私下查證。他們不必彼此欣賞也能共享正確測量，不必事事同意也能避免傷害。有一瞬間，答案看似「有希望的」，再次檢查卻顯示事情更複雜。他們找到提及「資訊」的舊資料，改變了對情況的理解。這承諾聽起來「每月的」，直到團隊拿它與書面規則比對。

The plan improved when someone ordinary pointed out an obvious problem. Eli felt embarrassed for missing it, then thanked the speaker. Mae rewrote the instructions in language a newcomer could understand. Headmistress Sol asked whether such details were worth the delay. The group answered by demonstrating the failure that the simpler instructions prevented. Their progress was not measured by applause. It was measured by how well the next person could use what they had learned. A line of sunlight met a patch of shadow without either one disappearing. The old register mentioned property in a line that was easy to miss. They agreed to represent only after someone explained the likely result. The discussion returned to situation, because small facts mattered as much as bold promises.

一位平凡的人指出明顯問題後，計畫反而更好。艾莉因漏看而尷尬，仍謝謝對方。梅用新手能理解的文字重寫指示。索爾校長問這些細節是否值得耽誤時間。團隊示範新指示能避免的失敗，以行動回答。他們的進展不靠掌聲衡量，而看下一個人能否使用學到的方法。一線陽光與影子交會，卻沒有任何一方消失。舊登記簿在一行不顯眼的地方提及「財產／產權標記」。他們同意只有在有人解釋可能結果後，才進行「代表」。討論再度回到「情況」，因為微小事實和大膽承諾同樣重要。

A small result finally stood in front of them. They shared tea, visited a small bookstore, and decided to make room for ordinary happiness. There was no perfect reward and no voice from the sky promising that everything would now become easy. Eli and Mae checked the practical details once more, then let the people most affected decide how the result would be used. Even Headmistress Sol had to acknowledge that the process was visible. It was enough for one day. The larger problem still waited, but no longer in quite the same shape. No one could explain the connection to spice yet, but the clue was preserved. Eli asked a quiet question about sword rather than assuming the note was correct. Mae asked if anyone had tried to weep before the situation became urgent.

眼前終於有了小小成果。她們喝茶、逛小書店，決定為普通的快樂留位置。沒有完美獎賞，也沒有天音保證從此萬事輕鬆。艾莉與梅再次檢查實務細節，然後讓最受影響的人決定如何使用成果。連索爾校長也得承認過程透明。今天這樣已經足夠。更大的問題還在，但形狀已不完全相同。雖然還沒人能解釋與「香料」的關係，這條線索仍被保留下來。艾莉提出一個關於「長劍」的問題，而不是直接相信那張字條。梅詢問是否有人在情況緊急前曾試著「哭泣」。
