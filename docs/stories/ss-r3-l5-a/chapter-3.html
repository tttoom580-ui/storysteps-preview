有兩個名字的證人

The Witness with Two Names

Oren chose a table facing the ferry ramp, where he could see the departure board without turning his head. He put two statements down and covered their signatures with his broad fingers. 'You want the names first,' he said. Aya asked for the dates instead. Darien moved the tea away from the papers, and Oren began describing a night crossing by the length of the tide. Aya asked which part he had seen from his boat and which part another person had told him. He uncovered the first signature slowly. They examined a witness statement signed with two aliases on a clean table, with a witness close enough to see every movement.

奧倫選了面向渡輪坡道的桌子，不必轉頭就能看見班次表。他放下兩份證詞，用寬厚手指遮住簽名。『妳先想要名字吧。』雅卻先問日期。戴倫把茶移開，奧倫依潮水變化描述一次夜間航程。雅詢問哪些是他從船上親眼看見，哪些出自別人轉述。他慢慢露出第一個簽名。 他們在乾淨桌面上查看使用兩個化名簽署的證詞，讓見證人能看見所有動作。

The witness, a beloved boatmaker named Oren, met them beneath a faded sign. He was cautious after decades of aggression along the border. An attorney had once told him that a biological sample could diminish doubt, but no laboratory had kept one. Aya was bound by a narrower scope: confirm what Oren saw, not what people imagined. A security executive had made refugees into easy prey and added layer after layer of rumor. Darien tried to straighten the witness's old map while a ferry shuttle waited. The strap on Oren's bag had broken, and a brass clock gave one final tick. Oren could only shrug when Aya asked whether the courier was a double agent. He preferred realism to heroic stories. Aya respected his viewpoint: she would not specialize in inventing certainty, nor accept a virtual reconstruction as proof of a physical journey. When Oren finally pointed to the clinic's transport code, she understood why the same signature appeared twice: one name could protect a messenger, while another could conceal the person who gave the order.

深受居民敬愛的造船師奧倫在褪色招牌下與他們見面。他歷經邊境數十年的衝突，因此格外謹慎。律師曾告訴他生物樣本可能減少疑點，但實驗室並未留下樣本。雅能確認的範圍更有限：只證實奧倫所見，不把想像當事實。保安高層曾把難民當成獵物，謠言一層層堆上去。渡輪接駁船等待時，戴倫努力攤平老地圖；奧倫提袋的背帶斷了，銅鐘也敲過最後一下。被問到信差是否為雙面間諜，他只能聳肩。他重視現實，不喜歡英雄傳說。雅尊重他的觀點，不以虛擬重建取代實際路程的證據。當奧倫指向診所運輸編碼，她終於理解兩個簽名可能分別保護送信者與掩蓋下令者。

Outside, the immediate danger continued: a security executive had ordered the terminal closed. Aya finally settled on a concrete course: to ask the witness about the dates before accusing anyone. A worker dragged a chain across the ramp while they were still comparing the statements. The security executive's closure order meant Oren would soon lose his last ferry home. Aya turned the notebook so he could read her summary and asked him to correct it before leaving. He struck out 'arms' and wrote 'medical crates.' Darien asked whether the boxes had been opened. Oren shook his head and drew the marking he had seen on their sides. Aya kept the drawing beside his words rather than replacing his description with a more certain one.

外頭的危險還在持續：保安主管下令關閉碼頭。 雅最後選擇一條具體做法：先詢問證人日期，不急著指控任何人。 比對尚未結束，工人已拉起封鎖坡道的鐵鍊。保安主管的關閉命令讓奧倫快要趕不上最後一班船。雅轉過筆記，請他離開前修正摘要。他劃掉『軍火』，寫下『醫療箱』。戴倫問是否打開過，奧倫搖頭，畫出箱側標記。雅把圖留在他的敘述旁，沒有改寫成更肯定的說法。

The immediate outcome was real, although incomplete: the witness identified a concealed medical shipment. The code Oren drew corresponded to a clinic shipment. Beneath it, he added a depot name and an arrow showing where the crates had been transferred. Aya copied those details into a separate travel note, leaving his aliases on the protected statement. At the chain, Oren stopped and asked whether she would print his story as a confession. 'You described what you saw,' she answered. He nodded, took his repaired bag from Darien, and stepped aboard. Aya watched until the ferry cleared the terminal, then unfolded the road map toward the freight depot.

目前的成果確實存在，雖然還不完整：證人指出一批隱藏的醫療物資。 奧倫畫的代碼對應診所貨運。他又加上貨站名稱與表示轉運方向的箭頭。雅把這些另抄成行程筆記，化名仍留在受保護的證詞內。走到鐵鍊前，奧倫問她會不會把故事當成自白刊登。『你描述的是你看見的事。』她答。他點頭，接過戴倫修好的提袋登船。雅等渡輪離港後，才攤開通往貨站的地圖。
