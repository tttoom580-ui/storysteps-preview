儀器室

The Instrument Room

驛站密信：兩枚印章之下 · 第 8 / 25 章 · 4000–5000 · 英中對照 · 校訂中

A demolition notice had been pasted across the annex door. Four days remained. The caretaker insisted the sensor could not be moved without destroying its alignment, while Darien thought they should carry it out before the builders arrived. Aya asked the technician to show them what could be measured in place. She laid the patent notebook on a dry bench and drew a boundary around the unsafe sockets. They would need light, a reference clock, and someone able to distinguish an actual measurement from a machine repeating its last setting. The object at the center of their visit was a technical notebook containing an unusual equipment patent. The technician disconnected the damaged supply cable and brought a portable lamp before anyone was allowed to approach the exposed machine casing.

附樓門上貼著拆除告示，只剩四天。看守人堅稱搬動感測器會破壞校準，戴倫卻想搶在工人前搬走。雅請技師示範哪些能原地測量，把專利筆記放在乾燥工作臺，圈出危險插座。他們需要照明、參照時鐘，也需要能分辨真測量與重播舊設定的人。 這趟查訪的核心物件是記載奇特設備專利的技術筆記。技師先拔掉損壞電源線，拿來攜帶燈，才讓任何人靠近裸露機殼。

The room smelled of dust and a commodity once used to clean lenses. Aya approached with caution because the place was full of electrical hazards, not because she believed its dreadful superstition. A caretaker had treated every ordinary chore as a secret ritual. The technician, an undergraduate then, had dedicated years to developing fluency with the old machines. She now offered clarity: the patent described a rotating sensor, and the premium paid for its parts had been perfectly legal. An official delegation had inspected the interior, yet its report confused a cracked shed with a secure chamber. No one was the sole owner of the instrument; its makers had shared the design. Darien learned to steer its manual dial while Aya wrote down the venue and timing of each measurement. The results might generate a new line of exploration, but that did not mean every recorded number was ethical to publish. Some values belonged to patients. Aya preserved the machine's clock reading and kept the personal records closed.

儀器室瀰漫灰塵與舊清潔藥劑的味道。雅保持警覺，是因為電氣危險，而非相信恐怖迷信。看守人把日常雜務說成神祕儀式。技師當年仍是大學生，花了多年熟悉老機器，如今能清楚說明：專利涉及旋轉感測器，零件溢價雖高卻合法。官方代表團檢查過內部，報告卻把裂開的棚屋誤寫成安全室。儀器並非某個人的獨家財產，設計由多人共享。戴倫學著轉動手動旋鈕，雅則記下每筆測量的地點和時間。數字能開啟新探索，不代表全部適合公開；其中一部分屬於病患。雅保存機器時間紀錄，繼續封存個資。

The annex was scheduled for demolition in four days. The technician asked Darien to turn the dial one mark at a time. At the third mark, a strip of old paper advanced beneath the glass. Aya read its time against the maintenance entry in the notebook. 'Does that prove departure?' she asked. The technician showed her the separate mark made when a loaded vehicle crossed the sensor. Only that mark counted. They repeated the reading without touching the patient file stored underneath. Darien's first sketch had joined the two records; he tore it up and drew their separate paths correctly.

壓力並非幻想：附樓預定四天後拆除。 技師請戴倫每次把旋鈕轉一格。第三格時，玻璃下舊紙帶向前移動。雅將時間與筆記維護紀錄比較，問是否能證明離站。技師指出裝載車輛通過時另有記號，只有那個才算。他們再讀一次，沒有碰下面的病患檔。戴倫第一張圖把兩種紀錄連在一起，於是撕掉重畫分別路徑。

By evening, they could state one result with confidence: an old sensor logged the missing shipment's departure time. The departure time survived the second reading. It placed the shipment on the road while an official summary claimed it was still awaiting permission. Aya asked the technician to sign only that comparison, leaving ownership of the equipment outside her statement. On a cabinet near the exit, Darien noticed an old courier number identical to one in the transport papers. A burial reference followed it. The caretaker knew the hillside cemetery and offered directions, but Aya first copied the demolition notice so the instrument would not vanish while they followed the new lead.

傍晚，他們能有把握地寫下一項結果：老感測器記下失蹤貨物離站時間。 離站時間經第二次讀取仍相同。官方摘要聲稱貨物仍在等許可，實際上已上路。雅請技師只簽這項比較，不把設備所有權混進證詞。出口櫃子上，戴倫注意到與運輸文件相同的信差號，後面跟著安葬索引。看守人知道山坡墓園，願意指路，但雅先抄下拆除告示，免得追查新線索時儀器已消失。
