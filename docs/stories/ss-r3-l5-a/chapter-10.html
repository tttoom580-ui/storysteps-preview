The Clinic's Missing Page

驛站密信：兩枚印章之下 · 第 10 / 25 章 · 4000–5000 · 英中對照 · 校訂中

用互動版查詞、朗讀與收藏

Aya met the independent physician beneath the hospital's glass dome, where visitors queued for appointments. He asked her not to spread the anonymous note on a public desk. She folded it inward and showed him only the budget references. The transport certificate listed a quantity the clinic should have received, while the adjacent building appeared in a mortgage entry. Darien asked whether the figures could describe a secret treatment program. The physician sent for a supply clerk before answering. He would explain the medical abbreviations, but inventory movements belonged to someone else's records. Before discussing a confidential clinic budget and a certificate of transport, Aya invited its keeper to describe the ordinary process of filing.

雅在醫院玻璃圓頂下見到獨立醫師，旁邊都是掛號人潮。他請她別把匿名信攤在公桌，她往內折起，只出示預算索引。運輸證明列出診所應收數量，鄰棟卻出現在抵押條目。戴倫問是否可能是祕密治療計畫，醫師先找物資員再回答。他能說明醫療縮寫，庫存流動則屬另一套紀錄。 談到診所保密預算與運輸證明前，雅先請保管人說明平常歸檔流程。

An anonymous note accused the hospital of an illegal abortion program. Aya would not betray the women named in it by publishing a rumor. The director was arrogant, yet his manner did not establish guilt. An exhausted butcher from the night market offered to testify about a late delivery; he recalled a bronze badge, not a patient's name. A physician explained that genetics and the movement of a molecule were scientific fields, not evidence of a government conspiracy. The hospital had tried to integrate records across its emergency sector after a flood. Its initiative was practical, although the contract's credibility remained in doubt. A mortgage on the clinic's adjacent building was ridiculous for a publicly owned site, and a vertical storage chart showed medicines vanishing before their scheduled arrival. The river's wildlife had suffered that winter as well, but the physician would not invent a link. His testimony could sustain a limited finding: public supplies had gone elsewhere. The exact destination still required financial records.

匿名紙條指控醫院實施非法墮胎計畫。雅不願公開未證實的指控，進而背叛信中被點名的女性。主任傲慢，但態度不能證明有罪。一位夜市屠夫願作證，記得深夜貨運員的銅徽章，卻不認識病人。醫師解釋遺傳學與分子運作是科學領域，不是政府陰謀的證據。水災後醫院試圖整合急診紀錄，這項倡議有實際用途，但合約可信度仍可疑。市有建物竟背負抵押借款，垂直倉儲圖也顯示藥品在預定抵達前消失。當年河道野生動物同樣受害，但醫師不願硬說相關。證詞只足以支持一項有限結論：公有物資流向別處；確切去處仍須查財務紀錄。

Their immediate difficulty was clear: an influential entrepreneur tried to purchase the hospital archive. The choice Aya made was to ask an independent physician what the medical entries actually meant. The entrepreneur's representative arrived with a purchase proposal for the archive. Aya asked the hospital administrator whether the patients had been told their files were included. The administrator removed one attachment from the proposal and went to consult his supervisor. While they waited, the supply clerk matched dispatch numbers to invoices from a private company. No patient's diagnosis was needed for that comparison. Aya asked the physician to mark precisely which claims in the anonymous note the budget could not establish. Darien watched him cross out the most dramatic sentence without hesitation.

眼前的難題很明確：有影響力的企業家試圖買下醫院檔案。 雅決定請獨立醫師說明醫療欄位的真正意義。 企業家代表帶著購買檔案的提案抵達。雅問院方是否告知病患，其檔案也在交易內。行政員抽出一份附件去找主管。等待時，物資員將出貨號與私人公司發票配對，這不需病患診斷。雅請醫師標明預算無法證實匿名信哪些說法。戴倫看著他毫不猶豫劃掉最聳動的一句。

They ended the visit with a limited finding: the records showed medicine diverted to a private company, not a secret experiment. The records supported diverted medicine, not the experiment described in the note. Aya wrote both halves of that finding because omitting the second would leave the rumor alive. The butcher recognized a bronze token among the transport papers and described where he had seen its badge before. Darien compared its shape with a courier-service illustration, finding a useful lead rather than an identification of a person. They requested the token's registration history. Aya left the protected medical pages with the physician and carried only the numbered financial copies toward the records court.

查訪以一項有限發現告終：紀錄顯示藥品轉入私人公司，而非祕密實驗。 紀錄支持藥品被轉運，不支持匿名信所述實驗。雅把兩者都寫下，否則謠言仍會留下。肉販認出運輸文件裡的青銅牌，描述見過徽章的地方。戴倫和信差制度圖比對形狀，得到線索，沒有據此認定人。他們申請牌子登記沿革。雅把受保護病歷留給醫師，只帶編號財務副本前往紀錄法院。
