邊境之外的職員

A Clerk Beyond the Frontier

驛站密信：兩枚印章之下 · 第 6 / 25 章 · 4000–5000 · 英中對照 · 校訂中

用互動版查詞、朗讀與收藏

The clerk changed their meeting place twice before settling on a borrowed seminar room beyond the checkpoint. Aya arrived early enough to see the neutral observer unlock it. She left the visitor sheet blank where it asked for the witness's home address. Darien waited by the service road with the route map visible on his knees, so nobody could mistake his notebook for a concealed weapon. When the clerk entered, he kept his briefcase pressed against his chest until the observer explained who would hold each copy of his statement. They examined a customs register hidden in a clerk's briefcase on a clean table, with a witness close enough to see every movement.

職員兩度更改地點，最後選定檢查站外借用的研討室。雅提早抵達，看著中立見證人開門。訪客表上要求住址的欄位，她留白。戴倫在勤務道路旁等候，將路線圖攤在膝上，免得筆記被誤認為藏匿武器。職員進門時仍抱緊公事包，直到見證人說明每份證詞副本會交給誰。 他們在乾淨桌面上查看藏在職員公事包裡的海關登記簿，讓見證人能看見所有動作。

An abrupt warning reached Aya while she was crossing a pedestrian bridge: the clerk had escaped into exile. An activist offered help, but the message could have been a trap. Aya took a briefcase containing blank forms and spent the journey trying to contemplate what duty required. Darien warned that a hostile officer might disrupt any meeting. “A government is an entity,” he said, “not a single person with a single mind.” The clerk had once supervised injections at a frontier clinic and knew how easily an innocent label could frighten the naive. His masculine voice shook when he described an obligation to repay the neighbors who hid him. They met near a broken electrical outlet, where surplus supplies had been stacked for a seminar. Aya promised not to disclose his new address. The clerk rolled up his trousers to reveal an old injury on his thigh. He would describe the register, but he would not return to the checkpoint. Aya accepted that limit. A frightened witness could still make a brave, careful choice.

雅走過人行橋時收到急報：職員已逃往境外。一名行動者願意幫忙，但消息可能是陷阱。她帶著裝滿空白表格的公事包上路，思索責任何在。戴倫說敵意軍官可能破壞會面。「政府是一個組織，」他說，「不是只有一個念頭的人。」職員曾管理邊境診所的注射作業，知道天真的人多容易被標籤嚇到。他以顫抖的低沉嗓音談到報答收留他的鄰居。他們在一處損壞的插座旁見面，附近堆著研討會剩餘物資。雅保證不洩漏新住址。職員捲起褲管，露出大腿舊傷；他願意解釋登記簿，卻不願回檢查站。雅接受這條界線。害怕的證人仍能做出勇敢而審慎的選擇。

Outside, the immediate danger continued: the clerk had received threats from an unidentified officer. Aya finally settled on a concrete course: to meet the clerk with a neutral observer rather than in secret. He opened the register at a page repaired with brown tape. Aya read out one cargo number and waited while he found its second appearance. The dates differed; the weight did not. Darien asked whether a clerk might simply have entered the shipment twice. The witness pointed to two inspection signatures from opposite sides of the frontier. He could identify the handwriting but would not repeat the threatening officer's name without seeing the original message. Aya recorded that condition and asked the observer to arrange a secure way for him to provide it later.

外頭的危險還在持續：職員受到不明軍官的威脅。 雅最後選擇一條具體做法：由中立見證人陪同會見，而不私下接觸。 他翻開一頁用褐色膠帶修補的登記簿。雅念出貨號，等他找出第二筆。日期不同，重量相同。戴倫問是否只是重複登錄，職員指出邊境兩側各有檢驗簽名。他認得筆跡，卻表示未見威脅訊息原件前，不願說出軍官姓名。雅記下條件，請見證人安排日後安全提供原件的方法。

The immediate outcome was real, although incomplete: the register showed the same shipment crossing the border twice. At the end, the clerk asked Aya to return the register to its case before he stood up. He wanted to carry it out himself. She placed her copies beside the observer's and counted every page aloud. The repeated shipment had a storage reference at Salt Quay, a place Darien knew from his courier years. Outside, he started to explain an old shortcut, then saw the clerk flinch at the sound of an approaching vehicle. They waited inside until it passed. Only after the witness had left safely did Aya ask Darien to draw the warehouse road.

目前的成果確實存在，雖然還不完整：登記簿顯示同一批貨跨境兩次。 結束時，職員請雅先把簿子放回箱中，才願起身。他想親自帶走。雅將自己的副本放在見證人那份旁，逐頁出聲清點。重複貨運有鹽碼頭儲藏索引，戴倫送信時熟悉那裡。門外他剛想介紹捷徑，卻看到職員因車聲畏縮，於是先留在室內等車離去。證人安全離開後，雅才請戴倫畫出倉庫道路。
