抽屜裡的請願書

The Petition in the Drawer

The petition arrived in court with a frayed string around its signatures. Several former postal workers had signed in pencil, afraid ink might make the document impossible to withdraw. The filing clerk asked Aya to replace those pages. She requested the rule requiring that change, then offered to let each worker confirm the signature before an examiner. Darien spotted his former colleague waiting at the rear door and brought him a chair. This was not the hearing either man had imagined when they first put on postal uniforms; today they were asking permission to inspect the system they had served. The object at the center of their visit was a signed petition from former postal workers.

請願書用磨損的繩子綁著簽名頁送達法院。幾名前郵政工人用鉛筆簽，怕墨水讓文件無法撤回。書記官請雅換頁，她索取依據，提議由每個工人在審查員前確認簽名。戴倫看見前同事等在後門，替他拿椅子。兩人穿上郵政制服時從沒想像過，今天會站在這裡，請求檢視曾服務的制度。 這趟查訪的核心物件是前郵政工人簽署的請願書。

Aya asked the clerk to accelerate the search, but he warned her that fatigue would not make an institution more accurate. A veteran judge offered a compliment for her careful dates, not for her suspicions. The main component of the filing was a petition demanding that the government deploy a neutral examiner. In the grim courtroom, a small child began to quiver as people argued about who had the greatest stake in the old conflict. Aya explained that a specimen was not necessarily proof unless its history could be traced. The recommendation sounded trivial to some spectators, whose specialty was loud certainty. Yet a medical officer said he would undergo a new review if the court required it. A realm of official secrecy had once prevented ordinary communities from learning to thrive. The present task was more modest: discover whether one sample had ever been registered. When the judge ordered a minimal search, Aya accepted the narrow victory. A serious investigation did not need to win everything in a single afternoon.

雅請求加快查找，書記官提醒她疲勞不會讓機關更準確。資深法官稱讚她日期記得仔細，卻沒替猜測背書。請願書的主要部分要求政府派出中立審查員。陰沉法庭裡，大人爭論舊衝突的利害關係，一名孩子聽得發抖。雅指出，若不能追溯保存過程，檢體也不一定是證據。有些旁聽者覺得這建議瑣碎，他們擅長的只是高聲斷言。醫官則願依法接受重新檢查。過往官方保密讓許多社區無法發展，現在的任務卻比較具體：確認樣本曾否登記。法官下令進行最低限度搜索，雅接受這項有限的勝利。嚴肅調查不必一個下午贏下所有事情。

The court planned to close the petition for lack of witnesses. The court intended to close the petition for lack of witnesses. Aya placed the hospital's numbered copies beside the petition and asked for a search of the registration index, not immediate access to every laboratory file. The judge asked what a failed search would prove. 'Only that we did not find the entry in that index,' she answered. Darien lowered the broader demand he had prepared. A worker confirmed one pencil signature at a time, while the clerk listed the dates and the rooms a neutral examiner would be permitted to inspect.

壓力並非幻想：法院打算因證人不足而結束請願。 法院想以證人不足結案。雅把醫院編號副本放在請願書旁，要求搜尋登記索引，沒有要求立刻打開所有實驗室檔。法官問若搜尋失敗能證明什麼，她答：『只證明在那套索引沒找到。』戴倫放下原先擬好的廣泛要求。工人逐一確認鉛筆簽名，書記官列出中立審查員可查日期與房間。

By evening, they could state one result with confidence: the court reopened one narrow section of the inquiry. The order reopened only a narrow section, but it assigned a clerk and a deadline. Aya copied those details onto the workers' receipt. A veteran examiner lingered after the hearing to say he remembered a specimen that had never appeared in the final register. He could not remember its number. Instead of supplying one from her notes, Aya asked him to write down the circumstances of his recollection. A reference to the railway shelter emerged in his account. She would follow that lead while the court's search proceeded, keeping memory and registration evidence on separate pages. She also invited the retired examiner to authenticate the filing sequence, asking him to identify the records he would need before beginning that separate task.

傍晚，他們能有把握地寫下一項結果：法院重新啟動調查中的一小部分。 命令只重啟一小部分，卻指定了承辦人與期限。雅把細節抄上工人收據。資深審查員散庭後說記得一份未進最終登記的樣本，卻想不起編號。雅沒用自己筆記提示，請他寫下記憶情境。他的敘述提到鐵路收容所。法院搜尋期間，她會追這條線，將記憶與登記證據分頁保存。她也邀這位退休審查員驗證立案順序，請他先指出開始這項獨立工作需要哪些紀錄。
