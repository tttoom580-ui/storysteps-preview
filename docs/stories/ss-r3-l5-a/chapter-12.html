從未失蹤的女人

The Woman Who Was Never Missing

驛站密信：兩枚印章之下 · 第 12 / 25 章 · 4000–5000 · 英中對照 · 校訂中

用互動版查詞、朗讀與收藏

A crowd outside the continental railway shelter was calling for Mara to be released. Inside, she was helping a child mend a coat. Aya waited until she had finished, then asked where she would prefer to talk. Mara chose a table beside an open exit and put her old railway ticket on it. Darien stayed out of earshot when she requested it. The shelter worker showed Aya the visitor rules, including a resident's right to end an interview. Aya read that line aloud to Mara before asking about the government paper she had brought. They examined a relocation script printed on government stock paper on a clean table, with a witness close enough to see every movement.

大陸鐵路收容所外人群喊著釋放瑪拉，裡面她卻在替孩子補外套。雅等她忙完，詢問想在哪裡談。瑪拉選了開放出口旁的桌子，放下舊車票，並請戴倫離開能聽見的距離。工作人員出示訪客規則，包含住民可隨時結束訪談。雅先向瑪拉念這一條，才問她帶來的公用紙文件。 他們在乾淨桌面上查看印在公用紙上的遷移話術稿，讓見證人能看見所有動作。

People had called the woman a hostage and claimed someone meant to kidnap her. Aya found that assumption abnormal. Before confronting anyone, she asked to acquaint herself with the shelter's rules. The woman, Mara, was an observer who had chosen temporary safety; no one was holding her against her will. A broken bolt on the door had made the room look like a prison, but the exits were open. Mara mumbled that discrimination was the norm in the frontier camps. A missionary group had once provided food during an outbreak, and officials later used the group's stationery to disguise orders. The false script created a sensation among eager viewers, some of whom called every cautious witness a traitor. Mara described forced labor that resembled slavery, but did not claim her own case proved every allegation. Aya asked which documents were compatible with that account. Mara produced an old ticket and a viable timeline. The truth was quieter than the rumor, and far more useful.

有人說瑪拉被當成人質，甚至遭綁架。雅覺得這個假設很不正常。她先弄清收容所規則，才接觸當事人。瑪拉是自行選擇暫住安全住所的觀察員，沒有被拘禁。門閂損壞使房間看似監獄，其實出口都開著。瑪拉低聲說邊境營地裡歧視很常見。傳教團體曾在疫情時供餐，後來官員卻冒用其公文紙掩飾命令。假的訪談稿在觀眾間造成轟動，許多人把謹慎證人罵成叛徒。瑪拉描述接近奴役的強迫勞動，但沒有把自己遭遇說成能證明全部指控。雅詢問可對照哪些文件。瑪拉交出舊車票，建立可行的時間線。真相沒有傳聞聳動，卻更有用。

Outside, the immediate danger continued: rumors falsely described a protected witness as a hostage. Aya finally settled on a concrete course: to let the woman choose whether and when to speak. Through the window, a reporter shouted that a locked door proved abduction. Mara rose, opened the door herself, and returned without going outside. 'I am not giving them a performance,' she said. Aya asked whether she wanted any public correction at all. Mara authorized a brief statement about her freedom to leave, excluding her location and previous addresses. Then she unfolded the relocation script. Aya compared its official stock mark with the ticket date, asking which instructions Mara had actually received and which she had learned of only later from other residents.

外頭的危險還在持續：謠言把受保護證人說成人質。 雅最後選擇一條具體做法：讓女子自己決定是否及何時發言。 窗外記者喊鎖門就是綁架證據。瑪拉起身自己開門，沒出去又坐回來。『我不表演給他們看。』雅問她是否想公開更正。她准許簡短說明可自由離開，不可透露位置與過去住址。接著她攤開遷移話術稿。雅把公用紙印記與車票日期比較，問哪些指示是親自收到，哪些後來才聽其他住民說。

The immediate outcome was real, although incomplete: she revealed a transport order that contradicted official practice. The transport order did not follow the official procedure described on its printed form. Mara pointed to an unusual mission-agency signature and remembered seeing it copied onto other sheets. Aya did not ask her to authenticate those missing pages. She requested permission to trace this signature, and Mara granted it while refusing a photograph of herself. Before leaving, Aya read back the statement for the people outside. Mara removed one phrase that made her sound grateful for protection she had chosen on her own terms. The corrected words went to Mira; the original ticket stayed with its owner.

目前的成果確實存在，雖然還不完整：她揭露一紙違反官方慣例的運輸命令。 運輸命令不符合表格上所印的官方程序。瑪拉指出特殊的宗教援助機構簽名，記得其他紙上也曾複寫。雅沒請她鑑定不在場的文件，只取得追查這個簽名的許可；瑪拉拒絕拍自己照片。離開前，雅念出給外面人群的聲明。瑪拉刪去一句，因為那讓自主選擇保護的她像在謝恩。更正文字交給米拉，車票原件仍由本人保管。
