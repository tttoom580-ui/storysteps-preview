午夜之後的郵戳

The Postmark After Midnight

驛站密信：兩枚印章之下 · 第 1 / 25 章 · 4000–5000 · 英中對照

Aya pressed her shoulder against the station gate while Darien lifted its sagging latch. Inside, dust lay thick everywhere except on the shelf beneath the alarm bell. A square patch had been wiped clean. The keeper said he had locked the building himself, then handed Aya a key still wrapped in his handkerchief. She asked him to leave the packet exactly where he had found it. Twenty-two years earlier, this station had handled the flood warnings that never reached her mother. Today someone had apparently returned to its abandoned sorting room. Before discussing a sealed packet dated twenty-two years earlier, Aya invited its keeper to describe the ordinary process of filing. Aya had been told her mother died in that flood, twenty-two years earlier, but nobody had shown her the complete record.

雅用肩膀頂住驛站大門，戴倫抬起下垂的門閂。屋內處處積塵，唯獨警鈴下的架子有塊方形區域被擦乾淨。保管人說是自己鎖的門，隨後遞出仍包在手帕裡的鑰匙。雅請他別移動郵包。二十二年前，這座驛站處理過始終沒有送到她母親手上的洪災警告。如今顯然有人又進入了廢棄的分信室。 談到一只日期寫在二十二年前的密封郵包前，雅先請保管人說明平常歸檔流程。雅被告知母親在二十二年前那場洪災喪生，卻從沒有人給她看過完整紀錄。

The last batch of undelivered letters was waiting under a cracked lamp when Aya Lin arrived. A narrow circuit of copper wire surrounded the station's old alarm bell. Her mentor had once called this place a decent example of public service under a harsh regime, yet the government now wanted to dissolve it. Aya was no idiot: a sealed envelope was not automatically a breakthrough. She began an inquiry by photographing the rim of the official stamp and recording the packet's precise placement. A nonprofit heritage group had offered to implement a restoration plan, but its projection of costs ignored the strain on nearby families. Darien Hale, a retired courier, tightened the saddle of his motorcycle outside. He said the threat might terrify the last witness into silence. Aya would not let fear triple the value of an untested rumor. Regardless of the ministry's announcement, she would reinforce the record first. The witness said someone had moved the packet at midnight. That detail mattered more than the dramatic seal: a person could move a letter, but not easily explain a date that belonged to another decade.

林雅抵達時，最後一批未送達的信還堆在破燈下。舊警鈴旁繞著一圈銅線。她的導師曾稱這裡是嚴苛政權下仍有基本公共服務的例子，但政府現在要解散驛站。雅不會天真地把密封信當成重大突破；她先查問，拍下公章邊緣，記錄郵包擺放的位置。一個非營利文資團體提出修復計畫，成本預估卻忽略周邊家庭的負擔。退休信差戴倫在外頭調整機車座墊，提醒她有人可能恐嚇最後的證人。雅不願讓恐懼把未證實的傳聞放大三倍。無論部會怎麼宣布，她都要先鞏固紀錄。證人說午夜有人搬動郵包；那比醒目的封蠟更重要，因為人可以搬信，卻很難解釋跨越二十二年的日期。

Their immediate difficulty was clear: the ministry planned to clear the station before sunrise. The choice Aya made was to register the packet openly before examining its contents. Darien reached for his knife, expecting her to cut the seal. Aya pushed a blank receipt toward him instead. 'Write what you can see from there.' He described the string and its single knot; the keeper counted the stamps. Only after all three signed did Aya lift the flap. The ministry's clearance notice lay beside her elbow, giving them until sunrise. She called Mira with the notice number and asked her to obtain a copy, so an official could not later deny the deadline had existed.

眼前的難題很明確：部會打算在日出前清空驛站。 雅決定先公開登記郵包，再查看內容。 戴倫拿出小刀，以為她要割開封口。雅卻把空白收據推向他。『寫下你從那裡看得見的東西。』他描述繩子與唯一的結，保管人數了郵票。三人簽字後，雅才揭開封口。部會的清空通知就在她手肘邊，期限是日出。她把通知編號告訴米拉，請她取得副本，免得官員事後否認曾設期限。

They ended the visit with a limited finding: the packet contained a current photograph inside an old envelope. The photograph showed a building with a new plastic sign, not a scene preserved from the flood. Aya placed it on a separate sheet marked 'modern insertion.' Darien recognized the approach road but could not remember the building's present name. Then the keeper turned the envelope toward the lamp: beneath the original mark was another, naming the river archive. Aya copied that address. Before leaving, she put the signed registration receipt in the keeper's hand and asked him to telephone if the clearance crew arrived early. Their next destination was written on the envelope itself.

查訪以一項有限發現告終：老信封裡竟裝著一張近日拍攝的照片。 照片裡的建築掛著新塑膠招牌，並非洪災年代留下的影像。雅把它另放在註明『現代放入物』的紙上。戴倫認得進出的道路，卻想不起建築現名。保管人將信封轉向燈光，舊郵戳下還有一枚，寫著河岸檔案館。雅抄下地址，離開前把簽好的登記收據交給保管人，請他若清空工人提早到來便打電話。下一站就寫在信封上。
