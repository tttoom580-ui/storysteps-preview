陌生人的總帳

A Ledger of Strangers

驛站密信：兩枚印章之下 · 第 7 / 25 章 · 4000–5000 · 英中對照 · 校訂中

Aya could hear paper crackling in a metal drum before she saw the warehouse chief. He called the labels obsolete and held another bundle over the flames. She asked him to show her the disposal authorization. Darien stayed between the drum and a stack of empty vaccine boxes without touching either. When the chief went to find his order, Aya photographed the labels still waiting on his desk. Three hands had filled the delivery ledger, but the same storage code followed entries written weeks apart. She copied the code twice to avoid a hurried mistake. Before discussing a delivery ledger written in three different hands, Aya invited its keeper to describe the ordinary process of filing.

雅還沒看見倉庫主管，就聽見鐵桶裡紙張燃燒聲。他稱標籤已過期，手拿另一疊靠近火焰。雅要求查看銷毀授權。戴倫站在火桶與空疫苗箱之間，兩者都沒碰。主管去找命令時，雅拍下桌上待處理標籤。三種筆跡寫成的總帳中，隔了幾週的紀錄都跟著同一儲藏碼，她抄兩遍以免匆忙出錯。 談到由三種筆跡寫成的配送總帳前，雅先請保管人說明平常歸檔流程。

The hidden accounts aroused an uneasy feeling, for the ledger contained an array of oddly precise charges. One column described a controversy over the quarantine corridor; another listed corrupt officials, regional cuisine, and even a pirate ship used as a joke by exhausted clerks. Aya refused to derive an accusation from humor alone. She noticed that custody seals had been cut and that a telephone line was disconnected. The warehouse required genuine expertise to manage fragile vaccines, yet the equipment was not sturdy. Someone had allowed unsafe conditions to infect workers while an infrastructure grant went unpaid. A sports league had sponsored a tournament nearby, and its banners covered a doorway marked for emergency removal. Darien found the intended recipient of a medical crate and said her testimony was indispensable. They could comprehend why she feared being named: the clerk who had challenged the order lost his job. Still, the work was worthwhile. Aya copied the public figures and left the private patients' names sealed. She would not make vulnerable people pay for the investigation.

秘密帳本使人不安，裡頭排列著精確得古怪的費用。一欄記載隔離走廊爭議，另一欄列出腐敗官員、地方菜餚，甚至把一艘海盜船當作疲憊職員的笑話。雅不會從玩笑推導罪名。她發現保管封條被割斷、電話線斷開。倉庫本應由熟悉脆弱疫苗的專家管理，設備卻不牢靠。有人放任不安全的環境使工人感染，基礎建設補助也未撥款。附近體育聯盟贊助錦標賽，布條恰好蓋住標著緊急移除的門。戴倫找到醫療箱的預定收件者，認為她的證詞不可或缺。他們理解她怕被公開姓名，因為先前質疑命令的職員已失業。雅只抄錄公開數字，將病患個資繼續封存。她不願讓弱勢者替調查付代價。

Their immediate difficulty was clear: the warehouse chief had begun destroying obsolete labels. The choice Aya made was to compare the ledger with public cargo weights before raising an alarm. The public weighbridge sheets were pinned behind a tournament banner. Darien held its corner while Aya compared incoming and outgoing weights. A clerk protested that empty crates could account for the difference. She agreed and asked for the crate weights, then subtracted them in a column he could inspect. The remaining figure was too large to be explained that way. Rather than announce stolen medicine, she wrote a request for the private storage register. The clerk checked her arithmetic again and finally initialed it, leaving the explanation of the missing weight open.

眼前的難題很明確：倉庫主管開始銷毀舊標籤。 雅決定先以公開貨重紀錄比對總帳，再提出警告。 公開地磅紀錄釘在賽事布條後。戴倫掀起一角，雅比較進出貨重。館員說空箱也有重量，她同意並索取空箱數字，在他能檢查的欄位扣除。餘額仍太大。她沒有宣布藥品遭竊，而是申請私人儲藏登記簿。館員又核算一次，終於簽上姓名縮寫，缺少重量的原因仍待解釋。

They ended the visit with a limited finding: a code showed that medicine had been diverted into private storage. The private storage code matched the intended destination of the medical crate, not the public clinic named on its dispatch label. Aya put the two copies side by side for the recipient to examine. The woman would confirm the delivery number, but refused to give the patients' names. Aya covered those lines before Mira arrived to photograph the page. One reference led to the research annex beneath the signal tower. Darien wrote down its caretaker's hours. The warehouse chief returned with an unsigned disposal sheet, and Aya attached a copy of it to her preservation request.

查訪以一項有限發現告終：一組代碼揭露藥品被轉進私人倉庫。 私人儲藏碼對上醫療箱的實際去處，而非出貨標籤寫的公立診所。雅把兩份副本並排給收件者看。女子願確認貨號，拒絕提供病患姓名。米拉來拍照前，雅已遮住那些欄位。索引指向信號塔下研究附樓，戴倫記下看守人的值班時間。主管帶回一張未簽字的銷毀單，雅將副本附在保全申請後。
