雨中的貨車

Freight in the Rain

驛站密信：兩枚印章之下 · 第 4 / 25 章 · 4000–5000 · 英中對照

The morning at a freight depot outside the mountain town began with the sort of delay that rarely appears in official histories. A gate was locked, someone had lost a key, and the first witness had another job to attend to. Aya refused to mistake inconvenience for conspiracy. She bought tea for Darien, who studied the faces passing the entrance. The authorities had taught him to notice who looked nervous; his years away from the service had taught him that nervousness was not guilt. They waited, speaking about the flood of twenty-two years ago. Aya still remembered hearing that her mother had died then, although no one had ever shown her the complete record.

山城外的貨運站的早晨從史書很少記下的耽擱開始：門被鎖住、鑰匙遺失、第一名證人還得趕去工作。雅不把不便當成陰謀。她替戴倫買茶，他則觀察入口的人。當局過去教他辨認緊張的人；離職多年後他明白緊張不等於有罪。兩人等待時談起二十二年前的洪災。雅仍記得當年聽說母親死了，卻從來沒有人給她看過完整紀錄。

Before discussing a shipment rulebook with a torn medical page, Aya invited its keeper to describe the ordinary process of filing. She listened for what should have happened on a normal day, not merely for suspicious details. Darien was surprised by her patience. He had spent years receiving urgent instructions, but urgency had often been used to prevent questions. The keeper explained the difference between an original, a working copy, and a later summary. Aya labeled each one in plain language. She also asked who might have had legitimate access. Finding a name on a page would mean little unless they knew whether that person could have written, received, or simply read it.

談到醫療頁面遭撕掉的貨運規則手冊前，雅先請保管人說明平常歸檔流程。她想知道正常日子應發生什麼，而非只找可疑處。戴倫驚訝她這麼有耐心。他多年來接收緊急指令，但緊急常被用來阻止提問。保管人解釋原件、工作副本與事後摘要的不同。雅用白話逐一標記，也詢問誰原本就有合法存取權。若不知道某人是能寫、能收，還是只能閱讀文件，即使找到名字也沒有多少意義。

The depot was built for freight rather than people, and its driveway had become a river of mud. Aya fought boredom by checking each civic guideline against the actual housing records. A mechanic described an innovative system that should stimulate safer travel, yet recent orders seemed to intensify danger. The truck's windshield was cracked; inside a tin box lay medication for displaced families. The driver had a noticeable bruise and had started to vomit from exhaustion. He did not want to plead for sympathy, only to rest. Darien argued that morality was not separate from evidence: prolonged questioning could ruin a witness's memory. Aya heard an eloquent speech from the manager about national duty, but it sounded like despair disguised as pride. She noticed a trait shared by every altered document—a stamp slightly below the approved line. Rather than prolong the driver's pain, she recorded the stamp, arranged treatment, and postponed their interview. His safety was not an obstacle to the investigation. It was part of the reason the investigation existed at all.

貨站只為貨物設計，車道在雨裡成了泥河。雅對照公民守則和住宅紀錄，藉此排遣等待的煩悶。技工解釋一套原本應提高安全的創新系統，但新命令反而加劇危險。卡車擋風玻璃碎裂，鐵盒內放著流離失所家庭需要的藥品。司機身上有明顯瘀青，疲累得嘔吐。他沒有求同情，只求休息。戴倫說道德與證據無法分開：過度盤問可能破壞記憶。主管高談國家義務，語氣雄辯，雅聽來卻像把絕望裝成自豪。她發現每份遭修改文件的印章都稍微低於核准線。她沒有延長司機的痛苦，而是記錄印章、安排治療，延後訪談。保護他不是妨礙調查，正是調查的理由。

Their immediate difficulty was clear: the depot manager wanted to send the cargo away overnight. In the corridor, Darien suggested moving faster and making a public announcement. Aya understood the temptation, especially when a document could disappear within hours. Yet a dramatic accusation might warn the very person who controlled the missing records. She telephoned Mira and asked her to report only what could be checked independently. Mira agreed, though she warned that careful stories rarely traveled as quickly as angry ones. Aya looked at the clock and accepted the cost of patience. If they were wrong, a stranger could lose a livelihood. If they were right, the evidence would still need to survive a hostile examination.

眼前的難題很明確：貨運站主管想連夜把貨物送走。走廊裡戴倫提議加快行動，立刻公開消息。雅理解這種衝動，尤其文件可能幾小時內消失；但激烈指控也可能提前警告掌握紀錄的人。她致電米拉，請她只報導可以獨立核實的事。米拉同意，卻提醒審慎故事通常傳得不如憤怒快。雅看看時鐘，接受耐心的代價。若他們錯了，陌生人可能失去生計；若他們對了，證據仍得經得起敵意檢驗。

The choice Aya made was to keep the sick driver safe before questioning him. She put the request in writing, leaving room for the person receiving it to refuse or suggest a safer method. Darien noticed how carefully she avoided asking anyone to become a hero. In his former life, he had seen officials demand sacrifice from people who had the fewest options. Aya wanted witnesses to remain owners of their own stories. She also insisted on keeping a record of what the team had not discovered. A blank space could become important later, but filling it with a convenient answer now would make the record dishonest. They signed the page and set it beside the other verified notes.

雅決定先照顧生病司機，再向他詢問。她把請求寫下，讓接收者能拒絕或提出更安全的方法。戴倫發現她從不要求別人當英雄。他過去看過官員要選擇最少的人犧牲。雅希望證人仍擁有自己的故事，也堅持記下團隊尚未發現的部分。空白日後可能重要，但現在用方便的答案填滿，只會使紀錄失真。他們簽下這頁，放到其他已核實筆記旁。

They ended the visit with a limited finding: the driver remembered three consecutive changes in the loading schedule. Aya read it aloud so that anyone present could correct a mistaken name or number. No one objected. Darien offered a tired smile, aware that agreement on one fact did not end the larger dispute. The old system had survived by making every question sound disloyal. Their new method was deliberately ordinary: ask, compare, record, and leave space for correction. A final detail changed their plans for the following morning: the changes were authorized from the provincial housing office. Aya folded her notes without folding the original document. When they stepped outside, the street was still busy. A town could carry on while its history was being reconsidered.

查訪以一項有限發現告終：司機記起裝貨時間表連改三次。雅大聲讀出，讓在場者能糾正錯誤名字或數字。沒有人反對。戴倫疲倦地微笑，知道一項事實達成共識不等於大爭議結束。舊制度讓所有提問看似不忠，他們的方法刻意平凡：詢問、比對、紀錄，保留修正空間。最後一個細節改變了隔天安排：變更由省住宅辦公室授權。雅折好筆記，不折原件。走到外頭，街道仍熱鬧。小鎮能在重新理解歷史的同時繼續生活。
