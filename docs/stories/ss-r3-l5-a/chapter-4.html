雨中的貨車

Freight in the Rain

驛站密信：兩枚印章之下 · 第 4 / 25 章 · 4000–5000 · 英中對照

用互動版查詞、朗讀與收藏

Rainwater ran from the freight shed into the wheel ruts. Darien parked above the flooded driveway while Aya followed a worker carrying a dry tarpaulin. The shipment rulebook lay under it, its medical page torn along the binding. The worker showed her the remaining corner, where part of a loading time survived. Aya asked him to bring the day's other copies. Before he could answer, the depot manager called for the truck to leave. From inside its cab came a sound of retching, followed by the driver's request for water. Before discussing a shipment rulebook with a torn medical page, Aya invited its keeper to describe the ordinary process of filing.

雨水從貨棚流進車轍。戴倫把車停在積水車道上方，雅跟著拿乾帆布的工人走。貨運手冊蓋在帆布下，醫療頁沿裝訂處被撕走，殘角還留著部分裝貨時間。雅請工人拿來當天其他副本，尚未得到回答，貨站主管就催卡車離開。駕駛室傳出乾嘔聲，隨後司機開口要水。 談到醫療頁面遭撕掉的貨運規則手冊前，雅先請保管人說明平常歸檔流程。

The depot was built for freight rather than people, and its driveway had become a river of mud. Aya fought boredom by checking each civic guideline against the actual housing records. A mechanic described an innovative system that should stimulate safer travel, yet recent orders seemed to intensify danger. The truck's windshield was cracked; inside a tin box lay medication for displaced families. The driver had a noticeable bruise and had started to vomit from exhaustion. He did not want to plead for sympathy, only to rest. Darien argued that morality was not separate from evidence: prolonged questioning could ruin a witness's memory. Aya heard an eloquent speech from the manager about national duty, but it sounded like despair disguised as pride. She noticed a trait shared by every altered document—a stamp slightly below the approved line. Rather than prolong the driver's pain, she recorded the stamp, arranged treatment, and postponed their interview. His safety was not an obstacle to the investigation. It was part of the reason the investigation existed at all.

貨站只為貨物設計，車道在雨裡成了泥河。雅對照公民守則和住宅紀錄，藉此排遣等待的煩悶。技工解釋一套原本應提高安全的創新系統，但新命令反而加劇危險。卡車擋風玻璃碎裂，鐵盒內放著流離失所家庭需要的藥品。司機身上有明顯瘀青，疲累得嘔吐。他沒有求同情，只求休息。戴倫說道德與證據無法分開：過度盤問可能破壞記憶。主管高談國家義務，語氣雄辯，雅聽來卻像把絕望裝成自豪。她發現每份遭修改文件的印章都稍微低於核准線。她沒有延長司機的痛苦，而是記錄印章、安排治療，延後訪談。保護他不是妨礙調查，正是調查的理由。

Their immediate difficulty was clear: the depot manager wanted to send the cargo away overnight. The choice Aya made was to keep the sick driver safe before questioning him. The manager said another delay would spoil the delivery. Aya pointed to the driver's shaking hand on the steering wheel and asked who would accept responsibility if he collapsed on the road. Darien fetched the depot's first-aid attendant and moved the ignition key onto the desk in full view. Nobody had to seize the cargo. It could remain under cover while the driver was examined. Aya wrote down the manager's departure instruction and asked him to sign it; he pushed the page back without touching the pen. She noted that refusal beneath the time.

眼前的難題很明確：貨運站主管想連夜把貨物送走。 雅決定先照顧生病司機，再向他詢問。 主管說再延誤會毀掉配送。雅指著司機放在方向盤上顫抖的手，問若他在路上倒下，誰願負責。戴倫找來急救人員，當眾把鑰匙放到桌上。貨物不必扣押，可以留在棚下等待司機受檢。雅記下主管催促離站的指示，請他簽字。他沒碰筆就把紙推回，她在時間下方註明拒簽。

They ended the visit with a limited finding: the driver remembered three consecutive changes in the loading schedule. After resting, the driver recognized the surviving corner of the rulebook. He remembered being told three different loading times, each replacing the previous instruction. Aya set out three slips of paper and let him arrange them without prompting him with names. He changed their order once, then explained the change. The final instruction bore a provincial office code. Darien found that office on the route map while Aya arranged a later interview, after treatment. They left copies of the three slips with the driver so he could correct his recollection when he was no longer exhausted.

查訪以一項有限發現告終：司機記起裝貨時間表連改三次。 休息後，司機認出手冊殘角。他記得三個不同裝貨時間，每次新指示都取代上一次。雅擺出三張紙，讓他自行排序，沒有先說人名。他改了一次順序並解釋原因。最後指示帶有省級機關代碼。戴倫在路線圖上找到機關，雅約好治療後再訪。三張紙的副本留給司機，讓他不再疲憊時仍能修正記憶。
