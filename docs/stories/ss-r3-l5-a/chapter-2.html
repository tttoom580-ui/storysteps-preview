A brass plaque at the River Archive named the donor in letters larger than the institution's own. Aya stopped beneath it to read a smaller notice about copying charges. The clerk quoted a price that would consume their day's travel money. Mira asked for the rule authorizing it. While he searched his drawer, Aya noticed that the donation ledger and folded survey map shared a reference number. She set her request beside the register, asking for both rather than allowing the map to be treated as an isolated decoration. The object at the center of their visit was a donation ledger and a folded survey map. The clerk fetched a second receipt book, allowing Aya to check that the disputed number had not been assigned to two separate gifts.

河岸檔案館的銅牌上，捐助者姓名比館名還大。雅停下讀影印收費的小告示。館員報出的價格足以花掉他們一天的交通費。米拉詢問收費依據，館員翻抽屜時，雅注意到捐款簿與折疊測繪圖有相同索引號。她把申請放在登記簿旁，要求同時調閱兩件，免得地圖被當成孤立的裝飾品。 這趟查訪的核心物件是捐款簿和折疊的測繪圖。館員拿來第二本收據簿，雅因此能確認爭議號碼並非分配給兩份不同贈與。

The architecture of the archive was impressive, but Aya found its records strangely incomplete. A donor had paid for excessive repairs and demanded that the staff highlight his generous ideology instead of the missing pages. An indigenous fishing family had provided the original map; now its members were treated as outsiders. Mira Voss, a journalist trained in careful journalism, insisted that a source's reputation did not render a claim true. She offered Aya tea and a brief shoulder massage after hours of reading, then returned to the documents. Their quest would persist despite adverse publicity. In a folder of salmon permits, Aya found a series of receipts with one curious variation in the signature. The sponsor threatened prosecution if they tried to rip the map from its frame. Darien called the threat absurd, but refused to touch the paper without a clerk present. That choice mattered. By leaving the original where it was and making a witnessed request, they protected the evidence and the people who had once trusted the public archive.

檔案館的建築令人讚嘆，紀錄卻缺得離奇。捐助者付了過高的整修費，要求職員突顯他的理念，別提失蹤頁面。原圖其實來自原住漁家，如今家人反被當作外來者。受過新聞訓練的記者米拉堅持，來源名聲不會自動讓說法成真。她替長時間閱讀的雅端茶、輕揉肩膀，然後繼續查資料。即使宣傳不利，調查仍要持續。雅在鮭魚許可文件夾裡找到一連串收據，同一簽名卻有細微差異。贊助者威脅，若有人把地圖從框中撕下，就要提告。戴倫覺得荒謬，仍拒絕在沒有館員見證時觸碰原件。正因如此，他們保住了證據，也守住當年相信公共檔案制度的人。

The archive's private sponsor wanted the map returned immediately. The sponsor's assistant arrived carrying an empty map case and asked the clerk to unlock the frame. Darien moved toward him; Aya caught his sleeve. Instead of blocking the doorway, she asked the clerk to read the accession entry aloud. It recorded a gift from the fishing family, without a return date. The assistant's demand was now a request that could be recorded and challenged. Aya applied for an independent copy under the public access rules. Mira photographed the application number while the original remained behind glass, still available to the next reader. Darien admitted that his service file bore the same seal as a missing border order. He had never approved that order and could establish only where he himself had been assigned. Aya added his account to the notes without treating it as corroboration of the ledger.

By evening, they could state one result with confidence: two different names appeared beside the same old border payment. On the copy, one payment appeared beside two names. Darien laid a ruler beneath the entries to make sure they had not mistaken adjoining rows for a single transaction. Aya checked the receipt number at the far edge, where the frame had concealed it. It matched. That did not explain the different signatures, but it gave them something precise to ask. A faded route on the survey crossed the fishermen's cooperative. Mira wrote its opening hour in their notebook, and Aya folded only the copy before they returned the ledger to its clerk.

傍晚，他們能有把握地寫下一項結果：同一筆舊邊境款項旁出現兩個不同名字。 副本上，同一筆付款旁有兩個名字。戴倫用尺對齊欄位，確認不是把相鄰兩列當成一筆交易。雅核對原先被畫框遮住的收據號，確實相同。這還不能解釋簽名差異，卻讓他們能提出精確問題。測繪圖上一條褪色路線經過漁民合作社，米拉把營業時間記進筆記。歸還帳簿時，雅只折起副本。
