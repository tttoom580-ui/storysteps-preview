墓園裡的名字

A Name at the Cemetery

驛站密信：兩枚印章之下 · 第 9 / 25 章 · 4000–5000 · 英中對照 · 校訂中

用互動版查詞、朗讀與收藏

The groundskeeper opened his hut but did not bring out the oldest register. Its shelf was empty, with a strip of pale wood showing where the binding had protected it from sunlight. Aya asked when he had last used it. He answered by naming a burial, not a date, and led them to the stone. Darien read the courier's serial number while Aya compared it with the permit. A work renewal written seven years after the supposed death lay in the same envelope. She asked the groundskeeper to explain why he had kept the documents together. They examined a burial permit marked with a courier's serial number on a clean table, with a witness close enough to see every movement.

管理員打開小屋，卻沒拿出最古老的登記簿。架子空著，書脊原先遮陽處留下一條淡色木紋。雅問最後何時用過，他沒說日期，而是指出一次葬禮，帶他們到墓前。戴倫讀信差序號，雅比對安葬許可，同信封還有官方死亡日期七年後的工作續證。她問管理員為何將兩件放在一起。 他們在乾淨桌面上查看寫有信差序號的安葬許可，讓見證人能看見所有動作。

A former ally offered to join their expedition, but Aya wondered whether the invitation was a bid to distract them. Near the cemetery's rear gate, a group of volunteers prepared mint tea and tuna sandwiches. A squat stone hut served as their lounge; its exterior smelled of damp leaves rather than the stink that frightened visitors expected. The premier had ordered a compulsory inspection after a reported assault. Nobody had been injured, yet two officers tried to snatch the groundskeeper's notebooks. Darien stepped forward but refused to embrace the rumor that every guard was corrupt. Above them the galaxy was invisible in daylight, while the old memorial letters remained plainly legible. The groundskeeper could manifest his citizenship through a registration card, yet he was treated like a stranger in his own town. Aya copied the inscription and noted the exact time. If the courier had died when officials claimed, he could not have renewed a work permit seven years later. The contradiction was physical, simple, and difficult to explain away.

昔日盟友提議加入尋訪，雅卻懷疑那是分散注意力的手段。墓園後門附近，志工泡薄荷茶、準備鮪魚三明治。矮石屋是休息室，外牆聞起來是潮濕樹葉，而非遊客想像的臭味。總理因一份襲擊報告下令強制檢查，實際卻沒有人受傷；兩名警員仍想奪走管理員的筆記。戴倫上前阻止，但不願以此認定所有守衛腐敗。白天看不見銀河，墓碑舊字卻清晰可辨。管理員明明有公民證明，仍被當成外人。雅抄錄刻文與時間。假如信差真在官方宣稱的年份死亡，便不可能七年後更新工作許可。這是實體、簡單、難以搪塞的矛盾。

Outside, the immediate danger continued: someone had removed the oldest cemetery register. Aya finally settled on a concrete course: to interview the groundskeeper without revealing the witness's identity. When the officers reached for his notebooks, the groundskeeper held them against the hut wall. Aya asked whether they had a receipt for the removed register. Neither produced one. She gave them the permit number and requested a written record of anything they took now. Darien stayed beside the doorway, leaving the path open. The groundskeeper agreed to answer questions inside, away from the volunteers, provided Aya did not publish his name. She wrote that condition before asking about the renewal and let him inspect every line of her notes.

外頭的危險還在持續：有人移走墓園最古老的登記簿。 雅最後選擇一條具體做法：不透露證人身分，先訪問墓園管理員。 軍官伸手拿筆記本，管理員把它壓在小屋牆上。雅問移走舊簿是否開了收據，兩人都拿不出。她報出許可號，要求此刻若帶走物品必須留下書面紀錄。戴倫站在門邊，讓通道保持暢通。管理員願在屋內避開志工回答，條件是不公開姓名。雅先記條件，才問續證，並讓他逐行檢查筆記。

The immediate outcome was real, although incomplete: the permit proved that a supposed dead courier had lived seven more years. The registration details ruled out a simple coincidence of names: both documents used the same serial number. The groundskeeper had attached the later permit because he, too, had noticed the impossible dates. Aya could now show that the official death account was wrong, although she could not say where the courier had spent those years. A seal on the grave marker named the city hospital. She took a rubbing with permission, keeping the inscription separate from the witness's private notes. As they descended toward the harbor, Darien stopped calling the man 'the dead courier.' That description had become a question.

目前的成果確實存在，雖然還不完整：許可證明據稱死亡的信差又活了七年。 登記細節排除了同名巧合，兩份都有相同序號。管理員把後來的許可附在一起，正因也注意到日期不可能。雅能指出官方死亡說法錯誤，仍不知道信差那些年在哪裡。墓碑印記寫著市立醫院。她獲准拓印，與證人私記分開保存。下山往港口時，戴倫不再稱他『死去的信差』，那個稱呼已成為疑問。
