第一次公開質問

The First Public Question

At the provincial hall, a clerk tried to seat Aya behind the transport company's representatives. She asked for a place where the projected route comparison would remain visible. Darien unrolled their map in the corridor while Mira counted the people waiting to enter. A former postal worker brought his own appointment book and asked whether he could show it to the committee. Aya checked its dates against the report and helped him request a speaking turn. When the doors opened, he carried the book himself rather than handing his experience to her. The object at the center of their visit was a report comparing the original route with the revised one.

省議會館員想讓雅坐在運輸公司代表後方，她要求能看見路線比較投影片的位置。戴倫在走廊攤開地圖，米拉數著等候入場的人。一名前郵政工人帶來任職紀錄，問能否向委員會出示。雅核對報告日期，協助申請發言。他入場時自己拿著簿子，沒有把經歷交給雅代述。 這趟查訪的核心物件是比較原始與改版路線的報告。

Attendance at the hearing was higher than anyone expected. A contemporary historian spoke about a controversial corporation that had purchased the old border lines. Aya refused to contend that every employee was guilty. She could defy an unfair order without inventing a new enemy. A guard's bruise was displayed as proof of violence, although nobody could establish who caused it. The company hoped an endorsement from a respected resident would facilitate its public offering. Aya brought a careful statistical comparison and explained each segment of the transport record. Her simple outfit drew less attention than the contradiction on the screen. “The essence of this claim,” she said, “is that a route changed after approval.” The irony was obvious: the same institution that praised a smooth political transition had buried the movement of ordinary families. Darien offered another perspective, describing a border ritual that required every courier to sign twice. Overall, their testimony did not identify a criminal. It did establish that the documents deserved protection, and that was enough for the first vote.

聽證的出席人數超過預期。一位當代史學者談到收購邊境路線的爭議企業。雅不主張員工人人有罪；她能反抗不公命令，卻不必製造新敵人。有人把警衛的瘀青當成暴力證據，但誰造成的無法確認。企業想用受尊敬居民的背書促成公開募資。雅帶來謹慎的統計比較，逐段解釋運輸紀錄。她簡單的服裝不如螢幕上的矛盾醒目。「這項主張的核心，」她說，「是路線在核准後被改動。」諷刺的是，同一機關讚揚政治轉型順利，卻埋沒普通家庭的遷移。戴倫提出另一角度：舊驛站儀式要求信差簽兩次名。整體證詞還不能確認罪犯身分，但足以讓議會保護文件。第一次表決，他們贏得暫緩銷毀。

A powerful corporation had offered to sponsor the hearing. The company offered to pay the hearing's expenses. Its representative placed a proposed sponsorship notice beside the microphone, already bearing the committee's crest. Aya asked whether payment would include a right to approve the transcript. The chair paused and ordered the notice removed until its terms were examined. Darien wanted a vote on responsibility immediately; Aya asked instead for a vote stopping the destruction of the records. The former postal worker stood up to support that request, saying his appointment book could not replace the public files against which it had to be checked.

壓力並非幻想：大型企業表示願意贊助聽證。 公司提出支付聽證費用，代表把印有委員會徽章的贊助公告放在麥克風旁。雅問付款是否包含核准逐字稿的權利。主席停頓後，要求先撤下公告審查條款。戴倫想立即表決責任，雅則要求先表決停止銷毀紀錄。前郵政工人起身支持，說他的任職簿不能取代必須用來核對的公檔。

By evening, they could state one result with confidence: the council ordered a temporary pause on archive destruction. The temporary pause passed, but the clerk asked which rooms it covered. Aya named the station, the river archive, and the depot files already cited in the hearing. The committee attached their reference numbers to the written order. A reluctant member added that future discoveries would require separate applications. Outside, Mira read the order back to the postal worker, who noticed a mistyped depot number. They returned before the clerk left. The corrected copy mentioned a customs register across the frontier, giving Aya a narrower and more difficult journey to plan than a public victory speech.

傍晚，他們能有把握地寫下一項結果：議會命令暫停銷毀檔案。 暫停銷毀獲通過，書記官卻問涵蓋哪些房間。雅列出聽證已引用的驛站、河岸檔案館與貨站檔案，委員會把索引號附進命令。一名不情願的委員補充，新發現須另申請。門外，米拉念給郵政工人聽，他發現貨站號碼打錯。他們趕在書記官下班前回去。更正本提到境外海關簿，雅得規劃一趟範圍更窄、難度更高的旅程。
