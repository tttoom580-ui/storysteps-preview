承諾的價格

The Price of a Promise

1996，藍線裁縫舖的第二把尺 · 第 11 / 25 章 · 3000–4000 · 英中對照 · 校訂中

The provisional ruling changed the town's conversation. At the barber's shop, men argued about capitalism and whether a small workshop deserved protection. At the school gate, parents asked whether competition would reduce prices. Shuning refused to make either argument dominant. She wanted to circulate the actual conditions: two designs, clear return rights, paid labor, and a public code for comparing supplies. Her handwritten notices traveled faster than the school office expected. A teacher warned that praise could breed false confidence. "People may think you've already won," he said. Shuning agreed that the eventual result remained uncertain. She pinned an empty chart beside the notices, leaving room for failures as well as successes. An honest record needed space for both.

暫行決議改變了小鎮的談話。理髮店裡有人爭論資本主義與小工作室是否值得保護；校門口家長則問競爭能否壓低價格。書寧不願讓任何一種論點主導。她只想傳閱實際條件：兩種版型、清楚的退貨權、付薪勞動，以及公開比較材料的規範。手寫公告傳得比校辦公室預期快。老師提醒讚美可能滋長虛假的信心：「大家會以為妳已經贏了。」書寧同意，最後結果仍未確定。她在公告旁貼上一張空白表格，留著記失敗，也記成果。誠實紀錄需要兩者的位置。

Yang invited Shuning to negotiate a shared fabric purchase. By ordering together, their two companies could expand the range of sizes without creating enormous leftover stock. Jianhe was wary. He thought the agreement might be an invasion of Blue Thread's independence. Yang gave a graceful reply: no one would be required to reveal private customers or surrender designs. Only material specifications and delivery dates would be shared. Shuning asked for a written indication of how canceled orders would be handled. Yang took out a dusty ledger and showed her how a previous contract had a painful consequence: a costly wreck of unsold fabric. "I learned from that," she said. "Cooperation without exit terms is another kind of trap."

慧蘭邀書寧談共同採購布料。兩家公司一起下單，能提供更多尺寸而不留下大量庫存。建和很警惕，覺得這可能侵犯藍線獨立性。慧蘭卻優雅而從容地回答：不要求透露私人顧客，也不用交出設計，只分享材料規格與交貨日期。書寧要求寫明取消訂單如何處理。慧蘭拿出積灰帳本，說明上一次合約帶來嚴重後果：留下大批賣不出去的布，造成慘重損失。「我從中學到教訓，」她說，「沒有退出條件的合作，也是陷阱。」

At the weekend sports festival, the town held a marathon along the harbor. Yating worked at the water station and saw a child rebel against the instruction to wear the official running vest. The child's skin had been irritated by its rough label. A volunteer first tried persuasion, then asked the child what alternative would help. Shuning, who was watching the finish line, suggested turning the label outward for that day and reporting the problem afterward. It was a small repair, not a new uniform. The solution also showed why rules needed room for exceptions. No authority was weakened because one student was allowed to run without pain. The race ended, and the child reached the line smiling.

週末運動節，小鎮沿海港辦馬拉松。雅婷在水站服務，看到一名孩子反抗穿官方背心的要求，因為粗糙標籤磨得皮膚發紅。一名志工先試著說服，後來改問孩子需要什麼協助。正在看終點線的書寧建議當天先把標籤翻到外面，賽後再正式回報。這只是小修補，不是重新做制服，也說明規則需要例外的空間。容許一名學生不痛地跑步，不會削弱任何人的權威。賽事結束，孩子笑著跑過終點。

Before returning to the shop, the family visited the harbor history room. A broken telegraph key lay beside an early solar lamp. Deyuan said the telegraph once seemed as miraculous as any modern machine, but the town had survived when it stopped working. The lamp still functioned when enough light reached its panel. Shuning thought of the comparison without turning it into a slogan. New technology did not make old skills worthless, and old skills did not excuse unsafe work. Back at Blue Thread, a little girl reached up to tickle Xiulan's hand, then stopped when Xiulan winced. The child apologized. Xiulan told her the hand needed rest, not pity. Their future had to include that simple truth.

回店前，一家人去了港口歷史室。壞掉的電報鍵放在早期太陽能燈旁。德元說電報當年也像奇蹟，停用後鎮上仍然活得下去；那盞燈只要照到足夠陽光還能亮。書寧記下這個比較，卻沒拿來做口號。新科技不會讓舊技能毫無價值，舊技能也不能替危險工作開脫。回藍線後，小女孩伸手想搔秀蘭的手心，秀蘭一皺眉，她便立即停下並道歉。秀蘭說手需要休息，不需要可憐。未來的計畫必須包含這個簡單事實。

By Monday, Yang sent a draft agreement. It stated the minimum quality checks, the method for correcting shortages, and the right of each workshop to decline an order that exceeded its staff's capacity. Jianhe read every line. He still distrusted the arrangement, but he could name the risk precisely rather than merely fear a rival's success. Shuning added a separate note requiring the school to receive the same material report as the two suppliers. If the shared purchase went ahead, they would not hide its details behind a pleasant image of cooperation. The next week's task was clear: obtain samples, price the labor honestly, and let the evidence decide. Nobody signed that evening. They needed one more day to think, and time to ask the workers who would fulfill the agreement.

星期一，慧蘭寄來協議草稿，寫明最低品質檢查、缺料修正方式，以及工作量超出人力時各店可以拒絕接單。建和逐行讀完。他仍不信任這個安排，卻已能明確指出風險，而不只是害怕對手成功。書寧補上一條：學校也應收到與兩家公司相同的材料報告。若真聯合採購，不能用漂亮的合作形象遮住細節。下週任務很清楚：取得樣布、誠實估工資，讓證據決定。當晚誰也沒有簽名。他們還需要一天思考，也要詢問實際執行合約的工人。
