Two Lines on One Form

1996，藍線裁縫舖的第二把尺 · 第 8 / 25 章 · 3000–4000 · 英中對照 · 校訂中

The school posted a bulletin about its new uniform survey. The form divided students by gender and offered only two designs: skirts for girls, trousers for boys. Shuning expected trouble, but she did not want abstract arguments to dominate the meeting before students had spoken. She went to acquire copies of the form and asked Counselor Luo to invite families to comment privately. A student who had recently served as a bridegroom's helper at a wedding said he liked formal trousers but not the tight school version. A girl who played the cello preferred a skirt on stage and trousers for cycling. Their choices were practical, not a contest over whose identity mattered more. Luo listened without trying to decide for them.

學校公布新制服問卷。表格依性別分類，只提供女生裙裝、男生褲裝兩種版型。書寧預料會有爭議，卻不想在學生說話前就讓抽象議論主導會議。她特地取得問卷影本，請羅老師邀請家庭私下回應。一名曾在婚禮協助新郎的學生說，他喜歡正式西褲，但不喜歡學校這種太緊的褲子。拉大提琴的女生喜歡舞台裙裝，騎車時卻更想穿褲子。這些選擇很實際，不是比誰的身分較重要。羅老師傾聽，沒有替他們決定。

At the consultation, a learned retired teacher worried that the proposed options would create jealousy between students who could afford custom clothing and those who could not. The suggestion deserved attention. Outside the hall, the school garden stood on fertile soil, but inside the room nobody could assume that choices grew freely without support. Shuning used the school's basic data software to estimate the extra cost of allowing either design to every student. She was careful to mark the estimate as provisional; the program could calculate averages, not predict individual preferences. Yang Huilan also attended and offered her company's manufacturing figures. Her enthusiasm surprised Shuning. "I can produce both designs," Yang said. "But I need a systematic ordering process, or late changes will overwhelm the factory." They agreed to compare procedures instead of competing over speeches.

諮詢會上，一位學識豐富的退休教師擔心，給得起訂製服的人和給不起的人之間會產生嫉妒。這值得認真看待。會堂外的校園花園長在肥沃土壤上，但室內的人不能假定選擇不需要支持就會自然生長。書寧用學校的基本資料軟體估算讓所有學生自由選款所增加的成本，並註明那只是暫估，程式能算平均值，不能預測每個人的偏好。慧蘭也出席，帶來公司的生產數據。她的熱情讓書寧意外。「兩種我都能做，」慧蘭說，「但要有系統的訂購流程，否則臨時修改會壓垮工廠。」兩人決定比較程序，不比誰演講動聽。

The intensity of the discussion rose when a council member said the proposal might invade school tradition. An elderly parent replied that tradition had changed many times before. From the hall window they could see the gulf between the island and the mainland, where boats crossed regardless of which shore passengers came from. Shuning resisted the temptation to use the view as a grand metaphor. She stated a narrow proposal: both skirt and trouser patterns would be available at the same published price, with alterations priced separately and assistance for families who needed it. Nothing in the suggestion required a student to explain personal circumstances. The chair placed the proposal on the record as a possible rule, not a final decision.

一名議員說提案可能侵犯校園傳統，討論的激烈程度立刻升高。一名年長家長回答，傳統本來就變過很多次。會場窗外可以看到本島與外島間的海灣，船不問乘客來自哪一岸，照樣往返。書寧忍住把風景說成大道理的衝動，只提出狹義方案：裙裝、褲裝都按公開相同價格供應，修改另列價格，必要時提供補助。學生無須解釋個人情況。主席把提案記錄成可能規則，而不是立即決議。

Later, the students practiced their presentation in the music room. A young composer had written a brief piece to mark the start of the school festival. The teacher gave a cue, and the children entered on time. Shuning noticed that the orchestra functioned because each player knew when to listen as well as when to act. That observation helped her revise the order form. Instead of a single box filled out by the school, it would have three stages: the student's choice, a confirmation of size, and final approval before cutting. Together, the stages would constitute a record without collecting unnecessary personal information. Even a civilian committee could review the method if questions arose.

之後學生在音樂教室練習發表。一名年輕作曲者替校慶寫了短曲，老師給出進場提示，孩子們準時奏起。書寧發現，樂團能合作，不只因為每個人知道何時演奏，也因為知道何時聽。這幫助她改訂購單：不再由學校填一個總欄位，而是分成學生選款、確認尺寸、剪裁前最後核可三階段。這些階段合起來會形成可查紀錄，卻不收集不必要的私人資訊。若日後有疑問，一般民間委員也能審查流程。

On the way home, Xiulan asked whether offering more choices would make the work impossible. Shuning said it could, if they tried to promise every possible style. Their plan therefore kept a limited range of well-tested patterns and a clear route for unusual needs. At the shop, Yating pinned up the new order form and wrote two names beside it: the person making the choice and the person checking the record. Neither name was the shop owner's by default. The rule felt small, but it gave the future customers a place in the process. Shuning went to bed without knowing whether the committee would accept it. Uncertainty was not a defeat; it was the honest state of an unfinished decision.

回家路上，秀蘭問提供更多選擇會不會讓工作變得不可能。書寧說會，如果他們承諾做出所有想像得到的款式。因此方案只保留少數經測試的版型，特殊需求另有明確申請流程。回店後，雅婷把新訂購單釘牆上，旁邊寫兩個人：做選擇的人，以及覆核紀錄的人；兩個名字都不預設是店主。規則看似很小，卻讓未來穿制服的人在流程中有了位置。書寧入睡時還不知道委員會會不會接受。事情未定不等於失敗，只是決定尚未完成的真實狀態。
