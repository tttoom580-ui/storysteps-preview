渡輪上的試穿

The Ferry Fitting

1996，藍線裁縫舖的第二把尺 · 第 3 / 25 章 · 3000–4000 · 英中對照

用互動版查詞、朗讀與收藏

On Saturday, Shuning took the ferry to the outer island school, where students often missed the first morning boat. Teacher Luo had assigned her a quiet classroom for a fitting session. Shuning carried water, a notebook, and a box of raisin buns for the volunteers, each labeled with its calorie count for students who needed dietary information. She also brought a photography card showing how different sleeves moved when a person lifted a bag. A lifeguard at the pier offered to help with boarding, but Shuning declined to make him responsible for everyone. Instead, she checked the ramp herself and asked each family what assistance they wanted. The maximum number of students in the room would be six, because privacy mattered and everyone needed time to concentrate rather than rush. She wrote that limit on the door before anyone arrived.

星期六，書寧搭渡輪到外島學校。那裡的學生常趕不上清晨第一班船。羅老師借她一間安靜教室試穿。她帶了水、筆記本，以及給志工的葡萄乾麵包；每個麵包都標明熱量，供有飲食需求的學生參考，還有一張展示舉手提包時袖子如何移動的攝影卡。碼頭救生員主動表示願幫忙登船，但書寧不把所有人的安全都交給他。她自己檢查坡道，並逐一詢問家庭需要什麼協助。教室同時最多六人，因為隱私重要，也要讓每個人有時間專心，不必匆忙。她在學生抵達前就把上限寫在門上。

One mother arrived carrying her son's schoolbag and a list of housework he completed every evening. "He can do all this," she said, "but the uniform buttons defeat him." In the context of a fishing household, the boy's fingers worked with wet ropes more often than with tiny buttons. He did not want an expensive or luxurious jacket. He wanted to dress without help. Shuning asked him to proceed at his usual pace while she observed, and she offered three types of fasteners for him to try. When a shop assistant made a careless joke about his hands, Shuning stopped the fitting. "We defend a customer's choice here," she said. "We do not insult the person who has asked us for help."

一位母親帶著兒子的書包與家務清單來了。「這些他都能做，」她說，「偏偏制服鈕扣難倒他。」在漁家生活裡，男孩的手指更常碰濕繩索，不是小鈕扣。他不要昂貴華麗的外套，只想自己穿好衣服。書寧請他照平常速度操作，觀察後拿出三種扣具試用。助理隨口拿他的手開玩笑，書寧立即停下試穿：「我們在這裡維護顧客的選擇，不羞辱來求助的人。」

By noon the fitting session produced an incredible result: four students preferred different fasteners, yet all four garments could share the same basic pattern. The percentage of students who requested a change was higher than Shuning had expected, although the group was too small to represent the whole school. Luo called the result an inspiration for a wider trial. Shuning corrected her gently. "It's a useful beginning, not proof that every family agrees." A parent asked whether the school would treat these altered uniforms as inferior. Luo promised to bring the question to the principal, but she would not speak for the entire board. The students listened closely. Adults rarely admitted the boundary of their own authority in front of them.

中午時，試穿得出令人驚訝的結果：四名學生偏好四種不同扣具，卻能共用同一套基本紙樣。提出修改需求的學生比例比書寧預期高，但樣本太小，不能代表整所學校。羅老師說這是擴大試驗的靈感。書寧溫和提醒：「這是有用的開始，不等於每個家庭都同意。」一名家長問，學校會不會把改過的制服當成次等品。羅老師答應帶去問校長，卻不替整個董事會做保證。學生們聽得很仔細；大人在他們面前承認權限邊界，並不常見。

The afternoon brought an intermediate problem. One boy's jacket fit while standing but pulled against his shoulders when he sat at a desk. The new supplier had measured only his chest. Shuning used the school's ordinary wooden chairs to test movement rather than relying on a mirror. A volunteer suggested a quicker measurement; Shuning explained that a fast result would be useless if the child could not write comfortably. For the next assignment, each student would wear a sample at home and describe it after a normal school day. Their notes would be anonymous in the summary. The families could return the samples without explaining why, and no one would lose a place in the school queue.

下午遇到更進一步的問題：一名男孩站著時外套合身，坐到桌前卻拉緊肩膀。新供應商只量了胸圍。書寧改用學校平常的木椅試動作，不只照鏡子。有志工提議快速量完就好，書寧解釋，如果孩子不能舒服寫字，量得快也沒用。下一份試穿作業是讓學生把樣衣帶回家，經過正常上課日後描述感受。摘要會匿名呈現；家庭可以不說理由就退還樣衣，也不會因此失去候補順序。

On the return ferry, the assistant apologized for the joke. Shuning accepted the apology but asked her to write down a new rule for fittings: the person wearing the clothing has the final word about comfort. As the boat crossed the darkening water, the assistant noticed a student waving from the shore. He had finished buttoning his sample without his mother's help. It was a small achievement, and Shuning did not photograph him. She simply waved back. Her priority remained a reliable process, not a moving picture for the shop window. Back on the mainland, she sent Luo a plain report with numbers, unanswered questions, and the names of the families who had agreed to further contact.

回程船上，助理為玩笑道歉。書寧接受道歉，但請她把一條新規則寫下：穿衣服的人，對舒適與否有最後決定權。船行過暮色水面時，助理看見岸上一名學生向他們揮手。男孩已經不用母親幫忙就扣好了樣衣。這是小成就，書寧卻沒有拍照，只向他揮手。她的優先事項仍是可靠的流程，不是櫥窗裡一張動人的照片。回到本島，她寄給羅老師一份平實報告，列出數字、尚未回答的問題，以及同意後續聯絡的家庭姓名。
