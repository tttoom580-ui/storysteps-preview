四項量測

Four Measurements

1996，藍線裁縫舖的第二把尺 · 第 4 / 25 章 · 3000–4000 · 英中對照

The school agreed to let the trial continue, but requested a formal diagram of every altered seam. Shuning asked Jianhe to accompany an independent mechanic to inspect Blue Thread's old sewing machine. A loose belt made a dreadful noise, yet the machine itself was sound. The mechanic offered a manual and showed her where to tighten the guard without touching the motor. "A sewing machine is not a miracle," he said. "It needs an ordinary maintenance record." Shuning had thought the trial depended only on better designs. Now she saw that safe equipment was essential to producing them on time. Xiulan welcomed the inspection. She was tired of being called outstanding for working around preventable dangers. A functioning machine would be more useful than praise.

學校同意延續試辦，但要求每一處改縫都附正式圖解。書寧請建和陪同一位獨立機械師，檢查藍線的老縫紉機。鬆掉的皮帶發出刺耳聲響，但機身仍健全。機械師留下操作手冊，教她如何在不碰馬達的情況下緊固防護罩。「縫紉機不是奇蹟，」他說，「只是需要平常的保養紀錄。」書寧原以為試辦只靠好版型，現在才看見安全設備同樣是準時交貨的必要條件。秀蘭很歡迎檢查，她已厭倦別人稱讚她能克服本可避免的危險。正常運作的機器比稱讚有用。

Before lunch, an exchange program coordinator visited with a diplomat who was touring local schools. The guest spoke about a similar uniform project abroad and gave Shuning a draft of its guidelines. They were only a reference, not an order from the ministry. He offered a greeting to the students, then watched them draw lines on a paper pattern. One child called the stitching machine a satellite because its spinning wheel looked like an orbit. The guest laughed and asked if the design was experimental. Shuning answered that it was, but explained the limits: the trial measured comfort, fabric strength, and time, not long-term academic performance. She refused to give a magnificent speech about changing education when the evidence was still small.

午餐前，一名交流計畫協調員帶著參訪學校的外交官來到店裡。客人談起海外類似的制服計畫，留下指南草稿。那只是參考，不是教育主管機關的命令。他向學生親切問候，看著他們在紙樣上畫線。一名孩子說縫紉機像衛星，因為轉輪像在繞軌道。訪客笑著問這是不是實驗性設計。書寧說是，但清楚說明限制：試辦測的是舒適、布料強度與時間，並非長期學業表現。證據還太少，她不願發表一場宣稱改變教育的華麗演說。

In the afternoon, a child developed an itch beneath a new collar, and the parents feared the shared sample might carry a germ. His grandmother worried about germs from shared clothing. Shuning stopped the fitting, washed the test pieces, and labeled each one for a single wearer. There was no reason to flee the room or pretend that every itch was an infection. The child wanted a break; they gave it to him. While waiting, Shuning sketched four measurements on the diagram: shoulder movement, seated comfort, washing change, and labor time. Those four figures could strengthen the school's review. They would not prove that every pattern was safe, but they would make decisions less dependent on personal preference. When the boy returned, he chose a different collar and asked for another trial day.

下午，一名孩子穿上新領子後覺得發癢，家長擔心共用樣衣上會有細菌，祖母擔心共用衣物的細菌。書寧立刻暫停試穿，把試布洗乾淨，也替每片布標明專屬試穿者。沒必要倉皇逃離教室，也不能把所有發癢都當成感染。孩子想休息，他們就讓他休息。等待時，書寧在圖表上列出四項量測：肩部活動、坐姿舒適、洗滌變化、工時。這四組數據足以強化校方審查，雖不能保證所有版型安全，卻能減少單憑喜好的判斷。男孩回來後換了一種領口，要求再試一天。

That evening, Shuning found a hesitation in Xiulan's voice when they discussed the next week's orders. Her mother had been hiding a swollen finger beneath a thimble. "I can finish the work," Xiulan insisted. "I know," Shuning replied, "but I don't know whether you should." They agreed to loosen the deadline rather than work through another painful night. Jianhe offered to transport the samples in his van so Xiulan would not have to carry them to the ferry. The family also set aside one Sunday for recreation without discussing business. Xiulan smiled at the idea, though it seemed unfamiliar. Out beyond the harbor, the horizon grew silver under the moon. The family still faced an uncertain season, but the first safe change had begun inside its own workshop.

晚上，書寧聽見秀蘭談到下週訂單時遲疑的語氣。母親一直把腫脹的手指藏在頂針下面。「我能做完。」秀蘭堅持。「我知道，」書寧答，「但我不確定妳應不應該這樣做。」他們決定放寬期限，不再熬夜忍痛。建和答應開車送樣衣，讓秀蘭不用扛著貨去搭渡輪。家人也留下週日一天作休閒時間，不談生意。秀蘭對這個陌生的提議笑了。港外的地平線在月光下變成銀色。前路依然不確定，但第一項真正安全的改變，已經從家裡的工作室開始。

The following morning, Shuning returned to the school to watch the children move through the corridor during break. A new banner promised the attraction of fashionable uniforms, but the narrow doorway told a different story. Students carrying instruments moved slowly, and a boy on crutches had to stop when two groups met. Shuning drew the traffic pattern in her notebook. The exercise did not turn her into an architect or a doctor. It simply made clear why sleeves, pockets, and bags needed to be tested in the places where children actually used them. Luo agreed to mark the corridor with temporary tape during the next trial. The tape could be removed if it confused anyone. Shuning took the proposal home, where Xiulan suggested moving the fitting table away from the shop entrance for the same reason.

隔天早晨，書寧回學校觀察下課時孩子們穿過走廊的情況。新布條寫著「時尚制服的吸引力」，但狹窄的門口說的是另一件事。拿樂器的學生走得很慢，拄拐杖的男孩在兩群人相遇時只好停下。書寧把動線畫進筆記本。這不會讓她變成建築師或醫生，只讓她看清袖子、口袋與書包必須在孩子真正使用的空間裡測試。羅老師同意下次用可撕膠帶暫時標出走廊動線；若造成困擾，就立即移除。回家後秀蘭也建議把試穿桌移離店門口，理由相同。
