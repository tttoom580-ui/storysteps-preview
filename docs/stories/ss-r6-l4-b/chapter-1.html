The Second Ruler

1996，藍線裁縫舖的第二把尺 · 第 1 / 25 章 · 3000–4000 · 英中對照

用互動版查詞、朗讀與收藏

In November 1995, Xu Shuning returned to Wangpu carrying a wooden ruler, a cardboard case of patterns, and a cushion for her mother's sewing chair. Her parents lived in a cottage behind Blue Thread Tailors, two streets from the harbor. The shop window still displayed the navy uniforms her mother had cut for twenty-three years. Across the road, a fierce advertisement promised a complete school uniform for less than the price of one hand-sewn jacket. Shuning had worked as a pattern maker in Taichung. She knew the advertisement was not necessarily dishonest; large factories could lower costs. Yet the bright letters could not convey what happened when a standard jacket failed to fit a real child. She set down her case and asked to see the accounts before anyone made a decision.

一九九五年十一月，許書寧帶著木尺、裝滿紙樣的紙箱，以及給母親縫紉椅的坐墊回到望浦。父母住在藍線裁縫舖後方的小屋，距離港口只有兩條街。櫥窗還掛著母親剪裁了二十三年的深藍色制服。對街一面醒目的廣告說，一整套制服的價格比手縫外套還便宜。書寧曾在臺中擔任打版師，知道大型工廠確實能壓低成本，廣告未必說謊。但那行字說不出標準外套穿不上某個孩子時會發生什麼。她放下紙箱，要求先看帳本，再談任何決定。

Her father, Deyuan, called her a genius and tried to flatter her into promising a rescue. Her mother, Xiulan, gave a dry giggle. "I don't want a genius," she said. "I want someone who can read the electricity bill." Shuning found the bill under an exercise leaflet showing a parachute club's weekend workout. The club had once ordered embroidered badges from Blue Thread. Now even those small jobs had disappeared. Her brother, Jianhe, arrived with a visual presentation about selling the sewing machines. He had a reasonable defense: both parents were tired, and the shop had lost money for seven consecutive months. Shuning refused to turn his concern into a family quarrel. She wanted to know whether the machines were the burden or the way they were being used.

父親德元稱她是天才，想用稱讚換得她一句「我會救店」。母親秀蘭乾笑兩聲：「我不要天才，我要會看電費單的人。」書寧在一張跳傘社團週末體能訓練傳單下找到帳單。那個社團以前會向藍線訂做繡章，現在連這種小訂單也沒了。哥哥建和帶來圖表簡報，主張賣掉縫紉機。他的理由並非無理：父母太累，店裡連續七個月虧損。書寧沒有把他的擔憂當成吵架的藉口。她想分清楚，究竟是機器拖累了家庭，還是經營機器的方式出了問題。

That afternoon, school counselor Luo visited with a student, Pei An, who wore a leg brace. The uniform supplier's samples were nearly identical, but the stiff seams pressed against An's brace when she sat down. Her discomfort was not a matter of fashion. Luo placed a written reference on the counter: the school would allow a trial of alternative patterns, provided the clothing passed the same safety tests. "The students possess different bodies," Luo said. "A school should be prepared for that diversity." Shuning asked An whether she wished to be measured. An hesitated, then agreed on the condition that her measurements would not appear in a public presentation. Shuning accepted immediately. A fitting was a private conversation, not a public performance.

下午，學校輔導老師羅老師帶著學生佩安來訪。佩安穿著腿部支架，供應商的樣衣幾乎一模一樣，但硬縫線在她坐下時壓住支架。這不是愛不愛漂亮的問題。羅老師放上一封書面說明：只要符合相同的安全標準，校方願意試穿不同版型。「學生的身體各不相同，學校應該容納這種差異。」羅老師說。書寧先問佩安願不願意量尺寸。佩安猶豫後同意，條件是尺寸不能放進公開簡報。書寧立刻答應：量身是私人對話，不是公開表演。

A neighbor, who had watched the fitting from the door, said he was pessimistic about the experiment. "You can't make a tremendous business out of exceptions." Shuning resisted the urge to answer sharply. Instead, she asked him to be a witness as she removed an old photograph from the window. The child in the photograph was now an adult and had requested its removal two months earlier. Leaving it on display was a violation of a customer's wishes, whatever the law might say. Shuning placed the photograph in a labeled envelope. The small act mattered because she did not want her plan to depend on claiming authority over anyone's body or image. Even a useful sample needed permission.

一位鄰居站在門口看完量身，悲觀地說：「靠少數例外，做不出多大的生意。」書寧忍住反駁的衝動，反而請他見證自己撤下櫥窗裡一張舊照片。照片中的孩子如今已成年，兩個月前就要求撤照，店裡卻仍擺著。無論法律怎麼規定，這都是違背顧客意願的做法。書寧把照片放進標明姓名的信封。這個小動作很重要：她不願靠替別人決定身體或形象來經營，即使是有用的樣品，也應先取得同意。

At supper, Xiulan said her true wish was neither a heroic victory nor a spiritual duty to preserve the family name. She wanted Sunday afternoons free, and she wanted to stop hiding pain in her fingers. Jianhe wanted their parents to have savings; Deyuan wanted his old customers treated with care. Shuning offered a proposal: six weeks to test two adjustable uniform patterns, record every paid hour, and compare the results with the new supplier's price. Nobody would borrow money or sign a sale agreement during that period without a family discussion. An's fitting would be the first test, but An could withdraw whenever she wished. After a long silence, Xiulan brought out a second wooden ruler. "Then use mine," she said. "And listen when I tell you a measurement is wrong."

晚餐時，秀蘭說，她真正的願望既不是轟轟烈烈地救店，也不是替祖先守業的精神義務。她想要週日下午的自由，也不想再隱瞞手指疼痛。建和希望父母有積蓄；德元希望老顧客受照顧。書寧提議試辦六週：製作兩種可調式制服版型、記錄每一個有薪工時，再與新供應商價格比較。期間不得未經家庭討論就借錢或簽賣店契約。佩安的試穿會是第一件，但她隨時可以退出。沉默許久後，秀蘭取出第二把木尺：「那就用我的。量錯的時候，也要聽我說。」
