A Decision with Conditions

1996，藍線裁縫舖的第二把尺 · 第 10 / 25 章 · 3000–4000 · 英中對照 · 校訂中

The school committee's first hearing filled the town hall with parents, suppliers, teachers, and a few local reporters. Shuning prepared a diagram to illustrate the three-stage fitting process and helped install a sample pattern on a wooden display. Yang Huilan brought a competing price table. An elected member rose to congratulate both women on doing more work than the committee had expected, but warned that the budget was critical. The school could not afford a dramatic resolution that collapsed when the first invoices arrived. Shuning agreed. She had not come to demand a revolution. She had come to ask that students be offered a practical choice within a published cost limit, with refunds and safety checks stated in plain language.

學校委員會第一次聽證會擠滿家長、供應商、老師和地方記者。書寧準備用來說明三階段試穿的圖表，並協助把紙樣安裝到木展示架上。慧蘭帶來競爭報價。民選委員起身恭喜兩人做了超過預期的工作，但提醒預算非常緊要。學校負擔不起第一張帳單來就崩潰的戲劇性決議。書寧同意。她不是來要求革命，而是希望在公開預算上限內給學生實用選擇，並把退費及安全檢查寫成人人看得懂的條款。

The hearing turned uneasy when a parent accused the school of planning to forbid cheaper jackets. Luo offered a brief apology for the confusing notice. The committee had not forbidden them; it had postponed all final purchases until testing was complete. A surgeon from the district clinic spoke about the importance of a sleeve that could be rolled up without strain. She mentioned a pregnant nurse who wore a special apron during work, but she did not reveal the woman's name. The surgeon's example was about fit, not a medical order for students. Shuning appreciated the care. Too many people in the room were eager to transform one personal example into a rule for everyone.

一名家長指控學校計畫禁止購買便宜外套，會場氣氛緊張。羅老師為先前不清楚的通知簡短道歉：學校不是禁止，而是在測試完成前暫緩最終採購。區域診所的外科醫師談起袖子能輕鬆捲起的重要性，也提到一位孕期護理師工作時需要特殊圍裙，但沒有透露姓名。醫師舉的例子關於合身，不是替學生下醫囑。書寧很欣賞這份謹慎。會場裡太多人想把某個人的例子，直接變成所有人的規定。

At the back of the hall, a history teacher had placed a small exhibit on local civilization: a settler's cloth bag, a drawing of an old harbor tomb, and a photograph of the first school building. Beside it, the science club displayed a poster about an explosion in a laboratory and the bandage instructions used during drills. None of these objects offered more than a mere historical comparison or directly proved what uniform should be bought. They reminded the committee that a school served many purposes and that safety had to be specific. The chair insisted on keeping relevant and irrelevant evidence separate. Shuning felt relieved. An object from the past could inspire a question, but it could not answer a present contract by itself.

會場後方，歷史老師設了一處地方文明小展：早期移居者的布袋、港口古墓的繪圖和第一所校舍照片。科學社也展出實驗室爆炸事故海報，以及演練時的繃帶使用說明。這些物件至多只是歷史對照，不能直接證明該買哪一套制服，卻提醒委員會：學校有許多功能，安全要具體討論。主席堅持區分相關與不相關的證據。書寧鬆了口氣。過去的東西可以啟發問題，卻無法單獨回答現在的合約。

One committee member asked whether the school needed a website to publish the trial results. The computer teacher explained that the local college had a small website reached through a slow telephone connection. Most families would not have access, so paper notices would remain the official channel. The plan was not glamorous, but it was fairer. Then Yang pointed to a suspicious number in Blue Thread's estimate: machine maintenance appeared only once. Jianhe stood up and admitted he had overlooked the annual cost. Shuning corrected the total on the board before speaking again. For a moment, her face burned. She could either protect the presentation or protect the truth; she chose the second.

有委員問學校是否該建立網站公開試辦結果。電腦老師說地方學院有個需要慢速電話連線的簡易網站，但大部分家庭無法上網，所以紙本公告仍應是正式管道。計畫不華麗，卻更公平。接著慧蘭指出藍線估價中的一個可疑數字：機器保養費只算一次。建和起身承認漏算年度費用。書寧立刻在黑板上修正總額，才繼續發言。她的臉一陣發燙。她可以保護簡報，也可以保護真相；她選了後者。

At sunset, the committee issued a provisional resolution. Even in a relatively prosperous district, the committee recognized that some families needed assistance. For ninety days, the school would allow two approved uniform designs, keep the low-cost standard option, and pay a small alteration voucher to families who met an openly stated need. All suppliers could submit materials for independent testing. No company would receive an exclusive contract during the trial. The decision was a bargain, not a victory parade. Several parents remained uncertain about the practical details. Xiulan listened quietly while Shuning read it aloud. "I can work ninety days," her mother said, "but not ninety nights." Shuning wrote that condition in the shop's own plan. A fair public policy would mean little if her family's private workplace continued to demand hidden sacrifices.

日落時，委員會作出暫行決議：即使在相對富裕的地區，委員會也承認某些家庭需要協助。試辦九十天，學校准許兩種合格版型，保留低價標準款，並向符合公開條件的家庭提供少額修改補助券。所有供應商都可送件接受獨立檢驗。試辦期間不給任何公司獨家合約。這是一場協商，不是勝利遊行。仍有幾位家長對執行細節感到不確定。秀蘭聽書寧念完，安靜地說：「九十個白天我可以，九十個晚上不行。」書寧把這條件寫入自家工作計畫。若家庭工作室仍要求隱形犧牲，再公平的公共政策也沒有意義。
