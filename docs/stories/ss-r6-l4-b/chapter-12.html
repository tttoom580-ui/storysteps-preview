失蹤的送貨單

The Missing Delivery Note

1996，藍線裁縫舖的第二把尺 · 第 12 / 25 章 · 3000–4000 · 英中對照 · 校訂中

用互動版查詞、朗讀與收藏

The first joint fabric delivery arrived with six heavy bins of cloth, each bin carrying a clear weight label, and a small wooden box of buttons. The agent who drove the truck asked Yating to sign a receipt before unloading. She refused politely because the procedure required a count first. In the warehouse, Shuning noticed that one box had been opened and resealed. Nothing clearly proved a burglar had entered; the tape might have broken during loading. She asked the driver to stay while they examined the contents. A silver bracelet lay under the invoice, apparently forgotten by a worker. Shuning put it in a labeled envelope instead of treating it as a clue to someone's guilt. The missing delivery note was the greater problem: without it they could not confirm who had accepted the cloth.

第一批聯合採購的布送來時，卡車上有六大箱布，每箱都標上重量，還有一個裝鈕扣的木盒。司機兼送貨代理要求雅婷先簽收再卸貨。她禮貌拒絕，因為規定應先清點。倉庫裡，書寧發現一箱被打開又封回，卻沒有證據證明遭竊；封箱膠帶也可能運送時破裂。她請司機留下來一起核對。發票下還有一只銀手環，像是工人遺忘的。書寧把它裝進標籤信封，不把它當成指控誰有罪的線索。真正的大問題是送貨單不見了；沒有單據，就無法確認誰接收了布。

Jianhe called Yang, who was clearly upset but did not dismiss the report. A definite answer required counting every roll against the purchase order. They spread the cloth on clean tables and recorded the lengths, colors, and condition. Three rolls were short by nearly a meter, while the others were correct. Yang made a comment that small errors could happen at a busy warehouse, but she also acknowledged the consequence for a tailor working with narrow margins. She promised a replacement only after her own records were checked. Shuning agreed to wait, though the delay would create hardship for the families waiting on uniforms. She decided to commit the disputed quantities to a separate ledger rather than blend them with accepted stock.

建和打電話給慧蘭，慧蘭很不高興，卻沒有駁回報告。確定答案需要逐捲對照採購單。他們把布攤在乾淨桌上，記下長度、顏色與狀態。三捲各短了將近一公尺，其餘正確。慧蘭評論說忙碌倉庫難免出小錯，也承認這對利潤微薄的裁縫影響很大。她要等查完自家紀錄才答應補貨。書寧同意等待，雖然延誤會讓等制服的家庭難受。她決定把爭議數量記入獨立帳本，不與已收貨品混在一起。

That afternoon, the school history club visited the workshop to collect stories from elderly residents. A student asked Deyuan whether tailoring was an art from a vanished era. He laughed and pointed to a durable work apron he had sewn twenty years earlier. "This has survived more fashion changes than I can remember," he said. Another student mentioned the fame of a local singer whose early costumes Xiulan had repaired. Xiulan refused to turn the singer's private fittings into a public story without permission. Her faithful memory of the work was not a license to disclose personal details. The students recorded what she chose to share: the cut of the sleeves, the time spent repairing seams, and the price she had charged.

下午，學校歷史社來工作室訪問長者。一名學生問裁縫是不是消失時代的藝術。德元大笑，指著二十年前做的耐用工作圍裙：「這件衣服活過的時尚變化，比我記得的還多。」另一名學生提起一位成名歌手，早年服裝曾由秀蘭修補。秀蘭拒絕在未經同意下把私人試衣過程講給大家。她忠實記得工作，不代表能公開私事。學生最後記錄的是她願分享的部分：袖子的剪法、修補時間和當年收費。

In the evening, a rumor began to circulate that the warehouse staff had committed a crime. Shuning warned the family not to repeat it. The opened box and missing paper did not amount to proof of theft or deliberate destruction. Yang's records showed the truck had stopped at a second loading bay, where another clerk had been asked to occupy the office during a shift change. The workers were under pressure from rising inflation and unpredictable orders, but pressure alone did not establish what had happened. Shuning chose to pursue a reconstruction of the delivery route and an ordinary inventory check. A tragic accusation could ruin a person's livelihood; she would not make one simply because a story sounded dramatic.

晚上，鎮上開始傳出倉庫員工犯罪的流言。書寧提醒家人不要轉述。箱子開過、紙張不見，還不足以證明偷竊或故意破壞。慧蘭的紀錄顯示卡車曾停另一個裝貨區，交班時另一名職員受命暫時使用辦公室。工人承受通貨膨脹和訂單不穩的壓力，但壓力本身不能證明事件如何發生。書寧決定追查並重建運送路線，也做平常盤點。一項悲劇性的指控可能毀掉人的生計，她不會只因故事戲劇化就下結論。

The next day, Shuning watched the missing note emerge from a folder used for a tribal cultural festival order. A clerk had filed it beside a similarly numbered invoice, then forgotten to move it after a late shift. The bracelet belonged to that clerk, who thanked Shuning for returning it. The short cloth was a separate packing mistake, and Yang provided replacements. Shuning documented both findings, including the parts still uncertain. The incident left the workshop tired, but it changed their habits. A delivery would no longer be accepted by trust alone, and a missing piece of paper would no longer become a reason to accuse someone. Shuning told Yating that careful work sometimes meant slowing down in front of an impatient crowd.

隔天，書寧看著失蹤的送貨單從一份原住民文化節訂單資料夾裡被找出。職員因編號相近誤放，夜班後又忘了移回。銀手環也屬於她，她向書寧道謝。短碼布料是另一項包裝失誤，慧蘭同意補足。書寧把兩項發現都寫成紀錄，包括仍不確定的部分。這件事讓大家疲憊，卻改變了習慣：收貨不能只憑信任，少一張紙也不能立刻把人當嫌犯。她告訴雅婷，仔細工作有時就是要在催促的人群前慢下來。
