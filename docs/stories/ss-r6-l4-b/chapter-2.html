Cloth Has a History

1996，藍線裁縫舖的第二把尺 · 第 2 / 25 章 · 3000–4000 · 英中對照

The next morning, Shuning visited the district museum with Xiulan's old fabric sample book. Its pages held an astonishing landscape of colors: sea-blue, clay-red, and the faded green of a mill that had closed years earlier. A retired curator, Lin, described the harbor's first textile voyage, when bolts of cloth had arrived by fishing boat rather than truck. In the museum garden stood a replica fort built from rough stone. Beyond it, a fossil display attracted children on a school trip. Shuning had not come for decoration. She wanted to learn why the old uniforms survived years of washing while a cheap new sample had split after three days. Mr. Lin suggested testing the thread before judging it by appearance.

隔天早晨，書寧帶著秀蘭的舊布樣冊去了地方博物館。冊頁裡有海藍、土紅，還有多年前停業紡織廠留下的褪綠色，像一幅布料風景。退休館員林先生談起港口早期的布匹運送：那時布是靠漁船而不是卡車進來的。庭院裡有仿製石堡，一旁的化石展正吸引校外教學的孩子。書寧不是為了尋找漂亮花樣，而是想知道舊制服為何洗了多年仍結實，新樣布卻三天就裂開。林先生建議先測線，再談外觀。

At the science classroom, students were preparing a fair about the atom. One poster showed an atomic diagram, while another explained the combination of heat, water, and detergent that weakens some dyes. Their teacher laughed when Shuning asked whether schoolchildren could solve a tailoring problem. "They can at least ask useful questions," she said. The class placed delicate strips of cloth in labeled jars and recorded how much each one stretched. Their experiment could not guarantee the strength of an entire jacket, but it revealed a surprising extent of shrinkage in the cheapest fabric. Shuning let her gaze follow their careful hands rather than pretending to understand every calculation. She wrote down the limits of their test beside its results.

學校科學教室正在準備原子主題展。一張海報畫著原子結構，另一張解釋熱、水與清潔劑的組合如何使某些染料變弱。書寧問小學生是否真能幫忙解決裁縫問題，老師笑說：「至少能問出好問題。」學生把細緻布條放進標籤瓶，記錄伸長程度。實驗不能保證整件外套的強度，卻揭露最便宜布料縮水程度出乎意料。書寧的目光追隨孩子們仔細操作的手，不假裝自己懂得全部計算。她把試驗限制和結果寫在一起。

During lunch, museum volunteer Sari brought her daughter to pick up a repaired bag. Sari had moved to Taiwan through immigration several years earlier and now worked as a messenger between ships and warehouses. She spoke about how intimate a uniform can feel: it touches the skin all day, but strangers often decide its shape. Her daughter gave a shy murmur when asked whether she liked her school's stiff collar. "I keep scratching it," she whispered. Shuning was tempted to promise a soft replacement immediately, yet she knew that promises needed materials and time. She asked for permission to retain a small piece of the worn collar and compare it with the sample book. Sari agreed, provided she could see the final report.

午餐時，博物館志工莎莉帶女兒來取修好的提袋。莎莉幾年前移居臺灣，現在替船舶與倉庫傳遞文件。她說制服其實非常貼身，一整天碰著皮膚，版型卻常由陌生人決定。女兒被問及硬領子是否舒服時，輕聲嘟囔：「一直會刺癢。」書寧很想馬上答應替換成柔軟領子，卻知道承諾也要有材料和時間。她詢問能否留下一小段磨損領布，拿來和布樣冊比較。莎莉同意，條件是自己要能看最後報告。

Back at the shop, Xiulan used a magnifying glass to inspect the fibers, then said they needed to revise the sewing order. A productive afternoon did not mean rushing. First she would wash a test patch, then press it, and only then cut the full pattern. Jianhe disliked the extra step because it looked expensive on the accounts. Shuning showed him how one returned jacket could cost more than ten careful tests. A second question concerned psychological comfort: children who feared standing out might refuse a perfectly useful alternative design. Shuning decided to invite them to choose between plain adjustments and visible changes. She did not want a special uniform to become a public label. Her mother's face relaxed when she saw the new list.

回到店裡，秀蘭用放大鏡檢查纖維，隨即說必須修訂縫製次序。有效率的下午不等於趕工：先洗試布、再熨燙，最後才裁完整紙樣。建和覺得這一步在帳上看來很貴。書寧讓他看，一件退貨的成本可能高過十次仔細測試。還有心理感受問題：害怕與眾不同的孩子，可能拒絕明明很實用的改良制服。她決定提供外觀不變的調整與明顯改款兩種選擇，不讓特殊制服成為公開標籤。母親看到新的流程單，神情終於放鬆。

That evening, Deyuan found a short note Xiulan had tucked into the sample book twenty years before. She had written that fabric changes with people, weather, and labor; no pattern remains perfect forever. He read it aloud, then admitted that he had treated his wife's knowledge as something the family could use without paying for it. Shuning added her mother's time to the trial budget. She also put Mr. Lin's historical notes in a separate folder, clearly marked as background rather than scientific proof. They would not claim that old cloth was automatically better. By the end of the week, they would have three washed samples, two fitting appointments, and a genuine question to take back to the school: which design stayed comfortable after a full day?

晚上，德元在樣冊裡找到秀蘭二十年前寫的一張紙：布會隨人、天氣和勞動而改變，沒有任何紙樣能永遠完美。他念出來，也承認自己一直把妻子的本事當成不用付錢的家庭資源。書寧把母親的工時列入試辦預算。她也把林先生的歷史筆記另放一個資料夾，註明那是背景，不是科學證據。他們不會因為布料老，就宣稱它一定比較好。到週末，店裡將有三份洗過的樣布、兩次試穿預約，以及一個要帶回學校的真問題：哪一種衣服穿一整天仍然舒服？
