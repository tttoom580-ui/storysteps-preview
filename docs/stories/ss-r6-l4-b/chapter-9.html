A Refund in Public

1996，藍線裁縫舖的第二把尺 · 第 9 / 25 章 · 3000–4000 · 英中對照 · 校訂中

用互動版查詞、朗讀與收藏

One Monday, a student arrived with a jacket whose sleeve had been attached at an awkward angle. His mother wanted a refund, not a long apology. Yating frowned because she had helped sew the sleeve and feared she would lose her wages. Shuning asked her to explain the circumstances. The jacket had been altered after an interruption in the workshop, when a telephone call brought urgent news from the harbor. That circumstance explained the confusion but did not excuse it. Nobody had recorded who resumed the order. "A mistake can happen," Shuning said, "but we must not mislead a customer about why it happened." She offered the mother a full refund or a new jacket made without charge. The mother chose the refund and requested a written explanation.

星期一，一名學生拿來袖子縫得歪斜的外套。他母親要的是退費，不是漫長道歉。雅婷皺起眉頭，因為那條袖子她也縫過，擔心因此被扣薪。書寧請她說明情況：工作室曾因港口急電中斷，後來由誰接手訂單卻沒記錄。這項情況解釋混亂，卻不能當作推卸責任的理由。「失誤可能發生，」書寧說，「但我們不能對顧客誤導原因。」她提供全額退款或免費重做兩種選擇。母親選擇退費，並要求書面說明。

At lunch, Deyuan suggested solving the problem quietly so Blue Thread would not lose face. Jianhe argued that a public record could damage the shop's economic future. Shuning disagreed with both. Their content should include the actual mistake, the customer's remedy, and the prevention step, without naming the student. She decided to attach a notice to the repair book and let Yating help write the rules that would instruct future workers. Yating admitted she had been clumsy with the new machine, but the worktable had also been left crowded and no second check was assigned. Shuning did not treat the apprentice's modesty as proof she deserved punishment. She wanted to repair the system that allowed one human error to reach a customer.

午餐時德元建議私下解決，免得藍線丟臉。建和擔心公開紀錄傷害經濟前途。書寧都不同意：說明內容應包括實際錯誤、顧客補救與預防步驟，但不揭露學生姓名。她決定把公告附進修補簿，請雅婷一起寫下日後指導工人的規則。雅婷承認自己操作新機時笨拙，但工作桌也太擠，並且沒分配第二次覆核。書寧不把學徒的謙遜視為該受罰的證明，她想修正容許單一人為失誤直接送到客人手上的流程。

The next day, Shuning presented the mistake at a meeting with other small tailors. One shopkeeper began to lean back and gave a long frown. Yating passed around crunchy rice crackers without interrupting him. "If you announce every error, customers will peep into every corner of our shops." Another shopkeeper, who spoke with a strong southern accent, said he had returned money to three families after a similar problem. His honesty changed the atmosphere. They discussed how to distinguish a routine alteration from a manufacturing fault, and when a refund should be automatic. For the first time, Shuning thought of a shared repair code between shops. It would not be revolutionary because it sounded bold; it might be revolutionary because a child would no longer need to argue alone for a correct sleeve.

隔天，書寧在小裁縫同業會議上提出失誤。一位店主皺眉向後靠。雅婷默默遞上酥脆米餅，不打斷他說話。那人說：「錯事都公告，客人不是會想偷看我們每個角落？」另一位帶南部口音的店主說，他因類似問題替三個家庭退過錢。他的坦率改變氣氛。大家開始討論如何區分一般修改與製造瑕疵，以及何時應自動退費。書寧第一次想到不同店可以共用一套修補規範。它不會因為說法激進就成為革命性措施，而可能因為孩子不必獨自爭論一條袖子，才真正帶來改變。

At home, a radio program about the homeland and a new nuclear power debate played softly through Yating's earphone. The host spoke of old crafts with such warmth that Xiulan gave a wink to her daughter. "They would love our story," she said. Shuning smiled, then asked a harder question: what could they honestly claim? Their first trial had shown improved comfort in a small group, but they had not proven long-term durability or a profit large enough to support every worker. The conclusion made the evening less exciting. Still, the mother who had requested a refund returned the next morning with a new order for her younger child. She said she did not require perfection. She required a shop that would correct a failure without making her feel small.

回家時雅婷耳機裡播著談故鄉及核能議題的廣播節目，主持人說得十分溫暖，秀蘭向女兒眨眼：「他們會喜歡我們的故事。」書寧笑了，卻問更困難的問題：他們真能主張什麼？第一輪試辦只顯示少數學生舒適改善，還沒證明長期耐用，也沒證明利潤足以支撐每名員工。這結論讓晚上少了些興奮。但要求退費的母親隔天又來，替年幼孩子下了新訂單。她說自己不要求完美，只要店家出錯時能改正，不讓她覺得卑微。

Shuning revised the return form before closing. It now had boxes for fault type, chosen remedy, and the person who verified the result. The customer could keep a copy, and the shop would keep another. The family agreed that wages would not be deducted automatically from an apprentice who reported a mistake. Deliberate dishonesty would be handled separately. Jianhe checked the final figures: the refund cost money, but the two-hour meeting had produced a clear procedure that might prevent more losses. Shuning placed the note on the wall beside her mother's second ruler. It was an unremarkable Tuesday, and that was precisely why the work mattered. Ordinary days were when the new habits had to survive.

打烊前，書寧修訂退貨單，增加瑕疵種類、顧客選擇的補救方式與覆核者姓名。客人和店各保留一份。一家人同意，學徒主動通報失誤，不得因此自動扣薪；故意不誠實則另行處理。建和檢查最後數字：退費確實花錢，但兩小時會議建立的明確流程，也許能防止更大的損失。書寧把新規定貼在母親第二把尺旁邊。這只是平凡的星期二，卻正因此重要。新習慣必須能在平常日子裡活下來。
