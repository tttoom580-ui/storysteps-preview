A Portable Stage

1996，藍線裁縫舖的第二把尺 · 第 5 / 25 章 · 3000–4000 · 英中對照

By early December, a school concert offered the workshop an unexpected test. The orchestra needed portable cloth panels to soften the hall's echo, and the music teacher wanted simple uniforms for students who performed while seated. Shuning was not their idol, nor did she pretend to be a stage designer. She listened to the teacher's objection that heavy fabric might block a fire exit. Then she built a small model from paper and thread, showing how the panels could fold away quickly. Her mother suggested an imaginative pattern that used short, repeated seams. The work was distinct from the school uniform order, but it tested the same question: could flexible sewing make a public space safer without making people look as though they needed special treatment?

十二月初，學校音樂會帶給工作室一場意外的考驗。管弦樂團需要可攜式布幕降低禮堂回音，音樂老師也希望替坐著演奏的學生製作簡單制服。書寧不是大家崇拜的舞台設計師，也不假裝是。她先聽老師反對厚布遮住消防出口的理由，再用紙和線做小模型，展示布幕如何快速收折。母親提出富想像力的短線縫法。這份工作不同於學校制服訂單，卻在測試相同的問題：有彈性的縫製能否讓公共空間更安全，又不把需要調整的人當成特例？

The family could not afford to abandon ordinary repairs for the concert. Jianhe drew a timetable and had to adjust the labor allowance after counting Xiulan's hours correctly. A student borrowed a device that could enlarge the pattern onto the wall so volunteers could trace it. Another student decorated the trial panel with a canoe from the island school's emblem. When the teacher worried the drawing might look childish, the student explained an idiom his grandfather used: every boat needs a shore. The canoe represented safe return, not decoration for its own sake. The student had counted every syllable in the motto so it could be sung clearly. Shuning kept the drawing, but asked the student to sign a simple permission note. Using another person's art required agreement, even when no money changed hands.

家裡不能為音樂會放棄一般修補訂單。建和畫出時程表，在正確計入秀蘭工時後必須調整人力津貼。學生借來一台可以放大紙樣的裝置，把紙樣投到牆上，讓志工描線。另一位學生在試幕上畫了外島學校標誌的獨木舟。老師擔心圖案太孩子氣，學生解釋祖父常說的成語：「每條船都需要岸。」獨木舟代表平安回來，不只是裝飾。學生還數過座右銘裡的每個音節，讓大家能清楚唱出來。書寧保留圖案，卻請學生簽簡單授權。即使沒有金錢往來，使用別人的畫也需要同意。

On rehearsal day, the first panel slipped from its stand, making the conductor seize his music sheets before they fell. No one was hurt, but the impact frightened the younger players. Shuning felt anxiety rise in her throat. An organizer proposed hiding the mistake so that the school's image would remain favorable. Shuning refused. She recorded the failed connection, removed the panel, and called a short meeting with teachers and technicians. The new frame would need two safety ties rather than one. Their prosperity as a business would mean nothing if they ignored an obvious risk. The teacher, who had expected an argument, thanked her for treating the problem seriously instead of turning it into a contest of pride.

彩排當天，第一片布幕從架上滑下，指揮趕緊抓住快掉落的樂譜。無人受傷，但聲響嚇到年幼團員，書寧喉頭湧上一陣焦慮。主辦人提議隱瞞失誤，好讓學校形象維持良好。書寧拒絕，記下鬆脫接點、撤下布幕，再請老師與技術員開短會。新框架需要兩條固定帶，而不是一條。若忽略明顯風險，生意再興旺也沒有意義。原本以為她會爭辯的老師，反而感謝她認真處理問題，不把它變成面子之爭。

The revised panels passed a second test, but the final approval remained with the school's safety officer. Parents gathered at the door to protest the first failure. Shuning answered their questions without pretending she could remove every danger. One parent complained that the shop was taking advantage of the students. Another said the project had taught his daughter more than a month of slogans about responsibility. The discussion changed when the safety officer demonstrated how the panels could be taken down in seconds. Shuning did not ask anyone to applaud. She simply gave each family a written account of what had happened and what had been changed. For the first time, a difficult public conversation did not end with everyone choosing a side.

改過的布幕通過第二次測試，但最後核准仍由學校安全人員決定。家長在門口聚集，抗議第一次失誤。書寧逐一回答，沒有假裝能清除所有危險。一名家長抱怨店家利用學生，另一名家長說這個計畫教會女兒的，比一個月的責任口號還多。安全人員當場示範布幕幾秒內可以拆卸，討論才逐漸改變。書寧沒有要求大家鼓掌，只交給每個家庭一份書面說明，寫清楚發生什麼、改過什麼。第一次，艱難的公開討論沒有以眾人分邊站作結。

The concert ended with a quiet song rather than a grand celebration. Xiulan watched from the back row, her injured hand resting in her lap. Afterward, Jianhe counted the real income and found the job had earned a modest profit even after paid hours were included. Shuning placed the figures beside the uniform trial's results. These were two different markets, not proof that the old shop could survive anything. Yet they showed one possibility worth testing further: careful design, transparent costs, and clear consent could be services of their own. On the way home, Xiulan said she would teach the younger workers the new seam, but only on mornings when her fingers did not hurt. Nobody argued with that condition.

音樂會以一首安靜的曲子結束，沒有盛大慶功。秀蘭坐在後排，受傷的手放在膝上。散場後，建和算出真正收入；即使把工時薪資計入，工作仍有一點利潤。書寧把數字和制服試辦結果並列。這是兩種不同市場，不能證明老店什麼都撐得過去，但至少顯示一條值得繼續測的路：仔細設計、透明成本、明確同意，本身就能成為服務。回家途中，秀蘭說願意教年輕工人新縫法，但只能在手指不痛的上午。這一次，沒有人反對她的條件。
