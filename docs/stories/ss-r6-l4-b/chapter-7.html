後門的水盆

The Basin at the Back Door

1996，藍線裁縫舖的第二把尺 · 第 7 / 25 章 · 3000–4000 · 英中對照 · 校訂中

Yating's first paid week began with an academic visitor who wanted to examine the trial rather than praise it. Professor He arrived with an accountant and an unusually plain briefcase. He offered admiration for the careful records, but then asked a question that might annoy Deyuan: "Where is the cost of the water used for washing samples?" Nobody had written it down. Shuning took him to a basin near the back door. It held rinse water from one afternoon's work. The family had treated the water as free because the bill came once every two months. The accountant showed them how to distribute the expense fairly. Shuning thanked both visitors, although she disliked hearing how incomplete her figures had been.

雅婷第一個有薪工作週開始時，一位學者來訪，目的不是稱讚，而是檢查試辦。何教授和一名會計師提著樸素公事包到店。他欣賞詳實紀錄，卻問了可能讓德元不高興的問題：「洗樣布的水費記在哪裡？」沒人記。書寧領他到後門的水盆，盆裡是午後沖洗過的水。家裡一直把水當免費資源，只因帳單兩個月才來一次。會計師教他們如何合理分攤。書寧向兩人道謝，雖然聽見數字仍不完整時，她心裡並不好受。

In the afternoon, the chemistry teacher from the school brought a small aquarium. It was not intended as proof of environmental damage. She used it to demonstrate how a closed system changes when even a little soap is added. A fish should not be put at risk for a classroom lesson, so the tank contained only colored beads and water. The teacher, fluent in both chemistry and ordinary language, explained a characteristic of the new fabric: some treatments stayed on its surface after washing. Her students had drawn charts showing the contribution of each ingredient to a garment's feel. Shuning took notes, then asked whether their tests were relevant to skin safety or only to water quality. "Mostly the second," the teacher answered. The distinction prevented a tempting but false claim.

下午，學校化學老師帶來一個小水族箱，不是為了證明污染，而是用它示範密閉系統加一點肥皂就會發生什麼變化。她不願用活魚冒險，所以箱裡只有彩珠和水。精通化學、也擅長淺白說明的老師解釋新布料的一項特性：有些處理劑洗後仍留在表面。學生畫的圖表顯示不同成分對布料手感的貢獻。書寧記下，再問測試與皮膚安全有關，還是只能說明水質。「主要是後者。」老師答。這個區分阻止了書寧提出誘人卻不正確的說法。

The professor also questioned the shop's plans for expansion. If Blue Thread hired four more workers before it secured orders, its costs could defeat the entire trial. Xiulan agreed. She preferred a small, reliable workshop to a prime location with expensive rent. Shuning felt fortunate to have someone challenge her assumptions before she signed a lease. She asked Yating what would motivate her to stay for another month. "A chance to learn pattern cutting," the apprentice said, "not only to pull loose threads." They wrote a training schedule with clear pay for study hours. Deyuan thought his old rhythm would be disrupted, but he agreed to teach one afternoon a week. He was learning that knowledge could be shared without surrendering the shop's identity.

教授也質疑擴張計畫。若藍線還沒確定訂單就先雇四名工人，成本可能讓試辦失敗。秀蘭同意，她寧可經營可靠的小工作室，也不要租昂貴的黃金地段。書寧慶幸在簽租約前有人挑戰自己的假設。她問雅婷什麼能讓她下月繼續留下。「有機會學打版，而不只是剪線頭。」學徒答。他們寫下訓練時程，學習時數同樣付薪。德元覺得自己的老節奏會被打亂，仍答應每週教一個下午。他開始懂得，分享本事不等於失去店的身分。

That evening, the teacher sent the class a short report with three possible actions: collect more samples, ask a qualified laboratory for testing, or change the washing process without making a claim about disease. Shuning chose the third as an immediate step and the second as a future goal. The shop would measure its rinse water and pay for proper disposal. Deyuan complained that every new measure created another expense. "Then it belongs on the bill," Xiulan replied. The family sat quietly in the shade of the front awning as the rain stopped. Nobody had won a contest that day. Yet they had identified an invisible cost, and for the first time the family treated that discovery as progress rather than embarrassment.

當晚老師寄來簡報，列出三種作法：繼續收集樣本、請合格實驗室檢測，或先改善洗滌流程，但不宣稱能預防疾病。書寧選擇第三項立刻做，第二項列為後續目標。店裡開始量測洗滌用水，並負擔適當處理費。德元抱怨新措施樣樣都要錢。「那就把它寫進帳裡。」秀蘭答。雨停後，一家人坐在遮棚陰影下，誰也沒有贏得比賽。但他們找到了看不見的成本，第一次把發現問題當作進步，而不是丟臉。

Before closing, Yating noticed the shop's old clock had stopped again. She could have ignored it, but she asked permission to open the back panel. Inside, a loose spring made the hands waken only when the case was tapped. She repaired it under Deyuan's supervision and set it to the town hall bell. He praised her steady work. The moment seemed minor beside the school's contract, but Shuning marked it as a contribution in the training log. Skill did not emerge only from a formal certificate. As Yating locked the door, she said she would strive to finish her first pattern before the next fitting day. Her voice was stronger than it had been the previous week.

打烊前，雅婷發現老掛鐘又停了。她原本可以不管，卻先請准打開背板。裡面一根鬆彈簧讓指針只有敲到外殼時才會醒來。她在德元指導下修好，再對準鎮公所鐘聲。德元稱讚她做事穩定。跟學校合約相比，這似乎是小事，但書寧仍把它列作訓練紀錄裡的貢獻。技能不只來自正式證書。雅婷鎖門時說，下次試穿前一定要完成第一張紙樣。她的聲音比上週有力得多。
