染色背後的顏色

The Color Behind the Dye

1996，藍線裁縫舖的第二把尺 · 第 6 / 25 章 · 3000–4000 · 英中對照 · 校訂完成

In January, Shuning visited the textile warehouse run by Yang Huilan, representative of the large supplier competing for the school's order. Yang looked conservative in a plain jacket, but her questions were sharp. "You are a consultant now?" she asked. Shuning replied that she was a tailor trying to understand what a consumer paid for. They examined stacks of dyed cotton, linen, and finished goods. Yang said the cheaper fabric came from a factory outside the district, where workers ran long shifts. Shuning did not know whether the arrangement was unlawful, and she would not accuse anyone without evidence. She asked whether the factory could enclose a written explanation of its wage and safety practices with each shipment. Yang was surprised, then said that a supplier ought to be able to explain its own prices.

一月，書寧拜訪競爭校服訂單的大型供應商代表楊慧蘭所經營的布料倉庫。慧蘭穿著保守的素色外套，問話卻很銳利。「妳現在是顧問了？」她問。書寧說自己只是想弄清消費者究竟買了什麼的裁縫。兩人檢查染色棉布、亞麻與成品貨堆。慧蘭說較便宜的布來自區外工廠，那裡工人輪長班。書寧不知道安排是否合法，也不會無證據就指控。她詢問每批貨能否附上工資與安全程序的書面說明。慧蘭吃了一驚，接著說供應商確實應能說明自己的價格。

The warehouse's orientation tour began beside a microscope. A technician showed how dye attached to fibers and why excessive heat could weaken the thread. On one table stood a stereo playing music for the staff. Near another sat a basin of rinse water that smelled strongly of chemicals. Shuning was ignorant of the exact formula and said so. She asked the technician to explain it rather than pretend to know. The technician warned that the samples were safe to touch after washing, but undiluted materials workers must isolate them in a separate workroom. Shuning recorded that distinction. Luxury fabric was not automatically safer, nor was ordinary cloth automatically dangerous. The meaningful questions were the worker's exposure, the cleaning method, and the finished material's test record.

倉庫導覽從顯微鏡旁開始。技術員示範染料如何附著纖維，以及過熱為何會使線變弱。一張桌上有播放音樂的音響，另一邊則擺著氣味濃重的沖洗盆。書寧不懂確切化學配方，便坦白承認，請技術員解釋，不裝作熟悉。技術員說樣布洗淨後可以接觸，但工人必須把未稀釋原料隔離在另一間作業室。書寧記下這個區分。高級布不一定比較安全，普通布也不必然危險，真正需要問的是工人的暴露程度、清潔方式與成品檢驗紀錄。

At lunchtime, Xiulan phoned from the shop. A timid apprentice, Yating, had applied for work but began to tremble when asked about her sewing experience. She had been taught that a young woman should accept any occupation offered to her without asking about wages. Shuning told Xiulan to show Yating the hourly rate before offering a trial. "There is no grace in making someone grateful for unpaid work," she said. Her mother agreed, then admitted that using relatives as free helpers had become habitual in the family. They would have to change that habit themselves. Shuning put down the receiver with a knot in her stomach. It was easy to demand fairness of a big factory; harder to apply the same test to one's own home.

午餐時秀蘭打電話來：一名膽怯的學徒雅婷應徵工作，被問起縫紉經驗時開始顫抖。她從小聽人說，年輕女性有工作就該接受，不要多問薪水。書寧請母親先出示時薪，再談試做。「讓人感激無薪勞動，不是什麼恩惠。」她說。秀蘭同意，也承認家裡把親戚幫忙當免費資源早已成習慣，他們得自己改掉。書寧掛了電話，胃裡有種打結的感覺。要求大工廠公平很容易，把相同標準用在自己家卻難得多。

Yang eventually showed Shuning a sheet of possible investment costs. Switching to stronger fabric would increase the price per jacket, but reducing rejected sizes might recover part of that money. They disagreed about the risk. Yang feared the school would choose the lowest price regardless of comfort; Shuning feared that keeping prices low by hiding labor would only move the cost to workers. Neither woman raised her voice. Yang asked whether Shuning could share the pilot measurements without identifying children. Shuning agreed only to aggregated figures and the testing method. The exchange was an unexpected beginning: they might compete for a contract while cooperating on honest evidence. On the train home, Shuning wrote that point at the top of her notebook.

最後慧蘭拿出可能的投資成本表。換強韌布會提高每件外套單價，但退貨尺寸減少或許能抵回部分費用。兩人對風險看法不同。慧蘭怕學校只看最低價；書寧怕靠隱藏勞動壓價，最後只把成本轉嫁給工人。兩人沒有提高音量。慧蘭問能否拿到不具名的試穿數據。書寧只同意提供彙整數字和測試方法。這段交流形成意外的開始：她們可能競爭同一張合約，也能合作提供真實證據。回程火車上，書寧把這點寫在筆記本最上方。

Back at Blue Thread, Yating had completed a careful seam and was waiting for criticism. Xiulan instead asked her to name one thing she would improve. "The table is too low," Yating said. Deyuan felt a flash of rage at the criticism, but he tried the chair himself and realized she was right. Within an hour, Jianhe raised the work surface on sturdy blocks. Shuning watched the young woman relax and accepted that the shop needed changes in more than fabric. Outside, the winter rain had begun. She pinned Yang's price sheet to the wall beside their own labor record, then covered both with a blank page headed QUESTIONS. For the first time, she believed the two companies could be compared without inventing a villain. What would matter was what each could prove and what each would be willing to change.

回到藍線，雅婷已縫好一道整齊的線，正等著挨批評。秀蘭卻請她說出一項想改善的地方。「桌子太低了。」雅婷說。德元受到批評時一陣憤怒，但自己坐下試過後明白雅婷說得對。不到一小時，建和用堅固墊塊抬高工作檯。書寧看著年輕女孩放鬆，明白店裡需要改變的不只有布。門外冬雨開始落下，她把慧蘭的價目表和自家工時表並排釘牆上，再用一張寫著「待查問題」的空白紙蓋住。她第一次相信，不必憑空製造壞人，也能比較兩家公司；重要的是各自能證明什麼、願意改變什麼。
