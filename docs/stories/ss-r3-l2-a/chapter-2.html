山門的最後一枚銅牌 · 第 2 / 25 章 · 1000–2000 · 英中對照

Before the morning bell, Nian stepped into the market outside the north gate with a folded sheet in her hand. She had been taught that a person's rank showed the value of their work, yet she had seen kind people turned away from lessons for lack of money. A fresh report gave her a reason to ask again: the students searched for an older account book. Jun waited by the door with the previous day's figures. They agreed on one simple rule for their work. They would ask before accusing, copy before correcting, and show their findings to those whose lives appeared in the numbers. The mountain could keep its secrets for a while, but not forever.

晨鐘響前，念拿著摺好的紙走進北門外的市集。她曾被教導，人的等級顯示工作的價值；然而她看過善良的人只因缺錢就被拒於課堂之外。新的報告讓她有理由再次提問：學生尋找舊帳冊。俊帶著前一天的數字等在門邊。他們為自己的工作訂下一條簡單規則：指控前先詢問，改動前先抄錄，並把發現展示給數字所涉及的人。山門可以暫時保住秘密，卻不可能永遠如此。

An ordinary record brought the first real change: a barber kept a dated copy beneath his bench. Jun read the line twice, then asked a bystander to confirm the date. Nian did not claim to know who had caused the mistake. A record could show a result without showing every reason behind it. They drew a simple copy of the page and left the original where it belonged. The work was less exciting than finding a secret door, but both knew why it mattered. If a master challenged their report, they could bring another witness and show the same line again. It was better to have a small true fact than a large false story.

一項普通紀錄帶來第一個真正變化：理髮師在長椅下保存了有日期的副本。俊把那一行讀了兩次，再請旁觀者確認日期。念不敢宣稱自己知道是誰造成錯誤。紀錄可以顯示結果，卻未必說得出背後所有理由。他們畫下書頁的簡單副本，把原件留在原處。這工作不像發現祕門般刺激，但兩人明白其重要。如果師父質疑報告，他們可以再找見證人，把同一行展示一遍。小小的真事實，比宏大的假故事更好。

Gao arrived before the ink on the copy was dry. He listened for less than a minute and then announced: Gao insisted that only the new edition could be read. A few people nodded because they were afraid of losing their place. Nian did not ask them to fight him. She asked Gao whether he could show the rule that supported his statement. He called her question disrespectful. She replied that respect did not require silence, especially when children and sick people were waiting for an answer. Jun made a note of the exchange but stayed quiet. Nian wanted Gao's words recorded exactly, including anything that might later explain his decision.

副本上的墨水還沒乾，高管事就到了。他聽不到一分鐘便宣布：高管事堅持只能使用新版。有些人怕失去自己的位置，只好點頭。念沒有要求他們對抗高管事，而是問他能否出示支持說法的規則。他說這問題不尊重人。念回答，尊重不等於沉默，尤其孩子和病人還在等答案。俊記下這段對話，沒有插嘴。念希望準確記錄高管事的話，包括日後可能解釋他決定的細節。

At the market, the barber held his old book at an angle so that the lantern would light its pages. A deer crossed the road, while a dove rested on the shop board. Nian told him that she would appreciate any help, but he should avoid giving away his only copy. The book was the first edition of the school rules, and its heavy cover was painful to lift with his injured hand. Jun could spot a particular line about the minority of students who could not pay. It said that no organization, not even one led by its president, could remove that protection without a public vote. The truth existed on the page, though the new textbook left it out. The barber offered them sour tea in a huge cup, and Nian drank it carefully. For the first time, a forgotten record seemed more useful than a powerful speech. The old protections still exist, even if the new managers forget them.

在市場裡，理髮師把舊書斜拿著，好讓燈籠照亮書頁。一隻鹿穿過路面，白鴿則停在店門口的木板上。念說自己會感謝任何幫助，但希望他別交出唯一的副本。這本書是山門規則的初版，厚重封面讓他受傷的手抬得很痛。俊發現其中有一行特別提到付不起費用的少數學生。那裡寫著，任何組織，即使由會長領導，也不能在沒有公開投票的情況下取消這項保護。真相確實存在於書頁上，然而新版課本卻刪掉了。理髮師用巨大杯子招待他們喝酸茶，念小心地喝下。第一次，一筆被遺忘的紀錄似乎比有力的演說更有用。舊有保障依然存在，即使新管事選擇忘記。

The group could have waited for an order from above. Nian did not believe that waiting would protect anyone, so Jun photographed the page and returned it to its owner. She first explained what the plan could prove and what it could not. People who feared being named were allowed to stay outside the public list. People who wanted to testify checked their own words before signing. Jun offered to carry the papers, but Nian asked him to read them aloud as well. A choice made together needed more than two hands and one pen. It needed the people affected to understand the cost, the risk, and the reason for taking that next step.

眾人原可等候上級指示。念卻不相信光等能保護任何人，所以俊拍下書頁並把原件還給主人。她先說明計畫能證明什麼、不能證明什麼。害怕被公開姓名的人可以不列在名單上；願意作證的人則在簽名前核對自己的話。俊願意搬文件，念卻請他也把內容念出來。共同選擇需要的不只是兩隻手和一枝筆，還需要受影響的人了解代價、風險，以及踏出下一步的理由。

The afternoon was almost over when the result became clear: they learned that the smallest group had once been protected. Some students cheered, while others looked worried about what Gao would do next. Nian asked them to report both feelings honestly. She had seen how easy it was for a small victory to turn into a story of perfect heroes and terrible enemies. Real people were rarely that simple. A fair school would need ways to examine the conduct of its friends as carefully as the conduct of its rivals. She put the day's facts on the public board and made sure the empty spaces were marked as unknown, not filled with guesses.

下午快結束時，結果終於明朗：他們發現過去的規則曾保護人數最少的一群。有些學生歡呼，有些則擔心高管事下一步會做什麼。念請大家誠實記下兩種感受。她看過小勝利如何輕易變成完美英雄與可怕敵人的故事，真實的人卻很少那麼簡單。公平的山門需要同樣仔細地檢查朋友和對手的行為。她把當天事實寫上公告板，確保空白處標成『未知』，而不是塞進猜測。

In the last quiet minutes, Jun found a new question in the record: the record named a witness who had moved to Lantern Village. He asked Nian if they had enough strength to follow it tomorrow. She thought of the tired workers and the children waiting at home. They needed rest, not a speech about never giving up. She closed the notebook and asked everyone to return safely. The question would still be there in the morning. Outside, the evening wind moved through the roofs of the small school. It sounded like pages turning. Nian did not know what the next page would show, but she knew how to read it carefully.

最後幾分鐘安靜下來，俊在紀錄中發現新的問題：紀錄寫出一名已搬到燈村的見證人。他問念明天還有沒有力氣追查。她想到疲累的工人和在家等待的孩子。大家需要休息，而不是一篇永不放棄的演說。她合起筆記本，請每個人平安回去。問題隔天早上仍會在那裡。外頭晚風穿過小山門屋頂，聲音像翻動書頁。念不知道下一頁有什麼，卻知道要如何仔細閱讀。
