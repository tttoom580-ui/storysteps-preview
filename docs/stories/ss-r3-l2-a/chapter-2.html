The Second Register

山門的最後一枚銅牌 · 第 2 / 25 章 · 1000–2000 · 英中對照

用互動版查詞、朗讀與收藏

The address on the old clerk's note led Nian to a barber at the lower market. His customers were waiting outside when she asked about the school's first register. He looked past her toward the mountain before lifting a cloth beneath his bench. The book had been kept there through years of hair clippings and repairs. Jun asked to photograph the cover before the barber opened it. Inside, a payment list was followed by a rule protecting students who could not afford full fees. Nian remembered the six empty hands in the training court. Their complaint had sounded like a request for kindness. This page suggested they were asking for something the school had already promised. She asked the barber how the book had reached his shop.

舊書記字條上的地址，把念帶到山下市場的理髮店。客人在外面等候，她詢問第一冊帳本。理髮師先望向山門，才掀起椅下的布。這本書多年來一直藏在碎髮與修理工具之間。俊先拍封面，再請他翻開。付款表後面有一條保障無力繳足費用學生的規則。念想起練功場的六雙空手。他們的申訴原像在求好心，這頁卻顯示是在索取山門早已答應的東西。她問書怎麼到了店裡。

The barber had received it from a retired storekeeper who wanted a village copy preserved. He could explain that transfer, but not every change the masters had made since. Nian wrote down the limit of his knowledge. Gao arrived with a new edition and declared the older page useless. Jun opened the two books at the same rule. The new edition skipped directly from payments to discipline; it contained no decision canceling the protection. Gao tapped the newer cover as if its clean cloth settled the issue. The barber laid his injured hand across his own book. Nian asked both men to leave the pages open long enough for photographs. She did not need to take anybody's only copy away to show that two records differed.

理髮師從退休庫管手上收到此書，對方想保留村裡的一份。他能說明這次轉交，卻不知道長老之後改了哪些規則。念記下他所知的範圍。高管事帶著新版出現，宣稱舊頁無用。俊把兩本翻到同一條；新版直接從付款跳到紀律，沒有取消保障的決議。高管事敲敲乾淨封面，彷彿這就夠了。理髮師用受傷的手護住舊書。念請兩人把頁面攤著，讓俊拍照。要顯示差異，不必拿走任何人僅有的副本。

At the market, the barber held his old book at an angle so that the lantern would light its pages. A deer crossed the road, while a dove rested on the shop board. Nian told him that she would appreciate any help, but he should avoid giving away his only copy. The book was the first edition of the school rules, and its heavy cover was painful to lift with his injured hand. Jun could spot a particular line about the minority of students who could not pay. It said that no organization, not even one led by its president, could remove that protection without a public vote. The truth existed on the page, though the new textbook left it out. The barber offered them sour tea in a huge cup, and Nian drank it carefully. For the first time, a forgotten record seemed more useful than a powerful speech. The old protections still exist, even if the new managers forget them.

在市場裡，理髮師把舊書斜拿著，好讓燈籠照亮書頁。一隻鹿穿過路面，白鴿則停在店門口的木板上。念說自己會感謝任何幫助，但希望他別交出唯一的副本。這本書是山門規則的初版，厚重封面讓他受傷的手抬得很痛。俊發現其中有一行特別提到付不起費用的少數學生。那裡寫著，任何組織，即使由會長領導，也不能在沒有公開投票的情況下取消這項保護。真相確實存在於書頁上，然而新版課本卻刪掉了。理髮師用巨大杯子招待他們喝酸茶，念小心地喝下。第一次，一筆被遺忘的紀錄似乎比有力的演說更有用。舊有保障依然存在，即使新管事選擇忘記。

Jun photographed the page and returned the register to its place beneath the bench. Before they left, the barber pointed out a small signature beside the old rule. The woman who had witnessed its adoption was still alive, he believed, although she had moved to Lantern Village. Nian asked him to send a request rather than give them a private address without permission. He agreed to deliver it with the next market cart. Outside, Jun showed her the photographs. One corner had reflected the lamp, hiding a word. They went back and took that picture again. Their first useful discovery could have been spoiled by a careless image. Nian thanked the waiting customers for their patience, and the barber finally called the next one inside.

俊拍完那頁，把帳本放回椅下。離開前，理髮師指出規則旁的小簽名。他相信當年見證通過此規的女子仍健在，只是已搬到燈村。念請他代轉請求，不要未經同意給私人地址。他答應隨下一輛市場車送去。出門後，俊給念看照片，一角反光遮住一個字。兩人回店補拍，否則第一個重要發現就會被粗心照片損壞。念謝過等候的客人，理髮師終於叫下一位進來。
