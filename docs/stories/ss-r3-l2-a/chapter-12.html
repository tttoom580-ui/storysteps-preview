錯誤的帳單

The Wrong Bill

山門的最後一枚銅牌 · 第 12 / 25 章 · 1000–2000 · 英中對照 · 校訂中

用互動版查詞、朗讀與收藏

At the market, the merchant stood behind a counter with his deliveries spread before him. Someone had written dishonest beside his name on the public complaint board. Nian asked the clerk to cover that accusation until the count was checked. The merchant had supplied the kitchen for years; she would not treat those years as proof of innocence, but neither would she begin with an unsupported verdict. Jun placed the cook's note beside the bill. One listed half the quantity of the other. Gao called the difference a harmless error in writing. If so, Nian said, correcting it should be straightforward. She asked for customers and carriers who had actually seen the boxes, rather than people who had heard rumors about the merchant's wealth. Two carriers stepped forward from the loading yard.

市場裡，商人站在櫃後，把送貨紀錄攤開。有人在申訴板他的名字旁寫「不誠實」。念請書記先蓋住指控，等核數再說。多年供貨不等於無罪證明，卻也不該先判罪。俊把廚師字條和帳單並排，一份數量只有另一份一半。高管事說是無害筆誤。念說，若如此，更正應當簡單。她要找真正見過箱子的顧客和運送者，而非聽聞商人財富的人。兩名搬運工從裝卸場走上前。

The carriers counted the remaining marked boxes and described the load they had brought uphill. Jun measured a standard container while the merchant opened his store book to the relevant date. The figures agreed with the cook's smaller count. One carrier pointed out a second unloading place behind a locked hall. He had assumed that delivery belonged to another order. Nian drew the two destinations on the back of a clean form and asked him to identify only the journey he remembered. The merchant had been blamed for a shortage that appeared after his goods passed the gate. Gao tried to end the exercise by offering to pay the difference. The merchant refused the money until his name was cleared on the same board where the accusation had appeared that morning.

搬運工點剩下有標記的箱子，說明送上山的載量。俊量標準容器，商人翻店帳到當天，數字都和廚師較小的數量吻合。一名工人指出鎖廳後還卸過一次貨，他原以為屬另一張訂單。念在乾淨表單背面畫兩個目的地，請他只指認記得的路程。短缺出現在貨物進門後，商人卻被怪罪。高管事想付差額結束查驗，商人拒收，要求先在早上指控他的同一塊板上還他清白。

In the countryside market, a customer asked the shopkeeper to measure the number of boxes under a blank label. One box contained fries and pudding for the guards; another held ink and paper. A dentist had ordered a new teapot, while a child searched for lost slippers near the door. Such ordinary details made it harder to blame a merchant for every shortage. Nian stood beside a sign marked with the direction of the capital and wrote down the sudden changes in the delivery count. There was danger in accusing people without proof. The shopkeeper looked scared, and his hands hardly stopped shaking. Nian said she would regard him with respect until the facts were clear. They agreed to settle the disagreement with a signed count, not with a story invented by someone who had never seen the goods.

在鄉間市場，一位顧客請店主測量空白標籤下有多少箱子。一箱裝著給守衛的薯條和布丁，另一箱裝油墨與紙。牙醫訂了一個新茶壺，一個孩子則在門旁找走失的拖鞋。這些平凡細節讓人更難把每次短缺都怪罪到商人頭上。念站在指向首都方向的標誌旁，寫下運送數量突然發生的變化。沒有證據就指責他人十分危險。店主看起來害怕，雙手幾乎無法停止顫抖。念說，在事實明朗前，她都會尊重他。雙方同意以簽名清點的方式解決爭議，而不採信從未見過貨物之人編造的故事。

The signed count cleared that merchant of the charge. Nian carried it to the clerk herself and watched the correction replace the covered accusation. The merchant's daughter read the new entry before going back to the shop. Jun asked the carrier whether he would show them the locked hall. He could point out the entrance, he said, but he had never been allowed inside. Nian added that limit to the account. On the way uphill they passed the winter garden, where rainwater ran through the storage doorway. The carrier recognized its repair mark as the same one used on the hall receipt. Nian did not stop him from making his delivery, but asked the garden keeper to meet them. The goods behind the locked door were one question; food already getting wet could not wait for its answer.

簽名點貨表還了商人清白。念親自帶給書記，看著更正取代蓋住的指控。商人的女兒讀過，才回店。俊問搬運工能否指鎖廳，他說可指出入口，從未獲准進去。念記下限制。上山途中經冬季菜園，雨水正穿過儲藏門。工人認出修理標記，和廳堂收據上相同。念沒耽誤送貨，另請菜園管理人見面。鎖門後的貨物是一個問題，正在淋濕的食物卻不能等答案。
