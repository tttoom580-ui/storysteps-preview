The Painted Seal

山門的最後一枚銅牌 · 第 9 / 25 章 · 1000–2000 · 英中對照 · 校訂中

用互動版查詞、朗讀與收藏

The notice beside the printing-room door no longer listed the restored villages. Someone had replaced it during the night with a page claiming their exclusion was an old decision. Nian recognized the date from the reserve-building account, but the official title beneath it seemed unfamiliar. Jun fetched the photograph they had made yesterday. The wording differed, although the border and school seal looked almost identical. Gao accused the students of inventing the difference after failing to win their argument. Nian asked him to leave both copies where the printers could examine them. Her anger would not establish which page was older. The printer took the newly posted notice off the wall by its clean edge and placed it beside the photograph. Nobody was allowed to write on it during the comparison.

印刷房門旁公告又不見恢復身分的村名。有人夜裡換上一頁，聲稱排除兩村是舊決定。念認得日期，正是建儲備那年，下面官銜卻陌生。俊取出昨日照片。兩版邊框與印記幾乎相同，文字卻不同。高管事反指學生因爭論失利而捏造差異。念請他保留兩份給印刷師查看。憤怒不能證明哪張較舊。印刷師捏著乾淨邊角取下新公告，放在照片旁，比對時不准任何人往上寫字。

Two printers from different shops agreed to compare the marks. The first noticed that the paper stock had not existed on the date printed beneath the notice. The second compared the seal with a dated school sample and found that the new image copied a damaged edge exactly. A genuine old seal would not have carried damage that appeared years later. Jun wrote their explanations separately so neither man's conclusion would quietly become the other's. Nian asked them to describe a copy as a copy, without naming its maker. The evidence could establish that much. It could not tell them whose hand had ordered the page. Gao called the exercise disrespectful, but the printer asked which technical point he wished to challenge. He did not identify one before leaving the room.

兩家不同店鋪的印刷師同意檢查。第一位指出，這種紙在公告所印年份尚未出現。第二位和有日期的山門樣本比對，發現新印記連受損邊緣都照抄；真正舊印不會帶著多年後才出現的破損。俊分別記下兩人的說明，免得一人的結論悄悄變成另一人的。念請他們只稱副本為副本，不點製作者。證據能證明這麼多，卻不能說誰下令。高管事說不敬，印刷師問他要反駁哪個技術點；他離開前沒指出任何一點。

A printer lifted the lid from a box of ink, and a little liquid spilled across a painted image. The new notice was still alive with bright color, but its message was wrong. Nian read the changed title aloud, and a burst of laughter came from the room when Jun pointed out a silly mistake. A symbol at the exit showed the position of the real school seal. The workers expressed relief when the copy was found. Nobody wanted to forgive the person who had ordered it yet, but Nian said the result mattered more than an angry speech. A tube for carrying papers had been hidden behind a wall painting of a shark. One child suggested that the fish had eaten the solution. The joke helped improve their spirits. Then a printer with a strong character asked them to keep the false paper as evidence, instead of tearing it apart. His sister brought delicious rice for everyone. Nian wanted to express her thanks to the careful workers before the room closed.

印刷師打開墨盒蓋子，少量墨汁灑到一幅圖像上。新公告的顏色依然鮮亮，訊息卻是錯的。念大聲讀出被改過的官銜；俊指出一個愚蠢的錯誤時，房間裡爆出笑聲。出口旁的符號標明真正山門印章的位置。工人找到副本後鬆了一口氣。大家還不願原諒下令改動的人，但念說結果比憤怒的演說重要。裝紙的圓筒藏在一幅鯊魚畫後面。孩子說一定是那條魚吞了答案，這個玩笑讓眾人心情好些。接著，一位性格堅定的印刷師請他們保存假公告作證據，不要撕碎。她的妹妹還送來美味的米飯。念想在房間關閉前向細心的工人表達謝意。

The printers signed a report confirming that the latest notice was a copy rather than the original order it claimed to be. Nian asked the clerk to post the correction beside it, with the false page protected behind a clear cover. Tearing it up would make tomorrow's explanation depend on their memory. Jun returned to the strange title beneath the date. The office named there had been abolished before the paper was produced. That detail suggested someone had copied an older form carelessly. Nian requested a list of the people allowed to use those forms. Outside, the restored village names went back onto the current notice. A farmer touched his own entry, then left for the field. The corrected page mattered because it let him return to work without losing his place in the meeting.

印刷師簽報告，確認最新公告是冒充原命令的副本。念請書記把更正貼旁邊，假頁加透明護套保存。撕掉它，明日就只能憑記憶說明。俊再看那個奇怪官銜：紙張製成前，該職已廢除。這像是有人粗心照抄舊表。念要求查看誰獲准使用這類表格。外頭，兩村名字回到現行公告。農民摸摸自己那行，便回田裡。更正之所以重要，是讓他安心工作，也不會失去會議的位置。
