種子日曆

The Calendar of Seeds

山門的最後一枚銅牌 · 第 7 / 25 章 · 1000–2000 · 英中對照 · 校訂完成

用互動版查詞、朗讀與收藏

The second village's receipt lay beside a planting calendar on the farmer's table. Seed had arrived too late for the lower fields, and the farmers wanted the reserve opened before the upper fields lost their chance as well. Nian followed their fingers across the dates. A delay that looked small in the office could mean a whole season without food here. Jun found a note in the older calendar setting aside seed for difficult weather. The reserve was not meant to reward a successful harvest; it existed for a season like this one. Nian asked the farmers to mark which fields could still be planted. They placed stones along the table edge, one for each household ready to work before sunset if the seed became available.

第二村的收據放在農民桌上的播種曆旁。種子來得太晚，下田已錯過時節；大家希望在上田也失去機會前開儲備。念跟著手指看日期。辦公室裡看似不大的耽擱，在這裡可能是一季無糧。俊在舊曆找到為惡劣天候預留種子的註記。儲備不是獎勵好收成，而是為這種季節存在。念請農民標出仍可播種的田。桌沿擺上一顆顆石頭，每顆代表一戶，只要種子到手就能在日落前動工。

Gao's reply called the reserve a prize for the strongest students. Nian brought the calendar to the storehouse clerk and asked him to read the original instruction in front of the farmers. The clerk read slowly, then admitted that urgency was one of its conditions. Jun proposed starting with a small test field rather than distributing every bag without a plan. The farmers chose a field whose workers were ready and whose soil could still receive seed. Nian asked them to leave enough reserve for the next urgent request. The argument changed from which family deserved praise to which ground could still produce food. The clerk unlocked a small compartment and weighed the first bag. Each household's stone remained on the table until its need had been considered.

高管事回覆，儲備是給最強學生的獎品。念把舊曆帶給倉庫書記，請他當農民的面讀原指示。書記慢慢念，承認急迫需求是條件之一。俊建議先種小片試驗田，不要毫無計畫分完。農民選了工人已就緒、土壤仍可播種的一塊。念請大家留下足夠儲備給下一個急需者。討論從哪個家族值得表揚，轉成哪片土地還能生產食物。書記開小隔間，秤第一袋。每戶的石頭都留在桌上，直到需求被討論。

Nian went out for a jog before the meeting, passing a rocky path where a turtle moved with care. The climate was dry, and the calendar showed the last safe period for planting. An old farmer had tried to advance the season, but his boxes could not contain enough water or seed. In a humble hut he introduced Nian to a principle written by his father: each living being needed a place to grow. There was no ideal answer for every field; even a desert could teach patience. Jun took a mop and cleaned a table so the farmers could spread their records. The first step was small, yet they could see progress. Thus they counted seeds before counting points. When a child held up a toothbrush and called it a lucky tool, everyone laughed. Their victory was not a great harvest, only the chance to plant before it was too late.

會議前念出去慢跑，經過一條多石的小路，看到烏龜小心前進。氣候乾燥，日曆顯示已到安全播種期的最後階段。老農人曾想提前季節，但箱子裝不下足夠的水或種子。他在簡陋小屋裡向念介紹父親寫下的一項原則：每個生命都需要成長的地方。沒有適用所有田地的理想答案；甚至沙漠也能教人耐心。俊拿拖把清理桌面，好讓農民攤開紀錄。第一步雖小，他們卻看得見進展。因此，他們先數種子，不先算分數。一個孩子舉起牙刷，說那是幸運工具，大家都笑了。他們的勝利不是豐收，而是及時播種的機會。

Nian carried the calendar to the field so the starting date could be entered beside the actual work. Farmers planted the test rows before night, and Jun helped stretch a line between them. The result was modest enough to measure: seed in the ground, workers named, reserve remaining. While the clerk counted the unused bags, he mentioned a chest sent toward the northern road. Its destination did not appear on the calendar. Nian wrote down the chest number without stopping the planting. The farmer beside her pressed the last seed into soil and asked whether a distant order could take this chance away again. She could not promise it would never happen. She could promise that this field's record would stay in the village as well as the school, where neither side could quietly change it alone.

念把曆帶到田邊，讓開工日記在真正勞作旁。農民夜前種完試驗行，俊幫忙拉直線。成果小而能量度：種子入土、工人具名、儲備有餘。書記點剩袋時提到一箱運往北路，曆上沒有目的地。念記箱號，沒有停下播種。旁邊農民壓入最後一顆種子，問遠方命令會不會再奪走機會。她無法保證永不發生，只能保證這片田的紀錄村裡和山門各留一份，任何一方都不能獨自悄改。
