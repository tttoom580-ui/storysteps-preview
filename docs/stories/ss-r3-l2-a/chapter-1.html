空了的銅牌盒

The Empty Token Box

山門的最後一枚銅牌 · 第 1 / 25 章 · 1000–2000 · 英中對照

用互動版查詞、朗讀與收藏

At first light, Nian returned to the lower training court. The mountain school had many beautiful halls, but she had learned that a hall could be beautiful and still hide an unfair rule. She was nineteen, old enough to keep records and young enough for the masters to dismiss her questions. Today the problem was plain: six apprentices had received no training tokens. Jun, her twenty-year-old friend, walked beside her with a small notebook. They had promised not to make a charge they could not support. They would write what they saw, ask who had decided it, and leave room for an honest answer.

天亮時，念回到下層練功場。山門有許多美麗堂舍，但她已學到，美麗的堂舍也可能藏著不公平的規則。她十九歲，年紀足以負責記錄，卻又年輕得讓師父們輕視她的疑問。今天的問題很明白：六名學徒沒拿到修行銅牌。二十歲的朋友俊帶著小筆記本同行。他們約定不提出無法證實的指控。他們會記下所見、追問決定者是誰，也保留讓人誠實回答的空間。

The first useful sign came from an ordinary piece of evidence: the public list gave twice as many tokens to the master’s relatives. Nian did not touch it until a second person had seen where it lay. Jun drew a small mark on his page, then wrote the date and the names of those present. That care was slow work. Sometimes a student waiting at the door asked why they did not simply take the paper and run. Nian answered that a fair account had to remain fair even when it described someone they disliked. If a record could be changed in anger, it would not help the next person who needed it.

第一個有用線索來自平凡的證據：公開榜單把兩倍銅牌分給長老親屬。第二個人確認放置位置前，念沒有碰它。俊在頁上做小記號，再寫下日期及在場者姓名。這種謹慎很費時間。有時等在門口的學生問，他們為什麼不拿起文件直接跑掉。念回答，公平帳目即使記載自己不喜歡的人，也必須保持公平。如果一份紀錄能在憤怒中任意更改，它就無法幫助下一個需要它的人。

Steward Gao came with two assistants and a voice that filled the room. His claim was familiar: Steward Gao called the list a private school matter. He asked Nian whether she wanted to become master of the school. She said no. She wanted the people who worked here to understand the decisions that affected their food, lessons, and future. Gao laughed at the word future as if it were a child's toy. A few students lowered their eyes. Jun stayed beside Nian but did not speak for her. This was her question to raise, and she would decide how far to carry it.

高管事帶著兩名助手到來，聲音響徹房間。他的說法並不陌生：高管事聲稱榜單屬於山門內務。他問念是不是想當山門掌門。她說不是。她只希望在此工作的人了解影響他們食物、課程與未來的決定。高管事嘲笑『未來』一詞，彷彿那是孩子的玩具。幾名學生低下頭。俊站在念身邊，卻沒有代她發言。這是她提出的問題，該走多遠也由她決定。

It was a windy morning, and the balcony above the court shook in the cold air. Nian watched a male operator carry a box toward the stone base, then approach the private office at great speed. Beside the field, children played on a seesaw. She had a belief that a school could develop better rules, even when an elder tried to deny the need for change. For instance, a secondary training class should not receive nothing merely because the main class was richer. The head teacher called her question a challenge to tradition and said the whole nation could not change for one student. Nian answered that she wanted to discuss a small, clear account, not rule the nation. A forgotten subway tunnel below the court had once carried goods to the mountain; its locked entrance was another example of a useful place that the school refused to share.

那天風很大，練功場上方的陽臺在寒風中搖晃。念看見一名男操作員把箱子搬向石製底座，接著快速走向私人辦公室。田地旁，孩子們正在玩翹翹板。她相信山門能建立更好的制度，即使長老想否認改革的必要。舉例來說，第二層級的練功班不該只因主要班級較富裕，就什麼也拿不到。掌門說她的問題挑戰傳統，整個國家不能為一名學生改變。念回答，她只想討論一份清楚的小帳目，不想統治國家。練功場下方一條被遺忘的地下鐵隧道，從前曾運貨上山；它上鎖的入口，也是山門不肯分享有用場所的另一個例子。

Nian had learned that a public claim needed a public action. Instead of hiding the problem in her own room, Nian copied both columns and asked for the original rule. The choice carried a risk. Someone might lose a lesson, a wage, or a friend's trust while the school argued. She asked the people around her whether they agreed to put their names beside the record. Those who said no were allowed to leave. Those who said yes were given time to read it first. When Jun put down his pen, Nian noticed that he had left a space for later corrections. That empty space mattered to her as much as the first signature.

念已學會，公開主張需要公開行動。她沒有把問題藏在自己房裡，而是念抄下兩欄並要求查看原規。這項選擇有風險。山門爭論時，有人可能失去課程、薪資或朋友的信任。她詢問周圍的人是否願意在紀錄旁簽名。說不的人可以離開；說好的人也有時間先閱讀內容。俊放下筆時，念注意到他預留了日後更正的空間。那塊空白對她而言，和第一個簽名一樣重要。

By evening, the day had brought one result that could be checked: the apprentices formed a line to sign a shared complaint. It was not the end of the dispute. The powerful teachers still had rooms, supplies, and friends who would defend them. The apprentices had only their shared notes and the patience to read them again. Nian wrote the result on the public board without adding a line of praise for herself. Some people wanted a hero, but she knew a hero could make the same mistake as a master. A rule that depended on one good person was still a weak rule. They needed a way for ordinary people to correct it.

傍晚時，這一天帶來一項可以核對的結果：學徒排隊在共同申訴書上簽名。爭議還沒結束。掌權的老師仍有房間、物資和願意替他們辯護的朋友。學徒只有共同筆記，以及重新閱讀的耐心。念把結果寫上公告板，沒有加上一句讚美自己的話。有人想要英雄，但她知道英雄也可能犯下與掌門相同的錯。依賴一位好人的制度仍然脆弱。他們需要的是讓一般人能修正制度的方法。

Before leaving, Nian found another question in the notes: a missing box number suggested that the account had been changed. She looked at Jun, who was already closing the last window. He asked whether they could stop for the night. They could, she said, because tired people made poor judges. But tomorrow they would follow the question, wherever it led. Outside, the mountain paths were dark and the lamps in the village were small. Nian could not promise that the school would change quickly. She could promise that nobody would have to accept a secret number merely because an important person had written it. She folded the page and kept it dry until morning.

離開前，念在筆記裡找到另一個問題：一個消失的箱號顯示帳目遭到改動。她望向正在關最後一扇窗的俊。俊問今晚能不能停下。她說可以，因為疲累的人容易做出糟糕判斷。但明天他們會繼續追查，不管線索通往哪裡。外頭山路黑暗，村裡燈火微弱。念無法保證山門很快改變，卻能保證人們不必只因某位大人物寫下數字，就接受不透明的結果。她摺起紙頁，妥善保存到早晨。
