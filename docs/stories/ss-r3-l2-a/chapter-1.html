空了的銅牌盒

The Empty Token Box

山門的最後一枚銅牌 · 第 1 / 25 章 · 1000–2000 · 英中對照

用互動版查詞、朗讀與收藏

Six apprentices stood beside the token box after everyone else had gone to lessons. Nian counted their empty hands before she counted the remaining copper pieces. At nineteen, she had been trusted to copy the distribution list, but nobody had asked her to explain it. Now one of the boys asked whether an empty box meant he should stop coming. Jun, a year older, pulled the public list off its hook with the clerk's permission. The relatives of a senior master had received twice the ordinary share. Nothing beside their names explained why. Nian put the list back where the six could see it. She would not tell them that the difference was fair merely because she had copied it neatly yesterday.

其他人都去上課後，六名學徒仍站在銅牌盒旁。念先數過他們空空的手，再數剩下的銅牌。十九歲的她受託抄配額表，卻從沒有人要她解釋內容。現在，一個男孩問，盒子空了是否就不該再來。比她大一歲的俊得到書記同意，取下公開名單。某位長老親屬拿了普通份額的兩倍，名字旁卻沒有理由。念把名單放回六人看得見的地方。她不會因為昨天自己抄得整齊，就說這差別公平。

Gao arrived while Nian was copying the two columns onto a second sheet. He called the matter private and reached for her page. She moved it toward the clerk rather than hiding it behind her back. If the school had a rule allowing different shares, she wanted the clerk to show it. Gao said she was too young to understand the needs of important families. One apprentice began apologizing for asking. Nian stopped writing and asked the boy what lesson he had hoped to attend. He named the basic afternoon class, not a special privilege. Jun wrote the class beside his empty allocation. The difference between a favor and a promised lesson was becoming visible in the numbers, even before anyone supplied the missing rule.

念正把兩欄抄到另一張紙時，高管事來了。他說這是內務，伸手要拿她的紙。她把紙移向書記，沒有藏到背後。如果山門有允許不同配額的規則，她想請書記出示。高管事說她太年輕，不懂重要家族的需要。一名學徒開始為提問道歉。念停筆，問他原本想上什麼課。他說的是下午基礎班，並非特殊待遇。俊把課名寫在空配額旁。即使還沒找到規則，數字已逐漸顯出恩惠和承諾課程的差別。

It was a windy morning, and the balcony above the court shook in the cold air. Nian watched a male operator carry a box toward the stone base, then approach the private office at great speed. Beside the field, children played on a seesaw. She had a belief that a school could develop better rules, even when an elder tried to deny the need for change. For instance, a secondary training class should not receive nothing merely because the main class was richer. The head teacher called her question a challenge to tradition and said the whole nation could not change for one student. Nian answered that she wanted to discuss a small, clear account, not rule the nation. A forgotten subway tunnel below the court had once carried goods to the mountain; its locked entrance was another example of a useful place that the school refused to share.

那天風很大，練功場上方的陽臺在寒風中搖晃。念看見一名男操作員把箱子搬向石製底座，接著快速走向私人辦公室。田地旁，孩子們正在玩翹翹板。她相信山門能建立更好的制度，即使長老想否認改革的必要。舉例來說，第二層級的練功班不該只因主要班級較富裕，就什麼也拿不到。掌門說她的問題挑戰傳統，整個國家不能為一名學生改變。念回答，她只想討論一份清楚的小帳目，不想統治國家。練功場下方一條被遺忘的地下鐵隧道，從前曾運貨上山；它上鎖的入口，也是山門不肯分享有用場所的另一個例子。

By the time the wind eased, the six apprentices had decided to sign one complaint together. Nian read each sentence aloud before accepting the first name. She included the larger allocations without calling their recipients thieves. The clerk checked the figures and then noticed a break in the box numbers. A delivery had been counted, but its box did not appear on the distribution sheet. Jun searched the next page while Nian asked who kept the earlier registers. Gao told them that old books would only confuse them. That answer made the earlier records more important. Nian left a copy of the complaint with the clerk and took a receipt for it. The six returned to the court together, carrying no tokens yet, but no longer carrying six separate doubts.

風稍小時，六名學徒決定合寫申訴。念先逐句朗讀，才收第一個簽名。她列出較大配額，卻沒有稱領取者為賊。書記核過數字，發現箱號中斷：一批貨已入帳，配額表卻沒有那個箱子。俊查下一頁，念問誰保存早期帳本。高管事說舊書只會使他們混亂，反倒讓舊紀錄更值得查。念留一份申訴給書記，拿到收據。六人一同回到練功場，還沒有銅牌，卻不再各自懷疑自己。
