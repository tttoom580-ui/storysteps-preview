The Names in the Stone

山門的最後一枚銅牌 · 第 8 / 25 章 · 1000–2000 · 英中對照 · 校訂完成

用互動版查詞、朗讀與收藏

The northern-road receipt named two villages missing from the current membership list. Without membership, the clerk said, neither could ask about the reserve chest. Nian spread the older calendar beside the newer list. The villages had contributed labor when the reserve was built. Jun suggested finding someone who remembered that work, and the barber's messenger directed them to an elder near Lantern Village. He was ill and could not walk to the school, but he asked them to visit the stone beside his courtyard. Its lower edge carried marks the newer list did not explain. Nian brought a stool so he could sit during the examination. They would not trade his health for a dramatic account of the past. His daughter stayed beside him and decided when the visit should end.

北路收據列出兩座不在現行會員名單的村莊。書記說，沒有身分就不能問儲備箱。念把舊曆和新名單攤一起；兩村當年出力建儲備。俊建議找記得工程的人，理髮師的信使帶他們去燈村一位長者家。老人病著，無法到山門，卻請他們看院旁石碑。下緣的記號，新名單無從說明。念搬來凳子，讓他坐著查看，不用健康交換戲劇性的往事。女兒陪在旁邊，決定何時結束拜訪。

The elder identified two family signs and explained how they had been used on grain bags. Gao's assistant, sent to observe the visit, said old carvings could not establish present membership. Nian agreed that a stone alone would not answer every question. She asked whether the assistant would compare the signs with the reserve-building accounts. Jun traced their positions on a drawing without moving or scraping the stone. The elder corrected one line and pointed to a smaller mark beneath it. It named the work crew, not an individual owner. His daughter fetched a cloth bag from the house bearing the same sign. The assistant wrote that match down reluctantly. A present-day object and an old record could now be compared with the place where the elder said the work began.

老人認出兩個家族記號，說明它們如何用在糧袋上。高管事派來旁觀的助手說，舊刻痕不能證明現行身分。念同意石碑不能回答一切，便問助手是否願和儲備工程帳目比對。俊畫下位置，沒有移動或刮石頭。老人更正一條線，指出下面更小的記號；那是工班，不是個人主人。女兒從屋裡拿出同樣記號的布袋。助手不情願地記下吻合處。現在的物件、舊紀錄和老人指出的工程起點，終於能互相比較。

An elder told them that an ancient record survived from the last century. He was an adult when the first reserve was built, and his identity appeared beside two village names. He had once worked as an aid worker, carrying grain in his lap while crossing the old bridge. Now he was weak with fever, but he could still give clear instruction. Jun bent backward to see the writing beneath a stone. A stranger offered to design a new sign, yet Nian wanted to maintain the original marks. A child dropped a peach, and its juice left a tear in a paper copy. The elder smiled at the typical confusion of a busy visit. He had used the same sign on seed bags for many years. Beyond the wall, students could swim in a pool; the simple joy made Nian remember whom the records should protect. An artist asked the elder to pose beside the marks, but he chose to sit and rest.

老人告訴他們，一份古老紀錄從上個世紀保存至今。第一座儲備庫建成時他已成年，名字就在兩座村落旁。他曾是救援工人，過舊橋時把穀物捧在腿上。如今他發著燒，仍能給出清楚指示。俊向後彎身，查看石塊底下的文字。一位陌生人提議設計新的標誌，念卻想保留原來的刻痕。一個孩子弄掉桃子，汁液把紙本副本弄出一道裂痕。老人笑看忙亂探訪中這種常見混亂。他多年來都把同樣記號用在種子袋上。牆外，學生能在池裡游泳；單純的快樂讓念想起這些紀錄究竟該保護誰。畫家請老人站在刻痕旁擺姿勢，他卻選擇坐下休息。

At the school, Jun placed his tracing beside the original membership entries. The two omitted villages matched the recorded contributors. Ren asked the clerk to restore their right to speak at the next meeting while any remaining dispute was examined. Nian carried that decision back without claiming the whole question settled. The elder's daughter read it aloud while her father rested. He asked whether the reserve chest carried the same sign. Jun checked the number in his notebook: it belonged to the token box whose entry had disappeared from the first list. Nian felt the separate complaints drawing together. Before leaving, she asked the daughter which parts of their visit could appear in a public report. The family chose the marks and the account, keeping the elder's illness outside the record.

回到山門，俊把拓繪和原始會員資料並排，兩個被漏村莊正是記錄中的出力者。任老師要求書記先恢復兩村下次會議發言權，再查剩餘爭議。念帶回決定，沒有宣稱全案已定。女兒在父親休息時朗讀。老人問儲備箱是否同樣有記號。俊查看編號，發現正是第一張表消失的銅牌箱。念感覺各件申訴正匯到一起。離開前，她問哪些拜訪內容可公開。家人選了記號與帳目，讓老人的病留在紀錄之外。
