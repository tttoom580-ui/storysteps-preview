醫舍門口

The Clinic Door

山門的最後一枚銅牌 · 第 4 / 25 章 · 1000–2000 · 英中對照

At the clinic, Nian found a delivery mark beside a shelf that held only empty jars. The nurse had already turned two children away for lack of herbs. She showed Nian the carriers' list: the full order had entered the school gate, but several packages had been redirected to the private guest house. Jun offered to fetch them immediately. The nurse stopped him. She needed to know what had actually arrived there and which patients required treatment first. Nian pulled a clean sheet from the desk and asked the nurse to list needs without the students' family ranks. The first name belonged to a kitchen worker who had hurt his hand. He looked embarrassed to be placed before a visiting official with an expensive coat.

診療所的空罐旁，竟有已收貨的記號。護士因為缺藥，已請兩名孩子空手回去。她給念看運送表：整批貨進了山門，幾包卻被改送私人客舍。俊想立刻去拿，護士攔住他。她得先知道客舍實際收到什麼，也得判斷誰急需治療。念抽出白紙，請護士只列病人需求，不列家族位階。第一位是手受傷的廚工。他排在穿昂貴外衣的官員前，顯得不好意思。

Gao argued that generous donors should receive first choice because the clinic depended on them. The nurse asked him which donation included permission to leave a child untreated. He had no paper to show her. Nian did not try to decide treatment herself. She carried the nurse's count to the guest-house clerk and requested a matching count there. Several unopened packets remained in a cupboard, marked for visitors who had not yet arrived. The clerk checked the nurse's list and agreed that those packets could be returned for present needs. Jun carried them while Nian stayed to receive a signed transfer. It was slower than simply taking the cupboard key, but the medicine would reach the clinic with its source and quantity accounted for.

高管事主張慷慨捐贈者應優先，因為診療所仰賴他們。護士問，哪項捐贈授權他們讓孩子無藥可用？他拿不出文件。念沒有擅自決定治療，而是帶護士的數量表到客舍，要求對照。櫃裡還有幾包未拆的藥，留給尚未抵達的客人。書記核對病人表，同意把藥送回現有病人使用。俊搬藥，念留下收簽名移交單。這比直接拿鑰匙慢，卻能讓藥連同來源與數量紀錄一起回到診療所。

The clinic director showed Nian a branch of the herb store and the wooden material used for its shelves. A worker had cut his hand, and the nurse cleaned the blood before a disease could spread. The entire population of the lower court depended on this clinic, but the office wanted to combine its medicine with the private guest house supply. A generous woman had donated papaya, bread, and a hamburger for the patients. She asked whether the children might escape their hunger for one night. Nian could imagine the relief, yet her real target was the medicine. She checked the delivery network and found a score beside every student's name, as if health were a game. She had the sense that those scores were worth less than a person's need. Therefore she asked for a list of every missing item, then helped carry wood to keep the clinic warm.

醫舍主管帶念查看藥材庫的一個分部，以及架子使用的木料。一名工人割傷手，護理師先清理血跡，以免疾病傳播。整個下層練功場的人都仰賴這間醫舍，辦公室卻想把它的藥品與私人客舍的庫存合併。一位慷慨的婦人捐了木瓜、麵包和漢堡給病人，她問孩子們今晚能否不再挨餓。念可以想像那份安心，但她真正的目標仍是藥物。她查看配送網絡，發現每位學生名字旁都有分數，彷彿健康是場比賽。她覺得那些分數遠不如人的需要重要。因此，她要求列出每項缺少的物品，還幫忙搬木柴讓醫舍保持溫暖。

The returned packets did not fill the whole gap. After the last patient had been seen, the nurse gave Nian a neutral count of what remained missing. There was no complaint against an individual on that page, only ordered amounts, received amounts, and the difference. Nian asked when the next delivery would come. Three days, the nurse said, not tomorrow as the office notice claimed. Jun checked the carrier's schedule and found the same date. They left enough wood for the evening fire and took the corrected schedule to the lower court. The argument about rank had produced a practical duty: people needed an honest date so they could seek help elsewhere. Nian promised to return with the reserve question before the clinic's last packet was gone.

送回的藥仍不足。最後一位病人看完，護士給念一份中立短缺表，只列訂購、收取和差額，不指控個人。念問下一批何時到。護士說三天後，不是公告寫的明天。俊核對運輸時程，日期相同。兩人留下晚間取暖的木材，帶正確時程回練功場。位階爭論帶出一項實際責任：大家需要真實日期，才知道是否另尋協助。念答應，在最後一包藥用完前，再來追問儲備物資。
