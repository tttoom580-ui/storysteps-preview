醫舍門口

The Clinic Door

山門的最後一枚銅牌 · 第 4 / 25 章 · 1000–2000 · 英中對照

用互動版查詞、朗讀與收藏

There was a line of waiting people at the crowded clinic when Nian came to listen. Some wanted food, some wanted answers, and some wanted only to know that their names had not been forgotten. The newest complaint was clear: healing herbs had been sent to the private guest house. Nian asked them to give one account at a time. Jun sat close enough to hear but far enough to let each speaker choose their own words. They both knew that anger could point toward a real problem, but it could not tell the whole story. They would need dates, people, and a place to check each claim before making a public charge.

念來到擁擠的醫舍傾聽時，已經有一排人等待。有些人需要食物，有些人需要答案，也有人只希望自己的名字沒有被遺忘。最新的申訴很清楚：治療藥草被送到私人客舍。念請大家一次陳述一件事。俊坐在能聽清楚、又不妨礙發言者選擇用詞的位置。他們都知道，憤怒可能指向真問題，卻無法說完整故事。公開指控前，他們還需要日期、人名與能核對每項說法的地方。

The clue was neither a sword nor a magical stone. It was this: the clinic list showed empty shelves beside a full delivery mark. At first, several people called it unimportant. Nian disagreed, because small facts often stayed in a room after loud people had gone away. Jun took out his pencil and asked the group to say what the clue proved and what it did not prove. They wrote both answers on the board. This made the discussion slower, but it stopped guesses from becoming facts. A student who had doubted them helped correct a number. Nian thanked that student in front of everyone. Correcting the record was part of the job, not a defeat.

線索不是寶劍，也不是神奇石頭，而是這件事：醫舍名單顯示空架旁卻寫著已送達。一開始幾個人說它不重要。念不同意，因為吵鬧的人離去後，小事實往往仍留在原地。俊拿出鉛筆，請眾人說明這線索證明了什麼，以及不能證明什麼。兩種答案都寫上板子。討論因此變慢，卻能防止猜測變成事實。一名原本懷疑他們的學生幫忙修正數字。念當眾向他道謝。更正紀錄是工作的一部分，不是失敗。

The argument grew sharper when Gao said: Gao argued that wealthy donors deserved first choice. He had authority over rooms and supplies, and he knew how to make young people feel small. Nian remembered what the nurse had told her: when someone asks for help, first find out what they need. So she asked for one clear reason behind the order. Gao offered a speech about tradition. Jun quietly showed the relevant date on the ledger. An old worker, who had been silent until then, asked whether tradition meant keeping a promise or hiding a mistake. Gao did not answer him. Nian let that unanswered question remain on the board.

高管事說出這番話時，爭論更尖銳了：高管事主張富裕捐助者有優先選擇權。他掌管房間和物資，也知道怎麼讓年輕人覺得自己渺小。念想起護理師的話：有人求助時，先弄清楚需要什麼。於是她請他給出命令背後的一個明確理由。高管事談起傳統，俊則安靜地在帳冊上指出相關日期。一位一直沉默的老工人問，傳統究竟是守住承諾，還是掩飾錯誤？高管事沒有回答。念把這個未回答的問題留在板上。

The clinic director showed Nian a branch of the herb store and the wooden material used for its shelves. A worker had cut his hand, and the nurse cleaned the blood before a disease could spread. The entire population of the lower court depended on this clinic, but the office wanted to combine its medicine with the private guest house supply. A generous woman had donated papaya, bread, and a hamburger for the patients. She asked whether the children might escape their hunger for one night. Nian could imagine the relief, yet her real target was the medicine. She checked the delivery network and found a score beside every student's name, as if health were a game. She had the sense that those scores were worth less than a person's need. Therefore she asked for a list of every missing item, then helped carry wood to keep the clinic warm.

醫舍主管帶念查看藥材庫的一個分部，以及架子使用的木料。一名工人割傷手，護理師先清理血跡，以免疾病傳播。整個下層練功場的人都仰賴這間醫舍，辦公室卻想把它的藥品與私人客舍的庫存合併。一位慷慨的婦人捐了木瓜、麵包和漢堡給病人，她問孩子們今晚能否不再挨餓。念可以想像那份安心，但她真正的目標仍是藥物。她查看配送網絡，發現每位學生名字旁都有分數，彷彿健康是場比賽。她覺得那些分數遠不如人的需要重要。因此，她要求列出每項缺少的物品，還幫忙搬木柴讓醫舍保持溫暖。

Nian put down her pencil long enough to listen to everyone at the table. One person wanted safety, another needed medicine, and a third feared losing a scholarship. Their needs were not all the same. She proposed a step they could check before the next meeting: Nian asked the nurse to record patients before arguing about rank. Jun wrote the plan in plain language and asked for corrections. A woman pointed out that an earlier sentence might put a witness at risk, so they changed it. Nian did not see that correction as weakness. It meant the plan belonged to more people than its first writer. They signed only after the last person had time to read.

念放下鉛筆，先聽完桌邊每個人的話。有人需要安全，有人需要藥物，還有人怕失去獎學金。他們的需求並不相同。念提出一個下次開會前可以檢查的步驟：念請護理師先記錄病人，而不是爭論等級。俊用平易文字寫下計畫並徵求修正。一位婦人指出前一句可能危及證人，於是大家改掉了。念不把修正當成弱點，那表示計畫屬於比原作者更多的人。直到最後一個人讀完，眾人才簽名。

By sunset, the argument had produced a small but real change: the nurse supplied a neutral count of missing medicine. Nian knew some people would call it too little. Perhaps they were right. But the weakest students could not live on a promise about a wonderful future. They needed a meal, a lesson, or a medicine bag now. She asked the group to note who had benefited that day and who was still waiting. The second list was as important as the first. Jun carried the board outside, where anyone passing the gate could read it. Their work was still unfinished, but its result was no longer hidden.

日落時，爭論已帶來微小但真實的變化：護理師提供一份中立的缺藥統計。念知道有人會說這太少，也許他們說得對。但最弱勢的學生不能只靠美好未來的承諾生活，現在就需要食物、課程或藥袋。她請眾人記下當天誰受益、誰仍在等待。第二份名單與第一份同樣重要。俊把公告板搬到外面，讓經過山門的人都能閱讀。他們的工作仍未完成，但成果已不再隱藏。

After the room emptied, Nian checked the final note: the next delivery would arrive in three days, not one. That question was not an answer, and she refused to write it as one. Jun waited while she marked the source and date. He asked whether she ever wished she had chosen an easier path. She laughed and said that learning magic might be easier than persuading people to count honestly. But her work had already given her something rare: people who disagreed with her could still correct her notes. They walked down the hill without a guard or a great speech. Above them, the school's lamps stayed on for the next day's work.

房間清空後，念查看最後一條筆記：下一批藥三天後才會到，而非一天。那是問題，不是答案，她不肯把它寫成結論。俊等她標上來源與日期，問她是否曾希望選一條容易些的路。她笑著說，學法術或許都比勸人誠實算帳簡單。但她的工作已給了她一件罕見的東西：不同意她的人，仍然可以修正她的筆記。他們沒有守衛陪同，也沒有偉大演說，就這樣走下山坡。身後山門的燈還亮著，準備迎接明天的工作。
