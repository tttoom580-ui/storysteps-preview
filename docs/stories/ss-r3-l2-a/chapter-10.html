規則與旗幟

The Rule and the Flag

山門的最後一枚銅牌 · 第 10 / 25 章 · 1000–2000 · 英中對照 · 校訂完成

用互動版查詞、朗讀與收藏

At the training field, teams were tying their shoes beneath a notice promising points only to the fastest runners. Nian arrived to check the supply allocations for the event and found nothing assigned to slower participants. One team included a student recovering from an ankle injury. They asked whether stopping for him would mean losing their water allowance. Jun brought the posted school law from the gate. It promised supplies for safe participation, not only prizes for winning. The director said the fastest team deserved the greatest reward. Nian agreed that a prize could recognize speed, then asked which rule permitted basic support to disappear. The recovering student sat on a bench while the officials argued. His friends remained beside him instead of taking their places without him on the starting line.

練習場上，各隊在只獎勵最快跑者的公告下綁鞋帶。念來查活動物資，卻找不到給慢隊的配額。一隊有剛恢復腳傷的學生，大家問，若停下等他，是否連飲水都失去。俊從門口取來山門公開規章：上面承諾保障安全參與，不只是獎勵勝者。主任說最快的隊伍值得最多回報。念同意速度可以得獎，再問哪條規則允許取消基本支援。受傷學生坐在凳上，朋友陪他，沒有獨自去起跑線。

Nian recorded every team member's participation and every reported injury. Jun marked where the water stations should stand. When Gao insisted that only the finish order mattered, a team captain asked him to read the full event rule aloud. Others repeated the request. The director finally brought the rule book, and the promise of safe supplies could be heard across the field. Nian proposed keeping two records: one for the race result and another for support actually provided. Winning would not be erased, and care would no longer vanish beneath it. The director allowed the stations to open while the broader allocation was reviewed. The injured student chose a shorter supervised route. His team entered together, aware that their final place might suffer, but certain that taking part would not cost them access to water.

念記下隊員參與和每件受傷通報，俊標出飲水站。高管事堅稱只看終點次序，一位隊長請他完整朗讀規則，其他人也跟著要求。主任終於拿來規則簿，安全物資承諾傳遍場地。念提議分兩份紀錄：一份記賽果，一份記實際支援。勝利不被抹去，照顧也不再消失。主任准許先開飲水站，再複查配額。腳傷學生選擇較短、有人監督的路線。隊友一同出發，知道名次可能落後，卻確定參與不會讓自己失去飲水。

Before the race, Nian checked her notebook and drew a straight track from the valley to the flag on the flat hill. She wrote the distance for each active team and used a small sign to indicate where a runner could turn forward. A full cycle would be safe only if everyone could stop for water. Gao showed no concern for that rule. He said the winners would gain riches, while a single slow student would get nothing. But the school law did not say that. One girl carried a wallet in her training suit and asked whether money could change a score. The question made the others fall into silence. A runner noticed that an old record had the word sex beside student names; Nian covered that private field before sharing her notes. The event would occur as planned, but fairness now had a clear place on the track.

競賽前，念查看筆記本，從山谷到平坦山丘上的旗子畫出筆直跑道。她寫下各支活躍隊伍的距離，又用小記號指示跑者能向前轉彎的位置。完整一圈只有在大家都能停下喝水時才安全。高管事卻完全不關心這項規則。他說冠軍會得到財富，而單獨落後的學生什麼也沒有；山門法律卻不是這樣寫的。一個女孩穿著練功服，口袋裡有錢包，她問金錢是否能改變分數。這問題讓大家沉默。一位跑者注意到舊紀錄把『性別』欄寫在姓名旁，念在分享筆記前蓋住那個私人欄位。比賽仍會照計畫舉行，但公平如今在跑道上有了明確的位置。

After the race, the fastest runners stood with the last team while Nian compared the two records. Everyone had needed water, even those who finished first. A winning captain signed the request to keep basic supplies separate from prizes. That signature made the director pause longer than Nian's earlier argument had done. He agreed to meet all the captains the next morning. Jun copied the time onto the notice before anyone went home. Nian helped the recovering student carry his shoes to the bench. He asked whether he had slowed the whole dispute as well as his team. She showed him the winners' signature. His need had revealed a rule that protected them too. The field was emptying, but the next day's conversation would begin with teams that had already learned to speak across the finish order.

賽後，最快跑者和最後一隊一起站著，等念比對兩份紀錄。即使冠軍也需要水。一位獲勝隊長簽下請求，要把基本支援和獎品分開。這個簽名比念早先的爭論更使主任停頓。他答應翌晨見所有隊長，俊在人散前把時間抄上公告。念幫傷腳學生把鞋搬到凳邊。他問自己是否連整場爭議都拖慢了。她指給他看冠軍簽名。他的需要揭開一條也保護對方的規則。場地漸空，但明日會談已有能跨越名次發言的隊伍。
