村裡的引擎

The Village Engine

山門的最後一枚銅牌 · 第 6 / 25 章 · 1000–2000 · 英中對照 · 校訂中

用互動版查詞、朗讀與收藏

Reed Village's planting machine stood in a shed beside baskets of unsown seed. Its owner turned the handle for Nian, and the engine coughed once before stopping. The village had paid a fuel charge to the school, but its budget showed that allowance removed. Jun compared the charge number with the festival entry from the evening class. They matched. Nian asked the owner to leave the engine as it was until the local repair worker could examine it. A dry tank could explain a failure, but forcing an old machine to turn might also break a part. The farmers gathered without their tools. They had expected another promise from the mountain, and she had brought questions instead. She asked what they needed to plant before the weather changed.

蘆村播種機停在堆滿未播種子的棚旁。主人轉動把手，機器咳了一聲便停。村裡已向山門繳燃料費，預算卻刪掉配額。俊核對編號，和夜課查出的節慶支出相同。念請主人先別再試，等地方修理工查看。油箱空能解釋故障，硬轉老機器卻也可能損壞零件。農民沒帶工具就圍過來。他們原以為山上又送來承諾，她卻帶來問題。她問，天候變化前，大家需要什麼才能播種。

The repair worker found that the line needed cleaning as well as fuel. Nian asked which school tools would help, then made a borrowing list for the storekeeper. Gao sent word that villagers had no right to use school equipment. Jun carried the older rule to the shed: village contributions supported the same stores. Rather than break the lock, the farmers invited the storekeeper to bring the requested tools and watch their use. He arrived at noon, nervous and carrying only half the list. The repair worker adjusted the plan. Two students cleaned parts while an experienced farmer checked the line. Nian stood outside with the storekeeper and recorded each tool that entered. He gradually stopped looking toward the road for an order to call everything back.

修理工發現除了缺油，管線也要清潔。念問需要哪些山門工具，列好借用單。高管事傳話說村民無權動用。俊把舊規則帶到棚裡：村莊的貢獻也支持同一倉庫。農民沒有破鎖，而是請庫管親自帶工具來，旁觀使用。他中午才來，神情緊張，只帶一半。修理工調整做法，兩名學生清零件，有經驗的農民檢查管線。念在外面陪庫管，逐件記錄工具進出。他漸漸不再老望路口，擔心有人命令立刻收回。

Reed Village was in a crisis over a broken engine, but people still found time to play badminton near the road. A clever neighbor brought cabbage soup and a clean pillow for the workers. A journal from the local school explained that village culture included helping anyone in need, not only a single powerful individual. A fireman who had once worked by the shore joined the repair. He said the engine was a human problem, not a magical affair, and every person could participate. Nian felt a quiet excitement when the first part began to move, then pushed the machine forth with Jun. A child who had lost one sock offered the other as a joke. Gao called their action theft and threatened prison, but the villagers promised to govern the tool together. The repair gave the children a reason to hope, not simply a reason to celebrate.

蘆村正因引擎故障陷入危機，但人們仍在路邊抽空打羽毛球。一名聰明的鄰居帶來高麗菜湯和乾淨枕頭給工人。當地學校的一本日誌說，村落文化包括幫助每個需要的人，而不只是一位有權勢的人。一名曾在海岸工作的消防員也加入修復。他說引擎是人的問題，不是神奇事務，每個人都可以參與。第一個零件開始轉動時，念感到一陣安靜的興奮，接著和俊把機器向前推。一名掉了一隻襪子的孩子還拿另一隻開玩笑。高管事指控他們偷竊，甚至威脅送監，但村民答應共同管理工具。這次修復讓孩子們有了希望，而不只是慶祝的理由。

When the engine finally turned steadily, nobody let the children climb onto it for a celebration. The repair worker ran a short test before opening the shed doors. Seed moved through the machine without spilling, and the farmers signed their own report of the repair. Nian asked them to include the borrowed tools and remaining fuel, so the next worker would know what had changed. Jun returned every item to the storekeeper. As the last wrench was counted, a visitor from another village asked why Reed had received fuel while his people had received only a bill. He produced a receipt from his pocket. Nian offered him the dry corner of the shed to explain. The repaired engine had solved today's breakdown, while its accounts were beginning to reveal a wider failure.

引擎終於穩定轉動，大家也沒有讓孩子爬上去慶祝。修理工先短測，再開棚門。種子順利通過機器，沒有灑落，農民簽好自己的修復紀錄。念請他們加上借用工具與剩餘燃料，讓下位工人知道變化。俊逐件還給庫管。最後一支扳手點清時，別村來客問，為何蘆村有油，他們卻只收到帳單。他從口袋掏出收據。念請他到棚內乾燥處說明。修好的機器解決今日故障，帳目卻開始揭開更大問題。
