一杯信任

A Cup of Trust

山門的最後一枚銅牌 · 第 11 / 25 章 · 1000–2000 · 英中對照 · 校訂中

用互動版查詞、朗讀與收藏

The captains' agreement did not calm every student. In the lower dormitory, younger apprentices worried that the argument would divide the school into hostile groups. Nian suggested a shared meal before the next hearing. The cook offered tables if families brought what they could, and Jun made a seating plan that mixed classes rather than separating ranks. By evening, strangers were passing bowls to one another across the room. Nian sat near a student who had supported Gao during the race dispute. He expected her to challenge him. Instead she asked whether his team had received enough water. He said yes, then admitted that he had assumed everybody else had too. A practical answer opened a conversation neither would have risked beneath the angry notices at the school gate.

隊長的協議沒有讓所有人放心。下層宿舍的年輕學徒怕爭論把山門分成敵對群體。念提議下次聽證前共餐，廚師答應提供桌子，各家量力帶菜。俊排座位時混合班級，不按位階分桌。傍晚，陌生人互傳碗盤。念坐在一位比賽爭議時支持高管事的學生旁。他以為她要質問，結果她問他的隊伍水夠不夠。他說夠，接著承認自己原以為人人如此。實際回答開啟一段他們在山門憤怒公告前都不敢冒險的談話。

Jun asked each table to propose one fair rule without choosing a leader for the room. One family wanted equal turns at the clinic; another wanted clear notice before fees changed. A wealthy apprentice suggested keeping receipts for shared purchases. Nian wrote that suggestion beside the others and asked him how the system should handle a missing receipt. He offered to help design it. Gao called the gathering a trap and questioned the cook's right to use school food. The cook showed the donations and his purchase receipt. Ren checked them before asking the guard to step back. Nobody needed to turn the cook into a martyr to defend the meal. The evidence was sitting beside the rice pot, available to anyone who wished to read it. The discussion resumed with a rule about recording gifts honestly.

俊請每桌提出一條公平規則，不替全場選領袖。一家要診療公平輪候，一家要費用變動前通知。富裕學徒建議公共採買保留收據，念把它和其他建議並列，問少了收據怎麼辦。他願幫忙設計制度。高管事稱共餐是陷阱，質疑廚師使用山門糧食的權利。廚師出示捐贈表和採買收據。任老師查過才請守衛退後。保護這頓飯，不必把廚師塑成烈士；證據就在飯鍋旁，誰都能看。討論繼續，下一條是誠實記錄贈與。

A beginner sat with a mug of tea and a bunch of grapes, afraid that the school would charge for the shared meal. Nian explained that the goal was not to chase gold, but to show the importance of trust. A cook opened a jam jar with an iron tool and said that food was simply food, whatever a family's religion. He told them a true story of neighbors who had helped one another during a storm. Jun emphasized the usual rule: every voice had value. The social gathering felt like a success until someone tried to set a trap by claiming the cook had taken school goods. A guard raised his hand as if to shoot an arrow, but Teacher Ren stopped him. The cook showed a receipt, and the charge was dropped. Nian felt that one honest meal could teach more than a week of public argument. Ren said, 'I emphasize the same lesson whenever I share a grape with a hungry child: nobody should be left out.'

一名初學者拿著茶杯和一串葡萄坐著，擔心山門會為共餐收費。念解釋目標不是追逐金子，而是表現信任的重要。廚師用鐵工具打開果醬罐，說無論家庭信奉什麼宗教，食物就是食物。他講了一個鄰人在風暴中互助的真實故事。俊強調一項平常的規則：每種聲音都有價值。這場社交聚會原本很成功，直到有人設下圈套，指控廚師偷拿山門物資。一名守衛抬起手，像要射箭，卻被任老師制止。廚師出示收據，指控隨即撤銷。念覺得，一頓誠實的飯可能比一週公開爭論教得更多。任老師說：『每當我把一顆葡萄分給挨餓的孩子，我就會強調同一堂課：不能把任何人排除在外。』

After supper, Nian helped wash bowls with the student who had expected an argument. He said he had trusted Gao because the office always sounded certain. She admitted that she had once copied its numbers without asking why. Neither confession settled the accounts, but it made another conversation possible. The cook waited until the last family had left before showing Jun a delivery note. He remembered receiving fewer sacks than the quantity charged to the school. He had kept silent because merchants were already being blamed for shortages. Nian asked whether he would describe the delivery at the hearing. He agreed if the merchant and carrier could answer too. Jun arranged a visit to the market. The meal ended without a new leader, but with a witness who no longer had to speak into an empty room.

飯後，念和那位原以為會吵架的學生洗碗。他說自己曾信任高管事，因為辦公室總顯得確定。念承認，自己也曾照抄數字而不問理由。兩份承認沒有結清帳目，卻讓下次談話成為可能。最後一家離開後，廚師拿出送貨單。他記得收到的袋數比山門付費的少，因為商人已被怪罪短缺，所以一直不敢說。念問他願不願聽證說明。他答應，條件是商人與運送者也能回答。俊安排市場參訪。共餐沒產生新領袖，卻多了一位不必對空房發言的證人。
