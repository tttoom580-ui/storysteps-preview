一堂課的價錢

The Cost of a Lesson

山門的最後一枚銅牌 · 第 5 / 25 章 · 1000–2000 · 英中對照

用互動版查詞、朗讀與收藏

At first light, Nian returned to the student dining hall. The mountain school had many beautiful halls, but she had learned that a hall could be beautiful and still hide an unfair rule. She was nineteen, old enough to keep records and young enough for the masters to dismiss her questions. Today the problem was plain: a new fee made the evening class too expensive. Jun, her twenty-year-old friend, walked beside her with a small notebook. They had promised not to make a charge they could not support. They would write what they saw, ask who had decided it, and leave room for an honest answer.

天亮時，念回到學生食堂。山門有許多美麗堂舍，但她已學到，美麗的堂舍也可能藏著不公平的規則。她十九歲，年紀足以負責記錄，卻又年輕得讓師父們輕視她的疑問。今天的問題很明白：新收費使夜課變得負擔不起。二十歲的朋友俊帶著小筆記本同行。他們約定不提出無法證實的指控。他們會記下所見、追問決定者是誰，也保留讓人誠實回答的空間。

The first useful sign came from an ordinary piece of evidence: the school had counted volunteer work as paid instruction. Nian did not touch it until a second person had seen where it lay. Jun drew a small mark on his page, then wrote the date and the names of those present. That care was slow work. Sometimes a student waiting at the door asked why they did not simply take the paper and run. Nian answered that a fair account had to remain fair even when it described someone they disliked. If a record could be changed in anger, it would not help the next person who needed it.

第一個有用線索來自平凡的證據：山門把義務工作算成收費教學。第二個人確認放置位置前，念沒有碰它。俊在頁上做小記號，再寫下日期及在場者姓名。這種謹慎很費時間。有時等在門口的學生問，他們為什麼不拿起文件直接跑掉。念回答，公平帳目即使記載自己不喜歡的人，也必須保持公平。如果一份紀錄能在憤怒中任意更改，它就無法幫助下一個需要它的人。

Steward Gao came with two assistants and a voice that filled the room. His claim was familiar: Gao warned that a protest could cost Nian her place. He asked Nian whether she wanted to become master of the school. She said no. She wanted the people who worked here to understand the decisions that affected their food, lessons, and future. Gao laughed at the word future as if it were a child's toy. A few students lowered their eyes. Jun stayed beside Nian but did not speak for her. This was her question to raise, and she would decide how far to carry it.

高管事帶著兩名助手到來，聲音響徹房間。他的說法並不陌生：高管事警告抗議會讓念失去學籍。他問念是不是想當山門掌門。她說不是。她只希望在此工作的人了解影響他們食物、課程與未來的決定。高管事嘲笑『未來』一詞，彷彿那是孩子的玩具。幾名學生低下頭。俊站在念身邊，卻沒有代她發言。這是她提出的問題，該走多遠也由她決定。

The college kitchen had become a place of arguments about expense. A lady in a blue sweater swept the floor while a fellow student tried to swallow a hard bun. The new class fee would double each family's burden, although the evening lesson needed only a lamp and a few books. Jun drew a small chart to show the function of each payment. The most expensive feature was not food production or teacher time, but a private room that students seldom used. One student uploaded the chart, and the message spread with the spirit of a soccer team defending its goal. Another called the protest a perfect chance to cause trouble. Gao used the frightening word terrorist, but nobody had threatened anyone. Nian handed a tissue to a crying child and asked for a fair selection of costs. She wanted useful lessons, not a victory bought with fear. She asked the helper to sweep the hall and upload the final cost table only after both sides checked it.

學院食堂成了爭論費用的地方。一位穿藍毛衣的女士一邊掃地，一名同學一邊勉強吞下硬麵包。新學費會讓各家負擔加倍，然而夜課其實只需要油燈和幾本書。俊畫了小圖表，標出各項付款的用途。最貴的項目不是食物生產或教師時間，而是學生很少使用的私人房間。一名學生上傳圖表，消息像足球隊守護球門的精神一樣傳開。另一人說抗議是惹事的完美機會。高管事甚至用了『恐怖分子』這個嚇人的詞，但沒人威脅過任何人。念遞給哭泣的孩子一張面紙，要求公平選擇開支。她想要有用的課程，不想用恐懼換取勝利。她請助手先清掃大廳，等雙方確認後再上傳最終費用表。

Nian had learned that a public claim needed a public action. Instead of hiding the problem in her own room, Jun compared the real cost of lamps, food, and teachers. The choice carried a risk. Someone might lose a lesson, a wage, or a friend's trust while the school argued. She asked the people around her whether they agreed to put their names beside the record. Those who said no were allowed to leave. Those who said yes were given time to read it first. When Jun put down his pen, Nian noticed that he had left a space for later corrections. That empty space mattered to her as much as the first signature.

念已學會，公開主張需要公開行動。她沒有把問題藏在自己房裡，而是俊比較油燈、食物與師資的實際成本。這項選擇有風險。山門爭論時，有人可能失去課程、薪資或朋友的信任。她詢問周圍的人是否願意在紀錄旁簽名。說不的人可以離開；說好的人也有時間先閱讀內容。俊放下筆時，念注意到他預留了日後更正的空間。那塊空白對她而言，和第一個簽名一樣重要。

By evening, the day had brought one result that could be checked: three teachers agreed to open one free class. It was not the end of the dispute. The powerful teachers still had rooms, supplies, and friends who would defend them. The apprentices had only their shared notes and the patience to read them again. Nian wrote the result on the public board without adding a line of praise for herself. Some people wanted a hero, but she knew a hero could make the same mistake as a master. A rule that depended on one good person was still a weak rule. They needed a way for ordinary people to correct it.

傍晚時，這一天帶來一項可以核對的結果：三位老師同意開設一堂免費課。爭議還沒結束。掌權的老師仍有房間、物資和願意替他們辯護的朋友。學徒只有共同筆記，以及重新閱讀的耐心。念把結果寫上公告板，沒有加上一句讚美自己的話。有人想要英雄，但她知道英雄也可能犯下與掌門相同的錯。依賴一位好人的制度仍然脆弱。他們需要的是讓一般人能修正制度的方法。

Before leaving, Nian found another question in the notes: a hidden expense was recorded under festival services. She looked at Jun, who was already closing the last window. He asked whether they could stop for the night. They could, she said, because tired people made poor judges. But tomorrow they would follow the question, wherever it led. Outside, the mountain paths were dark and the lamps in the village were small. Nian could not promise that the school would change quickly. She could promise that nobody would have to accept a secret number merely because an important person had written it. She folded the page and kept it dry until morning.

離開前，念在筆記裡找到另一個問題：一筆隱藏開支被列在節慶服務之下。她望向正在關最後一扇窗的俊。俊問今晚能不能停下。她說可以，因為疲累的人容易做出糟糕判斷。但明天他們會繼續追查，不管線索通往哪裡。外頭山路黑暗，村裡燈火微弱。念無法保證山門很快改變，卻能保證人們不必只因某位大人物寫下數字，就接受不透明的結果。她摺起紙頁，妥善保存到早晨。
