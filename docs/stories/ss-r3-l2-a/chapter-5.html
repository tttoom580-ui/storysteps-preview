一堂課的價錢

The Cost of a Lesson

山門的最後一枚銅牌 · 第 5 / 25 章 · 1000–2000 · 英中對照

用互動版查詞、朗讀與收藏

The evening classroom remained dark because half its students had not paid the new fee. Nian found them in the dining room comparing the coins they had brought. One student had enough for the lesson only if he skipped the next two meals. Jun asked to see the cost sheet. It charged for teacher hours that three teachers said they had offered freely. The largest other charge paid for a private reception room the class never entered. Nian carried the sheet to the kitchen table rather than asking the hungry students to wait outside an office. A teacher came with a record of her unpaid hours. She had thought her work made the class affordable; now she saw it was being sold back to the students.

夜課教室仍黑著，因為半數學生繳不起新費用。念在食堂找到他們，大家正比較帶來的硬幣。一個學生若付學費，就得少吃兩餐。俊查看成本表，裡面竟計入三位老師自願提供的教學時數。另一大筆費用，是學生從沒進過的私人接待室。念把表帶到廚房桌邊，不讓飢餓的學生在辦公室外等。一位老師拿來無薪工時紀錄。她本以為自願服務能降低學費，如今卻發現自己的勞動被轉賣給學生。

Gao warned Nian that public protest could cost her place. She asked whether correcting a double charge counted as protest. Jun drew separate columns for lamps, food, teachers, and the reception room, then invited the clerk to supply missing receipts. The teacher crossed out a charge beside her own name and signed the correction. That small act encouraged the others to bring their records forward. Nobody claimed the school could run without money. They wanted to know which expenses belonged to this class. When Gao used a frightening accusation against the group, the cook asked him to identify a threat anyone had made. The room fell quiet enough for the scratching of Jun's pencil to be heard. He finished the comparison without adding the accusation to the students' names.

高管事警告念，公開抗議會使她失去學籍。她問，更正重複收費是否也算抗議。俊分列燈油、食物、師資與接待室，請書記補收據。老師刪掉自己名字旁的收費，簽下更正。這個小動作鼓勵其他人拿出紀錄。沒人認為山門不用錢運作；大家想知道哪些支出屬於這堂課。高管事用可怕的指控形容他們，廚師便請他指出有人做了什麼威脅。房裡安靜得能聽見鉛筆聲。俊完成比對，沒有把指控加在學生姓名旁。

The college kitchen had become a place of arguments about expense. A lady in a blue sweater swept the floor while a fellow student tried to swallow a hard bun. The new class fee would double each family's burden, although the evening lesson needed only a lamp and a few books. Jun drew a small chart to show the function of each payment. The most expensive feature was not food production or teacher time, but a private room that students seldom used. One student uploaded the chart, and the message spread with the spirit of a soccer team defending its goal. Another called the protest a perfect chance to cause trouble. Gao used the frightening word terrorist, but nobody had threatened anyone. Nian handed a tissue to a crying child and asked for a fair selection of costs. She wanted useful lessons, not a victory bought with fear. She asked the helper to sweep the hall and upload the final cost table only after both sides checked it.

學院食堂成了爭論費用的地方。一位穿藍毛衣的女士一邊掃地，一名同學一邊勉強吞下硬麵包。新學費會讓各家負擔加倍，然而夜課其實只需要油燈和幾本書。俊畫了小圖表，標出各項付款的用途。最貴的項目不是食物生產或教師時間，而是學生很少使用的私人房間。一名學生上傳圖表，消息像足球隊守護球門的精神一樣傳開。另一人說抗議是惹事的完美機會。高管事甚至用了『恐怖分子』這個嚇人的詞，但沒人威脅過任何人。念遞給哭泣的孩子一張面紙，要求公平選擇開支。她想要有用的課程，不想用恐懼換取勝利。她請助手先清掃大廳，等雙方確認後再上傳最終費用表。

Three teachers agreed to open one free class while the fee was reviewed. They set a limit of one week and asked the school to provide the lamp oil already bought for instruction. Nian helped carry the benches back, but she did not announce a permanent victory. The volunteers could not replace a fair budget forever. After the first lesson began, Jun showed her a charge hidden under festival services. It described fuel that should have gone to Reed Village's seed engine. Nian looked from the line to the classroom lamp. Money assigned to shared work kept finding its way into private events. She copied the charge number and asked the village messenger whether the engine had run that week. His answer took them down the mountain the following morning.

三位老師同意在費用複查期間開一堂免費課，期限一週，並請山門提供已為教學購買的燈油。念幫忙搬回長凳，沒有宣布永久勝利，因為志工不能永遠代替公平預算。第一堂課開始後，俊給她看一筆藏在節慶服務下的支出，竟是蘆村播種機本應使用的燃料。念望望那行，再望教室燈火。分配給公共工作的錢，一再流向私人活動。她抄下費用編號，問村裡信使，機器這週是否運轉。答案讓他們翌晨下山。
