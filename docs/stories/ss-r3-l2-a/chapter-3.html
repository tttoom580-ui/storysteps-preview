山門的最後一枚銅牌 · 第 3 / 25 章 · 1000–2000 · 英中對照

The summons reached Nian before breakfast. She was accused of removing forbidden school records, although the book remained beneath the barber's bench. Gao had placed a copy of her photograph on Teacher Ren's table and circled the school stamp. Jun brought his wider photograph, which showed the stamp beside the words public shelf. Nian wanted Ren to dismiss the accusation immediately. Instead he asked her to describe how she had obtained permission and where the original now stood. She answered each question, including the second photograph taken to repair the glare. Ren called the barber to confirm her account. Gao objected that ordinary tradespeople should not decide a student's future. Ren said a witness's occupation did not erase what that witness had seen.

念早餐前收到傳喚，被指拿走禁閱紀錄，然而書仍在理髮椅下。高管事把她的照片放在任老師桌上，圈出山門印記。俊拿出範圍更大的照片，印記旁寫著「公開書架」。念想請老師立刻駁回指控，任老師卻先問她如何得到同意、原件如今在哪裡。她逐項回答，也說明為補反光而重拍。任老師聯繫理髮師核實。高管事反對讓普通商人決定學生前途，任老師說，職業不能抹去見證人親眼所見。

Gao then demanded a test of Nian's fitness to remain at the school. Ren accepted a practical exercise but refused an immediate expulsion. He placed a faulty training lamp on the table and asked her to show her method before touching a tool. Nian described the parts, checked that the power was off, and asked Jun to hold the instruction sheet where everyone could see it. Her fingers shook when she opened the case. Finding a broken connection in front of a hostile room felt harder than tracing a number in quiet. Ren waited. Nian compared the diagram with the actual wire and pointed to the difference before repairing it. The lamp lit after the safety check, while Gao's demand still lay unanswered on the table.

高管事接著要求測驗念是否配留在山門。任老師接受實作，卻拒絕立刻逐出她。他把故障訓練燈放在桌上，請她先說方法再碰工具。念描述零件、確認斷電，請俊把說明紙舉給大家看。開外殼時她的手在抖。在充滿敵意的房間找斷線，比安靜追數字更難。任老師等她。念比對圖和實際電線，指出差別，再完成修復。安全確認後燈亮了，高管事的逐出要求仍擱在桌上。

When the alarm sounded, Nian kept calm and walked into the hall. A classic drawing of a cowboy hung beside a broken dryer; someone had used it for a joke during last week's tour. On the table stood an empty cell, an oil lamp, and a small model boat with a paper sail. Ren gave a direct instruction: no one could press a switch or strike the machine until the class had checked every object. A young student with a fresh haircut offered to help. Another had brought a lost lamb from the yard, and its soft cry stopped the room from feeling like a prison. Gao demanded punishment for Nian, but Ren said his duty was to hear the facts. Nian found a false connection, repaired the wire, and made the light shine with one clean shot of power. A fair trade of evidence, she said, should matter more than a loud threat.

警鈴響起時，念保持冷靜，走進大廳。壞掉的烘乾器旁掛著一幅典型的牛仔畫；上週參觀時有人拿它開玩笑。桌上放著空電池槽、油燈和一艘有紙帆的小模型船。任老師直接指示：在檢查過每樣物品之前，不准按開關，也不准敲打機器。一名剛理髮的年輕學生主動幫忙，另一人則從院裡帶來走失的小羊，牠輕柔的叫聲讓大廳不像監獄。高管事要求處罰念，但任老師說自己的職責是聽取事實。念找出錯誤接線，修好電線，讓燈以一次乾淨的供能亮起。她說，公平交換證據應該比大聲威脅更重要。

Ren recorded that Nian could remain while the disputed records received a fair hearing. It was a limited decision, and Nian felt its limits when Gao asked for the photographs to be surrendered. Ren kept one copy with the complaint and returned another to Jun. The material would be examined, not quietly destroyed. As the room emptied, Nian sat beside the repaired lamp until her hands stopped trembling. Jun asked whether she had been frightened. She said yes, and asked why he had not answered every question for her. He said he could confirm their visit, but he could not take her examination. She laughed for the first time that morning. They left with an appointment for the clinic, where a shortage might explain another part of the missing supplies.

任老師記錄，爭議資料接受公平聽證前，念可以留校。這只是有限決定；高管事要求交出照片時，她更清楚感受到限制。任老師留一份附在申訴上，另一份還給俊。資料要受檢查，不能悄悄銷毀。人散後，念坐在修好的燈旁，等雙手不再發抖。俊問她怕不怕。她說怕，也問他為何不代答所有問題。他說自己能證實參訪，卻不能替她受考。她那天第一次笑了。兩人帶著診療所約定離開，那裡的短缺也許能說明另一部分失蹤物資。
