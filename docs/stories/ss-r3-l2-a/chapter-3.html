考核鐘聲

The Trial Bell

山門的最後一枚銅牌 · 第 3 / 25 章 · 1000–2000 · 英中對照

用互動版查詞、朗讀與收藏

Nian arrived at the hall for novice trials after walking past the kitchen fires. She was tired from the previous day's meeting, but the problem waiting for her was too important to ignore: Nian was accused of taking forbidden records. She had no special sword and no famous family name. What she did have was a careful hand, a pencil, and the memory of people who had trusted her with their stories. Jun joined her a moment later. He reminded her that their work belonged to the whole group, not to the person who carried the notebook. They chose a place where the public could watch, and began with the simplest question.

念走過廚房灶火，來到初階考核堂。前一天的會議使她疲累，但眼前的問題太重要，不能忽視：念被控偷取禁閱紀錄。她沒有特別的寶劍，也沒有顯赫家世。她有的是細心的手、一枝鉛筆，以及那些託付故事之人的信任。俊隨後到來，提醒她這份工作屬於整個群體，而不是拿著筆記本的人。他們選了民眾能旁觀的位置，從最簡單的問題開始。

Jun found a detail the last visitor had overlooked: her copy bore the stamp of a public shelf. Nian asked him to write the exact place where the evidence had been kept. They also recorded who had opened the room and who had watched. A younger apprentice offered to make the story sound more dramatic. Nian thanked him, then refused. The people who would read the report needed an account they could test, not words chosen to win applause. She knew their work could fail. A missing date or an unfair guess would give Gao an easy excuse to hide every other fact. So they checked the small things first.

俊找到上一位訪客忽略的細節：她的副本蓋著公開書架印記。念請他寫下證據原本存放的確切位置，也記錄開門的人和在場旁觀者。一位較年輕的學徒想把故事寫得更戲劇化。念道謝後仍拒絕了。讀報告的人需要能檢驗的敘述，而不是為贏得掌聲挑選的詞句。她知道工作可能失敗，缺少一個日期或不公平的猜測，就可能讓高管事有藉口隱藏其餘事實。所以他們先核對小細節。

Steward Gao had supporters in every important room, and today they followed him inside. He repeated his position without looking at the public board: Gao demanded her removal before hearing a reply. Nian could feel the others waiting for her to shout back. She wanted to, but raised voices would not return a missing bag of medicine. She asked a narrower question instead: who had signed the order, and where was the original copy? Gao gave an answer about loyalty to the school. Nian wrote it down, then asked the same question once more. People at the back leaned forward. For a moment, the quiet in the room was stronger than his anger.

高管事在每個重要房間都有支持者，今天他們也跟著進來。他沒看公開公告板，就再次表明立場：高管事要求未聽答辯就將她逐出。念感覺其他人都在等她大聲反駁。她也想如此，但提高音量不會找回失蹤的藥袋。她改問更精確的問題：誰簽了命令，原件在哪裡？高管事回答的是忠於山門的重要。念把話記下，再問一次同樣的問題。後排的人向前傾身。那一刻，室內的安靜比他的怒氣更有力量。

When the alarm sounded, Nian kept calm and walked into the hall. A classic drawing of a cowboy hung beside a broken dryer; someone had used it for a joke during last week's tour. On the table stood an empty cell, an oil lamp, and a small model boat with a paper sail. Ren gave a direct instruction: no one could press a switch or strike the machine until the class had checked every object. A young student with a fresh haircut offered to help. Another had brought a lost lamb from the yard, and its soft cry stopped the room from feeling like a prison. Gao demanded punishment for Nian, but Ren said his duty was to hear the facts. Nian found a false connection, repaired the wire, and made the light shine with one clean shot of power. A fair trade of evidence, she said, should matter more than a loud threat.

警鈴響起時，念保持冷靜，走進大廳。壞掉的烘乾器旁掛著一幅典型的牛仔畫；上週參觀時有人拿它開玩笑。桌上放著空電池槽、油燈和一艘有紙帆的小模型船。任老師直接指示：在檢查過每樣物品之前，不准按開關，也不准敲打機器。一名剛理髮的年輕學生主動幫忙，另一人則從院裡帶來走失的小羊，牠輕柔的叫聲讓大廳不像監獄。高管事要求處罰念，但任老師說自己的職責是聽取事實。念找出錯誤接線，修好電線，讓燈以一次乾淨的供能亮起。她說，公平交換證據應該比大聲威脅更重要。

A few apprentices wanted to rush to the head office. Others wanted to turn back. Nian suggested a third way: Teacher Ren required a practical test instead of an accusation. She told them to keep an exact copy and ask two people to witness every transfer. The instruction was not exciting, and one boy complained that no story would be written about such boring work. Jun smiled. He said that the best ending would be a school where nobody needed to become famous just to receive a fair share. Nian agreed, but she also warned that taking action might bring another difficult hearing. Every person was free to choose whether to continue.

幾個學徒想衝到總部，其他人卻想退回去。念提出第三條路：任老師要求以實作測試代替指控。她請大家保留準確副本，並讓兩人見證每次移交。這指示不刺激，一個男孩抱怨沒人會寫這種無聊工作的故事。俊笑了。他說最好的結局，是學校不必有人出名也能得到公平配額。念表示同意，但也警告行動可能帶來另一場艱難聽證。每個人都可以自由選擇是否繼續。

When the bell rang for the evening meal, the workers could point to one result: Nian earned the right to remain until a fair hearing. Nian let herself enjoy that much. Jun found a seat by the door and asked if she thought the masters would finally agree. She said the answer was uncertain. A person could accept a fact and still refuse a fair change. The new rule would have to work on an ordinary day, when no one was watching and no crowd was ready to clap. She wrote the agreed result in three copies, one for the school, one for the villages, and one for the public reading room.

晚餐鐘響時，工人已能指出一項結果：念贏得留到公平聽證舉行的權利。念允許自己為此高興。俊在門邊找到座位，問她師父們是否終於會同意。她說答案還不確定。人可能接受事實，卻仍拒絕公平改革。新規則必須在平凡的日子也能運作，即使沒人監督、也沒有群眾準備鼓掌。她把商定的結果抄成三份，一份給山門，一份給村莊，一份放在公開閱覽室。

The public board was almost full, but one question remained: the hearing was delayed but the students kept their evidence. Nian drew a line beneath it so nobody would mistake a question for a proved charge. Jun packed their papers inside a cloth bag. On the way home they passed an old woman carrying firewood, and both stopped to help her. She thanked them without asking about the council or the masters. The simple exchange reminded Nian why the accounts mattered. They were not lines written for a grand battle. They were promises about who could eat, work, study, and rest. Tomorrow the question on the board would lead them forward again.

公開公告板幾乎寫滿，卻還留著一個問題：聽證延期，學生卻保住了證據。念在下方畫線，免得有人把疑問誤認為已證實的指控。俊把紙頁收進布袋。回家路上，他們遇到一名搬木柴的老婦人，便停下幫忙。她道謝時沒問議事會或師父的事。這簡單互動提醒念帳目為何重要。那些不是為了偉大戰役而寫的線條，而是關於誰能吃飯、工作、學習與休息的承諾。明天，板上的問題又會帶他們向前。
