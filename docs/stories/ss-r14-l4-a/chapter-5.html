假的英雄

The False Hero

零點拍賣：把睡眠還給城市 · 第 5 / 25 章 · 3000–4000 · 英中對照 · 校訂中

A newly formed rescue agency asked Li to endorse its campaign: “Give your sleep to a champion, and everyone will be saved.” The agency had decorated its reception desk with banners and heroic portraits. One showed a masked fighter fighting a witch; another showed a robber stealing time from a child. Both pictures promised terror and victory in equal measure. Li asked the director why the campaign did not mention the people who would lose their rest. He said such detail would discourage donations. “Then your slogan is shameful,” she answered. “A choice is not real if you hide its cost.” A camera recorded her reply, and by sunset the short exchange had spread through the market.

新成立的救援機構請江梨替宣傳活動背書：「把睡眠交給冠軍，所有人就會得救。」機構在接待櫃檯掛滿旗幟和英雄肖像。一張畫著蒙面鬥士對抗女巫，另一張畫著強盜偷走孩子的時間。兩張圖都同時販賣恐懼與勝利。江梨問負責人，為何宣傳沒提到誰會失去休息。他說這些細節會打消捐贈意願。「那你的口號就很可恥。」江梨回答，「如果隱瞞代價，選擇就不是真正的選擇。」攝影機拍下回答，傍晚前短片已傳遍市場。

The director invited an influential city delegate to praise the fighter's behavior. She described the plan as fundamental to public safety and said the emergency congress would adopt it within days. Li's conscience would not let her stay silent. She had seen the musician collapse and Samir lose an hour without permission. “Please publish the actual interaction between a gifted hour and a tired body,” she said. “Show the outcome for people who bid six hours, not just for the person who receives the prize.” The delegate called her primitive for thinking about individual bodies instead of citywide victory. Li did not insult her in return. She requested figures, dates, and a method that anyone could check.

負責人邀請一名有影響力的市議代表，稱讚格鬥選手的行為。代表說這項計畫是公共安全的根本措施，緊急議會會在幾天內採納。江梨的良心讓她無法沉默。她看過樂手昏倒，也看過薩米爾在沒有許可下失去一小時。「請公開贈出時數對疲憊身體造成的實際影響，」她說，「讓大家看見出價六小時的人會怎樣，而不只是獲獎的人得到什麼。」代表嘲笑她思想原始，只關心個人的身體，不重視全市勝利。江梨沒有回罵，只要求數據、日期，以及任何人都能檢查的方法。

During the reception, a child recognized the fighter as a man who had pushed the musician aside on the first night. The child spoke in a small voice, but a reporter heard. The fighter denied the story and accused the child of seeking attention. Li did not allow an angry crowd to surround him. The child's safety mattered more than turning a witness into a weapon. Nari took the child to a quiet room, where an adult the child trusted could sit beside her. A camera from the street later showed the push, although it could not explain the man's intention. It was evidence of an action, not permission to invent every detail about the person who had done it.

接待活動上，一名孩子認出格鬥選手就是第一夜推開樂手的人。孩子聲音很小，但記者聽見了。選手否認，指控孩子想引人注意。江梨不讓憤怒的人群包圍他。孩子的安全比把證人當成武器重要。娜里帶孩子進安靜房間，讓她信任的成年人陪著。街頭攝影機後來拍到推人的動作，卻無法解釋對方的意圖。那是行為的證據，不是讓人任意捏造整個人的許可。

Li asked the delegation to pause the proposed law until people could see the consequences. The director warned that such hesitation might horrify donors who believed the crisis could be fixed quickly. Li answered that she was not asking anyone to cherish delays for their own sake. She wanted the city to understand what it was buying. A reporter asked whether she thought every donor had been fooled. She said no; some people had given freely and knowingly, while others had been pressured or wrongly recorded. The distinction was important. Without it, a fair correction could become another unfair punishment. By the end of the hearing, the delegate agreed to release a sample of anonymous bid records the next day.

江梨請代表團先暫停提案，直到民眾看得見後果。負責人警告，這種猶豫可能讓相信危機可以迅速解決的捐款者感到驚恐。江梨說，她不是為了拖延而珍惜拖延，而是希望城市知道自己究竟買了什麼。記者問她，是不是認為每個捐贈者都受騙。她說不是：有人確實在了解情況後自由捐出，也有人受到施壓或被錯誤登記。這個區別很重要，否則公平的更正又會變成另一種不公平的懲罰。聽證結束時，代表同意次日公開一部分去識別化的出價紀錄。

Later, Nari removed the decoration from the agency's reception desk. She had been hired to clean there and was allowed to tidy the public area, but she did not throw away the materials. Instead, she placed them in storage and wrote a note explaining where they could be found. The director protested that she was spoiling his campaign. Nari answered that patients needed a clear view of the doorway more than a wall of heroic pictures. The photographer tested the line of sight and admitted she was right. One small change made the entrance easier to use without forcing anyone to agree with Li.

後來，娜里把機構接待櫃檯的裝飾撤下。她受雇清潔那裡，有權整理公共區域，但沒有把材料丟掉，而是收進倉庫，留下紙條說明存放位置。負責人抗議她破壞宣傳。娜里回答，病人需要清楚看到門口，勝過一整面英雄肖像。攝影師測試視線後承認她說得對。一項小改動就讓入口更方便使用，並不需要逼任何人同意江梨的想法。

Outside the building, a boy pointed at the sunset scenery painted behind the fighter's portrait. “Does the winning person get to sleep in that place?” he asked. Li did not know. The auction's prize was a protected hall, not a promise of a better life. She told him they would have to read the contract before deciding. As the crowd moved away, Kena counted those who had volunteered to share records: eleven workers, three students, and one retired teacher. None owned the scoreboard. Yet together they had more information than the agency had chosen to display. Li understood that the first real contest was not about who could remain awake longest. It was about who had the right to speak for a sleeping person.

建築外，一名男孩指著選手肖像背後的夕陽風景問：「贏的人就可以睡在那個地方嗎？」江梨不知道。拍賣的獎品是一座受保護的大廳，不代表能得到更好的生活。她說必須先讀合約才能判斷。人群散去時，珂娜數了自願分享紀錄的人：十一名工人、三名學生、一名退休教師。他們沒有人擁有計分板，合在一起卻比機構選擇公開的資料還多。江梨明白，第一場真正的競賽不是誰能撐得最久不睡，而是誰有權替一個睡著的人說話。
