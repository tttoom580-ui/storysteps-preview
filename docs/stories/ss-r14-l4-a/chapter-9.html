The Clerk Who Kept a Copy

零點拍賣：把睡眠還給城市 · 第 9 / 25 章 · 3000–4000 · 英中對照 · 校訂中

用互動版查詞、朗讀與收藏

A former auction clerk named Qi Du appeared at the library with a battered autobiography in his bag. He had written about the years when the city opened its first public sleeping halls. At that time, the halls stood beside an association of volunteer shelters, and admission was free. Qi was bald, quiet, and reluctant to make declarations. He had resigned after a minister ordered the association's records moved to private machinery. “I made a copy,” he said, “because the original system could be restored if the new one failed.” Li did not ask him to become a hero. She asked him what part of the copy he could verify from personal memory, and what part had to be checked elsewhere.

一名前拍賣職員杜棋帶著一本破舊自傳出現在圖書館。他寫過城市第一批公共休息大廳開放的年代。當時大廳設在志工避難協會旁，入場免費。杜棋禿頭、安靜，不願隨便發表宣言。他在一名部長要求把協會紀錄搬進私人機器系統後辭職。「我留下了副本，」他說，「萬一新系統失敗，還有可能恢復舊系統。」江梨沒有要求他當英雄，而是問他哪些內容憑親身記憶能確認、哪些需要到別處核查。

Qi showed them a forecast prepared by the city before the sale. It warned that a private operator might charge higher fees after taking control. A handwritten quotation from the report had later been changed from “may arise” to “will never arise.” That difference had consequences: the public had been told there was no risk. Li asked Ada to compare the two versions. The archive copy still carried its original date and signatures. Qi also had a list of vacancies created when the old staff had been dismissed. Several workers on that list were now being charged for sleeping in rooms they had once maintained. Li wondered who had benefited from rewriting the warning.

杜棋拿出出售前市府做的預測報告，警告私人業者接管後可能提高收費。報告的一句手寫引文後來從「可能發生」變成「絕不會發生」。這項差異造成後果：民眾被告知毫無風險。江梨請艾達比較兩個版本。檔案副本仍保留原日期和簽名。杜棋還有舊員工被解雇後出現的職缺名單。名單上好幾個人如今必須為住進自己曾維護的房間付費。江梨想知道，是誰從改寫警告中獲益。

The game interrupted their conversation with a demand to halt all unlicensed record viewing. Two guards approached the library's rusty back door. Li asked them which rule had been violated. Neither could identify one. Nari offered them water, while Qi called the council office and described the documents without sharing private names. The guards waited in the doorway. Kena's curiosity almost sent her outside to photograph their faces, but Li stopped her. Their disagreement was with the order, not with the dignity of every person wearing a uniform. By the time the guards received a reply, the library had logged the request and the reason it was refused.

遊戲突然打斷談話，命令停止所有未經授權的文件查閱。兩名保全走向圖書館生鏽的後門。江梨問他們違反了哪條規則，兩人都說不出來。娜里遞水給他們；杜棋打電話到議會辦公室，說明文件內容而不透露私人姓名。保全在門口等候。珂娜好奇得差點跑出去拍他們的臉，江梨攔住她。他們反對的是命令，不是任何穿制服者都該失去尊嚴。等到保全收到回覆，圖書館已記下這次要求與拒絕理由。

Qi took them to the station's old records room, where a fireplace had been covered with plaster. Inside lay a manual for a mechanical override. It had nothing to do with burning coal; the fireplace was simply the only compartment the private operator had not emptied. The manual described a procedure for releasing sleeping hours held without consent. It required a public ledger, two independent witnesses, and a complete overnight rest period in which no person was forced to bid. Li stared at the last condition. The game had encouraged people to remain awake and compete, making the repair procedure seem impossible. Yet the text had been present for years, waiting for someone to use it.

杜棋帶她們到車站舊檔案室，那裡的壁爐口早被灰泥封住。裡面藏著一本機械式覆核手冊。它與燒煤無關，壁爐只是私人業者唯一沒有清空的隔間。手冊記載釋放未經同意扣押睡眠時數的方法：需要公開帳冊、兩名獨立證人，以及一整段沒有人被迫出價的夜間休息期。江梨盯著最後一項條件。遊戲一直鼓勵人們熬夜競爭，使修復程序看起來不可能。然而文字多年來一直存在，只等有人去使用。

Qi remembered the consequent loss of jobs when the private operator took control. At the station café, a cook had once served lobster pasta to the dismissed workers at a farewell meal; now the restaurant displayed a vacancy for an overnight cleaner. The staff depended on those hours to pay rent. Qi remembered being dependent on the same fragile wages himself. Li did not declare that every manager intended to violate labor rules. She wanted to restore the old records and learn who had approved each change. Qi agreed to help, provided that his own account could remain private. He had kept the manual so that a future worker would have a choice, not so that he would become a public figure.

杜棋記得私人業者接管後接踵而來的失業。當年車站咖啡館的廚師曾煮龍蝦義大利麵，替遭解雇工人辦送別餐；如今餐廳貼著夜班清潔工的職缺。員工仰賴那些工時付房租。杜棋記得自己也曾依賴同樣不穩定的工資。江梨沒有宣稱每個經理都想違反勞動規則，她想恢復舊紀錄，查清每次改動由誰核准。杜棋願意幫忙，條件是自己的帳戶仍可保密。他當年留手冊，是為讓後來的工人有選擇，不是想讓自己成為公眾人物。

Qi warned that an ancient-looking manual was not enough to prove a current mechanism worked. Li agreed. They would first test the procedure on a single disputed account, with consent from its owner and a record of every step. A shelter offered a room for the test, but no volunteer would be asked to give up sleep to make it happen. Li returned to the library with the copy wrapped in dry cloth. At midnight the auction announced that there were sixteen nights left before the public halls became a prize. Qi sat down for the first time that day. His hands shook. He admitted that he had been frightened of the machinery ever since he left his job. Li told him he could help without carrying the whole case alone.

杜棋提醒，古老的手冊不足以證明現在機制仍有效。江梨同意。他們要先在單一爭議帳戶上測試，取得帳戶主人同意，並完整記錄過程。一處避難所提供房間，但不會要求志工為了測試放棄睡眠。江梨把副本包在乾布裡，帶回圖書館。午夜時拍賣宣布，公共大廳還有十六夜就會成為獎品。杜棋那天第一次坐下，雙手微微發抖。他承認自從離職，就一直害怕那套機械。江梨告訴他，可以幫忙，卻不必獨自扛下整件事。
