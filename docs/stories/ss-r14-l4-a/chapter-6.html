借來時數的聲音

The Sound of Borrowed Hours

零點拍賣：把睡眠還給城市 · 第 6 / 25 章 · 3000–4000 · 英中對照 · 校訂中

用互動版查詞、朗讀與收藏

The sixth night began with an anniversary concert marking a hundred years since the old railway opened. A conductor in a worn blue coat had volunteered to lead a small orchestra, but the auction turned every note into a demand for bids. Thin cords hanging from the ceiling flashed as the frequency of the music changed. Li, Kena, and Samir entered with ear protection and a clear agreement to leave if anyone became dizzy. The hall's floor was coated in coarse dust and cleaning foam. An old poster celebrated a dynasty that had built the station. Li read the dates aloud. The building had lasted because many kinds of labor had kept it standing, not because one celebrated ruler had worked without sleep.

第六夜以一場鐵路通車百週年音樂會開始。一名穿舊藍外套的指揮自願帶領小樂團，拍賣系統卻把每個音符變成催促出價的命令。天花板垂下的細繩隨著樂聲頻率變動而閃光。江梨、珂娜和薩米爾戴著護耳設備進場，事先約好有人頭暈就離開。大廳地板覆著粗糙灰塵與清潔泡沫。一張舊海報歌頌建造車站的王朝。江梨大聲讀出日期。建築能留存百年，是因為許多人的勞動共同維護，不是某位受讚頌的統治者從不睡覺。

A city campaign manager assured the audience that a single courageous champion could complete the next trial. He asked people to imitate the winning fighter's movements and surrender hours on his behalf. The imitation caused the screen to register dozens of transfers at once. Samir noticed something strange: every transfer produced the same electrical pulse, even when its sender stood elsewhere. Li asked him to devise a test that did not require anyone to donate another minute. He clipped a meter to an empty cord and compared its signal with the conductor's score. The pulse came at a fixed frequency unrelated to the music or to any human gesture. Someone was replaying a recorded command.

市府宣傳主管向觀眾保證，只要有一名勇敢的冠軍就能完成下一場試煉。他請大家模仿勝利選手的動作，代替選手交出時數。模仿動作讓螢幕一次登記數十筆轉移。薩米爾發現奇怪的事：每筆轉移都產生相同電脈衝，即使轉移者站在不同位置。江梨請他設計不必再捐出時間的測試。他把測量器接上空繩，拿訊號與指揮的樂譜比較。脈衝以固定頻率出現，與音樂或人的手勢無關。有人在重播錄好的命令。

The conductor stopped his rehearsal when Li explained the result. He was diligent about every small note and wanted the evaluation to be fair. He suggested playing in silence, letting the musicians move their hands while the speakers stayed off. The auction still announced donations. Then they turned on a recorded concert in an empty hall, and the board again registered new hours. No human donor had been present. Kena laughed nervously; the discovery seemed too obvious now. Li asked her to write both failures and successes in a notebook. A person studying psychology later might ask why the audience had believed a loud announcement instead of checking whether anybody had signed a form.

指揮聽完結果後停止排練。他一向仔細對待每個音符，也希望評估公平。他建議大家不出聲演奏，只動手而關閉喇叭。拍賣仍宣布有人捐贈。接著他們在空大廳播放錄好的音樂，面板再度登記新時數，現場卻根本沒有捐贈者。珂娜緊張地笑了；這個發現現在看來似乎過於明顯。江梨請她把失敗與成功都記在筆記本裡。日後研究心理學的人也許會問，為何觀眾相信響亮的公告，卻沒有先確認是否有人真的簽了文件。

Nari came with mineral water and sandwiches for the workers. She said that needy families had been offered food in exchange for their last two hours of sleep. The campaign had complicated a simple choice: people could refuse the auction, but then they might have nothing to eat. Li promised to raise the matter at the morning council meeting. She could not assure the families that there would be enough food tomorrow. She could, however, ask the shelters to publish supplies and allow residents to request help without bidding. They placed a table beside the railway door. The first people who approached it looked frightened, as though every free thing must hide a cost.

娜里帶來礦泉水和給工人的三明治。她說有經濟困難的家庭被要求交出最後兩小時睡眠，才可換取食物。宣傳活動把簡單選擇變複雜了：人們可以拒絕拍賣，但可能因此沒東西吃。江梨答應在早上的市議會提起這件事。她不能保證明天食物一定足夠，卻可以要求避難所公開物資數量，讓居民不用出價也能申請協助。他們把桌子放在鐵路大門旁。第一批靠近的人一臉害怕，彷彿每件免費物品後面都一定藏著代價。

The campaign manager tried to complicate the discovery by claiming that the recorded pulses were merely a musical effect. Samir replayed the recording with every speaker unplugged. The same pulse remained in the auction log. Li asked an independent sound engineer to repeat the test on another machine. The engineer agreed to do so after a proper night's rest, refusing the offered speed bonus. The delay was useful: it gave the team time to write a clear procedure that a skeptical observer could follow. Tomorrow's result might challenge their theory, and Li welcomed that possibility.

宣傳主管試圖把發現複雜化，說錄好的脈衝只是音樂效果。薩米爾拔掉每個喇叭再播放紀錄，拍賣日誌仍出現相同脈衝。江梨請獨立音響工程師在另一台機器上重測。工程師同意好好睡過一夜再做，不接受速度獎勵。延遲反而有用：小隊得以寫出清楚程序，讓存疑的觀察員能照著做。明天的結果可能推翻她們的假設，江梨歡迎這種可能。

Just before midnight, the conductor wrote a short message on the back of his program: “Our work is not the property of the loudest speaker.” He gave it to Li, who copied the pulse measurements beside it. The recorded donations were a mechanism, not a mystery of human will. If they could show who controlled that mechanism, the people whose hours had disappeared might challenge the claims. Li reflected on her factory days, when a schedule could hide work by labeling it as rest. She had once helped prepare such a schedule without questioning the label. Now she wanted the records to reflect what people had actually done. The station clock made no sound when the next night started.

午夜前，指揮在節目單背面寫下一句話：「我們的勞動不屬於聲音最大的人。」他把紙交給江梨，江梨在旁邊抄下脈衝測量結果。被重播的捐贈是一套機制，不是什麼神祕的人類意志。如果查得出誰控制機制，失去時數的人就可能質疑索取。江梨想起在工廠時，排班表只要把工作標成休息，就能藏起工時。她曾幫忙做過這種表，卻沒質疑名稱。如今她希望紀錄能反映人們真正做過什麼。下一夜開始時，車站鐘沒有發出聲音。
