沒有贏家的山丘

The Hill Without a Winner

零點拍賣：把睡眠還給城市 · 第 7 / 25 章 · 3000–4000 · 英中對照 · 校訂中

用互動版查詞、朗讀與收藏

The seventh trial opened in a mountainous district where frost covered the morning path. A helicopter waited above the hospital, but the game required a high bid before it could carry supplies. Li refused to treat the helicopter as the only option. A farmer offered two mules and a safe lower road, provided someone checked the surroundings and the weight of each box. The farmer's small herd had already been frightened by the aircraft noise. A fierce competitor called the animal route a waste of time. Li asked him for an estimate of total travel time, including loading, landing, and the risk of another delay. He offered no figures. She wrote the first measurement herself.

第七場試煉開在山區，晨間小路覆著霜。一架直升機停在醫院上方，遊戲卻要求先付高額出價才能運送補給。江梨拒絕把直升機當成唯一選項。一名農夫願意提供兩頭騾子和較安全的低地道路，條件是有人先檢查周遭環境與每箱重量。農夫的小群牲畜已被飛機聲嚇到。一位激烈的競爭者說動物路線浪費時間。江梨問他估算過完整運輸時間沒有，包括裝載、降落以及再次延誤的風險。他拿不出數字。她自己寫下第一筆量測。

A young technician with a diploma in aircraft repair volunteered to analyze the helicopter's fuel gauge. She found a slight fault and recommended grounding the machine until it was fixed. The competitor opposed her decision, saying that one percent of risk should not matter during a crisis. “Accuracy matters more when people are scared,” she replied. Her estimate was not a promise that the machine would crash. It was enough information to avoid asking a pilot to ignore a warning. Li thanked her for making the fault visible. The machine stayed on the ground, and the farmer prepared the mules. Nobody had to pretend that choosing the slower road was a remarkable act of courage.

一名持有航空維修文憑的年輕技師自願分析直升機油量表。她發現細微故障，建議維修前停飛。競爭者反對，說危機中百分之一的風險不該重要。「人害怕時，準確度反而更重要。」她回答。她的估算不是說飛機一定會墜毀，而是提供足夠資訊，不必要求駕駛忽略警告。江梨感謝她揭露問題。直升機留在地上，農夫準備騾子。沒有人需要假裝選擇慢路是什麼非凡的勇氣。

Along the path, a plumber joined the team to protect the village's water line from frozen soil. He had injured his shoulder the week before and could carry only light equipment. Li adapted the loading plan, giving him a map and a speaking role instead of heavy boxes. The route involved a bridge with a weight limit and a shelter whose doors were too narrow for the animals. Each obstacle required a different solution. Their mutual agreement was simple: anyone could call a stop, and nobody would lose food or respect for doing so. When the sun rose, the hospital received the first delivery. The game awarded them no points because no one had bid for speed.

途中，一名水管工加入隊伍，保護村內水管不受凍土破壞。他上週傷了肩膀，只能帶輕裝備。江梨調整裝載計畫，讓他負責地圖與口頭指示，不要求他搬重箱子。路上有一座限重橋，還有門窄得無法讓騾子通過的避難所。每個障礙需要不同解法。他們的共同約定很簡單：任何人都能要求停止，沒有人會因為這樣而失去食物或尊重。太陽升起時，醫院收到第一批補給。遊戲沒給分數，因為沒有人為速度出價。

At the clinic, nurses explained that emergency welfare would be reduced if the auction took control of the hospital dormitory. A worker showed Li a chart claiming a twenty-percent reduction in waiting time after the new game began. The chart left out people who had fainted before reaching the counter. Li asked to see the original totals. She did not say the numbers were invented; she said the category being measured was too narrow. The doctor agreed to record both completed visits and people turned away. “If a patient cannot stay awake long enough to register,” she said, “that still counts as a person needing care.” The team added that sentence to its report.

診所護理師解釋，如果拍賣取得醫院宿舍管理權，緊急福利服務將被削減。一名工人拿出圖表，聲稱遊戲開始後候診時間減少百分之二十。圖表卻沒算進還沒抵達櫃檯就昏倒的人。江梨要求原始總數。她沒說數字一定是偽造，只說測量範圍太狹窄。醫師同意同時記錄完成看診及被拒於門外的人。「如果病人清醒不到完成掛號，」她說，「他仍然是需要照護的人。」隊伍把這句話寫進報告。

The competitor tried to oppose the decision again, his voice intense enough to frighten the mules. The farmer moved the animals away and asked him to lower his voice. “An animal can injure itself if it panics beside a bridge,” she explained. Li said the same principle applied to people: a rescue that caused a new emergency was not a success. The technician showed the group the faulty gauge rather than arguing about courage. After looking at the part, the competitor agreed to carry two light boxes by hand. He still preferred the helicopter, but he could work safely alongside people who disagreed with him.

競爭者又想反對決定，聲音激烈得嚇到騾子。農夫把動物帶開，請他小聲一點。「動物在橋邊驚慌可能弄傷自己。」她解釋。江梨說同樣原則適用於人：造成新緊急事故的救援不算成功。技師拿出故障油量表給眾人看，不再爭論勇氣。看過零件後，競爭者同意親手搬兩個輕箱。他仍偏好直升機，但已能與不同意自己的人安全合作。

On the return trip, Kena asked why the competitor had not offered his own hours for the helicopter if he believed the fast route was best. Li answered that belief and payment were different things; the game hid the cost by spreading it across tired people. The farmer gave them an apple each, then asked them not to photograph his animals for publicity. They respected the request. That night Li checked the delivery times against the game's official report. It listed the hill as a failure because the highest bidder had not won. The supplies were on the shelves, the crew was resting, and nobody had been left on the bridge. Li wrote a different result: DELIVERY COMPLETE, WITH REST.

回程時，珂娜問那位競爭者既然認為快路最好，為何不拿自己的時數替直升機出價。江梨說，信念和付費是兩回事；遊戲把代價分攤給疲憊的人，使它看似不存在。農夫每人送了一顆蘋果，又請她們不要拍攝牲畜做宣傳。她們尊重要求。當晚江梨把送達時間與遊戲報告比較。遊戲把山丘列成失敗，因為最高出價者沒贏。可是補給已放上架，隊伍正在休息，沒有任何人被留在橋上。江梨寫下另一種結果：「補給完成，並保有休息。」
