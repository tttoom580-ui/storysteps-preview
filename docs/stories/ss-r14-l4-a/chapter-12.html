A Ledger with No Perfect Page

零點拍賣：把睡眠還給城市 · 第 12 / 25 章 · 3000–4000 · 英中對照 · 校訂中

用互動版查詞、朗讀與收藏

On the twelfth night, the city agreed to test the old manual in a small room below the station. Its entrance was oval and led past a former miner's workshop filled with hardware. A drawing on the door showed a hawk carrying a vessel, an illustration from the first public sleep hall. Li felt dread when she saw the dust. The place had been closed for years, and absolute certainty about its safety was impossible. Samir tested the air, checked the supports, and asked everyone to fasten protective masks properly. He wanted discipline without punishment: anyone uncomfortable with the conditions could leave. The team needed a realistic test, not a performance of perfection.

第十二夜，市府同意在車站下方的小房間測試舊手冊。入口呈橢圓形，通過一處舊礦工工作間，裡面堆滿五金設備。門上畫著老鷹叼著容器，是第一座公共休息大廳的插畫。江梨看到灰塵感到不安。地方關閉多年，不可能對安全有絕對把握。薩米爾檢測空氣、檢查支撐，並請所有人固定好防護口罩。他要有紀律，但不以懲罰強制：任何人不舒服都可以離開。隊伍需要寫實的測試，不是表演完美。

The procedure called for inserting a blank card into the ledger machine and recording one disputed account. A woman named Mira volunteered because two strangers had taken hours from her after she fell asleep on a bus. She asked that her name remain private. Ada said the distribution of evidence could be limited to the people conducting the test, with a public summary issued later. Qi read the steps aloud, and Mira gave recognition that she understood she could withdraw at any time. The machine accepted the card, then displayed an error. Kena reached for the reset switch. Li stopped her. The error was part of the result; erasing it would make the procedure less reliable.

程序要求把空白卡插入帳冊機器，記錄一個有爭議的帳戶。一名叫米拉的女子自願參加，因為她在公車上睡著後，兩個陌生人拿走她的時數。她要求名字保密。艾達說，證據分發可先限制於測試人員，稍後再公開摘要。杜棋大聲讀出步驟，米拉確認自己明白可以隨時退出。機器接受卡片，接著顯示錯誤。珂娜伸手想按重設鍵，江梨攔住她。錯誤也是結果的一部分；抹掉只會讓程序更不可靠。

A printed report claimed the system's efficiency was near perfect. Samir examined the hardware and found that a worn wheel slipped whenever the machine tried to renew a credit. The manual required two people to witness that movement. Until now, operators had replaced the wheel's count with an estimate. Li asked Qi whether he knew who had authorized the shortcut. He did not. “We will resolve that separately,” she said. They tested the wheel without Mira's account and confirmed the fault. The machine needed repair before it could return even one hour. The disappointment was sharp, but no participant had lost an additional minute. That mattered as much as the failure itself.

一份印刷報告聲稱系統效率幾乎完美。薩米爾檢查五金部件，發現機器嘗試恢復信用時，一個磨損齒輪會打滑。手冊要求兩人共同見證轉動。至今操作員卻用估計值代替實際齒輪計數。江梨問杜棋知不知道誰准許這種捷徑，他說不知道。「那件事我們另外處理。」她說。他們先不用米拉的帳戶測試齒輪，確認故障。機器必須修好才能退回哪怕一小時。失望很強烈，但參與者沒有因此多失去一分鐘，這和失敗本身一樣重要。

A gracious engineer from the old mine offered to replace the wheel if she could first inspect the metal. Her father had died in an accident caused by false maintenance reports, so she understood the grief behind a missing number. She worked carefully, refusing the game screen's promise of a speed reward. By dawn the mechanism moved smoothly. Li still would not use it on Mira until an independent person repeated the test. She asked the city to appoint one observer who had not worked for the auction company. The request took time; the officer complained that they were delaying recovery. Li replied that speed without trust would force people to risk their own rest to prove they had been cheated.

一名親切的退休礦場工程師表示願意更換齒輪，但得先檢查金屬狀況。她父親曾因虛假維修報告引發的事故過世，她理解數字消失背後的悲痛。她仔細工作，拒絕遊戲螢幕的速度獎勵。天亮前，機構已順利轉動。江梨仍不願在獨立人員重做測試前使用米拉的帳戶。她要求市府指派一名未替拍賣公司工作過的觀察員。申請耗時，官員抱怨她們拖慢恢復。江梨回答，沒有信任的速度會迫使受害者拿自己的休息去冒險，才有辦法證明自己被騙。

When the test room closed, Mira asked whether the engineer's work would be paid even though the return had failed. Li said yes; the budget covered repairs and observation, not just successful outcomes. The city had often treated invisible maintenance as a waste, while celebrating dramatic rescues. Samir wrote down the hours each worker had actually spent. He refused to count the minutes of Mira's rest as labor owed to the team. She was the owner of her own time. The repair bill would go to the organization responsible for the machine, not to the person who had been charged by mistake.

測試室關閉時，米拉問工程師雖然沒成功返還，工作是否仍會支薪。江梨說會；預算包括維修和觀察，不只付成功結果。城市過去常把看不見的維護當浪費，卻慶祝戲劇化救援。薩米爾記下每名工人實際工作時間，拒絕把米拉休息的分鐘算成欠小隊的勞動。她的時間屬於她自己。維修帳單應由負責機器的組織支付，不是由被誤收費的人支付。

That afternoon Mira returned home and slept. The test was not finished, but she did not have to remain at the station merely to show commitment. Li sent her a plain explanation of what had happened: one card accepted, one mechanical fault found, no credit returned yet, no further credit taken. She included a date for the next check and a number Mira could call. In the margin, she wrote that the procedure could still fail. Nothing about the honest account was impressive. That was its value. People who trusted the record would not be surprised by an invented success later. They could decide what risk they were prepared to take when the machine was ready.

當天下午米拉回家睡了。測試尚未完成，但她不必為了表現誠意而一直待在車站。江梨傳給她一份簡單說明：一張卡被接受、找到一處機械故障、尚未退回信用額度、也未再扣取時數。她附上下次檢查日期與聯絡電話，在空白處註明程序仍可能失敗。這份誠實報告一點也不令人驚豔，卻正因如此有價值。信任紀錄的人不會日後被捏造的成功嚇一跳，也能在機器準備好時，自行決定願承擔什麼風險。
