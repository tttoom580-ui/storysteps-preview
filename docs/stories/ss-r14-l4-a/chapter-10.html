紫色信封裡的交易

The Offer in the Violet Envelope

零點拍賣：把睡眠還給城市 · 第 10 / 25 章 · 3000–4000 · 英中對照 · 校訂中

用互動版查詞、朗讀與收藏

On the tenth morning, Li received a violet envelope from the company operating the auction. It offered her a promotion to manager of a new sleep facility, with a private room and priority access for her family. In return she would sign a resignation from the public investigation. The letter called its terms generous and promised acceptance of her own hours as authentic credit. Li smelled perfume on the paper and wondered whether someone had chosen it to make a business offer feel personal. She did not tear the envelope up. She classified the claims: the salary could be checked, the promised safety could not, and the demand that she stop speaking was genuine enough to concern her.

第十天早晨，江梨收到拍賣營運公司的紫色信封。信裡提出升職：成為新睡眠設施的經理，享有私人房間，家人也能優先使用。交換條件是她簽署退出公共調查的辭職聲明。信稱待遇優厚，還保證她自己的時數會被接受為真正的信用額度。江梨聞到紙上的香水味，想知道是否有人故意讓商業邀約顯得私人。她沒有撕掉信封，而是把主張分類：薪水可以查證，安全承諾無法查證，要求她停止發言的條件則明確得令人擔憂。

Kena found a publisher willing to print the manual's public sections. The publisher asked them to remove every name that might identify a refugee living in the dormitory. Li agreed. The shelter housed people who had already crossed difficult borders; an emergency should not make their locations public. A photographer wanted a dramatic picture of a rescue at the entrance, but Nari refused to stage one. “Their dignity is not our advertisement,” she said. Kena prepared a plain diagram instead, showing how to challenge a disputed bid. The instruction sheet offered several options: submit with a helper, request a private hearing, or wait until rested enough to decide. No one had to appear on television to ask for help.

珂娜找到願意印刷手冊公共部分的出版社。出版人要求刪掉可能辨認出住在宿舍裡的難民的所有姓名。江梨同意。避難所住著已跨過困難邊境的人，危機不該公開他們的位置。攝影師想在入口拍一張戲劇化的救援照片，娜里拒絕演出。「他們的尊嚴不是我們的廣告。」她說。珂娜改畫一張簡單圖示，說明如何質疑爭議出價。說明單提供幾種選項：由助手協助提交、申請非公開聽證，或等休息夠了再決定。沒有人必須上電視才能求助。

At noon, the auction launched a demonstration in a polished facility with flowers, clean beds, and new stockings laid out as gifts. The atmosphere seemed calm, almost warm. But a worker noticed that the air was too dry and that the door could be locked from outside. The manager said such details differed from older buildings and should not embarrass the guests. Li asked for a real exit test. The manager called the request offensive. “A beautiful room is not a safe room merely because it looks beautiful,” she replied. A volunteer pressed the emergency button. Nothing happened. The company promised to fix the wiring before opening, which proved that the room had not been ready for guests.

中午，拍賣在一座擦得發亮的設施舉行示範。裡面有鮮花、乾淨床鋪，還擺著新長襪當禮物。氣氛平靜，幾乎溫暖。但一名工人發現空氣太乾，門還能從外面反鎖。經理說新建築和舊建築本來就有差異，不該讓賓客難堪。江梨要求做真正的出口測試。經理認為這要求冒犯人。「漂亮的房間不會只因漂亮就自動安全。」她回答。志工按下緊急按鈕，什麼都沒發生。公司承諾開幕前修好線路，也等於承認房間尚不能接待客人。

Li showed the resignation offer to Ada and Qi, then told them she had not decided how to respond. She was ambitious; she wanted a stable salary and somewhere her father could sleep without paying a bid. Kena seemed shocked that she had even considered the job. Li explained that a choice could be difficult without being shameful. After reading the contract, she found a clause giving the company access to personal rest records, including those of her relatives. There was no independent review and no way for her father to withdraw permission. That settled the question. She declined the offer in writing and kept a copy of the reply.

江梨把辭職邀約拿給艾達和杜棋看，說自己還沒決定如何回應。她也有野心，想要穩定薪水，希望父親有地方睡而不必出價。珂娜震驚她竟然認真考慮過。江梨解釋，選擇可以困難，卻不代表令人羞恥。讀完合約後，她發現一條款允許公司查看私人休息紀錄，甚至包含她的親屬。沒有獨立覆核，她父親也無法撤回許可。這使她決定拒絕。她以書面回覆，保留副本。

Li asked the council clerk to classify each promise in the violet letter by whether it could be independently verified. The clerk made three columns: money, access, and silence. The promised salary belonged in the first, the untested beds in the second, and the resignation demand in the third. Li realized that the offer would transform her from a witness into someone paid to ignore the witnesses. She could respect people who accepted different jobs without accepting that condition herself. The clerk filed the letter as evidence, not as an invitation that everyone should feel obliged to reject.

江梨請議會職員按是否能獨立查證，替紫色信裡的承諾分類。職員畫出三欄：金錢、使用權和沉默。承諾的薪水放第一欄，未測試的床位放第二欄，辭職要求放第三欄。江梨發現這份工作會把她從證人變成拿錢忽略證人的人。她可以尊重別人接受不同工作，卻不能接受自己被要求沉默。職員把信歸檔為證據，不是每個人都必須拒絕的邀請。

At dusk, her father called from a crowded apartment. He said he did not want to be used as the reason she accepted a dangerous contract. Li felt both relief and sadness. They had spent years avoiding conversations about money, because neither wanted the other to worry. Now they agreed to discuss needs directly. He needed a quiet bed and regular meals; she needed help sharing the cost, not permission to make every decision for him. The next auction round offered another promotion, this time in gold letters. Li ignored the glow. The only promise she trusted that evening was the one she had made to phone her father again after both had slept.

黃昏時，父親從擁擠公寓打電話來。他說不希望女兒因為自己就接受危險合約。江梨既鬆了口氣，又感到難過。他們多年來避談金錢，因為都不想讓對方擔心。如今兩人同意直接談需求。他需要安靜床位和規律飲食；她需要有人分擔開支，而不是替他做每個決定的權力。下一輪拍賣又用金字提出升職。江梨沒有理會光芒。那晚她唯一相信的承諾，是自己說好要在父女兩人都睡過以後，再打電話給他。
