A Breath Behind the Wall

零點拍賣：把睡眠還給城市 · 第 3 / 25 章 · 3000–4000 · 英中對照 · 校訂中

用互動版查詞、朗讀與收藏

On the third night, a narrow barrier appeared between the market and the public dormitory. Its red lights blinked every time someone tried to pass without bidding. A teenager rushed ahead, and the barrier closed on his coat. The pressure made him choke before Nari pulled the fabric free. The auction called the incident accidental and offered a mild apology. Li thought the risk was grave: an apology could not evaluate whether a passage might seriously injure someone tomorrow. A guard urged people to climb over, but the metal edges were sharp. “That is a brutal shortcut,” she said. “We need the reason the barrier closes, not a faster way to ignore it.”

第三夜，市場和公共宿舍之間出現一道狹窄屏障。每當有人想不出價就通過，紅燈便閃爍。一名少年向前衝，屏障夾住他的外套，讓他一度喘不過氣。娜里拉開布料，少年才得以呼吸。拍賣系統稱這是意外，給出一句溫和道歉。江梨認為風險嚴重：道歉不能代替評估通道明天是否會傷人。保全催人翻過去，但金屬邊緣尖銳。「那是殘酷的捷徑，」她說，「我們需要知道屏障為何關上，不是更快地忽略危險。」

A retired philosophy teacher named Wen lived beside the market. He quoted a philosopher who had written about the temptation to value speed over intelligence. Wen kept a notebook of the neighborhood's old sleeping arrangements. Its composition was simple: each family named the nights when someone needed help; others offered assistance only after agreeing to do so. He feared that the game would bring revenge against families unable to bid. Li noticed his tendency to speak for everyone and asked him not to decide for the people he meant to protect. He was offended at first, then nodded. Reform would not mean much if the new system repeated the same neglect of individual choices.

一名叫文伯的退休哲學教師住在市場旁。他引用哲學家的話，談人們多容易把速度看得比智慧重要。文伯留著一本記錄鄰里夜間互助方式的筆記。內容結構很簡單：每個家庭說明需要幫忙的夜晚，其他人只有在同意後才提供協助。他擔心遊戲會讓無力出價的家庭遭到報復。江梨察覺他有替所有人發言的傾向，便請他不要替想保護的人做決定。他起初有些受冒犯，後來點了頭。如果改革仍忽略個人選擇，就沒有太大意義。

Near the barrier, a branch from the mountainous park had broken through the road, leaving a twig pressed against a sensor. Kena found the opening by tracing marks in the wet dust. She removed the twig with an insulated tool while Samir watched the signal. The red light remained on, proving the plant had not been the main cause. Li wrote down the failed theory instead of hiding it. A school nurse arrived to check the teenager, who could now speak but wanted to rest. He thanked them with obvious gratitude. Li reminded him that he did not owe them a bid, a signature, or a story for the public screen. Help should not become another purchase.

屏障附近，山區公園的一段樹枝穿過路面，一根小細枝頂著感應器。珂娜沿著濕灰上的痕跡找到開口。她用絕緣工具移除細枝，薩米爾在旁監看訊號。紅燈仍亮著，證明植物不是主要原因。江梨把錯誤假設記下來，沒有掩飾。學校護理師來檢查少年；他已能說話，但需要休息。他真誠道謝。江梨提醒他，不必因此出價、簽名，也不必把自己的遭遇交給公開螢幕。援助不該變成另一筆交易。

The team tested the barrier with empty carts of different weights. It opened for a cart registered to a high bidder and stayed shut for an identical cart registered to the public dormitory. The result offended the guards more than any speech. Li did not call it proof of a hidden murderer or an evil machine with feelings. It proved a rule about ownership was being applied to a physical gate. Wen proposed writing a petition, and Kena proposed copying the test. Li chose both. If a gate treated people differently according to money, the test should be clear enough that others could reproduce it without trusting her intelligence or her character.

隊伍用不同重量的空推車測試屏障。登記在高出價者名下的推車能通過，登記在公共宿舍名下的相同推車卻被擋住。這個結果比任何演說都更讓保全難堪。江梨沒有把它說成發現了躲藏的殺人者，也沒有宣稱機器有惡意。它只證明一套關於所有權的規則正被施加在實體大門上。文伯建議寫請願書，珂娜建議複製測試。江梨兩件都選。如果大門按金錢區分人，就該讓其他人不用相信她的智慧或品格，也能自行重做測試。

At home, Wen found an older report about a neighborhood gate that had once closed whenever a delivery cart passed. The repair had required adjusting the sensor, not blaming the people who carried the goods. Li read the report beside the new test notes. She hoped this problem would prove just as ordinary, but she would not assume so. Kena proposed placing a warning at both ends of the passage until the mechanism was understood. By sunset, everyone using the market could see the warning and choose another path. The action reduced danger without pretending that the cause had been solved.

回家後，文伯找到一份舊報告，寫著某處社區大門曾在送貨推車經過時自動關閉。當年的修復方式是調整感應器，而非責怪搬貨的人。江梨把報告和新測試筆記放在一起看。她希望這次問題也同樣普通，卻不會直接假設如此。珂娜建議在弄懂機制前，先在通道兩端放警告。日落前，每個經過市場的人都能看到告示，選擇其他道路。這降低了危險，又沒有假裝原因已經解決。

They sent their results to three shelters. Two replied at once; the third had lost power. Li asked Wen to keep the copies somewhere dry, and he invited the group into his apartment. An old lamp gave off enough light to read by. Outside, the barrier blinked again, this time without anyone near it. Li listened to the empty street and felt an urge to rush out. Instead she checked that Kena and Samir had taken their required rest. The strongest argument would not come from a single exhausted witness. It would come from several people, awake enough to disagree with each other, seeing the same thing at different times.

他們把結果送往三處避難中心。兩處立刻回覆，第三處則停電了。江梨請文伯把副本存放在乾燥處，文伯邀大家進屋。一盞舊燈提供足夠的閱讀光線。外面的屏障又閃了，附近卻沒有人。江梨聽著空蕩的街，衝動地想跑出去。她還是先確認珂娜和薩米爾有沒有按照約定休息。最強的證據不會來自一名累得精疲力盡的證人，而會來自幾個仍清醒、甚至能互相不同意的人，在不同時刻看見同樣的結果。
