買不到任何東西的風暴

The Storm That Bought Nothing

零點拍賣：把睡眠還給城市 · 第 11 / 25 章 · 3000–4000 · 英中對照 · 校訂中

用互動版查詞、朗讀與收藏

A hurricane crossed the coast on the eleventh night, and the auction announced an emergency convention in the city's tallest skyscraper. Its commercial partners boasted that buying priority beds would guarantee protection from the storm. Li looked at the windows and noticed that their glass was vibrating. A weather officer accused her of spreading resistance to the official plan. She asked him to expose the actual inspection report instead of arguing about her loyalty. The report was informative but troublesome: the skyscraper's upper floors had not been approved for use during extreme wind. Some people had already paid three hours of sleep for rooms that should not be occupied. The company offered assurance without providing a safe building.

第十一夜，颶風越過海岸，拍賣在全市最高的摩天大樓宣布緊急會議。它的商業夥伴誇口，只要購買優先床位就能保證躲過風暴。江梨看見窗戶玻璃正在震動。一名氣象官員指控她抵抗官方計畫。她請對方公開實際檢查報告，而不是爭論她是否忠誠。報告資訊充分，卻也令人頭痛：摩天大樓上層沒有核准在強風中供人使用。有些人已付三小時睡眠，買下根本不該住人的房間。公司給出了保證，卻沒有提供安全建築。

At the ground floor, a manufacturer demonstrated emergency lights powered by a small hydrogen cell. The technology might help if the main supply failed, but the demonstration had not been consistent. Sometimes the lamp lit, and sometimes it did not. Samir inspected the connections without touching the sealed container. The manager said Li should be grateful for any contribution during a crisis. Li answered that she was grateful for people who worked carefully, not for claims that a failure was impossible. She asked for a second test with the fire officer present. The lamp went dark after three minutes. No one was hurt, and the manufacturer agreed to replace the faulty unit before any residents depended on it.

一樓的製造商展示用小型氫燃料電池供電的緊急燈。主電源失效時這或許有用，但示範結果並不一致：燈有時亮，有時不亮。薩米爾在不碰密封容器的情況下檢查接線。經理說，危機中有人願意貢獻就該感激。江梨回答，她感謝仔細工作的人，不感謝把故障說成不可能的宣稱。她要求消防官員到場做第二次測試。三分鐘後燈熄滅。沒有人受傷，製造商也同意在居民依賴設備之前換掉故障元件。

Nari organized a quiet recovery room on a lower floor. People who had suffered misfortune could sit there without being asked for an interview or a donation. A volunteer accused her of encouraging ignorance by not explaining every part of the auction. Nari pointed out that someone shaking with exhaustion might need relaxation before information. She offered a printed guide for those ready to read, and a chair for those who were not. Li admired the distinction. A public service could contribute useful knowledge without forcing people to perform gratitude. When a window cracked above them, everyone moved away on the officer's order. The best argument in the world could not stop a shard of glass.

娜里在較低樓層整理出安靜的恢復室。遭遇不幸的人能在此坐著，不會被要求接受採訪或捐贈。一名志工說她不解釋拍賣所有細節，是在鼓勵無知。娜里指出，累得發抖的人可能先需要放鬆，才有力氣接受資訊。她為準備閱讀的人提供印刷指南，為尚未準備好的人提供椅子。江梨欣賞這個區別。公共服務可以貢獻知識，不必強迫人表演感激。上方窗戶破裂時，所有人聽從消防官員指示後退。世上再好的論點也擋不住玻璃碎片。

Qi arrived carrying copies of the old manual in a waterproof case. He said the auction had never been intended to operate during a storm: the original rules required automatic suspension when a shared shelter could not be reached safely. The company had ignored that condition. Li checked the exact wording and found that two witnesses could trigger a pause by documenting a direct hazard. A resident and a fire officer signed the observation about the cracked window. The screen flashed: ROUND ELEVEN SUSPENDED. For the first time, the largest bidder's name disappeared before the bell. Li did not call that a complete recovery. The families were still frightened, and rain was still entering the building.

杜棋提著防水箱趕來，裡面是舊手冊副本。他說拍賣原本就不應在風暴中運作：舊規則要求，只要無法安全抵達共同避難處，系統就必須自動暫停。公司忽略了這項條件。江梨核對原文，發現兩名證人只要記錄直接危險，就能啟動暫停。一名居民和消防官員簽下裂窗觀察。螢幕閃出：「第十一輪暫停。」最大出價者的名字第一次在鐘響前消失。江梨沒有把這叫作完全恢復。家庭仍然害怕，雨水仍在滲入建築。

The company tried to accuse the weather officer of causing the suspension, although he had signed the hazard report only after inspecting the glass. A shopkeeper from the district's commerce association challenged the accusation. His members would lose sales if another false shelter opened during the storm. He asked the manufacturer to explain how it would manufacture replacement windows and whether the materials had been tested. The representative promised quick delivery but could not give a date. Li added the missing deadline to the official questions. The hurricane was not the time to reward confident speeches over practical safety.

公司試圖指控氣象官員造成暫停，雖然他是在檢查玻璃後才簽危險報告。地方商業協會的一名店主質疑這項指控。如果再有假安全避難所開在風暴中，協會店家也會受損。他要求製造商說明如何製造替換窗戶、材料是否經過測試。代表保證很快送達，卻給不出日期。江梨把缺少的期限寫入正式詢問。颶風中不該獎勵自信演說，而忽略實際安全。

After the storm shifted inland, volunteers moved people to a safer school. The company issued a statement about its excellent service, but Li kept a list of the rooms that had been unsafe. She recorded the duration of the suspension and the time each group reached shelter. This was not a victory for one loyal organization against another. The neighbors had contributed different skills and had refused a plan that failed inspection. Li hoped that the city's next decision would be based on such ordinary evidence. At midnight the auction resumed its countdown with fourteen nights remaining. The storm had not bought anything; it had merely revealed what the market would sell, even when the product was unsafe.

風暴移向內陸後，志工把人們轉移到較安全的學校。公司發表聲明，讚揚自身服務優良；江梨卻保留不安全房間清單，記錄暫停時長與各組抵達避難所的時間。這不是一個忠誠組織打敗另一個組織的勝利。鄰居們提供不同技能，拒絕沒有通過檢查的方案。江梨希望城市下一次決定能根據這種普通證據。午夜時拍賣恢復倒數，剩十四夜。風暴沒有買到任何東西；它只是揭露了市場即使商品不安全，也願意把它拿出來賣。
