The Name Under the Glove

零點拍賣：把睡眠還給城市 · 第 2 / 25 章 · 3000–4000 · 英中對照 · 校訂中

用互動版查詞、朗讀與收藏

Before dawn, an immigrant maintenance worker named Samir arrived to repair the auction hall's shutters. His shift had ended three hours earlier, but his employer had marked him available again. A famous fighter stood at the entrance in a disguise, collecting autographs for people who paid him an hour of sleep. He called the sale a convenience: no waiting, no difficult forms, no questions. Samir watched a little girl carve a star into wet clay by the door. Her dog rested one paw beside the shape. The fighter's assistant claimed the dog was dangerous and ordered it removed. Li noticed that the animal had not moved toward anyone. She asked whether cruelty could be justified simply by inventing a threat.

黎明前，移民維修工薩米爾來修拍賣大廳的捲門。他三小時前才下班，雇主卻又把他標成可以上工。入口站著一名喬裝的知名格鬥選手，替付出一小時睡眠的人簽親筆簽名。他把交易說成一種便利服務：不用排隊、不必填麻煩表格，也不必回答問題。薩米爾看見小女孩在濕黏土上刻一顆星，狗把一隻爪子放在星星旁。選手的助理聲稱狗很危險，命令把牠趕走。江梨注意到狗根本沒有接近任何人，便問：難道只要捏造威脅，就能替殘忍找理由嗎？

Kena showed Li the initial transfer record. The fighter had received hours from workers who could not be reached, including Samir. His name appeared beside an elastic wristband in a picture, but he had not signed a transfer. “That is my employer's photograph,” he said. “They use it to count who enters the workshop.” He described his timetable, which had little room for proper rest. Li told him she would record his feedback without promising instant justice. A prominent official entered and remarked that the auction would help businesses prosper. He had no answer when she asked why a stranger could exercise a privilege over a worker's sleep using an ordinary attendance photograph.

珂娜把最初的轉移紀錄拿給江梨看。格鬥選手收到數名聯絡不上的工人時數，其中包括薩米爾。照片裡，他的名字出現在彈性手環旁，但他從未簽署轉移文件。「那是雇主拍的照片，」他說，「用來記錄誰進工作間。」他說明自己的排班表，幾乎沒有完整休息時間。江梨告訴他，會把回饋記下，卻不能保證立即討回公道。一名知名官員走進來，評論拍賣將讓商家繁榮。江梨問，為什麼陌生人能憑普通的出勤照片取得支配工人睡眠的特權，他答不出來。

A warning sounded from the rear storage room. The game labeled it an explosive risk, and the assistant tried to shut the door with everyone inside. Samir recognized the noise as a loose spring in the shutter, not a bomb. He warned that the wrong response could ruin the entire entrance. Li did not accept his diagnosis without checking. They turned off the motor, placed a protective screen beside the work area, and let him inspect it from a safe distance. The sound came from a steel bar striking its cover. A few people laughed, calling the alarm mere mischief. Samir reminded them that prevention still mattered: a harmless noise could hide a different danger tomorrow.

後方倉庫響起警報。遊戲把它標成爆炸風險，助理便想把裡面所有人直接鎖住。薩米爾認出聲音來自捲門鬆動的彈簧，不是炸彈。他警告，錯誤處置可能毀掉整個入口。江梨沒有不經檢查就接受他的判斷。他們關閉馬達，在工作區旁放置防護板，讓他從安全距離查看。聲音來自鋼條撞擊外殼。有人笑說警報只是惡作劇，薩米爾卻提醒他們，預防仍然重要；今天無害的聲音，明天可能掩蓋別種危險。

The official offered to erase Samir's debt if he stopped speaking to Li. Samir said no. His refusal did not make him wealthy or safe, but it let him keep the truth about his own hours. Kena and Nari agreed to witness his statement, then sent a dispute through the board. The screen rejected it, claiming that an attendance picture counted as consent. That was a new problem, not a defeat. Li photographed the rejection and compared it with the written rule requiring two awake witnesses. She marked the contradiction in red. A child waiting nearby asked if a rule could change because someone important wanted it to. “It can be broken,” Li said. “That is why we keep a copy.”

官員表示，只要薩米爾不再和江梨說話，就能刪掉他的債務。薩米爾拒絕了。拒絕不會讓他變富有或立刻安全，但至少讓他守住自己的工時真相。珂娜和娜里同意為他作證，把異議送進面板。畫面卻駁回，聲稱出勤照片就等於同意。這是新問題，不是失敗的終點。江梨拍下駁回訊息，與要求兩名清醒見證人的書面規則比較，在矛盾處畫上紅線。旁邊等候的孩子問，規則會不會因為重要人物想改就改。「它可能被違反，」江梨說，「所以我們才要保留副本。」

The girl asked Samir whether a worker's photograph could be used like an autograph. He explained the difference slowly. His employer had been allowed to record attendance, not to give away his hours. A signature had a purpose, and that purpose mattered. Li wrote the distinction on a card for other workers: ASK WHAT THE DOCUMENT AUTHORIZES. People gathered around to read it. The fighter could still sell his own time if he wished, but he could not sell somebody else's. At the doorway, the clay star dried into a shape that belonged to the child who had made it.

女孩問薩米爾，工人的照片能不能當作親筆簽名使用。他慢慢解釋差別。雇主獲准記錄出勤，不代表能贈送他的時數。簽名有特定用途，而用途很重要。江梨替其他工人寫下一張卡：「先問文件授權了什麼。」人們圍過來讀。格鬥選手若願意，仍可賣自己的時間，卻不能賣別人的。門口的黏土星星乾成固定形狀，仍屬於親手做出它的孩子。

By evening, the fighter had removed his costume and his audience was gone. The girl's clay star remained outside, hardened by the wind. She asked Li whether adults had created the game because they hated sleeping. Li answered that some adults believed they could shift the cost of their comfort onto other people. She did not want the girl to think every decision was cruel, nor that an easy apology repaired harm. Samir found a safer latch and offered to return on his next paid shift, not tonight. Li accepted the limit. The night board announced a new round and displayed his disputed hour as unpaid. He still could not spend it, but the claim against him was now visible to everyone.

到了傍晚，格鬥選手脫下服裝，圍觀人群也散了。女孩刻的黏土星星被風吹乾，仍留在門外。她問江梨，是不是大人討厭睡覺才創造遊戲。江梨說，有些大人相信自己可以把舒服的代價轉嫁給別人。她不希望孩子認為每個決定都殘忍，也不希望她以為一句道歉就能修好傷害。薩米爾找到較安全的門閂，表示會在下次有薪班回來修，而不是今晚。江梨尊重他的界線。夜間面板宣布新一輪，將他有爭議的時數顯示為未支付。他暫時仍不能使用，但別人已能看見這筆不合理的索取。
