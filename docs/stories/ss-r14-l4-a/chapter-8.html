The Door That Looked Identical

零點拍賣：把睡眠還給城市 · 第 8 / 25 章 · 3000–4000 · 英中對照 · 校訂中

The eighth trial began near a hospital nursery, where two identical white doors stood beside a painted arch. One opened onto a safe stairwell; the other led to a storage room filled with broken equipment. The game challenged contestants to choose quickly. A technician named Edda noticed that the handwriting on the warning signs differed, although the printed numbers matched. Li asked everyone to hold their position while she checked the doors with a hose camera. A child could hear people inside the storage room. Edda equipped the team with gloves and a portable light. The game accused them of being lousy contestants. Li said that a defensible decision needed evidence, not speed.

第八場試煉開在醫院育嬰室附近，兩扇完全相同的白門立在彩繪拱門旁。一扇通向安全樓梯，另一扇通往堆滿損壞設備的儲藏室。遊戲挑戰參賽者快速選擇。名叫艾達的技師發現警告牌上的手寫字跡不同，雖然印刷編號一樣。江梨請大家停在原地，自己用軟管攝影機檢查門後。一名孩子聽見儲藏室裡有人聲。艾達替隊伍準備手套和攜帶式燈具。遊戲嘲笑她們是差勁的參賽者。江梨說，能夠被合理辯護的決定需要證據，而不是速度。

Inside the room were three people who had entered to find medicine, including a woman late in pregnancy. They had misunderstood a sign saying “temporary access” and thought it meant the room was open for public use. A fallen shelf had crushed a wooden box against the door. One person blamed an ancestor whose portrait hung in the corridor, saying the building was cursed. Li refused to turn guilt into a story about someone who could not answer. Samir worked with Edda to lift the shelf using proper supports. Their craft was less exciting than a heroic blow, but it allowed the people inside to leave without a second injury. The nurse checked the pregnant woman and arranged further care.

儲藏室裡有三個進去找藥的人，其中一名女子已懷孕後期。她們誤解「暫時通行」的標示，以為房間對外開放。倒塌的架子壓住木箱，擋住門。有人責怪走廊肖像中的祖先，說建築被詛咒。江梨拒絕把罪惡感變成針對無法回答之人的故事。薩米爾和艾達用正確支撐方式抬起架子。他們的工藝不如英雄一擊精彩，卻讓裡面的人不再受傷便能離開。護理師檢查孕婦，安排後續照護。

An official asked Li to launch a public contest in which teams would choose doors for prizes. She declined. The proposed contest would involve people who had never agreed to become part of a test. Instead she made a suggestion: install a physical label on each door and ask staff to verify it against a building plan. When the official objected that a printed plan might also be wrong, Edda agreed. They would need to compare it with actual openings and exits. A distinguished surgeon who worked on the floor signed the inspection record. His title did not make the finding correct; his direct observation helped. Li added her own name, along with the time and the limits of the inspection.

一名官員要江梨舉辦公開競賽，讓各隊猜門換獎品。她拒絕，因為那會牽涉從未同意成為測試對象的人。她改提建議：在每扇門裝上實體標籤，再由職員對照建築圖驗證。官員反對說印刷圖也可能有錯，艾達表示同意，因此必須再與實際門口和出口比對。一名在該樓層工作的資深外科醫師簽署檢查紀錄。頭銜不會自動讓結果正確，但他的親眼觀察有幫助。江梨也簽上名字，註明時間與檢查限制。

The hospital director revealed that the auction had copied door descriptions from an old safety manual. Some words had suffered a stroke of careless editing: a single line was missing, changing “staff only” into “all staff and visitors.” Edda showed the original paper with the mark still visible. Li felt anger, but she did not want to name a culprit before checking who had changed the document. She drafted a request for the editing history. The auction screen replied with a riddle: TWO DOORS LOOK THE SAME, BUT ONLY ONE HAS A PRICE. Kena turned the sentence around. “If the wrong door charges nothing,” she said, “is the right one being sold because we need it?”

院長透露，拍賣複製了舊安全手冊中的門口說明。有人改字時一筆疏忽，少了一條線，讓「僅限員工」變成「所有員工與訪客」。艾達拿出原紙，修改痕跡仍可見。江梨感到憤怒，但在查清誰改文件前，不願指認犯人。她寫信要求編輯歷程。拍賣螢幕用謎語回答：「兩道門一模一樣，只有一扇有價格。」珂娜把問題反過來想。「如果錯的門免費，」她說，「是不是因為我們需要對的門，所以有人拿它來賣？」

Contrary to the auction's claim, the identical doors had never been designed for a race. Edda found an old note stating that one door must remain locked until the other was cleared. Anyone who misunderstood the pair as a shortcut might walk into a storage hazard. Li asked her to place the note beside the real diagram and explain its limits in plain language. The hospital staff then checked the instruction by walking through the safe door with an empty cart. They used no patients in the test. The route worked, and the new label remained readable even when the lights were dim.

與拍賣宣稱的相反，兩扇相同的門從來不是為競速設計。艾達找到舊筆記，寫著必須先確認一扇清空，另一扇才可開啟。任何誤解兩扇門是捷徑的人都可能走進危險儲藏室。江梨請她把筆記放在真實圖示旁，用平易語言說明限制。醫院職員用空推車走過安全門檢查指示，沒有拿病人做測試。路線可行，而且燈暗時新標誌仍看得清楚。

That night, the team marked both doors with large temporary signs, following the surgeon's instructions. They left the damaged room closed and arranged for a later repair by paid staff. Li took no photographs of the patients. Their involvement in the event did not mean their bodies belonged in a public report. In her notebook she drew the two entrances with different colors and added the same phrase below each: VERIFY BEFORE USE. Kena asked whether a map could ever be perfect. Li replied that a useful record did not have to predict every future change. It had to make people aware of what had actually been checked, and what still needed attention.

當晚，隊伍照外科醫師指示，在兩扇門上掛上醒目的臨時標誌。損壞的房間繼續封閉，等日後由有薪員工修復。江梨沒有拍攝病人。參與事件不代表他們的身體就屬於公開報告。她在筆記本用不同顏色畫出兩個入口，下面寫同一句話：「使用前先確認。」珂娜問，地圖有可能永遠正確嗎？江梨回答，有用的紀錄不必預測未來所有變化，只需清楚讓人知道已確認哪些事情、還有哪些需要處理。
