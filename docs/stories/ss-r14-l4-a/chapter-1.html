安靜一夜的價格

The Price of a Quiet Night

零點拍賣：把睡眠還給城市 · 第 1 / 25 章 · 3000–4000 · 英中對照 · 校訂中

用互動版查詞、朗讀與收藏

At eleven fifty-nine, the copper clock above the old opera hall stopped. Li Jiang, who planned shifts for a crowded clothing factory, was leaving a late assembly about unpaid hours. A blue auction counter rose out of the pavement. Its slogan was persuasive: BUY A SAFE MORNING WITH ONE HOUR OF SLEEP. The city ministry called the event an unexplained phenomenon and advised everyone to follow the glowing route. Li noticed that every adult had received eight silver marks on a wrist display. The marks did not show money. They showed hours of rest. A youthful musician laughed as if the whole street were entertainment, then fell asleep standing when he pressed the number seven.

十一點五十九分，老歌劇院上方的銅鐘停了。江梨是服飾工廠的輪班規劃員，剛參加完關於無薪工時的集會。人行道上升起一個藍色拍賣計數器。標語很有說服力：「用一小時睡眠，買一個安全的早晨。」市府部門把事件稱為無法解釋的現象，建議大家跟著發光路線走。江梨注意到，每個成年人的手腕畫面都多了八道銀色記號。它們不是錢，而是休息時數。一名年輕樂手把整條街當作娛樂笑著，按下數字七後，卻站著睡了過去。

A security volunteer called the musician a coward, assuming he was pretending. Li checked his facial expression and saw that he could not focus his eyes. At the factory she had seen workers make dangerous mistakes after repeated night shifts. She refused to let the guard shake him awake. A cleaner carrying a broom helped her lower him onto a dry bench, while a woman with a shady business card offered to sell him two more hours. A broken cleaning bottle spilled acid across the tiles, forcing the crowd to change direction. Li asked the cleaner to keep people away from the stain. No game score could justify causing an injury when a safer space stood three steps away.

一名保全志工罵樂手是懦夫，以為他在裝睡。江梨觀察他的臉部表情，發現他的眼睛已無法聚焦。她在工廠見過人連續上夜班後犯下危險錯誤，因此不准保全搖醒他。一名拿掃帚的清潔員幫她把樂手扶到乾燥長椅上；一名拿著可疑名片的女人則想賣給他額外兩小時。破裂的清潔瓶讓酸液流過地磚，迫使人群轉向。江梨請清潔員阻止人靠近污漬。旁邊明明有安全位置，就不該為遊戲分數害人受傷。

Beneath the counter was a narrow notice in plain type. Li read it twice while the crowd shouted: “A transferred hour is valid only when the giver is awake and two witnesses confirm free choice. A disputed bid may be frozen before collection.” The official voice did not mention that sentence. It wanted speed, not questions. Li asked a nearby technician to construct a simple barrier around the bench with folding chairs. An older man warned that she should not interfere with the auction. She replied that she was not stopping anybody from bidding; she was stopping people from stepping on someone who had collapsed. Three strangers agreed to cooperate and took positions beside the chairs.

計數器下方有一行普通字體的小公告。人群吵鬧時，江梨讀了兩遍：「移轉休息時數時，給予者必須清醒，且由兩名見證人確認出於自由意願。遭到異議的出價，可在扣取前凍結。」官方聲音完全沒有提到這句話；它只催大家快走。江梨請附近的技師用折疊椅組成簡易圍欄。一名長者警告她不要干涉拍賣。她回答，自己沒有阻止任何人出價，只是不讓人踩到已經昏倒的人。三名陌生人同意合作，站在椅子旁。

A young apprentice named Kena pointed at the screen. Somebody had offered the sleeping musician's remaining hour to a prominent bidder, although his hands had never touched the transfer key. Li asked Kena to look at the record of usage rather than the amount shown at the top. The first seven hours had been spent through his own button; the eighth had been moved under an empty signature. When Li pressed DISPUTE, the glass counter demanded two witnesses. The cleaner and Kena both signed that the musician had been unconscious. A line turned yellow: COLLECTION FROZEN. The highest bidder lost the hour, but the musician did not immediately wake. The damage already done could not be canceled by correcting a number.

一名叫珂娜的年輕學徒指著畫面。有人把樂手剩下的一小時轉給知名競標者，雖然他的手從沒碰過轉移鍵。江梨要珂娜查看使用紀錄，而不是畫面上方的總額。前七小時是樂手自己按掉的，第八小時卻在沒有簽名的情況下被轉走。江梨按下「提出異議」後，玻璃計數器要求兩名證人。清潔員和珂娜都簽名證明樂手當時失去意識。一行字變黃：「扣取已凍結。」最高出價者失去了那一小時，但樂手沒有立刻醒來。數字修正了，之前造成的傷害卻不能就此取消。

Before the hall closed, Li measured the distance between the bench and the official rest door. It was only twenty steps, yet the auction made the distance feel impossible by placing a price above it. Nari helped the musician breathe steadily while Kena read the emergency notice aloud. Their small group agreed on a rule for tomorrow: nobody would claim to speak for a sleeping person. They would write down what the person had done before losing awareness, and leave every uncertain answer blank. Li had learned that an empty field could be more honest than a confident guess.

大廳關閉前，江梨量了長椅到正式休息室門口的距離。只有二十步，拍賣卻在門上標價，讓距離顯得難以跨越。娜里幫樂手維持平穩呼吸，珂娜大聲讀出緊急公告。小隊約好明天的一項規則：沒有人能自稱代表睡著的人發言。他們會記下當事人失去意識前確實做過的事，所有不確定的答案都留白。江梨學到，空白欄位有時比肯定的猜測誠實。

The auction offered a final lesson before midnight: “Unspent hours return after natural sleep. Spent hours do not.” Li copied the wording in her notebook. She did not know whether the rule was true, but it gave them something to test. The clock began moving again for exactly ten seconds, then stopped at midnight. A voice declared that twenty-four auction nights remained before ownership of the public sleeping halls would be assigned. Kena asked whether anyone could own a place where tired workers went to rest. Li looked at the cleaner, whose name was Nari, and at the musician on the bench. “We will find out what the game can actually take,” she said, “before we agree that it owns anything.”

午夜前，拍賣又出示一行規則：「未使用時數會在自然睡眠後恢復；已消耗時數不予退還。」江梨把原文抄進筆記本。她不知道規則是否真實，但至少有個可以測試的說法。鐘又走了十秒，隨後停在午夜。一個聲音宣布，公共休息大廳將在二十五個拍賣夜後決定歸屬。珂娜問，是否有人可以擁有疲憊工人用來休息的場所。江梨望向名叫娜里的清潔員，又看向長椅上的樂手。「在同意遊戲擁有任何東西之前，」她說，「我們要先查清楚，它到底能拿走什麼。」
