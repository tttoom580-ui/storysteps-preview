空桌上的來信

A Letter from the Empty Desk

零點拍賣：把睡眠還給城市 · 第 4 / 25 章 · 3000–4000 · 英中對照 · 校訂中

用互動版查詞、朗讀與收藏

The city library opened one room for people with frozen auction claims. Its librarian, a serious woman named Ada, refused to give anyone approval to use another person's account. “An urgent request is not the same as permission,” she told a volunteer. Ada had once worked as a translator for an embassy; she could read the formal language hidden in the auction contract. The small print placed emphasis on possession, as though a person who held a screen also possessed the sleeping hours displayed on it. Li thought that was nonsense. A machine could follow a person in orbit around the city without ever obtaining their consent. She asked Ada to mark the margin beside the sentence rather than erase it.

市立圖書館開放一個房間，讓拍賣時數被凍結的人使用。嚴肅的圖書館員艾達拒絕批准任何人動用別人的帳戶。「緊急請求不等於取得許可。」她對志工說。艾達過去曾替使館翻譯，讀得懂拍賣合約裡的正式語言。小字特別強調「持有」，彷彿持有畫面的人就擁有上面顯示的睡眠時數。江梨認為那是胡說。就算機器繞著城市追蹤某個人，也不代表取得對方同意。她請艾達在句子旁的空白處做記號，不要直接抹掉。

A researcher from the university presented an earnest proposal to measure how much sleep the game actually took. He promised that the project would accomplish a complete report in one week. Kena bounced a rubber ball against a table while waiting, and Ada asked her to stop. Its continual noise disturbed a worker trying to recover. The researcher looked disappointed when Li rejected his plan to watch patients through hidden cameras. “Your measurements cannot depend on passive subjects,” she explained. “People must be able to say no, even if their refusal leaves your chart incomplete.” He agreed to revise the method, although his original plan would have been easier to publish.

一名大學研究員提出真誠的計畫，希望量測遊戲到底拿走多少睡眠。他保證一週內能完成整份報告。珂娜等候時把橡皮球在桌邊彈來彈去，艾達要她停止；持續的聲響妨礙一名工人休息。江梨拒絕研究員暗中拍攝病人的方法，他顯得很失望。「你的測量不能建立在無法拒絕的被動受試者身上，」她解釋，「人有權說不，就算這會讓圖表不完整。」研究員同意修改方法，雖然原方案更容易發表。

On a borrowed page, the researcher's assistant had drawn a cartoon of an imaginary murderer chasing a sleeping clock. He said the rhyme beneath it was meant to amuse frightened children. Li appreciated his intention but asked them not to hang the picture near people who had lost family members. A woman at the library had been receiving threats from an unknown bidder; the joke would be painful to her. The assistant apologized and offered to make a different decoration. Ada suggested drawings of people asking for help and receiving an answer. Those pictures would not make the crisis disappear, but they might show children a form of action that did not require violence.

研究員助理在借來的紙上畫了一個想像中的殺人者追趕睡著的鐘，底下配了一段押韻的句子。他說那是為了逗受驚的孩子笑。江梨理解他的好意，仍請他別把圖掛在失去親人的人附近。圖書館有名女子一直受到陌生競標者威脅，那個笑話只會讓她痛苦。助理道歉，表示可以畫不同的裝飾。艾達建議畫人們求助、有人回應的場景。圖畫不能讓危機消失，卻可能讓孩子看見不必靠暴力也能採取行動。

Ada found a real letter addressed to the library, delivered before the auction began. It came from a former municipal clerk who had warned that the city planned to sell operating rights to its public sleeping halls. The clerk had requested a public hearing, but no one had answered. Li asked whether the document was genuine. Ada showed its receipt stamp, the signature, and the calendar entry confirming its arrival. The letter offered a possible motive, not proof of who had altered the current bids. They copied it, placed the original in protective paper, and asked a second librarian to witness the process. Li felt a small accomplishment: for once, they had a question that existed before the game did.

艾達找到一封拍賣開始前就寄到圖書館的信。寫信的是一名前市府職員，他警告城市準備出售公共休息大廳的營運權。他曾要求公開聽證，卻沒有人回覆。江梨問文件是否真實。艾達展示收件章、簽名，以及確認收件日期的行事曆。這封信提供可能的動機，卻不能證明是誰竄改了如今的出價。他們複印文件，把原件放進保護紙，並請第二名圖書館員見證。江梨感受到一點成就：他們終於有一個在遊戲出現之前就存在的問題。

The researcher returned with a new consent form. It asked what observations he would collect, how long he would keep them, and whether participants wanted their names removed. Ada read each question and suggested a larger blank space for refusal. Kena helped put the pages in order. The change delayed the study by two days, but it removed the need to guess whether a silent person had agreed. Li asked the assistant to keep the revised form alongside the first version. The difference between them was useful evidence of how a good intention could become a better practice.

研究員帶著新的同意表回來。表格說明會蒐集什麼觀察、保存多久，以及參與者是否希望刪去姓名。艾達逐題閱讀，建議為拒絕保留更大的空白區。珂娜幫忙把頁面排好。這項改動讓研究延後兩天，卻不再需要猜測沉默的人是否同意。江梨請助理把修訂版和第一版一起保存。兩者差異正好說明，好意如何透過修改變成更好的實踐。

That evening the auction offered a new reward to any person who could keep the library open all night. A few students volunteered, but Li refused to celebrate work done without rest. She asked them to make a rota with a fixed closing time for each individual, then offered to take only one shift herself. Ada pointed to the empty desk where the clerk's letter had rested. “We are not a place that belongs to the loudest bidder,” she said. The clock in the hall showed twelve without moving. Kena put away the rubber ball. There was no need to bounce a thing to prove the night had begun.

當晚，拍賣表示任何能讓圖書館整夜營業的人都可得到新獎勵。幾名學生自願幫忙，江梨卻拒絕慶祝沒有休息的勞動。她請大家排輪值表，替每個人設定明確下班時間，自己也只承擔一班。艾達指著前市府職員的信曾放過的空桌。「這裡不是屬於出價最大聲的人的地方。」她說。大廳的鐘停在十二點。珂娜收起橡皮球。沒有必要靠任何東西彈跳，來證明夜晚已經開始。
