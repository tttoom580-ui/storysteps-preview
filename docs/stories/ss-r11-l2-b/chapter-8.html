裝滿故事的架子

A Shelf Full of Stories

十點之後，還有人在等 · 第 8 / 25 章 · 1000–2000 · 英中對照 · 校訂中

用互動版查詞、朗讀與收藏

The old guard room had one high shelf and a large wooden box. Iris thought every missing file might be there. Noah gave a gentle warning: “We may find nothing. Please do not make that mean we failed.” They opened the box with permission from the office. Inside were an average of twenty notes for each year, far more than Iris expected. Some came from workers who missed the last bus. Others were written by visitors whom a guard had helped. The papers were not a map to treasure. They were a record of ordinary people being kind to each other.

舊警衛室裡有一層高高的架子和一個大木箱。怡思覺得所有遺失檔案可能都在這裡。諾亞溫柔提醒：「也可能什麼都找不到，別把那當成失敗。」兩人得到辦公室許可後才打開箱子。裡面每年平均有二十張便條，比怡思想像中多得多。有些出自錯過末班車的員工，有些是受到警衛幫助的訪客所寫。這些紙不是藏寶地圖，而是普通人互相善待的紀錄。

One note described a childhood barbecue beside the river. Another thanked a guard who had returned a set of chopsticks after a school dinner. A third described a heavy load of books carried up the stairs. “Why keep things as small as these?” Iris asked. Noah turned a page forward and showed her a line written in blue ink: “A small help can have a large meaning.” He said his grandmother had believed that the means of helping mattered. A kind action should never demand someone's story in return. Iris read the line again, slower this time.

有張便條寫到童年在河邊烤肉的記憶；另一張謝謝警衛在校宴後歸還一雙筷子；第三張則描述有人幫忙把沉重書籍搬上樓。「為什麼連這麼小的事也要保存？」怡思問。諾亞把一頁往前翻，指著藍墨水寫的一句話：「小小幫助也可能有大意義。」他說祖母一直相信，幫助人的方式很重要。善意不應要求別人用私人故事來交換。怡思又慢慢讀了一遍那句話。

Their visit was interrupted by the noise of a delivery cart. A worker had been given a command to clear the room before the next inspection. He apologized, but he could not leave their boxes everywhere. Iris asked for an hour. The worker agreed only if they kept the path open. Noah moved the heavy load away from the door, while Iris labeled each shelf. A little stone tied to an old string fell from one folder. It had a number painted on it. The string was tied around a small wooden stick. “Whatever this is, it belongs with its paper,” Iris said. She put it back without making a guess.

送貨推車的聲音打斷了他們。工人接到命令，必須在下次檢查前清空房間。他道了歉，卻不能讓箱子隨意堆在通道。怡思請求再給一小時。工人同意，條件是保持走道暢通。諾亞把重箱子搬離門口，怡思則替每一層架子貼標籤。資料夾裡掉出一顆綁著細繩的小石頭，上面漆了數字。繩子也綁著一根小木棒。「不管這是什麼，都應該和原本的紙放在一起，」怡思說。她沒有胡亂猜測，直接放了回去。

By the window, a note told the story of a servant in a school play who lost his costume before the show. In reality, he was a history student, and no one had needed to save a kingdom. Iris laughed at the difference between the play and the real event. Noah showed her a photograph of a young guard holding a lost hat. “That is my grandmother,” he said. For years he had feared the records would disappear because nobody had time to care for them. Seeing the photo made him quiet. Iris did not reach for his hand. She asked if he wanted to sit.

窗邊有張紙，記載某齣校園戲劇裡扮演僕人的學生在演出前弄丟戲服。現實裡，他只是歷史系學生，根本沒有人需要拯救王國。怡思被戲裡戲外的差距逗笑。諾亞拿出一張照片：年輕警衛拿著拾獲帽子站在門口。「那是我祖母，」他說。多年來他害怕紀錄會消失，只因沒有人有時間照顧它們。看著照片，他沉默下來。怡思沒有伸手牽他，而是問他想不想坐一下。

After a while Noah told her why the box mattered. His grandmother had once told him that fate was not a reason to leave another person alone in the rain. “You cannot fix every life,” she used to say. “You can open one door.” Iris remembered the first line she had written in her own notebook, almost the same thought. “Maybe we are not as different as we think,” she said. Noah looked at her and smiled. Then both of them noticed the clock. They had only ten minutes left, and their best chance was to take photographs of the folder numbers, not the private letters inside.

過了一會兒，諾亞說出箱子對他的意義。祖母以前告訴他，不能把命運當成讓別人獨自淋雨的理由。「你無法修好每個人的人生，」她常說，「但能替一個人開一扇門。」怡思想起自己第一天在筆記本寫下的句子，幾乎一模一樣。「或許我們沒有想像中那麼不同，」她說。諾亞看著她笑了。接著兩人同時看向時鐘。只剩十分鐘，他們最好的辦法是拍下資料夾編號，而不是裡面私人信件的內容。

Back at the station, Iris made a list of possible next steps. First, they would check the source of each folder's date. Second, they would ask the office how long the papers could stay in storage. Third, they would invite the former guard to tell the group what she was willing to share. Noah liked the plan. “We cannot solve a mystery by treating every life as our property,” he said. Iris agreed. She felt that the real discovery was not hidden in a box. It was in the way they were beginning to work together, with care and without hurry.

回到廣播站，怡思列出下一步。第一，確認每個資料夾日期的來源；第二，詢問辦公室紙本能保存多久；第三，邀請前警衛自行決定願意分享哪些故事。諾亞喜歡這個計畫。「我們不能把別人的人生當成自己的財產來解謎，」他說。怡思同意。她感到真正的發現並不藏在木箱裡，而是他們開始合作的方式：細心，且不急躁。
