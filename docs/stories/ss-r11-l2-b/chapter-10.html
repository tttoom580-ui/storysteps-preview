A Voice With Permission

十點之後，還有人在等 · 第 10 / 25 章 · 1000–2000 · 英中對照 · 校訂中

用互動版查詞、朗讀與收藏

A festival room became available for their next trial, but they were allowed to use it only for two hours. Iris gathered the volunteers and explained the plan. One student brought biscuits, another carried a model fox made from paper, and a third arrived with a wooden goose used by the theater club. A cook also delivered a yam with a note: “Please return this to the correct kitchen.” Noah wondered why so many strange things came to a lost-property event. “Because people lose things everywhere,” Iris said. She arranged them on a table at a proper distance from private records. Every display item had a clear sign saying whether visitors could touch it.

學校提供一間節慶活動室讓他們進行下一次試辦，但只能使用兩小時。怡思集合志工，說明流程。有人帶來餅乾，有人提著紙做的狐狸模型，第三位則搬來劇團的木鵝。廚師還送來一條番薯，附註：「請交還正確的廚房。」諾亞不懂為何失物招領活動總出現奇怪的東西。「因為人在任何地方都會丟東西，」怡思說。她把展品和私人紀錄保持適當距離。每樣展示物都貼了清楚標誌，說明訪客能否觸摸。

The first task was to measure the length of the path from the room to the office. A volunteer used a meter stick, while Noah checked the width of the door. Iris wrote the numbers in a small unit chart so visitors could see where a wheelchair would fit. The improvement was gradual, not sudden. They moved a table five centimeters, tested again, and moved it once more. A student offered congratulations before they were finished. Iris smiled but asked him to wait. A good result needed a real test, not only a cheerful message.

第一項任務是量出活動室到辦公室的路徑長度。志工拿公尺尺測量，諾亞檢查門的寬度。怡思把數字寫成簡單的單位表，讓訪客知道輪椅能在哪裡通行。改進是漸進的，不是一瞬間成功。他們先移動桌子五公分，重新測試，再搬一次。一名學生還沒等他們完成就說恭喜。怡思笑著請他再等一下。好結果需要真正測試，不只是愉快的祝賀。

A young musician offered to play a short musical piece between notices. Iris liked the idea, but she was careful with the sound level. The festival could have a gentle style without drowning out the messages. Noah glued a small sign beside the volume control. On it he wrote: “One notice per minute during the trial.” The students called the plan too strict. Iris explained that they needed to know what listeners could understand. If the sound failed, they would ignore the music and return to spoken instructions. A failed experiment would be useful only if they could explain why it failed.

一位年輕音樂家願意在公告之間演奏一段短樂曲。怡思喜歡這主意，卻謹慎控制音量。活動可以有溫柔的音樂風格，不必蓋過訊息。諾亞在音量鈕旁黏上小標籤，寫著：「試辦期間每分鐘一則公告。」學生嫌這規定太嚴。怡思說明，他們需要知道聽眾到底能理解多少。如果聲音出問題，就先忽略音樂，回到口語指示。實驗失敗只有在能說明原因時才有價值。

Halfway through the event, the gardener from the first night came to see them. He had found a second envelope in his shed, marked with the same little bus. Inside was a short note thanking the night driver who had carried an injured student home. Iris asked whether she could record him reading it. “Not all of it,” he said. “The student is still alive and does not like public attention.” They agreed to use one harmless sentence, and the gardener signed a clear permission form. That sentence was their first voice recording made with a full understanding of the limits.

活動進行到一半，第一晚那位園丁來看他們。他在工具棚裡找到第二個信封，上面也有小公車記號。裡面是一張短便條，感謝一位夜班司機曾送受傷學生回家。怡思問能否錄下園丁朗讀。「不要全部，」他說，「那位學生還健在，也不喜歡受到公眾注意。」他們同意只使用一句沒有私人內容的話，園丁也簽了清楚的同意書。這成了他們第一則真正了解使用界線、且取得許可的聲音紀錄。

During the final half hour, a child visited with her aunt and asked about a snail painted on the wall. She had seen a similar drawing during vacation at the countryside school. The aunt said the old night desk had used pictures of animals to guide people along the path, because signs with words were difficult for some visitors. Iris added the fact to her notes. The desk's former design was not simply cute. It was practical. Noah asked whether a new map could use the same idea, with clear text added for people who could not see the pictures.

最後半小時，一名小女孩跟阿姨來訪，問牆上的蝸牛畫是什麼意思。她在鄉下學校度假時看過類似圖案。阿姨說，過去夜間櫃檯會用動物圖案引導大家走路，因為有些訪客不容易讀懂文字標示。怡思把這件事記下。舊設計不只是可愛，而是很實用。諾亞問新的地圖可否沿用這個想法，另外加上清楚文字，照顧看不見圖案的人。

Before the room closed, the director checked the results. They had recorded seven clear messages, answered nineteen questions, and returned four items with proof. No private details had been broadcast. The director offered one more month of trial service and promised to discuss a permanent budget. “That took more courage than I expected,” Iris whispered. “It is not victory yet.” Noah nodded. “But it is a door.” When everyone had left, they shared the last biscuit by the window. Iris asked if he wanted to meet outside their shift one day. He considered the question. “Yes,” he said, “but let us finish this week first.” She appreciated the honest answer.

活動室關閉前，處長查看成果。他們錄了七則清楚公告，回答十九個問題，憑證明歸還四件物品，沒有公開任何私人資料。處長准許再試辦一個月，並答應討論長期預算。「這比我想像中需要更多勇氣，」怡思低聲說，「但還不能說勝利。」諾亞點頭：「但門已經開了。」眾人離開後，他們在窗邊分吃最後一塊餅乾。怡思問他是否願意有一天在值班之外見面。他認真想了一下。「願意，」他說，「但先把這個星期完成。」她喜歡這個誠實的回答。
