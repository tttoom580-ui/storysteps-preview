第一個歸還之夜

The First Return Night

十點之後，還有人在等 · 第 5 / 25 章 · 1000–2000 · 英中對照

用互動版查詞、朗讀與收藏

Friday's trial began with a strange appetite for noodles. A volunteer had cooked too many bowls, and the smell spread through the hall. Iris tried to give her full attention to the sound check, but thunder rolled outside and an electrical light went dark. Noah examined the connection between the old speaker and the wall. “It is not a serious fault,” he said. “The plug is loose.” Iris held the torch while he fixed it. She wanted to praise his brilliant work, but he pointed to the clear label she had placed by the switch. “That helped me more than my tools,” he answered.

星期五的試辦活動從大家突然想吃麵開始。一位志工煮了太多碗麵，香味傳遍大廳。怡思努力把注意力放在聲音測試上，外面卻雷聲轟隆，電燈也熄了一盞。諾亞檢查舊喇叭和牆面之間的電線連接。「不是大故障，」他說，「只是插頭鬆了。」怡思替他照明，讓他修好。她想稱讚他真是天才，諾亞卻指著她貼在開關旁的清楚標籤。「這比工具更幫得上忙，」他回答。

The first caller was a woman searching for a wool hat. She had made it for her son during a snowy winter, and a family picture showed its uneven edge. Noah checked the photograph in private. He did not ask the child to come or tell the audience about the family's domestic life. Iris recorded a simple notice about a hand-made hat and a place to claim it. When the owner came, she touched the soft cloth and smiled. “It still smells like home,” she said. Iris understood that an object could hold much more than its small mass or price.

第一位來電者在找一頂羊毛帽。那是她某年下雪的冬天親手替兒子織的，家庭照片能看出帽沿高低不齊。諾亞私下核對照片，不要求孩子出面，也不把一家人的家庭生活播給大家聽。怡思只錄了手工帽的簡短公告和認領地點。失主來領時，摸著柔軟的布笑了。「還聞得到家的味道，」她說。怡思發現，物品承載的東西往往遠超過重量或價格。

At eight, a conflict began over a small bird painted on a box. One student insisted the image showed a rooster. Another said it was a hen. Both claimed the same box, and a crowd started to watch. Iris asked them to step away from the recording table. “We do not choose a winner because people clap,” she said. Noah used their usual method: ask each person about a hidden mark, then compare it with the sealed list. Neither knew the answer. They decided to wait. The moment might have made an exciting program, but it would have been a poor way to return a box.

八點時，一個畫著小鳥的盒子引發爭執。一位學生堅稱畫的是公雞，另一位說是母雞。兩人都宣稱盒子是自己的，旁邊開始有人圍觀。怡思請兩人離開錄音桌。「不能因為有人鼓掌就決定誰贏，」她說。諾亞照慣用方法，請雙方描述看不見的記號，再比對封存清單。兩人都答不出來，他們決定等待。這段爭執也許能變成精彩節目，卻不是歸還物品的好方法。

During a break, Iris discovered an old card under a notebook. It read, “Whenever the last bus leaves, someone should still be here.” There was no name, only a date from many years ago. Iris wondered who had written it and why the last bus mattered to a lost-and-found office. She showed the card to Noah. His skin went pale for a moment. “My grandmother used to say that,” he replied. “She worked here before the office moved.” Iris could hear something deeper in his voice, but she did not ask him to tell the story on air.

休息時，怡思在筆記本下發現一張舊卡片。上面寫著：「每當末班車開走，這裡還是應該有人。」卡片只有多年前的日期，沒有署名。怡思好奇，是誰寫下這句話？末班車又為何和失物招領室有關？她拿給諾亞看。他的臉色一時變白。「我祖母以前常這樣說，」他回答，「搬遷前她就在這裡工作。」怡思聽得出他語氣裡藏著更深的情緒，卻沒有要求他上節目說出來。

The final return was simple. A student described the exact color of a lunch box and the small crack beside its handle. Her answer matched the private record, and she took it home. Iris wrote the numbers on the board. Three confirmed returns, two uncertain claims, and one canceled recording. The results were smaller than the crowd expected, but the visitors understood why. The director, who had come to watch, said, “You did not hide the mistakes. That gives me a reason to support another test.” Iris felt a quiet pride. She and Noah had not solved every case. They had shown that careful work could make a difference.

最後一件歸還很簡單。一名學生說得出便當盒的確切顏色，以及提把旁的小裂痕。答案與私人紀錄相符，她便把盒子帶回家。怡思把數字寫上白板：三件確認歸還、兩件有疑問的認領、一則取消的廣播。成果比圍觀者期待的少，但來訪者明白原因。前來觀察的處長說：「你們沒有隱瞞錯誤，這讓我有理由支持下一次測試。」怡思感到安靜的驕傲。她和諾亞沒解決每個案件，卻證明仔細工作能帶來改變。

Outside, rain became a thin mist. Iris offered Noah the last bowl of noodles and told him he could refuse if he had lost his appetite. He took it and laughed. “I may know how to repair a cable, but I do not know how to talk to you when you smile like that.” She nearly answered with a joke, then stopped. She liked him, but she wanted their work to remain good even if their feelings changed. “Start with asking about the card,” she said. Noah looked toward the empty hall. “Not tonight. Another day, when I am ready.” Iris nodded. For once, waiting felt like a kind of closeness.

外面的雨漸漸成了薄霧。怡思把最後一碗麵讓給諾亞，說如果沒胃口可以不吃。他接過碗笑了。「我會修電線，卻不知道妳這樣笑時該怎麼跟妳說話。」她差點用玩笑回答，卻停住了。她喜歡他，但希望即使感情有變，工作也能繼續好好做。「那就先談談卡片吧，」她說。諾亞望向空蕩大廳。「不是今晚。等我準備好，再找一天。」怡思點頭。這一次，等待反而像是一種親近。
