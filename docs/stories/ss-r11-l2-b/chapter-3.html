打不開的門

The Door That Would Not Open

十點之後，還有人在等 · 第 3 / 25 章 · 1000–2000 · 英中對照

用互動版查詞、朗讀與收藏

On the third night, the entrance to the store room would not open. A classmate named June attempted to pull it, but a loose metal piece caught her bag. She blamed the lock at once. Noah examined the door and found that the real cause was a bent sign behind the handle. “Do not force it,” he warned. Iris carried the sign to a table. The room held a supply of old tape, empty boxes, and a notebook from the final year of the former station. Nobody had looked inside for months. June joked that the door must be guarding a treasure. Iris was too busy checking for damage to laugh.

第三晚，儲藏室的入口打不開。名叫六月的同學試著拉門，卻讓袋子勾到鬆脫的金屬片。她立刻責怪門鎖。諾亞檢查後發現，真正原因是把手後方彎掉的告示牌。「不要硬拉，」他提醒。怡思把告示牌搬到桌上。房裡有舊膠帶、空紙箱，還有前一代廣播站最後一年的筆記本。幾個月都沒人進來看過。六月開玩笑說，門一定在守護寶藏。怡思忙著檢查損壞，沒空笑。

A cloudy sky made the room dark before dinner. Iris found a pair of earrings in a brown bag and an empty cereal box beside it. They seemed ordinary, but the notebook linked them to two separate reports. One was about a lost shopping bag; the other concerned a visitor who had handed a box to the wrong counter. “We must not cheat by putting the two stories together,” she said. Noah nodded. He wanted to continue the search, but their general rule was simple: keep every object's record with its own date and place. He wrote two numbers on clean paper and put them in separate folders.

陰雲讓房間在晚餐前就暗了下來。怡思在咖啡色袋子裡發現一對耳環，旁邊還有空麥片盒。兩樣東西看似普通，筆記本卻把它們連到兩份不同報告。一份關於遺失的購物袋，另一份關於訪客把盒子交錯櫃檯。「我們不能偷懶，把兩件事硬湊成一個故事，」她說。諾亞點頭。他想繼續查，但基本規則很簡單：每件物品的紀錄都必須保留原來的日期與地點。他在乾淨紙上寫好兩個號碼，分別放進資料夾。

The next problem came from a student runner who had left his scooter outside. He needed an answer quickly: his lost pass was required for a sports event tomorrow. Iris wanted to make an urgent announcement. Noah explained that a pass carried personal information, so they could only say that a card had been found, not what was printed on it. The student gave a nod and described the mark on its corner. He had no government card with him, but the office could use a private account check to confirm ownership. He thanked them for being careful even when he was in a hurry.

下一個問題來自把機車停在門外的田徑跑者。他急著要答覆：明天有運動活動，卻找不到通行證。怡思想立刻緊急廣播。諾亞說明，通行證含有私人資料，因此他們只能公告找到卡片，不能播報印在上面的資訊。學生點點頭，描述卡片角落的記號。他沒有帶政府核發的證件，但辦公室可以透過私人帳戶核對所有權。他謝謝兩人在他匆忙時仍然細心。

They carried a small recorder to the hall where volunteers operated a food table. Iris hoped to record the sound of normal campus life, not just stories of missing things. A volunteer asked whether a liberal policy meant everyone could take as much food as they wanted. Noah answered that the supply was limited, so fairness also required counting what remained. A nearby student was recording an interview for a public class project. Iris saw how one word, “fair,” could start three different conversations. She suggested a future episode about rules that help people rather than make them afraid.

兩人把小錄音機帶到志工經營的餐食桌旁。怡思希望錄下普通校園生活的聲音，而不只有遺失物的故事。一位志工問，寬鬆的政策是否代表每個人都能無限取食。諾亞回答，物資有限，公平也包含知道還剩多少。附近另一位學生正在為公共課程錄訪談。怡思發現，「公平」一詞竟能引出三種不同的對話。她提議將來做一集節目，談談規則如何幫助人，而不只是讓人害怕。

Back at the station, they found a link between the old notebook and the current label problem. A page said the desk had changed its numbering method during a school-wide move. Some boxes received new labels without the older labels being removed. The note was dated, signed, and easy to check. Iris felt foolish for almost turning a small error into a story about theft. Noah laughed gently and said, “A good attempt can still fail. What matters is what you do after it.” She opened a fresh page and wrote the cause, the evidence, and the next step.

回到廣播站後，他們發現舊筆記本與目前標籤問題之間的關聯。某頁記載，校方全面搬遷時，櫃檯曾改用新的編號方法。有些盒子加上新標籤，卻沒有撕掉舊標籤。這則紀錄有日期、簽名，容易查證。怡思覺得自己差點把小錯誤講成偷竊故事，實在很傻。諾亞溫和地笑道：「好的嘗試也會失敗，重要的是之後怎麼做。」她翻到新頁，寫下原因、證據與下一步。

At the end of the shift, June returned with a screwdriver and a little paint. She fixed the bent sign while Noah watched the door. The entrance opened without a sound. Iris recorded that sound anyway: a simple click and a breath of air. “You record very strange things,” June said. Iris agreed. In her headphones, the click meant something had finally moved. She sent the short file to Noah without a title. He replied with three words: “Door, not mystery.” She smiled, then added a fourth word to her own copy: “For now.”

值班結束時，六月帶著螺絲起子和少量油漆回來。她修好彎曲的告示牌，諾亞在旁協助看門。入口無聲地打開了。怡思仍錄下那個聲音：簡短一聲喀噠，接著是一口空氣。「妳連這種怪聲音都要錄，」六月說。怡思承認確實如此。在耳機裡，那一聲喀噠表示事情終於有了進展。她把短音檔傳給諾亞，沒有命名。他回覆三個字：「門，不是謎。」她微笑，自己又加上一句：「目前是。」
