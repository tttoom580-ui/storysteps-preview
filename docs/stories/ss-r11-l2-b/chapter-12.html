屋頂上的雨

Rain on the Roof

十點之後，還有人在等 · 第 12 / 25 章 · 1000–2000 · 英中對照 · 校訂中

用互動版查詞、朗讀與收藏

A storm came earlier than the weather report expected. Iris heard rain strike the roof above the lost-and-found room, followed by the bark of a dog outside. A student slipped on the wet step and twisted an ankle. Noah called for help and put a protective sign near the entrance. The injury was not serious, but it caused a delay. Iris canceled the next recording and moved the waiting people into a dry hall. She had learned that a useful program must not become more important than a person's safety. The room could wait. The student on the steps could not.

暴風雨比氣象預報更早來到。怡思聽見雨點打在失物招領室屋頂上，接著是門外狗的吠聲。一名學生在濕階梯上滑倒，扭傷腳踝。諾亞叫人協助，並在入口放上防護告示。傷勢不重，卻造成延誤。怡思取消下一段錄音，把等候的人帶到乾燥的大廳。她已學會，實用的節目不能比人的安全還重要。房間可以等，台階上的學生不能。

The office had an exact description of the emergency route, but the arrow on the floor had faded. Iris selected a bright sign from the supply closet. Noah clicked the old lamp into place so the stairs were easier to see. A visitor arrived carrying a guava and a spoon, both found in a bag near the gate. “I do not know why somebody keeps a spoon with fruit,” she said. Iris answered, “Maybe lunch was interrupted.” They smiled briefly, then returned to their work. Small jokes helped the room feel calm, but the rain still needed their full attention.

辦公室有緊急路線的精確說明，地上的箭頭卻褪色了。怡思從儲藏櫃挑出明亮標示，諾亞把舊燈喀噠一聲扣上，讓樓梯更容易看清。一位訪客帶來在校門旁袋子裡找到的芭樂和湯匙。「真不知道誰會把湯匙和水果放在一起，」她說。怡思回答：「也許午餐吃到一半就被打斷了。」眾人短暫一笑，又回去工作。小玩笑讓房間保持平靜，但他們仍得專注應付大雨。

One volunteer was upset because her bicycle had been damaged by water. She wrote an angry reply to the service notice and called the old roof an evil joke. Iris listened without treating the words as a personal attack. “You are right to ask for a safe place,” she said. “We cannot promise a dry room today.” Noah showed the volunteer another covered path and offered to help her travel to the repair shop after the storm. The young woman thanked them and took a breath. Iris added the blocked route to the public notice so no other visitor would meet the same problem.

一位志工很難過，因為腳踏車被雨水損壞。她在公告下面寫了生氣的回覆，說舊屋頂根本是邪惡的玩笑。怡思聽完，沒有把那些話當成人身攻擊。「妳有權要求安全的地方，」她說，「但我們今天無法保證房間乾燥。」諾亞指向另一條有遮雨棚的路，提出暴雨過後可陪她去修車店。年輕女子向他們道謝，深吸一口氣。怡思把封閉路線補上公開公告，避免其他來訪者遇到同樣問題。

The next morning the cultural center offered a dry room for the recorded notices. A local novelist who worked there had heard about the project. She asked whether the students wanted to turn the old letters into a novel. Iris said no, not without permission from the writers and their families. The novelist respected the answer and offered another idea: a public exhibit of the rules and the history, using only material people had agreed to share. “A story can be careful and still be interesting,” she said. Iris wrote that sentence down. It sounded like the kind of lesson she needed.

隔天早晨，文化中心願意借出乾燥空間播放預錄公告。在那裡工作的一位在地小說家聽說了計畫，問學生們要不要把舊信件改寫成小說。怡思說，在沒有作者與家屬同意前不行。小說家尊重她的回答，提出另一個點子：只使用當事人同意公開的材料，展示夜間服務規則與歷史。「故事可以小心，也可以有趣，」她說。怡思把這句話記下來。這正是她需要學習的事。

When Noah returned from checking the roof, Iris was writing a description of the new hall. He leaned upon the door frame and asked if she wanted tea. She did, but she could see he was tired. “You do not have to be protective of everybody every minute,” she told him. He laughed softly. “Somebody needs to be protective of you too.” The words stayed in the air. Iris looked at him, uncertain whether to change the subject. Instead she said, “I like working with you. I also like sitting beside you when nothing needs fixing.” Noah's face turned red, and for once he had no quick answer.

諾亞檢查完屋頂回來時，怡思正撰寫新場地說明。他靠著門框，問她要不要茶。她想喝，卻看得出他很累。「你不必每分鐘都照顧所有人，」她告訴他。他輕輕一笑：「也應該有人照顧妳。」那句話停在空氣裡。怡思望著他，一時不知道要不要換話題。最後她說：「我喜歡跟你一起工作，也喜歡沒有事要修的時候坐在你旁邊。」諾亞臉紅了，難得答不出話。

At the end of the day, they selected a safer room for the next week. The roof repairs would begin on Monday, but the workers needed clear access, so the lost-property boxes had to be moved with a signed list. Iris hated the extra work. Noah pointed out that the delay could protect the records from water damage. They carried the last box together. On top lay a note about a youth travel group that had once waited in the old station. One traveler had waited on a swing under the covered path, a detail still marked in the old map. Iris saw that the past and present were touching again. The storm had not ended their project. It had shown what a long-term service would need.

當天結束前，他們替下週選好更安全的房間。屋頂維修將在星期一開始，但工人需要暢通走道，因此失物箱都得按簽名清單搬走。怡思不喜歡多出來的工作。諾亞提醒，延遲反而能保護紀錄不被水損壞。兩人一起搬最後一箱。箱頂有張便條，寫著一個青年旅行團曾在舊站等待。有位旅人曾在遮雨走廊下的鞦韆上等候，舊地圖還標著那個地方。怡思感覺過去與現在又碰在一起。暴風雨沒有讓計畫結束，反而讓他們看清長期服務真正需要什麼。
