藍色信封

The Blue Envelope

十點之後，還有人在等 · 第 1 / 25 章 · 1000–2000 · 英中對照

用互動版查詞、朗讀與收藏

The lost-and-found room was smaller than Iris expected. It stood behind the university café, between a storage door and a window facing the garden. Iris, twenty-three, had come to record a short night program for her sound-design class. Instead, she found a table covered with missing things. A gardener with a gray beard was waiting there, holding a dry leaf inside a blue envelope. “I lost the letter that came with this,” he said. “The letter matters more than the leaf.” A student in pajamas hurried past the door. Iris realized that a campus did not really sleep at night.

失物招領室比怡思想像中還小。它位於大學咖啡館後方，夾在儲藏室門與一扇面向花園的窗之間。二十三歲的怡思原本是為了聲音設計課，來錄一段簡短的夜間節目，卻看見桌上堆滿遺失物。一位留著灰鬍子的園丁拿著藍色信封在等候，裡面放著一片乾葉。「我把原本和它放在一起的信弄丟了，」他說，「那封信比葉子重要。」一位穿著睡衣的學生匆匆經過門口。怡思發現，大學的夜晚其實從不真正入睡。

Noah arrived with a rubber case for an old microphone. He was twenty-five and studied machine repair, but he helped at the desk because the student office was short of workers. His personality seemed plain at first: he spoke slowly and wrote every number twice. “We divide the work,” he said. “You record a message. I check who may hear it.” Iris asked if the rules were really so serious. Noah showed her three lines on the wall: never say a full name on air, never describe a private paper, and never give an object away without proof. “Such rules protect people,” he said. She put away her recorder and listened.

二十五歲的諾亞帶著裝舊麥克風的橡膠盒走進來。他讀機械修護，因學生事務處人手不足，才來櫃檯幫忙。他起初給人的印象十分平實：說話慢，數字總要寫兩次。「我們分工，」他說，「妳錄訊息，我確認誰可以聽。」怡思問規定真的有那麼嚴格嗎。諾亞指著牆上的三行字：廣播不得說出全名、不得描述私人文件、沒有證明就不能交出物品。「這些規則是為了保護人，」他說。怡思收起錄音機，開始認真聽。

The evening became stranger when a woman came to ask about a lost bag. She did not want the bag itself. She wanted the tiny note tied to its handle, a note from someone she had not seen in ten years. Meanwhile, the café owner asked the office for a favor: could they return a box of watermelon slices left in the fridge before the temperature grew too high? Noah agreed to weigh the box and note the time. Another student asked if a chemical stain on a jacket was evidence of a crime. Iris answered carefully, “A stain is a fact. A crime is a claim. They do not mean the same thing.”

傍晚，一名女子前來尋找遺失的袋子。她其實不要袋子，而是想找回綁在提把上的小紙條；那是十年未見的人留給她的。與此同時，咖啡館老闆拜託他們幫個忙：冰箱裡有一盒被忘記的西瓜，能否在溫度升高前送回去？諾亞答應先替盒子秤重並記下時間。另一位學生問，外套上的化學污漬算不算犯罪證據。怡思謹慎回答：「污漬是事實，犯罪是指控，兩者不能畫上等號。」

At nine, Iris tested her first recording. “A blue envelope was found near the north gate. Please come to the office and describe what is inside.” She stopped before mentioning the letter. The words felt too cold. Noah did not tell her to sound more friendly; he asked what feeling she wanted the listener to have. “A chance to come back,” she said. They tried again. This time her voice was warmer, but the message was still clear. Iris began to relate the work to her class. A good sound could carry hope without giving away somebody's life. Noah smiled when she noticed that difference.

九點時，怡思試錄第一則廣播：「北門附近拾獲藍色信封。請失主到辦公室說明內裝物品。」她在說出信件內容前停了下來。這段話聽起來有點冰冷。諾亞沒有叫她裝得更親切，而是問她希望聽眾有什麼感覺。「還有機會找回東西，」她說。他們重新錄了一遍。這次聲音更溫暖，資訊卻仍清楚。怡思開始發現這份工作與課堂的關聯：好聲音能帶來希望，又不洩露別人的生活。諾亞看見她理解了這一點，露出微笑。

Before closing, an office manager called about a small tax form left in the room. The form belonged in a sealed drawer, not in an open box. Iris carried it there herself. She had expected a cheerful project with funny stories; now she understood that even an ordinary notice could hurt somebody if handled badly. She wrote “A voice can open a door” on her notebook. Noah checked the lock, then returned the gardener's envelope to the safe shelf. Its blue color was easy to see among brown bags. He asked, “Can you come tomorrow?” Iris answered, “Only if we can learn the stories without stealing them.”

關門前，一名辦公室主管打電話來問留在房內的小型稅務表單。那份表單應放進上鎖抽屜，而不是開放式箱子。怡思親自把它收妥。她原以為這會是充滿趣事的輕鬆課堂專案，如今才明白，就連普通公告，處理不好也可能傷人。她在筆記本寫下：「一個聲音可以打開一扇門。」諾亞確認門鎖，把園丁的藍色信封放回安全的架子。它在一排咖啡色袋子之間格外醒目。他問：「明天還來嗎？」怡思回答：「只要我們能了解故事，而不是偷走故事。」

Walking home, Iris reread the rules. She wondered why the gardener's letter had been lost while its envelope remained, and why Noah looked away whenever anyone mentioned the old radio room. These were questions, not proof of a secret. She decided not to make them into a dramatic report. Tomorrow she would ask ordinary questions first, and let people choose how much to tell. Her phone lit up with a message from Noah: “I forgot the key to the cabinet. I did remember your recording.” Iris laughed aloud under the streetlight. For the first time that day, she looked forward to another shift.

走回家的路上，怡思重新讀了那三條規定。她不懂為何園丁留下信封卻遺失信件，也不明白為何每當有人提起舊廣播室，諾亞就把視線移開。這些只是疑問，不是祕密的證據。她決定不把它們誇大成戲劇化的報導。明天，她會先問普通問題，再讓每個人自行決定願意說多少。手機亮起，是諾亞傳來的訊息：「我忘了拿櫃子的鑰匙，倒是記得妳的錄音。」怡思站在路燈下笑出聲來。這是她當天第一次期待下一次值班。
