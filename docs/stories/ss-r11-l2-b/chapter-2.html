一個盒子上的兩張標籤

Two Labels on One Box

十點之後，還有人在等 · 第 2 / 25 章 · 1000–2000 · 英中對照

用互動版查詞、朗讀與收藏

The next night, Iris found two names on the same box. The current label showed a student number; beneath it was an older card with another number. She wanted to display both numbers and ask the campus to hunt for the owner, but Noah covered the card with his hand. “Membership records are not a public game,” he said. The office had suffered a loss of trust after someone posted a student's address the year before. Iris saw why her idea was selfish. She wrote a new message asking the owner to describe the box without naming anyone. She would respond to questions in private, not make a show of her own self. Her first choice had been quick, not wise.

隔天夜裡，怡思發現同一個盒子上有兩個名字。目前的標籤寫著一個學號，底下則是另一張更舊的卡片，數字不同。她本想展示兩個號碼，請全校一起尋找失主，諾亞卻伸手蓋住了卡片。「會員資料不是公開遊戲，」他說。前年有人在網路公開學生住址，失物招領室因此失去部分信任。怡思明白自己的主意有點自私。她改寫公告，請失主描述盒子內容，而不透露姓名。她會私下回應問題，不拿自己的表現當作主角。她第一個念頭雖然快，卻不夠周到。

Their club adviser came with a plan for a three-night trial. Students could listen to short recorded notices through a small speaker by the door. The office would count successful returns, but a return would count only after the owner showed proof. A chess player offered to guide guests through the line, and an art student made the display beautiful with blue paper shapes. “Super!” Iris said, then noticed Noah's worried face. He feared that a bright display might promise more than their small team could do. The adviser agreed to write the trial limits in clear words above the speaker. The beauty of the plan, Iris thought, was that people could understand it before they joined.

社團指導老師帶來三晚試辦計畫。學生可以透過門邊的小喇叭聽簡短的預錄公告。辦公室會計算成功領回的案件，但必須等失主提出證明才算數。一名棋社學生自願引導排隊者，美術系學生則用藍色紙片把展示牆布置得很漂亮。「太棒了！」怡思說，隨即看見諾亞擔憂的表情。他怕亮眼的展示讓人期待超過小團隊的能力。指導老師同意把試辦限制清楚寫在喇叭上方。怡思心想，這套計畫的美好之處，在於人們參加之前就能先理解它。

At dinner, Iris brought a pear and a list of questions. Noah told her that his grandmother had once run the lost-property desk. She kept an old chain across the back room because the space held private documents. “That chain looked rude,” he remembered. “She said it was there to protect people, not shut them out.” Iris asked why his grandmother had left. Noah answered, “She retired. There is no great secret in every old thing.” He was not angry, but he did not offer more. Iris changed the subject and asked him to try the pear. He accepted half and gave the other half back.

吃晚餐時，怡思帶了一顆梨子和一張問題清單。諾亞說，祖母以前負責失物招領櫃檯。她在後房拉了一條舊鏈子，因為裡面存放私人文件。「那條鏈子看起來很不近人情，」他回憶，「她說不是要把人擋在外面，而是保護裡面的人。」怡思問祖母為何離開。諾亞說：「她退休了。不是每件舊東西都藏著大祕密。」他並沒有生氣，但也不願多說。怡思換了話題，請他吃梨子。他拿走一半，又把另一半留給她。

The first return came from a student whose bag contained a guide to a garden club. She could name a small mark on the handle, a mark not mentioned in the public message. Noah checked her answer against the sealed record and returned the bag. The woman smiled with such relief that Iris forgot to be nervous. Later a speaker made a sharp noise, and three students jumped. Iris canceled the next recording while Noah tested the cable. Their best result that night was not the number of bags returned. It was the fact that they stopped a poor system before it hurt anyone.

第一位來領物的學生帶走了一個裝著園藝社指南的袋子。她能說出提把上一個小記號，而那個記號從未出現在公開公告中。諾亞對照封存紀錄，確認後才交還。女生如釋重負地笑了，讓怡思忘了緊張。之後喇叭突然發出尖銳聲，嚇了三位學生一跳。怡思取消下一則錄音，讓諾亞檢查線材。那晚最好的成果不是找回多少袋子，而是他們在系統傷到任何人之前就先停下來修正。

Near closing time, a guest asked if the box with two labels belonged to her. Iris gave her the same fair test used for everyone else. The answer was wrong, and the guest looked embarrassed. “I thought it might be my sister's,” she said. Noah did not call her a liar. He invited her to leave a message about the correct box, if she wished. She thanked him. Iris saw that a person could be mistaken without being dishonest. She added one line to their plan: a failed claim should not become a public story. Even the worst moment could teach them a better rule.

快打烊時，一位來客詢問那個貼著雙標籤的盒子是否屬於她。怡思照同樣公平的方式核對資訊。答案不對，對方顯得很窘迫。「我以為可能是姊姊的，」她說。諾亞沒有說她是騙子，而是邀請她如果願意，可以留下真正遺失盒子的描述。她向他道謝。怡思明白，人可能搞錯，卻不一定不誠實。她在計畫上加上一條：未通過的認領不應變成公開故事。即使最糟的時刻，也能教會他們更好的規則。

When they finished counting, the adviser praised the team's growth. Four people had visited. Only one object had been returned, yet all four had heard what to do next. “Do you think that is enough?” Iris asked. Noah turned the small box in his hands and said, “We cannot measure trust in one night. We can give it room to grow.” She glanced at the second label again. The dates were six months apart, but the handwriting was the same. She did not tell him it was proof of fraud. She simply wrote both dates in her notebook and decided to check the record tomorrow.

統計結束後，指導老師稱讚團隊的進步。今晚有四人到訪，雖然只領回一件物品，但四人都知道接下來該怎麼做。「這樣就夠了嗎？」怡思問。諾亞捧著小盒子說：「信任不能用一晚量出來，但我們可以給它成長的空間。」她又看了一眼第二張標籤。兩個日期相隔半年，筆跡卻相同。她沒有把它說成舞弊證據，只把日期記進筆記本，決定明天去查紀錄。
