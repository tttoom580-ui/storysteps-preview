The Page That Fell Out

十點之後，還有人在等 · 第 9 / 25 章 · 1000–2000 · 英中對照 · 校訂中

用互動版查詞、朗讀與收藏

On Monday, Iris wrote an article for the student paper about the lost-and-found trial. She chose a daily record rather than a dramatic headline. Each chapter of the report described one change: better forms, private checks, and a safer path to the desk. Her partner on the paper, Min, brought a box of crayons to draw a map. A puppy belonging to the shopkeeper next door kept trying to steal a blue crayon. Iris laughed and moved the box higher. The small scene reminded her that useful work could still have room for ordinary joy.

星期一，怡思替學生報寫了一篇關於失物招領試辦的文章。她選擇按日紀錄，而非誇張標題。報告的每個章節描述一項改進：更好的表單、私下核對、以及通往櫃檯更安全的路。報社搭檔敏帶來一盒蠟筆畫地圖。隔壁店主養的小狗不斷想偷走一支藍色蠟筆。怡思笑著把盒子放高。這個小插曲提醒她，認真的工作也可以容納普通的快樂。

Min wanted to include a historical photograph of the guard desk. They found one in a public section of the university archive, but the page behind it had been pasted over with newer paper. A small nail held the frame together. Noah removed the frame without touching the old writing. “We should ask a trained worker before doing more,” he said. Iris felt a little fear that the missing page might be gone for good. Instead of pulling at it, she took the photo's reference number to the archive office. The clerk promised to search the catalog.

敏想加入舊警衛櫃檯的歷史照片。他們在大學檔案室的公開區找到一張，但照片背後的一頁被新紙覆蓋，框架還用小釘子固定。諾亞卸下外框，沒有碰觸舊字跡。「下一步應該先請受過訓練的人看，」他說。怡思有點害怕缺失的那頁再也找不回來。她沒有硬撕，而是把照片編號拿去檔案室。職員答應查目錄。

The next evening a legal studies student joined them to read the storage rules. She warned them not to treat an old note as a legal contract without checking its source. Iris showed her the station's current system and asked what a student volunteer could promise. “You can promise to follow the written procedure,” the student replied. “You cannot promise to find every lost thing.” Noah added that people must know whom to contact if an item was returned by mistake. They wrote the answer into the new guide and made that section shine with a simple yellow heading.

隔晚，一位法律系學生加入討論保管規則。她提醒，不可在沒確認來源前把舊便條當成法律契約。怡思展示目前的制度，詢問學生志工能承諾什麼。「你可以承諾遵守書面流程，」對方回答，「但不能保證每件遺失物都找得回來。」諾亞補充，若物品歸還錯誤，大家也必須知道要找誰。兩人把答案寫進新指南，為那一節加上醒目的黃色標題。

A visitor brought a painting wrapped in brown paper. The owner had written her name on the back, but Iris refused to put the name in the article. The clerk helped them paste a temporary number on the wrapping instead. The visitor asked if the work would succeed. “We are learning,” Iris said. “That is different from saying nothing can go wrong.” She remembered the student who had been embarrassed by a wrong claim. Unless a person agreed to speak, even a successful return would remain private. Their rule had become a habit rather than a sentence on the wall.

一位訪客帶來用咖啡色紙包好的畫作。畫家把名字寫在背面，但怡思拒絕把姓名登進報紙。職員協助把臨時編號貼在外包紙上。訪客問這套做法能成功嗎。「我們正在學，」怡思說，「這不等於保證永遠不出錯。」她想起曾因認領失敗而尷尬的學生。除非當事人願意公開，否則就連成功歸還也要保密。他們的規則已經不只是牆上的一句話，而開始成為習慣。

Later the archive clerk called. The photograph had been moved from a box of club awards into a file about the night service. An addition to the record explained why: years ago, a city bus driver had donated a small fund to keep the desk open after dark. The fund was not enough to pay a permanent worker, but it had paid for a light and emergency phone. Iris felt worse about the idea of selling the old desk as scrap. Noah asked her not to jump to a new conclusion. They would first see whether the fund still existed and who controlled it.

稍晚，檔案室職員來電。原來照片曾從社團獎項箱移到夜間服務檔案，新增的紀錄也解釋原因：多年前，一位市區公車司機捐過一筆小基金，讓櫃檯能在天黑後開放。那筆錢不足以支付長期員工薪資，卻曾負擔一盞燈和緊急電話。怡思想到有人提議把舊櫃檯當廢料賣掉，心裡更難受。諾亞請她別急著下結論。他們應該先確認基金是否仍在，以及由誰管理。

As they closed the paper office, Min told Iris that the story did not need a villain. It needed good questions and a way for readers to check the facts. Iris looked at the blue envelope in her bag, the one she had carried from the garden office for a private review. Its paper was thin, but its corner still held a tiny mark shaped like a bus. She realized she had seen that same mark in the archive photograph. She did not call it proof. She sent Noah a sketch of the mark and asked, “Do you have time to compare these tomorrow?” He replied, “Yes. Bring your patience.”

報社收工時，敏告訴怡思，這個故事不需要一名壞人，而需要好問題和讓讀者查證的方法。怡思望著袋中的藍色信封——那是園藝櫃檯交她私下協助檢視的。紙已很薄，角落卻仍有個像公車的小記號。她想起檔案照片上也見過同樣符號。她沒有把它說成證據，只把圖案畫給諾亞，問：「明天有空一起對照嗎？」他回覆：「有，記得帶上耐心。」
