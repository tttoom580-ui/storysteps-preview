下雨天也要有規則

A Rule for Rainy Days

十點之後，還有人在等 · 第 4 / 25 章 · 1000–2000 · 英中對照

用互動版查詞、朗讀與收藏

The student affairs department called a public meeting on Thursday. Anyone could attend, but Iris noticed that most people in the room were workers from the eastern side of campus, where the buses stopped early. The office director spoke in a firm voice. “A minor problem may become a major one if we promise things we cannot deliver,” she said. A model of the new online claim system stood on the table. Its purpose was speed. Iris asked whether people without phones could still report lost property. The director paused. Noah wrote down the question instead of answering for her.

星期四，學生事務處召開公開會議。任何人都可以參加，但怡思注意到，出席者大多是在校園東側工作的職員，那邊的公車很早就停駛。處長語氣堅定地說：「小問題若作出無法實現的承諾，也會變成大問題。」桌上放著新線上認領系統的模型，主要目的是加快速度。怡思問，沒有手機的人是否仍能通報失物。處長停了一下。諾亞把問題記下來，沒有替她作答。

One student brought a bird cage with an open wing-shaped door. He wanted to know whether it counted as dangerous property because a sick bird had once lived inside. Another student from the gymnasium asked about a bag with a comb, an umbrella, and a bottle of medicine. “Do not announce a person's drug treatment,” Noah reminded everyone. “We can describe the bag without telling private health facts.” The director asked them to write a standard rule, not decide every case from fear. Iris began to see that useful privacy was a practice, not simply a locked room.

一名學生帶來鳥籠，籠門敞開，形狀像翅膀。他想知道鳥籠算不算危險物品，因為裡面曾養過生病的鳥。另一位從體育館來的同學，詢問裝著梳子、雨傘和藥瓶的袋子。「別把別人的用藥公開播出，」諾亞提醒大家，「我們可以描述袋子，不談私人健康資料。」處長要求大家寫出標準規則，而不是每件事都憑恐懼決定。怡思漸漸明白，真正實用的隱私保障是一套做法，不只是鎖上一扇門。

At lunch, the library cook invited volunteers to fry eggs in a large pan. She joked that the station was starting to feel like a wolf: hungry for every lost story. Iris laughed, then wondered whether the joke was fair. Some people came only to recover a key, not to become the subject of a recording. She drew a line on her planning sheet. One side was labeled “return the object”; the other said “tell a story, only by choice.” Noah looked over her shoulder and nodded. “That difference will keep us honest,” he said.

午餐時，圖書館的廚師請志工用大平底鍋煎蛋。她開玩笑說，廣播站像一匹狼，餓得想吞下每個失物故事。怡思笑了，隨即想這個玩笑是否公平。有些人只是想找回鑰匙，不願成為錄音題材。她在企畫表中畫了一條線，一邊寫「歸還物品」，另一邊寫「只有自願時才說故事」。諾亞從她肩後望了一眼，點點頭說：「這個區別能讓我們保持誠實。」

The room grew noisy when a student proposed a motion to close the desk at night and move every request online. A wheelchair user objected. The website had not been tested with her screen reader, and the path to the daytime desk was blocked by construction. “I do not need encouragement,” she said. “I need a route that works.” Iris asked for ten minutes to test the site with her. They found that the final button had no text label. The online system might be useful, but it was not ready to replace a person at the desk.

一名學生提議停止夜間櫃檯服務，讓所有申請改走網路，會場頓時吵了起來。一位使用輪椅的同學反對。新網站尚未和她的螢幕閱讀器測試，白天櫃檯的通道又被工程圍住。「我不需要鼓勵的話，」她說，「我需要真的能走的路。」怡思請求十分鐘和她一起測試網站。他們發現最後一個按鈕沒有文字標籤。線上系統可能有用，卻還沒準備好取代櫃檯真人。

After the meeting, rain started to beat on the window. Noah opened an umbrella and walked Iris across the square. She asked whether he thought the director was cruel. “No,” he said. “She has a budget to protect. We have people to protect. We must show how those needs can meet.” Iris liked that he could disagree without making an enemy. Near the hall, she stopped under the umbrella and admitted she had nearly spoken over the student with the screen reader. Noah said, “You stopped. You asked her. That matters.” The rain made his words sound unusually close.

會後，雨開始敲打窗戶。諾亞撐起雨傘，陪怡思穿過廣場。她問他是否覺得處長很殘酷。「不，」他說，「她要守住預算，我們要守住人。得證明這兩種需求可以相容。」怡思喜歡他能不同意別人，卻不把對方當敵人。走到大廳旁，她停在傘下，承認自己剛才差點替使用螢幕閱讀器的同學發言。諾亞說：「但妳停下來了，也問她本人。這很重要。」雨聲讓他的話顯得格外親近。

That night they rewrote the proposal. The new standard would include a paper request form, an accessible online form, and a private desk for sensitive items. A volunteer from another region could help test the instructions. No one would need to explain a medical condition to claim an umbrella. They also planned an extra rule for days when rain damaged the public speaker. A paper notice would stay by the dry entrance, with a bell that volunteers could ring without electricity. Iris sent the draft to the director before midnight. She did not expect an easy yes. Still, she had proof that the problem was real, and she knew whom to ask about a solution.

當晚，他們重寫提案。新標準將包含紙本申請表、無障礙線上表單，以及處理敏感物品的私人櫃檯。來自其他校區的志工可以協助測試說明；任何人領回雨傘時都不必交代醫療情況。他們也為下雨損壞公共喇叭的日子增列規定。乾燥入口旁會留下紙本公告，也備好不用電就能由志工敲響的小鈴。午夜前，怡思把草案送交處長。她不指望對方輕易答應，但手上已經有問題確實存在的證據，也知道該找誰討論解法。
