葬禮後的第一封信

The First Letter After the Funeral

死去的偵探每天寄來一封信 · 第 1 / 25 章 · 2000–3000 · 英中對照 · 校訂中

用互動版查詞、朗讀與收藏

A day after Detective Vale’s funeral, his assistant Eve receives a letter predicting a broken clock at the city station. By sunrise, the rumor had traveled across Bridgeport faster than any honest explanation. Eve found Ian beside the letter archive, making a list of the things they had actually seen. There were fewer items on that list than on the rumor sheet. The difference mattered. A frightened person could mistake a guess for a promise, and a promise could send somebody toward a danger they had never agreed to face. The inspector called the risk acceptable, but Eve asked to see the safety conditions. Ian promised to advise the group only after reading the original instructions. A nervous worker rested a hand beneath his chin and studied the open notebook.

偵探維爾的葬禮隔天，助手伊芙收到信，預言車站的時鐘將被打壞。 日出時，流言已在橋港傳得比任何誠實解釋都快。伊芙看見伊恩守在信件檔案旁，列出兩人真正親眼見到的事。那張清單比流言單上的項目少得多，而差別至關重要。害怕的人可能把猜測誤認成承諾，承諾又可能把某人帶進他從未同意面對的危險。 督察說風險可以接受，但伊芙要求查看安全條件。伊恩答應讀過原始指示後才給團隊建議。一名緊張的工人把手托在下巴下，仔細看著打開的筆記。

The problem grew more complicated when Mayor Grey asked for a quick decision. Eve understood the attraction of a simple answer, but a forged future had already made simple answers expensive for other people. Ian traced a line in the letter archive and showed exactly where the available information ended. Neither of them wanted to make a public promise across that blank space. They agreed to measure one small fact before taking the next step. A traveler unpacked a coconut from the supply basket and shared it with the tired guards. Eve tried to connect the earlier warning with the mark beside the doorway. Ian called the order cruel because it would leave the weakest people behind.

格雷市長要求迅速決定後，問題變得更複雜。伊芙理解簡單答案的吸引力，但被偽造的未來早已讓別人替這些簡單答案付出高昂代價。伊恩沿著信件檔案畫出一道線，指出現有資訊到哪裡為止。兩人都不願跨過空白處作公開承諾，因此同意先測量一件小事再前進。 一名旅人從補給籃拿出椰子，分給疲倦的守衛。伊芙試著把先前的警告和門邊的記號連起來。伊恩認為這命令殘酷，因為它會把最弱勢的人留在原地。

The first disagreement was small, but it revealed the difference between their habits. Eve tended to notice patterns; Ian noticed who was missing from the room. Together they looked at the letter archive and named one question each. There was no need for either person to win the argument. A good plan would leave room for both questions and for the people whose answers might be different from theirs. A postal bag struck the office door, and Eve almost jumped. Each letter now felt like a clock she could not stop, but she refused to call a warning a sentence of fate. The medic wrote the correct dose on a card instead of trusting anyone to remember it. A slow drip of water came through the ceiling, marking the path of a hidden pipe. Eve felt a brief envy of people who seemed sure of their answers, then returned to work.

第一次分歧不大，卻顯露兩人的習慣差異。伊芙擅長注意規律，伊恩則察覺誰沒有出現在房裡。他們一起查看信件檔案，各自提出一個問題。爭論不必分出輸贏；好計畫應容納兩個問題，也容納答案可能不同的其他人。郵袋撞上事務所大門，伊芙幾乎跳起來。每封信都像她無法停止的時鐘，但她拒絕把警告當成命運判決。 醫護人員把正確劑量寫在卡片上，不要求大家憑記憶用藥。緩慢滴落的水從天花板滲出，標示出隱藏水管的位置。伊芙一度羨慕那些看似確定答案的人，隨後繼續工作。

No one cheered when their first attempt failed. Eve marked the failure in the letter archive, and Ian asked whether the result could be repeated safely. They chose a smaller version of the task, with a clear signal to stop if anything went wrong. The second attempt produced a different sound and a different answer. They still needed another witness, but now they knew what question that witness should be asked. Ian examined the postmark twice, because a small date could expose a large deception. They agreed to explore only the passage that the safety team had already inspected. The weather turned extreme by noon, and the team moved its supplies under cover. Eve refused to gamble with the safety of someone who had not agreed to the risk.

第一次嘗試失敗時，沒有人歡呼。伊芙把失敗記在信件檔案裡，伊恩詢問是否能安全重做。他們選擇規模更小的測試，並約定出錯時立即停止的訊號。第二次嘗試出現不同的聲音與答案。雖然還需要另一名證人，但他們終於知道該問對方什麼。伊恩檢查兩次郵戳，因為小小日期可能揭露巨大的謊言。 他們同意只探索安全小組檢查過的通道。到了中午天候變得極端惡劣，團隊把物資移到遮蔽處。伊芙拒絕拿未同意冒險者的安全來賭。

Ian read the last line aloud. Eve checked the date and kept a copy for the morning team. One quick glance at the old date told Ian that the sheet was not the latest copy. Fresh hay lay in a corner of the stable beside a broken wooden gate.

伊恩朗讀最後一行。伊芙核對日期，為早班小組留下副本。 伊恩瞥一眼舊日期，就知道這張紙並非最新版本。馬廄一角堆著新鮮乾草，旁邊是一扇壞掉的木門。

A new message arrived before the meeting could end. It mentioned a forged future, then claimed that only Mayor Grey had the authority to decide what happened next. Eve read it twice. The words sounded certain, but there was no date beside the claim and no name of the person who had checked it. Ian suggested saving a copy and asking for the original. The question was not whether to trust a powerful person. It was what anyone could verify. The envelope smelled of damp paper and printer’s ink. Eve remembered the detective smiling at bad clues; he had said that a wrong answer was useful if you knew why it was wrong. Someone had left clean laundry beside the emergency supplies, ready for people who might stay overnight. The apology sounded sincere, but Eve still expected the promised repair to be completed.

會議尚未結束，一則新訊息就送到了。訊息提及被偽造的未來，並宣稱只有格雷市長有權決定下一步。伊芙讀了兩遍。文字很肯定，卻沒有日期，也沒有查核者姓名。伊恩建議保留副本並索取原件。問題不是該不該相信有權勢的人，而是任何人能核實什麼。信封帶著濕紙與油墨的氣味。伊芙想起偵探曾對錯誤線索微笑；他說只要知道錯在哪裡，錯誤答案也有價值。 有人把乾淨衣物放在緊急物資旁，供可能需要過夜的人使用。道歉聽來誠懇，但伊芙仍期待承諾的修理真正完成。

Ian read the last line aloud. Eve checked the date and kept a copy for the morning team. Ian sat on a low stool to speak at eye level with a frightened child. A sign banned tobacco near the storage room because a spark could cause a fire.

伊恩朗讀最後一行。伊芙核對日期，為早班小組留下副本。 伊恩坐在矮凳上，與受驚的孩子平視交談。儲藏室附近的標誌禁止使用菸草，因為火星可能造成火災。

Eve left the letter archive open so Ian could add the next finding. Neither could see the whole road yet. A pair of twin lamps stood at the gate, but only one was still working. A guard gave a sharp whistle, the agreed signal for everybody to stop moving.

伊芙讓信件檔案保持打開，好讓伊恩補上新的發現。兩人都還看不見整段路。 大門有一對成雙的燈，卻只剩一盞還能發亮。守衛吹出尖銳口哨，那是大家約定停止移動的訊號。
