The Missing Technician

A personnel file showed that a technician named Chen Mo had vanished during a lighting trial. The morning offered no dramatic warning. Someone was carrying cups, someone else was checking a door, and the usual machinery made its familiar complaints. Only one detail refused to fit the story everyone had prepared to believe; once noticed, it made the whole place seem different. A reflection shivered in the empty window, then vanished as a bus passed. One instruction told the team to accomplish the limited repair; nobody could explain why it was urgent. Fang Nian proposed they amuse the tired children; Zhou Lu wanted the risk written down first. They found a reference to approval near the damaged camera record, but its date was unreadable.

人事檔案顯示技師陳墨在照明測試中失蹤。早晨沒有戲劇性的警告。有人端著杯子，有人檢查門，機械照常發出熟悉的雜音。只有一個細節無法塞進大家原本準備相信的故事；一旦看見它，整個地方都變得不同。空窗上的反光微微顫動，公車經過時便消失。一項指令要求團隊「成就；完成」，卻沒有人能解釋為何急迫。方念提議「逗樂；令人發笑」，周律希望先把風險寫清楚。他們在受損攝影紀錄旁找到提及「核准」的紀錄，日期卻看不清。

His last report warned that the lamps could imprint patterns of human motion onto reflective surfaces. The records did not tell a single story. One column described an intention; another showed what happened after real people entered the space. The difference was inconvenient, especially for those who had already spent money defending the original plan. Inconvenience was not a reason to erase it. A faint line of light separated the real doorway from its convincing copy. The plan called for them to bounce off the sealed door, and Zhou Lu asked who would check the result. The warning sounded continual to some listeners, although the actual measurements remained uncertain. One instruction told the team to disappoint the volunteers; nobody could explain why it was urgent. A witness called the result earnest; Fang Nian wrote the adjective beside the witness’s name.

他最後的報告警告燈具會在反光表面留下人類動作的圖樣。紀錄沒有說出單一故事。一欄描述意圖，另一欄顯示真實的人進入空間後發生了什麼。差異令人不便，尤其是那些已花錢維護原計畫的人。不便卻不是刪掉差異的理由。微弱光線把真門和逼真的複製影像分開。計畫要求他們「彈跳」，周律追問由誰查核結果。對部分聽眾而言，警告聽起來「持續的」，實際測量卻仍有疑點。一項指令要求團隊「失望」，卻沒有人能解釋為何急迫。見證人稱結果「認真的」，方念把形容詞記在見證人姓名旁。

Zhou Lu asked for the original test logs and refused to label Chen Mo a criminal without proof. The most powerful person present could have ended the argument with an order. That might even have made the next hour more efficient. It would not have made the order wise. Instead, questions were allowed to remain questions long enough for another person to object. The spare lamp stood ready, ordinary and more comforting than any spectacular projection. They found a reference to embassy near the damaged camera record, but its date was unreadable. The emphasis appeared in the older camera record, and Zhou Lu asked why the newer version omitted it. Nobody disputed the librarian; what troubled Zhou Lu was the silence around its origin. The team considered the margin a separate detail, not proof that the uncontrolled projection was safe.

周律要求原始測試紀錄，不在證據不足時指控陳墨犯罪。在場最有權力的人本可一聲令下結束爭論，甚至讓下一小時更有效率，但命令不會因此變明智。他們讓問題繼續存在，給另一個人提出異議的時間。備用燈準備就緒，平凡卻比任何壯觀投影更令人安心。他們在受損攝影紀錄旁找到提及「大使館」的紀錄，日期卻看不清。舊攝影紀錄出現了「重點」，周律追問新版為何漏掉。沒人質疑「圖書管理員」，讓周律困擾的是它的來歷沒有人提。團隊把「頁邊」視為獨立細節，不當成失控的投影安全的證據。

The logs ended at midnight with an instruction to keep every light burning. The evidence produced a consequence nobody could delegate away. Somebody would have to change a timetable, surrender a privilege, or admit that a previous decision had hurt people. Numbers were useful only when they could still lead to accountable actions. The city knew how to record people more easily than it knew how to protect them. A witness mentioned murderer during the review, so Fang Nian preserved the exact wording. They found a reference to nonsense near the damaged camera record, but its date was unreadable. The orbit appeared in the older camera record, and Zhou Lu asked why the newer version omitted it.

紀錄在午夜以「保持所有燈光亮著」的命令結束。證據造成無法推給別人的後果。有人必須改時程、交還特權，或承認先前決定傷害了人。數字只有能引向負責的行動，才真的有用。城市擅長記錄人，卻不如保護人那麼熟練。見證人在覆核時提到「殺人犯」，方念保留了原話。他們在受損攝影紀錄旁找到提及「荒謬的事」的紀錄，日期卻看不清。舊攝影紀錄出現了「運行軌道」，周律追問新版為何漏掉。

It would have been tempting to call the day a success and leave. But one person could not yet go home, and another still carried a cost nobody had agreed to repay. The group sat down again, this time without pretending that good intentions guaranteed a good outcome. Zhou Lu glanced toward Fang Nian and remembered the principle they had been arguing about: an image cannot replace responsibility. It sounded simple until it demanded another hour of work. The streetlamp threw a long shadow across the wall before Zhou Lu even moved. The warning sounded passive to some listeners, although the actual measurements remained uncertain. The team considered the possession a separate detail, not proof that the uncontrolled projection was safe. A witness mentioned researcher during the review, so Fang Nian preserved the exact wording.

把今天稱為成功就離開，很誘人。但還有人不能回家，也有人承擔著無人答應補償的代價。大家再次坐下，這次不假裝好意必然帶來好結果。周律望向方念，想起兩人不斷爭論的原則：影像不能取代責任。話雖簡單，真正實踐又需要多一小時工作。周律還沒動，路燈已在牆上投出長影。對部分聽眾而言，警告聽起來「被動的」，實際測量卻仍有疑點。團隊把「擁有權」視為獨立細節，不當成失控的投影安全的證據。見證人在覆核時提到「研究員」，方念保留了原話。

There would be easier stories to tell about this day. In those stories a brave person would find one clue, name one enemy and fix everything at once. The truth they carried was more difficult: a choice could be necessary and still leave work for the morning. Zhou Lu knew the next day would not begin from a clean slate. The camera record would preserve one more question, and Fang Nian would be there to challenge the first easy answer. A security screen could accuse a face without understanding whose hands controlled it. They found a reference to rhyme near the damaged camera record, but its date was unreadable. The translator appeared in the older camera record, and Zhou Lu asked why the newer version omitted it. The warning sounded urgent to some listeners, although the actual measurements remained uncertain.

關於今天，可以講得更簡單：勇敢的人找到一個線索，指出一個敵人，一切立刻解決。他們帶走的真相更難：一個選擇可以是必要的，也仍會留下明早要處理的工作。周律知道明天不會從零開始。攝影紀錄將保存又一個問題，方念也會在場質疑第一個容易的答案。保全螢幕能指控一張臉，卻不了解誰的手控制畫面。他們在受損攝影紀錄旁找到提及「童謠」的紀錄，日期卻看不清。舊攝影紀錄出現了「翻譯員」，周律追問新版為何漏掉。對部分聽眾而言，警告聽起來「緊急的」，實際測量卻仍有疑點。
