Lighting technician Zhou Lu woke to footage of his own silhouette stealing keys from a subway control room. It began with something that could be held in one hand, a small object too ordinary to attract attention. Yet the longer they looked, the less ordinary it became. The real problem was not its appearance but the decision someone had already made on its behalf. The city knew how to record people more easily than it knew how to protect them. A witness mentioned acid during the review, so Fang Nian preserved the exact wording. They found a reference to assembly near the damaged camera record, but its date was unreadable. The broom appeared in the older camera record, and Zhou Lu asked why the newer version omitted it.

燈光技師周律醒來，看見自己的黑色剪影在地鐵控制室偷鑰匙的監視畫面。事情從一個單手能拿起的小物件開始，普通得幾乎沒人會注意。可看得越久，它就越不尋常。真正的問題不在外表，而是有人早已代替它做出了決定。城市擅長記錄人，卻不如保護人那麼熟練。見證人在覆核時提到「胃酸」，方念保留了原話。他們在受損攝影紀錄旁找到提及「集會」的紀錄，日期卻看不清。舊攝影紀錄出現了「掃帚」，周律追問新版為何漏掉。

At the recorded time he had been asleep under a lamp that never went out. An attractive theory formed almost immediately. It explained the loudest clue and gave everyone someone to blame. Yet one quiet observation remained outside it. Rather than hide that observation, the group put it where every later explanation would have to face it. The streetlamp threw a long shadow across the wall before Zhou Lu even moved. Before they could construct a safe model, the group needed a witness who was free to object. One instruction told the team to cooperate with independent witnesses; nobody could explain why it was urgent. A witness mentioned copper during the review, so Fang Nian preserved the exact wording. They found a reference to counter near the damaged camera record, but its date was unreadable.

錄影時間他明明睡在一盞整夜沒熄的燈下。一個吸引人的理論幾乎立刻成形。它解釋了最顯眼的線索，也給大家一個可以責怪的人。但一項安靜的觀察留在理論之外。團隊沒有藏起它，而是把它放在所有後續解釋都必須面對的位置。周律還沒動，路燈已在牆上投出長影。在能夠「建構」之前，團隊需要一位能自由提出異議的見證人。一項指令要求團隊「合作」，卻沒有人能解釋為何急迫。見證人在覆核時提到「銅製的」，方念保留了原話。他們在受損攝影紀錄旁找到提及「櫃臺」的紀錄，日期卻看不清。

He saved the original footage and called his friend, forensic editor Fang Nian, before touching the camera system. A refusal sounded small compared with the danger outside. Even so, it changed what the group understood about its limits. No one should have to give up a private history, a safe route, or an honest answer merely to prove they were cooperative. A security screen could accuse a face without understanding whose hands controlled it. The coward appeared in the older camera record, and Zhou Lu asked why the newer version omitted it. Before they could entertain the waiting visitors, the group needed a witness who was free to object. Zhou Lu disliked the word facial in the summary because it hid a decision someone had made. Fang Nian proposed they interfere with the signal; Zhou Lu wanted the risk written down first.

他保存原始影像，先找鑑識剪輯師方念，不去動攝影系統。面對外面的危險，一次拒絕聽來很微小，卻改變了團隊對界線的理解。沒有人必須放棄私人經歷、安全路線或誠實答案，才能證明自己願意合作。保全螢幕能指控一張臉，卻不了解誰的手控制畫面。舊攝影紀錄出現了「膽小鬼」，周律追問新版為何漏掉。在能夠「娛樂」之前，團隊需要一位能自由提出異議的見證人。周律不喜歡摘要中的「臉部的」，因為它掩藏了某個人的決定。方念提議「干擾」，周律希望先把風險寫清楚。

The silhouette moved without his body and disappeared when the station lights changed. A surprising number of people had been waiting for that small result. Some had hoped to hear reassuring words; others needed permission to act. The result offered neither comfort nor permission by itself. It gave them a narrower, more honest problem to solve. Zhou Lu left the laboratory lights unchanged so the measurements could be repeated. They found a reference to ministry near the damaged camera record, but its date was unreadable. The opera appeared in the older camera record, and Zhou Lu asked why the newer version omitted it. The warning sounded persuasive to some listeners, although the actual measurements remained uncertain.

剪影沒有他的身體也會移動，車站換燈時就消失了。有令人意外的多人一直等著這個小結果。有人想聽安慰，有人需要行動許可。但結果本身既不提供安慰也不提供許可，只讓問題縮小、也更誠實。周律保持實驗室燈光原狀，讓測量可重做。他們在受損攝影紀錄旁找到提及「部會」的紀錄，日期卻看不清。舊攝影紀錄出現了「歌劇院」，周律追問新版為何漏掉。對部分聽眾而言，警告聽起來「有說服力的」，實際測量卻仍有疑點。

A small act of care interrupted the discussion. It solved no mystery and changed no official ruling. Yet it reminded them that the people named in the records were not pieces on a board; they had bodies, families and the right to decide what came next. Zhou Lu glanced toward Fang Nian and remembered the principle they had been arguing about: an image cannot replace responsibility. It sounded simple until it demanded another hour of work. Fang Nian rewound the footage without claiming that a camera could tell the whole truth. The team considered the phenomenon a separate detail, not proof that the uncontrolled projection was safe. 一個小小的照顧動作打斷討論。它沒有解開謎，也沒改變裁決，卻提醒大家紀錄裡的人不是棋子；他們有身體、家庭，也有決定下一步的權利。周律望向方念，想起兩人不斷爭論的原則：影像不能取代責任。話雖簡單，真正實踐又需要多一小時工作。方念倒轉影像，沒有說攝影機能說出全部真相。團隊把「現象」視為獨立細節，不當成失控的投影安全的證據。見證人在覆核時提到「路線」，方念保留了原話。報告把風險標為「可疑的」，這項判斷須經核查才能行動。

Before leaving, they checked the names once more. Names were easier to lose than equipment, especially when a system reduced people to numbers. The check took little time. It kept the next chapter of the problem from beginning with a convenient omission. Zhou Lu knew the next day would not begin from a clean slate. The camera record would preserve one more question, and Fang Nian would be there to challenge the first easy answer. Fang Nian labeled the unedited file before anyone could crop it into a story. The slogan appeared in the older camera record, and Zhou Lu asked why the newer version omitted it. Nobody disputed the usage; what troubled Zhou Lu was the silence around its origin. Zhou Lu disliked the word youthful in the summary because it hid a decision someone had made.

離開前，他們再次核對名字。名字比設備更容易消失，尤其當系統把人簡化成數字。檢查花不了多少時間，卻避免問題的下一章從方便的遺漏開始。周律知道明天不會從零開始。攝影紀錄將保存又一個問題，方念也會在場質疑第一個容易的答案。方念先標示未剪輯檔，不讓人裁成另一個故事。舊攝影紀錄出現了「口號」，周律追問新版為何漏掉。沒人質疑「使用情況」，讓周律困擾的是它的來歷沒有人提。周律不喜歡摘要中的「年輕的」，因為它掩藏了某個人的決定。
