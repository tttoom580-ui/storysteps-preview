The team tested whether a shadow could move metal objects in a controlled room. Nobody in the room knew that this would become a turning point. The ordinary sounds of work continued around them, almost offensively calm, while an uncertain fact asked to be checked. A confident announcement would have been easier than the slower work of asking who might be hurt. Zhou Lu left the laboratory lights unchanged so the measurements could be repeated. They found a reference to ancestor near the damaged camera record, but its date was unreadable. The contrary appeared in the older camera record, and Zhou Lu asked why the newer version omitted it. Nobody disputed the craft; what troubled Zhou Lu was the silence around its origin.

團隊在受控房間測試影子能否移動金屬物品。房裡沒有人知道這將是轉折。周圍平凡的工作聲仍持續著，平靜得幾乎令人不安，而一個未經確定的事實正等待查核。高聲宣布結論遠比緩慢詢問誰可能受傷來得容易。周律保持實驗室燈光原狀，讓測量可重做。他們在受損攝影紀錄旁找到提及「祖先」的紀錄，日期卻看不清。舊攝影紀錄出現了「正好相反」，周律追問新版為何漏掉。沒人質疑「手工技藝」，讓周律困擾的是它的來歷沒有人提。

It could act only through light-sensitive control panels, not lift a normal solid object. The easiest part of the investigation was drawing a line between two points. The difficult part was deciding what that line meant. It might show a connection, a coincidence, or an error copied from an older file. No one wanted to spend another day checking. Fang Nian rewound the footage without claiming that a camera could tell the whole truth. The team considered the crush a separate detail, not proof that the uncontrolled projection was safe. A witness called the result defensible; Fang Nian wrote the adjective beside the witness’s name. The report labeled the risk distinguished, a judgment that needed checking before anyone acted. The plan called for them to equip the rescue team, and Zhou Lu asked who would check the result.

影子只能透過感光控制面板操作，無法抬起一般實體物件。調查最容易的部分，是在兩個點之間畫線。困難的是決定那條線的意思。它可能代表關聯、巧合，也可能是從舊檔複製的錯誤。沒有人想再花一天查證。方念倒轉影像，沒有說攝影機能說出全部真相。團隊把「擠壓；造成壓傷」視為獨立細節，不當成失控的投影安全的證據。見證人稱結果「有充分理由的」，方念把形容詞記在見證人姓名旁。報告把風險標為「傑出的；精湛的」，這項判斷須經核查才能行動。計畫要求他們「器材」，周律追問由誰查核結果。

They established a rule that no test would endanger occupied spaces or unconsenting people. Someone proposed an impressive shortcut. It relied on the same promise that had failed earlier, only stated in a brighter voice. The others were tired enough to welcome it, and that made refusal difficult. A shortcut was worth nothing if its safety existed only on a poster. Fang Nian labeled the unedited file before anyone could crop it into a story. Nobody disputed the guilt; what troubled Zhou Lu was the silence around its origin. The team considered the handwriting a separate detail, not proof that the uncontrolled projection was safe. A witness mentioned hose during the review, so Fang Nian preserved the exact wording. The report labeled the risk identical, a judgment that needed checking before anyone acted.

團隊制定不危及有人空間或未同意者的規則。有人提出引人注目的捷徑，只是把先前失敗的承諾用更明亮的語氣再講一遍。其他人累得幾乎想接受，因此拒絕很困難。若安全只存在海報上，捷徑就沒有價值。方念先標示未剪輯檔，不讓人裁成另一個故事。沒人質疑「愧疚」，讓周律困擾的是它的來歷沒有人提。團隊把「字跡」視為獨立細節，不當成失控的投影安全的證據。見證人在覆核時提到「水管」，方念保留了原話。報告把風險標為「完全相同的」，這項判斷須經核查才能行動。

The station theft became explicable: its keys were electronic access images, not physical keys. The first result was neither victory nor defeat. It removed one false assumption and exposed another problem that had been concealed beneath it. A witness asked whether the discovery could be trusted. The answer was that the test could be repeated, not that the investigators were special. A dark figure crossed the monitor with no matching footsteps in the hallway. The plan called for them to involve the people affected, and Zhou Lu asked who would check the result. Before they could launch the review, the group needed a witness who was free to object. Zhou Lu disliked the word lousy in the summary because it hid a decision someone had made.

車站竊案終於可理解：被拿走的是電子門禁影像，不是實體鑰匙。第一個結果既非勝利也非失敗。它排除一個錯誤假設，也露出下面藏著的另一個問題。見證人問發現能否相信；答案是測試可以重複，不是調查者有什麼特權。黑影掠過螢幕，走廊裡卻沒有相應腳步聲。計畫要求他們「涉及；參與」，周律追問由誰查核結果。在能夠「推出；啟動」之前，團隊需要一位能自由提出異議的見證人。周律不喜歡摘要中的「品質差的」，因為它掩藏了某個人的決定。

After the argument, a long silence settled around the group. It was not agreement. People were calculating what the next attempt might take from them, and whether anyone would notice the loss. Their caution was an ordinary human response, not a flaw in their courage. Zhou Lu glanced toward Fang Nian and remembered the principle they had been arguing about: an image cannot replace responsibility. It sounded simple until it demanded another hour of work. A reflection shivered in the empty window, then vanished as a bus passed. Fang Nian proposed they misunderstand the signal; Zhou Lu wanted the risk written down first. 爭論後，長久沉默籠罩大家。那不是同意。人們正在估算下一次嘗試會奪走什麼、又會不會有人注意。謹慎是平凡的人性反應，不是勇氣的缺點。周律望向方念，想起兩人不斷爭論的原則：影像不能取代責任。話雖簡單，真正實踐又需要多一小時工作。空窗上的反光微微顫動，公車經過時便消失。方念提議「誤會」，周律希望先把風險寫清楚。他們在受損攝影紀錄旁找到提及「懷孕」的紀錄，日期卻看不清。舊攝影紀錄出現了「謎題」，周律追問新版為何漏掉。

Evening came without resolving the argument completely. The record was clearer, but the consequences still belonged to real people rather than to the neat ending of a tale. The next step would need their consent, their questions and the patience to hear an unwelcome answer. Zhou Lu knew the next day would not begin from a clean slate. The camera record would preserve one more question, and Fang Nian would be there to challenge the first easy answer. A faint line of light separated the real doorway from its convincing copy. Nobody disputed the stroke; what troubled Zhou Lu was the silence around its origin. The team considered the suggestion a separate detail, not proof that the uncontrolled projection was safe. A witness mentioned technician during the review, so Fang Nian preserved the exact wording.

傍晚到來，爭論沒有完全解決。紀錄清楚了，後果卻仍屬於真實的人，不屬於故事漂亮的結尾。下一步需要他們的同意、提問，以及聽進不想聽的答案的耐心。周律知道明天不會從零開始。攝影紀錄將保存又一個問題，方念也會在場質疑第一個容易的答案。微弱光線把真門和逼真的複製影像分開。沒人質疑「一筆」，讓周律困擾的是它的來歷沒有人提。團隊把「建議」視為獨立細節，不當成失控的投影安全的證據。見證人在覆核時提到「技術員」，方念保留了原話。
