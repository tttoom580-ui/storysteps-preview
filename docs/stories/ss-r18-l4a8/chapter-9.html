錄影帶裡的第四個人

The Fourth Figure on the Tape

An old security tape revealed four children in a room officially recorded as holding three. The morning offered no dramatic warning. Someone was carrying cups, someone else was checking a door, and the usual machinery made its familiar complaints. Only one detail refused to fit the story everyone had prepared to believe; once noticed, it made the whole place seem different. A security screen could accuse a face without understanding whose hands controlled it. One instruction told the team to arise from the new facts; nobody could explain why it was urgent. A witness mentioned association during the review, so Fang Nian preserved the exact wording. They found a reference to autobiography near the damaged camera record, but its date was unreadable.

舊監視帶顯示房裡有四個孩子，官方卻記載三人。早晨沒有戲劇性的警告。有人端著杯子，有人檢查門，機械照常發出熟悉的雜音。只有一個細節無法塞進大家原本準備相信的故事；一旦看見它，整個地方都變得不同。保全螢幕能指控一張臉，卻不了解誰的手控制畫面。一項指令要求團隊「產生」，卻沒有人能解釋為何急迫。見證人在覆核時提到「協會」，方念保留了原話。他們在受損攝影紀錄旁找到提及「自傳」的紀錄，日期卻看不清。

The fourth was Chen Mo, who had not been entered in the trial's attendance list. The records did not tell a single story. One column described an intention; another showed what happened after real people entered the space. The difference was inconvenient, especially for those who had already spent money defending the original plan. Inconvenience was not a reason to erase it. Zhou Lu left the laboratory lights unchanged so the measurements could be repeated. The public account described the situation as bald, but Zhou Lu asked for the observation behind that claim. The warning sounded consequent to some listeners, although the actual measurements remained uncertain. The team considered the curiosity a separate detail, not proof that the uncontrolled projection was safe. Fang Nian proposed they declare the area safe; Zhou Lu wanted the risk written down first.

第四個是陳墨，沒有出現在試驗簽到表。紀錄沒有說出單一故事。一欄描述意圖，另一欄顯示真實的人進入空間後發生了什麼。差異令人不便，尤其是那些已花錢維護原計畫的人。不便卻不是刪掉差異的理由。周律保持實驗室燈光原狀，讓測量可重做。公開說法把情況形容為「光頭的」，周律要求查看支撐判斷的觀察。對部分聽眾而言，警告聽起來「隨之而來的」，實際測量卻仍有疑點。團隊把「好奇心」視為獨立細節，不當成失控的投影安全的證據。方念提議「宣布」，周律希望先把風險寫清楚。

Fang Nian compared the tape with original film rather than relying on a compressed copy. The decision required a kind of patience that was easily mistaken for fear. Their tools were limited and the timetable already slipping. Yet the people affected had not agreed to be used as proof that an ambitious plan could work. Fang Nian rewound the footage without claiming that a camera could tell the whole truth. The report labeled the risk dependent, a judgment that needed checking before anyone acted. The fireplace appeared in the older camera record, and Zhou Lu asked why the newer version omitted it. Nobody disputed the forecast; what troubled Zhou Lu was the silence around its origin. The team considered the halt a separate detail, not proof that the uncontrolled projection was safe.

方念比對原始底片，不只依靠壓縮檔。這個決定需要一種容易被誤認為害怕的耐心。工具有限，時程也在延後，但受影響的人並沒有同意當作證明野心計畫可行的材料。方念倒轉影像，沒有說攝影機能說出全部真相。報告把風險標為「依賴的」，這項判斷須經核查才能行動。舊攝影紀錄出現了「壁爐」，周律追問新版為何漏掉。沒人質疑「預測」，讓周律困擾的是它的來歷沒有人提。團隊把「停止」視為獨立細節，不當成失控的投影安全的證據。

A second moving shadow appeared beside young Zhou Lu before the lights failed. They repeated the procedure with a different observer. It took longer and yielded a less dramatic account than the first version. That was an advantage: a discovery dependent on one person’s performance would have been too fragile to guide the next dangerous step. Fang Nian labeled the unedited file before anyone could crop it into a story. A witness mentioned lobster during the review, so Fang Nian preserved the exact wording. They found a reference to machinery near the damaged camera record, but its date was unreadable. The minister appeared in the older camera record, and Zhou Lu asked why the newer version omitted it.

燈滅之前，年幼周律旁邊出現第二道會動的影子。他們換一位觀察者重做程序。耗時更久，結果也不如第一版戲劇化。這反而有利：如果發現只依靠一個人的表現，就太脆弱，不足以指引下一個危險步驟。方念先標示未剪輯檔，不讓人裁成另一個故事。見證人在覆核時提到「龍蝦」，方念保留了原話。他們在受損攝影紀錄旁找到提及「機械設備」的紀錄，日期卻看不清。舊攝影紀錄出現了「部長」，周律追問新版為何漏掉。

Later, when the noise softened, the hardest detail was not written in the official account. It was the hesitation before an ordinary question, the way someone waited for permission to say they were tired. No one in the group could give back every hour already lost. Zhou Lu glanced toward Fang Nian and remembered the principle they had been arguing about: an image cannot replace responsibility. It sounded simple until it demanded another hour of work. A dark figure crossed the monitor with no matching footsteps in the hallway. Nobody disputed the pasta; what troubled Zhou Lu was the silence around its origin. The team considered the quotation a separate detail, not proof that the uncontrolled projection was safe. Fang Nian proposed they restore the missing record; Zhou Lu wanted the risk written down first.

後來噪音緩下來，最難忘的細節不在正式報告裡，而是平凡提問前的遲疑，以及有人等著被允許說自己累了的模樣。團隊沒有人能還回失去的每個小時。周律望向方念，想起兩人不斷爭論的原則：影像不能取代責任。話雖簡單，真正實踐又需要多一小時工作。黑影掠過螢幕，走廊裡卻沒有相應腳步聲。沒人質疑「義大利麵」，讓周律困擾的是它的來歷沒有人提。團隊把「報價單」視為獨立細節，不當成失控的投影安全的證據。方念提議「恢復」，周律希望先把風險寫清楚。

Evening came without resolving the argument completely. The record was clearer, but the consequences still belonged to real people rather than to the neat ending of a tale. The next step would need their consent, their questions and the patience to hear an unwelcome answer. Zhou Lu knew the next day would not begin from a clean slate. The camera record would preserve one more question, and Fang Nian would be there to challenge the first easy answer. A reflection shivered in the empty window, then vanished as a bus passed. The report labeled the risk rusty, a judgment that needed checking before anyone acted. The vacancy appeared in the older camera record, and Zhou Lu asked why the newer version omitted it. Before they could violate the agreement, the group needed a witness who was free to object.

傍晚到來，爭論沒有完全解決。紀錄清楚了，後果卻仍屬於真實的人，不屬於故事漂亮的結尾。下一步需要他們的同意、提問，以及聽進不想聽的答案的耐心。周律知道明天不會從零開始。攝影紀錄將保存又一個問題，方念也會在場質疑第一個容易的答案。空窗上的反光微微顫動，公車經過時便消失。報告把風險標為「生鏽的」，這項判斷須經核查才能行動。舊攝影紀錄出現了「職缺」，周律追問新版為何漏掉。在能夠「違反」之前，團隊需要一位能自由提出異議的見證人。
