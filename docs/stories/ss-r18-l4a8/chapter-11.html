A Face in the Negative

我的影子正在替我犯罪 · 第 11 / 25 章 · 3000–4000 · 英中對照 · 校訂中

Fang Nian restored the original negative and found Chen Mo's reflection behind the moving silhouette. The first sign of trouble was not a scream. It was a pause in an otherwise predictable routine, long enough for a careful person to notice. Others kept moving, and their movement made it tempting to do the same, but the pause deserved an explanation. The city knew how to record people more easily than it knew how to protect them. Fang Nian proposed they accuse a person without proof; Zhou Lu wanted the risk written down first. They found a reference to assurance near the damaged camera record, but its date was unreadable. The plan called for them to boast about an untested result, and Zhou Lu asked who would check the result.

方念修復原始負片，在移動剪影後找到陳墨的倒影。第一個麻煩徵兆不是尖叫，而是原本可預期的流程忽然停頓，長到足以讓細心的人察覺。其他人繼續前進，令人忍不住跟上，可那次停頓需要解釋。城市擅長記錄人，卻不如保護人那麼熟練。方念提議「指控」，周律希望先把風險寫清楚。他們在受損攝影紀錄旁找到提及「保證」的紀錄，日期卻看不清。計畫要求他們「吹噓」，周律追問由誰查核結果。

The reflection appeared only when the experimental lamp was connected to a remote transmitter. A neat explanation would have spared everyone an argument. Instead, the evidence refused to be neat. It contained a gap large enough to change the meaning of what came before it. Filling the gap with a guess would have been quick, and unkind to anyone trusting the answer. The streetlamp threw a long shadow across the wall before Zhou Lu even moved. Nobody disputed the commerce; what troubled Zhou Lu was the silence around its origin. Zhou Lu disliked the word consistent in the summary because it hid a decision someone had made. Fang Nian proposed they contribute to the shared plan; Zhou Lu wanted the risk written down first. They found a reference to convention near the damaged camera record, but its date was unreadable.

倒影只在實驗燈接上遠端發射器時出現。整齊的解釋本可省去爭吵，但證據偏偏不整齊。裡面有一道足以改變先前意義的缺口。拿猜測補起來雖快，對信任答案的人卻不公平。周律還沒動，路燈已在牆上投出長影。沒人質疑「商業」，讓周律困擾的是它的來歷沒有人提。周律不喜歡摘要中的「前後一致的」，因為它掩藏了某個人的決定。方念提議「貢獻」，周律希望先把風險寫清楚。他們在受損攝影紀錄旁找到提及「大會；集會」的紀錄，日期卻看不清。

They separated the video evidence from unverified theories about who controlled the shadow. The most powerful person present could have ended the argument with an order. That might even have made the next hour more efficient. It would not have made the order wise. Instead, questions were allowed to remain questions long enough for another person to object. A security screen could accuse a face without understanding whose hands controlled it. The plan called for them to expose a private witness, and Zhou Lu asked who would check the result. Nobody disputed the hurricane; what troubled Zhou Lu was the silence around its origin. The team considered the hydrogen a separate detail, not proof that the uncontrolled projection was safe. A witness mentioned ignorance during the review, so Fang Nian preserved the exact wording.

兩人區分影像證據與誰控制影子的未證實推測。在場最有權力的人本可一聲令下結束爭論，甚至讓下一小時更有效率，但命令不會因此變明智。他們讓問題繼續存在，給另一個人提出異議的時間。保全螢幕能指控一張臉，卻不了解誰的手控制畫面。計畫要求他們「揭露」，周律追問由誰查核結果。沒人質疑「颶風」，讓周律困擾的是它的來歷沒有人提。團隊把「氫」視為獨立細節，不當成失控的投影安全的證據。見證人在覆核時提到「無知」，方念保留了原話。

A distant transmitter switched on as soon as they named Chen Mo in the report. The evidence produced a consequence nobody could delegate away. Somebody would have to change a timetable, surrender a privilege, or admit that a previous decision had hurt people. Numbers were useful only when they could still lead to accountable actions. Zhou Lu left the laboratory lights unchanged so the measurements could be repeated. The report labeled the risk informative, a judgment that needed checking before anyone acted. The public account described the situation as loyal, but Zhou Lu asked for the observation behind that claim. Nobody disputed the manufacture; what troubled Zhou Lu was the silence around its origin.

他們在報告寫出陳墨姓名時，遠端發射器突然啟動。證據造成無法推給別人的後果。有人必須改時程、交還特權，或承認先前決定傷害了人。數字只有能引向負責的行動，才真的有用。周律保持實驗室燈光原狀，讓測量可重做。報告把風險標為「提供資訊的」，這項判斷須經核查才能行動。公開說法把情況形容為「忠於」，周律要求查看支撐判斷的觀察。沒人質疑「製造」，讓周律困擾的是它的來歷沒有人提。

The work left marks that a final report would never show: a dented cup, an unfinished meal, a message that had to be sent to somebody waiting at home. These things did not prove a theory. They explained why getting the theory right mattered. Zhou Lu glanced toward Fang Nian and remembered the principle they had been arguing about: an image cannot replace responsibility. It sounded simple until it demanded another hour of work. Fang Nian rewound the footage without claiming that a camera could tell the whole truth. The team considered the misfortune a separate detail, not proof that the uncontrolled projection was safe. A witness mentioned recovery during the review, so Fang Nian preserved the exact wording. They found a reference to relaxation near the damaged camera record, but its date was unreadable.

工作留下最終報告不會顯示的痕跡：凹掉的杯子、沒吃完的飯、必須傳給在家等候者的訊息。這些不能證明理論，卻說明理論為什麼必須正確。周律望向方念，想起兩人不斷爭論的原則：影像不能取代責任。話雖簡單，真正實踐又需要多一小時工作。方念倒轉影像，沒有說攝影機能說出全部真相。團隊把「災禍；不幸」視為獨立細節，不當成失控的投影安全的證據。見證人在覆核時提到「復原」，方念保留了原話。他們在受損攝影紀錄旁找到提及「休息時間」的紀錄，日期卻看不清。

Before leaving, they checked the names once more. Names were easier to lose than equipment, especially when a system reduced people to numbers. The check took little time. It kept the next chapter of the problem from beginning with a convenient omission. Zhou Lu knew the next day would not begin from a clean slate. The camera record would preserve one more question, and Fang Nian would be there to challenge the first easy answer. Fang Nian labeled the unedited file before anyone could crop it into a story. The resistance appeared in the older camera record, and Zhou Lu asked why the newer version omitted it. Nobody disputed the skyscraper; what troubled Zhou Lu was the silence around its origin. Zhou Lu disliked the word troublesome in the summary because it hid a decision someone had made.

離開前，他們再次核對名字。名字比設備更容易消失，尤其當系統把人簡化成數字。檢查花不了多少時間，卻避免問題的下一章從方便的遺漏開始。周律知道明天不會從零開始。攝影紀錄將保存又一個問題，方念也會在場質疑第一個容易的答案。方念先標示未剪輯檔，不讓人裁成另一個故事。舊攝影紀錄出現了「阻力」，周律追問新版為何漏掉。沒人質疑「摩天大樓」，讓周律困擾的是它的來歷沒有人提。周律不喜歡摘要中的「棘手的」，因為它掩藏了某個人的決定。
