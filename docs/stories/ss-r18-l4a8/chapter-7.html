A Childhood Written Over

我的影子正在替我犯罪 · 第 7 / 25 章 · 3000–4000 · 英中對照 · 校訂中

Zhou Lu's mother admitted she had signed consent for a harmless light study. The morning offered no dramatic warning. Someone was carrying cups, someone else was checking a door, and the usual machinery made its familiar complaints. Only one detail refused to fit the story everyone had prepared to believe; once noticed, it made the whole place seem different. Fang Nian rewound the footage without claiming that a camera could tell the whole truth. Nobody disputed the accuracy; what troubled Zhou Lu was the silence around its origin. One instruction told the team to adapt the existing plan; nobody could explain why it was urgent. Fang Nian proposed they analyze the original readings; Zhou Lu wanted the risk written down first.

周律母親承認曾同意一項看似無害的燈光研究。早晨沒有戲劇性的警告。有人端著杯子，有人檢查門，機械照常發出熟悉的雜音。只有一個細節無法塞進大家原本準備相信的故事；一旦看見它，整個地方都變得不同。方念倒轉影像，沒有說攝影機能說出全部真相。沒人質疑「準確度」，讓周律困擾的是它的來歷沒有人提。一項指令要求團隊「調整」，卻沒有人能解釋為何急迫。方念提議「分析」，周律希望先把風險寫清楚。

The researchers later changed the protocol without asking the children or their families. They compared the new account with what could be measured independently. It was an unglamorous exercise, made of repeated readings and questions that did not impress an audience. The result mattered precisely because it might prove the people asking those questions were wrong. Fang Nian labeled the unedited file before anyone could crop it into a story. They found a reference to competitor near the damaged camera record, but its date was unreadable. The diploma appeared in the older camera record, and Zhou Lu asked why the newer version omitted it. Nobody disputed the frost; what troubled Zhou Lu was the silence around its origin. The team considered the helicopter a separate detail, not proof that the uncontrolled projection was safe.

研究者後來未經孩子與家屬同意便修改程序。他們把新說法與能獨立測量的事情比較。這不光鮮，只是重複讀值與不會取悅觀眾的提問。結果重要，正因為它也可能證明提問者自己錯了。方念先標示未剪輯檔，不讓人裁成另一個故事。他們在受損攝影紀錄旁找到提及「競爭者」的紀錄，日期卻看不清。舊攝影紀錄出現了「文憑」，周律追問新版為何漏掉。沒人質疑「霜凍」，讓周律困擾的是它的來歷沒有人提。團隊把「直升機」視為獨立細節，不當成失控的投影安全的證據。

Zhou Lu asked his mother what she remembered without accusing her of knowing the hidden risks. Someone proposed an impressive shortcut. It relied on the same promise that had failed earlier, only stated in a brighter voice. The others were tired enough to welcome it, and that made refusal difficult. A shortcut was worth nothing if its safety existed only on a poster. A dark figure crossed the monitor with no matching footsteps in the hallway. A witness mentioned herd during the review, so Fang Nian preserved the exact wording. It might help to injure a volunteer, but Zhou Lu refused to mistake possibility for proof. The public account described the situation as intense, but Zhou Lu asked for the observation behind that claim. Nobody disputed the mule; what troubled Zhou Lu was the silence around its origin.

周律問母親記得哪些事，沒有指控她早知道風險。有人提出引人注目的捷徑，只是把先前失敗的承諾用更明亮的語氣再講一遍。其他人累得幾乎想接受，因此拒絕很困難。若安全只存在海報上，捷徑就沒有價值。黑影掠過螢幕，走廊裡卻沒有相應腳步聲。見證人在覆核時提到「羊群」，方念保留了原話。「使受傷」或許有幫助，周律卻拒絕把可能當成證明。公開說法把情況形容為「激烈的」，周律要求查看支撐判斷的觀察。沒人質疑「騾子」，讓周律困擾的是它的來歷沒有人提。

She remembered a boy called Chen Mo who had tried to stop the final experiment. The first result was neither victory nor defeat. It removed one false assumption and exposed another problem that had been concealed beneath it. A witness asked whether the discovery could be trusted. The answer was that the test could be repeated, not that the investigators were special. A reflection shivered in the empty window, then vanished as a bus passed. Zhou Lu disliked the word mutual in the summary because it hid a decision someone had made. Fang Nian proposed they oppose the unfair order; Zhou Lu wanted the risk written down first. They found a reference to percent near the damaged camera record, but its date was unreadable.

母親記得一名叫陳墨的少年曾試著停止最後測試。第一個結果既非勝利也非失敗。它排除一個錯誤假設，也露出下面藏著的另一個問題。見證人問發現能否相信；答案是測試可以重複，不是調查者有什麼特權。空窗上的反光微微顫動，公車經過時便消失。周律不喜歡摘要中的「共同的」，因為它掩藏了某個人的決定。方念提議「反對」，周律希望先把風險寫清楚。他們在受損攝影紀錄旁找到提及「百分比」的紀錄，日期卻看不清。

A small act of care interrupted the discussion. It solved no mystery and changed no official ruling. Yet it reminded them that the people named in the records were not pieces on a board; they had bodies, families and the right to decide what came next. Zhou Lu glanced toward Fang Nian and remembered the principle they had been arguing about: an image cannot replace responsibility. It sounded simple until it demanded another hour of work. A faint line of light separated the real doorway from its convincing copy. The plumber appeared in the older camera record, and Zhou Lu asked why the newer version omitted it. Nobody disputed the reduction; what troubled Zhou Lu was the silence around its origin. Zhou Lu disliked the word remarkable in the summary because it hid a decision someone had made.

一個小小的照顧動作打斷討論。它沒有解開謎，也沒改變裁決，卻提醒大家紀錄裡的人不是棋子；他們有身體、家庭，也有決定下一步的權利。周律望向方念，想起兩人不斷爭論的原則：影像不能取代責任。話雖簡單，真正實踐又需要多一小時工作。微弱光線把真門和逼真的複製影像分開。舊攝影紀錄出現了「水管工」，周律追問新版為何漏掉。沒人質疑「減少」，讓周律困擾的是它的來歷沒有人提。周律不喜歡摘要中的「不尋常的；顯著的」，因為它掩藏了某個人的決定。

Evening came without resolving the argument completely. The record was clearer, but the consequences still belonged to real people rather than to the neat ending of a tale. The next step would need their consent, their questions and the patience to hear an unwelcome answer. Zhou Lu knew the next day would not begin from a clean slate. The camera record would preserve one more question, and Fang Nian would be there to challenge the first easy answer. The spare lamp stood ready, ordinary and more comforting than any spectacular projection. A witness called the result slight; Fang Nian wrote the adjective beside the witness’s name. They found a reference to surroundings near the damaged camera record, but its date was unreadable. The welfare appeared in the older camera record, and Zhou Lu asked why the newer version omitted it.

傍晚到來，爭論沒有完全解決。紀錄清楚了，後果卻仍屬於真實的人，不屬於故事漂亮的結尾。下一步需要他們的同意、提問，以及聽進不想聽的答案的耐心。周律知道明天不會從零開始。攝影紀錄將保存又一個問題，方念也會在場質疑第一個容易的答案。備用燈準備就緒，平凡卻比任何壯觀投影更令人安心。見證人稱結果「微小的」，方念把形容詞記在見證人姓名旁。他們在受損攝影紀錄旁找到提及「周遭環境」的紀錄，日期卻看不清。舊攝影紀錄出現了「福利；福祉」，周律追問新版為何漏掉。
