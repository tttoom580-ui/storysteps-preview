The moving silhouette formed the words HELP CHEN on a projection screen. The plan looked respectable at first glance. It had dates, names, and a signature in the proper corner. But paper can be complete without being honest, and a polished surface does not remove the cost carried by those who have no place to object. The spare lamp stood ready, ordinary and more comforting than any spectacular projection. The warning sounded absolute to some listeners, although the actual measurements remained uncertain. The team considered the discipline a separate detail, not proof that the uncontrolled projection was safe. A witness mentioned distribution during the review, so Fang Nian preserved the exact wording.

移動剪影在投影幕上排出「幫助陳墨」。計畫乍看很正式，有日期、姓名，簽名也在正確角落。然而文件可以完整卻不誠實；漂亮的外觀不能消除無處提出異議的人所承擔的代價。備用燈準備就緒，平凡卻比任何壯觀投影更令人安心。對部分聽眾而言，警告聽起來「絕對的」，實際測量卻仍有疑點。團隊把「紀律」視為獨立細節，不當成失控的投影安全的證據。見證人在覆核時提到「分布」，方念保留了原話。

Its message could have been a warning or a trap, because no one knew who sent it. A neat explanation would have spared everyone an argument. Instead, the evidence refused to be neat. It contained a gap large enough to change the meaning of what came before it. Filling the gap with a guess would have been quick, and unkind to anyone trusting the answer. The city knew how to record people more easily than it knew how to protect them. They found a reference to dread near the damaged camera record, but its date was unreadable. The efficiency appeared in the older camera record, and Zhou Lu asked why the newer version omitted it. Before they could fasten the loose cover, the group needed a witness who was free to object. Zhou Lu disliked the word gracious in the summary because it hid a decision someone had made.

訊息可能是警告也可能是陷阱，沒人知道發送者是誰。整齊的解釋本可省去爭吵，但證據偏偏不整齊。裡面有一道足以改變先前意義的缺口。拿猜測補起來雖快，對信任答案的人卻不公平。城市擅長記錄人，卻不如保護人那麼熟練。他們在受損攝影紀錄旁找到提及「恐懼」的紀錄，日期卻看不清。舊攝影紀錄出現了「效率」，周律追問新版為何漏掉。在能夠「固定」之前，團隊需要一位能自由提出異議的見證人。周律不喜歡摘要中的「親切的」，因為它掩藏了某個人的決定。

Zhou Lu sent a question through an isolated light circuit rather than following the shadow blindly. A refusal sounded small compared with the danger outside. Even so, it changed what the group understood about its limits. No one should have to give up a private history, a safe route, or an honest answer merely to prove they were cooperative. The streetlamp threw a long shadow across the wall before Zhou Lu even moved. A witness mentioned grief during the review, so Fang Nian preserved the exact wording. They found a reference to hardware near the damaged camera record, but its date was unreadable. The hawk appeared in the older camera record, and Zhou Lu asked why the newer version omitted it. Nobody disputed the illustration; what troubled Zhou Lu was the silence around its origin.

周律透過隔離光路回問，而不是盲目跟隨影子。面對外面的危險，一次拒絕聽來很微小，卻改變了團隊對界線的理解。沒有人必須放棄私人經歷、安全路線或誠實答案，才能證明自己願意合作。周律還沒動，路燈已在牆上投出長影。見證人在覆核時提到「悲傷」，方念保留了原話。他們在受損攝影紀錄旁找到提及「電子硬體」的紀錄，日期卻看不清。舊攝影紀錄出現了「老鷹」，周律追問新版為何漏掉。沒人質疑「插圖」，讓周律困擾的是它的來歷沒有人提。

The answer named a sealed laboratory under the river. For a moment the whole plan seemed to fail. The expected signal never arrived, and the people waiting outside grew impatient. Then a smaller detail emerged, one that would have been lost if the group had covered the mistake with a confident announcement. A security screen could accuse a face without understanding whose hands controlled it. One instruction told the team to insert the missing page; nobody could explain why it was urgent. A witness mentioned miner during the review, so Fang Nian preserved the exact wording. The report labeled the risk oval, a judgment that needed checking before anyone acted.

回答指出河底有一座封閉實驗室。有一刻整個計畫像要失敗。預期訊號沒有來，外面等候的人越來越不耐。隨後出現更小的細節；若團隊用自信公告掩蓋錯誤，那個細節就會消失。保全螢幕能指控一張臉，卻不了解誰的手控制畫面。一項指令要求團隊「插入」，卻沒有人能解釋為何急迫。見證人在覆核時提到「礦工」，方念保留了原話。報告把風險標為「橢圓形的」，這項判斷須經核查才能行動。

A small act of care interrupted the discussion. It solved no mystery and changed no official ruling. Yet it reminded them that the people named in the records were not pieces on a board; they had bodies, families and the right to decide what came next. Zhou Lu glanced toward Fang Nian and remembered the principle they had been arguing about: an image cannot replace responsibility. It sounded simple until it demanded another hour of work. Zhou Lu left the laboratory lights unchanged so the measurements could be repeated. The perfection appeared in the older camera record, and Zhou Lu asked why the newer version omitted it. The warning sounded realistic to some listeners, although the actual measurements remained uncertain. The team considered the recognition a separate detail, not proof that the uncontrolled projection was safe.

一個小小的照顧動作打斷討論。它沒有解開謎，也沒改變裁決，卻提醒大家紀錄裡的人不是棋子；他們有身體、家庭，也有決定下一步的權利。周律望向方念，想起兩人不斷爭論的原則：影像不能取代責任。話雖簡單，真正實踐又需要多一小時工作。周律保持實驗室燈光原狀，讓測量可重做。舊攝影紀錄出現了「完美」，周律追問新版為何漏掉。對部分聽眾而言，警告聽起來「實際可行的」，實際測量卻仍有疑點。團隊把「辨識」視為獨立細節，不當成失控的投影安全的證據。

The last light in the room was not a sign of triumph. It was a reminder that somebody would return to check the work, and that a new person might notice something the experts had missed. They left the account open to correction rather than locking it inside a victory speech. Zhou Lu knew the next day would not begin from a clean slate. The camera record would preserve one more question, and Fang Nian would be there to challenge the first easy answer. Fang Nian rewound the footage without claiming that a camera could tell the whole truth. Fang Nian proposed they renew the inspection; Zhou Lu wanted the risk written down first. It might help to resolve the dispute fairly, but Zhou Lu refused to mistake possibility for proof. The vessel appeared in the older camera record, and Zhou Lu asked why the newer version omitted it.

房裡最後的燈不是勝利標誌，而是提醒有人會回來檢查，也可能有新人看見專家漏掉的事。他們讓紀錄保持可更正，而不是鎖進勝利演說裡。周律知道明天不會從零開始。攝影紀錄將保存又一個問題，方念也會在場質疑第一個容易的答案。方念倒轉影像，沒有說攝影機能說出全部真相。方念提議「更新；恢復」，周律希望先把風險寫清楚。「決定」或許有幫助，周律卻拒絕把可能當成證明。舊攝影紀錄出現了「容器」，周律追問新版為何漏掉。
