The lamp company proposed a scheduled blackout to erase all remaining shadow patterns. The first sign of trouble was not a scream. It was a pause in an otherwise predictable routine, long enough for a careful person to notice. Others kept moving, and their movement made it tempting to do the same, but the pause deserved an explanation. The streetlamp threw a long shadow across the wall before Zhou Lu even moved. The acceptance appeared in the older camera record, and Zhou Lu asked why the newer version omitted it. The warning sounded ambitious to some listeners, although the actual measurements remained uncertain. The team considered the atmosphere a separate detail, not proof that the uncontrolled projection was safe.

路燈公司提出預定全城停電，想清除所有影子圖樣。第一個麻煩徵兆不是尖叫，而是原本可預期的流程忽然停頓，長到足以讓細心的人察覺。其他人繼續前進，令人忍不住跟上，可那次停頓需要解釋。周律還沒動，路燈已在牆上投出長影。舊攝影紀錄出現了「接受；同意」，周律追問新版為何漏掉。對部分聽眾而言，警告聽起來「有企圖心的」，實際測量卻仍有疑點。團隊把「氣氛」視為獨立細節，不當成失控的投影安全的證據。

Its plan ignored hospitals and transit corridors that depended on the experimental lamps. The records did not tell a single story. One column described an intention; another showed what happened after real people entered the space. The difference was inconvenient, especially for those who had already spent money defending the original plan. Inconvenience was not a reason to erase it. A security screen could accuse a face without understanding whose hands controlled it. A witness called the result authentic; Fang Nian wrote the adjective beside the witness’s name. It might help to classify the report, but Zhou Lu refused to mistake possibility for proof. The plan called for them to differ from the first account, and Zhou Lu asked who would check the result. Nobody disputed the dignity; what troubled Zhou Lu was the silence around its origin.

計畫忽略了依賴實驗燈的醫院與交通走廊。紀錄沒有說出單一故事。一欄描述意圖，另一欄顯示真實的人進入空間後發生了什麼。差異令人不便，尤其是那些已花錢維護原計畫的人。不便卻不是刪掉差異的理由。保全螢幕能指控一張臉，卻不了解誰的手控制畫面。見證人稱結果「真實可靠的」，方念把形容詞記在見證人姓名旁。「分類」或許有幫助，周律卻拒絕把可能當成證明。計畫要求他們「有所不同」，周律追問由誰查核結果。沒人質疑「尊嚴」，讓周律困擾的是它的來歷沒有人提。

Zhou Lu warned authorities and demanded a staged replacement with emergency backups. Someone proposed an impressive shortcut. It relied on the same promise that had failed earlier, only stated in a brighter voice. The others were tired enough to welcome it, and that made refusal difficult. A shortcut was worth nothing if its safety existed only on a poster. Zhou Lu left the laboratory lights unchanged so the measurements could be repeated. One instruction told the team to embarrass the staff publicly; nobody could explain why it was urgent. A witness mentioned facility during the review, so Fang Nian preserved the exact wording. The report labeled the risk genuine, a judgment that needed checking before anyone acted. The public account described the situation as offensive, but Zhou Lu asked for the observation behind that claim.

周律通知主管機關，要求分區替換並設置備援。有人提出引人注目的捷徑，只是把先前失敗的承諾用更明亮的語氣再講一遍。其他人累得幾乎想接受，因此拒絕很困難。若安全只存在海報上，捷徑就沒有價值。周律保持實驗室燈光原狀，讓測量可重做。一項指令要求團隊「使人難堪」，卻沒有人能解釋為何急迫。見證人在覆核時提到「設施」，方念保留了原話。報告把風險標為「真實的」，這項判斷須經核查才能行動。公開說法把情況形容為「惡意的」，周律要求查看支撐判斷的觀察。

The company called him unstable and released a cropped video of his shadow. The evidence produced a consequence nobody could delegate away. Somebody would have to change a timetable, surrender a privilege, or admit that a previous decision had hurt people. Numbers were useful only when they could still lead to accountable actions. Fang Nian rewound the footage without claiming that a camera could tell the whole truth. Nobody disputed the option; what troubled Zhou Lu was the silence around its origin. The team considered the perfume a separate detail, not proof that the uncontrolled projection was safe. A witness mentioned promotion during the review, so Fang Nian preserved the exact wording.

公司稱他精神不穩，公布被裁切的影子影像。證據造成無法推給別人的後果。有人必須改時程、交還特權，或承認先前決定傷害了人。數字只有能引向負責的行動，才真的有用。方念倒轉影像，沒有說攝影機能說出全部真相。沒人質疑「選項」，讓周律困擾的是它的來歷沒有人提。團隊把「香水」視為獨立細節，不當成失控的投影安全的證據。見證人在覆核時提到「行銷」，方念保留了原話。

Later, when the noise softened, the hardest detail was not written in the official account. It was the hesitation before an ordinary question, the way someone waited for permission to say they were tired. No one in the group could give back every hour already lost. Zhou Lu glanced toward Fang Nian and remembered the principle they had been arguing about: an image cannot replace responsibility. It sounded simple until it demanded another hour of work. Fang Nian labeled the unedited file before anyone could crop it into a story. They found a reference to publisher near the damaged camera record, but its date was unreadable. The refugee appeared in the older camera record, and Zhou Lu asked why the newer version omitted it. Nobody disputed the rescue; what troubled Zhou Lu was the silence around its origin.

後來噪音緩下來，最難忘的細節不在正式報告裡，而是平凡提問前的遲疑，以及有人等著被允許說自己累了的模樣。團隊沒有人能還回失去的每個小時。周律望向方念，想起兩人不斷爭論的原則：影像不能取代責任。話雖簡單，真正實踐又需要多一小時工作。方念先標示未剪輯檔，不讓人裁成另一個故事。他們在受損攝影紀錄旁找到提及「出版社」的紀錄，日期卻看不清。舊攝影紀錄出現了「難民」，周律追問新版為何漏掉。沒人質疑「救援」，讓周律困擾的是它的來歷沒有人提。

They filed the result beside the earlier mistakes, not above them. Tomorrow would require a different question and perhaps a different kind of courage. For now, the important thing was that the people most affected could read what had happened and challenge the account if it was wrong. Zhou Lu knew the next day would not begin from a clean slate. The camera record would preserve one more question, and Fang Nian would be there to challenge the first easy answer. A dark figure crossed the monitor with no matching footsteps in the hallway. The team considered the resignation a separate detail, not proof that the uncontrolled projection was safe. A witness mentioned stocking during the review, so Fang Nian preserved the exact wording. It might help to transform the flawed arrangement, but Zhou Lu refused to mistake possibility for proof.

他們把結果放在先前的錯誤旁邊，而不是蓋過錯誤。明天需要不同的問題，也許需要不同的勇氣。眼下最重要的是受影響者能讀到經過，也能在紀錄錯誤時提出挑戰。周律知道明天不會從零開始。攝影紀錄將保存又一個問題，方念也會在場質疑第一個容易的答案。黑影掠過螢幕，走廊裡卻沒有相應腳步聲。團隊把「辭職；辭職信」視為獨立細節，不當成失控的投影安全的證據。見證人在覆核時提到「絲襪；長襪」，方念保留了原話。「轉變」或許有幫助，周律卻拒絕把可能當成證明。
