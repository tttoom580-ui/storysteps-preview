The Crime Nobody Reported

我的影子正在替我犯罪 · 第 5 / 25 章 · 3000–4000 · 英中對照 · 校訂中

The shadow appeared at a private warehouse and carried away a sealed box. The first sign of trouble was not a scream. It was a pause in an otherwise predictable routine, long enough for a careful person to notice. Others kept moving, and their movement made it tempting to do the same, but the pause deserved an explanation. A dark figure crossed the monitor with no matching footsteps in the hallway. The plan called for them to adopt a safer rule, and Zhou Lu asked who would check the result. Nobody disputed the agency; what troubled Zhou Lu was the silence around its origin. The team considered the behavior a separate detail, not proof that the uncontrolled projection was safe.

影子出現在私人倉庫，帶走一只封箱。第一個麻煩徵兆不是尖叫，而是原本可預期的流程忽然停頓，長到足以讓細心的人察覺。其他人繼續前進，令人忍不住跟上，可那次停頓需要解釋。黑影掠過螢幕，走廊裡卻沒有相應腳步聲。計畫要求他們「採用」，周律追問由誰查核結果。沒人質疑「機構」，讓周律困擾的是它的來歷沒有人提。團隊把「行為」視為獨立細節，不當成失控的投影安全的證據。

No theft had been reported, because the box held evidence of an illegal lamp experiment. They compared the new account with what could be measured independently. It was an unglamorous exercise, made of repeated readings and questions that did not impress an audience. The result mattered precisely because it might prove the people asking those questions were wrong. A reflection shivered in the empty window, then vanished as a bus passed. Fang Nian proposed they cherish the last message; Zhou Lu wanted the risk written down first. They found a reference to congress near the damaged camera record, but its date was unreadable. The conscience appeared in the older camera record, and Zhou Lu asked why the newer version omitted it. Nobody disputed the decoration; what troubled Zhou Lu was the silence around its origin.

沒人報案，因為箱中是非法照明實驗的證據。他們把新說法與能獨立測量的事情比較。這不光鮮，只是重複讀值與不會取悅觀眾的提問。結果重要，正因為它也可能證明提問者自己錯了。空窗上的反光微微顫動，公車經過時便消失。方念提議「珍惜」，周律希望先把風險寫清楚。他們在受損攝影紀錄旁找到提及「議會」的紀錄，日期卻看不清。舊攝影紀錄出現了「良心」，周律追問新版為何漏掉。沒人質疑「裝飾」，讓周律困擾的是它的來歷沒有人提。

Fang Nian documented the box's location without breaking its seals. Someone proposed an impressive shortcut. It relied on the same promise that had failed earlier, only stated in a brighter voice. The others were tired enough to welcome it, and that made refusal difficult. A shortcut was worth nothing if its safety existed only on a poster. A faint line of light separated the real doorway from its convincing copy. One instruction told the team to discourage a dangerous shortcut; nobody could explain why it was urgent. A witness called the result fundamental; Fang Nian wrote the adjective beside the witness’s name. It might help to horrify the witnesses with careless claims, but Zhou Lu refused to mistake possibility for proof. The public account described the situation as influential, but Zhou Lu asked for the observation behind that claim.

方念記錄箱子位置，沒有破壞封條。有人提出引人注目的捷徑，只是把先前失敗的承諾用更明亮的語氣再講一遍。其他人累得幾乎想接受，因此拒絕很困難。若安全只存在海報上，捷徑就沒有價值。微弱光線把真門和逼真的複製影像分開。一項指令要求團隊「使卻步」，卻沒有人能解釋為何急迫。見證人稱結果「根本的；基本的」，方念把形容詞記在見證人姓名旁。「使驚恐」或許有幫助，周律卻拒絕把可能當成證明。公開說法把情況形容為「有影響力的」，周律要求查看支撐判斷的觀察。

The shadow pointed to an address written on a warning label, then vanished at sunrise. For a moment the whole plan seemed to fail. The expected signal never arrived, and the people waiting outside grew impatient. Then a smaller detail emerged, one that would have been lost if the group had covered the mistake with a confident announcement. The spare lamp stood ready, ordinary and more comforting than any spectacular projection. Nobody disputed the interaction; what troubled Zhou Lu was the silence around its origin. The team considered the outcome a separate detail, not proof that the uncontrolled projection was safe. A witness called the result primitive; Fang Nian wrote the adjective beside the witness’s name.

影子指向警告標籤上的地址，日出時消失。有一刻整個計畫像要失敗。預期訊號沒有來，外面等候的人越來越不耐。隨後出現更小的細節；若團隊用自信公告掩蓋錯誤，那個細節就會消失。備用燈準備就緒，平凡卻比任何壯觀投影更令人安心。沒人質疑「相互作用」，讓周律困擾的是它的來歷沒有人提。團隊把「結果」視為獨立細節，不當成失控的投影安全的證據。見證人稱結果「古早的」，方念把形容詞記在見證人姓名旁。

After the argument, a long silence settled around the group. It was not agreement. People were calculating what the next attempt might take from them, and whether anyone would notice the loss. Their caution was an ordinary human response, not a flaw in their courage. Zhou Lu glanced toward Fang Nian and remembered the principle they had been arguing about: an image cannot replace responsibility. It sounded simple until it demanded another hour of work. The city knew how to record people more easily than it knew how to protect them. They found a reference to reception near the damaged camera record, but its date was unreadable. The robber appeared in the older camera record, and Zhou Lu asked why the newer version omitted it. Nobody disputed the scenery; what troubled Zhou Lu was the silence around its origin.

爭論後，長久沉默籠罩大家。那不是同意。人們正在估算下一次嘗試會奪走什麼、又會不會有人注意。謹慎是平凡的人性反應，不是勇氣的缺點。周律望向方念，想起兩人不斷爭論的原則：影像不能取代責任。話雖簡單，真正實踐又需要多一小時工作。城市擅長記錄人，卻不如保護人那麼熟練。他們在受損攝影紀錄旁找到提及「接待會」的紀錄，日期卻看不清。舊攝影紀錄出現了「強盜」，周律追問新版為何漏掉。沒人質疑「舞臺布景」，讓周律困擾的是它的來歷沒有人提。

Before leaving, they checked the names once more. Names were easier to lose than equipment, especially when a system reduced people to numbers. The check took little time. It kept the next chapter of the problem from beginning with a convenient omission. Zhou Lu knew the next day would not begin from a clean slate. The camera record would preserve one more question, and Fang Nian would be there to challenge the first easy answer. The streetlamp threw a long shadow across the wall before Zhou Lu even moved. Zhou Lu disliked the word shameful in the summary because it hid a decision someone had made. A witness mentioned terror during the review, so Fang Nian preserved the exact wording. They found a reference to witch near the damaged camera record, but its date was unreadable.

離開前，他們再次核對名字。名字比設備更容易消失，尤其當系統把人簡化成數字。檢查花不了多少時間，卻避免問題的下一章從方便的遺漏開始。周律知道明天不會從零開始。攝影紀錄將保存又一個問題，方念也會在場質疑第一個容易的答案。周律還沒動，路燈已在牆上投出長影。周律不喜歡摘要中的「可恥的」，因為它掩藏了某個人的決定。見證人在覆核時提到「恐懼」，方念保留了原話。他們在受損攝影紀錄旁找到提及「巫師」的紀錄，日期卻看不清。
