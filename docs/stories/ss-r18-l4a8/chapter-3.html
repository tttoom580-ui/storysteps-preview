The Lamp That Cast Two Shadows

我的影子正在替我犯罪 · 第 3 / 25 章 · 3000–4000 · 英中對照 · 校訂中

Fang Nian photographed Zhou Lu beneath a laboratory lamp and counted two separate shadows. Nobody in the room knew that this would become a turning point. The ordinary sounds of work continued around them, almost offensively calm, while an uncertain fact asked to be checked. A confident announcement would have been easier than the slower work of asking who might be hurt. A faint line of light separated the real doorway from its convincing copy. The report labeled the risk accidental, a judgment that needed checking before anyone acted. The barrier appeared in the older camera record, and Zhou Lu asked why the newer version omitted it. Before they could blink at the sudden light, the group needed a witness who was free to object.

方念在實驗室燈下拍攝周律，數出兩道分離的影子。房裡沒有人知道這將是轉折。周圍平凡的工作聲仍持續著，平靜得幾乎令人不安，而一個未經確定的事實正等待查核。高聲宣布結論遠比緩慢詢問誰可能受傷來得容易。微弱光線把真門和逼真的複製影像分開。報告把風險標為「意外的」，這項判斷須經核查才能行動。舊攝影紀錄出現了「柵欄」，周律追問新版為何漏掉。在能夠「眨眼」之前，團隊需要一位能自由提出異議的見證人。

Only the second moved when an old film projector flickered, and it stopped at a dark doorway. They compared the new account with what could be measured independently. It was an unglamorous exercise, made of repeated readings and questions that did not impress an audience. The result mattered precisely because it might prove the people asking those questions were wrong. The spare lamp stood ready, ordinary and more comforting than any spectacular projection. Zhou Lu disliked the word brutal in the summary because it hid a decision someone had made. Fang Nian proposed they choke on the thick dust; Zhou Lu wanted the risk written down first. They found a reference to composition near the damaged camera record, but its date was unreadable. The plan called for them to evaluate the new measurements, and Zhou Lu asked who would check the result.

只有第二道影子在老放映機閃爍時移動，並停在黑暗門口。他們把新說法與能獨立測量的事情比較。這不光鮮，只是重複讀值與不會取悅觀眾的提問。結果重要，正因為它也可能證明提問者自己錯了。備用燈準備就緒，平凡卻比任何壯觀投影更令人安心。周律不喜歡摘要中的「粗獷而堅固的」，因為它掩藏了某個人的決定。方念提議「阻斷」，周律希望先把風險寫清楚。他們在受損攝影紀錄旁找到提及「結構；組成」的紀錄，日期卻看不清。計畫要求他們「評估」，周律追問由誰查核結果。

They measured the movement from behind protective glass instead of trying to grab it. The most powerful person present could have ended the argument with an order. That might even have made the next hour more efficient. It would not have made the order wise. Instead, questions were allowed to remain questions long enough for another person to object. The city knew how to record people more easily than it knew how to protect them. Nobody disputed the gratitude; what troubled Zhou Lu was the silence around its origin. The team considered the grave a separate detail, not proof that the uncontrolled projection was safe. A witness mentioned intelligence during the review, so Fang Nian preserved the exact wording. The report labeled the risk mild, a judgment that needed checking before anyone acted.

兩人隔著防護玻璃量測移動，沒有伸手去抓。在場最有權力的人本可一聲令下結束爭論，甚至讓下一小時更有效率，但命令不會因此變明智。他們讓問題繼續存在，給另一個人提出異議的時間。城市擅長記錄人，卻不如保護人那麼熟練。沒人質疑「感激」，讓周律困擾的是它的來歷沒有人提。團隊把「嚴重的」視為獨立細節，不當成失控的投影安全的證據。見證人在覆核時提到「情報」，方念保留了原話。報告把風險標為「溫和的」，這項判斷須經核查才能行動。

The second shadow copied the outline of a missing technician's key card. They repeated the procedure with a different observer. It took longer and yielded a less dramatic account than the first version. That was an advantage: a discovery dependent on one person’s performance would have been too fragile to guide the next dangerous step. The streetlamp threw a long shadow across the wall before Zhou Lu even moved. The public account described the situation as mountainous, but Zhou Lu asked for the observation behind that claim. Nobody disputed the neglect; what troubled Zhou Lu was the silence around its origin. One instruction told the team to offend the people who trusted them; nobody could explain why it was urgent.

第二道影子模仿出失蹤技師門禁卡的輪廓。他們換一位觀察者重做程序。耗時更久，結果也不如第一版戲劇化。這反而有利：如果發現只依靠一個人的表現，就太脆弱，不足以指引下一個危險步驟。周律還沒動，路燈已在牆上投出長影。公開說法把情況形容為「山區的」，周律要求查看支撐判斷的觀察。沒人質疑「疏忽」，讓周律困擾的是它的來歷沒有人提。一項指令要求團隊「冒犯」，卻沒有人能解釋為何急迫。

After the argument, a long silence settled around the group. It was not agreement. People were calculating what the next attempt might take from them, and whether anyone would notice the loss. Their caution was an ordinary human response, not a flaw in their courage. Zhou Lu glanced toward Fang Nian and remembered the principle they had been arguing about: an image cannot replace responsibility. It sounded simple until it demanded another hour of work. A security screen could accuse a face without understanding whose hands controlled it. A witness mentioned philosopher during the review, so Fang Nian preserved the exact wording. They found a reference to reform near the damaged camera record, but its date was unreadable. The revenge appeared in the older camera record, and Zhou Lu asked why the newer version omitted it.

爭論後，長久沉默籠罩大家。那不是同意。人們正在估算下一次嘗試會奪走什麼、又會不會有人注意。謹慎是平凡的人性反應，不是勇氣的缺點。周律望向方念，想起兩人不斷爭論的原則：影像不能取代責任。話雖簡單，真正實踐又需要多一小時工作。保全螢幕能指控一張臉，卻不了解誰的手控制畫面。見證人在覆核時提到「哲學家」，方念保留了原話。他們在受損攝影紀錄旁找到提及「改革」的紀錄，日期卻看不清。舊攝影紀錄出現了「報復」，周律追問新版為何漏掉。

There would be easier stories to tell about this day. In those stories a brave person would find one clue, name one enemy and fix everything at once. The truth they carried was more difficult: a choice could be necessary and still leave work for the morning. Zhou Lu knew the next day would not begin from a clean slate. The camera record would preserve one more question, and Fang Nian would be there to challenge the first easy answer. Zhou Lu left the laboratory lights unchanged so the measurements could be repeated. Nobody disputed the tendency; what troubled Zhou Lu was the silence around its origin. The team considered the twig a separate detail, not proof that the uncontrolled projection was safe. A witness mentioned urge during the review, so Fang Nian preserved the exact wording.

關於今天，可以講得更簡單：勇敢的人找到一個線索，指出一個敵人，一切立刻解決。他們帶走的真相更難：一個選擇可以是必要的，也仍會留下明早要處理的工作。周律知道明天不會從零開始。攝影紀錄將保存又一個問題，方念也會在場質疑第一個容易的答案。周律保持實驗室燈光原狀，讓測量可重做。沒人質疑「傾向」，讓周律困擾的是它的來歷沒有人提。團隊把「樹枝」視為獨立細節，不當成失控的投影安全的證據。見證人在覆核時提到「催促」，方念保留了原話。
