A second recording showed the shadow cutting an emergency sign from a hospital corridor. The first sign of trouble was not a scream. It was a pause in an otherwise predictable routine, long enough for a careful person to notice. Others kept moving, and their movement made it tempting to do the same, but the pause deserved an explanation. The spare lamp stood ready, ordinary and more comforting than any spectacular projection. Nobody disputed the autograph; what troubled Zhou Lu was the silence around its origin. One instruction told the team to carve a careful mark; nobody could explain why it was urgent. A witness mentioned convenience during the review, so Fang Nian preserved the exact wording.

第二段錄影顯示影子剪下醫院走廊的緊急標示。第一個麻煩徵兆不是尖叫，而是原本可預期的流程忽然停頓，長到足以讓細心的人察覺。其他人繼續前進，令人忍不住跟上，可那次停頓需要解釋。備用燈準備就緒，平凡卻比任何壯觀投影更令人安心。沒人質疑「親筆簽名」，讓周律困擾的是它的來歷沒有人提。一項指令要求團隊「刻出」，卻沒有人能解釋為何急迫。見證人在覆核時提到「便利」，方念保留了原話。

Both crimes took place in spaces lit by the same experimental streetlamp company. They compared the new account with what could be measured independently. It was an unglamorous exercise, made of repeated readings and questions that did not impress an audience. The result mattered precisely because it might prove the people asking those questions were wrong. The city knew how to record people more easily than it knew how to protect them. They found a reference to cruelty near the damaged camera record, but its date was unreadable. The plan called for them to disguise the source of the message, and Zhou Lu asked who would check the result. The warning sounded elastic to some listeners, although the actual measurements remained uncertain. Zhou Lu disliked the word explosive in the summary because it hid a decision someone had made.

兩起案件都發生在同一家實驗路燈公司照明的場所。他們把新說法與能獨立測量的事情比較。這不光鮮，只是重複讀值與不會取悅觀眾的提問。結果重要，正因為它也可能證明提問者自己錯了。城市擅長記錄人，卻不如保護人那麼熟練。他們在受損攝影紀錄旁找到提及「殘忍」的紀錄，日期卻看不清。計畫要求他們「偽裝」，周律追問由誰查核結果。對部分聽眾而言，警告聽起來「有彈性的」，實際測量卻仍有疑點。周律不喜歡摘要中的「爆炸性的」，因為它掩藏了某個人的決定。

Zhou Lu reported the evidence to an investigator and offered an independently verified alibi. They argued quietly because shouting would only give the nearby cameras and strangers another story to tell. Beneath the disagreement lay a practical question: who would bear the consequences if the confident person turned out to be mistaken? No one volunteered to answer for somebody else. The streetlamp threw a long shadow across the wall before Zhou Lu even moved. A witness mentioned feedback during the review, so Fang Nian preserved the exact wording. They found a reference to immigrant near the damaged camera record, but its date was unreadable. The public account described the situation as initial, but Zhou Lu asked for the observation behind that claim. Nobody disputed the mischief; what troubled Zhou Lu was the silence around its origin.

周律向調查員報案，提供經獨立驗證的不在場證明。他們壓低聲音爭辯，因為大喊只會讓旁邊的鏡頭與陌生人又多一個故事。爭論底下是實際問題：自信的人若弄錯，誰承擔後果？沒有人願意代替別人回答。周律還沒動，路燈已在牆上投出長影。見證人在覆核時提到「回饋」，方念保留了原話。他們在受損攝影紀錄旁找到提及「移民」的紀錄，日期卻看不清。公開說法把情況形容為「最初的」，周律要求查看支撐判斷的觀察。沒人質疑「惡作劇」，讓周律困擾的是它的來歷沒有人提。

The investigator believed the footage had been altered, until a dark shape crossed her office wall. The first result was neither victory nor defeat. It removed one false assumption and exposed another problem that had been concealed beneath it. A witness asked whether the discovery could be trusted. The answer was that the test could be repeated, not that the investigators were special. A security screen could accuse a face without understanding whose hands controlled it. The team considered the paw a separate detail, not proof that the uncontrolled projection was safe. A witness mentioned prevention during the review, so Fang Nian preserved the exact wording. They found a reference to privilege near the damaged camera record, but its date was unreadable.

調查員原認為影像遭修改，直到黑影掠過她辦公室的牆。第一個結果既非勝利也非失敗。它排除一個錯誤假設，也露出下面藏著的另一個問題。見證人問發現能否相信；答案是測試可以重複，不是調查者有什麼特權。保全螢幕能指控一張臉，卻不了解誰的手控制畫面。團隊把「爪子」視為獨立細節，不當成失控的投影安全的證據。見證人在覆核時提到「預防」，方念保留了原話。他們在受損攝影紀錄旁找到提及「特權」的紀錄，日期卻看不清。

It would have been tempting to call the day a success and leave. But one person could not yet go home, and another still carried a cost nobody had agreed to repay. The group sat down again, this time without pretending that good intentions guaranteed a good outcome. Zhou Lu glanced toward Fang Nian and remembered the principle they had been arguing about: an image cannot replace responsibility. It sounded simple until it demanded another hour of work. Zhou Lu left the laboratory lights unchanged so the measurements could be repeated. The public account described the situation as prominent, but Zhou Lu asked for the observation behind that claim. Before they could prosper without taking unfair advantage, the group needed a witness who was free to object. The team considered the remark a separate detail, not proof that the uncontrolled projection was safe.

把今天稱為成功就離開，很誘人。但還有人不能回家，也有人承擔著無人答應補償的代價。大家再次坐下，這次不假裝好意必然帶來好結果。周律望向方念，想起兩人不斷爭論的原則：影像不能取代責任。話雖簡單，真正實踐又需要多一小時工作。周律保持實驗室燈光原狀，讓測量可重做。公開說法把情況形容為「醒目的」，周律要求查看支撐判斷的觀察。在能夠「繁榮」之前，團隊需要一位能自由提出異議的見證人。團隊把「評論；言語」視為獨立細節，不當成失控的投影安全的證據。

The question that followed was smaller and more frightening than the one they had brought to the room. It asked not whether they could win, but whether their answer would remain safe after they were gone. Nobody pretended to know yet, and that honesty held them together. Zhou Lu knew the next day would not begin from a clean slate. The camera record would preserve one more question, and Fang Nian would be there to challenge the first easy answer. Fang Nian rewound the footage without claiming that a camera could tell the whole truth. Fang Nian proposed they ruin the fragile evidence; Zhou Lu wanted the risk written down first. They found a reference to shift near the damaged camera record, but its date was unreadable. The timetable appeared in the older camera record, and Zhou Lu asked why the newer version omitted it.

後來的問題比進門時更小、也更可怕：不是能否獲勝，而是離開後答案是否仍安全。沒有人假裝已經知道，而這份誠實使大家繼續團結。周律知道明天不會從零開始。攝影紀錄將保存又一個問題，方念也會在場質疑第一個容易的答案。方念倒轉影像，沒有說攝影機能說出全部真相。方念提議「毀壞」，周律希望先把風險寫清楚。他們在受損攝影紀錄旁找到提及「輪班」的紀錄，日期卻看不清。舊攝影紀錄出現了「時刻表」，周律追問新版為何漏掉。
