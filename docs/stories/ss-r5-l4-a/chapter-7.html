沒有人的橋

The Bridge for Nobody

第九街區，不接受單人通關 · 第 7 / 25 章 · 3000–4000 · 英中對照 · 校訂中

用互動版查詞、朗讀與收藏

A loaded cart waited below the new bridge, its front wheels pressed against the first rise. Duan Ya crouched beside it and traced the height difference with her pencil while the judge praised the bridge's short line across the artificial gorge.

載貨車停在新橋下，前輪抵第一道高差。段芽蹲下用鉛筆比高度，評審還在讚美橋跨人工峽谷的短直線。

Accuracy mattered more than a competitor's bright diploma pinned to his jacket. Tang Yue told the group to analyze their notes, then adapt the plan to the actual width of the bridge rather than its attractive drawing.

正確性比競爭者夾在外套上的亮眼文憑更重要。唐悅請大家分析筆記，再依橋梁的真實寬度調整計畫，而不是相信漂亮圖畫。

The bridge entrance was high enough to block carts and bicycles.

橋的入口高得擋住手推車與腳踏車。

A survey marked the residents' ordinary transport habits in the margin. Artificial frost covered the rocks, and a toy helicopter circled overhead. A herd of model cattle blocked the lower path. Yu Qian said an intense rush could injure someone even in a controlled contest.

調查表的邊緣記著居民平常的移動習慣。人工霜覆蓋岩石，玩具直升機在上空盤旋。一群模型牛擋住下方道路。俞芊說，即使在受控競賽中，猛烈衝刺仍可能傷人。

Fang preferred strict compliance with the contest instructions; Duan Ya challenged their assumptions. Duan Ya tapped the loaded cart. "If the bridge is for everyone," she wrote, "everyone's ordinary things must fit too."

方澤傾向嚴守競賽指示，段芽則質疑那些假設。段芽敲敲裝滿物品的推車，寫道：「橋若給所有人用，他們每天帶的東西也得過得去。」

A cardboard mule pulled a small cart along the ramp. Mei and the plumber agreed on a mutual test, although Fang first opposed spending another ten minutes. The loaded cart climbed only twenty percent of the suggested slope.

紙板騾子拖著小推車走上坡道。梅嵐與水管工同意共同測試，儘管方澤起初反對多花十分鐘。載重車只能爬上建議坡度的百分之二十。

Mei asked the plumber to hold the cart on level ground before they attempted any slope. The load was ordinary market stock, not a special weight chosen to make the bridge fail. Fang checked the item list against the residents' survey and found that several families also moved bicycles along the old road. The bridge drawing showed walkers without any belongings. Tang asked the judge whether the proposed replacement covered those families or only the empty figures in the drawing. He told them to demonstrate the difference. They began below the rise, with the plumber holding a safety strap and nobody standing behind the cart. The wheels climbed a short distance, then stopped. Mei marked the position without pushing harder. Duan measured how much of the approach remained and sketched the sharp change in level. On the ground route, the same load could move steadily after the model cattle were removed by staff. Yu timed the journey from the same starting point and included the turn into the market. It was longer on the map and easier to complete. Fang had expected strict obedience to protect the team's standing; now he handed the judge both sheets and asked which users the bridge could genuinely serve. The judge checked the starting points before inviting public comments. Tang made space in their log for the district names those comments would bring.

試坡前，梅嵐請水管工在平地扶車。貨物是普通市場存貨，不是為讓橋失敗特製的重物。方澤把品項與居民調查比對，發現不少家庭也推腳踏車走舊路，橋圖卻只畫空手行人。唐悅問替代方案究竟包含那些家庭，還是只包含圖上的空人形。評審叫他們示範。他們從坡下開始，水管工握安全帶，車後不站人。輪子爬一小段就停，梅嵐不硬推，只標位置。段芽量剩餘坡段，畫突變高差。工作人員移開模型牛後，同樣貨物在地面路能穩穩移動。俞芊從同起點計時，包含轉入市場。圖上較長，卻較能完成。方澤原以為嚴守指示能保名次，現在把兩表交評審，問橋真正服務誰。評審先查起點，再邀公開意見，唐悅在共用簿留地方記它們帶來的街區名。

The recorded cart positions showed exactly where the bridge approach failed and where the ground route remained usable.

推車位置紀錄清楚顯示橋坡在哪失效、地面路哪裡仍可用。

A judge approved the measurement, not the proposed bridge, and invited public comments. The reduction in travel speed was slight, yet the change in safety was remarkable. They photographed the surroundings and argued that public welfare meant keeping this everyday route usable, not merely painting a grand bridge.

評審認可量測結果，卻不核准橋案，改要求公開意見。速度減少的幅度很小，安全性的改善卻很顯著。他們拍下周圍環境，主張公共福祉是維持日常路線可用，而非只把橋畫得氣派。

The comments revealed a district that officials had never visited.

意見揭露有個官員從未踏足的街區。
