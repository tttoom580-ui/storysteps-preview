空無一人的售票窗

The Empty Ticket Window

第九街區，不接受單人通關 · 第 9 / 25 章 · 3000–4000 · 英中對照 · 校訂中

用互動版查詞、朗讀與收藏

Fang found the carbon copy by following the line of rust beneath the ticket window. The paper was still held under the old frame, so he asked Mei to support the frame before anyone tried to draw it out.

方澤沿售票窗下的鏽線找到複寫紙，紙仍壓在舊框下。他先請梅嵐支框，沒讓人直接抽紙。

An association of former rail workers had stored an autobiography by a bald stationmaster. Tang Yue's curiosity arose because the book described a timetable revision, and a consequent loss of late-night service, in precise dates.

前鐵路工人協會保存了一本禿頭站長的自傳。唐悅因此產生好奇：書中以明確日期描述某次時刻表更改，以及隨之失去的夜間服務。

New machine labels had covered dates on the original paper notices.

新的機器標籤蓋住原本公告的日期。

Under a rusty frame, Fang discovered a carbon copy with a different approval number. A resident declared that she was dependent on the last train. Beside the closed fireplace, an old forecast predicted high passenger numbers; Fang explained that the prediction was not proof of where people had actually traveled.

方澤在生鏽框架下找到一張核准編號不同的複寫紙。居民宣告自己依賴末班列車。封住的壁爐旁，一份舊預測說乘客會很多；方澤解釋，預測不是人們實際去向的證據。

Tang Yue refused to accuse a named official without checking the copy's origin. "We know a label was replaced," Fang said. "We do not yet know who chose to replace it. Keep those facts separate."

唐悅拒絕在查明複本來源前指控具名官員。「我們知道標籤被換掉，」方澤說，「卻還不知道誰決定換。這兩件事必須分開。」

At a pretend café, a sign offered lobster pasta. The station machinery halted without warning, and an actor playing a minister demanded that customers move aside. Mei asked whether the scene was theater or an official test.

模擬咖啡館的牌子寫著龍蝦義大利麵。車站機械毫無預警停下，飾演部長的演員要求顧客讓路。梅嵐問，這究竟是戲劇還是正式測試。

The station attendant brought a shallow tray and watched them photograph the copy in place. Tang asked which office had stored it and whether the frame had been opened since the timetable changed. The attendant could answer the first question but not the second. Fang wrote both responses beside the image number. On the copy, a faint approval digit could be read in two ways. Duan held a blank card beside it to block the sunset lamp's glare, revealing a broken curve rather than a straight stroke. They compared it with the approval register instead of guessing from its resemblance to a familiar number. The register confirmed an earlier version with the late service still listed. Yu asked the resident who depended on that service whether she wanted her name included in their challenge. She preferred a description of the missed connection, and they used that. A mechanical announcement interrupted the comparison when the station display stopped. Mei checked with the attendant that the interruption was part of the stage, then waited for safe permission to resume. Their submission included the original image, the later notice, and a page identifying the remaining uncertainty about who changed it. When the premium-route pass was withdrawn, Tang requested a receipt for that decision as carefully as she had requested one for the evidence. The anonymous warning arrived folded inside the returned pass sleeve.

站務員拿淺盤，見證原位拍照。唐悅問哪個辦公室保管、時刻表變動後框是否開過；職員能答前者，不能答後者。方澤把兩項回應並列影號。複本一個淡數字有兩種讀法，段芽在旁舉白卡擋模擬夕陽反光，才看出斷曲線而非直筆。他們查核准簿，不靠猜像哪個熟悉數字。簿證實較早版本仍列晚班。俞芊問依靠晚車的居民是否願在異議書具名，她只想描述錯過轉乘，眾人照辦。車站展示忽然停機，廣播打斷比對。梅嵐先問職員是否是關卡安排，再等安全准許恢復。呈件包含原影像、新公告，以及仍不確定誰修改的說明。快速線通行證被收回時，唐悅對這項決定也像交證一樣要求收據。匿名警告摺在退還的證套裡。

Fang numbered the print comparisons and the missing-signature note before staff signed for custody of the copied evidence.

方澤替印痕比較和缺簽註記編號，再由職員簽收副本保管。

The station accepted their written challenge but removed their access to the premium route. A quotation from the original timetable helped restore the missing sequence of stops. The paper was rusty around its metal clip, but it showed no vacancy in the late shift. Fang said altering its date would violate the archive rule.

車站接受書面異議，卻取消他們進入快速路線的權限。原始時刻表中的引文幫助他們還原遺失的停靠順序。紙張邊的金屬夾生鏽，仍顯示夜班並無缺額。方澤說，更改日期就違反檔案規定。

Someone left an anonymous message claiming that the scoring office was not independent.

有人留下匿名訊息，指稱評分辦公室並不獨立。
