Chalk Before the Board

第九街區，不接受單人通關 · 第 10 / 25 章 · 3000–4000 · 英中對照 · 校訂中

The publisher's wall was barely wide enough for three journeys side by side. Duan measured it with chalk on her fingers while Tang Yue asked which details the participants had allowed the team to show in public.

出版人的牆剛夠並列三段旅程。段芽滿手粉筆量寬，唐悅則問參與者准許公開哪些細節。

The commission spoke of acceptance and an ambitious future in a cheerful atmosphere. Tang Yue asked for an authentic list of test journeys, because officials must classify real outcomes rather than applause from the crowd.

委員會在歡樂氣氛中談論接受結果與雄心勃勃的未來。唐悅要求提供真實的測試旅程清單，因為官員必須分類實際結果，而不是觀眾掌聲。

Official staff tried to remove the team's handwritten calculations.

工作人員試圖移除小隊的手寫計算。

A school publisher offered a blank wall for a public comparison chart. Two routes differed in length, but both had to protect each traveler's dignity. Fang tried not to embarrass the facility manager when he found a genuine mistake in the count, and let her inspect the figures beside him.

一位學校出版人願意提供空牆展示公開比較圖。兩條路長度不同，卻都必須維護每名旅客的尊嚴。方澤發現設施管理員確實算錯時，不願讓她難堪，便請她一起核對數字。

The group debated whether public criticism could embarrass those who made honest mistakes. An audience member called out, "So which team wins?" Tang Yue answered, "First, show me which families can actually arrive."

小隊討論公開批評是否會讓誠實犯錯者受辱。觀眾喊：「那到底哪隊贏？」唐悅回答：「先讓我看看哪些家庭真的能抵達。」

A poster used an offensive joke to promote a luxury option; the perfume shop next door had paid for the promotion. A local publisher offered them wall space for a clearer display without the insulting language.

海報用冒犯性的笑話宣傳豪華選項；旁邊香水店付了促銷費。當地出版商願讓小隊使用牆面，展示不含侮辱性語言的清楚資料。

One family agreed to share its waiting time but not the clinic it had visited. Fang replaced the destination name with a plain symbol and kept the original in the protected notebook. Mei drew the first journey as a continuous line, then stopped when Yu pointed out that the family had spent part of it standing still. They added separate blocks for walking, waiting, and asking for directions. A spectator who had praised the fastest team asked why one line was so much longer. Tang invited him to follow the blocks with his finger. He reached the wait at the lift and understood that the person had not simply chosen to walk slowly. The facility manager found a copied total that did not match the blocks. Fang checked it beside her and corrected the sum in chalk, leaving a small note showing what changed. That made the chart look less polished and easier to trust. When staff approached to remove the calculations, the publisher showed his permission for the wall. Tang moved the protected notebook into her bag and left the public figures visible. The petition began with a request to repeat the three journeys, rather than a demand to declare her team the winner. By the time the commissioners agreed to hear evidence, the chalk was wearing from visitors tracing the routes, and Mei had begun making a paper copy that would survive the evening cleaning.

一家同意分享等候時間，不公開所去診所。方澤把地名換普通符號，原件留受保護簿。梅嵐先畫連線，俞芊指出其中一段人其實站著，她才停筆，分畫步行、等候、問路區塊。一名讚最快隊的觀眾問為何有線特長，唐悅請他用手指跟著走。到電梯等待格，他明白那人並非選擇慢走。設施管理員發現抄來總數與區塊不合，方澤和她一起核，粉筆改算式，留註記說明變更。圖較不光鮮，卻更能信任。職員走來要移除計算，出版人出示牆面許可。唐悅收好私簿，留下公數。請願先要求重走三程，不要求宣布己隊冠軍。委員答應聽證時，粉筆已被訪客描路磨淡，梅嵐開始抄紙本，好在晚間清掃後仍保得住。

Their public chart displayed three test journeys with walking and waiting separated, while the private source pages stayed protected.

公圖分開三程的步行與等待，私來源頁仍受保護。

The audience signed an independent petition and the commissioners agreed to hear evidence. A refugee family in the simulation requested a rescue path for their grandmother. An actor's resignation letter hung beside a row of Christmas stockings. Tang Yue wanted to transform these personal stories into usable access records, not dramatic decorations.

觀眾簽署獨立請願，委員同意聽取證據。模擬情境中的難民家庭要求一條協助祖母撤離的路線。演員的辭職信掛在一排聖誕襪旁。唐悅希望把這些人的故事轉化為可用的通行紀錄，而非戲劇裝飾。

The city's contract required an evaluation before the hearing could change anything. The comparison chart earned the team its fourth review seal. Fang checked every stamp against the registration handbook; the hearing clause was real, but the office would hear them only after the next batch of tests. That delay angered Mei, who nevertheless agreed to preserve the evidence rather than stage an empty victory.

城市合約規定，聽證會若要改變結果，必須先進行評估。比較圖讓小隊取得第四枚複核印章。方澤依報名手冊核對每枚印章；聽證條款確實存在，但辦公室要求下批測試後才受理。這個拖延讓梅嵐生氣，她仍同意保存證據，而非宣告空洞的勝利。

Why are walking and waiting shown as separate blocks on the chart?

  1. They reveal where time is lost without assuming a traveler chose to walk slowly.
  2. They allow the team to publish the family's private clinic name.
  3. They ensure every journey appears to take exactly the same time.

  3. That the facility manager's private records be displayed on the wall.
