The Wind Tower

第九街區，不接受單人通關 · 第 11 / 25 章 · 3000–4000 · 英中對照 · 校訂中

Mei's sleeve snapped against the tower rail before the wind instrument reached its marked limit. She stepped back to the sheltered observation point, and Tang Yue asked the director to keep the upper walkway empty while they checked the readings.

風速儀還未到標線，梅嵐袖子已啪地撞欄。她退回遮蔽觀察點，唐悅請主任保持上層步道無人，先查讀數。

Fang would not accuse the contractor before checking the tower logs. Its director gave an assurance that the route was safe and began to boast about rising commerce; Tang Yue asked for consistent wind readings instead of a sales speech.

方澤在核實大樓日誌前不願指控承包商。主任保證路線安全，又開始誇耀商業繁榮；唐悅要求一致的風速讀數，而不是推銷演說。

The speed calculation ignored wind exposure along the upper walkway.

速度計算忽略上層步道的風力暴露。

An old manufacturing label supplied the tower's original safety limit. A builders' convention offered to contribute instruments for the test. One display described how a hurricane could expose weak walls, while another promoted hydrogen-powered equipment. Mei insisted that impressive technology did not replace basic safety measurements.

一張舊製造標籤提供大樓原始安全限制。建築業年會願捐助測試儀器。一面展示板說颶風可能暴露脆弱牆體，另一面介紹氫能設備。梅嵐堅持，再先進的科技也不能取代基本安全量測。

Fang said the evidence might discredit their own earlier estimates; Tang Yue agreed to publish it anyway. "The wind doesn't read the scoreboard," Mei told the tower manager. "Why should our report pretend it does?"

方澤說證據可能推翻他們之前的估算，唐悅仍同意公開。「風不會讀計分板，」梅嵐對大樓主管說，「我們的報告又為什麼要假裝它會？」

Calling residents ignorant would display the contractor's own ignorance, Yu Qian said. She wanted an informative notice that remained loyal to the people who had reported problems. A manufacture date on a railing revealed an earlier misfortune: one support had been replaced after a storm.

俞芊說，稱居民無知只會暴露承包商自己的無知。她要的是有資訊、有誠意且忠於通報者的公告。欄杆上的製造日期揭露一次過去事故：有根支柱曾在風災後更換。

Fang found that his earlier estimate had used a calm-weather journey from the sheltered side. He set it beside the tower log and circled the difference in location. The director offered to discard the old estimate quietly; Fang asked that it remain attached to the corrected report. Duan drew the observation points so later readers could see why the two sets differed. The technician showed them the instrument's scale and its last inspection date. From behind the barrier, they took readings at fixed intervals rather than walking into stronger gusts to prove a point. Yu wrote down when a visitor would have to stop and where that person could wait without blocking the next arrival. A stronger piece of glass on the neighboring tower did not tell them whether this walkway was safe, so Mei kept that comparison in a separate note. She checked the replaced rail support against its maintenance entry instead. By the final interval, the table showed several conditions under which their original travel time would be unrealistic. Tang put those conditions at the front of the report. The director could still argue for his route, but the conditions would travel with the numbers. At the review desk, the commission accepted the record without promising new scores. While Fang waited for its receipt, the technician asked whether he knew that the scoreboard's figures came from another company entirely.

方澤發現先前估算用的是無風時、遮蔽側旅程。他把估算與大樓簿並排，圈出位置差。主任願悄悄丟舊值，方澤要求附在修正報告。段芽畫觀測點，讓後來讀者知道兩組為何不同。技師展示儀器刻度與末次檢查日期。眾人在障礙後定時讀數，不走進強陣風證明勇敢。俞芊記何時訪客須停、哪裡可等而不擋來者。鄰棟強玻璃不能證明本步道安全，梅嵐把比較另註，再核換過的欄柱與維修項。末次讀數前，表已列出多種讓原旅時不實際的條件。唐悅把條件放報告首頁。主任仍可支持路線，條件卻須跟著數字。複核桌收紀錄，不保證新分數。方澤等收據時，技師問他可知計分板數值其實來自另一家公司。

The tower report identified each sheltered observation point and retained the earlier estimate beside the corrected wind readings.

高樓報告標每個遮蔽觀測點，讓舊估算與修正風讀值並存。

The commission admitted the record yet offered no assurance about future scoring. A troublesome elevator slowed the test, but a recovery crew offered a quiet room for relaxation between attempts. Tang Yue noted the resistance of the high windows and compared it with the neighboring skyscraper, where stronger glass had been installed.

委員會承認紀錄，卻不保證日後會修改評分。麻煩的電梯拖慢測試，復原小組提供安靜房間讓大家在測試之間休息。唐悅記下高窗承受的阻力，與鄰近裝設強化玻璃的摩天樓比較。

A technician revealed that the scoreboard received scores from a separate contractor.

技術員透露計分板的分數來自另一家承包商。

Why does Fang keep his earlier calm-weather estimate in the report?

  1. The director requires all strong-wind readings to be discarded.
  2. It shows why the corrected wind-related estimate differs.
  3. The earlier estimate proves the upper walkway is always safe.
