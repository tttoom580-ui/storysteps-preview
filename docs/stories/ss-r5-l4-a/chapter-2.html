不存在的簽名

The Signature That Wasn't There

第九街區，不接受單人通關 · 第 2 / 25 章 · 3000–4000 · 英中對照

用互動版查詞、朗讀與收藏

A delivery cart stood with one wheel against the square's high curb. Duan Ya found its route on the paper plan, then turned her tablet toward the guide; the same corner was blank on the digital map.

廣場上，一輛送貨車的車輪抵著高路緣。段芽在紙圖找到路線，再把平板轉向導覽員：數位地圖同一角卻是空白。

A collector asked for an autograph on the route card, but the five refused to carve a name into public property. The square advertised convenience for everyone; hiding the inaccessible curb behind painted flowers felt like cruelty disguised as decoration.

收藏者要求在路線卡上簽名，五人卻拒絕在公共財產上刻字。廣場宣稱提供人人便利；用彩繪花朵遮住難以通行的路緣，實際上是把殘酷偽裝成裝飾。

A printed timetable assumed every delivery rider could jump a high curb.

印製的時刻表假設送貨員都能跳上高路緣。

Duan Ya found that an earlier route included a ramp omitted from the digital copy. An elastic strap kept a fake explosive prop from falling onto a display. A recent immigrant who worked as a guide gave useful feedback: the initial map showed the ramp, but the updated one did not.

段芽發現舊路線有坡道，數位版本卻刪掉了。一條彈性帶固定著展示用的假爆裂物道具。擔任導覽員的新移民提出有用回饋：初版地圖畫了坡道，更新版本卻刪掉了。

Fang wanted to file a formal complaint, but Mei feared losing their only remaining hour. "We can ask the next office to sign," Fang suggested. Duan Ya shook her head. "A signature will help only if they check what disappeared."

方澤主張正式申訴，梅嵐卻怕耗掉僅剩的一小時。「我們可以請下一個辦公室簽核，」方澤提議。段芽搖頭：「簽名要有幫助，得先查清什麼消失了。」

A little mischief appeared on the floor: a painted paw print led to a locked office. A prevention notice said that only prominent guests enjoyed the privilege of entering, although the passage was marked public on the printed plan.

地板上有個惡作劇般的爪印圖案，通往上鎖辦公室。預防事故的告示聲稱只有知名貴賓享有進入特權，但紙圖明明把通道標成公共空間。

The guide could not authorize a correction, but she knew where the previous plan was kept. Fang requested its date before asking who had removed the ramp. The older sheet bore an approval stamp and a space for a later check that nobody had signed. Mei wanted to begin the walk while Fang handled the forms. Tang gave them separate tasks within sight of one another: Fang would copy the approval details, and Mei would inspect the ramp without moving the loaded cart. Its surface was sound, but a display stand blocked the lower landing. They asked the attendant to move the stand and noted its former position on Duan's sketch. Only then did Yu try the route with the cart empty, keeping a guide at the far end. Fang timed the passage from the shop door rather than from the top of the curb. Starting at the curb would have hidden the very delay they were investigating. When the station accepted their photographs, it stamped the submission but left the ranking unchanged. Tang asked for the reason in the shared log. The attendant wrote that an unsigned update needed review by another office. Nobody promised the missing signature could be obtained before the hour ended. Outside, the rival captain was already waiting with a different offer, and Fang kept the original plan folded inside their common notebook.

導覽員無權改圖，卻知道舊圖放哪裡。方澤先問日期，沒有先問誰刪坡道。舊頁有核准章，後續檢查欄卻沒人簽。梅嵐想趁方澤辦表單先走路線，唐悅讓兩人在彼此視線內分工：方澤抄核准資料，梅嵐查看坡道，先不動載貨車。坡面良好，下端平臺卻被展示架擋住。他們請管理員移架，段芽在圖上記原位，俞芊才推空車試走，遠端有導覽員等候。方澤從店門起算，不從路緣上方算，免得藏掉正在查的延誤。關卡收照蓋收件章，排名不變，唐悅要求把理由寫進共用簿。職員寫下：未簽核更新須另一辦公室審查。沒人保證能在這一小時結束前補簽。外頭競爭隊長已等著提出另一交易，方澤把原圖摺入共用筆記。

Their submission preserved the photographed paper plan, the measured ramp, and the request for a dated sign-off.

呈件保留紙圖照片、坡道量測，以及要求註日期簽核的申請。

The station accepted the evidence but refused to alter the ranking immediately. A shopkeeper remarked that businesses could not prosper if every delivery shift ended in a detour. Fang replied that a broken timetable could ruin a business too, and wrote down the actual times instead of guessing.

關卡收下證據，卻拒絕立即修正排名。店主評論說，每一班送貨都繞路，商家便難以興盛。方澤回答，錯誤的時刻表同樣會毀掉生意，於是記下每班實際時間，不再猜測。

A rival team offered to trade a signature for a shortcut, raising an ethical question.

競爭隊伍提議用簽名交換捷徑，引出新的倫理問題。
