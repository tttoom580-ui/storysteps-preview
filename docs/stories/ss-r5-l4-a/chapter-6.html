兩份時刻表的節慶

The Festival of Two Timetables

第九街區，不接受單人通關 · 第 6 / 25 章 · 3000–4000 · 英中對照 · 校訂中

用互動版查詞、朗讀與收藏

The festival's last performance was still playing when Mei Lan began counting stacked chairs. The conductor showed her a work log with one time for the final bow and another for the moment the cleaning crew could actually leave.

節慶最後演出還在進行，梅嵐已開始數疊好的椅子。指揮給她看工作簿：謝幕一個時間，清潔工真正能離開又是另一個。

An anniversary campaign filled the festival street with flags. Its coarse paper banners were falling apart, and the organizers tried to assure the teams that the damage would not complicate the morning route.

週年紀念宣傳活動讓節慶街掛滿旗幟。粗糙紙製布條已開始破損，主辦者仍向小隊保證，損壞不會使早晨路線更複雜。

The official schedule gave visiting performers priority over local staff.

官方時刻表讓外來表演者優先於在地工作者。

A conductor's work log revealed that cleaners needed an extra late bus. The orchestra conductor had tied a cord around his notebook. Mei admired his courageous decision to remain after the show; together they would devise a diligent method for counting the late workers who still needed transport.

指揮員的工作紀錄顯示清潔人員需要一班末班車。樂團指揮用細繩綁住筆記本。梅嵐佩服他演出後仍留下的勇敢決定；他們將一起設計勤勉細緻的方法，統計還需要交通工具的晚班工人。

Mei worried the added bus would consume their budget; Yu Qian asked for a fairer estimate. "A parade ends when the guests leave," Mei said. The conductor corrected her: "For the workers, it ends when the chairs are stacked."

梅嵐擔憂加班車會耗光預算，俞芊要求更公平的估算。「遊行在賓客離開時結束，」梅嵐說。指揮糾正她：「對工作人員來說，要等椅子疊好才結束。」

A display about the old dynasty used foam bricks and played bells at a fixed frequency. Fang's evaluation found that its timetable was an imitation of a historical schedule, not the operating plan for today's staff.

舊王朝展覽以泡棉磚搭景，按固定頻率敲鐘。方澤評估後發現，展示的時刻表只是模仿歷史班次，並非今日員工真正使用的計畫。

Fang drew a line between those times and asked how many workers remained after the visitors' bus departed. The conductor counted musicians first, then stopped when a cleaner pointed to the temporary kitchens. Cooks and stall assistants were still working there. Yu walked to the kitchen entrance with the shift supervisor and asked for totals, leaving personal names out of the route sheet. Tang compared the totals with the bus's seats and the time needed to reach the stop. One worker used a cart to collect cups, so Mei checked where that cart could be stored before boarding. Simply adding a departure time would not help if the only storage gate had already closed. With the organizer's permission, they moved the staff waiting point beside the open service yard. Duan drew a sign showing the new stop and the last boarding time. The conductor asked whether the sign meant the bus would wait indefinitely. Tang added a clear limit and a number for reporting delays. Their trial included the last worker's walk from the kitchen, rather than ending when the first musician sat down. The loop took longer than the public schedule allowed and carried the staff home. At the review table, Mei put the lost points beside the boarding totals. The rival captain approached after the announcement, looking at the totals rather than the bright score above them.

方澤連兩時刻，問賓客車離開後還有多少員工。指揮先數樂手，清潔工卻指臨時廚房，廚師與攤位助手仍在工作。俞芊和班長走到廚房入口，只問總數，不把私名寫入路線表。唐悅比對座位與走到車站所需時間。一名工人用車收杯，梅嵐先查上車前哪裡能存車；若唯一存放門已關，只加發車時間也無用。經主辦者許可，他們把等候點移到開著的服務院旁。段芽畫新站與最後上車時間。指揮問車是否會無限等候，唐悅加明確限時與通報延誤電話。試走從最後工人離廚房算，不在第一位樂手坐下就結束。循環比公開表長，卻能載員工回家。梅嵐在複核桌把失分與上車總數並列。公告後，競爭隊長走來，看的是總數，不是上方亮分數。

The loop drawing now included boarding times for the last workers, with a clear limit on how long the bus would wait.

循環圖納入最後工人上車時間，也標車輛等待的明確上限。

The loop worked but cost points because the score ignored workers' journey home. The volunteer labor office offered water containing mineral salts to the workers. Yu Qian warned that needy families could not rely on good intentions alone; their psychology of travel reflected whether the last bus would actually arrive.

新路線可行，卻因評分忽略工人的返家路程而扣分。志工勞務辦公室為工人提供含礦物質的飲水。俞芊提醒，困頓家庭不能只靠善意生活；他們對交通的心理感受，反映末班車究竟會不會來。

A rival captain quietly asked to compare notes rather than race them.

競爭隊長私下要求交換紀錄，而不是繼續比快。
