最慢的鐘

The Slowest Clock

第九街區，不接受單人通關 · 第 5 / 25 章 · 3000–4000 · 英中對照

The city replica brightened around a mock transport terminal beneath a glass roof, and Tang Yue counted the familiar faces before looking at the puzzle. The assignment asked them to get five passengers across the terminal before its paired clocks reach zero. Their five-person entry was legal only while they kept one common log, and the exit required every member to sign. The rule was simple enough to understand; what interested Tang Yue was how easily people might forget it under pressure. The transport agency asked the teams to adopt a new queuing rule. Tang Yue noticed that the behavior of the visitors changed whenever the host praised the contest's 'congress of champions.' She cherished the chance to show a better alternative.

城市模型在玻璃屋頂下的模擬轉運站四周亮起，唐悅先點清熟悉的隊友才去看謎題。這一關要求他們在雙時鐘歸零前讓五位乘客穿越轉運站。五人必須共用一本紀錄簿，出口也得由每名成員簽字；規則並不難懂，唐悅在意的是，人們在壓力下多容易忘記它。交通機構要求隊伍採用新的排隊規則。唐悅注意到主持人稱頌「冠軍大會」時，訪客的行為就改變了。她珍惜這次展示替代方案的機會。

The first difficulty was not a locked door but a claim that looked reasonable at first glance: one clock measures vehicles while the other measures actual walking time. Fang Ze, the team’s auditor, copied the exact wording rather than changing it to suit their theory. Mei Lan, who built stage scenery for a living, examined the actual surface. Yu Qian, a nursing assistant, waited beside the slowest walker and checked whether there was room to turn.

第一個困難不是上鎖的門，而是一項乍看合理的說法：一座鐘測車輛，另一座鐘測真實步行時間。擔任稽核員的方澤逐字抄下原文，沒有為配合自己的理論擅改內容。以製作舞臺布景為業的梅嵐檢查實際地面；護理助理俞芊陪著走得最慢的人，確認有沒有轉身空間。

Near the next marker, something did not match the official design: a child-sized route uses fewer steps but requires climbing a steep stair. Fang noted the difference in the left column of the evidence sheet, leaving the right column blank for an independent check. Mei wanted to touch the equipment immediately, but Tang Yue asked her to wait for permission. A useful discovery could become useless if the team damaged the very object it intended to measure. The decoration above the desk could not silence Fang's conscience. He refused to discourage the slowest participant and argued that the fundamental purpose of the test was safe access. Yu Qian said it would horrify the families to learn they were scored as obstacles.

下一個記號附近，有件事和官方設計不相符：兒童尺寸的路少走幾步，卻必須爬陡梯。方澤把差異寫在證據表左欄，右欄留待獨立複核。梅嵐想立刻碰設備，唐悅請她先等許可。有用的發現若在測量前就把物件弄壞，也會失去效力。桌上方的裝飾壓不住方澤的良心。他拒絕讓最慢的參與者洩氣，指出測試的根本目的應是安全通行。俞芊說，家庭若得知自己被評成障礙，必會感到驚恐。

It was not an argument between good people and bad people. The disagreement inside the team was practical: the team debates whether scoring speed can justify excluding someone. Tang Yue let both sides finish. A quick choice could protect their place in the contest, while a careful choice could protect people who would use the real streets after the contest ended. Fang wrote the competing assumptions in different boxes, and Yu Qian insisted that the group state what evidence would change their minds. "I thought the first clock was the rule," Fang admitted. "It is a rule," Tang Yue said, "but not the whole journey."

這不是好人與壞人的爭執。小隊內的分歧很實際：小隊討論評分快速是否能合理化排除他人。唐悅讓雙方把話說完。快速選擇可能保住競賽名次，謹慎選擇則可能保護比賽結束後真正使用道路的人。方澤把不同假設填進分開的格子，俞芊堅持大家先說清楚，什麼證據會改變自己的看法。「我以為第一座鐘就是規則，」方澤承認。「它是一部分規則，」唐悅說，「卻不是整段旅程。」

Fang drew three columns across a fresh sheet: prediction, observation, and uncertainty. The stage was supposed to help them get five passengers across the terminal before its paired clocks reach zero, yet the published score showed only the fastest number. Duan Ya checked the measurements, and Mei named the practical limits of their tools. They needed enough detail for a stranger to repeat the experiment without relying on the original team. An influential observer watched every interaction as the team tested the entrance. The outcome showed that the primitive counting system recorded feet crossing a line, not people safely reaching the reception desk.

方澤在新紙張上畫出三欄：預測、觀察、不確定性。這一關原本要讓他們在雙時鐘歸零前讓五位乘客穿越轉運站，公布的分數卻只有最快數字。段芽核對量測，梅嵐指出工具的實際限制。他們需要留下足夠細節，讓陌生人不靠原隊伍也能重做實驗。一位有影響力的觀察員注視小隊測試入口時的每次互動。結果顯示，原始的計數系統記錄的是踏過線的腳步，不是安全抵達接待處的人。

Their test was deliberately ordinary: they divide tasks, carry signs to the platform, and time each person fairly. Tang Yue counted the steps without pushing anyone to move faster. When the figures differed, Fang left both values on the page until they understood why. Mei kept the equipment in place; Yu Qian checked that nobody felt forced to continue. The five knew that a public plan built on coercion would still be a bad plan, however handsome its final diagram appeared.

他們刻意採用平凡的測試方法：他們分工搬指示牌到月臺，公平量測每人的時間。唐悅計算步數，沒有催促任何人加速。兩組數值不同時，方澤先把兩者都保留在紙上，直到查出原因。梅嵐固定設備，俞芊確認沒有人被迫繼續。五人明白，靠強迫建立的公共方案仍是壞方案，不管最後圖畫得多漂亮。

After a second check, the outcome stood: all passengers arrive; they earn a third review seal and unlock an audit request. Fang circled the measured facts and drew a line beneath every guess that still needed testing. Tang Yue thought this might be the contest’s unplanned achievement. The ranking could tell them which team was fastest today, but it could not decide by itself what every street should look like tomorrow. A painted robber chased a witch across a backdrop of mountain scenery. The children enjoyed the scene, but Tang Yue called it shameful that the contest used imaginary terror to distract from an ordinary broken timetable.

第二次核對後，結果依舊：乘客全部到達，取得第三枚複核印章，解鎖稽核申請權。方澤圈起量得的事實，在仍需驗證的猜測下畫線。唐悅認為，這也許是競賽未料到的成果：排名能說明今天誰最快，卻不能獨自決定明天每條街應該長什麼樣子。彩繪強盜在山景布幕上追趕女巫。孩子們喜歡這一幕，唐悅卻認為競賽用虛構恐懼轉移人們對錯誤時刻表的注意，十分可恥。

As they prepared to leave, one final detail refused to fit the clean report: the board announces that audit requests must wait until the final round. Tang Yue entered it beneath a question mark rather than an accusation. She had learned to distrust easy endings, especially ones that arrived before the people most affected had spoken. They had not conquered the city model; they had made one part of it less mysterious, and that would have to be enough for tonight. The team now held three review seals. The handbook required four for a hearing, and the fourth could not be awarded for mere protest. Tang Yue put the stamps beside the measured time sheets. The difference between a public claim and a tested claim was becoming a visible pile of paper.

準備離開時，最後一項細節仍無法納入整齊的報告：公告板卻說複核申請要等最後一輪才處理。唐悅把它寫在問號下，而不是直接當成指控。她已學會懷疑太容易的結局，尤其是受影響最大的人尚未開口時。他們沒有征服城市模型，只讓其中一部分不再神祕；今晚先做到這樣就夠了。小隊如今握有三枚複核印章。手冊規定四枚才能開聽證，第四枚不能只靠抗議取得。唐悅把印章放在時間測量表旁。公眾聲稱與受驗證聲稱的差別，漸漸成為一疊看得見的紙。
