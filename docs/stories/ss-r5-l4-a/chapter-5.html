最慢的鐘

The Slowest Clock

第九街區，不接受單人通關 · 第 5 / 25 章 · 3000–4000 · 英中對照

用互動版查詞、朗讀與收藏

Two clocks hung above the terminal, but their hands moved when different things happened. Fang watched the first jump as a model vehicle crossed a sensor; the second kept counting while the passengers were still searching for the reception desk.

轉運站上方掛兩座鐘，卻因不同事件而動。方澤看第一座在模型車越過感測器時跳動；乘客還找接待處，第二座仍在計時。

The transport agency asked the teams to adopt a new queuing rule. Tang Yue noticed that the behavior of the visitors changed whenever the host praised the contest's 'congress of champions.' She cherished the chance to show a better alternative.

交通機構要求隊伍採用新的排隊規則。唐悅注意到主持人稱頌「冠軍大會」時，訪客的行為就改變了。她珍惜這次展示替代方案的機會。

One clock measured vehicles while the other measured actual walking time.

一座鐘測車輛，另一座鐘測真實步行時間。

A child-sized route used fewer steps but required climbing a steep stair. The decoration above the desk could not silence Fang's conscience. He refused to discourage the slowest participant and argued that the fundamental purpose of the test was safe access. Yu Qian said it would horrify the families to learn they were scored as obstacles.

兒童尺寸的路少走幾步，卻必須爬陡梯。桌上方的裝飾壓不住方澤的良心。他拒絕讓最慢的參與者洩氣，指出測試的根本目的應是安全通行。俞芊說，家庭若得知自己被評成障礙，必會感到驚恐。

The team debated whether scoring speed could justify excluding someone. "I thought the first clock was the rule," Fang admitted. "It is a rule," Tang Yue said, "but not the whole journey."

小隊討論評分快速是否能合理化排除他人。「我以為第一座鐘就是規則，」方澤承認。「它是一部分規則，」唐悅說，「卻不是整段旅程。」

An influential observer watched every interaction as the team tested the entrance. The outcome showed that the primitive counting system recorded feet crossing a line, not people safely reaching the reception desk.

一位有影響力的觀察員注視小隊測試入口時的每次互動。結果顯示，原始的計數系統記錄的是踏過線的腳步，不是安全抵達接待處的人。

Tang asked the attendant to explain the start and finish conditions before they tried the route. He pointed to a line on the floor and then to the far platform, never mentioning the desk. Yu asked a passenger where she thought the journey ended. She pointed at the desk because that was where she could ask for the next connection. Fang added that answer to the timing sheet. On the short stair, Duan found a sign low enough for a child to read but a turn too tight for Mei's equipment case. They left the case closed and measured its outline rather than forcing it onto the steps. Mei offered to carry the signs by the longer line while Tang accompanied the last passenger. Fang recorded each arrival separately. The floor counter clicked twice when one walker stepped back to retrieve a dropped card. Its total now exceeded the number of passengers, though nobody extra had arrived. The attendant repeated the movement himself and confirmed the error. At the desk, Tang compared the actual arrivals with both clocks and the sensor total. Their third seal recognized the discrepancy, not a miraculous new speed. Mei wanted to celebrate the open audit request immediately, but Duan pointed to the four-seal condition in the handbook. Tang laid the three seals in order and asked Fang to record what they had earned and what they still needed.

唐悅先請職員說明起終點。他指地線與遠月臺，沒提接待處。俞芊問乘客認為旅程在哪結束，她指接待處，因那裡能問下一段轉乘。方澤把回答寫進計時表。短梯邊，段芽找到孩子看得到的低牌，轉彎卻窄到梅嵐工具箱過不去。他們不硬搬，只量關著的箱子輪廓。梅嵐沿長路搬牌，唐悅陪最後乘客，方澤逐人記抵達。有人退一步撿卡，地板計數器響兩次，總數超過人數，卻沒新人到。職員親自重做，證實錯誤。到接待處，唐悅把真抵達人數與兩鐘、感測總數比較。第三枚章認可的是差異，不是神奇速度。梅嵐想立刻慶祝稽核申請解鎖，段芽卻指手冊四章條件。唐悅排好三章，請方澤記清已取得與仍需取得的權利。

The timing sheet recorded every passenger's arrival and the movement that had made the floor counter register twice.

計時表記每位乘客抵達，也記造成地板計數器重算的動作。

All passengers arrived; they earned a third review seal and unlocked an audit request. A painted robber chased a witch across a backdrop of mountain scenery. The children enjoyed the scene, but Tang Yue called it shameful that the contest used imaginary terror to distract from an ordinary broken timetable.

乘客全部到達，取得第三枚複核印章，解鎖稽核申請權。彩繪強盜在山景布幕上追趕女巫。孩子們喜歡這一幕，唐悅卻認為競賽用虛構恐懼轉移人們對錯誤時刻表的注意，十分可恥。

The board announced that audit requests must wait until the final round. The team now held three review seals. The handbook required four for a hearing, and the fourth could not be awarded for mere protest. Tang Yue put the stamps beside the measured time sheets. The difference between a public claim and a tested claim was becoming a visible pile of paper.

公告板卻說複核申請要等最後一輪才處理。小隊如今握有三枚複核印章。手冊規定四枚才能開聽證，第四枚不能只靠抗議取得。唐悅把印章放在時間測量表旁。公眾聲稱與受驗證聲稱的差別，漸漸成為一疊看得見的紙。
