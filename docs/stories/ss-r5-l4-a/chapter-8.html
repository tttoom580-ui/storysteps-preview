The Glass Apartment

第九街區，不接受單人通關 · 第 8 / 25 章 · 3000–4000 · 英中對照 · 校訂中

Behind the apartment lobby's shining doors, a stroller wheel caught in the closing gap. Yu Qian stopped the empty stroller with her foot and asked the manager to keep the entrance open while the family explained where they needed to go.

公寓大廳亮門後，嬰兒車輪卡進正在關閉的縫。俞芊用腳擋住空車，請管理員先開著入口，讓家庭說要去哪裡。

A portrait of the building owner's ancestor hung in the lobby. Contrary to the brochure, the entrance was not a fine piece of craft: a heavy door could crush a stroller wheel. Yu Qian asked whether calling it defensible made it safe.

大廳掛著屋主祖先的肖像。與手冊說法相反，入口不是精良工藝：沉重的門可能壓壞嬰兒車輪。俞芊問，稱它合理就代表安全嗎？

The published plan assumed every visitor could carry a child up the stairs.

公布的圖假設所有訪客都能抱孩子上樓。

A tenant's handwritten note identified a working service lift behind the kitchen. A distinguished architect had signed the old plan. Staff offered to equip the team with a hose and ladder, but Duan Ya felt guilt about testing without permission. The architect's handwriting on the approved sheet clearly required an accessible lift.

房客的手寫便條指出廚房後方有一部可運作的服務電梯。一位知名建築師曾在舊圖簽名。人員願提供水管和梯子等裝備，段芽卻因未先取得許可而感到不安。核准表上建築師的筆跡清楚要求設置無障礙電梯。

Yu Qian insisted on asking residents what they needed instead of assigning a universal route. "Ask the families," Yu Qian said. "A drawing cannot tell us whether waiting twenty minutes with a child feels safe."

俞芊堅持詢問住戶需求，而非替所有人安排單一路線。「問問家庭吧，」俞芊說，「一張圖不會告訴我們，帶著孩子等二十分鐘是否安心。」

The doors were identical, and an invitation to launch a guided tour did not involve actual residents. Mei called the invitation lousy, while Fang apologized for having misunderstood the tenant's concerns about waiting in the corridor.

每扇門都一模一樣。邀請書要他們推出導覽，卻沒有讓真正住戶參與。梅嵐認為邀請很糟，方澤則為自己誤解住戶對走廊等待的擔憂道歉。

The family wanted the nursery first and the clinic afterward. The printed tour route led to the clinic and then back upstairs, with no place to leave the stroller. Duan copied the two destinations in the family's chosen order. The tenant's note sent them toward the kitchen, where a service lift was waiting behind a plain door. Mei checked its posted capacity with the technician before putting anything inside. The group used an empty stroller and bags to test the space, leaving the child with a parent in the lobby. Fang timed the doors opening, loading, travel, and return; he had first planned to time only the upward journey. When a second stroller reached the landing, they discovered why that would be misleading. One family would have to wait through another cycle. Yu asked the parents where that wait would feel manageable. They chose a bench visible from the nursery desk rather than a blank corridor by the kitchen. Tang requested permission to mark the waiting point on the public map. The manager offered a guided escort instead, but the families wanted a route they could use without arranging a tour. Duan added the lift's capacity and the expected cycle time in large writing. Their map now showed a usable journey with a real wait, rather than a promise that every door would open immediately for every visitor.

他們想先托育、再診所，印好的導覽線卻先診所再折返樓上，沒有存車處。段芽按家庭選的順序記目的地。房客便條把他們引向廚房，素門後有服務電梯。梅嵐先和技師查標示載量，才放東西。小隊用空車與袋測空間，孩子留大廳由家長照顧。方澤量開門、裝載、行駛與回程；原本只想量上行，第二輛車來時才明白那會誤導：另一家須再等一輪。俞芊問哪裡等比較能接受，家長選托育櫃檯看得到的長椅，不選廚房旁空走廊。唐悅請准在公共圖標等待點。管理員提議派導覽陪同，家庭要的卻是不必預約導覽也能走的路。段芽用大字寫載量與預期週期。地圖呈現可行但需等候的旅程，不再承諾每扇門都會立即為每位來客開。

Duan's revised family route included permission for the lift test, its limited capacity, and the full cycle time.

段芽的家庭修正版包含電梯試用許可、有限載量與完整週期。

Their plan reduced strain but required honest warnings about waiting times. A notice about pregnancy care listed clinic hours beside a difficult riddle for visitors. Yu Qian made a simple suggestion: ask the technician to measure the lift cycle. With one stroke of her pencil, Duan Ya replaced the impossible shortcut on their map.

規劃降低負擔，卻必須誠實警示等候時間。孕期照護公告寫著診所開放時間，旁邊竟設有難解謎題。俞芊提出簡單建議：請技術員量測電梯往返時間。段芽一筆劃掉地圖上不可行的捷徑。

The tower manager claimed a perfect score despite not meeting a single resident.

大樓管理員宣稱自己得到滿分，卻沒和一位住戶談過話。
