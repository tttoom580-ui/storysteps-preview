Five Names on One Gate

第九街區，不接受單人通關 · 第 1 / 25 章 · 3000–4000 · 英中對照

At registration, Tang Yue wrote five names in the gate ledger and passed the pen along. The handbook required a shared log and every member's signature at the exit; its smaller review desk also accepted evidence of faulty instructions.

報名時，唐悅在門口名冊寫下五人姓名，再傳筆。手冊要求共用紀錄簿，出口須全員簽字；旁邊較小的複核桌也收指示錯誤的證據。

Outside the assembly hall, someone had used acid to strip old glue from a notice and left a broom across the walkway. Mei said they should construct a clear entrance, and Tang Yue asked the staff to cooperate rather than blame one another.

集會廳外，有人用酸液除去公告上的舊膠，卻把掃帚橫放在人行道上。梅嵐說應先建出暢通入口；唐悅請工作人員合作，而不是互相指責。

The fastest-looking street ended at a gate built for one person.

看似最快的街道，盡頭卻是只容一人通行的門。

A maintenance drawing showed a second gate with five signature boxes. The copper ticket counter carried a cartoon of a coward who fled every challenge. A guide tried to entertain the visitors with it, but Tang Yue watched his serious facial expression when he noticed the narrow door.

維修圖上畫著第二道門，旁邊有五格簽名欄。銅製售票櫃檯上畫著總是逃避挑戰的懦夫。導覽員想藉此娛樂觀眾；唐悅卻注意到他發現窄門時嚴肅的臉部表情。

Mei wanted to test the tempting short street, while Yu Qian asked who would be left behind. "One person could pass that gate," Mei said. Yu Qian pointed at the five empty boxes. "Then why are there five spaces for names?"

梅嵐想測試誘人的捷徑，俞芊則問誰會被落下。「一個人就能過那道門，」梅嵐說。俞芊指著五個空格：「那為什麼要留五個簽名欄？」

A ministry clerk warned that players must not interfere with the simulated opera performance. Yet the supposedly persuasive instructions failed to describe the strange phenomenon: two identical arrows pointed toward different exits.

一名主管部門職員警告參賽者不得干擾模擬歌劇演出。然而那份看似有說服力的指示，卻無法解釋奇怪現象：兩支相同箭頭竟指向不同出口。

Fang Ze, who audited accounts for a living, asked whether a team could use the wider gate without buying a faster ticket. The clerk pointed to an unfilled box on the maintenance drawing. Mei Lan set down her scenery tools and measured the space between its posts. Yu Qian, a nursing assistant, asked everyone to stand where they could turn without bumping one another. Duan Ya drew the arrangement in her architecture notebook, leaving room beside each figure to show the bags they carried. Their first attempt stopped when Fang's satchel caught the ticket counter. He had measured his own shoulders and forgotten the things that traveled with him. Mei shifted the queue line instead of pulling him through. The clerk then brought the original gate form, which did indeed contain five spaces. Tang asked him to watch a complete group pass before stamping anything. The short street saved steps for the first person, but the others had to wait behind its narrow turn. At the wider gate, each member could see who was still approaching. Duan marked the waiting space on her drawing while Fang recorded the full group time. When they returned to the clerk, they brought both measurements and the folded maintenance sheet. Tang could not yet explain the contest's ranking, but she could show exactly which entrance would let them finish the first stage together.

以稽核帳目為業的方澤問，不買快票能否走寬門。書吏指維修圖的空格。梅嵐放下布景工具，量兩門柱間距；護理助理俞芊請大家站到能轉身、不撞人的位置。段芽在建築筆記畫出人形，旁邊留出隨身袋的空間。第一次嘗試，方澤的包勾住櫃檯，只好停下。他量了肩寬，卻忘了隨身物。梅嵐移排隊線，沒有硬拉他過門。書吏拿來原門表，果然有五格。唐悅請他先看完整隊伍通過再蓋章。短街讓第一人少走幾步，其他人卻卡在窄彎後等待。寬門那邊，每人都看得見尚未到的隊友。段芽在圖上標等待空間，方澤記全隊時間。回複核桌時，他們帶著兩組數值和摺好的維修圖。唐悅還不能解釋排名，卻能確切指出哪個入口能讓他們一起完成第一關。

Duan kept both routes on the same sheet, with the whole-group timing written below each entrance.

段芽把兩條路留同頁，在各入口下寫全組計時。

All five signed the wider exit and earned a review seal instead of a speed bonus. They drew the safer route beneath a shady awning, ignoring a slogan that promised victory to the fastest team. Duan Ya checked the map's actual usage with a youthful volunteer, who admitted it had never been tested by a whole group.

五人簽下較寬出口，取得一枚複核印章，而非速度獎勵。他們在陰暗遮棚下畫出較安全的路線，不理會宣稱最快者必勝的標語。段芽和年輕志工核對地圖的實際用途，志工承認從未讓完整隊伍測試。

The public screen ranked their team last even though nobody had been abandoned.

公開螢幕把他們排在最後，儘管沒有人被遺下。
