霧中的人行橋

The Footbridge and the Fog

The city replica brightened around a painted mountain street above the city model, and Tang Yue counted the familiar faces before looking at the puzzle. The assignment asked them to compare a narrow bridge and a longer protected path. Their five-person entry was legal only while they kept one common log, and the exit required every member to sign. The rule was simple enough to understand; what interested Tang Yue was how easily people might forget it under pressure. An accidental opening in the painted barrier revealed an old stair. Fang blinked at the steep drop. The warning used the brutal word 'failure' for anyone who slowed down, and thick theatrical fog made Duan Ya choke.

城市模型在城市模型上方的山坡街道四周亮起，唐悅先點清熟悉的隊友才去看謎題。這一關要求他們比較窄橋與較長的安全步道。五人必須共用一本紀錄簿，出口也得由每名成員簽字；規則並不難懂，唐悅在意的是，人們在壓力下多容易忘記它。彩繪障礙物間意外露出一道舊樓梯。方澤望著陡坡眨了眨眼。警告牌殘酷地把放慢腳步的人叫作「失敗者」，濃厚的舞臺霧更讓段芽嗆咳。

The first difficulty was not a locked door but a claim that looked reasonable at first glance: dense stage fog hides the safety rail and makes rushing hazardous. Fang Ze, the team’s auditor, copied the exact wording rather than changing it to suit their theory. Mei Lan, who built stage scenery for a living, examined the actual surface. Yu Qian, a nursing assistant, waited beside the slowest walker and checked whether there was room to turn.

第一個困難不是上鎖的門，而是一項乍看合理的說法：濃厚舞台霧遮住護欄，匆忙通過很危險。擔任稽核員的方澤逐字抄下原文，沒有為配合自己的理論擅改內容。以製作舞臺布景為業的梅嵐檢查實際地面；護理助理俞芊陪著走得最慢的人，確認有沒有轉身空間。

Near the next marker, something did not match the official design: the floor markings continue beyond the fog where the handrail is strongest. Fang noted the difference in the left column of the evidence sheet, leaving the right column blank for an independent check. Mei wanted to touch the equipment immediately, but Tang Yue asked her to wait for permission. A useful discovery could become useless if the team damaged the very object it intended to measure. The music's composition was calm, but the team had to evaluate a grave safety risk. Tang Yue thanked a volunteer with genuine gratitude for bringing an inspection sheet; intelligence here meant checking the sheet, not showing off clever guesses.

下一個記號附近，有件事和官方設計不相符：霧後的地面標記延伸到扶手最堅固之處。方澤把差異寫在證據表左欄，右欄留待獨立複核。梅嵐想立刻碰設備，唐悅請她先等許可。有用的發現若在測量前就把物件弄壞，也會失去效力。音樂編曲平靜，小隊卻必須評估嚴重的安全風險。唐悅真心感謝一位志工送來檢查表；此處的判斷力是核實表單，而非炫耀聰明猜測。

It was not an argument between good people and bad people. The disagreement inside the team was practical: Yu Qian argues for the safer line; a spectator calls their caution weakness. Tang Yue let both sides finish. A quick choice could protect their place in the contest, while a careful choice could protect people who would use the real streets after the contest ended. Fang wrote the competing assumptions in different boxes, and Yu Qian insisted that the group state what evidence would change their minds. "I don't need you to admire my courage," Yu Qian said. "I need you to hold the measuring tape where I can see it."

這不是好人與壞人的爭執。小隊內的分歧很實際：俞芊主張安全路線，一位觀眾卻譏笑他們太膽小。唐悅讓雙方把話說完。快速選擇可能保住競賽名次，謹慎選擇則可能保護比賽結束後真正使用道路的人。方澤把不同假設填進分開的格子，俞芊堅持大家先說清楚，什麼證據會改變自己的看法。「我不需要你讚賞我的勇敢，」俞芊說，「我需要你把捲尺放到我看得見的位置。」

Fang drew three columns across a fresh sheet: prediction, observation, and uncertainty. The stage was supposed to help them compare a narrow bridge and a longer protected path, yet the published score showed only the fastest number. Duan Ya checked the measurements, and Mei named the practical limits of their tools. They needed enough detail for a stranger to repeat the experiment without relying on the original team. A mild breeze crossed the mountainous model. An old philosopher's quotation warned against neglect, but Mei wondered whether reciting it would offend residents who needed practical help. They went to inspect the handrail instead.

方澤在新紙張上畫出三欄：預測、觀察、不確定性。這一關原本要讓他們比較窄橋與較長的安全步道，公布的分數卻只有最快數字。段芽核對量測，梅嵐指出工具的實際限制。他們需要留下足夠細節，讓陌生人不靠原隊伍也能重做實驗。微風吹過山地模型。舊哲學家的引文提醒人們別疏忽；梅嵐卻問，光背引文是否會冒犯真正需要協助的居民。於是他們轉去檢查扶手。

Their test was deliberately ordinary: they measure each bend, find a stable resting point, and guide another team across. Tang Yue counted the steps without pushing anyone to move faster. When the figures differed, Fang left both values on the page until they understood why. Mei kept the equipment in place; Yu Qian checked that nobody felt forced to continue. The five knew that a public plan built on coercion would still be a bad plan, however handsome its final diagram appeared.

他們刻意採用平凡的測試方法：小隊測量每個彎角，找到穩固休息點，還帶領另一隊通過。唐悅計算步數，沒有催促任何人加速。兩組數值不同時，方澤先把兩者都保留在紙上，直到查出原因。梅嵐固定設備，俞芊確認沒有人被迫繼續。五人明白，靠強迫建立的公共方案仍是壞方案，不管最後圖畫得多漂亮。

After a second check, the outcome stood: the other team reaches safety first but shares its witness card with them. Fang circled the measured facts and drew a line beneath every guess that still needed testing. Tang Yue thought this might be the contest’s unplanned achievement. The ranking could tell them which team was fastest today, but it could not decide by itself what every street should look like tomorrow. The team wanted reform, not revenge against the judge who had mocked them. Fang noticed a tendency to hurry whenever spectators shouted. He placed a twig beside the safe turn, and Yu Qian urged everyone to slow their steps.

第二次核對後，結果依舊：另一隊先抵達安全處，卻將見證卡借給他們。方澤圈起量得的事實，在仍需驗證的猜測下畫線。唐悅認為，這也許是競賽未料到的成果：排名能說明今天誰最快，卻不能獨自決定明天每條街應該長什麼樣子。小隊想要改革，不想向嘲笑他們的評審報復。方澤發現觀眾一叫嚷，大家就容易急著走。他在安全轉角放根細枝作記號，俞芊催促眾人放慢腳步。

As they prepared to leave, one final detail refused to fit the clean report: an unsigned inspection report points toward the contest's secret scoring office. Tang Yue entered it beneath a question mark rather than an accusation. She had learned to distrust easy endings, especially ones that arrived before the people most affected had spoken. They had not conquered the city model; they had made one part of it less mysterious, and that would have to be enough for tonight.

準備離開時，最後一項細節仍無法納入整齊的報告：一份沒簽名的檢查報告指向競賽的祕密評分室。唐悅把它寫在問號下，而不是直接當成指控。她已學會懷疑太容易的結局，尤其是受影響最大的人尚未開口時。他們沒有征服城市模型，只讓其中一部分不再神祕；今晚先做到這樣就夠了。
