霧中的人行橋

The Footbridge and the Fog

第九街區，不接受單人通關 · 第 3 / 25 章 · 3000–4000 · 英中對照

用互動版查詞、朗讀與收藏

Fog rolled low across the footbridge, swallowing the painted edge before the team reached it. Duan Ya covered her mouth and stopped; Tang Yue raised a hand to tell the stage operator they needed a pause before choosing either route.

霧貼著步橋流來，小隊還未到，彩繪邊線已被吞沒。段芽掩嘴停下；唐悅舉手告訴舞臺操作員，選路前需要暫停。

An accidental opening in the painted barrier revealed an old stair. Fang blinked at the steep drop. The warning used the brutal word 'failure' for anyone who slowed down, and thick theatrical fog made Duan Ya choke.

彩繪障礙物間意外露出一道舊樓梯。方澤望著陡坡眨了眨眼。警告牌殘酷地把放慢腳步的人叫作「失敗者」，濃厚的舞臺霧更讓段芽嗆咳。

Dense stage fog hid the safety rail and made rushing hazardous.

濃厚舞台霧遮住護欄，匆忙通過很危險。

The floor markings continued beyond the fog where the handrail was strongest. The music's composition was calm, but the team had to evaluate a grave safety risk. Tang Yue thanked a volunteer with genuine gratitude for bringing an inspection sheet; intelligence here meant checking the sheet, not showing off clever guesses.

霧後的地面標記延伸到扶手最堅固之處。音樂編曲平靜，小隊卻必須評估嚴重的安全風險。唐悅真心感謝一位志工送來檢查表；此處的判斷力是核實表單，而非炫耀聰明猜測。

Yu Qian argued for the safer line; a spectator called their caution weakness. "I don't need you to admire my courage," Yu Qian said. "I need you to hold the measuring tape where I can see it."

俞芊主張安全路線，一位觀眾卻譏笑他們太膽小。「我不需要你讚賞我的勇敢，」俞芊說，「我需要你把捲尺放到我看得見的位置。」

A mild breeze crossed the mountainous model. An old philosopher's quotation warned against neglect, but Mei wondered whether reciting it would offend residents who needed practical help. They went to inspect the handrail instead.

微風吹過山地模型。舊哲學家的引文提醒人們別疏忽；梅嵐卻問，光背引文是否會冒犯真正需要協助的居民。於是他們轉去檢查扶手。

The operator reduced the fog and left the clock running. Mei objected, but Yu asked first whether Duan could breathe comfortably. A volunteer opened the side screen, letting a gentle current clear their waiting place. From there, the team could see the floor line continuing toward a stronger handrail. Fang photographed the line before anyone stood on it, then gave the tape's far end to a member of the other team. Measuring together was slower than guessing the curve. It also kept the tape from dragging over the drop. At the resting point, they marked the space needed for two people to pass without one turning sideways. A spectator urged them to hurry because the other team was already near the exit. Tang asked that team whether its last walker could wait at the safe turn and confirm the route for those behind. Their captain agreed and later signed a witness card describing exactly where the rail had supported them. Mei attached the card to the measurements rather than treating it as a gift of points. Before leaving, Duan copied the unsigned inspection report's office number. The fog machine's hiss returned after they were clear. Fang could still hear the spectators, but the person holding his measuring tape had become more useful than the people urging him to run.

操作員減霧，計時卻不停。梅嵐抗議，俞芊先問段芽能否舒服呼吸。志工拉開側幕，微風吹清等待處。從那裡能看見地線延向較強扶手。方澤在有人踩前拍線，把捲尺遠端交給另一隊成員。一起量比猜彎道慢，卻也避免尺帶拖過陡邊。休息點上，他們標出兩人能相錯、不必側身的空間。觀眾催快，因另一隊已近出口。唐悅問那隊最後的步行者能否在安全彎等候，替後方確認路線。隊長答應，後來簽見證卡，說清哪段扶手支撐過他們。梅嵐把卡附在量測旁，沒有把它當贈送分數。離開前，段芽抄下未簽檢查表的辦公室編號。等眾人離開，霧機又嘶響。方澤仍聽見觀眾，卻發現替自己握尺的人，比催跑的人有用。

Mei attached the safe-turn measurements to the other team's witness card, preserving the route another group had checked.

梅嵐把安全彎量測附在另一隊見證卡旁，保留別組查過的路。

The other team reached safety first but shared its witness card with them. The team wanted reform, not revenge against the judge who had mocked them. Fang noticed a tendency to hurry whenever spectators shouted. He placed a twig beside the safe turn, and Yu Qian urged everyone to slow their steps.

另一隊先抵達安全處，卻將見證卡借給他們。小隊想要改革，不想向嘲笑他們的評審報復。方澤發現觀眾一叫嚷，大家就容易急著走。他在安全轉角放根細枝作記號，俞芊催促眾人放慢腳步。

An unsigned inspection report pointed toward the contest's secret scoring office.

一份沒簽名的檢查報告指向競賽的祕密評分室。
