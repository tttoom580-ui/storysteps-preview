橢圓形蓄水池

The Oval Reservoir

第九街區，不接受單人通關 · 第 12 / 25 章 · 3000–4000 · 英中對照 · 校訂中

A thin ring of water had reached the oval tank's base when Yu Qian tested the dry rail with her palm. The nearest path still looked clear, but the maintenance record described what happened when a pump stopped.

橢圓槽底已圍一薄圈水，俞芊用掌試乾扶手。最近路看似仍通，維修簿卻描述抽水機停轉後的情況。

The rule demanded absolute safety and steady discipline, but the water distribution board rewarded only efficiency. Yu Qian admitted she felt dread when the shortest corridor passed a rising tank without a railing.

規則要求絕對安全和穩定紀律，供水分配看板卻只獎勵效率。俞芊坦承，一條沒有扶手的最短通道貼著上升的水槽，讓她感到恐懼。

The official path was shortest only when the pumps ran perfectly.

官方路線只有在抽水機完美運作時才最短。

An illustrated maintenance record identified a backup vessel used during outages. Mei used spare hardware to fasten a loose guard, thanking a gracious volunteer for holding the light. On the wall, a small painting showed a hawk above a river; the artist's note described the grief caused by an old flood.

圖解維護紀錄指出停機時會用到的備用槽。梅嵐用備用五金固定鬆動護欄，並感謝一位親切志工幫忙照明。牆上小畫畫著河上的老鷹，藝術家的說明提到一次舊洪水造成的悲傷。

Mei wanted to rush across before the water rose; Yu Qian warned that haste hid risk. Yu Qian touched the dry rail. "A fast route that works only on perfect days isn't an emergency route," she said.

梅嵐想趁水位升高前衝過去，俞芊提醒急躁會掩蓋風險。俞芊摸著乾燥扶手說：「只在完美日子才有用的快速路線，不是緊急路線。」

A clear illustration showed a miner carrying an oval vessel through the tunnel. Fang wanted to insert the drawing into their report but cautioned that the picture's perfection did not prove the real corridor was open.

一張清晰插畫畫著礦工扛著橢圓形容器穿過隧道。方澤想把圖插入報告，卻提醒大家，圖畫完美不代表真實走廊可以通行。

Tang asked the operator to explain how the controlled outage would end before anyone entered the room. He showed the switch and the marked water limit. Mei secured the loose fitting from the dry side, with a volunteer holding the light where she requested. They did not turn off a real public pump; the training tank had its own isolated controls. Fang copied that limit into the report so nobody would mistake their trial for permission to interfere with city water. When the training pump paused, a shallow sheet spread across the short corridor. Duan marked its edge from behind the barrier and compared it with the backup vessel's illustrated position. The longer line stayed dry, but the vessel's handle projected into the walking space. Mei asked the operator to move it to the approved place before they measured that route. Yu walked once at her own pace and asked a second volunteer to try without hearing her directions. The volunteer hesitated at an old arrow pointing toward the wet floor. Tang replaced it with a temporary warning after receiving permission, and Duan noted the change on the sketch. Their final time included the pause to read that warning. When the water was drained, Fang checked the maintenance page's supplier entry for their source list. Its name matched the scoring contractor's, a connection he recorded without yet claiming what it meant.

入室前，唐悅請操作員說清受控停機如何結束。他指開關與限水線。梅嵐在乾側固定鬆接件，志工按她指的位置照燈。不是關真公共泵，訓練槽有獨立控制；方澤把限制抄入報告，免被誤作可干預城市供水的許可。訓練泵暫停，淺水鋪過短廊。段芽站障礙後標水邊，比備用槽圖示。長線仍乾，槽把手卻伸入步行空間。梅嵐請操作員先移到核准位才量。俞芊照自己步速走一次，再請不先聽她指示的志工試。那人停在指濕地的舊箭頭前。唐悅獲准換臨時警示，段芽註記改動。最後計時包含讀警示的停頓。排水後，方澤整理來源表，注意到維修頁供應商與評分承包商同名；他記連結，沒有先斷定含意。

The dry-route drawing showed the secured fittings, measured water edge, moved vessel, and replacement warning.

乾路圖標出固定接件、水邊量測、移動容器與新警告。

The safe route succeeded and its evidence became part of the public review file. The revised plan was realistic and earned recognition from the inspector. Tang Yue resolved to renew the wet-floor warning, while Mei carefully moved the backup vessel to its approved place outside the walking line.

安全路線成功，證據納入公開複核檔案。修改後的方案務實可行，得到檢查員認可。唐悅決心更新濕滑地板警示，梅嵐小心把備用容器移到步行線外的核准位置。

The file unexpectedly listed a supplier with the same name as the scoring contractor.

檔案意外列出一家與評分承包商同名的供應商。
