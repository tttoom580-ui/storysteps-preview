The Engineer Who Stayed

逃出沒有出口的虛擬城市 · 第 3 / 25 章 · 4000–5000 · 英中對照 · 校訂中

Ilya reveals he once designed the city's storage scheduler. A child waved at the public camera without knowing what system watched from behind it. There was no dramatic sound to announce a discovery. Qiao Han simply stopped walking and called Ilya Sen back to the table. They had argued about methods before, and each remembered the disagreement. Yet a shared problem did not require identical opinions. It required two people willing to inspect the same evidence, admit when they did not understand it, and protect the people whose lives were already being altered by the investigation.

伊利亞坦言自己曾設計城市的儲存排程器。 孩子向公共攝影機揮手，不知道鏡頭背後是哪個系統在看。 發現線索時沒有戲劇性的聲響。喬涵只是停下腳步，把伊利亞・森叫回桌旁。兩人以前爭過方法，彼此都記得那場分歧。但共同問題不要求意見完全一致，而是需要兩個人願意檢視相同證據、承認自己不懂的地方，並保護那些生活已因調查而改變的人。

He left a warning that the administrator later removed. Qiao Han read the sentence twice and refused to underline the most frightening word. A document could contain an error without proving who had made it, or why. Ilya Sen proposed three mundane explanations and one unlikely possibility. They began with the mundane ones, recording the conditions of each test before touching the next piece of evidence. That restraint frustrated the watchers, but it prevented the truth from becoming another performance prepared for an eager audience.

他留下的警告後來被管理員刪除。 喬涵把那句話讀了兩遍，沒有急著在最可怕的詞下畫線。文件出錯並不能證明是誰做的，或為何如此。伊利亞・森提出三個平常的解釋及一個不大可能的可能性。他們先測試平常的解釋，在接觸下一件證物前寫下每項測試條件。這份克制讓旁觀者不耐，卻防止真相又變成取悅觀眾的表演。

Near the back of the room, technician Tess listened more carefully than the officials. That quiet attention altered the conversation. The question was no longer whether Qiao Han could win an argument; it was whether the people affected would have a choice after the argument ended. Ilya Sen suggested asking for permission before copying personal material. A few witnesses left without answering, and their refusal was recorded without resentment. Those who stayed offered smaller, more useful facts than any grand confession.

房間後方，技師泰絲聽得比官員還仔細。那份安靜的專注改變了談話。問題不再是喬涵能否贏得爭辯，而是爭辯結束後受影響的人是否仍有選擇。伊利亞・森建議複製私人資料前先取得許可。幾名證人沒有回答便離開，他們的拒絕被如實記錄，沒有人怨恨。留下的人提供了比任何驚天告白都更小、卻更有用的事實。

For a moment the challenge from Administrator Rook appeared to settle everything. The door was guarded, the papers were stamped, and everyone knew who had the authority to speak. Then Qiao Han asked a narrower question that nobody had prepared to answer. Ilya Sen supported the request with a dated observation, not a speech. The room grew quiet. They had not defeated a powerful opponent, but they had shown that procedure could protect an inconvenient question as well as an official answer.

有一瞬間，洛克管理員的挑戰似乎決定了一切：門有人守、文件蓋了章、誰有發言權人人清楚。接著喬涵提出一個更窄、也沒人準備好回答的問題。伊利亞・森拿出有日期的觀察紀錄支持請求，而不是演說。房間安靜下來。他們還沒有擊敗強大的對手，卻證明程序也能保護不方便的提問，不只保護官方答案。

There was a practical reason not to hurry. Every mistake would be paid for by people whose names might never appear in the final report. Qiao Han asked technician Tess what ordinary service must continue during the test. Ilya Sen wrote down the answer and found a second route for anyone needing help. The others complained about the delay until they saw the consequences of doing it the faster way. Speed mattered, but only when it did not shift danger onto the least powerful.

不能急躁有很實際的理由：每次錯誤都會由名字可能從不出現在報告裡的人承擔。喬涵問技師泰絲測試期間哪些日常服務必須持續。伊利亞・森記下答案，並為需要幫忙的人找出第二條路。其他人原本抱怨拖延，直到看見較快做法的後果。速度很重要，但不能把危險轉嫁給最無權勢的人。

On a second sheet, Qiao Han found an old cross-reference index. Its headings were not proof of any theory; they were search terms left by previous workers. Entry 1 listed “abuse”, “consultation”, “contempt”, “correspondent”, “creek”. Entry 2 listed “deliberate”, “deputy”, “diplomatic”, “ecosystem”, “extinct”. Entry 3 listed “gut”, “notify”, “odds”, “phase”, “pier”. Entry 4 listed “sneak”, “successor”, “sustainable”, “swap”, “trophy”. Ilya Sen insisted that a label could not replace a witness, an observed result, or a decision that people had actually approved. The index remained in the archive as a way to retrace the team's work, not as a shortcut to a verdict.

另一張紙上，喬涵找到舊交叉索引。標題並不能證明任何理論，只是前人留下的搜尋詞。第1欄列著“abuse”, “consultation”, “contempt”, “correspondent”, “creek”。第2欄列著“deliberate”, “deputy”, “diplomatic”, “ecosystem”, “extinct”。第3欄列著“gut”, “notify”, “odds”, “phase”, “pier”。第4欄列著“sneak”, “successor”, “sustainable”, “swap”, “trophy”。伊利亞・森堅持，標籤不能取代證人、實際觀察的結果，或當事人真正同意的決定。這份索引留在檔案中，方便後人回查工作，而非作為跳到結論的捷徑。

Qiao Han touched the subway exit and felt its painted metal ripple like water. Ilya showed her the diagnostic layer beneath the street. Each resident shared memory blocks with another, so a forced logout might clear a process that was not truly empty. She tried the command only inside an isolated test box. The display still reported an unexpected deletion. From that moment she treated every exit as a public safety problem, not a personal shortcut.

喬涵觸摸地鐵出口，感覺上漆金屬像水般波動。伊利亞讓她看街道底下的診斷層。每名居民都與別人共用記憶區塊，因此強制登出可能清掉並非空白的程序。她只在隔離測試箱嘗試指令，螢幕仍報告一次意外刪除。從此她把每個出口當公共安全問題，而不是私人捷徑。

This was still the beginning. Qiao Han could have pretended to understand the whole system after one strange discovery, but the ordinary people around her deserved better than certainty performed for applause. She wrote one modest promise at the bottom of the page: nothing would be changed without a way to measure the consequence and a way for those affected to refuse. Ilya Sen signed beside it. Neither expected the promise to remain easy.

故事仍在起點。喬涵原可在一次古怪發現後假裝理解整套系統，但身旁普通人值得比博掌聲的篤定更好的對待。她在頁尾寫下一句謹慎承諾：任何改動都要有量測後果的方法，也要讓受影響者有拒絕之路。伊利亞・森在旁簽名。兩人都不認為承諾會一直容易。

Not every observer liked the result. Some wanted proof of a larger crime; others hoped nothing had gone wrong at all. Qiao Han showed them the same measurements without changing the story to suit either audience. Ilya Sen pointed out the remaining gaps and proposed another independent check. The meeting ended with fewer dramatic conclusions than people had expected. It also ended with a schedule, named responsibilities, and a promise that mistakes would remain visible rather than quietly disappearing.

不是每名觀察者都喜歡結果。有人想證明更大的罪，有人希望一切根本沒出錯。喬涵把相同讀數展示給兩邊，沒有為迎合任何人改寫故事。伊利亞・森指出尚存空缺，提出再做一次獨立查驗。會議的戲劇性結論少於預期，卻有了時程、具名責任，以及不再悄悄抹除錯誤的承諾。

By the time the lamps went out, Qiao Han had written a short report in plain language. It named the people protected, the records verified, and the questions that still belonged to tomorrow. Ilya Sen read it and removed a sentence that sounded more certain than the evidence allowed. Qiao Han thanked him, though the correction stung. Trust, they were learning, did not mean agreeing about every claim. It meant being able to say no before a mistake became dangerous.

燈熄時，喬涵已用簡單文字寫好短報告，列出受到保護的人、驗證過的紀錄，以及要留到明天的問題。伊利亞・森讀後刪去一句過度確定的話。喬涵雖覺刺耳，仍向他道謝。兩人正在學習，信任不是對每項主張都同意，而是在錯誤變危險前有能力說不。
