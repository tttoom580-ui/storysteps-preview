Rowan asks Lena to record every lost memory, refusing to hide the cost of her magic. Lena felt the meaning of this new event before the full crowd understood it. In Ash Vale, each unusual sign could become a rumor by evening, and rumors were hard to pull back once they had spread. Rowan stood beside the nearest witness and asked for the sequence of actions, not a dramatic conclusion. Some details contradicted what they had learned the day before. That was disappointing and useful at the same time. Lena opened a fresh page, wrote the date at the top, and decided the truth would need more than one voice.

羅文要求莉娜記下每段失落記憶，不願隱瞞魔法代價。 群眾尚未完全理解時，莉娜已感到這件新事的分量。在灰燼谷，每個異常徵兆到了晚上都可能變成謠言，而謠言一旦傳開就很難收回。羅文站到最近的證人身邊，詢問行動的先後順序，而非戲劇性的結論。有些細節與昨天得到的資訊矛盾，既令人失望也很有用。莉娜翻開新的一頁，在上方寫下日期，決定真相需要不止一個人的聲音。

News of the plan reached the Memory Wardens before sunset. The official reply was quick, polished, and strangely certain. Lena read it twice, looking for the questions it refused to answer. Rowan noticed that it described a public problem as a private disagreement, although many witnesses had been affected. They did not accuse anyone merely because the answer sounded unpleasant. Instead, they requested the original documents and the name of the person responsible for storing them. A rival deserved the chance to respond to a clear question, but not the right to hide evidence behind an impressive signature.

日落前，計畫的消息傳到記憶守衛。官方回覆快速、漂亮，而且確定得古怪。莉娜讀了兩次，尋找其中刻意避開的問題。羅文注意到它把影響多名證人的公共問題描述成私人爭執。他們沒有因回答難聽就隨意指控，而是要求原始文件與保管人的姓名。對手有機會回答清楚的問題，卻沒有權利躲在漂亮簽名後藏匿證據。

The discussion was interrupted by somebody who had not been invited. The visitor carried a story about the Memory Wardens, but the story mixed known facts with guesses. Lena asked the visitor to separate what had been seen from what had merely been heard. Rowan offered water and a chair, not a promise that every claim would be believed. A useful detail emerged only after the visitor stopped trying to sound certain. The team compared it with the earlier notes and found a new question. They could not solve it that night, so they wrote it down without pretending otherwise.

一位不請自來的訪客打斷討論。此人帶來關於記憶守衛的故事，卻把已知事實和猜測混在一起。莉娜請訪客分開親眼所見與道聽塗說。羅文遞上水和椅子，卻不承諾全部相信。訪客不再假裝很確定後，才說出有用細節。團隊對照早期筆記，發現新問題。當晚無法解決，他們便把問題寫下，不假裝已經找到答案。

During a pause in the investigation, Rowan unfolded an old language ledger. Five marked entries on the first line read "beast", "bowling", "budget", "decade", "drunk". The next line held "expressive", "frighten", "geography", "greedy", "kit". None was a magic password or an answer by itself; each was a fragment of a larger history. Lena asked who had written the marks, why they had been kept, and whether they belonged to the same account. It would have been easy to declare the puzzle solved simply because the words were now visible. Instead, they copied the terms, recorded the source, and returned to the unresolved problem outside the room.

調查暫停時，羅文攤開一本舊語詞紀錄。第一行的五個註記寫著「beast」、「bowling」、「budget」、「decade」、「drunk」；下一行則有「expressive」、「frighten」、「geography」、「greedy」、「kit」。它們都不是魔法密碼，也無法單獨解答問題；每個詞只是更大段歷史的片段。莉娜詢問誰寫下這些字、為何保存，以及它們是否屬於同一份記錄。只因文字可見就宣稱解開謎題很容易，但他們只抄下詞條、標明來源，便回到室外仍未解決的問題。

A messenger reached Ash Vale carrying two conflicting reports. Lena read them slowly rather than asking everyone to trust the louder witness. Rowan placed the memory journal beside the papers and drew a line under the part they could verify. It was tempting to guess what had happened, especially when people were afraid. Yet one wrong guess could send a friend into danger. The group wrote down the times, the names, and the things nobody had seen clearly. Someone objected to wasting time. Lena replied that a moment spent checking facts was less expensive than a mistake that could not be repaired.

一名信使帶著兩份互相矛盾的報告抵達灰燼谷。莉娜慢慢讀完，沒有要大家相信聲音較大的證人。羅文將記憶日誌放在紙張旁，在可以確認的地方畫線。尤其人們害怕時，猜測發生了什麼很容易；但一次猜錯就可能把朋友送入危險。團隊記下時間、姓名和沒有人看清的事。有個人抗議浪費時間。莉娜回答，花一點時間查證，總比犯下無法補救的錯誤便宜。

During a pause in the investigation, Rowan unfolded an old language ledger. Five marked entries on the first line read "lace", "necktie", "passenger", "permit", "professor". The next line held "regret", "risk", "semester", "visible", "voter". None was a magic password or an answer by itself; each was a fragment of a larger history. Lena asked who had written the marks, why they had been kept, and whether they belonged to the same account. It would have been easy to declare the puzzle solved simply because the words were now visible. Instead, they copied the terms, recorded the source, and returned to the unresolved problem outside the room.

調查暫停時，羅文攤開一本舊語詞紀錄。第一行的五個註記寫著「lace」、「necktie」、「passenger」、「permit」、「professor」；下一行則有「regret」、「risk」、「semester」、「visible」、「voter」。它們都不是魔法密碼，也無法單獨解答問題；每個詞只是更大段歷史的片段。莉娜詢問誰寫下這些字、為何保存，以及它們是否屬於同一份記錄。只因文字可見就宣稱解開謎題很容易，但他們只抄下詞條、標明來源，便回到室外仍未解決的問題。

After the others left, Rowan asked Lena whether working together still felt like a choice. Feelings had grown while danger filled their days, but that did not give one person power over another. Lena said that trust needed room for disagreement, even when the answer hurt. Rowan accepted this without trying to turn it into a promise. For a moment they stood close enough to hear each other's breath, then returned to the unfinished work. They did not need a crowd to decide how they felt. The courage they were learning was quieter than any spell and more difficult to fake.

其他人離開後，羅文問莉娜，一起工作如今是否仍是自由選擇。危險充滿日常時，感情也漸漸長出來，但這不給任何人支配另一人的權利。莉娜說，信任必須容得下不同意見，即使答案可能傷人。羅文接受了，沒有把話逼成承諾。兩人有一刻近得聽見彼此呼吸，然後繼續未完工作。他們不需要人群替自己決定感情。正學習的勇氣比任何法術都安靜，也更難假裝。

Before leaving, Lena wrote one line at the bottom of the page: the day's answer could still be corrected. Rowan checked that people affected by the decision could read it and reply. A messenger brought news that the next meeting would concern 'The Village Clinic'. Neither knew precisely what this new matter would demand. They did know what should not be traded away to save a few hours: honest records, a safe path, and the right of someone weaker to disagree. Lena carried the unfinished notes toward Ash Vale, listening to the footsteps behind them and thinking of the question that morning would bring.

離開前，莉娜在紙頁底下寫道：今天的答案仍然可以訂正。羅文確認受決定影響的人能讀到並回應。信使傳來消息，下次會議將處理〈村莊診所〉。兩人都不確定新事件要求什麼，卻知道哪些東西不能只為節省幾小時就被交換掉：誠實紀錄、安全道路，以及弱者提出異議的權利。莉娜帶著未完成的筆記走向灰燼谷，聽著背後腳步聲，想著明早會出現的問題。
