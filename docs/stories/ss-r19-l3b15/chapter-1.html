One Life, One Forgotten Day

每治好一個人，騎士就會忘記我一天 · 第 1 / 25 章 · 2000–3000 · 英中對照 · 校訂中

The child stopped bleeding before Lena finished her healing prayer. Outside the clinic, rain tapped a soldier's shield. Rowan, the knight who had walked beside her for three weeks, turned toward her with an unfamiliar expression. 'I'm sorry,' he said. 'Have we met?' Lena thought he was joking until she mentioned the bridge where they had first spoken. Rowan remembered the bridge, the rain, and his horse. He remembered everything except the day he had met her. His missing memory was the precise price of the child she had saved, a debt neither of them had agreed to pay.

莉娜的治癒祈禱還沒念完，孩子的傷口已停止流血。診所外，雨滴敲著士兵的盾牌。三週來與她同行的騎士羅文轉過頭，露出陌生的神情。「抱歉，」他說，「我們認識嗎？」莉娜以為他在開玩笑，直到她提起兩人初次交談的橋。羅文記得橋、雨和自己的馬，卻不記得遇見她的那一天。這段消失的記憶，正好是她救下孩子付出的代價，而他們從未同意支付這筆債。

The discussion was interrupted by somebody who had not been invited. The visitor carried a story about the Memory Wardens, but the story mixed known facts with guesses. Lena asked the visitor to separate what had been seen from what had merely been heard. Rowan offered water and a chair, not a promise that every claim would be believed. A useful detail emerged only after the visitor stopped trying to sound certain. The team compared it with the earlier notes and found a new question. They could not solve it that night, so they wrote it down without pretending otherwise.

一位不請自來的訪客打斷討論。此人帶來關於記憶守衛的故事，卻把已知事實和猜測混在一起。莉娜請訪客分開親眼所見與道聽塗說。羅文遞上水和椅子，卻不承諾全部相信。訪客不再假裝很確定後，才說出有用細節。團隊對照早期筆記，發現新問題。當晚無法解決，他們便把問題寫下，不假裝已經找到答案。

A messenger reached Ash Vale carrying two conflicting reports. Lena read them slowly rather than asking everyone to trust the louder witness. Rowan placed the memory journal beside the papers and drew a line under the part they could verify. It was tempting to guess what had happened, especially when people were afraid. Yet one wrong guess could send a friend into danger. The group wrote down the times, the names, and the things nobody had seen clearly. Someone objected to wasting time. Lena replied that a moment spent checking facts was less expensive than a mistake that could not be repaired.

一名信使帶著兩份互相矛盾的報告抵達灰燼谷。莉娜慢慢讀完，沒有要大家相信聲音較大的證人。羅文將記憶日誌放在紙張旁，在可以確認的地方畫線。尤其人們害怕時，猜測發生了什麼很容易；但一次猜錯就可能把朋友送入危險。團隊記下時間、姓名和沒有人看清的事。有個人抗議浪費時間。莉娜回答，花一點時間查證，總比犯下無法補救的錯誤便宜。

During a pause in the investigation, Rowan unfolded an old language ledger. Five marked entries on the first line read "berry", "circus", "deed", "fairy", "fancy". The next line held "heater", "hut", "jazz", "jet", "magician". None was a magic password or an answer by itself; each was a fragment of a larger history. Lena asked who had written the marks, why they had been kept, and whether they belonged to the same account. It would have been easy to declare the puzzle solved simply because the words were now visible. Instead, they copied the terms, recorded the source, and returned to the unresolved problem outside the room.

調查暫停時，羅文攤開一本舊語詞紀錄。第一行的五個註記寫著「berry」、「circus」、「deed」、「fairy」、「fancy」；下一行則有「heater」、「hut」、「jazz」、「jet」、「magician」。它們都不是魔法密碼，也無法單獨解答問題；每個詞只是更大段歷史的片段。莉娜詢問誰寫下這些字、為何保存，以及它們是否屬於同一份記錄。只因文字可見就宣稱解開謎題很容易，但他們只抄下詞條、標明來源，便回到室外仍未解決的問題。

The first test gave the team a little hope and one new problem. Lena insisted they record both results. Rowan watched the boundary of the working area and told observers to keep their distance. The second test was slower, but it showed exactly where the old method failed. A careless account would have called the effort a complete success. They knew better. Each person signed the record only for the part personally checked, leaving space for another witness to disagree. The honest gaps in the notes were not weaknesses; they were signs that the writers understood the limits of what they knew.

第一次測試帶來一點希望，也帶來一個新問題。莉娜堅持同時記下兩項結果。羅文守著工作區邊界，請旁觀者保持距離。第二次測試更慢，卻準確指出舊方法的失敗位置。草率的紀錄會把行動說成完全成功，但他們知道不能如此。每個人只為親自確認的部分簽名，也留空間讓其他證人提出異議。筆記裡誠實的空白不是弱點，而是執筆者理解自身所知有限的證明。

During a pause in the investigation, Rowan unfolded an old language ledger. Five marked entries on the first line read "miracle", "pint", "political", "quit", "rob". The next line held "screw", "signal", "spin", "tame", "various". None was a magic password or an answer by itself; each was a fragment of a larger history. Lena asked who had written the marks, why they had been kept, and whether they belonged to the same account. It would have been easy to declare the puzzle solved simply because the words were now visible. Instead, they copied the terms, recorded the source, and returned to the unresolved problem outside the room.

調查暫停時，羅文攤開一本舊語詞紀錄。第一行的五個註記寫著「miracle」、「pint」、「political」、「quit」、「rob」；下一行則有「screw」、「signal」、「spin」、「tame」、「various」。它們都不是魔法密碼，也無法單獨解答問題；每個詞只是更大段歷史的片段。莉娜詢問誰寫下這些字、為何保存，以及它們是否屬於同一份記錄。只因文字可見就宣稱解開謎題很容易，但他們只抄下詞條、標明來源，便回到室外仍未解決的問題。

After the others left, Rowan asked Lena whether working together still felt like a choice. Feelings had grown while danger filled their days, but that did not give one person power over another. Lena said that trust needed room for disagreement, even when the answer hurt. Rowan accepted this without trying to turn it into a promise. For a moment they stood close enough to hear each other's breath, then returned to the unfinished work. They did not need a crowd to decide how they felt. The courage they were learning was quieter than any spell and more difficult to fake.

其他人離開後，羅文問莉娜，一起工作如今是否仍是自由選擇。危險充滿日常時，感情也漸漸長出來，但這不給任何人支配另一人的權利。莉娜說，信任必須容得下不同意見，即使答案可能傷人。羅文接受了，沒有把話逼成承諾。兩人有一刻近得聽見彼此呼吸，然後繼續未完工作。他們不需要人群替自己決定感情。正學習的勇氣比任何法術都安靜，也更難假裝。

Before leaving, Lena wrote one line at the bottom of the page: the day's answer could still be corrected. Rowan checked that people affected by the decision could read it and reply. A messenger brought news that the next meeting would concern 'The Honest Journal'. Neither knew precisely what this new matter would demand. They did know what should not be traded away to save a few hours: honest records, a safe path, and the right of someone weaker to disagree. Lena carried the unfinished notes toward Ash Vale, listening to the footsteps behind them and thinking of the question that morning would bring.

離開前，莉娜在紙頁底下寫道：今天的答案仍然可以訂正。羅文確認受決定影響的人能讀到並回應。信使傳來消息，下次會議將處理〈誠實日誌〉。兩人都不確定新事件要求什麼，卻知道哪些東西不能只為節省幾小時就被交換掉：誠實紀錄、安全道路，以及弱者提出異議的權利。莉娜帶著未完成的筆記走向灰燼谷，聽著背後腳步聲，想著明早會出現的問題。
