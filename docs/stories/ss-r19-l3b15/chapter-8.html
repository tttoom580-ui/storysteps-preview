The Refused Cure

每治好一個人，騎士就會忘記我一天 · 第 8 / 25 章 · 2000–3000 · 英中對照 · 校訂中

Lena rejects the bargain, and Rowan supports care based on need rather than rank. Lena felt the meaning of this new event before the full crowd understood it. In Ash Vale, each unusual sign could become a rumor by evening, and rumors were hard to pull back once they had spread. Rowan stood beside the nearest witness and asked for the sequence of actions, not a dramatic conclusion. Some details contradicted what they had learned the day before. That was disappointing and useful at the same time. Lena opened a fresh page, wrote the date at the top, and decided the truth would need more than one voice.

莉娜拒絕交易，羅文支持依需求而非身分提供照護。 群眾尚未完全理解時，莉娜已感到這件新事的分量。在灰燼谷，每個異常徵兆到了晚上都可能變成謠言，而謠言一旦傳開就很難收回。羅文站到最近的證人身邊，詢問行動的先後順序，而非戲劇性的結論。有些細節與昨天得到的資訊矛盾，既令人失望也很有用。莉娜翻開新的一頁，在上方寫下日期，決定真相需要不止一個人的聲音。

'No healing choice belongs to one person alone when its cost falls on another; both must know the truth.' Rowan said, reading from the page they had written together. The sentence sounded simple until it was tested by actual need. A stranger asked for an exception, claiming the danger was too urgent for ordinary rules. Lena listened and checked whether anyone else would pay for that exception. The answer was not comfortable. It required time, an explanation, and a promise to review the decision when new evidence appeared. Lena could feel the crowd waiting for a dramatic command. Instead, the group made a smaller choice they could explain to the people who would live with it.

「當治癒的代價由另一個人承擔，選擇就不能只屬於一個人；雙方都必須知道真相。」羅文讀出兩人一起寫下的句子。這話聽來簡單，直到真正的需求開始考驗它。一名陌生人以危險太急迫為由要求例外。莉娜傾聽後先確認是否有其他人得替這個例外付代價。答案並不舒服：他們需要時間、說明，以及在新證據出現時重審的承諾。莉娜感覺人群等著一道戲劇性命令，團隊卻做出較小、能向承擔後果的人解釋的選擇。

There was still a distance between understanding a problem and solving it. Lena and Rowan walked through Ash Vale with a fresh drawing, asking the same question in different places. A child noticed a mark that adults had missed. An elder remembered a change made years before. The clues did not fit together at once, but neither did they cancel each other out. Lena marked each source and refused to invent a missing answer. They returned with dirty shoes and a clearer idea of what needed a second visit. Sometimes the small witness was the one who showed the safest way forward.

理解問題與解決問題之間仍有距離。莉娜和羅文拿著新畫的圖走過灰燼谷，在不同地方問相同問題。一個孩子注意到大人忽略的記號，一位長者想起多年前的變化。線索沒有立即拼合，卻也不互相抵銷。莉娜標註每項來源，不替空白捏造答案。他們帶著髒鞋子回來，更清楚哪些地方需要再訪。有時指向安全道路的，正是最不起眼的證人。

During a pause in the investigation, Rowan unfolded an old language ledger. Five marked entries on the first line read "baggage", "buffalo", "bullet", "citizen", "controller". The next line held "cooker", "crispy", "grab", "lately", "litter". None was a magic password or an answer by itself; each was a fragment of a larger history. Lena asked who had written the marks, why they had been kept, and whether they belonged to the same account. It would have been easy to declare the puzzle solved simply because the words were now visible. Instead, they copied the terms, recorded the source, and returned to the unresolved problem outside the room.

調查暫停時，羅文攤開一本舊語詞紀錄。第一行的五個註記寫著「baggage」、「buffalo」、「bullet」、「citizen」、「controller」；下一行則有「cooker」、「crispy」、「grab」、「lately」、「litter」。它們都不是魔法密碼，也無法單獨解答問題；每個詞只是更大段歷史的片段。莉娜詢問誰寫下這些字、為何保存，以及它們是否屬於同一份記錄。只因文字可見就宣稱解開謎題很容易，但他們只抄下詞條、標明來源，便回到室外仍未解決的問題。

When the plan was discussed, Rowan did not agree with everything Lena said. Their argument began over a detail that seemed small, but it soon exposed a difference in what each considered fair. A volunteer offered to settle the issue by a quick vote. Lena asked to hear from the people who would bear the greatest risk first. The answer changed the proposal. Nobody enjoyed admitting they had missed something, yet the room became quieter after the correction. It was easier to make a useful choice when disagreement was allowed to stay in the open.

討論計畫時，羅文並不完全同意莉娜的話。爭論始於一個看似微小的細節，卻揭露了兩人對公平的不同理解。有志工提議立刻投票解決，莉娜則要求先聽取風險最高者的意見。回答改變了提案。沒有人喜歡承認自己漏看了什麼，但訂正後房間反而安靜。當分歧能公開存在時，人們更容易做出有用的選擇。

During a pause in the investigation, Rowan unfolded an old language ledger. Five marked entries on the first line read "owe", "permission", "process", "request", "sexual". The next line held "shadow", "talkative", "wealthy", "web", "yell". None was a magic password or an answer by itself; each was a fragment of a larger history. Lena asked who had written the marks, why they had been kept, and whether they belonged to the same account. It would have been easy to declare the puzzle solved simply because the words were now visible. Instead, they copied the terms, recorded the source, and returned to the unresolved problem outside the room.

調查暫停時，羅文攤開一本舊語詞紀錄。第一行的五個註記寫著「owe」、「permission」、「process」、「request」、「sexual」；下一行則有「shadow」、「talkative」、「wealthy」、「web」、「yell」。它們都不是魔法密碼，也無法單獨解答問題；每個詞只是更大段歷史的片段。莉娜詢問誰寫下這些字、為何保存，以及它們是否屬於同一份記錄。只因文字可見就宣稱解開謎題很容易，但他們只抄下詞條、標明來源，便回到室外仍未解決的問題。

After the others left, Rowan asked Lena whether working together still felt like a choice. Feelings had grown while danger filled their days, but that did not give one person power over another. Lena said that trust needed room for disagreement, even when the answer hurt. Rowan accepted this without trying to turn it into a promise. For a moment they stood close enough to hear each other's breath, then returned to the unfinished work. They did not need a crowd to decide how they felt. The courage they were learning was quieter than any spell and more difficult to fake.

其他人離開後，羅文問莉娜，一起工作如今是否仍是自由選擇。危險充滿日常時，感情也漸漸長出來，但這不給任何人支配另一人的權利。莉娜說，信任必須容得下不同意見，即使答案可能傷人。羅文接受了，沒有把話逼成承諾。兩人有一刻近得聽見彼此呼吸，然後繼續未完工作。他們不需要人群替自己決定感情。正學習的勇氣比任何法術都安靜，也更難假裝。

Before leaving, Lena wrote one line at the bottom of the page: the day's answer could still be corrected. Rowan checked that people affected by the decision could read it and reply. A messenger brought news that the next meeting would concern 'The Broken Bridge'. Neither knew precisely what this new matter would demand. They did know what should not be traded away to save a few hours: honest records, a safe path, and the right of someone weaker to disagree. Lena carried the unfinished notes toward Ash Vale, listening to the footsteps behind them and thinking of the question that morning would bring.

離開前，莉娜在紙頁底下寫道：今天的答案仍然可以訂正。羅文確認受決定影響的人能讀到並回應。信使傳來消息，下次會議將處理〈斷橋〉。兩人都不確定新事件要求什麼，卻知道哪些東西不能只為節省幾小時就被交換掉：誠實紀錄、安全道路，以及弱者提出異議的權利。莉娜帶著未完成的筆記走向灰燼谷，聽著背後腳步聲，想著明早會出現的問題。
