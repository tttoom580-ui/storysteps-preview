By noon, rumors had outrun the facts. Some people wanted a villain; others wanted a hero who could end the argument without asking another question. Severin blames Orren and orders all former palace workers detained without individual charges. Iris had worked long enough to distrust both shortcuts. In the city of Blackgate, the first task was to protect the evidence before anyone could turn it into a weapon.

到了中午，謠言早已跑得比事實快。有人想找反派，也有人想找英雄，好在不再追問的情況下結束爭論。賽維林指控奧倫，命令將所有舊宮員工不分罪責拘押。伊莉絲工作多年，已經不相信這兩種捷徑。在黑門城，當務之急是先保護證據，免得任何人將它變成武器。

Black lines spread across a wall where a maintenance seal had cracked. Iris pressed her palm near the stone but did not touch it; the instrument showed that the surface was colder than the air. Repair was possible, yet each hour of neglect made the next repair more expensive.

維護封印裂開的牆上延伸出黑線。伊莉絲把手掌靠近石面卻沒有碰觸；儀器顯示石面比空氣更冷。修復並非不可能，但每疏忽一小時，下一次修繕就更加昂貴。

A witness asked whether the inquiry would make tomorrow safer. Iris could not promise that. The truth could establish responsibility, but it would not repair a broken machine, feed a hungry child, or return lost time on its own. Orren suggested a temporary protection that could be checked after sunrise. They wrote down its limits in plain language and invited the people affected to challenge them.

一位證人問調查是否能讓明天更安全，伊莉絲無法保證。真相能確立責任，卻不會自行修好機器、餵飽孩子或歸還失去的時間。奧倫提出一項日出後可以檢驗的暫時保護措施。他們用白話寫清限制，邀請受影響的人提出異議。

The overlooked details in the witness file made the question harder, not easier: Lira brought a thermometer calibrated in Celsius and a probe designed to measure changes in river heat. The machine's upper wheel turned slowly because friction had damaged its bearings. A stray dog began to growl when inspectors approached the abandoned loading dock. The lawyer said a forged instruction could induce workers to override a safe setting. The fishermen who inhabit these shores would need a permanent place on the safety board. Nothing in these records excused the people who had made the dangerous decision.

證人檔案裡被忽視的細節使問題更加複雜，而不是更簡單： 伊莉絲帶來以攝氏度校準的溫度計，以及測量河水熱能變化的探針。 機器上方的輪子轉得很慢，因為摩擦已損傷軸承。 檢查員靠近廢置裝貨區時，一隻流浪狗開始低吼。 律師說偽造指令可能誘使工人覆寫安全設定。 居住在這片海岸的漁民需要在安全委員會中擁有永久席次。 紀錄中沒有任何一項能替做出危險決定的人開脫。

For once the numbers did not merely decorate a report; they contradicted the order that had been issued in their name. Iris refuses to enforce the order and calls an emergency assembly of district delegates. Orren asked whether a mistake could explain the result. Iris agreed to test that possibility too. They checked an independent source, marked every assumption, and made copies before the originals could be transferred to an office beyond public reach.

這一次數字不只是報告裝飾，而是直接反駁以數字名義發布的命令。伊莉絲拒絕執行，召集各區代表舉行緊急會議。奧倫詢問是否可能只是出錯，伊莉絲也同意檢查這種可能。他們核對獨立來源、標記每項假設，在原件被移往公眾無法接觸的辦公室前留下副本。

A second bundle of observations brought the consequences closer to ordinary life: It was ironic that the trust's security account had been active long after its owner died. A retired mechanic lived on a grassy mound near the station. A peacock from a nearby garden wandered close to the temporary generator fence. A pianist played for patients while the clinic's backup system was tested. Nessa used a narrow probe to inspect the edge of the plate without touching the engraved letters. Taken alone, no detail settled the case; together they showed why a public answer mattered.

第二疊觀察紀錄使後果貼近普通人的生活： 諷刺的是，信託保全帳戶在原持有人死後很久仍活躍。 一名退休技師住在測站附近的草丘上。 附近花園的一隻孔雀走近臨時發電機圍欄。 診所測試備電系統時，一位鋼琴家為病人演奏。 妮莎用細探針檢查印版邊緣，沒有碰到刻字。 單獨看來沒有一項細節能結案，合在一起卻說明了公開答覆為何重要。

The debate sharpened when Hero Severin demanded a decision before the evidence could be copied. Iris replied that urgency and secrecy were not the same thing. They established a temporary limit, published the reason for it, and scheduled an independent review. The result satisfied nobody completely. That was acceptable; the purpose of the process was to make abuse harder, not to produce applause.

當英雄賽維林要求在證據複製前就做決定，辯論變得尖銳。伊莉絲回應說緊急和保密不是同一回事。團隊訂立暫時限制、公開理由，並排定獨立審查。結果沒有讓任何人完全滿意，但程序的目的本來就不是得到掌聲，而是使濫權更困難。

The team kept the concrete observations alongside the disputed theory: The respective duties of the city and company had been confused in the old emergency plan. Lira admitted that her previous shortsighted policy had made a second approval optional. Maelin tried to simplify the public explanation without removing the legal qualification from the original. The oldest Sun Engine stood under a narrow strait, its copper pipes concealed in the foundation of an abandoned customs house. The superficial inspection had missed heat damage deep inside the relay cabinet. Iris asked the clerk to preserve the original wording rather than improve the story.

團隊把具體觀察與有爭議的理論並列保存： 舊緊急計畫混淆了市府與公司的各自責任。 莉拉承認過去短視的政策使第二道核准變成可選。 伊莉絲試圖簡化公開說明，但不刪去原文的法律限定語。 最古老的太陽引擎位於狹窄海峽下方，銅管藏在廢棄海關屋的地基裡。 表面的檢查漏掉了繼電器櫃內部深處的熱損傷。 伊莉絲要求書記保存原本措辭，而不是把故事修飾得更好聽。

Black lines spread across a wall where a maintenance seal had cracked. Iris pressed her palm near the stone but did not touch it; the instrument showed that the surface was colder than the air. Repair was possible, yet each hour of neglect made the next repair more expensive.

維護封印裂開的牆上延伸出黑線。伊莉絲把手掌靠近石面卻沒有碰觸；儀器顯示石面比空氣更冷。修復並非不可能，但每疏忽一小時，下一次修繕就更加昂貴。

The record continued with small facts that would be lost in a confident speech: No officer was allowed to suppress a witness statement merely because it contradicted the palace account. The symmetry of the instruments mattered because each team had to measure the same change independently. An expired access token had been used to send the dormant account's last message. The public committee tried to unify the three separate emergency notification systems. The nurse emphasized the urgency of restoring the clinic's refrigerated medical supply. Orren marked what could be verified and left the rest open to challenge.

紀錄接著列出那些在自信演說中容易消失的小事實： 任何官員都不得只因證詞與王宮說法相反就壓下證言。 儀器的對稱配置很重要，因為每組人都必須獨立測量同一項變化。 最後一封休眠帳戶訊息使用了一枚過期存取憑證。 公共委員會試圖整合三套分離的緊急通知系統。 護士強調恢復診所冷藏醫療用品供應的急迫性。 奧倫標記能查證的部分，其餘保留給人質疑。

A message arrived after the public doors had closed. It carried no grand declaration, just a name and a time that did not match the official schedule. Iris resisted the urge to call it proof. Orren opened a fresh page in the journal and recorded the discrepancy exactly. They had won time, not certainty, and there were still people who might lose everything if they hurried.

公共大門關閉後，一則訊息送到。它沒有豪言壯語，只有一個名字和一個對不上官方時程的時間。伊莉絲克制住把它當成證明的衝動。奧倫翻開日誌新的一頁，逐字記錄差異。他們贏得的是時間，不是確定性；若急於求成，仍有人可能失去一切。
