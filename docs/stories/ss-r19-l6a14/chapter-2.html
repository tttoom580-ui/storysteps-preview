沒有魔王的王座廳

The Throne Room Without a King

魔王遭全民罷免後，世界更危險了 · 第 2 / 25 章 · 5000–6000 · 英中對照 · 校訂中

A messenger arrived before the bells had finished ringing. The news sounded impossible only because everyone had been trained not to question the institution that delivered it. Severin occupies the palace and orders the black seals removed as symbols of tyranny. Iris asked the messenger to repeat the part that had been omitted from the written notice. It was there, in the omission, that the day's real story began.

鐘聲尚未結束，信使已經抵達。消息之所以聽來不可能，只因每個人都習慣不質疑傳遞消息的制度。賽維林接管宮殿，命人拆掉象徵暴政的黑色封印。伊莉絲請信使重述書面公告遺漏的部分。這一天真正的故事，就從那處缺漏開始。

The ancient seals slowed the rift only by absorbing measurable energy from willing keepers. A seal could be inspected, repaired, or divided among communities; no ruler could manufacture that strength out of fear.

古老封印只能吸收自願守護者可測量的能量來減緩裂隙。封印能檢查、修理或分配各社區承擔，沒有統治者能從恐懼中憑空製造力量。

Black lines spread across a wall where a maintenance seal had cracked. Iris pressed her palm near the stone but did not touch it; the instrument showed that the surface was colder than the air. Repair was possible, yet each hour of neglect made the next repair more expensive.

維護封印裂開的牆上延伸出黑線。伊莉絲把手掌靠近石面卻沒有碰觸；儀器顯示石面比空氣更冷。修復並非不可能，但每疏忽一小時，下一次修繕就更加昂貴。

The evidence did not arrive as a confession. It came through a date that did not fit, a measurement that had been repeated, and a witness who knew exactly what the official account had left out. Iris asked Orren not to describe suspicion as proof. If the accusation was wrong, an innocent person would suffer. If it was right, careless language could still give the guilty a way to escape.

證據不是以自白形式出現，而是一個對不上的日期、一項重複測量的結果，以及一位知道官方說法遺漏何事的證人。伊莉絲請奧倫不要把懷疑說成證明。若指控錯了，無辜者會受害；若指控正確，草率措辭仍可能讓有罪者脫身。

The overlooked details in the witness file made the question harder, not easier: The imperial maps described the people of the upper valley as aboriginal clans; the term was not an excuse to erase their treaties. Small settlements abound along the ridges, where families tended terraced gardens under extreme weather. Iris tried to align their route with the royal charts, but the marked crossing had been destroyed months earlier. The substation's altitude made it safe from river water but difficult for older residents to reach. The magistrate concealed his annoyance when Iva asked him to repeat a legal question slowly. Orren marked what could be verified and left the rest open to challenge.

證人檔案裡被忽視的細節使問題更加複雜，而不是更簡單： 帝國地圖把上游居民稱為原住部族，但這種稱呼並不能成為抹去他們條約的理由。 山脊遍布小聚落，家家在嚴酷天候下照顧梯田。 伊莉絲試著讓路線與王室地圖對齊，卻發現標示的渡口早在數月前就毀了。 變電站的海拔讓它不易淹水，卻使年長居民難以前往。 伊娃請地方法官慢慢重述法律問題時，他掩飾住自己的不耐。 奧倫標記能查證的部分，其餘保留給人質疑。

A small act of honesty unsettled the room more than a shouted threat could have done. A nursery wall collapses where one seal stood, although no enemy has attacked. Iris watched a witness decide not to change a difficult detail merely because it complicated the preferred story. Orren recorded the correction openly. Their inquiry would be slower now, but its shape belonged less to the powerful and more to the people who had lived through the event.

一個小小的誠實舉動，帶來比喊叫威脅更強的震動。一面曾立有封印的育兒院牆突然坍塌，現場卻沒有任何敵人入侵。伊莉絲看見證人沒有因為細節讓主流說法變複雜就改口。奧倫公開記錄修正。調查會因此變慢，卻能較少受權貴支配，也更貼近親歷者。

A second bundle of observations brought the consequences closer to ordinary life: Iva gave a blunt answer: her neighbors had agreed to store grain, never to lose their homes. The clerk entered crossing in the testimony without deciding what it proved. Maelin would not discard a damaged draft until both sides had inspected its surviving lines. The river guard received a medical discharge note but still had to answer questions about his shift. Their joint endeavor would be assessed through measurements, not a flattering press release. Nothing in these records excused the people who had made the dangerous decision.

第二疊觀察紀錄使後果貼近普通人的生活： 伊娃直截了當地回答：鄰居同意保管糧食，從未同意失去家園。 書記將「crossing」記入證詞，沒有立即判定它能證明什麼。 伊莉絲不願在雙方檢查殘存字句前丟棄破損草稿。 河岸衛兵拿到出院證明後，仍須回答有關值班的問題。 雙方共同努力將以測量結果評估，而非以討好的新聞稿。 紀錄中沒有任何一項能替做出危險決定的人開脫。

No one in the room was permitted to declare that a good intention canceled a harmful action. Iris insisted on recording the error, identifying who had authorized it, and protecting those affected before assigning wider blame. Orren drew a simple boundary around what they could do tonight. The line looked disappointingly modest, yet it was the first promise that did not require another person to surrender a right.

室內沒有人能宣稱善意可以抵銷傷害。伊莉絲堅持記錄錯誤、確認授權者，並在追究更大責任前保護受害者。奧倫畫出今晚能做到的清楚界線。這條線看似微不足道，卻是第一個不要求別人放棄權利的承諾。

The team kept the concrete observations alongside the disputed theory: The first hurdle was proving that the old seal belonged to an actual village council. Lira changed her posture when the resident began speaking, listening without interruption. The interpreter demanded precision in the word for custody because a careless synonym could transfer ownership. The officer announced that travelers must undergo rigorous questioning, and that no royal family member could veto the security rules. At a wind-battered terrace, the party found an elderly widow repairing her roof. Taken alone, no detail settled the case; together they showed why a public answer mattered.

團隊把具體觀察與有爭議的理論並列保存： 第一道障礙是證明舊印章確實屬於村議會。 居民開始發言時，莉拉調整姿勢，沒有打斷對方。 譯員要求精確使用「保管」一詞，因為草率的同義詞可能轉移所有權。 軍官宣布旅人都須接受嚴格盤查，任何王室成員也不能否決安全規則。 在飽受狂風吹襲的梯田邊，隊伍找到一名修屋頂的年長寡婦。 單獨看來沒有一項細節能結案，合在一起卻說明了公開答覆為何重要。

The record continued with small facts that would be lost in a confident speech: A workers' uprising was not the issue; they were requesting the safety rights already promised. Someone had ordered soldiers to usher displaced families down the mountain, while sending the food intended for them to private storehouses. The versatile backup unit could power either a clinic refrigerator or an emergency radio. The local council chose not to veto the temporary food route merely because the palace disliked it. The widow refused payment but asked that her testimony be carried to an independent court. Iris asked the clerk to preserve the original wording rather than improve the story.

紀錄接著列出那些在自信演說中容易消失的小事實： 這不是工人起義；他們只是要求已被承諾的安全權利。 有人下令士兵引導流離失所的家庭下山，卻把原本供應他們的食物送到私人倉庫。 多功能備用裝置可供應診所冰箱或緊急無線電。 地方議會沒有只因王宮不喜歡就否決臨時糧食路線。 寡婦拒絕收錢，只求她把證詞交給獨立法庭。 伊莉絲要求書記保存原本措辭，而不是把故事修飾得更好聽。

On the way out, Iris passed people waiting for a result that would affect their meals, their homes, or their ability to say no. None of them had the luxury of treating the dispute as an intellectual puzzle. Orren paused to answer a child's practical question before they left. The answer was incomplete, but unlike the old official notice, it admitted the uncertainty out loud.

離開時，伊莉絲經過等待結果的人群，這項結果將影響他們的餐食、家園或說不的能力。他們不能奢侈地把爭論當成智力謎題。奧倫停下回答一名孩子實際的問題。答案並不完整，卻與舊公告不同：它坦白承認不確定之處。
