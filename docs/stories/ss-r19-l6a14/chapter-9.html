The Price of a Seal

The first sign of trouble was not an explosion or a scream. It was a familiar procedure producing a result nobody could defend. Iris tests a temporary seal with volunteers who can stop the work whenever the strain grows dangerous. Iris checked the time, the witnesses, and the rule that authorized the action. The three did not agree. Outside, ordinary life continued, which made the error more frightening rather than less.

麻煩的第一個徵兆不是爆炸或尖叫，而是熟悉程序得出了誰也無法辯護的結果。伊莉絲與志工測試臨時封印，所有人都能在負荷過高時停手。伊莉絲核對時間、證人與授權規則，三者卻互相矛盾。外頭的平凡生活照常進行，反而讓這個錯誤更加可怕。

Black lines spread across a wall where a maintenance seal had cracked. Iris pressed her palm near the stone but did not touch it; the instrument showed that the surface was colder than the air. Repair was possible, yet each hour of neglect made the next repair more expensive.

維護封印裂開的牆上延伸出黑線。伊莉絲把手掌靠近石面卻沒有碰觸；儀器顯示石面比空氣更冷。修復並非不可能，但每疏忽一小時，下一次修繕就更加昂貴。

Orren arrived with a second account that neither confirmed nor dismissed the first. They compared the routes by which each record had reached the room, then placed the uncertainties beside the facts. Iris could feel the pressure to announce a solution before anyone became frightened. That pressure was precisely why a second witness mattered: a conclusion that could not survive an independent question would be dangerous, even if it happened to be right.

奧倫帶來第二份說法，既未證實也未推翻第一份。兩人比對紀錄抵達房間的途徑，再把不確定之處和事實並列。伊莉絲感受到眾人希望立即宣布解方，以免恐懼擴大的壓力。但也正因這種壓力，第二位證人才重要：無法通過獨立質問的結論，即使碰巧正確，也可能很危險。

The overlooked details in the witness file made the question harder, not easier: The public statement might alienate frightened investors, but hiding the altered seal would be worse. A black blot on the purchase order concealed the first digit of a serial number. Residents shared chili soup while waiting for the procurement hearing to begin. The envoy explained that continuity of village access was guaranteed in the oldest agreement. The cumulative delays had increased costs far beyond the original repair estimate. Iris asked the clerk to preserve the original wording rather than improve the story.

證人檔案裡被忽視的細節使問題更加複雜，而不是更簡單： 公開聲明可能疏遠害怕的投資人，但隱瞞改動封條更糟。 採購單上的黑墨漬蓋住序號第一個數字。 居民等候採購聽證開始時，分享辣椒湯。 使者解釋，最早的協議保障村民持續使用這條路；後來的修正案逐漸刪除渡口相關文字，累積的省略使權利在沒有任何正式廢止令的情況下消失。 累積延誤讓成本遠超原始維修估算。 伊莉絲要求書記保存原本措辭，而不是把故事修飾得更好聽。

The contradiction reached the table just as the meeting was about to end. The results prove that no single person must be forced to suffer indefinitely. For a long moment no one spoke. Iris understood why silence felt comfortable; each answer threatened someone's settled account of how the world worked. Orren broke the stillness by asking who would be harmed if they simply filed the discovery away. No one could answer without naming a real person.

會議即將結束時，矛盾證據送上桌面。結果證明，沒有任何一個人必須被迫無限期承受痛苦。一時之間無人開口。伊莉絲明白沉默為何讓人舒服：任何答案都可能動搖某人對世界的既定理解。奧倫打破沉默，問若把發現歸檔，究竟誰會受害。沒人能在不指名真實人物的情況下回答。

A second bundle of observations brought the consequences closer to ordinary life: Lira made a decisive choice to publish the damaged-cabinet test before the vote. No worker was dispensable merely because an executive feared an embarrassing question. A visitor wore jasmine perfume that matched the scent noted by the night guard. The river lodge provided temporary rooms for families displaced during repairs. A maple tree near the entrance helped the courier identify the delivery location. Orren marked what could be verified and left the rest open to challenge.

第二疊觀察紀錄使後果貼近普通人的生活： 莉拉果斷決定在表決前公布受損櫃體測試。 不能因主管害怕尷尬提問，就把任何工人當成可有可無。 某位訪客使用茉莉香水，與夜班警衛記下的氣味一致。 河邊旅舍為維修期間搬離的家庭提供臨時房間。 入口旁的楓樹協助快遞員辨認送貨地點。 奧倫標記能查證的部分，其餘保留給人質疑。

There were two tempting shortcuts. One would protect the reputation of Hero Severin and leave the public guessing. The other would expose every private detail at once and call that transparency. Iris rejected both. The issue was who carries the burden of keeping the world intact, not whether the team could win an argument in a single afternoon. A smaller step, supported by independent observers, could protect people while the wider dispute remained open.

有兩種誘人的捷徑：一種保住英雄賽維林的名聲，讓大眾自行猜測；另一種把所有私人細節一次揭露，並稱之為透明。伊莉絲兩者都拒絕。核心是誰有責任承擔維持世界完整的代價，不是團隊能否在一個下午贏得爭論。由獨立觀察者支持的一小步，即使爭議未結，也能先保護民眾。

The team kept the concrete observations alongside the disputed theory: The mistress of the house showed a dated guest book recording the translator's last visit. In the next room, people debated how to mobilize villagers for a public meeting without provoking soldiers. Maelin began to muse about how many borders had been changed by ordinary-looking words. The whistleblower's persistence finally persuaded investigators to examine the old account. A socialist council member supported public oversight without claiming every private firm was guilty. Nothing in these records excused the people who had made the dangerous decision.

團隊把具體觀察與有爭議的理論並列保存： 女主人出示附日期的訪客簿，記錄老譯員最後一次來訪。 隔壁房裡，人們討論如何動員村民出席公開集會，又不激怒士兵。 伊莉絲開始沉思，究竟有多少邊界是被看似普通的字詞改變。 吹哨者的堅持終於讓調查員檢查舊帳戶。 一名社會主義議員支持公共監督，沒有宣稱所有私人企業都有罪。 紀錄中沒有任何一項能替做出危險決定的人開脫。

Black lines spread across a wall where a maintenance seal had cracked. Iris pressed her palm near the stone but did not touch it; the instrument showed that the surface was colder than the air. Repair was possible, yet each hour of neglect made the next repair more expensive.

維護封印裂開的牆上延伸出黑線。伊莉絲把手掌靠近石面卻沒有碰觸；儀器顯示石面比空氣更冷。修復並非不可能，但每疏忽一小時，下一次修繕就更加昂貴。

The record continued with small facts that would be lost in a confident speech: Employees had a chance to socialize after the hearing without being questioned by reporters. The access log covered a span of seven hours, with one unexplained gap near midnight. The author had been ordered to trek into the mountains and test the border granary before civilians were evacuated. A vigorous debate arose over whether the corrected map should be released immediately. A waterproof cover protected the pages, and the calculations were still legible. Taken alone, no detail settled the case; together they showed why a public answer mattered.

紀錄接著列出那些在自信演說中容易消失的小事實： 聽證後，員工終於能不受記者盤問地社交。 門禁紀錄涵蓋七小時，其中午夜附近有一段無法解釋的空白。 作者奉命徒步進入山區，在平民撤離前測試邊境穀倉。 對於是否立刻公開更正地圖，現場展開激烈辯論。 防水封套保存了紙頁，計算仍清晰可讀。 單獨看來沒有一項細節能結案，合在一起卻說明了公開答覆為何重要。

The chapter ended without an easy reassurance. Iris had learned one thing that could be checked and another that still needed an answer. Orren wrote both on the same page so that relief would not erase doubt. Their decision had moved the crisis forward, but it had also drawn the attention of Hero Severin. By morning, the next question would reach people far beyond this room.

這一天沒有簡單的安慰。伊莉絲知道了一件能查證的事，也留下另一件尚待回答的事。奧倫把兩者寫在同一頁，免得鬆一口氣就抹掉疑問。他們的決定使危機向前，也引起英雄賽維林注意。明早下一個問題將傳到房間外更遠的地方。
