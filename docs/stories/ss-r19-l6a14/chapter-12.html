城門下的學校

The School Beneath the Gate

魔王遭全民罷免後，世界更危險了 · 第 12 / 25 章 · 5000–6000 · 英中對照 · 校訂中

The room smelled of dust, hot metal, and rain carried in on people's coats. Someone had left an ordinary object on the central table, yet no one wanted to touch it. A school built inside an old tunnel provides records of repairs conducted by children’s families for generations. Iris saw that the object mattered less for what it was than for the decision it proved had been made. There would be no honest way back to ignorance.

室內有灰塵、熱金屬與人們外套帶進的雨味。有人把平凡物件放在中央桌上，卻沒有人願意碰它。城門下的學校保留了數代家庭共同維護裂隙的紀錄。伊莉絲明白重要的不是物件本身，而是它證明了何種決定已經發生。如今已不可能誠實地假裝無知。

Black lines spread across a wall where a maintenance seal had cracked. Iris pressed her palm near the stone but did not touch it; the instrument showed that the surface was colder than the air. Repair was possible, yet each hour of neglect made the next repair more expensive.

維護封印裂開的牆上延伸出黑線。伊莉絲把手掌靠近石面卻沒有碰觸；儀器顯示石面比空氣更冷。修復並非不可能，但每疏忽一小時，下一次修繕就更加昂貴。

The evidence did not arrive as a confession. It came through a date that did not fit, a measurement that had been repeated, and a witness who knew exactly what the official account had left out. Iris asked Orren not to describe suspicion as proof. If the accusation was wrong, an innocent person would suffer. If it was right, careless language could still give the guilty a way to escape.

證據不是以自白形式出現，而是一個對不上的日期、一項重複測量的結果，以及一位知道官方說法遺漏何事的證人。伊莉絲請奧倫不要把懷疑說成證明。若指控錯了，無辜者會受害；若指控正確，草率措辭仍可能讓有罪者脫身。

The overlooked details in the witness file made the question harder, not easier: A small brook flowed behind the pump house and concealed part of its old foundation. The canal rescue team removed fragile coral samples from a science display before flooding. Lira refused to deprive residents of access while the lease remained in dispute. Workers used a platform to elevate the backup batteries above the projected flood line. The lease had an expiration date, but no one could find its required public notice. Taken alone, no detail settled the case; together they showed why a public answer mattered.

證人檔案裡被忽視的細節使問題更加複雜，而不是更簡單： 抽水站後方的小溪遮住一部分舊地基。 洪水前，運河救援隊移走科學展示裡脆弱的珊瑚樣本。 租約仍有爭議時，莉拉拒絕剝奪居民的通行權。 工人用平台把備用電池抬高到預測淹水線以上。 租約有到期日，卻沒有人找得到法定公開通知。 單獨看來沒有一項細節能結案，合在一起卻說明了公開答覆為何重要。

A small act of honesty unsettled the room more than a shouted threat could have done. The records reveal that the first demon king was appointed as a custodian, not born as a ruler. Iris watched a witness decide not to change a difficult detail merely because it complicated the preferred story. Orren recorded the correction openly. Their inquiry would be slower now, but its shape belonged less to the powerful and more to the people who had lived through the event.

一個小小的誠實舉動，帶來比喊叫威脅更強的震動。紀錄顯示第一位魔王原是受託守護者，並非天生命定的統治者。伊莉絲看見證人沒有因為細節讓主流說法變複雜就改口。奧倫公開記錄修正。調查會因此變慢，卻能較少受權貴支配，也更貼近親歷者。

A second bundle of observations brought the consequences closer to ordinary life: The retired mechanic's failing eyesight made written testimony easier than reading small diagrams. A hearty meal was prepared for everyone stranded by the storm, including the visiting officials. A rescue bird seemed to hover above the water gate as the current rose. The inland authorities had demanded fees for boats using an island landing they did not own. The lawyer's insistence on public notice changed the entire lease negotiation. Iris asked the clerk to preserve the original wording rather than improve the story.

第二疊觀察紀錄使後果貼近普通人的生活： 退休技師視力衰退，書面作證比閱讀小圖更容易。 為所有因暴風雨受困的人準備了豐盛餐點，包括外來官員。 水位上漲時，一隻救援鳥似乎在水閘上方盤旋。 內陸當局曾向使用其無所有權島岸碼頭的船隻收費。 律師堅持公開通知，改變了整場租約談判。 伊莉絲要求書記保存原本措辭，而不是把故事修飾得更好聽。

Iris had expected the hardest part to be exposing the secret. Instead, it was choosing what to do after a partial truth became public. The people who had been ignored did not all want the same remedy. Orren proposed that each affected group could submit a written objection and receive a specific answer. The method would take time, but it recognized them as participants rather than scenery.

伊莉絲原以為最難的是揭露祕密，後來才發現，部分真相公開後如何行動才是難題。曾被忽略的人並不全想要相同補救。奧倫提議每個受影響群體都能提出書面異議並取得具體答覆。方法需要時間，但終於把他們當成參與者，而不是背景。

The team kept the concrete observations alongside the disputed theory: The vault displayed a coral ornament that had never been included in its lease. A visiting legislator asked whether the emergency access rule was enforceable after the trial. The majestic river painting concealed a modest note written by the original marsh owner. Anika used the storm's momentum to persuade rival officers to open a shared shelter. The monarch's seal appeared on the contract, but nobody could locate her signed approval. Orren marked what could be verified and left the rest open to challenge.

團隊把具體觀察與有爭議的理論並列保存： 密庫展示一件從未列入租約的珊瑚飾物。 來訪議員詢問試辦結束後，緊急通行規則是否仍可執行。 雄偉河景畫下藏著原沼地所有者寫的簡短註記。 安妮卡利用風暴帶來的迫切感，說服對立官員開放共用避難所。 契約上有君主印章，卻無人找到她親筆核准。 奧倫標記能查證的部分，其餘保留給人質疑。

Black lines spread across a wall where a maintenance seal had cracked. Iris pressed her palm near the stone but did not touch it; the instrument showed that the surface was colder than the air. Repair was possible, yet each hour of neglect made the next repair more expensive.

維護封印裂開的牆上延伸出黑線。伊莉絲把手掌靠近石面卻沒有碰觸；儀器顯示石面比空氣更冷。修復並非不可能，但每疏忽一小時，下一次修繕就更加昂貴。

The record continued with small facts that would be lost in a confident speech: An animal shelter asked for reliable heating to protect newborn offspring during power cuts. A petrol generator was retained as a backup only after its emissions were tested. The operator had to rotate the valve slowly to prevent a sudden pressure change. Maelin warned that the false fee might strangle the island's small repair trade over time. The physician explained that exhausted muscles could fail during a critical spell, just as poor surgical instruments could fail in an emergency. Nothing in these records excused the people who had made the dangerous decision.

紀錄接著列出那些在自信演說中容易消失的小事實： 動物收容所要求可靠暖氣，以保護圍城期間剛出生的幼獸。 汽油發電機通過排放測試後才被保留作備用。 操作員必須緩慢旋轉閥門，以免壓力突然變化。 伊莉絲警告，不實收費長期可能扼殺島上小型修船業。 醫師解釋，疲憊肌肉可能在關鍵施法時失效，就像不良外科器械在急救時會出問題。 紀錄中沒有任何一項能替做出危險決定的人開脫。

When the lamps were lowered, Iris returned to a broken black seal. It was neither proof of innocence nor a shortcut to justice. It was a reminder that the next decision would be witnessed by people who no longer trusted easy promises. Orren remained at the door while the final record was sealed. Outside, the city of Blackgate waited for news that had to be more precise than a victory speech.

燈光調暗時，伊莉絲再次看向一枚裂開的黑色封印。它既不是清白證明，也不是通往正義的捷徑，而是在提醒眾人：下一個決定會由不再相信輕易承諾的人見證。奧倫守在門邊，最後一份紀錄被封存。外頭的黑門城等待的消息，必須比勝利演說更精確。
