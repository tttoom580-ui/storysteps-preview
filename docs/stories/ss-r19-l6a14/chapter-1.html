紅天之下的選舉

An Election Under a Red Sky

魔王遭全民罷免後，世界更危險了 · 第 1 / 25 章 · 5000–6000 · 英中對照 · 校訂中

The demon king lost the election at sunset. At midnight, the moon cracked. Iris had spent six months making sure every ballot could be counted twice and every resident, human or demon, could challenge a false name. She had defended the recall against people who feared the king’s power. When the final numbers appeared, the crowd cheered and an orderly carried the empty throne into storage. King Orren said nothing. He removed his black ring, set it beside the royal warrant, and walked away without guards. Iris thought that the story had ended. Then every streetlamp in Blackgate went out at exactly the same moment.

魔王在日落時輸掉選舉。午夜時，月亮裂開了。伊莉絲花了六個月，確保每張選票都能重算兩次，也確保每位居民，不論人類或魔族，都可以對假姓名提出異議。她曾頂著畏懼魔王權力的反對者，捍衛這次罷免。最終票數公布時，群眾歡呼，勤務員把空王座搬進倉庫。魔王奧倫沒有說話。他取下黑色戒指，放在王室令狀旁，沒有帶守衛就離開。伊莉絲以為故事結束了，接著黑門城每盞路燈在同一瞬間熄滅。

The first crack appeared at the junction of the old market and the east wall. A cart wheel slipped into it; a porter with a limp pulled a frightened child clear just before the stones folded inward. It was not an earthquake. The movement followed the pale lines carved beneath the street. Iris had seen those lines in an archive, labeled an anchor system for ancient magic, but no report explained what they held in place. She ordered the gates closed and asked for a comparative survey of the damage. Hero Severin called the precautions an annoyance. “The world is finally free,” he said. “Stop looking for monsters.”

第一道裂縫出現在舊市集與東城牆交會處。馬車輪滑進裂口，一名跛腳搬運工趕在石塊向內折陷前把受驚的孩子拉出來。那不是地震，地面變形沿著街底刻出的蒼白線條延伸。伊莉絲曾在檔案裡看過這些線，標示為古代魔法的錨定系統，卻沒有報告解釋它們固定著什麼。她下令關閉城門，要求比較測量損傷。英雄賽維林稱這些預防措施只會添麻煩。「世界終於自由了，」他說，「別再到處找怪物。」

In the emergency hall, volunteers were broiling fish while families gathered around the last working furnace. A widow held a sleeping infant to her bosom; another woman looked through a donation crate for a warm shirt and bra after the collapse ruined her home. These ordinary needs made the disaster more real than any legend. A bank teller brought notice that a gangster had offered a bribe to make the maintenance fund disappear from public accounts. Iris refused to abbreviate the witness’s name, though the man trembled when the clerk read it aloud. The account was applicable to twelve districts, yet only one had ever been told why the money was collected.

緊急大廳裡，志工炙烤魚，家庭圍在最後一座仍能使用的暖爐旁。一名寡婦把睡著的嬰兒抱在胸前；另一位婦女因房子倒塌，在捐贈箱尋找暖和上衣和胸罩。這些日常需求比任何傳說都更能說明災難的真實。一名銀行職員帶來通知：黑幫分子曾提出賄賂，要讓維護基金從公共帳目消失。伊莉絲拒絕縮寫證人姓名，即使書記朗讀時那人全身發抖。這筆基金適用十二個街區，卻只有一區曾被告知收費原因。

Iris found an old engineer behind the furnace. One eyelid drooped from an injury he had received while repairing a seal twenty years earlier. He asked permission to narrate what the official history had omitted. The first demon king, he explained, had not conquered Blackgate. The city had elected him to carry a burden that no one community could bear alone. Every month he had moved some of that strain through an underground network. The power was finite; remove the black seals without replacing them, and the border between worlds would open. Iris looked at the new hero, who was already instructing workers to destroy the remaining seals.

伊莉絲在暖爐後方找到一名老工程師。他一隻眼皮因二十年前修補封印時受傷而下垂。他請求允許敘述官方歷史遺漏的部分。工程師說，第一位魔王不是征服黑門城，而是被城市選出來，承擔任何單一社區都無法獨力負荷的工作。每個月，他都透過地下網路分散負擔。力量是有限的；若拆掉黑色封印而沒有替代方案，兩個世界的邊界就會打開。伊莉絲看向新英雄，他已開始命令工人拆除剩下的封印。

“Do not summon the former king,” Severin warned. “His followers will only ask him to take the crown again.” Iris refused that false choice. Restoring a person’s power was not the same as consulting his knowledge. She wrote a temporary safety order on virgin parchment and invited representatives from every district to inspect the damaged lines. The city could flourish without a demon monarch, but only if the burden were visible and shareable. Her first proposal would require twenty-four volunteers and twelve hours of precise measurement. Severin demanded that she cancel it. Iris showed him the casualty reports and asked him to read them himself.

「別召回前魔王，」賽維林警告，「他的追隨者只會要求他重戴王冠。」伊莉絲拒絕這個假兩難。恢復一個人的權力，不等於向他請教知識。她在全新羊皮紙上寫下臨時安全令，邀請各區代表檢查受損線條。黑門城可以沒有魔王而繁榮，但前提是負擔必須看得見，也能共享。她的第一份方案需要二十四名志工和十二小時精密測量。賽維林要求取消，伊莉絲拿出傷亡報告，請他親自閱讀。

The old engineer raised the diagram toward the moon. Its crack had widened, and beyond the red edge something enormous seemed to turn in its sleep. Iris did not believe a problem became acceptable because a hero had inherited it. She sent a messenger after Orren, bearing neither an apology nor a crown, but one question: “What have you been keeping outside our sky?” When the messenger reached the empty road, a reply was already nailed to the gate. It was written in Orren’s hand: “Do not reopen the throne. Open the maintenance journal.” Beneath that sentence was a second warning. “You have six days before the first seal fails.”

老工程師把圖紙舉向月亮。裂縫已經變寬，紅色邊緣後方似乎有巨大之物在睡夢中轉身。伊莉絲不認為問題交給英雄繼承就會變得可以接受。她派信使追上奧倫，帶去的不是道歉或王冠，而是一個問題：「你一直把什麼擋在我們的天空之外？」信使抵達空路時，城門上已釘好回信。奧倫的筆跡寫著：「不要重開王座。打開維護日誌。」底下還有第二句警告：「第一枚封印將在六天後失效。」
