The Banished Engineer

魔王遭全民罷免後，世界更危險了 · 第 6 / 25 章 · 5000–6000 · 英中對照 · 校訂中

The room smelled of dust, hot metal, and rain carried in on people's coats. Someone had left an ordinary object on the central table, yet no one wanted to touch it. Iris finds an engineer exiled for warning that the black seals were a form of public infrastructure. Iris saw that the object mattered less for what it was than for the decision it proved had been made. There would be no honest way back to ignorance.

室內有灰塵、熱金屬與人們外套帶進的雨味。有人把平凡物件放在中央桌上，卻沒有人願意碰它。伊莉絲找到遭放逐的工程師，他曾警告眾人黑色封印其實是公共基礎設施。伊莉絲明白重要的不是物件本身，而是它證明了何種決定已經發生。如今已不可能誠實地假裝無知。

Black lines spread across a wall where a maintenance seal had cracked. Iris pressed her palm near the stone but did not touch it; the instrument showed that the surface was colder than the air. Repair was possible, yet each hour of neglect made the next repair more expensive.

維護封印裂開的牆上延伸出黑線。伊莉絲把手掌靠近石面卻沒有碰觸；儀器顯示石面比空氣更冷。修復並非不可能，但每疏忽一小時，下一次修繕就更加昂貴。

A witness asked whether the inquiry would make tomorrow safer. Iris could not promise that. The truth could establish responsibility, but it would not repair a broken machine, feed a hungry child, or return lost time on its own. Orren suggested a temporary protection that could be checked after sunrise. They wrote down its limits in plain language and invited the people affected to challenge them.

一位證人問調查是否能讓明天更安全，伊莉絲無法保證。真相能確立責任，卻不會自行修好機器、餵飽孩子或歸還失去的時間。奧倫提出一項日出後可以檢驗的暫時保護措施。他們用白話寫清限制，邀請受影響的人提出異議。

The overlooked details in the witness file made the question harder, not easier: The city council could allege misconduct, but Maelin asked it to identify the specific page and signature. People had tried to beautify the hall with flowers for the midsummer festival, but protest notices covered the gates. Maelin traced the caption to a committee meeting where no qualified river interpreter had been present. The contract dispute reached its climax when the two shipping weights appeared together. Nessa used a special lamp to extract faint writing from beneath the later ink without damaging the sheet. Orren marked what could be verified and left the rest open to challenge.

證人檔案裡被忽視的細節使問題更加複雜，而不是更簡單： 市議會可以指控不當行為，但伊莉絲要求指出具體頁碼與簽名。 人們原本用花朵美化大廳，迎接仲夏節，如今大門卻貼滿抗議公告。 伊莉絲查出圖說來自一場沒有合格河語口譯員出席的委員會議。 當兩張出貨重量表一起出現時，契約爭議達到高潮。 妮莎利用特殊燈具辨認後加墨水下的淡字，沒有損壞紙張。 奧倫標記能查證的部分，其餘保留給人質疑。

For once the numbers did not merely decorate a report; they contradicted the order that had been issued in their name. His blueprints show that Orren's personal power was only one component; the council could distribute the load. Orren asked whether a mistake could explain the result. Iris agreed to test that possibility too. They checked an independent source, marked every assumption, and made copies before the originals could be transferred to an office beyond public reach.

這一次數字不只是報告裝飾，而是直接反駁以數字名義發布的命令。他的藍圖證明奧倫的力量只是一部分，議會原本可以分擔負荷。奧倫詢問是否可能只是出錯，伊莉絲也同意檢查這種可能。他們核對獨立來源、標記每項假設，在原件被移往公眾無法接觸的辦公室前留下副本。

A second bundle of observations brought the consequences closer to ordinary life: A delegation of academy students had arrived after an extracurricular debate class. Their teacher was hospitable, offering water and a shady bench to witnesses who had traveled far. The usually jolly driver grew quiet when he recognized the private auction seal. Workers offered to mow the neglected path so elderly witnesses could reach the hall. The clerk, a cheerful man with a large mustache, agreed to write the words exactly as spoken. Nothing in these records excused the people who had made the dangerous decision.

第二疊觀察紀錄使後果貼近普通人的生活： 一群學院學生參加課外辯論課後來到法庭。 他們的老師十分好客，為遠道而來的證人準備飲水與陰涼長椅。 平日開朗的車夫認出私人拍賣印章時沉默了。 工人提議修剪荒廢小徑，方便年長證人到會堂。 留著大鬍子的開朗書記答應逐字記錄。 紀錄中沒有任何一項能替做出危險決定的人開脫。

No one in the room was permitted to declare that a good intention canceled a harmful action. Iris insisted on recording the error, identifying who had authorized it, and protecting those affected before assigning wider blame. Orren drew a simple boundary around what they could do tonight. The line looked disappointingly modest, yet it was the first promise that did not require another person to surrender a right.

室內沒有人能宣稱善意可以抵銷傷害。伊莉絲堅持記錄錯誤、確認授權者，並在追究更大責任前保護受害者。奧倫畫出今晚能做到的清楚界線。這條線看似微不足道，卻是第一個不要求別人放棄權利的承諾。

The team kept the concrete observations alongside the disputed theory: A grandmother told him to stop nagging and gave him an ounce of sweet tea. The old cafeteria became an oasis for night workers exhausted by the investigation. The sealing wax weighed less than an ounce, yet its broken edge offered a useful comparison. The civic court met beside a public garden on the outskirts of the capital. In the afternoon, a member of parliament appeared with a packet of engineering drawings. Taken alone, no detail settled the case; together they showed why a public answer mattered.

團隊把具體觀察與有爭議的理論並列保存： 一位祖母要他別再嘮叨，遞給他一小杯甜茶。 舊餐廳成為調查期間疲憊夜班工人的休息綠洲。 封蠟不到一盎司重，破損邊緣卻提供有用的比對依據。 公民法庭位於京城郊區的公共花園旁。 下午，一名國會議員帶著工程圖出席。 單獨看來沒有一項細節能結案，合在一起卻說明了公開答覆為何重要。

Black lines spread across a wall where a maintenance seal had cracked. Iris pressed her palm near the stone but did not touch it; the instrument showed that the surface was colder than the air. Repair was possible, yet each hour of neglect made the next repair more expensive.

維護封印裂開的牆上延伸出黑線。伊莉絲把手掌靠近石面卻沒有碰觸；儀器顯示石面比空氣更冷。修復並非不可能，但每疏忽一小時，下一次修繕就更加昂貴。

The record continued with small facts that would be lost in a confident speech: The nobleman's methods were ruthless but often lawful on paper: he delayed funding, demanded impossible permits, and threatened public employees with dismissal. A sculptor among them carried a model of the underground chambers, built from plaster so ordinary citizens could understand the design. The repair team preferred simplicity in emergency instructions over impressive technical language. A simultaneous release of both chambers was therefore forbidden until the second inspection. Public confidence fell into a slump when the palace refused to explain the disputed caption. Iris asked the clerk to preserve the original wording rather than improve the story.

紀錄接著列出那些在自信演說中容易消失的小事實： 這名貴族的方法冷酷，但在紙面上常常合法：延後經費、索取不可能取得的許可，並以解雇威脅公務員。 其中一位雕塑家帶著石膏製的地下艙室模型，好讓一般市民看懂設計。 維修團隊偏好簡單的緊急指示，而非炫目的技術詞。 因此，在第二次檢查以前，兩個艙室不得同時放能。 王宮拒絕解釋爭議圖說，公眾信心因此陷入低潮。 伊莉絲要求書記保存原本措辭，而不是把故事修飾得更好聽。

On the way out, Iris passed people waiting for a result that would affect their meals, their homes, or their ability to say no. None of them had the luxury of treating the dispute as an intellectual puzzle. Orren paused to answer a child's practical question before they left. The answer was incomplete, but unlike the old official notice, it admitted the uncertainty out loud.

離開時，伊莉絲經過等待結果的人群，這項結果將影響他們的餐食、家園或說不的能力。他們不能奢侈地把爭論當成智力謎題。奧倫停下回答一名孩子實際的問題。答案並不完整，卻與舊公告不同：它坦白承認不確定之處。
