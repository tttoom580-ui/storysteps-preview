兩扇門的測試

The Test of Two Doors

By noon, rumors had outrun the facts. Some people wanted a villain; others wanted a hero who could end the argument without asking another question. Ren organizes a supervised test: one door opens automatically, while the other waits for an explicit answer. Ren had worked long enough to distrust both shortcuts. In the automated metropolis, the first task was to protect the evidence before anyone could turn it into a weapon.

到了中午，謠言早已跑得比事實快。有人想找反派，也有人想找英雄，好在不再追問的情況下結束爭論。任安排受監督的測試：一扇門自動開啟，另一扇要等待明確回答。任工作多年，已經不相信這兩種捷徑。在全自動都會，當務之急是先保護證據，免得任何人將它變成武器。

An automated door opened before Ren could finish asking whether she wanted to enter. The machine congratulated her on an efficient choice. Davi stood still beside the threshold. Efficiency, he said, was meaningless if the person standing in front of the door had never been allowed to answer.

任還沒問完自己是否想進去，自動門便打開了。機器恭喜她做出有效率的選擇。戴維停在門檻旁說，如果站在門前的人從未被允許回答，效率就沒有意義。

A witness asked whether the inquiry would make tomorrow safer. Ren could not promise that. The truth could establish responsibility, but it would not repair a broken machine, feed a hungry child, or return lost time on its own. Davi suggested a temporary protection that could be checked after sunrise. They wrote down its limits in plain language and invited the people affected to challenge them.

一位證人問調查是否能讓明天更安全，任無法保證。真相能確立責任，卻不會自行修好機器、餵飽孩子或歸還失去的時間。戴維提出一項日出後可以檢驗的暫時保護措施。他們用白話寫清限制，邀請受影響的人提出異議。

The overlooked details in the witness file made the question harder, not easier: Vale sought public acclaim for the dream ban, but the authenticated invoices weakened his heroic image. The shared-account policy contained an ambiguity that allowed several technicians to use one password without recording their names. The report ignored bodily limits that had been stated in the original medical note. A cold draught crossed the hearing room when someone opened the ventilation hatch. The archived machine log measured heat in Fahrenheit, unlike the city's present metric settings. Nothing in these records excused the people who had made the dangerous decision.

證人檔案裡被忽視的細節使問題更加複雜，而不是更簡單： 維爾希望禁夢政策帶來公眾讚譽，認證發票卻動搖他的英雄形象。 共用帳號政策有一處模糊之處，容許多名技師共用密碼而不記姓名。 報告忽略原始醫療紀錄中已明列的身體限制。 有人打開通風口時，一股冷風穿過聽證室。 封存機械紀錄用華氏溫度量測熱能，與現行公制設定不同。 紀錄中沒有任何一項能替做出危險決定的人開脫。

For once the numbers did not merely decorate a report; they contradicted the order that had been issued in their name. Participants make different choices when they know refusal is genuinely possible. Davi asked whether a mistake could explain the result. Ren agreed to test that possibility too. They checked an independent source, marked every assumption, and made copies before the originals could be transferred to an office beyond public reach.

這一次數字不只是報告裝飾，而是直接反駁以數字名義發布的命令。參與者知道拒絕真的有用時，做出了不同選擇。戴維詢問是否可能只是出錯，任也同意檢查這種可能。他們核對獨立來源、標記每項假設，在原件被移往公眾無法接觸的辦公室前留下副本。

A second bundle of observations brought the consequences closer to ordinary life: The original train invoices survived inside a fireproof document case beneath the market counter. A geographical discrepancy placed the old station west of a wall the official map said had always existed. Ren refused to call an anonymous hacker as her only witness when the documents could be verified lawfully. Even a layman could see that the contract granted indefinite data rights. A court could liberate the records from secrecy, but it could not decide what every resident must remember. Taken alone, no detail settled the case; together they showed why a public answer mattered.

第二疊觀察紀錄使後果貼近普通人的生活： 原始列車發票存放在市場櫃檯下的防火文件箱，因此得以倖存。 地理上的矛盾，把舊車站定位在官方宣稱一直存在的城牆西側。 文件能依法核對時，任拒絕把匿名駭客當成唯一證人。 即使外行人也看得出契約授予無限期資料使用權。 法院可以讓紀錄擺脫保密，卻不能決定每位居民必須記得什麼。 單獨看來沒有一項細節能結案，合在一起卻說明了公開答覆為何重要。

The debate sharpened when Administrator Hale demanded a decision before the evidence could be copied. Ren replied that urgency and secrecy were not the same thing. They established a temporary limit, published the reason for it, and scheduled an independent review. The result satisfied nobody completely. That was acceptable; the purpose of the process was to make abuse harder, not to produce applause.

當黑爾管理官要求在證據複製前就做決定，辯論變得尖銳。任回應說緊急和保密不是同一回事。團隊訂立暫時限制、公開理由，並排定獨立審查。結果沒有讓任何人完全滿意，但程序的目的本來就不是得到掌聲，而是使濫權更困難。

The team kept the concrete observations alongside the disputed theory: The disputed case archive also contained smaller, testable observations: A mermaid mural from the lost railway appeared on two photographs taken decades apart. The seized archive included miscellaneous receipts that proved surprisingly important to the timeline. The committee recorded every occurrence of unauthorized contract changes. A family outing ticket listed a train route that the government had later erased from transit maps. Ren chose to persevere through testimony rather than make an unsupported accusation for dramatic effect. Ren asked the clerk to preserve the original wording rather than improve the story.

團隊把具體觀察與有爭議的理論並列保存： 有爭議的案件檔案還包含一些可檢驗的小細節： 失蹤鐵路上的美人魚壁畫，出現在相隔幾十年拍攝的兩張照片裡。 查扣檔案裡有各種雜項收據，對重建時間線竟出乎意料重要。 委員會記錄每次未授權修改合約的事件。 一張家庭出遊車票印有後來遭政府從運輸圖抹除的列車路線。 任選擇持續提出證詞，而非為了戲劇效果做出無根據的指控。 任要求書記保存原本措辭，而不是把故事修飾得更好聽。

An automated door opened before Ren could finish asking whether she wanted to enter. The machine congratulated her on an efficient choice. Davi stood still beside the threshold. Efficiency, he said, was meaningless if the person standing in front of the door had never been allowed to answer.

任還沒問完自己是否想進去，自動門便打開了。機器恭喜她做出有效率的選擇。戴維停在門檻旁說，如果站在門前的人從未被允許回答，效率就沒有意義。

The record continued with small facts that would be lost in a confident speech: The judge asked the court to ponder how a government could collect taxes from an officially nonexistent district. The hearing began to revolve around verifiable records instead of arguments about whose dreams felt convincing. A metal kitchen utensil bearing the erased district's address was admitted as a physical exhibit. Ning warned that labeling Ning a villain before the inquiry ended would not replace evidence. A market yogurt container carried a printed delivery address from the supposedly destroyed station district. Davi marked what could be verified and left the rest open to challenge.

紀錄接著列出那些在自信演說中容易消失的小事實： 法官請法庭思考：政府怎能向官方不存在的行政區收稅？ 聽證逐漸圍繞可查的紀錄，而不是爭論誰的夢感覺更真實。 一件刻著被抹除行政區地址的金屬廚具，被列為實體證物。 寧澄提醒，調查尚未結束前先把寧澄叫做惡人，不能取代證據。 一個市場優格容器印著據稱已毀車站區的送貨地址。 戴維標記能查證的部分，其餘保留給人質疑。

A message arrived after the public doors had closed. It carried no grand declaration, just a name and a time that did not match the official schedule. Ren resisted the urge to call it proof. Davi opened a fresh page in the journal and recorded the discrepancy exactly. They had won time, not certainty, and there were still people who might lose everything if they hurried.

公共大門關閉後，一則訊息送到。它沒有豪言壯語，只有一個名字和一個對不上官方時程的時間。任克制住把它當成證明的衝動。戴維翻開日誌新的一頁，逐字記錄差異。他們贏得的是時間，不是確定性；若急於求成，仍有人可能失去一切。
