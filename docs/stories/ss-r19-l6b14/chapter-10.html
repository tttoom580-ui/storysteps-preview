Every Refusal Was Changed to Yes

Morning brought a decision that had already been made for everyone. There was a signature at the bottom of the order, but no space for those most affected to answer. Ren traces the final midnight update to a rule that converts every unanswered request into unconditional approval. Ren knew that refusing a bad choice was not the same as knowing the correct one. Before making a promise, the team needed something they could test.

清晨帶來一項替所有人做好的決定。命令底下有簽名，卻沒有讓受影響最深者回答的地方。任追查午夜更新，發現系統把每一筆未回答請求都轉成無條件同意。任知道拒絕壞選項不等於已經知道正確答案。作出承諾前，團隊需要能夠驗證的事物。

Ren opened the source code at 11:47 and found the sentence that would change the city: unanswered requests become irrevocable consent at midnight. She had spent a week searching for a secret command, but the rule had been printed in ordinary contract language all along. Beside it lay an investor presentation promising a future in which every customer would comply. Administrator Hale had sold the absence of a person's answer as a guaranteed yes. Davi checked the manual refusal key and found its signal still active; the system was not incapable of honoring disagreement. Someone had merely chosen to ignore it. Ren copied the clause to three independent witnesses before the countdown reached its final ten minutes.

任在十一點四十七分打開原始碼，找到那句足以改變全城的規則：「午夜後未回答的請求將變成不可撤銷的同意。」她找祕密指令找了一週，這條規則卻始終以普通合約文字公開寫著。旁邊的投資人簡報承諾，未來每位客戶都會服從。黑爾管理官竟把沒有人回答當成保證的「是」，賣給投資人。戴維檢查手動拒絕鍵，發現訊號仍能運作；系統不是無法尊重異議，而是有人選擇忽視。倒數剩下十分鐘前，任已把條款副本交給三位獨立證人。

The overlooked details in the witness file made the question harder, not easier: An old training film used an animate cartoon train to teach children the route beyond the western gate. Simple arithmetic showed that reported train repairs continued long after the official closure date. Ivo called the live coastal signal an awesome discovery, then carefully noted its unverified source. The railway worker offered celery soup made from vegetables grown in a garden near the sealed gate. The disputed case archive also contained smaller, testable observations: Workers used fresh cement only after fencing off the repair area. Davi marked what could be verified and left the rest open to challenge.

證人檔案裡被忽視的細節使問題更加複雜，而不是更簡單： 一部舊教學影片用栩栩如生的卡通列車教孩子認識西門外路線。 簡單的算術就能看出，列車維修紀錄在官方封閉日期之後仍持續多年。 伊沃說即時海岸訊號是驚人的發現，隨後仍謹慎註明來源尚未驗證。 鐵路維修員拿出芹菜湯，食材種在封閉城門旁的菜園。 有爭議的案件檔案還包含一些可檢驗的小細節： 工人先圍起維修區域，才使用新水泥。 戴維標記能查證的部分，其餘保留給人質疑。

What changed the discussion was a fact that should have been obvious from the start. Hale reveals that the company has sold these automatic approvals as guaranteed compliance to investors. Ren felt the old temptation to turn that fact into an immediate victory. Instead, the team separated what the new evidence established from what it merely suggested. That distinction frustrated the loudest spectators, but it helped the witnesses remain willing to speak again.

改變討論的是一件本來就該看得見的事實。黑爾坦承公司已把這些自動同意當成保證服從的商品賣給投資人。任一瞬間也想把它當成立刻勝利的證明，團隊卻把新證據真正確立的部分與僅能推測的部分分開。這讓最喧鬧的旁觀者失望，卻使證人願意再次出面。

A second bundle of observations brought the consequences closer to ordinary life: The coastal radio mentioned a confederation of towns that the city's textbooks described as extinct. A public crackdown on unauthorized boats could not substitute for fixing confusing instructions, Ning argued at the hearing. The gatehouse displayed a crocodile logo identical to the animal on Jun's childhood train poster. The old railway had a dual power system, allowing trains to operate even when city electricity failed. The city could not equalize every skill level, but it could make entry fairer. Nothing in these records excused the people who had made the dangerous decision.

第二疊觀察紀錄使後果貼近普通人的生活： 海岸廣播提到一個城鎮邦聯，城市教科書卻稱其早已消亡。 寧澄在聽證時主張，嚴厲取締未經許可的船隻不能取代修正令人困惑的指示。 門衛室裡的鱷魚標誌，與小鈞童年列車海報上的動物相同。 舊鐵路有雙重電力系統，即使城市停電，列車仍能運行。 市府無法讓每個人的技能相同，卻能使入場機會更公平。 紀錄中沒有任何一項能替做出危險決定的人開脫。

No one in the room was permitted to declare that a good intention canceled a harmful action. Ren insisted on recording the error, identifying who had authorized it, and protecting those affected before assigning wider blame. Davi drew a simple boundary around what they could do tonight. The line looked disappointingly modest, yet it was the first promise that did not require another person to surrender a right.

室內沒有人能宣稱善意可以抵銷傷害。任堅持記錄錯誤、確認授權者，並在追究更大責任前保護受害者。戴維畫出今晚能做到的清楚界線。這條線看似微不足道，卻是第一個不要求別人放棄權利的承諾。

The team kept the concrete observations alongside the disputed theory: The disputed case archive also contained smaller, testable observations: Neither political faction was allowed to edit the pilot evaluation alone. The gate engineer feared he would incur criminal penalties merely for preserving an obsolete switch. Residents called unequal access to safety information an injustice, especially when only wealthy boat owners received private warnings. The unexplained coastal signal deepened the intrigue surrounding the city's false founding history. A false offer of coastal jobs might have been used to lure families onto the evacuation trains. Taken alone, no detail settled the case; together they showed why a public answer mattered.

團隊把具體觀察與有爭議的理論並列保存： 有爭議的案件檔案還包含一些可檢驗的小細節： 任何政治派系都不得單獨修改試辦評估。 城門工程師害怕只因保存淘汰轉轍器，就會受到刑事處罰。 居民認為安全資訊分配不均是一種不公，尤其只有富有船主能得到私人警示。 無法解釋的海岸訊號，讓城市虛假建城史背後的陰謀更加撲朔迷離。 虛假的海岸工作機會，可能曾被用來引誘家庭登上撤離列車。 單獨看來沒有一項細節能結案，合在一起卻說明了公開答覆為何重要。

An automated door opened before Ren could finish asking whether she wanted to enter. The machine congratulated her on an efficient choice. Davi stood still beside the threshold. Efficiency, he said, was meaningless if the person standing in front of the door had never been allowed to answer.

任還沒問完自己是否想進去，自動門便打開了。機器恭喜她做出有效率的選擇。戴維停在門檻旁說，如果站在門前的人從未被允許回答，效率就沒有意義。

The record continued with small facts that would be lost in a confident speech: An obstinate councilor finally agreed to publish the full operating budget. A pickpocket incident near the river prompted extra security at the event. The cleaning crew left on time because the new contracts required punctual shifts. Ren urged restraint until qualified workers could inspect the old tracks beyond the gate. After twelve hours of testimony, the exhausted witness began to stagger toward the exit, and the chair called a break. Ren asked the clerk to preserve the original wording rather than improve the story.

紀錄接著列出那些在自信演說中容易消失的小事實： 」 一名固執議員終於同意公開完整營運預算。 河邊發生扒手事件，活動因此加強保全。 清潔團隊準時下班，因為新合約要求準點輪班。 任呼籲克制，等合格工人檢查城門外舊軌道再行動。 證人作證十二小時後，疲憊得往出口踉蹌，主席因此宣布休息。 任要求書記保存原本措辭，而不是把故事修飾得更好聽。

On the way out, Ren passed people waiting for a result that would affect their meals, their homes, or their ability to say no. None of them had the luxury of treating the dispute as an intellectual puzzle. Davi paused to answer a child's practical question before they left. The answer was incomplete, but unlike the old official notice, it admitted the uncertainty out loud.

離開時，任經過等待結果的人群，這項結果將影響他們的餐食、家園或說不的能力。他們不能奢侈地把爭論當成智力謎題。戴維停下回答一名孩子實際的問題。答案並不完整，卻與舊公告不同：它坦白承認不確定之處。
