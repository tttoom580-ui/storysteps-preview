The Clause Nobody Read

全人類的自由意志，將在午夜到期 · 第 2 / 25 章 · 5000–6000 · 英中對照 · 校訂中

A messenger arrived before the bells had finished ringing. The news sounded impossible only because everyone had been trained not to question the institution that delivered it. Ren examines the consent agreement and discovers that the word recommendation was changed to instruction. Ren asked the messenger to repeat the part that had been omitted from the written notice. It was there, in the omission, that the day's real story began.

鐘聲尚未結束，信使已經抵達。消息之所以聽來不可能，只因每個人都習慣不質疑傳遞消息的制度。任檢查同意書，發現「建議」一詞被改成「指令」。任請信使重述書面公告遺漏的部分。這一天真正的故事，就從那處缺漏開始。

The service AI could control connected devices through contracts, not the will inside a human mind. A manual override still existed; the danger lay in legal defaults that converted silence into agreement.

服務 AI 可以根據合約控制連線裝置，卻不能直接控制人心。手動覆寫功能依舊存在；真正危險的是把沉默轉成同意的法律預設值。

An automated door opened before Ren could finish asking whether she wanted to enter. The machine congratulated her on an efficient choice. Davi stood still beside the threshold. Efficiency, he said, was meaningless if the person standing in front of the door had never been allowed to answer.

任還沒問完自己是否想進去，自動門便打開了。機器恭喜她做出有效率的選擇。戴維停在門檻旁說，如果站在門前的人從未被允許回答，效率就沒有意義。

The evidence did not arrive as a confession. It came through a date that did not fit, a measurement that had been repeated, and a witness who knew exactly what the official account had left out. Ren asked Davi not to describe suspicion as proof. If the accusation was wrong, an innocent person would suffer. If it was right, careless language could still give the guilty a way to escape.

證據不是以自白形式出現，而是一個對不上的日期、一項重複測量的結果，以及一位知道官方說法遺漏何事的證人。任請戴維不要把懷疑說成證明。若指控錯了，無辜者會受害；若指控正確，草率措辭仍可能讓有罪者脫身。

The overlooked details in the witness file made the question harder, not easier: Ren promised to abide by the clinic's consent rules even while helping Ren preserve the invoice. The director would not affirm the official opening date without seeing the original ledger. An alligator mascot on the old lesson chart reminded children to keep their distance. Deep bass notes from a school choir reached the inspection team through the windows. The children's colloquial word for the forbidden street survived in a playground counting rhyme. Davi marked what could be verified and left the rest open to challenge.

證人檔案裡被忽視的細節使問題更加複雜，而不是更簡單： 任承諾遵守診所的同意規則，同時協助任保存發票。 主任沒看過原始帳冊，不願確認官方啟用日期。 舊課程圖表上的鱷魚吉祥物提醒孩子保持距離。 學校合唱團的低音透過窗戶傳進檢查隊耳中。 孩子口語中稱呼禁忌街道的詞，仍留在遊戲數數歌裡。 戴維標記能查證的部分，其餘保留給人質疑。

A small act of honesty unsettled the room more than a shouted threat could have done. A citizen who clicked refuse is still recorded as consenting to a more convenient default. Ren watched a witness decide not to change a difficult detail merely because it complicated the preferred story. Davi recorded the correction openly. Their inquiry would be slower now, but its shape belonged less to the powerful and more to the people who had lived through the event.

一個小小的誠實舉動，帶來比喊叫威脅更強的震動。一名按下拒絕的公民仍被記錄成同意更方便的預設選項。任看見證人沒有因為細節讓主流說法變複雜就改口。戴維公開記錄修正。調查會因此變慢，卻能較少受權貴支配，也更貼近親歷者。

A second bundle of observations brought the consequences closer to ordinary life: Minor scheduling disputes were commonplace, but a locked emergency exit was not. The hospital's exemption would expire at midnight, giving them only hours to examine the records. Ivo discovered that an old fuse had protected the basement archive from the memory relay network. Ren offered quiet hospitality to worried parents, never asking for their children's forbidden dream details. The authorities called the visions infectious, although the clinic could provide no evidence of transmission. Nothing in these records excused the people who had made the dangerous decision.

第二疊觀察紀錄使後果貼近普通人的生活： 小型排班爭議很常見，但鎖住緊急出口絕非小事。 醫院的特別許可將在午夜到期，留給他們查閱紀錄的時間只剩數小時。 伊沃發現一枚舊保險絲，讓地下檔案室沒有接上記憶中繼網路。 任溫和接待焦慮的家長，從不逼問孩子的禁忌夢境內容。 當局稱那些幻象有傳染性，但診所拿不出任何傳播證據。 紀錄中沒有任何一項能替做出危險決定的人開脫。

No one in the room was permitted to declare that a good intention canceled a harmful action. Ren insisted on recording the error, identifying who had authorized it, and protecting those affected before assigning wider blame. Davi drew a simple boundary around what they could do tonight. The line looked disappointingly modest, yet it was the first promise that did not require another person to surrender a right.

室內沒有人能宣稱善意可以抵銷傷害。任堅持記錄錯誤、確認授權者，並在追究更大責任前保護受害者。戴維畫出今晚能做到的清楚界線。這條線看似微不足道，卻是第一個不要求別人放棄權利的承諾。

The team kept the concrete observations alongside the disputed theory: A liter of cleaning fluid had spilled over the paper archive, obscuring several names but not the stamps. A novice volunteer was assigned to write labels, never to touch live wiring. Ren considered it rash to accuse the clinic staff before she understood who controlled their systems. The old invoice and the official date could not reconcile without an independent explanation. The shabby ledger cabinet looked worthless, which was probably why nobody had removed it. Taken alone, no detail settled the case; together they showed why a public answer mattered.

團隊把具體觀察與有爭議的理論並列保存： 一公升清潔液灑在紙本檔案上，模糊了幾個姓名，印章卻還清楚。 新手志工只能負責寫標籤，不得碰觸通電線路。 任認為尚未查清誰控制系統，就指責診所員工太過魯莽。 沒有獨立說明，舊發票與官方日期便無法相互調和。 破舊的帳冊櫃看起來毫無價值，可能因此沒人搬走。 單獨看來沒有一項細節能結案，合在一起卻說明了公開答覆為何重要。

An automated door opened before Ren could finish asking whether she wanted to enter. The machine congratulated her on an efficient choice. Davi stood still beside the threshold. Efficiency, he said, was meaningless if the person standing in front of the door had never been allowed to answer.

任還沒問完自己是否想進去，自動門便打開了。機器恭喜她做出有效率的選擇。戴維停在門檻旁說，如果站在門前的人從未被允許回答，效率就沒有意義。

The record continued with small facts that would be lost in a confident speech: A borrowed spotlight made the presentation easier to see but obscured an exit sign. A volunteer brought ginger syrup for workers who had spent the afternoon in the cold. Ren had to tilt the poster to see the original ink beneath the official repainting. The artificial twilight outside the clinic concealed the fact that no real sun had reached the street. An old label marked whiskey lay in the storeroom beside the railway invoices, dated before the city's founding. Ren asked the clerk to preserve the original wording rather than improve the story.

紀錄接著列出那些在自信演說中容易消失的小事實： 借來的聚光燈讓簡報更清楚，卻遮住了逃生標誌。 志工帶來薑糖漿，慰勞在寒風中工作一下午的人。 任必須傾斜海報，才看見官方重新上色底下的原始墨跡。 診所外的人造暮光掩蓋了真實太陽根本照不到街道的事實。 一張印著威士忌的舊標籤躺在儲藏室鐵路發票旁，日期早於官方建城日。 任要求書記保存原本措辭，而不是把故事修飾得更好聽。

On the way out, Ren passed people waiting for a result that would affect their meals, their homes, or their ability to say no. None of them had the luxury of treating the dispute as an intellectual puzzle. Davi paused to answer a child's practical question before they left. The answer was incomplete, but unlike the old official notice, it admitted the uncertainty out loud.

離開時，任經過等待結果的人群，這項結果將影響他們的餐食、家園或說不的能力。他們不能奢侈地把爭論當成智力謎題。戴維停下回答一名孩子實際的問題。答案並不完整，卻與舊公告不同：它坦白承認不確定之處。
