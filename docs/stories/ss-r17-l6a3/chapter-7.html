It began not with a battle but with an ordinary interruption in the border valley. A mountain keeper shows Ina records proving that dragon eggs were moved before the first human raid. At first, no one considered the interruption important enough to preserve. Ina thought otherwise. A forgotten detail could become a map, and a map could be used to control where frightened people were permitted to go. Through its windows she could see a garden of one hundred acre plots, each carefully divided among noble families. A wealthy bachelor on the board objected to being excluded from the safety committee. Employees organized a charity boxing exhibition to support families hurt by the outage. The disciplinary rules required an independent hearing before any worker could be fired.

事情不是從戰鬥開始，而是從邊境山谷裡一次平凡的中斷開始。山中守護者出示帳冊，證明龍蛋在第一次人類襲擊前就已轉移。起初沒人認為這件事值得留下紀錄，但伊娜想法不同。遭忽略的細節可能變成地圖，而地圖也可能被用來控制驚恐的人可以去哪裡。 窗外花園分成大片土地，每塊以一英畝計算，整齊分給貴族家族。 董事會上一位富有的單身漢反對自己被排除在安全委員會外。 員工舉辦慈善拳擊表演，支援停電受影響的家庭。 紀律規則要求解雇任何工人前先有獨立聽證。

Soryn came back carrying a practical objection rather than an inspiring speech. The transfer orders carry the emblem of a dragon noble allied with a human mining family. They had both seen people harmed when an assumption hardened into policy. A signed report could be wrong, and an emotional memory could be right, but neither became proof merely by being repeated. They agreed to seek a measurement from an independent observer. In the border witnesses’ records, even ordinary details had been preserved: An emigrant engineer explained why the old contract translation was unclear to new workers. The emperor tried to equate dissent with treason. His expression did not falter, but the room seemed to grow colder.

索林帶回的是實際的異議，而不是振奮人心的演說。轉移命令帶著一名龍族貴族的印記，對方與人類礦商家族結盟。他們都見過假設硬化成政策後如何傷人。有簽名的報告可能是錯的，強烈的記憶也可能是真的；兩者都不會因為反覆提起就變成證據。他們決定找獨立觀察員再測量一次。邊境證人的紀錄連尋常細節也留下了： 一名移居海外的工程師解釋舊合約翻譯為何讓新工人難懂。 皇帝試圖把異議等同叛國。 他的表情沒有動搖，房內卻彷彿更冷了。

“We can tell the truth without pretending we know everything,” Ina said. Soryn replied that a partial truth was difficult to defend against people who controlled the doors. She answered that a perfect story with no verifiable source was more dangerous. They divided the work: one would protect the witnesses, while the other checked the physical records and the sequence of events. The clinic requested backup power for its fertility treatment laboratory. Ina answered that sacrifice could not mean forsake people whose names were absent from reports. A gay employee asked whether the revised benefits would cover his husband equally.

「我們能說實話，不需要假裝知道一切。」伊娜說。索林回答，當對方掌控所有門口時，局部真相比較難捍衛。她則認為沒有可驗證來源的完美故事更危險。他們分配工作：一人保護證人，另一人核查實體紀錄和事件時序。 診所要求為生育治療實驗室提供備用電力。 伊娜回答，犧牲不能意味著拋棄那些連名字都不在報告裡的人。 一名男同性戀員工詢問新福利是否同等保障他的丈夫。

The argument moved from a private corridor into a formal room, where every statement would be copied. A senior official from the royal delegation demanded certainty before granting access. Ina offered a less comfortable bargain: a limited preservation order now, followed by independent examination and a chance for every accused person to respond. That was enough to prevent an irreversible decision. At the intersection of two estate paths, the gloom of approaching rain made the palace garden seem smaller. The route had been damaged by a landslide years earlier and never fully repaired. The children needed warm food to nourish their bodies before another winter arrived.

爭論從私下走廊進入正式會議室，每句話都會留下副本。王室使團的一名高級官員要求先確定真相才准查資料。伊娜提出比較不舒服的交換：先給有期限的保全命令，再獨立檢查，也讓所有被指控者有回應機會。這足以擋下一個不可逆的決定。邊境證人的紀錄連尋常細節也留下了： 法庭保護工人安全否決權後，工作場所的陰鬱稍稍散去。 在莊園兩條小徑交叉處，將至的雨天陰鬱讓宮廷花園顯得更小。 該路線多年前遭山崩破壞，從未完全修復。 孩子們需要溫暖食物滋養身體，才能迎接下一個冬天。

Soryn accepts an independent inquiry despite furious objections from his own council. Even those who agreed with the choice could see its cost. Time was running out, supplies were not unlimited, and a mistake might travel far beyond this room. Soryn asked whether they should conceal the uncertainty until the crisis passed. Ina shook her head. People could not make free choices if the information reaching them had already been edited. 儘管議庭怒不可遏，索林仍同意獨立調查。即使同意這個選擇的人，也看得出代價。時間不多、資源有限，一次錯誤可能傳得很遠。索林問是否該先隱瞞不確定性，等危機過後再說。伊娜搖頭。若資訊早被剪裁，人們便無法作出自由的選擇。 他主張正統軍事教義要求禁止未經授權的邊境旅行。 他主張正統軍事教義要求禁止未經授權的邊境旅行。 暫時法院命令在爭議租約受審期間保住公共通行權。

Later, under dim lights, Ina reread the original question. The dispute was larger now, but its center remained unchanged: who had the right to make an irreversible choice for someone else? Soryn stayed until the last copy was sealed. Neither spoke about victory. They knew the next door would open only because today’s evidence had been preserved and today’s mistakes had been admitted. The safety committee reached a unanimous decision to postpone the untested repair. He knew something about the cost of the family's vanity. A woodpecker struck a roadside pole while the technicians inspected the power lines.

晚些時候，在微弱燈光下，伊娜重讀最初的問題。爭端已經擴大，但核心沒變：誰有權替別人作出不可逆的選擇？索林留到最後一份副本封存。他們沒有談勝利，只知道下一扇門之所以能打開，是因為今天保住了證據，也承認了錯誤。 安全委員會一致決定延後尚未測試的維修。 他深知家族虛榮的代價。 技師檢查電線時，一隻啄木鳥啄著路邊電線桿。
