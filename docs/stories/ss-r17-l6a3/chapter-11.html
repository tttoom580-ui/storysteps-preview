國王的私下提議

The King's Private Offer

成為惡龍的首席談判官 · 第 11 / 25 章 · 5000–6000 · 英中對照 · 校訂中

Ina arrived with the unpleasant certainty that the simplest explanation was probably the one that had been prepared for public consumption. The king offers Ina a title if she brings home a treaty that grants permanent mining rights. The place was full of people who needed a quick answer, yet the evidence still belonged to those whose lives it could change. Instead of making a dramatic accusation, Ina marked the hour, the witnesses, and the smallest observable detail in a paper notebook. The repair room felt hotter than thirty Celsius when the cooling fan stopped. The machine's upper wheel turned slowly because friction had damaged its bearings. A stray dog began to growl when inspectors approached the abandoned loading dock. The lawyer said a forged instruction could induce workers to override a safe setting.

伊娜抵達時，心裡很清楚：最簡單的解釋往往是事先準備好要給大眾聽的版本。國王允諾封爵，只要伊娜帶回永久礦權條約。現場的人都急需答案，但證據仍屬於那些生活可能被它改變的人。她沒有戲劇化地指控誰，而是先在紙本筆記上記下時刻、證人，以及最小卻可觀察的細節。 散熱風扇停轉時，維修室溫度超過攝氏三十度。 機器上方的輪子轉得很慢，因為摩擦已損傷軸承。 檢查員靠近廢置裝貨區時，一隻流浪狗開始低吼。 律師說偽造指令可能誘使工人覆寫安全設定。

There was a moment of quiet when Soryn set the new material on the table. The private offer contradicts his public instructions to negotiate peace on equal terms. What mattered was not how surprising the discovery sounded, but whether anyone could repeat the test. Ina copied the relevant names carefully. If one person later changed the account, the original would still be available for comparison. In the border witnesses’ records, even ordinary details had been preserved: The fishermen who inhabit these shores would need a permanent place on the safety board. It was ironic that the trust's security account had been active long after its owner died. A retired mechanic lived on a grassy mound near the station.

索林把新資料放上桌時，四周安靜了一瞬。私下提議與國王公開宣稱的平等和平談判相矛盾。重要的不是發現有多驚人，而是別人能不能重做同一項測試。伊娜仔細抄下相關姓名；如果日後有人更改說法，原件仍可供比對。邊境證人的紀錄連尋常細節也留下了： 居住在這片海岸的漁民需要在安全委員會中擁有永久席次。 諷刺的是，信託保全帳戶在原持有人死後很久仍活躍。 一名退休技師住在測站附近的草丘上。

“Will you trust me?” Soryn asked. “With the evidence, yes,” Ina answered, “but not with a blank signature.” It was the sort of answer that could sound cold until someone noticed how carefully she had protected the other person’s right to disagree. Together they made a plan with a backup route, a deadline, and a record of who had accepted each responsibility. A peacock wandered through the courtyard, absurdly elegant among broken walls. A pianist played for patients while the clinic's backup system was tested. Investigators used a thermal probe to identify the relay's overheating point.

「妳信任我嗎？」索林問。「證據上可以，空白授權書不行。」伊娜回答。這聽起來很冷淡，直到人們注意到她也小心保護對方拒絕的權利。他們一起訂下有備用路線、期限，以及明確責任分工的計畫。 一隻孔雀在院中遊走，在破牆之間顯得荒謬地優雅。 診所測試備電系統時，一位鋼琴家為病人演奏。 調查員使用熱探針找出繼電器過熱點。

A representative of the royal delegation arrived with an offer to resolve the matter quietly. Quietly, in this case, meant without giving affected people access to the original. Ina declined. She knew an office could be useful and still be dangerously wrong. Her objection was narrow and specific: the proposed decision relied on a document whose origin had not yet been independently verified. In the border witnesses’ records, even ordinary details had been preserved: The respective duties of the city and company had been confused in the old emergency plan. The urgency of the investigation did not justify shortsighted risks. She refused to simplify the repair history into a tale with only one villain. A vessel crossing the strait was delayed by the same storm approaching the city.

王室使團的代表前來，提議私下解決。所謂私下，就是不讓受影響的人看到原件。伊娜拒絕了。她知道一個機關可以有用，也可能犯下危險的錯。她的反對具體而有限：提議中的決定依賴一份來源還沒接受獨立驗證的文件。邊境證人的紀錄連尋常細節也留下了： 舊緊急計畫混淆了市府與公司的各自責任。 調查再緊急，也不能合理化目光短淺的風險。 她拒絕把修繕歷史簡化成只有一個反派的故事。 一艘穿越海峽的船因同場暴風雨而延誤。

She records the message, refuses the reward, and tells the dragon council exactly what was proposed. A few witnesses thanked Ina; others accused her of doing too little. She accepted both reactions without offering a promise she could not keep. Soryn wrote down the complaints, including the ones directed at their own conduct. They were not building a monument to themselves. They were building a process that would still function after they left. The superficial inspection had missed heat damage deep inside the relay cabinet. He remembered being ordered to suppress an alarm after the engine froze a fishing boat. The symmetry of the instruments mattered because each team had to measure the same change independently.

她留下訊息紀錄，拒絕獎賞，向龍族議庭完整說明提議。幾名證人向伊娜道謝，另一些人指責她做得不夠。她接受兩種反應，不許下無法兌現的承諾。索林寫下抱怨，包括針對他們自己行為的部分。他們不是為自己立紀念碑，而是建立自己離開後仍能運作的程序。 表面的檢查漏掉了繼電器櫃內部深處的熱損傷。 他記得曾被命令壓下引擎凍住漁船後發出的警報。 儀器的對稱配置很重要，因為每組人都必須獨立測量同一項變化。

By nightfall the newest report was signed by observers who were free to disagree. Ina stood beside Soryn and looked at the distance between them and a seven-day agreement that both peoples can freely accept. Progress felt less like triumph than like leaving a reliable mark on a difficult road. Someone would challenge that mark tomorrow. This time, there would be a public record with which to answer. An expired access token had been used to send the dormant account's last message. The public committee tried to unify the three separate emergency notification systems. The urgency of the investigation did not justify shortsighted risks.

夜幕降臨前，新的報告由可以提出異議的觀察者簽署。伊娜站在索林身旁，看著他們與雙方自願接受的七日協議之間的距離。進展不像勝利，比較像在艱難路上留下可靠的記號。明天有人會質疑，而這一次將有公開紀錄可以回答。 最後一封休眠帳戶訊息使用了一枚過期存取憑證。 公共委員會試圖整合三套分離的緊急通知系統。 調查再緊急，也不能合理化目光短淺的風險。
