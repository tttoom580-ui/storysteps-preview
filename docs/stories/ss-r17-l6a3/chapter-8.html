第二天的火

The Second Day's Fire

Ina arrived with the unpleasant certainty that the simplest explanation was probably the one that had been prepared for public consumption. A military depot burns just as the negotiators announce the egg inquiry. The place was full of people who needed a quick answer, yet the evidence still belonged to those whose lives it could change. Instead of making a dramatic accusation, Ina marked the hour, the witnesses, and the smallest observable detail in a paper notebook. An accumulation of sediment had changed the waterways, and the official map was no longer accurate. A vendor offered each inspector a sealed beverage, but they declined gifts during the audit. The blond driver in the delivery photograph confirmed that he had used the wrong route. Only the independent laboratory could certify the chemical condition of the stored batteries.

伊娜抵達時，心裡很清楚：最簡單的解釋往往是事先準備好要給大眾聽的版本。談判者宣布龍蛋調查時，一座軍用倉庫突然起火。現場的人都急需答案，但證據仍屬於那些生活可能被它改變的人。她沒有戲劇化地指控誰，而是先在紙本筆記上記下時刻、證人，以及最小卻可觀察的細節。 累積的泥沙改變了水道，官方地圖已不準確。 供應商給每位檢查員密封飲料，但眾人在稽核期間拒絕收禮。 送貨照片裡的金髮司機證實自己走錯了路。 只有獨立實驗室能證明儲存電池的化學狀況。

There was a moment of quiet when Soryn set the new material on the table. Dragon-scale fragments at the scene have been planted above human boot prints made afterward. What mattered was not how surprising the discovery sounded, but whether anyone could repeat the test. Ina copied the relevant names carefully. If one person later changed the account, the original would still be available for comparison. In the border witnesses’ records, even ordinary details had been preserved: The warehouse manager demanded official clearance before the team opened the locked crates. A copy of those figures survived in the harbor office, where a careful official had refused to deduct unexplained losses. At noon, a provincial official presented a report declaring that all missing equipment had been destined for hospital repairs.

索林把新資料放上桌時，四周安靜了一瞬。現場的龍鱗碎片被人放在人類靴印上方，顯示證物是後來加上的。重要的不是發現有多驚人，而是別人能不能重做同一項測試。伊娜仔細抄下相關姓名；如果日後有人更改說法，原件仍可供比對。邊境證人的紀錄連尋常細節也留下了： 倉庫經理要求團隊開啟鎖箱前先取得正式許可。 港務處保留了一份數字副本，一名謹慎的官員拒絕扣除來源不明的損失。 中午，一名省府官員提交報告，宣稱所有失蹤設備都注定要送去修理醫院。

“Will you trust me?” Soryn asked. “With the evidence, yes,” Ina answered, “but not with a blank signature.” It was the sort of answer that could sound cold until someone noticed how carefully she had protected the other person’s right to disagree. Together they made a plan with a backup route, a deadline, and a record of who had accepted each responsibility. The inspection team could exert pressure through lawful requests, not threats of punishment. The officer who delivered them had once been praised for heroic service during the first border winter; now he feared being branded a traitor for providing facts. A retired army lieutenant volunteered to organize transport without taking control of the inquiry.

「妳信任我嗎？」索林問。「證據上可以，空白授權書不行。」伊娜回答。這聽起來很冷淡，直到人們注意到她也小心保護對方拒絕的權利。他們一起訂下有備用路線、期限，以及明確責任分工的計畫。 檢查團隊可以依法提出要求施壓，而非恐嚇處罰。 送來資料的軍官曾因第一個邊境寒冬的英勇服務受到嘉獎，如今卻害怕提供事實會被說成叛徒。 一名退役陸軍中尉志願安排運輸，沒有接管調查。

A representative of the royal delegation arrived with an offer to resolve the matter quietly. Quietly, in this case, meant without giving affected people access to the original. Ina declined. She knew an office could be useful and still be dangerously wrong. Her objection was narrow and specific: the proposed decision relied on a document whose origin had not yet been independently verified. In the border witnesses’ records, even ordinary details had been preserved: Literate workers could read the manual, but the team also prepared diagrams for everyone else. The provincial court stood beside a swamp where lotus flowers opened at dawn. That error had made their petitions appear marginal, even when hundreds signed them. Two security routes overlap near the gate, creating a blind spot in the records.

王室使團的代表前來，提議私下解決。所謂私下，就是不讓受影響的人看到原件。伊娜拒絕了。她知道一個機關可以有用，也可能犯下危險的錯。她的反對具體而有限：提議中的決定依賴一份來源還沒接受獨立驗證的文件。邊境證人的紀錄連尋常細節也留下了： 識字的工人能閱讀手冊，團隊也替其他人準備圖解。 省級法庭位於沼澤旁，黎明時蓮花盛開。 這個錯誤使他們的請願看來無足輕重，即使有數百人簽名也一樣。 兩條保全路線在大門附近重疊，造成紀錄盲點。

Ina demands firefighters first and refuses to let either army turn the flames into a pretext for assault. A few witnesses thanked Ina; others accused her of doing too little. She accepted both reactions without offering a promise she could not keep. Soryn wrote down the complaints, including the ones directed at their own conduct. They were not building a monument to themselves. They were building a process that would still function after they left. The provincial court stood beside a swamp where lotus flowers opened at dawn. The new technician's qualification was checked by an independent professional board. Outside, the retired teacher offered the group simple refreshments.

伊娜先要求救火，拒絕任一軍隊把火災當成攻擊藉口。幾名證人向伊娜道謝，另一些人指責她做得不夠。她接受兩種反應，不許下無法兌現的承諾。索林寫下抱怨，包括針對他們自己行為的部分。他們不是為自己立紀念碑，而是建立自己離開後仍能運作的程序。 省級法庭位於沼澤旁，黎明時蓮花盛開。 新技師的資格由獨立專業委員會核對。 法庭外，退休教師為一行人準備簡單茶點。

By nightfall the newest report was signed by observers who were free to disagree. Ina stood beside Soryn and looked at the distance between them and a seven-day agreement that both peoples can freely accept. Progress felt less like triumph than like leaving a reliable mark on a difficult road. Someone would challenge that mark tomorrow. This time, there would be a public record with which to answer. The team tried to retrieve a broken seal without disturbing the surrounding evidence. The provincial court stood beside a swamp where lotus flowers opened at dawn. “My vocation is to keep people safe,” he said.

夜幕降臨前，新的報告由可以提出異議的觀察者簽署。伊娜站在索林身旁，看著他們與雙方自願接受的七日協議之間的距離。進展不像勝利，比較像在艱難路上留下可靠的記號。明天有人會質疑，而這一次將有公開紀錄可以回答。 團隊試圖取回破損封條，同時不干擾周圍證物。 省級法庭位於沼澤旁，黎明時蓮花盛開。 「我的志業是讓人安全，」他說，「不是保住大臣的名聲。
