距離烈火還有七天

Seven Days Until Fire

成為惡龍的首席談判官 · 第 1 / 25 章 · 5000–6000 · 英中對照 · 校訂中

用互動版查詞、朗讀與收藏

It began not with a battle but with an ordinary interruption in the border valley. Diplomat Ina Voss reaches the border town under orders to obtain a dragon surrender within seven days. At first, no one considered the interruption important enough to preserve. Ina thought otherwise. A forgotten detail could become a map, and a map could be used to control where frightened people were permitted to go. The legal clerk refused to abbreviate the witness's full name on a disputed record. At the river market, war refugees lined up for bread beneath an old anchor displayed outside the sailors' guild. A warrant authorized capture, not execution; the distinction was applicable even to an enemy of the throne. The public archive stored a copy of every switching order issued that week.

事情不是從戰鬥開始，而是從邊境山谷裡一次平凡的中斷開始。外交官伊娜・沃斯抵達邊境城鎮，接到命令：七天內取得龍族投降。起初沒人認為這件事值得留下紀錄，但伊娜想法不同。遭忽略的細節可能變成地圖，而地圖也可能被用來控制驚恐的人可以去哪裡。 法務書記拒絕在有爭議的紀錄上縮寫證人全名。 河岸市場裡，戰爭難民在水手公會門外的舊船錨下排隊領麵包。 逮捕令授權的是拘捕，不是處決；即使對王座的敵人，這項區別仍然適用。 公共檔案室保存了那週每份切換命令的副本。

Soryn came back carrying a practical objection rather than an inspiring speech. The dragon envoy refuses surrender but offers to discuss a ceasefire if civilian wells remain open. They had both seen people harmed when an assumption hardened into policy. A signed report could be wrong, and an emotional memory could be right, but neither became proof merely by being repeated. They agreed to seek a measurement from an independent observer. In the border witnesses’ records, even ordinary details had been preserved: An elderly resident pressed the emergency card against her bosom as she waited. A worker reported that her bra had been taken with her uniform during the inspection. A contractor had offered a bribe for access to the missing hour of footage.

索林帶回的是實際的異議，而不是振奮人心的演說。龍族使者拒絕投降，卻表示若平民水井維持開放，願意討論停火。他們都見過假設硬化成政策後如何傷人。有簽名的報告可能是錯的，強烈的記憶也可能是真的；兩者都不會因為反覆提起就變成證據。他們決定找獨立觀察員再測量一次。邊境證人的紀錄連尋常細節也留下了： 一位年長居民等待時，把緊急卡片緊貼胸前。 一名女工反映，檢查時她的胸罩與制服一同被拿走。 一名承包商曾為取得失蹤那一小時的影像提出賄賂。

“We can tell the truth without pretending we know everything,” Ina said. Soryn replied that a partial truth was difficult to defend against people who controlled the doors. She answered that a perfect story with no verifiable source was more dangerous. They divided the work: one would protect the witnesses, while the other checked the physical records and the sequence of events. Smoke rose from a kitchen where volunteers were broiling fish above coals for the hungry. The minister offered a comparative report instead of the original. His eyelid twitched whenever soldiers passed.

「我們能說實話，不需要假裝知道一切。」伊娜說。索林回答，當對方掌控所有門口時，局部真相比較難捍衛。她則認為沒有可驗證來源的完美故事更危險。他們分配工作：一人保護證人，另一人核查實體紀錄和事件時序。 廚房冒出炊煙，志工在炭火上炙烤魚，供飢餓的人食用。 議事廳下方有保存軍令的檔案庫，然而眼前封存的文件竟標著明天的日期。 每當士兵經過，他的眼皮便會抽動。

The argument moved from a private corridor into a formal room, where every statement would be copied. A senior official from the royal delegation demanded certainty before granting access. Ina offered a less comfortable bargain: a limited preservation order now, followed by independent examination and a chance for every accused person to respond. That was enough to prevent an irreversible decision. In the border witnesses’ records, even ordinary details had been preserved: Their reserve power was finite, so the clinic had to prioritize essential equipment. The safety fund would let small neighborhood repair workshops flourish after the blackout. A hired gangster threatened the courier who had delivered the original maintenance sheets. The freight van vanished at the junction between the riverside road and the tunnel.

爭論從私下走廊進入正式會議室，每句話都會留下副本。王室使團的一名高級官員要求先確定真相才准查資料。伊娜提出比較不舒服的交換：先給有期限的保全命令，再獨立檢查，也讓所有被指控者有回應機會。這足以擋下一個不可逆的決定。邊境證人的紀錄連尋常細節也留下了： 備用電力有限，因此診所必須優先供應必要設備。 安全基金將讓社區小修理工坊在停電後蓬勃發展。 一名受雇黑幫分子威脅遞送原始維修表的快遞員。 貨車在河岸道路與隧道交會的路口消失。

Ina promises no impossible magic and demands a written list of each side's actual grievances. Even those who agreed with the choice could see its cost. Time was running out, supplies were not unlimited, and a mistake might travel far beyond this room. Soryn asked whether they should conceal the uncertainty until the crisis passed. Ina shook her head. People could not make free choices if the information reaching them had already been edited. A porter with a limp identified the sealed box he had carried upstairs. He said he could narrate the entire pursuit if she promised the guards would not drag him away. An orderly guided exhausted families to separate waiting rooms in the emergency clinic.

伊娜不會魔法，也不許下無法實現的承諾，只要求雙方書面列出真正的怨由。即使同意這個選擇的人，也看得出代價。時間不多、資源有限，一次錯誤可能傳得很遠。索林問是否該先隱瞞不確定性，等危機過後再說。伊娜搖頭。若資訊早被剪裁，人們便無法作出自由的選擇。 一名跛行的搬運員辨認出自己搬上樓的封盒。 他表示，只要公主保證守衛不把他拖走，便願意敘述整場追捕經過。 勤務員引導疲憊家庭前往緊急診所不同的候診室。

Later, under dim lights, Ina reread the original question. The dispute was larger now, but its center remained unchanged: who had the right to make an irreversible choice for someone else? Soryn stayed until the last copy was sealed. Neither spoke about victory. They knew the next door would open only because today’s evidence had been preserved and today’s mistakes had been admitted. A bank teller confirmed that the suspicious transfer had occurred before the outage. The alleged contract was printed on virgin paper, unlike the stained original file. A warrant authorized capture, not execution; the distinction was applicable even to an enemy of the throne.

晚些時候，在微弱燈光下，伊娜重讀最初的問題。爭端已經擴大，但核心沒變：誰有權替別人作出不可逆的選擇？索林留到最後一份副本封存。他們沒有談勝利，只知道下一扇門之所以能打開，是因為今天保住了證據，也承認了錯誤。 銀行出納確認可疑轉帳發生於停電之前。 所謂合約印在全新紙上，與有污漬的原檔不同。 逮捕令授權的是拘捕，不是處決；即使對王座的敵人，這項區別仍然適用。
