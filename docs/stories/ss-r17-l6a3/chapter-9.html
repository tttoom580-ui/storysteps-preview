礦坑裡的證人

The Witness in the Mine

成為惡龍的首席談判官 · 第 9 / 25 章 · 5000–6000 · 英中對照 · 校訂中

用互動版查詞、朗讀與收藏

The morning had already acquired the look of an emergency before Ina reached the scene. Workers trapped behind a collapsed tunnel reveal that a mining machine can imitate the heat of dragon breath. An official wanted a clean story, with a beginning that could be announced and an ending that would require no questions. Ina had learned that reality rarely showed such courtesy. She made room for the people who had been ordered to wait outside. The public statement might alienate frightened investors, but hiding the altered seal would be worse. Ink had been applied over one route to blot out the former border crossing, but sunlight exposed its outline. Residents shared chili soup while waiting for the procurement hearing to begin. The envoy explained that continuity of village access was guaranteed in the oldest agreement.

在伊娜抵達現場前，早晨已經有了緊急事件的模樣。困在坍塌坑道後的工人透露，一台礦機可以模仿龍息的高熱。官員想要一套簡潔的故事，開頭能宣布，結尾又不用回答問題。她知道現實很少如此客氣，於是請那些被命令等在門外的人進來說話。 公開聲明可能疏遠害怕的投資人，但隱瞞改動封條更糟。 有人用墨塗抹一條路，想掩蓋昔日邊界渡口，但陽光照出它的輪廓。 居民等候採購聽證開始時，分享辣椒湯。 使者解釋，最早的協議保障村民持續使用這條路；後來的修正案逐漸刪除渡口相關文字，累積的省略使權利在沒有任何正式廢止令的情況下消失。

Soryn examined the account twice, once as a friend and once as a witness who might have to defend every word. Their employer threatened to seize family homes if anyone explained the machine's purpose. The difference between what they suspected and what they could establish mattered. They placed the new fact beside the previous chapter’s unanswered question and looked for a second source that had not been copied from the first. In the border witnesses’ records, even ordinary details had been preserved: The cumulative delays had increased costs far beyond the original repair estimate. They showed a decisive increase in heat extraction on the night the orphanage burned. No worker was dispensable merely because an executive feared an embarrassing question.

索林把紀錄看了兩次：第一次以朋友身分，第二次則像一位必須為每個字負責的證人。雇主威脅若有人揭露機器用途，就沒收他們的家屋。懷疑和能確立的事實必須分清楚。他們把新線索放在前一章未解的問題旁，尋找不曾從同一份資料抄出的第二來源。邊境證人的紀錄連尋常細節也留下了： 累積延誤讓成本遠超原始維修估算。 作者最後寫下一個簡短問題：「為什麼測試開始後，撤離卻被取消？ 不能因主管害怕尷尬提問，就把任何工人當成可有可無。

A crowd gathered around the disputed notice. “You have to choose a side,” someone shouted. Ina answered that she had chosen one already: the side of a claim that survived questions. Soryn did not smile, but moved the most vulnerable bystanders away from the growing argument. The pair could disagree privately without turning their disagreement into a public threat. A visitor wore jasmine perfume that matched the scent noted by the night guard. The river lodge provided temporary rooms for families displaced during repairs. A maple tree near the entrance helped the courier identify the delivery location.

人群圍住有爭議的公告。「妳必須選邊站！」有人喊。伊娜回答自己早已選擇：站在經得起提問的主張那一邊。索林沒有微笑，只把最脆弱的旁觀者帶離爭執。他們可以私下不同意對方，卻不能把分歧變成公開威脅。 某位訪客使用茉莉香水，與夜班警衛記下的氣味一致。 河邊旅舍為維修期間搬離的家庭提供臨時房間。 入口旁的楓樹協助快遞員辨認送貨地點。

The institution was not a single villain. It contained frightened clerks, dedicated workers, ambitious supervisors, and people who had learned to hide mistakes. the royal delegation wanted the incident classified as routine. Ina asked whose definition of routine was being used and whose losses were absent from the statistics. Nobody at the table could answer without consulting the sealed records. In the border witnesses’ records, even ordinary details had been preserved: The next witness was Ina, an envoy from the eastern federation whom the border kingdom officially called a rebel mistress of spies. In the next room, people debated how to mobilize villagers for a public meeting without provoking soldiers. Ina paused to muse over how often public trust had been treated as private property. The whistleblower's persistence finally persuaded investigators to examine the old account.

這個機構不是單一惡棍。裡面有害怕的書記、盡責的工人、野心勃勃的主管，也有學會掩蓋錯誤的人。王室使團想把事件歸類為例行事項。伊娜追問：是誰定義例行？又有誰的損失被統計漏掉？桌邊沒人能在不看封存紀錄的情況下回答。邊境證人的紀錄連尋常細節也留下了： 下一名證人是東方聯邦使者伊絲拉；邊境王國官方稱她是指揮間諜的叛軍女頭目。 隔壁房裡，人們討論如何動員村民出席公開集會，又不激怒士兵。 見遙停下來沉思，公共信任究竟有多少次被當成私人財產。 吹哨者的堅持終於讓調查員檢查舊帳戶。

Ina arranges safe housing and records each account separately to prevent coached testimony. The decision did not repair everything. It altered one procedure, protected one person, or saved one piece of evidence; on a day when almost everyone demanded a miracle, that was worth recording. Ina insisted that the result and its limits appear together in the official minutes. Anything less would turn a modest gain into tomorrow’s misleading legend. A socialist council member supported public oversight without claiming every private firm was guilty. Employees had a chance to socialize after the hearing without being questioned by reporters. The access log covered a span of seven hours, with one unexplained gap near midnight.

伊娜安排安全住所，分別記錄每個人的證詞，避免串供。這個決定沒有修好一切，只改變一道程序、保護一個人，或留下了一份證據；當幾乎所有人都要求奇蹟時，這仍值得記錄。伊娜堅持把成果和限制一起寫入正式紀錄。否則小小的進展又會變成明天誤導人的傳說。 一名社會主義議員支持公共監督，沒有宣稱所有私人企業都有罪。 聽證後，員工終於能不受記者盤問地社交。 門禁紀錄涵蓋七小時，其中午夜附近有一段無法解釋的空白。

The lights beyond the window did not offer comfort. They showed how many people were still waiting. Ina understood that the investigation might eventually expose her own earlier errors, and she resolved not to erase them. Soryn suggested a short rest before the next hearing. She agreed, because exhaustion could damage judgment as thoroughly as deliberate deception. The author had been ordered to trek into the mountains and test the border furnace before civilians were evacuated. Their disagreement was vigorous but not violent. A waterproof cover protected the pages, and the calculations were still legible.

窗外的燈光沒有帶來安慰，只照出還有多少人在等待。伊娜明白調查最終可能揭露自己過去的錯誤，決定不去抹除。索林建議下一場聽證前短暫休息。她同意，因為疲憊和蓄意欺騙一樣可能損害判斷。 作者奉命徒步進入山區，在平民撤離前測試邊境熔爐。 他們爭論得激烈，卻沒有暴力。 防水封套保存了紙頁，計算仍清晰可讀。
