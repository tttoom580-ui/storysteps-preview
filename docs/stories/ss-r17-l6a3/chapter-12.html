白骨之橋

The Bridge Made of Bones

成為惡龍的首席談判官 · 第 12 / 25 章 · 5000–6000 · 英中對照 · 校訂中

The morning had already acquired the look of an emergency before Ina reached the scene. A narrow bridge holds the remains of people killed in an earlier crossing, and both peoples claim the dead. An official wanted a clean story, with a beginning that could be announced and an ending that would require no questions. Ina had learned that reality rarely showed such courtesy. She made room for the people who had been ordered to wait outside. The royal summer estate stood where a clear brook met the sea. The canal rescue team removed fragile coral samples from a science display before flooding. The document's expiration date had been extended without a public notice, depriving inland communities of a chance to object. Workers used a platform to elevate the backup batteries above the projected flood line.

在伊娜抵達現場前，早晨已經有了緊急事件的模樣。狹窄橋梁留有過去過橋時罹難者的遺骨，雙方都宣稱死者屬於自己。官員想要一套簡潔的故事，開頭能宣布，結尾又不用回答問題。她知道現實很少如此客氣，於是請那些被命令等在門外的人進來說話。 王室夏宮位於清澈小溪入海之處，古老噴泉裝飾著珊瑚，私人港口上方豎著雄偉的拱門。 洪水前，運河救援隊移走科學展示裡脆弱的珊瑚樣本。 一名質疑延長案的議員突然被拒於國會門外。 工人用平台把備用電池抬高到預測淹水線以上。

Soryn examined the account twice, once as a friend and once as a witness who might have to defend every word. A healer identifies names from a shared plague register, proving that the old disaster affected humans and dragons together. The difference between what they suspected and what they could establish mattered. They placed the new fact beside the previous chapter’s unanswered question and looked for a second source that had not been copied from the first. In the border witnesses’ records, even ordinary details had been preserved: The lease had an expiration date, but no one could find its required public notice. The retired mechanic's failing eyesight made written testimony easier than reading small diagrams. He needed rest, hearty meals, and time away from cold reservoirs.

索林把紀錄看了兩次：第一次以朋友身分，第二次則像一位必須為每個字負責的證人。醫者透過共同疫病名冊辨識姓名，證明舊災害同時傷及人與龍。懷疑和能確立的事實必須分清楚。他們把新線索放在前一章未解的問題旁，尋找不曾從同一份資料抄出的第二來源。邊境證人的紀錄連尋常細節也留下了： 租約有到期日，卻沒有人找得到法定公開通知。 退休技師視力衰退，書面作證比閱讀小圖更容易。 他需要休息、豐盛的餐食，並暫時遠離低溫儲能庫。

A crowd gathered around the disputed notice. “You have to choose a side,” someone shouted. Ina answered that she had chosen one already: the side of a claim that survived questions. Soryn did not smile, but moved the most vulnerable bystanders away from the growing argument. The pair could disagree privately without turning their disagreement into a public threat. “Nor do you owe me a simpler story.” Across the terrace, a lantern hovered in the warm air. If such restrictions continued, they could strangle trade between the inland farms and the sea. Her insistence frustrated the royal security chief.

人群圍住有爭議的公告。「妳必須選邊站！」有人喊。伊娜回答自己早已選擇：站在經得起提問的主張那一邊。索林沒有微笑，只把最脆弱的旁觀者帶離爭執。他們可以私下不同意對方，卻不能把分歧變成公開威脅。 」露台另一端，一盞燈籠漂浮在暖風中。 這些限制若持續，可能扼殺內陸農田與海岸之間的貿易。 王室安全長對她的堅持很不滿。

The institution was not a single villain. It contained frightened clerks, dedicated workers, ambitious supervisors, and people who had learned to hide mistakes. the royal delegation wanted the incident classified as routine. Ina asked whose definition of routine was being used and whose losses were absent from the statistics. Nobody at the table could answer without consulting the sealed records. In the border witnesses’ records, even ordinary details had been preserved: The temporary lease allowed access without transferring ownership of the pump house. A legislator who had questioned the extension was suddenly denied entry to parliament. Coral decorated its old fountains, and a majestic arch rose above the private harbor. The new gate gained momentum as the winch began pulling it slowly upward.

這個機構不是單一惡棍。裡面有害怕的書記、盡責的工人、野心勃勃的主管，也有學會掩蓋錯誤的人。王室使團想把事件歸類為例行事項。伊娜追問：是誰定義例行？又有誰的損失被統計漏掉？桌邊沒人能在不看封存紀錄的情況下回答。邊境證人的紀錄連尋常細節也留下了： 臨時租約允許通行，但不轉讓抽水站所有權。 一名質疑延長案的議員突然被拒於國會門外。 伊娜來此檢查一份租約：皇帝藉此准許軍隊使用附近的水站。 絞盤慢慢往上拉時，新閘門逐漸有了動能。

Ina proposes a jointly tended memorial instead of another monument to victory. The decision did not repair everything. It altered one procedure, protected one person, or saved one piece of evidence; on a day when almost everyone demanded a miracle, that was worth recording. Ina insisted that the result and its limits appear together in the official minutes. Anything less would turn a modest gain into tomorrow’s misleading legend. The chairman behaved like a monarch, but corporate authority still had legal limits. An animal shelter asked for reliable heating to protect newborn offspring during power cuts. A petrol generator was retained as a backup only after its emissions were tested.

伊娜提議共同照護紀念碑，而不是再造一座勝利紀念物。這個決定沒有修好一切，只改變一道程序、保護一個人，或留下了一份證據；當幾乎所有人都要求奇蹟時，這仍值得記錄。伊娜堅持把成果和限制一起寫入正式紀錄。否則小小的進展又會變成明天誤導人的傳說。 董事長的行徑像君主，企業權力仍有法律界線。 動物收容所要求可靠暖氣，以保護停電期間剛出生的幼獸。 汽油發電機通過排放測試後才被保留作備用。

The lights beyond the window did not offer comfort. They showed how many people were still waiting. Ina understood that the investigation might eventually expose her own earlier errors, and she resolved not to erase them. Soryn suggested a short rest before the next hearing. She agreed, because exhaustion could damage judgment as thoroughly as deliberate deception. The operator had to rotate the valve slowly to prevent a sudden pressure change. If such restrictions continued, they could strangle trade between the inland farms and the sea. The physician explained that exhausted muscles could fail during a critical spell, just as poor surgical instruments could fail in an emergency.

窗外的燈光沒有帶來安慰，只照出還有多少人在等待。伊娜明白調查最終可能揭露自己過去的錯誤，決定不去抹除。索林建議下一場聽證前短暫休息。她同意，因為疲憊和蓄意欺騙一樣可能損害判斷。 操作員必須緩慢旋轉閥門，以免壓力突然變化。 這些限制若持續，可能扼殺內陸農田與海岸之間的貿易。 醫師解釋，疲憊肌肉可能在關鍵施法時失效，就像不良外科器械在急救時會出問題。
