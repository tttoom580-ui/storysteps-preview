The Ember Court

成為惡龍的首席談判官 · 第 6 / 25 章 · 5000–6000 · 英中對照 · 校訂中

The morning had already acquired the look of an emergency before Ina reached the scene. Soryn invites Ina to a dragon council beneath a mountain where every speaker must identify whom they represent. An official wanted a clean story, with a beginning that could be announced and an ending that would require no questions. Ina had learned that reality rarely showed such courtesy. She made room for the people who had been ordered to wait outside. “We allege deliberate treason,” he declared, though no test had occurred yet. The contractor had promised to beautify the riverside warehouse before its safety inspection. Its stone entrance was decorated with carved birds and a caption praising impartial justice. The contract dispute reached its climax when the two shipping weights appeared together.

在伊娜抵達現場前，早晨已經有了緊急事件的模樣。索林邀伊娜進入山底龍族議庭，每名發言者都必須說明代表對象。官員想要一套簡潔的故事，開頭能宣布，結尾又不用回答問題。她知道現實很少如此客氣，於是請那些被命令等在門外的人進來說話。 「我們指稱這是蓄意叛國，」他說，儘管測試根本還未進行。 承包商曾承諾在安檢前美化河岸倉庫。 石門飾有鳥類雕刻，下方的題字讚美公正司法。 當兩張出貨重量表一起出現時，契約爭議達到高潮。

Soryn examined the account twice, once as a friend and once as a witness who might have to defend every word. The younger dragons demand revenge for their stolen eggs while elders question whether the eggs were actually taken by humans. The difference between what they suspected and what they could establish mattered. They placed the new fact beside the previous chapter’s unanswered question and looked for a second source that had not been copied from the first. In the border witnesses’ records, even ordinary details had been preserved: Ina asked the judge to extract the precise accusation from the speech and place it in the record. A delegation of academy students had arrived after an extracurricular debate class. Their teacher was hospitable, offering water and a shady bench to witnesses who had traveled far.

索林把紀錄看了兩次：第一次以朋友身分，第二次則像一位必須為每個字負責的證人。年輕龍族要求為失竊龍蛋復仇，長老卻質疑是否真是人類偷走。懷疑和能確立的事實必須分清楚。他們把新線索放在前一章未解的問題旁，尋找不曾從同一份資料抄出的第二來源。邊境證人的紀錄連尋常細節也留下了： 伊娜要求法官從演說中抽取具體指控，寫入紀錄。 一群學院學生參加課外辯論課後來到法庭。 他們的老師十分好客，為遠道而來的證人準備飲水與陰涼長椅。

A crowd gathered around the disputed notice. “You have to choose a side,” someone shouted. Ina answered that she had chosen one already: the side of a claim that survived questions. Soryn did not smile, but moved the most vulnerable bystanders away from the growing argument. The pair could disagree privately without turning their disagreement into a public threat. The retired mechanic stayed jolly even when the executives questioned the generator's strange name. Workers asked permission to mow the overgrown grass around the emergency exit. The clerk, a cheerful man with a large mustache, agreed to write the words exactly as spoken.

人群圍住有爭議的公告。「妳必須選邊站！」有人喊。伊娜回答自己早已選擇：站在經得起提問的主張那一邊。索林沒有微笑，只把最脆弱的旁觀者帶離爭執。他們可以私下不同意對方，卻不能把分歧變成公開威脅。 即使主管質疑發電機奇怪的名稱，退休技師仍很愉快。 工人請求修剪緊急出口旁長得過高的草。 留著大鬍子的開朗書記答應逐字記錄。

The institution was not a single villain. It contained frightened clerks, dedicated workers, ambitious supervisors, and people who had learned to hide mistakes. the royal delegation wanted the incident classified as routine. Ina asked whose definition of routine was being used and whose losses were absent from the statistics. Nobody at the table could answer without consulting the sealed records. In the border witnesses’ records, even ordinary details had been preserved: A grandmother told him to stop nagging and gave him an ounce of sweet tea. A baker explained that his neighborhood had become a temporary oasis for families driven from the frontier. One ounce of damaged insulation was sealed for laboratory comparison with the other samples. The civic court met beside a public garden on the outskirts of the capital.

這個機構不是單一惡棍。裡面有害怕的書記、盡責的工人、野心勃勃的主管，也有學會掩蓋錯誤的人。王室使團想把事件歸類為例行事項。伊娜追問：是誰定義例行？又有誰的損失被統計漏掉？桌邊沒人能在不看封存紀錄的情況下回答。邊境證人的紀錄連尋常細節也留下了： 一位祖母要他別再嘮叨，遞給他一小杯甜茶。 一位麵包師說，他的街區如今成了邊境流亡家庭暫時的綠洲。 一盎司受損絕緣材料被封存，供實驗室與其他樣本比對。 公民法庭位於京城郊區的公共花園旁。

Ina proposes a jointly witnessed search instead of bargaining over unproven accusations. The decision did not repair everything. It altered one procedure, protected one person, or saved one piece of evidence; on a day when almost everyone demanded a miracle, that was worth recording. Ina insisted that the result and its limits appear together in the official minutes. Anything less would turn a modest gain into tomorrow’s misleading legend. In the afternoon, a member of parliament appeared with a packet of engineering drawings. The nobleman's methods were ruthless but often lawful on paper: he delayed funding, demanded impossible permits, and threatened public employees with dismissal. A local sculptor offered a warehouse sketch that showed the original side entrance.

伊娜提議共同見證搜查，而不是拿未證實的指控當談判籌碼。這個決定沒有修好一切，只改變一道程序、保護一個人，或留下了一份證據；當幾乎所有人都要求奇蹟時，這仍值得記錄。伊娜堅持把成果和限制一起寫入正式紀錄。否則小小的進展又會變成明天誤導人的傳說。 下午，一名國會議員帶著工程圖出席。 這名貴族的方法冷酷，但在紙面上常常合法：延後經費、索取不可能取得的許可，並以解雇威脅公務員。 一名在地雕塑家提供倉庫素描，顯示原有側門。

The lights beyond the window did not offer comfort. They showed how many people were still waiting. Ina understood that the investigation might eventually expose her own earlier errors, and she resolved not to erase them. Soryn suggested a short rest before the next hearing. She agreed, because exhaustion could damage judgment as thoroughly as deliberate deception. The repair team preferred simplicity in emergency instructions over impressive technical language. A simultaneous release of both chambers was therefore forbidden until the second inspection. The minister's supporters called the decision a slump in imperial authority.

窗外的燈光沒有帶來安慰，只照出還有多少人在等待。伊娜明白調查最終可能揭露自己過去的錯誤，決定不去抹除。索林建議下一場聽證前短暫休息。她同意，因為疲憊和蓄意欺騙一樣可能損害判斷。 維修團隊偏好簡單的緊急指示，而非炫目的技術詞。 因此，在第二次檢查以前，兩個艙室不得同時放能。 大臣支持者稱這是邊境王國權威的衰退。
