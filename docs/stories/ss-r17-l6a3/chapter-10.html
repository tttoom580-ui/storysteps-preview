公共水井

A Public Well

成為惡龍的首席談判官 · 第 10 / 25 章 · 5000–6000 · 英中對照 · 校訂中

It began not with a battle but with an ordinary interruption in the border valley. The first five-day review confirms both villages have received water, but the disputed pass remains dangerous. At first, no one considered the interruption important enough to preserve. Ina thought otherwise. A forgotten detail could become a map, and a map could be used to control where frightened people were permitted to go. The supplier sought a concession that would grant it control over the canal for a decade. A cynical investor said patients were useful only when their stories moved the market. The old procurement rule treated silence as consent by default, which the new board rejected. An emergency fund should not deplete the clinic budget merely to improve quarterly profit.

事情不是從戰鬥開始，而是從邊境山谷裡一次平凡的中斷開始。第一階段五日審查確認兩座村莊都有水，爭議山口卻仍危險。起初沒人認為這件事值得留下紀錄，但伊娜想法不同。遭忽略的細節可能變成地圖，而地圖也可能被用來控制驚恐的人可以去哪裡。 供應商尋求特許權，想控制運河十年。 一位憤世嫉俗的投資人說，病人故事只有能推動市場時才有用。 舊採購規則預設沉默即同意，新董事會予以否決。 緊急基金不該只為改善季度利潤就耗盡診所預算。

Soryn came back carrying a practical objection rather than an inspiring speech. A local council member proposes restricting all access to military personnel until the summit. They had both seen people harmed when an assumption hardened into policy. A signed report could be wrong, and an emotional memory could be right, but neither became proof merely by being repeated. They agreed to seek a measurement from an independent observer. In the border witnesses’ records, even ordinary details had been preserved: Soldiers handed out sweet buns, and the fragrance of crushed flowers covered the smell of oil along the river. No official would be permitted to intrude on protected witness meetings without a court order. A former jockey now driving delivery vans recognized the unusual generator model.

索林帶回的是實際的異議，而不是振奮人心的演說。地方議員提議在峰會前只准軍人通行。他們都見過假設硬化成政策後如何傷人。有簽名的報告可能是錯的，強烈的記憶也可能是真的；兩者都不會因為反覆提起就變成證據。他們決定找獨立觀察員再測量一次。邊境證人的紀錄連尋常細節也留下了： 士兵發放甜麵包，壓碎花朵的香氣蓋住河邊油味。 沒有法院命令，任何官員都不得闖入受保護的證人會面。 一名曾當賽馬騎師的廂車司機認出了不尋常的發電機型號。

“We can tell the truth without pretending we know everything,” Ina said. Soryn replied that a partial truth was difficult to defend against people who controlled the doors. She answered that a perfect story with no verifiable source was more dangerous. They divided the work: one would protect the witnesses, while the other checked the physical records and the sequence of events. The lawyer insisted on a literal reading of the signed annex rather than the chairman's memories. Worker morale improved after the company published the failed-test results without punishment. She declined, saying that no state should prosecute a person merely to obtain someone else's custody.

「我們能說實話，不需要假裝知道一切。」伊娜說。索林回答，當對方掌控所有門口時，局部真相比較難捍衛。她則認為沒有可驗證來源的完美故事更危險。他們分配工作：一人保護證人，另一人核查實體紀錄和事件時序。 律師堅持按簽署附約的字面意思解讀，而非採信董事長的記憶。 公司公布失敗測試結果且未任意處罰後，工人士氣改善。 她拒絕了，表示國家不能為取得另一個人的拘押權而起訴無辜者。

The argument moved from a private corridor into a formal room, where every statement would be copied. A senior official from the royal delegation demanded certainty before granting access. Ina offered a less comfortable bargain: a limited preservation order now, followed by independent examination and a chance for every accused person to respond. That was enough to prevent an irreversible decision. In the border witnesses’ records, even ordinary details had been preserved: The maintenance inspector checked for radioactive materials because an old sensor carried a misleading label. The chancellor's lawyer made a sharp retort about national morale, claiming that questioning the border furnace would deplete public confidence. A young courier came forward with a screwdriver and a box of broken clock parts. Outside, an elderly vendor offered each departing witness a serving of warm soup.

爭論從私下走廊進入正式會議室，每句話都會留下副本。王室使團的一名高級官員要求先確定真相才准查資料。伊娜提出比較不舒服的交換：先給有期限的保全命令，再獨立檢查，也讓所有被指控者有回應機會。這足以擋下一個不可逆的決定。邊境證人的紀錄連尋常細節也留下了： 維修檢查員因舊感測器標示誤導而檢查有無放射性物質。 宰相律師尖銳反駁，談起國民士氣，聲稱質疑邊境熔爐會耗盡公眾信心。 一名年輕信差帶著螺絲起子和一箱壞時鐘零件上前。 庭外，一名年長攤販給離開的每位證人一份熱湯。

Ina persuades the delegates to place civilian water monitors on the road instead. Even those who agreed with the choice could see its cost. Time was running out, supplies were not unlimited, and a mistake might travel far beyond this room. Soryn asked whether they should conceal the uncertainty until the crisis passed. Ina shook her head. People could not make free choices if the information reaching them had already been edited. The tower's splendor seemed irrelevant beside the dark apartments across the river. A spontaneous murmur passed through the audience. His stepfather, the palace maintenance chief, had told him to keep quiet.

伊娜說服代表在道路安排平民水源監督員。即使同意這個選擇的人，也看得出代價。時間不多、資源有限，一次錯誤可能傳得很遠。索林問是否該先隱瞞不確定性，等危機過後再說。伊娜搖頭。若資訊早被剪裁，人們便無法作出自由的選擇。 相較河對岸漆黑的公寓，大樓的華麗顯得毫無意義。 旁聽席傳出自發的低語。 他的繼父是宮廷維修主管，曾命他保持沉默。

Later, under dim lights, Ina reread the original question. The dispute was larger now, but its center remained unchanged: who had the right to make an irreversible choice for someone else? Soryn stayed until the last copy was sealed. Neither spoke about victory. They knew the next door would open only because today’s evidence had been preserved and today’s mistakes had been admitted. The nervous clerk began to stutter before the judge invited her to speak slowly. A legal aid fund would subsidize travel for frontier witnesses who could not afford the journey. The military had claimed its newest heat reservoirs contained harmless synthetic stone.

晚些時候，在微弱燈光下，伊娜重讀最初的問題。爭端已經擴大，但核心沒變：誰有權替別人作出不可逆的選擇？索林留到最後一份副本封存。他們沒有談勝利，只知道下一扇門之所以能打開，是因為今天保住了證據，也承認了錯誤。 緊張的書記開始口吃，法官請她慢慢說。 法律援助基金將補助無力負擔旅費的邊境證人。 軍方聲稱最新儲能庫採用無害的合成石材；學院在丟棄的隔熱材料中發現少量放射性礦物痕跡，但含量不足以確認疾病原因。
