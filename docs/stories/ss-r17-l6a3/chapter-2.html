灰燼谷

The Valley of Ash

成為惡龍的首席談判官 · 第 2 / 25 章 · 5000–6000 · 英中對照 · 校訂中

Ina arrived with the unpleasant certainty that the simplest explanation was probably the one that had been prepared for public consumption. A ruined farming valley reveals scorch marks from human artillery rather than dragon flame. The place was full of people who needed a quick answer, yet the evidence still belonged to those whose lives it could change. Instead of making a dramatic accusation, Ina marked the hour, the witnesses, and the smallest observable detail in a paper notebook. An aboriginal community upstream requested access to the substation's emergency warning system. Small settlements abound along the ridges, where families tended terraced gardens under extreme weather. Ina asked the technicians to align the two timestamps before comparing the footage. At that altitude the air was thin enough to make breathing difficult.

伊娜抵達時，心裡很清楚：最簡單的解釋往往是事先準備好要給大眾聽的版本。遭毀的農業山谷留下的是人類火砲焦痕，不是龍焰。現場的人都急需答案，但證據仍屬於那些生活可能被它改變的人。她沒有戲劇化地指控誰，而是先在紙本筆記上記下時刻、證人，以及最小卻可觀察的細節。 上游原住民族社群要求能接收到變電站的緊急警報。 山脊遍布小聚落，家家在嚴酷天候下照顧梯田。 伊娜請技師先對齊兩組時間戳，再比對影像。 這樣的海拔使空氣稀薄，呼吸格外費力。

There was a moment of quiet when Soryn set the new material on the table. The commander calls the difference irrelevant because the crown has already named the dragons aggressors. What mattered was not how surprising the discovery sounded, but whether anyone could repeat the test. Ina copied the relevant names carefully. If one person later changed the account, the original would still be available for comparison. In the border witnesses’ records, even ordinary details had been preserved: The guide's annoyance was visible. “The capital still draws bridges where only wreckage remains,” she said, blunt as a hammer. A student described the dark pedestrian crossing where a cyclist had nearly struck him.

索林把新資料放上桌時，四周安靜了一瞬。指揮官說差別無關緊要，因為王室早將龍族定為侵略者。重要的不是發現有多驚人，而是別人能不能重做同一項測試。伊娜仔細抄下相關姓名；如果日後有人更改說法，原件仍可供比對。邊境證人的紀錄連尋常細節也留下了： 嚮導的惱怒溢於言表。 「京城還在畫橋，這裡卻只剩殘骸，」她直言不諱。 學生描述漆黑的行人穿越道，說自己差點被自行車撞到。

“Will you trust me?” Soryn asked. “With the evidence, yes,” Ina answered, “but not with a blank signature.” It was the sort of answer that could sound cold until someone noticed how carefully she had protected the other person’s right to disagree. Together they made a plan with a backup route, a deadline, and a record of who had accepted each responsibility. No technician was allowed to discard the damaged seal before independent photographs were taken. The inspector recorded an unexpected electrical discharge near the damaged control panel. Their joint endeavor would be assessed through measurements, not a flattering press release.

「妳信任我嗎？」索林問。「證據上可以，空白授權書不行。」伊娜回答。這聽起來很冷淡，直到人們注意到她也小心保護對方拒絕的權利。他們一起訂下有備用路線、期限，以及明確責任分工的計畫。 完成獨立拍照前，技師不得丟棄受損封條。 檢查員記錄損壞控制板附近一次意外放電。 雙方共同努力將以測量結果評估，而非以討好的新聞稿。

A representative of the royal delegation arrived with an offer to resolve the matter quietly. Quietly, in this case, meant without giving affected people access to the original. Ina declined. She knew an office could be useful and still be dangerously wrong. Her objection was narrow and specific: the proposed decision relied on a document whose origin had not yet been independently verified. In the border witnesses’ records, even ordinary details had been preserved: The first hurdle was getting both companies to accept a technician chosen by workers. “Yet they announced he was discharged for desertion.” The princess maintained a careful posture while examining the seal; her anger could not improve the evidence. The team needed precision because a three-minute error could distort the entire timeline. A rigorous inspection revealed a damaged timestamp seal but an undamaged outer casing.

王室使團的代表前來，提議私下解決。所謂私下，就是不讓受影響的人看到原件。伊娜拒絕了。她知道一個機關可以有用，也可能犯下危險的錯。她的反對具體而有限：提議中的決定依賴一份來源還沒接受獨立驗證的文件。邊境證人的紀錄連尋常細節也留下了： 第一道障礙是讓兩家公司接受工人推選的技師。 」伊娜檢查封印時努力保持鎮定，因為怒氣不能改善證據。 團隊必須精確，因為三分鐘誤差可能扭曲整段時間線。 嚴格檢查發現時間戳封條損壞，外殼卻完好。

Ina protects a farmer's testimony and orders the weapons inspected by neutral blacksmiths. A few witnesses thanked Ina; others accused her of doing too little. She accepted both reactions without offering a promise she could not keep. Soryn wrote down the complaints, including the ones directed at their own conduct. They were not building a monument to themselves. They were building a process that would still function after they left. At a wind-battered terrace, the party found an elderly widow repairing her roof. A workers' uprising was not the issue; they were requesting the safety rights already promised. A volunteer usher led witnesses through the side entrance to protect their privacy.

伊娜保護農夫證詞，要求中立鐵匠檢驗武器。幾名證人向伊娜道謝，另一些人指責她做得不夠。她接受兩種反應，不許下無法兌現的承諾。索林寫下抱怨，包括針對他們自己行為的部分。他們不是為自己立紀念碑，而是建立自己離開後仍能運作的程序。 在飽受狂風吹襲的梯田邊，隊伍找到一名修屋頂的年長寡婦。 這不是工人起義；他們只是要求已被承諾的安全權利。 志工引導員帶證人從側門進入，以保護隱私。

By nightfall the newest report was signed by observers who were free to disagree. Ina stood beside Soryn and looked at the distance between them and a seven-day agreement that both peoples can freely accept. Progress felt less like triumph than like leaving a reliable mark on a difficult road. Someone would challenge that mark tomorrow. This time, there would be a public record with which to answer. The versatile backup unit could power either a clinic refrigerator or an emergency radio. The workers retained a safety veto over repairs that failed the written threshold. Such a promise, the widow said, sounded more honest than a royal blessing.

夜幕降臨前，新的報告由可以提出異議的觀察者簽署。伊娜站在索林身旁，看著他們與雙方自願接受的七日協議之間的距離。進展不像勝利，比較像在艱難路上留下可靠的記號。明天有人會質疑，而這一次將有公開紀錄可以回答。 多功能備用裝置可供應診所冰箱或緊急無線電。 工人保有對不符合書面門檻之維修工作的安全否決權。 寡婦說，這樣的承諾比王室祝福更誠實。
