The First Day's Bargain

成為惡龍的首席談判官 · 第 5 / 25 章 · 5000–6000 · 英中對照 · 校訂中

Ina arrived with the unpleasant certainty that the simplest explanation was probably the one that had been prepared for public consumption. Water carriers negotiate a temporary path through the disputed pass while armed escorts wait outside the village. The place was full of people who needed a quick answer, yet the evidence still belonged to those whose lives it could change. Instead of making a dramatic accusation, Ina marked the hour, the witnesses, and the smallest observable detail in a paper notebook. The company academy offered a practical course on emergency power rather than executive slogans. A false accusation against the warehouse team was withdrawn after the access records were checked. A banquet was taking place across the courtyard, where donors praised the war effort. A relief grant originally intended for fertilizer purchases had been redirected without explanation.

伊娜抵達時，心裡很清楚：最簡單的解釋往往是事先準備好要給大眾聽的版本。運水工談成暫時通行爭議山口的路線，武裝護衛只能留在村外。現場的人都急需答案，但證據仍屬於那些生活可能被它改變的人。她沒有戲劇化地指控誰，而是先在紙本筆記上記下時刻、證人，以及最小卻可觀察的細節。 公司學院開設緊急供電實務課，而非宣傳主管口號。 查核門禁紀錄後，對倉庫團隊的不實指控遭撤回。 庭院對面正在舉行宴會，捐助者讚美戰爭。 原訂購買肥料的救助補助遭不明轉用。

There was a moment of quiet when Soryn set the new material on the table. The route appears harmless until Ina notices a supply convoy carrying more chains than medical goods. What mattered was not how surprising the discovery sounded, but whether anyone could repeat the test. Ina copied the relevant names carefully. If one person later changed the account, the original would still be available for comparison. In the border witnesses’ records, even ordinary details had been preserved: The riverside fishery requested a dedicated backup line to protect its refrigerated catch. The theft allegation began to fracture under the weight of its own chronology. Technicians learned to harness surplus power without bypassing the required safety checks.

索林把新資料放上桌時，四周安靜了一瞬。路線看似無害，伊娜卻發現補給車隊載的鎖鏈比醫療物資還多。重要的不是發現有多驚人，而是別人能不能重做同一項測試。伊娜仔細抄下相關姓名；如果日後有人更改說法，原件仍可供比對。邊境證人的紀錄連尋常細節也留下了： 河岸漁業要求專用備電線路，以保護冷藏漁獲。 竊取指控開始因自身時間線的矛盾而瓦解。 技師學習利用剩餘電力，不繞過必要安全檢查。

“Will you trust me?” Soryn asked. “With the evidence, yes,” Ina answered, “but not with a blank signature.” It was the sort of answer that could sound cold until someone noticed how carefully she had protected the other person’s right to disagree. Together they made a plan with a backup route, a deadline, and a record of who had accepted each responsibility. Each operator wore headphones to hear control-room instructions over the storm warnings. The clinic reviewed its oxygen intake records to calculate emergency battery demand. In the courtyard, a troupe rehearsed a ceremonial dance called the Maiden's Promise, though its lyrics had been rewritten to glorify the war.

「妳信任我嗎？」索林問。「證據上可以，空白授權書不行。」伊娜回答。這聽起來很冷淡，直到人們注意到她也小心保護對方拒絕的權利。他們一起訂下有備用路線、期限，以及明確責任分工的計畫。 每位操作員戴耳機，以在風暴警報聲中聽清控制室指示。 診所檢視氧氣攝入紀錄，計算緊急電池需求。 庭院裡，一個劇團排練名為〈少女的承諾〉的儀式舞蹈，但歌詞早被改成頌揚戰爭。

A representative of the royal delegation arrived with an offer to resolve the matter quietly. Quietly, in this case, meant without giving affected people access to the original. Ina declined. She knew an office could be useful and still be dangerously wrong. Her objection was narrow and specific: the proposed decision relied on a document whose origin had not yet been independently verified. In the border witnesses’ records, even ordinary details had been preserved: An offshore contractor had received the suspicious payment three weeks before the blackout. Now she allowed them to enter a sealed lecture hall, but only as prospective witnesses, not celebrated heroes. A resident asked for a referendum on the long-term public ownership of the canal land. Ina proposed to refine the test schedule instead of rushing a ceremonial reopening.

王室使團的代表前來，提議私下解決。所謂私下，就是不讓受影響的人看到原件。伊娜拒絕了。她知道一個機關可以有用，也可能犯下危險的錯。她的反對具體而有限：提議中的決定依賴一份來源還沒接受獨立驗證的文件。邊境證人的紀錄連尋常細節也留下了： 一家離岸承包商在停電前三週收到可疑款項。 如今她允許他們進入封閉講堂，但只能以潛在證人身分入內，不能當成受歡迎的英雄。 一名居民要求針對運河土地的長期公共所有權舉辦公投。 伊娜建議改進測試時程，而非趕著辦象徵性重啟。

She pauses the convoy and publishes the inspection without branding the drivers guilty. A few witnesses thanked Ina; others accused her of doing too little. She accepted both reactions without offering a promise she could not keep. Soryn wrote down the complaints, including the ones directed at their own conduct. They were not building a monument to themselves. They were building a process that would still function after they left. Several students quietly began to rejoice; others feared that the academy would be closed for allowing dissent. She remembered his objections to the academy's militarized experiments, and how she had dismissed them as resentment. The operating room remained serene after its generator switched smoothly to backup power.

她暫停車隊，公開檢驗結果，不把司機直接定罪。幾名證人向伊娜道謝，另一些人指責她做得不夠。她接受兩種反應，不許下無法兌現的承諾。索林寫下抱怨，包括針對他們自己行為的部分。他們不是為自己立紀念碑，而是建立自己離開後仍能運作的程序。 幾名學生悄悄歡欣，其他人則害怕學院因容許異議而被關閉。 她記得當年他反對學院把實驗軍事化，自己卻把反對當作怨恨。 手術室的發電機順利切換備電後，室內依舊平靜。

By nightfall the newest report was signed by observers who were free to disagree. Ina stood beside Soryn and looked at the distance between them and a seven-day agreement that both peoples can freely accept. Progress felt less like triumph than like leaving a reliable mark on a difficult road. Someone would challenge that mark tomorrow. This time, there would be a public record with which to answer. A neighborhood pastor gave a short sermon about caring for people before property. The board refused to shuffle expenses between accounts to make the trial look cheaper. A tenant demanded proof that her rent would not rise because of the emergency repair.

夜幕降臨前，新的報告由可以提出異議的觀察者簽署。伊娜站在索林身旁，看著他們與雙方自願接受的七日協議之間的距離。進展不像勝利，比較像在艱難路上留下可靠的記號。明天有人會質疑，而這一次將有公開紀錄可以回答。 社區牧師簡短講道，提醒人們應先照顧人，再談財產。 董事會拒絕在帳戶間調換支出，營造試辦更便宜的假象。 一名房客要求保證不會因緊急維修而漲租。
