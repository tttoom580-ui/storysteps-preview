A Very Small Candidate

When the ballot box opened, Mochi the hamster had more votes than any student. At first, Tomo thought the problem could be solved before the next bell. A closer look showed that someone else's wishes mattered too. Hana listened without interrupting, while Ms. Lin watched the group from nearby. They did not have to agree on everything immediately. There was time to separate a real observation from a story someone had only imagined. Tomo took out the class duty chart and asked what should be checked first.

票箱打開時，倉鼠麻糬得到的票竟比任何學生都多。 起初，拓真以為下次鐘響前就能解決問題。但仔細看過後才發現，別人的願望也很重要。花音沒有打斷，靜靜傾聽，林老師則在附近看著大家。眾人不必立刻同意每件事，還有時間分清真實觀察與想像中的說法。拓真拿出班務表，問大家應先確認什麼。

Tomo was afraid that saying the wrong thing would make the day worse. Hana answered that staying silent could also leave a misunderstanding in place. They practiced one honest sentence and checked that it did not blame anyone unfairly. This took longer than making a joke, but the result was clearer. Near the table, Ms. Lin nodded and let the young people try their own words before offering advice. Hana asked how to use a.m. in a sentence about their day. In the English practice book, bell appeared on the next line. Tomo read breakfast aloud, then checked the meaning with Hana. Hana drew a small picture of cake in the margin of the class story page. The word couch gave the group a new example to discuss.

拓真害怕說錯話會讓事情變糟。花音回答，保持沉默有時也會讓誤會繼續存在。他們練習說出一句誠實的話，再確認沒有不公平地責怪任何人。這比開玩笑花時間，結果卻更清楚。桌旁的林老師點頭，先讓年輕人用自己的話嘗試，再提供建議。 花音問起，如何用「a.m.」（上午（a.m.））寫一句與今天有關的話。 英文練習本的下一行出現「bell」（鐘聲；鈴鐺）。 拓真朗讀「breakfast」（早餐），再和花音確認意思。 花音在班級故事頁的空白處畫了「cake」（蛋糕）的圖。 「couch」（沙發）這個字讓小組多了一個可以討論的例子。

Tomo asked whether a pet could really be class monitor. There was a quiet kind of courage in trying again. Tomo noticed that the last attempt had taught them something, even without a perfect result. Hana offered practical help and asked which part would be most useful. They worked together for a short time and then stopped to check whether they were still following the rule. Ms. Lin praised the careful check more than the speed of the work. Hana put cover on the board without turning it into a test. They found early in a short reading exercise near the end of the page. Tomo drew a tiny sign beside exercise so it would be easier to remember. Hana chose foreign as a word to explain kindly to a new learner. Hana drew a small picture of fruit in the margin of the class story page.

拓真問，一隻寵物真的能當班長嗎？ 願意再試一次，是一種安靜的勇氣。拓真發現，就算上次結果不完美，大家仍學到了一些事。花音提供實際協助，先問哪一部分最有用。他們一起工作一小段時間，再停下來確認是否仍遵守規則。林老師稱讚的不是速度，而是這份細心。 花音把「cover」（外罩；覆蓋物）寫在板上，卻沒有把它變成考試。 他們在頁面末尾的短文練習裡找到「early」（提早）。 拓真在「exercise」（運動）旁畫上小記號，幫助記憶。 花音選了「foreign」（外文的），準備耐心向初學者說明。 花音在班級故事頁的空白處畫了「fruit」（水果）的圖。

They began by asking who might be affected by the next step. Hana thought about the people who had not spoken yet, and Tomo thought about the rule they had already promised to follow. One person offered a quick answer, but the group asked for a reason. A good question was not an attack. It was a way to make the answer safer and kinder. Ms. Lin reminded them that everyone could ask for a pause. Hana asked how to use he in a sentence about their day. In the English practice book, heavy appeared on the next line. Tomo read hobby aloud, then checked the meaning with Hana. Another card carried monkey and a simple drawing by a classmate. The word salad gave the group a new example to discuss.

大家先問，下一步會影響到誰。花音想到還沒發言的人，拓真則想到自己早已承諾遵守的規則。有人很快提出答案，其他人卻請他說明理由。好問題不是攻擊，而是讓答案更安全、更體貼的方法。林老師提醒大家，每個人都可以要求暫停。 花音問起，如何用「he」（他；他的）寫一句與今天有關的話。 英文練習本的下一行出現「heavy」（沉重的）。 拓真朗讀「hobby」（興趣），再和花音確認意思。 另一張圖卡上有「monkey」（猴子），旁邊是同學的簡筆畫。 「salad」（沙拉）這個字讓小組多了一個可以討論的例子。

Ms. Lin postponed the result until the class understood the election rules. The answer made someone smile, but Tomo still asked one final question: was everyone comfortable with what came next? Hana listened to the replies, including the quiet ones. They changed a small detail when one person needed something different. Nothing about the change made the story less meaningful. It helped the Mochi’s safe home belong to more than one person, which was why the work had begun. Hana put shall on the board without turning it into a test. They found sun in a short reading exercise near the end of the page. Tomo drew a tiny sign beside teach so it would be easier to remember. Hana drew a small picture of tooth in the margin of the class story page. A small study card showed write, which Tomo copied beside today's notes.

林老師暫緩宣布結果，要全班先弄清選舉規則。 答案讓某個人笑了，但拓真仍問最後一個問題：大家都能接受下一步嗎？花音聽取回答，包括輕聲說出的意見。有人有不同需求，他們就修改一個小細節。這種修改沒有讓故事失去意義，反而讓麻糬的安全小窩不只屬於一個人；這也正是大家當初開始的原因。 花音把「shall」（要不要／應否）寫在板上，卻沒有把它變成考試。 他們在頁面末尾的短文練習裡找到「sun」（太陽）。 拓真在「teach」（教）旁畫上小記號，幫助記憶。 花音在班級故事頁的空白處畫了「tooth」（牙齒）的圖。 一張小小練習卡上寫著「write」（寫），拓真把它抄在今日筆記旁。

The hallway grew quiet, and Tomo finally had time to think. A good friend had not supplied every answer. Instead, Hana had left space for another idea and had stayed when the first idea failed. That was harder to notice than a loud victory, but it was real. Tomo made a promise to remember the person as well as the task. The Mochi’s safe home was important; so were the people who helped shape it. With that thought, the next chapter of their ordinary and extraordinary days felt possible.

走廊逐漸安靜，拓真終於有時間思考。好朋友不是替自己提供每個答案；花音反而留下思考空間，也在第一個想法失敗時陪在身旁。這比熱鬧的勝利難以察覺，卻是真實的。拓真答應自己，不只記得任務，也要記得參與的人。麻糬的安全小窩重要，幫忙塑造它的人也同樣重要。帶著這個念頭，他們平凡又不平凡的日子可以繼續寫下去。
