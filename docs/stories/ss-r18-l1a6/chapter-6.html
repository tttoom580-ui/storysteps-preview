今天起，倉鼠當班長 · 第 6 / 25 章 · 1000以下 · 英中對照 · 校訂中

Three students spoke about their plans to help the class. The light through the window looked ordinary, but the news did not. Tomo stopped for a moment, feeling both curious and unsure. Hana did not laugh at the question, even when it sounded strange. They knew from earlier mistakes that moving too fast could make a simple problem harder. The classroom offered enough space for a calm talk. On the first page of the class duty chart, Tomo wrote what was known and left a blank space for what remained uncertain.

三名同學說明自己想怎麼服務班級。 窗外的光線和平常一樣，消息卻一點也不平常。拓真停了一會兒，既好奇又不確定。就算問題聽來奇怪，花音也沒有取笑。他們從先前的錯誤學到，行動太快反而可能讓小問題變複雜。教室有足夠空間讓大家平靜討論。拓真在班務表第一頁寫下已知的事，也留下空白給尚未確定的部分。

They began by asking who might be affected by the next step. Hana thought about the people who had not spoken yet, and Tomo thought about the rule they had already promised to follow. One person offered a quick answer, but the group asked for a reason. A good question was not an attack. It was a way to make the answer safer and kinder. Ms. Lin reminded them that everyone could ask for a pause. Hana put arrive on the board without turning it into a test. They found before in a short reading exercise near the end of the page. Tomo drew a tiny sign beside boat so it would be easier to remember. Hana drew a small picture of box in the margin of the class story page. A small study card showed coffee, which Tomo copied beside today's notes.

大家先問，下一步會影響到誰。花音想到還沒發言的人，拓真則想到自己早已承諾遵守的規則。有人很快提出答案，其他人卻請他說明理由。好問題不是攻擊，而是讓答案更安全、更體貼的方法。林老師提醒大家，每個人都可以要求暫停。 花音把「arrive」（抵達）寫在板上，卻沒有把它變成考試。 他們在頁面末尾的短文練習裡找到「before」（在……之前）。 拓真在「boat」（船）旁畫上小記號，幫助記憶。 花音在班級故事頁的空白處畫了「box」（箱子）的圖。 一張小小練習卡上寫著「coffee」（咖啡），拓真把它抄在今日筆記旁。

Hana asked everyone to judge plans instead of popularity. Tomo chose the part of the work that could be done carefully. Hana handled a different task and promised to report back before making any large change. A short question from Ms. Lin caught an error in their first version. Instead of hiding it, they made a neat correction on the page. That correction did not erase their effort. It showed that the group was learning how to be responsible for its own work. Hana asked how to use drive in a sentence about their day. In the English practice book, except appeared on the next line. Tomo read finally aloud, then checked the meaning with Hana. Another card carried free and a simple drawing by a classmate. The word house gave the group a new example to discuss.

花音請大家根據計畫而不是人氣投票。 拓真選擇自己能謹慎完成的工作，花音負責另一項任務，並答應在重大更改前先回報。林老師的一個簡短問題，讓大家發現第一版中的錯誤。他們沒有掩飾，而是在頁面上整齊修正。這項修正沒有抹去先前的努力，反而證明小組正在學習對自己的工作負責。 花音問起，如何用「drive」（開車）寫一句與今天有關的話。 英文練習本的下一行出現「except」（除了）。 拓真朗讀「finally」（終於），再和花音確認意思。 另一張圖卡上有「free」（免費的），旁邊是同學的簡筆畫。 「house」（房屋）這個字讓小組多了一個可以討論的例子。

Tomo was afraid that saying the wrong thing would make the day worse. Hana answered that staying silent could also leave a misunderstanding in place. They practiced one honest sentence and checked that it did not blame anyone unfairly. This took longer than making a joke, but the result was clearer. Near the table, Ms. Lin nodded and let the young people try their own words before offering advice. Hana put lion on the board without turning it into a test. They found see in a short reading exercise near the end of the page. Tomo drew a tiny sign beside shape so it would be easier to remember. Hana chose sign as a word to explain kindly to a new learner. A small study card showed stupid, which Tomo copied beside today's notes.

拓真害怕說錯話會讓事情變糟。花音回答，保持沉默有時也會讓誤會繼續存在。他們練習說出一句誠實的話，再確認沒有不公平地責怪任何人。這比開玩笑花時間，結果卻更清楚。桌旁的林老師點頭，先讓年輕人用自己的話嘗試，再提供建議。 花音把「lion」（獅子）寫在板上，卻沒有把它變成考試。 他們在頁面末尾的短文練習裡找到「see」（看見）。 拓真在「shape」（形狀）旁畫上小記號，幫助記憶。 花音選了「sign」（標誌），準備耐心向初學者說明。 一張小小練習卡上寫著「stupid」（笨的），拓真把它抄在今日筆記旁。

Tomo became the student monitor, while Mochi kept his funny honorary badge. Ms. Lin asked the group to explain its choice in everyday words. Tomo tried once, paused, and then gave a shorter answer. Hana added an important detail that had been left out. The explanation became clear enough for another person to check. That mattered more than making their decision sound grand. The Mochi’s safe home could grow only if other people understood how it worked. Hana asked how to use terrible in a sentence about their day. In the English practice book, top appeared on the next line. Tomo read uncle aloud, then checked the meaning with Hana. Another card carried wave and a simple drawing by a classmate. The word weekend gave the group a new example to discuss.

拓真當選學生班長，麻糬則保有逗趣的榮譽徽章。 林老師請小組用平常的話解釋選擇。拓真試了一次，停頓後改用更短的回答。花音補充了一個漏掉的重要細節。解釋變得足夠清楚，讓別人也能核對。這比把決定說得偉大更重要。只有其他人理解做法，麻糬的安全小窩才有機會繼續成長。 花音問起，如何用「terrible」（嚴重的；糟糕的）寫一句與今天有關的話。 英文練習本的下一行出現「top」（頂端）。 拓真朗讀「uncle」（舅舅），再和花音確認意思。 另一張圖卡上有「wave」（揮手），旁邊是同學的簡筆畫。 「weekend」（週末）這個字讓小組多了一個可以討論的例子。

Outside, the air was cooler than it had been at lunch. Tomo looked back once and saw the classroom in the evening light. It seemed like an ordinary place, but there was more to it now: a remembered question, a mistake corrected, and a choice made together. Hana waved from a few steps away. They still had their separate dreams and small worries, yet they also shared one clear memory. Whatever happened next, they could return to that memory without needing to pretend the day had been perfect.

外面的空氣比午餐時涼了些。拓真回頭望了一眼晚光中的教室。它看似平凡，現在卻多了值得記住的問題、改正的錯誤，以及共同做出的選擇。花音在幾步外揮手。他們仍有各自的夢想和小煩惱，也有一段共同的清晰回憶。無論接下來發生什麼，都不必假裝今天完美，就能想起它的價值。
