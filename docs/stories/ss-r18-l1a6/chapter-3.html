The class held a meeting while Mochi ran in his safe exercise wheel. Nobody had planned for this moment. The room was full of ordinary sounds, yet Tomo felt that something important had changed. Hana waited beside the door and let the first surprise settle. It was tempting to find an easy answer before anyone knew the whole story. Instead, they looked carefully at what was truly in front of them. Tomo opened the class duty chart and made a small note about the day.

班上開會時，麻糬在安全的滾輪裡跑步。 沒有人預料到這一刻。教室充滿平常的聲音，拓真卻感覺有件重要的事改變了。花音站在門旁，讓最初的驚訝慢慢沉澱。大家很容易在弄清事情之前就急著下結論，但他們決定先看清眼前真正發生的事。拓真翻開班務表，記下今天的小小發現。

The first idea sounded simple, and that was useful. Still, Tomo wanted to know whether it respected everyone's choice. Hana suggested changing only one part rather than throwing the whole plan away. They drew two small boxes on a page: what we can do and what we must ask about. The list made the unusual class election easier to discuss without turning a friend into an enemy. Tomo read about aloud, then checked the meaning with Hana. Another card carried beach and a simple drawing by a classmate. The word bird gave the group a new example to discuss. Hana put born on the board without turning it into a test. They found bridge in a short reading exercise near the end of the page.

第一個想法聽起來很簡單，這是好事。但拓真還想確認是否尊重每個人的選擇。花音建議只修改其中一部分，不必推翻整個計畫。他們在紙上畫兩個方框：我們能做的事，以及必須先詢問的事。有了這份清單，討論特別的班級選舉便容易多了，也不會把朋友當成敵人。 拓真朗讀「about」（關於），再和花音確認意思。 另一張圖卡上有「beach」（海灘），旁邊是同學的簡筆畫。 「bird」（鳥）這個字讓小組多了一個可以討論的例子。 花音把「born」（出生的）寫在板上，卻沒有把它變成考試。 他們在頁面末尾的短文練習裡找到「bridge」（橋）。

Tomo proposed an honorary title and a new election for the real duties. The plan became real only when someone took the first small action. Tomo checked the time, Hana checked the shared materials, and Ms. Lin confirmed which steps needed adult approval. The group left a clear record so that a person arriving later could understand the decision. They also agreed on what to do if something went wrong. It was not exciting in the way stories often promised, but it let everyone move forward with confidence. Tomo drew a tiny sign beside clean so it would be easier to remember. Hana drew a small picture of cow in the margin of the class story page. A small study card showed every, which Tomo copied beside today's notes. Hana asked how to use far in a sentence about their day. In the English practice book, follow appeared on the next line.

拓真提議讓麻糬當榮譽班長，真正職務另行選舉。 有人做出第一個小小行動時，計畫才真正開始。拓真確認時間，花音檢查共用材料，林老師確認哪些步驟需要大人同意。小組留下清楚紀錄，讓稍後到的人也能理解決定。他們還約好若事情出錯該怎麼辦。這不像故事裡常見的刺激冒險，卻讓每個人都更有信心往前走。 拓真在「clean」（乾淨的）旁畫上小記號，幫助記憶。 花音在班級故事頁的空白處畫了「cow」（牛）的圖。 一張小小練習卡上寫著「every」（每個），拓真把它抄在今日筆記旁。 花音問起，如何用「far」（遠的）寫一句與今天有關的話。 英文練習本的下一行出現「follow」（跟隨）。

For a minute, the group spoke all at once. Tomo raised a hand and suggested taking turns. Hana repeated the question in simpler words, so that even a quiet student could follow the talk. Nobody needed to win an argument. They needed to learn which part of the problem could be handled now and which part needed more help. The unusual class election remained difficult, but it no longer felt impossible. Tomo read happen aloud, then checked the meaning with Hana. Another card carried into and a simple drawing by a classmate. The word line gave the group a new example to discuss. Hana put pull on the board without turning it into a test. They found road in a short reading exercise near the end of the page.

有一分鐘，大家幾乎同時開口。拓真舉起手，提議輪流說話。花音用更簡單的詞重述問題，讓安靜的學生也能跟上。沒有人需要爭贏；重要的是分清哪些事現在能做、哪些事需要更多協助。特別的班級選舉仍然困難，卻不再讓人覺得無法處理。 拓真朗讀「happen」（發生），再和花音確認意思。 另一張圖卡上有「into」（進入），旁邊是同學的簡筆畫。 「line」（線條、路線）這個字讓小組多了一個可以討論的例子。 花音把「pull」（拉動）寫在板上，卻沒有把它變成考試。 他們在頁面末尾的短文練習裡找到「road」（道路）。

Everyone agreed that a hamster should not carry a human job. They gathered the papers and put the shared things back in their places. Tomo could feel that the room had changed even though the desks had not moved. Hana pointed to the page where the group had written its first worry. The words were still there, but they no longer told the whole story. Now there was also an action, a result, and a reason to look forward to the next meeting. Hana drew a small picture of shoe in the margin of the class story page. Hana chose side as a word to explain kindly to a new learner. A small study card showed station, which Tomo copied beside today's notes. Hana drew a small picture of table in the margin of the class story page. In the English practice book, water appeared on the next line.

大家同意，倉鼠不該承擔人類的工作。 他們整理紙張，把共用物品放回原處。雖然桌子沒有移動，拓真卻覺得房間已經不同。花音指出小組寫下第一個擔憂的那一頁。那些字還在，卻已無法概括整件事。現在多了行動、結果，以及期待下次見面的理由。 花音在班級故事頁的空白處畫了「shoe」（鞋子）的圖。 花音選了「side」（側邊），準備耐心向初學者說明。 一張小小練習卡上寫著「station」（車站），拓真把它抄在今日筆記旁。 花音在班級故事頁的空白處畫了「table」（桌子）的圖。 英文練習本的下一行出現「water」（水）。

Near the end of the day, Tomo stayed beside Hana for one more minute. They remembered how strange the morning had felt. A problem that once seemed to fill the whole room had become several smaller questions. Each question could be handled in turn. They did not know what the following day would bring, and that was all right. What they had learned was not a magic answer but a habit: notice a change, ask with care, and let people choose for themselves. Tomo closed the class duty chart and left with a lighter heart.

一天快結束時，拓真在花音身邊多待了一分鐘。他們想起早上的奇怪感受。原本像占滿整間房間的難題，現在分成幾個較小的問題，可以逐一處理。他們不知道明天會帶來什麼，但這沒有關係。今天學到的不是神奇答案，而是一種習慣：留意變化、仔細發問，也讓別人自己選擇。拓真闔上班務表，帶著較輕鬆的心情離開。
