The Cleaning Chart

今天起，倉鼠當班長 · 第 8 / 25 章 · 1000以下 · 英中對照 · 校訂中

Some students claimed that caring for the cage counted as cleaning the whole room. The day began with a small question and soon became more interesting than anyone expected. Tomo could hear other students talking, but did not hurry to join their guesses. Hana pointed out a detail that had been easy to miss. They stood together in the classroom, where the same familiar objects suddenly seemed to tell a different story. Tomo kept the class duty chart close, not as a perfect answer, but as a place to begin.

有些同學說照顧籠子就算打掃整間教室。 這一天從一個小疑問開始，後來卻比大家預想的更有趣。拓真聽到其他同學交談，沒有急著加入猜測。花音指出了一個容易忽略的細節。他們站在教室裡，熟悉的東西忽然像在說另一段故事。拓真把班務表帶在身邊，那不是完美答案，而是一個開始。

For a minute, the group spoke all at once. Tomo raised a hand and suggested taking turns. Hana repeated the question in simpler words, so that even a quiet student could follow the talk. Nobody needed to win an argument. They needed to learn which part of the problem could be handled now and which part needed more help. The unusual class election remained difficult, but it no longer felt impossible. Tomo drew a tiny sign beside baseball so it would be easier to remember. Hana chose beef as a word to explain kindly to a new learner. A small study card showed behind, which Tomo copied beside today's notes. Hana asked how to use break in a sentence about their day. In the English practice book, different appeared on the next line.

有一分鐘，大家幾乎同時開口。拓真舉起手，提議輪流說話。花音用更簡單的詞重述問題，讓安靜的學生也能跟上。沒有人需要爭贏；重要的是分清哪些事現在能做、哪些事需要更多協助。特別的班級選舉仍然困難，卻不再讓人覺得無法處理。 拓真在「baseball」（棒球）旁畫上小記號，幫助記憶。 花音選了「beef」（牛肉），準備耐心向初學者說明。 一張小小練習卡上寫著「behind」（在後面），拓真把它抄在今日筆記旁。 花音問起，如何用「break」（斷掉；破裂）寫一句與今天有關的話。 英文練習本的下一行出現「different」（死亡）。

Hana made a chart separating classroom duties from adult pet care. Once everyone understood the choice, the next step was smaller than it had seemed. Tomo spoke first and waited for an answer. Hana watched the other person's face instead of trying to hurry the talk. There was room to say no, to ask for an explanation, or to think for another minute. The group used the class duty chart as a guide, not as an order that could never be changed. Tomo read dress aloud, then checked the meaning with Hana. Another card carried easy and a simple drawing by a classmate. The word fly gave the group a new example to discuss. Hana put ghost on the board without turning it into a test. They found girl in a short reading exercise near the end of the page.

花音畫出表格，把教室職務和由大人負責的寵物照顧分開。 當所有人都了解選擇後，下一步其實沒有想像中困難。拓真先開口，然後等待回答。花音注意對方的表情，沒有催促。大家可以說不、要求解釋，或多想一分鐘。小組把班務表當作指引，而不是絕不能改變的命令。 拓真朗讀「dress」（裙子；洋裝），再和花音確認意思。 另一張圖卡上有「easy」（容易的），旁邊是同學的簡筆畫。 「fly」（飛）這個字讓小組多了一個可以討論的例子。 花音把「ghost」（幽靈）寫在板上，卻沒有把它變成考試。 他們在頁面末尾的短文練習裡找到「girl」（女孩）。

The first idea sounded simple, and that was useful. Still, Tomo wanted to know whether it respected everyone's choice. Hana suggested changing only one part rather than throwing the whole plan away. They drew two small boxes on a page: what we can do and what we must ask about. The list made the unusual class election easier to discuss without turning a friend into an enemy. Tomo drew a tiny sign beside give so it would be easier to remember. Hana chose group as a word to explain kindly to a new learner. A small study card showed heart, which Tomo copied beside today's notes. Hana asked how to use nice in a sentence about their day. In the English practice book, officer appeared on the next line.

第一個想法聽起來很簡單，這是好事。但拓真還想確認是否尊重每個人的選擇。花音建議只修改其中一部分，不必推翻整個計畫。他們在紙上畫兩個方框：我們能做的事，以及必須先詢問的事。有了這份清單，討論特別的班級選舉便容易多了，也不會把朋友當成敵人。 拓真在「give」（給予）旁畫上小記號，幫助記憶。 花音選了「group」（團體），準備耐心向初學者說明。 一張小小練習卡上寫著「heart」（心），拓真把它抄在今日筆記旁。 花音問起，如何用「nice」（好的）寫一句與今天有關的話。 英文練習本的下一行出現「officer」（管理員）。

Nobody could use Mochi as an excuse to avoid their work. Before calling the task finished, Tomo went back over the facts. One detail had changed, and another still needed an answer. Hana wrote both down rather than hiding the uncertain part. They could be pleased with progress without pretending that every worry had disappeared. By comparing the new result with the first note, they could see what had actually improved. The Mochi’s safe home still mattered, but now the group knew one more way to protect it. Tomo read outside aloud, then checked the meaning with Hana. Another card carried park and a simple drawing by a classmate. The word taste gave the group a new example to discuss. Hana put walk on the board without turning it into a test. They found why in a short reading exercise near the end of the page.

沒人能再拿麻糬當藉口逃避工作。 在宣布完成前，拓真重新檢查事實。有一個細節改變了，另一個仍有待解答。花音把兩者都寫下，沒有隱藏不確定之處。大家可以為進展高興，卻不必假裝所有擔憂都消失。比對新結果與第一張筆記後，他們看出真正改善的部分。麻糬的安全小窩依然重要，現在小組又多學會一種守護它的方法。 拓真朗讀「outside」（外面），再和花音確認意思。 另一張圖卡上有「park」（公園），旁邊是同學的簡筆畫。 「taste」（品嚐）這個字讓小組多了一個可以討論的例子。 花音把「walk」（步行）寫在板上，卻沒有把它變成考試。 他們在頁面末尾的短文練習裡找到「why」（為什麼）。

When the last bell sounded, Tomo saw that Hana was waiting by the familiar door. The two had not said everything perfectly. They had even disagreed about a detail, yet the disagreement had made their plan clearer. The moment did not need a large speech. A small smile and one honest thank-you were enough. At home, Tomo would remember the most surprising part of the day and write it on the next page. Tomorrow the unusual class election might look different again. This time they would know how to begin.

最後一聲鈴響時，拓真看見花音在熟悉的門旁等待。兩人不是每句話都說得完美，甚至為某個細節意見不同，但那場分歧讓計畫更清楚。這一刻不需要長篇大論，一個微笑和真誠的道謝就夠了。回家後，拓真會把今天最驚訝的事寫在下一頁。明天特別的班級選舉也許又有不同面貌，但這次他們知道如何開始。
