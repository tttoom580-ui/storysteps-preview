水壺投票

The Water Bottle Vote

今天起，倉鼠當班長 · 第 4 / 25 章 · 1000以下 · 英中對照 · 校訂中

Students argued over whether the room needed a second water station. The day began with a small question and soon became more interesting than anyone expected. Tomo could hear other students talking, but did not hurry to join their guesses. Hana pointed out a detail that had been easy to miss. They stood together in the classroom, where the same familiar objects suddenly seemed to tell a different story. Tomo kept the class duty chart close, not as a perfect answer, but as a place to begin.

同學爭論教室是否需要第二個飲水站。 這一天從一個小疑問開始，後來卻比大家預想的更有趣。拓真聽到其他同學交談，沒有急著加入猜測。花音指出了一個容易忽略的細節。他們站在教室裡，熟悉的東西忽然像在說另一段故事。拓真把班務表帶在身邊，那不是完美答案，而是一個開始。

The first idea sounded simple, and that was useful. Still, Tomo wanted to know whether it respected everyone's choice. Hana suggested changing only one part rather than throwing the whole plan away. They drew two small boxes on a page: what we can do and what we must ask about. The list made the unusual class election easier to discuss without turning a friend into an enemy. Another card carried blow and a simple drawing by a classmate. The word boy gave the group a new example to discuss. Hana put butterfly on the board without turning it into a test. They found cheap in a short reading exercise near the end of the page. Tomo drew a tiny sign beside cloud so it would be easier to remember.

第一個想法聽起來很簡單，這是好事。但拓真還想確認是否尊重每個人的選擇。花音建議只修改其中一部分，不必推翻整個計畫。他們在紙上畫兩個方框：我們能做的事，以及必須先詢問的事。有了這份清單，討論特別的班級選舉便容易多了，也不會把朋友當成敵人。 另一張圖卡上有「blow」（吹），旁邊是同學的簡筆畫。 「boy」（男孩）這個字讓小組多了一個可以討論的例子。 花音把「butterfly」（蝴蝶）寫在板上，卻沒有把它變成考試。 他們在頁面末尾的短文練習裡找到「cheap」（便宜的）。 拓真在「cloud」（雲）旁畫上小記號，幫助記憶。

Hana gathered reasons from both sides instead of asking Mochi to choose. The plan became real only when someone took the first small action. Tomo checked the time, Hana checked the shared materials, and Ms. Lin confirmed which steps needed adult approval. The group left a clear record so that a person arriving later could understand the decision. They also agreed on what to do if something went wrong. It was not exciting in the way stories often promised, but it let everyone move forward with confidence. Hana chose drop as a word to explain kindly to a new learner. A small study card showed factory, which Tomo copied beside today's notes. Hana asked how to use healthy in a sentence about their day. In the English practice book, off appeared on the next line. Tomo read service aloud, then checked the meaning with Hana.

花音蒐集雙方理由，沒有要麻糬替大家決定。 有人做出第一個小小行動時，計畫才真正開始。拓真確認時間，花音檢查共用材料，林老師確認哪些步驟需要大人同意。小組留下清楚紀錄，讓稍後到的人也能理解決定。他們還約好若事情出錯該怎麼辦。這不像故事裡常見的刺激冒險，卻讓每個人都更有信心往前走。 花音選了「drop」（掉落），準備耐心向初學者說明。 一張小小練習卡上寫著「factory」（工廠），拓真把它抄在今日筆記旁。 花音問起，如何用「healthy」（健康的）寫一句與今天有關的話。 英文練習本的下一行出現「off」（關掉；關閉）。 拓真朗讀「service」（服務（社團）），再和花音確認意思。

For a minute, the group spoke all at once. Tomo raised a hand and suggested taking turns. Hana repeated the question in simpler words, so that even a quiet student could follow the talk. Nobody needed to win an argument. They needed to learn which part of the problem could be handled now and which part needed more help. The unusual class election remained difficult, but it no longer felt impossible. Another card carried shake and a simple drawing by a classmate. The word she gave the group a new example to discuss. Hana put sir on the board without turning it into a test. They found square in a short reading exercise near the end of the page. Tomo drew a tiny sign beside story so it would be easier to remember.

有一分鐘，大家幾乎同時開口。拓真舉起手，提議輪流說話。花音用更簡單的詞重述問題，讓安靜的學生也能跟上。沒有人需要爭贏；重要的是分清哪些事現在能做、哪些事需要更多協助。特別的班級選舉仍然困難，卻不再讓人覺得無法處理。 另一張圖卡上有「shake」（搖晃；顫抖），旁邊是同學的簡筆畫。 「she」（她；她的）這個字讓小組多了一個可以討論的例子。 花音把「sir」（先生）寫在板上，卻沒有把它變成考試。 他們在頁面末尾的短文練習裡找到「square」（方形的）。 拓真在「story」（故事）旁畫上小記號，幫助記憶。

The class voted for a safe place away from books and electrical cords. They gathered the papers and put the shared things back in their places. Tomo could feel that the room had changed even though the desks had not moved. Hana pointed to the page where the group had written its first worry. The words were still there, but they no longer told the whole story. Now there was also an action, a result, and a reason to look forward to the next meeting. Hana chose tall as a word to explain kindly to a new learner. A small study card showed tomato, which Tomo copied beside today's notes. Hana asked how to use town in a sentence about their day. In the English practice book, without appeared on the next line. Tomo read worker aloud, then checked the meaning with Hana.

全班投票選出遠離書本與電線的安全位置。 他們整理紙張，把共用物品放回原處。雖然桌子沒有移動，拓真卻覺得房間已經不同。花音指出小組寫下第一個擔憂的那一頁。那些字還在，卻已無法概括整件事。現在多了行動、結果，以及期待下次見面的理由。 花音選了「tall」（高的），準備耐心向初學者說明。 一張小小練習卡上寫著「tomato」（番茄），拓真把它抄在今日筆記旁。 花音問起，如何用「town」（小鎮）寫一句與今天有關的話。 英文練習本的下一行出現「without」（沒有）。 拓真朗讀「worker」（工人），再和花音確認意思。

When the last bell sounded, Tomo saw that Hana was waiting by the familiar door. The two had not said everything perfectly. They had even disagreed about a detail, yet the disagreement had made their plan clearer. The moment did not need a large speech. A small smile and one honest thank-you were enough. At home, Tomo would remember the most surprising part of the day and write it on the next page. Tomorrow the unusual class election might look different again. This time they would know how to begin.

最後一聲鈴響時，拓真看見花音在熟悉的門旁等待。兩人不是每句話都說得完美，甚至為某個細節意見不同，但那場分歧讓計畫更清楚。這一刻不需要長篇大論，一個微笑和真誠的道謝就夠了。回家後，拓真會把今天最驚訝的事寫在下一頁。明天特別的班級選舉也許又有不同面貌，但這次他們知道如何開始。
