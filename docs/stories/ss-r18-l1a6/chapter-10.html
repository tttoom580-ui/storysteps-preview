麻糬不是玩具

Mochi Is Not a Mascot Toy

今天起，倉鼠當班長 · 第 10 / 25 章 · 1000以下 · 英中對照 · 校訂中

A club asked to borrow Mochi for a noisy school show. The light through the window looked ordinary, but the news did not. Tomo stopped for a moment, feeling both curious and unsure. Hana did not laugh at the question, even when it sounded strange. They knew from earlier mistakes that moving too fast could make a simple problem harder. The classroom offered enough space for a calm talk. On the first page of the class duty chart, Tomo wrote what was known and left a blank space for what remained uncertain.

一個社團想借麻糬參加吵鬧的校園演出。 窗外的光線和平常一樣，消息卻一點也不平常。拓真停了一會兒，既好奇又不確定。就算問題聽來奇怪，花音也沒有取笑。他們從先前的錯誤學到，行動太快反而可能讓小問題變複雜。教室有足夠空間讓大家平靜討論。拓真在班務表第一頁寫下已知的事，也留下空白給尚未確定的部分。

Tomo was afraid that saying the wrong thing would make the day worse. Hana answered that staying silent could also leave a misunderstanding in place. They practiced one honest sentence and checked that it did not blame anyone unfairly. This took longer than making a joke, but the result was clearer. Near the table, Ms. Lin nodded and let the young people try their own words before offering advice. A small study card showed apple, which Tomo copied beside today's notes. Hana asked how to use between in a sentence about their day. In the English practice book, blind appeared on the next line. Tomo read brother aloud, then checked the meaning with Hana. Another card carried center and a simple drawing by a classmate.

拓真害怕說錯話會讓事情變糟。花音回答，保持沉默有時也會讓誤會繼續存在。他們練習說出一句誠實的話，再確認沒有不公平地責怪任何人。這比開玩笑花時間，結果卻更清楚。桌旁的林老師點頭，先讓年輕人用自己的話嘗試，再提供建議。 一張小小練習卡上寫著「apple」（蘋果），拓真把它抄在今日筆記旁。 花音問起，如何用「between」（在……之間）寫一句與今天有關的話。 英文練習本的下一行出現「blind」（失明的；看不見的）。 拓真朗讀「brother」（弟弟），再和花音確認意思。 另一張圖卡上有「center」（中央），旁邊是同學的簡筆畫。

Hana said no after speaking with the teacher and the pet's family. There was a quiet kind of courage in trying again. Tomo noticed that the last attempt had taught them something, even without a perfect result. Hana offered practical help and asked which part would be most useful. They worked together for a short time and then stopped to check whether they were still following the rule. Ms. Lin praised the careful check more than the speed of the work. The word half gave the group a new example to discuss. Hana put hit on the board without turning it into a test. They found ice in a short reading exercise near the end of the page. Tomo drew a tiny sign beside kick so it would be easier to remember. Hana chose love as a word to explain kindly to a new learner.

花音與老師、飼主家庭商量後拒絕。 願意再試一次，是一種安靜的勇氣。拓真發現，就算上次結果不完美，大家仍學到了一些事。花音提供實際協助，先問哪一部分最有用。他們一起工作一小段時間，再停下來確認是否仍遵守規則。林老師稱讚的不是速度，而是這份細心。 「half」（一半）這個字讓小組多了一個可以討論的例子。 花音把「hit」（擊中）寫在板上，卻沒有把它變成考試。 他們在頁面末尾的短文練習裡找到「ice」（冰）。 拓真在「kick」（踢）旁畫上小記號，幫助記憶。 花音選了「love」（喜愛），準備耐心向初學者說明。

They began by asking who might be affected by the next step. Hana thought about the people who had not spoken yet, and Tomo thought about the rule they had already promised to follow. One person offered a quick answer, but the group asked for a reason. A good question was not an attack. It was a way to make the answer safer and kinder. Ms. Lin reminded them that everyone could ask for a pause. A small study card showed net, which Tomo copied beside today's notes. Hana asked how to use online in a sentence about their day. Hana drew a small picture of pencil in the margin of the class story page. Tomo read right aloud, then checked the meaning with Hana. Another card carried simple and a simple drawing by a classmate.

大家先問，下一步會影響到誰。花音想到還沒發言的人，拓真則想到自己早已承諾遵守的規則。有人很快提出答案，其他人卻請他說明理由。好問題不是攻擊，而是讓答案更安全、更體貼的方法。林老師提醒大家，每個人都可以要求暫停。 一張小小練習卡上寫著「net」（細線網），拓真把它抄在今日筆記旁。 花音問起，如何用「online」（線上的）寫一句與今天有關的話。 花音在班級故事頁的空白處畫了「pencil」（鉛筆）的圖。 拓真朗讀「right」（正確的），再和花音確認意思。 另一張圖卡上有「simple」（簡單的），旁邊是同學的簡筆畫。

The club drew a cartoon hamster and thanked them for the idea. The answer made someone smile, but Tomo still asked one final question: was everyone comfortable with what came next? Hana listened to the replies, including the quiet ones. They changed a small detail when one person needed something different. Nothing about the change made the story less meaningful. It helped the Mochi’s safe home belong to more than one person, which was why the work had begun. The word spend gave the group a new example to discuss. Hana put still on the board without turning it into a test. They found teacher in a short reading exercise near the end of the page. Tomo drew a tiny sign beside voice so it would be easier to remember. Hana chose way as a word to explain kindly to a new learner.

社團改畫卡通倉鼠，還向大家道謝。 答案讓某個人笑了，但拓真仍問最後一個問題：大家都能接受下一步嗎？花音聽取回答，包括輕聲說出的意見。有人有不同需求，他們就修改一個小細節。這種修改沒有讓故事失去意義，反而讓麻糬的安全小窩不只屬於一個人；這也正是大家當初開始的原因。 「spend」（花費（時間））這個字讓小組多了一個可以討論的例子。 花音把「still」（仍然）寫在板上，卻沒有把它變成考試。 他們在頁面末尾的短文練習裡找到「teacher」（老師）。 拓真在「voice」（聲音）旁畫上小記號，幫助記憶。 花音選了「way」（方法），準備耐心向初學者說明。

Outside, the air was cooler than it had been at lunch. Tomo looked back once and saw the classroom in the evening light. It seemed like an ordinary place, but there was more to it now: a remembered question, a mistake corrected, and a choice made together. Hana waved from a few steps away. They still had their separate dreams and small worries, yet they also shared one clear memory. Whatever happened next, they could return to that memory without needing to pretend the day had been perfect.

外面的空氣比午餐時涼了些。拓真回頭望了一眼晚光中的教室。它看似平凡，現在卻多了值得記住的問題、改正的錯誤，以及共同做出的選擇。花音在幾步外揮手。他們仍有各自的夢想和小煩惱，也有一段共同的清晰回憶。無論接下來發生什麼，都不必假裝今天完美，就能想起它的價值。
