安靜的午睡

A Quiet Nap

今天起，倉鼠當班長 · 第 5 / 25 章 · 1000以下 · 英中對照 · 校訂中

Excited children kept tapping the cage while Mochi tried to sleep. At first, Tomo thought the problem could be solved before the next bell. A closer look showed that someone else's wishes mattered too. Hana listened without interrupting, while Ms. Lin watched the group from nearby. They did not have to agree on everything immediately. There was time to separate a real observation from a story someone had only imagined. Tomo took out the class duty chart and asked what should be checked first.

興奮的孩子不斷拍籠子，麻糬想睡也睡不成。 起初，拓真以為下次鐘響前就能解決問題。但仔細看過後才發現，別人的願望也很重要。花音沒有打斷，靜靜傾聽，林老師則在附近看著大家。眾人不必立刻同意每件事，還有時間分清真實觀察與想像中的說法。拓真拿出班務表，問大家應先確認什麼。

They began by asking who might be affected by the next step. Hana thought about the people who had not spoken yet, and Tomo thought about the rule they had already promised to follow. One person offered a quick answer, but the group asked for a reason. A good question was not an attack. It was a way to make the answer safer and kinder. Ms. Lin reminded them that everyone could ask for a pause. The word although gave the group a new example to discuss. Hana put bean on the board without turning it into a test. They found deal in a short reading exercise near the end of the page. Tomo drew a tiny sign beside dig so it would be easier to remember. Hana chose during as a word to explain kindly to a new learner.

大家先問，下一步會影響到誰。花音想到還沒發言的人，拓真則想到自己早已承諾遵守的規則。有人很快提出答案，其他人卻請他說明理由。好問題不是攻擊，而是讓答案更安全、更體貼的方法。林老師提醒大家，每個人都可以要求暫停。 「although」（雖然）這個字讓小組多了一個可以討論的例子。 花音把「bean」（豆子）寫在板上，卻沒有把它變成考試。 他們在頁面末尾的短文練習裡找到「deal」（處理；作決定）。 拓真在「dig」（挖）旁畫上小記號，幫助記憶。 花音選了「during」（在……期間），準備耐心向初學者說明。

Tomo put up a clear sign asking people not to touch the cage. Tomo chose the part of the work that could be done carefully. Hana handled a different task and promised to report back before making any large change. A short question from Ms. Lin caught an error in their first version. Instead of hiding it, they made a neat correction on the page. That correction did not erase their effort. It showed that the group was learning how to be responsible for its own work. A small study card showed frog, which Tomo copied beside today's notes. Hana asked how to use height in a sentence about their day. In the English practice book, hen appeared on the next line. Tomo read least aloud, then checked the meaning with Hana. Another card carried long and a simple drawing by a classmate.

拓真貼上清楚的標誌，請大家不要碰籠子。 拓真選擇自己能謹慎完成的工作，花音負責另一項任務，並答應在重大更改前先回報。林老師的一個簡短問題，讓大家發現第一版中的錯誤。他們沒有掩飾，而是在頁面上整齊修正。這項修正沒有抹去先前的努力，反而證明小組正在學習對自己的工作負責。 一張小小練習卡上寫著「frog」（青蛙），拓真把它抄在今日筆記旁。 花音問起，如何用「height」（高度）寫一句與今天有關的話。 英文練習本的下一行出現「hen」（母雞）。 拓真朗讀「least」（至少），再和花音確認意思。 另一張圖卡上有「long」（長的），旁邊是同學的簡筆畫。

Tomo was afraid that saying the wrong thing would make the day worse. Hana answered that staying silent could also leave a misunderstanding in place. They practiced one honest sentence and checked that it did not blame anyone unfairly. This took longer than making a joke, but the result was clearer. Near the table, Ms. Lin nodded and let the young people try their own words before offering advice. The word minute gave the group a new example to discuss. Hana put Ms. on the board without turning it into a test. They found neck in a short reading exercise near the end of the page. Tomo drew a tiny sign beside point so it would be easier to remember. Hana chose put as a word to explain kindly to a new learner.

拓真害怕說錯話會讓事情變糟。花音回答，保持沉默有時也會讓誤會繼續存在。他們練習說出一句誠實的話，再確認沒有不公平地責怪任何人。這比開玩笑花時間，結果卻更清楚。桌旁的林老師點頭，先讓年輕人用自己的話嘗試，再提供建議。 「minute」（分鐘）這個字讓小組多了一個可以討論的例子。 花音把「Ms.」（女士）寫在板上，卻沒有把它變成考試。 他們在頁面末尾的短文練習裡找到「neck」（脖子）。 拓真在「point」（指向；重點）旁畫上小記號，幫助記憶。 花音選了「put」（放置），準備耐心向初學者說明。

The room grew quieter, and Mochi curled up peacefully. Ms. Lin asked the group to explain its choice in everyday words. Tomo tried once, paused, and then gave a shorter answer. Hana added an important detail that had been left out. The explanation became clear enough for another person to check. That mattered more than making their decision sound grand. The Mochi’s safe home could grow only if other people understood how it worked. A small study card showed race, which Tomo copied beside today's notes. Hana asked how to use safe in a sentence about their day. In the English practice book, snow appeared on the next line. Tomo read sorry aloud, then checked the meaning with Hana. Another card carried win and a simple drawing by a classmate.

教室安靜下來，麻糬安心縮成一團。 林老師請小組用平常的話解釋選擇。拓真試了一次，停頓後改用更短的回答。花音補充了一個漏掉的重要細節。解釋變得足夠清楚，讓別人也能核對。這比把決定說得偉大更重要。只有其他人理解做法，麻糬的安全小窩才有機會繼續成長。 一張小小練習卡上寫著「race」（賽跑），拓真把它抄在今日筆記旁。 花音問起，如何用「safe」（安全的）寫一句與今天有關的話。 英文練習本的下一行出現「snow」（雪）。 拓真朗讀「sorry」（抱歉），再和花音確認意思。 另一張圖卡上有「win」（贏得），旁邊是同學的簡筆畫。

The hallway grew quiet, and Tomo finally had time to think. A good friend had not supplied every answer. Instead, Hana had left space for another idea and had stayed when the first idea failed. That was harder to notice than a loud victory, but it was real. Tomo made a promise to remember the person as well as the task. The Mochi’s safe home was important; so were the people who helped shape it. With that thought, the next chapter of their ordinary and extraordinary days felt possible.

走廊逐漸安靜，拓真終於有時間思考。好朋友不是替自己提供每個答案；花音反而留下思考空間，也在第一個想法失敗時陪在身旁。這比熱鬧的勝利難以察覺，卻是真實的。拓真答應自己，不只記得任務，也要記得參與的人。麻糬的安全小窩重要，幫忙塑造它的人也同樣重要。帶著這個念頭，他們平凡又不平凡的日子可以繼續寫下去。
