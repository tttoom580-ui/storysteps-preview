A pencil disappeared and someone joked that the hamster had stolen it. Nobody had planned for this moment. The room was full of ordinary sounds, yet Tomo felt that something important had changed. Hana waited beside the door and let the first surprise settle. It was tempting to find an easy answer before anyone knew the whole story. Instead, they looked carefully at what was truly in front of them. Tomo opened the class duty chart and made a small note about the day.

一支鉛筆不見了，有人開玩笑說是倉鼠偷走。 沒有人預料到這一刻。教室充滿平常的聲音，拓真卻感覺有件重要的事改變了。花音站在門旁，讓最初的驚訝慢慢沉澱。大家很容易在弄清事情之前就急著下結論，但他們決定先看清眼前真正發生的事。拓真翻開班務表，記下今天的小小發現。

For a minute, the group spoke all at once. Tomo raised a hand and suggested taking turns. Hana repeated the question in simpler words, so that even a quiet student could follow the talk. Nobody needed to win an argument. They needed to learn which part of the problem could be handled now and which part needed more help. The unusual class election remained difficult, but it no longer felt impossible. They found as in a short reading exercise near the end of the page. Tomo drew a tiny sign beside bedroom so it would be easier to remember. Hana chose believe as a word to explain kindly to a new learner. Hana drew a small picture of clock in the margin of the class story page. Hana asked how to use close in a sentence about their day.

有一分鐘，大家幾乎同時開口。拓真舉起手，提議輪流說話。花音用更簡單的詞重述問題，讓安靜的學生也能跟上。沒有人需要爭贏；重要的是分清哪些事現在能做、哪些事需要更多協助。特別的班級選舉仍然困難，卻不再讓人覺得無法處理。 他們在頁面末尾的短文練習裡找到「as」（當……的時候）。 拓真在「bedroom」（臥室）旁畫上小記號，幫助記憶。 花音選了「believe」（相信），準備耐心向初學者說明。 花音在班級故事頁的空白處畫了「clock」（時鐘）的圖。 花音問起，如何用「close」（關閉）寫一句與今天有關的話。

Tomo checked beneath the desks instead of opening the cage. Once everyone understood the choice, the next step was smaller than it had seemed. Tomo spoke first and waited for an answer. Hana watched the other person's face instead of trying to hurry the talk. There was room to say no, to ask for an explanation, or to think for another minute. The group used the class duty chart as a guide, not as an order that could never be changed. In the English practice book, day appeared on the next line. Tomo read dream aloud, then checked the meaning with Hana. Another card carried error and a simple drawing by a classmate. The word hear gave the group a new example to discuss. Hana put just on the board without turning it into a test.

拓真檢查桌子底下，沒有擅自打開籠子。 當所有人都了解選擇後，下一步其實沒有想像中困難。拓真先開口，然後等待回答。花音注意對方的表情，沒有催促。大家可以說不、要求解釋，或多想一分鐘。小組把班務表當作指引，而不是絕不能改變的命令。 英文練習本的下一行出現「day」（一天）。 拓真朗讀「dream」（夢），再和花音確認意思。 另一張圖卡上有「error」（錯誤），旁邊是同學的簡筆畫。 「hear」（聽見）這個字讓小組多了一個可以討論的例子。 花音把「just」（只是、剛好）寫在板上，卻沒有把它變成考試。

The first idea sounded simple, and that was useful. Still, Tomo wanted to know whether it respected everyone's choice. Hana suggested changing only one part rather than throwing the whole plan away. They drew two small boxes on a page: what we can do and what we must ask about. The list made the unusual class election easier to discuss without turning a friend into an enemy. They found laugh in a short reading exercise near the end of the page. Tomo drew a tiny sign beside nature so it would be easier to remember. Hana chose noise as a word to explain kindly to a new learner. A small study card showed proud, which Tomo copied beside today's notes. Hana asked how to use rainbow in a sentence about their day.

第一個想法聽起來很簡單，這是好事。但拓真還想確認是否尊重每個人的選擇。花音建議只修改其中一部分，不必推翻整個計畫。他們在紙上畫兩個方框：我們能做的事，以及必須先詢問的事。有了這份清單，討論特別的班級選舉便容易多了，也不會把朋友當成敵人。 他們在頁面末尾的短文練習裡找到「laugh」（笑）。 拓真在「nature」（自然）旁畫上小記號，幫助記憶。 花音選了「noise」（噪音），準備耐心向初學者說明。 一張小小練習卡上寫著「proud」（驕傲的），拓真把它抄在今日筆記旁。 花音問起，如何用「rainbow」（彩虹）寫一句與今天有關的話。

The pencil was behind a shoe, and Mochi was innocent. Before calling the task finished, Tomo went back over the facts. One detail had changed, and another still needed an answer. Hana wrote both down rather than hiding the uncertain part. They could be pleased with progress without pretending that every worry had disappeared. By comparing the new result with the first note, they could see what had actually improved. The Mochi’s safe home still mattered, but now the group knew one more way to protect it. In the English practice book, rich appeared on the next line. Tomo read sky aloud, then checked the meaning with Hana. Another card carried time and a simple drawing by a classmate. The word whether gave the group a new example to discuss. Hana put yard on the board without turning it into a test.

鉛筆卡在鞋子後面，麻糬完全無辜。 在宣布完成前，拓真重新檢查事實。有一個細節改變了，另一個仍有待解答。花音把兩者都寫下，沒有隱藏不確定之處。大家可以為進展高興，卻不必假裝所有擔憂都消失。比對新結果與第一張筆記後，他們看出真正改善的部分。麻糬的安全小窩依然重要，現在小組又多學會一種守護它的方法。 英文練習本的下一行出現「rich」（富有的）。 拓真朗讀「sky」（天空），再和花音確認意思。 另一張圖卡上有「time」（時間），旁邊是同學的簡筆畫。 「whether」（是否）這個字讓小組多了一個可以討論的例子。 花音把「yard」（院子）寫在板上，卻沒有把它變成考試。

Near the end of the day, Tomo stayed beside Hana for one more minute. They remembered how strange the morning had felt. A problem that once seemed to fill the whole room had become several smaller questions. Each question could be handled in turn. They did not know what the following day would bring, and that was all right. What they had learned was not a magic answer but a habit: notice a change, ask with care, and let people choose for themselves. Tomo closed the class duty chart and left with a lighter heart.

一天快結束時，拓真在花音身邊多待了一分鐘。他們想起早上的奇怪感受。原本像占滿整間房間的難題，現在分成幾個較小的問題，可以逐一處理。他們不知道明天會帶來什麼，但這沒有關係。今天學到的不是神奇答案，而是一種習慣：留意變化、仔細發問，也讓別人自己選擇。拓真闔上班務表，帶著較輕鬆的心情離開。
