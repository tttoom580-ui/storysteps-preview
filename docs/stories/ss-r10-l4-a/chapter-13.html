五塔會議

The Five-Tower Conference

The first five-tower conference took place in a school garden where spring flowers had begun to blossom. The city appointed Ning to explain the tests, but she asked representatives from every district to speak first. A children's chorus rehearsed nearby, and the rhythm of their song reminded her of the old evacuation notes. Some residents called the shield's history a legend; others blamed a curse for every malfunction. Ning said neither story could replace measurements. She sketched five connected circles on a board and summarized the evidence: independent instruments had observed pulses during coordinated movement, while the underground vessel could distribute that power only within tested limits. The proposal was difficult, but it had a foundation in repeated observations.

第一次五塔會議在學校花園舉行，春天的花剛開始開放。城市任命沈寧說明測試，但她請各區代表先發言。附近孩子們正練習合唱，歌聲節奏使她想起舊疏散紀錄。有些居民把屏障歷史當成傳說，也有人把每次故障歸咎於詛咒。沈寧說，兩種故事都不能取代測量。她在板上畫五個相連的圓圈，摘要說明：獨立儀器在同步移動時量到脈衝，而地下容器只有在測試過的限制內才能分配力量。提案很難，但基礎是重複觀察。

A local economics teacher asked what the new network would cost. Ning admitted that the estimate covered equipment but not all the labor required for regular inspection. The teacher said a beautiful technical design that ignored workers would leave the next generation with the same problem. Salim welcomed the criticism and proposed paid training for residents near each tower. Qiao asked that fighters be trained in rescue as well as competition. Some delegates were miserable about losing the old ranking privileges, while others remembered friends who had been excluded. Ning made the discussion less personal by presenting choices with prices, risks, and responsibilities beside each one. A universal shield could not be built on invisible work.

一位地方經濟學教師問新網路要花多少錢。沈寧承認估算包含設備，卻尚未算入定期巡檢的全部勞動。教師說，忽略工人的漂亮技術設計，只會把同樣問題留給下一代。薩林接受批評，提議在每座塔附近提供有薪居民訓練。羅喬要求武者也學救援，而不只練競賽。有些代表因失去舊排名特權而痛苦，其他人則想起曾被排除的朋友。沈寧把每個選項的成本、風險和責任並排列出，讓討論少一點人身攻擊。普遍適用的屏障不能建立在看不見的勞動上。

The conference paused when a student fainted in the warm hall. A nurse brought oxygen and moved the crowd back, while the teacher opened a side door. The student recovered quickly. Ning made a note that even a leisurely meeting required an emergency plan. One delegate had suggested holding the next demonstration on a narrow bridge above the river because the pictures would be memorable. After the medical interruption, the proposal no longer seemed wise. Ning recommended a wide square with accessible exits and sufficient shade. The audience approved the change without a vote. She was relieved: the group was beginning to apply safety reasoning to ordinary choices, not just to the shield itself.

會議期間，一名學生在溫暖的大廳昏倒。護理師帶來氧氣，請人群退開，教師則打開側門。學生很快恢復。沈寧記下，即使悠閒的會議也需要緊急計畫。有代表先前提議在河上狹橋做下次示範，認為照片會令人難忘。醫療事件發生後，這提案不再明智。沈寧推薦有寬敞出口和足夠遮蔭的廣場。聽眾不經投票就同意更改。她鬆了口氣：大家開始把安全思考用於日常選擇，而不只用在屏障上。

Han Yu presented a memorable letter from the original shield builders. Its final paragraph said their work would be fulfilled only when no district had to face a storm alone. Ning cautioned that a sentence about hope was not a technical specification. Still, the letter explained why builders from distant villages had shared materials rather than fought over rankings. The teacher added that shared costs could reduce overall expense if every district maintained its own valves. Lei asked about responsibility if the network failed. Ning proposed a public log with clear owners for each connection and an independent review after every incident. She had learned that collective effort required individual accountability, or a shared system could become a place where everyone blamed everyone else.

韓瑜展示最早屏障建造者的一封難忘信件。最後一段寫著，他們的工作只有在沒有街區必須獨自面對風暴時才算履行。沈寧提醒，希望的句子不是技術規格，但信件解釋了為何不同村落的建造者願意共享材料，而不是爭排名。教師補充，若各區維護自己的閥門，共同負擔可能降低總成本。雷川問，網路失效時誰負責。沈寧提議為每個接頭建立明確負責人和公開日誌，並在每次事件後獨立檢討。她知道，集體努力也需要個別問責，否則共享系統可能變成人人互相推責的地方。

At sunset, delegates signed a preliminary agreement for low-risk tests. They did not authorize forced participation, and the document required medical observers at every location. Ning accepted this limited arrangement with relief, but a final detail bothered her. The center tower still operated under a private contract, while the other four were public. If the center withheld its timing signal, the network could not synchronize. The sponsor's representative said the contract could not be discussed in public. Qiao asked to see the clause, and the representative refused. The conference closed with the garden chorus singing softly. Ning listened, remembering how five different voices could remain distinct while sharing a rhythm. The next struggle would concern who controlled that rhythm.

日落時，各區代表簽署低風險測試的初步協議。文件不允許強制參與，並要求每處都有醫護觀察員。沈寧接受這項有限安排，卻仍在意最後一個細節。中央塔仍由私人合約管理，其他四塔是公有。中央若拒發時序訊號，網路就無法同步。贊助商代表說合約不可公開討論。羅喬要求查看條款，遭到拒絕。花園裡孩子們輕聲合唱，會議就此結束。沈寧聽著歌，想起五種不同聲音仍可在保持自身特點時共享節奏。下一場爭論將決定誰有權控制這個節奏。

A volunteer had scarcely finished drawing a red stripe beside the safe entrance when the wind changed. The new marking helped late arrivals find the correct gate without pushing through the crowded hall. Ning asked the school to keep the entrance free of chairs and boxes. These small changes did not prove the five-tower theory, but they showed that the conference had already made one public space safer.

志工才剛在安全入口旁畫好紅色條紋，風向就變了。新標記讓晚到者不必穿過擁擠大廳，也能找到正確門口。沈寧請學校不要在入口放椅子或箱子。這些小改變不能證明五塔理論，卻顯示會議已使一個公共空間更安全。
