鉛筆寫的時刻表

A Timetable Written in Pencil

都市高武：我替弱者校準屏障 · 第 2 / 25 章 · 3000–4000 · 英中對照

用互動版查詞、朗讀與收藏

At dawn, Ning found a new timetable pinned outside the arena. It moved the workers' shifts without their consent and assigned a dangerous night inspection to people who had never received training. A prominent sponsor had added his autograph to the notice, as though a famous signature were enough to make it fair. The convenience was obvious for the wealthy districts: their shield tests would proceed first. Near the gate, an immigrant cleaner named Salim was removing the old sign. He had carved the original inspection times into a wooden board for workers who could not read the small print. Ning asked him to keep the board. He replied that keeping evidence should not be a privilege reserved for officials.

清晨，沈寧發現競技場外貼了新時刻表。它沒有徵得同意就更改工人的輪班，還把危險的夜間巡檢交給沒受訓的人。一位知名贊助商在公告上留下親筆簽名，彷彿名人的署名就能證明安排公平。富裕街區因此能先做屏障測試，對他們確實方便。大門旁，一名叫薩林的移民清潔員正拆下舊公告。他曾為看不清小字的工人，把原本的巡檢時間刻在木板上。沈寧請他留下木板。他說，保存證據不該是官員才有的特權。

A child was playing mischief with a stray dog's collar when a loud signal sounded. The dog raised one paw and hid beneath a bench. Ning saw an elastic strap connecting the collar to a trial sensor. The strap could have snapped toward the child's face. Worse, a sealed crate beside the bench carried a warning about explosive pressure. The officials had disguised risky equipment as harmless festival decorations. Qiao helped move the child away, while Salim reported the unsafe crate. Ning called this cruelty through carelessness, not a small accident. She insisted on injury prevention before any attempt to collect new shield data. The sponsor's assistant answered that safety measures would ruin the public show.

一個孩子正在捉弄流浪狗的項圈，突然響起一聲尖銳訊號。狗抬起一隻爪子，躲到長椅下。沈寧看見項圈上有條彈性帶，連著試驗感測器。彈性帶如果斷裂，可能彈到孩子臉上。更糟的是，長椅旁的密封箱寫著爆裂壓力警告。官員把危險設備偽裝成無害的節慶裝飾。羅喬把孩子帶遠，薩林則通報危險箱。沈寧認為這是粗心造成的殘酷對待，不是小意外。她堅持先預防傷害，再收集新的屏障數據。贊助商助理卻說，安全措施會毀掉表演。

In the square, the first public test began with twelve volunteers. Ning asked for feedback after every round, including from people who chose not to participate. One elderly woman remarked that the floor shook more under a slow group step than under Qiao's strongest kick. That observation helped Ning revise the initial plan. The volunteers would walk in three small groups with a clear space between them, while the technicians measured both the ground and the shield. When the groups changed their shift of movement together, the barrier brightened for two seconds. No weapon was required. Salim wrote the result where everyone could see it, beside the original timetable. People began to ask whether the city might prosper through cooperation instead of competition.

廣場上的第一次公開測試由十二名志工開始。沈寧每回合後都徵求回饋，連選擇不參與的人也可以發言。一位老婦人指出，大家慢慢一起踏步時，地板的震動比羅喬最強的一腳還明顯。這項觀察幫沈寧修正最初計畫。志工分成三小組行走，組間保持空隙，技師同時測量地面與屏障。當各組一起改變移動節奏時，屏障亮了兩秒。完全不需要武器。薩林把結果寫在原時刻表旁，讓所有人看見。居民開始問：城市是否能靠合作，而不是競爭，繼續繁榮？

The sponsor's assistant offered to fund the next round if Ning removed the names of the cleaners from the report. He claimed that the prominent fighter's name would attract more attention. Ning refused. Without the workers' records, there would be no honest account of the test. Qiao returned the funding letter unsigned, surprising both the assistant and herself. She had been trained to win and had never expected to defend the right of a cleaner to be named. Later the technician who managed the arena sent Ning a private message: the new timetable was not an isolated change. Similar schedules had appeared at six other towers. Someone was moving labor away from the districts with the oldest equipment just before the storm season.

贊助商助理表示，只要沈寧從報告刪掉清潔工的名字，就願意資助下一輪測試。他說知名武者的名字更有宣傳效果。沈寧拒絕了。沒有工人的紀錄，測試報告就不誠實。羅喬把資助信退回去，連簽名都沒有。這讓助理意外，也讓她自己意外。她一直受訓追求勝利，從沒想到有一天要替清潔工的署名權說話。稍後，管理競技場的技師私下傳訊給沈寧：新時刻表不是孤立事件。另六座塔也出現相同安排。有人正在風暴季前，把人力從設備最老舊的街區撤走。

Ning spent the night comparing the handwritten boards with the official schedule. She could see why the sponsor preferred the disguise of a neat calendar: no one watching a duel would notice missing inspectors in a distant district. Salim brought tea and said that he would share the names of willing witnesses, but not without asking them first. Ning agreed. Their work needed trust rather than fear. At midnight a second message arrived, this time with a photograph of a damaged tower. Under the picture someone had written, 'The first failure will be called sabotage.' Ning knew a warning might be sincere or mischievous. She marked it unverified, asked for the original photograph, and prepared to travel east at sunrise.

沈寧整夜比對手寫木板與官方時程。她明白贊助商為何偏愛整齊日曆的偽裝：觀看決鬥的人，不會注意遠方街區少了巡檢員。薩林送來茶，表示願意提供願作證者的姓名，但必須先徵求本人同意。沈寧答應了。他們需要的是信任，不是恐懼。午夜，第二則訊息到來，附著一座受損塔的照片。照片下寫著：「第一次故障將被稱為蓄意破壞。」沈寧知道警告可能真誠，也可能只是惡作劇。她標記為尚未核實，要求原始照片，並準備日出後前往東區。
