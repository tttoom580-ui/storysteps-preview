The Gallery of Small Signals

The central tower's owner refused the shared-control request, so Ning searched for a lawful alternative signal path. Han invited the team to a science gallery built in the former observatory. A lecturer there had spent years studying how groups of insects moved without a conductor. Beside an exhibit about the life of a hive, tiny sensors recorded bees returning to their home. A child asked whether one flea could make the same pattern. The lecturer said the two animals behaved differently, but both showed how movement could be measured without deciding who was strongest. Ning was careful to distinguish an interesting comparison from proof. A gallery demonstration could inspire a test; it could not replace one.

中央塔所有者拒絕共享控制，沈寧因此尋找合法的替代訊號路線。韓瑜邀大家參觀由舊天文臺改建的科學藝廊。那裡一位講師多年研究昆蟲群體如何不靠指揮者移動。蜂巢生活展品旁，細小感測器記錄蜜蜂返巢。一個孩子問，單隻跳蚤能否形成同樣模式。講師說兩種動物行為不同，但都能說明如何測量動作，而不必判定誰最強。沈寧小心區分有趣比較與真正證明。藝廊示範能啟發測試，不能取代測試。

Behind the exhibits stood an old telescope on a movable platform. The platform used a single knob to adjust its direction, and a metal container held the original timing gears. Min discovered that the observatory had once sent clock signals to the five towers. The records described only partial connections, but the equipment was real. Ning knelt beside the cabinet to examine a loose wire. When she sprinkled a little dry dust across the joint, she could see the movement caused by each pulse. The museum curator requested careful preservation of the original parts. Ning agreed to leave the old machine untouched and build an independent copy from approved materials. The simplest choice was not always the quickest one.

展品後方有座架在可移動平台上的舊望遠鏡。平臺靠一個旋鈕調方向，金屬容器裡保存原始計時齒輪。敏敏發現天文臺以前曾向五座塔傳送時鐘訊號。紀錄只提到部分連線，但設備確實存在。沈寧跪在櫃旁檢查鬆線，輕輕灑上少量乾燥粉塵，就能看到每次脈衝造成的移動。館長要求小心保存原件。沈寧同意不觸碰舊機器，而用核准材料另做一份。最簡單的選擇不一定最快。

The team invited a former arena addict to review the new public displays. He admitted that he had once watched scores so obsessively that he missed his father's birthday celebration. Now he wanted information that described people rather than ranked them. Ning showed him the five-tower graph, including its uncertainty. He suggested an intensive training session for volunteers so they could identify false alarms without depending on a single expert. Salim added that training should be respectful of people with limited time, including parents and shift workers. The lecturer agreed to teach several short classes instead of one exhausting course. Ning recorded the suggestion as promising, but asked for feedback before calling it successful.

團隊邀一位過去沉迷競技場積分的居民檢查新公共顯示器。他坦承曾看排名看得太著迷，連父親生日慶祝都錯過。如今他希望資訊描述的是人，而不是替人排序。沈寧展示五塔圖表，連不確定範圍也標明。他建議為志工辦密集訓練，讓大家不必只依靠一名專家就能辨認假警報。薩林補充，課程應尊重時間有限的人，包括父母和輪班工人。講師同意開幾堂短課，而不是一場令人疲憊的長課。沈寧記下這項有希望的提議，但要求先取得回饋才判定成功。

During the trial lesson, a volunteer pointed out that the screen made a small change look like an enormous victory. The graph had been drawn without a visible scale. Min corrected it, and the apparent improvement shrank. Everyone laughed at the embarrassment, but Ning thanked the volunteer. Satisfaction with a pretty picture was not the purpose of the work. The class then practiced comparing a live sensor with an independent meter, checking both before making a decision. The task was ordinary and sometimes slow. Yet when a false warning sounded, three people noticed the disagreement and asked for a pause before anyone ran toward the exit. The lesson had already protected a room full of visitors.

試教時，一名志工指出螢幕把小變化畫得像巨大勝利，因為圖表沒標明刻度。敏敏修正後，看似驚人的改善立即縮小。大家尷尬地笑了，沈寧卻感謝志工。對漂亮圖表感到滿意不是工作的目的。課程接著練習比對即時感測器和獨立儀表，在決定前先查兩者。工作普通而緩慢，但假警報響起時，三個人立刻注意到差異，先要求暫停，而不是衝向出口。這堂課已經保護了一屋子的訪客。

By dusk the observatory's copied clock could send a weak timing pulse to two public towers without entering the private control room. It did not provide energy; it merely gave workers a shared beat for testing. Ning reported the difference clearly because a promising result could otherwise become another myth. The museum agreed to host future classes, while Min kept the original drawings in public custody. As they packed their tools, the sky turned an unfamiliar green. The weather office called it an early edge of the main storm. Ning checked the clock and understood that their careful work now had a deadline. The question was no longer whether a shared rhythm existed, but whether enough people could use it safely before the real pressure arrived.

黃昏時，天文臺複製的時鐘能向兩座公有塔送出微弱時序脈衝，不必進私人控制室。它不提供能量，只給工人一個共同測試節拍。沈寧清楚報告差別，因為有希望的結果很容易變成另一個神話。博物館同意日後開課，敏敏把原始圖紙交由公共單位保管。他們收拾工具時，天空轉成陌生的綠色。氣象單位說這是主風暴的前緣。沈寧看了時鐘，明白謹慎工作如今有期限。問題已不只是共享節奏是否存在，而是足夠多人能否在真正壓力抵達前安全使用它。

The most useful aspect of the gallery experiment, namely the separate clock signal, was also its limitation. It told towers when to compare their movements, but it did not create energy from nothing. Ning asked the lecturer to repeat this distinction at the next training session. If volunteers understood the limit, they were less likely to risk their health for an impossible promise.

藝廊實驗最有用的面向，也就是獨立時鐘訊號，同時也是它的限制。它告訴防護塔何時比較動作，並不是憑空創造能量。沈寧請講師在下次訓練時重複說明。志工理解限制後，就較不會為不可能的承諾冒健康風險。
