沒有排名的避難所

A Shelter Without Rankings

都市高武：我替弱者校準屏障 · 第 10 / 25 章 · 3000–4000 · 英中對照

The southern shelter opened beneath a former publishing facility. Refugees from the edge of the city waited there while workers repaired their damaged houses. Ning found that admission depended on an arena rank, although the public notice promised equal acceptance. The manager said the policy had been changed as part of his promotion. Ning called the ranking offensive because it denied dignity to people who could not fight. He argued that the facility needed a way to classify visitors. She agreed that identifying medical needs and available beds was necessary, but an authentic safety record did not require a champion's score. Together with the nurses, she offered another option: count people and resources, not victories.

南區避難所設在一座舊出版設施地下。城市邊緣的難民住家受損，暫時在此等待修理。沈寧發現入所資格取決於競技排名，雖然公告承諾平等接納。管理員說這是他升職後的新政策。沈寧認為排名具有冒犯性，因為它剝奪無法戰鬥者的尊嚴。他辯稱設施需要方法分類訪客。沈寧同意必須辨識醫療需求和床位，但真正的安全紀錄不必依賴冠軍分數。她和護理師提出另一個選項：計算人數與資源，而不是勝負。

At the reception desk, a woman apologized because her stockings had torn during the evacuation. She wore a faint perfume and seemed embarrassed to ask for new clothes. Qiao found a clean set in the supply room and assured her that there was no reason for shame. A publisher who had donated books to the shelter offered to print clearer instructions in several languages. The manager admitted that his current signs differed from the actual rules. Ning asked the team to make them genuine and simple. They listed the opening hours, the procedure for medical help, and the right to ask for a correction without losing a bed. The atmosphere slowly changed from suspicion to cautious trust.

接待櫃檯前，一名女子因疏散時長襪破了而道歉。她身上有淡淡香水味，似乎不好意思要求新衣物。羅喬到物資間找來一套乾淨衣服，告訴她不必羞愧。捐贈書籍給避難所的一名出版者提議用多種語言印製更清楚的指示。管理員承認目前告示與實際規定不同。沈寧請團隊把內容改得真實又簡單，列出開放時間、尋求醫療協助的程序，以及提出更正而不失去床位的權利。現場氣氛從猜疑慢慢轉為謹慎的信任。

A rescue team arrived with three residents from a collapsed apartment block. One man needed oxygen, while another wanted news about his missing daughter. Ning asked the nurses to classify needs by urgency and check the count twice. Qiao helped carry supplies but did not interfere with medical decisions. When the manager tried to praise her as the shelter's only true hero, she pointed to the exhausted staff. A nurse had submitted her resignation that morning because the former policy forced her to reject people she could have helped. Ning invited her to review the new procedures before deciding whether to stay. The nurse agreed to finish her shift and think about the offer.

救援隊帶來三名從倒塌公寓撤出的居民。一名男子需要氧氣，另一人想知道失蹤女兒的消息。沈寧請護理師依緊急程度分類需求，並重算人數。羅喬幫忙搬物資，不干涉醫療判斷。管理員試圖稱讚她是避難所唯一真正的英雄，她卻指向疲憊的工作人員。一名護理師早上已提出辭職，因為舊制度逼她拒絕本可以幫助的人。沈寧邀她先看看新程序，再決定是否留下。護理師同意完成這班工作後再考慮。

At a meeting that afternoon, an ambitious official demanded a transformation of the shelter into a training center for future fighters. He claimed the city would be stronger if everyone learned to fight. Ning answered that some residents needed a quiet place to recover and others could choose training later. A woman recovering from surgery explained that she was not a failed fighter; she was a person who needed rest. Her words brought unexpected silence. The council representative agreed to keep the shelter separate from the arena, provided the staff published a transparent account of costs. Min offered to help with the numbers. The choice was less dramatic than a tournament, but it left residents with a real place to sleep.

下午會議上，一名野心勃勃的官員要求把避難所改造成未來武者訓練中心。他認為每個人都學會戰鬥，城市就會更強。沈寧回答，有些居民需要安靜復原，其他人之後可以自行決定是否受訓。一名術後復原的女子說，自己不是失敗的武者，而是一個需要休息的人。全場忽然安靜。議會代表同意把避難所和競技場分開，條件是公開透明的成本帳目。敏敏願意協助計算。這個選擇不如比賽戲劇化，卻讓居民真正有地方睡覺。

In the evening, Ning compared the shelter's new resource list with the arena's promotion budget. The city had spent more on one victory ceremony than on a month of medical supplies. She did not argue that celebrations should never happen, but she asked the council to explain the difference. The report also revealed a genuine possibility: if the five towers shared power, some shelter equipment could serve as safe signal points during a storm. That idea would require testing, accessible exits, and permission from the people who lived there. Ning wrote it as a proposal rather than an order. The next morning, a warning appeared on every screen: a pressure hurricane was moving faster than the original forecast had predicted.

晚上，沈寧把避難所的新物資清單和競技場宣傳預算比較。城市在一場冠軍慶典上的支出，比一整個月的醫療物資還多。她不認為慶典永遠不該辦，但要求議會解釋差異。報告也顯示一個真實可能：五座塔若能共享能量，某些避難所設備可在風暴時當安全訊號點。這想法需要測試、無障礙出口，以及居住者同意。沈寧把它寫成提案，而非命令。隔天早晨，每個螢幕都出現警告：壓力颶風比原始預報更快逼近。

Outside, a refugee family asked whether the new rules would transform their position from unwanted guests into ordinary residents with needs and choices. Ning answered that a rule mattered only if staff used it fairly each day. The nurse who had considered leaving agreed to lead a week of training, with time for rest and a written right to resign afterward. No one called that decision a victory, but the waiting line grew calmer.

門外，一個難民家庭問新規定是否能改變他們的處境，讓他們不再是不受歡迎的客人，而是有需求和選擇權的一般居民。沈寧說，規則只有每天被公平執行才有意義。原本想離職的護理師同意帶領一週訓練，並保有休息時間與之後書面辭職的權利。沒人稱這項決定為勝利，等待的人群卻平靜多了。
