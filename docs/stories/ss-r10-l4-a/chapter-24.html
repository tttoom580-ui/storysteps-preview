The Constitution of a Shield

都市高武：我替弱者校準屏障 · 第 24 / 25 章 · 3000–4000 · 英中對照

The main wave struck on the date of the city's annual championship. The arena commander wanted to follow the conventional plan and send one fighter to the center of the stage. Ning said the concept might raise morale but could not replace the five-tower procedure. The emergency constitution adopted by the council required observation, independent measurements, and safe exit routes. She had confidence in that rule because it had already been tested during smaller storms. The commander scolded her for delaying a symbolic triumph. Qiao replied that obedience to a dramatic order would not protect the shelters if the cable broke. She stayed at the control center, ready to assist without pretending to be the city's only answer.

主風暴恰好在城市年度冠軍賽的日期襲來。競技場指揮官想沿用傳統計畫，派一名武者站到舞臺中央。沈寧說這個概念或許能鼓舞士氣，卻不能取代五塔程序。議會通過的緊急憲章要求觀察、獨立測量及安全出口。她對這規則有信心，因為小風暴時已實測。指揮官責罵她延誤象徵性勝利。羅喬回答，盲目服從戲劇性命令，若纜線斷裂也救不了避難所。她留在控制中心，準備協助，而不假裝自己是城市唯一答案。

A technician reported that the east tower's support had shifted. The commander recommended pushing more power through it, but Min showed a clear warning on the graph. More load could collapse the joint. Ning intended to lower that section and lengthen the time over which the other towers carried the force. The choice would reduce protection briefly near the arena, yet it would preserve the wider network. The commander called it stingy to save equipment while citizens were frightened. Ning answered that protecting equipment was not the goal; keeping the shield operating for the longest possible time was. A broken tower could give no protection at all. The medical teams agreed that a controlled reduction was safer than sudden collapse.

技師通報東塔支座移位。指揮官建議增加輸出，敏敏卻在圖表上指出明確警告：再加負載可能使接頭崩潰。沈寧打算降低該區輸出，延長其他塔分攤力量的時間。這會暫時減弱競技場附近防護，卻能保住更大網路。指揮官說市民害怕時還節省設備太吝嗇。沈寧回答，保護設備本身不是目標，而是讓屏障盡可能長時間運作。壞掉的塔完全無法保護人。醫療隊同意受控降低，比突然崩潰安全。

The public broadcast carried a biography of an earlier champion who had lost his career after refusing an unsafe order. It was not an imaginary hero from a fantasy film; his signed notes survived in the library. Han used the record to explain why honest refusal could be an act of mercy rather than cowardice. A worker who had been struggling with depression told the shelter staff he was too tired to take part in the rhythm practice. The nurse thanked him for speaking and offered quiet rest. His choice did not threaten the network. Ning included that example in her public instructions, regarding consent as a continuing decision rather than a single signature at the start.

公共廣播播出一位早期冠軍的傳記，他曾因拒絕不安全命令而失去職涯。這不是奇幻電影裡想像的英雄，他的簽署筆記仍在圖書館。韓瑜用紀錄說明，誠實拒絕也可能是憐憫與勇氣，而非膽怯。一名長期情緒低落的工人告訴避難所員工，自己太累，無法參加節拍練習。護理師謝謝他坦白，提供安靜休息。他的選擇沒有威脅網路。沈寧把這個例子加入公共指示，因為她把同意視為持續的選擇，而不是開頭簽一次名就算數。

At midday, the central control room lost its decorative screens. The measurement instruments continued to work on reserve power, and the crews switched to printed charts. Salim kept the communication line open to the rural districts, which were still receiving heavy wind. One team reported damaged roofs but no injuries; another requested more blankets. Ning asked the commander to help move supplies rather than argue about the old arena plan. He hesitated, then gave the order. Qiao led the carriers through a protected corridor. For several hours there was no speech about champions, only plain instructions repeated until every team understood them. The shield remained smaller than its advertised size, but the hospitals stayed open.

中午，中央控制室的裝飾螢幕停止運作。測量儀器仍靠備用電力運轉，工班改用紙本圖表。薩林保持和鄉村地區的通訊，那裡仍遭強風侵襲。一隊通報屋頂受損但無人受傷，另一隊要求更多毯子。沈寧請指揮官幫忙調度物資，別再爭舊競技計畫。他猶豫後下令。羅喬帶搬運員走受保護走廊。幾個小時裡沒有冠軍演說，只有簡明指示一再重複，直到每隊都懂。屏障實際範圍比宣傳的小，但醫院仍開著。

The printed emergency guide was short enough for a tired volunteer to digest before a shift. It named the responsible technician, the current limit, and the place to request help. Han refused to add a grand slogan to its cover. People working through the storm needed direct instructions more than praise. Ning checked the wording with the nurses, then placed a copy at every control desk.

印好的緊急指南夠短，疲憊志工能在輪班前理解消化。上面寫著負責技師、目前限制和求助地點。韓瑜拒絕在封面加宏大口號。正在風暴中工作的人，需要的是直接指示，勝過讚美。沈寧和護理師確認用詞，把副本放到每個控制桌上。

By evening, the five towers were still connected. They had not defeated the storm, which continued beyond the city, but they had slowed its force over neighborhoods with open shelters. The council recorded the day's decisions and asked an independent team to review them after the emergency. Ning supported the review, including questions about her own judgment. The old establishment had treated a perfect score as the end of discussion. She wanted a system in which people could disagree, correct, and learn. When she finally sat down, a small message appeared from the weather office: the strongest wave had passed the western ridge. The city was not yet safe, but it had survived the hour everyone feared most.

傍晚五塔仍保持連線。他們沒有擊敗仍在城外持續的風暴，卻減緩了開放避難所街區承受的力量。議會記下當天決策，要求緊急事件後由獨立團隊審查。沈寧支持，包括檢視她自己的判斷。舊體制把完美分數當成討論終點，她希望新制度能讓人不同意、修正與學習。她終於坐下時，氣象單位傳來簡訊：最強氣浪已越過西方山脊。城市尚未完全安全，但撐過了大家最害怕的一小時。
