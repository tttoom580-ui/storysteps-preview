電池的芭蕾

A Ballet of Batteries

都市高武：我替弱者校準屏障 · 第 18 / 25 章 · 3000–4000 · 英中對照

On the morning of Min's hearing, the city museum hosted a public ballet performance. The dancers had offered their rehearsal as a safe timing test because their movements were precise and repeatable. Ning arrived with a battery-powered sensor and a thoughtful warning: the performers were not human generators, and no one would be pushed to dance longer than planned. A physician checked that the floor was secure. One dancer chewed a piece of bread while waiting, and another fetched water from the hall. Their ordinary preparation mattered. An admirable performance would not justify exhausting the people who created it, no matter how gigantic the shield graph looked on the screen.

敏敏聽證會當天上午，市立博物館舉行公開芭蕾舞演出。舞者願把排練作安全時序測試，因為動作精確又可重複。沈寧帶來電池供電感測器，也先提醒：表演者不是人體發電機，沒有人會被要求超時跳舞。醫師確認地板安全。一名舞者等候時咀嚼麵包，另一人去取水。這些普通準備都很重要。再值得欽佩的表演，也不能以耗盡創作者為代價，不論螢幕上的屏障圖表多麼巨大。

The rehearsal followed the same rhythm used in the public-square test, but the dancers changed direction at a landmark painted on the floor. The graph rose when their movement became aligned. It fell during a difficult turn, although the dancers were working harder. Ning used the difference to convince a skeptical colleague that effort and useful synchronization were not identical. The colleague asked whether the result was a divine sign. Ning smiled and showed the measured timing. No supernatural explanation was necessary. The team's motivation was to keep shelters safe, and the better reflection of their work was a stable signal rather than applause. The dancers approved releasing the anonymized readings after checking the report.

排練沿用廣場測試的節奏，但舞者在地上標記的地標處轉向。動作對齊時圖表上升，困難轉身時即使舞者更努力，數值反而下降。沈寧用差異說服懷疑的同事：用力和有效同步不是同一件事。同事問這是否神聖徵兆，沈寧微笑展示測得的時間差。不需要超自然解釋。團隊的動機是讓避難所安全，穩定訊號比掌聲更能反映成果。舞者查看報告後，同意釋出匿名讀數。

At the hearing, the manager claimed that Min had no proper identification to inspect central-tower records. She produced her work card, the council's temporary authorization, and the regulation requiring independent inspection. The manager then argued that the records were private company property. Ning pointed to the public contract: equipment designs could remain confidential, but safety measurements had to be available to authorized inspectors. Nevertheless, she avoided calling the man a liar without proof of his intention. The panel reviewed both documents and confirmed Min's status. The manager was ordered to restore her access and stop threatening her employment. The finding did not settle ownership of the tower, but it prevented one person from blocking an urgent safety check.

聽證會上，經理聲稱敏敏沒有正式身分識別權限，不能檢查中央塔紀錄。她出示工作證、議會臨時授權及要求獨立巡檢的規範。經理改口說資料屬私人公司財產。沈寧指出公共合約規定：設備設計可以保密，安全測量必須提供給獲授權檢查員。然而，她沒有證據證明對方意圖時，仍避免直接稱他說謊。小組審閱兩份文件，確認敏敏的身分，命令經理恢復權限並停止威脅她的工作。裁定沒有解決塔的所有權，卻阻止一個人擋下緊急安全檢查。

After the hearing, Min admitted she had suffered nightmares about losing her job. She had imagined the city turning its back on her while the shield failed. Ning did not offer an easy cure. They ate a late meal together and reviewed the actual next steps: copy the data, inspect the control room, invite a second specialist, and keep the workers informed. The conversation helped Min feel less alone. Qiao arrived with the ballet recording and asked whether her next arena match should go ahead. Ning said it could, if the arena remained safe and the spectators understood that a victory was not proof of shield power. Qiao chose to use the match to explain that distinction publicly.

聽證會後，敏敏坦承自己一直做失去工作的惡夢，夢見屏障失效時整座城市都背對她。沈寧沒有提供簡單的治癒說法。兩人一起吃了很晚的一餐，檢查接下來的實際步驟：備份資料、巡檢控制室、邀第二名專家、讓工人知道進度。談話讓敏敏不再那麼孤單。羅喬帶著芭蕾排練紀錄來，問下一場競技是否應照常。沈寧說若場地安全、觀眾也明白勝利不是屏障供能的證據，比賽可以繼續。羅喬決定用比賽公開解釋這個差別。

The city broadcast the ballet test in the evening. A host tried to call it a miracle of rhythm, but Han corrected the language and explained the limits of the data in ordinary grammar. Viewers could see the same signal appear in the square, the observatory, and now the museum. Three settings were not enough for certainty about the coming storm, yet they weakened the claim that fighting was the only source of power. When the program ended, the weather office reported another sharp rise in pressure. Ning checked the central control room's schedule. The next full-link test would have to happen before the arena's owner found another reason to postpone it.

晚間城市播出芭蕾測試。主持人想稱它為節奏奇蹟，韓瑜卻修正用詞，用一般人能理解的文法解釋資料限制。觀眾看見相同訊號出現在廣場、天文臺，如今又在博物館。三個環境仍不足以確定主風暴能否被擋下，卻削弱了「只有戰鬥能供能」的主張。節目結束時，氣象單位通報氣壓再次急升。沈寧查看中央控制室日程。下一次完整連線測試必須在競技場所有者又找到藉口延後之前完成。

Min said the hearing had once felt like a nightmare without an ending. Now she had a written decision, a colleague beside her, and work she still wanted to do. She knew the ruling could be challenged, so she kept copies of the evidence. Ning asked if she needed a rest before the next test. Min accepted a full evening off rather than pretending she had no limits.

敏敏說聽證會曾像沒有盡頭的惡夢。如今她有書面決定、身旁同事，以及自己仍想做的工作。她知道裁定可能受到挑戰，因此保存證據副本。沈寧問她下次測試前是否需要休息。敏敏接受一整晚休假，而非假裝自己沒有極限。
