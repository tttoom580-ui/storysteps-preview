議會的公式

The Council Formula

The city council met again to examine the center tower's contract. An aggressive sponsor warned that any challenge to his access rights would damage the economy. Ning replied that an essential public shield required accountable control, even when private companies supplied equipment. A competitive auction might choose a manufacturer, but it could not determine whether the safety figures were true. She brought a formula showing how the five towers exchanged timing signals and how one missing pulse could weaken the entire network. The calculation was open to criticism, which she considered a strength. Two independent engineers checked it in front of the council and found the same result within a small error range.

市議會再次開會，檢查中央塔合約。一名好鬥的贊助商警告，挑戰他的使用權會傷害經濟。沈寧回答，必要的公共屏障需要可問責的控制制度，即使設備由私人公司提供。競爭性招標可以選製造商，不能決定安全數字是否真實。她帶來五塔交換時序訊號的公式，說明少一個脈衝就可能削弱整個網路。計算可以接受批評，她認為這是優點。兩名獨立工程師當場查核，得到誤差範圍很小的相同結果。

A young council member who had recently immigrated to the city asked whether residents could see the raw data without paying a fee. The sponsor referred to a confidential appendix and said public access would invite misuse. Salim asked for proof that ordinary residents had ever caused such harm. None was offered. A keen group of teachers proposed an exhibit explaining the readings in simple language. Moreover, the librarian volunteered to store printed copies for people without personal devices. The sponsor called the teachers naive. One teacher answered that keeping the public ignorant would make every prediction less trustworthy, because no one outside the company could notice an error before it spread.

一名最近移居城市的年輕議員問，居民能否免費看到原始數據。贊助商提到保密附錄，說公開使用權可能招來濫用。薩林要求提出一般居民曾造成此類傷害的證據，對方拿不出來。熱切的教師團體提議辦展覽，用簡單文字說明讀數。此外，圖書館員願意保存紙本，供沒有個人裝置的人查閱。贊助商說教師天真。一名教師回答，讓公眾無知只會使每項預測更不可信，因為公司外沒人能在錯誤擴散前發現它。

The committee considered a penalty for anyone who altered safety records without leaving a traceable correction. Some members wanted the penalty applied to every mistake, even an honest one. Ning opposed that version. If workers were punished for reporting their own errors, they would hide more problems. She suggested a clear difference between transparent correction, careless practice, and deliberate falsification. Lei told the council that he had once been ashamed to admit a failed rescue, and that silence had delayed the next team's preparation. His testimony did not excuse mistakes, but it showed the value of learning from them. The committee revised the proposal to reward early reporting and investigate concealed changes.

委員會考慮對私自更改安全紀錄且不留下可追溯更正的人施加懲罰。有些議員希望連誠實錯誤也一律處罰。沈寧反對：如果工人承認自己出錯就會受罰，只會隱瞞更多問題。她建議區分透明更正、疏失與故意造假。雷川向議會坦承，他曾羞於承認救援失敗，沉默延誤了下一支隊伍的準備。他的證詞不是替錯誤開脫，而是指出從錯誤學習的價值。委員會修正提案，獎勵及早通報，並調查被掩蓋的修改。

Ning's acquaintance from the engineering school arrived with a new forecast. The approaching main storm would reach the coast sooner than previously estimated. Several members urged the council to hasten its vote and adopt the strongest available plan without discussion. Ning agreed that time was short, but said haste should not erase minimum checks. She pointed to the emergency exits, the independent sensor lines, and the medical teams already prepared for the trial. Those could be inspected within hours. She asked the council to refer the contested contract clause to an independent legal panel while allowing a strictly supervised temporary connection. The suggestion offered a way to act without pretending the larger dispute was settled.

沈寧在工程學校認識的人帶來新預報：主要風暴抵達海岸的時間比原先估計更早。幾名議員催促快點投票，不經討論就採取看起來最強的計畫。沈寧同意時間緊迫，卻說匆忙不該取消最低限度檢查。她指出緊急出口、獨立感測線和已準備好的醫療隊，這些都能在數小時內查驗。她請議會把有爭議的合約條款交給獨立法律小組，同時容許嚴格監督下的臨時連線。這項建議能讓大家採取行動，又不假裝更大的爭議已解決。

The council approved the temporary connection by a narrow margin. It also required that volunteers receive the same safety information whether they were fighters, cleaners, teachers, or unemployed residents. Ning called that a small act of tolerance in a city trained to divide people by rank. Qiao received a message offering a large reward if she withdrew her support. She declined and preserved the message as evidence. Outside, supporters and critics shouted at one another. Ning did not try to silence disagreement. She asked them to read the published conditions and judge the first trial by its results. If the experiment failed, she wanted the record to show exactly what had happened and who had chosen to stop it.

議會以些微差距通過臨時連線，也要求志工不論是武者、清潔員、教師或失業居民，都能取得相同安全資訊。沈寧認為，對一座習慣依排名區分人群的城市，這是小小的包容。羅喬收到訊息，對方承諾只要她撤回支持就給高額獎勵。她拒絕並保存訊息作證。會場外，支持者和批評者互相叫喊。沈寧沒有試圖壓制不同意見，而是請大家閱讀公布的條件，用首次試驗結果來判斷。如果試驗失敗，她希望紀錄能說清楚發生了什麼，以及是誰決定停止。

The librarian explained that even the postage for sending paper copies to distant villages would need a budget. Several council members had forgotten that residents beyond the city center used the same shield. Ning added the small expense to the plan and asked who would receive the reports. A system described as public should not quietly end where convenient transport and fast connections ended.

圖書館員說，把紙本寄到遠方村落的郵資也必須編列預算。幾名議員差點忘記市中心以外的居民同樣依靠這座屏障。沈寧把小額費用加進計畫，並詢問報告要寄給誰。被稱為公共的制度，不能悄悄止於交通方便、連線快速的地方。
