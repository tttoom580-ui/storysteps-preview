The Rusty Factory

都市高武：我替弱者校準屏障 · 第 9 / 25 章 · 3000–4000 · 英中對照

A new fault arose in the southern district when machinery at a former metal factory began to vibrate beneath the shield line. The factory association wanted permission to restore production, but the official forecast warned that the building would not survive another pressure storm. Ning inspected the rusty equipment before making a recommendation. A bald mechanic showed her a photograph from his autobiography, describing the day workers had saved the site from a fire. Their old fireplace was still visible behind a wall of bricks. Ning valued his memory but asked for current measurements. Past courage could explain why people cared about a place; it could not prove that a dangerous machine was safe to operate today.

南區一座舊金屬工廠的機械在屏障線下震動，新故障因而發生。工廠協會希望獲准恢復生產，官方預報卻警告建築撐不過下一場壓力風暴。沈寧先檢查生鏽設備才提出建議。一名禿頭技工拿出自傳裡的照片，講述工人昔日如何從火災中救下工廠。舊壁爐仍藏在磚牆後。沈寧珍惜他的記憶，卻要求現今的實測值。過去的勇氣可以解釋人們為何珍惜一個地方，不能證明今天的危險機器可以安全運作。

A minister arrived for a tour and declared that the empty factory was proof that safety rules harmed commerce. The district had a high job vacancy rate, and he promised that reopening would solve it. Ning asked for the complete inspection record. When he refused, Qiao halted the public demonstration, even though the official event would have helped her popularity. The minister accused her of violating an agreement. She replied that the agreement required a safe site, and the forecast had not been checked. Workers nearby offered practical details about the vibration. Their curiosity had led them to notice that the shaking grew stronger whenever a tower sent its daily power report to the arena.

一名部長到場巡視，宣稱空蕩蕩的工廠證明安全規則傷害商業。地方工作職缺比率很高，他承諾重開工廠就能解決。沈寧要求完整巡檢紀錄，遭拒絕後，羅喬停止公開示範，雖然那場活動本能增加她的知名度。部長指控她違反協議。她回答協議要求場地安全，而預報還沒被查核。旁邊工人提供振動的實際細節。他們的好奇心使他們注意到，每當防護塔向競技場傳送每日功率報告，震動就會加強。

Salim found a quotation in an old maintenance handbook: a system that cannot be shut down safely is already failing. The team followed that advice and turned off one feeder line. The machinery fell silent, and the shield remained stable for nine minutes. They repeated the test with a second independent sensor. The consequent result was difficult for the minister to explain: the factory's vibration came from a reporting device rather than from essential shield power. Ning refused to declare the entire factory harmless. Its walls still needed repairs, and the workers required protective equipment. The tests simply showed that one alarming sound had been assigned the wrong cause.

薩林在舊維修手冊裡找到一段引文：「無法安全停機的系統，早已開始失效。」團隊依照這個原則關掉一條供電線。機械安靜下來，屏障卻維持穩定九分鐘。他們再用第二個獨立感測器重測。隨之出現的結果令部長難以解釋：工廠震動來自回報裝置，而不是必要的屏障能源。沈寧沒有因此宣稱整座工廠無害。牆壁仍須修理，工人也需要防護設備。測試只證明一種令人害怕的聲音被歸錯原因。

During lunch, the mechanic cooked pasta for the team in a clean temporary kitchen. A child asked why the factory pond contained no lobster, even though his grandfather remembered catching them there. The mechanic said the water had changed after a chemical spill, and the animals had disappeared. Ning recorded the story as a possible environmental concern, not as proof of the machinery fault. The distinction mattered to her. Several problems could exist in the same place without sharing a single cause. The workers agreed to form a repair association with an elected safety representative. They would not reopen heavy production until the walls passed an independent inspection, even if the decision delayed their wages.

午餐時，技工在乾淨的臨時廚房煮義大利麵給大家吃。一個孩子問，為何祖父記得池塘裡曾有龍蝦，現在卻一隻都看不到。技工說化學物質外洩改變了水質，動物因而消失。沈寧把這故事記成可能的環境問題，而不是機械故障的證據。她認為這個區別重要：同一地方可能同時有數個問題，原因卻不一定相同。工人同意成立維修協會，選出安全代表。即使延遲工資，他們也不會在獨立檢查通過前重啟重型生產。

Back at the control room, Min compared the factory timing with the five-tower map. The reporting machine was sending a second, stronger signal whenever the arena announced a championship score. That extra signal did not help protect the district; it merely made the official graph look impressive. Ning copied the raw data, then asked the minister's office to explain the device's purpose. No answer came. Instead a public statement declared that curiosity about old equipment was distracting experts from the coming storm. Ning read it without surprise. The practical question was now clear: who had installed a system that rewarded dramatic numbers while drawing maintenance time away from the machines that actually held the shield together?

回到控制室，敏敏把工廠時序與五塔地圖比對。競技場每公布一次冠軍積分，回報機器就送出第二次、更強的訊號。額外訊號對保護街區毫無幫助，只是讓官方圖表看起來漂亮。沈寧備份原始資料，請部長辦公室解釋裝置用途，卻沒有回答。取而代之的是一則公開聲明，說對舊設備的好奇正在分散專家迎戰風暴的注意力。沈寧看了並不意外。現在真正的問題已經清楚：誰裝上這套獎勵戲劇性數字的系統，還讓維修人力離開真正維持屏障的機器？

Another question could arise when the factory reopened: would the district become dependent on a single private reporting machine again? The mechanic wanted the answer in writing before he agreed to work there. Ning helped him prepare a request for independent inspection, but left the final choice to the workers. Their wages mattered, and so did the right to return to a place whose hazards had been honestly described.

工廠重開時可能還會出現另一個問題：地方是否又要依賴單一私人回報機器？技工希望在同意回去工作前取得書面答案。沈寧幫他準備獨立檢查申請，但把最後選擇留給工人。薪水很重要，能回到危險已獲誠實說明的工作場所也同樣重要。
