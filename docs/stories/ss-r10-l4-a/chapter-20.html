The council invited a detective to examine the altered timing reports. The investigator's first concern was communication: many workers believed they had to choose between loyalty to their employer and honesty about danger. Ning said loyalty should protect people, not a convenient story. An elderly man who had once begged for shelter stood at the hearing beside a respected carrier who had delivered emergency supplies. Their backgrounds were diverse, yet both had suffered when the ranking system refused to recognize ordinary needs. A private company claimed that its popularity made it the rightful owner of the network. Ning answered that public safety was not a costume anyone could wear merely by winning applause.

議會邀一名偵探調查被改動的時序報告。調查員首先關心的是溝通：許多工人以為必須在忠於雇主和誠實通報危險之間二選一。沈寧說，忠誠應保護人，而不是方便的故事。一名曾為避難資格苦苦哀求的老者，站在長期配送緊急物資的受尊敬搬運員旁。兩人背景不同，卻都在排名制度拒絕承認普通需求時受苦。私人公司宣稱自己夠受歡迎，因此理當擁有網路。沈寧回答，公共安全不是誰得到掌聲就能穿上的戲服。

A literary teacher offered to compose a public explanation of the procurement contract. She wanted readers to understand the difference between a license to maintain equipment and a right to control every signal. The first draft was long and tense. Han helped make it shorter, while an attorney checked that the wording remained accurate. The teacher was frustrated that people preferred a dramatic duel to a careful document. Ning reminded her that clarity could also be compelling. Furthermore, the explanation would include a step-by-step method for residents to obtain public safety readings without exposing anyone's private medical record. The team refused to publish personal addresses merely to prove that a district had received help.

一名文學教師願替公眾撰寫採購合約的解說，希望讀者理解維護設備的許可，和控制所有訊號的權利不同。初稿冗長又緊張。韓瑜協助縮短，律師核對用詞仍準確。教師很沮喪，覺得大家寧願看戲劇性決鬥，也不想讀嚴謹文件。沈寧提醒她，清楚本身也有吸引力。此外，說明將附上居民取得公開安全讀數的逐步方法，卻不暴露任何人的私人醫療紀錄。團隊拒絕只為證明某區得到協助，就公布個人住址。

During a break, the detective found a greasy envelope beneath an office drawer. Inside was a list of unauthorized changes to the shield graph, each approved with the same manager's code. The evidence was strong enough to ask formal questions, but not yet to establish who had typed the numbers. Ning insisted on preserving the envelope and interviewing the staff without threats. One witness said the manager had offered to withdraw an earlier complaint against her if she changed the report. She had refused and saved the message. The detective thanked her and explained how her privacy would be protected during the investigation. No one would be asked to sacrifice their livelihood merely to make the report more dramatic.

休息時，偵探在辦公抽屜下找到油膩信封，裡面列著屏障圖表未經授權的修改，每次都用了同一經理代碼核准。證據足以提出正式問題，卻還不能證明究竟由誰輸入數字。沈寧堅持保存信封、無威脅地訪問員工。一名證人說經理曾表示，只要她改報告，就撤回對她的舊申訴。她拒絕並保存訊息。偵探向她道謝，解釋調查中如何保護隱私。沒人必須為了使報告更戲劇化，就犧牲生計。

At the afternoon hearing, the council recognized the shield system's pioneer builders and reviewed the original license. The contract allowed the company to inspect and repair the central tower, but public authorities retained the right to issue emergency timing commands. The panel ordered a supervised connection for the coming storm, with independent observers and a public shutdown procedure. The company's representative protested. The mayor answered that the order did not confiscate the equipment or settle every financial dispute; it applied the emergency clause already in the agreement. Ning felt relief, but not triumph. The connection still had to be tested, and the newest pressure forecast left almost no spare day.

下午聽證會上，議會表揚屏障系統先驅建造者，並檢視最早許可合約。合約允許公司巡檢和修理中央塔，但公共機關保留發送緊急時序指令的權利。小組命令風暴期間以獨立觀察員和公開停機程序監督連線。公司代表抗議。市長回答，這項命令不是沒收設備，也非解決全部財務糾紛，而是執行合約本就有的緊急條款。沈寧鬆了一口氣，卻沒有得勝的感覺。連線還得測試，而最新氣壓預報幾乎沒有多餘一天。

Back at the workshop, the team prepared equipment for the final trial. Qiao carried boxes, Min verified the sensor seals, and Salim checked which neighborhoods needed transport assistance. Ning wrote down the refusal rights of every volunteer and confirmed that emergency medical care would be available. A young worker asked whether they would finally be called heroes. She answered that people might use the word, but it was not the job's purpose. The purpose was to make sure that when the wind came, the weakest street had a shield as dependable as the strongest arena. Outside, a low siren began to sound. The central tower was ready for a test, but the pressure storm had started its approach.

回到工作間，團隊準備最後試驗設備。羅喬搬箱子，敏敏核對感測器封條，薩林確認哪些街區需要運輸協助。沈寧記下所有志工拒絕參與的權利，也確認緊急醫療服務隨時可用。一名年輕工人問他們終於會不會被叫英雄。她回答，人們也許會這樣說，但那不是工作的目的。目的在於風來時，最弱勢的街道也有和最強競技場同樣可靠的屏障。外頭低沉警笛響起。中央塔準備好接受測試，壓力風暴也開始逼近。

Outside the hearing room, the elderly man said he had been treated as a beggar simply because he had no arena rank. His frustration had lasted for years, but he now wanted something practical: a clear way to request shelter when storms returned. Ning showed him the new public application desk and asked a volunteer to walk through the instructions with him. He left carrying a paper form, not a promise of fame.

聽證室外，老者說自己只因沒有競技排名，就被當成乞丐。他多年的挫折沒有消失，如今卻只想要實際的東西：下次風暴來時，能清楚申請避難的辦法。沈寧帶他看新的公共申請櫃檯，請志工陪他讀一遍指示。他帶走的是紙本表單，不是出名的承諾。
