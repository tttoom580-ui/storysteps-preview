The hurricane arrived first as a change in air pressure around the skyscrapers. Loose windows rattled, while the shield graph remained strangely calm. At the emergency convention, the tower manufacturer boasted that its new system offered complete assurance against storm damage. Ning accused no one, but she asked how the claim had been tested. The engineer on duty admitted that the company had used a closed laboratory rather than a full building. The official response was consistent with earlier speeches: people should trust the strongest technology. Ning wanted more informative evidence. She showed a comparison of the laboratory graph and the shaking windows. The patterns differed at the exact moment the hurricane entered the city.

颶風最先以摩天大樓四周的氣壓變化到來。鬆動的窗戶不停震動，屏障圖表卻異常平靜。在緊急大會上，防護塔製造商吹噓新系統能完全保證避免風暴損害。沈寧沒有先指控任何人，而是問這項主張如何測試。值班工程師承認，公司只在封閉實驗室測試，沒有使用整棟大樓。官方說法仍和以往一致：市民應相信最強科技。沈寧要求更有資訊價值的證據，拿出實驗室圖表與震動窗戶的比較。颶風進城的那一刻，兩種圖形明顯不同。

A maintenance worker reported a leak from a hydrogen storage unit on a nearby roof. The gas was used for emergency transport balloons and had to remain separate from electrical sparks. The company called the report troublesome, but Qiao insisted that no demonstration could continue near the leak. A loyal team of technicians cut the power to the affected roof and directed residents to a lower floor. Their resistance to the manager's orders delayed a televised performance, not the rescue. Ning recorded each decision and its cost. She also told the workers they could refuse dangerous tasks without losing their pay. The first public task was recovery, not the protection of anyone's reputation.

維修工通報附近屋頂的氫氣儲存裝置外洩。那種氣體用於緊急運輸氣球，必須遠離電火花。公司說報告很麻煩，羅喬卻堅持外洩附近不得繼續示範。一群忠誠的技師切斷受影響屋頂的電源，引導居民到較低樓層。他們抵抗經理命令，延誤的是電視表演，不是救援。沈寧記下每項決定和成本，也告訴工人可以拒絕危險工作而不失去薪水。此時第一要務是復原，不是維護任何人的名聲。

The storm injured no one at that building, but two outer districts lost power. A commerce representative said the interruption was a misfortune that could not have been prevented. Ning asked whether the old five-tower network had been connected. It had not. A cable intended for shared power still lay inside an unopened crate, because the manufacturer preferred to demonstrate each tower separately. Ignorance about the network had become expensive. Min offered to contribute the measured timing data, and Salim organized transport of the spare cable. They would not claim that connection alone solved the hurricane. It might distribute force more evenly, but any faulty joint could also pass damage into another district.

那棟大樓沒有人受傷，但外圍兩個街區停電。商業代表說這是不幸，原本無法預防。沈寧問舊五塔網路是否已經接上，答案是否定的。一條本該用來共享功率的纜線仍躺在未開封的箱子裡，因為製造商喜歡各塔分開表演。對網路的無知成了高昂代價。敏敏願意提供實測時序資料，薩林安排運送備用纜線。他們不會聲稱一接上就能解決颶風。連線可能平均分配力量，也可能讓錯誤接頭把損害傳到別區。

At the city hospital, the nurses offered a room where exhausted workers could rest. Lei arrived after carrying supplies through a flooded street. He said he no longer considered relaxation a sign of weakness. Ning saw how the past ten days had changed him: he listened to the doctors before offering a plan. He brought news that an arena manager had been selling private access to supposedly public maintenance reports. The charge was serious, and Ning asked for receipts rather than a rumor. Lei produced three, each naming a different district. The team scanned them with the owners' permission and submitted a formal question to the council. It was not a criminal verdict; it was a reason to examine the records.

市立醫院提供一間房，讓疲憊的工人休息。雷川搬運物資穿過淹水街道後也到了。他說自己不再把放鬆當作軟弱。沈寧看出這十天已改變了他：他會先聽醫師意見，再提出計畫。他帶來消息，說競技場經理把本應公開的維修報告當私人資料出售。指控嚴重，沈寧要求收據，而不是謠言。雷川拿出三張，分別寫著不同街區。團隊徵得持有人同意後掃描，向議會正式提問。這不是刑事判決，而是檢查紀錄的理由。

By evening the hurricane weakened, but the central shield's reserve was lower than anyone had expected. Min calculated that each false report had hidden a small loss, and the losses had grown into a dangerous gap. The manufacturer promised to replace several meters without charge. Ning requested independent inspection before accepting the offer. She did not want citizens to mistake a free replacement for proof that the system was sound. The mayor finally allowed the five-tower connection to be tested with a low current the following day. The first pressure storm had revealed a hard truth: loyalty to the city meant identifying failure before it became a tragedy. It did not mean remaining silent when powerful people made comforting claims.

傍晚颶風逐漸減弱，但中央屏障的儲備量比任何人預期都低。敏敏計算出每份假報告都藏了一點損失，累積後形成危險缺口。製造商答應免費更換幾個儀表。沈寧要求先做獨立檢查，不想讓居民把免費更換誤認成系統可靠的證明。市長終於同意隔天以低電流測試五塔連線。第一場壓力風暴揭露了艱難事實：對城市忠誠，意味著在故障變成悲劇前指出它，而不是權貴提出安慰性說法時保持沉默。

Before the council meeting ended, Min asked permission to expose the difference between the public screen and the raw sensor readings. A company representative said the records might reveal how they manufacture the equipment. Ning accepted redaction of genuine production secrets, but not of safety results. The council ordered both sides to prepare a version that independent inspectors could verify the next morning.

議會散會前，敏敏請求公開公共螢幕與原始感測數值的差異。公司代表說紀錄可能揭露設備如何製造。沈寧同意遮蔽真正的生產機密，但不能遮蔽安全結果。議會要求雙方隔天早晨提供獨立檢查員可驗證的版本。
