Bread Before Points

全世界都升級了，只有我看得見隱藏任務 · 第 11 / 25 章 · 2000–3000 · 英中對照 · 校訂中

用互動版查詞、朗讀與收藏

Sera's receiver needed volunteers at five places before sunset. The first was a bakery on a windy slope. A union of delivery workers had stored emergency food there, but their account had been frozen after someone accused them of stealing. The public notice called them a criminal group. Yu arrived to find a slender baker rolling up his sleeve to carry a sack of flour. A cold breeze entered through broken glass. A little bloody water from a cut on the man's wrist had stained a towel; another worker was already dressing the wound. The bakery smelled of pepper and warm bread, yet families waited outside with hunger in their eyes.

瑟拉的接收器需要在日落前連上五處志工站。第一站是斜坡上的麵包店。配送工會在此存放緊急食物，帳戶卻因有人指控偷竊而遭凍結。公開公告把他們稱為犯罪團體。韓宇抵達時，一名瘦高麵包師正捲起袖子搬麵粉袋。冷風從破玻璃吹入。男人手腕割傷流出的帶血清水染紅了毛巾，另一名工人正在替他包紮。店裡飄著胡椒和熱麵包香氣，門外的家庭卻眼帶飢餓地等著。

The hidden quest said DELIVER THE PARCEL THAT HAS NO OWNER. A small parcel sat by the counter with no address, only a photograph of two children. The bakery manager admitted that it contained bread for a family whose housing records had vanished after the upgrade. He had tried to use his own credit to help them, but the system rejected the purchase. "It would be easy to pretend the parcel never existed," he said. Yu shook his head. They checked the original order with the delivery driver and found a paper receipt. The family was real, and their missing account had not made them less deserving of food.

隱藏任務寫著：「送出沒有主人的包裹。」櫃檯旁有個沒有地址的小包裹，只貼著兩名孩子的照片。麵包店經理承認裡面是給一戶家庭的麵包；他們的住房紀錄在升級後消失。經理想用自己的信用額度幫他們買食物，系統卻拒絕交易。「假裝包裹不存在比較容易，」他說。韓宇搖頭。他們跟配送司機核對原始訂單，找到一張紙本收據。這戶人家真實存在；帳戶消失不代表他們就不值得得到食物。

While they worked, a child reached out to lick a spoon covered in jam. Her mother pulled it back gently and offered her a slice of loaf instead. Yu asked the driver to carry the parcel to the correct building and bring back a signed delivery note. Lian guarded the bakery door as several angry players demanded the warehouse keys. She did not attack them. She read the union's food distribution rules aloud and asked for a neutral witness. The players had been told that the bread was a prize for high-level fighters. When they learned that it was donated emergency food, two of them stepped away from the argument.

忙碌時，一個孩子伸手想舔沾著果醬的湯匙。母親輕輕收回湯匙，改給她一片麵包。韓宇請司機把包裹送到正確大樓，帶回簽收紀錄。連雨守著麵包店門口，數名憤怒玩家要求交出倉庫鑰匙。她沒有攻擊，而是大聲念出工會的食物分配規則，請一位中立人士見證。那些玩家原本被告知，麵包是高等級戰士的獎品。得知它其實是捐贈的緊急糧食後，有兩人退出爭論。

The driver returned with the signed note and an emotional message from the family. Yu refused to put their private address on the public screen. Instead he recorded the delivery as verified and asked Sera to test the receiver. Her equipment sent one short call across the city: FOOD REQUEST COMPLETED; DISTRIBUTION POINT OPEN. The message contained no heroic title and offered no fortune to anyone. It was useful because another shelter could understand it. The baker smiled and gave Yu a warm loaf for the road. "This isn't a reward for winning," he said. "It's supper. Don't forget to eat while you're trying to save everyone."

司機帶著簽收單和那戶人家充滿感情的訊息回來。韓宇拒絕把他們的私人住址放上公開面板，只記錄「配送已查證」，再請瑟拉測試接收器。設備傳出一道短訊：「食物請求完成；配送站已開放。」訊息沒有英雄稱號，也不給任何人財富。它的價值在於其他避難所能夠理解。麵包師笑著送韓宇一條熱麵包。「這不是勝利獎品，」他說，「是晚餐。想救所有人，也別忘了吃飯。」

The baker asked Yu to help pitch a canvas cover over the waiting line before the evening rain. A twist in the cloth caught the wind, and two volunteers struggled to keep its corners steady. One wanted to resist every instruction from the union leader because of the earlier false accusation. Yu suggested they separate the argument about the account from the immediate need for shelter. The volunteers agreed to work together for ten minutes, then return to the disputed records. When the cover stood safely, the baker called everyone inside for warm tea. Yu had not ended the disagreement, but he had prevented it from making the families suffer. He copied that practical choice into the test report.

麵包師請韓宇幫忙在等待隊伍上方搭起帆布遮棚，以防傍晚下雨。布料上的一個扭結迎風拉扯，兩名志工努力固定邊角。其中一人因先前的錯誤指控，想抵抗工會領袖的一切指示。韓宇建議先把帳戶爭議與眼前避雨需求分開。他們同意合作十分鐘，再回頭處理有爭議的紀錄。遮棚安全架好後，麵包師邀請大家進屋喝熱茶。韓宇沒有結束所有爭論，卻防止爭論讓家庭受苦。他把這項務實選擇也寫進測試報告。

At the end of the street, Yu saw the system's official explanation for the frozen account: POSSIBLE THEFT; INVESTIGATION PENDING. He understood why the workers had been treated with suspicion, but a pending claim was not proof. He asked the union leader to preserve the shipment papers instead of destroying the notice in anger. Lian agreed to carry a copy to headquarters. The first receiver light turned green. Four more remained. The city still looked like a game board from a distance, full of bright paths and large numbers. Up close it was a place where a family could not eat until someone believed a paper receipt.

街道盡頭，韓宇看見系統對凍結帳戶的官方說明：「疑似偷竊，調查中。」他明白工會為何遭到懷疑，但尚待調查的指控不是證據。他請工會領袖保留出貨文件，不要氣得撕毀公告。連雨同意帶副本回總部。第一盞接收器燈變成綠色，還剩四處。從遠方看，城市仍像個遊戲棋盤，滿是明亮道路和巨大數字。靠近後才知道，這裡也會有一戶人家，非得有人相信紙本收據才能吃到飯。
