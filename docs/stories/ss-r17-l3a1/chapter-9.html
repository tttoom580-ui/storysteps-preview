假勝利的體育館

The Stadium of False Victories

全世界都升級了，只有我看得見隱藏任務 · 第 9 / 25 章 · 2000–3000 · 英中對照 · 校訂中

用互動版查詞、朗讀與收藏

The ice stadium had become a noisy market. A buffet stood beneath the scoreboard, but a meal cost more than most families could pay. A designer sold costly armor beside a display of porcelain cups. Someone had hung a brass kangaroo from the ceiling as a joke, and a humorous sign promised free tangerines to anyone who defeated the arena champion. Yu saw cold mist rising from the rink and thought of the people waiting outside in the icy wind. The public system called the stadium a place of endless victory. The hidden screen said: RECOVER THE ORIGINAL RECORDING BEFORE IT IS SEALED.

冰上體育館變成吵鬧的市場。自助餐擺在記分板下方，但一頓飯的價格高得多數家庭付不起。一名設計師在瓷杯展示架旁販賣昂貴盔甲。有人開玩笑地把黃銅袋鼠吊在天花板，還貼出幽默告示，承諾任何擊敗競技場冠軍的人都能免費領橘子。韓宇看見冰場飄起寒霧，想到外面寒風中等待的人。公開系統稱這座體育館是無盡勝利之地；隱藏畫面卻寫著：「在原始錄影被封存前取回它。」

The recorder room smelled of cleaning liquid. A worker used a brush to scrub the floor while another tried to tighten a loose camera mount with a hammer. Their technical supervisor looked exhausted. He told Yu that the arena displayed selected moments from every victory, skipping what happened before and after. In one clip, a champion seemed to save fifty people by himself. In the original file, volunteers had already opened the exit, carried water, and cleared the ice. The champion had fought a creature in another part of the building. Both things were true, but the edited story gave credit to only one person.

錄影室裡有清潔液的氣味。一名工人用刷子刷洗地板，另一人拿鐵鎚固定鬆動的攝影機架。技術主管看來精疲力盡。他告訴韓宇，競技場只播放每場勝利的精選片段，跳過前後發生的事。一段影片中，冠軍似乎獨自救出五十人。原始檔案卻顯示，志工早已打開出口、送水並清理冰面。冠軍是在大樓另一處與怪物作戰。兩件事都是真的，但剪輯後的故事只把功勞給了一個人。

A guard threatened to take the files if Yu kept asking questions. Lian put herself between the guard and the supervisor, without drawing her sword. She reminded him that preserving original evidence was not a crime. The designer offered to repair the camera cover, and an elderly vendor brought a clean storage box for the recorder. Yu marked the broken seal on the original drive. They photographed it before replacing the cover with a new one. Every step had a witness. When the guard finally stepped away, the supervisor took a long breath and admitted that he had feared losing his job if he refused the edits.

一名守衛威脅韓宇，再追問下去就要沒收檔案。連雨站到守衛與主管之間，卻沒有拔劍。她提醒對方，保存原始證據不是犯罪。設計師主動修理攝影機外蓋，年長攤販則拿來乾淨的錄影機保存盒。韓宇標記原始硬碟上破損的封條。大家先拍照，再換上新蓋子。每一步都有證人在場。守衛終於退開時，主管長長吐出一口氣，承認自己若拒絕剪輯，便害怕失去工作。

Yu did not want to turn the supervisor into another public enemy. He asked him to write down who had requested each edit and what had been changed. The hidden task accepted a checked record, not an angry confession. A message from Director Jiang Mo arrived while they worked. It said that technical errors were normal during a global crisis and should not be used to weaken the strongest fighters. Yu replied that a fair record could honor fighters without erasing everyone else. He had no reason to skip the uncomfortable part of the truth. The supervisor nodded and agreed to deliver a copy to the public library.

韓宇不想把主管變成另一個全民公敵。他請主管記下每一次剪輯是誰要求、改動了什麼。隱藏任務接受可查證的紀錄，而不是憤怒的自白。工作進行時，江默主任傳來訊息，說全球危機中的技術錯誤很正常，不該被用來削弱最強戰士。韓宇回覆，公平紀錄可以肯定戰士，也不必抹去其他人。他沒有理由跳過真相中令人不舒服的部分。主管點頭，同意把副本送到公共圖書館。

Before leaving the stadium, Yu heard two guards argue over the edited video. One warned that the director could make life hell for anyone who opposed him. The other said threats were no reason to hide a record. Yu asked them not to threaten the supervisor who had agreed to help. He wanted the correction handled through evidence, not revenge. Lian pointed out that strong players also suffered when their honest work was turned into a false legend. A victory nobody could question would eventually be hard to believe. The supervisor promised to keep a copy of every future edit, with the editor's name and the reason for changing it. Yu wrote down that promise and the date.

離開體育館前，韓宇聽見兩名守衛爭論剪輯影片。一人警告，主任能把反對者的日子變成人間地獄；另一人則說，威脅不是隱藏紀錄的理由。韓宇請他們不要威脅願意協助的主管。他希望用證據完成更正，而不是報復。連雨指出，強者的真實工作若被改成虛假的傳說，也會受到傷害。不能被質疑的勝利，終有一天會變得難以相信。主管承諾保存每次剪輯副本，註明編輯姓名與修改原因。韓宇記下日期和這項承諾。

Outside, the mist thinned. The stadium screen still showed the champion, but the full rescue timeline now ran along the bottom. Some viewers cheered; others complained that the new version was less exciting. Yu watched a child point to her mother carrying blankets in the video. The mother was not famous and had never wanted to be. Yet the child looked proud. Lian bought a single tangerine from the vendor and shared it with Yu. They had won no duel. They had simply returned an ordinary person's work to the story where it belonged. The next hidden arrow pointed to the central archive, directly under the tower.

外頭的寒霧逐漸散去。體育館螢幕仍播放冠軍的畫面，底下卻開始顯示完整救援時間表。有些觀眾歡呼，另一些抱怨新版不夠刺激。韓宇看見一個孩子指著影片裡搬運毯子的母親。母親並不有名，也從未想成名，但孩子的表情充滿驕傲。連雨向攤販買了一顆橘子，和韓宇分著吃。他們沒有贏得決鬥，只是把平凡人的工作放回原本應有的故事裡。下一道隱藏箭頭指向高塔正下方的中央檔案庫。
