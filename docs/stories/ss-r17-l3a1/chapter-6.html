總督的空旅館

The Governor's Empty Inn

全世界都升級了，只有我看得見隱藏任務 · 第 6 / 25 章 · 2000–3000 · 英中對照 · 校訂中

The road to the tower crossed a suburb ruled by a player who called himself governor. He had turned an old inn into a prize office, and his banners celebrated every achievement in bright gold letters. A large sign promised that the highest rank would receive a desirable home, clean water, and a special cocktail at sunset. Families below Level 10 waited outside with tired faces. Yu found the offer doubtful. He saw a hidden arrow leading behind the inn, where volunteers were trying to dump a pile of wet blankets. The public mission wanted him to challenge the governor in a duel. The hidden one asked him to find out why the shelter beds were empty.

通往高塔的道路穿過一片郊區，那裡由自稱總督的玩家統治。他把舊旅館改成獎品辦公室，旗幟上用金字慶祝每項成就。巨大的告示承諾，排名最高的人可得到理想的住宅、乾淨的水和日落時的特製雞尾酒。十級以下的家庭疲倦地等在外頭。韓宇對這項提議心存懷疑。他看見一道隱藏箭頭指向旅館後方，志工正努力丟棄一堆溼毯子。公開任務要他向總督決鬥，隱藏任務卻問：為什麼避難床位空著？

In the rear yard, a cook named Pal was struggling to move a heavy pole that held up the laundry line. A bowl of jelly shook on the table beside him. He told Yu the inn had enough rooms, but the governor had locked them to raise their value. "He calls us lazy because our levels are low," Pal said. "He says a superior player deserves comfort first." Yu looked through a window and counted twelve unused beds. He felt angry, but anger alone was not a reliable plan. He asked Pal whether the building still belonged to the city. The cook showed him the original registration, which described it as a public storm shelter.

後院裡，一位叫阿帕的廚師正在努力移動支撐晾衣繩的沉重長桿。旁邊桌上的一碗果凍不斷晃動。他告訴韓宇，旅館房間足夠，總督卻把門鎖起來抬高價值。「他說我們等級低就是懶，」阿帕說，「還說優秀的玩家該優先享受舒適。」韓宇透過窗戶數到十二張閒置的床。他很生氣，卻知道光生氣不是可靠的計畫。他問阿帕這棟樓是否仍屬於市政府。廚師拿出原始登記資料，上面明確寫著這是公共暴風雨避難所。

The governor arrived wearing a red coat and a silver razor at his belt. He called Yu a devil for questioning the rules. Lian stepped forward, but Yu asked her not to fight. Instead they invited everyone waiting outside to read the registration. A retired teacher explained that the shelter could not refuse people during a declared emergency. A carpenter checked the locks, and the cook found the spare keys. The governor laughed until the crowd began to speak with one voice. Their unity did not come from having equal skills. It came from agreeing that no child should sleep in the rain while safe beds stood empty.

總督穿著紅外套走來，腰間掛著一把銀色剃刀。他罵韓宇是質疑規則的惡魔。連雨上前一步，韓宇卻請她不要動手。他們改為邀請門外等待的人一起閱讀登記資料。退休教師解釋，緊急狀態下公共避難所不得拒絕民眾。木匠檢查鎖具，廚師找出備用鑰匙。總督原本還在嘲笑，直到人群開始用同一道聲音說話。他們的團結不是來自技能相同，而是共同認定：不能讓孩子在雨中睡覺，卻讓安全的床位空著。

The governor tried to change the subject. He promised to wed the town's richest merchant and build a greater hall someday, as though that would answer what was happening now. Yu placed his mark on the registration seal so the witnesses could photograph it clearly. The retired teacher compared the paper with the city's emergency notice. When the evidence matched, the governor's guards opened the rooms. The hidden quest rewarded Yu with an old service trail on his map. It led beyond the outer wall, away from the tower's bright entrance. Yu knew that leaving the main road would make his journey longer, but it might reveal the name missing from every list.

總督試圖轉移話題。他宣稱自己將與鎮上最富有的商人結婚，將來還會蓋更大的會堂，彷彿這能回答眼前的問題。韓宇把標記放在登記印章上，好讓證人拍清楚。退休教師把文件與市府緊急公告對照。證據相符後，總督的守衛打開了房門。隱藏任務獎勵韓宇一條舊服務步道地圖。那條路通往外牆之外，遠離高塔明亮的入口。韓宇知道離開主路會讓旅程變長，卻也許能揭露那個不在任何名單上的名字。

At the inn, a young woman asked whether the open rooms were really a lasting achievement or only a victory for one night. Yu said the paper registration had to be protected, and someone would need to check that the door remained open tomorrow. Pal volunteered to keep a daily log, and the retired teacher offered to review it with a second witness. Their work would not appear on a ranking board. The governor watched from his office, quieter now, while families carried blankets into the hall. Lian suggested that the old emergency shelter rules be posted beside the new game notices. Yu agreed. The better rule was the one ordinary people could still use after the hero left.

在旅館，一名年輕女子問，開放房間究竟是持久的成就，還是只有今晚的勝利。韓宇說，紙本登記資料必須保護，也需要有人隔天確認門仍開著。阿帕自願記錄每日情況，退休教師答應和第二名證人共同檢查。他們的工作不會出現在排行榜。總督從辦公室看著，這次安靜多了；家庭則搬著毯子進大廳。連雨建議把舊緊急避難規則貼在遊戲公告旁。韓宇同意。更好的規則，是英雄離開後普通人仍能使用的那一條。

As they walked away, Pal brought them bread wrapped in a clean cloth. "You never asked me for my level," he said. Yu answered that the cook's help had been enough. Lian noticed that the new trail pointed toward the same outer district where the missing player had last been seen. She suggested they stop at the next safe station before dark. The system announced that the governor's event was complete and praised the strongest participant. Yu checked the public record. His name was absent, and so were the names of the families who had opened the shelter. Only the governor appeared, receiving a prize for keeping order.

他們離開時，阿帕帶來用乾淨布巾包好的麵包。「你從沒問過我幾級，」他說。韓宇回答，廚師提供的幫助已經足夠。連雨注意到新步道指向那名失蹤玩家最後出現的外圍區。她建議入夜前先在下一個安全站休息。系統宣告總督事件完成，並讚揚最強參與者。韓宇查閱公開紀錄，自己的名字不在上面，那些開放避難所的家庭也不在。榜單上只有總督，因為「維持秩序」而獲得獎品。
