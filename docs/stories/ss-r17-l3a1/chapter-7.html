橋下的暴風雨

The Storm Under the Bridge

全世界都升級了，只有我看得見隱藏任務 · 第 7 / 25 章 · 2000–3000 · 英中對照 · 校訂中

Rain struck the outer road just as the group reached a highway bridge. An automatic gate shut across the tunnel, trapping an old automobile on one side and a flock of people on the other. The public system called the storm a Level 40 water event. It offered rewards for killing a lightning creature above the river. Yu saw a different message: KEEP THE LOWER PASSAGE FROM FLOODING. A child tried to climb the fence while his mother held a wet bundle of clothes. Lian carried him back to level ground. The sound of water rushing beneath their feet made everyone afraid they might drown before any monster arrived.

隊伍抵達高速公路橋時，大雨正猛烈打在外圍道路上。自動柵門關閉隧道，一邊困住一輛舊汽車，另一邊則擋住一群人。公開系統把暴風雨稱為四十級水系事件，提供擊殺河上閃電怪物的獎勵。韓宇看見不同訊息：「防止下層通道淹水。」一個孩子想爬過圍欄，母親則抱著溼透的衣物。連雨把孩子抱回平坦路面。腳下洪水奔流的聲音，讓所有人擔心還沒遇見怪物就會溺水。

The nearest rest stop smelled of dumpling soup. A volunteer was trying to heat food in a microwave, but a broken faucet had soaked the floor near the plug. An egg lay cracked on the counter, its yellow yolk spreading toward a bag of rice. Yu noticed that these small troubles distracted people from a larger one. A nest of birds hung beneath the road sign, and water was rising toward it. A young driver wanted to use his strength to force open the gate. Yu asked him to wait until the engineer checked where the extra water would go. Opening a gate without that answer could pollute the only clean well nearby.

最近的休息站飄著水餃湯的香氣。一名志工想用微波爐熱食物，破損水龍頭卻把插座附近的地板弄濕。一顆雞蛋裂在檯面上，黃色蛋黃正流向一袋米。韓宇注意到，這些小麻煩讓大家忽略了更大的問題。路牌下方掛著鳥巢，水位正往那裡升。一名年輕司機想用蠻力打開柵門。韓宇請他先等工程師確認多餘的水會流向何處。如果沒弄清楚就開門，可能污染附近唯一的乾淨水井。

The engineer arrived with a shovel and a diagram of the original drains. A protection wall had blocked one channel after a landslide, and the new game map had hidden the warning. Yu used his mark to locate the old access point. The workers dug out the narrow channel while the children watched from the upper floor. Lian stayed ready to move anyone in danger. A huge shadow moved through the storm above them. The public display called it a storm beast, but the engineer said it was only a broken advertisement screen spinning in the wind. They had nearly fought a picture while the tunnel filled with real water.

工程師帶著鏟子和原排水圖趕到。山崩後，一道防護牆堵住其中一條水道，新遊戲地圖又遮住警告。韓宇用標記找出舊維修入口。工人挖開狹窄的排水道，孩子們從上層觀看。連雨隨時準備撤離危險中的人。暴風雨裡，一道巨大黑影從頭頂移過。公開畫面把它叫作風暴獸，工程師卻說那只是一面在強風中轉動的破廣告螢幕。他們差點為了一張影像戰鬥，真正的洪水卻正在灌滿隧道。

A woman with a wet coat offered the workers juicy oranges from her car. Another volunteer found dry cloth to clothe the coldest children. Their ordinary acts made the rest stop feel like a real shelter. Yu's hidden screen reported that the drain was clearing. Then it showed a warning about mental strain: using TRACE for too long could leave him unable to see ordinary signs. He stopped the skill at once and let the engineer finish. The story of the storm would belong to everyone who had worked there, not to his small glowing mark. He wondered whether treating another person's help as his own victory would be a different kind of sin.

一名穿著溼外套的女子從車裡取出多汁的橘子送給工人。另一名志工找到乾布，幫最冷的孩子換上衣物。他們平凡的舉動讓休息站真正像個避難所。韓宇的隱藏面板顯示排水正逐漸恢復，接著出現一則精神負荷警告：過度使用「追跡」會讓他連普通標示也看不清楚。他立刻停止技能，讓工程師接手完成。他知道風暴的故事屬於所有在場工作的人，不屬於自己那個小小的光點。他想，把別人的貢獻全算成自己的勝利，會不會也是另一種過錯？

After the rain eased, the engineer noticed an exposed, almost naked wire hanging near the rest stop. He had the power isolated before replacing its damaged covering. Yu had walked past the same wire twice without recognizing the danger. The discovery worried him more than the game monster. Seeing invisible tasks had not made his ordinary senses perfect. He told Lian that he wanted someone else to check every route he marked. She answered that the rule should apply to all of them, including the strongest fighters. Before leaving, they put up a clear notice about the closed lower passage and the approved upper crossing. It would remain useful even when the system's bright symbols disappeared.

雨勢減弱後，工程師注意到休息站附近有條幾乎裸露的電線懸在半空。他先隔離電源，再替受損處換上外層保護。韓宇先前走過那條電線兩次，竟都沒有認出危險。這發現比遊戲怪物更讓他擔心。看得見隱藏任務，不代表他的普通感官就完美無缺。他告訴連雨，希望往後每條標記道路都由其他人再檢查。連雨說這條規則應適用每個人，包括最強戰士。離開前，他們張貼清楚告示，說明下層通道封閉、上層過橋處已獲核准。即使系統的亮光消失，告示仍有用。

At last the road committee opened the upper path for a limited number of people. The bridge remained closed until a proper inspection could be completed. There was no shining gate or grand treasure, only a safe queue and bowls of warm soup. A system notice announced that the lower passage had been "cleared by a champion." Yu looked around at the shovel, the wet clothes, the exhausted workers, and the sleeping birds. He wrote those details in his notebook so they would not disappear behind one heroic title. When the rain weakened, a familiar hidden arrow returned. It pointed toward an old library beside the highway.

最後，道路委員會只開放上層通道讓少量人員通行。橋梁其餘部分要等正式檢查後才重新開放。現場沒有發光的城門或巨大寶物，只有安全排隊的人群與一碗碗熱湯。系統公告說下層通道已被「冠軍玩家」清除。韓宇環顧鏟子、溼衣、疲憊工人和沉睡的鳥群，把這些細節寫進筆記本，免得它們全被一個英雄稱號蓋過。雨勢減弱時，熟悉的隱藏箭頭再次出現，指向高速公路旁的一座舊圖書館。
