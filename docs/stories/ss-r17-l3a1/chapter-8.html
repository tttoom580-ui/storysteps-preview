The Library's Missing Names

全世界都升級了，只有我看得見隱藏任務 · 第 8 / 25 章 · 2000–3000 · 英中對照 · 校訂中

The library was not part of the main game map. Its door carried a poster warning about a monster, and someone had tied the handles tight with wire. Yu felt an ache in his shoulder from the long journey. A hasty player offered a discount on a magic key if they would hurry inside. Lian refused. She wanted to locate the real entrance first. Behind a bookcase, they heard a complaint from an elderly volunteer who had been locked in during the evacuation. The hidden quest read: RELEASE THE RECORDS, NOT THE CREATURE. Yu suspected that the library held something more dangerous to the ranking system than a monster.

圖書館不在主遊戲地圖上。門上貼著怪物警告海報，還有人用鐵線把門把綁得緊緊的。長途跋涉讓韓宇肩膀痠痛。一名急躁的玩家表示，只要他們趕快進去，魔法鑰匙就能打折。連雨拒絕了，她想先找出真正入口。書櫃後傳來一名年長志工的抱怨聲；她在撤離時被鎖在裡面。隱藏任務寫著：「釋放紀錄，而不是釋放怪物。」韓宇懷疑圖書館收藏著某種比怪物更令排行榜系統害怕的東西。

The volunteer led them into a reading room where a portrait of the city's first rescue leader hung beside a swan-shaped lamp. A grasshopper rested on its cracked base. The room had once been used for leisure classes, but the system now called it a high-risk combat hall. The woman explained that a local group had tried to organize names of missing people there. A violent raid had stopped them, and officials later described the meeting as a monster attack. Yu listened carefully. He could not tell whether the woman's memory was complete, so he asked to see the photographs and dates she had saved.

志工帶他們進入閱覽室。牆上掛著城市第一位救援隊長的肖像，旁邊是一盞天鵝形燈。燈座裂縫上停著一隻蚱蜢。這間房過去是休閒課程教室，如今系統卻稱它為高風險戰鬥大廳。女子解釋，一群在地居民曾在此整理失蹤者姓名，卻被一次暴力突襲阻止。官員事後把那場會議描述為怪物襲擊。韓宇仔細傾聽。他不確定女子的記憶是否完整，因此請她拿出保留的照片與日期。

On a table lay a unique device shaped like a small rocket. It had been built to carry paper messages over blocked streets, not to explode. The volunteer had wanted to inform the outer districts about safe shelters, but the radio network failed. Lian found the old launch instructions and checked them with a technician. They decided not to use the device until the empty area beyond the wall had been inspected. Yu's hidden screen highlighted a second bookcase. Behind it lay a folder marked FIRST DAY: PEOPLE NOT COUNTED. He was conscious of how easily a dramatic story about monsters could hide a quiet list of real lives.

桌上放著一個形似小火箭的獨特裝置。它原本用來把紙訊息送過封鎖街道，不是爆炸武器。志工曾想通知外圍地區有哪些安全避難所，無線電網路卻失效。連雨找到舊發射說明，請技師一同查驗。他們決定，在牆外空地完成檢查前不使用這個裝置。韓宇的隱藏面板標出第二座書櫃。書櫃後有一份資料夾，封面寫著：「第一天：未被計入的人」。他清楚意識到，一則戲劇化的怪物故事，竟能如此輕易遮住真正的人命名單。

They copied the records in front of witnesses. Some names belonged to people who had died before receiving a level. Others belonged to living residents whose screens had never appeared. The volunteer asked them not to publish private addresses. Yu agreed. The hidden quest was about restoring the ability to ask for help, not exposing every detail of another person's life. Lian guarded the doorway while the technician prepared a safe message for neighboring shelters. They released only verified numbers and contact points. The library lights came back on, one room at a time. For the first time, Yu had evidence that the game's public counting rule was incomplete.

他們在證人面前複製紀錄。有些名字屬於尚未獲得等級就已去世的人；另一些則是仍活著、卻從未出現面板的居民。志工請他們不要公開私人地址，韓宇答應了。隱藏任務的重點是恢復求助的機會，不是揭露每個人的私事。連雨守在門口，技師則準備傳給附近避難所的安全訊息。他們只發布經確認的人數與聯絡地點。圖書館的燈逐間亮起。韓宇第一次握有證據，證明遊戲的公開計數規則並不完整。

The elderly volunteer later showed Yu an old police report about the raid. It used the word violence but did not name the people who had been hurt. The report also mixed confirmed facts with guesses about why the crowd had gathered. Yu asked her to preserve both the original and a corrected timeline. They invited witnesses to add statements only if they wished. Some people wanted to speak publicly; others chose private testimony. The library became a place where a story could be checked without demanding that everyone share the same memory. When the corrected notice was posted, Yu saw the hidden task grow a little dimmer. The missing people were beginning to have a voice.

年長志工後來讓韓宇看一份關於突襲的舊警方報告。裡面用了「暴力」一詞，卻沒有寫出誰受了傷。報告還把已確認事實與對集會原因的猜測混在一起。韓宇請她保留原件，也另做更正時間表。他們邀請證人自願補充陳述。有些人想公開發言，有些則選擇私下作證。圖書館成了能夠核對故事的地方，而不是要求所有人有相同記憶的場所。更正公告貼出時，韓宇看見隱藏任務的光淡了一些。那些消失的人，正慢慢重新得到聲音。

At the exit, a line of text appeared behind the official scoreboard: SOME PEOPLE WERE NEVER ASKED TO PLAY. The sentence stayed in Yu's mind long after the screen vanished. Lian asked whether he wanted to stop; his skill was making him tired, and the tower was still far away. Yu admitted he was afraid. He had imagined hidden quests as secret rewards, not as records of people left outside a system. "Then we change what we can verify," she said. Together they marked the library as a safe information point on a paper map. The next road led to an ice stadium full of traders and broken cameras.

出口處，官方排行榜背後出現一行字：「有些人從未被問過是否要參加遊戲。」畫面消失很久後，韓宇仍忘不了這句話。連雨問他要不要停下；他的技能已讓他疲憊，高塔又還很遠。韓宇坦承自己害怕。他原以為隱藏任務是祕密獎勵，沒想到裡面記錄著被系統排除的人。「那就先改變我們能驗證的部分，」她說。他們一起在紙地圖上把圖書館標成安全資訊站。下一條路通向滿是商人和故障攝影機的冰上體育館。
