不存在的街道

The Street That Wasn't There

全世界都升級了，只有我看得見隱藏任務 · 第 2 / 25 章 · 2000–3000 · 英中對照 · 校訂中

By dawn, an ordinary avenue had become a steep road toward a floating headquarters. The public system called it the safest route to the central shelter. Travelers hurried past an exhibition hall decorated with a red ribbon, while tourist maps still showed the old street numbers. Yu noticed an odd shadow behind the elevator doors: a narrow line that ended in an empty square. His hidden screen gave that square a name, THE FORGOTTEN CRADLE. He did not tell the crowd to follow it. First he asked Lian to survey the broken floor, and she found that the public route passed directly beneath unstable glass.

到了黎明，原本平凡的大道變成通往浮空總部的陡峭道路。公開系統稱它為前往中央避難所最安全的路。旅人匆匆走過掛著紅緞帶的展覽館，觀光地圖卻還標著舊街號。韓宇注意到電梯門後有一條古怪的影子：一道細線通往空白方格。他的隱藏面板為那方格取了名字：「被遺忘的搖籃」。他沒有立刻叫人群跟上，先請連雨勘查裂開的地板。連雨發現公開路線正好經過不穩固的玻璃下方。

Inside the hall, a magical leopard made of paper paced among festival models. It was not attacking anyone. It guarded a small cradle containing an infant whose mother had gone for water. A broken pipe had spilled almost a gallon into the room, and an organic plant display had collapsed across the exit. A frightened guard wanted to spit at the animal, thinking it was a monster. Yu stopped him. "Look at its paws," he said. "They stop whenever the baby cries." The animal was a warning, not a threat. Lian held the fallen frame while Yu went to find the child's mother.

展廳裡，一隻由紙張構成的魔法豹正在祭典模型間踱步，卻沒有攻擊任何人。牠守著一張小搖籃，裡面有個嬰兒，母親出去取水了。破裂的水管讓將近一加侖的水流進房間，有機植物展示架又倒在出口前。一名受驚的警衛想朝動物吐口水，以為牠是怪物。韓宇阻止他。「看看牠的腳，」他說，「嬰兒一哭，牠就停下。」那隻豹是警示，不是威脅。連雨扶住倒下的架子，韓宇去尋找孩子的母親。

At the stairs he met a woman wearing a firework costume for a canceled festival. She carried a bag of paper stars and had used her imagination to decorate the hall for children the night before. When she heard about the baby, she nearly fell. Yu gave her a moment to breathe, then guided her around the wet floor. She recognized the paper leopard as part of an interactive game designed by the museum. The system had made the model move but had left its original rule intact: stay beside anyone who could not walk away. The mother thanked it before lifting the child, and the leopard quietly folded into a flat sheet.

在樓梯間，他遇到一名穿著煙火表演服的女子。她為取消的祭典帶來一袋紙星星，前一晚還運用想像力為孩子們裝飾展廳。聽到嬰兒的事，她差點站不穩。韓宇讓她先喘口氣，再帶她繞過濕滑地面。女子認出紙豹是博物館互動遊戲的一部分。系統讓模型動了起來，卻保留原本的規則：守在無法自行離開的人身旁。母親抱起孩子前先向紙豹道謝，紙豹便安靜地折回一張平面紙片。

A loud voice ordered everyone to wipe the floor and climb the public stairs. Yu showed Lian a crack growing in the ceiling. He wanted to reach the shelter, but he refused to trade speed for a dangerous shortcut. They found a maintenance door beside the elevator; the hidden line on his screen pointed there. Behind it ran an old service passage with handrails and emergency lights. A volunteer inspected its supports and confirmed that it was safe for a small group. Yu marked the first turn. A dozen people went through, including the mother and child. Their careful progress felt slower than victory on a game screen, but it left no one behind.

廣播催促所有人擦乾地板，沿公開樓梯往上走。韓宇讓連雨看天花板上正在延伸的裂縫。他也想快點抵達避難所，卻不願用危險捷徑換取速度。他們在電梯旁找到一道維修門；隱藏線條正指向那裡。門後是一條有扶手和緊急燈的舊服務通道。志工先檢查支撐結構，確認小隊通行安全。韓宇標記第一個轉彎，十二個人依序通過，包括那對母子。他們慎重的腳步，比遊戲畫面的勝利慢得多，卻沒有把任何人留在後面。

A volunteer from the exhibition hall later asked why Yu had trusted a paper leopard rather than the official warning. He answered that he had not trusted the animal blindly. They had watched its behavior, checked the damaged floor, and spoken to the person who knew the exhibit. The paper model was a clue, not a judge. The volunteer copied that explanation onto a sign beside the new path, then added an arrow in large letters for anyone who could not see the tiny system map. Yu thanked her. The public notice still used exciting words like MAGIC DANGER, but the new sign said something more helpful: THIS WAY IS OPEN AFTER INSPECTION. PLEASE WALK SLOWLY.

展廳志工後來問韓宇，為何相信紙豹，而不相信官方警告。他回答，自己並沒有盲目相信那隻動物。大家觀察牠的行為、檢查受損地板，也詢問認識展品的人。紙模型是線索，不是裁判。志工把這段解釋寫成標示，放在新通道旁，再用大字加上箭頭，讓看不見小小系統地圖的人也能辨認。韓宇向她道謝。官方告示仍使用「魔法危險」等刺激字眼，新標示卻更實用：「此路經檢查後開放，請緩慢步行。」

At the end of the passage, the floating headquarters appeared again, now with a second entrance. A message flashed: HIDDEN QUEST COMPLETE. PUBLIC MAP CORRECTION AVAILABLE. Yu expected the correction to spread at once, but it remained visible only to him. He realized that seeing a safer path did not automatically give others access to it. "We need signs they can read," he told Lian. They took the canceled exhibition's bright ribbon and tied it along the safe rails. When the first family reached the shelter, Yu noticed a new symbol beside his skill: a tiny empty notebook. It waited for evidence, not applause.

通道盡頭，浮空總部再度出現，這次多了一個入口。訊息閃過：隱藏任務完成。可修正公開地圖。韓宇以為修正會立刻傳給所有人，卻發現仍只有自己看得見。他明白，看見安全道路不等於別人就能走那條路。「我們需要所有人都讀得懂的標示，」他對連雨說。他們取下取消展覽的鮮豔緞帶，沿著安全扶手綁起來。第一戶人家抵達避難所時，韓宇看見技能旁多出一個小小的空白筆記本符號。它等待的是證據，不是掌聲。
