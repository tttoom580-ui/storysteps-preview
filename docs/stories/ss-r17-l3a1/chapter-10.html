一個名字的代價

The Price of a Name

全世界都升級了，只有我看得見隱藏任務 · 第 10 / 25 章 · 2000–3000 · 英中對照 · 校訂中

用互動版查詞、朗讀與收藏

The central archive looked like a palace built above a bay of blue light. A banker managed its entrance, counting points like coins and charging one penny for every page viewed. A historic beam crossed the ceiling, holding a long row of receivers. The public system praised the archive's champion for protecting important records. Yu asked who decided what was important. The banker pointed to a board listing only high-level players. An old scholarship file lay in a drawer beneath it, almost forgotten. Yu's hidden screen responded to the file, not the board: FIND THE PERSON WHO PAID FOR A PLACE BUT WAS NEVER GIVEN ONE.

中央檔案庫像一座建在藍色光灣上的宮殿。入口由一名銀行家管理，他像數錢一樣計算積分，每看一頁都要付一分錢。歷史悠久的橫梁跨過天花板，支撐著一整排接收器。公開系統讚揚檔案庫的冠軍保護了重要紀錄。韓宇問，誰決定什麼才算重要。銀行家指向一塊只列高等級玩家的榜板。榜板下方抽屜裡，則有一份幾乎被遺忘的舊獎學金檔案。韓宇的隱藏畫面回應的不是榜板，而是檔案：「找到付出代價卻從未得到位置的人。」

The file belonged to a student named Sera, who had built a receiver to carry emergency calls between remote shelters. She had been promised credit for her work, but her name had vanished from the latest record. The archive officer said the delay was normal, then tried to postpone their meeting until after the tower ceremony. Yu noticed a leak under the filing cabinet and helped move the books away from the water. A creative young clerk offered to make copies while the original pages dried. Lian stood watch at the door. They did not steal the file; they asked for the archive's public-access copy and recorded the request number.

檔案屬於名叫瑟拉的學生。她曾打造一部接收器，讓偏遠避難所之間能傳遞求救訊息。她原本應得到署名，名字卻從最新紀錄消失。檔案員說延誤很正常，接著試圖把會面推遲到高塔典禮結束後。韓宇注意到文件櫃底下漏水，便幫忙把書本移離濕處。一名富有創意的年輕職員提議影印文件，同時讓原件晾乾。連雨在門口守望。他們沒有偷走檔案，而是正式申請公開存取副本，並記下申請編號。

Sera was waiting in a narrow office above the archive. She wore a coat with a broken zipper and carried a small case holding the first receiver. She spoke in a normal voice, but her hands shook when she explained what had happened. On the day the world changed, she had tried to report that some people received no screens at all. The central network registered her warning as noise. A fearful crowd had then blamed her device for the chaos. She stopped sending messages, and headquarters awarded a different technician for keeping the channel clear. Yu finally understood why the hidden system kept pointing toward people missing from the lists.

瑟拉正在檔案庫上方的狹窄辦公室等待。她的外套拉鍊壞了，手上提著裝著第一部接收器的小箱子。她說話聲音正常，但解釋經過時雙手微微發抖。世界改變那天，她試圖回報有些人完全沒有面板。中央網路卻把警告登記成雜訊。害怕的人群甚至責怪她的設備製造混亂。她因此停止傳送訊息，總部則獎勵另一名技師，稱讚他讓頻道保持乾淨。韓宇終於明白，為什麼隱藏系統一直指向名單上消失的人。

Director Jiang Mo entered before Yu could ask another question. He was younger than Yu expected, with tired eyes and a calm voice. He offered to decrease the public penalty on low-level players if Yu handed over the hidden task records. "We need one clear story," Jiang said. "A mob follows heroes, not footnotes." Yu answered that a clear story could still be false. He showed the director the original receiver and Sera's dated messages. The audio produced an echo of voices asking for help. Jiang did not deny that the messages existed. He said the system had been built to show only calls it could process quickly.

韓宇還來不及問下一個問題，江默主任便走了進來。他比韓宇想像中年輕，眼神疲憊、語氣平靜。他提出條件：只要韓宇交出隱藏任務紀錄，就降低低等級玩家受到的公開懲罰。「我們需要一個清楚的故事，」江默說，「人群追隨英雄，不會追隨註腳。」韓宇回答，清楚的故事仍可能是假的。他讓主任看第一部接收器與瑟拉留下的日期訊息。錄音回響著一聲聲求救。江默沒有否認這些聲音存在，只說系統原本就是設計來顯示能快速處理的求救。

Sera took the receiver from its worn holder and showed Yu the small label on the back. It carried a serial number, a repair date, and a line reading FOR EVERY RESIDENT. She said that the label had been written before the game arrived. The promise had never mentioned levels or prizes. Jiang looked at the words for a long moment, then asked whether the receiver could survive a citywide test. Sera answered with careful limits. It could carry brief verified requests, but not unlimited messages or personal files. Yu was relieved that she did not pretend it could do everything. A real solution, he was learning, included clear reasons to stop or ask for help.

瑟拉把接收器從磨舊的支架取下，讓韓宇看背後小標籤。上面有序號、維修日期，還寫著「供每位居民使用」。她說，標籤是在遊戲降臨前寫的，承諾從未提及等級或獎品。江默望著字句很久，接著問接收器能否承受全城測試。瑟拉謹慎回答：它能傳送簡短且經查證的請求，卻不能承載無限訊息或私人檔案。韓宇很慶幸她沒有假裝設備無所不能。他正逐漸學到，真正的解決方法也必須清楚說明何時應停下或求助。

"Then speed has become the rule instead of safety," Yu said. Jiang looked toward the city, where the champion ceremony was beginning. He did not order anyone arrested. Instead he gave Yu one day to prove that an open help channel could work without causing panic. If he failed, the hidden records would be locked away. Yu accepted only after Sera agreed to participate. This was not a bargain over her life or name. It was a chance for her to test the receiver herself. The hidden quest changed its wording: RESTORE THE FIRST UNANSWERED CALL. For once, the task did not point away from the tower. It pointed straight into its center.

「那速度就取代了安全，變成唯一規則，」韓宇說。江默望向城裡，冠軍典禮正要開始。他沒有下令逮捕任何人，而是給韓宇一天證明開放求助頻道可以運作，而且不會引發恐慌。如果失敗，隱藏紀錄就會被封存。韓宇在瑟拉同意參與後才接受。這不是拿她的人生或名字做交易，而是讓她親自測試接收器的機會。隱藏任務文字改變了：「恢復第一通沒有得到回應的求救。」這一次，任務沒有指向高塔之外，而是直直指向它的中心。
