港口收費門

The Harbor Toll

全世界都升級了，只有我看得見隱藏任務 · 第 5 / 25 章 · 2000–3000 · 英中對照 · 校訂中

用互動版查詞、朗讀與收藏

The road led to a harbor where Level 30 players charged families to cross a bridge. The gate stood on rough pavement that had been repaired in a hurry. A trader claimed the toll was a scientific fee for protecting the public, but he refused to show any records. A former athlete with a medal on his chest told Yu that the toll had ruined his new career as a medical courier. He had dragged a cart of flour, bandages, and medicine all morning, only to find the bridge locked. Yu grew eager to investigate. A hidden clue appeared beside the gate: THE ROUTE WAS PUBLIC BEFORE THE WORLD CHANGED.

道路通向港口，一群三十級玩家向過橋家庭收取費用。大門建在匆忙修補、仍很粗糙的人行道上。一名商人聲稱這是保護公眾所需的科學收費，卻拒絕提供任何紀錄。一名胸前別著獎牌的前運動員告訴韓宇，收費門毀了他剛開始的醫療配送工作。他整個上午拖著裝有麵粉、繃帶與藥物的推車，卻發現橋被鎖上。韓宇很想查個清楚。大門旁浮出一道隱藏線索：「世界改變前，這條道路屬於公眾。」

Lian could break the lock with her sword, but people might fall if the damaged bridge opened too quickly. They asked an engineer to check the structure and a volunteer to copy the old city map. In a warehouse, a man dressed as a knight guarded a stack of boxes. He looked horrible and threatening in the dark, yet he was only an actor who had stayed behind to protect supplies. He showed them the original bridge agreement. The local tribe of fishing families had donated stone and labor to build it, and a public board had promised free passage during emergencies. Nothing in that agreement allowed a private battle group to collect money.

連雨能用劍砍斷鎖頭，但受損的橋若太快開放，可能讓人跌落。他們先請工程師檢查結構，再請志工影印舊市區圖。在倉庫裡，一名打扮成騎士的男子守著一堆箱子。他在黑暗中看來可怕又具威脅性，其實只是留下保護物資的演員。他拿出橋梁原始協議。當地漁民家族共同出石材與人力建造這座橋，公共委員會承諾緊急時可免費通行。協議裡沒有任何條款允許私人戰鬥團體收錢。

The public mission offered experience for defeating the toll leader. Yu's hidden task asked him to restore access without harming the workers who had been ordered to guard the gate. He traced the emergency release on the engineer's diagram. The knight helped organize a safe queue, and Lian stood between the armed players and the families. The athlete used his cart to bring water to those waiting. When the toll leader threatened them, Yu calmly read the agreement aloud. A crowd of witnesses repeated the promise of free passage. Faced with that evidence, several guards stepped aside. The leader could no longer pretend that everyone agreed with him.

公開任務提出擊敗收費隊長便能得到經驗值；韓宇的隱藏任務卻要求恢復通行，而且不能傷害那些奉命守門的工作者。他沿著工程師圖面標記緊急開門裝置。騎士演員協助安排安全隊伍，連雨則站在武裝玩家與家庭之間。運動員推著車替等待的人送水。收費隊長威脅眾人時，韓宇平靜地念出原始協議。一群證人跟著重複「緊急時免費通行」的承諾。面對證據，好幾名守衛讓到旁邊。隊長再也不能假裝所有人都同意他。

The engineer opened only the inspected half of the bridge. People crossed in small groups, led by volunteers carrying lamps. An elderly woman thanked Yu for finding another way; her family could finally reach the clinic. The hidden screen displayed a new word beneath MARK: TRACE. It allowed him to keep one verified route on his screen for a full minute instead of ten seconds. He was still Level 1. It was a modest benefit, but it changed how the group could plan. Yu wrote down the agreement number, the engineer's safety limit, and every name that had consented to be a witness. He would not erase their choices for the sake of an easy victory.

工程師只開放檢查過的半邊橋面。志工提著燈帶領人們分小組通過。一名老婦感謝韓宇找到另一條路；她的家人終於能前往診所。隱藏面板在「標記」下新增一個字：「追跡」。現在他能把經過查證的路線保留在螢幕上一分鐘，不再只有十秒。他仍然是一級。這項好處不大，卻改變了團隊規劃路線的方式。韓宇記下協議編號、工程師的安全限制，以及所有自願作證的人名。他不會為了輕鬆獲勝就抹去別人的選擇。

An engineer then asked the courier to drag his cart onto the repaired part of the bridge for a weight check. The courier refused to move until the engineer confirmed the limit. Yu watched them measure the cart's load and compare it with the permitted volume of traffic for the first hour. Their patience made the narrow crossing safer than any loud victory speech. A merchant nearby complained that the free route would reduce his profits. The old agreement did not promise him a monopoly, and the council offered a separate place where he could sell supplies at clearly posted prices. Yu added that distinction to his notebook. Public access and private trade did not need to destroy one another.

工程師接著請配送員把推車拖到修復過的橋段，進行負重檢查。配送員等工程師確認上限後才願意移動。韓宇看著他們測量推車載重，與第一小時允許的通行量比較。這份耐心比高聲宣布勝利更能讓窄橋安全。一名商人抱怨免費路線會降低利潤。舊協議沒有保障他的壟斷權，議會也提供另一處攤位，讓他以清楚標示的價格販售物資。韓宇把這項區別寫進筆記本。公共通行和私人交易不必互相毀滅。

Before they left, the trader quietly approached Yu. "The director knows you can see what the rest of us cannot," he whispered. "He says that makes you a danger to the game." Yu did not answer at once. A new hidden mission shone above the distant central tower: FIND THE FIRST PLAYER WHO WAS NEVER COUNTED. He thought of the children, the nurse, the mother in the exhibition hall, and the families at the bridge. Their safety had never appeared on the public scoreboard. Lian read the worry on his face. "We can walk away," she said. "Or we can go together." Yu closed the notebook and chose the road toward the tower.

離開前，商人悄悄走近韓宇。「主任知道你看得見我們看不見的東西，」他低聲說，「他認為你危害遊戲。」韓宇沒有立刻回答。遠方中央塔上亮起一項新的隱藏任務：「找到第一位從未被計入的玩家。」他想到孩子、護理師、展廳裡的母親，以及橋上的家庭。他們的安全從未出現在公開排行榜上。連雨看懂他臉上的擔憂。「我們可以離開，」她說，「也可以一起去。」韓宇合上筆記本，選擇朝高塔的道路前進。
