最弱的技能

The Weakest Skill

全世界都升級了，只有我看得見隱藏任務 · 第 1 / 25 章 · 2000–3000 · 英中對照 · 校訂中

用互動版查詞、朗讀與收藏

On Monday morning, the sky above South River City broke into blue squares. Every person received a floating screen. People cheered when numbers appeared above their heads. A delivery driver became Level 12, and a school cook reached Level 19. Han Yu, twenty-three and late for his laundry shift, stared at a single line: LEVEL 1. SKILL: MARK. EFFECT: Make one small mark visible for ten seconds. His twin cousins laughed nervously when they saw it. Yu felt envy, but only for a moment. A second screen appeared behind the first, so faint that nobody else gave it a glance. HIDDEN QUEST: RETURN THE LOST WHISTLE BEFORE THE GATE CLOSES.

星期一早上，南河市上空的天空裂成一塊塊藍色方格。每個人面前都浮出一道螢幕。當頭頂出現數字，人們興奮地歡呼。一名送貨司機成了十二級，學校廚師則達到十九級。二十三歲、正趕去洗衣店上班的韓宇，卻只看見一行字：等級一。技能：標記。效果：讓一個小記號顯示十秒鐘。他的雙胞胎表弟妹看到後，緊張地笑了。韓宇有一瞬間感到羨慕。接著，第一道面板後方浮出第二道淡得幾乎看不見的畫面，其他人連瞥一眼都沒有。隱藏任務：在大門關閉前，歸還遺失的口哨。

The first monster arrived before anyone understood the rules. It looked like a box of broken mirrors on four thin legs. A Level 16 player struck it with a bright sword, but the creature split into two. Yu ducked behind a stool outside the laundry shop. Water began to drip from a torn pipe onto the hot pavement. The system called the street an acceptable training zone; Yu thought that was cruel when frightened children were still outside. A nurse hurried past and asked for the correct dose of medicine in her emergency bag. He could not advise her about treatment, but he could connect her with the trained medic waiting inside the nearby shelter.

第一隻怪物在大家弄懂規則前就出現了。牠像一個長著四條細腿的破鏡箱。十六級玩家用發亮的劍砍牠，怪物卻分裂成兩隻。韓宇躲到洗衣店門外的一張矮凳後面。破裂的水管開始滴水到發熱的人行道上。系統把這條街稱為「可以接受的訓練區」，但還有受驚孩子困在外面，韓宇只覺得這說法殘酷。一名護理師匆忙經過，問起急救袋內藥物的正確劑量。他無法提供用藥建議，卻能帶她去找附近避難所裡的合格醫護人員。

The hidden arrow led behind a market, where old crates held coconut drinks, a bundle of hay for a festival display, and a sign banning tobacco. Those objects seemed foolish in the middle of an invasion. Then Yu heard a child whistle twice. A boy named Ren was trapped on the other side of an iron gate, holding his sister's hand. Their grandmother sat on the ground with her chin shaking. The main mission said DEFEAT THREE MIRROR BEASTS. The hidden one said FIND THE GATE KEEPER'S LOST TOOL. Yu understood that the two missions offered very different ways to spend the next five minutes.

隱藏箭頭指向市場後方。那裡的舊木箱裝著椰子飲料、祭典展示用的一捆乾草，以及禁止菸草的告示。在怪物入侵時，這些東西看起來荒唐極了。隨後，韓宇聽見一個孩子吹了兩下口哨。名叫阿任的男孩被困在鐵門另一邊，緊握妹妹的手。他們的祖母坐在地上，下巴不停發抖。普通任務寫著「擊敗三隻鏡獸」，隱藏任務卻寫著「尋找守門人遺失的工具」。韓宇明白，這兩道任務會把接下來五分鐘帶向完全不同的方向。

He found the whistle beneath a cart and called to the gate keeper, a woman repairing the lock. She had a sincere answer: "The gate opens by hand, but only if someone holds the lever from this side." Yu could not lift the heavy lever alone. He asked the nurse and two shopkeepers to help, and none of them cared about his level. For ten seconds his tiny mark shone on the hidden release pin. Together they opened the gate. There was no golden weapon, no magical crown, only a short message: TWO PEOPLE SAFE. SERVICE PATH UNLOCKED. The sound of the children running past him was reward enough to make him explore further.

他在推車底下找到了口哨，叫住正在修鎖的守門女子。她誠懇地回答：「門可以手動打開，但得有人從這側一直壓著拉桿。」韓宇一個人抬不起沉重的拉桿。他請護理師和兩名店主協助，沒有人在意他的等級。他那個微弱的標記在隱藏插銷上亮了十秒。眾人合力打開大門。沒有金色武器，也沒有魔法王冠，只有一行簡短訊息：兩人安全。服務通道解鎖。孩子們跑過他身邊的聲音，已足夠讓他願意繼續探索。

As the shelter opened, Ren asked Yu whether the bright numbers above people's heads could tell who was kind. Yu admitted he did not know. He remembered the nurse stopping to help strangers while stronger players chased a monster down the street. The boy decided to keep his whistle on a string so it would not be lost again. Yu asked permission to copy the old gate's instructions into his notebook. He wrote the warning in ordinary words, then drew a simple picture of the release pin. If another family reached the same gate without him, perhaps they could still find the answer. He began to understand the difference between discovering a hidden task and making its solution useful.

避難所開放時，阿任問韓宇，人們頭上發亮的數字能不能看出誰善良。韓宇坦承不知道。他想到護理師停下來幫助陌生人，當時強大的玩家卻正追著怪物跑過街道。男孩決定把口哨掛在繩子上，免得再次遺失。韓宇徵得同意，把舊大門的操作說明抄進筆記本。他用普通的話寫下警告，又畫出插銷的簡單圖示。如果另一戶人家在沒有他陪伴時抵達同一道門，也許仍找得到答案。他開始明白，發現隱藏任務與讓解法對別人有用，是兩件不同的事。

A young fighter named Lian Yu saw the open gate and stopped. Her armor displayed LEVEL 18, yet she asked Yu how he had found the release pin. He told her the truth. She could not see the hidden text even when he pointed directly at it. "Then we don't gamble with what only one person can see," she said. "We check every clue together." Yu agreed. His ability could mark a detail, not prove its meaning; an extreme mistake might cost lives. Beyond the gate, a second message appeared: THE CITY IS NOT SHORT OF HEROES. IT IS SHORT OF PEOPLE WHO NOTICE. Yu folded the lost whistle into his pocket and followed Lian toward the shelter.

一名叫連雨的年輕戰士看見敞開的門，停下腳步。她的盔甲顯示十八級，卻仍開口問韓宇怎麼找到插銷。他說出真相。即使他直接指向文字，她也看不見那些隱藏訊息。「那我們就不能拿只有一個人看見的東西去賭，」她說，「每條線索都要一起確認。」韓宇點頭。他的能力只能標出細節，不能證明細節代表什麼；一次極端的誤判就可能奪走人命。門後又浮出一道訊息：城市不缺英雄，缺的是願意留心的人。韓宇把失而復得的口哨收進口袋，跟著連雨走向避難所。
