無人觀眾的演唱會

A Concert for No Audience

全世界都升級了，只有我看得見隱藏任務 · 第 4 / 25 章 · 2000–3000 · 英中對照 · 校訂中

The name belonged to Mira, a junior musician living in a barn outside the wall. Her dream was to hold a concert for the families who had fled there. A public message described the place as a frozen danger zone. Yu expected ice, but found only dim lamps, wet straw, and frightened travelers. Mira wore a red collar from her old stage costume and protected a precious box of instruments. A novelist and a painter were helping children decorate the shelter. Their work seemed small beside the city battle, yet people smiled for the first time that week. Yu wondered whether ambition could mean building a room where others felt safe rather than winning a rank.

那個名字屬於米拉，一名住在城牆外穀倉裡的年輕音樂人。她夢想為逃難到這裡的家庭辦一場演唱會。公開訊息把這裡描述成冰封危險區。韓宇以為會看見冰，結果只有昏暗燈光、潮濕稻草與受驚的旅人。米拉穿著舊舞台服的紅領子，保護一箱珍貴樂器。一名小說家和一名畫家正在幫孩子裝飾避難所。與城內大戰相比，他們的工作看似渺小，卻讓人們這星期第一次露出笑容。韓宇想，也許雄心不一定是提高排名，而是建立一個讓別人安心的空間。

The trouble began when the speakers gave no response. A loose cable made the music freeze whenever someone touched the control panel. A squirrel had bitten part of the cover, and rainwater entered through a hole above the door. The public system called Mira's request a foolish distraction. Yu disagreed. He watched an older traveler try to calm a child with a joke about roast onions. Their shared humor did more than the loud warnings outside. Yu asked a technician to inspect the wires while Lian carried the speakers to a dry table. His mark could show a damaged place, but only the technician could decide whether the equipment was safe.

麻煩從喇叭毫無反應開始。鬆脫的電線讓任何人碰到控制面板時，音樂都會突然停止。一隻松鼠咬破了部分外殼，雨水也從門上方的破洞滲進來。公開系統稱米拉的請求是愚蠢的分心行為。韓宇不同意。他看著一名年長旅人用烤洋蔥的笑話安撫孩子。他們分享的幽默，比外頭的大聲警告更有用。韓宇請技師檢查電線，連雨則把喇叭搬到乾桌上。他的標記可以指出受損處，但只有技師能判斷設備是否安全。

The hidden mission read: LET EVERYONE HEAR ONE TRUE VOICE. It did not demand perfect music. Mira said she feared the public system would confuse the sound of fear with the sound of celebration. Yu suggested that she begin without speakers. She began with one simple song. The painter tapped a drum, a traveler shook a small bell, and children followed the rhythm with their hands. A water spray from the repaired roof surprised them halfway through, but nobody ran. The technician had made the power line safe, and the group simply moved under another beam. Every person chose whether to join or watch.

隱藏任務寫著：「讓每個人聽見一道真實的聲音。」它沒有要求完美音樂。米拉說她擔心公開系統會把恐懼的聲音和慶祝的聲音混為一談。韓宇建議她先不用喇叭唱。她唱起一首簡單的歌。畫家輕敲鼓面，旅人搖起小鈴，孩子跟著用手打拍子。半途修好的屋頂意外噴下一點水，卻沒有人逃跑。技師已讓電線恢復安全，大家只是移到另一根橫梁下。每個人都能選擇參與或旁觀。

After the song, the hidden screen awarded no experience points. Instead it displayed a clear vision of the next road: someone had moved warning signs to send refugees toward a private toll gate. Lian's first response was anger. Yu asked her to wait until they checked the road. He was learning that visible evidence mattered more than what a screen claimed. Mira offered them food and a map drawn by the children. "You brought us time," she told him. "That was enough." For the first time, Yu understood that his skill did not need to become a weapon to matter. He could carry important stories between people who could not meet.

歌唱完後，隱藏面板沒有發放經驗值，反而清楚顯示下一條道路的影像：有人移動了警告標誌，讓難民走向私人收費門。連雨第一個反應是生氣。韓宇請她等確認路況後再行動。他漸漸明白，能被查驗的證據比面板聲稱的結果更重要。米拉拿食物和孩子畫的地圖送給他們。「你替我們爭取了時間，」她說，「這就夠了。」韓宇第一次明白，他的能力不必變成武器，也能發揮作用。他可以替無法見面的人傳遞重要故事。

During the final rehearsal, the cook found an onion rolling beneath a chair. It had fallen from a basket of roast vegetables prepared for the refugees. Mira laughed and asked a young musician to tap the chair with a wooden spoon in time with the song. The child did so, making the adults laugh as well. Yu noticed that the hidden quest brightened when people began to join freely, not when the music became louder. He asked Mira whether she minded if he recorded the sound for the other shelters. She agreed only after the families approved. The recording would carry a little courage down the road without turning anyone's private sorrow into a public performance.

最後彩排時，廚師找到一顆滾到椅子底下的洋蔥，原來是難民晚餐用的烤蔬菜籃掉出來的。米拉笑著請年輕樂手用木湯匙輕敲椅子，跟上歌曲節奏。孩子照做，連大人也笑了。韓宇注意到，當人們自願加入時，隱藏任務的光才變亮，並不是音樂聲變大時。他問米拉能否替其他避難所錄下聲音。米拉在取得各家庭同意後才答應。錄音會把一點勇氣送上道路，但不會把任何人的私人悲傷變成公開表演。

At sunset a message arrived from headquarters. Director Jiang Mo wanted to meet the Level 1 player who had cleared three hidden tasks without fighting a boss. The invitation was polite, almost warm, but it included a demand to surrender his notebook for inspection. Yu showed it to Lian. She suggested they carry copies instead of the only original. Mira added a line to the map: LISTEN BEFORE YOU LABEL. They left the barn after the final song, with the sound of simple music following them into the darker road. Yu did not know what Jiang wanted, but he knew which questions he would ask first.

日落時，總部傳來訊息。江默主任想見這位沒有打倒首領、卻完成三道隱藏任務的一級玩家。邀請語氣禮貌，幾乎稱得上溫暖，卻附帶要求：交出筆記本供檢查。韓宇把訊息給連雨看。她建議帶副本，不要交出唯一正本。米拉又在地圖上加了一行：「貼標籤前，先傾聽。」最後一首歌結束後，他們離開穀倉，簡單的樂聲一路跟著他們走向昏暗的道路。韓宇不知道江默想要什麼，卻已知道自己要先問哪些問題。
