The Basement Classroom

全世界都升級了，只有我看得見隱藏任務 · 第 12 / 25 章 · 2000–3000 · 英中對照 · 校訂中

The second receiver stood in the basement of a closed kindergarten. A foggy window faced an empty playground, and children's alphabet cards covered one wall. A caretaker had made a collection of messages from families who could not enter the main shelter. Her desk held a metal clip, a bottle of shampoo, and a powder used to clean the old floor. A handmade paper cock hung above the door from a spring festival. Yu thought the room looked ordinary, but the hidden screen described it as a FORBIDDEN LEARNING ZONE. He wondered why a place built for children had suddenly become dangerous to the game's rules.

第二部接收器位於一間已關閉幼兒園的地下室。霧濛濛的窗戶面向空蕩的遊樂場，牆上貼滿兒童字母卡。看守員收集了許多訊息，來自無法進入主要避難所的家庭。她的桌上放著金屬夾、洗髮精和清潔舊地板的粉末。門上方掛著春節活動做的紙公雞。韓宇覺得這房間很普通，隱藏面板卻稱它為「禁止學習區」。他不明白，一個為孩子而設的地方，為什麼突然對遊戲規則構成危險。

The caretaker, Nia, introduced herself as a queer community volunteer and explained that the children's families trusted her with private information. "Some of us had no screens," she said. "The others were told we were previous players who had failed. But we were never given a chance." A cigarette smell came from a stairwell where strangers had waited during the storm. Nia had put up a no-smoking sign because several children had breathing problems. Yu did not ask her to reveal personal names. He asked whether she could show him the failed receiver messages without sharing the children's details. She appreciated his honesty and opened the log.

看守員妮亞介紹自己是一名酷兒社區志工，並解釋孩子們的家庭信任她，才把私人資料交給她。「我們當中有人根本沒有面板，」她說，「其他人卻被告知我們是以前失敗的玩家。事實上，我們從沒得到參加的機會。」樓梯間飄來香菸味，暴風雨時有陌生人在那裡等候。妮亞貼了禁菸告示，因為幾個孩子有呼吸問題。韓宇沒有要求她公開個人姓名，只問能否查看失敗的接收訊息，同時保護兒童資料。妮亞欣賞他的誠實，打開了紀錄。

The log contained a meaningful pattern. Calls from registered players were delivered at once. Calls from people without levels were labeled as invalid and then hidden. A form offered to hire more fighters, but there was no way to request a teacher, translator, or safe room. Yu knew that liberty meant little if a person could not make a simple request. Sera asked Nia to help test a second channel that did not require a level number. The two women checked each entry together, carefully separating real emergency needs from duplicates. Their work was slower than an automatic score, but it gave the forgotten messages a place to go.

紀錄裡有一個重要模式：已登記玩家的求救會立刻送達；沒有等級的人送出的訊息則被標成無效，隨後隱藏。系統表單能招募更多戰士，卻無法請求教師、翻譯或安全教室。韓宇明白，如果連簡單求助都做不到，自由就沒有多少意義。瑟拉請妮亞協助測試第二條不要求等級編號的頻道。兩人仔細核對每筆資料，區分真正的緊急需求與重複訊息。這比自動計分慢，卻讓被遺忘的訊息有了去處。

Nia taught Yu how to weave a list of requests without exposing the people behind them. She grouped needs by location and urgency, kept private names offline, and wrote a short reason for every choice. Her wisdom came from years of practical work, not from an impressive level. When Sera's test message arrived, a small printer clicked beside the alphabet cards. It revealed a plain sentence: CHILDCARE HELP REQUESTED. The hidden quest accepted it immediately. Yu realized that the system was capable of carrying the message after all. Something in its public rules had chosen not to deliver it.

妮亞教韓宇如何編排求助名單，同時不暴露背後的人。她依地點和緊急程度分類，把私人姓名留在線下，並為每個決定寫下簡短理由。她的智慧來自多年實務工作，不是顯赫等級。瑟拉的測試訊息抵達時，字母卡旁的小印表機發出聲響。紙上只有一句樸素文字：「請求托育支援。」隱藏任務立刻接受。韓宇發現系統其實有能力傳送這則訊息，只是公開規則選擇不送。

Nia placed a row of colored cards on the table so children could report where they felt safe without writing personal names. A green card meant a helper was present, a yellow card meant supplies were needed, and a red card meant the room should not be used. Yu checked the colors with the parents before the printer sent the results. Some asked for different symbols because color alone was hard for them to see. Nia changed the design and added simple shapes. The hidden screen recorded the correction as part of the quest. For Yu, it was a lesson in how good information could still exclude people if its form was chosen without asking them.

妮亞在桌上排出彩色卡片，讓孩子不用寫私人姓名，也能回報哪裡讓他們感到安全。綠卡表示有人協助，黃卡表示需要物資，紅卡表示房間不應使用。韓宇先和家長核對顏色意義，才讓印表機送出結果。有些人表示，單靠顏色很難辨識，希望加上不同符號。妮亞修改設計，加入簡單圖形。隱藏面板把這次修正記成任務的一部分。對韓宇來說，這是一堂課：即使資訊本身良好，若沒有詢問使用者，呈現方式仍可能排除一些人。

As they climbed back upstairs, Nia stopped by the doorway. She said she wanted the new channel to remain open even after powerful players lost interest in the experiment. Yu promised only what he could control: he would carry the test records, name the system's limits, and ask for public oversight. He would not make a grand promise that depended on someone else's permission. Lian smiled at that answer. The second receiver light turned green. Outside, the fog lifted from the playground, showing a path to an orchard district that had been hidden beneath the game's bright main road.

他們爬回樓上時，妮亞停在門口。她希望強者對實驗失去興趣後，新頻道仍能開放。韓宇只承諾自己能控制的事：帶走測試紀錄、清楚說明系統限制，並要求公開監督。他不願替必須仰賴別人批准的事做出空泛保證。連雨聽了微笑。第二盞接收器燈轉綠。外頭霧氣從遊樂場散去，露出通往果園區的道路；那條路原本被遊戲明亮的主幹道遮住。
