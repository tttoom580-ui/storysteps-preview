The Greenhouse Checkpoint

全世界都升級了，只有我看得見隱藏任務 · 第 3 / 25 章 · 2000–3000 · 英中對照 · 校訂中

用互動版查詞、朗讀與收藏

The first supply checkpoint occupied a greenhouse built over an old school garden. A senior inspector refused entry to anyone below Level 10, even though the sign outside promised shelter to all. Yu could not afford to waste an hour arguing. He watched a boy in an apron carry a bucket toward a broken pump while a younger girl held a lollipop in one hand. Their energetic teacher kept the line calm. Above the door, a public mission offered points for killing a giant ox-shaped guardian. The hidden mission was different: RESTORE WATER BEFORE THE SEEDLINGS FADE. Yu asked why the gate cared more about a monster than about the children inside.

第一處物資檢查站設在舊學校花園改建的溫室裡。一名資深檢查員拒絕讓十級以下的人進入，雖然門外的告示承諾所有人都能避難。韓宇沒有時間花一小時爭吵。他看見一個穿圍裙的男孩提著水桶走向壞掉的幫浦，另一名小女孩手裡拿著棒棒糖。精力充沛的老師努力讓隊伍保持冷靜。門上方的公開任務提供積分，要求打倒一隻巨大的牛形守衛。隱藏任務卻是：「在幼苗枯萎前恢復供水」。韓宇問，為什麼大門更在乎怪物，而不是裡面的孩子。

A pit had opened beside the central tank, exposing a loose plug and a short cable covered in dirt. The inspector called the repair ongoing but had forbidden volunteers from touching it. That sounded reasonable until Yu learned that the work crew had been sent to fight the ox. He could not cast a powerful spell or drag the tank alone. Instead, he marked the cable's safe connection point and asked the school electrician to examine it. Lian stood near the edge to keep people from falling. The teacher read the emergency manual aloud. No one pretended that a Level 1 mark was a replacement for trained hands.

中央水槽旁裂出一個坑洞，露出鬆脫的插頭，以及沾滿泥土的短電纜。檢查員說修復正在進行，卻禁止志工碰觸。這聽來合理，直到韓宇得知工程隊被派去打那頭牛。他無法施展強力法術，也沒辦法一個人拖動水槽。他改為標記電纜的安全接點，請學校電工仔細檢查。連雨站在坑邊防止有人掉落，老師則大聲念出緊急操作手冊。沒有人假裝一級標記能取代專業人員的雙手。

Then a spy icon appeared above the ceiling. It belonged to a camera drone, not to a person. The inspector admitted that the headquarters was watching how long the checkpoint took to clear. "They want a dramatic victory," he said, "not a boring repair." Yu opened the maintenance entry and found a note showing that the ox was only a guard for the seed bank. A painted penguin on the children's wall pointed toward a second water valve. The electrician tested it, opened it slowly, and sent clean water into the dry beds. The giant animal sat down. Its task had never been to attack the school.

這時天花板浮出一個間諜圖示。那屬於監視無人機，不是某個人。檢查員承認總部正觀察關卡花多久才能通過。「他們要看戲劇性的勝利，」他說，「不是無聊的維修。」韓宇翻開維護紀錄，發現那頭牛其實只是種子庫的守衛。兒童牆上的企鵝圖畫正指向第二道水閥。電工檢查後緩緩開啟水閥，乾燥的苗床重新得到清水。巨獸坐了下來。牠的任務從來不是攻擊學校。

By afternoon the children were sharing a simple dessert under clean glass. Yu did not ask for a reward, but the hidden screen placed a small line under his name: COMMUNITY TRUST +1. A second line showed the checkpoint's public report as incomplete. The inspector looked ashamed. He explained that he had once entered a false number because headquarters wanted quick results. "I can correct it," he said. Yu answered that the parents deserved to know what had happened. The teacher offered to write the report together, with names removed where privacy required it. Their choice was less exciting than the battle people had expected, but it was far more useful.

下午，孩子們在潔淨的玻璃屋下分享簡單甜點。韓宇沒有要求報酬，隱藏面板卻在他的名字下加上一行：社區信任加一。第二行顯示檢查站的公開報告仍不完整。檢查員露出羞愧神情。他承認自己曾因總部急著要結果而填入錯誤數字。「我可以改正，」他說。韓宇回答，家長有權知道發生了什麼事。老師提議共同寫報告，在需要保護隱私的地方刪去姓名。他們的選擇不像預期中的戰鬥那麼刺激，卻有用得多。

The inspector brought an updated entry sheet to the children before Yu left. He had written that the repair was completed by an electrician, a teacher, and volunteers. No dramatic battle was listed. The boy with the apron asked whether the ox was a real animal or a program. Yu told him the answer was still uncertain, but they knew it had stopped guarding the water once the broken valve worked. The boy drew a picture of the scene and placed it beside the penguin on the wall. The teacher offered to keep a copy of the report at school. If the public story changed again, they would still have a record of what the children had seen.

韓宇離開前，檢查員把修訂過的入場表交給孩子。他寫明這次修復由電工、教師和志工共同完成，沒有列入戲劇化的戰鬥。穿圍裙的男孩問，那頭牛是真動物還是程式。韓宇說答案仍不確定，但他們知道水閥修好後，牛就停止守護水槽。男孩畫下當時場景，把畫貼在牆上的企鵝旁。老師答應在學校保存報告副本。若公開故事日後又被改寫，他們仍有孩子親眼所見的紀錄。

As they left, the public mission declared the guardian defeated even though the ox was sleeping peacefully beside the flowers. The claim bothered Yu. If the system could call a repair a battle, perhaps other results were also being described in strange ways. Lian checked her sword and laughed at the idea of a fighter losing to an electrician. "Good," she said. "Maybe the next door will be easier if we listen first." A new hidden entry appeared, this one carrying a human name instead of a monster's title. Yu copied it into his notebook before the image disappeared. Someone beyond the city wall had asked for help.

他們離開時，公開任務宣告守衛已遭擊敗，儘管那頭牛仍安靜睡在花朵旁。這說法讓韓宇不安。如果系統能把維修寫成戰鬥，那其他成果是否也被奇怪地描述了？連雨檢查長劍，笑著說戰士居然輸給電工。「很好，」她說，「下道門也許先聽人說話就能比較容易通過。」一道新的隱藏紀錄浮現，這次寫著人名，而不是怪物稱號。韓宇趁影像消失前，把它抄進筆記本。城牆外有人求救。
