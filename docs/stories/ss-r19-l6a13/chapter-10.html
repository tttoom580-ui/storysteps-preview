候選人就是債務

The Candidate Is the Debt

神明集體罷工，人類開始選舉新神 · 第 10 / 25 章 · 5000–6000 · 英中對照 · 校訂中

Morning brought a decision that had already been made for everyone. There was a signature at the bottom of the order, but no space for those most affected to answer. At the first televised debate, Liora proves that every new god must inherit the entire hidden debt of the previous system. Liora knew that refusing a bad choice was not the same as knowing the correct one. Before making a promise, the team needed something they could test.

清晨帶來一項替所有人做好的決定。命令底下有簽名，卻沒有讓受影響最深者回答的地方。在第一場公開辯論中，莉歐拉證明每位新神都必須繼承舊制度全部祕密債務。莉歐拉知道拒絕壞選項不等於已經知道正確答案。作出承諾前，團隊需要能夠驗證的事物。

On the public debating floor, Liora placed the miracle invoices beneath the voting bell and asked the candidates to name the cost of the office they hoped to win. Nobody answered. She unfolded the founding charter, which described divine office as a debt inherited by whoever took it. The chancellor seized the paper and declared that the only person allowed to expose that debt was the person required to pay it. Then he read the final candidate list. Liora's name stood alone. She had never registered; the system had registered her by taking her question as consent. A thousand voters saw it happen. Nemi leaned close and said, 'The ballot isn't choosing a god. It's choosing the next person the city can sacrifice.'

在公開辯論場，莉歐拉把奇蹟帳單放在投票鐘下，請候選人說明自己想爭取的職位要付出什麼代價。沒有人回答。她展開建國憲章，發現神職被寫成由繼任者承接的債務。宰相奪走紙張，宣稱只有必須償還債務的人才有資格揭露。接著他讀出最終候選名單，裡頭只有莉歐拉。她從未報名，系統卻把她的提問視為同意。上千名選民親眼看見。妮米靠近她耳邊低聲說：「這張選票不是在選神，是在選下一個可以犧牲的人。」

The overlooked details in the witness file made the question harder, not easier: The supplier sought a concession that would grant it control over the canal for a decade. A cynical investor said patients were useful only when their stories moved the market. The old procurement rule treated silence as consent by default, which the new board rejected. An emergency fund should not deplete the clinic budget merely to improve quarterly profit. Soldiers handed out sweet buns, and the fragrance of crushed flowers covered the smell of oil along the river. Nemi marked what could be verified and left the rest open to challenge.

證人檔案裡被忽視的細節使問題更加複雜，而不是更簡單： 供應商尋求特許權，想控制運河十年。 一位憤世嫉俗的投資人說，病人故事只有能推動市場時才有用。 舊採購規則預設沉默即同意，新董事會予以否決。 緊急基金不該只為改善季度利潤就耗盡診所預算。 士兵發放甜麵包，壓碎花朵的香氣蓋住河邊油味。 妮米標記能查證的部分，其餘保留給人質疑。

What changed the discussion was a fact that should have been obvious from the start. Varos announces her name as the only eligible candidate, though she never registered. Liora felt the old temptation to turn that fact into an immediate victory. Instead, the team separated what the new evidence established from what it merely suggested. That distinction frustrated the loudest spectators, but it helped the witnesses remain willing to speak again.

改變討論的是一件本來就該看得見的事實。瓦洛斯竟宣布莉歐拉是唯一合格候選人，儘管她從未報名。莉歐拉一瞬間也想把它當成立刻勝利的證明，團隊卻把新證據真正確立的部分與僅能推測的部分分開。這讓最喧鬧的旁觀者失望，卻使證人願意再次出面。

A second bundle of observations brought the consequences closer to ordinary life: No official would be permitted to intrude on protected witness meetings without a court order. A former jockey now driving delivery vans recognized the unusual generator model. The clerk recorded the literal wording of the ownership claim without adding her opinion. The chancellor's lawyer made a sharp retort about national morale, claiming that questioning the prophecy relay would deplete public confidence. The court could prosecute fraud only after evidence met the required standard. Nothing in these records excused the people who had made the dangerous decision.

第二疊觀察紀錄使後果貼近普通人的生活： 沒有法院命令，任何官員都不得闖入受保護的證人會面。 一名曾當賽馬騎師的廂車司機認出了不尋常的發電機型號。 書記逐字記下所有權主張，沒有加入個人意見。 宰相律師尖銳反駁，談起國民士氣，聲稱質疑預言中繼站會耗盡公眾信心。 證據達到法定標準後，法庭才能起訴詐欺。 紀錄中沒有任何一項能替做出危險決定的人開脫。

No one in the room was permitted to declare that a good intention canceled a harmful action. Liora insisted on recording the error, identifying who had authorized it, and protecting those affected before assigning wider blame. Nemi drew a simple boundary around what they could do tonight. The line looked disappointingly modest, yet it was the first promise that did not require another person to surrender a right.

室內沒有人能宣稱善意可以抵銷傷害。莉歐拉堅持記錄錯誤、確認授權者，並在追究更大責任前保護受害者。妮米畫出今晚能做到的清楚界線。這條線看似微不足道，卻是第一個不要求別人放棄權利的承諾。

The team kept the concrete observations alongside the disputed theory: The maintenance inspector checked for radioactive materials because an old sensor carried a misleading label. The chancellor's lawyer made a sharp retort about national morale, claiming that questioning the Sun Engine would deplete public confidence. A young courier came forward with a screwdriver and a box of broken clock parts. Outside, an elderly vendor offered each departing witness a serving of warm soup. The tower's splendor seemed irrelevant beside the dark apartments across the river. Taken alone, no detail settled the case; together they showed why a public answer mattered.

團隊把具體觀察與有爭議的理論並列保存： 維修檢查員因舊感測器標示誤導而檢查有無放射性物質。 宰相律師尖銳反駁，談起國民士氣，聲稱質疑太陽引擎會耗盡公眾信心。 一名年輕信差帶著螺絲起子和一箱壞時鐘零件上前。 庭外，一名年長攤販給離開的每位證人一份熱湯。 相較河對岸漆黑的公寓，大樓的華麗顯得毫無意義。 單獨看來沒有一項細節能結案，合在一起卻說明了公開答覆為何重要。

There were children behind the temple steps who could remember the taste of bread but not the people who had taught them to bake it. Liora did not mistake their silence for gratitude. Every miracle that filled a bowl and emptied a memory left a different kind of hunger behind.

神殿階梯後方的孩子記得麵包滋味，卻不記得教自己烤麵包的人。莉歐拉沒有把沉默誤認為感激。每場填飽碗、卻掏空記憶的奇蹟，都會留下另一種飢餓。

The record continued with small facts that would be lost in a confident speech: A spontaneous round of applause followed the announcement that workers could read the records. The boy began to stutter when he saw his stepfather at the back of the hall. The nervous clerk began to stutter before the judge invited her to speak slowly. The city agreed to subsidize emergency batteries for low-income patients during the trial. The military had claimed its newest heat reservoirs contained harmless synthetic stone. Liora asked the clerk to preserve the original wording rather than improve the story.

紀錄接著列出那些在自信演說中容易消失的小事實： 宣布工人可以查閱紀錄後，現場自發響起掌聲。 少年在庭後看見繼父，說話開始結巴。 緊張的書記開始口吃，法官請她慢慢說。 市府同意在試辦期間補助低收入病人的緊急電池。 軍方聲稱最新儲能庫採用無害的合成石材；學院在丟棄的隔熱材料中發現少量放射性礦物痕跡，但含量不足以確認疾病原因。 莉歐拉要求書記保存原本措辭，而不是把故事修飾得更好聽。

On the way out, Liora passed people waiting for a result that would affect their meals, their homes, or their ability to say no. None of them had the luxury of treating the dispute as an intellectual puzzle. Nemi paused to answer a child's practical question before they left. The answer was incomplete, but unlike the old official notice, it admitted the uncertainty out loud.

離開時，莉歐拉經過等待結果的人群，這項結果將影響他們的餐食、家園或說不的能力。他們不能奢侈地把爭論當成智力謎題。妮米停下回答一名孩子實際的問題。答案並不完整，卻與舊公告不同：它坦白承認不確定之處。
