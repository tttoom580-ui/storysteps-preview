The Candidate Who Cannot Heal

Morning brought a decision that had already been made for everyone. There was a signature at the bottom of the order, but no space for those most affected to answer. A celebrated healer enters the race, but Liora finds the names of patients whose recovery was secretly paid for by strangers. Liora knew that refusing a bad choice was not the same as knowing the correct one. Before making a promise, the team needed something they could test.

清晨帶來一項替所有人做好的決定。命令底下有簽名，卻沒有讓受影響最深者回答的地方。名醫宣布參選，莉歐拉卻找到病人康復所需代價被轉嫁給陌生人的名單。莉歐拉知道拒絕壞選項不等於已經知道正確答案。作出承諾前，團隊需要能夠驗證的事物。

There were children behind the temple steps who could remember the taste of bread but not the people who had taught them to bake it. Liora did not mistake their silence for gratitude. Every miracle that filled a bowl and emptied a memory left a different kind of hunger behind.

神殿階梯後方的孩子記得麵包滋味，卻不記得教自己烤麵包的人。莉歐拉沒有把沉默誤認為感激。每場填飽碗、卻掏空記憶的奇蹟，都會留下另一種飢餓。

Nemi arrived with a second account that neither confirmed nor dismissed the first. They compared the routes by which each record had reached the room, then placed the uncertainties beside the facts. Liora could feel the pressure to announce a solution before anyone became frightened. That pressure was precisely why a second witness mattered: a conclusion that could not survive an independent question would be dangerous, even if it happened to be right.

妮米帶來第二份說法，既未證實也未推翻第一份。兩人比對紀錄抵達房間的途徑，再把不確定之處和事實並列。莉歐拉感受到眾人希望立即宣布解方，以免恐懼擴大的壓力。但也正因這種壓力，第二位證人才重要：無法通過獨立質問的結論，即使碰巧正確，也可能很危險。

The overlooked details in the witness file made the question harder, not easier: The cleaning team used bleach only after investigators recorded the stains near the door. The union requested a comprehensive list of every visitor with access to the storage area. A credible witness described what she heard but refused to guess who issued the command. Lira praised the workers' dedication to preserving damaged equipment instead of concealing it. The guest room held an old dresser and a thermometer with a cracked glass stem. Taken alone, no detail settled the case; together they showed why a public answer mattered.

證人檔案裡被忽視的細節使問題更加複雜，而不是更簡單： 調查員記錄門旁污漬後，清潔隊才使用漂白水。 工會要求完整列出所有能進入儲藏區的訪客。 可信的證人描述聽到的話，拒絕猜測誰下命令。 莉拉讚賞工人保存損壞設備而非藏匿的投入。 客房裡有一座舊五斗櫃，以及玻璃管裂開的溫度計。 單獨看來沒有一項細節能結案，合在一起卻說明了公開答覆為何重要。

The contradiction reached the table just as the meeting was about to end. The healer offers to quit if the real cost is released; Varos threatens to close the hospitals. For a long moment no one spoke. Liora understood why silence felt comfortable; each answer threatened someone's settled account of how the world worked. Nemi broke the stillness by asking who would be harmed if they simply filed the discovery away. No one could answer without naming a real person.

會議即將結束時，矛盾證據送上桌面。名醫願意在代價公開後退出，瓦洛斯卻以關閉醫院威脅眾人。一時之間無人開口。莉歐拉明白沉默為何讓人舒服：任何答案都可能動搖某人對世界的既定理解。妮米打破沉默，問若把發現歸檔，究竟誰會受害。沒人能在不指名真實人物的情況下回答。

A second bundle of observations brought the consequences closer to ordinary life: The guard's familiarity with the corridor helped reconstruct the route taken that night. The office hierarchy did not give the chairman permission to disregard witness protection rules. The landlady of the nearby boarding house remembered an unusual delivery before dawn. Beyond it, the marsh looked lush and peaceful, concealing collapsed drainage channels. The villagers still mourn the ferryman lost during last year's unsafe winter crossing. Liora asked the clerk to preserve the original wording rather than improve the story.

第二疊觀察紀錄使後果貼近普通人的生活： 警衛對走廊的熟悉協助重建當晚路徑。 辦公室階級制度不允許董事長無視證人保護規則。 附近寄宿屋的女房東記得黎明前一件異常送貨。 更遠處的沼澤看似草木茂盛而平靜，實際卻藏著崩毀的排水道。 村民仍哀悼去年冬季危險渡口事故中失去的渡船夫。 莉歐拉要求書記保存原本措辭，而不是把故事修飾得更好聽。

Liora had expected the hardest part to be exposing the secret. Instead, it was choosing what to do after a partial truth became public. The people who had been ignored did not all want the same remedy. Nemi proposed that each affected group could submit a written objection and receive a specific answer. The method would take time, but it recognized them as participants rather than scenery.

莉歐拉原以為最難的是揭露祕密，後來才發現，部分真相公開後如何行動才是難題。曾被忽略的人並不全想要相同補救。妮米提議每個受影響群體都能提出書面異議並取得具體答覆。方法需要時間，但終於把他們當成參與者，而不是背景。

The team kept the concrete observations alongside the disputed theory: The teacher hoped a fair treaty would nurture trust among children speaking different languages. Rain pressed against the windows, and the river threatened to overflow its banks. A cleaner had begun to overhear the order before the supervisor closed the door. The company's new report could refute a rumor only by presenting verifiable records. A gull circled the quay where a long-abandoned concert hall once hosted a symphony every spring. Nemi marked what could be verified and left the rest open to challenge.

團隊把具體觀察與有爭議的理論並列保存： 老師希望公平條約能培養使用不同語言的孩子間的信任。 雨水敲打窗戶，河水幾乎溢出河岸。 主管關門前，清潔員已無意間聽到那項命令。 公司新報告只能用可驗證紀錄反駁謠言。 一隻海鷗盤旋在碼頭上，附近那座廢棄音樂廳從前每年春天都會演奏交響樂。 妮米標記能查證的部分，其餘保留給人質疑。

There were children behind the temple steps who could remember the taste of bread but not the people who had taught them to bake it. Liora did not mistake their silence for gratitude. Every miracle that filled a bowl and emptied a memory left a different kind of hunger behind.

神殿階梯後方的孩子記得麵包滋味，卻不記得教自己烤麵包的人。莉歐拉沒有把沉默誤認為感激。每場填飽碗、卻掏空記憶的奇蹟，都會留下另一種飢餓。

The record continued with small facts that would be lost in a confident speech: The site superintendent admitted that he had approved a broken gate's temporary use. The clerk entered symphony in the testimony without deciding what it proved. The infirmary nurse placed a thermometer beside an ill courier and offered him rest before questioning. Lira said transparent testimony mattered more than turning the hearing into public turmoil. Volunteers prepared wholesome meals for interviewees who had missed the last bus. Nothing in these records excused the people who had made the dangerous decision.

紀錄接著列出那些在自信演說中容易消失的小事實： 現場總監承認自己曾批准暫時使用損壞閘門。 書記將「symphony」記入證詞，沒有立即判定它能證明什麼。 醫務室護士把溫度計放在生病信差旁，讓他先休息再接受詢問。 莉拉說透明證詞比把聽證變成公眾騷動更重要。 志工為錯過末班車的受訪者準備營養均衡的餐點。 紀錄中沒有任何一項能替做出危險決定的人開脫。

When the lamps were lowered, Liora returned to a cracked voting bell. It was neither proof of innocence nor a shortcut to justice. It was a reminder that the next decision would be witnessed by people who no longer trusted easy promises. Nemi remained at the door while the final record was sealed. Outside, the twelve-bell capital waited for news that had to be more precise than a victory speech.

燈光調暗時，莉歐拉再次看向一口裂開的投票鐘。它既不是清白證明，也不是通往正義的捷徑，而是在提醒眾人：下一個決定會由不再相信輕易承諾的人見證。妮米守在門邊，最後一份紀錄被封存。外頭的十二鐘都等待的消息，必須比勝利演說更精確。
