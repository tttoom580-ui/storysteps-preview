十二座空祭壇

Twelve Empty Altars

神明集體罷工，人類開始選舉新神 · 第 2 / 25 章 · 5000–6000 · 英中對照 · 校訂中

A messenger arrived before the bells had finished ringing. The news sounded impossible only because everyone had been trained not to question the institution that delivered it. Liora visits each temple and discovers identical notices announcing a strike with no explanation of the gods’ demands. Liora asked the messenger to repeat the part that had been omitted from the written notice. It was there, in the omission, that the day's real story began.

鐘聲尚未結束，信使已經抵達。消息之所以聽來不可能，只因每個人都習慣不質疑傳遞消息的制度。莉歐拉走訪每座神殿，發現十二張完全相同的罷工公告，卻沒有任何神明提出要求。莉歐拉請信使重述書面公告遺漏的部分。這一天真正的故事，就從那處缺漏開始。

A miracle never created energy or food from nothing; it moved a cost into a ledger, usually without telling the person who paid. The election bell counted a vote only when the person holding it could still say no.

奇蹟從不會憑空創造能源或食物，只會把代價轉進帳簿，而且常未告知付費者。只有選民仍能說不，投票鐘才會計入選票。

There were children behind the temple steps who could remember the taste of bread but not the people who had taught them to bake it. Liora did not mistake their silence for gratitude. Every miracle that filled a bowl and emptied a memory left a different kind of hunger behind.

神殿階梯後方的孩子記得麵包滋味，卻不記得教自己烤麵包的人。莉歐拉沒有把沉默誤認為感激。每場填飽碗、卻掏空記憶的奇蹟，都會留下另一種飢餓。

The evidence did not arrive as a confession. It came through a date that did not fit, a measurement that had been repeated, and a witness who knew exactly what the official account had left out. Liora asked Nemi not to describe suspicion as proof. If the accusation was wrong, an innocent person would suffer. If it was right, careless language could still give the guilty a way to escape.

證據不是以自白形式出現，而是一個對不上的日期、一項重複測量的結果，以及一位知道官方說法遺漏何事的證人。莉歐拉請妮米不要把懷疑說成證明。若指控錯了，無辜者會受害；若指控正確，草率措辭仍可能讓有罪者脫身。

The overlooked details in the witness file made the question harder, not easier: An aboriginal community upstream requested access to the substation's emergency warning system. Small settlements abound along the ridges, where families tended terraced gardens under extreme weather. Liora tried to align their route with the royal charts, but the marked crossing had been destroyed months earlier. At that altitude the air was thin enough to make breathing difficult. The magistrate concealed his annoyance when Iva asked him to repeat a legal question slowly. Nemi marked what could be verified and left the rest open to challenge.

證人檔案裡被忽視的細節使問題更加複雜，而不是更簡單： 上游原住民族社群要求能接收到變電站的緊急警報。 山脊遍布小聚落，家家在嚴酷天候下照顧梯田。 莉歐拉試著讓路線與王室地圖對齊，卻發現標示的渡口早在數月前就毀了。 這樣的海拔使空氣稀薄，呼吸格外費力。 伊娃請地方法官慢慢重述法律問題時，他掩飾住自己的不耐。 妮米標記能查證的部分，其餘保留給人質疑。

A small act of honesty unsettled the room more than a shouted threat could have done. Nemi, a former temple singer, recognizes the notices as copies printed before the gods disappeared. Liora watched a witness decide not to change a difficult detail merely because it complicated the preferred story. Nemi recorded the correction openly. Their inquiry would be slower now, but its shape belonged less to the powerful and more to the people who had lived through the event.

一個小小的誠實舉動，帶來比喊叫威脅更強的震動。前神殿歌者妮米認出，這些公告早在神明消失前就已印製。莉歐拉看見證人沒有因為細節讓主流說法變複雜就改口。妮米公開記錄修正。調查會因此變慢，卻能較少受權貴支配，也更貼近親歷者。

A second bundle of observations brought the consequences closer to ordinary life: Iva gave a blunt answer: her neighbors had agreed to store grain, never to lose their homes. The clerk entered crossing in the testimony without deciding what it proved. Liora refused to discard an outdated map while its original annotations remained unexamined. The harbor discharge record showed when the packed cases had left the boat. Their joint endeavor would be assessed through measurements, not a flattering press release. Nothing in these records excused the people who had made the dangerous decision.

第二疊觀察紀錄使後果貼近普通人的生活： 伊娃直截了當地回答：鄰居同意保管糧食，從未同意失去家園。 書記將「crossing」記入證詞，沒有立即判定它能證明什麼。 原始註解還沒查清，瑪拉拒絕丟棄舊地圖。 港口卸貨紀錄寫明包裝展箱何時離船。 雙方共同努力將以測量結果評估，而非以討好的新聞稿。 紀錄中沒有任何一項能替做出危險決定的人開脫。

No one in the room was permitted to declare that a good intention canceled a harmful action. Liora insisted on recording the error, identifying who had authorized it, and protecting those affected before assigning wider blame. Nemi drew a simple boundary around what they could do tonight. The line looked disappointingly modest, yet it was the first promise that did not require another person to surrender a right.

室內沒有人能宣稱善意可以抵銷傷害。莉歐拉堅持記錄錯誤、確認授權者，並在追究更大責任前保護受害者。妮米畫出今晚能做到的清楚界線。這條線看似微不足道，卻是第一個不要求別人放棄權利的承諾。

The team kept the concrete observations alongside the disputed theory: The first hurdle was getting both companies to accept a technician chosen by workers. Lira changed her posture when the resident began speaking, listening without interruption. The interpreter demanded precision in the word for custody because a careless synonym could transfer ownership. The officer announced that travelers must undergo rigorous questioning, and that no royal family member could veto the security rules. At a wind-battered terrace, the party found an elderly widow repairing her roof. Taken alone, no detail settled the case; together they showed why a public answer mattered.

團隊把具體觀察與有爭議的理論並列保存： 第一道障礙是讓兩家公司接受工人推選的技師。 居民開始發言時，莉拉調整姿勢，沒有打斷對方。 譯員要求精確使用「保管」一詞，因為草率的同義詞可能轉移所有權。 軍官宣布旅人都須接受嚴格盤查，任何王室成員也不能否決安全規則。 在飽受狂風吹襲的梯田邊，隊伍找到一名修屋頂的年長寡婦。 單獨看來沒有一項細節能結案，合在一起卻說明了公開答覆為何重要。

There were children behind the temple steps who could remember the taste of bread but not the people who had taught them to bake it. Liora did not mistake their silence for gratitude. Every miracle that filled a bowl and emptied a memory left a different kind of hunger behind.

神殿階梯後方的孩子記得麵包滋味，卻不記得教自己烤麵包的人。莉歐拉沒有把沉默誤認為感激。每場填飽碗、卻掏空記憶的奇蹟，都會留下另一種飢餓。

The record continued with small facts that would be lost in a confident speech: A workers' uprising was not the issue; they were requesting the safety rights already promised. A volunteer usher led witnesses through the side entrance to protect their privacy. The versatile backup unit could power either a clinic refrigerator or an emergency radio. The local council chose not to veto the temporary food route merely because the palace disliked it. The widow refused payment but asked that her testimony be carried to an independent court. Liora asked the clerk to preserve the original wording rather than improve the story.

紀錄接著列出那些在自信演說中容易消失的小事實： 這不是工人起義；他們只是要求已被承諾的安全權利。 志工引導員帶證人從側門進入，以保護隱私。 多功能備用裝置可供應診所冰箱或緊急無線電。 地方議會沒有只因王宮不喜歡就否決臨時糧食路線。 寡婦拒絕收錢，只求她把證詞交給獨立法庭。 莉歐拉要求書記保存原本措辭，而不是把故事修飾得更好聽。

On the way out, Liora passed people waiting for a result that would affect their meals, their homes, or their ability to say no. None of them had the luxury of treating the dispute as an intellectual puzzle. Nemi paused to answer a child's practical question before they left. The answer was incomplete, but unlike the old official notice, it admitted the uncertainty out loud.

離開時，莉歐拉經過等待結果的人群，這項結果將影響他們的餐食、家園或說不的能力。他們不能奢侈地把爭論當成智力謎題。妮米停下回答一名孩子實際的問題。答案並不完整，卻與舊公告不同：它坦白承認不確定之處。
