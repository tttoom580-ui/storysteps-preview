The Thirteenth Ballot

神明集體罷工，人類開始選舉新神 · 第 5 / 25 章 · 5000–6000 · 英中對照 · 校訂中

By noon, rumors had outrun the facts. Some people wanted a villain; others wanted a hero who could end the argument without asking another question. Nemi finds an obsolete ballot design in which the thirteenth choice means none of the gods rather than another ruler. Liora had worked long enough to distrust both shortcuts. In the twelve-bell capital, the first task was to protect the evidence before anyone could turn it into a weapon.

到了中午，謠言早已跑得比事實快。有人想找反派，也有人想找英雄，好在不再追問的情況下結束爭論。妮米找到舊版選票，上面的第十三個選項不是新統治者，而是「不選任何神明」。莉歐拉工作多年，已經不相信這兩種捷徑。在十二鐘都，當務之急是先保護證據，免得任何人將它變成武器。

There were children behind the temple steps who could remember the taste of bread but not the people who had taught them to bake it. Liora did not mistake their silence for gratitude. Every miracle that filled a bowl and emptied a memory left a different kind of hunger behind.

神殿階梯後方的孩子記得麵包滋味，卻不記得教自己烤麵包的人。莉歐拉沒有把沉默誤認為感激。每場填飽碗、卻掏空記憶的奇蹟，都會留下另一種飢餓。

The argument was not merely about who held authority. It was about what happened when an official decision met a household, a workplace, or a body that could not obey it safely. Liora asked Nemi to describe the smallest practical consequence. Only then did they realize that the proposed remedy would reproduce the very harm it claimed to prevent. They changed the plan before presenting it in public.

爭論不只是誰有權威，而是官方決定落到家庭、職場或無法安全服從的身體上時會發生什麼。莉歐拉請妮米說明最小、最實際的後果。他們這才發現提議中的補救會重複它聲稱要防止的傷害，因此在公開發表前修改了計畫。

The overlooked details in the witness file made the question harder, not easier: The company academy offered a practical course on emergency power rather than executive slogans. A false accusation against the warehouse team was withdrawn after the access records were checked. A banquet was taking place across the courtyard, where donors praised the war effort. A relief grant originally intended for fertilizer purchases had been redirected without explanation. The riverside fishery requested a dedicated backup line to protect its refrigerated catch. Liora asked the clerk to preserve the original wording rather than improve the story.

證人檔案裡被忽視的細節使問題更加複雜，而不是更簡單： 公司學院開設緊急供電實務課，而非宣傳主管口號。 查核門禁紀錄後，對倉庫團隊的不實指控遭撤回。 庭院對面正在舉行宴會，捐助者讚美戰爭。 原訂購買肥料的救助補助遭不明轉用。 河岸漁業要求專用備電線路，以保護冷藏漁獲。 莉歐拉要求書記保存原本措辭，而不是把故事修飾得更好聽。

What changed the discussion was a fact that should have been obvious from the start. Someone has scraped that option off the public printing plates. Liora felt the old temptation to turn that fact into an immediate victory. Instead, the team separated what the new evidence established from what it merely suggested. That distinction frustrated the loudest spectators, but it helped the witnesses remain willing to speak again.

改變討論的是一件本來就該看得見的事實。有人把這個選項從公開印版上刮掉。莉歐拉一瞬間也想把它當成立刻勝利的證明，團隊卻把新證據真正確立的部分與僅能推測的部分分開。這讓最喧鬧的旁觀者失望，卻使證人願意再次出面。

A second bundle of observations brought the consequences closer to ordinary life: The inspection revealed a small fracture in the connector supporting the temporary generator. Technicians learned to harness surplus power without bypassing the required safety checks. Each operator wore headphones to hear control-room instructions over the storm warnings. The clinic reviewed its oxygen intake records to calculate emergency battery demand. In the courtyard, a troupe rehearsed a ceremonial dance called the Maiden's Promise, though its lyrics had been rewritten to glorify the war. Nemi marked what could be verified and left the rest open to challenge.

第二疊觀察紀錄使後果貼近普通人的生活： 檢查發現支撐臨時發電機的接頭有一道小裂縫。 技師學習利用剩餘電力，不繞過必要安全檢查。 每位操作員戴耳機，以在風暴警報聲中聽清控制室指示。 診所檢視氧氣攝入紀錄，計算緊急電池需求。 庭院裡，一個劇團排練名為〈少女的承諾〉的儀式舞蹈，但歌詞早被改成頌揚戰爭。 妮米標記能查證的部分，其餘保留給人質疑。

There were two tempting shortcuts. One would protect the reputation of Chancellor Varos and leave the public guessing. The other would expose every private detail at once and call that transparency. Liora rejected both. The issue was whether a miracle may be taken from a citizen without consent, not whether the team could win an argument in a single afternoon. A smaller step, supported by independent observers, could protect people while the wider dispute remained open.

有兩種誘人的捷徑：一種保住瓦洛斯宰相的名聲，讓大眾自行猜測；另一種把所有私人細節一次揭露，並稱之為透明。莉歐拉兩者都拒絕。核心是奇蹟能否未經同意就向公民索取代價，不是團隊能否在一個下午贏得爭論。由獨立觀察者支持的一小步，即使爭議未結，也能先保護民眾。

The team kept the concrete observations alongside the disputed theory: A young apprentice described an accusation against Daven: he had supposedly stolen a design for offshore heat collectors from the academy. Now she allowed them to enter a sealed lecture hall, but only as prospective witnesses, not celebrated heroes. A resident asked for a referendum on the long-term public ownership of the canal land. Daven invited Lira into the old workshop where they had learned to refine copper anchors. The nurses could finally rejoice when the new clinic batteries passed their first test. Nothing in these records excused the people who had made the dangerous decision.

團隊把具體觀察與有爭議的理論並列保存： 一名年輕學徒說，達文據稱偷走學院的離岸熱能採集器設計。 如今她允許他們進入封閉講堂，但只能以潛在證人身分入內，不能當成受歡迎的英雄。 一名居民要求針對運河土地的長期公共所有權舉辦公投。 達文帶莉拉進入舊工坊，他們以前在這裡學習精煉銅製媒介。 新診所電池通過首次測試後，護理師終於能高興起來。 紀錄中沒有任何一項能替做出危險決定的人開脫。

There were children behind the temple steps who could remember the taste of bread but not the people who had taught them to bake it. Liora did not mistake their silence for gratitude. Every miracle that filled a bowl and emptied a memory left a different kind of hunger behind.

神殿階梯後方的孩子記得麵包滋味，卻不記得教自己烤麵包的人。莉歐拉沒有把沉默誤認為感激。每場填飽碗、卻掏空記憶的奇蹟，都會留下另一種飢餓。

The record continued with small facts that would be lost in a confident speech: Some shareholders began to resent the safety veto, calling it an obstacle to profit. The operating room remained serene after its generator switched smoothly to backup power. A neighborhood pastor gave a short sermon about caring for people before property. The board refused to shuffle expenses between accounts to make the trial look cheaper. A singer borrowed Lira's scarf and explained that her tenant family had been evicted when army engineers demanded their land. Taken alone, no detail settled the case; together they showed why a public answer mattered.

紀錄接著列出那些在自信演說中容易消失的小事實： 部分股東開始怨恨安全否決權，稱它阻礙獲利。 手術室的發電機順利切換備電後，室內依舊平靜。 社區牧師簡短講道，提醒人們應先照顧人，再談財產。 董事會拒絕在帳戶間調換支出，營造試辦更便宜的假象。 一位歌手借了莉拉的圍巾，說自己的租戶家庭因軍方工程師徵地而遭驅逐。 單獨看來沒有一項細節能結案，合在一起卻說明了公開答覆為何重要。

The chapter ended without an easy reassurance. Liora had learned one thing that could be checked and another that still needed an answer. Nemi wrote both on the same page so that relief would not erase doubt. Their decision had moved the crisis forward, but it had also drawn the attention of Chancellor Varos. By morning, the next question would reach people far beyond this room.

這一天沒有簡單的安慰。莉歐拉知道了一件能查證的事，也留下另一件尚待回答的事。妮米把兩者寫在同一頁，免得鬆一口氣就抹掉疑問。他們的決定使危機向前，也引起瓦洛斯宰相注意。明早下一個問題將傳到房間外更遠的地方。
