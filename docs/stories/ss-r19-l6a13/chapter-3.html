入冬前的投票

A Vote Before Winter

神明集體罷工，人類開始選舉新神 · 第 3 / 25 章 · 5000–6000 · 英中對照 · 校訂中

The first sign of trouble was not an explosion or a scream. It was a familiar procedure producing a result nobody could defend. Chancellor Varos announces a public election for the next god, promising warmth to every district that registers early. Liora checked the time, the witnesses, and the rule that authorized the action. The three did not agree. Outside, ordinary life continued, which made the error more frightening rather than less.

麻煩的第一個徵兆不是爆炸或尖叫，而是熟悉程序得出了誰也無法辯護的結果。瓦洛斯宰相宣布舉行新神選舉，承諾凡提前登記的街區都會得到暖氣。莉歐拉核對時間、證人與授權規則，三者卻互相矛盾。外頭的平凡生活照常進行，反而讓這個錯誤更加可怕。

A miracle never created energy or food from nothing; it moved a cost into a ledger, usually without telling the person who paid. The election bell counted a vote only when the person holding it could still say no.

奇蹟從不會憑空創造能源或食物，只會把代價轉進帳簿，而且常未告知付費者。只有選民仍能說不，投票鐘才會計入選票。

There were children behind the temple steps who could remember the taste of bread but not the people who had taught them to bake it. Liora did not mistake their silence for gratitude. Every miracle that filled a bowl and emptied a memory left a different kind of hunger behind.

神殿階梯後方的孩子記得麵包滋味，卻不記得教自己烤麵包的人。莉歐拉沒有把沉默誤認為感激。每場填飽碗、卻掏空記憶的奇蹟，都會留下另一種飢餓。

Beyond the meeting room, people were already finding ways to survive. They traded tools, shared the location of safe passages, and argued over fair turns without waiting for permission. Liora listened longer than the schedule allowed. Nemi reminded the officials that no policy was complete until it made sense to the people expected to live with it. Their notes began with those ordinary acts of cooperation.

會議室外，人們早已開始尋求生存方法：交換工具、分享安全路線的位置，無須等候許可便爭論公平輪班。莉歐拉聽得比議程允許的更久。妮米提醒官員，政策若不能讓實際生活其中的人理解，就不算完整。他們從這些平凡合作開始記錄。

The overlooked details in the witness file made the question harder, not easier: An advisory plaque still praised the Empire's conquest of the eastern frontier, but most houses were empty. A mural celebrated royal conquest, but the community diary described families driven from their homes. Lira searched for evidence of a conspiracy rather than assume it. Someone had left a cucumber sandwich beside the abandoned recording equipment. The portable radio emitted a feeble signal from the edge of the substation. Nothing in these records excused the people who had made the dangerous decision.

證人檔案裡被忽視的細節使問題更加複雜，而不是更簡單： 告示牌仍讚揚帝國征服東部邊境，但大多數房屋已空無一人。 壁畫歌頌王室征服，社群日記卻記錄被迫離家的家庭。 莉拉沒有先認定陰謀存在，而是尋找證據。 有人在廢置錄音設備旁留下一份小黃瓜三明治。 便攜無線電從變電站邊緣發出微弱訊號。 紀錄中沒有任何一項能替做出危險決定的人開脫。

Then the next piece of evidence changed the shape of the case. An unregistered quarter receives heat anyway, proving the promise is not a law of nature. Liora read the statement twice, searching for the sentence that would make the contradiction disappear. There was none. Nemi did not reach for a dramatic accusation; instead, they listed three people who could verify the finding separately. The delay would be unpopular, but a dramatic discovery deserved more care, not less.

接著出現的新證據改變了整起案件的輪廓。未登記的街區卻仍然得到熱能，證明宰相的承諾不是不可改變的自然法則。莉歐拉把陳述讀了兩遍，試圖找出能消除矛盾的句子，卻沒有找到。妮米沒有立刻提出轟動指控，而是列出三位可分別驗證發現的人。延遲會不受歡迎，但重大發現更需要謹慎。

A second bundle of observations brought the consequences closer to ordinary life: A firecracker exploded outside the gate, and the officers checked whether it was a signal or prank. The mechanic began to fume when his accurate warning was described as a rumor. The delivery map was stained with grease from the night-shift kitchen. A careful inspection might lessen the danger to villages still connected to the hidden pipes. An abandoned river liner stood at the dock, its passenger deck still painted with cheerful blue stars. Taken alone, no detail settled the case; together they showed why a public answer mattered.

第二疊觀察紀錄使後果貼近普通人的生活： 門外一枚爆竹炸響，官員檢查究竟是暗號還是惡作劇。 技師見自己的準確警告被說成謠言，開始氣憤。 送貨地圖沾有夜班廚房的油脂。 仔細檢查或許能減輕仍連著暗管的村莊所面臨的危險。 一艘廢棄的河上客輪停在碼頭，乘客甲板上仍畫著開朗的藍色星星。 單獨看來沒有一項細節能結案，合在一起卻說明了公開答覆為何重要。

The debate sharpened when Chancellor Varos demanded a decision before the evidence could be copied. Liora replied that urgency and secrecy were not the same thing. They established a temporary limit, published the reason for it, and scheduled an independent review. The result satisfied nobody completely. That was acceptable; the purpose of the process was to make abuse harder, not to produce applause.

當瓦洛斯宰相要求在證據複製前就做決定，辯論變得尖銳。莉歐拉回應說緊急和保密不是同一回事。團隊訂立暫時限制、公開理由，並排定獨立審查。結果沒有讓任何人完全滿意，但程序的目的本來就不是得到掌聲，而是使濫權更困難。

The team kept the concrete observations alongside the disputed theory: The old orphanage had been promised funding that was never entered in its bank account. Residents compared the recurring blackouts to a plague that never seemed to end. The community requested recreational lighting for its sports field only after clinics were safe. The video editor warned that online debate had become savage toward the unknown driver. A broken swing moved in the wind beside a shrub growing through the steps. Liora asked the clerk to preserve the original wording rather than improve the story.

團隊把具體觀察與有爭議的理論並列保存： 舊孤兒院被承諾的經費從未存入銀行帳戶。 居民把反覆神殿沉默比作似乎永不結束的災難。 社區表示診所安全後，再考慮運動場休閒照明。 影像編輯警告網路討論對不明司機已變得殘酷。 破損的鞦韆在風中搖動，階梯縫裡長出灌木。 莉歐拉要求書記保存原本措辭，而不是把故事修飾得更好聽。

There were children behind the temple steps who could remember the taste of bread but not the people who had taught them to bake it. Liora did not mistake their silence for gratitude. Every miracle that filled a bowl and emptied a memory left a different kind of hunger behind.

神殿階梯後方的孩子記得麵包滋味，卻不記得教自己烤麵包的人。莉歐拉沒有把沉默誤認為感激。每場填飽碗、卻掏空記憶的奇蹟，都會留下另一種飢餓。

The record continued with small facts that would be lost in a confident speech: The technician explained that a local slang term had been mistaken for an equipment code. The second camera could surpass the first in clarity without identifying the people shown. For workers the damaged seal came to symbolize years of ignored safety warnings. A textile pattern on the borrowed coat helped establish when it had been photographed. The clinic kept its vaccine refrigerator running while the citywide evidence review continued. Nemi marked what could be verified and left the rest open to challenge.

紀錄接著列出那些在自信演說中容易消失的小事實： 技師解釋某個地方俚語被誤認成設備代碼。 第二台攝影機雖然清晰度勝過第一台，仍無法辨認人物。 對工人而言，破損封條象徵多年被忽略的安全警告。 外借大衣的紡織圖案協助確認它何時被拍攝。 全市證據審查期間，診所仍維持疫苗冰箱運作。 妮米標記能查證的部分，其餘保留給人質疑。

A message arrived after the public doors had closed. It carried no grand declaration, just a name and a time that did not match the official schedule. Liora resisted the urge to call it proof. Nemi opened a fresh page in the journal and recorded the discrepancy exactly. They had won time, not certainty, and there were still people who might lose everything if they hurried.

公共大門關閉後，一則訊息送到。它沒有豪言壯語，只有一個名字和一個對不上官方時程的時間。莉歐拉克制住把它當成證明的衝動。妮米翻開日誌新的一頁，逐字記錄差異。他們贏得的是時間，不是確定性；若急於求成，仍有人可能失去一切。
