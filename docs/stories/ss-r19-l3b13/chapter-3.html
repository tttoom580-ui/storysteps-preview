A Clock Without Hands

未婚夫每說一次愛我，時間就倒退一分鐘 · 第 3 / 25 章 · 2000–3000 · 英中對照 · 校訂中

Elara finds a handless clock in the old nursery, beside a portrait of the duchess. Elara felt the meaning of this new event before the full crowd understood it. In Clockhaven, each unusual sign could become a rumor by evening, and rumors were hard to pull back once they had spread. Julian stood beside the nearest witness and asked for the sequence of actions, not a dramatic conclusion. Some details contradicted what they had learned the day before. That was disappointing and useful at the same time. Elara opened a fresh page, wrote the date at the top, and decided the truth would need more than one voice.

艾拉拉在舊育兒室找到無指針時鐘，旁邊掛著公爵夫人的畫像。 群眾尚未完全理解時，艾拉拉已感到這件新事的分量。在鐘港，每個異常徵兆到了晚上都可能變成謠言，而謠言一旦傳開就很難收回。朱利安站到最近的證人身邊，詢問行動的先後順序，而非戲劇性的結論。有些細節與昨天得到的資訊矛盾，既令人失望也很有用。艾拉拉翻開新的一頁，在上方寫下日期，決定真相需要不止一個人的聲音。

There was a practical problem behind the dramatic story. A route could look short on paper and still be impossible for a tired person to use. Elara walked it at a normal pace while Julian counted the steps and listed obstacles. At the narrowest point they stopped because the safest answer was not the fastest one. Someone suggested using the silver clock as proof that everything was under control. Elara refused the easy claim. An impressive object could not replace a careful test, especially when strangers might have to trust it without knowing the story behind it.

戲劇性的故事背後有個實際問題。路線在紙上看起來很短，對疲憊的人卻可能走不了。艾拉拉以正常速度走一遍，朱利安數著步數並列出障礙。他們在最窄處停下，因為最安全的答案未必最快。有人提議用銀色時鐘證明一切都在掌握中，艾拉拉拒絕了這種輕率說法。再令人印象深刻的物件，也不能取代仔細測試，尤其當陌生人必須在不知道背景時相信它。

News of the plan reached Lady Morwen before sunset. The official reply was quick, polished, and strangely certain. Elara read it twice, looking for the questions it refused to answer. Julian noticed that it described a public problem as a private disagreement, although many witnesses had been affected. They did not accuse anyone merely because the answer sounded unpleasant. Instead, they requested the original documents and the name of the person responsible for storing them. A rival deserved the chance to respond to a clear question, but not the right to hide evidence behind an impressive signature.

日落前，計畫的消息傳到莫文夫人。官方回覆快速、漂亮，而且確定得古怪。艾拉拉讀了兩次，尋找其中刻意避開的問題。朱利安注意到它把影響多名證人的公共問題描述成私人爭執。他們沒有因回答難聽就隨意指控，而是要求原始文件與保管人的姓名。對手有機會回答清楚的問題，卻沒有權利躲在漂亮簽名後藏匿證據。

During a pause in the investigation, Julian unfolded an old language ledger. Five marked entries on the first line read "background", "bamboo", "bundle", "chat", "chip". The next line held "civil", "dealer", "deepen", "democratic", "dine". None was a magic password or an answer by itself; each was a fragment of a larger history. Elara asked who had written the marks, why they had been kept, and whether they belonged to the same account. It would have been easy to declare the puzzle solved simply because the words were now visible. Instead, they copied the terms, recorded the source, and returned to the unresolved problem outside the room.

調查暫停時，朱利安攤開一本舊語詞紀錄。第一行的五個註記寫著「background」、「bamboo」、「bundle」、「chat」、「chip」；下一行則有「civil」、「dealer」、「deepen」、「democratic」、「dine」。它們都不是魔法密碼，也無法單獨解答問題；每個詞只是更大段歷史的片段。艾拉拉詢問誰寫下這些字、為何保存，以及它們是否屬於同一份記錄。只因文字可見就宣稱解開謎題很容易，但他們只抄下詞條、標明來源，便回到室外仍未解決的問題。

The discussion was interrupted by somebody who had not been invited. The visitor carried a story about Lady Morwen, but the story mixed known facts with guesses. Elara asked the visitor to separate what had been seen from what had merely been heard. Julian offered water and a chair, not a promise that every claim would be believed. A useful detail emerged only after the visitor stopped trying to sound certain. The team compared it with the earlier notes and found a new question. They could not solve it that night, so they wrote it down without pretending otherwise.

一位不請自來的訪客打斷討論。此人帶來關於莫文夫人的故事，卻把已知事實和猜測混在一起。艾拉拉請訪客分開親眼所見與道聽塗說。朱利安遞上水和椅子，卻不承諾全部相信。訪客不再假裝很確定後，才說出有用細節。團隊對照早期筆記，發現新問題。當晚無法解決，他們便把問題寫下，不假裝已經找到答案。

During a pause in the investigation, Julian unfolded an old language ledger. Five marked entries on the first line read "doughnut", "downtown", "leap", "mighty", "pigeon". The next line held "poll", "poverty", "react", "sake", "substance". None was a magic password or an answer by itself; each was a fragment of a larger history. Elara asked who had written the marks, why they had been kept, and whether they belonged to the same account. It would have been easy to declare the puzzle solved simply because the words were now visible. Instead, they copied the terms, recorded the source, and returned to the unresolved problem outside the room.

調查暫停時，朱利安攤開一本舊語詞紀錄。第一行的五個註記寫著「doughnut」、「downtown」、「leap」、「mighty」、「pigeon」；下一行則有「poll」、「poverty」、「react」、「sake」、「substance」。它們都不是魔法密碼，也無法單獨解答問題；每個詞只是更大段歷史的片段。艾拉拉詢問誰寫下這些字、為何保存，以及它們是否屬於同一份記錄。只因文字可見就宣稱解開謎題很容易，但他們只抄下詞條、標明來源，便回到室外仍未解決的問題。

After the others left, Julian asked Elara whether working together still felt like a choice. Feelings had grown while danger filled their days, but that did not give one person power over another. Elara said that trust needed room for disagreement, even when the answer hurt. Julian accepted this without trying to turn it into a promise. For a moment they stood close enough to hear each other's breath, then returned to the unfinished work. They did not need a crowd to decide how they felt. The courage they were learning was quieter than any spell and more difficult to fake.

其他人離開後，朱利安問艾拉拉，一起工作如今是否仍是自由選擇。危險充滿日常時，感情也漸漸長出來，但這不給任何人支配另一人的權利。艾拉拉說，信任必須容得下不同意見，即使答案可能傷人。朱利安接受了，沒有把話逼成承諾。兩人有一刻近得聽見彼此呼吸，然後繼續未完工作。他們不需要人群替自己決定感情。正學習的勇氣比任何法術都安靜，也更難假裝。

Before leaving, Elara wrote one line at the bottom of the page: the day's answer could still be corrected. Julian checked that people affected by the decision could read it and reply. A messenger brought news that the next meeting would concern 'The First Witness'. Neither knew precisely what this new matter would demand. They did know what should not be traded away to save a few hours: honest records, a safe path, and the right of someone weaker to disagree. Elara carried the unfinished notes toward Clockhaven, listening to the footsteps behind them and thinking of the question that morning would bring.

離開前，艾拉拉在紙頁底下寫道：今天的答案仍然可以訂正。朱利安確認受決定影響的人能讀到並回應。信使傳來消息，下次會議將處理〈第一位證人〉。兩人都不確定新事件要求什麼，卻知道哪些東西不能只為節省幾小時就被交換掉：誠實紀錄、安全道路，以及弱者提出異議的權利。艾拉拉帶著未完成的筆記走向鐘港，聽著背後腳步聲，想著明早會出現的問題。
