Elara uses the reversal to prevent a falling lantern, but learns that changing an event does not erase the hurt it caused. Elara felt the meaning of this new event before the full crowd understood it. In Clockhaven, each unusual sign could become a rumor by evening, and rumors were hard to pull back once they had spread. Julian stood beside the nearest witness and asked for the sequence of actions, not a dramatic conclusion. Some details contradicted what they had learned the day before. That was disappointing and useful at the same time. Elara opened a fresh page, wrote the date at the top, and decided the truth would need more than one voice.

艾拉拉利用倒流避免燈籠墜落，卻明白改變事件不會消除造成的傷害。 群眾尚未完全理解時，艾拉拉已感到這件新事的分量。在鐘港，每個異常徵兆到了晚上都可能變成謠言，而謠言一旦傳開就很難收回。朱利安站到最近的證人身邊，詢問行動的先後順序，而非戲劇性的結論。有些細節與昨天得到的資訊矛盾，既令人失望也很有用。艾拉拉翻開新的一頁，在上方寫下日期，決定真相需要不止一個人的聲音。

The first test gave the team a little hope and one new problem. Elara insisted they record both results. Julian watched the boundary of the working area and told observers to keep their distance. The second test was slower, but it showed exactly where the old method failed. A careless account would have called the effort a complete success. They knew better. Each person signed the record only for the part personally checked, leaving space for another witness to disagree. The honest gaps in the notes were not weaknesses; they were signs that the writers understood the limits of what they knew.

第一次測試帶來一點希望，也帶來一個新問題。艾拉拉堅持同時記下兩項結果。朱利安守著工作區邊界，請旁觀者保持距離。第二次測試更慢，卻準確指出舊方法的失敗位置。草率的紀錄會把行動說成完全成功，但他們知道不能如此。每個人只為親自確認的部分簽名，也留空間讓其他證人提出異議。筆記裡誠實的空白不是弱點，而是執筆者理解自身所知有限的證明。

'Love is a choice, not a spell; neither person may be bound by words stolen from the past.' Julian said, reading from the page they had written together. The sentence sounded simple until it was tested by actual need. A stranger asked for an exception, claiming the danger was too urgent for ordinary rules. Elara listened and checked whether anyone else would pay for that exception. The answer was not comfortable. It required time, an explanation, and a promise to review the decision when new evidence appeared. Elara could feel the crowd waiting for a dramatic command. Instead, the group made a smaller choice they could explain to the people who would live with it.

「愛是選擇，不是咒語；任何人都不該被從過去偷走的話語綁住。」朱利安讀出兩人一起寫下的句子。這話聽來簡單，直到真正的需求開始考驗它。一名陌生人以危險太急迫為由要求例外。艾拉拉傾聽後先確認是否有其他人得替這個例外付代價。答案並不舒服：他們需要時間、說明，以及在新證據出現時重審的承諾。艾拉拉感覺人群等著一道戲劇性命令，團隊卻做出較小、能向承擔後果的人解釋的選擇。

During a pause in the investigation, Julian unfolded an old language ledger. Five marked entries on the first line read "bleed", "bore", "cabin", "database", "democracy". The next line held "faint", "fright", "horn", "laughter", "lover". None was a magic password or an answer by itself; each was a fragment of a larger history. Elara asked who had written the marks, why they had been kept, and whether they belonged to the same account. It would have been easy to declare the puzzle solved simply because the words were now visible. Instead, they copied the terms, recorded the source, and returned to the unresolved problem outside the room.

調查暫停時，朱利安攤開一本舊語詞紀錄。第一行的五個註記寫著「bleed」、「bore」、「cabin」、「database」、「democracy」；下一行則有「faint」、「fright」、「horn」、「laughter」、「lover」。它們都不是魔法密碼，也無法單獨解答問題；每個詞只是更大段歷史的片段。艾拉拉詢問誰寫下這些字、為何保存，以及它們是否屬於同一份記錄。只因文字可見就宣稱解開謎題很容易，但他們只抄下詞條、標明來源，便回到室外仍未解決的問題。

There was still a distance between understanding a problem and solving it. Elara and Julian walked through Clockhaven with a fresh drawing, asking the same question in different places. A child noticed a mark that adults had missed. An elder remembered a change made years before. The clues did not fit together at once, but neither did they cancel each other out. Elara marked each source and refused to invent a missing answer. They returned with dirty shoes and a clearer idea of what needed a second visit. Sometimes the small witness was the one who showed the safest way forward.

理解問題與解決問題之間仍有距離。艾拉拉和朱利安拿著新畫的圖走過鐘港，在不同地方問相同問題。一個孩子注意到大人忽略的記號，一位長者想起多年前的變化。線索沒有立即拼合，卻也不互相抵銷。艾拉拉標註每項來源，不替空白捏造答案。他們帶著髒鞋子回來，更清楚哪些地方需要再訪。有時指向安全道路的，正是最不起眼的證人。

During a pause in the investigation, Julian unfolded an old language ledger. Five marked entries on the first line read "missing", "roar", "shortly", "sink", "skillful". The next line held "splash", "tune", "wander", "weapon", "weekly". None was a magic password or an answer by itself; each was a fragment of a larger history. Elara asked who had written the marks, why they had been kept, and whether they belonged to the same account. It would have been easy to declare the puzzle solved simply because the words were now visible. Instead, they copied the terms, recorded the source, and returned to the unresolved problem outside the room.

調查暫停時，朱利安攤開一本舊語詞紀錄。第一行的五個註記寫著「missing」、「roar」、「shortly」、「sink」、「skillful」；下一行則有「splash」、「tune」、「wander」、「weapon」、「weekly」。它們都不是魔法密碼，也無法單獨解答問題；每個詞只是更大段歷史的片段。艾拉拉詢問誰寫下這些字、為何保存，以及它們是否屬於同一份記錄。只因文字可見就宣稱解開謎題很容易，但他們只抄下詞條、標明來源，便回到室外仍未解決的問題。

After the others left, Julian asked Elara whether working together still felt like a choice. Feelings had grown while danger filled their days, but that did not give one person power over another. Elara said that trust needed room for disagreement, even when the answer hurt. Julian accepted this without trying to turn it into a promise. For a moment they stood close enough to hear each other's breath, then returned to the unfinished work. They did not need a crowd to decide how they felt. The courage they were learning was quieter than any spell and more difficult to fake.

其他人離開後，朱利安問艾拉拉，一起工作如今是否仍是自由選擇。危險充滿日常時，感情也漸漸長出來，但這不給任何人支配另一人的權利。艾拉拉說，信任必須容得下不同意見，即使答案可能傷人。朱利安接受了，沒有把話逼成承諾。兩人有一刻近得聽見彼此呼吸，然後繼續未完工作。他們不需要人群替自己決定感情。正學習的勇氣比任何法術都安靜，也更難假裝。

Before leaving, Elara wrote one line at the bottom of the page: the day's answer could still be corrected. Julian checked that people affected by the decision could read it and reply. A messenger brought news that the next meeting would concern 'The Old Bride's Name'. Neither knew precisely what this new matter would demand. They did know what should not be traded away to save a few hours: honest records, a safe path, and the right of someone weaker to disagree. Elara carried the unfinished notes toward Clockhaven, listening to the footsteps behind them and thinking of the question that morning would bring.

離開前，艾拉拉在紙頁底下寫道：今天的答案仍然可以訂正。朱利安確認受決定影響的人能讀到並回應。信使傳來消息，下次會議將處理〈舊新娘的名字〉。兩人都不確定新事件要求什麼，卻知道哪些東西不能只為節省幾小時就被交換掉：誠實紀錄、安全道路，以及弱者提出異議的權利。艾拉拉帶著未完成的筆記走向鐘港，聽著背後腳步聲，想著明早會出現的問題。
