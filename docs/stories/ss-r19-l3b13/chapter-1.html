消失的一分鐘

The Missing Minute

'I love you,' Julian whispered. Champagne rose from the floor into a broken glass. A fallen ribbon flew back to Elara's shoulder. A candle that had gone out burned again. Then Julian drew breath and said exactly the same three words, wearing the same frightened expression. Elara caught his wrist before the glass broke a second time. The engagement had been announced that morning. By evening she already knew that something was terribly wrong with time, and neither of them knew whether their promise had caused it. She held his hand, not to stop love, but to stop one more missing minute.

「我愛妳。」朱利安輕聲說。灑在地上的香檳倒流回破碎的杯子，落下的緞帶飛回艾拉拉肩上，幾秒前熄滅的蠟燭重新燃起。接著，朱利安吸了一口氣，用同樣驚惶的表情說出一模一樣的三個字。杯子第二次破裂前，艾拉拉抓住他的手腕。兩人今早才宣布訂婚，到了晚上，她已知道時間出了可怕的問題，卻沒人能確定是不是承諾造成的。她握住他的手不是為了阻止愛，而是為了阻止另一分鐘消失。

The discussion was interrupted by somebody who had not been invited. The visitor carried a story about Lady Morwen, but the story mixed known facts with guesses. Elara asked the visitor to separate what had been seen from what had merely been heard. Julian offered water and a chair, not a promise that every claim would be believed. A useful detail emerged only after the visitor stopped trying to sound certain. The team compared it with the earlier notes and found a new question. They could not solve it that night, so they wrote it down without pretending otherwise.

一位不請自來的訪客打斷討論。此人帶來關於莫文夫人的故事，卻把已知事實和猜測混在一起。艾拉拉請訪客分開親眼所見與道聽塗說。朱利安遞上水和椅子，卻不承諾全部相信。訪客不再假裝很確定後，才說出有用細節。團隊對照早期筆記，發現新問題。當晚無法解決，他們便把問題寫下，不假裝已經找到答案。

A messenger reached Clockhaven carrying two conflicting reports. Elara read them slowly rather than asking everyone to trust the louder witness. Julian placed the silver clock beside the papers and drew a line under the part they could verify. It was tempting to guess what had happened, especially when people were afraid. Yet one wrong guess could send a friend into danger. The group wrote down the times, the names, and the things nobody had seen clearly. Someone objected to wasting time. Elara replied that a moment spent checking facts was less expensive than a mistake that could not be repaired.

一名信使帶著兩份互相矛盾的報告抵達鐘港。艾拉拉慢慢讀完，沒有要大家相信聲音較大的證人。朱利安將銀色時鐘放在紙張旁，在可以確認的地方畫線。尤其人們害怕時，猜測發生了什麼很容易；但一次猜錯就可能把朋友送入危險。團隊記下時間、姓名和沒有人看清的事。有個人抗議浪費時間。艾拉拉回答，花一點時間查證，總比犯下無法補救的錯誤便宜。

During a pause in the investigation, Julian unfolded an old language ledger. Five marked entries on the first line read "berry", "circus", "deed", "fairy", "fancy". The next line held "heater", "hut", "jazz", "jet", "magician". None was a magic password or an answer by itself; each was a fragment of a larger history. Elara asked who had written the marks, why they had been kept, and whether they belonged to the same account. It would have been easy to declare the puzzle solved simply because the words were now visible. Instead, they copied the terms, recorded the source, and returned to the unresolved problem outside the room.

調查暫停時，朱利安攤開一本舊語詞紀錄。第一行的五個註記寫著「berry」、「circus」、「deed」、「fairy」、「fancy」；下一行則有「heater」、「hut」、「jazz」、「jet」、「magician」。它們都不是魔法密碼，也無法單獨解答問題；每個詞只是更大段歷史的片段。艾拉拉詢問誰寫下這些字、為何保存，以及它們是否屬於同一份記錄。只因文字可見就宣稱解開謎題很容易，但他們只抄下詞條、標明來源，便回到室外仍未解決的問題。

The first test gave the team a little hope and one new problem. Elara insisted they record both results. Julian watched the boundary of the working area and told observers to keep their distance. The second test was slower, but it showed exactly where the old method failed. A careless account would have called the effort a complete success. They knew better. Each person signed the record only for the part personally checked, leaving space for another witness to disagree. The honest gaps in the notes were not weaknesses; they were signs that the writers understood the limits of what they knew.

第一次測試帶來一點希望，也帶來一個新問題。艾拉拉堅持同時記下兩項結果。朱利安守著工作區邊界，請旁觀者保持距離。第二次測試更慢，卻準確指出舊方法的失敗位置。草率的紀錄會把行動說成完全成功，但他們知道不能如此。每個人只為親自確認的部分簽名，也留空間讓其他證人提出異議。筆記裡誠實的空白不是弱點，而是執筆者理解自身所知有限的證明。

During a pause in the investigation, Julian unfolded an old language ledger. Five marked entries on the first line read "miracle", "pint", "political", "quit", "rob". The next line held "screw", "signal", "spin", "tame", "various". None was a magic password or an answer by itself; each was a fragment of a larger history. Elara asked who had written the marks, why they had been kept, and whether they belonged to the same account. It would have been easy to declare the puzzle solved simply because the words were now visible. Instead, they copied the terms, recorded the source, and returned to the unresolved problem outside the room.

調查暫停時，朱利安攤開一本舊語詞紀錄。第一行的五個註記寫著「miracle」、「pint」、「political」、「quit」、「rob」；下一行則有「screw」、「signal」、「spin」、「tame」、「various」。它們都不是魔法密碼，也無法單獨解答問題；每個詞只是更大段歷史的片段。艾拉拉詢問誰寫下這些字、為何保存，以及它們是否屬於同一份記錄。只因文字可見就宣稱解開謎題很容易，但他們只抄下詞條、標明來源，便回到室外仍未解決的問題。

After the others left, Julian asked Elara whether working together still felt like a choice. Feelings had grown while danger filled their days, but that did not give one person power over another. Elara said that trust needed room for disagreement, even when the answer hurt. Julian accepted this without trying to turn it into a promise. For a moment they stood close enough to hear each other's breath, then returned to the unfinished work. They did not need a crowd to decide how they felt. The courage they were learning was quieter than any spell and more difficult to fake.

其他人離開後，朱利安問艾拉拉，一起工作如今是否仍是自由選擇。危險充滿日常時，感情也漸漸長出來，但這不給任何人支配另一人的權利。艾拉拉說，信任必須容得下不同意見，即使答案可能傷人。朱利安接受了，沒有把話逼成承諾。兩人有一刻近得聽見彼此呼吸，然後繼續未完工作。他們不需要人群替自己決定感情。正學習的勇氣比任何法術都安靜，也更難假裝。

Before leaving, Elara wrote one line at the bottom of the page: the day's answer could still be corrected. Julian checked that people affected by the decision could read it and reply. A messenger brought news that the next meeting would concern 'A Written Promise'. Neither knew precisely what this new matter would demand. They did know what should not be traded away to save a few hours: honest records, a safe path, and the right of someone weaker to disagree. Elara carried the unfinished notes toward Clockhaven, listening to the footsteps behind them and thinking of the question that morning would bring.

離開前，艾拉拉在紙頁底下寫道：今天的答案仍然可以訂正。朱利安確認受決定影響的人能讀到並回應。信使傳來消息，下次會議將處理〈書寫的承諾〉。兩人都不確定新事件要求什麼，卻知道哪些東西不能只為節省幾小時就被交換掉：誠實紀錄、安全道路，以及弱者提出異議的權利。艾拉拉帶著未完成的筆記走向鐘港，聽著背後腳步聲，想著明早會出現的問題。
