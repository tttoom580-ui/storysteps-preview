An anonymous complaint said a donor had been given access to recordings marked private. Yu requested the access log, restricted the affected accounts, and notified the speakers without identifying the complainant. The director objected to the interruption. The complaint arrived without a signature, but it included a time and a terminal number. Yu asked the technical team to preserve that period of the access log before anyone reset the machine. Han wanted to know who had complained. Mei asked instead which recordings could have been reached. The list was short enough to check file by file, although it took most of the afternoon. Yu prepared private notices that described the possible access without claiming that every listed file had been heard. Each recipient received the contact for asking further questions. The public statement gave the nature of the failure and the correction under way, leaving out details that would expose the same people again.

匿名申訴指控一名捐款人接觸了標為私密的錄音。林予調閱存取紀錄、限制相關帳戶，並在不揭露申訴人的前提下通知受訪者。館長反對流程被打斷。申訴沒有簽名，卻列時間與終端機編號。林予請技術組先保留該時段紀錄，才重設機器。韓館長想知道誰申訴，梅則問哪些錄音可能被開啟。清單不長，能逐檔檢查，仍花掉大半個下午。林予準備私人通知，描述可能存取，沒有聲稱每份檔案都被聽過。每位當事人收到追問窗口。公開聲明說明故障性質與正在做的修正，省略會再次暴露那些人的細節。

At a meeting with community attendants, Mei argued that a public institution could not demand trust while withholding basic information about its own errors. Yu agreed but drew a boundary around sensitive personal details.

在社區服務員會議上，梅說公共機構不能一邊隱瞞自身錯誤的基本資訊，一邊要求信任。林予同意，但也劃出保護私人敏感細節的界線。

The community science fair offered a gentler counterpoint to the audit. A chemist mixed harmless batter for an edible demonstration while a child imitated the chirp of a bird. An attendant used a loudspeaker to announce the schedule, and a census worker explained how families populate a district over time. Historical photographs showed oyster farmers near an iceberg-shaped sculpture and a martial arts school before an influenza outbreak. A lavish donor logo had been printed too close to a private testimony; Mei asked to move it. The team had to dispense information responsibly rather than dispense promises it could not fulfil. One volunteer let out a tired groan after a setback in the catalogue review. Yu told the skeptical group that their questions were invaluable, especially when a sponsor wanted the museum to withhold a problem. Yu asked volunteers to subscribe to the updated notices rather than depend on rumours passed through the corridors.

社區科學展為稽核提供較柔和的對照。化學師混合無害的可食麵糊作示範，孩子模仿鳥鳴。服務員用擴音器宣布時程，人口普查員解釋家庭如何逐漸聚居。老照片拍下冰山造形雕塑旁的牡蠣養殖戶，以及流感爆發前的武術學校。華麗的捐款人標誌印得離私密證詞太近，梅要求移開。團隊必須負責任地提供資訊，而非胡亂發放無法履行的承諾。目錄審查再遇挫折時，志工疲憊地呻吟。林予告訴持懷疑態度的人，問題非常寶貴，尤其當贊助人希望館方隱瞞困難時。林予請志工訂閱更新公告，而不要依賴走廊裡流傳的謠言。

Yan identified the access route and disabled a forgotten guest account. He then asked Yu whether she would have dinner with him after the review. She said no; her workday had been exhausting, and she did not want a conversation under pressure. After disabling the guest account, Yan asked another staff member to attempt the same route. The screen refused access. He recorded that result and the time the account had been closed, then handed the page to Yu before asking about dinner. Her refusal made him pause, but he did not reopen the work discussion as a reason to keep her there. At the station Yu bought a bowl of soup and sat where she could see the departure board. She read no private messages while eating. When a new technical update arrived, she marked it for the next workday and put the phone away. The evening remained hers even though the review was unfinished.

周晏找出存取路徑，關閉被遺忘的訪客帳號，接著問她審查後能否一起吃晚餐。林予拒絕。工作已讓她精疲力竭，她不想在壓力下談話。關閉訪客帳號後，周晏請另一名員工嘗試相同路徑，螢幕拒絕存取。他記下結果與關閉時間，把紙交給林予才問晚餐。她的拒絕讓他停頓，但他沒有重啟工作討論來留人。車站裡，林予買碗湯，坐在看得見離站告示的位置。吃飯時她沒有看私人訊息。新技術更新傳來，她標成下個工作日處理，便收起手機。審查尚未完成，晚上仍屬於她。

She ate alone at the station and watched passengers move through the evening. A refusal did not cancel their professional cooperation. She hoped he would understand the difference without asking her to teach it repeatedly.

她獨自在車站吃晚餐，看旅客走過。拒絕共餐並不取消工作合作。她希望周晏能理解其中差異，而不必一遍遍由她教導。
