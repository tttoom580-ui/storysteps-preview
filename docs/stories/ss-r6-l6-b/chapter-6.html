An Interview Beyond the River

你終於學會告白時，我已經準備離開 · 第 6 / 25 章 · 5000–6000 · 英中對照 · 校訂中

Yu took a morning train to North Shore for her interview. The director asked whether she could attain results while respecting a speaker's right to withdraw. She answered with Mrs. Wu's case, including the recording they had chosen not to display.

林予搭早班車去北岸面試。所長問她能否在尊重撤回權的同時達成成果。她以吳太太的案例作答，也說明那段他們選擇不公開的錄音。

The panel wanted an airtight plan for public collections, but Yu refused to pretend that consent could ever be reduced to a single perfect form. She proposed a visible review process with an independent appeal contact. One interviewer asked what she would do if a donor threatened to withdraw money when a speaker withdrew a recording. Yu asked whether the donation terms gave the donor authority over that recording. The panel member said they did not. She described how she would separate the funding discussion from the speaker's request and record both decisions. Another interviewer asked who would review her own judgment. She named the independent appeal contact in her proposal and explained that she would have to supply the records even if a decision were reversed. The panel grew quiet while they read. Yu resisted the urge to fill that silence with a promise that her method would make every dispute easy.

面試委員希望有滴水不漏的公共典藏計畫，林予卻拒絕假裝同意權能化成一張完美表單。她提議公開的審查程序與獨立申訴聯絡窗口。一名面試委員問，若受訪者撤回錄音，捐款人便威脅撤資，她會怎麼辦。林予先問捐贈條款是否給捐款人支配該錄音的權力，委員說沒有。她說明會分開處理資金討論與當事人申請，分別留下決定。另一人問誰審查她的判斷。她指出提案裡的獨立申訴窗口，解釋即使決定被推翻，自己也必須提供紀錄。委員安靜閱讀時，她忍住沒用『方法能讓每次爭議都容易』來填滿沉默。

The interview building hosted a temporary collection about food, politics, and natural history. A former congresswoman had donated a recipe for curry and an argument against food waste. An edible mushroom collection lay near an evergreen branch and the fin of a model fish. A little flake of paint floated in a sealed jar; a poster warned that vapor from solvents could damage a manuscript. Another room discussed merchandise sold to visitors and the monotony of jobs performed without rest. The children's guide invited families to roam rather than remain still, and to choose a pastime that did not create rubbish. A folk tale used the verb slay to describe a dragon's defeat, while a sly narrator reminded children that stories were not scientific reports. Yu appreciated a museum confident enough to distinguish imagination from fact and still hold each in esteem. Yu did not despise local trades, but during an eclipse demonstration she spotted redundancy in the captions and corrected it.

面試大樓裡設有食物、政治與自然史特展。前國會女議員捐出咖哩食譜，並留下反對食物浪費的辯論。可食菇類標本旁有常綠樹枝和魚模型的鰭。封罐中飄著一小片油漆，海報警告溶劑蒸氣可能傷害手稿。另一廳談遊客商品，以及無休工作帶來的單調。兒童導覽鼓勵家庭四處走動，選擇不製造垃圾的消遣。民間故事用『斬殺』描寫惡龍敗北，狡黠的旁白提醒孩子故事不是科學報告。林予欣賞一間能區分想像與事實、又同樣尊重兩者的博物館。林予並不輕視地方行業；日食展示時，她發現標示重複贅述，便加以更正。

Yan sent a short message asking whether the station tape had arrived safely. He did not ask where she was or why she was away. Yu replied with the inventory number and recognised, with surprise, how peaceful an ordinary professional exchange could feel.

周晏傳來簡短訊息，詢問車站錄音是否安全入庫。他沒有追問她的行蹤。林予回覆典藏編號，意外發現單純的工作交流也能令人安定。

On the return train she practised telling Han that her last day was unchanged. North Shore might not hire her. She still knew she wanted a workplace where the rules were stated before conflict, rather than invented when someone powerful objected. On the train she took out the question sheet she had prepared that morning. She had asked about staffing and the time available for answering withdrawal requests, but forgotten to ask where temporary employees could raise concerns. She added that question for any later interview. Across the aisle, a woman was rearranging flowers wrapped in newspaper; Yu watched a loose petal catch in the fold and thought of her own office drawer. It held eleven years of spare labels and old passwords written on scraps. Whatever North Shore decided, she would need to clear it carefully. She wrote a practical list for tomorrow, starting with the obsolete passwords rather than another speech to Han.

回程火車上，她練習告訴韓館長離職日不變。北岸不一定錄取她，但她確定想去一個規則在爭議前就寫清楚，而非權勢者反對時才臨時發明的地方。火車上，她拿出早上準備的問題紙。她問過人手與處理撤回要求的時間，卻忘了問臨時員工在哪裡反映疑慮，於是補上，留待可能的下次面試。走道對面一名女子整理報紙包著的花，一片花瓣卡在摺痕。林予想起辦公桌抽屜，裡面有十一年累積的備用標籤與寫在紙片的舊密碼。無論北岸如何決定，她都得仔細清理。她寫下明天的實際工作，從失效密碼開始，而不是再準備對館長演說。
