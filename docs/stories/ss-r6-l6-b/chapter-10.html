第一次試聽夜

The First Listening Night

你終於學會告白時，我已經準備離開 · 第 10 / 25 章 · 5000–6000 · 英中對照 · 校訂中

用互動版查詞、朗讀與收藏

The museum opened a small listening room for ten invited participants rather than the grand gala Han wanted. Every speaker could stop playback. Yu introduced the rule before the lights dimmed, then invited the guests to test the headphones themselves. A guest tried the stop control twice before taking her seat. The first time she pressed too lightly and looked toward Yan for help. He showed her the switch, then stepped back while she tested it herself. Yu kept the room lights on until everyone said they could find the controls. Han checked his watch, but nobody started the recording while the last participant was still adjusting a headset. When the song began, a man's shoulders dropped as he recognized the opening sound. He listened for a while and then chose to stop. The pause was brief, yet it changed the room: the instruction on the invitation had become something a guest could actually use.

館方只替十位受邀者開一場小型試聽，而非韓館長期待的盛大晚會。每位說話者都有權停止播放。林予在熄燈前說明規則，請來賓親自測試耳機。一名來賓坐下前試了兩次停止鍵。第一次按得太輕，她看向周晏求助。他指出開關，再退開讓她自己試。林予等每個人確認找得到控制鍵才熄燈。韓館長看手錶，最後一人還在調耳機時，仍沒有人先播放。歌聲開始，一名男子認出開頭聲音後肩膀放鬆。他聽了一會兒便選擇停止。停頓很短，卻改變房間：邀請函上的指示，成了來賓真正能使用的東西。

A volunteer noticed a label that called a disputed recording an official confession. Yu replaced the label with a precise description of the disagreement. She told Han that accurate uncertainty was better than a confident injustice. The volunteer showed Yu the word 'confession' on a printed card and asked who had supplied it. The source sheet described an interview with two conflicting accounts. Yu asked the speaker which description matched what had been agreed, then checked that answer against the form. The replacement label named the disagreement without deciding a question the evidence did not settle. Han said the longer wording would be harder to fit. A designer reduced the decorative border instead of shrinking the print. Yu watched the volunteer read the new version from an ordinary viewing distance. The card was ready only when its words and its size both allowed a visitor to understand what kind of recording would follow.

志工發現一張標示把有爭議的錄音稱作官方自白。林予改成精確說明異議的文字。她告訴韓館長，準確的不確定，比自信的冤屈更好。志工指出卡片上的『自白』，問是誰提供的字。來源表描述的其實是含有兩種矛盾說法的訪談。林予問受訪者哪種描述符合協議，再拿回答對照表格。替換標籤寫出分歧，沒有替證據未定的問題下判決。韓館長說字太長放不下。設計師縮掉裝飾邊框，沒有縮小字體。林予看志工從一般距離閱讀。文字與尺寸都能讓訪客理解接下來是哪種錄音，卡片才算完成。

The night began with playful recordings of a puppet that could animate a history lesson, followed by a child performing quick arithmetic beside a cement factory model. A guide called the sound awesome, then explained why the original workers might not have agreed. Old reports spoke of a confederation divided into opposing factions and a crackdown that caused widespread injustice. A drama group staged dual perspectives on a crocodile hunt, careful not to lure viewers into thinking the spectacle was documentary truth. An obstinate collector argued that the labels should equalize every opinion; Yu answered that evidence and speculation could not be given equal weight. An actor mimed a pickpocket and staggered theatrically while a punctual attendant demonstrated restraint. The audience enjoyed the intrigue, but the programme distinguished fiction from testimony in large, readable letters. Visitors tasted celery soup; Yu noted that an unfunded screening could incur costs for a school already short of money.

試聽夜先播放會讓歷史課活起來的木偶錄音，接著是孩子在水泥廠模型旁做心算。導覽員稱讚聲音精彩，也解釋當年的工人未必同意錄製。舊報告談邦聯內的派系對立，以及鎮壓造成的不正義。劇團用雙重視角演出獵捕鱷魚，提醒觀眾戲劇不是紀實。固執的收藏家要求各種觀點完全平等，林予說證據不能與猜測同等看待。演員模仿扒手，誇張地踉蹌，準時到場的服務員則示範克制。觀眾享受懸疑，節目仍以大字清楚區分虛構與證詞。訪客品嘗芹菜湯；林予提醒，沒有經費支援的放映會讓本已缺錢的學校增加開支。

Yan operated the controls and offered no speech about their private past. During the final song, his eyes met Yu's only once. He lowered the volume when a participant asked, proving that the new protocol could work in an ordinary room.

周晏操作控制台，沒有談私人往事。最後一首歌時，他只與林予對望一次。參與者要求降低音量，他立即照做，證明新程序能在普通房間運作。

After the guests left, Yu received an invitation to a second interview at North Shore. She did not tell Yan. She stepped outside with the letter held close, aware that the most important door of the evening opened somewhere beyond the museum.

來賓離開後，林予收到北岸第二次面試邀請。她沒告訴周晏，只把信握緊走到室外，明白當晚最重要的門，正在博物館之外打開。
