The Notice Beneath the Clock

你終於學會告白時，我已經準備離開 · 第 1 / 25 章 · 5000–6000 · 英中對照

Lin Yu filed her resignation before the trustees convened. Director Han had issued a directive to accelerate the annual exhibition, but the permission ledger was missing twenty-seven signatures. She told him that the date on the wall could not outrank the people on the tapes.

林予在董事們開會前送交辭呈。韓館長已發出指示，要提早年度展期，但授權簿缺少二十七個簽名。她告訴館長，牆上的日期不能凌駕錄音裡的人。

Han clasped the folder and warned that her departure would heighten public anxiety. Yu answered that withholding a problem would create a worse precedent. Mei, a community trustee, quietly asked for an independent count rather than another emotional argument. Lin Yu had spent eleven years recognizing the handwriting on these forms. Some signatures were firm, others shaky, and several speakers had asked a staff member to write for them. That morning she placed a blank sheet beside the ledger and numbered the missing permissions without filling any gaps from memory. Han asked whether a familiar voice could count as evidence of agreement. She turned the speaker's form toward him: its research box was marked, its exhibition box untouched. Mei began checking the next file. The first count took long enough for the meeting outside to start without them, but Yu stayed until they could distinguish missing paperwork from permission that had never been given.

韓館長緊抓資料夾，警告她的離職會加深公眾不安。林予回答，掩蓋問題才會建立更糟的先例。社區受託人梅則要求獨立清點，不再進行情緒爭論。林予做了十一年，認得表格上的筆跡。有些簽名有力，有些顫抖，還有人請館員代筆。那天早上，她把白紙放在授權簿旁，逐一編號，沒有憑記憶補空格。韓館長問熟悉的聲音能否算同意的證據。她把當事人的表格轉向他：研究欄已勾，展覽欄卻空著。梅開始核對下一份。第一次清點花了很久，外面的會議已開始，林予仍留下，直到能區分遺失的文件與從未取得的授權。

The lobby catalogue made the problem visible in miniature. One recording described a bear cub and a horse at full gallop; another concerned dental care in an old workers' district. A former dictator had once demanded unconditional loyalty from every janitor in the station. Beside that account stood a pilgrim's description of a roadside shrine. The museum's motto was 'Every voice returns home,' yet a panel called a forced relocation a voluntary transplant. A school group read about farmers who hoped to reap a better harvest, while a label for a glass dwelling misidentified its maker. Mei asked whether an archive that could preserve every detail was also capable of correcting an error. Yu pointed to the unchecked permission box beside the display. That small empty square, she said, mattered more than the grandeur of the exhibit.

大廳展件把問題縮成容易看見的模樣。一段錄音描述小熊與疾馳的馬，另一段談舊工人區的牙科醫療。曾有獨裁者要求車站每位清潔工無條件效忠；旁邊是朝聖旅人對路邊神龕的描述。館訓寫著『讓每個聲音回家』，展板卻把強迫遷居稱作自願移居。學生讀著農民期待收穫的故事；玻璃住屋模型的製作者也被標錯。梅問：保存細節的檔案館，能不能改正錯誤？林予指向展板旁未勾選的授權格。她說，這個小小的空格比整場展覽的氣派更重要。

Yan found Yu beside a stationary listening machine. He wanted to utter something personal, but she asked him first to identify which master files had been copied. His careful answer helped the audit, although it did not erase what he had failed to say years ago. Zhou Yan was thirty-eight and could usually recognize a damaged recording before anyone else heard the fault. Now he brought a list of copied files to the listening machine and waited while Yu matched them to the ledger. One filename had changed during transfer. He located its earlier name and wrote both beside the same inventory number. She asked him to send that list to Mei as well, so the count would not depend on a conversation between them. He opened a message, attached the document, and showed her the recipient before sending it. The small confirmation allowed her to return to the tapes instead of wondering whether another important statement would remain unsent.

周晏在停住的試聽機旁找到林予。他想說些私事，她卻先問原始檔案被複製了哪些。他謹慎的回答幫上稽核，卻無法抹去多年前他沒說出口的話。三十八歲的周晏往往比別人更早聽出錄音損壞。此刻他把已複製檔案清單帶到試聽機旁，等林予對照授權簿。一個檔名在轉錄時改過，他找到舊名，把兩者寫在同一典藏編號旁。她請他也寄給梅，讓清點不必依賴兩人的談話。他開啟郵件、附上文件，寄出前讓她看收件人。這項小確認使她能回到磁帶，不必猜又有哪句重要的話會始終沒寄出去。

That evening Yu returned to her small dwelling and submitted an application to North Shore Archive. She had served this museum for eleven years; her resignation was not a synonym for betrayal. It was the first decision she had made without consulting anyone else. The archive was two hours away by train, close enough to visit for an interview and far enough to require a new daily life.

當晚林予回到小住處，遞出北岸檔案所的申請。她為館方服務十一年；辭職不是背叛的同義詞。這是她第一次不先詢問別人，就替自己做決定。檔案所距離兩小時火車車程，近得可以前去面試，也遠得需要重新安排日常生活。
