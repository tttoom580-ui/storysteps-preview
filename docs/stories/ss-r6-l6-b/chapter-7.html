碼頭的口譯員

The Interpreter at the Pier

你終於學會告白時，我已經準備離開 · 第 7 / 25 章 · 5000–6000 · 英中對照 · 校訂完成

Back in Westhaven, a new donation arrived from dock workers who had spent decades recording songs. The boxes were bulky, the labels inconsistent, and the donors demanded that someone explain the consent process in their own languages.

回到西港後，碼頭工人送來多年錄製的歌謠。箱子很笨重，標籤彼此矛盾，捐贈者要求有人以他們熟悉的語言說明授權。

Yu hired an interpreter for a public meeting instead of asking a volunteer to translate legal terms informally. One donor said the recording belonged to the people who made it, not to whoever could afford the largest plaque. The interpreter asked the donors to describe what they understood by giving a recording to the museum. One man meant that the museum could keep his box dry; another expected every song to be played freely. The woman beside him wanted family members consulted before public use. Yu asked the interpreter to read those differences back before they touched the form. A donor moved his chair closer to hear and corrected the description of one song. It belonged to several singers, he explained, and his signature alone could not settle its use. Yu marked that recording for further contact instead of treating the arrival of the box as permission from everyone inside it.

林予為公開會議聘入口譯員，而不是請志工隨口翻譯法律詞彙。一名捐贈者說，錄音屬於創造它的人，不屬於付得起最大銘牌的人。口譯員請捐贈者說明，把錄音交給館方對他們意味什麼。一人只希望箱子保持乾燥，另一人希望每首歌自由播放，旁邊女子則要公開前先問家人。碰表格前，林予請口譯員讀回這些差異。一名捐贈者把椅子移近，修正某首歌的描述。他解釋那是幾個人一起唱的，不能只靠自己簽名決定用途。林予把它標為仍需聯絡，沒有把箱子到館視為箱中所有人的同意。

The dockside exhibition moved through centuries of working life. There was a handicraft boat with a small pebble tied to its sail and a map explaining how sailors navigate shifting channels. A photograph showed a horse drawn plow beside a plantation, while another portrayed royalty strolling under a lofty arch. A lesbian dockworker recalled the liberation of her union from a dictatorship, and the interpreter refused to let a careless summary erase that word. Schoolchildren watched a model vessel glide across the water. A sign described how an overwork culture could spur accidents. The guide deemed the older safety notice important even though its renewal was still pending. A sailor with a deep tan called the whole argument a trifle, then changed his mind when the workers explained the consequences.

碼頭展覽穿越數世紀勞動生活：手工小船的帆上綁著卵石，地圖解釋水手如何在變動航道航行；照片拍下種植園旁的馬拉犁，以及在高拱門下散步的王族。一名女同志碼頭工人回憶工會從獨裁體制獲得解放，口譯員拒絕讓草率摘要抹掉這個身分。學童看模型船滑過水面。展牌說明過勞文化如何促成事故；導覽認為舊安全公告雖待續期，仍很重要。曬得黝黑的水手原本把爭論當小事，聽見後果才改變看法。

Yan admitted that he had previously treated silence as approval. He did not demand that Yu comfort him. Instead he documented the error, prepared corrected labels, and offered each donor the option of restricting access. Yan brought the draft labels to the pier rather than asking everyone to return to the museum. The wind kept lifting their corners, so the interpreter held one flat while a singer read it. A line described the workers as anonymous. The singer pointed to the name he had supplied and asked why it had disappeared. Yan corrected the label, then asked whether the other performers wanted names printed or withheld. The answers differed. Yu watched him make space for each one on the form without rounding them into a group decision. At the end they played only a song whose terms were clear, and the other tapes went back into their boxes for a later discussion.

周晏承認自己過去把沉默視為默許。他沒有要求林予安慰，改以書面紀錄錯誤、準備修正標示，並讓每位捐贈者選擇限制存取。周晏把標籤草稿帶到碼頭，沒叫所有人再跑博物館。風不斷掀紙角，口譯員壓住一張，讓歌者閱讀。一行把工人描述為匿名，歌者指著自己提供的姓名，問為何消失。周晏改好後，再問其他表演者希望具名還是隱去姓名，答案各不相同。林予看著他在表格上逐一留空間，沒有把它們湊成群體決定。最後只播放條款清楚的一首歌，其餘磁帶放回盒裡，留待後續討論。

At the pier, Yu heard the singers test a recording that sounded like a street coming back to life. The memory moved her, but she knew the proper ending to that afternoon was not ownership. It was a written choice.

碼頭上，林予聽見歌者試播錄音，彷彿整條街復活。她很感動，卻知道那天下午最合宜的結局不是取得所有權，而是一個寫下來的選擇。
