The Label with Two Dates

你終於學會告白時，我已經準備離開 · 第 4 / 25 章 · 5000–6000 · 英中對照

用互動版查詞、朗讀與收藏

An exhibition designer offered an elegant aesthetic for the opening. Yu noticed that a fire photograph and an archaeological survey had been placed under one date, although the records were decades apart. She asked for a correction before approving the layout. The two dates had been joined by a decorative line that made the design look like a continuous history. Yu asked the designer to separate the images on screen. Without the line, the photographer's note became visible beneath the fire scene. A volunteer retrieved the survey folder and found a different year on its first page. The designer said moving one image would disturb the balance of the whole wall. Yu asked him to leave an empty space while the sources were checked. Visitors could understand a gap in the layout; they would have no way to discover a date the museum had confidently printed wrong. The designer saved the unfinished version instead of approving the attractive one.

展覽設計師提出優雅的視覺方案。林予發現火災照片與考古測量被標在同一年，實際卻相隔數十年。她要求先更正才核准版面。兩個日期被裝飾線連在一起，看起來像連續歷史。林予請設計師在螢幕上分開圖片。拿掉線，火災照片下的攝影者註記便露出來。志工調出測量夾，首頁寫著另一年。設計師說移動一張圖會破壞整面牆的平衡。林予請他先留空，等來源查明。觀眾能理解版面空缺，卻無從發現館方自信印錯的日期。設計師存下未完成版，沒有核准那張漂亮版面。

Han presided over a branding meeting where prestige mattered more than sources. He planned to privatize the listening room for donors. Mei challenged the proposal and demanded a public review of access hours.

韓館長主持品牌會議，談話裡聲望比來源重要。他打算把試聽室改供捐款人私人使用。梅反對，要求公開審查開放時間。

The design studio was crowded with objects that resisted easy classification. A badge from a former volunteer lay beside a shaver found after a station blaze. A disciple of a famous architect had donated sketches of a vineyard, but the lines were only approximate. A stray dog appeared beneath a photographer's veil in one negative. A farm record described seasonal rotation, while a device warranty had been stapled to an unrelated invoice. The curator hoped the restored objects would radiate authority. Yu answered that authority should come from evidence, not an implicit assumption that old paper deserved trust. She disliked making a fuss, but she wanted the public to understand how an initial error might grow into a polished myth. The inquiry did not enlighten everyone, yet it gave the volunteers a method for correcting future labels. A visitor admitted an addiction to collecting archaeology pamphlets, but Yu separated fascination from reliable provenance.

設計室擠滿難以分類的物件。舊志工徽章放在車站火災後找到的刮鬍刀旁；知名建築師門徒捐出葡萄園草圖，但線條只是近似。某張底片裡，一隻流浪狗出現在攝影師面紗下。農場紀錄描述作物輪作，設備保固單卻釘在無關發票上。策展人盼修復展品散發權威感。林予說權威應來自證據，而非舊紙必然可信的隱含假設。她不愛小題大作，卻希望觀眾理解最初的錯誤如何長成精緻神話。調查未能啟發每個人，至少提供志工日後更正標籤的方法。一名訪客承認自己沉迷蒐集考古學手冊，林予卻把蒐藏熱情與可靠來源分開。

Yan traced the error to a copied design file, not to the photographer. Yu insisted they retain the correction history. He admired her firmness without turning that admiration into another demand for her attention. Yan opened the older design file and the current one side by side. The same heading had been copied with the photograph, leaving the wrong year attached after the image changed. Yu asked for a record of which public copies used it. He found a leaflet as well as the wall label. A volunteer began collecting the remaining leaflets while Mei prepared a short correction for people who had already taken one home. Nobody blamed the photographer again once the transfer could be followed on screen. Yu kept the first leaflet in the project folder with its replacement, so that the next curator would understand why two versions existed and which could safely be distributed.

周晏追查發現錯誤來自複製的設計檔，不是攝影師。林予堅持保留更正歷程。他欣賞她的堅定，卻沒把欣賞變成索取她注意力的要求。周晏並排打開舊設計檔與現行版。照片連著標題一起複製，換圖後錯誤年份仍留著。林予請他查有哪些公開副本使用它。他找到傳單與牆面標籤。志工開始回收剩餘傳單，梅替已帶回家的人準備短更正。螢幕上看清轉換過程後，再沒有人責怪攝影師。林予把原傳單與替換版一起留在計畫夾，讓下任策展人理解為何有兩版，哪版才可繼續發放。

The corrected label went up at closing time. Yu stood beneath it and thought of the North Shore interview scheduled for Friday. An institution could change its label; she was free to change her address.

更正標籤在閉館前掛上。林予站在下面，想著週五安排的北岸面試。機構能更換標示，她也有自由改變住址。
