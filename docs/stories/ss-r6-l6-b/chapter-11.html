末頁的署名

The Credit on the Back Page

你終於學會告白時，我已經準備離開 · 第 11 / 25 章 · 5000–6000 · 英中對照 · 校訂中

A newspaper praised the listening night but credited Han for every correction. Yu did not seek acclaim; she did want the volunteers and speakers named accurately. She drafted a short note distinguishing public credit from private consent. The article lay folded at the front desk, where a volunteer had circled the paragraph about the corrections. Her name was absent, as were the names of the speakers who had requested them. Yu asked the group how they wanted their work described before drafting the response. One volunteer preferred a collective credit, while a speaker wanted no public name at all. The short note therefore named the review group and explained its role without turning private participants into a list of supporters. Mei checked the wording with the group. Yu sent the same approved version to the museum office and the newspaper, so neither could quietly substitute a more flattering account on the way to print.

報紙稱讚試聽夜，卻把所有更正都歸功韓館長。林予不追求掌聲，只希望志工與受訪者獲得正確署名。她寫了短箋，區分公開表揚與私人授權。報紙摺放櫃檯，志工圈出更正那段。沒有她的姓名，也沒有提出更正的受訪者。林予先問團隊想如何被描述，再起草回覆。一名志工要集體署名，一位受訪者卻不願公開姓名。因此短箋列出審查小組，解釋工作，而不把私人參與者變成支持者名單。梅與團隊核對措辭。林予把同一核准版本寄給館方與報紙，讓任何一方都不能在刊登途中偷偷換成更光彩的故事。

Han said the press statement contained harmless ambiguity. Yu asked him to show the signed record supporting his claim. He could not, and the museum issued a correction naming the community review group.

韓館長稱新聞稿只是無傷大雅的模糊說法。林予請他拿出支持署名的書面紀錄，他拿不出來，館方於是更正並列出社區審查小組。

The museum's miscellaneous education room explained how interpretation can go wrong. A display converted a Fahrenheit reading and a geographical coordinate for visitors unfamiliar with old notebooks. Nearby stood a fireproof box, a kitchen utensil, and a postcard from a family outing. A layman had mistaken a software hacker for the villain of a village legend, although the real incident was a routine security test. A mermaid mural showed that stories could liberate imagination without claiming documentary authority. Children tasted yogurt during a workshop, and an educator asked them to ponder why a planet seemed to revolve overhead. Each occurrence in the teaching log was dated. The collection had won acclaim because volunteers persevered through repairs, not because any single director could claim their bodily labour as his own. A cold draught entered through the high window, and Yu moved the fragile document away from the open air.

雜項教育室說明詮釋如何出錯。展品替不熟悉舊筆記的訪客換算華氏溫度與地理座標，旁邊有防火箱、廚房器具與家庭出遊明信片。外行人把軟體駭客誤當村莊傳說的壞人，實際事件卻是例行資安測試。美人魚壁畫說明故事能解放想像，而不必冒充紀實。孩子在工作坊品嘗優格，思考行星為何看似在頭頂旋轉。教學日誌把每次事件註明日期。館方獲得稱譽，是因志工堅持修復，不是某位館長能獨占眾人的身體勞動。一道冷風從高窗灌入，林予把脆弱文件移離風口。

Yan found an old note in which he had acknowledged Yu's work three years earlier but had never forwarded it. He told her that fact without demanding appreciation. She replied that an unsent note had protected nobody at the meeting when she needed help. Yan unfolded the old note on a clear patch of the workbench. It named the files Yu had checked and the decision management had overridden. The evidence was specific enough to have mattered at the time. He asked whether he should now send it to the review group. Yu said that was a question about the record, not something she could answer by forgiving him. He read the note again and added a dated explanation of why it had remained in his drawer. She left before he sent it. That evening she chose a case for her interview in which a speaker had changed a decision, rather than choosing the story of the colleague who had finally changed his mind.

周晏找到三年前寫下卻沒寄出的便條，內容承認林予的貢獻。他把事實告訴她，沒有要求感激。她回答，在自己最需要支持的會議裡，一張沒寄出的紙沒有保護任何人。周晏在工作桌清空的一角攤開舊便條，上面列出林予核對的檔案，以及管理階層推翻的決定。證據具體，當時原本能派上用場。他問現在是否該寄給審查組。林予說那是紀錄問題，不能靠自己原諒他來回答。他重讀便條，加上註明日期的說明，解釋為何一直留在抽屜。寄出前她便離開。當晚她替面試選了一個受訪者改變決策的案例，沒有選那位終於改變心意的同事的故事。

That night she prepared for her second interview. The public correction remained online, and Yu finally felt that the volunteers could defend their work without repeating her arguments. Leaving began to seem possible rather than merely necessary.

那晚她準備第二次面試。公開更正仍在線上，林予終於感到志工已能不依賴她的說法，替自己的工作辯護。離開開始變得不只必要，也有可能。
