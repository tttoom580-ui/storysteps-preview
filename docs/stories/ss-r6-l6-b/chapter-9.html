消失的第一分鐘

The Missing First Minute

你終於學會告白時，我已經準備離開 · 第 9 / 25 章 · 5000–6000 · 英中對照 · 校訂中

用互動版查詞、朗讀與收藏

A teacher arrived with an old cassette whose opening minute had vanished from the digital copy. The missing portion contained a student's request not to be named. Yu immediately suspended the exhibit rather than waiting for an embarrassed explanation.

一名老師帶來舊錄音帶，數位副本最前面一分鐘卻消失了。那一段有學生要求匿名的聲音。林予立即暫停展出，沒有等待令人難堪的解釋。

The team compared the cassette with its transfer log. No deliberate tampering was established, but the absence itself had changed the apparent permission. Yu asked the contractor for a forthcoming technical report and secured the original. The teacher pressed play and stopped it before the interview proper began. In the headphones a younger voice had asked not to be named. Yu looked at the digital transcript, which began with the answer to the next question. Its first line sounded cheerful and complete. The contractor's transfer sheet gave a duration that was shorter than the cassette's handwritten label. Neither figure explained who had removed the beginning. Yu made a comparison copy for the review and returned the original to secure storage. The teacher asked whether the former student would have to speak publicly to prove the request. Yu said the preserved recording was enough to begin checking; she would contact the person privately.

團隊比對卡帶與轉錄紀錄。尚無證據證明刻意修改，但缺失本身已改變表面上的同意內容。林予要求承包商提出後續技術報告，並保全原件。老師按播放，在正式訪談開始前停下。耳機裡，年輕聲音要求匿名。林予看數位逐字稿，它卻從下一題答案開始，第一行聽來歡快完整。承包商的轉錄表時長比卡帶手寫標籤短，兩個數字都沒說明誰移除開頭。她製作審查用比較副本，把原件送回安全儲存。老師問，昔日學生是否得公開說話證明要求。林予說，保存的錄音足以啟動查核，她會私下聯絡當事人。

The school-history boxes contained a brochure on literacy and a colourful lesson about a trout in a clear stream. A narrator explained why children cram before examinations and how teachers can administer, or administrate, an assessment fairly. One recording described a child with acne who wished to stay away from cameras; another traced a stepchild's discomfort during a family gathering. A model of a peninsula lay beside a piece of jade, and an old drawing depicted a bird's nostril in detail. Those images could illuminate a lesson, but none justified taking a person's voice into captivity. A conservator showed how to detach the damaged tape without destroying it. The students were joyous when their work returned, and a selective display allowed a small revival of the project without sacrificing the request for privacy. The student's refusal was a potent reminder that privacy could be a necessary condition of trust.

學校史料箱裡有識字教育手冊，以及清溪鱒魚的彩色課程。旁白解釋學生為何考前死背，教師如何公平施行測驗。一段錄音描述長青春痘而不願被拍攝的孩子；另一段談繼子女在家庭聚會的不自在。半島模型旁放著玉石，舊圖細畫鳥類鼻孔。這些圖像能照亮課堂，卻不能合理化把他人的聲音囚禁起來。修復員示範如何拆下受損磁帶而不毀壞它。學生取回作品時歡欣鼓舞，選擇性展示讓計畫部分重啟，也尊重當事人的隱私要求。那名學生的拒絕強烈提醒大家，隱私可能是建立信任的必要條件。

Yan resisted a colleague's suggestion that the absent minute was trivial. Years earlier he might have said the same thing. Now he understood why Yu needed an explanation before his sympathy could carry any weight.

周晏反對同事把消失的一分鐘當成小事。多年前他也許會說同樣的話；如今他懂得，林予需要先有解釋，自己的同情才有重量。

The student, now an adult, declined public use but allowed a private copy to remain for research. Yu recorded that distinction and issued a public correction without naming the student. It was not a dramatic victory, only a necessary one. The reply came as a short letter. The former student remembered the interview and wanted the voice kept for research, with the identifying details restricted. Yu asked a colleague to check the new catalogue view from an ordinary visitor account. The person's name no longer appeared, and the public audio remained suspended. She sent a copy of the revised description for the speaker to read before any permitted use resumed. Yan placed the comparison timecodes in the technical folder, leaving the question of authorship open. The teacher collected a corrected guide for the school. The project could return in a smaller form, but the missing minute would remain documented instead of being quietly repaired out of its history.

昔日學生如今已成年，拒絕公開使用，卻允許保留私人研究副本。林予記錄區別，並發布不具名的公開更正。這不是戲劇化的勝利，只是必要的勝利。回覆是一封短信。昔日學生記得訪談，希望聲音供研究使用，但限制身分細節。林予請同事從一般訪客帳戶檢查新目錄，姓名不再出現，公開音訊仍停用。允許用途恢復前，她寄出修訂描述讓當事人閱讀。周晏把比較時間碼放入技術夾，保留操作者待查問題。老師領取更正導覽。計畫能以較小形式回來，消失的一分鐘仍有紀錄，不會被悄悄修好後從歷史抹掉。
