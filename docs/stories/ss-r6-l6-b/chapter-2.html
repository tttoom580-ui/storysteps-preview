修復桌上的界線

The Repair Bench

你終於學會告白時，我已經準備離開 · 第 2 / 25 章 · 5000–6000 · 英中對照

Before speaking, Yu opened the shared record and checked which details had been written by staff and which came from the original speaker. It was a small distinction on the screen, but a decisive one for everyone whose voice would be heard. Yu returned a damaged tape to the repair bench. The licensing agreement would expire in eighteen days, and Han wanted the recording ready for a sponsor's reception. She insisted that technical restoration did not renew consent automatically. Through years of editing oral histories, Lin Yu had learned to distrust sentences that sounded complete without naming the person who had agreed to them. A recording could be technically perfect and still tell a dishonest story about who owned a voice.

開口之前，林予先打開共用紀錄，確認哪些細節由館員填寫，哪些來自原受訪者。螢幕上的區別很小，對所有聲音將被播放的人而言，卻具有決定性。林予把損壞的磁帶送回修復桌。授權將在十八天後到期，韓館長卻希望錄音趕上贊助人茶會。她堅持技術修復不等於自動續約。多年來整理口述歷史，林予已不再信任那些聽起來完整、卻沒有寫明誰同意的句子。錄音在技術上可以完美無瑕，卻仍可能對聲音的主人說出不誠實的故事。

The conversation was slow because the people around the table valued different outcomes. Yu refused to turn disagreement into a contest of personalities. She asked everyone to identify one practical step that could be completed before the next opening day. At the front desk, a novice volunteer had promised a caller immediate removal of a recording. Yu did not scold him. She showed him how to acknowledge the request, protect the original, and provide a definite review date. At the long table near the listening booths, everyone arrived carrying a different version of what the museum was supposed to protect. Some feared a cancelled event; others feared that a neighbour's story might be taken from them forever.

討論進行得很慢，因為桌邊每個人重視的結果不同。林予拒絕把歧見變成人格競賽，請大家各自指出下一次開館之前能完成的一項具體工作。櫃檯的新志工曾向來電者承諾立即撤下錄音。林予沒有斥責，只教他先受理請求、保護原件，並提供明確審查日期。在試聽室旁的長桌，每個人帶來的都是不同版本的『博物館究竟該保護什麼』。有人怕活動取消，有人怕鄰居的故事一旦被拿走，就再也回不來。

A travelling kitchen exhibition filled the neighbouring room with the language of hospitality. A panel showed an alligator carved into a wooden bowl, and speakers played deep bass notes from a delta festival. The guide used colloquial phrases that once sounded commonplace but now required explanation. The menu called for a liter of broth, syrup over cornbread, and a small measure of whiskey or whisky in an adult recipe. A shabby portrait was illuminated by an unnecessary spotlight; Yu moved it to protect the ink. A guest with an infectious laugh admired the display before developing a rash from the cleaning soap. Nobody was seriously ill. The episode nevertheless affirmed the museum's duty to abide by its safety rules instead of treating hospitality as an excuse to ignore them.

隔壁的巡迴廚房展充滿待客文化的語言。展板畫著刻在木碗上的短吻鱷，喇叭播放三角洲節慶的低音樂聲。導覽使用昔日稀鬆平常、今日卻需要解釋的口語。一份成人食譜列出一公升湯、淋在玉米麵包上的糖漿，以及少量威士忌。破舊肖像被多餘的聚光燈照著，林予將它移開保護墨跡。笑聲極具感染力的訪客欣賞展品後，卻因清潔皂出現紅疹。沒有人病重，但這件小事再次肯定館方必須遵守安全規則，不能以熱情待客為由忽視它們。

They had worked together long enough to predict each other's movements. That familiarity no longer guaranteed trust. When Yan offered to help, Yu specified the work and its limits, making clear that gratitude did not create an obligation to share private feelings. Yan replaced a blown fuse. He apologised for making a rash claim that the tape would be harmless once cleaned. Yu accepted the correction, not the idea that a repaired machine could reconcile every disagreement between them. Zhou Yan, the museum's senior sound engineer, knew the machinery better than almost anyone. He was thirty-eight, careful with fragile tapes, and much less practised at admitting that his silence could wound another person.

兩人共事多年，熟到可以預測對方的動作。但熟悉已不再保證信任。周晏提出幫忙時，林予明確列出工作與界線，表明感激不會形成分享私人感情的義務。周晏更換燒斷的保險絲。他為曾草率宣稱磁帶洗淨後就安全而道歉。林予接受更正，卻不接受修好機器就能和解所有分歧的想法。三十八歲的周晏是館內資深聲音工程師，幾乎沒有人比他更熟悉設備。他對脆弱的磁帶十分細心，卻不太擅長承認自己的沉默也會傷害別人。

There was still a train schedule folded inside her notebook. She had not accepted a new position, and nobody had promised that she would. Even so, the application was proof that she could act without first obtaining permission from the people she might disappoint. Outside at twilight, the entrance sign tilted toward the pavement. Yu photographed the damage for the safety log and left the building without waiting for Yan. A working relationship would need clear tasks, not another unfinished conversation after dark. Outside, buses came and went along the river road. Yu had applied for a position at the North Shore Public Archive, two hours away by train. She had not told her colleagues because she wanted the choice to become real before it became gossip.

筆記本裡仍夾著火車時刻表。她尚未接受新職，也沒有人保證會錄取她。但這份申請至少證明：她可以行動，不必先得到可能被她失望的人批准。暮色中，入口招牌朝人行道傾斜。林予拍照登記安全缺失，沒有等周晏便離開。工作關係需要清楚分工，而不是再來一次入夜後沒有結果的談話。窗外，巴士沿河岸道路來去。林予已申請距離兩小時車程的北岸公共檔案所職缺。她還沒告訴同事，因為她希望這個選擇先成真，再成為眾人口中的八卦。
