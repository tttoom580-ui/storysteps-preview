The Repair Bench

你終於學會告白時，我已經準備離開 · 第 2 / 25 章 · 5000–6000 · 英中對照

Yu returned a damaged tape to the repair bench. The licensing agreement would expire in eighteen days, and Han wanted the recording ready for a sponsor's reception. She insisted that technical restoration did not renew consent automatically. The tape case had a strip of red paper beneath its label. Yan thought it marked a repair priority, but Yu found the same color on an old permission folder. They opened both records before starting playback. The physical damage could be repaired today; the speaker's agreement still had its original end date. Yu asked Han which audience the reception would include and whether a copy would be kept afterward. His answers described a wider use than the signed form allowed. She wrote to the speaker with those specific questions, leaving the damaged tape on the bench while the repair team prepared equipment for work they were actually authorized to do.

林予把損壞的磁帶送回修復桌。授權將在十八天後到期，韓館長卻希望錄音趕上贊助人茶會。她堅持技術修復不等於自動續約。磁帶盒標籤下有一條紅紙。周晏以為那是優先修復記號，林予卻在舊授權夾看到相同顏色。他們先打開兩份紀錄才開始播放。實體損壞今天能修，受訪者的授權仍有原定到期日。林予問韓館長茶會有哪些聽眾，之後是否保存副本。他的回答比簽署表格允許的用途更廣。她寫信給當事人，逐一提出這些問題；受損磁帶留在桌上，修復組先準備真正獲准進行的工作。

At the front desk, a novice volunteer had promised a caller immediate removal of a recording. Yu did not scold him. She showed him how to acknowledge the request, protect the original, and provide a definite review date. The volunteer had written 'removed' beside the caller's name, although the file was still available at another terminal. Yu asked him to phone back with her beside him. His voice shook when he corrected the promise. The caller wanted to know whether anyone else could hear the recording that afternoon. Yu explained what had been restricted and what still required checking, then gave a time for the next response. Afterward the volunteer changed his entry to 'request received' and added the call time. He asked to keep the corrected example in the desk folder. Tomorrow's volunteer, he said, would probably be tempted to give the same quick reassurance if the telephone queue grew long again.

櫃檯的新志工曾向來電者承諾立即撤下錄音。林予沒有斥責，只教他先受理請求、保護原件，並提供明確審查日期。志工在來電者姓名旁寫了『已撤下』，檔案卻仍能在另一台終端機使用。林予請他當著自己的面回電。更正承諾時，他聲音發抖。來電者想知道當天下午還有誰能聽。林予解釋哪些已限制、哪些仍待檢查，再給下次回覆時間。之後志工把註記改為『已收申請』，補上通話時間。他想把更正範例留在櫃檯資料夾，說明天電話又排長隊時，下一位志工可能也會忍不住立刻安慰對方。

A travelling kitchen exhibition filled the neighbouring room with the language of hospitality. A panel showed an alligator carved into a wooden bowl, and speakers played deep bass notes from a delta festival. The guide used colloquial phrases that once sounded commonplace but now required explanation. The menu called for a liter of broth, syrup over cornbread, and a small measure of whiskey or whisky in an adult recipe. A shabby portrait was illuminated by an unnecessary spotlight; Yu moved it to protect the ink. A guest with an infectious laugh admired the display before developing a rash from the cleaning soap. Nobody was seriously ill. The episode nevertheless affirmed the museum's duty to abide by its safety rules instead of treating hospitality as an excuse to ignore them.

隔壁的巡迴廚房展充滿待客文化的語言。展板畫著刻在木碗上的短吻鱷，喇叭播放三角洲節慶的低音樂聲。導覽使用昔日稀鬆平常、今日卻需要解釋的口語。一份成人食譜列出一公升湯、淋在玉米麵包上的糖漿，以及少量威士忌。破舊肖像被多餘的聚光燈照著，林予將它移開保護墨跡。笑聲極具感染力的訪客欣賞展品後，卻因清潔皂出現紅疹。沒有人病重，但這件小事再次肯定館方必須遵守安全規則，不能以熱情待客為由忽視它們。

Yan replaced a blown fuse. He apologised for making a rash claim that the tape would be harmless once cleaned. Yu accepted the correction, not the idea that a repaired machine could reconcile every disagreement between them.

周晏更換燒斷的保險絲。他為曾草率宣稱磁帶洗淨後就安全而道歉。林予接受更正，卻不接受修好機器就能和解所有分歧的想法。

Outside at twilight, the entrance sign tilted toward the pavement. Yu photographed the damage for the safety log and left the building without waiting for Yan. A working relationship would need clear tasks, not another unfinished conversation after dark.

暮色中，入口招牌朝人行道傾斜。林予拍照登記安全缺失，沒有等周晏便離開。工作關係需要清楚分工，而不是再來一次入夜後沒有結果的談話。
