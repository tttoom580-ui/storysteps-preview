董事會前五天

Five Days to the Board

Five days remained before the first board review. A breakdown in the museum's telecommunications line delayed permission calls, while Han proposed to bypass the ordinary approval route. Yu refused to let the deadline become an excuse. The first unanswered call had been crossed off as though no reply meant agreement. Yu circled it again and asked the caller to explain what had happened. There had been no connection at all. She changed the entry before Han could use the total in his presentation, then arranged a separate list of people who still needed to be reached. The volunteer at the desk asked whether a written message would be useful for speakers who could not answer during working hours. Yu prepared one that described the actual exhibition, including the delayed preview. It took longer than Han's proposed shortcut, but it gave the next caller something accurate to read instead of improvising an urgent appeal.

董事會初次審查只剩五天。館內通訊系統故障，延誤授權電話，韓館長又提議繞過正常核准程序。林予拒絕讓期限成為藉口。第一通未接電話被劃掉，彷彿沒有回答便等於同意。林予重新圈起，請負責者說明。電話根本沒有接通。她趁韓館長把總數放進簡報前改好註記，另列仍需聯絡的人。櫃檯志工問，工作時無法接電話的人是否需要文字訊息。林予準備一份說明真正展覽內容、也包括延後預覽的文字。這比館長的捷徑慢，卻讓下個撥電話的人有準確內容可讀，不必臨時編出急迫懇求。

Mei gathered the missing signatures into a separate folder. A monetary contribution from a local sponsor had been recorded twice. Yu asked the accountant to mark the duplicate instead of quietly deleting it, so that the board could see the full audit trail. Mei held the two entries for the sponsor's payment against the bank record. The amount matched one deposit, not two. Han wanted the larger total left in the draft until the next meeting, arguing that a visible correction would distract the trustees. Yu asked the accountant to attach the deposit reference to both lines and mark which entry had been reversed. When the revised sheet reached the board, a trustee traced the change without asking Yu to explain it. The sponsor's preview was postponed with the uncertain permissions still listed. Yu packed the audit checklist beside her own copy of the resignation; the meeting had changed the exhibition timetable, but it had not changed the date she would leave.

梅把缺少的簽名集中存檔。一筆在地贊助款被重複登錄，林予請會計標出重複項而非悄悄刪去，讓董事會能看見完整稽核軌跡。梅拿兩筆贊助款對照銀行紀錄，金額只對上一筆入帳。韓館長希望草案保留較大的總數到下次會議，說顯眼的更正會讓董事分心。林予請會計在兩行附上入帳參考，標出撤銷的是哪筆。修正表送到董事會時，一名董事不必請她解釋就能追出變動。贊助預覽延後，未確認授權仍列在表上。她把稽核表與自己的辭呈副本一起收好。會議改了展覽時程，卻沒改她離開的日期。

A lively fundraiser filled the side gallery with strange fragments of the city's past. An old chestnut seller wore a silver accessory, while another photograph showed domestic fowl at a roadside market. A display of early rap recordings had a scratched disc with a long slash across its label. A sleeping guard's snore was captured by accident on a historic microphone. Someone offered champagne near the exhibit, and a conservator with a hoarse voice asked guests to keep their drinks away. A medical poster advertised hand lotion; a radio program described how one family's mentality survived a harsh winter. Mei said the nucleus of the public collection was its people, not its glamour. The event could evoke memories and unveil forgotten voices, but only if the organisers had the vigor to resist the cowardly temptation to hide inconvenient facts.

側廊的募款活動擠滿城市往事的奇異碎片。舊栗子攤販戴著銀色配件，另一張照片拍下路邊市場的家禽。早期饒舌唱片標籤有一道長刮痕，老麥克風甚至意外錄到守衛的鼾聲。有人在展品旁端香檳，一名嗓音沙啞的修復師請大家把飲料移遠。醫療海報廣告護手乳；廣播節目談某個家庭在寒冬仍保持的心態。梅說，公共典藏的核心是人，不是光鮮。活動能喚起記憶、揭露被忘記的聲音，前提是主辦者得有勇氣抵抗掩飾不便真相的怯懦。

Yan had been moody for days. Yu asked him directly whether he intended to finish the cataloguing work. He said yes and stopped trying to guess how much private conversation she owed him for his help.

周晏連日情緒不穩。林予直接問他是否打算完成編目。他回答會，也不再猜測她應該用多少私下談話來回報他的幫忙。

The board accepted the audit plan and postponed the sponsor's exclusive preview. It was a limited victory: nothing had been finally approved. Yu nevertheless left with the completed checklist and her resignation still intact.

董事會接受稽核計畫，延後贊助人的專屬預覽。這只是有限勝利，任何事項都還沒最終核准。林予帶著完成的檢核表離開，辭呈依然有效。
