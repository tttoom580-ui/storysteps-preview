A Voice That Can Say No

你終於學會告白時，我已經準備離開 · 第 3 / 25 章 · 5000–6000 · 英中對照

Mrs. Wu, a retired cashier, came to request the removal of an interview about her son's departure. She had agreed to a private research copy, not a public performance. Yu opened a lengthy review despite Han's impatience. Mrs. Wu brought her own copy in a grocery envelope. She had underlined the line about private research and folded the page so that it opened there immediately. Yu offered a seat away from the public queue and asked whether she wished to explain anything beyond the requested restriction. Mrs. Wu said she did not. Han began describing how moving her interview was, but Mei placed the agreement on the table between them. Yu checked which terminal carried the public version and called for it to be suspended during review. Only then did Mrs. Wu take her coat off. She had expected to spend the morning proving that her family's story was still hers.

退休收銀員吳太太前來要求撤下一段談兒子離家的訪談。她同意的是私人研究副本，不是公開播放。林予即使面對韓館長催促，仍啟動漫長審查。吳太太用買菜信封裝著自己的副本。私人研究那行已畫底線，紙也摺成一打開就看見那裡。林予請她坐到遠離公開隊伍的位置，問除了限制要求還想不想說明其他事情。吳太太說不用。韓館長開始形容訪談多動人，梅卻把協議放到兩人中間。林予查出公開版本在哪台終端機，致電要求審查期間先停用。直到這時吳太太才脫外套。她原以為整個早上都得證明家人的故事仍屬於自己。

Han called the objection a disgrace to a respected institution. Yu replied that dissent was permissible and could enrich the archive's credibility. Mei asked both sides to separate reputational fears from the speaker's actual written agreement.

韓館長稱這份異議有損聲譽。林予回答，提出反對是被允許的，也能增進檔案館可信度。梅要求雙方把名聲焦慮和受訪者真正簽署的同意書分開。

The consent cabinet held a surprising cross-section of lives. A carpenter who planned to emigrate had spoken with lifelong melancholy about leaving his hometown. A nurse described a routine checkup and the danger of noise loud enough to deafen factory workers. Another speaker recalled strict rules for waste disposal beside the curb. In a recording made for children, a woman addressed the narrator as 'madam' or 'ma'am' and laughed at their formal rivalry. The museum had registered a local trademark for its exhibitions, but that mark gave it no ownership of anyone's private grief. Yu asked the archivists to place each statement under scrutiny. No one was allowed to preach that a speaker must accept exposure for the public good. The decision reduced the suspense surrounding Mrs. Wu's request without pretending that every case would be simple.

授權櫃裡記錄著各種人生。計畫移民的木匠終生帶著鄉愁談故鄉；護理師談一般健檢與足以讓工人失聰的噪音。有人回憶路緣旁嚴格的廢棄物處理規範。兒童錄音裡，一名婦人稱旁白為『夫人』，並笑談兩人一本正經的競爭。館方擁有地方展覽商標，卻不能藉此擁有任何人的私密悲傷。林予要求每份陳述都經過檢視，禁止拿公益當說教理由，逼受訪者接受曝光。這項決定消除了吳太太申請的懸念，卻沒有假裝所有案件都容易。

Yan located an old cassette reminiscent of the sound Mrs. Wu remembered from childhood. He refused to duplicate it before she chose what she wanted. For the first time, Yu saw him put someone's choice ahead of the machinery. When Yan found the cassette, Mrs. Wu recognized the handwriting on its case before he played anything. She asked for the date, then wanted to hear only the first few seconds through headphones. Yu stayed where she could see the machine's controls. At Mrs. Wu's raised hand, Yan stopped. The woman smiled at a sound nobody else had heard and asked that it remain outside the exhibition. Yu read the limited terms back to her, including the way to request another review. Mrs. Wu corrected one word before signing. The final copy went into her envelope with the same care she had given the older agreement, and she checked the address printed at its bottom.

周晏找到一卷聲音讓吳太太想起童年的舊卡帶。她還沒決定前，他拒絕複製。林予第一次看見他把當事人選擇放在機器之前。找到卡帶時，吳太太還沒聽就認出盒上筆跡。她先問日期，再要求用耳機只聽開頭幾秒。林予留在能看見控制鍵的位置。吳太太一舉手，周晏就停止。她為只有自己聽見的聲音微笑，並要求不要放進展覽。林予讀回有限條款，包括日後如何再次申請審查。吳太太改了一個字才簽名。新副本小心放進信封，與她珍惜的舊協議並排；她還核對頁底地址。

Mrs. Wu signed a limited research permission but declined display. Yu recorded the decision and gave her a copy. The exhibition lost a beautiful recording; the museum gained a chance to practise what its motto claimed.

吳太太簽下有限度研究授權，卻拒絕展出。林予記錄決定並交給她副本。展覽失去一段漂亮錄音，博物館卻得到實踐館訓的機會。
