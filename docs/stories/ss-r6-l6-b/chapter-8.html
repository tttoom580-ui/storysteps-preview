A File Nobody Wanted

你終於學會告白時，我已經準備離開 · 第 8 / 25 章 · 5000–6000 · 英中對照 · 校訂中

用互動版查詞、朗讀與收藏

A retired physician discovered her recorded medical history on a public terminal. The agreement allowed an academic lecture but not unrestricted display. Yu disabled the public copy and began an incident review that did not disclose the patient's identity. The physician had recognized her own voice while a school group stood behind her. Yu asked the attendant to close the terminal first, then offered a room where they could speak without that audience. The woman wanted to know which parts had been heard and whether the file could be reached elsewhere. Yu could answer the first question only in part. She wrote down the limits of what they knew and arranged for the technical log to be checked. She did not ask the physician to demonstrate the problem again at the public screen. The review would use a protected copy, and the woman could receive an update without returning to the place where she had been exposed.

退休醫師發現自己的醫療口述史被放上公開終端機。原協議只同意學術演講，不允許無限制展示。林予關閉公開副本並啟動事件調查，過程不揭露病人身分。醫師認出自己聲音時，背後還站著一組學生。林予先請服務員關閉終端機，再提供房間私下談。女子想知道哪些部分被聽見，別處還能不能開啟。第一題林予只能部分回答。她記下已知範圍，安排核對技術紀錄，沒有要求醫師在公開螢幕重演問題。審查將用受保護副本，她不必回到遭曝光的位置，也能收到後續消息。

Han worried about the expenditure required to correct the catalogue. Yu argued that the cost belonged in the institution's budget, not on the person whose privacy had been affected. Mei supported a separate, confidential report.

韓館長擔心更正目錄的支出。林予主張費用應由機構預算負擔，不該轉嫁隱私受影響的人。梅支持另立保密報告。

The sensitive collection included a lecture by an astronomer on modern astronomy and a tiny prototype spacecraft used to explain an astronaut's equipment. A medical panel warned that an antibiotic should not be dispensed without proper advice and that pneumonia could require urgent care. A psychiatrist had recorded a private meditation for nurses; its use required precise permission. A disabled participant explained how a doorway could exclude people even when the building looked robust. A visitor from the eastern, sometimes labelled oriental, collection objected to the dated term, and the curator promised to orient the display more carefully. A public spokesperson mentioned nickel in a chemical demonstration, while another object documented an old sovereign's wardrobe. The museum applauded the volunteers who computerize fragile records, but Yu reminded them that making a file searchable also made privacy mistakes easier to spread. An irritable visitor asked whether an emergency team could evacuate her kin. A psychiatry trainee helped her follow the instructions calmly.

敏感典藏包含天文學家談現代天文的講座，還有用來說明太空人裝備的小型太空船原型。醫療展板警告抗生素須有正確指示才能發給病患，肺炎可能需要緊急照護。精神科醫師為護理師錄製私人冥想課，使用必須精確授權。身障參與者說，建築再堅固，一道門也可能排除人。來自東方文化典藏的訪客抗議舊稱呼不合時宜，策展人承諾重新調整。發言人談化學展示所用的鎳，另一件物品記錄昔日君主的衣櫥。館方稱讚把脆弱檔案數位化的志工，林予卻提醒，變得可搜尋，也讓隱私錯誤更容易擴散。一名易怒的訪客問緊急隊伍能否疏散她的家人；精神醫學實習生協助她冷靜遵循指示。

Yan confirmed that the terminal had been left in demonstration mode after training. He accepted responsibility for the technical setting without claiming to be the only person who had failed. Yu valued an explanation that did not erase the other failures. Yan found the training instructions still open beside the machine. A final step told staff to leave the sample collection ready for visitors, but the sample had been replaced during the session. He copied the instructions into the incident folder before correcting them. Yu asked him to test the revised setting using material approved for demonstration, with another technician watching. At the restart, the public menu showed only the permitted files. She asked for the second technician's result to be recorded separately. The physician's report would explain both the setting that had failed and the check that now kept the restricted file out of view. A reassuring statement alone would not tell her where the exposure had stopped.

周晏確認終端機在教育訓練後仍留在展示模式。他承認自己對設定有責任，卻不聲稱全案只有自己犯錯。林予重視這份不抹去其他疏失的說明。周晏發現訓練指示仍攤在機器旁。最後一步叫員工把示範典藏留給訪客，但訓練途中示範內容已被換掉。他先把指示複製到事件夾才修改。林予請他用獲准示範的材料測試新設定，由另一名技師觀看。重新啟動後，公開選單只顯示允許檔案。她要求第二名技師另記結果。給醫師的報告將說明失靈設定，以及現在哪項檢查阻止限制檔案出現。只有安慰聲明，無法告訴她曝光在哪裡停止。

Yu sent a summary to North Shore as an example of her incident practice, after removing protected details. She did not know whether honesty would cost her the job. She knew misrepresenting the case would cost something more lasting.

林予去除受保護細節後，把處理摘要當作事件管理案例寄給北岸。她不知道誠實是否會讓她失去工作，只知道扭曲事實的代價更長遠。
