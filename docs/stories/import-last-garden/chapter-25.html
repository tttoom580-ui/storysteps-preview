我們選擇的花園

The Garden We Choose

最後的花園 · 第 25 / 25 章 · 2000–3000 · 英中對照 · 校訂完成

The seed craft escaped from the Mirror with seconds to spare. Kel guided its tiny shape aboard Aurora, and a medical team rushed to meet the passengers. Lena's hands were burned, Adrian could barely stand, and Mira had a new bruise across her shoulder, but all three were alive. Noah ran toward them so quickly that he nearly knocked over a nurse. Their reunion was interrupted by an urgent broadcast announcing that the ship was finally free of the false route. For the first time in eighty years, no machine could silently decide where the passengers would go. The news brought both joy and fear, because freedom meant the future was uncertain.

種子艇在最後幾秒逃出「鏡面」。凱爾引導小艇返回「極光號」，醫療隊急忙迎接。莉娜雙手燙傷，亞德里安幾乎站不起來，米拉肩上又添瘀青，但三人都還活著。諾亞衝過來，差點撞倒護士。團聚被緊急廣播打斷：太空船終於擺脫虛假航線。八十年來第一次，再也沒有機器能暗中決定乘客要去哪裡。消息帶來喜悅也帶來恐懼，因為自由意味著未來不再確定。

In the weeks that followed, the council worked to build a small republic aboard the ship. Its members did not want to replace one emperor with another. Instead they agreed that major decisions should be public, leaders should receive an ordinary salary, and every department should have a voice. Orin faced an independent hearing for his role in the crisis. Some people were frank about wanting severe punishment; others feared that revenge would create new misery. The council promised fair procedures for every accused person, including the former governor. Lena learned that politics was not simply about defeating an enemy. It was about making rules that still mattered when feelings ran high.

接下來幾週，議會努力在船上建立小型共和制度。成員不想用新皇帝取代舊皇帝，而是同意重大決策公開、領導者只領一般薪資，各部門都有發言權。歐林因危機中的角色接受獨立聽證。有人坦率要求嚴懲，也有人擔心報復會帶來新痛苦。議會承諾所有被指控者，包括前總督，都適用公平程序。莉娜明白，政治不只是擊敗敵人，而是建立一套即使情緒激動也仍須遵守的規則。

The truth about Haven reached every passenger. Scientists planned a regional survey of several alternative worlds before any landing attempt. Nobody would be forced into sleep or moved overseas to a new colony without clear information and a genuine choice. Adrian returned to Garden Seven to recover, sitting beneath the coconut tree while Mara read to him. A small beetle-shaped robot explored the leaves, and a painted crab stood beside the water tank. They looked like odd toys, yet Adrian loved them. “I would bet my last meal that the children who made these were happier than the men who built the Mirror,” he said.

港灣星的真相傳到每位乘客耳中。科學家計畫對數顆候選世界進行區域調查，在真正登陸前先取得證據。沒有人能在資訊不足、未經真正選擇的情況下被迫沉睡或遷往海外新殖民地。亞德里安回七號花園休養，坐在椰子樹下聽瑪拉讀書。一隻甲蟲形機器人在葉間探索，水槽旁站著彩繪螃蟹。它們只是奇怪玩具，他卻非常喜歡。「我敢拿最後一餐打賭，」他說，「做這些玩具的孩子一定比造『鏡面』的人快樂。」

An editor from the ship's new public studio asked Lena to tell the story of the garden, the hidden stations, and the people who had helped them. Lena refused to describe herself as the only hero. She spoke about Sera protecting the roots, Ivo hiding the coordinates, Anika keeping records, Tomas sharing bread, Emi repairing machines, and Moss caring for plants inside a lonely asteroid. She also insisted that their victory had victims: injured workers, lost years, and families who would need time to heal. The editor thanked her for telling a story that did not pretend courage was enjoyable every moment.

太空船新公共攝影棚的編輯請莉娜講述花園、祕密中繼站，以及幫助過他們的人。莉娜拒絕把自己說成唯一英雄。她談瑟拉守護根系、伊沃藏好座標、安妮卡保存紀錄、托瑪斯分享麵包、艾米修理機器，以及苔蘚在孤獨小行星裡照顧植物。她也堅持勝利仍有受害者：受傷工人、失去的歲月，以及需要時間療傷的家庭。編輯感謝她講述不把勇氣描繪成每一刻都令人愉快的故事。

One afternoon the ship passed a small moon covered with dark pine forests. It was too cold for people to live there, but its trees were real. The sight sent a wave of excitement through the passengers. Some wanted to land immediately; others wanted more tests. Lena supported waiting. They had escaped a disastrous plan, and she would not destroy that achievement by rushing into another. Noah joined her on the observation deck and joked that they might spend their entire lives searching for the perfect planet. Lena replied that no world would be perfect. They needed one they could enter together, knowing the risks and choosing the work. An old tourist brochure advertised a romantic mountain motel on Earth, and Lena wondered how different real travel must have been from such perfect pictures.

一天下午，太空船飛過一顆佈滿深色松林的小衛星。那裡太冷，人類無法居住，但樹木是真實的。乘客們激動不已。有些人想立刻登陸，有些人希望做更多測試。莉娜支持等待。他們才逃離災難性計畫，不能因匆忙而毀掉成果。諾亞在觀景甲板上開玩笑，說他們也許會花一生尋找完美星球。莉娜回答，不會有完美世界。他們需要的，是能夠在理解風險後，一起選擇投入建設的地方。一張舊觀光手冊宣傳地球山間的浪漫汽車旅館，莉娜好奇真實旅行與這些完美圖片究竟相差多少。

That evening Lena planted Moss's seeds in a new garden open to every child. A school group arrived carrying drawings, questions, and more stuff than the small room could hold. A boy asked whether the garden would grow on their final world. Lena smiled. “I hope so. But its first job is to teach us how to care for each other while we travel.” Outside the window, the stars moved quietly past. Mara and Adrian stood together beside the door, and Noah waved from the path. Lena touched the first green leaf, remembering the whistle that had begun everything. Somewhere ahead a new home might be waiting, but the choice of how to live had already begun.

那天傍晚，莉娜把苔蘚的種子種進一座對所有孩子開放的新花園。一群學生帶著畫作、問題和多到小房間快裝不下的雜物來了。一個男孩問，這座花園會不會在最終的新世界裡長大。莉娜微笑：「希望會。但它的第一項工作，是在我們旅途中教會彼此如何照顧對方。」窗外星星安靜滑過。瑪拉與亞德里安一起站在門邊，諾亞從小徑揮手。莉娜碰了碰第一片綠葉，想起最初那聲哨響。前方也許有新家正等待，但人們已經開始選擇要怎樣生活。
