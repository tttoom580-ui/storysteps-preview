沒有窗戶的學校

The School Without Windows

最後的花園 · 第 14 / 25 章 · 2000–3000 · 英中對照 · 校訂完成

At dawn the group entered Aurora's central campus, a collection of classrooms built around an indoor playground. Student luggage was stacked beside a wall, ready for a field trip that had been canceled long ago. A large electronic headline above the entrance announced that the governor had ordered a temporary closure for safety. Mara knew another route through the old shopping mall, where most stores now held textbooks and emergency supplies. The children passed a dry fountain and a recreation room with a broken volleyball net. “I used to play here,” Lena said. She had not realized how small her world had become. At the corner, a bunch of students practiced how to breathe slowly during a pressure alarm, beside a painted tree trunk.

黎明時，一行人進入「極光號」中央校園，那是圍著室內遊樂場建造的教室群。學生行李堆在牆邊，原本要參加一趟早已取消的校外教學。入口大型電子標題宣布總督為安全起見暫時關閉學校。瑪拉知道另一條路，可穿過舊購物中心，多數店面如今放著教科書和緊急物資。孩子們經過乾涸噴泉與破損排球網的休閒室。「我以前常在這裡玩。」莉娜說。她從沒意識到自己的世界變得多麼狹小。轉角一群學生正在一棵彩繪樹幹旁，練習氣壓警報時如何緩慢呼吸。

Principal Nadi met them in the library. She was a skinny woman with a sharp gaze and a gentle voice. Her family had traveled from Earth generations earlier, but she still called its rivers and forests her native home because her grandmother's stories had made them real. Mara explained the crisis and asked Nadi to communicate the evidence to other teachers. Nadi replied that the schools needed guidance, not just alarming data. “Children will ask whether they are going to die,” she said. “Adults must answer honestly, without making them carry all our fear.”

校長娜迪在圖書館接見他們。她身材瘦削、目光銳利、聲音溫柔。家族幾代前已離開地球，她仍稱地球的河川森林為故鄉，因為祖母的故事使它們鮮活。瑪拉解釋危機，請娜迪向其他老師傳遞證據。娜迪回答，學校需要的不只是可怕數據，還需要引導。「孩子們會問自己是否快死了，」她說，「大人必須誠實回答，不能把我們所有恐懼都交給他們。」

Nadi showed them a storage room containing passports prepared for the colony. Each passport listed a child's birthplace as Haven, even though none had ever visited the planet. The government had already written a future in which every passenger belonged to its chosen world. Lena looked at her own name on a printed sheet and felt a sudden desire to tear it apart. Mara stopped her. “Keep it,” she said. “It proves how far the plan has gone.” In the next room, school savings accounts had been emptied to purchase equipment no teacher remembered requesting.

娜迪帶他們看儲藏室裡替殖民地準備的護照。每一本都把兒童出生地寫成港灣星，儘管沒有任何孩子去過那裡。政府已替所有乘客寫好未來，規定他們屬於政府選定的世界。莉娜看見自己印在文件上的名字，突然想把紙撕碎。瑪拉阻止她：「留下來，這能證明計畫已進行到什麼程度。」隔壁房間裡，學校儲蓄帳戶被掏空，用來購買連老師都不記得申請過的設備。

The teachers gathered in a science classroom, where a large tub held water samples from different decks. Nadi explained that a peaceful march would begin at the next shift change. Teachers and students who wished to join could carry signs demanding an open hearing. The aim was not to frighten younger children, but to make the adults with power listen. Noah worried that Orin would send armed guards. “Then we must be careful,” Nadi said, “and keep the children away from danger.” Mara suggested that the teachers also send representatives to the union and the medical staff. One student used a wooden spoon to stir a sample and was told that no worker or child should receive inferior water.

老師們在科學教室集合，巨大水盆裡裝著各甲板的水樣。娜迪說，下一次換班時將舉行和平遊行，願意參加的師生可舉牌要求公開聽證。目標不是嚇壞小孩子，而是要掌權的大人聽見。諾亞擔心歐林會派武裝警衛。「那我們就必須謹慎，」娜迪說，「讓年幼孩子遠離危險。」瑪拉建議老師們同時派代表聯繫工會與醫療人員。一名學生用木湯匙攪拌水樣，老師告訴他任何工人或孩子都不應得到劣質飲水。

The march moved slowly along the indoor avenue. At first only a few teachers joined, but soon mechanics, cooks, and nurses began to walk beside them. They carried a simple message: LET US CHOOSE WHERE WE LIVE. Lena saw children watching through classroom windows and felt both hope and responsibility. A security officer raised his hand to block the road. Nadi stopped immediately and asked to speak. Instead of shouting, she described the evidence and requested a public meeting. The officer looked at the crowd, uncertain. For the first time the people before him were not distant names on a government list.

遊行緩緩沿室內大道前進。起初只有幾位老師參與，很快機械師、廚師和護士也走在一旁。他們舉著簡單的字句：「讓我們選擇居住的地方。」莉娜看見孩子們從教室窗戶觀望，同時感到希望與責任。保全軍官舉手擋住道路。娜迪立刻停下，請求發言。她沒有大喊，而是描述證據，要求公開會議。軍官望著人群，顯得猶豫。此刻站在眼前的人，不再只是政府清單上的遙遠姓名。

The governor responded by cutting the lights in part of the campus. Emergency lamps came on, but some passages remained dark. Lena and Noah helped guide younger students back to their rooms while Mira assisted a child who had fallen. Nadi's staff moved calmly, following the safety plan they had prepared. By evening the march had ended without serious injury, and the demand for a hearing had reached every major department. Mara told Lena that public action did not always bring immediate victory. “Sometimes,” she said, “it gives people permission to imagine a different future.” The next alarm, however, came not from security but from the farm decks: FIRE DETECTED.

總督回應的方式，是切斷部分校園照明。緊急燈亮起，但一些通道仍然漆黑。莉娜與諾亞協助帶年幼學生回房，米拉則扶起跌倒的孩子。娜迪的教職員依事先準備的安全計畫冷靜行動。晚間遊行平安結束，公開聽證的要求也已傳到各大部門。瑪拉告訴莉娜，公共行動不會總能立刻勝利。「有時候，」她說，「它能讓人們敢於想像不同的未來。」但下一次警報並非來自保全，而是農場甲板：「偵測到火災。」
