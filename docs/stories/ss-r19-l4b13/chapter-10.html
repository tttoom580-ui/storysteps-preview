At the first public exhibition, their planned low lift succeeded but Li faltered in the final turn, and the crowd went quiet. Something had changed, but not everybody noticed it in the same order. Song Li listened to the account of the nearest worker, then to someone standing farther away. Their differences were not necessarily lies. Each person had been given a different view and a different reason to be afraid of admitting uncertainty. A score sheet could tell the judges what happened in a program, not why two people had chosen to stand on the ice together. Song Li kept those questions separate. Gu An noticed the word apology in a note that someone had added after the first inspection. The earlier photograph clearly showed a bandage; the newer version did not, so Song Li kept both.

首次公開表演中，低托舉成功，宋梨卻在最後轉身失誤，全場安靜下來。某件事改變了，但人們發現它的順序不同。宋梨先聽近處工作人員的描述，再聽遠處旁觀者說法。差異不一定是謊言：每個人看到的角度不同，承認不確定時所要承擔的風險也不同。分數表可以告訴裁判節目發生了什麼，卻說不清兩個人為何選擇一起站上冰面。宋梨把兩個問題分開。顧安注意到初次檢查後有人在筆記裡加上「道歉」一詞。較早的照片清楚顯示一項「繃帶」，新版卻沒有，宋梨因此保留兩張。

He chose not to hide the offer and let Li decide whether she still wanted to skate with him. Song Li refused to pretend the previous decision had solved everything. The practical result was still there, useful but narrow. For each thing the team knew, there were two things it had not measured. Song Li drew a line through the claims that depended on speculation and asked Gu An to keep the original record intact. The term bargain appeared in the revised file; Song Li checked its meaning against the original page. Gu An noticed the word civilization in a note that someone had added after the first inspection. Gu An repeated the instruction to congratulate, then asked whether anyone had the right to refuse.

顧安不隱瞞邀請，讓宋梨自行決定是否繼續搭檔。宋梨不願假裝前一次決定已解決所有問題。成果確實存在，卻有明確界限。每個已知事項旁都還有未測量的部分。宋梨劃掉靠猜測成立的說法，請顧安保留原始紀錄。修訂檔案出現「交易」一詞，宋梨拿原始頁面核對它的意思。顧安注意到初次檢查後有人在筆記裡加上「文明」一詞。顧安重述「恭賀」的指示，再問當事人是否有權拒絕。

A small disagreement changed the schedule. Song Li wanted to proceed before evidence disappeared; Gu An wanted another check because a wrong conclusion might hurt people who could not easily defend themselves. They did not settle the matter by choosing a leader. They wrote down what a short, reversible test could actually establish. An official memo described the plan as critical, but Song Li refused to treat an adjective as evidence. The term explosion appeared in the revised file; Song Li checked its meaning against the original page. On the next form, the action field read forbid, but the witness had never approved the operation.

一場小爭執改變了時程。宋梨想趁證據消失前行動；顧安要求再核對一次，因為錯誤結論可能傷害難以自辯的人。他們沒有靠選領袖解決爭議，而是列出短暫、可逆測試真正能證明什麼。官方備忘錄形容計畫「關鍵的」，宋梨不願把形容詞當作證據。修訂檔案出現「爆炸」一詞，宋梨拿原始頁面核對它的意思。下一張表單把動作寫成「禁止」，見證人卻從未同意這項操作。

Instead of inventing a romantic explanation, they openly described the injury limits and requested a revised qualifying schedule. The plan had been amended three times, and each change left a trace. Song Li valued those corrections more than a perfect-looking first draft. If the outcome proved disappointing, the group would still be able to identify which assumption needed repair. Gu An watched for the quiet participant who had not yet been invited to speak. On the next form, the action field read illustrate, but the witness had never approved the operation. Gu An repeated the instruction to install, then asked whether anyone had the right to refuse. The summary called the situation mere; Song Li asked which observation justified that description.

兩人沒有編造浪漫藉口，而是說明傷勢限制並要求調整資格賽安排。計畫改了三次，每次修改都留下痕跡。宋梨認為這些修正比看似完美的初稿更有價值。就算結果不理想，團隊仍能知道該修正哪一項假設。顧安也注意到那名一直沒有發言機會的人。下一張表單把動作寫成「圖解說明」，見證人卻從未同意這項操作。顧安重述「安裝」的指示，再問當事人是否有權拒絕。摘要以「僅僅的」形容情況，宋梨追問這種說法有何觀察根據。

An official version reached the room before the practical work was finished. Song Li read it once, then compared it with what they had actually measured. The difference was not always malicious, but it could still hurt somebody. Gu An asked that the correction be written in language the affected people could understand. A score sheet could tell the judges what happened in a program, not why two people had chosen to stand on the ice together. Song Li kept those questions separate. The summary called the situation pregnant; Song Li asked which observation justified that description. One witness used the word prosperous; Gu An noted that a description was not a measurement. A handwritten label read resolution, but no one could say who had written it there.

實際工作還沒完成，官方版本就傳到現場。宋梨讀了一次，再比對真正測得的結果。差異未必都是惡意，卻仍可能傷害人。顧安要求把更正寫成受影響者看得懂的語言。分數表可以告訴裁判節目發生了什麼，卻說不清兩個人為何選擇一起站上冰面。宋梨把兩個問題分開。摘要以「懷孕的」形容情況，宋梨追問這種說法有何觀察根據。一位證人用了「繁榮的」一詞，顧安指出形容並不等於測量。手寫標籤寫著「決議」，卻沒有人知道是誰留下的。

A witness asked whether the team was taking sides. Song Li answered that refusing to invent a fact was not the same as refusing to care. What mattered was whether the procedure protected someone whose story was less convenient to tell. Gu An listened without interrupting and wrote down the question exactly as it had been asked. A handwritten label read revolution, but no one could say who had written it there. The term settler appeared in the revised file; Song Li checked its meaning against the original page. Gu An noticed the word surgeon in a note that someone had added after the first inspection.

一名見證人問團隊是否偏袒。宋梨回答，不捏造事實不代表冷漠。真正重要的是程序能否保護那個故事不方便被說出口的人。顧安沒有打斷，逐字記下原問題。手寫標籤寫著「革命」，卻沒有人知道是誰留下的。修訂檔案出現「遷居者」一詞，宋梨拿原始頁面核對它的意思。顧安注意到初次檢查後有人在筆記裡加上「外科醫師」一詞。

A suspicious complaint arrived before the exhibition's score was announced, claiming Li had staged her stumble to win public sympathy. The organizers' website reproduced the allegation beside a photograph of her injured shoulder. Gu An found Li seated under a poster advertising a musical about an ancient tomb, absurdly cheerful in its gold lettering. He did not tell her to ignore the criticism. He offered his phone so she could see the original footage and decide what to challenge. 'The fall was real,' she said. 'So was the low lift we landed before it.' She asked the coach to file a factual report and the club to protect her medical privacy. The score placed them below the automatic qualification line. Thirty days had become one week, and now the next decision could not be made by applause.

表演分數公布前，主辦單位收到可疑投訴，說宋梨故意跌倒博取同情。官方網站竟把指控和她受傷肩膀的照片放在一起。顧安在一張宣傳古墓音樂劇的海報下找到宋梨，金色大字喜氣得荒謬。他沒有叫她別理會，而是把手機交給她，讓她看原始影片，自行決定反駁什麼。「跌倒是真的。」她說，「之前成功的低托舉也是真的。」她請教練提出事實報告，要求俱樂部保護醫療隱私。分數使他們落在自動晉級線外。三十天變成最後一週，下一個決定不能靠掌聲作出。
