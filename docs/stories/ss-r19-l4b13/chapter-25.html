Months later Li skated a short solo practice while Gu An taught beginners on the other side of the rink. Something had changed, but not everybody noticed it in the same order. Song Li listened to the account of the nearest worker, then to someone standing farther away. Their differences were not necessarily lies. Each person had been given a different view and a different reason to be afraid of admitting uncertainty. Gu An checked the lift against the rehabilitation plan, not the sponsor's desired photograph. Trust grew from stopping safely as often as from moving together. One witness used the word broke; Gu An noted that a description was not a measurement. A handwritten label read championship, but no one could say who had written it there.

數月後，宋梨在冰場一側練短版單人節目，顧安在另一側教初學者。某件事改變了，但人們發現它的順序不同。宋梨先聽近處工作人員的描述，再聽遠處旁觀者說法。差異不一定是謊言：每個人看到的角度不同，承認不確定時所要承擔的風險也不同。顧安依復健計畫檢查托舉，而非照贊助商想要的照片排動作。信任不只來自同步前進，也來自安全喊停。一位證人用了「身無分文的」一詞，顧安指出形容並不等於測量。手寫標籤寫著「錦標賽」，卻沒有人知道是誰留下的。

They agreed to reconsider competition after medical clearance, without turning the relationship into a performance contract. Song Li refused to pretend the previous decision had solved everything. The practical result was still there, useful but narrow. For each thing the team knew, there were two things it had not measured. Song Li drew a line through the claims that depended on speculation and asked Gu An to keep the original record intact. The term concerning appeared in the revised file; Song Li checked its meaning against the original page. Gu An noticed the word electronics in a note that someone had added after the first inspection. A handwritten label read ethnic, but no one could say who had written it there.

兩人同意等醫療許可後再決定是否參賽，不把戀愛當表演合約。宋梨不願假裝前一次決定已解決所有問題。成果確實存在，卻有明確界限。每個已知事項旁都還有未測量的部分。宋梨劃掉靠猜測成立的說法，請顧安保留原始紀錄。修訂檔案出現「關於」一詞，宋梨拿原始頁面核對它的意思。顧安注意到初次檢查後有人在筆記裡加上「電子設備」一詞。手寫標籤寫著「族群文化的」，卻沒有人知道是誰留下的。

A small disagreement changed the schedule. Song Li wanted to proceed before evidence disappeared; Gu An wanted another check because a wrong conclusion might hurt people who could not easily defend themselves. They did not settle the matter by choosing a leader. They wrote down what a short, reversible test could actually establish. An official memo described the plan as fantastic, but Song Li refused to treat an adjective as evidence. The term feast appeared in the revised file; Song Li checked its meaning against the original page. Gu An noticed the word finance in a note that someone had added after the first inspection.

一場小爭執改變了時程。宋梨想趁證據消失前行動；顧安要求再核對一次，因為錯誤結論可能傷害難以自辯的人。他們沒有靠選領袖解決爭議，而是列出短暫、可逆測試真正能證明什麼。官方備忘錄形容計畫「奇幻的」，宋梨不願把形容詞當作證據。修訂檔案出現「盛宴」一詞，宋梨拿原始頁面核對它的意思。顧安注意到初次檢查後有人在筆記裡加上「財務」一詞。

At closing time they met at center ice, held hands by choice, and left their next season unwritten. The plan had been amended three times, and each change left a trace. Song Li valued those corrections more than a perfect-looking first draft. If the outcome proved disappointing, the group would still be able to identify which assumption needed repair. Gu An watched for the quiet participant who had not yet been invited to speak. One witness used the word guilty; Gu An noted that a description was not a measurement. Gu An repeated the instruction to impose, then asked whether anyone had the right to refuse. The term offense appeared in the revised file; Song Li checked its meaning against the original page.

閉館時兩人在冰場中央相會，自願牽起手，沒有讓任何人提前寫好下一季。計畫改了三次，每次修改都留下痕跡。宋梨認為這些修正比看似完美的初稿更有價值。就算結果不理想，團隊仍能知道該修正哪一項假設。顧安也注意到那名一直沒有發言機會的人。一位證人用了「有罪的」一詞，顧安指出形容並不等於測量。顧安重述「強加」的指示，再問當事人是否有權拒絕。修訂檔案出現「罪行」一詞，宋梨拿原始頁面核對它的意思。

An official version reached the room before the practical work was finished. Song Li read it once, then compared it with what they had actually measured. The difference was not always malicious, but it could still hurt somebody. Gu An asked that the correction be written in language the affected people could understand. Gu An checked the lift against the rehabilitation plan, not the sponsor's desired photograph. Trust grew from stopping safely as often as from moving together. The term panel appeared in the revised file; Song Li checked its meaning against the original page. One witness used the word poisonous; Gu An noted that a description was not a measurement. Gu An repeated the instruction to preserve, then asked whether anyone had the right to refuse.

實際工作還沒完成，官方版本就傳到現場。宋梨讀了一次，再比對真正測得的結果。差異未必都是惡意，卻仍可能傷害人。顧安要求把更正寫成受影響者看得懂的語言。顧安依復健計畫檢查托舉，而非照贊助商想要的照片排動作。信任不只來自同步前進，也來自安全喊停。修訂檔案出現「審查小組」一詞，宋梨拿原始頁面核對它的意思。一位證人用了「有毒的」一詞，顧安指出形容並不等於測量。顧安重述「保存」的指示，再問當事人是否有權拒絕。

A witness asked whether the team was taking sides. Song Li answered that refusing to invent a fact was not the same as refusing to care. What mattered was whether the procedure protected someone whose story was less convenient to tell. Gu An listened without interrupting and wrote down the question exactly as it had been asked. A handwritten label read profession, but no one could say who had written it there. The term publication appeared in the revised file; Song Li checked its meaning against the original page. Gu An noticed the word register in a note that someone had added after the first inspection.

一名見證人問團隊是否偏袒。宋梨回答，不捏造事實不代表冷漠。真正重要的是程序能否保護那個故事不方便被說出口的人。顧安沒有打斷，逐字記下原問題。手寫標籤寫著「職業」，卻沒有人知道是誰留下的。修訂檔案出現「出版品」一詞，宋梨拿原始頁面核對它的意思。顧安注意到初次檢查後有人在筆記裡加上「登記」一詞。

After closing time Li and Gu An walked past the rink's little winter garden. Beside a heated shelter for rescued animals, a child was planting a hardy tree with a spade while a volunteer kept watch. Inside, an elderly tortoise moved with determined slowness toward a warm patch of light. The branches began to sway in the wind, and Li laughed at how much she admired an animal that never worried about a perfect score. Gu An offered his hand. She took it, then released it to adjust the strap of her own skate bag. Neither gesture changed anything between them. They had learned to balance with two people moving at different speeds, without pretending an injury could be wished away. Across the ice, the last lights went dark. Tomorrow they would train separately. Tonight they were simply going home together.

閉館後，宋梨和顧安走過冰場旁的小冬日花園。救援動物的暖棚旁，一名孩子拿著鏟子種耐寒小樹，志工在旁照看。棚內一隻年老陸龜慢慢走向暖光，步伐堅定。樹枝在風中搖擺，宋梨笑說自己居然那麼敬佩一隻從不擔心完美分數的動物。顧安伸出手，她牽了一會兒，又放開去調整自己的冰鞋袋。兩個動作都不改變他們之間的感情。他們學會兩個速度不同的人也能保持平衡，不假裝傷勢能靠願望消失。冰場另一端的最後一盞燈熄了。明天各自訓練，今晚只是一起回家。
