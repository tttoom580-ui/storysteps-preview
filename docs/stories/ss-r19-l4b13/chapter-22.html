The pair finished second, missing the title but achieving their first full safe national program together. Something had changed, but not everybody noticed it in the same order. Song Li listened to the account of the nearest worker, then to someone standing farther away. Their differences were not necessarily lies. Each person had been given a different view and a different reason to be afraid of admitting uncertainty. A score sheet could tell the judges what happened in a program, not why two people had chosen to stand on the ice together. Song Li kept those questions separate. Gu An noticed the word associate in a note that someone had added after the first inspection. A handwritten label read cease, but no one could say who had written it there.

兩人得到第二名，沒拿冠軍，卻完成第一次安全的全國賽全套表演。某件事改變了，但人們發現它的順序不同。宋梨先聽近處工作人員的描述，再聽遠處旁觀者說法。差異不一定是謊言：每個人看到的角度不同，承認不確定時所要承擔的風險也不同。分數表可以告訴裁判節目發生了什麼，卻說不清兩個人為何選擇一起站上冰面。宋梨把兩個問題分開。顧安注意到初次檢查後有人在筆記裡加上「助理」一詞。手寫標籤寫著「停止」，卻沒有人知道是誰留下的。

The audience rose before the score appeared, but Li looked first toward the medical official for confirmation she was well. Song Li refused to pretend the previous decision had solved everything. The practical result was still there, useful but narrow. For each thing the team knew, there were two things it had not measured. Song Li drew a line through the claims that depended on speculation and asked Gu An to keep the original record intact. The term claw appeared in the revised file; Song Li checked its meaning against the original page. One witness used the word constructive; Gu An noted that a description was not a measurement. A handwritten label read curve, but no one could say who had written it there.

分數還沒出來觀眾已起立，宋梨卻先看向醫療人員確認自己無恙。宋梨不願假裝前一次決定已解決所有問題。成果確實存在，卻有明確界限。每個已知事項旁都還有未測量的部分。宋梨劃掉靠猜測成立的說法，請顧安保留原始紀錄。修訂檔案出現「爪形鉤」一詞，宋梨拿原始頁面核對它的意思。一位證人用了「建設性的」一詞，顧安指出形容並不等於測量。手寫標籤寫著「弧線」，卻沒有人知道是誰留下的。

A small disagreement changed the schedule. Song Li wanted to proceed before evidence disappeared; Gu An wanted another check because a wrong conclusion might hurt people who could not easily defend themselves. They did not settle the matter by choosing a leader. They wrote down what a short, reversible test could actually establish. An official memo described the plan as delightful, but Song Li refused to treat an adjective as evidence. The summary called the situation economical; Song Li asked which observation justified that description. Gu An noticed the word economist in a note that someone had added after the first inspection.

一場小爭執改變了時程。宋梨想趁證據消失前行動；顧安要求再核對一次，因為錯誤結論可能傷害難以自辯的人。他們沒有靠選領袖解決爭議，而是列出短暫、可逆測試真正能證明什麼。官方備忘錄形容計畫「令人愉快的」，宋梨不願把形容詞當作證據。摘要以「節省成本的」形容情況，宋梨追問這種說法有何觀察根據。顧安注意到初次檢查後有人在筆記裡加上「經濟學家」一詞。

Li refused a sponsor's request to describe the result as a tragic defeat in need of a romantic rescue. The plan had been amended three times, and each change left a trace. Song Li valued those corrections more than a perfect-looking first draft. If the outcome proved disappointing, the group would still be able to identify which assumption needed repair. Gu An watched for the quiet participant who had not yet been invited to speak. Gu An noticed the word enforce in a note that someone had added after the first inspection. A handwritten label read glimpse, but no one could say who had written it there. The term grind appeared in the revised file; Song Li checked its meaning against the original page.

宋梨拒絕贊助商把結果說成需要愛情拯救的悲劇失敗。計畫改了三次，每次修改都留下痕跡。宋梨認為這些修正比看似完美的初稿更有價值。就算結果不理想，團隊仍能知道該修正哪一項假設。顧安也注意到那名一直沒有發言機會的人。顧安注意到初次檢查後有人在筆記裡加上「執行」一詞。手寫標籤寫著「瞥見」，卻沒有人知道是誰留下的。修訂檔案出現「磨損」一詞，宋梨拿原始頁面核對它的意思。

An official version reached the room before the practical work was finished. Song Li read it once, then compared it with what they had actually measured. The difference was not always malicious, but it could still hurt somebody. Gu An asked that the correction be written in language the affected people could understand. A score sheet could tell the judges what happened in a program, not why two people had chosen to stand on the ice together. Song Li kept those questions separate. A note about the infant lay beside the old register, with a date that Song Li could not verify. Gu An noticed the word invention in a note that someone had added after the first inspection. The earlier photograph clearly showed a kettle; the newer version did not, so Song Li kept both.

實際工作還沒完成，官方版本就傳到現場。宋梨讀了一次，再比對真正測得的結果。差異未必都是惡意，卻仍可能傷害人。顧安要求把更正寫成受影響者看得懂的語言。分數表可以告訴裁判節目發生了什麼，卻說不清兩個人為何選擇一起站上冰面。宋梨把兩個問題分開。一張談到「嬰兒」的便條放在舊登記簿旁，日期讓宋梨無法核實。顧安注意到初次檢查後有人在筆記裡加上「發明」一詞。較早的照片清楚顯示一項「水壺」，新版卻沒有，宋梨因此保留兩張。

A witness asked whether the team was taking sides. Song Li answered that refusing to invent a fact was not the same as refusing to care. What mattered was whether the procedure protected someone whose story was less convenient to tell. Gu An listened without interrupting and wrote down the question exactly as it had been asked. A handwritten label read merchant, but no one could say who had written it there. The report claimed merit, but Song Li wanted an example instead of a reassuring noun. One witness used the word moderate; Gu An noted that a description was not a measurement.

一名見證人問團隊是否偏袒。宋梨回答，不捏造事實不代表冷漠。真正重要的是程序能否保護那個故事不方便被說出口的人。顧安沒有打斷，逐字記下原問題。手寫標籤寫著「商人」，卻沒有人知道是誰留下的。報告聲稱有「價值」，宋梨要求具體例子，而不是安撫人的名詞。一位證人用了「適中的」一詞，顧安指出形容並不等於測量。

Even after the group agreed on the immediate step, another question remained. The answer would not arrive through a dramatic announcement. Someone would have to return to the place, compare the earlier notes, and accept the possibility that the next piece of evidence might spoil an attractive theory. Song Li prepared to do exactly that. Gu An checked the lift against the rehabilitation plan, not the sponsor's desired photograph. Trust grew from stopping safely as often as from moving together. Gu An noticed the word orphan in a note that someone had added after the first inspection. A handwritten label read pest, but no one could say who had written it there. The instructions used the verb sue without naming who would carry the risk; Song Li circled that gap.

即使大家同意眼前步驟，另一個問題仍在。答案不會靠戲劇性公告降臨；有人得返回現場、比對舊筆記，並接受新證據可能推翻漂亮理論。宋梨準備做的正是這件事。顧安依復健計畫檢查托舉，而非照贊助商想要的照片排動作。信任不只來自同步前進，也來自安全喊停。顧安注意到初次檢查後有人在筆記裡加上「孤兒」一詞。手寫標籤寫著「害蟲」，卻沒有人知道是誰留下的。指示裡用了「提告」這個動詞，卻沒說誰承擔風險；宋梨圈出這項缺漏。
