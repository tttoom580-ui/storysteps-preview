A celebrity account leaked a photo of Gu An helping Li off the ice and claimed the pair's closeness was staged for votes. Something had changed, but not everybody noticed it in the same order. Song Li listened to the account of the nearest worker, then to someone standing farther away. Their differences were not necessarily lies. Each person had been given a different view and a different reason to be afraid of admitting uncertainty. A score sheet could tell the judges what happened in a program, not why two people had chosen to stand on the ice together. Song Li kept those questions separate. A handwritten label read agent, but no one could say who had written it there. A note about the bin lay beside the old register, with a date that Song Li could not verify.

名人帳號流出顧安扶宋梨離冰場的照片，說兩人的親近都是為拉票演戲。某件事改變了，但人們發現它的順序不同。宋梨先聽近處工作人員的描述，再聽遠處旁觀者說法。差異不一定是謊言：每個人看到的角度不同，承認不確定時所要承擔的風險也不同。分數表可以告訴裁判節目發生了什麼，卻說不清兩個人為何選擇一起站上冰面。宋梨把兩個問題分開。手寫標籤寫著「代理人」，卻沒有人知道是誰留下的。一張談到「箱子」的便條放在舊登記簿旁，日期讓宋梨無法核實。

Gu An proposed competing with a lower difficulty level and accepting whatever score followed. Song Li refused to pretend the previous decision had solved everything. The practical result was still there, useful but narrow. For each thing the team knew, there were two things it had not measured. Song Li drew a line through the claims that depended on speculation and asked Gu An to keep the original record intact. Gu An pointed to the bracelet in the inventory and asked why its location had changed. A handwritten label read burglar, but no one could say who had written it there. The term comment appeared in the revised file; Song Li checked its meaning against the original page.

顧安提議以較低難度參賽，接受真正得到的分數。宋梨不願假裝前一次決定已解決所有問題。成果確實存在，卻有明確界限。每個已知事項旁都還有未測量的部分。宋梨劃掉靠猜測成立的說法，請顧安保留原始紀錄。顧安指向清單上的「手環」，追問其位置為何改變。手寫標籤寫著「竊賊」，卻沒有人知道是誰留下的。修訂檔案出現「評論」一詞，宋梨拿原始頁面核對它的意思。

A small disagreement changed the schedule. Song Li wanted to proceed before evidence disappeared; Gu An wanted another check because a wrong conclusion might hurt people who could not easily defend themselves. They did not settle the matter by choosing a leader. They wrote down what a short, reversible test could actually establish. The instructions used the verb commit without naming who would carry the risk; Song Li circled that gap. One witness used the word definite; Gu An noted that a description was not a measurement. A handwritten label read destruction, but no one could say who had written it there.

一場小爭執改變了時程。宋梨想趁證據消失前行動；顧安要求再核對一次，因為錯誤結論可能傷害難以自辯的人。他們沒有靠選領袖解決爭議，而是列出短暫、可逆測試真正能證明什麼。指示裡用了「承諾」這個動詞，卻沒說誰承擔風險；宋梨圈出這項缺漏。一位證人用了「明確的」一詞，顧安指出形容並不等於測量。手寫標籤寫著「毀壞」，卻沒有人知道是誰留下的。

Li demanded the club protect her rehabilitation privacy without issuing a false denial about feelings neither had defined. The plan had been amended three times, and each change left a trace. Song Li valued those corrections more than a perfect-looking first draft. If the outcome proved disappointing, the group would still be able to identify which assumption needed repair. Gu An watched for the quiet participant who had not yet been invited to speak. An official memo described the plan as durable, but Song Li refused to treat an adjective as evidence. The instructions used the verb emerge without naming who would carry the risk; Song Li circled that gap. Gu An noticed the word era in a note that someone had added after the first inspection.

宋梨要求俱樂部保障復健隱私，不要替兩人尚未定義的感情造假否認。計畫改了三次，每次修改都留下痕跡。宋梨認為這些修正比看似完美的初稿更有價值。就算結果不理想，團隊仍能知道該修正哪一項假設。顧安也注意到那名一直沒有發言機會的人。官方備忘錄形容計畫「耐用的」，宋梨不願把形容詞當作證據。指示裡用了「走出」這個動詞，卻沒說誰承擔風險；宋梨圈出這項缺漏。顧安注意到初次檢查後有人在筆記裡加上「年代」一詞。

An official version reached the room before the practical work was finished. Song Li read it once, then compared it with what they had actually measured. The difference was not always malicious, but it could still hurt somebody. Gu An asked that the correction be written in language the affected people could understand. A score sheet could tell the judges what happened in a program, not why two people had chosen to stand on the ice together. Song Li kept those questions separate. One witness used the word faithful; Gu An noted that a description was not a measurement. A handwritten label read fame, but no one could say who had written it there. The term hardship appeared in the revised file; Song Li checked its meaning against the original page.

實際工作還沒完成，官方版本就傳到現場。宋梨讀了一次，再比對真正測得的結果。差異未必都是惡意，卻仍可能傷害人。顧安要求把更正寫成受影響者看得懂的語言。分數表可以告訴裁判節目發生了什麼，卻說不清兩個人為何選擇一起站上冰面。宋梨把兩個問題分開。一位證人用了「忠實的」一詞，顧安指出形容並不等於測量。手寫標籤寫著「名聲」，卻沒有人知道是誰留下的。修訂檔案出現「困境」一詞，宋梨拿原始頁面核對它的意思。

A witness asked whether the team was taking sides. Song Li answered that refusing to invent a fact was not the same as refusing to care. What mattered was whether the procedure protected someone whose story was less convenient to tell. Gu An listened without interrupting and wrote down the question exactly as it had been asked. The term inflation appeared in the revised file; Song Li checked its meaning against the original page. On the next form, the action field read occupy, but the witness had never approved the operation. A handwritten label read procedure, but no one could say who had written it there.

一名見證人問團隊是否偏袒。宋梨回答，不捏造事實不代表冷漠。真正重要的是程序能否保護那個故事不方便被說出口的人。顧安沒有打斷，逐字記下原問題。修訂檔案出現「通貨膨脹」一詞，宋梨拿原始頁面核對它的意思。下一張表單把動作寫成「佔據」，見證人卻從未同意這項操作。手寫標籤寫著「程序」，卻沒有人知道是誰留下的。

Even after the group agreed on the immediate step, another question remained. The answer would not arrive through a dramatic announcement. Someone would have to return to the place, compare the earlier notes, and accept the possibility that the next piece of evidence might spoil an attractive theory. Song Li prepared to do exactly that. Gu An checked the lift against the rehabilitation plan, not the sponsor's desired photograph. Trust grew from stopping safely as often as from moving together. Gu An repeated the instruction to pursue, then asked whether anyone had the right to refuse. The summary called the situation tragic; Song Li asked which observation justified that description. One witness used the word tribal; Gu An noted that a description was not a measurement.

即使大家同意眼前步驟，另一個問題仍在。答案不會靠戲劇性公告降臨；有人得返回現場、比對舊筆記，並接受新證據可能推翻漂亮理論。宋梨準備做的正是這件事。顧安依復健計畫檢查托舉，而非照贊助商想要的照片排動作。信任不只來自同步前進，也來自安全喊停。顧安重述「追查」的指示，再問當事人是否有權拒絕。摘要以「悲劇的」形容情況，宋梨追問這種說法有何觀察根據。一位證人用了「部族的」一詞，顧安指出形容並不等於測量。
