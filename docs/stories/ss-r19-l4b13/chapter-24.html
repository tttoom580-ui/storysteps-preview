Li chose a gradual rehabilitation calendar while Gu An accepted a separate teaching role; neither career depended on permanent togetherness. Something had changed, but not everybody noticed it in the same order. Song Li listened to the account of the nearest worker, then to someone standing farther away. Their differences were not necessarily lies. Each person had been given a different view and a different reason to be afraid of admitting uncertainty. A score sheet could tell the judges what happened in a program, not why two people had chosen to stand on the ice together. Song Li kept those questions separate. An official memo described the plan as ambiguous, but Song Li refused to treat an adjective as evidence. The instructions used the verb await without naming who would carry the risk; Song Li circled that gap.

宋梨選擇循序復健，顧安接受獨立教學職務，兩人的職涯都不以永遠綁在一起為前提。某件事改變了，但人們發現它的順序不同。宋梨先聽近處工作人員的描述，再聽遠處旁觀者說法。差異不一定是謊言：每個人看到的角度不同，承認不確定時所要承擔的風險也不同。分數表可以告訴裁判節目發生了什麼，卻說不清兩個人為何選擇一起站上冰面。宋梨把兩個問題分開。官方備忘錄形容計畫「模糊的」，宋梨不願把形容詞當作證據。指示裡用了「等待」這個動詞，卻沒說誰承擔風險；宋梨圈出這項缺漏。

They told their coach after setting personal boundaries, rather than announcing a relationship to win publicity. Song Li refused to pretend the previous decision had solved everything. The practical result was still there, useful but narrow. For each thing the team knew, there were two things it had not measured. Song Li drew a line through the claims that depended on speculation and asked Gu An to keep the original record intact. Gu An noticed the word category in a note that someone had added after the first inspection. Gu An repeated the instruction to cope, then asked whether anyone had the right to refuse. The instructions used the verb correspond without naming who would carry the risk; Song Li circled that gap.

兩人先建立私人界線才告訴教練，沒有為宣傳而公開戀情。宋梨不願假裝前一次決定已解決所有問題。成果確實存在，卻有明確界限。每個已知事項旁都還有未測量的部分。宋梨劃掉靠猜測成立的說法，請顧安保留原始紀錄。顧安注意到初次檢查後有人在筆記裡加上「類別」一詞。顧安重述「應對」的指示，再問當事人是否有權拒絕。指示裡用了「通信」這個動詞，卻沒說誰承擔風險；宋梨圈出這項缺漏。

A small disagreement changed the schedule. Song Li wanted to proceed before evidence disappeared; Gu An wanted another check because a wrong conclusion might hurt people who could not easily defend themselves. They did not settle the matter by choosing a leader. They wrote down what a short, reversible test could actually establish. The term determination appeared in the revised file; Song Li checked its meaning against the original page. Gu An noticed the word divorce in a note that someone had added after the first inspection. Gu An repeated the instruction to ensure, then asked whether anyone had the right to refuse.

一場小爭執改變了時程。宋梨想趁證據消失前行動；顧安要求再核對一次，因為錯誤結論可能傷害難以自辯的人。他們沒有靠選領袖解決爭議，而是列出短暫、可逆測試真正能證明什麼。修訂檔案出現「決心」一詞，宋梨拿原始頁面核對它的意思。顧安注意到初次檢查後有人在筆記裡加上「離婚」一詞。顧安重述「確保」的指示，再問當事人是否有權拒絕。

They agreed to reconsider competition after medical clearance, without turning the relationship into a performance contract. The plan had been amended three times, and each change left a trace. Song Li valued those corrections more than a perfect-looking first draft. If the outcome proved disappointing, the group would still be able to identify which assumption needed repair. Gu An watched for the quiet participant who had not yet been invited to speak. People disagreed about evidence; Song Li separated the feeling from the facts they could test. The summary called the situation flexible; Song Li asked which observation justified that description. Gu An pointed to the gown in the inventory and asked why its location had changed.

兩人同意等醫療許可後再決定是否參賽，不把戀愛當表演合約。計畫改了三次，每次修改都留下痕跡。宋梨認為這些修正比看似完美的初稿更有價值。就算結果不理想，團隊仍能知道該修正哪一項假設。顧安也注意到那名一直沒有發言機會的人。大家對「證據」意見不同，宋梨把感受與可檢驗的事實分開。摘要以「彈性的」形容情況，宋梨追問這種說法有何觀察根據。顧安指向清單上的「洋裝」，追問其位置為何改變。

An official version reached the room before the practical work was finished. Song Li read it once, then compared it with what they had actually measured. The difference was not always malicious, but it could still hurt somebody. Gu An asked that the correction be written in language the affected people could understand. A score sheet could tell the judges what happened in a program, not why two people had chosen to stand on the ice together. Song Li kept those questions separate. One witness used the word grammatical; Gu An noted that a description was not a measurement. A handwritten label read hush, but no one could say who had written it there. The term innocence appeared in the revised file; Song Li checked its meaning against the original page.

實際工作還沒完成，官方版本就傳到現場。宋梨讀了一次，再比對真正測得的結果。差異未必都是惡意，卻仍可能傷害人。顧安要求把更正寫成受影響者看得懂的語言。分數表可以告訴裁判節目發生了什麼，卻說不清兩個人為何選擇一起站上冰面。宋梨把兩個問題分開。一位證人用了「語法的」一詞，顧安指出形容並不等於測量。手寫標籤寫著「安靜」，卻沒有人知道是誰留下的。修訂檔案出現「清白」一詞，宋梨拿原始頁面核對它的意思。

A witness asked whether the team was taking sides. Song Li answered that refusing to invent a fact was not the same as refusing to care. What mattered was whether the procedure protected someone whose story was less convenient to tell. Gu An listened without interrupting and wrote down the question exactly as it had been asked. The term limitation appeared in the revised file; Song Li checked its meaning against the original page. One witness used the word mechanical; Gu An noted that a description was not a measurement. A handwritten label read millionaire, but no one could say who had written it there.

一名見證人問團隊是否偏袒。宋梨回答，不捏造事實不代表冷漠。真正重要的是程序能否保護那個故事不方便被說出口的人。顧安沒有打斷，逐字記下原問題。修訂檔案出現「限制」一詞，宋梨拿原始頁面核對它的意思。一位證人用了「機械的」一詞，顧安指出形容並不等於測量。手寫標籤寫著「百萬富翁」，卻沒有人知道是誰留下的。

Even after the group agreed on the immediate step, another question remained. The answer would not arrive through a dramatic announcement. Someone would have to return to the place, compare the earlier notes, and accept the possibility that the next piece of evidence might spoil an attractive theory. Song Li prepared to do exactly that. Gu An checked the lift against the rehabilitation plan, not the sponsor's desired photograph. Trust grew from stopping safely as often as from moving together. An official memo described the plan as severe, but Song Li refused to treat an adjective as evidence. The term sightseeing appeared in the revised file; Song Li checked its meaning against the original page. Gu An wrote significance in the margin and asked how the team would recognize it in practice.

即使大家同意眼前步驟，另一個問題仍在。答案不會靠戲劇性公告降臨；有人得返回現場、比對舊筆記，並接受新證據可能推翻漂亮理論。宋梨準備做的正是這件事。顧安依復健計畫檢查托舉，而非照贊助商想要的照片排動作。信任不只來自同步前進，也來自安全喊停。官方備忘錄形容計畫「嚴重的」，宋梨不願把形容詞當作證據。修訂檔案出現「觀光」一詞，宋梨拿原始頁面核對它的意思。顧安在頁邊寫下「重要性」，追問團隊實際上如何辨認。
