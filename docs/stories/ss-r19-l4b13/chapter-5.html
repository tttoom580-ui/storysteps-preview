The club chose a sentimental love theme that neither skater liked, assuming the press would accept a staged romance more easily than a competitive pair. Something had changed, but not everybody noticed it in the same order. Song Li listened to the account of the nearest worker, then to someone standing farther away. Their differences were not necessarily lies. Each person had been given a different view and a different reason to be afraid of admitting uncertainty. Gu An checked the lift against the rehabilitation plan, not the sponsor's desired photograph. Trust grew from stopping safely as often as from moving together. The instructions used the verb abandon without naming who would carry the risk; Song Li circled that gap. Gu An noticed the word adjust in a note that someone had added after the first inspection.

俱樂部挑了兩人都不喜歡的煽情愛情配樂，以為媒體更愛假情侶而不是競技搭檔。某件事改變了，但人們發現它的順序不同。宋梨先聽近處工作人員的描述，再聽遠處旁觀者說法。差異不一定是謊言：每個人看到的角度不同，承認不確定時所要承擔的風險也不同。顧安依復健計畫檢查托舉，而非照贊助商想要的照片排動作。信任不只來自同步前進，也來自安全喊停。指示裡用了「拋下」這個動詞，卻沒說誰承擔風險；宋梨圈出這項缺漏。顧安注意到初次檢查後有人在筆記裡加上「調整」一詞。

Li signed the plan only after the club agreed she could stop any element without penalty or humiliation. Song Li refused to pretend the previous decision had solved everything. The practical result was still there, useful but narrow. For each thing the team knew, there were two things it had not measured. Song Li drew a line through the claims that depended on speculation and asked Gu An to keep the original record intact. A handwritten label read allowance, but no one could say who had written it there. The term anxiety appeared in the revised file; Song Li checked its meaning against the original page. Gu An noticed the word canoe in a note that someone had added after the first inspection.

俱樂部同意她可無條件停止動作後，宋梨才簽下訓練計畫。宋梨不願假裝前一次決定已解決所有問題。成果確實存在，卻有明確界限。每個已知事項旁都還有未測量的部分。宋梨劃掉靠猜測成立的說法，請顧安保留原始紀錄。手寫標籤寫著「津貼」，卻沒有人知道是誰留下的。修訂檔案出現「焦慮」一詞，宋梨拿原始頁面核對它的意思。顧安注意到初次檢查後有人在筆記裡加上「獨木舟」一詞。

A small disagreement changed the schedule. Song Li wanted to proceed before evidence disappeared; Gu An wanted another check because a wrong conclusion might hurt people who could not easily defend themselves. They did not settle the matter by choosing a leader. They wrote down what a short, reversible test could actually establish. Gu An noticed the word device in a note that someone had added after the first inspection. An official memo described the plan as distinct, but Song Li refused to treat an adjective as evidence. The term enlarge appeared in the revised file; Song Li checked its meaning against the original page.

Li and Gu An replaced it with a piece built around two independent melodies that gradually learned to meet. The plan had been amended three times, and each change left a trace. Song Li valued those corrections more than a perfect-looking first draft. If the outcome proved disappointing, the group would still be able to identify which assumption needed repair. Gu An watched for the quiet participant who had not yet been invited to speak. The summary called the situation favorable; Song Li asked which observation justified that description. Gu An noticed the word idiom in a note that someone had added after the first inspection. A handwritten label read idol, but no one could say who had written it there.

宋梨和顧安換成兩條獨立旋律逐漸交會的音樂。計畫改了三次，每次修改都留下痕跡。宋梨認為這些修正比看似完美的初稿更有價值。就算結果不理想，團隊仍能知道該修正哪一項假設。顧安也注意到那名一直沒有發言機會的人。摘要以「有利的」形容情況，宋梨追問這種說法有何觀察根據。顧安注意到初次檢查後有人在筆記裡加上「慣用語」一詞。手寫標籤寫著「偶像」，卻沒有人知道是誰留下的。

An official version reached the room before the practical work was finished. Song Li read it once, then compared it with what they had actually measured. The difference was not always malicious, but it could still hurt somebody. Gu An asked that the correction be written in language the affected people could understand. Gu An checked the lift against the rehabilitation plan, not the sponsor's desired photograph. Trust grew from stopping safely as often as from moving together. An official memo described the plan as imaginative, but Song Li refused to treat an adjective as evidence. 實際工作還沒完成，官方版本就傳到現場。宋梨讀了一次，再比對真正測得的結果。差異未必都是惡意，卻仍可能傷害人。顧安要求把更正寫成受影響者看得懂的語言。顧安依復健計畫檢查托舉，而非照贊助商想要的照片排動作。信任不只來自同步前進，也來自安全喊停。官方備忘錄形容計畫「有想像力的」，宋梨不願把形容詞當作證據。修訂檔案出現「影響」一詞，宋梨拿原始頁面核對它的意思。顧安注意到初次檢查後有人在筆記裡加上「反對意見」一詞。

A witness asked whether the team was taking sides. Song Li answered that refusing to invent a fact was not the same as refusing to care. What mattered was whether the procedure protected someone whose story was less convenient to tell. Gu An listened without interrupting and wrote down the question exactly as it had been asked. The term prosperity appeared in the revised file; Song Li checked its meaning against the original page.

一名見證人問團隊是否偏袒。宋梨回答，不捏造事實不代表冷漠。真正重要的是程序能否保護那個故事不方便被說出口的人。顧安沒有打斷，逐字記下原問題。顧安注意到初次檢查後有人在筆記裡加上「管弦樂隊」一詞。官方備忘錄形容計畫「便攜的」，宋梨不願把形容詞當作證據。修訂檔案出現「繁榮」一詞，宋梨拿原始頁面核對它的意思。

Even after the group agreed on the immediate step, another question remained. The answer would not arrive through a dramatic announcement. Someone would have to return to the place, compare the earlier notes, and accept the possibility that the next piece of evidence might spoil an attractive theory. Song Li prepared to do exactly that. A score sheet could tell the judges what happened in a program, not why two people had chosen to stand on the ice together. Song Li kept those questions separate. The term protest appeared in the revised file; Song Li checked its meaning against the original page. On the next form, the action field read seize, but the witness had never approved the operation. A handwritten label read syllable, but no one could say who had written it there.

即使大家同意眼前步驟，另一個問題仍在。答案不會靠戲劇性公告降臨；有人得返回現場、比對舊筆記，並接受新證據可能推翻漂亮理論。宋梨準備做的正是這件事。分數表可以告訴裁判節目發生了什麼，卻說不清兩個人為何選擇一起站上冰面。宋梨把兩個問題分開。修訂檔案出現「抗議」一詞，宋梨拿原始頁面核對它的意思。下一張表單把動作寫成「奪取」，見證人卻從未同意這項操作。手寫標籤寫著「音節」，卻沒有人知道是誰留下的。
