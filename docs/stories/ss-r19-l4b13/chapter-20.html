Warm-up pain returned sharply, and Li had to decide whether she could complete even the revised routine safely. Something had changed, but not everybody noticed it in the same order. Song Li listened to the account of the nearest worker, then to someone standing farther away. Their differences were not necessarily lies. Each person had been given a different view and a different reason to be afraid of admitting uncertainty. A score sheet could tell the judges what happened in a program, not why two people had chosen to stand on the ice together. Song Li kept those questions separate. The instructions used the verb absorb without naming who would carry the risk; Song Li circled that gap. Gu An noticed the word amateur in a note that someone had added after the first inspection.

暖身時肩部疼痛再度加劇，宋梨必須判斷修改後的表演是否仍然安全。某件事改變了，但人們發現它的順序不同。宋梨先聽近處工作人員的描述，再聽遠處旁觀者說法。差異不一定是謊言：每個人看到的角度不同，承認不確定時所要承擔的風險也不同。分數表可以告訴裁判節目發生了什麼，卻說不清兩個人為何選擇一起站上冰面。宋梨把兩個問題分開。指示裡用了「承擔」這個動詞，卻沒說誰承擔風險；宋梨圈出這項缺漏。顧安注意到初次檢查後有人在筆記裡加上「業餘的」一詞。

Li realized competition could be fierce without making opponents into villains. Song Li refused to pretend the previous decision had solved everything. The practical result was still there, useful but narrow. For each thing the team knew, there were two things it had not measured. Song Li drew a line through the claims that depended on speculation and asked Gu An to keep the original record intact. A handwritten label read charity, but no one could say who had written it there. The term departure appeared in the revised file; Song Li checked its meaning against the original page. Gu An noticed the word disaster in a note that someone had added after the first inspection.

宋梨發現競爭可以激烈，卻不需要把對手變成壞人。宋梨不願假裝前一次決定已解決所有問題。成果確實存在，卻有明確界限。每個已知事項旁都還有未測量的部分。宋梨劃掉靠猜測成立的說法，請顧安保留原始紀錄。手寫標籤寫著「慈善」，卻沒有人知道是誰留下的。修訂檔案出現「離場」一詞，宋梨拿原始頁面核對它的意思。顧安注意到初次檢查後有人在筆記裡加上「災難」一詞。

A small disagreement changed the schedule. Song Li wanted to proceed before evidence disappeared; Gu An wanted another check because a wrong conclusion might hurt people who could not easily defend themselves. They did not settle the matter by choosing a leader. They wrote down what a short, reversible test could actually establish. Gu An noticed the word dodge in a note that someone had added after the first inspection. An official memo described the plan as fatal, but Song Li refused to treat an adjective as evidence. The instructions used the verb furnish without naming who would carry the risk; Song Li circled that gap.

一場小爭執改變了時程。宋梨想趁證據消失前行動；顧安要求再核對一次，因為錯誤結論可能傷害難以自辯的人。他們沒有靠選領袖解決爭議，而是列出短暫、可逆測試真正能證明什麼。顧安注意到初次檢查後有人在筆記裡加上「閃躲」一詞。官方備忘錄形容計畫「致命的」，宋梨不願把形容詞當作證據。指示裡用了「提供」這個動詞，卻沒說誰承擔風險；宋梨圈出這項缺漏。

She withdrew from one risky element with her coach's support; Gu An accepted the change without blaming her. The plan had been amended three times, and each change left a trace. Song Li valued those corrections more than a perfect-looking first draft. If the outcome proved disappointing, the group would still be able to identify which assumption needed repair. Gu An watched for the quiet participant who had not yet been invited to speak. The term graduation appeared in the revised file; Song Li checked its meaning against the original page. Gu An noticed the word inspection in a note that someone had added after the first inspection. An official memo described the plan as nutritious, but Song Li refused to treat an adjective as evidence.

她在教練支持下刪去一項高風險動作，顧安沒有責怪。計畫改了三次，每次修改都留下痕跡。宋梨認為這些修正比看似完美的初稿更有價值。就算結果不理想，團隊仍能知道該修正哪一項假設。顧安也注意到那名一直沒有發言機會的人。修訂檔案出現「畢業」一詞，宋梨拿原始頁面核對它的意思。顧安注意到初次檢查後有人在筆記裡加上「檢查」一詞。官方備忘錄形容計畫「有營養的」，宋梨不願把形容詞當作證據。

An official version reached the room before the practical work was finished. Song Li read it once, then compared it with what they had actually measured. The difference was not always malicious, but it could still hurt somebody. Gu An asked that the correction be written in language the affected people could understand. A score sheet could tell the judges what happened in a program, not why two people had chosen to stand on the ice together. Song Li kept those questions separate. A handwritten label read professional, but no one could say who had written it there. The term protein appeared in the revised file; Song Li checked its meaning against the original page. Gu An noticed the word revision in a note that someone had added after the first inspection.

實際工作還沒完成，官方版本就傳到現場。宋梨讀了一次，再比對真正測得的結果。差異未必都是惡意，卻仍可能傷害人。顧安要求把更正寫成受影響者看得懂的語言。分數表可以告訴裁判節目發生了什麼，卻說不清兩個人為何選擇一起站上冰面。宋梨把兩個問題分開。手寫標籤寫著「專業的」，卻沒有人知道是誰留下的。修訂檔案出現「蛋白質」一詞，宋梨拿原始頁面核對它的意思。顧安注意到初次檢查後有人在筆記裡加上「修訂」一詞。

A witness asked whether the team was taking sides. Song Li answered that refusing to invent a fact was not the same as refusing to care. What mattered was whether the procedure protected someone whose story was less convenient to tell. Gu An listened without interrupting and wrote down the question exactly as it had been asked. Gu An noticed the word romance in a note that someone had added after the first inspection. The earlier photograph clearly showed a sculpture; the newer version did not, so Song Li kept both. The term site appeared in the revised file; Song Li checked its meaning against the original page.

一名見證人問團隊是否偏袒。宋梨回答，不捏造事實不代表冷漠。真正重要的是程序能否保護那個故事不方便被說出口的人。顧安沒有打斷，逐字記下原問題。顧安注意到初次檢查後有人在筆記裡加上「浪漫故事」一詞。較早的照片清楚顯示一項「雕塑」，新版卻沒有，宋梨因此保留兩張。修訂檔案出現「場地」一詞，宋梨拿原始頁面核對它的意思。

Even after the group agreed on the immediate step, another question remained. The answer would not arrive through a dramatic announcement. Someone would have to return to the place, compare the earlier notes, and accept the possibility that the next piece of evidence might spoil an attractive theory. Song Li prepared to do exactly that. Gu An checked the lift against the rehabilitation plan, not the sponsor's desired photograph. Trust grew from stopping safely as often as from moving together. The term split appeared in the revised file; Song Li checked its meaning against the original page. One witness used the word thorough; Gu An noted that a description was not a measurement. A handwritten label read yawn, but no one could say who had written it there.

即使大家同意眼前步驟，另一個問題仍在。答案不會靠戲劇性公告降臨；有人得返回現場、比對舊筆記，並接受新證據可能推翻漂亮理論。宋梨準備做的正是這件事。顧安依復健計畫檢查托舉，而非照贊助商想要的照片排動作。信任不只來自同步前進，也來自安全喊停。修訂檔案出現「差異」一詞，宋梨拿原始頁面核對它的意思。一位證人用了「徹底的」一詞，顧安指出形容並不等於測量。手寫標籤寫著「打呵欠」，卻沒有人知道是誰留下的。
