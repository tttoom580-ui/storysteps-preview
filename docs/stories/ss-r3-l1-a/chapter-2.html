河邊的影片

The River Video

The next morning was sunny. Mia's cousin Leo came to school with a short video on his phone. His family lived south of town, near a river and a field of sheep. Leo was a helpful guy. He was not famous, but he loved science and could make a broken light work with one small tool. “I made this video on Friday evening,” he said. “I was filming the river for my class.” He showed them the water, the grass, and the white sheep. Then a school truck passed over the bridge behind them. Mia asked him to stop the video. On the back of the truck was a tall box with a little piece of yellow paper.

隔天早上天氣晴朗。米雅的表弟里歐帶著手機裡的一段短片來學校。他的家住在城南，靠近河流和一片有羊的草地。里歐是個樂於助人的男孩。他並不出名，卻熱愛科學，還能用一個小工具修好故障的燈。「這是我星期五傍晚拍的影片，」他說，「我在拍河流，準備交作業。」他們看到河水、青草和白羊。接著，一輛學校貨車從後方的橋上開過。米雅請他暫停影片。車尾有一個高箱子，上頭貼著一小片黃色紙張。

“The paper may be like ours,” Ben said. He did not say it was the same paper. Mia asked Leo to bring the video to Ms. Lin. At the office, they looked at the screen together. Ms. Lin used her pen to add the time to their notes. It was six in the evening, after the last student left school. “But that truck carries chairs and sports things,” she said. “It could also carry our star. We need to ask the driver.” Leo smiled and bit his lip. “My uncle knows him,” he said. “They work near the river.” Mia thanked him, but she did not want anyone to make a phone call without the teacher.

「那張紙可能跟我們的很像，」班說。他沒有說兩張一定相同。米雅請里歐把影片拿給林老師看。在辦公室裡，他們一起看著螢幕。林老師用筆把時間加進筆記：傍晚六點，最後一位學生離校以後。「但是那輛貨車會載椅子和運動用品，」她說，「也有可能載過星星。我們需要問司機。」里歐笑了笑，咬著嘴唇。「我叔叔認識他，」他說，「他們在河邊附近工作。」米雅謝過里歐，但不希望有人繞過老師私下打電話。

The club could not look inside the truck. They could only ask about its trip. Ms. Lin said that the school would fill a form and send it to the bus and truck office. “That is how we use our power,” she told them. “We can ask a clear question instead of telling a story we cannot prove.” Jo was not happy. “Will they answer today?” she asked. “Maybe not,” said Ms. Lin. “They have other work, too.” Mia looked again at the video. She could see two people near the truck, but their faces were too small. It was wrong to guess who they were. She wrote only what the picture truly showed.

社團不能直接查看貨車裡面，只能詢問那天的行程。林老師說，學校會填好表格，送交車輛管理辦公室。「我們應該這樣使用自己的權力，」她說，「提出清楚的問題，而不是講一個還無法證明的故事。」喬有點不高興。「他們今天會回答嗎？」她問。「也許不會，」林老師說，「他們也有其他工作。」米雅再次看影片。她能看到貨車旁有兩個人，但臉太小，根本認不出來。隨便猜測身分是不對的。她只寫下畫面真正顯示的事情。

On the way home, Leo stopped by the school gate to talk to Ben. “I did not know my video could help anyone,” he said. “I only wanted to show the river to my class.” Ben told him that simple things could be useful when people looked at them with care. They checked one picture from the video again, but this time they did not try to guess the driver's face. Instead they looked for a clear sign on the truck. There was a white mark near the back wheel, and the school name was written above it. That helped them see that the truck really belonged to the school. Mia came over and asked Leo if he wanted his name in the club book. He said yes, but he did not want to be called the person who found the star. “I only brought a picture,” he said. “The group is doing the work.”

回家路上，里歐在校門旁停下來和班說話。「我不知道自己的影片能幫到別人，」他說，「原本只是想把河流拍給班上看。」班告訴他，只要仔細觀察，簡單的東西也可能有用。他們再次查看影片中的一張畫面，但這回沒有試著猜司機的臉，而是找貨車上的清楚標記。後輪附近有個白色記號，上方寫著學校名稱。這幫助他們確認，那輛車確實屬於學校。米雅走過來，問里歐願不願意把名字寫進社團筆記。他說願意，但不想被稱為找到星星的人。「我只是帶來照片，」他說，「真正努力的是大家。」

Later that day, the children made a new page in the club book. They drew the school, the bridge, and the field. Leo showed where he stood when he made the video. “They drove toward the old sports house,” he said. Mia turned to Ben. “Can you draw that part?” Ben nodded. “I can also draw the road from school.” They added the river to the map and made the line between the two buildings red. It did not mean they had found the missing prize. It meant they knew one place the truck had gone. For the first time, the search was reaching outside the school gate. Mia felt both hope and fear, and she let both feelings stay.

那天下午，孩子們在社團筆記本裡開了新的一頁。他們畫出學校、橋和草地。里歐指出拍影片時自己站的位置。「他們往舊體育館那邊開，」他說。米雅轉頭看班。「可以把那段路畫出來嗎？」班點頭。「我也可以把從學校過去的路畫上去。」他們把河流加進地圖，用紅線連接兩棟建築。這不代表他們找到了失物，只代表知道貨車曾去過一個地方。搜尋第一次延伸到校門外。米雅同時感到希望和害怕，並允許這兩種心情留在心裡。
