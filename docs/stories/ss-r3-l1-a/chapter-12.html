行星教室

The Room of Planets

星星不見的那一天 · 第 12 / 25 章 · 1000以下 · 英中對照 · 校訂中

On Wednesday, the club got an answer. The storage room was open for one hour, and Ms. Lin could take them there. Mia rode her bicycle to school and parked it near the gate. Ben wore jeans and carried the map on his own. “It is mine today,” he said proudly, “because I fixed the torn corner.” Ana picked up a loose piece of paper from the ground. It showed a planet and a large bow made of blue cloth. “This looks like a sign for the science show,” she said. The wind tried to take the paper from her hand, but she held it close to her body. “Some clues are small,” Mia said. “We need to see them before they fly away.”

星期三，社團終於得到答覆。儲藏室會開放一小時，林老師可以帶他們進去。米雅騎腳踏車到校，把車停在校門旁。班穿著牛仔褲，自己拿著地圖。「今天由我保管，」他驕傲地說，「因為我修好了破掉的角落。」安娜從地上撿起一張鬆脫的紙，上面畫著行星和一個用藍布做的大蝴蝶結。「這看起來像科學展的標記，」她說。風吹得紙差點脫手，她立刻把紙貼近身體。「有些線索很小，」米雅說，「飛走以前要先看清楚。」

The room was not dark. Light came through two high windows. One side held sports things, and the other side held models of the sun, moon, and planets. A small box of salt stood near a table because the science club had once used it for an experiment. Jo wanted to open every box, but Ms. Lin said, “We have one hour, so pick the boxes that match our notes.” Ben looked at the labels. “The second one has a yellow seven,” he said. The teacher opened it. There were stars made of paper, but not the old wooden star. Mia did not feel angry. She wrote the result in the book and moved on to the next case.

房間並不昏暗，陽光從兩扇高窗照進來。一邊放著體育用品，另一邊是太陽、月亮與行星模型。桌旁有一小盒鹽，因為科學社以前用它做過實驗。喬想把所有箱子都打開，但林老師說：「我們只有一小時，所以挑和筆記相符的箱子。」班看著標籤。「第二個有黃色七號，」他說。老師打開它，裡面有紙做的星星，卻沒有那顆舊木星星。米雅沒有生氣，只把結果寫進筆記，接著查看下一個箱子。

Soon they found a long case of blue cloth. Ana made a little bow with her hands to show how the cloth had been tied in the photo from Grandmother. “I wish we could see the back of the star,” she said. “Then we would know for sure.” Mia looked at the case and asked, “May we open this one?” The teacher checked its number. “This belongs to the music club,” she said. “I must ask that teacher first.” Ben stood by the door. “So we can wait,” he said. “If we rush, we may hurt something.” The children sat on the floor and talked about why the star mattered to them, not just to the school.

不久，他們找到一個裝著長條藍布的箱子。安娜用手比出一個蝴蝶結的形狀，示範奶奶照片裡布料的綁法。「真希望能看到星星背面，」她說，「那樣就能確定了。」米雅指著箱子問：「可以打開這個嗎？」老師核對編號後說：「這是音樂社的，我得先問他們的老師。」班站在門邊。「那我們就等等，」他說，「太急可能弄壞東西。」孩子們坐在地板上，聊起星星為什麼對他們自己也很重要，而不只是對學校有意義。

A younger student came to the science room because he wanted help with a new language word. He pointed at the model of a planet and asked how to say its name. Ana helped him read the word slowly and then asked him to try it again. Ben showed him a drawing of the planet and its ring. The child smiled when he got the word right. Mia watched and thought about the search. They had come to look for a lost star, but the room belonged to many students with different needs. Nobody should be made to leave simply because the art club had an important problem. Ms. Lin thanked the child for sharing the room with them. After he went back to class, Mia checked their list one last time. They had opened only the boxes they had permission to see. It was slow, but it was a fair way to work.

一名低年級學生來到科學教室，因為他想請人幫忙學一個新的語言單字。他指著行星模型，詢問名稱怎麼念。安娜幫他慢慢讀出來，再請他試一次。班給他看行星與行星環的圖。孩子念對時開心地笑了。米雅看著他，想到這次搜尋。他們是來找失蹤星星的，但這間教室同時屬於許多有不同需求的學生。美術社有重要的問題，也不代表能請其他人都離開。林老師謝謝孩子和大家共用教室。他回班後，米雅最後一次核對清單。他們只打開得到許可的箱子。雖然慢，卻是公平的工作方法。

When the hour ended, they still had not seen the silver star. On the way out, a teacher told them that spring was often a busy time for clubs. The room held things from many years. “Say what you need,” she told Mia, “and I will try to help next week.” Mia thanked her and made a note of the offer. It was easy to feel that everything belonged to the art club because they were looking for one lost thing. But the school had many groups, and every group had work of its own. Mia felt that sharing a room was like sharing a story. You could ask for a page, but you could not take the whole book. She was glad the teachers had helped them ask in the right way.

一小時結束時，他們仍沒見到銀色星星。離開時，一位老師說春天對各個社團通常很忙，這間房裝著多年留下的東西。「告訴我你們需要什麼，」她對米雅說，「下週我會試著幫忙。」米雅道謝，把這個提議記下。因為正在找一件失物，很容易覺得整間儲藏室都應該讓美術社使用；但學校有許多團體，每個團體也都有自己的工作。米雅覺得共用房間就像共讀一本故事書。你可以請別人借一頁，卻不能把整本拿走。她很高興老師們幫助他們用正確方式提出請求。
