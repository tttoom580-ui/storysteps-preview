藍色的教室紀錄

The Blue Room Book

星星不見的那一天 · 第 11 / 25 章 · 1000以下 · 英中對照 · 校訂中

The weather was cold on Tuesday, so Mia wore her blue coat to school. Ms. Lin called the club to the office after class. The school secretary had found a book with all the room keys and the times people used them. She placed the book on a desk under a small fan. “This goes back a thousand days,” she said, “but we only need last Saturday.” Mia could not see the page well because a large dictionary was lying over its corner. The secretary moved the dictionary and showed the lines. There was a name, a time, and the word “storage.” “That room is past the old temple road,” she said. “It is near the school garden and the big tree.”

星期二天氣很冷，米雅穿著藍色外套到學校。下課後，林老師叫社團到辦公室。學校秘書找到一本紀錄所有教室鑰匙與借用時間的簿子。她把簿子放在小風扇下的桌上。「這裡記了將近一千天的資料，」她說，「但我們只需要看上星期六。」米雅看不清楚那一頁，因為一本大字典蓋住了角落。秘書移開字典，讓大家看到一行行文字。上面有名字、時間，還有「儲藏室」這個字。「那間房在舊廟路的另一邊，」她說，「靠近學校花園和大樹。」

Mia wanted to run to the room right away, but the secretary asked them to wait. “It is not a place for students to go alone,” she said. “There may be old chairs, paint, and broken things.” Ben held his pants at the knees as he sat on the floor. “Can you call the person who has the key?” he asked. “Maybe,” the secretary said. “First I must see who is free.” A nurse came in from the hall to pick up a small box. She had been checking a child who felt sick. Mia noticed that even people with important jobs had to wait their turn at the office. She sat quietly and wrote one more sentence in the club book.

米雅想立刻跑去那間房，但秘書請大家等等。「那不是學生可以自己進去的地方，」她說，「裡面可能有舊椅子、顏料和壞掉的東西。」班坐在地上，雙手放在褲子的膝蓋處。「可以打電話給有鑰匙的人嗎？」他問。「也許可以，」秘書說，「但我得先看誰有空。」一名護士從走廊進來拿小箱子。她剛照顧過一名不舒服的孩子。米雅注意到，就算工作重要的人，來辦公室也得排隊等候。她安靜坐著，又在社團筆記本寫下一句話。

While they waited, the secretary opened a window. She could smell food from the school kitchen. “Someone is going to cook soup,” she said. “My car is outside, so I will go home after the meeting.” The bell rang. Ms. Lin came back and read the new note. “This is probably the room we should see next,” she said. “But please do not tell everyone the star is there. We still have no proof.” She used a simple sentence to show the difference: “The room was used on Saturday” was true; “The star is in the room” was only a guess. The club copied both sentences and marked them in different ways.

等待時，秘書打開窗戶。學校廚房傳來食物香氣。「有人準備煮湯了，」她說，「我的車停在外面，開完會就要回家。」鐘聲響起，林老師回來讀了新註記。「這大概是我們下一個該看的地方，」她說，「但別告訴大家星星一定在裡面。我們還沒有證據。」她用兩個簡單句子說明差別：「那間房星期六有人使用」是事實；「星星就在那間房」只是猜測。社團抄下兩句，並用不同方式做標記。

The secretary also showed Mia how a room book could be read. The name in one line told who had the key, and the time beside it told when the door opened. It did not tell what was inside the room. “That is why we keep more than one kind of record,” she said. “A room book can show where people went, but a box book should show where things went.” Mia thought this sounded clear. Ben drew two little books beside each other, one with a door on its cover and the other with a box. Jo said the books should be the same color so nobody would mix them up. Ana disagreed. She thought two different colors would be easier. Ms. Lin asked them to test both ideas before choosing. They made a plan to ask the younger students which system they understood faster. The club was learning to make its work easy for other people, too.

秘書還教米雅如何讀教室借用簿。每一行的姓名表示誰拿了鑰匙，旁邊的時間表示門何時打開；但這不代表能知道房裡放了什麼。「所以我們需要不同種類的紀錄，」她說，「教室簿可以顯示人去了哪裡，箱子簿則應顯示物品去了哪裡。」米雅覺得很清楚。班畫了兩本小簿子，一本封面有門，另一本有箱子。喬說兩本應該同色，才不會弄混；安娜不同意，認為不同顏色更好辨認。林老師請大家先測試再決定。他們計畫問問低年級學生，哪種方式更容易理解。社團也開始學習，如何讓自己的工作對別人更方便。

The group left the office just before lunch. Outside, they passed a tree whose leaves were moving in the wind. Mia thought about the people she had met since the star went missing. They had not all said the same thing, but most had tried to help. “What if we never find it?” she asked Ben. “Then we will still know how to ask better questions,” he answered. Mia smiled. She did not want to stop yet. The secretary had promised to give Ms. Lin an answer in the afternoon. They could wait and use that time to check the map again. The blue book had given them another door to knock on, and they would knock politely when the time came.

大家在午餐前離開辦公室。走到外面時，經過一棵葉子隨風搖動的大樹。米雅想到星星失蹤以來見過的人。他們說的話不完全一樣，卻大多很願意幫忙。「如果我們一直找不到呢？」她問班。「那我們至少也學會問更好的問題，」班回答。米雅笑了。她現在還不想停下來。秘書答應下午會給林老師回覆。他們可以等待，並趁這段時間再次核對地圖。那本藍色簿子又給了他們一扇可以敲的門，而到了適當時候，他們會有禮貌地敲門。
