A Story in the Town Square

星星不見的那一天 · 第 4 / 25 章 · 1000以下 · 英中對照

用互動版查詞、朗讀與收藏

The town hall stood beside a large square. It was a tall building, made from an old factory. A worker at the front desk smiled when Ms. Lin came with the children. “Good morning, sir,” Mia said to the man. He told them that the hall now gave a free service to local schools: they could use its rooms for music and art. A small boy was waiting with his mother near the door. He held a paper butterfly. A cloud passed over the sun, and a strong wind began to blow. The boy let the butterfly drop, and Ben ran to pick it up. “Here you are,” he said. The boy laughed and thanked him.

鎮公所大廳在一座大廣場旁，是由舊工廠改建成的高樓。林老師帶孩子們到櫃台時，工作人員露出笑容。「早安，先生，」米雅向他問好。他告訴大家，大廳現在為當地學校提供免費服務，學校可以借用房間辦音樂和美術活動。一個小男孩和媽媽正在門邊等候，手上拿著紙蝴蝶。雲遮住了太陽，強風開始吹起。男孩不小心讓蝴蝶掉落，班跑過去幫他撿起。「給你，」班說。男孩笑著道謝。

The clerk showed them a page about Friday's deliveries. “A school truck came here,” he said, “but the driver did not leave a star.” He pointed to a list with a large box and six chairs. Mia did not shake her head or say that he was wrong. She asked to see the rest of the page. There was a note beside the box: “Move to the stage room.” Ms. Lin asked where that room was. “At the back,” the man said, “near the old door.” He gave them a map because the long hall was easy to get lost in. Mia noticed that the map was cheap paper, but it was still very useful.

櫃台人員拿出星期五的送貨紀錄。「學校貨車有來這裡，」他說，「但司機沒有留下星星。」他指著一張列著一個大箱子和六張椅子的清單。米雅沒有搖頭，也沒有說他記錯了，而是請他讓她看清單的其餘部分。大箱子旁有一句備註：「移到舞台室。」林老師問舞台室在哪裡。「在後面，靠近舊門。」男子給了他們一張地圖，因為長長的走廊很容易迷路。米雅注意到地圖印在便宜的紙上，卻仍然非常有用。

Inside the stage room, a woman was putting tomato plants near the window. “We grow these for the town show,” she explained. “We want people to eat healthy food.” She told the children a story about a play from last year. During the play, the town lost power for ten minutes, and the actors kept singing without lights. Mia listened, then asked about the Friday box. “I did not open it,” the woman said. “One worker came to take it away.” She pointed toward another door. “He said it belonged to the school and should not stay here.” This answer was important because it changed the direction of the search again.

舞台室裡，一名女子正在窗邊擺放番茄盆栽。「這是鎮上表演要展示的，」她解釋，「我們希望大家吃得健康。」她向孩子們說起去年的一場表演。當時全鎮停電十分鐘，演員仍在沒有燈光的情況下繼續唱歌。米雅聽完後，問起星期五的箱子。「我沒有打開它，」女子說，「有個工人來把它搬走。」她指向另一扇門。「他說那是學校的東西，不應留在這裡。」這個回答很重要，因為它又讓搜尋方向改變了。

The clerk at the town hall showed them how people could ask to use a room. There was a small book by the desk. A person wrote a name, a date, and the time they needed. The clerk then checked that the room was free. Mia asked why the book mattered. “If two groups want the same room, they need a fair way to choose,” he said. “We cannot simply give it to the person who speaks the loudest.” Jo looked at the page and said the idea was good. Ms. Lin asked whether the school could use a similar book for stage things. The clerk said it might help. On the bus home, Mia made a drawing of a room book with a space for a box number. She did not know it then, but that drawing would become part of the club's answer at the end of the search.

鎮公所櫃台人員向他們展示，大家要如何申請使用房間。桌旁有一本小簿子，申請人會寫下姓名、日期和需要的時間，櫃台再確認房間是否空著。米雅問這本簿子有何重要。「如果兩個團體想用同一間房，就需要公平的選擇方式，」他說，「不能誰喊得最大聲就給誰。」喬看著頁面，覺得這個主意很好。林老師問，學校可不可以用類似簿子管理舞台用品。櫃台人員說也許能幫上忙。回程公車上，米雅畫了一本房間簿，特別留出填寫箱子編號的空格。當時她還不知道，這幅圖後來會成為社團最終解答的一部分。

Outside, the wind was still strong. Ben held the map with both hands so it would not fly off. Jo said, “It seems that every room sends us to another room.” Mia agreed, but she was not ready to stop. She thought about the empty wall at school and the people who were helping them. The clerk, the worker, the woman with the plants, and even the boy with the butterfly had been kind. None had asked for money. “Our town is full of people who care,” Mia said. “We should care about them, too. We will ask the next person kindly, and we will listen to the answer.” The club walked back toward the school with one more name to check.

走出大樓後，風還是很強。班用雙手抓緊地圖，以免它飛走。喬說：「好像每進一間房間，就又要去下一間。」米雅同意，但還不打算停下。她想到學校空白的牆，也想到一路幫助他們的人。櫃台人員、工人、照顧盆栽的女子，甚至拿紙蝴蝶的小男孩，都很友善。沒有人向他們收錢。「我們的小鎮有很多願意關心別人的人，」米雅說，「我們也要關心他們。下次問話時要有禮貌，也要好好聽回答。」社團帶著下一位要詢問的人的名字，往學校走去。
