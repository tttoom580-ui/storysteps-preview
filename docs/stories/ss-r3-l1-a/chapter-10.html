星星不見的那一天 · 第 10 / 25 章 · 1000以下 · 英中對照 · 校訂完成

On Monday, the school teacher brought an old computer to the library center. She helped the club look at pictures online from past school shows. There were more than a hundred pictures, and the students could spend only half an hour on the task. Mia's brother Sam came to help. He loved computers and could find things by name and date. “Try the words silver star,” he said. Ben sat between Mia and Sam with a pencil in his hand. Jo looked at a photo of children playing a ball game. One boy had to kick a ball into a net, but he hit it too far to the right. “There are many stories here,” Jo said. “We need a simple way to find ours.”

星期一，老師把一臺舊電腦帶到圖書館中心，協助社團查看網路上的歷年校園活動照片。照片超過一百張，學生只有半小時可用。米雅的哥哥山姆也來幫忙。他喜歡電腦，能依名稱和日期搜尋。「試試銀色星星這幾個字，」他說。班拿著鉛筆，坐在米雅與山姆之間。喬看到一張孩子們玩球的照片。有個男孩得把球踢進球網，卻把球踢得太偏右。「這裡有很多故事，」喬說，「我們需要簡單的方法找出自己的那一個。」

Sam enlarged a picture from last spring. The star stood at the center of a stage. An apple tree was painted behind it, and two paper birds were at its sides. “Look at the back,” Mia said. The picture was taken at an angle, and she could see a little red line on the wood. “That may be the cloth in Grandmother's picture.” The teacher moved the picture with the mouse. It was still too small. Ben did not want to make a blind guess. He drew what he could see and wrote “not sure” beside it. “That is the right way,” said Ms. Lin. “A drawing can show what you saw without making it more than it is.”

山姆放大一張去年春天的照片。星星立在舞台中央，後方畫著一棵蘋果樹，兩旁還有兩隻紙鳥。「看背面，」米雅說。照片拍攝角度有些偏，仍能看到木板上一條紅線。「那可能就是奶奶照片裡的紅布。」老師用滑鼠移動照片，可惜畫面還是太小。班不想胡亂猜測，便畫下看得見的部分，在旁邊寫「不確定」。「這樣做才對，」林老師說，「畫畫可以呈現你看到的東西，而不把它說得比實際更多。」

Another photo showed the same case being moved near the side door of the hall. The case had been marked with a seven, and a teacher stood beside it. “I know her voice,” Jo said. “She teaches music.” Mia was still not sure where the case had gone next. The photo was taken on a different date. “We love finding patterns,” Sam said, “but a similar box is not always the same box.” Mia felt that his words were right. They checked the dates again and made a small chart with three lines: what they knew, what they thought, and what they needed to ask. The chart was easy for every member to read.

另一張照片顯示同樣外形的箱子被搬到大廳側門附近。箱上標著七號，旁邊站著一位老師。「我認得她的聲音，」喬說，「她教音樂。」米雅仍不確定箱子後來去了哪裡，因為照片是在不同日期拍的。「我們很愛找出相同的地方，」山姆說，「但相似的箱子不一定就是同一個箱子。」米雅覺得他說得對。他們重新核對日期，製作了一張有三行的小表格：已知的、推測的、還需要詢問的。每位成員都能輕鬆讀懂。

After the library meeting, the children went to the lunch room. The cook had placed a bowl of ice beside the fruit drinks to keep them cool. Jo asked whether a photo could hold a memory the way ice held something cold. Ben laughed at the strange question. “A photo is not the same,” he said. “It keeps a picture of one moment. It does not tell us everything that happened before or after.” Mia liked that answer. She remembered the video with the wrong time and the old photo taken on another date. Both had helped, but only when the club checked them with other facts. She took out her pencil and wrote a new line in the book: “Ask what a picture shows, and ask what it cannot show.” She read the sentence to her friends. It was a simple rule they could use again.

圖書館會議後，孩子們去午餐室。廚師把一碗冰放在水果飲料旁，讓飲料保持涼爽。喬問，照片是否像冰能保存冷度那樣保存記憶。班因為這個奇怪的問題笑了。「不太一樣，」他說，「照片保存某一刻的畫面，卻不能告訴我們前後發生的所有事。」米雅喜歡這個回答。她想起時間錯誤的影片，以及在另一個日期拍攝的舊照片。兩者都曾幫忙，但前提是社團用其他事實核對。她拿出鉛筆，在筆記本寫下新的一行：「要問照片顯示了什麼，也要問它無法顯示什麼。」她把句子念給朋友聽。這是一條以後還能再用的簡單規則。

The half hour ended before they found a final answer. Sam turned off the computer, and the teacher thanked the group for working quietly. “We still need the case from Saturday,” Mia said as they packed up. “We should ask the play leader about it.” Ben put his pencil away. “I want to find the star,” he said, “but I also want to be right about how we find it.” Mia nodded. It was the first time she felt that the search could teach them something bigger than the story of one school prize. A good way of working would help them in the future, even after the star was back on the wall.

半小時結束了，他們仍沒有得到最後答案。山姆關掉電腦，老師感謝大家安靜地工作。「我們還需要找到星期六那個箱子的下落，」米雅一邊收東西一邊說，「應該問問戲劇負責人。」班收起鉛筆。「我想找到星星，」他說，「但我也想用正確的方法找到它。」米雅點頭。她第一次覺得，這次搜尋能教會他們的，也許比一件校園獎品的故事更重要。好的工作方式，會在星星回到牆上後，仍然幫助他們面對未來的事情。
