奶奶的節慶卡片

Grandmother's Festival Card

星星不見的那一天 · 第 9 / 25 章 · 1000以下 · 英中對照 · 校訂完成

On Sunday, Mia went to visit her grandmother near the lake. The street was quiet, and the town festival was still more than a week away. Grandmother had been a teacher at the school long ago. She was making rice and a dish with bananas for lunch. “Come in and sit,” she said. “You look quite tired.” Mia told her about the silver star and the yellow paper. Grandmother smiled with joy at the old story. “That star was made for the first school festival,” she said. “The children were so proud. We put a card behind it with their names and the year.” Mia looked up. “A card? There is nothing like that in the school book.”

星期天，米雅去湖邊探望奶奶。街上很安靜，鎮上的節慶活動還有一週多才開始。奶奶很久以前曾在那所學校教書。她正在準備白飯和一道香蕉料理當午餐。「進來坐，」奶奶說，「妳看起來很累。」米雅把銀色星星和黃色紙片的事告訴她。奶奶聽見這段老故事時，開心地笑了。「那顆星星是第一屆學校園遊會時做的，」她說，「孩子們都很驕傲。我們在背面放了一張卡片，寫著他們的名字和年份。」米雅抬頭。「卡片？學校紀錄裡沒有提到呀。」

Grandmother opened an old box and showed Mia a picture. On it, two students stood beside a large star. The back of the star was open, and a little card was held there with a piece of red cloth. “Are you sure that is our star?” Mia asked. “Yes,” Grandmother said, “but I have not seen it for many years.” Mia wanted to shout because this was such a good tip. Instead, she took a long breath and asked permission to make a copy of the picture. Grandmother said okay. “This is your club's work,” she said. “I can help, but I should not take the work from you.”

奶奶打開一個舊箱子，給米雅看一張照片。照片中有兩名學生站在大星星旁。星星背面有一個開口，裡面用一小片紅布固定著卡片。「妳確定那就是我們的星星嗎？」米雅問。「確定，」奶奶說，「但我很多年沒再見過它。」米雅高興得差點大叫，因為這是一條很好的提示。不過她先深呼吸，詢問能不能複製照片。奶奶說可以。「這是你們社團的工作，」她說，「我可以幫忙，但不該把工作從你們手上拿走。」

After lunch, Mia helped wash every dish. She also asked about the people in the picture. Grandmother said one man had become a doctor and one woman had moved to another city. “They may be far away now,” she said. “Please do not call them without asking the school first.” Mia agreed. It was easy to feel ready to do everything when a new clue came, but the club had promised to be careful. Before leaving, she put the copied picture in a safe place inside her bag. The back of the star was now as important as the front. If they found the right case, perhaps the card could help them be certain.

午餐後，米雅幫忙洗好每個盤子，也問起照片裡的人。奶奶說其中一名男子後來成為醫師，另一名女子搬到別的城市。「他們現在可能住得很遠，」奶奶說，「沒有先問過學校，不要直接打給他們。」米雅同意。找到新線索時，人很容易覺得自己已準備好做任何事，但社團答應過要小心。離開前，她把複製照片妥善放入包裡。現在星星的背面和正面一樣重要。如果找到正確的箱子，也許卡片能幫助大家確認。

Grandmother cut another banana and told Mia about her life as a young teacher. “We had very little money for shows,” she said. “So we used paper, wood, and things families gave us. The star was not expensive, but the children worked on it for many days.” Mia looked at the old photo again. One child was smiling with paint on his nose. Another was holding a brush so carefully that his fingers looked stiff. “They must have loved making it,” Mia said. Grandmother nodded. “They wanted to give the school something that every new class could see.” When Mia left, Grandmother put a small piece of cake in her bag for Ben and the others. “Tell your friends I wish them well,” she said. Mia promised she would. She was carrying more than food now; she had a story to protect.

奶奶又切了一根香蕉，向米雅說起年輕時當老師的生活。「我們做表演的錢很少，」她說，「所以會用紙、木頭，以及家長送來的東西。星星不貴，但孩子們花了好多天製作。」米雅再次看照片。一名孩子鼻子上沾著顏料，笑得很開心；另一名孩子小心拿著畫筆，手指看起來緊緊的。「他們一定很喜歡做它，」米雅說。奶奶點頭。「他們想送給學校一件每屆新生都能看到的東西。」米雅離開時，奶奶放了一小塊蛋糕進她的袋子，請她帶給班和其他朋友。「告訴大家我祝福他們，」奶奶說。米雅答應了。她如今帶走的不只食物，還有一段值得珍惜的故事。

At home, Mia sent the picture to Ms. Lin and waited for an answer. She did not put it online or show it to the whole school. The picture belonged to Grandmother, and some people in it were still alive. Later Ms. Lin wrote back: “Thank you. Bring it to our meeting tomorrow.” Mia felt a new kind of joy. Finding the star was still the goal, yet she was learning how to treat old things and other people's stories with care. The festival was coming. She hoped the star would be back by then. But even if it was not, she wanted the school to remember the children who made it and the friends who were searching for it now.

回到家後，米雅把照片傳給林老師，等老師回覆。她沒有把照片放上網路，也沒有拿給全校看。那是奶奶的照片，裡面的人有些還活著。晚些時候，林老師回信：「謝謝，明天開會時帶來。」米雅感受到一種新的喜悅。找到星星仍是目標，但她也正在學習如何珍惜老物件和別人的故事。節慶快到了，她希望到時星星已經回來。不過，即使沒有，她也希望學校記得當年製作星星的孩子，以及現在努力尋找它的朋友們。
