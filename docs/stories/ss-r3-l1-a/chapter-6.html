The Man with the Key

星星不見的那一天 · 第 6 / 25 章 · 1000以下 · 英中對照 · 校訂中

Mr. Green did not arrive at school until nine. He had to drive his uncle to a small house near the water before work. When he finally came, he carried a box and a cup of coffee. The children waited with Ms. Lin by the office door. “You may ask me anything about the move,” he said. “But I am not sure I can remember every box.” Mia held up the picture of the star. Mr. Green looked at it for a long time. “I did see that shape,” he said. “There was a silver thing on the top of a tall case. I moved that case from the town hall to the old music room.”

格林先生直到九點才到學校。他上班前得先開車送叔叔去水邊的一間小屋。他終於抵達時，手裡提著箱子和一杯咖啡。孩子們和林老師在辦公室門口等著。「關於搬運的事，你們都可以問我，」他說，「但我不確定每個箱子都記得。」米雅舉起星星的照片。格林先生看了很久。「我看過那種形狀，」他說，「一個高箱子的頂上有銀色的東西。我把那箱東西從鎮公所搬到舊音樂教室。」

Jo was ready to run to the music room, but Ms. Lin held up a hand. “First, thank him,” she said. “He gave us a useful answer.” The man smiled and showed them the sign on his box. It was a picture of a lion with the number seven under it. “The music club used this for a play,” he explained. “I had six boxes with the same sign. That is why I can remember this one.” Ben wrote the number in their book. Mia felt a wave of hope. She had thought the search was over when they reached the town hall. Now there was a new place, and they had a reason to go there.

喬立刻想衝向音樂教室，但林老師舉起手。「先謝謝他，」她說，「他給了我們有用的答案。」男子笑著給他們看箱上的標記：一隻獅子，底下寫著七號。「音樂社以前用它演過戲，」他解釋，「我搬了六個有相同標記的箱子，所以記得這一個。」班把編號記進筆記。米雅心裡湧上一股希望。她原以為搜尋到了鎮公所就要結束，現在有了新地點，也有了前往那裡的理由。

The old music room was beside a free reading room. A sign on the wall said that visitors must speak softly. Inside, they saw chairs, a piano, and a large paper boat from a school play. The boat had a long blue side and a white sail. Mia thought it looked funny beside the piano. “Do not call it stupid,” Ana told Jo when she laughed. “Someone made it with care.” Jo nodded. They checked the shelves with the music teacher. Some boxes held paper flowers. Another held an old lion hat. The silver star was not in any box they could open. Mia felt terrible for a moment, but she did not blame Mr. Green.

舊音樂教室在一間免費閱覽室旁。牆上的告示寫著訪客必須輕聲說話。教室裡有椅子、鋼琴，還有一艘學校戲劇用的大紙船。船的一側是長長的藍色，配著白色船帆。米雅覺得紙船放在鋼琴旁有些好笑。喬笑出聲時，安娜提醒她：「別說它笨。有人很用心地做了它。」喬點頭。他們和音樂老師一起檢查架子上的箱子。有些裝著紙花，另一個裝著舊獅子帽。但在獲准打開的箱子裡，沒看到銀色星星。米雅一時感到很難過，卻沒有責怪格林先生。

In the music room, the children found many things except the prize they wanted. There were hats, chairs, boxes, and even a paper fish with a very long tail. Ben drew the room so they could remember where each item had stood. Ms. Lin asked the music teacher how the objects were moved. He said that the children usually put labels on them, then the workers checked the labels and took the items to the right room. “What happens if a label is wrong?” Mia asked. “Then the box may go to the wrong room,” he answered. “That is why we should check two things, not only one.” Mia wrote “label and room list” in large letters. The star had not been found, but she had learned something that might explain how it was lost. It was possible to make a mistake without wanting to hurt anyone.

在音樂教室裡，孩子們找到很多東西，唯獨沒有他們想要的獎品。有帽子、椅子、箱子，甚至一隻有長尾巴的紙魚。班把房間畫下來，好記住物品原來的位置。林老師問音樂老師，道具是怎麼搬運的。老師說，孩子們通常先貼標籤，工人再檢查並把物品送到對應房間。「如果標籤錯了呢？」米雅問。「箱子就可能去錯房間，」老師回答，「所以要核對兩項資訊，不能只看一個。」米雅用大字寫下「標籤和房間清單」。雖然星星還沒找到，她卻學到一件可能解釋失蹤原因的事：人可能在沒有惡意的情況下犯錯。

Before they left, the music teacher showed Mia one more thing: a page from the weekend room book. It said that a large case had been taken out on Saturday. The name beside it was not Mr. Green's. It was the name of a woman who helped with plays. “We can see her tomorrow,” the teacher said. “Please do not go to her house. We will ask her here at school.” Mia thanked him. She could see that every small answer was like a part of a long road. She did not yet know where the road would end. But she was learning to walk it with care, one step at a time, without leaving her friends behind.

離開前，音樂老師又給米雅看一樣東西：週末教室借用紀錄的一頁。上面寫著一個大箱子在星期六被取走。簽名不是格林先生，而是一位協助戲劇活動的女子。「我們明天可以見到她，」老師說，「請不要去她家，會請她來學校談。」米雅道謝。她看得出每一個小答案都像長路上的一小段。她還不知道路的盡頭在哪裡，卻開始學會仔細地、一步一步走下去，而且不把朋友留在後面。
