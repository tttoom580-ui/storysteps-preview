空白的牆

The Empty Wall

星星不見的那一天 · 第 1 / 25 章 · 1000以下 · 英中對照

At seven a.m., the school bell rang across the yard. Mia came early because the art club had a big job. The spring fair was still several weeks away. In the main hall, a silver wooden star usually hung over the door. It was the school's oldest prize, made by students many years ago. This morning the wall was empty. Mia stopped with her breakfast in her hand. She had fruit, a small cake, and water, but she forgot to eat. “Where is our star?” she asked. Ben was standing by the door. He looked up, then looked at the floor. “It was here on Friday,” he said. “I saw it after the last class.”

早上七點，學校的鐘聲傳遍庭院。米雅提早到校，因為美術社有一件重要工作。春季園遊會還有好幾週才開始。大廳的門上平常掛著一顆銀色木星星。那是學校歷史最久的獎品，由多年前的學生製作。今天早上，牆上卻空空的。米雅拿著早餐停下腳步。她帶了水果、小蛋糕和水，卻忘了吃。「我們的星星去哪裡了？」她問。班站在門邊，先抬頭，再低頭看地板。「星期五還在這裡，」他說，「最後一堂課後我還看過。」

Their teacher, Ms. Lin, came from the art room with a heavy box. Inside were paper flowers, a cover for a table, and a picture of a monkey with one large tooth. The picture was for a play. A foreign student had drawn it, and the club wanted to teach younger children how to make animal masks. “Do not touch the empty hooks,” Ms. Lin said. “We must find out what happened first.” Mia moved an old couch away from the wall. Ben did the same on the other side. It felt like exercise, but neither of them spoke about being tired. There was no star behind the couch. There was only a small piece of yellow paper under its foot.

林老師抱著一個沉重的箱子，從美術教室走出來。箱裡有紙花、桌子的罩布，還有一張猴子露出一顆大牙齒的圖畫。那張畫是舞台劇要用的。一名外國學生畫了它，美術社原本要教小朋友做動物面具。「不要碰牆上空的掛鉤，」林老師說，「我們要先查清楚發生什麼事。」米雅把一張舊沙發移離牆邊，班也從另一側幫忙。這簡直像在運動，但兩人都沒抱怨疲倦。沙發後沒有星星，只有一小片黃色紙張壓在沙發腳下。

Mia liked drawing; it was her hobby. She took out her club book to write what they knew. The star had been there on Friday, and it was gone on Monday. The hooks were not broken. The yellow paper had a black number seven on it. “Shall we tell everyone that someone took it?” Ben asked. Mia shook her head. “No. We know it is missing. We do not know why.” The sun was coming through the high windows. For a moment the empty wall looked very bright. Ms. Lin put a hand on Mia's shoulder. “You may ask questions,” she said, “but you must be fair to people.”

米雅喜歡畫畫，那是她的興趣。她拿出社團筆記本，把目前知道的事寫下來。星星星期五還在，星期一卻不見了。掛鉤沒有壞。黃色紙片上有一個黑色的數字七。「我們要告訴大家有人把它拿走了嗎？」班問。米雅搖頭。「不行。我們知道它不見了，卻不知道原因。」太陽從高高的窗戶照進來。有一瞬間，空白的牆看起來格外明亮。林老師把手放在米雅肩上。「你們可以問問題，」她說，「但對待人一定要公平。」

That evening Mia asked her father why the school kept such an old prize. He said that a prize can hold a memory, even when it is made from simple wood. When Mia was younger, she had looked up at the silver star and wanted to make something good with her own hands. She remembered that wish now. At home she spread her notes across the kitchen table and drew the star from memory. One point was longer than the others, but she had never looked at the back. She wrote that the back should be checked if the star was found. She also drew the yellow paper beside the empty hook. Before bed she put the notes in a safe bag. It was a small act, but it made her feel ready to try again tomorrow.

那天晚上，米雅問爸爸，學校為什麼要保存那麼舊的獎品。爸爸說，獎品即使只是簡單的木頭做的，也能保存回憶。米雅小時候曾仰望銀色星星，希望有一天能親手做出好東西。現在她又想起那個願望。回家後，她把筆記攤在廚房桌上，憑記憶畫出星星。其中一個尖角比其他尖角長，她卻從沒看過星星的背面，便記下找到它後應檢查背面。她也在空掛鉤旁畫出黃色紙片。睡前，她把筆記放進安全的袋子。這只是一個小動作，卻讓她覺得自己準備好明天再試。

At lunch, the club sat around a table with bread and salad. Jo wanted to look in every room at once. Ana wanted to ask the school office for help. Ben wanted to draw a map of the hall. Mia listened to them all. She knew that a good search was not a race. The friends made three rules: ask before going into a room, move nothing without a teacher, and keep every clue in the book. After lunch they placed the yellow paper in a clear bag. They could not bring the star back that day. But they had a place to start, and they had a plan they all understood. The empty wall was no longer only a worry. It was a question they could work on together.

午餐時，社團成員圍坐在桌旁，吃著麵包和沙拉。喬想立刻搜尋每一間教室；安娜想請學校辦公室幫忙；班則想畫一張大廳地圖。米雅聽完每個人的意見。她知道好的搜尋不是比速度。朋友們訂下三條規則：進房間前先詢問、沒有老師陪同就不移動物品、所有線索都要記在本子裡。午餐後，他們把黃色紙片放進透明袋中。那天，他們沒能把星星帶回來，但有了起點，也有了每個人都懂的計畫。空白的牆不再只是煩惱，而是大家可以一起解答的問題。
