不準的時鐘

The Clock That Was Wrong

Ana came to school with a new idea. As she lay in her bedroom the night before, she had been thinking about the video. “I believe there is an error in the time,” she said. “Leo's phone said six, but the school clock near the gate said five when he came home.” Mia could not believe it at first. The video had been their best clue. Ben said, “Let's hear Leo's answer before we change anything.” They called him with Ms. Lin beside them. Leo checked his phone and began to laugh. “I was in another country last month,” he explained. “I did not set the clock to our time when I got back.”

安娜帶著新想法來學校。昨晚她躺在臥室時，一直想著那段影片。「我相信時間有錯，」她說，「里歐的手機顯示六點，但他回家時校門旁的鐘指著五點。」米雅一開始很難相信，因為影片是他們最重要的線索。班說：「先聽里歐怎麼說，再改筆記。」他們在林老師陪同下打電話給里歐。里歐檢查手機後笑了起來。「我上個月在另一個國家，」他解釋，「回來時忘了把時鐘改回這裡的時間。」

The children sat close together and looked at their map. Their search had not been a dream, but one wrong time could make a big difference. “Did the truck go to the sports house at five?” Mia asked. Leo checked the date and the light in the video. “Yes,” he said. “The sky was still bright.” Jo made a little noise because she was worried. “Does that mean everything we did was for nothing?” Mia shook her head. “No. We found the room sign and the man who moved the case. Those facts are still true.” She was proud of the group for finding the error before they had told the whole school a story about the driver.

孩子們靠在一起看地圖。他們的搜尋並不是一場夢，但一個錯誤時間就可能造成很大的差別。「所以貨車是在五點去體育館的嗎？」米雅問。里歐檢查日期和影片中的光線。「對，」他說，「天空當時還很亮。」喬因為擔心而發出一點聲音。「這代表我們之前做的全白費了嗎？」米雅搖頭。「沒有。我們找到了房間標記，也問到了搬箱子的人。那些事實沒有變。」她為社團能及時發現錯誤而感到驕傲，因為他們還沒向全校說司機做了什麼。

At the end of the day, rain stopped and a rainbow appeared over the school yard. Ms. Lin took the children outside to look at it. “Nature gives us many things we do not see at once,” she said. “We see more when we look again.” Ben looked up at the sky. “The red line on our map is still useful,” he said. “We just need a better time beside it.” Mia changed the note with a new pencil mark. She did not hide the old one. She wrote why they had changed it. Everyone could see what the first idea was and why the new one was better. The book was becoming a record, not a place for guesses.

放學時，雨停了，學校庭院上方出現一道彩虹。林老師帶孩子們出去看。「大自然有很多事物，不是第一眼就能看清楚，」她說，「再看一次，才會看見更多。」班抬頭望著天空。「地圖上的紅線還是有用，」他說，「只要把旁邊的時間改正就好。」米雅用鉛筆改了註記，卻沒有把舊記號完全擦掉。她寫下修改原因。每個人都能看到最初的想法，以及新答案為什麼更好。這本筆記正逐漸成為紀錄，而不是亂猜的地方。

On Friday night, Mia sat with her mother and talked about the wrong clock. Her mother said that she had once missed a bus because she read the time on a kitchen clock that was ten minutes slow. “I was upset,” she said, “but I did not blame the bus driver.” Mia laughed. “That sounds like our search,” she answered. “One wrong time made us think the truck was somewhere else.” Her mother asked whether the club could still use the video. Mia explained that they could, because the picture showed the truck on the bridge. Only the time needed to change. “Then you have done good work,” her mother said. “You did not throw away the whole picture because one part was wrong.” Mia slept better that night. The next day could bring another clue, and she was ready to look at it carefully.

星期五晚上，米雅和媽媽聊起錯誤時鐘。媽媽說自己曾因為廚房時鐘慢了十分鐘而錯過公車。「我很生氣，」她說，「但沒有責怪公車司機。」米雅笑了。「這很像我們這次搜尋，」她回答，「一個錯誤時間讓我們以為貨車去了別的地方。」媽媽問影片是否還能使用。米雅解釋可以，因為畫面確實拍到貨車過橋，只有時間需要修正。「那妳們做得很好，」媽媽說，「沒有因為一部分出錯，就把整張照片丟掉。」那晚米雅睡得比較安穩。隔天可能有另一條線索，她也準備好仔細看待。

Mia asked the others whether they wanted to stop for a day. Jo said she needed rest. Ana wanted more time to think. Ben said he could draw at home and bring the map back tomorrow. Mia agreed. “We have been working very hard,” she said. “The star matters, but you matter more.” They made a new plan: one short meeting each day, and no calls to people after school without a teacher. It was a small rule, yet it made everyone feel safe. That evening Mia looked out of her window. The rainbow was gone, and the sky was dark. She was not rich, and she had no special power. She only had time, friends, and a careful book. That was enough for now.

米雅問大家要不要休息一天。喬說她需要休息；安娜想多花點時間思考；班表示可以在家畫圖，明天再帶地圖來。米雅同意。「我們已經很努力了，」她說，「星星很重要，但你們更重要。」他們重新安排：每天只開一次短會，沒有老師在場就不在放學後打電話給任何人。規則很小，卻讓大家更安心。那晚米雅望向窗外。彩虹消失了，天空已經暗下。她不富有，也沒有特殊能力；她只有時間、朋友和一本仔細記錄的筆記。眼下這就夠了。
