公園裡的比賽

The Game in the Park

星星不見的那一天 · 第 8 / 25 章 · 1000以下 · 英中對照 · 校訂中

用互動版查詞、朗讀與收藏

On Friday, the school held a baseball game in the park. Mia's group helped serve lunch, so they brought bread, beef, and fruit. Jo wore a blue dress over her sports clothes because she had a play after the game. A girl from another class asked why the art club was helping with sports. “We are all one school,” Mia said. “It is easy to give a hand.” Behind the food table, Ben saw a large gray case. It had the same yellow sign as the missing paper. His heart began to beat faster. He wanted to open it, but he remembered their rule. He called Ms. Lin instead and asked her to look with him.

星期五，學校在公園舉辦棒球賽。米雅的小組幫忙準備午餐，帶來麵包、牛肉和水果。喬因為賽後要演戲，在運動服外面套了一件藍裙。一個別班女孩問，美術社為什麼要幫運動活動。「我們都是同一所學校的人，」米雅說，「伸出援手並不難。」食物桌後面，班看到一個大灰箱，上面有和黃色紙片相同的標記。他的心跳突然加快。他想打開，卻記得社團規則，於是叫林老師過來一起看。

A town officer was working outside the park gate to keep the road clear. He came to the table when Ms. Lin asked about the case. The officer said that it held costumes for a children's play. “There is a ghost costume and some bird wings,” he explained. “You can see them when the play leader gets here.” Jo wanted to break the lock with a stick, but Mia stopped her. “We do not break things to find our star,” she said. “That would make a different problem.” Ben pointed to the paper on the case. It said “Room Seven, Saturday.” This was the date when the big case had left the music room. They had found the right kind of case, if not the right one.

一名鎮上的警員在公園門外工作，協助維持道路暢通。林老師詢問箱子時，他走到桌邊，說裡面裝著兒童戲劇服裝。「有一件鬼裝，還有幾對鳥翅膀，」他解釋，「等戲劇負責人來了就能看。」喬想拿棍子敲開鎖，米雅阻止她。「我們不能為了找星星就破壞東西，」她說，「那只會製造另一個問題。」班指著箱上的紙，上面寫著「七號房，星期六」。這正是大箱子從音樂教室離開的日期。他們找到種類正確的箱子，卻還不能確定就是要找的那個。

After the game, the group took a walk around the park. Children ran across the grass. A paper bird began to fly when the wind caught it, and a small boy ran after it. Mia thought of the butterfly from the town hall. “We have seen many little things move with the wind,” she said. “Could the star have fallen?” Ben looked at the case. “It is wood. It is too heavy to fly,” he said. Ana smiled. “But the yellow paper could have come off another box.” The idea was nice, yet it meant the paper alone could not prove where the star was. Mia wrote that warning in the book so they would not forget.

比賽後，大家繞著公園散步。孩子們在草地上跑來跑去。一隻紙鳥被風吹得飛起，一名小男孩追著它跑。米雅想到鎮公所的小蝴蝶。「我們看過很多輕東西被風吹走，」她說，「星星會不會也掉下來了？」班看著大箱子。「它是木頭做的，太重了，不可能飛，」他說。安娜微笑。「不過黃色紙張可能從別的箱子上掉下來。」這個想法不錯，卻也表示單靠紙片無法證明星星在哪裡。米雅把這點警告寫進筆記，免得大家忘記。

At the park, Ana gave the group a little taste of the fruit drink she had made with her mother. She poured it into clean cups, then put the bottle away so it would not fall near the costumes. Mia thanked her and watched the play children get ready behind a low wall. They were very excited, but the leader told them to move slowly around the props. A small child asked why the art club had been asking about boxes for so many days. Mia told him that an old school prize was missing and that they were following a trail of rooms and lists. “Will you find it?” he asked. “I hope so,” she said. “But we will not open other people's boxes without permission.” The child nodded and went back to his group. Mia liked how one simple rule could be explained to anyone.

在公園裡，安娜讓社團朋友嚐了一點她和媽媽做的水果飲料。她把飲料倒進乾淨杯子，接著收起瓶子，免得它掉在戲服附近。米雅謝過她，看著表演的孩子在矮牆後準備。孩子們十分興奮，但負責人提醒他們在道具旁要慢慢走。一名小朋友問，美術社為什麼一連好幾天都在問箱子的事。米雅告訴他，學校有一件舊獎品不見了，他們正沿著房間與紀錄的線索搜尋。「你們會找到嗎？」孩子問。「我希望會，」她說，「但我們不會在沒有許可時打開別人的箱子。」孩子點頭，回到自己的團體。米雅很喜歡一條簡單規則能向任何人解釋清楚的感覺。

The play leader arrived near the end of the day. She gave the children a kind smile and opened the case herself. The children could see a ghost mask, a red shirt, and a bird hat. There was no silver star. “These are for our new group,” the woman said. “I can show you the other cases on Monday.” She asked them not to feel bad. Mia looked at the others and said, “We know one more place where the star is not.” It was not the answer they wanted, but it was an answer they could trust. The officer thanked them for keeping the park safe, and the friends went home without taking anything that did not belong to them.

戲劇負責人在傍晚前趕到。她友善地向孩子們微笑，親自打開箱子。裡面有鬼面具、紅襯衫和鳥帽子，沒有銀色星星。「這些是我們新團體要用的，」女子說，「星期一我可以帶你們看其他箱子。」她請孩子們不要失望。米雅看著朋友們說：「我們又知道一個星星不在的地方。」這不是想要的答案，卻是可以信任的答案。警員謝謝大家一起維持公園安全，朋友們沒拿走任何不屬於自己的東西，就各自回家了。
