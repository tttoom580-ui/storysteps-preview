停職通知

The Suspension Notice

The notice arrived with abrupt speed. Northline suspended Mei's access to the project database, claiming that her public questions might disrupt an internal review. The letter described the organization as a separate legal entity and instructed her to return her key card. Evan appeared at her desk carrying a polished briefcase. He called her naive for imagining that an activist could challenge a director without consequences. Mei wanted to shout, but she chose to contemplate the document's exact language. It did not accuse her of copying private information or destroying files. It merely restricted access while an inquiry proceeded. She asked for the relevant policy and a written list of the materials she was permitted to keep. Evan frowned. “You're making this hostile,” he said. “No,” Mei answered. “I am making it clear.” She left her work computer untouched and walked out with only her personal notebook and the documents that were already public.

停權通知來得突然。北線暫停梅存取專案資料庫的權限，理由是她的公開提問可能干擾內部審查。信中稱組織是獨立法人，要求她交回門禁卡。伊凡提著擦得發亮的公事包出現在桌旁，說她太天真，以為一名倡議者能挑戰主管而不付代價。梅想大喊，卻先仔細思考文件的措辭。信中沒有控告她複製個資或毀損檔案，只是要求調查期間限制權限。她索取適用政策，以及准許保留資料的書面清單。伊凡皺眉說：「妳把事情搞得很敵對。」「不，」梅回答，「我只是要把規則講清楚。」她沒有碰工作電腦，只帶走個人筆記本與已公開文件。

Outside the building, a pedestrian almost collided with a delivery bicycle. The rider fell and struck his thigh, but was able to stand after a volunteer helped him. Mei stopped to ask if he needed medical assistance. He declined an injection offered by a stranger who claimed to know first aid, and waited for the clinic staff instead. The small scene reminded Mei how easily concern could turn into an excuse for taking control of someone else's choice. Ruth met her at a nearby café and said the suspension might be an obligation under the agency's review rules, but it could also be used as pressure. “We cannot assume either one without reading the policy,” she said. Mei agreed. An old poster on the café wall showed explorers crossing a frontier; its confident picture seemed almost ridiculous beside the uncertain route ahead of her.

走出大樓，一名行人險些撞上送貨單車。騎士跌倒撞到大腿，幸好志工扶他後能站起來。梅停下詢問是否需要醫療協助。有人自稱懂急救，提議替他注射藥物，騎士拒絕，選擇等診所人員。這小事讓梅想到，關心他人也可能變成奪走對方選擇權的藉口。露絲在附近咖啡館見她，說停權或許是機構審查規則下的義務，也可能被拿來施壓。「沒看政策之前，兩種都不能直接認定。」梅同意。牆上舊海報畫著探險家跨越邊境，那幅自信滿滿的圖，與她眼前不確定的路形成諷刺對比。

Mara's community clinic held a seminar that afternoon. It was supposed to address labor rights, not Mei's dispute, but the questions were useful. A supervisor could supervise employees during an investigation; that did not automatically give the employer ownership of every idea an employee produced. Contract terms, evidence of creation, and ordinary workplace practices mattered. Mei learned that she should not promise to repay money she did not owe, nor should she demand access to materials that belonged to clients. A participant asked whether an intern had any protection against retaliation. Mara explained that the answer depended on the applicable rules and facts, and encouraged documenting events before making public claims. Mei realized that she had been treating her future as a single closed door. There might be other outlets for her skills, even if this internship ended badly.

下午瑪拉的社區法律診所舉辦研習會，主題原是勞動權益，並非梅的爭議，問答卻很有用。主管可在調查期間監督員工，但不代表雇主必然擁有員工想出的每個構想；契約條款、創作證據及工作慣例都很重要。梅學到，不該承諾償還自己不欠的錢，也不該要求存取客戶所有的資料。有人問實習生是否受到報復行為的保護。瑪拉說答案取決於適用規則和事實，鼓勵大家在公開指控前先記錄經過。梅發現自己一直把未來看成只有一扇關上的門；即使這次實習不順，自己的能力仍可能有別的出口。

Mei spent a quiet hour at a community job center after the seminar. Losing her key card had made her feel as if she had entered exile from the profession she loved. But the counselor showed her several possible paths, including temporary research work and an outlet for volunteers who wanted to develop public-information tools. None was guaranteed employment, and none could replace the experience she had hoped to gain at Northline. Still, she prepared applications based on the actual skills she could demonstrate: mapping, documentation, field interviews, and careful analysis. She did not include the unresolved dispute as a dramatic badge of courage. She described the project honestly and offered only the work she was authorized to show. That modest act of planning made tomorrow feel less entirely controlled by Evan.

研習會後，梅在社區就業中心安靜待了一小時。失去門禁卡讓她覺得自己像被放逐出喜愛的專業。職涯顧問卻給她看幾條可能的路，包括臨時研究工作，以及讓志工開發公共資訊工具的管道。沒有一項保證錄用，也無法取代她原想在北線得到的經驗；但她仍依據可證明的技能準備履歷：製圖、文件紀錄、田野訪談、審慎分析。她沒有把未決爭議包裝成勇敢勳章，只誠實描述專案，提供獲准展示的成果。這份平凡的規劃讓她感覺，明天不再完全由伊凡掌控。

Before evening, an anonymous message claimed that Mei had stolen the model and offered a surplus of supposed evidence. The attachment contained no original files, only cropped pictures. Mei did not forward them. She asked Mara how to report the message without enlarging its audience and saved the envelope in which a printed copy later arrived. The sender had chosen a masculine voice, calling a careful woman weak, but the insults revealed nothing about identity. Mei wrote a factual reply to Northline: she acknowledged receiving the suspension, requested clarification, and affirmed her willingness to cooperate through an independent reviewer. Then she spent an hour updating her résumé. She had not regained her position, but she had kept her judgment. Tomorrow she would meet Alina, who had discovered something unexpected at the warehouse near the canceled shelter.

傍晚前，一則匿名訊息聲稱梅偷了模型，還說握有大量所謂證據。附件沒有原始檔案，只有裁切過的圖片。梅沒有轉傳，先問瑪拉如何在不擴大傳播的情況下通報，並保留稍後寄來紙本時所用的信封。寄件人用充滿男性優越感的口氣，罵謹慎的女性軟弱，但這些侮辱並不能揭露身分。梅正式回信給北線，確認收到停權通知，要求釐清規則，並表示願意配合獨立審查。接著她花了一小時更新履歷。她還沒拿回職位，卻守住了判斷力。明天她要見艾莉娜，對方在取消的避難站附近倉庫發現一件意外的事。
