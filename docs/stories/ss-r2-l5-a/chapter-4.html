道路盡頭的家

The House at the End of the Road

實習結束前，我要拿回自己的名字 · 第 4 / 25 章 · 4000–5000 · 英中對照

Mei expected boredom during the neighborhood survey. Instead she found a broken driveway, a blocked footpath, and a woman carrying medication for her father through heavy traffic. Mrs. Huang lived in a small housing complex behind the freight yard. She showed Mei how trucks parked across the only smooth entrance. On hot afternoons her father could not reach the old shelter without help. The revised map sent him even farther. Mei recorded the route but not his diagnosis, following the clinic's privacy guideline. A civic volunteer named Alina joined them and explained that earlier residents had pleaded for a shaded crossing. The agency had promised an innovative design, yet the new arrows made the original problem worse. Mei's anger began to intensify, but she kept her questions specific: where did residents walk, how long did it take, and which barriers could be moved?

梅以為社區勘查會很無聊，卻看到破損的車道、封住的人行道，以及一位提著父親藥物穿越繁忙車流的女士。黃太太住在貨運場後的小型住宅區，帶梅看卡車如何堵住唯一平坦的入口。炎熱午後，她父親沒有協助便到不了舊避難站；新版地圖使距離更遠。梅遵循診所的隱私指引，只記路線，不記病名。公民志工艾莉娜加入，說明居民早先曾懇求設置遮蔭通道。機構承諾創新設計，新箭頭卻讓原本的問題惡化。梅愈來愈生氣，但仍提出明確的問題：居民走哪條路、需要多久、哪些障礙物可以搬走？

Behind the complex, a retired teacher named Mr. Lin offered them water from a tin cup. He was eloquent about the area's history, describing how neighbors once organized to repair the path themselves. “They call this a matter of morality,” he said, pointing toward the agency's colorful poster, “but kindness is not useful if nobody can enter the building.” Mei noticed that he did not ask her to punish anyone. He wanted a safe route before the coming heat wave. Alina suggested a temporary ramp to stimulate a wider discussion, but Mrs. Huang warned that a poorly built ramp might cause another accident. They tested a possible detour and measured how a wheelchair turned at each corner. The difference was noticeable. A few meters on paper could become several exhausting minutes on the ground.

住宅區後方，一位退休教師林先生用錫杯端水給大家。他口才很好，說起鄰居過去如何自發修路。「他們說這關乎道德，」他指著機構彩色海報說，「但若人根本進不了建築，善意就沒有用。」梅注意到他沒有要求懲罰誰，他只希望熱浪來前能有安全路線。艾莉娜提議先設臨時坡道，藉此激發更多討論，黃太太卻提醒，若坡道搭得不好，也可能釀成意外。他們測試繞路方案，量測輪椅在每個轉角如何通行。差異很明顯：地圖上幾公尺的距離，實地可能變成數分鐘的艱難路程。

Near the end of the visit, a young volunteer suddenly became ill and had to sit by a wall. He said the heat made him feel ready to vomit. Mei called the clinic instead of guessing what treatment he needed. The incident could prolong their survey, but she refused to rush him. It also reminded her that a plan might look perfect in a meeting room and still fail when people were tired or frightened. At the clinic, staff suggested that the city provide clear public information about heat injuries without exposing private patients. A simple leaflet would be more helpful than a dramatic publicity campaign. Mei wrote her field notes that night, separating what she observed from what others reported. She did not yet know whether the altered map was dishonest. She knew, with painful certainty, that the route was dangerous.

訪查快結束時，一位年輕志工突然不舒服，靠牆坐下，說熱得想吐。梅沒有自行猜測該如何治療，而是打電話給診所。這會延長勘查時間，但她拒絕催促他。這件事提醒她：計畫在會議室裡再完美，也可能在疲憊或恐懼的現實中失敗。診所人員建議市府提供清楚的中暑防治資訊，但不能透露病患個資。一張實用傳單會比戲劇化的宣傳活動有用。當晚梅整理田野筆記，明確區分親眼觀察與他人口述。她仍不知道地圖修改是否涉及欺瞞，但已痛切確定那條路不安全。

On the return walk, the group passed a repair shop where a mechanic was cleaning a bus windshield. He explained that the dusty glass made it difficult for drivers to see people crossing near the freight yard. Mrs. Huang began to plead for another warning sign, but the mechanic suggested checking the road layout before choosing a solution. Mei admired that practical trait. She could feel despair whenever the list of problems grew longer, yet each observation made the next question more precise. Alina marked the crossing on a paper map and asked the city officer to inspect it. No one promised that a single sign would make every trip safe. They simply agreed to examine the evidence, including how sunlight struck the windshield at the hour residents most often crossed.

回程時，眾人經過修車行，一名技工正在清理公車擋風玻璃。他說粉塵使司機難以看見貨運場附近的行人。黃太太開始懇求多設一面警告標誌，技工卻建議先檢查道路配置，再決定方法。梅欣賞他務實的特質。問題清單愈長，她愈可能感到絕望，但每項觀察也讓下一個問題更精確。艾莉娜在紙圖上標出路口，請市府官員查勘。沒有人保證一面標誌能讓每次出行都安全；大家只是同意檢查證據，包含居民最常過路時陽光如何照射擋風玻璃。

The next morning she sent the city office a short set of measurements and asked whether the emergency crossing could be restored while the larger question remained open. She did not demand an immediate verdict on Evan. Alina offered to gather signatures, but Mei suggested first showing residents the exact proposal so they could decide for themselves. By evening a small group had agreed to request a temporary route. It was not a spectacular response, and the agency might still refuse. Yet it was the first practical benefit of Mei's inquiry. For the people who lived behind the freight yard, her name on a report mattered less than a path they could safely use. Mei would not forget that difference when the struggle for authorship became louder.

隔天早晨，梅把簡短測量資料寄給市府，詢問能否在大問題尚未查清前先恢復緊急通道。她沒有要求立刻裁定伊凡有錯。艾莉娜提議蒐集連署，梅則建議先讓居民看見提案內容，再自行決定。傍晚，一小群居民同意要求設置臨時路線。這不是轟動的回應，機構仍可能拒絕，卻是梅的調查帶來的第一項實際利益。對住在貨運場後方的人而言，報告上的署名遠不如一條能安全行走的道路重要。當爭取作者身分的聲浪愈來愈大時，梅不會忘記這個差別。
