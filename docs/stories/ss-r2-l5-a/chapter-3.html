A Timestamp That Refused to Move

The archive receipt named Omar Bell, a quiet engineer who specialized in route modeling. Mei rode the morning shuttle to the city office and found him examining a virtual copy of the plan. He gave a small shrug when she asked whether he remembered her model. “I remember its scope,” he said. “It included two shelters that the executive presentation removed.” He was cautious because the records also contained sensitive biological and disability information. Mei agreed that personal data must remain protected. What she needed was only the submission time and a comparison between two public designs. Omar printed a receipt bearing her initials and the date. The page did not prove why the design had changed, but it did establish that Mei's version was earlier. For the first time, she held evidence that someone outside Northline could independently confirm.

檔案收據上的收件人是歐瑪・貝爾，一名擅長路線模型的安靜工程師。梅搭早晨接駁車到市府辦公室，找到正在檢查虛擬地圖的他。被問到是否記得她的模型時，他聳了聳肩。「我記得它的涵蓋範圍，」他說，「它有兩個在執行長簡報中消失的避難站。」他格外謹慎，因為紀錄中也含有敏感的生物與身心障礙資訊。梅同意個資必須保護。她只需要提交時間，以及兩份公開設計的比較。歐瑪列印帶有她姓名縮寫與日期的收據。那張紙無法證明路線為何改動，卻確立她的版本比較早。她第一次握有能由北線以外的人獨立確認的證據。

At lunch, Ruth introduced Mei to attorney Mara Cho, who offered general advice through a community clinic. Mara warned that a believable account required realism. “We cannot turn every rude remark into legal aggression,” she said. “And we cannot treat every missing file as proof of theft.” The team would need to separate a claim about authorship from a claim about public safety. One layer of evidence might establish who made the original design; another would show who approved a later change; a third might show whether the change caused measurable harm. Mei felt bound by this careful approach, but she also felt protected by it. Her beloved grandmother used to say that a knot pulled too hard would only tighten. Mara's method offered a way to straighten the threads without tearing them. They agreed to seek documents through ordinary public channels.

午餐時，露絲介紹梅認識社區法律診所的律師瑪拉・趙。瑪拉提醒她，可信的說法需要貼近現實。「不能把每句無禮的話都當成法律上的攻擊，」她說，「也不能把每份不見的檔案都視為偷竊的證據。」團隊必須分開處理署名與公共安全兩項主張。一層證據證明最初設計者，一層顯示誰批准後續修改，第三層則看變更是否造成可量測的傷害。梅覺得這種謹慎綁住了手腳，但也受到保護。她摯愛的祖母常說，繩結拉得太急只會更緊。瑪拉的方法像是在不扯斷繩線的前提下，把線頭理直。她們決定透過正常公開管道取得文件。

In the afternoon, Omar opened the change history on a second monitor. Each revision appeared as a small tick on a timeline. He pointed to one strange event: a manager's account had replaced the shelter list after the system had recorded the final review. That single action did not identify a person at the keyboard; shared passwords could diminish the value of an apparent clue. Mei carefully attached a strap to her heavy document case and made copies of the relevant public screenshots. “Someone could prey on our uncertainty,” she said, “if we tell the story before we know what the logs mean.” Omar agreed. He said his viewpoint had shifted after seeing how a senior manager presented the report as his own. The engineer would describe what the record showed, but not what he could not know. Mei respected that limit. It made his evidence stronger.

下午，歐瑪在第二個螢幕打開修改紀錄。時間線上的每次修訂都像一個小刻痕。他指出一個怪異事件：系統記錄最終審查後，某主管帳號竟更換了避難站清單。但這個操作不能證明是誰坐在鍵盤前；共用密碼會降低線索的價值。梅替沉重文件箱扣好背帶，複製相關的公開截圖。「如果我們還不懂紀錄就先講故事，」她說，「有人可能利用我們的不確定性。」歐瑪同意。他說自己看見高階主管把報告當成個人作品時，觀點才開始改變。他只會說明紀錄能證明的事，不猜測未知的部分。梅尊重這個界線，因為它讓證詞更有力量。

Omar offered one practical lesson before Mei went home. “People who specialize in software systems can recognize suspicious changes,” he said, “but their expertise does not make them witnesses to every decision.” He showed her a log entry whose time appeared to be one hour late because the server used another time zone. That small detail could easily become a false accusation in an emotional presentation. Mei added a note about the clock difference to her timeline and asked whether the archive could preserve its original settings. She saw that careful work sometimes meant weakening an exciting claim rather than strengthening it. The point was not to win every argument in the room. It was to create an account that remained reliable when a stranger checked the same evidence months later.

梅回家前，歐瑪又給她一個實用提醒。「專精軟體系統的人能辨認可疑修改，」他說，「但這不代表他們親眼看過每一項決定。」他展示一條看似晚一小時的紀錄，其實只是伺服器使用另一個時區。這種細節很容易在情緒化簡報中變成錯誤指控。梅在時間線加上時鐘差異的註記，詢問檔案室是否能保存原始設定。她看見謹慎工作有時意味著削弱令人興奮的主張，而非加強它。重點不是贏過房間裡每個人，而是建立數月後陌生人用同一份證據查核時仍可靠的說法。

Before leaving, Mei asked Omar one final question. Could a manager's account have been used by someone else? “Yes,” he replied, “but the city may have an access log that narrows the possibilities.” Mara advised Mei not to request that log as an employee with special access. Instead she would ask the records officer which portions could be disclosed lawfully and which required independent review. Mei left the office without a dramatic victory. Still, her case had gained a firm beginning and a reliable witness. On the shuttle home, she watched the river slide beneath the bridge and considered the distance between suspicion and proof. It was wider than she had hoped. But there was a path across it, and she could now see the first stones.

離開前，梅問歐瑪最後一個問題：主管帳號有可能被別人使用嗎？「可能，」他回答，「但市府的存取紀錄或許能縮小範圍。」瑪拉建議梅不要利用員工特殊權限去調閱紀錄，而是詢問檔案官哪些資訊可合法公開、哪些必須由獨立人員審查。梅沒有帶著戲劇性的勝利離開，但她的案件已有穩固的起點與可靠證人。回程接駁車上，她看著橋下流動的河水，思考懷疑與證明之間的距離。那比預期更遠，但已經有一條可以跨越的路，她看得見最初幾塊踏腳石。
