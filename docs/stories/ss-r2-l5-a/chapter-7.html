A Missing Copy

實習結束前，我要拿回自己的名字 · 第 7 / 25 章 · 4000–5000 · 英中對照 · 校訂中

Alina led Mei through a corridor behind the public warehouse. There, a sturdy storage cabinet contained an array of maps from last year's emergency exercises. A label on one box read ROUTE TESTS, but a section of its contents had been removed. Alina suspected that a corrupt employee had taken the files. Mei warned that the accusation could arouse a controversy without giving them a useful answer. They needed the records schedule and a responsible custodian, not a rumor. Omar checked the public register and found that the missing bundle had been transferred to the city records office for scanning. The change had been routine, not a secret removal. Mei felt embarrassed by her initial worry, yet relieved. A false accusation could infect a genuine investigation and disconnect people who might otherwise cooperate. Their small correction strengthened the team's credibility.

艾莉娜帶梅穿過公共倉庫後方的走廊。堅固的儲物櫃裡放著去年緊急演練的一系列地圖。有個箱子標著「路線測試」，部分內容卻不見了。艾莉娜懷疑腐敗員工拿走文件。梅提醒她，這種指控會引發爭議，卻未必帶來答案。她們需要保存年限表與保管人，而不是流言。歐瑪查公開登記簿，發現遺失的那一疊文件只是移交市府檔案室掃描。那是例行作業，不是秘密移除。梅為起初的擔心感到尷尬，卻鬆了一口氣。錯誤指控可能污染真正的調查，切斷本可合作的人們之間的聯繫。這次小小的修正，反而增強團隊的可信度。

While waiting for the documents, they examined the neighborhood's infrastructure. The cooling center was beside a sports league office, a community kitchen famous for its mixed cuisine, and a theater preparing a pirate play. Those places seemed unrelated, yet each helped residents find shelter during heat emergencies. The kitchen could provide water; the theater had a large entrance; the league stored portable chairs. Mei understood that expertise did not always belong to people with official titles. A cook knew how crowds moved, and a volunteer knew which doors stayed unlocked. Their knowledge was indispensable to the model. A records clerk finally arrived and explained the rules of custody: requests must be logged, originals must remain in the archive, and copies must identify their sources. Mei accepted the restrictions without complaint.

等待文件時，他們查看社區基礎設施。避暑中心旁是運動聯盟辦公室、以混合料理聞名的社區廚房，以及正在排練海盜劇的劇院。這些地方看似無關，熱浪發生時卻都能協助居民：廚房提供水，劇院有寬敞入口，聯盟儲存可攜椅子。梅明白，專業知識不只屬於有正式頭銜的人；廚師知道人潮如何移動，志工知道哪些門會開。這些知識對模型不可或缺。檔案員終於到場，解釋保管規則：每次調閱都要登記，原件留在檔案室，副本須註明來源。梅毫無怨言地接受限制。

The archived materials included a tournament evacuation exercise and a list of participants who had agreed to share their observations. One recipient of the old report was a logistics manager who had since changed jobs. His comments described a footbridge that could not support a heavy crowd, and the report derived a safer detour from those measurements. The detour matched Mei's first model. It did not match Evan's. Omar proposed a reconstruction of the decision process, including the data used, the proposed routes, and the signed approvals. Mei insisted on separating the design question from any allegation of personal corruption. “An empty box does not prove a crime,” she told Alina, “but an unexplained revision deserves an answer.” Alina nodded. Their work was slower than a dramatic confrontation, but it was finally becoming a worthwhile investigation.

舊檔包含一次競賽活動的疏散演練，以及同意分享觀察的參與者清單。舊報告的一名收件人是已轉職的物流主管；他的意見指出，人行橋無法承受太多人同時通行，而報告根據測量推導出較安全的繞路方案。那條路與梅的第一版模型相符，卻不符合伊凡的版本。歐瑪提議重建決策過程，包含資料來源、候選路線與簽核紀錄。梅堅持把設計問題與個人貪腐指控分開。「空箱子不能證明犯罪，」她告訴艾莉娜，「但沒有解釋的修改值得追問。」艾莉娜點頭。他們的進度比戲劇化對質慢，卻終於成為值得投入的調查。

The records clerk helped them derive a list of missing approvals from the publicly available index. The exercise was almost disappointingly simple: compare the date of each route revision with the date of its authorization. They discovered several harmless gaps caused by late scanning, which made the remaining unexplained gap more significant. Mei thanked the clerk for showing the method instead of merely handing over a conclusion. She wanted residents to be able to repeat the comparison without special software or insider access. Alina proposed a public workshop where participants could practice with fictional examples before handling real records. That way the community would learn to ask better questions without accidentally exposing private information. The idea gave the investigation a purpose that could outlast its immediate dispute.

檔案員協助大家從公開索引推導出缺少的核准項目。方法簡單得令人失望：比較每次路線修訂與其授權日期。他們發現幾個只是掃描延遲造成的無害缺口，反而讓剩下那個無法解釋的缺口更重要。梅感謝檔案員教方法，而不是只交結論。她希望居民不需特殊軟體或內部權限，也能重做比較。艾莉娜提議舉辦公開工作坊，先用虛構案例練習，再處理真實紀錄。如此一來，社區能學會問更好的問題，也不會不小心暴露個資。這個構想讓調查擁有超越眼前爭議的價值。

That night Nadia called. Someone had offered her a sensational story claiming that Mei's team had been trying to hack the city archive. She refused to print it without evidence. Mei described the authorized visit and suggested checking the sign-in sheet with the records office. Nadia was surprised that Mei would point a reporter toward documents that might expose her own mistakes. Mei said accuracy mattered more than looking perfect. If she demanded that other people admit errors, she should do the same. The next morning Nadia confirmed the visit had been approved. More importantly, the clerk remembered an earlier request for the same route tests. It came from a contractor connected to Northline, weeks before the public design changed. The missing copy was no longer the mystery. Its earlier reader was.

當晚娜迪亞來電。有人向她兜售聳動消息，說梅的團隊企圖駭入市府檔案庫；她沒有證據就拒絕刊登。梅說明調閱經過，建議向檔案室查核簽到表。娜迪亞很意外：梅竟主動讓記者查可能暴露自己錯誤的文件。梅說，準確比看起來完美更重要；若她要求別人承認錯誤，自己也該做到。隔天娜迪亞確認調閱合法。更重要的是，檔案員記得早前有人也要求看同一份路線測試；那人來自與北線有關的承包商，時間比公開設計修改還早幾週。消失的副本已不是謎團；更值得查的是那位更早的讀者。
