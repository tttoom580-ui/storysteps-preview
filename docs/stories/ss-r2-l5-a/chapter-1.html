The Name Beneath the Diagram

實習結束前，我要拿回自己的名字 · 第 1 / 25 章 · 4000–5000 · 英中對照

The first batch of visitor maps came out of the printer just before dawn. Mei Chen lifted one by its rim and stared at the route across the river. Three months earlier, she had designed that route during her placement at Northline Civic Lab, a nonprofit group that helped neighborhoods prepare for dangerous summer heat. Her mentor, Ruth Park, had called the proposal a breakthrough because it connected cooling shelters rather than treating each building as an isolated stop. Mei had even mapped the old tram circuit, where a broken bridge made ordinary directions useless. The work was not glamorous, but it was decent work, and she had taken care with every crossing. Now the map carried the name of executive director Evan Vale. Mei checked the small gray line beneath the title. Her own name had vanished, as if ink could dissolve a person. She did not cry. She photographed the page and placed it beside her notebook.

第一批訪客地圖在天亮前印出。梅・陳捏著紙張邊緣，凝視跨河路線。三個月前，她在非營利組織「北線公民實驗室」實習時設計了這條路線；組織協助社區因應夏季高溫。導師露絲・朴曾稱這份提案是突破，因為它把各個避暑站連接成網絡，而非把每棟建築視為孤立的停靠點。梅甚至繪製舊電車環線，指出斷橋如何使一般指示失效。這工作不華麗，卻紮實；她仔細檢查每個路口。如今地圖卻印著執行長伊凡・維爾的名字。她再看標題下的灰色小字，自己的名字竟消失了，彷彿油墨能讓一個人憑空蒸發。她沒有哭，只拍下紙張，放在筆記本旁。

By nine o'clock, Northline's conference room was full. Evan announced that the city would implement his design next month. A projection showed blue arrows moving through poor districts, but Mei noticed that two emergency shelters were missing. She raised a question during the inquiry period. Evan smiled and called the change a minor improvement to a complicated regime of transport rules. When she asked who approved it, he told her not to be an idiot in front of donors. His words made the room painfully quiet. Ruth later found Mei near the bicycle racks, where an abandoned saddle rested against a wall. “You can ask for credit,” Ruth said, “but your stronger question is why the map changed.” Mei knew that speaking up could strain her chances of a permanent job. Still, silence would terrify her more than rejection. She wrote down exactly what Evan had said, including the time and the people present.

上午九點，會議室擠滿了人。伊凡宣布市府下月會實施「他的」設計。投影畫面上，藍色箭頭穿越貧困街區，梅卻發現兩處緊急避難站消失了。她在提問時段提出疑問。伊凡微笑著說，那只是為了配合複雜交通規則而做的小修改。她追問誰批准時，他竟叫她別在捐助者面前當笨蛋。房間瞬間安靜。稍後露絲在自行車架旁找到梅，牆邊靠著一個被遺棄的車座。「妳可以要求署名，」露絲說，「但更重要的是，地圖為什麼改了。」梅知道發聲可能影響轉正機會；但比起被拒絕，沉默更令她害怕。她逐字記下伊凡的話，也記下時間與在場者。

Back at her desk, Mei opened the project archive. Northline had once required every designer to save a dated version before a public presentation. Evan had recently tried to reinforce a new policy that gave managers complete control over final files, regardless of who had produced the drafts. Yet the old server still recorded earlier changes. Mei found her first model, a list of safety tests, and a comment explaining why the western shelter mattered. The revised model would triple the walking distance for some residents. She made no secret copies of private medical data; she saved only her own sketches, public route measurements, and the server's visible revision identifiers. Ruth warned her not to confront Evan with an accusation she could not yet prove. Mei agreed. That evening she placed the printed map in a folder marked QUESTIONS, not ANSWERS. The distinction felt small, but it was the first choice she could defend.

回到座位後，梅打開專案檔案庫。北線過去規定，設計師在公開發表前都要儲存標明日期的版本。伊凡最近試圖強化新政策，讓主管能全面控制最終檔案，不論草稿由誰製作。然而舊伺服器仍留有較早的修改紀錄。梅找到第一版模型、安全測試清單，以及說明西區避難站重要性的註記。新版模型會讓部分居民步行距離變成三倍。她沒有私自複製醫療個資，只保存自己的草圖、公開路線測量和伺服器可見的修訂識別碼。露絲提醒她，不要在尚未證實前直接指控伊凡。梅同意了。當晚，她把印出的地圖放進標著「問題」而非「答案」的資料夾。差別雖小，卻是她第一個能夠清楚辯護的選擇。

The next morning, Mei asked the records clerk whether Northline still kept an external backup. He answered that the original archive had been transferred when the office changed its software, but he could not release anything without permission. Instead of arguing, she requested the written retention policy and booked a meeting with Ruth. A careful inquiry would take longer than a public complaint, perhaps longer than the five weeks left in her internship. Mei feared that the decision to wait might look weak. She reminded herself that a strong case was more than a dramatic speech. It needed a clear sequence: a design, a dated record, an alteration, and someone willing to explain what each document meant. Outside, workers painted fresh arrows on the pavement. They followed Evan's new map, not hers. Mei watched them for a minute, then started a timeline. She would find out whose decision had sent those arrows in the wrong direction.

隔天早晨，梅向檔案員詢問北線是否仍保留外部備份。對方說，辦公室更換軟體時原始資料庫已轉移，但未獲授權不能交出資料。梅沒有爭吵，而是索取書面保存政策，並約露絲會面。審慎調查會比公開抱怨花更多時間，甚至可能超過她剩下的五週實習。她怕等待看起來像怯弱，卻提醒自己：有力的案件不只是激昂的演說，還需要清楚的順序——設計、標日期的紀錄、修改，以及能說明每份文件意義的人。窗外工人正在路面畫上新的箭頭；他們依照的是伊凡的新地圖，而不是她的。梅看了一會兒，開始建立時間線。她要查清楚，是誰把那些箭頭引向錯誤的方向。
