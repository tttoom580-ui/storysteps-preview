The First Hearing

The hearing began beneath a bronze dome in the municipal hall. Nadia sat among the press, while a city analyst prepared the public displays. Evan arrived with the confident smile of an entrepreneur who expected applause. He called Mei arrogant and claimed that a junior intern could not understand the sector's complex needs. Mei resisted the impulse to answer an insult with another. Instead she presented the dated submission receipt, the visible revision trail, and the route measurements. “My initiative was to integrate existing shelters into a safer network,” she explained. “I am asking who changed that design and under what authority.” The chair requested that each witness testify only to what they personally knew. Omar described the records. Mrs. Huang described her father's route. Their accounts were different kinds of evidence, and neither was offered as a complete solution.

聽證在市政廳青銅圓頂下開始。娜迪亞坐在記者席，市府分析員準備公開展示資料。伊凡帶著企業家期待掌聲般的自信微笑入場，稱梅傲慢，說初階實習生不懂產業的複雜需求。梅忍住用侮辱回擊的衝動，改為提出標日期的提交收據、可見的修訂歷史與路線測量。「我的主動提案是把現有避難站整合成較安全的網絡，」她解釋，「我只問誰改了設計，依據什麼權限。」主席要求證人只就親自知道的事作證。歐瑪說明紀錄，黃太太描述父親的路線。兩者是不同種類的證據，沒有人說任何一項可以單獨解決全部問題。

The contractor's lawyer argued that Northline had an anonymous license to modify route data. Mara replied that permission to edit did not establish original authorship or guarantee the safety of every edit. Evan tried to make the debate ridiculous by comparing the project to genetics: many people contributed small parts, he said, just as countless molecules made a living body. Mei replied that collaborative work could still credit its contributors accurately. She also asked why the altered western path passed by a private property recently offered for sale with a large mortgage. The chair ruled that the property's finances were outside the hearing's immediate scope. Mei accepted the ruling. A rumor about property could damage her credibility if she used it as evidence before it was verified. She stayed with the questions the panel could actually answer.

承包商律師主張，北線獲得匿名授權可修改路線資料。瑪拉回應，編輯許可不代表原始作者身分，也不能保證每項修改安全。伊凡以遺傳學比喻企圖讓爭論顯得荒唐：許多人都貢獻小部分，就像無數分子組成生命。梅回答，合作作品仍能準確列出貢獻者。她也問新版西區路線為何經過一塊最近出售、背負高額抵押貸款的私人土地。主席裁定，土地財務問題不屬本次聽證直接範圍。梅接受裁定；如果未查證就把房產傳聞當證據，只會損害可信度。她把焦點留在聽證小組真正能回答的問題上。

A community witness mentioned the rising cost of groceries, including meat from the local butcher, to explain why residents could not simply pay for private transport. Another described wildlife along the river and the need to sustain safe pedestrian paths without damaging habitats. The subjects seemed distant from copyright and authorship, yet they exposed the route's real purpose. The panel then examined a record showing that the city's project officer had approved an earlier design, not the one later printed. This was the first independent confirmation of an unauthorized-looking change, although the precise authority remained disputed. The chair ordered a limited technical audit and a temporary safety assessment. Mei did not declare victory. The order protected the inquiry from being dismissed as a personal quarrel.

一名社區證人提到食品雜貨漲價，包括當地肉舖的肉價，說明居民不能隨便付錢搭私人交通工具。另一人談到河岸野生動物，以及如何在不破壞棲地的前提下維持安全步道。這些議題看似離署名與著作權很遠，卻說明路線真正要服務的目的。小組接著檢視一份紀錄，顯示市府專案官批准的是較早版本，而非後來印出的版本。這是首次由獨立來源確認存在看似未經授權的變更，儘管權限仍有爭議。主席下令進行有限技術稽核，並先做臨時安全評估。梅沒有宣布勝利；這項命令使調查不再能被簡化成私人爭吵。

Before the panel reconvened, a witness asked that maps near an abortion clinic not reveal details about individual visitors. The chair agreed that such medical information was unrelated to route authorship and deserved protection. Omar then explained that a gas molecule and a dust particle were different things, and that a sensor could react differently to each without making all readings false. He also showed a vertical diagram of the stairway that the revised route had treated as level ground. Mei refused to betray the residents' trust merely to strengthen a presentation. She asked the clerk to remove personal examples from the public slide and retain only verified measurements. The changes made the display less dramatic but more accurate. Mara told her that protecting irrelevant private details was not a weakness in her case. It was evidence of sound judgment.

小組恢復開會前，一名證人要求避孕與人工流產診所附近的地圖不要揭露個別訪客資訊。主席同意，這些醫療資訊與路線署名無關，應受保護。歐瑪接著解釋，氣體分子和灰塵顆粒並不相同，感測器可能對兩者有不同反應，但這不代表所有讀數都錯。他還展示樓梯的垂直剖面圖，指出新版路線竟把階梯視為平坦地面。梅拒絕為了讓簡報更有說服力，就背叛居民的信任。她請書記從公開投影片移除個人案例，只保留經確認的測量數據。修改後畫面較不戲劇化，卻更準確。瑪拉說，保護無關的私人細節不會削弱案件，反而顯示健全判斷。

After the hearing, Nadia asked how Mei felt about the decision. “Relieved, but not finished,” she answered. A city official had promised to preserve the relevant files, and an independent reviewer would compare approved plans with issued maps. Those steps mattered more than a loud headline. Ruth met Mei on the steps and told her that the real test might come when the audit discovered mistakes in Mei's own work. “Then those mistakes should be corrected too,” Mei said. Across the square, Evan spoke urgently into his phone. He still possessed influence, and the inquiry might expose other departments as well. Mei had earned a chance for a fair technical review, not a guaranteed outcome. On the ride home she watched the city lights slide across the window and began preparing the documents she would be willing to place under the same scrutiny as everyone else's.

聽證後，娜迪亞問梅對裁定有何感想。「鬆了口氣，但還沒結束。」梅說。市府官員承諾保存相關檔案，獨立審查員也將比較獲批准計畫與正式發布地圖。這些步驟比響亮標題更重要。露絲在階梯上迎接她，提醒真正的考驗可能是稽核找到梅自己的錯誤。「那也應該修正。」梅回答。廣場對面，伊凡正急切地講電話。他仍有影響力，而調查也可能波及其他部門。梅贏得的是公平技術審查的機會，不是保證的結果。回家車上，她望著車窗滑過的城市燈光，開始準備願意接受同等檢驗的資料。
