沒有署名的提議

The Offer with No Signature

實習結束前，我要拿回自己的名字 · 第 5 / 25 章 · 4000–5000 · 英中對照

The public meeting had excellent attendance. Residents filled every chair, and two people stood at the back with a video camera. Evan presented a contemporary approach to emergency mobility, insisting that the revised plan was a reasonable transition from an old system to a new one. Mei listened as he described the project's essence without mentioning the original designer. When the chair invited questions, she stood up and explained her measurements. A corporate representative called her findings controversial and said that a large corporation could not adjust plans for every resident. Mei did not defy him merely for the pleasure of winning an argument. She showed the exact segment where the walking distance had changed and asked who had endorsed the change. The chair wrote down the question, and the meeting record captured the silence that followed.

公開會議出席踴躍，椅子全坐滿，後排還有兩個人拿攝影機。伊凡提出一套當代緊急交通方案，堅稱新版計畫是從舊系統轉向新系統的合理過渡。梅聽他談計畫的核心，卻完全不提原始設計者。主席開放提問時，梅起身說明自己的測量結果。一位企業代表稱她的發現充滿爭議，說大型公司不能為每位居民調整計畫。梅並非為了爭一口氣而挑戰他；她展示步行距離變化的確切路段，問到底是誰核可了修改。主席把問題記下，會議紀錄也留下隨之而來的沉默。

During the break, Evan offered Mei a private arrangement. He would provide a positive recommendation and a paid position if she accepted a modest acknowledgment in the final report. He described the offering as generous, then joked that she should buy a new outfit for her first day. Mei felt a bruise of humiliation where his words landed. “A recommendation cannot replace a truthful record,” she replied. He warned her that interns rarely understood the overall perspective of public projects. Mei admitted she could not yet see every constraint. That was exactly why she wanted the records reviewed. Ruth watched from the doorway but did not answer for her. The irony was painful: the man who had removed her name now offered to put it back as a favor. Mei refused the bargain without raising her voice.

休息時，伊凡私下提出交換：只要梅接受在最終報告中獲得一點致謝，他就給她正面推薦與有薪職位。他形容這是慷慨的提議，還開玩笑說她該買套新衣服迎接第一天上班。梅感覺羞辱像瘀青一樣落在心上。「推薦信不能取代真實紀錄，」她回答。伊凡警告她，實習生很少了解公共計畫的整體觀點。梅承認自己無法掌握每項限制，也正因如此才要求審查紀錄。露絲站在門邊看著，沒有替她回答。諷刺的是，刪掉她名字的人如今把恢復署名當作恩惠。梅沒有提高音量，就拒絕了交易。

Outside, the residents began their usual evening ritual of checking on elderly neighbors. Alina brought a statistical summary from the field survey. Mara examined it and pointed out that the sample was small; it could facilitate an inquiry, but it could not settle every question about the city's population. Mei appreciated the correction. She asked the meeting chair to establish an independent review of the route, the change authorization, and the authorship record. The request concerned public safety and transparent process, not a personal revenge campaign. The chair agreed to place it on the next agenda. As the crowd dispersed, Mei saw Evan talking quietly to a board member. She did not pretend the battle was over. Yet she had achieved a limited, concrete result: the meeting minutes now contained her question and the promise of a review.

會場外，居民開始照例在晚間探望高齡鄰居。艾莉娜帶來田野調查的統計摘要。瑪拉檢視後指出樣本偏小；它可以促成調查，卻不能解答整座城市的人口問題。梅接受這項修正，向主席要求獨立審查路線、修改授權與作者紀錄。這項要求關乎公共安全與透明程序，不是個人報復。主席同意把議案排進下次議程。人群散去時，梅看見伊凡與一名董事低聲談話。她並未假裝戰鬥結束了，但已取得一項有限而具體的成果：會議紀錄留下了她的問題，以及會進行審查的承諾。

After the session, two board members continued to contend that a good outcome should be enough, regardless of whose name appeared on the cover. Mei asked them to imagine a bridge built without a clear engineering record. If the bridge needed repairs, someone would have to know who made the calculations and which revisions followed. “You need not endorse my whole argument,” she told them. “Just explain how the next engineer can check this work.” One member admitted that the question deserved a written answer. The other remained unconvinced. Mei made a note of both responses without turning disagreement into evidence of bad faith. She was learning that persuasion could be slow even when a question was reasonable, and that she would need to live with some people disagreeing.

會後兩名董事仍主張，只要結果良好就夠了，封面寫誰的名字不重要。梅請他們想像一座沒有清楚工程紀錄的橋；若日後需要修復，總得有人知道誰做了計算、後來又修正什麼。「你們不必認同我全部論點，」她說，「只要解釋下一位工程師如何查核即可。」一名董事承認這值得書面回答，另一位仍不服。梅記下兩者反應，沒有把不同意見當成惡意的證據。她逐漸明白，即使問題合理，說服也可能很慢，而且得接受有些人始終不同意。

On the bus home, Mei opened the email in which she had first submitted the design. The attachments still carried her name, but Northline controlled the final report. She could not force the organization to agree with her account. She could, however, build a record that other people could inspect. Her friend asked whether recovering credit was really worth risking a career. Mei answered that the issue was not simply recognition. A city should know who designed a safety system, who modified it, and why. If those answers remained hidden, a future mistake would be harder to correct. She sent Mara a clean timeline, locked the folder, and went to sleep. Five chapters into the struggle, the truth had not won; it had finally earned a place on the agenda.

回家公車上，梅打開最初提交設計的電子郵件。附件仍有她的名字，但北線掌握最終報告。她不能強迫組織接受自己的說法，卻能建立讓其他人查閱的紀錄。朋友問她，為了拿回署名而冒職涯風險是否值得。梅回答，問題不只是獲得肯定。城市應該知道安全系統由誰設計、誰修改，以及為什麼修改；若答案被掩蓋，日後出錯將更難修正。她把整理好的時間線寄給瑪拉，鎖上資料夾，準備睡覺。走到第五章，真相還沒贏；它終於得到一個正式議程的位置。
