同一片海的兩張地圖

Two Maps of the Same Sea

An old avenue along the harbor led to a private service door behind the center. In its former visitor room, a cradle from a family exhibition stood beneath a model submarine. Workers had begun to decorate the room for a reopening that was never announced. The public elevator was locked, but a paper drawing showed another small lift behind the wall. Nina noticed that the exhibition plan and the safety map gave that lift different numbers. She asked Jae to photograph both labels before anyone moved the display case.

港邊一條舊大道通往觀測中心後方的私人維修門。昔日的參觀室裡，一張家庭展覽用的搖籃放在模型潛艇下方。工人曾開始裝飾房間，準備一場從未公告的重新開放。公共電梯上了鎖，但紙本圖面顯示牆後還有一座小型升降機。戴寧發現展覽計畫與安全地圖給那座升降機不同的編號。她請佳恩先拍下兩個標籤，不要急著搬動展示櫃。

The emergency store contained one firework for a harbor show, a sealed gallon of cleaning fluid, and a binder labeled HEADQUARTERS. At first Nina thought it might be a child's imagination exercise, because a drawing of a leopard was pinned to the cover. But the binder held dated inspection orders. A technician had marked one page with the word magical in red ink, mocking a repair that apparently required no tools. The comment was odd, but the engineer's initials next to it matched an entry in Leya's notebook.

緊急儲藏室裡放著一枚港口表演用的煙火、一加侖密封清潔液，以及寫著「總部」的資料夾。戴寧起初以為那是兒童想像力練習，因為封面釘著一張豹的圖畫。但資料夾內是按日期排列的檢查命令。一位技師用紅筆把某次不用工具的「修理」嘲諷成魔法般的奇蹟。這個註解很奇怪，旁邊的工程師縮寫卻與樂雅筆記中的一筆紀錄相同。

A guide said the center once kept an organic garden on the roof. Green ribbon marked the safe paths for visitors. Nina saw salt water spit from a damaged joint near the service lift, although the surrounding floor looked dry. Beyond it, a steep ladder descended toward the old bridge. The newest survey had called that passage closed, while an older survey listed it as a temporary escape route. This was no harmless difference of style. A threat had been hidden by replacing a clear warning with a single word: “unused.”

一名導覽員說，中心屋頂以前有一座有機花園，綠絲帶用來標示遊客的安全動線。戴寧看見維修升降機附近的接頭噴出鹹水，周圍地面卻似乎乾燥。再往裡走，一道陡梯通往舊橋道。最新版勘查圖把通道標為封閉，舊版卻把它列為臨時逃生路。這不是無傷大雅的用詞不同，而是有人用「停用」一字取代清楚警示，把威脅藏了起來。

A tourist photograph lay between the two plans, its corners wet. Jae used a clean cloth to wipe the protective cover, not the photograph itself. The picture showed two layers of glass, separated by an inspection space that the newer plan did not identify. Nina traced the visible steel ribs without touching the panel. If the layers had separate pressure sensors, a knock could travel through both at different times. She was not ready to explain the sound, but at least she knew why a single window on the map might be misleading.

兩張圖之間夾著一張遊客照片，角落受了潮。佳恩用乾淨布擦拭保護套，而沒有直接擦照片。照片裡有兩層玻璃，中間隔著一個新版圖面沒有標出的檢查空間。戴寧隔空沿著可見的鋼肋比對位置，沒有觸碰面板。如果兩層玻璃各有壓力感測器，一次敲擊就可能在不同時間傳過兩層。她還無法解釋聲音，但至少知道地圖上的單片窗戶為何會誤導人。

Fang insisted that the public map was the only approved map. Nina asked him to show the approval date and the name of the person who checked the replacement. He produced a cover page but not the full signed plan. The difference mattered because moving an emergency route could change how people escaped during a leak. Jae suggested that they postpone the search until the coast office sent an independent copy. Nina agreed, though the delay felt painful. Guessing at a hidden route would not bring Leya home safely.

方主任堅稱公開地圖才是唯一核准的版本。戴寧請他提出核准日期及審核替換圖面的人名，他只拿出封面，卻沒有完整簽核圖。這個差異很重要，因為改變逃生路線可能影響漏水時人們如何撤離。佳恩建議先暫停搜尋，等海岸辦公室送來獨立保存的副本。戴寧雖然覺得拖延難受，仍然同意。靠猜測闖進隱藏通道，無法安全帶樂雅回家。

That afternoon they measured the distance between two visible bolts. On the old drawing the bolts formed the border of an inspection space; on the new one they appeared to hold a solid wall. The real distance matched the old drawing. Jae placed a harmless paper marker by the service door so the team could return to the same point. Nina called the finding evidence of a missing space, not proof that anyone had trapped Leya there. They needed a second source before taking the next step.

當天下午，他們量了兩顆可見螺栓之間的距離。舊圖上，螺栓圍出一處檢查空間；新圖上，它們卻像是固定整片實心牆。實際距離符合舊圖。佳恩在維修門旁放了一張不影響設備的紙標記，方便團隊返回同一位置。戴寧把發現記為「存在被省略的空間」，而不是「有人把樂雅困在裡面」的證明。下一步還需要第二項來源。

When the three knocks returned at dusk, Nina stood where both glass layers were visible. The second blow seemed to arrive slightly later at the outer frame. Jae's camera registered the delay, although neither knew its cause. They left the frame untouched. Back at the table, Nina placed the two maps, the photograph, and the new measurement in separate clear sleeves. She drew a question mark beside the missing space. For the first time she could describe exactly what the map failed to show.

傍晚三聲敲擊再次響起，戴寧站在能同時看見兩層玻璃的位置。第二下敲擊似乎在外層框架稍晚傳來，佳恩的攝影機也錄到了這個延遲，雖然兩人還不知道原因。他們沒有碰框架。回到桌旁，戴寧把兩張圖、照片及新測量值分裝進透明護套，在被省略的空間旁畫上問號。她終於能精確說明新版地圖漏掉了什麼。
