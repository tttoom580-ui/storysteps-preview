牆後的錄音機

A Recorder Behind the Wall

玻璃海牆的第三聲 · 第 9 / 25 章 · 2000–3000 · 英中對照 · 校訂中

用互動版查詞、朗讀與收藏

The access panel was fixed with brass screws beneath an abandoned buffet sign. It would be costly to remove the whole wall, so a designer from the rescue office suggested using a narrow camera. Jae brought a small hammer, not to strike the sea glass, but to loosen the metal cover on a safe interior frame. A worker muttered that the job was hell. Nina found his remark more humorous than useful, yet she understood his frustration. The floor felt icy beneath their boots, and each step required care.

檢查面板以黃銅螺絲固定在一塊廢棄自助餐招牌下方。拆整面牆的成本太高，救援辦公室的設計師建議改用狹窄攝影機。佳恩帶來小鐵鎚，不是為了敲海玻璃，而是鬆開安全內框上的金屬蓋。一名工人嘀咕這份工作簡直是地獄。戴寧覺得這句話比技術建議有趣，但也理解他的挫折。腳下地板冰冷，每一步都得小心。

A mist of salt drifted from the corner, where a porcelain cup stood beside an old recorder. Jae had to scrub dirt from the outer label before he could read the serial number, but he did not damage the original seal. The recorder had an unfamiliar skip switch that could repeat a short sound. Nearby, a stadium poster and a tangerine peel suggested the room had been used for storage, not only equipment. Nina asked the team to list the items separately so no object was given a false connection to Leya. A cartoon kangaroo on the recorder box showed that the machine had once been used for children's lessons, long before the recent warning failures.

角落飄出淡淡的鹽霧，一只瓷杯放在舊錄音機旁。佳恩得先刷掉外標籤的泥污，才能讀出序號，但沒有破壞原有封條。錄音機上有個陌生的跳轉開關，可以重複短聲。附近還有體育場海報與橘皮，暗示房間也被拿來堆物，而不只是放設備。戴寧請團隊逐項列出物品，避免把無關東西硬說成樂雅的線索。 錄音機盒上的卡通袋鼠顯示，這台機器早在最近的警示故障前，就曾用於兒童課程。

A technical manual warned that careless handling could threaten the pressure sensors. Jae used a plastic wrench to tighten the safe cover after photographing its position. On the recorder's storage card, Nina found repeated files whose names matched the hours of the sea-wall knocks. They were not recordings of the knocks; they were short alert sounds used for training. The distinction mattered because the recorder could explain a whistle, not a physical pressure rise. Nina wrote that limit beside the finding before anyone exaggerated it.

技術手冊警告，不小心的操作可能威脅壓力感測器。佳恩拍下位置後，用塑膠扳手鎖緊安全外蓋。戴寧在錄音機的記憶卡裡找到重複檔案，檔名與海牆敲擊時段相同。但那不是敲擊錄音，而是訓練用短警示聲。這個差別很重要：錄音機能解釋哨聲，不能解釋真正的壓力上升。她先把限制寫在發現旁邊，免得其他人誇大。

The storage card also held a short instruction in Leya's voice. She said the west service room was difficult to reach when the bridge pressure changed and asked that the old valve map be kept near the rescue entrance. The sentence stopped before she gave a room number. Nina replayed the file once through a private headset and copied the wording exactly. She did not play it over the center's public speaker. The family's consent would be needed before such a personal recording became part of any public report.

記憶卡裡還有樂雅聲音的一段短指示。她說橋道壓力變動時，西側維修室很難抵達，因此要求把舊閥門地圖放在救援入口附近。句子在說出房間編號前就中斷了。戴寧透過私人耳機重聽一次，逐字記下，沒有在中心公共喇叭播放。若要把這段私人錄音放入公開報告，仍須取得家屬同意。

Fang claimed the message was an unfinished training exercise. Nina could not rule that out simply from its length. But the recording's file date matched the week of Leya's complaint, and the old valve map was missing from the official binder. She requested the original memory card for independent review. Fang refused at first, saying it belonged to the center. The rescue officer explained that preserving evidence during a missing-person search did not mean publishing it. Fang finally allowed a documented copy while keeping the original under seal.

方主任主張那是尚未完成的訓練練習。戴寧不能光憑長度就排除這種可能。但錄音日期與樂雅抱怨的那一週相符，正式資料夾裡又缺了舊閥門圖。她要求把原記憶卡交由獨立人員查核。方主任起初拒絕，說那是中心財產。救援官員解釋，失蹤搜索期間保存證據，不等於公開內容。方主任最後允許在封存原件的情況下製作有紀錄的副本。

The contractor who had moved the acoustic panels arrived to answer questions. He recalled reconnecting the speaker after being told to make the closed center sound occupied, so strangers would not enter. He could identify the order, but not the person who wrote it, because the instruction came through a shared work account. Nina recorded this uncertainty instead of choosing a convenient suspect. The contractor had helped create the ghost story without necessarily knowing about Leya's disappearance.

搬動吸音板的承包商到場回答問題。他記得有人要求他重新接上喇叭，讓封閉中心聽起來有人使用，以免陌生人進入。他能辨識那張工單，卻說不出指示由誰寫下，因為它來自共用工作帳號。戴寧把不確定性記下，而沒有挑一個方便的嫌疑人。承包商確實幫忙製造了鬼故事，卻未必知道樂雅失蹤的事。

Late that night, the three knocks returned. This time Nina could hear the pump cycle begin just before them. Jae marked the time without moving near the glass. The old tape, the sound sensor, and the pump chart now told compatible stories. They still did not tell where Leya was. Nina turned off the private recorder and placed the missing valve map at the top of tomorrow's request list. The sounds were becoming less mysterious; the physical search was becoming more urgent.

深夜，三聲敲擊又回來了。這次戴寧聽見泵浦在敲擊前開始一輪運轉。佳恩記下時間，沒有靠近玻璃。舊錄音、聲音感測器及泵浦圖表如今說出彼此相容的故事，仍然不能說明樂雅在哪裡。戴寧關掉私人錄音機，把失蹤的閥門圖放在明天申請清單最上方。聲音愈來愈不神祕，實際搜索卻愈來愈迫切。
