沒有遊客鑰匙的房間

The Room with No Visitor Key

玻璃海牆的第三聲 · 第 3 / 25 章 · 2000–3000 · 英中對照 · 校訂完成

用互動版查詞、朗讀與收藏

The center could no longer afford a careless search. In the former teaching kitchen, an apron hung above a bucket filled with dry sand. A lamp cast long shadows across the table, where students had once been served dessert after science workshops. Nina found dirt inside a sealed equipment box and wondered how it had arrived there. A dramatic notice warned against unauthorized entry, but the room's actual key log was missing. Jae, always energetic in emergencies, asked permission to examine the lock without opening it. Nina recorded the request first.

觀測中心已經承擔不起一次粗心的搜索。舊教學廚房裡，一條圍裙掛在盛滿乾沙的桶子上方。燈光把長長的影子投在桌上，學生以前參加科學工作坊後會在這裡吃甜點。戴寧在密封設備箱裡發現泥土，想知道它怎麼進來的。一張措辭誇張的告示警告不得擅自進入，房間的實際鑰匙紀錄卻不見了。遇到緊急情況總是精力充沛的佳恩，請求在不開門的情況下檢查鎖。戴寧先記錄這項請求。

A faded entry ticket had been placed beneath a greenhouse photograph. The inspector's signature was visible on the back, along with the date of an ongoing review. Beside it sat a lollipop wrapper printed with a cartoon ox and penguin. Nina disliked the childish clutter, but she made herself look carefully. Under the wrapper was a narrow diagram of a service pit, its corner fixed with a metal plug. A senior worker said the drawing had been used in a safety game to teach children not to spy through dangerous openings. Leya's old notes said the colors on this drawing would fade when exposed to sea water, which made an original copy especially valuable.

一張褪色入場券壓在溫室照片下方。背面還看得見檢查員的簽名與一項持續審查的日期。旁邊有張棒棒糖包裝紙，印著卡通公牛和企鵝。戴寧不喜歡這堆幼稚雜物，但仍逼自己細看。包裝紙底下是一張狹窄維修坑的示意圖，角落用金屬塞固定。一位資深員工說，這張圖曾用於兒童安全遊戲，教孩子不要從危險開口偷偷窺探。 樂雅的舊筆記指出，這張圖上的顏色接觸海水後會褪色，因此原件尤其珍貴。

The diagram mattered for a reason unrelated to the game. It showed a second cable passing behind the locked room and reaching the hidden space between the glass walls. Nina checked the line against the electrical panel but did not touch its switches. The panel contained the number of a training recorder. Tao remembered that a recorded voice once guided visitors through the lower corridor. He had believed the recorder was removed years earlier. Nina asked him to describe the voice without guessing whose voice it might resemble.

這張圖重要的原因與遊戲無關。它畫出一條第二組電纜，從鎖住的房間後方通往兩層玻璃之間的隱藏空間。戴寧把線路與配電盤比對，卻沒有動開關。配電盤上寫著一台訓練錄音機的編號。阿卓記得以前有錄音引導遊客穿過下層走廊，他一直以為設備早在多年前拆除。戴寧請他只描述聲音本身，不要猜測它像誰。

A maintenance clerk brought copies of the digital entry log. Every line between midnight and morning showed the same time, down to the second. That could happen if a faulty program copied one field into every row. It could also happen if someone had replaced real records with a false file. Nina asked the clerk to preserve the computer exactly as found and to locate the daily paper register. Jae checked the door seal from outside. Neither wanted to lose a useful clue by forcing a lock too soon.

維修文書送來電子門禁紀錄的副本。午夜到清晨的每一行都顯示完全相同的時間，連秒數也一致。這可能是故障程式把同一個欄位複製到每一行，也可能有人用假檔案取代真實紀錄。戴寧請文書原樣保存電腦，並尋找每日的紙本登記簿。佳恩從外側檢查門的封條。兩人都不願因太早強行開鎖，而失去有用的線索。

Fang offered a simple explanation: Leya had entered through the teaching room, heard the false voice, and left by herself. But his account did not explain the missing paper log, the second cable, or why the equipment room still had power. Nina separated each claim on a new sheet. She could not reject the whole story merely because she disliked Fang. She needed an observation that would distinguish an ordinary faulty system from a deliberate change. The training recorder's serial number offered a promising place to begin.

方主任提出一套簡單說法：樂雅從教學室進入，聽見錯誤錄音後，便自行離開。但這個說法解釋不了紙本日誌失蹤、第二組電纜，或設備室為何仍有供電。戴寧把每項主張分別寫在新紙上。她不能只因為不喜歡方主任就全部否定，必須找到能區分普通故障與刻意改動的觀察結果。訓練錄音機的序號正是一個有希望的起點。

In the afternoon, the rescue office confirmed that no exit camera had recorded Leya leaving. The absence of a picture was not proof that she remained inside, since one camera had been broken. But combined with her untouched bicycle and work bag, it made the director's certainty difficult to accept. Nina called Leya's family and explained what was known, what was unknown, and why the team was moving slowly. The family asked her to keep searching without taking unnecessary risks. She gave them her direct number.

下午，救援辦公室確認沒有任何出口攝影機拍到樂雅離開。沒有畫面並不能證明她仍在裡面，因為其中一台攝影機早已故障。不過，再加上原封未動的腳踏車和工作袋，方主任的確定口氣便很難令人接受。戴寧致電樂雅家人，解釋已知與未知之處，以及團隊為何緩慢行動。家人請她繼續找，但不要做不必要的冒險。她留下自己的直通電話。

That night, Nina replayed the guard's recording through headphones, far from the live speaker. Immediately before the whistle, she heard a soft click that occurred at the same point on both copies. She marked the location and asked Jae to compare it with the training recorder's automatic start sound. The test would happen the next morning with the public system disconnected. It was a small step, yet it offered a way to answer one frightening question without trusting the frightening voice.

當晚，戴寧在遠離現場喇叭的地方，戴耳機重聽警衛錄音。每份錄音的哨聲前，完全同一處都有輕微的喀噠聲。她標記位置，請佳恩將其與訓練錄音機自動啟動的聲音比較。測試會在隔天早上切斷公共系統後進行。雖然只是小小一步，卻提供了不必相信那道可怕聲音，也能回答一個問題的方法。
