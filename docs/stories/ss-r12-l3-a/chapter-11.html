濕袖子上的墨跡

Ink on a Wet Sleeve

玻璃海牆的第三聲 · 第 11 / 25 章 · 2000–3000 · 英中對照 · 校訂中

用互動版查詞、朗讀與收藏

A bloody red mark on the old floor appeared frightening until Nina identified it as dried paint. A breeze moved through the upper door, carrying the smell of food from the rescue kitchen. The clerk had filed a request for credit to repair a broken light, but an anonymous note called him a criminal. Nina rejected that label. She could see he was emotional, and a cruel rumor might ruin his small fortune in savings. The real problem was finding the missing observer before her food and water ran out.

舊地板上血紅色的痕跡看起來嚇人，直到戴寧認出那是乾掉的油漆。微風穿過上層門，把救援廚房的食物香氣帶進來。文書曾申請賒帳修理一盞壞燈，匿名紙條卻罵他罪犯。戴寧拒絕這種標籤。她看得出他情緒激動，殘酷的謠言可能毀了他辛苦存下的一小筆財富。真正的問題仍是趕在失蹤觀測員糧水耗盡前找到她。

At lunch the crew felt hunger but worked carefully. Tao warned Jae not to lick salt from his fingers after handling equipment. A loaf of bread and a sealed parcel sat beside a tin of pepper. Through the window Nina saw the rise and fall of a fishing boat, as steady as a pitch used by a musician. A fisherman claimed he had seen Leya at dawn, but admitted he had been too far away to identify her face. Nina asked him not to pretend certainty in order to help. The team shared its meal slowly; even ordinary routines became important when attention was stretched across a long search.

午餐時隊員感到飢餓，仍然小心工作。阿卓提醒佳恩，碰完設備不要舔手指上的鹽。桌邊放著一條麵包、一件密封包裹及一罐胡椒。窗外漁船隨水面起伏，節奏像音樂家定下的音高。一名漁夫說清晨見過樂雅，但也承認距離太遠，看不清臉。戴寧請他不要為了幫忙，就假裝自己十分確定。 隊員緩慢吃完午餐；在漫長搜索中注意力被拉扯時，普通作息也變得重要。

A narrow passage descended beside the sea wall, but Nina would resist requests to enter without approval. A torn sleeve lay near a slender pipe on the downward slope. Someone had used the fabric to twist a temporary marker around a cable. The union representative and Jae both identified the knot as a common work method, not a secret sign. Nina hurt her wrist while lifting a cover and immediately reported the minor injury. Concealing a small accident, she said, was how dangerous habits became part of a workplace.

一條狹窄通道沿著海牆往下，但戴寧會抵抗尚未核准就進入的要求。斜坡上一根細管旁有半截破袖子，有人把布扭成臨時標記繫在電纜上。工會代表和佳恩都認出那是常見工作結法，不是祕密暗號。戴寧掀開蓋板時弄傷手腕，立刻通報這個輕傷。她說，隱瞞小事故正是讓危險習慣變成職場常態的方法。

The fabric carried a number written in waterproof ink. It matched a missing room on the oldest plan. Nina asked a textile specialist to examine whether the sleeve had been torn recently. The specialist would not promise an exact date, but could compare it with samples from Leya's work clothes. Meanwhile, the team inspected the pipe using a camera from the safe upper walkway. The pipe curved toward a dry chamber that was separated from the main route by a pressure door.

布料上用防水墨水寫著一個數字，與最舊圖面上的失蹤房間一致。戴寧請紡織專家檢查袖子是否最近才被撕開。專家不保證能判定精確日期，但可以與樂雅工作服的樣本比較。與此同時，團隊從安全的上層步道用攝影機檢查細管。管線彎向一處乾燥艙室，那裡與主路之間隔著一道承壓門。

The coast office confirmed that the room once served as an emergency rest station. It had an independent air bottle, a manual light, and a speaking tube. The bottle's last inspection date was uncertain, so nobody could assume it still held safe air. Nina added a request for a remote breathing-air measurement. Jae said that knowing the room existed was not enough; they had to know what conditions might exist inside it. The rescue plan grew more complex, but also more honest.

海岸辦公室確認，房間以前是緊急休息艙，有獨立空氣瓶、手動照明與傳聲管。空氣瓶最後一次檢查日期不明，因此不能假定裡面仍有安全空氣。戴寧追加遠端空氣品質測量的要求。佳恩說，知道房間存在還不夠，必須知道裡面可能是什麼環境。救援計畫變得複雜，卻也更誠實。

Nina played Leya's short message to an independent officer with the family's permission. Together they checked the pauses, room number, and background hum. The hum resembled the manual air fan shown on the old drawing. That did not prove Leya remained in the room today, but it suggested she had visited it recently. The officer asked for a new recording of the fan, made from outside the sealed passage. Nina agreed. Each source had to stand on its own before it could support another.

戴寧取得家屬同意後，向一名獨立官員播放樂雅的短錄音。他們一起檢查停頓、房間編號與背景低鳴。低鳴很像舊圖上手動送風機的聲音。這不能證明樂雅現在仍在房內，卻暗示她近期曾到過。官員要求從封閉通道外另外錄下送風機聲，戴寧同意。每項來源都必須先能獨立成立，才能支持其他證據。

Before the evening watch, Jae placed a small listening device near the safe end of the speaking tube. The device captured nothing resembling speech, only a slow mechanical vibration. Nina marked the negative result and arranged another check at low tide. She did not want silence to become a false assurance. A person who was exhausted or injured might be unable to answer. The team would continue looking for physical signs without demanding that Leya prove she deserved rescue.

傍晚值班前，佳恩在傳聲管安全的一端放上小型收音裝置。裝置沒有錄到像人聲的東西，只有緩慢的機械震動。戴寧標記這項陰性結果，安排低潮再測。她不願把沉默誤當安全證明。疲倦或受傷的人可能無法回答，團隊會繼續找實體跡象，而不要求樂雅先證明自己值得被救。
