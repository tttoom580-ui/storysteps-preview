玻璃上的三聲敲擊

Three Knocks at the Glass

Nina Dai had been told that her visit to Blue Bay Underwater Center was acceptable only if she stayed beside the guide. She was twenty-nine and had come to advise the safety office after a young observer, Leya, vanished. At the entrance, the guide Tao rested his chin on his hand, staring at an unopened carton of coconut milk. Nina tried to connect her recorder to the building clock. The display showed a date that was two days old. She wrote the difference down before anyone could offer an excuse.

戴寧被告知，只要留在導覽員身邊，她進入藍灣水下觀測中心的申請就可以接受。她二十九歲，是為了年輕觀測員樂雅失蹤一事，前來向安全單位提供建議。入口處，導覽員阿卓用手托著下巴，望著一盒未開封的椰奶。戴寧試著把錄音設備連上建築物的時鐘，螢幕卻顯示兩天前的日期。她在其他人找藉口前先記下這個差異。

The director, Fang, called the rescue request a cruel way to frighten the staff. A medical cabinet listed the proper dose of emergency medicine; above it, a drip of salt water gathered beneath the ceiling. Nina did not envy Fang's confidence. She wanted to explore the closed corridor and test the instruments separately. Outside the thick window, the sea was calm, which made the warning about extreme pressure seem strange. Tao reminded her that calm water at the surface said little about the force against glass below it.

站長方主任把救援申請稱為殘酷的恐嚇，說它只會讓員工害怕。醫療櫃標著緊急藥物的適當劑量；上方的天花板下方卻凝著一滴鹹水。戴寧不羨慕方主任的自信。她想分開探索封閉走廊，逐一測試儀器。厚玻璃外的海面看起來平靜，讓極端水壓的警告顯得奇怪。阿卓提醒她，水面平靜並不代表水下玻璃承受的力量很小。

Nina refused to gamble with the crew's safety. She took a quick glance at a supply cupboard where someone had stored hay for an old children's sea-farm display. Beside it hung a bag of laundry, still damp from the evacuation two weeks earlier. The objects looked ordinary, but their position mattered: the cupboard blocked a narrow inspection hatch. Tao said the display had been moved in a hurry, after the building was declared safe. Nina photographed the hatch and asked who had ordered the move. Tao could not remember a name.

戴寧拒絕拿工作人員的安全賭一把。她匆匆瞥向補給櫃，看見裡面存著一把乾草，是舊兒童海岸農場展示用的。旁邊掛著一袋仍有些潮濕的待洗衣物，據說是兩週前撤離時留下的。這些物品看似普通，擺放的位置卻很重要：櫃子擋住了一扇狹窄的檢查孔。阿卓說，建築被宣布安全後，展品曾被匆忙搬動。戴寧拍下檢查孔，追問是誰下令搬的；阿卓想不起名字。

A sincere night guard finally described what he had heard. He had been sitting on a stool near an empty tobacco tin when three blows sounded from the sea wall. His twin brother had once worked as a diver there, so he knew how a metal tool could sound underwater. Yet the last blow was followed by a whistle from an unused speaker. The guard had reported the whistle, not the knocks, because he feared being laughed at. Nina thanked him for telling the difference. One small distinction could matter more than a dramatic story.

一位坦誠的夜班警衛終於描述了自己聽見的聲音。當時他坐在矮凳上，身邊有個空菸草罐，海牆忽然傳來三次敲擊。他的雙胞胎兄弟曾在此當潛水員，因此他知道金屬工具在水下會發出什麼聲音。然而最後一下敲擊之後，還傳來停用喇叭發出的哨聲。警衛怕被嘲笑，所以只報告哨聲，沒提敲擊。戴寧謝謝他說清楚差別。小小的區別，有時比戲劇化的故事更重要。

At the corridor entrance, three rules had been printed on a steel plate. First, when three knocks sound beyond the main glass, do not knock back; read the pressure gauge and report the time. Second, when the corridor lamps turn green, stay above the white floor line until the air fan has reset. Third, at low tide, the west relief valve must be opened and checked before the lower bridge is crossed. A handwritten addition said, “No exception for a familiar voice.” Nina copied every word. None of the rules told people that a ghost lived below the sea.

走廊入口的鋼板上印著三條規則。第一，主玻璃外傳來三聲敲擊時，不要回敲；先讀壓力錶，再報告時間。第二，走廊燈轉綠時，待在白色地線以上，直到送風機重啟。第三，低潮時通過下層橋道前，必須先開啟並確認西側減壓閥。有人用手寫補上一句：「即使聽見熟悉的聲音，也不例外。」戴寧把每個字抄下來。規則裡沒有一條說海底住著鬼。

Jae, the independent rescue diver, arrived with a dry bag of instruments. Nina explained that she would not enter the lower level until they understood the pressure history. Jae wanted to search for Leya immediately, but accepted the limit after seeing a crack in an old bolt. They agreed on a first task: compare the wall clock, pump clock, and handwritten guard log. The three devices could disagree without anyone lying. If someone had changed a record, however, the disagreement might reveal what had been changed and when.

獨立救援潛水員佳恩帶著裝有儀器的防水袋抵達。戴寧說，在弄清楚壓力歷史之前，她不會進入下層。佳恩想立刻尋找樂雅，但看見舊螺栓上的裂痕後，接受了這項限制。兩人同意先比較牆上時鐘、泵浦時鐘及警衛手寫日誌。三種裝置即使不同步，也不表示有人說謊；但若有人改動紀錄，差異也可能揭示改動的內容與時間。

Just before closing, the sound came again: one knock, a pause, and two more. Nobody moved toward the glass. Nina read the gauge aloud while Jae filmed its needle. The pressure rose for several seconds, then returned to its earlier level. The guard's face turned pale, but his hands remained away from the wall. Nina sealed the recordings in separate envelopes and asked Tao to sign the time sheet. Leya was still missing. Yet for the first time, the frightened team had a measurement instead of another frightening rumor.

臨近關閉時，那個聲音再次出現：一下敲擊，停頓，接著兩下。沒有人走近玻璃。戴寧讀出壓力錶數值，佳恩拍攝指針。壓力上升數秒，隨後回到原來的水平。警衛臉色發白，雙手仍遠離海牆。戴寧把錄音分開封入信封，請阿卓在時間表簽名。樂雅依然失蹤，但這支受驚的團隊第一次擁有了測量數據，而不只是另一則令人害怕的傳言。
