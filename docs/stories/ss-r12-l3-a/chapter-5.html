藏著節奏的泵浦

A Pump with a Secret Beat

玻璃海牆的第三聲 · 第 5 / 25 章 · 2000–3000 · 英中對照 · 校訂中

用互動版查詞、朗讀與收藏

An athlete from the rescue team arrived to carry equipment, but Nina explained that speed would not benefit them unless the system was understood. Jae had trained for a diving career and knew how an injured person's chest could be affected by sudden pressure changes. The first clue came from a paper chart found behind the pump panel. Someone had tried to drag the chart out and erase the midnight entries. Nina was eager to examine the paper beneath a side light. Its faint marks remained visible even where the ink had been rubbed away.

救援隊派來一位體能出色的隊員搬運裝備，但戴寧解釋，在弄清系統之前，速度再快也沒有好處。佳恩接受過潛水職業訓練，知道突然的壓力變化會如何影響傷者胸腔。第一項線索是一張藏在泵浦面板後的紙本圖表。有人試著把圖拉出來，擦除午夜的紀錄。戴寧急著在側光下檢查紙面，即使墨水被擦掉，淡淡的壓痕仍然可見。

The display area smelled of flour from a canceled cooking class, but the nearby harbor pump carried a horrible metallic rattle. A child's costume of a knight hung behind a locked door, with a medal pinned to its sleeve. These harmless decorations gave Nina an idea: the lock's metal plate could act as a simple sound marker. She asked a technician to place a measuring device on the frame without striking the main glass. The rough vibration that came back matched the shortest part of the knock sequence.

展示區還飄著取消的烹飪課留下的麵粉氣味，但附近的港口泵浦發出可怕的金屬震動聲。鎖住的門後掛著一套兒童騎士裝，袖子別著獎章。這些無害的裝飾讓戴寧想到，門鎖金屬片可以當作簡單的聲音標記。她請技師把測量裝置裝到框架上，不碰主玻璃。傳回的粗糙震動，與敲擊序列最短的部分吻合。

The walkway had been scheduled for pavement work, but the repair card described only a surface inspection, not a completed repair. A scientific report from a former trader in coastal equipment described how a nearby fishing tribe measured tide by sound. Nina did not treat the story as an engineering manual, but one passage mentioned changes in water volume beside an intake pipe. She compared it with the pump chart. If changing volume moved a loose steel arm, the arm might strike the frame three times in one cycle.

步道原本排定鋪面工程，但維修卡只記錄了表面檢查，沒有證明修理完成。另一份科學報告出自一位做過海岸設備買賣的商人，記錄附近漁業部落如何以聲音判斷潮汐。戴寧沒有把民俗故事當工程手冊，但其中一段提到進水管旁水量的變化。她拿來對照泵浦圖表：若水量改變讓鬆動的鋼臂移動，它便可能在一次週期敲擊框架三下。

They requested a controlled test at a safe operating level. The supervisor agreed only after confirming that the sea wall was not under unusual pressure. With the rescue office watching, the pump started and a loose arm moved against a protected frame. One knock came first, then two close together. Nina compared the exact seconds with the recorded pattern. This time both timing and vibration were close. She called it a strong mechanical explanation, while stressing that the damaged arm still needed direct inspection before anyone entered the lower corridor.

他們申請在安全運轉水平下做受控測試。主管確認海牆並未承受異常壓力後才同意。救援辦公室在旁監看，泵浦啟動，一支鬆動鋼臂碰到有保護的框架。先傳來一下，接著是靠得很近的兩下。戴寧把精確秒數與錄音模式比較，這次時間與震動都相當接近。她稱之為強而有力的機械解釋，同時強調仍須直接檢查損壞鋼臂，才能讓人進入下層走廊。

The team cheered for a moment, then returned to work. Discovering the source of the knocks did not find Leya. It did, however, make the first rule understandable: striking the damaged wall in response might confuse the sensor and disturb a weak frame. Tao admitted that he had knocked back on two previous nights because he thought someone was calling for help. Nina did not shame him. She recorded the action and asked the safety office to check whether either response appeared in the older pressure history.

團隊短暫歡呼，隨即回到工作。找到敲擊來源並不代表找到樂雅，但它讓第一條規則有了合理解釋：回敲受損海牆，可能干擾感測器，也可能擾動脆弱的框架。阿卓承認，他前兩夜曾回敲，以為有人在求救。戴寧沒有責怪他，而是記下行為，請安全辦公室檢查那些回應是否留在舊壓力紀錄中。

Nina asked Fang why the pump had remained in service after several complaints. Fang replied that the maintenance budget had been cut, and that no sensor had officially failed. Jae pointed out that a machine need not stop working to become dangerous. A loose arm could keep turning until one day it hit the wrong part. The old report had listed a replacement but no signed completion date. That blank line now mattered more than the center's claim that every routine inspection had been done.

戴寧問方主任，為何泵浦收到多次抱怨後仍繼續使用。方主任說維修預算被刪減，且沒有感測器被正式判定失效。佳恩指出，機器不必完全停擺才會危險。鬆動的鋼臂可以一直轉，直到有一天撞上不該撞的部位。舊報告列了更換計畫，卻沒有簽名完成日期。這道空白如今比中心宣稱每次例行檢查都完成還重要。

At the fifth-day meeting, Nina presented three findings: the frightening voice came from a reused training file, the missing space existed between two glass layers, and the knocks matched a loose pump arm. She listed three unanswered questions beside them: who reconnected the voice, who removed the inspection space from the public map, and where Leya had gone. The team agreed that solving one fear should not make them careless about the others. The next stage would focus on the green lamps and ventilation system.

第五天的會議上，戴寧提出三項發現：恐怖人聲來自被重用的訓練檔案；兩層玻璃之間確實存在圖面被省略的空間；三聲敲擊與鬆動泵浦鋼臂吻合。旁邊還列著三個未解問題：誰重新接上人聲？誰從公開圖面刪除檢查空間？樂雅去了哪裡？團隊同意，解開一種恐懼不代表可以忽略其他危險。下一階段將集中處理綠燈與通風系統。
