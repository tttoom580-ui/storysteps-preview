海灣底下的閥門

The Valve Beneath the Bay

玻璃海牆的第三聲 · 第 10 / 25 章 · 2000–3000 · 英中對照 · 校訂中

用互動版查詞、朗讀與收藏

A banker representing the center's insurance office arrived at the bay with a portable light beam. The former rescue champion Jae used it to examine a narrow access slot. Nina praised the creative way he kept equipment clear of the pressure boundary. On the new map, the water level seemed to decrease toward a blank wall; on the old map, a service corridor continued behind it. Their voices produced a short echo. Nina felt fearful for Leya, but she would not let fear replace the need for a measured route. A small mob of curious spectators had gathered at the fence, but the guard kept them away from the closed route. Nina chose to postpone any walk across the bridge until the real pressure readings had been checked.

代表中心保險單位的銀行人員來到海灣，帶著一支能投出集中光束的手電筒。曾獲救援比賽冠軍的佳恩，用它查看狹窄的檢查槽。戴寧稱讚他用創意方式讓設備遠離壓力邊界。新圖上，水位似乎朝空白牆面下降；舊圖上，維修走廊卻從那裡繼續延伸。說話聲產生短短的回音。戴寧擔心樂雅，卻不願讓害怕取代測量路線的必要性。 一小群好奇圍觀者聚集在圍欄外，但警衛讓他們遠離封閉路線。戴寧決定延後所有跨橋行動，直到真正的壓力讀數完成查核。

The site's historic visitor archive had a brass holder for maps. Behind it they found a stain from a slow leak, which a former crowd of tourists had never seen. The building's normal tour route ran along a decorated palace model. Nina paid a penny at the archive copier rather than removing the only old map. Fang requested a postponement of all lower-level tests, but the rescue officer permitted a limited survey from the safe side. A sealed receiver on the wall showed that the west valve's position sensor might still work.

這座有歷史的觀測站遊客檔案室有一個黃銅地圖架。架後有緩慢滲漏留下的痕跡，昔日成群遊客從未看見。一般參觀路線沿著裝飾華麗的宮殿模型前進。戴寧在檔案影印機投了一枚小錢幣，沒有把唯一的舊地圖帶走。方主任要求延後所有下層測試，但救援官員允許從安全一側做有限勘查。牆上一個密封接收器顯示西側閥門的位置感測器可能仍能運作。

A scholarship student who once worked here had warned that someone might steal the cable during a renovation. Nina found a damaged zipper on an old tool bag beside the receiver. The bag contained a diagram showing a west relief valve and a pressure gate beyond it. Its handwritten note said the valve should be checked at low tide, not at any convenient hour. Jae compared the note with the tide tables and the third rule. All three sources gave the same warning, so they planned a remote valve test for the next low tide.

一位曾在這裡工作的獎學金學生警告過，整修期間有人可能偷走電纜。戴寧在接收器旁一個舊工具袋上看見壞掉的拉鍊。袋裡有張圖，畫出西側減壓閥與後方的壓力閘門。手寫註記說應在低潮確認閥門，而不是隨時想試就試。佳恩把註記與潮汐表及第三條規則比對，三份來源都發出相同警告，因此團隊規劃下次低潮時遠端測試閥門。

The rescue office explained the test procedure in clear steps. A sensor would report whether the valve changed position; a second gauge would show whether pressure actually dropped. They would not cross the lower bridge merely because a light turned green or a motor made a sound. Nina asked everyone to repeat the conditions before the test. A mechanic said he was glad someone had finally separated “valve open” from “safe to enter.” Those phrases had been treated as the same thing in the newer staff guide.

救援辦公室以清楚步驟解釋測試程序。一個感測器會回報閥門是否改變位置，第二個壓力錶則顯示壓力是否真的下降。團隊不會因為燈變綠或馬達發聲，就直接穿過下層橋道。戴寧要求每個人在測試前複述條件。一名機械師說，他很高興終於有人把「閥門打開」與「可以安全進入」區分開來。新版員工手冊一直把兩句話當成同一件事。

At low tide, Jae watched the remote screen as the valve command was sent. Its position light changed, but the pressure gauge barely moved. The valve could turn without releasing enough pressure. They stopped the test and kept the bridge closed. Nina compared the result with the old repair slip, which warned about a narrow blocked outlet. The slip had been labeled completed despite lacking a measurement. Once again, a checked box had been mistaken for a checked physical condition.

低潮時，佳恩看著遠端螢幕，團隊送出開閥指令。位置燈改變了，壓力錶卻幾乎不動。閥門可能轉動，卻沒有釋放足夠壓力。他們停止測試並維持橋道封閉。戴寧拿結果與舊維修單比較，上面警告出口狹窄且可能堵塞。工單雖沒有測量結果，卻被標成完成。再次有人把勾選過的方格誤認為檢查過的實體狀況。

The first ten days had changed the investigation. The knocks were mechanical, the ghost voice was a recording, and the green lamps warned of a ventilation interruption. The west valve existed, yet it did not reduce pressure as the manual promised. Each discovery came with a limit that the team wrote openly beside it. Leya was not rescued, but the path to a possible hiding place was clearer. Nina asked the coast authority to send an independent engineering crew before anyone attempted the bridge.

前十天改變了調查方向。敲擊是機械震動，鬼聲是錄音，綠燈提醒通風中斷。西側閥門確實存在，卻沒有像手冊說的那樣降壓。每項發現都有其限制，團隊公開寫在旁邊。樂雅還沒獲救，但通往可能藏身位置的路線逐漸清楚。戴寧請海岸主管機關派獨立工程隊來，在有人嘗試通過橋道前先行檢查。

Nina called Leya's mother and gave a careful report. “We have found an old route and confirmed a dangerous valve problem,” she said. “We have not proved where Leya is.” Her mother thanked her for avoiding promises she could not keep. Nina ended the call, looked at the tide chart, and marked the next safe testing window. To find a person alive, the team needed patience that had a purpose, not patience that excused doing nothing.

戴寧打電話給樂雅母親，謹慎報告：「我們找到一條舊路，也確認閥門有危險問題，但尚未證明樂雅在哪裡。」母親謝謝她不作無法兌現的承諾。戴寧結束通話，望著潮汐圖，標出下一個安全測試時段。要活著找到一個人，團隊需要有目的的耐心，而不是拿耐心當作什麼都不做的藉口。
