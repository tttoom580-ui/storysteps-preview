封條底下的簽名

The Signature Under the Seal

玻璃海牆的第三聲 · 第 6 / 25 章 · 2000–3000 · 英中對照 · 校訂中

用互動版查詞、朗讀與收藏

Fang said the team had already achieved enough and should leave the lower rooms alone. At a staff meeting, someone served a weak fruit cocktail while the director described a desirable reopening schedule. Nina found his certainty doubtful. A maintenance order warned that workers must not dump used pump fluid into the sea, but the completion box had been filled with a rubber stamp. The paper carried a date from the night Leya disappeared. Nina asked who had held the original document and whether anyone had seen it before the stamp was applied. She hoped to achieve a reliable repair record, not portray a frightened colleague as a devil when a paper trail was still incomplete.

方主任說團隊已取得足夠成果，應該別再碰下層房間。員工會議上，有人端來清淡的水果調飲，主任則說明理想的重新開放時程。戴寧對他的篤定感到懷疑。一張維修單警告員工不得把用過的泵浦液體倒進海裡，完成欄卻只蓋了一個橡皮章。日期正好是樂雅失蹤當夜。戴寧追問原件曾由誰保管，以及有沒有人在蓋章前看過它。 她希望達成可靠的維修紀錄，而不是在文件線索尚不完整時，把害怕的同事描繪成惡魔。

An archived letter from the governor's office described the center as a small inn for visiting researchers, although no one now slept there. A jar of jelly and a note from an old pal of Tao's lay beside a pile of discarded signs. Nina used a wooden pole to point at the labels from a safe distance. The rank of each person on the sign-off list was clear, yet the supposed checker had been abroad that week. Fang dismissed the problem as a clerical error. Nina asked for travel records instead of an argument.

州長辦公室的一封舊信把中心形容成來訪研究者的小旅舍，雖然現在沒有人在此過夜。阿卓一位老朋友留下的果醬和便條，躺在一堆丟棄的告示旁。戴寧用木桿在安全距離外指出標籤。簽核名單上的職級很清楚，據稱負責檢查的人卻在那一週出國。方主任說只是文書錯誤。戴寧沒有爭吵，而是要求查看出入境紀錄。

The laboratory kept a razor for opening sample packages, a reliable hand pump, and a map of the suburb above the bay. Its new meter was advertised as superior to the older one, but no independent test report could be found. A faint trail of dried salt crossed the cabinet where the maintenance orders were stored. Nina photographed it, then asked the technician whether the cabinet had been wet. He said water entered during a storm. The dates did not agree: the storm came three days after the papers were signed.

實驗室裡有用於拆樣品包裝的剃刀、一台可靠的手動泵，以及海灣上方郊區的地圖。新儀表被宣稱比舊款優越，卻找不到獨立測試報告。存放維修單的櫃子外有一條淡淡的乾鹽痕跡。戴寧拍照後問技師櫃子是否進水。他說是暴風雨造成的，但日期對不上：暴風雨發生在文件簽名的三天後。

A wall poster praised unity between different departments and showed two workers who had wed during the center's first year. Nina smiled at the old photograph but returned to the order sheet. Personal stories could make a place feel trustworthy; they could not replace technical evidence. She asked the city archive for the original stamp record and requested that any inspection be witnessed by someone outside Fang's office. Jae agreed. Without independent witnesses, even an honest repair might later become impossible to prove.

牆上的海報讚美各部門的團結，還有兩位在中心創辦第一年結婚的員工合照。戴寧對舊照片笑了笑，隨即回到維修單。私人故事能讓地方顯得值得信任，卻無法取代技術證據。她向市府檔案室索取印章原始使用紀錄，也要求未來檢查有方主任辦公室以外的人在場見證。佳恩表示同意。沒有獨立見證，即使是真實的修理，日後也可能無法證明。

Tao led them to a cabinet of old paper shift cards. One card listed the pump as stopped, although the pressure chart showed a full cycle at that hour. Nina kept both documents side by side rather than deciding which was wrong. If the chart was correct, the stop record had been altered. If the card was correct, the clock or chart required investigation. A third source was needed. The rescue crew took a copy of the power-meter history, which had been stored by a separate contractor.

阿卓帶他們到存放舊紙本輪班卡的櫃子。一張卡說那個時間泵浦停止運作，壓力圖卻顯示完整週期。戴寧把兩份文件並排保存，沒有立刻判定哪份錯誤。若壓力圖正確，停止紀錄被改動；若輪班卡正確，則要查時鐘或圖表。仍需要第三份來源。救援隊調出由另一家承包商保存的電表歷史副本。

At night, the power record arrived. It showed a sudden rise when the paper card claimed the pump was silent. The separate meter made a deliberate change more likely, although no one could yet name the person responsible. Nina wrote a preservation request covering the stamps, digital accounts, and original paper. Fang complained that she was turning a repair into an accusation. She replied that an accusation would name a person; her request named records that must not disappear.

夜裡，電表紀錄送到了。紙卡聲稱泵浦安靜的時間，電力使用量卻突然升高。這份獨立紀錄讓刻意改動的可能性增加，但還不能指認誰負責。戴寧提出保存要求，涵蓋印章、電子帳號和紙本原件。方主任抱怨她把維修變成指控。她回答，指控會點名某個人；她的要求只列出不能消失的紀錄。

The unanswered question was no longer whether the center contained strange sounds. The team could describe two ordinary machines and the times they operated. What mattered now was why approved records did not match those machines. Nina placed Leya's missing-person notice above the documents, a reminder that the investigation served a human being, not a competition between engineers. She asked Jae to prepare a safe route for testing the green warning lamps in the morning.

未解的問題已不再是中心為何有奇怪聲音。團隊能描述兩台普通機器及其運作時段。如今重要的是核准紀錄為何與機器不符。戴寧把樂雅的失蹤公告放在文件上方，提醒眾人，調查是為了人，不是工程師之間的競賽。她請佳恩準備隔天測試綠色警示燈的安全路線。
