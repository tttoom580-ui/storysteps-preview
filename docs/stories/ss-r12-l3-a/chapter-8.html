留在箱裡的抱怨

The Complaint That Stayed in the Box

玻璃海牆的第三聲 · 第 8 / 25 章 · 2000–3000 · 英中對照 · 校訂中

用互動版查詞、朗讀與收藏

Nina woke with an ache in her neck after a short night. The records room held a tall bookcase full of closed files, including a complaint signed by Leya two days before she vanished. Leya wrote that she had remained conscious during the green-lamp event and had watched a worker receive a discount on replacement parts. Nina felt tempted to make a hasty accusation, but one payment record could not establish a motive. She asked the clerk to inform the independent auditors that the complaint needed protection.

經過一個短夜，戴寧醒來時脖子隱隱作痛。紀錄室的高書櫃塞滿封存檔案，其中有一份樂雅在失蹤前兩天簽名的抱怨書。樂雅寫道，綠燈事件中她一直保持清醒，還看見一名工人購買替換零件時取得折扣。戴寧很想立刻提出指控，但單一付款紀錄無法證明動機。她請文書通知獨立稽核員，這份抱怨需要妥善保存。

The education wing offered quiet leisure activities, and its floor map could help them locate the old files. A painted monster on the wall pointed toward a room that staff had tried to organize after the last school visit. A portrait of the center's founder hung above a poster promising to release safety reports every year. Nina looked for the latest release and found only the cover sheet. A rocket model, a paper swan, and a box tied with tight string had been placed on top of the missing-report shelf. A grasshopper drawing from the children's collection had also been left on the shelf; she kept it with the other unrelated educational props.

教育區提供安靜的休閒活動，地板圖可以幫助他們找到舊檔案。牆上一隻畫出來的怪獸指向一間教室，員工曾在最後一場學校參訪後試著整理那裡。創辦人的肖像掛在一張海報上方，海報承諾每年公布安全報告。戴寧尋找最新版，卻只找到封面。一個火箭模型、一隻紙天鵝，以及一個以繩子綁緊的盒子，被放在缺少報告的書架上。 兒童收藏中的一張蚱蜢畫也留在架上；她把畫與其他無關的教學道具放在一起。

The box contained a unique set of handwritten repair slips. Some mentioned violence during a past labor dispute, but none proved that anyone had attacked Leya. Nina paid attention to the dates instead. The slips documented three separate ventilation failures, each followed by a note saying the warning light had returned to white. The system had restarted, but no document said the cause had been removed. That distinction explained how an office could report a completed incident while leaving the real problem for the next worker.

盒子裡是一套獨特的手寫維修單。有些提到過去勞資爭議中的暴力事件，卻沒有任何一張能證明有人攻擊樂雅。戴寧把注意力放在日期上。工單記錄了三次各自獨立的通風故障，每次後面都註明警示燈已回到白色。系統雖重啟，卻沒有文件說明原因已排除。這個差別解釋了為何辦公室能把事件列為結案，卻把真正問題留給下一位員工。

Jae proposed searching the lower classroom because Leya had signed out a measuring kit there. Nina agreed only to a remote check. They connected an inspection camera to a protected socket and guided it along the empty passage. The camera found a dropped pencil and a shoe mark near a sealed door. Neither item could identify a person without further evidence. Jae resisted the urge to rush in. He saved the video and marked the position on the older map.

佳恩提議搜索下層教室，因為樂雅曾在那裡借出測量工具。戴寧只同意遠端檢查。他們把檢查攝影機接上受保護的插座，沿空走廊前進。攝影機在封閉門旁找到一支掉落的鉛筆與一個鞋印。若沒有更多證據，兩件物品都無法證明身分。佳恩克制直接衝入的衝動，保存影片並把位置標在舊圖上。

That evening, Fang gave the team a list of people who had access to the records room. Nina compared it with the sign-in notebook and found one name missing from the list. The name belonged to a contractor, not a station employee. The omission might have been routine, but it narrowed the question of who could have moved the report. Instead of accusing the contractor, Nina requested the checkout record from the building's key supplier. It was another source outside Fang's direct control.

當晚，方主任給團隊一份能進入紀錄室的人員清單。戴寧與簽到簿比對，發現清單漏了一個名字。那人是承包商，不是站內員工。遺漏可能只是例行疏忽，卻縮小了「誰可能移動報告」的範圍。戴寧沒有指控承包商，而是向大樓鑰匙供應商索取領用紀錄。那又是一份不在方主任直接控制下的資料。

The night guard recognized Leya's handwriting on a folded warning note. It said, “The dry room has a number, even if the public map does not.” He remembered that she had asked about the old service lift. Nina did not tell the family that she had found Leya; she had not. But she could report a credible direction for the search. She asked the team to identify all dry spaces reachable from the missing inspection corridor without breaking the pressure boundary.

夜班警衛認出一張摺起來的警告紙是樂雅的字跡，上面寫著：「乾燥室有編號，即使公開地圖沒有。」他記得樂雅曾問起舊維修升降機。戴寧沒有告訴家人自己找到樂雅，因為事實還不是如此。但她能報告一個可信的搜索方向。她請團隊列出所有能從被省略的檢查走廊抵達、又不需破壞壓力邊界的乾燥空間。

Before bed, Nina read the complaint again. Its last line was not a demand for revenge. Leya had written, “Please check the old room before someone else is hurt.” Nina put a copy of that sentence beside the search map. She wanted the next morning's work to answer Leya's warning rather than turn her into a symbol for a public argument. At last the scattered records had begun to point toward a physical place.

睡前，戴寧再讀一次抱怨書。最後一句不是要求報復，而是樂雅寫的：「請在其他人受傷前檢查舊房間。」戴寧把這句話的副本放在搜索地圖旁。她希望隔天的工作能回應樂雅的警告，而不是把她變成公共爭論的符號。散落的紀錄終於開始指向一個具體位置。
