綠燈亮起時要留下

When Green Means Stay

玻璃海牆的第三聲 · 第 7 / 25 章 · 2000–3000 · 英中對照 · 校訂中

用互動版查詞、朗讀與收藏

An automatic timer controlled the old air fans, but its display was hidden behind a model automobile. Workers had used the hall to clothe rescue dolls for an education fair. A committee record said no visitor should enter the lower room during a ventilation test, because someone might drown if rising water blocked the exit. Near the entrance, a dumpling container sat under a dripping faucet. Nina asked the cleaning worker to move it; food and water beside electrical controls could confuse an already difficult inspection.

舊送風機由自動計時器控制，螢幕卻被一輛模型汽車擋住。員工曾在這裡替救援教學人偶穿衣。一份委員會紀錄說，通風測試期間不得讓遊客進入下層，因為若上漲的海水堵住出口，可能有人溺水。入口附近，一盒水餃放在滴水的水龍頭下。戴寧請清潔員工移開；把食物與水放在電氣控制器旁，只會讓困難的檢查更混亂。

Beyond a fence, a flock of seabirds circled a bright window. Tao offered Nina a juicy orange while she watched lightning over the bay. She refused food until the test was finished; she needed her attention clear. A mental-health notice beside the control panel advised staff to stop work if a warning caused panic. Below it, an old microwave and a naked sensor wire shared the same shelf. Someone had left the wire unprotected. Nina marked that hazard and asked for a barrier before anyone touched the panel.

圍欄外，一群海鳥在亮著光的窗外盤旋。阿卓遞給戴寧一顆多汁的柳橙，她正看著海灣上方的閃電。她表示測完再吃，現在需要專心。控制盤旁的心理健康告示提醒員工，若警報引起恐慌就應停止工作。告示下方，一台舊微波爐和裸露的感測器電線放在同一層架上。有人沒有保護好電線。戴寧標記危險，要求先設置隔離物，再讓人接觸控制盤。

A bird's nest had been found near the outside intake, and the service note warned that dust could pollute the filters. Yet the newer report contained no record of filter protection. An old shovel lay on a cart under a poster titled “The Sin of Waste.” In the staff refrigerator, a broken egg had left its yolk on the shelf. Nina was irritated by the disorder, but she knew a messy room was not proof of a dangerous air system. She requested the filter readings, which would answer a more useful question.

外側進氣口旁曾發現鳥巢，維修單警告灰塵可能污染濾網。新版報告卻沒有濾網保護的紀錄。一把舊鏟子放在標題為「浪費之罪」的海報下方推車上。員工冰箱裡還有一顆破蛋，蛋黃流在架上。戴寧被混亂弄得心煩，但她知道雜亂房間不等於空氣系統危險。她要求讀取濾網數據，好回答更有用的問題。

During the controlled test the corridor lamps turned green, just as the second rule predicted. The group stayed above the white floor line. A fan slowed, then stopped, and a fine smell like cleaning liquid reached them from a vent. Jae took a sample from the safe side of the barrier. After two minutes the backup fan started and the green lamps returned to white. The sequence showed that green was a warning state rather than an invitation to enter, despite the usual meaning of that color.

受控測試期間，走廊燈果然像第二條規則所說轉成綠色。大家待在白色地線上方。一台送風機減速後停止，通風口飄來淡淡的清潔液氣味。佳恩在隔離線安全側採集樣本。兩分鐘後，備用送風機啟動，綠燈回到白色。這個順序顯示綠色代表警告，而不是一般人以為的「可以通行」。

Fang said the old lights had always used green for a pause. Nina asked where that meaning was explained on the public signs. There was no answer. She also asked why the fan stopped. Tao produced an earlier work order mentioning salt crystals inside a relay. If the relay had not been replaced, the warning could still be valid. Jae refused to enter the lower corridor until the air sample was reviewed. Nina accepted his decision without trying to turn courage into pressure.

方主任說舊系統一直用綠燈表示暫停。戴寧問公開告示哪裡有解釋這個意思，沒有人能回答。她又問風機為何停止。阿卓拿出一張早期工單，提到繼電器內有鹽結晶。如果繼電器沒有更換，警告仍可能有效。佳恩拒絕在空氣樣本完成檢查前進入下層，戴寧接受他的決定，沒有用「勇敢」逼他冒險。

The technician pointed out that the repair date on the relay case was newer than the date in the computerized work history. They photographed the difference from a safe platform. Nina requested the contractor's purchase invoice, not just an explanation from Fang. If a replacement relay had been purchased, there should be a trace outside the director's office. If none existed, they would need to examine why the case carried a fresh label. The team had learned to ask questions that left evidence behind.

技師指出，繼電器外殼上的維修日期比電腦工作紀錄還新。他們從安全平台拍下差異。戴寧要求承包商的採購發票，而不只是方主任的說法。如果真的買過新繼電器，主任辦公室之外應該還留有痕跡；如果沒有，就必須查外殼為何貼著新標籤。團隊開始學會提出能留下證據的問題。

Before nightfall, the sample report showed a small but real increase in irritating material during the fan stop. It was below an immediate emergency limit, yet enough to justify the floor-line rule. Nina placed an updated notice at the entrance, explaining the lamps in plain language. She then asked the rescue office for a plan to maintain breathable air during any later search. Saving Leya required more than finding a door; it required keeping the people behind and in front of that door safe.

傍晚前，樣本報告顯示風機停止時，刺激性物質有輕微但真實的上升。數值低於立即危險的限制，卻足以支持地線規則。戴寧在入口放上更新告示，以白話解釋燈號。她接著請救援辦公室規劃日後搜尋時如何維持可呼吸空氣。救出樂雅不只要找到門，還得保護門裡和門外的人。
