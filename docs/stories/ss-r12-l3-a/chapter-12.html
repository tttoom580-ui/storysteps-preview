地下室裡的字母

Letters in the Basement

玻璃海牆的第三聲 · 第 12 / 25 章 · 2000–3000 · 英中對照 · 校訂中

用互動版查詞、朗讀與收藏

The old training room had an alphabet painted above a basement stairwell. A forgotten cigarette packet and a metal clip lay beside the door. Tao showed Nina a paper drawing of a farm cock, once used to teach children about the coast's morning sounds. The classroom's collection was strange for an underwater center, but it explained why many old props still crowded the storage shelves. Outside, foggy weather kept rescue boats close to shore. Nina asked the office to hire a certified electrician before opening another panel.

舊訓練室的地下樓梯上方畫著字母表。門邊放著一包被遺忘的香菸和一枚金屬夾。阿卓給戴寧看一張農場公雞的畫，那是以前教孩子認識海岸晨間聲音的教材。水下中心收藏這些東西很奇怪，卻也說明舊道具為何仍擠滿倉庫架。外面霧氣濃厚，救援艇只能靠近岸邊。戴寧要求辦公室聘請合格電工，再開啟其他面板。

In a binder marked honesty, Nina found a kindergarten lesson on careful learning. Children had been told that liberty meant being free to ask a question and receive a meaningful answer. Between the pages was white powder from an old craft activity. The previous inspection report had called the lower room “empty,” but supplied no photograph. Jae said the word queer was written in the margin beside that claim, meaning odd rather than describing a person. They agreed to reveal the note only with its context, so nobody would misunderstand it. Nina asked them to preserve the page numbering, because a missing page could hide the very date they needed to check.

在標著「誠實」的資料夾裡，戴寧找到一堂幼兒園的細心學習課。教材告訴孩子，自由意味著能提問並獲得有意義的答覆。頁間有舊手作活動留下的白色粉末。前一份檢查報告把下層房間稱為「空的」，卻沒有附照片。佳恩指出有人在旁邊寫了 queer，意思是「古怪」，並非指某個人的身分。兩人同意，公開這項註記時必須保留語境，以免誤解。 戴寧請大家保留原本頁碼，因為少了一頁，就可能藏住他們需要查核的日期。

A small bottle of shampoo had leaked onto a shelf where children once learned to weave paper strips. Nina cleaned only the outside of the plastic case, protecting the documents inside. She thought of the wisdom in Leya's note: check the old room before others are hurt. The paper list included a service telephone number that no longer appeared on the new map. Jae dialed it from an isolated line. Instead of a voice, the receiver gave three short tones and a low fan noise.

一小瓶洗髮精漏在曾教孩子編織紙條的架上。戴寧只清潔塑膠盒外側，保護裡面的文件。她想起樂雅筆記裡的智慧：在其他人受傷前檢查舊房間。紙本清單有一支新版地圖已沒有的維修電話。佳恩用隔離線路撥打，接收器沒有傳出人聲，卻有三個短音及低沉風扇聲。

The three tones were not the same as the sea-wall knocks. They occurred at equal intervals and sounded electronic. Nina compared them with the service telephone manual. It identified the pattern as a request for manual confirmation that the dry chamber was receiving air. The system expected a worker to check a separate indicator before answering. The rule against responding to a familiar voice applied to the public speaker, but this line was a different system with its own printed procedure. Nina refused to confuse them.

這三個音與海牆敲擊不同。它們間距相等，聽起來像電子音。戴寧拿來與維修電話手冊比對，確認那是乾燥艙室送風狀態需要人工確認的訊號。系統要求工作人員先看另一個指示器，才能應答。不要回答熟悉聲音的規定是針對公共喇叭；這條線路是另一套有印刷程序的系統。戴寧拒絕混淆它們。

The indicator could not be seen from the upper room. However, the manual described a safe remote test port. An engineer checked the port for damage, attached a measuring lead, and confirmed a small regular current. That suggested the chamber's air fan still had power. It did not prove that the air was breathable or that anyone was inside. Nina wrote both statements on the board. She was learning that good news often came with a sentence explaining what it could not tell them.

上層房間看不到那個指示器，但手冊描述了一個安全的遠端測試端口。工程師檢查端口是否損壞、接上測量線，確認有穩定的小電流。這暗示艙室送風機仍有電力，卻不證明空氣可呼吸，也不證明裡面有人。戴寧把兩種說法都寫上白板。她明白，好消息常常需要附上一句：它還不能告訴我們什麼。

Fang offered to open the pressure door with a master key. Jae refused because the pressure across it remained unequal. The director looked offended, but a closed door was less dangerous than a sudden flow of water. Nina asked the engineer to prepare a camera and air sampler for the remote port. Fang signed the request after a rescue officer explained the risk in simple terms. Nobody had to win an argument; they had to protect a person they could not yet see.

方主任提出用萬用鑰匙打開承壓門，佳恩拒絕了，因為門兩側壓力仍不相等。主任看起來不高興，但一扇關著的門，比突然湧入的水安全。戴寧請工程師為遠端端口準備攝影機與空氣採樣器。救援官員用淺白語言解釋風險後，方主任簽了申請。沒有人非得贏得爭論；真正的工作是保護尚未看見的人。

At the end of the day, the camera entered only a short distance. It showed a clean patch of floor beyond the sealed chamber, unlike the dusty passage outside. Nina could not know who had cleaned it or when. The image nevertheless justified looking more closely. She sent the picture to Leya's family with an explanation of its limits. Her mother replied with a short message: “Please keep asking the right questions.” Nina saved it beside the search record.

當天結束時，攝影機只伸入短短一段，卻拍到封閉艙室裡一塊乾淨的地面，和外面積灰的通道不同。戴寧不知道誰在何時清潔過，但影像值得繼續調查。她把照片連同限制說明傳給樂雅家人。母親回覆一句：「請繼續問對的問題。」戴寧把這句話保存在搜索紀錄旁。
