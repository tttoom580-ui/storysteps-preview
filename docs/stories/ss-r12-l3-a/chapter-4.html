不會呼吸的聲音

The Voice That Had No Breath

玻璃海牆的第三聲 · 第 4 / 25 章 · 2000–3000 · 英中對照

Nina's ambition was not to become famous for solving a mystery. She wanted workers to trust their equipment. Beyond the center, an old barn near the coast stored rescued supplies. Tao brought a dog collar from the barn because the tiny bell on it sounded like a tone in the recording. A concert poster on the wall confused Jae: its printed start time matched the timestamp of a supposed emergency call. Nina asked them not to mix unrelated details yet. Coincidence was possible, and every comparison needed its own test. The matching dates might confuse a visitor, but Nina would not call them evidence without knowing who printed each item.

戴寧的抱負不是靠解開謎案成名，而是讓工作人員能信任設備。中心外的海岸附近有座舊穀倉，存放撤離後的補給品。阿卓從那裡帶來一條狗項圈，因為上面的小鈴鐺聽起來像錄音裡的提示音。牆上的演唱會海報讓佳恩困惑：印刷的開始時間竟與某次所謂緊急通話的時間戳相同。戴寧請大家暫時別把無關細節混在一起。巧合可能存在，每項比較都需要自己的測試。 日期相同可能讓參觀者困惑，但戴寧不知道每件物品由誰印製，便不會把它們稱為證據。

The rehearsal room was dim, and a broken cooling unit made the air almost freeze near one wall. A technician joked with dry humor that the center needed a junior novelist rather than an engineer to explain its ghosts. Beneath a pile of onion skins from an old cooking demonstration lay a painter's signed invoice. It was a precious document, because the invoice listed the date when acoustic panels had been moved. Nina photographed it and asked for a formal response from the maintenance company rather than an informal promise.

排練室光線昏暗，一台故障的冷卻機讓某面牆附近冷得幾乎結冰。技師用冷幽默開玩笑，說中心需要的不是工程師，而是一位初出茅廬的小說家來解釋鬼魂。舊烹飪示範留下的一堆洋蔥皮下，壓著一張油漆師傅簽名的發票。這份文件很珍貴，因為它列出吸音板搬動的日期。戴寧拍照存證，並要求維修公司正式答覆，而不是口頭保證。

Outside the room, a roast-food cart from a past festival blocked the spray nozzle of a cleaning system. A squirrel had once appeared in a visitor photograph taped to the cart, though no squirrel belonged underwater. Nina gave the wall one gentle tap with a test tool while Jae measured the returning sound. This was not the main sea window, so the first rule did not forbid the action. A traveler watching from the hall said the tone sounded different from the frightening knocks. Nina wrote that observation down without calling it a final vision of the truth.

房外，一台舊節慶的烤食物餐車擋住清潔系統的噴嘴。車上貼著一張遊客照片，裡面甚至有隻松鼠，雖然水下根本不會有松鼠。戴寧在非主海窗的牆面，用測試工具輕敲一次，佳恩測量回傳的聲音。第一條規則禁止敲主玻璃，並不禁止這項測試。走廊上的一名旅客說，那音色與令人害怕的敲擊不同。戴寧記下觀察，但沒有把它當成真相的最終畫面。

Jae unplugged the public speaker under the supervisor's written permission. Then they played the training file through a separate portable speaker, set to a low and measured volume. The voice contained the exact same breathless pause and click that appeared on the guard's tape. There was no live person speaking those words on the emergency channel. Tao stared at the machine with relief and anger. It explained one part of the haunting, but not why someone had reconnected the speaker after its supposed removal.

佳恩取得主管書面許可後，拔掉公共喇叭的連線。接著他們把訓練檔案用另一台可攜式喇叭播放，音量調低並測量。檔案裡的無呼吸停頓與喀噠聲，和警衛錄音完全一致。當時緊急頻道裡並沒有真人說那些話。阿卓又鬆口氣又生氣地望著機器。這解釋了怪談的一部分，卻沒有說明一台據稱早已拆除的喇叭，為何被重新接上。

Fang said the voice was harmless and asked that the center's reputation be protected. Nina agreed that a recording alone was not a crime. But using it where staff expected a real emergency signal could make people take unsafe actions. She asked the office to keep the recording disabled until a full check was finished. Jae placed a signed tag on the disconnected cable. This decision was modest, reversible, and safer than declaring the entire building either haunted or completely safe.

方主任說那道聲音沒有危害，希望大家保護中心的名聲。戴寧同意，錄音本身不是犯罪。但如果把它放在員工期待真實緊急訊號的地方，可能導致不安全的行動。她要求辦公室在全面檢查前維持錄音停用。佳恩在拔除的線上貼了簽名封條。這個決定克制、可逆，也比直接宣布整棟建築鬧鬼或完全安全更有根據。

The next test used a sensor rather than a human ear. Nina recorded an ordinary pump cycle, then compared its frequency with the three knocks. Two peaks were similar, but their timing did not yet match. She would not call that proof. They needed to know when the pump was running on the night Leya disappeared. The schedule on Fang's screen was incomplete. The handwritten shift card might be more useful, but it was kept in the director's locked cabinet.

下一項測試用的是感測器，而不是人的耳朵。戴寧錄下正常泵浦週期，再把頻率與三聲敲擊比較。兩個波峰相近，時間卻尚未吻合，所以她不稱之為證明。他們需要知道樂雅失蹤當夜泵浦何時運作。方主任螢幕上的排程不完整，手寫輪班卡可能更有用，但它被收在主任上鎖的櫃子裡。

Before leaving, Nina saw a new mark on the pressure chart, made after the public speaker had been disconnected. This time the sea-wall knocks had occurred without any accompanying voice. The two sounds were independent. She circled their separate times and showed the result to Tao. He nodded slowly. They had been afraid of one mysterious event, when in fact they were facing at least two ordinary systems whose problems had become tangled together.

離開前，戴寧在壓力圖上看見新記號，時間是在公共喇叭斷線之後。這次海牆敲擊沒有伴隨任何人聲。兩種聲音彼此獨立。她圈出各自的時間，指給阿卓看。他緩緩點頭。大家原以為害怕的是一件神祕事件，實際上卻是至少兩套普通系統的問題糾纏在一起。
