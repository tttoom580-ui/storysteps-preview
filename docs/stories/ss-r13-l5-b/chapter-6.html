雪中的議會

A Council in the Snow

借火山河：冬路的兩個修士 · 第 6 / 25 章 · 4000–5000 · 英中對照 · 校訂中

The first repaired branch warmed the village school, but someone cut the outer pipe before dawn. A message on the wall accused the travelers of helping the guild. Yan followed the damage marks and found that the pipe had been struck with a farm axe, not the fine blade used by a guild inspector. He refused to blame the nearest angry farmer without proof.

第一條修復的支線讓村學恢復溫暖，但黎明前有人切斷外側管道。牆上的字條指控兩名旅人替公會工作。晏舟檢查傷痕，發現管線遭農斧砍擊，而非監察員慣用的細刃。他拒絕在沒有證據下指控附近最生氣的農人。

Cheng discovered that a neighboring town had been told the village was stealing its heat. Two communities had therefore been pushed into competing over the same limited current. He invited representatives from both towns to share a room rather than shout across the border. Yan placed the gauge between them and promised that its readings would belong to everyone present.

程觀發現鄰鎮被告知，燼谷正在偷走他們的熱量。兩個社區因而被迫爭奪同一股有限熱流。他邀兩鎮代表同坐一室，而不是隔著界線吼叫。晏舟將量表放在中央，承諾讀數屬於所有在場者。

The council's agenda began with a warning to beware of false rumors. A municipal nominee wanted to cling to the old charter, saying the federal court would protect it. A street actor used a popular genre of comic songs to turn the shortage into a metaphor about a winter game: when one team stole an inning, the other team never got to bat. The joke briefly overtook the angry speeches.

議會議程首先提醒大家提防謠言。一名市政候選人固守舊章程，稱聯邦法庭會保護它。一位街頭藝人用流行的喜劇曲風，把短缺比成冬季球賽：一隊偷走一局，另一隊就永遠沒有上場機會。笑話暫時蓋過憤怒演說。

They learned that the sabotaged pipe had been used for a secret nighttime delivery. The axe blow was clumsy, but the delivery seal on the cart was genuine. Cheng took a rubbing of the seal and sent a duplicate to the provincial school. For the first time, the men could compare a public distribution order with a hidden private shipment.

他們發現遭破壞的管道曾用來進行祕密夜間輸送。斧痕粗糙，車上的派送封印卻是真的。程觀拓印封印，再送一份副本去省學府。兩人第一次能對照公開分配命令與隱藏的私人貨運。

The joint meeting nearly failed when each town accused the other of taking an unfair share. Yan placed two identical cups beneath the demonstration pipe and let warm water enter one before the other. The second town's leader expected his own cup to remain empty. Instead the gauges showed that an unannounced private branch was consuming the missing flow. The revelation did not absolve either town of careless maintenance, but it proved the quarrel had been aimed at the wrong target. A boy asked whether a rumor could overtake a wagon. Cheng smiled sadly and said it already had. The leaders agreed to inspect the private branch together, and the farmer with the axe gave his name voluntarily, admitting he had damaged a pipe after believing the false message.

聯合會議差點失敗，因兩鎮互控搶走不公平的份額。晏舟把兩只相同杯子放到示範管下，讓溫水先後注入。第二鎮首領原以為自己那杯會空著，量表卻顯示有條未公告的私人支線吃掉了水流。發現不能抹去兩鎮各自的維護疏忽，卻證明雙方吵錯了對象。一名男孩問謠言能否超過貨車，程觀苦笑說早已超過。首領同意共同查暗管；拿斧子的農人也自願報名，承認自己相信假訊後曾砍壞管道。

A healer displayed the skeleton of a mountain fish, explaining how a single toxic spill could harm every organism downstream. Beside it lay a broken rib from an old miner and a jar of vinegar once used to clean copper. The residents chose a rigid safety rule: no group could use armed men to shield an unannounced shipment. One widow began to sob during a debate about theology and fate; the chairman reminded them that rebellion was not their only choice.

醫者展示山魚骨架，解釋一次有毒排放就可能傷害下游每種生物。旁邊有舊礦工的一根斷肋骨，以及曾用來清潔銅器的醋罐。居民訂下嚴格安全規則：任何團體不得用武裝人員掩護未公告的運送。一名寡婦在神學與命運的辯論中哭了起來；主席提醒眾人，反抗暴動不是唯一選擇。

They had different ways of dealing with fear. Yan wanted to put his hands on the damaged thing and discover whether it could still be repaired. Cheng wanted to find who had made the decision, who had been warned, and who had been denied the chance to object. One method could not replace the other. A perfect account did not warm a child, and a warm room did not explain why the child had been left in the cold. When their arguments grew sharp, they walked outside and named what each was trying to protect. They usually returned with a smaller, better question. That evening neither tried to win. Yan checked the physical limits of the proposed work; Cheng checked its authority. Only after the two answers matched did they permit themselves a little hope.

兩人處理恐懼的方式不同。晏舟總想親手碰觸損壞的東西，看看還能否修復。程觀則想追問誰下了決定、誰受過警告、誰沒有機會反對。一種方法不能取代另一種。完美帳冊無法替孩子取暖，暖房也無法解釋孩子為何受凍。爭論尖銳時，他們便到屋外，說出各自想保護什麼，回來時往往帶著更小、更好的問題。那晚誰都不爭勝；晏舟檢查工程的物理限制，程觀檢查執行權限。兩個答案一致後，他們才允許自己抱一點希望。

No single witness was required to carry the whole story. Some people knew the paths, others knew the accounts, and still others remembered what had happened to a neighbor. Cheng separated those voices carefully, while Yan matched them against the metal and water before him. In the spaces where nothing matched, they wrote questions rather than invented answers.

沒有人必須獨自承擔整個故事。有些人認得路，有些人懂帳，另一些人記得鄰居經歷。程觀仔細分開這些聲音，晏舟再用眼前的金屬與水比對。凡是對不起來的地方，他們只寫下問題，不虛構答案。
