A Creek That Remembers

借火山河：冬路的兩個修士 · 第 3 / 25 章 · 4000–5000 · 英中對照 · 校訂中

Below the ridge lay a creek whose shallow water should have remained unfrozen. Instead, white plates covered its surface. Across the water stood a ruined pier used decades earlier to carry fire reeds upstream. Cheng suspected the current guild had built its new bypass along that forgotten route; Yan wanted to examine the stone joints before offering an explanation.

山脊下方有條本不應結冰的淺溪，如今水面卻覆著白色冰片。對岸是一座廢棄渡頭，數十年前曾運送導熱銅簧上山。程觀懷疑公會沿著舊路建造新旁通管；晏舟則想先檢查石縫，再提出解釋。

A small boatman refused their first request to cross. His father had once worked there and lost his wages after reporting an unsafe pipe. Cheng began to argue, then stopped. He had learned that people were not evidence boxes to be opened with a clever question. Yan paid for the boat as an ordinary passenger and waited for the boatman to speak freely.

一名小船夫拒絕他們第一次渡河請求。他的父親曾在此工作，卻因通報不安全管線而遭扣薪。程觀正要爭論，卻住了口。他已學會人不是能靠巧妙問話撬開的證據盒。晏舟按普通乘客付船資，安靜等船夫自願開口。

A provincial deputy arrived for a consultation, speaking with diplomatic courtesy but showing contempt for the boatman's account. Yan called that attitude an abuse of office. A correspondent taking notes asked whether the warming creek's ecosystem could recover, or whether its rare river moss was already extinct. They chose a deliberate first phase of testing and promised to notify every household before the pipe was moved.

一名省署副手來諮詢，口氣彬彬有禮，卻蔑視船夫的說法。晏舟直指那是濫用職權。記錄現場的通訊記者問：小溪的生態系是否還能恢復，稀有河苔是否已滅絕？眾人選擇審慎進行第一階段測試，並承諾移動管線前通知每戶居民。

At sunset, the boatman showed them an old brass fitting that matched their ledger's drawing. It had been repaired twice, each time with a different alloy. The repairs proved only that someone had maintained the route, not who ordered its recent use. The two men chose to preserve the fitting and record where it came from rather than announce a convenient culprit.

日落時，船夫展示一個與帳冊圖樣相同的舊銅接頭。它修補過兩次，每次使用的合金不同。這只能證明有人維護路線，卻不能證明最近是誰下令使用。兩人決定保留接頭並記下來源，而非急著宣布方便的嫌疑人。

The boatman finally led them below the pier, where the water had carved a narrow tunnel through the foundation. Yan could see three different periods of work in the fittings: the original public line, a later emergency repair, and a recent hidden connection. The last had used an expensive seal available only to licensed workshops. The discovery narrowed the list of possible suppliers, but did not identify the installer. Cheng drew the tunnel and had the boatman approve the drawing before making a copy. Above them, the river ice cracked with the sound of a plate breaking. They climbed back before dark and left a warning marker so no child would follow their footprints into the weakened foundation. Evidence was useful only if finding it did not create another victim.

船夫終於領兩人走到渡頭下方，溪水已在地基鑿出狹窄通道。晏舟辨出三個時期的接頭：原來的公共支線、後來的緊急修理、最近的隱藏連接。最後一處用了只有持照工坊才有的昂貴封口。發現縮小了供應商範圍，卻不能指認安裝者。程觀畫出通道，請船夫確認才抄副本。頭頂冰面發出盤子碎裂般的聲音。他們趕在天黑前爬回岸上，留下警示標記，避免孩子沿腳印走進脆弱地基。若尋找證據又造出受害者，證據便失去意義。

The odds of a quick victory were poor. A young worker tried to sneak away with the fitting, then admitted he had been promised a trophy for finding it. The foreman's successor offered to swap it for a clean new part, claiming the change would be sustainable. Cheng felt the truth in his gut: a fresh fitting would erase the record of repairs. Yan refused the exchange without accusing the worker of a larger crime.

迅速獲勝的機率很低。一名年輕工人想偷偷帶走接頭，後來承認有人答應找到它就給獎牌。工頭的繼任者提出拿新接頭交換，說這樣才可永續使用。程觀直覺知道，新接頭會抹去修補痕跡。晏舟拒絕交換，卻沒有把工人說成更大的罪犯。

When the lamps were put out, the room felt larger than it had during the day's arguments. Yan could hear water traveling through bronze somewhere beyond the wall. The sound was ordinary, yet it reminded him that no furnace worked by admiration alone. Somebody had to clean the channel, replace the seals, and stay awake when the gauge drifted toward danger. Cheng had once considered those workers the last line of an account; now he asked their names before he asked their wages. Yan noticed the change but did not praise him for it. Trust, he thought, was not a prize awarded after one generous act. It was a habit made of truthful reports, corrected mistakes, and promises small enough to keep. They set their tools by the door and checked the next day's route before sleeping.

燈熄後，房間彷彿比白日爭論時寬敞。晏舟聽見牆外某處有水流過銅管。聲音很平凡，卻提醒他冬爐不能只靠讚美運作；總要有人清理管道、換掉封口，並在量表逼近危險時保持清醒。程觀從前把這些工人當成帳目最末一行，如今先問名字才問薪資。晏舟看出改變，卻沒有因此讚揚他。信任不是一次善行後就能領到的獎賞，而是誠實回報、修正錯誤與履行微小承諾形成的習慣。他們把工具放在門邊，確認隔日路線才睡。

They wanted the work to survive their departure. Yan trained someone at each stop to read a warning gauge, while Cheng taught a volunteer how to copy an order without changing its meaning. A stranger might have called the lessons dull. The two men considered them the beginning of freedom, because a community that understood its own tools did not have to wait for a master to rescue it.

兩人希望工作在他們離開後仍能繼續。晏舟每站教一人讀警示量表，程觀則教志工抄錄命令而不改變意思。陌生人或許覺得課程無趣；兩人卻認為這是自由的開端，因為懂得自己工具的社區，不必等會長來救。
