The two travelers returned to the valley for a public decision. The only repair they could recommend immediately was small: reopen one old branch and reduce the private city's intake for three nights. It would not make the poorest homes comfortable, but it might prevent more people from falling ill while a larger solution was designed.

兩名旅人回到山谷，參加公開決策。眼下唯一可建議的修繕很有限：重新打開一條舊支線，並連續三夜減少私人城市的熱量輸入。這不能讓最窮的住戶立刻舒適，卻可能在大方案完成前防止更多人生病。

Cheng read aloud the guild's own figures. A few residents accused him of trying to escape his former employer's wrongdoing. He did not deny having signed the books. Instead, he explained when he had been misled and offered to submit himself to an independent review. Yan realized his companion was asking the village to judge his actions without excusing them.

程觀念出公會自己的數據。有些居民指控他想逃避前雇主的惡行。他沒有否認曾簽帳，反而說明自己何時受騙，並願接受獨立審查。晏舟這才明白，同伴是在請村民不替他開脫，卻也依實際行為評判他。

A former peasant demanded a constitutional limit on the guild, while a young democrat argued that no emergency should cancel a fair hearing. The mayor warned against exaggeration: a single episode of cold weather did not prove every official was corrupt. Still, a massive fleet of fuel carts had been redirected during the recession, and retail prices had risen. Greed was one possible explanation, not yet a verdict.

一名出身農家的居民要求憲制限制公會；年輕民主派則主張緊急情勢也不能取消公平聽證。鎮長提醒大家別誇大：一次寒流事件不能證明所有官員都腐敗。然而經濟衰退期間，大批燃料車隊確實改道，零售價格也上漲。貪婪可能是原因之一，卻還不是裁決。

When the vote came, the old women who tended the communal ovens spoke first. They insisted on an emergency warming room for those without fuel, not merely an improved pipe for wealthier houses. The council approved that condition. Yan made a careful sketch; Cheng copied every vote, including objections, before they began their first authorized repair.

投票時，負責公共烤爐的長者首先發言。她們堅持替無燃料的人設置緊急暖房，而不能只替富裕住家改善管線。議會通過這項條件。晏舟仔細畫圖，程觀在開始第一次合法修繕前抄下每張票，包括反對意見。

The emergency branch took three hours to open. Yan had to scrape old mineral scale away with a wooden tool to avoid damaging the copper. Cheng watched the times and asked residents to check how much heat reached each stove. One widow reported no change. Instead of claiming victory, Yan traced her pipe and discovered a closed household valve that had not appeared on the town map. Opening it warmed her kitchen without reducing the school furnace. The council updated the map that evening and assigned a second worker to inspect forgotten houses. When the village prepared a modest meal for the repair crew, Cheng declined the honor of a special seat. He sat with the people whose complaints had forced them to improve the plan.

緊急支線花了三小時才打開。晏舟用木工具刮除老舊水垢，以免傷銅。程觀計時，請居民查看各爐獲得多少熱。有名寡婦回報沒有變化。晏舟沒有宣稱大功告成，而是沿她家管線查找，發現鎮圖漏掉一只關閉的家用閥。打開後她的廚房暖了，村學卻未降溫。議會當晚更新圖紙，並指派第二名工人檢查被遺忘的房子。村民為修繕隊備下簡餐，程觀婉拒特座，和那些用申訴迫使計畫改善的人一起坐。

An herbalist offered a warming herb as an incentive for families to attend the meeting. When a loose valve began to rattle, an angry resident threatened to smash it; Yan stopped him before anyone crossed that threshold. The council agreed to recruit trained helpers and publish substantial measurements. Their tactic was neither revenge nor silence. It was a widespread record that ordinary people could check.

一名草藥師提供暖身藥草，鼓勵家人參與會議。鬆動閥門開始喀喀作響時，憤怒居民想砸碎它；晏舟在事態越界前制止對方。議會同意招募受訓助手，並公開足夠的實測數據。他們的策略既非報復，也非沉默，而是讓一般人都能查核的廣泛紀錄。

At supper they spoke about their lives before the journey. Yan had once believed skill was enough: if he could mend a broken joint, surely the people nearby would benefit. Cheng had once believed proper signatures were enough: if every page carried a seal, surely the decision behind it was fair. The winter had exposed the weakness of both beliefs. A repaired joint could serve the wrong master, and a correct seal could approve a cruel division. Neither man could return to his old certainty. But that did not mean surrendering the work they loved. It meant inviting other people to question it, especially those who would suffer if the next decision failed. Yan pushed the warm bowl toward Cheng, and Cheng returned the borrowed pen before its ink froze.

晚餐時，他們談起上路前的生活。晏舟曾認為技術就夠了：只要修好接頭，附近的人自然受益。程觀曾認為正確簽章就夠了：每張紙都有印，背後決定自然公平。寒冬暴露兩種信念的弱點。修好的接頭可能服務錯誤主人，正確的印也可能批准殘酷分配。兩人都回不到舊有的確信，卻不等於放棄熱愛的工作，而是邀請別人質疑，尤其是決策失敗時要受苦的人。晏舟把暖碗推給程觀；程觀在墨水結冰前歸還借來的筆。

They always carried one empty page at the back of the ledger. It was reserved for a mistake they had not yet understood. On the first night Cheng had laughed at the habit; later he came to value it as much as any signed account. A record that permitted correction could protect a town long after the person who wrote it was gone.

帳冊最後一頁始終留白，專記尚未理解的錯誤。第一晚程觀曾笑這習慣，後來卻覺得它和簽過字的帳同樣重要。容許修正的紀錄，能在作者離去很久後繼續保護城鎮。
