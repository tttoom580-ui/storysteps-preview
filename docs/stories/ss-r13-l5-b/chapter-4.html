冬日的算術

The Arithmetic of Winter

借火山河：冬路的兩個修士 · 第 4 / 25 章 · 4000–5000 · 英中對照 · 校訂中

用互動版查詞、朗讀與收藏

The provincial school allowed independent measurements only during daylight. Its elderly teacher had once trained furnace inspectors, and she made both travelers state their assumptions before touching her instruments. Heat flowed downhill through bronze reeds, she explained, but the rate depended on wall thickness and the temperature difference between the two ends. No one could simply wish those limits away.

省學府只許白天進行獨立測量。年邁教師曾培訓爐管監察員，她要求兩位旅客先說明假設，再碰她的儀器。她解釋，熱沿銅簧往低處傳，但流速取決於管壁厚度與兩端溫差；沒有人能靠許願忽略這些限制。

Cheng calculated the households missing from the guild's report. The number was small compared with the capital's population, yet each household represented people who slept beside an empty stove. Yan measured a second pipe and noticed that its thickness differed from the approved plan. It was not proof of theft, but it was a sound reason to demand a full inspection.

程觀計算公會報告漏掉的戶數。相較於京城人口，數字很少；但每一戶都有必須在空爐旁睡覺的人。晏舟量出第二條管子的厚度與核准設計不同。這不等於偷竊的證據，卻足以合理要求全面檢查。

They could anticipate an argument about fiscal responsibility: no council wished to pay for an old hazard. A commuter from the coast explained that marine humidity had changed the pipes' corrosion rate. The teacher compared their diameter and showed a distinctive mark on the older metal. Her faculty had recorded a fraction of that mark in an earlier survey, although layers of bureaucracy had hidden the report.

他們預料議會會爭論財政責任：沒有人願替舊危險埋單。一名沿海通勤者解釋，海洋濕氣已改變管線腐蝕速度。老師比較管徑，指出舊金屬上獨特的痕跡。她的教研團隊曾在早期調查記下部分痕跡，卻被層層官僚程序掩埋。

The teacher offered to supervise a comparison between the original route and the new bypass. Before they began, she warned them that a cold day might distort readings. Yan welcomed the objection; reliable work did not become weaker when someone named its uncertainty. They agreed to publish raw observations, including results that might disappoint the villages.

老師願意主持原路線與新旁通管的比較，開始前先提醒寒冷天氣可能使讀數失真。晏舟樂於接受反對意見：可靠的工作不會因有人指出不確定性而變弱。大家同意公開原始觀察結果，包括可能讓村民失望的數據。

At first the teacher allowed only Yan to touch the pressure rig. Cheng waited until she invited him to explain the financial model, then showed how the guild had counted the same unit of heat twice under different labels. The students argued over whether the duplicate entry was intentional. The teacher made them stop and distinguish an incorrect total from deliberate deception. She then repeated the lesson with a simple jar of warm water: the water could be poured from one cup to another, but it did not double merely because two cups had received it. Several students laughed at the simplicity. One of them asked to help inspect future invoices, and Cheng gave him a sample with the personal details covered. The school had acquired more than evidence; it had gained a method that could be taught.

老師起初只准晏舟碰壓力架。程觀等她邀請才解釋財務模型，指出公會如何以不同標籤把同一單位熱量算了兩次。學生爭論這是否故意，老師讓他們先分清「總數錯誤」與「刻意欺騙」。她用一壺溫水重教一遍：水可從一杯倒到另一杯，卻不會因進過兩個杯子就加倍。有些學生笑它太簡單。其中一人主動要求幫忙查發票，程觀給他遮去個資的樣本。學府得到的不只證據，還有能教下去的方法。

One clerk called the matching marks a coincidence, while another wrote a denial before hearing the results. A healer added that cold stress could affect a child's hormone balance and raise winter mortality, though those claims required medical review. The school drew parallel charts for engineering and health evidence. Their final plea was practical: let qualified practitioners uncover the missing records, and reassure the province with verifiable figures rather than promises.

一名文書說痕跡吻合只是巧合，另一人在聽結果前就寫好否認書。醫者補充，寒冷壓力可能影響兒童荷爾蒙平衡、提高冬季死亡率，但這些說法仍需醫療審查。學府為工程與健康證據分別畫出平行圖表。他們最後的請求很實際：讓合格專業人員找出缺失紀錄，用可查核的數字安定全省，而非空頭承諾。

Neither man was especially good at receiving thanks. Yan preferred to check a newly mended joint instead of listening to speeches. Cheng looked for the person who had been left out of the list of acknowledgments. Gradually they learned to accept gratitude without treating it as proof that their choices were perfect. A good outcome could contain a mistake; a failed test could reveal an important truth. They wrote both kinds in the same book. When one of them became exhausted, the other took the watch, not because the agreement required it but because the road was easier to face with someone who would speak plainly. They were still learning the shape of that partnership, and each new town gave them a different test of it.

兩人都不太擅長接受感謝。晏舟寧可檢查新修的接頭，也不想聽演說；程觀則留意致謝名單漏掉誰。他們慢慢學會接受感激，卻不把它當成選擇完全正確的證明。好結果仍可能藏錯誤，失敗測試也能揭露真相。兩者都被記進同一本書。當一人疲憊，另一人接手守夜，不是協議要求，而是有個願說真話的人同行，路更容易走。這段夥伴關係仍在學習之中，每個新城鎮都帶來不同考驗。

They had started the journey as two strangers who disagreed about nearly everything. On this road they learned to disagree without walking away. Yan could point to a damaged joint; Cheng could explain which office had approved it. The truth often lay between those observations, not inside either one alone. They carried the work forward because another town would need both answers before choosing its own future.

上路時他們是幾乎事事意見相左的陌生人。在這條路上，他們學會爭論而不轉身離去。晏舟能指出破損接頭，程觀能解釋哪個單位批准了它。真相往往在兩種觀察之間，不完全屬於任何一方。他們繼續前進，因為下一個城鎮在決定未來前仍需要兩種答案。
