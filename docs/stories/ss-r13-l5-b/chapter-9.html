The Gate of Sandals

借火山河：冬路的兩個修士 · 第 9 / 25 章 · 4000–5000 · 英中對照 · 校訂中

To inspect the hidden distribution chamber, the travelers needed testimony from people who had worked there. A former gate attendant agreed to meet them outside the town boundary. He arrived riding a patient donkey, accompanied by a retired bodyguard who worried that the guild would threaten his family if he spoke openly.

要檢查隱藏分流室，兩名旅人需要前員工的證詞。前關口侍者答應在鎮界外見面，騎著一頭溫順驢子前來，身邊還有一名退役護衛。護衛擔心他公開發言後，公會會威脅他的家人。

Cheng offered to keep the account confidential until the man had legal protection. Yan inspected the attendant's rough map and found that one of its symbols matched the market warehouse. The witness remembered two versions of the dispatch order, one before a winter celebration and one after it. The difference involved a single valve, yet that valve could change the direction of an entire stream of heat.

程觀答應在證人獲得法律保護前保密。晏舟檢查粗糙地圖，發現一個符號與市場倉庫相同。證人記得派送命令有兩版，一版在冬慶前，一版在冬慶後。差別只有一只閥門，卻足以改變整股熱流方向。

The witness pointed to a patch beside a stone pyramid that marked the old boundary. A sandal lay there, forgotten during an earlier evacuation, beside a souvenir bell. The guild claimed its employees comprised only eligible city residents, yet the investigator found records showing it had chosen to discriminate against migrant workers. The man became passionate when he described how vulnerable families had been denied a warm residence.

證人指向舊界碑金字塔旁的一塊補丁地。一隻草鞋遺留在那裡，旁邊還有紀念小鐘，都是先前撤離時留下的。公會宣稱員工僅包括合格的城內居民，調查員卻查到它曾歧視外來勞工。證人談到弱勢家庭被拒絕入住暖房時，語氣激動。

They agreed to visit the gate at dawn, when an independent investigator could accompany them. The retired guard would not be forced to identify himself; his recollection could guide inspection but could not alone establish guilt. Yan respected Cheng's caution, although he worried that delay would cost another village its fuel.

他們約好黎明去關口，屆時會有獨立調查員同行。退役護衛不必被迫表明身分；他的回憶可引導檢查，卻不能單獨證明有罪。晏舟尊重程觀的謹慎，雖然擔心拖延會害下一個村莊失去燃料。

The gate inspection began with the witness standing outside the room, where he could leave whenever he wished. Yan opened the valve's cover in the presence of two neutral technicians. The hidden branch was real, but its seal had been replaced so recently that the metal did not identify the original installer. Cheng wrote the limitation before recording the discovery. The investigator found a second dispatch book in a locked shelf, obtained permission to copy it, and compared the two versions. Their dates disagreed by six days. One clerk had once been punished for asking about those dates; the investigator offered him a separate protected interview. As the group departed, the old donkey refused to cross a frozen patch until Yan spread sand over it. The animal's caution made everyone smile after a difficult morning.

關口檢查開始時，證人在房外等候，想走隨時能走。晏舟在兩名中立技師面前打開閥蓋。暗支線確實存在，但封口最近換過，金屬無法辨出最初安裝者。程觀先寫明限制，才記錄發現。調查員經批准抄錄鎖櫃裡的第二本派送帳，兩個版本的日期差了六天。曾有文書因質疑日期而受懲，調查員安排受保護的獨立訪談。眾人離開時，老驢不肯踏過冰面，直到晏舟灑上沙子。艱難上午之後，動物的謹慎讓大家露出笑容。

Yan poured fluid through a clear test tube and showed how a hidden branch could undo the valve's supposed limit. Cheng checked whether the witness's version was still valid before asking a therapist to offer support. Their report would incorporate the physical test, the witness's account, and the attendant's right to remain anonymous. The guard, who had brought his old ass to carry supplies, finally agreed to sign a sealed statement.

晏舟把流體注入透明測試管，展示暗支線如何使閥門原有限制失效。程觀確認證人的版本仍有效，並請療癒師提供支持。報告將納入物理測試、證人陳述，以及侍者維持匿名的權利。護衛原本牽著老驢運送物資，最後也同意簽署封存證詞。

Most travelers passed a town by remembering its market or the view from its gate. Yan remembered the location of its oldest heat pipe, the depth of its well, and the sound a valve made when its seal loosened. Cheng remembered the person who had asked an uncomfortable question and the official who had answered too quickly. Together those memories formed a different kind of map. It showed not only roads and rivers, but the distances between a decision and its consequences. They had promised to return rather than disappear after collecting evidence. A promise like that was inconvenient: it meant hearing criticism from people they believed they had helped. But the road seemed more honest when it could be traveled in both directions. They marked the next return visit before crossing the border.

多數旅人記得城鎮的市集或城門景色。晏舟記得最老熱管的位置、井深，以及封口鬆動時閥門的聲音。程觀記得誰問了不舒服的問題、哪名官員答得過快。兩種記憶拼成另一種地圖：上面不只道路河流，還有決定與後果之間的距離。他們承諾收集證據後仍會回訪，而非消失。這承諾很麻煩，因為還得聽自以為幫助過的人的批評。但一條能雙向走的道路才更誠實。他們在越界前記下下一次回訪日期。

Whenever they disagreed late at night, Cheng would put away the ledger and ask Yan what they could test in the morning. Yan would answer with a list of limits: the water available, the age of the pipe, the time before another household became cold. Naming limits did not make them hopeless. It gave them a place from which to begin an honest experiment.

深夜意見不合時，程觀會收起帳冊，問晏舟明早能測試什麼。晏舟則列出限制：可用水量、管齡，以及下一戶受凍前的時間。說出限制不代表絕望，反而讓人知道該從哪裡展開誠實試驗。
