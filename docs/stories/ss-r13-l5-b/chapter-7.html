借火山河：冬路的兩個修士 · 第 7 / 25 章 · 4000–5000 · 英中對照 · 校訂中

Following the secret delivery marks, Yan and Cheng reached a former colonial fort above a great scrap yard. Its walls had been built to control mountain trade. Now the Nine Furnaces Guild used the yard to store damaged heat reeds and to hide older designs that had never passed inspection. Guards were less concerned with danger than with keeping former workers from speaking.

循著祕密派送痕跡，晏舟與程觀抵達大型廢料場上方的舊殖民堡壘。它的高牆原本是為控制山地貿易而建；如今九爐會利用廢料場存放破損銅簧，並藏匿從未通過檢驗的舊設計。守衛擔心的似乎不是危險，而是前工人開口。

A young cataloger named Bo offered to show them the discarded parts if they promised to identify him only as an unnamed witness. Yan refused to steal the pieces; he proposed measuring them with a ruler in full view of the workers. Cheng negotiated a written record that the yard would keep the originals until an independent surveyor arrived.

年輕目錄員阿柏願意展示廢件，條件是他只能被稱作匿名證人。晏舟拒絕偷取零件，提出在工人眼前用尺測量。程觀則談妥書面紀錄：原物留在廢料場，等獨立測量師到場。

The yard's acquisition records included a bonus for every chunk of copper delivered. A worker showed them calcium deposits inside an old pipe, while a sophomore from the engineering school struggled to comprehend why the narrow opening mattered. Yan used a spacious chalkboard to draw the pipe's spine and the point where pressure could penetrate a weakened joint. The apprentice understood its dangerous implication immediately.

廢料場的收購紀錄記載，每交出一塊銅料就有額外獎金。工人展示管內的鈣質沉積；一名工程學院二年級生卻不懂狹窄開口為何重要。晏舟在寬大的黑板上畫出管線主幹，以及壓力可能穿透薄弱接縫的地方。學徒立刻明白其中危險的意涵。

The inspection revealed a narrow opening that could not carry the volume shown on the guild's invoice. Someone had charged villages for more heat than the channel could physically deliver. Yet the discrepancy did not reveal where the extra payments went. That was Cheng's task; Yan could identify an impossible machine, not the person who profited from it.

檢查發現管口太窄，根本無法輸送公會發票宣稱的熱量。有人向村莊收取了超過設備實際容量的費用。但差額還不能說明多付的錢去了哪裡。那是程觀的任務；晏舟能證明機器不可能如此運轉，卻不能直接指出誰獲利。

Bo led them through an aisle where discarded valves were arranged by year rather than by value. Several carried the same inspection number despite different sizes. Yan put them side by side and asked the apprentices to measure them without knowing which one came from the guild's new plan. The results were consistent. Cheng wrote the numbers in the order the workers called them, then let Bo inspect the page. When a guard demanded they hand over the paper, Cheng showed the yard's written permission and offered a copy to the guard as well. The man returned to his post without another threat. Bo's greatest relief was not that the travelers were brave; it was that they had not asked him to become a thief to help them.

阿柏帶他們走過按年份而非價值排列的廢閥走道。幾件尺寸不同的零件竟刻著相同檢查號碼。晏舟把它們排在一起，請學徒在不知道新計畫零件是哪件的情況下量測，結果一致。程觀按工人報數順序記錄，再讓阿柏確認。守衛要求交出紙張時，程觀展示廢料場的書面許可，也給守衛一份副本。守衛不再威脅。阿柏最安心的不是旅人很勇敢，而是他們沒有要求他靠偷竊來幫忙。

The fort's backyard held stacks of rusty scrap under surveillance. A republican councilor insisted the old flag was merely symbolic and disapproved of their visit, calling independent inquiry risky. Cheng copied the transit logs while Yan refused a secret payment to leave. By night they had a useful measurement and a lawful record of where it came from, but they still lacked proof of the financial destination.

堡壘後院堆著一疊疊生鏽廢料，受到監控。一名共和派議員稱舊旗只是象徵，反對兩人來訪，認為獨立調查太冒險。程觀抄錄運輸日誌，晏舟拒絕拿錢離開。夜裡他們已有有用的測量值及合法來源紀錄，仍缺少金流目的地的證據。

Before leaving each place, they asked three questions: Who would inspect the system tomorrow? Who could stop it if it became unsafe? Where would someone find the record of what had changed? Those questions sounded less glorious than a cultivator's oath, but they outlived the excitement of any single rescue. Yan made the answers visible beside the pipes. Cheng gave the local readers a second copy in words they could understand. When a resident disagreed, he did not hurry to erase the objection; he included it with the evidence. The winter had taken away their belief in simple heroes, but it had left them a more difficult kind of hope: a place where ordinary people could make a decision and change it when the facts demanded.

每次離開一地，他們都問三件事：明天誰檢查系統？不安全時誰能停機？變更紀錄要去哪裡找？這些問題比修士誓言不起眼，卻能活得比一次救援的興奮更久。晏舟把答案寫在管線旁；程觀以居民看得懂的文字留下第二份。有人不同意時，他不急著刪掉異議，而是與證據一同保存。寒冬帶走了他們對簡單英雄的信念，卻留下更艱難的希望：普通人能作決定，也能在事實改變時修正它。

The next morning they checked the instruments before speaking to anybody. Yan trusted the gauge only after comparing it with the hand-built measure he had carried since his apprenticeship. Cheng checked that a witness could withdraw a statement without losing food or shelter. Their precautions slowed them, but a slow and honest answer was better than a quick one that exposed someone else to danger.

隔天早上，兩人開口說話前先檢查儀器。晏舟把量表與學徒時代自製的量具對照後才相信讀數。程觀則確保證人即使撤回陳述，也不會失去食物或住所。防護程序拖慢他們，但緩慢誠實的答案總比讓別人陷入危險的快速結論好。
