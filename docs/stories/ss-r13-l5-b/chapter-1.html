沒有暖意的禮拜堂

The Chapel Without Heat

借火山河：冬路的兩個修士 · 第 1 / 25 章 · 4000–5000 · 英中對照 · 校訂中

On the first snowless winter morning in Cinder Valley, Yan Zhou found ice inside the village ovens. The mountain was warm below the soil, yet the houses above it had gone cold. A traveling cultivator might have blamed a hostile spirit; Yan was a repairer of bronze heat pipes, and he knew the frost formed wherever someone had redirected the underground current.

燼谷迎來第一個沒有降雪的冬日清晨時，晏舟發現村裡的烤爐內竟結了冰。山的土層以下仍有熱意，地面房屋卻冷得異常。路過的修士或許會怪罪惡靈；晏舟是修理銅製導熱管的工匠，他知道只要有人轉移地底熱流，就會出現這種霜。

Cheng Guan arrived carrying a ledger he had refused to destroy. Years earlier he had signed the guild's accounts without knowing what the columns meant. Yan distrusted anyone who had worked for the Nine Furnaces Guild. Still, Cheng showed him a red line connecting the valley's ovens to the capital's pleasure baths, and neither man could pretend it was harmless.

程觀帶來一本他拒絕銷毀的帳冊。多年前，他在不明白欄位意義的情況下替九爐會簽過帳。晏舟不信任曾替公會工作的人；然而程觀指給他看，一條紅線竟把山谷的烤爐接往京城的享樂浴池，兩人都無法裝作這件事無害。

No one had any certainty about the cause until a cluster of elders met at the abandoned chapel. The guild offered compensation but would not disclose its calculations at the public forum. A generator kept its representatives warm, while nearby herb beds began to wither. Some apprentices were envious of the guild's metropolitan towers; others feared that an immense fortune had been purchased with their valley's habitat and heritage.

直到一群長老在廢棄禮拜堂聚集，眾人才有機會查明原因。公會承諾補償，卻不肯在公開論壇揭露計算方式。發電機讓代表們保持溫暖，旁邊的藥草圃卻逐漸枯萎。有些學徒羨慕公會的都會高塔；另一些人則擔心，龐大的財富竟是以山谷的棲地與文化遺產換來。

Yan proposed a modest first step: close the private bypass for exactly one hour, record how much warmth returned, and reopen it before the city suffered. Cheng insisted that the villagers must witness the measurement. The two men agreed on a rule they would carry throughout their journey: heat could be moved but never created, and no community could be secretly charged for another community's comfort.

晏舟提出一項有限的第一步：關閉私人旁通管整整一小時，記錄回流的熱量，再在城裡受影響前打開。程觀堅持讓村民在場見證。兩人於是約定一路遵守的原則：熱能只能移動，無法憑空創造；任何地方都不該在不知情下替其他地方的舒適付費。

After the elders signed, Yan carefully turned the test valve while Cheng called out each reading. The hearth at the village school warmed first. A baker put his palm against the wall and smiled, then stepped away so others could test it for themselves. Across the square, the chapel's old bell rang once. The guild's representative immediately claimed the change was accidental, but he had no instrument to support that claim. Cheng recorded the response without adding a judgment of his own. At the end of the hour they reopened the bypass as promised. The warmth faded, proving that the change had been temporary and measurable. For the villagers that was both painful and hopeful: it showed the cold was not simply the mountain's will, and that a decision had been made somewhere beyond their roofs.

長老簽字後，晏舟小心轉動測試閥，程觀逐次報出讀數。村學的爐火先暖起來。麵包師以掌心貼牆露出笑容，隨即讓出位置給他人驗證。廣場另一頭，舊禮拜堂的鐘只響了一下。公會代表立刻宣稱變化只是意外，卻拿不出儀器支持。程觀記下說法，不自行定罪。一小時後，他們依承諾重新打開旁通管。暖意逐漸消失，證明這變化確實是暫時、可測量的。對村民而言，結果既痛苦又帶來希望：寒冷不是山的意志，而是有人在屋頂之外作了決定。

Yan felt an impulse to break the pipe immediately, but Cheng offered a better insight: the contract contained an optional provision for independent observation. The visiting supervisor called the matter a simple transaction and tried to leave. On the steps he made Yan stumble; Cheng caught the measuring instrument before it shattered. The villagers signed a request for a witnessed test, turning a private argument into a public promise.

晏舟一時衝動想立刻砸斷管子，程觀卻提出更好的見解：契約內有一條可選擇的獨立觀測條款。到訪的主管把事件說成簡單交易，打算離開；他在階梯上害晏舟踉蹌，程觀及時接住儀器。村民共同簽署見證測試申請，將私人爭執化為公開承諾。

The mountain paths changed color as the weather shifted, but the argument below them remained familiar. People wanted warmth, and nobody wished to be the one asked to surrender it. Yan understood that desire too well to call it selfish by default. Cheng had learned that fair rules could not begin by pretending everyone had the same resources. So they asked households what minimum heat meant in practice: enough to cook, enough to protect a patient, enough to keep a water pipe from bursting. Answers differed, but the act of asking changed the meeting. The argument became a set of needs that could be measured and negotiated rather than a contest between proud banners. For a little while the winter road felt passable again.

天氣改變，山路顏色也變，但山下爭論始終相似：人人都想取暖，沒人願意被要求犧牲。晏舟太理解這份願望，不會先認定它自私。程觀學會公平規則不能假裝人人資源相同。於是他們詢問各家最低熱量真正意味什麼：能煮飯、保護病人、阻止水管爆裂。答案各不相同，但這一問改變了會議。爭執從旗幟間的較量，變成能測量與協商的需求。那一刻，冬路似乎又能走了。

An older traveler once told Yan that the best repair was the one no one noticed. He had believed that for years. Now he wondered whether an invisible repair could ever be enough if the reason for the break remained secret. Cheng suggested that they should make not the machine but its control visible. That thought followed them across the road long after the traveler had gone.

年長旅人曾告訴晏舟，最好的修繕是沒有人注意到的修繕。他相信了多年，如今卻懷疑，若破損原因仍被隱藏，無形的修理是否足夠。程觀建議，應該讓人看見的是機器的管理權，而不只是機器本身。旅人走遠後，這想法仍陪他們走了好長一段路。
