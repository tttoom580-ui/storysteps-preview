借火山河：冬路的兩個修士 · 第 8 / 25 章 · 4000–5000 · 英中對照 · 校訂中

The road led into Thread Market, a border settlement where traders changed flags more often than they changed shoes. It had enough inns for travelers and too few heated rooms for its own families. Yan wanted to find the guild's warehouse before dark; Cheng wanted to understand why every receipt was stamped with a code that belonged to no registered firm.

道路通往線市，一座商販換旗比換鞋還快的邊境聚落。這裡旅店足以招待過客，當地家庭卻缺少暖房。晏舟想在天黑前找到公會倉庫；程觀則想查清為何每張收據都蓋著不屬於任何登記公司的代碼。

An elderly innkeeper showed them a street plan altered during the last cold wave. It moved relief stations away from the old quarter, where the poorest workers lived. The change had been described as efficient, but it left people walking farther in freezing weather. Cheng wrote down the distance between stations and tested it with Yan instead of trusting a clerk's estimate.

一名年長旅店主人展示上次寒潮期間修改的街圖。圖上救助站從貧困勞工居住的舊區撤走。改動被說成有效率，卻讓居民在寒風中走得更遠。程觀記下站距，與晏舟實走測試，而不是相信文書估算。

A gathering outside the guild salon turned into chaos when visitors learned that relief tickets did not entitle every household to fuel. Some called the code an alien mark, while others blamed a myth about mountain spirits. Yan asked for comprehension rather than panic: the visible spectrum of lamps showed which streets had power. A ragged porter explained that the actual shortage began when a strategic delivery had been suspended.

公會會館外的集會在訪客得知救助票並非每戶都可領燃料後陷入混亂。有些人說代碼是異族符號，另一些人則怪罪山靈傳說。晏舟要大家理解而非驚慌：燈光可見光譜能顯示哪些街道有能源。一名衣衫襤褸的搬運工說，真正的短缺始於一批策略性補給被暫停。

At the warehouse, the code appeared again on a crate of costly glass valves. The same letters also marked a large empty room whose windows faced the wealthy district. Yan realized the room could be a hidden distribution chamber. They asked permission to inspect it; the manager declined, but he did not prevent them from photographing the publicly posted safety plan.

倉庫裡，一箱昂貴的玻璃閥上再次出現相同代碼。面向富人區的一間大空屋也刻著這串字。晏舟察覺那裡可能是隱藏的分流室。他們要求進入檢查，經理拒絕，但沒有阻止他們拍下張貼在公共區域的安全圖。

In the market square, the innkeeper proposed a trial queue marked by colored ropes. Families with sick relatives could approach first, while others received numbered times rather than waiting in the freezing street. Cheng made the labels clear enough for people who could not read the city dialect. Yan inspected the heating pipes near the queue and found that a narrow vein of hot water passed beneath the pavement without entering the public kitchen. He drew a safe connection but refused to install it until the district approved the transfer. The meeting lasted past sunset. To Yan's surprise, the wealthier merchants agreed to lend benches and blankets, provided the rules would apply to their own workers as well. Cooperation began with practical conditions, not instant affection.

市集廣場上，旅店主人提議用不同色繩劃分隊伍。病人家屬先取暖，其他人領號碼與時段，不必在寒街久等。程觀把標示寫得讓不懂城內方言的人也看得懂。晏舟查隊伍附近暖管，發現路面下有一道細細熱水脈絡，卻沒接進公共廚房。他畫出安全接法，但堅持等區府批准調度才施工。會議直到日落。出乎晏舟意料，富商同意借長椅與毯子，條件是規則也適用自家工人。合作始於實際條件，而不是瞬間的友愛。

Cheng needed flexibility, not a theoretical victory. He offered a temporary plan to minimize waiting, with a separate line for sick residents and travelers whose visa had expired. The manager tried to stall, claiming that no change was possible whatsoever. Yan proposed a modest tribute to the volunteer cooks instead of another ceremony for rich donors. For once the market's loudest voices agreed to test a real solution.

程觀需要的是彈性，而非理論上的勝利。他提出暫時縮短排隊時間，讓病人及簽證過期的旅人另排一隊。經理試圖拖延，聲稱無論如何都不可能改變。晏舟提議向志工廚師致謝，而不是再為富豪捐款人辦典禮。市場上最吵的人們這次同意測試真正的辦法。

Rumors traveled faster than their boots. By the time they reached a new door, someone had usually turned their last careful statement into an extravagant accusation or a miraculous promise. Cheng began each meeting by correcting those inventions, even when the corrections disappointed allies. Yan opened his tool case and demonstrated a small fact that anyone could observe. Some people left; others stayed to ask better questions. The men did not measure success by how many voices praised them. They measured it by whether a worker could understand the risk, whether a resident could refuse a transfer, and whether the next inspection could repeat the last result. On nights when they doubted themselves, those modest tests were enough to keep their feet on the road.

謠言跑得比他們的腳步還快。到了下一扇門口，通常有人已把上次謹慎發言說成驚人指控或神奇承諾。程觀每次開會先修正這些錯誤，即使讓支持者失望。晏舟則打開工具箱，示範任何人都能看見的小事實。有人離席，有人留下來問更好的問題。兩人不以獲得多少稱讚衡量成功，而是看工人能否理解風險、居民能否拒絕調度、下一次檢查能否重現結果。自我懷疑的夜晚，這些樸素標準已足以支持他們繼續上路。

Wherever the path led, children were the first to ask difficult questions. They wanted to know why a rich street shone while a poor one froze, and whether the men could bring the warmth back. Yan answered the second question with measurements, not promises. Cheng answered the first with copies of the rules. Each child was free to ask again when the answers proved incomplete.

不論走到哪裡，孩子總是最先問難題。他們想知道為何富裕街道明亮、貧困街道結冰，以及兩人能否帶回暖意。晏舟用測量回答第二題，不作空頭保證；程觀用規則副本回答第一題。只要答案仍不完整，每個孩子都可以再問。
