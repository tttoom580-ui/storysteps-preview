借火山河：冬路的兩個修士 · 第 2 / 25 章 · 4000–5000 · 英中對照 · 校訂中

The route to the nearest provincial station crossed a narrow ridge. Yan carried the heavy bronze gauge; Cheng carried names of households that had lost their cooking fire. They needed both kinds of evidence. A reading without a witness might be dismissed as a mistake, while a complaint without a reading could be called a rumor.

前往最近的省級驛站，必須越過狹窄山脊。晏舟背著沉重銅量表；程觀背著失去灶火的家戶名冊。他們需要兩種證據。沒有見證人的讀數會被當成失誤；沒有讀數的申訴則可能被說成謠言。

At the ridge's highest turn, a supply sled slipped and threatened to take its driver down the slope. Yan set the gauge aside to save the driver. Cheng watched the instrument disappear into a snowdrift and understood that his companion would value a stranger's life above even their only proof. The driver later revealed that the sled belonged to the guild.

在山脊最高的彎道，補給雪橇滑落，險些把車夫拖下坡。晏舟放下量表救人。程觀看見儀器消失於雪堆，明白同伴會把陌生人的性命看得比唯一的證據更重要。車夫事後才說，雪橇其實屬於公會。

A sensor beside the road confirmed an extraordinary rise in heat loss. The corporate agent claimed the change was beneficial, because a new engine could convert waste warmth into power at its destination. Yet the villages faced a winter epidemic of lung illness. Yan had gone on an errand to buy medical herbs, and now he had to haul the damaged gauge back uphill before it could plunge into a ravine.

路旁感測器證實熱量流失異常增加。企業代理人宣稱這是有益的變化，因為新機器能在終點把廢熱轉為電力；山村卻正在面對冬季肺病流行。晏舟原本只是出門買藥草，現在還得將損壞的量表拖回山坡，以免它墜入深谷。

Inside a wayside kitchen, the rescued driver handed them a sealed note from his dispatch board. It showed that wagons of coal had been rerouted although no villages had ordered them. Cheng did not ask for the man's loyalty; he asked only permission to copy the schedule accurately, leaving the man's name concealed until he could choose whether to testify.

在路邊廚房裡，獲救車夫遞出一張從派車板取下的封存便條。紙上顯示煤車在沒有村莊下單的情況下被改道。程觀沒有要求車夫效忠，只請求準確抄下班表，並暫時隱去他的姓名，讓他自行選擇是否作證。

Recovering the gauge took most of the afternoon. The driver insisted on helping even after Yan told him that rescue created no debt. When they finally dug out the instrument, its glass cover was cracked but its brass needle still moved. Yan marked the damage with red ink so no later reader would mistake the altered readings for a perfect result. Cheng added the driver's account on a separate page. The driver then revealed he had refused two earlier deliveries because the cargo list omitted the village clinics. That refusal had cost him a week's wages. Yan offered to repair his sled rather than buy his silence; the driver accepted the repair and kept the right to tell his story in his own words. By evening a difficult rescue had become an unexpected alliance.

挖回量表花了大半個下午。車夫堅持幫忙，即使晏舟說救人不會產生欠債。終於挖出時，玻璃蓋已碎，銅針仍能移動。晏舟用紅墨標示損傷，以免後人把讀數誤認為完美結果。程觀另頁記下車夫陳述。車夫隨後說，自己曾拒絕兩次運送，因貨單漏掉村診所，代價是一週薪資。晏舟提出修雪橇，而非花錢買沉默；車夫接受修理，也保有自行陳述的權利。傍晚時，一場艱難救援竟成了意外結盟。

The driver served hot stew and spoke as a willing respondent, not a captive witness. His reminder was simple: an official signature did not erase a company's liability. Cheng felt the temptation to turn every detail into an accusation, but Yan held up a torch and asked them to trim the torn map instead. Their small transformation began with careful records, not dramatic speeches; even the most vocal villagers accepted that distinction.

車夫端出熱燉菜，自願回答問題，而非被迫作證。他提醒眾人：官員的簽字不會抹去公司的責任。程觀很想把每個疑點都變成指控，但晏舟舉起火把，請大家先裁去地圖的破損邊緣。他們的轉變始於仔細記錄，而非激昂演說；連最愛大聲抱怨的村民也接受這個差別。

The most difficult part of their work was knowing when not to act. A loose pipe invited immediate repair; a frightened witness invited immediate reassurance; an angry town invited a speech. Yet haste could destroy the very evidence they needed, or leave the next village without heat. They learned to pause just long enough to describe the danger in plain language, identify who could stop the work, and make a record before changing anything. That routine looked unimpressive compared with the grand movements of famous cultivators. Yan no longer minded. Each time a child found a warm place to sleep or an exhausted worker could leave a dangerous machine, the ordinary routine had proved its worth. Cheng stored the signed forms beside their food, where neither man would forget them.

工作最難的部分，是知道什麼時候不該動手。鬆動管子催促人立刻修，受驚證人需要立刻安慰，憤怒城鎮則渴望一場演說。但倉促可能毀掉證據，也可能讓下一村無火可用。兩人學會停下片刻，用白話描述危險、確認誰有權停工，並在改動前留下紀錄。這套程序比著名修士的浩大招式不起眼，晏舟如今卻不介意。每當孩子能睡在暖處、疲憊工人能離開危險機器，普通程序就證明了價值。程觀把簽名表放在食物旁，免得兩人遺忘。

A repair changed more than the sound of a pipe. It changed where people slept, how far they walked for soup, and whether an elderly neighbor could stay at home. Yan began drawing those consequences beside every technical plan. Cheng made sure local residents could challenge the drawing. Their maps grew crowded, but they became much more useful than the elegant pages the guild had once displayed.

一次修理改變的不只是管線聲音，還有居民睡在哪裡、要走多遠才領得到湯，以及年長鄰居能否留在家中。晏舟開始在每張技術圖旁畫出這些後果；程觀確保地方居民有權質疑。圖紙變得擁擠，卻遠比公會昔日展示的漂亮頁面有用。
