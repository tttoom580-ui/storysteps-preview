The Drought Terminal

Beyond the border town, winter air turned dry enough to crack wood. The last municipal heat terminal stood at the entrance to a protected wilderness. An institute of mountain engineering offered the men a laboratory bench, but only if they would test the guild's official model alongside their own. Yan accepted; a fair comparison was worth more than an easy victory.

離開邊鎮後，冬天的空氣乾燥到能使木頭裂開。最後一座市政熱力終站位於荒野保護區入口。山地工程研究所願意借給他們實驗台，條件是同時測試公會官方模型與兩人的模型。晏舟答應了；公平比較比輕鬆獲勝更重要。

Inside the institute, Yan found that the official plan assumed a full underground water table. Recent wells showed a much lower level. Because the pipes used water to carry warmth, that mistake magnified losses at every junction. Cheng asked the institute to preserve both the original plan and the revised data, including the date when the river began to fall.

在研究所裡，晏舟發現官方設計假定地下水充足，最近井測卻顯示水位已大幅下降。管線靠水搬運熱量，這個錯誤便放大了每個接點的損失。程觀請研究所保存原計畫及修正資料，並記下河水何時開始下降。

An engineer with an allergy to furnace smoke reminded them that the drought affected more than pipes. The bulk of relief food had to be grilled or boiled, but poor digestion was spreading among exhausted workers. A counselor tried to prevent a quarrel during the collaboration, asking each person to cite a descriptive observation rather than exclaim at every unexpected reading. No one could compel the truth to appear through anger.

一名對爐煙過敏的工程師提醒他們，乾旱傷害的不只管道。大部分救濟食物需要烤或煮，但疲累勞工的消化不良日益普遍。輔導員在合作過程中阻止爭吵，要求每人引用具描述性的觀察，而不是每次讀數意外就驚叫。憤怒無法迫使真相現身。

The institute proposed a trial with a fixed time limit and an independent safety observer. If the test failed, the men would publish that failure. If it succeeded, the results would still need to be checked elsewhere. Cheng liked the arrangement because it kept hope tied to a method rather than a heroic promise.

研究所提議限時測試，並由獨立安全觀察員在場。若測試失敗，兩人就公開失敗；若成功，結果仍須其他地點複驗。程觀欣賞這安排，因為希望因此繫於可重複的方法，而不是英雄式承諾。

The controlled test took place in a sealed stone shed. Technicians agreed beforehand that if the pressure reached the red line, anyone could order a stop. Yan kept his hand near the valve but let an institute operator lead the work. Cheng watched the official witness copy each number. When the supply water fell below the model's minimum, heat loss increased exactly as the new calculations predicted. The experiment could not reveal who had concealed the low water readings, but it showed why the official plan was unsafe. The team repeated the test with a safer flow, then published both sets of results. That evening the institute director admitted the school had received warning letters years ago and promised to search its neglected archives rather than deny their existence.

受控測試在封閉石棚進行。技師事先同意壓力到紅線時，任何人都能叫停。晏舟把手放在閥邊，卻讓研究所操作員主導。程觀看著官方見證人逐數抄錄。供水跌到模型下限時，熱損果然如新計算般上升。試驗不能指出誰隱藏低水位讀數，卻證明官方方案不安全。團隊以較安全流量重做，並公開兩組結果。當晚主任承認學校多年以前收到警告信，答應去查荒廢檔案，而非否認它們存在。

The preliminary model looked almost like a thriller, with a hidden channel and a surprising variable, yet the staff rejected any radical conclusion before repeated tests. Yan was briefly overwhelmed when the gauge turned red, but Cheng reminded him that rational decisions must include uncertainty. The terminal director finally authorized one controlled test inside the wilderness boundary, with the results open to every affected village.

初步模型有如驚悚故事，既有暗道又有意外變數，但職員拒絕在重複驗證前下激進結論。量表變紅時，晏舟一度不知所措；程觀提醒他，理性決策必須包含不確定性。終站主任最後批准在荒野邊界內進行一次受控測試，並向所有受影響村莊公開結果。

The day taught them how easily a useful measurement could become a weapon in the wrong hands. Yan could explain a gauge to anyone willing to stand beside him, but an argument made from numbers could still hide the people who paid the cost. Cheng therefore wrote two columns in his notebook: what the instruments showed, and what witnesses had reported. He left a third column blank for questions neither source could answer. Before they slept, they read the three columns aloud to each other. Neither enjoyed hearing the limits of his own knowledge. Yet the disagreement made the notebook stronger, and the people whose lives it described deserved nothing less than an honest record. They agreed that an uncertain answer, spoken plainly, was safer than a confident answer built to impress a crowd. The next decision would begin with that distinction.

這一天讓兩人明白，有用的測量數字多容易在錯誤的人手裡變成武器。只要有人願站在旁邊，晏舟就能解釋量表；但由數字組成的論點仍可能藏起真正付出代價的人。程觀因此在筆記寫了兩欄：儀器顯示的事，以及證人說的事；第三欄留給兩者都無法回答的問題。睡前，他們把三欄念給彼此聽。承認知識有限並不舒服，分歧卻讓筆記更加可靠，也才對得起紀錄中的人。他們同意，坦白說出的不確定答案，勝過為取悅群眾而設計的肯定說法。下一次決定將從這個分別開始。

At the end of the meeting Yan washed his hands in cold water, taking care not to waste the little warmth remaining in the pipe. Cheng laid their papers on a dry board and checked every date. Neither wanted to repeat the errors of people who had once hidden behind difficult language. If a village could not understand the record, they would write it again before moving on.

會議結束後，晏舟用冷水洗手，注意別浪費管中僅剩的熱。程觀把紙攤在乾板上，逐一核對日期。他們都不願重蹈那些以艱澀語言掩飾錯誤的人的覆轍。若村莊看不懂紀錄，他們就重寫，然後才上路。
