賭場樓上的檔案室

The Archive Above the Casino

借火山河：冬路的兩個修士 · 第 11 / 25 章 · 4000–5000 · 英中對照 · 校訂中

The next clue led to a private archive above a casino in the southern district. The building's owner, an elderly heir to a furnace workshop, agreed to let the men examine a small collection of old plans. He had kept them intact after the guild replaced his father's apprentices with cheaper labor. He demanded one condition: no document would leave without a recorded copy.

下一條線索通往南區賭場樓上的私人檔案室。屋主是老爐坊繼承人，答應兩人查閱少量舊圖紙。他在公會用便宜勞工取代父親學徒後，一直保存這些完整文件。他提出唯一條件：任何文件離開前都必須留有紀錄副本。

Yan located a diagram of the first winter furnace, whose output had been deliberately limited to protect distant farms. Cheng found notes from a labor dispute, including proof that safety complaints had been removed from official minutes. They photographed the notes in order and sealed each copy with the owner's wax stamp. The old man watched in silence, then asked whether his father's workshop would finally be remembered for its warnings rather than its debts.

晏舟找到第一座冬爐的圖紙，原本就設了出力上限，以保護遠方農田。程觀翻出勞資糾紛筆記，內有安全投訴遭刪除官方紀錄的證據。兩人依序拍下筆記，並以屋主蠟印封存各份副本。老人默默觀看，最後問父親的工坊是否終於能因警告而被記得，而不只因債務。

The heir was apt to characterize the guild as a pack of wolves, and the old casino had certainly fascinated many spectators. Yet a clinical report from the workshop told a quieter story: workers had begun to gasp in the smoke, and an injured apprentice carried a permanent scar. A wheelchair stood near the workbench, where the heir's father once taught safety lessons in a weekly workshop.

老人很容易把公會形容成狼群，舊賭場也的確吸引許多旁觀者。但工坊的臨床報告講述了更平靜的事實：工人開始在煙裡喘氣，一名受傷學徒留下永久疤痕。工作台旁有張輪椅，老人的父親從前就在那裡每週教安全課。

That night, a guard claimed someone had tried to break into the archive. Yan checked the windows and found scratches older than their visit. Cheng insisted they should not link a possible theft to the guild without a time record. The guard, relieved, agreed to keep a dated watch log. Their inquiry survived another temptation to turn suspicion into a dramatic story.

那夜，警衛說有人試圖闖進檔案室。晏舟檢查窗戶，發現刮痕早在他們到訪前便存在。程觀堅持，沒有時間紀錄就不能把可能的竊案歸咎公會。警衛放心後，同意開始記錄有日期的巡查日誌。他們再次抵住把疑心編成戲劇故事的誘惑。

The heir placed two drawings beside each other. One showed the first furnace with dozens of small isolation valves; the later drawing had replaced them with a single central control. The replacement was cheaper and easier for the guild to command, but it also allowed one operator to divert warmth from distant villages without their knowledge. Yan traced the change with a dry finger. Cheng asked the heir what his father had said when the new design appeared. The old man recalled a letter requesting a public demonstration that never took place. They found a carbon copy in a folder of rejected petitions. It offered no secret solution, only proof that someone had asked the right question before the harm occurred. Yan considered that question part of the workshop's true inheritance.

繼承人把兩張圖並排：第一座冬爐有許多小型隔離閥；後來的圖卻用單一中央控制取代。新設計便宜、方便公會命令，卻也容許一人不通知遠村就改道熱流。晏舟以乾燥手指沿變更處滑過。程觀問老人的父親見到新圖說過什麼。老人記得父親寫信要求公開示範，卻從未舉行。他們在退回請願書中找到複寫本。那不是祕密解方，只證明早有人在傷害發生前問對問題。晏舟認為這份提問才是工坊真正的遺產。

Outside, dogs began to howl as the sheer cold settled over the roofs. The heir asked whether copyright could keep the original drawing from being sold as a guild invention. Cheng recommended an archive upgrade rather than a public accusation. A damaged rifle from an old militia hung over the door, but the real refuge was the paper itself: despite years of neglect, the records had survived possible theft and fire.

屋外，嚴寒降臨屋頂，狗群開始嚎叫。老人問著作權是否能防止原圖被公會當成自己發明出售。程觀建議先改善檔案保存，而非公開指控。門上掛著舊民兵隊的破步槍，但真正的避難所是紙本本身：儘管多年疏於照料，紀錄仍從可能的竊盜與火災中存活。

The mountain paths changed color as the weather shifted, but the argument below them remained familiar. People wanted warmth, and nobody wished to be the one asked to surrender it. Yan understood that desire too well to call it selfish by default. Cheng had learned that fair rules could not begin by pretending everyone had the same resources. So they asked households what minimum heat meant in practice: enough to cook, enough to protect a patient, enough to keep a water pipe from bursting. Answers differed, but the act of asking changed the meeting. The argument became a set of needs that could be measured and negotiated rather than a contest between proud banners. For a little while the winter road felt passable again.

天氣改變，山路顏色也變，但山下爭論始終相似：人人都想取暖，沒人願意被要求犧牲。晏舟太理解這份願望，不會先認定它自私。程觀學會公平規則不能假裝人人資源相同。於是他們詢問各家最低熱量真正意味什麼：能煮飯、保護病人、阻止水管爆裂。答案各不相同，但這一問改變了會議。爭執從旗幟間的較量，變成能測量與協商的需求。那一刻，冬路似乎又能走了。

An older traveler once told Yan that the best repair was the one no one noticed. He had believed that for years. Now he wondered whether an invisible repair could ever be enough if the reason for the break remained secret. Cheng suggested that they should make not the machine but its control visible. That thought followed them across the road long after the traveler had gone.

年長旅人曾告訴晏舟，最好的修繕是沒有人注意到的修繕。他相信了多年，如今卻懷疑，若破損原因仍被隱藏，無形的修理是否足夠。程觀建議，應該讓人看見的是機器的管理權，而不只是機器本身。旅人走遠後，這想法仍陪他們走了好長一段路。
