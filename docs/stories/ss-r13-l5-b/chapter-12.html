玻璃競技場的審查

The Trial in the Glass Arena

借火山河：冬路的兩個修士 · 第 12 / 25 章 · 4000–5000 · 英中對照 · 校訂中

用互動版查詞、朗讀與收藏

The guild held its annual technology demonstration inside a glass arena. Its master promised an unlimited winter furnace and invited the provincial council to watch. Yan knew the claim violated the first rule of cultivation engineering: bronze could distribute existing heat, never produce it without a source. Cheng persuaded the event organizers to permit an independent calculation, though they refused to cancel the show.

公會在玻璃競技場舉行年度技術展示。會長承諾一座無限供暖的冬爐，並邀省議會觀摩。晏舟知道這違反修行工程的第一條規則：銅器只能分配既有熱量，不能沒有來源就憑空產熱。程觀說服主辦方容許獨立計算，雖然他們不肯取消表演。

Before the audience, Yan connected a transparent pipe to the furnace. When its hidden reservoir emptied, the flame fell despite the guild's confident claims. The crowd fell silent. Cheng stated exactly what the demonstration showed: a limited reservoir had been concealed. Whether the same method had been used to charge villages remained a separate question that required invoices and testimony.

晏舟在觀眾面前把透明管接上冬爐。當隱藏的儲熱槽用盡，火焰不顧公會信誓旦旦的宣稱而降低。人群靜了下來。程觀明確指出展示能證明的事：有限儲槽遭到隱藏。至於同一手法是否用來向村莊收費，仍需發票及證詞才能回答。

A celebrity patron had sponsored the arena, and a crystal display flashed beside his expensive portfolio. He wanted to ban further questions, claiming the travelers' intent was fraud and threatening a lawsuit. The judge asked for evidence instead of theatrical gestures. A healer who had prescribed nutrition to exhausted workers was almost shoved from the stage after someone made a random remark about his prescription.

一名名人贊助者出資興建競技場，昂貴的投資組合旁閃著水晶顯示器。他想禁止追問，宣稱旅人的意圖是詐欺，並威脅提告。法官要求證據，而不是戲劇姿態。曾替疲憊工人開營養處方的醫者，因有人隨口評論處方而險些被推下台。

A guild official angrily demanded that the men apologize for embarrassing the master. Yan offered to repeat the test under their supervision; Cheng offered a public copy of the calculations. The judge appointed a neutral technician to examine the equipment. For once, the loudest voice in the hall did not control what the instruments had recorded.

公會官員憤怒要求兩人為羞辱會長道歉。晏舟願在對方監督下重做測試；程觀則願公開計算副本。法官指派中立技師檢查設備。這一次，大廳裡最大聲的人沒有支配儀器已記下的結果。

The glass arena became unbearably quiet after the technician repeated the failed demonstration. People who had come for entertainment now faced an uncomfortable question: where had the hidden reservoir received its heat? The guild offered to close the event and issue refunds. Cheng agreed that spectators could leave, but the apparatus and its logs had to remain under neutral supervision. A young stage worker said the hidden tank had been added only that morning. She hesitated to testify, fearing that her wages would be reduced. Yan did not press her. When the crowd's noise began to depress her confidence, the judge arranged a private interview with an adviser present. The worker left with control over her own statement, and the glass tank stayed untouched until the inspector could measure it.

技師重複失敗展示後，玻璃競技場安靜得難受。原本來看娛樂的人面對不舒服的問題：暗儲槽的熱從哪裡來？公會提出結束活動並退票。程觀同意觀眾可以離開，但設備與日誌必須留給中立監管。年輕舞台工人說儲槽是當天早上才裝的，卻因怕被扣薪而不敢作證。晏舟沒有逼她。人群噪音漸漸壓低她的信心，法官便安排顧問陪同的私人訪談。工人保有對陳述的控制，玻璃槽則留在原處供檢查員測量。

Yan told the crowd not to poke the unstable rack holding the gauges. During a later session, the technician reversed the flow and showed how plural branches could drain a residential district. Cheng's ultimate request was limited: preserve the equipment and publish the test. It was enough to change the next hearing without pretending that one spectacle had settled the entire case.

晏舟提醒群眾別戳動放量表的不穩架子。後續場次中，技師倒轉水流，展示多條支線如何抽走住宅區的熱。程觀最後的要求很有限：保存設備並公開測試。這足以改變下一次聽證，而不必假裝一場表演就能解決整個案件。

The most difficult part of their work was knowing when not to act. A loose pipe invited immediate repair; a frightened witness invited immediate reassurance; an angry town invited a speech. Yet haste could destroy the very evidence they needed, or leave the next village without heat. They learned to pause just long enough to describe the danger in plain language, identify who could stop the work, and make a record before changing anything. That routine looked unimpressive compared with the grand movements of famous cultivators. Yan no longer minded. Each time a child found a warm place to sleep or an exhausted worker could leave a dangerous machine, the ordinary routine had proved its worth. Cheng stored the signed forms beside their food, where neither man would forget them.

工作最難的部分，是知道什麼時候不該動手。鬆動管子催促人立刻修，受驚證人需要立刻安慰，憤怒城鎮則渴望一場演說。但倉促可能毀掉證據，也可能讓下一村無火可用。兩人學會停下片刻，用白話描述危險、確認誰有權停工，並在改動前留下紀錄。這套程序比著名修士的浩大招式不起眼，晏舟如今卻不介意。每當孩子能睡在暖處、疲憊工人能離開危險機器，普通程序就證明了價值。程觀把簽名表放在食物旁，免得兩人遺忘。

A repair changed more than the sound of a pipe. It changed where people slept, how far they walked for soup, and whether an elderly neighbor could stay at home. Yan began drawing those consequences beside every technical plan. Cheng made sure local residents could challenge the drawing. Their maps grew crowded, but they became much more useful than the elegant pages the guild had once displayed.

一次修理改變的不只是管線聲音，還有居民睡在哪裡、要走多遠才領得到湯，以及年長鄰居能否留在家中。晏舟開始在每張技術圖旁畫出這些後果；程觀確保地方居民有權質疑。圖紙變得擁擠，卻遠比公會昔日展示的漂亮頁面有用。
