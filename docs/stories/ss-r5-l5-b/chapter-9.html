活證人的界線

The Boundary of a Living Witness

The inner gate was guarded by an aging donkey, or ass, whose stubborn refusal to move amused the sentries. A retired bodyguard used the animal to mark the boundary beyond which unofficial visitors could not pass. The lawful witnesses had to comprise two living adults willing to sign the entry form. Luo qualified as a former examiner but could not serve alone. Jiang asked the guard what protection the second witness would receive. Whatever came next would be judged against the same rules, including those that protected someone they disliked.

內門由一頭老驢守著，牠固執地不肯挪動，惹得守衛發笑。一名退休保鑣利用這頭驢標示非官方訪客不能跨越的界線。合法見證人必須由兩名願意簽署入場表的成年活人組成。羅衍有昔日考官資格，卻不能單獨擔任。江沐詢問第二名見證人能獲得什麼保護。無論接下來發生什麼，都要遵守同一套規則，包括保護他們不喜歡的人的規則。

The guard promised not to discriminate between candidates who had lost powers and those still eligible for advancement. He pointed to a container of blue fluid used to keep old ink legible. Jiang wanted to incorporate the guard's promise into the written entry terms. Luo noticed that the form named examiners as supervisors but gave witnesses no right to leave. They insisted the missing exit clause be added before anyone signed. They named the risk before accepting it, and neither treated the other person’s agreement as automatic.

守衛承諾不歧視失去能力的應試者，也不剝奪仍符合晉升資格者的權利。他指出一瓶讓舊墨水仍可辨讀的藍色液體。江沐要求把守衛承諾寫進入場條件。羅衍發現表格把考官列為監督者，卻沒有見證人離場的權利。他們堅持先補上出口條款才簽名。他們先說出風險才決定承擔，誰也沒有把對方的同意視為理所當然。

An investigator from the capital arrived with a passionate speech and a patch showing the bureau's new pyramid emblem. She said the missing memory records belonged to the state. Jiang replied that custodianship did not cancel the candidate's right to refuse publication. Luo accepted her offer to inspect the threshold but refused to let her enter the mirror chamber without an independent witness. She agreed reluctantly, and the three examined the floor together. For now they kept the copy dry and the door unsealed, leaving the next witness free to decide.

都城調查員帶著熱情的演說來到門前，胸口貼著官署新金字塔徽章。她說遺失的記憶紀錄屬於國家。江沐回答，保管權不會取消應試者拒絕公開的權利。羅衍接受她檢查門檻的提議，卻拒絕讓她在沒有獨立見證人時進入鏡房。她勉強同意，三人一起查看地面。眼下他們先把副本保存乾燥，門也不封死，讓下一名證人能自由決定。

In the nearby residence, a survivor wore only one sandal because her injured foot could not bear a tight shoe. She kept a carved souvenir from the trial and met weekly with a therapist who helped her handle nightmares. Jiang asked for her consent before viewing the object. Luo explained that they did not need her medical history, only the date carved beneath its base. She offered the date and kept the rest private. They looked for a second source before moving, since the first account might describe only part of the event. The evening ended without triumph; it ended with a record that tomorrow’s readers could inspect for themselves.

附近住所裡，一名倖存者只穿一隻涼鞋，因為受傷的腳無法承受緊鞋。她保留一件試煉雕刻紀念品，每週也會見治療師，處理惡夢。江沐在觀看物品前先徵求同意。羅衍解釋他們不需要病歷，只需要底座刻下的日期。她提供日期，其餘內容仍保密。兩人出發前先尋找第二份來源，因為第一份證詞也許只描述了事情的一部分。這個夜晚沒有勝利歡呼，只有一份明天的讀者能親自查閱的紀錄。

The investigator offered to undo the revised form, but the guard reminded her that the valid version already carried signatures from both parties. The survivor's date made the official timeline vulnerable to challenge: it showed a gift had been issued three days after the alleged closing. Luo refused to call that proof of a secret prison. Jiang agreed that the next step was modest but powerful: ask the ministry to explain an object whose existence its report had denied. They considered what the record omitted: the names of helpers, the price of delay, and the right to tell another story. The audience heard two possible explanations, and the pair made clear what observation would distinguish one from the other. Jiang Mu stayed because he chose to, and Luo Yan accepted that choice without asking for a promise about tomorrow.

調查員想撤銷新表格，守衛提醒她有效版本已由雙方簽署。倖存者提供的日期讓官方時間線面臨質疑：那件禮物是在據稱關閉後三天才發出。羅衍拒絕因此認定存在祕密監獄。江沐也同意，下一步雖小卻有力：要求官署解釋報告中宣稱不存在的物品。他們也想著紀錄漏掉的部分：幫助者的姓名、拖延的代價，以及訴說另一種故事的權利。聽眾聽見兩種可能解釋，兩人也說明要用哪項觀察來區分。江沐是自己選擇留下，羅衍接受這個選擇，沒有要求他承諾明天的事。
