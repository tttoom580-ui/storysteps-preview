The Chamber of Consent

The second hearing took place in a former training arena after the bureau agreed to lift its temporary ban on independent observers. A celebrity ascension champion arrived wearing a clear crystal pendant and declared that anyone who entered the trial had accepted its costs. Jiang asked him to identify the precise form on which that consent was recorded. The champion could not, and a survivor quietly requested permission to answer instead. The next door remained closed, and for once that delay felt like a choice rather than another punishment.

第二次聽證在昔日訓練競技場舉行，因為官署同意解除暫時禁止獨立觀察者的命令。一名成名的飛升冠軍戴著透明水晶墜飾到場，宣稱凡入試者早已接受代價。江沐要求他指出記載同意的確切表格。冠軍無法回答，一位倖存者輕聲要求由自己回答。下一扇門仍緊閉，但這次的延遲終於像一種選擇，而不是另一種懲罰。

The survivor said the ceremony had been designed to depress doubt through repeated songs and bright lights. She did not accuse every teacher of fraud, but she remembered the intent behind one instruction: nobody could leave after receiving a number. The academy's lawyer warned of a lawsuit if her statement was published. Luo answered that the hearing could record her words under a protected identity, with every allegation marked as testimony rather than established fact. Whatever came next would be judged against the same rules, including those that protected someone they disliked.

倖存者說，儀式用反覆歌曲和強光壓低眾人的疑問。她沒有指控所有老師詐欺，卻記得一條指示的意圖：領取編號後任何人都不能離開。學院律師警告公開證詞會引發訴訟。羅衍回答，可用受保護身分記錄她的話，並把每項指控標明為證詞，而非已證實的事實。無論接下來發生什麼，都要遵守同一套規則，包括保護他們不喜歡的人的規則。

A health official produced a portfolio of notes about nutrition during the examination. In the margin, the word candidate appeared in the plural on one sheet but the singular on another. Jiang began to poke holes in the chronology, pointing to meals delivered for twenty-seven people after the supposed evacuation. The official accepted that the notes were genuine but asked them not to publish names until families had been contacted. They named the risk before accepting it, and neither treated the other person’s agreement as automatic.

衛生官員提出一套關於試煉期間營養供應的資料集。頁邊有一張把應試者寫成複數，另一張卻寫成單數。江沐開始指出時間線的漏洞：據稱撤離後，仍有二十七人份的餐食送入。官員承認筆記真實，但要求通知家屬以前不要公布姓名。他們先說出風險才決定承擔，誰也沒有把對方的同意視為理所當然。

In a residential room beside the arena, medicine shelves stood in a rack beneath a written prescription register. Some labels used random numbers, while others carried the initials of named patients. Luo compared the handwriting rather than opening any bottle. Jiang worried that showing medical details would create another violation. Together they drew a boundary around the material: dates and inventory totals could be examined, diagnoses would remain private. He touched the edge of the damaged ledger, then stopped before the fragile paper could tear beneath his fingers. For now they kept the copy dry and the door unsealed, leaving the next witness free to decide.

競技場旁的住宅區房間裡，藥瓶架擺在書面處方登記冊下方。有些標籤使用隨機數字，有些寫病人姓名縮寫。羅衍只比對字跡，不打開藥瓶。江沐擔心展示醫療細節會構成另一種侵犯。他們一起劃清資料界線：日期與庫存總數可查，診斷必須保密。他輕觸受損帳冊邊緣，隨即停手，以免脆弱紙張在指間裂開。眼下他們先把副本保存乾燥，門也不封死，讓下一名證人能自由決定。

After a tense session, the tribunal refused to reverse the entire seal but ordered the academy to preserve every remaining mirror image. A guard tried to shove reporters away; Luo intervened before anyone fell. Their ultimate goal was not a spectacular opening of the stairs, he reminded the crowd, but an accountable record of who had signed and who had been denied choice. The champion stayed behind and admitted that he, too, had never seen the original consent form. The promise between them was not that they would always agree, but that disagreement would never be treated as betrayal. They looked for a second source before moving, since the first account might describe only part of the event. The evening ended without triumph; it ended with a record that tomorrow’s readers could inspect for themselves.

緊張聽證後，審查庭拒絕全面解除封印，但命令學院保存所有剩餘鏡像。守衛試圖推開記者，羅衍出面阻止，以免有人跌倒。他提醒大家，終極目標不是轟動地重啟石階，而是能交代誰簽了名、誰被剝奪選擇的紀錄。飛升冠軍留下來，承認自己也沒看過原始同意書。他們的約定不是永遠意見一致，而是絕不把分歧視為背叛。兩人出發前先尋找第二份來源，因為第一份證詞也許只描述了事情的一部分。這個夜晚沒有勝利歡呼，只有一份明天的讀者能親自查閱的紀錄。
