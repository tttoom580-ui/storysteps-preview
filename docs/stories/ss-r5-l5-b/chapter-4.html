The Diameter of a Lie

Luo and Jiang tried to anticipate the archive ministry's objections before filing their petition. They discovered that bureaucracy could turn a simple request into twenty separate forms. By coincidence, a commuter from the capital recognized the seal on their map. She warned that the bureau would issue a denial unless they could prove the exact diameter of the forged stamp. Jiang borrowed a lens and began measuring before the office opened. No witness was asked to risk another memory merely to make the investigators feel that their work was moving faster. Jiang Mu returned the borrowed key before the deadline and kept a signed receipt, however inconvenient that seemed. The decision did not solve the mystery, but it prevented a preventable harm, and that mattered to them both.

羅衍與江沐遞交申請前，先試著預料檔案部門會提出什麼異議。他們發現官僚程序能把簡單請求變成二十張表格。巧的是，一名從都城通勤的人認出地圖上的印章。她警告若無法證明偽印的確切直徑，官署就會否認。江沐借來放大鏡，在辦公室開門前開始測量。他們不會要求證人再冒一次失去記憶的風險，只為讓調查者覺得進度更快。江沐在期限前歸還借來的鑰匙，並保留簽收證明，儘管這看來麻煩。這個決定沒有解開謎團，卻避免了一件本可預防的傷害；對兩人來說這同樣重要。

The stamp had a distinctive split line that a university faculty member had documented years earlier. The ministry called the request a fiscal burden, even though the examination record required only a fraction of the office's budget. Luo argued that the original witness faced a greater hazard from forced publicity. Jiang copied the budget report without arguing about motives. He wanted the numbers to remain useful whether the bureau was hostile or merely careless. Several observers wanted an immediate answer, but the pair refused to name a culprit before checking who had access. The two men made separate notes, then exchanged them only after each had finished his own account of the scene. They carried the unanswered question into the next room instead of pretending the first answer had been complete.

印章有一道獨特裂線，一名大學教員數年前便記錄過。部門聲稱查詢會增加財政負擔，雖然所需費用只占預算一小部分。羅衍指出，強迫公開姓名給原證人的危險更大。江沐抄下預算報告，沒有急著推測動機；無論官署是敵視還是粗心，數字都應可受查核。幾名旁觀者想立刻得到答案，兩人卻拒絕在查清誰有權接觸資料前指認犯人。兩人分開記筆記，等各自寫完對現場的描述，才交換比對。他們把未解的問題帶進下一間房，沒有假裝第一個答案已經完整。

A medical practitioner explained why an old candidate's hormone readings could not establish consent: they reflected stress, not a freely chosen decision. A report from the marine province compared the trial's mortality figures to unrelated mountain accidents. Luo placed the reports in parallel columns, showing that the comparison erased important differences. One family made a plea to keep the dead person's identity private until the evidence had been reviewed. For the first time, the abandoned examination felt less like a legend and more like an event involving actual people. They measured the gap twice, drew a clear mark, and refused to continue until the observer agreed with the reading. Their route became longer, yet the evidence would survive even if they later changed their minds about what it meant.

一名醫療專業人員解釋，舊應試者的荷爾蒙數值不能證明同意，因為數值反映壓力，不代表自由決定。來自海洋省份的報告把試煉死亡率與無關的山難比較。羅衍將兩份資料平行排列，指出這樣的比較掩蓋重要差異。一戶家屬懇求在證據審查前保護亡者身分。那場被遺棄的考核第一次不像傳說，而像一件牽涉真實人物的事件。他們量了兩次間距，畫下清楚的標記，直到觀察者確認讀值才繼續。路線因此變長，但即使日後改變對證據的理解，證據本身仍會保留下來。

A sympathetic clerk tried to reassure them, then admitted that no one had yet been permitted to uncover the full chamber record. She brought a policy note establishing that two witnesses could request a supervised mirror viewing without reopening the stairs. Jiang read it three times, searching for a hidden cost. The right was narrow, but it existed, and the clerk agreed to stamp their request with the date of receipt. Even a correct conclusion could do harm if they published private names before the people involved were ready. Luo Yan checked the witness column while Jiang Mu compared the paper with the rubbing he carried in a narrow case. He did not promise that everything would be restored. He promised only that nobody would be erased without being heard.

一名同情他們的書吏想安撫兩人，隨後坦承至今沒有人獲准查閱完整試煉房紀錄。她帶來一則政策註記：兩名見證人可申請在監督下觀看稽核鏡，無須重啟石階。江沐讀了三遍，尋找隱藏代價。權利雖小，卻確實存在；書吏同意在申請書上蓋收件日期。即使結論正確，若在當事人準備好之前公開私人姓名，也可能造成傷害。羅衍核對見證人欄位，江沐則拿狹長匣中的拓印與紙張比對。他沒有承諾一切都能恢復，只承諾不再有人未獲聆聽就遭抹除。

The bureau still refused immediate entry, but it could no longer pretend that a proper petition had not arrived. Luo chose not to accuse the commuting witness of being a spy, even when her name appeared on a ministry visitor list. Jiang proposed a safer test: compare the stamp's damage with an earlier rubbing held by a neutral faculty archivist. The pair left with a hearing date and a question that had become measurable. They checked the wording aloud, because a rule that sounded harmless in a whisper could become cruel when enforced. Before leaving, they repeated the safety condition and confirmed that every participant understood where the exit stood. The next door remained closed, and for once that delay felt like a choice rather than another punishment.

官署仍拒絕立刻准入，卻不能再假裝沒收到正式申請。即使通勤證人的名字出現在部門訪客名單上，羅衍也不願直接指控她是間諜。江沐提出更安全的檢驗：把裂痕與中立大學檔案員保管的舊拓印比對。兩人離開時拿到聽證日期，也得到一個可以量測的問題。他們把條文大聲讀出，因為小聲說來無害的規則，一旦強制執行，可能變得殘酷。離開之前，他們重述安全條件，確認每位參與者都知道出口在哪裡。下一扇門仍緊閉，但這次的延遲終於像一種選擇，而不是另一種懲罰。
