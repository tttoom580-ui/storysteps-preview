The Nominee and the Skeleton

The municipal council placed the trial archive on its weekly agenda, although the chair warned the public to beware of unsupported accusations. Luo found himself clinging to an old federal inspection rule that permitted independent copies of dangerous records. Jiang was less hopeful: he had watched officials praise transparency while locking the only readable door. They agreed to ask for copies first, keeping their next demand small enough to be testable. The old trial had taught people to mistake silence for agreement; both men now insisted that a quiet person could still refuse. Jiang Mu drew the route in the margin, and Luo Yan added the time beside each turn so another team could check it. They named the risk before accepting it, and neither treated the other person’s agreement as automatic.

市議會將試煉檔案排入每週議程，主席卻提醒大眾要提防沒有證據的指控。羅衍緊抓一條舊聯邦稽查規則，因為它容許替危險紀錄製作獨立副本。江沐比較悲觀；他見過官員一邊讚揚透明，一邊鎖起唯一能讀到真相的門。兩人同意先要求副本，把下一個訴求控制在可驗證範圍內。舊試煉讓人誤把沉默當成同意；兩人如今堅持，安靜的人仍然有拒絕的權利。江沐在頁邊畫出路線，羅衍在每個轉折旁標上時間，讓其他人也能查核。他們先說出風險才決定承擔，誰也沒有把對方的同意視為理所當然。

The town theater had once staged the Ascension Trial as a heroic genre, with each chamber announced like an inning in a public game. Jiang called that performance a dangerous metaphor because real people had paid its price. A municipal archivist showed them rehearsal notes in which the final chamber was called a library, not a battlefield. Luo realized the old audience might have misunderstood the trial's purpose even before it was sealed. For now they kept the copy dry and the door unsealed, leaving the next witness free to decide.

鎮上的劇院曾把登天試煉演成英雄傳奇，每一間試煉房都像球賽的一局那樣被宣布。江沐認為這個隱喻很危險，因為真正付出代價的是人。市府檔案員拿出彩排筆記，當中最後一房被稱為圖書室，而不是戰場。羅衍發現，在試煉被封之前，觀眾可能早已誤解它的目的。眼下他們先把副本保存乾燥，門也不封死，讓下一名證人能自由決定。

A council nominee claimed that the examination measured whether an organism could survive pure spirit pressure. Her campaign speech tried to overtake the discussion of missing names by invoking an ancient rebellion. Jiang politely asked whether she had ever entered the archive. She had not. Luo disliked her politics but insisted she could still examine the evidence. The audience heard a disagreement rather than a threat, and the nominee agreed to read the original document. The promise between them was not that they would always agree, but that disagreement would never be treated as betrayal. They looked for a second source before moving, since the first account might describe only part of the event. The evening ended without triumph; it ended with a record that tomorrow’s readers could inspect for themselves.

一位議會候選人聲稱，考試是在測量生物能否承受純粹靈壓。她在競選演說中提起古代叛亂，試圖蓋過姓名失蹤的討論。江沐禮貌地問她是否進過檔案室；答案是否。羅衍不認同她的立場，仍堅持她有權查證。觀眾聽見的是分歧，不是威脅，候選人答應閱讀原件。他們的約定不是永遠意見一致，而是絕不把分歧視為背叛。兩人出發前先尋找第二份來源，因為第一份證詞也許只描述了事情的一部分。這個夜晚沒有勝利歡呼，只有一份明天的讀者能親自查閱的紀錄。

Behind the theater they found a training model: a wooden skeleton whose ribs were fastened by a rigid metal frame. It was designed to shield a student's body while the audit mirror recorded movement. The equipment suggested that the original trial had once included nonlethal practice, a detail missing from official accounts. Jiang copied the maker's mark while Luo checked that no one had replaced the frame with a later exhibit. They considered what the record omitted: the names of helpers, the price of delay, and the right to tell another story. The audience heard two possible explanations, and the pair made clear what observation would distinguish one from the other. Jiang Mu stayed because he chose to, and Luo Yan accepted that choice without asking for a promise about tomorrow.

劇院後方有一具訓練模型：木製骷髏的肋骨由僵硬金屬架固定，原本用來保護學生身體，讓稽核鏡記錄動作。這件設備顯示最初的試煉可能包含不致命的練習，官方說明卻完全沒有提到。江沐拓下製作者標記，羅衍則確認骨架沒有被後來的展品替換。他們也想著紀錄漏掉的部分：幫助者的姓名、拖延的代價，以及訴說另一種故事的權利。聽眾聽見兩種可能解釋，兩人也說明要用哪項觀察來區分。江沐是自己選擇留下，羅衍接受這個選擇，沒有要求他承諾明天的事。

An older spectator began to sob when she saw the skeleton. She had taught a course on theology and insisted the trial was never meant to demand blind sacrifice. She also remembered a toxic cleaning mixture that smelled sharply of vinegar, used to erase inscriptions from the practice walls. Luo asked for the purchase register instead of treating the memory as proof. Jiang promised the woman she would not become a symbol without consent. They signed below the witnesses, not above them, because the people who bore the cost had to remain visible.

一名年長觀眾看見模型時開始啜泣。她曾教授神學課程，堅稱試煉從不該要求盲目犧牲。她還記得一種有毒清潔藥劑，聞起來像刺鼻的醋，曾被用來清除練習牆上的字。羅衍索取採購簿，沒有直接把記憶當證明。江沐答應不會未經同意就把她塑造成象徵。他們把名字簽在見證人下方，而不是上方，因為承受代價的人必須被看見。
