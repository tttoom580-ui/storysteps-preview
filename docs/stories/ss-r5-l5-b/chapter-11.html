The First Mirror Opens

封存試煉：未被允許遺忘的人 · 第 11 / 25 章 · 4000–5000 · 英中對照 · 校訂中

At the institute's first supervised viewing, Luo proved apt at identifying old control marks, though the custodian acted as if they had entered a casino rather than a public archive. She tried to characterize every damaged record as deliberate deception. A clinical specialist stopped her, explaining that deterioration could result from age or handling. Jiang insisted on inspecting the mirror's edge before anyone was allowed to switch it on. The silence after the discovery was not empty; it gave each person time to understand what might change. Luo Yan asked whether Jiang Mu wanted him beside the door. A quiet yes was enough, and he stayed. A second chance meant nothing if its terms could not be refused, so they wrote the conditions in plain language.

在研究所第一次監督觀看時，羅衍擅長辨認舊控制記號，保管員的態度卻像眾人闖進賭場，而非公共檔案室。她試圖把每份損壞紀錄都描述成故意欺騙。臨床專家制止她，解釋老化或搬運也會造成損傷。江沐堅持開鏡之前先檢查邊框。發現之後的沉默並非空白，而是讓每個人理解接下來可能改變什麼。羅衍問江沐是否要他守在門邊；江沐輕聲說好，羅衍就留下。若不能拒絕條件，第二次機會就毫無意義，因此他們把條件寫成易懂文字。

An archivist raised a copyright concern about making images of the mirror. Jiang said the purpose was evidence preservation, not commercial publication. A moving line inside the glass began to fascinate the observers, who gasped when a woman's hand appeared beside a sealed form. The custodian identified her as the heir to the old academy. Luo could not confirm the face, but the ring on the hand matched a photograph from the clinic. The test of their partnership was whether each could keep his own goal while still protecting the other person’s choice. Jiang Mu asked to read the sentence again. Luo Yan did, more slowly, and left room for a different interpretation. There was no shortcut through another person’s suffering; they followed the measured path and kept the lamps lit.

檔案員提出拍攝稽核鏡可能涉及著作權的疑慮。江沐說拍攝是為了保全證據，不是商業出版。玻璃裡一道移動的光線吸引觀察者，當封存表格旁出現女人的手時，眾人倒抽一口氣。保管員說她是舊學院繼承人。羅衍無法確認臉孔，但手上的戒指與診所照片吻合。這段搭檔關係的考驗，是兩人能否保有自己的目標，同時尊重對方的選擇。江沐要求再讀一次句子；羅衍放慢速度朗讀，也容許另一種解釋。不該拿別人的苦難當捷徑；他們沿實測路線前進，讓燈持續亮著。

From the outer yard came a long howl, and someone suggested that a spirit beast was warning them away. The mirror remained intact, its last recorded action showing a signature attached after the room had been cleared. The clinical specialist did not recommend immediate entry into the next chamber. A former student asked for refuge from the reporters outside, and Jiang walked her to a private waiting room without asking for her history. In the margin, a small blank space remained for someone whose account had not yet been collected.

外院傳來長長的嚎叫，有人認為靈獸在警告他們離開。稽核鏡依舊完整，最後紀錄的動作卻顯示一張簽名表是在清場後才被貼上。臨床專家不建議立即進入下一房。一名昔日學生想避開外頭記者，江沐沒有追問她的過去，便帶她到私密候客室。頁邊留下一小塊空白，準備給尚未收錄的證詞。

One guard carried a ceremonial rifle that had never been loaded; his cheek bore a scar from the old examination. The sheer height of the mirror made the viewers feel small, but Luo refused to treat anyone as a spectator at a performance. He asked the guard whether he remembered the order to clear the chamber. The man did, but would testify only if the academy stopped using his injury in its advertisements. Before sleep they compared their notes, corrected one mistake each, and decided not to hide either correction.

一名守衛扛著從未上彈的儀仗步槍，臉頰留有舊試煉疤痕。稽核鏡高得令人感到渺小，羅衍卻拒絕把任何人當成表演觀眾。他詢問守衛是否記得清場命令。守衛記得，但只有在學院不再用他的傷作廣告時才願作證。睡前兩人比對筆記，各自修正一個錯誤，並決定不隱瞞任何修改。

Before leaving, Jiang noticed that a workshop invoice listed an upgrade to the mirror's recording mechanism after the alleged closure. Another entry reported theft of a small lens, while a later receipt showed a wheelchair brought into the sealed wing. Those details could point to maintenance or unauthorized use; neither explanation was secure yet. Luo requested copies of both records. Outside, he asked Jiang whether seeing the signature had changed his judgment. Jiang said it had changed his questions. A person could be brave and frightened at the same time; the task was to choose with open eyes. They could have concealed their disagreement, but instead stated both views to the witnesses and invited correction. They could not control the entire institution, but they could control whether their own account was faithful.

離開前，江沐發現工坊發票列出一項在據稱封閉後才進行的稽核鏡升級。另一條紀錄報告一枚小鏡片失竊，稍後的收據又顯示有人把輪椅送入封閉側翼。這些可能是維修，也可能是未授權使用，尚不能確定。羅衍要求複製兩份紀錄。出門後，他問江沐看到簽名是否改變判斷；江沐說，改變的是他想問的問題。一個人可以同時勇敢又恐懼；重要的是睜大眼睛作出選擇。他們本可隱藏歧見，卻向證人坦承兩種看法，並邀請對方指正。他們無法掌控整個制度，卻能決定自己的敘述是否忠實。
