會改寫姓名的山路

The Road That Changes Names

封存試煉：未被允許遺忘的人 · 第 2 / 25 章 · 4000–5000 · 英中對照

用互動版查詞、朗讀與收藏

The most beneficial route to the old records crossed a ridge where stone markers could convert a traveler's written name into a gate number. A corporate foundation had purchased the land below, promising a museum as the destination of its redevelopment. Luo found the purchase suspicious, but Jiang warned him that a contract alone proved no crime. Their first errand was to retrieve the land map without stepping on the marked stones.

前往舊紀錄最有利的路線必須翻過山脊；沿途石標會把旅人的書面姓名轉換成門號。一家企業基金會買下山下土地，聲稱改建後的終點是博物館。羅衍懷疑交易有問題，江沐卻提醒他，單憑合約不能證明犯罪。兩人的第一項差事，是不踩上石標地取回土地圖。

At the hill clinic, an epidemic of lost sleep had spread among former candidates, yet one elderly attendant displayed an extraordinary memory for physical routes. She helped them haul a chest of maps from a locked cellar. The foundation denied liability for the clinic's closure, and its lawyer urged them to leave. Luo felt a sudden plunge in confidence when the attendant called him by his brother's name; she had known both men before the trial.

山間診所裡，昔日應試者正普遍受到失眠症困擾；一名年長侍者卻對路線保有非凡記憶。她協助兩人從鎖著的地窖拖出裝地圖的箱子。基金會否認對診所關閉負有責任，律師也催他們離開。侍者突然喊出羅衍弟弟的名字時，羅衍信心陡然下墜；原來她在試煉前就認識兄弟倆。

Jiang attached a reminder to the chest: nobody could treat the map as testimony until its source was confirmed. The first respondent at the clinic remembered a bell on the eastern ridge, whereas a broken sensor there recorded three bells. Jiang asked both accounts to remain visible. The temptation to dismiss the respondent was strong, but Luo knew a mistaken witness could still lead them toward a real object that the officials had ignored.

江沐在箱子上貼上提醒：來源確認之前，不能把地圖當成證詞。診所的第一位受訪者記得東方山脊上有一座鐘，壞掉的感測器卻記錄過三次鐘響。江沐要求兩種說法都保留。羅衍原想否定受訪者，最後明白即使記憶有誤，也可能帶他們找到官員忽略的實物。

In the kitchen the attendant served a bowl of stew while Luo repaired a torch that used a harmless blue flame. She described the old examination as a transformation of fear into obedience, not the ascent promised on its posters. Jiang carefully trimmed the damaged map's edges and found a second date beneath a pasted label. The woman became vocal when she saw it: that was the day she had been ordered to burn the attendance roll.

廚房裡，侍者端上燉菜；羅衍則修好一支使用無害藍焰的火把。她說舊考試帶來的轉變不是海報宣稱的飛升，而是讓恐懼變成服從。江沐小心修整地圖破邊，發現貼紙下面另有日期。侍者看到後大聲說，那正是她被命令焚燒簽到名冊的一天。

Before the chest went back downstairs, the attendant asked Luo to carry it through the laundry yard. The front path crossed a strip of numbered stone that she would not approach. Luo had assumed her hesitation came from age; now he saw how carefully she placed each foot along the unmarked edge. He moved a washing bench so she could pass without brushing the stones. Inside the chest, one map had a crease running through the earlier date. Jiang laid a clean sheet over it and made a light pencil tracing, leaving the original label attached. Removing it completely would have made a clearer picture but destroyed the overlap that showed which date came first. The attendant watched him work, then corrected the location of the cellar on his sketch. She had hidden the chest behind flour sacks during the clinic's last inspection. Luo wrote down where she had found it and who had helped her move it. He did not repeat his brother's name. She had already apologized once, and he did not want her confusion to become another burden she carried for him.

箱子送回樓下前，侍者請羅衍從曬衣院搬過去。前路橫著一道刻號石帶，她不願接近。羅衍原以為她因年老才遲疑，這時才注意到她每一步都小心踩在無字邊沿。他搬開洗衣凳，讓她不必擦過石頭。箱裡有張地圖的摺痕穿過較早的日期。江沐覆上乾淨紙張輕輕描摹，保留原貼紙。全撕下雖會更清楚，卻會破壞能判斷日期先後的重疊處。侍者看了一會兒，修正草圖上地窖的位置：診所最後一次受檢時，她曾把箱子藏在麵粉袋後。羅衍記下發現地點和幫忙搬運的人。他沒再提弟弟的名字；她已經道過一次歉，他不想讓她的誤認變成替他背負的另一個重擔。

The foundation offered a reward for the maps, calling them obsolete property. Jiang chose to photograph every seal first. Luo carried the original back to the clinic instead of risking the ridge after dark. Their small success was not the discovery of a secret master, but evidence that someone had altered an attendance date. The attendant agreed to testify only if her sleeping patients would not be identified; both investigators accepted that limit.

基金會出價收購地圖，稱它們已是過時財產。江沐選擇先拍下每道印記，羅衍則把原件送回診所，不冒險在天黑後越過山脊。他們的小小進展不是找到神祕主使，而是確認有人修改簽到日期。侍者答應作證，條件是不能揭露失眠病患身分；兩人都接受。
