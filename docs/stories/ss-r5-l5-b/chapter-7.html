學徒書脊上的線

The Spine of the Apprentice's Book

封存試煉：未被允許遺忘的人 · 第 7 / 25 章 · 4000–5000 · 英中對照 · 校訂完成

用互動版查詞、朗讀與收藏

Jiang visited the academy's backyard to examine a book that the monastery had acquired after the sealing. Its acquisition record included a bonus paid to the finder and an invoice for calcium-based ink. A former sophomore student, now an adult bookbinder, remembered the volume arriving wrapped in wet cloth. She let them photograph the cover but required them to leave the fragile object in her custody. They wrote down exactly what they knew, rather than letting a convincing guess become a fact that no one could challenge. A clerk offered a shortcut; they requested the ordinary route in writing, including the name of whoever would approve it. At sunset they agreed on one limited next step, with a clear point at which either of them could call a halt.

江沐到學院後院查看一本寺院在試煉封閉後取得的書。取得紀錄列有給發現者的獎金，以及購買含鈣墨水的發票。一名當年就讀二年級、如今已成年的裝訂師記得那本書裹著濕布送來。她允許兩人拍封面，卻要求脆弱的原件必須留在她手上。他們記下確實知道的事，不讓看似合理的猜測變成無人能質疑的事實。一名書吏提供捷徑；他們要求書面載明正常程序，以及核准者的姓名。日落時，他們只同意下一個有限步驟，並清楚約定任一方何時能要求停止。

The book contained a chunk of colonial history copied into a modern examination manual. Luo could comprehend the rules but not why someone had inserted an unrelated tax table. Jiang did not disapprove of old methods simply because they were old; he questioned the alteration because two kinds of ink crossed the same date. Under angled light, one page revealed a name scraped away and replaced by a printed number. The decision did not solve the mystery, but it prevented a preventable harm, and that mattered to them both.

書裡有一大段殖民時代史，被抄進現代試煉手冊。羅衍看得懂規則，卻不懂為何有人插入一張無關的稅表。江沐並不是因為方法古老就反對，而是兩種墨水跨越同一日期，顯示有改寫。斜光下，一頁露出遭刮除的姓名，上面被印上數字代替。這個決定沒有解開謎團，卻避免了一件本可預防的傷害；對兩人來說這同樣重要。

The implication was serious: the authority might have changed the rules after candidates entered. A pointed instrument had been used to penetrate the top layer of paper, leaving a pattern that matched a former republican office stamp. Luo said the accusation was risky without a second original. Jiang agreed, then found a repair note confirming that the same paper stock had been ordered in two different years. They carried the unanswered question into the next room instead of pretending the first answer had been complete.

這個跡象含意重大：管理機關可能在應試者入場後才改動規則。一支尖銳工具曾刺穿紙張表層，留下的圖樣與昔日共和政體官署的印章相符。羅衍說，沒有第二份原件就作出指控太危險。江沐同意，隨後找到修復註記，證實同種紙材曾於不同年份下訂。他們把未解的問題帶進下一間房，沒有假裝第一個答案已經完整。

A scrap of thread caught between the pages matched the binding on a spacious storage case. The bookbinder placed it beside the book's spine rather than removing it. The apprentice who first carried the volume had been a sophomore, not an authorized keeper; his signature alone could not authenticate the contents. Luo made a careful stack of authenticated receipts, keeping the suspicious items separate until their order of custody could be established. Their route became longer, yet the evidence would survive even if they later changed their minds about what it meant.

頁間夾著一小片線頭，與一個寬大存放箱的裝訂線相同。裝訂師將它放在書脊旁，沒有立刻取出。最早搬運此書的學徒當年只是二年級生，不是正式保管者；他的簽名不足以證明內容真偽。羅衍把已驗證收據整齊疊好，暫時將可疑物分開，等待確認保管順序。路線因此變長，但即使日後改變對證據的理解，證據本身仍會保留下來。

The academy's surveillance office had kept a symbolic map of every parcel's transit, though its camera footage was lost. Jiang traced the route to an unused examination wing. The map did not prove anyone had entered that wing, but it identified a clerk who had signed for delivery. They requested an interview, and the bookbinder chose to accompany them as a witness. For once, the evidence moved without being taken away from its rightful keeper. Even a correct conclusion could do harm if they published private names before the people involved were ready. Luo Yan checked the witness column while Jiang Mu compared the paper with the rubbing he carried in a narrow case. He did not promise that everything would be restored. He promised only that nobody would be erased without being heard.

學院監控室保留一份象徵性標示各包裹轉運路徑的地圖，雖然監視影像已遺失。江沐追查到一座停用考場側翼。地圖不能證明有人進入，卻指出哪位書吏簽收貨物。他們要求訪談，裝訂師也自願作為見證人同行。這一次，證據在不被原保管者奪走的情況下前進了一步。即使結論正確，若在當事人準備好之前公開私人姓名，也可能造成傷害。羅衍核對見證人欄位，江沐則拿狹長匣中的拓印與紙張比對。他沒有承諾一切都能恢復，只承諾不再有人未獲聆聽就遭抹除。
