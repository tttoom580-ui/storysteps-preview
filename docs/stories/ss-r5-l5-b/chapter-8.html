The Bazaar of Borrowed Memories

封存試煉：未被允許遺忘的人 · 第 8 / 25 章 · 4000–5000 · 英中對照 · 校訂中

In a bazaar outside the examination wall, merchants sold stories that sounded alien to the people who had lived them. The crowd fell into chaos when a buyer claimed his purchase entitled him to a surviving candidate's childhood memories. Luo demanded proof of ownership, while Jiang asked whether the buyer understood what he was buying. True comprehension could not come from a label stamped onto a sealed jar. The test of their partnership was whether each could keep his own goal while still protecting the other person’s choice. Jiang Mu asked to read the sentence again. Luo Yan did, more slowly, and left room for a different interpretation. There was no shortcut through another person’s suffering; they followed the measured path and kept the lamps lit.

考場外的市集販賣著對親歷者而言十分陌生的故事。一名買家聲稱購買物品便有權取得某位倖存應試者的童年記憶，人群立刻陷入混亂。羅衍要求所有權證據，江沐則問買家是否理解自己買的是什麼。真正的理解，不可能只靠封罐上的標籤取得。這段搭檔關係的考驗，是兩人能否保有自己的目標，同時尊重對方的選擇。江沐要求再讀一次句子；羅衍放慢速度朗讀，也容許另一種解釋。不該拿別人的苦難當捷徑；他們沿實測路線前進，讓燈持續亮著。

The bazaar's organizer praised flexibility but refused a public gathering about consent. Jiang proposed a quieter meeting to minimize the danger to vulnerable sellers. A fortune reader repeated a myth that the trial stairs could restore anyone's forgotten name. Luo corrected her: the written memory taken by the stairs could not be returned by wish or ritual. The woman surprised him by changing her sign instead of arguing. In the margin, a small blank space remained for someone whose account had not yet been collected.

市集主辦人讚揚彈性，卻拒絕舉辦討論同意權的公開聚會。江沐建議改成小型會談，降低賣家遭受風險。占卜者重述一則神話，說試煉石階能恢復被遺忘的姓名。羅衍糾正她：石階奪走的書面記憶，無法靠願望或儀式取回。她沒有爭辯，反而修改招牌。頁邊留下一小塊空白，準備給尚未收錄的證詞。

A ragged traveler offered them a clue in a tea salon. He had worked at the hall during a shortage of safe lamps and remembered the color spectrum of the mirror's warning beam. Jiang found a matching description in the bookbinder's manual. The traveler asked for food, not money, and refused to let his face appear in any public report. Luo accepted the condition without pressing for a heroic portrait. Before sleep they compared their notes, corrected one mistake each, and decided not to hide either correction.

一名衣衫襤褸的旅人在茶沙龍提供線索。他曾在安全燈不足時於大殿工作，記得稽核鏡警示光束的色譜。江沐在裝訂師的手冊裡找到吻合描述。旅人只要求食物，不要金錢，也拒絕在公開報告露臉。羅衍接受這個條件，不強求塑造英雄形象。睡前兩人比對筆記，各自修正一個錯誤，並決定不隱瞞任何修改。

A dealer tried to stall the interview, arguing that the clues held strategic value. Jiang demanded that the dealer suspend all sales of the marked jars until their sources could be checked. The dealer answered with a theoretical claim about ownership, but he could not produce a single signed transfer record. Luo asked two customers to witness a temporary seal. This kept the disputed objects intact without declaring every merchant guilty. They could not control the entire institution, but they could control whether their own account was faithful.

一名商人想拖延訪談，說線索具有策略價值。江沐要求來源核實前暫停販售有標記的罐子。商人提出一套理論上的所有權說法，卻拿不出任何簽署過的移轉紀錄。羅衍請兩名顧客見證暫時封存。這讓爭議物品保持原狀，也沒有把所有商人都當罪犯。他們無法掌控整個制度，卻能決定自己的敘述是否忠實。

The old traveler asked for no tribute. He showed them a thin blue vein of ink inside a broken jar and a visitor visa issued before the trial closed. Neither document proved that memories were stored there; the jar might have been merely decorative. Yet the visa placed a forgotten inspector at the site, and that was not nothing whatsoever. Jiang offered the man a choice: testify privately or simply keep his story. He chose the first. The simplest question remained the hardest: who had been allowed to choose, and who had merely been told to obey? A warning bell sounded once. They stepped back, checked the floor, and resumed only when the signal was explained. The question of trust had changed: it was no longer who seemed pure, but who allowed a record to be checked.

老旅人不要求任何貢品。他指出破罐內一道細細的藍色墨脈，以及試煉關閉前發出的訪客簽證。兩件物品都不能證明罐內存著記憶，罐子也可能只是裝飾；但簽證確實顯示一位被遺忘的稽查員到過現場，這並非毫無價值。江沐讓旅人選擇私下作證或保留故事，他選擇前者。最簡單的問題仍然最難：誰曾被允許選擇，誰只是被命令服從？警鐘響了一聲；他們先後退、檢查地面，確認訊號意義後才繼續。信任的問題已經改變：不再是誰看起來純潔，而是誰願意讓紀錄接受檢查。
