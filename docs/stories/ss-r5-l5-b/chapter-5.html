At the fifth-day hearing, the tribunal announced that its constitutional rules protected an applicant's right to examine public records. One democrat on the council called this an embarrassing episode, while another accused the investigators of exaggeration. Outside, a fleet of delivery wagons waited for a merchant's shipment. Jiang saw a sealed box among them and feared that greed for the trial's rare instruments would outrun the search for truth. One torn page could mislead an entire room, so they placed it beside the original seal and checked the date twice. One offered to carry the heavy case, and the other accepted without turning the gesture into a debt. In the margin, a small blank space remained for someone whose account had not yet been collected.

第五天聽證時，審查庭宣布憲制規則保障申請人查閱公共紀錄的權利。一名支持民主的議員說這是令人難堪的事件，另一人則指控調查者誇大其詞。門外，一列運貨車等著商人的貨物。江沐看見其中一箱封條，擔心有人貪圖試煉珍器的利益，遠比查清真相積極。一張殘頁就可能誤導整間屋子，因此他們將它放在原印旁，仔細核對了兩次日期。一人主動提議替對方提沉重的箱子，另一人坦然接受，沒有把體貼變成欠債。頁邊留下一小塊空白，準備給尚未收錄的證詞。

An elderly peasant brought an herb bundle for the former candidates, refusing the incentive offered by a powerful collector to stay silent. The collector's guards stood beside a massive locked trunk, and metal fittings began to rattle when someone shifted it. Luo asked for a count of every item removed from the hall. He knew an inventory could not prove who had ordered the substitution, but it could stop further objects from disappearing unrecorded. They separated the evidence from their anger, although anger was often the only thing that kept them on their feet. They left a copy in public custody and another with the witnesses, so no single official could silently replace both. Before sleep they compared their notes, corrected one mistake each, and decided not to hide either correction.

一位年長農人帶來一包草藥給昔日應試者，拒絕一名有權勢收藏家提供的噤聲獎勵。收藏家的警衛站在巨大鎖箱旁，有人移動箱子時金屬扣件嘎嘎作響。羅衍要求逐件清點移出大殿的物品。他知道清單不能證明誰指使調包，但至少能防止物品在毫無紀錄下繼續消失。他們努力把證據與憤怒分開，儘管憤怒時常是支撐他們站穩的唯一力量。他們把一份副本交公家保管，另一份留給證人，避免任何一名官員悄悄替換兩份紀錄。睡前兩人比對筆記，各自修正一個錯誤，並決定不隱瞞任何修改。

The hearing took place during a recession, and several clerks feared losing their jobs if the bureau's payments stopped. Jiang proposed to recruit independent conservators rather than threaten the staff. A retail dealer confirmed that a forged seal had been offered for sale; he refused to name the buyer without protection. Luo nearly let frustration smash his patience, but he used practical tactics: preserve the sales receipt and request a protected interview. A person could be brave and frightened at the same time; the task was to choose with open eyes. They could have concealed their disagreement, but instead stated both views to the witnesses and invited correction. They could not control the entire institution, but they could control whether their own account was faithful.

聽證時正值經濟衰退，幾名書吏擔心部門付款停止會失業。江沐提議招募獨立修復師，而不是威脅職員。一名零售商證實有人曾兜售偽造印章，卻不願在缺乏保護時透露買家。羅衍差點因焦躁失去耐心，最後改採具體策略：保全收據，再申請受保護的訪談。一個人可以同時勇敢又恐懼；重要的是睜大眼睛作出選擇。他們本可隱藏歧見，卻向證人坦承兩種看法，並邀請對方指正。他們無法掌控整個制度，卻能決定自己的敘述是否忠實。

The judges found substantial reason to suspect a changed record but said the threshold for blaming any individual had not been reached. Their verdict granted access to one original sheet and required a neutral witness. The decision was less than the families had hoped for, yet it corrected a widespread claim that all evidence had been destroyed. Jiang laid his mother's rubbing beside the returned page. The split line of the stamp matched perfectly. The simplest question remained the hardest: who had been allowed to choose, and who had merely been told to obey? A warning bell sounded once. They stepped back, checked the floor, and resumed only when the signal was explained. The question of trust had changed: it was no longer who seemed pure, but who allowed a record to be checked.

審查者認為有充分理由懷疑紀錄遭改動，但指認個人的證明門檻尚未達到。裁定准許查閱一張原頁，並要求中立見證人。這不如家屬期待的多，卻修正了「所有證據都已燒毀」的廣泛說法。江沐將母親的拓印放在歸還的紙張旁，印章裂線完全吻合。最簡單的問題仍然最難：誰曾被允許選擇，誰只是被命令服從？警鐘響了一聲；他們先後退、檢查地面，確認訊號意義後才繼續。信任的問題已經改變：不再是誰看起來純潔，而是誰願意讓紀錄接受檢查。

A faint pencil mark beneath the page contained two surnames. One belonged to Jiang's mother; the other belonged to the deputy mentioned by the creek clerk. Luo wanted to announce the discovery, but Jiang asked to wait until both dates had been checked. Their first five days had produced a lawful entry point, a preserved page, and an alliance that neither man had expected. The next question was why the genuine seal had been used on a false instruction. Every repaired line on the map offered a possibility, not a guarantee, and their next decision had to respect that difference. For a moment their hands met over the same page. Neither pulled away, but the conversation stayed focused on the case. When the bell fell silent, each man asked the other whether to proceed. This time both answers were freely given.

紙張下方的淡鉛筆記號寫著兩個姓氏：一個屬於江沐的母親，另一個就是溪邊書吏提過的副手。羅衍想公布發現，江沐要求先核對兩個日期。五天之內，他們取得合法查閱途徑、保住一頁真跡，也建立了原本沒想到的合作。下一個問題是：為何真印章會蓋在假命令上？地圖上每條修復的線都只是可能性，不是保證；下一個決定必須尊重這個差別。兩人的手一度在同一頁紙上碰到；誰都沒有退開，談話卻仍聚焦於案件。鐘聲停下後，兩人互問是否繼續；這一次，兩個答案都是自由作出的。
