The First Page Returned

封存試煉：未被允許遺忘的人 · 第 5 / 25 章 · 4000–5000 · 英中對照

用互動版查詞、朗讀與收藏

At the fifth-day hearing, the tribunal announced that its constitutional rules protected an applicant's right to examine public records. One democrat on the council called this an embarrassing episode, while another accused the investigators of exaggeration. Outside, a fleet of delivery wagons waited for a merchant's shipment. Jiang saw a sealed box among them and feared that greed for the trial's rare instruments would outrun the search for truth.

第五天聽證時，審查庭宣布憲制規則保障申請人查閱公共紀錄的權利。一名支持民主的議員說這是令人難堪的事件，另一人則指控調查者誇大其詞。門外，一列運貨車等著商人的貨物。江沐看見其中一箱封條，擔心有人貪圖試煉珍器的利益，遠比查清真相積極。

An elderly peasant brought an herb bundle for the former candidates, refusing the incentive offered by a powerful collector to stay silent. The collector's guards stood beside a massive locked trunk, and metal fittings began to rattle when someone shifted it. Luo asked for a count of every item removed from the hall. He knew an inventory could not prove who had ordered the substitution, but it could stop further objects from disappearing unrecorded.

一位年長農人帶來一包草藥給昔日應試者，拒絕一名有權勢收藏家提供的噤聲獎勵。收藏家的警衛站在巨大鎖箱旁，有人移動箱子時金屬扣件嘎嘎作響。羅衍要求逐件清點移出大殿的物品。他知道清單不能證明誰指使調包，但至少能防止物品在毫無紀錄下繼續消失。

The hearing took place during a recession, and several clerks feared losing their jobs if the bureau's payments stopped. Jiang proposed to recruit independent conservators rather than threaten the staff. A retail dealer confirmed that a forged seal had been offered for sale; he refused to name the buyer without protection. Luo nearly let frustration smash his patience, but he used practical tactics: preserve the sales receipt and request a protected interview.

聽證時正值經濟衰退，幾名書吏擔心部門付款停止會失業。江沐提議招募獨立修復師，而不是威脅職員。一名零售商證實有人曾兜售偽造印章，卻不願在缺乏保護時透露買家。羅衍差點因焦躁失去耐心，最後改採具體策略：保全收據，再申請受保護的訪談。

The judges found substantial reason to suspect a changed record but said the threshold for blaming any individual had not been reached. Their verdict granted access to one original sheet and required a neutral witness. The decision was less than the families had hoped for, yet it corrected a widespread claim that all evidence had been destroyed. Jiang laid his mother's rubbing beside the returned page. The split line of the stamp matched perfectly.

審查者認為有充分理由懷疑紀錄遭改動，但指認個人的證明門檻尚未達到。裁定准許查閱一張原頁，並要求中立見證人。這不如家屬期待的多，卻修正了「所有證據都已燒毀」的廣泛說法。江沐將母親的拓印放在歸還的紙張旁，印章裂線完全吻合。

A faint pencil mark beneath the page contained two surnames. One belonged to Jiang's mother; the other belonged to the deputy mentioned by the creek clerk.

紙張下方的淡鉛筆記號寫著兩個姓氏：一個屬於江沐的母親，另一個就是溪邊書吏提過的副手。

The neutral witness arrived with a shallow tray lined in plain cloth. Before the returned sheet touched it, she asked Jiang to show which edge had been scorched and which had been cut. His mother's rubbing preserved a corner that the original no longer possessed. Luo began to point at the surnames, but the witness stopped his hand above the paper. A shadow from his sleeve was making the pencil marks look darker than they were. They moved the lamp and photographed the page from directly overhead. The second surname was still readable; one stroke in the first remained uncertain. Jiang asked that the uncertainty appear on the image label, although it concerned his own mother. Outside, the delivery men were loading the sealed box he had noticed earlier. Luo took its inventory number to the clerk instead of pursuing the wagon. It matched an authorized shipment of empty display frames. The discovery relieved him more than he expected. There was enough wrong with the archive without turning every departing box into proof of a crime.

中立見證人帶著鋪素布的淺盤到場。原頁放上去前，她請江沐指出哪邊燒焦、哪邊裁切。母親的拓印還保留著原頁已缺失的一角。羅衍想指姓氏，見證人把他的手攔在紙面上方：袖子的陰影讓鉛筆痕顯得比實際深。他們移燈，從正上方拍照。第二個姓仍可讀，第一個有一筆卻不確定。江沐要求影像標籤註明疑點，即使關係到自己母親。外頭搬運工正把他先前留意的封箱裝車。羅衍沒有追車，而是拿箱號去問書吏，發現那是經核准運走的空展示框。他意外地鬆了一口氣。檔案已經有夠多問題，不必把每個出門的箱子都當成犯罪證明。

Luo wanted to announce the discovery, but Jiang asked to wait until both dates had been checked. Their first five days had produced a lawful entry point, a preserved page, and an alliance that neither man had expected. The next question was why the genuine seal had been used on a false instruction.

羅衍想公布發現，江沐要求先核對兩個日期。五天之內，他們取得合法查閱途徑、保住一頁真跡，也建立了原本沒想到的合作。下一個問題是：為何真印章會蓋在假命令上？
