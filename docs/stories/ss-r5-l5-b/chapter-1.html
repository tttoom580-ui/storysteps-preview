灰燼下的名冊

The Roll Beneath the Ash

封存試煉：未被允許遺忘的人 · 第 1 / 25 章 · 4000–5000 · 英中對照

Luo Yan did not come to the mountain chapel for comfort. He came because an announcement promised certainty about the sealed Ascension Trial, eleven years after twenty-seven candidates entered and their names vanished from public rolls. A cluster of families stood outside the door. The bureau offered compensation for lost belongings, never for the memories its examination had taken. Luo recognized the handwriting of his younger brother in the notice's margin.

羅衍來到山上禮拜堂，不是為了尋求安慰。他是因為一份公告聲稱能確定十一年前封存的登天試煉真相：二十七名應試者入場後，姓名便從公開名冊消失。門外聚集一群家屬。官署只願補償遺失物品，卻不提試煉奪走的記憶。羅衍認出公告頁邊是弟弟的字跡。

Jiang Mu, a thirty-year-old restorer of paper rubbings, asked the officials to disclose the inventory before the public forum began. The young clerks were envious of his access to private collections, though that access had never restored his mother's reputation. A generator behind the lectern powered the truth-display screen, but the screen showed only a polished summary. Jiang carried the scorched original from his mother's workshop and refused to surrender it.

三十歲的拓印修復師江沐要求官員在公開論壇開始前揭露庫存清單。年輕書吏羨慕他能接觸私人收藏，卻不知道這種便利始終無法替他母親洗清污名。講臺後的發電機供應真相展示屏電力，螢幕卻只播放經過修飾的摘要。江沐帶著母親工坊留下的燒焦原件，拒絕交出去。

The sealed hall stood above a protected habitat of silver moss, part of the valley's living heritage. Its immense stone steps had once promised advancement to anyone with enough skill. Luo felt an impulse to climb them immediately and search for his brother. Jiang stopped him with a question: if the stairs consumed a written memory every time someone crossed, whose name was Luo prepared to lose? The question forced him to look at the gate instead of the summit.

封閉的殿堂座落在銀苔保護棲地上方，也是山谷活生生的文化遺產。巨大石階過去宣稱凡是有本領的人都能向上晉升。羅衍衝動地想立刻登階尋弟。江沐問他：如果每踏過石階就會耗去一段寫下的記憶，他打算失去誰的名字？這句話迫使羅衍先看向門，而不是峰頂。

Luo's former training gave him one insight: the door had three fixed rules. Two living witnesses had to approve any entrance; the audit mirror kept the final action of each chamber; the stair erased a named written memory and could not reverse the loss. A metropolitan safety officer admitted that review was optional under an overlooked provision. Jiang asked that those words be copied into the minutes, where they could no longer be hidden beneath ceremony.

羅衍從過去的訓練想起一項關鍵洞察：這扇門有三條固定規則。任何入場都要兩名活著的見證人同意；稽核鏡會保留每間試煉房最後一個動作；石階會抹去一段有姓名的書面記憶，而且無法逆轉。都會派來的安全官承認，一條被忽略的條款把審查列為可選程序。江沐要求將這段話寫入會議紀錄。

A boy stumbled over the loose floorboards just as the supervisor reached for Jiang's document. Luo caught the boy rather than the document; Jiang recovered his page himself. The supervisor called the offer a fair transaction between the bureau and families.

監督官伸手要拿江沐文件時，一個男孩被鬆動地板絆倒。羅衍先扶住男孩，而江沐自己收回紙張。監督官稱官署與家屬的安排是一筆公平交易。

The boy's sleeve had caught on a nail beneath the board. Luo knelt to free the cloth and found that the loose plank covered a shallow channel leading toward the lectern. Warm air rose from it. He asked the boy to stand by his aunt, then called the safety officer over before touching anything else. Jiang held his paper above the dust while the officer fitted a wooden wedge beneath the plank. The channel was part of the hall's ventilation, not a hidden path into the trial. That distinction disappointed a man who had already begun calling it an escape tunnel. Jiang asked him to help move the waiting chairs away from the opening instead. When the officer finally brought the minutes, the promised review clause was missing. Jiang pointed to the empty space and waited while she added it. Luo watched the ink dry. Until then, he had thought their problem was getting through a locked gate; now he understood that keeping an ordinary sentence on an ordinary page might be the harder work.

男孩的袖子勾住地板下的釘子。羅衍跪下解開布料，發現鬆木板蓋著通往講臺的淺溝，暖風從裡頭升起。他先請男孩到姑姑身邊，再叫安全官過來，沒有碰其他東西。官員用木楔固定木板，江沐把文件舉高避開灰塵。溝道是大殿的通風設施，不是進入試煉的暗道。這個區別讓一個已喊著「逃生隧道」的男人失望；江沐改請他幫忙搬開洞口附近的椅子。安全官終於送來會議紀錄時，答應寫入的審查條款仍然缺席。江沐指著空白，等她補寫。羅衍看著墨跡乾透。他原以為難處在於進入鎖住的門，現在才明白，讓一句普通的話留在普通的紙上，可能更難。

Outside, silver moss began to wither under the hall's leaking heat. Luo and Jiang exchanged names, not promises, and agreed that the first proof must come from a living witness who could safely say no.

殿外的銀苔卻因殿內漏出的熱氣開始枯萎。羅衍與江沐交換姓名，沒有輕率許諾，只同意第一份證據必須來自能安全拒絕的活證人。
