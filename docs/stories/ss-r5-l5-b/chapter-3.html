A Clerk Beside the Creek

封存試煉：未被允許遺忘的人 · 第 3 / 25 章 · 4000–5000 · 英中對照

News of the archive's abuse reached a small mountain town before the investigators did. Jiang requested a private consultation with a retired clerk, but the man greeted Luo with contempt, calling all former examiners cowards. A newspaper correspondent followed them toward a creek, hoping for a dramatic confession. Jiang asked the reporter to remain on the far bank until the clerk had decided what he wished to disclose.

檔案遭濫用的消息比調查者更早傳進山城。江沐請求私下向退休書吏請教，書吏卻輕蔑地稱羅衍等昔日考官都是懦夫。一名報社記者跟著他們來到小溪，希望得到戲劇性自白。江沐請記者留在對岸，等書吏決定願意說什麼。

The clerk said a deliberate order had erased the candidates' identities. His former deputy was still alive but worked under a diplomatic name in the capital. The sealed trial had damaged the creek's ecosystem; once-common water insects were now nearly extinct. Luo wanted to believe every word, yet his gut told him the clerk was mixing memories with resentments. He asked for the date and the officer who had signed the directive.

書吏說，有人刻意下令抹除應試者身分。當年的副手仍在世，卻在都城以外交身份工作。封存試煉也破壞了溪流生態系，原本常見的水蟲幾乎滅絕。羅衍想相信一切，直覺卻提醒他對方可能把記憶與怨恨混在一起。他追問日期與簽署命令的官員。

Jiang promised to notify the witness before any public release. The odds of finding the original order looked poor, especially because the first phase of the inquiry had ended eleven years earlier. At an old river pier, a ferryman recognized the signature but admitted he had once tried to sneak a parcel past inspection. Luo wrote that admission beside the man's useful statement, ensuring that neither kindness nor embarrassment would remove context.

江沐保證公開任何內容之前會先通知證人。找到原命令的機會很小，因為第一階段調查早在十一年前結束。舊河碼頭上，一名渡夫認出簽名，也承認自己曾試著偷偷夾帶包裹通關。羅衍把這段自白寫在有用證詞旁，避免因好意或尷尬而刪掉脈絡。

The retired clerk named his successor as the person who had ordered duplicate stamps. He also described a sustainable repair plan for the creek that officials had abandoned after the trial closed. Jiang found two identical labels on different envelopes: someone had made a swap after the public hearing. A trophy displayed in the town office bore the older label, proving that at least one document had traveled through official hands.

退休書吏指出，他的繼任者曾下令製作重複印章。他也談到一項原可永續修復溪流的計畫，卻在試煉關閉後被官署放棄。江沐在不同信封上找到兩張相同標籤：有人在公開聽證後調包文件。鎮公所展示的一座獎盃上貼著舊標籤，證明至少有一份文件曾經過公家之手。

The ferryman would not bring the envelopes onto his boat because spray had already spotted the pier. Jiang borrowed the town office's drying rack and stretched a cloth above the papers. The retired clerk followed them there, irritated that Luo had written down his uncertainty about the signing date. He offered to make the statement firmer. Luo showed him the two dates without suggesting which one to choose. After a long look, the clerk admitted that he remembered the river being in flood, not the number on the calendar. That gave them something else to check in the ferry log. The clerk's anger cooled when he saw that Luo had kept his accusation beside the correction rather than crossing it out. At the office window, the correspondent raised his camera. Jiang lowered the blind before the witness had to ask. They agreed that the reporter could photograph the public trophy after the office keeper confirmed its label. Across the creek, the ferryman rang for his last passenger, and the clerk finally sat down to dictate what he had actually seen.

渡夫不肯把信封帶上船，因為水花已濺濕碼頭。江沐借來鎮公所的晾紙架，拉起布遮住文件。退休書吏跟到那裡，不滿羅衍記下他對簽署日期的不確定，表示可以把證詞說得更肯定。羅衍只給他看兩個日期，沒有暗示選哪個。書吏看了很久，承認記得的是河水暴漲，不是日曆數字。這讓他們能再去核對渡船日誌。看見羅衍保留原指控，也並列修正，而非塗掉，書吏的怒氣稍緩。記者在窗外舉起相機，江沐沒等證人開口就放下窗簾。他們同意，公所保管者確認獎盃標籤後，記者可拍公開展品。溪對岸，渡夫敲鐘招呼最後一位乘客，書吏終於坐下，口述自己真正親眼見過的事。

At night Luo told Jiang why he had resigned. He had signed a safety report after being shown a false chamber diagram, and his brother disappeared a week later. Jiang answered that his mother had been blamed for burning the roll, though the clerk's dates now suggested otherwise. Neither asked the other to forgive an absent person. They left the creek with two names to verify and one living witness safely out of the headlines.

夜裡，羅衍告訴江沐自己為何辭職：當年他看到一張假的試煉房圖後，簽署了安全報告；一週後弟弟失蹤。江沐則說母親被指控焚燒名冊，書吏提供的日期卻顯示事情未必如此。兩人都沒有要求對方原諒缺席的人。他們帶著兩個待查姓名離開溪邊，並讓活證人暫時遠離新聞。
