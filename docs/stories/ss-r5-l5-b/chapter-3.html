A Clerk Beside the Creek

封存試煉：未被允許遺忘的人 · 第 3 / 25 章 · 4000–5000 · 英中對照

News of the archive's abuse reached a small mountain town before the investigators did. Jiang requested a private consultation with a retired clerk, but the man greeted Luo with contempt, calling all former examiners cowards. A newspaper correspondent followed them toward a creek, hoping for a dramatic confession. Jiang asked the reporter to remain on the far bank until the clerk had decided what he wished to disclose. An official title did not make a statement true; a readable record and a chance to question it mattered more. He touched the edge of the damaged ledger, then stopped before the fragile paper could tear beneath his fingers. For now they kept the copy dry and the door unsealed, leaving the next witness free to decide.

檔案遭濫用的消息比調查者更早傳進山城。江沐請求私下向退休書吏請教，書吏卻輕蔑地稱羅衍等昔日考官都是懦夫。一名報社記者跟著他們來到小溪，希望得到戲劇性自白。江沐請記者留在對岸，等書吏決定願意說什麼。官銜不會讓說法自動成真；可讀的紀錄與提問的機會更加重要。他輕觸受損帳冊邊緣，隨即停手，以免脆弱紙張在指間裂開。眼下他們先把副本保存乾燥，門也不封死，讓下一名證人能自由決定。

The clerk said a deliberate order had erased the candidates' identities. His former deputy was still alive but worked under a diplomatic name in the capital. The sealed trial had damaged the creek's ecosystem; once-common water insects were now nearly extinct. Luo wanted to believe every word, yet his gut told him the clerk was mixing memories with resentments. He asked for the date and the officer who had signed the directive. The promise between them was not that they would always agree, but that disagreement would never be treated as betrayal. They looked for a second source before moving, since the first account might describe only part of the event. The evening ended without triumph; it ended with a record that tomorrow’s readers could inspect for themselves.

書吏說，有人刻意下令抹除應試者身分。當年的副手仍在世，卻在都城以外交身份工作。封存試煉也破壞了溪流生態系，原本常見的水蟲幾乎滅絕。羅衍想相信一切，直覺卻提醒他對方可能把記憶與怨恨混在一起。他追問日期與簽署命令的官員。他們的約定不是永遠意見一致，而是絕不把分歧視為背叛。兩人出發前先尋找第二份來源，因為第一份證詞也許只描述了事情的一部分。這個夜晚沒有勝利歡呼，只有一份明天的讀者能親自查閱的紀錄。

Jiang promised to notify the witness before any public release. The odds of finding the original order looked poor, especially because the first phase of the inquiry had ended eleven years earlier. At an old river pier, a ferryman recognized the signature but admitted he had once tried to sneak a parcel past inspection. Luo wrote that admission beside the man's useful statement, ensuring that neither kindness nor embarrassment would remove context. They considered what the record omitted: the names of helpers, the price of delay, and the right to tell another story. The audience heard two possible explanations, and the pair made clear what observation would distinguish one from the other. Jiang Mu stayed because he chose to, and Luo Yan accepted that choice without asking for a promise about tomorrow.

江沐保證公開任何內容之前會先通知證人。找到原命令的機會很小，因為第一階段調查早在十一年前結束。舊河碼頭上，一名渡夫認出簽名，也承認自己曾試著偷偷夾帶包裹通關。羅衍把這段自白寫在有用證詞旁，避免因好意或尷尬而刪掉脈絡。他們也想著紀錄漏掉的部分：幫助者的姓名、拖延的代價，以及訴說另一種故事的權利。聽眾聽見兩種可能解釋，兩人也說明要用哪項觀察來區分。江沐是自己選擇留下，羅衍接受這個選擇，沒有要求他承諾明天的事。

The retired clerk named his successor as the person who had ordered duplicate stamps. He also described a sustainable repair plan for the creek that officials had abandoned after the trial closed. Jiang found two identical labels on different envelopes: someone had made a swap after the public hearing. A trophy displayed in the town office bore the older label, proving that at least one document had traveled through official hands. The archive had survived for years; the challenge was to make its contents useful without turning them into another weapon. Luo Yan put the seal away instead of using it immediately, and Jiang Mu noticed the restraint without thanking him. They signed below the witnesses, not above them, because the people who bore the cost had to remain visible.

退休書吏指出，他的繼任者曾下令製作重複印章。他也談到一項原可永續修復溪流的計畫，卻在試煉關閉後被官署放棄。江沐在不同信封上找到兩張相同標籤：有人在公開聽證後調包文件。鎮公所展示的一座獎盃上貼著舊標籤，證明至少有一份文件曾經過公家之手。檔案存留多年；難題是如何讓內容發揮作用，卻不把它們變成另一件武器。羅衍沒有立刻使用印章，而是將它收好；江沐注意到了這份克制，卻沒有道謝。他們把名字簽在見證人下方，而不是上方，因為承受代價的人必須被看見。

At night Luo told Jiang why he had resigned. He had signed a safety report after being shown a false chamber diagram, and his brother disappeared a week later. Jiang answered that his mother had been blamed for burning the roll, though the clerk's dates now suggested otherwise. Neither asked the other to forgive an absent person. They left the creek with two names to verify and one living witness safely out of the headlines. The silence after the discovery was not empty; it gave each person time to understand what might change. Luo Yan asked whether Jiang Mu wanted him beside the door. A quiet yes was enough, and he stayed. A second chance meant nothing if its terms could not be refused, so they wrote the conditions in plain language.

夜裡，羅衍告訴江沐自己為何辭職：當年他看到一張假的試煉房圖後，簽署了安全報告；一週後弟弟失蹤。江沐則說母親被指控焚燒名冊，書吏提供的日期卻顯示事情未必如此。兩人都沒有要求對方原諒缺席的人。他們帶著兩個待查姓名離開溪邊，並讓活證人暫時遠離新聞。發現之後的沉默並非空白，而是讓每個人理解接下來可能改變什麼。羅衍問江沐是否要他守在門邊；江沐輕聲說好，羅衍就留下。若不能拒絕條件，第二次機會就毫無意義，因此他們把條件寫成易懂文字。
