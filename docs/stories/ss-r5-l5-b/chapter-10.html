沒有門的研究所

The Institute Without a Door

The old examination institute had issued certificates for research in bulk, including warnings about allergy risks from preservation dust. Jiang asked its registrar to cite a specific rule before refusing outside collaboration. The officer could find none. Luo placed the two-witness application beside the signed gate form and explained that they sought a supervised review of an existing mirror, not permission to restart the sealed examination. A clean copy would have been easier to read, but the crossed-out words revealed more about the decision that mattered. They refused to break the lock in secret; instead, they gathered two witnesses and recorded the reason for opening it. Nothing was settled forever, yet they had made one part of the future less dependent on secrecy.

舊試煉研究所曾大量核發研究證書，也包括保存粉塵的過敏風險警告。江沐要求登記官在拒絕外部合作前援引具體規則；對方找不到。羅衍把雙見證申請書放在簽署過的門表旁，說明他們申請的是在監督下查看既有稽核鏡，而不是重啟封存考試。乾淨的抄本比較好讀，然而劃掉的文字更能揭露關鍵決定如何形成。兩人拒絕私下撬鎖，而是找來兩名見證人，記錄開鎖的理由。沒有什麼能永遠塵埃落定，但他們已讓未來的一部分不再依賴祕密。

The registrar tried to compel a junior counselor to sign a statement describing the candidates as confused. The counselor refused; her descriptive notes recorded hunger and poor digestion, not an inability to consent. Jiang asked whether she could testify privately. She agreed if patient details remained sealed. Luo made sure she knew that refusing would not endanger her position, a promise the registrar disliked but could not lawfully contradict. They wrote down exactly what they knew, rather than letting a convincing guess become a fact that no one could challenge. A clerk offered a shortcut; they requested the ordinary route in writing, including the name of whoever would approve it. At sunset they agreed on one limited next step, with a clear point at which either of them could call a halt.

登記官試圖強迫一位年輕輔導員簽下「應試者意識混亂」的聲明。輔導員拒絕，因為她的描述性筆記只記錄飢餓與消化不良，不能證明沒有同意能力。江沐詢問能否私下作證，她要求病人細節封存後才答應。羅衍確保她知道拒絕不會危及職位；登記官不滿，卻無法依法反駁。他們記下確實知道的事，不讓看似合理的猜測變成無人能質疑的事實。一名書吏提供捷徑；他們要求書面載明正常程序，以及核准者的姓名。日落時，他們只同意下一個有限步驟，並清楚約定任一方何時能要求停止。

Outside the institute a drought had emptied the teaching pond. A student saw a portable grill in the courtyard and exclaimed that the staff must have burned the missing rolls there. Luo insisted that ash from cooking could not establish destruction of documents. The institute's caretaker offered an actual sample from the old waste bins. Jiang sealed it in a separate envelope with the collection date, careful not to promise what laboratory tests might find. No witness was asked to risk another memory merely to make the investigators feel that their work was moving faster. Jiang Mu returned the borrowed key before the deadline and kept a signed receipt, however inconvenient that seemed. The decision did not solve the mystery, but it prevented a preventable harm, and that mattered to them both.

研究所外的乾旱讓教學池塘見底。一名學生看見庭院的便攜烤爐，驚呼職員一定在那裡燒掉失蹤名冊。羅衍堅持，烹飪灰燼不能證明文件遭毀。看守提供舊垃圾桶裡的實際樣本，江沐用另一個信封封存，註明採集日期，不事先保證檢驗會得出什麼。他們不會要求證人再冒一次失去記憶的風險，只為讓調查者覺得進度更快。江沐在期限前歸還借來的鑰匙，並保留簽收證明，儘管這看來麻煩。這個決定沒有解開謎團，卻避免了一件本可預防的傷害；對兩人來說這同樣重要。

By afternoon the volume of rumors threatened to overwhelm the institute. The registrar issued a preliminary report proposing a radical ban on all private research. Jiang explained that such a rule was not rational: it punished accurate conservators along with careless dealers. Luo proposed instead a schedule for supervised viewing, a named custodian, and a process to challenge disputed entries. Two faculty members supported the narrower plan. They carried the unanswered question into the next room instead of pretending the first answer had been complete.

到了下午，流言幾乎淹沒整座研究所。登記官提出初步報告，建議激進地禁止所有私人研究。江沐說這並不理性：它把認真修復師與粗心商人一同處罰。羅衍提出較窄的方案，包括監督下的觀看時段、指定保管人與異議程序。兩名教員支持這個做法。他們把未解的問題帶進下一間房，沒有假裝第一個答案已經完整。

A terminal in the west wing displayed a list of mirror rooms under the heading Wilderness Examinations. An old teacher said the term belonged to a popular thriller rather than the actual curriculum. More importantly, the display contained a variable that counted entries without recording names. Jiang discovered that the count had increased after the public closing date. They did not touch the sealed switch. Their first ten days ended with a scheduled viewing and a question only the audit mirror could answer. Their route became longer, yet the evidence would survive even if they later changed their minds about what it meant.

西翼終端顯示「荒野試煉」名下的鏡房清單。一名老教師說，那只是暢銷驚悚小說借用的名稱，不是實際課程。更重要的是，顯示器有一項只計入場次數、不寫姓名的變數。江沐發現公開宣布關閉後，數字仍然增加。兩人沒有碰封存開關。第十天結束時，他們取得正式觀看時段，也留下只有稽核鏡能回答的問題。路線因此變長，但即使日後改變對證據的理解，證據本身仍會保留下來。
