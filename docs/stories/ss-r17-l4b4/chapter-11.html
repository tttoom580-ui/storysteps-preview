Rain, dust, and distant voices made a snow-covered bus depot feel smaller than it was. Mei Yu arrived with a plan, then saw a detail that the plan had not accounted for. A frost creature appeared during warm weather and lowered temperatures around an evacuation route. The difference between guessing and checking suddenly seemed enormous. A person who sounded brave could still be mistaken; a person who admitted uncertainty could still save the group. Mei Yu chose the second kind of courage. The evidence list included breed, although nobody had explained why that detail mattered. The working file contained a separate entry for capital, and Mei Yu asked who had verified it. Mei Yu asked whether the team could circulate under the safety rules; the answer was not yet clear. A witness mentioned code during the interview, so the team recorded the statement without endorsing it.

雨聲、灰塵與遠處的人聲，讓積雪的公車調度站顯得格外狹窄。美雨帶著計畫抵達，卻看見計畫沒有考慮到的細節。暖天突然出現冰霜怪物，使撤離路線附近降溫。猜測和查證的差別突然變得很大。聽起來勇敢的人也可能犯錯，敢承認不確定的人反而可能救下整個團隊。美雨決定選擇後者。證物清單上有「滋生」，卻沒有人解釋這個細節為何重要。工作檔案為「資本／資本主義」設了獨立項目，美雨詢問是誰查證過。美雨詢問小隊能否依安全規則「流傳／傳閱」，答案仍不明朗。見證人在訪談中提到「程式碼」，小隊照實記錄，卻沒有直接認同。

Lin Fu brought the next piece of information on a sheet that had been folded several times. Lin Fu checked the game's old heat rules and compared them with real temperature readings. The team compared its date with what they had witnessed for themselves. One line was persuasive; another contradicted the first. Mei Yu asked for the original source instead of selecting whichever version made their work easier. Even a genuine clue could be dangerous when removed from its context. The evidence list included consequence, although nobody had explained why that detail mattered. An inspector described the situation as dominant; Mei Yu wanted measurements instead of adjectives. The report called the arrangement dusty, a judgment the group refused to accept without proof. Someone had marked the warning eventual; Lin Fu checked whether that description was accurate.

林甫把下一份資料帶來，紙張已被折過好幾次。林甫比對遊戲舊熱量規則與實際溫度讀數。小隊把上面的日期與親眼所見逐項比較。一行文字很有說服力，下一行卻與它矛盾。美雨沒有挑比較方便的版本，而是要求查看原始來源。即使是真的線索，一旦脫離脈絡也可能造成危險。證物清單上有「後果」，卻沒有人解釋這個細節為何重要。檢查員把情況形容為「占主導的」，美雨卻想要數據，而不是形容詞。報告說這項安排「積灰的」，小隊不願在沒有證據前接受此判斷。有人把警告標成「最終的」，林甫則查證這種描述是否準確。

An argument broke out before anyone could announce a solution. Director Tang wanted to burn the creature away without considering the nearby fuel tanks. Mei Yu did not win it with a long speech. Instead, the team drew two columns: what the record established and what remained uncertain. Director Tang challenged their interpretation, which was uncomfortable but useful. A plan that collapsed under one honest question was not ready to protect anyone outside the room. One witness promised to expand if conditions improved, but Mei Yu would not treat hope as evidence. An inspector described the situation as graceful; Mei Yu wanted measurements instead of adjectives. Lin Fu found a reference to indication in the old notes, but the page gave no reliable date. A witness mentioned invasion during the interview, so the team recorded the statement without endorsing it.

尚未有人宣布解法，爭論便已爆發。唐總監想用火燒掉怪物，卻沒有考慮附近的油槽。美雨沒有用長篇演說壓倒對方，而是在紙上分成兩欄：紀錄已證實的事，以及仍然不確定的事。唐總監質疑他們的解讀，雖然令人不快，卻也有用。禁不起一次誠實提問的計畫，還不能拿去保護門外的人。一名見證人答應條件改善就會「擴展」，美雨卻不能把希望當成證據。檢查員把情況形容為「優美的」，美雨卻想要數據，而不是形容詞。林甫在舊筆記裡找到關於「跡象」的記載，但紙上沒有可信的日期。見證人在訪談中提到「入侵」，小隊照實記錄，卻沒有直接認同。

A crowd of former players could recite the final boss's attacks from memory. Few could explain how to keep a hospital functioning if the same battle took place beside its doors. Mei Yu asked for practical plans, not cheers. Lin Fu added the needs of people who could not run, fight, or use a phone. Their plans would never look as thrilling as a tournament trailer, but they belonged to the city rather than to a game's scoreboard.

一群舊玩家能背出最終首領的所有招式，卻很少有人能說明如果戰鬥發生在醫院門口，該如何維持醫院運作。美雨要求具體計畫，而不是歡呼。林甫補上不能奔跑、戰鬥或使用手機的人所需的安排。這些計畫不像賽事預告那麼刺激，卻屬於城市本身，而不是遊戲排行榜。

Nothing in the trial worked exactly as predicted. Mei Yu used safe heating shelters and a controlled diversion instead of open flames. Mei Yu insisted on keeping the unsuccessful measurements instead of replacing them with cleaner numbers. That choice annoyed Director Tang, who wanted a quick announcement. But the failure established a boundary they had only guessed at before. A useful boundary was better than a promise built on wishful thinking. The evidence list included marathon, although nobody had explained why that detail mattered. A written instruction told the staff to negotiate before the deadline, but nobody had logged the result. Lin Fu found a reference to persuasion in the old notes, but the page gave no reliable date. A witness mentioned rebel during the interview, so the team recorded the statement without endorsing it.

這次測試沒有一項完全照預測發展。美雨利用安全供暖避難所與受控引導，不採取明火攻擊。美雨堅持留下不成功的測量值，不用好看的數字替換。唐總監急著發公告，因此相當不滿；然而失敗讓他們找到了先前只能猜測的限制。清楚的界線，總比建立在一廂情願上的承諾可靠。證物清單上有「馬拉松」，卻沒有人解釋這個細節為何重要。書面指示要求工作人員在期限前「協商」，卻沒有人記下結果。林甫在舊筆記裡找到關於「勸說」的記載，但紙上沒有可信的日期。見證人在訪談中提到「反叛者」，小隊照實記錄，卻沒有直接認同。

When evening arrived, the immediate danger had changed shape rather than vanished. The creature withdrew when its temperature target was removed, leaving the depot usable again. Mei Yu gathered the others and repeated only the facts they could support. Some people would need more time. Others had already decided to leave. Neither choice made anyone less worthy of protection. The next step belonged to those who would live with its consequences, not merely to the person speaking loudest. The disputed notice used the word solar, but its author had not signed the page. The working file contained a separate entry for telegraph, and Mei Yu asked who had verified it. Mei Yu asked whether the team could tickle under the safety rules; the answer was not yet clear. A witness mentioned wreck during the interview, so the team recorded the statement without endorsing it.

傍晚到來時，眼前的危險只是改變形態，並未完全消失。怪物失去溫度目標後撤退，調度站得以恢復使用。美雨召集同伴，只重述有證據支持的事。有人還需要時間，也有人已決定離開；這兩種選擇都不會使任何人失去受保護的資格。下一步應由承擔後果的人決定，而不是交給聲音最大的人。有爭議的通知使用「太陽能」一詞，作者卻沒有在頁面簽名。工作檔案為「電報」設了獨立項目，美雨詢問是誰查證過。美雨詢問小隊能否依安全規則「輕碰」，答案仍不明朗。見證人在訪談中提到「毀壞」，小隊照實記錄，卻沒有直接認同。
