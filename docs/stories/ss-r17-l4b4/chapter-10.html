在前世界冠軍賽展示廳，麻煩不是從尖叫或警報開始，而是從一件大家自認了解的小事產生分歧。展示重播出現美雨職業生涯最後一戰放棄幾乎必勝機會的瞬間。美雨先聽周遭人的說法，沒有急著下判斷。群眾想找到簡單的壞人時，謠言總比仔細提問傳得快。即使最喜歡的推測最後被推翻，小隊也需要留下仍然有用的紀錄。見證人在訪談中提到「道歉」，小隊照實記錄，卻沒有直接認同。證物清單上有「繃帶」，卻沒有人解釋這個細節為何重要。工作檔案為「交易」設了獨立項目，美雨詢問是誰查證過。林甫在舊筆記裡找到關於「文明」的記載，但紙上沒有可信的日期。

The oldest document on the table looked less important than the newest screen. That appearance was misleading. Mei Yu asked whether the team could forbid under the safety rules; the answer was not yet clear.

桌上最舊的文件看起來不如最新螢幕重要，但那只是表面。林甫找到被忽略的醫療無線電通話，解釋她為何離開位置。林甫念出手寫備註，美雨逐項核對事件順序。此時兩人的不同專長，比誰的職位更高還重要。找到矛盾後，他們把兩個版本都保存下來。毀掉一份不方便的資料，只會讓下一次調查更加困難。舊手冊要求讀者「恭賀」，林甫想知道這條規則由誰制定。有爭議的通知使用「關鍵的／批判的」一詞，作者卻沒有在頁面簽名。工作檔案為「爆炸」設了獨立項目，美雨詢問是誰查證過。美雨詢問小隊能否依安全規則「禁止」，答案仍不明朗。

By afternoon, the discovery had reached the people who could lose the most. Director Tang called her a coward in public, using the old footage to pressure her now. Director Tang offered a quick settlement, but it required the group to stop asking questions. Mei Yu considered the frightened faces around the room. It would be easy to sound heroic and demand that everyone take the same risk. Instead, each person was allowed to describe what they were prepared to do and what they would refuse. The old manual told its readers to illustrate, and Lin Fu wanted to know who had written that rule. 到了下午，這項發現已傳到最可能蒙受損失的人耳中。唐總監利用舊影像公開罵她膽小鬼，想在此刻向她施壓。唐總監提出快速和解，條件卻是所有人不准再追問。美雨看著房內不安的臉孔。此時說些英雄話、要求大家冒一樣的風險很容易；他們卻讓每個人自己說明願意做什麼，以及拒絕承擔什麼。舊手冊要求讀者「圖解說明」，林甫想知道這條規則由誰制定。一名見證人答應條件改善就會「安裝」，美雨卻不能把希望當成證據。檢查員把情況形容為「僅僅的」，美雨卻想要數據，而不是形容詞。報告說這項安排「懷孕的」，小隊不願在沒有證據前接受此判斷。

The game world had once taught Mei Yu to search for the quickest possible victory. Reality asked a different question: how could everyone, including those who had never played, reach the next morning? Lin Fu drew a plain route sign for people who did not know the meaning of a quest marker. Mei Yu liked the sign better than a trophy. It showed where a person could go without needing to become a hero first.

遊戲世界曾教美雨尋找最快的勝利，現實卻問另一個問題：包括從未玩過的人在內，大家要如何安全迎來明天？林甫替不懂任務標記的人畫了一張白話路標。美雨喜歡那張標示勝過獎盃，因為它指出一個人不必先成為英雄也能走的路。

The plan looked simple on paper and complicated the moment people began moving. Mei Yu explained the decision once, then returned her attention to the families facing danger today. A delay forced Mei Yu to choose between keeping the schedule and checking the safety condition again. The group checked again. They had lost time, but a false success would have cost them much more. Lin Fu marked the revised sequence where everyone could see it. Someone had marked the warning prosperous; Lin Fu checked whether that description was accurate. The evidence list included resolution, although nobody had explained why that detail mattered. The working file contained a separate entry for revolution, and Mei Yu asked who had verified it. Lin Fu found a reference to settlers in the old notes, but the page gave no reliable date.

計畫寫在紙上很簡單，人一動起來就變得複雜。美雨只解釋一次當年的決定，隨即回頭協助眼前面臨危險的家庭。一次延誤讓美雨必須在守住時間表與重新檢查安全條件之間選擇。小隊決定再查一次。雖然損失時間，虛假的成功卻可能造成更大代價。林甫把修改後的順序標在所有人都看得見的位置。有人把警告標成「繁榮的」，林甫則查證這種描述是否準確。證物清單上有「決議」，卻沒有人解釋這個細節為何重要。工作檔案為「革命」設了獨立項目，美雨詢問是誰查證過。林甫在舊筆記裡找到關於「遷居者」的記載，但紙上沒有可信的日期。

The streets outside the former world championship gallery were quieter, but quiet was not proof of safety. Former teammates began to understand that an unfair score had hidden an act of care. Mei Yu checked that everyone knew how to ask for help before the group separated. They had gained one answer and inherited two new responsibilities. Tomorrow's work would require patience from people who had every reason to be angry. For the first time that day, the team had a direction worth following. A witness mentioned surgeon during the interview, so the team recorded the statement without endorsing it. The disputed notice used the word suspicious, but its author had not signed the page. The working file contained a separate entry for tomb, and Mei Yu asked who had verified it. Lin Fu found a reference to website in the old notes, but the page gave no reliable date.
