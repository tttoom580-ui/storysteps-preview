傳送門後的藥局

The Pharmacy Behind the Portal

The day began inside a neighborhood pharmacy street. Mei Yu had come expecting an ordinary task, but the room already contained the sort of silence that follows bad news. A portal opened behind a pharmacy, cutting patients off from needed medicine. Nobody could afford to confuse a confident explanation with a reliable observation. They had made that mistake before, and the cost was still visible in the people waiting outside. Mei Yu opened the notebook and decided to write down what could actually be tested. Someone had marked the warning conservative; Lin Fu checked whether that description was accurate. The evidence list included consultant, although nobody had explained why that detail mattered. The working file contained a separate entry for consumer, and Mei Yu asked who had verified it. Lin Fu found a reference to dye in the old notes, but the page gave no reliable date.

這一天從社區藥局街開始。美雨原以為只是處理例行工作，房間卻靜得像剛傳出壞消息。傳送門出現在藥局後方，切斷病人取得藥物的路。他們不能把說得肯定的解釋誤當成可靠觀察。大家以前犯過這種錯，代價仍寫在門外那些等待者的表情上。美雨打開筆記本，決定只記下真正能夠驗證的事情。有人把警告標成「保守的」，林甫則查證這種描述是否準確。證物清單上有「顧問」，卻沒有人解釋這個細節為何重要。工作檔案為「消費者」設了獨立項目，美雨詢問是誰查證過。林甫在舊筆記裡找到關於「染料」的記載，但紙上沒有可信的日期。

A new message reached Lin Fu while the group was still checking its first result. Lin Fu measured the portal's pulse and found a safe interval when nothing could pass through it. Mei Yu refused to celebrate too early. They had learned that two records could repeat the same lie when both came from a single office. Before treating the discovery as independent evidence, the group searched for a witness who had nothing to gain by agreeing with them. The old manual told its readers to enclose, and Lin Fu wanted to know who had written that rule. The evidence list included goods, although nobody had explained why that detail mattered. The working file contained a separate entry for grace, and Mei Yu asked who had verified it. The report called the arrangement habitual, a judgment the group refused to accept without proof.

小隊還在核對第一項結果時，林甫就收到新的消息。林甫測出傳送門的脈衝，找到暫時無法通行的安全空檔。美雨不肯太早慶祝。他們已學到，兩份紀錄如果來自同一辦公室，也可能只是重複同一個謊言。要把發現視為獨立證據前，大家先去尋找一個不會因附和他們而得利的見證人。舊手冊要求讀者「封入」，林甫想知道這條規則由誰制定。證物清單上有「貨品」，卻沒有人解釋這個細節為何重要。工作檔案為「從容」設了獨立項目，美雨詢問是誰查證過。報告說這項安排「習慣性的」，小隊不願在沒有證據前接受此判斷。

The easiest choice would have made Mei Yu popular for a single evening. Director Tang wanted players to rush inside and search for valuable game materials. Lin Fu reminded the group that their decision would still matter tomorrow, after the applause and anger faded. Nobody could offer a guarantee that every person would approve. They could, however, make the reasons public and leave a safe way for a witness to disagree. Someone had marked the warning ignorant; Lin Fu checked whether that description was accurate. One witness promised to invest if conditions improved, but Mei Yu would not treat hope as evidence. A written instruction told the staff to isolate before the deadline, but nobody had logged the result. Lin Fu found a reference to linen in the old notes, but the page gave no reliable date.

最省事的選擇，或許能讓美雨當晚受到歡呼。唐總監想讓玩家衝進門內，搜尋珍貴遊戲材料。林甫提醒大家，掌聲與怒氣消失後，決定明天仍然會造成影響。沒有人能保證所有人都滿意，但至少可以公開理由，並留下見證人安全表達反對的管道。有人把警告標成「不了解的」，林甫則查證這種描述是否準確。一名見證人答應條件改善就會「投資」，美雨卻不能把希望當成證據。書面指示要求工作人員在期限前「隔離」，卻沒有人記下結果。林甫在舊筆記裡找到關於「亞麻布」的記載，但紙上沒有可信的日期。

During her professional years, Mei Yu had watched a health bar disappear and understood it was only part of a game. In the real streets, people did not return when a screen restarted. Lin Fu listened to her explanations of old mechanics but checked every one against the present danger. Experience gave them useful questions, not permission to gamble with strangers' lives. The distinction made their decisions slower, and sometimes that slowness kept people safe.

職業選手時期，美雨看過血量條消失，也知道那只是遊戲。真實街道上的人卻不會在螢幕重啟後復活。林甫聽她解釋舊機制，卻把每項說法與眼前危險核對。經驗能帶來好問題，不代表可以拿陌生人的生命下注。這種區分讓決策變慢，有時正是這份謹慎保住了生命。

They agreed to test a limited part of the plan before touching the main system. Mei Yu coordinated with pharmacists and firefighters to move essential supplies during verified intervals. Mei Yu assigned roles according to practical skill rather than confidence. Lin Fu recorded each step, including the moments when someone had to stop. If the trial failed, the notes would explain why. If it worked, the notes would help the next group repeat it without depending on a hero who happened to be present. A witness mentioned luxury during the interview, so the team recorded the statement without endorsing it. The evidence list included microscope, although nobody had explained why that detail mattered. The working file contained a separate entry for occupation, and Mei Yu asked who had verified it. Lin Fu found a reference to orientation in the old notes, but the page gave no reliable date.

他們同意先做小範圍測試，不直接碰主要系統。美雨與藥師、消防員協調，在驗證過的空檔搬運必需物資。美雨依照實際專長分派工作，不看誰說話最有自信。林甫記錄每個步驟，連必須停止的時刻也不漏掉。如果測試失敗，筆記能說明原因；如果成功，下一批人也能按紀錄重做，不必依賴恰好在場的英雄。見證人在訪談中提到「奢華」，小隊照實記錄，卻沒有直接認同。證物清單上有「顯微鏡」，卻沒有人解釋這個細節為何重要。工作檔案為「職業」設了獨立項目，美雨詢問是誰查證過。林甫在舊筆記裡找到關於「職前說明」的記載，但紙上沒有可信的日期。

The result was smaller than the promise they had hoped to make, but it was real. The supplies reached patients, but the portal left an unfamiliar symbol on the back wall. Lin Fu closed the notebook only after marking the unresolved questions. Mei Yu was tired of victories that depended on hiding their cost. This one could be examined in daylight. And the detail they had not solved was already pointing toward the next part of the investigation. A witness mentioned rage during the interview, so the team recorded the statement without endorsing it. The evidence list included stereo, although nobody had explained why that detail mattered. An inspector described the situation as timid; Mei Yu wanted measurements instead of adjectives. Mei Yu asked whether the team could tremble under the safety rules; the answer was not yet clear.

結果比原先希望宣布的成果小，卻是真的。物資送到病人手中，傳送門卻在後牆留下陌生符號。林甫直到標出所有未解問題，才闔上筆記本。美雨早已厭倦靠隱瞞代價換來的勝利；這次的結果可以在日光下接受查證。那個尚未解開的細節，也已指向調查的下一步。見證人在訪談中提到「盛怒」，小隊照實記錄，卻沒有直接認同。證物清單上有「立體聲」，卻沒有人解釋這個細節為何重要。檢查員把情況形容為「膽小的」，美雨卻想要數據，而不是形容詞。美雨詢問小隊能否依安全規則「顫抖」，答案仍不明朗。
