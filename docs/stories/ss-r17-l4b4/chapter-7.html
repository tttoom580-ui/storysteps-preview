At the game developer's corporate lobby, the first sign of trouble was not a scream or an alarm. It was a small disagreement about something everyone believed they understood. The developer's contract revealed that a live-reality experiment had continued after official safety approval expired. Mei Yu listened to the surrounding voices before making a judgment. Rumors traveled faster than careful questions, especially when the public wanted a simple villain. The team needed a record that would remain useful even if their favorite explanation proved false. The disputed notice used the word academic, but its author had not signed the page. The working file contained a separate entry for accountant, and Mei Yu asked who had verified it. Lin Fu found a reference to admiration in the old notes, but the page gave no reliable date. The old manual told its readers to annoy, and Lin Fu wanted to know who had written that rule.

在遊戲公司大廳，麻煩不是從尖叫或警報開始，而是從一件大家自認了解的小事產生分歧。開發商合約揭露，現實連線實驗在安全核准過期後仍持續。美雨先聽周遭人的說法，沒有急著下判斷。群眾想找到簡單的壞人時，謠言總比仔細提問傳得快。即使最喜歡的推測最後被推翻，小隊也需要留下仍然有用的紀錄。有爭議的通知使用「學術的」一詞，作者卻沒有在頁面簽名。工作檔案為「會計師」設了獨立項目，美雨詢問是誰查證過。林甫在舊筆記裡找到關於「欣賞」的記載，但紙上沒有可信的日期。舊手冊要求讀者「使困擾」，林甫想知道這條規則由誰制定。

The oldest document on the table looked less important than the newest screen. That appearance was misleading. Lin Fu compared its dates with the first portal sightings and found a direct overlap. Lin Fu read the handwritten notes aloud while Mei Yu checked the sequence of events. Their different skills mattered more than either person's rank. When they found a contradiction, they preserved both versions. Destroying an inconvenient record would only make the next investigation harder. The evidence list included aquarium, although nobody had explained why that detail mattered. The working file contained a separate entry for basin, and Mei Yu asked who had verified it. Lin Fu found a reference to characteristic in the old notes, but the page gave no reliable date. A witness mentioned chemistry during the interview, so the team recorded the statement without endorsing it.

桌上最舊的文件看起來不如最新螢幕重要，但那只是表面。林甫把日期與首批傳送門目擊紀錄比對，發現完全重疊。林甫念出手寫備註，美雨逐項核對事件順序。此時兩人的不同專長，比誰的職位更高還重要。找到矛盾後，他們把兩個版本都保存下來。毀掉一份不方便的資料，只會讓下一次調查更加困難。證物清單上有「水族館」，卻沒有人解釋這個細節為何重要。工作檔案為「水池」設了獨立項目，美雨詢問是誰查證過。林甫在舊筆記裡找到關於「特徵」的記載，但紙上沒有可信的日期。見證人在訪談中提到「化學反應」，小隊照實記錄，卻沒有直接認同。

By afternoon, the discovery had reached the people who could lose the most. Director Tang claimed the accident was unavoidable because the technology was new. Director Tang offered a quick settlement, but it required the group to stop asking questions. Mei Yu considered the frightened faces around the room. It would be easy to sound heroic and demand that everyone take the same risk. Instead, each person was allowed to describe what they were prepared to do and what they would refuse. The evidence list included contribution, although nobody had explained why that detail mattered. The working file contained a separate entry for defeated, and Mei Yu asked who had verified it. Lin Fu found a reference to expansion in the old notes, but the page gave no reliable date. Someone had marked the warning fluent; Lin Fu checked whether that description was accurate.

到了下午，這項發現已傳到最可能蒙受損失的人耳中。唐總監說技術新，事故無法避免。唐總監提出快速和解，條件卻是所有人不准再追問。美雨看著房內不安的臉孔。此時說些英雄話、要求大家冒一樣的風險很容易；他們卻讓每個人自己說明願意做什麼，以及拒絕承擔什麼。證物清單上有「捐贈」，卻沒有人解釋這個細節為何重要。工作檔案為「擊敗」設了獨立項目，美雨詢問是誰查證過。林甫在舊筆記裡找到關於「擴張」的記載，但紙上沒有可信的日期。有人把警告標成「流暢的」，林甫則查證這種描述是否準確。

The game world had once taught Mei Yu to search for the quickest possible victory. Reality asked a different question: how could everyone, including those who had never played, reach the next morning? Lin Fu drew a plain route sign for people who did not know the meaning of a quest marker. Mei Yu liked the sign better than a trophy. It showed where a person could go without needing to become a hero first.

遊戲世界曾教美雨尋找最快的勝利，現實卻問另一個問題：包括從未玩過的人在內，大家要如何安全迎來明天？林甫替不懂任務標記的人畫了一張白話路標。美雨喜歡那張標示勝過獎盃，因為它指出一個人不必先成為英雄也能走的路。

The plan looked simple on paper and complicated the moment people began moving. Mei Yu requested independent access to the test records while keeping private players' data protected. A delay forced Mei Yu to choose between keeping the schedule and checking the safety condition again. The group checked again. They had lost time, but a false success would have cost them much more. Lin Fu marked the revised sequence where everyone could see it. The disputed notice used the word fortunate, but its author had not signed the page. A written instruction told the staff to motivate before the deadline, but nobody had logged the result. The report called the arrangement prime, a judgment the group refused to accept without proof. Someone had marked the warning relevant; Lin Fu checked whether that description was accurate.

計畫寫在紙上很簡單，人一動起來就變得複雜。美雨要求獨立查看測試紀錄，同時保護玩家個資。一次延誤讓美雨必須在守住時間表與重新檢查安全條件之間選擇。小隊決定再查一次。雖然損失時間，虛假的成功卻可能造成更大代價。林甫把修改後的順序標在所有人都看得見的位置。有爭議的通知使用「幸運的」一詞，作者卻沒有在頁面簽名。書面指示要求工作人員在期限前「激勵；促使」，卻沒有人記下結果。報告說這項安排「主開關」，小隊不願在沒有證據前接受此判斷。有人把警告標成「相關的」，林甫則查證這種描述是否準確。

The streets outside the game developer's corporate lobby were quieter, but quiet was not proof of safety. The experiment used the same energy grid that powered public transport. Mei Yu checked that everyone knew how to ask for help before the group separated. They had gained one answer and inherited two new responsibilities. Tomorrow's work would require patience from people who had every reason to be angry. For the first time that day, the team had a direction worth following. The evidence list included rhythm, although nobody had explained why that detail mattered. The working file contained a separate entry for shade, and Mei Yu asked who had verified it. Mei Yu asked whether the team could strive under the safety rules; the answer was not yet clear. The old manual told its readers to waken, and Lin Fu wanted to know who had written that rule.

遊戲公司大廳外的街道變安靜了，但安靜不代表安全。實驗竟與大眾運輸共用同一套能源網路。美雨先確認大家知道如何求助，才讓小隊分開。他們得到一個答案，也多了兩份責任。明天的工作需要那些本來就有理由憤怒的人保持耐心。至少在這一天結束時，小隊終於有了一個值得追查的方向。證物清單上有「節奏」，卻沒有人解釋這個細節為何重要。工作檔案為「陰影」設了獨立項目，美雨詢問是誰查證過。美雨詢問小隊能否依安全規則「努力」，答案仍不明朗。舊手冊要求讀者「喚醒」，林甫想知道這條規則由誰制定。
