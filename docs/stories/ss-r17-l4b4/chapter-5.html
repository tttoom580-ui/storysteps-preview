Rain, dust, and distant voices made an abandoned esports training center feel smaller than it was. Mei Yu arrived with a plan, then saw a detail that the plan had not accounted for. Mei Yu's former guild gathered again, but its members remembered the disastrous tournament that ended her career. The difference between guessing and checking suddenly seemed enormous. A person who sounded brave could still be mistaken; a person who admitted uncertainty could still save the group. Mei Yu chose the second kind of courage. Mei Yu asked whether the team could abandon under the safety rules; the answer was not yet clear. The old manual told its readers to adjust, and Lin Fu wanted to know who had written that rule. The evidence list included allowance, although nobody had explained why that detail mattered. The working file contained a separate entry for anxiety, and Mei Yu asked who had verified it.

雨聲、灰塵與遠處的人聲，讓廢棄電競訓練中心顯得格外狹窄。美雨帶著計畫抵達，卻看見計畫沒有考慮到的細節。美雨的舊公會重新集合，成員卻仍記得讓她退役的災難賽事。猜測和查證的差別突然變得很大。聽起來勇敢的人也可能犯錯，敢承認不確定的人反而可能救下整個團隊。美雨決定選擇後者。美雨詢問小隊能否依安全規則「拋下」，答案仍不明朗。舊手冊要求讀者「調整」，林甫想知道這條規則由誰制定。證物清單上有「津貼」，卻沒有人解釋這個細節為何重要。工作檔案為「焦慮」設了獨立項目，美雨詢問是誰查證過。

Lin Fu brought the next piece of information on a sheet that had been folded several times. Lin Fu found the old match logs showed she had abandoned a winning strategy to help an injured teammate. The team compared its date with what they had witnessed for themselves. One line was persuasive; another contradicted the first. Mei Yu asked for the original source instead of selecting whichever version made their work easier. Even a genuine clue could be dangerous when removed from its context. Lin Fu found a reference to canoe in the old notes, but the page gave no reliable date. A witness mentioned device during the interview, so the team recorded the statement without endorsing it. The disputed notice used the word distinct, but its author had not signed the page. A written instruction told the staff to enlarge before the deadline, but nobody had logged the result.

林甫把下一份資料帶來，紙張已被折過好幾次。林甫查到舊賽紀錄，顯示她放棄致勝戰術是為了救受傷隊友。小隊把上面的日期與親眼所見逐項比較。一行文字很有說服力，下一行卻與它矛盾。美雨沒有挑比較方便的版本，而是要求查看原始來源。即使是真的線索，一旦脫離脈絡也可能造成危險。林甫在舊筆記裡找到關於「獨木舟」的記載，但紙上沒有可信的日期。見證人在訪談中提到「裝置」，小隊照實記錄，卻沒有直接認同。有爭議的通知使用「明顯不同的」一詞，作者卻沒有在頁面簽名。書面指示要求工作人員在期限前「放大」，卻沒有人記下結果。

An argument broke out before anyone could announce a solution. Director Tang offered a large reward if the guild obeyed his command without questions. Mei Yu did not win it with a long speech. Instead, the team drew two columns: what the record established and what remained uncertain. Director Tang challenged their interpretation, which was uncomfortable but useful. A plan that collapsed under one honest question was not ready to protect anyone outside the room. The report called the arrangement favorable, a judgment the group refused to accept without proof. A witness mentioned idiom during the interview, so the team recorded the statement without endorsing it. The evidence list included idol, although nobody had explained why that detail mattered. An inspector described the situation as imaginative; Mei Yu wanted measurements instead of adjectives.

尚未有人宣布解法，爭論便已爆發。唐總監開出高額獎勵，條件是公會不得質疑他的命令。美雨沒有用長篇演說壓倒對方，而是在紙上分成兩欄：紀錄已證實的事，以及仍然不確定的事。唐總監質疑他們的解讀，雖然令人不快，卻也有用。禁不起一次誠實提問的計畫，還不能拿去保護門外的人。報告說這項安排「有利的」，小隊不願在沒有證據前接受此判斷。見證人在訪談中提到「慣用語」，小隊照實記錄，卻沒有直接認同。證物清單上有「偶像」，卻沒有人解釋這個細節為何重要。檢查員把情況形容為「有想像力的」，美雨卻想要數據，而不是形容詞。

A crowd of former players could recite the final boss's attacks from memory. Few could explain how to keep a hospital functioning if the same battle took place beside its doors. Mei Yu asked for practical plans, not cheers. Lin Fu added the needs of people who could not run, fight, or use a phone. Their plans would never look as thrilling as a tournament trailer, but they belonged to the city rather than to a game's scoreboard.

一群舊玩家能背出最終首領的所有招式，卻很少有人能說明如果戰鬥發生在醫院門口，該如何維持醫院運作。美雨要求具體計畫，而不是歡呼。林甫補上不能奔跑、戰鬥或使用手機的人所需的安排。這些計畫不像賽事預告那麼刺激，卻屬於城市本身，而不是遊戲排行榜。

Nothing in the trial worked exactly as predicted. Mei Yu asked every member to choose a role with a clear safety limit instead. Mei Yu insisted on keeping the unsuccessful measurements instead of replacing them with cleaner numbers. That choice annoyed Director Tang, who wanted a quick announcement. But the failure established a boundary they had only guessed at before. A useful boundary was better than a promise built on wishful thinking. Lin Fu found a reference to impact in the old notes, but the page gave no reliable date. A witness mentioned objection during the interview, so the team recorded the statement without endorsing it. The evidence list included orchestra, although nobody had explained why that detail mattered. An inspector described the situation as portable; Mei Yu wanted measurements instead of adjectives.

這次測試沒有一項完全照預測發展。美雨要求每個成員自行選擇職務，訂出明確安全界線。美雨堅持留下不成功的測量值，不用好看的數字替換。唐總監急著發公告，因此相當不滿；然而失敗讓他們找到了先前只能猜測的限制。清楚的界線，總比建立在一廂情願上的承諾可靠。林甫在舊筆記裡找到關於「影響」的記載，但紙上沒有可信的日期。見證人在訪談中提到「反對意見」，小隊照實記錄，卻沒有直接認同。證物清單上有「管弦樂隊」，卻沒有人解釋這個細節為何重要。檢查員把情況形容為「便攜的」，美雨卻想要數據，而不是形容詞。

When evening arrived, the immediate danger had changed shape rather than vanished. Half the guild stayed, and the other half promised to share information without joining the fight. Mei Yu gathered the others and repeated only the facts they could support. Some people would need more time. Others had already decided to leave. Neither choice made anyone less worthy of protection. The next step belonged to those who would live with its consequences, not merely to the person speaking loudest. Lin Fu found a reference to prosperity in the old notes, but the page gave no reliable date. A witness mentioned protest during the interview, so the team recorded the statement without endorsing it. One witness promised to seize if conditions improved, but Mei Yu would not treat hope as evidence. The working file contained a separate entry for syllable, and Mei Yu asked who had verified it.

傍晚到來時，眼前的危險只是改變形態，並未完全消失。一半公會留下，另一半不參戰，但答應提供資訊。美雨召集同伴，只重述有證據支持的事。有人還需要時間，也有人已決定離開；這兩種選擇都不會使任何人失去受保護的資格。下一步應由承擔後果的人決定，而不是交給聲音最大的人。林甫在舊筆記裡找到關於「繁榮」的記載，但紙上沒有可信的日期。見證人在訪談中提到「抗議」，小隊照實記錄，卻沒有直接認同。一名見證人答應條件改善就會「奪取／扣押」，美雨卻不能把希望當成證據。工作檔案為「音節」設了獨立項目，美雨詢問是誰查證過。
