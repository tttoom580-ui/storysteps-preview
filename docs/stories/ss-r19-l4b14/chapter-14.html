Nian received offers to tell her story on reality shows, but most contracts required her to attack coworkers and exaggerate her distress. Something had changed, but not everybody noticed it in the same order. Chen Nian listened to the account of the nearest worker, then to someone standing farther away. Their differences were not necessarily lies. Each person had been given a different view and a different reason to be afraid of admitting uncertainty. The audience saw a star crossing a stage. Backstage workers saw dozens of precise tasks completed under deadlines. Chen Nian knew that a just account needed both views. The summary called the situation audio; Chen Nian asked which observation justified that description. On the next form, the action field read criticize, but the witness had never approved the operation.

陳念收到實境節目邀約，多數合約卻要求攻擊同事並誇大痛苦。某件事改變了，但人們發現它的順序不同。陳念先聽近處工作人員的描述，再聽遠處旁觀者說法。差異不一定是謊言：每個人看到的角度不同，承認不確定時所要承擔的風險也不同。觀眾看見明星走過舞台，後台員工看見數十項趕在期限前完成的精細工作。陳念知道公正說法需要兩種視角。摘要以「音訊」形容情況，陳念追問這種說法有何觀察根據。下一張表單把動作寫成「批評」，見證人卻從未同意這項操作。

Nian showed the costume log distinguishing the substitute from the insured original and the damaged duplicate. Chen Nian refused to pretend the previous decision had solved everything. The practical result was still there, useful but narrow. For each thing the team knew, there were two things it had not measured. Chen Nian drew a line through the claims that depended on speculation and asked Yu Zhi to keep the original record intact. A handwritten label read cunning, but no one could say who had written it there. The term delight appeared in the revised file; Chen Nian checked its meaning against the original page. On the next form, the action field read deserve, but the witness had never approved the operation.

陳念出示服裝紀錄，清楚區分替代品、保險真品與受損複製品。陳念不願假裝前一次決定已解決所有問題。成果確實存在，卻有明確界限。每個已知事項旁都還有未測量的部分。陳念劃掉靠猜測成立的說法，請余知保留原始紀錄。手寫標籤寫著「狡猾精明的」，卻沒有人知道是誰留下的。修訂檔案出現「喜悅」一詞，陳念拿原始頁面核對它的意思。下一張表單把動作寫成「值得」，見證人卻從未同意這項操作。

A small disagreement changed the schedule. Chen Nian wanted to proceed before evidence disappeared; Yu Zhi wanted another check because a wrong conclusion might hurt people who could not easily defend themselves. They did not settle the matter by choosing a leader. They wrote down what a short, reversible test could actually establish. On the next form, the action field read encounter, but the witness had never approved the operation. A handwritten label read frame, but no one could say who had written it there. The summary called the situation harsh; Chen Nian asked which observation justified that description.

一場小爭執改變了時程。陳念想趁證據消失前行動；余知要求再核對一次，因為錯誤結論可能傷害難以自辯的人。他們沒有靠選領袖解決爭議，而是列出短暫、可逆測試真正能證明什麼。下一張表單把動作寫成「遇見」，見證人卻從未同意這項操作。手寫標籤寫著「畫格」，卻沒有人知道是誰留下的。摘要以「嚴厲的」形容情況，陳念追問這種說法有何觀察根據。

She declined them and continued training, deciding she wanted a career built on work rather than an accusation. The plan had been amended three times, and each change left a trace. Chen Nian valued those corrections more than a perfect-looking first draft. If the outcome proved disappointing, the group would still be able to identify which assumption needed repair. Yu Zhi watched for the quiet participant who had not yet been invited to speak. The instructions used the verb identify without naming who would carry the risk; Chen Nian circled that gap. Yu Zhi noticed the word input in a note that someone had added after the first inspection. A handwritten label read literature, but no one could say who had written it there.

她拒絕邀約並繼續實習，希望靠作品而不是指控建立職涯。計畫改了三次，每次修改都留下痕跡。陳念認為這些修正比看似完美的初稿更有價值。就算結果不理想，團隊仍能知道該修正哪一項假設。余知也注意到那名一直沒有發言機會的人。指示裡用了「辨認」這個動詞，卻沒說誰承擔風險；陳念圈出這項缺漏。余知注意到初次檢查後有人在筆記裡加上「輸入」一詞。手寫標籤寫著「文學」，卻沒有人知道是誰留下的。

An official version reached the room before the practical work was finished. Chen Nian read it once, then compared it with what they had actually measured. The difference was not always malicious, but it could still hurt somebody. Yu Zhi asked that the correction be written in language the affected people could understand. The audience saw a star crossing a stage. Backstage workers saw dozens of precise tasks completed under deadlines. Chen Nian knew that a just account needed both views. A handwritten label read loan, but no one could say who had written it there. The term nationality appeared in the revised file; Chen Nian checked its meaning against the original page. One witness used the word peculiar; Yu Zhi noted that a description was not a measurement.

實際工作還沒完成，官方版本就傳到現場。陳念讀了一次，再比對真正測得的結果。差異未必都是惡意，卻仍可能傷害人。余知要求把更正寫成受影響者看得懂的語言。觀眾看見明星走過舞台，後台員工看見數十項趕在期限前完成的精細工作。陳念知道公正說法需要兩種視角。手寫標籤寫著「借用」，卻沒有人知道是誰留下的。修訂檔案出現「國籍」一詞，陳念拿原始頁面核對它的意思。一位證人用了「奇特的」一詞，余知指出形容並不等於測量。

A witness asked whether the team was taking sides. Chen Nian answered that refusing to invent a fact was not the same as refusing to care. What mattered was whether the procedure protected someone whose story was less convenient to tell. Yu Zhi listened without interrupting and wrote down the question exactly as it had been asked. Yu Zhi noticed the word pickle in a note that someone had added after the first inspection. A handwritten label read retire, but no one could say who had written it there. The term surgery appeared in the revised file; Chen Nian checked its meaning against the original page.

一名見證人問團隊是否偏袒。陳念回答，不捏造事實不代表冷漠。真正重要的是程序能否保護那個故事不方便被說出口的人。余知沒有打斷，逐字記下原問題。余知注意到初次檢查後有人在筆記裡加上「醃黃瓜」一詞。手寫標籤寫著「退休」，卻沒有人知道是誰留下的。修訂檔案出現「手術」一詞，陳念拿原始頁面核對它的意思。

Even after the group agreed on the immediate step, another question remained. The answer would not arrive through a dramatic announcement. Someone would have to return to the place, compare the earlier notes, and accept the possibility that the next piece of evidence might spoil an attractive theory. Chen Nian prepared to do exactly that. Yu Zhi had learned how quickly an audience could mistake silence for guilt. He chose to protect an original recording before saying anything that might become another headline. The summary called the situation sympathetic; Chen Nian asked which observation justified that description. Yu Zhi noticed the word torture in a note that someone had added after the first inspection. A handwritten label read virtue, but no one could say who had written it there.

即使大家同意眼前步驟，另一個問題仍在。答案不會靠戲劇性公告降臨；有人得返回現場、比對舊筆記，並接受新證據可能推翻漂亮理論。陳念準備做的正是這件事。余知早知道觀眾多容易把沉默當成有罪。他選擇先保護原始錄影，再說可能變成標題的話。摘要以「同情的」形容情況，陳念追問這種說法有何觀察根據。余知注意到初次檢查後有人在筆記裡加上「折磨」一詞。手寫標籤寫著「美德」，卻沒有人知道是誰留下的。
