Her Name in the Credits

At a fashion show Nian watched her own name roll across the screen as lead makeup artist, not as the girl from a scandal. Something had changed, but not everybody noticed it in the same order. Chen Nian listened to the account of the nearest worker, then to someone standing farther away. Their differences were not necessarily lies. Each person had been given a different view and a different reason to be afraid of admitting uncertainty. Yu Zhi had learned how quickly an audience could mistake silence for guilt. He chose to protect an original recording before saying anything that might become another headline. One witness used the word broke; Yu Zhi noted that a description was not a measurement. A handwritten label read championship, but no one could say who had written it there.

時裝秀上陳念看著自己以首席化妝師身分出現在片尾，而不是「醜聞中的女孩」。某件事改變了，但人們發現它的順序不同。陳念先聽近處工作人員的描述，再聽遠處旁觀者說法。差異不一定是謊言：每個人看到的角度不同，承認不確定時所要承擔的風險也不同。余知早知道觀眾多容易把沉默當成有罪。他選擇先保護原始錄影，再說可能變成標題的話。一位證人用了「身無分文的」一詞，余知指出形容並不等於測量。手寫標籤寫著「錦標賽」，卻沒有人知道是誰留下的。

Their work remained separate, and their conversations grew more honest because neither had to perform a role. Chen Nian refused to pretend the previous decision had solved everything. The practical result was still there, useful but narrow. For each thing the team knew, there were two things it had not measured. Chen Nian drew a line through the claims that depended on speculation and asked Yu Zhi to keep the original record intact. The term concerning appeared in the revised file; Chen Nian checked its meaning against the original page. Yu Zhi noticed the word electronics in a note that someone had added after the first inspection. A handwritten label read ethnic, but no one could say who had written it there.

工作各自獨立，兩人的對話反而因不必表演角色而更真實。陳念不願假裝前一次決定已解決所有問題。成果確實存在，卻有明確界限。每個已知事項旁都還有未測量的部分。陳念劃掉靠猜測成立的說法，請余知保留原始紀錄。修訂檔案出現「關於」一詞，陳念拿原始頁面核對它的意思。余知注意到初次檢查後有人在筆記裡加上「電子設備」一詞。手寫標籤寫著「族群文化的」，卻沒有人知道是誰留下的。

A small disagreement changed the schedule. Chen Nian wanted to proceed before evidence disappeared; Yu Zhi wanted another check because a wrong conclusion might hurt people who could not easily defend themselves. They did not settle the matter by choosing a leader. They wrote down what a short, reversible test could actually establish. An official memo described the plan as fantastic, but Chen Nian refused to treat an adjective as evidence. The term feast appeared in the revised file; Chen Nian checked its meaning against the original page. Yu Zhi noticed the word finance in a note that someone had added after the first inspection.

一場小爭執改變了時程。陳念想趁證據消失前行動；余知要求再核對一次，因為錯誤結論可能傷害難以自辯的人。他們沒有靠選領袖解決爭議，而是列出短暫、可逆測試真正能證明什麼。官方備忘錄形容計畫「奇幻的」，陳念不願把形容詞當作證據。修訂檔案出現「盛宴」一詞，陳念拿原始頁面核對它的意思。余知注意到初次檢查後有人在筆記裡加上「財務」一詞。

Yu Zhi waited outside with two ordinary tickets to a small concert; she took one, smiling because it was her own choice. The plan had been amended three times, and each change left a trace. Chen Nian valued those corrections more than a perfect-looking first draft. If the outcome proved disappointing, the group would still be able to identify which assumption needed repair. Yu Zhi watched for the quiet participant who had not yet been invited to speak. One witness used the word guilty; Yu Zhi noted that a description was not a measurement. Yu Zhi repeated the instruction to impose, then asked whether anyone had the right to refuse. The term offense appeared in the revised file; Chen Nian checked its meaning against the original page.

余知在外頭拿著兩張小型演唱會門票等她，她接過其中一張，笑的是這次選擇完全屬於自己。計畫改了三次，每次修改都留下痕跡。陳念認為這些修正比看似完美的初稿更有價值。就算結果不理想，團隊仍能知道該修正哪一項假設。余知也注意到那名一直沒有發言機會的人。一位證人用了「有罪的」一詞，余知指出形容並不等於測量。余知重述「強加」的指示，再問當事人是否有權拒絕。修訂檔案出現「罪行」一詞，陳念拿原始頁面核對它的意思。

An official version reached the room before the practical work was finished. Chen Nian read it once, then compared it with what they had actually measured. The difference was not always malicious, but it could still hurt somebody. Yu Zhi asked that the correction be written in language the affected people could understand. Yu Zhi had learned how quickly an audience could mistake silence for guilt. He chose to protect an original recording before saying anything that might become another headline. The term panel appeared in the revised file; Chen Nian checked its meaning against the original page. One witness used the word poisonous; Yu Zhi noted that a description was not a measurement. Yu Zhi repeated the instruction to preserve, then asked whether anyone had the right to refuse.

實際工作還沒完成，官方版本就傳到現場。陳念讀了一次，再比對真正測得的結果。差異未必都是惡意，卻仍可能傷害人。余知要求把更正寫成受影響者看得懂的語言。余知早知道觀眾多容易把沉默當成有罪。他選擇先保護原始錄影，再說可能變成標題的話。修訂檔案出現「審查小組」一詞，陳念拿原始頁面核對它的意思。一位證人用了「有毒的」一詞，余知指出形容並不等於測量。余知重述「保存」的指示，再問當事人是否有權拒絕。

A witness asked whether the team was taking sides. Chen Nian answered that refusing to invent a fact was not the same as refusing to care. What mattered was whether the procedure protected someone whose story was less convenient to tell. Yu Zhi listened without interrupting and wrote down the question exactly as it had been asked. A handwritten label read profession, but no one could say who had written it there. The term publication appeared in the revised file; Chen Nian checked its meaning against the original page. Yu Zhi noticed the word register in a note that someone had added after the first inspection.

一名見證人問團隊是否偏袒。陳念回答，不捏造事實不代表冷漠。真正重要的是程序能否保護那個故事不方便被說出口的人。余知沒有打斷，逐字記下原問題。手寫標籤寫著「職業」，卻沒有人知道是誰留下的。修訂檔案出現「出版品」一詞，陳念拿原始頁面核對它的意思。余知注意到初次檢查後有人在筆記裡加上「登記」一詞。

Nian's first show as lead makeup artist ended with her name on the credits, where she could read it without anyone else's permission. In the lobby a local animal shelter held a small fundraiser; its poster showed a tortoise resting beneath a painted tree. A volunteer outside used a spade to plant a real sapling, while the paper decorations began to sway above the entrance. Yu Zhi waited beside the shelter table with two ordinary concert tickets, not a publicity contract or a camera crew. 'You don't have to come,' he said. 'I know,' Nian replied, and smiled at how simple that answer felt. She checked that her assistants had been paid, placed her makeup brushes safely in their case, and stepped outside. The night belonged neither to a scandal nor to the singer. It belonged to the person she had worked to become.

陳念第一次以首席化妝師身分完成時裝秀，片尾名單上有她的名字，不必再經任何人批准才能看見。大廳裡本地動物收容所正在小型募款，海報畫著一隻陸龜在樹下休息。門外志工拿著鏟子種真正的小樹，入口上的紙飾在風中搖動。余知在攤位旁等她，手上只有兩張普通演唱會票，沒有宣傳合約，也沒有攝影團隊。「妳不用一定要來。」他說。「我知道。」陳念回答，因為這句話如此簡單而微笑。她先確認助理們領到薪水，把化妝刷收妥，才走到戶外。這一夜不屬於醜聞，也不屬於歌手，而屬於她努力成為的那個人。
