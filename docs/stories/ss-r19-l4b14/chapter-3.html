Nian drafted a precise statement explaining when she handled the case and asking viewers not to target other staff while records were checked. Something had changed, but not everybody noticed it in the same order. Chen Nian listened to the account of the nearest worker, then to someone standing farther away. Their differences were not necessarily lies. Each person had been given a different view and a different reason to be afraid of admitting uncertainty. Yu Zhi had learned how quickly an audience could mistake silence for guilt. He chose to protect an original recording before saying anything that might become another headline. A handwritten label read assign, but no one could say who had written it there. The term calorie appeared in the revised file; Chen Nian checked its meaning against the original page.

陳念起草精確聲明，說明自己何時接觸盒子，請大家查證期間不要攻擊其他員工。某件事改變了，但人們發現它的順序不同。陳念先聽近處工作人員的描述，再聽遠處旁觀者說法。差異不一定是謊言：每個人看到的角度不同，承認不確定時所要承擔的風險也不同。余知早知道觀眾多容易把沉默當成有罪。他選擇先保護原始錄影，再說可能變成標題的話。手寫標籤寫著「指派」，卻沒有人知道是誰留下的。修訂檔案出現「卡路里」一詞，陳念拿原始頁面核對它的意思。

Yu Zhi possessed an unedited backstage recording but warned that publishing it would reveal a coworker's private medical information. Chen Nian refused to pretend the previous decision had solved everything. The practical result was still there, useful but narrow. For each thing the team knew, there were two things it had not measured. Chen Nian drew a line through the claims that depended on speculation and asked Yu Zhi to keep the original record intact. On the next form, the action field read concentrate, but the witness had never approved the operation. People disagreed about context; Chen Nian separated the feeling from the facts they could test. The instructions used the verb defend without naming who would carry the risk; Chen Nian circled that gap.

余知持有未剪輯後台影像，但指出公開它會洩漏同事的私人病情。陳念不願假裝前一次決定已解決所有問題。成果確實存在，卻有明確界限。每個已知事項旁都還有未測量的部分。陳念劃掉靠猜測成立的說法，請余知保留原始紀錄。下一張表單把動作寫成「專注」，見證人卻從未同意這項操作。大家對「脈絡」意見不同，陳念把感受與可檢驗的事實分開。指示裡用了「支持」這個動詞，卻沒說誰承擔風險；陳念圈出這項缺漏。

A small disagreement changed the schedule. Chen Nian wanted to proceed before evidence disappeared; Yu Zhi wanted another check because a wrong conclusion might hurt people who could not easily defend themselves. They did not settle the matter by choosing a leader. They wrote down what a short, reversible test could actually establish. A note about the ferry lay beside the old register, with a date that Chen Nian could not verify. Yu Zhi pointed to the housework in the inventory and asked why its location had changed. An official memo described the plan as incredible, but Chen Nian refused to treat an adjective as evidence.

一場小爭執改變了時程。陳念想趁證據消失前行動；余知要求再核對一次，因為錯誤結論可能傷害難以自辯的人。他們沒有靠選領袖解決爭議，而是列出短暫、可逆測試真正能證明什麼。一張談到「渡輪」的便條放在舊登記簿旁，日期讓陳念無法核實。余知指向清單上的「家務」，追問其位置為何改變。官方備忘錄形容計畫「驚人的」，陳念不願把形容詞當作證據。

The agency rejected her wording because it refused to praise the company, strengthening her suspicion that reputation mattered more than truth. The plan had been amended three times, and each change left a trace. Chen Nian valued those corrections more than a perfect-looking first draft. If the outcome proved disappointing, the group would still be able to identify which assumption needed repair. Yu Zhi watched for the quiet participant who had not yet been invited to speak. A handwritten label read inspiration, but no one could say who had written it there. The term insult appeared in the revised file; Chen Nian checked its meaning against the original page. Yu Zhi noticed the word intermediate in a note that someone had added after the first inspection.

公司拒絕發表，因為內容沒有歌頌公司，更讓她懷疑公司重形象不重真相。計畫改了三次，每次修改都留下痕跡。陳念認為這些修正比看似完美的初稿更有價值。就算結果不理想，團隊仍能知道該修正哪一項假設。余知也注意到那名一直沒有發言機會的人。手寫標籤寫著「靈感」，卻沒有人知道是誰留下的。修訂檔案出現「侮辱」一詞，陳念拿原始頁面核對它的意思。余知注意到初次檢查後有人在筆記裡加上「中間版本」一詞。

An official version reached the room before the practical work was finished. Chen Nian read it once, then compared it with what they had actually measured. The difference was not always malicious, but it could still hurt somebody. Yu Zhi asked that the correction be written in language the affected people could understand. Yu Zhi had learned how quickly an audience could mistake silence for guilt. He chose to protect an original recording before saying anything that might become another headline. Yu Zhi pointed to the lifeguard in the inventory and asked why its location had changed. An official memo described the plan as luxurious, but Chen Nian refused to treat an adjective as evidence. The term maximum appeared in the revised file; Chen Nian checked its meaning against the original page.

實際工作還沒完成，官方版本就傳到現場。陳念讀了一次，再比對真正測得的結果。差異未必都是惡意，卻仍可能傷害人。余知要求把更正寫成受影響者看得懂的語言。余知早知道觀眾多容易把沉默當成有罪。他選擇先保護原始錄影，再說可能變成標題的話。余知指向清單上的「救生員」，追問其位置為何改變。官方備忘錄形容計畫「豪華的」，陳念不願把形容詞當作證據。修訂檔案出現「最大容量」一詞，陳念拿原始頁面核對它的意思。

A witness asked whether the team was taking sides. Chen Nian answered that refusing to invent a fact was not the same as refusing to care. What mattered was whether the procedure protected someone whose story was less convenient to tell. Yu Zhi listened without interrupting and wrote down the question exactly as it had been asked. The term pace appeared in the revised file; Chen Nian checked its meaning against the original page. Yu Zhi noticed the word percentage in a note that someone had added after the first inspection. A handwritten label read photography, but no one could say who had written it there.

一名見證人問團隊是否偏袒。陳念回答，不捏造事實不代表冷漠。真正重要的是程序能否保護那個故事不方便被說出口的人。余知沒有打斷，逐字記下原問題。修訂檔案出現「步調」一詞，陳念拿原始頁面核對它的意思。余知注意到初次檢查後有人在筆記裡加上「百分比」一詞。手寫標籤寫著「攝影」，卻沒有人知道是誰留下的。

Even after the group agreed on the immediate step, another question remained. The answer would not arrive through a dramatic announcement. Someone would have to return to the place, compare the earlier notes, and accept the possibility that the next piece of evidence might spoil an attractive theory. Chen Nian prepared to do exactly that. The audience saw a star crossing a stage. Backstage workers saw dozens of precise tasks completed under deadlines. Chen Nian knew that a just account needed both views. A handwritten label read priority, but no one could say who had written it there. The instructions used the verb proceed without naming who would carry the risk; Chen Nian circled that gap. Yu Zhi pointed to the raisin in the inventory and asked why its location had changed.

即使大家同意眼前步驟，另一個問題仍在。答案不會靠戲劇性公告降臨；有人得返回現場、比對舊筆記，並接受新證據可能推翻漂亮理論。陳念準備做的正是這件事。觀眾看見明星走過舞台，後台員工看見數十項趕在期限前完成的精細工作。陳念知道公正說法需要兩種視角。手寫標籤寫著「優先事項」，卻沒有人知道是誰留下的。指示裡用了「繼續進行」這個動詞，卻沒說誰承擔風險；陳念圈出這項缺漏。余知指向清單上的「葡萄乾」，追問其位置為何改變。
