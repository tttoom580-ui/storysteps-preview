Yu Zhi invited Nian to a closed rehearsal where a new song described losing one's name under a crowd's judgment. Something had changed, but not everybody noticed it in the same order. Chen Nian listened to the account of the nearest worker, then to someone standing farther away. Their differences were not necessarily lies. Each person had been given a different view and a different reason to be afraid of admitting uncertainty. Yu Zhi had learned how quickly an audience could mistake silence for guilt. He chose to protect an original recording before saying anything that might become another headline. A handwritten label read accent, but no one could say who had written it there. The term attach appeared in the revised file; Chen Nian checked its meaning against the original page.

余知邀陳念參加閉門排練，新歌唱的是人在群眾評斷下失去名字。某件事改變了，但人們發現它的順序不同。陳念先聽近處工作人員的描述，再聽遠處旁觀者說法。差異不一定是謊言：每個人看到的角度不同，承認不確定時所要承擔的風險也不同。余知早知道觀眾多容易把沉默當成有罪。他選擇先保護原始錄影，再說可能變成標題的話。手寫標籤寫著「口音」，卻沒有人知道是誰留下的。修訂檔案出現「附件」一詞，陳念拿原始頁面核對它的意思。

The entry established access but not wrongdoing; she asked the analyst to verify the signature before identifying anyone publicly. Chen Nian refused to pretend the previous decision had solved everything. The practical result was still there, useful but narrow. For each thing the team knew, there were two things it had not measured. Chen Nian drew a line through the claims that depended on speculation and asked Yu Zhi to keep the original record intact. Yu Zhi noticed the word circumstance in a note that someone had added after the first inspection. An official memo described the plan as clumsy, but Chen Nian refused to treat an adjective as evidence. The term content appeared in the revised file; Chen Nian checked its meaning against the original page.

A small disagreement changed the schedule. Chen Nian wanted to proceed before evidence disappeared; Yu Zhi wanted another check because a wrong conclusion might hurt people who could not easily defend themselves. They did not settle the matter by choosing a leader. They wrote down what a short, reversible test could actually establish. The summary called the situation crunchy; Chen Nian asked which observation justified that description. Yu Zhi pointed to the earphone in the inventory and asked why its location had changed. An official memo described the plan as economic, but Chen Nian refused to treat an adjective as evidence.

一場小爭執改變了時程。陳念想趁證據消失前行動；余知要求再核對一次，因為錯誤結論可能傷害難以自辯的人。他們沒有靠選領袖解決爭議，而是列出短暫、可逆測試真正能證明什麼。摘要以「酥脆的」形容情況，陳念追問這種說法有何觀察根據。余知指向清單上的「耳機」，追問其位置為何改變。官方備忘錄形容計畫「經濟的」，陳念不願把形容詞當作證據。

Nian praised the music but asked him not to turn her experience into promotional material without permission. The plan had been amended three times, and each change left a trace. Chen Nian valued those corrections more than a perfect-looking first draft. If the outcome proved disappointing, the group would still be able to identify which assumption needed repair. Yu Zhi watched for the quiet participant who had not yet been invited to speak. A handwritten label read frown, but no one could say who had written it there. The term homeland appeared in the revised file; Chen Nian checked its meaning against the original page. On the next form, the action field read instruct, but the witness had never approved the operation.

陳念喜歡歌，卻要求不要未經同意把她的遭遇變成宣傳素材。計畫改了三次，每次修改都留下痕跡。陳念認為這些修正比看似完美的初稿更有價值。就算結果不理想，團隊仍能知道該修正哪一項假設。余知也注意到那名一直沒有發言機會的人。手寫標籤寫著「皺眉」，卻沒有人知道是誰留下的。修訂檔案出現「故鄉」一詞，陳念拿原始頁面核對它的意思。下一張表單把動作寫成「指示」，見證人卻從未同意這項操作。

An official version reached the room before the practical work was finished. Chen Nian read it once, then compared it with what they had actually measured. The difference was not always malicious, but it could still hurt somebody. Yu Zhi asked that the correction be written in language the affected people could understand. Yu Zhi had learned how quickly an audience could mistake silence for guilt. He chose to protect an original recording before saying anything that might become another headline. The instructions used the verb mislead without naming who would carry the risk; Chen Nian circled that gap.

實際工作還沒完成，官方版本就傳到現場。陳念讀了一次，再比對真正測得的結果。差異未必都是惡意，卻仍可能傷害人。余知要求把更正寫成受影響者看得懂的語言。余知早知道觀眾多容易把沉默當成有罪。他選擇先保護原始錄影，再說可能變成標題的話。余知注意到初次檢查後有人在筆記裡加上「中斷」一詞。官方備忘錄形容計畫「俯身」，陳念不願把形容詞當作證據。指示裡用了「誤導」這個動詞，卻沒說誰承擔風險；陳念圈出這項缺漏。

A witness asked whether the team was taking sides. Chen Nian answered that refusing to invent a fact was not the same as refusing to care. What mattered was whether the procedure protected someone whose story was less convenient to tell. Yu Zhi listened without interrupting and wrote down the question exactly as it had been asked. The term modesty appeared in the revised file; Chen Nian checked its meaning against the original page. One witness used the word nuclear; Yu Zhi noted that a description was not a measurement. A handwritten label read peep, but no one could say who had written it there.

一名見證人問團隊是否偏袒。陳念回答，不捏造事實不代表冷漠。真正重要的是程序能否保護那個故事不方便被說出口的人。余知沒有打斷，逐字記下原問題。修訂檔案出現「審慎」一詞，陳念拿原始頁面核對它的意思。一位證人用了「核能的」一詞，余知指出形容並不等於測量。手寫標籤寫著「偷瞄」，卻沒有人知道是誰留下的。

Even after the group agreed on the immediate step, another question remained. The answer would not arrive through a dramatic announcement. Someone would have to return to the place, compare the earlier notes, and accept the possibility that the next piece of evidence might spoil an attractive theory. Chen Nian prepared to do exactly that. The audience saw a star crossing a stage. Backstage workers saw dozens of precise tasks completed under deadlines. Chen Nian knew that a just account needed both views. A handwritten label read refund, but no one could say who had written it there. The term revolutionary appeared in the revised file; Chen Nian checked its meaning against the original page. Yu Zhi noticed the word wink in a note that someone had added after the first inspection.

即使大家同意眼前步驟，另一個問題仍在。答案不會靠戲劇性公告降臨；有人得返回現場、比對舊筆記，並接受新證據可能推翻漂亮理論。陳念準備做的正是這件事。觀眾看見明星走過舞台，後台員工看見數十項趕在期限前完成的精細工作。陳念知道公正說法需要兩種視角。手寫標籤寫著「退款」，卻沒有人知道是誰留下的。修訂檔案出現「革命性的」一詞，陳念拿原始頁面核對它的意思。余知注意到初次檢查後有人在筆記裡加上「眨眼」一詞。
