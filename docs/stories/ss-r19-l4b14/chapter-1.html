The Theft on Trending Now

By noon the internet had decided Chen Nian was a thief. The photograph was simple enough to convey in a single glance: a trainee makeup artist holding the velvet case of a stolen necklace behind a famous singer's dressing room. But Nian remembered who had put the case in her hand, and she remembered that it was already empty. Across the stage, technicians rolled a painted cottage into position for the evening's music video. Nobody stopped for her question. Her employee badge still opened the side corridor, while her message inbox filled with insults from strangers. Then Yu Zhi appeared at the doorway. He did not tell her to be brave or grateful. 'I want the original recording preserved,' he told security. 'All of it, not the part that makes a good headline.'

中午之前，網路已認定陳念是小偷。那張照片一眼就能傳達指控：實習化妝師在知名歌手的化妝間後拿著失竊項鍊的天鵝絨盒。但陳念記得誰把盒子交給她，也記得當時盒子已經空了。舞台另一端，技師正把音樂錄影帶用的小屋布景推到定位。沒有人停下來聽她解釋。她的員工證仍能打開側廊，訊息信箱卻塞滿陌生人的辱罵。這時余知站在門口。他沒有叫她勇敢，也沒要求她感謝。「我要保存原始錄影。」他對保全說，「全部，不是適合寫標題的那一段。」

Nian sat on the cushion beneath the dressing-room mirror and tried not to touch her phone again. Her first defense had been a sentence typed in panic: I DIDN'T STEAL IT. The agency had refused to post it, claiming that an apology would show more diversity and maturity than a denial. She looked at the original shift schedule instead. At the minute when the missing necklace was supposedly taken, she was helping a dancer cover a bruise with stage makeup; three people had watched. But the camera angle showed only the velvet case in her hands two minutes later. Nian wrote down those two minutes as carefully as she would blend foundation across an uneven patch of skin.

陳念坐在化妝鏡下的軟墊椅上，努力不再碰手機。她最早的辯護是慌亂打出的句子：「我沒有偷。」公司拒絕發布，說道歉比否認更能展現多元與成熟。她改去看原始班表。據稱項鍊被拿走的那分鐘，她正在替舞者用舞台妝遮住瘀青，有三個人目睹。但鏡頭只拍到兩分鐘後她手上拿著天鵝絨盒。她仔細記下這兩分鐘，就像她會細心地把粉底推勻在凹凸不平的皮膚上。

The online attacks were fierce. One account called Nian a genius thief; another tried to flatter the agency by praising its 'decisive protection' of the singer. Nobody asked the people who had worked beside her. A senior stylist sent a private message: Keep the original schedule. Don't let them replace it with a cleaned version. Nian saved the message but did not publish the sender's name. She understood how quickly the crowd would turn on a new target. Across the corridor, Yu Zhi stood listening to the head of publicity describe the scandal as a problem of fan emotion. 'No,' he said at last. 'It is a question of what the recording shows.' The head of publicity stopped smiling.

網路攻擊猛烈得可怕。一個帳號稱陳念是天才小偷，另一個奉承公司「果斷保護」歌手的決策。沒有誰去問與她共事的人。資深造型師私訊：「保留原始班表，別讓他們換成整理過的版本。」陳念存下訊息，卻沒有公開對方姓名，因為群眾隨時會轉向下一個目標。走廊另一頭，余知聽公關主管把事件說成粉絲情緒問題。「不。」他終於開口，「問題是錄影究竟拍到了什麼。」公關主管不再微笑。

A dancer passing the makeup room gave Nian a quick, anxious giggle, then apologized for sounding nervous. She had been rehearsing a stunt using a bright paper parachute, a prop that now lay tangled in the corridor beside the empty necklace display. The dancer was pessimistic about the investigation. 'They already wrote the headline,' she said. Nian asked whether she could describe the moment the box changed hands, without agreeing to go on camera. The dancer nodded. The assistant had brought the case to Nian and said the clasp was damaged. This was the first independent account matching Nian's memory. She wrote it down and asked the dancer to correct anything she had misunderstood.

一名路過化妝間的舞者緊張地笑了一聲，立刻為自己的不安道歉。她正在排練使用鮮豔紙降落傘的特技，那個道具此刻纏在走廊裡、空項鍊展示櫃旁。舞者對調查很悲觀。「標題都寫好了。」她說。陳念問她是否願意描述盒子轉手的那一刻，不必上鏡頭。舞者點頭。助理把盒子交給陳念時說的是扣環損壞。這是第一份與陳念記憶相符的獨立說法。她寫下經過，請舞者糾正任何可能記錯的地方。

Yu Zhi did not possess the authority to order the agency to clear Nian's name, although fans imagined every celebrity could command the people around him. At the afternoon presentation he refused to repeat the theft accusation as fact. Instead, he asked the legal department for a reference to the original security recording and a documented chain of custody. The manager offered him a few selected frames. He asked for the whole file. Nian noticed that he did not describe himself as her rescuer. He described a procedure that any accused worker should be entitled to request. When the meeting ended, he handed Nian the independent lawyer's contact details and left her alone to decide whether to call.

余知並沒有命令公司替陳念平反的權力，雖然粉絲總以為明星能指揮周遭所有人。下午簡報上，他拒絕把失竊指控說成事實，而是向法務部門要求原始監視錄影的參考資料與完整保管鏈。經紀主管給他幾張挑過的影格，他要的是整個檔案。陳念注意到，他沒有把自己說成拯救她的人，只說每位被指控員工都應該有權提出這類要求。會議結束後，他把獨立律師聯絡方式交給陳念，讓她自行決定是否致電。

The singer's public image was built around a spiritual, almost untouchable perfection. Nian had spent months seeing the tremendous work of ordinary people that made that image possible: hairpins, missed dinners, carefully washed brushes, and workers holding their breath before a cue. To accuse one of them without checking the recording was a violation of trust that no beautiful song could erase. She returned to the dressing room to collect her own kit. Under the mirror lay the first test strip she had painted that morning, time-stamped and signed. Beside it was a note: The case was empty when it arrived. She recognized the handwriting as her assistant's, and the writer had not yet spoken to the investigators.

歌手公眾形象帶著精神性、幾乎不可觸及的完美感。陳念工作數月，卻看見塑造那形象需要普通人付出多麼巨大的勞動：髮夾、錯過的晚餐、仔細洗淨的刷具，以及等待提示時屏住呼吸的工作者。不核對錄影就指控其中一人，是任何美妙歌曲都無法消除的信任違規。她回化妝間收自己的工具，在鏡下找到早上畫的第一張試色條，標有時間和簽名。旁邊還有一張便條：「送來時盒子就是空的。」她認出字跡屬於助理，而那個人還沒向調查者開口。

Nian examined the visual timeline the agency had prepared. It showed the moment she touched the box but not the assistant who had brought it; a witness had been cropped out as neatly as a wrinkle from a fashion photograph. Beyond the door, dancers finished their last workout while stage lights warmed the painted walls. Yu Zhi waited as Nian marked the missing twenty-two seconds. 'You don't have to give me the footage,' she said. 'I need an independent copy and the right to decide what happens to my name.' He nodded and asked security to seal the memory card. A new message flashed on Nian's phone before they could leave: APOLOGIZE TONIGHT OR LOSE EVERY JOB YOU WILL EVER HAVE.

陳念檢查公司準備的視覺時間線。上面有她碰到盒子的瞬間，卻沒有遞盒子的助理；一名證人被裁掉，就像時尚照片裡的皺紋被修得乾乾淨淨。門外，舞者完成最後一輪體能訓練，舞台燈光烘熱了布景牆。余知等待陳念標出消失的二十二秒。「你不必直接把影片交給我。」她說，「我要獨立副本，也要有權決定我的名字怎麼被使用。」他點頭，請保全封存記憶卡。兩人還沒離開，陳念手機又跳出訊息：「今晚道歉，否則你這輩子別想再有工作。」
