A prop manager showed that the insured necklace had a stage duplicate; the viral accusation treated the two pieces as identical. Something had changed, but not everybody noticed it in the same order. Chen Nian listened to the account of the nearest worker, then to someone standing farther away. Their differences were not necessarily lies. Each person had been given a different view and a different reason to be afraid of admitting uncertainty. The audience saw a star crossing a stage. Backstage workers saw dozens of precise tasks completed under deadlines. Chen Nian knew that a just account needed both views. A handwritten label read conservative, but no one could say who had written it there. The term consultant appeared in the revised file; Chen Nian checked its meaning against the original page.

道具經理證明保險項鍊有舞台複製品，熱門指控卻把兩件物品混為一談。某件事改變了，但人們發現它的順序不同。陳念先聽近處工作人員的描述，再聽遠處旁觀者說法。差異不一定是謊言：每個人看到的角度不同，承認不確定時所要承擔的風險也不同。觀眾看見明星走過舞台，後台員工看見數十項趕在期限前完成的精細工作。陳念知道公正說法需要兩種視角。手寫標籤寫著「保守的」，卻沒有人知道是誰留下的。修訂檔案出現「顧問」一詞，陳念拿原始頁面核對它的意思。

Nian accepted the contact information but insisted she would make decisions about her own testimony. Chen Nian refused to pretend the previous decision had solved everything. The practical result was still there, useful but narrow. For each thing the team knew, there were two things it had not measured. Chen Nian drew a line through the claims that depended on speculation and asked Yu Zhi to keep the original record intact. Yu Zhi noticed the word consumer in a note that someone had added after the first inspection. A handwritten label read dye, but no one could say who had written it there. The instructions used the verb enclose without naming who would carry the risk; Chen Nian circled that gap.

陳念接受聯絡資料，堅持由自己決定如何作證。陳念不願假裝前一次決定已解決所有問題。成果確實存在，卻有明確界限。每個已知事項旁都還有未測量的部分。陳念劃掉靠猜測成立的說法，請余知保留原始紀錄。余知注意到初次檢查後有人在筆記裡加上「消費者」一詞。手寫標籤寫著「染料」，卻沒有人知道是誰留下的。指示裡用了「封入」這個動詞，卻沒說誰承擔風險；陳念圈出這項缺漏。

A small disagreement changed the schedule. Chen Nian wanted to proceed before evidence disappeared; Yu Zhi wanted another check because a wrong conclusion might hurt people who could not easily defend themselves. They did not settle the matter by choosing a leader. They wrote down what a short, reversible test could actually establish. The term goods appeared in the revised file; Chen Nian checked its meaning against the original page. Yu Zhi noticed the word grace in a note that someone had added after the first inspection. An official memo described the plan as habitual, but Chen Nian refused to treat an adjective as evidence.

一場小爭執改變了時程。陳念想趁證據消失前行動；余知要求再核對一次，因為錯誤結論可能傷害難以自辯的人。他們沒有靠選領袖解決爭議，而是列出短暫、可逆測試真正能證明什麼。修訂檔案出現「貨品」一詞，陳念拿原始頁面核對它的意思。余知注意到初次檢查後有人在筆記裡加上「從容」一詞。官方備忘錄形容計畫「習慣性的」，陳念不願把形容詞當作證據。

The duplicate was missing from inventory, while the original remained in a locked safe. The plan had been amended three times, and each change left a trace. Chen Nian valued those corrections more than a perfect-looking first draft. If the outcome proved disappointing, the group would still be able to identify which assumption needed repair. Yu Zhi watched for the quiet participant who had not yet been invited to speak. An official memo described the plan as ignorant, but Chen Nian refused to treat an adjective as evidence. The term invest appeared in the revised file; Chen Nian checked its meaning against the original page. On the next form, the action field read isolate, but the witness had never approved the operation.

道具複製品不見了，真品其實仍鎖在保險箱。計畫改了三次，每次修改都留下痕跡。陳念認為這些修正比看似完美的初稿更有價值。就算結果不理想，團隊仍能知道該修正哪一項假設。余知也注意到那名一直沒有發言機會的人。官方備忘錄形容計畫「不了解的」，陳念不願把形容詞當作證據。修訂檔案出現「投資」一詞，陳念拿原始頁面核對它的意思。下一張表單把動作寫成「隔離」，見證人卻從未同意這項操作。

An official version reached the room before the practical work was finished. Chen Nian read it once, then compared it with what they had actually measured. The difference was not always malicious, but it could still hurt somebody. Yu Zhi asked that the correction be written in language the affected people could understand. Yu Zhi noticed the word linen in a note that someone had added after the first inspection. A handwritten label read luxury, but no one could say who had written it there. The term microscope appeared in the revised file; Chen Nian checked its meaning against the original page.

實際工作還沒完成，官方版本就傳到現場。陳念讀了一次，再比對真正測得的結果。差異未必都是惡意，卻仍可能傷害人。余知要求把更正寫成受影響者看得懂的語言。觀眾看見明星走過舞台，後台員工看見數十項趕在期限前完成的精細工作。陳念知道公正說法需要兩種視角。余知注意到初次檢查後有人在筆記裡加上「亞麻布」一詞。手寫標籤寫著「奢華」，卻沒有人知道是誰留下的。修訂檔案出現「顯微鏡」一詞，陳念拿原始頁面核對它的意思。

A witness asked whether the team was taking sides. Chen Nian answered that refusing to invent a fact was not the same as refusing to care. What mattered was whether the procedure protected someone whose story was less convenient to tell. Yu Zhi listened without interrupting and wrote down the question exactly as it had been asked. The term occupation appeared in the revised file; Chen Nian checked its meaning against the original page. Yu Zhi noticed the word orientation in a note that someone had added after the first inspection. A handwritten label read rage, but no one could say who had written it there.

一名見證人問團隊是否偏袒。陳念回答，不捏造事實不代表冷漠。真正重要的是程序能否保護那個故事不方便被說出口的人。余知沒有打斷，逐字記下原問題。修訂檔案出現「職業」一詞，陳念拿原始頁面核對它的意思。余知注意到初次檢查後有人在筆記裡加上「職前說明」一詞。手寫標籤寫著「盛怒」，卻沒有人知道是誰留下的。

Even after the group agreed on the immediate step, another question remained. The answer would not arrive through a dramatic announcement. Someone would have to return to the place, compare the earlier notes, and accept the possibility that the next piece of evidence might spoil an attractive theory. Chen Nian prepared to do exactly that. Yu Zhi had learned how quickly an audience could mistake silence for guilt. He chose to protect an original recording before saying anything that might become another headline. A handwritten label read stereo, but no one could say who had written it there. The summary called the situation timid; Chen Nian asked which observation justified that description. 即使大家同意眼前步驟，另一個問題仍在。答案不會靠戲劇性公告降臨；有人得返回現場、比對舊筆記，並接受新證據可能推翻漂亮理論。陳念準備做的正是這件事。余知早知道觀眾多容易把沉默當成有罪。他選擇先保護原始錄影，再說可能變成標題的話。手寫標籤寫著「立體聲」，卻沒有人知道是誰留下的。摘要以「膽小的」形容情況，陳念追問這種說法有何觀察根據。下一張表單把動作寫成「顫抖」，見證人卻從未同意這項操作。
