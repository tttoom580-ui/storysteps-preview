Yu Zhi offered Nian access to a lawyer and independent digital analyst, without asking her to sign an exclusive contract with him. Something had changed, but not everybody noticed it in the same order. Chen Nian listened to the account of the nearest worker, then to someone standing farther away. Their differences were not necessarily lies. Each person had been given a different view and a different reason to be afraid of admitting uncertainty. Yu Zhi had learned how quickly an audience could mistake silence for guilt. He chose to protect an original recording before saying anything that might become another headline. The instructions used the verb abandon without naming who would carry the risk; Chen Nian circled that gap. Yu Zhi noticed the word adjust in a note that someone had added after the first inspection.

余知主動提供律師與獨立數位分析員的資源，沒有要求陳念簽任何獨家契約。某件事改變了，但人們發現它的順序不同。陳念先聽近處工作人員的描述，再聽遠處旁觀者說法。差異不一定是謊言：每個人看到的角度不同，承認不確定時所要承擔的風險也不同。余知早知道觀眾多容易把沉默當成有罪。他選擇先保護原始錄影，再說可能變成標題的話。指示裡用了「拋下」這個動詞，卻沒說誰承擔風險；陳念圈出這項缺漏。余知注意到初次檢查後有人在筆記裡加上「調整」一詞。

Nian and the cleaner signed separate descriptions before comparing them, keeping their statements independent. Chen Nian refused to pretend the previous decision had solved everything. The practical result was still there, useful but narrow. For each thing the team knew, there were two things it had not measured. Chen Nian drew a line through the claims that depended on speculation and asked Yu Zhi to keep the original record intact. They did not settle the matter by choosing a leader. They wrote down what a short, reversible test could actually establish. Chen Nian valued those corrections more than a perfect-looking first draft. If the outcome proved disappointing, the group would still be able to identify which assumption needed repair. What mattered was whether the procedure protected someone whose story was less convenient to tell. Yu Zhi listened without interrupting and wrote down the question exactly as it had been asked. 一名見證人問團隊是否偏袒。陳念回答，不捏造事實不代表冷漠。真正重要的是程序能否保護那個故事不方便被說出口的人。余知沒有打斷，逐字記下原問題。余知注意到初次檢查後有人在筆記裡加上「管弦樂隊」一詞。官方備忘錄形容計畫「便攜的」，陳念不願把形容詞當作證據。修訂檔案出現「繁榮」一詞，陳念拿原始頁面核對它的意思。

Even after the group agreed on the immediate step, another question remained. The answer would not arrive through a dramatic announcement. Someone would have to return to the place, compare the earlier notes, and accept the possibility that the next piece of evidence might spoil an attractive theory. Chen Nian prepared to do exactly that. The audience saw a star crossing a stage. Backstage workers saw dozens of precise tasks completed under deadlines.
