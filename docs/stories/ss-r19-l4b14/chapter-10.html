At a press conference Yu Zhi released a legally reviewed timeline and redacted camera stills, with Nian's approval and the coworkers' privacy protected. Something had changed, but not everybody noticed it in the same order. Chen Nian listened to the account of the nearest worker, then to someone standing farther away. Their differences were not necessarily lies. Each person had been given a different view and a different reason to be afraid of admitting uncertainty. The audience saw a star crossing a stage. Backstage workers saw dozens of precise tasks completed under deadlines. Chen Nian knew that a just account needed both views. Yu Zhi noticed the word apology in a note that someone had added after the first inspection. The earlier photograph clearly showed a bandage; the newer version did not, so Chen Nian kept both.

記者會上余知經陳念同意公開法律審閱過的時間表與遮蔽個資的影像。某件事改變了，但人們發現它的順序不同。陳念先聽近處工作人員的描述，再聽遠處旁觀者說法。差異不一定是謊言：每個人看到的角度不同，承認不確定時所要承擔的風險也不同。觀眾看見明星走過舞台，後台員工看見數十項趕在期限前完成的精細工作。陳念知道公正說法需要兩種視角。余知注意到初次檢查後有人在筆記裡加上「道歉」一詞。較早的照片清楚顯示一項「繃帶」，新版卻沒有，陳念因此保留兩張。

Nian praised the music but asked him not to turn her experience into promotional material without permission. Chen Nian refused to pretend the previous decision had solved everything. The practical result was still there, useful but narrow. For each thing the team knew, there were two things it had not measured. Chen Nian drew a line through the claims that depended on speculation and asked Yu Zhi to keep the original record intact. The term bargain appeared in the revised file; Chen Nian checked its meaning against the original page. Yu Zhi noticed the word civilization in a note that someone had added after the first inspection. Yu Zhi repeated the instruction to congratulate, then asked whether anyone had the right to refuse.

陳念喜歡歌，卻要求不要未經同意把她的遭遇變成宣傳素材。陳念不願假裝前一次決定已解決所有問題。成果確實存在，卻有明確界限。每個已知事項旁都還有未測量的部分。陳念劃掉靠猜測成立的說法，請余知保留原始紀錄。修訂檔案出現「交易」一詞，陳念拿原始頁面核對它的意思。余知注意到初次檢查後有人在筆記裡加上「文明」一詞。余知重述「恭賀」的指示，再問當事人是否有權拒絕。

A small disagreement changed the schedule. Chen Nian wanted to proceed before evidence disappeared; Yu Zhi wanted another check because a wrong conclusion might hurt people who could not easily defend themselves. They did not settle the matter by choosing a leader. They wrote down what a short, reversible test could actually establish. An official memo described the plan as critical, but Chen Nian refused to treat an adjective as evidence. The term explosion appeared in the revised file; Chen Nian checked its meaning against the original page. On the next form, the action field read forbid, but the witness had never approved the operation.

一場小爭執改變了時程。陳念想趁證據消失前行動；余知要求再核對一次，因為錯誤結論可能傷害難以自辯的人。他們沒有靠選領袖解決爭議，而是列出短暫、可逆測試真正能證明什麼。官方備忘錄形容計畫「關鍵的」，陳念不願把形容詞當作證據。修訂檔案出現「爆炸」一詞，陳念拿原始頁面核對它的意思。下一張表單把動作寫成「禁止」，見證人卻從未同意這項操作。

The footage disproved the central accusation, yet a manager unexpectedly challenged the authenticity of the original files. The plan had been amended three times, and each change left a trace. Chen Nian valued those corrections more than a perfect-looking first draft. If the outcome proved disappointing, the group would still be able to identify which assumption needed repair. Yu Zhi watched for the quiet participant who had not yet been invited to speak. On the next form, the action field read illustrate, but the witness had never approved the operation. Yu Zhi repeated the instruction to install, then asked whether anyone had the right to refuse. The summary called the situation mere; Chen Nian asked which observation justified that description.

影像推翻核心指控，經紀主管卻突然質疑原始檔案的真偽。計畫改了三次，每次修改都留下痕跡。陳念認為這些修正比看似完美的初稿更有價值。就算結果不理想，團隊仍能知道該修正哪一項假設。余知也注意到那名一直沒有發言機會的人。下一張表單把動作寫成「圖解說明」，見證人卻從未同意這項操作。余知重述「安裝」的指示，再問當事人是否有權拒絕。摘要以「僅僅的」形容情況，陳念追問這種說法有何觀察根據。

An official version reached the room before the practical work was finished. Chen Nian read it once, then compared it with what they had actually measured. The difference was not always malicious, but it could still hurt somebody. Yu Zhi asked that the correction be written in language the affected people could understand. The audience saw a star crossing a stage. Backstage workers saw dozens of precise tasks completed under deadlines. Chen Nian knew that a just account needed both views. The summary called the situation pregnant; Chen Nian asked which observation justified that description. One witness used the word prosperous; Yu Zhi noted that a description was not a measurement. A handwritten label read resolution, but no one could say who had written it there.

實際工作還沒完成，官方版本就傳到現場。陳念讀了一次，再比對真正測得的結果。差異未必都是惡意，卻仍可能傷害人。余知要求把更正寫成受影響者看得懂的語言。觀眾看見明星走過舞台，後台員工看見數十項趕在期限前完成的精細工作。陳念知道公正說法需要兩種視角。摘要以「懷孕的」形容情況，陳念追問這種說法有何觀察根據。一位證人用了「繁榮的」一詞，余知指出形容並不等於測量。手寫標籤寫著「決議」，卻沒有人知道是誰留下的。

A witness asked whether the team was taking sides. Chen Nian answered that refusing to invent a fact was not the same as refusing to care. What mattered was whether the procedure protected someone whose story was less convenient to tell. Yu Zhi listened without interrupting and wrote down the question exactly as it had been asked. A handwritten label read revolution, but no one could say who had written it there. The term settler appeared in the revised file; Chen Nian checked its meaning against the original page. Yu Zhi noticed the word surgeon in a note that someone had added after the first inspection.

一名見證人問團隊是否偏袒。陳念回答，不捏造事實不代表冷漠。真正重要的是程序能否保護那個故事不方便被說出口的人。余知沒有打斷，逐字記下原問題。手寫標籤寫著「革命」，卻沒有人知道是誰留下的。修訂檔案出現「遷居者」一詞，陳念拿原始頁面核對它的意思。余知注意到初次檢查後有人在筆記裡加上「外科醫師」一詞。

The singer's careful statement was already on the website when the agency director called the original memory card suspicious. On the screen, the dressing room looked ordinary: a velvet case, a worker passing it to Nian, then twenty-two seconds hidden by the cropped photograph. A tomb-themed set piece blocked the lower camera angle, which was why the director said the footage could not be trusted. Nian refused both blind faith and automatic rejection. She asked the independent analyst to preserve the card's checksum and the storage register. Yu Zhi stepped back so the cameras could see whose decision it was. The correction went live. For eight seconds Nian believed the worst was over. Then the director announced the agency had found a different file, and the room turned toward him.

歌手的審慎聲明已登上網站，經紀主管卻突然說原始記憶卡可疑。畫面中的化妝間平凡無奇：天鵝絨盒、一名員工把它交給陳念，接著是被裁切照片隱藏的二十二秒。下方鏡頭又被古墓主題布景擋住，主管就拿此說影片不可靠。陳念不願盲信，也不願隨意否定。她請獨立分析員保存記憶卡校驗碼與入庫紀錄。余知退後，讓鏡頭拍清楚做決定的人是她。更正發布了。八秒鐘內陳念以為最糟的事已過去，主管卻宣布公司找到另一個檔案，所有人又望向他。
