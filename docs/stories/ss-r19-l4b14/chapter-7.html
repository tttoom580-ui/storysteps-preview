A sponsor proposed a scripted apology in which Nian would accept blame for 'confusion' even though no theft had been established. Something had changed, but not everybody noticed it in the same order. Chen Nian listened to the account of the nearest worker, then to someone standing farther away. Their differences were not necessarily lies. Each person had been given a different view and a different reason to be afraid of admitting uncertainty. Yu Zhi had learned how quickly an audience could mistake silence for guilt. He chose to protect an original recording before saying anything that might become another headline. One witness used the word academic; Yu Zhi noted that a description was not a measurement. A handwritten label read accountant, but no one could say who had written it there.

贊助商提出安排陳念為「混亂」道歉，儘管根本沒有確定竊案。某件事改變了，但人們發現它的順序不同。陳念先聽近處工作人員的描述，再聽遠處旁觀者說法。差異不一定是謊言：每個人看到的角度不同，承認不確定時所要承擔的風險也不同。余知早知道觀眾多容易把沉默當成有罪。他選擇先保護原始錄影，再說可能變成標題的話。一位證人用了「學術的」一詞，余知指出形容並不等於測量。手寫標籤寫著「會計師」，卻沒有人知道是誰留下的。

The duplicate was missing from inventory, while the original remained in a locked safe. Chen Nian refused to pretend the previous decision had solved everything. The practical result was still there, useful but narrow. For each thing the team knew, there were two things it had not measured. Chen Nian drew a line through the claims that depended on speculation and asked Yu Zhi to keep the original record intact. The term admiration appeared in the revised file; Chen Nian checked its meaning against the original page. On the next form, the action field read annoy, but the witness had never approved the operation. The earlier photograph clearly showed a aquarium; the newer version did not, so Chen Nian kept both.

道具複製品不見了，真品其實仍鎖在保險箱。陳念不願假裝前一次決定已解決所有問題。成果確實存在，卻有明確界限。每個已知事項旁都還有未測量的部分。陳念劃掉靠猜測成立的說法，請余知保留原始紀錄。修訂檔案出現「欣賞」一詞，陳念拿原始頁面核對它的意思。下一張表單把動作寫成「使困擾」，見證人卻從未同意這項操作。較早的照片清楚顯示一項「水族館」，新版卻沒有，陳念因此保留兩張。

A small disagreement changed the schedule. Chen Nian wanted to proceed before evidence disappeared; Yu Zhi wanted another check because a wrong conclusion might hurt people who could not easily defend themselves. They did not settle the matter by choosing a leader. They wrote down what a short, reversible test could actually establish. The earlier photograph clearly showed a basin; the newer version did not, so Chen Nian kept both. The term characteristic appeared in the revised file; Chen Nian checked its meaning against the original page. Yu Zhi noticed the word chemistry in a note that someone had added after the first inspection.

一場小爭執改變了時程。陳念想趁證據消失前行動；余知要求再核對一次，因為錯誤結論可能傷害難以自辯的人。他們沒有靠選領袖解決爭議，而是列出短暫、可逆測試真正能證明什麼。較早的照片清楚顯示一項「水池」，新版卻沒有，陳念因此保留兩張。修訂檔案出現「特徵」一詞，陳念拿原始頁面核對它的意思。余知注意到初次檢查後有人在筆記裡加上「化學反應」一詞。

She refused a statement that would admit something she had not done, risking her short-term work contract. The plan had been amended three times, and each change left a trace. Chen Nian valued those corrections more than a perfect-looking first draft. If the outcome proved disappointing, the group would still be able to identify which assumption needed repair. Yu Zhi watched for the quiet participant who had not yet been invited to speak. Yu Zhi noticed the word contribution in a note that someone had added after the first inspection. A handwritten label read defeat, but no one could say who had written it there. The term expansion appeared in the revised file; Chen Nian checked its meaning against the original page.

陳念拒絕承認沒做過的事，即使可能失去短期工作。計畫改了三次，每次修改都留下痕跡。陳念認為這些修正比看似完美的初稿更有價值。就算結果不理想，團隊仍能知道該修正哪一項假設。余知也注意到那名一直沒有發言機會的人。余知注意到初次檢查後有人在筆記裡加上「捐贈」一詞。手寫標籤寫著「擊敗」，卻沒有人知道是誰留下的。修訂檔案出現「擴張」一詞，陳念拿原始頁面核對它的意思。

An official version reached the room before the practical work was finished. Chen Nian read it once, then compared it with what they had actually measured. The difference was not always malicious, but it could still hurt somebody. Yu Zhi asked that the correction be written in language the affected people could understand. Yu Zhi had learned how quickly an audience could mistake silence for guilt. He chose to protect an original recording before saying anything that might become another headline. The summary called the situation fluent; Chen Nian asked which observation justified that description. One witness used the word fortunate; Yu Zhi noted that a description was not a measurement. Yu Zhi repeated the instruction to motivate, then asked whether anyone had the right to refuse.

實際工作還沒完成，官方版本就傳到現場。陳念讀了一次，再比對真正測得的結果。差異未必都是惡意，卻仍可能傷害人。余知要求把更正寫成受影響者看得懂的語言。余知早知道觀眾多容易把沉默當成有罪。他選擇先保護原始錄影，再說可能變成標題的話。摘要以「流暢的」形容情況，陳念追問這種說法有何觀察根據。一位證人用了「幸運的」一詞，余知指出形容並不等於測量。余知重述「激勵；促使」的指示，再問當事人是否有權拒絕。

A witness asked whether the team was taking sides. Chen Nian answered that refusing to invent a fact was not the same as refusing to care. What mattered was whether the procedure protected someone whose story was less convenient to tell. Yu Zhi listened without interrupting and wrote down the question exactly as it had been asked. A handwritten label read prime, but no one could say who had written it there. The summary called the situation relevant; Chen Nian asked which observation justified that description. Yu Zhi noticed the word rhythm in a note that someone had added after the first inspection.

一名見證人問團隊是否偏袒。陳念回答，不捏造事實不代表冷漠。真正重要的是程序能否保護那個故事不方便被說出口的人。余知沒有打斷，逐字記下原問題。手寫標籤寫著「主開關」，卻沒有人知道是誰留下的。摘要以「相關的」形容情況，陳念追問這種說法有何觀察根據。余知注意到初次檢查後有人在筆記裡加上「節奏」一詞。

Even after the group agreed on the immediate step, another question remained. The answer would not arrive through a dramatic announcement. Someone would have to return to the place, compare the earlier notes, and accept the possibility that the next piece of evidence might spoil an attractive theory. Chen Nian prepared to do exactly that. The audience saw a star crossing a stage. Backstage workers saw dozens of precise tasks completed under deadlines. Chen Nian knew that a just account needed both views. Yu Zhi noticed the word shade in a note that someone had added after the first inspection. Yu Zhi repeated the instruction to strive, then asked whether anyone had the right to refuse. The instructions used the verb waken without naming who would carry the risk; Chen Nian circled that gap.

即使大家同意眼前步驟，另一個問題仍在。答案不會靠戲劇性公告降臨；有人得返回現場、比對舊筆記，並接受新證據可能推翻漂亮理論。陳念準備做的正是這件事。觀眾看見明星走過舞台，後台員工看見數十項趕在期限前完成的精細工作。陳念知道公正說法需要兩種視角。余知注意到初次檢查後有人在筆記裡加上「陰影」一詞。余知重述「努力」的指示，再問當事人是否有權拒絕。指示裡用了「喚醒」這個動詞，卻沒說誰承擔風險；陳念圈出這項缺漏。
