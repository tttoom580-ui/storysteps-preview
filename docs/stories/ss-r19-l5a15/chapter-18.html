The neighborhood maintenance committee needed volunteers to repair storm drains. Urian offered to work under the same schedule as everyone else. He carried heavy stones but was not placed in charge of the crew. A teenager who had once feared his horns taught him where the pipes connected. Urian listened carefully, and when he made a mistake the teenager corrected him in front of the others. Nobody bowed. That absence of ceremony pleased him more than praise. At lunch a resident asked whether he expected favorable immigration treatment for his labor. "No," he said. "The drain was blocked in the street where I live." Mira later added the work record to the file as evidence of residence, not moral redemption.
