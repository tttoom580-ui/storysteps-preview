Mira discovered that Urian's royal status had not expired when his kingdom ended. The migration database still classified him as a wartime threat, although the last battle had ended twenty years ago. He offered to surrender his crown, which he had been using as a flowerpot. Mira explained that an object could not erase a historical record. She requested the treaty and the order that had created his designation. Outside the office, Urian's apprentice An carried flowers to the neighbors who had complained about the shop. Two refused them; a third asked whether the strange roses were dangerous. An answered with the growing instructions, not a plea for sympathy. Mira watched and understood that the application concerned real people sharing a street, not a demon trying to win a prize.
