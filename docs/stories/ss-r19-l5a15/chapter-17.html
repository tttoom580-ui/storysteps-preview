Mira helped establish a public archive of wartime property claims and missing persons. Survivors could choose whether their names would appear online or remain sealed for investigators. Urian contributed records from his former palace under a documented transfer, without gaining control of the archive. Some files cleared people who had been falsely accused; others opened difficult investigations. Mira's father had left notes explaining how old address systems had separated families. Reading them, she understood why he had spent his final years drawing maps. One evening An brought a box of plain white flowers to the archive. He set them by the entrance without a sign. There were too many names for one bouquet to represent.
