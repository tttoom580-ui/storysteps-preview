An elderly woman brought a name to the flower shop: her sister, who had died when Urian's army crossed the northern pass. She did not ask for a bouquet. She asked that the name be remembered without being used as advertising. Urian wrote it on a small card and placed the card in a quiet corner of the shop after asking permission. He did not add his signature. An then showed him the growing flowers near the window, ones the woman had helped plant before the argument began. For a while they stood together without speaking. Later Mira visited and understood that historical responsibility would not have a neat final chapter. It could, however, have a place in the ordinary routines of a changing city.
