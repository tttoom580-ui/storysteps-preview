Spring reached the market slowly. Urian's shop reopened after repairs, with ordinary flowers on one side and carefully labeled magical plants on the other. An had become skilled enough to manage orders without his teacher's help. Mira joined a new office that reviewed residency cases across species. She kept the old rejection form framed behind her desk, not as a trophy but as a reminder that a computer's answer was not always the law. Urian received letters from people whose families had been harmed during the war; some asked questions, some wanted nothing from him. He answered those he was permitted to answer and respected silence. At closing time, a child asked whether demons could grow roses. Urian held out a seed. "Anyone can try," he said. "But you still have to water them."

春天慢慢回到花市。花店修好後重開，一邊賣普通花，一邊放著標示清楚的魔法植物。小安已能獨立接單。米拉加入新成立的跨物種居留審查窗口，在桌後掛著舊退件表，不是戰利品，而是提醒電腦答案未必等於法律。烏利安收到戰時受害者家屬的信，有些追問，有些不願再接觸。他在允許的範圍內回答，也尊重沉默。打烊時，孩子問惡魔會不會種玫瑰。烏利安遞出一粒種子：「任何人都能試，但還是得記得澆水。」
