A widow recalled him repairing the stone steps outside her home. A young shopkeeper said he had frightened her simply by opening his wings in a narrow alley. Mira recorded all three accounts without choosing the most flattering. The clerk rejected their statements because the official form asked for a human family relationship, not neighborhood knowledge. Mira asked the clerk to cite the law requiring such a relationship. He found none. The rejection was a software rule, not an act of parliament.
