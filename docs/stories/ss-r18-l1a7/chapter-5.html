一杯溫水

A Cup of Warm Water

零分魔法師的第一堂課 · 第 5 / 25 章 · 1000以下 · 英中對照 · 校訂中

A younger pupil cried because her practice cup had broken. The light through the window looked ordinary, but the news did not. Nolan stopped for a moment, feeling both curious and unsure. Iris did not laugh at the question, even when it sounded strange. They knew from earlier mistakes that moving too fast could make a simple problem harder. The magic academy offered enough space for a calm talk. On the first page of the protection journal, Nolan wrote what was known and left a blank space for what remained uncertain.

一名低年級學生因練習杯破了而哭泣。 窗外的光線和平常一樣，消息卻一點也不平常。諾蘭停了一會兒，既好奇又不確定。就算問題聽來奇怪，艾莉絲也沒有取笑。他們從先前的錯誤學到，行動太快反而可能讓小問題變複雜。魔法學院有足夠空間讓大家平靜討論。諾蘭在保護紀錄簿第一頁寫下已知的事，也留下空白給尚未確定的部分。

For a minute, the group spoke all at once. Nolan raised a hand and suggested taking turns. Iris repeated the question in simpler words, so that even a quiet student could follow the talk. Nobody needed to win an argument. They needed to learn which part of the problem could be handled now and which part needed more help. The uncertain shield remained difficult, but it no longer felt impossible. The word although gave the group a new example to discuss. Iris put bean on the board without turning it into a test. They found deal in a short reading exercise near the end of the page. Nolan drew a tiny sign beside dig so it would be easier to remember. Iris chose during as a word to explain kindly to a new learner.

有一分鐘，大家幾乎同時開口。諾蘭舉起手，提議輪流說話。艾莉絲用更簡單的詞重述問題，讓安靜的學生也能跟上。沒有人需要爭贏；重要的是分清哪些事現在能做、哪些事需要更多協助。不穩定的護盾仍然困難，卻不再讓人覺得無法處理。 「although」（雖然）這個字讓小組多了一個可以討論的例子。 艾莉絲把「bean」（豆子）寫在板上，卻沒有把它變成考試。 他們在頁面末尾的短文練習裡找到「deal」（處理；作決定）。 諾蘭在「dig」（挖）旁畫上小記號，幫助記憶。 艾莉絲選了「during」（在……期間），準備耐心向初學者說明。

Nolan fetched warm water and asked if she wanted company. Once everyone understood the choice, the next step was smaller than it had seemed. Nolan spoke first and waited for an answer. Iris watched the other person's face instead of trying to hurry the talk. There was room to say no, to ask for an explanation, or to think for another minute. The group used the protection journal as a guide, not as an order that could never be changed. A small study card showed frog, which Nolan copied beside today's notes. Iris asked how to use height in a sentence about their day. In the English practice book, hen appeared on the next line. Nolan read least aloud, then checked the meaning with Iris. Another card carried long and a simple drawing by a classmate.

諾蘭拿來溫水，問她要不要有人陪。 當所有人都了解選擇後，下一步其實沒有想像中困難。諾蘭先開口，然後等待回答。艾莉絲注意對方的表情，沒有催促。大家可以說不、要求解釋，或多想一分鐘。小組把保護紀錄簿當作指引，而不是絕不能改變的命令。 一張小小練習卡上寫著「frog」（青蛙），諾蘭把它抄在今日筆記旁。 艾莉絲問起，如何用「height」（高度）寫一句與今天有關的話。 英文練習本的下一行出現「hen」（母雞）。 諾蘭朗讀「least」（至少），再和艾莉絲確認意思。 另一張圖卡上有「long」（長的），旁邊是同學的簡筆畫。

The first idea sounded simple, and that was useful. Still, Nolan wanted to know whether it respected everyone's choice. Iris suggested changing only one part rather than throwing the whole plan away. They drew two small boxes on a page: what we can do and what we must ask about. The list made the uncertain shield easier to discuss without turning a friend into an enemy. The word minute gave the group a new example to discuss. Iris put Ms. on the board without turning it into a test. They found neck in a short reading exercise near the end of the page. Nolan drew a tiny sign beside point so it would be easier to remember. Iris chose put as a word to explain kindly to a new learner.

第一個想法聽起來很簡單，這是好事。但諾蘭還想確認是否尊重每個人的選擇。艾莉絲建議只修改其中一部分，不必推翻整個計畫。他們在紙上畫兩個方框：我們能做的事，以及必須先詢問的事。有了這份清單，討論不穩定的護盾便容易多了，也不會把朋友當成敵人。 「minute」（分鐘）這個字讓小組多了一個可以討論的例子。 艾莉絲把「Ms.」（女士）寫在板上，卻沒有把它變成考試。 他們在頁面末尾的短文練習裡找到「neck」（脖子）。 諾蘭在「point」（指向；重點）旁畫上小記號，幫助記憶。 艾莉絲選了「put」（放置），準備耐心向初學者說明。

No spell appeared, and he learned that helpfulness did not need magic. Before calling the task finished, Nolan went back over the facts. One detail had changed, and another still needed an answer. Iris wrote both down rather than hiding the uncertain part. They could be pleased with progress without pretending that every worry had disappeared. By comparing the new result with the first note, they could see what had actually improved. The learning safely still mattered, but now the group knew one more way to protect it. A small study card showed race, which Nolan copied beside today's notes. Iris asked how to use safe in a sentence about their day. In the English practice book, snow appeared on the next line. Nolan read sorry aloud, then checked the meaning with Iris. Another card carried win and a simple drawing by a classmate.

沒有魔法出現，他也明白，幫助別人不一定需要魔法。 在宣布完成前，諾蘭重新檢查事實。有一個細節改變了，另一個仍有待解答。艾莉絲把兩者都寫下，沒有隱藏不確定之處。大家可以為進展高興，卻不必假裝所有擔憂都消失。比對新結果與第一張筆記後，他們看出真正改善的部分。安全地學習依然重要，現在小組又多學會一種守護它的方法。 一張小小練習卡上寫著「race」（賽跑），諾蘭把它抄在今日筆記旁。 艾莉絲問起，如何用「safe」（安全的）寫一句與今天有關的話。 英文練習本的下一行出現「snow」（雪）。 諾蘭朗讀「sorry」（抱歉），再和艾莉絲確認意思。 另一張圖卡上有「win」（贏得），旁邊是同學的簡筆畫。

Outside, the air was cooler than it had been at lunch. Nolan looked back once and saw the magic academy in the evening light. It seemed like an ordinary place, but there was more to it now: a remembered question, a mistake corrected, and a choice made together. Iris waved from a few steps away. They still had their separate dreams and small worries, yet they also shared one clear memory. Whatever happened next, they could return to that memory without needing to pretend the day had been perfect.

外面的空氣比午餐時涼了些。諾蘭回頭望了一眼晚光中的魔法學院。它看似平凡，現在卻多了值得記住的問題、改正的錯誤，以及共同做出的選擇。艾莉絲在幾步外揮手。他們仍有各自的夢想和小煩惱，也有一段共同的清晰回憶。無論接下來發生什麼，都不必假裝今天完美，就能想起它的價值。
