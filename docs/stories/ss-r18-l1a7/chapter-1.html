Nolan's magic exam ended with a large zero beside his name. The light through the window looked ordinary, but the news did not. Nolan stopped for a moment, feeling both curious and unsure. Iris did not laugh at the question, even when it sounded strange. They knew from earlier mistakes that moving too fast could make a simple problem harder. The magic academy offered enough space for a calm talk. On the first page of the protection journal, Nolan wrote what was known and left a blank space for what remained uncertain.

諾蘭的魔法考試結束了，名字旁寫著一個大大的零。 窗外的光線和平常一樣，消息卻一點也不平常。諾蘭停了一會兒，既好奇又不確定。就算問題聽來奇怪，艾莉絲也沒有取笑。他們從先前的錯誤學到，行動太快反而可能讓小問題變複雜。魔法學院有足夠空間讓大家平靜討論。諾蘭在保護紀錄簿第一頁寫下已知的事，也留下空白給尚未確定的部分。

The first idea sounded simple, and that was useful. Still, Nolan wanted to know whether it respected everyone's choice. Iris suggested changing only one part rather than throwing the whole plan away. They drew two small boxes on a page: what we can do and what we must ask about. The list made the uncertain shield easier to discuss without turning a friend into an enemy. Iris asked how to use a.m. in a sentence about their day. In the English practice book, bell appeared on the next line. Nolan read breakfast aloud, then checked the meaning with Iris. Iris drew a small picture of cake in the margin of the class story page. The word couch gave the group a new example to discuss.

第一個想法聽起來很簡單，這是好事。但諾蘭還想確認是否尊重每個人的選擇。艾莉絲建議只修改其中一部分，不必推翻整個計畫。他們在紙上畫兩個方框：我們能做的事，以及必須先詢問的事。有了這份清單，討論不穩定的護盾便容易多了，也不會把朋友當成敵人。 艾莉絲問起，如何用「a.m.」（上午（a.m.））寫一句與今天有關的話。 英文練習本的下一行出現「bell」（鐘聲；鈴鐺）。 諾蘭朗讀「breakfast」（早餐），再和艾莉絲確認意思。 艾莉絲在班級故事頁的空白處畫了「cake」（蛋糕）的圖。 「couch」（沙發）這個字讓小組多了一個可以討論的例子。

Iris asked him to stay for a lesson instead of leaving in shame. The plan became real only when someone took the first small action. Nolan checked the time, Iris checked the shared materials, and Professor Vale confirmed which steps needed adult approval. The group left a clear record so that a person arriving later could understand the decision. They also agreed on what to do if something went wrong. It was not exciting in the way stories often promised, but it let everyone move forward with confidence. Iris put cover on the board without turning it into a test. They found early in a short reading exercise near the end of the page. Nolan drew a tiny sign beside exercise so it would be easier to remember. Iris chose foreign as a word to explain kindly to a new learner. Iris drew a small picture of fruit in the margin of the class story page.

艾莉絲請他留下學習，不要因為羞愧離開。 有人做出第一個小小行動時，計畫才真正開始。諾蘭確認時間，艾莉絲檢查共用材料，維爾教授確認哪些步驟需要大人同意。小組留下清楚紀錄，讓稍後到的人也能理解決定。他們還約好若事情出錯該怎麼辦。這不像故事裡常見的刺激冒險，卻讓每個人都更有信心往前走。 艾莉絲把「cover」（外罩；覆蓋物）寫在板上，卻沒有把它變成考試。 他們在頁面末尾的短文練習裡找到「early」（提早）。 諾蘭在「exercise」（運動）旁畫上小記號，幫助記憶。 艾莉絲選了「foreign」（外文的），準備耐心向初學者說明。 艾莉絲在班級故事頁的空白處畫了「fruit」（水果）的圖。

For a minute, the group spoke all at once. Nolan raised a hand and suggested taking turns. Iris repeated the question in simpler words, so that even a quiet student could follow the talk. Nobody needed to win an argument. They needed to learn which part of the problem could be handled now and which part needed more help. The uncertain shield remained difficult, but it no longer felt impossible. Iris asked how to use he in a sentence about their day. In the English practice book, heavy appeared on the next line. Nolan read hobby aloud, then checked the meaning with Iris. Another card carried monkey and a simple drawing by a classmate. The word salad gave the group a new example to discuss.

有一分鐘，大家幾乎同時開口。諾蘭舉起手，提議輪流說話。艾莉絲用更簡單的詞重述問題，讓安靜的學生也能跟上。沒有人需要爭贏；重要的是分清哪些事現在能做、哪些事需要更多協助。不穩定的護盾仍然困難，卻不再讓人覺得無法處理。 艾莉絲問起，如何用「he」（他；他的）寫一句與今天有關的話。 英文練習本的下一行出現「heavy」（沉重的）。 諾蘭朗讀「hobby」（興趣），再和艾莉絲確認意思。 另一張圖卡上有「monkey」（猴子），旁邊是同學的簡筆畫。 「salad」（沙拉）這個字讓小組多了一個可以討論的例子。

A loose shelf almost fell, and a pale shield appeared near Iris. They gathered the papers and put the shared things back in their places. Nolan could feel that the room had changed even though the desks had not moved. Iris pointed to the page where the group had written its first worry. The words were still there, but they no longer told the whole story. Now there was also an action, a result, and a reason to look forward to the next meeting. Iris put shall on the board without turning it into a test. They found sun in a short reading exercise near the end of the page. Nolan drew a tiny sign beside teach so it would be easier to remember. Iris drew a small picture of tooth in the margin of the class story page. A small study card showed write, which Nolan copied beside today's notes.

一座鬆動書架差點倒下，艾莉絲旁邊忽然出現淡淡的護盾。 他們整理紙張，把共用物品放回原處。雖然桌子沒有移動，諾蘭卻覺得房間已經不同。艾莉絲指出小組寫下第一個擔憂的那一頁。那些字還在，卻已無法概括整件事。現在多了行動、結果，以及期待下次見面的理由。 艾莉絲把「shall」（要不要／應否）寫在板上，卻沒有把它變成考試。 他們在頁面末尾的短文練習裡找到「sun」（太陽）。 諾蘭在「teach」（教）旁畫上小記號，幫助記憶。 艾莉絲在班級故事頁的空白處畫了「tooth」（牙齒）的圖。 一張小小練習卡上寫著「write」（寫），諾蘭把它抄在今日筆記旁。

Outside, the air was cooler than it had been at lunch. Nolan looked back once and saw the magic academy in the evening light. It seemed like an ordinary place, but there was more to it now: a remembered question, a mistake corrected, and a choice made together. Iris waved from a few steps away. They still had their separate dreams and small worries, yet they also shared one clear memory. Whatever happened next, they could return to that memory without needing to pretend the day had been perfect.

外面的空氣比午餐時涼了些。諾蘭回頭望了一眼晚光中的魔法學院。它看似平凡，現在卻多了值得記住的問題、改正的錯誤，以及共同做出的選擇。艾莉絲在幾步外揮手。他們仍有各自的夢想和小煩惱，也有一段共同的清晰回憶。無論接下來發生什麼，都不必假裝今天完美，就能想起它的價值。
