A Friend with Shaking Hands

Iris grew nervous before a group presentation and could not hold her notes. At first, Nolan thought the problem could be solved before the next bell. A closer look showed that someone else's wishes mattered too. Iris listened without interrupting, while Professor Vale watched the group from nearby. They did not have to agree on everything immediately. There was time to separate a real observation from a story someone had only imagined. Nolan took out the protection journal and asked what should be checked first.

分組報告前，艾莉絲緊張得握不穩筆記。 起初，諾蘭以為下次鐘響前就能解決問題。但仔細看過後才發現，別人的願望也很重要。艾莉絲沒有打斷，靜靜傾聽，維爾教授則在附近看著大家。眾人不必立刻同意每件事，還有時間分清真實觀察與想像中的說法。諾蘭拿出保護紀錄簿，問大家應先確認什麼。

They began by asking who might be affected by the next step. Iris thought about the people who had not spoken yet, and Nolan thought about the rule they had already promised to follow. One person offered a quick answer, but the group asked for a reason. A good question was not an attack. It was a way to make the answer safer and kinder. Professor Vale reminded them that everyone could ask for a pause. In the English practice book, answer appeared on the next line. Nolan read bicycle aloud, then checked the meaning with Iris. Another card carried body and a simple drawing by a classmate. The word bow gave the group a new example to discuss. Iris put jeans on the board without turning it into a test.

大家先問，下一步會影響到誰。艾莉絲想到還沒發言的人，諾蘭則想到自己早已承諾遵守的規則。有人很快提出答案，其他人卻請他說明理由。好問題不是攻擊，而是讓答案更安全、更體貼的方法。維爾教授提醒大家，每個人都可以要求暫停。 英文練習本的下一行出現「answer」（答案；回答）。 諾蘭朗讀「bicycle」（自行車），再和艾莉絲確認意思。 另一張圖卡上有「body」（身體），旁邊是同學的簡筆畫。 「bow」（鞠躬）這個字讓小組多了一個可以討論的例子。 艾莉絲把「jeans」（牛仔褲）寫在板上，卻沒有把它變成考試。

Nolan offered to practice with her in an empty study room. Nolan chose the part of the work that could be done carefully. Iris handled a different task and promised to report back before making any large change. A short question from Professor Vale caught an error in their first version. Instead of hiding it, they made a neat correction on the page. That correction did not erase their effort. It showed that the group was learning how to be responsible for its own work. They found language in a short reading exercise near the end of the page. Nolan drew a tiny sign beside mine so it would be easier to remember. Iris chose of as a word to explain kindly to a new learner. A small study card showed own, which Nolan copied beside today's notes. Iris asked how to use pick in a sentence about their day.

諾蘭提議在空教室陪她練習。 諾蘭選擇自己能謹慎完成的工作，艾莉絲負責另一項任務，並答應在重大更改前先回報。維爾教授的一個簡短問題，讓大家發現第一版中的錯誤。他們沒有掩飾，而是在頁面上整齊修正。這項修正沒有抹去先前的努力，反而證明小組正在學習對自己的工作負責。 他們在頁面末尾的短文練習裡找到「language」（語言）。 諾蘭在「mine」（我的）旁畫上小記號，幫助記憶。 艾莉絲選了「of」（的、屬於），準備耐心向初學者說明。 一張小小練習卡上寫著「own」（自己的），諾蘭把它抄在今日筆記旁。 艾莉絲問起，如何用「pick」（挑選）寫一句與今天有關的話。

Nolan was afraid that saying the wrong thing would make the day worse. Iris answered that staying silent could also leave a misunderstanding in place. They practiced one honest sentence and checked that it did not blame anyone unfairly. This took longer than making a joke, but the result was clearer. Near the table, Professor Vale nodded and let the young people try their own words before offering advice. In the English practice book, planet appeared on the next line. Nolan read salt aloud, then checked the meaning with Iris. Another card carried say and a simple drawing by a classmate. The word second gave the group a new example to discuss. Iris put so on the board without turning it into a test.

諾蘭害怕說錯話會讓事情變糟。艾莉絲回答，保持沉默有時也會讓誤會繼續存在。他們練習說出一句誠實的話，再確認沒有不公平地責怪任何人。這比開玩笑花時間，結果卻更清楚。桌旁的維爾教授點頭，先讓年輕人用自己的話嘗試，再提供建議。 英文練習本的下一行出現「planet」（星球）。 諾蘭朗讀「salt」（鹽），再和艾莉絲確認意思。 另一張圖卡上有「say」（說），旁邊是同學的簡筆畫。 「second」（第二個）這個字讓小組多了一個可以討論的例子。 艾莉絲把「so」（所以）寫在板上，卻沒有把它變成考試。

He found he could steady a friend with words rather than magic. Professor Vale asked the group to explain its choice in everyday words. Nolan tried once, paused, and then gave a shorter answer. Iris added an important detail that had been left out. The explanation became clear enough for another person to check. That mattered more than making their decision sound grand. The learning safely could grow only if other people understood how it worked. They found some in a short reading exercise near the end of the page. Nolan drew a tiny sign beside soon so it would be easier to remember. Iris chose spring as a word to explain kindly to a new learner. A small study card showed wind, which Nolan copied beside today's notes. Iris asked how to use wish in a sentence about their day.

他發現，不用魔法也能用言語安定朋友。 維爾教授請小組用平常的話解釋選擇。諾蘭試了一次，停頓後改用更短的回答。艾莉絲補充了一個漏掉的重要細節。解釋變得足夠清楚，讓別人也能核對。這比把決定說得偉大更重要。只有其他人理解做法，安全地學習才有機會繼續成長。 他們在頁面末尾的短文練習裡找到「some」（一些）。 諾蘭在「soon」（很快）旁畫上小記號，幫助記憶。 艾莉絲選了「spring」（春天），準備耐心向初學者說明。 一張小小練習卡上寫著「wind」（風），諾蘭把它抄在今日筆記旁。 艾莉絲問起，如何用「wish」（希望）寫一句與今天有關的話。

The hallway grew quiet, and Nolan finally had time to think. A good friend had not supplied every answer. Instead, Iris had left space for another idea and had stayed when the first idea failed. That was harder to notice than a loud victory, but it was real. Nolan made a promise to remember the person as well as the task. The learning safely was important; so were the people who helped shape it. With that thought, the next chapter of their ordinary and extraordinary days felt possible.

走廊逐漸安靜，諾蘭終於有時間思考。好朋友不是替自己提供每個答案；艾莉絲反而留下思考空間，也在第一個想法失敗時陪在身旁。這比熱鬧的勝利難以察覺，卻是真實的。諾蘭答應自己，不只記得任務，也要記得參與的人。安全地學習重要，幫忙塑造它的人也同樣重要。帶著這個念頭，他們平凡又不平凡的日子可以繼續寫下去。
