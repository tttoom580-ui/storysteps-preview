Nolan showed the wrong exit sign to the safety teacher. Nobody had planned for this moment. The room was full of ordinary sounds, yet Nolan felt that something important had changed. Iris waited beside the door and let the first surprise settle. It was tempting to find an easy answer before anyone knew the whole story. Instead, they looked carefully at what was truly in front of them. Nolan opened the protection journal and made a small note about the day.

諾蘭把錯誤出口標示交給安全老師。 沒有人預料到這一刻。魔法學院充滿平常的聲音，諾蘭卻感覺有件重要的事改變了。艾莉絲站在門旁，讓最初的驚訝慢慢沉澱。大家很容易在弄清事情之前就急著下結論，但他們決定先看清眼前真正發生的事。諾蘭翻開保護紀錄簿，記下今天的小小發現。

The first idea sounded simple, and that was useful. Still, Nolan wanted to know whether it respected everyone's choice. Iris suggested changing only one part rather than throwing the whole plan away. They drew two small boxes on a page: what we can do and what we must ask about. The list made the uncertain shield easier to discuss without turning a friend into an enemy. A small study card showed apple, which Nolan copied beside today's notes. Iris asked how to use between in a sentence about their day. In the English practice book, blind appeared on the next line. Nolan read brother aloud, then checked the meaning with Iris. Another card carried center and a simple drawing by a classmate.

第一個想法聽起來很簡單，這是好事。但諾蘭還想確認是否尊重每個人的選擇。艾莉絲建議只修改其中一部分，不必推翻整個計畫。他們在紙上畫兩個方框：我們能做的事，以及必須先詢問的事。有了這份清單，討論不穩定的護盾便容易多了，也不會把朋友當成敵人。 一張小小練習卡上寫著「apple」（蘋果），諾蘭把它抄在今日筆記旁。 艾莉絲問起，如何用「between」（在……之間）寫一句與今天有關的話。 英文練習本的下一行出現「blind」（失明的；看不見的）。 諾蘭朗讀「brother」（弟弟），再和艾莉絲確認意思。 另一張圖卡上有「center」（中央），旁邊是同學的簡筆畫。

The teacher changed the sign and thanked the pupils for checking. The plan became real only when someone took the first small action. Nolan checked the time, Iris checked the shared materials, and Professor Vale confirmed which steps needed adult approval. The group left a clear record so that a person arriving later could understand the decision. They also agreed on what to do if something went wrong. It was not exciting in the way stories often promised, but it let everyone move forward with confidence. The word half gave the group a new example to discuss. Iris put hit on the board without turning it into a test. They found ice in a short reading exercise near the end of the page. Nolan drew a tiny sign beside kick so it would be easier to remember. Iris chose love as a word to explain kindly to a new learner.

老師更換標示，感謝孩子們細心檢查。 有人做出第一個小小行動時，計畫才真正開始。諾蘭確認時間，艾莉絲檢查共用材料，維爾教授確認哪些步驟需要大人同意。小組留下清楚紀錄，讓稍後到的人也能理解決定。他們還約好若事情出錯該怎麼辦。這不像故事裡常見的刺激冒險，卻讓每個人都更有信心往前走。 「half」（一半）這個字讓小組多了一個可以討論的例子。 艾莉絲把「hit」（擊中）寫在板上，卻沒有把它變成考試。 他們在頁面末尾的短文練習裡找到「ice」（冰）。 諾蘭在「kick」（踢）旁畫上小記號，幫助記憶。 艾莉絲選了「love」（喜愛），準備耐心向初學者說明。

For a minute, the group spoke all at once. Nolan raised a hand and suggested taking turns. Iris repeated the question in simpler words, so that even a quiet student could follow the talk. Nobody needed to win an argument. They needed to learn which part of the problem could be handled now and which part needed more help. The uncertain shield remained difficult, but it no longer felt impossible. A small study card showed net, which Nolan copied beside today's notes. Iris asked how to use online in a sentence about their day. Iris drew a small picture of pencil in the margin of the class story page. Nolan read right aloud, then checked the meaning with Iris. Another card carried simple and a simple drawing by a classmate.

有一分鐘，大家幾乎同時開口。諾蘭舉起手，提議輪流說話。艾莉絲用更簡單的詞重述問題，讓安靜的學生也能跟上。沒有人需要爭贏；重要的是分清哪些事現在能做、哪些事需要更多協助。不穩定的護盾仍然困難，卻不再讓人覺得無法處理。 一張小小練習卡上寫著「net」（細線網），諾蘭把它抄在今日筆記旁。 艾莉絲問起，如何用「online」（線上的）寫一句與今天有關的話。 艾莉絲在班級故事頁的空白處畫了「pencil」（鉛筆）的圖。 諾蘭朗讀「right」（正確的），再和艾莉絲確認意思。 另一張圖卡上有「simple」（簡單的），旁邊是同學的簡筆畫。

Felix saw that a zero-point student had helped without using a spell. They gathered the papers and put the shared things back in their places. Nolan could feel that the room had changed even though the desks had not moved. Iris pointed to the page where the group had written its first worry. The words were still there, but they no longer told the whole story. Now there was also an action, a result, and a reason to look forward to the next meeting. The word spend gave the group a new example to discuss. Iris put still on the board without turning it into a test. They found teacher in a short reading exercise near the end of the page. Nolan drew a tiny sign beside voice so it would be easier to remember. Iris chose way as a word to explain kindly to a new learner.

菲利克斯看見零分學生即使不施法也能幫忙。 他們整理紙張，把共用物品放回原處。雖然桌子沒有移動，諾蘭卻覺得房間已經不同。艾莉絲指出小組寫下第一個擔憂的那一頁。那些字還在，卻已無法概括整件事。現在多了行動、結果，以及期待下次見面的理由。 「spend」（花費（時間））這個字讓小組多了一個可以討論的例子。 艾莉絲把「still」（仍然）寫在板上，卻沒有把它變成考試。 他們在頁面末尾的短文練習裡找到「teacher」（老師）。 諾蘭在「voice」（聲音）旁畫上小記號，幫助記憶。 艾莉絲選了「way」（方法），準備耐心向初學者說明。

Near the end of the day, Nolan stayed beside Iris for one more minute. They remembered how strange the morning had felt. A problem that once seemed to fill the whole room had become several smaller questions. Each question could be handled in turn. They did not know what the following day would bring, and that was all right. What they had learned was not a magic answer but a habit: notice a change, ask with care, and let people choose for themselves. Nolan closed the protection journal and left with a lighter heart.

一天快結束時，諾蘭在艾莉絲身邊多待了一分鐘。他們想起早上的奇怪感受。原本像占滿整間房間的難題，現在分成幾個較小的問題，可以逐一處理。他們不知道明天會帶來什麼，但這沒有關係。今天學到的不是神奇答案，而是一種習慣：留意變化、仔細發問，也讓別人自己選擇。諾蘭闔上保護紀錄簿，帶著較輕鬆的心情離開。
