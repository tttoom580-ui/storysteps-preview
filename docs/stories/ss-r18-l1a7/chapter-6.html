The Library Steps

零分魔法師的第一堂課 · 第 6 / 25 章 · 1000以下 · 英中對照 · 校訂中

A cart rolled toward the library steps while a pupil was reading nearby. Nobody had planned for this moment. The room was full of ordinary sounds, yet Nolan felt that something important had changed. Iris waited beside the door and let the first surprise settle. It was tempting to find an easy answer before anyone knew the whole story. Instead, they looked carefully at what was truly in front of them. Nolan opened the protection journal and made a small note about the day.

一輛推車朝圖書館階梯滑去，旁邊有學生正在閱讀。 沒有人預料到這一刻。魔法學院充滿平常的聲音，諾蘭卻感覺有件重要的事改變了。艾莉絲站在門旁，讓最初的驚訝慢慢沉澱。大家很容易在弄清事情之前就急著下結論，但他們決定先看清眼前真正發生的事。諾蘭翻開保護紀錄簿，記下今天的小小發現。

For a minute, the group spoke all at once. Nolan raised a hand and suggested taking turns. Iris repeated the question in simpler words, so that even a quiet student could follow the talk. Nobody needed to win an argument. They needed to learn which part of the problem could be handled now and which part needed more help. The uncertain shield remained difficult, but it no longer felt impossible. Iris put arrive on the board without turning it into a test. They found before in a short reading exercise near the end of the page. Nolan drew a tiny sign beside boat so it would be easier to remember. Iris drew a small picture of box in the margin of the class story page. A small study card showed coffee, which Nolan copied beside today's notes.

有一分鐘，大家幾乎同時開口。諾蘭舉起手，提議輪流說話。艾莉絲用更簡單的詞重述問題，讓安靜的學生也能跟上。沒有人需要爭贏；重要的是分清哪些事現在能做、哪些事需要更多協助。不穩定的護盾仍然困難，卻不再讓人覺得無法處理。 艾莉絲把「arrive」（抵達）寫在板上，卻沒有把它變成考試。 他們在頁面末尾的短文練習裡找到「before」（在……之前）。 諾蘭在「boat」（船）旁畫上小記號，幫助記憶。 艾莉絲在班級故事頁的空白處畫了「box」（箱子）的圖。 一張小小練習卡上寫著「coffee」（咖啡），諾蘭把它抄在今日筆記旁。

The librarian stopped the cart before it reached anyone. Once everyone understood the choice, the next step was smaller than it had seemed. Nolan spoke first and waited for an answer. Iris watched the other person's face instead of trying to hurry the talk. There was room to say no, to ask for an explanation, or to think for another minute. The group used the protection journal as a guide, not as an order that could never be changed. Iris asked how to use drive in a sentence about their day. In the English practice book, except appeared on the next line. Nolan read finally aloud, then checked the meaning with Iris. Another card carried free and a simple drawing by a classmate. The word house gave the group a new example to discuss.

圖書館員在推車碰到人之前就把它攔下。 當所有人都了解選擇後，下一步其實沒有想像中困難。諾蘭先開口，然後等待回答。艾莉絲注意對方的表情，沒有催促。大家可以說不、要求解釋，或多想一分鐘。小組把保護紀錄簿當作指引，而不是絕不能改變的命令。 艾莉絲問起，如何用「drive」（開車）寫一句與今天有關的話。 英文練習本的下一行出現「except」（除了）。 諾蘭朗讀「finally」（終於），再和艾莉絲確認意思。 另一張圖卡上有「free」（免費的），旁邊是同學的簡筆畫。 「house」（房屋）這個字讓小組多了一個可以討論的例子。

The first idea sounded simple, and that was useful. Still, Nolan wanted to know whether it respected everyone's choice. Iris suggested changing only one part rather than throwing the whole plan away. They drew two small boxes on a page: what we can do and what we must ask about. The list made the uncertain shield easier to discuss without turning a friend into an enemy. Iris put lion on the board without turning it into a test. They found see in a short reading exercise near the end of the page. Nolan drew a tiny sign beside shape so it would be easier to remember. Iris chose sign as a word to explain kindly to a new learner. A small study card showed stupid, which Nolan copied beside today's notes.

第一個想法聽起來很簡單，這是好事。但諾蘭還想確認是否尊重每個人的選擇。艾莉絲建議只修改其中一部分，不必推翻整個計畫。他們在紙上畫兩個方框：我們能做的事，以及必須先詢問的事。有了這份清單，討論不穩定的護盾便容易多了，也不會把朋友當成敵人。 艾莉絲把「lion」（獅子）寫在板上，卻沒有把它變成考試。 他們在頁面末尾的短文練習裡找到「see」（看見）。 諾蘭在「shape」（形狀）旁畫上小記號，幫助記憶。 艾莉絲選了「sign」（標誌），準備耐心向初學者說明。 一張小小練習卡上寫著「stupid」（笨的），諾蘭把它抄在今日筆記旁。

Nolan felt his hands warm but did not claim credit for the librarian's work. Before calling the task finished, Nolan went back over the facts. One detail had changed, and another still needed an answer. Iris wrote both down rather than hiding the uncertain part. They could be pleased with progress without pretending that every worry had disappeared. By comparing the new result with the first note, they could see what had actually improved. The learning safely still mattered, but now the group knew one more way to protect it. Iris asked how to use terrible in a sentence about their day. In the English practice book, top appeared on the next line. Nolan read uncle aloud, then checked the meaning with Iris. Another card carried wave and a simple drawing by a classmate. The word weekend gave the group a new example to discuss.

諾蘭感覺雙手發熱，卻沒有搶走圖書館員的功勞。 在宣布完成前，諾蘭重新檢查事實。有一個細節改變了，另一個仍有待解答。艾莉絲把兩者都寫下，沒有隱藏不確定之處。大家可以為進展高興，卻不必假裝所有擔憂都消失。比對新結果與第一張筆記後，他們看出真正改善的部分。安全地學習依然重要，現在小組又多學會一種守護它的方法。 艾莉絲問起，如何用「terrible」（嚴重的；糟糕的）寫一句與今天有關的話。 英文練習本的下一行出現「top」（頂端）。 諾蘭朗讀「uncle」（舅舅），再和艾莉絲確認意思。 另一張圖卡上有「wave」（揮手），旁邊是同學的簡筆畫。 「weekend」（週末）這個字讓小組多了一個可以討論的例子。

Near the end of the day, Nolan stayed beside Iris for one more minute. They remembered how strange the morning had felt. A problem that once seemed to fill the whole room had become several smaller questions. Each question could be handled in turn. They did not know what the following day would bring, and that was all right. What they had learned was not a magic answer but a habit: notice a change, ask with care, and let people choose for themselves. Nolan closed the protection journal and left with a lighter heart.

一天快結束時，諾蘭在艾莉絲身邊多待了一分鐘。他們想起早上的奇怪感受。原本像占滿整間房間的難題，現在分成幾個較小的問題，可以逐一處理。他們不知道明天會帶來什麼，但這沒有關係。今天學到的不是神奇答案，而是一種習慣：留意變化、仔細發問，也讓別人自己選擇。諾蘭闔上保護紀錄簿，帶著較輕鬆的心情離開。
