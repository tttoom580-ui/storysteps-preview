A Small Blue Ring

零分魔法師的第一堂課 · 第 7 / 25 章 · 1000以下 · 英中對照 · 校訂中

A blue ring appeared around Nolan's wrist whenever he felt serious concern. The day began with a small question and soon became more interesting than anyone expected. Nolan could hear other students talking, but did not hurry to join their guesses. Iris pointed out a detail that had been easy to miss. They stood together in the magic academy, where the same familiar objects suddenly seemed to tell a different story. Nolan kept the protection journal close, not as a perfect answer, but as a place to begin.

每當諾蘭真心擔心別人，手腕就會浮現小藍環。 這一天從一個小疑問開始，後來卻比大家預想的更有趣。諾蘭聽到其他同學交談，沒有急著加入猜測。艾莉絲指出了一個容易忽略的細節。他們站在魔法學院裡，熟悉的東西忽然像在說另一段故事。諾蘭把保護紀錄簿帶在身邊，那不是完美答案，而是一個開始。

Nolan was afraid that saying the wrong thing would make the day worse. Iris answered that staying silent could also leave a misunderstanding in place. They practiced one honest sentence and checked that it did not blame anyone unfairly. This took longer than making a joke, but the result was clearer. Near the table, Professor Vale nodded and let the young people try their own words before offering advice. They found as in a short reading exercise near the end of the page. Nolan drew a tiny sign beside bedroom so it would be easier to remember. Iris chose believe as a word to explain kindly to a new learner. Iris drew a small picture of clock in the margin of the class story page. Iris asked how to use close in a sentence about their day.

諾蘭害怕說錯話會讓事情變糟。艾莉絲回答，保持沉默有時也會讓誤會繼續存在。他們練習說出一句誠實的話，再確認沒有不公平地責怪任何人。這比開玩笑花時間，結果卻更清楚。桌旁的維爾教授點頭，先讓年輕人用自己的話嘗試，再提供建議。 他們在頁面末尾的短文練習裡找到「as」（當……的時候）。 諾蘭在「bedroom」（臥室）旁畫上小記號，幫助記憶。 艾莉絲選了「believe」（相信），準備耐心向初學者說明。 艾莉絲在班級故事頁的空白處畫了「clock」（時鐘）的圖。 艾莉絲問起，如何用「close」（關閉）寫一句與今天有關的話。

Iris measured the light without asking anyone to act frightened. There was a quiet kind of courage in trying again. Nolan noticed that the last attempt had taught them something, even without a perfect result. Iris offered practical help and asked which part would be most useful. They worked together for a short time and then stopped to check whether they were still following the rule. Professor Vale praised the careful check more than the speed of the work. In the English practice book, day appeared on the next line. Nolan read dream aloud, then checked the meaning with Iris. Another card carried error and a simple drawing by a classmate. The word hear gave the group a new example to discuss. Iris put just on the board without turning it into a test.

艾莉絲量測光亮，沒有要求任何人假裝害怕。 願意再試一次，是一種安靜的勇氣。諾蘭發現，就算上次結果不完美，大家仍學到了一些事。艾莉絲提供實際協助，先問哪一部分最有用。他們一起工作一小段時間，再停下來確認是否仍遵守規則。維爾教授稱讚的不是速度，而是這份細心。 英文練習本的下一行出現「day」（一天）。 諾蘭朗讀「dream」（夢），再和艾莉絲確認意思。 另一張圖卡上有「error」（錯誤），旁邊是同學的簡筆畫。 「hear」（聽見）這個字讓小組多了一個可以討論的例子。 艾莉絲把「just」（只是、剛好）寫在板上，卻沒有把它變成考試。

They began by asking who might be affected by the next step. Iris thought about the people who had not spoken yet, and Nolan thought about the rule they had already promised to follow. One person offered a quick answer, but the group asked for a reason. A good question was not an attack. It was a way to make the answer safer and kinder. Professor Vale reminded them that everyone could ask for a pause. They found laugh in a short reading exercise near the end of the page. Nolan drew a tiny sign beside nature so it would be easier to remember. Iris chose noise as a word to explain kindly to a new learner. A small study card showed proud, which Nolan copied beside today's notes. Iris asked how to use rainbow in a sentence about their day.

大家先問，下一步會影響到誰。艾莉絲想到還沒發言的人，諾蘭則想到自己早已承諾遵守的規則。有人很快提出答案，其他人卻請他說明理由。好問題不是攻擊，而是讓答案更安全、更體貼的方法。維爾教授提醒大家，每個人都可以要求暫停。 他們在頁面末尾的短文練習裡找到「laugh」（笑）。 諾蘭在「nature」（自然）旁畫上小記號，幫助記憶。 艾莉絲選了「noise」（噪音），準備耐心向初學者說明。 一張小小練習卡上寫著「proud」（驕傲的），諾蘭把它抄在今日筆記旁。 艾莉絲問起，如何用「rainbow」（彩虹）寫一句與今天有關的話。

The ring faded when Nolan tried to make it glow for applause. The answer made someone smile, but Nolan still asked one final question: was everyone comfortable with what came next? Iris listened to the replies, including the quiet ones. They changed a small detail when one person needed something different. Nothing about the change made the story less meaningful. It helped the learning safely belong to more than one person, which was why the work had begun. In the English practice book, rich appeared on the next line. Nolan read sky aloud, then checked the meaning with Iris. Another card carried time and a simple drawing by a classmate. The word whether gave the group a new example to discuss. Iris put yard on the board without turning it into a test.

諾蘭想為了掌聲讓它發光時，藍環卻消失了。 答案讓某個人笑了，但諾蘭仍問最後一個問題：大家都能接受下一步嗎？艾莉絲聽取回答，包括輕聲說出的意見。有人有不同需求，他們就修改一個小細節。這種修改沒有讓故事失去意義，反而讓安全地學習不只屬於一個人；這也正是大家當初開始的原因。 英文練習本的下一行出現「rich」（富有的）。 諾蘭朗讀「sky」（天空），再和艾莉絲確認意思。 另一張圖卡上有「time」（時間），旁邊是同學的簡筆畫。 「whether」（是否）這個字讓小組多了一個可以討論的例子。 艾莉絲把「yard」（院子）寫在板上，卻沒有把它變成考試。

When the last bell sounded, Nolan saw that Iris was waiting by the familiar door. The two had not said everything perfectly. They had even disagreed about a detail, yet the disagreement had made their plan clearer. The moment did not need a large speech. A small smile and one honest thank-you were enough. At home, Nolan would remember the most surprising part of the day and write it on the next page. Tomorrow the uncertain shield might look different again. This time they would know how to begin.

最後一聲鈴響時，諾蘭看見艾莉絲在熟悉的門旁等待。兩人不是每句話都說得完美，甚至為某個細節意見不同，但那場分歧讓計畫更清楚。這一刻不需要長篇大論，一個微笑和真誠的道謝就夠了。回家後，諾蘭會把今天最驚訝的事寫在下一頁。明天不穩定的護盾也許又有不同面貌，但這次他們知道如何開始。
