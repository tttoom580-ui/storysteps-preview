水晶測試

The Crystal Test

零分魔法師的第一堂課 · 第 11 / 25 章 · 1000以下 · 英中對照 · 校訂中

A crystal could read the strength of a spell but could not read its purpose. The day began with a small question and soon became more interesting than anyone expected. Nolan could hear other students talking, but did not hurry to join their guesses. Iris pointed out a detail that had been easy to miss. They stood together in the magic academy, where the same familiar objects suddenly seemed to tell a different story. Nolan kept the protection journal close, not as a perfect answer, but as a place to begin.

一顆水晶能測量咒語強度，卻無法讀出施法的目的。 這一天從一個小疑問開始，後來卻比大家預想的更有趣。諾蘭聽到其他同學交談，沒有急著加入猜測。艾莉絲指出了一個容易忽略的細節。他們站在魔法學院裡，熟悉的東西忽然像在說另一段故事。諾蘭把保護紀錄簿帶在身邊，那不是完美答案，而是一個開始。

They began by asking who might be affected by the next step. Iris thought about the people who had not spoken yet, and Nolan thought about the rule they had already promised to follow. One person offered a quick answer, but the group asked for a reason. A good question was not an attack. It was a way to make the answer safer and kinder. Professor Vale reminded them that everyone could ask for a pause. Iris asked how to use be in a sentence about their day. In the English practice book, blue appeared on the next line. Nolan read call aloud, then checked the meaning with Iris. Iris drew a small picture of car in the margin of the class story page. The word cold gave the group a new example to discuss.

大家先問，下一步會影響到誰。艾莉絲想到還沒發言的人，諾蘭則想到自己早已承諾遵守的規則。有人很快提出答案，其他人卻請他說明理由。好問題不是攻擊，而是讓答案更安全、更體貼的方法。維爾教授提醒大家，每個人都可以要求暫停。 艾莉絲問起，如何用「be」（是、成為）寫一句與今天有關的話。 英文練習本的下一行出現「blue」（藍色的）。 諾蘭朗讀「call」（叫；打電話），再和艾莉絲確認意思。 艾莉絲在班級故事頁的空白處畫了「car」（汽車）的圖。 「cold」（寒冷的）這個字讓小組多了一個可以討論的例子。

Nolan asked Professor Vale why the school used only one kind of score. Nolan chose the part of the work that could be done carefully. Iris handled a different task and promised to report back before making any large change. A short question from Professor Vale caught an error in their first version. Instead of hiding it, they made a neat correction on the page. That correction did not erase their effort. It showed that the group was learning how to be responsible for its own work. Iris put cook on the board without turning it into a test. They found dictionary in a short reading exercise near the end of the page. Nolan drew a tiny sign beside fan so it would be easier to remember. Iris chose maybe as a word to explain kindly to a new learner. A small study card showed nurse, which Nolan copied beside today's notes.

諾蘭問教授，學校為何只用一種分數評價學生。 諾蘭選擇自己能謹慎完成的工作，艾莉絲負責另一項任務，並答應在重大更改前先回報。維爾教授的一個簡短問題，讓大家發現第一版中的錯誤。他們沒有掩飾，而是在頁面上整齊修正。這項修正沒有抹去先前的努力，反而證明小組正在學習對自己的工作負責。 艾莉絲把「cook」（烹煮）寫在板上，卻沒有把它變成考試。 他們在頁面末尾的短文練習裡找到「dictionary」（字典）。 諾蘭在「fan」（風扇）旁畫上小記號，幫助記憶。 艾莉絲選了「maybe」（也許），準備耐心向初學者說明。 一張小小練習卡上寫著「nurse」（護理師），諾蘭把它抄在今日筆記旁。

Nolan was afraid that saying the wrong thing would make the day worse. Iris answered that staying silent could also leave a misunderstanding in place. They practiced one honest sentence and checked that it did not blame anyone unfairly. This took longer than making a joke, but the result was clearer. Near the table, Professor Vale nodded and let the young people try their own words before offering advice. Iris asked how to use pants in a sentence about their day. In the English practice book, past appeared on the next line. Nolan read probably aloud, then checked the meaning with Iris. Another card carried secretary and a simple drawing by a classmate. The word sentence gave the group a new example to discuss.

諾蘭害怕說錯話會讓事情變糟。艾莉絲回答，保持沉默有時也會讓誤會繼續存在。他們練習說出一句誠實的話，再確認沒有不公平地責怪任何人。這比開玩笑花時間，結果卻更清楚。桌旁的維爾教授點頭，先讓年輕人用自己的話嘗試，再提供建議。 艾莉絲問起，如何用「pants」（長褲）寫一句與今天有關的話。 英文練習本的下一行出現「past」（過去的）。 諾蘭朗讀「probably」（可能），再和艾莉絲確認意思。 另一張圖卡上有「secretary」（秘書），旁邊是同學的簡筆畫。 「sentence」（句子）這個字讓小組多了一個可以討論的例子。

The professor promised to bring the question to the faculty meeting. Professor Vale asked the group to explain its choice in everyday words. Nolan tried once, paused, and then gave a shorter answer. Iris added an important detail that had been left out. The explanation became clear enough for another person to check. That mattered more than making their decision sound grand. The learning safely could grow only if other people understood how it worked. Iris put temple on the board without turning it into a test. They found thousand in a short reading exercise near the end of the page. Nolan drew a tiny sign beside tree so it would be easier to remember. Iris chose under as a word to explain kindly to a new learner. A small study card showed weather, which Nolan copied beside today's notes.

教授答應把問題帶到教師會議。 維爾教授請小組用平常的話解釋選擇。諾蘭試了一次，停頓後改用更短的回答。艾莉絲補充了一個漏掉的重要細節。解釋變得足夠清楚，讓別人也能核對。這比把決定說得偉大更重要。只有其他人理解做法，安全地學習才有機會繼續成長。 艾莉絲把「temple」（寺廟）寫在板上，卻沒有把它變成考試。 他們在頁面末尾的短文練習裡找到「thousand」（一千）。 諾蘭在「tree」（樹）旁畫上小記號，幫助記憶。 艾莉絲選了「under」（在……下方），準備耐心向初學者說明。 一張小小練習卡上寫著「weather」（天氣），諾蘭把它抄在今日筆記旁。

When the last bell sounded, Nolan saw that Iris was waiting by the familiar door. The two had not said everything perfectly. They had even disagreed about a detail, yet the disagreement had made their plan clearer. The moment did not need a large speech. A small smile and one honest thank-you were enough. At home, Nolan would remember the most surprising part of the day and write it on the next page. Tomorrow the uncertain shield might look different again. This time they would know how to begin.

最後一聲鈴響時，諾蘭看見艾莉絲在熟悉的門旁等待。兩人不是每句話都說得完美，甚至為某個細節意見不同，但那場分歧讓計畫更清楚。這一刻不需要長篇大論，一個微笑和真誠的道謝就夠了。回家後，諾蘭會把今天最驚訝的事寫在下一頁。明天不穩定的護盾也許又有不同面貌，但這次他們知道如何開始。
