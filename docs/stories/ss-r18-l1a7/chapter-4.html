An Old Rule Book

零分魔法師的第一堂課 · 第 4 / 25 章 · 1000以下 · 英中對照 · 校訂中

The class found an old book about three kinds of protective magic. At first, Nolan thought the problem could be solved before the next bell. A closer look showed that someone else's wishes mattered too. Iris listened without interrupting, while Professor Vale watched the group from nearby. They did not have to agree on everything immediately. There was time to separate a real observation from a story someone had only imagined. Nolan took out the protection journal and asked what should be checked first.

全班找到一本介紹三種保護魔法的舊書。 起初，諾蘭以為下次鐘響前就能解決問題。但仔細看過後才發現，別人的願望也很重要。艾莉絲沒有打斷，靜靜傾聽，維爾教授則在附近看著大家。眾人不必立刻同意每件事，還有時間分清真實觀察與想像中的說法。諾蘭拿出保護紀錄簿，問大家應先確認什麼。

They began by asking who might be affected by the next step. Iris thought about the people who had not spoken yet, and Nolan thought about the rule they had already promised to follow. One person offered a quick answer, but the group asked for a reason. A good question was not an attack. It was a way to make the answer safer and kinder. Professor Vale reminded them that everyone could ask for a pause. Another card carried blow and a simple drawing by a classmate. The word boy gave the group a new example to discuss. Iris put butterfly on the board without turning it into a test. They found cheap in a short reading exercise near the end of the page. Nolan drew a tiny sign beside cloud so it would be easier to remember.

大家先問，下一步會影響到誰。艾莉絲想到還沒發言的人，諾蘭則想到自己早已承諾遵守的規則。有人很快提出答案，其他人卻請他說明理由。好問題不是攻擊，而是讓答案更安全、更體貼的方法。維爾教授提醒大家，每個人都可以要求暫停。 另一張圖卡上有「blow」（吹），旁邊是同學的簡筆畫。 「boy」（男孩）這個字讓小組多了一個可以討論的例子。 艾莉絲把「butterfly」（蝴蝶）寫在板上，卻沒有把它變成考試。 他們在頁面末尾的短文練習裡找到「cheap」（便宜的）。 諾蘭在「cloud」（雲）旁畫上小記號，幫助記憶。

Professor Vale explained that protection could not be forced by a threat made on purpose. Nolan chose the part of the work that could be done carefully. Iris handled a different task and promised to report back before making any large change. A short question from Professor Vale caught an error in their first version. Instead of hiding it, they made a neat correction on the page. That correction did not erase their effort. It showed that the group was learning how to be responsible for its own work. Iris chose drop as a word to explain kindly to a new learner. A small study card showed factory, which Nolan copied beside today's notes. Iris asked how to use healthy in a sentence about their day. In the English practice book, off appeared on the next line. Nolan read service aloud, then checked the meaning with Iris.

教授解釋，保護魔法不能靠故意製造的危險強迫發動。 諾蘭選擇自己能謹慎完成的工作，艾莉絲負責另一項任務，並答應在重大更改前先回報。維爾教授的一個簡短問題，讓大家發現第一版中的錯誤。他們沒有掩飾，而是在頁面上整齊修正。這項修正沒有抹去先前的努力，反而證明小組正在學習對自己的工作負責。 艾莉絲選了「drop」（掉落），準備耐心向初學者說明。 一張小小練習卡上寫著「factory」（工廠），諾蘭把它抄在今日筆記旁。 艾莉絲問起，如何用「healthy」（健康的）寫一句與今天有關的話。 英文練習本的下一行出現「off」（關掉；關閉）。 諾蘭朗讀「service」（服務（社團）），再和艾莉絲確認意思。

Nolan was afraid that saying the wrong thing would make the day worse. Iris answered that staying silent could also leave a misunderstanding in place. They practiced one honest sentence and checked that it did not blame anyone unfairly. This took longer than making a joke, but the result was clearer. Near the table, Professor Vale nodded and let the young people try their own words before offering advice. Another card carried shake and a simple drawing by a classmate. The word she gave the group a new example to discuss. Iris put sir on the board without turning it into a test. They found square in a short reading exercise near the end of the page. Nolan drew a tiny sign beside story so it would be easier to remember.

諾蘭害怕說錯話會讓事情變糟。艾莉絲回答，保持沉默有時也會讓誤會繼續存在。他們練習說出一句誠實的話，再確認沒有不公平地責怪任何人。這比開玩笑花時間，結果卻更清楚。桌旁的維爾教授點頭，先讓年輕人用自己的話嘗試，再提供建議。 另一張圖卡上有「shake」（搖晃；顫抖），旁邊是同學的簡筆畫。 「she」（她；她的）這個字讓小組多了一個可以討論的例子。 艾莉絲把「sir」（先生）寫在板上，卻沒有把它變成考試。 他們在頁面末尾的短文練習裡找到「square」（方形的）。 諾蘭在「story」（故事）旁畫上小記號，幫助記憶。

Nolan wrote the rule down before attempting another lesson. Professor Vale asked the group to explain its choice in everyday words. Nolan tried once, paused, and then gave a shorter answer. Iris added an important detail that had been left out. The explanation became clear enough for another person to check. That mattered more than making their decision sound grand. The learning safely could grow only if other people understood how it worked. Iris chose tall as a word to explain kindly to a new learner. A small study card showed tomato, which Nolan copied beside today's notes. Iris asked how to use town in a sentence about their day. In the English practice book, without appeared on the next line. Nolan read worker aloud, then checked the meaning with Iris.

諾蘭在下次練習前先把規則寫下來。 維爾教授請小組用平常的話解釋選擇。諾蘭試了一次，停頓後改用更短的回答。艾莉絲補充了一個漏掉的重要細節。解釋變得足夠清楚，讓別人也能核對。這比把決定說得偉大更重要。只有其他人理解做法，安全地學習才有機會繼續成長。 艾莉絲選了「tall」（高的），準備耐心向初學者說明。 一張小小練習卡上寫著「tomato」（番茄），諾蘭把它抄在今日筆記旁。 艾莉絲問起，如何用「town」（小鎮）寫一句與今天有關的話。 英文練習本的下一行出現「without」（沒有）。 諾蘭朗讀「worker」（工人），再和艾莉絲確認意思。

The hallway grew quiet, and Nolan finally had time to think. A good friend had not supplied every answer. Instead, Iris had left space for another idea and had stayed when the first idea failed. That was harder to notice than a loud victory, but it was real. Nolan made a promise to remember the person as well as the task. The learning safely was important; so were the people who helped shape it. With that thought, the next chapter of their ordinary and extraordinary days felt possible.

走廊逐漸安靜，諾蘭終於有時間思考。好朋友不是替自己提供每個答案；艾莉絲反而留下思考空間，也在第一個想法失敗時陪在身旁。這比熱鬧的勝利難以察覺，卻是真實的。諾蘭答應自己，不只記得任務，也要記得參與的人。安全地學習重要，幫忙塑造它的人也同樣重要。帶著這個念頭，他們平凡又不平凡的日子可以繼續寫下去。
