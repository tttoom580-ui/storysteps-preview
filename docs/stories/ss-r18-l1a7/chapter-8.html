The Rival's Question

Top student Felix said that a spell without a score was useless. At first, Nolan thought the problem could be solved before the next bell. A closer look showed that someone else's wishes mattered too. Iris listened without interrupting, while Professor Vale watched the group from nearby. They did not have to agree on everything immediately. There was time to separate a real observation from a story someone had only imagined. Nolan took out the protection journal and asked what should be checked first.

優等生菲利克斯說，沒有分數的咒語毫無用處。 起初，諾蘭以為下次鐘響前就能解決問題。但仔細看過後才發現，別人的願望也很重要。艾莉絲沒有打斷，靜靜傾聽，維爾教授則在附近看著大家。眾人不必立刻同意每件事，還有時間分清真實觀察與想像中的說法。諾蘭拿出保護紀錄簿，問大家應先確認什麼。

Nolan was afraid that saying the wrong thing would make the day worse. Iris answered that staying silent could also leave a misunderstanding in place. They practiced one honest sentence and checked that it did not blame anyone unfairly. This took longer than making a joke, but the result was clearer. Near the table, Professor Vale nodded and let the young people try their own words before offering advice. Nolan drew a tiny sign beside baseball so it would be easier to remember. Iris chose beef as a word to explain kindly to a new learner. A small study card showed behind, which Nolan copied beside today's notes. Iris asked how to use break in a sentence about their day. In the English practice book, different appeared on the next line.

諾蘭害怕說錯話會讓事情變糟。艾莉絲回答，保持沉默有時也會讓誤會繼續存在。他們練習說出一句誠實的話，再確認沒有不公平地責怪任何人。這比開玩笑花時間，結果卻更清楚。桌旁的維爾教授點頭，先讓年輕人用自己的話嘗試，再提供建議。 諾蘭在「baseball」（棒球）旁畫上小記號，幫助記憶。 艾莉絲選了「beef」（牛肉），準備耐心向初學者說明。 一張小小練習卡上寫著「behind」（在後面），諾蘭把它抄在今日筆記旁。 艾莉絲問起，如何用「break」（斷掉；破裂）寫一句與今天有關的話。 英文練習本的下一行出現「different」（死亡）。

Nolan asked Felix to compare what the exam measured with what safety required. There was a quiet kind of courage in trying again. Nolan noticed that the last attempt had taught them something, even without a perfect result. Iris offered practical help and asked which part would be most useful. They worked together for a short time and then stopped to check whether they were still following the rule. Professor Vale praised the careful check more than the speed of the work. Nolan read dress aloud, then checked the meaning with Iris. Another card carried easy and a simple drawing by a classmate. The word fly gave the group a new example to discuss. Iris put ghost on the board without turning it into a test. They found girl in a short reading exercise near the end of the page.

諾蘭請他比較考試測量的能力與安全真正需要的事。 願意再試一次，是一種安靜的勇氣。諾蘭發現，就算上次結果不完美，大家仍學到了一些事。艾莉絲提供實際協助，先問哪一部分最有用。他們一起工作一小段時間，再停下來確認是否仍遵守規則。維爾教授稱讚的不是速度，而是這份細心。 諾蘭朗讀「dress」（裙子；洋裝），再和艾莉絲確認意思。 另一張圖卡上有「easy」（容易的），旁邊是同學的簡筆畫。 「fly」（飛）這個字讓小組多了一個可以討論的例子。 艾莉絲把「ghost」（幽靈）寫在板上，卻沒有把它變成考試。 他們在頁面末尾的短文練習裡找到「girl」（女孩）。

They began by asking who might be affected by the next step. Iris thought about the people who had not spoken yet, and Nolan thought about the rule they had already promised to follow. One person offered a quick answer, but the group asked for a reason. A good question was not an attack. It was a way to make the answer safer and kinder. Professor Vale reminded them that everyone could ask for a pause. Nolan drew a tiny sign beside give so it would be easier to remember. Iris chose group as a word to explain kindly to a new learner. A small study card showed heart, which Nolan copied beside today's notes. Iris asked how to use nice in a sentence about their day. In the English practice book, officer appeared on the next line.

大家先問，下一步會影響到誰。艾莉絲想到還沒發言的人，諾蘭則想到自己早已承諾遵守的規則。有人很快提出答案，其他人卻請他說明理由。好問題不是攻擊，而是讓答案更安全、更體貼的方法。維爾教授提醒大家，每個人都可以要求暫停。 諾蘭在「give」（給予）旁畫上小記號，幫助記憶。 艾莉絲選了「group」（團體），準備耐心向初學者說明。 一張小小練習卡上寫著「heart」（心），諾蘭把它抄在今日筆記旁。 艾莉絲問起，如何用「nice」（好的）寫一句與今天有關的話。 英文練習本的下一行出現「officer」（管理員）。

Felix admitted he had never thought about the difference. The answer made someone smile, but Nolan still asked one final question: was everyone comfortable with what came next? Iris listened to the replies, including the quiet ones. They changed a small detail when one person needed something different. Nothing about the change made the story less meaningful. It helped the learning safely belong to more than one person, which was why the work had begun. Nolan read outside aloud, then checked the meaning with Iris. Another card carried park and a simple drawing by a classmate. The word taste gave the group a new example to discuss. Iris put walk on the board without turning it into a test. They found why in a short reading exercise near the end of the page.

菲利克斯承認自己從未想過兩者差別。 答案讓某個人笑了，但諾蘭仍問最後一個問題：大家都能接受下一步嗎？艾莉絲聽取回答，包括輕聲說出的意見。有人有不同需求，他們就修改一個小細節。這種修改沒有讓故事失去意義，反而讓安全地學習不只屬於一個人；這也正是大家當初開始的原因。 諾蘭朗讀「outside」（外面），再和艾莉絲確認意思。 另一張圖卡上有「park」（公園），旁邊是同學的簡筆畫。 「taste」（品嚐）這個字讓小組多了一個可以討論的例子。 艾莉絲把「walk」（步行）寫在板上，卻沒有把它變成考試。 他們在頁面末尾的短文練習裡找到「why」（為什麼）。

The hallway grew quiet, and Nolan finally had time to think. A good friend had not supplied every answer. Instead, Iris had left space for another idea and had stayed when the first idea failed. That was harder to notice than a loud victory, but it was real. Nolan made a promise to remember the person as well as the task. The learning safely was important; so were the people who helped shape it. With that thought, the next chapter of their ordinary and extraordinary days felt possible.

走廊逐漸安靜，諾蘭終於有時間思考。好朋友不是替自己提供每個答案；艾莉絲反而留下思考空間，也在第一個想法失敗時陪在身旁。這比熱鬧的勝利難以察覺，卻是真實的。諾蘭答應自己，不只記得任務，也要記得參與的人。安全地學習重要，幫忙塑造它的人也同樣重要。帶著這個念頭，他們平凡又不平凡的日子可以繼續寫下去。
