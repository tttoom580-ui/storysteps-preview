With parents and teachers present, Nora joined a school evening visit to the roof. Before anyone called the day strange, Nora had noticed how quiet the school kitchen could become when people were worried. The smallest sounds seemed clear, and the warm smell of fresh bread made the place feel both familiar and new. Rather than act as if every answer was obvious, Nora opened the moon notebook and began with what had really happened. In the corner of the moon notebook, Nora found the word “dress” beside an old drawing and stopped to ask what it meant. “heart,” Lune read from the practice page, then tried to connect the word to something they could actually see. A little note used “walk” as its key word; Nora kept the note because the rest of the line was still useful.

在家長和老師陪同下，諾拉參加學校屋頂夜間活動。在人們說這一天奇怪之前，諾拉就注意到，當大家擔心時，學校廚房會變得多麼安靜。細微的聲音變得清楚，新鮮麵包的溫暖香氣也讓這地方既熟悉又陌生。諾拉沒有裝作一切都很明白，而是打開月亮筆記本，從真正發生的事情開始。在月亮筆記本的角落，諾拉看見一幅舊畫旁寫著「dress」，於是停下來詢問意思。「heart。」露恩讀出練習頁上的字，接著試著把它和眼前真正看得到的東西連起來。一張小筆記以「walk」為關鍵字；諾拉留下它，因為剩下的句子仍然有用。

Nobody could say yet how the story would end. Nora found this uncomfortable, especially with the extra lunch tray still waiting for an answer. Lune suggested they take turns speaking, because repeating the same fear would not create a new solution. The moon looked pale behind the new building across the street. This was the point where a simple wish met the world as it really was. Nora wanted to help, but wanting did not make every road safe or every rule fair. Lune asked what the other people involved might lose if the wrong choice were made. The answer took longer than either had expected. They agreed not to build the rest of their plan on a fact they had never checked. The word “beef” appeared in an example someone had written earlier, and Lune asked if the example fit their situation. “Let us keep ‘girl’ in our word notes,” Nora said. “We can check its meaning again if we need it.” In the corner of the moon notebook, Nora found the word “park” beside an old drawing and stopped to ask what it meant.

月亮躲在街對面新大樓後方，看起來十分蒼白。這正是簡單願望遇上真實世界的時刻。諾拉想幫忙，但只靠想法不會讓所有路變安全，也不會讓所有規則公平。露恩問，如果選錯了，牽涉其中的人可能失去什麼。兩人花了比預期更長的時間才回答。他們同意，不把整個計畫建立在尚未核實的事實上。「beef」出現在前人留下的例句裡，露恩問這個例子是否適合現在的情況。「把『girl』留在詞彙筆記裡吧，」諾拉說，「有需要時再確認意思。」在月亮筆記本的角落，諾拉看見一幅舊畫旁寫著「park」，於是停下來詢問意思。

The group returned to the moon notebook and looked at the first line again. It now seemed to ask a different question. Nora asked Lune to explain the idea in plain words, as if telling it to someone who had just arrived. When the idea sounded confusing, they changed the plan rather than blaming the listener. Nora circled “baseball” on a reading sheet, not to win a test but to remember the meaning of the whole sentence. Lune's voice said that the old moon window could no longer see the sky. The answer brought relief, but it also carried a new responsibility. Nora did not look away from that part. Lune asked who should hear the news next, and whether it belonged to them to share it. They chose the careful answer over the exciting one, though excitement was still in the air. 露恩的聲音說，舊月窗已經看不見天空。答案帶來安心，也帶來新的責任。諾拉沒有迴避這一部分。露恩問下一個該讓誰知道消息，以及他們是否有權公開。他們選擇審慎的回答，而非最刺激的那個，雖然空氣中仍充滿興奮。一張小筆記以「fly」為關鍵字；諾拉留下它，因為剩下的句子仍然有用。諾拉在閱讀單上圈出「officer」，不是為了考高分，而是為了理解整句話。

The clock moved on. Nora felt tired, yet there was a good kind of energy left after honest work. Nobody had to say that the day was perfect. They only had to remember what had worked and what still needed care.
