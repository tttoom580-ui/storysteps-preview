Nora drew a map showing where the tall buildings blocked the roof's view of the moon. The school kitchen had its own familiar rhythm. Nora knew the sound of footsteps there, yet something about this moment called for a closer look. It was tempting to make a quick story out of a small surprise. Instead, Nora looked for one thing that could be checked and one person who deserved to be heard. There was room in the moon notebook for questions as well as answers, and today the questions were beginning to matter. A little note used “grandmother” as its key word; Nora kept the note because the rest of the line was still useful. Nora circled “ready” on a reading sheet, not to win a test but to remember the meaning of the whole sentence. The word “treat” appeared in an example someone had written earlier, and Lune asked if the example fit their situation.
