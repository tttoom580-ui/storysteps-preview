The head teacher let Nora watch the empty kitchen with a friend and an adult nearby. Before anyone called the day strange, Nora had noticed how quiet the school kitchen could become when people were worried. The smallest sounds seemed clear, and the warm smell of fresh bread made the place feel both familiar and new. Rather than act as if every answer was obvious, Nora opened the moon notebook and began with what had really happened. The word “drop” appeared in an example someone had written earlier, and Lune asked if the example fit their situation. “Let us keep ‘she’ in our word notes,” Nora said. “We can check its meaning again if we need it.” In the corner of the moon notebook, Nora found the word “without” beside an old drawing and stopped to ask what it meant.
