Nora tried not to rush, because a wrong guess could make the problem harder for someone nearby. The moon notebook was close at hand, ready for the facts that would be easy to forget once people began talking. A little note used “boat” as its key word; Nora kept the note because the rest of the line was still useful. Nora circled “free” on a reading sheet, not to win a test but to remember the meaning of the whole sentence. The word “stupid” appeared in an example someone had written earlier, and Lune asked if the example fit their situation.
