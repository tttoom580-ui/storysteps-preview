河邊的清單

The River List

鈴聲過後的每一封信 · 第 2 / 25 章 · 1000以下 · 英中對照

Ada's cousin Tom came to the post office with a map of the south side of town. His family had lived beside the river for a long time. “I can bring you some new street names,” he said. “I can also help carry the empty boxes, but I will not read anyone's mail.” Ada smiled. Tom was a helpful guy, even when he talked too much. The sun was bright and the day was sunny. They put the map on the desk and began to fill in the houses that had new numbers after last year's rain.

艾達的表弟湯姆帶著鎮南地圖來到郵局。他的家人在河邊住了很久。「我可以帶來一些新的街名，」他說，「也能幫忙搬空箱，但不會看任何人的信。」艾達笑了。湯姆是個熱心的小伙子，雖然有時話很多。陽光明亮，天氣晴朗。他們把地圖攤在桌上，開始填上去年大雨後改過門牌的房子。

Mr. Lee showed Ada a simple tool for measuring the walk from one corner to another. He said the power of good mail service came from clear names, not from walking fast. Tom had a video from a town science show. In it, children used water and sand to learn why the river changed its shape. “Can I show this to Mara?” he asked. “The video is public,” Ada said, “so yes.” She checked the map again. One old road had moved away from the river after a flood. It might be the road Mara wanted.

李先生拿出一個簡單工具，教艾達量出街角之間的路程。他說，好郵務服務的力量來自清楚的姓名，而不是走得快。湯姆帶來一段鎮上科學展的影片，孩子們用水和沙了解河流為何改變形狀。「我可以給瑪拉看嗎？」他問。「這是公開影片，可以。」艾達回答。她又看了一次地圖。有一條舊路曾因洪水而離開河邊，那可能就是瑪拉要找的路。

They walked to a farm with three sheep behind a low wall. A woman there knew the old bridge but not Mara's mother's name. “My parents moved here later,” she explained. Tom opened his mouth, then pressed his lip together. He wanted to guess, but Ada shook her head. “We can write down that this person does not know,” she said. “That is helpful information too.” The woman gave them the number of the town map office, not a private address. Ada thanked her and placed the paper in a clear pocket.

他們走到一座農場，低牆後有三隻羊。農場主人知道舊橋，卻不認識瑪拉的母親。「我的父母比較晚才搬來。」她解釋。湯姆張開嘴，又抿住嘴唇。他很想猜，艾達卻搖頭。「我們可以記下這個人不知道，」她說，「這也是有幫助的資訊。」農場主人給他們的是鎮公所地圖室的電話，而非私人地址。艾達道謝後，把紙放進透明袋。

Mara met them near the river after lunch. She said her mother had loved stories about a long bridge and a white house. Tom was ready to point at every white house in sight. Ada asked him to stop. “There are many white houses,” she said. “A color is not an address.” Mara laughed, then said she was glad they did not pretend to know. They went to the town library and read a public book of old street names. The book showed that the south road once had another name, Willow Road.

午餐後，瑪拉在河邊和他們會合。她說母親很愛講長橋與白房子的故事。湯姆差點指向眼前每一棟白房子，艾達請他停下。「白房子很多，」她說，「顏色不是地址。」瑪拉笑了，說很高興他們不會裝作知道。他們到圖書館查看公開的舊街名冊。書上顯示，南邊那條路以前叫柳樹路。

In the evening, Ada's family came for dinner. Her aunt asked why she was spending time with a stranger. Ada said Mara had not asked for special treatment. She was using public maps like everyone else, and she had waited while Ada did her real job. Her father said a small town needed both kindness and rules. Ada thought of that as she washed the dishes. She could offer Mara a chair, a map, and good company, but she could not promise to find a house that might be gone.

晚上，艾達的家人來吃飯。阿姨問她為什麼花時間陪陌生人。艾達說，瑪拉沒有要求特殊待遇，只像其他人一樣使用公開地圖，也在她工作時耐心等候。父親說，小鎮同時需要善意與規矩。艾達洗碗時一直想著這句話。她能給瑪拉椅子、地圖與陪伴，卻不能保證找到一間可能已不存在的房子。

Tom wanted to add a note about the famous old bridge at the bottom of the map. Ada said the bridge was an easy place to remember, but a clear street name was more important for a letter. “A visitor may know the bridge and still not know where a house is,” she said. Tom agreed and drew the bridge in the corner, away from the addresses. When they finished, the map was easier to use. He was pleased that his drawing could help, and Ada was pleased that they had checked the details together.

湯姆想在地圖底部加一條關於著名舊橋的註記。艾達說橋容易記住，但對信件來說清楚的街名更重要。「訪客可能知道橋，卻仍不知道房子在哪裡。」她說。湯姆同意，把橋畫在角落，離地址遠一點。完成後，地圖更容易使用。湯姆高興自己的畫能幫忙，艾達也高興兩人一起核對了細節。

The next morning was on Ada's mind as she went to bed. She would take the letters to the farm road first and check the new house numbers one by one. Mara could come to the library, where Tom would show her the old video. Nobody needed to hurry. The river had changed its path over many years; people needed time to find their way too. Ada put her work shoes by the door and left the public map on her desk. She was learning that a list could make a hard day easier for everyone.

睡前，艾達想著隔天的安排。她要先把信送往農場路，逐戶核對新門牌。瑪拉可以到圖書館，由湯姆播放那段舊影片。誰都不必著急。河流花了許多年改變路線，人找路也需要時間。艾達把工作鞋放在門邊，把公開地圖留在桌上。她漸漸明白，一張清單可以讓大家的辛苦少一些。
