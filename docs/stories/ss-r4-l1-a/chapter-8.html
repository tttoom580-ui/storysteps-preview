公園的一天

A Day at the Park

The town held a baseball game in the park. Ada did not need to work all day, but she had a short delivery to make. A girl in a red dress waited outside the gate for a birthday card. Behind her stood a group of children with different colored hats. “Can you give this to my grandmother?” one child asked. Ada explained that he could place it in the post box, where it would follow the right route. “That is easy,” he said. Ada smiled. The officer at the gate helped keep bicycles away from the walking path.

小鎮在公園舉行棒球賽。艾達今天不用工作一整天，但有一趟簡短的信要送。一名穿紅洋裝的女孩在門外等生日卡，身後是一群戴著不同顏色帽子的孩子。「妳能把這個交給我奶奶嗎？」一個孩子問。艾達解釋可以投入郵筒，讓信走正確的路線。「這很簡單。」孩子說。艾達微笑。門口的警員協助讓自行車離開行人道。

A ball flew over a low wall and landed near the mail bag. Ada stepped back, and a player ran to catch it. No letter was lost. A boy said the ball had moved like a ghost. Everyone laughed, but Ada still checked the bag. She found it closed and dry. Mara arrived with a small box of food. There was beef, bread, and fruit inside. “I made too much,” she said. “Would you like a taste?” Ada agreed after she finished her work. They sat under a tree and watched the game.

一顆球飛過矮牆，落在信袋旁。艾達退了一步，一名球員跑來撿球。沒有信掉出來。一個男孩說球飛得像鬼一樣。大家笑了，但艾達仍檢查信袋，確認拉好了、也保持乾燥。瑪拉帶來一小盒食物，裡面有牛肉、麵包和水果。「我做太多了，」她說，「要不要嚐嚐？」艾達完成工作後答應。她們坐在樹下看比賽。

Mara asked why Ada liked the post office. Ada thought for a while. “I like the walk, and I like knowing that a person is waiting,” she said. “Some days the work is not nice. The bag is heavy, and the weather can change. But I know why I do it.” Mara put one hand over her heart. She said that when she first came to town, she felt alone. Now she knew the name of a shop worker, a teacher, and a girl who played baseball. Those small meetings mattered to her.

瑪拉問艾達為什麼喜歡郵局。艾達想了一會兒。「我喜歡走路，也喜歡知道有人在等信，」她說，「有些日子工作不輕鬆，袋子重、天氣又變。但我知道自己為什麼做。」瑪拉把手放在心口，說剛到鎮上時很孤單，現在已認識一名店員、一位老師，還有一個打棒球的女孩。那些小小的相遇對她很重要。

At the other side of the park, an older man asked whether the local office could help him read a card. He could see the words, but they were too small. Ada said she could show him where to find a reading service at the library. She would not read a private letter aloud in front of a group without permission. “Why not?” a child asked. “Because it belongs to him,” Ada said. “A kind act must still be his choice.” The man thanked her, and the child nodded as if he had learned a new rule.

公園另一側，一名老人問郵局能不能幫他讀卡片。他看得見字，但字太小。艾達說可以告訴他圖書館的閱讀協助在哪裡。沒有得到許可，她不會在一群人面前大聲讀私人信。「為什麼？」一個孩子問。「因為信屬於他，」艾達說，「好意也必須讓他自己選。」老人道謝，孩子也點頭，彷彿學到了一條新規矩。

Later a player fell and needed a break. The game stopped for a few minutes while friends brought water. Nobody asked him to stand before he was ready. Mara noticed how the town cared for its people. “Maybe that is what my mother remembered,” she said. “Not only a house, but a way of living.” Ada liked the thought. It made the old envelope feel less like a puzzle and more like a story that could still be shared, even if the building in the picture had changed.

後來一名球員跌倒，需要休息。比賽暫停幾分鐘，朋友們拿來水。沒有人要他還沒準備好就站起來。瑪拉注意到鎮民照顧彼此的方式。「也許這就是媽媽記得的，」她說，「不只是一間房子，而是一種生活方式。」艾達喜歡這個想法。舊信封不再只是謎題，更像一個即使房子改變了仍能分享的故事。

A little kite began to fly above the park after the game. Its owner ran with it, then stopped when he saw the children near the path. He did not want the long string to catch anyone. Mara helped him move to the open field. Ada watched the kite climb higher. She thought it was like a letter in one small way: someone sent it out, but had to care about the path it took. A good day in the park was not made by winning alone. It came from the people giving one another room to play and rest.

比賽後，一只小風箏在公園上空飛起。主人拉著它跑，看到路旁有孩子便停下來，不希望長線勾到別人。瑪拉幫他移到空地。艾達看著風箏升高，覺得它有點像信：有人把它送出去，但仍要注意它經過的路。公園裡的好日子不是只靠贏球得來，也是人們互相留出遊戲與休息空間的結果。

When the park grew quiet, Ada walked home slowly. The game had been fun, but she remembered the people more than the score. She had made a delivery, protected a man's privacy, and shared lunch with Mara. The old post office was a small place in a larger town, yet its work touched many lives. Ada wanted the city office to see that. She decided to ask Mr. Lee whether the town could keep a clear record of the ways people used the service, not only the number of letters.

公園漸漸安靜時，艾達慢慢走回家。比賽很好玩，但她記住的更多是人，而不是比分。她送了信、保護了老人的隱私，也和瑪拉共進午餐。舊郵局在小鎮中只是一個小地方，工作卻連著許多人的生活。艾達希望城市辦公室也看見這一點。她決定問李先生，是否能記錄居民使用服務的方式，而不只統計信件數量。
