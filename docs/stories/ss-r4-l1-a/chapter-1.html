第一聲鈴響

The First Bell

鈴聲過後的每一封信 · 第 1 / 25 章 · 1000以下 · 英中對照

At six thirty a.m., the bell above the post office door made a soft sound. Ada was early for her first day as the town's new mail worker. The morning sun was warm, but the bag of letters was heavy. Mr. Lee, the old postman, was ready to teach her the roads. He put a cover over a box near the window. “That box is for letters that need a new address,” he said. Ada wrote this in a small blue book. On the couch beside them sat a young woman with a red envelope in her hand.

上午六點半，郵局門上的鈴鐺輕輕響了。艾達提早到班，今天是她擔任小鎮新郵務員的第一天。早晨的陽光很暖，但信袋很重。老郵差李先生準備教她認識路線。他替窗邊的一個箱子蓋上罩布。「這箱是給需要查新地址的信。」他說。艾達把這件事寫進藍色小本子。旁邊沙發上坐著一位年輕女子，手裡拿著紅色信封。

The woman said her name was Mara. She had come from a foreign country and wanted to find a house from an old letter. “My mother told me about this town,” she said, “but she did not leave a street number.” Ada saw that the envelope had a picture of a monkey with one big tooth. It looked funny, yet Mara did not laugh. “It was my mother's hobby to draw animals,” Mara said. Mr. Lee told them not to open a letter that belonged to another person. They could ask questions, but they had to keep private words private.

女子說自己叫瑪拉。她從國外來，想找一封舊信中提到的房子。「媽媽跟我說過這個鎮，」她說，「但沒有留下門牌號碼。」艾達看到信封上畫著一隻有一顆大牙齒的猴子。圖畫看來好笑，瑪拉卻沒有笑。「畫動物是媽媽的興趣。」瑪拉說。李先生提醒她們，不可以打開屬於別人的信。他們能提出問題，但私人文字必須保密。

For breakfast, Mr. Lee brought fruit, bread, and a little cake. Ada made a salad for lunch and put it in the back room. “I shall take the river road first,” she said. “Not alone,” said Mr. Lee. “Today we go together. There is a hill at the end, and carrying the bag is good exercise.” Mara asked if she could walk with them as far as the old school. Ada said yes, as long as Mara did not touch the mail. The three went out under the sun, and the bell rang once more behind them.

早餐時，李先生帶來水果、麵包和一小塊蛋糕。艾達準備了午餐沙拉，放進後面的房間。「我先走河邊那條路。」她說。「不能一個人，」李先生回答，「今天我們一起走。路尾有座小山，背信袋也很像運動。」瑪拉問能否跟他們走到舊學校。艾達同意，只要求瑪拉不要碰郵件。三個人走到陽光下，身後的鈴鐺又響了一次。

At the first house, a boy waited by the gate for a postcard. He asked Mara if she was a movie star. “No,” she said with a smile, “I am looking for someone who knew my mother.” The boy gave Ada a drawing for his grandfather. Ada checked the name before she took it. She learned that a letter was not only paper. It could hold a wish, a question, or a small part of a family day. Mr. Lee showed her where to stand so that no one had to walk into the road to meet the postman.

第一間屋子的門口，一個男孩正在等明信片。他問瑪拉是不是電影明星。「不是，」瑪拉微笑說，「我在找認識我媽媽的人。」男孩交給艾達一張要給祖父的畫。艾達先確認姓名才收下。她明白信不只是紙張，也可以裝著心願、問題，或一個家庭平凡日子的一小部分。李先生示範郵差該站在哪裡，才能讓住戶不必走到馬路上取信。

At noon they came back to the office. Ada's arms hurt, and she wanted to sit on the couch. Mara thanked them and said she would stay at the small inn. “Can we look for the old house tomorrow?” she asked. “We can look at public town maps,” Ada answered, “but we cannot use other people's letters to find it.” Mara nodded. Mr. Lee put a blank page on the table and asked Ada to make a list of what they knew. The list was short, and that was all right. A good beginning did not need a quick answer.

中午，他們回到郵局。艾達的手臂痠了，很想坐在沙發上。瑪拉向兩人道謝，說會住在小旅店。「明天可以找那間舊房子嗎？」她問。「我們能看公開的鎮上地圖，」艾達回答，「但不能利用別人的信查地址。」瑪拉點頭。李先生在桌上放了張白紙，請艾達列出已知的事情。清單很短，但沒有關係。好的開始不一定要立刻得到答案。

Mr. Lee gave Ada one more small job before they left. “Please write the names of the roads on this clean page,” he said. She did not copy the names from memory. She looked at the public map and checked every letter. Mara watched her and asked why a street name mattered so much. Ada answered, “One wrong word can take a letter to the wrong home.” Mara nodded. She looked again at the old words on her red envelope, then placed it carefully in her bag. They were both learning to pay attention to little things.

出門前，李先生又給艾達一件小工作。「請把道路名稱寫在這張乾淨紙上。」他說。艾達沒有憑記憶抄寫，而是看公開地圖，逐字核對。瑪拉看著她，問街名為何那麼重要。艾達回答：「一個錯字就可能把信送到錯的家。」瑪拉點頭，再看一次紅信封上的舊字，仔細收進袋子。她們都在學著注意小事情。

Before going home, Ada went over her notes. There was a name, an old picture, and a road to learn. The letters for today were all in the right hands. Mara still had her red envelope and the choice to keep it closed. Ada felt tired and happy at the same time. She did not know whether the old house was still standing. She did know that tomorrow she could learn one more road, carry one more letter, and listen to one more person. Outside, the bell moved gently in the evening wind.

下班前，艾達重看筆記。上面有一個姓名、一張舊圖畫，以及一條有待熟悉的路。今天的信都交到了正確的人手中。瑪拉仍握著紅信封，也保有不打開它的選擇。艾達疲倦卻快樂。她不知道舊房子是否仍在，但知道明天可以多認一條路、多送一封信、多聽一個人的故事。窗外，門鈴在晚風裡輕輕搖動。
