時鐘與雨

The Clock and the Rain

As the clock reached eight, rain began to tap on Ada's bedroom window. She had a dream about letters flying over the town like birds. She woke with a laugh, then heard a strange noise outside. The old yard gate was moving in the wind. It was not a person trying to get in. Ada closed it and went back inside. She believed a good morning began with one clear look at the facts, not a guess made in fear. Then she called Mr. Lee to ask whether the river road was still safe.

時鐘走到八點時，雨開始敲打艾達臥室的窗。她夢見信像鳥一樣飛過小鎮，醒來時笑了一聲，接著聽見屋外有奇怪的聲響。原來是院子的舊門在風裡擺動，不是有人想闖入。她關好門，回屋後心想，好的一天應該從仔細看事實開始，而不是害怕時亂猜。然後她打電話給李先生，問河邊道路是否安全。

At work she found an error on the route board. Someone had written the new road number beside the wrong street. “It was just a small mistake,” a helper said. Ada answered that a small mistake could send a letter far from home. She fixed it, marked the time, and asked another worker to check. Mara arrived with warm tea and news from the library. The old map was ready for them to see, but the library would close early because of the rain. “We can go another day,” Mara said. Ada was proud of her patience.

上班後，艾達發現路線板上有一個錯誤。有人把新門牌號碼寫在錯的街名旁。「只是小錯。」幫手說。艾達回答，小錯也可能讓信離家很遠。她修正、記下時間，再請另一位同事核對。瑪拉帶著熱茶和圖書館的消息來。老地圖已經能看了，但圖書館因大雨提早關閉。「改天再去也可以。」瑪拉說。艾達為她的耐心感到驕傲。

The rain stopped for a short time at noon. A rainbow came out across the gray sky, and children ran to see it. Ada could have hurried down the wet hill, but she chose the longer path. She met an older woman who had many letters to send to her rich brother in the city. The woman was worried about the cost. Ada showed her the simple prices and helped her choose. “You have given me time to think,” the woman said. Ada answered that no one should feel pushed to spend money before understanding a choice.

中午雨暫停，灰色天空出現一道彩虹，孩子們跑出去看。艾達本可以趕著走濕滑的山坡，卻選了較長的路。她遇見一位老婦人，想寄很多信給城市裡富有的哥哥，卻擔心費用。艾達說明簡單的價格，幫她選擇。「妳給了我思考的時間。」老婦人說。艾達回答，任何人都不該在還沒理解選項前，就被催著花錢。

In the afternoon Mr. Lee told Ada what he knew about the possible closing. The city office was counting how often people used the local mail service. A small office might have shorter hours next year. “But nothing is decided today,” he said. Ada asked whether they could tell the town the facts. Mr. Lee said yes, but only after checking the written notice. The notice was at the city office and would arrive by mail tomorrow. For now they could record real requests from local people and keep doing their work.

下午，李先生告訴艾達他知道的關閉傳聞。城市辦公室正在統計居民使用當地郵務服務的次數。小郵局明年可能縮短營業時間。「但今天還沒有任何決定。」他說。艾達問能否告訴鎮民事實。李先生說可以，但要先確認書面通知。通知在城市辦公室，明天會寄到。現在他們可以記錄居民真正提出的需求，並繼續做好工作。

That evening Mara stood by the window and watched the clouds. “My mother always said the sky here was wide,” she said. “I thought I would find her old home on the first day.” Ada put the red envelope on the table where Mara could take it back at once. “It is still yours,” she said. “You decide when to read it.” Mara answered that she might never open it in this town, and that was also fine. The two drank tea and listened to the quiet rain without trying to fill every moment with words.

傍晚，瑪拉站在窗邊看雲。「媽媽總說這裡的天空很寬，」她說，「我原本以為第一天就能找到她的老家。」艾達把紅信封放在桌上，讓瑪拉隨時可以拿回。「它仍然是妳的，」她說，「何時讀，由妳決定。」瑪拉說，也許她永遠不會在這個鎮打開它，那也沒關係。兩人喝著茶，聽安靜的雨，沒有努力用話語填滿每一刻。

At dinner, Ada said she could hear the rain even with the windows closed. Her father asked whether she still believed the town could keep its office. She said she did not know the final answer, but she said, “I believe we should do the work well today”. He pointed toward the trees in the yard. “Nature does not follow our plans,” he said. “The river rises and the roads change. People can learn to prepare.” Ada looked at the wet leaves and thought of the new route. She could not control rain, but she could check a sign before taking a step.

晚餐時，艾達說窗戶關著也聽得見雨。父親問她是否仍相信小鎮能保住郵局。她說不知道最終結果，但她說：「我相信今天應把工作做好。」。父親指向院子裡的樹。「大自然不照我們的計畫走，」他說，「河水會漲，道路會變，人卻可以學著準備。」艾達看著濕葉，想到新路線。她無法控制下雨，卻能在踏步前先看清告示。

Before bed, Ada looked at the new work list. She had fixed one error, given one woman a fair choice, and carried every letter on her route. She had not found Mara's old house, and the office still faced a question about its future. Yet the day had not been empty. She wrote a short note beside the clock: “Do what is right today. Ask about tomorrow when tomorrow comes.” Outside, the rain washed the yard clean, and the town grew quiet for the night.

睡前，艾達看看新的工作清單。她改正了一個錯誤，讓一位婦人公平地選擇寄送方式，也把路線上的信送完。她還沒找到瑪拉的老家，郵局的未來也仍有疑問。但這一天不是空白的。她在時鐘旁寫下一句提醒：「今天先做好對的事。明天的問題，明天再問。」窗外，雨把院子洗乾淨，小鎮漸漸安靜。
