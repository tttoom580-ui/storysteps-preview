A Letter in Two Languages

鈴聲過後的每一封信 · 第 12 / 25 章 · 1000以下 · 英中對照 · 校訂中

用互動版查詞、朗讀與收藏

Ada rode her bicycle to the school because a teacher had asked a question about mail. A family at the school spoke a different language at home. Their child wanted to answer a letter but did not know every English word. The teacher said the child could use his own language too. Ada bowed to the family when she met them and asked what help they wanted. “We want to write the address right,” the mother said. Ada showed them how to place the name, the number, and the street on the paper, one line at a time.

艾達騎著自行車到學校，因為一位老師有郵件問題。學校裡有個家庭在家使用另一種語言。他們的孩子想回信，卻不懂每個英文單字。老師說，孩子也能使用自己的語言。艾達見到一家人時微微鞠躬，問他們需要什麼幫忙。「我們想把地址寫對。」母親說。艾達一行一行示範，把姓名、號碼和街名寫在紙上。

A girl in jeans held a book about the planet and a pencil that was almost too short to use. “This book is mine,” she said, “but the letter is not. It belongs to my friend.” Ada asked her to pick a clean piece of paper and begin a new reply. The girl wanted to say something kind about spring and the wind. Ada did not tell her which words to choose. The teacher helped her spell one difficult word. By the second try, the girl could write the sentence on her own.

一個穿牛仔褲的女孩拿著一本關於地球的書，還有一支快短到不能用的鉛筆。「書是我的，」她說，「但信不是。信是朋友的。」艾達請她選一張乾淨紙，開始寫回信。女孩想寫些關於春天與風的溫暖話語。艾達沒有替她決定用詞。老師協助拼出一個難字。第二次嘗試時，女孩已能自己寫出句子。

At the school kitchen, someone asked Ada to carry a small box of salt and fruit to a party table. She had time, so she helped after putting her mail bag in a safe place. Mara came to see the lesson. She said her mother had written letters in two languages when she lived abroad. “I used to wish she had written in only one,” Mara said. “Now I am glad she kept both. They show different parts of her life.” Ada listened without asking to read the letters. She was beginning to see how a language could feel like home.

學校廚房裡，有人請艾達幫忙把一小箱鹽和水果搬到聚會桌。她有時間，便先把信袋放在安全處，再去幫忙。瑪拉來看課程。她說母親住在國外時曾用兩種語言寫信。「以前我希望她只寫一種，」瑪拉說，「現在很高興她兩種都保留，代表人生不同部分。」艾達聽著，沒有要求看那些信。她開始理解語言也能讓人感到像回家。

Later, Ada asked the teacher if she could use some of the public lesson ideas at the post office. She wished to make simple cards that showed where to put an address. The teacher agreed, but said the school must check them before they were given to children. Ada said that was fair. She had already made a mistake in her first draft: she had left out a space for the second line of an address. She fixed it and asked Mara to look at the new card. Mara said the picture was clear even without many words.

後來，艾達問老師能否把一些公開教材用在郵局。她想製作簡單小卡，示範地址要寫在哪裡。老師同意，但說發給孩子前，學校需要先檢查。艾達認為合理。她第一版卡片已有一個錯誤：忘了留第二行地址的位置。她修正後請瑪拉看看新卡。瑪拉說，圖像很清楚，不需要太多字也看得懂。

The school day ended with a short reading. Each child could say a sentence, show a drawing, or listen quietly. Ada liked that there was more than one good way to join. She made notes about the questions children asked: where a letter goes, how long it takes, and what happens if the name is not clear. These were useful questions for adults too. She wanted a public answer sheet at the post office. It would save time and make the service easier for people who were new to town.

學校這天以短短的閱讀活動結束。孩子可以說一句話、展示圖畫，也能安靜聽。艾達喜歡參加方式不只一種。她記下孩子們提出的問題：信會去哪裡、要寄多久、名字不清楚會怎樣。這些對大人也有用。她想在郵局放一張公開的問答表，既能省時間，也讓剛搬來的人更容易使用服務。

A boy made a small bow at the end of the lesson and thanked the teacher. Then he asked Ada why his whole body felt tired after writing only two lines. Ada smiled and said that learning a new thing could take a lot of care. She did not ask him to write more at once. “You can rest, then try again soon,” she said. The boy chose to draw a picture while his hand rested. When he finished, he put the paper in his school bag and said he would show his family at home. Ada was glad he could learn at his own pace.

課程結束時，一個男孩鞠躬謝謝老師，接著問艾達，為什麼只寫兩行字，全身就累了。艾達笑說，學新東西可能很需要專注。她沒有要他立刻多寫。「可以休息，很快再試。」她說。男孩讓手休息，改畫圖。完成後，他把紙放進書包，說回家要給家人看。艾達很高興他能按自己的步調學習。

On her way home, Ada stopped at a high place where she could see the river and the school. She thought about the words the children had written. Not every sentence was perfect, but each one was a real message from one person to another. She wanted the city office to understand that part of the work too. A town post office could be a place where a child learned to write to a friend, and where an older person learned a new way to ask for help. That work did not fit neatly into one number.

回家途中，艾達在高處停了一會兒，從那裡能看見河與學校。她想著孩子們寫下的文字。不是每句都完美，但每句都是一個人送給另一個人的真實訊息。她希望城市辦公室也理解這種工作。小鎮郵局可以是孩子學著寫信給朋友的地方，也是長者學習新求助方式的地方。這些工作無法整齊地放進單一數字裡。
