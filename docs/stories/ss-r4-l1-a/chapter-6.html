舊屋旁的小船

The Boat at the Old House

Before Ada could leave the post office, a small boat arrived at the river landing. It carried a box of letters for the houses across the water. The road to those houses was closed for work, so the boat driver had agreed to help. Ada checked the box before she took it. “I cannot drive a boat,” she said, “but I can see that the names are right.” The driver waved to her and showed her a sign on the landing. It said that the boat would not run in terrible weather. “Safety first, even on a busy day,” he said.

艾達還沒離開郵局，一艘小船就停靠河岸碼頭。船上有一箱要送到對岸住家的信。通往那些房子的路正在施工，所以船夫答應幫忙。艾達接箱前先檢查。「我不會開船，」她說，「但我能確認姓名正確。」船夫向她揮手，指著碼頭的告示。上面寫著天氣惡劣時船不開。「再忙也要安全第一。」他說。

A man at the landing said he was Mara's uncle, but Mara had never heard his name. Ada did not give him any information about Mara. She asked him to leave his own name and a way for Mara to answer if she wanted. “I am not calling you stupid,” Ada said when the man looked hurt. “I follow the same rule for everyone.” He nodded after a moment. He pointed to the top of a hill, where an old house stood behind a row of trees. “That is where my family lived,” he said. “It may be the house in her picture.”

碼頭有個男子說自己是瑪拉的叔叔，但瑪拉從沒聽過這個名字。艾達沒有告訴他瑪拉的任何消息，只請他留下姓名和聯絡方式，讓瑪拉自行決定是否回覆。「我不是說您笨，」男子受傷地看她時，艾達解釋，「我對每個人都遵守同一條規則。」他想了想，點頭。他指向山頂樹木後的一幢舊屋。「我家以前住那裡，」他說，「也許就是她畫上的房子。」

At the post office, Mara looked at the man's name. “I do not know him,” she said, “and I am not ready to meet him.” Mr. Lee said that was her choice. He made coffee, and the three sat at the back table. Ada drew the shape of the hill and marked the house with a question mark. “We have a place to check,” she said, “not a person to trust yet.” Mara looked thankful. She had come a long way, and the new questions felt heavy. Ada told her that they could stop looking for a day if she needed rest.

在郵局裡，瑪拉看了那名男子的名字。「我不認識他，」她說，「也還沒準備好見他。」李先生說，這是她的選擇。他泡了咖啡，三人坐在後面的桌旁。艾達畫出山丘形狀，在舊屋旁標上問號。「我們多了一個能查的地方，」她說，「還不是一個可以立刻信任的人。」瑪拉感激地望著她。她從遠方來，新的問題讓她感到沉重。艾達說，如果需要休息，可以暫停尋找一天。

The next delivery took Ada past a house with a stone lion by the gate. A woman there was glad to see her. “The old postman brought my husband's letters here for forty years,” she said. “I hope the new post office worker will stay.” Ada finally asked why so many people spoke about the office closing. The woman said someone from the city had asked how many letters came each day. Nobody knew what it meant, and that made them afraid. Ada did not make a promise she could not keep. She said she would ask her boss for the facts.

下一趟送信時，艾達經過一戶門旁立著石獅子的房子。屋主見到她很開心。「老郵差四十年來都把我丈夫的信送到這裡，」她說，「希望新郵務員也能留下來。」艾達終於問，為什麼這麼多人提到郵局可能關閉。女子說，城市來的人曾問每天收多少封信。沒人知道那代表什麼，因此大家害怕。艾達沒有作無法兌現的承諾，只說會向主管查明事實。

On the weekend, Ada went to the river without her mail bag. Mara came too. They watched the boat move slowly across the water. Mara said the search felt like a wave: one day she felt near her answer, and the next day she felt far away. Ada said they could take one step at a time. “We found a road name, and we found a house to look at from the public path,” she said. “That is enough for now.” They bought fruit from a stand and walked home before the sky grew dark.

週末，艾達沒背信袋，去了河邊。瑪拉也一起來。她們看著小船緩緩過河。瑪拉說，尋找的心情像浪一樣：有時覺得快找到答案，隔天又覺得很遠。艾達說可以一步一步走。「我們找到一個街名，也找到一棟能從公共步道查看的房子，」她說，「現在這樣就夠了。」她們在攤位買了水果，天黑前走回家。

At four o'clock the last boat did not arrive. Ada checked the notice and saw that the service had stopped because the water was high. “There is no free ride tonight,” the boat worker said. “Everyone must use the long road, except those who can safely wait until tomorrow.” Ada thanked him for telling people clearly. She did not ask him to make one more trip for her letters. She took the box back to the office and marked the change in the work book. A late letter was a problem, but a dangerous trip would be a worse one.

四點時，末班船沒有抵達。艾達看公告才知道水位高，服務已暫停。「今晚沒有免費接駁，」船務員說，「大家要走遠路，除了可以安全等到明天的人。」艾達謝謝他說明清楚。她沒有要求為信件多跑一趟，而是把箱子帶回郵局，在工作簿記下異動。信遲到是個問題，但危險的航程更糟。

At home, Ada wrote a note to herself about the new route. The boat might be useful for some deliveries, but it could not replace every road. Someone had to check the weather, the times, and the cost. She also wrote about the man at the landing, using only what he had said in public. She kept Mara's choice out of the work book. Ada was still new at this job, yet she knew that a good worker needed more than strong arms. She needed to listen, ask, and know when to say no.

回家後，艾達替新路線寫筆記。小船能幫忙送一些信，但無法取代每條道路。必須有人核對天氣、班次與費用。她也記下碼頭男子公開說過的話，卻沒有把瑪拉的私人選擇寫進公務簿。艾達雖然剛上任，已知道好員工需要的不只是有力氣的手臂。她還得會聽、會問，也要懂得何時說不。
