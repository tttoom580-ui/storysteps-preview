節慶明信片

The Festival Card

鈴聲過後的每一封信 · 第 9 / 25 章 · 1000以下 · 英中對照 · 校訂中

The autumn festival was coming, and Ada's bag was full of cards. On the way back from the lake, she met a grandmother who had made a dish of rice and banana for her family. “Please take this card to my sister,” she said. Ada checked the street and number. “Okay,” she answered. The woman asked Ada to stay and eat, but the delivery was not finished. Ada promised to visit after work if she still had time. She liked how a small card could bring joy to a home far away.

秋季慶典快到了，艾達的信袋裝滿明信片。從湖邊回來的路上，她遇見一位奶奶，正替家人做香蕉米飯。「請把這張卡送給我妹妹。」奶奶說。艾達核對街名門牌。「好。」她回答。奶奶請艾達留下吃飯，但送信還沒結束。艾達答應如果下班後有時間就來。她喜歡一張小卡片能給遠方的家帶去喜悅。

Mara was helping put signs along the festival street. She held one card in her hand and looked quite ready to speak in front of a crowd. “I want to tell the story of my mother's picture,” she said. “But I am not sure I want to show her letter.” Ada told her she could share only the part she wished to share. A story was hers to tell. Mara smiled and wrote a short line about the old garden on a clean card. She put the red envelope back into her bag.

瑪拉正在節慶街道旁掛標誌。她拿著一張卡片，看起來準備好在眾人面前說話。「我想講媽媽那張畫的故事，」她說，「但不確定要不要展示她的信。」艾達告訴她，只需要分享她願意分享的部分。故事由她自己決定怎麼說。瑪拉笑了，在乾淨卡片上寫下舊花園的短短一句話，又把紅信封放回包裡。

At the food stand, a man gave Ada a useful tip. He said an old street board was kept at the town hall, where it had hung many years ago. Ada asked who owned the board now. The man said the town did, and everyone could see it. “Are you sure?” Ada asked. He showed her the public notice with the hall's opening hours. She thanked him. It was better to check a fact than run to the wrong place. After lunch she and Mara planned to go there on the next open day.

食物攤旁，一位男子給艾達一個有用的提示。他說鎮公所保存著一塊舊街牌，多年前曾掛在路邊。艾達問現在是誰保管。男子說屬於鎮上，人人都能看。「您確定嗎？」艾達問。他拿出寫有公所開放時間的公告給她看。艾達道謝。查證比跑錯地方好。午餐後，她和瑪拉計畫下一個開放日去看看。

A child came running with tears in his eyes. Someone had shouted at him because he dropped a festival card. Ada helped him look. The card was lying beside a flower pot, not lost in the street. She picked it up and let him check the name. “You do not have to shout at yourself too,” she said. “A mistake can be fixed.” The child took a deep breath. He put the card in a dry pocket and promised to walk, not run, near the crowded stand.

一個孩子流著淚跑來，因為他弄掉節慶卡片，有人大聲責備他。艾達幫他找。卡片躺在花盆旁，並沒有掉在街上。她撿起來，讓孩子核對姓名。「你不用再對自己大吼，」她說，「犯錯可以補救。」孩子深吸一口氣，把卡片放進乾燥口袋，答應在擁擠的攤位附近慢慢走，不再跑。

When the music began, Mara stood with the other people and read her short note. She did not mention the name on the red envelope. Instead, she spoke about a garden, a road that had moved, and the people who kept helping a stranger. Ada stood at the back, proud of her friend. They had not found the old house, but Mara was making a new memory in the town. After the reading, she said she wanted to write another card to her mother, even though her mother could no longer answer.

音樂響起時，瑪拉站在人群前讀出短文。她沒有說出紅信封上的姓名，只談花園、移動過的道路，以及一直幫助陌生人的居民。艾達站在後面，為朋友感到驕傲。她們雖然還沒找到老房子，瑪拉卻已在鎮上創造新的回憶。讀完後，她說想再寫一張卡給媽媽，即使媽媽已不能回信。

The grandmother invited Mara to sit after the festival. She told her that a kind treat could be as simple as a warm cup of tea. Mara listened to stories from the woman's long life, but did not ask for names the woman wanted to keep private. The grandmother spoke about friends who had moved and letters that had come years later. “Sometimes a message arrives after we need it,” she said, “but it can still be good to read.” Mara thought of her mother and held the red envelope gently. She thanked the woman for the time they had shared.

慶典後，奶奶邀瑪拉坐下。她說，一份暖心的招待可以簡單到只是一杯熱茶。瑪拉聽她說漫長人生的故事，沒有追問她想保密的人名。奶奶談到搬走的朋友，以及多年後才收到的信。「有時訊息在最需要的時候過了才來，」她說，「但讀到仍可能是好事。」瑪拉想到母親，輕握紅信封，謝謝奶奶願意分享時間。

That evening Ada looked back at the festival. It had been busy, with many people and bright lights. Yet she remembered one quiet scene best: Mara folding her note and choosing what to keep private. The town could give her maps and stories, but not the right to take that choice away. Ada put the next day's mail into order before going home. She felt ready for the city office's letter, which might arrive soon. Whatever it said, she wanted to answer with real work and clear facts.

那晚，艾達回想慶典。現場忙碌、人多，燈也明亮。但她記得最清楚的是安靜的一幕：瑪拉摺起筆記，選擇什麼要留給自己。鎮民可以給她地圖與故事，卻不能奪走這個選擇。艾達回家前先整理隔天的信。她覺得自己準備好迎接城市辦公室那封可能快到的通知。無論上面寫什麼，她都想用實際工作和清楚事實回應。
