The Oxygen Vote

Mei arrived with the unpleasant certainty that the simplest explanation was probably the one that had been prepared for public consumption. The landing council discovers that stored oxygen will last only eleven weeks if the whole population moves at once. The place was full of people who needed a quick answer, yet the evidence still belonged to those whose lives it could change. Instead of making a dramatic accusation, Mei marked the hour, the witnesses, and the smallest observable detail in a paper notebook. The cleaning team used bleach only after investigators recorded the stains near the door. The union requested a comprehensive list of every visitor with access to the storage area. A credible witness described what she heard but refused to guess who issued the command. Mei praised the workers' dedication to preserving damaged equipment instead of concealing it.

廖梅抵達時，心裡很清楚：最簡單的解釋往往是事先準備好要給大眾聽的版本。登陸議會發現，若全體居民同時下船，儲存氧氣只夠十一週。現場的人都急需答案，但證據仍屬於那些生活可能被它改變的人。她沒有戲劇化地指控誰，而是先在紙本筆記上記下時刻、證人，以及最小卻可觀察的細節。 調查員記錄門旁污漬後，清潔隊才使用漂白水。 工會要求完整列出所有能進入儲藏區的訪客。 可信的證人描述聽到的話，拒絕猜測誰下命令。 廖梅讚賞工人保存損壞設備而非藏匿的投入。

There was a moment of quiet when Theo set the new material on the table. Private contractors demand that paying passengers receive habitats first, contrary to the fleet's charter. What mattered was not how surprising the discovery sounded, but whether anyone could repeat the test. Mei copied the relevant names carefully. If one person later changed the account, the original would still be available for comparison. The expedition notes preserved details of daily colonial life: The guest room held an old dresser and a thermometer with a cracked glass stem. The guard's familiarity with the corridor helped reconstruct the route taken that night. The office hierarchy did not give the chairman permission to disregard witness protection rules.

西奧把新資料放上桌時，四周安靜了一瞬。私人承包商要求付費乘客優先取得居住艙，違反艦隊章程。重要的不是發現有多驚人，而是別人能不能重做同一項測試。廖梅仔細抄下相關姓名；如果日後有人更改說法，原件仍可供比對。遠征筆記也記下殖民地日常生活的細節： 客房裡有一座舊五斗櫃，以及玻璃管裂開的溫度計。 警衛對走廊的熟悉協助重建當晚路徑。 辦公室階級制度不允許董事長無視證人保護規則。

“Will you trust me?” Theo asked. “With the evidence, yes,” Mei answered, “but not with a blank signature.” It was the sort of answer that could sound cold until someone noticed how carefully she had protected the other person’s right to disagree. Together they made a plan with a backup route, a deadline, and a record of who had accepted each responsibility. The landlady of the nearby boarding house remembered an unusual delivery before dawn. Beyond it, the marsh looked lush and peaceful, concealing collapsed drainage channels. He had spent years trying to nurture survivors who were taught to mourn in silence.

「妳信任我嗎？」西奧問。「證據上可以，空白授權書不行。」廖梅回答。這聽起來很冷淡，直到人們注意到她也小心保護對方拒絕的權利。他們一起訂下有備用路線、期限，以及明確責任分工的計畫。 附近寄宿屋的女房東記得黎明前一件異常送貨。 更遠處的沼澤看似草木茂盛而平靜，實際卻藏著崩毀的排水道。 他多年來努力照顧那些被要求默默哀悼的倖存者。

A representative of the colony corporation arrived with an offer to resolve the matter quietly. Quietly, in this case, meant without giving affected people access to the original. Mei declined. She knew an office could be useful and still be dangerously wrong. Her objection was narrow and specific: the proposed decision relied on a document whose origin had not yet been independently verified. The expedition notes preserved details of daily colonial life: He had spent years trying to nurture survivors who were taught to mourn in silence. Rain pressed against the windows, and the river threatened to overflow its banks. A cleaner had begun to overhear the order before the supervisor closed the door. The company's new report could refute a rumor only by presenting verifiable records.

殖民公司的代表前來，提議私下解決。所謂私下，就是不讓受影響的人看到原件。廖梅拒絕了。她知道一個機關可以有用，也可能犯下危險的錯。她的反對具體而有限：提議中的決定依賴一份來源還沒接受獨立驗證的文件。遠征筆記也記下殖民地日常生活的細節： 他多年來努力照顧那些被要求默默哀悼的倖存者。 雨水敲打窗戶，河水幾乎溢出河岸。 主管關門前，清潔員已無意間聽到那項命令。 公司新報告只能用可驗證紀錄反駁謠言。

Mei insists on public medical priorities and a transparent phased landing plan. A few witnesses thanked Mei; others accused her of doing too little. She accepted both reactions without offering a promise she could not keep. Theo wrote down the complaints, including the ones directed at their own conduct. They were not building a monument to themselves. They were building a process that would still function after they left. A seagull circled the river dock while technicians inspected the abandoned van. The site superintendent admitted that he had approved a broken gate's temporary use. A recorded symphony played in the lobby, strangely calm beside the anxious witnesses.

廖梅堅持公開醫療優先順序，採透明的分階段登陸計畫。幾名證人向廖梅道謝，另一些人指責她做得不夠。她接受兩種反應，不許下無法兌現的承諾。西奧寫下抱怨，包括針對他們自己行為的部分。他們不是為自己立紀念碑，而是建立自己離開後仍能運作的程序。 技師檢查廢置廂車時，一隻海鷗繞著河港盤旋。 現場總監承認自己曾批准暫時使用損壞閘門。 大廳播放錄製的交響樂，與焦慮證人形成奇特反差。

By nightfall the newest report was signed by observers who were free to disagree. Mei stood beside Theo and looked at the distance between them and a self-governing colony that does not erase its first inhabitants. Progress felt less like triumph than like leaving a reliable mark on a difficult road. Someone would challenge that mark tomorrow. This time, there would be a public record with which to answer. A thermometer showed that the overheated cabinet had not cooled to safe levels. Mei said transparent testimony mattered more than turning the hearing into public turmoil. Volunteers prepared wholesome meals for interviewees who had missed the last bus.

夜幕降臨前，新的報告由可以提出異議的觀察者簽署。廖梅站在西奧身旁，看著他們與不抹除首批居民的自治殖民地之間的距離。進展不像勝利，比較像在艱難路上留下可靠的記號。明天有人會質疑，而這一次將有公開紀錄可以回答。 溫度計顯示過熱櫃體尚未降至安全溫度。 見遙說透明證詞比把聽證變成公眾騷動更重要。 志工為錯過末班車的受訪者準備營養均衡的餐點。
