The Settlement Beyond the Ridge

Mei arrived with the unpleasant certainty that the simplest explanation was probably the one that had been prepared for public consumption. The hidden settlement's delegates arrive and explain how their ancestors survived the ordered evacuation. The place was full of people who needed a quick answer, yet the evidence still belonged to those whose lives it could change. Instead of making a dramatic accusation, Mei marked the hour, the witnesses, and the smallest observable detail in a paper notebook. The supplier sought a concession that would grant it control over the canal for a decade. A cynical investor said patients were useful only when their stories moved the market. The old procurement rule treated silence as consent by default, which the new board rejected. An emergency fund should not deplete the clinic budget merely to improve quarterly profit.

廖梅抵達時，心裡很清楚：最簡單的解釋往往是事先準備好要給大眾聽的版本。隱藏聚落的代表抵達，解釋祖先如何在強制撤離中存活。現場的人都急需答案，但證據仍屬於那些生活可能被它改變的人。她沒有戲劇化地指控誰，而是先在紙本筆記上記下時刻、證人，以及最小卻可觀察的細節。 供應商尋求特許權，想控制運河十年。 一位憤世嫉俗的投資人說，病人故事只有能推動市場時才有用。 舊採購規則預設沉默即同意，新董事會予以否決。 緊急基金不該只為改善季度利潤就耗盡診所預算。

There was a moment of quiet when Theo set the new material on the table. Their community refuses to surrender water rights simply because a new fleet has a colonization charter. What mattered was not how surprising the discovery sounded, but whether anyone could repeat the test. Mei copied the relevant names carefully. If one person later changed the account, the original would still be available for comparison. The expedition notes preserved details of daily colonial life: Soldiers handed out sweet buns, and the fragrance of crushed flowers covered the smell of oil along the river. Reporters were not allowed to intrude into confidential witness interviews. A former jockey now driving delivery vans recognized the unusual generator model.

西奧把新資料放上桌時，四周安靜了一瞬。他們拒絕僅因新艦隊持有殖民許可就交出水權。重要的不是發現有多驚人，而是別人能不能重做同一項測試。廖梅仔細抄下相關姓名；如果日後有人更改說法，原件仍可供比對。遠征筆記也記下殖民地日常生活的細節： 士兵發放甜麵包，壓碎花朵的香氣蓋住河邊油味。 記者不得闖入保密的證人訪談。 一名曾當賽馬騎師的廂車司機認出了不尋常的發電機型號。

“Will you trust me?” Theo asked. “With the evidence, yes,” Mei answered, “but not with a blank signature.” It was the sort of answer that could sound cold until someone noticed how carefully she had protected the other person’s right to disagree. Together they made a plan with a backup route, a deadline, and a record of who had accepted each responsibility. The lawyer insisted on a literal reading of the signed annex rather than the chairman's memories. Worker morale improved after the company published the failed-test results without punishment. Investigators could prosecute fraud only after assembling reliable evidence of intent.

「妳信任我嗎？」西奧問。「證據上可以，空白授權書不行。」廖梅回答。這聽起來很冷淡，直到人們注意到她也小心保護對方拒絕的權利。他們一起訂下有備用路線、期限，以及明確責任分工的計畫。 律師堅持按簽署附約的字面意思解讀，而非採信董事長的記憶。 公司公布失敗測試結果且未任意處罰後，工人士氣改善。 調查員只有蒐集可靠意圖證據後，才能起訴詐欺。

A representative of the colony corporation arrived with an offer to resolve the matter quietly. Quietly, in this case, meant without giving affected people access to the original. Mei declined. She knew an office could be useful and still be dangerously wrong. Her objection was narrow and specific: the proposed decision relied on a document whose origin had not yet been independently verified. The expedition notes preserved details of daily colonial life: The maintenance inspector checked for radioactive materials because an old sensor carried a misleading label. The chancellor's lawyer made a sharp retort about national morale, claiming that questioning the colony reactor would deplete public confidence. A young courier came forward with a screwdriver and a box of broken clock parts. Outside, an elderly vendor offered each departing witness a serving of warm soup.

殖民公司的代表前來，提議私下解決。所謂私下，就是不讓受影響的人看到原件。廖梅拒絕了。她知道一個機關可以有用，也可能犯下危險的錯。她的反對具體而有限：提議中的決定依賴一份來源還沒接受獨立驗證的文件。遠征筆記也記下殖民地日常生活的細節： 維修檢查員因舊感測器標示誤導而檢查有無放射性物質。 宰相律師尖銳反駁，談起國民士氣，聲稱質疑殖民反應爐會耗盡公眾信心。 一名年輕信差帶著螺絲起子和一箱壞時鐘零件上前。 庭外，一名年長攤販給離開的每位證人一份熱湯。

Mei recognizes their right to negotiate boundaries and begins a public inventory of shared resources. A few witnesses thanked Mei; others accused her of doing too little. She accepted both reactions without offering a promise she could not keep. Theo wrote down the complaints, including the ones directed at their own conduct. They were not building a monument to themselves. They were building a process that would still function after they left. The tower's splendor seemed irrelevant beside the dark apartments across the river. A spontaneous murmur passed through the audience. A witness explained that her stepfather had worked on the first version of the old substation.

廖梅承認他們有權協商邊界，開始公開盤點共享資源。幾名證人向廖梅道謝，另一些人指責她做得不夠。她接受兩種反應，不許下無法兌現的承諾。西奧寫下抱怨，包括針對他們自己行為的部分。他們不是為自己立紀念碑，而是建立自己離開後仍能運作的程序。 相較河對岸漆黑的公寓，大樓的華麗顯得毫無意義。 旁聽席傳出自發的低語。 一名證人解釋繼父曾參與舊變電站的初版工程。

By nightfall the newest report was signed by observers who were free to disagree. Mei stood beside Theo and looked at the distance between them and a self-governing colony that does not erase its first inhabitants. Progress felt less like triumph than like leaving a reliable mark on a difficult road. Someone would challenge that mark tomorrow. This time, there would be a public record with which to answer. The nervous clerk began to stutter before the judge invited her to speak slowly. The city agreed to subsidize emergency batteries for low-income patients during the trial. A synthetic seal material had been used in the forged authorization package.

夜幕降臨前，新的報告由可以提出異議的觀察者簽署。廖梅站在西奧身旁，看著他們與不抹除首批居民的自治殖民地之間的距離。進展不像勝利，比較像在艱難路上留下可靠的記號。明天有人會質疑，而這一次將有公開紀錄可以回答。 緊張的書記開始口吃，法官請她慢慢說。 市府同意在試辦期間補助低收入病人的緊急電池。 偽造授權封套使用了合成封條材料。
