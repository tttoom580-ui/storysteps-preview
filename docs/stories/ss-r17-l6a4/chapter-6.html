航誌裡的孩子

The Children in the Logbook

星際殖民者沒有收到回航許可 · 第 6 / 25 章 · 5000–6000 · 英中對照 · 校訂中

用互動版查詞、朗讀與收藏

It began not with a battle but with an ordinary interruption in the planet Nacre. The abandoned station's logbook lists more than two thousand births on Nacre before the Meridian was built. At first, no one considered the interruption important enough to preserve. Mei thought otherwise. A forgotten detail could become a map, and a map could be used to control where frightened people were permitted to go. “We allege deliberate treason,” he declared, though no test had occurred yet. The contractor had promised to beautify the riverside warehouse before its safety inspection. Its stone entrance was decorated with carved birds and a caption praising impartial justice. The contract dispute reached its climax when the two shipping weights appeared together.

事情不是從戰鬥開始，而是從珠母星裡一次平凡的中斷開始。廢棄站的航誌記載，在「子午線號」建造前，珠母星已有兩千多名新生兒。起初沒人認為這件事值得留下紀錄，但廖梅想法不同。遭忽略的細節可能變成地圖，而地圖也可能被用來控制驚恐的人可以去哪裡。 「我們指稱這是蓄意叛國，」他說，儘管測試根本還未進行。 承包商曾承諾在安檢前美化河岸倉庫。 石門飾有鳥類雕刻，下方的題字讚美公正司法。 當兩張出貨重量表一起出現時，契約爭議達到高潮。

Theo came back carrying a practical objection rather than an inspiring speech. Population totals suddenly drop to zero after an unexplained evacuation decree. They had both seen people harmed when an assumption hardened into policy. A signed report could be wrong, and an emotional memory could be right, but neither became proof merely by being repeated. They agreed to seek a measurement from an independent observer. The expedition notes preserved details of daily colonial life: Mei asked the judge to extract the precise accusation from the speech and place it in the record. An extracurricular engineering club helped sort community reports without handling confidential files. Their teacher was hospitable, offering water and a shady bench to witnesses who had traveled far.

西奧帶回的是實際的異議，而不是振奮人心的演說。人口總數在一道不明撤離令後突然歸零。他們都見過假設硬化成政策後如何傷人。有簽名的報告可能是錯的，強烈的記憶也可能是真的；兩者都不會因為反覆提起就變成證據。他們決定找獨立觀察員再測量一次。遠征筆記也記下殖民地日常生活的細節： 廖梅要求法官從演說中抽取具體指控，寫入紀錄。 一個課外工程社團協助分類社區通報，但不碰機密檔案。 他們的老師十分好客，為遠道而來的證人準備飲水與陰涼長椅。

“We can tell the truth without pretending we know everything,” Mei said. Theo replied that a partial truth was difficult to defend against people who controlled the doors. She answered that a perfect story with no verifiable source was more dangerous. They divided the work: one would protect the witnesses, while the other checked the physical records and the sequence of events. The retired mechanic stayed jolly even when the executives questioned the generator's strange name. Workers asked permission to mow the overgrown grass around the emergency exit. A supervisor with a white mustache signed the original warehouse inventory.

「我們能說實話，不需要假裝知道一切。」廖梅說。西奧回答，當對方掌控所有門口時，局部真相比較難捍衛。她則認為沒有可驗證來源的完美故事更危險。他們分配工作：一人保護證人，另一人核查實體紀錄和事件時序。 即使主管質疑發電機奇怪的名稱，退休技師仍很愉快。 工人請求修剪緊急出口旁長得過高的草。 一名留白鬍子的主管簽署了倉庫原始清冊。

The argument moved from a private corridor into a formal room, where every statement would be copied. A senior official from the colony corporation demanded certainty before granting access. Mei offered a less comfortable bargain: a limited preservation order now, followed by independent examination and a chance for every accused person to respond. That was enough to prevent an irreversible decision. The expedition notes preserved details of daily colonial life: A grandmother told him to stop nagging and gave him an ounce of sweet tea. The old cafeteria became an oasis for night workers exhausted by the investigation. One ounce of damaged insulation was sealed for laboratory comparison with the other samples. The missing battery modules might have been stored on the outskirts of the city.

爭論從私下走廊進入正式會議室，每句話都會留下副本。殖民公司的一名高級官員要求先確定真相才准查資料。廖梅提出比較不舒服的交換：先給有期限的保全命令，再獨立檢查，也讓所有被指控者有回應機會。這足以擋下一個不可逆的決定。遠征筆記也記下殖民地日常生活的細節： 一位祖母要他別再嘮叨，遞給他一小杯甜茶。 舊餐廳成為調查期間疲憊夜班工人的休息綠洲。 一盎司受損絕緣材料被封存，供實驗室與其他樣本比對。 失蹤的電池模組可能存放在城市郊區。

Theo asks that the missing colonists be counted as people, not as obsolete equipment in the report. Even those who agreed with the choice could see its cost. Time was running out, supplies were not unlimited, and a mistake might travel far beyond this room. Theo asked whether they should conceal the uncertainty until the crisis passed. Mei shook her head. People could not make free choices if the information reaching them had already been edited. A visiting parliament observer requested that the public hearing remain open to workers. The nobleman's methods were ruthless but often lawful on paper: he delayed funding, demanded impossible permits, and threatened public employees with dismissal. A local sculptor offered a warehouse sketch that showed the original side entrance.

西奧要求在報告中把失蹤殖民者視為人，而非淘汰設備。即使同意這個選擇的人，也看得出代價。時間不多、資源有限，一次錯誤可能傳得很遠。西奧問是否該先隱瞞不確定性，等危機過後再說。廖梅搖頭。若資訊早被剪裁，人們便無法作出自由的選擇。 一名國會觀察員要求公開聽證繼續向工人開放。 這名貴族的方法冷酷，但在紙面上常常合法：延後經費、索取不可能取得的許可，並以解雇威脅公務員。 一名在地雕塑家提供倉庫素描，顯示原有側門。

Later, under dim lights, Mei reread the original question. The dispute was larger now, but its center remained unchanged: who had the right to make an irreversible choice for someone else? Theo stayed until the last copy was sealed. Neither spoke about victory. They knew the next door would open only because today’s evidence had been preserved and today’s mistakes had been admitted. The repair team preferred simplicity in emergency instructions over impressive technical language. Two simultaneous transfers made it difficult to trace the original emergency payment. The company's shares entered a slump after the investigation became public.

晚些時候，在微弱燈光下，廖梅重讀最初的問題。爭端已經擴大，但核心沒變：誰有權替別人作出不可逆的選擇？西奧留到最後一份副本封存。他們沒有談勝利，只知道下一扇門之所以能打開，是因為今天保住了證據，也承認了錯誤。 維修團隊偏好簡單的緊急指示，而非炫目的技術詞。 兩筆同時進行的轉帳使原始緊急款項難以追蹤。 調查公開後，公司的股價陷入低迷。
