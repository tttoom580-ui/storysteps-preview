The morning had already acquired the look of an emergency before Mei reached the scene. The first lander reaches a valley filled with plants that appear to match the seed catalogue from Earth. An official wanted a clean story, with a beginning that could be announced and an ending that would require no questions. Mei had learned that reality rarely showed such courtesy. She made room for the people who had been ordered to wait outside. An aboriginal community upstream requested access to the substation's emergency warning system. Reports of earlier power failures abound in the neighborhood's handwritten complaint register. Mei asked the technicians to align the two timestamps before comparing the footage. The substation's altitude made it safe from river water but difficult for older residents to reach.

在廖梅抵達現場前，早晨已經有了緊急事件的模樣。第一艘登陸艇抵達山谷，植物外形與地球的種子目錄高度相似。官員想要一套簡潔的故事，開頭能宣布，結尾又不用回答問題。她知道現實很少如此客氣，於是請那些被命令等在門外的人進來說話。 上游原住民族社群要求能接收到變電站的緊急警報。 社區手寫申訴簿裡充滿過去停電的通報。 廖梅請技師先對齊兩組時間戳，再比對影像。 變電站的海拔讓它不易淹水，卻使年長居民難以前往。

Theo examined the account twice, once as a friend and once as a witness who might have to defend every word. Botanist Theo Sen discovers that several species were artificially modified before the colony fleet launched. The difference between what they suspected and what they could establish mattered. They placed the new fact beside the previous chapter’s unanswered question and looked for a second source that had not been copied from the first. The expedition notes preserved details of daily colonial life: The guide's annoyance was visible. “The capital still draws bridges where only wreckage remains,” she said, blunt as a hammer. A student described the dark pedestrian crossing where a cyclist had nearly struck him.

西奧把紀錄看了兩次：第一次以朋友身分，第二次則像一位必須為每個字負責的證人。植物學家西奧・森發現，幾種植物早在殖民艦出發前就經過人工改造。懷疑和能確立的事實必須分清楚。他們把新線索放在前一章未解的問題旁，尋找不曾從同一份資料抄出的第二來源。遠征筆記也記下殖民地日常生活的細節： 嚮導的惱怒溢於言表。 「京城還在畫橋，這裡卻只剩殘骸，」她直言不諱。 學生描述漆黑的行人穿越道，說自己差點被自行車撞到。

A crowd gathered around the disputed notice. “You have to choose a side,” someone shouted. Mei answered that she had chosen one already: the side of a claim that survived questions. Theo did not smile, but moved the most vulnerable bystanders away from the growing argument. The pair could disagree privately without turning their disagreement into a public threat. No technician was allowed to discard the damaged seal before independent photographs were taken. The inspector recorded an unexpected electrical discharge near the damaged control panel. Their joint endeavor would be assessed through measurements, not a flattering press release.

人群圍住有爭議的公告。「妳必須選邊站！」有人喊。廖梅回答自己早已選擇：站在經得起提問的主張那一邊。西奧沒有微笑，只把最脆弱的旁觀者帶離爭執。他們可以私下不同意對方，卻不能把分歧變成公開威脅。 完成獨立拍照前，技師不得丟棄受損封條。 檢查員記錄損壞控制板附近一次意外放電。 雙方共同努力將以測量結果評估，而非以討好的新聞稿。

The institution was not a single villain. It contained frightened clerks, dedicated workers, ambitious supervisors, and people who had learned to hide mistakes. the colony corporation wanted the incident classified as routine. Mei asked whose definition of routine was being used and whose losses were absent from the statistics. Nobody at the table could answer without consulting the sealed records. The expedition notes preserved details of daily colonial life: The first hurdle was getting both companies to accept a technician chosen by workers. “Yet they announced he was discharged for desertion.” The princess maintained a careful posture while examining the seal; her anger could not improve the evidence. The team needed precision because a three-minute error could distort the entire timeline. A rigorous inspection revealed a damaged timestamp seal but an undamaged outer casing.

這個機構不是單一惡棍。裡面有害怕的書記、盡責的工人、野心勃勃的主管，也有學會掩蓋錯誤的人。殖民公司想把事件歸類為例行事項。廖梅追問：是誰定義例行？又有誰的損失被統計漏掉？桌邊沒人能在不看封存紀錄的情況下回答。遠征筆記也記下殖民地日常生活的細節： 第一道障礙是讓兩家公司接受工人推選的技師。 」廖梅檢查封印時努力保持鎮定，因為怒氣不能改善證據。 團隊必須精確，因為三分鐘誤差可能扭曲整段時間線。 嚴格檢查發現時間戳封條損壞，外殼卻完好。

The crew seals the samples and postpones planting imported crops until testing is complete. The decision did not repair everything. It altered one procedure, protected one person, or saved one piece of evidence; on a day when almost everyone demanded a miracle, that was worth recording. Mei insisted that the result and its limits appear together in the official minutes. Anything less would turn a modest gain into tomorrow’s misleading legend. At a wind-battered terrace, the party found an elderly widow repairing her roof. A workers' uprising was not the issue; they were requesting the safety rights already promised. A volunteer usher led witnesses through the side entrance to protect their privacy.

船員封存樣本，等檢測完成才種植進口作物。這個決定沒有修好一切，只改變一道程序、保護一個人，或留下了一份證據；當幾乎所有人都要求奇蹟時，這仍值得記錄。廖梅堅持把成果和限制一起寫入正式紀錄。否則小小的進展又會變成明天誤導人的傳說。 在飽受狂風吹襲的梯田邊，隊伍找到一名修屋頂的年長寡婦。 這不是工人起義；他們只是要求已被承諾的安全權利。 志工引導員帶證人從側門進入，以保護隱私。

The lights beyond the window did not offer comfort. They showed how many people were still waiting. Mei understood that the investigation might eventually expose her own earlier errors, and she resolved not to erase them. Theo suggested a short rest before the next hearing. She agreed, because exhaustion could damage judgment as thoroughly as deliberate deception. The versatile backup unit could power either a clinic refrigerator or an emergency radio. The workers retained a safety veto over repairs that failed the written threshold. A widow asked the board to remember patients who depended on home oxygen machines.

窗外的燈光沒有帶來安慰，只照出還有多少人在等待。廖梅明白調查最終可能揭露自己過去的錯誤，決定不去抹除。西奧建議下一場聽證前短暫休息。她同意，因為疲憊和蓄意欺騙一樣可能損害判斷。 多功能備用裝置可供應診所冰箱或緊急無線電。 工人保有對不符合書面門檻之維修工作的安全否決權。 一名寡婦請董事會記住依賴家用氧氣機的病人。
