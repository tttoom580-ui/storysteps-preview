天空並不空

The Sky Is Not Empty

星際殖民者沒有收到回航許可 · 第 9 / 25 章 · 5000–6000 · 英中對照 · 校訂中

It began not with a battle but with an ordinary interruption in the planet Nacre. A distant transmitter sends a narrow signal using an obsolete Earth emergency code. At first, no one considered the interruption important enough to preserve. Mei thought otherwise. A forgotten detail could become a map, and a map could be used to control where frightened people were permitted to go. The public statement might alienate frightened investors, but hiding the altered seal would be worse. A black blot on the purchase order concealed the first digit of a serial number. Residents shared chili soup while waiting for the procurement hearing to begin. The envoy explained that continuity of village access was guaranteed in the oldest agreement.

事情不是從戰鬥開始，而是從珠母星裡一次平凡的中斷開始。遠方發射器用過時的地球緊急代碼送出窄頻訊號。起初沒人認為這件事值得留下紀錄，但廖梅想法不同。遭忽略的細節可能變成地圖，而地圖也可能被用來控制驚恐的人可以去哪裡。 公開聲明可能疏遠害怕的投資人，但隱瞞改動封條更糟。 採購單上的黑墨漬蓋住序號第一個數字。 居民等候採購聽證開始時，分享辣椒湯。 使者解釋，最早的協議保障村民持續使用這條路；後來的修正案逐漸刪除渡口相關文字，累積的省略使權利在沒有任何正式廢止令的情況下消失。

Theo came back carrying a practical objection rather than an inspiring speech. The code identifies the sender as an independent settlement, not a government station. They had both seen people harmed when an assumption hardened into policy. A signed report could be wrong, and an emotional memory could be right, but neither became proof merely by being repeated. They agreed to seek a measurement from an independent observer. The expedition notes preserved details of daily colonial life: The cumulative delays had increased costs far beyond the original repair estimate. They showed a decisive increase in heat extraction on the night the orphanage burned. No worker was dispensable merely because an executive feared an embarrassing question.

西奧帶回的是實際的異議，而不是振奮人心的演說。代碼顯示發信者是獨立聚落，不是政府站。他們都見過假設硬化成政策後如何傷人。有簽名的報告可能是錯的，強烈的記憶也可能是真的；兩者都不會因為反覆提起就變成證據。他們決定找獨立觀察員再測量一次。遠征筆記也記下殖民地日常生活的細節： 累積延誤讓成本遠超原始維修估算。 作者最後寫下一個簡短問題：「為什麼測試開始後，撤離卻被取消？ 不能因主管害怕尷尬提問，就把任何工人當成可有可無。

“We can tell the truth without pretending we know everything,” Mei said. Theo replied that a partial truth was difficult to defend against people who controlled the doors. She answered that a perfect story with no verifiable source was more dangerous. They divided the work: one would protect the witnesses, while the other checked the physical records and the sequence of events. A visitor wore jasmine perfume that matched the scent noted by the night guard. The river lodge provided temporary rooms for families displaced during repairs. A maple tree near the entrance helped the courier identify the delivery location.

「我們能說實話，不需要假裝知道一切。」廖梅說。西奧回答，當對方掌控所有門口時，局部真相比較難捍衛。她則認為沒有可驗證來源的完美故事更危險。他們分配工作：一人保護證人，另一人核查實體紀錄和事件時序。 某位訪客使用茉莉香水，與夜班警衛記下的氣味一致。 河邊旅舍為維修期間搬離的家庭提供臨時房間。 入口旁的楓樹協助快遞員辨認送貨地點。

The argument moved from a private corridor into a formal room, where every statement would be copied. A senior official from the colony corporation demanded certainty before granting access. Mei offered a less comfortable bargain: a limited preservation order now, followed by independent examination and a chance for every accused person to respond. That was enough to prevent an irreversible decision. The expedition notes preserved details of daily colonial life: The next witness was Mei, an envoy from the eastern federation whom the fleet officially called a rebel mistress of spies. In the next room, people debated how to mobilize villagers for a public meeting without provoking soldiers. Mei paused to muse over how often public trust had been treated as private property. The whistleblower's persistence finally persuaded investigators to examine the old account.

爭論從私下走廊進入正式會議室，每句話都會留下副本。殖民公司的一名高級官員要求先確定真相才准查資料。廖梅提出比較不舒服的交換：先給有期限的保全命令，再獨立檢查，也讓所有被指控者有回應機會。這足以擋下一個不可逆的決定。遠征筆記也記下殖民地日常生活的細節： 下一名證人是東方聯邦使者伊絲拉；殖民艦隊官方稱她是指揮間諜的叛軍女頭目。 隔壁房裡，人們討論如何動員村民出席公開集會，又不激怒士兵。 見遙停下來沉思，公共信任究竟有多少次被當成私人財產。 吹哨者的堅持終於讓調查員檢查舊帳戶。

Mei offers a conversation without demanding coordinates or allegiance in return. Even those who agreed with the choice could see its cost. Time was running out, supplies were not unlimited, and a mistake might travel far beyond this room. Theo asked whether they should conceal the uncertainty until the crisis passed. Mei shook her head. People could not make free choices if the information reaching them had already been edited. A socialist council member supported public oversight without claiming every private firm was guilty. Employees had a chance to socialize after the hearing without being questioned by reporters. The access log covered a span of seven hours, with one unexplained gap near midnight.

廖梅提出通話，不要求對方交換座標或效忠。即使同意這個選擇的人，也看得出代價。時間不多、資源有限，一次錯誤可能傳得很遠。西奧問是否該先隱瞞不確定性，等危機過後再說。廖梅搖頭。若資訊早被剪裁，人們便無法作出自由的選擇。 一名社會主義議員支持公共監督，沒有宣稱所有私人企業都有罪。 聽證後，員工終於能不受記者盤問地社交。 門禁紀錄涵蓋七小時，其中午夜附近有一段無法解釋的空白。

Later, under dim lights, Mei reread the original question. The dispute was larger now, but its center remained unchanged: who had the right to make an irreversible choice for someone else? Theo stayed until the last copy was sealed. Neither spoke about victory. They knew the next door would open only because today’s evidence had been preserved and today’s mistakes had been admitted. The author had been ordered to trek into the mountains and test the colony reactor before civilians were evacuated. Their disagreement was vigorous but not violent. Every emergency sensor needed a waterproof housing before the coming storm.

晚些時候，在微弱燈光下，廖梅重讀最初的問題。爭端已經擴大，但核心沒變：誰有權替別人作出不可逆的選擇？西奧留到最後一份副本封存。他們沒有談勝利，只知道下一扇門之所以能打開，是因為今天保住了證據，也承認了錯誤。 作者奉命徒步進入山區，在平民撤離前測試殖民反應爐。 他們爭論得激烈，卻沒有暴力。 每個緊急感測器都必須在風暴來前裝上防水外殼。
