星際殖民者沒有收到回航許可 · 第 7 / 25 章 · 5000–6000 · 英中對照 · 校訂中

Mei arrived with the unpleasant certainty that the simplest explanation was probably the one that had been prepared for public consumption. A hidden hangar contains the hull of a second colony ship whose existence Earth denied. The place was full of people who needed a quick answer, yet the evidence still belonged to those whose lives it could change. Instead of making a dramatic accusation, Mei marked the hour, the witnesses, and the smallest observable detail in a paper notebook. The trust owned one acre of land beside the canal, but ownership did not erase public access. A wealthy bachelor on the board objected to being excluded from the safety committee. Employees organized a charity boxing exhibition to support families hurt by the outage. The disciplinary rules required an independent hearing before any worker could be fired.

廖梅抵達時，心裡很清楚：最簡單的解釋往往是事先準備好要給大眾聽的版本。隱藏機庫裡有第二艘殖民船船殼，地球卻否認它存在。現場的人都急需答案，但證據仍屬於那些生活可能被它改變的人。她沒有戲劇化地指控誰，而是先在紙本筆記上記下時刻、證人，以及最小卻可觀察的細節。 信託持有運河旁一英畝土地，但所有權不會抹去公共通行權。 董事會上一位富有的單身漢反對自己被排除在安全委員會外。 員工舉辦慈善拳擊表演，支援停電受影響的家庭。 紀律規則要求解雇任何工人前先有獨立聽證。

There was a moment of quiet when Theo set the new material on the table. Its engine records show that the previous crew left not because Nacre was unlivable, but because someone ordered a silent departure. What mattered was not how surprising the discovery sounded, but whether anyone could repeat the test. Mei copied the relevant names carefully. If one person later changed the account, the original would still be available for comparison. The expedition notes preserved details of daily colonial life: An emigrant engineer explained why the old contract translation was unclear to new workers. The emperor tried to equate dissent with treason. His expression did not falter, but the room seemed to grow colder.

西奧把新資料放上桌時，四周安靜了一瞬。引擎紀錄顯示，前船員離開不是因為珠母星不宜居，而是有人命令祕密撤離。重要的不是發現有多驚人，而是別人能不能重做同一項測試。廖梅仔細抄下相關姓名；如果日後有人更改說法，原件仍可供比對。遠征筆記也記下殖民地日常生活的細節： 一名移居海外的工程師解釋舊合約翻譯為何讓新工人難懂。 皇帝試圖把異議等同叛國。 他的表情沒有動搖，房內卻彷彿更冷了。

“Will you trust me?” Theo asked. “With the evidence, yes,” Mei answered, “but not with a blank signature.” It was the sort of answer that could sound cold until someone noticed how carefully she had protected the other person’s right to disagree. Together they made a plan with a backup route, a deadline, and a record of who had accepted each responsibility. The clinic requested backup power for its fertility treatment laboratory. Mei would not forsake her obligations merely to keep the board's favor. A gay employee asked whether the revised benefits would cover his husband equally.

「妳信任我嗎？」西奧問。「證據上可以，空白授權書不行。」廖梅回答。這聽起來很冷淡，直到人們注意到她也小心保護對方拒絕的權利。他們一起訂下有備用路線、期限，以及明確責任分工的計畫。 診所要求為生育治療實驗室提供備用電力。 見遙不願為保住董事會好感而背棄自己的義務。 一名男同性戀員工詢問新福利是否同等保障他的丈夫。

A representative of the colony corporation arrived with an offer to resolve the matter quietly. Quietly, in this case, meant without giving affected people access to the original. Mei declined. She knew an office could be useful and still be dangerously wrong. Her objection was narrow and specific: the proposed decision relied on a document whose origin had not yet been independently verified. The expedition notes preserved details of daily colonial life: The workplace gloom lifted slightly after the tribunal protected the workers' safety veto. At the intersection of two estate paths, the gloom of approaching rain made the palace garden seem smaller. The route had been damaged by a landslide years earlier and never fully repaired. The children needed warm food to nourish their bodies before another winter arrived.

殖民公司的代表前來，提議私下解決。所謂私下，就是不讓受影響的人看到原件。廖梅拒絕了。她知道一個機關可以有用，也可能犯下危險的錯。她的反對具體而有限：提議中的決定依賴一份來源還沒接受獨立驗證的文件。遠征筆記也記下殖民地日常生活的細節： 法庭保護工人安全否決權後，工作場所的陰鬱稍稍散去。 在莊園兩條小徑交叉處，將至的雨天陰鬱讓宮廷花園顯得更小。 該路線多年前遭山崩破壞，從未完全修復。 孩子們需要溫暖食物滋養身體，才能迎接下一個冬天。

Mei locks the hangar under shared custody instead of claiming salvage rights. A few witnesses thanked Mei; others accused her of doing too little. She accepted both reactions without offering a promise she could not keep. Theo wrote down the complaints, including the ones directed at their own conduct. They were not building a monument to themselves. They were building a process that would still function after they left. An orthodox interpretation of the old lease favored the trust, but the law required public notice. The prohibition on unsafe work could not be canceled through a private annex. A provisional court order preserved public access while the disputed lease was reviewed.

廖梅將機庫交由各方共同保管，而非主張打撈所有權。幾名證人向廖梅道謝，另一些人指責她做得不夠。她接受兩種反應，不許下無法兌現的承諾。西奧寫下抱怨，包括針對他們自己行為的部分。他們不是為自己立紀念碑，而是建立自己離開後仍能運作的程序。 按照傳統解讀，舊租約有利信託，但法律仍要求公開告知。 禁止危險作業的規定不能透過私人附約取消。 暫時法院命令在爭議租約受審期間保住公共通行權。

By nightfall the newest report was signed by observers who were free to disagree. Mei stood beside Theo and looked at the distance between them and a self-governing colony that does not erase its first inhabitants. Progress felt less like triumph than like leaving a reliable mark on a difficult road. Someone would challenge that mark tomorrow. This time, there would be a public record with which to answer. The safety committee reached a unanimous decision to postpone the untested repair. He knew something about the cost of the family's vanity. A woodpecker struck a roadside pole while the technicians inspected the power lines.

夜幕降臨前，新的報告由可以提出異議的觀察者簽署。廖梅站在西奧身旁，看著他們與不抹除首批居民的自治殖民地之間的距離。進展不像勝利，比較像在艱難路上留下可靠的記號。明天有人會質疑，而這一次將有公開紀錄可以回答。 安全委員會一致決定延後尚未測試的維修。 他深知家族虛榮的代價。 技師檢查電線時，一隻啄木鳥啄著路邊電線桿。
