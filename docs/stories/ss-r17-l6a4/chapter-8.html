The morning had already acquired the look of an emergency before Mei reached the scene. Digging near a dry river exposes buried sensors that still record a persistent underground heat source. An official wanted a clean story, with a beginning that could be announced and an ending that would require no questions. Mei had learned that reality rarely showed such courtesy. She made room for the people who had been ordered to wait outside. An accumulation of sediment had changed the waterways, and the official map was no longer accurate. A vendor offered each inspector a sealed beverage, but they declined gifts during the audit. The blond driver in the delivery photograph confirmed that he had used the wrong route. Only the independent laboratory could certify the chemical condition of the stored batteries.

在廖梅抵達現場前，早晨已經有了緊急事件的模樣。乾河附近挖掘出埋藏感測器，至今仍記錄地下持續熱源。官員想要一套簡潔的故事，開頭能宣布，結尾又不用回答問題。她知道現實很少如此客氣，於是請那些被命令等在門外的人進來說話。 累積的泥沙改變了水道，官方地圖已不準確。 供應商給每位檢查員密封飲料，但眾人在稽核期間拒絕收禮。 送貨照片裡的金髮司機證實自己走錯了路。 只有獨立實驗室能證明儲存電池的化學狀況。

Theo examined the account twice, once as a friend and once as a witness who might have to defend every word. The original terraforming plan concealed a reactor beneath what settlers had been promised would be farmland. The difference between what they suspected and what they could establish mattered. They placed the new fact beside the previous chapter’s unanswered question and looked for a second source that had not been copied from the first. The expedition notes preserved details of daily colonial life: The warehouse manager demanded official clearance before the team opened the locked crates. A copy of those figures survived in the harbor office, where a careful official had refused to deduct unexplained losses. At noon, a provincial official presented a report declaring that all missing equipment had been destined for hospital repairs.

西奧把紀錄看了兩次：第一次以朋友身分，第二次則像一位必須為每個字負責的證人。原始地球化計畫把反應爐藏在原本承諾作為農田的地下。懷疑和能確立的事實必須分清楚。他們把新線索放在前一章未解的問題旁，尋找不曾從同一份資料抄出的第二來源。遠征筆記也記下殖民地日常生活的細節： 倉庫經理要求團隊開啟鎖箱前先取得正式許可。 港務處保留了一份數字副本，一名謹慎的官員拒絕扣除來源不明的損失。 中午，一名省府官員提交報告，宣稱所有失蹤設備都注定要送去修理醫院。

A crowd gathered around the disputed notice. “You have to choose a side,” someone shouted. Mei answered that she had chosen one already: the side of a claim that survived questions. Theo did not smile, but moved the most vulnerable bystanders away from the growing argument. The pair could disagree privately without turning their disagreement into a public threat. The inspection team could exert pressure through lawful requests, not threats of punishment. A heroic rescue story could not substitute for the full record of warehouse risks. A retired army lieutenant volunteered to organize transport without taking control of the inquiry.

人群圍住有爭議的公告。「妳必須選邊站！」有人喊。廖梅回答自己早已選擇：站在經得起提問的主張那一邊。西奧沒有微笑，只把最脆弱的旁觀者帶離爭執。他們可以私下不同意對方，卻不能把分歧變成公開威脅。 檢查團隊可以依法提出要求施壓，而非恐嚇處罰。 英雄式救援故事不能取代倉庫風險的完整紀錄。 一名退役陸軍中尉志願安排運輸，沒有接管調查。

The institution was not a single villain. It contained frightened clerks, dedicated workers, ambitious supervisors, and people who had learned to hide mistakes. the colony corporation wanted the incident classified as routine. Mei asked whose definition of routine was being used and whose losses were absent from the statistics. Nobody at the table could answer without consulting the sealed records. The expedition notes preserved details of daily colonial life: Literate workers could read the manual, but the team also prepared diagrams for everyone else. The canal's lotus flowers were untouched, though the adjacent storage fence was broken. That error had made their petitions appear marginal, even when hundreds signed them. Two security routes overlap near the gate, creating a blind spot in the records.

這個機構不是單一惡棍。裡面有害怕的書記、盡責的工人、野心勃勃的主管，也有學會掩蓋錯誤的人。殖民公司想把事件歸類為例行事項。廖梅追問：是誰定義例行？又有誰的損失被統計漏掉？桌邊沒人能在不看封存紀錄的情況下回答。遠征筆記也記下殖民地日常生活的細節： 識字的工人能閱讀手冊，團隊也替其他人準備圖解。 運河裡的蓮花完好，旁邊儲藏圍籬卻破損。 這個錯誤使他們的請願看來無足輕重，即使有數百人簽名也一樣。 兩條保全路線在大門附近重疊，造成紀錄盲點。

Mei suspends farming permits in that district and publishes the sensor data. The decision did not repair everything. It altered one procedure, protected one person, or saved one piece of evidence; on a day when almost everyone demanded a miracle, that was worth recording. Mei insisted that the result and its limits appear together in the official minutes. Anything less would turn a modest gain into tomorrow’s misleading legend. A provincial transport official reviewed the company's hazardous-cargo permit. The new technician's qualification was checked by an independent professional board. Volunteers brought refreshments to the residents waiting outside the warehouse.

廖梅暫停該區農耕許可，公開感測資料。這個決定沒有修好一切，只改變一道程序、保護一個人，或留下了一份證據；當幾乎所有人都要求奇蹟時，這仍值得記錄。廖梅堅持把成果和限制一起寫入正式紀錄。否則小小的進展又會變成明天誤導人的傳說。 一名省級交通官員檢視公司的危險貨物許可。 新技師的資格由獨立專業委員會核對。 志工為倉庫外等待的居民送來茶點。

The lights beyond the window did not offer comfort. They showed how many people were still waiting. Mei understood that the investigation might eventually expose her own earlier errors, and she resolved not to erase them. Theo suggested a short rest before the next hearing. She agreed, because exhaustion could damage judgment as thoroughly as deliberate deception. The team tried to retrieve a broken seal without disturbing the surrounding evidence. The warehouse entrance bordered a swamp that became unsafe after heavy rain. “My vocation is to keep people safe,” he said.

窗外的燈光沒有帶來安慰，只照出還有多少人在等待。廖梅明白調查最終可能揭露自己過去的錯誤，決定不去抹除。西奧建議下一場聽證前短暫休息。她同意，因為疲憊和蓄意欺騙一樣可能損害判斷。 團隊試圖取回破損封條，同時不干擾周圍證物。 倉庫入口鄰近沼澤，豪雨後變得危險。 「我的志業是讓人安全，」他說，「不是保住大臣的名聲。
