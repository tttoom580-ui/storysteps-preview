The Silent Arrival

星際殖民者沒有收到回航許可 · 第 1 / 25 章 · 5000–6000 · 英中對照 · 校訂中

Mei arrived with the unpleasant certainty that the simplest explanation was probably the one that had been prepared for public consumption. After forty years in transit, Commander Mei Liao brings the colony ship Meridian into orbit around the promised planet Nacre. The place was full of people who needed a quick answer, yet the evidence still belonged to those whose lives it could change. Instead of making a dramatic accusation, Mei marked the hour, the witnesses, and the smallest observable detail in a paper notebook. The legal clerk refused to abbreviate the witness's full name on a disputed record. At the river market, war refugees lined up for bread beneath an old anchor displayed outside the sailors' guild. The preservation rule was applicable even to the chairman who proposed the emergency vote. The public archive stored a copy of every switching order issued that week.

廖梅抵達時，心裡很清楚：最簡單的解釋往往是事先準備好要給大眾聽的版本。航行四十年後，廖梅艦長帶著殖民船「子午線號」進入約定星球珠母星的軌道。現場的人都急需答案，但證據仍屬於那些生活可能被它改變的人。她沒有戲劇化地指控誰，而是先在紙本筆記上記下時刻、證人，以及最小卻可觀察的細節。 法務書記拒絕在有爭議的紀錄上縮寫證人全名。 河岸市場裡，戰爭難民在水手公會門外的舊船錨下排隊領麵包。 證據保全規則即使對提出緊急表決的董事長也適用。 公共檔案室保存了那週每份切換命令的副本。

There was a moment of quiet when Theo set the new material on the table. Earth's return channel is silent, although the last navigation packet still carries a recent authentication mark. What mattered was not how surprising the discovery sounded, but whether anyone could repeat the test. Mei copied the relevant names carefully. If one person later changed the account, the original would still be available for comparison. The expedition notes preserved details of daily colonial life: An elderly resident pressed the emergency card against her bosom as she waited. A worker reported that her bra had been taken with her uniform during the inspection. A contractor had offered a bribe for access to the missing hour of footage.

西奧把新資料放上桌時，四周安靜了一瞬。地球回傳頻道沉默無聲，但最後一包導航資料仍有近期驗證標記。重要的不是發現有多驚人，而是別人能不能重做同一項測試。廖梅仔細抄下相關姓名；如果日後有人更改說法，原件仍可供比對。遠征筆記也記下殖民地日常生活的細節： 一位年長居民等待時，把緊急卡片緊貼胸前。 一名女工反映，檢查時她的胸罩與制服一同被拿走。 一名承包商曾為取得失蹤那一小時的影像提出賄賂。

“Will you trust me?” Theo asked. “With the evidence, yes,” Mei answered, “but not with a blank signature.” It was the sort of answer that could sound cold until someone noticed how carefully she had protected the other person’s right to disagree. Together they made a plan with a backup route, a deadline, and a record of who had accepted each responsibility. Volunteers used a small grill to broil vegetables for families at the center. The minister offered a comparative report instead of the original. His eyelid twitched whenever soldiers passed.

「妳信任我嗎？」西奧問。「證據上可以，空白授權書不行。」廖梅回答。這聽起來很冷淡，直到人們注意到她也小心保護對方拒絕的權利。他們一起訂下有備用路線、期限，以及明確責任分工的計畫。 志工用小烤架炙烤蔬菜，供服務中心的家庭食用。 議事廳下方有保存軍令的檔案庫，然而眼前封存的文件竟標著明天的日期。 每當士兵經過，他的眼皮便會抽動。

A representative of the colony corporation arrived with an offer to resolve the matter quietly. Quietly, in this case, meant without giving affected people access to the original. Mei declined. She knew an office could be useful and still be dangerously wrong. Her objection was narrow and specific: the proposed decision relied on a document whose origin had not yet been independently verified. The expedition notes preserved details of daily colonial life: Their reserve power was finite, so the clinic had to prioritize essential equipment. The safety fund would let small neighborhood repair workshops flourish after the blackout. A hired gangster threatened the courier who had delivered the original maintenance sheets. The freight van vanished at the junction between the riverside road and the tunnel.

殖民公司的代表前來，提議私下解決。所謂私下，就是不讓受影響的人看到原件。廖梅拒絕了。她知道一個機關可以有用，也可能犯下危險的錯。她的反對具體而有限：提議中的決定依賴一份來源還沒接受獨立驗證的文件。遠征筆記也記下殖民地日常生活的細節： 備用電力有限，因此診所必須優先供應必要設備。 安全基金將讓社區小修理工坊在停電後蓬勃發展。 一名受雇黑幫分子威脅遞送原始維修表的快遞員。 貨車在河岸道路與隧道交會的路口消失。

Mei refuses to release the landers until her crew independently measures the atmosphere. A few witnesses thanked Mei; others accused her of doing too little. She accepted both reactions without offering a promise she could not keep. Theo wrote down the complaints, including the ones directed at their own conduct. They were not building a monument to themselves. They were building a process that would still function after they left. A porter with a limp identified the sealed box he had carried upstairs. He said he could narrate the entire pursuit if she promised the guards would not drag him away. An orderly guided exhausted families to separate waiting rooms in the emergency clinic.

廖梅拒絕立刻放出登陸艇，要求船員先獨立測量大氣。幾名證人向廖梅道謝，另一些人指責她做得不夠。她接受兩種反應，不許下無法兌現的承諾。西奧寫下抱怨，包括針對他們自己行為的部分。他們不是為自己立紀念碑，而是建立自己離開後仍能運作的程序。 一名跛行的搬運員辨認出自己搬上樓的封盒。 他表示，只要公主保證守衛不把他拖走，便願意敘述整場追捕經過。 勤務員引導疲憊家庭前往緊急診所不同的候診室。

By nightfall the newest report was signed by observers who were free to disagree. Mei stood beside Theo and looked at the distance between them and a self-governing colony that does not erase its first inhabitants. Progress felt less like triumph than like leaving a reliable mark on a difficult road. Someone would challenge that mark tomorrow. This time, there would be a public record with which to answer. A bank teller confirmed that the suspicious transfer had occurred before the outage. The alleged contract was printed on virgin paper, unlike the stained original file. A warrant authorized capture, not execution; the distinction was applicable even to an enemy of the throne.

夜幕降臨前，新的報告由可以提出異議的觀察者簽署。廖梅站在西奧身旁，看著他們與不抹除首批居民的自治殖民地之間的距離。進展不像勝利，比較像在艱難路上留下可靠的記號。明天有人會質疑，而這一次將有公開紀錄可以回答。 銀行出納確認可疑轉帳發生於停電之前。 所謂合約印在全新紙上，與有污漬的原檔不同。 逮捕令授權的是拘捕，不是處決；即使對王座的敵人，這項區別仍然適用。
