It began not with a battle but with an ordinary interruption in the planet Nacre. The restored channel reveals a delayed recording from Earth announcing a political crisis and the collapse of the sponsoring corporation. At first, no one considered the interruption important enough to preserve. Mei thought otherwise. A forgotten detail could become a map, and a map could be used to control where frightened people were permitted to go. A small brook flowed behind the pump house and concealed part of its old foundation. The canal rescue team removed fragile coral samples from a science display before flooding. Mei refused to deprive residents of access while the lease remained in dispute. Workers used a platform to elevate the backup batteries above the projected flood line.

事情不是從戰鬥開始，而是從珠母星裡一次平凡的中斷開始。恢復的頻道收到地球延遲錄音，宣布政治危機及資助公司的崩潰。起初沒人認為這件事值得留下紀錄，但廖梅想法不同。遭忽略的細節可能變成地圖，而地圖也可能被用來控制驚恐的人可以去哪裡。 抽水站後方的小溪遮住一部分舊地基。 洪水前，運河救援隊移走科學展示裡脆弱的珊瑚樣本。 租約仍有爭議時，見遙拒絕剝奪居民的通行權。 工人用平台把備用電池抬高到預測淹水線以上。

Theo came back carrying a practical objection rather than an inspiring speech. No current government can verify the old ownership claims in the colony charter. They had both seen people harmed when an assumption hardened into policy. A signed report could be wrong, and an emotional memory could be right, but neither became proof merely by being repeated. They agreed to seek a measurement from an independent observer. The expedition notes preserved details of daily colonial life: The lease had an expiration date, but no one could find its required public notice. The retired mechanic's failing eyesight made written testimony easier than reading small diagrams. A volunteer offered a hearty meal to the crews who remained at the canal overnight.

西奧帶回的是實際的異議，而不是振奮人心的演說。如今沒有政府能證實舊殖民章程中的所有權聲明。他們都見過假設硬化成政策後如何傷人。有簽名的報告可能是錯的，強烈的記憶也可能是真的；兩者都不會因為反覆提起就變成證據。他們決定找獨立觀察員再測量一次。遠征筆記也記下殖民地日常生活的細節： 租約有到期日，卻沒有人找得到法定公開通知。 退休技師視力衰退，書面作證比閱讀小圖更容易。 志工給留守運河的工班一頓豐盛餐點。

“We can tell the truth without pretending we know everything,” Mei said. Theo replied that a partial truth was difficult to defend against people who controlled the doors. She answered that a perfect story with no verifiable source was more dangerous. They divided the work: one would protect the witnesses, while the other checked the physical records and the sequence of events. “Nor do you owe me a simpler story.” Across the terrace, a lantern hovered in the warm air. The inland districts also required clear warning messages, not just the waterfront neighborhoods. The lawyer's insistence on public notice changed the entire lease negotiation.

「我們能說實話，不需要假裝知道一切。」廖梅說。西奧回答，當對方掌控所有門口時，局部真相比較難捍衛。她則認為沒有可驗證來源的完美故事更危險。他們分配工作：一人保護證人，另一人核查實體紀錄和事件時序。 」露台另一端，一盞燈籠漂浮在暖風中。 內陸區域也需要清楚的警報訊息，不只濱水社區。 律師堅持公開通知，改變了整場租約談判。

The argument moved from a private corridor into a formal room, where every statement would be copied. A senior official from the colony corporation demanded certainty before granting access. Mei offered a less comfortable bargain: a limited preservation order now, followed by independent examination and a chance for every accused person to respond. That was enough to prevent an irreversible decision. The expedition notes preserved details of daily colonial life: The temporary lease allowed access without transferring ownership of the pump house. A visiting legislator asked whether the emergency access rule was enforceable after the trial. The majestic executive tower looked small from the muddy work site beside the canal. The new gate gained momentum as the winch began pulling it slowly upward.

爭論從私下走廊進入正式會議室，每句話都會留下副本。殖民公司的一名高級官員要求先確定真相才准查資料。廖梅提出比較不舒服的交換：先給有期限的保全命令，再獨立檢查，也讓所有被指控者有回應機會。這足以擋下一個不可逆的決定。遠征筆記也記下殖民地日常生活的細節： 臨時租約允許通行，但不轉讓抽水站所有權。 來訪議員詢問試辦結束後，緊急通行規則是否仍可執行。 從運河旁泥濘工地望去，宏偉的總部大樓也顯得渺小。 絞盤慢慢往上拉時，新閘門逐漸有了動能。

Mei tells the settlers that uncertainty does not erase their duty to protect existing residents. Even those who agreed with the choice could see its cost. Time was running out, supplies were not unlimited, and a mistake might travel far beyond this room. Theo asked whether they should conceal the uncertainty until the crisis passed. Mei shook her head. People could not make free choices if the information reaching them had already been edited. The chairman behaved like a monarch, but corporate authority still had legal limits. An animal shelter asked for reliable heating to protect newborn offspring during power cuts. A petrol generator was retained as a backup only after its emissions were tested.

廖梅告訴移民，不確定性不會抹去保護現有居民的責任。即使同意這個選擇的人，也看得出代價。時間不多、資源有限，一次錯誤可能傳得很遠。西奧問是否該先隱瞞不確定性，等危機過後再說。廖梅搖頭。若資訊早被剪裁，人們便無法作出自由的選擇。 董事長的行徑像君主，企業權力仍有法律界線。 動物收容所要求可靠暖氣，以保護停電期間剛出生的幼獸。 汽油發電機通過排放測試後才被保留作備用。

Later, under dim lights, Mei reread the original question. The dispute was larger now, but its center remained unchanged: who had the right to make an irreversible choice for someone else? Theo stayed until the last copy was sealed. Neither spoke about victory. They knew the next door would open only because today’s evidence had been preserved and today’s mistakes had been admitted. The operator had to rotate the valve slowly to prevent a sudden pressure change. If such restrictions continued, they could strangle trade between the inland farms and the sea. The physician explained that exhausted muscles could fail during a critical spell, just as poor surgical instruments could fail in an emergency.

晚些時候，在微弱燈光下，廖梅重讀最初的問題。爭端已經擴大，但核心沒變：誰有權替別人作出不可逆的選擇？西奧留到最後一份副本封存。他們沒有談勝利，只知道下一扇門之所以能打開，是因為今天保住了證據，也承認了錯誤。 操作員必須緩慢旋轉閥門，以免壓力突然變化。 這些限制若持續，可能扼殺內陸農田與海岸之間的貿易。 醫師解釋，疲憊肌肉可能在關鍵施法時失效，就像不良外科器械在急救時會出問題。
