地球沉默協定

The Earth Silence Protocol

The morning had already acquired the look of an emergency before Mei reached the scene. An engineer discovers that Earth's silence comes from a deliberate receiver filter installed before departure. An official wanted a clean story, with a beginning that could be announced and an ending that would require no questions. Mei had learned that reality rarely showed such courtesy. She made room for the people who had been ordered to wait outside. The repair room felt hotter than thirty Celsius when the cooling fan stopped. Excessive friction inside the relay produced the unusual vibration recorded during the test. A stray dog began to growl when inspectors approached the abandoned loading dock. The lawyer said a forged instruction could induce workers to override a safe setting.

在廖梅抵達現場前，早晨已經有了緊急事件的模樣。工程師發現地球的沉默源自出發前安裝的接收過濾器。官員想要一套簡潔的故事，開頭能宣布，結尾又不用回答問題。她知道現實很少如此客氣，於是請那些被命令等在門外的人進來說話。 散熱風扇停轉時，維修室溫度超過攝氏三十度。 繼電器內過度摩擦造成測試中記錄的異常震動。 檢查員靠近廢置裝貨區時，一隻流浪狗開始低吼。 律師說偽造指令可能誘使工人覆寫安全設定。

Theo examined the account twice, once as a friend and once as a witness who might have to defend every word. The filter was meant to hide messages challenging the colony company's ownership of Nacre. The difference between what they suspected and what they could establish mattered. They placed the new fact beside the previous chapter’s unanswered question and looked for a second source that had not been copied from the first. The expedition notes preserved details of daily colonial life: Many families still inhabit older buildings without reliable backup electricity. It was ironic that the trust's security account had been active long after its owner died. A retired mechanic lived on a grassy mound near the station.

西奧把紀錄看了兩次：第一次以朋友身分，第二次則像一位必須為每個字負責的證人。過濾器旨在遮蔽質疑殖民公司擁有珠母星的訊息。懷疑和能確立的事實必須分清楚。他們把新線索放在前一章未解的問題旁，尋找不曾從同一份資料抄出的第二來源。遠征筆記也記下殖民地日常生活的細節： 許多家庭仍居住在沒有可靠備電的老建築裡。 諷刺的是，信託保全帳戶在原持有人死後很久仍活躍。 一名退休技師住在測站附近的草丘上。

A crowd gathered around the disputed notice. “You have to choose a side,” someone shouted. Mei answered that she had chosen one already: the side of a claim that survived questions. Theo did not smile, but moved the most vulnerable bystanders away from the growing argument. The pair could disagree privately without turning their disagreement into a public threat. A peacock from a nearby garden wandered close to the temporary generator fence. A pianist played for patients while the clinic's backup system was tested. Investigators used a thermal probe to identify the relay's overheating point.

人群圍住有爭議的公告。「妳必須選邊站！」有人喊。廖梅回答自己早已選擇：站在經得起提問的主張那一邊。西奧沒有微笑，只把最脆弱的旁觀者帶離爭執。他們可以私下不同意對方，卻不能把分歧變成公開威脅。 附近花園的一隻孔雀走近臨時發電機圍欄。 診所測試備電系統時，一位鋼琴家為病人演奏。 調查員使用熱探針找出繼電器過熱點。

The institution was not a single villain. It contained frightened clerks, dedicated workers, ambitious supervisors, and people who had learned to hide mistakes. the colony corporation wanted the incident classified as routine. Mei asked whose definition of routine was being used and whose losses were absent from the statistics. Nobody at the table could answer without consulting the sealed records. The expedition notes preserved details of daily colonial life: The respective duties of the city and company had been confused in the old emergency plan. The urgency of the investigation did not justify shortsighted risks. The nurse asked the engineers to simplify the battery instructions for home patients. A vessel crossing the strait was delayed by the same storm approaching the city.

這個機構不是單一惡棍。裡面有害怕的書記、盡責的工人、野心勃勃的主管，也有學會掩蓋錯誤的人。殖民公司想把事件歸類為例行事項。廖梅追問：是誰定義例行？又有誰的損失被統計漏掉？桌邊沒人能在不看封存紀錄的情況下回答。遠征筆記也記下殖民地日常生活的細節： 舊緊急計畫混淆了市府與公司的各自責任。 調查再緊急，也不能合理化目光短淺的風險。 護士請工程師簡化居家病人的電池使用說明。 一艘穿越海峽的船因同場暴風雨而延誤。

Mei disables the filter with observers present, preserving a record of every configuration change. The decision did not repair everything. It altered one procedure, protected one person, or saved one piece of evidence; on a day when almost everyone demanded a miracle, that was worth recording. Mei insisted that the result and its limits appear together in the official minutes. Anything less would turn a modest gain into tomorrow’s misleading legend. The superficial inspection had missed heat damage deep inside the relay cabinet. No director could suppress a failed test merely to preserve the company's image. The diagram's symmetry made one missing sensor unusually easy to notice.

廖梅在觀察員見證下停用過濾器，保留每項設定變更紀錄。這個決定沒有修好一切，只改變一道程序、保護一個人，或留下了一份證據；當幾乎所有人都要求奇蹟時，這仍值得記錄。廖梅堅持把成果和限制一起寫入正式紀錄。否則小小的進展又會變成明天誤導人的傳說。 表面的檢查漏掉了繼電器櫃內部深處的熱損傷。 董事不得只為保護公司形象而壓下失敗測試。 圖面對稱的配置讓一個缺失的感測器格外明顯。

The lights beyond the window did not offer comfort. They showed how many people were still waiting. Mei understood that the investigation might eventually expose her own earlier errors, and she resolved not to erase them. Theo suggested a short rest before the next hearing. She agreed, because exhaustion could damage judgment as thoroughly as deliberate deception. An expired access token had been used to send the dormant account's last message. The public committee tried to unify the three separate emergency notification systems. The nurse emphasized the urgency of restoring the clinic's refrigerated medical supply.

窗外的燈光沒有帶來安慰，只照出還有多少人在等待。廖梅明白調查最終可能揭露自己過去的錯誤，決定不去抹除。西奧建議下一場聽證前短暫休息。她同意，因為疲憊和蓄意欺騙一樣可能損害判斷。 最後一封休眠帳戶訊息使用了一枚過期存取憑證。 公共委員會試圖整合三套分離的緊急通知系統。 護士強調恢復診所冷藏醫療用品供應的急迫性。
