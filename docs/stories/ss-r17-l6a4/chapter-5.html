The Message Hidden in Static

星際殖民者沒有收到回航許可 · 第 5 / 25 章 · 5000–6000 · 英中對照 · 校訂中

The morning had already acquired the look of an emergency before Mei reached the scene. A technician extracts an old warning from static on the planet's northern relay tower. An official wanted a clean story, with a beginning that could be announced and an ending that would require no questions. Mei had learned that reality rarely showed such courtesy. She made room for the people who had been ordered to wait outside. The company academy offered a practical course on emergency power rather than executive slogans. A false accusation against the warehouse team was withdrawn after the access records were checked. A banquet was taking place across the courtyard, where donors praised the war effort. A relief grant originally intended for fertilizer purchases had been redirected without explanation.

在廖梅抵達現場前，早晨已經有了緊急事件的模樣。技師從行星北方中繼塔的雜訊中取出舊警告。官員想要一套簡潔的故事，開頭能宣布，結尾又不用回答問題。她知道現實很少如此客氣，於是請那些被命令等在門外的人進來說話。 公司學院開設緊急供電實務課，而非宣傳主管口號。 查核門禁紀錄後，對倉庫團隊的不實指控遭撤回。 庭院對面正在舉行宴會，捐助者讚美戰爭。 原訂購買肥料的救助補助遭不明轉用。

Theo examined the account twice, once as a friend and once as a witness who might have to defend every word. The warning says a previous colony vanished after its governing council suppressed a radiation report. The difference between what they suspected and what they could establish mattered. They placed the new fact beside the previous chapter’s unanswered question and looked for a second source that had not been copied from the first. The expedition notes preserved details of daily colonial life: The riverside fishery requested a dedicated backup line to protect its refrigerated catch. The inspection revealed a small fracture in the connector supporting the temporary generator. Technicians learned to harness surplus power without bypassing the required safety checks.

西奧把紀錄看了兩次：第一次以朋友身分，第二次則像一位必須為每個字負責的證人。警告稱前一座殖民地在治理議會壓下輻射報告後消失。懷疑和能確立的事實必須分清楚。他們把新線索放在前一章未解的問題旁，尋找不曾從同一份資料抄出的第二來源。遠征筆記也記下殖民地日常生活的細節： 河岸漁業要求專用備電線路，以保護冷藏漁獲。 檢查發現支撐臨時發電機的接頭有一道小裂縫。 技師學習利用剩餘電力，不繞過必要安全檢查。

A crowd gathered around the disputed notice. “You have to choose a side,” someone shouted. Mei answered that she had chosen one already: the side of a claim that survived questions. Theo did not smile, but moved the most vulnerable bystanders away from the growing argument. The pair could disagree privately without turning their disagreement into a public threat. Each operator wore headphones to hear control-room instructions over the storm warnings. The clinic reviewed its oxygen intake records to calculate emergency battery demand. In the courtyard, a troupe rehearsed a ceremonial dance called the Maiden's Promise, though its lyrics had been rewritten to glorify the war.

人群圍住有爭議的公告。「妳必須選邊站！」有人喊。廖梅回答自己早已選擇：站在經得起提問的主張那一邊。西奧沒有微笑，只把最脆弱的旁觀者帶離爭執。他們可以私下不同意對方，卻不能把分歧變成公開威脅。 每位操作員戴耳機，以在風暴警報聲中聽清控制室指示。 診所檢視氧氣攝入紀錄，計算緊急電池需求。 庭院裡，一個劇團排練名為〈少女的承諾〉的儀式舞蹈，但歌詞早被改成頌揚戰爭。

The institution was not a single villain. It contained frightened clerks, dedicated workers, ambitious supervisors, and people who had learned to hide mistakes. the colony corporation wanted the incident classified as routine. Mei asked whose definition of routine was being used and whose losses were absent from the statistics. Nobody at the table could answer without consulting the sealed records. The expedition notes preserved details of daily colonial life: An offshore contractor had received the suspicious payment three weeks before the blackout. Now she allowed them to enter a sealed lecture hall, but only as prospective witnesses, not celebrated heroes. A resident asked for a referendum on the long-term public ownership of the canal land. Mei proposed to refine the test schedule instead of rushing a ceremonial reopening.

這個機構不是單一惡棍。裡面有害怕的書記、盡責的工人、野心勃勃的主管，也有學會掩蓋錯誤的人。殖民公司想把事件歸類為例行事項。廖梅追問：是誰定義例行？又有誰的損失被統計漏掉？桌邊沒人能在不看封存紀錄的情況下回答。遠征筆記也記下殖民地日常生活的細節： 一家離岸承包商在停電前三週收到可疑款項。 如今她允許他們進入封閉講堂，但只能以潛在證人身分入內，不能當成受歡迎的英雄。 一名居民要求針對運河土地的長期公共所有權舉辦公投。 廖梅建議改進測試時程，而非趕著辦象徵性重啟。

Mei broadcasts the warning to the fleet and establishes an independent hazard board. The decision did not repair everything. It altered one procedure, protected one person, or saved one piece of evidence; on a day when almost everyone demanded a miracle, that was worth recording. Mei insisted that the result and its limits appear together in the official minutes. Anything less would turn a modest gain into tomorrow’s misleading legend. Several students quietly began to rejoice; others feared that the academy would be closed for allowing dissent. Some shareholders began to resent the safety veto, calling it an obstacle to profit. The operating room remained serene after its generator switched smoothly to backup power.

廖梅把警告播放給全艦隊，設立獨立風險委員會。這個決定沒有修好一切，只改變一道程序、保護一個人，或留下了一份證據；當幾乎所有人都要求奇蹟時，這仍值得記錄。廖梅堅持把成果和限制一起寫入正式紀錄。否則小小的進展又會變成明天誤導人的傳說。 幾名學生悄悄歡欣，其他人則害怕學院因容許異議而被關閉。 部分股東開始怨恨安全否決權，稱它阻礙獲利。 手術室的發電機順利切換備電後，室內依舊平靜。

The lights beyond the window did not offer comfort. They showed how many people were still waiting. Mei understood that the investigation might eventually expose her own earlier errors, and she resolved not to erase them. Theo suggested a short rest before the next hearing. She agreed, because exhaustion could damage judgment as thoroughly as deliberate deception. A neighborhood pastor gave a short sermon about caring for people before property. The board refused to shuffle expenses between accounts to make the trial look cheaper. A tenant demanded proof that her rent would not rise because of the emergency repair.

窗外的燈光沒有帶來安慰，只照出還有多少人在等待。廖梅明白調查最終可能揭露自己過去的錯誤，決定不去抹除。西奧建議下一場聽證前短暫休息。她同意，因為疲憊和蓄意欺騙一樣可能損害判斷。 社區牧師簡短講道，提醒人們應先照顧人，再談財產。 董事會拒絕在帳戶間調換支出，營造試辦更便宜的假象。 一名房客要求保證不會因緊急維修而漲租。
