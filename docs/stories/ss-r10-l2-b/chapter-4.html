空蕩蕩的體育館

The Empty Gym

把市場的聲音，留到明天 · 第 4 / 25 章 · 1000–2000 · 英中對照

用互動版查詞、朗讀與收藏

The market's eastern hall closed for repairs, so the team had to attend a meeting in the university gym. Inside, a broken bird cage, a rusty pan, and an old comb stood on a table of objects that sellers had offered for the sound display. The student department wanted to use them as funny decorations. May objected. The objects were private memories, not toys. A woman explained that the cage had once protected an injured bird; its open door was part of the story. Evan asked the group to listen before moving anything. That small pause changed the motion of the meeting.

市場東側大廳因維修關閉，團隊只好到大學體育館開會。館內桌上放著一個壞鳥籠、一只生鏽的平底鍋和一把舊梳子，都是攤商願意借給聲音展的物件。學生事務部門想把它們當成有趣的裝飾。慧提出反對。那些是私人的記憶，不是玩具。一位女士說，鳥籠以前保護過受傷的小鳥；籠門敞開也是故事的一部分。安澤請大家移動物件前先聽清楚。這個小小停頓改變了會議的方向。

One seller brought a small box of medicine and asked if the recording could mention her son's drug treatment. May said health details should stay private unless the family clearly wanted them shared. Another seller from the coastal region spoke about a wolf he had seen during a mountain trip, then laughed because no one believed him. Evan suggested a firm rule: only stories approved by their owners could be played, and a minor child needed a parent or guardian's permission. Mrs. Fen called that a fair standard. No one wanted to build a cheerful show from someone else's pain.

一位攤商帶來一小盒藥，問錄音能不能提到兒子的藥物治療。慧說，健康細節應保持私密，除非家人明確希望公開。另一位來自沿海地區的攤商提到自己登山時見過狼，隨後笑了，因為沒有人相信。安澤提出明確規則：只有經故事主人核准的內容才能播放；若涉及未成年孩子，還需要家長或監護人同意。芬姨認為這是公平的標準。沒有人希望用別人的痛苦做出熱鬧的展覽。

For lunch, the students made fried rice on a safe kitchen stove. Evan tried to fry an egg and broke it into the wrong pan. May laughed and handed him a clean spoon. Their work had been serious all morning, so the simple mistake helped everyone relax. A volunteer brought a purple umbrella, a paper wing from a school play, and a drawing of a running track. She said she wanted to encourage children to describe sounds they remembered. May answered that the purpose was not to collect everything but to help people decide what they wished others to hear.

午餐時，學生在安全的廚房爐具上做炒飯。安澤試著煎蛋，卻把蛋打進錯的鍋子。慧笑著遞給他乾淨湯匙。大家整個上午都很嚴肅，這個小失誤讓氣氛放鬆。一名志工帶來紫色雨傘、校園戲劇用的紙翅膀，以及跑道圖畫。她說希望鼓勵孩子描述記得的聲音。慧回答，目的不是把一切收集起來，而是讓人自己決定想讓別人聽見什麼。

By the end, the group chose three simple areas for the first show: work sounds, family memories, and sounds that should remain unheard. The last area would contain no private files, only signs explaining a person's right to say no. Evan had expected an argument over equipment, but the real question had become ownership. He thanked May for speaking when everyone else was ready to approve the decorations. She smiled. He had started the day as the person who cared about cables and speakers; now he was helping protect the spaces between people's words.

會議結束時，團隊決定第一場展覽分成三區：工作的聲音、家人的記憶，以及應該保留安靜的聲音。最後一區不放私人檔案，只用標示說明每個人都有拒絕權。安澤原以為大家會為設備爭吵，真正的問題卻變成了所有權。他謝謝慧在眾人準備批准裝飾時勇敢發言。慧笑了。他早上還只是關心電線和喇叭的人，現在卻開始保護別人話語之間應有的空白。

After the meeting, a volunteer asked if every object needed its own form. May said they could group small items in one list only when all owners agreed. She pointed to the open bird cage and reminded the team that the object had a condition attached to it: the door must remain open. Evan drew a simple mark beside the photograph so the next shift would not forget. He also suggested writing who had permission to touch fragile things. A student called the rules boring. Mrs. Fen asked him whether he would feel differently if the object belonged to his grandmother. The student thought for a moment and nodded. May did not demand an answer from everyone. It was enough that they had begun to think about the people who would come after them, including those who had not attended this meeting.

會後，一位志工問是否每件物品都需要獨立表格。慧說，只有在所有主人同意時，才能把小物件合併列在一張清單上。她指著敞開的鳥籠，提醒團隊這件物品附帶一個條件：籠門必須保持打開。安澤在照片旁畫了簡單記號，避免下一班忘記。他還建議寫清楚誰可以碰易碎物品。一名學生說規則很無聊。芬姨問他，如果那是祖母的東西，想法還會一樣嗎？學生想了想，點頭。慧沒有要求每個人立刻回答。大家開始替後來的人設想，包括沒有參加這場會議的人，已經足夠。

When they carried the objects back, the cage owner asked May to keep its door open. The request seemed small, but May wrote it down beside the permission form. Evan offered to hold the box while she worked. For a moment their hands touched on the handle, and both looked away with matching awkward smiles. Then May said, “We have to get this to the right shelf.” Evan answered, “I know. I am not asking you to drop it.” Their laughter echoed through the empty gym, a sound no machine needed to save.

搬回物件時，鳥籠主人特別請慧讓籠門保持打開。要求看似微小，慧卻把它寫在同意書旁。安澤伸手幫她扶住箱子。兩人的手在提把上碰到一下，又同時帶著尷尬的笑容移開視線。「我們得把它放回正確的架子，」慧說。「我知道，」安澤答，「我可沒叫妳把它丟下去。」兩人的笑聲在空蕩體育館裡回響，而那是無須機器保存的聲音。
