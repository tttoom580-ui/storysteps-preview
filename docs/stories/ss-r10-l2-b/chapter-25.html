帶回家的聲音

The Sound We Carry Home

把市場的聲音，留到明天 · 第 25 / 25 章 · 1000–2000 · 英中對照

用互動版查詞、朗讀與收藏

The final listening day began with an ordinary sound: a shopkeeper pulling up a metal door. May stood near the entrance, watching the new committee welcome its first visitors. A chart showed the hours and the right to ask for a recording to be removed. The signs had a simple appearance, with no company name covering the voices. A curious child stopped at the border between the game area and the quiet room, then chose to enter slowly. That small choice reminded May of their first aim: not to collect the most noise, but to make a place where people could decide how to be heard.

最後聆聽日以一個平凡聲音開場：店主拉開金屬店門。慧站在入口，看著新委員會迎接第一批訪客。圖表列出開放時間與要求移除錄音的權利。標示外觀簡單，沒有公司名字蓋過人的聲音。一個好奇的孩子停在遊戲區與安靜室的界線，最後選擇慢慢走進去。這個小小選擇讓慧想起最初的目標：不是收集最多噪音，而是打造讓人自行決定如何被聽見的地方。

A music teacher brought a classical recording but asked whether it would fit the market show. Evan thanked him and explained that the archive focused on voices and sounds connected to the market. He did not pretend to be a master of every kind of music. A visitor from another city asked about the old missing box, and Mrs. Fen laughed as she told the true story. The man seemed surprised that the team had once mistaken a careful worker for a thief. May said justice began with correcting one's own mistakes, not only pointing out those of others.

一位音樂老師帶來古典音樂錄音，詢問是否適合市場展覽。安澤道謝，解釋檔案主要收錄與市場有關的聲音。他沒有假裝自己是所有音樂的專家。外地訪客問起當年失蹤的箱子，芬姨笑著說出真相。那人似乎很驚訝，團隊曾把細心的工人誤會成小偷。慧說，正義始於修正自己的錯誤，而不只是指出別人的錯。

In the afternoon, a woman came with her niece, who had won a prize at school for a story about the market. The girl wanted to post her work beside the recordings. May asked her to show the text to her family first, and the girl agreed. Another visitor complained about garbage near the entrance. A trainee checked the bin and called the cleaning staff rather than blaming the nearest child. The day moved rapidly, but the new team did not forget the rules. They could answer questions, obey safety instructions, and still make visitors feel welcome.

下午，一位女士帶著姪女到場。女孩以市場故事在學校得獎，想把作品貼在錄音旁。慧請她先讓家人看過文字，女孩答應了。另一位訪客抱怨入口有垃圾。學員查看垃圾桶並通知清潔人員，沒有責怪附近的孩子。一天進展很快，但新團隊沒有忘記規則。他們能回答問題、遵守安全指示，也能讓訪客感到受歡迎。

At the closing ceremony, Evan stood beside May but let the new leaders speak. One thanked the sellers, the families, and the people who had said no. Another read the rules with careful spelling so they could be copied correctly. May felt a brief touch of anger when she remembered how easily the first notice had accused an innocent worker. But the anger had changed into a reason to do better. She looked at the empty chair and knew it belonged in the room as surely as the loudest recording. A place could honor absence without turning it into a show.

閉幕儀式時，安澤站在慧旁邊，卻讓新任負責人發言。一人感謝攤商、家人，以及曾經說不的人。另一人仔細逐字讀出規則，確保抄錄時拼字正確。慧想起第一張公告多麼容易就冤枉無辜工人，心裡短暫冒出怒意，但那怒意已變成想做得更好的理由。她看著空椅子，知道它和最大聲的錄音一樣，理所當然地屬於這間房。一個地方可以尊重缺席，而不把缺席變成表演。

When the visitors left, the committee posted the final report. It listed the files that remained, the ones removed by request, and the people responsible for future changes without exposing anyone's private reasons. Mrs. Fen turned off the main lights. She told May and Evan that the market did not need them to stay forever; it needed them to have taught others how to care. May felt proud but also light. The work had grown beyond her hands. Evan's new job would begin next month, while May would join the local teaching program. Their roads were different, but they had chosen to keep walking toward each other.

訪客離開後，委員會張貼最後報告。上面列出仍保存的檔案、依要求移除的檔案，以及未來變更的負責人，卻不揭露任何私人撤回理由。芬姨關掉主燈，告訴慧與安澤，市場不需要他們永遠留下，而是需要他們教會別人如何照顧這些記憶。慧驕傲，也感到輕盈。工作已超越自己的雙手。安澤下個月開始新工作，慧會加入本地教學計畫。兩人的路不同，卻決定繼續朝彼此走去。

After the ceremony, a rapid rain began, and the last visitors gathered beneath the awning. May watched the new leaders offer a dry route and tell people which doors were open. The small group did not seem afraid of questions anymore. Evan stood beside her with a paper bag of bread from the market. He said he had once thought the best recording was the one that kept every sound. May reminded him that they had now left many sounds unrecorded on purpose. The project was stronger for those empty spaces. He asked whether she wanted to save a final clip of the rain. She shook her head. “Not this one,” she said. “This one can just happen.” They listened until the rain softened, then left the market to its own ordinary evening.

儀式後，一陣急雨突然落下，最後的訪客聚在遮棚下。慧看著新負責人主動提供乾燥路線、告訴人們哪些門可以走。那群年輕人似乎已不再害怕問題。安澤站在她旁邊，手裡拿著市場買的麵包紙袋。他說自己以前認為最好的錄音就是不漏掉任何聲音。慧提醒他，他們如今刻意讓許多聲音沒有被錄下，而這些空白反而使計畫更堅強。他問她是否想存最後一段雨聲。她搖頭。「這段不用，」她說，「讓它自然發生就好。」兩人聽到雨勢轉小，才把市場留給它平凡的夜晚。

At the gate, Evan asked whether May wanted to hear the market once more before leaving. She closed her eyes. There were steps on stone, the soft call of a late seller, and laughter from the street. No microphone was on. May opened her eyes and said, “I think we can carry this home without saving it.” Evan smiled. He took her hand, and they walked into the evening together. Behind them the market kept its ordinary sounds, plus a new kind of quiet: the freedom to decide which stories would travel into tomorrow.

走到大門時，安澤問慧要不要在離開前再聽一次市場。她閉上眼睛。石地上的腳步聲、晚收攤的輕聲叫賣，以及街上的笑聲傳來。沒有一支麥克風開著。慧睜眼說：「我想，這些可以帶回家，不一定要存檔。」安澤笑了。他牽起她的手，兩人一起走進傍晚。身後的市場繼續發出平凡聲音，此外還多了一種新的安靜：人們有自由決定，哪些故事要走向明天。
