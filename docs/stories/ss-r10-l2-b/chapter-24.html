空著的一張椅子

One Empty Chair

把市場的聲音，留到明天 · 第 24 / 25 章 · 1000–2000 · 英中對照

用互動版查詞、朗讀與收藏

A week before the last event, Mrs. Fen noticed the absence of a former seller's recording from the program. May checked the list and found that the man's daughter had requested its removal months earlier. Some volunteers called it a failure because the show would feel incomplete. May said that honoring the request was a success, not a loss. The recording would remain absent. Evan asked the new team to adjust the scene so visitors would understand that the empty chair had meaning. It was a space left by choice, not a mistake waiting to be repaired.

最後活動前一週，芬姨發現節目中少了某位前攤商的錄音。慧查了清單，發現他的女兒幾個月前就要求撤下。有些志工認為這是失敗，因為演出看起來不完整。慧說，尊重要求是成功，不是損失。那份錄音會繼續缺席。安澤請新團隊調整場景，讓訪客理解空椅子也有意義。那是出於選擇留下的空間，不是等待修補的錯誤。

For the closing fair, a bakery offered salty bread, and a shop downstairs brought balloons. A volunteer arrived carrying a toy camel and an ape mask from a children's play. The decorations made everyone laugh, but May asked that they be kept away from the quiet room. She did not want the final event to behave like a loud party without room for people who needed calm. Evan supported the plan. The team set one space for stories and another for games, making it easier for visitors to choose what suited them.

閉幕活動時，麵包店提供鹹麵包，樓下商店帶來氣球。一位志工拿著兒童戲劇的玩具駱駝和猿猴面具，讓大家笑了起來。但慧請他們把裝飾放在安靜室之外。她不希望最後活動變成吵鬧派對，容不下需要安靜的人。安澤支持這個安排。團隊設立故事區與遊戲區，讓訪客更容易選擇適合自己的地方。

The new committee finished its list of responsibilities on the basis of what had worked. One group would handle permission, one would check equipment, and one would answer complaints. A former student offered to take everything over alone, but Mrs. Fen said the point was to survive beyond any single person's energy. May agreed. She felt a small nerve of fear when she thought of leaving her daily role, yet she knew that good work should be able to continue without its first makers. Evan reminded her that handing over a task was not the same as leaving the people behind.

新委員會根據已被證明有效的方式，完成職責清單。一組處理授權，一組檢查設備，一組回覆申訴。一位畢業學生想獨自接手全部工作，芬姨卻說，重點正是讓計畫不依賴某一人的力氣而存續。慧同意。想到自己要離開日常角色，她心裡仍有一絲恐懼，但也知道好工作應能在創辦者離開後繼續。安澤提醒她，交接工作不等於拋下人們。

During rehearsal, the young volunteers played a mixture of market sounds and then stopped before the empty chair. Their timing was not perfect, but the silence felt honest. One student said the memory of the removed voice could still be respected without playing it. May smiled. They had learned the lesson deeply enough to teach it without her. Evan gave her a look across the room, the kind that said he knew what this moment meant. Neither rushed to fill the silence with praise. They simply let the new team complete the rehearsal.

彩排時，年輕志工播放混合的市場聲，接著在空椅子前停下。節奏不完美，沉默卻很誠實。一名學生說，即使不播放，仍然能尊重那份被撤下聲音的記憶。慧笑了。他們已把這堂課學得足夠深，不用她也能教下去。安澤隔著房間看她一眼，那眼神表示他懂得此刻的意義。誰也沒有急著用稱讚填滿安靜，只是讓新團隊完成彩排。

A balloon escaped from a volunteer's hand and floated near the ceiling. A handsome young actor joked that he could catch it with a long stick, but May asked him not to climb on a chair. The actor agreed and let the balloon remain until a worker could reach it safely. Evan returned from carrying chairs with a flat bicycle tire and laughed at his own bad luck. May offered to walk with him to the repair stand. On the way, he asked whether their promise to keep dating would survive different work schedules. She said she could not promise an easy future, only honest effort and time to talk when things changed. He said that was what he wanted too. Neither needed a perfect plan. They had learned how to revise plans together without making the other person pay for every change.

一顆氣球從志工手裡飛走，飄到天花板附近。一位英俊年輕演員開玩笑說要用長棍去撈，慧請他不要站上椅子。他同意了，讓氣球留在原處，等工人安全取下。安澤搬完椅子回來，發現腳踏車輪胎沒氣，笑說自己運氣不好。慧說要陪他走去維修站。路上他問，兩人繼續交往的承諾能否撐過不同工作時間。慧說她無法保證未來容易，只能承諾誠實努力，遇到變化就花時間談。他說自己要的也是如此。他們不需要完美計畫；他們已學會一起修改安排，而不讓對方為每項變動付出代價。

Late that night, May and Evan ate the last pieces of bread by the open door. They spoke about their plans after graduation, and May promised to visit the city where he would work when her own schedule allowed. He promised to visit the market without turning every date into a recording session. They laughed. The old hall had seen mistakes, anger, and repairs, but it had also held a kindness that could not be saved in any file. May was tired and hopeful at once. Tomorrow the visitors would come; tonight the room was enough.

那天深夜，慧與安澤在開著的門邊吃完最後幾塊麵包。他們談畢業後的計畫，慧答應等自己的時間允許，會去他工作的城市探望。他也答應回市場時，不會把每次約會都變成錄音行程。兩人笑了。老大廳見過錯誤、憤怒與修繕，卻也容納一種任何檔案都存不住的善意。慧同時疲倦又充滿希望。明天訪客會來，今晚這間房已足夠。
