把市場的聲音，留到明天 · 第 11 / 25 章 · 1000–2000 · 英中對照

用互動版查詞、朗讀與收藏

The note about removing recordings had been written by an author named Mr. Lin, who once worked at the market theater. He arrived at the fair with a narrow folder and a pale face. He said a student had played part of his wedding speech at a private test, even though he had permitted it only for a family event. May listened without trying to defend the team. Evan checked the file record and found that a volunteer had included the speech in a draft by mistake. “We disagree about many things,” May told Evan later, “but neither of us believes a mistake becomes harmless because it was small.”

那張詢問錄音撤回的紙條，出自曾在市場劇場工作的作家林先生。他帶著一個窄資料夾來到活動現場，臉色蒼白。他說，一名學生在私人試播中播放了他婚禮致詞的一段，然而他只同意在家庭活動使用。慧專心聽，沒有急著替團隊辯護。安澤查了檔案紀錄，發現志工誤把致詞放進草稿。「我們很多事情看法不同，」慧稍後對他說，「但都不會因為錯誤小，就認為沒有傷害。」

The recording had not reached the public stage. Even so, Mr. Lin asked how to make sure it would never be played by a new student. Evan showed him the storage folder and the list of people with access. They agreed to remove the draft, record the action, and keep only a note saying permission had been withdrawn. A guard from the building office watched as the deletion was checked. He asked for no fee and offered to help with future checks. Mr. Lin finally relaxed. “You have solved the practical puzzle,” he said. “Now please remember the human relationship behind it.”

錄音尚未在公開舞臺播放。但林先生仍問，要如何確保新一屆學生不會再播。安澤展示儲存資料夾和可存取人員名單。大家同意刪掉草稿、記錄處理過程，僅保留已撤回授權的紀錄。大樓管理處的警衛在旁確認刪除。他不收費，還願意幫忙日後的查核。林先生終於放鬆。「你們解決了實務難題，」他說，「現在也請記得背後的人際關係。」

During the break, a crow called from a tree beside the street. A waiter carried tea past a poster for a forest walk, and an old priest stopped to ask about the community singers. The sounds were ordinary, yet May found them comforting after the tense talk. Evan measured the display stand in centimeters to make sure it would not block the door. “You are very skilled at finding another detail to fix,” she said. He raised an eyebrow. “And you are very skilled at finding another reason for me to fix it.” She laughed despite her tired eyes.

休息時，街邊樹上的烏鴉叫了幾聲。服務員端著茶走過森林步道海報，一位老牧師停下來詢問社區歌手的活動。這些聲音平凡，卻在緊張談話後讓慧覺得安定。安澤用公分量展示架，確認它不會擋住門。「你很擅長找出更多需要修的細節，」她說。他挑起眉毛：「妳也很擅長找出更多理由讓我去修。」即使疲憊，慧還是笑了。

At sunset, Mr. Lin returned with a different story. He offered a short memory of the old theater, but said he wanted to read the final version before anyone heard it. May thanked him and handed him the corrected form. This time there was no broad promise about ownership, nor any pressure to agree immediately. They left a blank space for the limits he chose. He decided to wait a week. Evan said the show could continue without that file. May felt relief that the project had grown strong enough to hear a refusal.

日落時，林先生又帶來另一個故事。他願意談一段老劇場的回憶，但要求任何人聽到前，都先讓他看過最後版本。慧向他道謝，遞上修正後的表格。這回沒有過度寬泛的所有權承諾，也沒有逼他立刻同意。表格留有空白，讓他寫下使用限制。他決定再等一週。安澤說，沒有這份檔案，展覽一樣可以繼續。慧鬆了口氣，覺得計畫終於堅強到能聽見拒絕。

The revised floor map showed each table's position using a centimeter ruler, but Mr. Lin said the numbers were less important to him than the people they affected. He asked May to include a short note explaining why the private wedding speech was no longer part of the program. May answered that even the note should not reveal the content of his request without permission. He agreed to a general statement about withdrawn material. At the next rehearsal, someone joked that the empty space made the program look rude, as if it had forgotten a guest. Evan suggested saying nothing about a specific person and simply explaining the right to withdraw. Mr. Lin approved that approach. May felt the decision preserved both the exhibit's honesty and his privacy. It was a form of respect she wished more people had offered him years earlier.

修改後的平面圖以公分標出桌位，但林先生說，數字對他不如受影響的人重要。他請慧簡短說明，私人婚禮致詞為何不在節目中。慧回答，即使是說明，也不該未經同意揭露申請內容。他同意使用概括的撤回聲明。下次彩排時，有人笑說空白讓節目顯得無禮，好像忘了請客人。安澤建議不要提特定人物，只解釋撤回權。林先生核准這種處理。慧覺得這保住了展覽的誠實，也保住他的隱私。她希望多年前曾有更多人以同樣方式尊重他。

When they locked the booth, Evan asked May whether she wanted to cancel their visit to the book fair. She looked surprised that he remembered. “No,” she said, “I still want to go. But we each need one afternoon when our whole world is not this project.” He agreed and promised not to bring a recorder. They stood beneath the streetlight planning which books to look for. May had expected the day to end with disappointment; instead, it ended with a small, possible future that belonged only to them.

鎖上展間時，安澤問慧是否想取消去書展的約定。慧驚訝他還記得。「不，」她說，「我還想去。但我們都需要一個下午，讓世界不只是這個計畫。」他同意，並答應不帶錄音器。他們站在街燈下，討論要找哪些書。慧本以為這一天會以失望結束，最後卻有了一個小小、可能成真的未來，而且只屬於他們。
