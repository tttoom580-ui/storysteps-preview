A Name Under Erasure

第十三道潮痕：鐘樓不肯說的時間 · 第 12 / 25 章 · 4000–5000 · 英中對照 · 校訂中

Vela found an abnormal entry in the archive register. A man who had signed an inspection report twenty years earlier was listed as both an observer and a person under detention. Iona asked to acquaint herself with the full record before drawing a conclusion. The clerk suggested that the two entries might be compatible because one referred to a temporary administrative hold. Yet an old report spoke of a hostage taken during a labor conflict. The word frightened several families. Iona promised to confront the contradiction without declaring that a person had been kidnapped merely because one document used a dramatic term.

薇拉在檔案登記簿中找到一筆異常記載：二十年前簽署檢查報告的男子，同時被列為觀察員和受拘留者。伊歐娜要求先熟悉完整紀錄再下結論。書記說兩筆資料可能相容，因為一筆指短暫行政留置。另一份舊報告卻提到勞資衝突中的人質，讓好幾個家庭驚慌。伊歐娜承諾正面處理矛盾，卻不會只因文件使用戲劇化字眼，就斷言某人被綁架。

An independent observer from the continental workers' association joined the meeting. He explained that the old harbor had once operated under labor norms that permitted severe discrimination. Some workers had been treated almost as property, and later writers described the system with the term slavery. The association had collected testimony but could not independently verify every account. Iona asked that the document preserve the witnesses' own words while distinguishing those claims from proven administrative facts. The goal was neither to excuse past abuse nor to invent certainty. The descendants of those workers deserved an honest record more than a comforting story.

來自大陸工人協會的獨立觀察員出席會議。他解釋，舊港口曾受允許嚴重歧視的勞動規範管理。一些工人幾乎被當成財產，後人因此以奴役形容那套制度。協會蒐集過證詞，卻無法獨立驗證所有敘述。伊歐娜要求保留證人的原話，同時區分主張與已證實的行政事實。目的既不是替過往虐待開脫，也不是虛構確定性。那些工人的後代值得一份誠實紀錄，而不只是令人安心的故事。

In the old script of a harbor play, a missionary had once accused a gatekeeper of being a traitor for refusing an unlawful order. A museum viewer asked whether the performance could be used as evidence. Vela said the play represented public memory, not a signed official report. A retired actor began to mumble the closing lines, and the hall fell quiet. Iona asked the archivist to keep the script with the cultural collection, clearly labeled. The emotional sensation it created might guide a question, but the investigation still required dates, witnesses, and verifiable records before condemning anyone.

一齣舊港口戲劇的劇本中，曾有傳教士把拒絕非法命令的守門員罵成叛徒。博物館觀眾問能不能把戲當證據。薇拉說，那是公共記憶的表現，不是簽署過的正式報告。一名退休演員輕聲喃喃念出結尾台詞，整個大廳安靜下來。伊歐娜請檔案員把劇本留在文化收藏中，清楚標明性質。戲帶來的強烈感受可以引導提問，但要譴責任何人，仍需要日期、證人和可查核紀錄。

A warehouse ledger then supplied a more concrete clue. It recorded a stock of iron bolts transferred to North Lock on the same date as the disputed detention. The delivery might be unrelated, but the quantities matched the number of missing pieces in the old measuring frame. Iona asked whether the transfer order was still viable as evidence after decades of damage. Vela pointed to the intact signatures and duplicate inventory, suggesting that several details could be checked. The team copied the page and left the original in protective storage. Their next task was to find someone who could explain why the bolts were moved.

接著，一本倉庫帳冊帶來更具體的線索。它記載在有爭議拘留事件的同一天，有一批鐵螺栓被送往北閘。運送可能毫無關聯，但數量恰與舊測量架缺失的零件數相符。伊歐娜問，經過數十年損壞，這張轉運單還能否作為可行證據。薇拉指出尚存的簽名與重複盤點紀錄，表示有些細節仍能查核。團隊只抄下頁面，把原件留在保護庫中。他們下一步要找出能解釋螺栓移動原因的人。

The archived testimony included an allegation that guards had tried to kidnap a union organizer during the old dispute. The word was disturbing, and some listeners wanted the council to issue an immediate condemnation. Iona asked the observer to distinguish the act described from the label used by a frightened witness. The surviving record showed that the organizer had been held in a locked office for several hours; it did not establish who issued the instruction or whether any lawful authority existed. The attorney proposed preserving the testimony and referring the historic incident to a separate review. The tide inquiry could not responsibly decide that legal question in passing.

保存的證詞中，有人指稱衛兵曾在舊爭議期間企圖綁架工會組織者。字眼令人不安，有些聽眾要求議會立刻譴責。伊歐娜請觀察員區分證人描述的行動，與受驚之下使用的標籤。現存紀錄顯示，組織者曾被關在上鎖辦公室數小時，卻無法證明是誰下令，或當時有無合法權限。律師提議保存證詞，把歷史事件轉交另一項審查。潮汐調查無法在附帶討論中負責任地裁決那個法律問題。

That evening the attorney warned Iona that a compelling mystery could push witnesses into answering questions they did not understand. She agreed to give each person time to read a plain-language description of the inquiry. Anyone could decline. A family that feared renewed attention could ask to provide only a location or date, rather than its entire history. Iona was disappointed by how little they had proved that day. Then she remembered the childhood fear of being named publicly for another person's mistake. Restraint did not erase the past. It made room for the people who still had to live with it.

當晚，律師提醒伊歐娜，動人的謎團有時會逼證人回答不理解的問題。她同意給每個人時間閱讀白話調查說明，任何人都可以拒絕。害怕再次被關注的家庭，可以只提供地點或日期，不必交出全部家史。伊歐娜對當天證實的事很少感到失望，隨即想起自己小時候害怕因別人的錯被公開點名。克制不會抹去過去，卻能為仍須活在過去影響中的人留下空間。
