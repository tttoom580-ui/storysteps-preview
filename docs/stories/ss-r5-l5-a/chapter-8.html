清水的代價

A Price for Clear Water

第十三道潮痕：鐘樓不肯說的時間 · 第 8 / 25 章 · 4000–5000 · 英中對照 · 校訂中

The council's new contractor offered an impressive device to generate instant tide forecasts. Its patent was displayed beside a large price list, and buyers were told to pay a premium for a private mode that would show water levels inside individual homes. Iona urged caution. The clarity of a display did not guarantee the clarity of its evidence. The sole undergraduate on the inspection team, Rami, noticed that the machine's interior panel lacked a manufacturing number. The vendor called checking it a dull chore. Rami replied that verifying a number was less dreadful than telling families a false hour to evacuate.

議會的新承包商展示一台看似厲害的裝置，聲稱能立即產生潮汐預測。機器專利展示在價目表旁，購買者若付較高費用，還能使用顯示個別住家水位的私人模式。伊歐娜提醒眾人謹慎：畫面清楚不代表證據清楚。檢查團隊唯一的大學生拉米發現，機器內側面板缺少製造編號。業者說核對編號只是乏味雜務。拉米回答，檢查數字總比告訴家庭錯誤撤離時間好得多。

A delegation from the northern farms came to the testing venue carrying sketches of their irrigation channels. They asked whether water had become a commodity to be sold only to people able to afford expensive forecasts. Iona said the city must dedicate public observations to public safety. Private services could exist, but an ethical warning system could not reserve accurate tide times for paying customers. The delegates shared their drawings on the condition that personal farm boundaries remained hidden. Sani used the sketches to compare the machine's output with observed channels. The first difference appeared before the demonstration officially began.

北方農場代表團帶著灌溉渠道草圖來到測試場地，問海水是否已變成只有買得起昂貴預報的人才能享用的商品。伊歐娜說，城市必須把公共觀測獻給公共安全。私人服務可以存在，但合乎倫理的警示系統不能只把正確潮汐時間留給付費者。代表同意提供草圖，條件是隱去私人農地界線。薩妮用草圖比較機器輸出與實際渠道，正式示範尚未開始，就看見第一個差異。

The vendor's salesman tried to steer the discussion toward superstition, accusing the farmers of trusting moon tales instead of science. Rami pointed out that exploration began by testing claims, not by mocking witnesses. He asked the vendor to shed the assumption that an elegant instrument could never fail. The salesman hesitated, then admitted the device had been calibrated using the same council reference point already under dispute. Iona wrote the admission on the test form and asked for an independent baseline. No one needed to decide whether the machine was fraudulent that day. They only needed to know whether it should guide evacuation before its readings were checked.

業務員試圖把討論轉向迷信，說農民相信月亮傳說而非科學。拉米指出，探索從驗證主張開始，而不是嘲笑證人。他請業者放下精緻儀器絕不會失靈的假設。業務員遲疑後承認，裝置是用已受質疑的議會基準點校準。伊歐娜把這番話記入測試表，要求使用獨立基準。當天不必判定機器是否詐欺，只需先確定它未經查核前是否適合指導撤離。

Ada asked every participant to repeat the proposed safety message in their own words. Several people spoke different dialects, and the vendor's polished language was less useful than the simple directions the farmers could translate. Rami practiced fluency by explaining a threshold without technical terms. Lera corrected him whenever he used a phrase that could be misunderstood as a guarantee. This small exercise changed the mood of the room. People no longer watched a brilliant machine in silence; they asked what a forecast meant, which measurements supported it, and what would happen if the display failed during rain.

艾達請每位參與者用自己的話重述安全訊息。現場有人說不同方言，業者流暢的宣傳話語，反而不如農民能翻譯的簡單指示有用。拉米練習用不含術語的流利說法解釋警戒門檻。蕾拉只要聽見可能被誤認為保證的詞句，就立刻糾正。這個小練習改變了全場氣氛。人們不再安靜仰望聰明機器，而是詢問預測代表什麼、有哪些測量支持，以及雨中畫面失靈時要怎麼辦。

At the end of the inspection, the contractor offered to lend the device for a month if the council agreed not to publish the calibration issue. Iona refused that condition and proposed a transparent pilot with independent observers. The contractor's engineer accepted, though the salesman protested. They sealed the settings and wrote down which numbers would be compared. No household would be charged during the trial. The farmers received copies of the schedule, and Rami was given permission to observe alongside a senior technician. Progress did not arrive as a miracle invention; it arrived as a test that both sides could repeat.

檢查結束時，承包商提議免費借用機器一個月，條件是議會不得公開校準問題。伊歐娜拒絕，改提有獨立觀察者的透明試用。工程師接受了，業務員卻抗議。眾人封存設定，寫下將比對哪些數值。試用期間不向家庭收費。農民拿到時程副本，拉米也獲准與資深技師一起觀測。進步不是以神奇發明的姿態降臨，而是從雙方都能重做的測試開始。

As evening settled, Iona inspected the old marker with Rami. He noticed an overlooked screw hole beneath the bronze plate. Its position matched a drawing of the removable frame found in the corridor. Ada's memory, the photographs, and the machine's faulty baseline now pointed to a common physical question: where had the first marker truly stood? Iona asked the archive to preserve drawings from the period before the frame was removed. She also invited Malrec's engineers to examine the hole. The next step was not to win a debate over technology. It was to find a measurement everyone could locate on the actual stone.

黃昏時，伊歐娜與拉米檢查舊標記。拉米發現青銅板下方一個被忽略的螺絲孔，位置與石廊所見可拆式測量架圖紙相符。艾達的記憶、照片與機器錯誤基線，現在都指向同一個實體問題：第一個標記究竟曾在哪裡？伊歐娜請檔案館保全測量架移除前的圖紙，也邀請馬爾雷克的工程師檢查孔洞。下一步不是在科技辯論中取勝，而是找到任何人都能在石頭上定位的測量點。
