舊螺栓的重量

The Weight of an Old Bolt

Before the independent survey could begin, Ada discovered a missing component in the clock tower: a brass bolt that should have secured the original measuring frame. Iona asked the team to accelerate the search without skipping documentation. A veteran mechanic offered a compliment to Rami for noticing the empty hole, then admitted that even experienced workers had overlooked it. The workshop's institution had kept repair records for decades, but its index was incomplete. Iona requested only the pages relevant to the frame. The inspection needed a minimal collection of dependable facts, not the entire private history of everyone who had worked there.

獨立測量尚未開始，艾達便在鐘樓發現一個缺失的零件：固定原始測量架的黃銅螺栓。伊歐娜請團隊加快搜尋，卻不能跳過紀錄。資深機械師稱讚拉米發現空孔，也承認老手們多年來都忽略了它。工坊機構保存了幾十年的維修記錄，索引卻不完整。伊歐娜只索取與測量架相關的頁面。檢查需要的是最少量而可靠的事實，而不是每個曾在此工作的人全部的私人歷史。

At the workshop, workers were preparing to deploy a portable lift. A tired apprentice showed signs of fatigue, so Tomas asked him to rest rather than climb. The repair leader looked grim but accepted the delay. They searched for the bolt inside a drawer marked “neutral parts,” where fittings from several lock systems had been mixed together. Sani warned that a similar shape did not prove an original match. The team measured threads, weight, and wear, placing each candidate in a numbered tray. Their slow method frustrated the watching councilors, yet it prevented them from fastening a convenient substitute into a historic hole and calling it proof.

工坊裡，工人正準備部署便攜式升降架。一名學徒明顯疲憊，托馬斯請他休息，不要冒險攀爬。修理隊長神色嚴肅，仍接受延誤。他們在標示「中性零件」的抽屜裡尋找螺栓，裡面混著好幾種水閘系統的配件。薩妮提醒，外形相似不代表就是原件。大家逐一測量螺紋、重量和磨耗，放入編號托盤。這種緩慢做法令旁觀議員煩躁，卻防止他們把方便的替代零件塞進古老孔洞，然後宣稱找到了證據。

A public petition asked the council to publish its earlier recommendation on land development. Iona understood the stake: if the old frame had stood farther seaward, the northern flats might never have been vacant public land. But she also understood that the difference between a broken instrument and a deliberate fraud was not trivial. She asked the archivist to examine the repair dates before making any allegation. Malrec's office objected that the new survey would undergo unnecessary delays. Ada replied that a day spent checking a bolt might spare the city years of rebuilding a wall in the wrong place.

一份公共請願要求議會公開先前的土地開發建議。伊歐娜了解其中利害：若舊測量架原本位於更靠海處，北方灘地可能從未是所謂的空閒公地。但她也知道，儀器故障與蓄意舞弊之間的差別絕非小事。她請檔案館先核對維修日期，再作指控。馬爾雷克辦公室抗議新測量會承受不必要的延遲。艾達回答，花一天檢查螺栓，可能讓城市免於花好幾年重建蓋錯位置的牆。

At midday, a storm warning made the workers' hands quiver. Tomas asked whether they should abandon the task. Iona decided to remove only the exposed tools and keep the evidence cabinet sealed. The mechanical specialty of an older engineer proved useful: he knew how the frame had once been lifted without damaging the stone. His knowledge offered a method, not permission to improvise beyond the plan. They rehearsed the movement on a wooden replica, then postponed the actual lift. The city would thrive only if its urgent work remained safe enough for people to finish it tomorrow.

中午的風暴警報讓工人雙手發抖。托馬斯問是否該放棄工作。伊歐娜決定先撤走外露工具，繼續封存證據櫃。一名老工程師的機械專長此時派上用場：他知道從前如何不傷石壁就把測量架抬起。他提供的是方法，而非超出計畫臨時施工的許可。大家先在木製模型上演練，正式吊升則延期。城市若想繁榮，急迫工作也必須安全到讓人有機會在明天完成。

In the cramped workshop, Rami found a labeled specimen of brass taken from the old tower when Ada was young. The metal's composition belonged to the realm of materials science, not political loyalty. Sani offered to compare the sample with the candidate bolt, while a clerk checked the dates on the specimen container. The comparison might support a shared source of metal, but it could not prove that a particular worker had installed the bolt. Iona asked everyone to resist the temptation to turn a technical result into an accusation. The value of their work lay in keeping those boundaries visible even when the public wanted a quicker ending.

狹窄工坊裡，拉米找到一份有標籤的黃銅樣本，是艾達年輕時從舊鐘樓取下的。金屬成分屬於材料科學的領域，不是政治忠誠的問題。薩妮願意比對樣本與候選螺栓，書記同時核對樣本容器上的日期。這項比較可能支持兩者來自同一批金屬，卻不能證明特定工人安裝了螺栓。伊歐娜請大家不要把技術結果直接變成指控。即使公眾渴望更快的結局，維持這些界線正是工作價值所在。

When the sky cleared, the archivist returned with a photograph of the original bolt beside an old measuring rule. The image was grainy, but the recorded dimensions matched one candidate closely. Iona resisted declaring victory. She asked the workshop to document the comparison in writing and allow an independent inspection. The council now had a physical possibility worth testing, the neighborhood had clearer warnings, and the sealed documents remained intact. Iona slept for the first time in days without hearing the faulty bell in her dreams. Tomorrow they would examine the frame's original height, not merely the object that had held it in place.

天空放晴後，檔案員帶回一張舊照片，原始螺栓旁放著測量尺。照片雖模糊，記錄的尺寸卻與其中一枚候選零件高度相符。伊歐娜沒有宣布勝利，而是請工坊書面記錄比對，接受獨立檢查。議會得到值得測試的實體線索，居民得到更清楚的警示，封存文件也保持完整。她終於有一晚睡覺沒再夢見故障潮鐘。明天他們要調查的是測量架原本的高度，不只是固定它的物件。
