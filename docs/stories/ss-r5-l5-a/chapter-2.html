兩種海岸線的地圖

The Map With Two Shores

第十三道潮痕：鐘樓不肯說的時間 · 第 2 / 25 章 · 4000–5000 · 英中對照

The archive occupied a building whose architecture combined a narrow lighthouse with the former customs hall. Its keeper, Vela, greeted Iona with adverse news: the newest plans had been removed for a private exhibition. An absurd explanation followed. According to the receipt, a donor had paid for the plans to be displayed in a room that had been closed for eight years. Iona requested the lending log rather than accusing the keeper. Excessive suspicion, she knew, could destroy the cooperation she needed. Vela found an older map and used a small blue ribbon to highlight a shoreline different from the official one.

檔案館所在的建築結合了狹長燈塔與舊海關大廳的建築形式。館員薇拉帶來不利消息：最新圖紙已被借去私人展覽。更荒謬的是，借閱收據記載捐助者付錢把圖紙展示在一間關閉八年的房間。伊歐娜沒有立即指責館員，而是要求查看出借紀錄。她知道過度猜疑可能毀掉必要的合作。薇拉找到一張更舊的地圖，用藍色細帶標出一條與官方版本不同的海岸線。

A visiting journalist named Nira asked to photograph the map. She had studied journalism in the northern ports, where newspapers were frequently punished for reporting unpopular facts. As an outsider she did not know the local quarrels, but that distance helped her notice the map's indigenous place names. The council's modern ideology described the northern flats as empty land. The earlier map showed fishing huts, wells, and a ceremonial path used by families long before the stone locks existed. Nira offered to publish a series of articles, but Iona asked her to distinguish what the map showed from what it could not establish.

來訪的記者妮拉想拍攝地圖。她曾在北方港口研習新聞學，知道報紙可能因報導不受歡迎的事實而遭懲罰。身為外地人，她不了解本地舊怨，反而更快注意到圖上的原住居民地名。議會的新意識形態將北方灘地稱為無人之地，舊圖卻畫著漁屋、水井和早在石閘修建前就存在的祭儀小路。妮拉想發表系列報導，伊歐娜要求她清楚區分地圖能證明與不能證明的事。

On the walk back, Tomas paused at a fish stall and bought salmon for his aunt. He stretched his shoulders while his aunt gave him a vigorous massage and complained about the heavy gates. Iona showed them a small variation in the water marks, only to discover that Tomas's aunt had recorded the same pattern for years. The old woman's notebook was not an official instrument, and it would not render a laboratory measurement unnecessary. Still, it offered dates that could be compared. Iona copied those dates with permission. She began to understand that the city's quest for precision had overlooked people who watched the sea every day.

回程時，托馬斯在魚攤買了鮭魚給姑媽。他伸展肩膀，姑媽用力替他按摩，同時抱怨沉重的水閘。伊歐娜拿出水痕的一點變化，才發現姑媽多年來記下相同規律。這本私人的筆記不能取代實驗室測量，卻能提供可供比對的日期。伊歐娜徵得同意後抄下那些日期。她開始理解，城市追求精準的過程中，竟忽略了每天看海的人。

Malrec's secretary arrived carrying a warning about prosecution for unlawful possession of municipal maps. The threat looked serious until Vela pointed to the public-access rule written on the archive door. It permitted viewing and transcription, though not the removal of originals. Iona would persist in checking the records, but she refused to rip a page from its binding or conceal its source. The secretary returned empty-handed. Nira noticed how quickly the man's confidence had disappeared once someone read the actual rule aloud. A city could survive disagreement, Iona thought, if no one person controlled the meaning of every document.

馬爾雷克的秘書帶來警告，聲稱擅自持有市政地圖可能遭到起訴。這話聽來嚴重，直到薇拉指出檔案館門上的公開閱覽規則：民眾可以閱讀和抄錄，卻不得取走原件。伊歐娜會持續查閱，也不會撕下任何一頁或隱瞞來源。秘書空手離開。妮拉注意到，一旦有人把規則原文讀出來，那人的自信便迅速消失。伊歐娜想，一座城市若不由單一人物決定每份文件的意義，就能承受不同意見。

Ada joined them at sunset with two transparent sheets. When she placed them over the old chart, the variation in the northern shoreline became clear: the entire boundary had shifted inland by the width of a market street. Yet the lighthouse foundation had not moved. The maps had been drawn with different reference points. A rough sketch alone could not explain who ordered the change, but the shift answered one practical question. Families walking according to the newer map might enter a basin that filled before the bell rang. Iona asked for an immediate second warning and invited the council to witness the next measurement.

日落時艾達帶著兩張透明薄片趕來。疊在舊圖上之後，北岸線的差異變得清楚：整條界線向內陸移動了約一條市場街的寬度，燈塔地基卻沒有改變。兩張圖使用了不同的基準點。草圖還無法證明是誰下令修改，但至少解開了一個實際問題：依新版地圖行走的家庭，可能在潮鐘響起前踏入積水的盆地。伊歐娜要求立即發布第二次警示，也邀請議會見證下一次測量。

That evening Vela apologized for first treating Iona as a possible thief. Iona replied that fear was understandable when important records were missing. They agreed on a simple protection: every future visitor could see the same index of borrowed plans, with personal addresses hidden. Nira would describe the missing maps as missing, not stolen, until a signature or witness supported the stronger claim. Outside the archive, two children argued about which colored shoreline represented home. Neither colored line was a house, Iona reminded them. The map mattered because actual people would have to live with the consequences of whichever line the city accepted.

晚上，薇拉為一開始把伊歐娜當成可能的竊賊道歉。伊歐娜說，重要紀錄失蹤時感到害怕很正常。她們同意採取簡單的保護措施：未來每位訪客都能看到相同的借閱目錄，私人地址則隱去。妮拉在沒有簽名或證人支持前，只會稱地圖「下落不明」，不會說成「遭竊」。館外兩個孩子爭論哪條彩色海岸線才是家。伊歐娜提醒他們，線條本身不是房屋，但城市選擇相信哪一條線，會真實改變人的生活。
