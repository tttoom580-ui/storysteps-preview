The Bell That Rang Late

The tide bell rang eleven minutes late on the morning Iona Wei returned to Kelshore. She had carried a batch of calibrated weights from the western workshops, expecting an ordinary inspection. Instead, the copper circuit behind the bell had been opened and carefully closed again. Her first thought was not of a breakthrough but of a decent explanation: someone might have repaired it without recording the work. Below the tower, families were already crossing the exposed flats. At the wrong hour, even a small error could send salt water rushing over the path. Iona marked the bell's position before touching anything.

伊歐娜・魏回到凱爾岸的那天早晨，潮鐘晚響了十一分鐘。她帶著從西區工坊取來的一批校準砝碼，本以為只是例行檢查，卻發現鐘後的銅製電路曾被拆開，又被仔細接回。她首先想到的並非重大突破，而是合理的解釋：或許有人維修卻忘了登記。鐘樓下方，居民已經走上退潮露出的灘地。只要時間算錯，海水就可能漫過通道。伊歐娜先標記鐘擺的位置，沒有立刻動手。

Her former mentor, Ada Sorn, arrived with a notebook wrapped in oilcloth. Ada now worked for a nonprofit that mapped safe coastal routes, and she had spent twenty years warning that the council's tide projection rested on an untested assumption. The council had approved the placement of a new bronze marker, but the marker was still missing. Iona asked whether the inquiry could begin before the next flood. Ada said the city could implement temporary warnings immediately; it did not have to wait for a perfect explanation. They agreed to place a watcher on the lower stairs and preserve the tower's maintenance book.

她從前的導師艾達・索恩帶著油布包裹的筆記本趕來。艾達如今在繪製海岸安全路線的非營利團體工作，二十年來一直提醒市議會：官方潮汐預測建立在未經驗證的假設上。議會已批准安置新的青銅標記，標記卻始終沒有送到。伊歐娜問能否在下次漲潮前展開調查。艾達說，城市可以先實施臨時警示，不必等到所有問題都有答案。她們決定安排人員守住低處階梯，同時保全鐘樓的維修簿。

At noon, Councilor Malrec demanded that the assembly dissolve its emergency committee. He called Iona an idiot for suggesting that the official schedule might be wrong. The current regime, he explained, controlled the locks by a single timetable; changing it could undermine trade. Iona answered without raising her voice. Regardless of who made the error, the stone rim of the old measuring basin carried a fresh waterline above the scheduled mark. A clerk photographed it, while two fishers independently measured the same height. The evidence did not identify an offender. It did show that the danger was not merely a rumor.

中午，議員馬爾雷克要求議會解散緊急委員會。他罵伊歐娜是笨蛋，因為她膽敢質疑官方時刻表。馬爾雷克解釋，現行制度只依一份表格控制水閘；改動它可能破壞貿易。伊歐娜沒有提高音量。她指出，不論誰犯了錯，舊測量池的石質邊緣都有一道新水線，位置高於預定刻度。一名書記拍下照片，兩位漁民也各自量出相同高度。這些證據尚不能指出犯錯的人，卻證明危險絕非空穴來風。

Tomas, a dock porter, offered his mule and saddle to carry wooden barriers down to the market road. The effort put visible strain on the old animal, so Iona borrowed two handcarts rather than asking it to haul a triple load. She refused to terrify the residents with an announcement of certain disaster. Her notice stated exactly what they knew: the bell had been late once, the high-water mark disagreed with the timetable, and people should avoid the flats until a fresh observation. Parents thanked her for the narrow warning. Merchants complained that it would reduce their sales. Both reactions mattered.

碼頭搬運工托馬斯願意借出騾子與鞍具，把木製路障運到市場道路。老騾子顯然承受很大負擔，於是伊歐娜另借了兩輛手推車，不讓牠負擔三倍重量。她不願用「災難一定會發生」嚇壞居民。公告只列已知事實：潮鐘曾晚響一次，新水痕與時刻表不符，在重新觀測前應避免走上灘地。家長感謝這份有限的警示，商人卻抱怨生意受損。兩種反應都不能被忽略。

Before dusk Iona returned to the tower with Ada and a neutral locksmith. They tightened no screws and moved no weights; their task was simply to reinforce the chain of custody around the evidence. Three signatures sealed the maintenance book inside a dry cabinet. Then Iona noticed that the chalk outline of the missing bronze marker had been drawn on the wrong side of the basin. The wall was not cracked there, and the stone had not shifted. Someone had wanted future measurements to start from a different point. She slept badly that night, thinking less about enemies than about every person who trusted the bell.

黃昏前，伊歐娜與艾達及一名中立鎖匠回到鐘樓。他們沒有鎖緊螺絲，也沒有移動砝碼，只是加強證據的保管程序。三人簽名後，將維修簿封入乾燥的櫃中。接著伊歐娜發現，失蹤青銅標記的粉筆輪廓竟畫在測量池錯誤的一側。那裡的牆面沒有裂縫，石頭也未移動。有人似乎希望未來所有測量都從另一個起點開始。那晚她睡得很差，擔心的不是敵人，而是每一個相信潮鐘的人。

On the harbor steps, a child asked why grown people needed six signatures to decide whether water was high. Iona told him that signatures did not change the sea. They allowed another person to check who had observed it and when. The child considered this and drew a second line beside the official one, careful not to make the lines touch. Iona kept the drawing. The first day's work had produced no culprit, yet it had given the neighborhood a safer route and a question that could be tested in daylight. Tomorrow she would open the old tide records, beginning with the years no one liked to discuss.

港口台階上，一個孩子問：大人為何需要六個簽名才能判斷水位高不高？伊歐娜告訴他，簽名無法改變海水，只能讓別人查證是誰在什麼時候觀察到它。孩子想了想，在官方刻線旁又畫一條線，小心不讓兩條線相碰。伊歐娜收下那張畫。第一天仍沒有查出犯錯者，卻已為街坊找到更安全的路，也提出一個可以在白天驗證的問題。明天她會打開舊潮汐紀錄，從沒有人願意談起的那些年份查起。
