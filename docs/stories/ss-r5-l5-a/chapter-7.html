The Broken Corridor

第十三道潮痕：鐘樓不肯說的時間 · 第 7 / 25 章 · 4000–5000 · 英中對照 · 校訂中

The old stone corridor under North Lock had been closed for years. Its walls held an array of carved symbols showing when the gate was rebuilt, but the last three marks had been scraped away. Their removal could arouse controversy, especially because rumors accused every successive council of corrupt dealings. Iona refused to turn a suspicion into a verdict. Sani derived the approximate age of the scrape from the exposed mineral surface, then explained that her expertise could not identify the person who made it. That distinction was indispensable to the inquiry. The team marked the corridor's entrance and called for a qualified structural inspector. Sani also warned that polluted water could infect anyone entering unprotected.

北閘下方的舊石廊已封閉多年。牆面刻著一組標誌，記錄水閘每次重建的年份，最後三道卻被刮除。這樣的移除很容易引發爭議，尤其市民傳說歷屆議會都有貪腐交易。伊歐娜拒絕把懷疑直接當成判決。薩妮根據外露礦物表面推估刮痕年代，同時說明自己的專業無法辨認刮痕是誰造成。這項區分對調查不可或缺。團隊標記石廊入口，並請來合格的結構檢查員。薩妮也提醒，受污染的水可能使沒有防護的人感染。

A faulty switch could disconnect the corridor lights from the lock's main circuit. Tomas wanted to carry a sturdy lamp inside, but the inspector warned that the old infrastructure might collapse under repeated footsteps. Instead they lowered a camera through an existing ventilation shaft. The image showed an abandoned storage niche, its floor littered with rope and a broken metal ring. On the niche wall was a symbol belonging to the former harbor league. Some citizens called that league a band of pirates; others remembered it as a rescue society. Iona wrote down both descriptions without adopting either as established history.

一個故障開關可能使石廊照明與水閘主電路斷開。托馬斯想帶堅固的提燈進去，檢查員卻警告，老舊基礎設施可能承受不了反覆踩踏。於是大家從既有通風口降下攝影機。畫面顯示一處廢棄儲物龕，地上散著繩索和破損金屬環。牆上有前港灣聯盟的標誌。有人稱聯盟是海盜組織，也有人記得他們是救難團體。伊歐娜把兩種描述都記下，沒有把任何一種當成已證實的歷史。

News of the pictures spread rapidly. A cook at the market said the old league had protected the town's cuisine during a famine, carrying grain through dangerous waters. A merchant claimed the same men had stolen ships. Both stories might contain fragments of truth. Iona asked the archive to locate the league's legal custody records and the names of their goods' original recipients. She also requested evidence of any official order for the corridor's closure. The process was slow, but worthwhile if it prevented an innocent family from being blamed for something done before they were born.

照片很快傳遍全城。一名市場廚師說，昔日聯盟曾在饑荒期間冒險運糧，保住城裡的飲食與生計；商人則聲稱同一群人偷過船。兩種故事都可能包含部分真相。伊歐娜請檔案館尋找聯盟的財物保管紀錄、物資原始收受人姓名，以及任何正式的封廊命令。過程緩慢，卻很值得，因為這可以避免無辜家庭替出生前發生的事受責備。

A tournament of local sailing teams was scheduled for the weekend, and its organizers feared that the investigation would cancel it. Fen proposed using the gathering to recruit volunteers for a safety demonstration instead. Iona approved only after the competitors agreed that participating would be optional and separate from any testimony. The sailing league provided ropes, and the market donated drinking water. Nobody would enter the broken corridor. They could demonstrate the warning lamps on the upper road and show residents how the new tide record was made. A public event could be useful without pretending a festival was an engineering test.

週末原定舉辦本地帆船隊錦標賽，主辦方擔心調查導致取消。芬建議利用聚會招募安全示範志工。伊歐娜要求先取得參賽者同意，並確保參加純屬自願、與作證無關，才接受提案。帆船聯盟提供繩索，市場捐贈飲水。沒有人會進入危險石廊，大家只在高處道路示範警示燈，向居民說明新潮汐紀錄如何製作。公開活動可以有用，卻不能把節慶假裝成工程試驗。

The images from the shaft revealed a second clue. Beneath the old harbor symbol lay a narrow row of holes that matched the spacing of the missing bronze reference marker. Ada compared the pattern with a drawing from her early apprenticeship. She had once seen a removable measuring frame attached there, long before Malrec joined the council. Its loss might explain why the official map later changed its baseline. Still, the frame's absence did not explain the fresh scratch on the tower's current marker. Iona separated the two questions in her notes, refusing to force every discrepancy into one dramatic story.

通風口影像帶來第二個線索。舊港灣標誌下方有一排細孔，間距與失蹤青銅基準標記相符。艾達把圖案與學徒時期的圖紙比對，想起自己曾看過一個可拆式測量架固定在那裡，時間早在馬爾雷克進入議會之前。測量架遺失，可能解釋後來官方地圖基線的變化，但無法解釋鐘樓現有標記上的新刮痕。伊歐娜在筆記中把兩個問題分開，不肯把每個矛盾都強行塞進同一個戲劇化故事。

By evening the inspector filed a narrow recommendation: leave the corridor closed, install a remote gauge, and inspect the outer wall after the next low tide. Iona supported it, even though a more dramatic entrance might have pleased the crowd. The public demonstration went ahead without incident. Children practiced reading the warning lamps, while adults learned where to report a blocked stair. The league's old symbol remained unexplained, but it no longer served merely as an accusation. It had become a location, a set of holes, and a question for the archive. Iona went home carrying copies of the images, not a legend.

傍晚，檢查員提出範圍明確的建議：石廊繼續封閉、設置遠端水位計，並在下次低潮檢查外牆。伊歐娜支持這個作法，雖然戲劇性闖入或許更能取悅人群。公開示範順利舉行，孩子練習看警示燈，大人學習回報被堵住的階梯。舊聯盟符號仍未有答案，卻不再只是用來指控的傳說，而是具體的位置、孔洞與檔案問題。伊歐娜帶著影像副本回家，沒有帶回一個傳奇。
