The hearing took place beneath the old customs dome, where a bronze clock hung above the public gallery. An anonymous note had accused Iona of trying to betray the harbor guild. Malrec called the note proof that her initiative lacked credibility. Iona answered that an unsigned accusation had to be tested like any other claim. The chair allowed a local entrepreneur to explain his plan for a vertical warehouse beside the tide basin. He argued that trade could sustain the city if more goods moved through one concentrated sector. The proposal sounded ambitious, but it depended on the same disputed baseline.

聽證在舊海關的圓頂下舉行，青銅時鐘懸在旁聽席上方。一張匿名紙條指控伊歐娜背叛港口行會。馬爾雷克說這證明她的倡議毫無公信力。伊歐娜回答，沒有署名的指控和其他主張一樣需要驗證。主席允許一名本地企業家說明要在潮汐測量池旁興建垂直倉庫的計畫。他認為集中運輸能支撐城市經濟。提案看似雄心勃勃，卻依賴同一個有爭議的基準點。

A butcher who supplied the harbor kitchens testified that carts had been redirected away from the northern farms. His account did not prove the council was arrogant or dishonest, but it gave dates and transport numbers that another office could verify. The attorney asked him whether the change had affected food access. He said yes, particularly after rain, and agreed to testify about the routes he had personally traveled. One councilor called the concern ridiculous because the distances were small on a map. Iona invited the councilor to walk the road with a loaded cart before judging. The witness's work involved bodies and weight, not merely lines on paper.

一名供應港口廚房的屠夫作證，說貨車曾被改道、遠離北方農場。他的說法未必能證明議會傲慢或不誠實，卻提供了其他單位可查驗的日期和運輸編號。律師問改道是否影響食物供應，他說是，尤其雨後，也同意針對自己實際行經的路線作證。一名議員覺得地圖上距離不遠，稱這種擔心荒謬。伊歐娜邀請他在下雨天親自推滿載貨車走一趟。證人面對的是身體與重量，而不只是紙上的線條。

Sani presented samples from the northern flats. Their molecules could reveal the age of salt deposits only within a range; they could not provide a precise political history. A physician in the gallery asked whether the research involved human genetics. Sani explained that it did not, and that questions about abortion or medical care were entirely outside the hearing. Iona thanked her for setting those limits plainly. Too often, public arguments borrowed scientific words as if they were decorations that made every claim stronger. A trustworthy specialist knew when to stop. The chair instructed the minutes to reflect exactly what the samples could and could not demonstrate.

薩妮展示北方灘地的樣本。分子分析可以估計鹽分沉積年代的範圍，卻不能提供精確的政治歷史。旁聽席一名醫師問研究是否涉及人類遺傳學。薩妮解釋完全沒有，也說墮胎或醫療照護議題都不在本次聽證範圍。伊歐娜感謝她把界線說清楚。公共辯論常借用科學詞彙，彷彿那些字眼能讓任何論點更有力。可信的專家知道何時該停下。主席要求會議紀錄準確呈現樣本能與不能證明的事。

The harbor's mortgage records were then read aloud. A portion of the northern flats had been promised as security for the proposed warehouse. The document was real, but its terms would take effect only if a boundary survey established the land as unused. That condition brought the altered map into sharper focus. Iona asked the clerk to integrate the mortgage's date with the map's revision history, while keeping the ownership question open. An environmental volunteer also requested protection for nearby wildlife. The chair accepted that concern as relevant to planning, not as proof of anyone's crime.

接著宣讀港口抵押紀錄。北方灘地一部分被列為預定倉庫的擔保品。文件是真的，但條款須等界線測量確認土地無人使用才會生效。這項條件讓遭改動的地圖更加重要。伊歐娜請書記把抵押日期與地圖修訂歷史併列核對，暫不判定所有權。同時，環境志工要求保護附近野生動物。主席接受這是規劃必須考慮的事項，而不是任何人犯罪的證據。

During the recess, the entrepreneur approached Iona and confessed that he had assumed the council map was reliable. He offered to fund a new measurement if the method and results would be public. Iona accepted the idea only on the condition that independent observers selected the instruments. He agreed. Malrec objected, saying the delay threatened hundreds of jobs. Iona replied that buildings founded on an uncertain waterline threatened jobs and lives alike. The gallery did not erupt in applause. People were tired and concerned about their wages. For once, the debate ended with an actual plan rather than a winning speech.

休庭時，那名企業家走向伊歐娜，承認自己一直以為議會地圖可靠。他提議出資重新測量，只要方法和結果公開即可。伊歐娜同意討論，條件是由獨立觀察員挑選儀器。企業家接受了。馬爾雷克反對，說延誤將威脅數百份工作。伊歐娜回答，建立在不確定水線上的建築同樣威脅工作和生命。旁聽席沒有爆出掌聲。人們疲倦，也擔心薪水。這次辯論終於以實際計畫結束，而不只是誰說贏了。

Five days after the first market hearing, the city formally recognized a limited discrepancy between its reference mark and physical observations. It did not yet name a responsible person. Iona asked for the independent survey to begin before any sale or construction at North Lock. The chair approved a temporary suspension of the land transaction, subject to a review date. At home, Ada put the old metal tag beside the child's drawing with two separate lines. The tag's holes and the child's imagined mark were not evidence of the same kind. Still, they reminded Iona why the argument mattered: a shoreline was not an abstract possession. It was where people lived.

第一次市場聽證五天後，城市正式承認官方基準標記與實地觀測存在有限差異，但尚未指認責任人。伊歐娜要求，在北閘土地出售或施工前先進行獨立測量。主席同意暫停交易，並訂出複審日期。回家後，艾達把舊金屬標籤放在孩子畫的兩條線旁。標籤孔洞與孩子想像的刻痕不是同一類證據，卻都提醒伊歐娜，這場爭論為何重要：海岸線不只是抽象財產，而是真實有人生活的地方。
