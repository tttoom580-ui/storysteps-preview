A row of homes stood beside the old freight driveway where traders loaded their wagons. Residents had complained of boredom during ordinary council sessions; now the prospect of losing their housing made every meeting urgent. A young speaker named Lera was more eloquent than most officials, but she refused to turn despair into a performance. She asked for one civic guideline that people could follow that afternoon. Iona suggested marking a safe upper route with washable paint, then asking residents to check its steps, slopes, and resting places before calling it complete. The proposal met silence, followed by several practical questions.

舊貨運車道旁有一整排住家，商人常在那裡裝貨。居民平日覺得議會會議無聊，如今面臨失去住房的可能，每場會議都變得迫切。年輕發言人蕾拉比多數官員更善於表達，卻不願把絕望當成表演。她只要求一條當天下午就能實行的公民安全準則。伊歐娜提議以可洗掉的顏料標記高處安全路線，請居民實地檢查階梯、坡度與休息點，再決定是否完成。現場先是沉默，接著有人提出許多實際問題。

The route team included an elderly man who needed medication, a child who became sick on steep paths, and a carter whose windshield was scratched so badly that he could hardly see through it. Iona insisted that an innovative design was worthless if it excluded them. Tomas used a tin lantern to mark a resting platform. Lera asked whether a longer route would prolong the journey beyond the warning time. They walked it twice and noted every delay. The experiment was simple and sometimes embarrassing, but the results were noticeable: a supposedly safe stairway had no rail and became slippery when wet.

路線小隊有一名需服藥的長者、一名走陡坡會不舒服的孩子，以及一名擋風玻璃刮痕嚴重、幾乎看不清前方的車夫。伊歐娜堅持，若把他們排除，創新設計就沒有價值。托馬斯用錫製提燈標出休息平台。蕾拉問改走長路是否會延長行程，超過警報時間。大家走了兩次，記下每次耽擱。實驗簡單得有些令人難為情，成果卻很明顯：一段號稱安全的樓梯沒有扶手，下雨還會濕滑。

During the second trial the child began to vomit, and the team stopped without argument. Her mother pleaded for a shorter path and worried that the tests would intensify pressure on families already frightened by the bell. Iona agreed to suspend the trial and bring a physician to review the physical risks. No one treated the child's suffering as a useful demonstration. A volunteer offered water and a quiet room. When the child recovered, her mother described a sheltered alley that did not appear on the council plan. It was not an official road, but it might become a safer alternative after inspection.

第二次試走時，那名孩子開始嘔吐，大家立刻停下。母親懇求找到較短的路，也擔心測試會加重早已被潮鐘嚇壞的家庭壓力。伊歐娜同意暫停，並請醫師評估身體風險。沒有人把孩子的痛苦當成有用的示範。志工送上水與安靜房間。孩子恢復後，母親提到一條議會地圖上沒有的遮蔭小巷。那不是正式道路，經過檢查後卻可能成為更安全的替代路線。

Malrec accused Iona of trying to stimulate public fear for political advantage. Lera answered that his statement confused caution with panic and ignored the morality of sending people along a route that had never been tested. The argument grew sharp, but Iona broke it with a question: would the council supply two engineers for the alley inspection? Malrec reluctantly agreed. Tomas offered to carry the equipment. Their practical compromise did not settle who had altered the tide records, yet it prevented that mystery from swallowing the urgent need for an accessible route. The meeting ended with a timetable that named actual tasks and responsible people.

馬爾雷克指責伊歐娜企圖刺激大眾恐懼以獲取政治利益。蕾拉回答，他把謹慎混同恐慌，也忽略了讓人走上未經測試路線的道德問題。爭論越來越尖銳，伊歐娜卻只問一件事：議會是否願意派兩名工程師檢查小巷？馬爾雷克勉強同意。托馬斯則負責搬運設備。這個務實妥協還不能解開潮汐紀錄遭竄改的謎，卻避免讓謎團吞掉當下需要一條無障礙通路的急務。會議最後列出具體工作與負責人。

Ada later told Iona that patience was a trait she had learned too slowly. “Some people will always mistake it for weakness,” she said. Iona answered that a city could not be measured only by how loudly its leaders argued. She spent the evening reviewing the path's turning points with the residents, who corrected three names and marked one private gate as unavailable. The revisions made the plan less impressive but more trustworthy. Before bed she returned to the tower drawing and traced the missing bronze marker's outline again. Its placement still pointed toward the road Malrec wanted closed permanently.

艾達後來告訴伊歐娜，耐心是她花了太久才學會的性格特質。「總有人把耐心誤認為軟弱。」伊歐娜回答，衡量一座城市不能只看領袖爭得多大聲。當晚她與居民一起核對路線轉角，修正了三處地名，也標出一扇不能通行的私人鐵門。修訂後的圖看起來不再那麼漂亮，卻更可信。睡前她再次描繪鐘樓圖上失蹤的青銅標記輪廓；它的位置依舊指向馬爾雷克希望永久封閉的道路。

The next morning the route passed its first careful inspection. The sheltered alley required a handrail and permission from two property owners, so no one declared it open yet. Lera prepared a public notice in plain language rather than a triumphant announcement. People could use the temporary upper road immediately, and they would know exactly why the shortcut remained closed. Iona considered that a small victory: the residents had not merely been informed of a decision; they had helped make it. In the margin of her notebook she wrote the date beside a new clue: the proposed road closure and the changed tide reference appeared in the same council budget.

隔天早晨，這條路通過第一輪審慎檢查。遮蔭小巷還需要裝設扶手、取得兩位地主同意，因此沒有人宣布它立即開放。蕾拉擬出平實的公告，沒有寫成勝利宣言。居民可以立刻使用臨時高處道路，也清楚知道近路為何暫時封閉。伊歐娜認為這是小小勝利：居民不只是被告知決定，而是真正參與了決定。她在筆記邊欄寫下日期和新線索：道路封閉提案與潮汐基準變更，竟出現在同一份議會預算中。
