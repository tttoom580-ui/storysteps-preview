麻繩測量線

A Measuring Line of Rope

第十三道潮痕：鐘樓不肯說的時間 · 第 3 / 25 章 · 4000–5000 · 英中對照

At dawn the council's executive surveyor attempted to stop the independent observation. His aggression made the quay workers retreat, but an attorney hired by the fisher families read the public measurement rule aloud. The beloved harbor had never been exempt from inspection. Iona kept a cautious distance and asked the surveyor to name the exact objection. He said the river's biological deposits could distort the stone marks. That was possible. Iona proposed taking samples from each layer rather than assuming the accusation settled the matter. The gathering watched him sign the sampling sheet, bound by the same procedure as everyone else.

清晨，議會的測量主管試圖阻止獨立觀測。他的攻擊性態度讓碼頭工人退開，但漁民家庭聘請的律師當眾讀出公開測量規則。這座眾人珍愛的港口從來不曾豁免檢查。伊歐娜保持謹慎距離，要求主管說清反對理由。他認為河流帶來的生物沉積物可能扭曲石刻。這確實有可能，因此伊歐娜提議逐層採樣，而非把指控當作結論。眾人看著主管在採樣單上簽字，和其他人一樣受程序約束。

Sani, a coastal ecologist, had come by shuttle from the southern marsh. Her specialty was the organisms that lived between stones, not the operation of clockwork. She had chosen to specialize in coastal life rather than machinery. She explained the scope of her findings carefully: a sample could reveal recent deposits, but not the exact hour of every past tide. She put each specimen in a separate jar and used a strap to secure the wooden case. Tomas helped her straighten the measuring rope across the basin. Every tick of Ada's pocket clock was written beside a measurement, creating a sequence another team could repeat. For Iona, this modest realism was more valuable than a dramatic speech.

海岸生態學家薩妮搭乘接駁船從南方沼澤來到現場。她專精的是石縫間的生物，不是鐘錶機械。她選擇專門研究海岸生物，而非機械。她謹慎說明研究範圍：樣本能顯示近期沉積，卻不能指出每一次舊潮水的確切時間。她把各份樣本分裝入瓶，並用綁帶固定木箱。托馬斯協助拉直橫過測量池的繩索。艾達懷錶的每一聲滴答，都配上一筆讀數，讓其他隊伍可以重做實驗。對伊歐娜而言，這種務實的態度比戲劇性的演說更有價值。

Malrec proposed a virtual demonstration using painted boards to represent the highest waterline. From his viewpoint the plan would save time and protect trade, and several merchants agreed. Sani refused to treat a painted board as a substitute for observation. “A model is useful when we know its limits,” she said. The surveyor gave a helpless shrug, then admitted that the council's old instrument had not been calibrated that morning. Iona did not make fun of him. She asked him to record the missing calibration beside the other measurements. One honest gap could diminish the danger of a false certainty.

馬爾雷克提議以彩繪木板進行虛擬示範，代表最高水位。他認為這樣可以節省時間、保護商業活動，不少商人同意。薩妮拒絕把彩繪板當成實際觀測的替代品。「知道限制，模型才有用，」她說。測量主管無奈地聳肩，終於承認議會舊儀器當天早上沒有校準。伊歐娜沒有嘲笑他，只請他把缺少校準的事實記在其他數值旁。一個誠實承認的缺口，反而能降低虛假確信造成的危險。

A boy slipped near the quay, and Tomas caught him before he fell into the basin. Someone called the boy prey for a careless wave, but Iona disliked the word; the sea did not hunt children. Workers tied a temporary guard rope while Sani checked the stones. A delivery boat was late, and the boy's mother worried about missing her shift. Iona allowed her to leave after taking her contact information for the witness list. Nobody should be forced to sacrifice a day's wages merely to make a committee look complete. Their new rules had to protect the people they asked for help.

一名男孩在碼頭邊滑了一跤，托馬斯及時拉住他，沒讓他跌入測量池。有人說孩子成了粗心浪花的獵物，伊歐娜不喜歡這個說法：海水並不會刻意捕獵孩子。工人綁起臨時護繩，薩妮則檢查石面。一艘運貨船晚到，男孩母親擔心上班遲到。伊歐娜記下聯絡方式後便讓她離開。沒有人應該為了讓委員會看起來完整而犧牲一天薪水；新規則必須先保護願意幫忙的人。

By late afternoon, the independent reading exceeded the official forecast by a clear margin. Sani placed the collected jars beside the measuring rope and signed only the statement her field could support. Ada signed for the clock, Tomas for the rope, and the council surveyor for the instrument's missing calibration. Their conclusions were different, but their observations could be assembled without pretending everyone agreed. Iona copied the results into two books and left one in the archive. The other traveled with the neighborhood safety team. When the evening bell sounded, it was seven minutes behind the observed change of the current.

午後，獨立讀數明顯高於官方預報。薩妮把樣本瓶排在繩索旁，只簽下自己專業範圍能支持的結論。艾達為時鐘簽名，托馬斯為繩索簽名，議會測量員則註明儀器未校準。各人判斷不同，觀測資料仍可以彙整，不必假裝所有人已經同意。伊歐娜抄成兩本紀錄，一本存入檔案館，一本交給社區安全隊。晚鐘響起時，已比實際水流變化慢了七分鐘。

The reading did not reveal who had moved the reference line. It gave the neighborhood something more immediate: a reason to close the lower walkway two bells earlier than the council schedule. Iona announced that recommendation alongside the uncertainty in her methods. Malrec promised to contest it at a full hearing. She welcomed the hearing, provided that everyone could examine the rope measurements before speeches began. A child carried her written notice to the market, holding the paper high so it would not touch the puddles. She watched him go and decided that the next step would be checking who could enter the clock tower after dark.

讀數仍無法指出誰移動了基準線，卻讓街坊得到更立即的成果：有理由在官方時程前兩次鐘聲就關閉低窪步道。伊歐娜公布建議，也同時說明方法中的不確定處。馬爾雷克承諾在完整聽證會上反對她。她歡迎聽證，只要求所有人能先看測量資料，再聽演說。一個孩子把通知拿去市場，高高舉著紙張，不讓它碰到積水。伊歐娜目送他離開，決定下一步調查誰能在夜間進入鐘樓。
