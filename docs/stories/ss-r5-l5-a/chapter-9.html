The Night Without Stars

A fierce storm arrived during the night expedition to the outer seawall. Iona's ally Nira traveled with the observers, carrying an inventory manifest and a lantern. The sky looked like a dark galaxy with its stars hidden, while the harbor lights formed a jagged line along the water. A watchman reported an assault near the old cemetery and urged everyone to turn back. Iona would not allow the report to distract the team from immediate safety, but neither would she ignore it. They contacted the harbor guards and stayed together on the raised path until the area was cleared.

夜間前往外海堤的勘查途中，猛烈風暴襲來。伊歐娜的盟友妮拉跟著觀察隊，帶著貨物清單和提燈。天空像被遮住星辰的黑暗銀河，港口燈火沿水面形成鋸齒般的線。一名守夜員通報舊墓園附近發生襲擊，請所有人折返。伊歐娜沒有讓消息分散隊伍對立即安全的注意，也沒有忽略它。他們聯絡港衛，在確認區域安全前一起留在高處通道。

At a sheltered lounge in the harbor office, a clerk made an urgent bid to recover several missing metal tags. They had been kept in a locked cabinet beside rolls of mint-colored signal cloth. The cabinet's exterior was intact, but the rear hinge had been loosened. The premier harbor officer wanted to describe the loss as theft immediately. Nira asked whether an inventory had been completed. “Not yet,” he conceded. Iona requested a joint count, with the guards watching but not directing the result. A missing tag might be important to the marker puzzle; a careless accusation might do more harm.

在港務辦公室的避風休息室，一名書記急著尋回幾枚失蹤的金屬標籤。它們原本放在上鎖櫃中，旁邊是薄荷綠色的信號布。櫃子外側完整，後方鉸鏈卻被鬆開。首席港衛官想立刻稱之為竊盜。妮拉問是否已盤點，他承認尚未完成。伊歐娜要求聯合清點，由警衛旁觀而不主導結果。失蹤標籤或許與基準標記有關，但草率指控也可能造成更大傷害。

Outside, a young porter tried to snatch a dry cloak from the supply rack. Tomas stopped him and learned that the boy had been sleeping in a squat behind the fish market. The place had begun to stink after seawater filled its drains, and the boy feared losing even his blanket. Iona did not excuse the attempted theft, but she asked the shelter volunteers to offer a safe bed before questioning him further. The boy spoke of a man carrying metal plates toward the northern road. He could not identify the man's face, only a coat with a silver clasp. Iona recorded the limits of his statement.

戶外，一名年輕搬運工伸手想搶走物資架上的乾斗篷。托馬斯攔住他，才知道男孩一直睡在魚市後方的非法佔用空屋。海水灌進排水道後，那裡開始發臭，男孩連唯一的毯子都怕失去。伊歐娜沒有替搶奪辯解，卻先請收容志工給他安全床位，再慢慢詢問。他說看過一名男子抱著金屬板往北路走去；認不出臉，只記得外套有銀色扣環。伊歐娜記下證詞的限制。

A market cook brought warm tuna broth to the night workers. Over the meal, people discussed whether citizenship gave them any right to see the harbor's safety records. Iona said the tide warnings were public information, regardless of a person's papers, while private witness statements required consent. The clerk agreed to post an accessible inventory after addresses were removed. Some guards wanted compulsory questioning of every porter; the attorney warned that such a measure would frighten useful witnesses away. The group settled on voluntary interviews with a clear record of who asked each question and how the answers were stored.

一名市場廚師送來熱鮪魚湯，讓夜班人員暖身。用餐時，有人討論公民身分是否才能查看港口安全紀錄。伊歐娜說，潮汐警報屬公共資訊，不應只提供給有證件的人，但私人證詞仍須取得同意。書記答應遮去地址後張貼可供查閱的盤點表。部分警衛主張強制盤問每名搬運工，律師提醒，這反而可能嚇跑有用的證人。眾人最後決定採自願訪談，清楚記錄詢問者與答案保管方式。

At dawn, the missing tags were found in a crate marked for an old signal-tower repair. The crate had not been stolen; it had been moved without a transfer note. Yet one tag was unlike the others. Its holes matched the pattern from the sealed corridor, and the date stamped on its back preceded the current council map by thirty years. Iona embraced Tomas briefly, then caught herself celebrating too soon. A tag could show what had been manufactured, not where it had actually been installed. They carried it to Vela for cataloging and arranged a public comparison with the old drawing.

黎明時，失蹤標籤在一只標明供舊信號塔維修使用的箱子裡找到。箱子並未遭竊，只是移動時沒有填轉交單。但其中一枚標籤與其他不同：孔洞與封閉石廊圖案相符，背面日期比議會現有地圖早三十年。伊歐娜短暫擁抱托馬斯，又提醒自己別太早慶祝。標籤只能證明物件曾被製造，未必證明裝在哪裡。他們把標籤交給薇拉建檔，安排與舊圖公開比對。

The night's confusion taught Iona a hard lesson. Rumors of assault, a missing crate, and a frightened boy had briefly seemed like pieces of one plot. They were three different matters, each requiring its own evidence. At the next briefing she named what was verified and what remained uncertain. The watchman still deserved a careful inquiry into the reported attack. The porter deserved safe lodging and a fair interview. And the metal tag deserved a proper examination. Keeping those stories separate did not weaken the larger investigation. It prevented a striking coincidence from becoming a false explanation.

那一夜的混亂讓伊歐娜學到困難的一課。襲擊傳言、失蹤箱子與受驚男孩，一度看似同一陰謀的線索，其實是三件需要各自證據的事。下一次簡報，她清楚分別說明已查證與尚未知曉的內容。守夜員通報的襲擊仍值得查明；搬運工需要安全住處與公平訪談；金屬標籤則需要正式檢驗。把故事分開並未削弱整體調查，反而避免吸引人的巧合變成錯誤解釋。
