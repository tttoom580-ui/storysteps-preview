下雨天的訪客

The Rainy Visitor

貓咪咖啡館的祕密客人 · 第 1 / 25 章 · 1000以下 · 英中對照 · 校訂中

Aya began her first shift at Rosa's cat cafe just as a heavy rain starts. The rain made the window a little blurry, but it did not hide the quiet life inside the cafe. Cups touched plates, a chair moved, and someone laughed over a small mistake. Aya noticed how carefully Rosa watched the space beneath the tables. A cat could fit where a person could not, and that meant a dropped fork or a loose bag might become a danger. The cafe was not just a place for cute photographs. It was a place where someone had to keep choosing kindness in practical, sometimes unexciting ways. A tiny bug crossed the dry edge of the window. The camera on the counter stayed off until permission was given. One fact mattered: the group had to check before judging. Rosa offered a friendly smile instead of a quick judgment. Aya wondered what a small choice today might mean for the future.

小雅在蘿莎的貓咪咖啡館開始第一天工作，外面剛好下起大雨。 雨讓窗玻璃有些模糊，卻遮不住店裡安靜的生活。杯盤碰出輕響，椅子移動，有人為一個小錯笑了。小雅注意蘿莎總是仔細看桌下。貓能鑽進人過不去的地方，因此掉落的叉子或鬆開的袋子都可能危險。咖啡館不只是拍可愛照片的地方，也需要有人用實際、有時不那麼刺激的方式持續選擇善意。 一隻小蟲爬過窗邊乾燥的角落。櫃檯上的相機保持關閉，等待取得拍攝同意。有一件事實很重要：大家必須查證後再判斷。蘿莎給了個親切的微笑，沒有急著評斷。小雅想知道今天的小選擇會如何影響未來。

A black-and-white cat walked in with a dry blue ribbon tied loosely around its collar. The information was small enough to overlook. Aya nearly moved on, but Rosa wanted to know what it meant for the people involved. They talked through two possibilities, then checked which parts could actually be supported. In the cafe, other things were happening at the same time; it would have been easy to mix them together. 'Let's keep these separate,' Aya said. One careful question led to another, and the situation became clearer without anyone needing to raise a voice or make a dramatic accusation. A glass of juice waited at the side of the table. Nobody wanted a lie to take the place of a clear answer. The middle of the page was left blank for a new idea. A little arrow on the map pointed north. Aya read the notice by the door before making a plan.

一隻黑白相間的貓走進門，項圈上鬆鬆繫著乾燥的藍絲帶。 這項資訊很小，幾乎會被忽略。小雅正想往下走，蘿莎卻想知道它對相關的人代表什麼。他們討論兩種可能，再確認哪些有證據支持。咖啡館同時還發生其他事，很容易混淆。「先把它們分開。」小雅說。仔細的一問接著一問，不必大聲說話或戲劇性指控，情況也漸漸清楚。 桌子一側放著一杯果汁。沒有人希望謊言取代清楚的答案。頁面的中間留白，準備寫下新點子。地圖上的小箭頭指向北方。小雅先讀過門旁公告，才開始做計畫。

Rosa checked that the cat was safe and placed water near the warm window. The next step sounded small when Aya said it aloud. That was a good sign. A clear action was easier to try than a large promise that no one understood. Rosa repeated the plan to make sure both had heard it in the same way. When one part sounded unfair, they changed the wording. The plan left space for a person to ask a question or take a break. Aya noticed a quiet sense of relief. The problem remained real, but it no longer needed to be faced through shouting, rushing, or guessing about another person's feelings. The other chair stayed free for anyone who needed it. A picture of a pet sleeping safely hung near the entrance. Someone mentioned a picnic planned for the next free afternoon. "We can reach that corner if we move carefully," Aya said. A red mark showed the part of the page still unfinished.

蘿莎確認貓咪安全後，在溫暖的窗邊放了一碗水。 小雅把下一步說出口時，聽起來只是一件小事。這反而是好現象。明確行動比大家聽不懂的大承諾更容易嘗試。蘿莎重複計畫，確認兩人理解相同。有一部分聽來不公平，他們便改了說法。計畫也讓人能提問和休息。小雅感到些許輕鬆。問題依然存在，卻不必靠吵鬧、急躁或猜測別人的心思來面對。 另一張椅子留給有需要的人。入口附近掛著寵物安心睡覺的圖片。有人提到下一個空閒下午的野餐計畫。「只要小心移動，我們就能到那個角落。」小雅說。紅色記號標出頁面尚未完成的部分。

The cat dropped a sealed paper on the table, looked at Aya, and meowed twice. When the room grew quiet, Rosa turned down the lights near the window and left the message shelf in order. Aya thought of all the stories guests had brought through the door. Some ended with laughter; others needed more than one meeting. A cat might have helped somebody notice a letter, but the hard work of talking and forgiving still belonged to the people involved. Aya was beginning to understand why Rosa cared about keeping the room welcoming even when old feelings made that difficult. A small star sticker marked the page they wanted to keep. They checked the first step and then moved to the second. "This is what we can check," Aya explained. "Let us use the notes we already have," Rosa suggested. A woman near the door asked a quiet question.

貓把一張封好的紙放到桌上，看著小雅喵了兩聲。 店裡安靜下來時，蘿莎調暗窗邊燈光，把留言架整理好。小雅想起客人帶進店裡的各種故事，有的以笑聲結束，有的需要不只一次見面。貓也許幫人注意到某封信，但交談、理解和原諒這些困難工作，仍需要當事人自己完成。小雅逐漸明白，為什麼蘿莎即使心裡有舊傷，仍努力讓這間店保持溫暖。 他們想保存的那一頁貼著小星星貼紙。他們查好第一步，然後進入第二步。「這就是我們能查證的部分。」小雅說明。「先用手邊已有的筆記吧。」蘿莎提議。門邊一位女士輕聲提出問題。

The notebook was becoming thicker with small observations. Aya had once imagined that a good story needed one dramatic moment to make everything clear. Real days did not behave that way. One small choice led to another, and people sometimes changed their minds for good reasons. Rosa helped put today's paper in the right place. They could return to it later and see exactly why they had acted as they did. That mattered more than making the day sound exciting to someone who had not been there.

筆記本裡的小觀察越來越厚。小雅以前以為好故事需要一個戲劇性瞬間讓一切清楚，真實生活卻不是這樣。一個小選擇連著下一個選擇，人也可能因合理理由改變心意。蘿莎幫忙把今天的紙收在正確位置。日後再翻開，就能知道當初為什麼那樣行動。這比向沒到場的人說得刺激更重要。

When Aya turned the last page of the notes, one line stood out: people deserve the chance to explain their own choices. It was easy to forget that in a busy day, especially when everyone wanted a quick ending. Rosa had reminded Aya more than once to keep an open mind. The lesson was not that people should wait forever. It was that acting with care usually made the next action safer and fairer. Aya closed the book and made a quiet plan to remember that tomorrow.

小雅翻到筆記最後一頁，看見一行話特別醒目：每個人都應有機會解釋自己的選擇。忙碌時很容易忘記，尤其人人都想快點結束。蘿莎不只一次提醒小雅保持開放心態。這不是要大家永遠等待，而是說仔細行動通常會讓下一步更安全、更公平。小雅合上本子，默默決定明天也要記住。
