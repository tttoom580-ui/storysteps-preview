After the busy part ended, Aya and Rosa found a quiet minute. There were still things they wanted to say, yet neither felt a need to win the conversation. Aya asked what had been most difficult. Rosa answered honestly and then asked the same question back. They were surprised to hear two different answers. That did not make either one wrong. The talk helped them see that working together did not mean thinking exactly alike. It meant taking those differences seriously enough to make a plan both could understand.

忙碌告一段落後，小雅和蘿莎有了片刻安靜。他們還有話想說，卻不必在對話中爭贏。小雅問最困難的是哪部分。蘿莎誠實回答，也反問同一個問題。兩人的答案竟然不同，卻不代表誰錯了。這場談話讓他們理解，合作不必想法完全一致，而是認真看待差異，做出雙方都能理解的計畫。

The small objects from the day were back in their places. The important words were written down, and the people involved had heard the result. Aya could not know every part of the future, but there was comfort in knowing what to do first: ask, check, and listen. Rosa said goodbye with a tired smile. Aya returned the smile. Somewhere beyond the familiar cafe, another ordinary day was waiting. It would bring its own surprises, but they did not have to meet them without friends or without a plan.

今天的小物件都回到了原位。重要的話已寫好，相關的人也知道結果。小雅不可能知道未來每件事，卻因知道第一步該怎麼做而安心：發問、查證、傾聽。蘿莎疲憊地笑著道別，小雅也回以微笑。在熟悉的咖啡館之外，又有平凡的一天等待著，會帶來新的驚喜，但他們不必獨自、毫無計畫地面對。
