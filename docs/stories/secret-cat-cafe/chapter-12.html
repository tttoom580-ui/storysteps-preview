A woman in a green coat arrived to collect the yellow umbrella. On rainy days the street outside the cafe turned silver. Umbrellas passed the window like moving flowers, and people entered with wet sleeves and tired faces. Aya was learning to notice both the human visitors and the animals who sometimes came by. Rosa told her that a funny cat story could be shared without turning the cat into a performer. They checked the floor for puddles and kept the door area clear. Every guest had a place to sit, and every visiting animal needed a way to leave if it became afraid. Someone had asked Rosa a question about actor. The class reading card used afternoon in an example sentence. Aya remembered a drawing of band seen earlier that week. "We can celebrate when the others are ready," Aya suggested. A notice near the entrance mentioned corner.

一名穿綠外套的女士走進來領黃傘。 雨天咖啡館外的街道變得銀亮，雨傘像移動的花朵經過窗前，客人帶著濕袖子和疲憊表情進門。小雅學著同時注意人類訪客和偶爾來訪的動物。蘿莎說有趣的貓故事可以分享，卻不應把貓變成表演者。他們檢查地面積水，保持門口通暢。每位客人都有位子，每隻來訪動物若害怕也應能自由離開。 有人曾問蘿莎關於演員的問題。課堂閱讀卡用下午造了一個例句。小雅想起本週稍早看到的樂團圖畫。「等其他人準備好，我們就能慶祝。」小雅提議。入口附近的告示提到轉角。

Rosa froze because the woman was her sister Lily, visiting town on business. For several seconds, nobody spoke. Aya was thinking about the new fact and the way it touched earlier choices. Rosa drew a short line under the important part of the notes. They agreed that a record was useful only if it was clear enough to correct. If a number, name, or time was uncertain, they marked it as uncertain. That extra care took a little longer, but it protected people who were not in the room to explain themselves. At last, Aya could see a practical question worth asking next. "We can die when the others are ready," Aya suggested. The class reading card used glasses in an example sentence. Aya said the word I in a careful answer. A small poster on the wall included the word key. A notice near the entrance mentioned last.

蘿莎愣住了，對方正是因公回鎮上的姊姊莉莉。 好幾秒沒有人開口。小雅思考新事實與先前選擇的關係。蘿莎在筆記重要處畫了短線。他們同意紀錄要清楚，才方便日後更正。數字、姓名或時間若不確定，就標明不確定。這樣雖然多花時間，卻能保護不在場、無法替自己說話的人。最後小雅終於想到下一個值得問的實際問題。 「等其他人準備好，我們就能枯死。」小雅提議。課堂閱讀卡用眼鏡造了一個例句。小雅仔細回答時說出了「我」。牆上的小海報寫著鑰匙這個字。入口附近的告示提到最後的。

Aya steps away and gave the sisters room to decide whether to speak. Making a choice was harder than making a wish. Aya could imagine a quick solution, but a real plan needed to fit the time, the place, and the people taking part. Rosa listened while Aya explained the steps aloud. They stopped at one unclear part, changed it, and started again. 'If somebody says no, we need another way,' Aya said. Rosa agreed that helping was not the same as deciding for others. Together they wrote a short plan anyone involved could question, improve, or leave if it was not right for them. A small poster on the wall included the word media. They needed more time to check the last note. At three o’clock, the hall grew quiet. They could stop or try again after a short rest. Someone had asked Rosa a question about package.

小雅退開，讓姊妹自己決定要不要交談。 做選擇比許願難。小雅可以想像快速解法，但真正的計畫必須配合時間、地點與參與者。蘿莎聽小雅逐步說明，在不清楚的地方停下修改，再重新開始。「如果有人說不，我們要有其他辦法。」小雅說。蘿莎同意，幫忙不等於替別人做決定。他們一起寫下簡明計畫，讓相關的人都能提問、改善或選擇退出。 牆上的小海報寫著媒體這個字。他們需要更多時間核對最後一張紙。三點整時，大廳安靜下來。他們可以停止，或休息一下再試。有人曾問蘿莎關於包裹的問題。

Pepper meowed at both of them, and Lily said she had missed the cafe's smell. Before closing, Aya wiped the tables and checked that the water bowl was clean. Rosa counted the message cards waiting for the people who had chosen to receive them. Neither task was grand enough for a newspaper story, yet both helped the cafe feel safe. Outside, rain rolled down the window in clear lines. Aya wondered what the cat would do on a sunny day, when there was no need to seek shelter. Whatever the answer, the little visitor would still have a choice, and so would the people whose notes were kept inside. Someone had asked Rosa a question about play. The day felt poor to Aya, so Rosa asked what was wrong. A notice near the entrance mentioned supermarket. The open book contained a short story about ticket. "What does tired mean in this note?" Aya asked.

胡椒對兩人喵叫，莉莉說她很懷念店裡的味道。 打烊前，小雅擦桌子、確認水碗乾淨。蘿莎點算等著交給自願收件者的留言卡。兩件事都不夠轟動，卻都讓咖啡館更安全。窗外雨水沿玻璃流下。小雅想知道晴天時貓咪會做什麼，因為那時不用來躲雨。不論答案如何，小訪客仍有自己的選擇，留在店裡的留言收件人也是如此。 有人曾問蘿莎關於演奏的問題。小雅覺得今天有些不足的，蘿莎便問怎麼了。入口附近的告示提到超市。翻開的書有一則談到門票的小故事。「這張筆記裡的「疲倦的」是什麼意思？」小雅問。

Before leaving, Aya gave the day one last careful look. It had contained a surprise, a question, a decision, and a result. Those parts did not always arrive in the order people wanted. Rosa asked whether they were ready for tomorrow. 'Ready to listen,' Aya answered, 'even if tomorrow changes the plan.' The reply was small, but it was true. They put away the things they had used, thanked the people nearby, and made sure nobody was left expecting a promise that had never been agreed to.

離開前，小雅最後仔細回顧這一天：有驚喜、問題、決定，也有結果。事情不一定照人希望的順序發生。蘿莎問明天準備好了嗎？「準備好傾聽了，」小雅回答，「即使明天又要改計畫。」這句話很簡單，卻是真的。他們收拾用品、謝謝附近的人，也確認沒有讓誰期待一個從未答應的承諾。

That evening, Aya tried to describe the day in just two sentences. The first sentence was about the problem. The second was about what people did together. Both mattered, and neither would have made sense alone. Aya left space beneath them for another note tomorrow. Rosa sent a short reminder to rest. Aya smiled at it. A person could care deeply about a goal and still eat dinner, talk with family, and sleep. Whatever happened next, those ordinary things were part of the answer too.

那天晚上，小雅試著用兩句話描述今天。第一句是問題，第二句是大家一起做的事。兩句都重要，單獨看卻不完整。小雅在下面留空，準備明天再記。蘿莎傳來簡短提醒，要記得休息。小雅笑了。人可以非常在意目標，同時仍要吃晚餐、陪家人說話、好好睡覺。不論接下來如何，這些平常的事也是答案的一部分。
