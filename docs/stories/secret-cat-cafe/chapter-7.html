The library confirmed Pepper had an owner and a safe indoor route through a shared courtyard. The rain made the window a little blurry, but it did not hide the quiet life inside the cafe. Cups touched plates, a chair moved, and someone laughed over a small mistake. Aya noticed how carefully Rosa watched the space beneath the tables. A cat could fit where a person could not, and that meant a dropped fork or a loose bag might become a danger. The cafe was not just a place for cute photographs. It was a place where someone had to keep choosing kindness in practical, sometimes unexciting ways. The paper was checked by Rosa. Somebody had packed chocolate for a short break later. Someone had asked Rosa a question about class. They needed to collect all the forms before the end of the day. The open book contained a short story about course.

圖書館確認胡椒有主人，也有穿過共用庭院的室內安全路線。 雨讓窗玻璃有些模糊，卻遮不住店裡安靜的生活。杯盤碰出輕響，椅子移動，有人為一個小錯笑了。小雅注意蘿莎總是仔細看桌下。貓能鑽進人過不去的地方，因此掉落的叉子或鬆開的袋子都可能危險。咖啡館不只是拍可愛照片的地方，也需要有人用實際、有時不那麼刺激的方式持續選擇善意。 這張紙由蘿莎核對。有人為稍後的休息準備了巧克力。有人曾問蘿莎關於課堂的問題。他們必須在今天結束前收齊所有表格。翻開的書有一則談到課程的小故事。

A rain gate was recently closed, making Pepper take a different entrance. For several seconds, nobody spoke. Aya was thinking about the new fact and the way it touched earlier choices. Rosa drew a short line under the important part of the notes. They agreed that a record was useful only if it was clear enough to correct. If a number, name, or time was uncertain, they marked it as uncertain. That extra care took a little longer, but it protected people who were not in the room to explain themselves. At last, Aya could see a practical question worth asking next. The class reading card used flower in an example sentence. A notice near the entrance mentioned hour. Someone had asked Rosa a question about housewife. "What does large mean in this note?" Aya asked. "Let me check the last line," Rosa said.

雨棚門最近關上，才讓胡椒改走其他入口。 好幾秒沒有人開口。小雅思考新事實與先前選擇的關係。蘿莎在筆記重要處畫了短線。他們同意紀錄要清楚，才方便日後更正。數字、姓名或時間若不確定，就標明不確定。這樣雖然多花時間，卻能保護不在場、無法替自己說話的人。最後小雅終於想到下一個值得問的實際問題。 課堂閱讀卡用花造了一個例句。入口附近的告示提到小時。有人曾問蘿莎關於家庭主婦的問題。「這張筆記裡的「大的」是什麼意思？」小雅問。「讓我檢查最後一行。」蘿莎說。

Rosa and the volunteers arrange a calm visit without forcing the cat to travel. Making a choice was harder than making a wish. Aya could imagine a quick solution, but a real plan needed to fit the time, the place, and the people taking part. Rosa listened while Aya explained the steps aloud. They stopped at one unclear part, changed it, and started again. 'If somebody says no, we need another way,' Aya said. Rosa agreed that helping was not the same as deciding for others. Together they wrote a short plan anyone involved could question, improve, or leave if it was not right for them. Someone had asked Rosa a question about list. Aya remembered a drawing of main seen earlier that week. The open book contained a short story about note. The popular activity drew more visitors than expected. A drawing of a rabbit hung on the classroom notice board.

蘿莎和志工安排平靜探訪，不強迫胡椒送信。 做選擇比許願難。小雅可以想像快速解法，但真正的計畫必須配合時間、地點與參與者。蘿莎聽小雅逐步說明，在不清楚的地方停下修改，再重新開始。「如果有人說不，我們要有其他辦法。」小雅說。蘿莎同意，幫忙不等於替別人做決定。他們一起寫下簡明計畫，讓相關的人都能提問、改善或選擇退出。 有人曾問蘿莎關於清單的問題。小雅想起本週稍早看到的主要的圖畫。翻開的書有一則談到筆記的小故事。熱門活動吸引了超出預期的訪客。教室公告欄上掛著一張兔子的圖畫。

Pepper chose to enter the cafe on its own and sat beside the message shelf. When the room grew quiet, Rosa turned down the lights near the window and left the message shelf in order. Aya thought of all the stories guests had brought through the door. Some ended with laughter; others needed more than one meeting. A cat might have helped somebody notice a letter, but the hard work of talking and forgiving still belonged to the people involved. Aya was beginning to understand why Rosa cared about keeping the room welcoming even when old feelings made that difficult. "We need a real answer, not only a nice story," Aya said. The class reading card used start in an example sentence. A notice near the entrance mentioned there. They waited until the responsible person arrived. A notice near the entrance mentioned west.

胡椒自己走進咖啡館，坐在留言架旁。 店裡安靜下來時，蘿莎調暗窗邊燈光，把留言架整理好。小雅想起客人帶進店裡的各種故事，有的以笑聲結束，有的需要不只一次見面。貓也許幫人注意到某封信，但交談、理解和原諒這些困難工作，仍需要當事人自己完成。小雅逐漸明白，為什麼蘿莎即使心裡有舊傷，仍努力讓這間店保持溫暖。 「需要真實答案，不只是好聽故事。」小雅說。課堂閱讀卡用開始造了一個例句。入口附近的告示提到那裡。他們等到負責的人來了才行動。入口附近的告示提到西方。

Before leaving, Aya gave the day one last careful look. It had contained a surprise, a question, a decision, and a result. Those parts did not always arrive in the order people wanted. Rosa asked whether they were ready for tomorrow. 'Ready to listen,' Aya answered, 'even if tomorrow changes the plan.' The reply was small, but it was true. They put away the things they had used, thanked the people nearby, and made sure nobody was left expecting a promise that had never been agreed to.

離開前，小雅最後仔細回顧這一天：有驚喜、問題、決定，也有結果。事情不一定照人希望的順序發生。蘿莎問明天準備好了嗎？「準備好傾聽了，」小雅回答，「即使明天又要改計畫。」這句話很簡單，卻是真的。他們收拾用品、謝謝附近的人，也確認沒有讓誰期待一個從未答應的承諾。

That evening, Aya tried to describe the day in just two sentences. The first sentence was about the problem. The second was about what people did together. Both mattered, and neither would have made sense alone. Aya left space beneath them for another note tomorrow. Rosa sent a short reminder to rest. Aya smiled at it. A person could care deeply about a goal and still eat dinner, talk with family, and sleep. Whatever happened next, those ordinary things were part of the answer too.

那天晚上，小雅試著用兩句話描述今天。第一句是問題，第二句是大家一起做的事。兩句都重要，單獨看卻不完整。小雅在下面留空，準備明天再記。蘿莎傳來簡短提醒，要記得休息。小雅笑了。人可以非常在意目標，同時仍要吃晚餐、陪家人說話、好好睡覺。不論接下來如何，這些平常的事也是答案的一部分。
