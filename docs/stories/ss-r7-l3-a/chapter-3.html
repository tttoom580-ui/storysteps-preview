檢查坑

The Inspection Pit

零號月台的三條規則 · 第 3 / 25 章 · 2000–3000 · 英中對照

用互動版查詞、朗讀與收藏

The station could not afford a second long closure, Vale claimed, but Iva refused to treat money as a reason to ignore a missing worker. At the maintenance entrance she found an apron hanging above a bucket of dirty water. Someone had used fresh tape to cover a warning about the inspection pit. A notice nearby described a recent casting call for a historical play, with dessert promised to every volunteer, so the area had seemed busy and harmless. Yet new footprints crossed the dirt between the notice and the pit. Arun photographed their direction rather than guessing whose shoes had made them. The prints led to a narrow maintenance bridge. One side had no rail, and fresh metal dust covered its edge.

維爾聲稱車站已負擔不起再度長期關閉，以薇卻拒絕把金錢當成忽略失蹤員工的理由。她在維修入口找到一件掛在水桶上方的圍裙，桶裡全是髒水。有人用新膠帶蓋住檢查坑的警告。旁邊公告徵求歷史戲劇的演員，還答應每名志工都有甜點，因此這裡看起來一直熱鬧而無害。然而新的鞋印穿過公告與坑洞之間的泥土。阿倫先拍下足跡方向，而不是猜測鞋子的主人。鞋印通往一座狹窄的維修橋，橋的一側沒有護欄，邊緣還散著新鮮金屬粉末。

An energetic guide tried to make the scene sound dramatic, saying the old station sometimes swallowed people whole. Iva asked for the entry log instead. The last proper inspection record had been altered after Dara signed it: one paragraph about an unstable switch had begun to fade under cleaning fluid. Behind the maintenance desk stood an indoor greenhouse display, its warm lamp still on, even though the power list said the whole room was dark. An official inspector had written "ongoing risk" beside that lamp months earlier. This was a pattern now, not a single mistake. If electricity still reached unused exhibits, it could also reach the unmarked train. Arun traced a wire from the lamp to a locked cabinet and found a recent strip of replacement tape.

一名活力充沛的導覽員試著把現場講成戲劇化故事，說老車站偶爾會把人整個吞下去。以薇要求的卻是進出紀錄。最後一份正式檢查記錄在達拉簽名後被改過：提到不穩定開關的段落，被清潔液弄得逐漸褪色。維修桌後方有一座室內溫室展區，保溫燈仍亮著，雖然配電表說整間房都斷電。幾個月前一位正式檢查員已在燈旁寫下「持續風險」。如今這已成為一再出現的模式，而非單一失誤。若電力仍供給棄用展品，也可能供給那列無車次的列車。阿倫沿著燈線追到上鎖的櫃子，找到一條新貼的替換膠帶。

The cabinet contained absurd objects from an old children's fair: a penguin puppet, an ox mask, and a jar of lollipop sticks. They were there because the museum had used the same room for storage and maintenance, a decision that made every search harder. Under the box of masks lay the real electrical diagram, folded around a rubber plug. Iva spread it over a dry board and compared it with the public diagram. The switch that controlled the demonstration train also powered a signal inside the inspection pit. In the official drawing, that connection did not exist. A senior technician had signed an objection beside the original line, warning that anyone could mistake an automatic train for a running passenger service. The objection carried Dara's initials.

櫃子裡塞著舊兒童園遊會的荒唐物品：企鵝布偶、公牛面具，以及一罐棒棒糖的紙棒。博物館把同一個房間同時用來儲物與維修，才讓搜尋變得如此困難。面具盒下壓著真正的電路圖，還裹著一個橡膠塞。以薇把圖攤在乾燥木板上，與公開版本比較。控制展示列車的開關，也會供電給檢查坑內的一個訊號器。正式圖面卻完全沒有這條連線。一名資深技師在原圖旁簽下反對意見，警告任何人都可能把自動展示車誤認為正在營運的客車。反對意見旁有達拉的姓名縮寫。

Vale returned with a security officer and said Iva was acting like a spy rather than a safety investigator. She answered by showing him the two conflicting diagrams without accusing him of making the changes. "One diagram was used to certify the exhibit," she said. "The other shows what the cables actually do. We need the maintenance history before anyone touches this switch." The officer agreed to place temporary tape across the bridge. Iva did not tell him that the three knocks had sounded again while he spoke, each one separated by an equal pause. She drew the pattern in her notebook and held the page beside Dara's initials. There was a chance a trapped worker was signaling through metal. There was also a chance the sound came from a motor. Either possibility required careful testing, not a brave leap into the pit.

維爾帶著一名保全回來，指責以薇像間諜，不像安全調查員。她沒有直接指控他造假，而是拿出兩張互相矛盾的圖。「其中一張被用來認證展示設備，」她說，「另一張才畫出電纜真正的作用。我們得先查維修歷史，才能讓任何人碰這個開關。」保全同意暫時用封鎖帶隔開維修橋。以薇沒有告訴他，剛才說話時三次敲擊又響起了，每次間隔都完全相同。她把節奏記在筆記本上，將紙頁放到達拉的縮寫旁。可能有人被困在金屬後面發出訊號，也可能只是馬達造成的聲音。不論哪一種，都需要謹慎測試，而不是英勇地跳進坑裡。

The exhibition director later said he had permitted actors to cast shadows across the pit during a rehearsed scene, and that the sound of wheels was part of the same performance. Iva asked for the rehearsal plan. It mentioned lighting but said nothing about a powered carriage moving near an unprotected drop. The difference was not minor. A visitor could interpret an ordinary performance as permission to cross the line. Arun arranged for the museum to cover the pit with a tested temporary barrier, supervised by a qualified engineer. Only after the barrier was secure did the staff collect the children's props from the cabinet. Iva wrote Dara's initials beside the missing electrical revision and signed the finding herself. She refused to let the director's story or the frightened guide's ghost tale decide what had happened. The documents and the equipment would have to agree.

展覽主管後來說，他曾允許演員在排練時把影子投向檢查坑，輪聲也是表演的一部分。以薇要求排練計畫，發現上面只寫燈光，完全沒提通電車廂會在沒有防護的坑邊移動。這差異不小。訪客可能把普通表演誤認成可以跨線的許可。阿倫請博物館在合格工程師監督下，安裝經測試的臨時遮擋。護欄安全後，員工才從櫃裡取出兒童道具。以薇在缺失的電路修訂旁寫下達拉縮寫，也親自簽署發現。她不讓主管的說法或導覽員的鬼故事決定真相，文件和設備必須能互相吻合。
