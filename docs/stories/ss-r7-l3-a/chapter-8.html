消失的一小時

The Missing Hour

零號月台的三條規則 · 第 8 / 25 章 · 2000–3000 · 英中對照 · 校訂中

用互動版查詞、朗讀與收藏

Iva felt a dull ache behind her eyes after a night of work, but the missing hour in the security record bothered her more. A bookcase blocked the camera control panel, and the museum's complaint forms were stored on its highest shelf. Arun asked whether she was still fully conscious and able to continue; she answered yes, then accepted a break. The director offered a discount on tickets for the coming season, as if cheaper admission could settle questions about a missing person. On the break-room window sat a wooden grasshopper from a child's toy collection. Its movable legs reminded Iva of the mechanical train. She realized that the camera's blank period began exactly when the demonstration cable had been tested.

工作整夜後，以薇眼後有一陣悶痛，但保全紀錄消失的一小時更讓她不安。一座書櫃擋住監控控制盤，博物館的投訴表單則收在書櫃最高層。阿倫問她是否仍清醒、能否繼續，她回答可以，卻也接受短暫休息。館長提出新季票折扣，彷彿便宜一點的門票就能解決失蹤案。休息室窗台放著一隻兒童玩具收藏裡的木製蚱蜢，活動式腳讓以薇想起機械列車。她忽然理解，攝影機空白的時段，恰好從展示鋼索開始測試的時刻起算。

Vale ordered the staff not to inform the public before the investigation ended. Iva said a hasty promise of silence would put visitors at risk; she could protect private details while reporting a safety problem. She had hoped to spend her leisure weekend with family, but now had to locate the backup recorder. A child once called the carriage a monster, according to a visitor note. The guide had tried to organize a cheerful answer, calling it a friendly ghost. Iva was interested not in the child's fear but in the time written on the note. The sighting happened during the same missing hour, on a day when the carriage was officially listed as out of service. That made two sources disagree with the official schedule.

維爾命令員工在調查結束前不得通知大眾。以薇說草率承諾沉默會危及遊客；保護私人細節和通報安全問題可以同時做到。她原本希望在閒暇週末陪家人，如今卻得先找出備用錄影器。一張遊客便條寫著，有孩子曾把車廂叫作怪物。導覽員試圖安排歡樂的回答，說那是友善的幽靈。以薇在意的不是孩子恐懼，而是便條上的時間。目擊發生在同一段消失的小時內，那天車廂在官方紀錄上明明列為停用。第二個來源也與正式時刻表互相矛盾。

In the portrait gallery, a new poster announced the release of a video about the station's future. It showed a rocket, a swan, and other bright shapes painted on a tunnel wall, but it cut away before the red door appeared. The video editor had used a tight frame, claiming the space beyond the wall was not unique enough to film. The editor said Vale had also removed a historical scene showing violence during a protest. Iva spoke to her without assuming that every edit was deception. The woman said she had received a list of approved camera angles from Vale. One excluded angle would have shown a small security camera above the red door. Iva requested the raw footage and the written editing order. They were separate forms of evidence, and she wanted both rather than a convenient explanation.

肖像展廳掛出新海報，宣布將發布介紹車站未來的影片。片中出現火箭、天鵝及畫在隧道牆上的鮮明圖案，鏡頭卻在紅門出現前切走。剪輯師使用了很緊的構圖，聲稱牆後空間不夠獨特，不值得拍攝。剪輯師說維爾還刪掉一段描寫昔日抗議暴力事件的歷史畫面。以薇沒有假定每項剪輯都是欺騙，而是詢問拍攝程序。女子說她收到維爾列出的核准攝影角度，其中一個被排除的角度，原本能拍到紅門上方的小型監視器。以薇要求保存原始影片和書面剪輯命令。兩者是不同種類的證據，她都需要，而不是方便的解釋。

The raw file arrived on a small drive late that evening. It showed Dara walking toward the red door at 10:14, then carrying a tool case back at 10:19. The edited public video began at 11:20, after a gap that could not be explained by the approved list of camera angles. Arun compared the clock above the door with the clock embedded in the footage. For several seconds, the wall clock seemed to move backward. Iva did not call it a time loop. A camera can be replayed; a clock can be reset; a person can be misled by either. She recorded the exact frames and asked the editor whether the footage had been copied from an earlier rehearsal. The editor looked frightened, then said, "Ask me when Vale isn't in the room."

當晚稍後，原始影片透過小型儲存裝置送來。畫面顯示達拉十點十四分走向紅門，十點十九分提著工具箱回來。公開剪輯版卻從十一點二十分開始，中間的空白無法用核准拍攝角度解釋。阿倫比較門上方的牆鐘與影片內嵌時間，發現有幾秒鐘牆鐘看起來在倒走。以薇沒有把它叫成時間循環。攝影機可以重播，時鐘可以重設，兩者都可能誤導人。她記下確切影格，問剪輯師影片是否複製自較早的排練。女子看起來很害怕，終於說：「維爾不在房間時再問我。」

Later, the editor met Iva in the safe upper office with a lawyer of her own choosing. She explained that her manager had asked her to replace one continuous shot with two shorter clips from different nights. She had complied because she believed the request concerned privacy, not safety. The replacement made the wall clock appear to reverse because the shots had different dates. Iva asked for the editing history, then gave the woman a chance to correct her own statement before signing it. Nothing required her to identify Dara as a victim or accuse the director of a crime. The useful facts were the original timestamps, the written request, and the location of the removed shot. By the end of the conversation, the missing hour was no longer empty: it had become a sequence of identifiable edits that could be checked independently.

稍後，剪輯師帶著自己選的律師到安全的樓上辦公室見以薇。她解釋主管曾要求把一段連續鏡頭換成兩段不同夜晚的短片，她以為是為了隱私而非安全，於是照做。不同日期的鏡頭接在一起，才讓牆鐘看似倒走。以薇要求剪輯歷史，並讓女子簽名前有機會修正自己的陳述。沒有人要求她把達拉叫作受害者，或指控館長犯罪。有用的事實是原始時間戳記、書面要求和被刪鏡頭的位置。談話結束時，消失的一小時不再空白，而是一串可由他人獨立查證的剪輯動作。
