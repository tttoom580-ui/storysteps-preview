Letters Under Glass

零號月台的三條規則 · 第 12 / 25 章 · 2000–3000 · 英中對照 · 校訂中

用互動版查詞、朗讀與收藏

The museum's school gallery began with a painted alphabet and a model basement classroom. It smelled faintly of an old cigarette, though smoking had been forbidden for years. A metal clip held a photograph of a cock standing beside a school garden. The collection had been assembled by volunteers who remembered the foggy mornings when children passed through the station on their way to lessons. A teacher asked Iva why the city had hired an outside investigator instead of trusting the museum. "Because Dara asked for an independent check," Iva answered. "And because honesty matters more when an institution is popular." The teacher nodded and offered a box of kindergarten drawings showing exits that were missing from the new map.

博物館的學校展區從彩繪字母表和地下室教室模型開始。空氣中還留著淡淡的舊菸味，儘管這裡已禁菸多年。金屬夾夾著一張照片：一隻公雞站在校園花園旁。這批收藏由志工整理，記錄有霧清晨孩子們經車站去上課的日子。一名教師問以薇，為什麼市府聘請外部調查員，而不相信博物館。她答道：「因為達拉要求獨立檢查，而且機構越受歡迎，誠實越重要。」教師點頭，交出一盒幼兒園畫作，上面畫著現代地圖已經消失的出口。

A learning exhibit celebrated liberty of movement through the city. Iva found the idea meaningful, because a safe exit is a form of freedom that people often notice only when it disappears. A glass cabinet held bags of colored powder from a previous festival and a photograph of queer families marching for equal access to public space. The text beside the photograph had recently been replaced with a shorter version that removed the group's demand for accessible transport. Mrs. Rowe asked who had approved that change. Vale said it was a matter of space. The original contributor disagreed. "You can shorten a sentence," she told him, "but you cannot erase why we were there." Iva recorded her statement and asked for the prior exhibit files.

一個學習展區歌頌城市裡自由移動的權利。以薇覺得這件事很有意義，因為安全出口也是自由的一種，人們往往要到它消失才會注意。玻璃櫃裡放著上屆慶典的彩色粉末包，以及酷兒家庭為平等使用公共空間而遊行的照片。照片旁文字最近被換成較短版本，刪掉了團體要求無障礙交通的訴求。羅女士問誰批准更改。維爾說只是空間不足，原提供者卻不同意。「可以縮短句子，」她告訴維爾，「但不能刪去我們為何站在那裡。」以薇錄下她的陳述，並要求提供舊展覽檔案。

The archive assistant showed Iva a small cabinet of shampoo and fabric samples used in the old railway laundry lessons. A woven cloth contained a tiny pocket, stitched to hide a duplicate key. The assistant said that earlier generations had been wise enough to keep spare exits and spare records, because one locked door could turn routine work into danger. Inside the pocket was not a key but a folded maintenance checklist. It revealed that the red door had been tested every year until the redevelopment contract changed. No test had been recorded for the past three years. Arun scanned the paper while the assistant watched. They kept the original in the same pocket until the preservation team could take it safely.

檔案助理帶以薇查看放著洗髮精與布樣的小櫃子，原本是舊鐵道洗衣課程的教材。一塊編織布料有個小口袋，縫製方式像是要藏備用鑰匙。助理說前人有智慧，會保留備用出口與副本紀錄，因為一扇鎖門就可能讓日常工作變成危險。口袋裡不是鑰匙，而是摺好的維護檢查表。表格顯示在重建合約變更前，紅門每年都受測試，過去三年卻沒有任何紀錄。阿倫在助理見證下掃描紙張，原件先留在口袋裡，等待證物保存小組安全取走。

A second letter explained why Dara had been working late. She was not searching for a ghost; she wanted to reveal the missing tests before the reopening. She had booked a routine check of the service corridor and asked Vale for a second staff member to attend. The request had been marked "not necessary." Iva traced the date through the scheduling system and found that Dara had entered the corridor alone under a permit issued for two people. This did not prove what happened after the door closed, but it identified a failure in supervision before the disappearance. Iva wrote to the safety authority that night. She also sent a direct message to Dara's family, confirming that the investigation was active while refusing to share unverified theories.

第二封信解釋了達拉為何深夜工作。她不是在找鬼，而是想在重新開放前揭露缺失的測試。她預約了例行維修走廊檢查，並請維爾安排第二位同仁陪同，申請卻被標記為「不需要」。以薇沿著日期查排班系統，發現達拉憑一張原應由兩人使用的許可證，獨自進入走廊。這還無法證明關門後發生什麼，但明確指出失蹤前就有監督失敗。以薇當晚寫信給安全機關，也直接通知達拉家屬調查仍在進行，卻拒絕分享尚未查證的猜測。

Before going home, the archive assistant asked whether the city would hire a professional conservator to protect the collection. Iva said that was a reasonable proposal, though it belonged to the museum's future budget rather than the immediate rescue plan. The assistant then showed her how volunteers used to weave family letters into public exhibits without changing their words. "There is wisdom in knowing which details belong to a speaker," she said. Iva agreed, remembering the editor who had been told to remove an entire interview demand. They created a list of documents requiring consent before public release and a separate list that investigators could review under the formal process. Preserving evidence did not mean publishing every private memory. It meant keeping an accurate record while respecting the people whose lives were inside it.

回家前，檔案助理問市府是否會雇用專業文物保存員保護收藏。以薇說這是合理提案，但屬於博物館未來預算，不是眼前救援計畫。助理接著示範，志工從前如何把家庭信件編織進展覽，卻不改動原話。「知道哪些細節屬於說話的人，是一種智慧，」她說。以薇同意，想起被要求刪掉受訪者完整訴求的剪輯師。她們建立兩份清單：一份列出公開前需要取得同意的文件，一份列出調查員依法可以審查的資料。保存證據不等於公布每段私人記憶，而是留下正確紀錄，同時尊重生活被寫進檔案的人。
