被拆掉的封條

The Broken Seal

零號月台的三條規則 · 第 9 / 25 章 · 2000–3000 · 英中對照 · 校訂中

用互動版查詞、朗讀與收藏

A brass plate on the control room door claimed that the museum had been restored at great public expense. The restoration banquet was described in a photo of a buffet, but the actual repair list suggested the costly part of the work had never been finished. A young designer had asked for new safety signs and a replacement door frame. Instead, the purchase record listed decorative panels and a large hammer set. Iva made the contractor explain the difference in writing. He muttered that the project had been hell for everyone involved. "That may be true," she said, "but the question is whether the door opens." She wanted technical facts, not a story about how difficult the job had felt.

控制室門上的黃銅牌寫著博物館曾投入大量公帑修復。自助餐宴會照片紀念了修復完成，但實際維修清單顯示最昂貴的一部分工程根本沒有完工。一名年輕設計師曾要求新安全標誌和替換門框，採購紀錄卻改列裝飾板與一套大鎚。以薇要求承包商書面解釋差異。對方咕噥說這項計畫讓每個人都像待在地獄。「也許是真的，」以薇回答，「但問題是門能不能打開。」她需要技術事實，而不是工程多辛苦的故事。

A humorous poster showed a kangaroo wearing a guard's cap and hopping through an icy tunnel. The poster had been printed for a winter festival, when white mist from a stage machine was meant to make the train look mysterious. The scene had been built beside a porcelain display, and the old tape recorder used for the sound effects remained on a shelf. Arun played the tape at low volume. It contained one bell, not two. The second bell heard on Platform Zero must therefore come from another source. Iva opened the recorder case in the presence of the museum technician and found that its original label was covered with a modern sticker. Under the sticker was the date of a flood drill conducted three months earlier.

一張幽默海報畫著袋鼠戴警衛帽，在冰冷隧道裡跳躍。海報為冬季節慶印製，當時用舞台機器產生白色薄霧，讓列車看起來神祕。場景設在瓷器展區旁，製作音效的舊錄音機仍擺在架上。阿倫以低音量播放錄音，發現只有一聲鐘，不是兩聲。因此零號月台的第二聲鐘必定來自其他來源。以薇在博物館技師見證下打開錄音機外殼，發現原標籤被現代貼紙遮住；貼紙下方竟是三個月前一次防洪演習的日期。

Maintenance workers had been told to scrub the floor and seal the red door after that drill, but no safety officer had approved the instruction. One worker admitted that he had skipped the final meeting because he was called to repair seating at the nearby stadium. He returned to find the door already shut. He also found a tangerine peel in a tool tray, a small detail he remembered because food was prohibited in the maintenance corridor. Iva did not treat the peel as proof of a crime; it only showed that someone may have used the corridor after workers left. She asked the worker to describe the tray and its location on a dated statement. Physical memory could be useful, but it needed checking.

維修工人收到指示，要在那次演習後刷洗地面並封住紅門，但沒有安全主管批准。某位工人承認自己翹掉最後會議，因為被叫去附近體育場修座位。他回來時門就已經關上。他還在工具盤發現橘子皮；這細節讓他印象深刻，因為維修走廊禁止飲食。以薇沒有把果皮當成犯罪證明，它只能說明工人離開後可能還有人使用走廊。她請工人以附日期的陳述寫清楚工具盤及位置。實物記憶可能有用，卻需要查核。

The contractor began to threaten a lawsuit, saying the museum would lose investors if Iva continued. Arun quietly tightened a loose lamp bracket while a qualified technician watched; he would not tighten or touch the red door itself. Iva looked at the technical drawing and realized that the old seal had served a double purpose. It had kept floodwater out of a service room, but it also marked a passage that must remain accessible during emergencies. Someone had taken off the seal, moved the door, and painted a new warning line without filing a revision. That left a clear sequence of physical work, separate from the rumors about ghosts. Iva prepared a preservation order for every tool list, test result, and photograph of the altered doorway.

承包商開始威脅要提告，說以薇繼續追查會讓博物館失去投資者。阿倫在合格技師監督下，悄悄把鬆動的燈架鎖緊；他絕不碰紅門的固定件。以薇看著技術圖，意識到舊封條有雙重作用：既防止洪水進入維修室，也標示緊急時必須可通行的走道。有人拆了封條、移動門片，又畫上新的警告線，卻沒有送出圖面修訂。這構成一串明確的實體工程順序，和鬼故事傳言完全不同。以薇準備要求保存所有工具清單、測試結果及改門前後照片。

The next inspection order instructed staff not to skip any door test, even if a reporter or investor arrived early. Arun placed a small card beside each completed task with the technician's name and the time. Workers who had once been told to make the room look perfect now began to report every fault they saw. One discovered a loose rail, another found an outdated exit arrow, and a third corrected a mistaken cable label. None of those findings was dramatic, but together they restored confidence in the procedure. Iva was careful not to confuse confidence with proof that every danger had vanished. The red door remained closed to visitors, and the crew had not yet found a safe path to Dara. She ended the day by checking that the original report and the amended report were both preserved.

下一份檢查命令要求員工不得跳過任何門禁測試，即使記者或投資者提早到場也一樣。阿倫在每件完成工作旁放小卡，記下技師姓名與時間。過去被要求把展廳弄得完美的工人，現在開始報告看到的每項故障。一人發現扶手鬆動，另一人找到過期出口箭頭，第三人更正錯誤纜線標籤。這些發現並不戲劇化，合起來卻能恢復程序的可信度。以薇小心不把信心誤當成所有危險都消失的證明。紅門仍不對遊客開放，隊伍也尚未找到能安全到達達拉身邊的路。她以確認原始報告與修訂版都妥善保存，結束這一天。
