Two Clocks

零號月台的三條規則 · 第 10 / 25 章 · 2000–3000 · 英中對照 · 校訂完成

A banker on the museum board had funded a model of the coastal bay. A beam of light moved over it to celebrate the city's first transport champion, a teacher who had proposed creative routes between villages. Iva noticed that the model's lights would decrease in brightness whenever the bell rang downstairs. The echo in the stairwell made the bell sound farther away than it was. This was a historic exhibit, but its wiring was quite modern. She asked the holder of the electrical permit to show the complete diagram. He refused at first, saying the paper was in a locked cabinet. Only after an independent engineer arrived did he produce it. The diagram placed the loudspeaker and the platform clock on the same control cable.

博物館董事會的一名銀行家贊助了海灣模型。模型上的一道光束移動，紀念城市第一位交通先驅——曾提出創意村落路線的教師。以薇注意到，每當樓下鐘響，模型燈光亮度就會降低。樓梯間的回音讓鐘聲聽起來比實際位置更遠。展覽介紹的是歷史，配線卻相當現代。她要求電力許可證持有人拿出完整圖面。對方起初拒絕，說文件鎖在櫃子裡。直到獨立工程師到場，他才把圖拿出來。圖上顯示喇叭和月台鐘接在同一條控制纜線。

A leak from the ceiling was growing, and a small mob of reporters waited above the stairs for news. Vale told them everything was normal, then offered a tour of a model palace instead of answering questions. A schoolchild had left a single penny in a donation bowl by the exhibit. Iva asked Vale to postpone the tour, not the safety review. The receiver in the timing cabinet had a setting marked "festival mode," and that setting caused the wall clock to jump forward by eleven minutes after the first bell. The service clock, a separate mechanical instrument, kept true time. Rule two now made sense: the two bells were not a mystery, but a warning that the public clock might have been altered.

天花板漏水愈來愈嚴重，一小群記者在樓梯上方等消息。維爾告訴他們一切正常，卻帶大家參觀模型宮殿，避而不答。展區捐款碗裡有個學童留下的一枚便士。以薇要求維爾延期的是參觀活動，而不是安全審查。定時控制箱裡的接收器有一個「慶典模式」，第一聲鐘響後，這個模式會讓牆鐘往前跳十一分鐘。另一只獨立機械維修鐘卻維持真正時間。第二條規則終於合理了：兩聲鐘並非謎團，而是警告公開時鐘可能遭修改。

One scholarship student who had worked at the museum accused a colleague of trying to steal the old mechanism. Iva asked him to separate what he had seen from what he had heard. He had seen the colleague carry a case with a broken zipper; he had not seen the clock inside it. The student apologized for spreading an accusation before checking the facts. Arun showed him how the clock control worked without touching the suspect cabinet. They set a test time on a spare receiver while filming both clocks and the independent time source. The public clock jumped; the service clock did not. They repeated the test twice with the same result. Iva sent the record to the safety authority, along with the prior wiring diagram and the permit number.

一名曾在博物館打工的獎學金學生指控同事企圖偷走舊機械。以薇請他分清親眼所見與聽來的消息。他親眼見到同事提著拉鍊壞掉的工具箱，卻沒看到箱裡有鐘。學生為查證前就散布指控而道歉。阿倫示範鐘的控制方式，但沒有觸碰涉案控制箱。他們在備用接收器上設定測試時間，同時拍攝兩只鐘與獨立時間源。公開牆鐘跳了，維修鐘沒動。他們又做兩次，結果相同。以薇把紀錄、舊配線圖及許可號碼一起送往安全主管機關。

That evening, Iva explained their first major finding to the volunteer committee. The carriage had moved without a route number because its display power had been disconnected from its motion system. The strange bells came from a programmed show, and the wall clock had been changed deliberately. None of that proved that Vale had harmed Dara, but it made the official story of an accidental disappearance less convincing. A maintenance ledger listed work on Door C at a time when the director said no one had entered the service corridor. Arun checked the ledger's ink against the same pen used on the special tickets. The results were not decisive; the paper needed expert comparison. Iva placed all copies in numbered envelopes and ordered the red door secured from public access, but not locked from the tunnel side.

當晚，以薇向志工委員會說明第一項重大發現。車廂沒有車次卻能移動，是因為目的地螢幕的供電與行走系統被分開；怪鐘聲來自事先設定的表演，而牆鐘被刻意改動。這些尚不能證明維爾傷害了達拉，卻讓「意外失蹤」的官方說法更難相信。維修簿記著C門在某個時刻施工，而維爾當時聲稱無人進入工作走廊。阿倫把簿上的墨水與特別車票所用筆墨比較，結果還不能下定論，必須由專家檢驗。以薇把影本放進編號信封，要求隔離紅門的公眾側，但不得把隧道內側反鎖。

A fearful visitor asked whether the station's clocks had really gone backward in time. Iva took him to the public demonstration screen and showed a recording of the test. The mechanical service clock had kept moving forward, while the electronic wall display had jumped to a different number. "The machine changed what we saw," she explained, "not what happened." Arun demonstrated the difference by placing two ordinary watches beside the screen. The visitor frowned, then laughed softly at his earlier fear. Iva did not mock him. Someone had designed the exhibit to encourage exactly that mistake, and the museum had failed to explain its risks. She ended the briefing with a practical message: when two records disagree, keep both, check the source of each, and do not enter a restricted place just to prove that you are brave.

一名害怕的訪客問車站時鐘是否真的讓時間倒流。以薇帶他到公開示範螢幕，播放測試錄影。機械維修鐘始終往前走，電子牆鐘的顯示卻跳到另一個數字。「機器改變的是我們看到的東西，」她說，「不是已發生的事。」阿倫把兩只普通手錶放在螢幕旁示範差異。訪客皺眉，接著對自己先前的恐懼輕笑。以薇沒有嘲笑他。有人刻意設計展覽讓人產生那種誤解，博物館也沒說清楚風險。說明最後，她給出實用建議：兩份紀錄矛盾時保留兩者、檢查來源，絕不要只為了證明自己勇敢就進入限制區。
