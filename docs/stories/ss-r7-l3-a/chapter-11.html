The Union Box

零號月台的三條規則 · 第 11 / 25 章 · 2000–3000 · 英中對照 · 校訂中

The old staff room smelled of paint, not blood, but a label on a first-aid box still read "bloody injury report" from a past accident. Through an open vent came a cool breeze. A retired porter explained that the museum had once received credit for restoring a worker memorial, yet the archive described the dead porter as a criminal. His daughter had spent years trying to correct that claim. The tale was emotional, and Iva listened without promising that a family memory was proof of every detail. She asked for names, dates, and the original employment file. The porter pointed to a wooden chest beneath the window. It belonged to the old union, which had kept copies whenever managers took the public records away.

舊員工室聞起來是油漆味，而非血腥味，但急救箱上仍貼著早年事故留下的「流血受傷報告」標籤。開著的通風口送來一陣涼風。一名退休搬運員解釋，博物館曾因修復工人紀念碑而得到好評，檔案卻把那名過世搬運員描述成罪犯。他的女兒多年來都試著更正說法。故事令人情緒激動，以薇仔細聆聽，卻沒有把家屬記憶當作每個細節都已證實。她請對方提供姓名、日期和原始任職資料。搬運員指向窗下木箱，那是舊工會的箱子；每當管理者拿走公開紀錄，工會便會保留副本。

Inside the chest lay a parcel of letters tied with a slender cord. There was a note about hunger during a strike, and a sketch of a dog trying to lick a dropped loaf of bread. A jar of pepper had spilled across the papers, leaving small black dots. Iva asked Arun to photograph the stack as found before moving a single sheet. The letters described a manager's pitch to convince workers that a steep slope could serve as an emergency exit. One worker had refused to pretend the slope was safe. He wrote that wet wheels would twist on the narrow turn and that passengers would have no room to resist the movement of a crowd. The warning sounded remarkably close to what the investigators had seen in the new freight passage.

木箱裡有一包用細繩綁好的信。一張便條談到罷工時的飢餓，還畫著小狗想舔掉落的長條麵包。胡椒罐曾在文件上打翻，留下細小黑點。以薇請阿倫先拍下原本堆放狀態，再移動任何紙張。信中記錄某位經理的說服話術，想讓工人相信陡坡也可以當緊急出口。一名工人拒絕假裝那條坡道安全。他寫道，潮濕車輪在狹窄轉彎處會扭偏，乘客也沒有空間抵擋人群的推擠。這份警告與調查員在新貨運通道見到的問題驚人地相似。

Mrs. Rowe found a photograph tucked into the sleeve of a union coat. In it, two workers stood by the red door and held a board listing the emergency steps. One worker's wrist was wrapped in cloth after a minor injury. On the board, rule three was clearly visible, and a second line said the service clock must be checked before anyone descended the ramp. Iva compared the photograph with the modern sign. The words matched, but the order had changed. The current sign made the clock rule sound like a mysterious ritual; the old one explained it as a practical check before movement. That difference mattered. If someone had removed the explanation, visitors might obey the words without understanding the danger they were meant to prevent.

羅女士在一件工會外套的袖子裡找到照片。照片中的兩名工人站在紅門旁，舉著列出緊急程序的木牌。一名工人的手腕因輕傷裹著布。木牌上清楚可見第三條規則，下面還寫著下坡道前必須核對維修鐘。以薇把照片與現代告示比較。字句相符，但順序已經改變。現代告示讓核對時鐘像神秘儀式，舊牌卻解釋那只是移動前的實際檢查。這個差異很重要：若有人拿掉原因，參觀者可能只記得服從文字，卻不懂規則所要避免的危險。

The retired porter said the union had once made a fortune in careful records, not money: a record could protect a worker long after witnesses had forgotten details. Iva agreed with the principle but checked the dates anyway. She asked the safety authority to preserve the original box, arranged for a neutral historian to examine it, and gave the retired porter a signed receipt. Vale's lawyer called before nightfall and demanded that the documents be returned immediately. Iva answered that the documents would remain accessible through a lawful review and that their source had been recorded. She would not let a compelling family story replace evidence, but neither would she let the museum erase evidence simply because it came from workers rather than managers.

退休搬運員說工會真正的財富不是金錢，而是細緻的紀錄：證人忘記細節多年後，文件仍可能保護工人。以薇認同原則，仍逐一核對日期。她請安全主管機關保存原箱，安排中立歷史學家檢查，也交給退休搬運員一張簽名收據。維爾的律師在天黑前致電，要求立刻歸還文件。以薇回答，文件會依法供審查，來源也已紀錄。她不會讓動人的家族故事取代證據，也不會因為文件出自工人而非管理者，就任由博物館抹掉它們。

The union box changed the mood of the inquiry. Until then, Vale had spoken as though the station's history belonged to its management alone. Now Iva could point to signed records made by the workers who built and maintained the passages. She did not imagine that every union account was beyond criticism, and she did not ask the retired porter to prove an entire century of injustice. What mattered was the independent description of the same dangerous slope, recorded before the current redevelopment began. Arun added the photograph of the original warning board to a side-by-side comparison with the new sign. The missing explanation was visible even to someone who knew nothing about engineering. Iva wrote a short statement for the review committee: the station had not lost its safety knowledge by accident; later decisions had separated instructions from the reasons that made them useful.

工會箱子改變了調查氣氛。直到此刻，維爾說話時都像車站歷史只屬於管理者。如今以薇能拿出建造與維護通道的工人親自簽署的紀錄。她沒有想像每份工會資料都不會錯，也沒有要求退休搬運員證明一整個世紀的不公。重要的是，現在的重建開始之前，就有獨立文件描述同一條危險坡道。阿倫把原始警告木牌的照片與新版告示並排，遺失的解釋即使不懂工程也看得出。以薇給審查委員會寫下簡短聲明：車站不是意外失去安全知識，而是後來的決策把指示與它之所以必要的原因分開了。
