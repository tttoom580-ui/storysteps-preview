電線裡的水

Water in the Wires

零號月台的三條規則 · 第 7 / 25 章 · 2000–3000 · 英中對照 · 校訂中

用互動版查詞、朗讀與收藏

The museum's flood alarm was advertised as automatic, but an old automobile engine from the transport gallery ran whenever the warning system was tested. Its vibration concealed a dangerous rattle in the drainage pipes. The volunteer committee had noticed the noise and asked for repairs before the reopening. Vale had replied that the museum would clothe every visitor in a raincoat if the weather became bad, as though dry clothing could prevent people from drowning underground. Iva read that exchange aloud to Arun. A committee member who sold dumplings at the weekend market confirmed that the letter was real. She also said a public faucet near the entrance had been disconnected after visitors reported brown water. Iva asked where that water now went.

博物館宣稱淹水警報是自動的，但每次測試警報時，運輸展區的一具舊汽車引擎也會運轉，震動掩蓋排水管內危險的碰撞聲。志工委員會早已注意到噪音，要求重新開幕前修理。維爾卻回信說天氣不佳時會給每位遊客穿雨衣，好像乾衣服就能防止人在地下淹死。以薇把那封信讀給阿倫聽。一名週末在市場賣水餃的委員證實信件是真的。她還說入口旁的公共水龍頭因遊客投訴褐色水而被拆掉。以薇問她現在那些水究竟流向何處。

Beyond a fence in the rear yard, a flock of pigeons gathered around a pipe dripping warm water. Workers had once sold juicy fruit from that yard, but the benches were now stacked beside a wall. Lightning crossed the sky as Iva examined the pipe. She recognized a cable used to power the public microwave in the volunteers' kitchen. The pipe and cable had been fastened together with an unsuitable plastic tie. Arun, who had worked through difficult mental health periods after a past accident, asked to stop for a few minutes before approaching the junction. Iva agreed at once. Being careful was not cowardice. They returned with proper tools and photographed the tie without handling the wet cable.

後院圍籬之外，一群鴿子聚在滴著溫水的管子旁。工人曾在此販售多汁水果，如今長椅卻堆在牆邊。以薇檢查管線時，天空閃過一道閃電。她認出一條供給志工廚房微波爐的電纜；那條電纜竟和水管用不合規格的塑膠束帶綁在一起。阿倫曾在事故後經歷艱難的心理健康時期，因此請求接近接點前先停幾分鐘。以薇立刻同意。謹慎並不是懦弱。他們帶著合適工具回來，在不碰濕電線的情況下拍下束帶。

The children's nature display showed a naked tree trunk, a bird's nest, and a map of the local wetland. The volunteer who built it said the museum had promised not to pollute the drainage channel below. That promise mattered now. Iva traced the broken pipe to a valve connected to the old shelter's flood protection system. The valve had been turned toward the exhibits rather than the outside channel. A shovel leaned against the wall, its handle still muddy. Arun noticed a mark from a maintenance cart beside it, but he refused to call the mud a person's footprint until he had a scale photograph. A handwritten note described the change as a "temporary improvement." No engineer had signed it.

兒童自然展廳放著一段光禿的樹幹、一個鳥巢和一幅本地濕地地圖。製作展廳的志工說博物館曾承諾不污染下方排水道。如今那個承諾有了重要意義。以薇沿著破裂水管追查，找到連往舊避難空間防洪系統的閥門。閥門被轉向展廳，而不是外側排水渠道。牆邊倚著一把鏟子，木柄仍沾著泥。阿倫注意到旁邊有維修車留下的印記，但在拍下有比例尺的照片前，不肯把泥跡說成某人的腳印。一張手寫便條把更動稱為「臨時改善」，卻沒有任何工程師簽名。

Vale called their questions a sin against the museum's reputation. Iva pointed to a simple test: place a container under the safe drain and measure what happens when a technician opens the outside valve. She made sure nobody stood in the channel first. The water changed direction, and the level in the basement stopped rising. One egg left from the volunteer breakfast had broken on a table; its yellow yolk was spreading toward a stack of maps. Arun moved the maps to a dry shelf and smiled briefly at the ordinary mess in the middle of an extraordinary investigation. The test proved there was a working way to drain the station. It also showed that somebody had deliberately closed that route, even as the rainy season approached.

維爾稱他們的問題是對博物館名譽犯下的罪。以薇則提出簡單測試：在安全排水口下放容器，由技師打開外側閥門，再量測水流方向。她先確認渠道裡沒有人。水真的改變方向，地下室的水位也不再上升。志工早餐留下的一顆蛋在桌上碎了，黃色蛋黃正向一疊地圖蔓延。阿倫把地圖移到乾燥架上，對這場非凡調查中如此平凡的混亂短暫一笑。測試證明車站確實有可用的排水方式，也顯示雨季將近時，有人刻意把那條路關閉。

During the meeting, the dumpling seller put down the last dumpling from her lunch and asked a question no one had wanted to hear. "If rain reaches the lower corridor at night," she said, "could someone drown there before the public alarm sounds?" The qualified engineer answered that the risk depended on the drain and the ability to leave, both of which had failed testing. Iva thanked the woman for asking plainly. She then separated the immediate action from the longer repair: the lower rooms would remain closed now, while a new drain and alarm would require independent certification later. Vale protested that the question frightened people. The volunteer replied that she would rather be frightened by a true warning than comforted by a false promise. Iva wrote down that sentence, not as proof, but as a reminder of who safety procedures were for.

會議中，賣水餃的女子放下午餐最後一顆水餃，問出大家不願聽的問題。「如果雨水半夜進入下層走廊，」她說，「有人會不會在警報響起前就淹死？」合格工程師回答，風險取決於排水與逃生能力，而兩者都沒通過測試。以薇感謝女子直白發問，隨後把立即措施和長期修復分開：下層房間現在就要關閉，新排水與警報則須日後獨立認證。維爾抗議問題嚇壞了人。志工回答，她寧可被真實警告嚇到，也不要被錯誤承諾安慰。以薇記下這句話，不是把它當證據，而是提醒自己安全程序究竟為誰存在。
