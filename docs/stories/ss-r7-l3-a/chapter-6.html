互相矛盾的車票

Tickets That Disagree

零號月台的三條規則 · 第 6 / 25 章 · 2000–3000 · 英中對照 · 校訂中

用互動版查詞、朗讀與收藏

To achieve a fair inquiry, Iva requested every admission ticket printed for the past month. In the archive she found a cocktail invitation for a private opening party, advertising the reopened station as the city's most desirable destination. A cartoon devil grinned beside the date. Dara had written "doubtful" across the invitation, and someone had tried to dump the whole box in a cleaning bin. Arun rescued the papers without changing their order. One ticket showed Platform Zero open at midnight; another, printed only minutes later, said the same platform was closed for repairs. Both carried Vale's signature. Iva sorted them by serial number and found a missing run of thirty tickets, all issued on the night Dara disappeared.

為了達成公平調查，以薇要求調閱過去一個月印出的所有入場票。檔案室裡有一張私人開幕派對的雞尾酒邀請卡，把重新開放的車站宣傳為全城最令人嚮往的目的地。日期旁畫著一隻咧嘴笑的惡魔。達拉在邀請卡上寫了「可疑」，有人則試圖把整盒紙本都倒進清潔垃圾桶。阿倫照原順序救回文件。一張票寫著零號月台午夜開放；幾分鐘後印出的另一張，卻說同一月台因維修而關閉。兩張都帶著維爾簽名。以薇按照流水號排序，發現缺少整整三十張票，全在達拉失蹤那晚發出。

An old photograph showed a former governor standing outside an inn with a glass of jelly dessert. The picture was irrelevant to the missing worker, but its frame concealed a pile of service notices. One notice described how a wooden pole once held a warning sign over the underground passage. Another gave the old station a high rank in a public accessibility survey. Iva's childhood pal, an auditor from the suburb, arrived to help verify the notices. She did not claim to know the answer in advance. She checked the paper, dates, and seals while Iva inspected the frame. On the reverse was a recent note: "Move the warning pole before the evening rehearsal." The instruction had no signature and no safety reason.

一張老照片拍到前任總督站在旅館外，手裡拿著果凍甜點。照片與失蹤案看似無關，畫框後卻藏了一疊維修通知。其中一張記錄從前有根木桿，把警告牌架在地下通道上方；另一張寫著車站曾在公共無障礙評比中排名很高。以薇童年時的好友，如今在郊區擔任稽核員，也到場幫忙查核通知。她沒有假裝早知答案，而是一張張檢查紙質、日期與印章，以薇則查看畫框。畫框背面有近期便條：「晚間排演前移走警告桿。」指令沒有署名，也沒有安全理由。

Vale called the newer ticket system superior because it was faster, but speed did not make it reliable. The technician demonstrated how a trail of numbers led from the ticket printer to a computer in the director's office. That computer was not merely selling entry passes; it could activate the demonstration carriage and change the destination screen. The software contained a setting labeled "unity mode," which combined the public show and the private service route. Vale claimed it was an old name chosen before he joined the museum. Iva asked to preserve the settings as found. When Arun disconnected the printer from the public network, the screen on the empty train flickered and finally displayed a route number: ZERO. It appeared for three seconds and went dark again.

維爾說新售票系統比較優越，因為速度快，但速度快不代表可靠。技師示範一串號碼如何把售票機連到館長辦公室的電腦。那部電腦不只賣票，還能啟動展示車廂並更改目的地螢幕。軟體有一個稱為「統一模式」的設定，會把公開演出與私人維修路線結合。維爾聲稱那是他進館前就有的舊名稱。以薇要求先保存原有設定。當阿倫將售票機與公共網路分離，空車廂上的螢幕閃爍，終於出現車次號碼：零。三秒後，它再次熄滅。

Vale warned that a delayed opening would ruin a planned wedding for a couple who had chosen the station as the place to wed. Iva sympathized, but she could not approve an unsafe route to protect a photograph or a celebration. She asked the couple to meet her in a safe upper hall and explained the evidence without sharing Dara's private information. They were disappointed, yet relieved to learn the risks before inviting elderly guests underground. One of them remembered being offered a special ticket without a serial number. The couple gave it to Iva voluntarily. Its blank back held a faint pressure mark, as though another form had been written on top of it. Under angled light, the words "DOOR C — NIGHT ACCESS" became visible. Iva now knew the missing tickets led toward the same red door.

維爾警告延期開幕會毀掉一場婚禮，因為一對新人選了車站作為結婚場所。以薇雖然同情，卻不能為了照片或慶祝活動批准不安全的路線。她邀請新人到安全的樓上大廳見面，只說明證據，不透露達拉的私人資料。兩人失望，但慶幸在邀請年長賓客下樓前得知風險。其中一人記得自己曾拿到一張沒有流水號的特別車票。兩人自願把票交給以薇。空白背面有淡淡筆壓痕，像是上面曾壓著另一張表單。在斜射光下，「C門——夜間通行」幾個字浮現。以薇終於知道，失蹤的車票都指向同一扇紅門。

While checking the boxes, Arun found an old razor in a museum shaving kit. The item was harmless behind glass, but its storage tag was dated the same night as the missing ticket run. He showed Iva the tag instead of opening the case. The ticket office kept a separate log for objects moved into exhibits, and that log showed a delivery to Door C at 11:40. The destination was listed as "temporary storage," although no storage request had been approved. Iva asked for the name of the person who had received the delivery; the signature was too blurred to read. She refused to guess. Instead, she ordered a scan of the original sheet and compared it with other entries written by the same clerk. The record was another small link in the route that Dara had followed. Alone, it was weak; beside the special ticket and altered drawing, it mattered.

整理箱子時，阿倫在博物館剃鬚工具包裡發現一把舊刮鬍刀。它隔著玻璃並無危險，但儲物標籤日期與失蹤車票批次相同。他先把標籤給以薇看，沒有打開展示盒。售票辦公室另有展品搬運簿，上面記著十一點四十分送往C門的一批物品。目的地寫「臨時儲藏」，卻沒有任何經批准的儲藏申請。以薇要求交貨簽收者姓名，簽名卻模糊不清，她拒絕猜測。她安排掃描原件，與同一位職員寫的其他資料比較。這是達拉所走路線的另一個細小連結。單獨看證明力很弱，放在特別車票與更改過的圖旁卻有意義。
