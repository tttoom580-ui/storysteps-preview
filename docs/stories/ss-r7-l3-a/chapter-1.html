沒有車次的列車

The Train Without a Number

零號月台的三條規則 · 第 1 / 25 章 · 2000–3000 · 英中對照

用互動版查詞、朗讀與收藏

North Gate Station had been closed for eleven years and was being prepared to reopen as a tram museum, yet someone had asked safety investigator Iva Chen to inspect it before dawn. The request came from Dara, a maintenance technician who had stopped answering messages. At the entrance a guard insisted that a temporary certificate was acceptable, but Iva wanted to see the actual inspection order. She asked him to advise the director that she was here. He rubbed his chin, set down a carton of coconut water, and tried to connect the desk telephone to an office upstairs. No one answered. Behind him stood three rules, printed in black above the old ticket gate. Rule one said, "Never board a train without a route number." Rule two said, "After two bells, compare the wall clock with the service clock." Rule three said, "Never lock the red maintenance door while anyone is beyond it."

北門車站已關閉十一年，如今正準備改建為電車博物館並重新開放，卻有人請安全調查員以薇在天亮前前來檢查。發出請求的是維修技師達拉，如今她已不再回覆訊息。入口警衛堅持臨時證明也算可以接受，但以薇想先看正式檢查命令。她請警衛通知館長自己已到場。警衛摸著下巴，放下一盒椰子水，試著接通樓上辦公室的電話，卻沒有人接聽。他背後的舊驗票口上方印著三條黑字規則。第一條：「沒有車次的列車絕不可搭乘。」第二條：「兩聲鐘響後，要比較牆鐘與維修鐘。」第三條：「只要紅色維修門後還有人，就不得將門反鎖。」

Iva had seen cruel shortcuts in buildings scheduled for reopening, especially when a deadline mattered more than human safety. She carried a measured dose of medicine for a recurring headache, but the drip of water from the ceiling disturbed her more than the pain. Her assistant, Arun, arrived with a small lamp and an honest confession: he sometimes felt envy toward investigators who could explore abandoned places without considering repair costs. Iva said that was a misunderstanding. Every doorway could hide a person, and every choice needed a reason. They crossed the public hall, calling Dara's name. The lights above Platform Zero came on one by one, although the electrical schedule said the circuit had no power. A metal carriage rolled silently toward them. Its destination screen was dark, and there was nobody in the driver's seat.

以薇見過準備重新開放的建築採取殘酷的省事做法，尤其在期限比人命更受重視時。她帶著控制反覆頭痛的定量藥物，但天花板滴下的水聲比疼痛更令她不安。助手阿倫帶著小燈到達，坦承自己有時羨慕那些能探索廢棄場所、卻不必考慮修繕費用的調查員。以薇說那是誤解：每扇門後面都可能有人，每個選擇都要有根據。兩人穿過公共大廳，不斷呼喊達拉的名字。零號月台上方的燈逐盞亮起，儘管電力表寫著該迴路已經斷電。一節金屬車廂無聲地朝他們滑來。目的地螢幕一片漆黑，駕駛座也空無一人。

The museum's brochure promised an extreme historical experience, but Iva refused to gamble with a machine whose controls were unknown. She took one glance at its empty window and ordered Arun behind the painted safety line. Nearby, the station's lost-property display contained a bale of hay from an old freight service and a bag of railway laundry, both labeled as authentic objects. The exhibit seemed harmless until Iva noticed a fresh wheel mark crossing its dust. The carriage was not simply moving along the public track; it had passed through a narrow door beside the display. She photographed the mark and measured its width. That single observation gave them something stronger than a ghost story: a physical route they could follow without stepping into the carriage.

博物館宣傳冊承諾提供極端逼真的歷史體驗，但以薇拒絕拿控制方式不明的機器冒險。她瞥了一眼空蕩的車窗，就命令阿倫退到地面安全線後。附近的失物展櫃放著舊貨運服務留下的一捆乾草，以及裝著鐵道洗滌衣物的袋子，都標示為真實文物。展品原本毫無危險，直到以薇發現灰塵上有一道新鮮輪痕。車廂並不只沿公開展示的軌道移動；它還穿過展櫃旁一扇狹窄的門。她拍照並量了輪痕寬度。這項觀察比鬼故事更有力：他們找到一條可追查的實體路線，而且不必走進車廂。

A sincere message had been pinned beneath the rule board: "If I disappear, check the third rule first. —Dara." A duplicate of the note lay on a broken stool, beside an old tin that smelled of tobacco. The museum's earliest guards had been twin brothers, according to a photograph hanging nearby; one carried a whistle and the other kept the platform clock. Iva studied the twins' positions instead of their faces. Their hands rested on opposite sides of the red door. At that moment the first bell rang from a speaker over the empty carriage. Arun started forward, certain they had found Dara's train. Iva stopped him. "A bell is a sound," she said. "It isn't a timetable. We check what makes it ring." In the darkness behind the red door, something knocked exactly three times.

規則板下方釘著一則誠懇的留言：「如果我失蹤，先查第三條規則。——達拉。」同樣內容的另一張便條放在一張損壞的凳子上，旁邊是一只帶有菸草氣味的舊鐵盒。根據附近照片，博物館最早的警衛是一對雙胞胎兄弟；一人帶著哨子，另一人負責月台鐘。以薇沒有研究他們的臉，反而看手的位置：兩人的手分別放在紅門兩側。就在這時，空車廂上方的喇叭響起第一聲鐘。阿倫以為已找到達拉的列車，正要向前，以薇卻攔住他。「鐘聲只是聲音，」她說，「不是時刻表。我們要查的是誰讓它響。」黑暗中的紅門後方，忽然傳來整整三次敲擊。

Before leaving the platform, Iva fixed a numbered evidence tag to the outside of the red door without touching its lock. She asked the guard to preserve the original camera files and to write down who had entered the museum since the previous evening. Arun made a drawing of the empty carriage, marking its wheels, the unlit destination display, and the position where the three knocks sounded loudest. The drawing was not a conclusion; it was a way for another engineer to repeat their observations. Iva called Dara's emergency contact and said only what could be confirmed: the technician was missing, a formal search had begun, and trained people were examining the building. On her way out, the first bell rang once more. She asked Arun to record it and mark the position of the loudspeaker, so they could later compare it with the service clock. She underlined that difference in her notebook.

離開月台前，以薇在不碰門鎖的情況下，把編號證物標籤貼在紅門外側。她請警衛保存原始監視檔，寫下前一晚至今所有進館者。阿倫畫出空車廂，標明車輪、熄滅的目的地螢幕，以及三次敲擊最清楚的位置。圖畫不是結論，而是讓其他工程師能重做觀察的方法。以薇打給達拉的緊急聯絡人，只說確認的事實：技師失蹤、正式搜索已開始、受訓人員正檢查建築。離開時第一聲鐘又響了。她請阿倫錄下鐘聲並標記喇叭的位置，以便之後與維修鐘比較。她把這項差異在筆記本裡畫了線。
