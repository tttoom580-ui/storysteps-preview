地板下的聲音

Voices Under the Floor

零號月台的三條規則 · 第 4 / 25 章 · 2000–3000 · 英中對照

用互動版查詞、朗讀與收藏

Iva's ambition had never been to become famous for dramatic rescues. She wanted buildings to be safe before anybody needed saving. The museum's freight gallery made that goal unusually difficult. A painted barn, complete with wooden gates, hid old repair tools behind a wall of stage scenery. A child's collar lay beside a photograph of a farewell concert held here decades earlier. A volunteer said the items had been donated by railway families, but could not explain why the barn door was bolted shut from the inside. Iva asked Arun to hold the lamp against a gap. In the dim light they could see the underside of a recently moved floor panel. The same fine metal dust they had found near the inspection pit covered its hinges.

以薇的志向從來不是因為戲劇化救援而出名；她希望建築在有人需要被救之前就已安全。博物館的貨運展廳讓這個目標格外困難。一座塗色的穀倉舞台布景，隔著木門藏起舊修理工具。孩童的衣領擺在幾十年前於此舉辦的告別音樂會照片旁。志工說這些物品由鐵道家庭捐贈，卻解釋不了為何穀倉門從內側上了栓。以薇請阿倫把燈照向縫隙。在昏暗光線裡，他們看見最近被移動過的地板底部，鉸鏈上覆著和檢查坑邊相同的細金屬粉。

Arun wanted to freeze the scene exactly as it was and call a larger team. Iva agreed to preserve the evidence, but asked him to listen before leaving. The guide had used humor to hide her nervousness, joking that the floor was haunted by a junior novelist whose stories nobody bought. That detail seemed too specific to Iva. An old list named a novelist who had written about the station's first flood, and a local painter had illustrated her book. The gallery displayed a precious first edition beside replicas of onions and roast vegetables from a railway dining car, an arrangement meant to show ordinary family life. Beneath the display stood a small speaker. It played a loop of knocking whenever visitors stepped on the painted mark. Iva unplugged nothing; she documented the mechanism first.

阿倫想讓現場原封不動，立即叫更大的支援隊。以薇同意保存證據，但請他離開前先聽一聽。導覽員用幽默掩飾緊張，開玩笑說地板住著一名作品賣不出去的初級小說家幽靈。這個細節在以薇聽來太具體了。舊名單裡真的有一位小說家，寫過車站的第一次水災，而一名在地畫家替那本書畫插圖。展廳把珍貴的初版放在仿製的舊餐車洋蔥與烤蔬菜旁，想呈現當年家庭生活。展品下面竟有一只小喇叭，遊客踩到畫出的記號時就反覆播放敲擊聲。以薇沒有拔掉插頭，而是先記錄這個機關。

Vale's first response was relief. He said the mystery had been solved and the investigation could end. Iva asked why the speaker played three knocks, a pattern similar to the signal Dara had used in her maintenance training. Nobody answered. A repair worker demonstrated a water spray used to clean the exhibition, and a squirrel crossed the empty track as he reached for the switch. Iva tapped the speaker cabinet; its vibration did not match the deeper sound from beyond the red door. She made two recordings and compared their intervals. The exhibit played three knocks with a short final pause. The real sound had equal gaps. It was the difference between a performance and a deliberate message. Iva showed Vale the recordings, not her conclusion, because she wanted to see whether he would mention the second sound on his own.

維爾的第一個反應是鬆一口氣。他說謎團既然解開，調查就可以結束。以薇問他為何喇叭播放的剛好是三次敲擊，與達拉維修訓練使用的訊號相似。沒有人回答。一名維修工示範清潔展區的噴水裝置，正伸手碰開關時，一隻松鼠跑過空軌道。以薇輕敲喇叭外殼；它的震動和紅門後方更深沉的敲擊不同。她錄下兩段聲音並比較間隔。展品播放的三聲，最後一個停頓較短；真正的聲音每次間隔都相等。這是表演與刻意傳訊的差別。以薇拿錄音給維爾看，卻暫時不說結論，想知道他會不會主動提起第二種聲音。

A traveler named Mrs. Rowe arrived to identify the first-edition book. She had been a teacher in the old station neighborhood and remembered why the red door mattered. During one storm, workers had used it to carry children to a dry tunnel, and the writer had described that rescue. Mrs. Rowe's vision was poor now, so she held the page close and read slowly. "Nobody is safe just because a sign says so," she quoted. "A safe passage is one that opens." Iva asked whether the book mentioned a clock. Mrs. Rowe turned a page and pointed to an illustration: two clocks, one above the public platform and one inside the service corridor, disagreed by eleven minutes. Arun looked up at the museum wall, where a new clock had been mounted over an older circular mark.

一名叫羅女士的旅人到場辨認初版書。她曾在舊車站附近教書，記得紅門為何重要。某次暴風雨期間，工人曾用那道門把孩子送往乾燥隧道，小說家也把那次救援寫進書裡。羅女士如今視力不佳，於是把書頁拿近，慢慢讀道：「標誌說安全，不代表人就安全。真正安全的通道是能打開的通道。」以薇問書裡是否提到時鐘。羅女士翻頁，指向一幅插圖：公開月台的牆鐘和維修走廊裡的鐘，時間竟相差十一分鐘。阿倫抬頭看博物館牆面，發現新鐘正裝在一圈更老的圓形痕跡上。

The old writer's notes helped Iva understand why the museum's mixture of ordinary objects could confuse a visitor. A painted onion from a dining-car exhibit stood directly beside a replica rescue ladder, and the labels did not say which one was safe to touch. She asked Arun to tap the floor once while the guide stood by the hidden speaker. They recorded the staged sound, then moved to the red door and listened to the deeper knock without approaching the barrier. The two recordings sounded similar to someone hearing them casually, but they had different pauses and vibrations. Iva attached both audio files to the inspection report, with the location and time of each. She also thanked the guide for correcting her earlier account. The woman had been frightened, yet she had stayed to help verify the facts rather than defend her first explanation.

舊作家的筆記幫以薇理解，博物館混雜的日常物件為何可能讓遊客困惑。餐車展的彩繪洋蔥緊挨著救援梯複製品，標籤卻沒說哪個可以觸碰。她請阿倫在導覽員站在隱藏喇叭旁時，輕敲地板一下。他們錄下表演聲，再移到紅門旁，在不靠近隔離線的情況下聽更深沉的敲擊。隨便聽的人可能覺得兩段聲音相似，但停頓與震動不同。以薇把兩段錄音連同地點與時間附上檢查報告，也感謝導覽員更正先前說法。女子雖害怕，仍留下協助查核，而沒有堅持第一個解釋。
