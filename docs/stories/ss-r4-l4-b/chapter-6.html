顧問的價碼

The Consultant's Price

1997，青禾市場的第二張招牌 · 第 6 / 25 章 · 3000–4000 · 英中對照 · 校訂中

用互動版查詞、朗讀與收藏

A consultant from Harbor Development arrived carrying photographs of bright shops and a sample of imported linen. He said the company would invest in the new market if the vendors agreed to a twenty-year management contract. The proposal impressed several conservative shopkeepers who preferred a familiar landlord to a risky experiment. Mei-ling was interested in the clean counters but asked who would own the equipment. The consultant replied with a long speech about luxury and progress, never giving a direct answer. Yingshu requested the full agreement. "You may inspect it," he said, "but you cannot take the original away."

港灣開發公司的顧問帶著明亮店面的照片和一塊進口亞麻布樣品來了。他說只要攤商同意二十年經營契約，公司就願意投資新市場。幾位保守的店主很心動，認為熟悉的房東總比冒險試驗可靠。美玲喜歡乾淨櫃檯，卻問設備所有權屬誰。顧問長篇談論奢華與進步，始終沒直接回答。映書要求完整契約。「可以看，」他說，「但原件不能帶走。」

At her mother's table, Yingshu read each condition aloud. The company could raise rents every year, choose which goods were sold, and isolate stalls that failed its standards. One clause allowed it to change a vendor's occupation category without agreement. Another required every consumer complaint to pass through the company's office before reaching the seller. Samir, who dyed cloth as well as sold dried fruit, worried that his goods would be classified as unsuitable. "They could keep me out without saying so," he said. Yingshu copied the relevant lines and enclosed them in a letter asking for clarification.

映書在母親桌邊逐條朗讀。公司可以逐年調租、決定販售商品，並隔離不符標準的攤位。有一條甚至允許公司不經同意改變攤商的職業類別；另一條規定顧客抱怨必須先經公司辦公室，才能傳到店主。兼賣染布的沙米爾擔心自己的商品被列為不合規。「不用明講，他們就能把我排除。」他說。映書抄下條文，附在要求釋疑的信裡。

The consultant returned the following day and told the vendors not to be ignorant of modern business practices. Xiu-ying's face filled with rage. Before she could speak, Yingshu asked the man to define the standards in writing. "An insult will not help us compare the choices," she told her mother later. "Neither will a promise that has no figures behind it." The remark cost her some popularity among people who wanted a fierce public answer. Yet she was grateful for the grace with which her mother had kept quiet. A legal question on paper would be more useful than a shouted argument.

顧問隔天又來，叫攤商不要對現代商業一無所知。秀英氣得臉色發紅。她還沒開口，映書就要求對方把標準寫清楚。「辱罵不能幫我們比較選項，」映書後來對母親說，「背後沒數字的保證也不行。」有些人期待當場強硬反擊，因此她失去了一點人氣。但她感激母親以克制而有風度的方式忍住了怒氣。書面的法律問題，比吵架更有用。

That afternoon, an inspector examined food samples in a school laboratory. She let the vendors look through a microscope at water collected near the fish stalls. Tiny organisms moved across the glass. Yingshu felt her hands tremble, not because she believed the market should close, but because the evidence made the sanitation problem real. The inspector explained that the sample alone could not identify every risk. She recommended repeated tests and repaired drains. Mei-ling stood beside the door, timid at first, then asked how long the tests would take. Her question led to a practical schedule.

下午，檢驗員在學校實驗室檢查食物樣本，還讓攤商透過顯微鏡觀看魚攤附近採集的水。玻片上有微小生物在動。映書雙手微微顫抖，不是因為她認定市場該關，而是證據讓衛生問題變得真實。檢驗員說單一樣本不能判定所有風險，建議重複測試並修好排水。美玲起初怯生生站在門邊，後來問檢測要多久。這個問題讓大家開始訂出可行時間表。

The old textile seller brought a piece of cloth that had lost its color under years of sun. She used it to explain why a contract mattered: a buyer could admire the bright dye in a sample, but the goods had to survive ordinary use. "I've seen fine fabric become rags," she said. "If you promise twenty years, I want to know what happens in the second year." Beside her, the school caretaker adjusted a broken stereo that played music too loudly. The scene was plain and crowded. Yingshu preferred it to the consultant's beautiful room, where every surface had appeared untouched.

老布販帶來一塊多年曝曬而褪色的布，借它說明契約的重要：買家可以欣賞樣品鮮明的染色，但商品得經得起日常使用。「我看過好布變破布，」她說，「承諾二十年，我要先知道第二年會怎樣。」旁邊學校工友正在調整一台音量過大的故障音響。現場樸素又擁擠。映書反而比較喜歡這裡，勝過顧問那間所有表面都像沒人碰過的漂亮辦公室。

That evening, the vendors held an orientation for anyone who wanted to understand the proposal. They divided its promises into rent, ownership, safety, and the right to leave. No one was asked to vote immediately. A seller known for her habitual silence asked whether her son could inherit a stall if she retired. The draft seemed to say no. Several people were shocked, but Yingshu reminded them that a draft could be negotiated. The company had offered a possibility, not a command. The vendors' task was to decide whether its benefits justified the cost, and what changes would make it acceptable.

傍晚，攤商辦了一場說明會，讓有興趣的人理解提案。他們把承諾拆成租金、所有權、安全及退出權，沒有要求當場表決。一位習慣保持沉默的店主問，退休後兒子能否繼承攤位。草案看起來不行。眾人震驚，映書卻提醒大家草案可以談判。公司提出的是可能方案，不是命令。攤商要決定其好處是否值得代價，以及哪些條件必須修改。

When the chairs were put away, Xiu-ying gave her daughter a bowl of noodles. "I wanted to shout at him," she admitted. "So did I," Yingshu replied. "But the best answer is one that he has to answer back." Their disagreement had not disappeared. Xiu-ying still liked the idea of a new roof; Yingshu was still afraid of losing independent work. They agreed on one thing: the next meeting would begin with the questions the consultant had left unanswered. If he came again, he would face ordinary people who had read the same pages he had.

收好椅子後，秀英端麵給女兒。「我真的很想罵他。」她承認。「我也是，」映書說，「但最好的回應，是他不得不回覆的問題。」母女分歧沒有消失。秀英仍喜歡新屋頂，映書仍怕失去獨立經營權。但她們同意下一場會議要從顧問沒答的問題開始。若他再來，面對的將是讀過同一份契約的普通攤商。
