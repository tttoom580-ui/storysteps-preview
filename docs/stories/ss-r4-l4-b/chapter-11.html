等待的代價

The Cost of Waiting

1997，青禾市場的第二張招牌 · 第 11 / 25 章 · 3000–4000 · 英中對照 · 校訂中

用互動版查詞、朗讀與收藏

Summer was approaching, and every week of uncertainty cost the market money. The accountant compared the town's plan with private offers and explained how different forms of capitalism shaped who took a risk and who received a profit. The developer's contract would make the company the dominant manager, while individual vendors would carry most daily expenses. Yingshu did not treat the word capitalism as a personal accusation. She wanted to know the consequences of each clause. When the report began to circulate, a few sellers accused her of trying to rebel against business itself. She answered by inviting them to check the numbers.

夏天將近，每週的不確定都讓市場付出代價。會計師比較鎮方方案與私人提議，說明不同形式的資本主義如何分配風險和利潤。開發商契約將讓公司成為主要管理者，攤商卻負擔大部分日常成本。映書沒有把「資本主義」當成人身指控，她只想知道每個條款的後果。報告開始流傳後，幾位店主指責她反抗商業本身。她邀他們一起核對數字。

At the post office, a retired operator showed her the dusty remains of an old telegraph machine. In its day, a short code could send an urgent message across the coast. "Speed matters," he said, "but an eventual answer is useless if the message was wrong." The remark stayed with her. The town planned to make a decision before the end of June, yet many vendors had not received personal information about their possible stalls. Yingshu asked the clerk to expand the mailing list and record every returned letter. She knew a graceful announcement could not replace successful delivery.

郵局裡，退休電報員讓她看一台滿是灰塵的舊電報機。當年短短一組密碼，就能把緊急訊息傳遍海岸。「速度重要，」他說，「但訊息若錯了，遲來的答案就沒用。」這話留在映書心裡。鎮方打算六月底前決定，許多攤商卻仍未收到個別搬遷資訊。她請職員擴充郵寄名單並登記退件。她明白優雅的公告無法取代真正送達。

One afternoon, a farmer complained that the crowded market had encouraged rats to breed near grain sacks. Mei-ling agreed that cleaning must improve, then asked whether the proposed new site had a pest-control budget. The town engineer found no such item in the published estimate. That was another indication that the comparison was incomplete. Yingshu asked the farmer to join the safety group instead of treating the complaint as an invasion by outsiders. He accepted after she promised the group would visit the grain store first. Nobody wanted to defend dirt simply because it belonged to the past.

一天下午，農民抱怨擁擠市場讓老鼠在米袋旁繁殖。美玲同意應改善清潔，接著問新場地是否列有病媒防治預算。工程師在已公開估價裡找不到這一項。這又顯示比較不完整。映書邀農民加入安全小組，不把他的抱怨視為外人入侵。他在她承諾先查米倉後答應加入。沒人想只因髒亂屬於過去，就替它辯護。

A school group proposed a weekend marathon along the waterfront to raise money for temporary storage. Their teacher had built a small solar-powered sign for the event and hoped it would attract visitors. Yingshu thanked them but warned that charity could not be the foundation of a long-term budget. They could collect money for a clearly named task, not pretend to replace public duties with goodwill. The students agreed to pay for a shared noticeboard and first-aid supplies. The teacher said she admired the distinction. Yingshu was relieved; young people deserved enthusiasm that did not turn into a burden they could not carry.

學校團體提議在海濱辦週末馬拉松，替臨時倉儲募款。老師製作了一塊小型太陽能告示牌，希望吸引訪客。映書感謝他們，卻提醒慈善不能成為長期預算基礎。他們可以為明確項目募款，不能假裝善意能取代公共責任。學生同意支付共用公告板與急救用品。老師說她佩服這樣的區分。映書鬆了口氣；年輕人的熱情不該變成他們扛不起的責任。

The developer's lawyer finally offered to negotiate the management terms. Yingshu brought the accountant, Mei-ling, and two vendors who supported the new building. She made no attempt at grand persuasion. Instead she asked whether a tenant could leave without losing a deposit, and whether rent increases would have limits. The lawyer answered some questions and postponed others. When the meeting ended, the group had no agreement, but it did have a list of negotiable clauses and another list the company refused to change. That distinction was more useful than pretending the two sides were either friends or enemies.

開發商律師終於願意談管理條件。映書帶了會計師、美玲和兩位支持新建築的攤商。她沒有打算靠精彩說辭說服對方，而是問租客能否不失訂金而退出、租金漲幅有無上限。律師回答一部分，延後另一部分。會議結束時還沒有協議，但大家拿到兩張清單：可談條款，以及公司拒絕修改的條款。這比假裝雙方只能當朋友或敵人更有用。

Walking home, A-Zhan found a child's wooden toy in the gutter, a little cart whose wheel had come off. He repaired it with a wire and returned it to the boy outside the market. The boy laughed when the wheel began to turn and said the rough wood tickled his palm. Yingshu watched them and thought about what a repair could and could not do. It could keep a useful thing from becoming a wreck. It could not make the thing last forever, or decide who deserved it. The market would need more than affection. It would need rules for the day after the celebration.

回家路上，阿湛在水溝邊找到一輛輪子掉了的兒童木玩具車。他用鐵絲修好，還給市場外的小男孩。輪子重新轉動時，孩子笑說粗木頭搔得手心發癢。映書看著他們，想著修理能做和不能做的事。修理能讓有用物品不致變成廢物，卻不能保證它永遠不壞，也無法決定誰該擁有它。市場需要的不只是感情，還需要慶祝結束後照樣能運作的規則。

Before bed, she checked the town calendar. There were only five weeks until the planned vote. Xiu-ying asked whether her daughter still intended to return to her city job. Yingshu said that job had been temporary, and the company had already closed its local office. "I haven't decided what to do next," she admitted. Her mother did not press. Instead she asked whether Yingshu would stay through the vote. "Yes," she said. It was a smaller promise than a lifetime commitment, but she could keep it. For now, the market needed a reliable date more than a beautiful declaration.

睡前，她核對鎮上行事曆，離預定表決只剩五週。秀英問女兒還打算回城市工作嗎。映書說原本職位只是短期約聘，公司在當地的辦公室也已關閉。「下一步我還沒決定。」她坦承。母親沒有追問，只問她會不會待到表決。「會。」映書說。這比一生的承諾小，卻是她能做到的。眼下市場更需要可靠日期，而不是漂亮宣言。
