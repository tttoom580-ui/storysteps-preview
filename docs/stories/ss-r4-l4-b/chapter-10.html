The First Public Test

1997，青禾市場的第二張招牌 · 第 10 / 25 章 · 3000–4000 · 英中對照 · 校訂中

The first public trial took place on a Saturday in May. The vendors had permission to install six temporary tables on the high street, provided that they kept the fire lane open. A volunteer surgeon from the clinic checked the first-aid box and laid out bandages. A pregnant customer asked where she could sit if the line grew long. Yingshu showed her the shaded bench and explained the nearest toilet route. Safety was not a mere decoration added to the sales plan; it was a critical condition of opening at all. Mei-ling repeated the instructions to every helper before customers arrived.

五月的一個星期六，第一次公開試營運開始。攤商獲准在高街擺六張臨時桌，但必須保持消防通道暢通。診所志工外科醫師檢查急救箱，排好繃帶。一名懷孕顧客問隊伍太長時可以在哪裡坐，映書指給她看有遮蔭的長椅，並解釋最近的廁所路線。安全不是營業計畫上的裝飾，而是能否開張的關鍵條件。客人到來前，美玲已對每名幫手重複說明。

A retired teacher offered a display about the area's earliest settlers. His photographs included an old tomb near the river and a street that had vanished after a flood. Yingshu agreed to place the display at the far end, where it would not block the food queue. A boy asked whether civilization was simply the building of newer things. The teacher smiled. "It is also how we care for people who cannot choose the time they live in." Yingshu liked the answer, but she insisted the display identify uncertain dates. An attractive story could still be wrong.

退休教師提供一組介紹地方早期移居者的照片，其中有河邊古墓，還有洪水後消失的街道。映書同意把展板放在最遠端，以免擋住買食物的隊伍。一名男孩問文明是不是就是不斷蓋新東西。老師笑著說：「也包括我們怎麼照顧無法選擇自己所處年代的人。」映書喜歡這答案，但堅持對不確定的日期作註記。動人的故事仍可能有誤。

At ten, a cooking stove gave a sharp pop. Someone shouted "explosion," and the crowd stepped back. The safety leader called out, "I forbid anyone from approaching until the gas supply has been checked." There had been a small burner failure, not a spreading fire, but Yingshu ordered the stove shut down and recorded the incident. The vendor who operated it offered an apology and agreed to use cold food for the rest of the day. No one was injured. They could not congratulate themselves on a safe event while pretending that a frightening moment had not happened.

十點時，爐子忽然「砰」一聲。有人喊「爆炸」，人群立刻後退。安全負責人大聲下令：「燃氣供應未檢查前，我禁止任何人靠近。」這是小型爐頭故障，沒有蔓延成火災，但映書仍下令停用，並記錄事故。操作爐具的攤商道歉，答應當天剩下時間改賣冷食。沒有人受傷。然而，若假裝驚險一刻沒發生，就不能自稱辦了安全的活動。

The developer's representative came with another bargain: free signs and a discount on future rent in exchange for allowing the company to call the trial its own project. A-Zhan found the offer tempting. Yingshu asked to see a written agreement and learned that the discount would last only three months. She illustrated the difference on the blackboard, comparing a year's rent with and without the offer. Some people were suspicious of her motives, asking whether she opposed everything. "I oppose surprises hidden in small print," she replied. "A useful offer should survive a clear calculation."

開發商代表又帶來一筆交易：免費招牌和未來租金折扣，交換公司把試營運稱作自家計畫。阿湛覺得條件不錯。映書要求書面文件，才發現折扣只維持三個月。她在黑板上畫出一年租金有折扣與無折扣的差異。有人懷疑她動機，問她是否什麼都反對。「我反對藏在小字裡的意外。」她說，「好提案應該經得起清楚計算。」

At lunchtime, the school brought a computer technician who maintained a small website for the regional college. In 1997 few market customers used the internet, so he proposed posting trial results online only as an additional public record. The paper noticeboard and cassette recording would remain the main ways to reach people. Yingshu agreed and asked him to preserve earlier versions rather than replace them silently. The technician said the website could show dates and a short explanation of each change. That arrangement impressed her more than any bright computer screen could have.

午餐時，學校找來一名替附近學院維護小型網站的電腦技術員。1997年市場顧客很少上網，他因此只提議把測試結果加貼到網路上，作為額外公開紀錄。紙本公告和卡帶錄音仍是主要通知方式。映書同意，並要求保留舊版，不能偷偷覆蓋。技術員說網站可列日期和各次修改的簡要說明。這安排比任何明亮螢幕更令她滿意。

By late afternoon, the stalls had sold enough food to cover their direct expenses. The accountant congratulated the group but refused to call the day profitable until unpaid work and repairs were counted. Yingshu proposed a resolution: continue the trial for two more weekends under stricter equipment rules, then submit all results to the town. The vendors approved it by a show of hands, with dissent recorded. Nobody called the decision a revolution. Still, the simple act of deciding together felt different from receiving orders. It turned a crowd of worried shopkeepers into an organization capable of correcting itself.

下午晚些時候，攤位收入足以支付直接支出。會計師向大家道賀，但拒絕在計入無薪工時與修繕前稱它獲利。映書提出決議：在更嚴格的設備規範下，再試營運兩個週末，之後把全部結果送交鎮公所。攤商舉手通過，反對意見也照實記錄。沒有人稱這是革命，但一起決定的感受，確實不同於單方面接收命令。一群憂心的店主，開始成為能夠修正自己的組織。

After the tables were removed, Xiu-ying and Yingshu sat on the curb sharing a simple meal. They had not reached a prosperous future, and the construction decision remained open. Yet the old woman could see a path forward: a repaired drain, a legal place to trade during construction, and contracts whose terms could be understood. She put one hand on her daughter's arm. "I did not think you would stay long enough to see the work," she said. Yingshu looked at the empty high street. "Neither did I," she answered. The town lights came on, and neither of them stood up at once.

桌子收走後，秀英和映書坐在路緣吃簡單晚餐。他們還沒有迎向繁榮的未來，施工決策也未定。但老人已能看見一條路：修好的排水、工程期間合法營業的場地，以及讀得懂條款的契約。她把手放在女兒手臂上。「我原以為妳不會待到看見這些工作。」映書望著空下來的高街回答：「我也沒想到。」街燈亮了，母女誰也沒有立刻起身。
