打不開的門

The Door That Could Not Open

1997，青禾市場的第二張招牌 · 第 4 / 25 章 · 3000–4000 · 英中對照

用互動版查詞、朗讀與收藏

On Wednesday, a mechanic named Uncle Shen accompanied Yingshu to the western exit. The metal door looked strong until he tried to push it open. It moved only a few inches. A bolt had shifted, and the heavy frame scraped against the floor. "This is essential," Shen said. "You cannot call a passage safe because it exists on a diagram." He showed her a repair manual, but he would not loosen the bolt while people were using the corridor. Without an inspection, a sudden release might injure someone. Yingshu canceled the test walk and reported the problem at once.

星期三，修理工沈叔陪映書檢查西側出口。鐵門看似堅固，推起來卻只能動幾寸。門栓移位，沉重門框磨著地板。「這是根本問題，」沈叔說，「圖上有出口，不代表真能安全通行。」他拿出維修手冊，卻不肯在有人使用走道時鬆開門栓。未經檢查，突然彈開可能傷人。映書立刻取消動線測試並通報問題。

The town engineer arrived with a young assistant carrying an experimental measuring device. She recorded the width of the exit, the force needed to open it, and the condition of the floor. The assistant said the repair might be straightforward but needed authorized work. Yingshu asked for a written draft of the safety findings before making any public claim. When the engineer hesitated, Yingshu explained that the findings might be read by people unfamiliar with technical language. "A clear report will strengthen trust," she said, "even when the news is unpleasant." The engineer agreed to return the next day.

鎮公所的工程師帶著助理和試驗用量測器來了。她量了出口寬度、推門所需的力及地板狀況。助理說修理可能不難，但必須由合格人員施工。映書要求先有書面安全報告草稿，再對外宣稱結果。工程師有些猶豫，映書解釋一般民眾未必熟悉專業用語。「報告越清楚，越能增加信任，即使消息不好。」工程師答應隔天再來。

Outside, a tour guide was explaining the harbor to visitors. She called the market a magnificent attraction from the old days, then waved toward the unsafe door as if it were part of the scenery. Yingshu interrupted politely to explain the closure. The guide apologized and changed her route. One visitor said he worked as a diplomat and asked whether the town would preserve the market's international character. Yingshu gave him no grand greeting about culture. She told him that Samir's rented stall and the fish seller's broken drain were both more immediate than a beautiful speech. He nodded.

外頭，一名導遊正在向遊客介紹港口。她稱市場是往昔留下的壯麗景點，還指著那道不安全的門，彷彿它只是風景的一部分。映書禮貌打斷，說明封閉情況。導遊道歉並更換路線。一名自稱外交官的遊客問鎮上會不會保存市場的國際特色。映書沒有發表華麗的文化致詞，只說沙米爾租攤的權利與魚販的壞排水，比漂亮演說更迫切。對方點了頭。

The school had offered a satellite photograph of the coast as an educational display. On it, the market appeared as a small gray rectangle, hardly different from a warehouse. From that height, no one could see a mother counting coins or a child choosing an orange. Yingshu used the image to show the scale of the drainage area, but warned the audience that one picture could not explain daily life. A printed map, a photograph, and a person's account would need to be compared. Each could answer a question the others could not. Her approach produced one outstanding result: people began offering specific feedback.

學校提供一張海岸衛星照片作為教學展示。照片裡市場只是一個小灰色長方形，和倉庫沒兩樣。從那麼高的地方，沒人看得見母親數硬幣或孩子挑橘子。映書用照片說明排水區域的尺度，卻提醒大家，一張圖無法解釋日常生活。印刷地圖、照片與當事人口述必須互相對照，各自能回答別的資料回答不了的問題。她的方法帶來一項出色的成果：人們開始提出具體回饋。

At lunchtime, A-Zhan wanted to flee the endless questions and go fishing. Yingshu admitted she had felt the same hesitation. The sea stretched to the horizon, and for a moment she envied people who could leave their work at the door. But when an elderly customer came looking for his usual recreation club, she learned that the market courtyard was also where retirees played chess. He asked whether their benches would be moved. That detail was absent from the building plan. Yingshu wrote it down and promised only to ask, not to guarantee an answer she did not have.

午餐時間，阿湛想逃離問不完的問題，跑去釣魚。映書承認自己也曾遲疑。海一路延伸到地平線，她一度羨慕能把工作留在門外的人。但一位老顧客來找平常的休閒棋友時，她才知道市場中庭也是退休老人下棋的地方。老人問長椅會搬去哪裡；建築圖完全沒寫。映書記下，只答應提出問題，不保證自己還不知道的答案。

The engineer returned before sunset. The door could be repaired, she said, but the water pipe beside it had leaked for years. That moisture created a place where germs could grow, and a simple coat of paint would not solve the problem. She suggested closing the passage until both faults were fixed, then conducting a controlled test. Yingshu thanked her for the specific advice. She knew some vendors would be angry about losing an exit during business hours, yet calling it safe would be worse. She posted the findings in large letters with a smaller list of repair steps.

工程師日落前回來，說門能修，但旁邊水管漏了多年，潮濕環境容易滋生病菌，單靠刷漆不能解決。她建議關閉走道，修好兩項故障後再做受控測試。映書感謝這些明確建議。她知道攤商會因白天少了一個出口而生氣，但明知不安全卻宣稱安全更糟。她用大字張貼結果，下面另列維修步驟。

That night, Xiu-ying asked why her daughter had stopped talking about saving the building. "Because I am beginning to understand what saving a market means," Yingshu said. "A building can survive while the people who use it disappear. People can survive while the old building changes." Her mother folded the daily receipts, considering the difference. Then she handed Yingshu a spare key and told her to open the stall in the morning. It was not a promise of agreement. It was a quiet invitation to share the work long enough to make a serious choice.

那晚秀英問，女兒為什麼不再一直說要保住建築。「因為我開始明白，救市場到底是什麼意思。」映書答。「建築可能留下，人卻被趕走；人也可能好好活下來，即使建築需要改變。」母親一邊折收據一邊想著兩者的不同，最後把備用鑰匙交給她，請她明早開攤。那不是表示完全同意，而是邀請她共同承擔工作，直到能做出認真的選擇。
