魚攤底下

Under the Fish Stalls

1997，青禾市場的第二張招牌 · 第 7 / 25 章 · 3000–4000 · 英中對照 · 校訂中

用互動版查詞、朗讀與收藏

The smell beneath the fish stalls grew worse after a hot night. Mei-ling carried a basin of water to the school laboratory, where an academic researcher from the regional college offered to help. He explained that water chemistry could reveal the source of some odors, but only careful sampling would distinguish a leaking drain from waste entering at another point. The market's expansion over the years had covered several old pipes. Yingshu admired the researcher's patience, yet she asked him to speak in fluent, everyday language without technical shortcuts. "We need to understand what the evidence can and cannot show," she said.

炎熱一夜後，魚攤底下的臭味更重。美玲端著一盆水到學校實驗室，附近學院的學術研究員願意協助。他說水質化學分析可指出部分異味來源，但必須謹慎採樣，才能分辨漏水管與其他地方流入的廢水。市場多年擴張，覆蓋了好幾條老管線。映書佩服他的耐心，仍請他以流暢易懂的日常語言說明，不要用太多專業簡寫。「我們需要知道證據能顯示什麼，也需要知道它不能顯示什麼。」

Samir knew an accountant who volunteered at the town library. She helped calculate the market's repair expenses against the cost of moving. The result did not defeat either side's argument. Some stalls could afford temporary closure, while others would lose their entire prime season. The accountant said the most characteristic mistake in public estimates was counting only money paid out, not the work people stopped earning. "The figures are relevant only if we show the assumptions," she said. Yingshu asked her to put the assumptions at the top of every page rather than hide them in small notes.

沙米爾認識一位在鎮圖書館當志工的會計師。她協助比較市場修繕費與搬遷成本。結果沒有直接擊敗任何一方：有些攤位能負擔短期停業，有些卻會失去全年最旺的一季。會計師說公開估價最典型的錯誤，是只算已付出的錢，沒算因此賺不到的收入。「只有假設列清楚，數字才有關聯。」她說。映書請她把假設放到每頁最上方，而不是藏在小字裡。

In the afternoon, the schoolchildren returned with a model aquarium. They placed a stone beside a clear tube to show how a narrow opening could change water flow. Their teacher admitted the model was imperfect, but it motivated neighbors to ask useful questions. An elderly man complained that the display would annoy serious engineers. The researcher surprised him by saying simple models helped people understand where to look next. Yingshu felt fortunate to have both groups in the room: people who knew how to measure, and people who knew where the market flooded every summer.

下午，學童帶著水族箱模型回來。他們在透明管旁放石頭，展示狹窄開口如何改變水流。老師承認模型不完美，卻成功鼓勵鄰居提出好問題。一位老人抱怨這會惹惱認真的工程師。研究員卻說簡單模型能幫人理解下一步要看哪裡。映書慶幸房裡有兩類人：懂測量的人，以及知道市場每年夏天在哪裡淹水的人。

At dusk, Mei-ling walked the team along the rear lane. A shade tree had grown through the edge of a concrete cover, leaving a gap beside the drain. They heard water with an uneven rhythm beneath their feet. Rather than open the cover themselves, they marked the spot and requested an official inspection. A-Zhan wanted to work immediately. "We strive to protect the market," he said, "so why can't we fix one pipe?" Yingshu answered that untrained hands might create a greater danger. Their contribution today was a reliable location, not an unsafe repair.

黃昏時，美玲帶大家走後巷。遮蔭樹的根長進混凝土蓋板邊緣，在排水口旁留下缺口。他們聽到腳下水聲節奏不均。眾人沒有自行掀蓋，而是標出位置，請官方檢查。阿湛想立刻動手。「我們努力保護市場，怎麼連一條管都不能修？」映書回答，沒受訓的人可能造成更大危險。他們今天的貢獻是找出可靠位置，而不是冒險施工。

The next morning, the town inspector confirmed the broken joint and found a second problem farther downhill. She proposed repairing both in stages while keeping the fish area closed. Mei-ling could have protested, but instead asked for a temporary washing point near the bus stop. Other vendors offered to share water and space. Their support moved Yingshu more than the formal report. She realized that admiration did not have to mean agreeing with every decision. Mei-ling was angry about the closure and still willing to follow a plan that protected her customers.

隔天早上，鎮檢查員確認了斷裂接頭，還在更低處找到第二個問題。她建議分段修復，期間關閉魚攤區。美玲本可抗議，卻先問能否在公車站旁設臨時清洗點。其他攤商願意分享水和空間。這份支持比正式報告更讓映書感動。她明白敬佩不代表每個決定都要同意。美玲對停業很生氣，卻仍願遵守保護顧客的方案。

A-Zhan had to waken Yingshu before daylight with a fresh list of requests. He had spent half the night measuring how long it took older customers to walk from the bus. His notes were messy but careful. The accountant converted them into a table, and the researcher agreed to compare the results with the new plan. There was no single miracle in their work. A question became a measurement, a measurement became a limit, and a limit became something people could discuss. Yingshu felt the market growing quieter, not because its problems were gone, but because the conversation had gained direction.

天亮前，阿湛得把映書喚醒，帶來新的需求清單。他半夜還在量長者從公車走到市場要多久，筆記亂卻仔細。會計師把紀錄整理成表格，研究員答應和新規劃比較。這份工作沒有單一奇蹟。問題變成測量，測量形成限制，限制又成為能討論的東西。映書覺得市場安靜了些，不是問題消失，而是大家的談話開始有方向。

When the inspection report arrived, the team posted it beside the children's water model. The cracked pipes required repair, the drainage design needed a professional review, and the site could not safely return to full service at once. No one tried to turn that into a victory. Xiu-ying read the report twice, then moved her goods away from the damp wall without waiting to be asked. "I still want a proper roof," she told Yingshu. "I know," her daughter replied. "Now we have a better idea of what that roof must stand above."

檢查報告到達後，大家把它貼在孩子的水流模型旁。裂管必須修、排水設計要由專業人員檢核，而且不能立即全面恢復營業。沒有人把這當作勝利。秀英把報告讀了兩遍，主動把商品搬離潮濕牆邊。「我還是想要一個好屋頂。」她對映書說。「我知道，」女兒答，「現在我們比較清楚，屋頂底下必須先處理什麼。」
