A Number on the Wrong Line

1997，青禾市場的第二張招牌 · 第 9 / 25 章 · 3000–4000 · 英中對照 · 校訂中

用互動版查詞、朗讀與收藏

The first printed cost report arrived in an envelope with three attachments. Yingshu spread the pages on the stall table and saw that the estimated price of temporary storage had been placed under permanent construction. That made relocation appear cheaper than it really was. The clerk said it might be a clumsy accounting mistake, not a plan to mislead anyone. Yingshu agreed that the circumstance mattered. She marked the line, wrote the exact amount, and asked for a refund policy if vendors had already paid deposits based on the wrong figure. "An error can hurt people even without an enemy," she told A-Zhan.

第一份印刷成本報告裝在附有三份附件的信封裡。映書把紙攤在攤桌上，發現臨時倉儲費竟列在永久工程項目下，使搬遷看起來比實際便宜。書記說這可能只是笨拙的會計錯誤，不一定有人刻意誤導。映書同意應先釐清情況。她標出那行、寫下確切金額，並問若攤商已按錯誤數字繳訂金，退款規則是什麼。「沒有壞人，錯誤也可能傷人。」她告訴阿湛。

The accountant helped her compare the economic effect on each kind of stall. A baker needed daily electricity; a fruit seller could manage without it for a few hours; the fish seller depended on ice throughout the day. The report had treated all three as equal. "This is not a revolutionary discovery," the accountant said, "only work that should have been done before the estimate was published." Yingshu liked her modesty but was not content with the original estimate. She herself had once believed that a clever argument could solve the market's problems. Now she knew that accurate sums, checked patiently, could be more powerful than any speech.

會計師幫她比較不同攤位的經濟影響。麵包店每天需要電，水果攤可以短暫停電，魚販卻整天需要冰。報告把三者當成同樣條件。「這不是革命性的發現，」會計師說，「只是估價公開前本就該做的工作。」映書喜歡她的謙遜，卻不滿足於原先的估價。她以前以為聰明的辯論能解決市場問題，如今知道耐心核對的正確數字可能比任何演說有力。

During a break, a visiting sailor with a northern accent asked for crunchy peanuts and tea. He spoke of his homeland across the water, where markets had survived storms by moving to higher ground. Yingshu asked how people had paid for the move. He could not answer, but he mentioned a cooperative that shared cold storage. She wrote down the idea as a lead, not as proof. A-Zhan leaned over the counter to peep at her notes and received a gentle frown. "Stop spying on your own sister," she said. He answered with a wink and offered to carry the next box.

休息時，一位帶北方口音的水手來買香脆花生和茶。他談起海另一邊的故鄉，說當地市場靠搬到高地度過風暴。映書問搬遷費誰出，他不知道，但提到有合作社共用冷藏設備。她把這想法記作線索，不當作證明。阿湛彎過櫃檯偷瞄她的筆記，換來姐姐一個皺眉。「別偷看自己姐姐的東西。」她說。阿湛眨眨眼，說願意幫她搬下一箱。

At the school, a science teacher was preparing an exhibition on energy. One display explained nuclear power with a simple diagram, while another showed how a market could lose food during an electrical interruption. Yingshu borrowed the second chart, making sure not to confuse the two topics. A student wearing an earphone offered to copy the report onto a cassette tape for older listeners who had trouble reading fine print. The teacher instructed him to record only the approved text and label the tape with its date. A recorded correction might reach people whom the printed version missed.

學校裡，科學老師正準備能源展。一個展板用簡圖介紹核能，另一個展示市場停電時食物可能損失多少。映書借了第二張圖，刻意不把兩個主題混為一談。一名戴耳機的學生提議把報告錄成卡帶，讓看不清小字的長者也能聽。老師指示他只錄已核准的文字，並標明日期。錄音更正也許能傳達給印刷版沒照顧到的人。

The developer's office finally answered. It called the disputed amount "a minor classification issue" and denied responsibility for deposits collected by local agents. Yingshu did not accept the wording. She asked the town accountant to explain whether the money had moved at all and requested copies of the relevant receipts. If the figure was corrected, she wanted all public versions of the plan marked with a revision date. Otherwise, a vendor might keep relying on the old page. The request was tedious, but she could already hear customers repeating the misleading total as if it were certain.

開發公司終於回信，稱爭議金額只是「輕微分類問題」，並否認對地方代理人收取的訂金負責。映書不接受這種說法。她請鎮會計人員查清款項是否真正轉移，並要求相關收據影本。若數字修正，所有公開版本都應標上修訂日期，否則攤商仍可能依據舊頁做決定。這要求很繁瑣，但她已聽見顧客把錯誤總額當成確定的事四處轉述。

In the evening, Xiu-ying found her daughter eating cold noodles with one hand while checking receipts with the other. "You are allowed to have a life," she said. "I do have one," Yingshu answered, though she knew the reply was thin. She missed the city friends she had left and the steady wage she no longer earned. Her mother placed the old account book beside the new folder. The two books looked different, but both asked the same question: what did the family owe, and what could it safely promise? Yingshu closed them before midnight.

晚上，秀英看見女兒一手吃冷麵、一手對收據。「妳也可以有自己的生活。」她說。「我有啊。」映書回答，自己也知道這句話勉強。她想念城市的朋友和不再有的固定薪水。母親把舊帳本放在新資料夾旁。兩本外觀不同，問的卻是同一件事：家裡欠什麼，又能安全承諾什麼？映書在午夜前合上它們。

The next morning, the corrected report arrived. It did not prove that the whole redevelopment was dishonest. It did prove that a public estimate could change after scrutiny. Yingshu posted both versions side by side, with the difference circled and explained. A few residents called her stubborn. Others thanked her for refusing to turn uncertainty into accusation. A-Zhan, still embarrassed by his earlier peep at her notebook, offered to put new dates on every copy. She accepted. In a market built from ordinary transactions, trust would have to be made just as carefully, one receipt at a time.

隔天早上，更正後的報告送到了。它沒有證明整個改建計畫不誠實，卻證明公開估價經過檢視可以被修正。映書把新舊版並排張貼，圈出差異並解釋。有居民說她固執，也有人感謝她不把不確定變成指控。阿湛還為偷看筆記的事尷尬，主動幫每份副本標新日期。映書接受了。在靠日常交易建立的市場裡，信任也只能這樣小心地、一張收據一張收據地累積。
