比道路更老的地圖

A Map Older Than the Road

1997，青禾市場的第二張招牌 · 第 2 / 25 章 · 3000–4000 · 英中對照

用互動版查詞、朗讀與收藏

The town office smelled of paper, floor wax, and rain. Yingshu took a seat beneath a faded photograph of an old coastal fort. A messenger brought her a folder marked Qinghe Renewal. Inside was a modern plan drawn over a much older landscape. She gazed at the blue line behind the market and recognized it as the stream her grandmother had described. On the new drawing the line disappeared beneath a parking area. The clerk said the water had been diverted decades ago. When Yingshu asked to see the drainage report, he admitted the folder did not contain one.

鎮公所裡有紙張、地板蠟和雨水的味道。映書坐在一張老海防堡壘照片下等候。傳令員送來標著「青禾更新」的資料夾，裡面是覆蓋在舊地貌上的新規劃圖。她盯著市場後方的藍線，認出那是祖母說過的溪流。新圖上那條水線消失在停車場下面。職員說河道幾十年前就改道了；映書問排水報告在哪裡，他承認資料夾沒有。

A building consultant entered and called the old market a fossil from another economic age. Yingshu disliked the word but understood his point: broken pipes would not fix themselves. He said the combination of a new roof and modern drains would guarantee protection. "To what extent?" she asked. He had no figures ready. "A guarantee is not a feeling," Yingshu said, more gently than she felt. She did not demand that he abandon the plan. She asked him to revise his claim until it matched what engineers had actually tested. He wrote her question on the cover.

一位建築顧問走進來，稱老市場是另一個經濟年代留下的「化石」。映書不喜歡這個說法，但也知道破管線不會自己修好。顧問說新屋頂和現代排水系統結合，一定能保障安全。「保障到什麼程度？」她問。對方拿不出數字。「保證不是一種感覺。」映書盡量溫和地說。她沒有要求他放棄計畫，只請他把說法修正到符合工程師實際測試的內容。顧問把問題寫在封面上。

On the bus home, she sat beside Mr. Huang, who had worked at the nearby rice mill for forty years. He remembered when the stream flooded the mill yard, and he had kept rainfall measurements in a notebook. The figures were delicate because different years had been recorded with different instruments. Yingshu asked if she could copy them, promising to retain the original dates and the notes about faulty equipment. On the seat ahead, a school poster showed an atom with a tiny center. "Atomic science studies things too small for us to see," Mr. Huang said. "Drainage is different, but a small measurement error can also change the result." Yingshu smiled and returned to the rainfall figures.

回程公車上，她遇到在附近碾米廠工作四十年的黃先生。他記得溪水淹過米廠院子，也保存了雨量筆記。那些數字很難直接比較，因為不同年份用過不同儀器。映書問能不能抄錄，並承諾保留原始日期及設備故障註記。前方座位貼著學校海報，上面畫著中心極小的原子。「原子科學研究人眼看不見的微小事物，」黃先生說，「排水雖然不同，但量測時小小的誤差，也能改變結果。」映書笑了笑，繼續看雨量數字。

At the market, a new vendor named Samir was checking his immigration documents against the business permit he kept in a locked drawer. He explained that he had lived in Qinghe for seven years and that the market had been his first place of work. Some neighbors discussed the plan as though only families born in town belonged there. Yingshu listened to Samir's account of the long voyage that had brought his parents across the sea, and asked which exit he used at night. The question was ordinary, but it gave their conversation an intimate honesty. Safety could not depend on a person's birthplace.

市場裡，新攤商沙米爾正把移民證件與鎖在抽屜裡的營業許可核對。他說自己已在青禾住七年，市場是他的第一份工作。有些鄰居談起改建，彷彿只有在鎮上出生的人才有資格留下。映書聽他說父母跨海來此的漫長旅程，接著問他夜間從哪個出口離開。這問題很平常，卻讓談話變得真誠而貼近。安全不該取決於出生地。

Xiu-ying spread the old and new maps across a table. A murmur rose from the vendors as they noticed that the proposed replacement stalls were farther from the morning bus stop. For sellers who carried baskets on foot, ten extra minutes could make a productive day impossible. Mei-ling placed a wet towel over one corner so the paper would not blow away. She traced the hidden stream with one finger. "If they build over it without a proper drain, we may get beautiful shops and flooded floors," she said. The others did not argue. They asked how to check.

秀英把新舊兩張圖攤在桌上。攤商發現新攤位離早班公車站更遠，四周響起低低議論。對徒步搬籃子的攤商而言，多走十分鐘就可能失去最有生產力的早晨。美玲用濕毛巾壓住紙角，沿著被遮住的溪線滑過手指。「沒有妥善排水，店面再漂亮，也可能天天淹水。」大家沒有立即爭吵，而是問要如何查證。

That night, Yingshu borrowed an illustrated science book from the school library. It described how rain moved through soil, stone, and concrete, and how a narrow channel could change the direction of an entire flow. She was not an engineer, so she marked questions rather than conclusions. Her mother watched her read and said the work seemed to have a psychological effect: Yingshu looked less frightened once the problem had names. Yingshu agreed. Fear had made the plan seem enormous; a map gave her several smaller problems that people might solve one at a time.

那晚，映書向學校圖書館借了一本圖解科學書，裡面說明雨水如何穿過泥土、石頭與混凝土，以及狹窄水道如何改變整股水流。她不是工程師，因此標記的是問題而非結論。秀英看著女兒讀書，說這工作似乎有心理上的效果：問題有了名字，映書就不再那麼害怕。映書承認。恐懼讓改建計畫看似龐大到無法碰觸；地圖卻把它拆成一個個或許能解決的小問題。

Before sleeping, she copied three lines into the new notebook: the old watercourse, the missing drainage report, and the distance to the bus stop. She enclosed a note requesting the rainfall records and addressed it to the engineering office. A handwritten letter would take longer than a telephone call, but it would leave a record. The following morning she would collect the first replies from vendors about their daily routes. Nobody had offered her a complete solution. For the first time, she did not expect one. She only wanted each promise to survive a careful question.

睡前她把三件事抄入新筆記本：舊水道、缺少的排水報告、以及到公車站的距離。她附上請求雨量紀錄的便條，寄往工程課。手寫信比打電話慢，卻會留下紀錄。隔天早上，她打算蒐集攤商每天行走的路線。沒有人給她完整解方，而她第一次不再期待有人一次解決所有問題。她只希望每個承諾都經得起仔細追問。
