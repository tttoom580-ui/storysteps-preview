The Vessel of Forgotten Dreams

Soren's maps led to a distribution hub beside the river. Workers there fastened metal cases onto an oval vessel that traveled below the city through maintenance canals. Every case held a hardware unit containing memories collected from sleeping residents. Zhixia felt absolute dread when she recognized her own childhood voice in one sample. A miner named Ivo explained that the engines used old underground tunnels once dug for coal. He had kept a record of every shipment because his union required discipline and efficiency. Now those records could reveal how long the extraction program had operated without consent.

索倫的地圖指向河邊的配送中心。工人把金屬箱固定在一艘橢圓形水下載具上，讓它沿城市維修水道移動。每只箱子裡都有儲存沉睡市民記憶的硬體。知夏竟在一個樣本裡聽見自己童年的聲音，感到絕對的恐懼。礦工艾沃解釋，引擎沿用以前挖煤留下的地下通道。他因工會要求紀律與效率，保存每次運送紀錄，如今那些紀錄能證明抽取計畫在未經同意下運作多久。

The shipment records also contained a notice promising to renew each donor's agreement automatically. Ivo explained that nobody had actually asked the donors whether they wanted another year of treatment. A checkbox on the intake form counted as lifelong permission. Zhixia showed him how to compare the signatures against the records of patients who had been unconscious at the time. The contradiction was obvious. Some forms had been signed days after the patients were admitted; others carried identical handwriting across different names. Ivo rubbed his forehead, ashamed that he had transported the cases for so long without questioning what they held.

運送紀錄還附著一份通知，承諾自動續簽每位捐贈者的同意。艾沃說根本沒有人實際問過捐贈者願不願再接受一年療程。入院表上一個方格竟被當成終身許可。知夏教他把簽名和當時昏迷患者的紀錄對照，矛盾非常明顯。有些表格在病人入院後數天才簽，有些不同姓名竟有一模一樣字跡。艾沃搓著額頭，羞愧於自己多年運送箱子卻從沒追問裡頭裝什麼。

They boarded the vessel disguised as maintenance staff. Kai fastened the door behind them while Zhixia inserted a copied access chip into the control panel. The ship's walls displayed illustrations of cheerful volunteers donating energy, but the real manifests told a different story. Most donors were children, injured workers, and elderly people who had been promised treatment in exchange for a signature they did not understand. Zhixia felt grief rise like cold water. Kai reached for her hand, then stopped, respecting the anger she needed to feel. Instead he helped photograph the labels, one careful frame at a time.

他們偽裝成維修人員登船。凱固定艙門，知夏把複製門禁晶片插入控制盤。船壁畫著快樂志工捐贈能量的插圖，真正的運送清單卻不同：多數捐贈者是孩童、受傷勞工與老人，曾被承諾只要簽下自己不懂的字，就能換取治療。知夏胸口的悲傷像冷水上湧。凱伸手想握住她，卻停下來，尊重她需要感受的怒氣；他只是幫忙一格一格拍下標籤。

An alarm sounded near the northern lock. The vessel had been scheduled for automatic renewal of its cargo license, and the system demanded recognition from a senior officer. Ivo offered a realistic solution: stop the motors and wait for the patrol to pass. Soren worried that delays would expose them. Zhixia studied the lock's mechanical rhythm, then copied the access signal through her pulse. The panel accepted it for nine seconds—enough to open the emergency hatch. They escaped into a dry tunnel carrying one complete case. None called the operation perfection. It was simply proof that an ordinary worker's knowledge could defeat a system designed to exclude him.

北方水閘附近響起警報。載具即將自動更新貨運許可，系統要求高階官員認證。艾沃提出務實辦法：停機等巡邏過去。索倫擔心耽擱會暴露。知夏研究水閘的機械節奏，再用脈息模仿門禁訊號。控制盤接受了九秒，剛好足以開啟緊急艙口。他們帶著一只完整箱子逃入乾燥隧道。沒有人說行動完美；這只是證明，普通工人的知識足以擊敗專為排除他而設的系統。

When the vessel reached the deepest lock, Zhixia briefly heard Yan singing through the pipes. She pressed her ear to the wall, unsure whether the sound was real or a memory released by the hardware. Kai asked her to move away from the dangerous machinery, but she begged for one more second. The melody stopped before its final note. She wrote down the missing beat and kept walking. Her sister had left instructions scattered across a city that preferred to forget her. Finding those pieces was painful, yet it made the distance between them feel measurable rather than endless.

載具到達最深水閘時，知夏突然聽見林晏的歌聲從管線傳來。她把耳朵貼上牆，不確定那是真聲音，還是硬體釋出的記憶。凱要她遠離危險機械，她請他再給一秒。旋律在最後音符前停住。她寫下缺少的那一拍，繼續往前。姊姊把指示撒在一座偏好遺忘她的城市各處，找到碎片很痛苦，卻也讓姐妹間的距離變得可以衡量，而非無窮無盡。

Back at Lower Nine, Vela opened the case and found thousands of tiny recordings. A gracious voice asked people to sleep; a child's laugh followed; then there was only static. Zhixia recognized an unfamiliar hawk insignia on the lid. It belonged to the city founder's private guard, a group supposedly dissolved decades earlier. Vela resolved to trace its surviving members. She thanked Ivo for preserving the manifests, but he shook his head. "I was never brave," he said. "I just hated throwing records away." Zhixia smiled sadly. Sometimes the smallest habits kept truth alive until someone was ready to recognize it.

回到第九下城，薇拉打開箱子，發現數千段微型錄音：一個優雅聲音要人睡下，接著是孩子笑聲，最後只剩雜訊。知夏注意到箱蓋有陌生的鷹徽，那是據說數十年前解散的建城者私衛隊標記。薇拉決定追查仍存活的成員。她感謝艾沃保存運送清單，艾沃卻搖頭：「我從沒勇敢過，只是討厭丟紀錄。」知夏難過地微笑。有時，最小的習慣能讓真相活到有人準備認出它的那天。
