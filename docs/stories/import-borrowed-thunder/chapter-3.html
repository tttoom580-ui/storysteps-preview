屏障外的學校

The School Beyond the Barrier

Kai led Zhixia past the northern barrier toward a mountainous district that the city rarely showed on official maps. The air there was mild, yet the conditions inside the school were brutal. Broken heaters, empty medicine drawers, and crowded classrooms testified to years of neglect. A retired philosopher named Teacher Shen was keeping a record of children whose pulse had vanished after academy tests. Some had suffered accidental injuries; others returned silent, unable to recognize their families. Shen believed the cases formed a pattern, but he lacked the intelligence network to evaluate them safely. Zhixia showed him Yan's numbers. For the first time, his calm expression changed.

凱帶知夏穿過北方屏障，來到官方地圖幾乎不畫的山區。那裡氣候溫和，學校內的處境卻十分殘酷。壞掉的暖爐、空藥櫃與擠滿人的教室，都是多年忽視的證據。退休思想家沈老師記錄著參加學院測驗後失去脈息的孩子。有些意外受傷，有些回家後沉默，連家人也認不出。沈老師相信這些案例有共同模式，卻沒有足夠的情報網安全評估。知夏拿出姊姊留下的數字，他平靜的表情第一次變了。

"Your sister was asking for reform," Shen said. "They called it revenge because that word made her easier to condemn." He took a twig and drew three circles in the dirt, explaining how the city divided residents into protectors, workers, and energy reserves. Zhixia wanted to argue that a person could belong to more than one circle. Shen urged her to keep the thought. The ruling council had a tendency to punish anyone who offended its categories, and even a grave injury could be hidden with the right paperwork. Through the classroom window, Zhixia watched a boy blink in time with the thunder shield. She realized her strange hearing was not hers alone.

「妳姊姊要求的是改革，」沈老師說，「他們把她說成復仇者，因為這個詞比較方便定罪。」他拿一根小樹枝，在泥地上畫三個圈，說明城市如何把居民分成保衛者、勞工與能源儲備。知夏想反駁，人明明可以同時屬於不只一個圈。沈老師要她記住這個想法。統治議會一向傾向處罰冒犯分類的人，連嚴重傷勢都能用文件掩蓋。透過教室窗戶，她看見一個男孩的眨眼正與雷盾同步。她終於明白自己的奇怪聽力並非獨有。

The school had no official emergency plan that matched its actual rooms. Its printed chart assumed the eastern exit was open, although the gate had been locked for years. Zhixia and Kai walked the grounds before the afternoon lesson, counting windows and testing doors. The children followed them, offering practical advice that adults had never requested. One knew a dry passage below the kitchen; another knew which stairs became slippery in rain. By the time they finished, the students had drawn a safer map than the district administration had produced in ten years. Zhixia could not decide whether to feel proud of them or angry at the neglect that made their work necessary.

學校沒有符合實際空間的緊急計畫。印好的圖表假設東側出口能開，然而那道門已鎖了多年。知夏與凱在下午上課前走遍校園，清點窗戶、測試門鎖。孩子們一路跟著，提供大人從未問過的實用建議。有人知道廚房底下的乾燥通道，有人知道哪段樓梯下雨會滑。走完時，學生畫出的安全地圖已比區公所十年的成果更好。知夏不知道該為孩子驕傲，還是為逼他們做這些事的忽視而憤怒。

At lunch Shen invited them to sit with the teachers instead of taking a special guest table. Meals were simple, and the conversation moved between serious stories and jokes about the school's stubborn heating system. Kai was surprised to discover that several instructors had once been ranked fighters. They had left their careers when ordered to ignore injuries caused by mandatory tests. Their decision had cost them homes and social standing, yet none asked to be called heroic. Shen explained that a community survived through repeated ordinary choices: tell the truth, share what you have, come back tomorrow. Zhixia wrote those three actions beneath the third beat in her notebook.

午餐時沈老師請兩人和教師同桌，不設特別貴賓席。飯菜簡單，大家一會兒談沉重往事，一會兒笑學校難搞的暖氣。凱驚訝得知幾名教師以前也是有等級的武者。他們因被要求無視強制測驗造成的傷害而離開職涯，付出住房與社會地位的代價，卻沒有一人要求被稱為英雄。沈老師說社群靠反覆的平凡選擇活下去：說真話、分享所有、明天再回來。知夏把這三件事寫在筆記第三拍的下面。

A warning siren suddenly rose from the valley. The school's old air filter failed, and gray dust began to choke the younger children. Kai lifted the damaged vent clear, while Zhixia guided them outside by following the different rhythms of their breathing. A teacher stumbled and struck her head; others stood frozen, afraid to break the evacuation rules. Zhixia did not wait for permission. She tore cloth into masks, ordered the strongest students to carry water, and kept calling names until everyone answered. When it was over, Shen expressed his gratitude without smiling. "Now you see the cost of a barrier," he said. "Sometimes it keeps danger out. Sometimes it keeps help away."

山谷忽然響起警報。學校老舊的空氣過濾器壞了，灰塵讓年幼孩子幾乎窒息。凱搬開損壞的風管，知夏則循著孩子們不同的呼吸節奏，帶大家出去。有位老師跌倒撞到頭；其他人卻怕違反撤離規則而僵在原地。知夏不等批准，撕布做口罩，請較強壯的學生搬水，一直點名直到每個人回應。事後沈老師嚴肅地道謝：「現在妳知道屏障的代價了。有時它擋住危險，有時它也擋住援助。」

At dusk Shen played them a short musical composition that Yan had recorded on the school's battered piano. Beneath the melody lay the same forbidden third beat that Zhixia had heard inside the copper dial. One boy said Yan had taught him a trick: instead of forcing your pulse to grow louder, listen for the smaller voices beside it. His words struck Zhixia harder than the academy's displays of power. She had spent years believing she was the only person without a rank. Here, in a school officially called useless, she found people who could hear the city clearly. Kai looked toward the illuminated towers, and for once he seemed unsure which side of the barrier deserved to be called strong.

黃昏時，沈老師播放一小段姊姊在學校破鋼琴上錄下的樂曲。旋律底下竟藏著知夏在銅盤裡聽見的第三拍。一名男孩說，林晏教過他一個技巧：別拼命把自己的脈息變大聲，而要聽見旁邊更小的聲音。這句話比學院炫耀的力量更震撼知夏。她多年來一直相信自己是唯一沒有等級的人；如今卻在被官方稱為無用的學校裡，找到一群能清楚聽見城市的人。凱望向發光的高塔，第一次不確定屏障哪一邊才該被稱為強者。
