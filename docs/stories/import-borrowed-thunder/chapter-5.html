第九下城的女巫

The Witch of Lower Nine

借雷之城 · 第 5 / 25 章 · 3000–4000 · 英中對照

用互動版查詞、朗讀與收藏

The city's welfare agency sent Zhixia a notice ordering her to abandon the opera hall and report for mandatory retraining. Instead she followed Mina's directions to Lower Nine, a district where discarded machines and old decorations covered the streets. The residents called their engineer Aunt Vela a witch because she could make broken devices behave like living creatures. Vela had adopted dozens of children who failed the academy's assessment. She taught them to repair water pumps and read official notices rather than hide from the world. "Fear makes people easy to govern," she told Zhixia. "But a conscience is harder to frighten."

城市福利機構送來通知，命令知夏放棄歌劇院工作、接受強制再訓練。她反而照米娜給的方向，前往街上滿是廢棄機器與舊裝飾的第九下城。居民稱工程師薇拉阿姨為「女巫」，因為她能讓破設備像活物一樣動起來。薇拉收養了許多學院測驗落榜的孩子，教他們修水泵、讀公文，而不是躲起來。「恐懼使人容易被統治，」她對知夏說，「但良心沒那麼容易被嚇住。」

Vela held a reception for new children every month, though she owned neither a proper hall nor enough chairs. The children decorated the walls with scenery drawn from places they remembered: beaches, train stations, small apartments, and mountains beneath unfamiliar skies. One painting showed the city as a giant mouth swallowing tiny figures. The image seemed to horrify Kai more than any threat the council had made. He asked the painter why she had chosen it. "Because grown-ups say the city is hungry," the girl replied. Vela did not correct the metaphor. She only asked whether the child wanted to draw a door in the mouth, so the people inside could find a way out.

薇拉每個月都為新來的孩子辦迎新會，雖然既沒有像樣大廳，也沒有足夠椅子。孩子用記憶中的風景畫裝飾牆面：海灘、車站、小公寓和陌生天空下的山。一張畫把城市畫成吞噬小人的巨大嘴巴，凱看了竟比聽到議會威脅還驚恐。他問孩子為何這樣畫。「因為大人說城市餓了。」女孩回答。薇拉沒有糾正隱喻，只問她願不願意在嘴裡畫一扇門，讓裡面的人能逃出。

In her workshop, Vela showed them a primitive device that measured pulse interaction rather than raw strength. The result horrified Kai. Zhixia, officially unranked, created a stronger cooperative field with three children than the champion could produce alone. Vela explained the fundamental flaw of the rankings: they measured how loudly one person could command energy, not how well people could share it. An influential congress member had once asked her to keep that finding secret. She refused, and her laboratory was shut down. Her career had ended, but the rescued children had become her real research team.

薇拉在工作坊展示一臺測量脈息「互動」而非原始強度的簡陋裝置。結果讓凱震驚。官方判為零級的知夏，和三個孩子一起產生的協同場，竟比冠軍獨自發出的更強。薇拉解釋等級制度的根本缺陷：它衡量一個人能多大聲命令能量，卻不衡量人能如何共享。曾有位極具影響力的議員要求她保密，她拒絕後實驗室就被關了。她失去事業，受她救助的孩子卻成了真正的研究團隊。

Their lesson was interrupted by a robber trying to steal the workshop batteries. Kai caught him with one effortless movement, but Vela stopped him from calling the police. The man had a sick daughter and had mistaken a box of decorations for medical supplies. His behavior was shameful, she said, yet punishment alone would not solve the need that drove it. She offered medicine and asked him to repair the damaged lock. Zhixia watched him accept the task with trembling hands. She cherished this district's refusal to divide people neatly into villains and heroes, even when its generosity made daily life harder.

一名搶匪闖進工作坊想偷電池，打斷了課程。凱一下就制伏他，薇拉卻阻止報警。男人有個生病的女兒，把一箱裝飾品錯認成醫療物資。薇拉說他的行為可恥，但只有懲罰解決不了背後的需要。她提供藥物，要求他修好被弄壞的鎖。知夏看著他雙手發抖地接下工作。她珍惜這個地方不急著把人分成惡人與英雄的態度，即使這份慷慨讓生活更不容易。

After the children slept, Vela explained how Lower Nine had been built. It began as a temporary shelter for injured construction workers, then grew into a district nobody wanted to acknowledge. Residents repaired pipes, kept clinics open, and taught apprentices without any of the resources granted to prestigious schools. The city described them as dependent, yet every power failure sent officials down to request their help. Zhixia understood that the district's greatest skill was not its ingenious machinery. It was the habit of refusing to leave a problem to someone else merely because helping brought no reward. She wondered whether that habit might eventually save the entire city.

孩子睡後，薇拉解釋第九下城的起源。它原本是受傷建築工人的臨時庇護所，後來成了沒有人願承認的街區。居民修水管、維持診所、教徒弟，卻沒有名校資源。城市稱他們依賴他人，每次停電時官員卻都下來求助。知夏明白這區最大的本事不是巧妙機械，而是遇到問題時，即使沒有回報也不把責任丟給別人的習慣。她想，也許正是這習慣，有一天能救下整座城市。

That night Vela revealed why she had waited for Zhixia. Yan had built the cooperative meter before she disappeared, and its last data file included a map of the city's central engine. The likely outcome of the next power increase was not greater safety but a wave of terror beneath the outer neighborhoods. Zhixia wanted to rush the tower at once. Vela discouraged that plan. "An agency survives because a thousand people maintain it," she said. "If you want to change the structure, you must understand the people inside it." The old engineer gave Zhixia a new task: enter the academy, learn its secrets, and come back with allies rather than ashes.

那晚薇拉終於說出為何等知夏來。協同測量器是林晏失蹤前做的，最後一份資料包含城市中央引擎地圖。下一次加大供能很可能不會帶來更多安全，而會把恐怖災潮推向外圍街區。知夏想立刻衝進高塔，薇拉卻勸阻：「機構能存在，是因為有上千人維持它。妳想改變結構，就得理解裡面的人。」老工程師交給知夏新任務：進入學院，學會它的祕密，帶著盟友回來，而不是只帶回灰燼。
