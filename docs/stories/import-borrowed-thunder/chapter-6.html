建城紀念日

Anniversary of the Founding

借雷之城 · 第 6 / 25 章 · 3000–4000 · 英中對照 · 校訂中

用互動版查詞、朗讀與收藏

Thunder City's hundredth anniversary began with a campaign celebrating the ruling dynasty of martial families. Academy students marched behind a conductor who controlled their steps through a high-frequency cord attached to the plaza speakers. Zhixia entered the procession as an assistant to Kai. She wore a coarse training jacket and tried to imitate the diligent students surrounding her. Above them, portraits of rulers promised that courage would always be rewarded. Beneath the polished road, however, hundreds of needy citizens performed unseen labor to keep the shield alive. Zhixia could hear their tired pulses slipping between the official drums.

霆京建城百年紀念日以統治武學世家的宣傳活動開場。學院學生在指揮者帶領下行進，腳步受廣場喇叭接出的高頻導線控制。知夏以凱的助理身分混入隊伍，穿上粗糙訓練外套，模仿周圍勤奮的學員。高處統治者肖像承諾勇氣必受獎賞，光滑道路下卻有數百名窮困市民做著無人看見的勞動，只為讓護盾繼續運作。知夏聽得到他們疲倦的脈息從官方鼓聲間漏出。

Zhixia's new position allowed her to visit the trainees' dining room. There she met an apprentice whose parents worked beneath the city and who sent most of his wages home. He had memorized every training exercise but had never been allowed to see a map of the lower plants. She asked whether he wanted to learn what his parents did. He said yes, then glanced at the camera above the doorway. For all the academy's speeches about independence, fear followed students even to dinner. Zhixia understood that sharing information would require quiet protection as well as loud celebration. She gave the apprentice an anonymous page from the miners' records and asked him to read it somewhere safe.

新身分讓知夏能進學員餐廳。她在那裡認識一名學徒，父母在下城工作，他把大多數薪水寄回家。學徒背得出每項訓練動作，卻從未被允許看地下工廠的地圖。知夏問他想不想知道父母在做什麼。他說想，接著瞥向門上的攝影機。學院不停談獨立，恐懼卻連晚餐都跟著學生。知夏明白，分享資訊除了公開慶祝，也需要安靜保護。她交給他一頁匿名礦工紀錄，請他到安全地方再讀。

The academy required an evaluation of every recruit before the celebration ended. A stern instructor assured Zhixia that nobody with zero rank would pass. Vela had warned her not to copy the champions; imitation would simply conceal her unusual gift. Instead Zhixia studied the test's frequency and devised a quiet technique that reflected incoming blows into the practice floor. A cushion of pale light formed at her feet like foam on a dark wave. The judges stared. One called it cheating; another called it a new branch of psychology. Kai knew what it really meant. Her ability could coordinate an entire team, and the academy had never thought to measure that.

慶典結束前，學院要求對每位新人進行評估。嚴厲教官向知夏保證，零級者不可能過關。薇拉提醒過她，不要複製冠軍，模仿只會掩蓋她的特殊天賦。知夏於是研究測驗頻率，自行設計安靜的技巧，把迎面攻擊引回訓練地板。腳邊形成一層淡光墊，像暗浪上的泡沫。評審都愣住了，一人說她作弊，另一人說這屬於新心理學分支。凱卻知道真正意義：她能協調整支隊伍，而學院從來沒打算測量這種能力。

A mineral detector began to shriek when she stepped off the platform. The academy's power source contained stones taken from the mountains, but Vela's notes suggested that the stones merely carried human pulse. The detector reacted to the stolen energy still trapped in Zhixia's sleeve after she touched the copper dial. A young engineer quietly urged her to leave before officials demanded an explanation. Zhixia asked him why an institution that praised courageous action punished curiosity. He had no answer. Yet he gave her a maintenance schedule, carefully marked with the hours when the engine room was empty.

知夏離開平台時，一臺礦物偵測器忽然尖叫。學院能源含有從山區開採的石頭，但薇拉的筆記說那些石頭只負責傳導人的脈息。偵測器感應到她碰過銅盤後，仍殘留在袖口的被竊能量。一位年輕工程師催她趕快離開，免得官員追問。知夏反問，為什麼歌頌勇敢行動的機構卻懲罰好奇心？他答不上來，卻把維修時間表交給她，特別標明引擎室沒人的時段。

Before sleeping, Kai showed Zhixia the chamber where the academy stored worn training equipment. He confessed that his first championship had depended on a borrowed pulse booster. Nobody had told him where its power came from, and he had never asked. The admission embarrassed him more than losing a match would have. Zhixia told him that regret could become useful only if it changed his next decision. Together they marked safe exit routes on the dormitory plan, using ordinary pencils instead of glowing displays. It felt almost foolishly small beside the empire of towers, but each line represented someone who might someday need to find a door quickly.

睡前，凱帶知夏去學院的舊訓練器材儲藏間。他坦承第一次奪冠曾依靠借來的脈息增幅器。沒人告訴他力量從哪裡來，而他也沒問。承認這件事，比輸掉比賽更令他難堪。知夏說後悔只有改變下一個決定才有用。他們一起用普通鉛筆在宿舍圖上標示安全出口，而不是使用炫目顯示器。這舉動和高塔帝國相比幾乎微不足道，但每條線都代表將來可能急著找門的人。

At night Kai and Zhixia stood on the roof of the dormitory. Fireworks colored the city, but far below the outer districts were almost dark. Kai confessed that he had once accepted the dynasty's story because it was simpler than questioning his own success. Zhixia did not shame him. "We can complicate our lives by admitting the truth," she said, "or we can complicate other people's lives by refusing it." Kai laughed softly at her seriousness. Then they agreed on a plan: he would become her formal training partner, and she would teach him how to listen before striking. For both of them, courage was beginning to mean something different from victory.

夜裡凱與知夏站在宿舍屋頂。煙火染亮城市，遠處外區卻幾乎全黑。凱承認自己曾接受世家的故事，因為那比質疑自身成功容易。知夏沒有羞辱他：「我們可以承認真相，讓自己的生活複雜一些；也可以拒絕真相，讓別人的生活變得更難。」凱對她的認真輕笑。他們最後約定：由凱成為她的正式陪練，她則教他出手前先傾聽。對兩人而言，勇氣開始不再只等於獲勝。
