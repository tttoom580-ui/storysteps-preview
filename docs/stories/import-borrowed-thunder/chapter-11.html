摩天大樓的氫氣

Hydrogen in the Skyscraper

The convention had also invited students to discuss the city's commerce and its dependence on the energy towers. One student suggested that anyone who questioned the system should simply leave. An older shopkeeper replied that moving elsewhere was a privilege, not a universal choice. His family had operated the same bakery for four generations, and every new safety fee cut into their ability to pay workers. Zhixia listened as an economist tried to explain how the government had shifted costs onto neighborhoods with the least political influence. The argument was complicated, but the shopkeeper's final question was not: "Why must those with the smallest choice carry the largest risk?"

年會也邀學生討論城市商業與對能源高塔的依賴。一個學生提議，不喜歡制度的人大可以離開。年長店主回答，能搬到別處是一種特權，不是人人都有選擇。他家四代經營麵包店，每一筆新增安全費都壓縮付薪水的能力。知夏聽經濟學家解釋政府如何把成本轉嫁給政治影響力最低的社區。爭論很複雜，店主最後的問題卻不難懂：「為什麼選擇最少的人，得承擔最大的風險？」

The winter hurricane reached Thunder City at noon, bending the banners around the tallest skyscraper. Inside, a convention of energy manufacturers was celebrating a new hydrogen storage system. Kai's invitation gave Zhixia access to the hall, where speeches boasted of recovery from earlier disasters. An informative display explained how energy could be manufactured and stored with consistent efficiency. Zhixia noticed one missing diagram: none showed where the pulse came from. When she asked the presenter, he accused her of ignorance and directed guards toward her. Kai stepped between them, insisting that a public convention required public questions.

冬季颶風在正午抵達霆京，吹彎最高摩天大樓四周的旗幟。樓內正舉行能源製造商年會，慶祝新的氫氣儲存系統。凱的邀請函讓知夏進入會場，裡頭演說吹噓城市已從先前災害復原。展板仔細介紹能量如何以穩定效率製造與儲存，知夏卻發現缺了一張圖：沒有任何一張說明脈息來源。她提問時，解說員指控她無知，示意警衛前來。凱擋在中間，堅持公開會議就該容許公開提問。

A loyal engineer named Soren offered to show them the equipment after the crowd left. He had worked on resistance circuits meant to prevent a total blackout. Years ago, he had contributed to the first prototype with Yan, and his assurance of safety had helped win funding. Now he looked troubled. The hydrogen tanks were ordinary, he explained; the suspicious part was a smaller device that converted human dreams into electrical output. Its inventor had labeled the process "relaxation recovery," claiming donors slept comfortably throughout. Soren had believed the term until patients began waking with memories missing. He had reported the misfortune, but his supervisor advised silence.

一位忠心的工程師索倫提出散場後帶他們看設備。他曾參與製作防止大停電的電阻迴路，幾年前還與林晏共同研發第一代原型，當時他的安全保證幫計畫取得資金。如今他滿臉不安。他說氫氣罐很普通，真正可疑的是一臺把人類夢境轉成電力的小裝置。發明者把它稱為「放鬆式回收」，聲稱捐贈者全程睡得舒適。索倫一直相信，直到病人醒來後失去記憶。他通報了不幸事故，主管卻勸他保持沉默。

Zhixia asked whether the records could expose the truth. Soren led them to a locked cabinet containing reports on troublesome test cases. One patient had forgotten his daughter; another no longer recognized the street where he grew up. Some complained, while others believed the official explanation that stress caused the confusion. Zhixia understood why. The city had surrounded them with convincing words for so long that its mistakes looked like their own weaknesses. She copied the files with Soren's permission. At the bottom of the final page she found Yan's note: "A shield that survives by emptying the people inside it has already failed."

知夏問資料能否揭露真相。索倫帶他們走到上鎖的櫃子，裡面是棘手測試案例報告：有人忘記女兒，有人認不出成長的街道。部分病人抱怨，另一些則相信官方所說「壓力造成混亂」。知夏明白原因。城市長年用令人信服的措辭包圍他們，讓制度的錯誤看起來像個人的弱點。她獲得索倫同意後複製檔案，並在最後一頁底部看見林晏的筆記：「若護盾必須掏空裡面的人才能存活，它其實早就失敗。」

As the storm intensified, the upper building began to sway. The presenters fled, but Soren refused to abandon the workers trapped in the manufacturing floor. Zhixia used her listening ability to locate a damaged support cable, while Kai and the guards carried people to the stairs. Their resistance to panic impressed the employees more than the convention's earlier assurances. When everyone was safe, Soren made a decision. He would leave his prestigious post and join Vela's team below the city. "A loyal worker must know what he is loyal to," he said. "I thought it was the building. It should have been the people."

暴風加強，高樓開始搖晃。講者紛紛逃走，索倫卻不肯丟下受困製造樓層的工人。知夏循著聲音找出損壞的支撐纜線，凱與警衛則把人送下樓。員工看到他們抵抗恐慌的行動，比先前年會的安全保證更受感動。人都安全後，索倫決定辭去優渥職務，加入下城的薇拉團隊。「忠心的員工要知道自己忠於什麼，」他說，「我以為是大樓，其實應該是人。」

After the storm evacuation, Soren walked Zhixia through the building one last time. They passed offices where employees had pinned family pictures above their desks and laboratories where apprentices had left unfinished cups of tea. He told her he had loved his work, even as he came to distrust the people who directed it. Zhixia understood. Machines were not evil because powerful people had misused them, just as trained fighters were not evil because the academy rewarded cruelty. The distinction mattered if the city was ever to rebuild. They would need engineers who could repair what was damaged rather than destroy everything associated with the old system.

暴風撤離後，索倫最後一次帶知夏走過大樓。他們經過員工在桌上釘家人照片的辦公室，也走過徒弟留下未喝完茶的實驗室。他說自己熱愛工作，即使逐漸不信任指揮工作的人。知夏懂。機器不會因權勢者濫用就變邪惡，就如學院獎賞殘酷，不代表每個受訓武者都壞。這個區別對未來重建很重要。城市需要能修復受損事物的工程師，而不只是摧毀所有與舊制度有關之物的人。
