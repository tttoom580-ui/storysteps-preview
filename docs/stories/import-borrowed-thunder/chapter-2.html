登記冊裡的簽名

An Autograph in the Registry

借雷之城 · 第 2 / 25 章 · 3000–4000 · 英中對照

The academy's entrance hall was built to inspire confidence. A giant mural showed the first defenders standing beneath a perfect blue sky, while speakers repeated stories about poor children who had risen to greatness. Zhixia searched the paintings for anyone using a wheelchair or carrying an ordinary toolbox. She found none. When an attendant asked why she was staring, she said she wanted to know whether the academy had ever honored someone for preventing an accident rather than winning a fight. The attendant looked confused. That question did not appear on the official tour, and there was no display he could point to in reply.

學院入口大廳專為激發信心而建。巨大壁畫描繪第一代守護者站在完美藍天下，喇叭不斷重播窮孩子如何成為強者的故事。知夏在畫裡找坐輪椅的人或拿普通工具箱的人，卻一個也找不到。接待員問她為何一直看，她說想知道學院是否曾表揚防止意外的人，而不只是打贏比賽的人。接待員困惑地望著她。官方導覽從不問這種問題，也沒有展品能回答。

The academy's public timetable promised that every student, including an immigrant's child, could prosper through effort. Zhixia arrived at the entrance with an initial invitation from an unknown sponsor and watched a prominent champion sign an autograph for smiling parents. The convenience of the system was obvious: one test, one rank, one future. Its cruelty was just as obvious. A girl with a damaged leg was shifted into a service queue without anyone reading her excellent written scores. Zhixia disguised herself as a maintenance assistant, tying elastic bands around her sleeves, and slipped toward the assessment room to learn what happened to applicants whose strength had no number.

武學院公開的時間表保證，任何人——包括移民子女——都能靠努力興旺發展。知夏帶著匿名贊助者的初步邀請來到入口，看著一位知名冠軍替歡笑的家長簽名。制度的便利一目了然：一次考試、一個等級、一種未來；它的殘酷也同樣明顯。一名腿部受傷的女孩，即使筆試極好，仍被直接移往勞務隊伍。知夏把自己偽裝成維修人員，用鬆緊帶綁好袖口，溜向測驗室，想弄清力量沒有數字的人究竟會被送去哪裡。

Behind a glass wall, a mechanical paw pressed against each candidate's wrist. The machine collected more than muscle data; Zhixia saw thin blue threads moving from skin to a sealed chamber. A technician dismissed her questions as harmless mischief until an explosive pulse cracked the glass. The boy inside screamed. The official guide offered a cheerful remark about minor equipment faults, but Zhixia grabbed an emergency lever marked PREVENTION and shut the machine down. The room fell silent. A supervisor warned that touching registered devices without privilege could ruin her prospects. "Then let my prospects be ruined," she answered, while the rescued boy struggled to catch his breath.

玻璃牆後，一隻機械爪按上每位考生的手腕。機器不只收集肌肉資料；知夏看見細藍絲線從皮膚流向密封艙。一名技師把她的問題當成無聊惡作劇，直到突發性的能量爆裂震裂玻璃。艙內男孩大叫。導覽員仍笑著說只是小型設備故障，知夏卻一把拉下寫著「預防」的緊急桿，停掉機器。室內頓時無聲。主管警告，沒有特權卻亂碰登記設備會毀掉她的前途。「那就毀掉吧。」她回答，同時扶住喘不過氣的男孩。

Outside, a repair worker quietly handed her a torn strip of paper. Someone had tried to carve a sequence of numbers into the desk below the testing unit. The worker had copied them before the furniture was replaced. He told Zhixia that the academy's public promise concealed a second system, one that could shift the ownership of a person's pulse without consent. He had once tried to report it, but every request for feedback returned unopened. "A city cannot prosper by feeding on children," he said. Zhixia folded the paper. Whatever the numbers meant, she knew they could end a career—or save a life.

到了外面，一位維修工悄悄遞給她一張撕下的紙。他說有人曾試圖在測試臺下雕刻一串數字，家具被換掉前他先抄了下來。工人告訴知夏，學院的公開承諾掩蓋著第二套系統，能在未經同意下轉移一個人的脈息所有權。他曾通報，卻從沒收到任何意見回覆。「一座城市不能靠吞噬孩子繁榮。」知夏摺好紙條。她不知道數字意味著什麼，卻明白它或許會結束一份工作，也可能救下一條命。

On the way out, Zhixia passed the girl who had been sent to the service queue. The girl was repairing a loose bench with borrowed tools, working more carefully than the academy's own maintenance staff. Zhixia knelt to help hold the frame. They spoke for only a minute, but the girl told her something important: a failed assessment did not cancel the skills she already possessed. She intended to become an engineer, whether the academy recognized the title or not. Zhixia promised to remember her name. It was a modest promise, yet after seeing how easily records could be altered, she began to consider promises of memory as serious work.

離開時，知夏遇見先前被送去勞務隊伍的女孩。她正借工具修鬆掉的長椅，比學院維修員更仔細。知夏蹲下替她扶住椅架。兩人只談了一分鐘，女孩卻說了重要的話：測驗不及格，不代表原有能力就被取消。無論學院承不承認，她都要成為工程師。知夏答應記住她的名字。這是個小承諾，但在看見紀錄多容易被修改後，她開始把記住一個人當成嚴肅工作。

That evening the champion from the gate found her by the tram stop. His name was Kai, and he had witnessed her rescue through an office window. "I'm supposed to keep you quiet," he said, "but I watched that machine nearly kill my brother last year." He wanted to help, not as a celebrated fighter but as someone tired of being useful to a lie. He offered her access to the academy archives and warned that the next qualifying round would begin in two days. Zhixia did not immediately trust him. Yet she accepted the keycard and wrote the date beside Yan's numbers. The shift between them was small: the city had given her a famous stranger, and the stranger had given her a reason to investigate.

當晚，那位入口的冠軍在電車站找到她。他叫凱，曾從辦公室窗邊看見她救人。「照規定我該讓妳閉嘴，」他說，「但去年那台機器差點殺了我弟弟。」他想幫忙，不是以風光武者的身分，而是厭倦當謊言的工具。他提供學院檔案室的門禁卡，也提醒兩天後會有下一輪資格賽。知夏並沒有立刻信任他，卻收下卡片，把日期寫在姊姊留下的數字旁。兩人的轉變很小：城市把一位名人送到她面前，名人則給了她繼續調查的理由。
