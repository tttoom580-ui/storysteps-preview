The Library That Moved

借雷之城 · 第 4 / 25 章 · 3000–4000 · 英中對照

用互動版查詞、朗讀與收藏

Teacher Shen sent them to a traveling librarian named Mina, whose electric truck crossed the outer districts without the council's approval. She kept translations of banned papers, including an urgent report from a foreign embassy and a researcher's notes on the city's artificial moon. The council claimed the moon traveled in a harmless orbit above Thunder City; Mina had found evidence that the object was fixed to the shield itself. Each continual adjustment of the moon drained power from underground sleepers. Zhixia read the report in the narrow margin of a stolen schoolbook. The facts were written plainly, but their implications were difficult to believe.

沈老師派他們找一位開電動卡車穿梭外區的流動圖書管理員米娜。她保留禁書譯本，包括外國使館的緊急報告與研究員對城市人造月亮的筆記。議會宣稱月亮沿著無害軌道運行；米娜卻查出它其實固定在護盾上。月亮每一次持續調整，都在抽取地下沉睡者的能量。知夏從偷來的課本窄邊讀到報告，文字明明寫得很清楚，內容卻令人難以相信。

Mina had once been a translator for the embassy whose report she now carried. She knew exactly where its emphasis had changed between versions, and she asked Zhixia to read both aloud. The official translation made every warning sound hypothetical; the original described people already harmed. Zhixia realized how much power lived in a single verb. Her own writing had always been practical lists and personal letters, never anything intended for public debate. Mina told her that accuracy mattered more than elegant language. "If you describe a wound as a possibility after it has already happened," she said, "you are not being cautious. You are hiding it."

米娜以前曾是那座使館的翻譯員，十分清楚她手上的報告在哪些地方改變了重點。她請知夏把兩個版本都朗讀出來。官方譯本把每項警告寫得像假設，原文卻明確說已有傷者。知夏終於意識到，一個動詞也握有很大的力量。她以前只寫實用清單與私人信件，從沒想過要參與公共辯論。米娜告訴她，準確比文雅重要：「若傷害已發生，妳卻還寫成可能性，這不是謹慎，而是掩蓋。」

Mina tested them before opening her deeper archive. "A murderer can hide behind good intentions," she said, "and a passive witness can help him by saying nothing. Which of you would risk your possessions to stop it?" Kai answered with an earnest promise, but Mina shook her head. She wanted accomplishments rather than speeches. Zhixia placed her only family photograph on the table and offered to exchange it for an hour of access. Mina's sharp face softened. "Keep that," she said. "The price of knowledge should never be the last thing you remember of someone." She granted approval and pulled a false panel from the truck wall.

米娜在開放深層檔案前先考驗他們。「殺人者也能躲在好意後面，」她說，「消極的目擊者若什麼都不說，就是幫凶。你們誰願意冒失去財物的風險阻止？」凱認真保證，米娜卻搖頭；她要的是真實成就，不是演說。知夏把唯一一張家人照片放上桌，願用它換一小時閱覽。米娜銳利的臉變得柔和：「留著吧。求知不該讓你失去對某個人最後的記憶。」她准許兩人進去，拉開卡車牆面的假板。

Behind the panel were diagrams of rotating shields, a child's notebook filled with rhymes, and copies of complaints that the city had dismissed as nonsense. A researcher had written that artificial pulse extraction worked only when the subject was conscious enough to dream. That explained why the underground beds never allowed deep sleep. Kai bounced a stress ball against his palm to hide his discomfort. Zhixia noticed that one translation had been deliberately altered: the word 'consent' had become 'compliance.' The change was small and devastating. Someone had replaced the right to choose with the duty to obey, then used that mistake as a law.

假板後藏著旋轉護盾圖、寫滿押韻句子的童年筆記，以及被城市斥為胡說的投訴副本。一位研究員寫道，人工抽取脈息只在當事人清醒到仍能作夢時才有效，這解釋了為何地下床位不讓人進入深眠。凱把壓力球在掌心彈來彈去，想掩飾不安。知夏注意到一份翻譯遭刻意竄改：「同意」變成「服從」。差別雖小，後果卻極大。有人把選擇權換成服從義務，再把這個錯誤當成法律。

While Kai checked the road outside, Zhixia helped Mina repair the truck's book shelves. Each title had been arranged not by subject but by the person who had rescued it. A mathematics manual bore a baker's name; a book of poems belonged to a former prison guard; a children's story had been carried out of a flooded clinic. Mina called this her map of trust. Whenever someone said the outer districts were helpless, she pointed to the shelves and explained how knowledge had passed from hand to hand. Zhixia imagined a library where every citizen could contribute something instead of paying for permission to enter.

凱在外面查看道路時，知夏幫米娜修卡車書架。書不是依學科排，而是按救出它的人分類。數學手冊貼著麵包師姓名，詩集來自前獄警，童書由水災中的診所救出。米娜稱這是她的信任地圖。每當有人說外區毫無能力，她就指著書架，說知識如何一手傳過一手。知夏想像一座每位市民都能貢獻，而不是付錢求入場的圖書館。

Before they could copy everything, a patrol vehicle arrived. Mina did not panic. She started a recorded comedy show through her loudspeakers and invited the officers to laugh at an absurd sketch about a dancing satellite. While the patrol watched the screen, Zhixia slipped the important pages into a thin envelope. Their amusement lasted long enough for Kai to move the truck behind a freight carriage. As the library rolled away, Mina admitted she had been disappointed by many heroes before. "Do not become one who saves people only when there is an audience," she warned. Zhixia held the envelope close, thinking of the faceless sleepers whose approval no public ceremony could ever obtain.

他們還沒影印完，巡邏車就到了。米娜毫不慌張，直接透過擴音器播放喜劇，邀警員看一段衛星跳舞的荒謬短劇。巡邏隊看著螢幕笑時，知夏把重要頁面塞進薄信封。這段娛樂時間剛好夠凱把卡車移到貨車後。圖書館駛離時，米娜承認自己對許多英雄失望過：「別成為只在有觀眾時才救人的那種人。」知夏抱緊信封，想著那些不知姓名的沉睡者；任何公開儀式都不可能替他們取得真正的同意。
