難民列車

The Refugee Train

借雷之城 · 第 10 / 25 章 · 3000–4000 · 英中對照 · 校訂中

用互動版查詞、朗讀與收藏

A damaged train rolled into the southern station carrying refugees from an outer settlement that the shield had failed to protect. The atmosphere of the city changed at once. Some residents brought blankets; others classified newcomers as an offensive threat to public order. Zhixia joined the rescue effort at a temporary facility where children waited in silence. One boy held a perfume bottle belonging to his mother; another clutched a single red stocking. Their possessions were small, but each was an authentic piece of a home that might no longer exist. Zhixia felt embarrassed by how long she had believed official maps described the whole world.

一列損壞的列車開進南站，載來外圍聚落的難民；護盾沒能保護他們。全城氣氛立刻變了。有人送毛毯，也有人把新來者分類成擾亂秩序的威脅。知夏加入臨時設施的救援工作，孩子們在那裡安靜等待。一個男孩握著母親的香水瓶，另一個攥著單獨一隻紅色長襪。東西雖小，卻都是可能已不存在的家的真實碎片。知夏為自己曾那麼久相信官方地圖描繪了整個世界，而感到難堪。

Volunteers were asked to classify supplies before the next train arrived, but the labels on the storage shelves had been written for an emergency no one had actually studied. "High priority" meant medicine for ranked officers, while infant food and blankets were placed near the bottom. Zhixia gathered the volunteers and rewrote the categories based on immediate need. The change caused an argument with station officials, who feared being blamed for disobeying procedure. A nurse suggested keeping both lists and noting every difference. Zhixia agreed. It would let them save people now and show later exactly where the official plan had failed.

下一班列車到站前，志工受命替物資分類，但架上的標籤是依某份從未真正檢討的緊急計畫寫的。「最高優先」指供應有等級官員的藥品，嬰兒食品與毛毯反而排在後面。知夏召集志工，按眼前需求重寫分類，卻和站務官員起了爭執；對方怕違反程序被責怪。一名護士建議兩份清單都保留，逐項記錄差異。知夏同意，這樣既能現在救人，也能日後證明官方計畫錯在哪裡。

One of the arriving families carried a box of seeds they had rescued from their garden. There were no jewels, no expensive documents, only carefully folded paper packets marked with the names of vegetables. Zhixia helped them find a warm corner and listened as the father explained which plants survived cold nights. He had expected the city to value soldiers and engineers but never gardeners. Zhixia told him Lower Nine had a small greenhouse where his knowledge would be welcome. The father's shoulders loosened. For the first time since the train arrived, he began planning for spring instead of mourning the home he had lost.

一戶新到家庭帶著從菜園搶救的種子箱，沒有珠寶、昂貴文件，只有一包包折好的紙袋，上面標著蔬菜名稱。知夏幫他們找暖和角落，聽父親解釋哪些植物耐寒。他本以為城市只珍惜士兵和工程師，不會重視園丁。知夏告訴他第九下城有小溫室，大家會歡迎他的知識。男人肩膀鬆了。列車到站後，他第一次開始規畫春天，而不只是哀悼失去的家。

The academy offered a choice: accept the refugees as unranked laborers, or send them back beyond the barrier. Kai argued for a genuine third option—temporary housing with education and medical care. A senior official claimed such acceptance would reduce the city's dignity. "Dignity belongs to people," Zhixia answered, "not to walls." Her words gained unexpected support from a publisher visiting the station. He offered to print the refugees' stories, but asked whether the accounts would differ from the city's approved narrative. Zhixia told him truth was not a promotion campaign. She would include uncertainty where witnesses disagreed, without allowing those disagreements to erase what everyone had seen.

學院提出兩個選項：把難民收作零級勞工，或送回屏障外。凱提出真正的第三條路——提供臨時住所、教育與醫療。高官聲稱接納他們會降低城市尊嚴。「尊嚴屬於人，」知夏回答，「不屬於牆。」一位來站內的出版商意外支持她，提議印製難民故事，卻問這些敘述是否會與核准版本不同。知夏說真相不是宣傳活動。若見證者有歧見，她會保留不確定性，但不允許歧見抹去所有人共同看見的事。

During a night inspection, Zhixia discovered that the station's medical scanning machines resembled those at the academy. She checked the readings and found a concealed option to transform stored patient data into pulse credits for the city. A nurse had already refused to use the setting and had been threatened with resignation. Zhixia photographed the screen, then helped the nurse disable the function without shutting down emergency care. It was a delicate decision: the machine also supplied warmth and oxygen to injured passengers. For the first time she understood why rebellion required technical knowledge as well as conviction.

夜間巡檢時，知夏發現站內醫療掃描器與學院設備幾乎相同。她查看讀數，找到隱藏選項，竟可將病患資料轉換為城市的脈息額度。一名護士拒絕使用，因而受到逼迫離職的威脅。知夏拍下畫面，再和護士一起關閉那項功能，同時不停止急救照護。這決定很細微，因為機器仍供應傷患溫度與氧氣。她第一次真正理解，反抗除了信念，還需要技術知識。

At dawn a small girl asked Zhixia why the city called her family a burden. Zhixia had no polished answer. She sat beside the child and helped draw the lost settlement from memory: a market, a school, three trees, and a hill with a blue roof. The child made Zhixia promise not to let officials replace these details with the single word 'refugee.' When the train departed for a safer station, the girl waved through the glass. Zhixia kept the drawing. It reminded her that an ambitious plan to save a city would mean nothing if it forgot the people for whom the city was supposed to exist.

天亮時，一名小女孩問知夏，為什麼城市說她的家人是負擔。知夏答不出漂亮話，只坐到女孩身旁，依她的記憶畫出失去的家鄉：市場、學校、三棵樹，以及有藍色屋頂的山坡。女孩要知夏答應，不讓官員用「難民」兩字取代所有細節。列車前往較安全車站時，女孩隔窗揮手。知夏收好圖畫。這提醒她，再雄心勃勃的救城計畫，如果忘了城市究竟為誰而存在，都毫無意義。
