Forecast of a Broken Moon

The next morning a bald minister appeared on every screen to declare that the academy incident had been caused by rebellious students. His speech quoted a forecast promising ten more years of stable protection. Zhixia watched from a café near the old machinery district, where an association of repair workers had gathered to hear her account. A retired engineer brought his autobiography, which described helping install the original shield. He pointed to a rusty hinge in a photograph and said the artificial moon could not possibly be moving as claimed. If the minister's quotation was false, then the consequent safety calculations were false as well.

隔天早晨，一位禿頭部長出現在所有螢幕上，宣稱學院事故是叛逆學生造成的。他引用一份預報，保證未來十年防護穩定。知夏在舊機械區的咖啡館觀看，附近聚集了願意聽她說明的維修工人協會。一位退休工程師帶來自傳，裡頭記載他協助安裝初代護盾的經過。他指著照片裡生鏽的鉸鏈，說人造月亮不可能按照官方說法運行。如果部長引用的內容是假的，那後續安全計算也一定有問題。

In the café's rear office, the workers showed Zhixia a list of maintenance vacancies. Each vacancy represented a repair the city had chosen not to make. The council had claimed no qualified candidates existed, yet dozens of experienced technicians had applied and been refused because of their low pulse ranks. One woman said she hoped to restore the old machines before they became dangerous. She had even offered to train younger workers for free. The ministry had rejected her proposal without explanation. Kai stared at the paperwork. It was the first time he understood exclusion not as an abstract complaint but as a sequence of decisions recorded in ordinary forms, each one capable of hurting people far beyond the person whose name appeared on it.

咖啡館後方辦公室裡，工人拿出維修職缺清單。每個空缺都代表一項城市決定不去完成的維修。議會聲稱沒有合格人選，但幾十位經驗豐富的技師早已申請，只因脈息等級太低被拒。其中一名女性希望在老機器變危險前修復，甚至願免費訓練年輕工人。管理部沒解釋就駁回。凱盯著文件。他第一次不是把排除當成抽象抱怨，而是看見它由一連串普通表格上的決定組成，每個決定都可能傷害表格名字以外的人。

Zhixia asked the retired engineer what he missed most about the city's early years. He did not mention its rulers or famous battles. Instead he described repair crews who used to eat together after night shifts, sharing stories about children and aging parents. Over time the ministry had separated the crews by rank and replaced their shared workshop with private offices. The engineer said this made errors harder to detect because nobody wanted to admit a mistake before a superior. Zhixia remembered the school children drawing their own evacuation map. She began to suspect the most dangerous change in the city had not been technological at all. It had been the loss of conversation.

知夏問退休工程師最懷念城市初期什麼。他沒有提統治者或著名戰役，而是形容夜班維修隊以前會一起吃飯，聊孩子和年老父母。後來管理部依等級分開各隊，公共工作坊也換成私人辦公室。工程師說這使錯誤更難被發現，因為沒人敢在上司面前認錯。知夏想到山區孩子自己畫的撤離圖，開始懷疑城市最危險的改變也許根本不是科技，而是失去了對話。

Curiosity drew more workers to the meeting, including a woman who had lost her job when she refused to violate a maintenance rule. She spoke of vacant posts in the lower plants and of technicians who had been ordered to halt repairs just before inspection days. The pattern was clear: faults were allowed to arise, then blamed on unranked workers. Kai asked why anyone accepted such a system. "Because our families are dependent on the pay," the woman replied. "The council knows that." Zhixia had not expected an answer so simple or so devastating. She began recording testimony instead of arguing.

好奇心吸引更多工人來開會。其中一名女性因拒絕違反維修規則而丟了工作。她說地下工廠有許多職缺空著，技師卻常被要求在檢查日前停止修繕。模式很清楚：故障被放任發生，再怪罪零級勞工。凱問為何有人接受這樣的制度。「因為家人靠薪水生活，」女人說，「議會知道。」知夏沒有料到答案如此簡單，也如此傷人。她開始記錄證詞，不再只與人爭辯。

During lunch, the café owner placed lobster pasta before them, apologizing that the kitchen fireplace had failed. The ordinary meal made the minister's threats seem briefly far away. Then a citywide alert interrupted their conversation: the moon was deviating from its approved path. The official announcer blamed an approaching storm. The retired engineer shook his head and showed them a calculation on a napkin. The change was linked to an underground power demand, not the weather. Zhixia stared at the numbers. They matched the sequence carved beneath the academy testing machine, which meant Yan had known this failure was coming.

午餐時咖啡館老闆端來龍蝦義大利麵，抱歉地說廚房壁爐壞了。這頓尋常餐點讓部長的威脅一度變得遙遠。忽然全城警報打斷談話：人造月亮偏離核准路線。官方播報怪罪逼近的風暴。退休工程師搖頭，在餐巾紙上寫出計算，證明偏移與地下供能需求有關，而不是天氣。知夏凝視數字，它們和學院測試機下刻的序列完全相符——林晏早就知道故障會發生。

The workers offered Zhixia a hidden route into the lower plant, but they warned that the ministry would call it sabotage if she entered without permission. She promised not to endanger anyone who helped her. Then she asked each person to leave a description of what they had witnessed and whether their name could safely be used. Some agreed to sign. Others chose anonymity. Zhixia accepted both. Resistance, she was learning, was not a performance of fearlessness. It was the slow construction of trust between people who had more than themselves to lose.

工人們願意提供通往地下工廠的祕密路線，卻警告她，未經許可進入一定會被部長說成破壞行動。知夏保證不會連累協助者，接著請每個人寫下見證內容，並標明姓名是否能公開。有人簽名，也有人選擇匿名。她都接受。她漸漸明白，反抗不是表演無所畏懼，而是讓那些不只會失去自己的人，慢慢在彼此之間建立信任。
