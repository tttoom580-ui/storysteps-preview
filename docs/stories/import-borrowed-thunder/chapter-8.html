The Handwriting of an Ancestor

The faulty roof engineer had disappeared, but his workshop contained a drawing of an ancient gear and a riddle written in identical handwriting to Yan's. The line read: THE HEART YOU CRUSH WILL STILL BEAT IN THE WALL. Zhixia brought it to a distinguished craft historian, who explained that her ancestors had helped equip the first city shield. The original design was defensible: volunteers would share small amounts of energy during emergencies. The later council had expanded the system until participation became compulsory. In the workshop's dusty corner, Zhixia found a hose and an old pressure gauge marked with her grandmother's initials.

批准危險屋頂工程的工程師失蹤了，但他的工作坊裡留著古老齒輪圖與一道謎語，字跡竟與林晏完全相同：「你壓碎的心，仍會在牆裡跳動。」知夏把圖拿給一位知名工藝史家。他說林家祖先曾協助裝備第一代城市護盾，最初設計是可辯護的：緊急情況下讓志願者分享少量能量。後來議會不斷擴張，參與竟變成強制。工作坊積塵角落還有一條水管和一只舊壓力表，上面刻著祖母名字縮寫。

Contrary to the heroic tale taught at school, the early shield had been maintained by gardeners, healers, and other people without martial rank. The historian pointed to an old pregnancy ledger showing that workers were offered rest and medical care rather than punished for temporary weakness. Later administrators had called that policy lousy and expensive. Their replacement rules were shorter and far more severe. It was easy to misunderstand the past when only the winners wrote its textbooks. Zhixia had once misunderstood her grandmother's anger as resentment toward the city itself. Now she saw that the older woman had been furious because she loved the city enough to challenge its betrayal of ordinary people.

和學校教的英雄故事相反，初代護盾其實由園丁、醫者與其他沒有武力等級的人維護。史家指著一本舊孕期登記簿，記載懷孕工人可休息、接受醫療，而不是因暫時虛弱受罰。後來的管理者把政策說成糟糕又昂貴，替代規定更短、更苛刻。當只有勝者編寫課本時，人很容易誤解過去。知夏以前誤解祖母的憤怒，以為她怨恨城市本身；如今才懂老人是因為愛這座城，才憤怒於它背叛普通人。

The historian invited them to launch a small test in a sealed chamber, using parts from the old shield equipment. Zhixia connected the gauge and traced a neat stroke of blue light along the gear teeth. The device responded, but it also released a recorded voice: "My daughter, if you hear this, do not confuse guilt with duty." The speaker was her grandmother, who had helped design the first system. Zhixia's mother had always described her as a traitor, but the recording suggested something else. The woman had tried to stop later modifications when she understood how they would involve unwilling citizens.

史家請他們利用第一代護盾的零件，在密封室啟動小測試。知夏接上壓力表，沿齒輪邊緣描出一筆整齊藍光。裝置回應了，卻也播出錄音：「女兒啊，如果妳聽到這句話，別把罪惡感誤認為責任。」說話者是參與初代設計的祖母。知夏的母親一直說她是叛徒，但錄音顯示另一個故事：當祖母明白後續改造將牽連不情願的市民，她曾設法阻止。

The historian took them into the workshop's rear courtyard, where a row of broken clocks stood beneath a roof of glass. Every clock had stopped at a different hour. He explained that the founder's assistants had used them to test whether volunteers could maintain separate rhythms without losing their identities. The experiment had been abandoned when later rulers preferred a single centralized command. Zhixia placed her hand on one cracked face and listened. The mechanism was silent, yet she imagined all those different hours sounding together. Perhaps freedom was not the absence of shared timing. Perhaps it was the ability to join a rhythm and leave it again by choice.

史家帶他們到後院，玻璃屋頂下擺著一排壞掉的鐘，每只停在不同時間。原來建城者的助手曾用它們測試志願者能否保持不同節奏，同時不失去自我。後來統治者偏好中央統一命令，實驗便被放棄。知夏把手按在裂開的鐘面上。機械早已無聲，她卻想像那些不同時辰一起響起。或許自由不是完全沒有共同節奏，而是能選擇加入，也能選擇離開。

A second recording came from Yan. She had interviewed a pregnant technician who worked below the hospital. The woman described seeing energy transferred from patients during routine examinations. She had reported the practice, only to be told she had misunderstood the rules. Yan promised to protect her identity. Zhixia listened twice, stunned by the tenderness in her sister's voice. Yan was not only investigating a machine; she had been defending people who could not safely speak for themselves. The historian warned that the council would consider possession of these recordings a threat, and urged the group to hide them until they had stronger evidence.

第二段錄音竟是林晏的聲音。她訪問過一位在醫院地下工作的懷孕技師。那女人說自己看見病患在例行檢查時被抽走能量，通報後卻遭告知是「誤解規則」。林晏承諾保護她的身分。知夏聽了兩遍，震撼於姊姊聲音裡的溫柔。林晏不只在調查機器，也在保護無法安全發聲的人。史家警告，議會會把持有錄音視為威脅，建議他們先藏好，等掌握更多證據再公開。

As they left, Zhixia received a suggestion from Kai's old trainer: join the council's next public hearing and demand an explanation. She hesitated. The academy had shown how easily officials could twist a brave act into evidence of guilt. Yet the ancestor's message had changed her. She no longer wanted simply to uncover a secret and run. She wanted the original promise of protection returned to the people who had built it. That night she copied the gear's shape into her notebook, adding a note beneath it: a city is not a possession. It is an agreement, and agreements can be changed.

離開時，凱的舊教練建議知夏參加議會下一場公開聽證，要求解釋。她猶豫了。學院早已證明，官員多容易把勇敢行動扭成有罪證據。但祖母的留言改變了她。她不再只想揭開祕密後逃走，而是想讓保護城市的最初承諾，重新回到建城者的後代手中。那晚她把齒輪形狀抄進筆記，在下面寫道：一座城市不是財產，它是一份協議；而協議可以改變。
