競技場屋頂的雪

Snow on the Tournament Roof

借雷之城 · 第 7 / 25 章 · 3000–4000 · 英中對照 · 校訂中

The tournament opened with artificial frost drifting across the academy roof. Zhixia's competitor was a diploma-winning athlete from the eastern towers, famous for intense attacks and remarkable accuracy. The crowd gave her no chance. A helicopter circled overhead to record the contest for the council, while traders outside offered bets on the percentage by which she would lose. Kai reminded Zhixia to adapt to the surroundings rather than fight their strength. She watched the snow settle on the metal railings and noticed that every pulse strike melted it in a different pattern. The differences gave her a map of the arena before the bell rang.

競技賽在學院屋頂的人造霜雪中開幕。知夏的對手是東塔一名拿過文憑的運動好手，攻擊猛烈、準確度驚人。觀眾根本不看好知夏。直升機在上空盤旋，替議會拍攝比賽；場外攤商則下注她會以多少百分比輸掉。凱提醒她適應周遭環境，不要硬碰硬。知夏看著雪落在金屬欄杆上，發現每一次脈息攻擊都會融出不同形狀。鐘聲未響，這些差異已替她畫出競技場地圖。

The first exchange was brutal. Zhixia avoided a strike by a slight turn of her shoulder, but the next blow drove her into the floor and nearly injured her knee. She analyzed the rhythm rather than the pain. Her opponent had a tendency to repeat attacks after every loud crowd reaction; when the cheers rose, the fighter became predictable. Zhixia waited, then redirected the next strike toward an empty support column. Ice burst outward, and the arena's overhead sensors registered her first point. The audience fell silent. She had not defeated her opponent through greater force, only through mutual timing with the world around her.

第一輪交手非常激烈。知夏輕轉肩膀避過一擊，下一拳卻把她摔向地面，膝蓋差點受傷。她分析的是節奏，不是疼痛。對手有個習慣：每次觀眾大聲歡呼，接著就重複相同攻勢。知夏等待，再把下一擊引向空支柱。冰霜四散，場上的感測器登記了她第一分。觀眾安靜下來。她不是用更大的力量取勝，而是和周遭環境配合好時機。

A plumbing failure interrupted the final round. A broken pipe beneath the roof sent water toward the seating section, and panicked spectators surged against the exits like a herd. Zhixia heard the support beams strain before anyone else did. She abandoned the match, borrowed a maintenance mule to move barricades, and shouted directions to the emergency crew. Her opponent joined her, helping reduce the pressure against the main gate. The rescue cost Zhixia the official victory, yet it prevented hundreds of people from being crushed. When the judges asked if she opposed their ruling, she said the welfare of the crowd mattered more than her score.

最後一輪因水管故障中斷。屋頂下方的破管讓水沖向觀眾席，驚慌的人像獸群般擠向出口。知夏比所有人更早聽到支柱吃力的聲音，立刻放棄比賽，借來維修用的小騾形搬運機移開路障，對救援隊喊出方向。對手也來幫忙，減輕主門壓力。她因此失去官方勝利，卻避免數百人被擠傷。評審問她是否反對判決，她只說民眾福祉比自己的分數重要。

The broken roof revealed another secret. A plumber who had repeatedly asked the academy to repair the pipes showed Zhixia copies of rejected work orders. Engineers had recommended a reduction in crowd capacity by twenty percent, but the academy wanted a full audience for its broadcasts. The plumber spoke without drama, explaining how a small leak could weaken a supporting wall over time. He had worked there for thirteen years and knew every valve by sound. Zhixia thought of the way officials rewarded martial competitors for spectacular victories while ignoring specialists who prevented disaster. She photographed his records and promised not to reduce his testimony to a footnote.

損壞的屋頂還揭露另一個祕密。一名多次要求學院修水管的水電師傅，拿出遭退回的工單。工程師建議把觀眾容納量減少百分之二十，學院卻希望轉播時滿座。師傅平靜說明，小小漏水如何日積月累削弱支撐牆。他在那裡工作十三年，聽聲音就知道每個閥門。知夏想到官員獎賞武者華麗勝利，卻忽略防止災難的專家。她拍下紀錄，保證不會把他的證詞縮成註腳。

The opponent she had faced returned to apologize after the judges left. Her name was Jun, and she had been raised to believe that being second meant being worthless. Watching Zhixia abandon the contest had confused her more than defeat. "I thought you wanted to prove you were stronger than me," Jun said. Zhixia replied that she wanted to know whether strength could protect people, not merely impress them. Jun sat beside her on the wet steps and admitted she had never asked that question of her own training. Before parting, she offered access to the academy's safety committee. It was not friendship yet, but it was the beginning of honest respect.

評審離開後，剛才的對手回來道歉。她叫君，自小被教成第二名就等於沒價值。看到知夏放棄競賽，她比輸掉還困惑。「我以為妳想證明比我強。」君說。知夏回答，她只想知道力量能否保護人，而不只是讓人驚嘆。君坐到濕臺階旁，承認自己訓練多年，從沒問過這件事。離開前，她願提供學院安全委員會的接觸方式。這還不算友情，卻是誠實尊重的開始。

That evening a janitor from Lower Nine approached Zhixia with a folded notice. Its language was dry and bureaucratic, but the meaning was remarkable: the academy had secretly reduced the safety budget for the arena to pay for a new combat tower. Kai showed it to his former rival, who had witnessed the flooding. She agreed to testify. Zhixia realized the competition had given her something more useful than a trophy. It had given her an ally from a world that had once dismissed her as worthless. Together they decided to find the engineer who had approved the faulty roof.

當晚，一位來自第九下城的清潔工拿著折好的通知找到知夏。公文語氣乾硬，內容卻驚人：學院暗中削減競技場安全預算，只為興建新的戰鬥塔。凱把通知拿給目睹水患的前對手看，對方同意作證。知夏明白，競賽給她的東西比獎盃更有用——一個原本輕視她的世界裡，終於有人願意站在她身旁。他們決定一起找出批准危險屋頂工程的人。
