歌劇院下的雷聲

The Bell Below the Opera

借雷之城 · 第 1 / 25 章 · 3000–4000 · 英中對照

The morning assembly filled Thunder City Square with banners proclaiming the ministry's favorite slogan: STRENGTH IS SAFETY. Lin Zhixia, nineteen, stood behind the ticket counter of the old opera hall, polishing a copper dial with a cloth dampened by weak acid. The city had ordered all youthful workers to attend a combat demonstration, yet the hall's manager insisted that someone stay to sweep with a broom. He called her a coward for accepting such a duty. Zhixia did not answer. The dial beneath her fingers vibrated every time the invisible shield above the city flashed, and she could hear a second beat hidden inside the official rhythm.

清晨的集會讓霆京廣場滿是旗幟，上頭印著管理部最愛的口號：「力量就是安全。」十九歲的林知夏站在老歌劇院售票櫃檯後，用沾了稀酸液的布擦拭銅製圓盤。市府要求所有年輕工作者去看武技示範，但劇院經理堅持留一人拿掃帚清掃，還嘲笑接受工作的她是膽小鬼。知夏沒有回嘴。每當城市上空的無形護盾閃光，她指下的圓盤就會震動，而且她聽得出官方節奏裡還藏著第二種拍子。

Before the demonstration, the square had been full of people discussing ordinary plans. A baker argued with his brother about flour prices. A delivery rider checked the battery on his bicycle. Two children tried to guess which martial champion would appear beneath the flags. Zhixia liked these scattered voices more than the official music. Each person carried a private rhythm, and together they made the city sound alive. She wished Yan could hear it with her. Since her sister disappeared, their mother had refused to mention the academy at dinner. Silence had become another household rule, one Zhixia obeyed only when her mother was too tired to argue.

示範開始前，廣場上到處有人聊著日常計畫。麵包師傅和兄弟爭麵粉價格，送貨騎士查看腳踏車電池，兩個孩子猜今天哪位武術冠軍會出現在旗幟下。知夏喜歡這些零碎聲音勝過官方音樂，每個人都有自己的節奏，合起來讓城市像活著。她希望姊姊能和她一起聽。林晏失蹤後，母親不再准許家人在餐桌上提學院，沉默變成家庭裡另一條規矩；只有母親累到不想爭時，知夏才敢違反。

A persuasive officer took the stage to explain the phenomenon of pulse energy. He said the ministry would construct new training towers and cooperate with every neighborhood willing to provide recruits. His facial expression remained serene even when someone asked why unranked citizens could not use the protected northern route. "Security requires difficult choices," he replied. Zhixia noticed two men in shady uniforms writing down the questioner's name. The opera house was supposed to provide entertainment, not politics, but the city had turned every public performance into a lecture about obedience. She slid a hand under the counter and secretly recorded the dial's movement on her old phone.

一名善於說服人的官員登臺解釋「脈息能量」的現象。他說管理部將建造新的訓練塔，並與願意提供學員的各區合作。即使有人問起，為什麼沒有武力等級的市民不能走受保護的北方路線，他的臉部表情仍十分平靜。「安全總需要艱難取捨。」知夏卻看見兩名穿著可疑制服的人記下發問者的姓名。歌劇院本該提供娛樂，如今全城的表演都成了服從課。她把手伸到櫃檯下，偷偷用舊手機錄下圓盤的震動。

When the noon siren sounded, a training drone dropped out of formation and struck the square. People screamed and ran. Zhixia saw the drone's path before its engine turned, as if the copper dial had mapped the route directly onto her nerves. She shouted for the crowd to move left, then pushed a child behind a stone pillar. A ranked fighter tried to interfere, demanding that she wait for orders. The drone hit the empty pavement where the child had stood. Later the officer thanked the fighter in public and ignored Zhixia. Only the child, who had seen everything, pressed a paper star into her palm.

正午警報響起，一架訓練無人機忽然偏離隊形，直衝廣場。人群尖叫四散。知夏竟在引擎轉向之前就看見它的軌跡，彷彿銅盤已把路線直接刻進她的神經。她喊大家往左跑，把一個孩子推到石柱後。持有高等級的武者試圖干涉，命令她等待指示。無人機撞上孩子剛才站的位置。事後官員在眾人面前感謝武者，對知夏隻字未提；只有親眼看見的孩子，把一顆紙星星塞進她手裡。

The child's paper star remained warm in Zhixia's pocket after the drone crashed. She considered returning it, but the square had emptied and the officers were already cleaning the scene. They swept away fragments of broken metal as though removing debris could erase the panic. She went back to the opera hall and wrote down everything she remembered, including the timing of the siren and the direction of the wind. Her father had taught her that an accurate record was a kind of shelter. It could not undo what happened, but it could keep the story from being replaced by a more convenient one. For the first time, she understood why Yan had kept so many notebooks.

無人機墜毀後，孩子給的紙星星仍在知夏口袋裡帶著暖意。她想還回去，廣場卻已清空，官員忙著清理現場。他們掃走金屬碎片，好像只要沒有殘骸，驚慌就沒發生。知夏回劇院寫下所有記憶，包括警報時間與風向。父親曾教她，準確紀錄是一種庇護。它無法讓事情沒發生，卻能防止故事被替換成更方便的版本。知夏第一次懂得姊姊為何留下那麼多筆記。

After sunset the old hall grew quiet, but the copper dial would not stop singing. Zhixia found a card under it describing the machine's usage, written in her missing sister's handwriting. Yan had vanished three years ago while studying at the city's highest martial academy. One instruction was underlined: NEVER MATCH THE THIRD BEAT. Zhixia tested the rhythm against the shield anyway, carefully stopping before the forbidden note. For an instant the dark roof became transparent. She saw hundreds of people sleeping in narrow underground beds while wires took light from their foreheads. Then the vision vanished, leaving her with one terrible thought: perhaps the shield did not protect everyone equally because it was built out of some people's stolen lives.

日落後老劇院安靜下來，銅盤卻依舊低鳴。知夏從盤底找到一張使用說明，字跡竟屬於三年前失蹤的姊姊林晏。姊姊當時就讀全城最高等的武學院。卡片上有一句畫了底線：「絕不可配合第三拍。」知夏仍小心試著讓節奏與護盾同步，只在禁忌音符前停住。一瞬間屋頂像變透明了，她看見幾百個人睡在地下狹床上，電線正從他們額頭抽取光。幻象消失後，她只剩一個可怕念頭：也許護盾無法平等保護每個人，是因為它本來就由一些人被偷走的生命建成。
