Ren learns part of a music charm and part of a farming technique, creating a tool no instructor can classify. A narrow strip of daylight crossed the table in Aster Kingdom. Beside it lay the skill notebook, covered with marks that only made sense to someone who had been there. Yui asked Ren to start from the beginning, not from the conclusion everybody wanted. The first account was untidy and sometimes embarrassing. That was a reason to ask careful questions, not a reason to throw it away. Outside, footsteps hurried past the door. A witness mentioned banker in a hurried account; Ren wrote it down without guessing its importance. Ren found bay on the list of details that the first report had left unexplained. 任學會一部分音樂咒術及耕作技巧，做出教官無法歸類的工具。 一道細窄日光照在星辰王國的桌面上，旁邊攤著技能筆記，上面滿是只有親臨現場的人才看得懂的記號。優衣請任從事情的開頭講起，而不是從大家想聽的結論開始。最初的說法雜亂，甚至令人難堪，但那是需要仔細提問的理由，不是丟棄它的理由。門外腳步聲匆匆經過。 一名證人在匆忙的敘述中提到「銀行家」；任先記下來，沒有猜測它有多重要。任在第一份報告沒有說明的細節清單上，找到「海灣」。一名證人在匆忙的敘述中提到「光束」；任先記下來，沒有猜測它有多重要。

A messenger came from Lord Halden with an offer that sounded generous until Ren read the last line. The offer protected the person making it, not the people who would carry out the work. Yui reached for the skill notebook and wrote down the missing condition. They were not refusing cooperation. They were refusing to confuse cooperation with obedience. That distinction changed the way the next conversation began. Ren found champion on the list of details that the first report had left unexplained. At first the change seemed creative; a second observation made the team less certain. The plan mentioned a need to decrease; neither of them accepted that instruction without a reason.

哈登領主派來使者提出一項看似大方的條件，直到任讀到最後一行才發現問題。條件保護的是提出者，不是實際工作的人。優衣拿起技能筆記記下缺少的前提。他們並非拒絕合作，而是拒絕把合作和服從混為一談。這個區別改變了下一場談話的方式。 任在第一份報告沒有說明的細節清單上，找到「冠軍」。起初這項變化看來「有創意的」，第二次觀察卻讓團隊不再那麼確定。計畫提到需要「減少」；兩人都不願毫無理由便接受這項指示。

In a quieter corner, Ren and Yui tried to understand each other without an audience. One favored action because people needed help now; the other feared that a rushed action could shut the safest route. Both concerns were real. Ren admitted that the skill notebook did not answer every question. Yui admitted that waiting forever was not a plan either. They decided to separate the urgent work from the larger decision. The class panels shone above the students’ hands, but Ren’s remained empty. He copied the movement from a carpenter rather than a wizard, and the first attempt hurt his fingers. A witness mentioned echo in a hurried account; Ren wrote it down without guessing its importance. At first the change seemed fearful; a second observation made the team less certain. Yui wrote historic beside the early proposal, then added a question mark because opinions differed.

到了安靜角落，任與優衣試著在沒有觀眾的情況下理解彼此。一人主張立刻行動，因為有人正在求助；另一人擔心匆忙行動會堵住最安全的路。兩種擔憂都真實。任承認技能筆記無法回答一切，優衣也承認無限等待不是計畫。兩人決定把緊急工作和較大的決策分開。同學手上的職業面板發著光，任的面板卻仍空白。他模仿的是木匠而非巫師的動作，第一次練習弄痛了手指。 一名證人在匆忙的敘述中提到「迴盪」；任先記下來，沒有猜測它有多重要。起初這項變化看來「害怕的」，第二次觀察卻讓團隊不再那麼確定。優衣在初步提案旁寫下「歷史的」，又加了問號，因為意見並不一致。

The test seemed almost foolish at first. They used plain tools, asked a witness to describe what could be seen, and repeated the same action twice. Ren kept the skill notebook open so that Yui could challenge each step. On the second attempt, a detail changed. That difference did not solve the whole mystery, but it ruled out the easiest explanation. For the first time that day, they had something firmer than a rumor. Ren practiced the movement until his hands remembered it; the empty class panel never offered a shortcut. Someone asked whether holder was connected to the trouble, and the room fell quiet for a moment. Yui circled the entry for leak and asked whether the detail belonged to this incident. The margin of the skill notebook contained a reference to mob, which Yui marked for a later check.

起初，測試看起來幾乎有點傻。他們只使用簡單工具，請證人描述眼前的情形，並把相同動作重做兩次。任攤開技能筆記，讓優衣可以質疑每一步。第二次嘗試時，一個細節改變了。那不能解開整個謎團，卻排除了最簡單的解釋。他們終於握有比流言更可靠的東西。任反覆練習到雙手記住動作；空白職業面板從未提供捷徑。 有人問「持有人」是否與麻煩有關，房間一時間安靜下來。優衣圈起「漏水」那一欄，問這個細節是否真的屬於本次事件。技能筆記的頁邊提到了「群眾」，優衣做上記號，準備日後查證。

Yui read the last line aloud. Ren checked the date and kept a copy for the morning team. Yui wrote normal beside the early proposal, then added a question mark because opinions differed. The margin of the skill notebook contained a reference to palace, which Yui marked for a later check.

優衣朗讀最後一行。任核對日期，為早班小組留下副本。 優衣在初步提案旁寫下「正常的」，又加了問號，因為意見並不一致。技能筆記的頁邊提到了「宮殿」，優衣做上記號，準備日後查證。

Someone suggested that Ren should keep the discovery secret until the whole argument was over. Yui objected: a secret about danger could also harm people who never had a chance to ask questions. They separated private names from public facts and prepared a short notice. The notice was less dramatic than the rumor, but it explained what could be used now and what still needed an independent check. At the old school desk, Ren wrote down every failed attempt in his skill notebook. The blank panel did not praise him, but he could see what his hands had learned. Yui circled the entry for penny and asked whether the detail belonged to this incident. Yui wanted to postpone, but agreed to wait for one more safe test.

有人建議任等爭議結束再公開發現。優衣反對：危險的祕密也可能傷害那些根本沒有機會提問的人。他們把私人姓名與公共事實分開，準備簡短公告。公告不如流言驚人，卻清楚說明現在能使用什麼、哪些仍需獨立檢查。任坐在舊課桌前，把每次失敗都記進技能筆記。空白面板沒有稱讚他，但他看得出雙手已學會什麼。 優衣圈起「便士」那一欄，問這個細節是否真的屬於本次事件。優衣想要「延後」，但同意先等一次安全測試的結果。

Yui read the last line aloud. Ren checked the date and kept a copy for the morning team. Yui circled the entry for receiver and asked whether the detail belonged to this incident. The margin of the skill notebook contained a reference to scholarship, which Yui marked for a later check.

優衣朗讀最後一行。任核對日期，為早班小組留下副本。 優衣圈起「接收器」那一欄，問這個細節是否真的屬於本次事件。技能筆記的頁邊提到了「獎學金」，優衣做上記號，準備日後查證。

Ren left the skill notebook open so Yui could add the next finding. Neither could see the whole road yet. Yui wanted to steal, but agreed to wait for one more safe test. The margin of the skill notebook contained a reference to zipper, which Yui marked for a later check.

任讓技能筆記保持打開，好讓優衣補上新的發現。兩人都還看不見整段路。 優衣想要「偷竊」，但同意先等一次安全測試的結果。技能筆記的頁邊提到了「拉鍊」，優衣做上記號，準備日後查證。
