空白職業面板

The Empty Class Panel

全班穿越，只有我沒有職業 · 第 1 / 25 章 · 2000–3000 · 英中對照 · 校訂中

用互動版查詞、朗讀與收藏

Every student receives a heroic class in the new world, but Ren’s panel contains only a blank space. By sunrise, the rumor had traveled across Aster Kingdom faster than any honest explanation. Ren found Yui beside the skill notebook, making a list of the things they had actually seen. There were fewer items on that list than on the rumor sheet. The difference mattered. A frightened person could mistake a guess for a promise, and a promise could send somebody toward a danger they had never agreed to face. The inspector called the risk acceptable, but Ren asked to see the safety conditions. Yui promised to advise the group only after reading the original instructions. A nervous worker rested a hand beneath his chin and studied the open notebook.

全班在異世界獲得英雄職業，只有任的面板是一片空白。 日出時，流言已在星辰王國傳得比任何誠實解釋都快。任看見優衣守在技能筆記旁，列出兩人真正親眼見到的事。那張清單比流言單上的項目少得多，而差別至關重要。害怕的人可能把猜測誤認成承諾，承諾又可能把某人帶進他從未同意面對的危險。 督察說風險可以接受，但任要求查看安全條件。優衣答應讀過原始指示後才給團隊建議。一名緊張的工人把手托在下巴下，仔細看著打開的筆記。

The problem grew more complicated when Lord Halden asked for a quick decision. Ren understood the attraction of a simple answer, but the class system had already made simple answers expensive for other people. Yui traced a line in the skill notebook and showed exactly where the available information ended. Neither of them wanted to make a public promise across that blank space. They agreed to measure one small fact before taking the next step. A traveler unpacked a coconut from the supply basket and shared it with the tired guards. Ren tried to connect the earlier warning with the mark beside the doorway. Yui called the order cruel because it would leave the weakest people behind.

哈登領主要求迅速決定後，問題變得更複雜。任理解簡單答案的吸引力，但職業制度早已讓別人替這些簡單答案付出高昂代價。優衣沿著技能筆記畫出一道線，指出現有資訊到哪裡為止。兩人都不願跨過空白處作公開承諾，因此同意先測量一件小事再前進。 一名旅人從補給籃拿出椰子，分給疲倦的守衛。任試著把先前的警告和門邊的記號連起來。優衣認為這命令殘酷，因為它會把最弱勢的人留在原地。

The first disagreement was small, but it revealed the difference between their habits. Ren tended to notice patterns; Yui noticed who was missing from the room. Together they looked at the skill notebook and named one question each. There was no need for either person to win the argument. A good plan would leave room for both questions and for the people whose answers might be different from theirs. At the old school desk, Ren wrote down every failed attempt in his skill notebook. The blank panel did not praise him, but he could see what his hands had learned. The medic wrote the correct dose on a card instead of trusting anyone to remember it. A slow drip of water came through the ceiling, marking the path of a hidden pipe. Ren felt a brief envy of people who seemed sure of their answers, then returned to work.

第一次分歧不大，卻顯露兩人的習慣差異。任擅長注意規律，優衣則察覺誰沒有出現在房裡。他們一起查看技能筆記，各自提出一個問題。爭論不必分出輸贏；好計畫應容納兩個問題，也容納答案可能不同的其他人。任坐在舊課桌前，把每次失敗都記進技能筆記。空白面板沒有稱讚他，但他看得出雙手已學會什麼。 醫護人員把正確劑量寫在卡片上，不要求大家憑記憶用藥。緩慢滴落的水從天花板滲出，標示出隱藏水管的位置。任一度羨慕那些看似確定答案的人，隨後繼續工作。

No one cheered when their first attempt failed. Ren marked the failure in the skill notebook, and Yui asked whether the result could be repeated safely. They chose a smaller version of the task, with a clear signal to stop if anything went wrong. The second attempt produced a different sound and a different answer. They still needed another witness, but now they knew what question that witness should be asked. Yui checked whether students with weaker classes were still included in the plan. They agreed to explore only the passage that the safety team had already inspected. The weather turned extreme by noon, and the team moved its supplies under cover. Ren refused to gamble with the safety of someone who had not agreed to the risk.

第一次嘗試失敗時，沒有人歡呼。任把失敗記在技能筆記裡，優衣詢問是否能安全重做。他們選擇規模更小的測試，並約定出錯時立即停止的訊號。第二次嘗試出現不同的聲音與答案。雖然還需要另一名證人，但他們終於知道該問對方什麼。優衣確認職業較弱的同學沒有被排除在計畫之外。 他們同意只探索安全小組檢查過的通道。到了中午天候變得極端惡劣，團隊把物資移到遮蔽處。任拒絕拿未同意冒險者的安全來賭。

Yui read the last line aloud. Ren checked the date and kept a copy for the morning team. One quick glance at the old date told Yui that the sheet was not the latest copy. Fresh hay lay in a corner of the stable beside a broken wooden gate.

優衣朗讀最後一行。任核對日期，為早班小組留下副本。 優衣瞥一眼舊日期，就知道這張紙並非最新版本。馬廄一角堆著新鮮乾草，旁邊是一扇壞掉的木門。

A new message arrived before the meeting could end. It mentioned the class system, then claimed that only Lord Halden had the authority to decide what happened next. Ren read it twice. The words sounded certain, but there was no date beside the claim and no name of the person who had checked it. Yui suggested saving a copy and asking for the original. The question was not whether to trust a powerful person. It was what anyone could verify. Ren used a length of rope, a cooking pot and a spell fragment to show why small skills could work together. The instructor had no box for such a result. Someone had left clean laundry beside the emergency supplies, ready for people who might stay overnight. The apology sounded sincere, but Ren still expected the promised repair to be completed.

會議尚未結束，一則新訊息就送到了。訊息提及職業制度，並宣稱只有哈登領主有權決定下一步。任讀了兩遍。文字很肯定，卻沒有日期，也沒有查核者姓名。優衣建議保留副本並索取原件。問題不是該不該相信有權勢的人，而是任何人能核實什麼。任用一段繩子、一只煮鍋和殘缺法術展示小技能如何合作。教官的分類表沒有這種結果的欄位。 有人把乾淨衣物放在緊急物資旁，供可能需要過夜的人使用。道歉聽來誠懇，但任仍期待承諾的修理真正完成。

Yui read the last line aloud. Ren checked the date and kept a copy for the morning team. Yui sat on a low stool to speak at eye level with a frightened child. A sign banned tobacco near the storage room because a spark could cause a fire.

優衣朗讀最後一行。任核對日期，為早班小組留下副本。 優衣坐在矮凳上，與受驚的孩子平視交談。儲藏室附近的標誌禁止使用菸草，因為火星可能造成火災。

Ren left the skill notebook open so Yui could add the next finding. Neither could see the whole road yet. A pair of twin lamps stood at the gate, but only one was still working. A guard gave a sharp whistle, the agreed signal for everybody to stop moving.

任讓技能筆記保持打開，好讓優衣補上新的發現。兩人都還看不見整段路。 大門有一對成雙的燈，卻只剩一盞還能發亮。守衛吹出尖銳口哨，那是大家約定停止移動的訊號。
