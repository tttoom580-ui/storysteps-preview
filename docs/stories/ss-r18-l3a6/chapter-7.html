Lord Halden selects only fighters for a royal mission and orders Ren to stay in the servants’ quarters. The morning began with a sound that did not belong to the ordinary rhythm of Aster Kingdom. Ren had barely opened the skill notebook when Yui pointed toward the people waiting outside. Some looked angry; others were worried about what would happen to them if nobody acted. Ren put the easiest solution aside. Before asking anyone to follow a plan, there had to be a way of knowing that the plan was safe. The first witness called the result automatic, but Ren asked what the witness had actually seen. Ren found automobile on the list of details that the first report had left unexplained. The plan mentioned a need to clothe; neither of them accepted that instruction without a reason.

哈登領主只挑戰鬥職業執行王室任務，命令任留在僕役宿舍。 早晨從一個不屬於星辰王國日常節奏的聲音開始。任剛打開技能筆記，優衣便指向門外等待的人。有些人憤怒，有些人則擔心無人採取行動後自己會遭遇什麼。任先放下最容易的解法，因為要請人照計畫行動之前，必須有方法確認計畫安全。 第一位證人形容結果「自動的」，但任要求對方說清楚實際看見什麼。任在第一份報告沒有說明的細節清單上，找到「汽車」。計畫提到需要「給穿衣」；兩人都不願毫無理由便接受這項指示。

There was an awkward silence after Ren questioned the official version. Lord Halden looked displeased, while Yui asked to see the record from the previous day. A clean sheet had appeared where there should have been a correction. Nobody could explain who had removed the earlier notes. Ren did not call that proof of a crime. It was proof that the record needed to be checked before anyone trusted it. Ren found committee on the list of details that the first report had left unexplained. The plan mentioned a need to drown; neither of them accepted that instruction without a reason. Someone asked whether dumpling was connected to the trouble, and the room fell quiet for a moment.

任質疑官方說法後，現場一陣尷尬的沉默。哈登領主看來很不高興，優衣則要求查看前一天的紀錄。原本應有修正記號的地方出現一張乾淨紙頁，沒人能說明誰移除了舊筆記。任沒有立刻稱它為犯罪證據，但它足以證明紀錄必須先經核對。 任在第一份報告沒有說明的細節清單上，找到「委員會」。計畫提到需要「溺水」；兩人都不願毫無理由便接受這項指示。有人問「水餃」是否與麻煩有關，房間一時間安靜下來。

The room smelled faintly of dust and rain. Yui noticed how tightly Ren held the skill notebook and asked whether the new plan felt too heavy. Ren laughed once, without much joy, and said that a map or a contract was easier to hold than a promise. They discussed the cost of being wrong, then wrote the limits of their knowledge at the top of the page. Only after that did the work feel possible again. Yui counted the students at the gate before the patrol moved. A knight’s shining shield could not make the journey safe if an unarmed classmate had been left behind. A witness mentioned faucet in a hurried account; Ren wrote it down without guessing its importance. Someone asked whether fence was connected to the trouble, and the room fell quiet for a moment. Yui circled the entry for flock and asked whether the detail belonged to this incident.

房裡有淡淡的塵土與雨味。優衣注意到任緊握技能筆記，便問新計畫是否讓人壓力太大。任苦笑一下，說拿著地圖或契約總比承擔承諾容易。兩人談過判斷錯誤的代價，把目前知識的限制寫在頁首，工作這才重新變得可行。巡邏隊出發前，優衣先清點大門口的學生。即使騎士盾牌閃亮，若把沒有武器的同學留在後方，旅程仍不安全。 一名證人在匆忙的敘述中提到「水龍頭」；任先記下來，沒有猜測它有多重要。有人問「圍籬」是否與麻煩有關，房間一時間安靜下來。優衣圈起「羊群」那一欄，問這個細節是否真的屬於本次事件。

They began with an experiment small enough to stop. Yui watched the entrance, while Ren compared what happened with the notes in the skill notebook. A result arrived that neither expected. Rather than hiding it, they wrote it down beside their original guess. The difference was useful because it told them where not to send anybody. In Aster Kingdom, a wrong turn was more than a mistake on paper; it could change a stranger's day. Yui checked whether students with weaker classes were still included in the plan. At first the change seemed juicy; a second observation made the team less certain. Yui circled the entry for lightning and asked whether the detail belonged to this incident. Yui wrote mental beside the early proposal, then added a question mark because opinions differed.

他們先做一項隨時能停止的小實驗。優衣守著入口，任則將觀察到的結果與技能筆記裡的記錄比對。結果出乎兩人意料，他們沒有藏起來，而是寫在原始猜測旁邊。這個差異很有用，因為它指出不該把任何人送往哪裡。在星辰王國，走錯路不只是紙上的錯誤，也可能改變陌生人的一天。優衣確認職業較弱的同學沒有被排除在計畫之外。 起初這項變化看來「多汁的」，第二次觀察卻讓團隊不再那麼確定。優衣圈起「閃電」那一欄，問這個細節是否真的屬於本次事件。優衣在初步提案旁寫下「心理的」，又加了問號，因為意見並不一致。

Yui read the last line aloud. Ren checked the date and kept a copy for the morning team. Yui circled the entry for microwave and asked whether the detail belonged to this incident. Yui wrote naked beside the early proposal, then added a question mark because opinions differed.

優衣朗讀最後一行。任核對日期，為早班小組留下副本。 優衣圈起「微波爐」那一欄，問這個細節是否真的屬於本次事件。優衣在初步提案旁寫下「光禿的」，又加了問號，因為意見並不一致。

In the corridor, Ren overheard a confident explanation that left out one uncomfortable fact. Yui had heard it too. Neither interrupted with an accusation. They found the skill notebook, checked the old note, and approached the speaker with the exact sentence in question. The answer showed confusion rather than malice. That discovery did not remove the danger, but it kept them from blaming the wrong person while the real problem continued. The class panels shone above the students’ hands, but Ren’s remained empty. He copied the movement from a carpenter rather than a wizard, and the first attempt hurt his fingers. Yui circled the entry for nest and asked whether the detail belonged to this incident. Yui wanted to pollute, but agreed to wait for one more safe test.

在走廊裡，任聽見有人自信解釋，卻漏掉一件令人不快的事實。優衣也聽見了。兩人沒有立刻指責，而是拿出技能筆記，核對舊紀錄，帶著確切原句詢問。對方的回答顯示是誤解而非惡意。這沒有解除危險，卻避免他們責怪錯人，讓真正問題繼續存在。同學手上的職業面板發著光，任的面板卻仍空白。他模仿的是木匠而非巫師的動作，第一次練習弄痛了手指。 優衣圈起「鳥巢」那一欄，問這個細節是否真的屬於本次事件。優衣想要「污染」，但同意先等一次安全測試的結果。

Yui read the last line aloud. Ren checked the date and kept a copy for the morning team. Yui circled the entry for protection and asked whether the detail belonged to this incident. The margin of the skill notebook contained a reference to shovel, which Yui marked for a later check.

優衣朗讀最後一行。任核對日期，為早班小組留下副本。 優衣圈起「保護」那一欄，問這個細節是否真的屬於本次事件。技能筆記的頁邊提到了「鏟子」，優衣做上記號，準備日後查證。

Ren left the skill notebook open so Yui could add the next finding. Neither could see the whole road yet. Yui circled the entry for sin and asked whether the detail belonged to this incident. The margin of the skill notebook contained a reference to yolk, which Yui marked for a later check.

任讓技能筆記保持打開，好讓優衣補上新的發現。兩人都還看不見整段路。 優衣圈起「罪惡」那一欄，問這個細節是否真的屬於本次事件。技能筆記的頁邊提到了「蛋黃」，優衣做上記號，準備日後查證。
