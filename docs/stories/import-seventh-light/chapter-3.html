平凡職責清單

A List of Ordinary Duties

第七盞燈 · 第 3 / 25 章 · 3000–4000 · 英中對照

The island council assigned Lia a room in the rescue station, but Tomas suspected the assignment was meant to keep her under observation. The building was plain; across from the equipment lockers, volunteers hung photographs of storms and departures. A lifeguard named Niko offered to show them the ferry schedule. He was seventeen, eager to make a good impression, and embarrassed that his first duty was housework: cleaning the kitchen after an overnight shift. “An incredible inspiration for an award-winning photographer,” he joked, holding up a burnt piece of toast. Lia told him that context mattered. People who saved lives still had to wash cups. Tomas smiled; he had been trying for months to defend the boy's right to be useful without pretending to be fearless.

島議會安排黎雅住進救援站，但托瑪斯懷疑這項任務分派是為了監視她。救援站樸素，器材櫃對面掛著多年風暴與離別的照片。十七歲救生員尼可主動拿渡輪時刻表給他們看。他急著留下好印象，卻又難為情，因為當夜班後第一件工作竟是家事：清理廚房。「得獎攝影師一定覺得這是驚人的靈感，」他舉起焦掉的吐司打趣。黎雅說情境很重要，救人者也得洗杯子。托瑪斯笑了；他一直想讓男孩明白，不假裝無懼也能成為有用的人。

Niko had prepared a ration chart that calculated each worker's daily calorie needs. He insisted that anyone who expected to keep a steady pace through storms should eat properly. His plan allowed a maximum of two passengers per rescue boat during rough water, even when wealthy visitors demanded luxurious treatment. One hotel manager had insulted him for refusing an extra seat. The rescue chief supported him: a small percentage of broken safety rules caused a large proportion of preventable accidents. Mira examined Niko's chart and added a column for fresh drinking water. “Excellent priorities,” she said. “Now let us concentrate on the fact that the spring east of the fort has stopped flowing.”

尼可準備了口糧表，計算每位工作者每天需要多少熱量。他堅持，要在暴風中保持穩定步調，就必須好好吃飯。救援船的規則是巨浪時最多載兩位乘客，即使有錢旅客要求豪華待遇也一樣。一名飯店經理曾因他拒絕加座而辱罵他。救援隊長支持尼可：少數違規往往造成大量可避免的事故。米拉看過他的表格，加了一欄飲水量。「優先順序很好，」她說，「現在集中注意力：堡壘東側的泉水已經斷流。」

Lia used photography to document the dry spring, the altered cliff, and footprints beneath a newly installed fence. Her editor messaged her to ask whether she had finally found proof of wrongdoing. “Not yet,” she replied. “Only proof that something has changed.” Tomas disliked the distinction. To him, his father's disappearance already explained everything. But Mira insisted that a fair investigation must proceed in stages. She offered an intermediate plan: measure the remaining wells, interview the families, and compare the results with older maps before accusing anyone. Tomas accepted the plan without enthusiasm. Later, he admitted to Lia that patience felt like a luxury when you feared somebody you loved might still be alive and waiting.

黎雅用攝影記錄乾涸泉水、被改動的山壁，以及新圍欄下的腳印。編輯傳訊問她終於找到不法證據了嗎。「還沒，」她回覆，「只證明有東西變了。」托瑪斯不喜歡這種區別。在他看來，父親失蹤已說明一切；米拉卻強調，公平調查必須分階段進行。她提出中程方案：測量其他水井、訪問住戶，並與舊地圖比對後再指控。托瑪斯不情願地答應。稍後他向黎雅承認，當你怕心愛的人還活著等待救援，耐心就像奢侈品。

By evening, Niko had brought them food wrapped in wax paper: dry biscuits, a few raisins, and fish cooked by his grandmother. He pointed to a narrow path on the eastern shore that the hotel called private property. Children still used it to collect shells, and the rescue team had once marked it as an emergency exit. The new fence blocked it. Lia stared at the handwritten map; two red lines had been placed exactly where her photograph suggested a seventh lighthouse should stand. Someone had not only changed the coast. Someone had changed the available routes to safety. She asked Niko to carry the chart to Rosa, but he asked to stay. “I was assigned to help,” he said, “and I intend to finish.”

傍晚尼可帶來蠟紙包著的食物：乾餅乾、葡萄乾與祖母煎的魚。他指著東岸一條被飯店稱作私有地的小路。孩子仍走那裡撿貝殼，救援隊也曾把它標為緊急出口；如今被新圍欄封住。黎雅望著手繪圖，發現兩條紅線正好落在照片顯示第七座燈塔的位置。有人不只改變海岸，也改變人們通往安全地帶的路。她請尼可將圖交給蘿莎，尼可卻想留下。「我的任務就是幫忙，」他說，「我會做完。」

A sudden siren interrupted their discussion. A passenger ferry was approaching too quickly through rising fog, with no response from its captain. Niko moved first, checking ropes, calling for volunteers, and preparing the smallest rescue boat. Lia stayed clear of the dock until the equipment was ready; the best story would mean nothing if her presence distracted people from their work. Tomas took the controls and steered toward the vessel, while Mira watched the depth markers with a torch. For a moment all their disagreements disappeared beneath a simpler obligation: bring people home. Yet when the ferry finally emerged, it was empty. On its deck lay a camera identical to the one Tomas's missing father had carried.

警報忽然打斷討論。一艘客運渡輪在濃霧中過快駛近，船長卻沒有回應。尼可率先檢查纜繩、呼叫志工，準備最小的救援艇。黎雅等器材就位才走到碼頭，因為再好的故事也不值得妨礙救援。托瑪斯掌舵，米拉持手電筒觀察水深標記。短暫片刻，所有爭執都被一個更簡單的責任取代：把人帶回家。然而渡輪穿出霧幕時，裡頭竟空無一人。甲板上放著一台相機，與托瑪斯失蹤父親帶走的那台一模一樣。
