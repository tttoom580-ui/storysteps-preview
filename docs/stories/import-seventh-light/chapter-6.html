上鎖倉庫裡的亞麻布

Linen in the Locked Warehouse

第七盞燈 · 第 6 / 25 章 · 3000–4000 · 英中對照 · 校訂中

用互動版查詞、朗讀與收藏

The morning after the lantern protest, Lia was summoned to meet Gavin Rowe, a conservative consultant hired by Helix Tide. He spoke in the polished language of investment, consumer confidence, and premium goods. The company, he said, wanted only to guide the island toward luxury tourism. He even enclosed a printed proposal showing new shops, clean docks, and salaries far higher than anything fishing could provide. Tomas listened in silence until Gavin described the eastern warehouse as an isolated storage area with no public value. At that, Rosa's hands began to tremble. She said the building once stored relief food and linen after storms, and that no outsider had the grace to call it useless while families still remembered standing there in wet clothes.

提燈抗議隔天早晨，黎雅被叫去見海潮螺旋公司聘來的顧問蓋文・羅威。這位保守派顧問說話圓滑，張口閉口都是投資、消費者信心與高級商品。他聲稱公司唯一目標，是引導島嶼走向奢華觀光，還附上一份包裝精美的計畫書，裡頭有新商店、整潔碼頭，以及遠高於漁業的薪資。托瑪斯一直沉默，直到蓋文把東側倉庫形容成與世隔絕、毫無公共價值的儲藏區。蘿莎的手立刻顫抖起來。她說，那棟建築在暴風雨後曾存放救濟糧與亞麻布，當年全家淋得發抖的人還活著，外來者沒有這種風度把它說成沒用。

That afternoon, Mira persuaded the guard to let them inspect the warehouse under the pretense of checking flood damage. Inside, old shelves carried bolts of linen stained with a blue dye. Under a microscope, the color showed tiny crystals similar to the ones found in the underground chamber. Niko discovered a stereo hidden behind boxes of canned goods. When he pressed play, Elena's voice filled the room. She said she had entered the building while tracing her father's last occupation routes, and that the dye was not decorative at all. It marked water that had leaked through the walls at every high tide. Anyone who followed the stains to their source, she warned, would find what the company had tried hardest to hide.

下午，米拉藉口檢查水患痕跡，說服守衛讓他們進倉庫。裡面老舊架子上堆著成匹亞麻布，表面染著藍色染料。放到顯微鏡下，那些顏色裡竟帶有與地底密室水晶相似的微小結晶。尼可又在一堆罐頭旁發現一台立體聲播放器。按下播放鍵，傳出的竟是艾蓮娜的聲音。她說自己曾沿著父親最後的工作路線潛入此地，而那些染料根本不是裝飾，而是用來標記每次滿潮時從牆壁滲出的海水。她警告，任何人若沿著染色痕跡追到源頭，就會看見公司最想藏起來的東西。

Gavin returned before sunset, his patience thinner than before. He accused them of stirring rage among timid residents who did not understand development. Lia answered that ignorance was not a natural condition; it was something powerful people cultivated when they wanted easy obedience. Gavin smiled as if she were a child. Then he asked her what occupation she preferred for the island's youth: fishing in dangerous weather or greeting visitors at profitable hotels. Lia admitted the question was clever and false at the same time. A community did not need to choose between poverty and surrender. The real issue was whether Helix Tide had the right to isolate emergency paths and hide dangerous data behind attractive slogans. Gavin's habitual smile did not answer her question. Calling residents ignorant was easier than showing them the warehouse records.

黃昏前，蓋文又回來了，耐性顯然比早上更薄。他指控他們煽動本就膽怯、又不懂發展的居民憤怒。黎雅回答，無知不是天生狀態，而是掌權者想要方便服從時刻意培養的結果。蓋文露出像對小孩微笑般的表情，反問她：島上年輕人究竟該做什麼職業？在危險海象中捕魚，還是在賺錢的飯店向觀光客問好？黎雅承認這問題聰明，卻也虛假。社群不必在貧窮與投降之間二選一，真正的問題在於，海潮螺旋有沒有權利封鎖逃生路、再用漂亮口號掩蓋危險數據。蓋文慣常掛著的微笑並沒有回答她的問題。稱居民無知，總比拿出倉庫紀錄容易。

That night Tomas traced the blue stains to a crack behind the warehouse furnace. A narrow shaft dropped toward the sea. Cold air blew upward, and from below came a dull murmur like machinery breathing in its sleep. Niko, usually talkative, became timid when the ladder shook beneath him, but he waited on the landing for the shaft to be checked.

當晚，托瑪斯沿著藍色痕跡追到倉庫火爐後的一道裂縫。狹窄豎井一路通往海邊，冷風自下往上灌，底部傳來像機器呼吸的低沉聲響。平常話多的尼可看見梯子搖晃，變得膽怯；這次他留在平台上，等候檢查。

Before anyone touched the ladder, Mira asked the guard to check the shaft from above. He brought a lamp, and Tomas held it while Niko tested the first rung from the landing. They agreed to leave the lower chamber immediately if water began rising. Lia stayed beside the opening with the guard, writing down the time. The narrow space offered a clue, but it was not worth losing another person to the sea. After the check, Tomas retrieved an oilcloth-wrapped message from the reachable lower landing while the others watched the water: FOLLOW WHERE THE DYE BLEEDS AT LOW TIDE. He folded the note with unusual care. His rage finally had an orientation, a route, and a witness willing to help him walk it.

任何人碰梯子前，米拉先請警衛從上方檢查豎井。警衛拿來燈，托瑪斯舉著，尼可則站在平台試第一個梯階。他們約定，水位一旦上升就立即離開底下空間。黎雅和警衛留在入口，記下時間。狹小空間雖有線索，卻不值得讓大海再奪走一個人。檢查後，托瑪斯從可以安全抵達的下層平台取回一封油布包住的訊息，其他人則留意水位：「退潮時，沿著染料滲出的方向走。」他異常仔細地摺好紙條。他的憤怒終於有了方向、一條路，以及願意陪他走下去的見證人。
