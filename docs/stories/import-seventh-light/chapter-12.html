The Faithful Agent

第七盞燈 · 第 12 / 25 章 · 3000–4000 · 英中對照 · 校訂中

用互動版查詞、朗讀與收藏

Elena confessed that they still needed an internal agent. Unexpectedly, one appeared before dawn: Mara, a clerk from Helix Tide's office, arrived carrying a bin full of copied invoices, a tribal bracelet, and a look of pure terror. She said a burglar had broken into the records room the previous night. The company blamed local teenagers, but Mara had seen the truth: executives were destroying their own files to erase the procedure for moving hazardous material. She had been faithful to her job for years, believing the work might protect the island from hardship and inflation. Now she knew her loyalty had been abused.

艾蓮娜坦白說，他們仍需要一名內應。沒想到，天亮前真的有人出現了：海潮螺旋辦公室文員瑪拉抱著一只裝滿發票副本、部族手環與驚恐神情的箱子跑來。她說昨夜紀錄室發生『竊賊闖入』事件，公司怪罪本地青少年，但她看見的真相完全不同：高層正在自行毀壞文件，好抹掉危險物質轉運程序。多年來她一直忠於職務，還以為自己的工作能讓島嶼免於艱困與通膨，如今才知道，那份忠誠只是被利用了。

Among the rescued papers were shipping records from several eras, occupancy lists for the platform, and a definite instruction to pursue legal action against anyone who photographed unauthorized cargo. Lewis nearly laughed at the panic behind such threats. Real power, he said, rarely sues unless it is afraid. Mara also described a tragic accident from months earlier: a worker exposed to leaking material had been quietly removed and never mentioned again. His family received money and an order to remain silent. Rosa touched the tribal bracelet Mara had brought and recognized the pattern. It belonged to that worker's mother.

那些救出的紙張裡，有分年度的運輸紀錄、平台入住名單，還有一則明確命令：任何拍到未經授權貨物的人，都要被追究法律責任。路易斯幾乎笑出來，因為在他看來，只有害怕的人才會急著用訴訟堵住別人的嘴。瑪拉還提到幾個月前一場悲劇：一名工人接觸到外洩物質後被悄悄帶走，從此再也沒有被提起。他的家屬收到錢，同時被命令保持沉默。蘿莎撫摸瑪拉帶來的部族手環，一眼就認出那是那位工人母親的東西。

Mara kept looking at the door as Lewis cleared a place for her papers. Rosa pulled a chair away from the window and asked whether she wanted the others to leave. Mara shook her head. She wanted witnesses, she said, but she did not want her home address written on a page that everyone could read. Lia put down her camera until they had agreed which details could be shared. Then she photographed the documents one at a time, leaving Mara's frightened face outside the frame. Mara had watched the destruction of records and feared her name would be next. She had not come for fame. Rosa let her occupy the sheltered chair and wait until she felt ready to let the details emerge in her own words.

路易斯替文件清出空間時，瑪拉一直回頭看門。羅莎把椅子拉離窗邊，問她是否希望其他人離開。她搖頭，說自己需要見證人，卻不想把住址寫在人人可讀的紙上。黎雅先放下相機，等大家確認哪些資訊能公開，再逐張拍文件，不把瑪拉驚恐的臉拍進去。瑪拉親眼看過紀錄遭到銷毀，害怕自己的名字就是下一個目標。她不是為了名聲而來。蘿莎讓她坐在較隱蔽的椅子上，等她準備好，再用自己的話逐一說出細節。

The bracelet required a different kind of care. Mara had picked it up after the worker was taken away; she could describe the desk where she found it, but not everything that had happened to its owner. Rosa asked permission before contacting his mother. Recognition might help establish a connection, she told Tomas, yet it would not prove the cause of an illness. Lewis began a list with three headings: what the papers said, what Mara had personally seen, and what someone still needed to verify. Mara read every line. At the bottom she corrected a date herself, pressing so hard that the pencil almost tore the wet paper. Nobody treated the correction as a betrayal. It made the account hers again, instead of another statement written by people who claimed to know her life better than she did.

手環需要另一種謹慎。工人被帶走後，瑪拉撿到它；她能描述發現它的桌子，卻不知道主人遭遇的全部事情。羅莎徵得同意才聯絡工人的母親。她告訴托瑪斯，辨認手環或許有助建立關聯，卻不能證明疾病的原因。路易斯分三欄記錄：文件寫了什麼、瑪拉親眼見到什麼，以及還要查證什麼。瑪拉逐行閱讀，最後親手改了一個日期，鉛筆幾乎劃破潮紙。沒人把修正當成背叛。這份陳述重新屬於她，不再是那些自稱更懂她人生的人替她寫的話。

The new evidence transformed the rescue station into an archive of resistance. Papers dried on clotheslines; comments were added in the margins; Tomas and Elena committed themselves to matching every name with a witness. Lia photographed each page before the ink could fade. Outside, rain began again, hard and durable, drumming on the roof like warning fingers. Whatever came next would no longer be a private mystery. It would be a public record, and that fact frightened the company more than any storm.

新的證據讓救援站瞬間變成反抗檔案室。紙張掛在晾衣繩上風乾，邊角被補上註解；托瑪斯與艾蓮娜則承諾，要替每一個名字都找到見證人。黎雅趕在墨水褪色前一一拍下。屋外雨勢又起，堅硬耐久地敲打屋頂，像一排警告的手指。接下來不再只是私人謎團，而將成為公開紀錄；這件事，比任何暴風都更讓公司害怕。
