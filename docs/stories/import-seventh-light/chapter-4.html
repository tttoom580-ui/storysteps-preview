Instructions from a Missing Man

第七盞燈 · 第 4 / 25 章 · 3000–4000 · 英中對照

用互動版查詞、朗讀與收藏

Mira accompanied Tomas aboard the empty ferry while Lia photographed the abandoned cabin. A folded diagram lay beneath the wheel, drafted in the handwriting of Tomas's father. It marked an experimental underwater passage connecting the fort to an offshore platform. Attached was a manual with three essential instructions: do not open the lower gate at high tide; do not loosen the pressure bolts without measuring the water; and if the horizon flashes green, flee to the northern ridge. Tomas read the final warning twice. He had spent a year wanting to believe his father had escaped. Now this sheet offered evidence that the missing man had been working on something far larger than a broken lighthouse.

米拉陪托瑪斯登上空渡輪，黎雅則拍攝遭棄置的船艙。方向盤下壓著一張手繪圖，筆跡正是托瑪斯父親的。圖上標了石堡通往離岸平台的實驗水道，旁附操作手冊與三項要點：滿潮不得開下閘門；未量水位不得鬆開壓力螺栓；若地平線閃綠光，立即逃往北脊。托瑪斯把最後一句讀了兩次。一年來他一直盼望父親逃出生天，而今紙張證明，父親參與的事遠大於修理一座壞燈塔。

They found more than tools in the cabin: a diplomat's visiting card, a broken satellite receiver, and a greeting signed by an engineer from another island. The message praised Tomas's father for his outstanding courage, then asked him to keep the project secret. Mira disliked the words “courage” and “secret” appearing together on an official page. Diplomacy, she told Niko, depended on honest disagreement as much as polite greetings. It was not a magician's trick for turning dangers invisible. Meanwhile Tomas studied the boat's engine. A mechanic could tell when a vessel had been moved recently; the oil was warm, and a fresh scrape crossed the metal guard. Someone had abandoned the ferry within the previous hour.

船艙裡不只有工具，還有外交官名片、損壞的衛星接收器，以及另一座島工程師簽署的問候信。信中稱讚托瑪斯父親有傑出的勇氣，隨後卻要求他保密。米拉不喜歡「勇氣」與「秘密」同時出現在公文上。她告訴尼可，外交除了禮貌問候，也需要誠實的歧見；它不是把危險變不見的魔術。托瑪斯則查看引擎。機械師能分辨船是否剛被開動：機油尚溫，金屬護罩也有新刮痕。有人在一小時內才把渡輪丟下。

At the rescue station, Rosa recognized a symbol in the manual. Decades ago the island had used the cave network for recreation tours, until a germ outbreak forced officials to close the passages. The story was familiar to every schoolchild, but the records had disappeared. Rosa remembered that her husband, a harbor worker, had described an underground chamber with magnificent blue crystals. He later swore never to speak of it again. When Lia asked why, Rosa hesitated. “I do not know what they threatened,” she said. “I only know he became quiet whenever he saw the company flag.” A silence settled over the room as firmly as dust on an unused desk.

回到救援站，蘿莎認出手冊裡的記號。幾十年前島上曾把洞穴系統開放遊憩導覽，直到一場病菌疫情迫使當局封閉水道。這故事每個學童都聽過，紀錄卻已不見。蘿莎記得丈夫曾說地下有一處長著壯麗藍色晶體的空間，之後便發誓絕不再提。黎雅問原因，蘿莎遲疑了。「我不知道他們威脅過什麼，」她說，「只知道他每次看到公司旗幟就會沉默。」房裡的寂靜像廢桌上的灰塵般厚重。

Mira wanted to strengthen their evidence before going public. She proposed a controlled test of the water channels using harmless colored markers and remote sensors. Tomas objected that the company would detect any unusual activity. Lia suggested another attraction: the annual lantern walk scheduled for the following evening. Hundreds of visitors would be watching the coast, creating enough movement to conceal a small team. Niko volunteered to carry the receiver up the ridge, where its signal might reach a satellite. The plan required everyone to trust measurements rather than rumors. It also required them to accept that the missing father might have left warnings precisely because he had known no rescue could reach him in time. Tomas's hesitation came from knowing how quickly a hidden test could be mistaken for an attack. Lia asked him to help write down the purpose before anyone set out.

米拉希望公開前先補強證據，提議用無害的染色標記與遙測感測器測試水道。托瑪斯擔心公司察覺異常。黎雅想到隔晚的年度提燈健行，數百遊客會望向海岸，足以掩護小隊活動。尼可自願背接收器上北脊，好讓訊號接通衛星。計畫要求每個人相信測量，而不是謠言；同時也得接受，失蹤父親留下警告，或許正因他知道救援已來不及。托瑪斯之所以遲疑，是因為他知道，秘密測試很容易被誤認成攻擊。黎雅請他先協助寫清楚目的，再讓任何人出發。

Before dusk, Tomas led Lia to the beach where his father had taught him to repair fishing nets. He admitted that every familiar place had become a question he could not answer. She told him her own father had vanished from her life in a much less dramatic way: he simply stopped coming home when she was twelve, leaving her mother to explain what no child could understand. They shared neither history nor explanation, but the feeling of an unfinished sentence was the same. In the fading light Tomas unfolded the manual once more. On its last page, beneath a drawing of seven lights, someone had added a line in fresh ink: THE SEA DOES NOT KEEP WHAT WE REFUSE TO NAME.

黃昏前，托瑪斯帶黎雅來到父親教他補漁網的海灘。他坦承每個熟悉的地方都變成無法回答的疑問。黎雅說自己的父親失去得沒那麼戲劇化：她十二歲時，父親只是不再回家，留下母親解釋孩子聽不懂的事。兩人的經歷不同，卻共享著一句話說到一半就停住的感覺。暮光裡，托瑪斯再次翻開手冊。最後一頁、七盞燈的畫下方，有人用新墨水寫著：「大海不會替我們保管不敢說出的名字。」
