積塵電報裡的密碼

Code from the Dusty Telegraph

第七盞燈 · 第 11 / 25 章 · 3000–4000 · 英中對照 · 校訂中

Following the map from the tomb, the group entered the old telegraph house above the harbor. Dusty coils, cracked glass, and a wrecked signal arm filled the room, yet someone had recently installed small solar panels on the roof. Elena emerged from behind a screen of rope and smiled at Tomas with a mixture of shame and relief. She had not betrayed the family, she said; she had stayed away because she feared anyone close to her would be watched. For months she had circulated coded notes through fish crates and ferry receipts, trying to negotiate with workers who wanted to help but feared the consequence of open rebellion.

沿著墓室裡的地圖，眾人來到港口上方一間老舊電報屋。屋內滿是積塵線圈、裂玻璃與毀損的訊號桿，但屋頂上顯然才剛裝好小型太陽能板。艾蓮娜從一排繩索後現身，對托瑪斯露出既羞愧又如釋重負的笑。她說自己沒有背叛家人，只是害怕任何靠近她的人都會被監視。數月來，她把密碼訊息藏進魚箱與渡輪收據間流通，試著和想幫忙、卻畏懼後果的工人談判。

Elena showed them a codebook written in her father's hand. It linked tide numbers to storage rooms and vessel routes. One entry described a dominant investor who wanted the island expanded into an energy port within the year. Another warned that capitalism became cruelest when it wore the language of rescue. Lia thought of Gavin's polished smile and felt an almost physical chill. The code also revealed an eventual schedule: on the next spring tide, waste drums would be moved into the underground chamber and sealed there permanently. If that happened, the entire eastern water system could be poisoned.

艾蓮娜拿出一本父親筆記寫成的密碼簿，裡頭把潮位數字和倉庫房號、船隻路線連結在一起。某一頁記錄了一位主導投資人，打算在一年內把島嶼擴張成能源港；另一頁則警告，資本主義最殘酷時，常披著救援的語言。黎雅想到蓋文那張光滑的笑臉，幾乎覺得一陣寒意可以摸得到。密碼還揭露了一個最終時程：下一次大潮時，廢料桶將被移入地下密室，永久封存。若真如此，整個東側水系都可能遭污染。

Tomas had imagined this reunion so often that he had prepared an angry speech. When Elena finally stood before him, he could remember none of it. He asked whether she had eaten. She laughed, then began to cry without making a sound. Lia gave them room and examined the table by the window. Beside a cold cup stood a stack of ferry receipts, each dated and folded in the same direction. Elena had been building an archive while everyone else thought she had abandoned them.

托瑪斯想像過無數次重逢，甚至準備好一番怒斥。可是艾蓮娜真正站在眼前時，他一句也想不起來，只問她吃飯沒有。她笑了，接著無聲地哭起來。黎雅留給兩人空間，走去查看窗邊桌子。冷掉的杯子旁疊著渡輪收據，每張都註明日期、朝同一方向折好。當大家以為她拋下他們時，她其實一直在建立檔案。

Before they used the codebook, Elena worked through one ordinary delivery with Lewis. A number that looked like a date referred to a room, while the following mark described the tide. Lewis checked the route against a receipt they already understood. Only then did they try the entry about the spring tide. They copied the result twice and stored the original apart from the working notes. Tomas wanted to carry the book himself, but Elena asked him to trust a plan that did not depend on one person staying safe. He gave the second copy to Lia. That small movement cost him more than he expected: he was learning that protecting his sister meant sharing responsibility, not keeping every danger in his own hands. Dust began to tickle Niko's nose, and he stepped outside rather than sneeze over the book. Lewis marked the verified entry as an indication, not a final answer. Secrecy could breed fear, Elena said, but calling her a rebel would not make her father's numbers disappear; no amount of persuasion could replace checking them.

使用密碼本前，艾蓮娜和路易斯先試解一筆普通送貨紀錄。看似日期的數字其實指房間，後面的記號才代表潮汐。路易斯拿已知收據核對路線，確認方法後，才解讀大潮那筆。他們抄了兩份結果，把原本與工作筆記分開保存。托瑪斯想親自帶著本子，艾蓮娜卻請他相信一個不必仰賴單一人平安的計畫。他把第二份副本交給黎雅。這小動作比想像中難：他正學習，保護妹妹代表分擔責任，而非把所有危險握在自己手裡。灰塵讓尼可鼻子發癢，他走到外面，以免朝密碼簿打噴嚏。路易斯把核對過的條目標為線索，而非最終答案。艾蓮娜說，保密會滋生恐懼，但就算把她叫作叛逆者，也不會讓父親留下的數字消失；再多勸說都不能取代查核。

That night Niko ran a marathon of errands between the rescue station, the school, and the harbor, carrying copies of the code. Children helped by memorizing short phrases and whispering them house to house. Rosa called it an invasion of truth. Tomas, seeing his sister again in the telegraph light, no longer felt only anger. There was loss, yes, and many questions still, but there was also a graceful stubbornness in Elena's survival that reminded him of their father. For the first time, their scattered clues had become a plan.

當晚，尼可像跑馬拉松一樣，在救援站、學校與港口之間來回奔波，傳送密碼副本。孩子們也幫忙，把短句背熟，再一戶一戶悄聲轉告。蘿莎說，這簡直像是真相對謊言的反向入侵。托瑪斯在電報燈下重新看見妹妹，不再只剩怒氣。的確有失落，也仍有太多問題，但艾蓮娜能活下來的那份優雅倔強，又讓他想起父親。第一次，零散線索終於開始變成可執行的計畫。
