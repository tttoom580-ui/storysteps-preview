海灣婚禮上的新郎

The Groom at the Gulf

第七盞燈 · 第 8 / 25 章 · 3000–4000 · 英中對照 · 校訂中

The mayor's nephew celebrated his marriage at the gulf resort, and the company sponsored every lantern, flower arch, and borrowed violin. Lia acquired a caterer's pass by agreeing to photograph the ceremony. It was almost absurdly convenient: the bridegroom wore a white suit, the guests received a printed bulletin, and the reception hall faced the restricted shore. Mira believed the wedding offered the perfect cue for slipping into the administrative office, where Helix Tide stored its software backups. Rosa disliked the plan, but admitted that a noisy celebration was often the best cover for serious work. Tomas, dressed in a server's jacket, looked so uncomfortable that Niko nearly laughed.

市長外甥在海灣渡假飯店舉行婚禮，而公司包下了每一盞燈、每一道花拱門，以及借來的提琴手。黎雅答應替婚禮拍照，因而取得餐飲工作證。整件事幾乎方便得荒謬：新郎穿白西裝，來賓手上有印好的婚禮快訊，而宴會廳的窗戶正對著受限東岸。米拉認為這正是潛入行政辦公室的最佳提示時機，因為海潮螺旋把軟體備份都留在那裡。蘿莎不喜歡這個計畫，但也承認，最喧鬧的慶典往往最適合掩護嚴肅行動。穿著侍者外套的托瑪斯滿身不自在，尼可差點笑出來。

Inside, the event revealed more than flowers and speeches. Company managers dominated the guest list, while actual island civilians were seated near the back like decorative cousins. A learned grandmother from the bride's family complained that men always arranged celebrations according to money and gender, then asked women to provide enthusiasm for free. Her sharp observation delighted Lia. Meanwhile, Lewis copied files from an open terminal and estimated the true intensity of the company's emergency plans. The documents classified local families by usefulness: fishermen for transport, teachers for childcare, older residents as relocation obstacles. The coldness of the system was far more shocking than any rumor.

走進宴會裡，眾人看到的不只是花與致詞。公司主管幾乎主宰賓客名單，真正的島上平民則像陪襯親戚，被安排在後排。一位來自新娘家的博學祖母抱怨，男人總依照金錢與性別安排慶典，卻要女人免費提供熱情。黎雅聽得心花怒放。與此同時，路易斯從一台未鎖的終端機複製檔案，估算公司緊急計畫的真正強度。文件竟把在地家庭分成功能類別：漁民負責運輸、教師負責托兒、老人則被視為安置障礙。這種系統性的冷酷，比任何謠言都更令人震驚。

Tomas and Niko slipped through a service corridor and found a wall map marked with abstract symbols. One icon represented the underground chamber, another the fuel depot, and a third the place where the seventh light had most often appeared. Attached to the board was a note about potential civilian resistance and methods to dominate local information. The phrasing made Tomas sick. These were not partners planning safety; they were occupiers planning obedience. Then footsteps sounded in the hall. Niko gave the agreed cue—a cough followed by a dropped spoon—and everyone froze while a guard passed by carrying champagne for the bridal table.

托瑪斯與尼可溜進一條服務走廊，發現牆上掛著一幅標滿抽象符號的地圖。一個圖示代表地底密室，一個代表燃料庫，第三個則標出第七盞燈最常出現的位置。地圖旁還附著一張便條，寫著如何面對『平民阻力』，以及主導地方資訊的方法。這種措辭讓托瑪斯一陣反胃。這不是在規劃安全的夥伴，而是規劃服從的占領者。就在這時，走廊傳來腳步聲。尼可依約發出暗號——先咳一聲，再故意掉落湯匙——所有人立刻僵住，只等一名端著香檳走向主桌的守衛經過。

At the dunes, Lewis separated the official bulletin from the files copied at the terminal. He wrote down where each had come from and who had handled the memory card. Rosa insisted that no guest's private photographs should be published with the evacuation material. Lia agreed and put the wedding pictures in a different folder. The guests had come to celebrate a marriage. Their faces were not evidence against the company, and the investigation had no reason to turn their happy evening into a public accusation. Lia made a systematic list of the files connected to the shore. She would not invade the wedding guests' privacy to make her report more dramatic. Even the composer's name on the music program belonged to the celebration, not their inquiry; whatever jealousy or family arguments the cameras had caught were irrelevant.

在沙丘邊，路易斯把正式公告與從電腦複製的檔案分開，記下各自來源及誰碰過記憶卡。羅莎堅持，不能把賓客私人照片和疏散資料一起公開。黎雅同意，將婚禮照片放進另一個資料夾。賓客是來慶祝婚姻，他們的臉不是指控公司的證據；調查也沒有理由讓他們愉快的夜晚變成公開指控。黎雅有系統地列出與海岸有關的檔案。她不會為了讓報導更戲劇化，就侵犯婚宴賓客的隱私。就連音樂節目單上的作曲家姓名，也屬於這場慶典，而非他們的調查；鏡頭拍到的嫉妒或家庭爭執，同樣與案情無關。

They escaped the resort with a memory card and a copy of the evacuation bulletin. Outside, the sea lay black and oddly fertile with reflected light from the wedding lanterns. Rosa met them near the dunes and looked at the papers in silence. “This constitutes intent,” Lewis finally said. “They are not merely preparing for an accident. They are preparing to manage the island after one.” Mira nodded. In the distance, the music ended, and the crowd cheered for the groom's final dance. At that exact moment, a thin seventh flash crossed the gulf. It made the newly decorated pier look less like a celebration and more like a trap.

眾人帶著記憶卡與一份疏散快報逃出渡假飯店。外頭海面漆黑，卻被婚禮燈籠映得異常豐饒。蘿莎在沙丘邊接應，接過文件後一語不發。最後是路易斯先開口：「這已構成故意行為。他們不只是在為意外做準備，而是在為『意外之後如何接管島嶼』做準備。」米拉點頭。遠方音樂聲止，賓客為新郎最後一支舞歡呼。就在那一刻，一道細長的第七道閃光劃過海灣。它讓那座新裝飾的碼頭看起來不再像慶典，而更像一個陷阱。
