The memory card contained a compressed audio archive and several scanned attachments. In one recording a man with a mainland accent instructed workers to transfer sealed drums at night. In another, a woman from the company's finance office complained that the island's economic circumstances made secrecy difficult, because everyone noticed when trucks moved after midnight. Lia listened through an earphone while Tomas leaned over her shoulder. The content was fragmentary, interrupted by static and the scrape of metal, yet certain words stood out with frightening clarity: nuclear shielding, waste inventory, emergency refund. Mira warned them not to leap beyond the evidence, but no one could mistake the direction of the clues.

記憶卡裡有一組壓縮音檔與幾份掃描附件。其中一段錄音裡，一名帶著外地口音的男子指示工人在夜裡轉移封存鐵桶；另一段裡，財務部的女人抱怨說，島上的經濟情況讓保密很困難，因為半夜有卡車移動，人人都會注意到。黎雅戴著耳機聽，托瑪斯則俯身在她肩旁。錄音內容零碎，夾雜雜訊與金屬刮擦，但有些字眼仍清楚得可怕：核防護、廢料清冊、緊急退款。米拉提醒大家，不要在證據之外亂下結論，可是沒人會看不出線索正指向哪裡。

Among the files was a photo of the old fort, annotated in clumsy handwriting. Someone had tried to illustrate how cargo moved from the harbor to the platform through a hidden lift. The margins were full of frowns, arrows, and one sarcastic wink drawn beside the phrase PUBLIC SAFETY. Niko peeped over the page and said the note looked like Elena's. Tomas was unsure. His sister had always written with modesty, never decorating her papers. Rosa suggested that long fear could change a person's style. When people wrote for survival instead of beauty, their handwriting often leaned toward urgency. That explanation, though simple, hurt Tomas more than he expected.

檔案裡還有一張舊石堡照片，旁邊用笨拙字跡註記。有人試圖說明貨物如何透過隱藏升降機，從港口運到平台。頁邊畫滿皺眉、箭頭，還在『公共安全』一詞旁諷刺地畫了一個眨眼記號。尼可探頭看過去，說那像艾蓮娜的字。托瑪斯卻拿不準；妹妹過去寫字總帶著一種克制，不會在頁邊亂畫。蘿莎說，長期恐懼會改變人的筆跡。當人寫字是為了活下來，不是為了漂亮時，筆畫就會傾向急促。這種簡單解釋，卻比任何複雜推理更刺痛托瑪斯。

By afternoon the company issued an innocent-looking refund notice for canceled sightseeing tours. That alone might have meant nothing, but the dates matched the nights described in the archive. Lia published a careful report on her website, choosing not to accuse anyone directly. Instead she laid out the circumstances, quoted the recordings, and asked why ordinary tourists had been cleared away whenever hidden transport occurred. The piece spread quickly. Some readers thanked her; others called her a revolutionary liar attacking the island's chance to prosper. Tomas expected the insults. What surprised him was how many messages came from emigrants who still called Morrow Island their homeland and begged for more information.

到了下午，公司又發布一則看似普通的退款通知，說是取消觀光行程。單看這件事本身不代表什麼，但那些日期正好對應檔案描述的夜晚。黎雅在自己的網站上發出一篇謹慎報導，沒有直接指控任何人，只陳列相關情況、引用錄音內容，並質問：為什麼每次暗中轉運貨物時，普通遊客都會被清走？文章迅速傳開。有人感謝她，也有人罵她是革命式騙子，企圖摧毀島嶼繁榮的機會。托瑪斯原以為自己早就準備好面對辱罵，沒想到更令他驚訝的是，竟有那麼多早已離島的人仍稱黎明島為故鄉，並懇求得到更多消息。

Before saving the report, Lia played the clearest recording to Mira without naming the speaker she suspected. Mira wrote what she could hear, then compared it with Lia's notes. They marked two uncertain words instead of filling them in from expectation. Tomas found the gaps frustrating, but he understood why they mattered. If they made the evidence sound more complete than it was, the company could use one careless sentence to discredit everything else they had found. Niko brought crunchy bread while they checked the words. Lia welcomed the interruption: hunger and haste could mislead a listener as easily as a poor recording.

存下報導前，黎雅讓米拉聽最清楚的錄音，卻沒先說自己懷疑誰。米拉寫下聽見的內容，再與黎雅筆記比較。兩個無法確定的字被標出，而非依期待補上。托瑪斯對空白很焦躁，仍明白它們的重要：只要把證據說得比實際完整，公司就可能抓住一句草率文字，否定他們所有發現。尼可在大家核對字句時拿來酥脆麵包。黎雅很歡迎這次短暫打斷：飢餓與急躁，和模糊的錄音一樣容易誤導聽者。

Near sunset, a child delivered a sealed envelope to the rescue station. Inside was a note signed only with the letter E. It instructed them to look under the old radio mast at low tide. “Come alone if you want truth,” the final line read, then added, almost playfully, “Bring no flags, bring no speeches.” Lia smiled despite the tension. Elena, if it was really Elena, had not lost her stubborn humor. Tomas folded the note and tucked it into his pocket. The sea outside darkened from silver to iron. Whatever waited under the mast, it no longer felt like a clue left for strangers. It felt like family calling home from inside a storm.

接近黃昏時，一名孩子把一只密封信封送到救援站。裡頭只有一張紙，署名僅有一個字母 E。信上要他們在退潮時前往舊無線電桿下方，還寫著：『若想要真相，就一個人來。』最後又像故意鬧脾氣般補上一句：『別帶旗幟，也別帶演講。』黎雅在緊張中仍忍不住笑了。如果那真是艾蓮娜，那她的固執幽默顯然還沒丟。托瑪斯把紙折好，收進口袋。窗外海色由銀轉鐵。無論舊桿下方等著什麼，那已不再像給陌生人的線索，而像一個身在風暴中的家人，終於朝故鄉發來呼喚。
