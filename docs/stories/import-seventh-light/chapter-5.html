提燈下的抗議

The Protest by Lantern Light

The lantern festival had always been a symbol of the island's prosperity. This year Helix Tide offered a generous allowance to every participating family, provided they agreed not to protest. Company officials had adjusted the parade route to abandon the eastern shore. Rosa objected publicly. “An allowance is not a price for silence,” she said. Her words affected people more deeply than the mayor's cheerful announcements. Lia watched a canoe cross the bay with a portable speaker tied to its bow. The canoe carried Niko, who used a childhood idiom to describe the new fence: a door painted on a wall is still a wall. His imaginative speech turned scattered complaints into a distinct demand: restore the emergency paths before the next storm.

提燈節原是黎明島繁榮的象徵。今年海潮螺旋公司給參與家庭優厚津貼，條件是不得抗議。公司人員更改遊行路線，放棄東岸。蘿莎公開反對：「津貼不是買沉默的價錢。」她的話比市長的歡樂宣告更能打動人。黎雅看到尼可乘獨木舟穿過海灣，船首綁著可攜喇叭。他用童年俗諺形容新圍欄：牆上畫著一扇門，終究還是一堵牆。這番富想像力的演講，把零散抱怨變成明確要求：下次風暴前恢復緊急通道。

The company sent an idol from a famous singing competition to lead the orchestra and distract the crowd. For a while it worked. Children cheered; parents held their lamps higher; the harbor rang with violins. Then a small electronic device in Mira's bag vibrated. Her dye experiment had begun to show a result: colored water released beside the fort was emerging near the company's new foundation. The impact was immediate. If ordinary tidal flow connected the two places, the firm's safety maps were misleading. Mira enlarged the photograph on her screen, careful not to announce a conclusion beyond the evidence. Her caution only increased Tomas's anxiety, because he could imagine what more disturbing results might come next.

公司請來選秀節目的偶像帶領管弦樂團，希望轉移群眾注意，一開始的確有效。孩子歡呼、家長高舉提燈，港口滿是小提琴聲。這時米拉包裡的小電子裝置震動：染色水實驗有結果了。從石堡放出的顏色，竟在公司的新地基旁浮現。影響立刻顯現：如果正常潮流讓兩處相通，公司安全地圖便具有誤導性。米拉把照片放大，小心不做超出證據的結論。她的謹慎反而加深托瑪斯的焦慮，因為他能想像接下來可能出現更可怕的結果。

A public officer seized Niko's speaker and demanded that the march end. Someone knocked over a lantern, and frightened children rushed toward the blocked eastern exit. Tomas pulled down one panel of temporary fence to create space. The crowd escaped without injury, but the officer accused him of damaging property. Helix Tide's lawyer filed an objection to the rescue team's actions, claiming the barrier had been installed under favorable conditions and with official approval. Lia recorded the entire incident. She knew the footage might protect Tomas, but it might also put the families who had helped him at risk. To keep their identities safe, she decided to publish the wide shot first and hold the close-ups.

一名公務員沒收尼可的喇叭，要求遊行結束。有人撞倒提燈，受驚孩子往被封的東側出口衝。托瑪斯拆下一片臨時圍欄，替人群開路，大家無傷撤離，但公務員指控他破壞財物。公司的律師反對救援隊行動，聲稱圍欄是在良好條件與官方核准下安裝。黎雅錄下全程。影像能保護托瑪斯，也可能讓協助者家庭暴露於危險。為了保護身分，她決定先公開遠景，暫時扣住近拍。

That evening the island council held an emergency meeting. The mayor argued that the port expansion would create jobs and attract investment; without it, the island might lose young families each year. Rosa acknowledged the fear behind his words. “But an improvement that takes away our only way out cannot be called progress,” she replied. A fisherman raised his hand and described a strange sound under his floor each midnight, as if a metal instrument were striking three careful syllables. Several neighbors nodded. Lia realized the noise seemed to match the rhythm of the seventh light. She asked permission to return the next night with sound equipment, and the council reluctantly agreed.

當晚島議會緊急開會。市長說港口擴建能創造工作、吸引投資，否則青年家庭將逐年離開。蘿莎承認他有真實的擔憂，但反駁：「奪走唯一逃生路的改善，不能叫進步。」一名漁夫舉手，說半夜地板下會傳出奇怪聲響，像金屬敲出三個清楚的音節。幾名鄰居點頭。黎雅發現那聲音似乎與第七盞燈的節奏一致，便申請隔晚帶錄音設備去調查，議會勉強同意。

Tomas had expected Rosa to demand revenge, but she asked him to apologize for breaking the fence without warning the people behind him. He was stunned. “I made an opening,” he protested. “You did,” she answered, “and you also frightened an old man who did not know where the metal would fall.” The criticism hurt because it was fair. Tomas found the man before midnight, apologized, and helped repair the damaged railing beside his house. When he returned, Lia was waiting with two cups of tea. “You can oppose a company without forgetting the person standing next to you,” she said. Beyond the bay, the forbidden platform shone with a light that moved against the direction of the tide.

托瑪斯原以為蘿莎會要他報復，沒想到她要求他向圍欄後的人道歉，因為拆欄前沒有先警告。他愣住：「我是開出逃生口。」蘿莎說：「是，但你也嚇壞一位不知道金屬會往哪裡倒的老人。」這批評刺痛他，因為有道理。他在午夜前找到老人、道歉，還替對方修好屋旁欄杆。回來時黎雅帶著兩杯茶等他。「你能反對一家公司，也不能忘記站在身旁的人，」她說。海灣之外，禁區平台上的光正逆著潮水方向移動。
