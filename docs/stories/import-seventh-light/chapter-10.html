The Green Horizon

Tomas went to the radio mast with Lia and Mira despite the note's request. Beneath the rusted base they found a hatch leading into a concrete service tunnel. Elena was not there, but she had left a medical kit, a map, and a breathless apology written on the wall: SORRY. TOO MANY EYES. A few meters deeper, a sudden explosion of steam burst from a cracked pipe. Niko, who had followed them against orders, was thrown backward and cut his arm on the metal grating. The wound was not fatal, but it bled enough to demand a bandage at once. Mira pressed cloth to the injury while Tomas cursed himself for not forbidding the boy to come.

托瑪斯仍與黎雅、米拉一起前往無線電桿，雖然紙條上明明寫著要單獨赴約。鏽蝕底座下藏著一扇通往混凝土維修道的艙門。艾蓮娜不在那裡，卻留下急救包、地圖，以及一行氣喘吁吁似的牆上道歉：『對不起，眼睛太多。』再往內幾公尺，一根破裂管線忽然噴出高壓蒸氣，像小型爆炸般轟然炸開。違反命令跟來的尼可被震得向後跌去，手臂在金屬柵格上劃開。傷口不致命，但血流得夠多，得立刻包紮。米拉一邊壓住傷口，一邊看著托瑪斯自責咒罵自己，怪自己沒有硬把少年留下。

The station doctor called a surgeon from the mainland ferry, and to everyone's surprise the man arrived within an hour. He worked quickly, installing temporary stitches and warning that the tunnel gas was suspicious. “This is no mere maintenance accident,” he said. The words altered the room. Lia illustrated the sequence in her notebook, careful to note each smell, sound, and burst of pressure. If the company later claimed the incident was ordinary, they would need something stronger than confident voices. Meanwhile Lewis found a bargain hidden in the project contracts: Helix Tide had promised tax relief only if the island accepted all risk from industrial accidents. The legal trap was as brutal as any weapon.

救援站的醫生從本島渡輪找來一位外科醫師，沒想到那人一小時內就趕到。他俐落地替尼可縫合，裝上暫時固定帶，並警告說，隧道裡的氣體狀況很可疑。「這不是普通維修意外，」他說。這句話立刻改變整個房間的氣氛。黎雅在筆記本上畫出事故順序，仔細記下每一種味道、聲音與壓力噴發。如果公司日後要把事情說成小事故，他們就得面對比自信口吻更強的東西。與此同時，路易斯在合約裡找出一筆可怕交易：只有在島方承擔全部工業意外風險時，海潮螺旋才會提供稅賦減免。那法律陷阱和任何武器一樣殘酷。

A buried room at the tunnel's end held stranger evidence still. There, beneath a carved slab, lay the tomb of Captain Morrow, the early settler who had founded the harbor colony. The chamber walls illustrated the island's older civilization: wave farmers, shell traders, and women who guided ships with polished mirrors. The history was not romantic nostalgia. It proved the eastern shore had always been a shared survival zone, never a private industrial strip. Rosa stood before the mural in silence. “My grandmother told me parts of this,” she whispered. “I thought they were stories told to children.” Tomas, still shaken, realized that the company had not only endangered the living. It had buried the dead a second time.

隧道盡頭還藏著更奇怪的證據。那裡在一塊雕刻石板下，竟埋著早年港灣拓居者、莫羅船長的墓室。四壁圖像描繪著更古老的島嶼文明：養潮人、貝殼商，以及以拋光鏡片指引船隻的女人。這不是浪漫懷舊而已，而是證明東岸從來都是共同生存地帶，不是私人產業區。蘿莎站在壁畫前久久不語，低聲說祖母曾講過其中一部分，她一直以為只是哄孩子的故事。仍驚魂未定的托瑪斯則忽然明白，公司不只危及活人，也把死人埋了第二次。

Niko wanted to return for his dropped torch, but the doctor made him sit beside the station window while an adult checked the bandage. Tomas apologized for speaking in anger. He should have made the boundary clear and arranged someone to stay with the boy. They agreed that Niko would help label photographs from the rescue station until his arm healed. The work disappointed him at first. Then Lia handed him the first envelope and asked him to make its date readable. A pregnant visitor was waiting for the same doctor, so Lia kept the doorway clear. She wanted to congratulate Niko on helping, but his injury was a critical reminder that useful work did not require another dangerous trip. The company's posters promised a prosperous future; any real revolution in the island's fortunes had to begin by protecting the people already there.

尼可想回去找掉落的手電筒，但醫師請他坐在救援站窗邊，由成人檢查繃帶。托瑪斯為自己憤怒的語氣道歉；他本應清楚劃定界線，並安排人陪男孩。他們約定，手臂復原前，尼可留在救援站協助標記照片。起初他很失望，直到黎雅遞來第一個信封，請他把日期寫清楚。同一位醫師還有一名懷孕的訪客要看診，黎雅因此讓開門口。她想稱讚尼可幫上忙，但他的傷勢也提醒大家一件關鍵的事：有用的工作不必靠另一次危險探險完成。公司的海報承諾繁榮未來；然而，島嶼命運若真要迎來重大變革，就必須先保護已住在這裡的人。

That evening Lia uploaded a short post to her website: no accusations, only evidence, photographs, and a resolution to continue. The response was immediate. Scholars offered help. Journalists called. A representative from Helix Tide sent a hostile message forbidding further entry into restricted areas. Lia read the threat twice and saved it carefully. The company was becoming careless, which meant the pressure was working. On the western ridge, the sunset turned the clouds green for one brief second. Everyone who saw it fell silent. The warning from Tomas's father's manual had been true. The horizon itself was beginning to signal trouble.

當晚，黎雅在網站上發了一則短文：不帶指控，只放證據、照片，以及繼續追查的決心。回應立刻湧來。學者願意幫忙，記者打電話上門，而海潮螺旋的代表則發來一則充滿敵意的訊息，明令禁止任何人再次進入管制區。黎雅把威脅信反覆看了兩次，小心存檔。公司開始變得粗心，意味著壓力已經奏效。西側山脊上，夕陽讓雲邊短短一瞬閃出綠光。所有看見的人都安靜了下來。托瑪斯父親手冊上的警告果然是真的——連地平線本身，都開始對他們發出訊號。
