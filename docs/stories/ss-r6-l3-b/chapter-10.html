聆聽章程

The Charter of Listening

當城市聽見我們 · 第 10 / 25 章 · 2000–3000 · 英中對照 · 校訂中

用互動版查詞、朗讀與收藏

Ms. Ho called a public meeting to decide how the station would handle both advertising and emergency messages. Aya proposed that safety information must never be interrupted by paid content. Kai added a technical rule: changes to the system required two independent signatures and a test record. The manager argued that this would slow ordinary business. The room did not agree with everything Aya said, and she was pleased. A rule that survived honest disagreement would be stronger than one approved because people were afraid to object.

何老師召開公開會議，決定電台如何處理廣告與緊急訊息。艾雅提議，安全資訊絕不能被付費內容中斷。凱恩增加技術規則：系統變更需要兩個獨立簽名及測試紀錄。經理說這會拖慢日常業務。現場沒有所有人都同意艾雅，但她很高興。能經得起誠實討論的規則，比人們因害怕反對而通過的規則更可靠。

The meeting secretary read each proposed rule twice, first in its original wording and then in the simpler version suggested by residents. Sometimes a shorter sentence was clearer; sometimes it removed an important condition. Aya asked the room to keep the condition even when it made the line less elegant. Kai drew a small example to show what could go wrong if a safety message lost its time. The exchange took longer than a quick vote, but people left knowing precisely what they had approved.

會議祕書把每項規則讀兩遍，先讀原文，再讀居民建議的白話版。有時短句更清楚，有時卻刪掉重要條件。艾雅請大家即使句子不夠漂亮，也保留必要條件。凱恩畫小例子，說明安全訊息若少了時間可能出什麼問題。這比快速投票花更久，但人們離開時確實知道自己通過了什麼。

A local bride arrived in a fashionable blue suit and called the volunteers her guardian angel. She was bold enough to ask why the warning board had no sign for visitors who could not hear. Aya determined to include text alerts as well as sound. A lamp flame flickered beside a silk handkerchief on the display table, and Kai moved it away from the papers. A bowl of melon and a small pearl pin decorated the room. No one needed to impress the visitors with expensive objects; they needed a moral reason for every rule.

一位當地新娘穿著時髦的藍色套裝到場，稱志工為她的守護天使。她大膽詢問，警告板為何沒有給聽不見聲音之訪客的標示。艾雅決定加入文字警示。展示桌旁的燈火在絲手帕邊搖動，凱恩把燈移離文件。室內還有一碗甜瓜與小珍珠別針作裝飾。不需要靠貴重物品讓訪客印象深刻；每項規則都需要道德上的理由。

Before the vote, Aya invited a hearing-impaired resident to explain how he received warnings. He used a phone and watched light signals at the ferry gate. The town had not tested whether those signals worked during a power failure. Kai wrote that weakness on the board, although it meant his own club's first design had been incomplete. Aya followed his example and admitted that her first script had used a street name unfamiliar to new residents. The meeting revised both plans instead of choosing a single perfect one.

投票前，艾雅邀請一位聽障居民解釋自己如何接收警報。他使用手機，也觀察渡輪閘門的燈光訊號。城鎮尚未測試停電時那些訊號是否有效。凱恩把缺點寫上白板，儘管這表示他社團的第一份設計不完整。艾雅也承認，最初的廣播稿用了新居民不熟悉的街名。會議修正兩份計畫，而不是硬選一份完美的。

After the meeting, Aya placed the signed charter on the station notice board beside the procedure for corrections. A passerby asked whether a person could complain anonymously. Aya said yes, but explained that some claims might be harder to verify without a way to contact the person again. The passerby nodded and chose to write a note without a name. The club did not force him to reveal himself for convenience. His question stayed with Aya as she considered the many kinds of trust a public station needed.

會後艾雅把簽署章程貼在電台公告板，旁邊放更正程序。路人問能否匿名申訴。艾雅說可以，但解釋若無法再聯絡，部分指控比較難查證。路人點頭，選擇不留姓名寫下紙條。社團沒有為了方便逼他揭露身分。艾雅一直記得這個問題，思考公共電台需要哪些不同形式的信任。

An optimistic baker served bread from a small stove, and the smell brought relief to a tense room. A child gave the air a playful punch, pretending to fight the bad signal, but her father reminded her that real safety came from planning. Someone stared at the wall diagram and asked how the structure of the tower affected reception. The tide was changing outside; somehow the room felt less divided than it had that morning. Aya drew a simple chart so people could see what would happen first, next, and last during a real emergency.

樂觀的麵包師用小爐子烤麵包，香味緩和了緊張氣氛。孩子朝空中玩笑似地揮一拳，假裝打倒壞訊號；父親提醒真正安全靠的是計畫。有人盯著牆上圖示，問高塔結構如何影響收訊。外頭潮水正在變化；不知怎地，室內的人比早上少了些對立。艾雅畫一張簡單圖表，讓大家看清真正緊急時第一步、第二步與最後一步會發生什麼。

The volunteers had to determine who would approve an emergency script before anyone touched the switch. A few people began to stare at the complicated form. Aya replaced its long headings with clear questions while leaving all the safeguards in place. The meeting tested the revised page aloud, and more hands rose to take part.

志工必須先確認誰負責核准緊急稿，任何人才可以碰開關。有些人開始盯著複雜表單發愣。艾雅把長標題改成清楚問題，同時保留所有安全措施。會議朗讀修正版，更多人舉手參與。

The charter passed with one important condition: residents would review it again after a trial, and any guest could ask to correct a recording. That night Aya and Kai sat on the steps outside the station. He told her he wanted to hear her own voice on the final program, not only the voices she collected for others. Aya replied that she had spent years learning to hide behind an edit. Speaking as herself would take more courage than winning a prize. Kai said he would listen whenever she decided to try.

章程附帶一項重要條件後通過：試用之後居民將再次檢視，任何來賓也能要求更正錄音。那晚艾雅與凱恩坐在電台外的階梯上。他說希望在最後節目聽見她自己的聲音，而不只是她替別人蒐集的聲音。艾雅回答，她花了多年學會藏在剪輯後面；以自己的身分說話，比得獎還需要勇氣。凱恩說，不管她何時決定嘗試，他都願意聽。
