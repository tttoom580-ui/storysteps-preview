錯誤的警報

The Wrong Warning

The following Monday, a voice from the emergency speaker told the town to leave the harbor, although the sky was clear. A minute later, the same speaker advertised a holiday sale. Shopkeepers called the station in anger. Aya asked everyone not to spread the message until the weather office confirmed it. Kai checked the time stamps and found a strange pattern: the advertisement was entering the system at the exact second reserved for a warning. The clubs recorded the incident without touching the live switch.

隔週星期一，緊急喇叭叫全鎮居民離開港口，天空卻一片晴朗。一分鐘後，同一個喇叭開始播放節慶特賣廣告。店家憤怒地打電話到電台。艾雅請大家先別轉傳，等氣象單位確認。凱恩查看時間紀錄，發現奇怪的模式：廣告在預留給警報的那一秒進入系統。兩社記下事件，沒有碰觸通電中的開關。

At the afternoon briefing, Ms. Ho placed two clocks on the table. One was the station clock, and the other came from the town office. They differed by nearly a minute. Kai said the time stamps on recordings would be hard to compare until the clocks agreed. Aya asked the official technician to adjust the station clock and note the change. The act looked minor, but it gave every later report a common point of reference. Their first success that day was not a repaired speaker; it was a trustworthy time.

下午簡報時，何老師把兩只時鐘放在桌上：一只是電台時鐘，另一只來自市府。兩者差了近一分鐘。凱恩說，時間不一致就難以比對錄音紀錄。艾雅請正式技師校正電台時鐘並記下變更。這舉動看似小事，卻給後續報告共同參考點。當天第一項成果不是修好的喇叭，而是值得信任的時間。

"Anyhow, we cannot assume this is harmless," the engineer said. A small bump in the old circuit could multiply a signal error, and moisture might let bacteria grow inside the damaged cabinet. The machine's creator had once received a prize for excellence, but that did not make its inner parts safe forever. On an old test chart, the word missile meant a simulated fast-moving object, not a real threat. The operator crossed it out so no one would misunderstand. Repair was a necessity, and money was the missing resource.

「無論如何，不能假定它沒有危害。」工程師說。舊電路裡一次小撞擊就可能使訊號錯誤倍增，水氣還可能讓細菌在受損機箱裡生長。機器的創作者曾因傑出表現得獎，但這不表示內部零件永遠安全。舊測試表上的「飛彈」只是模擬高速物體，並非真正的威脅。操作員劃掉這個字，避免誤解。維修是必要的，缺少的是資源。

The manager said the error was probably a simple mistake and asked them to stop asking questions. Aya replied that a public warning required a public correction. She did not accuse him of lying. Instead she requested a written account of which systems had operated during the test. Kai prepared a diagram simple enough for people without engineering training to understand. Their teacher approved a temporary rule: students could report, but only certified workers could change the station's wiring.

經理說錯誤大概只是疏失，要求他們停止追問。艾雅回答，公開的警報必須有公開的更正。她沒有指控經理說謊，而是要求書面說明測試時哪些系統運作。凱恩畫了一張讓沒有工程背景的人也看得懂的圖。何老師核准臨時規則：學生可以通報，但只有合格技術人員能更動電台線路。

The students prepared a page of common questions for callers. Who issued a message? When was it issued? Was it a test or a real warning? What action had been approved? Mina read the questions to an older volunteer, who asked them to use larger print. The group changed the layout rather than assuming that the volunteer should buy new glasses. Aya noticed how often good information became useless because its form ignored the person meant to receive it. She wrote that observation into the next planning meeting.

學生準備了一頁給來電者的常見問題：誰發布訊息？何時發布？這是測試還是真警報？核准的行動是什麼？米娜讀給年長志工聽，對方要求字體大一點。團隊改版面，而不是假設志工應自己買新眼鏡。艾雅注意到，好資訊常因形式忽略接收者而無用。她把這項觀察寫進下一次企畫會議。

A politician called the incident a scandal before any investigation was complete. A teenage volunteer suggested using a clever technique to make the speaker sound louder; Kai thanked her but explained why loudness alone could not fix a false message. The team ate stale bread while checking a summary of the log. Aya's stomach hurt from worry, yet she refused to name a suspect without evidence. Someone placed a tropical plant beside the window, and candle wax from last week's event stained the desk. Ordinary details made the room feel strangely calm.

調查還沒結束，一位政治人物就把事件稱為醜聞。青少年志工建議用巧妙技術把喇叭調得更大聲；凱恩謝謝她，但解釋音量不能修復錯誤訊息。團隊一邊吃不新鮮的麵包，一邊核對紀錄摘要。艾雅擔心得胃痛，仍拒絕在沒有證據時指認嫌疑人。有人把熱帶植物放在窗邊，上週活動留下的燭蠟沾著桌面。這些日常細節使房間意外地平靜。

That night Aya prepared a correction script. It named the error, gave the confirmed weather information, and explained how listeners could check future messages. Kai read every line as a listener, not an engineer. He asked her to shorten one sentence and add the time of the official report. She accepted both changes. At the end she signed the message as the student editor responsible for it. She could not control the weather or the station owner, but she could take responsibility for her words.

那天晚上艾雅寫了更正稿。它指出錯誤、提供已確認的天氣資訊，也說明聽眾如何查證往後的訊息。凱恩以聽眾而非工程師的角度讀每一句，請她縮短一處文字並加入官方報告的時間。她接受兩項修改。最後，她以負責的學生編輯身分署名。她無法控制天氣或電台所有者，卻能為自己的話負責。
