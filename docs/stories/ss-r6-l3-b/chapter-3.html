聲音拼成的地圖

A Map Made of Voices

當城市聽見我們 · 第 3 / 25 章 · 2000–3000 · 英中對照

用互動版查詞、朗讀與收藏

Wednesday's plan was to collect five short voices from five streets. Aya asked interviewees to describe where a visitor might get lost in heavy rain. Kai mapped the position of each recording and measured the weak places in the signal. Their methods were different, and the difference was useful. A market worker could identify the name of a crossing; an engineer could explain why the loudspeaker stopped working there. The first interviews were awkward, but none of the residents refused to help once the purpose was explained clearly.

星期三的計畫是從五條街收集五段短訪談。艾雅請受訪者描述豪雨時遊客容易迷路的地方。凱恩則標示每段錄音的位置，測量訊號微弱的區域。兩人的方法不同，而這份不同正好有用。市場工作者知道路口名稱，工程人員能解釋那裡的擴音器為何失效。第一次訪問有點尷尬，但目的說清楚後，居民都願意幫忙。

Their next stop was a small shop whose owner sold repaired radios. The dealer kept old maps beside the cash box because visitors often asked which bus would reach the market in bad weather. He could not tell them whether a speaker was broken, but he knew exactly who had stopped coming to the square when rain threatened. Aya listened while Kai marked the shop on his chart. A street was not merely a point on a map. It was the pattern of people who trusted one another enough to ask a question.

下一站是一間賣修好的收音機的小店。店主這位經銷商把舊地圖放在收銀箱旁，因為訪客常問壞天氣時哪班公車可到市場。他無法判斷喇叭是否故障，卻清楚知道一有下雨跡象，誰就不再來廣場。凱恩在圖上標示店址時，艾雅專心聽著。一條街不只是地圖上的一個點，也是人們互相信任、願意提出問題的關係網。

In the background of the market recording, bamboo leaves knocked against a sign. Aya carried a bundle of consent forms while Kai stopped to chat with a chip seller. The seller told them about a civil dispute between two street dealers, then changed the subject to a democratic meeting held downtown. Families had come to dine on cheap noodles and doughnut snacks before a public poll. They talked about poverty, but also about a mighty neighborhood that could react when officials listened. The recording gave those words substance instead of turning them into a speech for someone's sake.

市場錄音的背景裡，竹葉敲打招牌。艾雅抱著一疊同意書，凱恩停下來和賣洋芋片的小販聊天。小販提到兩位街商的民事糾紛，又轉而說起市中心辦過的民主集會。家庭來用餐，吃平價麵條與甜甜圈，接著參加公開民調。他們談貧窮，也談有力量、會在官員願意聽時作出回應的社區。這些錄音讓詞語有了實質內容，而非為了某人利益而編出的演說。

At the west crossing a woman named Nia declined to give her name on air. She was willing to describe the broken warning speaker, however. Aya separated her contact information from the recording and marked the file anonymous. Kai listened while she explained the choice to Nia. He had thought an interview meant collecting facts. Watching Aya protect a stranger taught him that an honest record also included the right to remain unnamed. They heard an old warning bell in the distance, quiet beneath the traffic.

在西側路口，妮雅不願在廣播中公開姓名，但願意描述故障的警示喇叭。艾雅把聯絡資料與錄音分開存放，檔案標成匿名。凱恩聽她向妮雅解釋這個選擇。他原以為訪談只是收集事實；看見艾雅保護陌生人，才懂得誠實的紀錄也包括不具名的權利。遠處響起一聲舊警鐘，幾乎被車聲蓋過。

The interviews were scheduled in short blocks so no participant had to remain longer than promised. At the end of each block, Aya played the last sentence back and asked whether the speaker was comfortable with it. Two people changed a word. One person asked for the whole recording to be erased. Aya did so and noted only that permission had been withdrawn. Kai watched the simple act closely. They did not gain another beautiful sound, but the choice made it more likely that others would trust the station next week.

訪談分成短時段，沒有人需要停留超過約定時間。每段結束後，艾雅播放最後一句，詢問說話者是否接受。兩人修改一個字，一人要求刪掉整段錄音。艾雅照做，只留下「同意已撤回」的紀錄。凱恩仔細看這個簡單動作。他們沒有多得到一段美妙聲音，卻使其他人下週更可能信任電台。

A pigeon made a sudden leap from a roof and nearly knocked over Kai's microphone. The seller laughed and pointed to a narrow alley where rainwater had once risen quickly. Aya asked whether the old map showed that danger. A local volunteer brought a new map with several streets missing. "A map can deepen fear if it leaves out the people who know the place," she said. They compared the official background notes with stories told beside the bamboo fence. No one was asked to agree with every detail; disagreement itself became part of the record.

一隻鴿子突然從屋頂躍起，差點撞倒凱恩的麥克風。小販笑著指出一條窄巷，雨水過去曾在那裡快速上漲。艾雅問舊地圖是否標出這個危險。一位志工帶來新地圖，卻漏掉好幾條街。「如果地圖沒有納入熟悉當地的人，反而會加深恐懼。」她說。他們比較官方背景筆記與竹籬旁居民的敘述。沒人被要求同意每個細節，分歧本身也成為紀錄的一部分。

That evening the pair played back their files in separate headphones. Their voices did not overlap: one had recorded weather, the other explanations. Yet together they showed exactly where an alert might fail. Ms. Ho told them that the board would accept a shared technical report, but each club must keep its own editorial decisions. Aya agreed immediately. She liked working with Kai more than she had expected, and she was determined that liking him would never replace the rules she asked others to follow.

當晚兩人戴著各自的耳機重聽檔案。他們的錄音沒有重疊：一方錄天氣，一方錄解釋。合起來卻清楚呈現警報可能失效的位置。何老師說，評審可以接受共同技術報告，但兩社必須保留各自的編輯決定權。艾雅立刻同意。她發現自己比預期更喜歡與凱恩合作，也下定決心：對他的好感不能取代她要求別人遵守的規則。
