一分鐘的代價

The Price of a Minute

當城市聽見我們 · 第 2 / 25 章 · 2000–3000 · 英中對照

用互動版查詞、朗讀與收藏

On Tuesday morning the clubs checked the station budget. The radio had received money for ten years, yet no one could show where the new alert equipment was listed. Aya wanted to question the manager at once. Kai asked for a quieter approach: collect the old bills, compare the labels, and test the emergency switch in front of witnesses. They disagreed about speed, not about the need for evidence. Aya wrote their two plans on the board and circled the steps they could safely do together.

星期二早上，兩社檢查電台預算。電台已接受資助十年，卻沒人說得出新的警報設備列在哪一項。艾雅想立刻質問經理，凱恩則主張更穩妥的做法：收集舊帳單、比較標籤，並在證人面前測試緊急開關。他們在速度上有歧見，對證據的重要性卻沒有。艾雅把兩份計畫寫在白板上，圈起可以安全合作的步驟。

The clerk showed them how a change in public equipment was normally approved. First a person described the problem, then a trained worker proposed the repair, and finally another person checked the result. Aya asked what happened if a sponsor offered to pay the full price. The clerk said money could change who paid but not who carried responsibility. That sentence stayed with her. She had been tempted to treat the missing invoices as proof of a dishonest person; now she saw that the larger risk lay in a process no one could explain.

職員示範公共設備變更通常如何核准：有人先描述問題，合格人員提出修理，再由另一人確認結果。艾雅問，若贊助商願意付全額，程序會怎樣。職員說，金錢可以改變付款者，不能改變責任歸屬。這句話留在她心裡。她本來差點把遺失發票當成某人不誠實的證明，如今看出更大的風險是沒人能解釋的程序。

An old festival poster showed a painted beast holding a bowling ball. Under it lay a budget sheet from a decade earlier, when the radio club had just begun. Someone had drawn a drunk sailor on the margin, perhaps to frighten the children away from the equipment room. A professor of geography explained that the bay made weather warnings difficult to hear. His expressive face became serious as he traced the coastline. "Do not be greedy for applause," he told the students. "A warning must first reach the passenger who cannot see the shore."

舊節慶海報畫著一頭拿保齡球的野獸。海報底下是一份十年前的預算表，當時廣播社才剛成立。有人在紙邊畫了醉酒的水手，也許是想嚇阻孩子靠近機房。地理學教授解釋，海灣地形使天氣警報難以被聽見。他表情豐富的臉在描繪海岸線時變得嚴肅。「不要貪求掌聲，」他對學生說，「警報應先傳到看不見海岸的乘客耳中。」

The accounting office kept a small metal box of signed receipts. Aya requested a copy, but the clerk said that students were allowed to inspect documents only with a staff member present. Kai did not push past the rule. He booked a supervised hour and invited Aya to bring a second witness. That careful respect for limits irritated her and reassured her at the same time. She remembered how easily she had once edited a client's words without asking who might be harmed by their removal.

會計室有個金屬小盒，裡面存放簽收單。艾雅要求影本，但職員說學生只能在教職員陪同下查閱。凱恩沒有硬闖，而是預約監督查閱時段，邀請艾雅再找一名證人。他尊重界線的謹慎態度讓她一面不耐煩、一面安心。她想起過去做廣告剪輯時，曾輕易刪掉客戶的話，卻沒問過這麼做會傷害誰。

During the lunch break, Mina from the Radio Club joined them. She asked whether the clubs were secretly planning to join and leave their other members without a choice. Aya immediately told her no. The technical test belonged to both groups, but each club would vote on any larger change. Kai repeated the rule in front of the clerk, making it part of the meeting notes. Mina seemed relieved. The conversation showed Aya that public consent was not only for interviewees; her own members needed it when decisions changed the shape of their club.

午休時，廣播社的米娜加入他們，問兩社是否打算偷偷合併，讓其他社員沒有選擇。艾雅立刻回答沒有。技術測試由兩組共同負責，但任何更大變更都要各社投票。凱恩在職員面前重述規則，讓它列入會議紀錄。米娜看來鬆了口氣。這段對話使艾雅明白，公開同意不只適用受訪者；決策會改變社團時，自己的社員也需要同意。

The repair kit contained a strip of lace used to tie loose wires, a forgotten necktie, and an unsigned permit for a temporary antenna. A note mentioned the previous semester and the risks of changing a transmitter without approval. Someone had written regret in red ink beside a receipt. The professor did not permit the students to test any live circuit that day. "Make the missing number visible first," he said. Outside, a voter asked why public equipment was hidden behind private invoices. Aya recorded the question with permission and promised that no answer would be invented.

修理工具包裡有一條用來綁鬆線的蕾絲、一條被忘記的領帶，以及一張沒有簽名的臨時天線許可證。便條提到上學期，還提醒未經核准更動發射器的風險。有人用紅筆在收據旁寫下「後悔」。教授不准學生當天測試通電中的電路。「先讓缺失的號碼看得見。」他說。外面一位選民問，公共設備為何藏在私人發票後面。艾雅徵求同意後錄下這問題，並承諾不會編造答案。

By dusk they found two invoices bearing the same equipment number but different prices. Neither paper proved fraud. It did prove that a public explanation was needed. Aya drafted an honest notice: the emergency switch would remain under staff control until an independent test. Kai added the name of the teacher responsible for the test, so listeners would know whom to contact. The clubs left the room without a winner. They had gained something more useful for now: a question that could be checked by anyone.

傍晚，他們找到兩張設備編號相同、價格不同的發票。光憑紙張不能證明有人詐欺，卻足以說明需要公開解釋。艾雅草擬誠實的公告：緊急開關在獨立測試前仍由教職員掌管。凱恩補上測試負責老師的姓名，讓聽眾知道可聯絡誰。兩社離開時沒有贏家，但暫時得到更有用的成果：一個任何人都能核對的問題。
