Whose Story Is It?

當城市聽見我們 · 第 12 / 25 章 · 2000–3000 · 英中對照 · 校訂中

用互動版查詞、朗讀與收藏

The next task was a monthly program of recorded memories. Aya interviewed Captain Wei, an elderly ferry worker who remembered the harbor before the new road. He was glad to speak, but he asked that a story about his family remain private. Aya placed that segment aside before editing. A journalist who had visited the station asked why she would remove such a moving passage. "Because the speaker asked me to," Aya answered. "An interesting story is not automatically mine to use."

下一項工作是每月一次的口述記憶節目。艾雅訪問年長渡輪工作者魏船長，他記得新道路興建前的港灣。他樂意分享，卻要求一段家人故事不要公開。艾雅在剪輯前先把那段放到一旁。來訪記者問她為何刪掉如此動人的內容。「因為說話的人這樣要求，」艾雅回答，「有趣的故事不會自動變成我的財產。」

When Aya edited the captain's story, she marked the private section with a note visible only to approved student editors. The note did not repeat any sensitive details. It simply said that permission had not been given. Kai helped her check that no copy remained in the public export folder. They made a list of places where a file might travel during editing. Aya had once believed that deleting a sentence from the final recording was enough. She now understood that care also meant checking the copies left behind.

艾雅剪輯船長故事時，替私人段落加上只有核准學生編輯看得見的註記，沒有重複敏感內容，只寫「未獲公開同意」。凱恩幫她確認公開匯出資料夾沒有留副本。他們列出剪輯時檔案可能流向的地方。艾雅以前以為從最終錄音刪掉句子就夠了，如今明白照顧也包括檢查留下的副本。

At the port, a bubble rose beside the boat as the captain showed them an old picture of a canyon far inland. He was still capable of drawing a safe route from memory, though he checked the current chart before speaking. A client once asked him to edit a weather story to sound cheerful; he refused. The ferry office had electricity again after last week's fault. A camera flash startled a bird near a hatch, and a hopeful student apologized. Accurate information, the captain said, mattered more than a beautiful photograph.

港口邊，一個氣泡在船旁升起，魏船長展示內陸峽谷的舊照片。雖然記憶力仍好，能畫出安全路線，他說話前還是先核對現行海圖。過去一位客戶要求他把天氣故事編得愉快些，他拒絕了。上週故障後，渡輪辦公室已恢復供電。相機閃光驚動艙口附近的鳥，一位充滿希望的學生趕緊道歉。船長說，準確資訊比漂亮照片重要。

Captain Wei signed the release for three passages, then crossed out a fourth. Aya photographed the signed form and read the choice back to him. Kai waited beside the door, careful not to make the interview feel like an examination. When they were alone, he asked Aya whether she regretted losing the strongest moment. She admitted that she did. But the regret was hers to carry, not the captain's to solve. She would rather keep a person's trust than own the perfect ending of his story.

魏船長簽准三段，劃掉第四段。艾雅拍下簽好的同意書，再把選擇讀給他確認。凱恩在門旁等待，小心不讓訪談像考試。兩人獨處時，他問艾雅是否後悔失去最感人的片段。她承認會後悔。但那是她該承擔的遺憾，不是船長該解決的問題。她寧可保有人們的信任，也不願擁有完美的故事結尾。

The captain visited the studio a second time to hear the completed segment. He sat quietly through the introduction and smiled at a passage about the ferry bell. Then he asked Aya to change one detail about the time of a storm. She checked the old chart, found that he was right, and corrected the recording before release. "Good," he said. "A story can still be mine even when I need help telling it." Aya thanked him for trusting the club with both his memory and his right to correct it.

船長第二次到攝影棚聽完成的段落。他安靜聽開場，聽到渡輪鐘聲時微笑。然後他請艾雅修正一次暴風雨的時間細節。她查舊海圖，發現船長是對的，於公開前改正錄音。「很好，」他說，「即使需要別人幫忙，我的故事仍可以屬於自己。」艾雅謝謝他把記憶與更正權都交託給社團。

A notice about public property explained that the recording could represent the captain's work only with his approval. The situation was more delicate than a simple sale. A cook brought tea with spice and asked whether his own memories could be included. On the windowsill stood a toy sword, a gift from the captain's grandson. A woman began to weep when she recognized a photograph of the old boat, but she asked not to be recorded. Aya thanked her and closed the microphone. Not every important moment needed to become a broadcast.

一張公共財產公告說明，錄音只有得到船長同意才能代表他的工作。情況比普通買賣複雜得多。廚師端來加香料的茶，問自己的回憶是否也能收進節目。窗邊放著孫子送給船長的玩具劍。一位女士認出老船照片開始落淚，卻要求不要被錄音。艾雅謝謝她，關掉麥克風。不是每個重要時刻都需要變成廣播。

At home, Aya listened to the approved sections again. The captain's voice did not sound heroic. He described ordinary decisions: checking the wind, learning the names of passengers, and never leaving a question unanswered merely because it seemed small. She wrote an opening that made room for his quiet style. The last line was her own: "We do not need every story. We need to care for the stories we are trusted to hear." She decided to read it herself during the next test.

回家後艾雅重聽獲准使用的段落。魏船長的聲音並不英雄式。他描述的是普通決定：查看風向、記住乘客姓名，以及不因問題看似小就拒絕回答。艾雅寫了一段開場，容得下他安靜的說話方式。最後一句是她自己寫的：「我們不需要所有故事；我們需要珍惜被託付聆聽的故事。」她決定下次測試時親自念出來。
