壞掉的高塔

The Broken Tower

當城市聽見我們 · 第 4 / 25 章 · 2000–3000 · 英中對照

用互動版查詞、朗讀與收藏

Thursday morning, they climbed the stairs to the old signal tower. The iron steps were narrow, and the door had been closed for months. A custodian opened it after checking their written permission. Aya photographed every label before anyone touched a switch. Kai looked at the floor, the ventilation, and the safety rail. He refused to start a test until the group could leave quickly in an emergency. A delay made their deadline harder, but Aya found herself supporting his decision.

星期四早上，他們爬上舊訊號塔的階梯。鐵梯狹窄，塔門也已封閉數月。管理員檢查書面許可後才開門。任何人碰開關之前，艾雅先拍下每張標籤。凱恩查看地板、通風設備與安全欄杆。他堅持要先確認緊急時能迅速撤離才願意測試。延誤使期限更緊，但艾雅發現自己支持他的決定。

A woman who worked in the tower's records room offered to knit a bright cover for the portable microphone. Aya thanked her but explained that the cover must not block ventilation or change the sound during testing. The woman laughed and said she could make a soft bag for carrying it instead. The exchange was gentle, yet it showed how public work benefited when suggestions were questioned without being mocked. Kai placed a drawing of the device beside the bag pattern so the two volunteers could check that the sizes matched.

在高塔紀錄室工作的女士想替可攜式麥克風編織漂亮套子。艾雅道謝，卻解釋套子不能阻擋通風或改變測試聲音。女士笑著說，那就改織柔軟的攜帶袋。交流很溫和，也顯示公共工作如何在不嘲笑提議的情況下質疑它而受益。凱恩把設備圖放在袋子樣板旁，讓兩位志工核對大小。

The van stood in an alley behind the tower, beside a cherry tree with dark fruit. A volunteer had shown bravery by reporting a loose cable instead of hiding the problem. The old control box had a solid metal cover, but a recent crash had bent one corner. A faded label carried a definition of the warning colors. Kai began to investigate the wiring while Aya, feeling dizzy from the height, sat down for a short nap. The caretaker offered a saucer of tea and a knitted scarf. She was thankful for its warmth.

廂型車停在塔後的小巷，旁邊有棵結了深色果實的櫻桃樹。一位志工勇敢地通報鬆脫的電纜，而不是掩蓋問題。舊控制箱有堅固的金屬蓋，但最近的一次撞擊已使角落彎曲。褪色標籤寫著警示顏色的定義。凱恩開始調查線路；艾雅因高度而頭暈，坐下短暫休息。管理員端來一小碟茶，又給她一條編織圍巾。她很感謝那份暖意。

They discovered that the warning lamp and the advertisement player shared one cable. A safety system could not depend on such an arrangement. Kai drew the wiring and marked the point where a repair should begin. Aya asked the custodian who had last authorized a change. He showed them a signed maintenance log, but the signature did not match the official staff list. Aya copied the page with the date visible. She would not accuse anyone yet; a name on a page was not proof of intent.

他們發現警示燈與廣告播放器共用一條電纜。安全系統不該依賴這種配置。凱恩畫出線路圖，標出維修應開始的位置。艾雅詢問管理員最後是誰核准更動。他拿出簽過名的維修紀錄，但簽名與正式職員名冊不符。艾雅連日期一起影印下來。她暫時不會指控任何人；紙上的姓名不能證明意圖。

When they reached the bottom floor, the custodian gave them a copy of the tower's public maintenance schedule. It listed several missed visits during the previous winter. Aya asked him to explain the dates rather than make a guess about why they were missed. He said staff had been reassigned after a storm. Kai marked that explanation as an interview, not a verified fact, until payroll records could confirm it. The custodian approved the wording. Aya was beginning to understand that an ordinary correction could prevent an extraordinary misunderstanding.

到了一樓，管理員給他們高塔公開維護時程的影本。上面列出前一個冬天好幾次未完成的巡檢。艾雅請他解釋日期，而不是猜測原因。他說暴風雨後人員被調去其他工作。凱恩把這說法註記為訪談，而非已查證事實，等人事紀錄確認。管理員同意措辭。艾雅漸漸懂得，普通的更正能避免不普通的誤解。

In the tower office, an old crime novel described a murder, but the danger outside the book was a neglected machine. A poster tried to sell a sexy image of radio work: bright lights and smiling faces, without showing the cables that kept people safe. "A broken warning can starve a town of useful information," Aya said. The caretaker offered them blankets when a cold wind entered the room. Kai smiled at the practical help. The work was neither glamorous nor easy, but the tower could serve the neighborhood again if its defects were reported plainly.

塔內辦公室有一本描寫謀殺案的舊推理小說，但書外真正的危險是被忽視的機器。海報把廣播工作包裝成性感迷人的形象：燈光閃亮、人人微笑，卻沒呈現維繫安全的電纜。「壞掉的警報會使城鎮缺乏有用資訊。」艾雅說。寒風吹進屋時，管理員送來毛毯。凱恩對這份實際幫助微笑。工作既不華麗也不輕鬆，但只要如實報告缺失，高塔仍能再次服務社區。

On the way down, Aya asked Kai why he had become so careful. He told her that a small accident at a past internship had taught him to value the person who says stop. She did not ask for more details. Instead she thanked him for saying stop today. At the bottom of the stairs, their hands almost met on the railing. Neither pretended it was an invitation. Aya pointed to the logbook and said they had a better reason to meet tomorrow: the unsigned repair order.

下樓時，艾雅問凱恩為何如此謹慎。他說以前實習時的一次小事故，讓他懂得珍惜願意喊停的人。艾雅沒有追問，只謝謝他今天喊了停。到了樓梯底部，兩人的手差點在扶手上碰到。誰也沒有把那當成邀請。艾雅指向紀錄簿，說明天還有更好的理由見面：那張沒簽名的維修單。
