A Drawer of Old Signals

當城市聽見我們 · 第 7 / 25 章 · 2000–3000 · 英中對照 · 校訂中

用互動版查詞、朗讀與收藏

At the college archive, the retired operator Mrs. Liu showed Aya and Kai a drawer of old recording cards. Long before digital advertisements, the station had used a second battery-powered transmitter. No one had included it in the recent equipment list. Mrs. Liu remembered the man who had maintained it and the path to his small workshop. The clubs received permission to examine the cards but not to remove them. Aya photographed each page with its catalog number in view.

在學院檔案室，退休操作員劉女士拿給艾雅與凱恩看一抽屜舊錄音卡。數位廣告出現之前，電台曾使用第二套電池發射器。最近設備清單裡卻沒有它。劉女士記得維修它的人，以及通往他小工坊的路。兩社獲准查閱卡片，但不能帶走。艾雅拍下每一頁，讓目錄編號也出現在照片裡。

Mrs. Liu took them to the shelf where the backup radio's original instructions were kept. Several pages had been repaired with clear tape, and the ink had faded at the folds. She explained that instructions should be copied before they became impossible to read. Aya asked who owned the recordings the station had made years earlier. Mrs. Liu said some belonged to the city archive, while others were private gifts with restrictions. Aya wrote the answer beside the catalog numbers. Old technology carried old promises, and they still counted.

劉女士帶他們到存放備用電台原始說明書的架子。幾頁用透明膠帶修補，摺線處墨水已褪色。她解釋，說明書要在字看不清前複製。艾雅問，電台多年前製作的錄音屬於誰。劉女士說，有些屬市府檔案，有些是附限制的私人贈品。艾雅把答案寫在目錄號碼旁。舊科技承載舊承諾，而那些承諾仍然有效。

The old emergency appeal had been written on a brick wall near the workshop: help the listener before helping the sponsor. Its paint looked awful, and an awkward cupboard blocked half the words. Inside were a dime, a glass jar, and a broken vase holding a dry lily. Mrs. Liu said that a radio signal should never explode into needless fear. The room smelled of a strange flavor from preserved fruit. A worker had once tried to invent an alarm using bits of junk, but the device had not been steady enough to trust.

舊緊急呼籲曾寫在工坊旁的磚牆上：先幫助聽眾，再幫助贊助商。油漆難看，一只擺得很彆扭的櫥櫃又擋住一半文字。裡面有一枚十分硬幣、一個玻璃罐和一只插著乾百合的破花瓶。劉女士說，廣播訊號不該炸成無謂的恐慌。房內有醃水果的奇特味道。以前有人想用廢物發明警報器，但裝置不夠穩定，不能信任。

The cards described a test route from the pier to the hill school. If the main power failed, a volunteer could carry a prepared message to the backup operator, who would read it on battery power. The method was old, but the principle mattered: one broken company service should never silence the whole town. Kai sketched the route. Aya wondered who would write the message and who would decide what it meant. They agreed those were different responsibilities.

卡片描述一條從碼頭通往山上學校的測試路線。如果主電力中斷，志工可以把準備好的訊息送到備用操作員手中，由他使用電池電源播出。方法雖老，道理仍重要：一家公司的服務故障，不該讓整座城鎮沉默。凱恩畫下路線。艾雅思考由誰撰寫訊息，又由誰決定其意義。他們同意那是不同責任。

Kai wanted to take the old transmitter outside and discover whether it worked. Mrs. Liu stopped him gently. It had not been inspected for years, and the battery was no longer safe. He agreed to arrange a certified check instead. Aya remembered how impatient she had been with his caution on the first day. This time she was glad he could change his plan when another person knew more. They left the transmitter untouched and took home only the approved copies of its instructions.

凱恩想把舊發射器帶到室外，看看是否還能用。劉女士溫和阻止：它多年未檢查，電池也不再安全。他同意安排合格檢測。艾雅想起第一天自己對他的謹慎很不耐煩，現在卻很高興他能在別人更了解情況時改變計畫。他們沒有碰發射器，只帶走核准影印的說明書。

Mrs. Liu showed them a photograph of her younger self, her face bright with a terrific smile. She had been paid a modest wage, but she loved the work. A note in the margin said, "Remind the next operator to test this sometime before the rainy season." Beneath it a careful drawing showed the position of the switch. Aya could see why the emergency lesson mattered to the flesh-and-blood people who had kept the station alive. She copied the warning, not the private photograph, into her notebook. Respect could be practical and quiet.

劉女士拿出年輕時的照片，臉上是極燦爛的笑容。她領的工資不高，卻熱愛工作。頁邊寫著：「記得提醒下一位操作員，在雨季前找個時間測試。」下面精細地畫著開關位置。艾雅明白，這套緊急課程對曾維持電台運作的真實、有血有肉的人有多重要。她只把警告抄進筆記本，沒有複製私人照片。尊重可以很實際，也很安靜。

On the way out, Kai offered to walk Aya to the bus stop. She answered that she preferred to finish sorting her notes first. He nodded and stayed only long enough to carry a heavy box back to its shelf. At the door she called after him and asked if he would like tea when she was done. The difference mattered to her: neither of them had made the invitation out of obligation. They met an hour later, still discussing a station that had once known how to listen.

離開時，凱恩提議陪艾雅走到公車站。她說想先整理筆記。他點頭，只留下幫忙把沉箱放回架上。到門邊時她又叫住他，問整理完後能否一起喝茶。這個差異對她很重要：邀請不是出於義務。一小時後兩人再次見面，仍談著那座過去懂得聆聽的電台。
