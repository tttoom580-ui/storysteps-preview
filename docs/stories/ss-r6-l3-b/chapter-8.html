發聲的許可

Permission to Be Heard

當城市聽見我們 · 第 8 / 25 章 · 2000–3000 · 英中對照 · 校訂中

用互動版查詞、朗讀與收藏

A good safety broadcast needed more than a working transmitter. It needed words that people understood and voices they trusted. Aya organized a listening table at the public market while Kai checked how far the speaker could be heard. Anyone could try the script, suggest a change, or choose not to be recorded. The first visitor was a woman who had worked on ferries for twenty years. She explained that some passengers knew the color of the harbor flags better than the names of streets.

好的安全廣播不只需要正常的發射器，也需要人們聽得懂的文字與信得過的聲音。艾雅在市場設置試聽桌，凱恩則測量喇叭的可聽範圍。任何人都能試讀稿件、提出修改，或選擇不錄音。第一位訪客是工作了二十年的渡輪女員工。她說有些乘客記得港旗的顏色，卻不熟悉街道名稱。

For the next listening exercise, Aya asked the volunteers to play a message at ordinary conversation volume. People near the table could hear it, but shoppers farther away could not. Kai marked the distance and measured the background noise. A woman pointed out that the sound became harder to understand when a ferry engine started. They repeated the test at the same places, taking care not to block the walking path. The important result was not one loud number but the conditions under which listeners actually understood the words.

下一次試聽練習，艾雅請志工以普通談話音量播放訊息。桌邊的人聽得見，遠一點的購物者卻聽不清。凱恩標記距離並測量背景噪音。一位女士指出，渡輪引擎啟動後更難聽懂。他們在相同地點重試，也小心不擋行人道路。重要結果不是單一很大的音量數字，而是聽眾真正理解文字時的條件。

A traveler arrived with too much baggage and asked where a buffalo statue stood. A market cook pointed toward the square, then laughed because the statue was actually a large metal bull. In a nearby stall, an electric cooker made crispy rice while a child tried to grab the controller. The vendor warned him about the hot surface. Lately people had complained about litter near the water, but the citizen group said the town owed more than signs to its workers. No one wanted a public warning that sounded like an order from someone who never visited the market.

旅客帶著過多行李前來，問水牛雕像在哪裡。市場廚師指向廣場，又笑著解釋那其實是金屬公牛。一旁攤位用電鍋煮脆米飯，孩子想抓控制器，小販提醒熱表面的危險。最近有人抱怨水邊垃圾，但公民團體說，城鎮對工作者的責任不只貼告示。大家都不想聽到一則像從沒到過市場的人下命令的警報。

A younger interviewee asked whether the program would include personal stories about relationships. Aya explained that permission to record was not permission to publish every detail. A listener could withdraw a story before the final edit. Kai helped her put that rule on a large card, using words simple enough for anyone to question. They had once argued about whether sound should come before explanation. Now they were designing a process in which people could hear, understand, and choose.

一位年輕受訪者問，節目會不會放入私人感情故事。艾雅解釋，允許錄音不代表允許公開每個細節。聽眾可以在最後剪輯前撤回故事。凱恩幫她把規則寫在大卡片上，選用人人都能提出疑問的簡單文字。他們曾爭論聲音或解釋哪個該優先；現在兩人設計的流程讓人能聽見、理解、再作選擇。

Mina asked the team to add a small box to every consent form: "May we contact you if we need to check a fact?" People could answer yes or no without changing their decision about the recording itself. Aya thanked her for separating those two choices. A resident might want to speak publicly but not receive future calls; another might agree to help confirm details while keeping her voice private. The form became longer by one sentence, yet the process became more respectful and much clearer.

米娜建議每張同意書增加一個小方格：「若需要核對事實，可以再聯絡您嗎？」受訪者可以說好或不好，而不影響是否同意錄音的決定。艾雅謝謝她把兩個選擇分開。有人願意公開發言，但不想接未來電話；另有人願意幫忙確認細節，卻想保留私人的聲音。表單只多一句話，流程卻更加尊重人，也更清楚。

A talkative visitor made a request to discuss sexual health on a future program. Aya said the topic deserved care, privacy, and a qualified guest, not jokes or quick answers. A wealthy donor later offered to pay for the whole hour if his name appeared on the station's web page in large letters. Kai watched Aya ask exactly what the donation would allow him to control. A shadow crossed the table when the afternoon sun moved. Somewhere a child began to yell, and Aya paused the test until the child was safe. She would not let urgency erase permission.

一位健談的訪客要求未來討論性健康。艾雅說這議題需要細心、隱私與合格來賓，而不是笑話或草率回答。之後一位富有捐款人提出贊助整個時段，條件是網站上把他的名字放大。凱恩看著艾雅精確詢問捐款會讓他控制哪些事項。午後陽光移動，一道陰影掠過桌面。遠處孩子開始大叫，艾雅暫停測試直到孩子安全。她不讓急迫感抹去同意權。

Aya turned the approved message into a short bullet list rather than a page of long instructions. "We owe people enough time to understand each step," she said. At the market table, visitors pointed to the first item and asked for an example. She added one without changing the official meaning.

艾雅把核准的訊息改成簡短項目符號清單，而非滿頁冗長指示。「我們有責任給人們足夠時間理解每一步。」她說。市場試聽桌前，訪客指著第一項要求舉例。她補上一個例子，沒有改變官方意思。

By sunset the team had thirty written suggestions and twelve approved recordings. More important, six people had said no, and those refusals had been honored. Kai told Aya that her listening table had changed his idea of a clear signal. A signal was not merely something a machine carried; it was a promise about the words sent through it. Aya accepted the compliment without treating it as a surrender in their competition. Saturday's hour still belonged to one club, at least under the current rules.

日落時，團隊拿到三十條書面建議和十二段獲准使用的錄音。更重要的是，有六個人說了不，而他們的拒絕都受到尊重。凱恩說，試聽桌改變了他對清楚訊號的理解：訊號不只是機器傳送的東西，也是關於其中話語的承諾。艾雅接受稱讚，卻不把它當成競賽中誰的讓步。按照現行規則，星期六的時段仍只屬於一個社團。
