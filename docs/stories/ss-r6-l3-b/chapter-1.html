碼頭傳來的聲音

A Voice at the Pier

當城市聽見我們 · 第 1 / 25 章 · 2000–3000 · 英中對照

用互動版查詞、朗讀與收藏

Aya Chen was twenty-eight and had returned to evening college after six years of editing advertisements. She wanted to make a program in which neighbors could tell their own stories without a company choosing the happy parts. On the first Monday of spring she arrived at Beacon Radio with a folder of interview requests. Kai Lin, thirty-one, was already there with the Field Sound Club. Both clubs wanted the same Saturday evening broadcast hour. Their teacher, Ms. Ho, said that the two proposals would be tested in public before a panel made its decision.

二十八歲的陳艾雅做了六年廣告剪輯，才回到成人學院進修。她想製作一個節目，讓鄰居自己說故事，而不是由公司挑選其中快樂的部分。春季第一個星期一，她帶著訪談邀請書來到燈塔電台。三十一歲的林凱恩已率領田野收音社等在那裡。兩個社團都想取得星期六晚間的同一個播出時段。何老師宣布，評審決定之前，兩份企畫都必須接受公開試播。

The station's public test used a simple wheel to show how sounds moved between places. A child gave the wheel a spin, and the pointer landed on a line marked "market." Aya asked her what sound she would choose from that place. "The woman who tells me when the bus comes," the child said. Kai stopped adjusting a dial and looked up. It was a better answer than any of the polished samples. Aya wrote it down, then asked the child's father whether the sentence might be used in the program. He agreed after hearing the purpose.

電台的公開測試用簡單的轉輪顯示不同地方的聲音如何流動。孩子轉了一下輪子，指針落在標著「市場」的格子。艾雅問她想挑市場裡哪種聲音。「那位告訴我公車幾點來的阿姨。」孩子說。凱恩停止轉動旋鈕，抬起頭來。那比任何精心準備的範例都好。艾雅記下回答，然後詢問孩子父親是否能在節目使用這句話。他聽完用途後同意。

A berry seller outside the pier offered Aya a warm drink. Beyond her stall, a small circus rehearsed beside a wooden hut, and a magician wearing fairy wings invited children to a free show. A jazz band practiced nearby. The scene looked fancy, but Aya preferred the ordinary sound of people greeting one another. When a jet crossed the sky, she held her recorder still rather than pretend the noise was a miracle. A volunteer brought a heater and a cup marked one pint. Every voice had a place, even when it was not beautiful.

碼頭外一位賣莓果的攤販遞給艾雅一杯熱飲。攤位後面，小馬戲團在木棚旁排練，一位戴著仙子翅膀的魔術師邀請孩子看免費表演。附近有爵士樂團練習。景色十分華麗，但艾雅更喜歡人們互相問候的日常聲響。噴射機飛過天空時，她握穩錄音機，沒有假裝噪音是奇蹟。志工送來暖爐，以及一只標有一品脫的杯子。每種聲音都有位置，即使它並不悅耳。

Kai played his trial recording first: the click of a ferry gate, the breathing of the sea, and a child's footsteps on wet stone. Aya admired his skill, but his track contained no names. "What if a person does not want to be recorded?" she asked. Kai replied that he would ask for permission before any public release. The answer surprised her. She had expected a rival who cared only about perfect sound; instead she heard someone trying to protect the people behind it. She still wanted to win the hour herself.

凱恩先播放試錄：渡輪閘門的喀噠聲、大海的呼吸聲，以及孩子踩過濕石頭的腳步。艾雅佩服他的技術，卻發現錄音沒有任何姓名。「如果有人不想被錄下來呢？」她問。凱恩表示，公開之前一定會徵求同意。這回答令她意外。她原以為對手只在乎完美音色，卻聽見一個想保護聲音背後之人的人。即便如此，她仍想自己贏得那個時段。

The teacher reminded the clubs that the contest would judge both content and care. A program might sound wonderful and still treat a guest badly. A technical recording might be accurate and still miss a person who needed help. Aya placed two empty columns on the notice board, one for what they had learned and one for what they had not checked. Kai added a third for the people responsible for each next step. The columns remained almost empty at first, but neither student was embarrassed. An honest beginning could be small.

老師提醒兩社，評比同時看內容與照顧。節目可能好聽，卻對來賓不好；技術錄音可能準確，卻漏掉需要幫助的人。艾雅在公告板畫兩個空欄，一欄記已學到的事，一欄記還沒核對的事。凱恩加上第三欄，列出下一步負責人。起初欄位幾乎是空的，但兩人都不難為情。誠實的開始可以很小。

Near the studio door, a public notice explained that the building deed belonged to the city. A political candidate had asked to use the station, but the rules forbade election advertisements during student programs. Aya refused to quit when a sponsor suggested that she should let one famous guest control the whole show. "That would rob the neighbors of their hour," she said. Kai tightened a loose screw in the microphone stand and gave the engineer a hand signal. A spinning fan made their papers fly; they worked together to tame the disorder. The schedule listed various communities, not just the richest ones.

攝影棚門邊的公告說明，建物契據屬於市府。一名政治候選人曾要求使用電台，但規則禁止學生節目播出選舉廣告。贊助商建議讓一位名人主導整個節目，艾雅拒絕放棄。「那樣會奪走鄰居自己的時段。」她說。凱恩替麥克風架鎖緊鬆動的螺絲，向技術員打出手勢。旋轉的風扇把紙張吹得四散，兩人一起控制混亂。時程表列著各種社群，而不只有最富裕的人。

Ms. Ho gave them seven days to prepare a shared technical test, not a shared program. Each club would still be judged separately. Aya copied the rules onto a card and asked Kai to check her wording. He asked her to listen to the emergency announcement that the station was required to carry. A cheerful advertisement began to play where the safety message should have been. Both of them stopped smiling. Their competition had just uncovered a problem that belonged to everyone, and neither could solve it by winning alone.

何老師給他們七天準備共同的技術測試，但不是共同節目。兩個社團仍將分別評分。艾雅把規則抄在卡片上，請凱恩確認措辭。凱恩則請她聽電台依法必須播送的緊急公告。原本該是安全訊息的位置，竟響起輕快的廣告。兩人都收起笑容。他們的競賽無意間發現了大家共同的問題；任何一方單靠獲勝都無法解決。
