空中的謠言

A Rumor on the Air

當城市聽見我們 · 第 9 / 25 章 · 2000–3000 · 英中對照 · 校訂完成

用互動版查詞、朗讀與收藏

The next morning an online message claimed that the backup radio had caused an injury during an earlier storm. It named no person and offered no date, but people repeated it as fact. Aya wanted to protect the club's reputation. Kai suggested they first ask the former operators whether any such incident had been recorded. They found no matching report in the safety log. That absence could not prove the rumor false, yet it was enough to make them cautious about repeating a frightening claim.

隔天早上，網路訊息聲稱備用電台曾在一場暴風雨中造成傷害。訊息沒有姓名或日期，卻被人當成事實轉傳。艾雅想保護社團聲譽，凱恩建議先問前操作員是否留有紀錄。安全日誌裡沒有對應報告。缺少記錄不代表謠言一定錯誤，但足以提醒他們別輕率重述嚇人的指控。

Ms. Ho reminded the class to distinguish a witness from a storyteller. A witness could describe what happened to them; a storyteller could create a scene that never took place. Both forms had value, but they needed labels. Aya thought of her old advertising work, where those categories were sometimes mixed to sell a feeling. She had always known the difference in theory. Now it mattered in a practical way: listeners might choose a dangerous route if an invented event sounded like an official report.

何老師提醒學生分辨證人與說故事的人。證人能描述自身經歷，創作者能創造從未發生的場景。兩者都有價值，卻必須標示清楚。艾雅想到舊廣告工作，那些類別有時為了販賣感覺而混在一起。理論上她一直知道差別；如今差別有實際後果：若虛構事件聽起來像官方報告，聽眾可能選到危險路線。

The old cabin beside the radio tower contained a paper database from before computers were common. On one page someone had written, "Do not let a false story bleed into a real warning." On another, a bored technician had drawn a horn and a lion that seemed to roar. Aya almost fainted in the heat, then laughed at herself when Kai offered water. Their laughter did not remove the fright of a missing page. A skillful volunteer found its number in a weekly index. The page had been borrowed for a school lesson about democracy, not stolen.

電台塔旁的小木屋裡有一套電腦普及前的紙本資料庫。一頁寫著：「不要讓假故事流入真警報。」另一頁上，無聊的技師畫了喇叭與彷彿在吼叫的獅子。艾雅熱得幾乎昏倒，凱恩遞水時她又笑自己太慌。笑聲沒有消除遺失頁面的恐懼。熟練的志工從每週索引找到編號；那頁紙是被借去教民主課，而不是遭竊。

They interviewed the teacher who had borrowed the page. She remembered using the old safety exercise to show students how rumors grow when people skip dates and sources. The exercise included a fictional story about a sinking boat. When students retold it, some had forgotten the word fictional. Aya saw how a harmless classroom example could become dangerous when separated from its context. She edited a short correction explaining what they knew and which questions remained open.

他們訪問借走紙頁的老師。她記得使用舊安全練習，教學生了解人們省略日期與來源時，謠言如何擴大。練習裡有艘下沉小船的虛構故事。學生轉述時，有人忘記了「虛構」兩字。艾雅明白，一個無害的課堂例子脫離脈絡後可能變得危險。她剪輯一則簡短更正，說明已知事項與仍待查的問題。

During the evening edit, Aya left the false rumor in a private research file with a note explaining why it was not broadcast. She used only the verified facts in the public segment. Kai offered to store a copy of the relevant log with the club's safety records, separate from the interviews. They agreed on a date to review it again. Keeping a file did not mean believing everything it contained. It meant making it possible to understand later how a decision had been reached.

晚間剪輯時，艾雅把錯誤謠言留在私人研究檔，註明為何沒有播出。公開段落只使用核實資訊。凱恩提議把相關日誌影本放進社團安全紀錄，與訪談分開。他們約定日後再檢視。保留檔案不代表相信其內容，而是讓未來的人能理解當時如何作出決定。

A fisherman said he had heard a horn shortly before his boat began to sink, but a different vessel had caused the splash. His lover had waited at the pier with a dry coat; both were safe now. He liked to wander through the harbor and tune an old radio by hand. "A warning is not a weapon to use against people you dislike," he told Aya. "It should tell us what to do next." The story was powerful because it remained careful about what the man had truly seen. No extra roar or dramatic missing person was added for excitement.

漁夫說，船快下沉前不久聽見號角，但濺起水花的是另一艘船。他的伴侶帶著乾外套在碼頭等他；如今兩人都平安。他喜歡在港區閒逛，親手調整舊收音機的頻率。「警報不是拿來對付討厭之人的武器，」他告訴艾雅，「它應告訴我們下一步怎麼做。」這故事之所以有力量，是因為忠實呈現他真正看見的事；沒有為了刺激而添上吼聲或失蹤者。

The old records could bore a visitor looking for excitement, but Aya studied them carefully. In a corner of one page, faint pencil marks showed that the teacher had labeled the boat story as an exercise. She photographed the mark with the whole page, because a small detail could change a rumor's meaning.

老紀錄可能讓想尋求刺激的訪客覺得無聊，但艾雅仔細閱讀。某頁角落淡淡鉛筆痕顯示老師曾把小船故事標為練習。她連整頁一起拍下，因為小細節可能改變謠言的意義。

That evening the correction aired during the student news segment. Aya spoke in a calm voice and invited listeners to send dated evidence if they had it. The message did not mock those who had worried. Kai waited outside the booth until she finished, then asked whether she wanted company on the walk home. She did. They spoke about their grandparents' old radios and the different kinds of silence they remembered. It was the first walk on which neither mentioned who might win the broadcast hour.

當晚更正在學生新聞時段播出。艾雅平靜地說話，也邀請有具日期證據的人提供資料。訊息沒有嘲笑擔憂的居民。凱恩在播音室外等她錄完，問她想不想有人陪走回家。她想。一路上他們談祖父母的舊收音機，以及各自記得的不同沉默。那是他們第一次走在一起，卻沒有談誰會贏得廣播時段。
