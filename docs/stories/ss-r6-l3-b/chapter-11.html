陸地測試

The Test on Dry Land

當城市聽見我們 · 第 11 / 25 章 · 2000–3000 · 英中對照 · 校訂中

The independent technician arrived with a clear plan: no live public warning would be sent during the test. Instead, the team would use a closed circuit and a speaker inside the college gym. This protected residents from another false alarm. Aya drafted a sample message that began with the words "This is only a test." Kai inspected the backup battery. The manager watched without speaking. If the system passed, the clubs could prepare for the next stage; if it failed, they would document the problem rather than hide it.

獨立技師帶著清楚的計畫到場：測試期間不對公眾播出任何真正警報，而是使用封閉電路和學院體育館內的喇叭，以避免再次誤報。艾雅起草以「這只是測試」開頭的範例訊息。凱恩檢查備用電池。經理默默旁觀。系統若通過，兩社就能進入下一階段；若失敗，他們會記錄問題，而不是隱瞞。

The independent technician placed a red tag on the input that had failed. Nobody could use it for a public warning until an authorized repair and new test were complete. Aya read the tag aloud for the students. It did not blame a person; it described a condition and the steps required to change it. She admired that precision. In her old job, people had often demanded a dramatic sentence where a plain one would have been more useful. Here a plain sentence could keep someone from using unsafe equipment.

獨立技師在故障輸入端貼紅色標籤。未經核准維修與重新測試，不得再用它發布公共警報。艾雅為學生讀出標籤。它沒有責怪個人，只描述狀態及解除限制所需步驟。她欣賞這份精確。過去職場常有人要求戲劇化句子，即使平實說法更有用。這裡一句平實文字就可能讓人免於操作不安全設備。

An accurate report from the county showed that a drainage ditch could overflow after heavy rain. A local diver offered to dive under the pier only if a trained safety team approved the operation. The kitchen's freezer held cold water for workers, and a can of gasoline stayed locked outside, far from the electrical equipment. The intelligent solution was not to act like heroes but to keep every loose cable out of the path. Aya asked the media to report the difference between a planned test and a real emergency. A woman whispered, "Bless the people who check first."

郡政府一份準確報告顯示，排水溝可能在大雨後溢流。當地潛水員表示，只有專業安全隊批准，才會潛到碼頭下方。廚房冷凍庫放著給工作者的冷飲水，一桶汽油則鎖在遠離電器的室外。明智的做法不是逞英雄，而是讓每條鬆動電纜都遠離通道。艾雅請媒體分清計畫測試與真正緊急狀況。一位女士低聲說：「願那些先檢查的人平安。」

The first sound was clear. The second was lost in a sudden click. Kai repeated the test with the same volume and found that a protective switch interrupted the speaker when the advertising player activated. He did not ask anyone to remove the protection. Instead he traced the conflict back to the shared input and wrote a repair recommendation. Aya read the report aloud to the witnesses. One worker pointed out a missing time stamp; she added it and signed the corrected page.

第一段聲音很清楚，第二段卻在一聲喀噠後消失。凱恩以相同音量重測，發現廣告播放器啟動時，保護開關會中斷喇叭。他沒有要求拆除保護裝置，而是沿著共用輸入追查衝突，寫下維修建議。艾雅當著證人讀出報告。一位工作者指出少了時間紀錄，她補上並在修正版簽名。

Kai showed Mina how to read the equipment chart but did not ask her to operate a live control. She traced the line from the battery to the speaker, then explained it back in her own words. He corrected one small error without laughing. Aya watched and remembered how confidence could grow when a teacher did not treat questions as weakness. The students agreed to include training time in the next budget. Buying a machine was not enough. Someone needed the skill and permission to use it safely.

凱恩教米娜讀設備圖，卻沒有要求她操作通電控制器。她沿線從電池看到喇叭，再用自己的話解釋。凱恩糾正一個小錯，沒有嘲笑。艾雅看著這一幕，想起老師若不把提問當弱點，人的自信就會長出來。學生同意下一份預算要包含訓練時間。買機器不夠，還需要具備技能與許可、能安全使用它的人。

The technician explained that a radio had to perform reliably under stress, not just in quiet weather. He carried plastic covers for the switches and a timber board to keep cables above the wet floor. Roughly twenty people observed, and the climb to the equipment room made them sweat. A medical volunteer checked whether anyone had strained a muscle. Aya repeated the theory in plain language: the wrong input could weaken a safety signal. Outside, violet flowers bent in the wind, a gentle reminder that the season was changing whether the equipment was ready or not.

技師解釋，廣播設備必須在壓力下可靠運作，而非只在風平浪靜時正常。他帶來塑膠開關罩與木板，讓電纜遠離濕地。約有二十人觀察，爬到機房使大家流汗。一位醫療志工確認沒有人拉傷肌肉。艾雅用白話重述原理：錯誤的輸入會削弱安全訊號。室外紫羅蘭被風吹彎，溫柔地提醒他們：不管設備準備好了沒有，季節都會改變。

When the visitors left, Kai sat down with his hands shaking from tiredness. Aya brought him water and asked whether he needed a break. He said yes. For several minutes they spoke of nothing technical. She told him she feared becoming the kind of producer who valued a successful show more than people. He said that naming the fear already helped her avoid it. She replied that fear was not enough; she would need others to challenge her when her own work became too important to question.

訪客離開後，凱恩疲倦得手微微發抖。艾雅送水來問他要不要休息。他說要。好幾分鐘裡，他們沒有談任何技術問題。艾雅說，她怕自己變成那種重視節目成功多過重視人的製作人。凱恩說能說出恐懼，已幫助她避免重蹈覆轍。艾雅回答，光害怕還不夠；當自己的作品重要到不容質疑時，她需要別人提醒自己。
