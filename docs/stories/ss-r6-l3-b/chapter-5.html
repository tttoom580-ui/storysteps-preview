會說不的聲音

A Voice That Says No

當城市聽見我們 · 第 5 / 25 章 · 2000–3000 · 英中對照

用互動版查詞、朗讀與收藏

On Friday, the sponsor's representative, Mr. Voss, visited the college. He offered new microphones if the clubs would play his company's opening message before every broadcast. The offer seemed generous, but the contract gave the sponsor the right to remove interviews he considered damaging. Aya read that line twice. Years earlier she had earned money by cutting difficult sentences from advertisements. She had returned to school precisely because she wanted to stop doing that. She declined the offer in front of the board.

星期五，贊助商代表沃斯先生到學院拜訪。他提議提供新麥克風，條件是每次播出前播放公司的開場訊息。提議看似慷慨，但合約賦予贊助商刪除其認為有損形象之訪談的權利。艾雅把那一條讀了兩次。多年前她靠從廣告剪掉難聽的句子賺錢；她回學校正是為了不再做那種事。她當著評審的面拒絕提議。

After the vote, the Radio Club created a new donation page that showed each gift and its conditions. A neighbor gave a small amount for replacement batteries; another offered a desk without asking to choose the guests. Aya checked that the club could return anything if the conditions changed. Mina asked whether refusing large donors meant every future episode would be plain. Aya said a modest program could still be beautiful if it made space for real voices. She refused to measure the worth of a person by the size of a gift.

投票後，廣播社建立新的捐款頁，列出每筆捐贈及條件。鄰居捐一小筆錢買電池，另一人送來桌子，也不要求挑選來賓。艾雅確認條件變更時社團可以歸還物品。米娜問拒絕大捐款是否表示未來每集都會很平淡。艾雅說，只要容得下真實聲音，樸素節目也可以很美。她不願用捐款金額衡量人的價值。

At the community kitchen, bacon steamed on a hot plate while volunteers rolled up a carpet for a recording booth. A child began to cough, and a worker opened the garage door for fresh air. Someone tried to complain about the noise, but the manager explained that the crowd had a considerable interest in the meeting. A fake microphone, made of cardboard, let children practice asking questions. Aya used a flashlight to find a missing motor part and a sack of ripe peanuts. One volunteer could not pronounce the sponsor's name, and nobody thought that mattered.

在社區廚房，培根在熱盤上冒著蒸氣，志工們則捲起地毯準備設立錄音間。一個孩子開始咳嗽，工作人員打開車庫門通風。有人抱怨噪音，但經理解釋，許多居民都很關切這次會議。紙板做的假麥克風讓孩子練習提問。艾雅用手電筒找到遺失的馬達零件與一袋成熟花生。有位志工念不出贊助商的名字，沒有人認為這很重要。

Kai did not praise Aya for being brave. He asked whether refusing the sponsor had cost her club a microphone it badly needed. She admitted that it had. He offered to lend a spare microphone for the test, with the loan and return date written down. Aya accepted the equipment but not a debt of affection. They both laughed at how formal the receipt looked. Then she grew serious: she wanted his technical help to remain independent of the question of whether they would ever be more than friends.

凱恩沒有稱讚艾雅勇敢，而是問拒絕贊助是否讓她的社團失去急需的麥克風。她承認確實如此。他提議借出一支備用麥克風，清楚寫下借還日期。艾雅接受設備，卻不把它變成人情上的感情債。兩人笑那張收據太正式。接著她認真表示：他的技術協助應獨立於他們未來是否超越朋友關係的問題。

That night her former supervisor sent a message offering a well-paid editing contract. The work would require her to remove complaints from a company's recorded customer interviews. Aya read it twice, feeling the old fear of uncertain income. She did not answer immediately. She checked her savings and the cost of the next semester, then wrote a polite refusal. She could not afford every dream at once, but she could choose which compromises she would not make again. She slept more easily after sending it.

那晚，前主管傳來一份待遇不錯的剪輯工作邀請，工作是從公司的顧客訪談錄音中去除抱怨。艾雅看了兩遍，感到過去對收入不穩的恐懼。她沒有立刻回答，而是先檢查存款與下學期費用，再禮貌拒絕。她無法一次負擔所有夢想，卻能選擇不再做哪些妥協。送出訊息後，她睡得比較安穩。

The sponsor's leaflet promised engagement with the public, but its fine print gave him almost complete control. Aya called that a matter of responsibility, not just taste. A child's jaw dropped when she explained why one small sentence could change a whole program. The cook offered a peanut snack and a bowl of soup; steam rose between the speakers. A woman spoke of sorrow after a violent storm had damaged her old house. The producer wanted to cut that part because it sounded sad. Aya said that the resident's own choice mattered more than a bright advertisement.

贊助商傳單承諾與公眾互動，但細則幾乎給了他完全控制權。艾雅說這是責任問題，不只是品味問題。一個孩子聽她解釋一個小句子如何改變整個節目，驚訝得下巴都掉了。廚師端來花生點心和湯，熱氣在說話的人之間升起。一位女士談起猛烈暴風雨毀壞老屋後的悲傷。製作人想剪掉那段，因為聽起來太傷感。艾雅說，居民自己的選擇比明亮的廣告更重要。

Before leaving, Mr. Voss warned that the station might lose all private support. Aya knew he might be right. She called a club meeting rather than make that risk her private burden. Members voted to keep their editorial independence and look for smaller donations. Kai attended only as an observer. Watching Aya return a difficult decision to the people who would live with it changed his view of her. He admired her not because she always sounded certain, but because she let other people disagree.

離開之前，沃斯警告電台可能失去所有私人支持。艾雅知道他也許說得沒錯。她召開社團會議，沒有把風險當成自己的私人負擔。社員投票決定維持編輯自主，改向較小額的捐款尋求協助。凱恩只以旁聽身分出席。看見艾雅把困難決定交還給必須承受後果的人，改變了他對她的看法。他欣賞她不是因為她總是確定，而是因為她允許別人不同意。
