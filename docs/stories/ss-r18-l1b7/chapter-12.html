雨中的尾巴

The Rainy Tail

Rain soaked Yuzu's coat and made hiding his tail impossible. At first, Yuzu thought the problem could be solved before the next bell. A closer look showed that someone else's wishes mattered too. Nami listened without interrupting, while Ms. Kato watched the group from nearby. They did not have to agree on everything immediately. There was time to separate a real observation from a story someone had only imagined. Yuzu took out the learning pass and asked what should be checked first.

大雨浸濕柚柚的外套，尾巴再也藏不住。 起初，柚柚以為下次鐘響前就能解決問題。但仔細看過後才發現，別人的願望也很重要。奈美沒有打斷，靜靜傾聽，加藤老師則在附近看著大家。眾人不必立刻同意每件事，還有時間分清真實觀察與想像中的說法。柚柚拿出學習許可證，問大家應先確認什麼。

They began by asking who might be affected by the next step. Nami thought about the people who had not spoken yet, and Yuzu thought about the rule they had already promised to follow. One person offered a quick answer, but the group asked for a reason. A good question was not an attack. It was a way to make the answer safer and kinder. Ms. Kato reminded them that everyone could ask for a pause. In the English practice book, actor appeared on the next line. Yuzu read afternoon aloud, then checked the meaning with Nami. Another card carried band and a simple drawing by a classmate. The word celebrate gave the group a new example to discuss. Nami put corner on the board without turning it into a test.

大家先問，下一步會影響到誰。奈美想到還沒發言的人，柚柚則想到自己早已承諾遵守的規則。有人很快提出答案，其他人卻請他說明理由。好問題不是攻擊，而是讓答案更安全、更體貼的方法。加藤老師提醒大家，每個人都可以要求暫停。 英文練習本的下一行出現「actor」（演員）。 柚柚朗讀「afternoon」（下午），再和奈美確認意思。 另一張圖卡上有「band」（樂團），旁邊是同學的簡筆畫。 「celebrate」（慶祝）這個字讓小組多了一個可以討論的例子。 奈美把「corner」（轉角）寫在板上，卻沒有把它變成考試。

Nami opened an umbrella while Yuzu walked beside her openly. Yuzu chose the part of the work that could be done carefully. Nami handled a different task and promised to report back before making any large change. A short question from Ms. Kato caught an error in their first version. Instead of hiding it, they made a neat correction on the page. That correction did not erase their effort. It showed that the group was learning how to be responsible for its own work. They found die in a short reading exercise near the end of the page. Yuzu drew a tiny sign beside glasses so it would be easier to remember. Nami chose I as a word to explain kindly to a new learner. A small study card showed key, which Yuzu copied beside today's notes. Nami asked how to use last in a sentence about their day.

奈美撐起傘，柚柚坦然走在她身旁。 柚柚選擇自己能謹慎完成的工作，奈美負責另一項任務，並答應在重大更改前先回報。加藤老師的一個簡短問題，讓大家發現第一版中的錯誤。他們沒有掩飾，而是在頁面上整齊修正。這項修正沒有抹去先前的努力，反而證明小組正在學習對自己的工作負責。 他們在頁面末尾的短文練習裡找到「die」（枯死）。 柚柚在「glasses」（眼鏡）旁畫上小記號，幫助記憶。 奈美選了「I」（我），準備耐心向初學者說明。 一張小小練習卡上寫著「key」（鑰匙），柚柚把它抄在今日筆記旁。 奈美問起，如何用「last」（最後的）寫一句與今天有關的話。

Yuzu was afraid that saying the wrong thing would make the day worse. Nami answered that staying silent could also leave a misunderstanding in place. They practiced one honest sentence and checked that it did not blame anyone unfairly. This took longer than making a joke, but the result was clearer. Near the table, Ms. Kato nodded and let the young people try their own words before offering advice. In the English practice book, media appeared on the next line. Yuzu read more aloud, then checked the meaning with Nami. Another card carried o’clock and a simple drawing by a classmate. The word or gave the group a new example to discuss. Nami put package on the board without turning it into a test.

柚柚害怕說錯話會讓事情變糟。奈美回答，保持沉默有時也會讓誤會繼續存在。他們練習說出一句誠實的話，再確認沒有不公平地責怪任何人。這比開玩笑花時間，結果卻更清楚。桌旁的加藤老師點頭，先讓年輕人用自己的話嘗試，再提供建議。 英文練習本的下一行出現「media」（媒體）。 柚柚朗讀「more」（更多），再和奈美確認意思。 另一張圖卡上有「o’clock」（整點鐘），旁邊是同學的簡筆畫。 「or」（或）這個字讓小組多了一個可以討論的例子。 奈美把「package」（包裹）寫在板上，卻沒有把它變成考試。

The younger pupils asked questions kindly instead of running away. Ms. Kato asked the group to explain its choice in everyday words. Yuzu tried once, paused, and then gave a shorter answer. Nami added an important detail that had been left out. The explanation became clear enough for another person to check. That mattered more than making their decision sound grand. The learning without hiding could grow only if other people understood how it worked. They found play in a short reading exercise near the end of the page. Yuzu drew a tiny sign beside poor so it would be easier to remember. Nami chose supermarket as a word to explain kindly to a new learner. A small study card showed ticket, which Yuzu copied beside today's notes. Nami asked how to use tired in a sentence about their day.

年幼學生溫柔地提問，沒有害怕逃開。 加藤老師請小組用平常的話解釋選擇。柚柚試了一次，停頓後改用更短的回答。奈美補充了一個漏掉的重要細節。解釋變得足夠清楚，讓別人也能核對。這比把決定說得偉大更重要。只有其他人理解做法，不必藏身的學習才有機會繼續成長。 他們在頁面末尾的短文練習裡找到「play」（演奏）。 柚柚在「poor」（不足的；昏暗的）旁畫上小記號，幫助記憶。 奈美選了「supermarket」（超市），準備耐心向初學者說明。 一張小小練習卡上寫著「ticket」（門票），柚柚把它抄在今日筆記旁。 奈美問起，如何用「tired」（疲倦的）寫一句與今天有關的話。

The hallway grew quiet, and Yuzu finally had time to think. A good friend had not supplied every answer. Instead, Nami had left space for another idea and had stayed when the first idea failed. That was harder to notice than a loud victory, but it was real. Yuzu made a promise to remember the person as well as the task. The learning without hiding was important; so were the people who helped shape it. With that thought, the next chapter of their ordinary and extraordinary days felt possible.

走廊逐漸安靜，柚柚終於有時間思考。好朋友不是替自己提供每個答案；奈美反而留下思考空間，也在第一個想法失敗時陪在身旁。這比熱鬧的勝利難以察覺，卻是真實的。柚柚答應自己，不只記得任務，也要記得參與的人。不必藏身的學習重要，幫忙塑造它的人也同樣重要。帶著這個念頭，他們平凡又不平凡的日子可以繼續寫下去。
