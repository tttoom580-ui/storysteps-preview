Yuzu laughed at a joke and his tail swept three pencils off a desk. The day began with a small question and soon became more interesting than anyone expected. Yuzu could hear other students talking, but did not hurry to join their guesses. Nami pointed out a detail that had been easy to miss. They stood together in the human school, where the same familiar objects suddenly seemed to tell a different story. Yuzu kept the learning pass close, not as a perfect answer, but as a place to begin.

柚柚聽笑話笑得太開心，尾巴把三支鉛筆掃落桌。 這一天從一個小疑問開始，後來卻比大家預想的更有趣。柚柚聽到其他同學交談，沒有急著加入猜測。奈美指出了一個容易忽略的細節。他們站在人類學校裡，熟悉的東西忽然像在說另一段故事。柚柚把學習許可證帶在身邊，那不是完美答案，而是一個開始。

Yuzu was afraid that saying the wrong thing would make the day worse. Nami answered that staying silent could also leave a misunderstanding in place. They practiced one honest sentence and checked that it did not blame anyone unfairly. This took longer than making a joke, but the result was clearer. Near the table, Ms. Kato nodded and let the young people try their own words before offering advice. They found by in a short reading exercise near the end of the page. Yuzu drew a tiny sign beside chocolate so it would be easier to remember. Nami chose class as a word to explain kindly to a new learner. A small study card showed collect, which Yuzu copied beside today's notes. Nami asked how to use course in a sentence about their day.

柚柚害怕說錯話會讓事情變糟。奈美回答，保持沉默有時也會讓誤會繼續存在。他們練習說出一句誠實的話，再確認沒有不公平地責怪任何人。這比開玩笑花時間，結果卻更清楚。桌旁的加藤老師點頭，先讓年輕人用自己的話嘗試，再提供建議。 他們在頁面末尾的短文練習裡找到「by」（經由；在旁）。 柚柚在「chocolate」（巧克力）旁畫上小記號，幫助記憶。 奈美選了「class」（課堂），準備耐心向初學者說明。 一張小小練習卡上寫著「collect」（收集），柚柚把它抄在今日筆記旁。 奈美問起，如何用「course」（課程）寫一句與今天有關的話。

He apologized and helped pick them up after changing back. There was a quiet kind of courage in trying again. Yuzu noticed that the last attempt had taught them something, even without a perfect result. Nami offered practical help and asked which part would be most useful. They worked together for a short time and then stopped to check whether they were still following the rule. Ms. Kato praised the careful check more than the speed of the work. In the English practice book, flower appeared on the next line. Yuzu read hour aloud, then checked the meaning with Nami. Another card carried housewife and a simple drawing by a classmate. The word large gave the group a new example to discuss. Nami put let on the board without turning it into a test.

他道歉，變回人形後幫忙撿起鉛筆。 願意再試一次，是一種安靜的勇氣。柚柚發現，就算上次結果不完美，大家仍學到了一些事。奈美提供實際協助，先問哪一部分最有用。他們一起工作一小段時間，再停下來確認是否仍遵守規則。加藤老師稱讚的不是速度，而是這份細心。 英文練習本的下一行出現「flower」（花）。 柚柚朗讀「hour」（小時），再和奈美確認意思。 另一張圖卡上有「housewife」（家庭主婦），旁邊是同學的簡筆畫。 「large」（大的）這個字讓小組多了一個可以討論的例子。 奈美把「let」（讓）寫在板上，卻沒有把它變成考試。

They began by asking who might be affected by the next step. Nami thought about the people who had not spoken yet, and Yuzu thought about the rule they had already promised to follow. One person offered a quick answer, but the group asked for a reason. A good question was not an attack. It was a way to make the answer safer and kinder. Ms. Kato reminded them that everyone could ask for a pause. They found list in a short reading exercise near the end of the page. Yuzu drew a tiny sign beside main so it would be easier to remember. Nami chose note as a word to explain kindly to a new learner. A small study card showed popular, which Yuzu copied beside today's notes. Nami asked how to use rabbit in a sentence about their day.

大家先問，下一步會影響到誰。奈美想到還沒發言的人，柚柚則想到自己早已承諾遵守的規則。有人很快提出答案，其他人卻請他說明理由。好問題不是攻擊，而是讓答案更安全、更體貼的方法。加藤老師提醒大家，每個人都可以要求暫停。 他們在頁面末尾的短文練習裡找到「list」（清單）。 柚柚在「main」（主要的）旁畫上小記號，幫助記憶。 奈美選了「note」（筆記；便條），準備耐心向初學者說明。 一張小小練習卡上寫著「popular」（受歡迎的），柚柚把它抄在今日筆記旁。 奈美問起，如何用「rabbit」（兔子）寫一句與今天有關的話。

The class laughed with him rather than at him. The answer made someone smile, but Yuzu still asked one final question: was everyone comfortable with what came next? Nami listened to the replies, including the quiet ones. They changed a small detail when one person needed something different. Nothing about the change made the story less meaningful. It helped the learning without hiding belong to more than one person, which was why the work had begun. In the English practice book, real appeared on the next line. Yuzu read start aloud, then checked the meaning with Nami. Another card carried there and a simple drawing by a classmate. The word until gave the group a new example to discuss. Nami put west on the board without turning it into a test.

全班和他一起笑，而不是嘲笑他。 答案讓某個人笑了，但柚柚仍問最後一個問題：大家都能接受下一步嗎？奈美聽取回答，包括輕聲說出的意見。有人有不同需求，他們就修改一個小細節。這種修改沒有讓故事失去意義，反而讓不必藏身的學習不只屬於一個人；這也正是大家當初開始的原因。 英文練習本的下一行出現「real」（真實的）。 柚柚朗讀「start」（開始），再和奈美確認意思。 另一張圖卡上有「there」（那裡），旁邊是同學的簡筆畫。 「until」（直到）這個字讓小組多了一個可以討論的例子。 奈美把「west」（西方；西門）寫在板上，卻沒有把它變成考試。

When the last bell sounded, Yuzu saw that Nami was waiting by the familiar door. The two had not said everything perfectly. They had even disagreed about a detail, yet the disagreement had made their plan clearer. The moment did not need a large speech. A small smile and one honest thank-you were enough. At home, Yuzu would remember the most surprising part of the day and write it on the next page. Tomorrow the school gate rule might look different again. This time they would know how to begin.

最後一聲鈴響時，柚柚看見奈美在熟悉的門旁等待。兩人不是每句話都說得完美，甚至為某個細節意見不同，但那場分歧讓計畫更清楚。這一刻不需要長篇大論，一個微笑和真誠的道謝就夠了。回家後，柚柚會把今天最驚訝的事寫在下一頁。明天校門規定也許又有不同面貌，但這次他們知道如何開始。
