The Fair Rule Meeting

The school board met to decide how visitors of different species could learn safely. The day began with a small question and soon became more interesting than anyone expected. Yuzu could hear other students talking, but did not hurry to join their guesses. Nami pointed out a detail that had been easy to miss. They stood together in the human school, where the same familiar objects suddenly seemed to tell a different story. Yuzu kept the learning pass close, not as a perfect answer, but as a place to begin.

校務會議討論不同物種如何安全參與學習。 這一天從一個小疑問開始，後來卻比大家預想的更有趣。柚柚聽到其他同學交談，沒有急著加入猜測。奈美指出了一個容易忽略的細節。他們站在人類學校裡，熟悉的東西忽然像在說另一段故事。柚柚把學習許可證帶在身邊，那不是完美答案，而是一個開始。

They began by asking who might be affected by the next step. Nami thought about the people who had not spoken yet, and Yuzu thought about the rule they had already promised to follow. One person offered a quick answer, but the group asked for a reason. A good question was not an attack. It was a way to make the answer safer and kinder. Ms. Kato reminded them that everyone could ask for a pause. Nami asked how to use basket in a sentence about their day. In the English practice book, belt appeared on the next line. Yuzu read check aloud, then checked the meaning with Nami. Another card carried color and a simple drawing by a classmate. The word dear gave the group a new example to discuss.

大家先問，下一步會影響到誰。奈美想到還沒發言的人，柚柚則想到自己早已承諾遵守的規則。有人很快提出答案，其他人卻請他說明理由。好問題不是攻擊，而是讓答案更安全、更體貼的方法。加藤老師提醒大家，每個人都可以要求暫停。 奈美問起，如何用「basket」（籃子）寫一句與今天有關的話。 英文練習本的下一行出現「belt」（腰帶）。 柚柚朗讀「check」（檢查），再和奈美確認意思。 另一張圖卡上有「color」（顏色），旁邊是同學的簡筆畫。 「dear」（親愛的）這個字讓小組多了一個可以討論的例子。

Yuzu asked to speak about his experience rather than have Nami speak for him. Yuzu chose the part of the work that could be done carefully. Nami handled a different task and promised to report back before making any large change. A short question from Ms. Kato caught an error in their first version. Instead of hiding it, they made a neat correction on the page. That correction did not erase their effort. It showed that the group was learning how to be responsible for its own work. Nami put hate on the board without turning it into a test. They found health in a short reading exercise near the end of the page. Yuzu drew a tiny sign beside hospital so it would be easier to remember. Nami chose if as a word to explain kindly to a new learner. A small study card showed joke, which Yuzu copied beside today's notes.

柚柚希望自己說明經驗，而不是由奈美代言。 柚柚選擇自己能謹慎完成的工作，奈美負責另一項任務，並答應在重大更改前先回報。加藤老師的一個簡短問題，讓大家發現第一版中的錯誤。他們沒有掩飾，而是在頁面上整齊修正。這項修正沒有抹去先前的努力，反而證明小組正在學習對自己的工作負責。 奈美把「hate」（討厭）寫在板上，卻沒有把它變成考試。 他們在頁面末尾的短文練習裡找到「health」（健康）。 柚柚在「hospital」（醫院）旁畫上小記號，幫助記憶。 奈美選了「if」（如果），準備耐心向初學者說明。 一張小小練習卡上寫著「joke」（玩笑），柚柚把它抄在今日筆記旁。

Yuzu was afraid that saying the wrong thing would make the day worse. Nami answered that staying silent could also leave a misunderstanding in place. They practiced one honest sentence and checked that it did not blame anyone unfairly. This took longer than making a joke, but the result was clearer. Near the table, Ms. Kato nodded and let the young people try their own words before offering advice. Nami asked how to use kite in a sentence about their day. In the English practice book, library appeared on the next line. Yuzu read often aloud, then checked the meaning with Nami. Another card carried picture and a simple drawing by a classmate. The word player gave the group a new example to discuss.

柚柚害怕說錯話會讓事情變糟。奈美回答，保持沉默有時也會讓誤會繼續存在。他們練習說出一句誠實的話，再確認沒有不公平地責怪任何人。這比開玩笑花時間，結果卻更清楚。桌旁的加藤老師點頭，先讓年輕人用自己的話嘗試，再提供建議。 奈美問起，如何用「kite」（風箏）寫一句與今天有關的話。 英文練習本的下一行出現「library」（圖書館）。 柚柚朗讀「often」（經常），再和奈美確認意思。 另一張圖卡上有「picture」（圖畫），旁邊是同學的簡筆畫。 「player」（演奏者；參與者）這個字讓小組多了一個可以討論的例子。

The board agreed to study a small supervised program. Ms. Kato asked the group to explain its choice in everyday words. Yuzu tried once, paused, and then gave a shorter answer. Nami added an important detail that had been left out. The explanation became clear enough for another person to check. That mattered more than making their decision sound grand. The learning without hiding could grow only if other people understood how it worked. Nami put send on the board without turning it into a test. They found thin in a short reading exercise near the end of the page. Yuzu drew a tiny sign beside those so it would be easier to remember. Nami chose useful as a word to explain kindly to a new learner. A small study card showed white, which Yuzu copied beside today's notes.

校務會議同意研究有人監督的小型學習計畫。 加藤老師請小組用平常的話解釋選擇。柚柚試了一次，停頓後改用更短的回答。奈美補充了一個漏掉的重要細節。解釋變得足夠清楚，讓別人也能核對。這比把決定說得偉大更重要。只有其他人理解做法，不必藏身的學習才有機會繼續成長。 奈美把「send」（寄送）寫在板上，卻沒有把它變成考試。 他們在頁面末尾的短文練習裡找到「thin」（薄的）。 柚柚在「those」（那些）旁畫上小記號，幫助記憶。 奈美選了「useful」（有用），準備耐心向初學者說明。 一張小小練習卡上寫著「white」（白色的），柚柚把它抄在今日筆記旁。

When the last bell sounded, Yuzu saw that Nami was waiting by the familiar door. The two had not said everything perfectly. They had even disagreed about a detail, yet the disagreement had made their plan clearer. The moment did not need a large speech. A small smile and one honest thank-you were enough. At home, Yuzu would remember the most surprising part of the day and write it on the next page. Tomorrow the school gate rule might look different again. This time they would know how to begin.

最後一聲鈴響時，柚柚看見奈美在熟悉的門旁等待。兩人不是每句話都說得完美，甚至為某個細節意見不同，但那場分歧讓計畫更清楚。這一刻不需要長篇大論，一個微笑和真誠的道謝就夠了。回家後，柚柚會把今天最驚訝的事寫在下一頁。明天校門規定也許又有不同面貌，但這次他們知道如何開始。
