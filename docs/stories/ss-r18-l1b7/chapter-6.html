Yuzu received a temporary learning pass while the adults reviewed safety. Nobody had planned for this moment. The room was full of ordinary sounds, yet Yuzu felt that something important had changed. Nami waited beside the door and let the first surprise settle. It was tempting to find an easy answer before anyone knew the whole story. Instead, they looked carefully at what was truly in front of them. Yuzu opened the learning pass and made a small note about the day.

大人評估安全期間，柚柚取得暫時學習許可。 沒有人預料到這一刻。人類學校充滿平常的聲音，柚柚卻感覺有件重要的事改變了。奈美站在門旁，讓最初的驚訝慢慢沉澱。大家很容易在弄清事情之前就急著下結論，但他們決定先看清眼前真正發生的事。柚柚翻開學習許可證，記下今天的小小發現。

For a minute, the group spoke all at once. Yuzu raised a hand and suggested taking turns. Nami repeated the question in simpler words, so that even a quiet student could follow the talk. Nobody needed to win an argument. They needed to learn which part of the problem could be handled now and which part needed more help. The school gate rule remained difficult, but it no longer felt impossible. Nami put arm on the board without turning it into a test. They found convenient in a short reading exercise near the end of the page. Yuzu drew a tiny sign beside deep so it would be easier to remember. Nami drew a small picture of desk in the margin of the class story page. A small study card showed do, which Yuzu copied beside today's notes.

有一分鐘，大家幾乎同時開口。柚柚舉起手，提議輪流說話。奈美用更簡單的詞重述問題，讓安靜的學生也能跟上。沒有人需要爭贏；重要的是分清哪些事現在能做、哪些事需要更多協助。校門規定仍然困難，卻不再讓人覺得無法處理。 奈美把「arm」（手臂）寫在板上，卻沒有把它變成考試。 他們在頁面末尾的短文練習裡找到「convenient」（方便的）。 柚柚在「deep」（深處的）旁畫上小記號，幫助記憶。 奈美在班級故事頁的空白處畫了「desk」（桌子）的圖。 一張小小練習卡上寫著「do」（做），柚柚把它抄在今日筆記旁。

Nami helped him put his own name on a desk instead of hiding behind hers. Once everyone understood the choice, the next step was smaller than it had seemed. Yuzu spoke first and waited for an answer. Nami watched the other person's face instead of trying to hurry the talk. There was room to say no, to ask for an explanation, or to think for another minute. The group used the learning pass as a guide, not as an order that could never be changed. Nami asked how to use envelope in a sentence about their day. In the English practice book, find appeared on the next line. Yuzu read fish aloud, then checked the meaning with Nami. Another card carried funny and a simple drawing by a classmate. The word history gave the group a new example to discuss.

奈美幫他把自己的名字貼上桌子，不用再躲在她後面。 當所有人都了解選擇後，下一步其實沒有想像中困難。柚柚先開口，然後等待回答。奈美注意對方的表情，沒有催促。大家可以說不、要求解釋，或多想一分鐘。小組把學習許可證當作指引，而不是絕不能改變的命令。 奈美問起，如何用「envelope」（信封）寫一句與今天有關的話。 英文練習本的下一行出現「find」（找到）。 柚柚朗讀「fish」（魚），再和奈美確認意思。 另一張圖卡上有「funny」（有趣），旁邊是同學的簡筆畫。 「history」（歷史）這個字讓小組多了一個可以討論的例子。

The first idea sounded simple, and that was useful. Still, Yuzu wanted to know whether it respected everyone's choice. Nami suggested changing only one part rather than throwing the whole plan away. They drew two small boxes on a page: what we can do and what we must ask about. The list made the school gate rule easier to discuss without turning a friend into an enemy. Nami put however on the board without turning it into a test. They found may in a short reading exercise near the end of the page. Yuzu drew a tiny sign beside mud so it would be easier to remember. Nami chose news as a word to explain kindly to a new learner. A small study card showed rise, which Yuzu copied beside today's notes.

第一個想法聽起來很簡單，這是好事。但柚柚還想確認是否尊重每個人的選擇。奈美建議只修改其中一部分，不必推翻整個計畫。他們在紙上畫兩個方框：我們能做的事，以及必須先詢問的事。有了這份清單，討論校門規定便容易多了，也不會把朋友當成敵人。 奈美把「however」（然而）寫在板上，卻沒有把它變成考試。 他們在頁面末尾的短文練習裡找到「may」（可能）。 柚柚在「mud」（泥巴）旁畫上小記號，幫助記憶。 奈美選了「news」（新聞），準備耐心向初學者說明。 一張小小練習卡上寫著「rise」（升起），柚柚把它抄在今日筆記旁。

For the first time, he sat in class as himself. Before calling the task finished, Yuzu went back over the facts. One detail had changed, and another still needed an answer. Nami wrote both down rather than hiding the uncertain part. They could be pleased with progress without pretending that every worry had disappeared. By comparing the new result with the first note, they could see what had actually improved. The learning without hiding still mattered, but now the group knew one more way to protect it. Nami asked how to use seat in a sentence about their day. In the English practice book, shop appeared on the next line. Yuzu read speak aloud, then checked the meaning with Nami. Another card carried surprised and a simple drawing by a classmate. The word welcome gave the group a new example to discuss.

他第一次以真正的自己坐在教室裡。 在宣布完成前，柚柚重新檢查事實。有一個細節改變了，另一個仍有待解答。奈美把兩者都寫下，沒有隱藏不確定之處。大家可以為進展高興，卻不必假裝所有擔憂都消失。比對新結果與第一張筆記後，他們看出真正改善的部分。不必藏身的學習依然重要，現在小組又多學會一種守護它的方法。 奈美問起，如何用「seat」（座位）寫一句與今天有關的話。 英文練習本的下一行出現「shop」（商店）。 柚柚朗讀「speak」（說話），再和奈美確認意思。 另一張圖卡上有「surprised」（驚訝），旁邊是同學的簡筆畫。 「welcome」（歡迎）這個字讓小組多了一個可以討論的例子。

Near the end of the day, Yuzu stayed beside Nami for one more minute. They remembered how strange the morning had felt. A problem that once seemed to fill the whole room had become several smaller questions. Each question could be handled in turn. They did not know what the following day would bring, and that was all right. What they had learned was not a magic answer but a habit: notice a change, ask with care, and let people choose for themselves. Yuzu closed the learning pass and left with a lighter heart.

一天快結束時，柚柚在奈美身邊多待了一分鐘。他們想起早上的奇怪感受。原本像占滿整間房間的難題，現在分成幾個較小的問題，可以逐一處理。他們不知道明天會帶來什麼，但這沒有關係。今天學到的不是神奇答案，而是一種習慣：留意變化、仔細發問，也讓別人自己選擇。柚柚闔上學習許可證，帶著較輕鬆的心情離開。
