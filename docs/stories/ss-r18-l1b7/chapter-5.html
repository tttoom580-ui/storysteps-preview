The principal said the rule began after a wild fox broke into the food room years ago. The light through the window looked ordinary, but the news did not. Yuzu stopped for a moment, feeling both curious and unsure. Nami did not laugh at the question, even when it sounded strange. They knew from earlier mistakes that moving too fast could make a simple problem harder. The human school offered enough space for a calm talk. On the first page of the learning pass, Yuzu wrote what was known and left a blank space for what remained uncertain.

校長說，多年前有野狐狸闖進食品室，學校才設下禁令。 窗外的光線和平常一樣，消息卻一點也不平常。柚柚停了一會兒，既好奇又不確定。就算問題聽來奇怪，奈美也沒有取笑。他們從先前的錯誤學到，行動太快反而可能讓小問題變複雜。人類學校有足夠空間讓大家平靜討論。柚柚在學習許可證第一頁寫下已知的事，也留下空白給尚未確定的部分。

For a minute, the group spoke all at once. Yuzu raised a hand and suggested taking turns. Nami repeated the question in simpler words, so that even a quiet student could follow the talk. Nobody needed to win an argument. They needed to learn which part of the problem could be handled now and which part needed more help. The school gate rule remained difficult, but it no longer felt impossible. The word bank gave the group a new example to discuss. Nami put farm on the board without turning it into a test. They found feed in a short reading exercise near the end of the page. Yuzu drew a tiny sign beside letter so it would be easier to remember. Nami chose many as a word to explain kindly to a new learner.

有一分鐘，大家幾乎同時開口。柚柚舉起手，提議輪流說話。奈美用更簡單的詞重述問題，讓安靜的學生也能跟上。沒有人需要爭贏；重要的是分清哪些事現在能做、哪些事需要更多協助。校門規定仍然困難，卻不再讓人覺得無法處理。 「bank」（銀行）這個字讓小組多了一個可以討論的例子。 奈美把「farm」（農場）寫在板上，卻沒有把它變成考試。 他們在頁面末尾的短文練習裡找到「feed」（餵食）。 柚柚在「letter」（信）旁畫上小記號，幫助記憶。 奈美選了「many」（許多），準備耐心向初學者說明。

Yuzu asked whether that mistake meant every fox should be banned. Once everyone understood the choice, the next step was smaller than it had seemed. Yuzu spoke first and waited for an answer. Nami watched the other person's face instead of trying to hurry the talk. There was room to say no, to ask for an explanation, or to think for another minute. The group used the learning pass as a guide, not as an order that could never be changed. A small study card showed moon, which Yuzu copied beside today's notes. Nami asked how to use movie in a sentence about their day. In the English practice book, oil appeared on the next line. Yuzu read sell aloud, then checked the meaning with Nami. Another card carried slow and a simple drawing by a classmate.

柚柚問，一隻狐狸犯錯，是否代表所有狐狸都該被禁止。 當所有人都了解選擇後，下一步其實沒有想像中困難。柚柚先開口，然後等待回答。奈美注意對方的表情，沒有催促。大家可以說不、要求解釋，或多想一分鐘。小組把學習許可證當作指引，而不是絕不能改變的命令。 一張小小練習卡上寫著「moon」（月亮），柚柚把它抄在今日筆記旁。 奈美問起，如何用「movie」（電影）寫一句與今天有關的話。 英文練習本的下一行出現「oil」（油）。 柚柚朗讀「sell」（販賣），再和奈美確認意思。 另一張圖卡上有「slow」（緩慢地），旁邊是同學的簡筆畫。

The first idea sounded simple, and that was useful. Still, Yuzu wanted to know whether it respected everyone's choice. Nami suggested changing only one part rather than throwing the whole plan away. They drew two small boxes on a page: what we can do and what we must ask about. The list made the school gate rule easier to discuss without turning a friend into an enemy. The word straight gave the group a new example to discuss. Nami put strange on the board without turning it into a test. They found string in a short reading exercise near the end of the page. Yuzu drew a tiny sign beside teenager so it would be easier to remember. Nami chose think as a word to explain kindly to a new learner.

第一個想法聽起來很簡單，這是好事。但柚柚還想確認是否尊重每個人的選擇。奈美建議只修改其中一部分，不必推翻整個計畫。他們在紙上畫兩個方框：我們能做的事，以及必須先詢問的事。有了這份清單，討論校門規定便容易多了，也不會把朋友當成敵人。 「straight」（直接地）這個字讓小組多了一個可以討論的例子。 奈美把「strange」（奇怪）寫在板上，卻沒有把它變成考試。 他們在頁面末尾的短文練習裡找到「string」（線）。 柚柚在「teenager」（青少年）旁畫上小記號，幫助記憶。 奈美選了「think」（思考），準備耐心向初學者說明。

The principal agreed to review the rule with the school board. Before calling the task finished, Yuzu went back over the facts. One detail had changed, and another still needed an answer. Nami wrote both down rather than hiding the uncertain part. They could be pleased with progress without pretending that every worry had disappeared. By comparing the new result with the first note, they could see what had actually improved. The learning without hiding still mattered, but now the group knew one more way to protect it. A small study card showed through, which Yuzu copied beside today's notes. Nami asked how to use toe in a sentence about their day. In the English practice book, topic appeared on the next line. Yuzu read where aloud, then checked the meaning with Nami. Another card carried yellow and a simple drawing by a classmate.

校長同意和校務會議重新檢視規則。 在宣布完成前，柚柚重新檢查事實。有一個細節改變了，另一個仍有待解答。奈美把兩者都寫下，沒有隱藏不確定之處。大家可以為進展高興，卻不必假裝所有擔憂都消失。比對新結果與第一張筆記後，他們看出真正改善的部分。不必藏身的學習依然重要，現在小組又多學會一種守護它的方法。 一張小小練習卡上寫著「through」（經過；透過），柚柚把它抄在今日筆記旁。 奈美問起，如何用「toe」（腳趾）寫一句與今天有關的話。 英文練習本的下一行出現「topic」（主題）。 柚柚朗讀「where」（哪裡），再和奈美確認意思。 另一張圖卡上有「yellow」（黃色），旁邊是同學的簡筆畫。

Outside, the air was cooler than it had been at lunch. Yuzu looked back once and saw the human school in the evening light. It seemed like an ordinary place, but there was more to it now: a remembered question, a mistake corrected, and a choice made together. Nami waved from a few steps away. They still had their separate dreams and small worries, yet they also shared one clear memory. Whatever happened next, they could return to that memory without needing to pretend the day had been perfect.

外面的空氣比午餐時涼了些。柚柚回頭望了一眼晚光中的人類學校。它看似平凡，現在卻多了值得記住的問題、改正的錯誤，以及共同做出的選擇。奈美在幾步外揮手。他們仍有各自的夢想和小煩惱，也有一段共同的清晰回憶。無論接下來發生什麼，都不必假裝今天完美，就能想起它的價值。
