Nami saw a fluffy tail move behind Yuzu's desk. Nobody had planned for this moment. The room was full of ordinary sounds, yet Yuzu felt that something important had changed. Nami waited beside the door and let the first surprise settle. It was tempting to find an easy answer before anyone knew the whole story. Instead, they looked carefully at what was truly in front of them. Yuzu opened the learning pass and made a small note about the day.

The first idea sounded simple, and that was useful. Still, Yuzu wanted to know whether it respected everyone's choice. Nami suggested changing only one part rather than throwing the whole plan away. She asked him quietly whether he needed help instead of shouting. The plan became real only when someone took the first small action. It was not exciting in the way stories often promised, but it let everyone move forward with confidence. They found country in a short reading exercise near the end of the page. Yuzu drew a tiny sign beside forget so it would be easier to remember. Nami chose guess as a word to explain kindly to a new learner. A small study card showed here, which Yuzu copied beside today's notes. Nami asked how to use late in a sentence about their day.

她沒有大叫，而是小聲問他需不需要幫忙。 有人做出第一個小小行動時，計畫才真正開始。柚柚確認時間，奈美檢查共用材料，加藤老師確認哪些步驟需要大人同意。小組留下清楚紀錄，讓稍後到的人也能理解決定。他們還約好若事情出錯該怎麼辦。這不像故事裡常見的刺激冒險，卻讓每個人都更有信心往前走。 他們在頁面末尾的短文練習裡找到「country」（全國；國家）。 柚柚在「forget」（忘記）旁畫上小記號，幫助記憶。 奈美選了「guess」（猜測），準備耐心向初學者說明。 一張小小練習卡上寫著「here」（這裡），柚柚把它抄在今日筆記旁。 奈美問起，如何用「late」（晚；遲）寫一句與今天有關的話。

For a minute, the group spoke all at once. Yuzu raised a hand and suggested taking turns. Nami repeated the question in simpler words, so that even a quiet student could follow the talk. Nobody needed to win an argument. They needed to learn which part of the problem could be handled now and which part needed more help. 有一分鐘，大家幾乎同時開口。柚柚舉起手，提議輪流說話。奈美用更簡單的詞重述問題，讓安靜的學生也能跟上。沒有人需要爭贏；重要的是分清哪些事現在能做、哪些事需要更多協助。校門規定仍然困難，卻不再讓人覺得無法處理。 英文練習本的下一行出現「lunch」（午餐）。 柚柚朗讀「pay」（支付；付款），再和奈美確認意思。 奈美在班級故事頁的空白處畫了「pen」（筆）的圖。 「piece」（一張；一小片）這個字讓小組多了一個可以討論的例子。 奈美把「pond」（池塘）寫在板上，卻沒有把它變成考試。

Yuzu admitted he was a fox who loved reading human books. They gathered the papers and put the shared things back in their places. 柚柚承認自己是狐狸，卻很喜歡人類的書。 他們整理紙張，把共用物品放回原處。雖然桌子沒有移動，柚柚卻覺得房間已經不同。奈美指出小組寫下第一個擔憂的那一頁。那些字還在，卻已無法概括整件事。現在多了行動、結果，以及期待下次見面的理由。 他們在頁面末尾的短文練習裡找到「reporter」（記者）。 柚柚在「serious」（認真）旁畫上小記號，幫助記憶。 奈美選了「sofa」（沙發），準備耐心向初學者說明。 奈美在班級故事頁的空白處畫了「telephone」（電話）的圖。 奈美問起，如何用「third」（第三）寫一句與今天有關的話。

Near the end of the day, Yuzu stayed beside Nami for one more minute. They remembered how strange the morning had felt. A problem that once seemed to fill the whole room had become several smaller questions. Each question could be handled in turn. They did not know what the following day would bring, and that was all right. What they had learned was not a magic answer but a habit: notice a change, ask with care, and let people choose for themselves. Yuzu closed the learning pass and left with a lighter heart.

一天快結束時，柚柚在奈美身邊多待了一分鐘。他們想起早上的奇怪感受。原本像占滿整間房間的難題，現在分成幾個較小的問題，可以逐一處理。他們不知道明天會帶來什麼，但這沒有關係。今天學到的不是神奇答案，而是一種習慣：留意變化、仔細發問，也讓別人自己選擇。柚柚闔上學習許可證，帶著較輕鬆的心情離開。
