A squirrel from the forest arrived at the school fence looking for Yuzu. Nobody had planned for this moment. The room was full of ordinary sounds, yet Yuzu felt that something important had changed. Nami waited beside the door and let the first surprise settle. It was tempting to find an easy answer before anyone knew the whole story. Instead, they looked carefully at what was truly in front of them. Yuzu opened the learning pass and made a small note about the day.

森林裡的一隻松鼠來到校園圍欄找柚柚。 沒有人預料到這一刻。人類學校充滿平常的聲音，柚柚卻感覺有件重要的事改變了。奈美站在門旁，讓最初的驚訝慢慢沉澱。大家很容易在弄清事情之前就急著下結論，但他們決定先看清眼前真正發生的事。柚柚翻開學習許可證，記下今天的小小發現。

The first idea sounded simple, and that was useful. Still, Yuzu wanted to know whether it respected everyone's choice. Nami suggested changing only one part rather than throwing the whole plan away. They drew two small boxes on a page: what we can do and what we must ask about. The list made the school gate rule easier to discuss without turning a friend into an enemy. A small study card showed around, which Yuzu copied beside today's notes. Nami asked how to use button in a sentence about their day. In the English practice book, dark appeared on the next line. Yuzu read evening aloud, then checked the meaning with Nami. Another card carried fall and a simple drawing by a classmate.

第一個想法聽起來很簡單，這是好事。但柚柚還想確認是否尊重每個人的選擇。奈美建議只修改其中一部分，不必推翻整個計畫。他們在紙上畫兩個方框：我們能做的事，以及必須先詢問的事。有了這份清單，討論校門規定便容易多了，也不會把朋友當成敵人。 一張小小練習卡上寫著「around」（周圍），柚柚把它抄在今日筆記旁。 奈美問起，如何用「button」（按鈕）寫一句與今天有關的話。 英文練習本的下一行出現「dark」（昏暗的）。 柚柚朗讀「evening」（傍晚），再和奈美確認意思。 另一張圖卡上有「fall」（落下），旁邊是同學的簡筆畫。

Yuzu spoke with the animal outside the gate under adult supervision. The plan became real only when someone took the first small action. Yuzu checked the time, Nami checked the shared materials, and Ms. Kato confirmed which steps needed adult approval. The group left a clear record so that a person arriving later could understand the decision. They also agreed on what to do if something went wrong. It was not exciting in the way stories often promised, but it let everyone move forward with confidence. The word have gave the group a new example to discuss. Nami put interesting on the board without turning it into a test. They found left in a short reading exercise near the end of the page. Yuzu drew a tiny sign beside menu so it would be easier to remember. Nami drew a small picture of potato in the margin of the class story page.

柚柚在大人陪同下於校門外和松鼠說話。 有人做出第一個小小行動時，計畫才真正開始。柚柚確認時間，奈美檢查共用材料，加藤老師確認哪些步驟需要大人同意。小組留下清楚紀錄，讓稍後到的人也能理解決定。他們還約好若事情出錯該怎麼辦。這不像故事裡常見的刺激冒險，卻讓每個人都更有信心往前走。 「have」（有）這個字讓小組多了一個可以討論的例子。 奈美把「interesting」（有趣的）寫在板上，卻沒有把它變成考試。 他們在頁面末尾的短文練習裡找到「left」（左側）。 柚柚在「menu」（菜單）旁畫上小記號，幫助記憶。 奈美在班級故事頁的空白處畫了「potato」（馬鈴薯）的圖。

For a minute, the group spoke all at once. Yuzu raised a hand and suggested taking turns. Nami repeated the question in simpler words, so that even a quiet student could follow the talk. Nobody needed to win an argument. They needed to learn which part of the problem could be handled now and which part needed more help. The school gate rule remained difficult, but it no longer felt impossible. A small study card showed rose, which Yuzu copied beside today's notes. Nami asked how to use smile in a sentence about their day. In the English practice book, sound appeared on the next line. Yuzu read stay aloud, then checked the meaning with Nami. Another card carried student and a simple drawing by a classmate.

有一分鐘，大家幾乎同時開口。柚柚舉起手，提議輪流說話。奈美用更簡單的詞重述問題，讓安靜的學生也能跟上。沒有人需要爭贏；重要的是分清哪些事現在能做、哪些事需要更多協助。校門規定仍然困難，卻不再讓人覺得無法處理。 一張小小練習卡上寫著「rose」（玫瑰），柚柚把它抄在今日筆記旁。 奈美問起，如何用「smile」（微笑）寫一句與今天有關的話。 英文練習本的下一行出現「sound」（聲音）。 柚柚朗讀「stay」（留下），再和奈美確認意思。 另一張圖卡上有「student」（學生），旁邊是同學的簡筆畫。

The squirrel brought news that other young animals wanted to learn too. They gathered the papers and put the shared things back in their places. Yuzu could feel that the room had changed even though the desks had not moved. Nami pointed to the page where the group had written its first worry. The words were still there, but they no longer told the whole story. Now there was also an action, a result, and a reason to look forward to the next meeting. The word sweet gave the group a new example to discuss. Nami put trouble on the board without turning it into a test. They found usually in a short reading exercise near the end of the page. Yuzu drew a tiny sign beside watch so it would be easier to remember. Nami chose worry as a word to explain kindly to a new learner.

松鼠帶來消息：其他小動物也想上學。 他們整理紙張，把共用物品放回原處。雖然桌子沒有移動，柚柚卻覺得房間已經不同。奈美指出小組寫下第一個擔憂的那一頁。那些字還在，卻已無法概括整件事。現在多了行動、結果，以及期待下次見面的理由。 「sweet」（甜蜜的）這個字讓小組多了一個可以討論的例子。 奈美把「trouble」（困擾）寫在板上，卻沒有把它變成考試。 他們在頁面末尾的短文練習裡找到「usually」（通常）。 柚柚在「watch」（觀看）旁畫上小記號，幫助記憶。 奈美選了「worry」（擔心），準備耐心向初學者說明。

Near the end of the day, Yuzu stayed beside Nami for one more minute. They remembered how strange the morning had felt. A problem that once seemed to fill the whole room had become several smaller questions. Each question could be handled in turn. They did not know what the following day would bring, and that was all right. What they had learned was not a magic answer but a habit: notice a change, ask with care, and let people choose for themselves. Yuzu closed the learning pass and left with a lighter heart.

一天快結束時，柚柚在奈美身邊多待了一分鐘。他們想起早上的奇怪感受。原本像占滿整間房間的難題，現在分成幾個較小的問題，可以逐一處理。他們不知道明天會帶來什麼，但這沒有關係。今天學到的不是神奇答案，而是一種習慣：留意變化、仔細發問，也讓別人自己選擇。柚柚闔上學習許可證，帶著較輕鬆的心情離開。
