Teacher Jon gathered the children in the old library and asked each to draw a safe route to the cellar. Their maps contradicted one another because nobody had ever measured the side passages. Jon took the oldest students on a supervised walk, marking low beams and emergency lamps. They practiced moving quietly, guiding younger children, and counting every person before closing a door. Some adults laughed at lessons without weapons. Then a power test left the main stairwell dark, and the children followed their maps to the second entrance without panic. Jon did not praise them as heroes. He praised their careful questions and reminded them that nobody was required to be brave alone.
