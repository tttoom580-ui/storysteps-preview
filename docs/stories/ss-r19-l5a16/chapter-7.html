A crack appeared in the main water tank. The station could not repair it without emptying a quarter of the reserve. The residents gathered to decide priorities. Xia asked that drinking and medical water be protected first; Yan argued that crops also needed enough to survive until the next rainfall. A parent protested that no child should ever be told to drink less. They calculated actual needs on the board rather than trading accusations. The plan gave each household a basic supply, reduced cleaning consumption, and saved a separate amount for seeds and treatment. A damaged pipe was replaced with salvaged metal. Everyone disliked some part of the ration, which made the public accounting more important. No one could secretly take a larger share without leaving a visible record.

主水箱出現裂縫，若要修理，必須先放掉四分之一存水。居民集會討論順位。夏禾要求先保住飲水與醫療，燕拓提醒作物也需撐到下次降雨。家長抗議不能再讓孩子少喝水。大家把實際需求寫在黑板上計算，不再互相指責。方案保障每戶基本用水、減少清潔用量，另留種子與治療水額。廢金屬修好破管。每個人都不滿意其中一部分，因此公開帳目更重要：誰也不能私下多拿而不留下紀錄。
