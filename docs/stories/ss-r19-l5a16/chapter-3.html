Farmer Yan Tuo found enormous footprints between the orchard rows. Everyone assumed the creatures were approaching the station. Instead of calling for weapons, he placed two empty buckets beside a noisy irrigation pump and waited from a safe ridge. The tracks turned toward the vibration, not toward the planted food. At dusk smoke from a broken generator drew another creature away from its usual route. Yan presented both observations at the safety meeting, careful not to claim he knew every species. Xia approved a controlled trial of quieter pumps. The next night fewer tracks crossed the orchard. The station had gained one possible advantage: understanding the animals might keep people safer than frightening them with explosions.

農夫燕拓在果園行間找到巨大的腳印。大家以為怪物衝著避難站來，他卻先在吵鬧的灌溉泵旁放兩個空桶，躲在安全山脊觀察。足跡朝震動而去，不是朝作物。傍晚發電機冒煙，又把另一隻生物引離原路。燕拓在安全會議報告兩次觀察，沒有自稱了解所有怪物。夏禾同意試用安靜泵浦。隔夜果園的腳印變少。理解生物習性，或許比用爆炸嚇唬牠們更能保護人。
