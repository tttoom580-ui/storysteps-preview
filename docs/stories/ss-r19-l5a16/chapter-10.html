Five hundred residents gathered beneath the old ventilation fans to vote on a new safety charter. They had survived for years under the orders of one station master; now they wanted rules that could be questioned. The charter established open inventories, an emergency committee, and a process to remove officials who concealed dangers. It also permitted residents to leave after receiving reliable route information. The former commander argued that hesitation would kill them. Jon asked whether unquestioned orders had saved Shelter Six. Silence followed. The vote passed, but Xia warned that a document could not stop creatures at the gate. "No," Yan said, "but it can stop us from becoming each other's danger." At dusk the first alarm sounded under the new rules.
