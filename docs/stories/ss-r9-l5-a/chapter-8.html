塵土專利

Patent of Dust

沙海種子庫：最後一袋雨 · 第 8 / 25 章 · 4000–5000 · 英中對照 · 校訂完成

用互動版查詞、朗讀與收藏

That morning, the seed exchange opened beneath a painted market canopy.

那天早上，種子交換會在彩繪市集棚下開幕。

A corporation claimed a patent on a strain kept by four villages.

一家公司聲稱擁有四個村落保存品系的專利。

Nara treated every offer with caution. Her first chore was to obtain clarity about what counted as a commodity and what remained communal knowledge. A delegation of growers asked her to dedicate time to their stories, even when the company called their concerns dreadful. An ethical exploration of seed history required fluency in several village languages, not merely the vocabulary of lawyers.

娜拉謹慎看待每個提議。她首先要釐清什麼算商品，什麼仍是社群共有知識。一個農友代表團請她花時間聽故事，即使公司把他們的疑慮說得很糟。合乎倫理的種子歷史探索，需要熟悉數種村落語言，而不只是律師的專業用語。

Its lawyer offered to purchase every surviving packet.

公司律師提出收購所有存活種包。

Inside the market, a generator hummed beside a painted interior wall. The firm promised a patent premium that would let the cooperative shed its debt, but demanded sole control of breeding. Tasa tried to steer the talk toward consent. Some traders called the old rules superstition; a visiting undergraduate asked for a public venue where keepers could answer without losing their rights. Solar panels could generate power, but they could not generate consent.

市場裡，一台發電機在彩繪內牆旁嗡嗡作響。公司承諾提供專利溢價，讓合作社擺脫債務，卻要求獨占育種控制權。塔莎試圖把話題帶回同意權。有些商人把舊規矩叫迷信；一名來訪的大學生則要求公開場地，讓保管人能回答問題而不失去權利。 太陽能板可以發電，卻無法創造同意。

Nara asked to see the source records before discussing any price.

娜拉要求先查看來源紀錄，再談價格。

The lawyer spread a printed certificate across the market table. Four village names appeared beneath the company's name, as if the order of the words could settle who had created the strain. Tasa placed her cloth label beside the certificate. Its spelling differed from the printed version. The lawyer called that a minor variation. A second keeper said it identified the village where her grandmother had first obtained the seed.

律師把印好的證書攤在市集桌上。四個村名列在公司名稱下面，彷彿字的順序就能決定誰培育了品系。塔莎把布標放在證書旁，拼法與印刷版本不同。律師說這只是小差異。另一位保管人卻說，那個名字指出祖母最初取得種子的村子。

Nara asked for the records that connected those labels to the company's application. The lawyer produced a purchase receipt signed by a trader. The trader had sold a bag of grain; nothing on the page said he had authority to sell a community's breeding knowledge. That absence did not by itself decide the claim. It did mean that the price now being offered could not answer the question the keepers were asking. Rui wrote down the difference between buying a physical packet and claiming control over every future crop descended from it.

娜拉要求查看連結布標與公司申請的紀錄。律師拿出商人簽名的收據。商人賣了一袋穀物，紙上卻沒有說他有權販售社群的育種知識。這項缺漏本身不能判定權利歸屬，但代表眼前價格無法回答保管人的問題。芮記下，買一包實物，和主張控制其後代每一次收成，是兩件不同的事。

The company representative warned that samples might deteriorate while people argued. Nara agreed that they needed protection. She asked whether the company would permit the remaining packets to stay sealed under shared observation while the source records were checked. The lawyer consulted his papers, then accepted a short preservation arrangement without withdrawing the claim. Tasa did not shake his hand for the camera. Instead, she asked the other keeper to help count the packets before the table was cleared. Their two cloth labels lay beside the new seals, neither translated into a company name. People who wanted an immediate victory left disappointed. The keepers left with their seeds still present, their names still readable, and time to find the documents a hurried sale would have made easier to ignore.

公司代表警告，爭論期間樣本可能變質。娜拉同意必須保護它們，便問公司是否願意在查核來源紀錄時，讓剩餘種包保持封存，由雙方共同觀察。律師查閱文件後，接受短期保全，卻沒有撤回主張。塔莎沒有為相機握手，反而請另一位保管人一起在清桌前數清種包。兩人的布標放在新封記旁，沒有被改成公司名字。想立即看見勝利的人失望離開；保管人帶著仍在眼前的種子、仍能閱讀的名字，以及尋找文件的時間離開。倉促出售原本可能讓這些文件更容易被忽略。

Two keepers exchanged cloth labels bearing the same plant's different village names. Neither wanted to choose a single name; both wanted future readers to know why there were two.

兩名保管人交換布標，標上同一植物在不同村落的名稱。她們都不想只選一個名字，而希望後人理解為何有兩個。

Evening brought one measurable gain: the company agreed to a short preservation order, not to surrender its claim.

傍晚帶來一項可量測的進展：公司同意短期保全命令，但沒有放棄主張。
