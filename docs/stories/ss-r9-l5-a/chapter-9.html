夜市地圖

The Night Market Map

沙海種子庫：最後一袋雨 · 第 9 / 25 章 · 4000–5000 · 英中對照 · 校訂完成

用互動版查詞、朗讀與收藏

After sunset, lamps lit a night bazaar built around a roofless observatory.

日落之後，燈火點亮建於露天觀星臺周圍的夜市。

An elderly vendor showed a map of wells absent from the new atlas.

一名老攤販拿出新版地圖上不存在的水井圖。

A woman in the lounge asked for help with her citizenship papers; the new compulsory registry had left her status unclear. Nearby, a vendor reported an assault during a failed bid for a stall. Nara would not let either story distract from the well search, but she refused to dismiss the harm. An ally from the cemetery district offered an old expedition chart marked with a mint-green symbol.

一名坐在休息室的女子請人協助辦理公民身分文件；新的強制登記使她的身分不明。附近攤販則報告有人在攤位競標失敗後遭到襲擊。娜拉不讓任何一件事分散找井的注意力，卻也拒絕忽視傷害。來自墓園區的盟友拿出一張舊探險圖，上面畫著薄荷綠記號。

A rumor linked the wells to a violent raid twenty years before.

傳言把那些水井與二十年前的一場暴力突襲連在一起。

The map's exterior cover showed a galaxy, though the manifest inside listed ordinary goods: dried tuna, rope and tools. The premier had once inspected the route from the rear of a convoy. A thief tried to snatch the chart, then had to squat behind a foul-smelling stall; the stink exposed his hiding place. Nara chose not to embrace the rumor that the premier had stolen water without a single verified measure.

地圖外封面畫著星河，裡面的貨物清單卻只是日常用品：乾燥鮪魚、繩索與工具。首相曾坐在車隊後方巡視這條路。一名小偷試圖搶走地圖，後來蹲藏在臭味濃烈的攤位後；惡臭暴露了他的藏身處。娜拉決定不接受首相偷水的傳言，因為仍沒有任何驗證過的測量。

After the arguments had been heard, Nara checked landmarks on foot instead of repeating the rumor.

各方意見都說完後，娜拉徒步核對地標，而不是轉述傳言。

The vendor held his map beneath a lamp until the old lines showed through the newer ink. A road that ended at the market continued on his sheet toward the sealed wells. Rui asked who had drawn the earlier boundary. He could name the surveyor, but she had died years before. Nara copied the landmarks rather than treating a dead person's reputation as a substitute for checking the ground.

攤販把地圖舉到燈下，讓舊線條透過較新的墨色顯現。新版道路止於市集，他的圖卻繼續通往封井。芮問舊界線是誰畫的，他說得出測量員姓名，但她多年前已過世。娜拉抄下地標，沒有用亡者聲望取代實地確認。

They went first to a broken stair marked near the observatory. Its lowest steps had vanished beneath sand, and a newer wall blocked the route shown on the map. Ilyas measured the corner from a surviving stone marker. The vendor watched from the street while they compared the distance. An old doorway appeared where the drawing predicted, although its wooden frame had been removed. That gave them a second point from which to look for the wells without entering houses or digging through private gardens.

他們先到觀星臺旁的斷階。最下面幾階被沙埋住，新牆則擋住圖上的路。伊利從尚存的石界標量起，攤販在街邊看他們比對距離。舊門口出現在地圖預示的位置，只是木框已拆。這給了他們第二個定位點，不必闖進住宅或挖私人花園，也能尋找水井。

At the first cover, the vendor dropped a small stone through a gap. There was no clear splash. He looked embarrassed and suggested that the map had wasted their evening. Nara asked him to stop before trying a larger stone. A quiet shaft could be dry, blocked, or simply deeper than they could judge by listening. Opening it without equipment would answer none of those questions safely. They marked both covers and recorded who controlled access to the courtyards. Rui left the old raid out of her account because nobody there could connect it to the water below. When the vendor folded his map, Nara asked permission to bring a copy to the water workers. His hand stayed on the paper for a moment. Then he pointed out a path that was wide enough for their equipment, but too narrow for the company's carts.

在第一個井蓋旁，攤販從縫隙投入小石子，沒有聽見清楚水聲。他尷尬地說，也許地圖浪費了大家一個晚上。娜拉請他別再試更大的石頭。無聲井道可能乾涸、堵住，也可能深得無法靠聽覺判斷。沒有設備就打開，無法安全回答任何一個問題。他們標出兩個井蓋，記下誰管理庭院入口。芮沒有把舊突襲寫進報導，因為現場無人能把它與井水連結。攤販摺圖時，娜拉請求帶副本給水務工人。他的手在紙上停了片刻，接著指出一條足夠讓設備通過、卻容不下公司大車的小路。

At sunset, the team could finally state a limited outcome: two covered wells were found, though their water was untested.

日落時，團隊終於能說出有限成果：眾人找到兩口封住的井，但還未測試水質。
