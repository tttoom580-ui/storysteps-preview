不見的簽名

The Missing Signatures

沙海種子庫：最後一袋雨 · 第 12 / 25 章 · 4000–5000 · 英中對照 · 校訂中

用互動版查詞、朗讀與收藏

By the time the sun reached the eastern ridge, Tasa led Nara to a shaded room below the old archive.

太陽升到東方山脊時，塔莎帶娜拉進入舊檔案庫底下的陰涼房間。

The seed-sharing page lacked signatures that the village book remembered.

種子共享頁面缺少村簿記載過的簽名。

A strange mark in the old register seemed abnormal. Nara asked Tasa to acquaint her with the village names, while Ilyas checked the bolt on its cabinet. The date was compatible with an earlier transfer but not with the official story. Nara decided to confront the discrepancy before the continental seed board. No one would be held hostage by an accusation, and no one could kidnap a keeper's testimony for private use.

舊登記簿上一道奇怪記號似乎不正常。娜拉請塔莎介紹村落姓名，伊利則檢查櫃子的螺栓。日期與更早的移轉相符，卻與官方說法不合。娜拉決定在大陸種子委員會前直面矛盾。沒有人應因指控而成為人質，也沒有人可以劫走保管人的證詞供私人使用。

A government agent called the missing names a clerical mistake.

政府代表把失蹤名字稱為文書疏失。

A missionary had once copied the original script. Rui could only mumble its unfamiliar dialect, so Tasa asked a village reader to help. The village norm required a second observer who could read it aloud. As a viewer of these records, Nara felt a sensation of shame: an old contract mentioned slavery and called an entire stock of seeds a gift. Tasa rejected the term traitor for any elder who had signed under pressure. The village's memory remained viable even when the paper was not. Tasa called the missing names a form of discrimination, not a harmless omission.

一名傳教士曾抄下原始文書。芮只能含糊念出不熟悉的方言，塔莎便請一名村中讀者協助。村落規範要求第二名能朗讀的觀察者。娜拉看著這些紀錄，感到一陣羞愧：舊合約提到奴隸制度，還把整批種子當成禮物。塔莎拒絕把任何在壓力下簽字的長者稱為叛徒。即使紙張腐朽，村落記憶仍然有生命力。 塔莎說，失蹤的名字是一種歧視，而不是無傷大雅的疏漏。

After the arguments had been heard, Nara copied both registers without declaring either one genuine.

各方意見都說完後，娜拉抄下兩份紀錄，沒有先宣稱哪份是真的。

Tasa knew where her sister's name should have stood because she remembered the table on which the village book had been signed. One leg was shorter than the others. Her sister had steadied it with a folded scrap while the witnesses wrote. The government representative smiled politely and called that a touching memory. Nara asked him to wait. A memory could not replace a signature, but it could help them find the people and objects connected with it.

塔莎知道姊妹名字應在何處，因為她記得簽村簿的那張桌子，有一條腿比較短。當時姊妹用摺紙墊穩，見證人才落筆。政府代表禮貌微笑，稱這是動人回憶。娜拉請他等等。記憶不能代替簽名，卻能協助尋找相關的人與物。

The archivist brought the village volume from a separate shelf. Its page recorded witnesses beside the seed transfer; the government copy listed only a shipment. Ilyas placed weights on the corners so the fragile paper would remain flat. Rui photographed the whole page before taking details. Tasa did not want her sister's address printed. They made a working copy with that line covered, preserving the full original for the people authorized to inspect it.

檔案員從另一架拿來村簿。村簿在種子移轉旁列出見證人，政府副本卻只列運送。伊利用鎮紙壓平脆弱紙角。芮先拍全頁，再拍細節。塔莎不希望姊妹住址刊出，他們便遮住那一行製作工作副本，完整原件仍留供獲准者查閱。

The representative suggested filling the blank from the village volume. That would make the two copies agree, he said. Nara refused to write a new signature where an original was missing. First they needed to establish which document had been copied from which, and what permission each was meant to record. The archivist checked the shelf register for a witness book removed during the drought. It named a receiving office, but not a return date. Tasa recognized the office's old location. She had passed it on the way to visit her sister. When they left the cool room, she carried no restored signature and no promise that every record would be recovered. She carried the address of the next place to look, written on paper she could read without asking a government official to explain it.

代表提議按村簿補上空白，說這樣兩份就一致了。娜拉拒絕在原簽名缺失處新添簽名。必須先釐清哪份抄自哪份、各自記錄何種許可。檔案員查找乾旱期間移出的見證簿，架位登記列了收件辦公室，卻沒有歸還日期。塔莎認得那個辦公室舊址，從前探望姊妹會經過。離開陰涼房間時，她沒有帶回簽名，也沒有得到所有紀錄都能找回的保證。她帶著下一個尋找地點的地址，寫在自己能閱讀、不必求官員解釋的紙上。

In the archive's cool shadow, Tasa touched the place where her sister's name should have been. She did not cry; she asked Nara to leave enough space to write it correctly.

檔案室的涼蔭裡，塔莎碰了碰本應寫著姊妹名字的地方。她沒有哭，只請娜拉留足空間，好把名字寫對。

At sunset, the team could finally state a limited outcome: the original transfer required an independent witness to be valid.

日落時，團隊終於能說出有限成果：原始移轉必須有獨立見證人才有效。
