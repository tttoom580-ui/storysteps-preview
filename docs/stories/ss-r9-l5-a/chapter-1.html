The Number Under the Wax

沙海種子庫：最後一袋雨 · 第 1 / 25 章 · 4000–5000 · 英中對照

用互動版查詞、朗讀與收藏

The moving seed vault stopped outside Sunreach at dawn.

移動種子庫在黎明停靠日衡城外。

A crate marked four hundred contained only twenty-eight living seeds.

一只標示四百粒的箱子只剩二十八粒活種子。

The first batch had passed through a warming circuit, yet the seeds stayed hard. Ilyas called the intact seals a decent sign; Nara said heat could dissolve wax without changing what lay beneath it. Her mentor suggested an inquiry rather than treating the clerk as an idiot. The nonprofit vault would implement a fresh inventory, because a numerical breakthrough meant nothing until another keeper could reproduce it.

第一批種子經過加溫電路，卻仍然堅硬。伊利認為封記完整是個還算不錯的跡象；娜拉說熱能溶掉蠟，卻不一定改變蠟下面的東西。她的導師建議展開調查，而不是把書記當笨蛋。這座非營利種子庫會重新清點，因為數字上的突破若不能由其他保管人重現，便毫無意義。

The ministry wanted the inventory corrected without an inspection.

部門要求不經檢查便修正庫存。

The placement of the crate beneath the projector gave the wall a false projection of abundance. Regardless of the water regime, Nara would reinforce its cracked rim before opening it again. A rider had left a saddle nearby; carrying too many samples had put strain on the animal. The idea that future losses might triple could terrify growers, so she refused to turn uncertainty into a prediction.

箱子放在投影機下方，讓牆上出現種子充足的錯誤投影。無論供水制度怎樣，娜拉都會先補強箱子的裂邊再打開。一名騎手把馬鞍留在附近；搬運過多樣本讓馬匹負擔沉重。未來損失可能增加三倍的想法足以嚇壞農友，因此她拒絕把不確定性說成預測。

Instead of offering a broad promise, Nara copied the seals and separated broken packets from intact ones.

娜拉抄下封記，將破損紙包與完整紙包分開。

The clerk reached for a clean inventory sheet. Nara covered it with her hand. A replacement sheet would make the difference disappear before anyone had explained it. She asked him to keep the original on the table and bring the receiving receipt. Its total was four hundred too, but the handwriting changed halfway through the line. That was worth recording, not enough to accuse its writer.

書記伸手拿一張乾淨庫存表，娜拉用手蓋住。若換掉表格，差異還沒得到解釋就會消失。她請他把原件留在桌上，拿來收貨單。單上的總數也是四百，但同一行的筆跡中途變了。這值得記錄，卻不足以指控寫字的人。

Ilyas fetched a shallow counting tray. Together they divided the contents into groups, leaving the damaged packets in their own corner. When a gust lifted the receipt, the clerk caught it against his chest so quickly that Nara mistook his movement for an attempt to hide it. He looked hurt. She moved the paper beneath a glass weight and asked him to finish the count with them. His second total agreed with hers. Whatever had happened before their arrival, he had helped establish what was present now.

伊利取來淺盤，三人把種子分組清點，破損紙包另放一角。風掀起收據時，書記飛快把它按在胸前，娜拉一度以為他要藏起文件。他露出受傷的神情。她拿玻璃鎮紙壓好收據，請他一起完成清點。他的第二次總數與她相符。無論他們抵達前發生什麼，他至少協助確認了眼前的內容。

Rui wanted to know whether she could report that hundreds of seeds had been stolen. Nara pointed to the empty spaces on the tray. They showed an unexplained difference; they did not show a thief. Some seeds might have died, some packets might have been moved, and some numbers might never have described this crate. Rui crossed out one word, then asked for a photograph of the receiving receipt instead. Nara agreed after the clerk covered his personal address. Before the box was closed again, all three checked its new seal. The clerk kept a copy of the count. Nara took another, along with a drawing of the old wax. For the first time that morning, the two sheets disagreed openly rather than pretending to be one reliable account. The clerk asked to sign beside his own count, rather than beneath the unexplained old total.

芮問能不能報導數百粒種子遭竊。娜拉指著盤中的空位：它們顯示尚未解釋的差異，沒有指出小偷。種子可能死去，紙包可能移走，也可能原本的數字根本不是這只箱子的。芮劃掉一個詞，改問能否拍攝收貨單。書記遮住私人住址後，娜拉同意了。重新關箱前，三人都檢查新封記。書記留一份清點副本，娜拉帶走另一份及舊蠟封圖。那天早上，兩份文件終於公開承認彼此不同，而非假裝是一份可信的紀錄。 書記要求只在自己清點的數字旁簽名，不在未解的舊總數下簽。

The clerk found one gray fingerprint beneath the broken wax. Nara placed it in the margin of her sketch, where neither the ministry nor her own imagination could smooth it away.

書記在破裂封蠟下找到一道灰色指紋。娜拉把它畫在草圖邊緣，讓部門與她自己的想像都無法隨意抹平。

By the time the shadows lengthened, the missing amount became a question no one could erase.

影子拉長時，消失的數量成了無法隨意抹去的問題。
