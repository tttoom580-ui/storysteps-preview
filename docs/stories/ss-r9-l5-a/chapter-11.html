一杯水

One Cup of Water

沙海種子庫：最後一袋雨 · 第 11 / 25 章 · 4000–5000 · 英中對照 · 校訂中

用互動版查詞、朗讀與收藏

That morning, a remote nursery counted its remaining cups of fresh water.

那天早上，偏遠育苗站一杯杯計算剩餘的淡水。

A newly planted tray would need more water than the schedule allowed.

新種的培養盤需要的水超過供水表允許的量。

The cold-night trial might accelerate germination, but even a compliment from the minister could not replace a tested component. Ilyas planned to deploy a minimal crew as fatigue spread through the nursery. Its grim walls belonged to an old institution that had once claimed to be neutral. Nara prepared a petition for independent supervision, then watched a seed leaf quiver in the lamp light.

夜間低溫試驗或許能加快發芽，但部長的稱讚無法取代測試過的零件。疲勞在育苗站蔓延，伊利計畫只派最少人力。陰暗牆壁屬於一個曾宣稱中立的老機構。娜拉準備請願書，要求獨立監督，再看著一片種葉在燈下微微顫動。

The ministry urged an accelerated launch to calm investors.

部門催促加快啟動，藉此安撫投資者。

The realm of plant science was full of recommendation and doubt. A veteran grower called the new soil mixture his specialty, but refused to stake all the surviving seed on one tray. A specimen could thrive or fail for reasons that seemed trivial. Everyone would undergo the same training before taking samples, so that a beautiful result would not hide a mistake in procedure.

植物科學領域充滿建議與疑問。一名資深農友說新的土壤配方是自己的專長，卻拒絕把所有倖存種子押在一盤上。一個樣本可能因看似微不足道的原因長好或失敗。所有人在採樣前都必須接受相同訓練，以免漂亮結果掩蓋程序錯誤。

Nara limited the next trial to twelve cells and requested public counters.

娜拉把下一次試驗限縮為十二格，並要求公開計量。

The nursery assistant had divided the watering schedule into neat rows, but none of them showed the hours when nearby households filled their containers. Nara asked the girl with the tea kettle to bring her mother to the gate. They arrived carrying the empty vessel between them. The mother did not object to the seedlings. She wanted to know why the nursery could announce a new trial before anyone had explained its share of the well.

育苗站助理把澆水表分成整齊行列，卻沒有一行顯示鄰居何時裝家用水。娜拉請拿茶壺的女孩帶母親到門口。母女抬著空壺前來。母親並非反對幼苗，而是想知道，為什麼還沒說明育苗站能分多少井水，就能宣布新試驗。

Ilyas set a meter at the nursery inlet and invited her to watch the reading. The twelve cells would receive the measured amount already allowed for testing. Enlarging the trial would require another discussion, not a quiet adjustment while the neighbors slept. The assistant worried that a smaller trial would make the report look weak. Nara asked her to write the question the twelve cells could actually answer. After two attempts, she described a comparison between soils rather than a promise to supply the city with grain.

伊利在入水口裝錶，邀她看讀數。十二格使用既有測試配額內的水，擴大試驗就得再討論，不能趁鄰居睡覺偷偷調整。助理擔心試驗太小，報告不好看。娜拉請她寫下十二格實際能回答的問題。修改兩次後，她寫的是土壤比較，而非承諾替全城供應穀物。

The ministry's visitor looked disappointed when he saw the empty spaces on the bench. He offered to send more trays without charge. Nara thanked him and asked who would supply their water. He had no answer. The assistant moved the unused trays into storage herself, leaving the meter where visitors could see it. At the gate, the girl tipped her kettle to show how little it held. Nara did not fill it from the trial allowance and pretend the dispute was solved. She walked with the family to the public tap, where the mother checked the day's household supply. The nursery could continue its limited work without taking that water. Whether twelve cells would be useful enough remained a question for the results, not for the empty spaces a visitor wished to fill.

部門訪客看見桌上的空位，露出失望，願意免費送更多培養盤。娜拉謝過，並問誰供應用水，他無法回答。助理自己把未用的盤收進庫房，把水錶留在訪客看得到的位置。門口，女孩傾斜茶壺，讓她看見壺有多小。娜拉沒有從試驗配額裝水，假裝爭議已解決，而是陪母女走到公共水龍頭，讓母親確認當天家用水。育苗站可以在不取用那份水的情況下繼續有限工作。十二格究竟夠不夠有用，必須看結果，而不是看訪客想填滿的空位。

A girl asked whether the nursery's single cup of water would be taken from her family's kettle. Nara showed the separate meter before asking the girl to believe any promise.

一名女孩問育苗站的一杯水會不會從她家的茶壺扣除。娜拉先展示獨立水錶，才請女孩考慮相信承諾。

Evening brought one measurable gain: the nursery conserved enough water for its neighbors.

傍晚帶來一項可量測的進展：育苗站因此保住了鄰居所需的水。
