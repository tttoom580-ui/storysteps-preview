三只陶盆

Three Pots

沙海種子庫：最後一袋雨 · 第 3 / 25 章 · 4000–5000 · 英中對照

用互動版查詞、朗讀與收藏

By the time the sun reached the eastern ridge, the old botanical school opened its courtyard to villagers.

太陽升到東方山脊時，舊植物學校把庭院開放給居民。

Two of three planted pots showed a pale root after a measured watering.

三盆種植實驗中有兩盆在定量澆水後長出淡色幼根。

The school's executive arrived with an attorney, expecting aggression from farmers protecting their beloved seed. Nara remained cautious. The biological difference between the pots was small, and one weak root might diminish by morning. She knew their argument was bound by the scope of the experiment: neither a fresh layer of soil nor a single promising shoot could establish a harvest.

學校主管帶著律師來到現場，預料保護心愛種子的農民會表現出攻擊性。娜拉仍保持謹慎。三盆之間的生物差異很小，一條虛弱的幼根明早也可能衰退。她知道論點受限於實驗範圍：無論新加一層土，還是出現一根有希望的幼芽，都不能證明必然有收成。

A council officer called the result a commercial miracle.

一名議會官員將結果稱為商業奇蹟。

A beetle became prey for a small bird outside the glasshouse, an ordinary scene of realism amid political speeches. Ilyas gave a shrug and used a shuttle to bring more water. He did not specialize in seeds, but he knew how to straighten a bent pipe and fasten its strap. Nara heard the timer tick; she recorded every viewpoint on a virtual board without letting the brightest screen replace the actual pots.

溫室外，一隻甲蟲成了小鳥的獵物；在政治演說之間，這是再真實不過的景象。伊利聳聳肩，用接駁車運來更多水。他不專精種子，卻知道如何拉直彎管、固定束帶。娜拉聽到計時器滴答作響；她把各種觀點記在虛擬看板上，卻不讓最亮的螢幕取代真正的陶盆。

After the arguments had been heard, Nara posted the water amounts and labeled the failed pot as prominently as the others.

各方意見都說完後，娜拉公布用水量，也同樣清楚地標示失敗的盆栽。

The officer asked Rui to stand where the empty pot would not appear in her photograph. Rui lowered the camera. Nara moved the three pots closer together, keeping their labels visible. If one was removed from the picture, readers would have no way to know that the same watering had produced different results. The officer said an encouraging image might secure money for another trial. Tasa asked what would happen when the next group expected every pot to grow.

官員請芮換個位置，別讓空盆進入照片。芮放下相機，娜拉則把三只盆移近，讓標籤都看得見。若照片少了一盆，讀者就無從知道同樣澆水得到不同結果。官員說，振奮人心的照片也許能爭取下一次試驗經費。塔莎問，下批參與者若期待每盆都發芽，該怎麼辦？

Ilyas brought the watering cup and set it beside the failed pot. A line scratched inside it marked the amount used that morning. The school gardener remembered that one pot had stood nearer the hot wall before the test began. Nara added that detail to its label, then asked whether the others had also been moved. Nobody could remember with confidence. The gardener offered to repeat the entire trial immediately. There were not enough prepared seeds for that, and Nara would not spend the reserve merely to obtain a tidier photograph.

伊利拿來量水杯，放在失敗的盆旁。杯內刻線標明早上用量。學校園丁想起，測試開始前，有一盆曾比較靠近熱牆。娜拉把這點補在標籤上，接著詢問其他盆是否也移動過。沒有人能確定。園丁願意立刻重做，但準備好的種子不足，娜拉不願只為取得好看的照片就消耗備用種子。

They placed the pots where the temperature could be compared and marked their positions on the ground. The gardener took responsibility for keeping them there until the next observation. Rui photographed his marked plan as well as the two roots. When the child approached with a cup, he stopped at the boundary instead of pouring extra water into the empty pot. Nara thanked him for asking first. More water might help, or it might change the only comparison they had. The child left his drawing beside the labels, giving the empty square as much space as the two living roots. The officer finally stepped aside. His proposed headline remained unwritten, while the gardener stayed to check the shade moving across the wall.

他們把盆放在可以比較溫度的位置，並在地面畫出標記。園丁負責在下次觀察前維持位置。芮除了兩條根，也拍下他的配置圖。孩子拿杯子走近，卻停在界線外，沒有直接替空盆加水。娜拉謝謝他先問。多澆水可能有幫助，也可能改變唯一的比較條件。孩子把畫放在標籤旁，讓空格與兩條活根占有一樣大的位置。官員終於讓開；他想要的標題仍未寫出，園丁則留下查看牆上的陰影如何移動。

At sunset, the team could finally state a limited outcome: the village gained a repeatable observation rather than a promise of harvest.

日落時，團隊終於能說出有限成果：村民得到的是可重複的觀察，而不是收成保證。
