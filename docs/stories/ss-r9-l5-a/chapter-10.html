A Test Under Witnesses

Three laboratories brought their equipment to a shared greenhouse.

三間實驗室把設備帶到共同溫室。

Identical seeds responded differently to salt and soil organisms.

相同種子對鹽分與土壤生物產生不同反應。

A clinic director warned that public talk about abortion could betray anonymous patients. A bronze sign outside the dome offered privacy, but an arrogant merchant tried to enter with a camera. Nara told him that credibility depended on respecting people, including the local butcher and every entrepreneur who paid to keep the clinic open. Their medical records had no place in the seed test.

診所主任提醒，公開談論墮胎可能洩露匿名病人的身分。圓頂屋外的青銅招牌承諾保護隱私，一名傲慢商人卻拿著相機想闖入。娜拉告訴他，可信度建立在尊重每個人之上，包括當地肉商與出資維持診所的創業者。他們的醫療紀錄不應出現在種子實驗裡。

The minister wanted one perfect headline before the council vote.

部長希望在議會表決前有個完美頭條。

The laboratories compared the seeds' genetics, while a technician explained how a test could identify a particular molecule in the soil. An initiative to integrate their data was more reliable than a ridiculous claim that one sector alone could sustain the whole basin. A researcher agreed to testify about a vertical growing chamber and the insects trapped in it. Outside, wildlife moved along a fence, indifferent to speeches but dependent on the water the scientists discussed. One family risked losing its home when its mortgage payment came due.

實驗室比較種子的遺傳特性，一名技術員則解釋測試如何辨認土壤中特定的分子。整合資料的倡議，比荒唐地聲稱單一產業就能支撐整個盆地更可靠。一名研究者同意作證，說明垂直栽培艙與困在其中的昆蟲。外頭的野生動物沿柵欄移動，不在乎演說，卻依賴科學家正在討論的水。 有個家庭在房貸到期時面臨失去住處的風險。

Instead of offering a broad promise, Nara published the failed trays and the measurement uncertainty.

娜拉公布失敗的培養盤與測量不確定性。

Each laboratory had brought its own preferred tray. One held water longer, one drained quickly, and the third had labels pasted over old marks. Before comparing results, Ilyas asked the technicians to explain those differences. A visitor complained that they had already waited long enough for an answer. Nara moved a chair toward the worktable and invited him to watch the preparations rather than wait for a speech.

三間實驗室各帶慣用培養盤，一種保水較久，一種排水快，第三種的新標籤蓋住舊字。比較結果前，伊利請技術員說明差異。一名訪客抱怨大家為答案等得夠久了。娜拉把椅子移到工作桌旁，邀他看準備過程，不必坐等演說。

The technicians agreed to record the starting conditions beside every tray. That did not make the equipment identical, but it made the differences visible. Rui read out one label incorrectly, confusing the amount of salt with the number of seeds. A technician corrected her before the figure entered her notes. At the far end of the table, the visitor began checking the labels himself. He found a tray whose watering time had not been written down. Its promising roots could remain on display, but they could not fairly be compared with the others as though nothing were missing.

技術員同意在每盤旁記下起始條件。設備並未因此相同，但差異變得看得見。芮一度把鹽量念成種子數，一名技術員在數字寫進筆記前更正她。桌子另一端，訪客開始自行檢查標籤，找到一盤未寫澆水時間。它有希望的根仍可展示，卻不能假裝資料完整，與其他盤公平比較。

The ministry's messenger returned to ask which tray should appear behind the minister during his address. Nara pointed to the full row, including the failures. He said the wall was too narrow. She suggested moving the address somewhere else. After he left, Ilyas examined the failed tray again, turning its label toward the light. A brown patch near one corner might explain something, or might be an unrelated mark. He asked the technician to keep it rather than wash it away. The visitor who had wanted a quick answer stayed to help carry the trays indoors. He now knew enough to ask why the laboratories disagreed. That was a harder question for the demonstration to answer, but it belonged to the results they actually had. The technician marked the unrecorded watering time as unknown, not zero. An empty field and a measured absence were different observations. The visitor copied that distinction onto his own page before helping with the trays.

部門信使回來問，部長致詞時該在身後放哪一盤。娜拉指向整排，包括失敗的。他說牆不夠寬，她建議換個地方致詞。信使走後，伊利再查看失敗培養盤，把標籤轉向光線。角落褐斑可能提供解釋，也可能毫不相關，他請技術員保留，不要洗掉。原本想要快速答案的訪客留下幫忙把盤搬進室內。如今他已能問，為何三間實驗室意見不同。這是示範更難回答的問題，卻屬於他們真正取得的結果。 技術員把未記錄的澆水時間標成未知，而非零。資料空缺與量到沒有發生，是不同觀察。訪客搬盤前，也在自己的紙上記下這個區別。

By the time the shadows lengthened, the experiment proved limited germination, not a drought-proof harvest.

影子拉長時，實驗只證明在有限條件下能發芽，並非保證耐旱收成。
