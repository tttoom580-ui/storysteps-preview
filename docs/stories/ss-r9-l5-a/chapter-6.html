邊境的水

Water at the Border

沙海種子庫：最後一袋雨 · 第 6 / 25 章 · 4000–5000 · 英中對照 · 校訂中

用互動版查詞、朗讀與收藏

By the time the sun reached the eastern ridge, the caravan reached a frontier where wells stood behind locked fences.

太陽升到東方山脊時，車隊抵達一處水井全被柵欄封鎖的邊境。

Families were buying drinking water with irrigation credits.

家庭正以灌溉點數購買飲水。

An abrupt order reached the frontier through a messenger with a briefcase. A local activist asked Nara to contemplate the cost of refusing it. The water company called itself a separate entity, while its hostile guards treated a family in exile as trespassers. Farther down the road, an old injection clinic was closed, and the last medical worker feared the loss of emergency power.

一名攜帶公事包的信使把突然下達的命令送到邊境。地方行動者請娜拉仔細想想拒絕命令的代價。供水公司自稱是獨立法人，敵意十足的守衛卻把流亡家庭當成闖入者。道路另一端的舊注射診所已關閉，最後一位醫護人員害怕緊急電力也會中斷。

The prefect threatened to arrest anyone who changed the schedule.

地方長官威脅逮捕任何更動供水表的人。

The prefect praised masculine courage and called the villagers naive. Tasa replied that courage did not cancel an obligation to protect children. A shop's only outlet faced the pedestrian path, and its owner promised to repay anyone hurt by the water delay. Nara proposed a public seminar, where two caretakers could supervise readings and measure the surplus rather than letting a single official claim it. The new order threatened to disrupt the clinic's supply route. A courier held a cold cloth against his injured thigh.

地方長官讚美陽剛的勇氣，並說村民太天真。塔莎回答，勇氣不能免除保護孩子的責任。一家店唯一的出口面向行人道，店主承諾補償因延後供水而受傷害的人。娜拉提議舉辦公開說明會，由兩名保管員監督讀數並測量剩餘水量，而不是任由一名官員宣稱掌握一切。 新命令可能中斷診所的補給路線。 一名信使把冷布壓在受傷的大腿上。

After the arguments had been heard, Nara asked an activist and a well keeper to witness the meter together.

各方意見都說完後，娜拉請一名行動者與井管員共同見證水錶。

The well keeper would not open the meter box while the activist stood beside him. He said the last inspection had ended with stones thrown at his office. The activist answered that the office had closed its shutters while families waited outside. Nara asked each to choose one person who could stand close enough to read the dial. Neither liked the other's choice, but both knew the two chosen witnesses.

行動者站在旁邊時，井管員不肯打開水錶箱。他說上次檢查最後有人向辦公室丟石頭。行動者回答，當時家庭在外等候，辦公室卻關上窗。娜拉請雙方各選一人，靠近讀錶。他們都不喜歡對方的人選，但都認識被選出的兩位見證人。

Ilyas checked that the dial was turning before they wrote anything down. A stopped meter could produce a perfectly neat account of water that had never been counted. One witness called out the figures; the other repeated them while the keeper entered the time. They changed places for the second reading. The figures matched, yet the increase was smaller than the amount charged to the households waiting at the fence. The keeper pointed to a separate pipe leading toward the clinic. Nobody had recorded whether its flow was included in the same total.

記錄前，伊利先確認錶盤正在轉動。停止的水錶也能替從未計量的水做出整齊帳目。一人念數字，另一人複誦，井管員記下時間；第二次讀取時兩人交換位置。數字相符，但增加量少於柵欄外家庭被收費的數量。井管員指向診所的另一條管線，沒有人記錄它是否納入同一總數。

The activist wanted the gate opened immediately. The keeper said he would lose his post. Nara asked them to leave the pipe untouched until someone could explain its route, while preserving the households' existing collection times. That did not give the families enough water, and several told her so. She put their complaint beside the readings rather than calling the visit a success. Before leaving, the witnesses each took a copy, and the keeper kept the original inside his office window where it could be read from the street. The activist handed his spare hat to the guard still standing in the sun. It was a small kindness between people who remained opposed. The numbers on the glass gave them a reason to meet again without pretending that the argument had ended.

行動者要求立刻開門，井管員說那會使自己失去工作。娜拉請他們先別動管線，等人查清走向，同時保留家庭原有取水時段。這仍不足以滿足家庭所需，幾名居民直接告訴她。她把抱怨寫在讀數旁，沒有把訪查稱為成功。離開前，兩名見證人各拿一份副本，井管員把原件放在辦公室窗內，街上的人也能閱讀。行動者把備用帽子遞給曬太陽的守衛。這是立場仍對立的人之間一點善意。玻璃上的數字讓他們有理由再見，不必假裝爭論已結束。

At sunset, the team could finally state a limited outcome: two independent readings exposed a gap in the official totals.

日落時，團隊終於能說出有限成果：兩份獨立讀數顯示官方總量有缺口。
