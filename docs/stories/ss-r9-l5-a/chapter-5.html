The Empty Water Seal

沙海種子庫：最後一袋雨 · 第 5 / 25 章 · 4000–5000 · 英中對照

用互動版查詞、朗讀與收藏

That morning, families filled a meeting hall beside the public cistern.

那天早上，居民擠滿公共蓄水池旁的集會廳。

The permit for the first planting had a water seal but no measured quota.

第一批播種許可有水印，卻沒有測量過的配額。

Attendance at the hall exceeded the council's estimate. One resident displayed a bruise received while defending the public tap, and others began to contend over who should control it. A contemporary corporation offered to endorse the new planting ritual in exchange for advertising. Nara said the essence of the proposal was controversial: it would facilitate publicity, not equitable water access.

到場人數超過議會估計。一名居民展示自己保護公用水龍頭時留下的瘀傷，其他人開始爭論誰該控制水源。一間當代企業表示願意支持新播種儀式，條件是換取廣告。娜拉說提案的核心很有爭議：它方便的是宣傳，不是公平取水。

The merchant council demanded an immediate demonstration.

商人議會要求立刻公開示範。

The irony was that the company's offering included a beautiful outfit for the ceremony but no measurement tools. Nara asked for the overall cost, divided it into a segment for pipes and another for repairs, and demanded a statistical estimate. From the perspective of the poorest homes, a transition to paid private wells would be a loss, no matter how impressive the public celebration appeared. Tasa would defy any vote that stole a village's right to refuse.

諷刺的是，公司的奉獻包括一套漂亮的典禮服裝，卻沒有任何測量工具。娜拉要求提出總成本，把它分成管線與維修部分，並索取統計估算。從最貧困家庭的角度看，轉向收費私人水井將是損失，不管公開慶典看起來多麼風光。 塔莎會反抗任何奪走村落拒絕權的表決。

Nara asked the growers to vote on a temporary hold and a shared water count.

娜拉請農友對暫緩播種及共同清點用水進行表決。

The chalk marks on the wall were shorter than the line printed on the company's display. A council assistant said the woman must have counted incorrectly. She opened her bag and took out the small container her household used every morning. Its handle had been repaired with wire. Nara asked the assistant to fill it from the public tap while the woman watched. Nobody had brought a vessel large enough to check the display's claim directly.

牆上的粉筆線比公司展示板上的短。議會助理說，老婦一定算錯了。她從袋裡拿出家中每天使用的小水桶，提把用鐵絲修過。娜拉請助理當著老婦的面從公共水龍頭裝水。現場沒有人帶來足以直接查核展示數字的大容器。

Ilyas found a measuring jug in the kitchen. Water from the household container did not reach the level the company called a standard allowance. The assistant began explaining that pressure varied between streets. That was precisely why a number on a poster could not settle the question. Nara asked for readings at the tap rather than an estimate based on the new pump's advertised capacity. The company representative offered to discuss the difference privately after the demonstration. The woman remained beside her chalk marks, waiting for an answer the whole hall could hear.

伊利在廚房找到量壺。家用水桶倒出的水，沒有達到公司所稱的標準配額。助理解釋各街水壓不同，這正好說明海報數字無法解決問題。娜拉要求水龍頭實際讀數，而非根據新水泵宣傳容量估算。公司代表願意等示範結束後私下討論差異，老婦卻站在粉筆線旁，等一個全廳都聽得見的回答。

Before the vote, Tasa asked whether a temporary hold would mean that families could no longer collect drinking water. Nara wrote two separate decisions on the board: household supply would continue, while the proposed planting demonstration would wait for a shared count. The assistant read both aloud. Several hands that had remained low during the argument now rose. Afterward, the woman drew another line beside her first one, using the amount they had actually measured. She did not erase the old line. The gap between them gave the local auditors their first task. Outside, the company workers folded their ceremonial cloth while the kitchen staff kept the jug on the counter. Tomorrow's visitors would need something they could fill, empty, and compare for themselves. The kitchen manager asked who would keep the measuring jug clean between visits. The assistant accepted that small duty and wrote his name beside it. The first shared check now had both a tool and someone responsible for making it usable.

表決前，塔莎問，暫緩是否意味家庭也不能領飲水。娜拉在板上分開寫兩項決定：家用供水繼續，規劃中的播種示範則等共同清點後再進行。助理把兩項都朗讀出來。原先在爭論中沒有舉起的幾隻手，此時升起。表決後，老婦按實際量到的水量，畫出另一條線，沒有擦掉舊線。兩線之間的差距成了地方查核員的第一項工作。外面，公司員工收起典禮布，廚房人員把量壺留在櫃臺。明天的訪客需要的是能親手裝滿、倒空、比對的東西。 廚房主管問訪客來訪之間誰清洗量壺。助理接下這件小事，把名字寫在旁邊。第一次共同查核如今有了工具，也有人負責維持工具可用。

Evening brought one measurable gain: the vote protected the remaining seed and authorized a local audit.

傍晚帶來一項可量測的進展：表決保護了剩餘種子，也授權地方查核。
