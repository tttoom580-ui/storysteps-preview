That morning, a salt-flat settlement welcomed Nara with dry tea and silence.

那天早上，鹽地聚落以淡茶與沉默迎接娜拉。

Elder Tasa knew a buried variety survived in salty ground.

長者塔莎知道有一個古老品系能在鹽地存活。

Calling the old salt houses useless was absurd, Tasa said. Their architecture sheltered grain from adverse winds; only excessive pumping had damaged the land. A donor's new poster tried to highlight his generosity, but its ideology ignored indigenous growers. A visiting journalist promised careful journalism, and Nara offered access only after the residents approved what could be filmed.

塔莎說，把舊鹽屋稱為廢物十分荒謬。那些建築能遮擋不利的強風；真正傷害土地的是過量抽水。一名捐助者的新海報試圖突顯他的慷慨，背後的觀念卻忽視在地原生耕作者。一位來訪記者承諾謹慎報導，娜拉則表示，只有居民批准後才能拍攝。

Outsiders had collected samples without asking the village.

外人曾未經同意便取走村子的樣本。

An outsider offered Tasa a massage for her stiff hands, but she preferred to keep planting. Her quest was simple: let a living variety persist without inviting prosecution over old ownership claims. A damaged page would rip if lifted too quickly. Beside it lay a series of fish-trade stamps, including salmon from the coast; their variation in ink helped date the document rather than render the grower's memory irrelevant.

一名外地人想替塔莎僵硬的手按摩，但她寧願繼續種植。她的追尋很簡單：讓活著的品系延續，而不因舊所有權爭議招致起訴。破損紙頁若抬得太快就會撕裂。旁邊有一連串魚貨貿易戳記，包括來自海岸的鮭魚；墨色的差異有助於判定文件年代，而非使農友的記憶變得無足輕重。

Nara requested permission for three small soil jars.

娜拉徵求帶走三小罐土樣的許可。

Tasa brought the jars to a low table and asked Nara which one would leave the village. Nara had expected a question about equipment. She put down the carrying case and explained that she hoped to take all three to the laboratory. Tasa moved one jar back toward herself. The last visitors had also promised to return what they borrowed. They had returned a report the village could not read.

塔莎把罐子帶到矮桌旁，問娜拉哪一只會離開村子。娜拉原以為她會問設備。她放下提箱，解釋自己希望把三只都送進實驗室。塔莎把一只拉回身邊。上一批訪客也承諾歸還借走的東西，最後卻只寄回村民看不懂的報告。

Nara opened the case again. There was room for two jars if the third remained here as a local comparison. That would make the experiment less convenient, but it would let Tasa check what had actually been removed. They tied matching threads around the lids and wrote down the depth and place where each soil sample had been taken. Tasa corrected the place names. Nara asked her to make those corrections on the laboratory labels as well, rather than hiding them on a private sheet. The younger keepers gathered close enough to see how the ties could be checked without opening the jars.

娜拉重新打開箱子。若留下一罐供地方比對，箱中仍放得下另外兩罐。實驗會比較麻煩，塔莎卻能查核究竟帶走了什麼。她們在罐蓋綁上相同線結，記下每份土樣的深度與地點。塔莎更正地名，娜拉請她連實驗室標籤一起改，不把更正藏在私人紙頁上。年輕保管人靠近觀看，學習不開罐也能檢查線結的方法。

The laboratory messenger arrived with a printed receipt that described the contents as donated material. Nara drew a line through that description. Tasa was lending soil for a named test, not handing over a plant variety or permission to sell it. The messenger said he could not change the form. Nara shut the carrying case and left it on the table. After a long pause, he offered to take a revised receipt to his supervisor before collecting anything. Tasa kept the third jar beside her chair. Only then did she offer Nara a second cup of tea. It was still weak, but this time she asked whether the road to the laboratory would be rough enough to break the glass. Nara wrapped the glass separately before leaving; Tasa's permission would be useless if careless transport destroyed the comparison.

實驗室信使帶來印好的收據，把內容寫成捐贈材料。娜拉劃掉這個說法。塔莎借出土壤供指定測試，並未讓出植物品系或販售許可。信使說自己不能改表格，娜拉便關上箱子，留在桌上。沉默許久後，他表示可以先帶修訂收據請主管確認，再來領取樣本。塔莎把第三只罐子留在椅邊，這才替娜拉續茶。茶依然淡，但這次她問，通往實驗室的路會不會顛簸到震破玻璃。 離開前娜拉把玻璃罐分別包好；若粗心運輸毀掉比對樣本，塔莎的許可也會失去意義。

Tasa refused the reporter's first photograph, then placed the three jars in the sunlight herself. She wanted the village's permission to be seen, not merely its hardship.

塔莎拒絕記者拍下第一張照片，隨後親手把三只罐子放到陽光下。她希望村子的同意權被看見，而不只是困境。

Evening brought one measurable gain: Tasa permitted the test but not the transfer of seed ownership.

傍晚帶來一項可量測的進展：塔莎允許測試，卻沒有同意轉移種子所有權。
