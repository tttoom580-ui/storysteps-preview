沙海種子庫：最後一袋雨 · 第 4 / 25 章 · 4000–5000 · 英中對照

用互動版查詞、朗讀與收藏

A dust storm met the caravan on the eastern service road.

沙塵暴在東側補給路迎上車隊。

The clinic and seed carriers shared the last usable shelter.

診所人員與運種車隊必須共用最後一處能用的避難棚。

The clinic's driveway was crowded with freight carts, not visitors seeking relief from boredom. A civic guideline put shelter before cargo, but the dispatcher pleaded that moving patients would prolong delivery. Tasa spoke with an eloquent calm that cut through despair. She said an innovative shelter could protect the seed cases too, if the drivers stopped arguing long enough to build it.

診所車道擠滿了貨車，並不是來此排遣無聊的人。公民指引要求先安置人員、再處理貨物，但調度員懇求不要延長送貨時間。塔莎用流暢而平靜的話語打破絕望。她說，只要司機停止爭吵片刻，一個有創意的避難棚就能同時保護種箱。

A driver insisted that the archive should outrank sick travelers.

一名駕駛堅稱種子庫比生病旅客更重要。

The storm would intensify, and the shortage of housing became noticeable. A nurse checked each person's medication while a boy began to vomit from the heat. Nara opened a tin of clean cups and asked a driver to wipe the windshield. Morality was not an abstract trait here: a little water might stimulate recovery, while a reckless departure could put everyone in danger. The driver did not plead for special treatment; he asked for a safer road.

風暴可能增強，住宿不足也愈來愈明顯。護士檢查每個人的藥品，一個男孩因高溫開始嘔吐。娜拉打開一只裝有乾淨杯子的錫盒，請司機擦淨擋風玻璃。此處的道德並不是抽象特質：一點水或許有助恢復，魯莽出發卻可能讓所有人涉險。 駕駛沒有懇求特殊待遇，只要求一條更安全的道路。

Instead of offering a broad promise, Nara delayed the journey and opened a dry storage bay for the patients.

娜拉延後旅程，開放乾燥貨艙供病患休息。

Opening the storage bay meant moving three heavy cases out of the way. The driver who had argued for the seeds held his arms across the first one. Its contents had survived two failed journeys, he said. Nara did not ask him to abandon it in the storm. She showed him the dry space beneath the raised loading ramp and asked whether his crew could secure the cases there while the patients rested inside.

開放貨艙就得先移走三只重箱。先前主張保護種子的駕駛攔住第一只，說裡面的東西已挺過兩趟失敗旅程。娜拉沒有要他把箱子丟進風暴。她指著升高裝貨坡道下的乾燥空間，問他的隊員能否把箱子固定在那裡，讓病患進艙休息。

The nurse inspected the bay before agreeing. A strong smell from an old fuel container made one corner unsuitable, so Ilyas removed the container and opened the upper vents. The driver found a clean sheet to hang between the resting area and the cargo. His hands shook while he fastened it. He explained that his own brother had once lost a season's seed during an emergency. Nara understood his anger better now, though it did not change which people needed shelter first. She asked him to help her check the cases after everyone had been moved.

護士先檢查貨艙才答應。舊燃料桶使一角氣味強烈，不適合休息，伊利便搬走容器，打開上方通風口。駕駛找到乾淨布單，隔開休息區與貨物，綁布時雙手發抖。他說，自己的哥哥曾在一次緊急事件裡失去整季種子。娜拉更明白他的憤怒，但這不會改變誰最需要先進避難處。她請他等人員安置好，再陪她一起檢查種箱。

A wheel jammed against the ramp just as the wind pushed sand through the yard. Instead of sending a sick traveler back outside, the crew passed the remaining medicine through a side opening. The nurse counted each parcel as it reached her. When the last patient was seated, the driver showed Nara the ropes around the cases. None had loosened. He had protected his cargo without closing the bay to the people beside it. Later, the boy searched for the dog and tried to share his drink. Watching Nara fetch a separate bowl, the driver quietly brought another cup for the nurse. They would still arrive late. For that afternoon, at least, lateness had bought something more useful than an argument about whose loss mattered most. Before departure, the nurse checked the count again. Every medicine parcel had reached the bay, including the one passed through the side opening.

風把沙吹進院子時，一只輪子卡住坡道。隊員沒有叫病人重新出去，而是從側邊開口傳進剩餘藥品，護士逐包清點。最後一位病人坐下後，駕駛帶娜拉看綁住箱子的繩索，沒有一條鬆脫。他保護了貨物，也沒有把身邊的人擋在艙外。稍後男孩找狗分享飲水，看著娜拉另外拿水碗，駕駛默默替護士添一杯水。他們仍會遲到，但至少這個下午，遲到換來的東西，比爭論誰的損失比較重要更有用。 出發前護士再次清點。每包藥都已送進貨艙，包括從側邊開口傳進的那包。

By the time the shadows lengthened, the medicine arrived intact and the samples stayed sealed.

影子拉長時，藥品完整送達，種子樣本也保持封存。
