重複計費的水渠

The Canal That Paid Twice

沙海種子庫：最後一袋雨 · 第 7 / 25 章 · 4000–5000 · 英中對照 · 校訂中

用互動版查詞、朗讀與收藏

A narrow canal divided the orchard district from the dry quarter.

一條狹窄水渠隔開果園區與乾燥街區。

The same water delivery appeared twice in two ledgers.

同一批送水量在兩本帳上出現兩次。

The double billing could arouse controversy among the canal workers. An array of old photographs showed a dark corridor beneath the intake station, but none proved the company corrupt. Nara asked who held custody of the originals and whether the extra charge might derive from a disconnected meter. Ilyas refused to disconnect anything until a technician with the right expertise arrived.

重複計費可能在水渠工人之間引起爭議。一系列舊照片顯示取水站下方有一條陰暗走廊，但沒有一張能證明公司腐敗。娜拉詢問誰保管原件，以及額外收費是否源自一個脫線的水錶。伊利拒絕在具備專業技術的人抵達前拆除任何裝置。

A union blamed the orchard workers while owners blamed strangers.

工會怪罪果園工人，業主則怪罪外來者。

A clean water supply was indispensable, especially when damaged infrastructure could infect a whole village. The labor league wanted to blame a mysterious pirate who sold stolen water credits. Instead, Nara recorded the actual recipient of each payment. She authorized removal of a broken valve, then checked whether the replacement was sturdy enough for the next irrigation tournament at the farmers' fair. The careful work was worthwhile. The fair's cuisine depended on vegetables grown beside the canal.

乾淨水源不可或缺，尤其當受損基礎設施可能讓整個村莊遭感染時。工會想怪罪一名販賣偷來點數的神祕水賊。娜拉則記下每筆款項真正的收款人。她准許拆除壞掉的閥門，再檢查替代品是否夠堅固，能應付農民市集下一場灌溉競賽。這樣謹慎的工作值得付出。 市集的菜餚仰賴水渠旁種植的蔬菜。

Instead of offering a broad promise, Nara traced the meter photographs through the village committee.

娜拉透過村委會追查水錶照片。

The two receipts had different headings but the same stain near the bottom. Rui laid them side by side and asked whether they were simply copies. The orchard worker shook his head. His family had paid both bills because one office called its charge delivery and the other called it access. He had thought refusing either payment would close the channel to his trees.

兩張收據的標題不同，底部卻有相同汙漬。芮把它們並排，問是否只是副本。果園工人搖頭。他家兩筆都繳，因為一間辦公室稱它為運送費，另一間稱為使用費。他以為拒繳任何一筆，果樹的水渠都會被關閉。

Nara asked him to mark the dates on a calendar. The deliveries fell on the same morning. Ilyas compared the meter photographs, looking for signs that one had been taken before the other. A loose wire appeared in exactly the same position in both pictures. That supported a connection between the accounts, though it did not explain who had requested the second payment. The committee clerk brought a book showing how money moved between the offices. Its page for that week had been corrected in a different ink. He placed it on the table himself and stayed while they copied it.

娜拉請他在日曆標日期，兩筆送水都落在同一天早上。伊利比對水錶照片，查看有無一張先拍、一張後拍的跡象。兩張圖裡鬆掉的電線位置完全一樣，支持兩筆帳互有關聯，卻不能解釋誰要求第二次付款。委員會書記帶來辦公室間款項流動的帳本。那週的頁面用另一種墨水改過。他主動把它放上桌，留下來陪他們抄錄。

An owner offered to refund the worker if the discussion stopped there. The worker almost agreed; he needed the money for a replacement valve. Tasa asked whether the neighboring households would receive the same offer. The owner had brought cash for only one family. Nara separated the immediate refund from the wider inquiry. Accepting money already owed should not require the worker to destroy the evidence or speak for everyone else. They copied his receipts before he took them home. At the door he began to tear the duplicate, then passed it back for safekeeping. The refund could repair one valve. Keeping the paper intact might help explain why an entire street had been charged for water it received only once. The clerk numbered the copied pages before returning the book to its shelf. A loose photograph would be easy to separate from the account it described. Rui included the page numbers in her notes, giving the committee a way to find the same entries again.

一位業主願意退款，只要討論到此為止。工人差點答應，他需要錢換閥門。塔莎問鄰近家庭是否也能得到同樣安排，業主卻只帶了一戶的現金。娜拉把立即退款與整體調查分開。拿回本來就欠他的錢，不應要求他銷毀證據或替所有人發言。他們先複印收據，才讓他帶回家。門口，他一度想撕掉重複收據，最後交回保管。退款能修一個閥門，完整保存紙張，或許能解釋整條街為何收到一次水卻付兩次錢。 書記把副本編頁才將帳本歸架。散落照片很容易與它描述的帳目分離，芮把頁碼也寫進筆記，讓委員會日後能找到同樣條目。

By the time the shadows lengthened, the duplicate charge was real but its author remained unknown.

影子拉長時，重複計費確實存在，但究竟由誰填入仍不清楚。
