A carefully planned date fails when Renji mistakes a human funeral custom for a birthday celebration. A narrow strip of daylight crossed the table in Northhaven. Beside it lay the soul contract, covered with marks that only made sense to someone who had been there. Renji asked Lila to start from the beginning, not from the conclusion everybody wanted. The first account was untidy and sometimes embarrassing. That was a reason to ask careful questions, not a reason to throw it away. Outside, footsteps hurried past the door. The instruction used the word anyhow, and Lila asked the team to agree on its meaning. If they chose to assume, someone would have to explain the result to the people outside. A witness mentioned bacteria in a hurried account; Lila wrote it down without guessing its importance.

蓮治把人間的喪禮習俗誤認成生日活動，精心安排的約會因而失敗。 一道細窄日光照在北港城的桌面上，旁邊攤著靈魂契約，上面滿是只有親臨現場的人才看得懂的記號。蓮治請莉拉從事情的開頭講起，而不是從大家想聽的結論開始。最初的說法雜亂，甚至令人難堪，但那是需要仔細提問的理由，不是丟棄它的理由。門外腳步聲匆匆經過。 指示用了「總之」這個詞，莉拉請團隊先釐清它的意思。如果他們選擇「假設」，就必須有人向外頭的人解釋結果。一名證人在匆忙的敘述中提到「細菌」；莉拉先記下來，沒有猜測它有多重要。

A messenger came from Examiner Noa with an offer that sounded generous until Lila read the last line. The offer protected the person making it, not the people who would carry out the work. Renji reached for the soul contract and wrote down the missing condition. They were not refusing cooperation. They were refusing to confuse cooperation with obedience. That distinction changed the way the next conversation began. Lila found bump on the list of details that the first report had left unexplained. A witness mentioned creator in a hurried account; Lila wrote it down without guessing its importance. Someone asked whether excellence was connected to the trouble, and the room fell quiet for a moment.

諾亞考官派來使者提出一項看似大方的條件，直到莉拉讀到最後一行才發現問題。條件保護的是提出者，不是實際工作的人。蓮治拿起靈魂契約記下缺少的前提。他們並非拒絕合作，而是拒絕把合作和服從混為一談。這個區別改變了下一場談話的方式。 莉拉在第一份報告沒有說明的細節清單上，找到「碰撞」。一名證人在匆忙的敘述中提到「創建者」；莉拉先記下來，沒有猜測它有多重要。有人問「卓越」是否與麻煩有關，房間一時間安靜下來。

In a quieter corner, Lila and Renji tried to understand each other without an audience. One favored action because people needed help now; the other feared that a rushed action could shut the safest route. Both concerns were real. Lila admitted that the soul contract did not answer every question. Renji admitted that waiting forever was not a plan either. They decided to separate the urgent work from the larger decision. Renji’s reaper badge flashed whenever someone mentioned his examination. Lila covered the light with a cloth and said she would not discuss her heart under an official clock. At first the change seemed inner; a second observation made the team less certain. Someone asked whether missile was connected to the trouble, and the room fell quiet for a moment. Renji wanted to multiply, but agreed to wait for one more safe test.

到了安靜角落，莉拉與蓮治試著在沒有觀眾的情況下理解彼此。一人主張立刻行動，因為有人正在求助；另一人擔心匆忙行動會堵住最安全的路。兩種擔憂都真實。莉拉承認靈魂契約無法回答一切，蓮治也承認無限等待不是計畫。兩人決定把緊急工作和較大的決策分開。只要有人提到考試，蓮治的死神徽章便閃光。莉拉拿布蓋住亮光，說不願在官方計時器下討論自己的心。 起初這項變化看來「內部的」，第二次觀察卻讓團隊不再那麼確定。有人問「飛彈」是否與麻煩有關，房間一時間安靜下來。蓮治想要「使倍增」，但同意先等一次安全測試的結果。

The test seemed almost foolish at first. They used plain tools, asked a witness to describe what could be seen, and repeated the same action twice. Lila kept the soul contract open so that Renji could challenge each step. On the second attempt, a detail changed. That difference did not solve the whole mystery, but it ruled out the easiest explanation. For the first time that day, they had something firmer than a rumor. Lila would not let an exam decide whether her feelings were real. Someone asked whether necessity was connected to the trouble, and the room fell quiet for a moment. Renji circled the entry for politician and asked whether the detail belonged to this incident. The margin of the soul contract contained a reference to resource, which Renji marked for a later check.

起初，測試看起來幾乎有點傻。他們只使用簡單工具，請證人描述眼前的情形，並把相同動作重做兩次。莉拉攤開靈魂契約，讓蓮治可以質疑每一步。第二次嘗試時，一個細節改變了。那不能解開整個謎團，卻排除了最簡單的解釋。他們終於握有比流言更可靠的東西。莉拉不允許考試決定自己的感情是否真實。 有人問「必要」是否與麻煩有關，房間一時間安靜下來。蓮治圈起「政治人物」那一欄，問這個細節是否真的屬於本次事件。靈魂契約的頁邊提到了「資源」，蓮治做上記號，準備日後查證。

When the group reported its findings, Examiner Noa praised the parts that sounded useful and ignored the warnings. Lila repeated the missing limits in front of everybody. That was uncomfortable, but Renji refused to soften the truth merely to protect the meeting. Their report did not demand instant agreement. It asked people to compare the evidence, suggest another test, and say openly which risks they were willing to accept. Renji wrote stale beside the early proposal, then added a question mark because opinions differed. The margin of the soul contract contained a reference to stomach, which Renji marked for a later check.

小組報告發現時，諾亞考官讚揚聽來有用的部分，卻忽略警告。莉拉當眾重述被漏掉的限制，氣氛因此不太舒服，但蓮治拒絕為維持場面而淡化真相。他們的報告不要求立刻同意，而是請大家比對證據、提出其他測試，並公開說明願意承受哪些風險。 蓮治在初步提案旁寫下「不新鮮的」，又加了問號，因為意見並不一致。靈魂契約的頁邊提到了「肚子」，蓮治做上記號，準備日後查證。

Someone suggested that Lila should keep the discovery secret until the whole argument was over. Renji objected: a secret about danger could also harm people who never had a chance to ask questions. They separated private names from public facts and prepared a short notice. The notice was less dramatic than the rumor, but it explained what could be used now and what still needed an independent check. After the rain, they shared hot tea beside a window full of reflected lanterns. No magic moved between them, and Lila found that silence less frightening than a prepared speech. Renji circled the entry for summary and asked whether the detail belonged to this incident. Renji wanted to suspect, but agreed to wait for one more safe test.

有人建議莉拉等爭議結束再公開發現。蓮治反對：危險的祕密也可能傷害那些根本沒有機會提問的人。他們把私人姓名與公共事實分開，準備簡短公告。公告不如流言驚人，卻清楚說明現在能使用什麼、哪些仍需獨立檢查。雨後，兩人在映著燈光的窗邊共飲熱茶。沒有魔法在他們之間流動，莉拉發現沉默反而比事先準備好的說詞更不令人害怕。 蓮治圈起「摘要」那一欄，問這個細節是否真的屬於本次事件。蓮治想要「嫌疑人」，但同意先等一次安全測試的結果。

Renji read the last line aloud. Lila checked the date and kept a copy for the morning team. Renji circled the entry for technique and asked whether the detail belonged to this incident. Renji wrote teenage beside the early proposal, then added a question mark because opinions differed.

蓮治朗讀最後一行。莉拉核對日期，為早班小組留下副本。 蓮治圈起「技巧」那一欄，問這個細節是否真的屬於本次事件。蓮治在初步提案旁寫下「青少年的」，又加了問號，因為意見並不一致。

Lila left the soul contract open so Renji could add the next finding. Neither could see the whole road yet.
