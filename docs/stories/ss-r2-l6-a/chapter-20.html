The Warrant Written in Advance

反派的辯護人，決定先替自己翻案 · 第 20 / 25 章 · 5000–6000 · 英中對照 · 校訂中

The recovered water samples strengthened the case, but Seren still faced the original charge of causing the archive fire. Lysa returned to the warrant that had disturbed her on the first day. A clerk named Fen had copied it in accordance with the chancellor's instructions. He claimed the date was an innocent clerical error. Seren suspected otherwise, but a hunch did not permit her to summon him as a criminal. She asked the inquiry to examine the sequence of drafts. The handwriting was sloppy in places, yet the same rare abbreviation appeared in three separate documents prepared before the fire. That repetition contradicted the prosecutor's claim of a single accidental mistake.

找回的水樣強化了案件，但瑟蓮仍面臨最初的檔案庫縱火指控。萊莎回頭檢視第一天就讓她起疑的逮捕令。書記芬恩依宰相指示抄寫，卻聲稱日期只是無心的文書錯誤。瑟蓮懷疑另有內情，但直覺不足以讓她把他直接當犯人傳喚。她請調查小組檢查草稿順序。某些字跡很潦草，但同一個罕見縮寫出現在火災前準備的三份不同文件裡。這種重複與檢察官所說「單一偶發錯誤」互相矛盾。

Fen lived in a modest house beside a pasture where sheep grazed. A child watched a caterpillar move clockwise around a wooden ornament. Seren waited outside while a neutral officer explained the summons. She would not arrive by surprise to intimidate a witness. Fen emerged carrying a compass and a ledger bound with a metal staple. He said the chancellor's secretary had instructed him to prepare an arrest form “for any disaster that might occur.” The wording was dubious. Seren asked whether the form mentioned a specific person. Fen hesitated, then admitted that Seren's name had been included before the supposed crime.

芬恩住在一間牧場旁的樸素房子裡，羊群在草地上吃草。一個孩子看著毛毛蟲沿木製裝飾順時針爬行。瑟蓮在屋外等候，讓中立官員說明傳喚理由。她不願突然上門威嚇證人。芬恩帶著指南針和一本用金屬釘裝訂的帳冊出來。他說宰相秘書指示他預先準備一張「萬一發生任何災難」即可使用的逮捕令。措辭十分可疑。瑟蓮問表格是否寫了特定人名。芬恩猶豫後承認，所謂犯罪發生之前，瑟蓮的姓名就已填上。

The inquiry summoned the secretary and asked for a full explanation. He argued that the palace often prepared emergency orders and that Seren's family connections made her a predictable suspect. Seren replied that preparation could not justify inventing guilt before evidence existed. One member of the panel defended the practice in the name of nationalism, insisting that loyalty to the crown required trust in ministers. Another member answered that true loyalty demanded lawful government. Their disagreement enhanced the hearing rather than weakening it. The public could see the arguments and examine the rules. Seren wanted no one exempt from the same standard; she wanted that standard to apply to any accused citizen.

調查小組傳喚宰相秘書，要求完整解釋。他主張王宮經常預先準備緊急命令，而瑟蓮的家族關係使她成為可預測的嫌犯。瑟蓮回答，預作準備不能合理化在證據出現前就編造罪名。一名小組成員以民族主義名義為此辯護，堅稱忠於王室就該信任大臣；另一人回答，真正的忠誠要求依法行政。兩人的分歧沒有削弱聽證，反而提升它的價值。公眾得以看到論點，檢驗規則。瑟蓮不要為自己取得特別豁免，只希望同一標準適用任何被告。

The secretary finally admitted that he had received a sealed order from Ormond's private office. A solitary courier delivered it at night, tiptoeing past guards and saluting the secretary alone. The document instructed Fen to prepare a warrant and leave a space for the hour. It did not state that the archive would be burned, but it named Seren as the person to detain if an incident occurred. Lysa asked for the seal to be authenticated. If genuine, it would connect Ormond's office to the plan to frame Seren; it would not yet identify the person who ignited the fire. The panel ordered the courier located and the private office's dispatch book preserved.

秘書終於承認，自己收到奧蒙德私人辦公室寄來的封口命令。一名獨行信差夜裡送達，踮腳走過守衛，只向秘書一人敬禮。文件要求芬恩準備逮捕令，並空出填寫時間的欄位。它沒有寫明檔案庫將遭焚燒，卻指定若發生事件，就拘捕瑟蓮。萊莎要求鑑定印章真偽。若屬實，文件將把奧蒙德辦公室與設局陷害瑟蓮連在一起，但尚未指出點火者。小組下令尋找信差，並保存私人辦公室的發文簿。

Fen explained that he had entered government service during adolescence, when he believed official documents were always trustworthy. Years of copying forms had trained him to obey instructions without reading their purpose. Seren asked whether he understood that his work had endangered an innocent person. He lowered his eyes and said yes. She did not offer instant forgiveness. Instead, she asked him to tell the full truth under oath and accept whatever lawful consequences followed. Accountability could be painful without becoming revenge. Fen agreed to preserve his remaining notes and submit them to the independent panel. It was a small, difficult beginning.

芬恩解釋，他在青春期就進入政府工作，那時相信官方文件永遠可靠。多年抄寫表格，使他習慣服從命令，不去想目的。瑟蓮問他是否理解自己的工作曾危及無辜者。他低頭說理解。她沒有立刻原諒他，而是請他宣誓說出完整真相，並接受依法產生的後果。問責可以令人痛苦，卻不必變成報復。芬恩同意保存剩餘筆記，提交獨立小組。這是一個很小、卻困難的開始。

When Seren returned home, she realized how close she had come to being sacrificed for administrative convenience. The arrest had been prepared like a routine form, and the trial had nearly completed the process. She thought of the countless other citizens who could not command an inquiry or find a court clerk willing to listen. Her intellect had helped, but so had Ada's sewing, Elen's registers, and Nera's cautious science. None of them alone could have overturned the story. The investigation now possessed a verified reason to examine the chancellor's dispatch book. For the first time, the allegation against Seren had become a question about the people who had written the allegation.

瑟蓮回家時才意識到，自己差點為行政便利而被犧牲。逮捕令像普通表格一樣預先準備，審判也差點把程序完成。她想到無數沒有能力要求調查、也遇不到願意傾聽之書記員的市民。她的思考能力有幫助，艾達的縫紉、艾蓮的登記簿、妮拉謹慎的科學也一樣重要。沒有任何一人單獨足以推翻原故事。如今調查取得了正當理由，可以查閱宰相的發文簿。瑟蓮遭受的指控，第一次變成了關於那些編造指控之人的問題。
