百畝沉默之地

A Hundred Acres of Silence

反派的辯護人，決定先替自己翻案 · 第 7 / 25 章 · 5000–6000 · 英中對照 · 校訂中

The parliamentary inquiry required testimony from outside the capital, so Seren went north again. She had learned that a hundred-acre estate near the border supplied water to the polluted mine. Its former manager, a bachelor named Jorin, now taught boxing to village children. Jorin was openly gay, and hostile officials had repeatedly used that fact to question his character rather than his accounts. Seren refused to equate prejudice with evidence. She asked only about the estate's records. Jorin agreed to talk, but his voice faltered when she mentioned the winter landslide that had buried the original water channel. He had lost colleagues there, and nobody in the capital had bothered to record their names.

議會調查要求取得首都以外的證詞，因此瑟蓮再次北上。她查到邊境附近一座百畝莊園曾向受污染的礦場供水。前任經理喬林是單身男子，如今教村裡的孩子拳擊。他公開自己的同性戀身分，而敵對官員一直拿這件事質疑他的人格，卻不去檢視帳冊。瑟蓮拒絕將偏見等同證據，只詢問莊園紀錄。喬林同意談話，但當她提到冬季土石流掩埋舊水道時，他的聲音顫抖了。他在災難中失去同事，首都竟沒人費心記下他們的姓名。

Gloom hung over the village, but the fields showed signs of fertility returning. An elderly emigrant had brought seeds from across the mountains, and families worked together to nourish the exhausted soil. A woodpecker tapped at the orchard fence while Jorin spread the old maps on a table. They marked an intersection where the mountain stream divided. Under an orthodox interpretation of property law, the estate owned the left bank, but the water itself belonged to the public. The chancellor's office had issued a prohibition on independent testing there. Seren asked whether that restriction had ever been approved by the elected council. Jorin said it had not.

陰鬱的氣氛籠罩村落，但田地已出現恢復肥沃的跡象。一位年老移民從山另一側帶來種子，村民合作滋養枯竭的土壤。一隻啄木鳥敲著果園圍籬，喬林則把舊地圖鋪在桌上。眾人標出山溪分流的交會點。按照傳統的土地法解釋，莊園擁有左岸，但水本身屬於公眾。宰相辦公室卻曾禁止在此進行獨立檢測。瑟蓮詢問那項禁令是否經選舉產生的議會同意，喬林說沒有。

A council meeting was called in the schoolhouse. Its provisional chairwoman warned that the village could lose its water license if it challenged the palace. Jorin answered that a license meant nothing if the river could no longer support a family. Some elders wanted to forsake the inquiry, and Seren respected their fear. She described the Rule of Three and offered them the choice of submitting collective evidence without revealing private households. She also explained the risk: testimony might expose illegal arrangements the village had made during the old uprising. No one was promised an easy victory. After long discussion, the council held a unanimous vote to release a public map of the spring channels, leaving household names sealed.

村民在校舍召開議會。臨時女主席警告，如果挑戰王宮，村落可能失去取水許可。喬林回答，若河水已無法養活家庭，許可證根本沒有意義。有些長者想放棄調查，瑟蓮尊重他們的恐懼。她說明三證法則，提出讓村民以集體名義提供證據、不揭露個別家庭的選項。她也明白告知風險：證詞可能暴露村人在昔日起義期間私下訂立的非法安排。沒有人獲得輕鬆勝利的保證。經過長時間討論，議會一致投票公布泉水水道圖，但將住戶姓名封存。

The map changed the case. A mine tunnel appeared directly above the water source, though the official provincial survey placed it farther east. The discrepancy was too large to be an ordinary drafting error. Lysa compared the survey with Jorin's measurements and found that the tunnel had been shifted on paper to avoid environmental restrictions. Rowan asked why nobody had exposed the deception earlier. Jorin replied that vanity had little to do with the silence; people had needed their wages. Workers who questioned the map faced disciplinary hearings, and families feared dismissal. Seren began to understand how a conspiracy could survive without every participant knowing its entire design.

這張地圖改變了案情。圖上顯示一條礦坑隧道正好位在水源上方，但官方省級測量圖卻把它畫在更東邊。差異太大，不可能只是普通的繪圖錯誤。萊莎把測量圖與喬林的數據比較，發現隧道在紙面上被移位，好避開環境限制。羅文問為何沒人更早揭發騙局。喬林回答，沉默與虛榮無關；人們需要工資。質疑地圖的工人可能遭到懲戒調查，家庭也害怕失去生計。瑟蓮開始懂得，一場陰謀可以在大多數參與者都不知道全貌的情況下長期存在。

Jorin's courage did not mean he never felt fear. When a messenger arrived with a notice threatening another disciplinary hearing, Seren saw his hands falter above the documents. He asked whether helping her was worth endangering the village again. She answered that the decision remained his. The evidence belonged to the community, and its members could withdraw from public appearances without losing access to clean water. Jorin took a long time to consider the choice. Finally he signed the map release, not because Seren persuaded him with grand promises, but because the council had already debated the risks together.

喬林勇敢，卻不代表他從不害怕。一名信差送來威脅再次進行懲戒調查的通知時，瑟蓮看見他的手在文件上方微微顫動。他問，幫助她是否值得讓村落再度涉險。她回答，決定仍屬於他們。證據由社區擁有，居民即使退出公開出席，也不該因此失去乾淨飲水。喬林思考許久，最後簽下地圖公開文件；不是因為瑟蓮許下偉大承諾，而是村議會早已共同討論過風險。

Before leaving, Seren met the children at the boxing school. Jorin taught them how to stand firmly and how to stop before someone was hurt. “Strength without a limit is just another form of fear,” he told them. Seren remembered those words when the council offered her a public declaration of support. She declined to make their struggle a decoration for her trial. Instead, she asked them to send a representative to the independent hearing and to keep copies of every map. The village would control its own testimony. On the journey home, Rowan said he was beginning to see why an accused woman might become the most careful defender of everyone else's rights.

離開前，瑟蓮去看拳擊學校的孩子。喬林教他們如何站穩，以及如何在對方受傷前停手。「沒有界限的力量，只是另一種恐懼。」他說。當村議會提出要公開支持瑟蓮時，她想起這句話。她拒絕把村民的抗爭變成自己審判中的裝飾，反而請他們派代表出席獨立聽證會，並保留每一張地圖的副本。村落應能掌握自己的證詞。返程時羅文說，他開始理解，為何一位被告反而可能成為最小心守護他人權利的人。
