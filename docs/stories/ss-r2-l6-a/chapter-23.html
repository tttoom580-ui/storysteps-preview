The Furnace Keeper

反派的辯護人，決定先替自己翻案 · 第 23 / 25 章 · 5000–6000 · 英中對照 · 校訂中

The fuel records led to Mara, the former caretaker of the archive furnace. She had moved to a distant town after the fire and feared that returning would invite arrest. The panel offered a protected interview. Mara arrived with a nearsighted apprentice whose face bore a small pimple, a detail so ordinary that Seren felt ashamed of how dramatically she had imagined the missing witness. Mara carried the original furnace manual and a lottery ticket used to mark a page. She explained that a cavity beneath the furnace had been modified to accept oversized crates. The alteration violated safety instructions. Someone had concealed that flaw before the inspection.

燃料紀錄指向瑪拉，檔案庫熔爐的前任管理員。她在大火後搬到遠方小鎮，擔心回來會被抓。調查小組提供受保護的訪談。瑪拉帶著一名近視學徒前來，學徒臉上有顆小痘痘，這樣平凡的細節讓瑟蓮為自己過分戲劇化地想像失蹤證人感到慚愧。瑪拉帶著原始熔爐操作手冊，用一張彩券夾著某頁。她解釋，熔爐下方有個凹槽被改裝，可以放進超大貨箱。改裝違反安全指示，而有人在檢查前隱瞞這個缺陷。

On the night of the fire, an officer ordered Mara to burn a collection of duplicate records. She asked for the two approvals required by statute, but he intimidated her with threats of dismissal. She refused to operate the furnace herself. Another worker did so, and flames spread upward through an unsealed ventilation shaft. The blaze caused a surge of smoke that could have suffocated the night staff. Mara helped avert a worse disaster by opening an emergency exit, then watched the records disappear. She had tried to report the event, but an official ordered her notes shredded. She preserved one carbon copy before leaving the city.

火災當晚，一名軍官命令瑪拉焚燒一批重複紀錄。她要求出示法律規定的兩份核准，對方卻以革職威脅她。她拒絕親自操作熔爐，另一名工人於是動手，火焰經由未封閉的通風管向上蔓延。大量濃煙湧出，險些讓夜班人員窒息。瑪拉打開緊急出口，避免更嚴重的災難，眼睜睜看著文件燒毀。她試圖報告事件，官員卻命令把筆記撕碎。離城前，她保留了一份複寫副本。

The panel examined Mara's copy with dismay. It identified the officer who had ordered the unlawful disposal, but the signature was not Ormond's. Seren reminded the investigators that the identity of a direct actor and responsibility for the broader plan were separate legal questions. A person could be liable for authorizing an unlawful operation without personally setting a fire. The disclosure also complicated the public narrative: the blaze had spread through unsafe equipment during a concealed disposal, rather than through the neat single act described in the novel. Mara had chosen to help the night staff, yet she still feared punishment for her earlier silence. Seren asked the panel to consider that silence in its context.

小組帶著錯愕檢視瑪拉的副本。上面寫著下令非法銷毀文件的軍官姓名，但簽名不是奧蒙德。瑟蓮提醒調查者，直接行為者是誰，和誰應對整體計畫負責，是不同的法律問題。某人可能因批准非法行動而承擔責任，即使他沒有親自放火。新揭露的情況也使公眾敘事更複雜：火焰是在隱蔽銷毀文件期間，經由不安全設備蔓延，而不是小說所描寫的單一清楚犯行。瑪拉曾選擇救助夜班人員，卻仍害怕因早期沉默受罰。瑟蓮請小組把沉默放回當時情境考量。

A legal scholar helped condense the technical findings into questions the court could answer. Was the disposal authorized? No evidence of the required approvals existed. Had Mara warned of the danger? Her manual and preserved copy supported that account. Who had changed the ventilation system? Maintenance invoices pointed to a contractor linked to Ormond's brother. The inquiry still needed to distinguish criminal intent from negligence. Seren insisted that the workforce, including temporary laborers whose emigration records made them difficult to locate, be given an inclusive opportunity to testify. The original trial had silenced workers and punished a noblewoman for their absence. This hearing would not repeat the error.

一名法學者協助把技術發現濃縮為法庭能回答的問題。銷毀是否獲准？沒有任何法定核准的證據。瑪拉是否提醒危險？她的手冊與保存副本支持其說法。誰改動通風系統？維修發票指向與奧蒙德兄弟有關的承包商。調查仍須區分犯罪故意與疏失。瑟蓮堅持所有勞動者，包括因移民紀錄而難以追蹤的臨時工，都要有公平的作證機會。原審沉默了工人，又因工人沒有出庭而懲罰一位貴族女子；這次聽證不能重蹈覆轍。

Mara also described the nights before the fire. She had reported unusual fuel deliveries, but her supervisor said the orders came from above and refused to identify the official. Seren asked why she had not gone directly to the court. Mara answered that she was a low-paid employee with no access to legal advice and no certainty that a complaint would be accepted. The answer did not erase responsibility, but it explained the pressure under which she had acted. The panel arranged independent counsel for her. Seren wanted the testimony to be thorough, yet she understood that people deserved help even when their accounts were imperfect.

瑪拉也描述火災前幾晚的情況。她曾報告燃料異常送達，主管卻說命令來自上頭，拒絕指出官員姓名。瑟蓮問她為何不直接向法院申訴。瑪拉回答，自己薪水低、沒有法律諮詢管道，也不確定投訴會被受理。這不能抹去責任，卻解釋她當時承受的壓力。小組安排獨立法律協助。瑟蓮希望證詞完整，也明白即使人們的敘述不完美，仍值得得到幫助。

At the end of the day, Mara signed her statement in the presence of two independent witnesses. Seren did not ask her to accuse anyone beyond what she had seen. The revelation was powerful because it clarified the sequence of events: secret disposal, unsafe modification, accidental spread, and an official effort to redirect blame. The story still contained painful gaps, but those gaps were now named. Seren left the chamber with a solemn sense of responsibility. She had sought to prove one accusation false; she had uncovered a system willing to endanger workers and erase records to protect itself. The final hearing would have to address both.

那天結束時，瑪拉在兩名獨立證人面前簽下證詞。瑟蓮沒有要求她指控超出親眼所見的事。這項揭露之所以強大，是因為它澄清了事件順序：秘密銷毀、不安全改裝、意外延燒，以及官方企圖轉嫁罪責。故事仍有痛苦的空白，但那些空白現在被清楚指出。瑟蓮帶著沉重責任感離開會議廳。她原本只想證明一項指控不實，最後卻揭露一套願意危害工人、抹去紀錄以保護自己的制度。最終聽證必須處理這兩件事。
