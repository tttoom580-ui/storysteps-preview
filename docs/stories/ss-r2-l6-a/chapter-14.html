The Admiral’s Two Seals

反派的辯護人，決定先替自己翻案 · 第 14 / 25 章 · 5000–6000 · 英中對照 · 校訂中

The panel requested help from Admiral Voss, who controlled navigation through the southern strait. His ships had carried sealed crates during the week of the fire. At the wharf he showed Seren two manifests bearing similar seals, but the cargo weights differed. The admiral insisted that his officers had followed orders. Seren did not inquire whether he liked Ormond. She asked who could issue a shipping command and how an officer was expected to differentiate an authorized change from a forgery. Voss admitted a shortcoming in the procedure: urgent orders could be transmitted verbally, then written down afterward. The weakness had allowed important records to go astray.

調查小組向掌管南方海峽航運的沃斯海軍上將尋求協助。他的船曾在火災當週運送封箱。上將在碼頭拿出兩份封印相似的貨物清單，重量卻不同。他堅持軍官只是奉命行事。瑟蓮沒有問他是否喜歡奧蒙德，只問誰有權發布運輸命令，以及軍官應如何區分合法變更與偽造。沃斯承認制度有個缺點：緊急命令可以先口頭傳達，事後再補記。這個漏洞讓重要紀錄得以流失。

The admiral's staff led them into an underpass connecting the docks to the customs building. There a messenger had once tucked a packet behind a loose tile. The hiding place lay within the radius of a public lantern, making it visible to workers but not to officers using the main corridor. Rowan found a paper strip with the name of a ship and its departure hour. It did not prove what the ship carried, but it gave the investigators a feasible route for tracing the twelfth crate. Seren ordered no arrest. She had promised herself not to inflict on another suspect the same procedural injustice that nearly killed her.

上將的部下帶眾人走進連接碼頭與海關的地下通道。曾有信使把文件塞在鬆動的磁磚後。藏匿處位於公共燈具照明半徑之內，工人看得到，走主走廊的軍官卻看不到。羅文找到一截寫著船名與出港時間的紙條。它不能證明船上裝什麼，卻為追查第十二箱提供可行路線。瑟蓮沒有命令逮捕任何人。她答應自己，絕不把差點害死她的程序不公，重新施加在另一個嫌疑人身上。

A nurse from the port clinic then brought an unexpected lead. The customs accountant, who suffered from diabetes, had requested several days of leave after handling the shipment. He later sought psychotherapy because of recurring nightmares. Rumors described him as weak, but the nurse warned against confusing illness with dishonesty. Seren agreed that medical treatment was not evidence of wrongdoing. The nurse could confirm only that the man had asked for help and feared his superior. She would contact him through a protected channel. The inquiry had already caused enough woe among families whose livelihoods depended on the harbor; public speculation about a patient's health would make it worse.

港口診所的一名護士帶來意外線索。負責貨運的海關會計患有糖尿病，在處理那批貨物後曾請假數日，後來又因反覆惡夢接受心理治療。傳言說他軟弱，護士卻警告不能把疾病與不誠實混為一談。瑟蓮同意，接受醫療並不是犯錯的證據。護士只能證實他曾求助，並害怕上司；她會透過受保護的管道聯絡他。調查已讓許多依賴港口維生的家庭承受痛苦，如果再公開猜測病人的健康狀況，只會讓情況更糟。

At court Ormond warned that exposing military shipments might doom the kingdom's security. The admiral answered that secrecy without accountability could ruin security just as surely. A brotherhood of officers gathered behind him, demanding clear written rules. Seren noticed that several women clerks remained outside the meeting. The palace called their work feminine and insignificant, although they prepared the registers upon which the officers relied. She asked the panel to invite them as technical witnesses. Their knowledge was not less important because it came from a desk rather than a ship. Under the present reign, a title determined who was heard; Seren wanted documents and competence to matter instead.

在法庭上，奧蒙德警告公開軍事貨運可能毀掉王國安全。上將回答，沒有問責的保密，同樣會毀掉安全。軍官們結成團體支持他，要求清楚的書面規範。瑟蓮注意到幾名女性書記被擋在會議外。王宮稱她們的工作「女人氣」而不重要，儘管軍官依賴的登記簿都是她們整理的。她要求小組讓她們以技術證人身分出席。知識不會因為來自書桌、不是甲板，就變得不重要。在如今的統治下，頭銜決定誰能發聲；瑟蓮希望文件與能力才是關鍵。

Voss also provided a list of officers who had been on leave when the disputed cargo moved. Seren crossed their names off the immediate witness list, rather than drawing suspicion from absence. The admiral seemed surprised. “I expected you to pursue every advantage,” he admitted. Seren answered that an advantage built on an unfair inference might collapse as soon as it reached court. She had learned this lesson by nearly dying under an unsupported accusation. Voss promised to distribute new instructions requiring written confirmation of emergency shipping orders. Even before the criminal case ended, a dangerous procedural gap could be closed.

沃斯另外提供一份軍官休假名單，列出爭議貨物移動時不在場的人。瑟蓮把他們從立即詢問名單中劃掉，而不是因為缺席就起疑。上將顯得意外。「我以為你會把每個優勢都用上。」他承認。瑟蓮回答，建立在不公平推論上的優勢，一進法庭就可能崩潰。她差點因缺乏證據的指控喪命，才學到這一課。沃斯承諾發布新指示，要求緊急運輸命令必須取得書面確認。即使刑事案件尚未結束，一個危險的程序漏洞仍能先被補上。

By evening one clerk produced an excess-fuel voucher signed by a private contractor. The signature was not Ormond's, but the company's address matched his brother's mine. The admiral agreed to preserve the ship's log and let an independent specialist examine both seals. Seren considered the new direction imperative, though it still could not prove who had ignited the archive. A cargo could be concealed for financial reasons, political reasons, or reasons no one yet imagined. The court would have to distinguish among them. She left the wharf with the feeling that the old novel's villain had been condemned because the story needed someone to blame; real justice would require something more exacting.

傍晚，一名書記找出私人承包商簽發的超額燃料憑單。簽名不是奧蒙德，但公司地址與其兄弟的礦場相同。上將同意保存船舶航海日誌，並讓獨立專家檢查兩枚印章。瑟蓮認為這條新線索十分重要，卻仍無法證明誰點燃了檔案庫。貨物可能因財務、政治，或尚未想到的其他原因而被隱藏。法庭必須區分這些可能。她離開碼頭時覺得，原小說的反派之所以被判有罪，只因故事需要一個可以責怪的人；真正的正義需要遠為嚴格的證明。
