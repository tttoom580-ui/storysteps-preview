The Treasury Audit

反派的辯護人，決定先替自己翻案 · 第 22 / 25 章 · 5000–6000 · 英中對照 · 校訂中

The treasury audit began with a disagreement between two accountants. One believed the mine's profits had been hidden in ordinary maintenance expenses; the other thought the errors reflected careless bookkeeping. Seren asked them to state their methods before choosing sides. Their findings would have to ascend through several levels of review before reaching the court. The most important defect appeared in a payment that had been divided among four shell companies. A royal anthem was playing in the square when the auditors uncovered it, and citizens gathered outside to hear rumors. Seren advised the panel to publish verified figures only. The noise of public expectation could not replace an audit.

國庫審計一開始，兩名會計就持不同意見。一人認為礦場利潤被藏在普通維修費裡，另一人認為只是記帳粗心。瑟蓮請他們先說明方法，不急著選邊。調查結果必須經過多層審查，才能呈交法院。最重要的缺失出現在一筆分散到四家空殼公司的款項。稽核員發現它時，廣場正奏著王室國歌，市民聚集聽傳言。瑟蓮建議小組只公開已驗證數字。公眾期待再大聲，也不能取代真正審計。

A pharmacist testified next. He had supplied healthful medicines to the harbor clinic, but a separate invoice listed heroin, then marketed as a strong pain medicine, in quantities far beyond documented medical need. The inquiry did not assume that every purchase was criminal; it required records of prescriptions and distribution. The pharmacist had grumbled about the discrepancy years earlier and been ignored. A nurse confirmed that some supplies had never reached patients. The funds had instead been moved through a charity managed by Ormond's stepmother. That connection warranted scrutiny, not automatic blame. Seren insisted the woman receive notice and an opportunity to answer.

接著出庭的是藥師。他曾向港口診所供應有益健康的藥品，但另一張發票列著當時被當作強效止痛藥販售的海洛因，數量遠超有紀錄的醫療需求。調查沒有假設每一筆購買都是犯罪，而是要求處方與配送紀錄。藥師多年前曾抱怨差異，卻被忽視。一名護士證實，部分物資從未到達病患手中，資金反而流向奧蒙德繼母管理的慈善機構。這項關聯需要調查，卻不能自動定罪。瑟蓮堅持正式通知那名婦人，讓她有機會回應。

The stepmother appeared before the panel, standing upright despite hostile whispers. She explained that she resided on the mainland and did not manage daily transfers. Her charity's books had been handled by a contractor appointed by Ormond's brother. Some believed her defensive manner showed guilt; others treated her age as proof of innocence. Seren rejected both assumptions. The woman produced receipts for poultry farms and winter shelters, showing that part of the charity's work was genuine. The auditors then isolated payments that lacked corresponding services. What emerged was a network of concealed transfers, not a simple story in which every person connected to Ormond must be corrupt.

奧蒙德的繼母出席調查，儘管四周竊語帶著敵意，她仍挺直站著。她解釋自己住在大陸本土，並不管理日常轉帳；慈善機構帳務由奧蒙德兄弟任命的承包商處理。有人認為她的防衛態度表示心虛，也有人把年紀當成無罪證明。瑟蓮都不接受。婦人提出家禽農場與冬季收容所的收據，顯示慈善工作確實有部分真實。接著稽核員分離出找不到對應服務的付款紀錄。逐漸浮現的是一張秘密轉帳網路，而不是「凡與奧蒙德有關的人都有罪」的簡單故事。

A sudden hailstorm struck the courthouse roof, forcing people to mingle in the narrow corridor. An elderly dockworker told Seren that he had once been called a barbarian when he protested unsafe work. The word had followed his family for years. He hoped the new hearings might be more inclusive. Seren told him that procedure could not erase every prejudice, but it could require officials to justify their decisions with evidence. Through the window she saw the city hedge bending under ice. Public trust was equally fragile. A single sensational accusation could do damage that a hundred careful corrections would struggle to repair.

一場突如其來的冰雹敲打法院屋頂，人們被迫擠在狹窄走廊裡交談。一名年老碼頭工人告訴瑟蓮，自己曾因抗議不安全工作被罵作「野蠻人」，那個詞多年來一直跟著他的家庭。他希望新的聽證能更具包容性。瑟蓮說，程序不能消除所有偏見，卻可以要求官員以證據證明決策正當。她透過窗戶看見城市綠籬在冰粒下彎曲。公眾信任同樣脆弱。一項聳動指控造成的傷害，可能要靠上百次謹慎更正才勉強修補。

A second burst of hail struck the roof while the auditors reviewed the accounts. The stepmother requested privacy for conversations about family intimacy, arguing that the inquiry should not turn personal relationships into public entertainment. Seren agreed, while emphasizing that financial transactions remained open to examination. The woman said she had chosen to reside outside the palace to remain resistant to pressure from its rival factions. Her explanation deserved consideration, not automatic belief. The auditors focused on bank transfers and authorized signatures, leaving speculation about affection and family resentment aside. Their findings would be stronger because they did not depend on treating a complicated household as a simple scandal.

稽核員檢查帳目時，第二陣冰雹打在屋頂。繼母要求私人親密關係的對話受到保護，主張調查不該把家務事變成公眾娛樂。瑟蓮同意，同時強調財務交易仍須接受檢查。婦人說自己選擇住在王宮外，正是為了抵抗內部權力鬥爭。她的解釋值得考慮，卻不能自動相信。稽核員專注銀行轉帳與授權簽名，把對情感及家族怨恨的猜測放在一旁。正因為不把複雜家庭當作簡單醜聞，調查結果才會更強。

By evening, auditors traced three suspicious payments to a company that had purchased fuel for the archive furnace. The amounts were small compared with the mine's huge revenue, but they matched the dates of the secret disposal order. Seren refused to speculate about trillion-unit fortunes or elaborate royal conspiracies. The evidence was enough to request a search of the contractor's furnace logs. The panel approved the request and preserved the original financial records. A polar wind crossed the square as Seren left. The case was becoming less dramatic and more concrete, one payment, signature, and decision at a time.

傍晚時，稽核員把三筆可疑付款追到一家曾替檔案庫熔爐購買燃料的公司。金額相對礦場巨額收入很小，日期卻與秘密銷毀命令吻合。瑟蓮拒絕胡亂猜測數兆財富或複雜的王室陰謀。現有證據已足以要求搜索承包商的熔爐日誌。小組批准申請並保存原始財務紀錄。瑟蓮離開時，極地般的冷風掃過廣場。這起案件正變得不那麼戲劇化，卻更具體：一筆付款、一個簽名、一項決定，逐步成形。
