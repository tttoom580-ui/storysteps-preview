害怕天空的信差

The Courier Who Feared the Sky

反派的辯護人，決定先替自己翻案 · 第 21 / 25 章 · 5000–6000 · 英中對照 · 校訂中

The courier named in Fen's testimony had disappeared from the palace payroll. Seren found him working at an aviation exhibition, repairing early gliders and explaining flight to visitors. He had chosen the place because the crowds made him difficult to follow. Beforehand, he had sent Lysa a booklet describing his deliveries, but he refused to sign it until his family was safe. The exhibition included an absurd model spacecraft, a prehistoric bird skeleton, and a preserved octopus. Children laughed at the exhibits while the courier, Aric, watched the entrances with an anxious wrinkle between his brows. His fear was not theatrical. Two men had been waiting outside his home for several nights.

芬恩提到的信差已從王宮薪資名單上消失。瑟蓮在一場航空展找到他，他正修理早期滑翔機，向訪客解釋飛行。他選擇這地方，因為人群讓跟蹤者難以靠近。他事先寄給萊莎一本記錄送件經過的小冊子，但拒絕在家人安全前簽名。展場裡有一架荒誕的太空船模型、一具史前鳥類骨骼，以及一隻浸製章魚。孩子們笑著看展示品，信差阿瑞克卻緊盯入口，眉心皺出焦慮的紋路。他的恐懼不是裝出來的：兩名男子已連續多晚守在他家外頭。

Aric said he had been ordered to carry an imperial dispatch from Ormond's office to the court clerk. He had not read it, but a second delivery contained a sealed list of names. When he asked why the same officials were receiving duplicate papers, his supervisor warned him not to obsess over matters above his rank. Shortly afterward, his sister began to receive threatening messages. Seren recognized the pattern as harassment designed to oppress witnesses without openly arresting them. She offered Aric access to a protected residence and a lawyer of his own choosing. She did not make his cooperation a condition of safety; a witness was a person, not a tool the inquiry owned.

阿瑞克說，他奉命把奧蒙德辦公室的王室公文送給法庭書記。他沒有讀過內容，但第二趟送的是一份封存姓名清單。他問為何同一批官員要收到重複文件，主管便警告他別執著於超越自己階級的事。此後不久，他的妹妹開始收到威脅訊息。瑟蓮認出，這是透過騷擾壓迫證人、卻不必公開逮捕的手法。她提供阿瑞克受保護的住處，以及自行選擇律師的權利。她沒有把合作列為換取安全的條件；證人是人，不是調查小組擁有的工具。

At the inquiry, a government organizer objected that the courier's testimony would endanger the dignity of the crown. Lysa replied that the crown's stature could not depend on suppressing inconvenient facts. The panel agreed to hear Aric in a closed session with a public summary released afterward. He confirmed delivering the prepared warrant before the fire. He also remembered a peculiar detail: the dispatch box had a cellular lining made from layered wax chambers, designed to prevent moisture damage. A craftsman later identified the design as exclusive to Ormond's private office. The physical box could be compared with existing records, although its contents had already been delivered.

聽證時，一名政府組織者抗議，聲稱信差證詞將損害王室尊嚴。萊莎回答，王權地位不該依靠壓下不方便的事實維持。小組同意先閉門聆訊阿瑞克，再公布不涉及身分安全的摘要。他證實在火災前就送出準備好的逮捕令。他還記得奇特細節：公文箱有蜂巢狀的分層蠟內襯，用來防止受潮。一位工匠後來辨認，這種設計只有奧蒙德私人辦公室使用。實體箱子可與現有紀錄比對，雖然其中的文件已送達。

A palace spokesman tried to oblige Aric to answer questions about his family, but the panel refused. Those details were irrelevant. Seren thought of the folklore told to children in the northern villages: a ruler who could command every word would eventually lose the ability to hear truth. The story was simple, yet its moral felt urgent. During the questioning, Aric admitted that he had remained silent because his pay supported his sister's education. He was ashamed of his silence. Seren told him shame could not be erased by a single brave statement, but neither did it need to define his entire life. He had chosen to speak now, and the inquiry would assess what he actually knew.

一名王宮發言人企圖迫使阿瑞克回答家人的問題，小組卻拒絕了，因為那些細節無關。瑟蓮想起北方村落對孩子說的民間故事：一位能命令所有人說話的統治者，終究會失去聽見真相的能力。故事簡單，道理卻十分迫切。詢問過程中，阿瑞克承認過去保持沉默，是因為薪水支撐妹妹的教育。他為沉默感到羞愧。瑟蓮告訴他，一次勇敢發言無法抹去全部羞愧，但沉默也不必定義他整個人生。他如今選擇說話，調查只會評估他真正知道的事。

The exhibition director told Seren that a child's curiosity could survive even an imposing government. One school group had arrived from a poultry-farming district after a journey of surprising duration. Their teacher repeated an old proverb about courage: danger was not less real merely because a person chose to look at it clearly. Seren watched the children examine models of flying machines and thought of the peril Aric had faced while delivering ordinary letters. The public often celebrated heroic gestures, but it rarely noticed the quiet decisions that made honest work possible. She hoped the new inquiry would at least record those decisions accurately.

展覽館長告訴瑟蓮，即使政府威嚴逼人，孩子的好奇心仍能存活。一群學生從養禽地區遠道而來，旅程時間出人意料地長。老師引用一句古老諺語談勇氣：人選擇正視危險，不代表危險就不存在。瑟蓮看孩子觀察飛行機模型，想到阿瑞克送普通信件時面對的危險。公眾常歌頌英雄舉動，卻很少留意使誠實工作成為可能的安靜決定。她希望新的調查至少能把這些決定準確記錄下來。

Before leaving the exhibition, Seren saw a notice that a new public science school was being considered. Its courses would include weather observation, health, and the history of inventions. She hoped children there might learn to question confident explanations without fearing punishment. The case had taught her that no single witness, however sincere, should carry the entire weight of truth. Aric's account corroborated the timing of the warrant. It did not prove the fire had been intentional. Seren noted both facts, then returned to the capital with the strange relief of someone who no longer needed certainty at every stage to continue.

離開展場前，瑟蓮看到一則新公共科學學校的籌設公告。課程將包括氣象觀測、健康與發明史。她希望孩子能學著質疑自信的解釋，而不必害怕懲罰。案件讓她明白，再誠實的單一證人，也不該承擔全部真相的重量。阿瑞克的說法證實逮捕令時間線，卻尚不能證明火災出於蓄意。瑟蓮把兩件事都記下，帶著一種奇怪的輕鬆返回首都：她不再需要每一步都有確定答案，才敢繼續前進。
