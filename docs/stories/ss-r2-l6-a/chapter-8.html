The Swamp Certificate

反派的辯護人，決定先替自己翻案 · 第 8 / 25 章 · 5000–6000 · 英中對照 · 校訂中

In the eighth week of the royal calendar, the spring floods turned the provincial road into a swamp. Seren had only two days before her stay expired. A surveyor named Iven agreed to guide her to an abandoned pumping station, where engineers had once measured the mine's drainage. He was blond, wore a faded lieutenant's coat, and refused to be called heroic. “Most of my work involved counting pipes,” he said. He had spent his vocation teaching young mechanics and wished to retrieve a ledger that could certify the position of the old water channel. A physical record, he explained, was harder to alter than a map drawn in a distant office.

王曆第八週的春洪把省道變成一片沼澤。瑟蓮的延期只剩兩天。一位名叫艾文的測量員同意帶她們到廢棄抽水站，工程師曾在那裡測量礦場排水。他有一頭金髮，穿著褪色的中尉外套，拒絕被稱為英雄。「我的工作大部分是在數管線。」他說。他多年來以教導年輕技工為志業，想取回一本能證明舊水道位置的帳冊。他解釋，實物紀錄比遠方辦公室繪製的地圖更難修改。

At the pumping station, lotus leaves floated in a pond the color of weak tea. Workers offered refreshments before entering the flooded basement. Iven checked their qualifications: only people familiar with the machinery could enter the lower chamber. He handed Seren a helmet and ordered Rowan to remain above. The captain looked offended until Seren reminded him that strength did not replace specialized training. An accumulation of sediment blocked the first valve. The team had to exert steady pressure without breaking the old pipe. Through a narrow opening, Iven recovered a metal plate bearing the station's original clearance number and the inspector's seal.

抽水站旁的池塘漂著蓮葉，水色像淡茶。工人在眾人進入淹水地下室前提供茶點。艾文先檢查專業資格：只有熟悉機械的人能進入下層。他交給瑟蓮一頂安全帽，命令羅文留在上方。隊長一臉不服，瑟蓮提醒他，體力不能取代專業訓練。大量泥沙堵住第一個閥門，團隊必須穩定施力，又不能壓壞老舊管線。艾文從一個狹窄開口取出金屬牌，上面刻著抽水站最初的通行許可號碼與稽查員印章。

The plate contradicted the new survey, but it raised another question. Why had the mine's drainage schedule changed in the first place? Iven led them to a control board whose panels overlapped like scales. A marginal note in the old handbook instructed operators to deduct emergency pumping hours from a separate budget. That meant records of unusually heavy use could be hidden from the ordinary books. Seren asked Lysa to compare the figures with the academy's medical dates. The increase in pumping began three weeks before the fever outbreak. No one called the correlation proof of cause. Still, the schedule supplied another testable link in the investigation.

金屬牌與新測量圖矛盾，卻引出另一個問題：礦場排水時間表究竟為何改動？艾文帶眾人查看控制盤，層層面板像鱗片般重疊。舊手冊邊緣的註記指示操作員，把緊急抽水時數從另一份預算扣除。這表示異常大量的抽水紀錄可以從一般帳冊中被藏起來。瑟蓮請萊莎把數字與學院醫療紀錄的日期比較。抽水量增加，始於熱病爆發前三週。沒有人把相關性直接稱為因果證據，但時間表至少提供了另一個可檢驗的連結。

On the return journey, a customs clerk insisted that their metal plate was destined for the provincial museum rather than a court. Seren showed him the written clearance issued by the magistrate. The clerk examined its seal and allowed the party to pass, then quietly admitted that several witnesses had been told to stay away from the capital. Their families were being watched. Seren listened without demanding their names. Being literate did not guarantee freedom to speak; neither did a uniform guarantee authority. She wrote down the clerk's words, identified their status as unverified, and gave him directions to the independent inquiry if he ever chose to testify.

回程時，一名海關書記堅稱金屬牌應送往省立博物館，而非法庭。瑟蓮出示法官簽發的書面通行許可，書記檢查封印後准許通過，又低聲承認，有幾名證人被命令不得前往首都，他們的家人也受到監視。瑟蓮聽著，卻沒有逼問姓名。識字不保證一個人能自由說話，穿制服也不保證擁有正當權力。她記下書記的話，標示其尚未查證，並告訴他如果哪天願意作證，該如何聯絡獨立調查小組。

Iven also taught the group how to read the station's maintenance symbols. The old manual used three kinds of marks: a circle for routine work, a triangle for a temporary repair, and a square for an alteration requiring outside approval. Several entries carried squares but no signatures. Seren resisted the temptation to declare each one unlawful. The marks showed what the engineers had expected, not necessarily what had happened. She asked the panel to compare the symbols with invoices and testimony. A good investigation turned strange marks into questions that could be answered, rather than into mysterious symbols with whatever meaning the observer preferred.

艾文也教眾人閱讀抽水站的維修符號。舊手冊使用三種記號：圓圈表示例行工作、三角形表示臨時維修、方形表示需要外部核准的改動。好幾筆紀錄畫了方形，卻沒有簽名。瑟蓮忍住把每一筆都宣告違法的衝動。符號顯示工程師原本預期什麼，卻未必證明實際發生什麼。她請小組把記號與發票、證詞交叉比對。好的調查會把奇怪標記轉成能回答的問題，而不是任由觀察者賦予它們喜歡的神祕意義。

Back at the inn, Seren drank a bitter beverage while Iven explained the numbers again. Each record possessed only a limited value, yet together they had begun to reveal a systematic concealment. The court could not convict a person simply because the story looked persuasive. Seren believed the same standard should protect people she opposed. Rowan offered to call the plate their final answer; she refused. “We have recovered a fact, not the whole truth,” she said. In the courtyard the floodwater fell, leaving the first firm stones visible beneath the mud. The path ahead was still dangerous, but for once it was a path they could actually see.

回到旅館後，瑟蓮喝著苦澀飲料，聽艾文再次解釋數字。每份紀錄的證明力都有限，但合在一起，已逐漸顯示系統性的隱瞞。法庭不能只因故事聽起來有說服力就定某人的罪。瑟蓮相信，同樣的標準也必須保護她的對手。羅文提議把金屬牌當成最終答案，她拒絕了。「我們找回一項事實，不代表已經找到全部真相。」她說。庭院裡洪水正緩緩退去，泥濘下露出第一批堅固石階。前方仍危險，但這一次，路終於是他們看得見的路。
