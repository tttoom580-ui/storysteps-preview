珊瑚下的租約

The Lease Beneath the Coral

反派的辯護人，決定先替自己翻案 · 第 12 / 25 章 · 5000–6000 · 英中對照 · 校訂中

用互動版查詞、朗讀與收藏

The reservoir inspection brought the panel to the southern strait, where coral grew beneath the piers and a narrow brook reached the sea. The coastline looked majestic, but the harbor's beauty concealed a contract signed years earlier. A legislator had leased the inland pumping rights to the chancellor's brother, granting a private company access to public water. Seren examined the lease while Rowan checked the storage shed for missing cargo. Its expiration date was two years away; a sudden closure would deprive the fishing families of their main supply. The discovery made the investigation more difficult. Proving a corrupt agreement would not, by itself, keep the coast alive.

水庫檢查把調查小組帶到南方海峽。碼頭下長著珊瑚，一條窄溪流入海中。海岸景色壯麗，卻藏著一份多年前簽下的契約。一名議員把內陸抽水權租給宰相的兄弟，讓私人企業得以使用公共水源。瑟蓮查看租約，羅文則搜索倉庫尋找失蹤貨物。租約還有兩年才到期；若突然關閉設施，漁民家庭就會失去主要供水。發現使調查更加困難。即使能證明契約腐敗，也不代表海岸居民能因此活下去。

Dr. Halden joined them under a temporary identity and brought sealed medical records. A surgical nurse accompanied him to ensure that the patients' names stayed private. They had measured symptoms among children and older residents, including changes in eyesight and persistent fatigue. Halden insisted that the data did not prove a single cause. Nonetheless, the pattern gave the inquiry momentum. A petrol engine at the station had operated during hours officially listed as shutdowns. The supervisor had been instructed to rotate the inspection crew so no worker saw the complete schedule. Seren asked the mechanic to explain exactly who gave that instruction and when.

霍爾登醫師以暫時身分加入調查，帶來封存的醫療紀錄。一名外科護理師陪同，確保病患姓名保密。他們測量了兒童與長者的症狀，包括視力變化與持續疲倦。霍爾登堅持，數據並不能證明只有單一病因。儘管如此，這些模式仍為調查增添動力。抽水站的汽油引擎曾在官方列為停工的時段運轉。主管受命輪流更換檢查人員，讓任何工人都看不到完整時程。瑟蓮要求技工精確說明，是誰在什麼時候下達命令。

A delegation from the fishermen arrived with hearty food and bitter complaints. One father held his youngest offspring, a sleeping toddler, and asked whether the inquiry would strangle their trade. Seren could not promise that restrictions would be painless. She offered a practical alternative: temporary public pumps, an independent monitor, and compensation funded through a court-supervised account. An older woman asked why a monarch should care about fishermen at all. Seren answered that government authority was justified only when it protected people rather than merely collecting their labor. She could not make the crown adopt that principle, but she could place the question in the public record.

漁民代表帶著豐盛的食物和滿腹抱怨來了。一位父親抱著熟睡的幼兒，問調查是否會扼殺他們的生計。瑟蓮無法保證限制措施毫無痛苦，便提出實際替代方案：暫設公共水泵、獨立監測人員，以及由法院監督帳戶支付補償。一位年長婦人問，國王為何應該關心漁民。瑟蓮回答，政府的正當性應來自保護人民，而不只是徵收他們的勞動。她無法迫使王室接受這原則，卻能把問題正式記入公共紀錄。

The next morning the weather changed. Dark birds hovered above the water, and the narrow brook carried a strange oily film. A worker produced a maintenance page bearing two seals, one public and one private. The private seal had been pressed over an older signature. Lysa used angled light to elevate the hidden writing from the paper, revealing the name of the same legislator who had approved the lease. It was suspicious, not necessarily fraudulent. Seren asked the panel to examine the seal impressions before drawing conclusions. Their insistence on procedure irritated impatient supporters, but a rushed accusation would give the company exactly the excuse it needed to discredit the investigation.

隔天清晨天氣轉變。深色鳥群盤旋水面，窄溪漂著奇怪油膜。一名工人拿出維修紀錄，上面有兩枚印章，一枚公家的、一枚私人的。私人印章壓在較舊的簽名上。萊莎利用斜射光線讓隱藏字跡浮現，發現那正是批准租約的議員姓名。情況可疑，卻未必已構成詐欺。瑟蓮請小組在下結論前先檢查印章壓痕。她們堅持程序，讓急躁的支持者十分不滿；但倉促指控只會送給公司一個抹黑調查的理由。

The fishermen asked that compensation be reviewed monthly, rather than paid once and forgotten. Seren agreed that a continuing problem required continuing oversight. Lysa drafted an appeal procedure for families whose applications were rejected. It listed the office responsible, the evidence required, and a deadline for receiving an answer. The agreement seemed technical compared with the question of who had set the archive on fire. Yet Seren had learned to value such details. A legal victory that could not be used by an exhausted parent or a busy worker would offer little relief outside the courtroom.

漁民要求補償金每月檢討，而不是支付一次後就被忘記。瑟蓮同意，持續存在的問題需要持續監督。萊莎替申請遭駁回的家庭草擬申訴程序，列出承辦單位、所需證據與答覆期限。和「誰放火燒檔案庫」相比，這份協議顯得技術性十足。然而瑟蓮已學會重視這些細節。如果疲累的家長或忙碌的工人無法實際使用一項法律勝利，那麼法庭外的人也得不到多少幫助。

When they returned inland, the fishermen sent a statement offering cooperation if their water access was protected. It was not a grand victory, only a workable agreement between people with different fears. Seren placed it beside the disputed lease. The kingdom's old laws were like the brook: narrow in places, blocked in others, but capable of carrying something valuable if carefully maintained. In the palace, meanwhile, Ormond demanded that the panel release its findings immediately. He claimed the delay harmed public confidence. Seren suspected a different reason. The more the investigators compared physical evidence with financial records, the fewer places remained for hidden agreements to survive.

回到內陸時，漁民送來一份聲明：若能保障取水權，他們願意合作。這不是驚天動地的勝利，只是不同恐懼之間達成的可行協議。瑟蓮把它放在有爭議的租約旁。王國舊法就像那條溪流，有些地方狹窄、有些地方堵塞，但只要仔細維護，仍能承載重要的東西。與此同時，奧蒙德在王宮要求小組立刻發布結論，聲稱拖延有害公信。瑟蓮懷疑真正原因不同：調查者越是比對物證與財務紀錄，秘密交易就越難藏身。
