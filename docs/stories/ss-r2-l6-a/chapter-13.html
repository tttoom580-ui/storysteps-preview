會唱歌的清單

The Inventory That Sang

反派的辯護人，決定先替自己翻案 · 第 13 / 25 章 · 5000–6000 · 英中對照 · 校訂中

The inquiry's accounting team found a discrepancy that looked like ordinary algebra. Twelve crates had been entered as outgoing goods, but the corresponding inventory showed only eleven departures. A junior clerk suggested a missing box had simply been overlooked. Seren asked for a discreet comparison of the ledgers rather than a public allegation. The records had been prepared at dusk on the day the orphanage received its delivery. One entry was written in pale blue ink, unlike the others. A reference encyclopedia identified the ink as common to legal offices, not freight companies. Such a difference could result from legitimate correction, but the missing approval signature made it worth examining.

調查小組的會計團隊發現一處看起來只是普通代數問題的差異。十二個貨箱記為出貨，但相對應的清單只有十一筆離站紀錄。一名初級書記認為只是漏記一箱。瑟蓮要求私下比對帳冊，不要公開指控。這些紀錄是在孤兒院收到貨物當天的傍晚整理的。其中一筆用淡藍色墨水寫成，與其他不同。一本參考百科指出，那種墨水常見於法律辦公室，而非貨運公司。差異可能來自合法更正，但缺少核准簽名，使它值得仔細檢查。

Ada examined the packing material. Inside an old suitcase she found a cloth dressing used to protect fragile laboratory glass. Its thread matched the orphanage's discarded liner. The finding could not identify a suspect, but it connected the documents to a physical object. Lysa began to conserve every small fragment, labeling its discovery and storage. She had once dismissed such work as tiresome, but Seren reminded her that a future court would ask not just what an object showed, but who had handled it. Each recorded step restrained the investigators' imagination. The more complete their evidence became, the less they needed a dramatic explanation.

艾達檢查包裝材料，在一只舊手提箱內找到保護脆弱實驗室玻璃器材的布襯。布線與孤兒院丟棄的內襯相符。這不能辨認嫌犯，卻把文件與實物連接起來。萊莎開始保存每一小片碎料，標註發現地點與保存方式。她曾覺得這工作煩瑣，瑟蓮卻提醒她，未來法庭要問的不只是物品顯示什麼，還包括誰經手過它。每一項紀錄都約束調查者的想像。證據越完整，就越不必依賴戲劇性的解釋。

At the orphanage, a visiting sportswoman organized a simple race for the children. She insisted on sportsmanship even when one team outnumbered the other. Seren watched from the gate, uncertain whether she should intrude on their pleasure. A little girl recognized her and began singing a lullaby that had haunted the missing doctor's dreams. Halden stopped walking. He said his daughter had learned the same tune from a nurse who worked in Ormond's residence. The words used antonyms—light and dark, lost and found—in a pattern that matched numbers on the shipping slips. Seren refused to declare the song a cipher merely because the coincidence was striking.

孤兒院裡，一位來訪的女運動員替孩子舉辦簡單的賽跑。即使一組人數比另一組多，她仍堅持運動精神。瑟蓮站在門口看，不確定自己是否該打擾他們的快樂。一名小女孩認出她，唱起一首讓失蹤醫師夢裡也難以忘懷的搖籃曲。霍爾登停下腳步，說他的女兒曾向宰相宅邸的護士學過同樣曲調。歌詞使用明與暗、失與得等反義詞，其節奏竟與運貨單數字排列相近。瑟蓮拒絕只因巧合顯眼，就宣告歌曲必然是密碼。

The doctor explained that his daughter had hidden among the patients after a palace servant warned her of danger. Her name was Evi. Seren's tentative plan was to locate the nurse and ask whether she had helped Evi escape. Rowan pointed out a more immediate issue: someone at the palace already knew that the doctor possessed the original report. Their opponents might be farther ahead than they supposed. Lysa reviewed the outgoing correspondence and found that a messenger had delivered a sealed notice to Ormond on the same day Halden disappeared. The external envelope was intact, but its contents were unknown. They could not infer guilt from the envelope alone.

醫師解釋，他女兒艾薇曾在一名宮廷僕役警告危險後，躲在病患之中。瑟蓮初步打算尋找那名護士，問她是否幫助艾薇逃走。羅文指出一個更迫切的問題：王宮裡已有人知道醫師持有原始報告。對手的進度也許比他們想的更快。萊莎查看寄出的信件，發現霍爾登失蹤當天，有信差把封口通知送給奧蒙德。信封外觀完整，內容卻不明。她們不能只憑信封推斷有罪。

Seren noticed the indifference with which one official regarded the orphanage's testimony. He called the children unreliable because they had confused dates during earlier interviews. She asked whether the same standard would be applied to nobles who gave inconsistent answers. The official could not explain the difference. Lysa proposed a better method: ask each child only about events personally observed, avoid leading questions, and compare their accounts with independent documents. The aim was neither to disbelieve children automatically nor to treat every memory as exact. It was to make room for truthful testimony without demanding impossible precision from frightened witnesses.

瑟蓮注意到，一名官員冷漠地看待孤兒院證詞。他說孩子早先訪談混淆日期，因此不可靠。她問，若貴族回答前後不一，是否也採同樣標準。官員無法解釋差別。萊莎提出更好的方法：只問孩子親眼所見之事，避免誘導性問題，再把說法和獨立文件比較。目的既不是自動不相信孩子，也不是把每段記憶當成絕對精準，而是讓害怕的證人有機會誠實說話，不必被要求達到不可能的精確度。

That night Seren made a chart with two columns: what the missing box might contain, and what the known evidence actually established. The first column multiplied every fear; the second remained stubbornly narrow. The inquiry was underway, not finished. An outward appearance of certainty would satisfy the crowd for an evening but might destroy the case later. Seren asked Lysa to mark the song as a possible lead rather than evidence and to safeguard the physical packing fragments. Somewhere between a clerk's calculations and a child's lullaby, someone had tried to hide a truth. Finding it would require patience and a willingness to be wrong.

當晚瑟蓮畫出兩欄表格：失蹤箱子可能裝了什麼，以及現有證據實際證明了什麼。第一欄讓恐懼不斷增生，第二欄卻固執地保持狹窄。調查已展開，卻尚未結束。表面上的確定感能讓群眾滿足一晚，卻可能日後毀了案件。瑟蓮請萊莎把歌曲列為可能線索，而非證據，並妥善保存實體包裝碎片。書記的計算與孩子的搖籃曲之間，有人試圖藏起真相。要找到它，需要耐心，以及承認自己可能猜錯的意願。
