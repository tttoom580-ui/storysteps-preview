洗得太乾淨的房間

A Room Washed Too Clean

反派的辯護人，決定先替自己翻案 · 第 4 / 25 章 · 5000–6000 · 英中對照

The shipping ticket led to a boarding house near the harbor. Its landlady, Mrs. Fern, had rented three rooms to men who called themselves royal inspectors. Now the upper floor smelled sharply of bleach. The woman claimed she had cleaned it after her tenants left, but Seren noticed that the wooden dresser was wet inside while the window frame remained dusty. This was not comprehensive cleaning; someone had tried to destroy particular stains or marks. Mrs. Fern's familiarity with the building was obvious. So was her terror. She spoke in hurried sentences and avoided looking at Rowan's badge. Seren dismissed the guards from the room before asking another question.

運貨單指向港口附近的一間寄宿公寓。房東費恩太太曾把三間房出租給自稱皇家稽查員的男子。如今樓上充滿刺鼻的漂白水氣味。她聲稱房客離開後自己清洗過房間，瑟蓮卻發現木製五斗櫃內側仍濕，窗框卻積著灰。這不是全面清潔；有人只想毀去特定污漬或記號。費恩太太顯然非常熟悉這棟房子，也顯然十分恐懼。她說話急促，不敢看羅文的徽章。瑟蓮先請衛兵離開，才繼續提問。

Mrs. Fern said her brother, the harbor superintendent, had ordered her to say nothing. In the kingdom's strict hierarchy, a superintendent could threaten a small business without issuing a written command. The men had paid generously for their rooms, then demanded access to the cellar at night. One evening she had begun to overhear their argument about an inventory that would not balance. She remembered the phrase “second copy,” but she had never seen the document. Seren explained that she would not publish the woman's name without permission. That promise could not guarantee safety, yet it gave Mrs. Fern a choice the authorities had denied her.

費恩太太說，她的兄長——港務總管——命令她不得說話。在王國嚴格的階級制度下，總管不必下達書面命令，也能威脅小本生意。那些男子付了很高的房租，接著要求夜裡自由進出地下室。有一天晚上，她無意間聽到他們為一份無法對平的物資清單爭吵。她記得「第二份副本」這幾個字，卻從未親眼看見文件。瑟蓮向她解釋，沒有得到許可，就不會公開她的姓名。這項承諾不能保證安全，卻至少給了她當局從未提供的選擇。

The cellar overlooked a lush courtyard where vines climbed the stone wall. A seagull cried above the water. Ada knelt beside a drainage channel and discovered a strip of dark paper caught in the grate. The ink had run because the pipe sometimes overflowed, but its embossed edge remained intact. On one side was a symbol from the royal archive; on the other, the impression of a seal used by Chancellor Ormond's office. Lysa wanted to call it a decisive clue. Seren would not. They needed a credible account of how the paper came there, and no one had witnessed its disposal. The distinction between a strong suspicion and proof could save them from another false conviction.

地下室望向一座藤蔓攀著石牆、植物茂盛的庭院；一隻海鷗在水面上方叫著。艾達跪在排水溝旁，從鐵柵裡發現一片被卡住的深色紙張。管線偶爾溢水，墨跡因此暈開，但紙緣的浮雕仍完整。一面印著皇家檔案庫標誌，另一面有奧蒙德宰相辦公室所用封印的壓痕。萊莎想稱它為決定性線索，瑟蓮卻不肯。她們還需要可靠的說法，解釋紙張如何出現在那裡；目前沒有人親眼見到它被丟棄。將強烈懷疑與證據區分開來，才能避免製造另一樁冤案。

In an upstairs room, they found an abandoned thermometer and a music program. The program announced a symphony performed for the winter memorial, when the city had gathered to mourn victims of the fever. Its date was the eighth, the day before the stolen shipment passed through the orphanage. A handwritten appointment on the back named the superintendent. Rowan initially dismissed the program as harmless. Ada asked why a public health inspector would carry a music program to a secret storage room. He had no answer. Seren praised Ada's dedication; her practical knowledge of fabrics, seams, and ordinary household habits often revealed more than the grand theories of officials.

樓上的房間還留著一支被丟棄的溫度計和一張音樂會節目單。節目單記載一場紀念冬季熱病死者的交響樂演奏，當天全城曾聚集哀悼。日期是八日，正是被盜貨箱經過孤兒院的前一天。節目單背面手寫的約見對象是港務總管。羅文起初認為那張紙無關緊要。艾達卻問：一名公共衛生稽查員，為何把音樂會節目單帶到秘密倉庫？他無法回答。瑟蓮稱讚艾達的投入；她對布料、縫線與日常家務的實際知識，往往比官員的宏大理論更能指出問題。

Outside, the harbor was in turmoil. Workers feared that another outbreak would close the docks, and sellers of supposedly wholesome food were raising prices. Seren asked the landlady whether she had tried to nurture any of the sick tenants. Mrs. Fern admitted she had carried soup to one young clerk and heard him crying over the missing inventory. His name was Tomas. He had since disappeared. Seren could not refute the official story by collecting rumors; she needed Tomas or a reliable record of his departure. She asked Rowan to search employment logs rather than threaten the frightened woman. The captain complied, although his expression suggested that he was reconsidering the methods he had been taught.

屋外的港口陷入騷動。工人害怕再次爆發疫病、碼頭被迫封閉，標榜健康營養的食物也趁機漲價。瑟蓮詢問房東是否照顧過生病的房客。費恩太太承認，她曾替一位年輕書記送湯，聽見他為失蹤的清單而哭泣。他名叫托馬斯，後來就失蹤了。瑟蓮不能只靠收集傳聞推翻官方故事；她需要找到托馬斯，或取得可靠的離職紀錄。她要羅文去查雇用紀錄，而不是威嚇這位害怕的婦人。隊長照辦了，雖然他的表情顯示，他開始反思自己受過的辦案訓練。

That night Seren returned to her guarded lodgings and spread the documents across the table. A blank space connected the mountain ledger, the orphanage receipt, and the boarding-house paper. Lysa feared that the gaps were too large. Seren agreed, then marked every uncertainty in red instead of hiding it. “We cannot refute a false story with a prettier false story,” she said. Mrs. Fern had asked them for help, not revenge. Elen had demanded respect for the mountain families. Mother Pell wanted medicine for her children. These people deserved more than a dramatic verdict. They deserved a process that would survive careful examination, even by someone who disliked Seren.

當晚瑟蓮回到受監視的住處，把文件鋪滿桌面。山區登記簿、孤兒院收據與寄宿公寓的紙片之間，仍有一大段空白。萊莎擔心缺口太大，瑟蓮同意，然後用紅筆標出每個不確定之處，而不是把它藏起來。「我們不能用一個更漂亮的謊言，反駁另一個謊言。」她說。費恩太太求的是幫助，不是報仇；艾蓮要求尊重山區家庭；佩爾嬤嬤希望孩子能得到藥品。這些人值得的不只是一場戲劇性的宣判，而是一套即使被討厭瑟蓮的人嚴格檢驗，也站得住腳的程序。
