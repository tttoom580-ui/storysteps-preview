礦工的往返信件

The Correspondence of the Miners

反派的辯護人，決定先替自己翻案 · 第 17 / 25 章 · 5000–6000 · 英中對照 · 校訂中

A boycott began at the mine when workers learned that the company had withheld safety reports. Their organizer asked Seren to address the crowd. She refused to direct their protest, offering instead to explain what the independent inquiry could and could not establish. An analytical report was being prepared, but its disclosure would require medical privacy safeguards. The workers wanted the mine to remain open under safe conditions; the owners wanted a lucrative contract to continue without interruption. Seren was asked to wrestle with an uncomfortable truth: the same decision could protect one family and threaten another. No slogan would solve that conflict.

礦工得知公司扣住安全報告後，開始發起抵制。組織者請瑟蓮向群眾演說。她拒絕指揮抗議，僅願意說明獨立調查能與不能證明的事項。一份分析報告正在準備，但公開前必須先保障醫療隱私。工人希望礦場在安全條件下繼續運作，業主則想讓有利可圖的契約不受干擾。瑟蓮被迫面對難題：同一個決定，可能保護一個家庭，也威脅另一個家庭。沒有任何口號能解決這種衝突。

Lysa reviewed years of correspondence between miners and management. Complaints about hygiene and ventilation appeared long before the fever. Many letters had received only a token acknowledgment. One father wrote that his son's illness was a preventable casualty of unsafe work. A company executive replied with a formal note of consolation while denying responsibility. Seren read the exchange twice. The father's anger could not prove a legal claim, but it showed that management had received warnings. She asked the archive team to preserve envelopes and dates, not merely quotations. A reliable history required the sequence of communication, including replies that seemed empty.

萊莎檢視礦工與管理層多年來的往返信件。關於衛生與通風的抱怨，遠在熱病前就已出現。許多信只收到象徵性的回覆。一名父親寫道，兒子的疾病是工作環境不安全造成、原可避免的傷害。企業主管則以正式慰問函回覆，同時否認責任。瑟蓮把信讀了兩遍。父親的憤怒不能直接證明法律主張，卻顯示管理層確實收到過警告。她要求檔案小組保存信封與日期，而非只摘錄句子。可靠的歷史需要完整溝通順序，包括看似空洞的回覆。

At a public clinic, a nurse tried to kindle interest in a free health program. Children sneezed in the corridor while parents waited for checkups. The clinic's layout was cramped, making privacy difficult. Seren helped move screens and chairs, then listened as a physician described the difference between medical screening and evidence of workplace exposure. A child shrieked when a thermometer slipped from his hand. The noise startled everyone, but the nurse calmed him without criticism. Seren wondered how often political debate turned ordinary frightened people into symbols. The answer was probably too often. She promised to keep the clinic's records separate from public speeches.

在公共診所，一名護士試圖提高民眾參與免費健康計畫的興趣。孩子在走廊打噴嚏，家長等著檢查。診所格局狹窄，難以維持隱私。瑟蓮協助搬動屏風與椅子，接著聽醫師解釋醫療篩檢與職場暴露證據的差別。一個孩子手中的溫度計滑落，嚇得尖叫，眾人都吃了一驚，護士卻溫和安撫。瑟蓮想，政治辯論究竟多常把普通又害怕的人變成象徵？恐怕太常。她承諾把診所紀錄與公開演說分開。

The investigation faced a tiresome delay when a company clerk failed to submit a required map. He claimed to have flunked a technical qualification test years earlier and not to understand the drawing. Iven offered to explain the symbols. Once the clerk understood them, he pointed to an overlooked column recording underground maintenance. The data showed that officials had known about repeated leaks. The pattern was not eternal or mysterious; it was the result of a layout that obscured important information from nonspecialists. Seren asked for a new public form that placed safety notices where workers would actually see them. Prevention required communication as much as machinery.

一家公司的書記未提交必要地圖，讓調查遇到煩人的拖延。他說自己多年前技術資格測驗不及格，看不懂圖。艾文主動解釋符號。書記理解後，指出一欄被忽略的地下維修紀錄。資料顯示官員早已知道多次漏水。這個模式既非永恆也不神祕，只是表格布局讓非專業人士看不見重要資訊。瑟蓮要求重新設計公共表格，讓安全通知放在工人真正能看到的地方。預防需要良好溝通，不只需要機器。

The clinic director hoped the new program would cultivate trust between workers and medical staff. She rejected promises of perfect health for eternity; medicine could offer care and preventive measures, not immortality. A student asked whether public notices could provide stimulation for greater participation. The director said they could, if they were accurate and respectful rather than alarming. Seren appreciated the distinction. The same facts could be presented as a warning, an invitation, or a threat. A government committed to public welfare should choose clarity over fear. That principle was difficult to maintain, especially when frightened citizens demanded immediate certainty.

診所主任希望新計畫能培養工人與醫護之間的信任。她拒絕保證永遠健康；醫療能提供照護與預防，卻不能讓人不死。一名學生問，公共公告能否刺激更多人參與。主任說可以，前提是內容準確、尊重人，而不是故意嚇人。瑟蓮欣賞這個區分。同樣的事實可以被說成警告、邀請，或威脅。致力公共福祉的政府，應選擇清楚而非恐懼。維持這原則並不容易，尤其是當害怕的市民要求立刻得到確定答案時。

When the boycott ended, the workers secured an independent safety representative and a timetable for repairs. The owners received permission for limited operations, subject to inspection. Neither side was satisfied, but the immediate danger was reduced. Seren kept the disputed letters in the public evidence vault, with private addresses concealed. Some correspondents had shown courage, others hesitation, and others outward obedience. Their differences mattered. She would not flatten them into heroes and villains just to make the case easier to tell. The book she remembered had treated suffering as fuel for a final confrontation; the living people around her needed quieter, more durable protections.

抵制結束後，工人爭取到獨立安全代表，以及維修時程。業主則得到有限營運許可，條件是接受檢查。雙方都不完全滿意，但眼前危險降低了。瑟蓮把有爭議的信件存入公共證據庫，同時遮去私人住址。寫信者有人勇敢、有人猶豫，也有人表面上服從。這些差異很重要。她不願只為讓故事容易講述，就把所有人壓平為英雄與反派。她記得的小說把痛苦當成最終對決的燃料，眼前活著的人卻需要更安靜、更持久的保障。
