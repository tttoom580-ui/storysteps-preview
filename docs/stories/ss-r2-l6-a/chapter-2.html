山口的寡婦

The Widow at the Mountain Crossing

反派的辯護人，決定先替自己翻案 · 第 2 / 25 章 · 5000–6000 · 英中對照

The north junction lay above the city, where an old stone crossing joined the mountain road. Seren traveled there under guard with Lysa, Ada, and Captain Rowan, the officer assigned to prevent her escape. As the carriage gained altitude, terraces of pale grain replaced the capital's crowded roofs. Rowan explained that the mountain's aboriginal communities had maintained the roads long before the monarchy existed. Their names had been discarded from official maps after an uprising thirty years earlier. To Seren, that omission mattered. Whoever controlled a map could decide which people seemed to belong to the kingdom, and which could be blamed without anyone asking questions.

北方交叉路口位在城區上方，古老的石頭通道連接著山路。瑟蓮在監視下與萊莎、艾達及奉命防止她逃跑的羅文隊長同行。馬車逐漸爬升，山坡層層穀田取代了首都擁擠的屋頂。羅文解釋，山區原住民族群遠在王權建立之前就維護著道路。三十年前一場起義之後，他們的地名被官方地圖刪去了。瑟蓮覺得這件事很重要：控制地圖的人，也能決定誰被視為王國的一員，誰又可以在無人質疑下被怪罪。

At the crossing, a widow named Elen operated a tiny registry office. She did not rise when Rowan entered. Her annoyance showed in the blunt way she asked him to wipe his boots. “My husband was discharged from the service for refusing to align a false inspection report with the minister's version,” she said. “I will not repeat his mistake by trusting a uniform.” Rowan kept a respectful posture and offered his credentials. Seren introduced herself without hiding her identity. Elen stared at her, then opened the registry door. “If you have come for the truth,” the widow said, “you will need more than an impressive escort.”

在山口，一位名叫艾蓮的寡婦經營小小的登記處。羅文進門時，她連起身都沒有，只是直截了當地叫他擦乾淨靴子，臉上的不耐十分明顯。「我丈夫拒絕把虛假的檢查報告改得符合大臣版本，因此被革職。」她說，「我不會再犯他那種相信制服的錯誤。」羅文端正站好，恭敬出示證件。瑟蓮則沒有隱瞞自己的身分。艾蓮盯著她看了一會兒，終於打開登記處的門。「如果你們是來找真相的，」寡婦說，「光靠威風的護衛可不夠。」

Registers abound in the office: toll payments, cart weights, bridge repairs, and the names of travelers. Elen had maintained them with remarkable precision. Her rigorous method required a second witness to sign every correction. The earliest entry for the disputed shipment described twelve sealed boxes traveling uphill before the fire. A later page claimed that the same boxes had been returned to the royal archive, but its signature was missing. This was the first substantial hurdle in the prosecution's account. Lysa copied the numbers and made Seren check them independently. “A versatile mind is useful,” she said, “but an accurate copy is more useful in court.”

這間辦公室裡有數不清的登記簿：過路費、車輛重量、橋樑維修、旅客名單，樣樣都有。艾蓮以驚人的精確度保存它們。她嚴格的工作方法要求每一處更正都有第二位證人簽名。有爭議的運貨紀錄最早一筆寫著：火災之前，有十二個封箱被運往山上。後來的一頁卻聲稱，同一批箱子已送回皇家檔案庫，但那頁缺少簽名。這是檢方說法遇到的第一個重大障礙。萊莎抄下數字，又請瑟蓮獨立核對。「靈活的頭腦很有用，」她說，「但在法庭上，一份準確的副本更有用。」

Elen ushered them onto a terrace overlooking the road. She pointed toward a ruined checkpoint. During the uprising, the council had imposed a veto on grain shipments to the hills. The restriction was never formally lifted, although merchants continued to use the road through private arrangements. “That gives anyone an excuse to call a delivery illegal,” she said. Seren understood the trap: if the shipment was admitted, the authorities could accuse the mountain people of smuggling; if it was denied, the missing boxes remained a mystery. The others wanted to move quickly, but Seren's endeavor was no longer merely to escape punishment. She would not save herself by allowing another community to be sacrificed.

艾蓮帶眾人走到俯瞰山路的梯田邊，指向一座毀壞的檢查站。起義期間，議會曾否決向山區運送糧食的申請；那項限制從未正式解除，商人卻依靠私下協議繼續通行。「因此任何人都能找藉口，說某一批貨物違法。」她說。瑟蓮明白這個陷阱：若承認貨物存在，當局就能指控山民走私；若否認，失蹤箱子的謎團就無從解開。其他人想趕快行動，但瑟蓮的努力已不再只是逃避刑罰。她不願藉犧牲另一個族群來拯救自己。

Before departing, Seren took a final look at Elen's office. Its records were modest, but they had outlasted changes in governors and official maps. She asked permission to have the oldest pages copied by an independent clerk, with the originals remaining in the village. Elen agreed only after the terms were written and signed. The arrangement taught Seren a practical lesson: evidence was strongest when the people who kept it did not have to surrender control to those who wanted to use it. Rowan signed as a witness without complaint, an act that made Elen regard him with slightly less suspicion.

離開前，瑟蓮最後望了艾蓮的辦公室一眼。紀錄雖樸實，卻比好幾任總督與官方地圖活得更久。她徵求許可，希望由獨立書記複製最舊的頁面，原件仍留在村裡。艾蓮要求條款寫清楚、簽好名才答應。這次安排教會瑟蓮一項務實原則：保管證據的人不必把控制權交給想使用證據的人時，證據反而更可靠。羅文毫無怨言地簽名作證，讓艾蓮對他的戒心稍微降低。

That evening Rowan discovered fresh wheel marks beside the ruined checkpoint. A recent repair had strengthened the bridge, though no public fund had paid for it. Elen produced the private repair order, written two days before the fire and signed with the initials O.M. Seren recognized them as those of Chancellor Ormond, the official who had encouraged her prosecution. The evidence did not establish his guilt, and she said so. Lysa approved her caution. They could not announce a conspiracy simply because the details aligned. They would need proof of who had authorized the shipment and where the boxes had gone. Still, Seren finally possessed something the novel had denied her: a direction in which to search.

那晚，羅文在廢棄檢查站旁發現新的車輪痕跡。橋梁近期曾被加固，卻沒有任何公共經費支付這筆費用。艾蓮拿出私人維修命令，簽發日期在火災前兩天，署名縮寫是 O.M.。瑟蓮認出，那是曾積極推動她被起訴的奧蒙德宰相的姓名縮寫。但她也指出，這還不能證明宰相有罪。萊莎贊同她的謹慎。她們不能只因細節相符，就直接宣布有陰謀；還必須證明是誰批准運送、箱子最終去了哪裡。然而，瑟蓮終於擁有原小說從未給過她的東西：一個可以繼續調查的方向。
