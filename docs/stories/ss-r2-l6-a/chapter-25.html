反派的辯護人，決定先替自己翻案 · 第 25 / 25 章 · 5000–6000 · 英中對照 · 校訂中

A year later, the former villainess opened a legal aid office beside the harbor. It occupied two rooms above a hostel for visiting witnesses, with a ramp for people who walked on a crutch. Her first apprentice was a young woman who had once been refused entry to the courts. The office's backbone was not a wealthy patron, but a transparent treasury grant and a public register of donations. Lysa helped compute its budget and publish the remainder at the end of every month. Amid the sound of printing presses, Seren felt a vibration through the floorboards. It reminded her of the night the old story had nearly ended, and of how much had changed since then.

一年後，昔日的反派千金在港口旁開了一間法律扶助辦公室。它設在接待外地證人的旅舍樓上，有兩間房，還設置供拄拐杖者使用的坡道。第一位學徒是過去曾被法院拒絕入內的年輕女子。辦公室的支柱不是有錢贊助人，而是一筆公開透明的國庫補助，以及捐款公開登記簿。萊莎協助計算預算，每個月底公布餘額。印刷機的聲響中，瑟蓮感覺地板傳來震動，想起舊故事差點結束的那一晚，以及此後改變的一切。

The trials of Ormond and the contractors were still proceeding through successive hearings. Several officials faced lawful detention, while others had been cleared after investigation. Seren refused demands that she personally choose their punishments. Some angry citizens had threatened to assassinate witnesses who changed their statements, and her office publicly condemned those threats. No one's testimony should become a lethal contest between rival crowds. The Rule of Three now included a new requirement that accused people receive copies of the evidence against them. The amendment had taken months of debate, but it ensured that no future court could abbreviate a person's name and hide the records used to condemn them.

奧蒙德與承包商的審判仍在一連串聽證中進行。有些官員依法接受羈押，另一些人在調查後獲釐清。瑟蓮拒絕那些要求她親自決定刑罰的聲音。有些憤怒市民曾威脅暗殺改變證詞的證人，她的辦公室公開譴責這些威脅。證詞不該變成敵對群眾間的致命競賽。三證法則如今加入新要求：被告必須取得對自己不利證據的副本。修法經過數月辯論，卻確保未來沒有法庭能只縮寫一個人的名字，再把用來定罪的紀錄藏起來。

Mother Pell visited with Evi and the children from the orphanage. One boy brought a small lizard in a wooden box and asked whether it could become the office mascot. Seren laughed and said they would need a safe enclosure and someone knowledgeable about its care. Evi, now studying at the new science school, showed the apprentice a lesson about vowels and consonants. She remembered how a single altered letter had helped locate the missing shipment. The lesson did not make her a permanent witness; she was free to dwell on other interests. Seren was pleased when Evi said she wanted to study rivers, not courtroom arguments.

佩爾嬤嬤帶著艾薇和孤兒院孩子來訪。一名男孩拿著木盒裡的小蜥蜴，問能否當辦公室吉祥物。瑟蓮笑著說需要安全籠舍，還得找懂得照護的人。如今在新科學學校上課的艾薇，向學徒展示母音與子音的課程。她記得，一個被修改的字母曾幫助大家找到失蹤貨物。但這堂課不代表她永遠必須當證人；她可以專注其他興趣。艾薇說自己想研究河流而非法庭辯論時，瑟蓮格外高興。

One afternoon Seren found the old novel's final page in a bundle of records salvaged from her former room. It described her corpse as the necessary price of peace. She stared at the sentence in stunned silence. The original Mira had once regarded it as a weak ending; the woman she had become regarded it as a warning about stories that treated a person as expendable. She did not know whether she would ever return to the world where she had edited that book. She did know she was living here, among people whose choices could not be reduced to a chapter outline. She closed the page and placed it in an archive box marked “unverified fiction.”

某天下午，瑟蓮在舊房間搶救出的文件裡找到原小說的最後一頁。那上面寫著，她的屍體是和平必要的代價。她驚愕地凝視句子。過去作為編輯的米拉只覺得那是個薄弱結局；如今的瑟蓮卻把它視為警告：有些故事把人當成可以被犧牲的道具。她不知道自己是否會回到編輯小說的原世界，但知道現在真實地活在這裡，身邊的人無法被縮減成章節提綱。她闔上書頁，把它放進標著「未經證實的虛構故事」的檔案盒。

Her apprentice asked one final question before closing the office: why did Seren still preserve documents that reminded her of nearly being executed? Seren answered that forgetting was sometimes easier than learning, but a public record existed to protect people who would come afterward. No one should be required to relive humiliation for another person's entertainment. The archive would preserve facts with care, restricting private details while keeping government action accountable. She gave the apprentice the key and trusted her to lock the door. Then they walked down to the harbor, where the lamps reflected on water that had finally become safe to drink.

關門前，學徒最後問了一個問題：為什麼瑟蓮仍保存差點被處刑的相關文件？瑟蓮回答，有時遺忘比學習更容易，但公共紀錄存在，是為了保護後來的人。沒有人應被迫重溫羞辱，供別人娛樂。檔案將謹慎保存事實，在保護私人細節的同時，讓政府行動保持可追責。她把鑰匙交給學徒，相信對方能好好鎖門。接著兩人走向港口，燈火映在終於能安全飲用的水面上。

That evening a visitor arrived frantic about an impending hearing. His brother had been accused of theft and had never seen the evidence. Seren listened, then asked the apprentice to prepare a request for disclosure. Outside, the harbor lamps glowed in anticipation of the night market. A bell rang; a distant machine gave a soft vibration; children laughed on the stairs. Seren felt no need to predict the outcome. She knew only the work that lay before her: ask clear questions, protect those whose voices were ignored, admit what remained unknown, and keep the records available. There would be no execution at the end of this story. There would be tomorrow, and people free to write what came after.

那天傍晚，一名訪客慌張上門，說兄弟即將受審，卻從未看過對自己不利的證據。瑟蓮耐心聆聽，接著請學徒準備證據開示申請。屋外，港口燈火亮起，夜市即將開張；鐘聲響起，遠方機器傳來柔和震動，孩子在樓梯上笑著。瑟蓮不再需要預測結果。她只知道眼前的工作：提出清楚問題、保護聲音被忽視的人、承認尚未知曉的事，並讓紀錄保持可查。這個故事的結尾不會有處刑；它會有明天，也會有人自由寫下之後的篇章。
