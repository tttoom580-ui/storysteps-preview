Letters on Walnut Paper

反派的辯護人，決定先替自己翻案 · 第 16 / 25 章 · 5000–6000 · 英中對照 · 校訂中

After the public vote, Seren visited the workshop chosen to manufacture water filters. A porter carried cartons of sand and charcoal through a courtyard where walnut trees shaded the benches. The manager displayed samples on thick cardboard and explained how each layer could purify contaminated water. The process was not miraculous. It required maintenance, testing, and honest reports when a filter failed. The factory also catered to schools, which meant its workers understood the dangers of shortcuts. Seren asked the manager to publish clear instructions rather than promise an effortless cure. The kingdom had suffered enough from beautiful promises that concealed poor engineering.

公開表決之後，瑟蓮拜訪被選中製造濾水器的工坊。一名搬運工把裝著沙與木炭的紙箱運過庭院，胡桃木在長椅上投下陰影。經理把樣品擺在厚紙板上，解釋每一層如何淨化受污染的水。過程沒有任何奇蹟，必須維護、檢測，且在濾器失效時誠實報告。工廠也承辦學校訂單，因此工人深知偷工減料的危害。瑟蓮要求經理公開清楚說明，而不是承諾毫不費力的治療。王國已被太多掩蓋劣質工程的美麗承諾傷害。

A physician measured workers' cholesterol during an unrelated health visit, and the manager pointed to the forms as an example of privacy handled well. No patient's results were posted on the wall. The relevant numbers were reported without names. Seren admired the principle, knowing she would have to use something similar with Halden's records. Ada meanwhile examined the factory's stationery. One letter bore distinctive calligraphy, precisely like that on the anonymous threat. Seren felt an indignant rush of anger, but she did not announce an accusation. The handwriting might belong to a common professional scribe, and the letter had been delivered openly. A resemblance was a lead, not a verdict.

一名醫師正在另一場健康訪視中測量工人的膽固醇，經理指著表格說明如何妥善保護隱私。沒有病患結果張貼在牆上，相關數字也以匿名方式通報。瑟蓮欣賞這原則，因為霍爾登的資料也需要類似處理。與此同時，艾達檢查工廠使用的信紙，其中一封信帶有特殊書法筆跡，和匿名威脅信幾乎一樣。瑟蓮憤怒湧上心頭，卻沒有立刻指控。字跡可能出自常見的專業抄寫員，而這封信是公開送達。相似只能成為線索，不能成為判決。

Their visit was interrupted by a disturbance at the canal. Two barges had collided, sending supply boxes downward into the water. Workers used long poles to paddle toward the drifting cargo. Rowan wanted to order everyone out, but the canal master urged him to let trained crews manage the recovery. Seren agreed and helped relay messages from the bank. One box contained a sample filter; another carried old documents scheduled for copying. The documents were rescued wet but readable. Their survival showed the value of the waterproof wrapping that Ada had recommended. The small accident reminded Seren that fragile evidence needed ordinary precautions against ordinary risks, not only guards against enemies.

運河傳來一陣騷動，打斷了眾人的參觀。兩艘駁船相撞，補給箱紛紛落入水中。工人用長竿划向漂流的貨物。羅文想命令所有人撤離，運河管理員卻請他讓受過訓練的人負責打撈。瑟蓮同意，在岸上協助傳遞訊息。一箱裝著濾水器樣品，另一箱則是準備複製的舊文件。文件雖被打濕，仍能辨讀，全靠艾達建議的防水包裝。這場小意外提醒瑟蓮，脆弱證據不只需要守衛防敵人，也需要普通防護來應付普通風險。

At the workshop, Lysa and the manager tried to mediate a dispute over who would pay for replacement equipment. The manager was persistent but not unreasonable; he wanted written guarantees before accepting public orders. A young apprentice began to rehearse the official speech, describing the filters as magical devices. Seren stopped him gently. “Tell people what the filter can do,” she said, “and what they must still test.” The apprentice looked disappointed, then asked practical questions. His willingness to revise the speech gave Seren more hope than a grand declaration would have done. She was learning to marvel not at promises of sudden transformation, but at patient correction.

回到工坊後，萊莎與經理試圖調解誰應支付替換設備費用的爭議。經理態度堅持，卻並非不講理；他希望在接受公共訂單前取得書面保證。一名年輕學徒開始排練官方演說，把濾水器說成魔法裝置。瑟蓮溫和打斷他：「告訴人們它能做什麼，也告訴他們哪些事情仍須檢測。」學徒看來失望，卻接著問起實際問題。他願意修改講稿，比任何宏大的宣言都更讓瑟蓮感到希望。她正學著驚嘆的，不是突如其來的變化承諾，而是耐心修正。

As the day ended, the canal master offered a practical suggestion: keep duplicate copies of important transport documents in separate buildings. One fire or flood would then be less likely to erase every record. Lysa wrote the proposal down. She had started the investigation as a frightened court clerk who feared contradicting her superiors. Now she was helping design safeguards that could protect people she had never met. Seren admired that change without claiming credit for it. Lysa had chosen the work herself. The two women were allies, not author and character, and neither was entitled to control the other's future.

一天結束時，運河管理員提出實際建議：重要運輸文件應在不同建築各存副本。如此一場火災或洪水就不容易抹去全部紀錄。萊莎把建議寫下。她起初只是害怕違抗上級的法庭書記，如今卻協助設計能保護素未謀面之人的防護措施。瑟蓮欣賞她的轉變，沒有把功勞攬到自己身上。萊莎自己選擇這份工作。兩人是盟友，不是作者與角色，誰也無權控制對方的未來。

Before leaving, Seren copied the suspicious letter and sent both versions to an independent handwriting specialist. The original remained sealed with the manager. Some people in the district were superstitious about the archive fire, claiming that a curse had followed anyone who spoke of the missing crates. Seren understood the desire for such an explanation. A curse required no names and no proof. But the river could not be cleansed by fear, and its victims could not be protected by superstition. She took the canal schedule back to the panel, aware that her slow inquiry was gathering ordinary objects into a story more difficult, and more useful, than the novel's simple revenge.

離開前，瑟蓮影印可疑信件的內容，將兩個版本送交獨立筆跡專家。原件仍由經理封存。區內有人迷信檔案庫大火，宣稱凡談到失蹤貨箱的人都會遭受詛咒。瑟蓮理解這種解釋為何吸引人：詛咒不需要姓名，也不需要證明。但恐懼無法淨化河水，迷信也保護不了受害者。她把運河時程表帶回小組，知道這場緩慢調查正把普通物件聚集成一個比原小說單純復仇更困難、也更有用的故事。
