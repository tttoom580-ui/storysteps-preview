頁邊的溫度

A Temperature in the Margin

反派的辯護人，決定先替自己翻案 · 第 11 / 25 章 · 5000–6000 · 英中對照 · 校訂中

The independent panel began by asking what the water had contained, not who had caused the fire. A scientist placed a glass thermometer beside two sealed samples. One read twelve degrees Celsius; the other was warmer by six. That difference could induce chemical changes when a mineral dissolved, he explained. Seren wanted to simplify the testimony for the public, but the scientist warned that excessive simplification could turn a useful clue into a superficial explanation. The panel therefore asked him to state his method, its limitations, and the conditions in which it could fail. Lysa recorded each answer so that a later examiner could repeat the test.

獨立調查小組首先詢問水裡含有什麼，而不是先找誰放火。一名科學家把玻璃溫度計放在兩份封存樣本旁。一份是攝氏十二度，另一份高出六度。他解釋，當礦物質溶入水中時，溫差可能引發化學變化。瑟蓮希望把證詞簡化給公眾理解，科學家卻提醒她，過度簡化會把有用線索變成膚淺解釋。於是小組請他說明方法、限制，以及可能失效的條件。萊莎逐一記錄，讓後來的審查者可以重做實驗。

A musician arrived next. She was a pianist who had played at the memorial symphony, where the superintendent had met an unidentified visitor. The visitor wore a peacock feather and paid for a token admitting its bearer to a private balcony. The pianist remembered an unusual exchange: one man gave the other a card marked with two circles in perfect symmetry. Seren initially thought the circles were a secret code. Rowan suggested they represented the twin reservoirs above the harbor. Their respective positions matched the old survey. The witness could not identify the visitor, but she could place the meeting at a precise hour, narrowing the gap in the timeline.

接著到場的是一名音樂家。她是紀念交響樂的鋼琴演奏者，港務總管曾在那場活動中見過一名身分不明的人。對方戴著孔雀羽飾，並付費購買可進入私人陽台的入場憑記。鋼琴家記得一段奇特的交換：一名男子交給另一人一張畫著兩個對稱圓圈的卡片。瑟蓮起初以為那是暗號，羅文則猜它代表港口上方的兩座水庫。兩個位置與舊測量圖相符。證人無法辨認訪客，卻能確定會面時間，縮小時間線的空白。

The chancellor's lawyer called the meeting irrelevant. “People inhabit a city,” he said, “and they meet one another.” Seren agreed that ordinary contact was not wrongdoing. The issue was whether officials had tried to suppress records of contaminated water after the meeting. A guard described a wagon passing a mound of stone toward the southern strait. Its wheels had made a low growl against the road. The lawyer objected that the witness's memory was imperfect. That was true, but his pay ledger placed him on duty that night. Seren asked the panel to compare independent sources rather than treat any single memory as complete.

宰相的律師稱那次會面毫無關聯。「人住在城市裡，本來就會互相見面。」他說。瑟蓮同意，普通接觸不等於犯罪。重點在於會面後，官員是否試圖壓下受污染水源的紀錄。一名守衛描述，有輛馬車經過石堆駛向南方海峽，車輪在路上發出低沉的隆隆聲。律師抗議證人的記憶不完整。這確實沒錯，但他的薪資簿證明當晚他在值勤。瑟蓮請調查小組比較獨立來源，而不是把任何一人的記憶當成完整真相。

As the hearing continued, friction developed among the investigators. Some wanted to publish every suspicion to maintain urgency; others feared that a shortsighted announcement would endanger witnesses. Seren proposed a shared ledger with three columns: confirmed fact, probable interpretation, and unanswered question. Each team could work independently while using the same categories. This would unify the inquiry without erasing disagreement. Lysa called the proposal ironic. The woman once described as the kingdom's selfish villain had become its most determined advocate of procedural limits. Seren did not smile. Limits were not proof of virtue. They were barriers against the temptation to mistake conviction for knowledge.

聽證持續進行，調查者之間開始摩擦。有人為保持緊迫感，想立刻公布每項懷疑；有人害怕短視的聲明會危及證人。瑟蓮提出共用帳冊，分成三欄：已確認的事實、可能的解釋、尚待解答的問題。各團隊仍可獨立工作，但使用相同分類。這能統一調查架構，而不抹去不同意見。萊莎說這很諷刺：昔日被寫成王國最自私反派的女人，竟成為程序界限最堅決的捍衛者。瑟蓮沒有笑。界限不能證明美德，卻可以防止人把堅定信念錯當成知識。

The scientist added that the two reservoirs had been built in different periods, with different equipment and maintenance practices. Their test results could not be combined without adjustment. Seren asked for an accessible explanation of the difference. He drew two simple diagrams on a board, showing where water entered, where samples were taken, and where contamination might have occurred. The diagrams did not prove a culprit, but citizens could now understand the next steps. Some listeners looked disappointed by the lack of a dramatic answer. Others stayed after the session to ask thoughtful questions about protecting their household wells.

科學家補充，兩座水庫建於不同年代，設備與維修方式也不一樣。未經調整就不能合併檢測結果。瑟蓮請他用容易理解的方式解釋差別。他在板上畫出兩張簡單圖表，標示水從何處進入、何處採樣、污染可能在哪個環節發生。圖表無法證明犯人是誰，市民卻終於理解下一步該怎麼做。有人因沒有戲劇性答案而失望，也有人會後留下，認真詢問如何保護家裡的井水。

By sunset the panel agreed to probe the two reservoirs separately. The scientist would oversee the tests, and the pianist would authenticate the ticket records. No one had solved the fire, but the inquiry had gained a workable structure. Seren remembered how the novel had rushed from accusation to punishment without asking which claims could be checked. She no longer wanted a different dramatic scene in its place. She wanted a sequence of small, accountable decisions. Outside the chamber, evening bells sounded, and a long row of citizens waited to submit observations about their water. The line was inconvenient, slow, and unmistakably hopeful.

日落時，小組同意分別調查兩座水庫，由科學家監督檢驗，鋼琴家則確認入場票紀錄。沒有人解開火災謎團，但調查終於有了可操作的架構。瑟蓮想起原小說如何從指控直接跳到刑罰，從不問哪些主張可以驗證。她不再想要另一個戲劇性場景取代它，只希望一連串小而可追責的決定。會議廳外，晚鐘響起，長長的市民隊伍正排隊提出水源觀察紀錄。過程麻煩、緩慢，卻毫無疑問地充滿希望。
