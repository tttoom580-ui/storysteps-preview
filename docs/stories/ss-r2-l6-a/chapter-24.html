三證法庭

The Court of Three Proofs

反派的辯護人，決定先替自己翻案 · 第 24 / 25 章 · 5000–6000 · 英中對照 · 校訂中

The final hearing began in a crowded hall. The chairperson of the independent panel presented the original fire report beside the revised findings. Lysa read the transcript of Mara's testimony, and the court heard how a concealed disposal operation had become a disastrous blaze. The prosecution's former theory—that Seren had sold a gate to criminals—rested on a witness who now admitted his statement had been prepared for him. The bank teller described threats from Ormond's secretary and provided a dated note from his supervisor. Seren did not celebrate his confession. He had helped put her life in danger, but the court still needed to decide his responsibility through fair procedures.

最終聽證在擁擠的大廳中開始。獨立小組主席將原始火災報告與修訂調查結果並列。萊莎朗讀瑪拉的證詞逐字稿，法庭聽見隱蔽銷毀行動如何變成災難性大火。檢方原本聲稱瑟蓮把城門賣給罪犯，但主要證人如今承認自己的說法是別人事先替他準備的。銀行出納描述奧蒙德秘書的威脅，並拿出主管留下的具日期便條。瑟蓮沒有為供詞歡慶。他曾讓她的生命陷入危險，但法庭仍須依公平程序決定其責任。

The judge asked the panel to formulate its conclusions according to the Rule of Three. It had documentary evidence: the predated warrant, unauthorized disposal order, and fuel payments. It had material traces: the altered furnace, damaged lock, and recovered samples. It had independent testimony: Elen, Mara, Aric, and the scientist each confirmed distinct parts of the timeline. None alone was sufficient, but the combined record left no credible basis for the original charge against Seren. The judge accordingly dismissed it and ordered her full release from supervised residence. A mechanical indicator beeped as the decision entered the registry. For a moment disbelief held the room silent.

法官要求小組依三證法則整理結論。文件證據包括提前製作的逮捕令、未經核准的銷毀命令，以及燃料付款；物證包括改裝熔爐、受損門鎖與找回的樣本；獨立證詞則來自艾蓮、瑪拉、阿瑞克與科學家，各自確認時間線不同部分。任何單項證據都不足，但合在一起，已沒有可信基礎支持對瑟蓮的原始指控。法官因此撤銷指控，命令完全解除居住監督。判決登錄時，一個機械指示器發出短促嗶聲。全場一時難以置信，安靜下來。

Ormond rose to protest that the ruling would weaken patriotic loyalty. The judge replied that loyalty did not require the court to pretend a false accusation was true. Ormond's office would face a separate prosecution for possible falsification, unlawful disposal, and obstruction. His brother's company would undergo a further financial inquiry. The court made no claim that every person connected to them was guilty. Seren listened carefully to the distinction. She had come to understand that a verdict was not a novel's final sentence, capable of swallowing all future questions. It was a decision about specific charges, reached through evidence, open to lawful review.

奧蒙德起身抗議，聲稱判決將削弱愛國忠誠。法官回答，忠誠不代表法庭必須假裝錯誤指控是真的。奧蒙德辦公室將因可能涉及偽造、違法銷毀與妨礙調查接受另案追訴，其兄弟的公司也將進一步接受財務調查。法庭沒有宣稱凡與他們有關的人都有罪。瑟蓮仔細聽著這個區分。她明白了，判決不是小說最後一句話，不能吞掉未來所有問題；它只是在證據基礎上針對特定指控作出的決定，仍可依法檢視。

Outside, a coalition of workers, students, and harbor families gathered under flowering vines. The streets were cozy with market stalls selling clams and warm bread, yet the atmosphere remained tense. Some citizens demanded immediate punishment for the chancellor; others feared that changing old institutions meant abandoning civilization. Seren answered a reporter's question with a quiet statement: “We do not civilize people by refusing to hear them.” A conscientious clerk wrote the sentence in the public register. The words caused a ripple of conversation, but Seren did not pretend one speech could repair the kingdom. Laws would need revision, budgets would need scrutiny, and victims would need actual assistance.

屋外，工人、學生與港口家庭組成的聯盟聚集在開花藤蔓下。街道上有賣蛤蜊與溫麵包的市集，氣氛舒適，情緒卻仍緊繃。有人要求立刻處罰宰相，也有人擔心改變舊制度等於放棄文明。記者提問時，瑟蓮平靜回答：「拒絕傾聽別人，不能使任何人變文明。」一名認真的書記把這句話寫進公共紀錄。它在人群間引起討論，但瑟蓮沒有假裝一場演說就能修復王國。法律需要修訂，預算需要監督，受害者需要實際協助。

When the judge finished, a small recording machine gave another beep and the clerk sealed the transcript. The fire had seemed ready to devour every trace of the truth; it had not succeeded. Seren could scarcely reckon the full stature of the people who had made the hearing possible. Their work was recorded not as a single heroic story but as many distinct contributions. Outside the window, ordinary vendors reopened their stalls. No one could guarantee that the kingdom would never repeat its mistakes. But witnesses could now ask for documents, investigators could be challenged, and an accused person no longer had to depend entirely on a ruler's favor.

法官宣讀完畢，一台小型記錄機再次發出嗶聲，書記封存逐字稿。那場大火一度像要吞噬所有真相痕跡，卻沒有成功。瑟蓮幾乎無法衡量協助完成聽證的眾人有多重要。他們的工作沒有被寫成單一英雄故事，而是保留為許多不同的貢獻。窗外普通攤販重新開店。沒人能保證王國不再犯錯，但如今證人能要求查閱文件、調查者能受到質疑，被告也不必再完全依靠統治者的恩惠。

That evening, Lysa walked with Seren beside the harbor. They watched tiny waves ripple against a wall built by sailors from many latitudes, including distant tropic ports. Seren reckoned that public life would remain difficult, but she no longer had to face it as a condemned character. The record had corrected a false judgment. It had not returned the burned papers or erased the injuries suffered by workers. Seren was free, and the freedom felt less like a prize than a responsibility to decide what she would do next. For once, no narrator was waiting to explain her choice before she made it.

傍晚，萊莎陪瑟蓮沿港口散步。細小波浪拍著海牆，那是來自不同緯度、包括遙遠熱帶港口的水手共同建造的。瑟蓮想，公共生活仍會艱難，但她不必再以被定罪的角色身分面對。紀錄糾正了一場錯誤判決，卻無法找回燒毀的紙張，也不能抹去工人受過的傷。她自由了，而這自由與其說是獎賞，不如說是一份責任：她要決定接下來做什麼。這一次，沒有旁白等著在她做決定前先替她解釋。
