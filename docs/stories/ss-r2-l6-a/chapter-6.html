The Sculptor’s Unsent Letter

反派的辯護人，決定先替自己翻案 · 第 6 / 25 章 · 5000–6000 · 英中對照 · 校訂中

By the sixth morning, officials had begun to allege that Seren had bribed the academy researchers. A newspaper printed her portrait beneath a caption calling her the kingdom's most ruthless liar. Seren wanted to answer every charge, but Lysa advised simplicity: a response without evidence would only provide another headline. Their next lead was a sculptor named Bell, who lived on the outskirts of the capital. Bell had designed the archive's stone entrance and, according to the missing doctor's notebook, helped conceal a small sealed case. A former parliament clerk, he understood how public commissions could disguise private transactions. He was their best chance of locating the doctor's original results.

第六天早晨，官員開始宣稱瑟蓮賄賂了學院研究人員。一家報紙在她的肖像下寫上標題，稱她是王國最無情的騙子。瑟蓮很想逐條反駁，萊莎卻建議保持簡潔：沒有證據的回應只會成為另一個新聞標題。她們接下來的線索是住在首都郊區的雕刻師貝爾。貝爾設計過檔案庫的石造入口；根據失蹤醫師的筆記，他還協助藏匿過一只封箱。貝爾曾任議會書記，理解公共工程如何掩蓋私人交易。他是她們找出醫師原始檢驗結果的最大希望。

Bell proved unexpectedly hospitable. A jolly man with a silver mustache, he served tea in a garden he had forgotten to mow. His apprentice, who pursued sculpture as an extracurricular interest, kept nagging him about his unfinished commissions. Bell admitted that the doctor had visited, carrying a case that weighed scarcely an ounce more than an empty box. “He said a city can become an oasis for the powerful while everyone else goes thirsty,” Bell recalled. He had not opened the case. Instead, he had embedded it behind a removable stone in an unfinished memorial. The stone was commissioned for the archive, but its installation had been delayed.

貝爾出乎意料地好客。他是個蓄著銀白鬍子的開朗男子，在一座忘了修剪的庭園裡招待眾人喝茶。他的學徒把雕刻當作課外興趣，不停催促他完成尚未交付的作品。貝爾承認那位醫師曾來訪，手上帶著一只重量幾乎與空箱相同的小盒子。「他說，一座城市能成為權貴的綠洲，卻讓其他人全都口渴。」貝爾回憶。貝爾沒有打開箱子，而是把它嵌進一座未完成紀念碑的可拆卸石塊後方。石碑原本要安裝在檔案庫，工程卻遭到延誤。

The memorial stood in a workshop behind a row of damaged roofs. Bell lifted the stone while Rowan secured the doorway. Inside they found not the expected laboratory report, but a clay tablet bearing a narrow extract of numbers. Lysa recognized the format: it was a series of water measurements, each tied to a location and hour. A second sheet described simultaneous inspections at the mine and harbor. If accurate, the records could demonstrate that contamination began upstream. Seren refused to announce a climax to the investigation. They still needed the full report and a person qualified to explain it. Bell's account, however, gave them a credible chain of custody for the tablet.

紀念碑立在一排受損屋頂後方的工作室裡。貝爾抬起石板，羅文守住門口。裡面不是預期的實驗室報告，而是一塊刻著簡短數字節錄的泥板。萊莎認出格式：那是一系列水質測量結果，每筆都附帶地點與時間。第二張紙記載礦場與港口進行的同步檢查。若資料準確，就能證明污染源頭在上游。瑟蓮不肯宣告調查已到高潮；她們仍需要完整報告，以及能夠解釋資料的專業人士。然而貝爾的說法，至少讓泥板的保管過程有了可信脈絡。

On their way back, they encountered a sudden slump in the road where rain had loosened the soil. Rowan insisted that the carriage turn around. Seren noticed workers using crushed stone to repair the damage; among them was a woman wearing the medical academy's old badge. She introduced herself as Hessa, a technician dismissed after asking to extract samples from the northern springs. Hessa recognized the same measurements on the tablet. She could identify a pattern, not the source. That distinction mattered to Seren. She invited Hessa to submit a signed statement and promised that independent specialists, not frightened officials, would examine the data. Hessa agreed on the condition that her former colleagues also receive protection.

回程時，一段路因雨水沖鬆土壤而突然塌陷。羅文堅持馬車繞道。瑟蓮注意到工人正用碎石修補，其中一名女子別著醫學院的舊徽章。她自稱海莎，曾因要求採集北方泉水樣本而遭解雇。海莎認出了泥板上的測量方式。她能辨認數據變化，卻無法直接指出源頭。瑟蓮認為這個區分至關重要。她邀請海莎提供具名證詞，並保證讓獨立專家而不是受驚的官員檢視資料。海莎答應了，條件是她以前的同事也必須得到保護。

Bell showed them one final commission, a fountain meant to beautify the western square. The design included figures from every province, yet the inscription praised only the palace. His apprentice began to nag him about changing it. Seren suggested inviting residents to choose the names that appeared beneath the sculpture. Bell laughed at first, then considered the idea seriously. A public monument could commemorate cooperation instead of pretending one ruler had solved every problem. The conversation seemed remote from the trial, but Seren thought otherwise. Records, statues, and books all taught people whose actions deserved to be remembered.

貝爾又帶她們看一件最後的委託作品：預定用來美化西廣場的噴泉。設計包含各省人物，銘文卻只歌頌王宮。他的學徒不停催他修改。瑟蓮提議邀請居民自行選擇雕像底下要刻的姓名。貝爾起初大笑，隨後認真思考。一座公共紀念碑可以紀念合作，而不是假裝所有問題都由一位統治者解決。這段對話看似與審判無關，瑟蓮卻不這麼認為。紀錄、雕像與書本，都在教人哪些行為值得被記住。

When Seren finally reached the capital, she found an anonymous warning pinned to her door: “The fire was only the beginning.” Lysa thought it might be an attempt to frighten them into silence. Rowan feared a genuine threat. Seren copied the message, sealed the original, and recorded who had touched it. The answer might be far less dramatic than the warning intended. That night the palace announced a special session of parliament to discuss an independent inquiry. For the first time the case could escape the control of a single prosecutor. Yet Seren understood that powerful people rarely surrendered their influence willingly. The next move might be aimed not at her life, but at the evidence itself.

瑟蓮終於回到首都時，發現門上釘著匿名警告：「那場火只是一個開始。」萊莎認為有人企圖嚇得她們閉嘴，羅文則擔心威脅確實存在。瑟蓮抄下文字、封存原件，並記錄曾碰觸它的人。真相可能遠比警告者想營造的恐怖氣氛平凡。當晚王宮宣布，議會將召開特別會議討論獨立調查。這起案件第一次可能脫離單一檢察官的控制。然而瑟蓮知道，有權勢的人很少甘願放棄影響力。對方下一步的目標，可能不是她的命，而是證據本身。
