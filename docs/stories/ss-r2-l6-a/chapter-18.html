海角外的護士

The Nurse Beyond the Cape

反派的辯護人，決定先替自己翻案 · 第 18 / 25 章 · 5000–6000 · 英中對照 · 校訂中

The search for Evi led Seren to a clinic beyond a narrow cape. The child had been kept under protective supervision after threats from a palace operative, but the arrangement now made her a captive. She could not leave, and her letters were screened without a court order. Seren asked the clinic's caretaker to broaden the safety plan rather than remove every precaution at once. The girl had endured a long ordeal. A second abrupt change could harm her further. The caretaker admitted that an official had dictated the restrictions and threatened to outlaw visits from anyone connected to the mine investigation. No judge had approved those limits.

尋找艾薇的線索，把瑟蓮帶到狹長海角外的診所。孩子因遭王宮特務威脅而接受保護性看護，但安排如今已近似監禁：她無法離開，信件也在沒有法院命令的情況下被審查。瑟蓮要求看護者擴大安全計畫，而不是一次撤掉所有防護。女孩已經歷長期折磨，突然再改變可能造成傷害。看護者承認限制來自官員口頭命令，對方還威脅禁止任何與礦場調查相關的人探視。沒有法官核准過這些限制。

Evi arrived carrying a small wooden animal carved from a dwarf pine. She was mischievous enough to pinch Rowan's sleeve when he stood too stiffly, but she fell silent whenever the palace was mentioned. Seren did not interrogate her. She introduced Lysa as an independent recorder, then let Evi decide whether to speak. After awhile the girl pointed to an excerpt from a songbook. One printed consonant had been altered in ink, turning a common word into the name of a shipping vessel. It was the same ship named on the paper found beneath the wharf. Evi explained that the nurse had taught her the song so she could remember where important letters were hidden.

艾薇抱著一隻用矮松雕成的木製小動物出現。她頑皮地捏了羅文僵直的衣袖一下，但一提到王宮就沉默。瑟蓮沒有盤問她，只介紹萊莎是獨立紀錄員，讓艾薇自己決定是否說話。過了一會兒，女孩指向歌本中的一小段文字。其中一個印刷子音被墨水改過，把普通詞語變成一艘運貨船的名字。那正是碼頭地磚下紙條記載的船名。艾薇解釋，護士教她這首歌，是為了讓她記得重要信件藏在哪裡。

The nurse, who had been absent, returned as a storm approached. She confessed that she had been ordered to deliver a sealed packet from Ormond's residence, but an intruder had tried to seize it. She hid the packet inside a shelf before fleeing. Her intention had been to protect Evi, not to manipulate the case. Seren asked Lysa to record the nurse's testimony separately from the child's. A tornado warning sounded along the coast, forcing the group into the cellar. When the danger passed, they found that rain had entered through a broken window. The shelf was wet, but the concealed packet survived inside a tin case.

護士先前不在，此時隨著暴風接近而返家。她承認自己奉命從奧蒙德宅邸送出一包封存文件，卻有入侵者企圖搶走。她逃離前把文件藏進架子。她原本想保護艾薇，而不是操弄案件。瑟蓮要萊莎把護士與孩子的證詞分開記錄。沿海響起龍捲風警報，眾人被迫躲進地下室。危險過後，雨水從破窗進入，架子已經濕透，但隱藏文件因裝在錫盒裡而保存下來。

Inside was a draft order to destroy obsolete archive records. Its date preceded the fire by four days. The statute cited in the document allowed disposal only after a public notice and two independent approvals; neither appeared on the page. A drawing showed the archive's old storage layout. Rowan used an optical viewer to zoom in on a scratched mark near the eastern door. The mark matched the damaged lock already photographed. The packet did not name an arsonist. It did show that someone had planned a disposal operation before the fire and had bypassed the law meant to protect the records. Seren sealed the documents without adding a conclusion that the evidence could not support.

盒裡是一份銷毀過時檔案的命令草稿，日期比火災早四天。文件引用的法條規定，銷毀前必須公開公告並取得兩位獨立核准者同意；但文件上兩者都沒有。附圖標出檔案庫舊儲藏區格局。羅文用光學檢視器放大東門附近的一道刮痕，那痕跡與先前拍下的受損門鎖位置相符。文件沒有指出縱火者身分，卻證明有人在大火前策畫清除檔案，且繞過了用來保護紀錄的法律。瑟蓮封存資料，沒有添加證據尚不能支持的結論。

The clinic's restoration would require repairs to its roof and a new policy on access to patient files. A wholesale merchant offered discounted supplies, but Seren insisted on obtaining competing bids. Good intentions were not a substitute for fair spending. Evi watched the adults discuss these details and asked whether the doctors would still have time for children. The nurse assured her that patient care remained the first priority. Seren was grateful for the question. Investigators often became absorbed in documents and forgot that institutions existed to serve living people. A rebuilt clinic would be a failure if its doors stayed closed to those who needed it.

修復診所需要整修屋頂，以及重新制定病歷查閱規範。一位批發商願意提供折扣物資，但瑟蓮堅持取得其他競爭報價。善意不能取代公平支出。艾薇看大人討論這些細節，問醫師是否還有時間照顧孩子。護士向她保證，病患照護仍是第一優先。瑟蓮感謝這個問題。調查者常沉迷文件，忘記制度存在是為了服務活生生的人。即使診所修好了，若大門仍對需要的人關閉，也算是失敗。

Evi asked whether her father could visit without being arrested. Seren answered carefully: the court would have to approve a safe meeting, and she could request it immediately. No one could promise that every official would behave properly. Still, the girl's rights were not suspended merely because powerful people feared her testimony. As the sea calmed, Seren watched the child carry her wooden animal toward the window. The story she had entered had treated children as helpless clues in adult plots. This child had remembered a song, made a choice, and helped preserve evidence. Her future should not depend on remaining useful to an investigation.

艾薇問，父親能不能來探望而不被抓。瑟蓮小心回答：法庭必須批准安全會面，她可以立即提出申請。沒人能保證每位官員都會守法，但權貴害怕證詞，不代表女孩的權利就因此消失。海面平靜後，瑟蓮看著艾薇帶著木製小動物走到窗前。她穿進來的故事曾把孩子當成成人陰謀裡無助的線索；這個孩子卻記住一首歌、自己作了決定，還協助保存證據。她的未來不應取決於對調查還有沒有用處。
