The Twelfth Crate

反派的辯護人，決定先替自己翻案 · 第 19 / 25 章 · 5000–6000 · 英中對照 · 校訂中

The ship's manifest led to a small isle beyond the southern strait. Seren traveled with Rowan, Lysa, and a maritime inspector whose proficiency in cargo records impressed even the admiral. A migrant sailor recognized the name of the vessel and said it had carried a packet marked as scientific equipment. The crew had been ordered to smuggle the packet past ordinary customs inspection. That instruction alone was unlawful, but it did not establish what lay inside. Seren asked the sailor to make a formal oath before a neutral magistrate. He agreed, provided his name remained protected until the court could guarantee his safe return.

船舶清單把瑟蓮一行帶到南方海峽外的小島。同行的有羅文、萊莎，以及一名貨運紀錄能力連上將都佩服的海事稽查員。一位移民水手認出船名，說船上曾載有標記為科學設備的包裹。船員被命令將其偷運過普通海關檢查。這項命令本身違法，卻無法證明裡面裝什麼。瑟蓮請水手在中立法官前正式宣誓。他同意，條件是法院能保障他安全返鄉之前，不公開姓名。

The island warehouse smelled of wet rope, vanilla, and a sharp chemical odor. A technician explained that the pleasant smell came from packing materials, while the sharp one might be ozone produced by old electrical equipment. Seren refused to infer danger from smell alone. The workers opened the recovered crate under supervision. Inside were several metal cylinders, a laboratory register, and a folded sketch of a nude figure used in an academy anatomy class. The sketch appeared irrelevant, but its reverse side carried the same blue ink found on the altered inventory. Lysa stored it separately. Investigators sometimes found valuable evidence on the back of something nobody thought important.

島上倉庫聞起來有濕繩、香草，以及刺鼻的化學氣味。技術員解釋，香味來自包裝材料，刺鼻味則可能是舊電器產生的臭氧。瑟蓮拒絕只憑氣味推斷危險。工人在監督下打開找回的貨箱，裡面有幾支金屬圓筒、一本實驗室登記簿，以及一張學院解剖課使用的裸體人物素描。素描看似無關，但背面竟有和遭修改清單相同的藍墨水。萊莎把它單獨保存。調查者有時會在大家以為不重要的物品背面，找到真正有價值的證據。

A glamorous visitor from the capital had once tried to purchase the crate, the warehouse keeper said. He remembered her confident stride and the scorn she showed toward local workers, but he did not know her name. Seren asked him to describe clothing and dates without guessing an identity. From the correspondence they could infer that Ormond's company wanted the cylinders returned before the inquiry began. They could not infer that the visitor had started the archive fire. The distinction was essential. Seren had learned how easily suspicion grew into public certainty, and she would not allow an outrageous allegation to replace missing evidence.

倉庫管理員說，一位打扮華麗的首都訪客曾試圖買下貨箱。他記得那人自信的步伐，以及她對本地工人的輕蔑，卻不知道姓名。瑟蓮請他描述衣著與日期，不要猜身分。從書信可推斷，奧蒙德兄弟的公司希望在調查前拿回圓筒，但不能因此推定那位訪客就是檔案庫縱火者。這個區分至關重要。瑟蓮已學到，懷疑多麼容易變成公眾的確定感，她不允許荒唐的指控取代缺失的證據。

That evening Seren walked to the shore to meditate on the case. She was mortal, vulnerable, and often wrong; the knowledge felt more useful than confidence. Rowan came to fortify the camp's defenses, not to offer reassurance she had not requested. They discussed the records, keeping speculation separate from observation. Across the water, the city lights shimmered. Seren realized that she had spent weeks trying to escape a fate written by another person. Now she cared more about the people whose lives the first story had ignored: the sailors, the children, the families who needed wages and clean water. Her purpose had changed without her asking permission from the book.

那晚瑟蓮走到岸邊，靜靜思考案件。她會死、會受傷，也經常犯錯；理解自己的有限，比空泛自信更有用。羅文來加固營地防禦，不是提供她沒有要求的安慰。兩人談著紀錄，把推測與觀察分開。海面對岸，城市燈光閃動。瑟蓮明白，自己花了數週企圖逃離別人寫好的命運，如今卻更在意第一個故事忽略的人：水手、孩子、需要工資與乾淨飲水的家庭。她的目標已改變，無須得到原書的許可。

The sailor's intonation changed when he described the night of the shipment. He spoke confidently about the route but hesitated over the visitor's clothing. Seren asked whether he invariably remembered faces well. He admitted that he did not, especially in darkness. The clerk recorded that limitation alongside the details he knew with certainty. It would have been easy to coach him toward a convenient identification, but doing so would compromise his credibility and betray the inquiry. Seren thanked him for saying “I do not know.” It was one of the most important sentences a witness could offer.

水手描述運貨之夜時，語調變了。他談路線很有把握，提到訪客衣著卻遲疑。瑟蓮問他是否總能準確記得人的面孔。他承認自己並不能，尤其在黑暗中。書記把這項限制和他確知的細節一起記下。要誘導他辨認一位方便的嫌犯並不難，但那會損害他的可信度，也背叛調查目的。瑟蓮感謝他願意說「我不知道」。這是證人能說出的最重要句子之一。

The inspection closed with an important result. The metal cylinders held original water samples, sealed before the fire and accompanied by matching register numbers. Independent tests would still be needed, but the samples could be compared with the academy's copies. Lysa sealed each cylinder and recorded every transfer. A messenger prepared the return packet for the capital. The crew had not solved the entire mystery, yet they had recovered the twelfth crate. Seren felt no triumph as the ship departed. Evidence mattered because it could survive her own wishes, not because it always confirmed them.

檢查最後得出重要結果。金屬圓筒內裝著大火前封存的原始水樣，附有對應的登記編號。仍需要獨立檢驗，但樣本可以與學院副本比對。萊莎逐支封存圓筒，記錄每次交接。一名信使準備把文件包送回首都。一行人尚未解開整個謎團，卻已找回第十二箱。船隻啟航時，瑟蓮沒有感到勝利狂喜。證據重要，是因為它能經得起她自己的願望檢驗，而不只是因為它總支持她的判斷。
