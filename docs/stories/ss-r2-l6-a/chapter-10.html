第十日

The Tenth Day

反派的辯護人，決定先替自己翻案 · 第 10 / 25 章 · 5000–6000 · 英中對照 · 校訂中

The tenth day opened with the splendor of a royal procession outside the courthouse. Inside, the prosecutor wore a cynical smile. He expected Seren to default on her promise of new evidence and return quietly to prison. Instead she presented Halden's verified dates, the orphanage ledger, and the metal certificate from the pumping station. Each had an identified custodian. None could, by itself, justify prosecuting the chancellor, but together they raised serious doubt about the story used to condemn Seren. The judge allowed Lysa to question the witnesses. Ormond sat behind a screen of synthetic silk, his expression hidden while the audience strained to hear every word.

第十天以法院外華麗的皇家遊行揭開序幕。法庭內，檢察官掛著冷笑，預期瑟蓮無法履行提交新證據的承諾，只能默默回監牢。她卻提出霍爾登證實的日期、孤兒院帳冊，以及抽水站的金屬證明牌。每件物證都有可查的保管人。它們單獨都不足以起訴宰相，合在一起卻足以嚴重動搖瑟蓮的定罪敘事。法官准許萊莎詢問證人。奧蒙德坐在合成絲綢屏風後，看不見表情，全場則努力聽清每一句話。

The prosecutor argued that the contaminated samples might contain a radioactive mineral, making their preservation dangerous. Nera answered that the academy had tested the material and that ordinary precautions were adequate for the documented concentrations. She produced her calibrated equipment and refused to exaggerate the results. When the prosecutor tried to intrude on her explanation, the judge told him to wait. A horse jockey outside had shouted that the entire case was a race between nobles, but the proceedings were moving slowly for a reason. No witness should be forced to compete for attention. Seren watched Nera's confidence grow as each technical question received a careful answer.

檢察官主張受污染樣本可能含放射性礦物，保存起來十分危險。妮拉回答，學院已檢驗該物質，在紀錄所示濃度下，採取一般防護措施便足夠。她提出校準過的儀器，也拒絕誇大結果。檢察官企圖打斷她的解釋，法官命令他等候。外頭有名賽馬騎師大喊這場訴訟只是貴族之間的競賽，然而程序之所以緩慢，正是有其必要。任何證人都不該被迫爭奪注意力。瑟蓮看著妮拉在一次次細緻回答專業問題時逐漸恢復信心。

Rowan next demonstrated how the archive lock had been opened. He used a screwdriver to lift a damaged cover, revealing a scratch hidden beneath the metal. The mark showed that the door had been forced after it was supposedly secured. A guard with a stutter verified the routine for closing the archive. He paused repeatedly but did not change his account. The prosecutor's sharp retort was that a scratch could have occurred earlier. Seren agreed. She did not claim it proved the fire was intentional. Its literal significance was narrower: the official report describing an undisturbed lock was inaccurate. That was enough to require a new investigation of the entrance.

接著羅文示範檔案庫的門鎖如何被打開。他用螺絲起子掀起一片受損蓋板，露出金屬下原本被遮住的刮痕。那道痕跡顯示，門在據稱上鎖之後還曾遭人強行開啟。一名有口吃的衛兵證實檔案庫的關門流程。他說話不時停頓，敘述卻始終一致。檢察官尖銳反駁，刮痕也可能更早出現。瑟蓮同意，沒有宣稱它能證明縱火。它的字面意義更有限：官方報告所稱「門鎖沒有遭干擾」並不正確。光是這點，就足以要求重新調查入口。

At the midday break, a clerk brought a serving of bread and vegetables. Seren barely tasted it. Her stepfather in the novel had been one of Ormond's closest allies, and she feared that an old family debt would be used against her. Lysa reminded her that personal history could explain motive without establishing a crime. Outside, citizens gathered under the fragrance of flowering trees. Their morale rose when the judge ordered the evidence sealed in the public registry rather than returned to Ormond's ministry. The ministry's old funds were nearly depleted, and a concealed plan to subsidize the mining company now became a legitimate subject for audit.

中午休庭時，書記送來一份麵包與蔬菜，瑟蓮幾乎沒嚐到味道。原小說裡，她的繼父是奧蒙德最親近的盟友之一，她擔心舊日家族債務被拿來攻擊自己。萊莎提醒她，個人歷史能解釋動機，卻不能直接證明犯罪。外頭花樹飄香，市民聚集。法官命令將證據封存於公共登記處，而不是交還宰相部會後，眾人士氣大振。該部會的舊基金已接近耗盡，一筆隱蔽的礦業補貼也因此成為合法審計對象。

Before the ruling, Seren had a brief opportunity to speak directly to the court. She did not ask the judge to admire her bravery. She asked that every new document be made available to both sides, that the witnesses receive protection, and that the accused be permitted to examine the record used against her. The prosecutor objected to the cost, but Lysa answered that a cheap trial ending in wrongful punishment was not truly economical. Seren remembered the narrow room in which she had first awakened. The difference between that morning and this one was not certainty; it was the chance for opposing claims to be tested.

裁定前，瑟蓮獲得短暫機會直接向法庭發言。她沒有要求法官欣賞自己的勇氣，只請求新文件同時開放雙方查閱、證人受到保護，以及被告能檢視用來指控自己的紀錄。檢察官抗議費用，萊莎回答，一場廉價卻造成冤罰的審判，根本算不上真正節省。瑟蓮想起剛醒來時那間狹窄房間。那個早晨和今天的差別，不是她已經確知一切，而是對立主張終於有機會接受檢驗。

Late in the afternoon, the judge announced a concession by the crown: the execution order would be suspended while an independent panel examined the archive fire and the mine records. Seren would remain under supervised residence, but she could question witnesses with Lysa present. The panel would have authority to order documents from the chancellor's office. It was not spontaneous mercy. It was the result of evidence and public rules that even the palace had difficulty ignoring. Seren had survived the deadline without proving every accusation false. The prosecutor's case was now subject to review, and Ormond would have to answer questions rather than merely ask them.

下午晚些時候，法官宣布王室讓步：在獨立小組調查檔案庫火災與礦場紀錄期間，行刑命令暫停。瑟蓮仍須住在監督居所，但可以在萊莎陪同下詢問證人。調查小組有權命令宰相辦公室提出文件。這不是突然降臨的仁慈，而是證據與公開法則共同作用的結果，連王宮也難以忽視。瑟蓮在期限前活了下來，卻仍未證明每一項指控都不實。檢方案件現在必須接受檢視，而奧蒙德也得開始回答問題，不再只是發問。
