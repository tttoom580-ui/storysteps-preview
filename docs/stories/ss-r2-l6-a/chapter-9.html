楓樹下的旅店

The Lodge Beneath the Maples

反派的辯護人，決定先替自己翻案 · 第 9 / 25 章 · 5000–6000 · 英中對照 · 校訂中

The physician's last address belonged to a lodge hidden behind maple trees near the eastern border. Seren reached it after a long trek through rain. A waterproof packet was tied beneath a bridge rail, exactly where the doctor's former assistant said couriers sometimes left messages. The packet contained a plain instruction: “Ask for the mistress of the house, not the owner.” The lodge was run by Mrs. Lark, a severe woman who served chili stew and jasmine tea without asking who they were. She had known Seren before the archive fire. Her expression suggested that this familiarity might alienate a guest rather than comfort her.

醫師最後的通訊地址，是東部邊境楓樹後的一間旅店。瑟蓮冒雨走了很長的路才抵達。橋梁欄杆下綁著一包防水文件，位置正是醫師昔日助理說信使偶爾會留下訊息的地方。包裡只有一句簡單指示：「找這棟房子的女主人，不要找產權所有人。」旅店由嚴厲的拉克太太經營，她端上辣椒燉菜與茉莉花茶，卻不問客人身分。她在檔案庫失火前就認識瑟蓮，而那份熟悉，似乎更可能讓客人難堪，而不是安心。

Mrs. Lark explained that Seren had once donated money to the clinic, then abruptly withdrawn it. The novelist had called this cruelty, but the actual correspondence told a more complicated story. Seren's signature appeared on the withdrawal, while a blot covered the name of the official who demanded it. The continuity of the handwriting showed that the same clerk had prepared both the donation and its cancellation. The money had not disappeared: it had been redirected to an emergency fund under an unfamiliar account. Seren asked who benefited, resisting the urge to claim that her fictional reputation was entirely invented. Some choices belonged to the previous Seren, and their consequences could not be erased by a new consciousness.

拉克太太解釋，瑟蓮曾捐款給診所，後來卻突然撤回。小說家把這寫成殘酷行為，實際書信卻呈現更複雜的情況。撤款單上有瑟蓮的簽名，但要求撤款的官員姓名被墨漬蓋住。字跡的連續性顯示，捐款與取消捐款的文書都由同一位書記準備。錢並沒有消失，而是被轉往陌生帳戶的緊急基金。瑟蓮詢問誰受益，同時努力忍住宣稱過去的瑟蓮完全無辜的衝動。有些選擇屬於先前的瑟蓮，新意識不能把那些後果一筆抹去。

A vigorous young organizer arrived after supper. He belonged to a socialist workers' society that demanded public oversight of the mines. Mrs. Lark allowed members to socialize at the lodge, although she rejected their most radical proposals. The organizer carried cumulative wage records showing how often miners had been ordered to work during officially recorded shutdowns. That evidence could be decisive if it matched the drainage measurements. He was reluctant to hand it to a noblewoman, fearing she would regard the workers as dispensable once her own trial was over. Seren offered to let the workers retain the original records while the inquiry received certified copies. He accepted that arrangement after reading its terms.

晚餐後，一名精力充沛的年輕組織者到訪。他屬於一個主張礦場接受公眾監督的社會主義工人協會。拉克太太容許成員在旅店聚會，卻反對他們最激進的主張。組織者帶來累積的工資紀錄，顯示礦工多次在官方宣稱停工的期間繼續工作。若能和排水數據相符，這些證據可能具有決定性。他不願把資料交給貴族女子，害怕她在自己案件結束後就把工人視為可拋棄的工具。瑟蓮提議由工人保管原件，調查小組只收經認證的副本。他讀過條款後接受了。

At midnight Mrs. Lark took them behind the kitchen to a locked storeroom. There they found the missing physician, Dr. Halden, alive but frightened. He had not fled because he lacked courage. His daughter had been threatened, and he could not risk exposing her location. Seren did not ask him to trust her instantly. She showed him the independent inquiry's draft safeguards and described each document already collected. Halden studied their dates and finally confirmed that his laboratory had found contaminated water before the fire. He had preserved a complete report, but refused to release it until the inquiry could guarantee that the patient names were concealed.

午夜時分，拉克太太帶她們穿過廚房，來到一間上鎖的儲物室。失蹤醫師霍爾登就在裡面，活著，卻十分恐懼。他不是因缺乏勇氣才躲起來；他的女兒受到威脅，他不敢冒險暴露她的所在。瑟蓮沒有要求他立刻信任自己，而是拿出獨立調查草擬的保護條款，逐份說明已收集到的文件。霍爾登檢查日期後，終於確認他的實驗室在大火前就發現水源污染。他保留了完整報告，但除非調查小組能保證病患姓名不被公開，否則不願交出。

Mrs. Lark had spent years trying to mobilize neighbors when winter illness spread through the valley. Her efforts now span three villages, but she refused to let the inquiry consume the clinic's limited resources. Seren offered paid staff to copy records without taking away the originals. The arrangement was modest and useful. It reminded her that an investigation could create burdens even while pursuing justice. Every visit interrupted a doctor, a nurse, or a family. If her defense merely transferred the cost of survival onto people with less money and power, it would repeat the injustice she claimed to oppose.

冬季疾病在山谷蔓延時，拉克太太多年來努力動員鄰居互助。她的工作如今跨越三個村落，卻不願讓調查耗盡診所有限資源。瑟蓮提議雇用人手抄寫紀錄，不拿走原件。安排樸素而有用。這提醒她，追求正義的調查也可能製造負擔。每一次造訪都打斷醫師、護士或家庭的生活。如果她的辯護只是把求生代價轉嫁給金錢與權力更少的人，就等於重複她口口聲聲反對的不公。

Seren lay awake listening to rain against the roof. She could muse about the irony of seeking refuge in a place once used to organize opposition to her class, but the situation demanded action. Persistence had brought the party to a living witness, yet trust still had to be earned. By morning she had drafted a proposal: independent doctors would verify the report, patients would remain anonymous, and miners would appoint their own representative. Halden signed a limited statement verifying the dates, not the entire conclusion. Seren accepted the boundary. With one day left, she rode toward the court carrying the first piece of evidence the chancellor could neither explain away as rumor nor dismiss as a forged date.

瑟蓮整夜聽著雨打屋頂，無法入睡。她可以感嘆，自己竟在一個曾被用來組織反對貴族階級的地方尋求庇護，但眼前需要的是行動。持續不懈的追查帶她們找到活著的證人，信任卻仍須慢慢建立。天亮前她草擬一份提案：由獨立醫師驗證報告，病患保持匿名，礦工自行指派代表。霍爾登只簽下一份證明日期的有限聲明，並未同意全部結論。瑟蓮接受這個界限。還剩一天，她帶著第一份宰相難以輕易說成傳聞、或指為日期造假的證據，騎馬趕往法庭。
