大火之前的判決

The Verdict Before the Fire

反派的辯護人，決定先替自己翻案 · 第 1 / 25 章 · 5000–6000 · 英中對照

On the morning of her execution, Mira woke inside a book she had once edited. In its final chapter, Lady Seren Vale was condemned for selling the eastern gate to a gangster, allowing a disastrous fire to destroy the royal archive. Mira remembered the paragraph because she had questioned its logic: why had the magistrate issued an arrest warrant before the fire began? Now she was Seren, her eyelid swollen from a sleepless night, her hands locked beneath the courtroom table. The officials had been told to abbreviate her name to S. Vale on the public list, as though a shorter name would make her easier to erase.

在行刑當天早晨，米拉醒來，發現自己身處一本曾經編輯過的小說。原書最後一章中，瑟蓮・維爾小姐因把東城門賣給黑幫分子、導致一場毀掉皇家檔案庫的大火而被判有罪。米拉記得那段文字，因為她曾質疑其中的邏輯：為何法官會在火災發生之前就簽發逮捕令？如今她成了瑟蓮，一隻眼皮因整夜未眠而浮腫，雙手被鎖在法庭的桌子底下。官員已奉命在公開名單上將她的名字縮寫成 S. Vale，彷彿名字短一點，她就更容易被抹去。

A court orderly offered her water, then withdrew it when the prosecutor shook his head. At the witness bench stood a bank teller who claimed to have seen a bribe change hands. Behind him was a guard with a noticeable limp. Both men used exactly the phrases Mira recalled from the novel, but the teller looked frightened rather than certain. Seren searched for an anchor in a room where everyone expected her silence. She found one in the date stamped on the warrant: the seventeenth. The fire had begun after midnight on the eighteenth. A comparative reading of the two official records should have exposed that difference, if anyone had been permitted to read them together.

一名法庭勤務員遞水給她，檢察官搖頭後，又把水收了回去。證人席上站著一位銀行出納，聲稱親眼看見賄賂交易。他身後有位跛腳的衛兵。兩人使用的措辭，和米拉記得的小說情節分毫不差；然而出納看起來不是確信，而是害怕。瑟蓮在這個所有人都等著她保持沉默的房間裡，努力尋找一個可以抓住的支點。她在逮捕令的日期章上找到了：十七日。火災卻在十八日午夜之後才發生。如果有人獲准把兩份官方紀錄並排閱讀，日期差異本該顯而易見。

The judge asked whether she wished to confess. Seren rose slowly and put a hand to her bosom, where a seamstress had hidden a folded receipt inside her dress. A strip cut from an old bra had been used to fasten the paper; the seamstress, Ada, had chosen that unlikely hiding place because guards searched bags, not hems. The receipt named an abandoned storehouse at the north junction. It was not a declaration of innocence. It was evidence that somebody had moved boxes away from the archive two days before it burned. Seren did not reveal the receipt yet. A person with finite chances must choose the moment of disclosure carefully.

法官詢問她是否願意認罪。瑟蓮緩緩站起，手按在胸前；一位名叫艾達的女裁縫，把摺好的收據藏在她衣服內。紙張用從舊胸罩剪下的布帶固定；艾達選了這個出人意料的藏處，因為守衛會搜袋子，卻不會檢查每一處衣縫。收據上寫著北方交叉路口的一座廢棄倉庫。這還不能證明她無罪，卻證明有人在檔案庫失火前兩天就把箱子搬走。瑟蓮尚未拿出收據。機會有限的人，必須慎選揭露證據的時機。

“Tell us the story yourself,” the prosecutor said. “Narrate how you entered the eastern district.” His smile was meant to make her flourish in anger, the way the fictional villain had. Seren refused the invitation. She asked a simpler question: “Which law is applicable when the accusation predates the alleged act?” A hush crossed the chamber. The judge's clerk, a young woman named Lysa, lifted her head. In this kingdom, the Rule of Three required the court to hear a document, a material trace, and independent testimony before imposing death. The novel had mentioned this rule early on and forgotten it at the trial.

「那就由你自己說吧。」檢察官開口，「敘述你如何進入東區。」他臉上的笑意，是想逼她像小說裡的反派那樣暴怒失態。瑟蓮拒絕上鉤，反而問了一個更簡單的問題：「如果指控的日期早於所指稱的行為，應適用哪一條法律？」法庭驟然安靜。法官的書記員萊莎抬起頭來。這個王國的「三證法則」要求法庭在判死刑前，必須聽取文件、物證與獨立證詞。原小說前段提過這條法律，卻在審判場景裡忘記了它。

A kitchen window stood open above the hall. Servants were preparing the midday meal, and the smell of onions being broiled drifted through the air. Ordinary life continued while Seren fought for hers. The prosecutor argued that the old parchment was a virgin sheet when the clerk wrote on it, untouched and beyond suspicion. Lysa answered that untouched paper could still carry a false date. The room changed. The teller swallowed. The limping guard stared at the floor. Seren understood that the court had not been waiting for truth; it had been waiting for a convenient ending. She could not force justice to arrive, but she could prevent that ending from passing unchallenged.

大廳上方的廚房窗戶開著，僕役正準備午餐，炙烤洋蔥的香氣飄進空氣。她為性命奮戰，平凡的日子卻照常前進。檢察官堅稱，書記員落筆時用的是一張全新、未經使用的羊皮紙，因此不可能有問題。萊莎回答，新紙照樣能被寫上虛假的日期。法庭的氣氛變了。出納吞了口口水，跛腳衛兵低頭望著地板。瑟蓮明白，這個法庭等的從來不是真相，而是一個方便的結局。她無法強迫正義立刻到來，卻能阻止那個結局在沒有質疑的情況下成立。

The judge consulted his colleagues and granted a ten-day delay. No acquittal, only time: the narrowest victory she could afford. Seren was ordered to remain under guard, but Lysa insisted that the prosecution must disclose the original ledger and permit a visit to the north junction. The crowd outside cheered neither side. As Seren descended the steps, she noticed Ada near the gate, pretending to bargain over cloth. Their small act of cooperation might yet flourish into a defense. In the story Mira remembered, Seren had died before speaking a single honest sentence. This time she had spoken, and one honest question had kept her alive.

法官與同僚商議後，准許延期十天。那不是無罪判決，只是時間，卻已是她能爭取到的最小勝利。瑟蓮仍須接受看守，但萊莎堅持檢方必須公開原始帳冊，並准許她前往北方交叉路口。門外的人群沒有為任何一方歡呼。瑟蓮走下階梯時，看見艾達在門邊假裝討價還價地買布。她們小小的合作，或許終能發展成完整的辯護。在米拉記得的故事裡，瑟蓮連一句真話都還沒說出口就死了。這一次，她說了；而一句誠實的疑問，替她留住了性命。
