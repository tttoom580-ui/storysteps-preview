改變礦場的表決

The Vote That Changed the Mine

反派的辯護人，決定先替自己翻案 · 第 15 / 25 章 · 5000–6000 · 英中對照 · 校訂中

The inquiry reached its first major decision at a public session of the academy. The chief examiner confirmed that the laboratory samples could be reproduced under controlled conditions. Their results supported contamination from the mine but not a precise account of who had concealed it. Seren asked the academy to ratify temporary safety standards immediately rather than wait for the criminal inquiry. The proposed plan included water filters, a medical subscription funded by local industries, and vocational training for workers who might lose their jobs. Several executives objected that the cost would bring the mine to the brink of closure. The panel listened but requested figures rather than predictions of ruin.

調查在學院公開會議上迎來第一項重大決定。首席審查員確認，實驗室樣本能在控制條件下重現結果。它們支持污染來自礦場，卻無法精確說明誰隱瞞了污染。瑟蓮請學院立即批准暫時性安全標準，而不是等待刑事調查結束。提案包括水質過濾器、由當地產業出資的醫療訂閱計畫，以及讓可能失業的工人接受職業訓練。數名企業主管反對，稱成本會讓礦場瀕臨關閉。小組願意聆聽，但要求他們提出數字，而非末日預言。

During a break, children from the orphanage visited a small traveling exhibition. A ceramic model showed the mountains and their streams, while a rescued chimpanzee watched the visitors from a shaded enclosure. A vendor offered food coupons to families waiting for the hearing. Seren saw the children chant a familiar tune and remembered the lullaby. For a moment she wanted to chase that clue, but Lysa warned against distraction. The hearing needed a clear decision on public safety. The missing crate could be investigated later without making frightened citizens wait for clean water. Seren returned to the chamber and asked the panel to vote on emergency measures first.

休息時，孤兒院的孩子參觀一場小型巡迴展。陶製模型展示山脈與溪流，一隻獲救的黑猩猩在遮蔭的圍欄內望著訪客。攤販向等待聽證的家庭發放食物兌換券。瑟蓮聽見孩子唱起熟悉的曲調，想起搖籃曲，瞬間想追查那條線索；萊莎卻提醒她別分心。聽證必須先對公共安全作出明確決定。失蹤箱子可以之後繼續追查，不能讓害怕的市民繼續等乾淨的水。瑟蓮回到會議廳，請小組先表決緊急措施。

An official attempted to frighten witnesses by reading out their old disciplinary records. Sergeant Dain, who supervised the gallery, stopped him and cited a rule protecting irrelevant private information. The intervention was instinctive but lawful. A company affiliate then argued that the miners had acted recklessly by working through shutdown notices. Jorin answered that employees had been ordered to report for duty or perish financially. Their choices were constrained by wages and family responsibilities. Seren did not excuse every unsafe action. She asked the panel to examine who had authority to change the schedule and who profited from the concealed operations. Responsibility, she argued, should follow power as well as action.

一名官員試圖念出證人的舊懲戒紀錄來威嚇他們。負責旁聽席的戴恩中士阻止了他，引用保護無關私人資訊的規定。他的反應近乎本能，卻合法。一家關係企業接著指責礦工不顧停工通知、魯莽工作。喬林回答，員工若不照命令上工，就可能失去經濟來源。工資與家庭責任限制了他們的選擇。瑟蓮沒有為所有不安全行為開脫，而是要求小組調查誰有權改變時程，以及誰從隱蔽運作中獲利。她主張，責任不只應追隨行為，也應追隨權力。

The vote was close, but the temporary plan passed. Workers would receive clean water and medical assistance, while the mine operated only under independent supervision. The academy would publish each test method and maintain a public record thereafter. Seren had hoped for a complete judgment against the company, yet she understood the value of a limited measure that could begin at once. Outside, a sparrow landed on a rail above the crowd. The sky remained gray, but the immediate danger had been reduced. The villagers did not cheer Seren's name. They cheered the announcement that water would be delivered the next morning.

表決差距很小，但臨時方案通過了。工人將獲得乾淨飲水與醫療協助，礦場則只能在獨立監督下運作。學院此後須公開每項檢測方法，並保存公共紀錄。瑟蓮原本希望直接作出針對公司的完整判決，卻理解一項立刻能實施的有限措施有其價值。外頭，一隻麻雀落在群眾上方的欄杆。天空仍灰暗，眼前的危險卻已降低。村民歡呼的不是瑟蓮的名字，而是隔天早晨開始送水的消息。

After the vote, a teacher invited Seren to observe a quiet yoga session for recovering workers. The participants hoped to regain strength, not to perform for visitors. One man said, “I aspire to return to my trade without risking another illness.” Another admitted that he had made a reckless decision by ignoring a leaking pipe because he feared his supervisor. Seren thanked them for speaking plainly. The safety program would need space for human mistakes as well as corporate responsibility. A rule that assumed every person was perfectly brave or perfectly informed would fail the first time ordinary fear entered the room.

表決後，一名教師邀瑟蓮旁觀為康復工人安排的安靜瑜伽課。參與者只希望恢復體力，並不是為訪客表演。一位男子說他渴望能回到原本工作，又不必冒再次生病的風險。另一人承認，自己因害怕主管而忽略漏水管線，是個魯莽決定。瑟蓮感謝他們坦誠。安全計畫必須容納人會犯錯的事實，也必須追究企業責任。若一條規則假設每個人都永遠勇敢、永遠掌握全部資訊，那麼普通恐懼一進門，規則就會失效。

After the session, Nera handed Seren a receipt for the academy's oldest laboratory supply contract. It named an examiner who had once worked for Ormond's brother, then moved to the royal archive. The signature looked ordinary, but the attached conditions were unusual: the company had permission to withdraw any duplicate sample without notifying the researchers. That power explained how the evidence could disappear. It did not explain the fire. Seren asked Lysa to preserve the contract and invite the former examiner to testify. A vote could protect the public, but the people who had manipulated evidence still had to be held accountable through a separate process.

會議結束後，妮拉交給瑟蓮一張學院最舊實驗室採購契約的收據。上面列著一名審查員，他曾替奧蒙德兄弟工作，之後轉往皇家檔案庫。簽名看似普通，附加條款卻不尋常：公司有權在不通知研究員的情況下取走任何重複樣本。這項權力說明證據為何能消失，卻仍無法解釋火災。瑟蓮請萊莎保管契約，並邀請前任審查員作證。表決能保護民眾，但操縱證據的人仍須透過另一套程序承擔責任。

What does the public vote establish?

  1. Immediate safety measures and independent mine oversight.
  2. Seren’s unconditional innocence.
