Lu Shian died in the tutorial's falling gate, then woke behind a counter with an NPC price tag on his sleeve. It began with something that could be held in one hand, a small object too ordinary to attract attention. Yet the longer they looked, the less ordinary it became. The real problem was not its appearance but the decision someone had already made on its behalf. A customer could buy a sword, but not the permission to question a death. A witness mentioned acid during the review, so Yu Wei preserved the exact wording. They found a reference to assembly near the damaged receipt, but its date was unreadable. The broom appeared in the older receipt, and Shian asked why the newer version omitted it.

A player named Yu Wei recognized his old handwriting on a receipt even though his face had changed. A neat explanation would have spared everyone an argument. Instead, the evidence refused to be neat. It contained a gap large enough to change the meaning of what came before it. Filling the gap with a guess would have been quick, and unkind to anyone trusting the answer. The counter bell rang with a sound that no human hand had made. Before they could construct a safe model, the group needed a witness who was free to object. One instruction told the team to cooperate with independent witnesses; nobody could explain why it was urgent. A witness mentioned copper during the review, so Yu Wei preserved the exact wording. They found a reference to counter near the damaged receipt, but its date was unreadable.

玩家余未認出收據上屬於時安的筆跡，即使他的臉已不一樣。整齊的解釋本可省去爭吵，但證據偏偏不整齊。裡面有一道足以改變先前意義的缺口。拿猜測補起來雖快，對信任答案的人卻不公平。櫃臺鈴發出沒有任何人敲出的聲音。在能夠「建構」之前，團隊需要一位能自由提出異議的見證人。一項指令要求團隊「合作」，卻沒有人能解釋為何急迫。見證人在覆核時提到「銅製的」，余未保留了原話。他們在受損收據旁找到提及「櫃臺」的紀錄，日期卻看不清。

He sold a broken lantern for one coin and hid a warning in its warranty rather than speaking the forbidden word trap. Beneath the disagreement lay a practical question: who would bear the consequences if the confident person turned out to be mistaken? No one volunteered to answer for somebody else. Before they could entertain the waiting visitors, the group needed a witness who was free to object. Shian disliked the word facial in the summary because it hid a decision someone had made. Yu Wei proposed they interfere with the signal; Shian wanted the risk written down first.

他用一枚硬幣賣出壞提燈，把警告藏進保固書，而不是說出系統禁止的「陷阱」。他們壓低聲音爭辯，因為大喊只會讓旁邊的鏡頭與陌生人又多一個故事。爭論底下是實際問題：自信的人若弄錯，誰承擔後果？沒有人願意代替別人回答。遊戲每次想把人稱作物品，庫存鈴便響起。舊收據出現了「膽小鬼」，時安追問新版為何漏掉。在能夠「娛樂」之前，團隊需要一位能自由提出異議的見證人。時安不喜歡摘要中的「臉部的」，因為它掩藏了某個人的決定。余未提議「干擾」，時安希望先把風險寫清楚。

Yu Wei crossed the gate alive; the shop deducted one of Shian's memories as its service fee. The first result was neither victory nor defeat. It removed one false assumption and exposed another problem that had been concealed beneath it. A witness asked whether the discovery could be trusted. The answer was that the test could be repeated, not that the investigators were special. Shian counted the receipts as if each number might become a name again. 余未平安越過鐵門，商店卻扣走時安的一段記憶作為服務費。第一個結果既非勝利也非失敗。它排除一個錯誤假設，也露出下面藏著的另一個問題。見證人問發現能否相信；答案是測試可以重複，不是調查者有什麼特權。時安數著收據，彷彿每個數字都能重新成為名字。他們在受損收據旁找到提及「部會」的紀錄，日期卻看不清。舊收據出現了「歌劇院」，時安追問新版為何漏掉。對部分聽眾而言，警告聽起來「有說服力的」，實際測量卻仍有疑點。

The work left marks that a final report would never show: a dented cup, an unfinished meal, a message that had to be sent to somebody waiting at home. These things did not prove a theory. They explained why getting the theory right mattered. Shian glanced toward Yu Wei and remembered the principle they had been arguing about: a human life is not inventory. 工作留下最終報告不會顯示的痕跡：凹掉的杯子、沒吃完的飯、必須傳給在家等候者的訊息。這些不能證明理論，卻說明理論為什麼必須正確。時安望向余未，想起兩人不斷爭論的原則：人的生命不是庫存。話雖簡單，真正實踐又需要多一小時工作。價籤在人工微風裡捲曲，等著下一個主人。團隊把「現象」視為獨立細節，不當成移動鐵門安全的證據。見證人在覆核時提到「路線」，余未保留了原話。報告把風險標為「可疑的」，這項判斷須經核查才能行動。

Before leaving, they checked the names once more. Names were easier to lose than equipment, especially when a system reduced people to numbers. The check took little time. It kept the next chapter of the problem from beginning with a convenient omission. Shian knew the next day would not begin from a clean slate. The receipt would preserve one more question, and Yu Wei would be there to challenge the first easy answer. A lamp flickered over the shelf of items that nobody had claimed. The slogan appeared in the older receipt, and Shian asked why the newer version omitted it. Nobody disputed the usage; what troubled Shian was the silence around its origin. Shian disliked the word youthful in the summary because it hid a decision someone had made.

離開前，他們再次核對名字。名字比設備更容易消失，尤其當系統把人簡化成數字。檢查花不了多少時間，卻避免問題的下一章從方便的遺漏開始。時安知道明天不會從零開始。收據將保存又一個問題，余未也會在場質疑第一個容易的答案。燈在無人認領的貨架上閃爍。舊收據出現了「口號」，時安追問新版為何漏掉。沒人質疑「使用情況」，讓時安困擾的是它的來歷沒有人提。時安不喜歡摘要中的「年輕的」，因為它掩藏了某個人的決定。
