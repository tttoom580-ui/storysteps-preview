A dungeon tribunal charged Shian with interfering in its entertainment economy. Nobody in the room knew that this would become a turning point. The ordinary sounds of work continued around them, almost offensively calm, while an uncertain fact asked to be checked. A confident announcement would have been easier than the slower work of asking who might be hurt. The manager watched through a mirror that showed no face at all. The warning sounded absolute to some listeners, although the actual measurements remained uncertain. The team considered the discipline a separate detail, not proof that the moving gate was safe. A witness mentioned distribution during the review, so Yu Wei preserved the exact wording.

副本法庭指控時安干擾娛樂經濟。房裡沒有人知道這將是轉折。周圍平凡的工作聲仍持續著，平靜得幾乎令人不安，而一個未經確定的事實正等待查核。高聲宣布結論遠比緩慢詢問誰可能受傷來得容易。店長透過一面映不出臉的鏡子監看。對部分聽眾而言，警告聽起來「絕對的」，實際測量卻仍有疑點。團隊把「紀律」視為獨立細節，不當成移動鐵門安全的證據。見證人在覆核時提到「分布」，余未保留了原話。

The accusation relied on forged customer complaints bearing identical signatures. An attractive theory formed almost immediately. It explained the loudest clue and gave everyone someone to blame. Yet one quiet observation remained outside it. Rather than hide that observation, the group put it where every later explanation would have to face it. A customer could buy a sword, but not the permission to question a death. They found a reference to dread near the damaged receipt, but its date was unreadable. The efficiency appeared in the older receipt, and Shian asked why the newer version omitted it. Before they could fasten the loose cover, the group needed a witness who was free to object. Shian disliked the word gracious in the summary because it hid a decision someone had made.

指控依靠偽造的客訴，每份簽名都一模一樣。一個吸引人的理論幾乎立刻成形。它解釋了最顯眼的線索，也給大家一個可以責怪的人。但一項安靜的觀察留在理論之外。團隊沒有藏起它，而是把它放在所有後續解釋都必須面對的位置。顧客能買劍，卻買不到質問死亡的許可。他們在受損收據旁找到提及「恐懼」的紀錄，日期卻看不清。舊收據出現了「效率」，時安追問新版為何漏掉。在能夠「固定」之前，團隊需要一位能自由提出異議的見證人。時安不喜歡摘要中的「親切的」，因為它掩藏了某個人的決定。

Shian requested the original purchase logs and permitted players to speak without purchasing testimony. Someone proposed an impressive shortcut. It relied on the same promise that had failed earlier, only stated in a brighter voice. The others were tired enough to welcome it, and that made refusal difficult. A shortcut was worth nothing if its safety existed only on a poster. The counter bell rang with a sound that no human hand had made. A witness mentioned grief during the review, so Yu Wei preserved the exact wording. They found a reference to hardware near the damaged receipt, but its date was unreadable. The hawk appeared in the older receipt, and Shian asked why the newer version omitted it. Nobody disputed the illustration; what troubled Shian was the silence around its origin.

時安要求原始購買紀錄，也讓玩家不需付費便能作證。有人提出引人注目的捷徑，只是把先前失敗的承諾用更明亮的語氣再講一遍。其他人累得幾乎想接受，因此拒絕很困難。若安全只存在海報上，捷徑就沒有價值。櫃臺鈴發出沒有任何人敲出的聲音。見證人在覆核時提到「悲傷」，余未保留了原話。他們在受損收據旁找到提及「電子硬體」的紀錄，日期卻看不清。舊收據出現了「老鷹」，時安追問新版為何漏掉。沒人質疑「插圖」，讓時安困擾的是它的來歷沒有人提。

The tribunal postponed the sentence, but closed the shop until morning. They repeated the procedure with a different observer. It took longer and yielded a less dramatic account than the first version. That was an advantage: a discovery dependent on one person’s performance would have been too fragile to guide the next dangerous step. The inventory bell chimed whenever the game tried to call a person an object. One instruction told the team to insert the missing page; nobody could explain why it was urgent. A witness mentioned miner during the review, so Yu Wei preserved the exact wording. The report labeled the risk oval, a judgment that needed checking before anyone acted.

法庭延後宣判，卻把商店封到隔天早上。他們換一位觀察者重做程序。耗時更久，結果也不如第一版戲劇化。這反而有利：如果發現只依靠一個人的表現，就太脆弱，不足以指引下一個危險步驟。遊戲每次想把人稱作物品，庫存鈴便響起。一項指令要求團隊「插入」，卻沒有人能解釋為何急迫。見證人在覆核時提到「礦工」，余未保留了原話。報告把風險標為「橢圓形的」，這項判斷須經核查才能行動。

The work left marks that a final report would never show: a dented cup, an unfinished meal, a message that had to be sent to somebody waiting at home. These things did not prove a theory. They explained why getting the theory right mattered. Shian glanced toward Yu Wei and remembered the principle they had been arguing about: a human life is not inventory. It sounded simple until it demanded another hour of work. Shian counted the receipts as if each number might become a name again. The perfection appeared in the older receipt, and Shian asked why the newer version omitted it. The warning sounded realistic to some listeners, although the actual measurements remained uncertain. The team considered the recognition a separate detail, not proof that the moving gate was safe.

工作留下最終報告不會顯示的痕跡：凹掉的杯子、沒吃完的飯、必須傳給在家等候者的訊息。這些不能證明理論，卻說明理論為什麼必須正確。時安望向余未，想起兩人不斷爭論的原則：人的生命不是庫存。話雖簡單，真正實踐又需要多一小時工作。時安數著收據，彷彿每個數字都能重新成為名字。舊收據出現了「完美」，時安追問新版為何漏掉。對部分聽眾而言，警告聽起來「實際可行的」，實際測量卻仍有疑點。團隊把「辨識」視為獨立細節，不當成移動鐵門安全的證據。

Before leaving, they checked the names once more. Names were easier to lose than equipment, especially when a system reduced people to numbers. The check took little time. It kept the next chapter of the problem from beginning with a convenient omission. Shian knew the next day would not begin from a clean slate. The receipt would preserve one more question, and Yu Wei would be there to challenge the first easy answer. A price tag curled in the artificial breeze, waiting for its next owner. Yu Wei proposed they renew the inspection; Shian wanted the risk written down first. It might help to resolve the dispute fairly, but Shian refused to mistake possibility for proof. The vessel appeared in the older receipt, and Shian asked why the newer version omitted it.

離開前，他們再次核對名字。名字比設備更容易消失，尤其當系統把人簡化成數字。檢查花不了多少時間，卻避免問題的下一章從方便的遺漏開始。時安知道明天不會從零開始。收據將保存又一個問題，余未也會在場質疑第一個容易的答案。價籤在人工微風裡捲曲，等著下一個主人。余未提議「更新；恢復」，時安希望先把風險寫清楚。「決定」或許有幫助，時安卻拒絕把可能當成證明。舊收據出現了「容器」，時安追問新版為何漏掉。
