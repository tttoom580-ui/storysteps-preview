真名的價錢

The Price of a True Name

An exhausted newcomer asked to buy the name of a missing friend. The morning offered no dramatic warning. Someone was carrying cups, someone else was checking a door, and the usual machinery made its familiar complaints. Only one detail refused to fit the story everyone had prepared to believe; once noticed, it made the whole place seem different. The inventory bell chimed whenever the game tried to call a person an object. One instruction told the team to arise from the new facts; nobody could explain why it was urgent. A witness mentioned association during the review, so Yu Wei preserved the exact wording. They found a reference to autobiography near the damaged receipt, but its date was unreadable.

疲憊的新玩家想買回失蹤朋友的名字。早晨沒有戲劇性的警告。有人端著杯子，有人檢查門，機械照常發出熟悉的雜音。只有一個細節無法塞進大家原本準備相信的故事；一旦看見它，整個地方都變得不同。遊戲每次想把人稱作物品，庫存鈴便響起。一項指令要求團隊「產生」，卻沒有人能解釋為何急迫。見證人在覆核時提到「協會」，余未保留了原話。他們在受損收據旁找到提及「自傳」的紀錄，日期卻看不清。

The shop listed a price in memories, yet its refund policy allowed a witness to make the same request for free. An attractive theory formed almost immediately. It explained the loudest clue and gave everyone someone to blame. Yet one quiet observation remained outside it. Rather than hide that observation, the group put it where every later explanation would have to face it. Shian counted the receipts as if each number might become a name again. The public account described the situation as bald, but Shian asked for the observation behind that claim. The warning sounded consequent to some listeners, although the actual measurements remained uncertain. The team considered the curiosity a separate detail, not proof that the moving gate was safe. Yu Wei proposed they declare the area safe; Shian wanted the risk written down first.

商店以記憶標價，但退款規則容許見證人免費提出相同申請。一個吸引人的理論幾乎立刻成形。它解釋了最顯眼的線索，也給大家一個可以責怪的人。但一項安靜的觀察留在理論之外。團隊沒有藏起它，而是把它放在所有後續解釋都必須面對的位置。時安數著收據，彷彿每個數字都能重新成為名字。公開說法把情況形容為「光頭的」，時安要求查看支撐判斷的觀察。對部分聽眾而言，警告聽起來「隨之而來的」，實際測量卻仍有疑點。團隊把「好奇心」視為獨立細節，不當成移動鐵門安全的證據。余未提議「宣布」，時安希望先把風險寫清楚。

Shian helped the newcomer collect witnesses instead of making a desperate sale. The decision required a kind of patience that was easily mistaken for fear. Their tools were limited and the timetable already slipping. Yet the people affected had not agreed to be used as proof that an ambitious plan could work. A price tag curled in the artificial breeze, waiting for its next owner. The report labeled the risk dependent, a judgment that needed checking before anyone acted. The fireplace appeared in the older receipt, and Shian asked why the newer version omitted it. Nobody disputed the forecast; what troubled Shian was the silence around its origin. The team considered the halt a separate detail, not proof that the moving gate was safe.

時安協助新人蒐集見證，不趁絕望交易。這個決定需要一種容易被誤認為害怕的耐心。工具有限，時程也在延後，但受影響的人並沒有同意當作證明野心計畫可行的材料。價籤在人工微風裡捲曲，等著下一個主人。報告把風險標為「依賴的」，這項判斷須經核查才能行動。舊收據出現了「壁爐」，時安追問新版為何漏掉。沒人質疑「預測」，讓時安困擾的是它的來歷沒有人提。團隊把「停止」視為獨立細節，不當成移動鐵門安全的證據。

The missing friend's name reappeared in the public registry, followed by a location code. The first result was neither victory nor defeat. It removed one false assumption and exposed another problem that had been concealed beneath it. A witness asked whether the discovery could be trusted. The answer was that the test could be repeated, not that the investigators were special. A lamp flickered over the shelf of items that nobody had claimed. A witness mentioned lobster during the review, so Yu Wei preserved the exact wording. They found a reference to machinery near the damaged receipt, but its date was unreadable. The minister appeared in the older receipt, and Shian asked why the newer version omitted it.

失蹤朋友的名字重回公開名冊，後面出現位置代碼。第一個結果既非勝利也非失敗。它排除一個錯誤假設，也露出下面藏著的另一個問題。見證人問發現能否相信；答案是測試可以重複，不是調查者有什麼特權。燈在無人認領的貨架上閃爍。見證人在覆核時提到「龍蝦」，余未保留了原話。他們在受損收據旁找到提及「機械設備」的紀錄，日期卻看不清。舊收據出現了「部長」，時安追問新版為何漏掉。

A small act of care interrupted the discussion. It solved no mystery and changed no official ruling. Yet it reminded them that the people named in the records were not pieces on a board; they had bodies, families and the right to decide what came next. Shian glanced toward Yu Wei and remembered the principle they had been arguing about: a human life is not inventory. It sounded simple until it demanded another hour of work. Yu Wei touched the doorframe before entering, checking which rules still held. Nobody disputed the pasta; what troubled Shian was the silence around its origin. The team considered the quotation a separate detail, not proof that the moving gate was safe. Yu Wei proposed they restore the missing record; Shian wanted the risk written down first.

一個小小的照顧動作打斷討論。它沒有解開謎，也沒改變裁決，卻提醒大家紀錄裡的人不是棋子；他們有身體、家庭，也有決定下一步的權利。時安望向余未，想起兩人不斷爭論的原則：人的生命不是庫存。話雖簡單，真正實踐又需要多一小時工作。余未進門前摸了門框，確認哪些規則仍然有效。沒人質疑「義大利麵」，讓時安困擾的是它的來歷沒有人提。團隊把「報價單」視為獨立細節，不當成移動鐵門安全的證據。余未提議「恢復」，時安希望先把風險寫清楚。

They filed the result beside the earlier mistakes, not above them. Tomorrow would require a different question and perhaps a different kind of courage. For now, the important thing was that the people most affected could read what had happened and challenge the account if it was wrong. Shian knew the next day would not begin from a clean slate. The receipt would preserve one more question, and Yu Wei would be there to challenge the first easy answer. Beyond the shop glass, the dungeon changed its corridors without asking the players. The report labeled the risk rusty, a judgment that needed checking before anyone acted. The vacancy appeared in the older receipt, and Shian asked why the newer version omitted it. Before they could violate the agreement, the group needed a witness who was free to object.

他們把結果放在先前的錯誤旁邊，而不是蓋過錯誤。明天需要不同的問題，也許需要不同的勇氣。眼下最重要的是受影響者能讀到經過，也能在紀錄錯誤時提出挑戰。時安知道明天不會從零開始。收據將保存又一個問題，余未也會在場質疑第一個容易的答案。櫥窗外，副本未徵詢玩家便改變走廊。報告把風險標為「生鏽的」，這項判斷須經核查才能行動。舊收據出現了「職缺」，時安追問新版為何漏掉。在能夠「違反」之前，團隊需要一位能自由提出異議的見證人。
