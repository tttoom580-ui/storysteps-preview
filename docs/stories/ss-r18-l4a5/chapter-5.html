沒有眼睛的店長

The Manager with No Eyes

A faceless manager appeared and demanded that Shian destroy the copied names. The morning offered no dramatic warning. Someone was carrying cups, someone else was checking a door, and the usual machinery made its familiar complaints. Only one detail refused to fit the story everyone had prepared to believe; once noticed, it made the whole place seem different. Yu Wei touched the doorframe before entering, checking which rules still held. The plan called for them to adopt a safer rule, and Shian asked who would check the result. Nobody disputed the agency; what troubled Shian was the silence around its origin. The team considered the behavior a separate detail, not proof that the moving gate was safe.

無臉店長現身，要求時安銷毀抄寫的姓名。早晨沒有戲劇性的警告。有人端著杯子，有人檢查門，機械照常發出熟悉的雜音。只有一個細節無法塞進大家原本準備相信的故事；一旦看見它，整個地方都變得不同。余未進門前摸了門框，確認哪些規則仍然有效。計畫要求他們「採用」，時安追問由誰查核結果。沒人質疑「機構」，讓時安困擾的是它的來歷沒有人提。團隊把「行為」視為獨立細節，不當成移動鐵門安全的證據。

Its contract forbade lying about prices but said nothing about publishing refunded purchases. The easiest part of the investigation was drawing a line between two points. The difficult part was deciding what that line meant. It might show a connection, a coincidence, or an error copied from an older file. No one wanted to spend another day checking. Beyond the shop glass, the dungeon changed its corridors without asking the players. Yu Wei proposed they cherish the last message; Shian wanted the risk written down first. They found a reference to congress near the damaged receipt, but its date was unreadable. The conscience appeared in the older receipt, and Shian asked why the newer version omitted it. Nobody disputed the decoration; what troubled Shian was the silence around its origin.

契約禁止虛報價格，卻沒有禁止公布退款紀錄。調查最容易的部分，是在兩個點之間畫線。困難的是決定那條線的意思。它可能代表關聯、巧合，也可能是從舊檔複製的錯誤。沒有人想再花一天查證。櫥窗外，副本未徵詢玩家便改變走廊。余未提議「珍惜」，時安希望先把風險寫清楚。他們在受損收據旁找到提及「議會」的紀錄，日期卻看不清。舊收據出現了「良心」，時安追問新版為何漏掉。沒人質疑「裝飾」，讓時安困擾的是它的來歷沒有人提。

Shian printed every name as a refund rather than trying to fight the manager. Someone proposed an impressive shortcut. It relied on the same promise that had failed earlier, only stated in a brighter voice. The others were tired enough to welcome it, and that made refusal difficult. A shortcut was worth nothing if its safety existed only on a poster. A blank receipt lay on the counter like a question nobody had dared to ask. One instruction told the team to discourage a dangerous shortcut; nobody could explain why it was urgent. A witness called the result fundamental; Yu Wei wrote the adjective beside the witness’s name. It might help to horrify the witnesses with careless claims, but Shian refused to mistake possibility for proof. The public account described the situation as influential, but Shian asked for the observation behind that claim.

時安把每個名字印成退款紀錄，沒有和店長硬拚。有人提出引人注目的捷徑，只是把先前失敗的承諾用更明亮的語氣再講一遍。其他人累得幾乎想接受，因此拒絕很困難。若安全只存在海報上，捷徑就沒有價值。空白收據躺在櫃臺上，像沒人敢問的問題。一項指令要求團隊「使卻步」，卻沒有人能解釋為何急迫。見證人稱結果「根本的；基本的」，余未把形容詞記在見證人姓名旁。「使驚恐」或許有幫助，時安卻拒絕把可能當成證明。公開說法把情況形容為「有影響力的」，時安要求查看支撐判斷的觀察。

The manager withdrew, and a hidden maintenance staircase became visible behind the shelves. For a moment the whole plan seemed to fail. The expected signal never arrived, and the people waiting outside grew impatient. Then a smaller detail emerged, one that would have been lost if the group had covered the mistake with a confident announcement. The manager watched through a mirror that showed no face at all. Nobody disputed the interaction; what troubled Shian was the silence around its origin. The team considered the outcome a separate detail, not proof that the moving gate was safe. A witness called the result primitive; Yu Wei wrote the adjective beside the witness’s name.

店長退去，貨架後顯露一座隱藏的維修樓梯。有一刻整個計畫像要失敗。預期訊號沒有來，外面等候的人越來越不耐。隨後出現更小的細節；若團隊用自信公告掩蓋錯誤，那個細節就會消失。店長透過一面映不出臉的鏡子監看。沒人質疑「相互作用」，讓時安困擾的是它的來歷沒有人提。團隊把「結果」視為獨立細節，不當成移動鐵門安全的證據。見證人稱結果「古早的」，余未把形容詞記在見證人姓名旁。

The place was still full of ordinary life. Someone laughed at a poor joke, someone swept a corner, and a tired worker counted the minutes to the next shift. Those details made the danger harder to ignore, because the people involved had lives outside this puzzle. Shian glanced toward Yu Wei and remembered the principle they had been arguing about: a human life is not inventory. It sounded simple until it demanded another hour of work. A customer could buy a sword, but not the permission to question a death. They found a reference to reception near the damaged receipt, but its date was unreadable. The robber appeared in the older receipt, and Shian asked why the newer version omitted it. Nobody disputed the scenery; what troubled Shian was the silence around its origin.

這地方仍充滿平凡生活。有人為難笑話發笑，有人掃地，疲憊的工作者算著距離換班還有幾分鐘。這些細節讓危險更難忽略，因為受影響者在謎題之外還有人生。時安望向余未，想起兩人不斷爭論的原則：人的生命不是庫存。話雖簡單，真正實踐又需要多一小時工作。顧客能買劍，卻買不到質問死亡的許可。他們在受損收據旁找到提及「接待會」的紀錄，日期卻看不清。舊收據出現了「強盜」，時安追問新版為何漏掉。沒人質疑「舞臺布景」，讓時安困擾的是它的來歷沒有人提。

Evening came without resolving the argument completely. The record was clearer, but the consequences still belonged to real people rather than to the neat ending of a tale. The next step would need their consent, their questions and the patience to hear an unwelcome answer. Shian knew the next day would not begin from a clean slate. The receipt would preserve one more question, and Yu Wei would be there to challenge the first easy answer. The counter bell rang with a sound that no human hand had made. Shian disliked the word shameful in the summary because it hid a decision someone had made. A witness mentioned terror during the review, so Yu Wei preserved the exact wording. They found a reference to witch near the damaged receipt, but its date was unreadable.

傍晚到來，爭論沒有完全解決。紀錄清楚了，後果卻仍屬於真實的人，不屬於故事漂亮的結尾。下一步需要他們的同意、提問，以及聽進不想聽的答案的耐心。時安知道明天不會從零開始。收據將保存又一個問題，余未也會在場質疑第一個容易的答案。櫃臺鈴發出沒有任何人敲出的聲音。時安不喜歡摘要中的「可恥的」，因為它掩藏了某個人的決定。見證人在覆核時提到「恐懼」，余未保留了原話。他們在受損收據旁找到提及「巫師」的紀錄，日期卻看不清。
