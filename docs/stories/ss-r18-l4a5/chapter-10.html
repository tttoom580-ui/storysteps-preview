Someone was carrying cups, someone else was checking a door, and the usual machinery made its familiar complaints. Only one detail refused to fit the story everyone had prepared to believe; once noticed, it made the whole place seem different. The counter bell rang with a sound that no human hand had made. Fear projections grew stronger whenever spectators paid for more dramatic scenes. An attractive theory formed almost immediately. It explained the loudest clue and gave everyone someone to blame. Yet one quiet observation remained outside it. Rather than hide that observation, the group put it where every later explanation would have to face it. The inventory bell chimed whenever the game tried to call a person an object. A witness called the result authentic; Yu Wei wrote the adjective beside the witness’s name. It might help to classify the report, but Shian refused to mistake possibility for proof. The plan called for them to differ from the first account, and Shian asked who would check the result. Nobody disputed the dignity; what troubled Shian was the silence around its origin.

每當觀眾付錢看更戲劇化的場面，恐懼投影就會變強。一個吸引人的理論幾乎立刻成形。它解釋了最顯眼的線索，也給大家一個可以責怪的人。但一項安靜的觀察留在理論之外。團隊沒有藏起它，而是把它放在所有後續解釋都必須面對的位置。遊戲每次想把人稱作物品，庫存鈴便響起。見證人稱結果「真實可靠的」，余未把形容詞記在見證人姓名旁。「分類」或許有幫助，時安卻拒絕把可能當成證明。計畫要求他們「有所不同」，時安追問由誰查核結果。沒人質疑「尊嚴」，讓時安困擾的是它的來歷沒有人提。

Yu Wei ordered the team to stop bidding and to cover every payment screen. A refusal sounded small compared with the danger outside. Even so, it changed what the group understood about its limits. No one should have to give up a private history, a safe route, or an honest answer merely to prove they were cooperative. Shian counted the receipts as if each number might become a name again. The report labeled the risk genuine, a judgment that needed checking before anyone acted. The public account described the situation as offensive, but Shian asked for the observation behind that claim.

余未要求隊伍停止出價，遮住每一面付款螢幕。面對外面的危險，一次拒絕聽來很微小，卻改變了團隊對界線的理解。沒有人必須放棄私人經歷、安全路線或誠實答案，才能證明自己願意合作。時安數著收據，彷彿每個數字都能重新成為名字。一項指令要求團隊「使人難堪」，卻沒有人能解釋為何急迫。見證人在覆核時提到「設施」，余未保留了原話。報告把風險標為「真實的」，這項判斷須經核查才能行動。公開說法把情況形容為「惡意的」，時安要求查看支撐判斷的觀察。

The projections faded without a battle, revealing prisoners behind a curtain. For a moment the whole plan seemed to fail. The expected signal never arrived, and the people waiting outside grew impatient. Then a smaller detail emerged, one that would have been lost if the group had covered the mistake with a confident announcement. 投影無須戰鬥便逐漸消散，簾後露出被囚的人。有一刻整個計畫像要失敗。預期訊號沒有來，外面等候的人越來越不耐。隨後出現更小的細節；若團隊用自信公告掩蓋錯誤，那個細節就會消失。價籤在人工微風裡捲曲，等著下一個主人。沒人質疑「選項」，讓時安困擾的是它的來歷沒有人提。團隊把「香水」視為獨立細節，不當成移動鐵門安全的證據。見證人在覆核時提到「行銷」，余未保留了原話。

The place was still full of ordinary life. Someone laughed at a poor joke, someone swept a corner, and a tired worker counted the minutes to the next shift. Those details made the danger harder to ignore, because the people involved had lives outside this puzzle. Shian glanced toward Yu Wei and remembered the principle they had been arguing about: a human life is not inventory. It sounded simple until it demanded another hour of work. A lamp flickered over the shelf of items that nobody had claimed. They found a reference to publisher near the damaged receipt, but its date was unreadable. The refugee appeared in the older receipt, and Shian asked why the newer version omitted it. Nobody disputed the rescue; what troubled Shian was the silence around its origin.

這地方仍充滿平凡生活。有人為難笑話發笑，有人掃地，疲憊的工作者算著距離換班還有幾分鐘。這些細節讓危險更難忽略，因為受影響者在謎題之外還有人生。時安望向余未，想起兩人不斷爭論的原則：人的生命不是庫存。話雖簡單，真正實踐又需要多一小時工作。燈在無人認領的貨架上閃爍。他們在受損收據旁找到提及「出版社」的紀錄，日期卻看不清。舊收據出現了「難民」，時安追問新版為何漏掉。沒人質疑「救援」，讓時安困擾的是它的來歷沒有人提。

The question that followed was smaller and more frightening than the one they had brought to the room. It asked not whether they could win, but whether their answer would remain safe after they were gone. Nobody pretended to know yet, and that honesty held them together. Shian knew the next day would not begin from a clean slate. The receipt would preserve one more question, and Yu Wei would be there to challenge the first easy answer.
