被遺忘之人的庫存

Inventory of Forgotten People

Shian found an inventory drawer full of name tags from players who had vanished. The first sign of trouble was not a scream. It was a pause in an otherwise predictable routine, long enough for a careful person to notice. Others kept moving, and their movement made it tempting to do the same, but the pause deserved an explanation. Beyond the shop glass, the dungeon changed its corridors without asking the players. One instruction told the team to accomplish the limited repair; nobody could explain why it was urgent. Yu Wei proposed they amuse the tired children; Shian wanted the risk written down first. They found a reference to approval near the damaged receipt, but its date was unreadable.

時安在庫存抽屜裡發現滿滿失蹤玩家的名牌。第一個麻煩徵兆不是尖叫，而是原本可預期的流程忽然停頓，長到足以讓細心的人察覺。其他人繼續前進，令人忍不住跟上，可那次停頓需要解釋。櫥窗外，副本未徵詢玩家便改變走廊。一項指令要求團隊「成就；完成」，卻沒有人能解釋為何急迫。余未提議「逗樂；令人發笑」，時安希望先把風險寫清楚。他們在受損收據旁找到提及「核准」的紀錄，日期卻看不清。

Each tag retained one unfinished wish, while official records counted the person as a cleared obstacle. An attractive theory formed almost immediately. It explained the loudest clue and gave everyone someone to blame. Yet one quiet observation remained outside it. Rather than hide that observation, the group put it where every later explanation would have to face it. A blank receipt lay on the counter like a question nobody had dared to ask. The plan called for them to bounce off the sealed door, and Shian asked who would check the result. The warning sounded continual to some listeners, although the actual measurements remained uncertain. One instruction told the team to disappoint the volunteers; nobody could explain why it was urgent. A witness called the result earnest; Yu Wei wrote the adjective beside the witness’s name.

每枚名牌保留了一個未完成的願望，官方紀錄卻只把他們算作已清除的障礙。一個吸引人的理論幾乎立刻成形。它解釋了最顯眼的線索，也給大家一個可以責怪的人。但一項安靜的觀察留在理論之外。團隊沒有藏起它，而是把它放在所有後續解釋都必須面對的位置。空白收據躺在櫃臺上，像沒人敢問的問題。計畫要求他們「彈跳」，時安追問由誰查核結果。對部分聽眾而言，警告聽起來「持續的」，實際測量卻仍有疑點。一項指令要求團隊「失望」，卻沒有人能解釋為何急迫。見證人稱結果「認真的」，余未把形容詞記在見證人姓名旁。

He copied the names onto invoices that living players could carry outside. A refusal sounded small compared with the danger outside. Even so, it changed what the group understood about its limits. No one should have to give up a private history, a safe route, or an honest answer merely to prove they were cooperative. The manager watched through a mirror that showed no face at all. They found a reference to embassy near the damaged receipt, but its date was unreadable. The emphasis appeared in the older receipt, and Shian asked why the newer version omitted it. Nobody disputed the librarian; what troubled Shian was the silence around its origin. The team considered the margin a separate detail, not proof that the moving gate was safe.

他把姓名抄到活玩家能帶出去的發票上。面對外面的危險，一次拒絕聽來很微小，卻改變了團隊對界線的理解。沒有人必須放棄私人經歷、安全路線或誠實答案，才能證明自己願意合作。店長透過一面映不出臉的鏡子監看。他們在受損收據旁找到提及「大使館」的紀錄，日期卻看不清。舊收據出現了「重點」，時安追問新版為何漏掉。沒人質疑「圖書管理員」，讓時安困擾的是它的來歷沒有人提。團隊把「頁邊」視為獨立細節，不當成移動鐵門安全的證據。

At the checkpoint, the invoices triggered a request for human review. For a moment the whole plan seemed to fail. The expected signal never arrived, and the people waiting outside grew impatient. Then a smaller detail emerged, one that would have been lost if the group had covered the mistake with a confident announcement. A customer could buy a sword, but not the permission to question a death. A witness mentioned murderer during the review, so Yu Wei preserved the exact wording. They found a reference to nonsense near the damaged receipt, but its date was unreadable. The orbit appeared in the older receipt, and Shian asked why the newer version omitted it.

在檢查點，發票觸發了人工覆核要求。有一刻整個計畫像要失敗。預期訊號沒有來，外面等候的人越來越不耐。隨後出現更小的細節；若團隊用自信公告掩蓋錯誤，那個細節就會消失。顧客能買劍，卻買不到質問死亡的許可。見證人在覆核時提到「殺人犯」，余未保留了原話。他們在受損收據旁找到提及「荒謬的事」的紀錄，日期卻看不清。舊收據出現了「運行軌道」，時安追問新版為何漏掉。

The work left marks that a final report would never show: a dented cup, an unfinished meal, a message that had to be sent to somebody waiting at home. These things did not prove a theory. They explained why getting the theory right mattered. Shian glanced toward Yu Wei and remembered the principle they had been arguing about: a human life is not inventory. It sounded simple until it demanded another hour of work. The counter bell rang with a sound that no human hand had made. The warning sounded passive to some listeners, although the actual measurements remained uncertain. The team considered the possession a separate detail, not proof that the moving gate was safe. A witness mentioned researcher during the review, so Yu Wei preserved the exact wording.

工作留下最終報告不會顯示的痕跡：凹掉的杯子、沒吃完的飯、必須傳給在家等候者的訊息。這些不能證明理論，卻說明理論為什麼必須正確。時安望向余未，想起兩人不斷爭論的原則：人的生命不是庫存。話雖簡單，真正實踐又需要多一小時工作。櫃臺鈴發出沒有任何人敲出的聲音。對部分聽眾而言，警告聽起來「被動的」，實際測量卻仍有疑點。團隊把「擁有權」視為獨立細節，不當成移動鐵門安全的證據。見證人在覆核時提到「研究員」，余未保留了原話。

Evening came without resolving the argument completely. The record was clearer, but the consequences still belonged to real people rather than to the neat ending of a tale. The next step would need their consent, their questions and the patience to hear an unwelcome answer. Shian knew the next day would not begin from a clean slate. The receipt would preserve one more question, and Yu Wei would be there to challenge the first easy answer. The inventory bell chimed whenever the game tried to call a person an object. They found a reference to rhyme near the damaged receipt, but its date was unreadable. The translator appeared in the older receipt, and Shian asked why the newer version omitted it. The warning sounded urgent to some listeners, although the actual measurements remained uncertain.

傍晚到來，爭論沒有完全解決。紀錄清楚了，後果卻仍屬於真實的人，不屬於故事漂亮的結尾。下一步需要他們的同意、提問，以及聽進不想聽的答案的耐心。時安知道明天不會從零開始。收據將保存又一個問題，余未也會在場質疑第一個容易的答案。遊戲每次想把人稱作物品，庫存鈴便響起。他們在受損收據旁找到提及「童謠」的紀錄，日期卻看不清。舊收據出現了「翻譯員」，時安追問新版為何漏掉。對部分聽眾而言，警告聽起來「緊急的」，實際測量卻仍有疑點。
