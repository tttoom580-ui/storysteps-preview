櫃臺外的聲音

A Voice Beyond the Counter

玩家死後會變成副本NPC · 第 3 / 25 章 · 3000–4000 · 英中對照 · 校訂中

Yu Wei returned after her team lost a scout in the mirror hall. The plan looked respectable at first glance. It had dates, names, and a signature in the proper corner. But paper can be complete without being honest, and a polished surface does not remove the cost carried by those who have no place to object. A blank receipt lay on the counter like a question nobody had dared to ask. The report labeled the risk accidental, a judgment that needed checking before anyone acted. The barrier appeared in the older receipt, and Shian asked why the newer version omitted it. Before they could blink at the sudden light, the group needed a witness who was free to object.

余未在隊伍於鏡廳失去斥候後再次回來。計畫乍看很正式，有日期、姓名，簽名也在正確角落。然而文件可以完整卻不誠實；漂亮的外觀不能消除無處提出異議的人所承擔的代價。空白收據躺在櫃臺上，像沒人敢問的問題。報告把風險標為「意外的」，這項判斷須經核查才能行動。舊收據出現了「柵欄」，時安追問新版為何漏掉。在能夠「眨眼」之前，團隊需要一位能自由提出異議的見證人。

Shian discovered that his NPC speech vanished whenever he mentioned a room's real casualty count. They compared the new account with what could be measured independently. It was an unglamorous exercise, made of repeated readings and questions that did not impress an audience. The result mattered precisely because it might prove the people asking those questions were wrong. The manager watched through a mirror that showed no face at all. Shian disliked the word brutal in the summary because it hid a decision someone had made. Yu Wei proposed they choke on the thick dust; Shian wanted the risk written down first. They found a reference to composition near the damaged receipt, but its date was unreadable. The plan called for them to evaluate the new measurements, and Shian asked who would check the result.

時安發現只要提及房間真正的死亡人數，NPC語音就會被消除。他們把新說法與能獨立測量的事情比較。這不光鮮，只是重複讀值與不會取悅觀眾的提問。結果重要，正因為它也可能證明提問者自己錯了。店長透過一面映不出臉的鏡子監看。時安不喜歡摘要中的「粗獷而堅固的」，因為它掩藏了某個人的決定。余未提議「阻斷」，時安希望先把風險寫清楚。他們在受損收據旁找到提及「結構；組成」的紀錄，日期卻看不清。計畫要求他們「評估」，時安追問由誰查核結果。

They agreed on a code: three taps meant stop, and a blank price meant the object was unsafe. They argued quietly because shouting would only give the nearby cameras and strangers another story to tell. Beneath the disagreement lay a practical question: who would bear the consequences if the confident person turned out to be mistaken? No one volunteered to answer for somebody else. A customer could buy a sword, but not the permission to question a death. Nobody disputed the gratitude; what troubled Shian was the silence around its origin. The team considered the grave a separate detail, not proof that the moving gate was safe. A witness mentioned intelligence during the review, so Yu Wei preserved the exact wording. The report labeled the risk mild, a judgment that needed checking before anyone acted.

兩人約定暗號：敲三下代表停止，空白標價代表物品不安全。他們壓低聲音爭辯，因為大喊只會讓旁邊的鏡頭與陌生人又多一個故事。爭論底下是實際問題：自信的人若弄錯，誰承擔後果？沒有人願意代替別人回答。顧客能買劍，卻買不到質問死亡的許可。沒人質疑「感激」，讓時安困擾的是它的來歷沒有人提。團隊把「嚴重的」視為獨立細節，不當成移動鐵門安全的證據。見證人在覆核時提到「情報」，余未保留了原話。報告把風險標為「溫和的」，這項判斷須經核查才能行動。

The scout survived behind a false wall, but the game marked the shop as suspicious. The room did not erupt in celebration. A few people checked the figures again, others examined the door, and somebody quietly asked who would pay for the repairs. That question belonged with the evidence. A clever answer was of little use if nobody could maintain it. The counter bell rang with a sound that no human hand had made. The public account described the situation as mountainous, but Shian asked for the observation behind that claim. Nobody disputed the neglect; what troubled Shian was the silence around its origin. One instruction told the team to offend the people who trusted them; nobody could explain why it was urgent.

斥候在假牆後活了下來，遊戲卻把商店標為可疑。房間沒有爆發歡呼。幾人重新核對數字，其他人檢查門，還有人低聲問誰出維修費。那問題和證據同樣重要。若無人能維持，再聰明的答案也沒有用。櫃臺鈴發出沒有任何人敲出的聲音。公開說法把情況形容為「山區的」，時安要求查看支撐判斷的觀察。沒人質疑「疏忽」，讓時安困擾的是它的來歷沒有人提。一項指令要求團隊「冒犯」，卻沒有人能解釋為何急迫。

The work left marks that a final report would never show: a dented cup, an unfinished meal, a message that had to be sent to somebody waiting at home. These things did not prove a theory. They explained why getting the theory right mattered. Shian glanced toward Yu Wei and remembered the principle they had been arguing about: a human life is not inventory. It sounded simple until it demanded another hour of work. The inventory bell chimed whenever the game tried to call a person an object. A witness mentioned philosopher during the review, so Yu Wei preserved the exact wording. They found a reference to reform near the damaged receipt, but its date was unreadable. The revenge appeared in the older receipt, and Shian asked why the newer version omitted it.

工作留下最終報告不會顯示的痕跡：凹掉的杯子、沒吃完的飯、必須傳給在家等候者的訊息。這些不能證明理論，卻說明理論為什麼必須正確。時安望向余未，想起兩人不斷爭論的原則：人的生命不是庫存。話雖簡單，真正實踐又需要多一小時工作。遊戲每次想把人稱作物品，庫存鈴便響起。見證人在覆核時提到「哲學家」，余未保留了原話。他們在受損收據旁找到提及「改革」的紀錄，日期卻看不清。舊收據出現了「報復」，時安追問新版為何漏掉。

Evening came without resolving the argument completely. The record was clearer, but the consequences still belonged to real people rather than to the neat ending of a tale. The next step would need their consent, their questions and the patience to hear an unwelcome answer. Shian knew the next day would not begin from a clean slate. The receipt would preserve one more question, and Yu Wei would be there to challenge the first easy answer. Shian counted the receipts as if each number might become a name again. Nobody disputed the tendency; what troubled Shian was the silence around its origin. The team considered the twig a separate detail, not proof that the moving gate was safe. A witness mentioned urge during the review, so Yu Wei preserved the exact wording.

傍晚到來，爭論沒有完全解決。紀錄清楚了，後果卻仍屬於真實的人，不屬於故事漂亮的結尾。下一步需要他們的同意、提問，以及聽進不想聽的答案的耐心。時安知道明天不會從零開始。收據將保存又一個問題，余未也會在場質疑第一個容易的答案。時安數著收據，彷彿每個數字都能重新成為名字。沒人質疑「傾向」，讓時安困擾的是它的來歷沒有人提。團隊把「樹枝」視為獨立細節，不當成移動鐵門安全的證據。見證人在覆核時提到「催促」，余未保留了原話。
