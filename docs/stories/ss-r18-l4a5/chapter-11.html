規則書被撕掉的一頁

The Missing Page of the Rulebook

玩家死後會變成副本NPC · 第 11 / 25 章 · 3000–4000 · 英中對照 · 校訂中

用互動版查詞、朗讀與收藏

Shian discovered a rulebook with page seventeen deliberately removed. The plan looked respectable at first glance. It had dates, names, and a signature in the proper corner. But paper can be complete without being honest, and a polished surface does not remove the cost carried by those who have no place to object. A customer could buy a sword, but not the permission to question a death. Yu Wei proposed they accuse a person without proof; Shian wanted the risk written down first. They found a reference to assurance near the damaged receipt, but its date was unreadable. The plan called for them to boast about an untested result, and Shian asked who would check the result.

時安找到一本被刻意撕去第十七頁的規則書。計畫乍看很正式，有日期、姓名，簽名也在正確角落。然而文件可以完整卻不誠實；漂亮的外觀不能消除無處提出異議的人所承擔的代價。顧客能買劍，卻買不到質問死亡的許可。余未提議「指控」，時安希望先把風險寫清楚。他們在受損收據旁找到提及「保證」的紀錄，日期卻看不清。計畫要求他們「吹噓」，時安追問由誰查核結果。

The page number matched the code printed on his NPC uniform. The easiest part of the investigation was drawing a line between two points. The difficult part was deciding what that line meant. It might show a connection, a coincidence, or an error copied from an older file. No one wanted to spend another day checking. The counter bell rang with a sound that no human hand had made. Nobody disputed the commerce; what troubled Shian was the silence around its origin. Shian disliked the word consistent in the summary because it hid a decision someone had made. Yu Wei proposed they contribute to the shared plan; Shian wanted the risk written down first. They found a reference to convention near the damaged receipt, but its date was unreadable.

頁碼和他NPC制服上的編號相同。調查最容易的部分，是在兩個點之間畫線。困難的是決定那條線的意思。它可能代表關聯、巧合，也可能是從舊檔複製的錯誤。沒有人想再花一天查證。櫃臺鈴發出沒有任何人敲出的聲音。沒人質疑「商業」，讓時安困擾的是它的來歷沒有人提。時安不喜歡摘要中的「前後一致的」，因為它掩藏了某個人的決定。余未提議「貢獻」，時安希望先把風險寫清楚。他們在受損收據旁找到提及「大會；集會」的紀錄，日期卻看不清。

He asked the rescued players to compare their tickets before reconstructing the missing text. A refusal sounded small compared with the danger outside. Even so, it changed what the group understood about its limits. No one should have to give up a private history, a safe route, or an honest answer merely to prove they were cooperative. The inventory bell chimed whenever the game tried to call a person an object. The plan called for them to expose a private witness, and Shian asked who would check the result. Nobody disputed the hurricane; what troubled Shian was the silence around its origin. The team considered the hydrogen a separate detail, not proof that the moving gate was safe. A witness mentioned ignorance during the review, so Yu Wei preserved the exact wording.

他請獲救玩家比對票券，再重建缺頁的內容。面對外面的危險，一次拒絕聽來很微小，卻改變了團隊對界線的理解。沒有人必須放棄私人經歷、安全路線或誠實答案，才能證明自己願意合作。遊戲每次想把人稱作物品，庫存鈴便響起。計畫要求他們「揭露」，時安追問由誰查核結果。沒人質疑「颶風」，讓時安困擾的是它的來歷沒有人提。團隊把「氫」視為獨立細節，不當成移動鐵門安全的證據。見證人在覆核時提到「無知」，余未保留了原話。

They learned that NPCs could petition for retirement after proving the shop had stopped causing harm. For a moment the whole plan seemed to fail. The expected signal never arrived, and the people waiting outside grew impatient. Then a smaller detail emerged, one that would have been lost if the group had covered the mistake with a confident announcement. Shian counted the receipts as if each number might become a name again. The report labeled the risk informative, a judgment that needed checking before anyone acted. The public account described the situation as loyal, but Shian asked for the observation behind that claim. Nobody disputed the manufacture; what troubled Shian was the silence around its origin.

他們發現NPC只要證明商店不再造成傷害，就能申請退休。有一刻整個計畫像要失敗。預期訊號沒有來，外面等候的人越來越不耐。隨後出現更小的細節；若團隊用自信公告掩蓋錯誤，那個細節就會消失。時安數著收據，彷彿每個數字都能重新成為名字。報告把風險標為「提供資訊的」，這項判斷須經核查才能行動。公開說法把情況形容為「忠於」，時安要求查看支撐判斷的觀察。沒人質疑「製造」，讓時安困擾的是它的來歷沒有人提。

A small act of care interrupted the discussion. It solved no mystery and changed no official ruling. Yet it reminded them that the people named in the records were not pieces on a board; they had bodies, families and the right to decide what came next. Shian glanced toward Yu Wei and remembered the principle they had been arguing about: a human life is not inventory. It sounded simple until it demanded another hour of work. A price tag curled in the artificial breeze, waiting for its next owner. The team considered the misfortune a separate detail, not proof that the moving gate was safe. A witness mentioned recovery during the review, so Yu Wei preserved the exact wording. They found a reference to relaxation near the damaged receipt, but its date was unreadable.

一個小小的照顧動作打斷討論。它沒有解開謎，也沒改變裁決，卻提醒大家紀錄裡的人不是棋子；他們有身體、家庭，也有決定下一步的權利。時安望向余未，想起兩人不斷爭論的原則：人的生命不是庫存。話雖簡單，真正實踐又需要多一小時工作。價籤在人工微風裡捲曲，等著下一個主人。團隊把「災禍；不幸」視為獨立細節，不當成移動鐵門安全的證據。見證人在覆核時提到「復原」，余未保留了原話。他們在受損收據旁找到提及「休息時間」的紀錄，日期卻看不清。

Evening came without resolving the argument completely. The record was clearer, but the consequences still belonged to real people rather than to the neat ending of a tale. The next step would need their consent, their questions and the patience to hear an unwelcome answer. Shian knew the next day would not begin from a clean slate. The receipt would preserve one more question, and Yu Wei would be there to challenge the first easy answer. A lamp flickered over the shelf of items that nobody had claimed. The resistance appeared in the older receipt, and Shian asked why the newer version omitted it. Nobody disputed the skyscraper; what troubled Shian was the silence around its origin. Shian disliked the word troublesome in the summary because it hid a decision someone had made.

傍晚到來，爭論沒有完全解決。紀錄清楚了，後果卻仍屬於真實的人，不屬於故事漂亮的結尾。下一步需要他們的同意、提問，以及聽進不想聽的答案的耐心。時安知道明天不會從零開始。收據將保存又一個問題，余未也會在場質疑第一個容易的答案。燈在無人認領的貨架上閃爍。舊收據出現了「阻力」，時安追問新版為何漏掉。沒人質疑「摩天大樓」，讓時安困擾的是它的來歷沒有人提。時安不喜歡摘要中的「棘手的」，因為它掩藏了某個人的決定。
