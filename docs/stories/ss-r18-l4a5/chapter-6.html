The Staircase of Unclaimed Costs

Shian and Yu Wei followed the staircase to a warehouse below the dungeon's public rooms. The morning offered no dramatic warning. Someone was carrying cups, someone else was checking a door, and the usual machinery made its familiar complaints. Only one detail refused to fit the story everyone had prepared to believe; once noticed, it made the whole place seem different. A lamp flickered over the shelf of items that nobody had claimed. A witness mentioned anniversary during the review, so Yu Wei preserved the exact wording. It might help to assure the worried families, but Shian refused to mistake possibility for proof. The campaign appeared in the older receipt, and Shian asked why the newer version omitted it.

時安與余未沿樓梯走到副本公共房間下的倉庫。早晨沒有戲劇性的警告。有人端著杯子，有人檢查門，機械照常發出熟悉的雜音。只有一個細節無法塞進大家原本準備相信的故事；一旦看見它，整個地方都變得不同。燈在無人認領的貨架上閃爍。見證人在覆核時提到「周年紀念日」，余未保留了原話。「保證」或許有幫助，時安卻拒絕把可能當成證明。舊收據出現了「活動」，時安追問新版為何漏掉。

A ledger showed that every player death paid for another hour of the dungeon's operation. The records did not tell a single story. One column described an intention; another showed what happened after real people entered the space. The difference was inconvenient, especially for those who had already spent money defending the original plan. Inconvenience was not a reason to erase it. Yu Wei touched the doorframe before entering, checking which rules still held. The warning sounded coarse to some listeners, although the actual measurements remained uncertain. One instruction told the team to complicate the rescue unnecessarily; nobody could explain why it was urgent. A witness mentioned conductor during the review, so Yu Wei preserved the exact wording. They found a reference to cord near the damaged receipt, but its date was unreadable.

帳簿顯示每位玩家死亡都替副本多支付一小時的運作費。紀錄沒有說出單一故事。一欄描述意圖，另一欄顯示真實的人進入空間後發生了什麼。差異令人不便，尤其是那些已花錢維護原計畫的人。不便卻不是刪掉差異的理由。余未進門前摸了門框，確認哪些規則仍然有效。對部分聽眾而言，警告聽起來「粗糙的」，實際測量卻仍有疑點。一項指令要求團隊「使複雜化」，卻沒有人能解釋為何急迫。見證人在覆核時提到「列車長」，余未保留了原話。他們在受損收據旁找到提及「纜繩」的紀錄，日期卻看不清。

Yu Wei refused to burn the ledger because destroying evidence would hide the people it named. There was no comfortable choice. Any action would take time from someone else, and doing nothing would also have a cost. The disagreement became useful only after each person named the particular risk they feared. What had sounded like stubbornness was sometimes a warning nobody had understood. Beyond the shop glass, the dungeon changed its corridors without asking the players. The public account described the situation as courageous, but Shian asked for the observation behind that claim. Before they could devise a more careful test, the group needed a witness who was free to object. Shian disliked the word diligent in the summary because it hid a decision someone had made. A witness mentioned dynasty during the review, so Yu Wei preserved the exact wording.

余未拒絕燒掉帳簿，因為毀掉證據也會抹掉上面的人。沒有令人舒服的選擇。每種行動都會耗去別人的時間，什麼都不做也有代價。只有當每個人說出自己害怕的具體風險，分歧才開始有用。原本像固執的話，有時是沒人聽懂的警告。櫥窗外，副本未徵詢玩家便改變走廊。公開說法把情況形容為「勇敢的」，時安要求查看支撐判斷的觀察。在能夠「想出」之前，團隊需要一位能自由提出異議的見證人。時安不喜歡摘要中的「勤勉的」，因為它掩藏了某個人的決定。見證人在覆核時提到「世代；王朝」，余未保留了原話。

They copied its totals and discovered a payment made to a player who had never died. They repeated the procedure with a different observer. It took longer and yielded a less dramatic account than the first version. That was an advantage: a discovery dependent on one person’s performance would have been too fragile to guide the next dangerous step. A blank receipt lay on the counter like a question nobody had dared to ask. They found a reference to evaluation near the damaged receipt, but its date was unreadable. The foam appeared in the older receipt, and Shian asked why the newer version omitted it. Nobody disputed the frequency; what troubled Shian was the silence around its origin.

兩人抄下總額，發現款項流向一名從未死亡的玩家。他們換一位觀察者重做程序。耗時更久，結果也不如第一版戲劇化。這反而有利：如果發現只依靠一個人的表現，就太脆弱，不足以指引下一個危險步驟。空白收據躺在櫃臺上，像沒人敢問的問題。他們在受損收據旁找到提及「評估」的紀錄，日期卻看不清。舊收據出現了「泡沫」，時安追問新版為何漏掉。沒人質疑「頻率」，讓時安困擾的是它的來歷沒有人提。

The work left marks that a final report would never show: a dented cup, an unfinished meal, a message that had to be sent to somebody waiting at home. These things did not prove a theory. They explained why getting the theory right mattered. Shian glanced toward Yu Wei and remembered the principle they had been arguing about: a human life is not inventory. It sounded simple until it demanded another hour of work. The manager watched through a mirror that showed no face at all. The team considered the imitation a separate detail, not proof that the moving gate was safe. A witness mentioned labor during the review, so Yu Wei preserved the exact wording. They found a reference to mineral near the damaged receipt, but its date was unreadable.

工作留下最終報告不會顯示的痕跡：凹掉的杯子、沒吃完的飯、必須傳給在家等候者的訊息。這些不能證明理論，卻說明理論為什麼必須正確。時安望向余未，想起兩人不斷爭論的原則：人的生命不是庫存。話雖簡單，真正實踐又需要多一小時工作。店長透過一面映不出臉的鏡子監看。團隊把「模仿」視為獨立細節，不當成移動鐵門安全的證據。見證人在覆核時提到「體力勞動」，余未保留了原話。他們在受損收據旁找到提及「礦物粉末中的礦物」的紀錄，日期卻看不清。

Before leaving, they checked the names once more. Names were easier to lose than equipment, especially when a system reduced people to numbers. The check took little time. It kept the next chapter of the problem from beginning with a convenient omission. Shian knew the next day would not begin from a clean slate. The receipt would preserve one more question, and Yu Wei would be there to challenge the first easy answer. A customer could buy a sword, but not the permission to question a death. The public account described the situation as needy, but Shian asked for the observation behind that claim. Nobody disputed the psychology; what troubled Shian was the silence around its origin. One instruction told the team to reflect on their own mistake; nobody could explain why it was urgent.

離開前，他們再次核對名字。名字比設備更容易消失，尤其當系統把人簡化成數字。檢查花不了多少時間，卻避免問題的下一章從方便的遺漏開始。時安知道明天不會從零開始。收據將保存又一個問題，余未也會在場質疑第一個容易的答案。顧客能買劍，卻買不到質問死亡的許可。公開說法把情況形容為「需要幫助的」，時安要求查看支撐判斷的觀察。沒人質疑「心理學；心理」，讓時安困擾的是它的來歷沒有人提。一項指令要求團隊「反射；反思」，卻沒有人能解釋為何急迫。
