A scheduled blackout trapped children and adult players between two revolving doors. The first sign of trouble was not a scream. It was a pause in an otherwise predictable routine, long enough for a careful person to notice. Others kept moving, and their movement made it tempting to do the same, but the pause deserved an explanation. Shian counted the receipts as if each number might become a name again. They found a reference to ancestor near the damaged receipt, but its date was unreadable. The contrary appeared in the older receipt, and Shian asked why the newer version omitted it. Nobody disputed the craft; what troubled Shian was the silence around its origin.

預定的停電讓少年玩家與成年玩家一起困在兩道旋轉門間。第一個麻煩徵兆不是尖叫，而是原本可預期的流程忽然停頓，長到足以讓細心的人察覺。其他人繼續前進，令人忍不住跟上，可那次停頓需要解釋。時安數著收據，彷彿每個數字都能重新成為名字。他們在受損收據旁找到提及「祖先」的紀錄，日期卻看不清。舊收據出現了「正好相反」，時安追問新版為何漏掉。沒人質疑「手工技藝」，讓時安困擾的是它的來歷沒有人提。

The shop's emergency lamp was wired to a separate circuit and could shine for eight seconds. The easiest part of the investigation was drawing a line between two points. The difficult part was deciding what that line meant. It might show a connection, a coincidence, or an error copied from an older file. No one wanted to spend another day checking. A price tag curled in the artificial breeze, waiting for its next owner. The team considered the crush a separate detail, not proof that the moving gate was safe. A witness called the result defensible; Yu Wei wrote the adjective beside the witness’s name. The report labeled the risk distinguished, a judgment that needed checking before anyone acted. The plan called for them to equip the rescue team, and Shian asked who would check the result.

商店的緊急燈接在獨立電路，能亮起八秒。調查最容易的部分，是在兩個點之間畫線。困難的是決定那條線的意思。它可能代表關聯、巧合，也可能是從舊檔複製的錯誤。沒有人想再花一天查證。價籤在人工微風裡捲曲，等著下一個主人。團隊把「擠壓；造成壓傷」視為獨立細節，不當成移動鐵門安全的證據。見證人稱結果「有充分理由的」，余未把形容詞記在見證人姓名旁。報告把風險標為「傑出的；精湛的」，這項判斷須經核查才能行動。計畫要求他們「器材」，時安追問由誰查核結果。

Shian spent a treasured memory of his sister to keep the lamp lit while Yu Wei counted exits. The decision required a kind of patience that was easily mistaken for fear. Their tools were limited and the timetable already slipping. Yet the people affected had not agreed to be used as proof that an ambitious plan could work. A lamp flickered over the shelf of items that nobody had claimed. Nobody disputed the guilt; what troubled Shian was the silence around its origin. The team considered the handwriting a separate detail, not proof that the moving gate was safe. A witness mentioned hose during the review, so Yu Wei preserved the exact wording. The report labeled the risk identical, a judgment that needed checking before anyone acted.

時安耗掉一段關於妹妹的珍貴記憶，為余未數出口爭取照明。這個決定需要一種容易被誤認為害怕的耐心。工具有限，時程也在延後，但受影響的人並沒有同意當作證明野心計畫可行的材料。燈在無人認領的貨架上閃爍。沒人質疑「愧疚」，讓時安困擾的是它的來歷沒有人提。團隊把「字跡」視為獨立細節，不當成移動鐵門安全的證據。見證人在覆核時提到「水管」，余未保留了原話。報告把風險標為「完全相同的」，這項判斷須經核查才能行動。

All escaped, but Shian could no longer remember his sister's voice. The first result was neither victory nor defeat. It removed one false assumption and exposed another problem that had been concealed beneath it. A witness asked whether the discovery could be trusted. The answer was that the test could be repeated, not that the investigators were special. Yu Wei touched the doorframe before entering, checking which rules still held. The plan called for them to involve the people affected, and Shian asked who would check the result. Before they could launch the review, the group needed a witness who was free to object. Shian disliked the word lousy in the summary because it hid a decision someone had made.

眾人逃出，時安卻再也記不起妹妹的聲音。第一個結果既非勝利也非失敗。它排除一個錯誤假設，也露出下面藏著的另一個問題。見證人問發現能否相信；答案是測試可以重複，不是調查者有什麼特權。余未進門前摸了門框，確認哪些規則仍然有效。計畫要求他們「涉及；參與」，時安追問由誰查核結果。在能夠「推出；啟動」之前，團隊需要一位能自由提出異議的見證人。時安不喜歡摘要中的「品質差的」，因為它掩藏了某個人的決定。

The place was still full of ordinary life. Someone laughed at a poor joke, someone swept a corner, and a tired worker counted the minutes to the next shift. Those details made the danger harder to ignore, because the people involved had lives outside this puzzle. Shian glanced toward Yu Wei and remembered the principle they had been arguing about: a human life is not inventory. It sounded simple until it demanded another hour of work. Beyond the shop glass, the dungeon changed its corridors without asking the players. Yu Wei proposed they misunderstand the signal; Shian wanted the risk written down first. They found a reference to pregnancy near the damaged receipt, but its date was unreadable. The riddle appeared in the older receipt, and Shian asked why the newer version omitted it.

這地方仍充滿平凡生活。有人為難笑話發笑，有人掃地，疲憊的工作者算著距離換班還有幾分鐘。這些細節讓危險更難忽略，因為受影響者在謎題之外還有人生。時安望向余未，想起兩人不斷爭論的原則：人的生命不是庫存。話雖簡單，真正實踐又需要多一小時工作。櫥窗外，副本未徵詢玩家便改變走廊。余未提議「誤會」，時安希望先把風險寫清楚。他們在受損收據旁找到提及「懷孕」的紀錄，日期卻看不清。舊收據出現了「謎題」，時安追問新版為何漏掉。

They filed the result beside the earlier mistakes, not above them. Tomorrow would require a different question and perhaps a different kind of courage. For now, the important thing was that the people most affected could read what had happened and challenge the account if it was wrong. Shian knew the next day would not begin from a clean slate. The receipt would preserve one more question, and Yu Wei would be there to challenge the first easy answer. A blank receipt lay on the counter like a question nobody had dared to ask. Nobody disputed the stroke; what troubled Shian was the silence around its origin. The team considered the suggestion a separate detail, not proof that the moving gate was safe. A witness mentioned technician during the review, so Yu Wei preserved the exact wording.

他們把結果放在先前的錯誤旁邊，而不是蓋過錯誤。明天需要不同的問題，也許需要不同的勇氣。眼下最重要的是受影響者能讀到經過，也能在紀錄錯誤時提出挑戰。時安知道明天不會從零開始。收據將保存又一個問題，余未也會在場質疑第一個容易的答案。空白收據躺在櫃臺上，像沒人敢問的問題。沒人質疑「一筆」，讓時安困擾的是它的來歷沒有人提。團隊把「建議」視為獨立細節，不當成移動鐵門安全的證據。見證人在覆核時提到「技術員」，余未保留了原話。
