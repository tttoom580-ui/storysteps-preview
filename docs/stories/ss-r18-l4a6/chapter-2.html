未證實傳言的攤亭

The Kiosk of Unverified Rumors

被踢出公會後，我成了最強情報商 · 第 2 / 25 章 · 3000–4000 · 英中對照 · 校訂中

Wen Xu opened his first information counter under a railway bridge. The plan looked respectable at first glance. It had dates, names, and a signature in the proper corner. But paper can be complete without being honest, and a polished surface does not remove the cost carried by those who have no place to object. The independent scouts spoke in short messages because long ones drew attention. Nobody disputed the autograph; what troubled Wen Xu was the silence around its origin. One instruction told the team to carve a careful mark; nobody could explain why it was urgent. A witness mentioned convenience during the review, so Rain preserved the exact wording.

溫序在鐵道橋下設立第一個情報櫃臺。計畫乍看很正式，有日期、姓名，簽名也在正確角落。然而文件可以完整卻不誠實；漂亮的外觀不能消除無處提出異議的人所承擔的代價。獨立斥候用短訊交談，因為長訊會引來注意。沒人質疑「親筆簽名」，讓溫序困擾的是它的來歷沒有人提。一項指令要求團隊「刻出」，卻沒有人能解釋為何急迫。見證人在覆核時提到「便利」，阿雨保留了原話。

Two buyers arrived with conflicting reports about the same dungeon entrance. The records did not tell a single story. One column described an intention; another showed what happened after real people entered the space. The difference was inconvenient, especially for those who had already spent money defending the original plan. Inconvenience was not a reason to erase it. The old guild tower glittered above the roofs, indifferent to the people below. They found a reference to cruelty near the damaged verified report, but its date was unreadable. The plan called for them to disguise the source of the message, and Wen Xu asked who would check the result. The warning sounded elastic to some listeners, although the actual measurements remained uncertain. Wen Xu disliked the word explosive in the summary because it hid a decision someone had made.

兩名買家提供對同一副本入口互相矛盾的報告。紀錄沒有說出單一故事。一欄描述意圖，另一欄顯示真實的人進入空間後發生了什麼。差異令人不便，尤其是那些已花錢維護原計畫的人。不便卻不是刪掉差異的理由。舊公會高塔在屋頂上閃亮，對底下的人毫不在意。他們在受損查證報告旁找到提及「殘忍」的紀錄，日期卻看不清。計畫要求他們「偽裝」，溫序追問由誰查核結果。對部分聽眾而言，警告聽起來「有彈性的」，實際測量卻仍有疑點。溫序不喜歡摘要中的「爆炸性的」，因為它掩藏了某個人的決定。

He labeled both claims unverified and charged only for the confirmed map. A refusal sounded small compared with the danger outside. Even so, it changed what the group understood about its limits. No one should have to give up a private history, a safe route, or an honest answer merely to prove they were cooperative. A courier bicycle passed the kiosk before the first public warning was posted. A witness mentioned feedback during the review, so Rain preserved the exact wording. They found a reference to immigrant near the damaged verified report, but its date was unreadable. The public account described the situation as initial, but Wen Xu asked for the observation behind that claim. Nobody disputed the mischief; what troubled Wen Xu was the silence around its origin.

他把兩份說法都標成未證實，只對驗證過的地圖收費。面對外面的危險，一次拒絕聽來很微小，卻改變了團隊對界線的理解。沒有人必須放棄私人經歷、安全路線或誠實答案，才能證明自己願意合作。第一張公開警報貼出前，一輛信使單車經過攤亭。見證人在覆核時提到「回饋」，阿雨保留了原話。他們在受損查證報告旁找到提及「移民」的紀錄，日期卻看不清。公開說法把情況形容為「最初的」，溫序要求查看支撐判斷的觀察。沒人質疑「惡作劇」，讓溫序困擾的是它的來歷沒有人提。

One customer returned angry, then admitted the cautious map had saved a teammate. The room did not erupt in celebration. A few people checked the figures again, others examined the door, and somebody quietly asked who would pay for the repairs. That question belonged with the evidence. A clever answer was of little use if nobody could maintain it. A torn guild emblem remained pinned behind the public counter. The team considered the paw a separate detail, not proof that an unstable dungeon route was safe. A witness mentioned prevention during the review, so Rain preserved the exact wording. They found a reference to privilege near the damaged verified report, but its date was unreadable.

一名顧客氣沖沖回來，最後承認謹慎的地圖救了隊友。房間沒有爆發歡呼。幾人重新核對數字，其他人檢查門，還有人低聲問誰出維修費。那問題和證據同樣重要。若無人能維持，再聰明的答案也沒有用。一枚撕裂的公會徽章仍別在公共櫃臺後。團隊把「爪子」視為獨立細節，不當成不穩定的副本路線安全的證據。見證人在覆核時提到「預防」，阿雨保留了原話。他們在受損查證報告旁找到提及「特權」的紀錄，日期卻看不清。

The work left marks that a final report would never show: a dented cup, an unfinished meal, a message that had to be sent to somebody waiting at home. These things did not prove a theory. They explained why getting the theory right mattered. Wen Xu glanced toward Rain and remembered the principle they had been arguing about: trust must be checked, not purchased. It sounded simple until it demanded another hour of work. Every new route had a date printed beside its promise. The public account described the situation as prominent, but Wen Xu asked for the observation behind that claim. Before they could prosper without taking unfair advantage, the group needed a witness who was free to object. The team considered the remark a separate detail, not proof that an unstable dungeon route was safe.

工作留下最終報告不會顯示的痕跡：凹掉的杯子、沒吃完的飯、必須傳給在家等候者的訊息。這些不能證明理論，卻說明理論為什麼必須正確。溫序望向阿雨，想起兩人不斷爭論的原則：信任必須查證而不能買斷。話雖簡單，真正實踐又需要多一小時工作。每條新路線的承諾旁都印了日期。公開說法把情況形容為「醒目的」，溫序要求查看支撐判斷的觀察。在能夠「繁榮」之前，團隊需要一位能自由提出異議的見證人。團隊把「評論；言語」視為獨立細節，不當成不穩定的副本路線安全的證據。

The question that followed was smaller and more frightening than the one they had brought to the room. It asked not whether they could win, but whether their answer would remain safe after they were gone. Nobody pretended to know yet, and that honesty held them together. Wen Xu knew the next day would not begin from a clean slate. The verified report would preserve one more question, and Rain would be there to challenge the first easy answer. Wen Xu touched the corner of the map, where an erased route left a pale mark. Rain proposed they ruin the fragile evidence; Wen Xu wanted the risk written down first. They found a reference to shift near the damaged verified report, but its date was unreadable. The timetable appeared in the older verified report, and Wen Xu asked why the newer version omitted it.

後來的問題比進門時更小、也更可怕：不是能否獲勝，而是離開後答案是否仍安全。沒有人假裝已經知道，而這份誠實使大家繼續團結。溫序知道明天不會從零開始。查證報告將保存又一個問題，阿雨也會在場質疑第一個容易的答案。溫序摸了地圖角落，擦掉的路線留下淡痕。阿雨提議「毀壞」，溫序希望先把風險寫清楚。他們在受損查證報告旁找到提及「輪班」的紀錄，日期卻看不清。舊查證報告出現了「時刻表」，溫序追問新版為何漏掉。
