Wen Xu received an expulsion notice during the Iron Crown guild's victory celebration. The morning offered no dramatic warning. Someone was carrying cups, someone else was checking a door, and the usual machinery made its familiar complaints. Only one detail refused to fit the story everyone had prepared to believe; once noticed, it made the whole place seem different. The old guild tower glittered above the roofs, indifferent to the people below. A witness mentioned acid during the review, so Rain preserved the exact wording. They found a reference to assembly near the damaged verified report, but its date was unreadable. The broom appeared in the older verified report, and Wen Xu asked why the newer version omitted it.

溫序在鐵冠公會慶功時收到逐出通知。早晨沒有戲劇性的警告。有人端著杯子，有人檢查門，機械照常發出熟悉的雜音。只有一個細節無法塞進大家原本準備相信的故事；一旦看見它，整個地方都變得不同。舊公會高塔在屋頂上閃亮，對底下的人毫不在意。見證人在覆核時提到「胃酸」，阿雨保留了原話。他們在受損查證報告旁找到提及「集會」的紀錄，日期卻看不清。舊查證報告出現了「掃帚」，溫序追問新版為何漏掉。

The council accused him of leaking a raid plan, but the leaked copy contained an error only the council had inserted. An attractive theory formed almost immediately. It explained the loudest clue and gave everyone someone to blame. Yet one quiet observation remained outside it. Rather than hide that observation, the group put it where every later explanation would have to face it. A courier bicycle passed the kiosk before the first public warning was posted. Before they could construct a safe model, the group needed a witness who was free to object. One instruction told the team to cooperate with independent witnesses; nobody could explain why it was urgent. A witness mentioned copper during the review, so Rain preserved the exact wording. They found a reference to counter near the damaged verified report, but its date was unreadable.

議會指控他洩漏突襲計畫，但外洩版本含有只有議會加入的錯誤。一個吸引人的理論幾乎立刻成形。它解釋了最顯眼的線索，也給大家一個可以責怪的人。但一項安靜的觀察留在理論之外。團隊沒有藏起它，而是把它放在所有後續解釋都必須面對的位置。第一張公開警報貼出前，一輛信使單車經過攤亭。在能夠「建構」之前，團隊需要一位能自由提出異議的見證人。一項指令要求團隊「合作」，卻沒有人能解釋為何急迫。見證人在覆核時提到「銅製的」，阿雨保留了原話。他們在受損查證報告旁找到提及「櫃臺」的紀錄，日期卻看不清。

He left with his notebooks rather than surrendering his sources for public humiliation. The decision required a kind of patience that was easily mistaken for fear. Their tools were limited and the timetable already slipping. Yet the people affected had not agreed to be used as proof that an ambitious plan could work. A torn guild emblem remained pinned behind the public counter. The coward appeared in the older verified report, and Wen Xu asked why the newer version omitted it. Before they could entertain the waiting visitors, the group needed a witness who was free to object. Wen Xu disliked the word facial in the summary because it hid a decision someone had made. Rain proposed they interfere with the signal; Wen Xu wanted the risk written down first.

他帶走筆記，不為了公開羞辱交出消息來源。這個決定需要一種容易被誤認為害怕的耐心。工具有限，時程也在延後，但受影響的人並沒有同意當作證明野心計畫可行的材料。一枚撕裂的公會徽章仍別在公共櫃臺後。舊查證報告出現了「膽小鬼」，溫序追問新版為何漏掉。在能夠「娛樂」之前，團隊需要一位能自由提出異議的見證人。溫序不喜歡摘要中的「臉部的」，因為它掩藏了某個人的決定。阿雨提議「干擾」，溫序希望先把風險寫清楚。

At dawn his old pass failed, and a street courier offered him an abandoned kiosk. The evidence produced a consequence nobody could delegate away. Somebody would have to change a timetable, surrender a privilege, or admit that a previous decision had hurt people. Numbers were useful only when they could still lead to accountable actions. Every new route had a date printed beside its promise. They found a reference to ministry near the damaged verified report, but its date was unreadable. The opera appeared in the older verified report, and Wen Xu asked why the newer version omitted it. The warning sounded persuasive to some listeners, although the actual measurements remained uncertain.

天亮後舊通行證失效，街頭信使帶他去一間廢棄攤亭。證據造成無法推給別人的後果。有人必須改時程、交還特權，或承認先前決定傷害了人。數字只有能引向負責的行動，才真的有用。每條新路線的承諾旁都印了日期。他們在受損查證報告旁找到提及「部會」的紀錄，日期卻看不清。舊查證報告出現了「歌劇院」，溫序追問新版為何漏掉。對部分聽眾而言，警告聽起來「有說服力的」，實際測量卻仍有疑點。

The work left marks that a final report would never show: a dented cup, an unfinished meal, a message that had to be sent to somebody waiting at home. These things did not prove a theory. They explained why getting the theory right mattered. Wen Xu glanced toward Rain and remembered the principle they had been arguing about: trust must be checked, not purchased. It sounded simple until it demanded another hour of work. Wen Xu touched the corner of the map, where an erased route left a pale mark. The team considered the phenomenon a separate detail, not proof that an unstable dungeon route was safe. A witness mentioned route during the review, so Rain preserved the exact wording. The report labeled the risk shady, a judgment that needed checking before anyone acted.

工作留下最終報告不會顯示的痕跡：凹掉的杯子、沒吃完的飯、必須傳給在家等候者的訊息。這些不能證明理論，卻說明理論為什麼必須正確。溫序望向阿雨，想起兩人不斷爭論的原則：信任必須查證而不能買斷。話雖簡單，真正實踐又需要多一小時工作。溫序摸了地圖角落，擦掉的路線留下淡痕。團隊把「現象」視為獨立細節，不當成不穩定的副本路線安全的證據。見證人在覆核時提到「路線」，阿雨保留了原話。報告把風險標為「可疑的」，這項判斷須經核查才能行動。

There would be easier stories to tell about this day. In those stories a brave person would find one clue, name one enemy and fix everything at once. The truth they carried was more difficult: a choice could be necessary and still leave work for the morning. Wen Xu knew the next day would not begin from a clean slate. The verified report would preserve one more question, and Rain would be there to challenge the first easy answer. The notice board was open to anyone who had a reason to doubt it. The slogan appeared in the older verified report, and Wen Xu asked why the newer version omitted it. Nobody disputed the usage; what troubled Wen Xu was the silence around its origin. Wen Xu disliked the word youthful in the summary because it hid a decision someone had made.

關於今天，可以講得更簡單：勇敢的人找到一個線索，指出一個敵人，一切立刻解決。他們帶走的真相更難：一個選擇可以是必要的，也仍會留下明早要處理的工作。溫序知道明天不會從零開始。查證報告將保存又一個問題，阿雨也會在場質疑第一個容易的答案。公告板向所有有理由質疑的人開放。舊查證報告出現了「口號」，溫序追問新版為何漏掉。沒人質疑「使用情況」，讓溫序困擾的是它的來歷沒有人提。溫序不喜歡摘要中的「年輕的」，因為它掩藏了某個人的決定。
