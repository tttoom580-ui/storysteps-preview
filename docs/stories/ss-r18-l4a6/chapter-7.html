A disguised guild officer purchased the same route three times under different names. Nobody in the room knew that this would become a turning point. The ordinary sounds of work continued around them, almost offensively calm, while an uncertain fact asked to be checked. A confident announcement would have been easier than the slower work of asking who might be hurt. Wen Xu touched the corner of the map, where an erased route left a pale mark. Nobody disputed the accuracy; what troubled Wen Xu was the silence around its origin. One instruction told the team to adapt the existing plan; nobody could explain why it was urgent. Rain proposed they analyze the original readings; Wen Xu wanted the risk written down first.

一名偽裝的公會幹部用不同姓名買下同一路線三次。房裡沒有人知道這將是轉折。周圍平凡的工作聲仍持續著，平靜得幾乎令人不安，而一個未經確定的事實正等待查核。高聲宣布結論遠比緩慢詢問誰可能受傷來得容易。溫序摸了地圖角落，擦掉的路線留下淡痕。沒人質疑「準確度」，讓溫序困擾的是它的來歷沒有人提。一項指令要求團隊「調整」，卻沒有人能解釋為何急迫。阿雨提議「分析」，溫序希望先把風險寫清楚。

The officer wanted the identities of everyone who had checked it. An attractive theory formed almost immediately. It explained the loudest clue and gave everyone someone to blame. Yet one quiet observation remained outside it. Rather than hide that observation, the group put it where every later explanation would have to face it. The notice board was open to anyone who had a reason to doubt it. They found a reference to competitor near the damaged verified report, but its date was unreadable. The diploma appeared in the older verified report, and Wen Xu asked why the newer version omitted it. Nobody disputed the frost; what troubled Wen Xu was the silence around its origin. The team considered the helicopter a separate detail, not proof that an unstable dungeon route was safe.

對方真正想買的是所有查核者的身分。一個吸引人的理論幾乎立刻成形。它解釋了最顯眼的線索，也給大家一個可以責怪的人。但一項安靜的觀察留在理論之外。團隊沒有藏起它，而是把它放在所有後續解釋都必須面對的位置。公告板向所有有理由質疑的人開放。他們在受損查證報告旁找到提及「競爭者」的紀錄，日期卻看不清。舊查證報告出現了「文憑」，溫序追問新版為何漏掉。沒人質疑「霜凍」，讓溫序困擾的是它的來歷沒有人提。團隊把「直升機」視為獨立細節，不當成不穩定的副本路線安全的證據。

Wen Xu sold the public route but refused the private source list. The most powerful person present could have ended the argument with an order. That might even have made the next hour more efficient. It would not have made the order wise. Instead, questions were allowed to remain questions long enough for another person to object. Coins changed hands more quietly than the stories told about them. A witness mentioned herd during the review, so Rain preserved the exact wording. It might help to injure a volunteer, but Wen Xu refused to mistake possibility for proof. The public account described the situation as intense, but Wen Xu asked for the observation behind that claim. Nobody disputed the mule; what troubled Wen Xu was the silence around its origin.

溫序出售公開路線，拒絕交出私人來源名單。在場最有權力的人本可一聲令下結束爭論，甚至讓下一小時更有效率，但命令不會因此變明智。他們讓問題繼續存在，給另一個人提出異議的時間。硬幣易手的聲音，比關於它們的傳言還輕。見證人在覆核時提到「羊群」，阿雨保留了原話。「使受傷」或許有幫助，溫序卻拒絕把可能當成證明。公開說法把情況形容為「激烈的」，溫序要求查看支撐判斷的觀察。沒人質疑「騾子」，讓溫序困擾的是它的來歷沒有人提。

The officer left empty-handed, and Wen Xu learned the guild was planning a dangerous raid. The room did not erupt in celebration. A few people checked the figures again, others examined the door, and somebody quietly asked who would pay for the repairs. That question belonged with the evidence. A clever answer was of little use if nobody could maintain it. Rain knew the price of a message delayed by even one checkpoint. Wen Xu disliked the word mutual in the summary because it hid a decision someone had made. Rain proposed they oppose the unfair order; Wen Xu wanted the risk written down first. They found a reference to percent near the damaged verified report, but its date was unreadable.

幹部空手離開，溫序也得知公會正在規劃危險突襲。房間沒有爆發歡呼。幾人重新核對數字，其他人檢查門，還有人低聲問誰出維修費。那問題和證據同樣重要。若無人能維持，再聰明的答案也沒有用。阿雨知道訊息只要在一個關卡延誤，代價有多大。溫序不喜歡摘要中的「共同的」，因為它掩藏了某個人的決定。阿雨提議「反對」，溫序希望先把風險寫清楚。他們在受損查證報告旁找到提及「百分比」的紀錄，日期卻看不清。

The work left marks that a final report would never show: a dented cup, an unfinished meal, a message that had to be sent to somebody waiting at home. These things did not prove a theory. They explained why getting the theory right mattered. Wen Xu glanced toward Rain and remembered the principle they had been arguing about: trust must be checked, not purchased. It sounded simple until it demanded another hour of work. Rain carried two copies of the map, in case a storm ruined the first. The plumber appeared in the older verified report, and Wen Xu asked why the newer version omitted it. Nobody disputed the reduction; what troubled Wen Xu was the silence around its origin. Wen Xu disliked the word remarkable in the summary because it hid a decision someone had made.

工作留下最終報告不會顯示的痕跡：凹掉的杯子、沒吃完的飯、必須傳給在家等候者的訊息。這些不能證明理論，卻說明理論為什麼必須正確。溫序望向阿雨，想起兩人不斷爭論的原則：信任必須查證而不能買斷。話雖簡單，真正實踐又需要多一小時工作。阿雨帶兩份地圖，以防第一份被暴風雨毀掉。舊查證報告出現了「水管工」，溫序追問新版為何漏掉。沒人質疑「減少」，讓溫序困擾的是它的來歷沒有人提。溫序不喜歡摘要中的「不尋常的；顯著的」，因為它掩藏了某個人的決定。

They filed the result beside the earlier mistakes, not above them. Tomorrow would require a different question and perhaps a different kind of courage. For now, the important thing was that the people most affected could read what had happened and challenge the account if it was wrong. Wen Xu knew the next day would not begin from a clean slate. The verified report would preserve one more question, and Rain would be there to challenge the first easy answer. The independent scouts spoke in short messages because long ones drew attention. A witness called the result slight; Rain wrote the adjective beside the witness’s name. They found a reference to surroundings near the damaged verified report, but its date was unreadable. The welfare appeared in the older verified report, and Wen Xu asked why the newer version omitted it.

他們把結果放在先前的錯誤旁邊，而不是蓋過錯誤。明天需要不同的問題，也許需要不同的勇氣。眼下最重要的是受影響者能讀到經過，也能在紀錄錯誤時提出挑戰。溫序知道明天不會從零開始。查證報告將保存又一個問題，阿雨也會在場質疑第一個容易的答案。獨立斥候用短訊交談，因為長訊會引來注意。見證人稱結果「微小的」，阿雨把形容詞記在見證人姓名旁。他們在受損查證報告旁找到提及「周遭環境」的紀錄，日期卻看不清。舊查證報告出現了「福利；福祉」，溫序追問新版為何漏掉。
