Someone was carrying cups, someone else was checking a door, and the usual machinery made its familiar complaints. Only one detail refused to fit the story everyone had prepared to believe; once noticed, it made the whole place seem different. Rain knew the price of a message delayed by even one checkpoint. One instruction told the team to accomplish the limited repair; nobody could explain why it was urgent. Rain proposed they amuse the tired children; Wen Xu wanted the risk written down first. They found a reference to approval near the damaged verified report, but its date was unreadable.

富有顧客出黃金想把危橋警告買斷。早晨沒有戲劇性的警告。有人端著杯子，有人檢查門，機械照常發出熟悉的雜音。只有一個細節無法塞進大家原本準備相信的故事；一旦看見它，整個地方都變得不同。阿雨知道訊息只要在一個關卡延誤，代價有多大。一項指令要求團隊「成就；完成」，卻沒有人能解釋為何急迫。阿雨提議「逗樂；令人發笑」，溫序希望先把風險寫清楚。他們在受損查證報告旁找到提及「核准」的紀錄，日期卻看不清。

Publishing the exact bridge controls would also reveal a vulnerable scout's hiding place. The easiest part of the investigation was drawing a line between two points. The difficult part was deciding what that line meant. It might show a connection, a coincidence, or an error copied from an older file. No one wanted to spend another day checking. Rain carried two copies of the map, in case a storm ruined the first. The plan called for them to bounce off the sealed door, and Wen Xu asked who would check the result. Wen Xu released a public hazard alert while withholding the scout's identifying details. The decision required a kind of patience that was easily mistaken for fear. Their tools were limited and the timetable already slipping. Yet the people affected had not agreed to be used as proof that an ambitious plan could work. The team considered the margin a separate detail, not proof that an unstable dungeon route was safe.

溫序發出公開危險警報，卻隱去斥候個人資料。這個決定需要一種容易被誤認為害怕的耐心。工具有限，時程也在延後，但受影響的人並沒有同意當作證明野心計畫可行的材料。獨立斥候用短訊交談，因為長訊會引來注意。他們在受損查證報告旁找到提及「大使館」的紀錄，日期卻看不清。舊查證報告出現了「重點」，溫序追問新版為何漏掉。沒人質疑「圖書管理員」，讓溫序困擾的是它的來歷沒有人提。團隊把「頁邊」視為獨立細節，不當成不穩定的副本路線安全的證據。

The bridge was closed without exposing its witness. For a moment the whole plan seemed to fail. The expected signal never arrived, and the people waiting outside grew impatient. 橋梁被關閉，見證人也沒有曝光。有一刻整個計畫像要失敗。預期訊號沒有來，外面等候的人越來越不耐。隨後出現更小的細節；若團隊用自信公告掩蓋錯誤，那個細節就會消失。舊公會高塔在屋頂上閃亮，對底下的人毫不在意。見證人在覆核時提到「殺人犯」，阿雨保留了原話。他們在受損查證報告旁找到提及「荒謬的事」的紀錄，日期卻看不清。舊查證報告出現了「運行軌道」，溫序追問新版為何漏掉。

It would have been tempting to call the day a success and leave. But one person could not yet go home, and another still carried a cost nobody had agreed to repay. The group sat down again, this time without pretending that good intentions guaranteed a good outcome. Wen Xu glanced toward Rain and remembered the principle they had been arguing about: trust must be checked, not purchased. It sounded simple until it demanded another hour of work. The last light in the room was not a sign of triumph. It was a reminder that somebody would return to check the work, and that a new person might notice something the experts had missed. They left the account open to correction rather than locking it inside a victory speech. Wen Xu knew the next day would not begin from a clean slate. 房裡最後的燈不是勝利標誌，而是提醒有人會回來檢查，也可能有新人看見專家漏掉的事。他們讓紀錄保持可更正，而不是鎖進勝利演說裡。溫序知道明天不會從零開始。查證報告將保存又一個問題，阿雨也會在場質疑第一個容易的答案。一枚撕裂的公會徽章仍別在公共櫃臺後。他們在受損查證報告旁找到提及「童謠」的紀錄，日期卻看不清。舊查證報告出現了「翻譯員」，溫序追問新版為何漏掉。對部分聽眾而言，警告聽起來「緊急的」，實際測量卻仍有疑點。
