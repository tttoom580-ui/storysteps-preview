The Raid That Should Not Happen

Iron Crown announced a raid against a sealed fortress without checking the lower tunnels. The plan looked respectable at first glance. It had dates, names, and a signature in the proper corner. But paper can be complete without being honest, and a polished surface does not remove the cost carried by those who have no place to object. Every new route had a date printed beside its promise. They found a reference to ancestor near the damaged verified report, but its date was unreadable. The contrary appeared in the older verified report, and Wen Xu asked why the newer version omitted it. Nobody disputed the craft; what troubled Wen Xu was the silence around its origin.

鐵冠宣布突襲封閉要塞，卻沒有檢查下層隧道。計畫乍看很正式，有日期、姓名，簽名也在正確角落。然而文件可以完整卻不誠實；漂亮的外觀不能消除無處提出異議的人所承擔的代價。每條新路線的承諾旁都印了日期。他們在受損查證報告旁找到提及「祖先」的紀錄，日期卻看不清。舊查證報告出現了「正好相反」，溫序追問新版為何漏掉。沒人質疑「手工技藝」，讓溫序困擾的是它的來歷沒有人提。

Wen Xu found rising gas readings from two unrelated sources. The easiest part of the investigation was drawing a line between two points. The difficult part was deciding what that line meant. It might show a connection, a coincidence, or an error copied from an older file. No one wanted to spend another day checking. Wen Xu touched the corner of the map, where an erased route left a pale mark. The team considered the crush a separate detail, not proof that an unstable dungeon route was safe. A witness called the result defensible; Rain wrote the adjective beside the witness’s name. The report labeled the risk distinguished, a judgment that needed checking before anyone acted. The plan called for them to equip the rescue team, and Wen Xu asked who would check the result.

溫序從兩個獨立來源看到升高的氣體讀值。調查最容易的部分，是在兩個點之間畫線。困難的是決定那條線的意思。它可能代表關聯、巧合，也可能是從舊檔複製的錯誤。沒有人想再花一天查證。溫序摸了地圖角落，擦掉的路線留下淡痕。團隊把「擠壓；造成壓傷」視為獨立細節，不當成不穩定的副本路線安全的證據。見證人稱結果「有充分理由的」，阿雨把形容詞記在見證人姓名旁。報告把風險標為「傑出的；精湛的」，這項判斷須經核查才能行動。計畫要求他們「器材」，溫序追問由誰查核結果。

He broadcast a warning to every team, including Iron Crown, despite the guild's hostility. The decision required a kind of patience that was easily mistaken for fear. Their tools were limited and the timetable already slipping. Yet the people affected had not agreed to be used as proof that an ambitious plan could work. The notice board was open to anyone who had a reason to doubt it. Nobody disputed the guilt; what troubled Wen Xu was the silence around its origin. The team considered the handwriting a separate detail, not proof that an unstable dungeon route was safe. A witness mentioned hose during the review, so Rain preserved the exact wording. The report labeled the risk identical, a judgment that needed checking before anyone acted.

他向所有隊伍廣播警告，也包括敵視他的鐵冠。這個決定需要一種容易被誤認為害怕的耐心。工具有限，時程也在延後，但受影響的人並沒有同意當作證明野心計畫可行的材料。公告板向所有有理由質疑的人開放。沒人質疑「愧疚」，讓溫序困擾的是它的來歷沒有人提。團隊把「字跡」視為獨立細節，不當成不穩定的副本路線安全的證據。見證人在覆核時提到「水管」，阿雨保留了原話。報告把風險標為「完全相同的」，這項判斷須經核查才能行動。

Several teams withdrew; the guild leader accused Wen Xu of sabotage. They repeated the procedure with a different observer. It took longer and yielded a less dramatic account than the first version. That was an advantage: a discovery dependent on one person’s performance would have been too fragile to guide the next dangerous step. Coins changed hands more quietly than the stories told about them. The plan called for them to involve the people affected, and Wen Xu asked who would check the result. Before they could launch the review, the group needed a witness who was free to object. Wen Xu disliked the word lousy in the summary because it hid a decision someone had made.

多支隊伍撤退，公會長卻指控溫序破壞行動。他們換一位觀察者重做程序。耗時更久，結果也不如第一版戲劇化。這反而有利：如果發現只依靠一個人的表現，就太脆弱，不足以指引下一個危險步驟。硬幣易手的聲音，比關於它們的傳言還輕。計畫要求他們「涉及；參與」，溫序追問由誰查核結果。在能夠「推出；啟動」之前，團隊需要一位能自由提出異議的見證人。溫序不喜歡摘要中的「品質差的」，因為它掩藏了某個人的決定。

After the argument, a long silence settled around the group. It was not agreement. People were calculating what the next attempt might take from them, and whether anyone would notice the loss. Their caution was an ordinary human response, not a flaw in their courage. Wen Xu glanced toward Rain and remembered the principle they had been arguing about: trust must be checked, not purchased. It sounded simple until it demanded another hour of work. Rain knew the price of a message delayed by even one checkpoint. Rain proposed they misunderstand the signal; Wen Xu wanted the risk written down first. They found a reference to pregnancy near the damaged verified report, but its date was unreadable. The riddle appeared in the older verified report, and Wen Xu asked why the newer version omitted it.

爭論後，長久沉默籠罩大家。那不是同意。人們正在估算下一次嘗試會奪走什麼、又會不會有人注意。謹慎是平凡的人性反應，不是勇氣的缺點。溫序望向阿雨，想起兩人不斷爭論的原則：信任必須查證而不能買斷。話雖簡單，真正實踐又需要多一小時工作。阿雨知道訊息只要在一個關卡延誤，代價有多大。阿雨提議「誤會」，溫序希望先把風險寫清楚。他們在受損查證報告旁找到提及「懷孕」的紀錄，日期卻看不清。舊查證報告出現了「謎題」，溫序追問新版為何漏掉。

The last light in the room was not a sign of triumph. It was a reminder that somebody would return to check the work, and that a new person might notice something the experts had missed. They left the account open to correction rather than locking it inside a victory speech. Wen Xu knew the next day would not begin from a clean slate. The verified report would preserve one more question, and Rain would be there to challenge the first easy answer. Rain carried two copies of the map, in case a storm ruined the first. Nobody disputed the stroke; what troubled Wen Xu was the silence around its origin. The team considered the suggestion a separate detail, not proof that an unstable dungeon route was safe. A witness mentioned technician during the review, so Rain preserved the exact wording.

房裡最後的燈不是勝利標誌，而是提醒有人會回來檢查，也可能有新人看見專家漏掉的事。他們讓紀錄保持可更正，而不是鎖進勝利演說裡。溫序知道明天不會從零開始。查證報告將保存又一個問題，阿雨也會在場質疑第一個容易的答案。阿雨帶兩份地圖，以防第一份被暴風雨毀掉。沒人質疑「一筆」，讓溫序困擾的是它的來歷沒有人提。團隊把「建議」視為獨立細節，不當成不穩定的副本路線安全的證據。見證人在覆核時提到「技術員」，阿雨保留了原話。
