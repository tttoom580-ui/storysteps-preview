The First False Review

Iron Crown's publicity team posted complaints claiming his maps had killed travelers. Nobody in the room knew that this would become a turning point. The ordinary sounds of work continued around them, almost offensively calm, while an uncertain fact asked to be checked. A confident announcement would have been easier than the slower work of asking who might be hurt. Coins changed hands more quietly than the stories told about them. The plan called for them to adopt a safer rule, and Wen Xu asked who would check the result. Nobody disputed the agency; what troubled Wen Xu was the silence around its origin. The team considered the behavior a separate detail, not proof that an unstable dungeon route was safe.

鐵冠宣傳組發出抱怨，聲稱他的地圖害死旅人。房裡沒有人知道這將是轉折。周圍平凡的工作聲仍持續著，平靜得幾乎令人不安，而一個未經確定的事實正等待查核。高聲宣布結論遠比緩慢詢問誰可能受傷來得容易。硬幣易手的聲音，比關於它們的傳言還輕。計畫要求他們「採用」，溫序追問由誰查核結果。沒人質疑「機構」，讓溫序困擾的是它的來歷沒有人提。團隊把「行為」視為獨立細節，不當成不穩定的副本路線安全的證據。

Each complaint listed an identical arrival time at three places too far apart to visit together. A neat explanation would have spared everyone an argument. Instead, the evidence refused to be neat. It contained a gap large enough to change the meaning of what came before it. Filling the gap with a guess would have been quick, and unkind to anyone trusting the answer. Rain knew the price of a message delayed by even one checkpoint. Rain proposed they cherish the last message; Wen Xu wanted the risk written down first. They found a reference to congress near the damaged verified report, but its date was unreadable. The conscience appeared in the older verified report, and Wen Xu asked why the newer version omitted it. Nobody disputed the decoration; what troubled Wen Xu was the silence around its origin.

每則抱怨都把三個相距太遠的地點寫成同一抵達時間。整齊的解釋本可省去爭吵，但證據偏偏不整齊。裡面有一道足以改變先前意義的缺口。拿猜測補起來雖快，對信任答案的人卻不公平。阿雨知道訊息只要在一個關卡延誤，代價有多大。阿雨提議「珍惜」，溫序希望先把風險寫清楚。他們在受損查證報告旁找到提及「議會」的紀錄，日期卻看不清。舊查證報告出現了「良心」，溫序追問新版為何漏掉。沒人質疑「裝飾」，讓溫序困擾的是它的來歷沒有人提。

Wen Xu published a verification method without releasing the complainants' private accounts. A refusal sounded small compared with the danger outside. Even so, it changed what the group understood about its limits. No one should have to give up a private history, a safe route, or an honest answer merely to prove they were cooperative. Rain carried two copies of the map, in case a storm ruined the first. One instruction told the team to discourage a dangerous shortcut; nobody could explain why it was urgent. A witness called the result fundamental; Rain wrote the adjective beside the witness’s name. It might help to horrify the witnesses with careless claims, but Wen Xu refused to mistake possibility for proof. The public account described the situation as influential, but Wen Xu asked for the observation behind that claim.

溫序公布查核方式，不公開投訴者的私人帳號。面對外面的危險，一次拒絕聽來很微小，卻改變了團隊對界線的理解。沒有人必須放棄私人經歷、安全路線或誠實答案，才能證明自己願意合作。阿雨帶兩份地圖，以防第一份被暴風雨毀掉。一項指令要求團隊「使卻步」，卻沒有人能解釋為何急迫。見證人稱結果「根本的；基本的」，阿雨把形容詞記在見證人姓名旁。「使驚恐」或許有幫助，溫序卻拒絕把可能當成證明。公開說法把情況形容為「有影響力的」，溫序要求查看支撐判斷的觀察。

Customers checked the times themselves and the false campaign collapsed. The evidence produced a consequence nobody could delegate away. Somebody would have to change a timetable, surrender a privilege, or admit that a previous decision had hurt people. Numbers were useful only when they could still lead to accountable actions. The independent scouts spoke in short messages because long ones drew attention. Nobody disputed the interaction; what troubled Wen Xu was the silence around its origin. The team considered the outcome a separate detail, not proof that an unstable dungeon route was safe. A witness called the result primitive; Rain wrote the adjective beside the witness’s name.

顧客自行核對時間，造假攻勢瓦解。證據造成無法推給別人的後果。有人必須改時程、交還特權，或承認先前決定傷害了人。數字只有能引向負責的行動，才真的有用。獨立斥候用短訊交談，因為長訊會引來注意。沒人質疑「相互作用」，讓溫序困擾的是它的來歷沒有人提。團隊把「結果」視為獨立細節，不當成不穩定的副本路線安全的證據。見證人稱結果「古早的」，阿雨把形容詞記在見證人姓名旁。

The place was still full of ordinary life. Someone laughed at a poor joke, someone swept a corner, and a tired worker counted the minutes to the next shift. Those details made the danger harder to ignore, because the people involved had lives outside this puzzle. Wen Xu glanced toward Rain and remembered the principle they had been arguing about: trust must be checked, not purchased. It sounded simple until it demanded another hour of work. The old guild tower glittered above the roofs, indifferent to the people below. They found a reference to reception near the damaged verified report, but its date was unreadable. The robber appeared in the older verified report, and Wen Xu asked why the newer version omitted it. Nobody disputed the scenery; what troubled Wen Xu was the silence around its origin.

這地方仍充滿平凡生活。有人為難笑話發笑，有人掃地，疲憊的工作者算著距離換班還有幾分鐘。這些細節讓危險更難忽略，因為受影響者在謎題之外還有人生。溫序望向阿雨，想起兩人不斷爭論的原則：信任必須查證而不能買斷。話雖簡單，真正實踐又需要多一小時工作。舊公會高塔在屋頂上閃亮，對底下的人毫不在意。他們在受損查證報告旁找到提及「接待會」的紀錄，日期卻看不清。舊查證報告出現了「強盜」，溫序追問新版為何漏掉。沒人質疑「舞臺布景」，讓溫序困擾的是它的來歷沒有人提。

The last light in the room was not a sign of triumph. It was a reminder that somebody would return to check the work, and that a new person might notice something the experts had missed. They left the account open to correction rather than locking it inside a victory speech. Wen Xu knew the next day would not begin from a clean slate. The verified report would preserve one more question, and Rain would be there to challenge the first easy answer. A courier bicycle passed the kiosk before the first public warning was posted. Wen Xu disliked the word shameful in the summary because it hid a decision someone had made. A witness mentioned terror during the review, so Rain preserved the exact wording. They found a reference to witch near the damaged verified report, but its date was unreadable.

房裡最後的燈不是勝利標誌，而是提醒有人會回來檢查，也可能有新人看見專家漏掉的事。他們讓紀錄保持可更正，而不是鎖進勝利演說裡。溫序知道明天不會從零開始。查證報告將保存又一個問題，阿雨也會在場質疑第一個容易的答案。第一張公開警報貼出前，一輛信使單車經過攤亭。溫序不喜歡摘要中的「可恥的」，因為它掩藏了某個人的決定。見證人在覆核時提到「恐懼」，阿雨保留了原話。他們在受損查證報告旁找到提及「巫師」的紀錄，日期卻看不清。
