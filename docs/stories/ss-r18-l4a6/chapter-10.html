The rescued guild sent a silver invitation asking Wen Xu to return as a subordinate. The morning offered no dramatic warning. Someone was carrying cups, someone else was checking a door, and the usual machinery made its familiar complaints. Only one detail refused to fit the story everyone had prepared to believe; once noticed, it made the whole place seem different. A courier bicycle passed the kiosk before the first public warning was posted. The acceptance appeared in the older verified report, and Wen Xu asked why the newer version omitted it. The warning sounded ambitious to some listeners, although the actual measurements remained uncertain. The team considered the atmosphere a separate detail, not proof that an unstable dungeon route was safe.

獲救的公會送來銀色邀請，請溫序以部屬身分回歸。早晨沒有戲劇性的警告。有人端著杯子，有人檢查門，機械照常發出熟悉的雜音。只有一個細節無法塞進大家原本準備相信的故事；一旦看見它，整個地方都變得不同。第一張公開警報貼出前，一輛信使單車經過攤亭。舊查證報告出現了「接受；同意」，溫序追問新版為何漏掉。對部分聽眾而言，警告聽起來「有企圖心的」，實際測量卻仍有疑點。團隊把「氣氛」視為獨立細節，不當成不穩定的副本路線安全的證據。

The offer omitted every source payment and accused him of damaging the guild's pride. A neat explanation would have spared everyone an argument. Instead, the evidence refused to be neat. It contained a gap large enough to change the meaning of what came before it. Filling the gap with a guess would have been quick, and unkind to anyone trusting the answer. A torn guild emblem remained pinned behind the public counter. A witness called the result authentic; Rain wrote the adjective beside the witness’s name. It might help to classify the report, but Wen Xu refused to mistake possibility for proof. The plan called for them to differ from the first account, and Wen Xu asked who would check the result. Nobody disputed the dignity; what troubled Wen Xu was the silence around its origin.

提議不提來源報酬，反而指控他傷害公會尊嚴。整齊的解釋本可省去爭吵，但證據偏偏不整齊。裡面有一道足以改變先前意義的缺口。拿猜測補起來雖快，對信任答案的人卻不公平。一枚撕裂的公會徽章仍別在公共櫃臺後。見證人稱結果「真實可靠的」，阿雨把形容詞記在見證人姓名旁。「分類」或許有幫助，溫序卻拒絕把可能當成證明。計畫要求他們「有所不同」，溫序追問由誰查核結果。沒人質疑「尊嚴」，讓溫序困擾的是它的來歷沒有人提。

Wen Xu returned the invitation with an itemized bill for the rescue. The most powerful person present could have ended the argument with an order. That might even have made the next hour more efficient. It would not have made the order wise. Instead, questions were allowed to remain questions long enough for another person to object. Every new route had a date printed beside its promise. One instruction told the team to embarrass the staff publicly; nobody could explain why it was urgent. A witness mentioned facility during the review, so Rain preserved the exact wording. The report labeled the risk genuine, a judgment that needed checking before anyone acted. The public account described the situation as offensive, but Wen Xu asked for the observation behind that claim.

溫序退回邀請，附上逐項列明的救援費用。在場最有權力的人本可一聲令下結束爭論，甚至讓下一小時更有效率，但命令不會因此變明智。他們讓問題繼續存在，給另一個人提出異議的時間。每條新路線的承諾旁都印了日期。一項指令要求團隊「使人難堪」，卻沒有人能解釋為何急迫。見證人在覆核時提到「設施」，阿雨保留了原話。報告把風險標為「真實的」，這項判斷須經核查才能行動。公開說法把情況形容為「惡意的」，溫序要求查看支撐判斷的觀察。

For the first time, Iron Crown had to pay him like any other customer. For a moment the whole plan seemed to fail. The expected signal never arrived, and the people waiting outside grew impatient. Then a smaller detail emerged, one that would have been lost if the group had covered the mistake with a confident announcement. Wen Xu touched the corner of the map, where an erased route left a pale mark. Nobody disputed the option; what troubled Wen Xu was the silence around its origin. The team considered the perfume a separate detail, not proof that an unstable dungeon route was safe. A witness mentioned promotion during the review, so Rain preserved the exact wording.

鐵冠第一次必須像其他客戶一樣付費給他。有一刻整個計畫像要失敗。預期訊號沒有來，外面等候的人越來越不耐。隨後出現更小的細節；若團隊用自信公告掩蓋錯誤，那個細節就會消失。溫序摸了地圖角落，擦掉的路線留下淡痕。沒人質疑「選項」，讓溫序困擾的是它的來歷沒有人提。團隊把「香水」視為獨立細節，不當成不穩定的副本路線安全的證據。見證人在覆核時提到「行銷」，阿雨保留了原話。

A small act of care interrupted the discussion. It solved no mystery and changed no official ruling. Yet it reminded them that the people named in the records were not pieces on a board; they had bodies, families and the right to decide what came next. Wen Xu glanced toward Rain and remembered the principle they had been arguing about: trust must be checked, not purchased. It sounded simple until it demanded another hour of work. The notice board was open to anyone who had a reason to doubt it. They found a reference to publisher near the damaged verified report, but its date was unreadable. The refugee appeared in the older verified report, and Wen Xu asked why the newer version omitted it. Nobody disputed the rescue; what troubled Wen Xu was the silence around its origin.

一個小小的照顧動作打斷討論。它沒有解開謎，也沒改變裁決，卻提醒大家紀錄裡的人不是棋子；他們有身體、家庭，也有決定下一步的權利。溫序望向阿雨，想起兩人不斷爭論的原則：信任必須查證而不能買斷。話雖簡單，真正實踐又需要多一小時工作。公告板向所有有理由質疑的人開放。他們在受損查證報告旁找到提及「出版社」的紀錄，日期卻看不清。舊查證報告出現了「難民」，溫序追問新版為何漏掉。沒人質疑「救援」，讓溫序困擾的是它的來歷沒有人提。

They filed the result beside the earlier mistakes, not above them. Tomorrow would require a different question and perhaps a different kind of courage. For now, the important thing was that the people most affected could read what had happened and challenge the account if it was wrong. Wen Xu knew the next day would not begin from a clean slate. The verified report would preserve one more question, and Rain would be there to challenge the first easy answer. Coins changed hands more quietly than the stories told about them. The team considered the resignation a separate detail, not proof that an unstable dungeon route was safe. A witness mentioned stocking during the review, so Rain preserved the exact wording. It might help to transform the flawed arrangement, but Wen Xu refused to mistake possibility for proof.

他們把結果放在先前的錯誤旁邊，而不是蓋過錯誤。明天需要不同的問題，也許需要不同的勇氣。眼下最重要的是受影響者能讀到經過，也能在紀錄錯誤時提出挑戰。溫序知道明天不會從零開始。查證報告將保存又一個問題，阿雨也會在場質疑第一個容易的答案。硬幣易手的聲音，比關於它們的傳言還輕。團隊把「辭職；辭職信」視為獨立細節，不當成不穩定的副本路線安全的證據。見證人在覆核時提到「絲襪；長襪」，阿雨保留了原話。「轉變」或許有幫助，溫序卻拒絕把可能當成證明。
