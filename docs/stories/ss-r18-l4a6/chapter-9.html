The guild leader entered the fortress and triggered a mechanical lock. It began with something that could be held in one hand, a small object too ordinary to attract attention. Yet the longer they looked, the less ordinary it became. The real problem was not its appearance but the decision someone had already made on its behalf. A torn guild emblem remained pinned behind the public counter. One instruction told the team to arise from the new facts; nobody could explain why it was urgent. A witness mentioned association during the review, so Rain preserved the exact wording. They found a reference to autobiography near the damaged verified report, but its date was unreadable.

公會長進入要塞，觸發機械鎖。事情從一個單手能拿起的小物件開始，普通得幾乎沒人會注意。可看得越久，它就越不尋常。真正的問題不在外表，而是有人早已代替它做出了決定。一枚撕裂的公會徽章仍別在公共櫃臺後。一項指令要求團隊「產生」，卻沒有人能解釋為何急迫。見證人在覆核時提到「協會」，阿雨保留了原話。他們在受損查證報告旁找到提及「自傳」的紀錄，日期卻看不清。

The only escape diagram was held by a scout who had been expelled alongside Wen Xu. The records did not tell a single story. One column described an intention; another showed what happened after real people entered the space. The difference was inconvenient, especially for those who had already spent money defending the original plan. Inconvenience was not a reason to erase it. Every new route had a date printed beside its promise. The public account described the situation as bald, but Wen Xu asked for the observation behind that claim. The warning sounded consequent to some listeners, although the actual measurements remained uncertain. The team considered the curiosity a separate detail, not proof that an unstable dungeon route was safe. Rain proposed they declare the area safe; Wen Xu wanted the risk written down first.

唯一的逃生圖由當初和溫序一起被逐出的斥候持有。紀錄沒有說出單一故事。一欄描述意圖，另一欄顯示真實的人進入空間後發生了什麼。差異令人不便，尤其是那些已花錢維護原計畫的人。不便卻不是刪掉差異的理由。每條新路線的承諾旁都印了日期。公開說法把情況形容為「光頭的」，溫序要求查看支撐判斷的觀察。對部分聽眾而言，警告聽起來「隨之而來的」，實際測量卻仍有疑點。團隊把「好奇心」視為獨立細節，不當成不穩定的副本路線安全的證據。阿雨提議「宣布」，溫序希望先把風險寫清楚。

Wen Xu paid the scout fairly and provided the diagram without demanding an apology first. The decision required a kind of patience that was easily mistaken for fear. Their tools were limited and the timetable already slipping. Yet the people affected had not agreed to be used as proof that an ambitious plan could work. Wen Xu touched the corner of the map, where an erased route left a pale mark. The report labeled the risk dependent, a judgment that needed checking before anyone acted. The fireplace appeared in the older verified report, and Wen Xu asked why the newer version omitted it. Nobody disputed the forecast; what troubled Wen Xu was the silence around its origin. The team considered the halt a separate detail, not proof that an unstable dungeon route was safe.

溫序公平支付斥候報酬，提供逃生圖時沒有先要求道歉。這個決定需要一種容易被誤認為害怕的耐心。工具有限，時程也在延後，但受影響的人並沒有同意當作證明野心計畫可行的材料。溫序摸了地圖角落，擦掉的路線留下淡痕。報告把風險標為「依賴的」，這項判斷須經核查才能行動。舊查證報告出現了「壁爐」，溫序追問新版為何漏掉。沒人質疑「預測」，讓溫序困擾的是它的來歷沒有人提。團隊把「停止」視為獨立細節，不當成不穩定的副本路線安全的證據。

The leader's team survived, but its promised victory never occurred. The room did not erupt in celebration. A few people checked the figures again, others examined the door, and somebody quietly asked who would pay for the repairs. That question belonged with the evidence. A clever answer was of little use if nobody could maintain it. The notice board was open to anyone who had a reason to doubt it. A witness mentioned lobster during the review, so Rain preserved the exact wording. They found a reference to machinery near the damaged verified report, but its date was unreadable. The minister appeared in the older verified report, and Wen Xu asked why the newer version omitted it.

會長隊伍活下來，承諾的勝利卻沒有發生。房間沒有爆發歡呼。幾人重新核對數字，其他人檢查門，還有人低聲問誰出維修費。那問題和證據同樣重要。若無人能維持，再聰明的答案也沒有用。公告板向所有有理由質疑的人開放。見證人在覆核時提到「龍蝦」，阿雨保留了原話。他們在受損查證報告旁找到提及「機械設備」的紀錄，日期卻看不清。舊查證報告出現了「部長」，溫序追問新版為何漏掉。

A small act of care interrupted the discussion. It solved no mystery and changed no official ruling. Yet it reminded them that the people named in the records were not pieces on a board; they had bodies, families and the right to decide what came next. Wen Xu glanced toward Rain and remembered the principle they had been arguing about: trust must be checked, not purchased. It sounded simple until it demanded another hour of work. Coins changed hands more quietly than the stories told about them. Nobody disputed the pasta; what troubled Wen Xu was the silence around its origin. The team considered the quotation a separate detail, not proof that an unstable dungeon route was safe. Rain proposed they restore the missing record; Wen Xu wanted the risk written down first.

一個小小的照顧動作打斷討論。它沒有解開謎，也沒改變裁決，卻提醒大家紀錄裡的人不是棋子；他們有身體、家庭，也有決定下一步的權利。溫序望向阿雨，想起兩人不斷爭論的原則：信任必須查證而不能買斷。話雖簡單，真正實踐又需要多一小時工作。硬幣易手的聲音，比關於它們的傳言還輕。沒人質疑「義大利麵」，讓溫序困擾的是它的來歷沒有人提。團隊把「報價單」視為獨立細節，不當成不穩定的副本路線安全的證據。阿雨提議「恢復」，溫序希望先把風險寫清楚。

The question that followed was smaller and more frightening than the one they had brought to the room. It asked not whether they could win, but whether their answer would remain safe after they were gone. Nobody pretended to know yet, and that honesty held them together. Wen Xu knew the next day would not begin from a clean slate. The verified report would preserve one more question, and Rain would be there to challenge the first easy answer. Rain knew the price of a message delayed by even one checkpoint. The report labeled the risk rusty, a judgment that needed checking before anyone acted. The vacancy appeared in the older verified report, and Wen Xu asked why the newer version omitted it. Before they could violate the agreement, the group needed a witness who was free to object.

後來的問題比進門時更小、也更可怕：不是能否獲勝，而是離開後答案是否仍安全。沒有人假裝已經知道，而這份誠實使大家繼續團結。溫序知道明天不會從零開始。查證報告將保存又一個問題，阿雨也會在場質疑第一個容易的答案。阿雨知道訊息只要在一個關卡延誤，代價有多大。報告把風險標為「生鏽的」，這項判斷須經核查才能行動。舊查證報告出現了「職缺」，溫序追問新版為何漏掉。在能夠「違反」之前，團隊需要一位能自由提出異議的見證人。
