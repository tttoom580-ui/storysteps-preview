失敗的地圖

The Map That Failed

A trusted map sent three teams into a corridor that had collapsed overnight. It began with something that could be held in one hand, a small object too ordinary to attract attention. Yet the longer they looked, the less ordinary it became. The real problem was not its appearance but the decision someone had already made on its behalf. The independent scouts spoke in short messages because long ones drew attention. The warning sounded absolute to some listeners, although the actual measurements remained uncertain. The team considered the discipline a separate detail, not proof that an unstable dungeon route was safe. A witness mentioned distribution during the review, so Rain preserved the exact wording.

一張可信的地圖把三隊人帶進夜裡塌方的走廊。事情從一個單手能拿起的小物件開始，普通得幾乎沒人會注意。可看得越久，它就越不尋常。真正的問題不在外表，而是有人早已代替它做出了決定。獨立斥候用短訊交談，因為長訊會引來注意。對部分聽眾而言，警告聽起來「絕對的」，實際測量卻仍有疑點。團隊把「紀律」視為獨立細節，不當成不穩定的副本路線安全的證據。見證人在覆核時提到「分布」，阿雨保留了原話。

The map was accurate when written, but no update had reached the final kiosk. Rumor traveled faster than any signed report. By the time the second witness arrived, strangers were repeating a conclusion that neither witness had actually made. The group had to slow down, separate observation from interpretation, and check which details could survive an honest question. The old guild tower glittered above the roofs, indifferent to the people below. They found a reference to dread near the damaged verified report, but its date was unreadable. The efficiency appeared in the older verified report, and Wen Xu asked why the newer version omitted it. Before they could fasten the loose cover, the group needed a witness who was free to object. Wen Xu disliked the word gracious in the summary because it hid a decision someone had made.

地圖製作時正確，更新卻沒有送到最後一個攤亭。傳言傳得比任何簽名報告都快。第二名見證人到場時，陌生人已在重複兩名證人都沒說過的結論。團隊必須放慢腳步，區分觀察與解讀，確認哪些細節經得起誠實提問。舊公會高塔在屋頂上閃亮，對底下的人毫不在意。他們在受損查證報告旁找到提及「恐懼」的紀錄，日期卻看不清。舊查證報告出現了「效率」，溫序追問新版為何漏掉。在能夠「固定」之前，團隊需要一位能自由提出異議的見證人。溫序不喜歡摘要中的「親切的」，因為它掩藏了某個人的決定。

Wen Xu apologized publicly, suspended sales and created an expiry rule for dangerous routes. The most powerful person present could have ended the argument with an order. That might even have made the next hour more efficient. It would not have made the order wise. Instead, questions were allowed to remain questions long enough for another person to object. A courier bicycle passed the kiosk before the first public warning was posted. A witness mentioned grief during the review, so Rain preserved the exact wording. They found a reference to hardware near the damaged verified report, but its date was unreadable. The hawk appeared in the older verified report, and Wen Xu asked why the newer version omitted it. Nobody disputed the illustration; what troubled Wen Xu was the silence around its origin.

溫序公開道歉，暫停販售，替危險路線設定期限。在場最有權力的人本可一聲令下結束爭論，甚至讓下一小時更有效率，但命令不會因此變明智。他們讓問題繼續存在，給另一個人提出異議的時間。第一張公開警報貼出前，一輛信使單車經過攤亭。見證人在覆核時提到「悲傷」，阿雨保留了原話。他們在受損查證報告旁找到提及「電子硬體」的紀錄，日期卻看不清。舊查證報告出現了「老鷹」，溫序追問新版為何漏掉。沒人質疑「插圖」，讓溫序困擾的是它的來歷沒有人提。

The injured teams received refunds and a seat in the new safety review. They repeated the procedure with a different observer. It took longer and yielded a less dramatic account than the first version. That was an advantage: a discovery dependent on one person’s performance would have been too fragile to guide the next dangerous step. A torn guild emblem remained pinned behind the public counter. One instruction told the team to insert the missing page; nobody could explain why it was urgent. A witness mentioned miner during the review, so Rain preserved the exact wording. The report labeled the risk oval, a judgment that needed checking before anyone acted.

受傷隊伍獲得退款，也得到安全審查席次。他們換一位觀察者重做程序。耗時更久，結果也不如第一版戲劇化。這反而有利：如果發現只依靠一個人的表現，就太脆弱，不足以指引下一個危險步驟。一枚撕裂的公會徽章仍別在公共櫃臺後。一項指令要求團隊「插入」，卻沒有人能解釋為何急迫。見證人在覆核時提到「礦工」，阿雨保留了原話。報告把風險標為「橢圓形的」，這項判斷須經核查才能行動。

After the argument, a long silence settled around the group. It was not agreement. People were calculating what the next attempt might take from them, and whether anyone would notice the loss. Their caution was an ordinary human response, not a flaw in their courage. Wen Xu glanced toward Rain and remembered the principle they had been arguing about: trust must be checked, not purchased. It sounded simple until it demanded another hour of work. Every new route had a date printed beside its promise. The perfection appeared in the older verified report, and Wen Xu asked why the newer version omitted it. The warning sounded realistic to some listeners, although the actual measurements remained uncertain. The team considered the recognition a separate detail, not proof that an unstable dungeon route was safe.

爭論後，長久沉默籠罩大家。那不是同意。人們正在估算下一次嘗試會奪走什麼、又會不會有人注意。謹慎是平凡的人性反應，不是勇氣的缺點。溫序望向阿雨，想起兩人不斷爭論的原則：信任必須查證而不能買斷。話雖簡單，真正實踐又需要多一小時工作。每條新路線的承諾旁都印了日期。舊查證報告出現了「完美」，溫序追問新版為何漏掉。對部分聽眾而言，警告聽起來「實際可行的」，實際測量卻仍有疑點。團隊把「辨識」視為獨立細節，不當成不穩定的副本路線安全的證據。

The question that followed was smaller and more frightening than the one they had brought to the room. It asked not whether they could win, but whether their answer would remain safe after they were gone. Nobody pretended to know yet, and that honesty held them together. Wen Xu knew the next day would not begin from a clean slate. The verified report would preserve one more question, and Rain would be there to challenge the first easy answer. Wen Xu touched the corner of the map, where an erased route left a pale mark. Rain proposed they renew the inspection; Wen Xu wanted the risk written down first. It might help to resolve the dispute fairly, but Wen Xu refused to mistake possibility for proof. The vessel appeared in the older verified report, and Wen Xu asked why the newer version omitted it.

後來的問題比進門時更小、也更可怕：不是能否獲勝，而是離開後答案是否仍安全。沒有人假裝已經知道，而這份誠實使大家繼續團結。溫序知道明天不會從零開始。查證報告將保存又一個問題，阿雨也會在場質疑第一個容易的答案。溫序摸了地圖角落，擦掉的路線留下淡痕。阿雨提議「更新；恢復」，溫序希望先把風險寫清楚。「決定」或許有幫助，溫序卻拒絕把可能當成證明。舊查證報告出現了「容器」，溫序追問新版為何漏掉。
