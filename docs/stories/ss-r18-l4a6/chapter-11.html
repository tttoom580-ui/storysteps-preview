七扇窗口的網路

The Network of Seven Windows

被踢出公會後，我成了最強情報商 · 第 11 / 25 章 · 3000–4000 · 英中對照 · 校訂中

用互動版查詞、朗讀與收藏

Seven independent kiosks agreed to share verified warnings across the city. Before anyone spoke, the room was divided between those who had been promised safety and those expected to provide it. The distinction was familiar, and that was precisely why it worried the people who had to act. Familiar arrangements could hide old mistakes remarkably well. The old guild tower glittered above the roofs, indifferent to the people below. Rain proposed they accuse a person without proof; Wen Xu wanted the risk written down first. They found a reference to assurance near the damaged verified report, but its date was unreadable. The plan called for them to boast about an untested result, and Wen Xu asked who would check the result.

七間獨立攤亭同意共享經查證的城市警報。尚未有人開口，房間便分成被承諾安全的人，以及被期待提供安全的人。這種分別太熟悉，反而令必須行動的人不安。看似理所當然的安排，很容易藏住 오래以前的錯誤。舊公會高塔在屋頂上閃亮，對底下的人毫不在意。阿雨提議「指控」，溫序希望先把風險寫清楚。他們在受損查證報告旁找到提及「保證」的紀錄，日期卻看不清。計畫要求他們「吹噓」，溫序追問由誰查核結果。

Their leaders disagreed over whether the network should have one owner. The records did not tell a single story. One column described an intention; another showed what happened after real people entered the space. The difference was inconvenient, especially for those who had already spent money defending the original plan. Inconvenience was not a reason to erase it. A courier bicycle passed the kiosk before the first public warning was posted. Nobody disputed the commerce; what troubled Wen Xu was the silence around its origin. Wen Xu disliked the word consistent in the summary because it hid a decision someone had made. Rain proposed they contribute to the shared plan; Wen Xu wanted the risk written down first. They found a reference to convention near the damaged verified report, but its date was unreadable.

各攤亭負責人對網路是否應有單一主人意見分歧。紀錄沒有說出單一故事。一欄描述意圖，另一欄顯示真實的人進入空間後發生了什麼。差異令人不便，尤其是那些已花錢維護原計畫的人。不便卻不是刪掉差異的理由。第一張公開警報貼出前，一輛信使單車經過攤亭。沒人質疑「商業」，讓溫序困擾的是它的來歷沒有人提。溫序不喜歡摘要中的「前後一致的」，因為它掩藏了某個人的決定。阿雨提議「貢獻」，溫序希望先把風險寫清楚。他們在受損查證報告旁找到提及「大會；集會」的紀錄，日期卻看不清。

Wen Xu proposed shared standards and separate ownership, with an independent appeal process. A refusal sounded small compared with the danger outside. Even so, it changed what the group understood about its limits. No one should have to give up a private history, a safe route, or an honest answer merely to prove they were cooperative. A torn guild emblem remained pinned behind the public counter. The plan called for them to expose a private witness, and Wen Xu asked who would check the result. Nobody disputed the hurricane; what troubled Wen Xu was the silence around its origin. The team considered the hydrogen a separate detail, not proof that an unstable dungeon route was safe. A witness mentioned ignorance during the review, so Rain preserved the exact wording.

溫序提出共同標準、各自所有，以及獨立申訴程序。面對外面的危險，一次拒絕聽來很微小，卻改變了團隊對界線的理解。沒有人必須放棄私人經歷、安全路線或誠實答案，才能證明自己願意合作。一枚撕裂的公會徽章仍別在公共櫃臺後。計畫要求他們「揭露」，溫序追問由誰查核結果。沒人質疑「颶風」，讓溫序困擾的是它的來歷沒有人提。團隊把「氫」視為獨立細節，不當成不穩定的副本路線安全的證據。見證人在覆核時提到「無知」，阿雨保留了原話。

The seven signed a charter that could survive one person's departure. A surprising number of people had been waiting for that small result. Some had hoped to hear reassuring words; others needed permission to act. The result offered neither comfort nor permission by itself. It gave them a narrower, more honest problem to solve. Every new route had a date printed beside its promise. The report labeled the risk informative, a judgment that needed checking before anyone acted. The public account described the situation as loyal, but Wen Xu asked for the observation behind that claim. Nobody disputed the manufacture; what troubled Wen Xu was the silence around its origin.

七人簽署章程，不會因任何一人離開而瓦解。有令人意外的多人一直等著這個小結果。有人想聽安慰，有人需要行動許可。但結果本身既不提供安慰也不提供許可，只讓問題縮小、也更誠實。每條新路線的承諾旁都印了日期。報告把風險標為「提供資訊的」，這項判斷須經核查才能行動。公開說法把情況形容為「忠於」，溫序要求查看支撐判斷的觀察。沒人質疑「製造」，讓溫序困擾的是它的來歷沒有人提。

After the argument, a long silence settled around the group. It was not agreement. People were calculating what the next attempt might take from them, and whether anyone would notice the loss. Their caution was an ordinary human response, not a flaw in their courage. Wen Xu glanced toward Rain and remembered the principle they had been arguing about: trust must be checked, not purchased. It sounded simple until it demanded another hour of work. Wen Xu touched the corner of the map, where an erased route left a pale mark. The team considered the misfortune a separate detail, not proof that an unstable dungeon route was safe. A witness mentioned recovery during the review, so Rain preserved the exact wording. They found a reference to relaxation near the damaged verified report, but its date was unreadable.

爭論後，長久沉默籠罩大家。那不是同意。人們正在估算下一次嘗試會奪走什麼、又會不會有人注意。謹慎是平凡的人性反應，不是勇氣的缺點。溫序望向阿雨，想起兩人不斷爭論的原則：信任必須查證而不能買斷。話雖簡單，真正實踐又需要多一小時工作。溫序摸了地圖角落，擦掉的路線留下淡痕。團隊把「災禍；不幸」視為獨立細節，不當成不穩定的副本路線安全的證據。見證人在覆核時提到「復原」，阿雨保留了原話。他們在受損查證報告旁找到提及「休息時間」的紀錄，日期卻看不清。

The question that followed was smaller and more frightening than the one they had brought to the room. It asked not whether they could win, but whether their answer would remain safe after they were gone. Nobody pretended to know yet, and that honesty held them together. Wen Xu knew the next day would not begin from a clean slate. The verified report would preserve one more question, and Rain would be there to challenge the first easy answer. The notice board was open to anyone who had a reason to doubt it. The resistance appeared in the older verified report, and Wen Xu asked why the newer version omitted it. Nobody disputed the skyscraper; what troubled Wen Xu was the silence around its origin. Wen Xu disliked the word troublesome in the summary because it hid a decision someone had made.

後來的問題比進門時更小、也更可怕：不是能否獲勝，而是離開後答案是否仍安全。沒有人假裝已經知道，而這份誠實使大家繼續團結。溫序知道明天不會從零開始。查證報告將保存又一個問題，阿雨也會在場質疑第一個容易的答案。公告板向所有有理由質疑的人開放。舊查證報告出現了「阻力」，溫序追問新版為何漏掉。沒人質疑「摩天大樓」，讓溫序困擾的是它的來歷沒有人提。溫序不喜歡摘要中的「棘手的」，因為它掩藏了某個人的決定。
