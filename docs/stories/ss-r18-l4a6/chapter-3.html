名叫雨的信使

A Courier Named Rain

被踢出公會後，我成了最強情報商 · 第 3 / 25 章 · 3000–4000 · 英中對照 · 校訂中

The courier Rain offered to deliver reports across districts that hated Iron Crown. The first sign of trouble was not a scream. It was a pause in an otherwise predictable routine, long enough for a careful person to notice. Others kept moving, and their movement made it tempting to do the same, but the pause deserved an explanation. Rain carried two copies of the map, in case a storm ruined the first. The report labeled the risk accidental, a judgment that needed checking before anyone acted. The barrier appeared in the older verified report, and Wen Xu asked why the newer version omitted it. Before they could blink at the sudden light, the group needed a witness who was free to object.

信使阿雨願意在敵視鐵冠的街區間傳送情報。第一個麻煩徵兆不是尖叫，而是原本可預期的流程忽然停頓，長到足以讓細心的人察覺。其他人繼續前進，令人忍不住跟上，可那次停頓需要解釋。阿雨帶兩份地圖，以防第一份被暴風雨毀掉。報告把風險標為「意外的」，這項判斷須經核查才能行動。舊查證報告出現了「柵欄」，溫序追問新版為何漏掉。在能夠「眨眼」之前，團隊需要一位能自由提出異議的見證人。

She insisted that sources choose what could be shared, because bad disclosure had ruined her family. A neat explanation would have spared everyone an argument. Instead, the evidence refused to be neat. It contained a gap large enough to change the meaning of what came before it. Filling the gap with a guess would have been quick, and unkind to anyone trusting the answer. The independent scouts spoke in short messages because long ones drew attention. Wen Xu disliked the word brutal in the summary because it hid a decision someone had made. Rain proposed they choke on the thick dust; Wen Xu wanted the risk written down first. They found a reference to composition near the damaged verified report, but its date was unreadable. The plan called for them to evaluate the new measurements, and Wen Xu asked who would check the result.

她堅持讓消息來源決定可分享的內容，因為錯誤揭露曾毀了她的家庭。整齊的解釋本可省去爭吵，但證據偏偏不整齊。裡面有一道足以改變先前意義的缺口。拿猜測補起來雖快，對信任答案的人卻不公平。獨立斥候用短訊交談，因為長訊會引來注意。溫序不喜歡摘要中的「粗獷而堅固的」，因為它掩藏了某個人的決定。阿雨提議「阻斷」，溫序希望先把風險寫清楚。他們在受損查證報告旁找到提及「結構；組成」的紀錄，日期卻看不清。計畫要求他們「評估」，溫序追問由誰查核結果。

Wen Xu wrote an escrow agreement that paid sources even when a report proved false in good faith. Someone proposed an impressive shortcut. It relied on the same promise that had failed earlier, only stated in a brighter voice. The others were tired enough to welcome it, and that made refusal difficult. A shortcut was worth nothing if its safety existed only on a poster. The old guild tower glittered above the roofs, indifferent to the people below. Nobody disputed the gratitude; what troubled Wen Xu was the silence around its origin. The team considered the grave a separate detail, not proof that an unstable dungeon route was safe. A witness mentioned intelligence during the review, so Rain preserved the exact wording. The report labeled the risk mild, a judgment that needed checking before anyone acted.

溫序寫下第三方保管協議，善意提供但後來證實錯誤的情報也能得到酬勞。有人提出引人注目的捷徑，只是把先前失敗的承諾用更明亮的語氣再講一遍。其他人累得幾乎想接受，因此拒絕很困難。若安全只存在海報上，捷徑就沒有價值。舊公會高塔在屋頂上閃亮，對底下的人毫不在意。沒人質疑「感激」，讓溫序困擾的是它的來歷沒有人提。團隊把「嚴重的」視為獨立細節，不當成不穩定的副本路線安全的證據。見證人在覆核時提到「情報」，阿雨保留了原話。報告把風險標為「溫和的」，這項判斷須經核查才能行動。

Independent scouts began to trust the kiosk with observations instead of rumors. They repeated the procedure with a different observer. It took longer and yielded a less dramatic account than the first version. That was an advantage: a discovery dependent on one person’s performance would have been too fragile to guide the next dangerous step. A courier bicycle passed the kiosk before the first public warning was posted. The public account described the situation as mountainous, but Wen Xu asked for the observation behind that claim. Nobody disputed the neglect; what troubled Wen Xu was the silence around its origin. One instruction told the team to offend the people who trusted them; nobody could explain why it was urgent.

獨立斥候開始把觀察資料而非謠言交給攤亭。他們換一位觀察者重做程序。耗時更久，結果也不如第一版戲劇化。這反而有利：如果發現只依靠一個人的表現，就太脆弱，不足以指引下一個危險步驟。第一張公開警報貼出前，一輛信使單車經過攤亭。公開說法把情況形容為「山區的」，溫序要求查看支撐判斷的觀察。沒人質疑「疏忽」，讓溫序困擾的是它的來歷沒有人提。一項指令要求團隊「冒犯」，卻沒有人能解釋為何急迫。

The work left marks that a final report would never show: a dented cup, an unfinished meal, a message that had to be sent to somebody waiting at home. These things did not prove a theory. They explained why getting the theory right mattered. Wen Xu glanced toward Rain and remembered the principle they had been arguing about: trust must be checked, not purchased. It sounded simple until it demanded another hour of work. A torn guild emblem remained pinned behind the public counter. A witness mentioned philosopher during the review, so Rain preserved the exact wording. They found a reference to reform near the damaged verified report, but its date was unreadable. The revenge appeared in the older verified report, and Wen Xu asked why the newer version omitted it.

工作留下最終報告不會顯示的痕跡：凹掉的杯子、沒吃完的飯、必須傳給在家等候者的訊息。這些不能證明理論，卻說明理論為什麼必須正確。溫序望向阿雨，想起兩人不斷爭論的原則：信任必須查證而不能買斷。話雖簡單，真正實踐又需要多一小時工作。一枚撕裂的公會徽章仍別在公共櫃臺後。見證人在覆核時提到「哲學家」，阿雨保留了原話。他們在受損查證報告旁找到提及「改革」的紀錄，日期卻看不清。舊查證報告出現了「報復」，溫序追問新版為何漏掉。

Evening came without resolving the argument completely. The record was clearer, but the consequences still belonged to real people rather than to the neat ending of a tale. The next step would need their consent, their questions and the patience to hear an unwelcome answer. Wen Xu knew the next day would not begin from a clean slate. The verified report would preserve one more question, and Rain would be there to challenge the first easy answer. Every new route had a date printed beside its promise. Nobody disputed the tendency; what troubled Wen Xu was the silence around its origin. The team considered the twig a separate detail, not proof that an unstable dungeon route was safe. A witness mentioned urge during the review, so Rain preserved the exact wording.

傍晚到來，爭論沒有完全解決。紀錄清楚了，後果卻仍屬於真實的人，不屬於故事漂亮的結尾。下一步需要他們的同意、提問，以及聽進不想聽的答案的耐心。溫序知道明天不會從零開始。查證報告將保存又一個問題，阿雨也會在場質疑第一個容易的答案。每條新路線的承諾旁都印了日期。沒人質疑「傾向」，讓溫序困擾的是它的來歷沒有人提。團隊把「樹枝」視為獨立細節，不當成不穩定的副本路線安全的證據。見證人在覆核時提到「催促」，阿雨保留了原話。
