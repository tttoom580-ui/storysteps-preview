永不結霜的屋子

The House That Never Froze

折春城的第四季：與你一起等春來 · 第 11 / 25 章 · 4000–5000 · 英中對照 · 校訂中

The most apt place to look for missing warmth was a casino that stood above the winter market. It advertised heated rooms at prices most residents could not pay. A visiting physician used clinical tests to show that prolonged cold had injured workers far from the greenhouse. Ji refused to characterize all patrons as guilty; some had come to the building because no other shelter was open. Shen listened to the pipes under its polished floor and found that the warm-water circuit sounded exactly like the line beneath the public seed bank. They needed permission to inspect the private connection, so they asked its owner for a meeting rather than breaking the doors.

最適合追查失蹤熱量的地方，是冬季市集上方那座賭場。它以大多數居民負擔不起的價格出租溫暖房間。一名到訪醫師用臨床檢測說明，長期寒冷已傷害遠離溫室的工人。季懷拒絕把所有顧客都說成有罪；有些人來此，只因其他避難處都沒開。沈舟聽著光亮地板下的管線，發現溫水環路的聲音與公共種子庫下方一模一樣。他們需要許可才能檢查私人接管，因此先向業主請求會面，而不是破門而入。

In the waiting room, the owner displayed a book whose copyright he claimed to hold. Its diagrams fascinated Shen until he noticed a drawing copied from the retired keeper's wooden trophy. A clerk gave a gasp when Ji pointed out that the original had been carved decades before the owner's company existed. The owner said his father was the heir of the engineer who built the network. Ji replied that inheritance of a drawing did not prove ownership of the land or permission to use its heat. They asked for the earliest dated contract. The owner, unexpectedly, produced one. It granted temporary access for a winter epidemic, but contained no permanent transfer rights.

等候室裡，業主展示一本他聲稱握有版權的圖冊。那些圖讓沈舟著迷，直到他認出其中一幅是抄自退休守門人的木獎盃。季懷指出，原始刻圖比業主公司早了數十年，書吏倒抽一口氣。業主說父親是網路建造者的繼承人。季懷回答，繼承圖紙不等於擁有土地，也不表示獲准使用土地的熱量。他們要求查看最早的註日期合約。出乎意料地，業主真的拿出一份：它只允許冬季疫病期間暫時使用，沒有永久轉移權。

A wolf's howl echoed through the hills while Ji studied the contract. Its wax seal remained intact, a fact that made the record important even if its interpretation was disputed. Shen recommended taking a certified copy to the growers rather than announcing victory. The casino's warm courtyard had once served as a refuge for travelers stranded in storms, and some residents wanted to preserve that function. Ji agreed. The problem was not shelter but the decision to fund it with unconsented cold elsewhere. If the owner opened his maintenance records, the town could find a safer allocation. He promised to consider the request and asked the men to return in the morning.

季懷研讀合約時，山裡傳來狼嚎。封蠟仍然完整，即使解釋有爭議，這份紀錄也因此重要。沈舟建議先拿經認證的副本給農人，而不是宣布獲勝。賭場溫暖的庭院過去是風雪受困旅客的避難所，有些居民希望保留這個功能。季懷同意。問題不在提供庇護，而是擅自讓其他地方受凍來支付代價。若業主公開維修紀錄，城鎮或許能找到更安全的分配方式。業主答應考慮，請兩人隔天再來。

Outside, a guard held a rifle beside the service entrance. His cheek bore an old scar, and his sheer exhaustion was more visible than his authority. A small crowd of spectators gathered when he asked the investigators to leave. Shen calmly explained that they would return under the owner's invitation, then stepped back. Ji noticed the guard's trembling hand and offered warm tea from his flask. The man accepted without gratitude, but did not point the weapon at anyone. The crowd dispersed. Ji had learned from the old growers that fear became harder to manage once people were made to perform bravery before an audience.

外面，一名警衛持步槍守在服務入口，臉頰有舊疤，疲憊比威勢更加明顯。警衛要求調查者離開時，一小群觀眾聚了過來。沈舟平靜解釋他們會按業主邀請返回，隨即退開。季懷看見警衛的手在抖，便遞上保溫壺裡的熱茶。那人沒說謝謝，卻也沒有把槍對準任何人。人群散去。季懷從老農人口中學到，當人被迫在眾目睽睽下展示勇敢，恐懼反而更難收拾。

The next day's inspection revealed theft of heat but not by the guard or the casino staff. A recent upgrade had connected the building to a hidden main, and the manager could not identify who ordered it. Near the service lift, a wheelchair user showed Shen a workshop where disabled artisans repaired winter heaters. Their group had asked for safe access to the network for years and received no answer. Ji wrote down their proposal: a public warming station could replace part of the casino's private supply. He began to imagine a restoration not measured only in the number of tiles returned, but also in who could reach warmth when winter came again.

隔天的檢查發現熱量被竊，卻不是警衛或賭場員工所為。最近一次升級把建築接上隱藏主管線，經理說不出是誰下令。在服務電梯旁，一名輪椅使用者帶沈舟去看身障工匠修理冬季暖具的工坊。他們多年來請求安全接入網路，始終沒有回應。季懷記下提議：公共取暖站可以取代賭場一部分私人供應。他開始想像一種復原方式，不只計算送回多少塊磚，也關心下次冬天來時，哪些人真的能取得溫暖。

The old diagrams continued to fascinate Shen after the casino inspection. He knew that fascination could be dangerous; a beautiful machine might invite him to overlook the people it injured. Ji asked whether he had ever wanted to build a device like the seasonal network. Shen admitted that he had, before learning how easily a technical promise could become a social burden. Ji told him that losing ambition was not the only way to avoid repeating a mistake. The public nursery needed new tools, ones that could be taken apart and understood by their users. Shen said he would try designing one, provided Ji agreed to be the first person to criticize it.

賭場檢查後，舊圖紙仍讓沈舟著迷。他知道這份著迷也有危險：美麗機器可能誘使他忽略受傷的人。季懷問他是否曾想建造季節網路那樣的裝置。沈舟承認曾經想過，直到學會技術承諾多容易變成社會負擔。季懷說，不必失去抱負才能避免重犯錯誤。公共育苗室需要可由使用者拆開、理解的新工具。沈舟說他願意試著設計一個，條件是季懷得答應成為第一個批評者。
