A Map with Two Rivers

折春城的第四季：與你一起等春來 · 第 3 / 25 章 · 4000–5000 · 英中對照

用互動版查詞、朗讀與收藏

In the inspection room, a stone tablet warned against abuse of the seasonal exchange. Someone had scraped out the next line. Ji requested a consultation with the town's oldest growers, who had maintained the slopes long before the greenhouse arrived. A junior officer treated their knowledge with contempt, but a traveling correspondent recorded their testimony carefully. The growers remembered a narrow creek that once carried snowmelt to the seed terraces. Now its bed lay dry even while the commercial gardens watered flowers after midnight. Ji realized that the missing spring was not one broken machine; it was a rearrangement of a living place.

檢查室裡，一塊石碑警告不可濫用季節交換術，下一行卻被人刮掉。季懷要求與鎮上最年長的農人商議；他們在溫室出現以前就照料山坡。一名年輕官員輕蔑地看待他們的知識，旅行記者卻仔細記下證詞。農人記得一條窄溪，從前把融雪帶往種子梯田。如今溪床乾涸，商業園圃卻仍在午夜澆花。季懷明白，失去的春天不是一臺壞機器那麼簡單，而是整個生活環境遭到重排。

Shen planned a deliberate test of the flow, with two observers and a timed shutoff. The deputy mayor called his plan undiplomatic, saying the city should not embarrass wealthy visitors. Shen answered politely that water did not change its behavior to protect anyone's reputation. Their test revealed a buried pipe beneath the creek, carrying warmth and moisture through a hidden channel. They sealed the inspection chamber without changing the settings, so no one could claim they had caused the damage. Ji watched how Shen made room for another person's fear without surrendering an observable fact. For the first time he wanted the carpenter to remain beside him after the investigation ended.

沈舟設計了一次慎重的水流測試，安排兩名觀察者與定時關閉裝置。副市長說這方案太不懂外交禮節，會讓富有訪客難堪。沈舟禮貌地回答：水不會為了誰的名聲改變自己的流動方式。測試發現溪下埋著一根管子，透過隱藏通道送走熱與水分。他們封好檢查室，不更動任何設定，免得被指控造成損害。季懷看見沈舟如何容納他人的恐懼，卻不放棄可觀察的事實；他第一次希望調查結束後，這名木匠仍留在身旁。

The pipe split the forest ecosystem in two. On the cold side, insects had disappeared and a bird thought extinct in the valley had lost its nesting ground. Ji trusted his gut when he saw fresh feathers near an empty nest, but he also knew that instinct was not evidence. He asked the growers to notify a wildlife healer and document the plants that remained. The healer identified three species whose seeds might survive if the soil warmed gradually. Ji knelt with her to collect samples, careful not to disturb the last roots. He began to see a way to restore the valley without repeating the exchange that had damaged it.

管子把森林生態系分成兩半。寒冷那側的昆蟲消失了，一種在谷地幾乎被認為滅絕的鳥也失去築巢處。季懷看見空巢旁的新羽毛，直覺告訴他鳥還活著，但他知道直覺不是證據。他請農人通知野生動物療癒師，記錄尚存植物。療癒師辨認出三種種子，只要土壤慢慢回暖，便可能存活。季懷和她跪著採樣，小心不驚動最後的根。他開始看見不必重蹈覆轍也能修復山谷的方法。

Their odds of finishing before the equinox looked poor. During the next phase of the search they found an old river pier, now surrounded by dry earth. Shen noticed fresh footprints behind it and resisted the urge to sneak after the owner. A hidden follower could be a frightened witness, not an enemy. Instead he placed a written invitation on the pier, stating that the investigation would hear any account privately. Ji thought the choice slow, but he understood its purpose when a woman approached at dusk. She carried a stamped maintenance token and asked them not to show her face to the city guards.

他們在春分前完成調查的機會看來不高。下一階段的搜尋讓他們找到一座舊河碼頭，如今四周只剩乾土。沈舟注意到碼頭後的新腳印，忍住偷偷尾隨的衝動：躲藏的人可能是害怕的證人，不一定是敵人。他在碼頭留下一封書面邀請，表示能私下聽取任何說法。季懷覺得這做法太慢，直到黃昏一名女子走近，才明白用意。她拿著蓋印的維修牌，請求兩人不要把她的臉展示給城衛。

The woman was the successor to a retired gate keeper. She proposed a sustainable experiment: swap one inactive tile with a working one for an hour, using paired witnesses to watch both ends. Her father had kept a small wooden trophy from the first successful winter rescue, and its underside contained the earliest diagram of the exchange network. He had also written a warning: heat moved in a circle, not a straight line. The young woman refused money for the diagram, asking instead for an honest record of what the test would cost. Ji promised to protect her name; Shen promised to calculate the risk before the tile moved.

女子是退休守門人的接班人。她提出一項可持續的實驗：讓一塊停用磚與一塊運作中的磚交換位置一小時，並安排見證人同時監看兩端。她父親留下當年第一次成功救援冬災的小木獎盃，底部藏有最早的交換網路圖。他也寫下警告：熱量沿環形流動，而非直線。女子不收圖紙的錢，只要求誠實記錄實驗的代價。季懷答應保護她的姓名，沈舟則承諾算清風險才移動磚塊。

The deputy mayor returned with an unexpectedly diplomatic offer: the city would allow a second test if the witnesses agreed to withhold private names. Ji accepted the privacy condition but refused a clause that would let the office rewrite the results before publication. Shen drew a clear line through the clause and asked the official to initial the change. The official hesitated, then signed. No friendship was born in that room, and neither side pretended that trust had been restored. But an agreement could now be checked against the same page by everyone who took part, and the woman from the pier kept an identical copy.

副市長帶著意外圓融的提議返回：只要見證人同意保護私人姓名，城市就允許第二次試驗。季懷接受隱私條件，卻拒絕一項讓官署在發布前改寫結果的條款。沈舟把條款清楚劃掉，請官員在更改處簽名。對方遲疑後仍簽了。這間房裡沒有因此產生友誼，雙方也不假裝信任已恢復。然而，如今每名參與者都能對照同一頁檢查協議，碼頭女子也保有一份相同副本。
