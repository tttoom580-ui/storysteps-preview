The observation team's agenda began with one question: who had written the emergency rules? Shen warned Ji to beware of accepting an old document merely because it looked familiar. Under the city archives, papers seemed to cling to one another in the damp. A federal inspector from the northern union had visited the town three years before and left a technical review. His notes showed that the original tiles were intended for rescue during blizzards, not for permanent luxury. Ji found the report behind a stack of outdated permits. He was relieved to see an outside perspective, then disappointed by the final page, which said the inspector's recommendations had never been implemented.

觀察團隊的議程從一個問題開始：誰寫下那些緊急規則？沈舟提醒季懷，別因舊文件看起來熟悉就毫不懷疑地接受。市立檔案庫下方潮溼，紙張幾乎黏在一起。三年前，北方聯盟的一名聯邦稽查員到訪，留下技術評估。他的筆記顯示，季節磚最初是為暴風雪救援設計，而非永久提供奢華生活。季懷在過時許可堆後找到報告。見到外部觀點令他鬆了一口氣，末頁卻使他失望：稽查員的建議從未落實。

An archive volunteer called the report a work of fantasy, a genre that officials could dismiss more easily than a measurement. Shen compared the accusation to a strange inning in a game that never allowed the visiting team to bat. The metaphor made Ji laugh despite his fatigue. Then a municipal engineer recognized the technical signature on the document. She had maintained an identical valve in the eastern district and could explain the readings line by line. Together they checked the original date and the later handwritten changes. For once, a witness's expertise mattered more than the title printed above a desk.

一名檔案志工把報告說成幻想作品，是官員更容易忽視的一種文類。沈舟把這種指控比作一場怪異球賽：客隊永遠沒有上場打擊的局數。這個比喻讓疲累的季懷笑了出來。接著一名市政工程師認出文件上的技術簽名。她在東區維修過相同的閥門，可以逐行解釋讀值。三人一起核對原始日期與後來的手寫修改。這一次，證人的專業比書桌上的官銜更有分量。

The council had selected a nominee to oversee next month's new greenhouse. His proposal described every living organism as a replaceable unit of production. Ji feared that his language would overtake the debate and provoke a rebellion among the growers. Instead of attacking the man, Ji invited him to inspect the damaged moss. The candidate knelt beside a hollow where a pale fungus had begun to spread. He admitted that he had never visited the lower lane. The admission did not make him trustworthy, but it opened a place for a specific question: would he support a pause while the missing heat was traced?

議會選出一名候選人，預備監督下個月的新溫室。他的計畫把每個活的有機體都描述成可替換的生產單位。季懷擔心這種說法會主導爭論，引發農人的反抗。他沒有攻擊對方，而是邀請候選人查看受損苔蘚。那人跪在一處長出蒼白真菌的洞旁，承認自己從未到過下坡街區。坦白不代表他值得信任，卻讓季懷有了具體問題可問：在追查失蹤熱量之前，他是否支持暫停轉移？

In an old training gallery, Shen examined a brass frame shaped like a human rib. Its joints were rigid, designed to shield the delicate mechanism inside. A miniature skeleton hung beside it so apprentices could learn how pressure traveled through a body. Ji heard someone sob behind a curtain. A young trainee had been ordered to erase two marks from the frame before inspection, but refused to complete the task. She was frightened that her family would lose its housing if she spoke. Shen told her she could choose silence for the moment. Ji offered a protected way to leave a sealed account without naming herself in public.

在舊訓練廊裡，沈舟檢查一架形似人類肋骨的銅框。接點僵硬，原本為了保護裡面精密機件；旁邊掛著一具小型骨架，供學徒學習壓力如何穿過身體。季懷聽見布簾後有人啜泣。一名年輕學徒被命令在檢查前抹掉銅框上兩個記號，卻拒絕執行。她害怕開口會讓家人失去住處。沈舟告訴她，暫時保持沉默也是她的選擇；季懷則提供封存證詞的方法，不必在公眾面前說出姓名。

The chapel library held a volume of practical theology about duty to the land. It warned that a community could become toxic when it treated living neighbors as fuel for its comfort. Beside the book stood jars of vinegar used to clean copper without damaging its stamps. Shen prepared a careful solution and revealed the hidden marks on the training frame. They matched the late-night tile route. He said nothing grand about destiny. He simply passed Ji the rubbing and waited while Ji checked it against the growers' map. Two independent records agreed, and the path to the next missing origin mark became visible.

小禮拜堂圖書室藏著一本談土地責任的實用神學書，警告社群若把活生生的鄰居當作舒適生活的燃料，便會變得有毒。書旁放著幾罐醋，能清潔銅器而不破壞印記。沈舟仔細調好溶液，讓訓練框上的隱藏記號浮現。那些記號與午夜季節磚的路線相符。他沒有大談命運，只把拓印遞給季懷，等季懷拿它和農人的地圖比對。兩份獨立紀錄一致，通往下一個失蹤來源標記的路終於顯現。

The apprentice returned at dawn to ask whether her sealed account had been accepted. Ji showed her the receipt without opening the envelope. He explained that only the independent panel could read it under the conditions she had chosen. Her shoulders relaxed, though she did not smile. Shen offered to teach her how to test the marks she had been ordered to erase. She looked toward the old training gallery and said she wanted to learn the repair properly, not spend her life hiding from it. Ji saw a small but important change: someone who had been treated as a disposable worker was beginning to decide what kind of worker she wished to become.

黎明時，那名學徒回來詢問封存證詞是否已受理。季懷讓她看收據，沒有拆開信封。他解釋只有獨立小組能依她選定的條件閱讀。她的肩膀放鬆了，雖然沒笑。沈舟提出教她測試那些曾被命令抹去的標記。她望向舊訓練廊說，希望正確學會修理，而不是一生都躲著它。季懷看見一個小而重要的改變：原本被當成可拋棄工人的人，開始決定自己想成為怎樣的工作者。
