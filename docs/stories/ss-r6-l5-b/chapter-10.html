沒有藉口的冬季

A Winter Without Excuses

折春城的第四季：與你一起等春來 · 第 10 / 25 章 · 4000–5000 · 英中對照 · 校訂中

Ji developed an allergy to the cheap dust masks used near the old pipes, so Shen carried the bulk of their equipment that morning. They had to cite each measurement accurately before presenting it to the neighborhood council. A collaboration between healers, engineers, and growers was finally beginning to work, though none of them agreed on every solution. Ji disliked needing help, a habit born from years of solitary fieldwork. Shen told him that sharing a load was not a confession of weakness. They set a pace both could manage and reached the survey station without worsening Ji's breathing.

季懷對舊管線附近使用的廉價防塵面罩過敏，因此當天早上大部分裝備由沈舟背負。他們在提交鄰里議會前，必須準確引用每一項測量。療癒師、工程師與農人的合作終於逐漸成形，雖然大家對解決方案並非全都同意。季懷不喜歡需要幫忙，這是長年獨自野外工作的習慣。沈舟說，分擔重量並不等於承認軟弱。兩人調整到彼此能承受的步調，平安抵達測量站，也沒有讓季懷呼吸惡化。

At the town office, a counselor recorded the residents' concerns in descriptive language rather than dismissing them as panic. One mother described how the frozen kitchen had disrupted her father's digestion and sleep. Another worried about a coming drought, since the spring reservoir had not been replenished. A council member began to exclaim that the city's prestige was under attack, but an old cook quietly invited him to visit the communal grill where families heated their meals. The invitation changed the discussion. Instead of debating reputation, officials began listing the supplies needed for a safe week.

鎮公所裡，輔導員用具體描述記錄居民的憂慮，而不是把它們斥為恐慌。一名母親說，結冰的廚房打亂父親的消化與睡眠；另一人擔心水庫未得到春水補充，將迎來乾旱。一位議員激動地喊著城市名聲受到攻擊，一名老廚師卻安靜地邀他去看家庭加熱晚餐的公共烤爐。這個邀請改變了討論方向。官員不再爭辯名譽，而開始列出安全度過一週需要的物資。

The engineering institute sent two observers who had never examined the underground line. The visitors felt overwhelmed by the competing reports, so Shen gave them a preliminary chart containing only confirmed readings. Ji rejected a radical plan to reverse the whole network overnight because the proposal ignored the risk of flooding. A rational approach required smaller tests, clear stop conditions, and the ability to restore the old setting if something failed. The engineers agreed to test one segment at dawn. Ji noticed that Shen had become a patient leader without ever announcing himself as one. He made careful decisions and invited people to challenge them.

工程研究所派來兩名尚未檢查過地下管線的觀察員。報告太多使他們不知所措，沈舟便先給一張只列已確認讀值的初步圖表。季懷反對一項激進計畫：在一夜之間把整個網路反向，因為提案忽略了洪水風險。理性的做法應當是分段測試、設定清楚停止條件，並在失敗時能恢復舊設定。工程師同意黎明測試一段。季懷發現沈舟不曾宣稱自己是領導者，卻漸漸成了有耐心的帶頭人；他慎重決定，也歡迎別人質疑。

The line ended at a disused freight terminal, where an apprentice had been reading a popular thriller to pass the nights. He joked that a mystery always needed one villain, but the measurements showed more than one variable: pressure, cold, distance, and how long each valve stayed open. Outside the terminal lay uninhabited wilderness, its soil still carrying traces of the original forest. Ji collected samples from both sides of the wall, then labeled them in front of the apprentice. The young man asked whether exposing the truth would make his employer close the station. Ji could not promise otherwise, but offered to include that risk in the report.

管線終點是停用的貨運終站，一名學徒靠讀暢銷驚悚小說打發夜晚。他玩笑說懸疑故事總需要一個反派，但測量顯示不只一個變數：壓力、溫度、距離，以及各閥開啟時間。終站外是無人居住的荒野，土壤仍留有原生森林痕跡。季懷從牆的兩側採樣，當著學徒的面貼上標籤。年輕人問，揭露真相會不會讓雇主關閉車站。季懷不能保證不會，卻願意把這個風險寫進報告。

The first controlled return of warmth succeeded for forty minutes. A strip of black earth softened, and the trapped spring water moved without overflowing. The test ended on schedule, even though spectators begged the engineers to keep it running. Shen insisted on stopping because they had promised the downstream families a limited trial. Ji understood the decision and supported it publicly. Their small success mattered not only because a garden could recover, but because the people watching had seen an agreement honored. At dusk, the two men walked home without speaking much. For once, the silence between them felt comfortable rather than empty.

第一次受控回暖成功持續了四十分鐘。一條黑色土帶軟化，被困住的春水開始流動，沒有溢出。即使圍觀者懇求工程師繼續，測試仍按時結束。沈舟堅持停止，因為他們曾答應下游家庭，這只是一項限時試驗。季懷理解，也公開支持他的決定。小小成功的重要，不只在於園圃可以復原，還在於旁觀者看見約定得到遵守。黃昏時兩人一路走回家，話不多；這次他們之間的沉默讓人安心，而不再空洞。

A merchant tried to compel the team to guarantee that no business would lose money during the transition. Ji declined the impossible demand and offered a clearer assurance: every affected group could inspect the results and propose adjustments. Shen showed how the stop mechanism worked with a hand-sized model. The merchant turned it twice, then asked for the instruction sheet to take home. Later Ji said he had expected another argument. Shen answered that people sometimes grew loud because no one had explained what a change would actually do. Information did not dissolve every conflict, but it gave an honest disagreement somewhere useful to begin.

一名商人試圖逼團隊保證，轉型期間沒有任何生意會賠錢。季懷拒絕這個不可能的要求，提出更清楚的保證：每個受影響群體都能檢查結果並提出調整。沈舟用手掌大小的模型示範停止機構。商人轉了兩次，接著索取說明書帶回家。後來季懷說，原以為又會吵一架。沈舟回答，人們有時提高聲量，是因為沒有人說清變更究竟會怎麼做。資訊無法消除每場衝突，卻能為誠實的歧見提供有用起點。
