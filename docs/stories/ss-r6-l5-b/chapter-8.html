遺忘姓名的市集

The Market of Forgotten Names

折春城的第四季：與你一起等春來 · 第 8 / 25 章 · 4000–5000 · 英中對照 · 校訂中

In the southern bazaar, the missing spring felt alien to travelers who had never seen the city warm. A sudden power failure threw the stalls into chaos, and people shouted instructions that few could hear. Ji's comprehension of the problem came from watching the seedlings rather than the crowd: when the light failed, their protective covers froze within minutes. Every licensed trader was supposed to be entitled to emergency shelter for perishable stock. Yet the door remained locked. Shen found the clerk with the key and asked him to explain which rule he was following. The clerk could point to no current order.

南方市集裡，對從未見過城內暖季的旅人而言，失去春天彷彿另一個陌生世界。一次突發停電使攤位陷入混亂，眾人喊著指示，卻少有人聽清。季懷看幼苗而非人群，才理解問題：燈熄後幾分鐘，保護罩就結了冰。照規定，每名有照商販都有資格把易壞貨品暫存緊急避難所，門卻鎖著。沈舟找到持鑰書吏，請他解釋依據哪條規則。書吏找不到一份現行命令。

The street elders wanted flexibility while the power returned. They held a gathering beneath the awnings and agreed to minimize unnecessary movement of heavy boxes. One speaker repeated an old myth that the city could survive without ordinary spring because the mountain spirits loved glass roofs. Ji challenged the tale gently, naming the insects and plants that needed real seasonal change. Shen explained why an exchange tile could borrow heat but could not make it. People were relieved to hear a limitation stated plainly. They had been promised miracles for so long that a practical account of what could not be done almost sounded like a promise of honesty.

街坊長者希望恢復供電時能靈活處理，便在棚下召開集會，同意盡量減少搬動重箱。一名發言者重述古老迷思，說山靈喜歡玻璃屋頂，城市不需要正常的春天也能活。季懷溫和地反駁，指出昆蟲與植物需要真正的季節變化。沈舟說明交換磚只能借熱，不能創造熱。人們聽見清楚的限制，反而鬆了口氣。長久以來他們聽過太多奇蹟保證，如今有人務實地說明做不到的事，竟近乎一種誠實的承諾。

A ragged woman led the men to the shuttered hair salon beside a communal kitchen. There, families had stored food through the winter, but the shortage of clean fuel made the shelter unsafe. A spectrum of colored stones on the sill recorded the warmth that once passed through the building. Shen held each stone to his ear, identifying one low note that did not belong. Ji found the same note marked in the seed keeper's oldest chart. Their evidence suggested that a maintenance line had been redirected below the market. The woman asked one thing in return: do not let the officials close the kitchen while investigating it.

一名衣衫破舊的女子帶兩人來到公共廚房旁已關門的美髮沙龍。這裡的家庭靠儲糧度冬，但乾淨燃料短缺，使避難處不再安全。窗臺上有一列彩石，色譜記錄著過去穿過建築的熱。沈舟把石頭逐一貼近耳邊，辨出一個不合調的低音。季懷在種子保管人的舊圖表上找到相同音記。證據顯示市集下方的一條維修線已改道。女子只要求一件事：調查期間不要讓官員關閉廚房。

Repair work began to stall when the gate bureau demanded a new strategic plan. Shen refused to suspend the emergency warming of the kitchen, explaining that its immediate safety was a separate issue from the theoretical debate over long-term ownership. The bureau chief said that preserving the transfer network mattered more than one meal service. Ji asked whether that judgment included the people who would go without food tonight. The chief left without answering. The elders voted to keep the kitchen open under supervised temporary power, and the workers recorded every hour consumed so the cost would not disappear into an untraceable account.

城門官署要求新策略計畫，讓修理工作陷入停頓。沈舟拒絕暫停公共廚房的緊急供暖，解釋眼前安全與長期所有權的理論辯論是兩回事。官署長說保存轉移網路比一場供餐更重要。季懷問他，這項判斷是否包含今晚會挨餓的人；對方沒有回答便走了。長者表決讓廚房在監督下使用臨時電力，也逐小時記錄耗能，避免代價消失在查不清的帳目裡。

The market held a small tribute for the people who had kept soup warm through the freeze. Shen found that a copper vein beneath the kitchen connected to the diverted line, but no worker possessed a visa to enter the privately guarded tunnel. Ji said they needed no secret entry whatsoever; they could request an independent safety inspection. It was slower than breaking a lock, but it would protect the kitchen from being called an illegal shelter. That night, as volunteers served the last bowls, Ji gave Shen the larger portion without comment. Shen noticed and quietly returned half, leaving two equal meals on the table.

市集舉行一場小小致謝，感謝冰封期間仍讓湯保持溫熱的人。沈舟發現廚房下方有一條銅礦脈接到改道的線路，但沒有任何工人持有進入私人守衛隧道的通行簽證。季懷說，他們完全不必秘密闖入；可以申請獨立安全檢查。這比破鎖慢，卻能保護廚房不被指作非法避難所。那晚志工送出最後幾碗湯，季懷不聲不響把較大的一份給了沈舟。沈舟注意到，默默分回一半，讓桌上留下兩份同樣的晚餐。

The city charter was meant to entitle every resident to emergency shelter, and Ji copied that exact line onto a sign beside the kitchen. He also added a contact address for people whose applications had been refused. Shen built a cover to keep the ink dry. The ragged woman from the salon read the sign twice and said she had never seen a rule written at a height she could comfortably reach. Ji looked at the wall, realizing that even a correct right could be made useless by the way it was presented. The group lowered the board and placed a second copy near the wheelchair ramp before the evening meal began.

城市憲章原本應使每個居民都有權獲得緊急庇護，季懷把那一行原文抄在廚房旁的告示上，並加上申請遭拒者可聯絡的地址。沈舟做了防雨蓋。沙龍那名衣衫破舊的女子讀了兩遍，說她從沒見過一條規則貼在自己能舒服閱讀的高度。季懷看著牆，明白即使權利真實存在，也可能因呈現方式而變得無用。大家把板子降低，晚餐開始前又在輪椅坡道附近放了第二份。
