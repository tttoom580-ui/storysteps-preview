玻璃屋頂下的聽證

A Hearing Beneath the Glass Roof

折春城的第四季：與你一起等春來 · 第 12 / 25 章 · 4000–5000 · 英中對照 · 校訂中

用互動版查詞、朗讀與收藏

The council booked the public arena for a hearing and imposed a ban on private security guards carrying weapons inside. A local celebrity offered to host the event, but Ji insisted that witnesses should not become entertainment. Above the entrance hung a crystal sign advertising the greenhouse festival. Shen stood beneath it and listened to the glass roof ring in the wind. One section sounded strained, and he asked the engineers to inspect it before the hall filled. The delay frustrated organizers, yet the inspection found a loose support. The hearing began late and safely, an outcome Ji considered more important than impressing the crowd.

議會租用公共競技場舉行聽證，禁止私人警衛帶武器入內。一名地方名人主動提出主持活動，季懷卻堅持證人不該變成娛樂表演。入口上方掛著宣傳溫室節的水晶招牌。沈舟站在下面聽風中玻璃屋頂的聲響，發現一段音色緊繃，便要求工程師在會場坐滿前檢查。主辦方因延誤而不滿，檢查卻真的找到鬆動支架。聽證延遲開始，但安全進行；季懷認為這比取悅群眾重要。

Witnesses described how the long cold could depress harvests and wages alike. A merchant accused the growers of fraud and threatened a lawsuit, claiming they intended to destroy lawful contracts. Ji answered that his intent was to examine whether consent had been obtained, not to punish anyone for owning a warm room. He presented the dated emergency agreement, the missing origin marks, and the independent soil readings. Shen then explained each measurement in ordinary language. Even residents who opposed the investigation could see the difference between a recorded fact and an accusation. The council requested the original maintenance orders rather than issuing an immediate punishment.

證人說明，漫長的寒冷會同時壓低收成與薪資。商人指控農人詐欺，威脅提起訴訟，聲稱他們意圖破壞合法合約。季懷回答，他要查的是是否真正取得同意，不是因為有人擁有溫暖房間就加以懲罰。他提出有日期的緊急協議、失蹤的來源標記與獨立土壤讀值。沈舟再用平實語言逐項解釋測量。即使反對調查的居民，也能看出紀錄事實與指控之間的差別。議會要求提交原始維修命令，而非立刻處罰誰。

The public healer argued that good nutrition depended on more than expensive winter produce. Several communities had lost access to ordinary beans, roots, and greens; the healer insisted on the plural because the harm reached beyond a single neighborhood. A councilor tried to poke holes in her figures by asking for every patient's name. She instead offered an anonymous portfolio of medical observations gathered with consent. Ji supported that choice. A person should not have to exchange private health information for a safer season. Outside the arena, volunteer cooks set out soup for anyone who wanted it. The meal reminded the audience that the hearing concerned daily life, not merely the prestige of the city's institutions.

公共療癒師指出，良好的營養不只靠昂貴的冬季蔬菜。數個不同社區都失去取得普通豆類、根菜和葉菜的機會；療癒師堅持使用複數形式，因為傷害不只波及單一鄰里。一名議員企圖藉由索取每位病患的名字來挑剔統計。療癒師改為提供經同意蒐集、已匿名的醫療觀察資料集。季懷支持她的選擇。任何人都不該以交出私人健康資訊作為換取安全季節的條件。場外，志工廚師替所有願意的人盛湯。那頓飯提醒觀眾，聽證討論的是日常生活，而不只是城市機構的面子。

A pharmacist described how a delayed prescription could become dangerous during a freeze. Her storeroom rack held several randomly chosen samples of heat-sensitive medicine from the residential districts. One package had spoiled while the private greenhouse's decorative flowers flourished. Shen asked the council to review both the water and medicine supply routes. The chair resisted, saying the hearing should concern only the seasonal tiles. Ji replied that a system's impact did not end at the edge of its drawing. The council agreed to invite medical experts to a second session, provided the additional evidence could be examined independently.

藥師說明，冰封期間延遲的處方可能造成危險。她的儲藏架放著從住宅區隨機選取的幾份溫度敏感藥品，其中一包已變質，私人溫室的裝飾花卻仍繁盛。沈舟請議會同時檢查水與藥品的供應路線。主席抗拒，認為聽證應只討論季節磚。季懷回答，一套系統的影響不會在圖紙邊緣自動停止。議會同意在第二場會議邀請醫療專家，條件是補充證據必須能接受獨立檢查。

A crowd began to shove toward the exit after a rumor spread that the greenhouse would shut down at once. Shen climbed the steps and explained that no order had been made to reverse the whole network. The ultimate goal was a stable return of spring, not a sudden collapse of winter services. Ji stood beside him and repeated the limited findings. The crowd settled when the chair confirmed their words. Later, under a quiet awning, Ji asked Shen whether he ever wanted to leave the city. Shen said he had once planned to travel forever, but he had begun thinking about what staying might require. Ji did not ask him to decide yet.

一項「溫室立刻關閉」的謠言傳出後，人群開始推擠出口。沈舟走上階梯，解釋沒有任何命令要求把整個網路立刻逆轉。最終目標是讓春天穩定回來，不是突然使冬季服務崩潰。季懷站到他身旁，重述有限的調查結果。主席確認後，人群才平靜。後來，在安靜的雨棚下，季懷問沈舟是否想過離開這座城。沈舟說，他曾經打算永遠旅行，現在卻開始想留下需要做些什麼。季懷沒有催他決定。

To avoid random selection becoming another excuse for unfairness, the second hearing drew its sample list in public. Ji watched the numbers emerge from a covered wooden box while two residents recorded them independently. Shen checked that the drawn households included those without permanent leases. The chair then read each condition of the medical review aloud. No witness was required to reveal private details that the inquiry did not need. The process was not exciting enough for the celebrity host, who left before noon. Ji was glad. A hearing designed for truth should not fail merely because it offered no spectacle for those who came to be entertained.

為避免隨機抽樣再次成為不公平的藉口，第二場聽證公開抽出樣本清單。季懷看著號碼從蓋住的木盒中抽出，兩名居民各自獨立記錄。沈舟確認抽中的家庭包含沒有永久租約者。主席接著大聲讀出醫療審查每項條件，不要求證人揭露調查無須使用的私人細節。程序不夠刺激，那名名人主持人中午前就走了。季懷反而高興：為真相設計的聽證，不應因為不能讓看熱鬧的人滿意就失敗。
