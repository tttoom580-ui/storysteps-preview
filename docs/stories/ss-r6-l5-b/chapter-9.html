The Wall That Chose a Side

折春城的第四季：與你一起等春來 · 第 9 / 25 章 · 4000–5000 · 英中對照 · 校訂中

The outer farms still used an ass to carry winter grain along the boundary road. A hired bodyguard tried to stop Ji and Shen at a private gate, claiming that the fields beyond comprised only company property. Ji showed the public-garden stamp and asked where the boundary had been surveyed. The guard looked uncertain. Shen offered to wait for an independent marker rather than push past him. Their patience brought an unexpected ally: the guard admitted that his own sister lived in the cold district. He was not authorized to open the gate, but he could tell them where the survey records were kept.

外圍農田仍用一頭驢沿邊界道路運冬糧。受雇保鑣在私人門口攔住季懷與沈舟，聲稱門後田地全部屬於公司。季懷拿出公共園圃的舊印章，詢問邊界在哪裡測量。保鑣猶豫了。沈舟提議等獨立界標到場，而不是硬闖。他們的耐心得到意外盟友：保鑣坦言自己的妹妹住在寒冷街區。他無權開門，卻可以告訴兩人測量紀錄保存在何處。

The original rules could not discriminate between rich and poor households when choosing who was eligible for seasonal relief. But the newest application used vague language about respectable residents. Shen measured the fluid pressure in a line behind the wall, while Ji asked the clerk to incorporate the low district's complaints into the hearing record. A copied form omitted every worker who lacked a permanent address. The clerk first said the omission was accidental, then discovered that an entire column had been deleted from the software. She agreed to preserve both versions, with the changes highlighted, before anyone corrected the records.

原始規則在決定誰有資格得到季節救援時，不得歧視富裕或貧窮住戶。但最新申請表用模糊文字要求「體面居民」。沈舟測量牆後管線的流體壓力，季懷則請書吏把下坡區的抱怨納入聽證紀錄。複製表格漏掉所有沒有固定地址的工人。書吏起先說是意外，後來發現軟體裡整欄都被刪掉了。她同意在修正前保留兩個版本，清楚標出差異。

An investigator from the growers' union joined them, a passionate woman who refused to confuse anger with proof. She found a patch of new mortar behind a small stone pyramid at the edge of the greenhouse estate. The pyramid had once marked a public water divide, not a religious site. Shen compared the mortar with the material used around the diverted maintenance pipe. Similar color was not enough to establish a connection, so they took samples under the owner's observation. Ji appreciated the caution. The case was becoming stronger because the investigators could name exactly what they did not yet know.

農人聯盟派來一名調查員，她熱情十足，卻拒絕把憤怒當證據。她在溫室領地邊緣的小石金字塔後找到一塊新補的灰泥。那座塔原本是公共分水界標，不是宗教建築。沈舟把灰泥與改道維修管周圍的材料比對；顏色相近不足以證明關聯，他們便在地主見證下採樣。季懷欣賞這份謹慎。案子之所以漸漸變強，是因為調查者能清楚指出還有哪些事不知道。

In a residence near the gate, a healer treated a child who had crossed the icy road in a torn sandal. The mother kept an old festival souvenir, a seed-shaped bell that had rung before the winters grew strange. A visiting therapist suggested that the child needed not only warmth but also the return of familiar routines. Ji agreed to organize an evening planting lesson in the common kitchen. He had nearly forgotten that restoring spring meant restoring the possibility of ordinary days. Shen helped build a low table that the children could reach, carefully sanding its corners so no one would be hurt.

守門處附近的住宅裡，一名療癒師照料一個穿著破涼鞋跨過冰路的孩子。母親保存著舊節慶紀念品，一枚種子形狀的小鈴，在冬季變得古怪前曾經響過。到訪的治療師說，孩子需要的不只是暖氣，還需要熟悉的生活節奏。季懷同意在公共廚房辦夜間種植課。他差點忘了，讓春天回來也代表平凡日子重新變得可能。沈舟協助做一張孩子夠得著的矮桌，仔細磨圓桌角，以免有人受傷。

The new evidence could not undo the months of cold, and even a valid document could conceal an unfair practice. Shen compared each version of the transfer schedule and discovered a column labeled vulnerable households. The public copy had left that column blank; the internal copy marked those homes as available sources of heat. Ji felt his hands tremble. He wanted to tell the city immediately, but the names belonged to families, not to him. The team published the method and totals while keeping addresses private. For the first time, the officials had to answer for a pattern rather than a single missing tile.

新證據無法抹去數月的寒冷，就算文件有效，也可能掩蓋不公平的作法。沈舟比對各版本的轉移時程，發現一欄寫著「脆弱住戶」。公開版把那欄留白，內部版卻把這些家庭標成可抽取熱量的來源。季懷雙手微微發抖。他想立刻告知全城，但姓名屬於那些家庭，不屬於他。團隊公開方法與總數，保護私人地址。官員第一次必須回答一整個模式的問題，而不再只是某塊失蹤的磚。

The survey team agreed that the protected lands comprise both the old seed field and the creek, even though the modern fence separated them. Ji asked the residents to describe how they used each place before the greenhouse was built. A woman remembered gathering herbs beside the water; a porter remembered carrying stones to repair its bank. Shen recorded the memories but checked them against the available maps. The stories did not always agree in detail, and that was acceptable. A common landscape was not an invented picture with every edge perfectly matched. It was a place where different people had worked, made mistakes, and still depended on the same water.

測量隊同意，受保護土地包含舊種子田與溪流，即使現代圍籬把兩處分開。季懷請居民形容溫室興建前如何使用土地。一名女子記得沿水採藥，一名搬運工記得搬石頭修溪岸。沈舟記錄回憶，也拿它們與現存地圖核對。故事在細節上不完全一致，並無不可。共同景觀不是每條線都畫得完美的虛構圖片，而是不同人曾勞動、犯錯，至今仍依賴同一片水的地方。
