學院後面的園圃

The Garden Behind the Academy

折春城的第四季：與你一起等春來 · 第 7 / 25 章 · 4000–5000 · 英中對照 · 校訂中

用互動版查詞、朗讀與收藏

The academy's recent acquisition included a backyard that once belonged to the public seed bank. An administrator had received a bonus for increasing its commercial yield, although a soil test showed falling calcium levels. Ji dug up a small chunk of old brick from beside the greenhouse gate. The brick carried a public-garden stamp from the time before the hill was privately managed. Shen brushed away the dirt and measured the edge. Neither expected a piece of building material to settle the dispute, but the stamp proved that at least one supposedly private plot had once been maintained for everyone.

學院最近買下的土地包含一處後院，原本屬於公共種子庫。管理員因提高商業產量而領了獎金，土壤測試卻顯示鈣含量下降。季懷從溫室門旁挖出一小塊舊磚，上面還有山坡尚未私有化時的公共園圃印章。沈舟刷掉泥土，量了邊緣。兩人不認為一塊建材就能解決爭議，但印章證明至少一片號稱私人所有的園地，過去曾由眾人共同維護。

A colonial-era map placed the orchard beside a different watercourse. Ji could not comprehend why modern records ignored that older stream. The academy's land officer said he would disapprove any attempt to change the property line before the next survey. Shen pointed out that recognizing the map's implication was not the same as moving a fence. They could investigate first. Using a shallow probe, he watched moisture penetrate the stone floor beneath the former seed room. A buried spring survived there. The discovery mattered because it offered a possible source for restoring the soil without taking warmth from another neighborhood.

殖民時期的地圖把果園畫在另一條水道旁，季懷不懂為何現代紀錄忽略了舊溪流。學院的土地官員說，在下次測量之前，他不會批准改動地界。沈舟指出，承認地圖可能意味著什麼，不等於立刻挪動籬笆；他們可以先調查。他用淺探針查看水氣滲入原種子室石地板下的情況，發現那裡仍有一道地下泉水。這項發現重要，因為它可能不必抽走別處的熱量，就能讓土壤回復。

An old republican statute protected the spring, but opening it too quickly would be risky. Ji gathered scrap boards to build a small channel under Shen's supervision. A university sophomore volunteered to carry tools and soon admitted that he had never worked outdoors. The seed room was spacious enough for a careful test, and the man learned how to keep his spine straight while lifting stones. Ji noticed Shen teaching without embarrassment or impatience. He had expected a wandering cultivator to show off mysterious powers. Instead Shen spent an hour explaining the weight of a bucket, which made the young volunteer stay for the next day's work.

一條舊共和時期法規保護那眼泉，但太快打開會有危險。季懷收集廢木板，在沈舟監督下搭建小水道。一名大學二年級學生自願搬工具，很快承認自己從未做過戶外勞動。種子室足夠寬敞，可以安全試驗；他也學會搬石頭時保持背脊筆直。季懷注意到沈舟教人既不使對方難堪，也不急躁。他本以為遊方修士會炫耀玄妙力量，沈舟卻花了一小時解釋水桶的重量，讓年輕志工願意隔天再來。

The group began to stack the rescued bricks by their original marks. A nearby tower carried surveillance devices for monitoring water theft, and the academy claimed the new garden was only symbolic. Shen asked to use the sensors for genuine measurements during the next transit of a season tile. The director hesitated, fearing that public data would challenge the academy's control. Ji offered a compromise: the reading could be witnessed by academy staff and neighborhood growers alike. They were not asking for ownership of the tower, only a way to compare readings from both sides of the wall. The director agreed to a one-day trial.

大家依原始記號把救出的磚堆疊起來。附近塔上有監視偷水行為的設備，學院卻說新園圃只有象徵意義。沈舟要求在下一次季節磚運送時，用感測器進行真正測量。主任遲疑了，擔心公開數據會挑戰學院的控制權。季懷提出折衷方案：由學院職員與鄰里農人共同見證讀值。他們不是要取得塔的所有權，只想比較圍牆兩側的數據。主任同意試行一天。

That evening, the rescued spring watered three seed trays. Ji chose not to plant every rare seed immediately. If the water failed, they would lose material that had taken generations to protect. Shen wrote the day's uncertainties beside the successful readings, refusing to disguise the limits of the test. The two men shared tea on the empty academy steps. Ji admitted that he had once ignored a warning about slow soil loss because a harvest looked healthy. Shen answered with a failure of his own: years ago, he had trusted the tone of a bell when he should have checked the cracked metal beneath it.

那晚，找回的泉水灌溉了三個育苗盤。季懷沒有立刻種下全部稀有種子；若水源失敗，便會失去數代人守護的材料。沈舟把當天的不確定處與成功讀值並列，不願掩飾試驗的限制。兩人在空蕩的學院臺階上喝茶。季懷承認，自己曾因收成看似健康而忽視土壤緩慢流失的警訊。沈舟也說起一次失敗：多年前，他相信鐘聲的音色，卻沒有檢查下方裂開的金屬。

When the last volunteer left, Shen closed the seed room's shutters and listened to the spring bubbling beneath the floor. The note was softer than the commercial pipes, but steady. Ji asked how he could hear such small differences. Shen explained that he did not hear secrets or intentions; he heard vibration, and then had to test what caused it. His former guild had encouraged students to talk as if hearing were knowledge. Ji said the distinction mattered to him too. An experienced grower could sense when a field was troubled, but a guess remained a guess until soil and weather supplied an answer. Neither of them needed to pretend to know more than he did.

最後一名志工離開後，沈舟關上種子室的百葉窗，聽地板下泉水冒泡。那音色比商業管線柔和，卻穩定。季懷問他如何聽出細小差異。沈舟解釋，他聽不到秘密或意圖，只聽得到振動，還必須測試原因。他過去的公會鼓勵學生把聽力說得像無所不知。季懷說，這個區別對自己也重要。有經驗的園丁能感覺田地出問題，但猜測仍是猜測，直到土壤與天氣給出答案。兩人都不必假裝知道超出自己所知的事。
