山開始學會傾聽

The Mountain Learns to Listen

折春城的第四季：與你一起等春來 · 第 4 / 25 章 · 4000–5000 · 英中對照

Before conducting the tile experiment, Shen tried to anticipate what could go wrong. The mountain bureau surrounded every request with bureaucracy, and the timing of a sudden inspection felt like more than coincidence. Several commuter wagons crossed the ridge each dawn, carrying workers to the profitable greenhouses while their own homes remained frozen. Ji interviewed three drivers separately. All three remembered the same extra cart after midnight, though each described a different driver. The contradiction mattered: it showed their memories were independent rather than rehearsed. Shen marked the road on a map and asked the bureau for traffic records instead of accusing a convenient stranger.

在進行季節磚實驗之前，沈舟先預想可能出錯的地方。山區官署讓每一項申請都陷入繁文縟節，突然安排的檢查時機也不像巧合。每天清晨，數輛通勤車越過山脊，把工人載往獲利豐厚的溫室，他們自己的家卻還在冰封。季懷分別訪問三名車夫。三人都記得午夜後多出一輛車，但形容的駕車者不同。矛盾反而重要，顯示記憶彼此獨立而非事先排練。沈舟把道路標在圖上，向官署要求車流紀錄，而不是指控一個容易懷疑的陌生人。

The bureau's denial was printed beside a round seal with a diameter twice that of the original. Shen recognized the distinctive hammer mark of a school workshop, not a government foundry. A retired faculty member from the sound academy explained that seals could be copied with soft metal, though each strike left its own fine lines. She let the men examine examples but would not identify a student without proof. Ji appreciated her restraint. They were not searching for someone to blame simply because that person knew a difficult skill. They wanted to know which document had actually authorized the missing transfers, and when its seal had been applied.

官署的否認聲明旁印著一枚圓形印章，直徑是原章的兩倍。沈舟認出那種獨特的錘痕來自學院工坊，而非官營鑄造所。一名退休的音律學院教師解釋，軟金屬可以複製印章，但每一次敲擊都會留下不同細紋。她讓兩人查看樣本，卻拒絕在沒有證據前指認學生。季懷欣賞她的克制。他們不是因為某人懂得困難技術，就急著找那人背罪；他們想知道究竟是哪份文件授權了失蹤的熱量轉移，以及印章何時蓋上。

The latest fiscal report treated the lost gardens as a tiny fraction of the city's property. Ji called that a hazard to public judgment: a fraction of land could still contain every meal a family grew. The healer showed them how cold disrupted a young animal's hormone balance, leaving it unable to find food even after a warm day. Shen revised the test plan to protect nesting sites and avoid the steepest slope. The change increased their work, but it meant the experiment would not sacrifice those who had already suffered. Ji wrote the new limits beside each participant's name and read them aloud before anyone signed.

最新財務報告把受損園圃寫成全市土地極小的一部分。季懷說這種表述會危害公眾判斷：面積再小，也可能包含一戶人家全部的口糧。療癒師示範寒冷如何打亂幼獸的荷爾蒙平衡，讓牠們即使遇到暖日也找不到食物。沈舟修改實驗方案，保護築巢區，避開最陡的坡。這增加了工作量，卻能避免再犧牲已受苦者。季懷在每位參與者姓名旁寫下新限制，簽署前一一讀給他們聽。

Downriver, marine birds had stopped nesting where snowmelt met the bay. A local doctor warned that mortality among the hatchlings had risen, although the cause remained uncertain. Shen drew two parallel routes for the returned water and offered a plea for a slower trial. The town practitioner agreed to observe the animals for several days, while fishermen took independent readings. Ji found this cooperation more powerful than a single heroic spell. The city had spent months pretending the valley could be understood from one office. Now many small observers were building a picture that no one person could control or easily erase.

下游的海鳥不再於融雪與海灣交會處築巢。當地醫師警告雛鳥死亡率上升，雖然原因還不確定。沈舟畫出兩條平行的回水路線，懇請採用更慢的試驗。診療師同意連續觀察動物數日，漁民則獨立記錄讀值。季懷覺得這種合作比單一英雄法術更有力量。城市幾個月來假裝可以只憑一間辦公室理解整座山谷；如今許多小小的觀察者正在建立一幅任何單一人物都不能控制或輕易抹去的圖像。

A messenger from the neighboring province arrived with maps of a similar winter failure. They did not offer a miraculous cure, but they did reassure Ji that a slow return of heat had worked elsewhere. Beneath the academy seal, Shen used angled light to uncover an older impression, made before the private greenhouse existed. The layered dates showed that the public manual had been altered after the first heat transfers. That night the men stayed in separate rooms at the field station, each with his own notes. When Ji could not sleep, he heard Shen tapping a quiet rhythm through the wall: a signal that neither of them was alone.

鄰省信使送來另一次冬季失常的地圖。他們沒有提供神奇解藥，卻讓季懷放心：緩慢回暖的方法在別處成功過。沈舟在學院印章下用斜光揭出一層更舊的印痕，時間早於私人溫室建成。重疊的日期顯示，公共手冊在第一批熱量轉移之後遭到改寫。那晚兩人住在野外站的不同房間，各自保存筆記。季懷睡不著時，聽見沈舟隔牆輕敲節奏，像在告訴他：你並非孤身一人。

That evening Ji washed the instruments in the field station's yard, letting the warm water run only as long as needed. Shen asked whether the seed keeper had always trusted him. Ji laughed. When he first came to the valley as a young grower, he had mistaken a dormant plant for a dead one and almost thrown it away. The keeper made him watch that pot for forty days. On the last day a pale shoot appeared. Shen answered with a quiet story of his own: a broken bell once taught him that silence could hold information, even if the maker feared what it meant. Both men remembered the lesson as they prepared the next test.

當晚，季懷在野外站院子清洗器具，只讓溫水流到真正需要的程度。沈舟問，種子保管人是否一向信任他。季懷笑了：他初到山谷做年輕農人時，曾把休眠植物誤認為死去，差點扔掉。保管人要他守著花盆四十天，最後一天才冒出淡色新芽。沈舟也安靜地說了一段往事：一口壞鐘曾教他，沉默也能保存資訊，即使製作者害怕其中意義。兩人帶著各自的教訓，準備下一次測試。
