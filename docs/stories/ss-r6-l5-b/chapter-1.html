春天沒有抵達的那一天

The Day Spring Failed to Arrive

折春城的第四季：與你一起等春來 · 第 1 / 25 章 · 4000–5000 · 英中對照

用互動版查詞、朗讀與收藏

There should have been certainty about the arrival of spring. The temple bells had rung at dawn for three hundred years, and every family knew the date. Instead, frost covered the steps of the abandoned chapel. A cluster of young plum trees stood brown beneath a sky as clear as glass. A public notice promised compensation for ruined gardens, yet offered no explanation. Ji Huai, thirty-six, had spent half his life raising medicinal plants. He placed a living root inside his coat and walked toward the square, troubled less by the cold than by the official insistence that nothing unusual had happened.

照理說，春天何時到來應當毫無疑問。三百年來，寺廟的鐘總在那天黎明響起，每戶人家都記得日期。如今，荒廢的小禮拜堂階梯卻結了霜，一叢年輕梅樹在玻璃般澄澈的天空下枯成褐色。公告承諾補償受損庭園，卻沒有解釋原因。三十六歲的季懷花了半輩子栽培藥草。他把一株還活著的根苗藏進外衣，走向廣場；比寒冷更令他不安的，是官府堅稱一切正常。

Shen Zhou, thirty-four, arrived with a carpenter's case and demanded that the gate office disclose its seasonal records. The clerk looked envious of his travel permit but refused to open a drawer. At the public forum, a little generator hummed beneath the stage, powering an enchanted board that displayed yesterday's temperature instead of today's. Shen struck the wooden frame lightly and heard a missing note. He had once studied sound cultivation, a discipline that let him detect an object's strain, not command it. Ji watched him work and realized that this stranger was checking a fact rather than asking people to trust a story.

三十四歲的沈舟帶著木匠工具箱抵達，要求城門辦事處公開季節紀錄。書吏羨慕他的旅行通行證，卻拒絕開抽屜。在公開論壇上，一臺小型發電機於臺下嗡嗡作響，供應靈板電力；板上顯示的是昨天的溫度，而不是今天。沈舟輕敲木框，聽見少了一個音。他曾學過聽音修行，能察覺物件承受的張力，卻不能命令物件改變。季懷看著他工作，明白這個陌生人是在核對事實，而非要求眾人相信傳說。

The city had been built around a hillside habitat where wild seeds survived winter. Those seeds were part of its heritage, not a private collection to be sold. Beyond the walls stretched an immense greenhouse district, warm even now, and Ji felt an impulse to break its gates. Shen stopped him, pointing to the dark frost under their feet. If heat had been taken from one neighborhood and placed in another, breaking the destination would not return the lost days. They needed to know the exchange rules. A vendor who had seen the greenhouse's midnight carts quietly offered to show them the tracks.

城市圍繞山坡上的野生種子棲地建成，那些種子是眾人的共同傳承，不是可供私人出售的收藏。城牆外有一整片巨大的溫室區，現在依然溫暖。季懷一時衝動，想砸開溫室大門。沈舟指著腳下暗色的霜阻止他：如果某個街區的熱量被移到另一處，砸壞接收熱量的地方，也不會讓失去的日子回來。他們需要先理解交換規則。一名看見午夜運貨車的攤商，悄悄表示願意帶他們看車轍。

Shen offered a practical insight. Every season tile could move warmth and moisture for seven days, but it created neither. A metropolitan engineer had designed the system as an emergency measure, and a forgotten provision required every tile to carry its origin mark. Public inspection was supposedly optional, although the original manual used the word only for minor repairs. Ji copied the text while Shen drew the shape of a missing screw. Their different skills became useful at once. Ji knew where stolen warmth would leave wounds in the soil; Shen knew where borrowed power would leave a change in the sound of metal.

沈舟提出一個實際的洞察：每塊季節磚可以搬動七天的熱與水分，卻無法憑空創造兩者。都會工程師原本把它設計為緊急措施；一項被遺忘的條款要求每塊磚都要標示來源。官府聲稱公開檢查可以自行選擇，但原始手冊說的「可選」其實只適用於小修理。季懷抄下文字，沈舟畫出缺少螺絲的形狀。他們的不同本事立刻派上用場：季懷知道被抽走的溫度會如何傷害土壤，沈舟知道借來的力量會如何改變金屬的聲音。

Near the warehouse, a child began to stumble on ice as the night supervisor hurried past. Ji caught the child and found cold damage spreading across a basket of seedlings. The supervisor insisted that the arrangement was an ordinary transaction between willing households. But no household in the poorest lane remembered agreeing to it. Outside, winter herbs had begun to wither. Shen showed Ji a small copper plate stamped with a garden number rather than a merchant's mark. Together they took the plate to the seed keeper, promising to examine it openly before accusing anyone. The missing spring had finally become a question with a traceable answer.

靠近倉庫時，一名孩子在冰上踉蹌，夜班主管卻急著擦身而過。季懷扶住孩子，發現籃裡的幼苗正被寒氣侵害。主管堅持這只是自願住戶間的普通交易，但最貧窮那條巷子的居民沒有一戶記得同意過。屋外的冬季藥草開始枯萎。沈舟向季懷展示一片小銅牌，上面刻的是園圃編號，而非商人記號。兩人把銅牌交給種子保管人，承諾在指控任何人之前先公開檢驗。失去的春天終於成為一個可以追查的問題。

Before they left the seed house, Ji asked why Shen had traveled to this city at all. Shen said a cracked bell in the next valley had been his intended destination. He had planned to stay only two days, finish a repair, and move on before anyone learned his history. Ji looked at the copper plate between them and wondered how many journeys had changed because someone stopped to answer a question. He did not ask for the whole history. Instead he offered Shen an empty drawer at his workshop, a place to keep tools without fearing they would be taken. Shen accepted the drawer, though not yet the invitation it quietly represented.

離開種子屋之前，季懷問沈舟究竟為何來到此城。沈舟說，隔壁山谷的一口裂鐘才是原定目的地。他本來打算只住兩天，完成修理後便離開，不讓任何人知道自己的過去。季懷看著兩人之間的銅牌，心想有多少旅程因為有人停下來回答問題而改變。他沒有追問所有往事，只給沈舟一個工坊的空抽屜，讓工具能放在不必擔心被拿走的地方。沈舟接受了抽屜，卻尚未接受那份安靜包含的邀請。
