一首歌的價格

The Price of a Song

1994，榕灣週報的最後一頁 · 第 8 / 25 章 · 3000–4000 · 英中對照 · 校訂中

用互動版查詞、朗讀與收藏

The town hall invited the weekly to cover a concert and wedding exhibition. Caiping expected ordinary announcements, but found a lively debate about who was paid for public culture. A young composer, Liao Min, had written a song for the opening. The program introduced the bridegroom of a council member's daughter as the event's chief supporter, while naming the composer only in small print. Min called that an abstract problem with a real cost: people praised the music but assumed it belonged to the wealthy family. Her enthusiasm had led her to accept a low fee; now she wondered whether silence would teach younger artists to do the same. Caiping asked who owned the work and whether the contract had been understood by everyone who signed it.

鎮公所邀週報採訪音樂會與婚禮展。采蘋原以為只是普通公告，卻遇上一場關於文化勞動報酬的爭論。年輕作曲家廖敏替開幕寫了歌曲，節目單把一名議員女兒的新郎列為主要支持者，作曲家名字卻縮在角落。廖敏說，這看似抽象，代價卻真實：大家稱讚音樂，卻以為作品屬於富裕家庭。當初熱情使她接受很低的酬勞，如今她擔心沉默會讓更年輕的創作者也照做。采蘋問清楚作品權屬，以及簽約者是否真正理解條款。

The town's cultural bulletin described the concert as a gift to every civilian in the district. But the printed budget included payments for flowers, lighting, and hired chairs, while leaving music under the word "voluntary." Min said she had been given a cue to begin at precisely seven, rehearsed for three weeks, and produced written scores. Those tasks constituted work, not simply a cheerful visit. Another musician warned that criticism could create jealousy between the performers and the family that had funded the hall. Caiping did not dismiss that concern. She asked the organizer to estimate the hours of each contributor and to explain how unpaid labor had been identified. A transparent record might help artists without turning every generous act into an accusation.

鎮上的文化公告把音樂會寫成送給全區民眾的禮物。預算卻列有鮮花、燈光與椅子的支出，音樂項目只寫「志願」。廖敏說，自己收到七點準時上場的提示，排練了三週，也寫出完整樂譜，這些都是工作，不只是開心來幫忙。另一名樂手擔心批評會造成演出者與出資家庭間的嫉妒。采蘋沒有否定他的顧慮，而是請主辦方估算所有參與者的工時，說明何以將部分勞動列為無薪。透明紀錄或許能幫助創作者，也不必把每份善意都說成惡意。

At the newspaper office, Xiaoyu brought in a new software program that could calculate subscriptions on a borrowed computer. It took an entire afternoon to acquire the correct disk and learn the commands. The machine was slow, but its systematic lists showed something surprising: more women than men had purchased the last three issues, and many lived in the farming villages beyond the gulf. Caiping cautioned against making a large claim about gender from a small set of buyers. The data showed who paid for the paper, not who read it or why. It also revealed a gulf between the weekday newspaper's subjects and its readers' interests. A growing number wanted information about land, family work, and schools rather than celebrity celebrations alone.

回到報社，小雨帶來一套可在借來的電腦上計算訂閱的軟體。她們花了一下午才取得正確磁片、學會操作。機器很慢，但系統化名單顯示一個意外現象：最近三期買報的女性比男性多，許多人住在海灣對岸的農村。采蘋提醒，不可只憑少量購買資料就對性別做大推論。數字只能說明誰付錢，不能說明誰閱讀或原因。它也顯示報導主題與讀者關心的事之間存在差距：越來越多人想讀土地、家庭勞動和學校消息，而不只是名人慶典。

The land beyond the gulf was fertile, and its vegetable farmers had been trying to protect a shared irrigation channel. One farmer described the intensity of recent disputes over water use. Developers wanted new houses; farmers feared the construction would invade the channel's narrow banks. Caiping was careful with the word "invade" because the land records had not yet been checked. She asked both groups to mark their proposed boundaries on separate maps. The disagreement could not be solved by a headline calling either side greedy. During the concert rehearsal, Min listened to this conversation and offered to organize a public meeting at the hall. She wanted the space to become useful between performances, rather than dominate the town's cultural calendar once a year.

海灣對岸的土地肥沃，種菜農民一直努力保護共用灌溉水道。一名農民形容近來用水爭議十分激烈。建商想蓋新房，農民怕工程侵占狹窄水岸。由於土地紀錄尚未核對，采蘋使用「侵占」一詞時格外謹慎，請雙方在不同地圖上標出主張的邊界。只靠標題罵任何一方貪婪，無法解決分歧。音樂會彩排時，廖敏聽見這段談話，主動提出在禮堂辦一場公開座談。她希望空間在演出之間也能服務地方，而不是一年只被盛會占據一次。

The revised concert program credited the composer in large letters, but did not solve the payment dispute. The organizer agreed to publish the original agreement and invite musicians to discuss future rates. Caiping printed the change as news, not as proof that the town had become fair overnight. A learned librarian advised Min to request written terms before accepting a public commission; the sponsor had learned that his generosity could be questioned without being denied. At dusk, children practiced the song outside the hall. Its melody was strong enough to travel across the square without a microphone. Caiping listened and thought about the weekly's own work. A paper could give a community a voice, but it could also take that voice away by giving credit to the wrong person.

修改後的節目單用大字標出作曲家姓名，但付款爭議仍未解決。主辦方同意公布原協議，邀樂手討論未來酬勞。采蘋把改動當作新聞刊出，不說小鎮從此就公平了。一名博學的圖書館員建議廖敏，接受公開委託前要先索取書面條款；贊助人也學會善意仍可接受質疑，而不代表他的貢獻被否定。黃昏時，孩子們在禮堂外練唱，旋律不用麥克風也傳遍廣場。采蘋聽著，想到週報的工作：報紙能給地方發聲機會，也可能因為把功勞寫錯名字而奪走聲音。
