A Word That Grew Too Large

1994，榕灣週報的最後一頁 · 第 9 / 25 章 · 3000–4000 · 英中對照 · 校訂完成

用互動版查詞、朗讀與收藏

A rumor about nuclear waste reached the weekly on a rainy Wednesday. A shopkeeper said that the old science exhibit near the closed mill had once carried a warning about "nuclear material," and that a secret shipment was now being planned. Xiaoyu frowned at the claim but wrote it down. A photograph attached to the letter showed a rusted sign with only half its content visible. Caiping decided they must not let a damaged sentence mislead the town. She asked for the original sign, the date of the photograph, and the circumstances in which it was taken. The shopkeeper insisted that his cousin had seen crates unloaded at night, but he could not identify their owner. Caiping labeled both stories unverified in her notebook rather than calling them evidence.

一個關於核廢料的傳聞在雨天星期三傳到週報。店主說，關閉造紙廠附近的舊科學展覽曾有「核材料」警告，如今有人計畫祕密運貨。小雨皺眉，仍把話記下。附信照片只拍到生鏽告示的一半內容。采蘋決定不能讓破碎的句子誤導全鎮，她要求查看原始告示、照片日期和拍攝情況。店主又說表兄在夜間見過有人卸下木箱，卻說不出貨主姓名。采蘋把兩個說法都標記為尚未查證，而不是直接當成證據。

They went to the museum storage room, where a retired guide spoke with a strong mountain accent. His homeland had been far from the coast, and he sometimes chose words that visitors misunderstood. He put on an earphone connected to an old recording and asked Xiaoyu to listen. The exhibit had explained how scientists studied the atom; the full title was "From the Atomic Age to Public Safety." The damaged sign belonged to that historical display. It did not describe the mill or any shipment. The guide gave them a clumsy wink, pleased to be useful, then grew serious. "You need modesty when you hear an unfamiliar word," he said. "Ask me to repeat it before you decide what I mean."

她們到博物館倉庫，遇見一名帶濃厚山區口音的退休導覽員。他的故鄉離海岸很遠，有些用字常被遊客誤會。他把耳機接上舊錄音機，請小雨聆聽。展覽其實介紹科學家研究原子的歷史，完整題目是「從原子時代到公共安全」。破損告示屬於那場歷史展，與造紙廠和任何貨運都無關。導覽員笨拙地眨眼，似乎很高興自己幫上忙，隨後又嚴肅起來：「聽到不熟悉的字，要有謙遜。先請我重複，再決定我在說什麼。」

At lunch a street vendor offered crunchy rice crackers, and Xiaoyu almost forgot to eat while checking the photograph's edges. The original negative proved that someone had cropped the sign. Still, neither the guide nor the photograph could explain the supposed night shipment. Caiping instructed Xiaoyu to keep the two claims separate. She called the port office, asked for the public cargo schedule, and noted an interruption in its records during a power failure. The office said the schedule could be repaired from paper receipts, but that would take several days. Caiping leaned back in her chair and considered the economic harm of an alarming false report. Local fish sellers were already losing customers because a rumor had spread faster than the weekly could investigate it.

午餐時，街頭攤販端來酥脆米餅，小雨忙著檢查照片邊緣，差點忘了吃。原始底片證明有人裁切過告示。不過導覽員與照片都無法解釋傳聞中的夜間運貨。采蘋指示小雨把兩項說法分開處理。她致電港務處索取公開貨運時刻，發現停電期間紀錄中斷。港務處說能靠紙本收據重建紀錄，但需要幾天。采蘋靠回椅背，思索錯誤警報造成的經濟傷害。魚販已開始失去顧客，因為傳聞的傳播比週報的調查更快。

A traveling university student visited with a photocopy of a revolutionary new computer guide, but its cheerful promises about instant access did not help them. Most residents had no connection to such systems, and the needed harbor records were not on any screen. She offered to peep at the port manager's private ledger through an office window. Caiping refused: curiosity did not grant permission to enter someone else's records. Instead, she requested the public files in writing. The student seemed embarrassed, then asked whether she could help organize the papers once they arrived. Caiping accepted. She explained that responsible investigation did not require every person to have the same skills; it required everyone to understand where the limits were.

一名返鄉大學生帶來介紹革命性新電腦技術的影印手冊，但其中對即時查詢的樂觀承諾幫不上忙。鎮上多數人無法連上這些系統，必要的港務紀錄也不在螢幕上。學生提議從辦公室窗外偷看港務主管的私人帳簿。采蘋拒絕了：好奇不代表有權閱覽他人的紀錄。她改以書面申請公開檔案。學生有些不好意思，隨後問能否在資料送達後協助整理。采蘋答應，並解釋負責任的調查不是人人都要有同樣技術，而是所有人都明白界線在哪裡。

When the records finally arrived, the supposed midnight crates turned out to contain replacement parts for fishing nets. The shopkeeper asked the paper for a refund on a paid notice he had placed warning customers about the alleged danger. Caiping agreed to return the fee and help him print a correction, but not to erase the original notice from the archive. He was surprised, then accepted. On Friday the front page reported both the disproved museum claim and the verified shipping records, with dates large enough to read. Her father said the story might disappoint people who wanted a great mystery. Caiping answered that a small truth could be more important than a great scare. Xiaoyu placed the uncropped photograph beside the article, allowing readers to see what had been missing.

公開紀錄送達後，所謂午夜木箱原來裝著漁網替換零件。店主先前付費刊登過警告顧客的公告，如今要求退款。采蘋同意退還刊費並協助更正，卻不會從存檔中抹除原公告。店主有些驚訝，最後接受了。星期五頭版同時說明博物館傳聞已被推翻，以及經核實的運貨紀錄，日期印得足夠清楚。父親說這也許會讓期待大陰謀的讀者失望。采蘋回答，小小的真相可能比驚人的恐嚇更重要。小雨把未裁切的完整照片放在文章旁，讓讀者看見原先被隱藏的部分。
