The Second Run

1994，榕灣週報的最後一頁 · 第 11 / 25 章 · 3000–4000 · 英中對照 · 校訂中

The weekly's tenth issue arrived with a letter from a retired telegraph operator. He had preserved a dusty code book from the old harbor station and wanted to show how urgent messages once traveled before telephone lines reached the outer villages. Caiping planned a history page, but her father wanted the space for advertisements. The newspaper still lost money, and one generous donation could not change the eventual consequence of unpaid bills. He suggested expanding circulation along the inland road, where new families were moving. Xiaoyu proposed that volunteers circulate sample copies at the town marathon. The idea seemed graceful on paper: runners would pass through every neighborhood, and a small table could introduce readers to the weekly. Caiping agreed only if the event organizer gave written permission and volunteers received reasonable breaks.

週報第十期發行時，一名退休電報員寄來信。他保存著港口舊電報站一本布滿灰塵的密碼簿，想介紹電話線通往外村之前，緊急訊息如何傳遞。采蘋準備做歷史專頁，但父親想賣給廣告主。週報仍在虧損，一筆善款也改變不了未繳帳單終將造成的後果。父親建議沿新住戶增加的內陸公路擴大發行；小雨則提議請志工在鎮上馬拉松傳閱並分送樣報。這主意在紙上很漂亮：跑者會經過各社區，小攤位可以介紹週報。采蘋同意，但要求先取得主辦單位書面許可，也得讓志工合理休息。

The marathon organizers were negotiating with a sponsor that sold solar lights for remote houses. Its director spoke at length about capitalism and the market's power to reward useful products. Caiping did not oppose selling lamps, but she asked whether the demonstration lamps worked during heavy rain. The director answered that models differed and gave her a test sheet. His public statement made the company sound like a dominant force in local technology, yet the figures showed it was still a small business. There was no shame in that. Caiping printed its actual sales range and checked that readers could distinguish an advertisement from a report. When the director offered to pay for a larger display, she negotiated the price without surrendering the article to his persuasion.

馬拉松主辦方正和一間販售偏遠住家太陽能燈的廠商談贊助。廠商主管長談資本主義與市場如何獎勵有用產品。采蘋並不反對賣燈，卻問展示燈具在大雨中能否運作。主管說型號不同，交給她測試表。他的公開發言把公司說得像地方科技的主導力量，實際數字卻顯示仍是小公司。這沒有什麼好丟臉。采蘋刊登真實銷量範圍，也確認讀者能區分廣告與新聞。主管願意付更多錢買大版面，她與他議價，卻不讓說服話術取代編輯決定。

On race day, Xiaoyu interviewed a teenager who called herself a rebel because she refused to join the school's winning team. She preferred a mixed group that welcomed slower runners. "Winning should not breed contempt," she said. Her words made Caiping smile, though she asked whether the girl wished to be quoted. At the finish line a child tried to tickle his exhausted father, who laughed despite aching legs. The scene was charming, but Xiaoyu saw an indication of a wider story in the volunteers carrying water to people who had fallen behind. Their contribution was not listed among the event's awards. She took notes on their work and checked the schedule with the first-aid coordinator, turning a brief encounter into a report about invisible labor.

比賽那天，小雨採訪一名自稱反叛者的少女，因為她拒絕加入學校的冠軍隊，而選擇容納較慢跑者的混合組。「勝利不該滋生輕視。」她說。采蘋聽了微笑，仍要確認少女是否願被引用。終點線旁，一個孩子想搔他筋疲力竭的父親癢，父親腿雖痠仍笑了。畫面很可愛，但小雨更注意到志工替落後者送水，暗示了另一則新聞。這些人的工作沒有列入頒獎名單。她記下服務內容，和急救協調員核對時刻，把短暫相遇寫成一篇關於隱形勞動的報導。

Later, a boat captain brought a photograph of a wreck that had blocked the old harbor entrance decades earlier. The retired telegraph operator explained how the station's coded warning prevented a second collision. He disliked modern newspapers that treated every accident as a story about villains. People had made errors, he said, but the more useful lesson was how the warning system changed. Caiping recognized the comparison to the present. A local business might look like an invasion to older shopkeepers, while new residents might see it as opportunity. The weekly's job was not to declare one feeling correct. It was to describe who bore costs, which promises could be checked, and which changes remained possible. She placed the old warning beside the new marathon route map.

稍後，一名船長帶來幾十年前船骸堵住舊港入口的照片。退休電報員解釋，電報站當年的密碼警報如何避免第二次撞船。他不喜歡現代報紙把每次事故都寫成壞人的故事。有人確實犯錯，但更有用的是警報制度怎麼改變。采蘋想到當下：新公司進入可能被老店家視為侵入，新住戶卻可能視為機會。週報不該只判定哪種感受正確，而應寫出誰承擔成本、哪些承諾可被查證、哪些改變仍可選擇。她把舊警報放在新馬拉松路線圖旁。

The event sold only thirty-two subscriptions, fewer than anyone had hoped. Shiming called the result a disappointment, but Xiaoyu brought back twenty addresses of people who wanted the paper delivered to a public reading room instead of their homes. That was useful information too. The editor calculated the true delivery cost, including time spent folding and sorting, and found a possible plan for shared bundles. Her father was tired, yet he listened as she explained the numbers. They agreed to test the route for a month before hiring more workers. Caiping had once believed that a newspaper survived through brilliant headlines. Now she saw another possibility: a paper might endure because people quietly carried it to one another, as the telegraph operator had once carried urgent messages across a broken line.

活動只賣出三十二份訂閱，比任何人預期都少。世明稱結果失望，但小雨帶回二十個地址，這些人希望報紙送到公共閱覽室，而不是住家。這也是有用資訊。采蘋把摺報、分裝與送貨工時計入，算出共用報包可能可行。父親雖疲倦，仍聽她說明數字。大家同意先測試一個月，再決定是否增聘人手。采蘋以前以為報紙要靠驚人的標題存活，如今她看見另一種可能：報紙也能靠人們默默替彼此傳遞，就像老電報員曾在斷線之間送出急訊。
