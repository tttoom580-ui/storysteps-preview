河水裡的顏色

The Color in the Water

1994，榕灣週報的最後一頁 · 第 6 / 25 章 · 3000–4000 · 英中對照 · 校訂中

用互動版查詞、朗讀與收藏

The dye workshop on the western bank bought the largest advertisement in Banyan Bay. Its owner, Mr. Peng, sold linen cloth and decorative goods to hotels, but he also paid wages to sixty local workers. When a reader sent the newspaper a bottle of blue river water, Caiping knew a report could threaten both the stream and those jobs. She called Peng and offered to visit the factory. He called her a conservative editor who feared modern industry, then agreed to a tour if she did not bring a camera. A consultant hired by the workshop explained its safety procedures. She spoke politely, but her repeated assurances sounded habitual, as if she had answered the same criticism many times. Caiping asked whether she could enclose the reader's question in a written request and receive a dated response.

西岸的染布工坊買下榕灣週報最大的一塊廣告。老闆彭先生把亞麻布與裝飾商品賣給飯店，也僱用六十名當地工人。當讀者寄來一瓶藍色河水時，采蘋知道報導可能同時影響溪流與就業。她打電話給彭先生，提議參觀工廠。他稱她是害怕現代工業的保守派編輯，卻仍同意讓她不帶相機參觀。工坊聘請的顧問解釋安全程序，語氣禮貌，但一再重複的保證聽來像習慣用語，彷彿已回答同樣的批評無數次。采蘋問能否把讀者的疑問附在書面請求中，請對方留下具日期的回覆。

The tour began with a safety orientation in a bright room where workers folded cloth with surprising grace. A stereo played soft music beside the cutting tables. The dye room, however, smelled sharply of heated chemicals, and one pipe disappeared beneath a locked door. The consultant said that visitors could not enter because the space was used to isolate hazardous materials. Caiping did not demand unlawful entry. Instead, she asked for the water-test records and the name of the laboratory. One worker, a timid young woman called Mei, watched from behind a rack of cloth. Her hands began to tremble when the owner approached. Caiping refused to interview her on the spot. After the tour she left a card with a public telephone number, explaining that any worker could contact the newspaper without having their name printed.

參觀從明亮摺布室的安全說明會開始，工人們把布摺得十分優雅。裁切桌旁的立體音響播放柔和音樂。染色室卻散發加熱藥劑的刺鼻氣味，有根管線從上鎖的門下延伸出去。顧問說那裡用來隔離危險材料，訪客不能進入。采蘋沒有要求違法闖入，而是索取水質檢驗紀錄與實驗室名稱。年輕又膽怯的女工阿梅躲在布架後看著，老闆靠近時，她的雙手開始顫抖。采蘋沒有當場逼問。參觀後，她留下附有公共電話號碼的名片，說任何工人都可以聯絡報社，且不必公開姓名。

Back at the office Xiaoyu wanted to print the blue bottle on the front page. "Any consumer would be angry," she said. Caiping replied that anger was not a measurement. A retired school scientist offered to look at a sample under a microscope, but warned that color alone did not prove a violation. The liquid might contain a harmless pigment or something that required special testing. Calling the factory ignorant or criminal before knowing the result would be unfair. Caiping approved a small notice asking residents to record when they noticed changes in the water, without touching it or entering the channel. She also wrote that they would seek independent analysis. This was less dramatic than the photograph Xiaoyu wanted, yet it gave readers a practical way to contribute evidence.

回到報社，小雨想把那瓶藍水放上頭版。「消費者看了一定生氣。」她說。采蘋回答，憤怒不是測量。退休科學老師願意用顯微鏡觀察樣本，但提醒顏色本身不能證明違規。液體可能含無害顏料，也可能需要專業檢驗。結果未明前，就稱工廠無知或犯罪並不公平。采蘋同意刊登一則小公告，請居民記錄何時看見水色變化，卻不要觸摸水或進入水道。她也寫明會尋求獨立分析。這比小雨想要的照片不刺激，卻讓讀者知道如何提出可用的證據。

Mr. Peng telephoned in a rage when he saw the notice. He claimed that the weekly wanted to ruin an honorable occupation and discourage investment. Caiping explained that the paper had reported no finding against him; it had asked for records. He said the advertisement paid for most of her mother's wages. She checked the figures and admitted that was true for the previous month. Nevertheless, she would not exchange silence for income. Peng's consultant eventually delivered two test reports, both taken before the blue water had appeared. The reports were useful but did not settle the current question. Caiping printed their dates beside the workshop's response. She invited the workers to explain their own routines, provided they could do so safely, and she separated their experiences from the company's official statements.

彭先生看見公告後怒氣沖沖打來電話，說週報想毀掉正當職業、阻止投資。采蘋解釋報紙並未認定他有罪，只是要求紀錄。他指出，上一個月的廣告費支付了母親大部分薪資。采蘋查帳後承認這是事實，但仍不能用沉默交換收入。彭先生的顧問最後送來兩份檢驗報告，採樣日期卻都早於藍水出現。資料有用，卻無法解答目前的問題。采蘋把日期和工坊回覆並列刊出，也邀請工人安全地說明自己的工作流程，並將個人經驗與公司的正式聲明分開呈現。

That night her mother described the first time she had worked for money. She had sewn sheets for a luxury hotel and been told that ordinary women should be grateful for any wage. She wanted the weekly to survive, but she would not ask Caiping to make the same bargain for her sake. Together they calculated how many small subscriptions could replace one large advertisement. The number was difficult, though not impossible. Caiping wrote a letter to readers explaining the risk and inviting them to decide whether independent reporting was worth its cost. She could not promise that the factory was guilty; she could promise that evidence would not be weighed according to the size of an advertisement. The blue water waited in a sealed cabinet, still a question rather than an answer.

那晚，母親談起自己第一次賺錢的經歷。她替豪華飯店縫床單，卻被告知普通女人有工資就該知足。她希望週報活下去，但不會要求采蘋為了自己做同樣的交易。母女一起算出要多少小額訂閱才能取代一筆大廣告費。數字很難達成，卻不是完全沒希望。采蘋寫信給讀者，說明風險，請大家決定獨立報導值不值得支持。她無法保證工廠有罪，能保證的是，證據的重量不會取決於廣告大小。那瓶藍水被封存在櫃裡，仍是一個問題，而不是答案。

Why does Caiping refuse to declare the factory guilty?

  1. The blue color alone does not establish the water's contents.
  2. The owner has never employed local workers.
  3. The factory has stopped making cloth.
