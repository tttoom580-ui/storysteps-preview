Numbers in the Creek

1994，榕灣週報的最後一頁 · 第 7 / 25 章 · 3000–4000 · 英中對照 · 校訂中

用互動版查詞、朗讀與收藏

An academic letter from the university changed the argument about the blue river. A chemistry lecturer offered to examine samples from several locations, provided they were collected by trained people. Her aquarium of local fish had recently shown unusual behavior, but she refused to claim that the dye workshop was responsible. The river basin included farms, homes, and a road where rainwater gathered. Each source had its own characteristic pattern. Xiaoyu was annoyed by the lecturer's caution. "If everyone waits for perfect evidence, nobody will act," she said. Caiping answered that they could act by asking questions and preventing contact with suspicious water without inventing an answer. They would write what was known, report the limits of testing, and keep children away from the sampling area.

一封來自大學的學術信件改變了藍水爭論。化學講師願意檢驗多處採集的水樣，前提是必須由受訓者取樣。她飼養當地魚類的水族箱最近也出現異常，但她不肯宣稱一定是染布工坊造成。整個流域包括農場、住宅，以及積水的道路，各個來源都有自己的特徵模式。小雨對講師的謹慎感到煩躁：「大家都等完美證據，就沒人做事了。」采蘋回答，她們可以提出問題、避免接觸可疑水源，不需要捏造答案。報導要寫明已知資訊與檢驗限制，也要讓孩子遠離採樣區。

The town accountant volunteered to review the testing budget. His contribution was less exciting than a microscope, but it was essential: the paper could not quietly spend subscription money that had been promised for printing. The lecturer asked the town to cover the sample collection, and the newspaper agreed to report the results regardless of whether they were favorable. A volunteer group planted shade trees near the upper creek, hoping to improve the habitat over time. The group leader wanted admiration for its work; the lecturer reminded him that trees were not a substitute for testing. He accepted the correction after a long pause. Caiping found herself fortunate to have people who could disagree without treating every question as an attempt to defeat one another.

鎮上的會計師自願檢查檢驗預算。他的貢獻不像顯微鏡那樣引人注意，卻很重要：週報不能私自拿原本答應用於印刷的訂閱費來採樣。講師請公所支付採樣成本，週報則同意無論結果是否好看，都會如實刊出。志工團體在上游種植遮蔭樹，希望長期改善棲地。團長很希望得到讚美；講師提醒他，種樹不能取代檢驗。他沉默好一會兒才接受。采蘋覺得自己很幸運，身邊有人可以意見不同，卻不把每個問題都視為要打敗對方的攻擊。

To make the science accessible, Xiaoyu asked a schoolteacher who was fluent in both Mandarin and the local language to translate the key findings. The teacher built a simple chart around the rhythm of the tides: water rising at dawn, falling after noon, and gathering beneath the old bridge. The pattern was relevant, but it could not explain everything. One child said the chart reminded him of a song; another thought it looked like a staircase. Their curiosity motivated the lecturer to prepare a small public lesson. The prime aim, she explained, was not to turn everyone into a scientist overnight. It was to help residents understand why a test from one place on one day should not be described as the condition of an entire river.

為了讓科學內容容易理解，小雨請一位能流利使用華語與地方語言的老師翻譯重點。老師依潮汐節奏畫出簡單圖表：清晨漲水、中午後退水、舊橋下積水。這個模式相關，卻無法解釋所有現象。一名孩子說圖像一首歌，另一個覺得像樓梯。孩子們的好奇心促使講師準備公開小課程。她說，主要目的不是一夜之間讓所有人變科學家，而是讓居民明白：某日某地的單次檢驗，不能被當成整條河的狀況。

Mr. Peng attended the lesson. He did not apologize, but he brought a record of a recent expansion to the factory's drainage equipment. The new filter had been installed three weeks earlier, close to the date of the reported water change. Caiping asked whether the installation might have caused a temporary release. The consultant said the question deserved examination and offered access to the maintenance log. Xiaoyu was tempted to call the meeting a victory. Instead, Caiping told her to check the dates first. The lecturer could identify some chemical traces in the samples but still needed more information to connect them to a source. The paper reported both facts: a possible connection and an important gap. Readers could judge how far the evidence actually reached.

彭先生也來旁聽。他沒有道歉，卻帶來工廠最近擴充排水設備的紀錄。新過濾器在三週前安裝，時間接近水色變化的通報。采蘋問安裝過程是否可能造成短暫排放。顧問表示值得調查，願意提供維修日誌。小雨很想稱會議是一場勝利，采蘋卻提醒她先核對日期。講師能辨認出樣本中的部分化學痕跡，但仍需要更多資料才能確認來源。報紙同時寫出兩個事實：可能的關聯，以及尚未補上的證據空缺。讀者才有辦法判斷證據究竟支持到哪一步。

In the newsroom, the old press began making an uneven beat. Shiming said the rollers needed cleaning and that they should not strive to print an extra edition while the machine was unreliable. Caiping agreed. The delay would disappoint readers waiting for results, but a damaged press could injure someone and destroy the next week's paper. That night she wrote a short bulletin saying when new data might arrive and which questions remained open. The next morning a boy brought them a hand-drawn map of the creek and asked whether it could be included. Caiping saw that the map might waken concern among neighbors who had grown tired of loud accusations. She asked the boy's guardian for permission and promised to label his drawing as a child's observation, not a scientific survey.

回到編輯室，老印刷機開始發出不穩定的節拍。世明說滾筒得清潔，不該在機器不可靠時硬拚增印一期。采蘋同意。延後會讓等待結果的讀者失望，但損壞的機器可能傷人，也可能毀掉下週的報紙。她當晚寫了短公告，說明新資料預計何時到達、哪些問題仍待解答。隔天，一名男孩帶來手繪溪流地圖，問能否刊登。采蘋發現，這張地圖可能喚起那些厭倦激烈指責的鄰居重新關心溪流。她徵求監護人同意，並答應清楚標示那是孩子的觀察，而非科學調查。
