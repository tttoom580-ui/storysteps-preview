The Lunch Survey

1994，榕灣週報的最後一頁 · 第 3 / 25 章 · 3000–4000 · 英中對照

用互動版查詞、朗讀與收藏

The first trial issue sold ninety-four copies, enough to pay the ink bill but not the wages. Caiping refused to call that success. On the same day, a school secretary arrived with an assignment: investigate why children on the island were missing lunch. A newspaper in the city had blamed the ferry schedule, but the secretary believed the problem was more complicated. Caiping and Xiaoyu took the early ferry with their notebooks and a camera for photography. A lifeguard checked the dock after rain, then told them to proceed only when the steps were dry. The island school did not have a luxurious dining hall. It had four wooden tables, a kitchen, and a notice about the maximum number of meals that could be prepared safely.

第一期試辦報紙賣出九十四份，足以支付油墨費，卻還付不起工資。采蘋不肯稱那是成功。同一天，學校祕書交給他們一項採訪任務：調查外島學生為何經常沒吃午餐。省城報紙把原因歸咎於渡輪時刻，但祕書相信事情沒那麼簡單。采蘋與小雨帶著筆記本和攝影用的相機搭上早班渡輪。雨後，一名救生員檢查碼頭，提醒他們等階梯乾燥再通行。島上學校沒有豪華餐廳，只有四張木桌、一間廚房，以及寫明可安全供餐最大份數的告示。

At first the principal asked them to concentrate on the percentage of late deliveries. Xiaoyu had counted eleven missed meals in one month, an incredible figure for a school with only fifty-two pupils. But the cook explained the context. Some families supplied their own food because of allergies; some children arrived late after helping with housework; and the boat's delays mattered only on two particular days. A single percentage could hide very different reasons. Caiping asked the cook whether she could defend her account with records. The woman produced a handwritten list of ingredients, dates, and people who had received refunds. The editor made no promise to print every number. She first needed to know who had been counted, by whom, and for what purpose.

一開始，校長要求她們把重點放在延遲送餐的百分比。小雨算出一個月有十一份午餐未送達；全校只有五十二名學生，這數字令人難以置信。但廚師解釋了背景：有些家庭因過敏自備餐點，有些學生幫忙做家事而遲到，渡輪延誤其實只影響其中兩天。單一百分比會掩蓋完全不同的原因。采蘋問廚師能否提出紀錄支持說法。她拿出手寫清單，列著食材、日期與已退款的人。編輯沒有承諾印出全部數字；她得先知道被計算的是誰、誰計算的、又為了什麼目的。

A visiting nutrition student brought labels showing the calorie content of sample lunches. She placed a raisin bun beside a bowl of rice to explain why energy alone did not describe a balanced meal. The children asked lively questions, while two teachers spoke quietly about their own working pace. A good lunch plan needed the kitchen staff to rest, they said; more speed was not always more care. The principal had called this an intermediate problem between transport and education. Caiping thought that description might help readers: the answer could not belong entirely to either department. She asked students what they wished adults would notice. One boy said he wanted time to eat without being told to hurry. That simple sentence became the heart of the article.

一名來訪的營養系學生帶來標示熱量的試餐標籤，把葡萄乾麵包放在白飯旁，解釋光有卡路里數字不等於均衡飲食。孩子們踴躍發問，兩名老師則悄悄談起員工的工作步調。好的午餐安排也應讓廚房人員休息，並非越快就越有照顧。校長稱這是介於運輸和教育之間的中介問題。采蘋認為這種說法對讀者有幫助：答案不可能完全歸單一部門處理。她問學生最希望大人注意什麼。一個男孩說，他想有足夠時間吃飯，不要一直被催。這句簡單的話成了報導核心。

Xiaoyu returned with an angry note from a parent who said the school had delivered an insult by calling home-packed food inadequate. The principal denied using that word. Caiping found that the original phrase came from a volunteer's rough draft, which had been copied without explanation into a notice. She asked the volunteer to describe what she meant. The woman had wanted to encourage children to receive fresh fruit when it was available, not to shame their parents. That difference did not erase the harm, but it suggested an accurate correction. Caiping asked both sides for permission to quote them and offered each the chance to check names and dates. She would not let a misunderstanding turn into a quarrel merely because angry words might sell more papers.

小雨收到一張憤怒的家長字條，批評學校用「不夠好」羞辱自帶便當的家庭。校長否認說過這話。采蘋查到，原句來自志工的粗略草稿，後來沒加說明就被抄進公告。她請志工解釋原意。志工本想鼓勵孩子在有供應時吃新鮮水果，並非羞辱家長。這個差別不會讓傷害消失，但提供了可準確更正的方向。采蘋徵求雙方同意引用，也讓每個人核對名字和日期。她不願因為憤怒言語比較能賣報，就把誤解擴大成爭吵。

On the return trip the ferry slowed as the sun sank behind the hills. Xiaoyu said she had expected a dramatic villain and instead found a collection of small decisions. Caiping replied that a clear priority could still emerge: protect children's health, publish true numbers, and give families a way to correct mistakes. Her own inspiration came from the students, not from a clever headline. She opened the notebook to a page labeled "What we know" and another labeled "What we cannot prove." The result would not tell the school how to cook every meal. It would explain why a fixed schedule, a safe kitchen, and respectful language had to work together. When they reached the harbor, Caiping felt tired but ready to write an article that might actually help someone.

回程渡輪在夕陽下放慢速度。小雨說，自己原以為會找到一個戲劇性的壞人，沒想到只有許多零散的決定。采蘋回答，依然可以找出明確的優先順序：維護孩子健康、公布真實數字，並讓家庭有更正錯誤的方式。她的靈感來自學生，而非機智的標題。她打開筆記本，一頁標著「已知」，另一頁標著「無法證明」。這篇報導不會教學校如何煮每一頓飯，而是說明固定時程、安全廚房與尊重人的用語必須一起運作。船抵達港口時，采蘋雖然疲憊，卻準備好寫下一篇真正可能幫得上忙的文章。
