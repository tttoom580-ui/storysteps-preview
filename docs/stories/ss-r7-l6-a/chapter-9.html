The Ledger with Two Dates

最後一幕，不准有人犧牲 · 第 9 / 25 章 · 5000–6000 · 英中對照 · 校訂中

用互動版查詞、朗讀與收藏

The printing apprentice returned with a second set of payment receipts. Their numbers matched the theatrical accounts, but the dates belonged to the month before the safety renovation was authorized. Someone had reserved funds before the work existed. Liora did not assume that a premature payment proved fraud; advance orders were common. Instead she asked which goods had been purchased and who had signed for delivery. The ledger contained a sequence of small charges made to a silent partnership called the Laurel House. Liora did not want to alienate tenants by publishing their private addresses. A dark blot of ink hid the original date on one repair invoice. The café served chili soup while Anet organized the meeting. The ledger’s continuity could be checked across successive monthly pages.

印刷學徒帶回第二組付款收據。編號與劇院帳目相符，日期卻落在安全翻修獲准的前一個月。有人在工程尚不存在時就預留資金。莉奧拉沒有認定提前付款就代表詐欺；預先訂貨並不少見。她改問購買了哪些商品，以及由誰簽收。帳簿記載一連串小額費用，都支付給一間名叫桂冠屋的隱名合夥組織。 莉奧拉不想因公開私人住址而疏遠承租戶。 一塊深色墨漬遮住一張維修發票的原始日期。 安妮特召集會議時，咖啡館供應辣椒湯。 帳簿的連續性可由逐月頁面交叉檢驗。

Anet added the charges in front of the tenants. The cumulative total passed the approved repair amount before she reached the final page. Jun pointed out two work entries that belonged to costume staff rather than the publicity firm. She was not dispensable to the inquiry because her own name was absent from the invoices; she could explain whose work they described. Liora asked for the ownership filings before drawing a decisive conclusion. Through the window, jasmine beside the meeting room hid the waiting guards from view. Anet shut the curtain and gave each tenant a separate page on which to identify only the charges they knew.

安妮特當租戶面加總費用，還沒到最後一頁，累計已超過核准修理額。阿筠指出兩筆屬於戲服人員的工作，不是宣傳公司。發票沒她姓名，不代表調查能省略她，因為她能說明費用描述誰的工作。莉奧拉要求先查所有權登記，再下決定性結論。窗外茉莉遮住等候衛兵。安妮特拉簾，給每位租戶分開的一頁，只標自己知道的費用。

Anet organized a public meeting with the market tenants, many of whom worked for festival subcontractors. They reported the same pattern: a business signed an agreement, a different business issued the invoice, and a third collected the payment. None of the tenants knew who controlled Laurel House. Liora requested the registry’s ownership filings and promised not to publish private home addresses. She understood that evidence gathered without care could harm the very people she wanted to protect. A caretaker let the visitors lodge their records in a lockable cupboard. A maple cabinet held older contracts from the theater’s first renovation. The former mistress of Laurel House had kept detailed household accounts. Anet worked to mobilize the tenants without telling them how to vote.

安妮特召集市場承租戶開會，其中許多人替節慶分包商工作。他們回報相同模式：一家商號簽約、另一家開發票、第三家收款。沒人知道誰掌控桂冠屋。莉奧拉要求查看公司登記的所有權資料，並承諾不公開私人住址。她明白，不慎蒐集證據也可能傷害她原本想保護的人。 一名看守讓訪客把紀錄存放在可上鎖的櫃子。 楓木櫃收藏著劇院第一次翻修時的舊合約。 桂冠屋的前女主人曾保存詳盡家用帳冊。 安妮特努力動員承租戶，但不指示他們如何投票。

The apprentice's persistence persuaded the registry clerk to bring the earlier filing book. A date there preceded the repair authorization, just as the receipts did. Liora began to muse on who might have noticed the overlap sooner, then stopped and copied the entry instead. A socialist delegate asked how citizens could scrutinize the public payments. Anet showed him the account page while keeping home addresses covered. The tenants chose to socialize after the formal meeting, away from palace guards. Liora stayed at the table with the clerk long enough to mark which ownership documents were still missing. The new dates narrowed the inquiry; they did not identify a rope cutter.

學徒鍥而不捨，終於讓登記書記拿出較早的登記簿。裡頭一個日期也像收據般早於修理核准。莉奧拉開始思索，誰本可更早注意到重疊，隨即停下，改抄條目。一名社會主義代表問市民如何監督公款，安妮特讓他看帳頁，仍蓋住住址。租戶選擇正式會議後遠離衛兵交談。莉奧拉陪書記留在桌邊，標出還缺哪些所有權文件。新日期縮小調查範圍，並未找出割繩者。

The ownership register revealed that Laurel House leased warehouses but owned no theater equipment. Its director was Corvin’s stepbrother, a fact he had not disclosed at the first hearing. This did not prove the director had cut a rope; it did establish that a private beneficiary had been absent from the public discussion. Liora submitted the finding with a request for Corvin to step aside from approving future repair invoices. His answer arrived within an hour: she would be removed as supervisor if she continued. The disputed invoices span three accounting periods rather than a single week. Two tenants had made the long trek from the northern wards to attend. Their vigorous questions forced the officials to explain each transfer. All copies went into a waterproof case before the rain began.

所有權登記顯示桂冠屋租賃倉庫，卻沒有劇院設備。董事是科爾文的繼兄弟，這層關係在第一次聽證中未揭露。這不代表董事切斷繩索，卻證明公共討論漏掉一名可能私下獲利的人。莉奧拉提交結果，要求科爾文迴避日後維修發票的審核。他一小時內就答覆：她若繼續追查，就將失去監督職位。 有爭議的發票橫跨三個會計期間，而非一週。 兩名承租戶從北區長途跋涉前來出席。 他們積極有力的提問迫使官員解釋每筆轉帳。 下雨前所有副本都放進防水箱。
