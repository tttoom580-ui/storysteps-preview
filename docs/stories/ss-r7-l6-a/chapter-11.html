牆裡的哨聲

The Whistle in the Wall

最後一幕，不准有人犧牲 · 第 11 / 25 章 · 5000–6000 · 英中對照 · 校訂中

A sound technician traced the strange whistle to an old signal pipe built into the theater wall. The pipe had been designed to warn fire crews, but its handle was hidden behind a newly installed panel. No visitor could have reached it from the audience seats. Liora ordered a reconstruction with the technician, the caretaker, and a neutral observer. When they pulled the handle from the service side, the exact sound from the preview returned. Someone familiar with the building had caused the interruption, although the motive remained unclear. The instrument recorded twenty-one degrees Celsius inside the service passage. Repeated friction had darkened the edge of the hidden signal handle. A guard began to growl when the technician requested another demonstration. Liora refused to induce Brin to accuse someone she had not seen.

音響技術員追查奇怪哨音，發現那是藏在劇院牆內的一條舊訊號管。它原本用來警告消防工班，操作把手卻被新裝的面板遮住。觀眾席上的訪客根本不可能碰到。莉奧拉請技術員、看守與中立觀察員重演。當他們從服務通道拉下把手，預演時的哨聲原樣重現。有人熟悉建築內部才會引發中斷，但動機仍不清楚。 儀器記錄服務走廊內攝氏二十一度。 反覆摩擦讓隱藏訊號把手的邊緣變黑。 技術員要求再示範一次時，衛兵不滿地低吼。 莉奧拉拒絕誘導布琳指控她沒有看見的人。

The technician asked the caretaker to leave the dust beneath the new panel untouched. A small mound showed where the edge had scraped the wall. Insects might inhabit the unused shaft, he explained, but they could not make the recorded tone. He pulled the handle with the observer watching and let the exact whistle answer him. It was ironic to hear the old warning device again after so much fear. Outside, a peacock called from the palace courtyard. Jun asked the technician to repeat the pipe sound alone so there would be no doubt which noise belonged on their recording.

技術員請保管人別碰新面板下的灰塵，小土堆顯出邊緣刮牆的位置。他說昆蟲也許住在閒置井道，卻不能發出錄音裡的音調。見證人看著他拉把手，完全相同的哨音響起。經過這麼多驚恐，再聽見舊警告裝置，格外諷刺。外頭王宮庭院孔雀叫了。阿筠請技術員單獨重複管音，免得日後分不清錄音裡哪個聲音屬於它。

A kitchen worker named Brin confessed that she had heard the whistle once before, during a private demonstration for Corvin’s staff. She had not mentioned it because her husband worked for the same contractor. Liora promised that Brin’s testimony would not be treated as proof that her husband had done anything wrong. Instead, they compared demonstration dates with work orders and the service gate log. An entry showed the signal panel had been installed without being added to the public safety plan. The rehearsal pianist recognized the whistle from an earlier private demonstration. The inspector used a narrow probe to examine the pipe without breaking it. The respective roles of the technician and caretaker were written down clearly. Corvin’s shortsighted demand for secrecy had made the alarm less useful.

廚房工人布琳坦承，她曾在科爾文員工的私人示範裡聽過同一種哨音。她先前沒說，因為丈夫也替同一家承包商工作。莉奧拉承諾，不會把布琳的證詞當成她丈夫犯罪的證據。他們改比對示範日期、施工單與服務門紀錄。其中一筆記載顯示訊號面板已被安裝，卻從未加進公開安全計畫。 排練鋼琴師認出那是先前私人示範聽到的哨音。 檢查員用細探針檢查管道，避免破壞。 技術員與看守各自的職責都清楚記錄。 科爾文短視地要求保密，讓警報更難發揮作用。

Jun helped simplify the pipe diagram for Brin, who knew the kitchen route but not the engineers' room names. Brin placed her finger beside the service door where she had heard the earlier demonstration. The nearby strait carried the theater horn's sound on clear nights, but this pipe ended inside the building. Nilo followed its line behind the panel's superficial paint and noted the newer hinges. Liora asked the clerk to preserve Brin's marked copy. She would not suppress it because the demonstration involved palace staff. The contractor could answer with an authorization, but an unsigned page could not do that work for him.

阿筠幫忙簡化管線圖，讓熟悉廚房路線、不熟工程房名的布琳看。布琳指著勤務門，先前示範時她就在那裡聽見聲音。附近海峽晴夜可傳劇院號角，這根管卻止於建築內。尼洛沿面板表層油漆後的管線追查，記下較新鉸鏈。莉奧拉請書記保存布琳標記的圖，不會因涉及王宮人員示範就壓下。承包商可拿授權回應，但未簽頁面不能替他做到。

The panel could still have been installed as a misguided emergency improvement, so Liora asked for the instruction that authorized it. The contractor produced a page with a blank signature field. Corvin wanted to treat the page as sufficient proof of permission. Liora rejected the shortcut and sent the question to the city inspector. Meanwhile the company made the old pipe visible on the safety map so no worker would mistake its sound for a stage effect again. The pipes lacked the symmetry shown on the official plan. A brass token identified the key issued for the signal cabinet. The union agreed to unify the warning signals across every rehearsal room. A sense of urgency guided the repair, but no one skipped the signed checks.

那面板仍可能只是用錯方法的應急改善，因此莉奧拉要求查閱核准安裝的指示。承包商交出一張簽名欄空白的文件。科爾文想把它當成已獲許可的證明，莉奧拉拒絕這個捷徑，把問題交給市府檢查員。同時劇團把舊訊號管標在安全圖上，讓工人以後不會再把哨音當成舞台效果。 管道排列缺少官方圖面所示的對稱性。 黃銅代幣標明訊號櫃鑰匙的領用編號。 工會同意統一各排練室的警示訊號。 修復很緊急，卻沒有人略過簽署查核。
