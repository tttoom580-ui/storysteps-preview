高處走廊

The Upper Gallery

最後一幕，不准有人犧牲 · 第 2 / 25 章 · 5000–6000 · 英中對照

The inspection began above the auditorium, where two narrow catwalks crossed beneath the roof. Nilo showed Liora a pulley that had been polished on one side and roughly filed on the other. A single photograph could not reveal when the damage occurred. She appointed Jun and a visiting carpenter as independent observers. The carpenter had traveled from the mountain provinces to build the festival scenery. He knew the materials, but he refused to make an accusation before the bolts had been counted. Liora found his restraint more useful than Corvin’s confident speeches. An aboriginal woodcarver explained why his community seasoned timber for years. The mountain workshops abound with skilled craftspeople, not cheap substitutes. The two catwalk marks did not align with the official building plan. At that altitude, even a small wind changed how suspended fabric moved.

檢查從觀眾席上方開始，兩條狹窄的工作走道在屋頂下交叉。尼洛讓莉奧拉看一個滑輪，一側磨得發亮，另一側卻有粗糙的銼痕。單憑一張照片無法判定損傷發生的時間。她指派阿筠與來自外地的木匠擔任獨立見證。木匠從山區省分前來搭建節慶布景，熟悉材料，卻拒絕在螺栓清點前指控任何人。莉奧拉覺得，這份克制遠比科爾文充滿自信的演說有用。 一名原住民族木雕師解釋族人為何要多年陰乾木材。 山區工坊有許多熟練工匠，不是廉價替代品。 兩條走道標記與官方建築圖並未對齊。 在那個高度，即使微風也會改變懸掛布料的動向。

Jun held the carpenter's lamp as he approached the narrow crossing. The superintendent laughed at the delay, and she turned away to hide her annoyance. Evi answered him with a blunt refusal to rehearse below them. Nilo asked for a second guardrail before anyone leaned farther over the gap. A worker offered to discard the cracked pulley to make room for the replacement. Nilo stopped his hand and laid a numbered cloth beneath the part instead. The carpenter could now count the bolts without losing fragments through the open floor. Jun wrote the part number beside the suspended rehearsal notice.

阿筠提著木匠的燈，陪他靠近狹窄交會通道。總監嘲笑拖延，她轉開臉壓下不悅。艾薇直率拒絕在他們下方排練。尼洛要求先加第二道護欄，才准人探身過空隙。工人想丟棄破裂滑輪，騰地方放替換品，尼洛攔住他的手，改在零件下鋪編號布。木匠於是能數螺栓，不致把碎片掉進開放地板。阿筠把零件編號寫到暫停排練公告旁。

The theater’s superintendent offered an easy solution: dismiss the youngest stagehand and call the damage carelessness. Liora asked for the maintenance schedule instead. Three employees had reported a door left open at midnight, but the official log listed no entry. One witness had seen a shadow; another had heard a cart. Neither could identify the visitor. Liora ordered a physical demonstration of the route from the loading dock to the upper gallery and required all participants to wear their own numbered badges. A discharge notice showed that a former rigger had left under pressure. Liora promised to endeavor to contact him without exposing his address. The first hurdle was obtaining an unbiased measurement of the roof beams. The carpenter adjusted his posture before leaning over the dangerous rail.

劇院總監提供簡單辦法：解雇年紀最小的舞台工，並將損傷歸咎於疏忽。莉奧拉卻要求查閱維修時程。三名員工曾回報午夜有扇門敞開，官方紀錄卻沒有任何進出。有人看見影子，另一人聽見推車聲，沒有人能辨識訪客。莉奧拉下令實地重走從卸貨碼頭到高處走廊的路線，並規定參與者必須佩戴個人編號徽章。 一份解職通知顯示前任吊掛工曾在壓力下離職。 莉奧拉承諾努力聯絡他，同時不暴露住址。 第一個障礙是取得屋梁的公正測量結果。 木匠俯身越過危險欄杆前，先調整姿勢。

From the upper terrace, Nilo could follow the rope's full travel. Jun called out each position while the carpenter marked each bolt with precision. Their measurements put one support beyond the point shown on the official plan. The workers requested a rigorous test of that section before another load was hung. Downstairs, a newspaper seller called their vote an uprising. Liora carried the measurement sheet past him without answering. She needed the missing maintenance entry, so she asked the caretaker to show the loading route once more, this time with the numbered badges visible at every door.

從上層平台，尼洛能看見繩索移動的完整路徑。阿筠喊出各位置，木匠精確標出每顆螺栓。測量顯示，一處支架超出官方圖面的位置。工人要求再掛重物前，嚴格測試該部分。樓下報販把投票稱作起義，莉奧拉拿著測量單經過，沒有回答。她需要缺失的維護紀錄，於是請保管人再示範卸貨路線，這次每道門都讓編號徽章清楚可見。

On the stairs, Evi confronted her. A supervisor who had once threatened to replace dancers could not expect gratitude merely for preventing one accident. Liora admitted that Evi had no reason to trust her yet. She offered the company a vote on whether rehearsals should remain suspended until the report was finished. The dancers voted to wait. Corvin called the decision an insult to authority, but for the first time the people who stood beneath the scenery had changed the schedule themselves. A steward tried to usher Evi away before she could answer. Nilo was a versatile engineer who also knew the old theater’s history. Corvin threatened to veto the inspection budget, though he lacked that authority. A widower who had repaired the roof offered his original measurements.

在樓梯間，艾薇當面質問她。曾以換掉舞者威脅別人的監督，不能只因阻止一次事故就期待感激。莉奧拉承認艾薇沒有理由立刻信任她。她提議讓劇團投票，決定是否暫停排練到檢查結束。舞者投票選擇等待。科爾文稱這決定侮辱權威，但這是第一次由站在布景下的人親自改變時程。 一名管事試圖在艾薇回答前把她引走。 尼洛是多才多藝的工程師，也熟悉舊劇院歷史。 科爾文威脅否決檢查預算，但他沒有那項權限。 一名曾維修屋頂的鰥夫提供原始測量紀錄。
