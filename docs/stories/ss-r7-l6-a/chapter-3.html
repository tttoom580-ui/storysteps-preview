布料與煙

Cloth and Smoke

最後一幕，不准有人犧牲 · 第 3 / 25 章 · 5000–6000 · 英中對照

用互動版查詞、朗讀與收藏

Jun took Liora through the costume workshops, which occupied a former hospital wing. Every seamstress knew the smell of hot iron, but yesterday’s smoke had come from a closed storage room, not the pressing tables. A charred strip of decorative fabric had been placed near a firework fuse. Jun had saved the strip because its weave differed from the cloth issued to the theater. Liora requested an examination from the textile guild and forbade anyone to announce arson before the guild had inspected both fibers. An advisory notice asked families to avoid the workshop during tests. Corvin described the festival as a conquest over the city’s gloomy past. Liora refused to allege a conspiracy before the fiber report arrived. The cook sliced cucumber for the children waiting outside the studio.

阿筠帶莉奧拉穿過戲服工坊；這裡原是醫院的一翼。每名縫紉師都熟悉熱熨斗的味道，但昨天的煙從上鎖儲藏間飄出，不是燙衣桌。有人把一條燒焦的裝飾布放在煙火引線附近。阿筠保存了布條，因為它的織法與劇院發放的布料不同。莉奧拉請紡織公會鑑定纖維，並禁止任何人在檢驗完成前公開宣稱有人縱火。 諮詢公告請家庭在測試期間避開工坊。 科爾文把節慶描述為對城市陰鬱過往的征服。 纖維報告出來前，莉奧拉拒絕指控有人陰謀串通。 廚師為等在工坊外的孩子切小黃瓜。

Jun found a feeble apprentice sitting beside the pressing tables and sent for medical help before asking him any questions. Behind the storage door, a line of black fume marked the ceiling. The inspector photographed the disconnected firecracker beneath the burnt cloth, then lifted its fuse with a clean tool. Grease stained the wrapping. Jun set an unopened workshop packet beside it for comparison; its wrapping was dry. Liora asked for both packets to be retained. The difference might help the guild trace the material, but it did not let her name whoever had carried it into the room.

阿筠看見虛弱學徒坐在熨燙桌旁，先叫醫護，沒急著問話。倉門後，黑色濃煙在天花板留下線痕。檢查員拍下焦布下斷開的鞭炮，再以乾淨工具提起引線。包裝有油脂。阿筠拿未開封工坊包裹作比較，那份包裝是乾的。莉奧拉要求兩份都保留。差異也許能幫公會追查材料，卻還不能讓她指認誰把東西帶進來。

Children from a nearby care home had been invited to sing at the coronation. Their teacher, Mara, said the organizer had moved the group beside the smoke exit without consulting her. Liora changed the route and asked the children what they understood about an emergency signal. One boy admitted that he had thought the red warning lantern was part of the play. The discovery shook Liora more than the scorched cloth. A safety rule no one could recognize was simply another decorative object. Opening the vents would lessen the smell without concealing the damaged cloth. Jun discovered a theater program liner containing the wrong safety instructions. The orphanage director demanded a separate evacuation practice for the children. One parent feared that smoke would spread like a plague through the old corridors.

附近安置機構的孩子受邀在加冕典禮歌唱。他們的老師瑪拉說，主辦人未經商量就把孩子安排在排煙出口旁。莉奧拉更改路線，並詢問孩子是否理解緊急訊號。一個男孩承認，他以為紅色警示燈只是戲劇的一部分。這項發現比燒焦布條更讓莉奧拉不安。無人認得的安全規則，根本只是另一件裝飾品。 打開通風口能減輕氣味，又不會掩蓋受損布料。 阿筠發現一張節目內頁印著錯誤安全指示。 育幼院院長要求為孩子安排獨立疏散練習。 一位家長擔心煙會像瘟疫般迅速蔓延舊走廊。

Mara began with recreational games so the youngest singers would listen before the evacuation practice. Jun replaced technical slang on the route card with simple directions, then asked a child to lead her to the marked door. He stopped at a shrub that narrowed the rear path. Liora had it cleared before the next group tried. A newspaper lay on the rehearsal bench, its savage portrait of her beside the guild's unanswered inquiry. She put the route card over it and watched the second child find the exit. This time he recognized the warning lantern and reached the door without prompting.

瑪拉先玩休閒遊戲，讓最小歌手在疏散練習前願意聽。阿筠把路線卡技術俚語換成簡單指示，請孩子帶她到標示門口。他被縮窄後路的灌木擋住，莉奧拉先清除，再讓下一組試。排練椅上有報紙，猛烈醜化她的肖像旁，是公會尚待回答的調查。她把路線卡放在報紙上，觀看第二名孩子找出口。這次他認出警示燈，沒人提示便到門口。

That evening a newspaper praised Corvin as the savior of public entertainment and described Liora as a vicious enemy of joy. The paper printed a portrait from the novel’s original illustration. She saw how the story had trained strangers to hate her before she could speak. Rather than demand a flattering portrait, she sent the newspaper the route-change notice and the guild’s receipt. Neither document proved who planted the fuse. Both proved that someone had begun reducing the danger. The new drill needed to surpass the previous one in clarity, not spectacle. A blue ribbon would symbolize an exit, never a fictional enemy. The textile guild promised an independent report on the scorched weave. The clinic confirmed that no child required a vaccine because of this smoke incident.

當晚報紙稱讚科爾文是公共娛樂的救星，卻說莉奧拉是快樂的惡毒敵人。報紙印出小說原版插圖中的肖像。她看見故事如何在她開口前，就訓練陌生人仇視她。她沒有要求改刊好看的照片，而是寄去動線更改通知與公會收據。這兩份文件都不能證明誰放置引線，卻能證明已有人的行動確實降低危險。 新演練必須在清晰度超越舊版，而非追求場面。 藍色緞帶象徵出口，絕不再被當成虛構敵人。 紡織公會承諾對燒焦的織紋提出獨立報告。 診所確認這次煙霧事件沒有孩子需要因此接種疫苗。
