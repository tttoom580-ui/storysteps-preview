谷地上方的磨坊

The Mill Above the Valley

最後一幕，不准有人犧牲 · 第 7 / 25 章 · 5000–6000 · 英中對照 · 校訂中

用互動版查詞、朗讀與收藏

The disputed pulleys came from a mountain mill whose owner had retired after a landslide. Liora traveled there with Nilo and the union inspector, avoiding a ceremonial escort that would frighten witnesses. The mill’s old ledger showed a small order for ordinary theater rope, but the palace invoice charged for reinforced cable. Its weight and price were different. The owner’s daughter had questioned the mismatch and was told to leave it alone. She handed over the ledger only after Liora agreed that the original would remain in her family’s custody. The mill family owned an acre of trees used to season replacement timber. The retired miller had been a bachelor when he began his craft, years before raising his daughter. A boxing club nearby offered its hall for a private witness interview. The contractor threatened disciplinary action against the daughter for refusing to sign.

有爭議的滑輪來自山區磨坊，老闆在一場土石流後退休。莉奧拉與尼洛及工會檢查員前往，刻意不用會嚇壞證人的儀仗隊。磨坊舊帳簿記載的是一小批普通劇場繩索，王宮發票卻以強化鋼索計費，重量和價格都不同。老闆的女兒曾質疑差異，卻被叫她別管。莉奧拉同意正本仍由家族保管後，她才交出帳簿供查驗。 磨坊家族有一英畝樹林，用來培養替換木料。 退休磨坊主是單身漢，把數十年奉獻給工藝。 附近的拳擊俱樂部提供場地進行私下證人訪談。 承包商威脅因女兒拒簽而採取紀律處分。

An emigrant worker met the visitors in the boxing hall and recalled the cart's weight from his last shift. Nilo asked him to describe how it handled on the slope before showing him either invoice. His account matched the lighter load in the mill ledger. When Corvin's representative entered, the daughter's voice began to falter. Liora asked the representative to wait outside until she had finished her account. She would not equate a disputed bill with sabotage. A farmer waiting to discuss soil fertility moved his meeting to the other room, leaving the daughter space to read her dates without an audience.

一名移居海外的工人在拳擊會館會見訪客，回憶最後一班時推車的重量。尼洛先請他描述斜坡上的車況，才給他看兩份發票。他的說法符合磨坊帳上的較輕負載。科爾文代表進來時，女兒聲音開始不穩。莉奧拉請代表在外面等，讓她先說完；爭議帳單不能等同蓄意破壞。等候討論土壤肥力的農夫把會議移到別室，留空間讓她不必當眾讀日期。

While Nilo compared the hardware, the daughter described pressure from the festival contractor to sign a new delivery date. She refused, because she remembered the day of a local funeral and knew the cart had not arrived. Liora asked her to explain the memory without turning it into an exact clock time. The three visitors could verify the ledger with shipping weights and toll receipts. By afternoon they had established that the royal invoice described materials the mill had never supplied. Nilo promised not to forsake the witness if the contractor retaliated. The miller spoke of a gay, carefree festival from his youth. A long gloom settled over the workshop when the altered bill was shown. The toll receipt came from the intersection where the valley road divided.

尼洛比較零件時，女兒說節慶承包商曾施壓，要她簽署新的交貨日期。她拒絕了，因為記得那天村裡辦喪事，貨車根本沒來。莉奧拉請她說明記憶，卻不要求說出無法確定的時刻。三名訪客可利用運輸重量和道路收費收據交叉核對帳簿。到了下午，他們證明王宮發票上所列的材料，磨坊根本沒有供應。 尼洛承諾承包商若報復，也不會拋棄證人。 磨坊主說起年輕時一場歡樂無憂的節慶。 修改過的帳單亮出後，工坊陷入長久陰鬱。 收費收據來自谷地道路分岔的交叉口。

The daughter unfolded the toll receipts along the ledger's edge. The earlier landslide had closed the southern road for nearly three weeks, including the new delivery date she had been asked to sign. Nilo traced the open northern route and compared its recorded weight with the invoice. The miller's orthodox habit of keeping every receipt let them follow the shipment without guessing. He offered to nourish the visitors with bread while certified copies were prepared. Corvin's representative claimed the family could not submit private books. Liora asked him to identify a prohibition in the panel's rules. He found none, and the daughter kept the original open for certification.

女兒沿帳簿邊攤開過路收據。早先山崩使南路封閉近三週，包含別人要求她簽的新送貨日。尼洛追出仍開放的北路，把登記重量與發票比對。磨坊主人保留每張收據的傳統習慣，讓他們不用猜就能追貨。他在準備認證副本時拿麵包款待訪客。科爾文代表說家人不能提交私人帳簿，莉奧拉請他指出委員會規則的禁令。他找不到，女兒便繼續攤開原件辦理認證。

The discovery did not identify who had altered the invoice. A careless accountant and a dishonest contractor could leave the same difference on paper. Liora paid for certified copies and arranged for the mill to speak at the next hearing, with travel costs covered regardless of testimony. On the return journey, Nilo admitted that he had once dismissed the mill as old-fashioned. He now understood that the family’s refusal to replace its ledger had protected everyone onstage. A provisional receipt recorded the transfer of copies, not the original ledger. The three visitors were unanimous about what the weight records established. Liora set aside her vanity and thanked the daughter for correcting her assumptions. A woodpecker tapped on the beam outside as the final signatures were made.

這項發現還不能指出是誰改了發票。粗心會計和不誠實承包商，都可能在紙上留下同樣差異。莉奧拉付費取得認證副本，安排磨坊代表出席下次聽證，不論證詞內容都支付交通費。回程路上，尼洛承認自己曾嫌這座磨坊落伍，如今才明白，家人堅持不更換舊帳簿的決定保護了舞台上的所有人。 臨時收據記錄副本交接，正本沒有移動。 三名訪客對重量紀錄所能證明的事情意見一致。 莉奧拉放下虛榮，感謝女兒糾正自己的假設。 最後簽名時，一隻啄木鳥在窗外木樑上啄擊。
