地板下的聲音

A Sound Beneath the Floor

最後一幕，不准有人犧牲 · 第 4 / 25 章 · 5000–6000 · 英中對照

用互動版查詞、朗讀與收藏

The orchestra had refused to rehearse because a low vibration reached the musicians whenever the main platform moved. Conductor Hale invited Liora beneath the stage, where an abandoned drainage channel ran past the old theater foundations. Water was seeping through a cracked wall. A brass marker showed that the platform’s lower support had once been part of a different building. Nilo suspected the recent renovation had concealed a hollow space, but he needed measurements to distinguish that possibility from ordinary settling. The crew used bleach only in an isolated cleaning area, never near the channel. Nilo requested a comprehensive map of every support beneath the orchestra pit. The landlady was a credible witness because she had preserved dated letters. Hale thanked the musicians for their dedication to keeping one another safe.

樂團拒絕排練，因為主舞台每次移動，樂手都會感到低沉的震動。指揮海爾邀莉奧拉走進舞台下方；一條廢棄排水道經過劇院舊地基，水從裂牆滲入。黃銅標記顯示舞台下部支撐原屬另一棟建築。尼洛懷疑最近的翻修掩蓋了一處中空空間，但他需要測量，才能將這個可能性與一般地基沉降區分。 工班只在隔離清潔區使用漂白水，不讓它接近水道。 尼洛要求一張涵蓋樂池下方所有支撐的完整地圖。 房東太太保存了註明日期的信，因此是可信的證人。 海爾感謝樂手致力彼此的安全。

The landlady led Jun behind the rental rooms, where a wooden dresser stood across the old access hatch. Jun asked for help moving it without dragging it over the weakened boards. The superintendent said the musicians' familiarity with the passage made a new inspection unnecessary. Liora asked him to open the hatch and show the engineer the water mark. He could not see it from the doorway. She invited the caretaker into the engineering discussion despite the usual hierarchy. The landlady put her damp envelope on the table, and Jun unfolded it under a dry cloth so the ink would not rub away.

房東太太帶阿筠到出租房後方，木製梳妝台擋著舊檢修口。阿筠請人幫忙搬，不在脆弱木板上拖行。總監說樂師熟悉通道，不必再檢查。莉奧拉請他開檢修口，讓工程師看水痕；從門邊根本看不見。她不依原有階級限制，邀保管人加入工程討論。房東太太把潮濕信封放在桌上，阿筠隔著乾布展開，避免墨跡磨掉。

A landlady who rented rooms to musicians remembered hearing the same noise during last spring’s storm. She had written to the former superintendent, who had replied that the sound was harmless. Liora located the letter and offered to preserve it without asking the woman to speculate about causes. The landlady cared less about court politics than about the tenants who walked home through the damp passage. She asked for a temporary barrier and a truthful public notice. Liora supplied both before returning to the technical questions. Outside, a lush courtyard hid the damaged drainage outlet from tourists. The orchestra gathered to mourn a former musician injured during renovation years ago. A patient mentor could nurture younger workers without demanding their silence. The engineer warned that overflow would reach the orchestra pit during heavy rain.

一名出租房間給樂手的房東太太記得，去年春季暴風雨也出現相同聲響。她曾寫信給前任劇院總監，得到的回覆卻說那聲音無害。莉奧拉找出信件，答應保存，同時不要求房東猜測原因。房東關心的不是宮廷政治，而是每天穿過潮濕通道回家的房客。她要求臨時圍欄及真實公告；莉奧拉先完成這兩項，再繼續討論技術問題。 外頭茂密的庭院把受損排水口藏在遊客視線外。 樂團聚在一起悼念多年前翻修時受傷的前團員。 有耐心的導師能培育年輕工人，不必要求他們噤聲。 工程師警告，大雨時溢流會進入樂池。

A violinist stopped Hale outside the pit. She had managed to overhear an instruction to block the drainage test, but could not identify the speaker. Liora recorded where she had stood and asked the contractor for the original order, giving him a chance to refute or confirm the account. The superintendent required a receipt before releasing the building keys. Jun wrote one with the room and return time. Outside, a seagull perched beside the flood gauge while Nilo checked the water level. The team could inspect the channel now; the disputed instruction would remain a separate question for the work-order review.

一名小提琴手在樂池外攔住海爾。她無意間聽見阻止排水測試的指示，卻認不出說話者。莉奧拉記下她站的位置，要求承包商出示原命令，讓他能反駁或確認。總監要求交鑰匙前先拿收據，阿筠寫明房間與歸還時間。外頭海鷗停在洪水刻度旁，尼洛核對水位。團隊如今可以檢查渠道，爭議指示則留給施工單審查，另外處理。

When the measurement team lowered a lantern into the channel, they discovered a new wooden brace fixed across an old drainage opening. Its nails bore the festival contractor’s mark. The brace could have been an emergency repair, or a shortcut that diverted water toward the foundations. Liora refused to call it either without the contractor’s work order. She had once mocked people who demanded paperwork. Now a missing work order might determine whether an entire audience could safely sit above the channel. Hale stopped the symphony at the first abnormal vibration. A thermometer showed the water below remained unusually cold after noon. The rumor caused turmoil among musicians whose rent was already overdue. The clinic prepared wholesome meals for workers on extended inspection duty.

測量隊把提燈放進水道時，發現一道新木撐架橫擋在舊排水口上，釘子刻有節慶承包商的標記。那可能是緊急修繕，也可能是把水導向地基的偷工捷徑。莉奧拉拒絕在拿到施工單前判定是哪一種。她過去曾嘲笑堅持留文件的人，如今一張缺失的施工單，卻可能決定整座觀眾席是否安全。 海爾在第一次出現異常震動時停止交響樂演奏。 溫度計顯示中午過後，下方積水仍異常冰冷。 謠言在房租早已逾期的樂手之間引發動盪。 診所為長時間值勤的檢查工人準備健康營養的餐點。
