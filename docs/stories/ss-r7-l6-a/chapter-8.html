寫好必死的角色

A Role Written to Die

最後一幕，不准有人犧牲 · 第 8 / 25 章 · 5000–6000 · 英中對照 · 校訂中

The cast discovered that the new final scene required Evi to stand beneath the moving chandelier while the audience sang. The director insisted the position was symbolic and therefore safe. Evi pointed out that symbols did not change gravity. She and several dancers petitioned for a different ending, not a smaller part. Liora promised them a rehearsal under stationary scenery where they could test the dramatic effect without the dangerous machinery. The director protested that only the original ending could move the royal audience. The accumulation of petitions made the director realize the cast would not retreat. Jun offered a warm beverage before the exhausting open rehearsal. A blonde dancer volunteered to demonstrate the new opening movement. The structural engineer refused to certify moving scenery without a load test.

演員發現新終幕要求艾薇站在移動的吊燈下，聽全場觀眾歌唱。導演堅稱那個位置具有象徵意義，所以是安全的。艾薇指出，象徵不會改變重力。她和數名舞者連署要求更換結局，不是縮小自己的角色。莉奧拉承諾讓他們在固定布景下排練，測試戲劇效果而不動用危險機械。導演抗議，說只有原版結局能感動王室觀眾。 連署書逐漸累積，讓導演明白演員不會退縮。 阿筠在疲憊的公開排練前提供熱飲。 一名金髮舞者自願示範新的開場動作。 結構工程師拒絕在未測試荷重前認證移動布景。

Evi asked the director to walk the route to every emergency exit before the stationary rehearsal. The company wanted written clearance, not his assurance from the stage. A clerk threatened to deduct their wages for signing the petition. Jun wrote down the threat and asked which rule authorized it. The clerk lowered his paper. Evi then moved the youngest dancers away from the lamp's marked position. Nobody was destined to die there, she said, whatever the script required. Jun shortened the repeated movements so the long practice would not exert needless pressure on injured knees. The director watched the cast prepare without touching the machinery.

靜態排練前，艾薇請導演實走每條緊急出口路線。劇團要的是書面通行許可，不是他站台上的保證。書記威脅因連署扣薪，阿筠記下威脅，問哪條規則准許。書記放低紙張。艾薇把最小舞者移離燈具標示位置，說無論劇本怎麼寫，沒人注定死在那裡。阿筠減少重複動作，避免長時間練習對受傷膝蓋施加不必要壓力。導演看著演員不碰機械，自行準備。

In an open rehearsal, Evi stepped out of the sacrificial circle before the music stopped. The other dancers followed her, leaving the lamp alone at center stage. Silence fell, then the younger audience members began to applaud. Jun proposed that the circle represent a choice no one had the right to impose. Liora recognized that the revision challenged the very story which had trapped her. It also gave the performers a reason to participate in protecting the new production. One critic called Evi heroic, but she asked for ordinary protections instead. A retired lieutenant offered to organize the audience without armed guards. The revised program was written plainly enough for a literate young reader. A painted lotus replaced the image of the sacrificial flame.

公開排練時，艾薇在音樂停止前走出象徵犧牲的圓圈。其他舞者跟上，只留下吊燈獨自在舞台中央。全場先是一片寂靜，接著年輕觀眾開始鼓掌。阿筠提議，圓圈象徵任何人都無權強迫別人承擔的選擇。莉奧拉明白這項修改正在挑戰把自己困住的故事，也讓表演者有理由參與保護新的製作。 一名評論家稱艾薇英勇，她卻只要求基本保護。 一名退役中尉提出不靠武裝衛兵維持觀眾秩序。 修改後的節目單文字清楚，識字的年輕讀者都能理解。 彩繪蓮花取代象徵犧牲火焰的圖像。

The director called the dancers' objection marginal and opened a rehearsal calendar to prove they had no time. Jun marked where the groups' schedules overlap: only the final scene required everyone together. A provincial troupe had offered another hall for the earlier sections, freeing that hour for the revised ending. Evi tried the first movement there, then invited another actor to take her place. His qualification would be his skill, she told the director, not whether he obeyed an unsafe instruction. They could disagree about the scene's meaning while using the same stationary marks on the floor. Jun penciled the new room beside each group's call time.

導演說舞者異議無關主線，打開排練表，想證明沒時間。阿筠標出各組時段重疊處，只有終幕需要全員。外省劇團願借另一廳給前段排練，於是能空出這一小時改結尾。艾薇在那裡試第一個動作，再邀另一演員接替。她告訴導演，資格在技巧，不在是否服從不安全指示。他們可以對場景意義有不同意見，同時使用地上同樣固定的標記。阿筠在各組集合時間旁寫下新房間。

After the rehearsal, a palace courier delivered notice that the revised choreography lacked the royal artistic seal. Corvin wanted the company to confuse artistic approval with permission to ignore safety rules. Liora separated the two: the cast would seek artistic review while maintaining its refusal to use the unsafe device. She told Evi that even a successful petition might lead to her dismissal. Evi answered that she would rather lose a role than teach the youngest dancers that obedience was the price of living. The theater sold refreshments in the lobby while the rehearsal continued. Jun could retrieve the original costume sketches from the locked cabinet. A shallow swamp beyond the outer gate would be closed during the festival. Evi said dancing was a vocation, not a promise to abandon her own life.

排練後，一名王宮信差送來通知，稱新編舞沒有獲得王室藝術印章。科爾文想讓劇團把藝術核准與忽視安全規則混為一談。莉奧拉把兩者分開：演員會申請藝術審查，同時維持拒絕使用危險設備的決定。她告訴艾薇，連署成功仍可能導致被解雇。艾薇回答，她寧可失去角色，也不願教最年輕的舞者把服從當成活命的代價。 排練期間，劇院在大廳販售點心飲料。 阿筠能從上鎖櫃子取回原始戲服草圖。 外門外的淺沼澤在節慶期間將封閉。 艾薇說舞蹈是志業，不是放棄生命的承諾。
