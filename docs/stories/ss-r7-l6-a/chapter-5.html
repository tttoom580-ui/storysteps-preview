十日暫緩

The Ten-Day Stay

最後一幕，不准有人犧牲 · 第 5 / 25 章 · 5000–6000 · 英中對照

用互動版查詞、朗讀與收藏

A public safety panel met in the academy hall, where the festival contractor presented a polished drawing of a flawless stage. Liora arrived with damaged rope, fiber samples, drainage measurements, and statements from performers. She told the panel that none of these alone proved a coordinated attack. Together they proved the existing safety certificate was incomplete. Corvin argued that a delay would ruin the royal banquet and deprive merchants of business. Anet answered that merchants were not helped by a collapse in front of their customers. The academy offered a neutral hall for the public review hearing. An accusation appeared in the newspaper before the inspection report was finished. The royal banquet could continue indoors without activating the suspended platform. A merchant complained that spoiled fertilizer was blocking his market delivery.

公共安全小組在學院大廳開會，節慶承包商展示一張光鮮亮麗、毫無瑕疵的舞台圖。莉奧拉帶來損傷繩索、纖維樣本、排水測量，以及表演者陳述。她告訴小組，單一資料都無法證明串通破壞，但合在一起足以證明原有安全認證並不完整。科爾文辯稱延期會毀掉王室宴會，也讓商人失去生意。安妮特回答，若觀眾眼前發生坍塌，商人同樣得不到好處。 學院提供中立大廳舉行公開審查聽證。 檢查報告尚未完成，報紙就刊出指控。 王室宴會可以在室內繼續，無須啟動停用的平台。 一名商人抱怨變質肥料阻擋市場貨車通行。

Anet placed the support plank on two stands before the panel. Beneath its fresh paint, the fracture ran farther than Corvin's drawing showed. Nilo demonstrated the harness on an empty frame rather than send a worker up to satisfy the audience. The sound team supplied headphones so panel members could hear the recorded warning clearly. A merchant from the fishery asked how the shutdown would affect the loading dock. Anet marked a separate access route on the closure plan. The same plan could protect people beneath the platform and keep ordinary deliveries moving, provided neither route crossed the sealed test area.

安妮特把支撐木板放在兩座架上。新漆下的裂痕比科爾文圖中更長。尼洛在空架示範安全吊帶，沒有為滿足觀眾就派工人上去。音響團隊提供耳機，讓委員清楚聽見警告錄音。漁場商人問封閉如何影響卸貨碼頭，安妮特在封場圖另標通行路。只要兩條路都不穿過封存測試區，同一計畫可以保護平台下的人，也讓一般送貨繼續。

The panel granted a ten-day suspension of the dangerous machinery, not a permanent closure of the theater. The decision allowed work on costumes, music, and nonstructural scenery to continue. Liora accepted the limits and announced that the rehearsal calendar would be published each morning. The union would choose two inspectors, while a separate engineer would test the disputed loads. Jun smiled at the decision, then reminded her that unpaid wages remained unpaid. A safer theater was not yet a fair one. The clinic recorded the apprentice’s smoke intake without exaggerating his injury. The maiden performance of the new ballet was postponed rather than canceled. An offshore shipment contained replacement parts that had not passed local tests. Each prospective contractor received the same written inspection requirements.

小組裁定危險機械停用十日，而非永久關閉劇院。戲服、音樂與非承重布景仍可繼續。莉奧拉接受限制，宣布每天早晨公開排練日程。工會將選出兩名檢查員，另由獨立工程師測試有爭議的荷重。阿筠對裁定露出笑容，隨即提醒她，積欠的薪水依然沒有支付。更安全的劇院，還不代表更公平。 診所如實記錄學徒吸入煙霧的情形，不誇大傷勢。 新芭蕾舞劇的首次演出改期，而不是取消。 一批離岸運來的替換零件尚未通過本地測試。 每名有意承攬的廠商都收到相同書面檢查規定。

Outside the hall, Anet gathered the performers around a copy of the ten-day order. One man confused it with the proposed referendum on arts funding, so she pointed to the machinery number and read the limitation aloud. Nilo could refine the brake only after independent review; music and sewing could continue in the marked rooms. Several dancers began to rejoice, then Jun asked about wages for the canceled shifts. Liora wrote that question beneath the schedule rather than promise an answer she could not fund. She could understand why workers might resent her concern while their own rent remained unpaid.

廳外，安妮特讓表演者圍看十日命令。一人把它與藝術經費公投提案混淆，她指機械編號，唸出限制。尼洛須待獨立審查後才能改良煞車，音樂與裁縫可在標示房間繼續。幾名舞者歡喜起來，阿筠卻問取消班次的工資。莉奧拉把問題寫在行程下面，沒有承諾無法籌款的答案。她明白工人房租尚未付清，可能對她的關心感到怨恨。

On the palace steps, a clerk handed Liora a sealed invitation to a private meeting with Corvin. The envelope suggested that her family titles could be restored if she withdrew the complaint. She remembered how the original Liora had chosen status over everyone beneath her. This time she sent the offer to the panel as a possible conflict of interest and asked for the next hearing to be public. She knew that ten days would pass quickly. She also knew that the story’s fatal ending no longer seemed inevitable. The previously noisy hall grew serene as the panel read the final conditions. A local minister gave a sermon about sacrifice, but the dancers did not applaud. The stagehands had to shuffle their schedules to maintain the safe work zones. A tenant of the market asked that access rules be posted in plain language.

在王宮台階上，一名書記交給莉奧拉封緘的私下會面邀請。信暗示只要她撤回申訴，就能恢復家族頭銜。她記得原著中的莉奧拉曾選擇地位，而不是腳下所有人的安全。這一次，她把邀請轉交小組，作為可能的利益衝突證據，並要求下次聽證公開進行。她知道十天轉眼即逝，也知道故事裡那場致命結局不再像以前一樣不可避免。 小組宣讀最終條件時，原本吵鬧的大廳平靜下來。 一位地方牧師布道談論犧牲，舞者卻沒有鼓掌。 舞台工人不得不調整工作時程，維持安全工作區。 一位市場承租戶要求用淺白文字公告通行規則。
