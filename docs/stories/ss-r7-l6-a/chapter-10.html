沒有吊燈的預演

The Preview Without a Lamp

最後一幕，不准有人犧牲 · 第 10 / 25 章 · 5000–6000 · 英中對照 · 校訂中

The temporary permit expired at midnight, and the city allowed one preview using a stationary set. Corvin arrived with inspectors of his own and promised an audience of ministers. Liora asked that the independent engineer’s conditions be read aloud before the doors opened. The company moved the dangerous chandelier into a locked workshop and replaced it with a painted shadow. Critics called the substitute plain, but Evi said that people had come to watch living dancers, not to witness the weight of a machine. The new permit was a limited concession, not full approval of the machinery. A cynical critic said safety rules would bore every royal guest. The contractor had failed by default to submit the required maintenance plan. Emergency supplies would deplete quickly if spectators blocked the exit.

臨時許可在午夜到期，市府准許用固定布景舉行一次預演。科爾文帶著自己的人員前來，承諾大臣會到場。莉奧拉要求開門前公開宣讀獨立工程師的條件。劇團把危險吊燈移進上鎖工坊，改用彩繪陰影代替。評論家嫌替代品平淡，艾薇卻說觀眾是來看活生生的舞者，不是來見證機器的重量。 新許可是有限度讓步，不是對機械全面核准。 一名憤世嫉俗的評論家說安全規則會讓王室賓客無聊。 承包商因未提交必要維修計畫而構成程序違約。 若觀眾堵住出口，緊急物資會很快耗盡。

Before the preview, the fragrance of fresh paint drifted from the lobby into the witness room. Liora asked guards not to intrude there while the inspector spoke to workers. Outside, a visiting jockey described how a sudden whistle could startle a horse or a person expecting music. Jun checked that the company knew the agreed stop signal before admitting the royal guests. The director read the stationary-set condition in its literal sense and ordered the old device left in the workshop. Evi tested her path around the painted shadow. Nothing on that route required a worker to step beneath a hanging lamp.

試演前，新漆香味從大廳飄進證人室。檢查員與工人交談時，莉奧拉請衛兵不要闖入。外面一名來訪騎師說，突來哨聲會驚動馬，也會嚇到本來期待音樂的人。阿筠先確認劇團認得約定停演訊號，才讓王室來賓入場。導演按字面遵守靜態布景條件，命舊裝置留在工坊。艾薇測試繞過彩繪陰影的路線，整條路都不必走到懸燈下。

Halfway through the performance, an unfamiliar whistle sounded from the service corridor. The stagehands stopped the scene under their agreed procedure. No one ran, and the emergency exits remained clear. Investigators found a visitor’s badge beside a cabinet that held the old pulley. It had no assigned name. Liora preserved the badge but refused the palace officer’s suggestion that it proved the union had staged the interruption. The company restarted only after the service corridor was cleared. The workers’ morale improved when their refusal was respected. A palace official threatened to prosecute whoever had stopped the music. Nilo confirmed that no radioactive substance was present in the old stage paint. Evi did not retort angrily; she pointed to the agreed emergency rules.

演到一半，服務走廊傳出陌生哨音。舞台工依先前議定的程序停止演出。沒有人奔跑，緊急出口也保持暢通。調查人員在存放舊滑輪的櫃子旁找到一張訪客證，上面沒有使用者姓名。莉奧拉保存證件，卻拒絕王宮官員的說法——證件不能證明工會自導自演。服務走廊清場後，劇團才重新開始。 工人拒絕危險工作獲尊重後，士氣有所提升。 王宮官員威脅起訴讓音樂停止的人。 尼洛確認舊舞台油漆沒有放射性物質。 艾薇沒有憤怒頂嘴，而是指向事先同意的應變規則。

After the whistle stopped the scene, Nilo found a screwdriver beneath the cabinet near the nameless badge. He asked the inspector to photograph both before either was lifted. In the lobby, the café gave a serving of soup to each worker waiting through the corridor search. Evi stayed by the stationary backdrop, whose painted splendor had replaced the glass. When the inspector cleared the route, she returned to her opening mark. The first applause was spontaneous; no usher had prompted it. Liora wrote the interruption time on the permit copy and asked for the restart time to be recorded beside it.

哨聲使場景停止後，尼洛在無名徽章旁的櫃下找到螺絲起子，請檢查員先拍下兩件位置，再提起。大廳咖啡攤給等候走廊搜索的每名工人一份湯。艾薇留在靜態背幕旁，彩繪華麗取代了玻璃。檢查員確認路線無礙，她回到起始標記。第一陣掌聲自發響起，沒有接待員帶頭。莉奧拉在許可副本寫下中斷時間，請人把重開時間記在旁邊。

The preview ended to uncertain applause. Some spectators admired the revised ending; others complained that a coronation should not include doubt. Liora counted the result differently. Not one performer had stood beneath untested equipment, and the interruption had been handled without injury. The anonymous badge would require investigation, but it had not been allowed to become an instant conviction. The original novel promised a glamorous tragedy. Her new version offered a smaller achievement: everyone went home alive. Evi’s stepfather watched from the back row and respected her choice. A nervous singer began to stutter when questioned by armed guards. The city agreed to subsidize public tickets only if safety terms were met. The team used synthetic cloth for the shadow instead of heavy glass ornaments.

預演在猶豫的掌聲中結束。有些觀眾喜歡修改後的結局，另一些抱怨加冕典禮不該留下疑惑。莉奧拉用另一種方式計算成果：沒有人站在未測試設備下，意外中止也沒有造成傷害。無名訪客證仍須追查，卻沒有立即成為定罪的藉口。原著承諾華麗的悲劇，而她的新版本只取得一項較小的成就：所有人都活著回家。 艾薇的繼父坐在後排觀賞，尊重她的選擇。 一名緊張的歌手遭武裝衛兵盤問時開始結巴。 市府同意只有符合安全條件才補助公共票券。 團隊以合成纖維布製作陰影，不再使用沉重玻璃裝飾。
