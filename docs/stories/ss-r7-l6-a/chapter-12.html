劇作家的頁邊

The Playwright’s Margin

最後一幕，不准有人犧牲 · 第 12 / 25 章 · 5000–6000 · 英中對照 · 校訂中

用互動版查詞、朗讀與收藏

The playwright’s widow invited Liora to inspect the original script after hearing the revised finale. She lived near the inner canal and guarded her husband’s papers fiercely. In the margins of his first draft, he had written that the heroine’s survival should depend on choosing solidarity over spectacle. Later versions reversed that ending and made the young dancer’s death appear noble. The widow had correspondence showing that the changes came from Corvin’s office, although the letters did not explain why the patron wanted them. The widow lived beside a brook that fed the old city canal. A coral ornament decorated the cover of the first manuscript. Liora would not deprive the widow of custody over her husband’s original pages. The older ending could elevate the dancers rather than glorify their deaths.

劇作家的遺孀聽說改寫終幕後，邀莉奧拉查看原稿。她住在內運河旁，極力保護丈夫留下的紙張。第一稿頁邊寫著：女主角能否活下來，取決於她是否選擇團結而非場面。後來版本顛倒結局，把年輕舞者的死亡寫成高尚行為。遺孀保留了往來信件，顯示修改要求來自科爾文辦公室，但沒有說明贊助人為何如此執著。 遺孀住在注入舊城運河的小溪旁。 第一份手稿封面有珊瑚裝飾。 莉奧拉不會剝奪遺孀保管丈夫手稿正本的權利。 舊結局能提升舞者的地位，而不是歌頌他們死亡。

The widow held an old letter close, then passed it back to Liora. Its expiration date concerned permission for one production, not the destruction of the first draft. Because her eyesight was fading, Jun enlarged the disputed margin before asking her to read it. The widow corrected one copied word and served a hearty lunch while they waited for the licensing papers. Near the canal, a paper lantern seemed to hover in the mist. Liora looked from its harmless light to the manuscript's later stage direction and asked when the heavy moving lamp had first entered the play.

遺孀把舊信拿近眼前，再交回莉奧拉。到期日限制的是一次演出授權，不是銷毀初稿。她視力衰退，阿筠先放大爭議頁邊，才請她讀。遺孀改正一個抄錯的字，等授權文件時招待豐盛午餐。運河旁紙燈籠像浮在霧裡。莉奧拉看看無害燈光，再看手稿後期舞台指示，問沉重活動燈具究竟何時加入戲中。

Nilo compared the first script with the technical plan. The fatal scene had originally required no moving weight at all. Its emotional center was an empty space where the dancers refused the command to sacrifice a member. The mechanical chandelier appeared only in a later artistic revision, at the same time the stage renovation budget expanded. Liora did not treat this as evidence of a murder plot. She treated it as a meaningful overlap between financial and creative decisions, one that required a clear explanation. The playwright had traveled inland to interview dancers before writing the first act. Evi admired the widow’s insistence that the cast approve any new use. The older license resembled a lease of rights, not a sale of the pages. A legislator had once praised the playwright for defending public theaters.

尼洛把初稿和技術圖面比較。原本的致命場景根本沒有任何移動重物，戲劇核心是一塊空地：舞者集體拒絕犧牲同伴的命令。機械吊燈直到後來的藝術修訂才出現，時間恰好與舞台翻修預算擴大重疊。莉奧拉沒有把這視為謀殺陰謀的證據，而是當成財務和創作決策可疑的重合點，必須獲得清楚解釋。 劇作家寫第一幕前曾到內陸訪談舞者。 艾薇欣賞遺孀堅持新使用方式須經演員同意。 舊授權像是權利租約，而非出售手稿。 一名立法者曾稱讚劇作家維護公共劇院。

Under the monarch's portrait, the widow set the first scenery drawing beside the revised song. It showed a majestic stairway with no moving glass above it. Evi read the final refrain quietly, then invited the others to join. The song gathered momentum as each voice entered without a signal from Liora. The playwright's offspring confirmed that they had no objection to the limited educational license, but the widow still asked the performers for their own answer. Jun wrote down their proposed changes on a separate sheet. The fragile original remained on its stand while the living cast began making a working script.

君主肖像下，遺孀把最初布景圖放在修改歌曲旁。圖上有雄偉階梯，上方卻無活動玻璃。艾薇輕讀最後疊句，邀其他人加入。每個聲音自行加入，歌曲漸有力量，沒有等莉奧拉發訊號。劇作家子女確認不反對有限教學授權，遺孀仍要問表演者自己的答案。阿筠把修改建議另記一張。脆弱原件留在架上，活著的演員開始製作工作劇本。

The widow asked that the original script not become another weapon against the performers. She offered to license the abandoned ending to the company, provided the cast approved its use. Evi and the dancers read it together and chose not to copy it exactly. They wanted to add their own words about work, fear, and refusal. Liora felt a strange relief. Although she had fallen into someone else’s novel, its people were no longer waiting for a single author to tell them what to do. A lamp fueled by petrol lit the narrow reading room when dusk arrived. Nilo helped rotate the fragile manuscript stand without touching its pages. Jun said a director must never strangle the cast’s chance to disagree. The widow recovered from surgical treatment before granting access to the archive.

遺孀要求原稿不要再成為攻擊演員的武器。她願將被棄用結局授權給劇團，但前提是演員同意使用。艾薇和舞者一起閱讀後，決定不逐字照搬。他們想加上自己對工作、恐懼與拒絕的話。莉奧拉感到奇異的輕鬆。即使她跌進別人的小說，裡面的人也不再等待唯一的作者告訴他們該怎麼做。 黃昏時，一盞煤油燈照亮狹窄閱覽室。 尼洛協助轉動脆弱的手稿架，沒有碰觸紙張。 阿筠說導演絕不能扼殺演員表示不同意見的機會。 遺孀在手術治療後康復，才同意開放檔案。
