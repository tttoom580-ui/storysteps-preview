海報工坊

The Poster Workshop

最後一幕，不准有人犧牲 · 第 6 / 25 章 · 5000–6000 · 英中對照 · 校訂中

A week into the suspension, posters appeared claiming that Liora intended to abolish the coronation. Their type matched the royal theater’s own print shop, although the shop had supposedly been closed. An apprentice printer showed Jun a discarded proof with a different message: the company had been ordered to present a new finale in which the villainess accepted responsibility for every failure. Liora recognized the scene from the novel, but the printed order was more useful than her memory. It carried the number of a live production account. The anonymous letter dared to allege that Liora wanted to ban the ceremony outright. Jun offered to beautify the old posters without changing their factual warnings. The poster caption falsely described the safety vote as a criminal rebellion. The playwright insisted that the original climax required a falling chandelier.

停用令進入第一週，街上出現海報，宣稱莉奧拉打算廢除加冕慶典。字型與王家劇院印刷坊相同，但坊間據稱早已關閉。一名印刷學徒給阿筠看遭丟棄的校樣，上面卻是另一段內容：劇團被要求演出新的終幕，讓惡女承擔所有失敗。莉奧拉認出小說裡的場景，但印刷命令比她的記憶更有用，因為上面有現行製作帳戶的編號。 匿名信指稱莉奧拉想全面禁止典禮。 阿筠提出美化舊海報，但不改動事實警告。 海報標題錯誤地把安全投票描述成犯罪叛亂。 劇作家堅稱原版高潮必須有吊燈墜落。

The pressman tried to sound jolly as he welcomed them, but his hand shook on the ledger clasp. His family remained hospitable, bringing stools into the cramped shop after hearing of the agent's threats. Jun asked where the discarded proof had lain before anyone moved it. The apprentice pointed to a tray shared with orders from an extracurricular drama club. Liora asked permission to extract the production account numbers, leaving the unrelated school orders covered. Jun wrote each copied number beside its page and asked the apprentice to check it. He corrected one digit that changed the account from costumes to publicity.

老印刷工努力開朗地招呼，手卻在帳簿扣上發抖。家人聽聞代理人威脅，仍熱情搬椅進狹小工坊。阿筠先問廢校樣原本在哪，學徒指向放有課外戲劇社訂單的紙盤。莉奧拉徵求同意擷取製作帳號，無關的學校訂單仍遮住。阿筠逐筆寫出抄錄號碼與頁碼，請學徒核對。他改正一個數字，帳戶類別便從戲服變成宣傳。

She asked the young printer to keep his own copy and to bring a guild representative to their next interview. Corvin’s agent arrived to demand the proofs, insisting the materials belonged to the palace. Liora answered that ownership did not erase a worker’s right to describe an instruction he had received. The agent left, but his departure was not victory. The print shop’s wages were paid through a subcontractor with the same address as the company repairing the damaged platform. A worker had to mow weeds behind the workshop to reveal its loading door. Corvin’s agent wore a theatrical false mustache that fooled no stagehand. Jun refused to nag frightened apprentices into making statements before they were ready. The break room felt like an oasis of calm amid the angry street protests.

她請年輕印刷工保留副本，下次接受訪談時帶公會代表同行。科爾文的代理人前來索取校樣，堅稱資料屬於王宮。莉奧拉回答，所有權不能抹除工人說明自己所接指示的權利。代理人離開了，但這不算勝利。印刷坊的工資由分包商支付，而那家公司的地址竟與修理受損平台的企業相同。 一名工人得先割去工坊後方雜草，才能露出卸貨門。 科爾文的代理人戴著假八字鬍，卻騙不了舞台工。 阿筠拒絕嘮叨催逼受驚學徒立即作證。 休息室在街頭激烈抗議中，像一片平靜綠洲。

A member of parliament arrived with the publicity contract, asking who had authorized its charge. The printer weighed an ounce of ink from the disputed batch and laid its supply ticket beside the proof. It connected that print run to a second shop on the outskirts, registered under another name. The apprentice took out a note warning him to surrender every copy. Its ruthless language had frightened him more than the agent's costume. Liora gave him a receipt for the copied note and asked the guild representative to arrange the next interview. The family kept the original ledger locked beneath the pressman's desk.

一名國會議員帶宣傳合約來，問誰核准費用。印刷工秤出爭議批次的一盎司油墨，把供應單放在校樣旁，將這批印件連到郊區另一間不同登記名的工坊。學徒拿出警告他交出所有副本的字條。殘酷措辭比代理人戲服更令他害怕。莉奧拉替字條副本開收據，請公會代表安排下次訪談。家人把原帳簿鎖在老印刷工桌下。

At dinner, Evi asked whether Liora intended to expose the entire production to protect herself. Liora replied that she wanted the company to continue, provided no one was required to risk a life in silence. The answer was harder to demonstrate than to say. She ordered a transparent ledger of the printing and repair contracts, then invited the workers to identify entries that did not match their hours. The first dispute concerned not treason but an apprentice who had never been paid for two nights of work. A sculptor who made stage figures recognized the printer’s distinctive paper. Liora preferred the simplicity of a signed receipt to a grand denial. The two payment requests had been filed on simultaneous dates at separate offices. The unpaid apprentice feared that another complaint would cause the shop’s business to slump.

晚餐時，艾薇問莉奧拉是否為了自保打算揭發整個製作計畫。莉奧拉回答，她希望劇團繼續，只是不該讓任何人在沉默中冒生命危險。說起來容易，證明卻難。她下令公開印刷與維修合約的帳目，請工人指出與工時不符的紀錄。第一項爭議不是叛國大罪，而是一名學徒兩個夜班的工錢從未收到。 製作舞台雕像的雕塑家認出印刷工使用的特殊紙張。 莉奧拉寧可相信簡單的簽收單，也不依賴誇大的否認。 兩份付款申請在相同日期送進不同辦公室。 未領薪的學徒擔心再次申訴會使商店生意下滑。
