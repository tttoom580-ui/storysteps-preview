市場的長坡道

The Market's Long Ramp

這次，我把自己的名字留在門上 · 第 7 / 25 章 · 5000–6000 · 英中對照 · 校訂完成

The market association asked Second Signal to redesign its evacuation route. The old sign was bulky, mounted far above eye level, and difficult to understand. Inspectors had deemed the passage acceptable years ago, but the standard had changed. Nora watched carts glide across the stone while wheelchair users struggled around a raised pebble border. “A route that only some people can navigate is not a common route,” Soren said. Nora agreed, then invited the merchants to show where deliveries could be moved without causing overwork. The first solution was not an elegant machine. It was a better path, marked by temporary tape and tested with people who actually used it.

市場協會請「第二訊號」重新設計疏散路線。舊指示牌又大又笨重，掛得太高，也難理解。檢查員多年前認定通道合格，標準卻已改變。諾拉看著推車滑過石板，輪椅使用者卻得繞開凸起的卵石邊。索倫說：「只有部分人能通行的路線，不算公共路線。」諾拉同意，並請攤商指出可以移動送貨區、又不會讓人過勞的位置。第一個解法不是優雅機器，而是一條更好的路：用暫時膠帶標記，再交由真正使用的人測試。

An interpreter attended the consultation so visitors who used sign language could take part. One speaker, a lesbian shopkeeper named Farah, explained that her partner had injured a knee during a rushed evacuation. She was not asking for a personal favor; she wanted a route that worked for strangers as well. Another merchant complained that public consultation had become a dictatorship of specialists. Farah replied that liberation from dangerous design would help everyone, not just a favored group. Nora made room for the merchant's practical objections while refusing to treat Farah's identity as a topic for debate. Their common question was whether the proposed ramp worked.

一位手語口譯員參與諮詢，讓使用手語的訪客也能發言。一位名叫法拉的女同志店主說，她的伴侶曾在倉促撤離時傷到膝蓋。她不是要求私人特權，而是希望連陌生人也能使用的路線。另一名商人抱怨，公共諮詢變成專家的獨裁。法拉回答，從危險設計中解放出來會幫助所有人，不只是特定群體。諾拉願意聽取商人的實際顧慮，卻拒絕把法拉的性傾向當作辯論題目。共同問題只有一個：坡道是否真的可用。

At the far end of the market, a handicraft stall displayed mats made on a small plantation outside town. Its owner had once used a plow on the family land before learning to weave. He suggested a lofty stone arch for the new entrance, but its cost would swallow the entire budget. Nora proposed movable rails and visible markings instead. A designer asked who would receive royalty payments if a pattern appeared on the signs. She suggested a written license with a clear fee, rather than treating the artist's labor as a trifle. The merchant looked surprised. In her former company, Nora remembered, attractive designs had often been taken without a fair conversation.

市場另一端的手工藝攤販展示城外小型農園製作的地墊。攤主過去在家田裡用犁耕作，後來才學編織。他提議為新入口建一座高聳石拱，但費用會吞掉整筆預算。諾拉改提活動欄杆與清晰標線。一位設計師問，如果指示牌使用他的圖案，誰能收到授權權利金。她建議簽訂清楚寫明費用的授權書，而不是把藝術家的勞動當成小事。攤主有些驚訝。諾拉記得，從前公司常未經公平協商就拿走漂亮設計。

The final trial took place on a warm afternoon. A volunteer with a tan from years of outdoor work pushed a test cart along the route, while an older resident took a slow stroll beside her. The raised border still caused trouble. Instead of blaming the users, the team moved the markers and measured again. A pending permit meant the permanent ramp could not be built immediately, but they could document the need and keep the temporary route clear. The residents' comments spurred the team to revise the drawings that evening. Nora began to see accessibility not as a decorative feature but as a continuous, shared responsibility.

最後一次試走在暖和的下午進行。一位多年戶外工作、皮膚曬黑的志工推著測試推車走路線，年長居民則在旁慢慢散步。凸起邊緣仍造成問題。團隊沒有責怪使用者，而是移動標記重新測量。永久坡道的許可尚未核准，無法立刻施工，但大家可以記錄需求並保持臨時通道暢通。居民意見促使團隊當晚修改圖紙。諾拉開始理解，無障礙不是裝飾性功能，而是一項持續、共同承擔的責任。

Farah suggested that a single awkward step should spur the designers to inspect the entire route, not merely patch the most visible problem. The next morning the team borrowed a measuring wheel and documented every doorway, slope, and resting place from the market to the clinic. They published the map in a draft form, with a note inviting corrections. When a resident showed them a blocked side exit, Nora did not insist that the original plan was close enough. They updated the drawing and asked the municipality who could legally remove the obstruction. Shared information was already making the neighborhood safer, even before a new device arrived.

法拉說，一處難走的階梯應促使設計師檢查整條路線，而不是只補上最顯眼的問題。隔天團隊借測距輪，記錄市場到診所沿路每扇門、坡度與休息點。他們把地圖以草案形式公開，邀請修正。有居民指出側門被堵住時，諾拉沒有堅稱原方案已經差不多。大家更新圖紙，向市府確認誰有法定權限移除障礙。即使新裝置還沒到，資訊共享已經開始改善社區安全。

At lunch a shopkeeper worried that public changes would drive away customers. Soren asked him to describe his busiest delivery hours, and the team adjusted the proposal to keep the loading area open. Farah agreed to test that version but reserved the right to reject it if access remained poor. Nora appreciated that nobody needed to surrender a legitimate concern in order to cooperate. The meeting was slower than a private order, but its result belonged to more people. She sent the revised design to the municipal planner and recorded that it was still provisional.

午餐時，店主擔心公共改造會趕走客人。索倫請他說明最忙的送貨時間，團隊便調整方案，保留卸貨區。法拉願意測試新版，但保留若通行仍不佳便拒絕的權利。諾拉欣賞合作不需要誰放棄合理顧慮。會議比私人命令慢，結果卻屬於更多人。她把修訂設計寄給市府規畫人員，並註明仍是暫定方案。
