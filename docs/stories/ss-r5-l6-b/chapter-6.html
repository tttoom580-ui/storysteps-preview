The Grant That Asked for Silence

這次，我把自己的名字留在門上 · 第 6 / 25 章 · 5000–6000 · 英中對照 · 校訂中

The city's innovation office offered a grant that sounded airtight until Mina read the last page. The money might help Second Signal attain its testing goals, but the agreement allowed a sponsor to approve public statements. A congresswoman touring the district praised the proposal, then admitted she had seen only the summary. Nora respected her candor. At a small restaurant they shared curry and edible herbs while Mina explained the hidden condition. Nora did not despise the sponsor, but she refused a clause that could eclipse safety warnings when they became inconvenient. The congresswoman suggested a public application process instead, where decisions and reasons would remain visible.

市府創新辦公室提出一項補助，條款初看天衣無縫，直到米娜讀完最後一頁。那筆錢可能幫助「第二訊號」達到測試目標，協議卻允許贊助者審核公開發言。一位巡視社區的女國會議員稱讚提案，隨後坦言自己只看過摘要。諾拉尊重她的坦白。幾人在小餐廳吃咖哩與可食用香草，米娜說明隱藏條件。諾拉並不鄙視贊助者，卻拒絕任何可能因安全警告不合時宜便將其掩蓋的條款。議員建議改走公開申請程序，讓決定與理由都看得見。

To save money, Edda repaired a display cabinet from an old aquarium. Its painted fin had broken off, and a flake of blue paint floated into the cleaning water. She found a safe way to remove the decoration without producing harmful vapor. In a corner stood boxes of unsold merchandise that the former print-shop owner had abandoned. Nora wanted to throw everything away as rubbish, but the team identified paper, shelves, and tools they could reuse. The routine work risked monotony, yet it offered a kind of esteem that glamorous launches never had. People finished a task and knew precisely what they had improved.

為了省錢，艾達修理了一座舊水族館的展示櫃。上面彩繪的魚鰭斷了，清洗水裡浮著一片藍漆。她找到安全移除裝飾的方法，避免產生有害蒸氣。角落堆著前印刷店主人留下的滯銷商品。諾拉原想全部當垃圾丟掉，團隊卻找出能再利用的紙張、層架與工具。例行工作容易單調，卻帶來那些華麗開幕式沒有的尊嚴感。每個人完成一項工作，都清楚知道自己改善了什麼。

Soren invited Nora to walk through an evergreen park after work. He spoke about his favorite pastime, drawing birds that roam along the river, and showed her a sketchbook with an amusingly sly fox on the cover. Nora told him that Julian had once claimed her quiet evenings were wasted opportunities. Soren said nobody was required to turn rest into a business advantage. He did not try to slay all her doubts with a clever speech. Instead he asked whether walking together had become uncomfortable. “Not tonight,” she answered. The simple permission to name her own comfort felt more valuable than an extravagant promise.

索倫邀諾拉下班後穿過常青樹公園。他談到自己的消遣，是畫沿河四處遊走的鳥，還給她看一本封面畫著狡黠狐狸的速寫簿。諾拉說朱利安從前認為她安靜的夜晚都在浪費機會。索倫回答，沒有人有義務把休息變成事業優勢。他沒有用一場機智演說試圖消滅她所有疑慮，而是問兩人同行是否讓她不自在。「今晚沒有，」她說。可以自己定義舒適與否的簡單許可，比奢華承諾更有價值。

Back at the workshop, Mina proposed building redundancy into their work agreements, too. If one person became ill, another trained person should understand the test logs; no project should depend on a single heroic founder. They drafted a small schedule and named two reviewers for every change. The grant money remained tempting, and the rent would arrive soon. Nevertheless Nora signed the application for transparent public funding rather than the private contract. It was a decision with measurable consequences: fewer tools this month and a longer testing period. She went home worried, but without the old sensation of having agreed to something she could not defend.

回到工作室，米娜建議不只設備，合作規則也要有備援。如果一人生病，另一位受訓者應能讀懂測試紀錄；專案不該依賴單一英雄式創辦人。團隊擬定簡短時程，為每次變更指定兩名審查者。私人補助仍很誘人，租金也快到期。諾拉最後簽署透明的公共補助申請，而不是私人合約。這選擇有可衡量的後果：本月工具更少，測試期更長。她帶著憂慮回家，卻不再有那種明知自己答應了無法辯護的事情的感覺。

The following week a reviewer asked whether the group had consulted residents beyond the market's best-organized households. Nora admitted they had not done enough. She asked Farah to suggest a fair invitation process and offered payment for the time community testers would spend. No one should be expected to work for free merely because a project used the language of public benefit. Soren reminded the team to provide large-print forms and a way to decline without explanation. When several residents refused, Nora recorded the response without resentment. A refusal was not a problem to overcome; it was information about the limits of their invitation.

次週審查員問，團隊是否諮詢過市場裡組織能力較弱的家庭。諾拉承認做得不夠，請法拉建議公平邀請方式，並提供社區測試者參與時間的酬勞。不能因為計畫自稱有公共利益，就期待大家免費工作。索倫提醒團隊準備大字體表格，並讓任何人不需說明理由就能拒絕。幾位居民拒絕時，諾拉如實記錄，沒有不滿。拒絕不是必須克服的問題，而是邀請範圍與方式的資訊。

Nora spent Sunday calculating how the slower schedule would affect rent and wages. She could afford three months of modest work if no additional grant arrived; beyond that, the team would need either paying contracts or an orderly pause. She shared the figures with everyone rather than presenting optimism as a command. Edda proposed fewer working hours instead of unpaid overtime, and Mina made sure the plan complied with employment rules. Nora agreed to review it in four weeks. She was learning that protecting a team sometimes meant admitting that money was limited before people built their lives around a promise.

星期日諾拉計算較慢時程對租金與薪水的影響。若沒有新補助，手上資金可支應三個月的適度工作；再往後就得取得有薪合約，或有秩序地暫停。她向所有人公開數字，而不是把樂觀當命令。艾達提議縮短工時，不做無薪加班；米娜確認計畫符合勞動規則。諾拉同意四週後再檢討。她正在學習，保護團隊有時是趁大家還沒把生活建立在承諾上之前，先承認錢有限。
