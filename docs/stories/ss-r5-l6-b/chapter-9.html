每條可能的路

The Library of Possible Routes

這次，我把自己的名字留在門上 · 第 9 / 25 章 · 5000–6000 · 英中對照 · 校訂中

用互動版查詞、朗讀與收藏

The peninsula library invited Nora to teach a workshop on emergency literacy. She prepared a brochure with large print, simple diagrams, and instructions that could illuminate a route when power failed. The librarian warned her not to cram every fact onto one page. A child who lived with a stepchild sibling asked whether a family could follow different routes and still meet safely. Nora said yes, if everyone agreed on a clear meeting point beforehand. That practical question helped her detach the design from the fantasy of a perfect household. Families were varied; a warning needed to serve them without judging their arrangements.

半島圖書館邀請諾拉主持防災識讀工作坊。她準備一本大字體、簡明圖示的小冊子，說明停電時如何照亮疏散路線。圖書館員提醒她，不要把每件事都硬塞進同一頁。一名和繼親手足同住的孩子問，家人能否走不同路線而安全會合。諾拉說可以，只要事先約好清楚的集合點。這個務實問題幫她把設計從「完美家庭」的幻想裡分離。家庭各有不同，警示系統應服務他們，而不是評判他們的組合。

A narrator from the local theater volunteered to read the instructions aloud. She had bright jade earrings and a joyous voice, but she quickly pointed out that some people could not follow speech at all. During a demonstration, she pressed one nostril to imitate a blocked nose and joked about how even ordinary discomfort changed one's attention. Nora told her not to make disability a comic trick, and the performer accepted the correction. They revised the exercise to invite participants to choose communication methods rather than imitate other people's bodies. The shift was small, yet it made the workshop kinder and more informative.

地方劇團的一名說書人自願朗讀指示。她戴著翠玉耳環，聲音歡快，卻很快指出有人根本無法依靠口語。示範時，她按住一側鼻孔，想用鼻塞開玩笑，說連一般不舒服都會改變注意力。諾拉提醒她別把障礙當成搞笑把戲，表演者接受了修正。兩人把活動改成讓參與者自由選擇溝通方式，而不是模仿別人的身體。改動雖小，卻讓工作坊更友善，也更有教育意義。

Outside, volunteers were planning the revival of an abandoned fish market. A stallholder offered fresh trout, while another explained how an old radio had once been a potent tool for warning boats. Nora described the forthcoming public test and emphasized that participation would be selective only to match safety requirements, never to exclude unpopular people. She remembered the sense of captivity she had felt whenever Julian approved her travel and friends. Now she was responsible for deciding how to avoid creating a similar imbalance with the people who trusted her. She published the selection criteria and asked the library to collect objections.

戶外，志工正計畫復興廢棄魚市。一名攤販提供新鮮鱒魚，另一名說明舊收音機曾是警告船隻的有力工具。諾拉介紹即將進行的公開測試，強調參與名額若有選擇性，只能出於安全條件，絕不能排斥不受歡迎的人。她想起從前每次旅行和交友都要朱利安同意的囚禁感。如今她也必須避免對信任自己的人造成相似的不平衡。她公開甄選條件，並請圖書館收集異議。

The workshop ended with a list of improvements rather than a celebration. More than half the participants wanted signs visible from lower angles, and several requested a tactile map. Soren asked Nora whether the results embarrassed her. She said they were the first evidence that the project mattered. The old company had treated correction as weakness. Here correction gave people a reason to return. Nora took the bus home carrying a stack of annotated brochures. No one had offered to restore her former title, but she felt that her work was gradually returning to the people it was meant to help.

工作坊結束時留下的是改善清單，不是慶功儀式。超過半數參加者希望標誌能從較低角度看清，幾人要求觸覺地圖。索倫問諾拉是否覺得難堪。她說，這些結果是計畫真正有意義的第一批證據。舊公司把修正視為軟弱，這裡卻因修正而讓人願意再來。諾拉抱著一疊寫滿註記的小冊子搭公車回家。沒有人答應恢復她過去的職銜，但她感到自己的工作正逐漸回到真正需要它的人身邊。

A nurse volunteering at the workshop discussed how to administer a controlled trial safely: no treatment would be given, no medical claims would be made, and anyone who wished to leave could do so immediately. A teenager asked if a sensor could help classmates who felt self-conscious about acne. Nora explained that skin conditions were outside the purpose of this warning project, though comfort and consent mattered in any wearable design. The exchange reminded everyone that a useful device still had limits. Their revised brochure included a clear description of those limits, printed in the same size as the promised benefits.

一位擔任志工的護理師談到如何安全地執行受控測試：不提供治療、不做醫療效果宣稱，任何人想離開都能立即退出。一名青少年問，感測器能不能幫助因青春痘而不自在的同學。諾拉解釋，皮膚狀況不屬這項警示計畫的範圍，但任何穿戴設計都應重視舒適與同意。這段交流提醒所有人，有用的裝置仍有界線。新版小冊子用與優點相同大的字體，清楚寫出限制。

After the session Nora reviewed the library's suggestions with Edda. Several people had misunderstood the difference between a test alert and an official warning. They replaced a decorative heading with an unmistakable instruction and added examples of what to do if the system showed no signal at all. Nora sent the draft to the emergency office for review instead of approving it herself. Edda asked if that delayed the project too much. Nora replied that a few extra days could prevent a misunderstanding at precisely the wrong moment. The team agreed to wait for the written response.

活動後諾拉與艾達檢視圖書館意見。好幾人弄不清測試警示與官方警報的差異。團隊把裝飾性標題換成明確指示，還加入系統完全沒有訊號時應採取的行動範例。諾拉把草案送緊急辦公室審查，不自己核准。艾達問這樣是否拖太久。諾拉回答，多花幾天可能避免最不該發生時的誤解。團隊同意等書面答覆。
