測試地板上的水

The Water on the Test Floor

這次，我把自己的名字留在門上 · 第 11 / 25 章 · 5000–6000 · 英中對照 · 校訂中

The new trial received local acclaim before it even began, which made Nora uneasy. Praise could hide ambiguity about what the equipment actually did. The team visited a coastal warehouse where a draught blew through gaps in the doors. Soren used a geographical map to choose safe exits while Edda checked the temperature in Fahrenheit for a visiting inspector. The site had fireproof partitions but a slippery floor. Nora insisted that volunteers wear protective shoes and report any bodily discomfort. A resident laughed that only a villain would demand so much paperwork for a simple light. Nora replied that real emergencies were rarely simple, and invited him to inspect the plan.

新測試甚至還沒開始，就得到地方讚譽，反倒讓諾拉不安。讚美可能掩蓋裝置實際功能的模糊之處。團隊到海邊倉庫勘查，門縫不停吹進穿堂風。索倫用地理地圖挑選安全出口，艾達則替外地檢查員用華氏溫度記錄環境。現場有防火隔間，地板卻很滑。諾拉堅持志工穿防護鞋，並回報任何身體不適。一位居民笑說，只有反派才會為一盞簡單的燈要求那麼多文件。諾拉回答，真正的緊急事件很少簡單，並邀他檢查計畫。

A volunteer who worked as a hacker in a legal security-testing team examined the demo computer. He found no secret access to Northline, but he warned that an ordinary layman might confuse a test message with a real evacuation order. Nora added the words “TRAINING ONLY” in large letters. The group brought miscellaneous supplies: tape, a plastic utensil for pointing at controls, and a jar of yogurt for lunch. A painted mermaid on the warehouse wall offered an unexpected landmark. Edda suggested identifying exits by pictures as well as numbers. The team tried several labels and kept the one that people could recognize without a long explanation.

一位合法資安測試團隊的駭客志工檢查展示電腦。他沒有發現連往北線的秘密管道，卻提醒一般外行人可能把測試訊息誤認成真正撤離命令。諾拉加上大字「僅供演練」。大家帶來各式雜物：膠帶、用來指向控制鍵的塑膠餐具，以及午餐優格。倉庫牆上畫著一位美人魚，意外成了好辨認的地標。艾達建議除了數字，也用圖案標示出口。團隊測試了多個標籤，只保留不必冗長解釋就能辨識的版本。

During the test an actual leak left water across the floor. Nora halted the exercise. Some observers wanted to persevere to complete the schedule, but Soren explained that water on an electrical test site was an occurrence requiring immediate review. Nobody was injured. They isolated the power, recorded the conditions, and arranged a later inspection. Nora went outside to ponder whether she had become too cautious. Soren reminded her that the whole purpose of the trial was to learn what could go wrong before a storm. The schedule could revolve around safety rather than the other way round. Nora finally agreed to treat the interruption as useful information.

測試中，真正的漏水在地板上積成一片。諾拉立即停止演練。部分旁觀者想堅持完成時程，索倫卻說，電氣測試場地出現水，是必須立刻檢討的事件。沒有人受傷。團隊隔離電源、記錄條件，安排日後檢查。諾拉走到戶外，思考自己是否變得太謹慎。索倫提醒，演練的目的本來就是在暴風雨前找出可能出錯的地方。時程應圍繞安全調整，而不是讓安全遷就時程。她終於同意把中斷當成有價值的資訊。

The warehouse manager asked what should happen to the trial after a real leak was discovered. Nora proposed an incident report that separated the building's maintenance responsibilities from the research team's decisions. The manager signed it and invited the safety officer to inspect the site. No one demanded that the volunteer who noticed the water prove her courage by continuing. Farah said this was the kind of ordinary protection people remembered long after a demonstration ended. The studio would return only after the area had been checked, and the review would become part of the published record.

倉庫經理問，發現真正漏水後，測試應如何處理。諾拉提議一份事件報告，明確區分建物維護與研究團隊的決定。經理簽名，請安全人員檢查現場。沒有人要求發現水的志工繼續測試來證明勇氣。法拉說，這種平凡保護才是人們在展示結束後長久記得的事。工作室只會在場地檢查完成後回來，這次審查也將納入公開紀錄。

The interruption changed the team's next design meeting. Instead of beginning with what the device could achieve, Nora asked what users might mistakenly believe it could achieve. They listed false assumptions: that lights would always work, that everyone carried a charged phone, and that every road would stay open. The answers were uncomfortable because several had appeared in their own early presentations. Edda volunteered to revise those materials. Nora thanked her and reminded everyone that correcting a written promise was not an embarrassment to hide. It was part of making the promise accurate enough to trust.

這次中斷改變了下一場設計會議。諾拉不再先問裝置能做到什麼，而是問使用者可能誤以為它能做到什麼。大家列出錯誤假設：燈永遠會亮、每人手機都有電、每條路都能通行。答案令人不舒服，因為有些甚至出現在團隊早期簡報中。艾達自願修訂資料。諾拉道謝，提醒大家改正書面承諾並非應隱藏的羞恥，而是使承諾準確可信的一部分。

Later they took a brief outing to the public pier. Nora told Soren she had once believed achievement could liberate her from every insecurity. Now she knew that praise did not cancel responsibility. He said his own work had failed people before, including a ramp too steep for ordinary use. He had documented the mistake and changed his practice. She appreciated that he offered experience rather than comfort alone. They did not kiss beneath the evening lights or turn honesty into a reward. Instead they planned tomorrow's inspection and watched the last ferry move into the harbor. The ordinary horizon felt wide enough for both uncertainty and hope.

稍晚，他們短暫到公共碼頭散步。諾拉告訴索倫，過去她以為成就能讓自己從所有不安中解放。如今她知道讚譽不能取消責任。索倫說自己的工作也曾讓人失望，其中一條坡道陡得根本不實用。他記錄錯誤，並改變後來的做法。她欣賞他提供的是經驗，而不只是安慰。兩人沒有在夜燈下接吻，也沒有把誠實變成獎勵，而是安排明天的檢查，望著末班渡輪駛入港灣。平凡的地平線，似乎足以容納不確定與希望。
