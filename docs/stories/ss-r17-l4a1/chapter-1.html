第五十八個星期一

The Fifty-Eighth Monday

At 7:12 on Monday morning, Xu Zhiyuan woke before his alarm. The copper hands of the clock pointed to exactly the same minute as they had fifty-seven times before. A delivery truck struck a loose drain cover outside, producing three metallic knocks. His neighbor swept the landing with a broom. An advertisement for the old opera house played through someone's radio. These small sounds were more reliable than his own heartbeat. On Friday, at 11:17 p.m., he had died again, and the city had returned to Monday. Every object had returned too. Only the terrible inventory in his mind remained. He reached for a notebook, then stopped. Anything he wrote today would vanish on Friday night. The next version of himself would have to remember it without paper.

星期一早上七點十二分，許知遠在鬧鐘響前醒來。時鐘上的銅製指針，和之前五十七次一樣，指著完全相同的分鐘。窗外一輛貨車壓過鬆動的排水孔蓋，發出三聲金屬敲擊。鄰居拿掃帚清掃樓梯平臺。有人用收音機播放舊歌劇院的廣告。這些細微聲響，比他自己的心跳還可靠。上個星期五晚上十一點十七分，他又死了一次，整座城市隨即回到星期一。所有物品也都恢復原狀，只有可怕的死亡記憶留在腦中。他伸手去拿筆記本，隨即停住。今天寫下的一切都會在星期五夜裡消失，下一輪的自己只能靠記憶保存。

The first deaths had come as a brutal shock. Later he learned their pattern: the south tunnel filled with water, the emergency lights failed, and a pulse passed through the streets when the city bell rang. He had tried forty different routes and constructed plans from memory. Once, he believed an official slogan promising that the safest exit lay beneath the opera house. Another time he reached a ministry office and begged its night guard to cooperate. On his thirty-ninth Friday, a shady man beside a ticket counter offered him a private key. All those choices ended with the same last sound. By the fifty-seventh death he no longer feared the moment itself. He feared what repeated failure was doing to his ability to care.

最初幾次死亡帶來猛烈衝擊。後來他摸清了模式：南邊隧道進水、緊急照明失效，城市鐘聲響起時，一道脈衝穿過街道。他試過四十條不同路線，也靠記憶建構計畫。有一次，他相信官方口號，認為最安全的出口在歌劇院地下。另一次，他抵達某個部會辦公室，請求夜班警衛合作。第三十九個星期五，一名可疑男子在售票櫃臺旁賣給他私人鑰匙。那些選擇都以相同的最後聲音告終。到了第五十七次死亡，他已不再害怕死亡本身，而是害怕一再失敗正逐漸奪走自己關心他人的能力。

Seventeen-year-old Zhiyuan had been a student assistant at the municipal transport agency before the phenomenon began. He knew how easily persuasive language could turn a poor plan into an official route. The city's annual safety assembly was scheduled for Wednesday, and a colorful poster promised entertainment after the speeches. In smaller print, the poster announced a new system for measuring emergency-exit usage. A careful reader would notice that the public had been invited to celebrate a system nobody had tested in public. He reviewed the poster while eating breakfast, searching for an overlooked detail. A drawing of the bell tower occupied the bottom corner. Beneath it, almost invisible, was a pair of tiny circles joined by a line. He had seen that symbol on the door that killed him last Friday.

循環開始前，十七歲的知遠在市立運輸機構當學生助理，協助整理風險報告。他知道有說服力的文字，如何輕易把糟糕計畫包裝成正式逃生路線。城市年度安全集會安排在星期三，彩色海報還承諾演講後有娛樂節目。較小的字則宣布一套測量緊急出口使用情況的新系統。細心的人會發現，市民被邀請慶祝的系統，根本沒有接受過公開測試。他一邊吃早餐一邊重新檢查海報，尋找漏看的細節。鐘塔插畫占了底部一角，下面藏著兩個幾乎看不見、以線相連的小圓圈。他上個星期五就在害死自己的那扇門上看過這個符號。

At nine, a woman in a gray coat appeared outside his office. She had a youthful face but an exhausted expression, as though she had spent several years awake. Her facial muscles barely moved when she asked whether he was Xu Zhiyuan. He expected another official warning. Instead she held out a plain envelope containing a photograph of the bell tower's basement. Someone had written two words on the back: FIFTY-SEVEN FRIDAYS. Zhiyuan felt acid rise in his throat. No one else should have known that number. The woman said her name was Tang Cheng and that she worked with old sound recordings. She did not ask him to trust her. She asked him to remember the fourth knock after the three familiar ones.

九點，一名穿灰外套的女子出現在辦公室外。她的臉看來年輕，神情卻極其疲倦，彷彿好幾年沒睡過。她問他是不是許知遠時，臉部肌肉幾乎沒有動。他原本以為又是官方警告，女子卻遞來一個普通信封，裡面有張鐘塔地下室的照片。照片背面寫著兩個英文詞：「五十七個星期五」。知遠覺得喉頭一陣酸澀。除了他，沒有人應該知道這個數字。女子自稱唐澄，在檔案館處理舊聲音紀錄。她沒有要求他信任自己，只請他記住，熟悉的三下敲擊之後，還會有第四下。

He almost called her a coward for waiting so long to speak, then recognized his own anger. She might be as frightened as he was. The office radio announced a brief interruption to the morning broadcast. Outside, the truck crossed the drain cover again, although its driver had already passed the corner. The three knocks sounded. A fourth followed from the direction of the bell tower, too far away to have come from the road. Tang watched his face and said she could interfere with the recorded signal for eight seconds, but never longer. It was not much of a gift. It was, however, the first evidence in fifty-seven weeks that someone else understood the impossible rules. Zhiyuan opened the office door and said, "Show me where the fourth sound begins."

他差點罵她是膽小鬼，責怪她為什麼拖了這麼久才說話，隨即察覺那只是自己的怒氣。她也可能和自己一樣害怕。辦公室收音機宣布早間節目將短暫中斷。外頭的貨車明明已過了轉角，卻再次壓上排水孔蓋。三下敲擊響起，第四下則從遙遠的鐘塔方向傳來，不可能是路面發出的聲音。唐澄觀察他的表情，說自己能讓錄製的訊號受到八秒干擾，卻不能更久。這不是多麼了不起的能力，卻是五十七個星期以來，首次有人證明她也理解那些不可能的規則。知遠打開辦公室門說：「帶我去看第四聲從哪裡開始。」
