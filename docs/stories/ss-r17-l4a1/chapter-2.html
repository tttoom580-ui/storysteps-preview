A Name in the Margin

Tang did not take Zhiyuan directly to the bell tower. She led him to a corner bakery where an immigrant owner was replacing a cracked window. He greeted her without surprise; in the present week they had known each other for two years. A prominent photograph above the counter showed the baker receiving an autograph from a visiting singer. Tang drew a timetable on a paper bag, marking Monday's fourth knock, Wednesday's public assembly, and Friday's final bell. "This is my initial map," she said. "I have checked it in many weeks, but I still need your feedback." Zhiyuan watched her write. The marks were not clues that would survive a reset. They were a way to discover whether they could reason together before the next one.

唐澄沒有直接帶知遠去鐘塔，而是領他到轉角的麵包店。一名移民老闆正在更換破裂的窗玻璃。他向唐澄打招呼時並不驚訝；在這個星期的時間線裡，他們已經認識兩年。櫃臺上方掛著一張醒目的照片，拍到老闆拿到來訪歌手的親筆簽名。唐澄在紙袋上畫出時刻表，標記星期一的第四聲、星期三的公開集會，以及星期五的最後鐘響。「這是我最初的圖，」她說，「我核對過好多輪，但還需要你的回饋。」知遠看她落筆。那些線條不是能留到下次循環的線索，而是讓兩人能在下一次重置前，先確認是否能一起推理。

The baker's dog put one paw on Zhiyuan's shoe. For an instant he remembered a Friday when the dog had escaped an explosive fire in a nearby shop while its owner remained trapped. He moved away too sharply. Tang noticed. She explained that during the first twenty loops she had thought the strange sounds were mischief arranged by a cruel stranger. Later, after watching the same shop fall into ruin again and again, she stopped calling the repetitions a trick. "The cruelty isn't a joke," she said. "Someone benefits from the reset." Zhiyuan asked why she had never spoken to him. She produced a newspaper clipping in which his signature appeared beside approval for the dangerous south-tunnel route. He stared at it. He had never given that approval.

麵包師的狗把一隻爪子搭在知遠鞋上。他突然想起某個星期五，附近商店發生爆炸性火災，狗逃出來了，主人卻被困在裡面。他動作太急，往後退了一步。唐澄注意到了。她解釋，最初二十輪裡，她一直以為那些怪聲是殘酷陌生人的惡作劇。後來，她看著同一家商店一再毀壞，才不再把重複當成戲弄。「這種殘酷不是玩笑，」她說，「有人從重置中得利。」知遠問她為何從未找過自己。唐澄拿出一張剪報，上面在危險的南隧道路線核准欄旁，印著他的簽名。他盯著紙看。那份核准從來不是他簽的。

Zhiyuan carved two short lines into the wooden table with a blunt pencil. He said those stood for the only two possibilities he could accept: either somebody had forged his name, or the system could disguise a forged record as genuine. Tang proposed a test. She would send the same question to the municipal clerk under two different names, one with official privilege and one without it. Within minutes the privileged request received an answer, while the other was rejected for lack of convenience. The answers were not about safety at all. They measured influence. Zhiyuan's first instinct was to confront the clerk, but Tang reminded him that an angry accusation might close their only access to the records.

知遠用鈍鉛筆在木桌上刻出兩道短線。他說自己目前只接受兩種可能：有人偽造他的名字，或系統能把偽造文件偽裝成真實紀錄。唐澄提出測試：她會用兩個不同身分向市府職員送出同一個問題，一個擁有官方特權，另一個沒有。幾分鐘後，有特權的詢問得到回覆，另一個則以便利性不足為由遭駁回。這些答覆根本不是在評估安全，而是在衡量影響力。知遠第一個念頭是去質問職員，但唐澄提醒他，憤怒的指控可能封死唯一能接觸紀錄的管道。

Their next stop was the prevention office behind the railway station. A clerk working the early shift took them into a room filled with elastic bands used for sealing evidence boxes. An official remark on the wall promised that public safety and business interests could prosper together. The clerk read the forged document, then pointed to a faint mark beneath Zhiyuan's name. It was not an ordinary signature; the letter shapes were identical to those created by a commercial stamp. She found another copy signed on a day when Zhiyuan had been assigned to a mountainous district. "I can show you the discrepancy," she said, "but I cannot promise the ministry will welcome it." Tang asked only that the original be preserved until the end of the week.

下一站是火車站後面的預防安全辦公室。一名值早班的職員帶他們走進堆滿彈性封條的房間。牆上的官方文字宣稱，公共安全與企業利益可以一起繁榮。職員讀完偽造文件，指出知遠姓名下方一道淡淡痕跡。那不是普通簽名，而是商業印章壓出的相同字形。她又找出另一份副本，簽署日期恰好是知遠被派往山區的日子。「我能指出兩份紀錄的差異，」她說，「但無法保證部會樂意接受。」唐澄只請她將原件保留到這個星期結束。

On the way out, the clerk asked a question Zhiyuan could not answer: why would anyone forge an employee's approval for an exit that failed every Friday? He had assumed the murderer wanted him dead. Now he considered a more disturbing possibility. His name might be useful precisely because he would be blamed after the disaster, when a replacement timeline finally survived. The idea shifted the shape of their investigation. They were no longer searching only for a machine that rewound time. They were searching for a future in which someone needed a convenient guilty man. Tang turned back at the station door. "When I left you alone in the earlier loops," she said, "I thought you were that man." Zhiyuan answered, "Then we should both check the evidence before deciding again."

離開時，職員問了一個知遠無法回答的問題：為什麼有人要替一條每個星期五都出事的出口，偽造員工核准？他一直以為兇手只想讓他死亡。現在他想到更令人不安的可能：他的名字也許正是為了在某條終於能延續的時間線裡，替災難承擔責任。調查因此改變方向。他們尋找的不再只是一部讓時間倒轉的機器，也是一個需要方便的替罪羊的未來。唐澄在車站門口回頭說：「前幾輪我沒來找你，是因為我以為你就是那個人。」知遠回答：「那這次我們都先查證，再下結論。」
