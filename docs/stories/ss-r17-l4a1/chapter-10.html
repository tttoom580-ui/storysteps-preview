未被計入的避難所

The Uncounted Shelter

Wednesday's first appointment was at a public finance office above a shopping facility. The atmosphere felt unusually cheerful for a place that handled emergency spending. A clerk offered them a genuine copy of the contract payment ledger after verifying their identities. The expenses had been classified as a promotional upgrade, not a safety repair. That difference mattered: an ambitious agency director could obtain money for a public celebration without submitting the rigorous tests required for critical equipment. Tang asked whether the classification could be corrected. The clerk explained that a correction would require an official application and supporting evidence. He could provide the ledger, but he had no option to change the budget alone. Zhiyuan thanked him for distinguishing what he could actually do from what he wished he could do.

星期三第一個約訪地點是商場上方的公共財務辦公室。處理緊急支出的地方，氣氛卻異常愉快。職員核對身分後，提供真正的合約付款簿副本。支出被分類為宣傳升級，而非安全修繕。這個差異非常重要：有野心的機構主管可以替公開慶典申請經費，而不必接受關鍵設備所需的嚴格測試。唐澄問分類是否能更正。職員解釋，更正需要正式申請及支持證據；他能提供帳簿，卻沒有權限獨自改動預算。知遠感謝他分清楚實際能做的事與自己希望能做的事。

Outside, they passed a perfume shop and a window displaying winter stockings despite the spring weather. A publisher had placed a poster there advertising a book about civic dignity. Zhiyuan felt embarrassed that he had never examined the budget behind the route he once helped evaluate. Tang told him that embarrassment was useful only if it changed his next action. They went to a temporary shelter near the river to test whether the official evacuation plan worked for those outside the city center. The manager had recently accepted several refugee families from a regional fire, and the shelter's kitchen was already crowded. Yet its address did not appear in the ministry's emergency routing software. The people most dependent on assistance were treated as though the building were empty.

外頭，他們經過香水店與陳列冬季長襪的櫥窗，雖然現在明明是春天。一家出版社在附近張貼宣傳市民尊嚴新書的海報。知遠感到難堪，因為自己過去協助評估路線，卻從未仔細看背後預算。唐澄說，難堪只有能改變下一步行動才有意義。他們到河邊的臨時避難所，測試官方疏散方案是否適用於市中心之外的人。管理員最近接納幾戶逃離區域火災的難民家庭，廚房本來就很擁擠。然而避難所地址完全不在部會的緊急路線軟體裡。最依賴協助的人，在系統眼中彷彿不存在。

A volunteer nurse described a rescue drill that had failed the previous month. The shelter's north exit opened into a closed yard, while the south gate led toward the flood tunnel. The manager had submitted a resignation letter after repeated requests for repairs were ignored, but the residents had persuaded her to stay for one more week. Zhiyuan asked her to list the building's actual capacity and the people who required assisted travel. He would not publish names or medical details. Together they created a count of needed places, walking times, and accessible exits. A promotion poster on the wall announced that the new city system would transform emergency care. Tang covered the poster with their measured figures, leaving it visible underneath for anyone who wanted to compare the two claims.

志工護理師描述上個月失敗的一次救援演練：避難所北出口通向封閉院子，南門則通往淹水隧道。管理員多次請求修繕無果，已經遞出辭職信，但住民勸她再留一星期。知遠請她列出建築實際容量，以及需要協助移動的人數。他不會公布姓名或醫療細節。大家一起製作表格，記下需要的位置、步行時間及無障礙出口。牆上的宣傳海報宣布新城市系統將徹底改變緊急照護。唐澄把實測數據貼在海報上，仍保留原文讓願意的人可以比較兩種說法。

The shelter manager questioned whether Zhiyuan's figures were authentic. He welcomed the challenge and invited a second volunteer to count independently. Their totals differed by one person because a child had been visiting the neighboring clinic. They corrected the record instead of pretending perfection. A family thanked them for taking their worries seriously, but the manager warned against easy acceptance. Numbers on paper would not carry a wheelchair through a narrow doorway. Zhiyuan proposed a small demonstration that afternoon using an empty chair and a weighted bag. It showed that the western service passage was wide enough but had a step that needed a removable ramp. The team reported both the usable path and the unresolved obstacle. The work was slow, but it was no longer imaginary safety.

避難所管理員質疑知遠的數據是否真實。他接受質疑，邀請第二名志工獨立清點。兩人的總數差一人，原來有名孩子去了隔壁診所。大家修正紀錄，而不是假裝完美。一戶家庭感謝他們認真看待憂慮，管理員卻提醒不能太容易就接受。紙上的數字無法替輪椅穿過窄門。知遠提議下午用空輪椅與負重袋做小型示範。結果西側服務通道夠寬，但有一道臺階，需要可拆卸坡板。小隊同時報告可用路徑與尚未解決的障礙。進度很慢，但不再只是想像中的安全。

By evening the corrected figures had reached the finance clerk and two independent shelter representatives. Tang noticed that the agency's automated routing map differed from their map at exactly three doors. The discrepancy could not be explained by weather alone. Someone had altered the available exits after the original equipment tests. Zhiyuan traced the changes to the same maintenance supplier named in the missing contract page. He did not yet know whether the alterations were accidental or offensive attempts to hide a problem. He had an appointment to attend: the ministry's grand safety assembly was beginning in an hour. He expected applause, speeches, and a carefully managed display. This time he would enter the hall carrying a question the official figures could not answer: who had been left outside the count?

傍晚時，更正後的數據已送到財務職員與兩位獨立避難所代表手上。唐澄發現機構自動路線圖與他們的圖，恰好有三扇門不一致，差異不能單靠天氣解釋。有人在原始設備測試之後改動了可用出口。知遠追查到那些變動，指向失蹤合約頁面上同一家維修供應商。他還不知道變更是意外，還是刻意掩蓋問題的惡意行為。現在他得赴一個約：部會盛大的安全集會，一小時後開始。他預期會有掌聲、演講和精心安排的展示。這次，他要帶進會場的是官方數據答不出的問題：究竟誰被排除在計數之外？
