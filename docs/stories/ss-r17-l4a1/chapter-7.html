繼電器上的霜

Frost on the Relay

我已經重複死在星期五十七次 · 第 7 / 25 章 · 3000–4000 · 英中對照 · 校訂中

用互動版查詞、朗讀與收藏

Chen lent them a maintenance map and a warning about the weather. The northern relay stood in a frost-covered clearing above a village, and a rescue helicopter could not land there when the wind became intense. Zhiyuan and Tang followed an old mule track, passing a herd of sheep and a small clinic. A plumber from the village agreed to guide them because he had repaired pipes near the relay fence. He carried a diploma from a technical school in his coat pocket, not to impress them but because officials had repeatedly denied his right to examine public equipment. "A certificate does not make me wiser than everyone else," he said, "but it should let me report a leak." The slight smile he gave Tang did not hide his frustration.

陳禾借給他們一張維修地圖，也提醒天氣狀況。北方繼電站位於村莊上方結霜的空地，風勢強時救援直升機無法降落。知遠與唐澄沿著運貨騾子走的舊道前進，經過一群羊與小診所。一名村裡的水管工答應帶路，因為他曾修理繼電站圍欄附近的管線。他口袋裡放著技術學校文憑，不是為了炫耀，而是官員一直否認他有檢查公共設備的資格。「證書不表示我比別人聰明，」他說，「但至少應該讓我通報漏水。」他對唐澄微微一笑，卻藏不住挫折。

At the relay they began to analyze the damaged connections. A copper plate was warm despite the frost, and a red indicator flashed whenever the underground drainage pump started. The plumber proposed a controlled test with the pump disconnected, provided the nearby homes were given advance notice. Zhiyuan agreed; accuracy mattered more than speed. Tang recorded the surroundings from a safe distance and counted each flash. With the pump on, the plate heated twice in seven minutes. With it off, the heating stopped. The result was remarkable, but a single experiment was not enough to prove causation. They repeated it after checking that nobody dependent on the village's water supply would be injured by the temporary interruption.

到了繼電站，他們開始分析損壞的連接點。銅板明明覆著霜卻是溫的，地下排水幫浦一啟動，紅色指示燈就閃爍。水管工提議暫時切斷幫浦做受控測試，但要先通知附近住戶。知遠同意；準確比速度重要。唐澄在安全距離外記錄周遭環境，數每次閃光。幫浦啟動時，銅板七分鐘內發熱兩次；關閉後，發熱停止。結果很驚人，但一次實驗不足以證明因果。他們先確認暫停供水不會傷害依賴村裡供水的人，才再次重複測試。

A competitor from a private equipment company arrived with a camera. He insisted their experiment violated company policy and opposed further access. The plumber asked whether the company could offer a safer alternative. It could not. Tang pointed out that the public had already paid for welfare and safety services, not a private contest over whose logo appeared on the fence. They negotiated a mutual observation: the company representative could watch from behind the barrier, but he could not touch the test controls. The second test showed the same pattern. A technician calculated that a twenty-percent reduction in the pump's operating time might reduce the dangerous current. Zhiyuan wrote the figure down but refused to treat a prediction as a completed repair.

一家私人設備公司的競爭者帶著相機到場，堅稱測試違反公司政策，反對他們繼續進入。水管工問公司能否提出更安全的替代方法，對方答不上來。唐澄指出，市民已經支付社會福利與安全服務費，不是為了看圍欄上掛誰的商標。他們談好互相觀察：公司代表可在防護欄後觀看，但不得觸碰測試控制。第二次測試得到相同模式。技師估算，若把幫浦運轉時間減少百分之二十，或許可以降低危險電流。知遠記下數字，卻拒絕把預測當成已完成的修復。

At lunch the plumber told them why he had chosen to adapt the old drainage system rather than build a new pipe. The village could afford neither a long shutdown nor the loss of its only pump. During a previous accident, an elderly resident had suffered a leg injury because the rescue route was blocked. That memory explained his careful manner. He did not want to win an argument and leave people without water. Tang showed him the condition from Chen's manual: a trial must not exclude those in greatest need. The plumber read it slowly and suggested adding a village representative to any further experiment. Zhiyuan agreed. In all fifty-seven earlier loops, he had never asked someone outside the city to help define a safe result.

午餐時，水管工解釋自己為何選擇調整舊排水系統，而不重鋪新管。村裡負擔不起長期停工，也不能失去唯一的幫浦。以前一次事故中，救援路線被堵住，害一名長者腿部受傷；這段記憶使他特別謹慎。他不想贏了爭論，卻讓居民沒水用。唐澄拿出陳禾手冊的條件：試驗不得排除最需要幫助的人。水管工慢慢讀完，建議今後每次測試都加入村民代表。知遠同意。他過去五十七次循環，從沒想過請市區以外的人共同定義安全結果。

On the descent, the private company's representative caught up with them. He quietly admitted that his manager had ordered him to photograph people who questioned the relay. The manager said every repeated Friday was a software error, not an actual event. The representative did not remember any earlier week, and Zhiyuan did not ask him to believe in time travel. Instead he showed the two tests and asked whether the company's own logs recorded the same changes. The representative promised to check. Tang warned Zhiyuan not to rely on that promise alone. A person without memory could be sincere today and take different instructions tomorrow. Their reliable advantage was not persuasion. It was a test that another person could repeat, even in a world that would soon forget it.

下山途中，那名私人公司代表追上他們。他低聲承認，經理命令他拍下質疑繼電站的人。經理說每個重複的星期五都只是軟體錯誤，不是真實事件。代表不記得任何前一輪，知遠也沒有要求他相信時間旅行。他只展示兩次測試，問公司自己的紀錄是否也有相同變化。代表答應查看。唐澄提醒知遠，不能只依靠對方承諾。沒有記憶的人今天可能真誠，明天卻會接受不同指令。他們的可靠優勢不是說服話術，而是一個任何人都能重做的測試，即使世界即將忘掉它。
