為災難鼓掌

Applause for a Disaster

我已經重複死在星期五十七次 · 第 11 / 25 章 · 3000–4000 · 英中對照 · 校訂中

用互動版查詞、朗讀與收藏

The Wednesday safety convention began beneath a skyscraper decorated with banners about the city's recovery plan. Director Hong boasted that the new warning network had reduced every serious risk. A display presented consistent green lines on a screen, but Zhiyuan noticed that the data ended before each scheduled Friday night. He and Tang had arranged for two shelter volunteers to contribute their own observations. One carried a notebook of exit widths; the other had a list of maintenance requests. They did not accuse Hong immediately. Their aim was to expose the difference between claims and measured conditions. A cheerful announcer compared the new technology to protection against a hurricane. Tang knew the forecast called for rain and river flooding, not a tropical storm, but the exaggeration made the audience applaud.

星期三的安全大會在摩天大樓裡開始，場內掛滿城市復原計畫的布條。洪季平主管自誇新警報網已降低所有重大風險。展示螢幕上有一條條一致的綠線，但知遠發現資料在每次預定的星期五夜間之前就截止。他和唐澄安排兩位避難所志工帶來自己的觀察。一人帶著出口寬度筆記，另一人帶著維修申請清單。他們沒有立刻指控洪季平，而是要揭露宣稱與實測條件的差異。歡快的主持人將新科技比作能抵擋颶風的保護。唐澄知道預報是降雨與河水上漲，不是熱帶風暴，但誇張說法仍讓觀眾鼓掌。

At the question session, Zhiyuan asked why the supplier's contract had excluded independent flood tests. Hong replied that the company was loyal to public welfare and that criticism from uninformed people could be troublesome. Zhiyuan showed the authentic ledger, then asked whether the director denied its wording. Hong offered no assurance that the tests had occurred. He did not answer. Instead he accused Zhiyuan of trying to manufacture a scandal for personal attention. Tang stood and read the original manual's warning about excluded residents. She did not ask the audience to believe in time travel. She asked them to imagine one ordinary Friday when a flooded tunnel and an energized relay operated together. Ignorance of a hazard, she said, did not make that hazard disappear. Several city technicians stopped clapping and began taking notes.

提問時，知遠問供應商合約為何排除了獨立洪水測試。洪季平回答，公司忠於公共福利，無知者的批評只會製造麻煩。知遠展示真正的付款簿，問主管是否否認上面的文字。洪季平沒有保證測試確實做過，也沒有正面回答，反而指控他為求關注而製造醜聞。唐澄站起來，朗讀原始手冊關於弱勢居民被排除的警告。她沒有要求聽眾相信時間旅行，只請他們想像一個普通的星期五：淹水隧道與持續送電的繼電器同時運作。她說，不知道危險，不會讓危險消失。幾位市府技師停止鼓掌，開始記筆記。

A laboratory representative tried to reassure the audience with an informative slide about hydrogen batteries and energy recovery. Tang pointed out that the battery demonstration had nothing to do with the south tunnel's emergency doors. The representative admitted the slide had been added for appearance. One elderly woman rose to describe her misfortune during a previous evacuation drill, when the door had locked before she reached it. Her recovery from a broken wrist had taken months. Hong dismissed it as an isolated incident. The woman replied that the word isolated did not help her open the door. The audience grew quiet. Zhiyuan recognized the moment: people were no longer listening for a spectacular answer; they were trying to decide whose account deserved a proper examination.

實驗室代表拿出介紹氫電池與能源回收的資訊性投影片，企圖安撫觀眾。唐澄指出電池展示與南隧道緊急門無關。代表承認投影片是為了好看才加的。一位長者站起來，描述先前疏散演練的不幸：她還沒走到，門就鎖了，手腕骨折後復原好幾個月。洪季平說那只是個別事件。長者回答，「個別」這個詞並不能幫她打開門。會場安靜下來。知遠認出那一刻的改變：人們不再等待驚人的答案，而是在判斷哪些說法值得正式調查。

At the back of the hall, the manufacturer of the relay stood beside a model of the tower. He had brought a device that demonstrated electrical resistance, and he invited the technicians to repeat a simple measurement. The reading did not match the official chart. Hong demanded an explanation. The manufacturer said the equipment had been altered after installation and he could not guarantee its current behavior. A representative of the city's commerce department whispered that the public ceremony might need to end. Tang requested a brief relaxation period for the audience rather than a rushed vote. During the break, the technicians compared notes and found two more discrepancies between the display and actual maintenance records. They agreed to report their findings under their own names.

會場後方，繼電器製造商站在鐘塔模型旁。他帶來能展示電阻的裝置，邀請技師重做簡單測量。讀數與官方圖表不符。洪季平要求解釋。製造商說設備安裝後曾被改動，他無法保證目前行為。市府商務部門代表低聲說，公開典禮恐怕得中止。唐澄要求給觀眾短暫休息，不要匆忙投票。休息時，技師比對筆記，發現展示與實際維修紀錄還有另外兩項不一致。他們同意以自己的名字提交發現。

The convention ended without a victory speech. Hong promised a new internal review, but Zhiyuan refused to confuse a promise with completed protection. The shelter volunteers sent their measurements to the public council. Tang preserved a copy of the manufacturer's admission and asked for an outside observer at the next test. When they stepped into the street, a screen on the tower changed from green to amber. It displayed a new phrase: TRIAL RESISTANCE INCREASED. Zhiyuan felt the familiar dread of Friday, though two days remained. The machine's persistent store was recording their challenge. It could not remember emotions, yet somebody with access to its summaries might learn what they had uncovered. Their next move would have to protect the witnesses as carefully as the technical evidence.

大會沒有以勝利演說作結。洪季平承諾啟動內部審查，但知遠拒絕把承諾當成已完成的保護。避難所志工把測量結果送交市議會。唐澄保存製造商承認的紀錄，並要求下次測試安排外部觀察者。兩人走到街上，鐘塔螢幕由綠轉黃，顯示新句子：「試驗阻力增加」。明明距離星期五還有兩天，知遠卻感到熟悉的恐懼。機器的永久記憶庫正在記錄他們的質疑。它或許不懂情緒，但能讀到摘要的人可能得知他們查出了什麼。下一步必須像保護技術證據一樣，保護提供證言的人。
