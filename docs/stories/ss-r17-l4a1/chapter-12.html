一週之外的房間

The Room Outside the Week

Wednesday morning's protest continued into the afternoon, but Zhiyuan and Tang had a more precise destination: the basement storage room behind the tower. Chen's manual identified it as an absolute isolation chamber, designed to preserve a summary of every experiment while the rest of the city returned to an earlier state. A young hardware specialist named Lin met them at the door. He explained that access required both a technical appointment and a public observer's approval. Tang showed the council's written request; Lin checked the document rather than assuming their confidence was enough. Inside, long shelves held oval vessels of sealed tape and a small glass window through which light moved like a slow current. The room's gracious caretaker offered chairs, but Zhiyuan felt dread at the thought of returning here after another death. The place felt ordinary, which made its purpose harder to accept.

星期三的抗議延續到下午，但知遠和唐澄有更明確的目的地：鐘塔後方的地下儲存室。陳禾手冊將它稱作完全隔離艙，設計用途是在其他城市狀態回到較早時間時，仍保存每次實驗摘要。一名叫林的年輕硬體專家在門口接待。他解釋，進入需要技術預約和公共觀察者核准。唐澄出示議會書面請求；林先生仔細核對文件，而不是因兩人很有自信就放行。室內長架放著橢圓形密封錄音容器，玻璃窗後有緩慢流動的光。管理員親切地提供椅子，但知遠一想到可能再死一次後回到這裡，就感到恐懼。這地方看來普通，反而讓它的用途更難以相信。

Lin opened a manual filled with illustrations. One showed a hawk circling above a mine, while a miner below marked the same route from the ground. The drawing explained why a machine's distant view and a human witness's local view must both be recorded. The storage system measured distribution and efficiency across districts. It could analyze broad patterns efficiently, but it had no direct experience of the people inside those patterns. Zhiyuan remembered the refuge shelter missing from the official map. He asked whether the system had recognized that absence in earlier cycles. Lin inserted a blank card, then selected the most recent trial summary. On the screen appeared a number: FIFTY-SEVEN. Below it were seventeen recorded deviations, most labeled UNRESOLVED. The machine had kept counting their failures even when the city forgot.

林先生打開一本滿是插圖的手冊。有幅圖畫著在礦場上方盤旋的鷹，以及地面上標記相同路線的礦工。這幅圖用來解釋，機器從遠處看到的景象，和人類見證者在現場所見，必須分別記錄。儲存系統會測量各區分布與運作效率，也能有效率地分析大範圍模式，卻沒有親身經歷那些模式裡人們的生活。知遠想起從官方地圖消失的避難所，問系統在以前的循環是否認出這個缺漏。林先生插入空卡，選取最近一次試驗摘要。螢幕出現一個數字：「五十七」。下方列出十七項偏差，大多標示「未解決」。城市忘記了，機器卻持續計數他們的失敗。

A second page displayed two names under the heading MEMORY RETENTION CHANNELS: XU ZHIYUAN and TANG CHENG. Zhiyuan gripped the edge of the desk. Tang did not appear surprised. Lin said the names had been inserted during a late software revision, apparently to renew an old observer arrangement. A margin note claimed the channels would help the machine recognize human grief after a reset. But no signature proved either person had agreed. The system had pursued technical perfection while ignoring discipline about consent. "We weren't chosen by destiny," Zhiyuan said. "We were installed as parts." Lin shook his head. "A person can never be a part to install," he answered. "This record describes a violation, not your identity."

第二頁的標題寫著「記憶保留通道」，下方列著兩個名字：「許知遠」「唐澄」。知遠握緊桌緣。唐澄看來並不驚訝。林先生說，名字是在一次晚期軟體修訂時插入，似乎是為了延續舊的觀察者安排。旁註宣稱通道能幫助機器辨認重置後的人類悲傷，但沒有任何簽名能證明兩人同意。系統追求技術完美，卻忽略取得同意的紀律。「我們不是被命運選中，」知遠說，「只是被安裝成零件。」林先生搖頭：「人不能當成零件安裝。這份紀錄描述的是侵犯，不是你的身分。」

To test what the store could and could not do, Lin asked each of them to dictate an observation into separate recorders. The machine summarized both statements but missed the sound of Tang's hand shaking against the microphone. That difference mattered. Recognition software could label fear, yet it did not contain the experience of being afraid. Zhiyuan resolved to use the stored summaries as evidence rather than as instructions. It was a realistic distinction between a record and a command. He asked Lin to fasten a paper seal around the newest printout and list everyone who had touched the equipment. Lin agreed, then noted that the storage room itself would survive each reset even though the printout outside it would not. If they were forced into another Monday, the machine would still hold the latest counts.

為了測試記憶庫能做什麼、不能做什麼，林先生請兩人分別對著錄音機描述觀察。機器能摘要兩段陳述，卻漏掉唐澄顫抖的手碰到麥克風的聲音。這個差別很重要。辨識軟體可以標記恐懼，卻不曾真正體驗害怕。知遠決定把儲存摘要當證據，而不是當指令。這是對紀錄與命令之差異的務實區分。他請林先生在最新列印資料上加封條，並列出接觸設備的人。林先生同意，又指出儲存室本身會在每次重置後保留狀態，但帶到室外的紙本不會。如果他們被迫再回星期一，機器仍會保存最新計數。

As they prepared to leave, Lin uncovered a hardware diagram showing two separate circuits feeding the final bell. One carried the public warning and could be paused for eight seconds. The other powered the south tunnel doors and could not be stopped safely until the pump had been isolated. A shaded line suggested that an alternative northern corridor could carry evacuation traffic during the changeover. Zhiyuan asked Lin to confirm it by a physical test, not simply by a perfect model. Lin promised to seek assistance from the northern plumber and a local survey team. Tang remained beside the screen showing their names. She finally said, "I knew about the channels before I found you. There is something else I have not told you."

準備離開時，林先生翻出硬體圖，顯示兩條分開供應最後鐘聲的電路。一條負責公共警報，可暫停八秒；另一條替南隧道門供電，必須先隔離幫浦才能安全停止。圖上陰影線暗示切換期間，可以由北方走廊承接疏散人流。知遠請林先生做實體測試，不要只依賴完美模型。林先生答應聯絡北方水管工與地方測量隊。唐澄仍站在寫著兩人名字的螢幕前，終於說：「我在找到你之前，就知道這兩條通道了。還有一件事，我一直沒告訴你。」
