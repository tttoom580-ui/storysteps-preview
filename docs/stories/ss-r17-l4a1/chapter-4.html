A Witness at the Opera House

The bus to the northern district passed an embassy, a public library, and a mural showing planets in orbit. Tang recognized the painted figures from an old opera production, where children had played the planets. She said a translator once worked backstage there, creating subtitles for visitors. The translator had been her mother. After an urgent call from the laboratory eight years earlier, her mother vanished while making a recording in the bell tower. Tang's voice remained earnest, but each word seemed carefully measured. Zhiyuan felt the urge to offer an easy explanation and resisted it. A disappearance in ordinary time was not proof of murder. He asked what they could verify. Tang answered that the last recording was still stored in a library box labeled as an amusement program.

通往北區的公車經過一座大使館、公共圖書館，還有描繪行星運行軌道的壁畫。唐澄認出畫中的角色，說那是舊歌劇製作裡由孩子扮演的行星。當年有位翻譯員在後臺工作，替外地訪客製作字幕。那個人就是她母親。八年前，母親接到實驗室的緊急電話，去鐘塔錄音後便失蹤。唐澄語氣誠懇，但每個字都像經過仔細衡量。知遠很想立刻給出簡單解釋，卻忍住了。發生在正常時間裡的失蹤，並不能直接證明謀殺。他問有什麼可以查證。唐澄說，最後一份錄音仍放在圖書館的箱子裡，標籤卻寫成娛樂節目。

A librarian named Wu welcomed them reluctantly. The box contained a collection of school rhymes, a photograph of the old stage, and one unlabeled tape. Wu said the tape had been copied with approval from a municipal researcher, but nobody could find the original permission form. In the margin of a typed catalog, Tang's mother had written, "Two signatures for a full stop; one voice for an emergency pause." The emphasis on the word full had been added later in red ink. Zhiyuan remembered a door in the south tunnel with two circles linked by a line. He had mistaken it for a company logo. Now it looked like a diagram of two separate controls that had to be used together.

吳姓圖書館員有些勉強地接待兩人。箱子裡裝著學校童謠集、舊舞臺照片，以及一卷沒有標籤的錄音帶。吳先生說，這卷錄音經市府研究員批准複製，卻沒人找得到原始同意書。在打字目錄的空白邊緣，唐澄母親寫著：「完整停止需要兩個簽名；緊急暫停只要一個聲音。」其中「完整」二字後來被紅筆特別強調。知遠想起南隧道那扇畫著兩個圓圈與連線的門。自己一直把它當成公司標誌，現在才覺得那是兩個獨立控制端必須一起使用的示意圖。

They listened to the tape in a small room above the stage. A cheerful voice began with nonsense about stars and dancing chairs. Children laughed. Then the background changed. A frightened engineer said, "Passive storage will continue even if the active system resets." Tang's mother's voice answered, "Who owns the recorded memories?" The engineer replied, "No person can claim possession of another person's week." The tape needle began to bounce under a continual low hum. A distant bell cut across the conversation. Wu stopped the machine, visibly disappointed that the recording was not merely a children's show. Tang asked him to play it again. On the second hearing they noticed two short taps before the bell, exactly like the circles on the old door.

他們在舞臺上方的小房間播放錄音。開頭是歡快聲音，說著星星和跳舞椅子的無意義台詞，孩子們笑個不停。接著背景改變。一名害怕的工程師說：「被動儲存即使在主動系統重置後也會繼續。」唐澄母親問：「錄下來的記憶屬於誰？」工程師回答：「沒有人能宣稱擁有別人的一個星期。」錄音帶的指針隨著持續不斷的低頻嗡鳴上下跳動，遠處鐘聲接著蓋過談話。吳先生停下機器，明顯失望它不只是兒童節目。唐澄請他重播。第二次聽時，兩人注意到鐘聲前有兩下短敲擊，和舊門上的兩個圓圈正好相符。

Zhiyuan tried to accomplish a simple test before leaving. He asked Tang to describe the tape's last ten seconds while he stood outside the room. Then he returned and gave his own description. They differed on the rhythm of the second tap. The disagreement amused Wu, who suggested both might be tired. Zhiyuan did not mind. He had spent fifty-seven deaths chasing perfect memories, and the mismatch proved why independent witnesses mattered. Neither observer could turn a guess into a fact merely by speaking with confidence. He asked the librarian to make two sealed copies and record each person's interpretation without correcting either one. Wu agreed, though his approval would last only until Friday's reset.

離開前，知遠想完成一個簡單測試。他站在房外，請唐澄描述錄音最後十秒，隨後回來說出自己的版本。兩人對第二下敲擊的節奏有不同記憶。吳先生覺得好笑，猜他們都累了。知遠不介意。他經過五十七次死亡，總在追逐完美記憶；這個差異反而證明獨立見證者為什麼重要。任何一人都不能只憑自信，把猜測說成事實。他請圖書館員製作兩份封存副本，分別記錄兩人的理解，不要替任何一方修正。吳先生同意了，雖然他的核准只會持續到星期五重置之前。

As they left the old theater, Tang unfolded a note she had hidden behind the tape case. Her mother had written, "If they call me a murderer, ask who ordered the final playback." Zhiyuan's first reaction was to deny that anyone could accuse a recording technician of such a crime. Then he remembered his own forged approval. The language of blame was already being prepared for both families. Tang looked at the evening sky, where the first star was visible above the roof. She said she did not need a story in which her mother was innocent by magic. She needed a record that could survive honest questions. Zhiyuan agreed. They had uncovered neither the killer nor the mechanism, but they had found a rule they could test: two independent witnesses, never one convenient story.

走出舊劇院時，唐澄展開一張藏在錄音帶盒後方的紙條。母親寫著：「如果有人叫我殺人犯，就去問是誰下令播放最後那段錄音。」知遠第一反應是否認有人會指控錄音技師犯下這種罪，接著想起自己那份偽造核准。有人早已替兩個家庭準備了推卸責任的說法。唐澄望向傍晚天空，屋頂上方已有第一顆星。她說自己不需要一個靠魔法證明母親無辜的故事，她需要能承受誠實質疑的紀錄。知遠點頭。他們還沒找到兇手或裝置機制，卻找到一條可以驗證的規則：兩位獨立見證者，而非一套方便的說詞。
