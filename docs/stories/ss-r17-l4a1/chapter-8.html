油漆底下的筆跡

Handwriting Under the Paint

我已經重複死在星期五十七次 · 第 8 / 25 章 · 3000–4000 · 英中對照 · 校訂中

By Tuesday afternoon the pair had returned to the school named on the mistaken envelope. A distinguished retired teacher welcomed them into a workshop where children learned basic craft skills. The wall displayed a riddle about a river that carried both water and memory, beside a painting of the children's ancestors. Beneath a strip of fresh paint, Tang noticed familiar handwriting. She gently scraped one edge with permission and uncovered a warning: "Do not launch the second circuit while the southern hose is open." The teacher said the words had been left by a visiting technician eight years earlier. He had covered them after the school received an angry letter from the agency, which called the warning a misunderstanding. Contrary to that claim, the old instruction described a real crush hazard if the service door closed during a flood. The teacher's guilt was obvious. He had believed silence would keep the children safe.

星期二下午，兩人回到寫在誤寄信封上的學校。一位受人尊敬的退休教師帶他們走進工藝教室，孩子們在這裡學習基本手作。牆上掛著一道謎語，問哪條河同時承載水與記憶，旁邊還畫著孩子們的祖先。唐澄在新油漆底下發現熟悉的筆跡。得到許可後，她輕輕刮開邊緣，露出警告：「南方水管開啟時，不得啟動第二電路。」教師說那是八年前來訪技師留下的文字，學校收到機構的警告信後，他把它蓋了起來，信裡稱警告是一場誤會。但與這種說法相反，舊指示確實記載，若洪水期間服務門關閉，可能造成擠壓傷害。教師的愧疚顯而易見；當時他以為保持沉默就能保護孩子。

The school's old equipment room held a hose, several identical connectors, and a box of notebooks. One page described a test that involved redirecting water around a narrow culvert. A lousy photocopy had nearly hidden the diagram. A line drawn with a single stroke divided two conditions: pump on, circuit off; pump off, circuit on. Zhiyuan recognized a practical rule behind the simple diagram. An electric loop might be safe in isolation yet dangerous when water supplied another path. The teacher offered to help them compare the dates of the school visit with the agency's public reports. Tang was careful not to involve the students directly. Their curiosity was valuable, but the children had not chosen the risk. The notebooks belonged to the school, and copies required the teacher's agreement.

學校舊器材室裡放著水管、幾個一模一樣的接頭，以及一箱筆記本。其中一頁描述一項實驗：將水改道繞過狹窄涵洞。品質很差的影印本差點把圖表遮住。一筆畫出的線把兩個條件分開：幫浦開、電路關；幫浦關、電路開。知遠看出簡單圖示背後的實際規則：電路單獨運轉時或許安全，但水會替電流提供另一條危險路徑。教師願意幫他們核對來訪日期與機構公開報告。唐澄小心避免讓學生直接參與。孩子們的好奇心寶貴，卻沒有選擇承擔風險。筆記本屬於學校，複製必須取得教師同意。

A mother visiting the workshop asked whether the emergency plan included people in late pregnancy. Her concern made the test's meaning less abstract. The official route required a steep climb and had no rest area; even if it remained dry, it might not be defensible as a universal exit. The teacher added that a neighbor recovering from a stroke could not manage those stairs either. Zhiyuan had memorized the route's length and width over dozens of loops. He had never considered how a route changed when the people using it changed. He asked the teacher to suggest alternative corridors suitable for slower travel. They marked two possible paths, then wrote clear limitations beside each rather than declaring either one safe.

一位到工房的母親問，緊急方案是否包含懷孕後期的人。她的顧慮讓測試的意義不再抽象。官方路線需要爬陡梯，也沒有休息區；即使路面保持乾燥，也未必能合理宣稱它適合所有人。教師補充，附近一名中風後正在復原的鄰居也無法走那些樓梯。知遠在數十次循環裡背熟路線長寬，卻從未想過使用者不同，路線的意義也會改變。他請教師提出適合慢速通行的替代走廊。他們標記兩條可能路徑，再在旁邊寫明限制，而不是直接宣稱安全。

The teacher brought out a folder from an attic shelf. It contained an old launch schedule and an agency letter that had been sent after the first school demonstration. The letter praised the children's involvement while ordering the staff to stop discussing the water circuit. Its signature was different from the one on Zhiyuan's forged approval, but the ink and mechanical spacing looked identical. Tang photographed both documents for comparison. A former classmate of the teacher, now a city technician, recognized the paper stock as equipment supplied only to the bell-tower laboratory. He agreed to visit on Wednesday, provided his name remained private until the documents were independently confirmed. The investigation was widening, and with it came a duty to protect people who might lose work for helping.

教師從閣樓架子拿出資料夾，裡面有舊啟用日程，以及首次校園展示後機構寄來的信。信一面稱讚孩子參與，一面命令教職員不要再討論水路電路。署名與知遠那份偽造核准不同，但墨色和機械字距看來相同。唐澄拍下兩份文件以便比對。教師一位如今任職市府技師的舊同學認出那種紙張，只供鐘塔實驗室使用。他答應星期三來看，但要求在文件獨立確認前保密姓名。調查範圍正在擴大，而保護可能因協助而失業的人，也成了他們的責任。

As the workshop closed, the teacher gave Tang a small painting made by her mother when she was a student. Its river was striped with blue and silver, a pattern the old laboratory later adopted for its warning labels. Tang stared at it with a look Zhiyuan could not read. She told him that her mother had hated being treated as someone who only translated other people's words. The painting proved she had been involved in the original design discussion. That did not establish what had happened to her, but it changed the questions Tang was ready to ask. Before leaving, she wrote a suggestion on the teacher's noticeboard: "Keep the original note visible, even when the explanation is unfinished." The teacher left the paint scraper beside the message.

工房關門前，教師交給唐澄一幅她母親學生時代畫的小畫。畫裡的河流有藍銀相間的條紋，舊實驗室後來將相同圖樣用作警告標籤。唐澄凝視著畫，神情讓知遠看不透。她說母親最討厭別人以為她只會翻譯別人的話。這幅畫證明母親參與過最初的設計討論。它尚不能說明母親發生了什麼，卻改變了唐澄想問的問題。離開前，她在教師公告板寫下一句建議：「就算解釋還不完整，也請保留原始筆記。」教師把刮漆刀留在那句話旁邊。

What warning was hidden beneath the paint?

  1. The school should replace all its desks.
  2. The second circuit should not launch while the southern hose was open.
  3. Children were forbidden to study rivers.

Why did they list limitations beside the alternative paths?

  1. They wanted to avoid pretending a route was safe for everyone.
  2. They wanted to hide the maps from the teacher.
