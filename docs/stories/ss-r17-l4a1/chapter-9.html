The Restaurant Receipt

Tuesday evening found them in a government building with a rusty elevator and a stone fireplace that had not been used for years. A bald records clerk named Wen kept the public appointment books. He was surprised when Tang asked about the minister's visits to the bell-tower laboratory. "That curiosity has consequences," he warned. "The last applicant who asked was removed from the list." Tang replied that they wanted to understand a schedule, not accuse a person without evidence. People dependent on those records deserved accurate answers. Wen showed them a page marked VACANCY beside the engineer Chen's departure. Beneath it was the name of a machinery supplier that had taken over the project. Zhiyuan recognized the company from the forged approval stamp. The replacement had been arranged before Chen resigned.

星期二傍晚，他們來到一棟政府大樓，電梯已生鏽，石造壁爐多年未用。禿頭的文姓檔案職員管理公開預約簿。唐澄問起部長拜訪鐘塔實驗室的紀錄，他相當驚訝。「這種好奇心會有後果，」他警告，「上次提問的申請人被從名單除掉了。」唐澄回答，他們只是想了解日程，並非沒有證據就指控別人。依賴這些紀錄的人應得到準確答覆。文先生拿出一頁紀錄，陳禾離職旁標註「職缺」。下面寫著接手計畫的機械供應商。知遠認出那家公司，就是偽造核准印章所屬的公司。更換承包商的安排竟早在陳禾辭職前就完成。

They went across the street to a small restaurant where a menu offered lobster and pasta. Zhiyuan ordered soup; he had once died in this restaurant during a Friday blackout and could not bear to sit near the kitchen. Tang noticed him studying the exits. She asked whether the city's newspaper forecast for Friday still predicted heavy rain. It did. The result of the relay failure would arise from ordinary water and electricity, not only from the strange time device. Wen joined them after his shift, carrying a quotation from a maintenance contract. The supplier promised to restore the entire emergency network for less money, but the fine print excluded independent flood tests. That omission explained the remarkably low price.

他們到街對面的小餐廳吃飯，菜單上有龍蝦與義大利麵。知遠點了湯；他曾在某次星期五停電時死在這家餐廳，現在無法忍受坐在廚房旁。唐澄注意到他一直看出口，便問報紙對星期五的天氣預報是否仍顯示大雨。答案是肯定的。繼電站故障的結果不只與詭異時間裝置有關，也來自普通的水與電。文先生下班後加入，帶來一份維修合約報價。供應商承諾用更少經費修復整套緊急網路，但細則排除了獨立洪水測試。這項遺漏解釋了異常低廉的價格。

Wen said the supplier's founder had published an autobiography describing himself as the savior of city transport. A passage declared that he could halt any operation with a single call. In reality, the maintenance contract gave him no authority over the public evacuation system. That power remained with the municipal agency. Tang suspected that the division of responsibility was deliberate: when something failed, each office could blame the other. The subsequent public notices differed in their explanations, sometimes citing rain, sometimes a software fault. Zhiyuan copied three dates from the records and asked Wen to sign that he had seen the same entries. Wen hesitated, then agreed on the condition that the original book stayed in his office.

文先生說供應商創辦人出版過自傳，自稱城市交通的救星。書裡一段文字宣稱，他一通電話就能停止任何運作。實際上，維修合約並沒有給他控制公共疏散系統的權限，那項權力仍在市府機構。唐澄懷疑這種責任分割是刻意的：出了問題，兩邊就能互相推諉。後來的公告解釋各不相同，有時歸咎大雨，有時說是軟體故障。知遠抄下紀錄中的三個日期，請文先生簽名證明看到相同條目。文先生猶豫後同意，條件是原始簿冊必須留在辦公室。

On the walk back to the station, Zhiyuan saw a poster for a city's annual seafood association dinner. He nearly laughed at the ordinary life that continued around their investigation. Families discussed meals, students argued about homework, and buses ran their usual routes. The repeating week could feel like an enormous prison to two people with memory, yet most residents still believed Friday would lead to Saturday. Tang said their job was not to destroy that expectation but to protect it. They had to avoid an early public accusation that might violate privacy or lead people into untested escape routes. A careful investigation would take longer than a dramatic announcement. They still had three days.

走回車站時，知遠看到城市海鮮協會年度晚宴的海報，差點因身邊照常運作的生活而笑出來。家庭討論晚餐、學生爭論作業、公車依平常路線行駛。對保有記憶的兩個人來說，重複的一週像座巨大監獄；但多數市民仍相信星期五之後就是星期六。唐澄說，他們的工作不是毀掉這份期待，而是保護它。他們必須避免過早公開指控，造成隱私侵犯，或把人趕向尚未測試的出口。謹慎調查比戲劇性的宣告更耗時間。距離星期五還有三天。

When they reached the tram stop, Wen called with one final detail. The contract's attached schedule had a missing page numbered seventeen. The following page began with the phrase "consequent emergency authorization." Someone had removed the page that explained which event would activate that authorization. Zhiyuan considered a direct search of the contractor's office, then remembered the warning about his forged signature. Breaking into a building would give the company a genuine reason to accuse him. Tang proposed a less dramatic route: find the invoice that paid for the missing work. Money would not explain time travel, but it might reveal who requested the dangerous shortcut. They agreed to visit the public finance desk first thing Wednesday, before the safety assembly began.

抵達電車站時，文先生打電話補充最後一個細節。維修合約附件的日程缺少第十七頁，下一頁開頭寫著「因此產生的緊急授權」。有人拿走了解釋什麼事件會啟動授權的頁面。知遠想直接搜查承包商辦公室，隨即記起自己的偽造簽名。如果闖入建築，對方就真有理由指控他。唐澄提出不那麼戲劇性的方式：先找付款發票。金錢不能解釋時間旅行，卻能揭露誰要求危險捷徑。兩人約好星期三一早先到公共財務櫃臺，趕在安全集會開始前行動。
