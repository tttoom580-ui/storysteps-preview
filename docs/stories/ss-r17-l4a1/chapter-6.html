The Engineer Who Left

我已經重複死在星期五十七次 · 第 6 / 25 章 · 3000–4000 · 英中對照 · 校訂中

Tuesday began with a thin sheet of fog over the northern hills. Zhiyuan and Tang had traveled before sunrise because the retired engineer, Chen He, worked a short shift at a cable railway. A conductor pointed them toward a workshop filled with old cord, copper plates, and boxes of mineral powder used to clean contacts. On the wall hung a faded anniversary photograph from the railway's hundredth year. A label called the first station a monument to the last local dynasty. Chen smiled at that description. "A railway does not live through history because its sign is impressive," he said. "It survives because somebody checks the brakes every morning." He offered them coarse tea and asked why two strangers wanted to discuss the bell tower rather than trains.

星期二開始時，北方山丘籠罩著一層薄霧。知遠與唐澄天亮前就出發，因為退休工程師陳禾現在在纜車鐵道做短班工作。一名列車長指引他們到工房，那裡堆著舊纜繩、銅板和清潔接點用的礦物粉末。牆上掛著鐵道百年週年的褪色照片，標籤將第一座車站稱為地方末代王朝的紀念物。陳禾看了笑說：「鐵道能留下來，不是因為招牌漂亮，而是每天早上有人檢查煞車。」他端出味道粗澀的茶，問兩個陌生人為何想談鐘塔，而不是火車。

Tang said her mother had recorded the laboratory's final rehearsal. Chen's smile vanished. He asked to hear a few seconds of the tape before he would explain anything. Zhiyuan played the part about passive storage. Chen listened twice and said the device was originally designed to study how a city could recover from disaster. The first evaluation measured changes in the frequency of warning sounds. A later campaign promised more: officials wanted to rewind the whole emergency response until they obtained a perfect result. "I did not devise that goal," Chen said. "We were told to make an imitation of a rehearsal, not to turn people's lives into repeated trials." Tang asked whether he knew the machine could reset time. He answered that he had seen the laboratory clocks return to an earlier hour during a controlled test.

唐澄說母親曾錄下實驗室最後一次排練。陳禾的笑容立刻消失。他要求先聽幾秒錄音，才願意解釋。知遠播放被動儲存的部分。陳禾聽了兩遍，說裝置原本是為了研究城市遭遇災難時如何復原。第一輪評估測量警報聲的頻率變化，後來的宣傳活動卻承諾更多：官員希望反覆倒轉整個緊急反應，直到得到完美結果。「那不是我想出的目標，」陳禾說，「我們被要求製作演習的模擬版本，不是把人的生命當成反覆試驗。」唐澄問他是否知道機器能讓時間重置。他回答，自己確實在受控測試中看過實驗室時鐘回到較早時刻。

Chen drew a diagram of two witness channels and one central memory store. He said a single witness could order an emergency pause, but two independent confirmations were needed to end a trial permanently. The store would retain only an evaluation summary from each repetition; human memory was supposed to remain unchanged by the test. To Zhiyuan this was the missing explanation. Two people might remember because their minds had somehow been connected to the witness channels. But Chen could not assure him that the connection was safe, nor did he understand why it had become permanent. "Every system has a limit," he said. "You need to identify yours before trying to be courageous." He pointed to a small mark indicating an eight-second interruption window.

陳禾畫出兩條見證者通道與一個中央記憶庫。他解釋，一名見證者可以下令緊急暫停，但永久結束測試必須有兩項獨立確認。記憶庫每次重複只保存評估摘要；原本的設計根本不打算讓人類記憶受到影響。知遠覺得這補上了關鍵解釋：兩個人之所以記得，可能是腦中的記憶意外連接到見證通道。但陳禾無法保證這種連接安全，也不明白為何會持續存在。「每個系統都有極限，」他說，「勇敢嘗試之前，先找出自己的極限。」他指向一個標示八秒中斷時間的小記號。

The conversation was interrupted by a diligent mechanic carrying a tray of foam covered in dark particles. He said the contact plates had been losing power every Friday evening since the town's newest safety equipment went online. Chen examined the particles and reflected that the railway's old cables were not directly connected to the municipal system. Their failure suggested electrical interference traveling through the valley. Zhiyuan asked whether a pulse could move along abandoned metal lines. Chen said yes, but that an ordinary rubber cord should not conduct it. They tested a short sample behind a shield. The sample remained cool. That result complicated Zhiyuan's theory that any nearby wire would kill them. The problem was narrower, which made it possible to investigate.

一名勤奮的技師端著覆滿深色顆粒的泡沫托盤進來，打斷談話。他說，自從城裡新安全設備啟用，接點板每到星期五傍晚就會失去電力。陳禾檢查顆粒後沉思，鐵道的舊電纜沒有直接接上市政系統，因此故障可能是沿山谷傳來的電力干擾。知遠問脈衝能不能沿廢棄金屬線傳播。陳禾說可以，但普通橡膠繩不應導電。他們在防護板後測試一小段材料，樣品沒有升溫。結果讓知遠原先認為所有附近電線都致命的推測變得複雜；問題其實範圍較小，因此也更有機會查清。

Before they left, Chen asked about their labor on the previous loops. Had they tried to save themselves or to test the whole city? Zhiyuan admitted he had mostly tried to reach the control room before Friday. Chen called that understandable psychology, not failure of character. Then he gave them a page from the original operating manual. Its last paragraph stated that no trial should continue if the number of needy residents excluded from a safe route increased. Tang read the sentence aloud. It was not a formula for stopping time. It was a condition the current system appeared to ignore. She folded the page carefully. On Wednesday they would compare it with the official safety assembly's public report. If the two disagreed, the disagreement itself could become their first defensible challenge.

離開前，陳禾問他們前幾輪付出的努力，是想救自己，還是想測試整座城市。知遠承認，他大多只想在星期五前衝進控制室。陳禾說那是可以理解的心理反應，並非品格失敗。接著他拿出原始操作手冊的一頁，最後一段寫著：若被安全路線排除的弱勢居民增加，就不得繼續測試。唐澄念出這句話。它不是停止時間的公式，卻是現行系統似乎忽略的條件。她小心折好頁面。星期三，他們將拿它與公開安全集會的報告比對；如果兩者矛盾，矛盾本身就能成為第一項可辯護的質疑。
