The Third Copy

我已經重複死在星期五十七次 · 第 3 / 25 章 · 3000–4000 · 英中對照 · 校訂中

Before noon, Zhiyuan and Tang reached a narrow archive above the river. A mild wind moved a twig across the steps. The building had been constructed as an earthquake barrier, not as an office, and the iron door looked brutal enough to survive a flood. An old intelligence officer named Qiu managed the files after retirement. He offered tea but would not open the restricted shelves. Tang thanked him with genuine gratitude and asked whether the archive had received any accidental deliveries from the municipal laboratory. Qiu blinked. He said the department had sent three copies of an emergency route evaluation. Two had been collected by officials. The third remained behind because a messenger had written the wrong number on its envelope.

中午以前，知遠和唐澄抵達河岸上方一座狹窄的檔案庫。溫和的風吹著一根小樹枝滑過階梯。這棟建築原本是地震防護設施，不是辦公室；厚重鐵門看來足以扛住猛烈洪水。退休情報官邱先生負責管理檔案。他端茶招待，卻不肯打開限制閱覽的架子。唐澄真誠向他道謝，問市立實驗室是否曾把文件誤送過來。邱先生眨眨眼，說部門送來三份緊急路線評估。兩份已被官員取走，第三份因信差寫錯信封編號，意外留下。

The third copy contained a composition of diagrams, rainfall predictions, and a short note about a device beneath the bell tower. Someone had evaluated the south tunnel as unsafe whenever the river level rose above a red mark. The official route poster ignored that condition. A separate page showed a grave risk: an electric relay would keep sending power to the tunnel doors during a flood. Zhiyuan had spent many Fridays trying to choke off the water with emergency gates, but those gates depended on the very power supply that might fail. A philosopher could argue about whether knowledge changed fate; Zhiyuan needed a working switch. Tang photographed the page, although both knew the photograph would disappear with the week.

第三份副本裡有圖表組合、降雨預測，以及一段關於鐘塔地下裝置的短註記。文件評估指出，當河水高過紅線，南隧道就不安全。官方路線海報卻忽略了這項條件。另一頁指出更嚴重的風險：洪水期間，電力繼電器仍會持續替隧道門供電。知遠先前很多個星期五都想用緊急閘門阻水，卻沒發現那些閘門正依賴可能失效的同一套供電系統。哲學家可以爭論知識能否改變命運；知遠需要的是一個真正能運作的開關。唐澄拍下頁面，雖然兩人都知道照片會隨一週結束而消失。

Qiu said his former colleagues had a tendency to neglect problems that could not be solved before a public ceremony. The ministry wanted to announce reform, and the mayor wanted photographs of smiling workers. Some reviewers feared that questioning the route would offend influential people. "That does not excuse them," Zhiyuan said. "If we spend our energy seeking revenge, we will miss the next warning." Tang was surprised by his restraint. In earlier loops, she had watched him shout at guards and rush alone into offices. This time he asked Qiu for the names of the people who had reviewed the third copy. Qiu answered with three names, then underlined one in blue: an engineer who had resigned after the flood-relay warning was removed.

邱先生說，舊同僚有個傾向：凡是在公開典禮前解決不了的問題，就當成不存在。部會想宣布改革，市長想拍下微笑員工的照片。有些審查者怕質疑路線會冒犯有權勢的人。「這不能作為藉口，」知遠說，「但如果我們把力氣全花在報復，就會錯過下一次警訊。」唐澄對他的克制感到意外。先前幾輪，她看過他怒罵警衛、獨自衝進辦公室。這次他只請邱先生說出審查第三份副本的人名。邱先生說了三個名字，並用藍筆畫下一位工程師：他在洪水繼電器警告遭刪除後離職。

The engineer lived near the mountainous northern edge of town. An old map said the journey took an hour, but Qiu warned that the hillside bus often ran late. Tang urged Zhiyuan to go now. He refused to abandon the archive until they understood why the third copy had survived. They inspected the envelope and discovered a second address written beneath the first. It belonged to a small school where children studied the river. On the back, in faded ink, was a sentence: "The witness is not the machine." The statement did not explain the loop, yet it resembled a correction to an earlier mistake. Zhiyuan wrote it in large letters on his notebook cover, then spoke it three times so he could remember it after another reset.

那名工程師住在城北山區邊緣。舊地圖寫著車程一小時，但邱先生提醒，山坡公車經常誤點。唐澄催知遠立刻出發。他卻不願在弄清第三份副本為何倖存前離開。他們檢查信封，發現原地址下方還寫著第二個地址，指向一所讓孩子研究河流的小學。背面褪色的墨水寫著：「見證者不是機器。」這句話尚未解釋循環，卻像是在糾正一項舊錯誤。知遠把它用大字寫在筆記本封面，再念出三遍，確保下一次重置後仍記得。

At the exit, Qiu suddenly remembered one more detail. The engineer had once said that a second human witness was required before the bell-tower device could complete its final cycle. Qiu had dismissed the warning as a figure of speech. Now Tang's face grew still. Zhiyuan looked from her to the file. He asked whether she had heard that phrase before. "Yes," she said after a long silence. "But I heard it from someone who died before this week began." For the first time, the shadow of another death lay between them. Zhiyuan wanted to demand an answer. Instead he closed the folder carefully. A clue offered under pressure could be as unreliable as an official poster. They would speak about the dead person on the bus, away from Qiu's crowded shelves.

出口處，邱先生突然記起另一個細節。那名工程師曾說過，鐘塔裝置要完成最後一輪，必須有第二名人類見證者。邱先生當時以為只是比喻。唐澄的臉色卻瞬間凝住。知遠在她和文件間來回看，問她以前是否聽過這句話。「聽過，」她沉默許久才說，「但告訴我的人，在這個星期開始前就已經死了。」另一樁死亡的陰影，第一次橫在兩人之間。知遠很想逼她回答，卻只是小心合上資料夾。被壓力逼出來的線索，可能和官方海報一樣不可靠。他們決定在公車上談死者的事，離開邱先生堆滿文件的書架。
