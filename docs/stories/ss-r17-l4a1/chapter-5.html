The Door That Counts People

我已經重複死在星期五十七次 · 第 5 / 25 章 · 3000–4000 · 英中對照 · 校訂中

On Monday evening the city held a reception at the old opera house. Guests admired decorations made from primitive gears, while a screen promoted the new emergency system as a triumph of human conscience. The influential director of the safety agency, Hong Jiping, welcomed a member of congress and several business owners. He talked about the fundamental duty of government to protect the public. Zhiyuan knew the speech almost word for word from earlier loops. Tang, meanwhile, watched the doors. Each time a guest entered, a counter above the stage added one to its display. But when a cleaner entered through the side passage, the number did not change. The system could see important visitors and ignore the workers who kept the building running.

星期一傍晚，城市在舊歌劇院舉辦接待會。來賓欣賞用原始齒輪製成的裝飾，螢幕則宣傳新的緊急系統是人類良知的勝利。安全機構主管洪季平歡迎一名議員和幾位企業主，談論政府保護人民的根本責任。知遠在前幾輪聽過這場演說，幾乎能逐字背出。唐澄則盯著門口。每當來賓走進，舞臺上方的計數器就加一；但清潔員從側門進來，數字卻沒有變。系統看得到重要賓客，卻無視維持建築運作的工人。

The cleaner was named Mei. She told them she had worked here since before the agency adopted its new equipment. She cherished the backstage rooms because her father had once built scenery for the theater. The director discouraged her from asking questions about the locked basement. "It's a technical matter," he said, smiling for a photographer. His behavior horrified Tang more than an open threat would have done. A public system had been allowed to classify people by their invitations rather than by whether they needed help. Tang asked Mei to accompany them downstairs and to tell them exactly what she saw. Mei considered the request, then agreed on the condition that nobody would use her name without permission.

清潔員叫阿梅。她說自己從安全機構採用新設備之前就已在這裡工作。她珍惜後臺房間，因為父親曾替劇院搭建布景。主管勸她別過問鎖住的地下室。「那是技術問題，」他一邊對攝影師微笑一邊說。他的行為比公開威脅更令唐澄震驚。公共系統竟然按邀請函分類人，而不是看誰需要幫助。唐澄請阿梅陪他們下樓，並如實說出看到的事。阿梅想了想，同意的條件是，任何人未經允許不得使用她的名字。

At the lower landing, someone had painted a witch beside a wizard on the wall, relics of an old children's performance. Beneath them was a steel door displaying two circular spaces for identification. A robber might have tried to force it. Zhiyuan remembered such an attempt from his fifteenth loop, when the lock had sent an alarm across the whole building. Instead he studied the interaction between the two circles and the visitor counter upstairs. He asked Mei to stand in one marked area while he stood in the other. Nothing happened. When Tang added a written witness number from the archive, a narrow message appeared: SECOND CONFIRMATION REQUIRED. They had reached a rule, not yet a key.

地下樓梯平臺的牆上畫著一名女巫和一名巫師，是舊兒童演出的遺跡。畫下是一扇鋼門，設有兩個圓形身分辨認區。搶匪也許會想直接硬闖。知遠想起第十五次循環裡，有人試過撬門，結果警報傳遍整棟樓。他這次改為觀察兩個圓圈與樓上訪客計數器之間的互動。他請阿梅站在一個標記內，自己站另一個，卻沒有反應。唐澄加入檔案中的書面見證編號後，窄小顯示器出現一行字：「需要第二次確認」。他們碰到了規則，卻還沒拿到鑰匙。

The sound of footsteps came from the stairs. Hong appeared with two guards and accused them of endangering the reception. He called their search a shameful attempt to create terror. Zhiyuan explained that people without invitations had been left out of the evacuation count. Mei gave her own account rather than waiting for him to speak for her. Hong said the outcome of the event was the only thing that mattered: no guest had been harmed. Tang asked whether the cleaners counted as guests. For a moment the director had no answer. Then he told them to leave. Their attempt to examine the basement ended without a discovery they could carry away. But Mei had seen the message, and she could describe it independently.

樓梯間傳來腳步聲。洪季平帶著兩名警衛現身，指控他們危害接待會。他說這是製造恐慌的可恥行為。知遠解釋，沒有邀請函的人被排除在疏散計數之外。阿梅自己說出所見，不等他替自己發言。洪季平說活動結果才最重要：沒有賓客受傷。唐澄問清潔員算不算賓客。主管一時答不出來，隨後叫他們離開。他們沒有帶走任何地下室資料，但阿梅親眼見到那行訊息，也能獨立描述。

Outside, a little boy asked Mei whether the old theater had really been home to a wizard. She laughed and said the only magic she knew was watching dusty scenery become a beautiful stage. Zhiyuan found the comparison strangely useful. The system did not create people; it created a narrow picture and called the picture complete. In earlier weeks he had tried to defeat it by reaching the control room alone. Tonight he had gained something harder to measure: a witness who was free to disagree with him. As the reception lights went dark, Tang checked the clock. It was still only Monday. They had four days before Friday, and the next clue lay with the engineer who had quit when the first warning was erased.

外頭一個小男孩問阿梅，舊劇院是否真的住過巫師。她笑說自己唯一見過的魔法，就是看滿是灰塵的布景變成美麗舞臺。知遠突然覺得這個比喻很有用。系統不是創造人，而是製造一幅狹窄的畫面，再宣稱那就是全部。前幾輪，他總想單獨抵達控制室並打敗它。今晚他得到更難衡量的東西：一名可以自由反對他的見證者。接待會燈光熄滅，唐澄看了看時鐘。現在還只是星期一。他們距離星期五有四天，而下一個線索，在那名因警告被刪除而離職的工程師手上。
