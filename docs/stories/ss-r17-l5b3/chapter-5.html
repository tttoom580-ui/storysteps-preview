虛假夏日花園

The Garden of False Summers

記憶拍賣所 · 第 5 / 25 章 · 4000–5000 · 英中對照 · 校訂中

Shen Ke visits a rehabilitation clinic for people harmed by illicit extractions. The auction room smelled of expensive perfume, though the merchandise was made of other people’s grief. Shen Ke watched the crowd move through the Neon Archive District and noticed how quickly fear became a rumor. Mara Qi wanted to act before the rumor hardened into policy. They spread the documents across a clean surface, placing dated facts on one side and guesses on the other. It was slower than making a bold announcement, but that small division gave them a way to ask questions without accusing innocent people. Nothing in the room could safely be ignored.

沈柯走訪收容非法擷取受害者的復健診所。 拍賣廳香水氣息濃重，然而商品是別人的悲傷。 沈柯看著人群穿過霓虹檔案區，注意到恐懼轉變成謠言有多快。齊瑪拉想在謠言變成政策前行動。兩人把文件攤在乾淨桌面，一邊放有日期的事實，另一邊放推測。這比發表強硬聲明慢，卻能在不指控無辜者的情況下追問。房間裡沒有什麼能安全地被忽略。

Three patients recall the same garden from different viewpoints. The detail mattered because it could be checked without reading anyone’s private thoughts. Mara Qi traced the ordinary steps that might have produced it, then asked what observation would prove their explanation wrong. Shen Ke made a separate copy and marked the time of collection. They had already learned that a convincing story and a reliable record were different things. One could persuade a room; only the other could endure a difficult question from someone outside it.

三名病患從不同角度記得同一座花園。 這個細節重要，是因為不用偷看任何人的私密想法也能查驗。齊瑪拉追出可能造成它的普通步驟，再問什麼觀察會推翻這項解釋。沈柯另存一份副本，記下收集時間。兩人已明白，動聽的故事與可靠紀錄是兩回事。前者能說服滿屋子的人，後者才能承受局外人的艱難質問。

When neurologist Idris arrived, Mara Qi offered a chair rather than a demand for testimony. The pause mattered. A witness could be useful without owing an investigator every detail of a private life. Shen Ke explained how the information would be stored, who could read it, and when it could be withdrawn. Once the rules were clear, the conversation became practical. They discussed routes, dates, signatures, and objects that could be examined by someone who had never met the people in the room.

神經醫師伊德里斯抵達時，齊瑪拉先遞上椅子，而不是要求作證。這份停頓很重要。證人即使提供線索，也不欠調查者自己全部的私生活。沈柯說明資訊會如何保存、誰能查閱、何時可以撤回。規則清楚後，談話變得務實。他們討論路線、日期、簽名與物件，這些都能由從沒見過當事人的人重新查驗。

Collector Orson had a familiar argument: a large institution could not survive if every ordinary person questioned its decisions. Shen Ke disagreed, not because institutions were always wrong, but because power made errors expensive for those unable to challenge them. Mara Qi refused to let the argument become personal. They requested a copy of the applicable rule and proposed a small independent test. If the official claim was true, the test should strengthen it. If it was false, the public deserved to know.

奧森收藏家提出熟悉的說法：若每個普通人都質疑決策，大型機構便無法運作。沈柯不同意，並非因為機構永遠錯，而是權力會讓無法申訴的人為錯誤付出巨大代價。齊瑪拉拒絕讓爭論私人化。他們要求適用規則的副本，並提出小型獨立測試。如果官方說法正確，測試理應使它更有力；若不正確，大眾就該知道。

By dusk the stakes had become visible beyond the papers. Someone had to keep the ordinary work of the Neon Archive District going while others argued over what the records meant. Shen Ke divided the tasks, leaving a clear path for anyone who wanted to step away. Mara Qi checked the safest route, and neurologist Idris reminded them that a clever plan could still fail people who had never agreed to be part of it. They changed the plan before asking anyone to take that risk.

黃昏時，紙張之外的風險已經可見。當其他人爭論紀錄意義時，仍有人得維持霓虹檔案區的日常工作。沈柯分配任務，也為想退出的人保留清楚道路。齊瑪拉檢查最安全的路線，神經醫師伊德里斯提醒大家：聰明計畫仍可能傷害從未同意參與的人。他們先修改計畫，再請別人承擔風險。

On a second sheet, Shen Ke found an old cross-reference index. Its headings were not proof of any theory; they were search terms left by previous workers. Entry 1 listed “constitutional”, “democrat”, “episode”, “exaggeration”, “fleet”. Entry 2 listed “greed”, “herb”, “incentive”, “massive”, “peasant”. Entry 3 listed “rattle”, “recession”, “recruit”, “retail”, “smash”. Entry 4 listed “substantial”, “tactic”, “threshold”, “verdict”, “widespread”. Mara Qi insisted that a label could not replace a witness, an observed result, or a decision that people had actually approved. The index remained in the archive as a way to retrace the team's work, not as a shortcut to a verdict.

另一張紙上，沈柯找到舊交叉索引。標題並不能證明任何理論，只是前人留下的搜尋詞。第1欄列著“constitutional”, “democrat”, “episode”, “exaggeration”, “fleet”。第2欄列著“greed”, “herb”, “incentive”, “massive”, “peasant”。第3欄列著“rattle”, “recession”, “recruit”, “retail”, “smash”。第4欄列著“substantial”, “tactic”, “threshold”, “verdict”, “widespread”。齊瑪拉堅持，標籤不能取代證人、實際觀察的結果，或當事人真正同意的決定。這份索引留在檔案中，方便後人回查工作，而非作為跳到結論的捷徑。

Shen Ke stood beneath the auction clock while buyers whispered the value of childhoods. The displayed memory seemed intimate, but he could not treat an image as proof that he had lived it. Idris explained the difference between a lived neural trace, a copied recording, and a manufactured composite. Mara offered to preserve the item’s biological key under seal. That was less exciting than bidding, and far more useful to someone looking for the truth.

沈柯站在拍賣時鐘下，聽買家低聲評估童年的價格。展示記憶雖然親密，他卻不能把影像當作自己親歷的證明。伊德里斯解釋親身神經痕跡、複製錄影與人造拼接品的不同。瑪拉願意封存物件的生物來源金鑰。這比競標無趣，卻更有助於尋找真相的人。

This was still the beginning. Shen Ke could have pretended to understand the whole system after one strange discovery, but the ordinary people around his deserved better than certainty performed for applause. He wrote one modest promise at the bottom of the page: nothing would be changed without a way to measure the consequence and a way for those affected to refuse. Mara Qi signed beside it. Neither expected the promise to remain easy.

故事仍在起點。沈柯原可在一次古怪發現後假裝理解整套系統，但身旁普通人值得比博掌聲的篤定更好的對待。她在頁尾寫下一句謹慎承諾：任何改動都要有量測後果的方法，也要讓受影響者有拒絕之路。齊瑪拉在旁簽名。兩人都不認為承諾會一直容易。

When the signal ended, nobody moved for several seconds. Then Mara Qi checked the final reading and gave Shen Ke a tired nod. The result was smaller than the bold promises circulating outside, but it was real. neurologist Idris asked who would maintain the improvement after the investigators left. That question changed the room. They began dividing responsibility among people who would still be present next month, rather than awarding credit to whoever spoke most loudly today.

訊號停止後，有幾秒鐘誰也沒動。接著齊瑪拉檢查最後讀數，疲憊地向沈柯點頭。結果比外頭流傳的豪言小，卻是真的。神經醫師伊德里斯問調查者離開後誰來維護改善。這個問題改變了整間屋子。眾人開始把責任分給下個月仍會在場的人，而不是把功勞給今天喊得最大聲的人。

That night Shen Ke returned to the beginning of the chapter in his notebook. A few hours earlier the problem had seemed to demand an immediate victory; now he understood why the next decision would require patience. Mara Qi brought tea and sat beside the window without asking for reassurance. They had not solved everything, but they had preserved a useful trail for the people who came after them. In the quiet, the work felt less like a contest and more like a promise.

那晚沈柯翻回筆記本本章最初的那一頁。數小時前，問題似乎要求立刻獲勝；此刻她才懂得下一個決定為何需要耐心。齊瑪拉端茶坐到窗邊，沒有要求安慰。他們尚未解決一切，卻為後來的人保留了可用線索。在寂靜中，工作不像競賽，而更像承諾。
