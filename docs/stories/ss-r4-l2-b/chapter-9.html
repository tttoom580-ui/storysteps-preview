舊報紙上的新問題

凌晨四點，咖啡還是熱的 · 第 9 / 25 章 · 1000–2000 · 英中對照 · 校訂中

用互動版查詞、朗讀與收藏

One afternoon Mrs. Lin invited Yun and Ren to read an old newspaper article about Blue Window. It described the café's first shopkeeper, a woman who had opened her door to late workers during a great flood. The article called the shop an important part of the town's historical street. Yet the photograph showed a different entrance and a much wider room. “A beautiful story is not a legal rule,” Mrs. Lin warned them. The landlord had asked for evidence that the café's night hours were worth keeping. Yun felt a sudden fear that their work would be judged by someone who had never sat there at midnight.

一天下午，林姨請芸和任讀一篇關於藍窗的舊報紙報導。文章寫著第一任店主是位女性，當年大水災時，她曾為夜班工作者開門。文章稱咖啡店是老街的重要一部分，但照片裡的出入口不同，房間也比現在寬。「美麗的故事不是法律規定，」林姨提醒。房東要求他們拿出證據，證明夜間營業值得保留。芸忽然害怕，他們的工作最後會由一個從未在午夜坐過這裡的人來評價。

Ren opened a new chapter in their record book. Each daily page would show guest numbers, safety checks, and any problem that came up. Yun suggested a second section for guests' words, but only if they wished to share them. A child who had visited with his father drew a blue window using a crayon. Mrs. Lin put the picture beside the old painting on the wall. “This is an addition,” she said, “not a replacement.” Yun liked that. The shop could remember its past without pretending that nothing had changed in eighty years.

任在紀錄本裡開了新的一章。每日日誌都會記錄客人數量、安全檢查，以及出現過的問題。芸提議增加第二部分，讓願意分享的客人留下話語。之前跟父親來過的一名孩子用蠟筆畫了一扇藍窗。林姨把畫掛在牆上舊畫旁。「這是增加一件作品，」她說，「不是取代舊的。」芸很喜歡這說法。小店能記得過去，卻不必假裝八十年間什麼都沒變。

To improve the system, Ren visited the town office and asked how businesses could report late-night safety information. He learned that some rules were different for shops that served food after midnight. A staff member gave him a clear list. Yun checked it and found one item they had missed: the emergency light above the back door did not shine brightly enough. She wanted to replace it at once. Mrs. Lin agreed, unless the electrician found another danger that needed attention first. The careful plan made Yun less afraid, because fear was easier to face when it became a task.

為了改善制度，任去了鎮公所，詢問商家如何申報深夜營業的安全資訊。他得知午夜後提供食物的店家，有些規則不同。承辦人給了他一份清楚清單。芸一項一項核對，發現他們漏掉一件事：後門上方的緊急照明不夠亮。她想立刻更換。林姨同意，但如果電工發現更急迫的危險，就必須先處理。詳細計畫讓芸比較不害怕，因為恐懼變成工作項目後，就比較容易面對。

The record book also brought a small surprise. A puppy belonging to the florist next door often slept near the front step. Some guests stopped to pet it, while others walked around because they were frightened of dogs. Ren placed a clear sign asking people to keep the path open. Yun thought the sign looked cold, so she added a friendly drawing and the words, “Please leave space for everyone.” The florist approved and promised to keep the puppy inside during busy hours. The problem was solved without making an enemy of a neighbor.

紀錄本也帶來一個小驚喜。隔壁花店養的小狗常睡在前門台階附近。有些客人停下摸牠，也有人怕狗而繞道。任放了一塊明確告示，請大家保持通道暢通。芸覺得牌子太冷冰冰，便加上友善的插畫，以及「請替每個人留出空間」的字句。花店老闆同意，答應尖峰時段讓小狗待在室內。問題解決了，也沒有把鄰居變成敵人。

When the week ended, Yun and Ren reviewed the notes. One night had been busy; another had been almost empty. A broken light had made them safer after they noticed it. There were also pages that showed nothing special had happened. “Those pages matter,” Ren said. “A quiet night is not a failure.” Yun thought about how much worse a storm could have been without the café. Their record could not promise that every future night would go well, but it could show what they did when things went wrong. Mrs. Lin asked them to keep writing until the landlord's visit.

週末時，芸和任一起檢查紀錄。有一晚非常忙，另一晚卻幾乎沒人。壞掉的燈被發現並換掉後，店裡更安全了。也有幾頁記錄顯示，那晚沒有特別的事發生。「那幾頁也重要，」任說。「安靜的晚上不是失敗。」芸想到，如果沒有這間咖啡店，暴雨時的情況可能更糟。他們的紀錄無法保證未來每個夜晚都平安，卻能呈現出問題出現時他們如何處理。林姨請他們一直寫到房東來訪。

Ren said that if the night team hoped to succeed, each partner in the work needed a clear role. Mrs. Lin should not carry every cost alone, and the workers should not be left to guess her decisions. Yun agreed. She added a place in the record book for questions that had not yet been answered. A blank space, she said, was more honest than a number made up to look complete.

任說，如果夜班團隊希望成功，每一名合作夥伴都需要清楚的角色。林姨不該獨自承擔所有成本，員工也不該只能猜測她的決定。芸同意，還在記錄本裡加上待解答問題的欄位。她說，留下空白比捏造一個看似完整的數字更誠實。

That morning, Yun stayed after work to paste the safety map beside the entrance. Ren held the paper while she fixed the last nail. He joked that she was becoming the café's chief keeper of straight lines. “Then you must be the keeper of careful numbers,” she answered. They stood together, looking at the map and the stories on the wall. Yun had begun to understand why she wanted the shop to survive. It was not only the money for her school fees. It was the chance to build a place where careful work and ordinary kindness could stand beside each other.

那天早上，芸下班後留下來把安全地圖貼在入口旁。任扶著紙，她固定最後一顆釘子。他開玩笑說她快成為咖啡店的直線總管了。「那你就是仔細數字的總管，」她回答。兩人並肩看著地圖與牆上的故事。芸開始明白自己為何希望小店能活下去。這不只是為了賺學費，也是為了有機會打造一個讓細心工作與平凡善意並肩存在的地方。
