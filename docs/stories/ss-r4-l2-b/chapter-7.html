午夜的傾聽者

The Midnight Listener

凌晨四點，咖啡還是熱的 · 第 7 / 25 章 · 1000–2000 · 英中對照 · 校訂中

At exactly midnight, a woman entered Blue Window carrying a small box of old china cups. Her name was Mrs. Han, and she had once been a radio listener who called every evening program. Now she lived alone and found the nights long. She asked Ren to write the café's address clearly on a card because she had taken the wrong bus twice. Yun set down a plate of toast and saw a silver necklace in the box. Mrs. Han said it had belonged to her sister, who had worked in this street when the café was young. She did not ask for pity, only a place to tell a story.

午夜整點，一名女子抱著一盒舊瓷杯走進藍窗。她叫韓女士，以前常在晚上打電話參加廣播節目，是忠實聽眾。如今她獨居，覺得夜晚很長。她請任把咖啡店地址清楚寫在卡片上，因為自己已經搭錯兩次公車。芸端來吐司，看見盒裡有條銀項鍊。韓女士說，那是姊姊留下的，姊姊曾在這條街工作，當時咖啡店還很新。她不求同情，只希望有地方能說個故事。

Her sister had been an expert at repairing cameras. A small lens from her first camera lay beside the necklace. Mrs. Han said the two sisters had wanted to open a shop together, but life had taken them in different directions. “I felt like a prisoner of other people's plans,” she said, then smiled at her own dramatic words. “Of course I was free to choose. It simply took me years to notice.” Yun did not turn the memory into advice. She asked what had made the sister happy. Mrs. Han replied, “She liked showing people things they had walked past without seeing.”

她的姊姊是修理相機的專家。項鍊旁放著一枚姊姊第一台相機的鏡片。韓女士說，姊妹倆曾想一起開店，但人生把兩人帶往不同方向。「我那時覺得自己像被別人的計畫囚住，」她說，隨即對誇張的說法笑了笑。「當然我一直有選擇的自由，只是花了好多年才注意到。」芸沒有急著給這段回憶下結論。她問姊姊什麼時候最快樂。韓女士答：「她喜歡讓人看見那些平常走過卻沒注意的事。」

Ren suggested creating a small memory corner where guests could leave a picture and a sentence, but none would be shown without permission. Mrs. Han loved the idea and asked to help with the form. Yun sorted the old cups by size while Ren wrote the rules. One cup had a naughty drawing of a cat, another a blue flower. “I can explain the origin of each one,” Mrs. Han offered. Yun thought the whole set would make a pleasant display. Ren warned that guests might not want their names beside their stories. They agreed to let people choose what to share.

任提議設一個小小的回憶角，讓客人留下照片和一句話，但沒有得到同意就不能公開。韓女士很喜歡，還主動幫忙設計表單。芸把舊杯子按大小分類，任則寫規則。有一只杯子畫著調皮的貓，另一只有藍花。「每只杯子的來歷我都能講，」韓女士說。芸覺得整套杯子會是很舒服的展示。任提醒，有人不願意把名字和故事放在一起。他們同意讓每個人自己決定要分享多少。

A customer who taught at the local college saw the display and asked whether it was a history project. Yun answered that it was first a place for listening. She had no special role as a writer or a teacher. She only knew how a good meal sometimes made people brave enough to speak. The teacher said that none of the stories should be taken as public property. Ren agreed. He wrote a clear line on the form: a guest could remove a story at any time. Mrs. Han read the words twice and gave him a grateful nod. “That,” she said, “is what respect looks like.”

一名在地方學院教書的客人看見展示，問這是不是歷史計畫。芸回答，它首先是一個讓人被傾聽的地方。她不是作家或老師，也沒有特別的身分。她只知道好好吃一頓飯，有時會讓人勇敢到願意開口。老師說，這些故事都不該被當成誰都能取用的公共財。任同意，在表單寫明：客人隨時可以撤下自己的故事。韓女士把那句話讀了兩次，感激地點頭。「這就是尊重的樣子，」她說。

When the shop grew quiet, Mrs. Han invited them to share a late-night toast. “To the people who are not here,” she said, raising her cup, “and to the people who still are.” Yun noticed that Ren seemed moved but did not speak. After Mrs. Han left, he told Yun that his father had once collected old photographs of the town. Since his illness, the boxes had remained closed. “I am afraid I will find things I do not understand,” he admitted. Yun did not offer to open them for him. “If you ever want company while you look,” she said, “you can ask.”

店裡安靜下來時，韓女士邀兩人一起舉杯。「敬那些不在這裡的人，」她舉起杯子說，「也敬仍然在這裡的人。」芸注意到任似乎被打動了，卻沒說話。韓女士離開後，任告訴芸，父親以前收集過小鎮的老照片。父親生病後，那幾箱照片一直沒打開。「我怕看到不懂的事情，」他坦白。芸沒有主動說要替他打開。「如果你哪天想要有人陪著看，」她說，「可以問我。」

Ren found an old radio in his father's box and showed Yun how its soft beat marked the start of a program. The actual sound was rough and full of noise, but Mrs. Han recognized it at once. She said the station had once read letters from people working at night. Yun asked whether the old recordings were public. Mrs. Han did not know, so they left the machine alone rather than playing a stranger's voice without permission.

任在父親的箱子裡找到一台舊收音機，讓芸聽節目開場時柔和的節拍。實際聲音粗糙又帶雜訊，韓女士卻立刻認出來。她說電台以前會讀夜班工作者寄來的信。芸問那些舊錄音能不能公開播放，韓女士也不確定。於是他們暫時把機器放回去，不在未經允許時播出陌生人的聲音。

That morning, Yun left the café with a new thought about her own plans. She had spent months believing that a good future meant working alone until no one could question her ability. The stories in the china cups suggested another way: people could be independent and still ask to be heard. Ren walked beside her to the bus stop and asked if she would teach him to make the bread she served that night. She answered yes, but only if he promised to wash the dishes afterward. “A fair price,” he said. She smiled all the way home.

那天早上，芸帶著對未來的新想法離開咖啡店。過去幾個月，她一直相信好日子就是一個人努力到沒人能質疑自己的能力。那些瓷杯裡的故事卻顯示了另一條路：人可以獨立，也可以希望有人傾聽。任陪她走到公車站，問她願不願意教自己做今晚供應的麵包。她答應，但條件是他得洗完全部餐具。「合理的價錢，」他說。她一路笑著回家。
