天亮前的婚禮

A Wedding Before Dawn

凌晨四點，咖啡還是熱的 · 第 11 / 25 章 · 1000–2000 · 英中對照 · 校訂中

用互動版查詞、朗讀與收藏

At two in the morning, a woman in a wedding dress entered Blue Window with mud on her shoes. She was not running away from her wedding. She was looking for a small place to hold one. Her partner was an emergency nurse, and their friends all worked different hours. A large party would have been impossible. “We want coffee, bread, and five people,” she explained. “Could we use your table after the night shift ends?” Yun raised an eyebrow. Ren asked about the date and the fee for food. Neither made a promise before asking Mrs. Lin, but both wanted to help.

凌晨兩點，一名穿婚紗的女子走進藍窗，鞋子沾滿泥巴。她不是逃婚，而是在尋找一個能舉辦小婚禮的地方。她的伴侶是急診護理師，朋友們上班時間都不同，根本不可能辦大型宴會。「我們想要咖啡、麵包，還有五個人，」她解釋。「夜班結束後，能借用你們的桌子嗎？」芸挑起眉。任問清日期和餐點費用。兩人都沒有在問過林姨之前就答應，卻都很想幫忙。

Mrs. Lin agreed to a short ceremony if the couple included the staff's rest time in their plan. The bride said a priest who had known her family would come, but he needed a safe path from the bus stop. The road near the forest was narrow, and people sometimes lost their way at night. Ren drew a map with a clear sign at each turn. Yun offered to author the menu card, using the couple's own words instead of a long speech. “We can be skilled at baking,” she told Ren, “but they should tell us what their day is meant to be.”

林姨答應讓他們辦一場簡短儀式，條件是安排時必須顧及員工的休息時間。新娘說，一位熟識家人的神父會來，但他需要從公車站走安全的路。靠近林地的路很窄，夜晚常有人迷路。任畫了地圖，每個轉彎處都做了明確標示。芸主動執筆寫菜單卡，但用新人的話，而不是一篇長演說。「我們也許很會烤麵包，」她對任說，「但這一天的意義應該由他們自己決定。」

On the evening before the wedding, the couple had a disagreement about music. One wanted an old love song; the other preferred silence after years of working in noisy wards. Yun listened and asked them to solve the puzzle together. The answer was simple: a short song before the vows, then quiet coffee afterward. Neither person had to win. Ren watched the conversation with interest. He had grown up believing that a good relationship meant never making someone upset. Now he began to see that honest differences were not always signs of trouble. Sometimes they were a chance to choose together.

婚禮前一晚，新人對音樂有了不同意見。一人想放舊情歌，另一人多年在吵雜病房工作，儀式後只想安靜。芸聽完，請兩人一起解開這道難題。答案很簡單：誓言前播放一小段歌，之後就安靜喝咖啡。沒有人非贏不可。任很有興趣地看著這段對話。他從小以為好關係就是永遠不讓對方不高興，現在開始理解，坦誠的差異未必是麻煩，有時只是一起做選擇的機會。

At three, a crow cried from the roof, and the waiter from the restaurant next door came to help carry chairs. He was pale from working late but happy to be included. Ren asked him to guard the door against noise from passing trucks, not to stop people from entering. A stranger asked if the café was opening for a public show. Yun explained that it was a private wedding, not a stage for anyone's curiosity. The stranger apologized. “You were not rude,” the waiter told her. “You protected a moment that belonged to someone else.” Yun thanked him and returned to the bread.

三點時，一隻烏鴉在屋頂叫著，隔壁餐廳的服務生來幫忙搬椅子。他因工作到很晚而臉色蒼白，卻很高興能參與。任請他留意門口經過的卡車噪音，不是攔住進門的人。一名陌生人問店裡是否要公開表演。芸解釋這是私人婚禮，不是讓大家好奇圍觀的舞台。陌生人道歉。「妳沒有無禮，」服務生說。「妳是在保護屬於別人的時刻。」芸謝過他，回去顧麵包。

On the day of the wedding, Yun measured the space between the tables. She moved one chair just a centimeter, then stepped back to check the path. The couple still had a small disagreement over where to place the flowers, so she invited them both to decide. Their plan would include a quiet song but neither a long dance nor a large crowd. Ren wrote each choice on a simple page and asked the couple to review it. They looked at the page together, crossed out one extra decoration, and thanked him for letting them change their minds. To Yun, the small corrections were part of the celebration, not signs that anything had gone wrong.

婚禮當天，芸量了桌子之間的空間。她把一張椅子移動一公分，再退後檢查動線。新人對花應該放哪裡仍有些分歧，她便請兩人一起決定。計畫會包含一段安靜歌曲，但既不會有冗長舞會，也不會有大量賓客。任把每項選擇寫在簡單清單上，請新人再核對。他們一起看清單，刪掉一項多餘裝飾，並謝謝任允許他們改變心意。對芸來說，這些小修正也是慶祝的一部分，不表示事情出了錯。

The ceremony lasted fifteen minutes. The bride and her partner promised to make room for one another's changing lives. No one spoke about love as a prize given to the person who worked hardest. They spoke about returning, listening, and asking for help. The priest gave a small blessing, and the guests ate warm rolls while morning light reached the tables. Ren stood beside Yun, their arms almost touching. “It was not grand,” he whispered. “It was enough,” Yun replied. For the first time she wondered what kind of promise she might want to make someday, and to whom.

儀式只持續十五分鐘。新人答應在彼此變動的人生裡留出空間。沒有人把愛情說成努力最多的人才能得到的獎品。他們談的是回到身邊、彼此傾聽、願意求助。神父給予簡短祝福，客人們吃著溫熱麵包，晨光慢慢照上桌面。任站在芸旁邊，兩人的手臂幾乎碰在一起。「一點也不盛大，」他輕聲說。「但很足夠，」芸回答。她第一次開始想，自己有一天會願意許下怎樣的承諾，又是對誰。
