消失的補給箱

The Missing Box

凌晨四點，咖啡還是熱的 · 第 3 / 25 章 · 1000–2000 · 英中對照

用互動版查詞、朗讀與收藏

The next week began under a cloudy sky. A delivery box of cups and cereal should have reached Blue Window before midnight, but nothing arrived. Yun made an attempt to call the supplier, only to find that the number had changed. Ren checked the entrance camera. It showed a runner on a scooter stopping outside, lifting a box, and leaving again. “Someone might think he tried to cheat us,” Yun said. “But we should not decide the cause before hearing him.” Ren nodded. In the general rush of city nights, one small mistake could look like a plan to hurt someone.

新的一週從陰雲密布的天空開始。一箱紙杯和穀片應該在午夜前送到藍窗，卻遲遲不見蹤影。芸試著打給供應商，才發現電話號碼已經換了。任檢查門口監視畫面，看見一名騎機車的快遞員停在外頭，抱起箱子又離開。「有人可能會認為他想騙我們，」芸說。「但我們還沒聽他說明，不能先認定原因。」任點頭。在城市忙亂的夜晚，一個小失誤看起來也可能像刻意傷人。

They found a link to the delivery company's new office through the town government website. The office was still open because its drivers operated at night. A tired clerk explained that the box had been taken to a bakery with the same street number. Yun knew that bakery: her old classmate Lei worked there. She called him, embarrassed to ask for a favor so late. Lei laughed and said they had already noticed the strange box. A little silver earring lay on top of it, probably dropped by the driver. He promised to keep both safe until the morning.

他們透過鎮公所網站找到配送公司新辦公室的連結。因為司機夜間工作，辦公室還開著。一名疲憊的職員解釋，箱子被送到門牌號碼相同的另一間麵包店。芸知道那間店；她以前的同學雷在那裡工作。她有些不好意思這麼晚打電話請人幫忙。雷笑著說，他們早就注意到那個奇怪的箱子。箱子上還有一只銀色耳環，可能是司機掉的。他答應把箱子和耳環都保管到早上。

Without the cups, the café could not continue its normal service. Yun proposed washing and using their old china cups for guests who stayed, but Ren reminded her that the washing machine needed an extra cycle. They counted their clean supply and chose a shorter menu. “No one can force us to pretend everything is fine,” Yun said. Ren posted a note at the counter explaining the delay. A woman waiting for a night bus read it and thanked them for being honest. Even when a business could not give people what they expected, it could still tell them the truth.

沒有紙杯，咖啡店就無法照常營業。芸提議把舊瓷杯洗乾淨，給內用客人使用，但任提醒她洗杯機還得多跑一輪。他們清點乾淨杯子的數量，決定縮短菜單。「沒人能逼我們裝作一切正常，」芸說。任在櫃檯貼出延誤公告。一名等夜班公車的女子看完後，謝謝他們願意說實話。就算店家暫時無法提供客人預期的服務，至少還能誠實說明情況。

At one, the scooter driver returned to search for his earring. He was only twenty, newly employed, and worried that his final warning from the company would cost him the job. He had followed an old map after his phone lost power. “I know I made a mistake,” he said, “but I did not take the box for myself.” Yun offered him tea and asked him to sit. Ren wrote down the exact address and marked the confusing entrance on a printed map. They could send a correction to the company without making the driver a public example of failure.

凌晨一點，機車快遞員回來找耳環。他才二十歲，剛開始工作，擔心公司最後一次警告會讓他丟掉飯碗。他的手機沒電後，只好照著舊地圖走。「我知道自己犯錯了，」他說。「但我沒有把箱子拿走私用。」芸請他喝茶、坐下。任記下正確地址，在印出的地圖上標出容易搞錯的入口。他們可以向公司要求修正路線，而不必把年輕司機公開當作犯錯的例子。

Yun later spoke with Lei about the night school they had both attended. He said the school offered a liberal arts course this year, but still had no useful class about running a small kitchen. “We learn how to make things,” he said, “but not how to keep a shop alive.” Yun remembered why she had left her course: her mother needed help at home, and the fees had become too heavy. She had not told Ren that part of her life. When he asked why the bakery mattered to her, she gave him the short answer. He accepted it without asking for more.

稍後，芸和雷聊起兩人以前就讀的夜校。雷說學校今年開了通識課，卻依然沒有教小廚房實際經營的課。「我們學會怎麼做產品，」他說，「卻不知道怎麼讓店活下去。」芸想起自己離開課程的原因：母親當時需要人照顧，學費也太沉重。她還沒把那段生活告訴任。當任問起她為何在意那間麵包店，她只簡短回答。任接受了，沒有繼續追問。

Before he left, the driver said he would operate his scooter only after checking the route and the battery. Yun hoped his company would correct the address too. She understood that one careful driver could reduce a mistake, but only the people in charge of the route could keep the same error from hurting the next worker. Ren marked the lesson in their own delivery book.

離開前，快遞員說以後操作機車出勤前，會先確認路線和電池。芸也希望公司能修正地址。她明白一名小心的司機可以減少錯誤，但要防止相同問題再傷害下一個人，還得靠負責管理路線的人。任把這個教訓記進店內的配送簿。

By dawn the supply problem was solved. Lei brought the box over, and the driver helped carry it to the storeroom. In return, Ren showed him the corrected address and offered to send the map to his manager. The driver gave a grateful nod. Yun wrote a short report in the shop book, making clear what they knew and what they did not. She had begun the shift angry at an unknown person. She ended it understanding that a fair system often starts with an accurate map and someone willing to listen. Outside, the clouds finally broke.

天亮前，補給問題解決了。雷把箱子送來，快遞員幫忙搬進儲藏室。任則給他看修正後的地址，表示願意把地圖寄給主管。快遞員感激地點了點頭。芸在店裡的紀錄本寫下簡短報告，清楚分開已知與未知的事。她在班初還對一個不認識的人生氣，結束時卻明白，公平的制度常常從一張準確地圖和一個肯聽的人開始。窗外，雲終於散了。
