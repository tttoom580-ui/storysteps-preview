The Recipe on the Shelf

凌晨四點，咖啡還是熱的 · 第 8 / 25 章 · 1000–2000 · 英中對照 · 校訂中

用互動版查詞、朗讀與收藏

A childhood memory came back to Yun when a guest asked for the same barbecue bread his grandmother used to make. He described a soft roll with a sweet edge and brought a photograph of children sharing it with chopsticks at a family meal. Yun said she could try, but she would not claim it was the same bread without knowing its source. The guest smiled and explained that his grandmother had kept her recipes on a high shelf. He had inherited a notebook, but one page had begun to disappear under an old water stain. He asked whether the café could help him read it.

一名客人點了祖母以前做的烤肉麵包，喚起了芸的童年記憶。客人描述那是一種邊緣帶甜味的軟麵包，還帶來一張家人聚餐、孩子們拿筷子分食的照片。芸說願意試做，但沒弄清食譜來源前，不會聲稱自己做的是同一種。客人笑了，說祖母把食譜放在高架上。他繼承了一本筆記，但其中一頁被舊水漬弄得快看不見，想問店裡能不能幫忙辨認。

Ren took the notebook with clean hands and laid it beside a smooth stone that kept the pages flat. The writing described how to load a tray and turn the bread at the right moment. In one sentence the grandmother used the old word servant to describe a helper in a rich household. Yun read it aloud, then stopped. “I do not want that word on our menu,” she said. “We can respect her cooking without copying every idea from her time.” The guest agreed. He said his grandmother had worked hard and would have liked to see people treated as equals.

任先洗淨雙手，把筆記本攤開，用一顆光滑石頭壓住書頁。字裡寫著怎樣把烤盤裝進烤箱、何時翻動麵包。有一句裡，祖母用舊時的「僕人」一詞形容富裕人家的幫手。芸念出來後停住了。「我不想把這個字放到菜單上，」她說。「我們可以尊重她的料理，不必把那個年代所有想法都複製下來。」客人同意。他說祖母努力工作了一輩子，應該也希望大家受到平等對待。

They had a choice about what the bread should mean now. The guest wanted to move forward with a version that honored his grandmother while fitting the life of the café. Yun measured the flour, and Ren found that the means listed in the notebook were simple: a bowl, a spoon, an oven, and time. There was no command to make the recipe secret. Instead, a note said, “Share it with whoever is hungry.” Yun looked at the guest. He gave her permission to write the recipe on the back of the menu with his grandmother's name.

現在，他們得決定這款麵包有什麼新意義。客人希望做一個既紀念祖母，又適合咖啡店生活的版本。芸量好麵粉，任發現筆記所列工具很簡單：碗、湯匙、烤箱和時間。裡面沒有命令誰把食譜藏起來，反而留了一句：「和任何肚子餓的人分享。」芸望向客人。對方同意她把食譜印在菜單背面，並寫上祖母的名字。

Reality arrived in the form of a broken shelf. As Ren reached for a baking tin, an old screw came loose and the shelf began to fall. Yun caught the tin while Ren held the wood. They put the heavy things down and called Mrs. Lin before trying to fix anything. “Whatever we build,” she said over the phone, “the safety of our people comes first.” Ren pointed at the weak spot with a wooden stick, marked it with a strip of red tape, and ordered a stronger support. The guest stayed to help move dishes to another table. He said his grandmother would have laughed at their serious faces.

現實卻隨著一片鬆動的架板闖進來。任伸手拿烤模時，一顆舊螺絲鬆脫，架子往下傾。芸接住烤模，任扶住木板。他們把重物放下，先打電話給林姨，沒有擅自修理。「不管我們要做什麼，」她在電話裡說，「人的安全永遠最重要。」任先用一根木棒指向脆弱處，再以一段紅膠帶標出位置，並訂購更牢固的支架。客人留下幫忙把餐具移到別桌，說祖母一定會笑他們表情太嚴肅。

By dawn the first batch was ready. Yun cut one roll in half and offered it to the guest before anyone else. He closed his eyes as he tasted it. “Not exactly the same,” he said. Yun felt a moment of disappointment, but he continued: “That is not a bad thing. This is her idea meeting your hands.” She thought about fate, and how easy it was to imagine that a life must follow one fixed plan. Her own hands, warm from the oven, suggested otherwise. Ren wrote the guest's words on a card, then asked if Yun wanted to keep it. She did.

天亮前，第一爐麵包出爐。芸切開一顆，先請客人嚐。他閉著眼咬了一口。「不是完全一樣，」他說。芸一瞬間有些失望，但他接著說：「這不是壞事，這是祖母的想法遇見了妳的雙手。」她想起命運這個詞，想到人很容易以為自己的人生只能走一條固定路。她剛碰過烤箱、仍暖暖的雙手卻告訴她不是如此。任把客人的話寫在卡片上，問芸要不要留下。她想留下。

After the first sale, Ren calculated the average cost of each roll. He kept the numbers beside the recipe so Yun could decide whether the price was fair. The bread had a gentle taste that reminded the guest of his grandmother's kitchen, though he said Yun's version was lighter. She smiled at the small difference. A living recipe, she thought, should be allowed to meet the hands that make it now.

第一批賣出後，任計算每顆麵包的平均成本，把數字放在食譜旁，讓芸決定售價是否合理。麵包味道柔和，讓客人想起祖母的廚房，雖然他說芸做的比較清爽。芸對這小小差異微笑。她想，活著的食譜應該允許自己與現在製作它的雙手相遇。

Later Yun learned that the notebook had come from a family who lived beyond the old bridge. The man whom they had helped was only visiting town for a few days. He thanked the workers for not treating his grandmother's recipe as a strange object from the past. The bread would be sold for a fair price, with the family's name on the menu and a free copy of the recipe available on request. For Yun, the meaning of the evening was clear. Tradition did not have to become a rule that stopped people from changing. It could be a gift that traveled onward.

後來芸得知，筆記本來自住在老橋另一邊的家庭，而他們幫忙的男子只回鎮上住幾天。他感謝店員沒有把祖母的食譜當成奇怪的古物。麵包將以合理價格販售，菜單會寫出家族姓名，客人想要也能免費索取食譜。對芸來說，這一晚的意義很清楚：傳統不必成為阻止人改變的規定，也能像禮物一樣繼續傳下去。
