穿過屋頂的雨

Rain Through the Roof

凌晨四點，咖啡還是熱的 · 第 12 / 25 章 · 1000–2000 · 英中對照 · 校訂中

用互動版查詞、朗讀與收藏

The roof worker was delayed by another job, and the next rain arrived before he could come. Water fell from the ceiling near the back shelf. Yun slipped while moving a box and twisted her ankle. She sat down at once, upset with herself. Ren brought a cold pack and called Mrs. Lin, who told them to close the wet area until someone could check it. A dog barked outside as a group of youth travelers passed by with their bags. One of them clicked the camera shutter and took a picture of the leaking roof, but Yun asked him not to post it without a full description of the situation.

屋頂工人被另一份工作耽擱，還沒來得及修理，下一場雨又到了。水從後方架子旁的天花板滴下。芸搬箱子時滑了一跤，扭傷腳踝，立刻坐下，氣自己太不小心。任拿來冷敷袋，打給林姨。林姨要他們關閉潮濕區域，等人來檢查。外頭有狗在叫，一群年輕旅人提著行李經過，其中一人拍下漏水屋頂。芸請他別只放一張照片上網，卻不完整說明情況。

The young traveler replied that he was writing a novel about his journey and wanted real places in it. Yun said that stories could help others understand a town, but an exact account mattered when people's work was involved. “You can say the roof leaks,” she told him. “That is true. Please also say we closed the area and called for repair.” He agreed and selected another photograph showing the safety tape. His friend offered a guava from a travel bag. Yun accepted it with a smile. She was glad the conversation had not turned into a fight about who owned the truth.

年輕旅人回覆，他正在寫一部旅途小說，想把真實地方放進故事。芸說，故事可以幫助別人了解小鎮，但牽涉到人們工作時，精確的描述很重要。「你可以寫屋頂漏水，」她說。「這是真的。也請你寫我們已關閉區域並通知維修。」對方同意，改選一張能看見安全警示帶的照片。他朋友從旅行袋拿出一顆芭樂請芸吃。芸笑著收下，很高興這段對話沒有變成爭奪誰擁有真相的爭吵。

Mrs. Lin asked Ren to prepare a protective cover for the dry equipment, but not to climb on the roof himself. He checked each kitchen item and placed a clean spoon beside Yun's chair so she could eat soup without getting up. She thanked him, then asked whether he was being kind because he felt sorry for her. Ren looked surprised. “No. You would do the same for me.” Yun remembered the easy way she had helped him learn to fry eggs. Caring for someone, she thought, did not have to mean treating them as weak. She leaned back and allowed herself to rest.

林姨請任替乾燥區的設備加上保護罩，但不准他自己爬屋頂。他逐件檢查廚具，還在芸的椅子旁放了一支乾淨湯匙，讓她不用起身就能喝湯。芸道謝，接著問他是不是因為可憐她才這麼好。任有些驚訝。「不是。換作我，你也會這樣。」芸想起自己輕鬆教他煎蛋的樣子。她心想，照顧別人不代表把對方看成軟弱的人。她靠回椅背，允許自己休息。

The waiting travelers spoke about the cultural places they had visited. One described an old wooden swing in the town park; another had seen a museum exhibit about tea. Yun listened as the dog continued to bark under the eaves. She wanted to travel someday, not because her life was bad, but because there were things beyond her usual streets. Ren noticed her interest and said he knew a quiet coastal path that was easy to walk. “Not with this ankle,” she answered. “Not today,” he agreed. The simple reply pleased her. He could imagine a future plan without turning it into an order.

等雨的旅人聊起他們造訪過的文化景點。一人形容鎮上公園的老木鞦韆，另一人說起看過的茶文化博物館展覽。屋簷下的狗仍不停叫著，芸一邊聽，一邊想自己有一天也要去旅行。不是因為現在的生活不好，而是平常走的街道之外還有很多事物。任注意到她的興趣，說他知道一條容易走的海邊小徑。「這隻腳踝可不行，」她回答。「今天不行，」他同意。這種簡單回應讓她開心；他能想像將來一起做的事，卻不把想像變成命令。

At dawn, the worker finally arrived and found the exact place where rain had entered. The damage was serious enough that the night kitchen would need to close for two evenings. Mrs. Lin was unhappy about the delay, but she refused to call the worker evil for being late. His earlier job had involved a family whose roof had fallen. Yun agreed that there was room for both disappointment and understanding. Ren wrote a clear reply to customers: drinks would be limited, and the shared table would remain open only if the dry front area was safe. They would not hide the problem.

天亮時，工人終於趕到，找到雨水滲入的確切位置。損壞程度不輕，夜間廚房得停用兩晚。林姨對延誤不高興，卻不願把晚到的工人罵成惡人。他前一個工作地點有戶人家屋頂真的塌了。芸也認為，失望與理解可以同時存在。任寫給客人的回覆很清楚：飲品將限量供應；只有前方乾燥區仍安全時，共享桌才會開放。他們不打算掩飾問題。

Upon returning to work after a short rest, Yun asked to read the roof report herself. The worker showed her which board had been replaced and which areas still needed watching. She wanted the next shift to have the same information. Ren helped put the report near the schedule, where anyone on duty could read it without searching through private messages.

短暫休息後重返工作時，芸要求親自讀屋頂報告。工人指出已更換的木板，以及仍需注意的地方。她希望下一班擁有同樣資訊。任協助把報告放在班表附近，讓值班者不必翻找私人訊息也能讀到。

When Yun was ready to go home, Ren offered his arm at the door. She took it for the step down, then let go when the ground was level. He did not look offended. “May I ask you something?” he said. “When you finish your baking course, do you want a job in a large hotel?” Yun thought for a moment. “I want to select my own work,” she said. “Maybe a bakery, maybe something smaller. I have not decided.” Ren nodded. “Then I hope the choice stays yours.” She carried those words with her all the way to the bus.

芸準備回家時，任在門口伸出手臂。她借力跨下台階，走到平地便放開，他沒有露出不高興的表情。「我可以問妳一件事嗎？」他說。「妳修完烘焙課，想去大飯店工作嗎？」芸想了一下。「我想自己選擇工作，」她說。「也許麵包店，也許更小的地方。我還沒決定。」任點頭。「那我希望選擇權一直在妳手上。」她一路帶著這句話走向公車站。
