街角的小推車

The Little Street Cart

凌晨四點，咖啡還是熱的 · 第 10 / 25 章 · 1000–2000 · 英中對照 · 校訂中

用互動版查詞、朗讀與收藏

To test whether Blue Window could serve more guests without crowding the room, Mrs. Lin allowed a two-night street cart trial. The cart was available from the local festival office, but the workers had to return it in proper condition. Yun chose a short menu of hot tea, biscuits, and steamed yam. Ren measured the length of the counter and found that it would fit against the wall. A market seller offered to lend them a painted sign featuring a fox, a goose, and a snail. The three animals were meant to show that quick and slow guests were all welcome.

為了測試藍窗能否服務更多客人，又不把店裡擠滿，林姨准許試辦兩晚的街角推車。推車可以向地方節慶辦公室借，但必須妥善歸還。芸選了短菜單：熱茶、餅乾和蒸地瓜。任量好檯面長度，確認它可以靠牆擺放。一名市場攤販願意借他們一塊彩繪招牌，上面有狐狸、鵝和蝸牛。三隻動物代表走得快或走得慢的客人都受歡迎。

They needed glue to fix a loose paper label, but Ren warned Yun not to place it near food. He found a safer way to attach the sign with string. The seller asked for a small fee per unit of tea sold, which Mrs. Lin wrote down in the account book. Yun felt proud of the gradual growth of the idea. A month earlier, she had worried only about making the next drink. Now she was thinking about clean water, safe streets, fair work, and whether a new style of service would fit the guests they wanted to help.

他們本想用膠水固定鬆掉的紙標籤，任卻提醒芸不要在食物旁使用。他改用繩子把牌子綁牢。攤販要求每賣出一份茶收一點租用費，林姨把費用記入帳冊。芸為這個點子逐步成長而高興。一個月前，她只擔心下一杯飲料做得好不好；現在她開始考慮乾淨的水、安全的街道、公平的工作，以及新服務方式是否符合他們想幫助的客人。

A young man carrying a small musical instrument stopped by. He played a soft tune with permission from the people standing nearby. An older woman asked whether the café was opening another branch. Yun explained that it was only a trial, not a permanent move. “Please do not ignore the people who cannot stand for long,” the woman said. Ren brought out two folding chairs, keeping the entrance clear. Later he counted how many guests used them. The numbers suggested that the chairs mattered more than the music, even though the music made the evening pleasant.

一名帶著小樂器的年輕男子停在推車前。他徵得附近人同意後，吹奏了一段柔和旋律。一名年長女子問咖啡店是不是要開分店。芸解釋這只是試辦，並非永久搬遷。「別忽略站不久的人，」女子提醒。任搬來兩張折疊椅，仍保持入口通暢。稍後，他記錄使用椅子的人數。數字顯示，椅子的重要性甚至超過音樂，雖然音樂讓夜晚很舒服。

During a quiet hour, Yun told Ren she had never taken a real vacation. She had moved between school, home, and jobs for years. Ren said he sometimes felt the same. His father was better now, but he still checked his phone every time it rang. Yun asked what he would do with one free day. “Maybe visit the sea,” he said. “Or sleep until noon without being sorry.” She laughed. “That is not a very grand dream.” He looked at her kindly. “Not every dream has to be grand. What would you do?” “Bake something just because I wanted to,” she replied.

空檔時，芸告訴任，她從來沒有真正放過假。這些年一直在學校、家裡和工作之間奔波。任說自己也常有相似感覺。父親現在好一些了，但電話一響，他還是會立刻查看。芸問他如果有一整天自由會做什麼。「也許去海邊，」他說。「或者睡到中午，完全不內疚。」她笑了。「這夢想不太宏大。」他溫和地看著她。「不是每個夢都得偉大。妳呢？」她答：「做一個只是因為自己想做而做的麵包。」

On the second night they gathered customer comments and counted the money. They had not sold very much, but they had reached several workers who had never noticed the café behind the bus stop. A few people asked for longer hours; others wanted a safer place to wait in the rain. Ren measured the distance from the cart to the door: only a few meters, but the path was uneven. He marked the dangerous stones on the map. Yun wrote congratulations to their first helper on a card and gave him a warm biscuit. He looked pleased, although the gift was small.

第二晚，他們收集客人意見並清點收入。賣得不算多，卻接觸到幾名以前沒注意過公車站後方咖啡店的夜班工作者。有些人想延長營業時間，也有人希望雨天能有更安全的等候處。任量出推車到門口的距離：只有幾公尺，路卻不平。他在地圖上標出危險石塊。芸在卡片上寫下祝賀，送給第一個來幫忙的人，還附了一塊熱餅乾。禮物雖小，對方看起來很高興。

Yun needed a little courage to show the final sales sheet to Mrs. Lin. She was proud of the cart, but the profit was small. Mrs. Lin thanked her for presenting both the good news and the problems. Ren added that the experiment had identified a safer way for guests to reach the front door. A two-night trial could not settle the whole business, yet it had turned several guesses into facts they could use.

芸需要一點勇氣，才把最終銷售表交給林姨。她為推車感到驕傲，但獲利不多。林姨感謝她同時呈現好消息與問題。任補充，實驗也找出客人到前門更安全的路線。兩晚試辦無法決定整間店的命運，卻把幾項猜測變成了可使用的事實。

The trial did not answer every question. Mrs. Lin still had to decide how much money the shop could spend, and the landlord might not care about a successful cart. But the workers had learned something important: a good service could change shape without losing its heart. Yun folded the fox-and-goose sign and put it away for another day. Ren asked whether she had enjoyed the experiment. “Yes,” she answered, “especially because we kept a record of what did not work.” He smiled. “Then perhaps we can learn our way into the future.” For once, the thought did not frighten her.

試辦並沒有回答所有問題。林姨仍得決定店裡能花多少錢，房東也未必在乎推車是否成功。但兩人學到一件重要的事：好的服務可以改變形式，而不失去核心。芸把狐狸與鵝的招牌摺好，留待下次使用。任問她是否喜歡這次實驗。「喜歡，」她說，「尤其我們還記錄了哪些事行不通。」他笑了。「那或許我們能一邊學，一邊走向未來。」這一次，這個念頭不再讓她害怕。
