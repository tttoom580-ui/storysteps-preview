凌晨四點，咖啡還是熱的 · 第 4 / 25 章 · 1000–2000 · 英中對照

On Friday the health department sent an inspector to check the café's night kitchen. Mrs. Lin asked Yun and Ren to attend the visit together. An earlier complaint had said that medicine was being stored beside food, and she wanted the matter handled in a proper way. Yun showed the inspector the shelves. The only drug in sight was a nurse's sealed medicine bag, left by mistake beside a chair. “That is private property, not kitchen stock,” she explained. Ren asked the nurse to collect it and wrote a note so the bag would not be touched again. The inspector thanked them for keeping records.

星期五，衛生部門派人來檢查咖啡店的夜間廚房。林姨請芸和任一起出席。之前有人投訴店內把藥物放在食物旁，她希望正確處理。芸帶稽查員看架子。現場唯一的藥品，是一名護理師不小心留在椅旁的密封藥袋。「那是客人的私人物品，不是廚房庫存，」她解釋。任請護理師領回，還寫下紀錄，避免再有人碰那袋藥。稽查員感謝他們有留下處理記錄。

The inspector also found a wire cage beside the eastern wall. It belonged to the garden club and held an injured bird while its wing healed. Yun knew that food and animals should not share the same room. She called the club and asked it to move the cage to the school yard. The student who arrived carried a comb in one hand and an umbrella in the other. He had been grooming his dog when he got the call. “Sorry for the trouble,” he said. Ren smiled. “The bird deserves care, and our guests deserve a clean kitchen. We can do both.”

稽查員也發現東側牆邊有個鐵籠。那是園藝社的，裡面有隻翅膀受傷、正在復原的小鳥。芸知道食物和動物不能放在同一個房間。她打電話請社團把籠子搬到校園院子。來的學生一手拿梳子，一手拿雨傘；接到電話時，他正在替狗梳毛。「不好意思添麻煩，」他說。任笑了。「小鳥需要照顧，客人也需要乾淨的廚房。兩件事都可以做好。」

At dinner time, a woman from the local gymnasium came to request a small meal service for workers finishing their exercise classes. Her group included several people with minor injuries who could not stand in a long line. Yun thought about how the customers moved through the narrow shop. She suggested placing a firm bench by the wall and leaving a clear path from the entrance. Ren drew the new layout. The woman liked the purpose of the plan: it would help anyone who needed a seat, not just her group. Mrs. Lin agreed to try the bench for one week.

晚餐時段，一名在附近體育館工作的女子來詢問，能否替運動課結束的員工提供簡單餐點。她的團體裡有幾位受了輕傷的人，不方便久站。芸想了想客人在狹窄店內的動線，提議靠牆放一張堅固長椅，並讓入口保持通暢。任畫下新配置圖。女子喜歡這個方案的目的：它會幫到所有需要座位的人，而不只她的團體。林姨同意試放一週。

Later, Yun tried to fry eggs in a shallow pan for the new evening menu. The oil made a sharp sound, and she moved the pan away from the heat. Ren was watching the motion of her hands. “You look as if the pan is part of you,” he said. She almost laughed. “It is not a magic trick. It is practice.” She had never liked being praised for talent when people forgot the hours behind a skill. Ren asked if she would encourage him to learn. She handed him a clean pan and let him begin with a low flame.

稍後，芸用淺鍋試煎新晚餐菜單的雞蛋。油發出尖銳聲響，她把鍋移離火源。任看著她的動作。「妳用鍋時像它是身體的一部分，」他說。她差點笑出來。「這不是魔術，是練習。」她向來不喜歡別人只稱讚天分，卻忽略技能背後的時間。任問她願不願意鼓勵他學。芸遞給他一只乾淨的鍋，讓他從小火開始。

The night brought a final small test. A customer arrived with a picture of a wolf and asked the staff to hang it above the quiet table. The man said it represented strength in his home region. Yun thought it was beautiful, but Ren worried the large eyes might trouble some tired guests. They did not reject the picture. Instead, they asked the man to let them place it near the front door, where there was more light and space. He agreed, and a young guest later stopped to admire it. The compromise cost nothing except a few minutes of honest talk.

當晚還有一個小考驗。一名客人帶著狼的圖畫來，希望掛在安靜桌上方。他說狼在故鄉象徵力量。芸覺得畫很美，任卻擔心那雙大眼睛會使疲倦的客人不安。他們沒有拒絕，而是問客人願不願意把畫掛在靠近大門、光線和空間都更多的地方。客人答應了，稍後還有年輕人停下欣賞。這次折衷沒花一毛錢，只花了幾分鐘的坦誠對話。

After the inspector left, Mrs. Lin told them the kitchen met the safety standard, though they had to keep the bird cage away and improve their cleaning schedule. Yun was relieved, yet she understood that safety was not a paper score. It was the daily care of small things: a clear floor, a private bag returned, a hot pan handled correctly. Ren placed the new bench by the wall, then held the door for a nurse with a heavy bag. Yun saw how closely his quiet habits matched the kind of place she wanted to help build. Neither of them spoke about it yet.

稽查員離開後，林姨說廚房符合安全標準，但鳥籠不能再進店，清潔時程也要改善。芸鬆了口氣，卻明白安全不是紙上的分數，而是每天對小事的照顧：保持地面通暢、歸還私人物品、正確拿熱鍋。任把新長椅放到牆邊，接著替背著重袋子的護理師開門。芸看見他的安靜習慣和自己想打造的地方多麼接近。兩人暫時都沒有說破。
