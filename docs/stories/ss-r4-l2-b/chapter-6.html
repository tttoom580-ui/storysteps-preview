日出前的避雨處

Shelter Before Sunrise

凌晨四點，咖啡還是熱的 · 第 6 / 25 章 · 1000–2000 · 英中對照 · 校訂中

用互動版查詞、朗讀與收藏

On the eve of the town's spring festival, a new storm crossed the western hills. The café was nearly full when a musician came in carrying a wet guitar case. Behind her was a crowd of people whose bus had stopped because water covered the sidewalk. Some wore thin jackets; one child held a toy giraffe above his head to keep it dry. Yun opened the side door and invited them toward the empty chairs. Ren checked the building's safe number of guests before bringing more stools. They could accept people for shelter, he said, but they still had to keep the doors and exits clear.

小鎮春季節日前夕，一場新暴雨越過西邊的山丘。咖啡店快坐滿時，一名音樂家抱著濕透的吉他盒走進來。她身後是一群公車停駛的乘客，因為人行道已經積水。有些人只穿薄外套，一個孩子把玩具長頸鹿舉過頭頂，不讓它淋濕。芸打開側門，招呼大家往空椅子走。任先確認建築物安全容納人數，才搬出更多凳子。他說可以收留避雨的人，但門口與逃生通道仍得保持暢通。

A town official arrived wearing a yellow raincoat. She had been sent to examine the streets and confirm which bus routes were safe. She was sensitive to questions about public safety because people had blamed her department for a flood the previous year. Yun offered her a quiet seat and asked for the latest road information, not an argument. The official pointed to a map and gave them a phone number to call if conditions changed. Ren copied the details beside the register. He knew the shop could not repair the roads, but it could help people make good choices while they waited.

一名穿黃色雨衣的鎮公所人員來了。她負責檢查街道，確認哪些公車路線安全。由於去年淹水時大家責怪過她的部門，她對公共安全問題特別敏感。芸請她坐到安靜處，詢問最新道路消息，而不是和她爭論。公所人員指著地圖，留下路況變動時可撥的電話。任把資訊抄在收銀台旁。他知道小店修不了道路，但能在等待時幫助人做出更好的決定。

The musician asked whether she could play a little music for the waiting guests. Yun said yes only after asking the others. Most agreed, but a tired man wanted quiet because he had just finished a long hospital shift. The musician understood and offered a different gift: she would write a short piece of poetry about the rain and put it on the noticeboard. The child with the giraffe asked her to include a friendly animal. She wrote about a small creature who carried a lantern through a storm, helping others find their way home. The story made the room smile without making a sound.

音樂家問能不能替等候的人演奏一點音樂。芸先徵詢其他客人，才答應。大部分人同意，但有個剛上完醫院長班的男子想要安靜。音樂家理解，改提議送另一份禮物：寫一首關於雨的小詩，貼在公告板上。抱長頸鹿的孩子請她放進一隻友善的動物。她寫了一個小生物在暴風雨中提燈，引導別人回家的故事。故事沒有發出聲音，卻讓整間店露出笑容。

At two o'clock Mrs. Lin called to ask how they were doing. Yun explained that the ceiling near the back door was leaking again, even after the earlier electrical repair. Mrs. Lin promised a roof worker would come when the weather cleared. “Safety is not a war between owners and workers,” she told them. “Tell me what must stop, and I will stop it.” Ren moved the waiting guests away from the wet corner. He was careful not to use words like emergency when the danger was under control. The best term, he thought, was simple: take care, then check again.

兩點時，林姨打來問情況。芸說後門附近的天花板又漏水了，雖然先前的電線問題已經修過。林姨答應天氣好轉就請屋頂工人來。「安全不是店主和員工之間的戰爭，」她說。「你們告訴我哪裡需要停用，我就會停用。」任把等待的人移離潮濕角落。他不想把已受控制的情況也稱為緊急危機。眼下最合適的字眼很簡單：先照顧，再檢查一次。

As the rain weakened, several guests offered to help clean. Yun chose tasks that were safe: folding blankets, gathering used cups, and checking that no one had left a bag. The official confirmed that buses would begin running again after sunrise. One woman thanked Yun for treating the group like people rather than a problem. Yun felt that a good café needed more than drinks. It needed clear rules, open eyes, and a way to remain calm when the world outside changed. She looked across the counter and found Ren already making a list of what they would improve.

雨漸小時，幾名客人主動幫忙整理。芸選了安全的工作：摺毯子、收用過的杯子、確認沒人忘記行李。公所人員表示日出後公車應能恢復行駛。一名女子謝謝芸把他們當作人，而不是麻煩。芸覺得好咖啡店不只需要飲料，也需要清楚的規則、留意細節的眼睛，以及外界變動時保持冷靜的方法。她望向櫃檯另一頭，任已經在列出需要改善的事項。

Before he went home, the child came back looking for his favorite toy. Yun could see the relief on his face when Ren returned the giraffe. The musician said the soul of her little poem had come from watching ordinary people help each other. She asked permission to take a photograph of the board, promising not to include the names of guests. Yun agreed and made sure the child's drawing was safe from the rain.

回家前，那個孩子跑回來找最喜歡的玩具。任把長頸鹿還給他，芸看見孩子臉上鬆了口氣。音樂家說，小詩的靈魂就來自看見普通人互相幫助。她徵得同意後拍下公告板，並承諾不拍進客人的姓名。芸答應，也確認孩子的畫沒有被雨弄濕。

When the last guest left, Ren found the toy giraffe beneath a chair. He ran to the door, but the child was gone. Yun suggested hanging a note without showing the child's name. The musician had left her poem behind, and its ending spoke of a warm window that stayed bright until morning. “You think we should show this to the landlord?” Ren asked. Yun looked at the chairs, still wet from people's coats. “Yes,” she said, “but the story alone is not enough. We must also show how we kept everyone safe.” He liked that answer. So did she.

最後一位客人離開後，任在椅子底下找到那隻玩具長頸鹿。他跑到門口，但孩子已經走了。芸提議張貼招領便條，不公布孩子的名字。音樂家把詩留在店裡，結尾寫著一扇溫暖的窗，直到早晨仍亮著。「妳覺得我們該把這首詩給房東看嗎？」任問。芸望著仍沾著外套雨水的椅子。「要，」她說，「但只有故事還不夠，我們也得說明怎麼讓每個人安全。」他喜歡這個回答。她自己也是。
