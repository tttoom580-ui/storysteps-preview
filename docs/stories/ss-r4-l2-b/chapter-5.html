窗上的雷聲

Thunder on the Window

凌晨四點，咖啡還是熱的 · 第 5 / 25 章 · 1000–2000 · 英中對照

The first heavy storm of the month arrived with a roll of thunder. Rain spread across the street so quickly that the café window looked like a wall of moving glass. Yun was making a bowl of noodle soup when the lights began to shake. Ren checked the electrical panel and found that one switch had become warm. He turned off that part of the kitchen at once. “We do not touch a fault we cannot understand,” he said. Yun agreed, although she hated wasting food. The guests moved away from the wet wall, and Mrs. Lin called an electrician.

這個月第一場大雷雨伴著雷聲而來。雨水迅速漫過街面，咖啡店窗戶像一整面流動的玻璃。芸正在做一碗麵湯，燈忽然閃動。任檢查配電盤，發現一個開關發熱。他立刻關閉那一區的廚房電源。「不懂的故障就不能亂碰，」他說。芸雖然不想浪費食物，仍然同意。客人們離開潮濕的牆邊，林姨則打電話找電工。

A brilliant idea came from an unlikely visitor. Mr. Gao the gardener had brought wool blankets for the garden club's evening event. He offered them to guests who were cold while the heater was off. A nurse wrapped one around an elderly man whose skin had turned pale from the rain. The man thanked her and asked whether the shop would remain open. Yun answered that safety came first, but they would offer warm water and food from the safe side of the kitchen. She paid attention to the electrician's instructions, determined not to mistake courage for careless action.

一個很棒的主意來自意想不到的訪客。園丁高先生原本替園藝社的晚間活動帶來幾條羊毛毯，現在把它們借給沒暖氣而發冷的客人。一名護理師替淋得臉色蒼白的長者披上毯子。長者道謝，問咖啡店會不會繼續營業。芸回答，安全要擺第一，但他們會從沒問題的廚房區供應溫水和食物。她仔細聽電工的指示，決心不把勇敢誤認成魯莽。

The electrician discovered that water had reached an old connection behind the refrigerator. “The problem is domestic,” he explained. “It is in this building, not in the town power line.” He showed Ren a safe method to keep the area dry until a proper repair could be made. Ren wrote every step in the maintenance book. Yun praised his careful notes, then found that they had a conflict about the menu. She wanted to serve the guests a free hot meal. He worried they did not have enough food left for everyone. Neither wanted to make a promise they could not keep.

電工發現水滲進冰箱後方老舊的接線處。「問題在這棟建築裡，」他解釋。「不是市區供電線路故障。」他教任在正式修繕前如何安全保持那個區域乾燥。任把每一步記在維護本裡。芸稱讚他記錄仔細，接著卻發現兩人對菜單有分歧。她想免費替客人做一頓熱餐；任擔心剩下的食物不夠每個人分。兩人都不願給出做不到的承諾。

They counted their supplies on the back table. The mass of dough was too small for full meals, but enough for a simple bread and soup set. Yun suggested smaller portions with an honest notice. A customer who raised roosters on a farm laughed at the size of the bread, then said it was better than leaving hungry people outside. A traveler showed them a photograph of his home in a snowy northern town and said their little café felt warmer than a fine restaurant. Yun began to discover that food could bring support without being large or expensive.

他們在後桌清點存貨。麵團的總量不足以做出每人一份的正餐，但可以做簡單的麵包配湯。芸建議縮小份量，並誠實公告。一名在農場養公雞的客人笑說麵包太小，接著又說，總比讓人餓著站在外面好。一位旅人拿出家鄉雪國小鎮的照片，說這間小店比華麗餐廳還溫暖。芸開始明白，食物不一定又大又貴，也能帶來支持。

As they worked, Ren asked why Yun had chosen baking. She told him she liked the way heat changed flour, water, and time into something a stranger could hold. She did not tell him the whole story about leaving school, but she mentioned the long months when her mother had needed help. “Whenever I could not fix a bigger problem, I could still make breakfast,” she said. Ren listened without giving advice. At last he told her that his father was recovering from an injury and sometimes needed company at medical visits. Both understood what it meant to divide time between work and home.

工作時，任問芸為什麼選烘焙。她說自己喜歡看熱度如何把麵粉、水和時間變成陌生人能捧在手上的食物。她沒有說完休學的全部故事，但提到母親曾經有很長一段時間需要照顧。「每當我解決不了更大的問題，至少還能做早餐，」她說。任沒有急著給建議，只安靜地聽。最後，他說父親正從傷勢中復原，有時需要人陪著回診。兩人都懂得在工作與家庭之間分配時間是什麼滋味。

When the guests finally ate, Yun noticed that the exhausted nurse had very little appetite. She offered a smaller portion without making the woman explain herself. The nurse thanked her, then gave the bread simple praise: it was warm, easy to eat, and arrived when she needed it. Yun treasured those words more than a grand compliment about her talent. They described what her work had actually done for someone.

客人終於開始吃東西時，芸注意到疲憊的護理師胃口不大。她改給小份食物，不要求對方解釋原因。護理師道謝，給麵包很簡單的稱讚：溫暖、容易入口，而且在正需要時送來。芸比起對天分的盛大讚美，更珍惜這幾句話，因為它們說出了她的工作真正替一個人做了什麼。

The rain slowed just before four. An electrician promised to return the next day, and Mrs. Lin closed the damaged part of the kitchen until then. The guests left slowly, some taking bread home in paper bags. Yun and Ren sat by the window with two cups of tea. They had disagreed, made a plan, and kept everyone safe without pretending to be heroes. “You are good at finding another way,” Ren said. “So are you,” Yun answered. Their hands rested on the same table, near but not touching. Outside, the wet street began to shine under the morning lights.

快到四點時，雨勢漸小。電工答應隔天再來，林姨則在那之前先關閉受損廚房區域。客人慢慢離去，有些人提著裝麵包的紙袋回家。芸和任坐在窗邊喝茶。他們意見不同過，也一起想出辦法，沒有假裝英雄，卻讓所有人平安。「妳很會找別的路，」任說。「你也是，」芸回答。他們的手放在同一張桌上，離得很近，卻沒有碰觸。窗外，濕漉漉的街道在晨光裡開始發亮。
