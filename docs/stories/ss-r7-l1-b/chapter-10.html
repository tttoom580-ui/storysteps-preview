風說現在還不能走

The Wind Says Wait

雪谷的七種聲音 · 第 10 / 25 章 · 1000以下 · 英中對照 · 校訂中

Dark clouds moved around the western hills one evening. Tavi had a button missing from her coat, but she did not go outside to look for it. The sky was changing fast. Some leaves began to fall from the trees, and the wind carried them past her window. She had a little menu of hot food on the table because Nell was coming for dinner. There were potatoes and a sweet cake. Tavi found the sound of leaves hitting the glass interesting, but she knew that a strong wind could also bring trouble. The friends agreed to stay home and watch the weather.

某個傍晚，烏雲繞過西邊山丘。塔薇外套掉了一顆鈕扣，卻沒有出去找，因為天空變得很快。幾片葉子從樹上落下，被風吹過窗邊。桌上擺著小小的晚餐菜單，奈兒要來吃飯，有馬鈴薯和甜蛋糕。塔薇覺得葉片敲擊玻璃的聲音很有趣，但她知道強風也可能帶來麻煩。朋友們同意留在屋內觀察天氣。

Nell brought a rose from her window and put it in a cup of water. She smiled, then looked at the sky and stopped smiling. The sound of wind had grown much louder. Ivo usually liked walking in the evening, but tonight he did not even ask. They had heard that some students planned to look for birds after dark. Tavi worried about them and asked a neighbor to pass on a clear warning. The message was simple: the upper path was closed for the night. No special sound was worth a trip into a storm.

奈兒帶來窗邊的一朵玫瑰，插進水杯。她先笑了，望向天空後卻收起笑容。風聲已變得更大。伊沃平常喜歡傍晚散步，今晚連問都沒問。大家聽說有些學生想在天黑後去尋鳥，塔薇為他們擔心，便請鄰居傳達清楚的警告。訊息很簡單：高處道路今晚封閉。任何特別聲音都不值得冒著暴風雨前往。

One student knocked on the door to ask why the group was not going out. Tavi invited him inside and showed him the map. The student thought courage meant going forward even when the way looked bad. Nell said that courage could also mean changing a plan when facts changed. Ivo put the missing button on the table; he had found it near the chair. Everyone laughed a little. They stayed by the warm fire and listened to the rain against the roof. It was a strong sound, but it did not tell them where spring was hiding.

一名學生敲門，問大家為什麼不出去。塔薇請他進屋，拿地圖給他看。學生以為勇敢就是路況不好也要前進。奈兒說，事實改變時願意修改計畫，也是一種勇敢。伊沃把那顆掉落的鈕扣放在桌上；他在椅子旁找到了。大家輕輕笑了，留在火邊聽雨打屋頂。聲音很大，卻不能告訴他們春天藏在哪裡。

During the storm, Tavi wrote three questions in her book. Which sounds came from wind? Which came from water? Which might be made by animals? She did not write an answer beside each question. The questions would help her listen after the storm. Nell checked the weather news, and Ivo closed the window. When everyone finished dinner, they packed the extra food for tomorrow. They knew the morning plan might change again. For now, the safest work was to wait, rest, and be ready to look at the valley with new eyes.

暴風雨期間，塔薇在本子寫下三個問題：哪些聲音來自風？哪些來自水？哪些可能是動物發出的？她沒有急著在每題旁寫答案。這些問題會幫助她在風雨後聆聽。奈兒查看天氣消息，伊沃關上窗戶。大家吃完晚餐，把剩下的食物包好留到明天。他們知道早晨的計畫也許會再改變。此刻最安全的工作就是等待、休息，準備重新看待山谷。

Late that night, the storm grew weaker. Tavi heard a low sound travel along the roof and then fade away. She wanted to open the door, but she stayed inside. A sound heard from a safe place was still a real sound. She marked the time and went to bed. The wind had not stopped the search forever. It had taught the group that patience was part of the work. Tomorrow they would check the paths before checking any new sound. That order mattered more than being the first to tell a story.

深夜，風雨漸弱。塔薇聽見一陣低聲沿著屋頂傳來，隨後慢慢消失。她想開門，卻仍待在屋裡。從安全地方聽見的聲音同樣是真實聲音。她記下時間，然後上床睡覺。強風沒有永遠阻止他們尋找，而是教會大家耐心也是工作的一部分。明天他們會先檢查道路，再聽新聲音。這個順序比搶先說故事更重要。

Before bed, Tavi looked at the potato soup left in the kitchen. She had made enough for everyone, and a little was still warm. The extra food made her smile because her friends would have a simple lunch the next day. She did not have to worry about the whole storm at once. She only needed to check the next safe step. Nell called to say the students had reached home, and Ivo said the town had closed the high road. Tavi wrote these facts beside the weather notes. After that, she put her book away. The wind outside still sounded strong, but there was no work she needed to do in the dark. Rest was part of staying ready. She went to sleep knowing that the morning could bring a new plan, and she would be able to change with it.

睡前，塔薇看著廚房剩下的馬鈴薯湯。她煮得夠大家吃，還有一些是溫的。想到朋友們明天能吃簡單午餐，她露出微笑。她不用一下子擔心整場風暴，只需要檢查下一步是否安全。奈兒打電話說學生都已到家，伊沃也說鎮上封閉了高處道路。塔薇把這些事實寫在天氣筆記旁，然後收好本子。外面的風聲仍很大，但黑夜中已沒有她必須完成的工作。休息也是保持準備的一部分。她安心入睡，知道早晨可能有新計畫，而自己也能隨之改變。
