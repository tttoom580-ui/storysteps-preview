收藏舊聲音的房間

The Room of Old Sounds

雪谷的七種聲音 · 第 8 / 25 章 · 1000以下 · 英中對照 · 校訂完成

The local museum had a small room for old sounds. Tavi borrowed a book about the valley and went there with Nell. The trip was not dangerous, and the museum stood near a train stop. Each visitor paid one dollar to help keep the room clean. The guide said anyone who could not pay could enter free. Tavi liked that idea. A visit was successful when a person learned something, not when the museum sold many tickets. Nell found an old file of notes about the first birds of spring. They were dated, so they could compare one year with another.

當地博物館有一間收藏舊聲音的小房間。塔薇借了一本介紹山谷的書，和奈兒前往。路途沒有危險，博物館就在火車站附近。每位訪客付一元協助清潔，但嚮導說付不起的人也能免費進入。塔薇喜歡這個想法。一次參觀成功與否，取決於學到了什麼，不是賣出多少票。奈兒找到一份舊檔案，記載了歷年的第一聲春鳥叫，因為標有日期，他們可以比較不同年份。

At noon, the guide asked the friends to listen to a short old recording. An animal on the tape said, “You may hear a high call or a low one. Do not make the mistake of thinking every bird must sound the same.” Tavi stopped to think. She had been waiting for the sound she remembered as a child. What if a new bird sounded different? The guide opened a box full of pictures showing local birds. He did not tell them which one would arrive first. That answer belonged to the animals and the weather, not to a museum wall.

中午，嚮導請朋友們聽一段短短的舊錄音。錄音中的動物說：「你可能聽見高音，也可能聽見低音。別誤以為每隻鳥叫聲都一樣。」塔薇停下來思考。她一直在等童年記得的那種聲音。如果今年先來的鳥不同呢？嚮導打開一箱當地鳥類圖片，沒有告訴他們哪一隻會先到。答案取決於動物和天氣，不是博物館的牆。

A small child grew bored and began to roll a wooden ball across the floor. His mother gently stopped him because another visitor was trying to listen. The guide showed him a safe place to play outside. A picture of a queen in a winter coat hung by the door, beside a fork and spoon from a very old kitchen. Ivo, who had joined them later, wondered why a fork belonged in a sound museum. The guide told him that it had been used to make a simple ringing sound at dinner. Everyday things had stories too.

一個小孩子覺得無聊，開始在地板上滾木球。母親溫柔地阻止他，因為另一位訪客正努力聽錄音。嚮導指給他一個安全的戶外遊玩處。門邊掛著穿冬衣的女王畫像，旁邊放著古老廚房的叉子和湯匙。稍後加入的伊沃好奇叉子為何在聲音博物館裡。嚮導告訴他，過去用餐時有人用它敲出清脆聲音。日常物品也有故事。

Tavi did not want to make a quick idea into a rule. She asked the guide to tell them how old the recording was and who had made it. He showed a date and a name. It was not proof about today's birds, but it was a useful piece of history. The friends listened once more. They heard both a high call and a low call in the tape. Now Tavi could hear the difference more clearly. She thanked the guide and promised to use the information with care, without calling it a new sound from this year's valley.

塔薇不想匆忙把一個想法變成規則。她請嚮導說明錄音年代和製作者。嚮導拿出日期與姓名。這不能證明今天有哪些鳥，卻是一份有用的歷史資料。朋友們又聽了一次，錄音裡同時有高低不同的叫聲。如今塔薇更能分辨差異。她向嚮導致謝，承諾小心使用資訊，不會把舊錄音當作今年山谷的新聲音。

On the train home, Nell looked through the pictures and said they still needed to hear the birds outside. Tavi agreed. A museum could help them ask better questions, but the animals in the valley would make their own sounds in their own time. She closed the book before the train reached the last stop. The day had not added a sound to the list. It had changed the way she listened. She now knew that waiting for one remembered song could make her miss another song that was already there.

回程火車上，奈兒翻看圖片，說他們仍得在戶外親耳聽鳥。塔薇同意。博物館能幫助提出更好的問題，但山谷裡的動物會在自己的時間發出自己的聲音。火車到終點前，她合上書。今天沒有為清單新增聲音，卻改變了她的聆聽方式。她知道若只等待記憶中的一首歌，可能錯過眼前已響起的另一首歌。

On the way out of the museum, a little boy said he had first thought the room would be boring. Now he wanted to ask the guide one more question. “What else can we learn from an old sound?” he asked. The guide said the recording also told them how people made and kept records in the past. “You can listen again tomorrow,” he told the boy, “and you may notice something new.” Tavi agreed. They did not need to take the recording home or call it a living bird. The boy looked through the old pictures one final time before leaving. Tavi felt that the day had given him a better way to use his curiosity. She thanked the guide for letting questions be part of the visit rather than rushing everyone from room to room.

離開博物館時，一名男孩說他起初以為房間會很無聊，如今卻想再問嚮導一個問題。「我們還能從舊聲音學到什麼？」他問。嚮導說錄音也能告訴大家，以前的人怎樣製作和保存紀錄。「明天你可以再聽，」他告訴男孩，「也許會注意到新的事。」塔薇同意。他們不用把錄音帶回家，更不能把它叫作活鳥。男孩離開前又看了一次舊圖片。塔薇覺得這次參觀讓他更懂得善用好奇心。她謝謝嚮導願意讓提問成為參觀的一部分，而不是催大家匆忙走過每個房間。
