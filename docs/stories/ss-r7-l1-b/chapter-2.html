暖石旁的會議

A Meeting by the Warm Stones

雪谷的七種聲音 · 第 2 / 25 章 · 1000以下 · 英中對照

The friends met at Nell's apartment, a small home cut into a warm hillside. Anybody could come to the meeting, so a few neighbors sat on flat stones by the door. Nell began by asking them not to guess about the weather. An old church stood across the valley, but its road was too icy today. “We can stay here,” she said. “The ground is clear, and we can hear each other.” A visiting reporter had come to learn why there were so few birds. Tavi asked him to write only what they knew. It was a serious question, not a game to win.

朋友們在奈兒的公寓開會，那是山坡裡一間溫暖的小屋。任何人都能參加，幾位鄰居便坐在門邊平石上。奈兒先請大家不要憑空猜測天氣。山谷對面有座舊教堂，但通往那裡的路今天太滑。「我們留在這裡就好，」她說，「地面安全，也聽得見彼此。」一位來訪的記者想知道為何鳥如此少。塔薇請他只寫已知事實。這是嚴肅的問題，不是要爭輸贏的遊戲。

An old deer from another country spoke next. She told them about a winter when birds arrived late. She had almost forgotten the date, but she still remembered the first small call from the hill. Ivo picked up a pen and drew three places on a piece of paper: the lower pond, the pine grove, and the meadow. Nell said they should visit the pond first. Water could change before trees did. They would take lunch, not stay away past dark, and call home if the trip changed. A small telephone lay on the shelf beside Nell's sofa.

接著，一隻來自另一個國家的老鹿說起往事。她記得有一年鳥兒到得很晚；日期幾乎忘了，但山上第一聲細小鳥叫仍記得清楚。伊沃拿起筆，在一張紙上畫出三處：低地池塘、松林及草地。奈兒建議先去池塘，因為水的變化可能早於樹木。他們會帶午餐、天黑前回來，行程變動時便打電話回家。奈兒的沙發旁架子上放著一部電話。

The reporter asked whether the town would pay for a guide. Nell explained that they needed no money for the short walk, but a guide might be needed for the high snow later. She showed a third path on the map, then crossed it out. It passed under loose rocks. “If we are late, we turn back,” she said. “If the ice is not safe, we turn back. If anyone feels tired, we stop.” Tavi listened to every word. She had wanted an answer at once, but she now saw that a good answer could take time.

記者問鎮上會不會付錢請嚮導。奈兒解釋，這段短路不需要花錢，但以後若要去高處雪地，可能得有嚮導。她指著地圖上的第三條路，隨即把它劃掉，因為那條路在鬆動岩石下方。「如果太晚，就折返；冰面不安全，就折返；有人累了，就停下。」塔薇仔細聽。她本來想立刻得到答案，如今知道好答案有時需要時間。

A little mole arrived with a bowl of hot soup. He could not see the far hill well, but he knew the sound of water under stones. He offered to describe it when they returned. Nell smiled because that was just the kind of help they needed. Here was a neighbor who could add knowledge without taking a dangerous walk. The visitors made room for him beside the fire. They did not ask him to prove anything on the spot. His experience was useful, and his comfort mattered as much as theirs.

一隻小鼴鼠端著熱湯來到。他看不清遠處山丘，卻熟悉石頭底下的水聲，願意等他們回來後協助辨認。奈兒微笑，因為這正是他們需要的幫助。眼前這位鄰居不用冒險走遠路，也能提供知識。大家在火旁讓出位子，沒有要求他當場證明什麼。他的經驗很有用，而他的舒適與其他人的舒適一樣重要。

At the end of the meeting, Tavi read the plan back to the group. She did not leave out the warnings. One neighbor said the notes sounded too careful for a search for spring. Nell answered that being careful was the reason they could go at all. The reporter put down his notebook and thanked them. Tomorrow he would walk only as far as the first safe turn. Tavi watched the neighbors go home. They had no new bird call yet, but now the valley had a clear way to look for one.

會議結束前，塔薇把計畫念給大家聽，連警告也沒有省略。有位鄰居說，尋找春天何必這麼小心。奈兒回答，正因為小心，他們才有機會出發。記者放下筆記本向大家道謝，明天他只會走到第一個安全的轉彎處。塔薇看著鄰居回家。雖然他們仍沒聽見新的鳥鳴，但山谷終於有了一個清楚的尋找方法。

After the visitors went away, Nell asked Tavi to help clean the meeting place. They folded the chairs, put away the cups, and checked the door. Tavi almost left the safety notes on the table. “Do not forget them,” Nell said. “Those notes belong to the whole plan.” Tavi put the paper in her bag and read the first line aloud once more. It said that a path must be checked on the day of a walk, not only the day before. She thought of the families who might read their report. They would need clear words and real dates, not a brave-sounding story. Ivo waited outside and kept the path free for the older deer. When all three friends reached home, they sent a short note to the others. Tomorrow's walk would begin only after the morning check. Everyone now knew what to expect.

訪客離開後，奈兒請塔薇一起打掃會議場所。他們收好椅子和杯子，也檢查了門。塔薇差點把安全筆記留在桌上。「別忘了它，」奈兒說，「那是整個計畫的一部分。」塔薇把紙收進袋子，又念了一遍第一行：步道應在出發當天檢查，而不只是前一天。她想起可能閱讀報告的家庭。大家需要清楚的字句與真實日期，不是聽起來勇敢的故事。伊沃在外頭等，並為老鹿留下通道。三位朋友都回家後，他們寄出一份簡短通知。明天早晨完成檢查後才會出發。現在每個人都知道該期待什麼。
