聽不見鳥聲的熊

The Bear Who Heard Nothing

雪谷的七種聲音 · 第 9 / 25 章 · 1000以下 · 英中對照 · 校訂中

A bear named Alden lived alone near the upper farms. He was brave on the mountain, but he had become lonely after his sister moved far away. A farmer told Tavi that Alden once knew every bird in the valley. They went to ask for his help, taking no meat or milk for wild animals along the way. They carried only their own food. Alden opened his door with a sad look. He had not heard birds for some time, and he feared that he had lost the skill to listen. Tavi said his ears were not the problem. Snow and time could change many things.

一隻名叫奧登的熊獨居在上方農地附近。他在山上很勇敢，但妹妹搬走後便有些孤單。一位農夫告訴塔薇，奧登過去認得山谷每一種鳥。三人前去請教，路上沒有帶肉或牛奶給野生動物，只帶自己的食物。奧登神情難過地開門。他已有一陣子沒聽見鳥，擔心自己失去聆聽能力。塔薇說問題未必在他的耳朵，雪和時間都可能改變許多事情。

The bear was a careful person, not someone who made up answers. He showed them a photograph of a tree with many small birds on it. A foreigner had taken the photo years ago. Alden could remember the sound, but he did not know where those birds had gone. “I want to keep this picture,” he said, “but I do not want to lose the world outside it.” Nell liked his words. They were looking for living sounds, not trying to bring back one exact day from the past. They asked whether he would walk with them on a short safe path near his home.

奧登是個謹慎的人，不會編造答案。他拿出一張樹上停著許多小鳥的照片，是多年以前一位外國人拍的。奧登記得鳥叫聲，卻不知道那些鳥去了哪裡。「我想保存這張照片，」他說，「但不想失去照片外的世界。」奈兒喜歡這番話。他們尋找的是活生生的聲音，不是要重現過去某一天。他們問奧登願不願在住家附近安全的小路上同行。

Alden said yes. Outside, they heard a small ring from a loose metal cup hanging near the wall. A singer from the village had once visited and given it to him. He laughed because that was not the bird sound he had hoped to find. “But it is a sound I know,” he said. The group stood still and listened to warm air moving along the roof. Alden pointed toward the trees. There was nothing moving on the branches, yet the wind made a thin song between them. Tavi added it to her notes as a possible wind sound, not a bird call.

奧登答應了。屋外，一只掛在牆邊的鬆動金屬杯發出輕響，那是村裡一位歌手以前送他的。他笑了，因為這不是他希望找到的鳥叫。「但這是我認識的聲音，」他說。大家靜靜聽著暖風沿屋頂流動。奧登指向樹木。樹枝上什麼也沒動，但風穿過枝間時發出細細的歌聲。塔薇把它記為可能的風聲，而不是鳥鳴。

Ivo went inside to use the toilet before they left. When he returned, he found Alden standing straighter and smiling. The bear said he would keep listening each morning, even if he heard only wind at first. Tavi told him that well-kept notes could show change over time. He did not have to solve the valley's question alone. Alden asked whose idea the search had been. “Mine at first,” Tavi answered, “but now it belongs to everyone who listens with care.” That answer made him feel less alone.

離開前，伊沃進屋使用廁所。回來時，他看見奧登站得更直，也露出了笑容。熊說他會每天早晨繼續聆聽，即使最初只有風聲。塔薇說仔細保存的筆記能顯示時間中的變化。他不必獨自解決山谷的問題。奧登問這個尋聲點子是誰想的。「一開始是我的，」塔薇回答，「現在屬於每個願意仔細聽的人。」這讓他不再那麼孤單。

They returned by the same path before the day grew cold. Alden did not join the long trip, but he agreed to tell them when the sounds near his house changed. He would write only what he heard. Tavi looked back once and saw him near his door with the photograph in his hand. He was not simply waiting for the past now. He was part of a new story that had not yet found its ending. As the valley turned blue with evening, the friends felt that their search was slowly bringing neighbors together.

他們在天冷之前沿原路回去。奧登不參加較遠的旅程，但答應只要住家附近的聲音改變，就通知大家。他只會寫下自己親耳聽到的事。塔薇回頭看見他拿著照片站在門旁。現在他不再只是等待過去，而是參與了一個尚未有結局的新故事。山谷在傍晚轉為藍色，朋友們感到這場尋聲正慢慢讓鄰居靠近彼此。

Before they left Alden's home, Tavi asked whether he wanted a copy of their sound notes. He said yes, but only if they wrote the date and the place beside every sound. He had learned that memories could grow softer over time, even when they remained dear. A written note would help him compare his mornings without guessing. Nell promised to bring a clean sheet next week. Ivo suggested that Alden also write about the days when nothing new happened. The bear thought for a moment and agreed. He had often felt ashamed of quiet days. Now he could see that a quiet day was not an empty one. He thanked his new friends and opened his window just a little, letting the cool air in without stepping into the snow.

離開奧登家前，塔薇問他要不要一份聲音紀錄。他說願意，但希望每個聲音旁都寫日期與地點。他已明白，即使珍貴的記憶也可能隨時間變模糊，書面記錄能幫他比較不同早晨，不必猜測。奈兒答應下週帶一張乾淨的紙。伊沃建議奧登也寫下沒有新聲音的日子。熊想了想，同意了。他以前常為安靜的日子感到羞愧，如今知道安靜並不等於空白。他謝謝新朋友，微微打開窗戶，讓冷空氣進來，卻不用踏入雪地。
