草地上的課

A Class in the Meadow

雪谷的七種聲音 · 第 7 / 25 章 · 1000以下 · 英中對照 · 校訂中

By the next week, children were asking about the search. A local teacher offered a short class in a dry meadow beside the west road. She told Tavi that learning by listening could be useful, but the class must stay by the main path. Tavi agreed. The teacher gave each child a list of safe things to hear: wind, water, feet, and leaves. “We collect notes, not animals,” she said. A young rabbit brought a large notebook and asked to help. Tavi let him take notes with the others. No one had to be the first to hear something to be part of the work.

下一週，孩子們開始問起尋聲的事。一位當地老師決定在西邊道路旁的乾草地上開一堂短課。她告訴塔薇，練習聆聽很有用，但全班必須待在主要小路旁。塔薇同意了。老師給每位孩子一份安全的聲音清單：風、水、腳步與葉子。「我們收集的是紀錄，不是動物，」她說。一隻小兔子帶著一本大筆記本來幫忙。塔薇讓他與大家一起記錄，沒有人非得第一個聽見聲音才能參與。

The main lesson started beside a flower pushing through the ground. There was no music around it. Yet when the wind moved the first leaves, the children heard a soft dry sound. The teacher asked them to stay quiet for one hour, with short breaks to rest. Some children thought this would be boring. Then the rabbit pointed toward a low branch, where two leaves touched again and again. Everyone heard a small sound. It was real, and it did not need to be loud to matter. Tavi put a new mark on her page.

主要課程從一朵剛穿出地面的花旁開始。花周圍沒有音樂，但風吹動第一批葉片時，孩子們聽見細小乾爽的沙沙聲。老師請大家安靜觀察一小時，中間可短暫休息。有些孩子以為會很無聊。接著小兔子指向一根低枝，兩片葉子不停相碰，大家都聽到細微的聲音。那是真實的聲響，並不需要很大才有意義。塔薇在紙上加上一個新記號。

At break time, an older neighbor brought chocolate and warm water. She said she had been a housewife for many years and had learned to hear changes in the weather while caring for her family. The children wanted to know what she heard first. She answered that it was usually the wind at her window, not a bird. Nell wrote this down as one more idea to test. The teacher reminded the class that a person's memory was helpful, but they could still check it against the sounds of today. That was how they learned together.

休息時，一位年長鄰居送來巧克力與溫水。她說自己多年來是家庭主婦，照顧家人時也學會聽出天氣變化。孩子們問她最早聽見什麼。她回答，通常是窗邊的風，不是鳥。奈兒把這個想法記下，準備日後測試。老師提醒大家，個人的記憶很有幫助，仍可以拿來和今天的聲音比較。大家就是這樣一起學習。

Some people had thought the search was popular only because it sounded exciting. Now the children could explain what they had learned. Tavi asked each one to draw or write a note about a real sound. The rabbit drew the leaves, another child drew the stream, and one drew a quiet place. Ivo put their work on a table where families could look without walking into the meadow. Tavi was pleased that the project could start more questions instead of giving every answer at once.

有人原以為尋聲活動受歡迎只是因為聽起來刺激。如今孩子們已能說明自己的發現。塔薇請每位孩子畫下或寫下一個真實聽見的聲音。小兔子畫葉片，另一個孩子畫小溪，還有孩子畫安靜的地方。伊沃把作品放在桌上，讓家人不必走進草地也能觀看。塔薇很高興，這個計畫能引出更多問題，而不必一次提供所有答案。

Before sunset, the teacher checked that nothing had been left behind. The grass still looked the same as it had in the morning. Tavi did not want the meadow to change just because people came to listen. The rabbit waved goodbye, and the class went home by the west road. The friends remained until the last family had gone. Their second clear spring sound was the light touch of new leaves. They would listen again later, but today they had learned how to share a discovery without taking it away from its home.

日落前，老師檢查大家沒有遺留任何東西。草地看起來仍和早晨一樣。塔薇不希望人們來聽聲音，就改變草地原來的模樣。小兔子揮手道別，全班從西邊道路回家。朋友們等到最後一個家庭離開。第二個清楚的春天聲音，是新葉輕輕相碰。他們以後會再聽，而今天已學會如何分享發現，卻不把它從原來的家帶走。

The teacher asked every student to look at their notes once more before taking them home. “Of course you may draw a sound in your own way,” she said, “but tell your reader what really happened.” Tavi liked the lesson. One child had written that the wind sang loudly in every tree, though the class had listened near only one small branch. He changed the sentence to describe the place where he stood. Another child had drawn a bird, even though she had heard only leaves. She decided to draw the leaves instead and save the bird for another day. No one was scolded for changing a page. The whole class understood that careful notes could become better with a second look. Tavi went home carrying many little pictures and a new respect for young listeners.

老師請每位學生在帶走筆記前再看一次。「當然，你可以用自己的方式畫聲音，」她說，「但要告訴讀者真正發生了什麼。」塔薇喜歡這堂課。有個孩子寫每棵樹都發出很大的風聲，但全班只在一根小樹枝旁聽過。他便修改句子，描述自己站的位置。另一個孩子畫了鳥，雖然她其實只聽見葉子，最後改畫葉片，決定改天再畫鳥。沒有人因修改紙張被責備。全班明白，仔細紀錄可以在再次檢查後變得更好。塔薇帶著許多小圖畫和對年輕聆聽者的新敬意回家。
