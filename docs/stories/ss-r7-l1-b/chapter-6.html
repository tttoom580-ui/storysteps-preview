泥地旁的座位

The Muddy Seat

雪谷的七種聲音 · 第 6 / 25 章 · 1000以下 · 英中對照 · 校訂中

A warmer day brought good news from the lower stream. Nell set a desk by her window and spread their notes across it. The deep snow near the bank had changed to soft mud. “Do we go now?” Ivo asked, lifting one arm. Tavi wanted to find the answer, but the soft ground could be unsafe. They decided to visit the public rest place first. It had a convenient seat and a roof where they could wait without stepping into the mud. Their plan was to look and listen from dry land, not to get close to every sound.

天氣變暖的那天，低地溪流傳來好消息。奈兒把書桌移到窗旁，攤開大家的紀錄。岸邊的深雪已變成軟泥。「我們現在去嗎？」伊沃舉起一隻手問。塔薇想找到答案，但鬆軟地面可能不安全。大家決定先去公共休息處，那裡有方便的座位和屋頂，可以不用踩進泥裡就等待。他們計畫站在乾地觀察與聆聽，而不是每個聲音都要靠近。

The rest place was beside a little shop that sold warm drinks. A sign welcomed visitors who wanted to sit. Nell took out an envelope with their map in it. She did not need to send the paper anywhere; the envelope simply kept it dry. A fish jumped in the open water, and Tavi gave a surprised cry. The sound was clear, but it came from a fish, not a bird. Ivo made a funny face at the fish and laughed. “Perhaps we have found the first sound of open water,” he said.

休息處旁有一間賣熱飲的小店。牌子歡迎想坐下的人。奈兒拿出一個裝地圖的信封，並不是要寄送，而是讓紙張保持乾燥。一條魚從沒有結冰的水面跳起，塔薇驚訝地叫了一聲。聲音很清楚，但來自魚，不是鳥。伊沃朝魚做了個滑稽表情，笑著說：「也許我們找到第一個開放水面的聲音了。」

A neighbor came to speak with them. He knew the history of the stream and remembered years when the water rose quickly. However, he did not claim to know today's weather. He said the ground may stay soft for several days. “The best place to stand can change with the rain,” he warned. Nell wrote the warning in large letters. Tavi looked at the little stream as its water rose and fell around stones. She was glad that the neighbor had come before they tried to cross the bank.

一位鄰居來和他們說話。他熟悉溪流的歷史，也記得有些年份水位上升得很快。然而，他沒有假裝知道今天的天氣。他說地面可能還要軟上好幾天。「最安全的站立位置會隨雨而改變，」他提醒。奈兒用大字記下警告。塔薇看著溪水繞過石頭，水面一高一低。她很慶幸鄰居在大家嘗試靠近岸邊之前就出現了。

Tavi wanted to share the news without making the stream sound safer than it was. The friends made a short note for the town board: some ice had opened, fish were moving, and the banks remained soft. They did not call it an open path. Ivo read the note aloud so everyone could understand it. The shop owner offered warm tea and said visitors were welcome to listen from the rest place. That was enough for the day. Their work was not to turn every discovery into a visit for the whole town.

塔薇想分享消息，卻不願讓溪流聽起來比實際安全。大家為鎮上公告板寫了短訊：部分冰面已融、魚開始活動，但河岸仍然鬆軟。他們沒有把岸邊稱為可通行的小路。伊沃大聲念出短訊，確保大家聽懂。店主提供熱茶，也歡迎訪客留在休息處聆聽。今天這樣就夠了。他們的工作並不是把每個發現都變成全鎮要去的景點。

That night, Tavi drew a small blue line on the map. She did not draw a road across it. The sound of water had changed, and now they had heard a fish as well. This was progress, even if it was not the song they first hoped for. Nell folded the notes into the envelope once more. Ivo promised to bring a better coat next time. Nobody had to hurry. The snow was leaving the valley in its own way, and the friends were learning how to notice without taking more than they needed.

那晚塔薇在地圖上畫一條小藍線，卻沒有在溪上畫道路。水聲已變，大家還聽見魚跳出水面。這是進展，即使不是最初期待的歌聲。奈兒再次把筆記折好，放進信封。伊沃答應下次帶更好的外套。沒有人需要趕時間。雪正依自己的方式離開山谷，而朋友們正在學習觀察，不多拿自己不需要的東西。

As the sun went down, Tavi read the shop owner's note about the stream. It said the water could rise after warm rain, even when no rain fell in town. She added that warning to the map and kept the riverbank closed. Nell said that they could still show people the safe covered seat. It had a good view and made no new path through the mud. Ivo offered to help clean the rest place once a week. Tavi liked the idea because it gave the neighbors something useful to do without pushing closer to the river. They made a small plan to ask the town who would care for the seat. It was a simple job, but it would help many people watch the changing season. Tavi felt that their search was slowly becoming a shared way of living with the valley.

日落時，塔薇讀著店主寫的溪流便條。上面說，暖雨過後水位可能上升，即使鎮上沒有下雨。她把警告加到地圖，繼續關閉河岸道路。奈兒說，大家仍可使用安全的有頂座位區。那裡視野好，也不必踩出穿越泥地的新路。伊沃願意每週幫忙清理休息處。塔薇喜歡這想法，因為鄰居有了有用的工作，不必為此更靠近溪流。他們決定詢問鎮上誰能負責座位。事情很簡單，卻能讓許多人觀察季節變化。塔薇感到尋聲正慢慢成為大家與山谷共同生活的方式。
