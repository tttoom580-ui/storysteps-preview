沒有鳥鳴的早晨

The Morning Without Birds

雪谷的七種聲音 · 第 1 / 25 章 · 1000以下 · 英中對照

In Cloudpine Valley, winter did not end on the same day every year. Tavi, a young red panda, woke before the sun. A small bug crept along the cold window. Tavi wondered why it had appeared so early. She opened the window and listened for the birds that usually returned before spring, but heard none. Her camera hung beside the door. She took a picture of the white hills, yet a picture could not show the silence. That was the first fact she wrote in her book. A friendly woman at the next house offered warm juice. Tavi thanked her but kept looking toward the trees. She had always loved the first sound of spring. Today the valley seemed to be holding its breath.

雲松谷的冬天並不是每年都在同一天結束。年輕的小熊貓塔薇在日出前醒來。一隻小蟲沿著冰冷的窗戶緩緩爬行。塔薇不明白牠為何這麼早就出現。她打開窗戶，想聽聽通常在春天前回來的鳥兒，卻一聲鳥鳴也沒聽見。相機掛在門邊，她拍下白色山丘，但照片無法呈現這片寂靜。這是她寫進筆記本的第一個事實。隔壁一位友善的女士送來溫熱果汁。塔薇道謝，仍望向樹林。她一直喜歡春天的第一個聲音。今天整座山谷卻像屏住了呼吸。

Her friend Ivo, a mountain goat, came from the north path. He had a red hat and a pet mouse in a warm little box. “I will not lie to you,” he said kindly. “I heard no birds on the north path either. But there may be birds on the other side of the hill.” Tavi stood in the middle of the yard and tried to notice every little noise. She heard a step, a falling leaf, and Ivo's mouse eating. No song came from the woods. They did not say that spring was gone. They said only that they had not heard it yet. It was an important difference, and Tavi marked it in her book.

她的朋友、山羊伊沃從北邊小路走來。他戴著紅帽子，還帶著一隻住在溫暖小盒裡的寵物老鼠。「我不會騙妳，」他溫和地說，「北邊小路上我也沒聽到鳥鳴。不過山丘另一邊可能還有鳥。」塔薇站在院子中央，努力注意每一個小聲音。她聽到腳步、落葉，以及伊沃的老鼠吃東西的聲音，卻沒有樹林裡的歌聲。他們沒有說春天消失了，只說目前還沒有聽見。這兩種說法並不相同，塔薇把差別寫進筆記本。

A picnic was planned for the first warm day. Other families wanted to walk up the high hill then, but the snow there was still deep. Tavi could not reach the old lookout safely. She would not use a dangerous road just to get an answer quickly. Instead, she drew a simple map of the lower valley. This was a place where young and old neighbors could walk together. A bright star was still high in the pale morning sky. Ivo pointed to it, then pointed to the low path. “One thing is far away,” he said. “The other is here under our feet. We can start here.”

大家原本計畫在第一個溫暖的日子野餐。其他家庭想走上高丘，但那裡的雪仍然很深。塔薇無法安全抵達舊瞭望台。她不願為了快速找到答案就使用危險的路。於是她畫了一張山谷低地的簡單地圖。年輕與年長的鄰居都能一起走這段路。清晨淡淡的天空仍掛著一顆明亮星星。伊沃先指星星，再指低處小路。「有個東西很遠，」他說，「另一個就在腳下。我們可以從這裡開始。」

Tavi wanted the future spring walk to be safe for everyone. She asked Ivo to help, but she also wanted someone who knew the ground under the snow. They went to see Nell, a marmot who lived near the warm rocks. Nell knew where small streams ran and where ice stayed thin. She agreed to join only if they turned back when a path felt wrong. “We are looking for sounds, not prizes,” she said. Tavi liked that rule. They made one more rule: never take an animal from its home simply to hear it make a sound.

塔薇希望未來的春季散步對每個人都安全。她請伊沃幫忙，也想找一位懂得雪下土地情況的朋友。他們去見住在溫暖岩石旁的土撥鼠奈兒。奈兒知道小溪流向，也知道哪些地方的冰很薄。她答應加入，但條件是道路一旦不對勁就立刻折返。「我們找的是聲音，不是獎品，」她說。塔薇喜歡這個原則。他們還訂下另一條規則：絕不為了聽叫聲，就把動物帶離自己的家。

By noon, the three friends had a plan, a pencil, and three warm coats. They would listen near the homes first, move farther only with care, and share what they learned with the valley. Nobody promised to bring spring back. Spring was not a thing that could be carried in a bag. Still, they could learn why the morning felt so different. Tavi closed her book and smiled at her friends. She no longer needed to wait alone beside her window. The search had begun, and her first real job was simple: listen before deciding what the silence meant.

中午前，三位朋友已備好計畫、鉛筆與三件暖外套。他們會先在住家附近聆聽，確認安全後才走遠，並把發現告訴山谷居民。沒有人承諾把春天帶回來，因為春天不是能裝進袋子的東西。不過，他們可以了解今天的早晨為何如此不同。塔薇合起筆記本，對朋友笑了。她再也不必獨自站在窗邊等待。尋找已經開始，而她的第一項工作很簡單：先聽，再判斷沉默的意思。

That afternoon, Tavi went back to the same window and compared what she heard at dawn with what she heard now. A cart passed outside. A family laughed on the road. The quiet in the woods was still there, but the whole valley had never been silent. She made two small columns in her book: sounds made by neighbors and sounds made by the land. It was not a perfect way to sort the world, yet it helped her ask a better question. Ivo returned to check that she had eaten. Nell brought a spare pencil and reminded her to rest. Tavi was grateful. She could look for the spring sounds without turning every hour into work. Before going to bed, she wrote, “We may hear something tomorrow, or we may hear nothing new. Both days can teach us.” The line made her feel ready for morning.

當天下午，塔薇回到同一扇窗邊，比較黎明與此刻聽見的聲音。外面有推車經過，路上有家庭在笑。樹林仍安靜，但整座山谷從未完全無聲。她在本子分成兩欄：鄰居製造的聲音，以及土地傳來的聲音。這不是完美的分類方法，卻幫她提出更好的問題。伊沃回來確認她吃過飯，奈兒帶來備用鉛筆，也提醒她休息。塔薇很感激。她可以尋找春聲，不必讓每個小時都變成工作。睡前，她寫下：「明天也許聽見新聲音，也許什麼都沒有。兩種日子都能教我們一些事。」這句話讓她準備好迎接早晨。
