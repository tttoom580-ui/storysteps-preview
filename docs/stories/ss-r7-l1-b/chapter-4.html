走錯的綠色道路

The Wrong Green Road

雪谷的七種聲音 · 第 4 / 25 章 · 1000以下 · 英中對照

The next day, the three friends met by an outdoor bath fed by warm mountain water. A large block of snow lay on the path beyond it. Both Nell and Ivo knew they had a choice: turn around or cross the open field to the east. The field looked green where the wind had moved the snow, but that did not make it safe. Tavi shook her head when a young animal wanted to run across. “We must not pass a place we have not checked,” she said. The child carried a cute wooden duck and went back to the bath with his family.

隔天，三人在一座有山泉暖水的露天浴池旁集合。前面的路被大雪塊擋住。奈兒和伊沃都知道有兩個選擇：折返，或穿過東邊的空地。風吹走部分積雪後，空地露出綠色，但那不代表安全。一隻小動物想跑過去時，塔薇搖頭說：「沒有檢查的地方，我們不能通過。」那孩子拿著可愛的木鴨，與家人一起回到浴池旁。

Nell took a bowl and a closed flask from her bag. She poured warm water into the bowl so Tavi could warm her cold hands. She did not pour it on the ground; warm water could make new ice later. Ivo made a medium-size mark in the snow at the safe stopping place. A gentle knock came from a wooden door nearby. Mrs. Alder, an old mountain sheep, asked what they were looking for. Tavi gave an honest answer. “We look for signs of spring, but we have not found a safe road ahead.” Mrs. Alder nodded. She knew the hill behind her house and offered to show them another way on a map.

奈兒從袋子取出碗和蓋緊的保溫瓶，把溫水倒進碗裡，讓塔薇暖一暖凍冷的雙手。她沒有把水倒在地上，因為熱水晚些可能重新結冰。伊沃在安全停步處的雪上留下中等大小的記號。附近木門傳來輕敲聲。年長的綿羊奧德太太問他們在找什麼。塔薇誠實回答：「我們在找春天的跡象，可是前面的路還不安全。」奧德太太點頭。她熟悉屋後山坡，願意在地圖上指出另一條路。

The family invited them to look at a drawing of the hill, not to walk it today. From the window, the friends could see a thin line where the snow had fallen away. The road below it was quiet, with no moving stones. But the surprise was on the drawing itself. Mrs. Alder had marked an old water course that ran beneath the field. That could explain why the surface looked green. The place was not open land at all. Warm water might be moving under it. Their careful choice had kept them away from weak ground.

這家人請他們先看看山坡圖，而不是今天就去走。從窗邊可以看到一條積雪退去的細線，下面的道路十分安靜，沒有石頭滾動。但驚喜在圖紙上：奧德太太標出空地下方一條舊水道。這可能解釋地表為何呈綠色。那裡並非普通空地，底下可能有暖水流動。三人因謹慎選擇，避開了可能鬆軟的地面。

Ivo made tea, and Nell wrote down the new information. Tavi looked carefully at Mrs. Alder's map. She did not add a new sound to the list because they had not heard one. They did add a place to avoid until the ground was checked by someone who knew it. The older sheep was pleased that the children listened. “A map is a promise to tell the truth,” she said. “It is not a reason to walk somewhere simply because a line looks short.” Tavi copied that thought into her book.

伊沃泡了茶，奈兒寫下新資料。塔薇仔細看奧德太太的地圖。因為沒有親耳聽到新的聲音，她沒有把什麼加進聲音清單。但他們標註了一處暫時不可進入的地方，直到熟悉地形的人完成檢查。老綿羊很高興年輕人願意聽。「地圖是說實話的承諾，」她說，「不是因為線畫得短就非走不可的理由。」塔薇把這句話抄進本子。

Back outside, Tavi told a passing driver about the blocked road. A taxi could bring people to the warm bath, but no car should continue toward the soft field. The driver agreed to tell others at the next stop. The friends walked home together. They had heard no new song, yet they had learned where not to go. In a long search, that knowledge might be as useful as finding a beautiful place. The child held up his little wooden duck at a window as they left, and Tavi waved back.

回到戶外，塔薇告訴一位經過的司機道路受阻。計程車可以把人載到暖浴池，但車輛不應繼續前往鬆軟空地。司機答應在下一站提醒其他人。朋友們一起走回家。他們沒有聽到新歌聲，卻知道哪些地方不能去。對長途探索來說，這份知識可能跟找到美景一樣重要。離開時，那孩子在窗邊舉起小木鴨，塔薇也揮手回應。

Tavi was still thinking about the old water course when she reached home. She drew the safe path in a strong line and the unknown field in a dotted line. Nell checked the drawing and suggested that they write the word closed next to the field. That word might keep another family from making the same mistake. Ivo wanted to tell everyone about the green ground, but Tavi asked him to explain why it could be soft too. They spoke with the driver before he began his next trip. The driver repeated the warning in his own words so they knew he understood. Tavi felt relieved. The day's best discovery was not a new sound; it was learning that a short path could hide a long risk. Their careful choice gave the next visitors a better chance to travel safely.

塔薇回家後仍在想舊水道。她用實線畫安全小路，用虛線畫未知空地。奈兒看過後，建議在空地旁寫上「封閉」，可能避免其他家庭犯相同錯誤。伊沃想向大家介紹綠色地面，但塔薇請他同時說明底下為何可能鬆軟。司機出發下一趟前，他們再次說明警告。司機用自己的話重述，讓大家確定他理解了。塔薇鬆了一口氣。這天最好的發現不是新聲音，而是短短的路可能藏著長長的危險。謹慎的決定讓後來的旅人更有機會平安出行。
