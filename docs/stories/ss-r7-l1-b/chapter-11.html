薄冰的規則

The Thin Ice Rule

雪谷的七種聲音 · 第 11 / 25 章 · 1000以下 · 英中對照 · 校訂中

In the morning, Tavi carried a basket of food to the small hospital. The storm had left several paths wet, and a young kite maker had slipped near the river. His health was good, but his leg needed rest. The nurse checked him and told his mother what to watch for. Tavi brought no big plan to the hospital; she came to help carry dry clothes. She thought it useful to hear how a careful adult made a choice. The nurse did not hate the weather. She simply said that weather could change a safe place into a dangerous one.

早上，塔薇提著一籃食物到小醫院。風雨讓幾條道路濕滑，一名做風箏的年輕人曾在河邊滑倒。他身體狀況良好，但腿需要休息。護士替他檢查，並告訴母親該注意什麼。塔薇到醫院不是來提出大計畫，而是幫忙搬乾衣服。她覺得聽一位謹慎的大人如何做選擇很有用。護士並不討厭天氣，只說天氣能讓原本安全的地方變得危險。

Ivo took a belt from his bag to hold a loose coat shut. Nell checked the color of the water shown in a picture taken from the safe path. It was brown after the rain. A friend asked if the group would go back to listen that afternoon. “Not if the banks are still soft,” Tavi said. “The sound can wait.” She wrote the rule on a white sheet of paper and placed it by the hospital door, where people could read it. A nurse added a picture of thin ice so even children would understand the warning.

伊沃從袋裡拿出皮帶，把鬆開的外套繫好。奈兒查看在安全小路拍下的溪水照片；雨後的水呈褐色。一位朋友問他們下午會不會再去聽。「如果河岸還軟，就不去，」塔薇說，「聲音可以等。」她把規則寫在白紙上，貼在醫院門邊，讓大家看得到。護士又加上一張薄冰圖，讓孩子也能理解警告。

A dear old neighbor, who used to work at the library, came to visit the injured boy. She had a picture of a player flying a kite on a safe field. She sent it to his family to help him feel less bored. He laughed and said he wanted to make a new kite when his leg was well. Tavi was happy to see his smile. But she reminded him that the right time to return outside would be decided with his family and the nurse, not by the arrival of warm weather alone.

一位曾在圖書館工作的親切老鄰居來探望受傷的少年。她帶了一張有人在安全草地放風箏的照片，送給家人，希望他不要太無聊。少年笑了，說等腿好了還想做一只新風箏。塔薇看見他的笑容很高興，但提醒他，何時能回到戶外應由家人與護士共同決定，不能只看天氣暖不暖。

At the door, Nell checked the list of places they had visited. Those marks were still useful, but there was no need to treat every place as a path for other visitors. One field could be safe for drawing and not safe for walking after rain. A kite could be fun on open ground and dangerous near wires. Tavi began to understand why people needed different rules for different tasks. She could not make the valley safe by writing one short sentence. She could make one good choice at a time.

到門口時，奈兒查看先前去過的地點清單。那些標記仍有用，但不代表每個地點都能當成訪客道路。某塊地平時適合畫畫，雨後卻不適合走；風箏在空地很有趣，靠近電線則危險。塔薇開始懂得，不同任務需要不同規則。她無法靠一句簡短的話就讓整座山谷安全，只能一次做出一個好的選擇。

That evening, the hospital nurse thanked Tavi for taking time to help. Tavi said the search for spring could wait while someone needed care. The nurse agreed. She told Tavi to keep listening, but also to keep seeing the people around her. “A valley is more than hills and water,” she said. “It is the neighbors who live there.” Tavi walked home thinking about that sentence. She still wanted to hear birds again, yet the day had taught her another kind of attention: listening when a person needed help.

傍晚，醫院護士謝謝塔薇花時間幫忙。塔薇說，有人需要照顧時，尋找春天可以等。護士表示同意，請她繼續聽，也別忘了看見身邊的人。「山谷不只是山丘和水，」她說，「也是住在這裡的鄰居。」塔薇一路想著這句話。她仍希望再次聽見鳥，今天卻學到另一種注意：當有人需要協助時，願意聆聽對方。

After the hospital visit, Nell asked Tavi to check the short notice once more before they put it on the town board. A child had drawn a joke picture of a kite wearing boots, and the nurse let it stay in the corner because it helped the young patient smile. Tavi made sure the warning itself was still easy to read. She knew people often looked quickly at a notice and might miss a long explanation. They decided to use simple words and one clear picture. Ivo offered to send the same information to the families who had asked about the stream. He would not send a private patient's story or name. Tavi thanked him for remembering that safety information could be shared without sharing personal details. The next time they heard a bird by the hospital, they would still begin by checking the ground under their feet.

離開醫院後，奈兒請塔薇再次檢查短公告，再貼到鎮上公告板。孩子畫了一張風箏穿靴子的搞笑圖，護士讓它留在角落，因為能讓小病人微笑。塔薇確認真正的警告仍容易閱讀。她知道人們常匆匆看公告，可能忽略太長的解釋。大家決定用簡單文字配一張清楚圖片。伊沃願意把同樣的資訊傳給詢問過溪流的家庭，但不會分享病人的私人故事或姓名。塔薇謝謝他記得，安全訊息可以分享，個人資料卻不必跟著公開。下次他們在醫院附近聽見鳥，也仍會先檢查腳下地面。
