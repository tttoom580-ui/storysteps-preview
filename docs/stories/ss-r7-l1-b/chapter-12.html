全鎮決定聆聽

The Town Decides to Listen

雪谷的七種聲音 · 第 12 / 25 章 · 1000以下 · 英中對照 · 校訂中

On a clear afternoon, the town met near the supermarket to hear news about the search. An actor from a visiting group offered to read Tavi's notes, but Tavi said she wanted to speak in her own voice. “I can do it,” she said. She put on her glasses and held the key to the meeting room. A small band was practicing at the next corner, so they asked the players to wait until the talk ended. They did not want to stop all music. They just needed a short time when everyone could listen to words without a loud sound over them.

一個晴朗的下午，小鎮在超市旁開會，聽尋聲計畫的消息。一位來訪劇團的演員願意幫塔薇念筆記，但塔薇說想用自己的聲音發言。「我可以，」她說。她戴上眼鏡，拿著會議室鑰匙。附近轉角有一支小樂隊練習，大家請樂手等談話結束再開始。他們不是要禁止所有音樂，只需要一小段能清楚聽人說話的時間。

Tavi showed the room the last map and explained what they had heard so far. She did not celebrate a bird call that had not happened. She told people about moving water and new leaves. She also explained why a path could be closed even when the sky looked bright. Some children wanted to play a game: each person would choose a sound and tell where it came from. Tavi liked the idea but asked the adults to make sure the game stayed near home. No one should die or get hurt for a game about spring.

塔薇展示最新地圖，說明目前聽到的東西。她沒有慶祝尚未出現的鳥叫，而是談流水與新葉，也解釋為何天空明亮時道路仍可能封閉。幾個孩子想玩遊戲：每人選一個聲音，說出它從哪裡來。塔薇喜歡這個主意，但請大人確保遊戲只在住家附近進行。沒有人該為了春天的遊戲受傷，甚至失去生命。

A visitor from the media asked if the town would sell a ticket for future walks. Nell answered that the short safe walks were free. They did not want poor families to be left out. A shop owner offered a package of warm socks for the group to share with anyone who needed them. Tavi thanked her and said they would take only what was needed. The room was full of questions, and the friends were tired by the end. Still, people seemed more interested in safe listening than in a fast story with a false happy ending.

一位媒體來訪者問，小鎮以後會不會賣步道門票。奈兒回答，安全的短程散步免費。他們不希望較貧困的家庭被排除。店主願意提供一包暖襪子，讓團隊分給需要的人。塔薇道謝，也說只會拿真正需要的數量。會議室裡有許多問題，結束時朋友們都累了。不過，大家看來更關心安全聆聽，而不是快速又虛假的快樂結局。

At four o'clock, the band came back. This time the players made gentle music, and the neighbors had space to sit or leave as they wished. Tavi did not call the music a spring sound from the hills. It was something the town had made, and that was beautiful in a different way. She thanked the players for waiting. The meeting gave her a new task: prepare a clear note showing which places were open, which were closed, and what help each kind of visitor might need.

四點鐘時，樂隊回來了。這次樂手演奏柔和的音樂，鄰居可以自由坐下或離開。塔薇沒有把這段音樂稱為山上傳來的春天聲音。這是小鎮居民創造的，具有另一種美。她謝謝樂手願意等候。會議給她一項新工作：準備清楚說明哪些地方開放、哪些封閉，以及不同訪客可能需要什麼協助。

Ivo locked the room after the last guest left. Nell carried the empty cups back to the kitchen. Tavi sat on a step and let her tired legs rest. She knew they had not solved the silence of the birds. Yet they had done something important: they had told the truth in public and listened to questions they had not thought to ask. The project was no longer just three friends with a book. It was a shared way of caring for the valley while waiting for the sounds to change.

最後一名來賓離開後，伊沃鎖上會議室。奈兒把空杯子送回廚房。塔薇坐在階梯上休息疲累的雙腿。她知道鳥兒的寂靜仍沒有答案，但他們已完成重要的事：公開說出事實，也聆聽自己沒想過的問題。這個計畫不再只是三個朋友拿著一本筆記本，而是大家一邊等待聲音變化、一邊共同照顧山谷的方法。

The next morning, Tavi practiced the short speech again by the window. “I heard water and leaves,” she began. “I did not see the bird that made the call.” She liked speaking in the first person because she could tell exactly what she had done. At ten o’clock, Nell came to review the public notes with her. They removed a line that sounded too sure about the high hill. Ivo put the clean copy in a folder where anyone could read it. The friends understood that a public talk was not the end of their work. The words on the page might guide visitors days later, after the weather had changed. That was why dates and warnings mattered. Tavi felt nervous about being wrong, yet she knew she could correct a note when better evidence arrived.

隔天早上，塔薇在窗邊再次練習短講。「我聽見水聲與葉聲，」她開始說，「我沒有看見發出叫聲的鳥。」她喜歡用第一人稱說話，因為能清楚交代自己做過什麼。十點鐘，奈兒來和她檢查公開筆記。他們刪去一句對高山情況過度確定的話。伊沃把乾淨的版本放進資料夾，供任何人閱讀。朋友們明白，公開報告不是工作的終點。幾天之後，天氣變了，紙上的文字仍可能影響訪客，因此日期與警告很重要。塔薇害怕寫錯，但知道有更好的證據時可以修正。
