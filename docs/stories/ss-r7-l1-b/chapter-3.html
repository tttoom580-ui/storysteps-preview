冰下的水聲

Water Under Ice

雪谷的七種聲音 · 第 3 / 25 章 · 1000以下 · 英中對照

All the paths near the lower pond looked white, but Nell knew the area well. She told Tavi to begin at a tree with three low branches. Two young goats were playing basketball near their home. Their ball made a loud sound against a wall. Tavi laughed, then stopped to define the sound they wanted to hear: not every noise was a sign of spring. Ivo asked the goats to keep playing; this search was not a reason to stop other people from having fun. The friends walked to the pond on a clear path that did not cross the ice.

低地池塘附近所有道路看來都是白的，但奈兒很熟悉這一帶。她要塔薇從一棵長著三根低樹枝的樹開始走。兩隻年輕山羊正在家旁打籃球，球撞牆時發出很大的聲音。塔薇笑了，隨後停下來界定他們要找的聲音：不是每個聲響都代表春天。伊沃請小山羊繼續玩，尋聲不是阻止別人玩樂的理由。三人沿著安全小路走向池塘，沒有踏上冰面。

Nell carried a modern little lamp, though it was still day. She also brought an eraser because maps can be wrong. Tavi had only a few marks on her page. They listened beside an orange plant pot left near the shore by a gardener. Under the ice came a soft tick, then another. Nell said it could be moving water, or it could be ice moving with the sun. “We need less guessing and more listening,” she said. Ivo took a note but left a little space beside it. One sound alone was not enough to tell the whole story.

奈兒帶了一盞新式小燈，雖然仍是白天。她也帶著橡皮擦，因為地圖可能畫錯。塔薇的筆記頁上只有少數記號。三人在岸邊園丁留下的橘色花盆旁聽著。冰下面傳來細小的喀聲，接著又一聲。奈兒說，那可能是流動的水，也可能是冰在陽光下移動。「少猜一些，多聽一些，」她說。伊沃做了紀錄，卻在旁邊留一點空白。單一聲音還不足以說明整件事。

A ranger came by at a quarter past one. He could not leave the main path because he had work to do. He asked them to send an email with their notes instead of making a long trip to his office. “You may also mail a paper copy,” he said, “but the road has heavy traffic in the afternoon.” Tavi wrote both choices down. She did not need to use the road today. She could go home before night and share the notes from there. The ranger added that some winter sounds grew louder when the day became warm.

一點十五分時，一位巡守員走來。他還有工作，不能離開主要道路，便請三人用電子郵件寄送紀錄，不必遠行到辦公室。「也可以郵寄紙本，」他說，「但下午道路交通很忙。」塔薇把兩種方法都記下來。今天不用走那條路，她可以天黑前回家，再分享筆記。巡守員還說，有些冬天的聲音會在天氣變暖時更加清楚。

At three p.m., the friends heard a thin line of water moving under the snow beside the bank. They could not see it, so Nell drew a small question mark rather than a picture of a stream. Tavi was happy with that. She had learned that good field notes tell what happened, not what the writer wished to happen. Ivo found a dry stick and set it beside the path to mark a safe place to stand. No one touched the ice. They stayed for five more minutes, then walked back before the wind grew cold.

下午三點，他們聽見岸邊積雪下似乎有一條細流在移動。因為看不到，奈兒畫了一個小問號，而不是直接畫溪流。塔薇很滿意。她學到好的野外紀錄應該寫發生的事，而不是作者希望發生的事。伊沃找到一根乾樹枝，放在小路邊標出安全站立的位置。沒有人碰冰面。五分鐘後，大家在風變冷前走回家。

At home, Tavi put the words “first possible water sound” on the page. The word possible mattered. Tomorrow the sun might be brighter, or the pond might sound different. She would compare both days before giving the sound a name. Nell warmed her paws, and Ivo hung the coats near the door. They were tired but not disappointed. The first trip had given them something better than a fast answer: a way to ask the same question again and check it with care.

回家後，塔薇在紙上寫下「第一個可能的水聲」。「可能」這兩個字很重要。明天陽光也許更亮，池塘也可能發出不同聲音。她決定比較兩天的結果，再為聲音命名。奈兒暖著爪子，伊沃把外套掛在門旁。他們累了，卻不失望。第一次出門帶來的不是快速答案，而是一套能再次提問並仔細查證的方法。

That evening, Tavi read her notes to her friends. The line about the small sound under the ice had a question mark beside it. “We will check it again when the weather is safe,” she said. “We will not change the word possible into certain just because we want a good story.” Nell agreed and drew a second box for the next visit. Ivo said he had learned to enjoy questions that were not finished yet. When a question stayed open, they had a reason to return with care and compare days. Tavi looked at the pale picture on her camera. It showed the pond, the dry path, and the far trees. It did not show the soft sound. That was why she needed words as well as pictures. She closed the book before dinner, knowing that a good search could leave room for tomorrow.

傍晚，塔薇把紀錄念給朋友聽。冰下細聲那行旁邊留著問號。「天氣安全時，我們會再檢查，」她說，「不能只因想說好故事，就把『可能』改成『確定』。」奈兒同意，在紙上畫了第二次拜訪的空格。伊沃說他開始喜歡尚未完成的問題。問題保持開放，就有理由日後小心回來比較。塔薇看著相機裡淡淡的照片，上面有池塘、乾路和遠樹，卻沒有聲音。因此她除了照片，也需要文字。晚飯前她合起本子，知道好的探索應為明天留下空間。
