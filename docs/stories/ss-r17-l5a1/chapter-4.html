A Clinic with No Walls

末日之後，我開了一家五星級餐廳 · 第 4 / 25 章 · 4000–5000 · 英中對照 · 校訂中

Winter boredom had become a luxury nobody could afford, yet An was surprised by how much civic work happened around one table. A retired driver brought his van to carry elderly patients; a student offered to translate medicine labels. In the clinic driveway, a mother sat in quiet despair because her child's fever had returned. Qiu asked the people around her not to offer confident predictions. She needed an honest account of the child's symptoms, a clean place to rest, and water without contamination. An brought a mild vegetable broth and waited while Qiu checked the temperature. The mother asked whether the soup would save her child. 'It can help her drink,' An said. 'The nurse will decide the treatment.'

冬季裡，無聊竟成了人們負擔不起的奢侈；唐安卻驚訝地發現，一張餐桌周圍能展開多少公民工作。退休司機開廂型車載高齡病患，學生主動幫忙翻譯藥品標籤。診所車道上，一名母親因孩子再次發燒而絕望地坐著。邱楠請旁人不要隨意給出肯定預測。她需要的是孩子症狀的誠實紀錄、乾淨休息處與未受污染的水。唐安送來清淡蔬菜湯，等邱楠量好體溫。母親問湯能不能救孩子。「它能幫她補充水分。」唐安說，「治療要由護理師決定。」

A merchant offered to pay the clinic's freight costs if it followed his private guideline for serving only registered residents. His company wanted a public demonstration of an innovative nutritional drink, and the offer made some volunteers uneasy. Housing records had been lost during the outbreak; families living in temporary shelters could not prove where they belonged. If the rule passed, it would intensify the danger faced by people without papers. An asked the volunteers to compare the drink's ingredients with those of ordinary soup. The expensive bottle contained less protein and almost the same salt. 'Innovation should solve a problem,' she said, 'not decorate a sales pitch.' The merchant withdrew his offer without an argument.

一名商人願意支付診所運費，條件是診所遵循他只服務登記居民的私人準則。公司希望公開展示一款號稱創新的營養飲品，讓志工不安。疫情期間住房紀錄大量遺失，住在臨時收容所的家庭無法證明住址。若採納規則，無證者的處境只會更危險。唐安請志工比較飲品與普通湯的成分。昂貴瓶裝飲料蛋白質較少，含鹽量卻幾乎相同。「創新應該解決問題，」她說，「不是裝飾推銷詞。」商人沒有爭辯就撤回提案。

Qiu carefully counted the remaining medication and refused to use an experimental supplement without permission. The child's breathing became noticeably easier after rest and fluids, but nobody claimed the improvement proved An's recipe was special. The mother tried to plead for more of the scarce medicine. Qiu explained why an unnecessary dose might prolong illness or cause harm. An stayed beside them, stirring a pot of oats over low heat. She began to understand that a meal could stimulate hope without replacing professional care. One volunteer said they had to choose between morality and survival. 'That's a false choice,' Qiu replied. 'Good care is how we survive together.'

邱楠仔細清點剩餘藥物，沒有同意前絕不使用實驗性補充品。孩子在休息與補水後，呼吸明顯順暢一些，但沒有人說這足以證明唐安的食譜特殊。母親懇求再多給一些稀缺藥物。邱楠解釋，不必要的劑量可能延長病程或帶來傷害。唐安留在一旁，用小火慢慢攪拌燕麥。她開始理解，一餐飯能激發希望，卻不能替代專業照護。志工說，大家不得不在道德與生存之間擇一。「那是假兩難。」邱楠回答，「好的照護正是我們一起活下去的方法。」

At the next clinic briefing, the mother gave an eloquent account of what had helped her child: steady observation, clean water, and a nurse who answered every question. An noticed a trait shared by the strongest volunteers. They did not compete to appear heroic; they watched carefully for small changes that were noticeable only after several hours. The mother described the child's first peaceful sleep in days. Qiu asked permission before writing it in the patient record. The story belonged to the family, even when it helped the clinic learn how to provide better care.

隔天診所簡報時，母親清晰動人地說明幫助孩子的是持續觀察、乾淨水與願意回答問題的護理師。唐安發現最可靠志工有一項共同特質：他們不爭著表現英勇，而是仔細觀察幾小時後才看得出來的明顯變化。母親描述孩子數日來第一次安穩入睡。邱楠徵得同意才寫入病歷。即使能幫診所學習，這段故事仍屬於家人。

A tin bowl fell from the shelf and startled a newly infected traveler waiting outside. He began to shake, then vomit near the gate. The guards raised their rifles, but Qiu asked them to keep distance without shooting. She treated him as a patient whose condition was uncertain. An brought water, gloves, and a covered bucket, then moved the other diners safely indoors. Shuo wiped the clinic windshield so the driver could see the road in fading light. The traveler accepted water from a long-handled cup and followed instructions to a separate observation tent. It was not a dramatic rescue, but it was a safer ending than panic would have given them.

一只錫碗從架上掉落，嚇到門外一名剛出現感染症狀的旅人。他開始顫抖，接著在門邊嘔吐。守衛舉起步槍，邱楠卻請他們保持距離，不要射擊。她把旅人當成病況不明的病患。唐安帶來水、手套和加蓋水桶，把其他食客安全帶入室內。林朔擦淨診所車輛的擋風玻璃，讓司機能在暮色中看清道路。旅人用長柄杯喝水，依指示走向獨立觀察帳篷。這不是戲劇性的救援，卻比恐慌能帶來的結局安全得多。

When the child finally fell asleep, her mother sat at the restaurant table and slowly ate her own bowl of oats. She had forgotten to eat since morning. An did not ask for praise or a review. She asked whether the porridge was too thick, and the mother said it needed a little more water. An smiled and wrote the advice down. The family would return to the clinic the next day, and An promised to keep a portion warm after the appointment. That night the volunteers cleaned the tent, shared accurate notes, and left the doors clear. Their work did not cure the city. It made one difficult night possible to endure.

孩子終於睡著時，母親坐在餐廳餐桌旁，慢慢吃自己那碗燕麥。她從早上就忘記進食。唐安沒有索求讚美或評價，只問粥是否太稠。母親說應該再加一點水。唐安微笑著記下建議。這家人隔天還要回診，唐安答應診後留一份熱食。那晚，志工清潔帳篷、交換準確筆記、保持出入口暢通。他們沒有治好整座城市，卻讓一個艱難夜晚變得可以熬過去。
