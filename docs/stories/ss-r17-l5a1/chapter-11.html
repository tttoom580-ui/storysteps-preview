測試，不是解藥

A Test, Not a Cure

末日之後，我開了一家五星級餐廳 · 第 11 / 25 章 · 4000–5000 · 英中對照 · 校訂中

用互動版查詞、朗讀與收藏

The discovery in the laboratory threatened to accelerate rumors faster than anyone could correct them. A visitor tried to compliment An by calling her soup a cure; she thanked him for the kindness and corrected the claim. Sol identified a component of the old filter that might explain why some foods smelled different after storage. Qiu agreed to deploy a small, noninvasive observation plan approved by the clinic, recording ordinary meals and symptoms without asking patients to take unknown substances. The work caused fatigue, especially for staff who already had full shifts. Even on a grim morning, An insisted the institution responsible for medical decisions must remain the clinic. Her kitchen could provide carefully prepared meals and honest records, nothing more.

實驗室的發現恐怕讓謠言加速傳播，比任何人澄清得更快。一名訪客想稱讚唐安，說她的湯是解藥；她感謝好意，卻立刻糾正。索爾找到舊濾材的一個組件，可能解釋某些食物存放後氣味為何改變。邱楠同意由診所核准一個非侵入性的小型觀察計畫，只記錄平常餐點與症狀，不讓病患接觸未知物質。工作造成疲勞，尤其對已經輪值的員工。即使早晨氣氛陰沉，唐安仍堅持，醫療決策機構必須是診所。廚房只能提供妥善餐食與誠實紀錄。

The trial used a minimal amount of staff time and recorded both improvements and failures. Mara served as a neutral witness and organized a petition asking the council not to advertise unsupported medical claims. In the observation room, a frightened patient watched his hands quiver as the soup cooled. Qiu explained that the realm of medical evidence demanded more than one result, and her recommendation was simply to continue normal care. An's specialty was flavor, not diagnosis, so she adjusted the meal for comfort without describing the change as treatment. The patient thanked her after eating two spoonfuls. No scientist could say why that small kindness mattered to him, but the importance of his consent was clear.

觀察計畫只占用少量人力，並同時記錄改善與失敗。瑪拉作為中立見證人，發起請願，要求議會不要宣傳沒有依據的醫療效果。觀察室裡，害怕的病人看著自己發抖的手，直到湯慢慢變涼。邱楠解釋，醫療證據領域需要超過單次結果；她的建議只是繼續正常照護。唐安的專長是調味，不是診斷，因此她調整餐點讓人吃得舒適，但不稱之為治療。病人吃了兩匙後道謝。沒有人能準確說明這點善意對他為何重要，但他必須自願同意的原則很清楚。

An refused to take any dangerous specimen from the old laboratory into her restaurant. The stakes were too high for a public kitchen full of children and elderly neighbors. 'I want people to thrive, not to become evidence in an experiment they never agreed to,' she said. Shuo helped the clinic undergo a review of its water system, which revealed a cracked gasket but no mysterious ingredient. A veteran doctor laughed at the idea that a trivial seasoning error had caused the entire disaster. He then stopped laughing and admitted that people were desperate for a simple story. The truth, however, might be a combination of damaged infrastructure, unsafe products, and failures of public communication.

唐安拒絕把舊實驗室的危險樣本帶進餐廳。對一間坐滿兒童與長者的公共廚房而言，風險太高。「我要人們好好生活，而不是在不知情下成為實驗證據。」她說。林朔協助診所檢查水系統，發現一處橡膠墊圈裂了，但沒有神祕成分。資深醫師笑說，一點微不足道的調味錯誤不可能引發整場災難。笑過後，他承認人人都渴望簡單故事。然而真相可能是受損基礎設施、不安全產品與公共溝通失敗的綜合結果。

The second-star review also asked whether the kitchen knew when to stop serving. An demonstrated the temperature chart and the procedure for discarding spoiled food. A few residents complained that throwing away any calories was wasteful. She understood the anger, especially with hunger so common, but illness could consume more supplies than a discarded bowl ever would. Qiu compared the costs with data from the clinic. The reviewers accepted the calculation and asked the team to explain it in plain language on the noticeboard.

第二星審查還問廚房知不知道什麼時候必須停止供餐。唐安展示溫度表及丟棄變質食物的程序。部分居民抱怨浪費熱量，唐安理解飢餓時的憤怒，但食物中毒消耗的物資可能遠超一碗被丟掉的湯。邱楠用診所數據比較代價。評審接受計算，請團隊在公告板用簡單文字解釋。

A public review accepted the kitchen's logs as evidence that patients were receiving better nutrition, not as proof of a universal cure. The council gave the restaurant its second star for reliable local supply and accurate reporting. An was relieved but not triumphant. The star would matter only if the outer settlements could keep receiving food during a disruption. Lele proposed training volunteers to cook in small groups, and An agreed on the condition that Qiu teach sanitation alongside recipes. They prepared printed instructions that included the limits of their findings in large letters. People could learn to make a nourishing soup without being asked to believe something untrue.

公開審查接受廚房紀錄，認定病患營養狀況獲得改善，卻不認為這能證明萬用解藥。議會因在地供應可靠與紀錄準確，給餐廳第二顆星。唐安鬆了口氣，沒有得意。只有外圍聚落在運輸中斷時仍能取得食物，這顆星才有意義。樂樂提議訓練志工分組煮飯，唐安同意，但要求邱楠在教食譜時同步教衛生。眾人印製大字說明，包括研究結果的限制。人們可以學會煮滋養的湯，不必先被要求相信不實的說法。

That evening An chose not to display the second star beside her portrait. Instead, she painted it above the water station where every diner washed their hands. Shuo looked surprised. 'Shouldn't you put it somewhere people can see from the street?' he asked. 'People who come to eat can see it,' she replied. 'The important part is what they do afterward.' At closing, Qiu reminded the workers to rest. For the first time, An accepted the instruction without argument. A restaurant that taught care but exhausted everyone who operated it would eventually fail the same standards it praised.

那晚，唐安沒有把第二顆星掛在自己照片旁，而是畫在每位食客洗手的水站上方。林朔很驚訝。「不該放在街上看得到的地方嗎？」他問。「來吃飯的人看得到就好。」她回答，「重要的是他們接著怎麼做。」關門時，邱楠提醒工作人員休息。唐安第一次沒有爭辯就照做。教別人照顧健康，卻把工作者累壞的餐廳，終究會違背自己稱讚的標準。
