菜園與掠奪者

The Garden and the Raiders

末日之後，我開了一家五星級餐廳 · 第 7 / 25 章 · 4000–5000 · 英中對照 · 校訂中

用互動版查詞、朗讀與收藏

The sight of green shoots could still arouse suspicion in Greyhaven: people feared that something living in contaminated soil might not be safe to eat. At the abandoned sports complex, a careful array of planting beds occupied the old tennis courts. A controversy had begun over who could use the harvest. One family wanted a locked garden; another wanted a communal kitchen. An walked down the cracked corridor toward the greenhouse and asked the growers what they had actually measured. The former caretaker admitted that a corrupt official had once charged them twice for water. His anger was understandable, but An wanted a remedy based on records, not another fight over rumors. The first job was a soil test.

在灰港城，看見綠芽仍可能引起懷疑：人們擔心受污染土壤長出的東西不能吃。廢棄運動中心裡，一排排整齊菜畦占據了舊網球場。誰有權使用收成，引起了爭議。一戶人家想把菜園鎖起來，另一戶希望成立公共廚房。唐安沿破裂走廊走向溫室，問種植者實際量過哪些數據。前管理員坦言，曾有貪腐官員對同一筆用水重複收費。他的憤怒可以理解，但唐安希望用紀錄解決，而不是靠流言再吵一場。第一件事是測土壤。

The restaurant's cuisine depended on seasonal vegetables, and the growers refused to give outsiders custody of their seed collection. An respected the decision. She asked which dishes they derived from their grandparents' recipes and offered to teach low-fuel steaming in exchange for a share of the beans. At dusk, a raid by river pirates interrupted the meeting. The attackers tried to steal batteries rather than food. Shuo ordered everyone to disconnect the irrigation pump before the thieves could damage its motor. The group retreated through a service passage without fighting. Their knowledge of the building proved more useful than strength, and nobody lost a life to protect a vegetable bed.

餐廳的料理仰賴當季蔬菜，種植者拒絕把種子收藏交給外人保管。唐安尊重這個決定。她詢問哪些菜源自祖父母的食譜，提議以低耗能蒸煮教學交換部分豆子。黃昏時，河上掠奪者突然來襲，打斷會議。他們想偷的是電池而非食物。林朔命令所有人先斷開灌溉幫浦電源，以免馬達遭破壞。眾人經維修通道撤退，沒有交戰。熟悉建築物比蠻力更有用，也沒有人為了守住菜畦而喪命。

Qiu's expertise in infection control became indispensable when several workers developed rashes after cleaning the flooded basement. She found no evidence that garden vegetables could infect people through ordinary contact, but warned against confusing a rash with proof of safety. The neighborhood's infrastructure had been damaged for years; raw water from the river needed treatment. A league of local growers agreed to publish test results instead of trading accusations. A tournament of cooking teams was postponed until they could guarantee safe ingredients. Some residents called the delay disappointing. An called it worthwhile because a celebration should never force vulnerable families to take an unknown risk.

幾名工人清理淹水地下室後起疹，邱楠的感染管制專業立刻變得不可或缺。她沒有發現菜園蔬菜經一般接觸就會傳染的證據，但提醒大家不能把皮疹當成安全證明。社區基礎設施損壞已久，河水必須先處理。當地種植聯盟同意公布檢驗結果，不再互相指控。烹飪隊的比賽延後，直到食材安全能獲確認。有人對延期失望；唐安卻說值得，因為慶典不應迫使脆弱家庭承擔未知風險。

Shuo returned to the garden the next morning to inspect the small pump. It needed a replacement gasket and a safer electrical cover, repairs the growers could make after training. Instead of taking the machine away, he demonstrated each step in front of them and waited while they repeated it. An brought bread for the workers and sat on the edge of a planting bed, careful not to crush the seedlings. A child asked why they did not simply build walls around everything good. An answered that walls could protect a garden, but food still needed people, paths, water, and trust to grow.

隔天林朔回菜園檢查小幫浦。設備需要新墊圈與更安全的電線蓋，受訓後種植者可以自行修理。他沒有把機器搬走，而是在眾人面前逐步示範，再等他們親手重做。唐安帶麵包給工人，坐在菜畦邊，留意別壓到幼苗。孩子問為什麼不把所有好東西都用牆圍住。唐安回答，牆能保護菜園，但食物仍需要人、道路、水與信任才能生長。

One recipient of the garden's produce was a hospital that had no fresh vegetables. An proposed removing the old security fencing only after installing a sturdy gate with clear access hours. The committee voted for partial removal, preserving one protected plot for seed research. Lele spent the afternoon building a signboard that explained which crops were ready and which were still under observation. The former caretaker brought three radishes to the restaurant as a test of trust. An washed them, sliced them, and served them raw only after checking the water report. Their crunch sounded louder than applause. More important, the growers returned the next day with a written agreement to share both harvests and decisions.

菜園的受贈者之一是一間缺乏新鮮蔬菜的醫院。唐安提議先裝好堅固且有明確開放時間的門，再拆除舊安全圍欄。委員會表決部分拆除，保留一塊受保護地供種子研究。樂樂花一下午製作告示板，說明哪些作物可採、哪些仍在觀察。前管理員帶了三根蘿蔔到餐廳，測試彼此的信任。唐安先洗淨切片，確認水質報告後才生食。那一聲清脆比掌聲還響。更重要的是，種植者隔天帶著共享收成與決策權的書面協議回來。

A week later, An learned that the battery thieves had been stealing power to keep a neonatal ward warm. This did not excuse the raid, but it changed what help might be useful. She met a representative from the ward with the growers present and asked whether a shared charging schedule could prevent another attack. They agreed to try one, with independent measurements and no private threats. The garden would remain open under its members' rules, while the hospital could request surplus vegetables honestly. An remembered the first lesson her mother taught: do not judge a soup before tasting it. She was discovering that the same rule sometimes applied to people.

一週後，唐安得知偷電池的人是為了維持新生兒病房的溫暖。這不能替襲擊開脫，卻改變了可以提供的幫助。她在種植者在場的情況下與病房代表見面，詢問共享充電時程能否避免再次襲擊。雙方同意試行，採獨立量測，不用私人威脅。菜園仍依成員規則開放，醫院則可以正當提出剩餘蔬菜需求。唐安想起母親第一堂課：湯沒嚐過，不要先下判斷。她發現這個道理有時也適用於人。
