The Road That Remembers

末日之後，我開了一家五星級餐廳 · 第 3 / 25 章 · 4000–5000 · 英中對照 · 校訂中

用互動版查詞、朗讀與收藏

On the third morning, a convoy returned from the eastern farms pursued by infected people. The guards reported aggression, yet Qiu noticed that the nearest wanderer moved only toward the smell of burned sugar. Her biological training made her cautious: behavior could suggest a cause, but one observation could not establish a cure. An sent out rice porridge rather than weapons. She had loved the old road because it led to her beloved grandmother's orchard, and the promise to protect its travelers bound her more firmly than any council order. When the shouting stopped, the infected group drifted away from the kitchen vent. The distance was small, but the danger had not vanished.

第三天早晨，一支來自東方農場的車隊被感染者追逐著返回。守衛通報攻擊行為，邱楠卻注意到最靠近的人影只朝焦糖氣味移動。她的生物學訓練讓她很謹慎：行為可以提示原因，但單次觀察不能證明有解藥。唐安派出去的是米粥而不是武器。她一直喜愛這條通往祖母果園的舊路，保護旅人的承諾比任何議會命令都更有約束力。喊叫停止後，感染者群遠離廚房排氣口。距離雖小，危險並未消失。

A visiting executive from the ration authority proposed reducing all portions to diminish the risk of attracting strangers. An spread a thin layer of vegetables over each bowl and asked who would eat after the reduction. 'Hungry people become easy prey for anyone controlling supplies,' she answered. The officer argued that realism required harsh choices. Qiu replied that realistic planning also required clear data and a defined scope. Their disagreement did not end with a speech. They recorded the number of diners, measured the kitchen's sound, and moved the serving line away from the front windows. The officer stayed to watch the experiment, an unexpected sign that he could still learn.

配給局一名主管主張減少所有人的份量，以降低吸引陌生人的風險。唐安在每碗粥上鋪一層薄薄蔬菜，問減量後還有誰吃得飽。「飢餓的人很容易成為控制物資者的獵物。」她說。官員認為現實主義就必須做嚴苛選擇。邱楠回答，務實規劃同樣需要明確數據與清楚範圍。爭論沒有停在演說上。他們記錄用餐人數、測量廚房音量，並把取餐隊伍移離前窗。官員留下來看實驗，意外顯示他還願意學習。

Shuo gave an exhausted shrug when the old delivery shuttle broke down. He had once specialized in repairing transport batteries, so he could straighten a bent contact without replacing the entire motor. Lele tightened a strap around the food boxes, then watched the clock tick while a patrol cleared the road. They planned to deliver soup to the clinic before sunset, but the shortest street passed an infected nest. Qiu suggested a longer route with more places to shelter. An accepted her viewpoint, although it meant carrying less food per trip. A cook who ignored the route would serve no one at all. They tested the straps twice and chose the safer road.

舊運輸車故障時，林朔疲憊地聳了聳肩。他過去專門修理運輸電池，所以能把彎曲的接點扳直，不必更換整個馬達。樂樂把背帶綁緊餐箱，看著時鐘滴答前進，等巡邏員清出道路。他們打算日落前把湯送到診所，但最短路線會經過感染者巢穴。邱楠建議走更長但有更多避難點的路。唐安接受她的觀點，儘管這表示每趟能送的餐食更少。忽略道路的廚師終究服務不了任何人。他們把背帶檢查兩次，選擇較安全的路。

A volunteer attorney visiting the clinic offered to review the delivery records without charging a fee. She warned that the council's authority did not disappear simply because people distrusted it, and any challenge would need proper documentation. An appreciated the distinction. The goal was not to humiliate an official but to make safe routes available to patients. She asked the attorney to teach Lele how to keep a receipt for every delivery. He practiced by labeling the empty bowls, which made the adults smile until they realized his method was remarkably clear.

一名到診所服務的志願律師願免費查閱送餐紀錄。她提醒眾人，對議會不信任不代表議會權限就消失；若要提出異議，仍須正式文件。唐安理解其中差別。目標不是羞辱官員，而是讓病患有安全路線。她請律師教樂樂為每趟運送保留收據。樂樂拿空碗練習貼標籤，惹得大人發笑，直到大家發現他的方式竟然十分清楚。

The authority wanted a virtual demonstration instead of an actual trip. An offered to show both: a map simulation of the risk and the real delivery record afterward. The convoy carried sixteen bowls, four bandages, and a handwritten note from the fisherwoman. It met no infected people, but the bridge was blocked by fallen concrete. Shuo spent an hour clearing a narrow passage while Lele counted the water left in their bottles. By the time they reached the clinic, the soup was warm rather than hot. A patient said it tasted like someone remembered she existed. An wrote that sentence in the ledger without attaching the patient's name.

配給局希望以虛擬示範代替實際運送。唐安提議兩者都做：先用地圖模擬風險，之後再公布真實運送紀錄。車隊帶著十六碗湯、四份繃帶和漁民的手寫便條。一路未遇感染者，橋卻被落下的混凝土堵住。林朔花一小時清出窄道，樂樂則計算瓶裡剩餘的水。抵達診所時，湯仍溫熱，卻不燙口。一名病患說這味道像是有人記得她仍然存在。唐安把這句話記進帳冊，但沒有寫出病人的名字。

Late that night, Qiu showed An the records of patients who had experienced brief moments of calm after eating balanced meals. The pattern was intriguing, but the records included only a handful of cases. 'If we call this a cure now, people may stop seeking proper care,' Qiu warned. An understood. Food could restore strength, comfort a frightened person, and buy time for treatment. It could not reverse five years of loss with one magical spoonful. She hung a notice beside the restaurant's door: 'We serve meals, not miracles. If you are ill, ask the clinic for help.' A few people looked disappointed; several more nodded with relief.

深夜，邱楠讓唐安看一些病人的紀錄：他們吃過均衡餐食後曾短暫平靜。這個現象很有意思，但病例只有少數。「現在就宣稱這是解藥，人們可能不再尋求正當醫療。」邱楠警告。唐安懂了。食物能補充體力、安慰害怕的人，也能爭取治療時間，卻無法靠一匙魔法把五年的失去全部逆轉。她在門邊掛上公告：「我們提供餐食，不提供奇蹟。生病請向診所求助。」有些人失望，更多人則安心點頭。
