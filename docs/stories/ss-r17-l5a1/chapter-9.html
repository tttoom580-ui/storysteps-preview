舊墓園的那條路

The Old Cemetery Route

末日之後，我開了一家五星級餐廳 · 第 9 / 25 章 · 4000–5000 · 英中對照 · 校訂中

用互動版查詞、朗讀與收藏

A hospital ally asked An to deliver food to a settlement behind the cemetery. The request followed an assault on a ration convoy, and the council had invited private bidders to offer security services. Every bid promised safety; none explained how residents without official citizenship papers would enter the protected zone. An said access to food should not be compulsory proof of political loyalty. She organized an expedition with Qiu and Lele and asked everyone to carry identification that the local nurses would accept. The road crossed the outer ruins, where the sky at night seemed full of a distant galaxy, indifferent to the tiny figures moving beneath it. An wondered how the world could remain so beautiful while so many people were frightened.

醫院的一位盟友請唐安向墓園後方聚落送餐。此前配給車隊遭到攻擊，議會邀請私人企業競標保全服務。每份標案都承諾安全，卻沒有說明缺少正式公民文件的居民要如何進入保護區。唐安表示，不應要求人們證明政治忠誠才能取得食物。她和邱楠、樂樂組成遠行隊伍，帶上當地護理師認可的身分資料。道路穿過外城廢墟，夜空像有一整條遙遠銀河，對腳下小小的旅人毫無感覺。唐安不禁想，世界怎能如此美麗，卻又讓那麼多人害怕。

A watchman in the old airport lounge offered them mint tea and warned that the missing convoy might be hiding in the rear of a deserted hotel. A premier trading house had once controlled the route, and its old logo remained on every gate. Lele almost let a stranger snatch his bag, but Qiu caught the strap in time. They took shelter and waited for daylight. An discovered that the hotel's emergency manifest listed more patients than the current clinic had received. Someone had redirected the convoy, though the record did not say why. She photographed the document and left the original untouched. Their purpose was to bring back people, not create another mystery by destroying its evidence.

舊機場休息室的看守端上薄荷茶，提醒他們失蹤車隊可能躲在廢棄旅館後方。過去一家首屈一指的商行控制此路，每道門都還留著它的標誌。陌生人差點搶走樂樂的包，幸好邱楠及時抓住背帶。眾人在避風處等天亮。唐安找到旅館緊急名冊，記載的病患比現在診所接收的人數多。有人把車隊轉往別處，但紀錄沒寫原因。她拍下文件，保留原件不動。他們此行是要找回人，而不是毀掉證據，製造另一個謎團。

Before dawn, a tired driver emerged from a service hatch and asked them to squat behind a wall because infected people gathered near the fuel station. The place had begun to stink of spoiled meat. He explained that the convoy had stopped when a bridge collapsed, then moved patients to an underground laundry room. The settlement had tuna in sealed cans, but no clean fuel to heat the food. An showed them how to prepare a cold grain salad with safe bottled water, then warned against using the suspect underground pipe. She checked each patient's dietary needs with the clinic nurse. No one called the improvised meal elegant. The important thing was that it did not make anyone sicker.

黎明前，疲倦的司機從維修口爬出來，叫他們蹲在牆後，因為加油站附近聚集了感染者。那裡散發腐肉惡臭。他說橋塌後車隊停下，再把病患移到地下洗衣房。聚落有密封鮪魚罐頭，卻沒有乾淨燃料加熱食物。唐安示範用安全瓶裝水製作冷穀物沙拉，提醒別用可疑地下管線的水。她與診所護理師逐一確認病患飲食需求。沒有人說這份臨時料理精緻，但重要的是它不會讓任何人病得更重。

At the exterior gate of the deserted hotel, Lele began to distract the guards with questions about the building's history. An asked him to stop when she realized the extra noise might attract infected people. They retreated behind an old service wall and checked the map instead. The driver pointed out a safer corridor that the council's new plan had missed. Even after years of ruin, former workers remembered passages that existed nowhere on official maps. An wrote their directions in the field notebook, including how confident each witness was about what they remembered.

在廢棄旅館外側門口，樂樂不斷問守衛建築歷史，分散了注意力。唐安發現多餘噪音可能吸引感染者，請他停下。眾人退到舊維修牆後，改查地圖。司機指出議會新方案漏掉的安全走廊。即使廢棄多年，老員工仍記得官方地圖沒有的通道。唐安把方向寫入現場筆記，也記下每位證人對記憶有多確定。

When the group returned, a council commentator accused An of staging the rescue to attract attention. She did not embrace the accusation or dismiss it with anger. Instead, she published the route, medical transfer times, and names of officials who had witnessed the handover, withholding patient identities. Mara offered to examine the records independently. The result was inconvenient for both sides: the first road closure had been reasonable, but later communication failures had caused unnecessary fear. An asked the council to reopen a public lounge for travelers and post a clear emergency map. The council accepted the map and postponed its private security contract. It was not victory over an enemy; it was one safer route through a city still learning to live.

車隊回來後，議會評論員指控唐安為了吸引目光才安排救援。她沒有接受指控，也沒有憤怒駁斥，而是公布路線、醫療轉送時間以及見證交接的官員姓名，同時隱去病患身分。瑪拉願意獨立查核。結果對兩邊都不完全方便：第一次封路有合理理由，但後續通報失誤確實造成不必要恐慌。唐安要求議會重開旅人公共休息處，張貼清楚緊急地圖。議會接受地圖，延後私人保全合約。這不是戰勝敵人，而是一座還在學習生活的城市，多了一條較安全的路。

At the end of the trip, An placed a can of tuna on the kitchen shelf beside the radishes and dried beans. The items came from different places and carried different stories, yet they could become one dinner if people made room for each other. She thought of the grave markers outside the cemetery, where families had left bowls of rice for those who would never come home. In the restaurant, she wanted to cook for the living without forgetting the dead. Her next menu would include a remembrance meal, free to anyone who needed a quiet table. Lele offered to set the chairs before sunrise, when the city was least likely to notice them working.

旅程結束時，唐安把一罐鮪魚放在蘿蔔與乾豆旁。食物來自不同地方，背後各有故事，卻能在彼此讓出位置後組成一頓晚餐。她想到墓園外的碑，家屬在那裡留下米飯給永遠回不了家的人。她希望在餐廳為生者煮飯時，也不忘死者。下一份菜單會增加一餐紀念餐，讓需要安靜座位的人免費用餐。樂樂提議黎明前先排好椅子，因為那時城市最不容易注意到他們工作。

Qiu compared familiar broth, unfamiliar broth, and plain water under the clinic’s ordinary care rules. Two volunteers briefly recalled names after the familiar meal; the others showed no clear change. The small sample proved neither a reliable treatment nor a cause. It did show that careful listening was more useful than announcing a miracle to frightened families.

邱楠依診所平常照護規定，比對熟悉的湯、陌生的湯與清水。兩名志願者在熟悉的食物後短暫想起名字，其他人沒有明確變化。少量樣本既不能證明可靠治療，也不能確認原因；但它顯示，仔細傾聽比向恐懼的家庭宣布奇蹟更有用。
