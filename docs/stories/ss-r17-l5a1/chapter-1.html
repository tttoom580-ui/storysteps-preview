A Table in the Ruins

末日之後，我開了一家五星級餐廳 · 第 1 / 25 章 · 4000–5000 · 英中對照 · 校訂中

Five years after the outbreak, Tang An returned to the restaurant where her mother had taught her to taste soup before adding salt. The kitchen's first batch of rice was grey with dust, and the solar circuit beneath the counter had almost burned through. Lin Shuo, a former electrical engineer, offered to repair it in exchange for one decent dinner. When the first pot began to boil, dried vegetable powder started to dissolve into clear broth. An called the meal an ordinary beginning, not a breakthrough. Yet a woman waiting outside pressed both hands against the glass as if she had seen a miracle. The sign above the door still promised five stars, a joke left behind by a world that no longer issued reviews.

疫情爆發五年後，唐安回到母親教她在加鹽前先嚐湯的餐廳。廚房裡第一批米沾滿灰塵，櫃檯下的太陽能電路也幾乎燒毀。前電機工程師林朔提出修理電路，交換一頓像樣的晚餐。第一鍋水沸騰時，乾燥蔬菜粉慢慢溶進清湯。唐安說這只是平凡的開始，談不上突破。然而，窗外等候的女人卻把雙手貼在玻璃上，彷彿看見奇蹟。門上的招牌依然寫著五星，那是已經沒有人打分數的舊世界留下的玩笑。

'Only an idiot would open a restaurant where infected people roam,' said the guard who controlled the crossing. An refused to trade him all her food for protection. She would implement a simple rule: everyone could eat, but every diner had to help clean or contribute supplies if able. She sent an inquiry to the neighborhood council and invited her old mentor, Nurse Qiu Nan, to inspect the cooking water. Qiu represented a nonprofit clinic and knew what hunger could do to patients. She explained that food placement mattered as much as quantity: soup near the doorway attracted dust, while covered bowls on the inner counter stayed safe. A kitchen should make people healthier, not merely make them stop complaining.

「只有笨蛋才會在感染者遊蕩的地方開餐廳。」掌管路口的守衛說。唐安拒絕用所有食物換取保護。她決定實施一條簡單規則：人人都能吃飯，但有能力的食客必須協助清潔或提供物資。她向街區議會提出詢問，並邀請昔日導師、護理師邱楠檢查煮食用水。邱楠服務於非營利診所，深知飢餓對病患的影響。她解釋，食物放置的位置與數量一樣重要：門口的湯容易沾灰，內側櫃檯上加蓋的碗則比較安全。廚房應該讓人更健康，而不只是讓人停止抱怨。

A faded projection on the wall showed the city's former restaurant rating system: sanitation, fairness, nourishment, sustainability, and community trust. Under the new ration regime, none of those measures mattered to the authorities; only stored calories did. An copied the five categories onto a clean board regardless of whether the council approved. Shuo used scrap metal to reinforce the back door, then bent a spoon against the rim of a cracked pot to test the thickness of its steel. They heard a bicycle saddle creak outside. A small courier had arrived carrying onions instead of weapons. For the first time, people were beginning to bring ingredients to a place they could have passed without stopping.

牆上褪色的投影顯示城市舊時餐廳評分的五項標準：衛生、公平、營養、永續與社區信任。新的配給制度只在乎儲存熱量，當局根本不看這些指標。唐安不論議會准不准，都把五項標準抄到乾淨的板上。林朔用廢鐵加固後門，又把湯匙抵在裂鍋的邊緣，檢查鋼材厚度。門外傳來腳踏車坐墊的嘎吱聲。一名年輕信差送來洋蔥，而不是武器。人們第一次開始把食材帶到原本可以直接走過的地方。

During the first quiet hour, An divided the remaining flour into portions and asked every volunteer to write down what they had used. No one liked counting a handful of onions when so many people were hungry, but the record helped them avoid promising meals they could not prepare. She also drew a small diagram of the front room showing where the children should wait, where water could be collected, and which door must never be blocked. Lele studied the picture, then asked to make a copy for the clinic. An gave him the chalk and watched him explain the plan to another volunteer.

第一段安靜時刻，唐安把剩餘麵粉分份，請每名志工寫下用量。人人都不喜歡在飢荒時清點幾顆洋蔥，紀錄卻能避免答應做不到的餐數。她也畫出前廳小圖，標明孩子等候區、取水處，以及絕不能堵住的門。樂樂仔細研究，問能否替診所複製一份。唐安把粉筆交給他，看著他向另一位志工解釋計畫。

The strain of carrying hot water across broken tiles made An's arms shake. The courier whispered that an approaching pack could terrify even experienced patrols. Shuo proposed building a silent entrance, while Qiu insisted on a clear evacuation route. By midnight, the line outside had grown to triple its original length, but nobody pushed to the front. An gave the courier the last bowl and asked his name. 'Lele,' he answered. He was seventeen and had spent most of his childhood learning which streets not to cross. An told him tomorrow's menu would depend on what they could grow, not on whatever an invisible system might produce. Five stars could wait. Tonight, eleven people went to sleep with warm food inside them.

穿過破碎地磚搬運熱水，使唐安的手臂因負荷而發抖。信差低聲說，靠近的感染者群足以嚇壞最有經驗的巡邏員。林朔提議搭建安靜的入口，邱楠則堅持保留清楚的撤離路線。午夜時，門外隊伍已變成原來三倍長，卻沒有人插隊。唐安把最後一碗湯交給信差，問他的名字。「樂樂。」他回答。十七歲的他，童年大半時間都在學習哪些街道不能走。唐安說明天的菜單取決於大家能種出什麼，而不是期待看不見的系統憑空變出食物。五星可以慢慢來。今晚，有十一個人帶著一肚子的溫暖入睡。

After the customers left, An washed every bowl twice and marked the broken shelves with chalk. Her mother had once said that a restaurant was not defined by its sign but by what happened to someone after leaving its table. The words seemed unbearably hopeful in a ruined city. Still, she wrote the first entry in a new ledger: 'Day one. Eleven meals served. No illness reported. More water needed.' Qiu added a warning about the untested well. Shuo added the exact number of watts the stove consumed. Lele drew a tiny star. Each note was small, but together they were a promise that tomorrow would be measured, rather than merely wished for.

客人離開後，唐安把每個碗洗了兩次，並用粉筆標出損壞的架子。母親曾說，一家餐廳的價值不在招牌，而在客人離開餐桌後發生了什麼。在廢墟城市裡，這句話樂觀得令人難受。她仍在新帳冊寫下第一筆：「第一天，供餐十一份，未通報不適，需要更多水。」邱楠加上尚未檢驗水井的警告。林朔寫出爐子消耗的確切瓦數。樂樂畫了一顆小星星。每個註記都很小，合在一起卻是一個承諾：明天要用紀錄來衡量，而不只是祈求。
