第一顆星

The First Star

末日之後，我開了一家五星級餐廳 · 第 5 / 25 章 · 4000–5000 · 英中對照 · 校訂中

用互動版查詞、朗讀與收藏

On the fifth day, the dining room filled with people whose attendance would once have counted as a successful opening. Lele arrived with a bruise on his elbow from repairing the window frames. A visiting designer said the restaurant needed a contemporary look to attract rich customers. An replied that she would contend for clean water before buying fashionable furniture. The designer accused her of being controversial, but even he accepted a hot meal. The largest corporation in the northern district sent a representative to ask whether the restaurant might become a commercial franchise. An invited him to stay for the public inspection instead. She wanted to see whether the kitchen deserved to keep its door open before discussing expansion.

第五天，餐廳坐滿了人。若在過去，這樣的出席人數已足以稱為開幕成功。樂樂修理窗框時撞傷手肘，帶著瘀青到場。來訪設計師說，餐廳需要當代風格才能吸引有錢顧客。唐安回答，在購買時髦家具前，她要先爭取乾淨用水。設計師說她愛惹爭議，自己卻也接受一碗熱餐。北區最大企業派代表詢問能否把餐廳發展成商業加盟。唐安請他先留下參加公開稽核。她想先知道廚房是否值得繼續開門，再談擴張。

The inspection began with an unusual challenge: each diner could point out one flaw, and nobody on the staff could defy a reasonable safety request. Qiu endorsed the procedure, not because it guaranteed perfection, but because it allowed people to correct mistakes before anyone was hurt. An said the essence of a restaurant was reliable service, not a clever title. Shuo developed a checklist to facilitate handwashing, storage, and fire safety. Lele looked at the greasy ceiling and laughed at the irony: they were preparing to earn a star while standing under a roof that still leaked. An handed him a ladder, and together they repaired the worst gap with a sheet of salvaged metal.

稽核從一個奇特挑戰開始：每名食客都可以指出一項缺點，工作人員不得無理拒絕合理的安全要求。邱楠支持這項程序，不是因為它保證完美，而是因為能在有人受傷前修正錯誤。唐安說，餐廳的本質是可靠服務，不是漂亮名號。林朔做了一份檢查表，方便管理洗手、儲存與消防。樂樂抬頭看油膩天花板，笑說真諷刺：他們正準備賺取一顆星，頭頂卻還漏雨。唐安遞給他梯子，兩人用回收鐵板補好最嚴重的破洞。

For the communal meal, an elderly resident brought a small offering of pickled radishes, still wrapped in a clean cloth. Everyone received the same basic outfit for kitchen work: apron, gloves, and a scarf for covering hair. The inspectors rated the overall procedure rather than judging one impressive dish. From her perspective, An could not call the restaurant successful if a person with limited mobility could not reach the table. The resident suggested moving the chairs farther apart. They changed the layout before serving, and the ordinary action felt like a ritual of welcome. For the first time in years, a meal could be shared without people first proving their place in a ration line.

社區聚餐時，一名長者帶來一小份用乾淨布包著的醃蘿蔔。所有協助廚務的人都領到同樣的基本裝備：圍裙、手套與頭巾。稽核員評估整體流程，而不是只看一道令人驚豔的菜。以唐安的角度，若行動不便的人無法抵達餐桌，餐廳就不能算成功。長者建議把椅子拉開一些。他們在供餐前調整了座位，這平凡的動作像一項歡迎儀式。多年來第一次，人們不用先證明自己在配給名冊上的位置，就能一起吃飯。

During cleanup, a young volunteer asked whether a star meant the customers could no longer criticize the food. An turned the question back to the committee. The members answered that recognition should increase accountability rather than end it. They agreed to leave a notebook beside the door for suggestions, with a separate page for safety complaints requiring immediate attention. Shuo installed a lockable box so people could report problems without exposing their names. By midnight it contained three notes: more chairs, softer porridge, and better light by the stairs. All three would be considered before breakfast.

收拾時，年輕志工問有星星後，客人是否就不能再批評餐點。唐安把問題交給委員會。委員回答，認可應增加問責，而不是結束它。大家同意門邊放意見本，另設需要立刻處理的安全申訴頁。林朔裝了一個可上鎖的箱子，讓人不必暴露姓名也能反映問題。午夜時，裡面有三張紙條：更多椅子、較軟的粥、樓梯旁更亮的燈。早餐前大家都會討論。

An divided the soup into each service segment and recorded how many portions came back untouched. The statistical notes showed that children preferred softer vegetables and older patients wanted smaller bowls. A transition to a larger kitchen might eventually make sense, but it would also increase costs and water use. The inspection committee voted on five conditions. Sanitation passed. Access passed with a warning. Food safety required more tests. Supply reliability remained uncertain. Community trust passed by a narrow margin. The restaurant received its first star, drawn not by a secret machine but by eleven elected neighbors. Lele nearly cried, then insisted it was smoke from the stove.

唐安把湯分到各供餐區段，記錄有多少份幾乎沒動就被退回。統計筆記顯示，孩子偏好較軟的蔬菜，年長病患需要小碗。日後轉型成更大的廚房也許合理，但成本與用水也會增加。稽核委員就五項條件投票：衛生通過，無障礙通行帶警告通過，食品安全還需更多檢測，供應穩定性仍不確定，社區信任則以微弱優勢通過。餐廳獲得第一顆星，不是秘密機器頒發，而是十一名選出的鄰居畫上的。樂樂幾乎哭了，卻堅稱是爐煙跑進眼睛。

After the guests went home, An turned off the stove and listened to rain striking the repaired roof. There were no dramatic fireworks, no hidden reward chest, and no voice announcing that the world had changed. But the kitchen smelled of clean rice, and the clinic had enough soup set aside for tomorrow. Shuo asked whether she was disappointed that the first star had taken so much work. 'No,' she said. 'I would be frightened by a star that came without anyone checking what we did.' She folded the inspection sheet and placed it next to her mother's old recipe book. One star was not a victory over the outbreak. It was permission from neighbors to try again.

客人回家後，唐安關掉爐火，聽雨水敲打修補過的屋頂。沒有炫目的煙火、隱藏獎勵箱，也沒有聲音宣布世界已經改變。但廚房飄著乾淨米飯的香氣，診所明天要用的湯也已留好。林朔問她，第一顆星付出這麼多工作，會不會失望。「不會。」她說，「如果一顆星不用任何人檢查就出現，我反而害怕。」她把稽核表折好，放在母親的舊食譜旁。一顆星不是戰勝疫情，而是鄰居們准許她再試一次。

The first star meant more than clean tables. One guest who had lost her speech after infection ate An’s warm mushroom broth and briefly recalled the way home. The memory faded before she reached the doorway. An wrote down the exact recipe and timing, because a fleeting human moment should not be sold as a permanent medical cure.

第一顆星不只代表桌子乾淨。一位感染後失去語言能力的客人喝下唐安的熱蘑菇湯，短暫想起回家的路。她走到門口前，記憶便消失了。唐安記下食譜與精確時間，因為一瞬間的人性光芒不該被當作永久解藥販售。
