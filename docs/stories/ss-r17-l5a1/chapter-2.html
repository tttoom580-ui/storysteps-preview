一條魚的價值

The Price of a Fish

末日之後，我開了一家五星級餐廳 · 第 2 / 25 章 · 4000–5000 · 英中對照 · 校訂中

用互動版查詞、朗讀與收藏

The council called the restaurant an absurd luxury during an adverse winter, and the building's broken architecture hardly supported An's case. A donor from Northpoint offered money for a freezer if she agreed to charge the poorest residents first. Qiu called that an excessive condition. An chose to highlight the clause instead of hiding it; the meeting could not make a fair choice if people did not know the price. She offered a smaller freezer built from salvaged panels. It would hold fewer portions, but no one would lose a meal to pay for its door. Shuo examined the wiring and warned that even generous gifts could become expensive when maintenance belonged to someone else.

議會說在惡劣的冬季開餐廳是荒唐奢侈，建築物破敗的結構也不利於唐安辯護。北岬區一名捐助者願意出錢買冷凍櫃，條件卻是先向最貧困的居民收費。邱楠說這要求太過分。唐安選擇把條款標出來，而不是藏起來；人們若不知道代價，就無法作公平決定。她改提議用回收板材製作小型冷凍櫃。容量雖少，卻不用犧牲任何人的餐食來買門。林朔檢查線路，提醒大家：即使是慷慨捐贈，若維修責任落在別人身上，也可能很昂貴。

A former teacher explained how the ration office's ideology divided people into 'productive' and 'dependent' mouths. An found the categories insulting. At the market she met an indigenous fishing family that had protected the western river through the first years of disaster. Their preserved salmon was precious, not because it was exotic, but because the family knew how to smoke it without wasting fuel. Mara, a journalist who still believed in careful journalism, asked permission before recording their account. She said the public deserved to know who produced the city's food. 'We are not a decoration for your article,' the fisherwoman replied. Mara put away her camera and helped carry the baskets.

一名退休教師說明配給辦公室的意識形態如何把人分成「有生產力」與「依賴者」兩種嘴巴。唐安認為這分類很侮辱人。在市場裡，她遇見一家在災變初期守護西河的原住漁民。他們保存的鮭魚十分珍貴，不是因為稀奇，而是因為全家知道如何省燃料燻製。仍相信審慎新聞工作的記者瑪拉，錄音前先請求同意。她說公眾有權知道食物由誰生產。「我們不是妳文章裡的裝飾。」女漁民回答。瑪拉收起相機，改為幫忙搬魚籃。

To the council's inspectors, An was an outsider asking too many questions. To the fisherwoman, she was simply someone who washed her hands before touching a knife. The elder gave Shuo a shoulder massage after he carried a heavy ice box up three flights of stairs; the gesture embarrassed him but made everyone laugh. Meanwhile, the donor's pressure continued to persist. A lawyer warned that prosecution for fraud would require evidence of actual deception, not just an offensive contract. An listened. Her quest was to keep the kitchen open and safe, not to win an argument by declaring enemies. She decided to publish every offer, every condition, and every refusal on the same noticeboard.

對議會稽查員而言，唐安是個問題太多的外來者。對女漁民而言，她不過是個拿刀前會洗手的人。林朔把重冰箱搬上三層樓後，長老替他按摩肩膀；他尷尬的模樣逗得大家發笑。另一方面，捐助者的施壓持續不斷。一名律師提醒，若要以詐欺起訴，必須有實際欺騙證據，而不只是令人反感的契約。唐安聽進去了。她的任務是讓廚房繼續安全營運，不是把所有反對者都宣布成敵人。她決定在同一面公告板公開每份提案、條件及拒絕理由。

Before the fish was cooked, An set aside a portion for a small tasting session with the people who had supplied it. She asked which flavors were traditional and which changes would feel disrespectful. The fisherwoman laughed and said a recipe was not a sacred museum object, but it was important to know who had taught it. Together they adjusted the salt and cooking time. Mara wrote down the family name beside the recipe, then checked the spelling twice. The restaurant had gained a dish, and the fishers had gained the right to tell its story in their own words.

烹魚前，唐安先留一部分，請提供食材的人試吃。她問哪些味道是傳統、哪些改動可能讓人不舒服。女漁民笑說食譜不是神聖博物館收藏，但應該知道最初是誰傳授。大家一起調整鹽量與烹煮時間。瑪拉把家族姓名寫在食譜旁，仔細核對拼字。餐廳多了一道菜，漁民也保有用自己語言說故事的權利。

The first trial dish used smoked fish in a hot grain soup. An refused to render the salmon tasteless by drowning it in salt. When Lele tried to rip the menu from its frame, she caught his wrist: the paper listed an old price, but it was still evidence of how the restaurant had changed. She prepared a series of small bowls and offered a variation without fish for people with allergies. The family watched carefully. Their approval mattered more to An than the council's applause, because the ingredients had been entrusted to her rather than purchased at a market. Qiu inspected the bowls and found no contamination. Only then did An announce that the evening meal was ready.

第一道試作菜用煙燻魚煮熱穀湯。唐安不願用鹽淹沒鮭魚原味，把它變得索然無味。樂樂想從框裡撕掉舊菜單時，她握住他的手腕：紙上雖寫著舊價錢，仍是餐廳如何改變的證據。她準備一系列小碗，另做一款不加魚的版本，供過敏者食用。漁民一家仔細看著。對唐安而言，他們的認可比議會掌聲更重要，因為食材是託付給她，而非她在市場買來。邱楠檢查餐碗，未發現污染，唐安這才宣布晚餐準備好了。

That night, Mara asked whether she could photograph the kitchen's tiny freezer. An agreed on the condition that the image include the repair notes and the person responsible for cleaning it. 'Otherwise people will think a machine saved us,' she said. Mara smiled at the word 'us.' At the door, the donor's messenger left without buying a meal. An did not celebrate his departure. She knew the city would need partnerships, funding, and difficult conversations. What she would not accept was a deal that turned hunger into a debt people could never repay. On the wall, Lele drew a second chalk circle beside the unfinished star.

當晚，瑪拉問能否拍攝廚房的小冷凍櫃。唐安同意，但條件是照片要拍進維修紀錄與負責清潔的人。「不然大家會以為是機器救了我們。」她說。瑪拉聽見「我們」一詞，微微一笑。門口，捐助者的使者沒有用餐就離開。唐安沒有慶祝，她知道城市需要合作、資金與艱難的對話。她不能接受的，是把飢餓變成人們永遠還不清的債。牆上，樂樂在尚未完成的星星旁畫了第二個粉筆圓圈。

After dinner, a mildly infected stranger tasted the ginger rice his mother used to cook. For ninety seconds he remembered her red scarf and said her name without prompting. Qiu recorded the event as a temporary return of memory, not a recovery from infection, and insisted that nobody repeat the meal without the patient’s informed agreement.

晚餐後，一名輕度感染的陌生人嚐到母親過去煮的薑汁飯。九十秒內，他想起母親的紅圍巾，並且不經提示說出她的名字。邱楠把事件記為短暫記憶恢復，而非感染痊癒，並堅持未經病人知情同意，不得再次進行。
