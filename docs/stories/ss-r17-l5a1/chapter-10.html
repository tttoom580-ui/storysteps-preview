被封住的實驗室

The Sealed Laboratory

末日之後，我開了一家五星級餐廳 · 第 10 / 25 章 · 4000–5000 · 英中對照 · 校訂中

用互動版查詞、朗讀與收藏

A damaged medical archive contained records about abortion care, emergency childbirth, and the right to informed treatment; An insisted those sensitive files remain private. An anonymous note then directed her to the sealed food laboratory beneath a bronze dome. The message sounded arrogant, as if its writer knew how to betray everyone without being noticed. Shuo persuaded the team to verify the location before visiting. Inside, they found a butcher's cold room converted into a research station and a row of locked sample cabinets. Qiu warned that old records had to be checked for credibility and that finding a laboratory did not prove it caused the outbreak. They took photographs without opening any dangerous containers.

受損醫療檔案裡有墮胎照護、緊急生產和知情治療權的紀錄；唐安堅持這些敏感資料必須保密。接著一封匿名紙條引她前往青銅圓頂下被封鎖的食品實驗室。紙條語氣傲慢，彷彿寫信者知道怎麼背叛所有人而不被發現。林朔說服團隊先核實地點再前往。裡面有一座由肉舖冷藏室改建的研究站，以及上鎖的樣本櫃。邱楠提醒，舊紀錄必須檢驗可信度，找到實驗室不代表它就是疫情源頭。他們只拍照，不打開危險容器。

Sol explained that the company's original entrepreneur had funded research into food preservation and human genetics. The project investigated whether particular bacteria could influence stress responses by releasing a molecule during fermentation. Its initial initiative had sounded promising, but the test plans were inconsistent. Some staff wanted to integrate medical monitoring into food distribution; others insisted on selling the product first. A note in the margin identified a failed batch that had caused severe agitation in animals. Qiu closed the folder and said there was no justification for experimenting with the material now. An agreed. She was running a restaurant, not a private clinic with permission to test unproven substances on hungry customers.

索爾解釋，公司的創辦企業家原先資助食品保存及人體遺傳學研究。計畫調查某些細菌在發酵時釋出的分子，是否能影響壓力反應。最初的倡議看似有希望，測試方案卻不一致。有些員工希望把醫療監測納入配餐流程，另一些人堅持先賣產品。頁邊註記一批失敗樣本曾使動物嚴重躁動。邱楠闔上資料夾，表示現在絕無理由再拿材料做實驗。唐安同意。她開的是餐廳，不是獲准在飢餓顧客身上測試未知物質的私人診所。

An found a ridiculous invoice showing that the company had charged a mortgage-sized fee to a rural hospital for a small fermentation unit. The finance sector had apparently treated desperate patients as a guaranteed market. One technician had protested that the machines were impossible to sustain without clean water, but the warning had been removed from later brochures. The laboratory had also published a list of animal trials, some with missing results. Mara agreed to request independent review rather than testify about conclusions they had not established. Shuo measured the vertical pipes leading out of the room. The system might connect to the municipal water network, but several sections were collapsed. They needed a qualified engineer and a safe inspection plan before following them.

唐安找到一張荒唐發票：公司向偏遠醫院收取近乎房貸規模的費用，只賣給對方一台小型發酵裝置。金融部門似乎把絕望的病患當成保證市場。有技術員警告，沒有乾淨用水就無法長期維持設備，後來的宣傳冊卻刪去了這項提醒。實驗室還公布過動物試驗清單，其中部分結果缺失。瑪拉同意尋求獨立審查，不替尚未證實的結論作證。林朔量測通往外面的垂直管線。系統可能連到市政供水網，但好幾段已坍塌。若要繼續追查，必須找合格工程師制定安全檢查計畫。

Qiu refused to let the old laboratory data replace present-day clinical care. Some pages were so damaged that even the dates could not be trusted, and other pages used technical terms differently from current practice. Sol made a list of what could be checked by independent specialists and what could only be preserved as historical evidence. An asked whether restoring the archive would help people understand their food supply. He said perhaps, provided they never used recovered equipment without testing it. The answer was modest, but it was a beginning the team could defend in public.

邱楠拒絕讓舊實驗室資料取代現今臨床照護。有些頁面損毀到連日期都不可信，其他頁面的技術術語又與現在不同。索爾列出哪些能由獨立專家查核，哪些只能保存成歷史證據。唐安問修復檔案是否能幫人理解食物供應。他說也許可以，但復原設備未經測試絕不能直接使用。答案保守，卻是團隊能公開辯護的起點。

Outside the dome, wildlife had returned to the abandoned courtyard. Birds nested in the old warning signs, and vines pushed between concrete blocks. An watched them for a long moment before returning to the restaurant. She was relieved to find the kitchen busy with people arguing about dinner, a problem both smaller and more urgent than what they had discovered. She instructed Lele to serve familiar dishes and not mention the laboratory until Qiu could explain the uncertainty without causing panic. The next day they would give a public account of where they had been and what they had actually found. That was how the second star would have to be earned: through careful work, not a frightening announcement.

圓頂外，野生動物已回到廢棄庭院。鳥兒在舊警告牌上築巢，藤蔓從水泥縫隙鑽出。唐安看了很久，才回到餐廳。她看見廚房忙著討論晚餐，反而鬆了一口氣；這問題比新發現小，卻更迫切。她請樂樂先供應大家熟悉的菜，不要在邱楠能完整說明不確定性前散播實驗室消息。隔天，他們會公開交代去了哪裡、真正找到什麼。第二顆星也必須這樣獲得：靠審慎工作，而非可怕的宣告。

At closing time, An examined the old cookbook beside the new laboratory photographs. One recorded her mother's instructions for making nourishing food with patience; the other showed how easily research could become a product before anyone understood its risks. She did not decide that science was evil or that tradition was always right. Both required careful hands and honest records. With that in mind, she wrote three questions for the public review: who owns the pipes, who approved the earlier tests, and what can be said about the samples without exposing anyone to danger? No question offered a quick ending to the disaster. All three were worth asking.

關門時，唐安把母親食譜放在新的實驗室照片旁。一邊記載如何耐心做出滋養人的食物；另一邊顯示研究在風險尚未被理解前，如何容易變成商品。她沒有因此認定科學邪惡，也沒有認定傳統永遠正確。兩者都需要小心的手與誠實紀錄。她寫下三個供公開審查的問題：管線歸誰、早年的試驗由誰核准、如何在不使任何人涉險的前提下說明樣本？沒有一個問題能快速結束末日，但都值得追問。
