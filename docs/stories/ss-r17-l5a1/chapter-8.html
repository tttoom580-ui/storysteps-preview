Who Owns a Recipe?

末日之後，我開了一家五星級餐廳 · 第 8 / 25 章 · 4000–5000 · 英中對照 · 校訂中

用互動版查詞、朗讀與收藏

An read the research notes with caution. The city's last food laboratory had treated fermented bean paste as a valuable commodity, but its reports lacked clarity about human testing. An wanted to dedicate the next week to understanding which traditional dishes survived without refrigeration. A delegation from the old university brought a dreadful warning: a company claimed ownership of any recipe prepared with its patented starter culture. The demand raised ethical questions about who could own food knowledge passed through generations. Qiu insisted that the kitchen's exploration be recorded and that patients consent before any experiment. A recipe could be shared freely without pretending that evidence of its medical effects already existed.

唐安謹慎地閱讀研究筆記。城市最後一間食品實驗室把發酵豆醬視為珍貴商品，報告卻未清楚說明人體測試。唐安打算用接下來一週研究哪些傳統料理不用冷藏也能保存。舊大學代表團帶來可怕消息：一家公司主張，只要使用它的專利菌種製作，食譜就歸它所有。這涉及代代相傳的飲食知識究竟能不能被誰獨占的倫理問題。邱楠要求所有探索留下紀錄，試驗前必須取得病患同意。食譜可以自由分享，卻不能假裝已證實它有醫療效果。

A former undergraduate researcher named Sol demonstrated impressive fluency in the laboratory's damaged code. His generator could keep the test chamber warm, but the equipment inside had not been serviced for years. The team inspected the interior before connecting power. They discovered that the company's patent covered a particular industrial filter, not the ordinary practice of fermenting vegetables. Its premium label had frightened people into buying unnecessary packets. The company had also offered to purchase the sole surviving set of community recipes. An refused. The old notebooks belonged to families who had shared them; she would not let a commercial claim erase their names from the pages.

曾是大學生研究員的索爾，熟練地展示如何操作受損的實驗室程式。他的發電機能讓測試室保溫，但室內設備多年未維護。團隊檢查內部後才接電。他們發現公司專利涵蓋的是特定工業濾材，並不是一般蔬菜發酵方法。昂貴標籤讓人誤以為必須購買不必要的菌包。公司還想買下社區僅存的一套食譜。唐安拒絕了。那些舊筆記屬於分享食譜的家庭，她不能讓商業主張把他們的名字從頁面抹去。

Shuo asked the group to steer clear of superstition when discussing the infected. A few survivors believed the smell of fermented cabbage could drive every zombie away, but earlier patrols had observed conflicting responses. The team designed an experiment with strict distance and safety limits, observing from behind reinforced glass. The result did not prove that the meal prevented infection. It suggested only that a calmer environment and a familiar smell sometimes reduced agitation in recently ill people. Qiu explained each limit publicly. She thanked the researchers for their work while refusing to call a preliminary observation a miracle. Their honesty impressed more listeners than any spectacular claim could have done.

林朔請眾人討論感染者時避開迷信。有些倖存者相信發酵白菜的味道可以驅走所有喪屍，但過往巡邏觀察並不一致。團隊設計嚴格限定距離與安全條件的試驗，隔著強化玻璃觀察。結果沒有證明食物能預防感染，只顯示較平靜的環境及熟悉的味道，有時能降低近期病患的躁動。邱楠公開說明每個限制。她感謝研究者，卻拒絕把初步觀察說成奇蹟。這份誠實比華麗宣傳更能打動聽眾。

To generate reliable results, the team kept a control record of ordinary meals served on the same days as the new fermented dishes. Sol made the forms simple enough for volunteers to complete without pretending to be laboratory scientists. A patient who did not want to participate still received dinner and treatment. An insisted that refusing an observation should never lead to a smaller portion. The researchers agreed, and the rule was printed above the kitchen sink. Nothing in the study was worth more than the dignity of someone asking for food.

為產生可靠結果，團隊在新發酵料理供應的同一天，也記錄普通餐食作對照。索爾讓表格簡單到志工不必假裝科學家也能填寫。不願參與觀察的病患仍照常用餐與接受治療。唐安堅持拒絕研究不能導致份量縮小。研究者同意，把規則印在洗手台上方。研究裡沒有任何東西比前來求食之人的尊嚴更重要。

That evening, An gave Sol a chore he had not expected: washing the empty jars. He laughed, then worked beside the farmers whose recipes he had read. At dinner, they compared different vegetable preparations and recorded how much fuel each required. Rather than shed responsibility by blaming the old company, Sol offered to help repair the lab's public records. The group chose the restaurant as the venue for the next discussion because it was accessible to people without university credentials. An served simple noodles, and everyone ate the same meal. What mattered was not a mysterious ingredient that could end the disaster. It was the growing ability to test, argue, correct, and share knowledge safely.

那晚，唐安交給索爾一件意想不到的雜務：洗空罐。他笑了，接著和那些食譜曾被他研究的農民並肩工作。晚餐時，大家比較不同蔬菜做法，記錄每道菜需要多少燃料。索爾沒有把責任全推給舊公司，而是主動協助修復實驗室公共紀錄。眾人選在餐廳舉行下次討論，因為這裡沒有大學學歷也能參與。唐安端出簡單麵食，大家吃著一樣的餐點。真正重要的不是一種能終結災難的神祕食材，而是逐漸建立起來的安全測試、辯論、修正與分享知識的能力。

Before closing, Qiu wrote three words beneath the star board: 'Evidence before hope.' An added a fourth: 'Hope nevertheless.' She believed people needed reasons to keep cooking and planting even when medicine had limits. The lab's recovered notes offered such a reason, but only if every uncertain result stayed marked as uncertain. Lele copied the instructions into a smaller notebook for the outer settlements, replacing technical language with drawings of pots, lids, and clean water. The next morning, the university team agreed to release its findings without charging for access. A small door in the disaster's long wall had opened, not through a secret power but through shared work.

關門前，邱楠在星星板下寫了三個字：「證據優先。」唐安補上：「仍要希望。」她相信，即使醫學有界限，人們仍需要繼續烹飪與耕種的理由。實驗室復原的筆記能提供一點理由，但前提是每個不確定結果都得標明不確定。樂樂把指示抄到較小的筆記本，畫出鍋、蓋與乾淨用水，讓外圍聚落也看得懂。隔天早晨，大學團隊同意免費公開研究成果。災難的高牆似乎開了一道小門，靠的不是祕密力量，而是共享勞動。
