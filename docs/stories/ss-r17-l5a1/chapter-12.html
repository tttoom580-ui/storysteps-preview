The Accusation

末日之後，我開了一家五星級餐廳 · 第 12 / 25 章 · 4000–5000 · 英中對照 · 校訂中

用互動版查詞、朗讀與收藏

The morning began with an abnormal noise from the refrigeration room. Shuo found that someone had loosened a bolt, leaving the cooler's power connection barely compatible with the salvaged generator. He had become acquainted with enough failure patterns to know this was probably not ordinary wear. When guards arrived to confront An, they accused her of stealing medical samples from the laboratory. A council officer claimed a continental shipping network had once supplied the facility and that the restaurant's new recipes resembled stolen research. An demanded a written inventory rather than accept discrimination against cooks who lacked official laboratory titles. She closed the kitchen temporarily so the investigators could check it without putting food at risk.

早晨，冷藏室傳來不尋常噪音。林朔發現有人鬆開螺栓，使冷藏櫃的電源連接幾乎無法與回收發電機相容。他熟悉故障模式，知道這不像正常磨損。守衛前來質問唐安，指控她從實驗室偷走醫療樣本。議會官員說過去有跨大陸運輸網供應設施，餐廳新食譜像偷來的研究。唐安要求提出書面庫存，而不是接受對缺少官方實驗室頭銜的廚師的歧視。她暫時關閉廚房，讓調查員能安全檢查，不危及食物。

Rumor soon turned the missing sample into a story about a hostage and an attempted kidnap, although no person had been reported missing. An recognized the danger of dramatic language. A traveling missionary had once helped the clinic find clean blankets, and now even he refused to repeat the claim without evidence. In the hallway, a witness began to mumble that a masked visitor had entered the cooler before sunrise. Qiu followed the normal procedure for taking statements: ask the observer what they saw, record uncertainty, and avoid suggesting an answer. Shuo checked the security script against the power logs. It showed the cooler door opening for thirty seconds, but no image identified the visitor.

傳聞很快把失蹤樣本變成挾持人質與企圖綁架的故事，儘管根本沒有人通報失蹤。唐安知道誇張措辭的危險。一名曾協助診所尋找乾淨毛毯的旅行傳教士，也不願在沒證據時轉述。走廊裡，一位證人喃喃說清晨前有蒙面人進過冷藏室。邱楠依正常程序取證：問目擊者真正看見什麼，記錄不確定之處，不誘導答案。林朔把監視程式與電力紀錄互相核對，只能證明冷藏室門曾開啟三十秒，無法辨識訪客。

A sudden sensation of dread passed through Lele when an angry crowd gathered outside. One speaker called the restaurant a form of slavery because workers helped clean after meals. An invited him to inspect the voluntary duty list. The pantry's food stock was counted in front of independent neighbors. Nothing on it matched the missing laboratory samples. A guard accused Shuo of being a traitor for questioning the warrant, but the captain stopped him. 'The question is whether the evidence is viable,' the captain said, 'not whether the accused person is popular.' Mara asked each viewer of the public recording to distinguish an allegation from an established fact. The mood slowly changed from shouting to listening.

怒群在門外聚集時，樂樂感到一陣恐懼。有人說餐廳要求食客幫忙清理，形同奴役。唐安邀請他查看自願工作名單。倉庫庫存由獨立鄰居在場清點，沒有任何東西與失蹤實驗室樣本相符。一名守衛指控林朔質疑搜查令是叛徒行為，隊長立刻制止。「問題是證據是否站得住腳，」隊長說，「不是被控者受不受歡迎。」瑪拉請觀看公開紀錄的人分清指控與已確立事實。氣氛慢慢從叫喊轉成傾聽。

The review chair reminded everyone that presuming guilt from an unfamiliar accent had become an unfortunate norm in the ration office. An asked whether the office would publish a correction to the rumor about the supposed kidnapping. The chair agreed to write a public statement listing only what the investigation had verified. Mara read it before publication and pointed out a sentence that still suggested blame without evidence. They changed the wording. The correction would not heal every relationship overnight, but it would prevent an official document from preserving a rumor as fact.

審查主席提醒眾人，配給辦公室把陌生口音當犯罪線索，竟已成不幸常態。唐安要求辦公室公開更正所謂綁架傳聞。主席同意只列出調查確定的事實。瑪拉發表前先讀一遍，指出其中一句仍在缺證情況下暗示有罪。大家修改措辭。更正不能一夕修復關係，但至少不讓官方文件把流言當事實保存。

By evening the investigation established that the loose bolt had been handled with a tool not kept in the restaurant. It did not identify who had used the tool. The council withdrew the theft allegation for lack of evidence and promised a separate review of the cooler. An refused to accuse a particular company without more facts, even though she suspected someone wanted to interrupt the food supply. Her team reopened the kitchen only after discarding all ingredients exposed to unsafe temperatures. The loss hurt, but serving questionable food would have cost far more. As she washed the shelves, An noticed how easily fear had become a substitute for truth.

傍晚時，調查確定鬆動螺栓曾被一種餐廳沒有的工具碰過，但仍無法辨認使用者。議會因證據不足撤回偷竊指控，承諾另行審查冷藏櫃。唐安懷疑有人想中斷供餐，卻仍拒絕在缺乏更多事實前指控某家公司。團隊丟棄所有曾暴露於不安全溫度的食材，這才重新開門。損失令人心痛，但供應有疑慮的食物代價更大。唐安洗著架子，發現恐懼竟如此容易取代真相。

Before sleeping, An wrote two columns in the ledger: what was known and what remained uncertain. Lele asked why she did not simply write that someone had attacked them. 'Because I do not know who did,' she answered. 'If I fill the empty space with a guess, the next person might mistake my anger for evidence.' She added a third column for tasks: repair the cooler, protect the food, and ask for independent inspection. Lele copied the list into his notebook, then drew a small question mark beside the star board. It stayed there until the investigation could honestly close.

睡前，唐安在帳冊畫了兩欄：已知事項與仍不確定事項。樂樂問為什麼不乾脆寫有人攻擊他們。「因為我不知道是誰。」她回答，「如果把空白填成猜測，下一個人可能把我的怒氣當成證據。」她再加第三欄待辦：修冷藏櫃、保護食物、尋求獨立檢驗。樂樂把清單抄進自己的筆記，然後在星星板旁畫了小問號。那個問號會留下，直到調查能誠實結案。

An carried a notebook of memory episodes to the hearing. She explained that taste could sometimes bring back a person’s remembered place or name for less than two minutes. Some infected people experienced nothing at all. The council wanted a cure it could advertise; she refused to promise one, even when the refusal cost the restaurant a generous supply contract.

唐安帶著短暫記憶事件的筆記出席聽證。她解釋，味道有時能讓感染者想起一個地點或姓名，但通常不到兩分鐘。有些感染者完全沒有反應。議會想要可以宣傳的解藥，她拒絕作出承諾，即使因此失去一張優渥供貨合約。
