七天的許可

The Seven-Day Permit

山路盡頭，還有未還的書 · 第 5 / 25 章 · 5000–6000 · 英中對照 · 基礎校訂完成

At the first public hearing, a contractor offered a bright new vehicle in exchange for exclusive control over the region's reading data. Some residents were tempted. A librarian could tell the difference between a book that was missing and a book that had never been counted. The same principle applied to people. Yuqing asked who had been left outside the survey before arguing about percentages. She knew this habit made meetings longer; she also knew that a short meeting could leave behind a very long injury. The bus needed a new battery accessory, not an expensive redesign. The mechanic reported a breakdown in the rear braking system. Drivers could not bypass the mountain checkpoint for the demonstration. A donor brought champagne to celebrate before the committee had voted. The school sold roasted chestnut snacks to support a reading event.

第一次公聽會上，承包商提出提供全新亮麗車輛，條件是獨家掌控當地閱讀資料。有些居民頗為心動。 館員分得清一本遺失的書和一本從未被計算的書。相同原則也適用於人。予晴在討論百分比前，先問誰從調查中被排除。她知道這會讓會議變長，也知道短促的會議可能留下很長久的傷害。 圖書車需要新的電池配件，不是昂貴的全面改造。 技師回報後煞車系統故障。 司機不能為示範行程繞過山區檢查站。 捐助者在委員會表決前就帶來香檳準備慶祝。 學校販售烤栗子點心支持閱讀活動。

Yuqing presented the corrected paper totals together with gaps she still could not explain. She asked for a seven-day permit, not a permanent victory. The technician and the driver argued over a time estimate until Yuqing suggested walking the route together. Their disagreement softened as the real slope appeared beneath their feet. Neither had been dishonest; they had measured different parts of the journey. The revised record would say so plainly, preserving the reason for the correction instead of rewriting the first report as though it had never existed. Yuqing said it was not cowardly to refuse an unsafe shortcut. The old bus photographs could evoke affection without proving financial viability. A children's picture book showed a flock of domestic fowl. The bus driver sounded hoarse after answering questions all afternoon. A nurse offered hand lotion to volunteers whose skin had cracked in the cold.

予晴同時提出更正的紙本數字與仍無法解釋的缺口。她要求七天試行許可，而不是永久勝利。 技師和司機爭論行車時間，直到予晴建議一起走一遍路。實際坡度在腳下展開，分歧才緩和。兩人都沒說謊，只是測量了不同路段。修訂紀錄會清楚寫出原因，保留更正理由，不把初版當成從未存在。 予晴說拒絕危險捷徑並不懦弱。 舊圖書車照片能喚起感情，卻不能證明財務可行。 兒童圖畫書畫著一群家禽。 圖書車司機回答問題一下午後嗓音沙啞。 護理師提供護手乳給寒冷中皮膚龜裂的志工。

Chengyue declined to speak for the readers. His written statement concerned only road safety and disclosed his previous paid work for the contractor. Her mother sometimes teased her about the engineer, and sometimes asked why she looked so tired. Yuqing answered the second question more readily. It was easier to discuss the route than a feeling that had no map. Yet she was beginning to understand that keeping her choices open did not mean keeping everyone at a distance. It meant deciding for herself when a door could open. The debate required a public-service mentality rather than nostalgia alone. Every monetary contribution had to appear in the open accounts. The moody sky warned the driver to shorten the return route. The nucleus of the proposal was a measurable minimum service for all villages. Children began to rap a rhyme about returning their books on time.

承岳拒絕代表讀者發言。他的書面說明只涉及道路安全，並揭露自己過去曾替承包商收費工作。 母親有時拿工程師打趣，有時問她為何如此疲倦。予晴比較容易回答後者。討論路線比談沒有地圖的感情簡單。但她開始明白，保留選擇不代表與所有人保持距離，而是由自己決定何時開門。 這場辯論需要公共服務的思維，不是只有懷舊。 每一筆金錢捐助都必須出現在公開帳目。 陰晴不定的天空提醒司機縮短回程路線。 提案的核心是為所有村莊提供可衡量的最低服務。 孩子開始用饒舌節奏唱準時還書的韻文。

The committee granted a limited permit and ordered independent financial comparison. The bus moved again, slowly, with its risks still written on the dashboard. The last light faded from the bus's windows. Yuqing thought about how much of her life had once been governed by the need to be useful. She was still useful, but usefulness was no longer the whole of her worth. She could go home, take a day off, or accept another job without proving that everyone else would be safe only if she remained. The council voted to slash a decorative budget rather than staff wages. A sleepy visitor began to snore during the long presentation. Poor telecommunications could not be ignored in the service design. The chair agreed to unveil the contract terms before any final vote. Yuqing admired the driver's vigor after weeks of difficult meetings.

委員會核發有限許可，要求獨立財務比較。圖書車再次緩慢上路，風險清單仍貼在駕駛座前。 最後一抹光從車窗退去。予晴想到自己過去多麼受「必須有用」的念頭支配。她仍有價值，但用途不再是她價值的全部。她可以回家、休一天假或接受新工作，而不必證明只有她留下才能讓大家安全。 議會表決刪減裝飾預算，而非員工薪資。 一名疲倦訪客在冗長簡報中打起鼾。 服務設計不能忽略電信通訊不良的問題。 主席同意在最終表決前公開契約條款。 經歷數週艱難會議後，予晴仍佩服司機的活力。

After the vote, the contractor's representative approached Yuqing with an invitation to a private dinner. He said the city would look favorably on a leader who could negotiate quietly. Yuqing offered instead to meet at the district office with a clerk present and minutes available afterward. She was not trying to humiliate him. She understood that companies could provide useful services, but nobody should receive privileged access to the decision simply because they could afford a restaurant. At home, she studied the seven-day permit and circled its expiration date. A temporary victory would be wasted if the next inspection was forgotten.

表決後，承包商代表邀予晴私下吃飯，說市府會欣賞懂得安靜協商的領導人。予晴改提議在區公所、有書記員在場且事後公開紀錄的情況下會面。她不是要羞辱對方。她明白企業能提供有用服務，但任何人都不該因付得起餐廳就取得特權。回家後，她研究七天許可並圈出到期日。若忘記下一次檢查，暫時勝利就毫無意義。
