有兩個名字的橋

The Bridge with Two Names

山路盡頭，還有未還的書 · 第 2 / 25 章 · 5000–6000 · 英中對照 · 校訂完成

用互動版查詞、朗讀與收藏

A narrow bridge carried both school buses and delivery vans. The official map called it open, while drivers had given it another name: the bridge that shook after rain. The village square changed character at every hour. Before breakfast it belonged to delivery drivers; after school it filled with children and grandparents. Yuqing listened to both groups rather than treating the loudest voices as a vote. She took down the name of anyone who offered to check a fact, but she never confused a willing witness with a source that required no verification. Every driver must abide by the bridge's posted weight limit. Chengyue asked the inspector to affirm which bolts had been replaced. An alligator in a nature book delighted the younger passengers. The music teacher played a quiet bass line during the waiting period. A volunteer rewrote the warning in colloquial language for local drivers.

一座窄橋同時承載校車與貨車。官方地圖標示正常通行，司機卻私下稱它為雨後會搖晃的橋。 村莊廣場每個時段都有不同面貌。早餐前是配送司機的地盤，放學後則聚滿孩子和祖父母。予晴聽取兩方意見，不把最大聲的意見當成表決。她記下願意查證者的姓名，卻不把願意作證的人視為不需核實的來源。 每位駕駛都必須遵守橋梁公告的載重限制。 承岳請檢查員確認哪些螺栓已經更換。 自然圖鑑中的鱷魚讓年幼乘客興奮不已。 等候時，音樂老師演奏一段低音旋律。 志工把警示改成當地司機熟悉的口語。

Chengyue checked its load restrictions with an independent inspector. Yuqing refused to put children on a demonstration ride merely to save the library's timetable. The council chamber was built for speeches, but the most useful work took place around the small table in the hallway. Readers pointed at the printed route with hands that trembled from cold or age. A driver corrected the angle of a turn; a student corrected a stop's name. These were not interruptions to expertise. They were the information expertise needed. Minor delays were commonplace, but a shaking bridge required special action. The temporary road permit would expire before the next heavy rainfall. A burned fuse had disabled the inspection trailer's light. Villagers offered hospitality to the inspectors without asking for favors. The school cook's infectious laughter eased the worried meeting.

承岳找獨立技師核對橋梁載重限制。予晴拒絕為了保住圖書車班表，就讓孩子搭乘示範車冒險。 議事廳適合演說，最有用的工作卻在走廊小桌旁發生。讀者用因寒冷或年紀而顫抖的手指著印刷路線；司機更正轉彎角度，學生更正站名。這些不是對專業的干擾，而是專業需要的資訊。 小幅誤點很常見，搖晃的橋卻需要特殊處理。 臨時道路許可會在下一次大雨前到期。 一枚燒壞的保險絲使檢查拖車的燈失效。 村民款待檢查員，並不要求交換好處。 學校廚師富有感染力的笑聲緩和了焦慮的會議。

At the school she asked Chengyue why he had not defended her eight years earlier. He said fear of losing a contract explained his silence, but did not excuse it. An old hurt still occupied part of their conversations, but it no longer controlled every word. Chengyue offered a factual correction where one was needed and silence where one was not. Yuqing did not promise forgiveness as a reward. She began to imagine meeting him again after the project ended, when neither would have a reason to stand beside the other except wanting to. The crew carried one liter of water for a controlled demonstration. A novice volunteer was told not to approach the closed bridge. It would be rash to send a full bus across before the load test. The accountant tried to reconcile the map dates with the road invoices. The shabby warning sign had become unreadable after years of rain.

在學校裡，她問承岳八年前為何不幫她說話。他坦承害怕失去合約可以解釋沉默，卻不能為沉默開脫。 舊傷仍佔著對話一部分，卻不再控制每一句。需要更正的地方，承岳提出事實；不需開口時，他選擇沉默。予晴不把原諒當獎賞承諾。她開始想像計畫結束後仍與他相見，屆時兩人站在一起，只因為彼此願意。 工作隊帶了一公升水進行受控示範。 新手志工被告知不得靠近封閉橋梁。 載重測試前就讓滿載巴士過橋太草率。 會計試圖核對地圖日期與道路工程發票。 破舊警告標誌多年受雨淋後已無法辨認。

A safer temporary stop was marked below the bridge. It added walking distance, so the group began arranging assisted deliveries rather than claiming the problem was solved. No one cut a ribbon that afternoon. Instead, the driver checked the tires, a volunteer copied the minutes, and a child waited patiently for the library's next visit. Yuqing felt the quiet satisfaction of an institution doing ordinary things well. There would be another difficult decision soon, but the group had begun to share the right to make it. A handheld spotlight showed the damaged joint under the bridge. The school served pear syrup with warm water to cold volunteers. A loaded truck could make the surface tilt dangerously. At twilight the inspection team sealed the crossing with barriers. An illustrated memoir mentioned whiskey, but the crew remained sober on duty.

眾人在橋下標記較安全的臨時停靠點。雖然步行距離增加，他們開始安排協助送書，而不假裝問題已經解決。 那天下午沒有剪綵。司機檢查輪胎，志工抄寫紀錄，一名孩子耐心等候圖書車下次到訪。予晴感到公共機構做好平凡小事的安靜滿足。不久還會有困難決定，但大家已開始共享決策權。 手持聚光燈照出橋下受損的接頭。 學校用溫水調梨糖漿招待受寒志工。 滿載貨車可能使路面危險地傾斜。 黃昏時，檢查隊以路障封閉通道。 一本插畫回憶錄提到威士忌，工作人員值勤時仍保持清醒。

The bridge inspector drew two bars on the blackboard at the school: one for the legal weight limit and one for the actual weight of the fully loaded library bus. The bar for the bus extended past the legal limit. Several parents stared at them without speaking. Yuqing asked the inspector to explain the difference without technical abbreviations, and he did. Outside, a mother said that she could carry her child's books herself if the bus stopped below the hill. Yuqing wrote that offer in the volunteer plan, along with the woman's request for a covered place to wait. The compromise was inconvenient, but it did not ask anyone to gamble with a child's safety.

橋梁檢查員在學校黑板上畫了兩道長條：一條是法定載重上限，一條是滿載圖書車的實際重量。代表圖書車重量的長條超過了法定上限。好幾位家長盯著黑板，沒有出聲。予晴請檢查員不用技術縮寫再說明一次，他照做。教室外，一位母親說若車停在山下，她願意自己幫孩子拿書。予晴把她的提議寫入志工計畫，也記下她希望有遮雨等候區的要求。折衷方案不方便，卻不用任何人拿孩子的安全下注。
