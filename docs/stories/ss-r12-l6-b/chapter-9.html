The Locked Notebook

山路盡頭，還有未還的書 · 第 9 / 25 章 · 5000–6000 · 英中對照 · 校訂完成

用互動版查詞、朗讀與收藏

A local historian offered journals that documented how children once crossed the mountain on foot to reach school. Some names were still sensitive. Yuqing had spent enough years in public service to know how easily a chart could make a difficult decision look inevitable. She carried a pencil and a dated notebook, because a reader might ask tomorrow what had really happened today. The important distinction was not between paper and technology. It was between a service that listened and a service that counted people only when they were easy to count. A teenager borrowed a guide explaining common causes of acne. Yuqing asked the clerk to administer the consent process in person. The brochure listed which notebook pages visitors could read publicly. No private diary should remain in captivity inside an institution's archive. The history class refused to cram names without understanding their stories.

地方史研究者提供日記，記錄孩子過去如何徒步翻山上學。有些人名至今仍涉及隱私。 予晴在公共服務工作多年，知道一張圖表很容易把艱難決定偽裝成必然。她隨身帶著鉛筆和註明日期的筆記本，因為明天可能有人會問今天究竟發生什麼。真正的差別不在紙本或科技，而在服務是否願意傾聽，還是只計算那些方便被計算的人。 一名青少年借走介紹青春痘常見成因的指南。 予晴請書記員親自執行同意程序。 小冊子列出哪些筆記頁面可供公開閱讀。 私人日記不該被機構檔案室永久囚禁。 歷史課拒絕只死背姓名而不理解故事。

Yuqing negotiated permission to quote only selected passages and to return original notebooks unopened where consent was missing. The volunteers disagreed on the next step, which Yuqing considered healthier than an artificial show of unity. She asked one person to state the practical risk and another to explain the possible benefit. The record would include both. When the same question returned a week later, nobody would need to depend on memory or personal loyalty to understand the decision. The archivist would detach only the pages approved for copying. A reader described the discomfort of seeing family letters displayed without permission. The forthcoming exhibition would show only excerpts with valid consent. Soft lamps could illuminate the writing without damaging old ink. A jade ornament lay inside the donated travel chest.

予晴協商後僅引用獲授權的片段，缺乏同意的原始筆記則保持封存歸還。 志工對下一步有不同意見，予晴認為這比假裝團結更健康。她請一人說明實際風險，另一人提出可能利益，兩者都記錄。等同一問題一週後再出現，沒有人需要靠回憶或私人忠誠才能理解決定。 檔案員只會拆出獲准複印的頁面。 一名讀者形容家書未經允許展出帶來的不安。 即將舉辦的展覽只展示取得有效同意的節錄。 柔和的燈能照亮文字而不傷害舊墨。 捐贈的旅行箱內放著一件玉飾。

At dusk Chengyue asked whether they could have dinner without discussing the project. She agreed on the condition that either person could leave when they wished. Yuqing's career did not fit neatly into the stories neighbors told about love. She enjoyed her work in the city and cared about people in the mountain villages. Neither fact canceled the other. When Chengyue spoke about the future, she asked him to use the word 'I' rather than 'we' until they had separately decided what each person wanted. The reunion was joyous, but Yuqing still protected the owners' privacy. The evening course connected family stories with practical literacy. The narrator of one memoir had deliberately hidden her own name. A cold wind made Chengyue's nostril sting as he closed the gate. A map of the peninsula drew interest from children planning a school trip.

黃昏時承岳問能否一起吃一頓不談工作的晚餐。她答應，條件是任何一方都能自由離開。 予晴的職涯不符合鄰居熟悉的愛情故事。她喜歡城市工作，也關心山村居民；兩者不互相抵銷。承岳談未來時，她請他先說「我」而不是「我們」，直到各自決定想要什麼。 重聚令人喜悅，予晴仍保護持有人的隱私。 夜間課程把家族故事與實用識字能力結合。 一部回憶錄的敘述者刻意隱去自己的名字。 承岳關門時，寒風刺得他的鼻孔發疼。 半島地圖引起準備校外旅行的孩子興趣。

The notebooks became a voluntary local-history class, not a public spectacle of private lives. A potent rumor could damage trust faster than an official correction spread. The revival of local history did not require exposing private notebooks. The historian was selective about which passages were suitable for public reading. A stepchild asked whether family stories could include more than one household. The school aquarium held trout rescued from an abandoned study tank.

筆記成為自願參與的地方史課程，而不是展示私人生活的公開表演。 強烈的謠言可能比正式更正傳播得更快，破壞信任。 地方史的復興不必以公開私人筆記為代價。 歷史研究者慎選適合公開朗讀的段落。 一名繼子女問家族故事能否包含不只一個家庭。 學校水族箱養著從廢棄研究缸救出的鱒魚。

At dinner Chengyue told Yuqing about the summer he had spent drawing roads for villages he had never visited. His drawings had looked exact, and the company had praised his speed. Only later had he learned that one road ended at a staircase a wheelchair user could not climb. Yuqing listened, then told him about the first year she had accepted a circulation total without asking where its missing receipts had gone. Neither story excused the other. They paid separately, then walked to the bus stop together. The evening had room for embarrassment, kindness, and silence without forcing either person to call it a date.

晚餐時，承岳說起某個夏天，他替從未去過的村莊畫道路圖。圖面精準，公司讚賞他的速度；很久以後他才知道一條路終點是輪椅使用者爬不上去的階梯。予晴聽完，也談自己第一年如何接受借閱總數，沒問遺漏的收據去了哪裡。兩人的故事無法互相開脫。他們各付各的餐費，再一起走到公車站。那晚容得下尷尬、善意和沉默，誰也不必強迫把它稱作約會。
