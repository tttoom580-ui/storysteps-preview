失蹤的地圖集

The Missing Atlas

山路盡頭，還有未還的書 · 第 7 / 25 章 · 5000–6000 · 英中對照 · 校訂中

用互動版查詞、朗讀與收藏

A valuable atlas vanished from the bus, and an impatient official publicly blamed a student who had been last on board. Every village had its own explanation of why the road mattered. Some counted school days; others counted hospital visits or the hours needed to reach a shop. Yuqing avoided ranking those stories by how touching they sounded. Instead she asked what each household needed, which requests could be met now, and which ones required a public decision about limited resources. A bulky atlas would not fit inside the narrow return slot. Yuqing would not deem the student guilty without checking the receipt. A history club discussed how dictatorship can silence dissent. A paper airplane seemed to glide through the quiet reading room. Students displayed handicraft bookmarks at the repair fair.

一本珍貴地圖集在車上消失，急躁的官員公開指責最後上車的學生。 每座村莊都有道路重要的理由。有人算上學日，有人算就醫次數或去商店所需時間。予晴不按故事的感人程度排序，而是詢問每戶需求、哪些現在能做到、哪些需要公共資源分配的正式決定。 一本笨重的地圖集塞不進狹窄還書口。 予晴不會未查收據就認定學生有罪。 歷史社團討論獨裁統治如何壓制異議。 紙飛機彷彿滑過安靜的閱覽室。 學生在修書市集展示手工書籤。

Yuqing reviewed sign-out sheets and found the atlas had been transferred to a repair shop before the student's visit. She insisted on correcting the accusation in the same public place. The evidence was neither dramatic nor easy to ignore. It lived in the ordinary details: a date on a receipt, a route that ended five minutes too early, a witness whose name had been recorded incorrectly. Yuqing laid the papers where everybody could see them and invited correction. If a record was hers, she was prepared to have it challenged by the same rules. An interpreter helped a visiting family understand the lending policy. A memoir by a lesbian teacher belonged on the shelf like any other life story. The library's liberation from rumors began with a written correction. The lofty shelves made several books impossible for Lian to reach. Young readers learned to navigate the catalogue without handing over private histories.

予晴檢查借出單，發現地圖集早在學生到訪前就送修了。她要求在同一個公開場合更正指控。 證據既不戲劇化，也不能輕易忽略；它藏在普通細節裡：收據日期、提早五分鐘結束的路線、被寫錯名字的證人。予晴把文件放在人人可見處，邀請更正。即使紀錄出自她本人，也應接受相同規則的質疑。 口譯員協助來訪家庭理解借閱規定。 一名女同志教師的回憶錄和其他人生故事一樣應該上架。 圖書館擺脫謠言束縛的第一步是書面更正。 高聳書架讓連安碰不到好幾本書。 年輕讀者學會使用目錄而不交出私人借閱紀錄。

Chengyue apologized to the student for having repeated the rumor without checking. Yuqing told him that changing conduct mattered more than sounding regretful. They found themselves waiting for the same bus more often than either expected. Sometimes they talked about the work; sometimes about the books they had loved before they became too busy to read. Yuqing liked discovering the ordinary parts of him, but she kept her calendar in her own pocket. An invitation could be pleasant without becoming an obligation. No volunteer should overwork to compensate for a weak public budget. A pebble trapped under the wheel had caused the return cart to shake. The disciplinary decision remained pending until the evidence was reviewed. A book on plantation history prompted careful discussion of forced labor. Farmers asked when they could plow the field beside the repair lane.

承岳向學生道歉，承認自己未查證就轉述傳聞。予晴告訴他，改變行為比表現後悔更重要。 兩人等同一班車的次數比預想多。有時談工作，有時聊忙碌前喜愛的書。予晴喜歡逐漸認識他平凡的一面，卻仍把自己的行事曆握在手裡。邀請可以令人開心，不必變成義務。 不該讓志工過勞來彌補不足的公共預算。 輪子下卡住的小石子讓還書推車晃動。 處分決定在證據審查完畢前保持待決。 一本種植園歷史書引發對強迫勞動的審慎討論。 農民詢問何時能犁整維修車道旁的田地。

The atlas returned with a repaired spine. More importantly, the student returned the next day without having to prove he was grateful. That night Yuqing called her mother and asked how the day had gone. Meiling described her own plans before asking about the books, and Yuqing smiled. The distinction was small but precious: two adults could care for each other without managing each other's life. She turned off her phone after writing a brief note for the morning shift and slept without waiting for anyone's permission. A children's tale about royalty did not change the rules for public grants. The unfair accusation could spur students to stop borrowing altogether. Yuqing invited the student to stroll through the reopened collection. An old guide explained how mountain sunlight could tan exposed skin. The chairman called privacy a trifle; the students challenged him immediately.

地圖集修好書脊送回。更重要的是，學生隔天也回來了，不必先證明自己有多感激。 那晚予晴打電話問母親過得如何。美玲先講自己的計畫，再問借書的事，予晴笑了。這小小差別珍貴：兩個成年人能互相關心，卻不必管理彼此人生。她寫好早班簡訊後關機，不等任何人允許便睡了。 兒童書中的王族故事不會改變公共補助規則。 不公平的指控可能促使學生完全不再借書。 予晴邀那名學生走進重新開放的藏書區散步。 舊指南說明山區日照如何曬黑外露皮膚。 主席把隱私說成小事，學生立刻提出反駁。

The accused student did not smile when the apology was announced. He asked why the original allegation had been placed on the notice board while the correction appeared only in a meeting file. Yuqing acknowledged the problem and had the correction posted in the same place, with the same visibility. She also arranged for a staff member to explain what would change in the sign-out process. Chengyue stayed at the edge of the group and let the student speak. The incident left a mark on Yuqing: a repaired object could return to its shelf, but a damaged reputation required deliberate public repair.

被指控的學生聽到道歉時沒有笑。他問為什麼原先的指控貼在公告欄，更正卻只出現在會議資料夾。予晴承認問題，安排用同樣醒目的方式把更正貼在原處，也請員工說明借出程序將如何改變。承岳站在旁邊，讓學生自己說話。這件事留給予晴深刻印象：物品修好能放回架上，受損名譽卻需要刻意而公開的修復。
