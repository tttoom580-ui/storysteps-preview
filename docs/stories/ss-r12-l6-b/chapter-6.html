母親自己的行事曆

The Mother's Own Calendar

山路盡頭，還有未還的書 · 第 6 / 25 章 · 5000–6000 · 英中對照 · 校訂中

用互動版查詞、朗讀與收藏

Yuqing's mother Meiling lived uphill and had arranged her own medical visits for years. Neighbors kept telling Yuqing she should move home permanently to care for her. The vehicle had never been beautiful in the way advertisers understood beauty. Its paint was scratched, and some shelves carried repairs in three different colors. Yet the children recognized its engine from halfway down the lane. Yuqing wanted to preserve that ordinary intimacy without pretending that affection could repair a brake or pay a driver a fair wage. An airtight storage box protected the rare maps from damp weather. The team hoped to attain a fair schedule before winter arrived. A visiting congresswoman asked residents about the road rather than lecturing them. Meiling made vegetable curry for the exhausted library volunteers. Yuqing did not despise neighbors who disagreed about funding.

予晴的母親美玲住在山坡上，多年來自行安排醫療回診。鄰居不斷說予晴應該永久搬回來照顧母親。 這輛車從不是廣告認定的美麗。車漆有刮痕，有些書架補過三種顏色，但孩子遠在巷子另一端就聽得出它的引擎聲。予晴想保留這種平凡親近，卻不會假裝感情能修好煞車或支付合理工資。 密封儲物箱保護珍稀地圖不受潮氣侵害。 團隊希望在冬季前達成公平班表。 來訪的國會女議員向居民詢問道路情況，而非訓話。 美玲為疲憊的圖書館志工煮了蔬菜咖哩。 予晴不鄙視對經費有不同意見的鄰居。

Meiling brought a written schedule showing the help she wanted and the help she did not want. She insisted that family affection should not erase adult consent. The team stopped at the school after lunch to check every entry against the original page. A clean digital screen was comforting, but it could not explain how a number reached that screen. Yuqing marked unresolved items with a visible question instead of replacing them with guesses. Her younger colleagues complained that this made progress look slow. She answered that progress which could be checked was worth the extra time. An astronomy book explained the next lunar eclipse to curious children. The pantry marked which donated plants were edible. Meiling said she valued Yuqing's esteem but would make her own decisions. An evergreen tree beside the stop offered shelter from the wind. A child asked whether a fish fin worked like the flap on the book box.

美玲帶來一張行事曆，明列自己需要與不需要的協助。她堅持親情不能抹去成年人的同意權。 午餐後團隊到學校把每一筆資料與原頁核對。乾淨的數位畫面讓人安心，卻說不清數字如何進到畫面。予晴把未解問題明白標記問號，不以猜測填入。年輕同事抱怨進度看來緩慢，她回答可查證的進展值得花時間。 天文書向好奇孩子解釋下一次月食。 餐食櫃標示哪些捐贈植物可以食用。 美玲說她珍惜予晴的敬重，但仍要自己做決定。 停靠站旁的常綠樹提供避風處。 孩子問魚鰭是否像書箱的活門那樣運作。

Chengyue met Meiling at a community meal and asked about her plans instead of asking for permission to court her daughter. Yuqing laughed at the surprise. Chengyue had a habit of offering practical help before asking whether help was wanted. Yuqing pointed it out one afternoon, not unkindly. He listened, put his tools away, and asked what she needed. The change was small enough to be believable. She appreciated its scale: trust seldom returned in a single magnificent gesture, and she no longer wanted it to. A flake of snow settled on the driver's route sheet. The school sold handmade merchandise to fund a reading day. A rotating schedule could prevent monotony and volunteer burnout. Reading detective stories was Meiling's favorite pastime. The council removed redundancy from three overlapping reporting forms.

承岳在社區餐會遇見美玲，問的是她自己的計畫，而不是請求追求女兒的許可。予晴忍不住笑了。 承岳習慣先伸手幫忙，才問別人要不要幫。某個下午，予晴不帶責備地指出來。他聽了，把工具收好，詢問她需要什麼。變化小得可信，她也珍惜這個尺度：信任很少在一次壯舉中恢復，她也不再希望如此。 一片雪花落在司機的路線表上。 學校販售手作商品籌措閱讀日經費。 輪值班表能避免單調與志工倦怠。 閱讀偵探小說是美玲最喜歡的消遣。 議會刪除三份重複報表中的冗餘內容。

They created a rotating delivery schedule that protected volunteers from exhaustion. Yuqing kept her city job application active. The old route sheet was returned to its folder beside the new one. The team did not destroy it, because the differences mattered. Yuqing asked the clerk to keep a signed copy where any resident could request it. Then she stepped outside, carried no more boxes, and allowed the day to end without searching for one last task to justify her presence. Children were free to roam the schoolyard, not the closed bridge. Volunteers collected rubbish around the temporary book stop. The fantasy novel described a knight who would slay a dragon. Yuqing recognized a sly attempt to hide a data fee in the contract. Warm vapor rose from the bowls on the community dinner table.

眾人建立輪值送書班表，防止志工過勞。予晴沒有撤回外地的職缺申請。 舊路線表放回新表旁的資料夾，團隊沒有銷毀它，因為差異值得留下。予晴請書記員保存可供居民申請的簽署副本。之後她走出去，不再多搬箱子，也不再尋找最後一件工作來證明自己該在場。 孩子可以在校園遊走，不能走上封閉橋梁。 志工清理臨時停靠站周圍的垃圾。 奇幻小說描述一名將屠龍的騎士。 予晴看出有人狡猾地想把資料費藏進合約。 社區晚餐桌上的熱湯碗升起水蒸氣。

That evening Meiling served dinner before Yuqing had even taken off her coat. Yuqing started to protest, worried that her mother had exhausted herself. Meiling pointed toward a chair and said she had invited two friends, shared the cooking, and taken a nap that afternoon. She did not need permission to enjoy hosting. Yuqing apologized for assuming otherwise. The conversation stayed with her the next day as she designed the volunteer rota. A good system should ask people what help they wanted instead of assigning them a helpless role. It should also allow a willing person to decline without being called selfish.

當晚，予晴還沒脫外套，美玲就端出晚餐。予晴正想抗議，擔心母親累壞了。美玲指著椅子說她邀了兩位朋友一起煮，下午也睡過午覺；她不需要別人批准才能喜歡招待客人。予晴為自己的假設道歉。隔天設計志工輪值表時，這段對話一直留在心裡。好制度應該問人想要什麼幫助，而不是直接把人放進無助的角色，也要讓願意付出的人能拒絕，而不被說自私。
