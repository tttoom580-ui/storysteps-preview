河面上方的小徑

The Footpath Above the River

山路盡頭，還有未還的書 · 第 12 / 25 章 · 5000–6000 · 英中對照 · 校訂中

A fallen tree blocked the footpath used by residents beyond the river. A rumor urged volunteers to carry books across a damaged suspension bridge. In the mountain villages, help often arrived with complicated expectations. An attendant kept the reading shelter open while the path remained closed. The school cook mixed pancake batter for the delayed visitors. The latest census showed households omitted from the original service map. A retired chemist helped explain why the old bridge paint was peeling. Birds began to chirp beyond the temporary barriers.

倒木堵住河對岸居民常走的小徑。有人鼓吹志工背書走過受損吊橋。 山村的幫助常伴隨複雜期待。 小屋管理員在步道封閉期間維持閱覽站開放。 學校廚師為滯留訪客調製煎餅麵糊。 最新人口普查顯示部分家庭被原始服務地圖遺漏。 退休化學師協助解釋舊橋油漆剝落的原因。 臨時圍欄外，鳥兒開始啁啾。

Yuqing stopped the crossing and worked with the fire service to establish a collection point on the safe side. Some residents called her timid. Several said no. That answer was more useful than praise for a polished presentation. The volunteer desk could dispense returned books only after proper logging. The driver's tired groan made Yuqing call for a rest break. A children's encyclopedia described an iceberg drifting far from shore. The fire service gave invaluable advice on the safe collection point. A lavish charity dinner would not repair the damaged path.

予晴阻止通行，和消防單位在安全側設置領書站。有居民說她膽小。好幾人說不知道。這個回答比稱讚簡報漂亮更有用。 志工服務台只能在完成登記後發放歸還書籍。 駕駛疲憊的呻吟讓予晴要求休息。 兒童百科描述一座漂離海岸的冰山。 消防單位對安全領書點提供極有價值的建議。 豪華慈善晚宴修不好受損步道。

Chengyue said she had not been timid; she had chosen a difficult restraint. She told him praise did not relieve either of them from documenting the delay. But remembering was not the same as returning. She decided that any new closeness had to be made in the present, with space for a refusal that did not require an argument. The new public logo included an open book and a winding road. A loudspeaker announcement directed visitors away from the closed bridge. A martial arts handbook stayed on the ordinary lending shelf. The clinic monitored a minor flu outbreak in the neighboring village. A travel guide featured oyster farming along the distant coast.

承岳說那不是膽小，而是艱難的克制。予晴提醒他，讚美也不能免除兩人記錄延誤的責任。但記起過去不等於回頭。她決定任何新的親近都必須在當下建立，且拒絕不需要長篇辯解。 新的公共標誌包含一本打開的書與彎曲道路。 擴音器公告引導訪客遠離封閉橋梁。 一本武術手冊仍放在一般借閱書架。 診所監測鄰村的小型流感疫情。 旅遊指南介紹遠方海岸的牡蠣養殖。

The delayed books arrived two days later through a licensed transport route, without any heroic shortcut. The meeting ended with a list of unresolved questions rather than applause. Yuqing knew some members found that disappointing. She did not. A public service survived by being revisited, checked, and changed, not by finding one person willing to carry all the danger. She wrote the next appointment on her calendar before closing her bag. The council agreed to populate the route map with verified household counts. The fallen tree was a setback, not a reason to abandon the service. Some residents remained skeptical of the proposed licensed detour. Yuqing refused to subscribe to the idea that delay meant failure. The operator could not withhold public safety notices from readers.

書籍兩天後循合格運輸路線送達，沒有靠冒險捷徑。 會議以未解問題清單而非掌聲收尾。有些成員失望，予晴卻不然。公共服務靠反覆檢查與調整生存，不靠某個人願意承擔全部風險。她收包前先在行事曆寫下下次會議。 議會同意以查證過的家戶數填入路線圖。 倒木是挫折，不是放棄服務的理由。 部分居民仍懷疑提議中的合格繞道路線。 予晴拒絕接受延誤就等於失敗的想法。 營運者不能向讀者扣留公共安全通知。

The villagers received the delay notice in two forms: a radio announcement and a printed sheet left at the clinic. Yuqing had learned that a digital alert was useless to someone whose phone signal vanished whenever the clouds gathered. The next morning an elderly woman asked why the library had not crossed the suspension bridge. Yuqing showed her the inspection notice and the replacement delivery date. The woman was disappointed but accepted the explanation. She asked for a mystery novel rather than an apology. Yuqing promised the title, not the exact hour, until the road authority confirmed the route.

村民透過兩種方式收到延誤通知：廣播與放在診所的紙本公告。予晴知道雲層聚集就沒訊號的人，收不到數位警報。隔天一名老婦人問圖書車為何不走吊橋。予晴拿出檢查通知與替代送達日期。婦人失望，卻接受說明。她要的是一本推理小說，不是道歉。予晴答應書名，但道路單位確認路線前不承諾確切時刻。
