The Contract in Small Print

山路盡頭，還有未還的書 · 第 11 / 25 章 · 5000–6000 · 英中對照 · 校訂中

The contractor's proposed app promised convenience while claiming unlimited rights to borrowers' histories. The small print excluded people using paper cards. The first version of any public plan was always tempted to erase its own weaknesses. Yuqing asked the team to write those weaknesses in the same typeface as its successes. A late arrival could be corrected, an unsafe crossing could be closed, and an unhappy reader could appeal. None of these acts would make the library look perfect, but each could make it trustworthy. The library's modest acclaim did not justify surrendering reader privacy. Yuqing asked the lawyer to remove ambiguity from the data clause. The privacy rule protected bodily autonomy as well as reading history. A cold draught blew through the old reading trailer's cracked window. The school thermometer displayed seventy degrees Fahrenheit in the morning.

承包商的應用程式主打便利，卻要求無限期使用借書紀錄的權利，細則還排除了紙本借書證使用者。 公共計畫的第一版總容易掩飾弱點。予晴要求團隊用和成功事項一樣清楚的字體寫下缺點。遲到可以更正，危險通道可以封閉，不滿的讀者可以申訴。這些事不會讓圖書館看起來完美，卻能讓它值得信任。 圖書館獲得一些讚譽，卻不足以成為交出讀者隱私的理由。 予晴要求律師消除資料條款中的模糊之處。 隱私規則保護身體自主，也保護借閱歷史。 冷風從舊閱覽拖車裂開的窗戶灌進來。 學校溫度計早晨顯示華氏七十度。

Yuqing asked a public-interest lawyer to compare the contract with existing library obligations. The lawyer identified options for a time-limited service without data ownership. On the return journey, Yuqing kept a list of things the team could not yet promise. The list was frustratingly long. Still, it gave her a practical way to decide what to do at dawn: ask the next person, inspect the next stop, check the next invoice. She had grown suspicious of grand solutions that required nobody to change the work of tomorrow. A fireproof cabinet held consent forms during the repair. A geographical survey confirmed the distance between remote stops. The security expert explained how a hacker could exploit weak passwords. Even a layman could see that the contract granted indefinite data rights. The new policy would liberate readers from compulsory app registration.

予晴請公益律師比對合約與既有公共圖書館義務。律師提出不轉移資料所有權的限期服務方案。 回程時，予晴保留一張團隊尚不能承諾的事項表，長得令人挫折。它卻能讓她決定黎明該做什麼：詢問下一個人、檢查下一個停靠站、核對下一張發票。她越來越懷疑那些不需要改變明天工作方式的宏大解方。 維修期間，防火櫃收納同意書。 地理測量確認偏遠停靠站之間的距離。 資安專家說明駭客可能如何利用薄弱密碼。 即使外行人也看得出契約授予無限期資料使用權。 新政策將讓讀者擺脫強制應用程式登記。

Chengyue admitted he once recommended the same contractor without reading the data clause. His disclosure cost him a consultant role. Yuqing had learned not to confuse an apology with a contract for the future. Chengyue could acknowledge the past; she could listen without giving him the answer he might hope to hear. When work brought them together, they spoke as adults responsible for their own mistakes. Even a tender moment did not erase the need for honest boundaries or the possibility of choosing different roads. Children listened to a mermaid story while adults reviewed legal clauses. The miscellaneous charges included unexplained network maintenance fees. Each occurrence of an unusual login had to be recorded independently. A library outing could not require parents to disclose private medical data. The staff promised to persevere through the difficult budget review.

承岳承認曾未細讀資料條款就推薦過同一廠商。他主動揭露後失去一份顧問工作。 予晴學會不把道歉和未來契約混為一談。承岳可以承認過去，她可以聽而不必給他期待的答案。工作讓他們相遇時，兩人以對自己過錯負責的成年人身分對話。即使溫柔時刻，也不會消除誠實界線或選擇不同道路的可能。 成人審閱法律條款時，孩子聽著人魚故事。 雜項費用含有未經說明的網路維護費。 每次異常登入事件都必須獨立記錄。 圖書館戶外活動不能要求家長公開私人醫療資料。 員工承諾堅持完成艱難的預算審查。

Yuqing refused the contract but invited the company to compete under the same open terms as others. A single key was convenient; a logged key was safer. She wished more decisions could be so simple. Chengyue needed time to ponder his undisclosed past advice. The meeting began to revolve around ownership rather than convenience. Every shared kitchen utensil was returned after the volunteer meal. Yuqing refused to turn the contractor into a cartoon villain without evidence. The children ate yogurt while the lawyers compared contract versions.

予晴拒絕合約，仍邀請該公司在相同公開條件下參與競標。一把鑰匙很方便，有紀錄的鑰匙更安全。她希望更多決定也能如此簡單。 承岳需要時間思索自己過去未揭露的建議。 會議開始圍繞所有權，而非便利性打轉。 志工餐後，每件共用廚具都歸回原位。 沒有證據前，予晴拒絕把承包商塑造成漫畫式惡棍。 律師比對合約版本時，孩子吃著優格。

When the contractor asked how a private company was supposed to earn money, Yuqing did not pretend profit was shameful. She asked the representative to price the actual delivery work, maintenance, and technical support without claiming ownership of personal borrowing histories. The representative said the resulting bid might be less attractive to investors. Yuqing answered that public procurement was not obliged to make every investment attractive. Chengyue, seated among the witnesses, looked down at the clause he had once overlooked. After the hearing he submitted a correction to his earlier recommendation rather than asking Yuqing to make the problem disappear for him.

承包商問私人公司如何賺錢時，予晴沒有假裝獲利可恥。她請代表替配送、維修和技術支援合理報價，但不得要求擁有個人借閱歷史。代表說這樣的標案可能不夠吸引投資者。予晴回答公共採購沒有義務讓每筆投資都誘人。承岳坐在證人席，低頭看著自己曾忽略的條款。會後他提出修正過去推薦的文件，而不是請予晴替他把問題消失。
