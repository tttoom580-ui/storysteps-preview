沒有人計算的收據

The Receipts Nobody Counted

山路盡頭，還有未還的書 · 第 3 / 25 章 · 5000–6000 · 英中對照 · 校訂中

用互動版查詞、朗讀與收藏

At a village classroom, four generations of borrowers laid out their handwritten slips. The city database had registered thirty-nine visits; the slips documented more than a hundred deliveries. The first version of any public plan was always tempted to erase its own weaknesses. Yuqing asked the team to write those weaknesses in the same typeface as its successes. A late arrival could be corrected, an unsafe crossing could be closed, and an unhappy reader could appeal. None of these acts would make the library look perfect, but each could make it trustworthy. The school cashier recognized the number on a faded reading receipt. A nurse offered a checkup to an elderly reader who felt dizzy. The bus pulled beside the curb rather than blocking the classroom entrance. The factory siren could deafen anyone standing too near its horn. Yuqing insisted that a mistake was not a lifelong disgrace.

村莊教室裡，四代借書人攤開手寫收據。市府資料庫登記三十九次到訪，紙本收據卻記下超過一百次送書。 公共計畫的第一版總容易掩飾弱點。予晴要求團隊用和成功事項一樣清楚的字體寫下缺點。遲到可以更正，危險通道可以封閉，不滿的讀者可以申訴。這些事不會讓圖書館看起來完美，卻能讓它值得信任。 學校出納認出一張褪色借閱收據上的號碼。 護理師替感到暈眩的高齡讀者做健康檢查。 圖書車停在路緣旁，沒有擋住教室入口。 工廠警報器的聲音可能震聾站得太近的人。 予晴堅持，一次錯誤不該成為一生的恥辱。

Yuqing separated verified slips from memories and asked two volunteers to check dates independently. She did not treat a moving story as a substitute for a valid record. On the return journey, Yuqing kept a list of things the team could not yet promise. The list was frustratingly long. Still, it gave her a practical way to decide what to do at dawn: ask the next person, inspect the next stop, check the next invoice. She had grown suspicious of grand solutions that required nobody to change the work of tomorrow. The old receipts were sorted for secure disposal after digital verification. A resident's dissent was recorded beside the proposed route change. One family planned to emigrate, yet wanted library access until departure. Books could enrich a child's world without forcing the family online. The lengthy paper trail showed five separate delivery dates.

予晴把可驗證的收據和回憶分開，請兩位志工獨立核對日期。她不讓動人的故事取代可檢驗的紀錄。 回程時，予晴保留一張團隊尚不能承諾的事項表，長得令人挫折。它卻能讓她決定黎明該做什麼：詢問下一個人、檢查下一個停靠站、核對下一張發票。她越來越懷疑那些不需要改變明天工作方式的宏大解方。 舊收據完成數位核對後才進行安全銷毀分類。 一名居民對路線變更的異議被記在旁邊。 一家人打算移民，仍希望離開前能繼續借書。 書籍能豐富孩子的世界，不必強迫家人上網。 冗長的紙本紀錄顯示五個不同的送書日期。

A retired teacher thanked Chengyue for coming but addressed the actual questions to Yuqing. She appreciated that he did not answer on her behalf. Yuqing had learned not to confuse an apology with a contract for the future. Chengyue could acknowledge the past; she could listen without giving him the answer he might hope to hear. When work brought them together, they spoke as adults responsible for their own mistakes. Even a tender moment did not erase the need for honest boundaries or the possibility of choosing different roads. The retired teacher described reading as a lifelong habit. A child politely asked, 'Madam, may I keep this atlas until Friday?' A note of melancholy entered the room when the last school route was mentioned. It was permissible to photograph receipts only with the owners' consent. Yuqing did not preach about civic duty; she displayed the actual numbers.

退休教師感謝承岳到場，實際問題卻向予晴提出。她很高興他沒有替她回答。 予晴學會不把道歉和未來契約混為一談。承岳可以承認過去，她可以聽而不必給他期待的答案。工作讓他們相遇時，兩人以對自己過錯負責的成年人身分對話。即使溫柔時刻，也不會消除誠實界線或選擇不同道路的可能。 退休教師形容閱讀是終身的習慣。 孩子禮貌問道：「女士，我能把這本地圖集借到星期五嗎？」 提到最後一條校車路線時，屋裡浮現一絲憂傷。 只有取得持有人同意後，拍攝收據才是被允許的。 予晴沒有說教公民義務，而是展示實際數字。

The first corrected figure was accepted provisionally, enough to prevent immediate disposal of the vehicle but not enough to guarantee funding. Yuqing put the annotated map into its folder and locked the cabinet in front of a witness. A single key was convenient; a logged key was safer. She wished more decisions could be so simple. Beyond the window, the road climbed into darkness, and somewhere above it a family was waiting for a storybook that had been promised, not a miracle that could never be delivered. A faded timetable was reminiscent of the first bus that served the valley. Old rivalry between villages could not decide the new schedule. The corrected counts remained open to independent scrutiny. The room fell into suspense as the clerk checked the final duplicate. A publisher's trademark on the cover helped identify the book edition.

第一筆更正數字獲得暫時接受，足以阻止圖書車立即報廢，卻還不能保證經費。 予晴在見證人面前把註記地圖收進資料夾並鎖櫃。一把鑰匙很方便，有紀錄的鑰匙更安全。她希望更多決定也能如此簡單。窗外道路通往黑暗，山上某個家庭正等一本承諾送達的故事書，而不是不可能兌現的奇蹟。 褪色時刻表令人想起第一輛服務山谷的圖書車。 村莊之間舊日的競爭不能決定新班表。 更正後的數字仍接受獨立審查。 書記員核對最後一張副本時，全場屏息等待。 封面上的出版社商標協助辨認書籍版本。

The oldest receipt belonged to a woman who had recently died, and her grandson asked that it not be shown at the public hearing. He wanted the city to understand that she had borrowed books until her final winter, but he did not want her last illness displayed as an argument. Yuqing agreed. The team counted the transaction and removed identifying details. Later, in the quiet classroom, she explained to the volunteers that accuracy and dignity were not rivals. A number could be checked without turning someone's private life into an exhibit. The grandson helped identify three more receipts before leaving.

最舊的一張收據屬於一位最近過世的女士。她的孫子要求不要在公開聽證展示。他希望市府知道祖母直到最後一個冬天都仍借書，卻不願她最後的病情被當成論據。予晴答應了。團隊把借閱計入統計，移除個資。稍後，在安靜教室裡，她向志工解釋準確與尊嚴不是對手。數字可以查驗，而不必把一個人的私生活變成展品。孫子離開前又協助辨認三張收據。
