雨中的路線

The Rain Route

山路盡頭，還有未還的書 · 第 8 / 25 章 · 5000–6000 · 英中對照 · 校訂完成

A shower exposed a problem with the temporary stopping area: water gathered where an accessible ramp should meet the ground. When the meeting began, Chengyue kept his drawings face down until he was asked for them. He had once filled every silence with an expert opinion. Now he looked at the people who would live with his calculations. Yuqing noticed the effort but did not turn it into instant absolution. The work would reveal more about him than a speech. A school nurse explained why antibiotic medicine should never be shared. Residents began to applaud after the revised road map was displayed. An astronomer visiting the school read aloud about winter constellations. The new team chose to computerize counts without eliminating paper receipts. A faulty ramp could disable access even when every book was available.

一場陣雨揭露臨時停靠區的問題：無障礙坡道應接地的位置積水。 會議開始時，承岳先把圖紙面朝下放著，直到有人詢問才攤開。他過去總用專業意見填滿每段沉默，如今卻看向實際要承受計算結果的人。予晴看見他的努力，卻不因此立刻原諒。工作會比演說更能說明他是誰。 學校護理師解釋為何抗生素藥品不該與人共用。 修訂道路地圖展示後，居民開始鼓掌。 到校的天文學家朗讀冬季星座的故事。 新團隊選擇電腦化統計，但不取消紙本收據。 故障坡道即使書本齊全，也會使讀者無法進入。

Chengyue proposed a revised approach, but a resident engineer challenged his calculation. The team tested both plans and published the assumptions. It was tempting to announce that the numbers had finally proved everything. Yuqing resisted. A total did not tell her which road was safe or how long a reader could wait in the cold. The measurements had to be placed beside real journeys, and each journey required permission from the person who lived it. That was how the project began to deserve public money. The practice team would evacuate the stop if the river reached the warning line. Every expenditure appeared in the temporary grant ledger. An irritable official complained about the extra testing time. Yuqing said kin should not make decisions for a reader without consent. The school offered a meditation class while the repair crew worked.

承岳提出修正方案，居民工程師卻質疑他的計算。團隊測試兩案，並公開各自的假設。 宣稱數字已證明一切相當誘人，予晴卻抵抗這種衝動。總數不會告訴她哪條路安全，或讀者能在寒風中等多久。測量必須與真實行程並列，而每次行程的紀錄都需要當事人同意。計畫才能逐漸配得上公共經費。 若河水達警戒線，演練團隊就會撤離停靠站。 每筆支出都列在臨時補助帳冊。 一名暴躁官員抱怨額外測試耗時。 予晴說親屬不能未經同意替讀者做決定。 維修團隊工作時，學校開辦冥想課。

Yuqing told Chengyue she admired his willingness to be corrected. He answered that he was learning to stop confusing authority with certainty. The mountain was beautiful from a distance and tiring at the end of a workday. Chengyue offered to walk with Yuqing to the next stop. She asked him whether he was offering company or hoping to settle an old debt. He paused, then said company. She accepted, because the honest answer gave her room to choose the length of the walk. A nickel coin fell from the cracked box of donated objects. Chengyue helped volunteers orient the ramp toward the level path. An art book explained oriental textile patterns with historical context. A reader recovering from pneumonia requested home delivery. The manufacturer brought a prototype of the accessible return mechanism.

予晴告訴承岳，她欣賞他願意接受更正。他回答自己正在學習不再把權威與確定性混為一談。 山遠看很美，一天工作結束卻令人疲倦。承岳提出陪予晴走到下一站。她問他是在邀請同行，還是想償還舊債。他停頓後說只是同行。誠實回答給了她決定同行多遠的空間，她才接受。 一枚五分硬幣從破裂的捐贈物箱中掉出。 承岳協助志工把坡道朝向平坦步道。 藝術書以歷史脈絡介紹東方織物圖樣。 一名正從肺炎康復的讀者請求到府送書。 製造商帶來無障礙還書機構的原型。

The rain stop stayed closed pending repairs. Borrowers received a home-delivery option without any extra fee. By evening, the team had more work than before, but at least the work had a name and an owner. Yuqing sent a copy of the decisions to each village and left space for correction at the bottom. She had never believed that a good ending meant every question was answered. A good public decision could be revised when new evidence arrived, without pretending that responsibility had disappeared. A medical reference on psychiatry stayed available without exposing readers' names. The second ramp was more robust under an independent load test. No sovereign authority could ignore the published accessibility requirements. The student spokesperson read the group's questions without interruption. Lian helped choose a wardrobe shelf for costumes used in story hour.

雨天停靠點在修復前持續關閉，借書人可選擇免費到府送書。 到了傍晚，團隊的工作比先前更多，但至少每件都有名稱與負責人。予晴把決議寄往各村，頁底留出更正欄。她從不相信好結局代表所有問題都有答案。好的公共決定能在新證據出現時修訂，也不假裝責任隨之消失。 精神醫學參考書照常提供，且不公開讀者姓名。 第二座坡道在獨立載重測試中更堅固。 即使是最高主權機關也不能忽視已公布的無障礙要求。 學生發言人不受打斷地讀出小組問題。 連安協助選擇說故事活動服裝的衣櫃架。

During the second rain test, an older reader walked to the temporary stop with a cane. He arrived safely but stood beneath a narrow roof that left one shoulder wet. Yuqing asked him whether the stop worked. He answered that it was better than before, but that improvement did not make it comfortable. She thanked him for the distinction. Chengyue measured the roof overhang and proposed a small extension that did not narrow the turning space. They recorded the cost and requested approval instead of building first and asking forgiveness later. The next test would include rain, not just dry weather.

第二次雨天測試時，一名高齡讀者拄杖走到臨時站點。他雖安全到達，卻站在太窄的屋簷下，一邊肩膀被淋濕。予晴問站點好不好用。他回答比以前好，但改善不等於舒適。她感謝這個區分。承岳測量屋簷外伸距離，提出不縮小迴轉空間的小型加蓋方案。他們登記費用並申請核准，不先蓋好再求原諒。下一次測試會真正遇雨，而非只有晴天。
