終點站的停駛公告

The Notice at the Last Stop

山路盡頭，還有未還的書 · 第 1 / 25 章 · 5000–6000 · 英中對照 · 校訂中

用互動版查詞、朗讀與收藏

Yuqing reached the mountain square to find a closure notice pasted over the timetable of the traveling library. The notice counted online loans but not the paper books delivered to homes without a reliable internet connection. Yuqing had spent enough years in public service to know how easily a chart could make a difficult decision look inevitable. She carried a pencil and a dated notebook, because a reader might ask tomorrow what had really happened today. The important distinction was not between paper and technology. It was between a service that listened and a service that counted people only when they were easy to count. The metal clasp on the return crate snapped in Yuqing's hand. Readers asked the district office to convene an open meeting. A picture of a bear cub marked the children's shelf. The mobile dental clinic used the same village square. One angry caller called the mayor a dictator, but Yuqing demanded evidence.

予晴抵達山區廣場時，發現流動圖書車時刻表上貼了停駛公告。公告只計算線上借閱，沒把送往網路連線不穩的住家的紙本圖書列入。 予晴在公共服務工作多年，知道一張圖表很容易把艱難決定偽裝成必然。她隨身帶著鉛筆和註明日期的筆記本，因為明天可能有人會問今天究竟發生什麼。真正的差別不在紙本或科技，而在服務是否願意傾聽，還是只計算那些方便被計算的人。 還書箱的金屬扣環在予晴手中斷裂。 讀者要求區公所召開公開會議。 兒童書架上貼著幼熊的圖片。 巡迴牙科診所也使用同一座村莊廣場。 一名憤怒來電者罵市長是獨裁者，予晴則要求提出證據。

A nine-year-old returned a book with a handmade receipt. Yuqing recognized the number: the same loan appeared as zero in the city report. She photographed the receipt only after the child's grandmother agreed. The volunteers disagreed on the next step, which Yuqing considered healthier than an artificial show of unity. She asked one person to state the practical risk and another to explain the possible benefit. The record would include both. When the same question returned a week later, nobody would need to depend on memory or personal loyalty to understand the decision. The closure directive had no signed explanation for missing paper loans. A remote dwelling could receive books even without an internet connection. In a storybook, horses gallop toward the mountain at dawn. The misleading figures could heighten distrust between neighboring villages. The school janitor offered a dry room for sorting returned books.

一名九歲孩子拿自製借書收據來還書。予晴發現那筆借閱在市府報表竟記為零；徵得祖母同意後，她才拍下收據。 志工對下一步有不同意見，予晴認為這比假裝團結更健康。她請一人說明實際風險，另一人提出可能利益，兩者都記錄。等同一問題一週後再出現，沒有人需要靠回憶或私人忠誠才能理解決定。 停駛指令沒有附上紙本借閱遺漏的簽署說明。 偏遠住所即使沒有網路，也應能收到圖書。 故事書中，馬群在清晨奔向山頭。 誤導性的數字可能加深鄰近村落的不信任。 學校清潔員提供乾燥房間整理還書。

Chengyue, the road engineer who had remained silent when her old proposal was blamed for a failed route, offered to drive her over the ridge. She accepted a public inspection, not a private favor. Yuqing's career did not fit neatly into the stories neighbors told about love. She enjoyed her work in the city and cared about people in the mountain villages. Neither fact canceled the other. When Chengyue spoke about the future, she asked him to use the word 'I' rather than 'we' until they had separately decided what each person wanted. The old library motto said that every reader deserved a place. A pilgrim asked whether the bus still carried local-history guides. A previous flood agreement offered a precedent for temporary deliveries. The contractor hoped to reap profits from exclusive digital membership. The library bus remained stationary until the driver completed safety checks.

承岳是當年她的舊提案遭指責時保持沉默的道路工程師。他提出載她走山路，她只接受有紀錄的公開勘查，不接受私人恩惠。 予晴的職涯不符合鄰居熟悉的愛情故事。她喜歡城市工作，也關心山村居民；兩者不互相抵銷。承岳談未來時，她請他先說「我」而不是「我們」，直到各自決定想要什麼。 圖書館舊座右銘寫著每位讀者都值得有自己的位置。 一位朝聖者詢問圖書車是否仍備有地方史指南。 過去的洪水協議提供了臨時配送的先例。 承包商希望從獨家數位會員制度獲取利潤。 駕駛完成安全檢查前，圖書車保持靜止。

The librarians obtained seven days to preserve the paper logs. Yuqing posted a question for every reader: who was missing from the city's numbers? The village lights came on one by one as the volunteers packed the books away. Several people still disagreed, and Yuqing was glad the record showed it. She had seen too many projects fail after being declared a triumph. This time the next inspection had a date, the next review had a public address, and the people who would be affected knew where to speak. Yuqing said a download was not a synonym for a successful reading visit. The school planned to transplant two trees away from the turning area. A community trustee demanded the complete service contract before voting. The children needed unconditional access to their borrowed books during the review. Yuqing refused to utter an accusation before checking the paper register.

圖書員爭取到七天保存紙本紀錄。予晴留下一個問題給所有讀者：市府數字漏掉了誰？ 志工收書時，村燈一盞盞亮起。仍有人不同意，予晴慶幸紀錄寫了下來。她見過太多被宣布勝利後就失敗的計畫。這次下一次檢查有日期，審查有公開地址，受影響的人也知道去哪裡發言。 予晴說下載次數不是成功閱讀服務的同義詞。 學校計畫把兩棵樹移植到迴車區以外。 一位社區受託人要求表決前公開完整服務契約。 審查期間，孩子們仍需要不附帶條件的借書權利。 核對紙本登記冊之前，予晴拒絕說出指控。

Before the meeting ended, a young courier showed Yuqing the notebook he kept inside his jacket. Whenever a resident returned a book at a doorstep, he wrote the date, the title, and the time the road was passable. His handwriting was uneven, but the sequence of dates was clear. Yuqing asked whether he would allow the notebook to be checked by someone outside the library. He agreed after she promised that the names of children could be concealed. When Chengyue offered to carry the notebooks, she kept them with their owner. Evidence did not become public property simply because it was useful.

會議結束前，一位年輕配送員給予晴看他放在外套裡的筆記本。每當居民在家門口還書，他就寫下日期、書名與當時道路是否可通行。他的字跡不整齊，日期順序卻很清楚。予晴問他是否願意讓圖書館以外的人核對。他在她承諾隱去孩子姓名後才答應。承岳提議幫忙拿筆記本時，她仍把它留在持有人手中。證據即使有用，也不會因此自動成為公共財產。
