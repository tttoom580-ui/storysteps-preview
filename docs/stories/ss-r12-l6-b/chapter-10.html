What Counts as a Reader

山路盡頭，還有未還的書 · 第 10 / 25 章 · 5000–6000 · 英中對照 · 校訂中

用互動版查詞、朗讀與收藏

At the second hearing, the contractor displayed impressive digital circulation rates. Yuqing compared them with the records of people who had no reliable connection. The village square changed character at every hour. An animate character in the new picture book taught children to question numbers. The teacher used simple arithmetic to compare loans and visits. The students called the corrected graph awesome, then asked how it was made. The lunch volunteer sliced celery beside the meeting table. Wet cement delayed access to the temporary book shelter.

第二次聽證中，承包商展示驚人的數位流通率。予晴拿它們與無法穩定上網的居民借閱紀錄比較。 村莊廣場每個時段都有不同面貌。 新圖畫書中栩栩如生的角色教孩子質疑數字。 教師用簡單算術比較借閱量與到訪次數。 學生稱更正圖表很棒，接著追問它如何製作。 午餐志工在會議桌旁切西洋芹。 未乾的水泥使臨時圖書棚無法立即開放。

A statistician explained the difference between total uses and unique readers. The council ordered both figures to appear in future reports. The council chamber was built for speeches, but the most useful work took place around the small table in the hallway. Readers pointed at the printed route with hands that trembled from cold or age. A driver corrected the angle of a turn; a student corrected a stop's name. These were not interruptions to expertise. They were the information expertise needed. A regional confederation of village councils demanded equal access to the records. A proposed crackdown on unregistered readers worried the volunteers. A child pointed to a crocodile on the library's animal poster. The report used a dual measure of total loans and unique borrowers. The committee tried to equalize opportunities without pretending every road was identical.

統計師說明總使用次數和不重複讀者人數的差別。議會要求往後報告同時列出兩項數字。 議事廳適合演說，最有用的工作卻在走廊小桌旁發生。讀者用因寒冷或年紀而顫抖的手指著印刷路線；司機更正轉彎角度，學生更正站名。這些不是對專業的干擾，而是專業需要的資訊。 村議會聯盟要求平等取得紀錄。 有人提議嚴查未登記讀者，令志工擔心。 孩子指著圖書館動物海報上的鱷魚。 報告採用總借閱量與不重複借書人的雙重指標。 委員會試圖讓機會更平等，卻不假裝每條路狀況相同。

Chengyue invited Yuqing to a weekend walk, but she postponed it until her hearing notes were complete. He accepted the refusal without bargaining. Yuqing did not promise forgiveness as a reward. Neither faction was allowed to alter the signed meeting minutes. Hidden data fees could incur costs for families without bank accounts. Yuqing called it an injustice to count only readers with internet service. Political intrigue was no substitute for a clear statistical definition. A flashy app could lure officials away from the needs of paper-card readers.

承岳邀予晴週末散步，她說要先完成聽證筆記。他沒有討價還價，尊重她的拒絕。予晴不把原諒當獎賞承諾。 任何派系都不准修改已簽署的會議紀錄。 隱藏資料費可能讓沒有銀行帳戶的家庭負擔費用。 予晴說只計算有網路服務的讀者是一種不公。 政治陰謀不能取代清楚的統計定義。 華麗應用程式可能引誘官員忽略紙本借書證讀者。

The committee extended the limited pilot for a month and reserved money for an independent accessibility audit. No one cut a ribbon that afternoon. There would be another difficult decision soon, but the group had begun to share the right to make it. An obstinate councillor still demanded the raw spreadsheet. A pickpocket warning appeared on the bus stop information board. The next delivery could be punctual only if the dangerous bridge was avoided. The chair showed restraint by allowing critics to finish their comments. Yuqing felt her confidence stagger when the last discrepancy appeared.

委員會延長一個月有限試辦，並保留無障礙獨立稽核經費。 那天下午沒有剪綵。不久還會有困難決定，但大家已開始共享決策權。 一名固執議員仍堅持索取原始試算表。 扒手警示貼在公車站資訊板上。 只有避開危橋，下一次配送才有可能準時。 主席克制自己，讓批評者把話說完。 最後一處數據差異出現時，予晴的信心一度動搖。

The public statistician answered the sharpest question from an elderly farmer, who asked whether a person borrowing twenty books mattered more than twenty people borrowing one. She said that the two measures described different realities and that neither could replace the other. Yuqing watched as several councillors changed notes on their papers. It was a small shift, but it mattered because the decision had previously treated one number as the whole truth. On the journey home she found a message from her city employer asking whether she still wanted to be considered for promotion. She answered yes before the bus left the valley.

公共統計師回答一名高齡農夫最尖銳的問題：一人借二十本書，是否比二十人各借一本更重要？她解釋兩種指標描述不同現實，不能互相取代。予晴看見幾位議員修改筆記。這只是小改變，卻重要，因為過去決策把單一數字當作全部真相。返程途中，她收到城市雇主訊息，問是否仍考慮升遷。圖書車離開山谷前，她回覆願意。
