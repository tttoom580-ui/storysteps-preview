寒冷天的室內練習

A Cold Day Inside

Cold weather came with a long rain. Tess called the team before anyone left home. She said the field was too wet, so they would meet at the sports center instead. June was in a car with her father when the call came. They turned toward the center and told everyone the road was clear. Rose brought a blue bag of soft balls that would not hurt the walls. "Maybe we can cook something after practice," Ben said, "if the room has a safe kitchen."

長長的一場雨帶來寒冷天氣。泰絲在大家出門前先打電話，通知球場太濕，改到運動中心集合。瓊接到電話時正和父親坐在車裡，他們轉往中心，也告訴大家道路沒有問題。蘿絲帶來一袋藍色的軟球，不會撞傷牆面。「如果這裡有安全的廚房，」班說，「也許練完後可以煮點東西。」

The secretary at the front desk showed them a small room under the stairs. A fan moved slowly above a table, and a tree outside the window bent in the wind. The room was large enough for seven people to stand in a circle, but not for a full game. Tess asked the nurse at the center whether the room was safe for light movement. The nurse checked the floor and said yes, as long as everyone wore shoes and moved slowly.

櫃檯的秘書帶他們去樓梯下的小房間。桌上方的風扇慢慢轉動，窗外的樹在風中彎下。房間夠七個人站成一圈，卻不能進行完整比賽。泰絲問中心的護理師，這裡適不適合輕度活動。護理師檢查地板，說只要穿好鞋並慢慢移動，就可以。

A dictionary stood on a shelf beside a stack of old books. Leo took it down because he wanted to learn a new sentence for giving a clear call. "Pass to me," he read. June said a short sentence was better than a long order. They played a quiet game in which each player said a name and rolled a soft ball across the floor. The ball sometimes went under the table. No one was angry when a turn took longer than expected.

架上放著一本字典，旁邊疊著舊書。里歐拿下字典，想學一句清楚要求傳球的英文。「傳給我。」他念道。瓊說，短句比長長的命令更好。他們玩一個安靜的遊戲，每個人先喊名字，再把軟球滾過地板。球偶爾滾到桌下，沒有人因為一回合比預期久就生氣。

At break time, the center cook made warm soup. Ben took off his wet pants and put on dry ones from his bag. The cook told them about a temple in the town that had held sports days for many years. "A thousand people once came to watch a game there," she said, though she did not know the exact number. Tess smiled. Their group of seven was small, but the cook treated them as if their first game was important.

休息時，中心的廚師煮了熱湯。班脫下濕褲子，換上袋裡的乾褲。廚師說鎮上的一座廟過去舉辦過很多年的運動日。「以前可能有一千人來看過比賽。」她說，但也不確定精確人數。泰絲笑了。他們只有七個人，但廚師對待他們的第一場比賽，彷彿那是一件重要的事。

Tess asked the team to look at pictures from their first friendly game. Leo had taken them on his phone. One picture showed Tess running after a player while another teammate stood alone. Another showed June making the pass that led to Ava's goal. "The past can teach us," Tess said, "but we do not have to stay in it." They wrote one new idea each, then put the pages in the same blue bag as the soft balls.

泰絲請大家看第一場友誼賽的照片，那些是里歐用手機拍的。其中一張顯示泰絲追著對手跑，另一名隊友卻獨自站在旁邊。另一張拍到瓊送出讓艾娃進球的傳球。「過去可以教我們，」泰絲說，「但我們不需要一直困在裡面。」每個人寫下一個新想法，把紙頁和軟球放進同一個藍袋子。

While waiting for the rain to slow, Tess asked the team one question: what could they carry from this small room into a large field? Rose said she had learned to watch feet before moving. Ava said she liked the way Ben waited for her name. Kai said the soft ball made it easier for him to try without fear. June said quiet words sometimes worked better than loud ones. Tess was surprised to hear how different the answers were. She had thought everyone had learned the same thing. Now she saw that a shared practice did not always give every person the same lesson. That was all right. They were not seven copies of one player. They were seven people, and each could notice something useful for the others.

等雨小一點時，泰絲問全隊：這間小房間學到的事，有哪些能帶到大球場？蘿絲說她學會移動前先看腳步；艾娃喜歡班先喊名字再等她回應的方式；凱說軟球讓他比較敢嘗試；瓊則說安靜的話有時比大聲喊叫有效。泰絲很驚訝，因為每個人的答案都不同。她原以為大家學到的是同一件事，現在才看見，同一場練習不一定給每個人同樣的收穫。沒有關係。他們不是七個複製出來的球員，而是七個人，每個人都能注意到別人用得上的東西。

Before going home, Tess looked at the weather report. It was probably going to rain again, so the next meeting might also be inside. She told everyone that a change of place was not a failure. There were many things they could learn without a large field. Rose stood by the door and said she liked the calm room. The others agreed. For the first time, bad weather had helped them notice the value of slow work.

回家前，泰絲查看天氣報告。接下來大概還會下雨，下一次也可能得在室內練習。她告訴大家，更換場地不代表失敗。沒有大片球場，他們還是有很多能學的事。蘿絲站在門旁，說她喜歡這間平靜的房間。其他人也同意。第一次，壞天氣反而讓大家看見慢慢練習的價值。
