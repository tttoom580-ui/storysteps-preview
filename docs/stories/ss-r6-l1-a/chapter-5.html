第一次測試

The First Race

第七個位置 · 第 5 / 25 章 · 1000以下 · 英中對照

用互動版查詞、朗讀與收藏

Tess set a race for the team, but it was not a race to win. Everyone had to run from one point to another, stop, and put a ball beside a bean bag. They could go as slowly as they needed. "Although this looks easy," Tess said, "we must be safe." Rose wanted to try first. She ran a long way and came back in less than one minute. Her neck was red, and she sat down for water. Tess told her she did not have to beat her time.

泰絲安排了一場測試，但這不是為了分出誰贏。每個人要從一個點跑到另一個點，停下來，再把球放到豆袋旁。大家可以照自己的速度完成。「雖然看起來很容易，」泰絲說，「安全仍然最重要。」蘿絲想先試，她跑了很長一段，還不到一分鐘就回來，脖子都紅了，坐下來喝水。泰絲告訴她，不必再打破自己的紀錄。

A frog jumped out of the grass near the goal. Ben stopped so he would not step on it. He pointed, and the other players waited. A hen from a nearby yard walked across the path a minute later. "Animals own this place too," June said with a laugh. Ava asked what the height of the goal should be. Tess used a rope, not a hard bar, and made the mark low enough that no one could hit a head on it.

一隻青蛙從球門旁的草叢跳出來。班停下腳步，免得踩到牠。他指給大家看，其他球員也跟著等待。一分鐘後，附近院子的一隻母雞又走過小路。「這裡也是動物的家，」瓊笑著說。艾娃問球門應該多高。泰絲用的是繩子而不是硬桿，並把標記放得夠低，免得任何人撞到頭。

Ms. Park came by with her aunt and watched a round. The older woman asked why Tess did not count who was first. Tess explained that a fast race would not tell them who could see a friend in need. They tried a new deal: each player had to point toward the next person before moving. If a friend was not ready, everyone waited. The change took more time, but the team began to smile instead of looking at the clock.

朴女士和阿姨經過，看了一輪測試。年長女士問泰絲，為什麼不記錄誰是第一名。泰絲解釋，跑得最快並不能說明誰看得見需要幫忙的朋友。他們試了一個新約定：每個人在移動前，要先指向下一位隊友。如果朋友還沒準備好，大家就等一下。這樣比較花時間，但球員開始微笑，不再只是盯著時鐘。

At the far end of the field, Kai found a small hole. He wanted to dig it a little deeper to show where the ground was weak, but Tess stopped him. "Don't make it worse," she said. They put a bright mark by the hole and told the field keeper. Kai said he was sorry for wanting to touch it before asking. Tess thanked him for noticing the problem early. Keeping people safe was a kind of team work too.

在球場遠端，凱發現一個小洞。他想再挖深一點，好讓大家看出地面有多脆弱，但泰絲阻止他。「別讓它更糟，」她說。他們在洞邊放上醒目的記號，並告訴場地管理員。凱為自己還沒詢問就想動手而道歉。泰絲感謝他及早發現問題。維護安全同樣是團隊合作的一部分。

During the break, Leo told a story about a trip to the mountains. It had been cold enough for snow, and he had learned to take one step at a time. "I was never the fastest," he said, "but I got home." Rose looked at him, then looked at her own tired feet. She was beginning to see that speed was only one part of a game. She offered to walk the final round beside June and keep the same slow pace.

休息時，里歐說起一次山上旅行。那裡冷到會下雪，他學會一次只走一步。「我從來不是最快的，」他說，「但我平安回家了。」蘿絲看看他，又看看自己疲倦的雙腳，開始明白速度只是比賽的一部分。她主動提出，最後一輪要陪瓊一起走，保持相同的慢步調。

Tess also asked each player to name at least one thing that was difficult. Ben said he found it hard to stop after running. Ava said she wanted to look up more often. June said she needed time to speak before someone kicked the ball to her. Rose said she still wanted to move too fast. Tess wrote all four answers and asked the others to add their own. The list was not a test they could pass or fail. It was a way to decide what to practice next. No one laughed at the things a friend found hard. The older woman who had watched the race said she liked that. In her view, a group grew better when people could tell the truth without feeling ashamed. Tess hoped they could keep that kind of trust on a game day.

泰絲也請每個人至少說出一件困難的事。班說跑完後很難立刻停下；艾娃說自己想更常抬頭；瓊說她需要有時間開口，再讓別人把球傳來；蘿絲說自己仍常太急。泰絲記下四個答案，也請其他人補充。這不是會通過或不及格的考試，而是幫助大家決定下一步練什麼。沒有人嘲笑朋友覺得困難的地方。看過測試的年長女士說，她很喜歡這樣。在她看來，人能不帶羞愧地說實話，團體才會變得更好。泰絲希望到了比賽日，他們也能保有這樣的信任。

They finished with no winner written on the page. Instead, Tess wrote what each person did well: Ben saw the frog, Kai saw the hole, and Rose slowed down for June. The list was not as exciting as a medal, but it showed work they could use in a real match. Before leaving, everyone promised to rest for a full day. A team could learn to run, Tess thought, but it also had to learn when to stop.

最後，紙上沒有寫下任何勝利者。泰絲記下的是每個人做得好的地方：班注意到青蛙，凱發現洞，蘿絲願意陪瓊放慢速度。這張清單或許不如獎牌令人興奮，卻記錄了真正比賽用得到的能力。離開前，大家約好休息一整天。泰絲想，球隊可以學著跑，但也必須學會何時停下來。
