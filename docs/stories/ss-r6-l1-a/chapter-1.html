空球場

The Empty Field

第七個位置 · 第 1 / 25 章 · 1000以下 · 英中對照

用互動版查詞、朗讀與收藏

At seven a.m., Tess heard a bell from the town hall. She was early, but the field was empty. She had a heavy bag with two balls, a notebook, and a cover for the wet grass. The morning sun was bright. Tess was twenty-three years old. She wanted to make a team for a seven-player soccer game. A notice said the town would hold a game in one month, but a team must have seven people. She had only one name: her own.

上午七點，泰絲聽見市民會堂傳來鈴聲。她提早到了，球場卻空無一人。她帶著一個沉重的袋子，裡面有兩顆球、一本筆記本，以及一塊可以蓋住濕草的布。早晨的太陽很明亮。二十三歲的泰絲想成立一支七人制足球隊。公告說，小鎮一個月後要舉辦比賽，但每隊必須有七個人。她的名單上只有一個名字：她自己。

A man named Ben came along the path after breakfast. He was not a great player, and he said so at once. "Soccer is my hobby," he told Tess. "I like to exercise, but I am not sure I can play in a real game." Tess said she would teach him the rules she knew. Ben laughed because her first lesson was to stand still and look around. She asked him to see where another player might be before he tried to kick the ball.

一個名叫班的男子吃過早餐，沿著小路走來。他不是出色的球員，自己也立刻承認了。「足球是我的興趣，」他告訴泰絲，「我喜歡運動，可是不確定能不能參加真正的比賽。」泰絲說會教他自己知道的規則。班笑了，因為第一堂課竟然是站著不動、看看四周。她請他在踢球前先觀察另一名球員可能站在哪裡。

At the edge of the field, Tess put her bag on an old couch left by the sports room door. A monkey picture was on its back, and a little wooden tooth hung from the picture. The town used the couch for a children's play last spring. Tess did not care about the funny furniture. She pulled out a pen to write Ben's name under hers. Two names were not seven, yet a blank page now had a beginning.

球場邊，泰絲把袋子放在體育室門口的一張舊沙發上。沙發背面畫著一隻猴子，畫旁還掛了一顆小木牙齒。去年春天，小鎮曾把這張沙發拿來當兒童戲劇的道具。泰絲並不在意這件有趣的家具。她拿出筆，把班的名字寫在自己的名字下面。兩個名字還不是七個，但空白的紙終於有了開頭。

At noon a woman from the town hall came out with fruit and a small cake. Her name was June. She had studied in a foreign country, where she had watched people play soccer in a park after work. She liked the idea of a team that did not ask for money. She gave them a salad and asked one clear question: "Is there room for someone who is slow?" Tess said, "Yes. We need people who can listen, not only people who can run."

中午，一名市民會堂的女子拿著水果和一小塊蛋糕走出來。她叫瓊，曾在國外讀書，看過人們下班後在公園踢球。她喜歡不需要花錢就能參加的球隊。她給兩人一份沙拉，並問了一個清楚的問題：「跑得慢的人也能加入嗎？」泰絲回答：「可以。我們需要會傾聽的人，不只是跑得快的人。」

June had one worry. "What shall we do if the other teams are much better?" she asked. Tess took the two balls from the bag and placed them in the grass. "We shall learn," she said. "A team is not ready on its first day." Ben pointed to the empty part of the field. He offered to make a small mark for each new person who came. June did not promise to play yet, but she asked when they would meet again.

瓊有個擔心。「如果其他隊伍比我們強很多，該怎麼辦？」她問。泰絲從袋子拿出兩顆球，放到草地上。「我們就學，」她說，「一支球隊不會在第一天就準備好。」班指著球場空著的地方，提議每來一個新隊員，就做一個小記號。瓊還沒有答應參加，卻問他們下次什麼時候見面。

Before June went inside, she asked Tess what the team would do if more people wanted to join. Tess had not thought about that. She sat beside Ben and opened her notebook to a clean page. They could add extra names later, but people needed to know what the team was like before making a choice. Ben suggested a short meeting with no ball, only questions. Tess liked that. She also wanted to ask the town hall about a safe place to practice. June offered to speak to someone she knew there. They talked about the day, the time, and what to say if a person was unsure. Nobody said they were going to win. Instead, they decided to tell the truth: the team was new, it would need work, and everyone would have a voice.

瓊進屋前，問泰絲如果還有更多人想加入，該怎麼辦。泰絲還沒有想過。她坐到班旁邊，翻開筆記本的空白頁。之後可以增加候補名單，但人們應先知道這是一支怎樣的球隊，再決定要不要加入。班建議辦一場沒有球的簡短聚會，只回答問題。泰絲喜歡這個主意，也想向市民會堂詢問安全的練習地點。瓊願意找她認識的人幫忙詢問。他們討論日期、時間，以及有人拿不定主意時該怎麼說。沒有人宣稱會贏球。他們決定說實話：球隊才剛成立，會需要努力，每個人都能發言。

Before leaving, Tess set one simple rule. No one had to be the best player, but everyone had to speak when something felt unsafe. She covered the balls, took the bag off the couch, and looked at the names once more. Ben wrote the next meeting time at the top of the page. June stood in the door and smiled. Tess had come expecting a full team and found only one person. She went home with a smaller hope, but it was a real one.

離開前，泰絲訂下一個簡單的規則：沒有人一定要是最好的球員，但只要覺得不安全，任何人都必須說出來。她把球蓋好，從沙發上拿起袋子，再看一次名單。班把下次集合時間寫在紙張最上方。瓊站在門口微笑。泰絲原以為可以找到整支隊伍，結果只遇到一個人。她帶著較小的希望回家，但那份希望是真實的。
