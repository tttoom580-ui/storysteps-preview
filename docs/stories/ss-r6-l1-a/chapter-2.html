七個名字

Seven Names

第七個位置 · 第 2 / 25 章 · 1000以下 · 英中對照

用互動版查詞、朗讀與收藏

On a sunny afternoon Tess went to the south side of town with Ben. They wanted to find more people for the soccer team. Ben brought his cousin Ava, who could run fast but had never played with a group. Ava had come with her family from a city near a river. She liked to watch science videos and ask why a ball moved in a new direction. Tess told her that asking questions could be helpful on a field.

一個晴朗的下午，泰絲和班來到小鎮南邊。他們想為足球隊找更多人。班帶來表妹艾娃，她跑得很快，卻沒有和團隊踢過球。艾娃和家人從一座河邊城市搬來。她喜歡看科學影片，也愛問球為什麼會朝另一個方向移動。泰絲告訴她，在球場上提出問題也可能很有幫助。

They met Kai by the river path. He had a small box of tools for his bicycle. He said he was only a regular guy and had no special power in his feet. Ava asked whether he could add his name to the list before he made a choice. Kai shook his head. "Show me the game first," he said. Tess did not press him. They would need seven players, but she would not fill the page with names from people who did not want to join.

他們在河邊小路遇到凱。凱帶著一小盒修腳踏車的工具。他說自己只是普通人，腳上沒有什麼特別的力量。艾娃問，能不能先把他的名字加到名單上，再讓他決定。凱搖搖頭。「先讓我看看怎麼踢吧。」他說。泰絲沒有催促他。他們需要七個球員，但她不願把不想加入的人的名字填進名單。

A famous singer was giving a free talk at the park. People were already waiting in a line, and a man held a sheep made from white paper above his head. It was part of a children's show. Ben's lip moved as he counted the people waiting near the path. "This is not the best place to ask," he said. "They came for music." Tess agreed. She wrote a short notice and put it on a board where anyone could read it later.

一位知名歌手正在公園舉辦免費談話活動。人們已經排起隊，一名男子把紙做的白羊舉在頭頂，那是兒童表演的道具。班一邊動著嘴唇，一邊數小路旁等候的人。「這裡不是招募的好地方，」他說，「他們是來聽音樂的。」泰絲同意，便寫了一張簡短公告，貼在任何人都能稍後閱讀的公告板上。

In the evening, June arrived with Rose and Leo. Rose liked to play in goal. Leo liked to pass the ball and let others make the final kick. They were friends from the same building, but they did not always agree. Rose wanted to play a serious game. Leo wanted a team where no one would shout at him for a mistake. Tess said that they could try both: work hard and still be kind. They chose to make a short video to show their idea.

傍晚，瓊帶著蘿絲和里歐來了。蘿絲喜歡當守門員，里歐喜歡傳球，讓別人完成最後一腳。他們住在同一棟樓，是朋友，卻不總是意見相同。蘿絲想認真比賽，里歐則希望沒有人會因為一次失誤對他大叫。泰絲說，兩件事可以同時做到：努力，也保持友善。他們決定拍一段短影片介紹球隊。

Ava used her phone to record the video. In it, Tess said that the team would share time on the field. Ben held the list, and the others stood behind him. They were not seven yet. Kai had watched from the path and then asked for the page. He wrote his name in the last open space. "I want to try," he said, "but I may change my mind if this does not feel right." Tess said that was fair. They now had seven real names.

艾娃用手機錄下影片。片中，泰絲說球隊會公平分配上場時間。班拿著名單，其他人站在他身後。他們原本還差一人。凱在小路旁看完後，走來要過名單，把名字寫進最後一格。「我想試試看，」他說，「但如果感覺不適合，我可能會改變主意。」泰絲說這很公平。現在他們真的有七個人的名字了。

Before the group went home, Kai opened his bicycle box and took out one small tool. He showed Ben how to fix a loose part on a bag. Ava asked if he could bring that tool to practice in case a piece of the goal needed work. Kai said it was better to tell the field manager about broken equipment. He was glad to help with his own things, but he did not want to change public sports gear without permission. Tess agreed. Then they stood together and read the seven names aloud. June asked each person whether their name was written correctly. Rose found one small error, and Tess fixed it. The page was now accurate. That mattered because the team would give the list to the town office, and no player should have to ask later why their name was wrong.

大家回家前，凱打開修車盒，拿出一件小工具，示範如何修好袋子上鬆動的零件。艾娃問他能不能把工具帶去練習，以防球門有地方需要維修。凱說，公共器材壞掉時，最好先告訴場地管理員。他樂意修自己的東西，卻不想在未獲許可下改動公用體育設備。泰絲同意。接著大家一起念出七個名字。瓊請每個人確認拼寫是否正確。蘿絲發現一個小錯誤，泰絲便修正。名單現在正確了，因為它要交給小鎮辦公室，誰也不該之後才發現自己的名字寫錯了。

Tess walked home beside Ava. Ava asked why Tess had not begged Kai to join sooner. "A name is not a promise we can take from someone," Tess answered. "He had to choose." Ava thought about that for a while. She had expected a team to be about a strong kick or a fast run. Now she saw that it could also be about asking, waiting, and listening. The list would stay full only if people wanted to return.

泰絲和艾娃一起走回家。艾娃問她為什麼沒有早點拜託凱加入。「我們不能從別人身上拿走一個承諾，」泰絲回答，「他得自己選。」艾娃想了一會兒。她原本以為球隊只關乎有力的射門或快速奔跑，現在看見也與提問、等待和傾聽有關。名單要一直滿著，得靠大家願意回來。
