自己的腳步

Own Steps

When spring wind came, the seven players were able to use the field again. Tess arrived on her bicycle and found a small bow tied to the gate. It was a welcome from the field manager. She picked a safe place for their bags, then called everyone together. "Today each of you will choose your own next step," she said. "I can give you an idea, but the choice is yours." Ben said he wished he could learn to turn without looking down at his feet.

春風吹來時，七名球員終於又能使用球場。泰絲騎腳踏車抵達，發現門上綁著一個小蝴蝶結，那是管理員的歡迎心意。她選了個安全地方放袋子，再集合大家。「今天每個人都要決定自己的下一步，」她說，「我能提供想法，但選擇由你們自己做。」班說他希望能不低頭看腳，就完成轉身。

Ava wanted to use her own speed in a better way. She said she was happy to run, but she did not want to leave her friends behind. June showed her how to stop for a second and look across the open space. She said the ball belonged to the team, not to any one person. Leo made a small sign on a card for the next pass. Tess watched the lesson and felt proud that June was teaching without being asked.

艾娃希望更好地運用自己的速度。她說自己喜歡跑，但不想把朋友留在後面。瓊示範停上一秒，再看看開放的空間。她說球屬於整支隊伍，不屬於某一個人。里歐在卡片上畫了一個代表下一次傳球的小記號。泰絲看著這堂課，很高興瓊不用人要求，就主動教其他人。

A strong wind moved the trees around the field. Ben found that his body turned more easily when he kept his head up. He tried again and did not fall. Rose picked a new place near the goal where she could see the whole field. Kai practiced saying "I am here" so that friends would know where to pass. It was a simple language lesson as well as a sports lesson. Everyone could hear him clearly even from the far side.

強風吹動球場周圍的樹。班發現抬起頭時，身體轉向比較容易。他再試一次，沒有跌倒。蘿絲在球門附近選了新位置，那裡能看見整個球場。凱練習說「我在這裡」，讓朋友知道該往哪裡傳。這既是體育課，也是簡單的語言課。即使站在遠處，大家也能清楚聽見他。

At the break, Tess gave each player some water and a little salt on their food. She reminded them that a body needs rest as well as work. A boy nearby showed them a drawing of the planet Earth with seven little people standing together. He said his teacher had asked the class to draw a wish for the world. Tess told him she liked his picture because the little people looked different but stood close enough to help one another.

休息時，泰絲讓每個人喝水，也在食物上放了一點鹽。她提醒大家，身體需要休息，也需要活動。附近一名男孩拿來一張畫，畫著地球以及七個站在一起的小人。他說老師請全班畫出對世界的心願。泰絲告訴男孩，她喜歡這張畫，因為小人們看起來不同，卻站得夠近，可以互相幫忙。

In the second part of practice, everyone had to make a small choice while the ball was moving. Tess did not say who should get it. The players called names, listened, and tried to find space. Some passes were good and some were poor, but the group stayed calm. Ben made his first clean turn and asked if anyone had seen it. Everyone said yes. He bowed as a joke, and Rose laughed so hard that she had to sit down.

練習後半段，每個人在球移動時都要自己做一個小決定。泰絲不指定誰接球。隊員們喊名字、傾聽並尋找空間。有些傳球很好，有些不理想，但整個團隊保持平靜。班第一次乾淨俐落地完成轉身，問大家有沒有看到。所有人都說有。他開玩笑地鞠躬，逗得蘿絲笑到坐下來。

Before the afternoon ended, Ben tried on the jeans he had brought for going home. He told June he had one question and wanted a clear answer. "If the ball comes to me too soon, may I let it go?" he asked. "Yes," June said. "You can ask a friend to take it instead." Kai offered to pick up the practice marks while they talked. Tess told him the last ones were hers to collect, but he said he was glad to help. Ben looked at the page and smiled. "This choice is mine," he said, touching the place where he had written his own plan. "And the team is ours." Tess answered that he was right. Soon everyone was ready to leave, and the field was clean again.

下午結束前，班換上帶來的牛仔褲，準備回家。他告訴瓊，自己有一個問題想得到明確答案。「如果球太快傳來，我能不能先讓過？」他問。「可以，」瓊說，「你可以請朋友接應。」大家談話時，凱主動撿起練習記號。泰絲說最後幾個原本是她負責，凱回答自己很樂意幫忙。班看著紙頁微笑，摸著自己寫下計畫的地方說：「這個選擇是我的，而球隊是我們的。」泰絲說他講得對。不久，大家準備離開，球場也再次被收拾乾淨。

Before the team left, Tess said she had a wish of her own. She wanted each player to feel that this team was partly theirs. "I may have started it," she said, "but I do not own it." The others thought about that for a moment. Kai said he would be glad to lead the next warm-up if people wanted him to. They agreed, and Tess wrote his name beside the next date. She had given away a small task and gained a stronger team.

離開前，泰絲說自己也有一個心願：希望每位球員都覺得這支球隊有一部分屬於自己。「也許是我先開始，」她說，「但它不是我一個人的。」大家想了一會兒。凱說，如果其他人願意，他很樂意帶下次的暖身。眾人同意，泰絲便把他的名字寫在下個日期旁。她交出一項小工作，卻得到更強的隊伍。
