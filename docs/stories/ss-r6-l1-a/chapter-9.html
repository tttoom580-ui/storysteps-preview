節慶後的散步

A Walk After the Festival

第七個位置 · 第 9 / 25 章 · 1000以下 · 英中對照 · 校訂中

用互動版查詞、朗讀與收藏

The town festival filled the main street with tables and music. Tess went with her grandmother, who had watched many games in her life. They bought rice and a banana dish, then sat by the lake. Her grandmother said she was ready to hear about the team's first loss. Tess told her the score and added that she was quite sure everyone had tried. "And did you have any joy?" her grandmother asked. Tess remembered Ava's goal and nodded.

小鎮節慶讓主要街道擺滿桌子，也充滿音樂。泰絲和祖母一起參加。祖母這一生看過很多比賽。兩人買了米飯和香蕉做的餐點，坐在湖邊。祖母說她已準備好聽泰絲說第一次失利的事。泰絲告訴她比分，也說自己相當確定大家都盡了力。「那你有感到快樂嗎？」祖母問。泰絲想起艾娃的進球，點了點頭。

A group of children came to visit the festival table. One child had a card with a picture of a street on it. Another carried a small dish with fruit. They asked Tess whether soccer was hard. She said the game was not easy, but people could help one another. A boy shouted, "I want to win every time!" His sister told him that even the best teams lost sometimes. Tess was surprised to hear her own new lesson from someone so young.

一群孩子來到節慶攤位。有個孩子拿著印有街道照片的卡片，另一個端著一小盤水果。他們問泰絲足球難不難。她說比賽不簡單，但人們可以互相幫助。一個男孩大喊：「我每次都要贏！」姐姐告訴他，就連最好的球隊也有輸的時候。泰絲很驚訝，竟從這麼年幼的人口中聽到自己剛學會的道理。

At one festival table, a small boy began to shout because he had lost a game. His grandmother walked over and asked him to speak more softly. She did not tell him he had to feel happy. She said he could feel upset, but he still had to treat the other child with care. Tess heard the words and thought of her soccer team. A loss could hurt, and a person might want to blame someone. But that wish did not make blaming fair. Tess went to the boy and asked if he wanted to help put the game pieces away. He nodded, and the two children worked side by side again. Her own grandmother smiled. "Sometimes a small action helps more than a long talk," she said.

節慶攤位旁，一個小男孩因為遊戲輸了而開始大喊。祖母走來，請他放低聲音。她沒有要求孩子一定要開心，而是說難過可以，但仍要好好對待另一個孩子。泰絲聽見後，想到自己的足球隊。輸球會難受，人也可能想責怪別人，但那不代表責怪就是公平的。她走向男孩，問願不願意一起把遊戲材料收好。男孩點頭，兩個孩子又肩並肩做事了。泰絲的祖母微笑說：「有時一個小行動，比一段長談更有用。」

At the water, her grandmother pointed to two boats on the lake. One moved quickly, while the other went slowly. Both were on their own way. "A team is not one person," she said. "If one player is tired, a friend can help." Tess said the town game was near and they had much to prepare. Her grandmother gave her a small tip: ask each person what part of the last game they wanted to change, and listen to the answer.

到了水邊，祖母指著湖上的兩艘船。一艘移動得快，另一艘慢慢前進，但都走在各自的路上。「球隊不是一個人，」她說，「如果有人累了，朋友可以幫忙。」泰絲說小鎮比賽快到了，還有很多準備要做。祖母給她一個小建議：問每個人最想改變上一場比賽的哪個部分，然後好好聽答案。

The next day Tess asked the team to meet near the same lake. Ben said he wanted to call names more clearly. Rose wanted to make the goal safer. Kai wanted to learn when a hard kick was useful and when it was not. June wanted to have more time to find her place. Leo wanted to try a new way of passing. Ava wanted a better first touch. Tess wrote every answer without saying whether it was right or wrong.

隔天，泰絲請球隊到同一座湖附近集合。班說他想更清楚地喊出隊友名字；蘿絲想把球門守得更穩；凱想知道什麼時候需要大力踢，什麼時候不需要；瓊想有更多時間找到位置；里歐想試新的傳球方式；艾娃則想讓第一次碰球更穩。泰絲把每個答案記下，沒有立刻評斷對錯。

They agreed to treat the next practice as a chance to help the person beside them. Tess said it was okay to feel sad after a loss, but it was not okay to put all the blame on one player. No one had done that, and she wanted to keep it that way. They made a plan with three steps and wrote it on a big card. Ava offered to bring the card to every meeting so they would not forget what they had promised.

大家同意，下次練習要把重點放在幫助身邊的人。泰絲說，輸球後難過是可以的，但把全部責任推給某個人不可以。目前沒有人那樣做，她希望以後也不會。他們訂了三個步驟的計畫，寫在大卡片上。艾娃主動提出，每次集合都帶著卡片，提醒大家記住承諾。

That evening Tess walked back along the street with her grandmother. There was less noise now, and workers were putting away the festival tables. Her grandmother said that a good coach did not need to speak every minute. Sometimes it was enough to ask a question and give people time to think. Tess looked at the plan in her hand. She had started the team to play soccer. Now she was learning how to make room for six other people.

傍晚，泰絲和祖母沿街走回家。節慶的喧鬧少了，工作人員正在收桌子。祖母說，好的教練不需要每分鐘都說話。有時候，只要問一個問題，給人思考的時間就夠了。泰絲看著手中的計畫。她成立球隊原本只是為了踢足球，現在卻在學著為另外六個人留下空間。
