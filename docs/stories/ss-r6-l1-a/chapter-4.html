小鎮廣場

The Town Square

Clouds covered the beach on the next practice day. A strong wind began to blow, and Sam said the sand was not safe. Tess did not ask him to change his mind. She took the team back toward town. A worker outside a small factory saw them with their bags. He pointed to a square beside the building. "You can do slow steps there," he said, "if you keep the path open." Tess thanked him and asked whether there was a person she should call first.

下一次練習日，雲層蓋住海灘。強風吹起，山姆說沙地不安全。泰絲沒有要求他改變主意，而是帶大家往鎮上走。一名小工廠的工人看見隊員帶著袋子，便指著建築旁的小廣場。「只要保持通道暢通，你們可以在那裡慢慢練腳步。」他說。泰絲道謝，並詢問是否應先聯絡負責人。

The owner came out, a tall woman named Ms. Park. She said the square was not a soccer field. There were windows and a road beside it. The team could not kick hard or let a ball roll into the street. "Can we practice without a ball?" Tess asked. Ms. Park looked at the group and agreed. Each person would use a small piece of paper as a mark on the ground. They could move between the marks and shake hands at the end.

老闆走了出來，是位高個子的女子，大家叫她朴女士。她說廣場並不是足球場，旁邊有窗戶和馬路。隊員不能用力踢球，也不能讓球滾到街上。「那我們不帶球練習可以嗎？」泰絲問。朴女士看了看大家，答應了。每人用一小片紙作為地面記號，在記號之間移動，結束時再彼此握手。

A boy watched from a window with a butterfly picture. He wanted to know why they were not playing a real game. Rose explained that there was more to soccer than kicking. She showed him how a player could move into an open space for a friend. The boy tried the steps on his side of the glass. His little sister made the same steps behind him. Both children laughed, and the team began to laugh too.

一個男孩從貼著蝴蝶圖案的窗邊看著他們，想知道為什麼不踢真正的比賽。蘿絲解釋，足球不只是踢球。她示範球員如何移到空位，好讓朋友傳球。男孩在玻璃另一側模仿步伐，妹妹也跟在身後做同樣的動作。兩個孩子笑了，隊員們也笑了起來。

Leo made a mistake and stepped off his paper mark. He said, "Sorry, sir," to an older man passing by, though the man had not been in his way. The man smiled and said it was good to see healthy people doing exercise without making a loud noise. June told a short story about her first sports class abroad. She had made the same mistake many times. Hearing that helped Leo try again without feeling small.

里歐走錯一步，離開了紙記號。他對一位經過的年長男子說：「對不起，先生。」其實他並沒有擋到人。男子微笑說，很高興看見健康的人運動，卻不製造很大的聲音。瓊講起她在國外第一次上體育課的短故事，說自己當時也犯過很多相同的錯。聽到這些，里歐不再覺得丟臉，願意重新試一次。

Before they left, the factory worker offered a cheap box for keeping paper marks dry. The team did not need to buy new things. Ava found a tomato rolling from a lunch bag and gave it back to its owner. A little girl nearly dropped her cup, but Ben caught it. Ms. Park said that careful hands were part of good service to others. Tess thanked the children, the worker, and Ms. Park for sharing the space for a short time.

離開前，工廠工人提供一個便宜的箱子，讓大家把紙記號收好以免受潮。隊員不需要買新東西。艾娃發現一顆番茄從便當袋滾出來，便還給主人。一個小女孩差點掉落杯子，班及時接住。朴女士說，做事小心也是服務他人的一種方式。泰絲感謝孩子們、工人和朴女士，願意短暫分享這塊空間。

When they reached the end of the street, rain began to fall. Tess said the practice was short, but not a loss. Their feet had learned where to go without a ball. Their eyes had learned to look for one another. And every person had helped keep the square safe. "Next time we need a field," Rose said. "Yes," Tess answered, "but today we learned how to move as a team." The rain made small marks on the town road behind them.

他們走到街尾時，開始下雨。泰絲說練習雖短，卻不算白費。雙腳學會了不靠球也能移動到對的位置；雙眼學會尋找隊友；每個人也幫忙維持廣場安全。「下次我們需要球場，」蘿絲說。「是啊，」泰絲回答，「但今天我們學會了像一支球隊那樣移動。」雨點在身後的小鎮道路上留下細小印記。

While they waited for the rain to stop, the team stood under a roof near the square. A gray cloud moved slowly over the factory, and water began to drop from the edge of the roof. Ben held his bag away from the water. Ms. Park came out again and asked whether anyone had left a paper mark on the ground. Tess counted them and found that one was missing. Leo looked back through the window and saw it by the door. He asked permission to take it. Ms. Park said yes, and he brought it back. The team learned that leaving a borrowed place clean was part of finishing practice. Ava said the square had taught them a strange lesson: sometimes the most useful part of a sports day happened after the ball was put away.

等雨停時，球隊站在廣場附近的屋簷下。一朵灰雲緩緩飄過工廠，水從屋簷邊一滴滴落下。班把袋子移開，避開滴水。朴女士又走出來，問有沒有紙記號留在地上。泰絲數了數，發現少一張。里歐從窗戶往回看，發現紙片就在門旁。他先詢問能不能取回。朴女士同意，他才去拿。球隊學到，把借來的地方收拾乾淨，也是完成練習的一部分。艾娃說，廣場教了大家一個奇妙的道理：有時運動日最有用的部分，竟在球收好之後才發生。
