A Place on the List

第七個位置 · 第 6 / 25 章 · 1000以下 · 英中對照 · 校訂完成

用互動版查詞、朗讀與收藏

The seven players arrived at the town sports house on a weekend morning. A sign at the top of the door said that the soccer game was free, but the team had to put its name on the list before Friday. Inside, a woman sat beside a box of forms and a cup of coffee. She asked Tess to show the seven names. Tess had brought a clean page. She felt as if the small team were stepping into something real for the first time.

週末早晨，七名球員來到小鎮的運動中心。門頂的告示寫著足球賽免費參加，但球隊必須在星期五前報名。裡面一名女子坐在一箱表格旁，桌上還有一杯咖啡。她請泰絲出示七個人的名字。泰絲帶來一張乾淨的紙，感覺這支小隊伍第一次真正踏進了一件重要的事。

The woman explained the rules before anyone signed. Every team must play with care. A player could ask to leave the field at any time. The game would stop for an injury or bad weather. Tess asked what would happen if one member could not come. The woman said they could list two extra players later, but only with those people's agreement. "Do not write a name just to fill a box," she warned. Tess was glad to hear a rule she already believed.

女子在大家簽名前說明規則。每支球隊都必須小心踢球；球員可以隨時要求離場；有人受傷或天氣惡劣時，比賽會停止。泰絲問，如果隊員不能來，該怎麼辦。女子說，他們之後可以增加兩位候補，但必須得到本人的同意。「不要只為了填滿格子就寫上名字。」她提醒道。泰絲很高興，這個規則正是她本來就相信的事。

They had to arrive at the field office before it closed, so Ben's uncle offered to drive the car by the short road. He told them that anyone could come along except people who still needed to be at work. Tess checked that no one was being left behind without a way home. At a stop, she saw Leo waiting near the gate. She gave a wave, and he came over with his bag. In the car, Leo asked what the team would do if someone was late on a real game day. Tess said they would follow the rules and not begin with someone who was not there. They could call to check that the person was safe, but they would not tell that person to rush. Everyone agreed that a safe arrival was more important than reaching the field one minute sooner.

他們必須在球場辦公室關門前到達，於是班的叔叔提議開車走較短的路。他說，除了還得上班的人，其他人都可以搭車。泰絲先確認沒有人因此被丟下，也沒有人回不了家。停車時，她看到里歐在門邊等候，便揮了揮手，讓他拿著袋子過來。車上，里歐問如果正式比賽日有人遲到，球隊該怎麼辦。泰絲說他們會照規則辦事，不會把還沒到的人當作已到場。他們可以打電話確認安全，但不會催對方趕路。大家同意，安全抵達比提早一分鐘到球場重要。

Ben's uncle drove them to a nearby field to look at the place for their first friendly match. They passed a house with a wooden lion over the gate. Farther on, a boat sat beside the road, far from the sea. Leo thought it had a funny shape. June waved to the driver and thanked him for bringing them. They got out carefully, took the bags from the back, and walked around the field rather than running onto it.

班的叔叔開車帶他們去附近的球場，看看第一場友誼賽的場地。沿途經過一間門上有木獅子的房子。更遠處，一艘船停在路旁，離海很遠。里歐覺得它的形狀很好笑。瓊向司機揮手，謝謝他載大家過來。他們小心下車，從後座拿出袋子，先繞著球場走一圈，沒有立刻衝進去。

Rose found a long crack in the ground near one goal. "That is terrible for a foot," she said. Tess took a picture and showed it to the manager. The manager did not call her stupid or tell her to keep quiet. He put a sign beside the crack and said a worker would repair it. "You did the right thing," he said. "We cannot see every problem by ourselves." Tess was pleased, yet she asked when they could return for a safe test.

蘿絲在球門旁發現一道很長的地面裂縫。「腳踩進去可就糟了。」她說。泰絲拍下照片給管理員看。管理員沒有罵她笨，也沒有叫她閉嘴。他在裂縫旁放上警示牌，說會請工人修理。「你們做對了，」他說，「我們不可能獨自看見所有問題。」泰絲很高興，但仍確認何時可以回來安全地測試場地。

At the end of the visit, Kai asked if the others could finally see him kick a ball hard. Tess said they should wait until the ground was fixed. He made a face, then nodded. In the car home, he said it felt strange to stop before doing something fun. June answered that waiting was easier when someone explained why. The team talked about the coming days and chose a short practice in the park instead. No one wanted a broken ankle for a nice picture.

參觀快結束時，凱問大家能不能終於看他大力踢一次球。泰絲說要等地面修好。他做了個鬼臉，還是點頭。回程車上，他說在好玩的事開始前就停下來很奇怪。瓊回答，如果有人解釋原因，等待就會容易些。大家討論接下來幾天的安排，改選公園裡的簡單練習。沒有人想為了一張好看的照片而弄傷腳踝。

That night Tess placed the signed page beside her bed. The team now had an official place in the town game, but there was still so much to learn. She remembered how the field manager had listened when Rose spoke. The girl who had once thought that being a leader meant having all the answers now felt differently. A leader could look, ask, and wait. She was ready to do those things again when the team met next weekend.

那晚，泰絲把簽好的報名表放在床邊。球隊現在正式取得小鎮比賽的參賽資格，但還有很多要學。她想起蘿絲說話時，場地管理員認真聆聽的樣子。她過去以為領導者必須有所有答案，現在想法不同了。帶領大家也可以是觀察、提問和等待。她已準備好在下個週末繼續這樣做。
