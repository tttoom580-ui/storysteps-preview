球網與冰塊

The Net and the Ice

第七個位置 · 第 10 / 25 章 · 1000以下 · 英中對照 · 校訂中

用互動版查詞、朗讀與收藏

The sports center opened its field for a short practice. Tess brought a new net, two pencils, and a simple drawing of where players should stand. The center had a teacher who knew soccer, and he agreed to watch for half an hour. "You have five people who can kick well," he said, "but you still need to look between them." Ava loved a quick run, yet today she had to learn when to stop and look to her right.

運動中心開放球場供短時間練習。泰絲帶來新球網、兩枝鉛筆和一張簡單的站位圖。中心有位懂足球的老師，答應觀察半小時。「你們有五個人踢得不錯，」他說，「但還需要注意彼此之間的空間。」艾娃喜歡快速奔跑，可是今天她必須學著停下腳步，往右邊看看。

Rose showed the team how to make a safe wall in front of the goal. Leo passed to Ben, and Ben made a soft kick. The ball hit the net and came back. A young brother of one player watched from a seat near the line. He was blind, so June told him what was happening in a clear voice. He listened and asked good questions about the sound of a kick. Tess said it was right to give people information they could use.

蘿絲示範在球門前如何安全站位。里歐傳給班，班輕輕踢了一腳。球碰到球網又彈回來。一名隊員的弟弟坐在邊線旁觀看。他看不見，所以瓊用清楚的聲音告訴他場上發生什麼事。他聽著，問了幾個關於踢球聲音的好問題。泰絲說，讓每個人得到能使用的資訊是對的。

During the next round, Kai ran too close to Rose. Their feet touched, and Kai fell. He was not badly hurt, but he felt pain near his knee. The teacher stopped the game and told him to sit down. June brought ice wrapped in a towel. Kai wanted to stand up at once. Tess said he should spend some time resting and ask a health worker if the pain did not go away. No goal was worth making an injury worse.

下一輪，凱跑得離蘿絲太近。兩人的腳碰到一起，凱跌倒了。他沒有受重傷，但膝蓋附近疼痛。老師停止練習，請他坐下。瓊拿來用毛巾包住的冰塊。凱想立刻站起來，泰絲說他應該休息一會兒，如果疼痛沒有消退，就詢問醫護人員。沒有任何一分值得讓傷勢變得更糟。

The teacher drew a line on the page to show how they could make more space. "When a friend has the ball, another friend can move away from the crowd," he explained. "That gives the ball a clear way to go." Ava practiced with no ball first. She could still hear the soft sound of Kai talking from his seat. He told her which parts were easy to understand and which were not. His words helped the team even while he rested.

老師在紙上畫了一條線，示範如何創造更多空間。「當朋友拿著球時，另一位朋友可以離開人群，」他解釋，「這樣球就有清楚的路線可走。」艾娃先不帶球練習。她仍聽得見坐著休息的凱輕聲說話。凱告訴她哪些部分容易看懂，哪些不容易。他雖然休息，話語卻仍幫助了球隊。

After the practice, Tess asked everyone if the plan was too hard. Ben said he liked having a picture, but he needed more time to remember it. Rose said the net was good, yet they should check it before every game. June asked if she could explain the picture again, using just three short steps. The others said yes. Tess was surprised that a second explanation helped her too. She had thought she understood the plan when she drew it.

練習後，泰絲問大家計畫是不是太難。班說他喜歡有圖可看，但需要更多時間記住。蘿絲說球網很好，不過每場都該先檢查。瓊問能不能再用三個短步驟解釋一次。大家都同意。泰絲驚訝地發現，第二次解說也幫助了自己。她原本以為畫圖時就已經完全理解了。

The teacher asked the team to write their three moves on a page that they could read online later. Ava used a pencil to draw a line from Rose to June and then to Leo. Under the drawing she wrote, "Pass, look, and move." Tess liked it because it gave everyone the same idea without too many words. A child came over with an apple and watched them. He asked if Tess loved soccer more than anything else. Tess said she loved playing, but she also loved her friends and the time they spent together. "If playing makes someone hurt or unhappy, we can change the way we play," she said. The boy took a bite of his apple and nodded as though it were a very good answer. Tess added, "I love playing with all of you, even when we lose."

老師請球隊把三個配合寫在紙上，之後也能在線上閱讀。艾娃拿鉛筆，畫出從蘿絲到瓊、再到里歐的傳球線。圖下寫著：「傳球、抬頭、移動。」泰絲喜歡這張圖，因為用不多的字，就讓大家有相同概念。一個孩子拿著蘋果走來旁觀，問泰絲是否最愛足球，勝過所有東西。泰絲說她愛踢球，但也愛朋友，以及相處的時間。「如果踢球讓人受傷或不快樂，我們可以改變踢法。」她說。孩子咬一口蘋果，點點頭，彷彿這是很好的答案。泰絲補充說：「即使輸球，我也喜歡和你們一起踢球。」

As they left the center, Kai said his knee felt better, but he would not play again that day. Tess thanked him for making a careful choice. The team did not learn a new way to win in only one afternoon. They did learn how to make room, how to stop when someone fell, and how to listen to a teacher without forgetting their own questions. For Tess, that was enough work for one day.

離開中心時，凱說膝蓋比較不痛了，但今天不會再踢。泰絲謝謝他做了小心的選擇。球隊沒有在一個下午就學會必勝的方法，卻學會了如何留出空間、有人跌倒時如何停下，以及如何聽老師說話又不忘提出自己的問題。對泰絲而言，這一天的練習已經很充實。
