第一場友誼賽

The First Friendly Game

The team met another group in the park for a friendly game. On the way, Ben talked about baseball, a game he used to play with his sister. Kai said soccer felt different because the ball had to stay near their feet. Ava wore a blue dress over her shorts because she had come straight from a family meal. She changed behind a screen in the sports house before the game. Rose gave everyone a nice look and asked if they felt ready to start.

球隊在公園與另一群人進行友誼賽。路上，班談起自己以前和妹妹玩的棒球。凱說足球不一樣，因為球必須留在腳邊。艾娃剛參加家庭聚餐，短褲外還穿著藍色洋裝。比賽前，她到運動中心的屏風後換裝。蘿絲看看大家，親切地問是否準備好開始。

An officer from the park stood outside the field and told both groups where the safe line was. The young girl who had asked about soccer by the station came to watch with her mother. Her family gave the players water and told them not to worry about the score. Tess felt her heart beat fast. She had waited many days to play against another team. She thought it would be easy to remember the lessons. Then the first ball went behind her before she could turn.

公園的一名管理人員站在球場外，告訴兩隊安全界線在哪裡。之前在車站問足球問題的小女孩也和母親來看。她的家人給球員送水，叫大家別太在意分數。泰絲心跳很快。她已等了好多天，終於能與另一隊交手。她以為記住練習內容很容易，沒想到開球後，第一顆球就在她轉身前從身後滑過。

The other team scored quickly. Tess wanted to run after every ball, but she could not do everything by herself. Ben called her name, and she made a pass toward him. The pass was too hard, and he lost it. Leo asked for the ball, but Tess did not hear him. A second goal came soon after that. The other players were kind and gave them time to drink. Tess knew they were not trying to be mean. They were simply better at playing together.

對手很快得分。泰絲想追每一顆球，卻無法獨自完成所有事。班喊了她的名字，她朝班傳球，力道卻太大，班沒能接住。里歐要求傳球，泰絲沒有聽見。沒多久，對手又進一球。對方球員很友善，給他們時間喝水。泰絲知道對手不是故意刁難，他們只是更懂得一起踢球。

At the break, a dog ran near the field, and its owner came to take it back. Ben said the dog looked like a little ghost because it had white fur. The joke made the team laugh, but Tess still felt bad. She wanted to give everyone a better plan. Rose told her not to talk about ten things at once. "Tell us one thing," she said. Tess took a breath. "When you have the ball, look for a friend before you kick," she answered.

中場休息時，一隻狗跑到球場附近，主人趕來把牠帶走。班說那隻狗白白的，像個小幽靈。這句玩笑逗笑大家，但泰絲還是很難過。她想給大家更好的計畫。蘿絲請她不要一次講十件事。「先說一件就好。」她說。泰絲深吸一口氣，回答：「拿到球時，踢之前先找朋友。」

In the second half, June sent a short pass to Ava. Ava kicked toward the goal. The ball touched the net for the first time, and everyone shouted. It was only one goal, while the other team had four, but it mattered. They had done what they practiced. The mother beside the field clapped, and the girl raised both hands. Tess did not taste victory, yet she felt something better than she had in the first half: they had found a way to play.

下半場，瓊把球短傳給艾娃，艾娃起腳射門。球第一次碰上球網，所有人都歡呼。那只是他們的一分，對方已有四分，但這一球很重要。他們做到了練習過的事。場邊的母親拍手，小女孩高舉雙手。泰絲沒有嚐到勝利的滋味，卻感覺比上半場好多了：他們終於找到一起踢球的方法。

After the game, a parent asked whether the players wanted beef sandwiches from a nearby table. Tess thanked her and said they would check what food everyone could eat first. Ben chose bread and fruit instead. A small kite began to fly over the park, high above the trees. Ava stopped to watch it and asked why its line stayed tight in the wind. June said the person holding it had to move with the air. The group began to walk slowly toward the station. They had much to think about, but no one wanted to talk as if one mistake had made the whole game bad. Tess asked each friend to tell her one moment that had worked. Everyone had an answer, and none was the same.

賽後，一名家長問球員要不要吃附近桌上的牛肉三明治。泰絲道謝，說要先確認大家能吃什麼。班改選麵包和水果。公園上空，一只小風箏飛過樹梢。艾娃停下來看，問為什麼風箏線能在風中保持拉緊。瓊說，拉線的人得配合風移動。大家慢慢走向車站。雖然有很多要反省的事，卻沒有人想把某次失誤說成整場都很糟。泰絲請每個朋友說出一個有效的瞬間。每個人都有答案，而且都不相同。

After the game, the team walked through the park together. No one tried to hide the score. They said they had lost one to four and would learn from it. Tess thanked the other players for the friendly game. One of them said, "You are new, but you listen well. Keep going." Tess wrote that line in her notebook. She had wanted to show the town a strong team. Instead, the team had shown her its first small sign of strength.

賽後，大家一起走過公園。沒有人想掩飾比分。他們坦承以一比四落敗，也說會從中學習。泰絲感謝對方願意進行友誼賽。對方一名球員說：「你們剛組隊，但很會傾聽。繼續加油。」泰絲把這句話記進筆記本。她原本想向小鎮展示強隊的樣子，結果是球隊先向她展示了自己的第一點力量。
