時間的聲音

The Sound of Time

The clock in the park was broken, so the team could not tell when their short practice should end. Tess put a small clock beside the field and asked Leo to watch it. A loud noise from a car made June jump. June told the others that she sometimes found it hard to hear a voice when many people spoke at once. "We can be close enough to see each other," Ava said. "We do not need to shout over the noise."

公園裡的時鐘壞了，大家不知道短時間練習何時該結束。泰絲在場邊放了一個小時鐘，請里歐注意時間。汽車傳來巨大的噪音，讓瓊嚇了一跳。瓊說，有很多人同時說話時，她有時很難聽清聲音。「我們可以站近一點，看得見彼此，」艾娃說，「不用為了蓋過噪音而大喊。」

Each person took a turn moving the ball in a small circle. Tess asked them to say a name before making a pass. Ben made an error and called out the wrong name, but everyone laughed gently and tried again. He said that in his dream he was always a wonderful player. "In my dream I never make mistakes," he said. Rose answered, "In my dream I am not afraid of them." They were surprised by how much better they played after that talk.

每個人輪流在小圈子裡傳球。泰絲請大家傳球前先喊出名字。班叫錯了名字，大家溫和地笑了一下，又重新開始。他說夢裡的自己總是很棒的球員。「在夢裡我從不犯錯。」他說。蘿絲回答：「在我的夢裡，我不害怕犯錯。」那段對話之後，他們驚訝地發現彼此踢得更好了。

A rainbow appeared high in the sky after a little rain. Kai looked up and said the colors made him feel lucky. Tess told him that nature could be beautiful and still bring danger; they would leave if they saw dark clouds again. Ben said he was proud of the team for remembering the rule without Tess telling them. "Do you believe a team can become strong just by listening?" he asked. Tess said she did not know, but listening was a good start.

一陣小雨過後，天空出現一道彩虹。凱抬頭看，說那些顏色讓他覺得幸運。泰絲提醒他，大自然可以很美，卻仍可能有危險；如果烏雲再出現，他們就會離開。班說，他很為大家感到驕傲，因為沒有人提醒，他們也記得那條規則。「你相信光靠傾聽，就能成為強隊嗎？」他問。泰絲說她不知道，但傾聽是個好的開始。

Later, June invited Tess to see the yard behind her home. Her family had a rich green garden, not because they had a lot of money, but because they spent time caring for it. June showed Tess a line of stones and said that she used it when she practiced careful steps. She sometimes trained alone beside her bedroom window. "I do not want my body to be a problem for the team," she said. Tess answered, "Your body is not a problem. Our plan must fit all seven of us."

稍後，瓊邀泰絲去看自家後院。她家有一座綠意豐富的花園，不是因為有很多錢，而是家人花時間照顧。瓊指著一排石頭，說她練習小步移動時常用到它們。有時，她會獨自在臥室窗邊訓練。「我不想讓自己的身體成為球隊的問題。」她說。泰絲回答：「你的身體不是問題，我們的計畫要適合全部七個人。」

They talked about whether a team should always move at the speed of its fastest player. Tess said no. A pass had to reach a person, not a number on a clock. June showed her a slow step and a turn. Tess copied it and found that her feet were not as sure as she had thought. They practiced together until the evening light changed. There was no audience, only two friends learning something new in a quiet yard.

她們討論一支球隊是否永遠應該跟著最快的人前進。泰絲說不是。傳球要傳到人的腳邊，不是傳給時鐘上的數字。瓊示範慢步移動與轉身，泰絲跟著做，發現自己的腳步並沒有想像中穩。她們一起練到暮色改變。沒有觀眾，只有兩個朋友在安靜的院子裡學新東西。

June made a small plan for the next day. She would stand where she could see all six friends, and she would raise a hand when she wanted the ball. The idea was simple, but Tess thought it might help when the field was loud. Ben tried it before leaving. He moved away, looked toward June, and held up one hand. She saw him at once and laughed. It was a happy laugh, not one at his mistake. Rose suggested that the team try the same sign during a quiet practice first. Everyone wanted to know whether it would work when the game became fast. Tess reminded them they did not have to find out all at once. They could test one thing, talk about it, and change it if it did not help.

瓊替隔天想了一個小計畫。她要站在能看見六位朋友的位置，想接球時便舉手。方法很簡單，泰絲卻認為球場吵鬧時可能很有幫助。班離開前先試一次：他移開腳步，望向瓊，舉起一隻手。瓊立刻看見，笑了起來。那是開心的笑，不是在嘲笑他的錯誤。蘿絲建議先在安靜的練習中測試同樣手勢。大家都想知道比賽快起來時是否還有用。泰絲提醒，答案不用一次找完。他們可以一次測一件事，討論之後，不合適再改。

On her way home, Tess thought of the sound in the park and the quiet in June's yard. Neither place was perfect, but each had taught them a new way to play. She wrote a note: "See the person before you pass." It was simple enough for everyone to remember. She hoped that in the coming game they would have time to look up, even if another team was running toward them. For now, she had learned to hear a friend without a shout.

回家路上，泰絲想起公園的噪音，以及瓊家院子的安靜。兩個地方都不完美，卻各自教會他們新的踢法。她寫下筆記：「傳球之前，先看見人。」這句話很簡單，所有人都能記住。她希望到了比賽時，即使有對手迎面跑來，大家也有時間抬頭。至少現在，她學會了不靠大喊也能聽懂朋友。
