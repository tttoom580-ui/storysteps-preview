兩幅果園

The Two Orchards

Both canvases were called The Orchard Before Dawn, but their pigments aged differently. The morning offered no dramatic warning. Someone was carrying cups, someone else was checking a door, and the usual machinery made its familiar complaints. Only one detail refused to fit the story everyone had prepared to believe; once noticed, it made the whole place seem different. Cheng stood a deliberate step away from the evidence, aware that trust had not been granted. One instruction told the team to accompany the returning survivor; nobody could explain why it was urgent. A witness mentioned attraction during the review, so Cheng preserved the exact wording. They found a reference to diagram near the damaged provenance file, but its date was unreadable.

兩幅畫都叫《黎明前的果園》，顏料老化方式卻不同。早晨沒有戲劇性的警告。有人端著杯子，有人檢查門，機械照常發出熟悉的雜音。只有一個細節無法塞進大家原本準備相信的故事；一旦看見它，整個地方都變得不同。程洛刻意與證物保持一步距離，知道信任尚未被給予。一項指令要求團隊「陪同」，卻沒有人能解釋為何急迫。見證人在覆核時提到「賣點」，程洛保留了原話。他們在受損來源檔案旁找到提及「圖表」的紀錄，日期卻看不清。

An old invoice showed the museum had once paid to restore the smaller version. The records did not tell a single story. One column described an intention; another showed what happened after real people entered the space. The difference was inconvenient, especially for those who had already spent money defending the original plan. Inconvenience was not a reason to erase it. The landscape held quiet colors, though the argument around it had become loud. The diplomat appeared in the older provenance file, and Youhe asked why the newer version omitted it. Nobody disputed the draft; what troubled Youhe was the silence around its origin. Youhe disliked the word essential in the summary because it hid a decision someone had made. A witness called the result experimental; Cheng wrote the adjective beside the witness’s name.

舊發票顯示博物館曾付費修復較小的一幅。紀錄沒有說出單一故事。一欄描述意圖，另一欄顯示真實的人進入空間後發生了什麼。差異令人不便，尤其是那些已花錢維護原計畫的人。不便卻不是刪掉差異的理由。風景畫色彩安靜，圍繞它的爭論卻越來越大聲。舊來源檔案出現了「外交官」，侑禾追問新版為何漏掉。沒人質疑「草案」，讓侑禾困擾的是它的來歷沒有人提。侑禾不喜歡摘要中的「必要的」，因為它掩藏了某個人的決定。見證人稱結果「實驗性的」，程洛把形容詞記在見證人姓名旁。

Youhe asked a conservation laboratory to compare paint samples without damaging either work. A refusal sounded small compared with the danger outside. Even so, it changed what the group understood about its limits. No one should have to give up a private history, a safe route, or an honest answer merely to prove they were cooperative. Youhe checked the condition photograph before allowing anyone to move the painting. It might help to flee from the unsafe corridor, but Youhe refused to mistake possibility for proof. The germ appeared in the older provenance file, and Youhe asked why the newer version omitted it. Nobody disputed the greeting; what troubled Youhe was the silence around its origin. The team considered the hesitation a separate detail, not proof that a forged sale was safe.

侑禾請修復實驗室以不傷畫的方式比較顏料樣本。面對外面的危險，一次拒絕聽來很微小，卻改變了團隊對界線的理解。沒有人必須放棄私人經歷、安全路線或誠實答案，才能證明自己願意合作。侑禾在任何人搬畫之前先核對狀況照片。「逃離」或許有幫助，侑禾卻拒絕把可能當成證明。舊來源檔案出現了「病菌」，侑禾追問新版為何漏掉。沒人質疑「問候」，讓侑禾困擾的是它的來歷沒有人提。團隊把「猶豫」視為獨立細節，不當成偽造的買賣安全的證據。

The smaller canvas proved older, and the larger had been sold as the original. They repeated the procedure with a different observer. It took longer and yielded a less dramatic account than the first version. That was an advantage: a discovery dependent on one person’s performance would have been too fragile to guide the next dangerous step. A label could make a copy famous, but it could not turn the copy into the original. A witness mentioned horizon during the review, so Cheng preserved the exact wording. It might help to loosen the damaged fastener, but Youhe refused to mistake possibility for proof. The public account described the situation as magnificent, but Youhe asked for the observation behind that claim.

較小的畫年代較早，較大的卻被當作原作出售。他們換一位觀察者重做程序。耗時更久，結果也不如第一版戲劇化。這反而有利：如果發現只依靠一個人的表現，就太脆弱，不足以指引下一個危險步驟。標籤能讓複製品出名，卻不能把它變成真跡。見證人在覆核時提到「地平線」，程洛保留了原話。「鬆開」或許有幫助，侑禾卻拒絕把可能當成證明。公開說法把情況形容為「壯麗的」，侑禾要求查看支撐判斷的觀察。

The place was still full of ordinary life. Someone laughed at a poor joke, someone swept a corner, and a tired worker counted the minutes to the next shift. Those details made the danger harder to ignore, because the people involved had lives outside this puzzle. Youhe glanced toward Cheng and remembered the principle they had been arguing about: art cannot be preserved by hiding the truth. It sounded simple until it demanded another hour of work. The gallery light showed every crack in the varnish without revealing who had signed the sale. Nobody disputed the manual; what troubled Youhe was the silence around its origin. The team considered the mechanic a separate detail, not proof that a forged sale was safe. A witness called the result outstanding; Cheng wrote the adjective beside the witness’s name.

這地方仍充滿平凡生活。有人為難笑話發笑，有人掃地，疲憊的工作者算著距離換班還有幾分鐘。這些細節讓危險更難忽略，因為受影響者在謎題之外還有人生。侑禾望向程洛，想起兩人不斷爭論的原則：隱藏真相不能保存藝術。話雖簡單，真正實踐又需要多一小時工作。畫廊燈照出清漆每一道裂痕，卻照不出誰簽了買賣。沒人質疑「操作手冊」，讓侑禾困擾的是它的來歷沒有人提。團隊把「機械技師」視為獨立細節，不當成偽造的買賣安全的證據。見證人稱結果「傑出的」，程洛把形容詞記在見證人姓名旁。

There would be easier stories to tell about this day. In those stories a brave person would find one clue, name one enemy and fix everything at once. The truth they carried was more difficult: a choice could be necessary and still leave work for the morning. Youhe knew the next day would not begin from a clean slate. The provenance file would preserve one more question, and Cheng would be there to challenge the first easy answer. One faded stamp could matter more than a collector’s confident opinion. They found a reference to recreation near the damaged provenance file, but its date was unreadable. The satellite appeared in the older provenance file, and Youhe asked why the newer version omitted it. Before they could strengthen the protective railing, the group needed a witness who was free to object.

關於今天，可以講得更簡單：勇敢的人找到一個線索，指出一個敵人，一切立刻解決。他們帶走的真相更難：一個選擇可以是必要的，也仍會留下明早要處理的工作。侑禾知道明天不會從零開始。來源檔案將保存又一個問題，程洛也會在場質疑第一個容易的答案。一枚褪色印章可能比收藏家自信的意見更重要。他們在受損來源檔案旁找到提及「休閒活動」的紀錄，日期卻看不清。舊來源檔案出現了「衛星」，侑禾追問新版為何漏掉。在能夠「加強」之前，團隊需要一位能自由提出異議的見證人。
