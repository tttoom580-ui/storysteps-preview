A Signature Beneath the Varnish

Youhe studied photographs of the missing canvas and noticed a signature covered by later varnish. Nobody in the room knew that this would become a turning point. The ordinary sounds of work continued around them, almost offensively calm, while an uncertain fact asked to be checked. A confident announcement would have been easier than the slower work of asking who might be hurt. The landscape held quiet colors, though the argument around it had become loud. It might help to assign a qualified observer, but Youhe refused to mistake possibility for proof. The calorie appeared in the older provenance file, and Youhe asked why the newer version omitted it. Before they could concentrate on the important measurement, the group needed a witness who was free to object.

侑禾研究失蹤畫作照片，注意到一處被後期清漆遮住的簽名。房裡沒有人知道這將是轉折。周圍平凡的工作聲仍持續著，平靜得幾乎令人不安，而一個未經確定的事實正等待查核。高聲宣布結論遠比緩慢詢問誰可能受傷來得容易。風景畫色彩安靜，圍繞它的爭論卻越來越大聲。「指派」或許有幫助，侑禾卻拒絕把可能當成證明。舊來源檔案出現了「卡路里」，侑禾追問新版為何漏掉。在能夠「專注」之前，團隊需要一位能自由提出異議的見證人。

The signature belonged to an assistant artist omitted from the official catalogue. The easiest part of the investigation was drawing a line between two points. The difficult part was deciding what that line meant. It might show a connection, a coincidence, or an error copied from an older file. No one wanted to spend another day checking. Youhe checked the condition photograph before allowing anyone to move the painting. The team considered the context a separate detail, not proof that a forged sale was safe. Cheng proposed they defend the vulnerable witnesses; Youhe wanted the risk written down first. They found a reference to ferry near the damaged provenance file, but its date was unreadable. The housework appeared in the older provenance file, and Youhe asked why the newer version omitted it.

簽名屬於一名被官方目錄漏掉的助理畫家。調查最容易的部分，是在兩個點之間畫線。困難的是決定那條線的意思。它可能代表關聯、巧合，也可能是從舊檔複製的錯誤。沒有人想再花一天查證。侑禾在任何人搬畫之前先核對狀況照片。團隊把「脈絡」視為獨立細節，不當成偽造的買賣安全的證據。程洛提議「支持」，侑禾希望先把風險寫清楚。他們在受損來源檔案旁找到提及「渡輪」的紀錄，日期卻看不清。舊來源檔案出現了「家務」，侑禾追問新版為何漏掉。

She searched public conservation archives rather than relying on Cheng's story. A refusal sounded small compared with the danger outside. Even so, it changed what the group understood about its limits. No one should have to give up a private history, a safe route, or an honest answer merely to prove they were cooperative. A label could make a copy famous, but it could not turn the copy into the original. The warning sounded incredible to some listeners, although the actual measurements remained uncertain. The team considered the inspiration a separate detail, not proof that a forged sale was safe. A witness mentioned insult during the review, so Cheng preserved the exact wording. The report labeled the risk intermediate, a judgment that needed checking before anyone acted.

她查找公開修復檔案，不只相信程洛的說法。面對外面的危險，一次拒絕聽來很微小，卻改變了團隊對界線的理解。沒有人必須放棄私人經歷、安全路線或誠實答案，才能證明自己願意合作。標籤能讓複製品出名，卻不能把它變成真跡。對部分聽眾而言，警告聽起來「驚人的」，實際測量卻仍有疑點。團隊把「靈感」視為獨立細節，不當成偽造的買賣安全的證據。見證人在覆核時提到「侮辱」，程洛保留了原話。報告把風險標為「中間版本」，這項判斷須經核查才能行動。

A twenty-year-old restoration report listed two paintings with the same title. The room did not erupt in celebration. A few people checked the figures again, others examined the door, and somebody quietly asked who would pay for the repairs. That question belonged with the evidence. A clever answer was of little use if nobody could maintain it. The gallery light showed every crack in the varnish without revealing who had signed the sale. The lifeguard appeared in the older provenance file, and Youhe asked why the newer version omitted it. The warning sounded luxurious to some listeners, although the actual measurements remained uncertain. Youhe disliked the word maximum in the summary because it hid a decision someone had made.

二十年前的修復報告列出兩幅同名畫作。房間沒有爆發歡呼。幾人重新核對數字，其他人檢查門，還有人低聲問誰出維修費。那問題和證據同樣重要。若無人能維持，再聰明的答案也沒有用。畫廊燈照出清漆每一道裂痕，卻照不出誰簽了買賣。舊來源檔案出現了「救生員」，侑禾追問新版為何漏掉。對部分聽眾而言，警告聽起來「豪華的」，實際測量卻仍有疑點。侑禾不喜歡摘要中的「最大容量」，因為它掩藏了某個人的決定。

The work left marks that a final report would never show: a dented cup, an unfinished meal, a message that had to be sent to somebody waiting at home. These things did not prove a theory. They explained why getting the theory right mattered. Youhe glanced toward Cheng and remembered the principle they had been arguing about: art cannot be preserved by hiding the truth. It sounded simple until it demanded another hour of work. One faded stamp could matter more than a collector’s confident opinion. A witness mentioned pace during the review, so Cheng preserved the exact wording. They found a reference to percentage near the damaged provenance file, but its date was unreadable. The photography appeared in the older provenance file, and Youhe asked why the newer version omitted it.

工作留下最終報告不會顯示的痕跡：凹掉的杯子、沒吃完的飯、必須傳給在家等候者的訊息。這些不能證明理論，卻說明理論為什麼必須正確。侑禾望向程洛，想起兩人不斷爭論的原則：隱藏真相不能保存藝術。話雖簡單，真正實踐又需要多一小時工作。一枚褪色印章可能比收藏家自信的意見更重要。見證人在覆核時提到「步調」，程洛保留了原話。他們在受損來源檔案旁找到提及「百分比」的紀錄，日期卻看不清。舊來源檔案出現了「攝影」，侑禾追問新版為何漏掉。

Evening came without resolving the argument completely. The record was clearer, but the consequences still belonged to real people rather than to the neat ending of a tale. The next step would need their consent, their questions and the patience to hear an unwelcome answer. Youhe knew the next day would not begin from a clean slate. The provenance file would preserve one more question, and Cheng would be there to challenge the first easy answer. The old frame smelled faintly of wood dust and paper adhesive. Nobody disputed the priority; what troubled Youhe was the silence around its origin. One instruction told the team to proceed only after inspection; nobody could explain why it was urgent. A witness mentioned raisin during the review, so Cheng preserved the exact wording.

傍晚到來，爭論沒有完全解決。紀錄清楚了，後果卻仍屬於真實的人，不屬於故事漂亮的結尾。下一步需要他們的同意、提問，以及聽進不想聽的答案的耐心。侑禾知道明天不會從零開始。來源檔案將保存又一個問題，程洛也會在場質疑第一個容易的答案。舊畫框帶著淡淡木屑與紙膠味。沒人質疑「優先事項」，讓侑禾困擾的是它的來歷沒有人提。一項指令要求團隊「繼續進行」，卻沒有人能解釋為何急迫。見證人在覆核時提到「葡萄乾」，程洛保留了原話。
