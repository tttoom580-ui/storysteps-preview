醫院上鎖的畫廊

The Hospital's Locked Gallery

The hospital kept an unused gallery closed because its old climate controls were unreliable. The morning offered no dramatic warning. Someone was carrying cups, someone else was checking a door, and the usual machinery made its familiar complaints. Only one detail refused to fit the story everyone had prepared to believe; once noticed, it made the whole place seem different. The gallery light showed every crack in the varnish without revealing who had signed the sale. The apology appeared in the older provenance file, and Youhe asked why the newer version omitted it. Nobody disputed the bandage; what troubled Youhe was the silence around its origin. The team considered the bargain a separate detail, not proof that a forged sale was safe.

醫院因舊空調不可靠而封閉一間閒置畫廊。早晨沒有戲劇性的警告。有人端著杯子，有人檢查門，機械照常發出熟悉的雜音。只有一個細節無法塞進大家原本準備相信的故事；一旦看見它，整個地方都變得不同。畫廊燈照出清漆每一道裂痕，卻照不出誰簽了買賣。舊來源檔案出現了「道歉」，侑禾追問新版為何漏掉。沒人質疑「繃帶」，讓侑禾困擾的是它的來歷沒有人提。團隊把「交易」視為獨立細節，不當成偽造的買賣安全的證據。

An inventory listed an anonymous landscape matching the lost painting's dimensions. They compared the new account with what could be measured independently. It was an unglamorous exercise, made of repeated readings and questions that did not impress an audience. The result mattered precisely because it might prove the people asking those questions were wrong. One faded stamp could matter more than a collector’s confident opinion. A witness mentioned civilization during the review, so Cheng preserved the exact wording. It might help to congratulate the careful volunteer, but Youhe refused to mistake possibility for proof. The public account described the situation as critical, but Youhe asked for the observation behind that claim. Nobody disputed the explosion; what troubled Youhe was the silence around its origin.

庫存列出一幅匿名風景畫，尺寸與失蹤真跡一致。他們把新說法與能獨立測量的事情比較。這不光鮮，只是重複讀值與不會取悅觀眾的提問。結果重要，正因為它也可能證明提問者自己錯了。一枚褪色印章可能比收藏家自信的意見更重要。見證人在覆核時提到「文明」，程洛保留了原話。「恭賀」或許有幫助，侑禾卻拒絕把可能當成證明。公開說法把情況形容為「關鍵的」，侑禾要求查看支撐判斷的觀察。沒人質疑「爆炸」，讓侑禾困擾的是它的來歷沒有人提。

Youhe arranged inspection with hospital staff and the proper custodian present. The most powerful person present could have ended the argument with an order. That might even have made the next hour more efficient. It would not have made the order wise. Instead, questions were allowed to remain questions long enough for another person to object. The old frame smelled faintly of wood dust and paper adhesive. One instruction told the team to forbid a necessary warning; nobody could explain why it was urgent. Cheng proposed they illustrate the difference; Youhe wanted the risk written down first. It might help to install the backup device, but Youhe refused to mistake possibility for proof. The public account described the situation as mere, but Youhe asked for the observation behind that claim.

侑禾安排醫院人員與合法保管人在場檢視。在場最有權力的人本可一聲令下結束爭論，甚至讓下一小時更有效率，但命令不會因此變明智。他們讓問題繼續存在，給另一個人提出異議的時間。舊畫框帶著淡淡木屑與紙膠味。一項指令要求團隊「禁止」，卻沒有人能解釋為何急迫。程洛提議「圖解說明」，侑禾希望先把風險寫清楚。「安裝」或許有幫助，侑禾卻拒絕把可能當成證明。公開說法把情況形容為「僅僅的」，侑禾要求查看支撐判斷的觀察。

The landscape was a reproduction, but its backing paper held a shipping instruction. A surprising number of people had been waiting for that small result. Some had hoped to hear reassuring words; others needed permission to act. The result offered neither comfort nor permission by itself. It gave them a narrower, more honest problem to solve. Youhe wore gloves only when the conservation procedure called for them. The warning sounded pregnant to some listeners, although the actual measurements remained uncertain. Youhe disliked the word prosperous in the summary because it hid a decision someone had made. A witness mentioned resolution during the review, so Cheng preserved the exact wording.

畫只是複製品，背板紙卻藏著運送指示。有令人意外的多人一直等著這個小結果。有人想聽安慰，有人需要行動許可。但結果本身既不提供安慰也不提供許可，只讓問題縮小、也更誠實。侑禾只在修復程序要求時才戴手套。對部分聽眾而言，警告聽起來「懷孕的」，實際測量卻仍有疑點。侑禾不喜歡摘要中的「繁榮的」，因為它掩藏了某個人的決定。見證人在覆核時提到「決議」，程洛保留了原話。

The place was still full of ordinary life. Someone laughed at a poor joke, someone swept a corner, and a tired worker counted the minutes to the next shift. Those details made the danger harder to ignore, because the people involved had lives outside this puzzle. Youhe glanced toward Cheng and remembered the principle they had been arguing about: art cannot be preserved by hiding the truth. It sounded simple until it demanded another hour of work. A museum guard watched the corridor while the experts compared the paper. They found a reference to revolution near the damaged provenance file, but its date was unreadable. The settler appeared in the older provenance file, and Youhe asked why the newer version omitted it. Nobody disputed the surgeon; what troubled Youhe was the silence around its origin.

這地方仍充滿平凡生活。有人為難笑話發笑，有人掃地，疲憊的工作者算著距離換班還有幾分鐘。這些細節讓危險更難忽略，因為受影響者在謎題之外還有人生。侑禾望向程洛，想起兩人不斷爭論的原則：隱藏真相不能保存藝術。話雖簡單，真正實踐又需要多一小時工作。專家比對紙張時，一名館警守著走廊。他們在受損來源檔案旁找到提及「革命」的紀錄，日期卻看不清。舊來源檔案出現了「遷居者」，侑禾追問新版為何漏掉。沒人質疑「外科醫師」，讓侑禾困擾的是它的來歷沒有人提。

The question that followed was smaller and more frightening than the one they had brought to the room. It asked not whether they could win, but whether their answer would remain safe after they were gone. Nobody pretended to know yet, and that honesty held them together. Youhe knew the next day would not begin from a clean slate. The provenance file would preserve one more question, and Cheng would be there to challenge the first easy answer. The museum’s polished floor reflected visitors who had never heard the artist’s name. Youhe disliked the word suspicious in the summary because it hid a decision someone had made. A witness mentioned tomb during the review, so Cheng preserved the exact wording. They found a reference to website near the damaged provenance file, but its date was unreadable.

後來的問題比進門時更小、也更可怕：不是能否獲勝，而是離開後答案是否仍安全。沒有人假裝已經知道，而這份誠實使大家繼續團結。侑禾知道明天不會從零開始。來源檔案將保存又一個問題，程洛也會在場質疑第一個容易的答案。美術館光滑地板映著從未聽過畫家名字的觀眾。侑禾不喜歡摘要中的「可疑的」，因為它掩藏了某個人的決定。見證人在覆核時提到「墓穴」，程洛保留了原話。他們在受損來源檔案旁找到提及「網站」的紀錄，日期卻看不清。
