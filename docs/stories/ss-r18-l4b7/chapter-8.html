偷走世界名畫的人是我的搭檔 · 第 8 / 25 章 · 3000–4000 · 英中對照 · 校訂中

At the customs depot, officers found that a painting-sized container had been rerouted decades earlier. The plan looked respectable at first glance. It had dates, names, and a signature in the proper corner. But paper can be complete without being honest, and a polished surface does not remove the cost carried by those who have no place to object. The old frame smelled faintly of wood dust and paper adhesive. The report labeled the risk abstract, a judgment that needed checking before anyone acted. The plan called for them to acquire the missing permission, and Youhe asked who would check the result. Nobody disputed the bridegroom; what troubled Youhe was the silence around its origin.

海關人員發現幾十年前有只畫作尺寸的貨櫃遭改道。計畫乍看很正式，有日期、姓名，簽名也在正確角落。然而文件可以完整卻不誠實；漂亮的外觀不能消除無處提出異議的人所承擔的代價。舊畫框帶著淡淡木屑與紙膠味。報告把風險標為「抽象的」，這項判斷須經核查才能行動。計畫要求他們「取得」，侑禾追問由誰查核結果。沒人質疑「新郎」，讓侑禾困擾的是它的來歷沒有人提。

The digital database lacked old entries, but paper receipts survived in a private warehouse. Rumor traveled faster than any signed report. By the time the second witness arrived, strangers were repeating a conclusion that neither witness had actually made. The group had to slow down, separate observation from interpretation, and check which details could survive an honest question. Youhe wore gloves only when the conservation procedure called for them. The team considered the bulletin a separate detail, not proof that a forged sale was safe. A witness called the result civilian; Cheng wrote the adjective beside the witness’s name. They found a reference to composer near the damaged provenance file, but its date was unreadable. The plan called for them to constitute a real threat, and Youhe asked who would check the result.

電子資料庫缺了舊紀錄，紙本收據卻留在私人倉庫。傳言傳得比任何簽名報告都快。第二名見證人到場時，陌生人已在重複兩名證人都沒說過的結論。團隊必須放慢腳步，區分觀察與解讀，確認哪些細節經得起誠實提問。侑禾只在修復程序要求時才戴手套。團隊把「公告」視為獨立細節，不當成偽造的買賣安全的證據。見證人稱結果「平民」，程洛把形容詞記在見證人姓名旁。他們在受損來源檔案旁找到提及「作曲家」的紀錄，日期卻看不清。計畫要求他們「構成」，侑禾追問由誰查核結果。

Youhe requested lawful access to certified copies rather than entering the warehouse herself. A refusal sounded small compared with the danger outside. Even so, it changed what the group understood about its limits. No one should have to give up a private history, a safe route, or an honest answer merely to prove they were cooperative. A museum guard watched the corridor while the experts compared the paper. Nobody disputed the cue; what troubled Youhe was the silence around its origin. One instruction told the team to dominate the discussion; nobody could explain why it was urgent. A witness mentioned enthusiasm during the review, so Cheng preserved the exact wording. They found a reference to estimate near the damaged provenance file, but its date was unreadable.

侑禾依法取得核證影本，不自行闖入倉庫。面對外面的危險，一次拒絕聽來很微小，卻改變了團隊對界線的理解。沒有人必須放棄私人經歷、安全路線或誠實答案，才能證明自己願意合作。專家比對紙張時，一名館警守著走廊。沒人質疑「提示音」，讓侑禾困擾的是它的來歷沒有人提。一項指令要求團隊「主導」，卻沒有人能解釋為何急迫。見證人在覆核時提到「熱情」，程洛保留了原話。他們在受損來源檔案旁找到提及「估計」的紀錄，日期卻看不清。

One receipt carried Cheng Luo's father's signature. The first result was neither victory nor defeat. It removed one false assumption and exposed another problem that had been concealed beneath it. A witness asked whether the discovery could be trusted. The answer was that the test could be repeated, not that the investigators were special. The museum’s polished floor reflected visitors who had never heard the artist’s name. The public account described the situation as fertile, but Youhe asked for the observation behind that claim. Nobody disputed the gender; what troubled Youhe was the silence around its origin. The team considered the gulf a separate detail, not proof that a forged sale was safe.

其中一張收據有程洛父親的簽名。第一個結果既非勝利也非失敗。它排除一個錯誤假設，也露出下面藏著的另一個問題。見證人問發現能否相信；答案是測試可以重複，不是調查者有什麼特權。美術館光滑地板映著從未聽過畫家名字的觀眾。公開說法把情況形容為「適合滋生的」，侑禾要求查看支撐判斷的觀察。沒人質疑「性別」，讓侑禾困擾的是它的來歷沒有人提。團隊把「海灣」視為獨立細節，不當成偽造的買賣安全的證據。

A small act of care interrupted the discussion. It solved no mystery and changed no official ruling. Yet it reminded them that the people named in the records were not pieces on a board; they had bodies, families and the right to decide what came next. Youhe glanced toward Cheng and remembered the principle they had been arguing about: art cannot be preserved by hiding the truth. It sounded simple until it demanded another hour of work. Cheng stood a deliberate step away from the evidence, aware that trust had not been granted. A witness mentioned intensity during the review, so Cheng preserved the exact wording. It might help to invade the private records, but Youhe refused to mistake possibility for proof. The jealousy appeared in the older provenance file, and Youhe asked why the newer version omitted it.

一個小小的照顧動作打斷討論。它沒有解開謎，也沒改變裁決，卻提醒大家紀錄裡的人不是棋子；他們有身體、家庭，也有決定下一步的權利。侑禾望向程洛，想起兩人不斷爭論的原則：隱藏真相不能保存藝術。話雖簡單，真正實踐又需要多一小時工作。程洛刻意與證物保持一步距離，知道信任尚未被給予。見證人在覆核時提到「強度」，程洛保留了原話。「入侵」或許有幫助，侑禾卻拒絕把可能當成證明。舊來源檔案出現了「嫉妒」，侑禾追問新版為何漏掉。

Before leaving, they checked the names once more. Names were easier to lose than equipment, especially when a system reduced people to numbers. The check took little time. It kept the next chapter of the problem from beginning with a convenient omission. Youhe knew the next day would not begin from a clean slate. The provenance file would preserve one more question, and Cheng would be there to challenge the first easy answer. The landscape held quiet colors, though the argument around it had become loud. The warning sounded learned to some listeners, although the actual measurements remained uncertain. The team considered the software a separate detail, not proof that a forged sale was safe. A witness called the result systematic; Cheng wrote the adjective beside the witness’s name.

離開前，他們再次核對名字。名字比設備更容易消失，尤其當系統把人簡化成數字。檢查花不了多少時間，卻避免問題的下一章從方便的遺漏開始。侑禾知道明天不會從零開始。來源檔案將保存又一個問題，程洛也會在場質疑第一個容易的答案。風景畫色彩安靜，圍繞它的爭論卻越來越大聲。對部分聽眾而言，警告聽起來「學養深厚的」，實際測量卻仍有疑點。團隊把「軟體」視為獨立細節，不當成偽造的買賣安全的證據。見證人稱結果「有系統的」，程洛把形容詞記在見證人姓名旁。
