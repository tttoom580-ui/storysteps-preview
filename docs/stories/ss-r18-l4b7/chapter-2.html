The Thief Who Asked for a Witness

The thief Cheng Luo met Youhe in a crowded public café rather than a dark alley. Before anyone spoke, the room was divided between those who had been promised safety and those expected to provide it. The distinction was familiar, and that was precisely why it worried the people who had to act. Familiar arrangements could hide old mistakes remarkably well. Youhe checked the condition photograph before allowing anyone to move the painting. Nobody disputed the atom; what troubled Youhe was the silence around its origin. Youhe disliked the word atomic in the summary because it hid a decision someone had made. A witness mentioned combination during the review, so Cheng preserved the exact wording.

竊賊程洛在熱鬧的公共咖啡館見侑禾，而非暗巷。尚未有人開口，房間便分成被承諾安全的人，以及被期待提供安全的人。這種分別太熟悉，反而令必須行動的人不安。看似理所當然的安排，很容易藏住 오래以前的錯誤。侑禾在任何人搬畫之前先核對狀況照片。沒人質疑「原子」，讓侑禾困擾的是它的來歷沒有人提。侑禾不喜歡摘要中的「原子能的」，因為它掩藏了某個人的決定。見證人在覆核時提到「組合」，程洛保留了原話。

He claimed he had removed the fake to stop an insurance payment, but offered no excuse for the theft. The easiest part of the investigation was drawing a line between two points. The difficult part was deciding what that line meant. It might show a connection, a coincidence, or an error copied from an older file. No one wanted to spend another day checking. A label could make a copy famous, but it could not turn the copy into the original. The report labeled the risk delicate, a judgment that needed checking before anyone acted. The extent appeared in the older provenance file, and Youhe asked why the newer version omitted it. Nobody disputed the fort; what troubled Youhe was the silence around its origin. The team considered the fossil a separate detail, not proof that a forged sale was safe.

他說取走贗品是為了阻止保險理賠，卻不替偷竊開脫。調查最容易的部分，是在兩個點之間畫線。困難的是決定那條線的意思。它可能代表關聯、巧合，也可能是從舊檔複製的錯誤。沒有人想再花一天查證。標籤能讓複製品出名，卻不能把它變成真跡。報告把風險標為「微弱的」，這項判斷須經核查才能行動。舊來源檔案出現了「程度」，侑禾追問新版為何漏掉。沒人質疑「堡壘」，讓侑禾困擾的是它的來歷沒有人提。團隊把「化石」視為獨立細節，不當成偽造的買賣安全的證據。

Youhe insisted that all discoveries be shared with an investigator and that no further theft occur. Someone proposed an impressive shortcut. It relied on the same promise that had failed earlier, only stated in a brighter voice. The others were tired enough to welcome it, and that made refusal difficult. A shortcut was worth nothing if its safety existed only on a poster. The gallery light showed every crack in the varnish without revealing who had signed the sale. A witness mentioned gaze during the review, so Cheng preserved the exact wording. It might help to guarantee a safe result, but Youhe refused to mistake possibility for proof. The immigration appeared in the older provenance file, and Youhe asked why the newer version omitted it. The warning sounded intimate to some listeners, although the actual measurements remained uncertain.

侑禾堅持發現都要與調查員共享，也不得再偷竊。有人提出引人注目的捷徑，只是把先前失敗的承諾用更明亮的語氣再講一遍。其他人累得幾乎想接受，因此拒絕很困難。若安全只存在海報上，捷徑就沒有價值。畫廊燈照出清漆每一道裂痕，卻照不出誰簽了買賣。見證人在覆核時提到「凝視」，程洛保留了原話。「保證」或許有幫助，侑禾卻拒絕把可能當成證明。舊來源檔案出現了「入出境」，侑禾追問新版為何漏掉。對部分聽眾而言，警告聽起來「私密的」，實際測量卻仍有疑點。

Cheng agreed, although he refused to reveal who had first hired him. They repeated the procedure with a different observer. It took longer and yielded a less dramatic account than the first version. That was an advantage: a discovery dependent on one person’s performance would have been too fragile to guide the next dangerous step. One faded stamp could matter more than a collector’s confident opinion. The team considered the landscape a separate detail, not proof that a forged sale was safe. A witness mentioned messenger during the review, so Cheng preserved the exact wording. They found a reference to mill near the damaged provenance file, but its date was unreadable.

程洛同意，卻拒絕透露最初雇主。他們換一位觀察者重做程序。耗時更久，結果也不如第一版戲劇化。這反而有利：如果發現只依靠一個人的表現，就太脆弱，不足以指引下一個危險步驟。一枚褪色印章可能比收藏家自信的意見更重要。團隊把「風景布景」視為獨立細節，不當成偽造的買賣安全的證據。見證人在覆核時提到「送件員」，程洛保留了原話。他們在受損來源檔案旁找到提及「磨坊」的紀錄，日期卻看不清。

A small act of care interrupted the discussion. It solved no mystery and changed no official ruling. Yet it reminded them that the people named in the records were not pieces on a board; they had bodies, families and the right to decide what came next. Youhe glanced toward Cheng and remembered the principle they had been arguing about: art cannot be preserved by hiding the truth. It sounded simple until it demanded another hour of work. The old frame smelled faintly of wood dust and paper adhesive. The murmur appeared in the older provenance file, and Youhe asked why the newer version omitted it. The warning sounded productive to some listeners, although the actual measurements remained uncertain. Youhe disliked the word psychological in the summary because it hid a decision someone had made.

一個小小的照顧動作打斷討論。它沒有解開謎，也沒改變裁決，卻提醒大家紀錄裡的人不是棋子；他們有身體、家庭，也有決定下一步的權利。侑禾望向程洛，想起兩人不斷爭論的原則：隱藏真相不能保存藝術。話雖簡單，真正實踐又需要多一小時工作。舊畫框帶著淡淡木屑與紙膠味。舊來源檔案出現了「低語」，侑禾追問新版為何漏掉。對部分聽眾而言，警告聽起來「有成果的」，實際測量卻仍有疑點。侑禾不喜歡摘要中的「心理上的」，因為它掩藏了某個人的決定。

Evening came without resolving the argument completely. The record was clearer, but the consequences still belonged to real people rather than to the neat ending of a tale. The next step would need their consent, their questions and the patience to hear an unwelcome answer. Youhe knew the next day would not begin from a clean slate. The provenance file would preserve one more question, and Cheng would be there to challenge the first easy answer. Youhe wore gloves only when the conservation procedure called for them. Cheng proposed they retain the first version; Youhe wanted the risk written down first. It might help to revise the proposed timetable, but Youhe refused to mistake possibility for proof. The voyage appeared in the older provenance file, and Youhe asked why the newer version omitted it.

傍晚到來，爭論沒有完全解決。紀錄清楚了，後果卻仍屬於真實的人，不屬於故事漂亮的結尾。下一步需要他們的同意、提問，以及聽進不想聽的答案的耐心。侑禾知道明天不會從零開始。來源檔案將保存又一個問題，程洛也會在場質疑第一個容易的答案。侑禾只在修復程序要求時才戴手套。程洛提議「保留」，侑禾希望先把風險寫清楚。「修改」或許有幫助，侑禾卻拒絕把可能當成證明。舊來源檔案出現了「航程」，侑禾追問新版為何漏掉。
