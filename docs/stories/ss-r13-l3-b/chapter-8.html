Consent Is Not a Form

讓水面記住我們的舞步 · 第 8 / 25 章 · 2000–3000 · 英中對照 · 校訂中

用互動版查詞、朗讀與收藏

The community day began with too much baggage at the door. One volunteer had brought a toy buffalo, another a bright yellow controller from an old game, and a retired cook arrived with a small electric cooker for lunch. Maya asked them to keep every object away from the route. The hall smelled of crispy bread, and visitors wanted to grab food before the meeting. Nia laughed, but she pointed to the main problem: lately, the organizers had collected too many stories without deciding how those stories might be used. A pile of papers was not the same as clear permission.

社區日一開始，門口就堆滿行李雜物。志工帶來玩具水牛，另一人拿來舊遊戲的黃色控制器，退休廚師則帶了小電鍋準備午餐。沐晴請他們把所有物件移離通道。廳裡飄著酥脆麵包香，訪客急著在會議前拿食物。倪雅笑著提醒真正的問題：最近主辦單位收了太多故事，卻未決定未來如何使用。成堆紙張不等於清楚的使用同意。

Nia introduced a simple process. The team would request permission for each recording, explain why it was needed, and offer a way to withdraw before publication. A citizen who wanted to share an experience without naming themselves could do so. Another visitor asked about a photograph of a crowd near the pool. Maya said that being visible in a public place did not automatically mean agreeing to an advertisement. She asked a volunteer to remove the photograph from the web until its use could be reviewed. The meeting was quiet as people realized that being invited to tell a story did not mean losing control of it.

倪雅介紹簡單流程：團隊每次錄音都須請求許可，說明用途，並在公開前提供撤回方式。想匿名分享經歷的居民也可以這樣做。另一位訪客問起泳池旁人群的照片。沐晴說，在公共場所入鏡，不代表自動同意拿去做廣告。她請志工先從網站撤下照片，等審查使用目的後再決定。當人們明白受邀說故事並不等於失去控制權，會場漸漸安靜下來。

A talkative man raised his hand and asked why the policy mentioned sexual harassment. Nia answered that a safe public project needed clear reporting routes for inappropriate behavior, not only rules about equipment. The organizers had to protect privacy and respond to complaints fairly. Maya read the paragraph aloud, avoiding language that blamed people for speaking up. A wealthy donor offered to pay for new posters if the team left the policy out. Maya thanked him and refused. The rules would remain visible even if a sponsor preferred a cleaner picture. She was beginning to understand that care had a cost.

一名健談男子舉手，問政策為何提到性騷擾。倪雅回答，安全的公共計畫不只有設備規範，也必須讓不當行為有清楚的通報管道。主辦者需要保護隱私，公平處理申訴。沐晴朗讀條文，避免把責任推給發聲者。有位富裕捐助者願意出錢印新海報，條件是刪去這段政策。沐晴道謝後拒絕。即使贊助商更喜歡漂亮形象，規則仍要讓人看見。她逐漸明白，照顧別人也需要付出代價。

After lunch, a child yelled when a cup fell from a bench. The sound made several people turn toward the doorway. Their shadows moved across the floor, long and narrow in the afternoon sun. Maya used the moment to test how people could signal a need for help without shouting. Some preferred a raised hand; others asked for a card or an accessible button. She wrote down each choice. One volunteer offered to build a simple online request page, while another promised to answer by telephone. No single method would suit everybody, and that was not a failure.

午餐後，一個孩子看見杯子從長椅掉下而大叫。大家轉向門口，午後陽光把影子拉得又長又窄。沐晴借這個機會測試，若不大喊，人們如何發出需要協助的訊號。有些人喜歡舉手，有些人希望有提示卡或容易操作的按鈕。她記下每項選擇。志工願意製作簡單的線上請求頁面，另一人答應接電話。沒有單一方法能適合所有人，這並不代表失敗。

Evan arrived after the meeting with a folder of repair estimates. He had nothing to say about recording rights, and Maya appreciated that he did not pretend otherwise. They walked along the quiet hall and checked the temporary signs. "I owe you dinner," she said. "You don't owe me anything for doing my job," he answered. She smiled and clarified that she was asking him to dinner as a person, not as an engineer. He stopped walking. "Then I would like that," he said, "after my inspection contract is finished, so neither of us has to wonder which role we are speaking from."

會後，以衡帶著一夾維修估價單抵達。他沒有對錄音權利發表意見，沐晴欣賞他不假裝自己是專家。兩人沿著安靜的大廳走，檢查臨時告示。「我欠你一頓晚餐。」她說。「做好工作不需要你還我什麼。」他回答。她笑著說明，這是以一個人的身分邀他吃飯，而不是以工程師身分。以衡停下腳步。「那我很願意，」他說，「等檢查合約結束後，這樣我們都不用猜自己是在用什麼身分說話。」

Nia turned the policy into a one-page guide with a clear bullet beside each important choice. It asked visitors not to litter in the hall, but it also told organizers not to confuse an untidy room with an unsafe person. Under the evening lights, Maya noticed a shadow behind the information desk and nearly called out. It was only a coat on a hanger. She decided not to yell when she was uncertain. Instead, she walked around the desk with a volunteer and checked the space. By the end of the day, the guide had been read aloud, offered in large print, and placed where a seated visitor could reach it. Consent had finally become something people could use.

倪雅把政策整理成一頁指南，每項重要選擇前都放上一個清楚的圓點。指南請訪客不要在廳內亂丟垃圾，也提醒主辦者別把房間凌亂與個人不安全混為一談。晚間燈光下，沐晴看見資訊桌後方有一道陰影，差點喊出聲；那只是一件掛著的外套。她決定不確定時先不要大吼，而是請志工一起走到桌後查看。當天結束前，指南已被朗讀、印成大字，也放在坐著的人拿得到的地方。同意終於成為人人可使用的安排。
