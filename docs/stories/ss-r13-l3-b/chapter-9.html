屋頂下的雨

Rain Under the Roof

讓水面記住我們的舞步 · 第 9 / 25 章 · 2000–3000 · 英中對照 · 校訂中

Rain reached the city before the weekly rehearsal. Water began to bleed through a cracked roof joint and drop into a bucket. A nearby drainage pipe made a low roar, and the sound was enough to frighten one visitor. Maya asked everyone to leave the affected zone. A skillful maintenance worker checked the doorway, while Evan observed from outside the marked area. A cabin-like storage room had a missing ceiling panel, so no one entered it. The team did not need a dramatic rescue. It needed to avoid turning a small leak into an injury.

每週排練開始前，雨就先抵達城市。水慢慢從龜裂的屋頂接縫滲出，滴進水桶。附近排水管發出低低的轟鳴，嚇到一名訪客。沐晴請所有人離開受影響區域。熟練的維修工檢查門口，以衡則在標線外觀察。一間像小木艙的儲物室少了天花板板片，沒有人進去。團隊不需要戲劇性的救援，而是需要防止小漏水演變成受傷事故。

Nia checked a database of public maintenance requests. Three earlier complaints mentioned the same corner, but each had been marked "closed" without a repair date. "Missing information is not the same as a missing person," she reminded the group. "Let's find the documents." She showed Maya a form with a space for the work order number. The floor near the bucket began to sink slightly under pressure from a trolley, so they moved the trolley away. No one used the old service lift. Every decision was small, but together they made the building less dangerous.

倪雅查閱公共維修申請資料庫，發現先前三則抱怨都提到同一角落，卻被標成「已結案」，沒有維修日期。「缺少資訊不等於有人失蹤，」她提醒大家，「先找文件。」她指給沐晴看表格上工單編號的位置。水桶附近的地板在手推車壓力下微微下陷，因此大家移開車子，沒有人使用舊貨梯。每個決定都很小，合起來卻讓建築少了一分危險。

When the rain eased, laughter returned. Two volunteers argued about the best tune for a quiet warm-up, and a third suggested recording the sound of a splash outside. Maya said they could explore that later, but only with dry equipment and agreement from everyone recorded. A young dancer confessed that she had been wandering through internet comments about the pool and had seen threats from strangers. Nia told her not to answer alone. The team saved the messages, used the reporting tools, and checked whether any threat was specific. A frightening comment was not a weapon, but it deserved care.

雨勢稍緩，笑聲又回來。兩名志工爭論安靜暖身該用哪段曲調，第三人提議錄下屋外水花飛濺的聲音。沐晴說以後可以試，但設備必須保持乾燥，也須取得所有被錄到的人的同意。一名年輕舞者承認自己一直在網路留言區徘徊，看到陌生人的威脅。倪雅要她不要獨自回覆。團隊保存訊息、使用通報工具，確認是否有具體威脅。嚇人的留言不是武器，卻值得認真處理。

A local reporter arrived shortly afterward and asked why the organization had hidden the leak. Maya answered that it had been discovered that morning and that the rehearsal had stopped immediately. She showed the written time without offering anyone's private phone number. The reporter asked about a former lover who supposedly owned the pool. Maya laughed at the strange rumor, then returned to the facts: the city held the deed, and the public record was available. She would not let gossip replace documents, even when a silly story might attract more readers than a repair report.

不久後，一名地方記者抵達，質問主辦者為何隱瞞漏水。沐晴回答，問題是當天早上才發現，排練也立刻停止。她出示記錄的時間，卻沒有提供任何人的私人電話。記者又問起傳說中擁有泳池的某位前情人。沐晴被荒唐謠言逗笑，接著把話題帶回事實：產權歸市府，公開紀錄可以查閱。她不願讓流言取代文件，即使荒唐故事比維修報告更容易吸引讀者。

That evening, Maya and Evan listened to the storm from opposite ends of a telephone call. She admitted that she had felt faint for a moment when the ceiling began to drip. Evan said fear did not make her incapable; it showed that she had noticed danger. They talked about music and childhood for the first time, without a clipboard between them. He asked whether she missed her work in the capital. She said she missed some friends but not the habit of measuring every success by applause. When the call ended, the rain was still falling, yet the next step seemed clearer.

當晚，沐晴與以衡隔著電話聽雨聲。她承認天花板開始滴水時，自己一瞬間幾乎昏厥。以衡說，恐懼不表示她沒有能力，反而表示她注意到了危險。他們第一次不隔著文件夾，聊起音樂與童年。他問她是否想念首都的工作。她說想念一些朋友，卻不想念凡事都用掌聲衡量成功的習慣。通話結束時雨還在下，但下一步似乎變得更清楚。

Outside the office, a vehicle horn sounded as traffic slowed near the flooded street. The noise gave Maya another moment of fright, but she stayed where the staff had marked a safe place. The rain had forced them to consider whether to bore a new opening through an old wall. Evan said that no one should propose drilling before a qualified engineer reviewed the structure. The council could practice democracy by discussing priorities openly, but public votes could not replace engineering judgment. A visitor who had begun to wander toward the closed passage was guided back to the main entrance. Maya wrote the incident down. She was learning to notice danger without turning it into a frightening legend.

辦公室外，一輛車按響喇叭，積水街道上的車流慢了下來。聲音讓沐晴又受了一驚，但她仍站在工作人員標定的安全區。雨勢迫使大家考慮是否要在舊牆鑽一個新開口。以衡說，合格工程師檢視結構之前，任何人都不應提議鑽牆。委員會可以藉公開討論優先順序實踐民主，但投票不能取代專業工程判斷。一位開始往封閉走道亂走的訪客被帶回主入口。沐晴把事件記下。她正學著注意危險，而非把危險講成可怕傳說。
