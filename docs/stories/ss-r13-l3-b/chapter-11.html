What the Walls Remember

讓水面記住我們的舞步 · 第 11 / 25 章 · 2000–3000 · 英中對照 · 校訂中

The engineering team began an operation to inspect the empty pool without filling it. Evan asked a colleague to measure the wall with accurate instruments. She found a loose bracket and a narrow ditch behind the old drainage cover. The crew did not dive into the pit; they used a camera attached to a long pole. Maya watched from the visitor line. She remembered the false stories that had surrounded the building for years. There was no need to bless the team with luck, she thought, when careful work could reduce danger. She asked what the findings meant and listened until the answer was clear.

工程團隊開始檢查空泳池的作業，不需要先注水。以衡請同事用精準儀器測量牆面。她找到鬆動的支架，以及舊排水蓋後方一道狹窄的溝槽。工作人員沒有潛入坑內，而是用長桿攝影機檢查。沐晴在訪客標線外觀看，想起多年來流傳的怪談。她心想，既然謹慎工作能降低危險，就不必只靠祈求祝福。她詢問發現代表什麼，並一直聽到自己真正理解。

In the old service room, a freezer once used for ice packs stood beside empty cans marked gasoline. Evan said the labels did not show whether fuel remained. The room had to be checked by the responsible contractor, not by curious volunteers. A violet stain covered part of a timber beam. It might be paint, or it might indicate moisture damage. The engineer would take a sample through approved procedures. Maya wrote the word "unknown" beside every unresolved point. She had spent her career trying to perform perfectly in public, yet here her most useful act was to admit what she did not know.

舊服務室裡，昔日存放冰敷包的冷凍櫃旁有幾個標著汽油的空罐。以衡說，標籤不能證明裡面現在是否還有燃料；必須由負責承包商檢查，而非好奇的志工。木樑上有一塊紫色污漬，可能是油漆，也可能與潮濕損壞有關。工程師將循核准程序取樣。沐晴在每個尚未解決的項目旁寫下「未知」。她一直努力在公共場合完美演出，現在最有用的行動卻是承認自己還不知道。

An inspector from the county office studied the original plans. He estimated that a repair could take roughly eight weeks, depending on what the team found behind the wall. Evan warned Maya not to treat that estimate as a promise. The inspector also noticed that old plastic pipes had been installed without a clear date. Nia asked whether the building would still serve people while repairs continued. The answer was yes, but only in the areas that passed inspection. They would keep the dry studio, hold meetings in the community hall, and avoid the closed corridor. The plan was smaller, but no longer imaginary.

縣府檢查員研究原始圖面，估計修復可能需要約八週，實際仍取決於牆後查到的情況。以衡提醒沐晴，不要把估計當成承諾。檢查員也注意到一些塑膠舊管，卻找不到安裝日期。倪雅問，修繕期間建築是否還能服務民眾。答案是可以，但只限通過檢查的區域。他們將保留乾燥教室，在社區大廳開會，避開封閉走道。計畫變小了，卻不再只是想像。

That afternoon, Maya led a movement session in the safe studio. Participants used medium-speed music, then chose to move faster or slower. One man who had lost muscle strength after a long illness sat down whenever he needed. A woman preferred to dance with only her hands. Nobody had to explain their body to earn a place. The room grew warm, and Maya began to sweat. She realized she had been holding her breath whenever someone moved differently from her original design. Her theory of one beautiful shared rhythm was beginning to weaken. In its place came many rhythms that could exist together.

當天下午，沐晴在安全教室帶領律動課。大家先用中速音樂練習，再自由選擇快慢。一名長期患病後肌力減弱的男子，需要時就坐下；另一名女子只想用雙手跳舞。沒有人必須解釋自己的身體才能參加。室內漸暖，沐晴開始流汗。她發現每當有人偏離她的原設計，她就會不自覺屏息。她原本相信一種完美共享節奏的理論，現在逐漸動搖，取而代之的是可以並存的許多節奏。

After the class, Evan waited outside with the public inspection note. He did not enter the rehearsal or judge the dance. Maya asked whether he would ever want to learn a movement with her after his city contract ended. "Probably badly," he said, smiling. "I build machines. They do not ask me to feel music." She told him that dancing did not require beautiful steps, only a willingness to notice. He promised to think about it. Then he passed her the report and returned to work. Their friendship was growing in the space between professional limits and personal curiosity.

課後，以衡帶著公開檢查紀錄在外頭等。他沒有進排練室，也沒有評價舞蹈。沐晴問他，等市府合約結束後，願不願跟她學一段動作。「大概會跳得很糟。」他笑著說，「我修的機器可不會要我感受音樂。」她說跳舞不需要漂亮舞步，只要願意留意身體。他答應考慮，接著把報告交給她，回去工作。兩人的友誼正在專業界線與個人好奇之間慢慢生長。

That night, Maya reviewed the inspector's chart and realized an intelligent decision did not have to be a fast one. The report described the wall, not the motives of the people who had worked there years before. She divided her questions into three groups: what had been measured, what still needed testing, and what the city had the power to approve. She asked Nia to check whether the summary was easy to read. Nia replaced several long sentences with short ones and added a drawing of the safe studio entrance. Neither woman claimed to know how soon swimming might return. They could tell residents exactly which classes were available now, and that was enough for a truthful announcement.

當晚，沐晴重看檢查圖表，明白聰明的決定不必做得很快。報告描述的是牆壁狀況，不是多年以前工作者的動機。她把問題分成三組：已測量的、仍待檢測的，以及市府有權核准的。她請倪雅確認摘要是否容易理解。倪雅把幾句長句改短，也加上安全教室入口的圖。他們都不宣稱知道何時能恢復游泳，卻能如實告訴居民現在有哪些課可上。對一則誠實公告而言，這就足夠了。
