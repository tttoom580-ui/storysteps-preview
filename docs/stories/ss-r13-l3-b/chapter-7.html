安全出口

The Safe Exit

讓水面記住我們的舞步 · 第 7 / 25 章 · 2000–3000 · 英中對照 · 校訂中

用互動版查詞、朗讀與收藏

An emergency alarm interrupted the rehearsal on Thursday. Nothing had caught fire; a worker had opened a cupboard beside a damaged cable and touched the wrong switch. The noise was awful, and the sudden darkness made the room feel awkward and small. Maya called for everyone to stop moving. A volunteer stood beside the old brick wall and guided people toward the marked exit. Nia checked whether those using mobility aids could pass. They could not: a heavy jar of tools blocked the turn. The team removed it only after the safety officer said the passage was clear enough to approach.

星期四，一聲緊急警報打斷排練。並沒有起火，而是一名工人打開損壞電纜旁的櫥櫃，誤碰開關。警報十分刺耳，突如其來的黑暗讓房間變得尷尬又狹小。沐晴請大家停止移動。志工站在舊磚牆旁，引導人們往標示出口走。倪雅檢查使用輔具者是否能通過，答案是否定的：一大罐工具擋在轉角。直到安全員確認附近可接近，團隊才移開障礙物。

Outside, a student held a dime he had found on the floor and joked that it was a lucky coin. Maya did not feel lucky. A warning should not have to frighten people before it was respected. The safety officer explained that an old alarm could explode into sound without indicating an actual explosion. He recorded the cause and asked the maintenance office to test the circuit. During the wait, Nia showed everyone a lily growing beside the gate. "Look at this," she said. "A pause can be useful. It gives us time to see what we usually rush past."

屋外，一名學生拿著在地板拾到的一角硬幣，開玩笑說那是幸運錢。沐晴卻不覺得幸運。警告不該非得先嚇到人才獲重視。安全員說明，舊警報可能突然爆出巨響，不代表真的有爆炸。他記錄原因，請維修單位檢測電路。等待時，倪雅指著門旁開出的百合。「看看這個，」她說，「暫停也有用，能讓我們看見平常匆匆錯過的事。」

A neighbor brought a small vase of flowers for the volunteers. In her kitchen, she said, she liked to invent a new flavor for soup whenever her family visited. Maya thanked her and promised that any future food event would label ingredients clearly. Another neighbor offered a box of junk left by the old club, including programs and ticket stubs. Maya accepted the papers but refused the broken electrical items. They had enough work already. Their wages came from a modest grant, and the team agreed to record every hour instead of expecting free labor from the people who cared most.

鄰居帶來一小花瓶花朵慰勞志工。她說，每當家人來訪，自己都喜歡在湯裡發明新口味。沐晴道謝，並答應未來若辦餐飲活動，會清楚標示食材。另一名鄰居送來一箱舊泳會留下的雜物，其中有節目單與票根。沐晴收下紙本，卻拒絕接收損壞電器。團隊已有太多事要做。大家的工資來自一筆小額補助，因此同意記錄每個工時，而非期待最在乎計畫的人免費勞動。

Later, Evan suggested a steady drill with only a few participants and an independent safety observer. It would not be a public performance. Maya thought that sounded terrific, although it meant another delay. "Remind me sometime that you can praise an idea," she teased. Evan said the idea was good precisely because they had not tried it before fixing the route. They traced the turn again and placed a clear sign where the jar had stood. Nia tested it from a seated position, then asked a second person to check the wording. The path finally worked for more than one kind of visitor.

後來以衡建議進行節奏穩定的小型演練，只邀少數參與者，並安排獨立安全觀察員，不作為公開演出。沐晴覺得這主意棒極了，雖然又得延期。「改天提醒我，你原來也會稱讚好點子。」她開玩笑說。以衡回答，這點子好，正是因為他們先修正動線才進行。大家重走轉角，在原本放工具罐的地方設立清楚標誌。倪雅坐著測試，再請第二個人確認文字。這條路終於適合不只一種訪客。

At the end of the drill, Maya expected a lesson about wires. Instead, Nia asked why people assumed the safest person should always lead. "Sometimes someone at the back sees the problem first," she said. Maya remembered how she had stopped the group by shouting from the center of the floor. Next time, she would give every participant a way to signal a concern. They agreed that anyone could stop a rehearsal without explaining themselves immediately. When the lights returned, the room seemed ordinary again. Yet the rules of the project had changed, and Maya thought that change was worth more than a perfect dance.

演練結束時，沐晴以為會得到一堂電線課；倪雅卻問，為何大家總以為最安全的人應站在前方帶領。「有時最後面的人才先看見問題。」她說。沐晴想起自己在場地中央大喊停止，下次她要讓每位參與者都能發出疑慮訊號。團隊同意，任何人都可以暫停排練，無須立刻解釋。燈光恢復後，房間看來又很普通，但計畫規則已改變。沐晴覺得這比一支完美舞蹈還有價值。

Maya began to see why a written public appeal could do more than a speech. She prepared a short letter asking the city to repair the exit, not merely to decorate it. The letter described the danger to flesh and bone if a crowded group were pushed toward an obstacle. It also asked for a fair wage for the workers who would move equipment and guide visitors. Nia edited the draft with her, refusing words that made disabled people sound helpless. When the letter was ready, Maya signed it as a project organizer and invited others to add their names only if they agreed with every request. A safer route was everyone's concern, not a private favor.

沐晴開始懂得，書面的公共呼籲有時比演說更有效。她寫了一封短信，要求市府修復出口，而不只是加上裝飾。信中說明，人潮被迫擠向障礙物時，可能傷及身體；也要求給予搬運器材與引導訪客的工作者公平工資。倪雅與她一起修訂，刪掉把身心障礙者寫得無助的詞句。信完成後，沐晴以主辦者身分簽名，只邀請認同全部訴求的人連署。安全路線是共同關切，不是私人恩惠。
