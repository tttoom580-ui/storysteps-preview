不存在的聲音

A Voice That Wasn't There

讓水面記住我們的舞步 · 第 5 / 25 章 · 2000–3000 · 英中對照 · 校訂中

用互動版查詞、朗讀與收藏

The first dry-deck rehearsal smelled of old carpet and fresh steam from a tea pot. A volunteer had cooked bacon and brought ripe tomatoes for the early crew. Maya entered the garage beside the pool and found a sack of spare lights, a flashlight, and a small motor on a table. She had planned to use a microphone so people could hear instructions from different seats. Instead, a cough came through the speaker before anyone spoke. It sounded like a warning from an empty room. Maya felt her jaw tighten. She turned the speaker off and asked everyone to wait.

第一次乾燥池畔排練時，空氣裡混著舊地毯與茶壺新冒出的蒸氣味。志工煎了培根，帶來熟透的番茄給早班工作者。沐晴走進泳池旁的車庫，看見桌上放著一袋備用燈具、手電筒和一顆小馬達。她原本計畫用麥克風，讓不同座位的人都能聽見指示；不料沒有人開口，喇叭就傳出咳嗽聲，像空房間發出的警告。她咬緊牙關，關掉喇叭，請大家先等一等。

A technician discovered that an old training recording was still connected to the sound system. The voice was not a ghost or a fake person standing behind the wall. It was a test message from years ago. Maya asked him to disconnect the recording and explain the controls to two witnesses. The interruption had caused considerable worry, but no one was hurt. Nia said that a clear announcement mattered more than a perfect rehearsal. "People have the right to know what happened," she reminded Maya. They wrote an incident note and moved the next practice outside, where everyone could hear without the old equipment.

技術員發現舊訓練錄音仍連接著音響系統。聲音不是鬼，也不是假裝有人躲在牆後，而是多年前的測試訊息。沐晴請他移除連接，並向兩位見證者說明控制方式。中斷讓大家相當擔心，但沒有人受傷。倪雅說，清楚告知比完美排練更重要。「人們有權知道發生了什麼。」她提醒沐晴。大家寫下事件紀錄，把下一段練習移到戶外，不再使用舊音響也能聽清楚指示。

During the break, a volunteer began to complain that the project was moving too slowly. Another person passed around a bag of peanut snacks and asked whether the delay would affect their jobs. Maya answered that it might, which was why the team needed a realistic schedule. She spoke carefully, trying to pronounce each instruction clearly, including the difference between an invitation and a promise. To engage the community honestly, they had to explain which activities were actually ready. The community's engagement depended on that truth. She would not announce a water class until the city had approved it. The voice in the loudspeaker had taught her an uncomfortable lesson about responsibility.

休息時，一名志工開始抱怨進度太慢。另一人傳著一袋花生點心，問延期是否會影響工作。沐晴回答有可能，因此團隊需要務實時程。她說話時仔細咬字，努力把每項指示發音清楚，包括「邀請」與「承諾」的差別。要誠實地邀請社區參與，他們必須說明哪些活動真的準備好了。社區參與建立在這份事實上。市府核准之前，她不會宣布水中課程。那道從喇叭傳出的聲音，教了她一堂令人不舒服的責任課。

At the end of the day, Maya and Evan walked through the empty foyer. A faded notice described a violent storm that had damaged the building years ago. Beneath it was a handwritten message from a former coach, expressing sorrow for the loss of the old swim club. Evan pointed to the date. The building had closed much later, after several different repair delays. It would be wrong to blame one storm for every problem. Maya photographed the notice and asked for the inspection timeline. It was easier to tell a dramatic story than a true one, but she was trying to learn the harder skill.

一天結束時，沐晴與以衡穿過空蕩的大廳。褪色告示記錄多年前一場猛烈暴風雨曾損壞建築。下方一位前教練留下手寫訊息，表達舊泳會消失的悲傷。以衡指著日期：建築是在更久以後、經歷好幾次維修延誤才關閉的。把每個問題都歸咎於同一場風暴並不正確。沐晴拍下告示，申請檢查時間表。講戲劇化的故事比講真實故事容易，但她正努力學習比較難的能力。

When everyone was gone, Nia invited Maya to sit on the dry tiles and listen to the room without a speaker. They heard a bus outside, a door closing, and footsteps moving at different speeds. "That could be music," Nia said. They tested a short piece with hand taps and ordinary breathing, leaving space for dancers who preferred silence. Maya had been worried about losing the grand sound she once imagined. Now she could hear rhythm without machinery. She wrote a new rehearsal plan: no recordings without agreement, no equipment without a check, and no one reduced to a decoration in somebody else's show.

所有人離開後，倪雅邀沐晴坐在乾燥磁磚上，不透過喇叭聆聽空間。她們聽見外面的公車、關門聲，以及不同速度的腳步聲。「這也可以是音樂。」倪雅說。她們用手掌輕拍與自然呼吸試做一小段，也替偏好安靜的舞者留下空間。沐晴原本擔心失去想像中的盛大音效，現在卻聽見不靠機器的節奏。她寫下新排練規範：未經同意不錄音，未經檢查不使用器材，也不讓任何人淪為他人表演中的裝飾。

At home, Maya read the messages from her class members. Several thanked her for stopping when the speaker failed. One asked whether the project would survive so many careful delays. She did not know. She replied that a public project should be able to tell the truth about its own limits. Before sleeping, she looked at a picture of herself as a teenage dancer in the old pool. The younger Maya seemed to expect every dream to come true if she worked hard enough. The older Maya had learned that other people carried dreams too. She closed the phone, already thinking about the next meeting.

回到家，沐晴讀著學員傳來的訊息。好幾人感謝她在音響故障時停止活動，有人問這個計畫能否經得起如此多次謹慎延期。她不知道答案。她回覆，公共計畫應該能誠實說出自身限制。睡前，她看著一張年少時在舊泳池跳舞的照片。照片裡的沐晴似乎相信，只要足夠努力，所有夢想都會成真。如今她懂得，其他人也各自背負夢想。她關上手機，心裡已在想下一場會議。
