水質檢測

The Water Test

讓水面記住我們的舞步 · 第 6 / 25 章 · 2000–3000 · 英中對照 · 校訂中

"Anyhow, we cannot assume the water will be clean," Evan told the planning team. He had received the first test report. Some pipes contained bacteria, and a section of the old wall had developed a soft bump near a drain. The city would need to examine the system before considering any water use. Maya looked at the empty pool and thought about how quickly a happy idea could become a problem when details were ignored. "Then we stay on dry ground," she said. "A creator should be able to change the form of a work without forgetting its purpose." Nia approved the decision.

「無論如何，我們不能假設水會是乾淨的。」以衡告訴規劃團隊。他已收到第一份檢測報告。有些管線檢出細菌，舊牆面靠近排水口處也鼓起一塊軟軟的凸處。在考慮任何水中使用之前，市府必須檢查整套系統。沐晴望著空泳池，想著忽略細節時，一個快樂的點子能多快變成問題。「那就留在乾地板上。」她說，「創作者應該能改變作品的形式，卻不忘記它的目的。」倪雅贊同這決定。

The inspection report had a summary with three concerns: water quality, an aging valve, and a missing maintenance date. There was no mention of a missile, although a rumor online claimed a military object had struck the pool years ago. A local politician had repeated that story in a short video. Evan refused to debate it without evidence. He placed photographs of the ordinary pipes beside the report and explained what each part did. "The most useful technique is to ask what we actually know," he said. Maya wrote that sentence where every volunteer could see it.

檢查報告摘要列出三項疑慮：水質、老舊閥門及缺失的保養日期。報告並未提到飛彈，網路上卻流傳多年前有軍事物體擊中泳池的謠言；一位地方政治人物甚至在短片裡重述。以衡不願在缺乏證據時爭辯。他把一般管線照片放在報告旁，逐一解釋功能。「最有用的技巧，就是問我們真正知道什麼。」他說。沐晴把這句話寫在志工都看得到的地方。

At lunch, Nia found that some bread had gone stale. Maya's stomach complained loudly as she searched for fresh food. A teenage volunteer laughed and offered fruit instead. The group planned to multiply the number of free workshops only when enough instructors were trained. They discussed the necessity of rest periods and quiet rooms, not only the number of seats. A volunteer suggested using tropical plants in the entrance hall, but another reminded everyone that plants needed care and could not block a path. The smallest choice seemed to carry another person's needs inside it.

午餐時，倪雅發現部分麵包已經不新鮮。沐晴找新食物時，肚子大聲抗議。一名青少年志工笑著拿水果給她。團隊決定，只有在合格帶領者足夠時，才增加免費工作坊的場次。大家討論休息時段與安靜房間的必要性，而非只計算座位。有志工建議入口擺熱帶植物，另一人提醒植物需要照顧，也不能阻擋動線。最小的選擇似乎都藏著別人的需要。

Maya wanted the project to show excellence, but she was learning that excellence could mean a careful refusal. She had imagined a bright inner courtyard filled with dancers; for now, that space would remain closed. A volunteer offered to clean the old signs using wax, but the historic labels required permission from the museum office. They decided to photograph the signs and request advice first. Nobody liked delay. Still, when Maya looked around the group, she saw people taking the shared plan seriously. It was not the easiest kind of progress, but it was the kind they could defend.

沐晴希望計畫展現卓越，現在卻學到，卓越有時代表審慎拒絕。她原本想像明亮的內院滿是舞者，目前那裡仍須封閉。有志工願意用蠟清理舊告示，但歷史標誌須先取得館務單位同意。大家決定先拍照並尋求建議。沒有人喜歡延誤，但沐晴環顧團隊，看見大家都認真對待共同計畫。這不是最輕鬆的進展，卻是能站得住腳的進展。

Before the meeting ended, Evan presented a resource chart. He marked what the city owned, what contractors would repair, and what volunteers could safely do. It was easy to suspect that every missing record concealed a secret; it was harder to find the correct document and ask a fair question. Maya asked him to explain the difference between a repair request and an approval. He did, slowly, with no impatience. When the others left, she said that his caution sometimes made her angry. Evan answered that her imagination sometimes made him impatient, too. They smiled because both could admit a limit without ending the partnership.

散會前，以衡展示資源表，標明市府擁有什麼、承包商應修什麼，以及志工能安全完成什麼。懷疑每一份缺失紀錄都藏著祕密很容易，找到正確文件並提出公平問題卻難得多。沐晴請他慢慢解釋維修申請與正式核准的差別，他沒有不耐煩。其他人離開後，她坦白他的謹慎有時讓自己生氣。以衡也承認她的想像有時使他沒耐性。兩人都能承認自己的限制，又不必因此終止合作，於是一起笑了。

At the exit, Ms. Luo brought a small paper model of the pool that she had kept for years. It showed the old shallow steps and a handrail no longer present. Maya recognized why her first drawing had been incomplete: she had measured the doors but not the changes that time had made inside the building. Nia proposed a walk-through with people who used different kinds of support. The city would have to approve the visitor route, yet the team could prepare questions now. Evan set a date for the next public report. For the first time that day, Maya felt the distance between wanting something and preparing it had become possible to cross.

離開時，羅女士帶來保存多年的泳池紙模型。模型有舊淺階與如今已不存在的扶手。沐晴明白初稿為何不完整：她量了門，卻未量時間在建築內留下的改變。倪雅提議邀不同支持需求的人一起走訪。訪客動線仍須市府核准，但團隊現在就能準備問題。以衡訂下下次公開報告的日期。那天她第一次覺得，想要一件事與真正準備好做它之間的距離，是可以跨越的。
