空泳池裡的第一步

The Empty Pool

讓水面記住我們的舞步 · 第 1 / 25 章 · 2000–3000 · 英中對照 · 校訂中

用互動版查詞、朗讀與收藏

The Southbank Pool had been empty for six years. Maya Lin stood at its edge with a berry drink in her hand, looking down at the faded blue tiles. As a girl, she had performed in a small circus show here, dressed as a fairy who could make water dance. The show was not fancy, but the applause had stayed in her memory. Now the city planned to sell the building to a private fitness company. Maya, thirty, had returned to teach movement classes. She was not ready to watch a public place disappear without asking who still needed it.

南岸游泳館已空置六年。林沐晴手裡拿著一杯莓果飲料，站在池邊望著褪色的藍色磁磚。她小時候曾在這裡參加一場小型馬戲表演，扮成能讓水起舞的仙子。表演談不上華麗，掌聲卻一直留在她的記憶裡。如今市府準備把建築賣給私人健身公司。三十歲的沐晴回來教授律動課，她還不願看著公共場所消失，卻沒有人問過誰仍需要它。

By the entrance, a handwritten sign announced a meeting about the building's deed. A broken heater leaned against the wall, beside a painted wooden hut once used for children's games. A jazz record lay in its open sleeve. A handwritten price list offered a pint of lemonade for the summer crowd. A poster showed a magician making a glass of water seem to spin. None of these objects was a miracle. They were ordinary signs that the pool had belonged to many different kinds of people. Maya photographed the poster, then put the record back where she found it. She wanted to preserve the place, not rob it of its past.

入口旁的手寫告示宣布將召開建築產權契據的討論會。一台壞掉的暖爐靠著牆，旁邊是過去供孩子遊戲的彩繪木屋。打開的唱片套裡放著一張爵士樂唱片。手寫價目表寫著，夏天可買到一品脫檸檬水。一張海報畫著魔術師讓杯中的水看似旋轉。這些都不是奇蹟，只是證明游泳館曾屬於各種不同的人。沐晴拍下海報，又把唱片放回原處。她想保存這個地方，而不是奪走它的過去。

A water jet above the shallow end suddenly gave a weak cough, although the pool had been drained. Evan Zhao, a thirty-three-year-old engineer invited by the city to make an initial inspection, found a loose screw near the old valve. He did not touch the control. He placed a red marker beside it and called the facility manager. "A strange noise is not a signal that the system is safe," he said. His calm manner helped Maya tame her excitement. She could imagine a performance already, but no one would enter the water until an independent safety review was complete.

淺水區上方的噴水口突然發出微弱的咳聲，雖然池水早已排空。受市府邀請進行初步檢查的三十三歲工程師趙以衡，在舊閥門附近發現一顆鬆動的螺絲。他沒有碰控制器，而是在旁邊放上紅色標記，並聯絡設施管理人。「奇怪的聲音不代表系統安全。」他說。他平靜的態度讓沐晴壓下興奮。她腦中已浮現表演畫面，但在獨立安全審查完成前，誰都不能下水。

At the meeting, the deputy mayor described the sale as an easy financial choice. Maya stood up and asked whether it was also a political choice. Residents had paid taxes for the pool and had used it for various activities, including school lessons and quiet exercise. If the city could not afford to reopen it for swimming, perhaps it could support dry-deck classes, art, and supervised future programs. "I won't quit because the first answer is no," Maya told the room. She proposed a three-month study, with public records and a clear rule: no promise of water use before safety approval.

會議上，副市長把出售案描述成簡單的財務選擇。沐晴起身問道，這是否也是一項政治決定。居民納稅維護泳池，也曾在此進行學校課程、溫和運動等各種活動。若市府負擔不起恢復游泳用途，或許仍可支持乾燥池畔的課程、藝術活動及日後受監督的計畫。「我不會因為第一個答案是拒絕就放棄。」她告訴大家。她提出三個月研究期，要求公開紀錄，也訂下一條明確規則：安全核准前，不能承諾使用水池。

After the crowd left, Evan showed Maya a page listing problems from the previous inspection. Someone had written "closed" beside each problem but had not recorded the repair. That was not proof of a crime, he explained; it was a reason to ask for the original work orders. Maya liked the difference. Her work as a dancer relied on trust between partners, and trust did not mean pretending every floor could hold every movement. She took a clean notebook from her bag and wrote a simple title: "Who can dance here, and what must change first?" The question felt larger than the old pool.

人群散去後，以衡拿給沐晴看上一回檢查的問題清單。有人在每個問題旁寫上「已結案」，卻沒有留下維修紀錄。他解釋，這不是犯罪的證據，而是應該索取原始工單的理由。沐晴欣賞這個區別。舞者之間的合作依賴信任，但信任不代表假裝每塊地板都能承受所有動作。她從包裡拿出乾淨筆記本，寫下標題：「誰能在這裡跳舞？我們得先改變什麼？」這問題似乎比舊泳池還大。

Outside, the evening buses drove past the gate. A father asked whether his daughter, who used a wheelchair, could join a movement class. Maya nearly answered yes at once. Then she saw the narrow doorway behind him. "I want her here," Maya said, "but I need to measure the entrances and ask what she likes to do." The father smiled at the second part. Evan offered to bring a measuring tape, not a promise. They agreed to meet again with the daughter, Nia, who was an adult artist herself. Maya walked home holding a plan, not a victory, and that felt like an honest beginning.

門外，傍晚的公車駛過大門。一位父親問，使用輪椅的女兒能否參加律動課。沐晴差點立刻答應，接著卻看到他身後狹窄的門口。「我希望她能來，」沐晴說，「但我必須先測量入口，也問她喜歡做什麼。」父親聽到後半句笑了。以衡答應帶捲尺來，沒有作出保證。他們約好再與女兒倪雅見面；她本身也是一名成年藝術家。沐晴帶著計畫而非勝利走回家，卻覺得這是誠實的開始。
