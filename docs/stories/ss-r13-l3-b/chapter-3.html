Who Gets to Speak?

讓水面記住我們的舞步 · 第 3 / 25 章 · 2000–3000 · 英中對照 · 校訂中

用互動版查詞、朗讀與收藏

Maya organized a listening table downtown, beside a bamboo screen outside a small café. She did not want the speakers' background to decide whose ideas mattered. A shopkeeper arrived with a bundle of old swimming tickets; a street food dealer brought a chair. Two teenagers stopped to chat while sharing a doughnut. A pigeon landed on the empty table and stole a crumb. The funny moment broke the tension. Then an older woman unfolded a picture of the pool from fifty years ago. "We didn't only swim," she said. "We learned to trust one another there." Maya wrote the words down without changing them.

沐晴在市中心一家小咖啡館外的竹屏風旁設置聆聽桌。她不希望發言者的背景決定意見是否重要。一位店主帶來一捆舊游泳票，街頭食品攤商則搬來椅子。兩名少年一邊分甜甜圈一邊聊天。鴿子飛到空桌上偷走麵包屑，這滑稽的一幕緩和了緊張。接著一位年長婦女打開五十年前的泳池照片。「我們不只是在那裡游泳，」她說，「還學著互相信任。」沐晴原樣記下這些話。

A student studying civil engineering asked to inspect the old floor plans. Another person wanted a democratic poll, but Nia warned that a simple majority could not make an unsafe entrance acceptable. Maya agreed. The public could vote on the purpose of the program, not on whether safety standards should be ignored. A volunteer used a small chip of blue tile to show how the wall had worn away. "We need to deepen the discussion," Nia said, "beyond whether people miss the place." She invited visitors to describe what they would need to take part, and what they would refuse.

一名土木工程學生希望查看舊樓面圖，另一個人提議進行民主投票。倪雅提醒大家，簡單多數決不能使不安全的入口變得可以接受。沐晴同意，公眾可以決定計畫的用途，卻不能投票決定忽略安全標準。一名志工拿著一小片藍磁磚，說明牆面如何磨損。「我們需要把討論深化，」倪雅說，「不能只問大家是否懷念這地方。」她邀請訪客描述參與所需的條件，以及不願接受的安排。

At noon, Maya invited the helpers to dine together at a modest restaurant. Professor Wu drew a rough map on a napkin. He marked neighborhoods where poverty made paid classes difficult to attend. A nurse suggested family sessions during hours when shift workers were awake. A parent asked for a quiet room, not a loud party. Every suggestion changed the project slightly. At first Maya did not know how to react; making a leap from one big stage show to dozens of small needs would require patience. Maya had once pictured a mighty dance performance filling the empty pool with sound. Now she could see a better possibility: a place where people might move, watch, rest, or simply belong without buying a membership.

中午，沐晴請幫忙的人到平價餐館一起吃飯。吳教授在餐巾紙上畫出簡單地圖，圈出因貧困而難以負擔付費課程的街區。護理師建議安排適合輪班工作者清醒時間的家庭課程。家長則希望有安靜房間，而不是吵鬧派對。每項建議都讓計畫稍微改變。沐晴一開始不知如何反應；要從一場大型舞台演出跨越到照顧數十種小需求，需要耐心。沐晴原先想像一場氣勢強大的舞蹈演出，讓空池充滿聲音；現在她看到更好的可能：人們不用付會員費，也可以活動、觀賞、休息，或只是自在地待著。

The city offered to help run a poll, but the forms included questions about income and health that were not needed for the project. Maya crossed them out. She asked only which activities people wanted and what access problems they had observed. Some residents reacted with surprise. "Aren't you going to collect everything for the sake of good research?" one asked. "No," Maya answered. "We should know why we ask before we ask." Nia nodded and suggested a separate box for anonymous concerns. By evening, the table held seventy-two useful responses and no names that the organizers had not needed.

市府提出協助民調，但表格包括與計畫無關的收入與健康問題。沐晴把它們劃掉，只問人們想參與哪些活動，以及觀察到哪些無障礙問題。有些居民很驚訝。「難道你不想為了做好研究，把資料全部收起來？」有人問。「不，」沐晴回答，「提問之前要先知道為什麼問。」倪雅點頭，建議另設匿名意見箱。傍晚時，桌上收集到七十二份有用回覆，卻沒有多留任何不必要的姓名。

Evan joined the group after work and studied the answers in silence. He found two complaints about a chemical smell near a storage room. Neither message proved what the substance was; both suggested a place to check. He drew a line around that section on the plan and reminded Maya not to invite visitors there. "You always make the room smaller," she joked. "I am trying to leave us a room we can use," he replied. She laughed, then stopped. It was the first time she had understood his caution as a form of care rather than a lack of imagination.

以衡下班後加入大家，默默閱讀意見。他找到兩則抱怨儲藏室附近有化學氣味的訊息。兩則都不能證明那是什麼物質，卻指出應檢查的位置。他在平面圖上圈出那一區，提醒沐晴不要帶訪客過去。「你總把空間越畫越小。」她開玩笑說。「我是想替我們留下真正能使用的空間。」他回答。沐晴笑了，隨後沉默。她第一次明白，他的謹慎不是缺乏想像力，而是一種關心。

Before packing up, Maya asked everyone to choose one action they could take next week. The student would compare plans. The nurse would ask colleagues about suitable times. A shopkeeper would lend folding chairs. Nia would write an access checklist that others could criticize. Maya would publish the results, including objections, without pretending that public agreement had already been reached. She closed the notebook feeling tired and strangely hopeful. The pool had not reopened. Yet people who had never shared a table before had begun to make decisions together, and that was real progress.

收攤前，沐晴請每個人選一件下週能完成的事。學生負責比對圖面；護理師向同事詢問適合的時間；店主借出摺疊椅；倪雅編寫一份可讓大家批評的無障礙檢查表。沐晴則負責公開所有結果，包括反對意見，不假裝大家已經達成共識。她合上筆記本，疲累中帶著奇異的希望。泳池還沒有開放，但從前不曾同桌的人已開始共同做決定，這就是真正的進展。
