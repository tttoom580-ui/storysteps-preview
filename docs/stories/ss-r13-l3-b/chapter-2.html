有條件的投票

A Vote With Conditions

讓水面記住我們的舞步 · 第 2 / 25 章 · 2000–3000 · 英中對照 · 校訂中

The next morning, Maya entered the community center behind a man wearing a bright necktie. The man, Professor Wu, taught local geography and had worked on a map of public buildings for a decade. He had come to discuss the pool, not to judge her dancing. He showed her how the old bus route had changed. One passenger now needed two connections to reach the center. Maya had called the place convenient because she lived nearby. Seeing the map, she began to regret the word. A useful public project had to work for people who did not share her journey.

隔天早上，沐晴跟著一位繫著亮色領帶的男子走進社區中心。這位吳教授教授地方地理，並已研究公共建築地圖十年。他來討論泳池，而不是評價她的舞蹈。他指出舊公車路線如何改變，如今有位乘客必須轉兩次車才能到社區中心。沐晴先前因為住得近，就說這裡很方便。看過地圖後，她開始後悔這個說法。真正有用的公共計畫，必須照顧行程不同於她的人。

The city committee had a small budget. The chair wanted a visible result before the next semester ended. Another voter asked whether money should go to repairs or to a new bowling center. A third member said a public pool could become a hungry beast that swallowed funds. Maya understood the concern. She had once run a dance class without a proper kit and had spent more than she earned. She showed the committee two plans: one for free dry-floor workshops, and another, only possible after inspection, for water movement classes. She refused to mix their costs or hide the risk.

市府委員會的預算很少。主席希望在下學期結束前看到明顯成果。另一位投票委員問，經費應用於維修，還是新的保齡球中心。第三位委員說，公立泳池可能變成吞噬經費的飢餓野獸。沐晴理解這種顧慮。她曾在器材不足的情況下開舞蹈班，結果支出大於收入。她提出兩個方案：一個是免費的乾地板工作坊，另一個必須先通過檢查，才可能開設水中律動課。她不願混淆成本，也不掩飾風險。

Nia Chen arrived with a small bag and a strip of white lace tied to its handle. The lace helped her recognize the bag among similar ones. As a performer, Nia was wonderfully expressive, but she disliked being invited only to "inspire" an audience. "I can help design the work," she said. "I don't want to be your symbol." Maya felt awkward and thanked her for being clear. A council member asked whether the entrance could frighten visitors using mobility aids. Nia answered that steps were a barrier, not a reason to fear the building. She asked for measurements and a real permit process.

倪雅帶著小包走進來，提把上繫著一條白色蕾絲，方便她從相似的包中辨認自己的東西。身為表演者，她的表達能力十分豐富，但她不喜歡被邀請去「激勵」觀眾，卻無法參與決策。「我可以一起設計作品，」她說，「我不想當你的象徵。」沐晴有些尷尬，也感謝她說得明白。有位委員問，入口是否會嚇到使用輔具的訪客。倪雅回答，階梯是障礙，不是害怕建築的理由；她要求實際尺寸與正式許可程序。

During the break, a man who smelled of beer pushed through the door. He was not drunk, but he looked tired and angry. He accused the committee of being greedy and said the private company would never listen to ordinary people. Maya agreed that the public deserved answers, yet she would not turn uncertainty into an accusation. She asked the chair to publish the proposal and give residents time to read it. Professor Wu added that transport, access, staffing, and repairs must be considered together. The committee returned to its seats with more questions than it had expected.

休息時，一名身上有啤酒氣味的男子推門進來。他並未喝醉，卻顯得疲累而憤怒。他指責委員會貪婪，認為私人公司絕不會聽普通人的聲音。沐晴同意居民有權得到答案，但她不願把不確定的事說成確定的指控。她請主席公開提案，讓居民有時間閱讀。吳教授補充，交通、無障礙、工作人員與維修必須一起考量。委員們帶著比原先更多的問題回到座位。

The vote was not a simple yes or no. Three members supported a short study; two opposed it. The city would permit supervised visits and dry-deck rehearsals, but no one could fill the pool or sell tickets to water activities. It was a narrow chance. Evan handed Maya a list of documents to request. Nia asked for the entrance survey to be public, including failures. "If we are honest about what doesn't work," she said, "we can choose what to change." Maya had planned to celebrate. Instead, she bought tea for everyone and opened a new page in the project notebook.

投票結果不是簡單的同意或反對。三名委員支持短期研究，兩名反對。市府准許受監督的參觀與乾燥池畔排練，但任何人都不能注水，也不能販售水中活動門票。這是一個有限的機會。以衡交給沐晴一份應申請的文件清單。倪雅要求入口調查公開，連失敗的地方都要列出。「只有誠實面對不能用的地方，才知道該改什麼。」她說。沐晴原本打算慶祝，最後只是請大家喝茶，並在計畫筆記本翻開新的一頁。

That night, Maya listened to the recording of the meeting. She heard herself interrupt Nia twice, each time in an attempt to be helpful. She sent a message apologizing and asked whether Nia wanted a shared leadership role. The reply came quickly: "Yes, with decision rights and pay." Maya wrote those words on a card. She knew that a good idea could become harmful if the people affected by it had no power. The budget would be tight, but she would rather begin with a small honest team than a large project that used people as decoration.

當晚，沐晴重聽會議錄音，聽見自己曾兩度打斷倪雅，每次都是想幫忙。她傳訊息道歉，並詢問倪雅是否願意共同領導。回覆很快來了：「願意，但要有決策權，也要有薪酬。」沐晴把這句話寫在卡片上。她明白，好點子若不讓受影響者擁有權力，也可能造成傷害。預算會很緊，但她寧願從一個誠實的小團隊開始，也不想把人當裝飾來支撐一個龐大的計畫。
