名字的價格

The Price of a Name

讓水面記住我們的舞步 · 第 10 / 25 章 · 2000–3000 · 英中對照 · 校訂中

用互動版查詞、朗讀與收藏

The SilverWave company offered to pay for the first season of programs. Its representative arrived in a fashionable coat and spoke in an optimistic voice. She carried a silk folder with a pearl-colored logo. The proposal would cover equipment, cleaning, and part of the staff wages. Maya was impressed by the bold numbers, but she stared at one line: SilverWave would have the final right to determine the program name. Another line gave the company control over photographs. Maya asked for time to read everything. A generous offer could still ask for more power than the community wished to give.

銀浪公司提出資助第一季活動。代表穿著時髦外套，語氣樂觀，帶來印著珍珠色標誌的絲質資料夾。提案願意負擔設備、清潔與部分員工工資。沐晴對大膽的金額印象深刻，卻盯著一行字：銀浪有權最終決定活動名稱。另一條款還賦予公司照片使用控制權。沐晴要求時間仔細閱讀。慷慨的提議，仍可能要求比社區願意交出的更多權力。

On the way home, Maya passed a wedding shop where a bride was trying on a dress. A flame from the baker's stove next door warmed the evening window. She bought a slice of melon and used her handkerchief to wipe juice from her hands. These ordinary moments made the contract feel even stranger. The program was supposed to offer people choices about movement and belonging; why should a company choose the name by which everyone would know them? She stopped by the river, where the tide moved steadily against the steps, and wrote three questions to ask at the next meeting.

回家路上，沐晴經過婚紗店，看見新娘正在試穿禮服。隔壁麵包店的爐火讓晚間櫥窗暖暖的。她買了一片甜瓜，用手帕擦掉手上的果汁。這些平凡時刻讓合約顯得更加奇怪。計畫原本要讓人們能選擇如何活動、如何歸屬，為何公司卻能決定大家被稱呼的名字？她在河邊停下，望著潮水穩定拍打階梯，寫下下次會議要問的三個問題。

Nia read the proposal the next morning. "The moral question is not whether money is evil," she said. "It is whether the people who use the place can still say no." She drew the structure of decision-making on a whiteboard: city ownership, independent safety approval, community program council, and separate funders. "Somehow these lines became blurred in the first draft," she said. Maya agreed to request a revision. The sponsor could support public activity without buying editorial control. They would explain their reasons to SilverWave rather than attack the company as a villain.

隔天早上，倪雅讀了提案。「道德問題不是金錢是否邪惡，」她說，「而是使用這裡的人還能不能說不。」她在白板上畫出決策架構：市府產權、獨立安全核准、社區活動委員會，以及分開的資助者。「不知道為什麼，初稿把這些界線弄模糊了。」她說。沐晴同意請求修訂。贊助商可以支持公共活動，卻不必買下編輯權。她們會向銀浪解釋理由，而不是把公司直接描繪成壞人。

The company's representative listened and gave a small sigh of relief. She said she could remove the photography condition but needed approval from her directors for the name. The conversation was not a battle won with a punch; it was a difficult negotiation. Maya asked the representative to put the change in writing. Nia offered to review the new wording with affected participants. Evan, whose role concerned the building, stayed out of the discussion. His restraint mattered. Maya was learning that support could mean giving her room to negotiate without someone else stepping forward to rescue her.

公司代表聽完後輕輕鬆了口氣。她說可以刪掉照片條款，但活動名稱仍須董事核准。這不是用拳頭打贏的戰鬥，而是一場艱難協商。沐晴請代表把變更寫成文件，倪雅則提出要和受影響的參與者一起審閱文字。以衡的工作涉及建築，便沒有介入討論。他的克制很重要。沐晴正學著理解，支持有時是留給她談判的空間，而非搶著上前拯救。

That night, Maya felt relief, although the answer was not final. She called Nia and asked whether she had done enough. "Enough for today," Nia replied. "Tomorrow we keep asking." Maya looked at the old pool plan spread across her desk. Its lines showed fixed lanes for swimmers, each one exactly the same width. Her new work would not be like that. Some people wanted a dance; others wanted a place to rest, move gently, or watch. The whole idea was to offer different paths without calling any one path a failure. She went to sleep with the contract still open.

當晚，雖然還沒有最後答案，沐晴仍感到鬆一口氣。她打電話問倪雅，今天做得是否足夠。「今天夠了，」倪雅回答，「明天繼續提問。」沐晴望著攤在桌上的舊泳池平面圖。圖上泳道固定，每一道都一樣寬。她的新作品不會如此整齊。有些人想跳舞，另一些人想休息、輕柔移動，或只是觀看。整個計畫就是提供不同道路，而不把任何一條稱作失敗。她帶著仍攤開的合約入睡。

On the following afternoon, Maya met a volunteer whose grandmother had painted an angel on the pool wall decades earlier. SilverWave had offered to cover the painting with its logo. The volunteer did not want to stop all repairs; she only asked for a public discussion before the wall changed. Maya promised to include that request in the next contract meeting. She did not want to impress the company with a perfect answer. Nor did she want to stare at every offer as if it hid a trap. She asked for written conditions, allowed time for review, and kept the old painting in the plans. That was a small decision, but it made the project feel more honest.

隔天下午，沐晴遇到一位志工，志工的祖母數十年前曾在泳池牆上畫天使。銀浪提議用公司標誌遮蓋畫作。志工並非反對所有維修，只希望牆面改變之前先公開討論。沐晴答應在下次合約會議提出。她不想靠完美答案讓公司留下印象，也不想把每個提議都當成陷阱死盯不放。她索取書面條件、留下審閱時間，並在圖面上保留舊畫。這個決定很小，卻讓計畫更誠實。
