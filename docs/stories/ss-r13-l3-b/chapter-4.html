巷子後的門

The Door Behind the Alley

讓水面記住我們的舞步 · 第 4 / 25 章 · 2000–3000 · 英中對照 · 校訂中

用互動版查詞、朗讀與收藏

An old alley ran behind the Southbank Pool. Evan brought a long cable and a small camera to examine the service passage without entering it. A repair van stood beside a tower that had once held the diving clock. Maya watched from the marked safe line. The passage looked solid in the morning light, but a dark stain reached halfway up the wall. "Water came through here," Evan said. He did not call it proof of recent flooding; a stain could be much older. He photographed its height, noted the warmth of the air, and asked for the records of the last storm.

南岸游泳館後方有條老巷。以衡帶著長電纜與小型攝影機，準備在不進入通道的情況下檢查維修走道。一輛修理廂型車停在昔日跳水計時鐘塔旁。沐晴站在安全標線之外觀看。走道在晨光中看似堅固，牆上的深色水痕卻高達半面牆。「水曾從這裡進來。」以衡說。他沒有斷定那是最近淹水的證據，因為水痕可能早已存在。他拍下高度、記錄空氣的溫度，並索取上一場暴雨的紀錄。

In the storage office, Ms. Luo, a retired swim coach, found a paper label tied to a box of emergency supplies. Inside lay a cherry-colored ribbon, a pair of needles used to knit winter scarves, and a cracked saucer. The objects belonged to the old swimming club, not the emergency kit. Maya wanted to laugh, but Ms. Luo looked serious. "People left in a hurry after the crash," she said. She meant a heavy pipe that had fallen years ago, not a vehicle accident. No one had been killed, but some rumors had grown into stories of murder. Maya wrote down the exact definition of the event before those stories spread again.

在儲藏辦公室，退休游泳教練羅女士找到一只貼著紙標籤的緊急物資箱。裡頭有櫻桃色緞帶、一雙用來編織冬季圍巾的針和裂掉的茶碟，都是舊泳會留下的東西，不是急救器材。沐晴正想笑，卻看到羅女士神情嚴肅。「那次撞擊事故後，大家走得很急。」她說。她指的是多年前掉下來的一根重管，而不是車禍。那次沒有人被殺，卻有謠言逐漸傳成謀殺故事。沐晴先寫下事件的正確定義，以免錯誤再流傳。

The camera returned an image of a loose metal bracket. Evan would investigate it through the city repair process. Nia suggested they also examine the public route from the alley to the dry deck. Maya walked it once and became dizzy after turning around a narrow corner. A place that seemed simple on a plan could be confusing in motion. "We don't need bravery to ignore a warning," Nia said. "We need people to notice it." They agreed to bring the access team back before placing any chairs. No visitor should have to starve through a long festival day, or stand in an unsafe passage just to see the dance.

攝影機拍回一張鬆動金屬支架的影像，以衡將透過市府維修程序展開調查。倪雅建議同時測量從巷子到乾燥池畔的公共動線。沐晴走了一次，在狹窄轉角來回轉身後感到暈眩。圖面上看似簡單的地方，移動時可能十分混亂。「我們不需要靠勇敢去無視警告，」倪雅說，「我們需要有人注意警告。」大家約定在擺放椅子之前請無障礙團隊重來一趟。參加一整天節慶的人不該餓肚子，也不該只是為了看舞蹈就站進危險通道。

At lunch, the volunteers made a quiet corner beside the entrance. A child took a nap on a blanket while the adults discussed signs. One visitor complained that their publicity poster showed a sexy, dramatic dancer rather than the actual community group. Maya felt embarrassed; she had approved the design without reading it carefully. She thanked the visitor and asked Nia to lead a revision meeting, with payment for the work. They chose a photograph of several people moving at different speeds. The poster now showed the project honestly and made its welcome easier to understand.

午餐時，志工在入口旁布置安靜角落。一個孩子在毯子上小睡，大人們討論告示。一名訪客抱怨宣傳海報只放了性感又戲劇化的舞者，而非真正的社區團隊。沐晴很尷尬；那是她未仔細檢查就通過的設計。她感謝訪客，並請倪雅帶領有酬勞的修改會議。大家選用一張不同速度移動者同框的照片。新海報誠實呈現計畫，也讓人更容易看懂歡迎的對象。

Before leaving, Maya looked again at the dark mark in the alley. A hurried observer might call it a disaster; a careless organizer might ignore it. She wanted to do neither. She asked Evan for a short written list of what the inspection had shown and what remained unknown. "Thankful isn't enough," she told him. "I want to pay for the time you spend." Evan smiled, then explained that his contract was with the city; she should not pay him privately. Keeping their roles separate would protect both of them. Maya nodded. She liked him a little more for saying no. Later, she returned alone to the public path and counted the number of turns between the front door and the dry deck. She noticed that every sign pointed toward the same locked gate, although another exit existed on the old plans. She wrote down the direction and took photographs from a distance. An incomplete map was not only a technical mistake; it could change a visitor’s decision during a crowded event. She promised herself that no performance would begin until the team had made a clear, tested route that everyone could understand.

離開前，沐晴又看了巷子裡的深色水痕。匆忙的人可能直接說那是災難，粗心的主辦者也可能完全忽略。她兩種都不想做，便請以衡列出短短的書面清單，說明檢查已確認什麼、還有哪些未知。「只有感謝不夠，」她說，「我想付你工作時間的費用。」以衡笑了，解釋自己的合約是與市府簽訂，她不該私下付錢。保持兩人的角色分際，才能保護彼此。沐晴點頭，因為他願意說不，又更喜歡了他一些。後來她獨自回到公共走道，數著前門到乾燥池畔之間需要轉彎幾次。她發現所有告示都指向同一道上鎖的大門，然而舊圖面上還有另一個出口。她在安全距離外記下方向並拍照。不完整的地圖不只是技術錯誤，也可能在人潮擁擠時改變訪客的判斷。她對自己承諾，在團隊測試並建立人人能懂的清楚路線前，絕不開始任何演出。
