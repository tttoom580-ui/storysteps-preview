The Missing Page

讓水面記住我們的舞步 · 第 12 / 25 章 · 2000–3000 · 英中對照 · 校訂中

用互動版查詞、朗讀與收藏

Maya found a missing page in the building archive. It belonged to a monthly maintenance report and described electricity problems near the west hatch. She set the file aside and asked the office for a complete copy rather than guessing what the absence meant. An elderly clerk who had worked there for years remembered a captain from the local rescue club who had reported a sudden flash in the old control panel. The captain was still alive. He had been a careful client of the center, not an official inspector, so his memories might help locate records but could not replace them.

沐晴在建築檔案裡發現少了一頁。那頁屬於每月維修報告，提到西側檢修口附近的電力問題。她把文件放在一旁，向辦公室索取完整副本，而不猜測缺頁的意思。一位工作多年的年長職員記得，當地救援社的一名隊長曾通報舊控制面板突然閃光。隊長仍健在。他是中心謹慎的使用者，不是正式檢查員；他的記憶能幫忙找到資料，卻不能取代資料。

The next day, the captain visited with a box of photographs. One showed children blowing a bubble above the pool, while another showed a wall painted like a deep canyon. The images made the place feel alive again. "We called that corner the canyon," he said. "It made a little sound when the wind came through." Maya asked to edit his account before sharing it, so he could correct details himself. He said he was capable of reading the draft and would prefer a printed copy. Nia arranged that without treating his age as a reason to make decisions for him.

隔天，隊長帶著一盒照片來訪。一張拍到孩子在泳池邊吹泡泡，另一張拍到畫成深峽谷的牆面。影像讓場所再次鮮活。「我們叫那個角落『峽谷』，」他說，「風通過時會發出一點聲音。」沐晴詢問是否能先編輯他的敘述，再由本人修正細節。他說自己有能力讀稿，也比較喜歡紙本。倪雅照辦，沒有因為他年紀大就替他作決定。

A city lawyer explained the situation at the next meeting. The pool was public property, and the city had to keep official records according to its rules. The missing page was a reason for an information request, not evidence that anyone had stolen it. Maya asked who could represent the community in that process. The lawyer answered that residents could make requests individually, but an elected program council might speak for the project. Nia suggested that the council include participants, workers, and people who had raised objections. Hopeful volunteers agreed to draft a fair selection process before choosing a leader.

下次會議上，市府律師解釋情況。泳池是公共財產，市府必須依規定保存正式紀錄。缺頁值得提出資訊申請，卻不表示有人偷走了它。沐晴問，誰可以代表社區進行程序。律師說居民能個別提出申請，而經選出的活動委員會也能代表專案。倪雅建議委員包括參與者、工作者和曾表達反對的人。抱持希望的志工同意，先設計公平遴選程序，再選領導者。

At a shared lunch, the retired cook added spice to a simple soup. A child asked why an old photograph showed a man holding a sword near the pool. Ms. Luo explained that it had been part of a play, not a real fight. The boy laughed, then became quiet when his grandmother began to weep over a picture of the first swimming club. Maya did not point a camera at her. She offered a chair and waited until the woman chose to speak. A good story needed trust more than it needed a dramatic picture. Sometimes the kindest choice was to leave a moment private.

共進午餐時，退休廚師在清湯裡加香料。一名孩子問，為何舊照片裡有人在泳池旁拿著劍。羅女士解釋，那是戲劇道具，不是真的打鬥。男孩笑了，之後看見祖母對著第一代泳會合照落淚，又安靜下來。沐晴沒有舉起攝影機，只搬來椅子，等老婦人自己選擇是否開口。好故事更需要信任，而非戲劇性照片。有時最溫柔的選擇，就是讓某一刻保有私人空間。

In the evening, Maya reread the report request she had prepared. The wording was clear, and the dates matched. She sent it through the official channel with copies to the community council. She had once believed that solving a problem meant finding the missing answer alone. Now she understood that a record became stronger when other people could check it. The file might arrive tomorrow or take weeks. Either way, the team could continue its safe classes while the questions were examined. She shut the office door with a feeling she had not expected: patience.

晚上，沐晴重讀準備好的資料申請書。用字清楚，日期也符合紀錄。她透過正式管道送出，副本交給社區委員會。過去她以為解決問題，就是獨自找到缺少的答案；現在她明白，能讓別人檢查的紀錄才更有力量。資料可能明天到，也可能花幾週。無論如何，團隊都能在查證同時繼續安全課程。她關上辦公室門，心裡有種意外的感覺：耐心。

The captain also brought an old schedule that nobody had requested. It listed a children’s holiday show, a repair day, and a meeting about water bills. Maya noticed the dates were ordinary; there was no dramatic event hidden in the spaces between them. Yet the schedule helped show that the building had been in regular use long after the first reported electrical problem. She copied the date of each event and asked the captain whether the pages could be scanned. He said yes to sharing the schedule but no to publishing his private address written on its back. Maya covered the address before making a copy. One small act of care made the document more useful, not less.

隊長還帶來一份沒人事先要求的舊時程表，列著兒童假日節目、維修日與水費會議。沐晴注意到日期都很平常，空白之間沒有戲劇性的神祕事件。然而這份表顯示，第一次電力問題通報之後，建築仍長期正常使用。她抄下每件活動的日期，問隊長能否掃描。他同意分享時程表，卻不願公開背面手寫的私人地址。沐晴先遮住地址才影印。一個小小的照顧動作，讓文件更有用，而非更沒用。
