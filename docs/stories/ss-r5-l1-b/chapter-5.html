Paper, Light, and a Plan

貝殼灣的影子節 · 第 5 / 25 章 · 1000以下 · 英中對照

用互動版查詞、朗讀與收藏

Sela wanted to make a story about a fish who thought the sea was too wide. The fish would meet other small animals and learn to ask for help. Daro liked the idea, but he said that too many scenes would take time. They decided to make three simple scenes and test each one. The small bank near the market opened before the friends started their work. A farm outside town offered old clean card boxes for their figures. Sela remembered to feed the fish at home before going to the hall. Uma wrote a letter to the town thanking it for the free room.

塞拉想寫一條覺得大海太寬的魚的故事。魚會遇到其他小動物，學習開口求助。達洛喜歡這個點子，但他說場景太多會耗費時間。於是他們決定先做三個簡單場景，逐一測試。市場附近的小銀行在朋友們開工前就開門了。 鎮外農場提供乾淨的舊紙箱，給他們製作角色。 塞拉記得去廳堂前先餵家裡養的魚。 烏瑪寫了一封信，感謝鎮上免費提供空間。

Uma drew a long line on a large sheet. She marked the days left before the festival and the jobs for each day. She asked the group to leave a little free time. A plan that filled every minute could break at the first surprise. The others saw that she was right. Many small tasks looked easier when they were put on one clear list. The moon would rise after the quiet show, not during the first test. Daro watched a short movie about moving paper shapes in a safe way. They did not put oil near the lamp because it was not needed.

烏瑪在大紙上畫了長線，標出距離節日剩下的日子，以及每天要做的事。她請大家留一點空檔，因為把每一分鐘都排滿的計畫，可能遇到第一個意外就失效。其他人知道她說得對。許多小任務寫在清楚的清單上後，看起來容易多了。 月亮會在安靜演出後升起，而不是第一次測試期間。 達洛看了一部短片，介紹如何安全地移動紙形。 他們不把油放在燈旁，因為根本不需要。

Daro showed how the paper fish could swim behind the cloth. Sela cut another figure to stand for the shore. It took more time than they guessed. One corner tore, and the stick would not stay on the paper. They changed the shape instead of hiding the problem. Sela said they would not sell food or toys at the free show. Daro moved the puppet in a slow line so young viewers could follow it. He cut one long straight edge from the sheet of strong paper. A strange shadow appeared when the stick bent behind the cloth.

達洛示範紙魚如何在布幕後游動。塞拉剪出另一個角色代表岸邊。這花的時間比預計多。一個角落裂開，小棒也黏不住。他們沒有掩飾問題，而是改了形狀。塞拉說，免費表演當天不會販售食物或玩具。 達洛讓玩偶緩慢移動，好讓年幼觀眾跟上。 他從厚紙上剪出一條長長的直邊。 小棒在白布後彎曲時，出現了奇怪的影子。

At midday, a few young visitors came to see the work. They wanted a loud ending with music. Sela explained that this was meant to be a quiet show. She invited them to make soft sounds with their hands instead. They tried it and found that a gentle rhythm could still feel exciting. They tested a short string but chose a firm stick for better control. One teenager showed the group how to make a soft beat with two fingers. Sela needed time to think before changing the last scene. Sunlight came through the window and made the paper figure easy to see.

中午時，幾位年輕訪客來看製作。他們想要用很大聲的音樂結尾。塞拉解釋這是安靜的表演，邀請他們改用雙手發出柔和聲音。大家一試，發現輕柔節奏也能令人興奮。他們測試短繩，最後選擇較好控制的硬棒。 一位青少年示範如何用兩根手指打出輕柔節拍。 塞拉需要時間思考，才決定要不要修改最後一場。 陽光穿過窗戶，讓紙角色看得很清楚。

Uma held the box open while Daro found a place for each figure. He moved his toe away before she lowered the lid. On the wall, a yellow card still marked the scene that needed work. Sela had wanted to finish it before going home, but the fish's loose mouth could wait. Their last topic was where to put each figure so the next day's work would begin without a long search. Daro drew the box on the plan and added a small mark for the fish. Sela put her pencil down. She could see tomorrow's job, and she did not have to do it tonight. The three scenes would grow one at a time.

烏瑪扶著箱子，達洛替每個角色找好位置。她放下箱蓋前，他先把腳趾移開。牆上的黃色卡仍標著需要修改的場景。塞拉原想回家前做完，但魚鬆動的嘴可以等。最後的話題，是每個角色該放哪裡，好讓明天不用找很久。達洛在配置圖畫出箱子，再替魚加一個小記號。塞拉放下鉛筆。她看得見明天的工作，卻不必今晚就完成。三個場景會一步一步長大。
