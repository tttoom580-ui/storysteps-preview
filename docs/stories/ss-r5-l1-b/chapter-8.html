The Old Room of Things

貝殼灣的影子節 · 第 8 / 25 章 · 1000以下 · 英中對照 · 校訂中

A small museum opened only twice a week near the town square. Sela, Daro, and Uma went there to learn how people had made pictures long ago. A guide showed them simple shapes cut from thin wood. The shapes looked very different from their paper fish, but the idea of light was familiar. Uma borrowed a book about old ways of making shadows on cloth. Daro thought the first room was boring until he saw the moving shapes. The guide said using a knife without care could be dangerous. A sign showed that a small model cost only one dollar to make.

鎮上廣場旁的小博物館每週只開兩天。塞拉、達洛與烏瑪前往了解古人如何製作圖像。導覽員展示了薄木板剪出的簡單形狀。它們看起來與紙魚很不一樣，但用光呈現影子的想法很熟悉。烏瑪借了一本介紹古代布幕影子技法的書。 達洛原本覺得第一間展室很無聊，直到看到會動的形狀。 導覽員說，不小心使用刀子可能很危險。 告示說，製作一個小模型只需一元。

The guide explained that one old scene belonged to a family. It was not free for other groups to claim as their own. Sela asked if they could learn the way the shapes moved while writing their own story. The guide smiled and said learning a method was different from taking someone's story. Each old figure had a different shape, even when the story was simple. The guide asked if anybody else wanted to try the model. Uma saved a file with the notes from their museum visit. At lunch, Daro used a fork to eat fruit beside the museum. No one worked alone. When one pair of hands became tired, another friend took a turn. Sela wanted every helper to feel that this new show was theirs, not only hers.

導覽員說，有一齣老戲屬於某個家族，別的團體不能拿來說是自己的。塞拉詢問，能不能學習形狀的移動方式，卻寫自己的故事。導覽員微笑回答：學習方法和拿走別人的故事是兩回事。每個舊角色都有不同的形狀，即使故事很簡單。 導覽員問，還有沒有別人想試模型。 烏瑪把博物館參訪筆記存成檔案。 午餐時，達洛在博物館旁用叉子吃水果。沒有人獨自工作。當一雙手累了，另一位朋友就接手。塞拉希望每位幫手都能覺得這場新表演屬於大家，而不是只屬於她。

Daro took careful notes. He liked the way a small wheel made a head turn. He tried a simple paper model at a table in the museum workshop. It worked, but the paper tore after three turns. He could see what was useful and what needed a stronger material. The museum hall was full of small tools and old pictures. Sela got an idea for a new way to move the fish's head. The friends learned to listen before asking questions about the old art. Daro saw his mistake when the weak paper broke during the test.

達洛仔細做筆記。他喜歡小輪子讓角色轉頭的方式，於是在博物館工作桌上試做一個紙模型。模型能動，但轉三次後紙就破了。他看出哪些做法有用，以及哪些地方需要更牢固的材料。博物館展廳擺滿小工具與舊照片。 塞拉想到讓魚頭移動的新方法。 朋友們學會先聽講解，再詢問古老藝術的問題。 脆弱的紙在測試中破裂時，達洛看出了自己的錯誤。

Uma found an old drawing of people sitting around a cloth screen. Some watched from chairs, and some sat on the floor. She pointed out that their own hall needed space for many kinds of bodies. The museum trip gave her ideas about how to keep the view open. The museum showed many ways people had made art before electric lights. They ate a small snack at noon before walking home. The guide showed a picture of a queen but said they could write a different story. Daro made a small wheel roll under the paper figure.

烏瑪找到一張舊畫，畫中人們圍著布幕坐下。有些坐椅子，有些坐地板。她指出，他們的廳堂也需要容納不同身體需求的空間。這趟博物館之行，讓她想到保持視線通暢的方法。博物館展示了人們在電燈出現前創作藝術的方法。 他們中午吃了小點心後才走回家。 導覽員展示女王畫像，但說他們可以自己寫不同的故事。 達洛讓小輪子在紙角色下面滾動。

On the walk home from the museum, Daro held the broken paper wheel flat between two boards. Their test had not been successful, but he could now show where the paper had torn. Sela asked whether a stronger piece would turn in the same way. They would have to try it. Uma said she would tell the guide what they learned and name the museum when they described the method. A train passed beyond the square, making the friends stop talking for a moment. When it was quiet again, Sela asked about their next fish. Uma smiled. 'You can make that story your own,' she said. 'We borrowed a way to move, not a life to put on stage.'

離開博物館時，達洛把破掉的紙輪夾平在兩塊板中。測試不成功，但他已能指出紙在哪裡裂開。塞拉問，換成較結實的紙是否也能同樣轉動；他們得試試。烏瑪說，會把結果告訴導覽員，介紹方法時也會說明博物館來源。一列火車從廣場遠處經過，朋友們暫停說話。安靜下來後，塞拉問起下一條魚。烏瑪微笑：「那個故事可以由你自己創作。我們借來的是移動的方法，不是把誰的人生搬上舞台。」
