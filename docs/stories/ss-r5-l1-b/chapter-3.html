A Fish on the Wall

On the third day, Daro brought a paper fish to the hall. He held it close to the lamp, and its shadow grew so large that only half of it showed. When he moved it back, the fish became very small. The friends learned that the space between light and paper mattered. All the friends watched the paper fish change size in the light. Uma marked an area on the floor where the table could stand. A basketball left in the hall rolled away when Daro moved the chairs. They were ready to begin a new test with a smaller gap. The old hall was changing one step at a time. Its walls were still plain, and its floor still showed years of use. Yet the friends could now picture children and parents enjoying a show there.

第三天，達洛帶來一條紙魚。他把魚靠近燈，影子就大到只看得見一半；往後移動時，魚又變得太小。朋友們學到，燈與紙之間的距離很重要。所有朋友都看著紙魚在燈光下改變大小。 烏瑪在地板標出可以放桌子的區域。 達洛搬椅子時，廳堂裡的一顆籃球滾走了。 他們準備縮小距離，開始新的測試。老廳堂正一步一步改變。牆面仍很樸素，地板也留著多年的痕跡。但朋友們已經能想像孩子和家長在這裡欣賞表演的樣子。

Uma placed a line on the floor for the lamp and another line for the puppet table. She told the friends to test the same place each time. Sela drew a fish that could open its mouth. Daro used two sticks to make the parts move in a simple way. Sela tried to define the best size for the fish on the screen. Uma planned to email the two guests a short invitation to watch. Sela used an eraser to remove a line that confused the fish's face. Only a few people were needed for this early test. The day did not go exactly as they had hoped. Still, they had learned one useful thing. They added it to their plan, so tomorrow would begin with a little more knowledge.

烏瑪在地上為燈畫一條線，也替玩偶桌畫一條線。她要大家每次都在同樣的位置測試。塞拉畫了一條會張嘴的魚，達洛用兩根小棒讓部件能簡單移動。塞拉試著訂出魚在布幕上最合適的大小。 烏瑪打算寄電子郵件，邀兩位客人來看。 塞拉用橡皮擦擦掉讓魚臉看起來不清楚的線。 這次初期測試只需要少數幾人。這一天並沒有完全照他們希望的方式進行。不過，他們仍學到一件有用的事。他們把它加進計畫，讓明天能從多一點的理解開始。

They called two neighbors to watch a short test. The fish moved, but the lines on its face were hard to see from the back. One neighbor could not tell where the fish was going. Sela was hurt for a moment, yet she thanked the visitor for saying what was unclear. The lamp stood on a firm table away from loose paper. Daro did not leave any sharp tools on the floor. A simpler fish with less detail looked clearer from the back. Uma did not mail an invitation because the two guests lived next door. Outside, the sound of the bay came through the open door. It reminded Sela why she wanted a show in this town. It was a place where different voices could meet.

他們請兩位鄰居來看短測試。魚動了，但坐在後方的人看不清臉上的線條。有位鄰居甚至看不出魚往哪裡去。塞拉一時有些難過，但仍感謝對方說出不清楚的地方。小燈放在穩固的桌上，遠離鬆散紙張。 達洛沒有把尖銳工具留在地上。 細節比較少的簡單魚形，從後面看更清楚。 兩位客人住隔壁，所以烏瑪沒有用郵寄邀請函。門外，海灣的聲音從敞開的門傳進來。這提醒塞拉為何想在這個小鎮辦演出：她希望不同的聲音能在這裡相遇。

Daro made the fish larger and cut out a round eye. Uma moved the chairs until the view was better. They tried again. Now the children could follow the fish from one side to the other. Sela understood that the test was not a judgment of her worth. It was a way to learn. The test gave their old hall a modern new purpose. The friends agreed to finish before night so no one would walk home in darkness. Sela kept an orange marker for drawing the fish's moving parts. A small plant near the entrance helped visitors find the right door. Sela listened to the others before she gave her answer. She had made this plan, but that did not mean every idea had to come from her. A better way could begin with any friend.

達洛把魚做大，並挖出圓形的眼睛。烏瑪移動椅子，直到視線更好。他們又試了一次。現在孩子們能看清魚從一端游到另一端。塞拉明白，測試不是在評判她的價值，而是學習的方法。這次測試讓舊廳堂有了現代的新用途。 朋友們同意天黑前結束，避免有人摸黑回家。 塞拉留了一支橘色筆，畫魚會動的部分。 入口旁的一株小植物，幫助訪客找到正確的門。塞拉先聽完大家的想法，才說出自己的答案。雖然計畫由她提出，卻不代表每個點子都得出自她。一個更好的方法可能從任何朋友開始。

That night, Sela wrote a note in her drawing book: Make the story easy to see. She did not need a perfect show on the first day. She needed to keep learning and to listen when something did not work. The small paper fish became the first real part of the play. The next practice was marked for three p.m. on the town calendar. A quarter of an hour was enough for the two guests to watch the test. The road outside had little traffic after the busy morning market. Sela said, "I will listen to the guests before making the next change." They took a short break and shared water. A hard job felt easier when everyone could say what they needed. After the break, they went back with a clearer plan.

那晚，塞拉在畫本上寫下：「讓故事容易看懂。」她不需要第一天就有完美演出，而是得持續學習，聽進不順利之處。那條小小紙魚，成了這齣戲第一個真正完成的部分。下一次練習排在鎮上日曆的下午三點。 兩位客人觀看測試，十五分鐘就夠了。 早市結束後，外面的路上車流不多。 塞拉說：「我會先聽客人的意見，再做下一步調整。」大家短暫休息，一起喝水。當每個人都能說出自己的需要時，辛苦的工作也變得比較容易。休息後，他們帶著更清楚的計畫回去做事。
