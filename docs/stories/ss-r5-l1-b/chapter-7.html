西邊的故事

Stories from the West

貝殼灣的影子節 · 第 7 / 25 章 · 1000以下 · 英中對照 · 校訂中

Sela did not want to tell only her own story. She visited the homes near the west beach and asked what scenes people remembered from childhood. Some were ready to talk; others wanted to keep their memories private. Sela thanked both groups. A story was a gift, not something she could take. Sela made notes by the shore while listening to the neighbors' stories. A child brought one square of chocolate to share after the talk. The local art class had many drawings of life near the sea. Uma asked for permission to collect family stories before using them in the play.

塞拉不想只說自己的故事。她走訪西邊海灘附近的住家，詢問人們童年記得的場景。有些人樂意分享，有些人想保留隱私。塞拉對兩種回答都表示感謝。故事是禮物，不是她可以任意取走的東西。塞拉在岸邊聽鄰居說故事時做筆記。 一個孩子帶來一小塊巧克力，想在談話後分享。 當地美術課畫了許多海邊生活的圖。 烏瑪先徵求同意，才把家族故事用進戲裡。

An older neighbor spoke about looking for small shells after school. Another remembered a mother singing while she washed clothes. A young rabbit wanted a funny scene with a fish in a hat. The friends laughed at the idea and asked if they could use it. The child gave a happy yes. Daro said a short course in puppet making might help young helpers. An older neighbor held a white flower while she spoke about her childhood. They stayed only one hour so the families could return to their work. A housewife told Sela that her work at home also held many stories. A new question came up, and nobody knew the answer at once. The friends did not hide that fact. They chose to try a small test and learn from what really happened.

一位年長鄰居談起放學後找小貝殼的回憶。另一人記得母親洗衣時唱歌。一隻小兔子想看戴帽子的魚出現在滑稽場景裡。朋友們聽了都笑了，並問能否採用這個點子；孩子開心答應。達洛說，簡短的玩偶製作課可能對年輕幫手有幫助。 一位年長鄰居談童年時，手裡拿著一朵白花。 他們只待一小時，讓家庭能回去做事。 一位家庭主婦告訴塞拉，家裡的工作也藏著許多故事。一個新問題出現了，沒有人立刻知道答案。朋友們沒有隱瞞這件事。他們決定先做小測試，再從實際發生的結果學習。

Back in the hall, Uma wrote every idea on a different card. She also wrote who had allowed the group to share it. Daro made a soft hat for the fish puppet. Sela looked at the cards and saw that the town had many kinds of stories, not one single story. A large book lay on Uma's table beside the notes from neighbors. Sela said she would let people say no without asking them to explain. Uma made a list of the ideas that neighbors agreed to share. The main idea was to tell a story that belonged to the group together.

回到廳堂，烏瑪把每個點子分別寫在卡片上，也記下誰同意分享。達洛替魚偶做了一頂柔軟帽子。塞拉看著卡片，發現小鎮有許多不同種類的故事，而非只有一個共同故事。烏瑪桌上放著一本大書，旁邊是鄰居的筆記。 塞拉說，如果人們拒絕，她不會要求解釋。 烏瑪列出鄰居同意分享的點子。 主要想法是說一個由大家共同創作的故事。

The group chose three ideas that could fit their short play. They kept the family song out because the neighbor asked them not to use it on stage. Sela told her friends that permission could change. Someone who had said yes before could still say no later, and they would listen. Daro made a note beside the idea about a fish with a hat. The funny fish became popular among the children who tested the play. A young rabbit said the fish should learn to ask for help. Sela asked whether the rabbit's story was real or an idea for a play.

大家選了三個適合短劇的點子。至於那首家族歌曲，鄰居不希望搬上舞台，他們便不用。塞拉告訴朋友，同意也可能改變；即使先前答應的人後來說不，他們仍會尊重。達洛在戴帽子的魚那個點子旁寫下備註。 滑稽的魚受到試看戲的孩子們喜愛。 一隻小兔子說，魚應該學會求助。 塞拉問小兔子的故事是真事，還是戲的點子。

Back at the hall, Sela looked at the empty place beside the shell card. It was for the song they would not use. She did not fill the space with another person's words. The neighbor's answer would stay no until that person chose something different. There were still three scenes to start with, and a funny fish with a new hat. Daro set the hat gently on the table while Uma read the names on the idea cards. Sela could hear the sea through the window. She had walked west looking for stories to bring home. Now she understood that listening also meant leaving some stories where they already belonged.

回到廳堂，塞拉看著貝殼卡旁的空位。那裡原本要放他們不會使用的歌曲。她沒有拿別人的話填滿它。除非那位鄰居自己改變想法，拒絕就仍是拒絕。他們還有三個場景可以開始，也有戴著新帽子的滑稽魚。達洛輕輕把帽子放桌上，烏瑪讀著點子卡上的姓名。海聲從窗外傳來。塞拉原先往西走，是想帶故事回來；如今她懂得，聆聽有時也表示，把一些故事留在它們原本所屬的地方。
