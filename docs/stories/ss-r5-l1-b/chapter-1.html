安靜的點子

A Quiet Idea

貝殼灣的影子節 · 第 1 / 25 章 · 1000以下 · 英中對照

In Shell Bay, the summer fair was always loud. Sela, a young capybara who liked drawing, saw her small neighbor cover his ears near the music. She asked why everyone had to enjoy the fair in the same way. She wanted to make something gentle, but she did not know where to start. A small bug crawled along the empty wall of the hall. Sela wondered whether this corner could become a quiet place for neighbors who needed a break. She used a camera to remember how the hall looked before their work began. The fact was simple: some neighbors went home when the music became too loud. Sela gave a friendly smile to a child who stood far from the crowd. Sela asked the child whether a quiet corner would make the fair easier to enjoy. He nodded and pointed away from the speakers. She offered him water and wrote down his suggestion. Her first plan came from listening, not guessing.

在貝殼灣，夏季節慶總是很吵。喜歡畫畫的年輕水豚塞拉，看見小鄰居在音樂旁摀住耳朵。她心想：為什麼每個人都得用同一種方式享受節日？她想做點溫柔的事，卻不知道從何開始。一隻小蟲沿著廳堂空白的牆面慢慢爬行。塞拉想，也許這個角落能成為需要休息的鄰居暫時避開喧鬧的地方。 她用相機記下開工前廳堂的樣子。 事實很簡單：有些鄰居一聽見太大聲的音樂就回家。 塞拉對站在人群外的孩子友善地笑了。塞拉問那個孩子，如果有個安靜角落，他會不會更願意留下來看節慶。孩子點點頭，指向遠離喇叭的地方。她遞給他水，把建議記下來。她的第一個計畫來自傾聽，而不是憑空猜想。

At the edge of town stood a plain hall by the sea. Inside was a white cloth left from an old dance. Sela held a small lamp behind it. Her hand made a large shape on the other side. The shape moved when she moved. Sela laughed with surprise. She pictured a future in which that child could enjoy a festival too. There was a small cup of juice on the old hall table. Her friend Daro, a hedgehog who made toys, arrived to help. He said they should never lie about whether a test really worked. Sela placed the white cloth in the middle of the room.

鎮邊有一座臨海的樸素廳堂。裡面留著一塊從前跳舞時用的白布。塞拉拿著小燈站在布後，另一邊立刻出現大大的手影。她一動，影子也動。塞拉驚喜地笑了。她想像未來那個孩子也能享受節慶。 舊廳堂的桌上有一小杯果汁。 會做玩具的刺蝟朋友達洛也來幫忙。他說，他們絕不該謊稱測試成功。 塞拉把白布放在房間中央。

Daro showed Sela how a piece of card could become the shadow of a fish on the cloth. Uma, a patient turtle, watched them from the open door. She said that a story made of light and shade might be good for people who liked quiet things. The north window let in soft light during the morning. Uma was the first to notice the shadow growing on the cloth. The other two friends moved closer to see what Daro had made. A neighbor's pet dog rested outside instead of coming into the workroom.

達洛示範如何用一片卡紙，在白布上投出魚的影子。很有耐心的烏龜烏瑪站在門口看著他們。她說，用光與影子說故事，或許很適合喜歡安靜的人。北邊的窗戶在早晨透進柔和的光。 烏瑪第一個注意到白布上的影子變大。 另外兩個朋友靠近，想看看達洛做了什麼。 鄰居的寵物狗待在外面休息，沒有進工作室。

The three friends did not plan a large party. They planned one short shadow show for neighbors who wanted a calm evening. Sela drew the hall and a little stage. Daro said he could make moving figures. Uma offered to check the time and ask what the town allowed. One family planned a picnic after the fair, away from the loud music. Sela could reach the lamp easily without stepping across Daro's tools. A red pencil lay on the table, but the friends chose plain black for the fish. One star appeared in the evening sky as they closed the hall.

三個朋友沒有打算辦盛大的派對。他們想為喜歡安靜夜晚的鄰居，準備一場短短的影子戲。塞拉畫下廳堂和小舞台；達洛說能製作會動的角色；烏瑪則願意確認時間與場地使用規定。有個家庭打算在節慶後去野餐，遠離吵雜的音樂。 塞拉不用跨過達洛的工具，就能輕鬆拿到燈。 桌上放著紅鉛筆，但朋友們選擇用純黑色畫魚。 他們關廳堂時，晚空出現了一顆星星。

Before they left, an older woman stopped at the open door. She had heard Sela talking about a quiet show. Would she need to join a game or sing, she asked. Sela said she could simply sit and watch. The woman smiled and asked when she could come back. Sela could not give her a date yet, so Uma offered to bring the news when the plan was ready. Then Daro put the fish flat in a box. They would use it again, but first they needed to ask about the hall. Sela looked at the white cloth one last time. This was no longer only an idea in her head. Someone was waiting to hear more.

離開前，一位年長婦人在開著的門邊停下。她聽到塞拉談安靜表演，便問是否得參加遊戲或唱歌。塞拉說，她可以只坐著看。婦人笑了，問什麼時候能再來。塞拉還無法給日期，烏瑪便答應，計畫確定後會通知她。接著，達洛把紙魚平放進箱。他們會再用它，但得先問清廳堂的事。塞拉最後看了一眼白布。這不再只是她腦中的點子；已有人等著聽更多消息。
