安靜並不容易

When Quiet Is Hard

貝殼灣的影子節 · 第 9 / 25 章 · 1000以下 · 英中對照 · 校訂中

用互動版查詞、朗讀與收藏

The friends invited a small group to watch their first linked scenes. Sela thought a silent show would be easy for everyone. She was wrong. A visitor who could not see the thin lines well asked for someone to say what was happening. Another visitor wanted less talking. Their wishes seemed to pull in different directions. A bear visiting the hall asked where to sit for the spoken version. Sela felt brave enough to ask visitors about their different needs. A farmer who came from outside town liked the silent version best. A foreigner visiting the bay asked whether the show used many words. Sela listened to the others before she gave her answer. She had made this plan, but that did not mean every idea had to come from her. A better way could begin with any friend.

朋友們邀請一小群人觀看第一次連起來的幾個場景。塞拉以為無聲表演對所有人都容易，結果她錯了。一位看不清細線的訪客希望有人說明劇情，另一位則想少聽些話。兩種願望看起來互相衝突。一隻來訪的熊問，有旁白的場次該坐哪裡。 塞拉鼓起勇氣，詢問訪客不同的需求。 一位鎮外農夫最喜歡無聲版。 一位來訪海灣的外國人詢問，表演會不會用很多話。塞拉先聽完大家的想法，才說出自己的答案。雖然計畫由她提出，卻不代表每個點子都得出自她。一個更好的方法可能從任何朋友開始。

Sela felt worried. She had made the show quiet because some neighbors did not like loud music. Now she saw that quiet did not always mean clear. Daro said they could test a spoken version with gentle words. Uma suggested a separate viewing time when the same story could be watched without speech. Uma said they should keep the choice open for every guest. One quiet visitor said he sometimes felt lonely at loud town events. They did not want to lose the calm feeling of the first idea. A neighbor offered some meat for dinner, but the helpers had already eaten.

塞拉很擔心。她因為鄰居不喜歡大聲音樂，才想做安靜的演出。現在她看見，安靜不一定表示清楚。達洛提議試做溫柔旁白版；烏瑪建議另排一個時段，讓人能不聽旁白觀看相同故事。烏瑪說，應為每位客人保留選擇。 一位安靜的訪客說，自己在吵鬧活動中有時感到孤單。 他們不想失去最初點子的平靜感。 一位鄰居拿肉來當晚餐，但幫手們已吃過了。

They asked both visitors what would help. The first wanted short lines that named each character. The second wanted to see the show without being asked questions. The friends wrote down these requests. They were not trying to make everybody the same; they were trying to give people a choice. A child brought a cup of milk and sat near the back. Sela said there was nothing wrong with wanting a quieter room. Every person could say what made watching the show easier. The photographer took a photograph only after asking the visitors.

他們問兩位訪客怎麼做會比較好。第一位希望有簡短句子介紹角色；第二位希望觀看時不必被問問題。朋友們把需求記下來。他們不是要把每個人變得一樣，而是想給人選擇。一個孩子帶著一杯牛奶，坐在後面。 塞拉說，想要比較安靜的房間沒有什麼不對。 每個人都可以說出怎樣觀看比較容易。 攝影師先徵求訪客同意，才拍下一張照片。

At the next test, Uma stood near the screen and quietly told the first part of the tale. Daro kept the figures large. Sela watched faces in the room, not only the moving shadows. One visitor leaned forward during the spoken part. In the next part, another enjoyed watching without a voice beside the screen. A small bell could ring once before each show, but not loudly. Sela felt sad when she learned that the first test had left someone out. A young singer offered to speak the short lines in a soft voice. Uma made sure the toilet was easy to find for all guests.

下一次測試時，烏瑪站在布幕旁，輕聲說明故事開頭。達洛把角色維持得夠大。塞拉觀察的不只是移動的影子，也看房間裡的表情。有聲片段中，一位訪客感興趣地前傾。接著無聲片段開始，另一位自在地觀看，不必聽布幕旁的人聲。每場戲前可以輕敲一次小鈴，但聲音不能太大。 塞拉得知第一次測試忽略了某人時，覺得難過。 一位年輕歌手願意用柔和聲音說短台詞。 烏瑪確認所有訪客都容易找到廁所。

Sela wrote two viewing times on the wall plan, then looked at the visitors still sitting near the back. Whose idea should she name beside the change? One visitor pointed to Uma; Uma pointed to the questions on Sela's page. The answer had grown from more than one voice. Sela drew a line between the two times to show that guests would not need to listen to the spoken part if they chose the quiet one. A warm room was not only a room with warm air. It was also a place where someone could say what did not work. Before she closed the hall, Sela asked the visitors whether she had understood them correctly.

塞拉在牆上寫下兩個觀賞時段，望向仍坐後排的客人。這項更動旁該寫誰的名字？一名訪客指烏瑪，烏瑪卻指向塞拉紙上的問題。答案來自不只一個聲音。塞拉在兩個時段間畫線，表示選安靜場的客人不必一起聽有聲場。溫暖的房間，不只是空氣溫暖，也是讓人能說出哪裡不適合自己的地方。關廳堂前，塞拉再問一次客人：她是否真的理解了他們的意思。
