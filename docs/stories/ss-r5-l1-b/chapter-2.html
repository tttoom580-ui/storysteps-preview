一座廳堂，許多聲音

One Hall, Many Voices

貝殼灣的影子節 · 第 2 / 25 章 · 1000以下 · 英中對照

用互動版查詞、朗讀與收藏

The next morning, Uma led Sela and Daro to the town office. A goat named Oren cared for public rooms. He listened to their idea and showed them the hall rules. The hall could be used for a free event, but the friends had to leave it clean and safe. Uma said anyone could come to the town office to ask about public rooms. Oren lived in a small apartment above the office with his family. Sela wanted to ask about every rule before promising a show. The old church bell rang while the friends walked toward the hall. Outside, the sound of the bay came through the open door. It reminded Sela why she wanted a show in this town. It was a place where different voices could meet.

隔天早上，烏瑪帶塞拉和達洛去鎮公所。負責公共空間的山羊歐倫聽了他們的點子，拿出廳堂規則。廳堂可以用來辦免費活動，但朋友們得保持乾淨、安全。烏瑪說，任何人都能到鎮公所詢問公共場地。 歐倫與家人住在公所樓上的小公寓。 塞拉想先問清所有規則，再承諾演出。 朋友們走向廳堂時，老教堂的鐘響了。門外，海灣的聲音從敞開的門傳進來。這提醒塞拉為何想在這個小鎮辦演出：她希望不同的聲音能在這裡相遇。

Oren said the town had to choose how to use the hall next month. Some people wanted it for boxes and old chairs. Others wanted a place to meet. If the shadow night worked, the town could see a new use for the room. Sela felt the weight of that chance. Oren made the rules clear so no one had to guess. Families from the country outside town sometimes visited the summer fair. Sela did not forget to thank Oren for listening to her plan. Daro said they should not guess how many people would fit in the room. Sela listened to the others before she gave her answer. She had made this plan, but that did not mean every idea had to come from her. A better way could begin with any friend.

歐倫說，鎮上必須決定下個月如何使用廳堂。有些人想把它當作放箱子和舊椅子的地方，另一些人想要聚會空間。如果影子夜成功，大家就能看到這個房間的新用途。塞拉感受到這次機會的重要。歐倫把規定說清楚，讓大家不用猜。 住在鎮外鄉間的家庭有時會來參加夏季節慶。 塞拉沒有忘記感謝歐倫聽她說明計畫。 達洛說，他們不應猜測房間能容納多少人。塞拉先聽完大家的想法，才說出自己的答案。雖然計畫由她提出，卻不代表每個點子都得出自她。一個更好的方法可能從任何朋友開始。

They walked through the hall with Oren. Uma counted doors and places to sit. Daro checked a low table that could hold the figures. Sela saw that a good idea still needed clear rules. The event would take place in three weeks, and every visitor had to be welcome. Here by the door, Uma found enough space for a safe path. The friends agreed that no helper should have to work late without a choice. They ate lunch under a tree while discussing their list of tasks. Sela said visitors would not have to pay to watch their play. They took a short break and shared water. A hard job felt easier when everyone could say what they needed. After the break, they went back with a clearer plan.

他們和歐倫一起走過廳堂。烏瑪數了門和座位的位置；達洛檢查一張能放角色的矮桌。塞拉發現，再好的點子也需要清楚的規則。活動將在三週後舉行，而且每位訪客都應受到歡迎。就在門邊，烏瑪找到足夠空間規劃安全通道。 朋友們同意，不應讓任何幫手被迫工作到很晚。 他們在樹下吃午餐，同時討論工作清單。 塞拉說，觀眾不必付錢就能看戲。大家短暫休息，一起喝水。當每個人都能說出自己的需要時，辛苦的工作也變得比較容易。休息後，他們帶著更清楚的計畫回去做事。

Outside, the three friends shared a small lunch. Daro wanted to start making figures at once. Uma asked them to write down the tasks first. Sela agreed. The three friends could each take a different kind of work, and no one needed to carry the whole plan alone. Uma used a blue pen to mark each task with its owner's name. Daro tested a small piece of card before making a larger figure. They passed a quiet pond on the walk back to the hall. A local reporter asked Sela what made a shadow show different from a concert. Everyone had a different job, and each job mattered. Even the quiet work behind the cloth helped the show come alive. Sela promised to name every helper when the day came.

到了外面，三人吃了一頓簡單的午餐。達洛想立刻動手做角色；烏瑪請大家先把工作寫下來。塞拉同意了。三個人可以分別負責不同任務，沒有人必須獨自承擔整個計畫。烏瑪用藍筆標上每項工作的負責人。 達洛先測試一小片卡紙，才製作更大的角色。 回廳堂的路上，他們經過一座安靜的池塘。 一位地方記者問塞拉，影子戲和演唱會有什麼不同。每個人有不同的工作，而每份工作都很重要。就連白布後面安靜的工作，也能讓表演活起來。塞拉答應在演出當天說出每位幫手的名字。

By evening, Sela had a short list on her wall. She would draw the story, Daro would make the figures, and Uma would work with the town. They would ask their neighbors what they needed before building a stage. The promise was simple, but they meant to keep it. Uma gave a serious answer about the need for safe doors and seats. An old sofa could stay in the side room if it did not block the path. Oren gave Uma his office phone number for questions about the hall. Their third task was to invite neighbors to share what they needed. No one worked alone. When one pair of hands became tired, another friend took a turn. Sela wanted every helper to feel that this new show was theirs, not only hers.

傍晚時，塞拉把簡短的工作清單貼在牆上。她負責畫故事，達洛做角色，烏瑪與鎮上聯絡。搭舞台之前，他們會先問鄰居需要什麼。承諾很簡單，但他們打算確實做到。烏瑪認真回答，說明安全門口和座位的必要性。 一張舊沙發可以留在側室，只要不擋住通道。 歐倫把公所電話號碼給烏瑪，供她詢問場地問題。 他們的第三項任務，是邀請鄰居分享需求。沒有人獨自工作。當一雙手累了，另一位朋友就接手。塞拉希望每位幫手都能覺得這場新表演屬於大家，而不是只屬於她。
