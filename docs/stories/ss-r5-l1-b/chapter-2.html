一座廳堂，許多聲音

One Hall, Many Voices

貝殼灣的影子節 · 第 2 / 25 章 · 1000以下 · 英中對照

用互動版查詞、朗讀與收藏

The next morning, Uma led Sela and Daro to the town office. A goat named Oren cared for public rooms. He listened to their idea and showed them the hall rules. The hall could be used for a free event, but the friends had to leave it clean and safe. Uma said anyone could come to the town office to ask about public rooms. Oren lived in a small apartment above the office with his family. Sela wanted to ask about every rule before promising a show. The old church bell rang while the friends walked toward the hall.

隔天早上，烏瑪帶塞拉和達洛去鎮公所。負責公共空間的山羊歐倫聽了他們的點子，拿出廳堂規則。廳堂可以用來辦免費活動，但朋友們得保持乾淨、安全。烏瑪說，任何人都能到鎮公所詢問公共場地。 歐倫與家人住在公所樓上的小公寓。 塞拉想先問清所有規則，再承諾演出。 朋友們走向廳堂時，老教堂的鐘響了。

Oren said the town had to choose how to use the hall next month. Some people wanted it for boxes and old chairs. Others wanted a place to meet. If the shadow night worked, the town could see a new use for the room. Sela felt the weight of that chance. Oren made the rules clear so no one had to guess. Families from the country outside town sometimes visited the summer fair. Sela did not forget to thank Oren for listening to her plan. Daro said they should not guess how many people would fit in the room.

歐倫說，鎮上必須決定下個月如何使用廳堂。有些人想把它當作放箱子和舊椅子的地方，另一些人想要聚會空間。如果影子夜成功，大家就能看到這個房間的新用途。塞拉感受到這次機會的重要。歐倫把規定說清楚，讓大家不用猜。 住在鎮外鄉間的家庭有時會來參加夏季節慶。 塞拉沒有忘記感謝歐倫聽她說明計畫。 達洛說，他們不應猜測房間能容納多少人。

They walked through the hall with Oren. Uma counted doors and places to sit. Daro checked a low table that could hold the figures. Sela saw that a good idea still needed clear rules. The event would take place in three weeks, and every visitor had to be welcome. Here by the door, Uma found enough space for a safe path. The friends agreed that no helper should have to work late without a choice. They ate lunch under a tree while discussing their list of tasks. Sela said visitors would not have to pay to watch their play.

他們和歐倫一起走過廳堂。烏瑪數了門和座位的位置；達洛檢查一張能放角色的矮桌。塞拉發現，再好的點子也需要清楚的規則。活動將在三週後舉行，而且每位訪客都應受到歡迎。就在門邊，烏瑪找到足夠空間規劃安全通道。 朋友們同意，不應讓任何幫手被迫工作到很晚。 他們在樹下吃午餐，同時討論工作清單。 塞拉說，觀眾不必付錢就能看戲。

Outside, the three friends shared a small lunch. Daro wanted to start making figures at once. Uma asked them to write down the tasks first. Sela agreed. The three friends could each take a different kind of work, and no one needed to carry the whole plan alone. Uma used a blue pen to mark each task with its owner's name. Daro tested a small piece of card before making a larger figure. They passed a quiet pond on the walk back to the hall. A local reporter asked Sela what made a shadow show different from a concert.

到了外面，三人吃了一頓簡單的午餐。達洛想立刻動手做角色；烏瑪請大家先把工作寫下來。塞拉同意了。三個人可以分別負責不同任務，沒有人必須獨自承擔整個計畫。烏瑪用藍筆標上每項工作的負責人。 達洛先測試一小片卡紙，才製作更大的角色。 回廳堂的路上，他們經過一座安靜的池塘。 一位地方記者問塞拉，影子戲和演唱會有什麼不同。

At the hall, Uma pointed to the old sofa in the side room. It could stay only if people could still reach the door. Daro moved a box and asked her to try the path again. This was a serious part of the plan, not a small job to leave until the night of the show. Sela wrote three tasks on a clean page. The first was the story, the second was the room, and the third was asking neighbors what they needed. Oren had given Uma his office phone number. She placed it beside the room plan. They now knew whom to ask when a question was too large for the three of them.

回到廳堂，烏瑪指向側室的舊沙發。只有不妨礙人走到門口，它才能留下。達洛搬開一個箱子，請她再走一次。這是計畫裡必須認真處理的部分，不能留到演出當晚。塞拉在乾淨紙上列三項工作：第一是故事，第二是房間，第三是問鄰居需要什麼。歐倫給了烏瑪辦公室電話，她把號碼放在配置圖旁。現在，遇到三個朋友無法決定的問題，他們知道該問誰。
