小小的燈光開關

The Little Light Switch

貝殼灣的影子節 · 第 10 / 25 章 · 1000以下 · 英中對照 · 校訂中

Daro built a small box to hold the lamp, but its switch was hard to find behind the screen. During practice he reached too far and knocked over a paper figure. Sela stopped the scene. They needed a safer way to change the light, especially while people were moving in the dark. Uma looked around the lamp table to find the hard-to-see switch. Daro tested a large button that was easy to feel with one finger. The room became dark only after the small path light was turned on. They checked the light before the evening practice began.

達洛做了一個小盒子放燈，但布幕後很難摸到開關。練習時，他伸手太遠，碰倒了紙角色。塞拉立刻停止。尤其在黑暗中有人移動時，他們需要更安全的開關方式。烏瑪在燈桌周圍找那個難看見的開關。 達洛測試一個用手指就容易摸到的大按鈕。 只有先打開小走道燈，房間才會變暗。 傍晚練習開始前，他們先檢查燈光。

Uma read the lamp guide and asked Oren to check the place for its wire. Oren said only the town worker could change the room's power parts. The friends decided not to touch those things. They could use a simple battery lamp on a firm table instead. A paper fish could fall from the table if it was not held in place. Sela asked if they could have a safer way to turn the light off. Daro said the new test was interesting because nobody needed to reach far. Uma put the spare lamp on the left side of the work table. Outside, the sound of the bay came through the open door. It reminded Sela why she wanted a show in this town. It was a place where different voices could meet.

烏瑪閱讀燈具說明，請歐倫檢查電線位置。歐倫說，只有鎮上的工作人員能修改房間的電力設備。朋友們決定不去碰那些東西，改用放在穩固桌上的簡單電池燈。紙魚若沒有固定好，就可能從桌上掉下來。 塞拉問，是否有更安全的方式關燈。 達洛說，新測試很有趣，因為沒有人需要伸手太遠。 烏瑪把備用燈放在工作桌左側。門外，海灣的聲音從敞開的門傳進來。這提醒塞拉為何想在這個小鎮辦演出：她希望不同的聲音能在這裡相遇。

Daro added a large button that was easy to feel. He tested it many times while standing still. Sela moved the figures without touching the lamp. Uma watched to be sure no path was blocked. The light went on and off at the right moments, and nothing fell. A small menu of light settings was taped beside the new button. Daro ate a potato snack after testing the light twenty times. A rose in a vase stood far from all the electric parts. The visiting neighbor gave Sela a smile when the path light worked.

達洛加裝一個容易摸到的大按鈕。他站在原地反覆測試。塞拉移動角色時不碰燈；烏瑪則確認走道沒有被擋住。光線在對的時刻亮起、熄滅，而且沒有東西倒下。一張簡單的燈光選單貼在新按鈕旁。 達洛測試二十次燈後，吃了馬鈴薯點心。 花瓶裡的玫瑰遠離所有電力設備。 走道燈正常運作時，來訪鄰居對塞拉笑了。

They asked a neighbor to watch the test from the last row. The visitor said that the shadow still showed clearly when the light changed. She also said the room felt safe because there was a small path light near the exit. The friends wrote down her words as useful advice. The sound of the little switch was soft enough for the quiet show. They could stay in the hall only until the town worker closed it. A student watched from the last row and said the fish remained clear. The lamp gave a sweet, soft light across the white cloth.

他們請一位鄰居從最後一排觀看測試。那位訪客說，燈光切換時仍能清楚看到影子。她還說，出口旁有微弱的走道燈，讓人覺得安全。朋友們把她的話記為有用建議。小開關的聲音很輕，適合安靜表演。 他們只能待到鎮上工作人員關廳堂為止。 一名學生從最後一排觀看，說魚影仍然清楚。 燈把柔和可愛的光投在白布上。

Daro put his hand by the new button and asked Sela to watch the fish. This time the light changed without his arm crossing the table. The old trouble had looked small until a paper figure fell. Now they could see why Sela had called a stop. Uma usually checked the doorway before the room went dark, and she asked them to leave time for that check in the play. Daro made a mark beside the light change. They did not want to worry a guest who stood up at that moment. Sela tried the move once more. The little button had not made the story more exciting. It had made room for the story to happen.

達洛把手放在新按鈕旁，請塞拉看魚。這次燈光切換時，他的手臂不必跨過桌面。紙角色掉下來之前，舊問題看似很小，如今大家明白塞拉為何喊停。烏瑪通常會在房間變暗前檢查門口，也請他們把這段時間排進戲裡。達洛在燈光切換旁做記號。他們不想讓此刻站起來的客人擔心。塞拉再試一次。小按鈕沒有讓故事更刺激，卻讓故事有機會好好發生。
