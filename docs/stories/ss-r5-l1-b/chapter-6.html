一場表演的代價

The Price of a Show

貝殼灣的影子節 · 第 6 / 25 章 · 1000以下 · 英中對照 · 校訂中

When the friends counted their supplies, they found only enough card for a few more figures. Sela wanted to buy bright paper and more lamps. Uma asked how they would pay for them. The show was meant to be free, so they could not use money they did not have. Daro lifted his arm to show the friends a sheet of clean card. Uma said the nearby store was convenient because they could walk there together. Sela looked into a deep box but found only a few usable pieces. The small desk by the wall became the place for their supply list.

朋友們清點材料時，發現卡紙只夠再做幾個角色。塞拉想買亮色紙和更多燈，烏瑪卻問錢從哪裡來。表演本來就是免費的，他們不能花沒有的錢。達洛抬起手臂，向朋友展示一張乾淨卡紙。 烏瑪說附近的店很方便，大家可以一起走去。 塞拉往深箱子裡看，只找到幾片可以用的材料。 牆邊的小書桌成了放材料清單的地方。

They made a list of what they truly needed. A safe lamp, strong card, and plain cloth came first. Nice colors and new boxes could wait. Daro suggested using old clean card from his workshop. He asked the people who owned it before taking any piece. Sela asked what they could do without buying any new tools. Uma put the store receipt into an envelope to keep it clean. Daro hoped to find strong paper among the clean old boxes. A drawing of a fish reminded the group why they needed the paper.

他們列出真正需要的物品：安全的燈、厚卡紙和素布最優先。漂亮的顏色與新盒子可以等。達洛提議使用工作室裡乾淨的舊卡紙，而且每拿一張都先問主人是否同意。塞拉問，不買新工具還能做些什麼。 烏瑪把店家收據放進信封，以免弄髒。 達洛希望從乾淨的舊箱中找到結實紙張。 魚的圖畫提醒團隊，為何需要那些紙。

A small store offered them some cloth for less money. Its owner did not ask for her name on the show. Sela thanked her and made sure everyone knew the gift had no hidden rule. Uma wrote the value in their records. That way the group could be open about the help it received. They told a funny story as they sorted the old supplies. Uma read the hall's history to learn what the town had used it for. However, the records did not say that the hall could store every old box. The group said a neighbor may help only if the neighbor truly wants to.

一家小店願意便宜提供一些布。店主沒有要求把名字放進演出裡。塞拉道謝，也確認這份贈與沒有附帶條件。烏瑪把價值記在紀錄中，讓團隊能公開說明收到的協助。整理舊材料時，他們說了一個有趣的故事。 烏瑪閱讀廳堂歷史，了解鎮上以前如何使用它。 然而，紀錄並沒有說廳堂可以存放所有舊箱子。 團隊說，鄰居只有自己願意才可以幫忙。

In the evening, Daro tried the old card. Some pieces bent too easily, while others worked well. He kept only the strong ones. Sela learned that a free show did not mean careless work. They could be careful with money and still make something good for their neighbors. They wiped the mud from their shoes before carrying the card inside. The news about the free show reached the small market that afternoon. Sela watched the paper fish rise slowly on the white cloth. There was a seat by the door for anyone who needed to rest.

傍晚，達洛測試舊卡紙。有些太容易彎，有些卻很好用。他只留下夠牢固的部分。塞拉明白，免費表演不代表可以隨便做。他們能省錢，也能為鄰居做出好作品。搬卡紙進屋前，他們先擦掉鞋上的泥。 那天下午，免費演出的消息傳到了小市場。 塞拉看著紙魚在白布上緩緩升起。 門邊設了一個座位，讓需要休息的人使用。

The shop owner watched Uma put the gift beside the paid items on her list. He was surprised that she wanted to show both. Uma said the town should know what the team had bought and what neighbors had offered. Sela asked Daro to speak if the old card broke in the next test. A gift did not have to be used if it could not do the job. The owner showed them another piece of cloth, but they did not take it before checking their money. On the walk back, Sela carried a smaller bag than she had hoped for. It was enough to start. At the door she turned the sign outward: every guest would be welcome, not only the people who could give.

店主看烏瑪把贈品與付費物品一併記在清單上，很驚訝她兩種都要列。烏瑪說，鎮上應知道團隊買了什麼，也知道鄰居提供什麼。塞拉請達洛在下次測試時，若舊卡紙破了就坦白說。東西雖是贈品，不適用就不必勉強用。店主拿出另一塊布，但朋友們先核對錢，沒有立刻收下。回程，塞拉的袋子比預期小，卻足夠開始工作。到門口，她把告示轉向外面：歡迎所有客人，不只歡迎有能力捐助的人。
