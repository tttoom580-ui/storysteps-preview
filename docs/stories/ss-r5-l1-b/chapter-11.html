給每位客人的座位

A Seat for Every Guest

貝殼灣的影子節 · 第 11 / 25 章 · 1000以下 · 英中對照 · 校訂中

用互動版查詞、朗讀與收藏

Uma invited several neighbors to try the seats during a short practice. A young bird wanted to sit near the screen, while an older goat asked for a chair with a back. Someone needed room for a walking stick. Sela saw how much care was needed before the first figure even moved. Uma brought a basket of clean cloth for people to use at the hall. Daro put his belt on a hook so it would not catch on a chair. The friends had to check the seats before anyone entered the room. Sela used a bright color to mark the safe path on her drawing.

烏瑪邀請幾位鄰居在短練習時試坐。小鳥想坐在布幕附近，年長的山羊想要有椅背的椅子，還有人需要放手杖的空間。塞拉發現，在第一個角色動起來之前，就需要很多照顧。烏瑪帶來一籃乾淨的布，供廳堂的人使用。 達洛把皮帶掛在鉤子上，避免勾到椅子。 朋友們必須在訪客進屋前檢查座位。 塞拉用亮色在圖上標出安全走道。

Daro put marks on the floor so the chairs would stay away from the clear path. He asked people to tell him when a seat felt too close. A thin line of light showed the way to the door. Uma made a drawing of the room and kept it beside the entrance. A dear older friend asked for a seat near the entrance. Sela said she would hate to make anyone feel unwelcome by accident. Uma thought about visitors' health and comfort, but did not ask for private details. The town hospital had taught workers how to keep public paths clear.

達洛在地上做記號，讓椅子不會擋住通道。他請大家在座位太擠時告訴他。細細的燈光標出了通往門口的路。烏瑪畫出房間配置圖，放在入口旁。一位親愛的年長朋友想坐在入口附近。 塞拉說，她不願意無意間讓任何人感到不受歡迎。 烏瑪考慮訪客的健康與舒適，卻不打聽私人細節。 鎮上的醫院曾教工作人員如何保持公共走道通暢。

One visitor said she had to take a short break every few minutes. She worried she would disturb the show. Sela told her that coming and going were both fine. The group would not stop the story to look at her. This answer made the visitor smile, and Sela felt she had done something right. Uma said that if a guest needed to stand up, they could do so. Daro told a gentle joke to help a worried child relax. A kite flew outside the window while the guests tried the chairs. Sela found a useful picture of the room in a library book.

一位訪客說自己每幾分鐘就需要休息一下，擔心會打擾表演。塞拉告訴她，進出都沒有關係，團隊也不會為了看她而中斷故事。那位訪客笑了，塞拉覺得自己做對了一件事。烏瑪說，如果客人需要站起來，就可以站起來。 達洛說了個溫和的笑話，幫擔心的孩子放鬆。 客人試坐椅子時，窗外有風箏飛過。 塞拉在圖書館的書中找到一張有用的室內配置圖。

The friends practiced their greeting. It was short: visitors could choose a seat, ask for help, or leave when needed. There was no test to pass and no right way to enjoy the show. Uma said this greeting mattered just as much as the words in the play. The friends often changed a chair after hearing a new request. Uma put the seating picture beside the hall entrance for everyone to see. A young ball player needed more space to rest a sore leg. Uma said they could send a simple guide to guests before the show.

朋友們練習簡短的歡迎詞：訪客可以選位子、要求協助，也能在需要時離開。來看表演不必通過測試，也沒有唯一正確的欣賞方式。烏瑪說，這段歡迎詞與戲裡的台詞同樣重要。朋友們聽到新需求後，常會調整一張椅子。 烏瑪把座位配置圖放在入口旁，讓大家看得到。 一名年輕球員需要較大的空間，讓痠痛的腿休息。 烏瑪說，他們可以在演出前寄給客人簡單指南。

Those two chairs by the back door had looked useful when the room was empty. With someone sitting in each, the way out became too narrow. Daro moved them while Uma watched from the doorway. Sela asked the guests to try leaving and returning during the fish's short journey. She kept moving the figure rather than turning toward every person who stood up. From the last row, the fish still showed clearly against the white cloth. One visitor came back smiling. She had taken the break she needed and had not become part of the show. Sela put the two extra chairs in the side room. More chairs did not always mean a better welcome.

空房間裡，後門旁那兩張椅子看似很有用；有人坐上去，出口卻變窄了。達洛搬椅子，烏瑪在門口看。塞拉請客人試著在魚的短旅途中離開、再回來。她繼續移動角色，不因有人起身就轉頭注視。從最後一排看，白布上的魚仍很清楚。一位客人微笑著回座。她得到需要的休息，也沒有變成表演的一部分。塞拉把兩張多餘椅子收進側室。椅子更多，不一定就表示歡迎做得更好。
