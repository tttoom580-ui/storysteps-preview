Names on the Program

貝殼灣的影子節 · 第 12 / 25 章 · 1000以下 · 英中對照 · 校訂中

用互動版查詞、朗讀與收藏

The town asked Sela for a simple program. She wrote the story title but did not know whose name should appear under it. She had drawn the first fish, yet many neighbors had helped. Daro made the figures, Uma planned the seats, and others shared ideas that they had allowed the group to use. A young actor offered to read a short line for the spoken show. The group met in the afternoon to decide how to name the helpers. A small band asked if its members could help with soft music. Sela wanted to celebrate the work of everyone behind the cloth.

鎮上請塞拉交一份簡單的節目單。她寫下故事名稱，卻不知道下面該放誰的名字。第一條魚雖然是她畫的，但許多鄰居都幫了忙。達洛做角色、烏瑪安排座位，其他人也同意團隊使用自己的點子。一位年輕演員願意替有聲版念一句短台詞。 團隊下午開會，決定如何列出幫手姓名。 一個小樂團詢問，團員能否幫忙演奏柔和音樂。 塞拉想慶祝每位在白布後工作的人的努力。

At a group meeting, Sela asked everyone what kind of credit they wanted. Some liked to see their names in print. Others preferred a quiet thank-you without a name. The friends did not make a single rule for all. Uma wrote down each person's answer. Daro drew a tiny shell in the corner of the program cover. Sela said no kind idea should die only because its maker was shy. Uma put on her glasses to read the small names in the draft. Sela wrote, 'I want every helper to choose how to be thanked.' Uma looked at the time and called everyone together.

在團隊會議上，塞拉問每個人希望如何被致謝。有些人喜歡名字印出來，另一些人想要沒有署名的低調感謝。朋友們沒有用一條規則套在所有人身上。烏瑪記下每個人的回答。達洛在節目單封面角落畫了小貝殼。 塞拉說，一個好點子不該只因創作者害羞就消失。 烏瑪戴上眼鏡，閱讀草稿上細小的姓名。 塞拉寫道：「我希望每位幫手都能選擇如何被感謝。」

Daro made a picture for the front of the program. It showed a fish looking toward a wide sea. He did not put a crown on the fish, because the story was about asking for help, not being the most important animal in the bay. Sela liked the simple drawing. The key to fair credit was asking, not making a guess. Daro checked the last name on the list before printing. A visitor from local media asked whether the show had a ticket price. Sela said more people had helped than she first remembered.

達洛替節目單封面畫了一張圖：魚望向寬廣的大海。他沒有替魚戴王冠，因為故事說的是求助，而非成為海灣最重要的動物。塞拉喜歡這張簡單的畫。公平署名的關鍵在於詢問，而不是猜測。 達洛在印製前確認清單上的最後一個名字。 地方媒體的一位來訪者詢問，演出是否有票價。 塞拉說，提供協助的人比她最初記得的還多。

Some neighbors asked where they could buy tickets. Uma explained that the show was free. They would give out paper tickets only to help count seats and keep the room from becoming too full. Nobody would earn money from the people watching. The hall belonged to the town. They planned to open the hall at six o’clock before the short show. Some helpers wanted their names on the page, or a quiet thank-you without a name. A small package of paper tickets arrived from the town office. Daro asked if children could play with a spare figure after the show. The old hall was changing one step at a time. Its walls were still plain, and its floor still showed years of use. Yet the friends could now picture children and parents enjoying a show there.

有鄰居問去哪裡買票。烏瑪解釋，表演免費，紙票只用來計算座位，避免房間過度擁擠。大家不會從觀眾身上賺錢，因為廳堂屬於整個小鎮。他們計畫在短劇前，六點鐘開放廳堂。 有些幫手希望名字登在紙上，或者只要不具名的低調道謝。 鎮公所送來一小包紙票。 達洛問孩子們能不能在演後玩備用角色。老廳堂正一步一步改變。牆面仍很樸素，地板也留著多年的痕跡。但朋友們已經能想像孩子和家長在這裡欣賞表演的樣子。

They were tired when the last program came out, so Uma asked everyone to read only one small part at a time. She checked the paper from the supermarket. Daro checked the picture. Sela read the names against the answers the helpers had given. A missing name could still be added before the next printing; a private name could still be taken out. Near the bottom, Uma wrote that each ticket cost nothing. Rich and poor neighbors would enter by the same door. Sela put the program beside the first fish drawing. The fish had begun with her pencil. The longer page beside it showed how far it had traveled through other people's hands.

最後一份節目單印好時，大家都累了。烏瑪請每人一次只讀一小部分：她檢查從超市買來的紙，達洛看圖，塞拉則把姓名與幫手先前的回答逐一比對。漏掉的名字仍能在下次印製前補上；希望保密的名字也能撤下。頁面底部，烏瑪寫明票券免費。富裕和貧窮的鄰居，會從同一扇門進來。塞拉把節目單放在第一張紙魚圖旁。魚從她的鉛筆開始，旁邊那張較長的紙，卻顯示它經過多少雙別人的手。
