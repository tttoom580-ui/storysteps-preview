柳樹下的課

Lessons Under the Willow

河流兩岸的紅繩 · 第 7 / 25 章 · 1000以下 · 英中對照 · 校訂中

用互動版查詞、朗讀與收藏

The children met by a large willow tree for a class about river safety. Luma, a rabbit, brought a list of places where people should not walk. She drew one flower beside each safe path. Bo brought a long piece of wood and let the children see the marks on it. “This is not a toy,” he told them. “It is a real tool for our work.” One child asked how many hours it would take to build a bridge. “More than one hour,” Bo said, and everyone laughed.

孩子們在大柳樹下上河流安全課。兔子露瑪帶來一張不能走的地方清單，並在每條安全路旁畫一朵花。波波拿出長木板，讓孩子看上面的記號。「這不是玩具，」他說，「這是我們工作用的真工具。」一名孩子問造橋需要幾小時。「不只一個小時。」波波回答，大家都笑了。

A housewife from the west hill brought chocolate for the helpers. “Please collect your food, but leave some for those who arrive later,” she said. Her cakes were popular, yet she did not want anyone to push. Fern gave the main rule for the day: “Stay away from the river until the adults say it is safe.” Nia wrote a note for families who could not come to the class. There was a course for older children too, with simple pictures of safe and unsafe ground.

西邊山丘的一位家庭主婦帶來巧克力給幫手。「可以拿食物，但要留一點給晚到的人。」她說。她的蛋糕很受歡迎，卻不希望大家推擠。芬說明今天最重要的規則：「大人宣布安全之前，不要靠近河流。」妮亞寫了通知給沒來上課的家庭。年紀較大的孩子也有一堂課，用簡單圖片比較安全與危險的地面。

A boy asked when the bridge team could start carrying heavy boards. “Not until the ground test is done,” Bo answered. He pointed to a drawing of three workers. The first worker looked at the mark. The second wrote down the answer. The third checked it again. The boy said, “But that takes time.” Bo agreed. “It does. A fast mistake can take much more time to fix.” Nia was pleased to hear the boy explain the answer to his younger sister.

一個男孩問修橋隊何時能開始搬重木板。「地面測試完成前不行。」波波回答，指著一張三名工人的圖。第一人看記號，第二人寫結果，第三人再檢查一次。男孩說：「可是那要花時間。」波波同意：「是啊，但做錯後要重來，可能更花時間。」妮亞高興地聽見男孩把道理解釋給妹妹聽。

Luma also taught the children how to ask a useful question. “A question is not just a way to show that you know something,” she said. “It can help another person see what is missing.” A little rabbit asked where the team would rest during the rain. That question gave Nia an idea. She moved the rest table to the high path, where it would stay dry. The children's class had already helped the real work.

露瑪也教孩子怎麼提出有用的問題。「提問不只是展示你懂什麼，」她說，「也可以幫別人看見漏掉的地方。」一隻小兔子問下雨時團隊要在哪裡休息。這個問題讓妮亞有了主意：她把休息桌移到高處的小路旁，這樣就不怕受潮。孩子的課堂已經幫到了真正的工程。

At the end, Nia asked every child to point to the new red rope by the river. They could all find it. The lesson had started with a list, but it ended with the children looking after one another. “We can help without touching tools,” said a little rabbit. “We can tell a friend where it is safe to walk.” Nia smiled. The children had understood the most important part of the plan.

課程結束時，妮亞請每位孩子指出河邊新設的紅繩。大家都找得到。這堂課從清單開始，最後卻變成孩子們互相照顧。「我們不用碰工具，也可以幫忙，」一隻小兔子說，「我們可以告訴朋友哪裡能安全走。」妮亞微笑。孩子們懂得了整個計畫最重要的部分。

After the class, the children played on the high field while the adults put up signs. A little bird brought a leaf to a child who was drawing the bridge. The child added it to the picture and said the bridge could have a green place beside it. Luma thought that was a good idea. A tree or a bench near the safe path could give people shade when they waited. She told the child that the first job was to make the path safe, and the green place could come later. The child understood that good ideas could wait their turn.

課後，孩子們在高處的空地玩耍，大人則去立標誌。一隻小鳥帶了片葉子給畫橋的孩子。孩子把葉子放進圖裡，說橋旁可以有綠地。露瑪覺得是好主意。安全路旁若有樹或長椅，人等待時就有遮蔭。她告訴孩子，第一要務是讓道路安全，綠地可以之後再做。孩子懂得好點子也可以排隊等待。

When the afternoon light grew soft, the children brought their drawings to Nia. Some were funny, some were bright, and some showed the bridge with too many flowers. She looked at each one and thanked its maker. Then she chose two pictures that could help people find the safe path. She explained why she could not put every picture on the warning sign. “A sign must be easy to read quickly,” she said. The children were not upset. They made a little wall in the classroom for the other pictures. Nia saw that saying no to one idea did not mean saying no to the child who had made it.

下午光線變柔時，孩子們把畫拿給妮亞。有的有趣、有的鮮豔，還有的把橋畫滿花朵。妮亞逐一欣賞並道謝，接著選出兩張能幫人找到安全路線的圖。她解釋為什麼不能把全部作品都放上警示牌。「標誌必須一眼看懂。」她說。孩子們沒有生氣，而是在教室裡做了作品牆展示其他畫。妮亞發現，拒絕一個點子不等於拒絕提出點子的孩子。
