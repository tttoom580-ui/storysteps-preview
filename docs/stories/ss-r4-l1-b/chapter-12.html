A Place for Every Package

河流兩岸的紅繩 · 第 12 / 25 章 · 1000以下 · 英中對照 · 校訂中

用互動版查詞、朗讀與收藏

At two o’clock in the afternoon, the town engineer arrived with a package of drawing tools. An actor from the village play helped carry it to the hall. His glasses fell when he bent down, and Nia picked them up. “I can carry the small box,” she said. “But I am tired. May we rest first?” The engineer agreed. No one had to act like a hero. They would work more carefully after a short rest. The last heavy box could wait until the next day.

下午兩點，鎮上的工程師帶著一包繪圖工具抵達。一位參加村劇的演員幫忙搬進集會所。他彎腰時眼鏡掉了，妮亞替他撿起來。「小盒子我可以拿，」她說，「但我累了，能先休息嗎？」工程師同意。沒有人必須裝成英雄。短暫休息後，他們反而能更仔細工作。最後那個重箱子可以留到明天。

A band was practicing for the coming village party. One member wanted to celebrate the new plan at once. “Please wait,” said Fern. “A plan is not a finished bridge.” The local media could ask questions, but the group would only share what they knew. Luma put a ticket for the summer play in the corner of the desk, next to the key to the storage room. A volunteer from the supermarket brought more food for the workers. Everyone ate together, whether they had money or were poor.

一支樂團正為即將到來的村莊聚會練習。有位團員想立刻慶祝新計畫。「請再等等，」芬說，「圖紙不等於造好的橋。」地方媒體可以提問，但團隊只會分享確定的資訊。露瑪把夏季戲劇的票放在桌角，旁邊是倉庫的鑰匙。超市的志工帶來更多食物。無論有錢或貧窮，大家都一起吃飯。

The village actor asked whether he could read the safety note aloud at the play. Fern agreed, as long as he did not tell the crowd that the bridge was ready. He practiced the short message with the band. People could hear it before they went home. Nia liked the plan. The actor was not a builder, yet he could help families learn where to walk. She added his name to the work list, beside the cooks and the people who moved wood.

村裡的演員問能否在戲劇演出時念出安全公告。芬同意，只要求別向觀眾說橋已可通行。他與樂團一起練習短公告，讓大家散場前都能聽到。妮亞喜歡這個方法。演員不是造橋工人，卻能幫家庭知道該走哪條路。她把他的名字加進工作清單，和廚師、搬木材的人並列。

Nia later opened the storage room with the key and saw that all the boxes were in the right place. The small tools were in one corner; the heavy ones were near the door. Bo said the order would make the next workday easier. She agreed, but added one more sign: “Ask before taking.” Even a well-meaning helper could make trouble by moving a tool another worker needed. They left the room knowing where each package belonged.

稍後妮亞用鑰匙打開倉庫，發現所有箱子都放在正確位置。小工具放一角，重工具靠門。波波說這樣下次工作更方便。妮亞同意，但又加了一張牌：「取用前先詢問。」即使出於好意，幫手若移動了別人正要用的工具，也可能造成麻煩。他們離開時，都清楚每個包裹該放在哪裡。

The engineer said that no one should die because a community wanted to save a little money. His words made the room quiet. Then he showed them how to test the ground before moving the first post. Nia asked him to explain it again in simple words. He did. She wrote the steps down so that everyone could read them. When he left, she did not feel that the work had become easy. She felt that the village now knew which questions it must answer.

工程師說，不應有人因為村子想省一點錢而喪命。這句話讓房間安靜下來。接著他示範如何在移動第一根柱子前測試地面。妮亞請他用簡單的話再說一次，他答應了。她把步驟記下來，讓所有人都能讀。工程師離開時，她並不覺得工作突然變容易了，而是覺得村子終於知道必須回答哪些問題。

When the engineer explained the ground test, Nia asked him to show the tool without putting it near the river. He used a box of sand on the hall floor. Everyone could see what happened when the weight moved. A child asked why the first bridge had lasted so many years and now needed to close. The engineer said wood changed with time, rain, and use. The safe answer was to check it, not to trust it only because it had worked yesterday. Nia wrote that sentence beside the picture of the old bridge.

工程師說明地面測試時，妮亞請他先在遠離河水的地方示範工具。他在集會所地板上用一箱沙做示範。大家都看得見重量移動時發生什麼事。一個孩子問，舊橋用了多年，為什麼現在要封閉。工程師說木頭會隨時間、雨水和使用而改變。安全的答案是檢查，不是只因昨天還能用就相信今天也可以。妮亞把這句話寫在舊橋照片旁。

The engineer stayed until the light began to fade. He asked Nia whether she had understood the difference between a drawing and a test. She answered that a drawing showed what they hoped would work, while a test showed what happened under set conditions. He smiled and said she had understood. Then he asked what she would do if the test gave a bad result. “Stop and change the plan,” she replied. He nodded. Nia felt she had learned more than how to build a bridge. She had learned how to tell the difference between hope and proof, and why both mattered in their own ways.

工程師待到天色漸暗，問妮亞是否理解圖紙和測試的差別。她回答，圖紙畫出大家希望可行的做法，測試則顯示在指定條件下真正發生什麼。他微笑，說她懂了。接著他問如果測試結果不好怎麼辦。「停下來，修改計畫。」她回答。工程師點頭。妮亞覺得自己學到的不只是造橋，還有如何分辨希望與證據，以及兩者各自為何重要。
