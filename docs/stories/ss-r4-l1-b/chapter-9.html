農夫的鐵環

The Farmer's Ring

河流兩岸的紅繩 · 第 9 / 25 章 · 1000以下 · 英中對照 · 校訂中

用互動版查詞、朗讀與收藏

A bear farmer named Sol came from the far hill with a wooden box. Inside was a strong iron ring that his father had used to lift stones. “Keep it safe,” he told Bo. “I would be sad to lose it.” Sol had been lonely since his wife moved to another village for work, but he was glad to have a useful job. Nia took a photograph of the ring and wrote whose it was on a small card. A gift and a loan were not the same thing. The card would help them return the ring when the work was done.

住在遠山丘的熊農夫索爾帶來一個木盒。盒裡有個堅固的鐵環，是他父親以前吊石頭用的。「好好保管，」他對波波說，「弄丟我會很難過。」自從妻子到鄰村工作後，索爾常感到孤單，但他很高興能做有用的事。妮亞替鐵環拍照，並在小卡上寫明它屬於誰。禮物和借物不同，卡片能幫他們在工程結束後把鐵環還回去。

A foreigner passing through the village asked if there was a warm place to eat. Luma took him to the meeting room. A singer was resting there with a cup of milk, a little meat, and some bread. The visitor pointed to the wall, where a picture showed the old bridge. “In my home village,” he said, “we keep a book of every repair.” He was brave enough to offer advice, but he did not pretend to know this river. He also asked where the toilet was. Everyone smiled at this very ordinary question.

一位路過村子的外地人問哪裡能吃到熱食。露瑪帶他去集會室。一位歌手正在那裡休息，桌上有牛奶、少量肉和麵包。訪客指著牆上的舊橋照片說：「在我的家鄉，我們有一本記下每次修理的書。」他勇於提出建議，卻沒有假裝了解這條河。他還問洗手間在哪裡，大家聽到這平常的問題都笑了。

Sol told Nia that every person in the village had something different to give. Some could lift wood; some could cook; some could look after children. He had once thought that only strong animals were useful at a building site. Now he saw the careful cards Nia made for borrowed things. “Without your list, I might never see my ring again,” he said. Nia laughed. “Then our list is just as useful as a big tool.” He agreed, and for the first time he offered to help write in the book.

索爾告訴妮亞，村裡每個人都能提供不同的幫助。有人搬木頭，有人煮飯，有人照顧孩子。他以前以為只有強壯的動物能幫工地的忙，現在看見妮亞為借用物品做的卡片，才改變想法。「沒有妳的清單，我可能再也找不到鐵環。」他說。妮亞笑了：「那我們的清單就跟大工具一樣有用。」他同意，也第一次主動提出幫忙記錄。

In the afternoon, the ring was tested with a small load on dry ground. Bo made sure it held firm, then took it off and put it back in the marked box. They did not leave it outside in the rain. The visitor from the other village watched with interest. He said he would take the idea of marked loan boxes back home. Nia liked that thought: useful knowledge could travel, even when a broken bridge stopped people for a time.

下午，團隊在乾地上用少量重量測試鐵環。波波確認它牢固，再把它拆下放回有標記的盒子。他們沒有把它留在雨中。鄰村訪客興致勃勃地觀看，說會把借物標記箱的做法帶回自己的家鄉。妮亞很喜歡這個想法：即使壞橋一時阻礙了人們，實用的知識仍可以傳出去。

Bo said that nothing would be placed above the water until the new bank posts were ready. Nia agreed. She made another card for the iron ring, marked it with a date, and put it beside the tool box. Sol watched her work and said, “Well done.” Later, when the room grew quiet, Nia saw him looking at the other helpers. She invited him to come back tomorrow. He had given them a tool, but they could give him friendship in return.

波波說，新河岸的柱子沒準備好以前，什麼都不會裝到水面上。妮亞同意。她替鐵環做了另一張卡片，寫上日期，放在工具箱旁。索爾看著她做，說：「做得很好。」之後，房間安靜下來，妮亞發現他在望著其他幫手，便邀他明天再來。他借給大家一件工具，而大家可以用友誼回報。

At home Sol wrote a letter to his wife. He told her he was helping to build a safe bridge and that the young workers had remembered his father's tool. He did not say the bridge was already built. He said the work was slow, and people took time to check every part. When his wife wrote back, she said she was proud that he was making new friends. Sol read the letter with a smile. The bridge was still only a plan and a few posts, yet it was already joining people in a different way.

回家後索爾寫信給妻子。他告訴她自己正幫忙造安全的新橋，年輕工人也珍惜父親留下的工具。他沒有說橋已經完成，只說進度緩慢，大家花時間檢查每個零件。妻子回信說，很高興他交到新朋友。索爾微笑讀信。新橋仍只有計畫和幾根柱子，卻已用另一種方式把人們連在一起。

On the following day Sol helped build a low shelf for the borrowed tools. He worked with another farmer, and they talked while they made it. He learned that many people in the village had felt alone during the long rainy weeks. The closed bridge had made it harder to visit friends, but working together gave them a place to meet. Nia thanked Sol for the shelf. He said he wanted to keep helping even after his iron ring was returned. Bo laughed and offered him a chair at the next planning meeting. Sol said yes before anyone had to ask him again.

隔天索爾幫忙做一個放借用工具的低架子。他和另一位農夫邊做邊聊天，知道村裡許多人在漫長雨季都曾感到孤單。橋封閉後探訪朋友更困難，但一起工作給了大家相聚的地方。妮亞謝謝索爾做架子。索爾說即使鐵環歸還後，他也想繼續幫忙。波波笑著邀他參加下次規劃會議，索爾馬上答應，不必再問第二次。
