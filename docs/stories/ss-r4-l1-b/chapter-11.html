白色的線

The White Line

河流兩岸的紅繩 · 第 11 / 25 章 · 1000以下 · 英中對照 · 校訂中

The next morning, Fern brought a basket of tools. A thin white belt from an old bag was inside. Bo used it to show where the new rail would stand. “Check every mark,” he said. “A small mistake at the start can hurt someone later.” A player from the village ball team offered to lift heavy wood, but Fern asked about his health first. He had a bad knee and had been to the hospital last month. They gave him a useful job with paper and pictures instead. Nobody wanted him to hurt himself for the bridge.

隔天早上，芬帶來一籃工具，裡面有舊袋子上的一條細白帶。波波用它示範新欄杆的位置。「每個記號都要檢查，」他說，「開始時的小錯誤，之後可能傷到人。」村裡球隊的一位球員想幫忙搬重木材，但芬先關心他的健康。他的膝蓋不好，上個月還去過醫院。大家改請他整理紙張與圖片，那同樣是有用的工作。沒有人希望他為了造橋受傷。

Luma sent a drawing to the families on the west hill. “Dear friends,” she wrote, “if you see an old sign fall, please tell us. Do not fix it by yourself.” The children made a kite in the library while the adults worked. One boy made a joke about painting the bridge purple. Luma laughed, but she said they would need clear colors for safety signs. Those signs had to be easy to see in rain. She often reminded the children that being helpful did not mean running into danger.

露瑪把圖寄給西山丘的家庭。「親愛的朋友們，」她寫，「如果看到舊標誌倒下，請告訴我們，不要自己修。」成人工作時，孩子們在圖書室做風箏。一個男孩開玩笑說要把橋漆成紫色。露瑪笑了，但提醒安全標誌要用清楚的顏色。那些標誌在雨中也必須容易看見。她常提醒孩子，幫忙不代表要跑進危險裡。

“The color of a sign matters,” Luma said. She showed two pictures. One had a red stop line; the other had only pale gray words. Everyone saw the first one more quickly. “We should send the stronger picture to the far hill,” she decided. Nia made a copy while Bo dried the white belt and put it away. They had found that a simple change could help people see danger sooner. The picture was not a pretty decoration. It did a real job for the village.

「標誌的顏色很重要。」露瑪說。她拿出兩張圖。一張有紅色停止線，另一張只有淡灰字。大家都更快看見第一張。「我們應該把比較清楚的圖送去遠山丘。」她決定。妮亞影印圖紙，波波把白帶晾乾收好。他們發現小小的改變，就能讓人更早看出危險。這張圖不是漂亮的裝飾，而是在替村子做實際的事。

At the hall, Fern checked the names of the people who had offered help. She found that one woman had written her name twice by mistake. Fern did not count her as two helpers. She asked the woman which day she really wanted to work. The woman chose the next morning. Nia smiled at this small act of care. A list could be made of marks and names, but it was still about real people with real needs.

在集會所裡，芬檢查願意幫忙的人名，發現一位女子誤把自己的名字寫了兩次。芬沒有把她算成兩位幫手，而是問她真正想在哪天來。女子選了隔天早上。妮亞喜歡這樣細心的處理。清單由名字和記號組成，但背後仍是有真實需要的人。

Nia did not hate the slow work of checking. She liked knowing that every mark had a reason. When they found one weak piece of wood, Bo put it in a separate basket. He did not try to hide it under good wood. Fern made a picture of the pile and added it to the report. By the end of the day, they had fewer pieces than before, but they knew more about what they could safely use.

妮亞不討厭緩慢的檢查工作。她喜歡知道每個記號都有原因。找到一塊脆弱的木頭時，波波把它另放進籃子，沒有藏在好木頭底下。芬拍下木堆照片，加進紀錄。那天結束時，可用木料比原先預想的少了，但大家更清楚哪些東西能安全使用。

The ball player with the bad knee was good at drawing straight lines. He made a new sign with clear arrows and put it beside the rest area. “I thought I could only help by carrying wood,” he told Nia. “Now I see that a picture can help just as much.” Nia thanked him. She remembered how he had looked sad when Fern asked him not to lift heavy things. His smile today showed that giving someone a safe job was not the same as telling them they were not needed.

膝蓋不好的球員很擅長畫直線。他做了一張有清楚箭頭的新標誌，放在休息區旁。「我以前以為只能搬木頭才算幫忙，」他對妮亞說，「現在發現一張圖也能很有用。」妮亞謝謝他。她記得芬請他不要搬重物時，他曾經很難過。今天他的笑容說明，安排安全的工作，不等於告訴別人不被需要。

The work group also practiced saying the safety rule aloud. One person pointed to the red sign, another said where the open road began, and a third showed the rest place. They changed roles and tried again. It felt like a simple game, and some helpers laughed. Yet Nia saw that the same answer came from different people. If one helper was home sick, another could still tell visitors what to do. Fern said this was how a village kept a useful plan alive: more than one person had to understand it. They put the new pictures by the doors before the next rain came.

工作隊也練習大聲說出安全規則。一人指紅牌，另一人說明開放道路從哪開始，第三人示範休息處的位置。大家交換角色再試一次。過程像簡單遊戲，有人笑了。妮亞卻注意到，換不同的人仍能給出相同答案。如果某位幫手生病在家，其他人也能指引訪客。芬說，這就是村子讓好計畫持續的方法：不只一個人要懂。下場雨前，他們把新圖片貼到門邊。
