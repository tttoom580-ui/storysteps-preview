村子的共同基金

The Small Village Fund

河流兩岸的紅繩 · 第 5 / 25 章 · 1000以下 · 英中對照

The village had a little money in the bank, but not enough for a new bridge. A farmer named May offered wood from a tree that had fallen on her farm. She did not want to sell wood that the animals might later need for food or homes. Bo went to feed the farm animals and thanked her. He carried a yellow letter to Fern. The letter said exactly which wood May could give. Nia read it twice. There were many ways to help, and not all of them meant giving money.

村子的銀行裡有一些存款，但不夠造新橋。一位名叫梅的農夫願意捐出農場上倒下的樹木。她不想把動物日後蓋家或取得食物所需的木材拿去賣。波波先去餵農場動物，再向她道謝。他帶了一封黃色的信給芬，上面清楚寫著梅願意提供哪些木頭。妮亞讀了兩遍。幫忙的方法很多，並不全是捐錢。

A teenager from the hill asked where the good rope was kept. He had seen a movie in which someone crossed a river on a string. “That looks easy,” he said. Bo shook his head. “A thin string is not a bridge.” He put a little oil on a tool, then moved it slowly so that nobody would hurt a toe. Moonlight came through the window of the work room. Nia thought about the strange film and the real river. The real world needed careful work, not a quick trick from a story.

山丘上的一位青少年問大家好繩子放在哪裡。他看過一部電影，裡面有人沿著細繩過河。「看起來很容易。」他說。波波搖頭：「細繩不能當橋。」他在工具上抹了一點油，再慢慢移動工具，免得傷到誰的腳趾。月光穿過工作室的窗戶。妮亞想著那部奇怪的電影和真正的河。現實需要仔細施工，不是故事裡的快速把戲。

The next evening a full moon rose over the river. Nia sat with Bo and tried to think of an answer to the money problem. They had a slow talk about what the village really needed. The children needed a safe way to school. The old mouse needed a rest place on the hill road. Farmers needed room for their carts. No one needed a costly sign or a fine new name for the bridge. Nia made a list of needs before anyone suggested spending more money.

隔天傍晚，滿月升到河面上方。妮亞和波波坐在一起，試著思考經費問題。他們慢慢討論村子真正需要什麼。孩子需要安全上學的路，老老鼠需要山路休息處，農夫需要推車通過的空間。沒有人需要昂貴招牌或漂亮的新橋名字。妮亞先列出需求，再讓大家提出花費建議。

Bo showed Nia how he kept old nails apart from new ones. Some could still be used for small things in the tool room, but not for the main bridge. He put them in different boxes and wrote a clear word on each lid. Nia liked this simple order. It did not make the village rich, but it kept good things from being wasted and weak things from being used in the wrong place. They were learning to be careful with every small gift.

波波給妮亞看他如何把舊釘子和新釘子分開。部分舊釘子仍可用於工具室的小東西，但不能用在主橋上。他把釘子放進不同盒子，盒蓋各寫清楚名稱。妮亞喜歡這樣簡單的整理方式。它不會讓村子有錢，卻能避免浪費好東西，也能防止脆弱物品用錯地方。他們正學著珍惜每份小小的心意。

Fern put a straight line across a sheet of paper. “The topic today is what we can safely use,” she said. One family could give nails; another could make lunch; another could look after young children. The group would write each gift down and ask before taking it. That way nobody would lose the thing they needed at home. Nia went to bed with a small but good feeling. They had little money, yet they were beginning to find more than money.

芬在紙上畫了一條筆直的線。「今天的主題是哪些東西能安全使用。」她說。一家能提供釘子，另一家能做午餐，還有一家能照顧小孩。團隊會記下每份心意，取用前先徵求同意，這樣沒有人會失去家中需要的東西。妮亞帶著小小的好心情上床睡覺。他們錢不多，卻開始找到比金錢更重要的東西。

The next morning the farmer showed the helpers where the fallen tree had been. Its roots were still in the ground, and small birds had made homes in the branches. Bo said they should take only the wood already cut and placed away from the birds. May agreed. Nia wrote the amount down and thanked the farmer. When they reached the village, some neighbors offered to carry the wood for free. Nia told them the work would be heavy and that it was fine to say no. The new bridge should begin with willing hands, not with people who felt forced to help.

隔天早上，農夫帶幫手去看倒樹原來的位置。樹根還在地裡，小鳥已在樹枝間築巢。波波說應只取已切好、放在不會打擾鳥的地方的木頭。梅同意了。妮亞記下數量，向農夫道謝。回到村裡，一些鄰居願意免費搬木頭。妮亞提醒工作很重，也可以拒絕。新橋應從自願幫忙的手開始，而不是讓人被迫出力。

Fern opened the village book and made three spaces on a new page. The first was for gifts, the second for things people lent, and the third for things they would have to buy. Nia helped her write the names. A piece of wood from May's farm went in the gift space. Sol's iron ring would go in the loan space when it arrived. Nails that no family could give went in the buying space. The plan was simple enough for children to read. When the workers knew where each thing came from, they could also know whom to thank and what had to go home after the bridge was finished.

芬翻開村莊紀錄本，在新頁上分三個區域。第一欄記禮物，第二欄記借物，第三欄記必須購買的東西。妮亞幫忙寫姓名。梅的農場木材放在禮物欄，索爾的鐵環送來後要放在借物欄，沒有家庭能提供的釘子則列在購買欄。計畫簡單到孩子也看得懂。當工人知道每樣東西從何而來，也就知道該謝謝誰，以及完工後哪些東西必須歸還。
