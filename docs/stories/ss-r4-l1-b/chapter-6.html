河流記得的事

What the River Remembers

河流兩岸的紅繩 · 第 6 / 25 章 · 1000以下 · 英中對照 · 校訂中

Bo took Nia to the old village shop, where a long desk stood by the door. Inside a drawer was an envelope full of papers about the bridge's history. Some pages showed the river after a very deep flood. One old picture showed fish near the top of the bank. “The water may rise higher than you think,” Bo said. Nia held the papers with one arm and used her other hand to keep them dry. She wanted to find the best place for the new bridge before the next rain.

波波帶妮亞去村裡的舊商店，門邊有張長書桌。抽屜裡有一個裝滿舊橋歷史文件的信封。有些頁面畫著大洪水過後的河流。其中一張舊照片顯示魚幾乎游到岸頂。「水可能漲得比妳想的高。」波波說。妮亞用一隻手臂抱著文件，另一隻手護住它們不被弄濕。她想在下一場雨前找出新橋的最佳位置。

The mud at the first site was thick. A seat on the old viewing path had fallen into it. Luma made a funny face when her boot stuck, but she did not step farther. “We have news,” Nia said when they returned. “This ground is not good enough.” Some people were surprised. They thought the nearest place would also be the most convenient. Fern welcomed their questions and let each person speak. “However,” she said, “a short walk is not worth a broken bridge.”

第一個地點的泥巴很厚。舊觀景路上的一張椅子已倒進泥裡。露瑪的靴子卡住時，做了個好笑的表情，但她沒有繼續前進。回來後妮亞說：「我們有新消息，這裡的土地不夠穩固。」有些人很驚訝；他們以為最近的地方也一定最方便。芬歡迎提問，讓每個人都能發言。「但是，」她說，「少走一點路，不值得賭上一座不安全的橋。」

“You are welcome to read these papers,” said the old shop owner. Nia thanked her and asked whether she could copy one page. The owner said yes, but asked her to keep the original in the dry drawer. Nia understood. The village history belonged to many people, and she should not take it without asking. Bo helped hold the paper flat while she copied the old water line. They carried the copy to the meeting, not the old book.

「歡迎妳來讀這些文件。」老店主說。妮亞道謝，問能否抄下一頁。店主同意，但希望原件留在乾燥的抽屜。妮亞明白，村子的歷史屬於許多人，不能不問就帶走。波波幫忙壓住紙張，讓她抄下舊水位線。他們拿著副本去開會，原書則留在店裡。

The old papers also spoke about a flood long before Nia was born. There was a short note about families who shared food when the path washed away. Nia read it out to the helpers. Some smiled and some grew quiet. She realized that her work was not a new problem with a new answer. People before her had faced the same river and had made hard choices. Their small notes could still help the village today.

舊文件還記著妮亞出生前很久的一場洪水，短短的筆記寫到小路被沖掉時，幾戶人家如何分享食物。妮亞念給幫手聽。有些人微笑，有些人安靜下來。她發現自己面對的不是一個全新的問題，也不是需要突然出現的新答案。前人同樣面對這條河、作出艱難的選擇，他們留下的小筆記如今仍能幫上忙。

Back at the meeting room, Nia asked, “What do we do now?” Bo spread out the old papers. He found a mark made during an earlier flood. It was higher than all their new marks. “We can learn from it,” he said. Fern thanked him for looking at the past. They did not finish their plan that day, but they made a better list of questions. The river had given them no easy answer. It had given them a reason to be careful.

回到集會室，妮亞問：「現在怎麼辦？」波波攤開舊文件，找到早年洪水留下的記號，比他們現在畫的都高。「我們可以從這個記號學習。」他說。芬謝謝他查閱過去的資料。他們當天沒有完成設計，卻整理出更好的問題清單。河流沒有給他們簡單答案，卻提醒他們必須謹慎。

Nia asked Fern what would happen if the river rose during the night. Fern showed her the old warning bell beside the meeting hall. The bell could call people to the high road, but nobody was to run down to the water. They checked the rope on the bell and made sure children knew the sound. Nia liked that this warning plan did not need a brave person standing in the rain. It needed people to hear, understand, and move away from danger. She wrote the bell rule in large letters at the top of the safety page.

妮亞問芬，如果夜裡河水上漲會怎樣。芬帶她看集會所旁的舊警示鐘。鐘聲能召大家往高路去，但任何人都不該跑下河邊。她們檢查鐘繩，也讓孩子認得鐘聲。妮亞喜歡這個不需要有人勇敢站在雨中的警報辦法。它需要大家聽到、理解，再離開危險。她用大字把警鐘規則寫在安全頁最上方。

Nia carried the copied flood page to the new site. She stood on the high path and looked from the old mark to the ground beside the river. Bo showed her where the first test would take place, away from the moving water. “I would like to begin today,” he said, “but the ground is too wet.” Nia agreed and put the paper back in its cover. They walked to the meeting hall and told Fern exactly what they had seen. There was no great new story to tell, only a clear report. Fern said a short true report was worth more than a long story built out of guesses. Nia wrote that down too.

妮亞把抄下的洪水頁帶到新工地。她站在高路上，從舊記號望向河旁土地。波波指出第一次測試的位置，遠離流動的水。「我希望今天就開始，」他說，「可是地太濕了。」妮亞同意，把紙放回護套。他們走回集會所，清楚向芬報告看見的事。沒有精彩的新故事可說，只有簡短明確的紀錄。芬說短短的真實報告，勝過一長段猜測組成的故事。妮亞也把這句話記下來。
