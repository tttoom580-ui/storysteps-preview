不施工的夜晚

A Night with No Work

河流兩岸的紅繩 · 第 10 / 25 章 · 1000以下 · 英中對照 · 校訂完成

The sky became dark early in the evening. A cold sound came from the river, and leaves began to fall around the work table. Nia checked that the children had gone home. One little student had lost a button from his coat. She found it near a rose bush and gave it back. “You usually stay late,” Luma said. “Please go home tonight.” Nia looked at the wet path. She knew that staying longer would not help. They could watch the river from the safe road, but they would not work in the dark.

傍晚天空很早就暗了。河邊傳來低低的聲響，葉子落在工作桌四周。妮亞確認孩子們都回家了。一名小學生外套的鈕扣掉了，她在玫瑰花叢旁找到並還給他。「妳通常待得很晚，」露瑪說，「今晚請回家。」妮亞看著濕滑的小路，知道多待也沒有用。他們可以從安全的道路觀察河水，卻不能在黑暗中施工。

In the hall, a family had made sweet potato soup. On the menu were bread and tea too. Bo sat around the long table with the others. “The sound of the water makes me worry,” said Luma. Fern smiled gently. “Worry can tell us to be careful. It must not make us do foolish things.” A visitor told an interesting story about rain in another valley. Nia listened, but she knew a story was not a measurement. They needed the marks on their own river bank.

集會所裡，有一家人煮了甜馬鈴薯湯。菜單上還有麵包和茶。波波和其他人圍著長桌坐下。「河水的聲音讓我擔心。」露瑪說。芬溫柔地笑了：「擔心可以提醒我們謹慎，但不能讓我們做傻事。」一位訪客講了另一個山谷下雨的趣事。妮亞聽著，卻知道故事不是測量。他們需要的是自己河岸上的記號。

Luma picked up the button and put it in the child's hand. The child gave her a small smile. There were only two buttons left on his coat, but he was warm enough to walk home. Nia checked the high road one more time from the meeting hall window. The signs were still standing. She wrote that fact in the log and closed the book. Some people would call this an empty day, but she knew the village had done something important: it had followed its own safety rule.

露瑪把鈕扣放回孩子手裡，孩子露出小小的微笑。他外套上只剩兩顆鈕扣，但仍穿得夠暖，可以回家。妮亞從集會所窗邊再看了一次高處道路，標誌仍然站著。她把事實記進紀錄本，然後合上書。有些人會覺得這是沒有成果的一天，但她知道村子完成了重要的事：大家遵守了自己的安全規則。

Bo said there was little wood left to sort, but that did not mean the work was done. The next step was to wait for dry weather, then check the marks on the bank. Fern agreed. She gave each worker a clear time to return so nobody would stand outside in the rain and wonder where the others were. Nia walked home more slowly than usual and slept well. She would be ready to make good choices again in the morning.

波波說要分類的木頭所剩不多，但這不代表工程已完成。下一步是等天乾，再檢查河岸的記號。芬同意，替每位工人安排明確的返回時間，免得有人站在雨中等別人，還不知道大家在哪裡。妮亞比平常走得更慢，回家後睡得很好。隔天早上，她會有精神再次作出好選擇。

When the rain became louder, Bo asked if someone should run to the bridge. “No,” Nia answered. “We have already placed signs and told the village. We can check them in daylight.” She stayed to help wash the bowls, then walked home with Luma. They had made no new bridge parts that day. Still, they had kept everyone away from trouble. Nia began to see that a safe stop could be a good day's work.

雨聲變大時，波波問是否該有人跑去橋邊。「不要，」妮亞回答，「我們已經放好標誌，也通知全村了。白天再去檢查。」她留下幫忙洗碗，然後與露瑪一起走回家。那天他們沒有做出任何新橋零件，卻讓大家遠離危險。妮亞開始明白，適時停工也可能是一天的重要成果。

Nia heard the warning bell only once that evening. It was a practice ring, made from the high road so nobody had to go near the river. The sound passed over the village roofs. Some animals came to the window; others moved to the meeting hall as planned. Fern thanked them for answering calmly. She said a warning was useful only when people knew what to do after hearing it. Nia checked the names of the families who had heard the bell. She would visit the two families that had not answered, but only when the weather was safe.

那天傍晚妮亞只聽見一次警鐘，那是從高路上進行的演練，不必靠近河水。鐘聲傳過村裡屋頂。一些動物走到窗前，其他人依計畫前往集會所。芬謝謝大家沉著應對，說警報只有在大家知道聽見後該做什麼時才有用。妮亞清點聽到鐘聲的家庭，打算等天氣安全再去拜訪沒有回應的兩家。

When Nia woke the next morning, the air was fresh. She did not run to the river. She ate breakfast first, read the night's notes, and went to the meeting hall at the set time. Bo and Luma were there already. They compared the weather report with the marks seen from the high road. The rain had stopped, but the bank was still wet. Fern said the team would use the day to mend signs and prepare tools. It was not the work Bo had hoped for, yet he agreed it was the work they could do safely. Nia began to trust this steady way of making decisions.

隔天妮亞醒來時，空氣很清新。她沒有立刻跑去河邊，而是先吃早餐、看夜間紀錄，再按約定時間到集會所。波波和露瑪已經到了。他們把天氣報告與從高路上看見的水位記號比較。雨停了，但河岸仍濕。芬決定今天修標誌、準備工具。這不是波波原本想做的工作，他仍同意這是目前能安全完成的事。妮亞開始信任這種穩穩作決定的方法。
