模型不能只靠猜

A Model, Not a Guess

河流兩岸的紅繩 · 第 8 / 25 章 · 1000以下 · 英中對照 · 校訂中

用互動版查詞、朗讀與收藏

Bo opened a book from the village library. The drawing inside showed how a bridge could hold a heavy cart. He said that a plan might look boring, but it could save lives. On the table were a fork, a roll of bread, and a file of old drawings. Each helper got one small job. Nia would count the wood. Luma would listen to people's needs. Fern would tell the village what was known and what was still an idea. Nobody would call the plan successful before a safe test.

波波打開從村圖書室借來的書。裡面的圖畫說明橋如何承受重車。他說計畫也許看起來無聊，卻能救命。桌上放著叉子、小餐包，以及裝著舊圖的檔案夾。每位幫手分到一項小工作。妮亞負責點木料，露瑪聽取村民需要，芬則向全村說明已知的事和仍只是想法的部分。在安全測試之前，沒人可以宣稱計畫成功。

A toy train stood near the museum wall. Bo used it to show how weight moved from one end of a bridge to the other. “Here is a mistake,” he said, pointing to a thin piece. “This part is too weak.” At noon, the queen of the garden bees flew past the window. Luma laughed at the grand name and counted the small coins in the box. They had only a few dollars left for nails. “What else can we use?” Nia asked. They had to look for sound wood, not simply cheap wood.

博物館牆邊放著一列玩具火車。波波用它說明重量如何從橋的一端移向另一端。「這裡有個錯誤，」他指著薄木片說，「這一段太弱。」中午，花園裡的蜂后飛過窗邊。露瑪笑著聽這個氣派的名字，一面清點盒裡的小錢。他們只剩幾元能買釘子。「還能用什麼？」妮亞問。他們得找結實的木材，而不能只找便宜的木材。

“It might be dangerous to make our model look too easy,” Bo said. “The real bridge will have water and moving carts under and over it.” He wrote one dollar on a card to show what a small part cost, then showed how many small parts they needed. Luma checked the count with him. The price was not high for one part, but many parts could cost a lot together. They kept a little money for parts that might need to be changed after a test.

「把模型做得太簡單，可能會很危險。」波波說，「真正的橋會面對流動的水和移動的推車。」他在卡片上寫下一元，示範一個小零件的價格，再說明需要多少個零件。露瑪和他一起核對數量。單個零件不貴，很多加起來卻可能花不少錢。他們保留一點預算，應付測試後可能需要更換的零件。

Later, Nia carried the model to a meeting with the older animals. She did not ask them to approve it only because it looked nice. She asked which part they could not understand and what they feared most. Mrs. Pine pointed to the space for walking. “A cart and an old turtle need enough room,” she said. Nia added that note to the file. A good plan could change before anyone picked up a hammer. That was its great strength.

稍後妮亞把模型帶到年長動物的會議。她不會只因模型好看就請大家通過，而是問哪部分不懂、最擔心什麼。松太太指著通行的空間說：「推車和老烏龜都需要足夠寬的地方。」妮亞把意見加進檔案。好計畫可以在任何人拿槌子前改變，這正是它的優點。

“You may speak even if you are new,” Fern told the young helpers. A little mouse pointed out that the model had no rail at the sides. Bo stopped and looked again. “Thank you,” he said. “I did not see that.” They drew two rails on the plan. The room was full of good questions. Nia wrote every change in the file so that nobody would forget why the plan looked different tomorrow.

「即使剛加入，也可以發言。」芬對年輕幫手說。一隻小老鼠指出模型兩側沒有欄杆。波波停下來再看一次。「謝謝，」他說，「我剛才沒發現。」大家在設計圖上補畫兩道欄杆。屋裡滿是好問題。妮亞把每次修改記入檔案，讓大家明天不會忘記設計為何不同。

That afternoon Nia asked Bo why the model had been useful even though it was too small to carry anyone. Bo said it helped people ask questions before they spent money or touched the river. They had found the missing rail, changed the walking space, and learned which parts needed better wood. “That is a lot for a little model,” Nia said. Bo smiled. He was not ashamed that the first drawing had been wrong. Each change had made the next drawing better. They packed the model in a box so others could use it at the next meeting.

下午妮亞問波波，為什麼模型小到不能載人，卻如此有用。波波說它讓大家在花錢、碰河水之前先問問題。他們發現欄杆缺失、修改通行空間，也知道哪些地方需要更好的木頭。「一個小模型做了好多事。」妮亞說。波波微笑，並不因第一張圖畫錯而羞愧。每次修改都讓下一張更好。他們把模型收進箱子，留給下次會議使用。

Before the meeting ended, Fern asked the helpers to say one part of the model that still needed checking. Bo named the rails. Luma named the space for carts. Nia named the ground under the first posts. The young mouse named the places where children might put their hands. Fern wrote all four on the board. She thanked the mouse again for noticing something the older helpers had missed. The little animal stood taller after that. Nia saw that asking a question could make someone feel like a true member of a team. She took the list home to read before the next meeting.

會議結束前，芬請每位幫手說出模型仍需檢查的一部分。波波說欄杆，露瑪說推車通道，妮亞說第一批柱子下方的地面，小老鼠說孩子可能把手放上去的位置。芬把四點全寫上板，並再次感謝小老鼠看見大人漏掉的事。小傢伙站得更挺了。妮亞發現，提出問題能讓一個人感受到自己真是團隊的一員。她帶清單回家，準備下次會議前再看。
