每個人的地圖

A Map for Everyone

河流兩岸的紅繩 · 第 3 / 25 章 · 1000以下 · 英中對照

All the helpers met in an open area near the river. They wanted to begin with a map, not a hammer. Luma drew the houses, the field for basketball, and the garden with one orange flower. Bo put a lamp on the table so everyone could see the paper. Fern asked the team to define a safe path: a path that did not touch the old bridge or the soft river bank. Nia drew three paths, then used an eraser to remove the one beside the deep mud. There were only a few good choices.

所有幫手在河邊的空地集合。他們決定先畫地圖，而不是急著拿槌子。露瑪畫下房屋、打籃球的場地，以及開著一朵橘色花的花園。波波在桌上放一盞燈，讓大家看得清紙張。芬請大家定義安全路線：不能碰舊橋，也不能走鬆軟的河岸。妮亞畫了三條路，再用橡皮擦掉靠近泥地的一條。真正可用的選擇不多。

Kit offered to send an email to the town office and to carry ordinary mail to houses across the hill. “A modern machine can send news quickly,” he said, “but a letter still needs feet.” The helpers watched carts use the road. There was less traffic at night, but the road was hard to see. At a quarter past seven pm, Fern asked everyone to leave the river. They could plant small signs tomorrow. Nia wrote in her book, “We will not work in the dark.” It was a simple rule that anyone could understand.

奇特主動幫忙寄電子郵件到鎮公所，並沿山路替住戶送一般郵件。「現代機器能迅速傳訊，」他說，「但信仍要靠腳送到。」幫手們觀察車子使用山路。夜晚交通比較少，路卻更難看清。晚上七點十五分，芬請大家離開河邊。明天再來立小標誌。妮亞在筆記本寫：「我們不在黑暗中施工。」這是人人都懂的簡單規則。

Nia took the map to the far side of the village. The small homes were close together there, and carts used the same narrow road as children. She asked one family when the road was busy. “Early morning and late afternoon,” they answered. The children showed her a place where they waited for the carts to pass. Nia drew that place with a thick line. The bridge team needed to think about the road around the bridge, not only the wood above the water.

妮亞把地圖帶到村子的遠端。那裡小房子挨得很近，推車和孩子共用狹窄的路。她問一家人什麼時候車最多。「清晨和傍晚。」他們回答。孩子們帶她看平常停下來等車通過的位置。妮亞用粗線畫下來。造橋團隊不只要想水面上的木頭，也得考慮橋周圍的道路。

Back in the hall, Fern asked each helper to explain one part of the map in plain words. Bo explained the river bank. Luma explained the homes and gardens. Nia explained why the hill road would stay open until the bridge was truly safe. No one had to speak for a long time. They only had to make one point that others could understand. The map looked less pretty than Luma's first drawing, but it told a much better story.

回到集會所，芬請每位幫手用簡單的話解釋地圖的一部分。波波說明河岸，露瑪解釋房屋和花園，妮亞說明為什麼新橋真正安全之前，山路都會保持開放。沒有人需要講很久，只要清楚說出一件別人能理解的事。地圖雖不如露瑪最初的畫漂亮，卻說明了更完整的情況。

On the way home, Bo stopped beside a small plant that had grown out of the old road. He did not pull it up. “A road is for people,” he said, “but it is part of a home for other living things.” Luma drew the plant on the map too. Nia felt that their work was growing larger. They were not only choosing where to walk. They were learning how to make a choice that was safe and fair for the whole village.

回家的路上，波波在舊道路邊一株小植物前停下。他沒有把它拔掉。「路是給大家走的，」他說，「可是它也是其他生命的家園一部分。」露瑪也把這株植物畫上地圖。妮亞覺得工作變得更大了。他們不只是在選走路的位置，也在學著替整個村子作出安全而公平的選擇。

At home Nia hung the map above her desk. There were lines that she liked and lines that she did not like, but she knew the drawing was still a work in progress. She took a fresh paper and copied the safest route for families who might need it tomorrow. She did not draw a smiling animal on the old bridge, because that might make it look open. Instead she drew a red rope and a clear stop sign. The map was not there to make her look clever. It was there to help people choose the right road.

回家後妮亞把地圖掛在書桌上方。圖上有她喜歡和不喜歡的線，但她知道設計還在進行中。她拿新紙張，抄下一條最安全的路給明天可能需要的家庭。她沒有在舊橋上畫笑臉動物，因為那會讓人誤以為已開放；她畫上紅繩與清楚的停止牌。地圖不是為了讓她看來聰明，而是幫人選對路。

Fern looked at the new map and asked Nia to put the date on it. “A map can change,” she said. “We need to know which one is new.” Nia wrote the date and placed the old version behind it. She did not throw the old map away, because it showed why some paths were no longer used. Luma added a small picture of the rest chair beside the hill. Bo marked the places that would need a ground test. Soon the map told not only where people could walk, but also where the team had questions. Nia was pleased that everyone could see both the answers and the things they still did not know.

芬看著新地圖，請妮亞寫上日期。「地圖會改變，」她說，「我們得知道哪張是新版。」妮亞寫好日期，把舊版放在後面。她沒有丟掉舊圖，因為舊圖能說明某些路為何不再使用。露瑪在山丘旁畫上休息椅，波波標出需要測試地面的地方。很快，地圖不只告訴大家可以走哪裡，也顯示團隊還有哪些問題。妮亞很高興，人人都能同時看到已知答案和仍未知道的事。
