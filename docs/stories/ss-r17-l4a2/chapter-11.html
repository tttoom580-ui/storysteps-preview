The Officer From Next Year

The day began inside a protected witness office. Hao Ran had come expecting an ordinary task, but the room already contained the sort of silence that follows bad news. A young officer from the future admitted the system had been trained on reports known to be false. Nobody could afford to confuse a confident explanation with a reliable observation. They had made that mistake before, and the cost was still visible in the people waiting outside. Hao Ran opened the notebook and decided to write down what could actually be tested. One witness promised to accuse if conditions improved, but Hao Ran would not treat hope as evidence. The working file contained a separate entry for assurance, and Hao Ran asked who had verified it. Hao Ran asked whether the team could boast under the safety rules; the answer was not yet clear. A witness mentioned commerce during the interview, so the team recorded the statement without endorsing it.

這一天從受保護證人辦公室開始。浩然原以為只是處理例行工作，房間卻靜得像剛傳出壞消息。一名來自未來的年輕警員承認，系統曾用已知虛假的報告訓練。他們不能把說得肯定的解釋誤當成可靠觀察。大家以前犯過這種錯，代價仍寫在門外那些等待者的表情上。浩然打開筆記本，決定只記下真正能夠驗證的事情。一名見證人答應條件改善就會「指控」，浩然卻不能把希望當成證據。工作檔案為「保證」設了獨立項目，浩然詢問是誰查證過。浩然詢問小隊能否依安全規則「吹噓」，答案仍不明朗。見證人在訪談中提到「商業」，小隊照實記錄，卻沒有直接認同。

A new message reached Yan Shu while the group was still checking its first result. Yan Shu found the officer's testimony matched records obtained before the meeting. Hao Ran refused to celebrate too early. They had learned that two records could repeat the same lie when both came from a single office. Before treating the discovery as independent evidence, the group searched for a witness who had nothing to gain by agreeing with them. The disputed notice used the word consistent, but its author had not signed the page. A written instruction told the staff to contribute before the deadline, but nobody had logged the result. Yan Shu found a reference to convention in the old notes, but the page gave no reliable date. The old manual told its readers to expose, and Yan Shu wanted to know who had written that rule.

小隊還在核對第一項結果時，顏書就收到新的消息。顏書發現他的證詞與會前取得的資料吻合。浩然不肯太早慶祝。他們已學到，兩份紀錄如果來自同一辦公室，也可能只是重複同一個謊言。要把發現視為獨立證據前，大家先去尋找一個不會因附和他們而得利的見證人。有爭議的通知使用「前後一致的」一詞，作者卻沒有在頁面簽名。書面指示要求工作人員在期限前「貢獻」，卻沒有人記下結果。顏書在舊筆記裡找到關於「大會；集會」的記載，但紙上沒有可信的日期。舊手冊要求讀者「揭露」，顏書想知道這條規則由誰制定。

The easiest choice would have made Hao Ran popular for a single evening. Inspector Yu tried to dismiss the witness as an artificial projection, although his equipment could be tested. Yan Shu reminded the group that their decision would still matter tomorrow, after the applause and anger faded. Nobody could offer a guarantee that every person would approve. They could, however, make the reasons public and leave a safe way for a witness to disagree. The evidence list included hurricane, although nobody had explained why that detail mattered. The working file contained a separate entry for hydrogen, and Hao Ran asked who had verified it. Yan Shu found a reference to Ignorance in the old notes, but the page gave no reliable date. Someone had marked the warning informative; Yan Shu checked whether that description was accurate.

最省事的選擇，或許能讓浩然當晚受到歡呼。郁督察想把證人說成人工投影，儘管他的裝備可以驗證。顏書提醒大家，掌聲與怒氣消失後，決定明天仍然會造成影響。沒有人能保證所有人都滿意，但至少可以公開理由，並留下見證人安全表達反對的管道。證物清單上有「颶風」，卻沒有人解釋這個細節為何重要。工作檔案為「氫」設了獨立項目，浩然詢問是誰查證過。顏書在舊筆記裡找到關於「無知」的記載，但紙上沒有可信的日期。有人把警告標成「提供資訊的」，顏書則查證這種描述是否準確。

Outside a protected witness office, a school bell rang with the stubborn indifference of an ordinary afternoon. Hao Ran wanted to worry about exams, friendships, and whether there would be enough time for dinner. Instead, strangers were discussing the crimes he had not committed. Yan Shu reminded him that the future was not a room where somebody had already placed every chair. A prediction could be examined, questioned, and changed by choices made in the present.

受保護證人辦公室外面響起下課鐘，像任何平凡午後一樣毫不在意。浩然本來只想煩惱考試、朋友與晚餐前有沒有空。如今陌生人卻討論他從未犯下的罪。顏書提醒他，未來不是別人早已排好所有椅子的房間。預測可以接受檢查、質疑，也可能因當下的選擇而改變。

They agreed to test a limited part of the plan before touching the main system. Hao Ran requested a neutral examination instead of demanding immediate belief. Hao Ran assigned roles according to practical skill rather than confidence. Yan Shu recorded each step, including the moments when someone had to stop. If the trial failed, the notes would explain why. If it worked, the notes would help the next group repeat it without depending on a hero who happened to be present. The disputed notice used the word loyal, but its author had not signed the page. The working file contained a separate entry for manufacture, and Hao Ran asked who had verified it. Yan Shu found a reference to misfortune in the old notes, but the page gave no reliable date. A witness mentioned recovery during the interview, so the team recorded the statement without endorsing it.

他們同意先做小範圍測試，不直接碰主要系統。浩然要求中立檢測，不急著要求所有人相信。浩然依照實際專長分派工作，不看誰說話最有自信。顏書記錄每個步驟，連必須停止的時刻也不漏掉。如果測試失敗，筆記能說明原因；如果成功，下一批人也能按紀錄重做，不必依賴恰好在場的英雄。有爭議的通知使用「忠於」一詞，作者卻沒有在頁面簽名。工作檔案為「製造」設了獨立項目，浩然詢問是誰查證過。顏書在舊筆記裡找到關於「災禍；不幸」的記載，但紙上沒有可信的日期。見證人在訪談中提到「復原」，小隊照實記錄，卻沒有直接認同。

The result was smaller than the promise they had hoped to make, but it was real. The future officer mentioned an emergency order signed by a person who had never been born. Yan Shu closed the notebook only after marking the unresolved questions. Hao Ran was tired of victories that depended on hiding their cost. This one could be examined in daylight. And the detail they had not solved was already pointing toward the next part of the investigation. The evidence list included relaxation, although nobody had explained why that detail mattered. The working file contained a separate entry for resistance, and Hao Ran asked who had verified it. Yan Shu found a reference to skyscraper in the old notes, but the page gave no reliable date. Someone had marked the warning troublesome; Yan Shu checked whether that description was accurate.

結果比原先希望宣布的成果小，卻是真的。未來警員提及一張緊急命令，簽署人竟是從未出生的人。顏書直到標出所有未解問題，才闔上筆記本。浩然早已厭倦靠隱瞞代價換來的勝利；這次的結果可以在日光下接受查證。那個尚未解開的細節，也已指向調查的下一步。證物清單上有「休息時間」，卻沒有人解釋這個細節為何重要。工作檔案為「阻力」設了獨立項目，浩然詢問是誰查證過。顏書在舊筆記裡找到關於「摩天大樓」的記載，但紙上沒有可信的日期。有人把警告標成「棘手的」，顏書則查證這種描述是否準確。
