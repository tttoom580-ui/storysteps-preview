在地下鐵月台，麻煩不是從尖叫或警報開始，而是從一件大家自認了解的小事產生分歧。陌生人拿三則未來新聞標題，想交換浩然母親的工作地址。浩然先聽周遭人的說法，沒有急著下判斷。群眾想找到簡單的壞人時，謠言總比仔細提問傳得快。即使最喜歡的推測最後被推翻，小隊也需要留下仍然有用的紀錄。見證人在訪談中提到「周年紀念日」，小隊照實記錄，卻沒有直接認同。一名見證人答應條件改善就會「保證」，浩然卻不能把希望當成證據。工作檔案為「活動」設了獨立項目，浩然詢問是誰查證過。報告說這項安排「粗糙的」，小隊不願在沒有證據前接受此判斷。

The oldest document on the table looked less important than the newest screen. That appearance was misleading. Yan Shu identified one headline as a cached draft from the municipal publicity server. When they found a contradiction, they preserved both versions. Destroying an inconvenient record would only make the next investigation harder. 桌上最舊的文件看起來不如最新螢幕重要，但那只是表面。顏書辨出其中一則其實是市府宣傳伺服器的快取草稿。顏書念出手寫備註，浩然逐項核對事件順序。此時兩人的不同專長，比誰的職位更高還重要。找到矛盾後，他們把兩個版本都保存下來。毀掉一份不方便的資料，只會讓下一次調查更加困難。舊手冊要求讀者「使複雜化」，顏書想知道這條規則由誰制定。證物清單上有「列車長／導體」，卻沒有人解釋這個細節為何重要。工作檔案為「纜繩」設了獨立項目，浩然詢問是誰查證過。報告說這項安排「勇敢的」，小隊不願在沒有證據前接受此判斷。

By afternoon, the discovery had reached the people who could lose the most. The stranger insisted the list was destiny and threatened to sell the address elsewhere. Inspector Yu offered a quick settlement, but it required the group to stop asking questions. Hao Ran considered the frightened faces around the room. It would be easy to sound heroic and demand that everyone take the same risk. Instead, each person was allowed to describe what they were prepared to do and what they would refuse. The working file contained a separate entry for dynasty, and Hao Ran asked who had verified it. Yan Shu found a reference to evaluation in the old notes, but the page gave no reliable date.

到了下午，這項發現已傳到最可能蒙受損失的人耳中。陌生人堅稱名單就是命運，還威脅把地址賣給其他人。郁督察提出快速和解，條件卻是所有人不准再追問。浩然看著房內不安的臉孔。此時說些英雄話、要求大家冒一樣的風險很容易；他們卻讓每個人自己說明願意做什麼，以及拒絕承擔什麼。舊手冊要求讀者「想出」，顏書想知道這條規則由誰制定。有爭議的通知使用「勤勉的」一詞，作者卻沒有在頁面簽名。工作檔案為「世代；王朝」設了獨立項目，浩然詢問是誰查證過。顏書在舊筆記裡找到關於「評估」的記載，但紙上沒有可信的日期。

A screen could display a name with perfect spelling and still tell a false story. Hao Ran watched people read the numbers beside his face as if numbers carried their own moral authority. Yan Shu noticed that a few bystanders were willing to ask where the information came from. That small question mattered. It created a place in the conversation for a human being whom the machine had reduced to a risk score.

螢幕能一字不差地寫出姓名，卻仍可能說出錯誤的故事。浩然看著眾人閱讀他臉旁的分數，彷彿數字天生擁有道德權威。顏書發現有些旁觀者願意詢問資訊來源。這個小問題很重要，因為它讓一個被機器縮成風險分數的人，重新在對話裡有了位置。

The plan looked simple on paper and complicated the moment people began moving. Hao Ran refused the trade and reported the attempted disclosure through a public kiosk. A delay forced Hao Ran to choose between keeping the schedule and checking the safety condition again. The group checked again. They had lost time, but a false success would have cost them much more. Yan Shu marked the revised sequence where everyone could see it. A witness mentioned foam during the interview, so the team recorded the statement without endorsing it. The evidence list included frequency, although nobody had explained why that detail mattered. The working file contained a separate entry for imitation, and Hao Ran asked who had verified it. Yan Shu found a reference to labor in the old notes, but the page gave no reliable date.

The streets outside an underground train platform were quieter, but quiet was not proof of safety. The kiosk returned an error naming an agency that officially would not exist until tomorrow. Hao Ran checked that everyone knew how to ask for help before the group separated. They had gained one answer and inherited two new responsibilities. Tomorrow's work would require patience from people who had every reason to be angry. For the first time that day, the team had a direction worth following. A witness mentioned mineral during the interview, so the team recorded the statement without endorsing it. The disputed notice used the word needy, but its author had not signed the page. The working file contained a separate entry for psychology, and Hao Ran asked who had verified it. Hao Ran asked whether the team could reflect under the safety rules; the answer was not yet clear.

地下鐵月台外的街道變安靜了，但安靜不代表安全。終端回傳一個錯誤，指向官方聲稱要到明天才成立的機構。浩然先確認大家知道如何求助，才讓小隊分開。他們得到一個答案，也多了兩份責任。明天的工作需要那些本來就有理由憤怒的人保持耐心。至少在這一天結束時，小隊終於有了一個值得追查的方向。見證人在訪談中提到「礦物粉末中的礦物」，小隊照實記錄，卻沒有直接認同。有爭議的通知使用「需要幫助的」一詞，作者卻沒有在頁面簽名。工作檔案為「心理學；心理」設了獨立項目，浩然詢問是誰查證過。浩然詢問小隊能否依安全規則「反射；反思」，答案仍不明朗。
