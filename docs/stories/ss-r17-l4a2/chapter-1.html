Rain, dust, and distant voices made a high school assembly hall feel smaller than it was. Hao Ran arrived with a plan, then saw a detail that the plan had not accounted for. A future-dated arrest order appeared beneath Hao Ran's desk and named him the city's most dangerous future criminal. The difference between guessing and checking suddenly seemed enormous. A person who sounded brave could still be mistaken; a person who admitted uncertainty could still save the group. Hao Ran chose the second kind of courage. Yan Shu found a reference to acid in the old notes, but the page gave no reliable date. A witness mentioned assembly during the interview, so the team recorded the statement without endorsing it. The evidence list included broom, although nobody had explained why that detail mattered. A written instruction told the staff to construct before the deadline, but nobody had logged the result.

雨聲、灰塵與遠處的人聲，讓高中集會禮堂顯得格外狹窄。浩然帶著計畫抵達，卻看見計畫沒有考慮到的細節。一張來自未來的逮捕令出現在浩然課桌下，稱他將成為全市最危險的罪犯。猜測和查證的差別突然變得很大。聽起來勇敢的人也可能犯錯，敢承認不確定的人反而可能救下整個團隊。浩然決定選擇後者。顏書在舊筆記裡找到關於「胃酸／酸氣」的記載，但紙上沒有可信的日期。見證人在訪談中提到「集會／集結」，小隊照實記錄，卻沒有直接認同。證物清單上有「掃帚」，卻沒有人解釋這個細節為何重要。書面指示要求工作人員在期限前「建構／擬定」，卻沒有人記下結果。

Yan Shu brought the next piece of information on a sheet that had been folded several times. Yan Shu spotted that the order's seal would not exist for another eleven minutes. The team compared its date with what they had witnessed for themselves. One line was persuasive; another contradicted the first. Hao Ran asked for the original source instead of selecting whichever version made their work easier. Even a genuine clue could be dangerous when removed from its context. Hao Ran asked whether the team could cooperate under the safety rules; the answer was not yet clear. A witness mentioned copper during the interview, so the team recorded the statement without endorsing it. The evidence list included counter, although nobody had explained why that detail mattered. The working file contained a separate entry for coward, and Hao Ran asked who had verified it.

顏書把下一份資料帶來，紙張已被折過好幾次。顏書發現逮捕令的印章，還有十一分鐘才會正式啟用。小隊把上面的日期與親眼所見逐項比較。一行文字很有說服力，下一行卻與它矛盾。浩然沒有挑比較方便的版本，而是要求查看原始來源。即使是真的線索，一旦脫離脈絡也可能造成危險。浩然詢問小隊能否依安全規則「合作」，答案仍不明朗。見證人在訪談中提到「銅製的」，小隊照實記錄，卻沒有直接認同。證物清單上有「櫃臺／計數器」，卻沒有人解釋這個細節為何重要。工作檔案為「膽小鬼」設了獨立項目，浩然詢問是誰查證過。

An argument broke out before anyone could announce a solution. Inspector Yu demanded surrender, although the supposed crime had neither a victim nor a date anyone could verify. Hao Ran did not win it with a long speech. Instead, the team drew two columns: what the record established and what remained uncertain. Inspector Yu challenged their interpretation, which was uncomfortable but useful. A plan that collapsed under one honest question was not ready to protect anyone outside the room. Hao Ran asked whether the team could entertain under the safety rules; the answer was not yet clear. Someone had marked the warning facial; Yan Shu checked whether that description was accurate. One witness promised to interfere if conditions improved, but Hao Ran would not treat hope as evidence. The working file contained a separate entry for ministry, and Hao Ran asked who had verified it.

尚未有人宣布解法，爭論便已爆發。郁督察要求投降，儘管所謂罪行沒有受害者，也沒有可信的日期。浩然沒有用長篇演說壓倒對方，而是在紙上分成兩欄：紀錄已證實的事，以及仍然不確定的事。郁督察質疑他們的解讀，雖然令人不快，卻也有用。禁不起一次誠實提問的計畫，還不能拿去保護門外的人。浩然詢問小隊能否依安全規則「娛樂」，答案仍不明朗。有人把警告標成「臉部的」，顏書則查證這種描述是否準確。一名見證人答應條件改善就會「干擾」，浩然卻不能把希望當成證據。工作檔案為「部會」設了獨立項目，浩然詢問是誰查證過。

The danger of the forecast was not that every part of it had to be wrong. Some warnings might have been useful, and Hao Ran knew it. The danger was treating a conditional warning as permission to punish someone before an act occurred. Yan Shu wrote the difference on the edge of the notebook. It was a simple sentence, but reading it again helped the group stay cautious when fear demanded an immediate answer.

預測真正的危險，不在於它每一部分必定都是錯的；浩然知道有些警告可能真的有用。危險是把附帶條件的警告，當成事情發生前就能處罰人的許可。顏書把差別寫在筆記本邊緣。這句話很簡單，卻能在恐懼催促大家立刻回答時，提醒小隊保持謹慎。

Nothing in the trial worked exactly as predicted. Hao Ran handed over a copy, not his phone, and requested a witness to the search. Hao Ran insisted on keeping the unsuccessful measurements instead of replacing them with cleaner numbers. That choice annoyed Inspector Yu, who wanted a quick announcement. But the failure established a boundary they had only guessed at before. A useful boundary was better than a promise built on wishful thinking. Yan Shu found a reference to opera in the old notes, but the page gave no reliable date. Someone had marked the warning persuasive; Yan Shu checked whether that description was accurate. The evidence list included phenomenon, although nobody had explained why that detail mattered. The working file contained a separate entry for routes, and Hao Ran asked who had verified it.

這次測試沒有一項完全照預測發展。浩然只交出影本，不交手機，並要求搜索時有見證人在場。浩然堅持留下不成功的測量值，不用好看的數字替換。郁督察急著發公告，因此相當不滿；然而失敗讓他們找到了先前只能猜測的限制。清楚的界線，總比建立在一廂情願上的承諾可靠。顏書在舊筆記裡找到關於「歌劇院」的記載，但紙上沒有可信的日期。有人把警告標成「有說服力的」，顏書則查證這種描述是否準確。證物清單上有「現象」，卻沒有人解釋這個細節為何重要。工作檔案為「路線」設了獨立項目，浩然詢問是誰查證過。

When evening arrived, the immediate danger had changed shape rather than vanished. The device predicted that Hao Ran would erase every citizen's identity record before Friday. Hao Ran gathered the others and repeated only the facts they could support. Some people would need more time. Others had already decided to leave. Neither choice made anyone less worthy of protection. The next step belonged to those who would live with its consequences, not merely to the person speaking loudest. The report called the arrangement shady, a judgment the group refused to accept without proof. A witness mentioned slogan during the interview, so the team recorded the statement without endorsing it. The evidence list included usage, although nobody had explained why that detail mattered. An inspector described the situation as youthful; Hao Ran wanted measurements instead of adjectives.

傍晚到來時，眼前的危險只是改變形態，並未完全消失。裝置預測浩然將在星期五前抹除所有市民的身分紀錄。浩然召集同伴，只重述有證據支持的事。有人還需要時間，也有人已決定離開；這兩種選擇都不會使任何人失去受保護的資格。下一步應由承擔後果的人決定，而不是交給聲音最大的人。報告說這項安排「可疑的／陰暗的」，小隊不願在沒有證據前接受此判斷。見證人在訪談中提到「口號」，小隊照實記錄，卻沒有直接認同。證物清單上有「使用情況」，卻沒有人解釋這個細節為何重要。檢查員把情況形容為「年輕的」，浩然卻想要數據，而不是形容詞。
