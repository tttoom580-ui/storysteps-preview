The day began inside the old television transmission center. Hao Ran had come expecting an ordinary task, but the room already contained the sort of silence that follows bad news. A news bulletin predicted a school evacuation that had not yet been announced by any teacher. Nobody could afford to confuse a confident explanation with a reliable observation. They had made that mistake before, and the cost was still visible in the people waiting outside. Hao Ran opened the notebook and decided to write down what could actually be tested. The working file contained a separate entry for ancestors, and Hao Ran asked who had verified it. Yan Shu found a reference to Contrary in the old notes, but the page gave no reliable date. A witness mentioned craft during the interview, so the team recorded the statement without endorsing it. The evidence list included crush, although nobody had explained why that detail mattered.

這一天從舊電視轉播中心開始。浩然原以為只是處理例行工作，房間卻靜得像剛傳出壞消息。新聞快訊預測學校即將撤離，然而教師根本未宣布。他們不能把說得肯定的解釋誤當成可靠觀察。大家以前犯過這種錯，代價仍寫在門外那些等待者的表情上。浩然打開筆記本，決定只記下真正能夠驗證的事情。工作檔案為「祖先」設了獨立項目，浩然詢問是誰查證過。顏書在舊筆記裡找到關於「正好相反」的記載，但紙上沒有可信的日期。見證人在訪談中提到「手工技藝」，小隊照實記錄，卻沒有直接認同。證物清單上有「擠壓；造成壓傷」，卻沒有人解釋這個細節為何重要。

A new message reached Yan Shu while the group was still checking its first result. Yan Shu preserved the broadcast signal and its separate editing metadata. Hao Ran refused to celebrate too early. They had learned that two records could repeat the same lie when both came from a single office. Before treating the discovery as independent evidence, the group searched for a witness who had nothing to gain by agreeing with them. An inspector described the situation as defensible; Hao Ran wanted measurements instead of adjectives. The report called the arrangement distinguished, a judgment the group refused to accept without proof. The old manual told its readers to equip, and Yan Shu wanted to know who had written that rule. The evidence list included guilt, although nobody had explained why that detail mattered.

小隊還在核對第一項結果時，顏書就收到新的消息。顏書保留播出訊號與獨立的剪輯資料。浩然不肯太早慶祝。他們已學到，兩份紀錄如果來自同一辦公室，也可能只是重複同一個謊言。要把發現視為獨立證據前，大家先去尋找一個不會因附和他們而得利的見證人。檢查員把情況形容為「有充分理由的」，浩然卻想要數據，而不是形容詞。報告說這項安排「傑出的；精湛的」，小隊不願在沒有證據前接受此判斷。舊手冊要求讀者「器材／設備」，顏書想知道這條規則由誰制定。證物清單上有「愧疚」，卻沒有人解釋這個細節為何重要。

The easiest choice would have made Hao Ran popular for a single evening. A television executive argued that publishing the forecast would make the evacuation more likely. Yan Shu reminded the group that their decision would still matter tomorrow, after the applause and anger faded. Nobody could offer a guarantee that every person would approve. They could, however, make the reasons public and leave a safe way for a witness to disagree. The working file contained a separate entry for handwriting, and Hao Ran asked who had verified it. Yan Shu found a reference to hose in the old notes, but the page gave no reliable date. Someone had marked the warning identical; Yan Shu checked whether that description was accurate. One witness promised to involve if conditions improved, but Hao Ran would not treat hope as evidence.

最省事的選擇，或許能讓浩然當晚受到歡呼。電視主管辯稱播出預測會讓撤離更有可能發生。顏書提醒大家，掌聲與怒氣消失後，決定明天仍然會造成影響。沒有人能保證所有人都滿意，但至少可以公開理由，並留下見證人安全表達反對的管道。工作檔案為「字跡」設了獨立項目，浩然詢問是誰查證過。顏書在舊筆記裡找到關於「水管」的記載，但紙上沒有可信的日期。有人把警告標成「完全相同的」，顏書則查證這種描述是否準確。一名見證人答應條件改善就會「涉及；參與」，浩然卻不能把希望當成證據。

Outside the old television transmission center, a school bell rang with the stubborn indifference of an ordinary afternoon. Hao Ran wanted to worry about exams, friendships, and whether there would be enough time for dinner. Instead, strangers were discussing the crimes he had not committed. Yan Shu reminded him that the future was not a room where somebody had already placed every chair. A prediction could be examined, questioned, and changed by choices made in the present.

舊電視轉播中心外面響起下課鐘，像任何平凡午後一樣毫不在意。浩然本來只想煩惱考試、朋友與晚餐前有沒有空。如今陌生人卻討論他從未犯下的罪。顏書提醒他，未來不是別人早已排好所有椅子的房間。預測可以接受檢查、質疑，也可能因當下的選擇而改變。

They agreed to test a limited part of the plan before touching the main system. Hao Ran asked the school to issue its own safety notice, based on real measurements. Hao Ran assigned roles according to practical skill rather than confidence. Yan Shu recorded each step, including the moments when someone had to stop. If the trial failed, the notes would explain why. If it worked, the notes would help the next group repeat it without depending on a hero who happened to be present. A written instruction told the staff to launch before the deadline, but nobody had logged the result. The report called the arrangement lousy, a judgment the group refused to accept without proof. The old manual told its readers to misunderstand, and Yan Shu wanted to know who had written that rule. The evidence list included pregnancy, although nobody had explained why that detail mattered.

他們同意先做小範圍測試，不直接碰主要系統。浩然請學校根據實際測量值發布安全通知。浩然依照實際專長分派工作，不看誰說話最有自信。顏書記錄每個步驟，連必須停止的時刻也不漏掉。如果測試失敗，筆記能說明原因；如果成功，下一批人也能按紀錄重做，不必依賴恰好在場的英雄。書面指示要求工作人員在期限前「推出；啟動」，卻沒有人記下結果。報告說這項安排「品質差的」，小隊不願在沒有證據前接受此判斷。舊手冊要求讀者「誤會」，顏書想知道這條規則由誰制定。證物清單上有「懷孕」，卻沒有人解釋這個細節為何重要。

The result was smaller than the promise they had hoped to make, but it was real. The network corrected the story, but viewers had already started leaving their homes. Yan Shu closed the notebook only after marking the unresolved questions. Hao Ran was tired of victories that depended on hiding their cost. This one could be examined in daylight. And the detail they had not solved was already pointing toward the next part of the investigation. The working file contained a separate entry for riddle, and Hao Ran asked who had verified it. Yan Shu found a reference to stroke in the old notes, but the page gave no reliable date. A witness mentioned suggestion during the interview, so the team recorded the statement without endorsing it. The evidence list included technician, although nobody had explained why that detail mattered.

結果比原先希望宣布的成果小，卻是真的。電視台更正消息時，部分觀眾已經離家避難。顏書直到標出所有未解問題，才闔上筆記本。浩然早已厭倦靠隱瞞代價換來的勝利；這次的結果可以在日光下接受查證。那個尚未解開的細節，也已指向調查的下一步。工作檔案為「謎題」設了獨立項目，浩然詢問是誰查證過。顏書在舊筆記裡找到關於「一筆／中風」的記載，但紙上沒有可信的日期。見證人在訪談中提到「建議」，小隊照實記錄，卻沒有直接認同。證物清單上有「技術員」，卻沒有人解釋這個細節為何重要。
