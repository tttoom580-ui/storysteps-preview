Two Forecasts of One Door

Rain, dust, and distant voices made the municipal forecast museum feel smaller than it was. Hao Ran arrived with a plan, then saw a detail that the plan had not accounted for. Two public screens showed the same doorway ending in both rescue and disaster, depending on which citizen opened it. The difference between guessing and checking suddenly seemed enormous. A person who sounded brave could still be mistaken; a person who admitted uncertainty could still save the group. Hao Ran chose the second kind of courage. The evidence list included accuracy, although nobody had explained why that detail mattered. A written instruction told the staff to adapt before the deadline, but nobody had logged the result. Hao Ran asked whether the team could analyze under the safety rules; the answer was not yet clear. A witness mentioned competitor during the interview, so the team recorded the statement without endorsing it.

雨聲、灰塵與遠處的人聲，讓市立預測博物館顯得格外狹窄。浩然帶著計畫抵達，卻看見計畫沒有考慮到的細節。兩塊公用螢幕對同一扇門給出兩種結局，取決於誰來開門。猜測和查證的差別突然變得很大。聽起來勇敢的人也可能犯錯，敢承認不確定的人反而可能救下整個團隊。浩然決定選擇後者。證物清單上有「準確度」，卻沒有人解釋這個細節為何重要。書面指示要求工作人員在期限前「調整」，卻沒有人記下結果。浩然詢問小隊能否依安全規則「分析」，答案仍不明朗。見證人在訪談中提到「競爭者」，小隊照實記錄，卻沒有直接認同。

Yan Shu brought the next piece of information on a sheet that had been folded several times. Yan Shu found that the forecast engine silently changed its assumptions after each spectator voted. The team compared its date with what they had witnessed for themselves. One line was persuasive; another contradicted the first. Hao Ran asked for the original source instead of selecting whichever version made their work easier. Even a genuine clue could be dangerous when removed from its context. The evidence list included diploma, although nobody had explained why that detail mattered. The working file contained a separate entry for frost, and Hao Ran asked who had verified it. Yan Shu found a reference to helicopter in the old notes, but the page gave no reliable date. A witness mentioned herd during the interview, so the team recorded the statement without endorsing it.

顏書把下一份資料帶來，紙張已被折過好幾次。顏書發現每次觀眾投票，預測引擎就暗中修改前提。小隊把上面的日期與親眼所見逐項比較。一行文字很有說服力，下一行卻與它矛盾。浩然沒有挑比較方便的版本，而是要求查看原始來源。即使是真的線索，一旦脫離脈絡也可能造成危險。證物清單上有「文憑」，卻沒有人解釋這個細節為何重要。工作檔案為「霜凍」設了獨立項目，浩然詢問是誰查證過。顏書在舊筆記裡找到關於「直升機」的記載，但紙上沒有可信的日期。見證人在訪談中提到「羊群」，小隊照實記錄，卻沒有直接認同。

An argument broke out before anyone could announce a solution. Inspector Yu defended the display as education, though no warning described its feedback loop. Hao Ran did not win it with a long speech. Instead, the team drew two columns: what the record established and what remained uncertain. Inspector Yu challenged their interpretation, which was uncomfortable but useful. A plan that collapsed under one honest question was not ready to protect anyone outside the room. One witness promised to injure if conditions improved, but Hao Ran would not treat hope as evidence. An inspector described the situation as intense; Hao Ran wanted measurements instead of adjectives. Yan Shu found a reference to mule in the old notes, but the page gave no reliable date. Someone had marked the warning mutual; Yan Shu checked whether that description was accurate.

尚未有人宣布解法，爭論便已爆發。郁督察聲稱展示只為教育，卻沒有任何警告解釋回饋迴路。浩然沒有用長篇演說壓倒對方，而是在紙上分成兩欄：紀錄已證實的事，以及仍然不確定的事。郁督察質疑他們的解讀，雖然令人不快，卻也有用。禁不起一次誠實提問的計畫，還不能拿去保護門外的人。一名見證人答應條件改善就會「使受傷」，浩然卻不能把希望當成證據。檢查員把情況形容為「激烈的」，浩然卻想要數據，而不是形容詞。顏書在舊筆記裡找到關於「騾子」的記載，但紙上沒有可信的日期。有人把警告標成「共同的」，顏書則查證這種描述是否準確。

The danger of the forecast was not that every part of it had to be wrong. Some warnings might have been useful, and Hao Ran knew it. The danger was treating a conditional warning as permission to punish someone before an act occurred. Yan Shu wrote the difference on the edge of the notebook. It was a simple sentence, but reading it again helped the group stay cautious when fear demanded an immediate answer.

預測真正的危險，不在於它每一部分必定都是錯的；浩然知道有些警告可能真的有用。危險是把附帶條件的警告，當成事情發生前就能處罰人的許可。顏書把差別寫在筆記本邊緣。這句話很簡單，卻能在恐懼催促大家立刻回答時，提醒小隊保持謹慎。

Nothing in the trial worked exactly as predicted. Hao Ran marked both outcomes as conditional and invited visitors to test them without giving personal details. Hao Ran insisted on keeping the unsuccessful measurements instead of replacing them with cleaner numbers. That choice annoyed Inspector Yu, who wanted a quick announcement. But the failure established a boundary they had only guessed at before. A useful boundary was better than a promise built on wishful thinking. One witness promised to oppose if conditions improved, but Hao Ran would not treat hope as evidence. The working file contained a separate entry for percent, and Hao Ran asked who had verified it. Yan Shu found a reference to plumber in the old notes, but the page gave no reliable date. A witness mentioned reduction during the interview, so the team recorded the statement without endorsing it.

這次測試沒有一項完全照預測發展。浩然把兩種結局都標為條件式，邀訪客在不提供個資下進行測試。浩然堅持留下不成功的測量值，不用好看的數字替換。郁督察急著發公告，因此相當不滿；然而失敗讓他們找到了先前只能猜測的限制。清楚的界線，總比建立在一廂情願上的承諾可靠。一名見證人答應條件改善就會「反對」，浩然卻不能把希望當成證據。工作檔案為「百分比」設了獨立項目，浩然詢問是誰查證過。顏書在舊筆記裡找到關於「水管工」的記載，但紙上沒有可信的日期。見證人在訪談中提到「減少」，小隊照實記錄，卻沒有直接認同。

When evening arrived, the immediate danger had changed shape rather than vanished. The supposedly fixed future changed in full public view. Hao Ran gathered the others and repeated only the facts they could support. Some people would need more time. Others had already decided to leave. Neither choice made anyone less worthy of protection. The next step belonged to those who would live with its consequences, not merely to the person speaking loudest. The disputed notice used the word remarkable, but its author had not signed the page. An inspector described the situation as slight; Hao Ran wanted measurements instead of adjectives. Yan Shu found a reference to surroundings in the old notes, but the page gave no reliable date. A witness mentioned welfare during the interview, so the team recorded the statement without endorsing it.

傍晚到來時，眼前的危險只是改變形態，並未完全消失。所謂固定的未來，在眾人眼前改變了。浩然召集同伴，只重述有證據支持的事。有人還需要時間，也有人已決定離開；這兩種選擇都不會使任何人失去受保護的資格。下一步應由承擔後果的人決定，而不是交給聲音最大的人。有爭議的通知使用「不尋常的；顯著的」一詞，作者卻沒有在頁面簽名。檢查員把情況形容為「微小的」，浩然卻想要數據，而不是形容詞。顏書在舊筆記裡找到關於「周遭環境」的記載，但紙上沒有可信的日期。見證人在訪談中提到「福利；福祉」，小隊照實記錄，卻沒有直接認同。
