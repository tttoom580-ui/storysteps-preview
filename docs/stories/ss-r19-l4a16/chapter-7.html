Engineers found that touching the red knob in a dream triggered a memory-consolidation routine, designed to reorganize trauma narratives. Something had changed, but not everybody noticed it in the same order. Meng Xun listened to the account of the nearest worker, then to someone standing farther away. Their differences were not necessarily lies. Each person had been given a different view and a different reason to be afraid of admitting uncertainty. At the clinic, a blank sleep chart did not mean an empty dream. A Li reminded the team that private experience deserved protection even when scientists wanted measurements. A Li wrote accuracy in the margin and asked how the team would recognize it in practice. A Li repeated the instruction to adapt, then asked whether anyone had the right to refuse.

工程師發現夢中摸紅門把會啟動記憶重整程序，原本用來整理創傷敘事。某件事改變了，但人們發現它的順序不同。孟尋先聽近處工作人員的描述，再聽遠處旁觀者說法。差異不一定是謊言：每個人看到的角度不同，承認不確定時所要承擔的風險也不同。診所裡空白的睡眠圖表不代表沒有夢。阿璃提醒團隊，哪怕科學家需要數據，私人經驗仍值得保護。阿璃在頁邊寫下「準確度」，追問團隊實際上如何辨認。阿璃重述「調整」的指示，再問當事人是否有權拒絕。

Its terms explicitly barred mass deployment, yet a later update had quietly enabled worldwide transmission. Meng Xun refused to pretend the previous decision had solved everything. The practical result was still there, useful but narrow. For each thing the team knew, there were two things it had not measured. Meng Xun drew a line through the claims that depended on speculation and asked A Li to keep the original record intact. The instructions used the verb analyze without naming who would carry the risk; Meng Xun circled that gap. A Li noticed the word competitor in a note that someone had added after the first inspection. The earlier photograph clearly showed a diploma; the newer version did not, so Meng Xun kept both.

服務條款明文禁止大規模部署，後來更新卻暗中開啟全球傳輸。孟尋不願假裝前一次決定已解決所有問題。成果確實存在，卻有明確界限。每個已知事項旁都還有未測量的部分。孟尋劃掉靠猜測成立的說法，請阿璃保留原始紀錄。指示裡用了「分析」這個動詞，卻沒說誰承擔風險；孟尋圈出這項缺漏。阿璃注意到初次檢查後有人在筆記裡加上「競爭者」一詞。較早的照片清楚顯示一項「文憑」，新版卻沒有，孟尋因此保留兩張。

A small disagreement changed the schedule. Meng Xun wanted to proceed before evidence disappeared; A Li wanted another check because a wrong conclusion might hurt people who could not easily defend themselves. They did not settle the matter by choosing a leader. They wrote down what a short, reversible test could actually establish. The earlier photograph clearly showed a frost; the newer version did not, so Meng Xun kept both. A note about the helicopter lay beside the old register, with a date that Meng Xun could not verify. A Li pointed to the herd in the inventory and asked why its location had changed.

一場小爭執改變了時程。孟尋想趁證據消失前行動；阿璃要求再核對一次，因為錯誤結論可能傷害難以自辯的人。他們沒有靠選領袖解決爭議，而是列出短暫、可逆測試真正能證明什麼。較早的照片清楚顯示一項「霜凍」，新版卻沒有，孟尋因此保留兩張。一張談到「直升機」的便條放在舊登記簿旁，日期讓孟尋無法核實。阿璃指向清單上的「羊群」，追問其位置為何改變。

The routine had been modified to erase location cues, making some people unable to find familiar places after waking. The plan had been amended three times, and each change left a trace. Meng Xun valued those corrections more than a perfect-looking first draft. If the outcome proved disappointing, the group would still be able to identify which assumption needed repair. A Li watched for the quiet participant who had not yet been invited to speak. On the next form, the action field read injure, but the witness had never approved the operation. An official memo described the plan as intense, but Meng Xun refused to treat an adjective as evidence. A note about the mule lay beside the old register, with a date that Meng Xun could not verify.

程序後來被改成刪除位置線索，導致部分人醒來找不到熟悉的地方。計畫改了三次，每次修改都留下痕跡。孟尋認為這些修正比看似完美的初稿更有價值。就算結果不理想，團隊仍能知道該修正哪一項假設。阿璃也注意到那名一直沒有發言機會的人。下一張表單把動作寫成「使受傷」，見證人卻從未同意這項操作。官方備忘錄形容計畫「激烈的」，孟尋不願把形容詞當作證據。一張談到「騾子」的便條放在舊登記簿旁，日期讓孟尋無法核實。

An official version reached the room before the practical work was finished. Meng Xun read it once, then compared it with what they had actually measured. The difference was not always malicious, but it could still hurt somebody. A Li asked that the correction be written in language the affected people could understand. At the clinic, a blank sleep chart did not mean an empty dream. A Li reminded the team that private experience deserved protection even when scientists wanted measurements. The summary called the situation mutual; Meng Xun asked which observation justified that description. On the next form, the action field read oppose, but the witness had never approved the operation. A handwritten label read percent, but no one could say who had written it there.

實際工作還沒完成，官方版本就傳到現場。孟尋讀了一次，再比對真正測得的結果。差異未必都是惡意，卻仍可能傷害人。阿璃要求把更正寫成受影響者看得懂的語言。診所裡空白的睡眠圖表不代表沒有夢。阿璃提醒團隊，哪怕科學家需要數據，私人經驗仍值得保護。摘要以「共同的」形容情況，孟尋追問這種說法有何觀察根據。下一張表單把動作寫成「反對」，見證人卻從未同意這項操作。手寫標籤寫著「百分比」，卻沒有人知道是誰留下的。

A witness asked whether the team was taking sides. Meng Xun answered that refusing to invent a fact was not the same as refusing to care. What mattered was whether the procedure protected someone whose story was less convenient to tell. A Li listened without interrupting and wrote down the question exactly as it had been asked. A handwritten label read plumber, but no one could say who had written it there. The term reduction appeared in the revised file; Meng Xun checked its meaning against the original page. One witness used the word remarkable; A Li noted that a description was not a measurement.

一名見證人問團隊是否偏袒。孟尋回答，不捏造事實不代表冷漠。真正重要的是程序能否保護那個故事不方便被說出口的人。阿璃沒有打斷，逐字記下原問題。手寫標籤寫著「水管工」，卻沒有人知道是誰留下的。修訂檔案出現「減少」一詞，孟尋拿原始頁面核對它的意思。一位證人用了「不尋常的；顯著的」一詞，阿璃指出形容並不等於測量。

Even after the group agreed on the immediate step, another question remained. The answer would not arrive through a dramatic announcement. Someone would have to return to the place, compare the earlier notes, and accept the possibility that the next piece of evidence might spoil an attractive theory. Meng Xun prepared to do exactly that. A patient wrote yesterday's address in a notebook before sleeping. The page stayed still, even when the dream altered what the patient could remember in the morning. A Li noticed the word slight in a note that someone had added after the first inspection. A handwritten label read surroundings, but no one could say who had written it there. The term welfare appeared in the revised file; Meng Xun checked its meaning against the original page.

即使大家同意眼前步驟，另一個問題仍在。答案不會靠戲劇性公告降臨；有人得返回現場、比對舊筆記，並接受新證據可能推翻漂亮理論。孟尋準備做的正是這件事。病人睡前把昨天的地址寫在筆記本裡。頁面不變，即使夢境改變了病人早晨能記起的事。阿璃注意到初次檢查後有人在筆記裡加上「微小的」一詞。手寫標籤寫著「周遭環境」，卻沒有人知道是誰留下的。修訂檔案出現「福利；福祉」一詞，孟尋拿原始頁面核對它的意思。
