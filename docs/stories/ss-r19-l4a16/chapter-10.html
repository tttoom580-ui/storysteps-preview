During a public demonstration, a relay tower transmitted a moving image of the red door despite the company denying it stored any door imagery. Something had changed, but not everybody noticed it in the same order. Meng Xun listened to the account of the nearest worker, then to someone standing farther away. Their differences were not necessarily lies. Each person had been given a different view and a different reason to be afraid of admitting uncertainty. A patient wrote yesterday's address in a notebook before sleeping. The page stayed still, even when the dream altered what the patient could remember in the morning. A Li wrote acceptance in the margin and asked how the team would recognize it in practice. An official memo described the plan as ambitious, but Meng Xun refused to treat an adjective as evidence.

公開示範時，中繼塔傳出紅門動態影像，公司卻一直否認存有紅門畫面。某件事改變了，但人們發現它的順序不同。孟尋先聽近處工作人員的描述，再聽遠處旁觀者說法。差異不一定是謊言：每個人看到的角度不同，承認不確定時所要承擔的風險也不同。病人睡前把昨天的地址寫在筆記本裡。頁面不變，即使夢境改變了病人早晨能記起的事。阿璃在頁邊寫下「接受；同意」，追問團隊實際上如何辨認。官方備忘錄形容計畫「有企圖心的」，孟尋不願把形容詞當作證據。

A Li noticed the missing memories clustered around addresses near network relay towers rather than randomly across cities. Meng Xun refused to pretend the previous decision had solved everything. The practical result was still there, useful but narrow. For each thing the team knew, there were two things it had not measured. Meng Xun drew a line through the claims that depended on speculation and asked A Li to keep the original record intact. The term atmosphere appeared in the revised file; Meng Xun checked its meaning against the original page. One witness used the word authentic; A Li noted that a description was not a measurement. A Li repeated the instruction to classify, then asked whether anyone had the right to refuse.

阿璃察覺遺失的記憶集中在中繼塔附近的地址，而不是全城隨機分布。孟尋不願假裝前一次決定已解決所有問題。成果確實存在，卻有明確界限。每個已知事項旁都還有未測量的部分。孟尋劃掉靠猜測成立的說法，請阿璃保留原始紀錄。修訂檔案出現「氣氛」一詞，孟尋拿原始頁面核對它的意思。一位證人用了「真實可靠的」一詞，阿璃指出形容並不等於測量。阿璃重述「分類」的指示，再問當事人是否有權拒絕。

A small disagreement changed the schedule. Meng Xun wanted to proceed before evidence disappeared; A Li wanted another check because a wrong conclusion might hurt people who could not easily defend themselves. They did not settle the matter by choosing a leader. They wrote down what a short, reversible test could actually establish. A Li repeated the instruction to differ, then asked whether anyone had the right to refuse. The report claimed dignity, but Meng Xun wanted an example instead of a reassuring noun. A Li noticed the word embarrass in a note that someone had added after the first inspection.

一場小爭執改變了時程。孟尋想趁證據消失前行動；阿璃要求再核對一次，因為錯誤結論可能傷害難以自辯的人。他們沒有靠選領袖解決爭議，而是列出短暫、可逆測試真正能證明什麼。阿璃重述「有所不同」的指示，再問當事人是否有權拒絕。報告聲稱有「尊嚴」，孟尋要求具體例子，而不是安撫人的名詞。阿璃注意到初次檢查後有人在筆記裡加上「使人難堪」一詞。

The broadcast proved the phenomenon was engineered; the unanswered question was why the system targeted memory of home. The plan had been amended three times, and each change left a trace. Meng Xun valued those corrections more than a perfect-looking first draft. If the outcome proved disappointing, the group would still be able to identify which assumption needed repair. A Li watched for the quiet participant who had not yet been invited to speak. A Li noticed the word facility in a note that someone had added after the first inspection. An official memo described the plan as genuine, but Meng Xun refused to treat an adjective as evidence. The summary called the situation offensive; Meng Xun asked which observation justified that description.

播送證明現象出自工程設計，未解的是系統為何偏偏攻擊「回家」的記憶。計畫改了三次，每次修改都留下痕跡。孟尋認為這些修正比看似完美的初稿更有價值。就算結果不理想，團隊仍能知道該修正哪一項假設。阿璃也注意到那名一直沒有發言機會的人。阿璃注意到初次檢查後有人在筆記裡加上「設施」一詞。官方備忘錄形容計畫「真實的」，孟尋不願把形容詞當作證據。摘要以「惡意的」形容情況，孟尋追問這種說法有何觀察根據。

An official version reached the room before the practical work was finished. Meng Xun read it once, then compared it with what they had actually measured. The difference was not always malicious, but it could still hurt somebody. A Li asked that the correction be written in language the affected people could understand. A patient wrote yesterday's address in a notebook before sleeping. The page stayed still, even when the dream altered what the patient could remember in the morning. The term option appeared in the revised file; Meng Xun checked its meaning against the original page. A Li noticed the word perfume in a note that someone had added after the first inspection. A handwritten label read promotion, but no one could say who had written it there.

實際工作還沒完成，官方版本就傳到現場。孟尋讀了一次，再比對真正測得的結果。差異未必都是惡意，卻仍可能傷害人。阿璃要求把更正寫成受影響者看得懂的語言。病人睡前把昨天的地址寫在筆記本裡。頁面不變，即使夢境改變了病人早晨能記起的事。修訂檔案出現「選項」一詞，孟尋拿原始頁面核對它的意思。阿璃注意到初次檢查後有人在筆記裡加上「香水」一詞。手寫標籤寫著「行銷」，卻沒有人知道是誰留下的。

A witness asked whether the team was taking sides. Meng Xun answered that refusing to invent a fact was not the same as refusing to care. What mattered was whether the procedure protected someone whose story was less convenient to tell. A Li listened without interrupting and wrote down the question exactly as it had been asked. A handwritten label read publisher, but no one could say who had written it there. The term refugee appeared in the revised file; Meng Xun checked its meaning against the original page. A Li noticed the word rescue in a note that someone had added after the first inspection.

一名見證人問團隊是否偏袒。孟尋回答，不捏造事實不代表冷漠。真正重要的是程序能否保護那個故事不方便被說出口的人。阿璃沒有打斷，逐字記下原問題。手寫標籤寫著「出版社」，卻沒有人知道是誰留下的。修訂檔案出現「難民」一詞，孟尋拿原始頁面核對它的意思。阿璃注意到初次檢查後有人在筆記裡加上「救援」一詞。

The research officer's resignation had been filed at the same hour the worldwide dream update began. In her desk lay a child's stocking, kept from the first experimental patient's ward, and a letter warning against using fear to transform therapy into forced data collection. Meng Xun read the letter twice. On the demonstration screen the red door opened by itself, not because anyone dreamed it but because a network relay had sent the animation. The viewers gasped. A Li refused the tempting line that the mystery had therefore been solved. 'We know someone transmitted the door,' she said. 'We still don't know which memories the system takes or who authorized it.' Meng's old implant identification flashed on the screen before the technician could hide the feed.

研究主管的辭職文件簽於全球夢境更新開始的同一小時。她的抽屜裡有一隻兒童長襪，來自第一名實驗病童的病房，還有一封信警告不能利用恐懼把治療轉化為強迫蒐集資料。孟尋讀了兩次。示範螢幕上，紅門自行開啟，不是因為有人夢到，而是網路中繼傳送了動畫。觀眾倒抽一口氣。阿璃拒絕就此宣稱謎團已解：「我們知道有人傳播紅門，卻還不知道系統拿走哪些記憶、又是誰批准。」技師來不及藏起畫面，孟尋舊植入裝置的識別碼已在螢幕上閃過。
