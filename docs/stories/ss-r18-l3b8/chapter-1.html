The Tail Under the Sofa

狼人室友，請把尾巴藏好 · 第 1 / 25 章 · 2000–3000 · 英中對照 · 校訂中

用互動版查詞、朗讀與收藏

University student Mina moves into a cheap apartment and finds a large gray tail sticking out from beneath the sofa. By sunrise, the rumor had traveled across Moonbridge faster than any honest explanation. Mina found Theo beside the apartment notebook, making a list of the things they had actually seen. There were fewer items on that list than on the rumor sheet. The difference mattered. A frightened person could mistake a guess for a promise, and a promise could send somebody toward a danger they had never agreed to face. A cup of berry tea warmed Mina while the rain tapped against the window. A faded circus poster hung beside the door, a reminder of the hall’s earlier life. The deed in the locked drawer named the true owner of the building.

大學生米娜搬進便宜公寓，發現灰色的大尾巴從沙發下露出。 日出時，流言已在月橋傳得比任何誠實解釋都快。米娜看見席歐守在公寓記事簿旁，列出兩人真正親眼見到的事。那張清單比流言單上的項目少得多，而差別至關重要。害怕的人可能把猜測誤認成承諾，承諾又可能把某人帶進他從未同意面對的危險。 雨點敲窗時，一杯莓果茶讓米娜暖和起來。門邊掛著褪色的馬戲團海報，提醒眾人這座廳堂過往的用途。鎖著的抽屜裡有一份產權契據，寫著建築物的真正所有人。

The problem grew more complicated when Landlord Pike asked for a quick decision. Mina understood the attraction of a simple answer, but a housing ban on beastfolk had already made simple answers expensive for other people. Theo traced a line in the apartment notebook and showed exactly where the available information ended. Neither of them wanted to make a public promise across that blank space. They agreed to measure one small fact before taking the next step. A tiny fairy charm hung from the lamp, casting moving shapes on the wall. The fancy invitation promised a perfect evening but said nothing about the people doing the work. The old heater clicked twice before it began warming the room.

派克房東要求迅速決定後，問題變得更複雜。米娜理解簡單答案的吸引力，但針對獸人的住房禁令早已讓別人替這些簡單答案付出高昂代價。席歐沿著公寓記事簿畫出一道線，指出現有資訊到哪裡為止。兩人都不願跨過空白處作公開承諾，因此同意先測量一件小事再前進。 一枚小小的仙子護符掛在燈上，在牆面投下移動的影子。華麗邀請函承諾完美夜晚，卻沒提及真正做事的人。老暖爐喀喀響了兩聲，才開始讓房間變暖。

The first disagreement was small, but it revealed the difference between their habits. Mina tended to notice patterns; Theo noticed who was missing from the room. Together they looked at the apartment notebook and named one question each. There was no need for either person to win the argument. A good plan would leave room for both questions and for the people whose answers might be different from theirs. The yellow scarf lay across the chair beside Theo’s folded coat. Mina had never expected a shared apartment to teach her so much about how to keep another person’s trust. A small hut behind the garden offered shelter from the sudden rain. Soft jazz played from a radio in the corridor, nearly covering the distant bell. A model jet on the shelf belonged to a visitor who had once dreamed of flying.

第一次分歧不大，卻顯露兩人的習慣差異。米娜擅長注意規律，席歐則察覺誰沒有出現在房裡。他們一起查看公寓記事簿，各自提出一個問題。爭論不必分出輸贏；好計畫應容納兩個問題，也容納答案可能不同的其他人。黃色圍巾搭在席歐摺好的外套旁。米娜從未料到共住的公寓會教她這麼多守護他人信任的事。 花園後方的小屋讓大家得以躲避突來的大雨。走廊的收音機播放輕柔爵士樂，幾乎蓋過遠方的鐘聲。架上的噴射機模型屬於一位曾夢想飛行的訪客。

No one cheered when their first attempt failed. Mina marked the failure in the apartment notebook, and Theo asked whether the result could be repeated safely. They chose a smaller version of the task, with a clear signal to stop if anything went wrong. The second attempt produced a different sound and a different answer. They still needed another witness, but now they knew what question that witness should be asked. Theo practiced his full-moon safety routine before the night became dangerous. A street magician made a coin disappear, but Mina was more interested in the missing receipt. Theo called the rescue a miracle; Mina thanked the people who had made it possible. The café sold a pint of warm soup to anyone caught in the storm.

第一次嘗試失敗時，沒有人歡呼。米娜把失敗記在公寓記事簿裡，席歐詢問是否能安全重做。他們選擇規模更小的測試，並約定出錯時立即停止的訊號。第二次嘗試出現不同的聲音與答案。雖然還需要另一名證人，但他們終於知道該問對方什麼。席歐在滿月夜出現危險前，就練習自己的安全程序。 街頭魔術師讓硬幣消失，米娜卻更關心不見的收據。席歐稱救援是奇蹟；米娜則感謝讓它成真的人。咖啡館向被暴風雨困住的人供應一品脫熱湯。

A practical question finally interrupted the argument: who would do the work after the visitors went home? Mina had no magical answer. Theo read the names in the apartment notebook, then asked the people involved whether they actually wanted those duties. Several agreed. One refused, and nobody punished that choice. The group adjusted the schedule rather than pretending consent had already been given. That small correction prevented a larger mistake. The argument became political when the council tried to control who could speak. Mina wanted to quit the unfair game, not abandon the people affected by it.

一個實際問題終於打斷爭執：訪客回家後，誰負責工作？米娜沒有魔法般的答案。席歐讀出公寓記事簿上的名字，再問相關人員是否真的願意承擔職責。幾人同意，一人拒絕，沒有人因此受罰。小組調整時程，而非假裝已取得同意。這項小小修正避免了更大的錯誤。 議會試圖控制發言權時，爭論變成政治問題。米娜想退出不公平的遊戲，卻不願拋下受影響的人。

A new message arrived before the meeting could end. It mentioned a housing ban on beastfolk, then claimed that only Landlord Pike had the authority to decide what happened next. Mina read it twice. The words sounded certain, but there was no date beside the claim and no name of the person who had checked it. Theo suggested saving a copy and asking for the original. The question was not whether to trust a powerful person. It was what anyone could verify. Mina found a strand of gray fur on her lecture notes and shook her head. Theo apologized from the kitchen, holding a plate of toast as a peace offering. A witness warned that someone planned to rob the archive, so Theo asked for better locks. A loose screw rolled out from beneath the broken machine and stopped beside Mina.

會議尚未結束，一則新訊息就送到了。訊息提及針對獸人的住房禁令，並宣稱只有派克房東有權決定下一步。米娜讀了兩遍。文字很肯定，卻沒有日期，也沒有查核者姓名。席歐建議保留副本並索取原件。問題不是該不該相信有權勢的人，而是任何人能核實什麼。米娜在課堂筆記上發現一根灰毛，無奈搖頭。席歐從廚房道歉，端著吐司當作賠罪。 證人警告有人打算搶劫檔案室，席歐因此要求加強門鎖。一顆鬆脫的螺絲從壞機器下滾出，停在米娜腳邊。

Theo read the last line aloud. Mina checked the date and kept a copy for the morning team. The two friends agreed that one clear signal would mean the test had to stop. A small wheel began to spin when Theo turned the handle.

席歐朗讀最後一行。米娜核對日期，為早班小組留下副本。 兩名朋友約定，一個明確訊號代表測試必須停止。席歐轉動把手後，小輪子開始旋轉。

Mina left the apartment notebook open so Theo could add the next finding. Neither could see the whole road yet. Mina knew that care meant more than trying to tame someone else’s fear. Various visitors offered different accounts, and none was erased just for being unusual.

米娜讓公寓記事簿保持打開，好讓席歐補上新的發現。兩人都還看不見整段路。 米娜明白關懷不是試圖馴服別人的恐懼。各式各樣的訪客提出不同說法，沒有人因說法不尋常就被抹除。
