Walking home in heavy rain, Mina tells Theo about her own fear of being abandoned by friends. At Moonbridge, a small crowd had already gathered before anyone knew what to call the trouble. Mina was holding the apartment notebook when Theo arrived with a question that did not have an easy answer. They could have waited for an official order. Instead, they stood where the problem could be seen and listened to the person who had first noticed it. The air felt unusually still, as though the whole street were waiting to hear who would speak first. The instruction used the word aside, and Mina asked the team to agree on its meaning. Mina found bubble on the list of details that the first report had left unexplained. A witness mentioned canyon in a hurried account; Mina wrote it down without guessing its importance.

大雨回家路上，米娜告訴席歐自己害怕被朋友拋下。 在月橋，大家還不知道該如何稱呼這場麻煩，一小群人便已聚集。米娜拿著公寓記事簿時，席歐帶著一個不容易回答的問題趕到。他們原本可以等待官方命令，卻選擇站在看得見問題的地方，先聽最早發現異狀的人說話。空氣異常安靜，彷彿整條街都在等待第一個開口的人。 指示用了「在一旁」這個詞，米娜請團隊先釐清它的意思。米娜在第一份報告沒有說明的細節清單上，找到「氣泡」。一名證人在匆忙的敘述中提到「峽谷」；米娜先記下來，沒有猜測它有多重要。

What worried Theo was not the loudest objection. It was the quiet question from someone who would have to live with the result. Mina repeated the question so that everyone could hear it, then left a little space for an answer. Landlord Pike had promised speed. Speed could be useful, but only if the group understood what it might lose. The apartment notebook could guide them; it could not make that choice for them. Nobody wanted to describe the situation as capable without explaining the reason in plain words. A witness mentioned captain in a hurried account; Mina wrote it down without guessing its importance. Someone asked whether client was connected to the trouble, and the room fell quiet for a moment.

讓席歐擔心的不是最大聲的反對，而是某個必須承受結果的人提出的小問題。米娜把問題重複一次，讓眾人聽清，也留出回答的時間。派克房東承諾快速解決。速度確實有用，但前提是大家知道可能失去什麼。公寓記事簿可以指引方向，無法替他們作決定。 沒有人願意在沒有清楚解釋理由時，就把情況稱為「有能力的」。一名證人在匆忙的敘述中提到「隊長」；米娜先記下來，沒有猜測它有多重要。有人問「客戶」是否與麻煩有關，房間一時間安靜下來。

For a moment Theo seemed ready to walk away. Mina closed the apartment notebook and asked what had gone wrong. The answer was ordinary: too many people had been talking about a decision without speaking to those who would bear its cost. Nothing dramatic happened as they listened. Yet by the time the conversation ended, they had changed their next step. It was a change neither could have achieved by giving another order. Mina found a strand of gray fur on her lecture notes and shook her head. Theo apologized from the kitchen, holding a plate of toast as a peace offering. The plan mentioned a need to edit; neither of them accepted that instruction without a reason. At first the change seemed elderly; a second observation made the team less certain. Theo circled the entry for electricity and asked whether the detail belonged to this incident.

有一瞬間，席歐幾乎要轉身離開。米娜闔上公寓記事簿，問到底哪裡出了問題。答案很普通：太多人在討論決策，卻沒有詢問將承受代價的人。聆聽時沒有戲劇性事件發生，但談話結束後，下一步已經改變。這不是多下一道命令就能達成的結果。米娜在課堂筆記上發現一根灰毛，無奈搖頭。席歐從廚房道歉，端著吐司當作賠罪。 計畫提到需要「編輯、從紀錄中刪除」；兩人都不願毫無理由便接受這項指示。起初這項變化看來「年長的」，第二次觀察卻讓團隊不再那麼確定。席歐圈起「電力」那一欄，問這個細節是否真的屬於本次事件。

Outside, a few spectators demanded something impressive. Mina ignored the request for a grand display and asked Theo to check the simple measuring tools. They had seen enough in Moonbridge to know that bright light and confident speeches could hide a broken detail. When the figures finally agreed, the result was modest. It was also something another team could verify without believing either of them on faith. Mina treated Theo’s secret as his own information, not a story she could sell. 外頭的旁觀者要求看些驚人的事。米娜沒有表演，而是請席歐檢查簡單的測量工具。他們在月橋已見過太多耀眼光芒與自信演說掩蓋故障細節的例子。數據終於一致時，成果其實不大，卻能讓另一支隊伍不必盲信任何人就自行驗證。米娜把席歐的祕密視為他的資訊，而非可拿來販售的故事。 有人問「閃光」是否與麻煩有關，房間一時間安靜下來。席歐圈起「艙口」那一欄，問這個細節是否真的屬於本次事件。席歐在初步提案旁寫下「有希望的」，又加了問號，因為意見並不一致。

席歐朗讀最後一行。米娜核對日期，為早班小組留下副本。 席歐圈起「資訊」那一欄，問這個細節是否真的屬於本次事件。席歐在初步提案旁寫下「每月的」，又加了問號，因為意見並不一致。

The pressure did not disappear with the evidence. Landlord Pike still controlled a room, a schedule, or an important signature. Mina could see why some neighbors wanted to surrender the argument and go back to normal life. Theo suggested a narrower demand: protect the people at immediate risk, then review the larger dispute in the open. It was not a perfect victory, but it kept a bad decision from becoming permanent. A full moon rose above Moonbridge as Theo checked the lock and emergency lamp. Mina stood outside the safe room, ready to help only if he asked. Theo circled the entry for property and asked whether the detail belonged to this incident. Theo wanted to represent, but agreed to wait for one more safe test.

證據沒有讓壓力消失。派克房東依然控制著房間、時程或重要簽名。米娜理解有些鄰居想放棄爭論、回歸正常生活。席歐提出較有限的要求：先保護眼前有危險的人，再公開檢視更大的爭議。這不是完美勝利，卻能避免錯誤決策永久化。滿月升上月橋上空，席歐檢查門鎖與緊急燈。米娜站在安全室外，準備在他提出請求時才協助。 席歐圈起「財產／產權標記」那一欄，問這個細節是否真的屬於本次事件。席歐想要「代表」，但同意先等一次安全測試的結果。

Theo read the last line aloud. Mina checked the date and kept a copy for the morning team. Theo circled the entry for situation and asked whether the detail belonged to this incident. The margin of the apartment notebook contained a reference to spice, which Theo marked for a later check.

席歐朗讀最後一行。米娜核對日期，為早班小組留下副本。 席歐圈起「情況」那一欄，問這個細節是否真的屬於本次事件。公寓記事簿的頁邊提到了「香料」，席歐做上記號，準備日後查證。

Mina left the apartment notebook open so Theo could add the next finding. Neither could see the whole road yet. Theo circled the entry for sword and asked whether the detail belonged to this incident. Theo wanted to weep, but agreed to wait for one more safe test.

米娜讓公寓記事簿保持打開，好讓席歐補上新的發現。兩人都還看不見整段路。 席歐圈起「長劍」那一欄，問這個細節是否真的屬於本次事件。席歐想要「哭泣」，但同意先等一次安全測試的結果。
