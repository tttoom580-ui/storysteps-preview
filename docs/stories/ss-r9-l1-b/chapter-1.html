A Stop with No Bus

蒲公英鎮的慢慢巴士 · 第 1 / 25 章 · 1000以下 · 英中對照

用互動版查詞、朗讀與收藏

At the north end of Dandelion Town, a red star was painted on an old wooden post. Nobody knew why the star was there. One morning, Koi, a young raccoon, saw a friendly woman standing beside it. The woman held a basket, a camera, and a little box for her pet bug. She wanted to reach the park for a family picnic, but her legs hurt when she walked far. The park was only on the other side of town. Still, for her it felt very far away. Koi offered her some juice and asked, “Is this a bus stop?” The woman laughed. “No. It has been an empty post for years.” Then she looked at the road and stopped laughing.

蒲公英鎮的北端，有根舊木柱上畫著一顆紅星。沒有人知道那顆星為何在那裡。一天早晨，年輕的浣熊柯伊看到一位友善的女士站在柱旁。她提著籃子、相機，以及裝著寵物小蟲的小盒子。她想到公園參加家庭野餐，卻不適合走太遠，因為雙腿會痛。公園明明只在鎮子的另一端，對她而言卻像遠在天邊。柯伊遞上果汁，問：「這是巴士站嗎？」女士笑了。「不是，這根柱子已空著好多年。」然後她望向道路，笑容慢慢消失。

Koi lived in the middle of town and worked with maps at the town hall. Her work was to notice small things other people missed: a broken path, a hard corner, or a sign hidden by leaves. On this morning she saw a fact she had never written down. A short road did not mean an easy trip. The woman explained that she could walk to the shop, but not all the way to the park. “I do not want anybody to carry me,” she said. “I just want the same choice other people have.” Koi promised not to lie about what she could do. She had no bus and did not know how to drive one. But she could use a pencil and ask questions.

柯伊住在鎮中心，工作是在鎮公所繪製地圖。她專門注意別人可能忽略的小事：破損的小路、難走的轉角，或被樹葉遮住的標誌。這天早上，她發現了一個從未記錄的事實：道路短，不代表旅程輕鬆。女士說她能走到商店，卻走不到公園。「我不想要別人抱著我走，」她說，「我只想有跟其他人一樣的選擇。」柯伊答應不會誇口說自己做得到的事。她沒有巴士，也不會開巴士；但她有鉛筆，能夠提問。

Koi sat beside the post and drew a line from the north road to the picnic field. She drew the school, the pond, and three small homes along the way. A bus might join these places in the future, she thought. It would not need to be fast. It would need to arrive when people needed it. The woman told Koi her name was Ressa. She said she would help by showing which places were hard to reach. They walked together for one block, then rested on a bench. Koi did not make her continue. She drew a small circle where the bench stood. A place to sit was part of a good road too.

柯伊坐在木柱旁，畫出一條從北路通往野餐草地的線。她把學校、池塘和沿途三間小屋也標上去。她想，也許未來會有一輛巴士，把這些地方連在一起。巴士不必很快，只需要在人們需要時到達。女士告訴她，自己叫瑞莎，願意指出哪些地方難以抵達。兩人一起走過一個街區，便在長椅上休息。柯伊沒有要求她繼續走，而是在長椅的位置畫了個小圈。能坐下來休息的地方，也是好道路的一部分。

In the afternoon, Koi showed the map to Dano, a pelican who had driven a delivery van for many years. He did not say yes at once. “A bus is not a toy,” he told her. “A driver needs a safe road, enough time, and a way for every passenger to get on.” He placed his wide feet on the map to show the size of a vehicle. Koi moved his feet away and drew two possible routes. One was short but went down a steep street. The other was longer and passed the clinic. Dano chose neither. “We need to look at both roads before we choose,” he said. Koi was glad he was careful.

下午，柯伊把地圖拿給鵜鶘丹洛看。丹洛開送貨車多年，卻沒有立刻答應。「巴士不是玩具，」他說，「司機需要安全的路、足夠的時間，也要讓每位乘客都上得了車。」他用兩隻寬腳掌壓在地圖上，示意車子的大小。柯伊把他的腳移開，畫出兩條可能的路線。一條較短，卻經過陡坡；另一條較長，會經過診所。丹洛兩條都沒選。「先看過兩條路，才能決定。」柯伊很高興他這麼謹慎。

Before evening, Koi returned to the red star. Ressa had gone home, but a child and his father stood there, looking down the empty road. The child asked if a real bus would come tomorrow. “Not tomorrow,” Koi answered. “First we must find out what this town needs.” She wrote down the child's wish to visit the park with his grandfather. Then she asked the father about work trips and the road to the farm. Every answer changed the map a little. At home, Koi put the paper on her desk. It was not a plan yet, only the beginning of one. She looked at the red star on her drawing and decided that an old empty post could become a place for a new beginning.

傍晚前，柯伊回到紅星木柱旁。瑞莎已回家，卻有一對父子站在那裡，望著空蕩蕩的道路。孩子問明天會不會真的有巴士。「明天還不會，」柯伊說，「我們得先知道鎮上需要什麼。」她記下孩子想與爺爺一起去公園的願望，又問父親平日上班的行程及通往農場的路。每一個答案都讓地圖有了些微變化。回家後，柯伊把紙攤在桌上。這還不是計畫，只是計畫的起點。她看著圖上的紅星，決定讓空了多年的木柱成為新開始的地方。
