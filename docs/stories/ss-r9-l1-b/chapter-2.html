池邊的桌子

The Table by the Pond

蒲公英鎮的慢慢巴士 · 第 2 / 25 章 · 1000以下 · 英中對照

The next day, Koi asked anybody who used the town roads to come to a meeting. She borrowed the front room of an apartment near the old church. A clear window looked over the pond. There were only six chairs and one long sofa, so Dano brought two stools from his garage. Koi put a piece of paper and a pen on every seat. Some people came early. Others were late because the hill road was slow. “Here is our first question,” Koi said. “Where do you want to go, and what stops you?” She did not ask people to guess the best route yet. She wanted to hear real trips, not make a beautiful map that nobody could use.

隔天，柯伊邀請所有使用鎮上道路的人來開會。她借了舊教堂旁一間公寓的前廳。從清楚明亮的窗戶望出去，就是池塘。房裡只有六張椅子與一張長沙發，丹洛便從車庫搬來兩張凳子。柯伊在每個座位放上紙和筆。有些人提早到，有些人因為山路難走而遲到。「第一個問題是，」柯伊說，「你想去哪裡？又是什麼讓你去不了？」她還不請大家猜最好的路線。她想聽真實的行程，而不是畫一張漂亮卻沒人用得上的地圖。

A reporter named Vale came from a nearby country town. He wanted to write about the new idea. “Will the bus be free?” he asked. Koi explained that someone would have to pay for fuel, repairs, and a driver. They could try a short service, but they could not promise a price before they knew the cost. Vale looked serious and wrote her answer down. At lunch, a mother said she often forgot a doctor's visit because she could not get there without a ride. A third neighbor said the market was too far when he carried heavy fruit. Koi began a list called “Trips We Cannot Make.” Everyone could add a reason, and no reason was too small.

一位叫維爾的記者從附近鄉鎮來，想報導這個新點子。「巴士會免費嗎？」他問。柯伊解釋，燃料、維修和司機都需要費用。他們可以試行短程服務，但算清成本以前不能承諾票價。維爾神情認真，記下她的回答。午餐時，一位母親說，她常忘了已約好的看診，因為沒有車便去不了。第三位鄰居則說，提著沉重水果走去市場太辛苦。柯伊列出一張名叫「我們做不到的行程」的清單。人人都能補充原因，再小的理由也值得聽。

Dano reminded everyone that a bus would not solve every problem. A long trip to the city, for example, needed a different service. “If we try to go everywhere, we may arrive nowhere on time,” he said. Ressa nodded from the sofa. She wanted to get to the park and the clinic, but she did not want a driver to hurry through the village. Koi asked the group to mark only places they had visited during the last month. That rule changed the discussion. People stopped naming famous places they liked in pictures and started talking about the roads outside their own homes. The pond and the north post received more marks than Koi expected.

丹洛提醒大家，巴士無法解決所有問題。例如到城市的長途旅程，就需要另一種服務。「如果什麼地方都想去，可能每一站都無法準時到達。」瑞莎坐在沙發上點頭。她想去公園與診所，卻不希望司機在村裡趕路。柯伊請大家只標出最近一個月真正去過的地方。這項規則改變了討論。人們不再提照片裡喜歡的名勝，而開始談住家外的道路。池塘和北邊木柱得到的記號，比柯伊想像中還多。

When the meeting ended, Vale asked if he could take a photograph of the map. Koi agreed, but she covered the names of people who had talked about health. “A useful story does not need every private name,” she said. Dano folded the stools. Ressa called her daughter on the telephone to say she would be home before dark. Koi checked the clock, then found a line in her notes she had almost missed: several people wanted a ride after school, not in the morning. She drew a little sun next to morning trips and a moon next to late ones. A good timetable, she realized, had to know more than places. It had to know people's days.

會議結束後，維爾問能不能拍下地圖。柯伊同意，卻先遮住那些談到健康問題的居民姓名。「有用的報導不需要所有人的私事。」丹洛收起凳子。瑞莎用電話告訴女兒，自己會在天黑前回家。柯伊看了時鐘，又注意到一條差點漏掉的筆記：有幾位居民要的是放學後的車，不是早上的車。她在早晨行程旁畫太陽，較晚的行程旁畫月亮。她開始明白，一張好的時刻表不只要知道地點，也要知道居民如何過日子。

Before leaving, Dano drew two columns on the board. One was for places people wanted to reach; the other was for places where they could wait safely. A boy noticed that the market appeared in the first column but not the second. Koi asked the group to think of a wide space near the market without blocking carts. A shopkeeper offered to walk there with her the next day. The meeting had given Koi more work, but also more help. She put a small circle beside the boy's question. A child's careful eyes had found a gap in the plan before a real rider had to face it.

離開之前，丹洛在板上畫了兩欄。一欄是人們想抵達的地方，另一欄是可以安全候車的地方。有個男孩注意到市場只出現在第一欄，第二欄卻沒有。柯伊請大家想一個靠近市場、又不擋住推車的寬敞位置。一位店主說隔天願意陪她去看。這場會議讓柯伊的工作變多，也讓幫手變多了。她在男孩的問題旁畫個小圈。孩子細心的眼睛，在真正的乘客遇到困難以前，就發現了計畫的缺口。

That night, Koi made three copies of the list. One would stay in the apartment, one would go to the town hall, and one would travel with Dano when they checked the roads. She made the letters large enough for Ressa to read. The page did not look exciting, but it held the voices of her neighbors. Koi set it beside her bed and tried to sleep. She was not worried that people had different wishes. She was worried that she might forget one of them. Before turning out the light, she wrote a final line in dark ink: “Ask before deciding. Check before promising.” This time she did not need to guess what work came next.

當晚，柯伊把清單抄成三份。一份留在公寓，一份送到鎮公所，另一份讓丹洛勘查道路時帶著。她把字寫得夠大，讓瑞莎容易閱讀。這張紙看起來不精彩，卻裝著鄰居的心聲。柯伊把它放在床邊，試著睡覺。她並不擔心大家的願望不同，而是怕自己漏掉某一個。熄燈前，她用深色墨水寫下最後一行：「決定之前先詢問，承諾之前先查明。」這次她知道接下來該做什麼。
