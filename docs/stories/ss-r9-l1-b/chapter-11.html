醫院前的白線

The White Line at the Hospital

蒲公英鎮的慢慢巴士 · 第 11 / 25 章 · 1000以下 · 英中對照 · 校訂中

用互動版查詞、朗讀與收藏

Koi returned to the hospital with Nika and a basket of drawing tools. They came to check the new waiting area. A nurse asked them to mark a white line well away from the emergency door. “Dear friends,” she said, smiling, “please do not make the door hard to reach.” Nika checked the color of the paint against the gray road. It was clear in the sun, but hard to see under a shadow. They tried a different shade of white and added a sign with a picture. The nurse explained that health came first; a bus stop was useful only if it kept the hospital entrance open. Koi wrote the rule in her book.

柯伊與妮卡帶著一籃畫圖工具回到醫院，檢查新的候車區。一位護士請她們在離急診門口夠遠的地方畫白線。「親愛的朋友們，」她笑著說，「可別把門口弄得難以出入。」妮卡檢查油漆顏色和灰色道路是否分得清楚。陽光下很明顯，陰影處卻難看見。她們試了另一種白色，並加上圖示。護士說，健康最重要；只有讓醫院入口保持通暢，站牌才算有用。柯伊把這條規則寫進本子。

A kite came down across the road as they worked. Two children ran after it, and Dano stepped forward to stop them before they reached moving cars. He did not hate games, but he hated the thought of a child being hurt because adults had made a confusing waiting place. He checked the crossing again and moved the sign a little farther from the road. One child made a joke about a bus that flew like a kite. Everybody laughed. Then Dano showed them exactly where to stand when a real bus arrived. “If you can remember this line,” he said, “you can help keep your friends safe.”

工作時，一只風箏落在馬路上。兩個孩子追過去，丹洛上前攔住，沒讓他們跑進車流。他並不討厭遊戲，卻很怕大人設了不清楚的候車區而讓孩子受傷。他重新檢查穿越道，把標誌往後移一點。有個孩子開玩笑說，巴士要是能像風箏飛就好了。大家都笑了。接著丹洛明確指出真車抵達時該站哪裡。「記住這條線，」他說，「你就能幫忙保護朋友。」

After lunch, Koi went to the library to find pictures of easy signs. She saw that some books used thin letters that could not be read from far away. Others used large shapes with short words. The librarian said those pictures often helped visitors who could not read small print. Koi made copies and sent them to Nika for the next test. A player from the school team also offered help. He often walked past the hospital after practice and could check whether the waiting space stayed clear in the evening. Koi thanked him, but asked him to stay on the walking path and report a problem rather than fix it alone.

午餐後，柯伊去圖書館找容易辨識的標誌範例。有些書使用細小字體，遠遠看不清楚；另一些使用大圖形和短文字。館員說，後者常能幫助不容易閱讀小字的訪客。柯伊複印圖片，寄給妮卡作下一次測試。一位校隊球員也願意幫忙。他練習完常經過醫院，可以確認傍晚候車區是否仍暢通。柯伊謝謝他，但請他只在步道上觀察、回報問題，不必自己動手修。

At the hospital gate, Ressa tried reading the sign from her usual walking distance. She could see the large bus picture but could not read the smallest time below it. Nika changed the size immediately. “Those numbers matter as much as the picture,” Ressa said. The nurse watched the test and offered one more idea: place the timetable where a person sitting down could see it. Koi bent her knees and looked from the height of a chair. The sign had been too high. They moved it lower without blocking the path. It was a simple change, but the kind of change they could not see by standing in one place.

在醫院門口，瑞莎從平常行走的距離試著讀標誌。她看得見大大的巴士圖案，卻讀不清下方最小的時間數字。妮卡立刻加大字體。「那些數字跟圖片一樣重要。」瑞莎說。護士看過測試，又提了個想法：讓坐著的人也看得到時刻表。柯伊彎下膝蓋，從椅子的高度觀察，才發現標誌原本太高。她們把它調低，又不擋住步道。這是簡單的改變，卻不是一直站在同一個位置就看得出來的。

Before the team packed up, the nurse showed them a seat belt used in a hospital transport chair. She explained that it was not the same as a bus belt, but both had to be checked before anyone moved. Dano promised to test the bus belts with the proper inspector. Koi wrote the lesson down, then asked Nika to send the final sign picture to the nurse. The nurse would show it to visitors who had trouble reading small print. This was a simple way to help without opening the bus too early. Koi left the hospital with a basket that was now full of notes instead of tools.

收拾前，護士讓大家看醫院運送椅上的安全帶。她解釋，這和巴士安全帶不同，但兩者都得在移動前確認。丹洛答應和正式檢查員一起測試巴士安全帶。柯伊記下教訓，又請妮卡把最後的標誌圖片寄給護士。護士會拿給不容易讀小字的訪客看。這是在不提早開放巴士的前提下，就能幫上忙的簡單方法。柯伊離開醫院時，籃子已不再裝工具，而裝滿筆記。

That evening, Koi went over the hospital stop with Dano. They had checked the road, the emergency door, the crossing, the letters, and the seats. But the nurse reminded them to look again on a busy day, when the hospital was full. Koi agreed to return during visiting hours. She pinned the latest picture beside her first map and could see how much had changed. The small white line on the road looked ordinary. To Koi, it held many conversations and careful choices. A good stop was not one good idea. It was many small, useful ideas tested by the people who needed them.

傍晚，柯伊與丹洛再次檢查醫院站。他們看過道路、急診入口、穿越道、字體與座椅，但護士提醒他們，還要在醫院忙碌、訪客很多的日子再看一次。柯伊答應探病時段回來。她把最新圖畫釘在第一張地圖旁，看出這段時間改了多少。馬路上的小白線看似普通，對柯伊卻包含了許多對話與謹慎的選擇。一個好站牌不是一個好點子，而是許多由真正需要它的人測試過的實用小點子。
