診所門前的歌

The Song at the Clinic

蒲公英鎮的慢慢巴士 · 第 9 / 25 章 · 1000以下 · 英中對照 · 校訂中

用互動版查詞、朗讀與收藏

Ressa wanted Koi to meet a bear named Odo who lived near the clinic. Odo had once been a singer, but he had stopped going out after his friend moved away. He felt lonely and sometimes sad. His house was warm, yet it was far from the market and the park. “A brave person is not someone who never needs help,” Ressa told him. Odo smiled at that. He invited Koi inside and showed her a photograph of a summer concert. She saw a whole group of friends standing beside him. “Whose idea was the concert?” she asked. “Mine,” Odo said. “But these days I cannot get to the hall without a ride.”

瑞莎想讓柯伊認識住在診所附近的熊奧多。奧多以前是歌手，朋友搬走後，他就很少出門，常感到孤單與難過。屋子雖溫暖，離市場和公園卻很遠。「勇敢不代表永遠不需要幫忙。」瑞莎對他說。奧多聽了笑笑，邀柯伊進屋，看一張夏日演唱會照片。照片裡，許多朋友站在他身旁。「那場音樂會是誰的主意？」柯伊問。「我的，」奧多說，「但現在沒有車，我去不了活動廳。」

At the clinic, a farmer was waiting with a bottle of milk. Another visitor carried a box of meat for the kitchen. They asked where the future bus would stop. Koi showed them a proposed place beside the wall, but the nurse said it would block the way to the toilet door. The clinic served many kinds of people, and that door had to stay clear. Koi made a new mark farther down the road. “Nothing is final,” she told the visitors. She was glad the nurse had spoken before they painted a sign. A bus stop beside a clinic needed more care than a pretty picture on a map.

到診所時，一位農夫拿著一瓶牛奶在等候，另一位訪客提著供廚房使用的一盒肉。他們問未來巴士會停在哪裡。柯伊指向牆邊的預定位置，護士卻說那會擋住廁所入口。診所服務各種居民，那扇門必須保持暢通。柯伊在較遠的路段重新做記號。「還沒有定案。」她告訴訪客。她很慶幸護士在畫標誌前提出了問題。診所旁的站牌需要的照顧，不是地圖上好看的圖案能代替的。

During lunch, a visitor from another country came to ask about the bus. He spoke slowly, because he was still learning the town's language. Koi listened and pointed to simple pictures on Nika's map. He said he could read numbers well, but not all the words. A person nearby tried to answer for him. Koi politely asked the visitor to speak for himself. He wanted to get from the clinic to the market, not to the far hill. Koi wrote down his trip and asked if the pictures helped. He said yes. A good sign, she saw, should welcome people even when they did not share every word.

午餐時，一位來自其他國家的訪客來問巴士的事。他說得很慢，因為還在學鎮上的語言。柯伊一邊聽，一邊指著妮卡地圖上的簡單圖示。他說自己很會讀數字，卻不認得所有文字。旁人想替他回答，柯伊禮貌地請訪客自己說。他想從診所到市場，不是去遙遠山丘。柯伊記下他的行程，問圖示有沒有幫助。訪客點頭。她明白，好標誌應該能歡迎不認識每個字的人。

Odo came to the clinic gate with his old friend Ressa. He had brought a little bell with a ring at the top. He said he used it before singing, but he did not want it on the bus. “People may need quiet,” he said. Koi thanked him for thinking of other passengers. Odo told her that he wanted to keep singing, but he did not want to lose his own choice about where and when. “Do not treat me like a poor old bear who must be taken somewhere,” he said. “Ask me where I want to go.” Koi promised to remember. She had learned that even kind help could feel wrong when it took away a person's voice.

奧多和老朋友瑞莎一起來到診所門口，手裡提著一個頂端有圓環的小鈴鐺。他說以前唱歌前會先搖鈴，但不想在巴士上使用。「有人需要安靜。」他說。柯伊謝謝他替其他乘客著想。奧多說自己想繼續唱歌，卻不想失去決定何時、去哪裡的權利。「別把我當成一定要被帶走的可憐老熊，」他說，「先問我想去哪。」柯伊答應記住。她學會了：即使出於好意，若奪走別人的發言權，也可能讓人難受。

Later, the visitor explained that someone had called him a foreigner as though he did not belong in the town. Koi told him that being new to a place did not make his needs less important. She asked which part of the timetable was hard to read. The visitor pointed to the small words under the clinic picture. Nika made the words larger and added a clock symbol. He tried reading the sign again and found his stop without help. Koi did not tell the story to the reporter with his name attached. What mattered was the better sign, not turning a person's uncomfortable moment into public news.

後來，那位訪客說，有人叫他「外國人」時，語氣彷彿他不屬於這座鎮。柯伊告訴他，新來一個地方並不代表需要就比較不重要。她問時刻表哪裡難懂。訪客指著診所圖示下方的小字。妮卡把文字加大，補上時鐘符號。他重新閱讀，終於能自己找到站點。柯伊沒有把他的姓名和這段不舒服的經驗一起交給記者。重要的是改善標誌，而不是把別人的難堪當成新聞。

That evening, Koi added two new lines to the route plan. One described a clear path to the clinic toilet. The other said that drivers should ask passengers before offering physical help. Dano read the lines and agreed. “A bus is a shared space, but each rider is still a person,” he said. Koi took the new map to Odo's home. He looked at the clinic stop and said it was close enough for him to walk. Then he asked if he could help make a quiet sound for the bus doors, so people with poor sight would know when they opened. Koi said they should test it with real passengers first. Odo was happy to try.

當晚，柯伊在路線計畫新增兩條說明。一條要求保持通往診所廁所的路線暢通；另一條規定司機在提供身體協助前，先詢問乘客。丹洛讀過後同意。「巴士是共用空間，但每位乘客仍是獨立的人。」他說。柯伊把新地圖帶給奧多。奧多看了診所站，認為那個距離自己走得到，又問能不能替巴士車門做一個安靜提示音，讓視力不好的乘客知道門何時打開。柯伊說應先請真正的乘客測試。奧多很樂意幫忙。
