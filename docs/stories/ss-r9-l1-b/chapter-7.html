西花園的課

A Class in the West Garden

蒲公英鎮的慢慢巴士 · 第 7 / 25 章 · 1000以下 · 英中對照 · 校訂中

On Saturday, Nika held a class in a large garden on the west side of town. She wanted neighbors to learn how to read the new route map. A rabbit brought chocolate for the children, and a housewife named Mrs. Fira brought flowers for the table. Koi made a list of the places people wanted to visit. The main rule of the class was simple: nobody had to be good at reading maps to ask for help. One older student had never used a map before. Nika let him move colored stones to show where he lived. By the end of the first hour, he could point to the park and find a safe walking path to the nearest stop.

星期六，妮卡在鎮西邊的大花園開了一堂課，教鄰居認識新路線圖。一隻兔子替孩子們帶來巧克力，家庭主婦菲拉太太則送了花來擺桌。柯伊列出大家想去的地方。課堂的主要原則很簡單：不擅長看地圖的人也能求助。有位年長學員從沒用過地圖。妮卡讓他用彩色石子指出自己住在哪裡。第一個小時結束前，他已能指出公園，並找到通往最近站點的安全步道。

Koi walked around the tables to collect questions. One child asked whether the bus would wait until every passenger arrived, even if a whole group was late. Dano, who had joined the class, explained that a bus needed a real start time. People at the next stop would be waiting too. They could make the times clear and leave a few minutes between stops, but the driver could not wait forever. A girl wrote a note asking for an early ride on the day her father went to the market. Nika thanked her and put the note on the board. There, every request was taken seriously, even when the answer could not be yes.

柯伊在桌間走動，收集問題。一個孩子問，如果整群乘客遲到，巴士會不會一直等。來上課的丹洛解釋，巴士必須有真正的發車時間，因為下一站的人也在等。他們能把時間寫清楚，站與站之間留幾分鐘，但司機不能永遠等下去。一個女孩寫紙條，希望爸爸去市場那天有早班車。妮卡謝謝她，把紙條貼在板上。每個要求都會被認真對待，即使答案不一定是「可以」。

When the class took a break, Koi saw that the west garden path was narrow. A real bus could not pass through it. Some neighbors hoped to move the stop closer to the flower beds, but that would take away the only safe walking space. Koi asked everyone to look at the garden from the gate. They saw an open road beyond the fence and a large tree with shade. “There is where we can begin,” she said. The garden could still be a popular meeting place, and people could walk a short way to the safe stop. A careful plan did not need to put a bus everywhere people liked to gather.

下課休息時，柯伊注意到西花園小徑很窄，真正的巴士根本無法開進去。有些鄰居希望把站點移近花圃，但那會佔去唯一安全的步道。柯伊請大家從大門口看花園。他們看到圍籬外有條開闊道路，還有一棵能遮陰的大樹。「我們可以從那裡開始。」她說。花園仍可作為受歡迎的聚會地點，大家走一小段路就能到安全站點。周全的計畫不必讓巴士開到每個人喜歡聚集的地方。

At the end of the course, Nika asked people to draw their own trip from home to a stop. She found that several children thought the line on the map meant the bus would come down their small streets. That was not real. She changed the picture, using a thick road line for the bus and a dotted line for walking. Koi helped her write a short explanation below it. They did not start printing maps until the children understood the difference. If young readers made the same mistake, adults might too. A few simple marks could save many confusing mornings later.

課程結束前，妮卡請大家畫出從家裡到站點的路。她發現有幾個孩子以為地圖上的線代表巴士會開進自家小巷，實際上並非如此。她修改畫法，把巴士路線畫成粗線，步行路段則畫成點線。柯伊幫忙在下方寫一小段說明。直到孩子們都明白差別，她們才準備印製地圖。如果小讀者會看錯，大人也可能會。一點清楚的標記，往後就能省去許多混亂的早晨。

Before the last visitor left the class, Nika invited everyone to try the map without her speaking. The rabbit pointed to the west stop and followed the dotted walking line with one finger. Mrs. Fira then tried the same thing, but stopped at a picture she did not understand. Nika thanked her for showing the problem. She had drawn a little flower to mean the garden, but the flower looked like a star when printed small. They changed it to a clear garden gate. Koi saw how a quiet question could improve a whole page. She made a note to test the new picture with people who had not attended the class.

最後一位訪客離開課堂前，妮卡請大家在她不提示的情況下試讀地圖。兔子指著西站，用手指沿點線走向花園。菲拉太太也試一次，卻在一個看不懂的圖示前停下。妮卡謝謝她指出問題。她原本畫小花代表花園，縮小印出後卻像星星。他們把圖示改成清楚的花園大門。柯伊看見，一個安靜的提問就能改善整張圖。她記下還要找沒上過課的人測試新圖片。

Mrs. Fira stayed after the other students left. She told Koi that she had spent years looking after her family and rarely went anywhere for herself. “I want to visit my sister on the west road,” she said. “That is not a big reason, but it is mine.” Koi made sure her wish was on the list. She had heard many requests for work, food, or health. She was glad to hear one about seeing a loved person. Dano closed the garden gate and said the real test of the route would be whether ordinary days became a little easier. Koi nodded. That was what the slow bus was meant to do.

其他學員離開後，菲拉太太留了下來。她說自己多年照顧家人，很少為了自己出門。「我想去西路探望妹妹，」她說，「這不是什麼偉大的理由，但它是我的理由。」柯伊確認這份心願已列入清單。她聽過不少關於工作、食物和健康的要求，很高興也有人只是想見親愛的人。丹洛關上花園大門，說真正的路線測試應該看平凡的日子是否更容易過。柯伊點頭。這正是慢慢巴士的目的。
