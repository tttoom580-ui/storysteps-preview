傍晚測試

A Test in the Evening

蒲公英鎮的慢慢巴士 · 第 10 / 25 章 · 1000以下 · 英中對照 · 校訂中

Dano found a small bus that the town could borrow for one evening. It had a clear door and a button near the driver, but it did not yet have the signs for the new route. Dano had checked that his driving permit covered this vehicle. Koi walked around it slowly, checking the windows and steps. A student from the school came to watch, carrying a menu from his mother's food stand. He had been helping her sell sweet potato cakes. “My mother usually closes after dark,” he said. “She worries about the walk home.” Koi asked him to watch the road during the test, not the bus itself. They needed to know where people walked and where a car might appear suddenly.

丹洛找到一輛小巴士，鎮上可以借用一個傍晚。車子有清楚的車門，司機旁也有按鈕，但還沒有新路線的標誌。丹洛已確認自己的駕駛許可適用於這輛車。柯伊慢慢繞車一圈，檢查窗戶與踏階。一位學生來看測試，手上拿著媽媽小吃攤的菜單。他平日幫忙賣甜馬鈴薯糕。「媽媽通常天黑後才收攤，」他說，「她很擔心回家的路。」柯伊請他觀察道路，而不只是看巴士。他們必須知道人們在哪裡走路，也要知道車子可能從哪裡突然出現。

The first turn went well. Then they came to a street lined with rose bushes. Dano pressed the turn button and moved slowly to the left. Koi heard a sharp sound as a branch touched the side mirror. Dano stopped. The branch was light, but it showed that the road was too narrow for the bus to pass comfortably. They did not try to push through. Instead, they walked the corner and measured the space again. The student said he had never found road measuring interesting before, but now he watched every little movement. One small sound had saved them from bigger trouble.

第一個轉彎很順利。接著，他們來到種滿玫瑰的街道。丹洛按下方向燈，慢慢向左轉。柯伊聽到尖銳一聲，原來樹枝碰到後照鏡。丹洛停車。枝條很輕，卻顯示道路太窄，巴士無法舒適通過。他們沒有硬開過去，而是下車重新量轉角。學生說，以前從不覺得量馬路有趣，現在卻仔細看每個小動作。一個細小的聲音，讓他們避開了更大的麻煩。

Ressa was waiting at a marked place near the park. She smiled when she saw the bus, but she did not try to get on because no passengers were allowed during this road test. Koi explained the rule and asked her to stay behind the line. Ressa agreed and used her watch to check the arrival time. The bus came seven minutes later than the draft timetable. “It is better to learn that now,” she said. “A real passenger might have a doctor to see.” Dano wrote down the delay. He had not wanted to disappoint anyone, but he would rather have a true number than a pleasant guess.

瑞莎在公園附近標好的位置等候。她看到巴士便笑了，卻沒有上車，因為這次路測還不開放乘客。柯伊解釋規則，請她待在候車線後。瑞莎答應，並用手錶記錄到站時間。巴士比草擬時刻表晚了七分鐘。「現在發現比較好，」她說，「真正的乘客可能要去看醫生。」丹洛記下延誤。他不想讓人失望，但比起好聽的猜測，他更願意有真實數字。

At the end of the test, they stayed in the parking area and shared what they had seen. The student had noticed a busy door near his mother's food stand. Koi showed him that it was not a safe place to wait. They found a quieter stop by a wide lamp instead. Dano said they had to choose between changing the road and changing the route. Road work would take time and money, while a new route might leave some houses farther away. Nobody had a perfect answer. Koi drew both choices on paper, and they agreed to ask the affected families before making another test.

測試結束後，大家留在停車區交流觀察。學生注意到媽媽小吃攤附近有扇經常開關的門。柯伊告訴他，那裡並不適合候車。他們在寬敞路燈旁找到了較安靜的站點。丹洛說，他們得在修改道路與更改路線之間做選擇。道路工程費時費錢，新路線則可能讓一些住家離站點更遠。沒有人有完美答案。柯伊在紙上畫出兩種選擇，大家同意先問受影響家庭的意見，再做下一次測試。

As Koi walked home, a leaf began to fall from a tree above the rose street. It landed on the narrow corner where the mirror had touched a branch. Koi paused, worried that the leaf meant more plants could fall into the road in a storm. She called Dano and asked him to look at the corner again in daylight. He thanked her, but reminded her not to worry about every leaf as though it were a danger. They needed to check the whole tree and listen to the people who cared for it. Koi allowed herself to smile at the sensible answer. She had begun to notice problems; now she had to learn which ones needed action.

柯伊走回家時，一片葉子從玫瑰街上方的樹落下，剛好掉在後照鏡碰到枝條的狹窄轉角。她停下來，擔心暴風雨時會有更多植物掉到路上。她打電話給丹洛，請他白天再看一次。丹洛謝謝她，也提醒她不必把每片落葉都當成危險。應該檢查整棵樹，並聽聽照顧樹木的人的意見。柯伊聽了，終於讓自己露出笑容。她已學會注意問題，如今還得學會哪些問題需要行動。

When Koi finally went home, the sky was dark, but she felt calm. The bus had not followed every line she had drawn. It had turned late, met a branch, and taken longer than expected. Even so, it had returned without anyone getting hurt. She wrote the true time beside each stop and added a wide space for notes. She also drew a small rose by the narrow corner so she would remember it. She did not want the next test to be perfect in a story. She wanted it to be safer in real life. On her desk, the first red-star map was growing into something more careful and more useful.

柯伊終於回家時，天已黑了，心裡卻很平靜。巴士沒有完全照她畫的線走，轉彎晚了、碰到樹枝，也比預期花更多時間。即使如此，車子平安回來，沒有人受傷。她在每站旁寫下真實時間，另留出寬敞的備註空間。她還在狹窄轉角旁畫了一朵小玫瑰，提醒自己別忘。她不想讓下一次測試只在故事裡完美，而是想讓現實中的測試更安全。桌上那張最初的紅星地圖，正逐漸變成更謹慎也更實用的東西。
