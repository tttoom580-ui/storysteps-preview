月升時的農場路

The Farm Road at Moonrise

蒲公英鎮的慢慢巴士 · 第 5 / 25 章 · 1000以下 · 英中對照

用互動版查詞、朗讀與收藏

The first farm on the south road had many workers, but no one was sure where a bus could turn safely. Koi visited with Dano and a teenager named Elmo, who helped feed the hens. Elmo carried a yellow string and a small bottle of oil for the farm gate. “My father wants to sell fruit at the market,” he said. “The road looks straight on your map, but the ground is not.” Koi followed him through the fields. One part of the road seemed hard and dry. A few steps later, her toe sank into soft soil. “That is strange,” she said. Elmo laughed. “Only if you have never walked here after rain.”

南路的第一座農場有許多工人，卻沒人確定巴士在哪裡能安全迴轉。柯伊與丹洛去探查，還有一位叫艾莫的少年帶路。艾莫平日幫忙餵雞，手裡拿著黃色繩子與一小瓶供農場大門使用的油。「爸爸想把水果拿到市場賣，」他說，「地圖上的路看起來筆直，地面卻不是。」柯伊跟他穿過田地。一段路似乎又硬又乾，走幾步後，她的腳趾卻陷進軟土裡。「真奇怪。」她說。艾莫笑了。「下雨後走過這裡就不奇怪了。」

They used the string to mark the dry part of the road and measured how much room a slow bus would need. Dano asked where rainwater went. Elmo showed them a narrow channel beside the fence. If they blocked it, water would run through the farm gate instead. Koi wrote a letter to the owner asking permission to test the wide yard, not the soft field. The owner read it carefully. “This is a new topic for us,” she said. “We think about crops every day, but not about big vehicles.” She agreed to a small test when the ground was dry. Nobody was allowed to drive a real bus into the yard yet.

他們用繩子標記乾燥路段，測量慢速巴士需要多大的空間。丹洛問雨水會往哪裡流，艾莫帶他們看圍籬旁的窄水溝。如果堵住水溝，水便會流進農場大門。柯伊寫信給農場主人，請求在寬闊院子測試，而不是在鬆軟田地裡。主人仔細讀完。「這對我們是個新題目，」她說，「我們每天想的是作物，不是大型車輛。」她同意等地面乾燥後做小型測試，但還不准真正的巴士開進院子。

That evening, the workers shared a simple movie made on Elmo's phone. It showed a wagon turning in the yard and passing the water channel without touching it. The moon rose as they watched. Dano said the film was helpful, but it could not show how heavy a full bus would be. “We must think through the whole trip,” he said. He asked Elmo to show the driver how to enter, stop, and leave without making workers step into the road. Koi marked the safe walking path in yellow. She also wrote down where fruit boxes were placed, because a clear yard in the morning could be crowded by afternoon.

當晚，工人們分享一段用艾莫手機拍的短影片。畫面裡一台手推車在院子轉彎，安全經過水溝。大家看影片時，月亮升起了。丹洛說影片很有用，卻無法顯示滿載巴士的重量。「整趟行程都得想清楚，」他說。他請艾莫示範司機如何進入、停車、離開，又不讓工人走到馬路上。柯伊把安全步行路線標成黃色，也記下水果箱平常放在哪裡，因為早晨空曠的院子，下午可能擠得滿滿的。

On the way back, Koi asked Dano whether they should stop at every farm. He shook his head. “A route with too many turns may be too slow for people going to the clinic. We can choose a place where several farms meet.” Koi looked at the map. Three fields shared one straight road near a large tree. That might be better than turning at each gate. She wrote the idea down, then added another question: where could workers stand when the weather was bad? They had learned enough to plan a safer stop, but not enough to open it. Koi liked knowing the difference between an idea and a promise.

回程時，柯伊問丹洛是不是每座農場都要設站。丹洛搖頭。「轉彎太多，去診所的人可能會等太久。我們可以選幾座農場交會的地方。」柯伊看著地圖。三片田地在一棵大樹旁共用同一條直路，也許比每個大門都轉進去好。她寫下想法，又加了一個問題：天氣不好時，工人要在哪裡等車？他們已經學到足以規劃更安全站點的事，卻還沒準備好開放。柯伊很喜歡自己終於分得清「想法」與「承諾」。

On the next day, Koi went to the town bank to ask how a small public fund could be recorded. The clerk did not give her money. Instead, he explained how the council could keep clear notes of what was received and spent. Koi copied his example onto a page beside her farm map. She had gone looking for a safe place to stop a bus, yet a road also needed a way to pay for care over time. She thanked the clerk, then visited the farm again. The owner was pleased to see that Koi was asking about costs before the service began, rather than waiting for trouble to arrive.

隔天，柯伊到鎮上的銀行詢問小額公共基金該如何記錄。行員沒有給她錢，而是說明議會應如何清楚記下收入與支出。柯伊把範例抄在農場地圖旁。她原本是在找安全停車的地方，如今也明白，維持道路服務需要長期經費。她謝謝行員，再次去農場。農場主人見她在服務開始前就先查清費用，而不是等出問題才處理，感到很高興。

Before bed, Koi visited Ressa to report the day's work. Ressa was listening to a song about a bright moon. She asked where the first bus would go. “We do not know yet,” Koi answered. “The farm, the pond, the school, and the clinic all matter.” Ressa pointed to the red star on the map. “Remember the people who cannot walk to any of those places,” she said. Koi nodded. That was why the route had started. As she walked home, the moon followed her over the fields. She thought of the yellow string stretched beside the water channel. A good line was not just the shortest line; it was one that respected the people and the land on both sides.

睡前，柯伊去向瑞莎報告今天的工作。瑞莎正在聽一首歌，歌裡唱著明亮的月亮。她問第一班巴士會去哪裡。「還不知道，」柯伊回答，「農場、池塘、學校與診所都很重要。」瑞莎指著地圖上的紅星。「別忘了那些連這幾個地方都走不到的人。」她說。柯伊點頭。這正是路線開始的原因。回家時，月亮一路跟著她走過田地。她想起水溝旁的黃色繩子。好的線不只是最短的線，也是能尊重兩側居民與土地的線。
