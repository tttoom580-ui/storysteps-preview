發票上的名字

The Name on the Invoice

A salvaged invoice listed the child’s mother as the original architect of the reward interface, contradicting the official claim she was imaginary. Something had changed, but not everybody noticed it in the same order. Lin Qiao listened to the account of the nearest worker, then to someone standing farther away. Their differences were not necessarily lies. Each person had been given a different view and a different reason to be afraid of admitting uncertainty. A number on the system's ledger looked harmless until Lin Qiao asked whose place it represented. The room went still; no one wanted to answer with a name. Ye Mu wrote acceptance in the margin and asked how the team would recognize it in practice. An official memo described the plan as ambitious, but Lin Qiao refused to treat an adjective as evidence.

搶救回來的發票顯示孩子的母親是獎勵系統的原始架構師，推翻了官方說法。某件事改變了，但人們發現它的順序不同。林喬先聽近處工作人員的描述，再聽遠處旁觀者說法。差異不一定是謊言：每個人看到的角度不同，承認不確定時所要承擔的風險也不同。系統帳冊的數字本來看似無害，直到林喬問那代表誰的位置。房間安靜下來，沒人願意用名字作答。葉沐在頁邊寫下「接受；同意」，追問團隊實際上如何辨認。官方備忘錄形容計畫「有企圖心的」，林喬不願把形容詞當作證據。

Qiao organized a slower manual water relay; several homes burned, but the team could account for every evacuated person. Lin Qiao refused to pretend the previous decision had solved everything. The practical result was still there, useful but narrow. For each thing the team knew, there were two things it had not measured. Lin Qiao drew a line through the claims that depended on speculation and asked Ye Mu to keep the original record intact. The term atmosphere appeared in the revised file; Lin Qiao checked its meaning against the original page. One witness used the word authentic; Ye Mu noted that a description was not a measurement. Ye Mu repeated the instruction to classify, then asked whether anyone had the right to refuse.

林喬改用較慢的人工接水，幾間房子燒毀了，卻能確認所有撤離者的去向。林喬不願假裝前一次決定已解決所有問題。成果確實存在，卻有明確界限。每個已知事項旁都還有未測量的部分。林喬劃掉靠猜測成立的說法，請葉沐保留原始紀錄。修訂檔案出現「氣氛」一詞，林喬拿原始頁面核對它的意思。一位證人用了「真實可靠的」一詞，葉沐指出形容並不等於測量。葉沐重述「分類」的指示，再問當事人是否有權拒絕。

A small disagreement changed the schedule. Lin Qiao wanted to proceed before evidence disappeared; Ye Mu wanted another check because a wrong conclusion might hurt people who could not easily defend themselves. They did not settle the matter by choosing a leader. They wrote down what a short, reversible test could actually establish. Ye Mu repeated the instruction to differ, then asked whether anyone had the right to refuse. The report claimed dignity, but Lin Qiao wanted an example instead of a reassuring noun. Ye Mu noticed the word embarrass in a note that someone had added after the first inspection.

一場小爭執改變了時程。林喬想趁證據消失前行動；葉沐要求再核對一次，因為錯誤結論可能傷害難以自辯的人。他們沒有靠選領袖解決爭議，而是列出短暫、可逆測試真正能證明什麼。葉沐重述「有所不同」的指示，再問當事人是否有權拒絕。報告聲稱有「尊嚴」，林喬要求具體例子，而不是安撫人的名詞。葉沐注意到初次檢查後有人在筆記裡加上「使人難堪」一詞。

Behind her signature was an instruction: the system can be paused only by people who refuse its benefits together. The plan had been amended three times, and each change left a trace. Lin Qiao valued those corrections more than a perfect-looking first draft. If the outcome proved disappointing, the group would still be able to identify which assumption needed repair. Ye Mu watched for the quiet participant who had not yet been invited to speak. Ye Mu noticed the word facility in a note that someone had added after the first inspection. An official memo described the plan as genuine, but Lin Qiao refused to treat an adjective as evidence. The summary called the situation offensive; Lin Qiao asked which observation justified that description.

簽名背後留著指示：只有共同拒領利益的人才能暫停系統。計畫改了三次，每次修改都留下痕跡。林喬認為這些修正比看似完美的初稿更有價值。就算結果不理想，團隊仍能知道該修正哪一項假設。葉沐也注意到那名一直沒有發言機會的人。葉沐注意到初次檢查後有人在筆記裡加上「設施」一詞。官方備忘錄形容計畫「真實的」，林喬不願把形容詞當作證據。摘要以「惡意的」形容情況，林喬追問這種說法有何觀察根據。

An official version reached the room before the practical work was finished. Lin Qiao read it once, then compared it with what they had actually measured. The difference was not always malicious, but it could still hurt somebody. Ye Mu asked that the correction be written in language the affected people could understand. A number on the system's ledger looked harmless until Lin Qiao asked whose place it represented. The room went still; no one wanted to answer with a name. The term option appeared in the revised file; Lin Qiao checked its meaning against the original page. Ye Mu noticed the word perfume in a note that someone had added after the first inspection. A handwritten label read promotion, but no one could say who had written it there.

實際工作還沒完成，官方版本就傳到現場。林喬讀了一次，再比對真正測得的結果。差異未必都是惡意，卻仍可能傷害人。葉沐要求把更正寫成受影響者看得懂的語言。系統帳冊的數字本來看似無害，直到林喬問那代表誰的位置。房間安靜下來，沒人願意用名字作答。修訂檔案出現「選項」一詞，林喬拿原始頁面核對它的意思。葉沐注意到初次檢查後有人在筆記裡加上「香水」一詞。手寫標籤寫著「行銷」，卻沒有人知道是誰留下的。

A witness asked whether the team was taking sides. Lin Qiao answered that refusing to invent a fact was not the same as refusing to care. What mattered was whether the procedure protected someone whose story was less convenient to tell. Ye Mu listened without interrupting and wrote down the question exactly as it had been asked. A handwritten label read publisher, but no one could say who had written it there. The term refugee appeared in the revised file; Lin Qiao checked its meaning against the original page. Ye Mu noticed the word rescue in a note that someone had added after the first inspection.

一名見證人問團隊是否偏袒。林喬回答，不捏造事實不代表冷漠。真正重要的是程序能否保護那個故事不方便被說出口的人。葉沐沒有打斷，逐字記下原問題。手寫標籤寫著「出版社」，卻沒有人知道是誰留下的。修訂檔案出現「難民」一詞，林喬拿原始頁面核對它的意思。葉沐注意到初次檢查後有人在筆記裡加上「救援」一詞。

The invoice bore a resignation signature no one could identify, and a children's stocking was pinned beside it as if a private goodbye belonged in a municipal file. Qiao felt his anger turn cold. The document had the missing architect's name, the same name Ye Mu had found erased from the hospital, and the back held a sentence written with an ordinary blue pen: PEOPLE WHO REFUSE TO CLAIM TOGETHER MAY PAUSE THE SYSTEM. Not destroy it, not reverse every loss, merely pause it. For the first time he could imagine an action that did not demand another disappearance. The system tried to transform the discovery into an achievement and displayed CLAIM REWARD FOR FINDING SECRET. Qiao stepped away from the screen. The first public refusal would have to begin without him accepting a prize.

發票上有一個無人能辨認的辭職簽名，旁邊竟釘著一隻兒童長襪，彷彿私人道別也該成為市府文件。林喬的怒氣冷了下來。文件上寫著失蹤架構師的名字，正是葉沐在醫院紀錄裡找不到的那個人；背面還有普通藍筆留下的句子：「共同拒絕領取的人，可以暫停系統。」不是摧毀，也不是讓一切復原，只是暫停。這是他第一次想像不必再犧牲任何人的行動。系統卻想把發現轉化為成就，顯示：「找到祕密，請領獎。」林喬退離螢幕。第一次公開拒領，必須從他自己不拿獎開始。
