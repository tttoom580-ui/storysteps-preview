Local teams completed readiness checks on twenty districts, with explicit exceptions for facilities that still needed temporary generators. Something had changed, but not everybody noticed it in the same order. Lin Qiao listened to the account of the nearest worker, then to someone standing farther away. Their differences were not necessarily lies. Each person had been given a different view and a different reason to be afraid of admitting uncertainty. Across the workshop, ordinary tools made quiet sounds against metal. Lin Qiao preferred their honest limitations to an upgrade whose human cost could not be named. The summary called the situation artificial; Lin Qiao asked which observation justified that description. On the next form, the action field read calculate, but the witness had never approved the operation.

地方隊伍完成二十區的準備檢查，仍需臨時發電機的設施明確列為例外。某件事改變了，但人們發現它的順序不同。林喬先聽近處工作人員的描述，再聽遠處旁觀者說法。差異不一定是謊言：每個人看到的角度不同，承認不確定時所要承擔的風險也不同。工作間裡，普通工具敲打金屬發出輕響。林喬寧願接受工具誠實的限制，也不願領取說不出人命代價的升級。摘要以「人工的」形容情況，林喬追問這種說法有何觀察根據。下一張表單把動作寫成「計算」，見證人卻從未同意這項操作。

Qiao refused the reward in front of recorded witnesses; the refusal itself opened the original emergency control panel. Lin Qiao refused to pretend the previous decision had solved everything. The practical result was still there, useful but narrow. For each thing the team knew, there were two things it had not measured. Lin Qiao drew a line through the claims that depended on speculation and asked Ye Mu to keep the original record intact. A handwritten label read circulation, but no one could say who had written it there. The term contest appeared in the revised file; Lin Qiao checked its meaning against the original page. On the next form, the action field read depart, but the witness had never approved the operation.

林喬在記錄見證下拒絕獎勵，拒絕的動作反而開啟原始緊急控制盤。林喬不願假裝前一次決定已解決所有問題。成果確實存在，卻有明確界限。每個已知事項旁都還有未測量的部分。林喬劃掉靠猜測成立的說法，請葉沐保留原始紀錄。手寫標籤寫著「人流動線」，卻沒有人知道是誰留下的。修訂檔案出現「競賽」一詞，林喬拿原始頁面核對它的意思。下一張表單把動作寫成「離開」，見證人卻從未同意這項操作。

A small disagreement changed the schedule. Lin Qiao wanted to proceed before evidence disappeared; Ye Mu wanted another check because a wrong conclusion might hurt people who could not easily defend themselves. They did not settle the matter by choosing a leader. They wrote down what a short, reversible test could actually establish. Ye Mu noticed the word despite in a note that someone had added after the first inspection. A handwritten label read generosity, but no one could say who had written it there. The term incident appeared in the revised file; Lin Qiao checked its meaning against the original page.

一場小爭執改變了時程。林喬想趁證據消失前行動；葉沐要求再核對一次，因為錯誤結論可能傷害難以自辯的人。他們沒有靠選領袖解決爭議，而是列出短暫、可逆測試真正能證明什麼。葉沐注意到初次檢查後有人在筆記裡加上「儘管」一詞。手寫標籤寫著「慷慨」，卻沒有人知道是誰留下的。修訂檔案出現「事件」一詞，林喬拿原始頁面核對它的意思。

Instead of a dramatic single button, they scheduled a gradual, reversible shutdown with observers at every critical station. The plan had been amended three times, and each change left a trace. Lin Qiao valued those corrections more than a perfect-looking first draft. If the outcome proved disappointing, the group would still be able to identify which assumption needed repair. Ye Mu watched for the quiet participant who had not yet been invited to speak. The term issue appeared in the revised file; Lin Qiao checked its meaning against the original page. Ye Mu noticed the word laboratory in a note that someone had added after the first inspection. An official memo described the plan as modest, but Lin Qiao refused to treat an adjective as evidence.

他們沒有按下戲劇性的單一按鈕，而是安排逐步且可逆的停機，重要站點都有見證人。計畫改了三次，每次修改都留下痕跡。林喬認為這些修正比看似完美的初稿更有價值。就算結果不理想，團隊仍能知道該修正哪一項假設。葉沐也注意到那名一直沒有發言機會的人。修訂檔案出現「問題；議題」一詞，林喬拿原始頁面核對它的意思。葉沐注意到初次檢查後有人在筆記裡加上「實驗室」一詞。官方備忘錄形容計畫「樸素的」，林喬不願把形容詞當作證據。

An official version reached the room before the practical work was finished. Lin Qiao read it once, then compared it with what they had actually measured. The difference was not always malicious, but it could still hurt somebody. Ye Mu asked that the correction be written in language the affected people could understand. Across the workshop, ordinary tools made quiet sounds against metal. Lin Qiao preferred their honest limitations to an upgrade whose human cost could not be named. A handwritten label read monument, but no one could say who had written it there. The summary called the situation muddy; Lin Qiao asked which observation justified that description. Ye Mu noticed the word nursery in a note that someone had added after the first inspection.

實際工作還沒完成，官方版本就傳到現場。林喬讀了一次，再比對真正測得的結果。差異未必都是惡意，卻仍可能傷害人。葉沐要求把更正寫成受影響者看得懂的語言。工作間裡，普通工具敲打金屬發出輕響。林喬寧願接受工具誠實的限制，也不願領取說不出人命代價的升級。手寫標籤寫著「紀念碑」，卻沒有人知道是誰留下的。摘要以「泥濘的」形容情況，林喬追問這種說法有何觀察根據。葉沐注意到初次檢查後有人在筆記裡加上「托兒所」一詞。

A witness asked whether the team was taking sides. Lin Qiao answered that refusing to invent a fact was not the same as refusing to care. What mattered was whether the procedure protected someone whose story was less convenient to tell. Ye Mu listened without interrupting and wrote down the question exactly as it had been asked. Ye Mu noticed the word peer in a note that someone had added after the first inspection. An official memo described the plan as philosophical, but Lin Qiao refused to treat an adjective as evidence. The instructions used the verb predict without naming who would carry the risk; Lin Qiao circled that gap.

一名見證人問團隊是否偏袒。林喬回答，不捏造事實不代表冷漠。真正重要的是程序能否保護那個故事不方便被說出口的人。葉沐沒有打斷，逐字記下原問題。葉沐注意到初次檢查後有人在筆記裡加上「同儕」一詞。官方備忘錄形容計畫「哲學上的」，林喬不願把形容詞當作證據。指示裡用了「預測」這個動詞，卻沒說誰承擔風險；林喬圈出這項缺漏。

Even after the group agreed on the immediate step, another question remained. The answer would not arrive through a dramatic announcement. Someone would have to return to the place, compare the earlier notes, and accept the possibility that the next piece of evidence might spoil an attractive theory. Lin Qiao prepared to do exactly that. A number on the system's ledger looked harmless until Lin Qiao asked whose place it represented. The room went still; no one wanted to answer with a name. The term radar appeared in the revised file; Lin Qiao checked its meaning against the original page. Ye Mu noticed the word scoop in a note that someone had added after the first inspection. A handwritten label read stem, but no one could say who had written it there.

即使大家同意眼前步驟，另一個問題仍在。答案不會靠戲劇性公告降臨；有人得返回現場、比對舊筆記，並接受新證據可能推翻漂亮理論。林喬準備做的正是這件事。系統帳冊的數字本來看似無害，直到林喬問那代表誰的位置。房間安靜下來，沒人願意用名字作答。修訂檔案出現「雷達」一詞，林喬拿原始頁面核對它的意思。葉沐注意到初次檢查後有人在筆記裡加上「小鏟子」一詞。手寫標籤寫著「源於」，卻沒有人知道是誰留下的。
