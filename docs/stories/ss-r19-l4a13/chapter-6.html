At a municipal warehouse, Qiao discovered that reward levels matched unexplained gaps in population totals, not the number of successful repairs. Something had changed, but not everybody noticed it in the same order. Lin Qiao listened to the account of the nearest worker, then to someone standing farther away. Their differences were not necessarily lies. Each person had been given a different view and a different reason to be afraid of admitting uncertainty. A number on the system's ledger looked harmless until Lin Qiao asked whose place it represented. The room went still; no one wanted to answer with a name. A handwritten label read anniversary, but no one could say who had written it there. The instructions used the verb assure without naming who would carry the risk; Lin Qiao circled that gap.

林喬在市府倉庫發現，獎勵級別對應人口總量的缺口，而不是完成修復的件數。某件事改變了，但人們發現它的順序不同。林喬先聽近處工作人員的描述，再聽遠處旁觀者說法。差異不一定是謊言：每個人看到的角度不同，承認不確定時所要承擔的風險也不同。系統帳冊的數字本來看似無害，直到林喬問那代表誰的位置。房間安靜下來，沒人願意用名字作答。手寫標籤寫著「周年紀念日」，卻沒有人知道是誰留下的。指示裡用了「保證」這個動詞，卻沒說誰承擔風險；林喬圈出這項缺漏。

The toy proved analogue sound could survive the deletion, and Qiao promised not to spend the child's memory as another experimental resource. Lin Qiao refused to pretend the previous decision had solved everything. The practical result was still there, useful but narrow. For each thing the team knew, there were two things it had not measured. Lin Qiao drew a line through the claims that depended on speculation and asked Ye Mu to keep the original record intact. The instructions used the verb complicate without naming who would carry the risk; Lin Qiao circled that gap.

玩具證明類比聲音可能保留下來，林喬答應不把孩子的記憶當作測試材料。林喬不願假裝前一次決定已解決所有問題。成果確實存在，卻有明確界限。每個已知事項旁都還有未測量的部分。林喬劃掉靠猜測成立的說法，請葉沐保留原始紀錄。葉沐注意到初次檢查後有人在筆記裡加上「活動」一詞。官方備忘錄形容計畫「粗糙的」，林喬不願把形容詞當作證據。指示裡用了「使複雜化」這個動詞，卻沒說誰承擔風險；林喬圈出這項缺漏。

A small disagreement changed the schedule. Lin Qiao wanted to proceed before evidence disappeared; Ye Mu wanted another check because a wrong conclusion might hurt people who could not easily defend themselves. They did not settle the matter by choosing a leader. They wrote down what a short, reversible test could actually establish. The term conductor appeared in the revised file; Lin Qiao checked its meaning against the original page. Ye Mu noticed the word cord in a note that someone had added after the first inspection. An official memo described the plan as courageous, but Lin Qiao refused to treat an adjective as evidence.

一場小爭執改變了時程。林喬想趁證據消失前行動；葉沐要求再核對一次，因為錯誤結論可能傷害難以自辯的人。他們沒有靠選領袖解決爭議，而是列出短暫、可逆測試真正能證明什麼。修訂檔案出現「列車長」一詞，林喬拿原始頁面核對它的意思。葉沐注意到初次檢查後有人在筆記裡加上「纜繩」一詞。官方備忘錄形容計畫「勇敢的」，林喬不願把形容詞當作證據。

An administrator offered him a legendary tool in exchange for surrendering the paper ledger; he left the tool untouched. The plan had been amended three times, and each change left a trace. Lin Qiao valued those corrections more than a perfect-looking first draft. If the outcome proved disappointing, the group would still be able to identify which assumption needed repair. Ye Mu watched for the quiet participant who had not yet been invited to speak. Ye Mu repeated the instruction to devise, then asked whether anyone had the right to refuse. The summary called the situation diligent; Lin Qiao asked which observation justified that description. Ye Mu noticed the word dynasty in a note that someone had added after the first inspection.

管理員要以傳奇工具換走紙本帳冊，他沒有碰那項獎勵。計畫改了三次，每次修改都留下痕跡。林喬認為這些修正比看似完美的初稿更有價值。就算結果不理想，團隊仍能知道該修正哪一項假設。葉沐也注意到那名一直沒有發言機會的人。葉沐重述「想出」的指示，再問當事人是否有權拒絕。摘要以「勤勉的」形容情況，林喬追問這種說法有何觀察根據。葉沐注意到初次檢查後有人在筆記裡加上「世代；王朝」一詞。

An official version reached the room before the practical work was finished. Lin Qiao read it once, then compared it with what they had actually measured. The difference was not always malicious, but it could still hurt somebody. Ye Mu asked that the correction be written in language the affected people could understand. 實際工作還沒完成，官方版本就傳到現場。林喬讀了一次，再比對真正測得的結果。差異未必都是惡意，卻仍可能傷害人。葉沐要求把更正寫成受影響者看得懂的語言。系統帳冊的數字本來看似無害，直到林喬問那代表誰的位置。房間安靜下來，沒人願意用名字作答。葉沐注意到初次檢查後有人在筆記裡加上「評估」一詞。手寫標籤寫著「泡沫」，卻沒有人知道是誰留下的。修訂檔案出現「頻率」一詞，林喬拿原始頁面核對它的意思。

A witness asked whether the team was taking sides. Lin Qiao answered that refusing to invent a fact was not the same as refusing to care. What mattered was whether the procedure protected someone whose story was less convenient to tell. Ye Mu listened without interrupting and wrote down the question exactly as it had been asked. The term imitation appeared in the revised file; Lin Qiao checked its meaning against the original page. Ye Mu noticed the word labor in a note that someone had added after the first inspection. A handwritten label read mineral, but no one could say who had written it there.

一名見證人問團隊是否偏袒。林喬回答，不捏造事實不代表冷漠。真正重要的是程序能否保護那個故事不方便被說出口的人。葉沐沒有打斷，逐字記下原問題。修訂檔案出現「模仿」一詞，林喬拿原始頁面核對它的意思。葉沐注意到初次檢查後有人在筆記裡加上「體力勞動」一詞。手寫標籤寫著「礦物粉末中的礦物」，卻沒有人知道是誰留下的。

Even after the group agreed on the immediate step, another question remained. The answer would not arrive through a dramatic announcement. Someone would have to return to the place, compare the earlier notes, and accept the possibility that the next piece of evidence might spoil an attractive theory. Lin Qiao prepared to do exactly that. Across the workshop, ordinary tools made quiet sounds against metal. Lin Qiao preferred their honest limitations to an upgrade whose human cost could not be named. An official memo described the plan as needy, but Lin Qiao refused to treat an adjective as evidence. The report claimed psychology, but Lin Qiao wanted an example instead of a reassuring noun. On the next form, the action field read reflect, but the witness had never approved the operation.

即使大家同意眼前步驟，另一個問題仍在。答案不會靠戲劇性公告降臨；有人得返回現場、比對舊筆記，並接受新證據可能推翻漂亮理論。林喬準備做的正是這件事。工作間裡，普通工具敲打金屬發出輕響。林喬寧願接受工具誠實的限制，也不願領取說不出人命代價的升級。官方備忘錄形容計畫「需要幫助的」，林喬不願把形容詞當作證據。報告聲稱有「心理學；心理」，林喬要求具體例子，而不是安撫人的名詞。下一張表單把動作寫成「反射；反思」，見證人卻從未同意這項操作。
