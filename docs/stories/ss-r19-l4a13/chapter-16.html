廣場地下的伺服器

The Server Beneath the Square

Engineers discovered a cooling shaft beneath the plaza, leading to a server cluster that calculated rewards from missing-person credits. Something had changed, but not everybody noticed it in the same order. Lin Qiao listened to the account of the nearest worker, then to someone standing farther away. Their differences were not necessarily lies. Each person had been given a different view and a different reason to be afraid of admitting uncertainty. A number on the system's ledger looked harmless until Lin Qiao asked whose place it represented. The room went still; no one wanted to answer with a name. Ye Mu noticed the word addict in a note that someone had added after the first inspection. A handwritten label read aspect, but no one could say who had written it there.

工程師發現廣場下方的冷卻井，通往以失蹤人口作額度計算的伺服器群。某件事改變了，但人們發現它的順序不同。林喬先聽近處工作人員的描述，再聽遠處旁觀者說法。差異不一定是謊言：每個人看到的角度不同，承認不確定時所要承擔的風險也不同。系統帳冊的數字本來看似無害，直到林喬問那代表誰的位置。房間安靜下來，沒人願意用名字作答。葉沐注意到初次檢查後有人在筆記裡加上「沉迷者」一詞。手寫標籤寫著「面向」，卻沒有人知道是誰留下的。

Qiao rejected the bargain and persuaded the child's family that a possible reunion could not justify an unseen victim. Lin Qiao refused to pretend the previous decision had solved everything. The practical result was still there, useful but narrow. For each thing the team knew, there were two things it had not measured. Lin Qiao drew a line through the claims that depended on speculation and asked Ye Mu to keep the original record intact. The term celebration appeared in the revised file; Lin Qiao checked its meaning against the original page. Ye Mu noticed the word container in a note that someone had added after the first inspection. Ye Mu repeated the instruction to distinguish, then asked whether anyone had the right to refuse.

林喬拒絕交易，也勸服孩子的家人：不能用不知名的犧牲換取可能的團聚。林喬不願假裝前一次決定已解決所有問題。成果確實存在，卻有明確界限。每個已知事項旁都還有未測量的部分。林喬劃掉靠猜測成立的說法，請葉沐保留原始紀錄。修訂檔案出現「慶祝」一詞，林喬拿原始頁面核對它的意思。葉沐注意到初次檢查後有人在筆記裡加上「容器」一詞。葉沐重述「區分」的指示，再問當事人是否有權拒絕。

A small disagreement changed the schedule. Lin Qiao wanted to proceed before evidence disappeared; Ye Mu wanted another check because a wrong conclusion might hurt people who could not easily defend themselves. They did not settle the matter by choosing a leader. They wrote down what a short, reversible test could actually establish. The earlier photograph clearly showed a flea; the newer version did not, so Lin Qiao kept both. A note about the gallery lay beside the old register, with a date that Lin Qiao could not verify. Ye Mu pointed to the hive in the inventory and asked why its location had changed.

一場小爭執改變了時程。林喬想趁證據消失前行動；葉沐要求再核對一次，因為錯誤結論可能傷害難以自辯的人。他們沒有靠選領袖解決爭議，而是列出短暫、可逆測試真正能證明什麼。較早的照片清楚顯示一項「跳蚤」，新版卻沒有，林喬因此保留兩張。一張談到「展覽廳」的便條放在舊登記簿旁，日期讓林喬無法核實。葉沐指向清單上的「蜂巢」，追問其位置為何改變。

They could shut it down locally, but an untested blackout might cut off life-support machines across the city. The plan had been amended three times, and each change left a trace. Lin Qiao valued those corrections more than a perfect-looking first draft. If the outcome proved disappointing, the group would still be able to identify which assumption needed repair. Ye Mu watched for the quiet participant who had not yet been invited to speak. One witness used the word intensive; Ye Mu noted that a description was not a measurement. Ye Mu repeated the instruction to kneel, then asked whether anyone had the right to refuse. The term knob appeared in the revised file; Lin Qiao checked its meaning against the original page.

他們能在現場關機，但未測試的停電可能使全城維生設備中斷。計畫改了三次，每次修改都留下痕跡。林喬認為這些修正比看似完美的初稿更有價值。就算結果不理想，團隊仍能知道該修正哪一項假設。葉沐也注意到那名一直沒有發言機會的人。一位證人用了「密集的」一詞，葉沐指出形容並不等於測量。葉沐重述「跪下」的指示，再問當事人是否有權拒絕。修訂檔案出現「旋鈕」一詞，林喬拿原始頁面核對它的意思。

An official version reached the room before the practical work was finished. Lin Qiao read it once, then compared it with what they had actually measured. The difference was not always malicious, but it could still hurt somebody. Ye Mu asked that the correction be written in language the affected people could understand. A number on the system's ledger looked harmless until Lin Qiao asked whose place it represented. The room went still; no one wanted to answer with a name. The term lecturer appeared in the revised file; Lin Qiao checked its meaning against the original page. On the next form, the action field read namely, but the witness had never approved the operation. An official memo described the plan as partial, but Lin Qiao refused to treat an adjective as evidence.

實際工作還沒完成，官方版本就傳到現場。林喬讀了一次，再比對真正測得的結果。差異未必都是惡意，卻仍可能傷害人。葉沐要求把更正寫成受影響者看得懂的語言。系統帳冊的數字本來看似無害，直到林喬問那代表誰的位置。房間安靜下來，沒人願意用名字作答。修訂檔案出現「講師」一詞，林喬拿原始頁面核對它的意思。下一張表單把動作寫成「也就是」，見證人卻從未同意這項操作。官方備忘錄形容計畫「部分的」，林喬不願把形容詞當作證據。

A witness asked whether the team was taking sides. Lin Qiao answered that refusing to invent a fact was not the same as refusing to care. What mattered was whether the procedure protected someone whose story was less convenient to tell. Ye Mu listened without interrupting and wrote down the question exactly as it had been asked. A handwritten label read preservation, but no one could say who had written it there. The summary called the situation promising; Lin Qiao asked which observation justified that description. One witness used the word respectful; Ye Mu noted that a description was not a measurement.

一名見證人問團隊是否偏袒。林喬回答，不捏造事實不代表冷漠。真正重要的是程序能否保護那個故事不方便被說出口的人。葉沐沒有打斷，逐字記下原問題。手寫標籤寫著「保存；保護」，卻沒有人知道是誰留下的。摘要以「有希望的」形容情況，林喬追問這種說法有何觀察根據。一位證人用了「有禮貌的」一詞，葉沐指出形容並不等於測量。

Even after the group agreed on the immediate step, another question remained. The answer would not arrive through a dramatic announcement. Someone would have to return to the place, compare the earlier notes, and accept the possibility that the next piece of evidence might spoil an attractive theory. Lin Qiao prepared to do exactly that. Across the workshop, ordinary tools made quiet sounds against metal. Lin Qiao preferred their honest limitations to an upgrade whose human cost could not be named. Ye Mu noticed the word satisfaction in a note that someone had added after the first inspection. A handwritten label read sprinkle, but no one could say who had written it there. A note about the telescope lay beside the old register, with a date that Lin Qiao could not verify.

即使大家同意眼前步驟，另一個問題仍在。答案不會靠戲劇性公告降臨；有人得返回現場、比對舊筆記，並接受新證據可能推翻漂亮理論。林喬準備做的正是這件事。工作間裡，普通工具敲打金屬發出輕響。林喬寧願接受工具誠實的限制，也不願領取說不出人命代價的升級。葉沐注意到初次檢查後有人在筆記裡加上「滿意」一詞。手寫標籤寫著「撒上（粉末）」，卻沒有人知道是誰留下的。一張談到「望遠鏡」的便條放在舊登記簿旁，日期讓林喬無法核實。
