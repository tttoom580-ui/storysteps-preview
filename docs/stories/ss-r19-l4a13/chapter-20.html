A blackout interrupted the gathering, but witnesses used paper lanterns and copied records in separate locations instead of relying on one screen. Something had changed, but not everybody noticed it in the same order. Lin Qiao listened to the account of the nearest worker, then to someone standing farther away. Their differences were not necessarily lies. Each person had been given a different view and a different reason to be afraid of admitting uncertainty. A number on the system's ledger looked harmless until Lin Qiao asked whose place it represented. The room went still; no one wanted to answer with a name. The term beggar appeared in the revised file; Lin Qiao checked its meaning against the original page. Ye Mu noticed the word carrier in a note that someone had added after the first inspection.

停電中斷集會，見證人改用紙燈並分散抄錄紀錄，不再仰賴單一螢幕。某件事改變了，但人們發現它的順序不同。林喬先聽近處工作人員的描述，再聽遠處旁觀者說法。差異不一定是謊言：每個人看到的角度不同，承認不確定時所要承擔的風險也不同。系統帳冊的數字本來看似無害，直到林喬問那代表誰的位置。房間安靜下來，沒人願意用名字作答。修訂檔案出現「乞討者」一詞，林喬拿原始頁面核對它的意思。葉沐注意到初次檢查後有人在筆記裡加上「搬運員」一詞。

The server's display finally showed the entire city's real missing-person count, far above the official figure. Lin Qiao refused to pretend the previous decision had solved everything. The practical result was still there, useful but narrow. For each thing the team knew, there were two things it had not measured. Lin Qiao drew a line through the claims that depended on speculation and asked Ye Mu to keep the original record intact. A small disagreement changed the schedule. Lin Qiao wanted to proceed before evidence disappeared; Ye Mu wanted another check because a wrong conclusion might hurt people who could not easily defend themselves. They did not settle the matter by choosing a leader. They wrote down what a short, reversible test could actually establish. Ye Mu noticed the word detective in a note that someone had added after the first inspection. An official memo described the plan as diverse, but Lin Qiao refused to treat an adjective as evidence. The term frustration appeared in the revised file; Lin Qiao checked its meaning against the original page.

一場小爭執改變了時程。林喬想趁證據消失前行動；葉沐要求再核對一次，因為錯誤結論可能傷害難以自辯的人。他們沒有靠選領袖解決爭議，而是列出短暫、可逆測試真正能證明什麼。葉沐注意到初次檢查後有人在筆記裡加上「偵探」一詞。官方備忘錄形容計畫「背景不同的」，林喬不願把形容詞當作證據。修訂檔案出現「挫折」一詞，林喬拿原始頁面核對它的意思。

The contractor admitted the system could be rolled back but claimed the names it erased could not be restored instantly. The plan had been amended three times, and each change left a trace. Lin Qiao valued those corrections more than a perfect-looking first draft. If the outcome proved disappointing, the group would still be able to identify which assumption needed repair. Ye Mu watched for the quiet participant who had not yet been invited to speak. The instructions used the verb furthermore without naming who would carry the risk; Lin Qiao circled that gap. An official version reached the room before the practical work was finished. Lin Qiao read it once, then compared it with what they had actually measured. The difference was not always malicious, but it could still hurt somebody. Ye Mu asked that the correction be written in language the affected people could understand. 實際工作還沒完成，官方版本就傳到現場。林喬讀了一次，再比對真正測得的結果。差異未必都是惡意，卻仍可能傷害人。葉沐要求把更正寫成受影響者看得懂的語言。系統帳冊的數字本來看似無害，直到林喬問那代表誰的位置。房間安靜下來，沒人願意用名字作答。官方備忘錄形容計畫「文學的」，林喬不願把形容詞當作證據。修訂檔案出現「忠誠」一詞，林喬拿原始頁面核對它的意思。下一張表單把動作寫成「取得」，見證人卻從未同意這項操作。

A witness asked whether the team was taking sides. Lin Qiao answered that refusing to invent a fact was not the same as refusing to care. What mattered was whether the procedure protected someone whose story was less convenient to tell. Ye Mu listened without interrupting and wrote down the question exactly as it had been asked. Ye Mu noticed the word pioneer in a note that someone had added after the first inspection. A handwritten label read popularity, but no one could say who had written it there. The report claimed privacy, but Lin Qiao wanted an example instead of a reassuring noun.

一名見證人問團隊是否偏袒。林喬回答，不捏造事實不代表冷漠。真正重要的是程序能否保護那個故事不方便被說出口的人。葉沐沒有打斷，逐字記下原問題。葉沐注意到初次檢查後有人在筆記裡加上「開創性的」一詞。手寫標籤寫著「受歡迎程度」，卻沒有人知道是誰留下的。報告聲稱有「隱私」，林喬要求具體例子，而不是安撫人的名詞。

Even after the group agreed on the immediate step, another question remained. The answer would not arrive through a dramatic announcement. Someone would have to return to the place, compare the earlier notes, and accept the possibility that the next piece of evidence might spoil an attractive theory. Lin Qiao prepared to do exactly that. Across the workshop, ordinary tools made quiet sounds against metal. Lin Qiao preferred their honest limitations to an upgrade whose human cost could not be named. The report claimed refusal, but Lin Qiao wanted an example instead of a reassuring noun. One witness used the word tense; Ye Mu noted that a description was not a measurement. Ye Mu repeated the instruction to withdraw, then asked whether anyone had the right to refuse.

即使大家同意眼前步驟，另一個問題仍在。答案不會靠戲劇性公告降臨；有人得返回現場、比對舊筆記，並接受新證據可能推翻漂亮理論。林喬準備做的正是這件事。工作間裡，普通工具敲打金屬發出輕響。林喬寧願接受工具誠實的限制，也不願領取說不出人命代價的升級。報告聲稱有「拒絕」，林喬要求具體例子，而不是安撫人的名詞。一位證人用了「緊繃的」一詞，葉沐指出形容並不等於測量。葉沐重述「退出；撤回」的指示，再問當事人是否有權拒絕。
