第一個沒有獎勵的日子

The First Unrewarded Day

Qiao repaired a neighborhood lift with ordinary tools and no interface announcement, while the child pressed her mother's old cassette into a new archive sleeve. Something had changed, but not everybody noticed it in the same order. Lin Qiao listened to the account of the nearest worker, then to someone standing farther away. Their differences were not necessarily lies. Each person had been given a different view and a different reason to be afraid of admitting uncertainty. Across the workshop, ordinary tools made quiet sounds against metal. Lin Qiao preferred their honest limitations to an upgrade whose human cost could not be named. Ye Mu noticed the word bankrupt in a note that someone had added after the first inspection. A handwritten label read dew, but no one could say who had written it there.

林喬只用普通工具修好社區電梯，沒有任何系統宣告，孩子則把母親的錄音帶放進新的典藏套。某件事改變了，但人們發現它的順序不同。林喬先聽近處工作人員的描述，再聽遠處旁觀者說法。差異不一定是謊言：每個人看到的角度不同，承認不確定時所要承擔的風險也不同。工作間裡，普通工具敲打金屬發出輕響。林喬寧願接受工具誠實的限制，也不願領取說不出人命代價的升級。葉沐注意到初次檢查後有人在筆記裡加上「破產」一詞。手寫標籤寫著「露水」，卻沒有人知道是誰留下的。

The archive could preserve absence and responsibility but could not promise to bring every missing person back. Lin Qiao refused to pretend the previous decision had solved everything. The practical result was still there, useful but narrow. For each thing the team knew, there were two things it had not measured. Lin Qiao drew a line through the claims that depended on speculation and asked Ye Mu to keep the original record intact. The term disability appeared in the revised file; Lin Qiao checked its meaning against the original page. Ye Mu noticed the word empire in a note that someone had added after the first inspection. Ye Mu repeated the instruction to endanger, then asked whether anyone had the right to refuse.

檔案能留下缺席與責任，卻不能承諾讓每個失蹤者回來。林喬不願假裝前一次決定已解決所有問題。成果確實存在，卻有明確界限。每個已知事項旁都還有未測量的部分。林喬劃掉靠猜測成立的說法，請葉沐保留原始紀錄。修訂檔案出現「身心障礙」一詞，林喬拿原始頁面核對它的意思。葉沐注意到初次檢查後有人在筆記裡加上「帝國」一詞。葉沐重述「危及」的指示，再問當事人是否有權拒絕。

A small disagreement changed the schedule. Lin Qiao wanted to proceed before evidence disappeared; Ye Mu wanted another check because a wrong conclusion might hurt people who could not easily defend themselves. They did not settle the matter by choosing a leader. They wrote down what a short, reversible test could actually establish. An official memo described the plan as enormous, but Lin Qiao refused to treat an adjective as evidence. The term graph appeared in the revised file; Lin Qiao checked its meaning against the original page. Ye Mu noticed the word haste in a note that someone had added after the first inspection.

一場小爭執改變了時程。林喬想趁證據消失前行動；葉沐要求再核對一次，因為錯誤結論可能傷害難以自辯的人。他們沒有靠選領袖解決爭議，而是列出短暫、可逆測試真正能證明什麼。官方備忘錄形容計畫「龐大的」，林喬不願把形容詞當作證據。修訂檔案出現「圖表」一詞，林喬拿原始頁面核對它的意思。葉沐注意到初次檢查後有人在筆記裡加上「倉促」一詞。

Ye Mu recorded the city's first day without reward-induced disappearance; the damage was not undone, but the next choice finally belonged to its people. The plan had been amended three times, and each change left a trace. Lin Qiao valued those corrections more than a perfect-looking first draft. If the outcome proved disappointing, the group would still be able to identify which assumption needed repair. Ye Mu watched for the quiet participant who had not yet been invited to speak. Ye Mu noticed the word impression in a note that someone had added after the first inspection. A handwritten label read including, but no one could say who had written it there. The term instinct appeared in the revised file; Lin Qiao checked its meaning against the original page.

葉沐記下全市第一個沒有因獎勵而失蹤的日子；傷害尚未復原，但下一個選擇終於屬於人們。計畫改了三次，每次修改都留下痕跡。林喬認為這些修正比看似完美的初稿更有價值。就算結果不理想，團隊仍能知道該修正哪一項假設。葉沐也注意到那名一直沒有發言機會的人。葉沐注意到初次檢查後有人在筆記裡加上「印象」一詞。手寫標籤寫著「包括」，卻沒有人知道是誰留下的。修訂檔案出現「本能」一詞，林喬拿原始頁面核對它的意思。

An official version reached the room before the practical work was finished. Lin Qiao read it once, then compared it with what they had actually measured. The difference was not always malicious, but it could still hurt somebody. Ye Mu asked that the correction be written in language the affected people could understand. Across the workshop, ordinary tools made quiet sounds against metal. Lin Qiao preferred their honest limitations to an upgrade whose human cost could not be named. The report claimed insurance, but Lin Qiao wanted an example instead of a reassuring noun. Ye Mu wrote intention in the margin and asked how the team would recognize it in practice. Ye Mu repeated the instruction to lengthen, then asked whether anyone had the right to refuse.

實際工作還沒完成，官方版本就傳到現場。林喬讀了一次，再比對真正測得的結果。差異未必都是惡意，卻仍可能傷害人。葉沐要求把更正寫成受影響者看得懂的語言。工作間裡，普通工具敲打金屬發出輕響。林喬寧願接受工具誠實的限制，也不願領取說不出人命代價的升級。報告聲稱有「保險」，林喬要求具體例子，而不是安撫人的名詞。葉沐在頁邊寫下「用意」，追問團隊實際上如何辨認。葉沐重述「延長」的指示，再問當事人是否有權拒絕。

A witness asked whether the team was taking sides. Lin Qiao answered that refusing to invent a fact was not the same as refusing to care. What mattered was whether the procedure protected someone whose story was less convenient to tell. Ye Mu listened without interrupting and wrote down the question exactly as it had been asked. A handwritten label read liar, but no one could say who had written it there. The term paragraph appeared in the revised file; Lin Qiao checked its meaning against the original page. Ye Mu noticed the word prompt in a note that someone had added after the first inspection.

一名見證人問團隊是否偏袒。林喬回答，不捏造事實不代表冷漠。真正重要的是程序能否保護那個故事不方便被說出口的人。葉沐沒有打斷，逐字記下原問題。手寫標籤寫著「騙子」，卻沒有人知道是誰留下的。修訂檔案出現「段落」一詞，林喬拿原始頁面核對它的意思。葉沐注意到初次檢查後有人在筆記裡加上「迅速的」一詞。

Outside the neighborhood archive, people had queued since morning with letters, photographs, and names they did not want forgotten. Some came from rural villages where no system had bothered to count the losses at all. Every submission bore a signature, or the mark of a witness when a signature was impossible, and no one was asked to surrender a memory in exchange. Lin Qiao watched the vast wall of blank spaces slowly acquire dates and voices. The archive would never be a machine that returned the missing. Its promise was smaller, more difficult, and voluntary: to keep asking whose absence a bright reward had concealed. A child pressed a cassette into Ye Mu's hands. 'This is my mother's song,' she said. No silver light appeared. The only sound was the click of an ordinary recorder beginning to play.

社區典藏室外，人們從早上就帶著信件、照片，以及不願被忘記的名字排隊。有些人來自偏遠鄉村，那裡的損失甚至從未被系統計算。每份資料都有簽名；無法簽名時則由見證人做記號，沒有人需要拿記憶來交換。林喬看著廣大的空白牆面慢慢添上日期與聲音。這座典藏室永遠不會是把失蹤者送回來的機器，它的承諾更小、更難，而且完全自願：不斷追問耀眼獎勵曾掩蓋誰的缺席。一個女孩把錄音帶交給葉沐。「這是媽媽唱的歌。」沒有銀光出現，只有普通錄音機開始播放時的一聲輕響。
