Archived census forms revealed blank lines where entire households should have been, and handwritten marginal totals no longer matched the official sum. Something had changed, but not everybody noticed it in the same order. Lin Qiao listened to the account of the nearest worker, then to someone standing farther away. Their differences were not necessarily lies. Each person had been given a different view and a different reason to be afraid of admitting uncertainty. Across the workshop, ordinary tools made quiet sounds against metal. Lin Qiao preferred their honest limitations to an upgrade whose human cost could not be named. Ye Mu noticed the word appoint in a note that someone had added after the first inspection. A handwritten label read blossom, but no one could say who had written it there.

They learned that individual resistance merely moved the cost elsewhere, and began coordinating across districts. Lin Qiao refused to pretend the previous decision had solved everything. A small disagreement changed the schedule. Lin Qiao wanted to proceed before evidence disappeared; Ye Mu wanted another check because a wrong conclusion might hurt people who could not easily defend themselves. They did not settle the matter by choosing a leader. They wrote down what a short, reversible test could actually establish. Lin Qiao valued those corrections more than a perfect-looking first draft. If the outcome proved disappointing, the group would still be able to identify which assumption needed repair. An official version reached the room before the practical work was finished. Lin Qiao read it once, then compared it with what they had actually measured. The difference was not always malicious, but it could still hurt somebody. Ye Mu asked that the correction be written in language the affected people could understand. A witness asked whether the team was taking sides. Lin Qiao answered that refusing to invent a fact was not the same as refusing to care. What mattered was whether the procedure protected someone whose story was less convenient to tell. Ye Mu listened without interrupting and wrote down the question exactly as it had been asked. 一名見證人問團隊是否偏袒。林喬回答，不捏造事實不代表冷漠。真正重要的是程序能否保護那個故事不方便被說出口的人。葉沐沒有打斷，逐字記下原問題。手寫標籤寫著「提案」，卻沒有人知道是誰留下的。指示裡用了「幾乎不」這個動詞，卻沒說誰承擔風險；林喬圈出這項缺漏。葉沐注意到初次檢查後有人在筆記裡加上「草圖」一詞。

Even after the group agreed on the immediate step, another question remained. The answer would not arrive through a dramatic announcement. 即使大家同意眼前步驟，另一個問題仍在。答案不會靠戲劇性公告降臨；有人得返回現場、比對舊筆記，並接受新證據可能推翻漂亮理論。林喬準備做的正是這件事。系統帳冊的數字本來看似無害，直到林喬問那代表誰的位置。房間安靜下來，沒人願意用名字作答。葉沐注意到初次檢查後有人在筆記裡加上「條紋」一詞。葉沐重述「整理」的指示，再問當事人是否有權拒絕。修訂檔案出現「通用的」一詞，林喬拿原始頁面核對它的意思。
