Jin waited at the park for a meeting that Momo forgot after using the extra hour. In this part of town, the same clocks could tell different stories. A clock in a busy kitchen meant dinner would be late. A clock in a classroom meant the next lesson would begin. The strange clock in the Saturday shop seemed to ask a harder question: what was an hour really worth? Momo felt the pull of an easy answer, but Jin had taught her to read a sign all the way to the end. She studied the little marks and remembered a promise made outside the shop. Time was not only a number; it belonged to the people who had agreed to spend it. Although the group was tired, they finished the final check. Somebody had packed bean for a short break later. He felt hurt when she called the missed meeting a small problem. At first, the discovery seemed to point in only one direction. Then Jin asked a quiet question that made Momo look twice. A useful observation should not depend on everybody liking the same answer. Momo wrote down what was certain and left a blank space for what they did not know. It felt a little strange to leave a page unfinished. Still, that blank space was honest. It meant they could learn something new tomorrow without having to pretend yesterday's guess had always been right. A drawing of a frog hung on the classroom notice board. Momo remembered a drawing of height seen earlier that week. A notice near the entrance mentioned hen. Momo said the word least in a careful answer. The long line moved slowly, and nobody was pushed.

茉茉說這只是小問題，阿進聽了很受傷。 一開始，這項發現似乎只有一個解釋。接著阿進輕聲問了一句，讓茉茉重新思考。有用的觀察不需要所有人喜歡同一個答案。茉茉寫下已確定的事，也為未知留下空白。讓頁面沒寫滿有點奇怪，但這份空白很誠實。它表示明天可以學到新東西，不必假裝昨天的猜測永遠正確。 教室公告欄上掛著一張青蛙的圖畫。茉茉想起本週稍早看到的高度圖畫。入口附近的告示提到母雞。茉茉仔細回答時說出了「至少」。長長的隊伍緩慢前進，沒有人被推擠。

She told him the truth about the shop and apologized without asking him to forgive her at once. Some people wanted to act immediately. Momo understood their worry, yet the choice would affect more than one person. Jin helped turn scattered suggestions into a plan with a beginning and an end. They wrote what each person could do, and what no one should be pushed to do. That last part mattered. 'A fair answer cannot depend on someone being too afraid to refuse,' Momo said. When they finally moved, it was with a plan that could be checked instead of a guess that only sounded confident. The open book contained a short story about minute. Ms. Chen signed the note after checking the facts. Momo remembered a drawing of neck seen earlier that week. The class reading card used point in an example sentence. "Put the paper beside the notebook," Momo said.

她坦白時間商店的事並道歉，卻沒有要求對方立刻原諒。 有些人希望立刻行動。茉茉理解焦急，但這個選擇會影響不只一人。阿進把零散建議整理成有開頭和結尾的計畫。他們寫下每人能做什麼，也寫下哪些事不能強迫別人做。後面這一點尤其重要。「公平的答案不能靠別人不敢拒絕。」茉茉說。最後他們按可以檢查的計畫行動，而不是跟著聽起來很有信心的猜測。 翻開的書有一則談到分鐘的小故事。陳老師核對事實後簽了字。茉茉想起本週稍早看到的脖子圖畫。課堂閱讀卡用指向造了一個例句。「把紙放在筆記本旁邊。」茉茉說。

Jin agreed to listen but wanted their next meeting to use ordinary time. The ledger held names, dates, and lines so short they seemed harmless. Momo knew better now than to read them as empty marks. Every mark belonged to a day someone might have wanted to spend another way. Jin asked whether the choice could be explained without using the shop's special words. Momo tried, using plain language about today, next Saturday, and the people who would be waiting. Once spoken aloud, the cost became easier to understand. It did not make the shop evil or good. It made the choice something that required care. A notice near the entrance mentioned race. The safe path was marked clearly for every visitor. A notice near the entrance mentioned snow. "I am sorry about that mistake," Momo said. The open book contained a short story about win.

阿進願意聽她說，卻希望下次見面只用正常時間。 帳本上寫著姓名、日期和看似無害的短短幾筆。茉茉現在明白每個記號都不是空的，它們屬於某個人原本可能用來做別的事的一天。阿進問能不能不用商店的特殊說法來解釋選擇。茉茉試著以今天、下週六和等候的人來說明。代價一旦說出口就容易理解。這不代表商店是好是壞，只代表選擇必須慎重。 入口附近的告示提到賽跑。安全路線清楚標示給每位訪客。入口附近的告示提到雪。「那個錯誤真的很抱歉。」茉茉說。翻開的書有一則談到贏得的小故事。

In the afternoon light, the shop looked ordinary again. That surprised Momo a little. A room, a street, or a simple object could stay the same while the people who used it learned something new. Jin checked that nothing needed to be carried away without permission. Momo checked the time and remembered an ordinary promise waiting outside the day's work. They had made progress, but other parts of life still deserved care. Neither of them wanted to turn an important project into a reason to forget everyone else.

午後光線下，小店又顯得很平凡，這讓茉茉有些驚訝。房間、街道或物品可能沒有改變，使用它們的人卻已學到新事。阿進確認沒有物品被擅自帶走。茉茉看了時間，想起工作以外還有平常的約定。事情有了進展，生活其他部分仍值得照顧。他們都不想因為一項重要計畫，就忘記身邊其他人。

When Momo turned the last page of the notes, one line stood out: people deserve the chance to explain their own choices. It was easy to forget that in a busy day, especially when everyone wanted a quick ending. Jin had reminded Momo more than once to keep an open mind. The lesson was not that people should wait forever. It was that acting with care usually made the next action safer and fairer. Momo closed the book and made a quiet plan to remember that tomorrow.

茉茉翻到筆記最後一頁，看見一行話特別醒目：每個人都應有機會解釋自己的選擇。忙碌時很容易忘記，尤其人人都想快點結束。阿進不只一次提醒茉茉保持開放心態。這不是要大家永遠等待，而是說仔細行動通常會讓下一步更安全、更公平。茉茉合上本子，默默決定明天也要記住。
