三條規則

The Three Rules

星期六的時間商店 · 第 2 / 25 章 · 1000以下 · 英中對照 · 校訂中

Momo returned after missing a deadline for a school art card. In this part of town, the same clocks could tell different stories. A clock in a busy kitchen meant dinner would be late. A clock in a classroom meant the next lesson would begin. The strange clock in the Saturday shop seemed to ask a harder question: what was an hour really worth? Momo felt the pull of an easy answer, but Jin had taught her to read a sign all the way to the end. She studied the little marks and remembered a promise made outside the shop. Time was not only a number; it belonged to the people who had agreed to spend it. "We can add when the others are ready," Momo suggested. Jin also wanted to hear another point of view. "Please bring the notebook tomorrow," Jin said. The plan looked simple, but one part needed checking. Momo remembered a drawing of cousin seen earlier that week.

茉茉錯過學校美術卡片的期限，於是再度走進店裡。 小鎮的鐘雖然走著相同時間，卻說著不同的故事。廚房裡的鐘代表晚餐要遲了，教室裡的鐘表示下一堂課將開始。週六小店裡的怪鐘卻提出更難的問題：一小時真正值多少？茉茉很想要簡單答案，但阿進教過她要把告示從頭讀到尾。她仔細看那些小刻痕，想起店外做過的一個承諾。時間不只是數字，也屬於同意一起度過它的人。 「等其他人準備好，我們就能增加、加入。」茉茉提議。阿進也想聽聽其他人的看法。「明天請把筆記本帶來。」阿進說。計畫看來簡單，但有一部分需要查證。茉茉想起本週稍早看到的表親、表哥圖畫。

Mrs. Yu said the borrowed hour was taken from Momo's own next Saturday, not from someone else. For several seconds, nobody spoke. Momo was thinking about the new fact and the way it touched earlier choices. Jin drew a short line under the important part of the notes. They agreed that a record was useful only if it was clear enough to correct. If a number, name, or time was uncertain, they marked it as uncertain. That extra care took a little longer, but it protected people who were not in the room to explain themselves. At last, Momo could see a practical question worth asking next. A small poster on the wall included the word family. "What does famous mean in this note?" Momo asked. The open book contained a short story about fill. The class reading card used guy in an example sentence. A helpful classmate offered to carry the notes.

余太太解釋借來的一小時要從茉茉自己的下個星期六扣除，不會從別人身上拿。 好幾秒沒有人開口。茉茉思考新事實與先前選擇的關係。阿進在筆記重要處畫了短線。他們同意紀錄要清楚，才方便日後更正。數字、姓名或時間若不確定，就標明不確定。這樣雖然多花時間，卻能保護不在場、無法替自己說話的人。最後茉茉終於想到下一個值得問的實際問題。 牆上的小海報寫著家人這個字。「這張筆記裡的「有名的」是什麼意思？」茉茉問。翻開的書有一則談到裝滿、填入的小故事。課堂閱讀卡用男子造了一個例句。一位熱心同學主動幫忙搬筆記。

They read three rules: one hour at most, no changing another person's choice, and a written record of every exchange. Making a choice was harder than making a wish. Momo could imagine a quick solution, but a real plan needed to fit the time, the place, and the people taking part. Jin listened while Momo explained the steps aloud. They stopped at one unclear part, changed it, and started again. 'If somebody says no, we need another way,' Momo said. Jin agreed that helping was not the same as deciding for others. Together they wrote a short plan anyone involved could question, improve, or leave if it was not right for them. Momo remembered a drawing of lip seen earlier that week. The class reading card used power in an example sentence. They talked about the river near the entrance and how to keep it safe. A small poster on the wall included the word science. A drawing of a sheep hung on the classroom notice board.

兩人讀了三項規則：最多借一小時、不能改變別人的決定、每次交換都要記錄。 做選擇比許願難。茉茉可以想像快速解法，但真正的計畫必須配合時間、地點與參與者。阿進聽茉茉逐步說明，在不清楚的地方停下修改，再重新開始。「如果有人說不，我們要有其他辦法。」茉茉說。阿進同意，幫忙不等於替別人做決定。他們一起寫下簡明計畫，讓相關的人都能提問、改善或選擇退出。 茉茉想起本週稍早看到的嘴唇圖畫。課堂閱讀卡用權力造了一個例句。他們討論入口附近的河流，以及如何保持安全。牆上的小海報寫著科學這個字。教室公告欄上掛著一張綿羊的圖畫。

Momo left without buying and thought carefully about her week. The ledger held names, dates, and lines so short they seemed harmless. Momo knew better now than to read them as empty marks. Every mark belonged to a day someone might have wanted to spend another way. Jin asked whether the choice could be explained without using the shop's special words. Momo tried, using plain language about today, next Saturday, and the people who would be waiting. Once spoken aloud, the cost became easier to understand. It did not make the shop evil or good. It made the choice something that required care. Momo remembered a drawing of south seen earlier that week. "What does sunny mean in this note?" Momo asked. "They may need more time," Jin answered. Someone had asked Jin a question about tool. A notice near the entrance mentioned video.

茉茉沒有立刻購買，而是回家仔細想想這一週。 帳本上寫著姓名、日期和看似無害的短短幾筆。茉茉現在明白每個記號都不是空的，它們屬於某個人原本可能用來做別的事的一天。阿進問能不能不用商店的特殊說法來解釋選擇。茉茉試著以今天、下週六和等候的人來說明。代價一旦說出口就容易理解。這不代表商店是好是壞，只代表選擇必須慎重。 茉茉想起本週稍早看到的南方圖畫。「這張筆記裡的「晴朗的」是什麼意思？」茉茉問。「他們可能需要更多時間。」阿進回答。有人曾問阿進關於工具的問題。入口附近的告示提到影片。

The notebook was becoming thicker with small observations. Momo had once imagined that a good story needed one dramatic moment to make everything clear. Real days did not behave that way. One small choice led to another, and people sometimes changed their minds for good reasons. Jin helped put today's paper in the right place. They could return to it later and see exactly why they had acted as they did. That mattered more than making the day sound exciting to someone who had not been there.

筆記本裡的小觀察越來越厚。茉茉以前以為好故事需要一個戲劇性瞬間讓一切清楚，真實生活卻不是這樣。一個小選擇連著下一個選擇，人也可能因合理理由改變心意。阿進幫忙把今天的紙收在正確位置。日後再翻開，就能知道當初為什麼那樣行動。這比向沒到場的人說得刺激更重要。

The small objects from the day were back in their places. The important words were written down, and the people involved had heard the result. Momo could not know every part of the future, but there was comfort in knowing what to do first: ask, check, and listen. Jin said goodbye with a tired smile. Momo returned the smile. Somewhere beyond the familiar shop, another ordinary day was waiting. It would bring its own surprises, but they did not have to meet them without friends or without a plan.

今天的小物件都回到了原位。重要的話已寫好，相關的人也知道結果。茉茉不可能知道未來每件事，卻因知道第一步該怎麼做而安心：發問、查證、傾聽。阿進疲憊地笑著道別，茉茉也回以微笑。在熟悉的小店之外，又有平凡的一天等待著，會帶來新的驚喜，但他們不必獨自、毫無計畫地面對。
