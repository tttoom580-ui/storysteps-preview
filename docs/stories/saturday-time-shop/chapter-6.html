星期六的時間商店 · 第 6 / 25 章 · 1000以下 · 英中對照 · 校訂中

Momo and Jin draw a week chart with colored squares for school, family, and rest. The shop window reflected the street so clearly that the clock inside seemed to float between passing people. Momo could hear a distant bicycle bell and someone laughing near the bakery. Those sounds belonged to ordinary Saturday hours. Inside the shop, the promise of an extra hour was written as neatly as a price on a jar. Momo wondered how something so simple could carry a cost she did not yet understand. Jin asked what could happen if everyone wanted more time but no one wanted to give any back. The question stayed with her longer than the sound of the bell. "We can arrive when the others are ready," Momo suggested. Before leaving, Momo checked the open list again. Momo remembered a drawing of boat seen earlier that week. Someone had asked Jin a question about box. Somebody had packed coffee for a short break later.

茉茉與阿進畫出一張星期表，用不同顏色表示學校、家人和休息。 櫥窗把街景映得很清楚，店內的時鐘彷彿浮在來往的人影間。茉茉聽見遠處的腳踏車鈴聲，也聽見麵包店附近有人笑。那些都是平常的週六時間。小店裡，多出一小時的承諾卻寫得像罐子上的標價一樣整齊。茉茉想不通這麼簡單的東西怎會有自己還不理解的代價。阿進問，如果每個人都想要更多時間，卻沒人願意歸還，會怎樣呢？這個問題留在心裡，比鈴聲還久。 「等其他人準備好，我們就能抵達。」茉茉提議。離開之前，茉茉又核對公開清單。茉茉想起本週稍早看到的船圖畫。有人曾問阿進關於箱子的問題。有人為稍後的休息準備了咖啡。

They discover that much of Momo's hurry came from saying yes to every request. The information was small enough to overlook. Momo nearly moved on, but Jin wanted to know what it meant for the people involved. They talked through two possibilities, then checked which parts could actually be supported. In the shop, other things were happening at the same time; it would have been easy to mix them together. 'Let's keep these separate,' Momo said. One careful question led to another, and the situation became clearer without anyone needing to raise a voice or make a dramatic accusation. The open book contained a short story about drive. Everyone agreed except one friend who needed more time. Finally, the last note was put into its proper place. A free chair was left for the next person. They talked about the house near the entrance and how to keep it safe.

他們發現茉茉一直趕時間，是因為對所有請求都說好。 這項資訊很小，幾乎會被忽略。茉茉正想往下走，阿進卻想知道它對相關的人代表什麼。他們討論兩種可能，再確認哪些有證據支持。小店同時還發生其他事，很容易混淆。「先把它們分開。」茉茉說。仔細的一問接著一問，不必大聲說話或戲劇性指控，情況也漸漸清楚。 翻開的書有一則談到開車的小故事。除了還需要時間的一位朋友，大家都同意。最後，最後一張紙也放回正確位置。留下一張空椅子給下一個人。他們討論入口附近的房屋，以及如何保持安全。

Momo practiced saying no to one extra job at the art club. The next step sounded small when Momo said it aloud. That was a good sign. A clear action was easier to try than a large promise that no one understood. Jin repeated the plan to make sure both had heard it in the same way. When one part sounded unfair, they changed the wording. The plan left space for a person to ask a question or take a break. Momo noticed a quiet sense of relief. The problem remained real, but it no longer needed to be faced through shouting, rushing, or guessing about another person's feelings. A drawing of a lion hung on the classroom notice board. "Can you see the small mark on this page?" Momo asked. A small poster on the wall included the word shape. The open book contained a short story about sign. "Calling someone stupid will not solve our problem," Momo said.

茉茉練習婉拒美術社的一份額外工作。 茉茉把下一步說出口時，聽起來只是一件小事。這反而是好現象。明確行動比大家聽不懂的大承諾更容易嘗試。阿進重複計畫，確認兩人理解相同。有一部分聽來不公平，他們便改了說法。計畫也讓人能提問和休息。茉茉感到些許輕鬆。問題依然存在，卻不必靠吵鬧、急躁或猜測別人的心思來面對。 教室公告欄上掛著一張獅子的圖畫。「你看得見這頁的小記號嗎？」茉茉問。牆上的小海報寫著形狀這個字。翻開的書有一則談到標誌的小故事。「說別人笨也不會解決問題。」茉茉說。

For the first time in days, she finished dinner without watching the clock. The shop's clock continued to tick in its unusual way, but no sound from it could decide what Momo valued most. Jin stood beside the door and waited instead of pulling her toward the street. That patience felt like a gift nobody had to repay. Momo checked the note in her pocket and noticed which promises had been made freely and which had come from fear of letting people down. They were not the same. She put the note away carefully, knowing that the next Saturday would arrive whether she borrowed from it or not. "That would be a terrible way to treat a friend," Jin said. The open book contained a short story about top. Momo remembered a drawing of uncle seen earlier that week. The class reading card used wave in an example sentence. A small poster on the wall included the word weekend.

好幾天以來，她第一次吃晚餐不必一直看時鐘。 商店的怪鐘依然滴答作響，但它的聲音不能替茉茉決定什麼最重要。阿進站在門邊等她，沒有急著把她拉回街上。這份耐心像一件不用歸還的禮物。茉茉查看口袋裡的紙條，想起哪些承諾出於真心，哪些只是怕讓別人失望而答應。兩者並不相同。她小心收起紙條，明白無論借不借時間，下個星期六都會到來。 「那樣對待朋友太糟糕了。」阿進說。翻開的書有一則談到頂端的小故事。茉茉想起本週稍早看到的舅舅圖畫。課堂閱讀卡用揮手造了一個例句。牆上的小海報寫著週末這個字。

Later, Momo looked back at the four parts of the day: what happened, what they learned, what they decided, and what followed. The list was simple enough to fit on a little page, but it held much more than a score or a rumor. Jin read the page and pointed to a place where the order could be clearer. Together they fixed it. They also left a line for the next person to write a question. That made the record useful even when the people who started it were not there.

稍後，茉茉回想今天的四部分：發生了什麼、學到了什麼、做了什麼決定，以及結果如何。清單雖然只有小小一頁，卻比一個分數或流言包含更多內容。阿進讀完後指出一處順序不清楚，兩人一起修改。他們還留下一行，讓之後接手的人能寫下問題。這樣，即使最初參與的人不在場，紀錄也依然有用。

That evening, Momo tried to describe the day in just two sentences. The first sentence was about the problem. The second was about what people did together. Both mattered, and neither would have made sense alone. Momo left space beneath them for another note tomorrow. Jin sent a short reminder to rest. Momo smiled at it. A person could care deeply about a goal and still eat dinner, talk with family, and sleep. Whatever happened next, those ordinary things were part of the answer too.

那天晚上，茉茉試著用兩句話描述今天。第一句是問題，第二句是大家一起做的事。兩句都重要，單獨看卻不完整。茉茉在下面留空，準備明天再記。阿進傳來簡短提醒，要記得休息。茉茉笑了。人可以非常在意目標，同時仍要吃晚餐、陪家人說話、好好睡覺。不論接下來如何，這些平常的事也是答案的一部分。
