Inside, an hour could be written on a receipt. Momo ran a finger just above the paper without touching anything that was not hers. The shop did not make the world quiet. Mrs. Yu repeated that time belonged to the person who chose to spend it. Some people wanted to act immediately. Momo understood their worry, yet the choice would affect more than one person. Jin helped turn scattered suggestions into a plan with a beginning and an end. They wrote what each person could do, and what no one should be pushed to do. That last part mattered. 'A fair answer cannot depend on someone being too afraid to refuse,' Momo said. When they finally moved, it was with a plan that could be checked instead of a guess that only sounded confident. In the afternoon light, the shop looked ordinary again. That surprised Momo a little. A room, a street, or a simple object could stay the same while the people who used it learned something new. Jin checked that nothing needed to be carried away without permission. Momo checked the time and remembered an ordinary promise waiting outside the day's work. They had made progress, but other parts of life still deserved care. Neither of them wanted to turn an important project into a reason to forget everyone else.

午後光線下，小店又顯得很平凡，這讓茉茉有些驚訝。房間、街道或物品可能沒有改變，使用它們的人卻已學到新事。阿進確認沒有物品被擅自帶走。茉茉看了時間，想起工作以外還有平常的約定。事情有了進展，生活其他部分仍值得照顧。他們都不想因為一項重要計畫，就忘記身邊其他人。

When Momo turned the last page of the notes, one line stood out: people deserve the chance to explain their own choices. It was easy to forget that in a busy day, especially when everyone wanted a quick ending. Jin had reminded Momo more than once to keep an open mind. The lesson was not that people should wait forever. It was that acting with care usually made the next action safer and fairer. Momo closed the book and made a quiet plan to remember that tomorrow.

茉茉翻到筆記最後一頁，看見一行話特別醒目：每個人都應有機會解釋自己的選擇。忙碌時很容易忘記，尤其人人都想快點結束。阿進不只一次提醒茉茉保持開放心態。這不是要大家永遠等待，而是說仔細行動通常會讓下一步更安全、更公平。茉茉合上本子，默默決定明天也要記住。
