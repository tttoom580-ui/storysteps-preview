Inside the shop, the promise of an extra hour was written as neatly as a price on a jar. Momo wondered how something so simple could carry a cost she did not yet understand. After the busy part ended, Momo and Jin found a quiet minute. There were still things they wanted to say, yet neither felt a need to win the conversation. Momo asked what had been most difficult. 忙碌告一段落後，茉茉和阿進有了片刻安靜。他們還有話想說，卻不必在對話中爭贏。茉茉問最困難的是哪部分。阿進誠實回答，也反問同一個問題。兩人的答案竟然不同，卻不代表誰錯了。這場談話讓他們理解，合作不必想法完全一致，而是認真看待差異，做出雙方都能理解的計畫。

On the way home, Momo kept thinking about one small moment from the day. It was not necessarily the loudest moment. Sometimes the most important change happened when somebody listened, stopped, or asked one more question. Jin waved goodbye at the corner. Momo waved back and felt ready to begin again when the next problem came. The story had moved forward, not because every difficulty was gone, but because they now knew something they had not known in the morning.
