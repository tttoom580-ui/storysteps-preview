街道之間的小店

A Shop Between Streets

星期六的時間商店 · 第 1 / 25 章 · 1000以下 · 英中對照 · 校訂中

Momo noticed a narrow shop that appeared only between the bakery and library on Saturday. Outside the shop, people planned their days with ordinary words: breakfast, work, a visit, and rest. Inside, an hour could be written on a receipt. Momo ran a finger just above the paper without touching anything that was not hers. The shop did not make the world quiet. It only made the value of small choices harder to ignore. Jin remembered a meeting they had promised to attend together. Momo looked at the time and wondered whether a promise could be protected simply by buying a new minute. The answer was not written on the price card. The small clock read 9 a.m. when Momo checked it. A bell rang once in the hall, marking the next part of the day. Momo remembered the breakfast that had helped the morning begin. A friend promised to bring a small cake for the break. Momo thought of the soft couch at home and wished for a rest.

茉茉發現星期六時，麵包店和圖書館中間會出現一間狹長小店。 小店外的人用平常字眼安排一天：早餐、工作、探訪和休息。店內，一小時卻能寫在收據上。茉茉的手指在紙上方輕輕移動，沒有碰不屬於自己的東西。小店沒有讓世界安靜，只是讓小選擇的價值更難忽視。阿進想起兩人約好一起參加的活動。茉茉看著時間，思考是否只要買到新的一分鐘就能守住承諾。價目卡上沒有答案。 茉茉看鐘時，時刻是上午九點。大廳響起一聲鈴，提醒大家進入下一個時段。茉茉想起幫自己開始一天的早餐。一位朋友答應休息時帶來小蛋糕。茉茉想起家裡柔軟的沙發，真想休息一下。

Its window offered one extra hour in neat blue writing, but no price was shown. The information was small enough to overlook. Momo nearly moved on, but Jin wanted to know what it meant for the people involved. They talked through two possibilities, then checked which parts could actually be supported. In the shop, other things were happening at the same time; it would have been easy to mix them together. 'Let's keep these separate,' Momo said. One careful question led to another, and the situation became clearer without anyone needing to raise a voice or make a dramatic accusation. A clean cover lay folded beside the shared equipment. Momo arrived early and waited beside the entrance. A short exercise helped everyone warm up and focus. A foreign visitor asked for a simple map. Fresh fruit waited in a bag for the afternoon break.

櫥窗以藍字寫著可買「多出的一小時」，卻沒標價。 這項資訊很小，幾乎會被忽略。茉茉正想往下走，阿進卻想知道它對相關的人代表什麼。他們討論兩種可能，再確認哪些有證據支持。小店同時還發生其他事，很容易混淆。「先把它們分開。」茉茉說。仔細的一問接著一問，不必大聲說話或戲劇性指控，情況也漸漸清楚。 共用器材旁整齊摺著一塊乾淨的罩布。茉茉提早抵達，在入口旁等候。短短的運動讓大家暖身，也比較能集中精神。一位外國訪客詢問簡單地圖。袋子裡放著下午休息時要吃的新鮮水果。

Momo asked the silver-haired owner Mrs. Yu whether the sign was a joke. The next step sounded small when Momo said it aloud. That was a good sign. A clear action was easier to try than a large promise that no one understood. Jin repeated the plan to make sure both had heard it in the same way. When one part sounded unfair, they changed the wording. The plan left space for a person to ask a question or take a break. Momo noticed a quiet sense of relief. The problem remained real, but it no longer needed to be faced through shouting, rushing, or guessing about another person's feelings. "He has a part in this too," Momo told Jin. The heavy bag was moved slowly to avoid an accident. Momo thought about a hobby that had taught patience. A cartoon monkey smiled from a child’s paper sticker. A teammate had packed salad in a lunch box.

茉茉詢問銀髮老闆余太太，這招牌是不是玩笑。 茉茉把下一步說出口時，聽起來只是一件小事。這反而是好現象。明確行動比大家聽不懂的大承諾更容易嘗試。阿進重複計畫，確認兩人理解相同。有一部分聽來不公平，他們便改了說法。計畫也讓人能提問和休息。茉茉感到些許輕鬆。問題依然存在，卻不必靠吵鬧、急躁或猜測別人的心思來面對。 「他也參與了這件事。」茉茉告訴阿進。沉重的袋子被緩緩搬開，避免意外。茉茉想起一項曾教會自己耐心的興趣。孩子的紙貼紙上有一隻微笑的卡通猴子。一名隊友把沙拉裝進午餐盒。

Mrs. Yu showed a clock with an empty space after twelve and asked Momo to return next Saturday. At closing time, the space between the bakery and library looked almost ordinary. Momo watched the shopkeeper place one more receipt in the book, then set the book down. No hidden clock could make a broken promise disappear. Jin waved from the street, ready to walk home at a normal pace. Momo joined him. For the first time that day, she did not count the minutes between one corner and the next. She noticed the shops, the people, and the part of Saturday that was still hers to enjoy. "Shall we ask before we decide?" Momo suggested. The sun came through the high window and warmed the floor. "Could you teach me that step?" Momo asked Jin. A child’s drawing showed a white tooth and a smiling face. Momo used a pen to write the result in the shared notebook.

余太太指著十二點後多出一格的鐘面，邀她下週六再來。 打烊時，麵包店和圖書館中間看起來幾乎很平常。茉茉看店主把最後一張收據放進帳本，再合上書。沒有任何祕密時鐘能讓失約消失。阿進在街上揮手，準備用正常步調回家。茉茉跟上他。這一天她第一次不計算兩個轉角之間的分鐘，反而看見街上的店、人，以及仍屬於自己的週六時光。 「我們先問清楚再決定，好嗎？」茉茉提議。陽光穿過高窗，溫暖了地板。「你能教我那個步驟嗎？」茉茉問阿進。孩子的畫上有一顆白牙和一張笑臉。茉茉用筆把結果寫進共用筆記本。

Later, Momo looked back at the four parts of the day: what happened, what they learned, what they decided, and what followed. The list was simple enough to fit on a little page, but it held much more than a score or a rumor. Jin read the page and pointed to a place where the order could be clearer. Together they fixed it. They also left a line for the next person to write a question. That made the record useful even when the people who started it were not there.

稍後，茉茉回想今天的四部分：發生了什麼、學到了什麼、做了什麼決定，以及結果如何。清單雖然只有小小一頁，卻比一個分數或流言包含更多內容。阿進讀完後指出一處順序不清楚，兩人一起修改。他們還留下一行，讓之後接手的人能寫下問題。這樣，即使最初參與的人不在場，紀錄也依然有用。

That evening, Momo tried to describe the day in just two sentences. The first sentence was about the problem. The second was about what people did together. Both mattered, and neither would have made sense alone. Momo left space beneath them for another note tomorrow. Jin sent a short reminder to rest. Momo smiled at it. A person could care deeply about a goal and still eat dinner, talk with family, and sleep. Whatever happened next, those ordinary things were part of the answer too.

那天晚上，茉茉試著用兩句話描述今天。第一句是問題，第二句是大家一起做的事。兩句都重要，單獨看卻不完整。茉茉在下面留空，準備明天再記。阿進傳來簡短提醒，要記得休息。茉茉笑了。人可以非常在意目標，同時仍要吃晚餐、陪家人說話、好好睡覺。不論接下來如何，這些平常的事也是答案的一部分。
