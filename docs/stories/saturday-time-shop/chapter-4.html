On the following Saturday, Momo woke to find breakfast was already over. Outside the shop, people planned their days with ordinary words: breakfast, work, a visit, and rest. Inside, an hour could be written on a receipt. Momo ran a finger just above the paper without touching anything that was not hers. The shop did not make the world quiet. It only made the value of small choices harder to ignore. Jin remembered a meeting they had promised to attend together. Momo looked at the time and wondered whether a promise could be protected simply by buying a new minute. The answer was not written on the price card. "We can blow when the others are ready," Momo suggested. A small poster on the wall included the word boy. A drawing of a butterfly hung on the classroom notice board. "A cheap answer can cost us trust," Momo said. A small poster on the wall included the word cloud.

Her family said the clock skipped from nine to ten although she felt no change. This new detail changed the way Momo saw the problem. It was a way of keeping an interesting story from becoming an unfair one. A small pause for checking seemed better than a large mistake. Someone had asked Jin a question about drop. 家人說時鐘從九點直接跳到十點，茉茉自己卻毫無感覺。 這個新細節改變了茉茉對問題的看法。把它接上最初的想法、宣布答案似乎很容易，但阿進提醒線索也可能有其他解釋。他們重新檢視資訊從哪裡來，別人能否核對。「如果我們錯了，就應該說出來。」茉茉說。這不是軟弱，而是避免有趣故事變成不公平的說法。花一點時間查證，總好過犯下大錯。 有人曾問阿進關於掉落的問題。他們討論入口附近的工廠，以及如何保持安全。健康點心讓疲累的大家恢復精神。茉茉解釋一個簡單想法時，用到了「關掉」這個詞。牆上的小海報寫著服務這個字。

Momo wrote down what she gained and what she lost instead of denying the cost. Before acting, Momo asked one more question: could the same goal be reached without making somebody else carry the cost? Jin thought for a while and suggested a smaller, clearer first step. It was less exciting than the plan in their heads, but it could be explained to anyone watching. Momo accepted the change. They spoke to the people responsible, checked the rule that applied, and agreed to return if they needed more help. Good intentions mattered, yet they were not a reason to skip a careful decision. The class reading card used shake in an example sentence. "She should have a choice," Momo said quietly. The open book contained a short story about sir. 茉茉把得到與失去的時間寫清楚，不再否認代價。 動手前，茉茉多問一句：能不能不讓別人承擔代價，也達到同樣目的？阿進想了一會，提出更小、更清楚的第一步。它沒有想像中刺激，卻能向旁人說清楚。茉茉接受修改。他們和負責的人談過、確認規則，也約好需要時再求助。好意很重要，卻不是跳過仔細決策的理由。 課堂閱讀卡用搖晃造了一個例句。「她也應該有選擇。」茉茉輕聲說。翻開的書有一則談到先生的小故事。牆上的小海報寫著方形的這個字。有人曾問阿進關於故事的問題。

She understood the first rule was true, and the borrowed hour was not free. At closing time, the space between the bakery and library looked almost ordinary. Momo watched the shopkeeper place one more receipt in the book, then set the book down. No hidden clock could make a broken promise disappear. Jin waved from the street, ready to walk home at a normal pace. Momo joined him. For the first time that day, she did not count the minutes between one corner and the next. She noticed the shops, the people, and the part of Saturday that was still hers to enjoy. The day felt tall to Momo, so Jin asked what was wrong. 她終於明白規則是真的，借來的時間並非免費。 打烊時，麵包店和圖書館中間看起來幾乎很平常。茉茉看店主把最後一張收據放進帳本，再合上書。沒有任何祕密時鐘能讓失約消失。阿進在街上揮手，準備用正常步調回家。茉茉跟上他。這一天她第一次不計算兩個轉角之間的分鐘，反而看見街上的店、人，以及仍屬於自己的週六時光。 茉茉覺得今天有些高的，阿進便問怎麼了。茉茉想起本週稍早看到的番茄圖畫。茉茉想起本週稍早看到的小鎮圖畫。茉茉提問時沒有責怪任何人。翻開的書有一則談到工人的小故事。

After the busy part ended, Momo and Jin found a quiet minute. There were still things they wanted to say, yet neither felt a need to win the conversation. Momo asked what had been most difficult. Jin answered honestly and then asked the same question back. They were surprised to hear two different answers. That did not make either one wrong. The talk helped them see that working together did not mean thinking exactly alike. It meant taking those differences seriously enough to make a plan both could understand.

忙碌告一段落後，茉茉和阿進有了片刻安靜。他們還有話想說，卻不必在對話中爭贏。茉茉問最困難的是哪部分。阿進誠實回答，也反問同一個問題。兩人的答案竟然不同，卻不代表誰錯了。這場談話讓他們理解，合作不必想法完全一致，而是認真看待差異，做出雙方都能理解的計畫。

On the way home, Momo kept thinking about one small moment from the day. It was not necessarily the loudest moment. Sometimes the most important change happened when somebody listened, stopped, or asked one more question. Jin waved goodbye at the corner. Momo waved back and felt ready to begin again when the next problem came. The story had moved forward, not because every difficulty was gone, but because they now knew something they had not known in the morning.

回家路上，茉茉一直想著今天的一個小片刻。那不一定是最熱鬧的一刻。有時真正重要的改變，就發生在有人願意聽、停下來，或多問一句時。阿進在轉角揮手再見，茉茉也揮手回應，覺得下次遇到問題時還能重新開始。故事之所以前進，不是因為困難全消失了，而是因為他們比早上多懂了一件事。
