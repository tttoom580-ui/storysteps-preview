A woman brought a receipt saying she borrowed an hour that she did not remember using. The shop window reflected the street so clearly that the clock inside seemed to float between passing people. Momo could hear a distant bicycle bell and someone laughing near the bakery. Those sounds belonged to ordinary Saturday hours. Inside the shop, the promise of an extra hour was written as neatly as a price on a jar. Momo wondered how something so simple could carry a cost she did not yet understand. Jin asked what could happen if everyone wanted more time but no one wanted to give any back. The question stayed with her longer than the sound of the bell. A small poster on the wall included the word answer. Momo remembered a drawing of bicycle seen earlier that week. The class reading card used body in an example sentence. Momo remembered a drawing of bow seen earlier that week. Someone had asked Jin a question about jeans.

一名女子拿著收據，說自己不記得借過上面記載的一小時。 櫥窗把街景映得很清楚，店內的時鐘彷彿浮在來往的人影間。茉茉聽見遠處的腳踏車鈴聲，也聽見麵包店附近有人笑。那些都是平常的週六時間。小店裡，多出一小時的承諾卻寫得像罐子上的標價一樣整齊。茉茉想不通這麼簡單的東西怎會有自己還不理解的代價。阿進問，如果每個人都想要更多時間，卻沒人願意歸還，會怎樣呢？這個問題留在心裡，比鈴聲還久。 牆上的小海報寫著答案這個字。茉茉想起本週稍早看到的自行車圖畫。課堂閱讀卡用身體造了一個例句。茉茉想起本週稍早看到的鞠躬圖畫。有人曾問阿進關於牛仔褲的問題。

The shop book showed her signature, but the date had been copied incorrectly. For several seconds, nobody spoke. Momo was thinking about the new fact and the way it touched earlier choices. Jin drew a short line under the important part of the notes. They agreed that a record was useful only if it was clear enough to correct. If a number, name, or time was uncertain, they marked it as uncertain. That extra care took a little longer, but it protected people who were not in the room to explain themselves. At last, Momo could see a practical question worth asking next. A notice near the entrance mentioned language. The open book contained a short story about mine. A piece of paper lay beside the book. Momo said the word own in a careful answer. Momo remembered a drawing of pick seen earlier that week.

商店紀錄有她的簽名，但日期抄錯了。 好幾秒沒有人開口。茉茉思考新事實與先前選擇的關係。阿進在筆記重要處畫了短線。他們同意紀錄要清楚，才方便日後更正。數字、姓名或時間若不確定，就標明不確定。這樣雖然多花時間，卻能保護不在場、無法替自己說話的人。最後茉茉終於想到下一個值得問的實際問題。 入口附近的告示提到語言。翻開的書有一則談到我的的小故事。書旁放著一張紙。茉茉仔細回答時說出了「自己的」。茉茉想起本週稍早看到的挑選圖畫。

Momo helped compare the two records without claiming the shop was always right. Making a choice was harder than making a wish. Momo could imagine a quick solution, but a real plan needed to fit the time, the place, and the people taking part. Jin listened while Momo explained the steps aloud. They stopped at one unclear part, changed it, and started again. 'If somebody says no, we need another way,' Momo said. Jin agreed that helping was not the same as deciding for others. Together they wrote a short plan anyone involved could question, improve, or leave if it was not right for them. Momo remembered a drawing of planet seen earlier that week. Somebody had packed salt for a short break later. "What would you like to say?" Jin asked. A small poster on the wall included the word second. Momo felt unsure, so Jin suggested a small test.

茉茉協助比對資料，沒有認定商店永遠不會出錯。 做選擇比許願難。茉茉可以想像快速解法，但真正的計畫必須配合時間、地點與參與者。阿進聽茉茉逐步說明，在不清楚的地方停下修改，再重新開始。「如果有人說不，我們要有其他辦法。」茉茉說。阿進同意，幫忙不等於替別人做決定。他們一起寫下簡明計畫，讓相關的人都能提問、改善或選擇退出。 茉茉想起本週稍早看到的星球圖畫。有人為稍後的休息準備了鹽。「你想說什麼呢？」阿進問。牆上的小海報寫著第二個這個字。茉茉不太確定，所以阿進提議小規模測試。

Mrs. Yu corrected the date openly and thanks the woman for checking. The shop's clock continued to tick in its unusual way, but no sound from it could decide what Momo valued most. Jin stood beside the door and waited instead of pulling her toward the street. That patience felt like a gift nobody had to repay. Momo checked the note in her pocket and noticed which promises had been made freely and which had come from fear of letting people down. They were not the same. She put the note away carefully, knowing that the next Saturday would arrive whether she borrowed from it or not. Momo said the word some in a careful answer. The next meeting would begin soon, so they checked the time. The open book contained a short story about spring. A small poster on the wall included the word wind. The class reading card used wish in an example sentence.

余太太公開更正日期，謝謝對方願意核對。 商店的怪鐘依然滴答作響，但它的聲音不能替茉茉決定什麼最重要。阿進站在門邊等她，沒有急著把她拉回街上。這份耐心像一件不用歸還的禮物。茉茉查看口袋裡的紙條，想起哪些承諾出於真心，哪些只是怕讓別人失望而答應。兩者並不相同。她小心收起紙條，明白無論借不借時間，下個星期六都會到來。 茉茉仔細回答時說出了「一些」。下一場會議很快要開始，他們先看時間。翻開的書有一則談到春天的小故事。牆上的小海報寫著風這個字。課堂閱讀卡用希望造了一個例句。

The notebook was becoming thicker with small observations. Momo had once imagined that a good story needed one dramatic moment to make everything clear. Real days did not behave that way. One small choice led to another, and people sometimes changed their minds for good reasons. Jin helped put today's paper in the right place. They could return to it later and see exactly why they had acted as they did. That mattered more than making the day sound exciting to someone who had not been there.

筆記本裡的小觀察越來越厚。茉茉以前以為好故事需要一個戲劇性瞬間讓一切清楚，真實生活卻不是這樣。一個小選擇連著下一個選擇，人也可能因合理理由改變心意。阿進幫忙把今天的紙收在正確位置。日後再翻開，就能知道當初為什麼那樣行動。這比向沒到場的人說得刺激更重要。

The small objects from the day were back in their places. The important words were written down, and the people involved had heard the result. Momo could not know every part of the future, but there was comfort in knowing what to do first: ask, check, and listen. Jin said goodbye with a tired smile. Momo returned the smile. Somewhere beyond the familiar shop, another ordinary day was waiting. It would bring its own surprises, but they did not have to meet them without friends or without a plan.

今天的小物件都回到了原位。重要的話已寫好，相關的人也知道結果。茉茉不可能知道未來每件事，卻因知道第一步該怎麼做而安心：發問、查證、傾聽。阿進疲憊地笑著道別，茉茉也回以微笑。在熟悉的小店之外，又有平凡的一天等待著，會帶來新的驚喜，但他們不必獨自、毫無計畫地面對。
