The river bridge had frozen halfway open, leaving thirty passengers on a tram over the black water. The private safety spell could not be restarted without a new license. Nera filed an emergency application from the riverbank while Ivo climbed the maintenance stairs with two volunteers. He did not touch the magical braces. Instead, they attached ordinary ropes to the tram's safety frame and moved the passengers one at a time. An attorney for Cinder House arrived before the final rescue and demanded that their equipment be confiscated. Nera displayed the court receipt: the judge had granted a temporary order protecting rescue work, not restoring the spell. A little girl stepped onto solid ground and asked whether the bridge would ever open again. Nera answered, "Not until we find out who is allowed to close it." Behind the attorney, a company engineer silently drew a strange seal on the fogged window.
