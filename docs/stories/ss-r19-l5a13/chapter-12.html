The forest communities had continued using the oldest water and healing spells for generations. Their elder, Fen, showed Nera the teaching stones where children practiced symbols that had no owner. A company surveyor claimed the stones were illegal copies. Fen asked him for the date of the first sale. He could not answer. Nera did not use the villagers merely as symbols in her case. She asked whether they wanted their names included in a public petition. Some agreed; others chose sealed testimony because they feared losing access to the market road. The village offered to teach emergency rune reading under its own rules. In return, Nera promised to include its representatives in any settlement. At the border, Ivo discovered a gate that could be disconnected safely from one irrigation line.
