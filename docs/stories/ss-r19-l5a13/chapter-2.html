Nera arrived to find the headmistress arguing with a silver-haired agent, while Ivo was connecting a bicycle generator to the kitchen's old exhaust fan. Without heating charms, the children could still have soup, but only if someone kept pedaling. Nera asked the agent whether the contract protected basic education. He pointed to an amendment nobody at the school had signed. A journalist named Sel joined the argument and photographed both versions. "Don't write that magic is dead," Ivo told her, handing a boy a bowl. "Write that someone is charging us for permission to use it."
