Rain began falling over the hillside settlements, but the expensive weather shield above the rich quarter turned every cloud eastward. Mud crept toward houses built on steep ground. The company called this a lawful purchase of clear skies. Nera called it a foreseeable danger and brought the rainfall maps to the council. Ivo and the hillside residents installed channels made of reclaimed roof metal. Their work saved three blocks, but a workshop collapsed, taking tools they could not replace. An activist demanded that Nera destroy the private shield immediately. She would not order a sabotage that might flood another neighborhood. Instead, she asked engineers to show where the redirected water went, then organized a public evacuation. That night a family invited Ivo to share their dry floor. He accepted, knowing that tomorrow they would need something stronger than temporary channels.
