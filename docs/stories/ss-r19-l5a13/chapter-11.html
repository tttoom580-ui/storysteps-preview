A linguist named Sora examined the rune code in an empty classroom. She placed two nearly identical words on the wall. In one, a single line had changed the phrase "maintained by the public" into "activated by permission." The underlying energy did not vanish when a license expired. A reading gate simply refused to recognize the older command. Ivo ran a test on an unplugged model so nobody would be harmed. The old rune still carried light into the lamp until the private gate interrupted it. "We thought we had to buy power," he said. "They are selling access to a switch." Nera asked Sora to document every step and invite the company's specialists to observe. The results would matter only if a neutral examiner could repeat them. She left with three sealed samples and a new risk: the private gates might be installed in every hospital wall.

語言學者索拉在空教室檢查符文程式。她把兩個幾乎相同的字掛上牆，其中一筆把「由公眾維護」改成了「經許可啟動」。許可到期時，能量並未消失；只是辨識閘門拒絕接受舊指令。伊沃先在離線模型上測試，避免傷及他人。舊符文仍能讓燈發亮，直到私人閘門介入。「我們以為得買電，」他說，「他們其實在賣開關。」奈拉要求索拉記錄全部步驟，並邀請公司技師見證。結果必須能由中立人士重現，才有證明力。她帶走三份封存樣本，也明白新風險：這種閘門可能裝在每一面醫院牆裡。
