試辦期的最後一夜

The Last Night of the Trial

紅磚街的星星課 · 第 24 / 25 章 · 1000以下 · 英中對照

The morning was rainy, and Nia thought the final outdoor visit might be lost. But the weather report showed a chance of clear sky after dinner. She called the center officer on her cellphone and asked whether the grass field had passed its safety check. He said the hole had been blocked, the path light fixed, and the work recorded. Nia asked for a copy of the check before inviting families. She also told everyone that the visit would end early if the ground became wet again. The date was the last Saturday of the six-week trial. She wanted to finish the class well, not rush to show a wonderful sight at any cost.

早上還在下雨，妮雅以為最後一次戶外參觀可能取消。不過天氣預報說晚餐後有機會放晴。她用手機打給中心安全管理員，問草地是否通過檢查。對方說洞口已隔離、步道燈修好，工程也有紀錄。妮雅要求在邀請家庭前拿到檢查資料，也告訴每個人，如果地面再次潮濕，活動會提早結束。這一天是六週試辦期的最後一個星期六。她希望好好完成課程，不願為了看美景而冒險。

At four, the clouds began to move away. Finn and his mother came to help carry paper cards across the center hall. Bo brought the class number board and checked that every item was on the list. He found one missing pencil, then saw it under a table in the kitchen. "It's not an important item," he said, "but a list helps us know what we have." Nia thanked him. A young kid from the neighborhood asked whether there would be a game. Nia said there would be a short matching activity, but this was not a race with winners. The main event was simply looking at the sky together.

四點時，雲開始散開。芬恩和媽媽來幫忙，把紙星卡搬過中心走廊。波帶來班上的數字板，依清單核對每件物品。他發現少了一枝鉛筆，最後在廚房桌下找到。「它不是什麼重要物品，」他說，「但清單能讓我們知道手上有什麼。」妮雅謝謝他。附近一個小孩問今晚有沒有遊戲。妮雅說會有簡單的配對活動，但不是爭奪冠軍的比賽。真正的重點只是大家一起看看天空。

At six, the families met by the field gate. Nia showed the safe path and asked everyone to keep it clear. A local farmer arrived on a horse but left the animal with a friend well away from the group. Nia did not invite the horse into the observing area. An old radio in the center hall played softly, but it was turned off before the group began. Tomas stood beside Ada near the entrance. He wanted to listen to the sky descriptions and feel the raised map again. "Present the stars in words too," he reminded Nia. She said she would, and she asked him to tell her if she spoke too quickly.

六點時，家庭們在草地大門集合。妮雅指示安全路線，請大家保持通暢。一位本地農夫騎馬到來，卻把馬交給朋友，留在遠離人群的地方。妮雅沒有邀請馬進入觀測區。中心走廊有台舊收音機輕輕播放著節目，但觀測開始前便關掉了。托馬斯和艾達站在入口附近。他想聽天空描述，也想再摸一次凸點地圖。「也要用說話介紹星星，」他提醒妮雅。她答應了，並請他在自己說太快時提醒。

The sky cleared enough for several bright stars to appear. Rae found the first one above the tree line, just as she had in the park. Finn found a second. The group stayed in place while Nia pointed slowly. She did not ask everyone to see the same number. Mrs. Sol sat on a bench and listened to Ada describe where the stars appeared. Bo used a small card to show the space between two lights. A new visitor said she had never been to a star lesson before. Nia invited her to ask one question. The woman asked whether she could come back if the class continued. "We hope so," Nia said, "but the board has not decided yet."

天空放晴到足以看見幾顆亮星。芮第一個找到樹線上方的星星，就像她上次在公園裡一樣。芬恩又找到第二顆。妮雅慢慢指出位置，大家留在原地觀察。她沒有要求每個人都看到相同數量。索爾奶奶坐在長椅上，聽艾達描述星星的位置。波拿小卡片表示兩個光點間的空間。一位新訪客說她從沒上過星星課。妮雅請她問一個問題。女士問，如果課程繼續，她能不能再來。「我們希望可以，」妮雅說，「但委員會還沒有決定。」

Before the evening group went outside, Nia asked families to meet inside the center and read the safety notice together. There was not much time between sunset and the end of the visit, so she kept the plan short. Ada checked the number of lamps and the route markers. One parent asked why the center needed to keep a written record of such a simple event. Nia explained that the next group would need to know where the equipment was stored and whether any part of the field needed work. They did not want to begin from nothing each time. The parent nodded and helped write the final check. By the time the group reached the gate, everyone knew where to stand and where to go if they needed help.

晚間小組出門前，妮雅請家庭先在中心集合，一起閱讀安全公告。日落到參觀結束之間的時間不多，所以她把計畫安排得很短。艾達檢查燈具數量和路線標記。一位家長問，這麼簡單的活動為什麼要留書面紀錄。妮雅解釋，下一個使用場地的小組得知道器材收在哪裡，以及草地有哪些部分需要維護。大家不想每次都從零開始。家長點點頭，幫忙寫好最後檢查。隊伍抵達大門時，每個人都知道該站哪裡、需要幫助時該往哪裡走。

When the visit finished, nobody wanted to hurry away. Nia invited families back into the center for a few minutes. They checked the equipment, store each piece in its box, and wrote down the end time. Finn gave Nia a simple drawing of the field, with people standing far apart under three stars. She thanked him and asked if she could keep it with the class work. He agreed. The trial was now finished, but there was still a meeting to come. Nia looked out at the clear patch of sky. She felt glad, not because the stars had followed her plan, but because the class had learned what to do even when the sky did not.

參觀結束後，沒有人急著離開。妮雅請家庭回中心待幾分鐘。大家檢查設備，把每樣東西收回收納箱，記下結束時間。芬恩送給妮雅一張簡單的草地畫，畫裡的人們在三顆星星下保持著間距。妮雅謝謝他，問能不能把畫留在班級作品裡。他同意。試辦期已經結束，接著還有委員會會議。妮雅望著窗外放晴的一角天空，感到高興。不是因為星星乖乖配合她的計畫，而是因為班上已經學會，即使天空不配合，也能知道該怎麼做。
