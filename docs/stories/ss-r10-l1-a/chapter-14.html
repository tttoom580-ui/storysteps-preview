A Name on the Wall

紅磚街的星星課 · 第 14 / 25 章 · 1000以下 · 英中對照

Nia's aunt Celia came to visit with a box of fresh lemons from her garden. She had owned a small business for many years and knew that a kind offer could still bring a hard question. "Have you already agreed to put a donor's name on the wall?" she asked. Nia said no. A woman had offered a clock, but they had chosen paper instead. Celia smiled. "Good. First ask what the class needs. Then ask what the gift means." She helped Nia make lemon water for the helpers. They washed their hands and put on clean gloves before touching the food, as Mara had taught them.

妮雅的阿姨希莉亞來訪，帶來一箱自家花園的新鮮檸檬。她經營小生意多年，知道即使是好意，也可能帶來難回答的問題。「妳已經答應把捐贈者的名字掛在牆上了嗎？」她問。妮雅說沒有。先前一位女士想送鐘，但班上最後改收紙張。希莉亞笑了。「很好，先問班上需要什麼，再問禮物代表什麼。」她幫妮雅替志工做檸檬水。大家依照瑪拉教的方法先洗手、戴上乾淨手套，再碰食物。

The class decided to paint a new sign for the door. Finn wanted to draw the sun and a flower. Tomas asked to have the room name printed in large clear letters, with a raised mark beside it. Bo offered to make the mark from thick paper. Nia asked each parent whether they wanted their child's name placed on a public board. Some parents agreed and some did not. She used only the names with permission. "The class belongs to all of us," she said. "But each person should choose what is shared." A visitor suggested painting the whole door blue. Arun said they could paint a removable sign, not the center's door without permission.

班上決定為教室畫一面新門牌。芬恩想畫太陽和花，托馬斯希望房名用大而清楚的字印出，旁邊加上可以摸到的記號。波願意用厚紙做記號。妮雅逐一詢問家長，是否願意把孩子的名字放在公開看板上。有的同意，有的不同意，她只使用獲准的名字。「教室是大家的，」她說，「但每個人都該自己決定要公開什麼。」一位訪客提議把整扇門漆成藍色。亞倫說，可以畫可拆的門牌，但不能沒經許可就漆中心的門。

After lunch, Celia told Nia why she cared about permission. When her father died, she had been young. A relative had given away some of his old drawings without asking the family. "I know the person wanted to help," she said, "but those papers were part of our memories." Nia listened. It was a sad story, but Celia did not ask anyone to feel sorry for her. She wanted them to learn that care and respect must go together. Nia looked at the old moon drawings on the wall. She made a note to ask again before using any student's picture in a public report.

午餐後，希莉亞告訴妮雅為什麼她那麼在乎同意。父親過世時，她還很年輕。一位親戚沒先詢問家人，就把父親的一些舊畫送了出去。「我知道那個人想幫忙，」她說，「但那些紙也是我們記憶的一部分。」妮雅靜靜聽著。這是個傷心的故事，但希莉亞沒有要求別人同情她。她只是想讓大家知道，關心和尊重應該一起存在。妮雅看向牆上的舊月亮畫，記下未來要在公開報告使用學生作品前再次取得同意。

In the afternoon, Ms. Vale came in her school uniform after a meeting. She said the school might lend some large books to the center. "I will ask the office first," she added. Nia thanked her. The new sign was almost dry, and Bo had paint on his shoulder. He wanted to keep working, but Nia suggested a rest. "Even a happy worker can get tired," she said. They sat under the window and drank water. Finn noticed that the sign was easier to read now. Tomas touched the raised mark and said he could find the correct room without asking anyone for help. Everyone was pleased.

下午，維爾老師開完會，穿著學校制服來訪。她說學校也許可以借幾本大字書給中心。「不過我要先向辦公室詢問，」她補充。妮雅向她道謝。新門牌快乾了，波的肩膀沾了顏料。他還想繼續做，妮雅卻建議休息。「就算開心工作，也會累，」她說。大家坐在窗下喝水。芬恩發現門牌現在更容易閱讀了。托馬斯摸摸凸起的記號，說自己不必開口求助，也能找到正確教室。每個人都很高興。

Celia stayed a little longer to look at the question board. She told Nia that her family had found comfort in sharing memories after her father's death. "No one has to tell a sad story," she added. "But if a person wants to share one, we can listen." Nia said she could agree with that. She did not want the class to use a family's grief to make a report sound important. Celia looked out at the garden, where new plants would grow through the warm months. "Have you ever noticed how one small change can help someone?" she asked. Nia thought of the low shelf, the raised map, and the wider path between chairs. None had cost much, but all had mattered. She thanked her aunt for reminding her that choices could show respect without making a grand display.

希莉亞多留了一會兒，看著課堂問題板。她告訴妮雅，父親過世後，全家曾從分享回憶中得到安慰。「沒有人一定要講悲傷的故事，」她補充，「但如果有人想說，我們可以聽。」妮雅說自己同意。她不想利用家人的難過，讓報告看起來更重要。希莉亞望向花園，溫暖的月份裡新植物將慢慢長大。「妳有沒有發現一點小改變就能幫到人？」她問。妮雅想到低書架、凸點地圖和椅子之間加寬的通道。它們都沒有花很多錢，卻都很重要。她謝謝阿姨提醒：不必盛大展示，選擇本身也能展現尊重。

Before Celia left, she asked Nia what she had learned from the day. Nia answered, "A gift is not always a simple yes, and a name is not always ours to share." Celia nodded. "Then you are growing into the work." Nia did not feel like a great teacher yet. She still needed help with costs, weather, and difficult questions. But she had made choices she could explain. The sign on the door did not have a rich person's name in large letters. It said, in clear blue print: Star Class—All Questions Welcome. That was the name the group had chosen together.

希莉亞離開前問妮雅，今天學到了什麼。妮雅回答：「收到禮物不一定只要說好，別人的名字也不是我們想公開就能公開。」希莉亞點點頭。「妳正在學著把工作做好。」妮雅還不覺得自己是偉大的老師。她仍需要別人幫忙處理費用、天氣和難題，但她已經做出可以說明理由的選擇。門上的新牌子沒有把富有捐贈者的名字寫得很大，而是用清楚的藍字寫著：「星星課——歡迎所有問題。」那是大家共同選出的名字。
