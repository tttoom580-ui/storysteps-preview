留在室內的那一夜

The Night We Stayed Inside

紅磚街的星星課 · 第 20 / 25 章 · 1000以下 · 英中對照

用互動版查詞、朗讀與收藏

The weather report showed strong rain for Saturday. Nia had planned an evening sky visit, but she changed it to an indoor lesson. A family had come from abroad that month and asked whether they could still join. Nia said yes and sent a short note about the change. When the rain began, water ran below the front windows, but it did not enter the room. Ada checked the door with Arun. "We don't need to hide the bad weather from people," Nia said. "We need to tell them what has changed." She put up a clear sign so nobody would stand outside waiting for a star that could not be seen.

天氣預報顯示星期六會下大雨。妮雅原本安排傍晚觀星，現在改為室內課。一個當月剛從國外搬來的家庭問，是否還能參加。妮雅說可以，並寄出課程變更的簡短通知。下雨時，水沿著前窗下方流過，但沒有進入室內。艾達和亞倫一起檢查門口。「我們不用把壞天氣藏起來，」妮雅說，「只要告訴大家有哪些改變。」她貼出清楚標示，避免有人站在外面等候看不見的星星。

The indoor lesson used a small machine that showed moving dots of light on the wall. It was not a real picture of the sky, and Nia made that clear. She set the machine on a low, stable table with the cord out of the walking path. Tomas wanted to know how the lights moved. Ada explained the turning part and let him feel the model only when it was off. Bo asked if they could make anything like it from paper. "We can make a simple part," Nia said. "But we will not open a machine that belongs to the center." The group made paper circles and moved them by hand.

室內課使用一台小機器，把移動的光點投在牆上。那不是真正的天空照片，妮雅也清楚說明。她把機器放在穩固的低桌上，電線避開行走路線。托馬斯想知道光點怎麼移動。艾達解釋轉動的部分，只在機器關閉時讓他觸摸模型。波問能不能用紙做出類似的東西。「我們可以做簡單的部件，」妮雅說，「但不能打開中心的機器。」於是大家用手推動紙圓片。

At lunch, Mara brought egg sandwiches with a little butter. She placed the food on a separate table far from the equipment. A child began to cry because she had hoped to see stars outside. Nia sat beside her and asked if she wanted to tell someone what felt hard. The child said she had waited all week. "I understand," Nia said. "I was looking forward to it too." She did not promise the rain would stop soon. Instead, she offered a choice between drawing the sky she hoped to see and helping move the paper models. The child chose the models. Her hands were soon busy.

午餐時，瑪拉帶來加了少量奶油的雞蛋三明治，把食物放在遠離設備的另一張桌上。一個孩子哭了，因為她很希望能在戶外看星星。妮雅坐到她旁邊，問她想不想說說難受的地方。孩子說自己等了一整個星期。「我懂，」妮雅說，「我也很期待。」她沒有保證雨很快會停，而是請孩子選擇：畫出希望看到的天空，或幫忙移動紙模型。孩子選了模型，手很快就忙起來。

A visiting doctor asked whether a bright flashing light could hurt anyone's eyes. Nia said the class was using a slow, steady light and that people could look away or leave if it bothered them. She checked the settings again. A parent pointed to the light level and asked whether it could go lower. Ada showed Nia how to turn it down. The room became softer. Tomas said the noise of the machine was harder for him than the light. Nia turned the sound off. No one had to endure an unpleasant part of the lesson just because the teacher liked the effect.

一位來訪的醫師問，快速閃爍的亮光是否可能讓某些人的眼睛不舒服。妮雅說，課程使用緩慢穩定的光，每個人都能在不適時移開視線或離開。她再檢查一次設定。有家長指著亮度問能不能調低，艾達教妮雅操作。房間的光變得柔和。托馬斯說，比起光，機器的聲音更讓他不舒服。妮雅把聲音關掉。沒有人需要只因老師喜歡某種效果，就忍受不舒服的課程內容。

The family from abroad arrived with a small toy fox whose tail had come loose. The child's father said he was the mother's husband, and he asked where they could sit without blocking the door. Arun showed them a place near the front. The child had brought homework from school: draw one thing she hoped to learn about the sky. She had drawn a star over a house. Nia asked whether she wanted to add the picture to the class wall. The child said she would rather take it home. "Of course," Nia replied. "You can keep it." The father thanked her. They stayed for the lesson, even though they had seen no real stars that night. The child left saying, "I hope I can draw the moon next time."

從國外搬來的家庭抵達，帶著一隻尾巴有些鬆脫的玩具狐狸。孩子的爸爸介紹自己是媽媽的丈夫，並問哪裡可以坐又不擋門。亞倫帶他們到前排的位置。孩子帶來學校作業：畫一件希望學到的天空知識。她畫了一座房子上方的星星。妮雅問她願不願意把畫貼在班級牆上。孩子說她比較想帶回家。「當然可以，」妮雅回答，「妳可以自己留著。」爸爸向她道謝。即使那晚沒有真正的星星，全家仍留下來上課。孩子離開時說：「我希望下次能畫月亮。」

Before the group went home, Nia read a number from the board: twenty-three different people had attended at least one lesson so far. She did not call it a hundred or try to make it sound larger. A father said that the real number mattered because it showed people had chosen to come. Nia agreed. The rain was still falling hard outside, but everyone left with a clear plan and a safe way home. She stayed to clean the tables and pack the machine with Arun. It had been a night without real stars, but it was not a night without learning.

回家前，妮雅讀出黑板上的數字：到目前為止，共有二十三個不同的人至少參加過一次課。她沒有把數字說成一百，也沒有想讓它聽起來更多。一位爸爸說，真正的人數很重要，因為它代表大家自己選擇來上課。妮雅同意。外面仍下著大雨，每個人卻都帶著明確計畫、安全回家。她留下來和亞倫收拾桌子及機器。這一夜沒有真正的星星，卻不是沒有學習的一夜。
