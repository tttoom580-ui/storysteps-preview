A Promise on the Screen

紅磚街的星星課 · 第 16 / 25 章 · 1000以下 · 英中對照

用互動版查詞、朗讀與收藏

A new problem appeared on Wednesday. Someone had posted a message online saying the star class would show a hundred falling stars on Saturday. Nia knew that was not correct. She was angry, not because the picture was ugly, but because families might come expecting something she could not give. Ada found that the post had started with a local writer who had misunderstood a short note about a possible sky event later in the year. "We need to correct it," Nia said. "Not attack the person." She wrote a calm message: The Saturday class will teach how to find a few stars. We cannot promise falling stars.

星期三出現一個新問題。有人在網路上貼文，說星星課星期六會展示一百顆流星。妮雅知道這不正確。她很生氣，不是因為圖片不好看，而是家庭可能帶著她無法實現的期待前來。艾達查到貼文來自一位本地作家，他誤解了之前一則關於今年晚些時候可能出現天象的簡短消息。「我們得更正，」妮雅說，「不是攻擊那個人。」她寫了一段平靜的公告：星期六的課會教大家找幾顆星，但不能保證有流星。

The writer came to the center the next day. He wore a green shirt and held a paper bag of cookies. "I made the mistake," he said. "I wanted people to be excited." Nia thanked him for coming, but explained that even a smart person must check facts before sharing them. "Can you change your post?" she asked. "Yes," he said. "I will put the correct words at the top." They sat together with a cup of tea. The man read the new message aloud, and Nia checked every sentence. He offered to bring a small note to the class, explaining how he had made the error.

隔天，那位作家來到中心。他穿著綠襯衫，拿著一袋餅乾。「我弄錯了，」他說，「我只是想讓大家興奮。」妮雅謝謝他願意來，也解釋再聰明的人分享資訊前都需要查證。「你可以修改貼文嗎？」她問。「可以，」他說，「我會把正確內容放在最上面。」他們一邊喝茶一邊核對新公告。男子把文字念出來，妮雅逐句確認。他也提出願意來班上，說明自己是怎麼看錯消息的。

On Saturday, the group began with a lesson about Earth and the sun. Ada used a lamp and a ball. She asked who knew what made day and night. Finn raised his hand, but Nia called on a new student first. The new girl said she had watched a television show about the stars, but could not remember the answer. Nia gave her time. "You may look at the model," she said. The girl watched the light fall on half the ball. "One side is bright and the other side is dark," she said. "Good," Nia replied. "Now we can ask why the two sides change as Earth turns."

星期六，大家先上地球與太陽的課。艾達用燈和球當模型，問誰知道白天和黑夜怎麼來的。芬恩舉手，但妮雅先請新學員回答。新來的女孩說，她看過介紹星星的電視節目，卻不記得答案。妮雅給她時間。「妳可以看看模型，」她說。女孩看見光照在球的一半。「一邊亮，一邊暗，」她說。「很好，」妮雅回答，「接下來我們可以問：地球轉動時，為什麼明暗兩側會改變？」

After the model, Nia asked students to make their own short notices. One child wrote, "Come and see every star!" Bo crossed out the word every. Another wrote, "Come and learn one way to find a star." The group liked the second notice better. They put up the corrected message near the door. Outside, a garden worker was making a small fire in a safe garden stove for a permitted event, and heat rose into the air. Nia took the class away from the smoke. She pointed out that safety and clear information mattered in the garden as much as in the classroom. No one needed to stand near the stove to learn about warm air.

模型活動後，妮雅請學生自己寫短公告。一個孩子寫：「來看所有星星！」波把「所有」兩字劃掉。另一位寫：「來學一種尋找星星的方法。」大家比較喜歡第二張，把修改後的訊息貼在門邊。外面有花園工作人員依規定在安全爐具裡生火準備活動，熱氣往空中升起。妮雅帶班上遠離煙霧。她指出，花園和教室一樣需要安全與清楚的資訊。沒有人需要靠近爐子，才學得會熱空氣的概念。

The writer had paid with a fifty-cent coin for one cookie at the corner shop, but he did not use that small gift to change how Nia felt. He knew trust could not be bought. Before the lesson, Finn ran too fast up the stairs and hurt his knee a little. Nia asked him to slow down and told his mother what had happened. The injury was small, but nobody wanted him to fall again. Inside, the main subject was how to correct an idea, not how to look clever. Nia asked everyone to wear comfortable shoes and read the new notice before beginning. Ada put the cookies on a table away from the lamps. They could share a treat after class, but the lesson itself would stay focused on checking words carefully.

那位作家在轉角小店用一枚五十美分的硬幣買了一塊餅乾，卻沒有拿這份小禮物來改變妮雅對事件的看法。他知道信任不是買得到的。上課前，芬恩跑上樓梯跑得太快，膝蓋有點撞疼。妮雅請他放慢腳步，並告訴媽媽發生什麼事。傷勢不大，但大家都不希望他再跌倒。進教室後，今天的主題是如何更正想法，而不是如何顯得聰明。妮雅請大家穿舒服的鞋子，先讀新公告再開始。艾達把餅乾放在遠離燈具的桌上。大家可以下課再分享點心，課程本身則專心練習仔細查證文字。

At the end, the writer stood up and spoke for one minute. He said he had copied words without reading the full note, and he was sorry. He did not ask the children to forgive him or give him a reward. He showed the corrected message and said he would check his sources next time. Nia thanked him for being clear. She had been worried that the mistake would hurt the class. Instead, the group had learned to ask a simple but important question: How do we know this is true? Nia put that question at the top of the board, where every new student could see it.

課程最後，作家站起來說了一分鐘。他承認自己沒有讀完原始說明，就複製其中的文字，並為此道歉。他沒有要求孩子原諒或給他獎賞，而是展示更正後的公告，說下次會先查證來源。妮雅謝謝他清楚說明。她原本擔心這次錯誤會傷害課程，結果全班反而學到一個簡單卻重要的問題：我們怎麼知道這是真的？妮雅把這句話寫在黑板最上方，讓每個新學員都能看見。
