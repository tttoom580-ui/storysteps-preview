誰能使用這間教室

Who Can Use the Room?

紅磚街的星星課 · 第 13 / 25 章 · 1000以下 · 英中對照

用互動版查詞、朗讀與收藏

On Monday, Mr. Hale told Nia that a city hotel had asked to use the center room on Saturdays. The hotel could pay more money than the star class. Nia felt worried. The room was small, but it had become important to everyone who came. "Is there a rule about who gets the space?" she asked. Mr. Hale said the city had a public-use rule: a new business could not simply take a room that people were already using. The center needed to review both requests. Nia asked what information the board would need. "Your class record," he said. "And a clear plan for the weeks ahead."

星期一，海爾先生告訴妮雅，市裡一家旅館想在星期六使用中心教室。旅館願意付的錢比星星課多。妮雅很擔心。房間雖小，卻已經對每位參加的人變得重要。「有規定決定誰可以使用空間嗎？」她問。海爾先生說，市府有公共使用規定：新商家不能只因付錢較多，就直接拿走正在使用的公共教室。中心需要審查兩邊的需求。妮雅問委員會需要哪些資料。「課堂紀錄，」他說，「以及接下來幾週的明確計畫。」

Nia went home feeling as if a gate had closed before her. She was twenty-five years of age and had not run a program like this before. Three years ago she had been a student in another city. She could borrow books and draw maps, but speaking to a board felt difficult. Her mother told her to put on a warm coat and take a short walk. "A small fear can feel like a giant rock if you keep holding it," she said. Nia walked along the street until she could think clearly. She did not need to win a fight with the hotel. She needed to explain what the class actually offered.

妮雅回家時，感覺像有扇大門在眼前關上。她二十五歲，從沒辦過這樣的課程。三年前她還在另一個城市當學生。借書和畫地圖她都會，但要向委員會報告卻很困難。媽媽叫她穿上暖外套，出去散散步。「小小的害怕如果一直抱著，也會像大石頭一樣重，」媽媽說。妮雅沿街走了一段，才重新理清思緒。她不需要和旅館打一場仗，只需要說明課堂真正帶來了什麼。

At the center, Ada collected the notes from each lesson. Everyone's words were kept in the same form, whether the speaker was a child, a parent, or a visitor. Tomas said he did not mind having his name on the touch-map page, but he wanted to read the sentence first. Nia showed him the large-print version and asked for his approval. Mrs. Sol wanted her comment about the bench included. Bo wanted the note about the slippery water saved too. Ada used a piece of rope to tie the pages together because the old folder was broken. They had a record of real work, not just a song of praise.

回到中心，艾達整理每堂課的紀錄。無論發言者是孩子、家長還是訪客，所有人的話都照原意保存。托馬斯不介意名字出現在觸摸地圖那一頁，但希望先讀過句子。妮雅拿給他大字版本，詢問是否同意。索爾奶奶想保留有關長椅的意見，波也想留下打翻水的紀錄。艾達用繩子把頁面綁在一起，因為舊資料夾已經壞了。他們手上有的是實際工作的紀錄，不只是讚美的歌。

A hotel worker came to the center to see the room. Nia met him at the door and offered to show the space. He said the hotel wanted a quiet place for a small group on Saturday mornings. Nia explained the class schedule and the public review. "We are not against your meeting," she said. "We only need to know whether our lessons can stay here." The worker nodded. He had never heard of the star class before. After looking at the chairs and maps, he said the hotel might prefer a room near its own building. He promised to tell his manager, but Nia did not count that as a decision.

一位旅館員工來中心看場地。妮雅在門口迎接，帶他參觀。員工說，旅館想在星期六早上找一個安靜房間給小團體開會。妮雅解釋課程時間和公共審查程序。「我們不是反對你們開會，」她說，「我們只需要知道課程能否繼續在這裡。」員工點頭。他以前從沒聽過星星課。看過椅子和地圖後，他說旅館也許更適合使用自家建築附近的空間。他答應告訴經理，但妮雅沒有把這句話當成正式決定。

To prepare for the review, Nia asked the group to stand in front of their work and say one thing that had changed. Finn stood by the moon model and explained that he now understood why it seemed to show different shapes. Bo stood beside the rulers and said their larger numbers were easier to read. Tomas sat by his touch map and described how moving the dots had improved it. Nia did not tell anyone what answer to give. She simply timed each explanation so every person had a chance to speak. The exercise showed her that she did not need to defend the class alone. The learners could describe their own work, and the board could hear more than one voice.

為了準備審查，妮雅請小組站在自己的作品前，說明一件改變過的事。芬恩站在月亮模型旁，解釋自己現在明白它為何看起來有不同形狀。波站在紙尺旁，說放大後的數字更容易讀。托馬斯坐在觸摸地圖旁，描述移動星點後如何改善使用。妮雅沒有告訴任何人該回答什麼，只是替每段說明計時，讓每個人都有發言機會。這個練習讓她看見，自己不必一個人替全班辯護。學員可以說明自己做的事，委員會也能聽見不只一種聲音。

That night, Nia made two copies of the class plan. One was for Mr. Hale and the board; the other would stay in the room where anyone could read it. She marked the number of visits, the lessons already completed, the cost of materials, and the changes made for safety. She left an empty space for tomorrow's questions. The plan did not promise that every night would be clear or every child would see a star. It promised honest lessons, open questions, and careful use of a shared room. Nia looked at the pages and felt a little more ready. Whatever the board decided, she could speak about work she had truly done.

當晚，妮雅複印兩份課程計畫。一份交給海爾先生與委員會，一份留在教室讓任何人閱讀。她記下參加次數、已完成的課程、材料花費，以及為安全所做的修改，還為明天的新問題留出空白。計畫沒有保證每個晚上都晴朗、每個孩子都能看到星星，而是承諾誠實教學、開放提問、好好使用公共教室。妮雅望著紙張，感覺準備得更好了。無論委員會如何決定，她都能說明自己確實做過的工作。
