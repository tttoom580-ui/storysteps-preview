A Light for Every Learner

紅磚街的星星課 · 第 21 / 25 章 · 1000以下 · 英中對照

The final week was busy from its first morning. Nia needed to prepare the last class and a simple show of student work for the center board. She did not want a grand party. She wanted people to see what the students had made and learned. Ada carried a ball, a paper airplane, and three different types of small light into the room. The airplane was for a lesson about how far a thing could travel; it was not a model of a star. Nia put a card on each item explaining what it could show. The class had learned that a model was only useful when people knew its limits.

最後一週從一開始就很忙。妮雅需要準備最後一堂課，也要替中心委員會做一場簡單的學生作品展示。她不想舉辦盛大的派對，只希望大家看見學生做過什麼、學到什麼。艾達帶進教室一顆球、一架紙飛機，以及三種不同的小燈。紙飛機是用來說明物品能飛多遠，不是星星模型。妮雅在每樣物品旁放一張卡片，說明它能展示什麼。全班已經明白，只有知道模型的限制，模型才有用。

A little child came through the door with her mother. The girl was bored at first because the room looked like an office instead of a playground. Finn gave her a simple job: place paper stars on a board from the lowest line to the highest. She liked that. Her mother brought small pieces of cheese for a snack, but kept the food away from the work tables. A soft smell of bread came from the kitchen below. In another room, someone played the piano. Nia closed the connecting door so students who needed quiet could work. She had learned that one bright light or one loud sound did not suit everyone.

一個小女孩跟媽媽走進門。她起初覺得無聊，因為房間看起來像辦公室，不像遊戲場。芬恩交給她一件簡單工作：把紙星星從最低到最高的線依序貼上。她喜歡這個任務。媽媽帶了小塊起司當點心，但讓食物遠離工作桌。樓下廚房傳來淡淡麵包香，另一個房間有人彈鋼琴。妮雅關上相通的門，讓需要安靜的學生可以工作。她已經知道，同一盞亮光或同一個大聲音不一定適合所有人。

At the touch-map table, Tomas showed a new visitor how to feel the raised dots. He did not speak for the whole class; he simply shared his own experience. The visitor asked if he could get a copy. Nia said the center could make a few more from cardboard, but every copy would need to be tested. "Please do not save time by making the dots too small," Tomas said. "Small dots can become hard to tell apart." Ada wrote that note on the work card. Nia watched the two men talk. She was pleased that Tomas no longer had to ask permission before offering an idea. It was his classroom too.

在觸摸地圖桌旁，托馬斯示範給新訪客看，如何摸凸起的星點。他沒有替全班發言，只分享自己的經驗。訪客問能不能拿到一份。妮雅說中心可以再用厚紙做幾張，但每張都需要測試。「請不要為了省時間，把凸點做得太小，」托馬斯說，「小點太近會很難分辨。」艾達把這句話記在工作卡上。妮雅看著兩人討論，很高興托馬斯現在不用先徵求許可，就能自然提出想法。這也是他的教室。

After lunch, Nia asked the students to choose what they wanted to show the board. Bo chose the two paper rulers, one old and one new. Rae chose her card with the single star from the park. Finn chose the moon ball. Tomas chose the touch map and the notes explaining why it had changed. Nia did not choose for them. "We have only one table for each group," she said. "You may decide what tells your story best." They placed the work in order, keeping the path to the bathroom clear. Nia took a step back and saw a room full of quiet choices, not just objects.

午餐後，妮雅請學生自己挑選要向委員會展示的作品。波選了新舊兩把紙尺，芮選了公園裡只看見一顆星的卡片，芬恩選了月亮球，托馬斯則選了觸摸地圖與修改說明。妮雅沒有替他們決定。「每組只有一張桌子，」她說，「你們可以選最能說明自己故事的作品。」大家依序擺好，並保持通往廁所的通道暢通。妮雅退後一步，看見一間充滿安靜選擇的房間，而不只是滿屋子的物品。

Nia laid out two kinds of star cards. One type had large letters and wide spaces. The other had raised dots and short words. She asked visitors to look at the cards or feel them, then say which one helped them understand the map. A little boy kept opening his mouth to ask a question and then closing it again. Nia knelt beside the table, not beside the equipment, and told him he could ask whenever he was ready. He finally said the two cards did not show exactly the same number of stars. Nia agreed. The raised map was a simpler model, so it left some stars out. The two maps were not enemies. Each offered a way into the lesson, and students could choose the one that worked for them.

妮雅擺出兩種星卡。一種字大、間距寬，另一種有凸點和短文字。她請訪客用眼睛看或用手觸摸，再說哪一張能幫自己理解地圖。一個小男孩好幾次張嘴想發問，卻又把嘴閉上。妮雅蹲在桌旁，避開器材，告訴他準備好就可以問。他終於說，兩張卡上的星星數量不完全一樣。妮雅同意，凸點圖是較簡單的模型，因此省略一些星點。兩張地圖不是互相競爭，而是提供不同的學習入口；學生可以選適合自己的那一張。

Tonight, Nia wrote in her notebook, the class will not promise a perfect sky. It will show real learning. She read the line aloud to Ada. Ada said it sounded simple enough for anyone to understand. Nia thought of the early mornings, the bad weather, and the many small changes they had made. None of that work was as beautiful as a large picture of the stars. But it was the reason the children could come back each week and feel welcome. She switched off the last lamp and left the room ready for the final Saturday.

那晚，妮雅在筆記本寫下：「我們不承諾完美的天空，只展示真實的學習。」她念給艾達聽。艾達說，這句話簡單得讓任何人都懂。妮雅想起早起的日子、壞天氣和他們做過的許多小修改。那些工作沒有巨大的星空圖片那麼漂亮，卻正是孩子們每週能再來、並感到受歡迎的原因。她關掉最後一盞燈，讓教室做好迎接最後一個星期六的準備。
