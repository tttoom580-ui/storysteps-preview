The palace locksmith lends a key on condition that nobody be blamed for his choice. Iris felt the meaning of this new event before the full crowd understood it. In Rosecourt, each unusual sign could become a rumor by evening, and rumors were hard to pull back once they had spread. Celia stood beside the nearest witness and asked for the sequence of actions, not a dramatic conclusion. Some details contradicted what they had learned the day before. That was disappointing and useful at the same time. Iris opened a fresh page, wrote the date at the top, and decided the truth would need more than one voice.

宮廷鎖匠借出鑰匙，條件是不能讓無辜者替他的決定受罰。 群眾尚未完全理解時，伊莉絲已感到這件新事的分量。在玫瑰宮，每個異常徵兆到了晚上都可能變成謠言，而謠言一旦傳開就很難收回。瑟莉亞站到最近的證人身邊，詢問行動的先後順序，而非戲劇性的結論。有些細節與昨天得到的資訊矛盾，既令人失望也很有用。伊莉絲翻開新的一頁，在上方寫下日期，決定真相需要不止一個人的聲音。

When the plan was discussed, Celia did not agree with everything Iris said. Their argument began over a detail that seemed small, but it soon exposed a difference in what each considered fair. A volunteer offered to settle the issue by a quick vote. Iris asked to hear from the people who would bear the greatest risk first. The answer changed the proposal. Nobody enjoyed admitting they had missed something, yet the room became quieter after the correction. It was easier to make a useful choice when disagreement was allowed to stay in the open.

討論計畫時，瑟莉亞並不完全同意伊莉絲的話。爭論始於一個看似微小的細節，卻揭露了兩人對公平的不同理解。有志工提議立刻投票解決，伊莉絲則要求先聽取風險最高者的意見。回答改變了提案。沒有人喜歡承認自己漏看了什麼，但訂正後房間反而安靜。當分歧能公開存在時，人們更容易做出有用的選擇。

At the end of the day, Iris sat beside the royal seal and tried to count what had genuinely changed. There was no sudden victory, but several people who had been afraid now knew whom to ask for help. Celia brought a second copy of the records and placed it out of reach of anyone who might remove the first. The plan had become less exciting and more dependable. That did not disappoint Iris. The question was never how to appear fearless. It was how to leave a safer place behind, even if the next person in charge had a different name.

一天結束時，伊莉絲坐在皇家印章旁，試著清點真正改變了什麼。沒有突然的勝利，但原本害怕的幾個人如今知道該向誰求助。瑟莉亞帶來第二份紀錄，放到即使第一份被拿走也不會一起消失的地方。計畫變得不那麼刺激，卻更可靠。伊莉絲並不失望。問題從來不是如何顯得無所畏懼，而是即使下個負責人換了名字，也能留下更安全的地方。

During a pause in the investigation, Celia unfolded an old language ledger. Five marked entries on the first line read "anyhow", "assume", "bacteria", "bump", "creator". The next line held "excellence", "inner", "missile", "multiply", "necessity". None was a magic password or an answer by itself; each was a fragment of a larger history. Iris asked who had written the marks, why they had been kept, and whether they belonged to the same account. It would have been easy to declare the puzzle solved simply because the words were now visible. Instead, they copied the terms, recorded the source, and returned to the unresolved problem outside the room.

調查暫停時，瑟莉亞攤開一本舊語詞紀錄。第一行的五個註記寫著「anyhow」、「assume」、「bacteria」、「bump」、「creator」；下一行則有「excellence」、「inner」、「missile」、「multiply」、「necessity」。它們都不是魔法密碼，也無法單獨解答問題；每個詞只是更大段歷史的片段。伊莉絲詢問誰寫下這些字、為何保存，以及它們是否屬於同一份記錄。只因文字可見就宣稱解開謎題很容易，但他們只抄下詞條、標明來源，便回到室外仍未解決的問題。

While others argued over a fast solution, Celia asked who had the right to make the final decision. Iris could have answered with a name or a title. Instead, they made a list of everyone whose home, work, or safety would change. The list was longer than expected. Some people could come to a meeting; others needed a visit or a written invitation. There was no way to make a difficult choice painless, but a choice made without listening would be worse. The team planned another discussion and made sure the invitation did not require wealth or rank.

其他人爭論最快解法時，瑟莉亞問誰有權作最後決定。伊莉絲本可回答一個姓名或頭銜，卻改列出家園、工作或安全會受到影響的所有人。清單比預期長。有些人能來開會，另一些需要拜訪或書面邀請。艱難選擇不可能毫無痛苦，但不傾聽就做決定更糟。團隊安排下一次討論，確保受邀不需要財富或階級。

During a pause in the investigation, Celia unfolded an old language ledger. Five marked entries on the first line read "politician", "resource", "stale", "stomach", "summary". The next line held "suspect", "technique", "teenage", "tropical", "wax". None was a magic password or an answer by itself; each was a fragment of a larger history. Iris asked who had written the marks, why they had been kept, and whether they belonged to the same account. It would have been easy to declare the puzzle solved simply because the words were now visible. Instead, they copied the terms, recorded the source, and returned to the unresolved problem outside the room.

調查暫停時，瑟莉亞攤開一本舊語詞紀錄。第一行的五個註記寫著「politician」、「resource」、「stale」、「stomach」、「summary」；下一行則有「suspect」、「technique」、「teenage」、「tropical」、「wax」。它們都不是魔法密碼，也無法單獨解答問題；每個詞只是更大段歷史的片段。伊莉絲詢問誰寫下這些字、為何保存，以及它們是否屬於同一份記錄。只因文字可見就宣稱解開謎題很容易，但他們只抄下詞條、標明來源，便回到室外仍未解決的問題。

After the others left, Celia asked Iris whether working together still felt like a choice. Feelings had grown while danger filled their days, but that did not give one person power over another. Iris said that trust needed room for disagreement, even when the answer hurt. Celia accepted this without trying to turn it into a promise. For a moment they stood close enough to hear each other's breath, then returned to the unfinished work. They did not need a crowd to decide how they felt. The courage they were learning was quieter than any spell and more difficult to fake.

其他人離開後，瑟莉亞問伊莉絲，一起工作如今是否仍是自由選擇。危險充滿日常時，感情也漸漸長出來，但這不給任何人支配另一人的權利。伊莉絲說，信任必須容得下不同意見，即使答案可能傷人。瑟莉亞接受了，沒有把話逼成承諾。兩人有一刻近得聽見彼此呼吸，然後繼續未完工作。他們不需要人群替自己決定感情。正學習的勇氣比任何法術都安靜，也更難假裝。

Before leaving, Iris wrote one line at the bottom of the page: the day's answer could still be corrected. Celia checked that people affected by the decision could read it and reply. A messenger brought news that the next meeting would concern 'A Quiet Theft'. Neither knew precisely what this new matter would demand. They did know what should not be traded away to save a few hours: honest records, a safe path, and the right of someone weaker to disagree. Iris carried the unfinished notes toward Rosecourt, listening to the footsteps behind them and thinking of the question that morning would bring.

離開前，伊莉絲在紙頁底下寫道：今天的答案仍然可以訂正。瑟莉亞確認受決定影響的人能讀到並回應。信使傳來消息，下次會議將處理〈安靜的偷取〉。兩人都不確定新事件要求什麼，卻知道哪些東西不能只為節省幾小時就被交換掉：誠實紀錄、安全道路，以及弱者提出異議的權利。伊莉絲帶著未完成的筆記走向玫瑰宮，聽著背後腳步聲，想著明早會出現的問題。
