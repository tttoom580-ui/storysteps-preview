宮廷最討厭的兩位千金，決定聯手造反 · 第 4 / 25 章 · 2000–3000 · 英中對照 · 校訂中

Iris finds a servant network carrying legal notices between palace corridors. Iris felt the meaning of this new event before the full crowd understood it. In Rosecourt, each unusual sign could become a rumor by evening, and rumors were hard to pull back once they had spread. Celia stood beside the nearest witness and asked for the sequence of actions, not a dramatic conclusion. Some details contradicted what they had learned the day before. That was disappointing and useful at the same time. Iris opened a fresh page, wrote the date at the top, and decided the truth would need more than one voice.

伊莉絲發現僕役透過宮廷走廊傳遞法律通知的網絡。 群眾尚未完全理解時，伊莉絲已感到這件新事的分量。在玫瑰宮，每個異常徵兆到了晚上都可能變成謠言，而謠言一旦傳開就很難收回。瑟莉亞站到最近的證人身邊，詢問行動的先後順序，而非戲劇性的結論。有些細節與昨天得到的資訊矛盾，既令人失望也很有用。伊莉絲翻開新的一頁，在上方寫下日期，決定真相需要不止一個人的聲音。

While others argued over a fast solution, Celia asked who had the right to make the final decision. Iris could have answered with a name or a title. Instead, they made a list of everyone whose home, work, or safety would change. The list was longer than expected. Some people could come to a meeting; others needed a visit or a written invitation. There was no way to make a difficult choice painless, but a choice made without listening would be worse. The team planned another discussion and made sure the invitation did not require wealth or rank.

其他人爭論最快解法時，瑟莉亞問誰有權作最後決定。伊莉絲本可回答一個姓名或頭銜，卻改列出家園、工作或安全會受到影響的所有人。清單比預期長。有些人能來開會，另一些需要拜訪或書面邀請。艱難選擇不可能毫無痛苦，但不傾聽就做決定更糟。團隊安排下一次討論，確保受邀不需要財富或階級。

There was a practical problem behind the dramatic story. A route could look short on paper and still be impossible for a tired person to use. Iris walked it at a normal pace while Celia counted the steps and listed obstacles. At the narrowest point they stopped because the safest answer was not the fastest one. Someone suggested using the royal seal as proof that everything was under control. Iris refused the easy claim. An impressive object could not replace a careful test, especially when strangers might have to trust it without knowing the story behind it.

戲劇性的故事背後有個實際問題。路線在紙上看起來很短，對疲憊的人卻可能走不了。伊莉絲以正常速度走一遍，瑟莉亞數著步數並列出障礙。他們在最窄處停下，因為最安全的答案未必最快。有人提議用皇家印章證明一切都在掌握中，伊莉絲拒絕了這種輕率說法。再令人印象深刻的物件，也不能取代仔細測試，尤其當陌生人必須在不知道背景時相信它。

During a pause in the investigation, Celia unfolded an old language ledger. Five marked entries on the first line read "alley", "bravery", "cable", "cherry", "crash". The next line held "definition", "dizzy", "investigate", "knit", "label". None was a magic password or an answer by itself; each was a fragment of a larger history. Iris asked who had written the marks, why they had been kept, and whether they belonged to the same account. It would have been easy to declare the puzzle solved simply because the words were now visible. Instead, they copied the terms, recorded the source, and returned to the unresolved problem outside the room.

調查暫停時，瑟莉亞攤開一本舊語詞紀錄。第一行的五個註記寫著「alley」、「bravery」、「cable」、「cherry」、「crash」；下一行則有「definition」、「dizzy」、「investigate」、「knit」、「label」。它們都不是魔法密碼，也無法單獨解答問題；每個詞只是更大段歷史的片段。伊莉絲詢問誰寫下這些字、為何保存，以及它們是否屬於同一份記錄。只因文字可見就宣稱解開謎題很容易，但他們只抄下詞條、標明來源，便回到室外仍未解決的問題。

News of the plan reached the Crown Council before sunset. The official reply was quick, polished, and strangely certain. Iris read it twice, looking for the questions it refused to answer. Celia noticed that it described a public problem as a private disagreement, although many witnesses had been affected. They did not accuse anyone merely because the answer sounded unpleasant. Instead, they requested the original documents and the name of the person responsible for storing them. A rival deserved the chance to respond to a clear question, but not the right to hide evidence behind an impressive signature.

日落前，計畫的消息傳到王冠議會。官方回覆快速、漂亮，而且確定得古怪。伊莉絲讀了兩次，尋找其中刻意避開的問題。瑟莉亞注意到它把影響多名證人的公共問題描述成私人爭執。他們沒有因回答難聽就隨意指控，而是要求原始文件與保管人的姓名。對手有機會回答清楚的問題，卻沒有權利躲在漂亮簽名後藏匿證據。

During a pause in the investigation, Celia unfolded an old language ledger. Five marked entries on the first line read "murder", "nap", "saucer", "sexy", "solid". The next line held "starve", "thankful", "tower", "van", "warmth". None was a magic password or an answer by itself; each was a fragment of a larger history. Iris asked who had written the marks, why they had been kept, and whether they belonged to the same account. It would have been easy to declare the puzzle solved simply because the words were now visible. Instead, they copied the terms, recorded the source, and returned to the unresolved problem outside the room.

調查暫停時，瑟莉亞攤開一本舊語詞紀錄。第一行的五個註記寫著「murder」、「nap」、「saucer」、「sexy」、「solid」；下一行則有「starve」、「thankful」、「tower」、「van」、「warmth」。它們都不是魔法密碼，也無法單獨解答問題；每個詞只是更大段歷史的片段。伊莉絲詢問誰寫下這些字、為何保存，以及它們是否屬於同一份記錄。只因文字可見就宣稱解開謎題很容易，但他們只抄下詞條、標明來源，便回到室外仍未解決的問題。

After the others left, Celia asked Iris whether working together still felt like a choice. Feelings had grown while danger filled their days, but that did not give one person power over another. Iris said that trust needed room for disagreement, even when the answer hurt. Celia accepted this without trying to turn it into a promise. For a moment they stood close enough to hear each other's breath, then returned to the unfinished work. They did not need a crowd to decide how they felt. The courage they were learning was quieter than any spell and more difficult to fake.

其他人離開後，瑟莉亞問伊莉絲，一起工作如今是否仍是自由選擇。危險充滿日常時，感情也漸漸長出來，但這不給任何人支配另一人的權利。伊莉絲說，信任必須容得下不同意見，即使答案可能傷人。瑟莉亞接受了，沒有把話逼成承諾。兩人有一刻近得聽見彼此呼吸，然後繼續未完工作。他們不需要人群替自己決定感情。正學習的勇氣比任何法術都安靜，也更難假裝。

Before leaving, Iris wrote one line at the bottom of the page: the day's answer could still be corrected. Celia checked that people affected by the decision could read it and reply. A messenger brought news that the next meeting would concern 'The Masquerade'. Neither knew precisely what this new matter would demand. They did know what should not be traded away to save a few hours: honest records, a safe path, and the right of someone weaker to disagree. Iris carried the unfinished notes toward Rosecourt, listening to the footsteps behind them and thinking of the question that morning would bring.

離開前，伊莉絲在紙頁底下寫道：今天的答案仍然可以訂正。瑟莉亞確認受決定影響的人能讀到並回應。信使傳來消息，下次會議將處理〈假面舞會〉。兩人都不確定新事件要求什麼，卻知道哪些東西不能只為節省幾小時就被交換掉：誠實紀錄、安全道路，以及弱者提出異議的權利。伊莉絲帶著未完成的筆記走向玫瑰宮，聽著背後腳步聲，想著明早會出現的問題。
