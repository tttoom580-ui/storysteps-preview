註腳裡的難民

The Refugees in the Footnote

星際移民局拒絕承認地球是人類母星 · 第 9 / 25 章 · 5000–6000 · 英中對照 · 校訂中

The first sign of trouble was not an explosion or a scream. It was a familiar procedure producing a result nobody could defend. The sealed files reveal that Earth secretly sheltered refugees from a war between older star empires. Maia checked the time, the witnesses, and the rule that authorized the action. The three did not agree. Outside, ordinary life continued, which made the error more frightening rather than less.

麻煩的第一個徵兆不是爆炸或尖叫，而是熟悉程序得出了誰也無法辯護的結果。封存文件顯示，地球曾祕密收容來自古老星際帝國戰爭的難民。瑪雅核對時間、證人與授權規則，三者卻互相矛盾。外頭的平凡生活照常進行，反而讓這個錯誤更加可怕。

A mother at the transit gate held a paper passport beside a scanner that had stopped recognizing her family. Maia could not turn a machine error into an abstract debate: missing citizenship meant missing medicine, work, and the right to travel home. Jalen copied the refusal notice before it could disappear.

轉運門前，一位母親拿著紙本護照，面對已無法辨認全家的掃描器。瑪雅無法把機器錯誤當成抽象辯論：沒有公民身分，就可能失去醫藥、工作與返家的權利。傑倫在拒絕通知消失前留下副本。

Jalen arrived with a second account that neither confirmed nor dismissed the first. They compared the routes by which each record had reached the room, then placed the uncertainties beside the facts. Maia could feel the pressure to announce a solution before anyone became frightened. That pressure was precisely why a second witness mattered: a conclusion that could not survive an independent question would be dangerous, even if it happened to be right.

傑倫帶來第二份說法，既未證實也未推翻第一份。兩人比對紀錄抵達房間的途徑，再把不確定之處和事實並列。瑪雅感受到眾人希望立即宣布解方，以免恐懼擴大的壓力。但也正因這種壓力，第二位證人才重要：無法通過獨立質問的結論，即使碰巧正確，也可能很危險。

The overlooked details in the witness file made the question harder, not easier: The public statement might alienate frightened investors, but hiding the altered seal would be worse. A black blot on the purchase order concealed the first digit of a serial number. Residents shared chili soup while waiting for the procurement hearing to begin. The committee demanded continuity in clinic service even if the trust's leadership changed. The expedition notes preserved details of daily colonial life: The cumulative delays had increased costs far beyond the original repair estimate. Maia asked the clerk to preserve the original wording rather than improve the story.

證人檔案裡被忽視的細節使問題更加複雜，而不是更簡單： 公開聲明可能疏遠害怕的投資人，但隱瞞改動封條更糟。 採購單上的黑墨漬蓋住序號第一個數字。 居民等候採購聽證開始時，分享辣椒湯。 委員會要求即使信託領導階層換人，診所服務也不能中斷。 遠征筆記也記下殖民地日常生活的細節： 累積延誤讓成本遠超原始維修估算。 瑪雅要求書記保存原本措辭，而不是把故事修飾得更好聽。

The contradiction reached the table just as the meeting was about to end. A footnote names the refugees as ancestors of people now considered the purest approved citizens. For a long moment no one spoke. Maia understood why silence felt comfortable; each answer threatened someone's settled account of how the world worked. Jalen broke the stillness by asking who would be harmed if they simply filed the discovery away. No one could answer without naming a real person.

會議即將結束時，矛盾證據送上桌面。註腳點名那些難民是如今被視為最純正合格公民者的祖先。一時之間無人開口。瑪雅明白沉默為何讓人舒服：任何答案都可能動搖某人對世界的既定理解。傑倫打破沉默，問若把發現歸檔，究竟誰會受害。沒人能在不指名真實人物的情況下回答。

A second bundle of observations brought the consequences closer to ordinary life: Yu made a decisive choice to publish the damaged-cabinet test before the vote. No worker was dispensable merely because an executive feared an embarrassing question. A visitor wore jasmine perfume that matched the scent noted by the night guard. The river lodge provided temporary rooms for families displaced during repairs. A maple tree near the entrance helped the courier identify the delivery location. Jalen marked what could be verified and left the rest open to challenge.

第二疊觀察紀錄使後果貼近普通人的生活： 陳宇果斷決定在表決前公布受損櫃體測試。 不能因主管害怕尷尬提問，就把任何工人當成可有可無。 某位訪客使用茉莉香水，與夜班警衛記下的氣味一致。 河邊旅舍為維修期間搬離的家庭提供臨時房間。 入口旁的楓樹協助快遞員辨認送貨地點。 傑倫標記能查證的部分，其餘保留給人質疑。

There were two tempting shortcuts. One would protect the reputation of Commissioner Voss and leave the public guessing. The other would expose every private detail at once and call that transparency. Maia rejected both. The issue was whether citizenship can depend on a government’s definition of human origin, not whether the team could win an argument in a single afternoon. A smaller step, supported by independent observers, could protect people while the wider dispute remained open.

有兩種誘人的捷徑：一種保住沃斯委員的名聲，讓大眾自行猜測；另一種把所有私人細節一次揭露，並稱之為透明。瑪雅兩者都拒絕。核心是公民權是否能由政府對人類起源的定義決定，不是團隊能否在一個下午贏得爭論。由獨立觀察者支持的一小步，即使爭議未結，也能先保護民眾。

The team kept the concrete observations alongside the disputed theory: The next witness was Yu, an envoy from the eastern federation whom the Empire officially called a rebel mistress of spies. In the next room, people debated how to mobilize villagers for a public meeting without provoking soldiers. Yu paused to muse over how often public trust had been treated as private property. The whistleblower's persistence finally persuaded investigators to examine the old account. One organizer identified herself as a socialist who wanted shared ownership of water stations, while another preferred independent village councils. Nothing in these records excused the people who had made the dangerous decision.

團隊把具體觀察與有爭議的理論並列保存： 下一名證人是東方聯邦使者伊絲拉；帝國官方稱她是指揮間諜的叛軍女頭目。 隔壁房裡，人們討論如何動員村民出席公開集會，又不激怒士兵。 陳宇停下來沉思，公共信任究竟有多少次被當成私人財產。 吹哨者的堅持終於讓調查員檢查舊帳戶。 一名組織者自稱社會主義者，主張水站共有；另一人則偏好獨立村議會。 紀錄中沒有任何一項能替做出危險決定的人開脫。

Maia could not turn a machine error into an abstract debate: missing citizenship meant missing medicine, work, and the right to travel home. Jalen copied the refusal notice before it could disappear.

轉運門前，一位母親拿著紙本護照，面對已無法辨認全家的掃描器。瑪雅無法把機器錯誤當成抽象辯論：沒有公民身分，就可能失去醫藥、工作與返家的權利。傑倫在拒絕通知消失前留下副本。

The record continued with small facts that would be lost in a confident speech: Employees had a chance to socialize after the hearing without being questioned by reporters. The access log covered a span of seven hours, with one unexplained gap near midnight. The author had been ordered to trek into the mountains and test the stellar collector before civilians were evacuated. A vigorous debate arose over whether the corrected map should be released immediately. Every emergency sensor needed a waterproof housing before the coming storm. Taken alone, no detail settled the case; together they showed why a public answer mattered.

紀錄接著列出那些在自信演說中容易消失的小事實： 聽證後，員工終於能不受記者盤問地社交。 門禁紀錄涵蓋七小時，其中午夜附近有一段無法解釋的空白。 作者奉命徒步進入山區，在平民撤離前測試恆星收集器。 對於是否立刻公開更正地圖，現場展開激烈辯論。 每個緊急感測器都必須在風暴來前裝上防水外殼。 單獨看來沒有一項細節能結案，合在一起卻說明了公開答覆為何重要。

The chapter ended without an easy reassurance. Maia had learned one thing that could be checked and another that still needed an answer. Jalen wrote both on the same page so that relief would not erase doubt. Their decision had moved the crisis forward, but it had also drawn the attention of Commissioner Voss. By morning, the next question would reach people far beyond this room.

這一天沒有簡單的安慰。瑪雅知道了一件能查證的事，也留下另一件尚待回答的事。傑倫把兩者寫在同一頁，免得鬆一口氣就抹掉疑問。他們的決定使危機向前，也引起沃斯委員注意。明早下一個問題將傳到房間外更遠的地方。
