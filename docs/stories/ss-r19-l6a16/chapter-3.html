失去姓名的律師

The Lawyer Who Lost His Name

星際移民局拒絕承認地球是人類母星 · 第 3 / 25 章 · 5000–6000 · 英中對照 · 校訂中

The first sign of trouble was not an explosion or a scream. It was a familiar procedure producing a result nobody could defend. Jalen, a public defender, loses his professional license because his grandparents were born on Earth. Maia checked the time, the witnesses, and the rule that authorized the action. The three did not agree. Outside, ordinary life continued, which made the error more frightening rather than less.

麻煩的第一個徵兆不是爆炸或尖叫，而是熟悉程序得出了誰也無法辯護的結果。公益律師傑倫因祖父母在地球出生而被撤銷執照。瑪雅核對時間、證人與授權規則，三者卻互相矛盾。外頭的平凡生活照常進行，反而讓這個錯誤更加可怕。

The immigration network could suspend automated services and issue legal labels, but it could not erase the physical records of history. Basic rights came from a civic charter; ancestry was evidence of a journey, not a measure of worth.

移民網路可以暫停自動服務並貼上法律標籤，卻無法抹去實體歷史紀錄。基本權利來自公民憲章；血緣只是遷徙線索，不是衡量價值的尺。

A mother at the transit gate held a paper passport beside a scanner that had stopped recognizing her family. Maia could not turn a machine error into an abstract debate: missing citizenship meant missing medicine, work, and the right to travel home. Jalen copied the refusal notice before it could disappear.

轉運門前，一位母親拿著紙本護照，面對已無法辨認全家的掃描器。瑪雅無法把機器錯誤當成抽象辯論：沒有公民身分，就可能失去醫藥、工作與返家的權利。傑倫在拒絕通知消失前留下副本。

Beyond the meeting room, people were already finding ways to survive. They traded tools, shared the location of safe passages, and argued over fair turns without waiting for permission. Maia listened longer than the schedule allowed. Jalen reminded the officials that no policy was complete until it made sense to the people expected to live with it. Their notes began with those ordinary acts of cooperation.

會議室外，人們早已開始尋求生存方法：交換工具、分享安全路線的位置，無須等候許可便爭論公平輪班。瑪雅聽得比議程允許的更久。傑倫提醒官員，政策若不能讓實際生活其中的人理解，就不算完整。他們從這些平凡合作開始記錄。

The overlooked details in the witness file made the question harder, not easier: An advisory plaque still praised the Empire's conquest of the eastern frontier, but most houses were empty. A mural celebrated royal conquest, but the community diary described families driven from their homes. Yu searched for evidence of a conspiracy rather than assume it. Someone had left a cucumber sandwich beside the abandoned recording equipment. The portable radio emitted a feeble signal from the edge of the substation. Nothing in these records excused the people who had made the dangerous decision.

證人檔案裡被忽視的細節使問題更加複雜，而不是更簡單： 告示牌仍讚揚帝國征服東部邊境，但大多數房屋已空無一人。 壁畫歌頌王室征服，社群日記卻記錄被迫離家的家庭。 陳宇沒有先認定陰謀存在，而是尋找證據。 有人在廢置錄音設備旁留下一份小黃瓜三明治。 便攜無線電從變電站邊緣發出微弱訊號。 紀錄中沒有任何一項能替做出危險決定的人開脫。

Then the next piece of evidence changed the shape of the case. He files a case in the orbital court, but its computers refuse to register him as a person entitled to speak. Maia read the statement twice, searching for the sentence that would make the contradiction disappear. There was none. Jalen did not reach for a dramatic accusation; instead, they listed three people who could verify the finding separately. The delay would be unpopular, but a dramatic discovery deserved more care, not less.

接著出現的新證據改變了整起案件的輪廓。他向軌道法庭提出訴訟，電腦卻拒絕將他登錄為有發言權的人。瑪雅把陳述讀了兩遍，試圖找出能消除矛盾的句子，卻沒有找到。傑倫沒有立刻提出轟動指控，而是列出三位可分別驗證發現的人。延遲會不受歡迎，但重大發現更需要謹慎。

A second bundle of observations brought the consequences closer to ordinary life: A firecracker exploded outside the gate, and the officers checked whether it was a signal or prank. The mechanic began to fume when his accurate warning was described as a rumor. The delivery map was stained with grease from the night-shift kitchen. A careful inspection might lessen the danger to villages still connected to the hidden pipes. An abandoned river liner stood at the dock, its passenger deck still painted with cheerful blue stars. Taken alone, no detail settled the case; together they showed why a public answer mattered.

第二疊觀察紀錄使後果貼近普通人的生活： 門外一枚爆竹炸響，官員檢查究竟是暗號還是惡作劇。 技師見自己的準確警告被說成謠言，開始氣憤。 送貨地圖沾有夜班廚房的油脂。 仔細檢查或許能減輕仍連著暗管的村莊所面臨的危險。 一艘廢棄的河上客輪停在碼頭，乘客甲板上仍畫著開朗的藍色星星。 單獨看來沒有一項細節能結案，合在一起卻說明了公開答覆為何重要。

The debate sharpened when Commissioner Voss demanded a decision before the evidence could be copied. Maia replied that urgency and secrecy were not the same thing. They established a temporary limit, published the reason for it, and scheduled an independent review. The result satisfied nobody completely. That was acceptable; the purpose of the process was to make abuse harder, not to produce applause.

當沃斯委員要求在證據複製前就做決定，辯論變得尖銳。瑪雅回應說緊急和保密不是同一回事。團隊訂立暫時限制、公開理由，並排定獨立審查。結果沒有讓任何人完全滿意，但程序的目的本來就不是得到掌聲，而是使濫權更困難。

The team kept the concrete observations alongside the disputed theory: The old orphanage had been promised funding that was never entered in its bank account. Residents compared the recurring blackouts to a plague that never seemed to end. The community requested recreational lighting for its sports field only after clinics were safe. The video editor warned that online debate had become savage toward the unknown driver. A broken swing moved in the wind beside a shrub growing through the steps. Maia asked the clerk to preserve the original wording rather than improve the story.

團隊把具體觀察與有爭議的理論並列保存： 舊孤兒院被承諾的經費從未存入銀行帳戶。 居民把反覆太陽衰減比作似乎永不結束的災難。 社區表示診所安全後，再考慮運動場休閒照明。 影像編輯警告網路討論對不明司機已變得殘酷。 破損的鞦韆在風中搖動，階梯縫裡長出灌木。 瑪雅要求書記保存原本措辭，而不是把故事修飾得更好聽。

A mother at the transit gate held a paper passport beside a scanner that had stopped recognizing her family. Maia could not turn a machine error into an abstract debate: missing citizenship meant missing medicine, work, and the right to travel home. Jalen copied the refusal notice before it could disappear.

轉運門前，一位母親拿著紙本護照，面對已無法辨認全家的掃描器。瑪雅無法把機器錯誤當成抽象辯論：沒有公民身分，就可能失去醫藥、工作與返家的權利。傑倫在拒絕通知消失前留下副本。

The record continued with small facts that would be lost in a confident speech: The technician explained that a local slang term had been mistaken for an equipment code. The second camera could surpass the first in clarity without identifying the people shown. For workers the damaged seal came to symbolize years of ignored safety warnings. A textile pattern on the borrowed coat helped establish when it had been photographed. The clinic kept its vaccine refrigerator running while the citywide evidence review continued. Jalen marked what could be verified and left the rest open to challenge.

紀錄接著列出那些在自信演說中容易消失的小事實： 技師解釋某個地方俚語被誤認成設備代碼。 第二台攝影機雖然清晰度勝過第一台，仍無法辨認人物。 對工人而言，破損封條象徵多年被忽略的安全警告。 外借大衣的紡織圖案協助確認它何時被拍攝。 全市證據審查期間，診所仍維持疫苗冰箱運作。 傑倫標記能查證的部分，其餘保留給人質疑。

A message arrived after the public doors had closed. It carried no grand declaration, just a name and a time that did not match the official schedule. Maia resisted the urge to call it proof. Jalen opened a fresh page in the journal and recorded the discrepancy exactly. They had won time, not certainty, and there were still people who might lose everything if they hurried.

公共大門關閉後，一則訊息送到。它沒有豪言壯語，只有一個名字和一個對不上官方時程的時間。瑪雅克制住把它當成證明的衝動。傑倫翻開日誌新的一頁，逐字記錄差異。他們贏得的是時間，不是確定性；若急於求成，仍有人可能失去一切。
