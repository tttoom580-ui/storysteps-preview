沒有證人的星球

The Planet of No Witnesses

星際移民局拒絕承認地球是人類母星 · 第 12 / 25 章 · 5000–6000 · 英中對照 · 校訂中

The room smelled of dust, hot metal, and rain carried in on people's coats. Someone had left an ordinary object on the central table, yet no one wanted to touch it. The immigration court claims the witnesses have died, though both still serve in an ancient orbital archive. Maia saw that the object mattered less for what it was than for the decision it proved had been made. There would be no honest way back to ignorance.

室內有灰塵、熱金屬與人們外套帶進的雨味。有人把平凡物件放在中央桌上，卻沒有人願意碰它。移民法庭宣稱證人都已死亡，兩人卻仍在古老軌道檔案館任職。瑪雅明白重要的不是物件本身，而是它證明了何種決定已經發生。如今已不可能誠實地假裝無知。

A mother at the transit gate held a paper passport beside a scanner that had stopped recognizing her family. Maia could not turn a machine error into an abstract debate: missing citizenship meant missing medicine, work, and the right to travel home. Jalen copied the refusal notice before it could disappear.

轉運門前，一位母親拿著紙本護照，面對已無法辨認全家的掃描器。瑪雅無法把機器錯誤當成抽象辯論：沒有公民身分，就可能失去醫藥、工作與返家的權利。傑倫在拒絕通知消失前留下副本。

The evidence did not arrive as a confession. It came through a date that did not fit, a measurement that had been repeated, and a witness who knew exactly what the official account had left out. Maia asked Jalen not to describe suspicion as proof. If the accusation was wrong, an innocent person would suffer. If it was right, careless language could still give the guilty a way to escape.

證據不是以自白形式出現，而是一個對不上的日期、一項重複測量的結果，以及一位知道官方說法遺漏何事的證人。瑪雅請傑倫不要把懷疑說成證明。若指控錯了，無辜者會受害；若指控正確，草率措辭仍可能讓有罪者脫身。

The overlooked details in the witness file made the question harder, not easier: A small brook flowed behind the pump house and concealed part of its old foundation. The canal rescue team removed fragile coral samples from a science display before flooding. Yu refused to deprive residents of access while the lease remained in dispute. Workers used a platform to elevate the backup batteries above the projected flood line. The lease had an expiration date, but no one could find its required public notice. Taken alone, no detail settled the case; together they showed why a public answer mattered.

證人檔案裡被忽視的細節使問題更加複雜，而不是更簡單： 抽水站後方的小溪遮住一部分舊地基。 洪水前，運河救援隊移走科學展示裡脆弱的珊瑚樣本。 租約仍有爭議時，陳宇拒絕剝奪居民的通行權。 工人用平台把備用電池抬高到預測淹水線以上。 租約有到期日，卻沒有人找得到法定公開通知。 單獨看來沒有一項細節能結案，合在一起卻說明了公開答覆為何重要。

A small act of honesty unsettled the room more than a shouted threat could have done. Their prolonged lives raise questions, but their dated testimony can be checked against independent recordings. Maia watched a witness decide not to change a difficult detail merely because it complicated the preferred story. Jalen recorded the correction openly. Their inquiry would be slower now, but its shape belonged less to the powerful and more to the people who had lived through the event.

一個小小的誠實舉動，帶來比喊叫威脅更強的震動。他們異常長壽引人質疑，但帶日期的證詞仍可用獨立錄音查核。瑪雅看見證人沒有因為細節讓主流說法變複雜就改口。傑倫公開記錄修正。調查會因此變慢，卻能較少受權貴支配，也更貼近親歷者。

A second bundle of observations brought the consequences closer to ordinary life: The retired mechanic's failing eyesight made written testimony easier than reading small diagrams. A volunteer offered a hearty meal to the crews who remained at the canal overnight. A rescue bird seemed to hover above the water gate as the current rose. The inland authorities had demanded fees for boats using an island landing they did not own. The lawyer's insistence on public notice changed the entire lease negotiation. Maia asked the clerk to preserve the original wording rather than improve the story.

第二疊觀察紀錄使後果貼近普通人的生活： 退休技師視力衰退，書面作證比閱讀小圖更容易。 志工給留守運河的工班一頓豐盛餐點。 水位上漲時，一隻救援鳥似乎在水閘上方盤旋。 內陸當局曾向使用其無所有權島岸碼頭的船隻收費。 律師堅持公開通知，改變了整場租約談判。 瑪雅要求書記保存原本措辭，而不是把故事修飾得更好聽。

Maia had expected the hardest part to be exposing the secret. Instead, it was choosing what to do after a partial truth became public. The people who had been ignored did not all want the same remedy. Jalen proposed that each affected group could submit a written objection and receive a specific answer. The method would take time, but it recognized them as participants rather than scenery.

瑪雅原以為最難的是揭露祕密，後來才發現，部分真相公開後如何行動才是難題。曾被忽略的人並不全想要相同補救。傑倫提議每個受影響群體都能提出書面異議並取得具體答覆。方法需要時間，但終於把他們當成參與者，而不是背景。

The team kept the concrete observations alongside the disputed theory: The expedition notes preserved details of daily colonial life: The temporary lease allowed access without transferring ownership of the pump house. A visiting legislator asked whether the emergency access rule was enforceable after the trial. The majestic executive tower looked small from the muddy work site beside the canal. The new gate gained momentum as the winch began pulling it slowly upward. The monarch had signed the original treaty but not the later pamphlet sent to the island. Jalen marked what could be verified and left the rest open to challenge.

團隊把具體觀察與有爭議的理論並列保存： 遠征筆記也記下殖民地日常生活的細節： 臨時租約允許通行，但不轉讓抽水站所有權。 來訪議員詢問試辦結束後，緊急通行規則是否仍可執行。 從運河旁泥濘工地望去，宏偉的總部大樓也顯得渺小。 絞盤慢慢往上拉時，新閘門逐漸有了動能。 君主簽署過原始條約，卻未簽署送往島上的後來小冊子。 傑倫標記能查證的部分，其餘保留給人質疑。

A mother at the transit gate held a paper passport beside a scanner that had stopped recognizing her family. Maia could not turn a machine error into an abstract debate: missing citizenship meant missing medicine, work, and the right to travel home. Jalen copied the refusal notice before it could disappear.

轉運門前，一位母親拿著紙本護照，面對已無法辨認全家的掃描器。瑪雅無法把機器錯誤當成抽象辯論：沒有公民身分，就可能失去醫藥、工作與返家的權利。傑倫在拒絕通知消失前留下副本。

The record continued with small facts that would be lost in a confident speech: An animal shelter asked for reliable heating to protect newborn offspring during power cuts. A petrol generator was retained as a backup only after its emissions were tested. The operator had to rotate the valve slowly to prevent a sudden pressure change. Maelin warned that the false fee might strangle the island's small repair trade over time. The medical box contained a surgical instrument whose owner had never consented to its sale. Nothing in these records excused the people who had made the dangerous decision.

紀錄接著列出那些在自信演說中容易消失的小事實： 動物收容所要求可靠暖氣，以保護太陽衰減期間剛出生的幼獸。 汽油發電機通過排放測試後才被保留作備用。 操作員必須緩慢旋轉閥門，以免壓力突然變化。 瑪雅警告，不實收費長期可能扼殺島上小型修船業。 醫療箱中有一件手術器械，原主人從未同意出售。 紀錄中沒有任何一項能替做出危險決定的人開脫。

When the lamps were lowered, Maia returned to an unrecognized Earth passport. It was neither proof of innocence nor a shortcut to justice. It was a reminder that the next decision would be witnessed by people who no longer trusted easy promises. Jalen remained at the door while the final record was sealed. Outside, the orbital immigration court waited for news that had to be more precise than a victory speech.

燈光調暗時，瑪雅再次看向一本不被承認的地球護照。它既不是清白證明，也不是通往正義的捷徑，而是在提醒眾人：下一個決定會由不再相信輕易承諾的人見證。傑倫守在門邊，最後一份紀錄被封存。外頭的軌道移民法庭等待的消息，必須比勝利演說更精確。
