變灰的護照

The Passport That Turned Gray

星際移民局拒絕承認地球是人類母星 · 第 1 / 25 章 · 5000–6000 · 英中對照 · 校訂中

用互動版查詞、朗讀與收藏

The immigration gate refused to recognize the planet beneath Maia’s feet. She placed her blue Earth passport against the scanner. The machine turned it gray and displayed a sentence she had never expected to read: EARTH IS NOT AN APPROVED HUMAN ORIGIN WORLD. Behind her, a line of travelers grew restless. Her elderly mother, who needed regular treatment, was refused medical transit at the same moment. An orderly offered a wheelchair but could not open the gate. Maia felt her mother’s hand press weakly against her bosom. The difference between a passport and a death sentence had suddenly become frighteningly small.

移民閘門拒絕承認瑪雅腳下這顆星球。她把藍色地球護照靠上掃描器，機器卻把它標成灰色，顯示一行從未想過會看見的文字：「地球並非經認可的人類發源地。」後方旅客排起長龍，越來越不耐煩。需要定期治療的年邁母親，同時被拒絕醫療轉運。勤務員推來輪椅，卻無法打開閘門。瑪雅感覺母親的手虛弱地按在胸前。護照與死刑判決的距離，突然近得令人害怕。

A federal clerk asked to abbreviate the birthplace field and choose a recognized colony instead. Maia refused to lie. She had spent twenty years working in the ancestral migration archive; she could recognize an altered registry even when it came from the highest authority. The new directive was supposedly applicable to every family with Earth ancestry. A warrant authorized inspectors to seize unrecognized travel papers. Yet its date preceded the meeting in which the law had allegedly been approved. Her colleague Jalen, a public defender, made a comparative copy before anyone could close the file.

聯邦書記要她縮短出生地欄位，改選一顆被承認的殖民行星。瑪雅拒絕說謊。她在祖源遷徙檔案室工作二十年，即使被修改的登記簿出自最高機關，她也認得。新指令據稱適用於所有具有地球祖源的家庭，令狀授權檢查官沒收未獲承認的旅行證件。但文件日期竟比據稱通過法律的會議還早。公益律師傑倫趁檔案尚未關閉前留下比較副本。

At the transfer station, emergency volunteers were broiling fish beside a portable heat panel. A widow with a limp tried to keep her granddaughter warm. The relief table offered coats, medication, and basic underwear, including a few bras for travelers whose luggage had been confiscated. Across the hall, a freight anchor held a stalled cargo ship against the dock. The vessel carried food to Earth but could not unload because its destination was no longer recognized. The supply was finite. No one had invented new hunger; an administrative decision had simply turned existing resources into something their owners could not reach.

轉運站裡，緊急志工在攜帶式加熱板旁炙烤魚。一名跛腳寡婦努力讓孫女保持溫暖。救濟桌提供外套、藥物與基本內衣，還有幾件胸罩給行李遭沒收的旅客。大廳另一側，貨輪的船錨把停擺貨船固定在碼頭。船上載著送往地球的食物，卻因目的地不再受承認而無法卸貨。供應有限。沒有人發明新的飢餓，只是一道行政決定讓現有資源變成原主人無法觸及的東西。

At the financial office, a teller reported that a gangster had offered him a bribe to sell newly stateless families’ property before any appeal could be heard. His eyelid shook as he identified the buyer’s account. Maia asked him to narrate the transaction, not speculate about its purpose. She had no interest in winning a dramatic accusation that would collapse under scrutiny. Jalen followed the payment route to a junction in the orbital trade registry, where the same private company had bid on hospitals and farms. A label that denied Earth’s history was already making someone rich.

金融辦公室的出納說，黑幫分子曾向他行賄，要求在上訴尚未審理前就賣掉新近失去國籍家庭的財產。他辨認買家帳戶時眼皮發抖。瑪雅請他敘述交易，不要猜測目的。她不想用經不起查核的轟動指控獲勝。傑倫順著付款路線查到軌道貿易登錄的一個交會節點，發現同一家私人公司正在競標醫院與農場。否認地球歷史的標籤，已經開始替某些人賺錢。

Jalen produced an old charter printed on virgin paper kept inside an oxygen-free case. The signatures appeared to authorize a temporary suspension of Earth’s origin status during an ancient refugee war. Maia examined the dates. One signer’s title was older than the federation; another had supposedly signed before humanity learned to cross the stars. “A signature is not a person,” she said. “And an archive entry is not permission to erase everyone descended from a planet.” Jalen asked what she could prove in the remaining seventy-two hours. “Not everything,” she answered. “But enough to stop them closing the court.”

傑倫拿出存放在無氧匣內、印於全新紙張上的舊憲章。簽名似乎授權在古代難民戰爭期間暫時停止承認地球發源地身分。瑪雅核對日期。一名簽署者的職稱比聯邦還古老；另一人據稱在人類學會星際航行前就簽名。「簽名不是一個人，」她說，「檔案條目也不是抹除某顆行星所有後裔的許可。」傑倫問她能在剩下七十二小時證明多少。「不是全部，」她回答，「但足夠阻止他們關閉法庭。」

The orbital court accepted their emergency application, but only after Maia stated the claim with painful precision: the case was not about whether Earth deserved to flourish more than other worlds. It was about whether anyone’s existing rights could vanish because a government changed a definition. Outside, the automated sign counted down sixty-nine hours. Maia turned to her mother, still waiting behind the gate. Then the old passport began to print a second line beneath the rejection notice. It was a message buried in the original firmware: IF EARTH IS NOT HOME, ASK WHO TAUGHT THE STARS TO LEAVE. Maia folded the paper beside her mother’s medicine card and promised that the appeal would name every family whose rights the scanner had refused.

軌道法庭終於接受緊急申請，但瑪雅必須痛苦而精準地陳述：案件不是地球是否比其他世界更值得繁榮，而是政府改變定義時，任何人原有的權利能否隨之消失。門外自動看板倒數至六十九小時。瑪雅轉向仍在閘門後等待的母親。這時舊護照竟在拒絕通知下方印出第二行字，那是埋在原始韌體裡的訊息：「如果地球不是家，就問問是誰教會星辰離開。」瑪雅把紙張與母親的藥卡折在一起，承諾上訴會記下每個遭掃描器剝奪權利的家庭姓名。
