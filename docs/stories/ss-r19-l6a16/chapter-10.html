否認發源的宣言

The Declaration of Non-Origin

星際移民局拒絕承認地球是人類母星 · 第 10 / 25 章 · 5000–6000 · 英中對照 · 校訂中

用互動版查詞、朗讀與收藏

Morning brought a decision that had already been made for everyone. There was a signature at the bottom of the order, but no space for those most affected to answer. Maia discovers that an Earth council once signed a declaration denying its status as a human origin world. Maia knew that refusing a bad choice was not the same as knowing the correct one. Before making a promise, the team needed something they could test.

清晨帶來一項替所有人做好的決定。命令底下有簽名，卻沒有讓受影響最深者回答的地方。瑪雅發現地球議會曾簽署宣言，否認自身為人類發源地。瑪雅知道拒絕壞選項不等於已經知道正確答案。作出承諾前，團隊需要能夠驗證的事物。

The sealed declaration lay between Maia and Commissioner Voss beneath the orbital court's cold light. Its headline said Earth had denied being humanity's origin. Voss had quoted those words in every broadcast. Maia turned the sheet over and found the expiration clause, written in an older hand: this designation would remain only until the refugee ships were safe. The document was not an act of shame. It was a shield meant to stop an ancient empire from locating displaced families. Voss had used that temporary shield as a permanent weapon against their descendants. Behind Maia, a refugee witness recognized her ancestor's signature and began to cry. Maia asked the judge to read the reverse side before ruling on a single word of the front.

封存宣言躺在瑪雅與沃斯委員之間，軌道法庭的冷光照著文件。標題說地球曾否認自己是人類起源地，沃斯在每次廣播裡都引用這幾個字。瑪雅翻到背面，發現另一種更古老筆跡寫的到期條款：此指定只維持到難民船安全為止。那不是羞恥宣言，而是保護流亡家庭免遭古老帝國追蹤的盾牌。沃斯卻把暫時盾牌當成永久武器，對付難民的後裔。瑪雅身後，一名難民證人認出祖先簽名，哭了起來。她請法官在裁定正面任何一個字之前，先讀完背面。

The overlooked details in the witness file made the question harder, not easier: The supplier sought a concession that would grant it control over the canal for a decade. A cynical investor said patients were useful only when their stories moved the market. The old procurement rule treated silence as consent by default, which the new board rejected. An emergency fund should not deplete the clinic budget merely to improve quarterly profit. Soldiers handed out sweet buns, and the fragrance of crushed flowers covered the smell of oil along the river. Jalen marked what could be verified and left the rest open to challenge.

證人檔案裡被忽視的細節使問題更加複雜，而不是更簡單： 供應商尋求特許權，想控制運河十年。 一位憤世嫉俗的投資人說，病人故事只有能推動市場時才有用。 舊採購規則預設沉默即同意，新董事會予以否決。 緊急基金不該只為改善季度利潤就耗盡診所預算。 士兵發放甜麵包，壓碎花朵的香氣蓋住河邊油味。 傑倫標記能查證的部分，其餘保留給人質疑。

What changed the discussion was a fact that should have been obvious from the start. The original document makes clear this was a temporary shield for refugees, not a permanent surrender of citizens' rights. Maia felt the old temptation to turn that fact into an immediate victory. Instead, the team separated what the new evidence established from what it merely suggested. That distinction frustrated the loudest spectators, but it helped the witnesses remain willing to speak again.

改變討論的是一件本來就該看得見的事實。原件清楚寫明，那只是保護難民的臨時安排，並非永久放棄公民權。瑪雅一瞬間也想把它當成立刻勝利的證明，團隊卻把新證據真正確立的部分與僅能推測的部分分開。這讓最喧鬧的旁觀者失望，卻使證人願意再次出面。

A second bundle of observations brought the consequences closer to ordinary life: No official would be permitted to intrude on protected witness meetings without a court order. A former jockey now driving delivery vans recognized the unusual generator model. The clerk recorded the literal wording of the ownership claim without adding her opinion. The chancellor's lawyer made a sharp retort about national morale, claiming that questioning the stellar collector would deplete public confidence. The court could prosecute fraud only after evidence met the required standard. Nothing in these records excused the people who had made the dangerous decision.

第二疊觀察紀錄使後果貼近普通人的生活： 沒有法院命令，任何官員都不得闖入受保護的證人會面。 一名曾當賽馬騎師的廂車司機認出了不尋常的發電機型號。 書記逐字記下所有權主張，沒有加入個人意見。 宰相律師尖銳反駁，談起國民士氣，聲稱質疑恆星收集器會耗盡公眾信心。 證據達到法定標準後，法庭才能起訴詐欺。 紀錄中沒有任何一項能替做出危險決定的人開脫。

No one in the room was permitted to declare that a good intention canceled a harmful action. Maia insisted on recording the error, identifying who had authorized it, and protecting those affected before assigning wider blame. Jalen drew a simple boundary around what they could do tonight. The line looked disappointingly modest, yet it was the first promise that did not require another person to surrender a right.

室內沒有人能宣稱善意可以抵銷傷害。瑪雅堅持記錄錯誤、確認授權者，並在追究更大責任前保護受害者。傑倫畫出今晚能做到的清楚界線。這條線看似微不足道，卻是第一個不要求別人放棄權利的承諾。

The team kept the concrete observations alongside the disputed theory: The maintenance inspector checked for radioactive materials because an old sensor carried a misleading label. The chancellor's lawyer made a sharp retort about national morale, claiming that questioning the colony reactor would deplete public confidence. A young courier came forward with a screwdriver and a box of broken clock parts. Outside, an elderly vendor offered each departing witness a serving of warm soup. The tower's splendor seemed irrelevant beside the dark apartments across the river. Taken alone, no detail settled the case; together they showed why a public answer mattered.

團隊把具體觀察與有爭議的理論並列保存： 維修檢查員因舊感測器標示誤導而檢查有無放射性物質。 宰相律師尖銳反駁，談起國民士氣，聲稱質疑殖民反應爐會耗盡公眾信心。 一名年輕信差帶著螺絲起子和一箱壞時鐘零件上前。 庭外，一名年長攤販給離開的每位證人一份熱湯。 相較河對岸漆黑的公寓，大樓的華麗顯得毫無意義。 單獨看來沒有一項細節能結案，合在一起卻說明了公開答覆為何重要。

A mother at the transit gate held a paper passport beside a scanner that had stopped recognizing her family. Maia could not turn a machine error into an abstract debate: missing citizenship meant missing medicine, work, and the right to travel home. Jalen copied the refusal notice before it could disappear.

轉運門前，一位母親拿著紙本護照，面對已無法辨認全家的掃描器。瑪雅無法把機器錯誤當成抽象辯論：沒有公民身分，就可能失去醫藥、工作與返家的權利。傑倫在拒絕通知消失前留下副本。

The record continued with small facts that would be lost in a confident speech: A spontaneous murmur passed through the audience. A witness explained that her stepfather had worked on the first version of the old substation. The nervous clerk began to stutter before the judge invited her to speak slowly. The city agreed to subsidize emergency batteries for low-income patients during the trial. The military had claimed its newest heat reservoirs contained harmless synthetic stone. Maia asked the clerk to preserve the original wording rather than improve the story.

紀錄接著列出那些在自信演說中容易消失的小事實： 旁聽席傳出自發的低語。 一名證人解釋繼父曾參與舊變電站的初版工程。 緊張的書記開始口吃，法官請她慢慢說。 市府同意在試辦期間補助低收入病人的緊急電池。 軍方聲稱最新儲能庫採用無害的合成石材；學院在丟棄的隔熱材料中發現少量放射性礦物痕跡，但含量不足以確認疾病原因。 瑪雅要求書記保存原本措辭，而不是把故事修飾得更好聽。

On the way out, Maia passed people waiting for a result that would affect their meals, their homes, or their ability to say no. None of them had the luxury of treating the dispute as an intellectual puzzle. Jalen paused to answer a child's practical question before they left. The answer was incomplete, but unlike the old official notice, it admitted the uncertainty out loud.

離開時，瑪雅經過等待結果的人群，這項結果將影響他們的餐食、家園或說不的能力。他們不能奢侈地把爭論當成智力謎題。傑倫停下回答一名孩子實際的問題。答案並不完整，卻與舊公告不同：它坦白承認不確定之處。
