A Clause Without an End

By noon, rumors had outrun the facts. Some people wanted a villain; others wanted a hero who could end the argument without asking another question. Voss invokes the old declaration as grounds for mass disenfranchisement, ignoring its expiration clause. Maia had worked long enough to distrust both shortcuts. In the orbital immigration court, the first task was to protect the evidence before anyone could turn it into a weapon.

到了中午，謠言早已跑得比事實快。有人想找反派，也有人想找英雄，好在不再追問的情況下結束爭論。沃斯援引舊宣言剝奪群眾公民權，卻忽略其中的到期條款。瑪雅工作多年，已經不相信這兩種捷徑。在軌道移民法庭，當務之急是先保護證據，免得任何人將它變成武器。

A mother at the transit gate held a paper passport beside a scanner that had stopped recognizing her family. Maia could not turn a machine error into an abstract debate: missing citizenship meant missing medicine, work, and the right to travel home. Jalen copied the refusal notice before it could disappear.

轉運門前，一位母親拿著紙本護照，面對已無法辨認全家的掃描器。瑪雅無法把機器錯誤當成抽象辯論：沒有公民身分，就可能失去醫藥、工作與返家的權利。傑倫在拒絕通知消失前留下副本。

A witness asked whether the inquiry would make tomorrow safer. Maia could not promise that. The truth could establish responsibility, but it would not repair a broken machine, feed a hungry child, or return lost time on its own. Jalen suggested a temporary protection that could be checked after sunrise. They wrote down its limits in plain language and invited the people affected to challenge them.

一位證人問調查是否能讓明天更安全，瑪雅無法保證。真相能確立責任，卻不會自行修好機器、餵飽孩子或歸還失去的時間。傑倫提出一項日出後可以檢驗的暫時保護措施。他們用白話寫清限制，邀請受影響的人提出異議。

The overlooked details in the witness file made the question harder, not easier: Yu brought a thermometer calibrated in Celsius and a probe designed to measure changes in river heat. Excessive friction inside the relay produced the unusual vibration recorded during the test. A stray dog began to growl when inspectors approached the abandoned loading dock. The lawyer said a forged instruction could induce workers to override a safe setting. The fishermen who inhabit these shores would need a permanent place on the safety board. Nothing in these records excused the people who had made the dangerous decision.

證人檔案裡被忽視的細節使問題更加複雜，而不是更簡單： 瑪雅帶來以攝氏度校準的溫度計，以及測量河水熱能變化的探針。 繼電器內過度摩擦造成測試中記錄的異常震動。 檢查員靠近廢置裝貨區時，一隻流浪狗開始低吼。 律師說偽造指令可能誘使工人覆寫安全設定。 居住在這片海岸的漁民需要在安全委員會中擁有永久席次。 紀錄中沒有任何一項能替做出危險決定的人開脫。

For once the numbers did not merely decorate a report; they contradicted the order that had been issued in their name. Maia finds two original witnesses who can testify about the treaty's limited purpose. Jalen asked whether a mistake could explain the result. Maia agreed to test that possibility too. They checked an independent source, marked every assumption, and made copies before the originals could be transferred to an office beyond public reach.

這一次數字不只是報告裝飾，而是直接反駁以數字名義發布的命令。瑪雅找到兩位原始證人，能說明條約有限的目的。傑倫詢問是否可能只是出錯，瑪雅也同意檢查這種可能。他們核對獨立來源、標記每項假設，在原件被移往公眾無法接觸的辦公室前留下副本。

A second bundle of observations brought the consequences closer to ordinary life: It was ironic that the trust's security account had been active long after its owner died. A retired mechanic lived on a grassy mound near the station. A peacock from a nearby garden wandered close to the temporary generator fence. A pianist played for patients while the clinic's backup system was tested. Amira brought a thermometer calibrated in Celsius and a probe designed to measure changes in river heat. Taken alone, no detail settled the case; together they showed why a public answer mattered.

第二疊觀察紀錄使後果貼近普通人的生活： 諷刺的是，信託保全帳戶在原持有人死後很久仍活躍。 一名退休技師住在測站附近的草丘上。 附近花園的一隻孔雀走近臨時發電機圍欄。 診所測試備電系統時，一位鋼琴家為病人演奏。 瑪雅帶來以攝氏度校準的溫度計，以及測量河水熱能變化的探針。 單獨看來沒有一項細節能結案，合在一起卻說明了公開答覆為何重要。

The debate sharpened when Commissioner Voss demanded a decision before the evidence could be copied. Maia replied that urgency and secrecy were not the same thing. They established a temporary limit, published the reason for it, and scheduled an independent review. The result satisfied nobody completely. That was acceptable; the purpose of the process was to make abuse harder, not to produce applause.

當沃斯委員要求在證據複製前就做決定，辯論變得尖銳。瑪雅回應說緊急和保密不是同一回事。團隊訂立暫時限制、公開理由，並排定獨立審查。結果沒有讓任何人完全滿意，但程序的目的本來就不是得到掌聲，而是使濫權更困難。

The team kept the concrete observations alongside the disputed theory: The respective duties of the city and company had been confused in the old emergency plan. Yu admitted that her previous shortsighted policy had made a second approval optional. The nurse asked the engineers to simplify the battery instructions for home patients. A vessel crossing the strait was delayed by the same storm approaching the city. The superficial inspection had missed heat damage deep inside the relay cabinet. Maia asked the clerk to preserve the original wording rather than improve the story.

團隊把具體觀察與有爭議的理論並列保存： 舊緊急計畫混淆了市府與公司的各自責任。 陳宇承認過去短視的政策使第二道核准變成可選。 護士請工程師簡化居家病人的電池使用說明。 一艘穿越海峽的船因同場暴風雨而延誤。 表面的檢查漏掉了繼電器櫃內部深處的熱損傷。 瑪雅要求書記保存原本措辭，而不是把故事修飾得更好聽。

A mother at the transit gate held a paper passport beside a scanner that had stopped recognizing her family. Maia could not turn a machine error into an abstract debate: missing citizenship meant missing medicine, work, and the right to travel home. Jalen copied the refusal notice before it could disappear.

轉運門前，一位母親拿著紙本護照，面對已無法辨認全家的掃描器。瑪雅無法把機器錯誤當成抽象辯論：沒有公民身分，就可能失去醫藥、工作與返家的權利。傑倫在拒絕通知消失前留下副本。

The record continued with small facts that would be lost in a confident speech: No official could suppress a valid correction merely because it embarrassed the palace. The symmetry of the instruments mattered because each team had to measure the same change independently. An expired access token had been used to send the dormant account's last message. The public committee tried to unify the three separate emergency notification systems. The nurse emphasized the urgency of restoring the clinic's refrigerated medical supply. Jalen marked what could be verified and left the rest open to challenge.

紀錄接著列出那些在自信演說中容易消失的小事實： 官員不能只因更正令王室難堪便壓下有效資料。 儀器的對稱配置很重要，因為每組人都必須獨立測量同一項變化。 最後一封休眠帳戶訊息使用了一枚過期存取憑證。 公共委員會試圖整合三套分離的緊急通知系統。 護士強調恢復診所冷藏醫療用品供應的急迫性。 傑倫標記能查證的部分，其餘保留給人質疑。

A message arrived after the public doors had closed. It carried no grand declaration, just a name and a time that did not match the official schedule. Maia resisted the urge to call it proof. Jalen opened a fresh page in the journal and recorded the discrepancy exactly. They had won time, not certainty, and there were still people who might lose everything if they hurried.

公共大門關閉後，一則訊息送到。它沒有豪言壯語，只有一個名字和一個對不上官方時程的時間。瑪雅克制住把它當成證明的衝動。傑倫翻開日誌新的一頁，逐字記錄差異。他們贏得的是時間，不是確定性；若急於求成，仍有人可能失去一切。
