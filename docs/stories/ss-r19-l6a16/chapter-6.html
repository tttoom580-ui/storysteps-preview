火星的異議

An Objection from Mars

星際移民局拒絕承認地球是人類母星 · 第 6 / 25 章 · 5000–6000 · 英中對照 · 校訂中

The room smelled of dust, hot metal, and rain carried in on people's coats. Someone had left an ordinary object on the central table, yet no one wanted to touch it. Martian citizens organize a hearing because the new Earth ruling threatens families spread across two planets. Maia saw that the object mattered less for what it was than for the decision it proved had been made. There would be no honest way back to ignorance.

室內有灰塵、熱金屬與人們外套帶進的雨味。有人把平凡物件放在中央桌上，卻沒有人願意碰它。火星公民組織聽證，因為新裁定威脅跨兩顆星球生活的家庭。瑪雅明白重要的不是物件本身，而是它證明了何種決定已經發生。如今已不可能誠實地假裝無知。

A mother at the transit gate held a paper passport beside a scanner that had stopped recognizing her family. Maia could not turn a machine error into an abstract debate: missing citizenship meant missing medicine, work, and the right to travel home. Jalen copied the refusal notice before it could disappear.

轉運門前，一位母親拿著紙本護照，面對已無法辨認全家的掃描器。瑪雅無法把機器錯誤當成抽象辯論：沒有公民身分，就可能失去醫藥、工作與返家的權利。傑倫在拒絕通知消失前留下副本。

A witness asked whether the inquiry would make tomorrow safer. Maia could not promise that. The truth could establish responsibility, but it would not repair a broken machine, feed a hungry child, or return lost time on its own. Jalen suggested a temporary protection that could be checked after sunrise. They wrote down its limits in plain language and invited the people affected to challenge them.

一位證人問調查是否能讓明天更安全，瑪雅無法保證。真相能確立責任，卻不會自行修好機器、餵飽孩子或歸還失去的時間。傑倫提出一項日出後可以檢驗的暫時保護措施。他們用白話寫清限制，邀請受影響的人提出異議。

The overlooked details in the witness file made the question harder, not easier: Yu continued to allege that the entire investigation was a publicity stunt. The contractor had promised to beautify the riverside warehouse before its safety inspection. Maia corrected a caption that wrongly described the marsh canoe as royal property. The contract dispute reached its climax when the two shipping weights appeared together. Yu asked the auditor to extract the original invoice data from the server. Jalen marked what could be verified and left the rest open to challenge.

證人檔案裡被忽視的細節使問題更加複雜，而不是更簡單： 陳宇繼續聲稱整場調查只是公關表演。 承包商曾承諾在安檢前美化河岸倉庫。 瑪拉更正一則錯把沼地獨木舟稱為王室財產的說明。 當兩張出貨重量表一起出現時，契約爭議達到高潮。 陳宇要求稽核員從伺服器擷取原始發票資料。 傑倫標記能查證的部分，其餘保留給人質疑。

For once the numbers did not merely decorate a report; they contradicted the order that had been issued in their name. The court tries to separate relatives into legally human and legally unclassified categories. Jalen asked whether a mistake could explain the result. Maia agreed to test that possibility too. They checked an independent source, marked every assumption, and made copies before the originals could be transferred to an office beyond public reach.

這一次數字不只是報告裝飾，而是直接反駁以數字名義發布的命令。法庭竟試圖把親人分成法律上的人類與未分類者。傑倫詢問是否可能只是出錯，瑪雅也同意檢查這種可能。他們核對獨立來源、標記每項假設，在原件被移往公眾無法接觸的辦公室前留下副本。

A second bundle of observations brought the consequences closer to ordinary life: An extracurricular engineering club helped sort community reports without handling confidential files. Their teacher was hospitable, offering water and a shady bench to witnesses who had traveled far. The usually jolly driver grew quiet when he recognized the private auction seal. Workers offered to mow the neglected path so elderly witnesses could reach the hall. A supervisor with a white mustache signed the original warehouse inventory. Nothing in these records excused the people who had made the dangerous decision.

第二疊觀察紀錄使後果貼近普通人的生活： 一個課外工程社團協助分類社區通報，但不碰機密檔案。 他們的老師十分好客，為遠道而來的證人準備飲水與陰涼長椅。 平日開朗的車夫認出私人拍賣印章時沉默了。 工人提議修剪荒廢小徑，方便年長證人到會堂。 一名留白鬍子的主管簽署了倉庫原始清冊。 紀錄中沒有任何一項能替做出危險決定的人開脫。

No one in the room was permitted to declare that a good intention canceled a harmful action. Maia insisted on recording the error, identifying who had authorized it, and protecting those affected before assigning wider blame. Jalen drew a simple boundary around what they could do tonight. The line looked disappointingly modest, yet it was the first promise that did not require another person to surrender a right.

室內沒有人能宣稱善意可以抵銷傷害。瑪雅堅持記錄錯誤、確認授權者，並在追究更大責任前保護受害者。傑倫畫出今晚能做到的清楚界線。這條線看似微不足道，卻是第一個不要求別人放棄權利的承諾。

The team kept the concrete observations alongside the disputed theory: A grandmother told him to stop nagging and gave him an ounce of sweet tea. The old cafeteria became an oasis for night workers exhausted by the investigation. One ounce of damaged insulation was sealed for laboratory comparison with the other samples. The missing battery modules might have been stored on the outskirts of the city. A visiting parliament observer requested that the public hearing remain open to workers. Taken alone, no detail settled the case; together they showed why a public answer mattered.

團隊把具體觀察與有爭議的理論並列保存： 一位祖母要他別再嘮叨，遞給他一小杯甜茶。 舊餐廳成為調查期間疲憊夜班工人的休息綠洲。 一盎司受損絕緣材料被封存，供實驗室與其他樣本比對。 失蹤的電池模組可能存放在城市郊區。 一名國會觀察員要求公開聽證繼續向工人開放。 單獨看來沒有一項細節能結案，合在一起卻說明了公開答覆為何重要。

A mother at the transit gate held a paper passport beside a scanner that had stopped recognizing her family. Maia could not turn a machine error into an abstract debate: missing citizenship meant missing medicine, work, and the right to travel home. Jalen copied the refusal notice before it could disappear.

轉運門前，一位母親拿著紙本護照，面對已無法辨認全家的掃描器。瑪雅無法把機器錯誤當成抽象辯論：沒有公民身分，就可能失去醫藥、工作與返家的權利。傑倫在拒絕通知消失前留下副本。

The record continued with small facts that would be lost in a confident speech: The nobleman's methods were ruthless but often lawful on paper: he delayed funding, demanded impossible permits, and threatened public employees with dismissal. A local sculptor offered a warehouse sketch that showed the original side entrance. The repair team preferred simplicity in emergency instructions over impressive technical language. Simultaneous inspections at both gates revealed that one crate could not have traveled as claimed. Public confidence fell into a slump when the palace refused to explain the disputed caption. Maia asked the clerk to preserve the original wording rather than improve the story.

紀錄接著列出那些在自信演說中容易消失的小事實： 這名貴族的方法冷酷，但在紙面上常常合法：延後經費、索取不可能取得的許可，並以解雇威脅公務員。 一名在地雕塑家提供倉庫素描，顯示原有側門。 維修團隊偏好簡單的緊急指示，而非炫目的技術詞。 兩處大門同時檢查，顯示一只木箱不可能按所稱路線運送。 王宮拒絕解釋爭議圖說，公眾信心因此陷入低潮。 瑪雅要求書記保存原本措辭，而不是把故事修飾得更好聽。

On the way out, Maia passed people waiting for a result that would affect their meals, their homes, or their ability to say no. None of them had the luxury of treating the dispute as an intellectual puzzle. Jalen paused to answer a child's practical question before they left. The answer was incomplete, but unlike the old official notice, it admitted the uncertainty out loud.

離開時，瑪雅經過等待結果的人群，這項結果將影響他們的餐食、家園或說不的能力。他們不能奢侈地把爭論當成智力謎題。傑倫停下回答一名孩子實際的問題。答案並不完整，卻與舊公告不同：它坦白承認不確定之處。
