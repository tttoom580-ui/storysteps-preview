菜單背後的地圖

The Map Behind the Menu

退房之前，請把真話說完 · 第 6 / 25 章 · 5000–6000 · 英中對照 · 校訂完成

Two weeks after Iris returned, the Lantern House hosted its first community workshop, not as a hotel but as a place to examine the old coastal path. Jules brought maps, Noah brought inspection photographs, and May served vegetable curry from the kitchen. Steam rose in pale vapor above the bowls. Iris insisted that any public claim be airtight: a dated source, a named keeper, and a clear explanation of what the evidence could not prove. Some visitors despised such caution. They wanted a dramatic announcement that would slay the developer's proposal in a single afternoon. Iris knew better. A persuasive story might attract attention, but attention was not the same as attaining a durable result.

以澄返鄉兩週後，燈屋旅館舉辦第一場社區工作坊。這次旅館不是提供住宿，而是讓大家一起研究舊海岸步道。朱爾帶來地圖，柏舟帶來檢查照片，美姨從廚房端出蔬菜咖哩，淡淡蒸氣從碗中升起。以澄堅持，任何公開主張都必須滴水不漏：有日期可查、有保管人可問，也要清楚說明證據不能證明什麼。某些訪客厭惡這種謹慎。他們想要一場能在下午就徹底擊敗開發案的戲劇性宣告。以澄明白，動人的故事也許吸引目光，但目光不等於長久的成果。

An elderly fisherman displayed a wooden fin from a ceremonial boat. He remembered an evergreen tree once marking the entrance to the public stairs. A flake of blue paint on the boat matched the faded rail in Jules' photograph, although nobody could establish when either surface had been repainted. The fisherman said following the coast had been his father's favorite pastime. After the flood, fences made residents roam inland to reach the harbor. This detour affected merchants, schoolchildren, and elderly neighbors, not merely tourists. Iris wrote down their experiences without promising that each memory would survive formal scrutiny. Their testimony mattered even where measurements were still missing.

一位年長漁夫展示祭典小船上的木製魚鰭裝飾。他記得公共階梯入口以前有一棵常綠樹。船上一片藍色漆屑，與朱爾照片裡褪色的欄杆顏色相近，但沒有人能確定兩處漆面何時重新上色。漁夫說，沿岸散步曾是他父親最喜愛的消遣。洪水之後，圍籬迫使居民繞到內陸才能抵達港口。這條遠路影響商人、學童與年長鄰居，並非只有遊客受影響。以澄記下他們的經驗，卻沒有保證每段記憶都能通過正式審查。即使測量資料仍缺漏，他們的證言依舊重要。

The local congresswoman sent an aide to observe. The aide offered help applying for a heritage grant but warned that government support might become entangled with publicity. Iris accepted the information without agreeing to an endorsement. She held the town's volunteers in high esteem and would not turn their work into free merchandise for a politician. Noah suggested a simple display to explain which routes remained accessible. Jules noticed redundancy in the old evacuation signs: two arrows pointed toward the same blocked stair while none indicated the safer inland lane. That design flaw had consequences during the flood. It also provided a question that engineers could test.

地方國會議員派助理旁聽。助理表示可協助申請文化保存補助，卻也提醒政府支持可能牽涉宣傳。以澄接受資訊，沒有同意背書。她很敬重鎮上的志工，不願把他們的勞動變成政治人物的免費宣傳商品。柏舟建議製作簡單展示，說明哪些道路仍可通行。朱爾注意到舊避難標誌有重複設計的問題：兩支箭頭都指向同一條封閉階梯，卻沒有標示較安全的內陸小路。這項設計缺陷曾在洪水時造成影響，也提出一個工程師可以檢驗的問題。

After lunch, Hana separated edible leftovers from rubbish and asked guests to wash their own cups. A child complained that learning about maps was monotonous. Mr. Ho rescued the mood by producing a storybook from the old library. It told of sailors who watched an eclipse and thought the sea had swallowed the sun. In the final illustration, villagers did not defeat a monster; they built a lighthouse together. The child asked whether the lighthouse in the hotel logo was the same one. May said the resemblance was intentional, but the old emblem belonged to the whole coastline. The room fell quiet as people considered what symbols could include or exclude.

午餐後，韓娜把可食用的剩菜與垃圾分開，請來賓自己洗杯子。一名孩子抱怨，學地圖實在單調乏味。何伯拿出舊圖書館的一本故事書替大家解圍。書裡的水手見到日食，以為太陽被大海吞沒。最後一幅插圖中，村民沒有擊敗怪物，而是合力建起燈塔。孩子問，旅館標誌上的燈塔是否就是那座。美姨說兩者確實刻意相似，但那個舊徽記屬於整段海岸。眾人安靜下來，思考一個象徵究竟能包容誰，又可能排除誰。

Noah walked Iris to the damaged gate after the workshop. He said his father had been accused of cutting the safety signs, yet the surviving photographs showed newer screws than those used in his father's maintenance period. Iris asked whether the conclusion could be verified. Noah smiled sadly. “You haven't changed the part of yourself that demands a proper question.” She wanted to say that she had changed everything else, but the answer felt too dramatic. They examined the metal plate under a streetlamp. A sly alteration was possible, but possibility did not identify a culprit. Iris photographed the screws and added them to the evidence log.

工作坊結束後，柏舟陪以澄走向受損的閘門。他說父親曾被控拆除安全指標，但現存照片裡的螺絲，比父親維修時期使用的款式更新。以澄問能否驗證。柏舟帶著一點難過微笑：「妳要求好問題的那一面，倒是沒變。」她很想說，除此之外，自己其他一切都變了，但這回答太戲劇化。他們在路燈下查看金屬板。確實有可能有人狡猾地動了手腳，但可能性不代表知道是誰。以澄拍下螺絲，把它們加入證據紀錄。

Before bed, Iris wrote a summary for the trustees. The grant might help with repairs; the public route needed professional surveying; and the kitchen had served a successful trial meal without wasting food. These were modest accomplishments, yet they broke the monotony of meetings that produced only accusations. She ended with a budget table and a request for permission to commission an independent boundary survey. May brought her a final cup of tea and asked whether she was glad to have come home. Iris considered the word home. “I am glad I came to ask the question,” she said. “I haven't decided the answer.”

睡前，以澄寫好給受託人的摘要：補助可能協助修繕，公共通道需要專業測量，而廚房成功供應了一餐試營運，沒有浪費食物。這些成果並不轟烈，卻打破只會互相指責的會議單調循環。她附上預算表，申請委託獨立界址測量。美姨端來最後一杯茶，問她是不是很高興回家。以澄想了想「家」這個字。「我很高興回來提出問題，」她說，「但還沒決定答案。」
