A Receipt with Two Dates

退房之前，請把真話說完 · 第 3 / 25 章 · 5000–6000 · 英中對照

用互動版查詞、朗讀與收藏

Iris spent the third morning at the municipal market, searching for a cashier who had once supplied linens to the Lantern House. An elderly vendor recognized Yun's photograph and produced a box of receipts that smelled of salt. A truck thundered past the curb, loud enough to deafen their conversation for several seconds. The vendor laughed and called herself “madam of the missing paperwork,” then offered Iris a chair. She had sold sheets to the hotel for thirty years, even after the supplier's daughter chose to emigrate. Her loyalty was not a lifelong contract, she explained. Yun had always paid promptly and never treated the workers as disposable.

第三天上午，以澄到市立市場尋找一位曾替燈屋旅館供應床單的收銀員。一位年長攤販認出雲奶奶的照片，拿出一盒帶著鹽味的收據。一輛卡車從路緣疾駛而過，轟隆聲大得讓兩人好幾秒無法交談。攤販笑著自稱「遺失文件的夫人」，又搬椅子給以澄。她替旅館供應床單已有三十年，就算她的女兒決定移民海外，生意也沒停。她說，這份忠誠不是終身契約；只是雲奶奶總準時付款，也從不把員工當成用過即丟的東西。

Back at the hotel, Hana returned from a medical checkup. The skin irritation was minor, but the clinic advised replacing the cleaning products and improving the disposal system. May supported the change. Mr. Qiu objected to every expense and called it a disgrace that a family business needed instructions for basic hygiene. Iris recorded his dissent in the meeting notes without removing his words. “You can disagree,” she told him, “but safety regulations are not optional.” The repair list grew lengthy, and each new item threatened the budget. Still, hiding problems to preserve appearances would only enrich a future buyer at the expense of the staff.

回到旅館時，韓娜剛做完健康檢查。皮膚刺激反應不嚴重，但診所建議更換清潔用品並改善廢棄物處理系統。美姨支持改變；邱先生卻反對每一筆開銷，還說家族企業連基本衛生都得有人教，實在丟臉。以澄沒有刪改他的話，而是把異議完整記在會議紀錄裡。「你可以不同意，」她對他說，「但安全規定不是可以自由選擇的。」修繕清單愈來愈長，每增加一項都壓迫預算。然而，為了好看而掩蓋問題，只會讓未來的買家獲利，代價卻由員工承擔。

Among the receipts Iris noticed one from the night of the great winter flood, eight years earlier. It described blankets delivered to the hotel at two in the morning. A handwritten second date appeared beside the official stamp. May explained that Yun had opened the ballroom as an emergency shelter, although an official report later claimed that the doors remained locked. Iris felt a sharp melancholy, reminiscent of the helplessness she had known when her career first collapsed. The report had been used to criticize Noah's father, who managed the old public stairs. Now a simple invoice suggested the hotel's role in that night had been badly misunderstood.

以澄在收據裡發現一張出自八年前冬季大洪水當晚的單據，上頭記載凌晨兩點有毛毯送達旅館。官方戳章旁又手寫了另一個日期。美姨解釋，當時雲奶奶打開宴會廳作為緊急避難所，但後來的官方報告卻宣稱門一直鎖著。以澄感到一陣尖銳的憂鬱，想起自己事業剛崩塌時那種無助。那份報告曾被拿來指責負責舊公共階梯的柏舟父親。如今，一張普通發票卻顯示，大家對那晚旅館所做的事可能有重大誤解。

Noah came to inspect a drainage pipe and found Iris reading the invoice for a third time. He did not preach forgiveness or ask her to trust him. Instead, he pointed to the two dates and suggested that one recorded delivery while the other recorded payment. “That would be permissible accounting practice,” Iris said, “if a ledger confirms it.” They examined Yun's old books together. Several pages carried the hotel's trademark lighthouse stamp; the relevant page did not. It was missing entirely. Noah said the municipality might have a duplicate. Iris insisted on requesting it officially rather than through his former employer, whose rivalry with local residents had become public knowledge.

柏舟來檢查排水管時，發現以澄第三次閱讀那張發票。他沒有說教要她原諒誰，也沒有要求她相信自己，只指出兩個日期可能分別代表交貨與付款。「這樣的記帳方式是可以接受的，」以澄說，「前提是帳冊能證實。」兩人一起查看雲奶奶的舊簿冊。好幾頁都蓋著旅館的燈塔商標章，但他們要找的那頁根本不存在。柏舟說，市政府或許保有副本。以澄堅持走正式程序提出申請，而不是透過他過去的雇主；那家公司與地方居民長年的對立，早已不是祕密。

The office clerk warned that the request would face scrutiny because the missing page belonged to a disputed public-safety file. Iris welcomed scrutiny, provided every party followed the same rules. As she completed the form, an old guest appeared at reception and asked whether he could reserve his former room. May explained that the hotel had not reopened. The man nodded and said he had no wish to create suspense; he merely wanted to know whether the lighthouse window still faced the sunrise. Iris took him upstairs to see it. She could not promise him a booking, but she could let him stand somewhere that still belonged to his memories.

辦公室職員提醒，因為缺頁涉及有爭議的公共安全檔案，申請將受到仔細審查。以澄並不排斥審查，只要求各方遵循同樣的規則。她填表時，一位老房客來到接待處，詢問能否預訂以前住過的房間。美姨解釋旅館尚未恢復營業。老人點頭，表示自己並不是故意製造懸念；他只是想知道，燈塔窗是否仍朝向日出。以澄帶他上樓看了一眼。她無法保證訂房，卻能讓他再站一次在屬於自己回憶的地方。

That evening Iris pinned copies of the invoice, the public report, and the missing-ledger notice to a board. Their contradictions formed no complete answer. They did, however, establish a question worth investigating: who had decided that the shelter never opened, and why had the community accepted that version? Iris remembered how easily professional gossip could become an official story when nobody demanded evidence. Noah offered to search his father's surviving notebooks, but asked for time to speak with his mother first. Iris agreed. She was learning that investigation did not grant anyone the right to enter another person's grief without permission.

那晚，以澄把發票、官方報告與帳冊缺頁通知的影本釘上公告板。它們彼此矛盾，還拼不出完整答案，卻證明有個問題值得追查：誰決定宣稱避難所從未開門，又為什麼全鎮接受了那個版本？以澄想起，當沒有人要求證據時，職場流言能多麼容易變成官方說法。柏舟表示願意翻找父親留下的筆記，但希望先花點時間與母親談。以澄同意了。她逐漸明白，調查的必要性並不代表誰都可以不經允許，闖進別人的悲傷。
