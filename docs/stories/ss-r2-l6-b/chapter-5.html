A Budget without Heroes

退房之前，請把真話說完 · 第 5 / 25 章 · 5000–6000 · 英中對照

At the end of the first week, the repair budget suffered its first serious breakdown. The roof needed new flashing, the kitchen drain required a full replacement, and the emergency stair could not remain an ornamental accessory. Iris spread invoices across the dining table. Noah suggested a temporary bypass around a damaged water line, provided the work was inspected and clearly marked. Qiu called the expense excessive. “A hotel survives through revenue,” Iris answered, “not through pretending that pipes obey our wishes.” She crossed out the idea of a champagne reopening party. The money would go toward safe plumbing, staff wages, and a modest reserve for surprises.

第一週結束時，修繕預算第一次嚴重失控。屋頂需要更換防水收邊，廚房排水管得整段換掉，緊急逃生階梯也不能只是裝飾配件。以澄把發票攤滿餐桌。柏舟建議在受損水管旁設置臨時繞道，前提是工程經過檢查並清楚標示。邱先生嫌費用太高。「旅館靠收入生存，」以澄回答，「不是靠假裝水管會聽話。」她刪掉香檳開幕酒會的預算，把錢轉給安全管線、員工薪水，以及一筆不大的緊急準備金。

Hana proposed a chestnut breakfast soup based on Yun's recipe, with roasted vegetables and local fowl from a farmer who could guarantee humane conditions. May worried that the menu seemed too plain for wealthy visitors. Iris asked the staff to taste it before judging. Mr. Ho arrived with a hoarse voice from sweeping dust and complained that cleaning supplies irritated his hands. Hana found a fragrance-free lotion and protective gloves. The small exchange revealed something the trustees had missed: a workplace mentality built around appearances could exhaust the very people who made hospitality possible. Iris scheduled a meeting where every employee, including temporary workers, could raise concerns.

韓娜提議依雲奶奶食譜做栗子早餐湯，搭配烤蔬菜與當地農夫供應、能保證人道飼養的家禽。美姨擔心菜色太樸素，吸引不了富裕旅客。以澄請員工先試吃再評論。何伯掃灰塵掃得聲音沙啞，抱怨清潔用品讓雙手不舒服；韓娜替他找來無香精乳液與防護手套。這個小小互動揭露受託人忽略的事：過度重視表面的職場心態，會耗盡真正讓待客工作得以成立的人。以澄安排了一場所有員工、包括臨時工都能表達疑慮的會議。

At the meeting, Noah admitted that the old telecommunications system might fail during a storm. Jules proposed a basic radio backup. Qiu dismissed the concern until Iris asked him to name an alternative. He had none. A moody silence followed, broken by a rap at the door. A local mechanic had brought a repaired generator and an estimate for the wiring. The man's cheerful vigor did not erase the price, but it gave the team a practical way forward. Iris invited him to explain the maintenance schedule rather than treating his expertise as a number at the bottom of an invoice.

會議上，柏舟坦言舊式電信系統可能在風暴時失效，朱爾則建議準備無線電備援。邱先生不以為然，直到以澄問他有何替代方案，他卻答不上來。氣氛變得陰沉，這時門上傳來敲擊聲。一名在地技師送來修好的發電機與配線估價單。他開朗又充滿活力，並不能讓價格消失，卻讓團隊找到實際出路。以澄請他親自說明保養計畫，而不是把他的專業縮減成發票底端的一個數字。

Noah showed Iris a photograph taken during the flood. It depicted a slash of water across the lower wall and several people carrying mattresses toward the ballroom. Behind them stood his father, guiding a stranger through the back gate. The image could evoke sympathy, but it could not yet settle who had locked the public stairs that night. “My father kept trying to unveil the truth,” Noah said. “He grew exhausted and stopped talking about it.” Iris resisted an easy answer. She had heard only fragments and had once mistaken silence for agreement. She asked Noah for the camera's original file, including its date information, before drawing conclusions.

柏舟給以澄看一張洪水當晚的照片。畫面中，低處牆面被一道水痕劃過，幾個人正搬著床墊前往宴會廳；他的父親站在後方，引導陌生人通過後門。照片能喚起同情，卻仍無法證明那晚是誰鎖住了公共階梯。「我父親一直試著揭開真相，」柏舟說，「後來筋疲力盡，就不再提了。」以澄克制住立即下結論的衝動。她過去只聽過片段消息，也曾把沉默誤認為贊同。她請柏舟交出相機原始檔及日期資訊，再作判斷。

Late at night Iris caught herself working through dinner. Noah was sleeping on the sofa in the office, and his gentle snore rose above the sound of the printer. She considered waking him to ask another question, then decided that urgency did not justify treating people as tools. Instead she made tea, reviewed the monetary risks, and calculated what the reopening could realistically earn. Her grandmother's notebook was full of stories about guests, but its numbers were precise. The nucleus of the business had always been simple: offer a safe bed, pay people fairly, and keep enough money for the next season. Romance was not part of that equation, at least not yet.

深夜，以澄發現自己又忙到忘記吃晚飯。柏舟睡在辦公室沙發上，輕微的鼾聲蓋過印表機的響動。她想叫醒他再問一個問題，卻決定不能以事情緊急為由，把人當成工具使用。她泡了杯茶，重新檢視財務風險，計算重開後能有多少實際收入。祖母的筆記滿是房客故事，但數字記得很精確。這門生意的核心始終簡單：提供安全床位、公平支付薪資，並留下足夠資金迎接下一季。至少此時，戀愛還不在這道算式裡。

In the morning Iris sent the trustees a revised plan. It replaced luxury furniture with repairs, explained every delay, and included a process for reporting staff concerns without punishment. Qiu said such caution looked cowardly. Iris answered that courage was not a refusal to calculate risk. May read the proposal and offered to train the new reception staff herself. Hana agreed to test a limited breakfast menu; Noah offered engineering advice under a separate paid contract. No one was expected to work for gratitude alone. For the first time the hotel had a budget that acknowledged both its broken walls and the people standing inside them.

早晨，以澄把修正計畫寄給受託人。計畫把豪華家具預算改成修繕費，說明每項延遲原因，並設立讓員工能提出疑慮、免受懲罰的管道。邱先生說這樣的謹慎看起來很懦弱。以澄回答，勇氣並不是拒絕計算風險。美姨讀完提案，願意親自訓練新接待人員；韓娜同意測試精簡早餐菜單；柏舟則以獨立付費契約提供工程建議。沒有人被期待只為感恩而工作。旅館終於有了一份同時看見破牆與牆內人們的預算。
