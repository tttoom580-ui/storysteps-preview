餐廳裡的保險絲

The Fuse in the Dining Room

退房之前，請把真話說完 · 第 2 / 25 章 · 5000–6000 · 英中對照

On the second morning, Iris discovered why the hotel lights flickered whenever the kitchen kettle boiled. A corroded fuse had survived beyond the date when its safety certificate should expire. Noah refused to sign the inspection form until an electrician tested the entire circuit. “We have to abide by the rules even when the deadline is unreasonable,” he said. Iris wanted to argue, but she knew he was right. To affirm their agreement, she wrote the essential repairs on the reception board where staff and trustees could see them. There would be no secret bargain that exchanged guest safety for an impressive opening date.

第二天早晨，以澄終於明白，為何廚房水壺一煮沸，旅館燈光就會閃爍。某個鏽蝕的保險絲早已超過安全證明的有效期限，卻仍在使用。柏舟拒絕在檢查表上簽名，除非電工先測試整個電路。「就算期限不合理，我們也得遵守規定，」他說。以澄原本想反駁，卻知道他說得對。為確認雙方的共識，她把必要的修繕事項寫在接待處公告板上，讓員工與受託人都看得見。她不會私下用房客安全去交換漂亮的開幕日期。

Aunt May began sorting souvenirs left by former guests. There was a wooden alligator from Florida, a record of a deep bass voice, and a glass bottle of whiskey with an unbroken seal. Beside them lay a notebook of colloquial expressions collected by Yun from visitors who spoke different languages. May said these objects were not expensive, but each held a story. To Iris, the ordinary collection made hospitality seem less like a glamorous industry and more like a promise kept in commonplace gestures: a dry towel, a late meal, a listening ear. She asked May to choose which objects belonged on display. The decision did not need a consultant's approval.

美姨開始整理過去房客留下的紀念品：一隻來自佛羅里達的木雕短吻鱷、一張低沉男低音的唱片，以及一瓶封口完整的威士忌。旁邊還有雲奶奶記錄的口語表達筆記，收集自說著不同語言的旅人。美姨說，這些東西雖然不昂貴，卻各自藏著故事。以澄忽然覺得，待客之道不像光鮮亮麗的產業，更像是日常細節裡被履行的承諾：乾毛巾、深夜熱食，還有願意傾聽的耳朵。她請美姨決定哪些物件值得展示，這件事不需要營運顧問批准。

The kitchen apprentice, Hana, was a novice at running a commercial stove but excellent at improvising breakfast. She offered Iris a liter of warm ginger tea and toast with citrus syrup. Her laughter was infectious; even Noah smiled when she declared that a shabby kitchen did not require shabby food. Then a leaking pipe made the shelf tilt and sent three plates sliding onto the floor. Nobody was injured, but Hana developed a red rash where cleaning liquid had touched her wrist. Iris stopped work, found the first-aid kit, and arranged a proper examination. The plates could be replaced. The people who cooked for guests could not.

廚房學徒韓娜還不熟悉商用爐具，卻很擅長即興準備早餐。她給以澄倒了一公升溫薑茶，端來淋著柑橘糖漿的吐司。她的笑聲極具感染力；當她宣告「破舊廚房不等於破爛料理」時，連柏舟也笑了。隨後漏水的管線使架子傾斜，三只盤子滑落地面。沒有人受傷，但韓娜的手腕接觸清潔液後冒出紅疹。以澄立刻停工，找到急救箱並安排正式檢查。盤子可以再買，替房客煮飯的人卻不能任意替換。

That afternoon a developer's representative arrived with a photographer, suggesting they put the most attractive rooms under a spotlight and hide the rest during investor visits. Iris declined. “A staged tour might sell a dream,” she said, “but it will not reconcile a dishonest report with a damaged building.” The representative called her decision rash. Noah remained quiet until the visitor left, then asked whether Iris wanted him to defend her at future meetings. She considered the question. Seven years ago, he had made decisions about her professional reputation without discussing them first. “Tell me what you know,” she answered. “I will decide what to say.” He accepted that boundary without argument.

下午，開發商的代表帶著攝影師來訪，建議在投資人參觀時，只讓最漂亮的客房成為聚光燈焦點，其餘部分全部藏起來。以澄拒絕了。「安排好的參觀也許能賣掉一個夢，」她說，「但不能讓不誠實的報告與受損的建築變得相容。」代表說她的決定太魯莽。柏舟等訪客離開後才問，以後會議上是否需要他替她辯護。以澄想了一下。七年前，他曾未經討論便做出影響她職業名聲的決定。「把你知道的告訴我，」她回答，「要說什麼由我自己決定。」他沒有爭辯，接受了這條界線。

Near twilight they tested the old porch lamps. The sea below had changed from silver to deep blue, and a fishing boat moved slowly toward the harbor. Iris stood beside Noah without pretending that a working light could resolve their history. He admitted he had kept a copy of a warning letter, one he believed she received during her final month at the hotel. She remembered no such letter. Instead of accusing him, she asked for its date and delivery record. He promised to bring both. Their silence was not comfortable, but it was honest, and that made it less frightening than the easy conversation they might have staged for one another.

近黃昏時，他們測試老門廊的燈。下方海面從銀色轉成深藍，一艘漁船緩緩駛入港灣。以澄站在柏舟身邊，沒有假裝一盞會亮的燈就能解決往事。他承認自己留有警告信的副本，以為她在離開旅館前的最後一個月就已收到。以澄卻完全不記得那封信。她沒有立刻責怪他，而是詢問日期與送達紀錄。他答應兩樣都帶來。此刻的沉默並不自在，卻很真實；比起兩人可能刻意演出的輕鬆談話，反倒沒有那麼可怕。

Before closing the reception desk, Iris updated her budget. The replacement wiring would cost more than planned, which meant postponing new curtains and advertising. She could still afford to pay the staff, but only if the kitchen operated on limited hours for the next two weeks. May agreed to explain the change to former guests. Hana volunteered to design a small breakfast menu. No one offered a magical solution, and Iris found that reassuring. When she turned off the last lamp, it stayed dark instead of flickering back on. Tomorrow's work would begin from a safer place, even if the hotel looked no more beautiful than it had at dawn.

關閉接待櫃檯前，以澄更新預算。更換電線的成本比預期高，意味著新窗簾與廣告得往後延。她仍付得起員工薪水，但接下來兩週，廚房必須縮短營業時段。美姨同意向老房客解釋變動，韓娜自願設計精簡早餐菜單。沒有人提出神奇的解方，而這反倒讓以澄安心。她關掉最後一盞燈，燈確實暗下來，不再忽明忽滅。明天的工作將從比較安全的地方開始，即使旅館看起來並沒有比清晨漂亮多少。
