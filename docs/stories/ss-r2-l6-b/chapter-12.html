早餐前的傳言

The Rumor Before Breakfast

退房之前，請把真話說完 · 第 12 / 25 章 · 5000–6000 · 英中對照 · 校訂中

用互動版查詞、朗讀與收藏

A rumor spread through Bluehaven before breakfast: the Lantern House had supposedly caused an outbreak of illness during its trial night. Iris received three frightened calls from former guests. She checked the reports carefully. Two people had felt unwell after attending a separate public banquet; none of the hotel's trial guests had reported the same symptoms. Nevertheless, Iris informed the local health office and decided to withhold new reservations until the facts could be checked. Hana groaned at the timing, then began reviewing kitchen records. May asked an attendant to contact every trial guest without suggesting answers. A setback deserved a response, but panic would not make the response more accurate.

早餐前，一則傳言迅速傳遍藍灣：燈屋旅館據說在試住之夜引發疾病群聚。以澄接到三通前房客驚慌的電話，仔細核對後發現，兩名不適者參加的是另一場公共宴會；旅館試住房客沒有人回報相同症狀。儘管如此，以澄仍通知地方衛生單位，並暫停接受新訂房，直到查證完成。韓娜為這個時機嘆了一聲，隨即開始核對廚房紀錄。美姨請接待人員聯繫每位試住房客，卻不暗示他們該如何回答。挫折確實需要回應，但恐慌不會讓回應更準確。

A food-safety chemist examined cleaning residues and explained how easily a harmless substance could be mistaken for a dangerous one without proper sampling. Iris did not ask him to dispense medical advice; his task was limited to the materials he could test. A health inspector examined temperatures, ingredient records, and the breakfast batter Hana had stored for a demonstration. The kitchen procedures met the requirements documented so far, though the inquiry was still open. A skeptical reporter wanted a dramatic headline. Iris gave the confirmed facts and a date for the next update. She refused to claim complete innocence before the investigation ended, just as she refused to accept unproven blame.

一名食品安全化學專家檢查清潔殘留物，解釋若沒有正確採樣，無害物質也很容易被誤認成危險物。以澄沒有要求他提供醫療建議；他的任務限於能檢驗的材料。衛生稽查員檢查溫度、食材紀錄，以及韓娜留作示範的早餐麵糊。目前紀錄顯示廚房程序符合要求，但調查尚未結束。一位持懷疑態度的記者想要戲劇性標題，以澄只提供已確認事實和下一次更新日期。她不會在調查完成前宣稱完全無責，也不會接受未證實的指責。

While the inquiry continued, Jules found a historical census that listed the Lantern House as a public shelter. The document used an old district code and included a small logo shaped like a lantern. The census had helped populate an official register of emergency buildings. An appendix described a wooden loudspeaker once used to announce high tides. Noah remembered his father repairing that system. The discovery was invaluable because it independently established the building's public role before the disputed flood. It did not identify the person who changed the later report, but it gave Iris another dated record. She made three copies and registered where each would be stored. No important evidence would depend on one fragile envelope.

調查持續期間，朱爾找到一份歷史人口普查，裡面把燈屋列為公共避難場所。文件使用舊行政區代碼，旁邊有小燈籠形狀的標誌。那份普查也曾用於填寫官方緊急避難建築名冊。附錄記載一個過去用來發布滿潮警告的木製擴音器。柏舟記得父親修過那套設備。這項發現非常寶貴，因為它獨立證明旅館在有爭議的洪水之前就具有公共功能。它無法指出誰改動了後來的報告，但至少又提供一份有日期的紀錄。以澄複製三份並登記存放位置，不讓重要證據只依靠一只脆弱信封。

In the courtyard, children from a nearby martial arts class waited for their parents while sparrows chirped in the roof beams. May offered them water and small slices of oyster bread left from a cooking lesson. Their teacher asked whether the hotel would subscribe to a neighborhood emergency-alert service. Iris agreed to review the fee after the safety budget was updated. Across the street a developer's banner advertised lavish waterfront apartments, illustrated with beaches that the public might no longer reach. The contrast angered Iris, but she kept her attention on the files. The best argument against an unfair plan would be a clearer, safer alternative.

庭院裡，附近武術班的孩子等待家長來接，麻雀在屋樑間啾啾叫著。美姨給孩子們水喝，還分一些料理課剩下的小片牡蠣麵包。老師問旅館是否願意訂閱社區緊急警報服務。以澄答應在安全預算更新後評估費用。街對面的開發商布條正在宣傳豪華海景公寓，圖片中有未來大眾可能再也到不了的沙灘。這對比令以澄生氣，但她仍把注意力放在文件上。反對不公平計畫最有力的方法，是提出更清楚、更安全的替代方案。

Three days later the health office reported that the available evidence did not link the illnesses to the hotel. Iris posted the exact finding rather than announcing triumph. Hana returned to planning food for a small reading event, including a dessert called Iceberg Cake, named for its white icing. The cake was modest, nothing like the lavish banquet the developer had promised. Yet guests had begun asking when the hotel might reopen. Their interest felt less like a demand for luxury and more like confidence in a team willing to admit uncertainty. Iris reminded everyone that the storm-season repairs still took priority.

三天後，衛生單位回覆，現有證據未顯示不適事件與旅館有關。以澄貼出原文結論，而不是宣布大獲全勝。韓娜重新規劃小型閱讀活動的點心，其中有款因白色糖霜得名的「冰山蛋糕」。那蛋糕樸實，遠不如開發商承諾的豪華宴席。然而，房客已開始詢問何時能重新營業。他們的關心不像是在要求奢華，反而更像是相信這支願意承認不確定性的團隊。以澄提醒大家，風暴季的修繕仍是首要任務。

Noah found Iris checking the notices under the reception lamp. He said the episode had tested her more severely than the trustees' accusations. She admitted it had. Her first instinct had been to defend the hotel immediately, but that might have discouraged honest reports from the guests. “You chose to investigate first,” he said. “It mattered.” Iris knew praise was not a substitute for proof, yet it was comforting to be seen clearly. They shared a quiet supper and discussed where the next set of safety signs should go. Outside, tidewater rose and fell, indifferent to the hotel's troubles. Inside, their work continued with a little more trust.

柏舟看見以澄在接待燈下檢查公告，說這次事件對她的考驗甚至超過受託人的指責。她承認確實如此。第一反應原本是立刻替旅館辯護，但那樣可能讓房客不敢如實回報症狀。「妳選擇先調查，」柏舟說，「這很重要。」以澄知道讚美不能取代證據，但被一個人準確看見，仍令她安心。兩人安靜吃了晚飯，討論下一批安全標誌該放在哪裡。屋外潮水漲落，對旅館的煩惱毫不在意；屋內的工作則在多一點信任中繼續。
