一夜之間改變的檔案

The File That Changed Overnight

The municipal archive finally sent the flood report, but Iris noticed an ambiguity in its title. The paper copy was dated eight years earlier; the digital summary claimed it had been revised only last month. Jules, who had studied digital preservation, asked whether a hacker might have altered the public entry. Iris refused to blame an invisible villain without proof. The archive clerk explained that a software migration could also change displayed dates. They requested the original scan, the revision log, and a clear account of the occurrence. As a layman in computer forensics, Noah agreed not to speculate beyond what the records showed.

市立檔案館終於寄來洪水報告，但以澄發現標題有一處模糊不清。紙本副本的日期是八年前，數位摘要卻顯示上個月才修訂。學過數位保存的朱爾問，是否有駭客改動公共紀錄。以澄拒絕在缺乏證據時，把責任推給看不見的壞人。檔案館職員解釋，軟體系統轉移也可能改變顯示日期。於是眾人索取原始掃描檔、修訂紀錄，以及對此情況的清楚說明。柏舟自承是電腦鑑識的外行，不會對文件顯示之外的部分亂猜。

Meanwhile Noah tested the hotel's fireproof doors and measured the cold draught entering through a broken seal. Hana brought hot yogurt bread from the oven, along with tea served in mismatched cups. A battered utensil box stood beside the stove, full of miscellaneous tools Yun had saved over decades. On its lid a mermaid sticker had almost worn away. Noah remembered drawing it for Iris when they were younger. The memory made them both smile, then fall quiet. Their shared past could be tender without excusing the decisions that had driven them apart. Iris asked him to finish the door measurements before discussing anything personal.

同時，柏舟測試旅館防火門，測量破損密封條吹進來的冷風。韓娜從烤箱端出熱優格麵包，配上用不成套杯子裝的茶。爐旁擺著一只磨損的餐具箱，裡面滿是雲奶奶數十年存下的雜項工具。箱蓋上的美人魚貼紙幾乎磨掉了。柏舟記得年輕時是自己畫給以澄的。這段回憶讓兩人先笑，隨後安靜下來。共同的過去可以溫柔，卻不能替造成分離的決定開脫。以澄請他先完成門扇測量，私事稍後再談。

When the archive sent its technical explanation, Iris learned that the file had been transferred between systems, but one attachment was still missing. The missing page recorded the opening of the hotel shelter. A retired clerk offered to search old storage if the team could provide the geographical code for the district. Jules found it on a map from the year of the flood. This practical progress won little public acclaim, yet it mattered. The clerk had not promised success, only a fair search. Iris sent thanks without gifts, knowing that praise and favors could be misunderstood when an official record was under examination.

檔案館提供技術說明後，以澄得知檔案確實曾在系統間轉移，但其中一份附件仍然失蹤。缺的那頁正好記錄旅館避難所何時開門。一位退休職員表示，只要團隊提供當年行政區的地理代碼，便願意翻找舊倉庫。朱爾在洪水當年的地圖上找到了代碼。這項務實進展沒有贏得多少公開讚譽，卻很重要。退休職員沒有保證找到，只承諾公平搜尋。以澄只寄去感謝信，不送禮，因為正在調查公務檔案時，讚美與人情很容易被誤會。

During a staff outing to inspect the coastal evacuation route, May complained of bodily fatigue and asked the group to slow down. Iris checked the temperature displayed in Fahrenheit on an imported weather instrument, then converted it using the manual instead of guessing. They paused near a bench where children were watching crabs move around a tide pool. Noah explained that the route must serve people who could not climb quickly. He suggested an additional resting place and a railing that would support different heights. Iris asked him to include estimated costs. They were learning to make care visible in drawings and budgets, rather than treating it as a private sentiment.

員工外出實地查看海岸疏散路線時，美姨說身體疲倦，希望大家放慢腳步。以澄看了進口氣象儀器上以華氏顯示的溫度，照說明書換算，而不是亂猜。眾人在長椅旁休息，孩子們正看著潮池裡的螃蟹。柏舟解釋，逃生路線必須也能服務無法快速爬階梯的人。他建議增設休息點與適合不同身高的扶手。以澄請他附上估價。他們逐漸學會把照顧人的心意落實在圖紙與預算，而不是只把它當成私人情感。

That night Noah's mother called him. She had heard about the public hearing and wanted to know whether her husband's name would become the center of another argument. Noah listened while Iris quietly left the room. When he found her later, he said his mother feared the town would revolve around the flood forever. Iris understood the wish to escape, but she also believed they must persevere long enough to correct the record. “We cannot force your mother to take part,” she said. “We can make certain she is not surprised by what we publish.” Noah thanked her. He looked relieved that she recognized the difference between evidence and entitlement.

那晚柏舟接到母親電話。她聽說公開聽證，擔心丈夫的名字又成為爭論中心。柏舟通話時，以澄安靜離開房間。之後他找到她，說母親害怕小鎮永遠圍繞著那場洪水打轉。以澄理解想逃離的心情，卻也認為他們至少得堅持到紀錄獲得更正。「我們不能逼你母親參與，」她說，「但可以確保公開的內容不會讓她措手不及。」柏舟向她道謝。她分得清證據與權利要求的差別，讓他顯然鬆了一口氣。

Alone at her desk, Iris began to ponder what restoring a reputation could actually accomplish. It would not liberate Noah's family from every painful memory. It might, however, prevent new decisions from being built on a lie. She checked the archive request one more time and pinned the missing attachment number above her calendar. The hotel had not become profitable; the public stair was still legally contested; and she had not forgiven everyone involved. Nevertheless, she could identify a set of honest next steps. Unlike the old story people told about the flood, her account would leave room for uncertainty until it was resolved.

獨自坐在書桌前，以澄思考修復名聲究竟能做到什麼。它無法讓柏舟一家從每段痛苦記憶中解放，卻可能阻止新的決策繼續建立在謊言上。她又檢查一次檔案申請，把缺失的附件編號釘在日曆上方。旅館還沒有獲利，公共階梯的法律爭議仍未解決，她也沒有原諒所有相關人士。然而，她能看見下一組誠實可行的步驟。與鎮上流傳多年的洪水故事不同，她的版本會在問題解決之前，為不確定性保留空間。
