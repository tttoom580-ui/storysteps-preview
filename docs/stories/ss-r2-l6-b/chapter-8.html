八號房的試住之夜

A Trial Night in Room Eight

退房之前，請把真話說完 · 第 8 / 25 章 · 5000–6000 · 英中對照 · 校訂中

The hotel held its first supervised trial night with five volunteer guests. Iris wanted to test check-in procedures before accepting payment. She had decided to computerize reservations, but kept printed copies in case the power failed. A retired astronomer booked room eight because its window faced the northern sky. He arrived with a small telescope and a heavy wardrobe trunk, then explained that he was recovering from pneumonia. He was no longer infectious, his doctor had said, and he had completed the prescribed antibiotic course. Iris thanked him for sharing the information and asked whether he needed any practical accommodations. His answer was simple: a chair near the window and time to rest.

旅館舉辦第一晚有工作人員監督的試住，邀請五位志願房客。以澄想先測試入住程序，再正式收費。她決定把訂房資訊電腦化，但也保留紙本副本，以防停電。一名退休天文學家預訂八號房，因為窗戶面向北方天空。他帶著小望遠鏡和沉重衣箱抵達，說自己正從肺炎中康復。醫師說他已沒有傳染性，也完成了處方抗生素療程。以澄感謝他告知，問他需要哪些實際協助。他只希望窗邊有把椅子，並有時間休息。

May had invited a former social worker whose specialty was psychiatry to review how staff responded to distressed visitors. The adviser stressed that hotel workers should not try to diagnose anyone. They could listen, offer privacy, and contact appropriate help when requested. Iris incorporated this advice into a short guide, along with a plan to evacuate guests if the alarms sounded. Noah tested the doors and discovered that a swollen frame could disable the emergency exit. He marked the fault and closed that corridor until repairs were complete. A robust safety procedure was more valuable than a beautiful demonstration that worked only while everyone remained calm.

美姨邀請一位曾在精神醫學領域工作的社工，檢視員工如何回應情緒困擾的旅客。顧問強調，旅館工作者不應自行診斷；他們可以傾聽、提供隱私，並在對方要求時聯繫適當協助。以澄把這項建議寫入簡短指引，也加入警報響起時的疏散計畫。柏舟測試門扇，發現一個受潮膨脹的門框可能讓緊急出口無法開啟。他標示故障，暫停使用那段走廊，直到修復完成。可靠的安全程序比只在人人冷靜時才有效的漂亮示範更有價值。

The accountant reviewed the night's expenditure and frowned at a single missing nickel in the cash drawer. Iris asked her to reconcile the figures without accusing the new receptionist. Behind the counter, a visitor complained that an old tourist map labeled several streets “Oriental Quarter.” Lena explained that oriental was an outdated and imprecise label for people and cultures, even if it still appeared in historical records. They agreed to preserve the map as an artifact, with a note explaining its context rather than using its language in current signs. Iris said a hotel could honor history without repeating every assumption inherited from it.

會計檢查當晚支出時，發現收銀櫃少了一枚五分鎳幣，皺起眉頭。以澄請她重新核帳，不要先指責新接待員。櫃檯後方，一位訪客抱怨舊旅遊地圖把幾條街標成「東方街區」。莉娜解釋，oriental 用來稱呼人群與文化已經過時且不精確，儘管這種用法仍可能出現在歷史文件中。大家同意把地圖當史料保存，附上語境說明，而不是沿用其中措辭製作新路牌。以澄說，尊重歷史，不表示必須重複它繼承下來的每個成見。

At dinner, a guest asked whether the town's old sovereign coin had once been used to pay room charges. May laughed and brought out a specimen from the hotel's tiny museum case. The discussion helped the visitors orient themselves in the town's history. The retired astronomer then suggested a prototype of a simple star-viewing chart for children. Hana asked the group to applaud the volunteers who had repaired the garden benches. Mr. Ho gave an embarrassed bow. Iris noticed how easily sincere recognition could soften a room full of strangers. The hotel's trial night was becoming a gathering rather than a performance put on for investors.

晚餐時，有房客問小鎮昔日的主權金幣是否曾用來支付房費。美姨笑著從旅館小展示櫃拿出一枚樣本。這場討論讓訪客更理解地方歷史。退休天文學家接著建議製作給孩子用的簡易觀星圖原型。韓娜請大家鼓掌感謝修好花園長椅的志工，何伯不好意思地鞠了個躬。以澄注意到，真誠的認可能多麼容易讓一屋子的陌生人放鬆。試住之夜正逐漸成為人們的聚會，而不是為投資者安排的一場表演。

Near midnight, the fire alarm sounded because steam had entered a faulty sensor. Several guests were irritable, and the astronomer moved slowly. Staff followed the written plan, helped everyone outside, and confirmed that the alarm was false. Noah and Iris reviewed the incident with the guests instead of hiding it. Their spokesperson for the evening, May, explained what would be repaired and when. The astronomer said he had felt safe because nobody had shouted at him to hurry. His words mattered more to Iris than any five-star rating. She had built a process that could adapt to people rather than demanding that people adapt to it.

午夜將近時，蒸氣進入故障感應器，火警警報突然響起。幾位房客變得煩躁，退休天文學家走得很慢。員工照書面計畫協助所有人到室外，確認警報為誤報。柏舟與以澄沒有掩蓋事故，而是與住客一起檢討。當晚的發言人美姨解釋將修好什麼、何時完成。天文學家說，他感到安全，是因為沒有人吼著叫他走快一點。對以澄而言，這句話比五星評價更重要。她建立的程序終於能適應人，而不是強迫人配合程序。

In the quiet after the trial, Noah found Iris sitting near the garden with her hands folded as if in meditation. He asked about her relatives, her kin in the city, and whether she had anyone to call when work became overwhelming. Iris said her friends were far away but real, and she would not sacrifice them for the hotel. Noah spoke of his mother, who had stopped visiting Bluehaven after his father's death. He wanted to make the town safe for her return but could not require her forgiveness. Iris appreciated that distinction. They watched the dark sea without touching. It was the first evening their silence felt chosen rather than imposed.

試住結束後的寧靜中，柏舟找到坐在花園旁的以澄。她雙手合攏，像是在冥想。他問她在城市裡的親族與朋友，當工作壓得她喘不過氣時，有沒有可以打電話的人。以澄說朋友雖遠，情誼卻是真實的，她不會為旅館犧牲那些關係。柏舟談起母親；父親過世後，母親便不再回藍灣。他希望讓小鎮成為母親願意重返的安全地方，卻不能要求她原諒任何人。以澄欣賞這種區別。兩人沒有碰觸，只望著暗色的海。這是第一次，他們的沉默像是自主選擇，而非被迫承受。
