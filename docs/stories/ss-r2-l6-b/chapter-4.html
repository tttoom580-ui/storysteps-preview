The Stairway Behind the Wall

退房之前，請把真話說完 · 第 4 / 25 章 · 5000–6000 · 英中對照

By the fourth day, Iris had acquired an addiction to comparing old photographs with the present building. Every image showed something the newer reports ignored. A brass badge worn by a former harbor guide appeared beside a stairway that no longer existed on official maps. The exact date was uncertain, but an approximate comparison of window frames placed the photograph at least thirty years earlier. Noah brought an archaeology student named Jules, who had studied the town's industrial ruins. Jules warned that a photograph was evidence of appearance, not necessarily ownership. Iris appreciated the distinction. She was beginning to distrust beautiful conclusions that arrived too quickly.

到了第四天，以澄幾乎迷上把老照片與現況逐一比對。每張照片都藏著新報告忽略的東西：一名舊港口嚮導胸前的銅製徽章，旁邊是一條早已從官方地圖消失的階梯。照片拍攝日期不明，但大致比較窗框樣式，至少可追溯到三十年前。柏舟帶來研究鎮上工業遺跡的考古學學生朱爾。朱爾提醒，照片只能證明外觀，不能直接證明產權。以澄很欣賞這項區別。她開始不相信太快得出的漂亮結論。

Jules had been a disciple of a retired preservation architect, though she disliked the word because it sounded too solemn. She pointed out that the staircase's aesthetic design matched the public quay rather than the private hotel. A faded government emblem stood beneath a layer of paint. Noah used a portable lamp to illuminate it. “This doesn't prove the land cannot be sold,” he said, “but it may prove the passage was built with public funds.” Jules hoped this evidence would enlighten officials who thought every old structure belonged to its nearest owner. Iris asked for a method of verification before celebrating. The possibility made the project's stakes clearer: reopening the Lantern House might preserve a route the entire town had once relied on during storms.

朱爾曾跟著退休的古蹟保存建築師學習，雖然她不喜歡被稱為「弟子」，覺得太嚴肅。她指出，階梯的美學設計與公共碼頭相同，而非旅館私人部分。褪色的政府徽記藏在一層油漆底下，柏舟用攜帶式燈具照亮它。「這還不能證明土地不能出售，」他說，「但可能證明通道是用公款興建的。」朱爾希望這項證據能啟發那些以為每座老建築都歸最近地主所有的官員。以澄沒有急著慶祝，而是先問如何驗證。這個可能性讓她更看清事情的重要性：重開燈屋，也許能保住全鎮風暴時曾依賴的一條路。

At the next trust meeting, Qiu tried to preside over the discussion before the other trustees arrived. He said the prestige of the town depended on welcoming a luxury marina, and accused Iris of making a fuss over forgotten stairs. The developer's draft plan would privatize the waterfront entrance while preserving a narrow viewing platform for tourists. Iris asked whether local fishermen had been consulted. Qiu said their approval was implicit in the project's economic benefits. “An assumption is not consent,” Iris replied. The third trustee, Ms. Wu, quietly requested that the minutes include this exchange. For the first time Iris noticed she might have an ally at the table.

下一次信託會議上，邱先生在其他受託人抵達前就想主持討論。他說，小鎮的聲望取決於能否吸引豪華遊艇碼頭，又指責以澄為遺忘的階梯大驚小怪。開發商的草案打算把海濱入口私有化，只留一小塊觀景平台給遊客。以澄問過漁民是否曾被徵詢意見。邱先生說，經濟利益已隱含他們的同意。「假設不等於同意，」以澄回答。第三名受託人吳女士輕聲要求把這段對話記進會議紀錄。以澄第一次察覺，桌邊或許有個盟友。

The following afternoon, a small blaze began in a rubbish bin after a worker dropped a cigarette. Staff extinguished it quickly, but smoke entered a storage room and damaged the old guest curtains. The incident exposed another flaw: the alarm system worked only in the main corridor. Iris ordered a complete safety review, even though the trustees had complained about previous delays. A technician said repairs came with a two-year warranty, provided the devices were tested on a regular rotation. His instructions sounded dull beside talk of grand development, but Iris knew that keeping people safe often required tasks nobody would photograph for a brochure.

隔天下午，一名工人丟棄香菸後，垃圾桶裡竄起一小片火焰。員工迅速撲滅，煙卻飄進儲藏室，燻壞舊客房窗簾。意外暴露另一個缺陷：警報系統只涵蓋主要走廊。儘管受託人已抱怨延誤，以澄仍要求全面檢查安全設備。技師說，修繕保固兩年，但須定期輪流測試裝置。比起宏偉的開發計畫，這些指示顯得乏味；以澄卻知道，保護人的安全往往仰賴沒人想拍進宣傳冊的瑣事。

That evening Noah found Iris alone near the boarded-up stairs, trying to read Yun's pencil notes through a veil of dust. A stray cat watched them from the stones. The sunset made the old walls radiate a copper glow, and beyond the hill lay a vineyard that had supplied the hotel's first dining room. Noah carried an old electric shaver in his tool bag; he had promised to repair it for Mr. Ho, who refused to replace anything that still had a chance. Iris smiled at this inconvenient kindness. When Noah asked whether she wanted company, she said yes. It was a small permission, neither a promise nor a denial of the past.

傍晚，柏舟發現以澄獨自站在封住的階梯附近，隔著塵幕閱讀雲奶奶的鉛筆筆記。一隻流浪貓在石頭上看著他們。夕陽讓舊牆散發銅色光澤，山丘後還有一片葡萄園，曾供應旅館第一代餐廳的葡萄。柏舟工具袋裡放著一把舊電動刮鬍刀；他答應替何伯修理，因為老人拒絕丟掉任何仍有修復可能的物品。以澄因這份麻煩的善意露出微笑。柏舟問她是否希望有人陪伴，她說好。那只是一個小小的允許，不代表承諾，也不代表否認過去。

Iris completed the day by placing a copy of the photograph beside the proposed marina map. The two images were separated by decades, yet the same corner of coastline appeared in both. She sent formal questions to the public works department and asked Jules to document the paint layers without damaging the emblem. A past mistake had taught Iris not to confuse determination with certainty. She still wanted the hotel to survive, but she no longer thought survival alone would count as success. If a private reopening sealed a public path, she would be saving the wrong thing. She wrote that sentence into her notebook, underlining it twice.

以澄把老照片與遊艇碼頭計畫圖並排，結束這一天。兩張圖像相隔數十年，卻都出現了同一段海岸。她向公共工程部門提出正式問題，請朱爾記錄油漆層次，但不得損壞徽記。過去的失誤教會以澄，決心不等於確定。她仍想救下旅館，卻不再認為只要旅館活下來就算成功。如果一家私人旅館的重開，反而封閉了公共通道，她救下的就不是對的東西。她把這句話寫進筆記本，畫了兩道底線。
