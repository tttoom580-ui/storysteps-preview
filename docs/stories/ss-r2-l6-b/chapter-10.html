The Offer at the Council Hall

退房之前，請把真話說完 · 第 10 / 25 章 · 5000–6000 · 英中對照 · 校訂中

On day thirty-five, the town council invited Iris to describe the hotel restoration. The hearing room was decorated with a mural of an ancient crocodile from a children's tale, a reminder that even serious public buildings had strange histories. The council clerk was punctual and insisted on a strict speaking order. Iris brought a short presentation with arithmetic anyone could verify: expected room revenue, repair costs, and wages. She explained that the hotel had a dual purpose, earning income while preserving access to the coastal route. One councillor called the idea awesome; another warned that enthusiasm could not replace structural certification. Iris agreed with both, refusing to turn applause into permission.

第三十五天，鎮議會邀請以澄說明旅館修復計畫。聽證廳牆上畫著童話裡的古老鱷魚，提醒大家連嚴肅的公共建築也有奇特的歷史。議會書記準時開場，堅持依規定順序發言。以澄準備了人人都能核對的簡短算術簡報：預估房費收入、修繕成本與薪資。她說明旅館的雙重用途，是賺取收入，也保留通往海岸的通道。一位議員說構想很棒，另一位則提醒熱情不能取代結構安全認證。以澄同意兩人的話，不把掌聲當成許可。

A representative of the regional fishing confederation spoke next. He described a recent crackdown on street vendors who used the public road, while private construction vehicles crossed the same area without interruption. The policy had created injustice for small traders. A faction of business owners supported the developer, hoping a marina would attract wealthy visitors. Iris listened without accusing them of selfishness. She knew they had bills to pay. Still, a plan that promised to equalize opportunity should not remove the only affordable entrance to the shore. She asked the council to publish route-access data before approving any new barrier. Several members requested a written proposal.

接著發言的是區域漁業聯盟代表。他描述近期針對使用公共道路的街頭攤販展開取締，私人施工車輛卻能不受干擾地通過同一區域。這種政策對小商販造成不公。部分商業人士組成派系支持開發商，希望遊艇碼頭能吸引富裕旅客。以澄沒有指責他們自私，而是仔細傾聽；她知道大家都有帳單要付。但聲稱要使機會平等的計畫，不應移除唯一負擔得起的海岸入口。她請議會在核准新障礙前公布道路使用資料，幾名議員要求她提交書面方案。

Outside the hall a young boy was accused of being a pickpocket after a wallet disappeared from a visitor's coat. A crowd began to form, eager for intrigue. Noah saw the boy standing rigid with fear. Iris asked everyone to exercise restraint while the staff checked the cloakroom. Minutes later the wallet appeared under a bench, where it had fallen during a presentation. The boy's grandmother thanked Iris, but she was embarrassed by the praise. Preventing an accusation from becoming a punishment was not heroism. It was a basic standard she wished had been applied to Noah's father after the flood.

廳外，一名遊客的錢包不見了，一個男孩因此被指控是扒手。人群很快聚集，似乎等著一場陰謀好戲。柏舟看見孩子因害怕而僵直站著。以澄請大家保持克制，讓工作人員先檢查衣帽間。幾分鐘後，錢包在長椅下找到，原來是在簡報時掉落。孩子的祖母向以澄致謝，她卻因讚美而不好意思。阻止指控直接變成懲罰，不是英雄行為；那只是她希望當年柏舟父親也能得到的基本待遇。

At lunch the developer's representative approached Iris with a proposal. If she withdrew the public-access claim, the company would pay for the hotel's new cement work and provide an advertising campaign designed to animate the quiet harbor. It would also reserve a luxury suite for investors. The offer was a powerful lure. Iris asked whether she would incur a penalty for discussing its terms with the trustees and local residents. The representative said secrecy would be preferable. “Then I cannot accept,” Iris replied. She had no objection to private investment, but a bargain affecting public passage could not be negotiated behind a closed door.

午餐時，開發商代表找上以澄，提出一個交換條件：只要她撤回公共通道主張，公司就支付旅館水泥修繕費，並提供廣告活動，讓沉寂的港灣熱鬧起來，還會預留豪華套房給投資人。這條件很有吸引力。以澄詢問，如果她向受託人與地方居民討論內容，是否會產生違約罰責。代表說最好保密。「那我不能接受，」以澄回答。她不反對私人投資，但影響公共通行的交易，不能在關起門的情況下談成。

Back at the Lantern House, Hana served celery soup while May reviewed the budget. The repair expenses would force them to stagger payments to contractors, with consent and clear dates. One supplier accepted; another refused. Iris did not call the refusal obstinate, because he had his own employees to support. Noah offered to help negotiate a different schedule, but Iris asked him to focus on the safety drawings instead. She wanted his expertise, not his rescue. As the light faded, the old lighthouse sign outside the kitchen flickered back to life after a careful repair. It was only a small improvement, yet everyone stopped to admire it.

回到燈屋後，韓娜端上芹菜濃湯，美姨與以澄重新檢視預算。修繕費會迫使他們在取得同意、明確告知日期後分期付款給承包商。一位供應商接受，另一位拒絕。以澄沒有說拒絕者頑固，因為他也有員工要養。柏舟提出協助協商替代付款時程，但以澄請他專心處理安全圖紙。她需要的是他的專業，不是他的拯救。暮色漸濃時，廚房外那塊舊燈塔招牌經過細心修理，終於重新亮起。改變雖小，每個人還是停下來欣賞。

That night Iris drafted a letter to the council explaining the public route dispute. She included both evidence and unresolved questions. Noah arrived with drawings that showed how an emergency gate could remain open to residents without exposing the hotel to dangerous traffic. They debated costs until their voices grew tired, then laughed at the number of revisions. The work gave them a common language that did not require pretending they had never hurt each other. Iris told him she could imagine trusting him professionally again. Noah did not ask whether that meant anything more. He simply asked what drawing she wanted to examine next.

當晚，以澄起草一封給議會的信，說明公共通道爭議，同時列出證據與尚未解決的問題。柏舟帶來圖紙，展示如何讓居民使用緊急閘門，又不讓危險車流進入旅館。兩人討論成本直到聲音疲倦，最後因修改版本太多而笑了。他們在工作中找到一種共同語言，不必假裝彼此從未受過傷。以澄告訴他，自己或許能重新在專業上信任他。柏舟沒有追問這是否還有其他含義，只問她下一張想看哪幅圖。
