A week later the independent surveyor arrived carrying a bulky instrument case. The trustees had not yet approved the full fee, so Iris authorized only the first stage under her operating budget. Noah showed the surveyor where a smooth pebble path disappeared beneath weeds. Seabirds seemed to glide through the wind beyond the sea wall. According to the preliminary measurements, the public stair had extended farther than the developer's map admitted. The surveyor refused to deem the disputed strip public land without additional records. Iris respected the caution. She wanted a route people could safely navigate, not a victory based on another inaccurate line.

一週後，獨立測量師帶著笨重的儀器箱抵達。受託人尚未核准全部費用，以澄便先在營運預算內批准第一階段測量。柏舟帶測量師到一條光滑鵝卵石小徑前，路在野草底下消失。海鳥在海堤外迎風滑翔。初步測量顯示，公共階梯過去延伸得比開發商地圖承認的還要遠。但測量師拒絕在缺乏其他紀錄時，就把有爭議的地帶認定為公地。以澄尊重這份謹慎。她想要的是人人能安全通行的路，不是另一條錯誤線條換來的勝利。

The survey attracted neighbors who had rarely entered the hotel. Lena, a professional interpreter, came with her wife and helped translate the discussion for visiting fishermen. One older man stumbled when he heard Lena introduce her wife; May simply offered both women chairs and continued the meeting. The hotel had no need to treat a lesbian couple as either a scandal or a symbol. Iris wanted every guest to be able to state a name and feel welcome. When a volunteer proposed announcing that the Lantern House had achieved complete liberation from discrimination, Lena shook her head. “A sign can promise respect,” she said, “but behavior has to prove it.”

測量工作吸引了很少進旅館的鄰居。職業口譯員莉娜與妻子一同前來，幫忙翻譯外地漁民的意見。一位老人聽見莉娜介紹妻子時愣了一下，美姨只是替兩位女士搬來椅子，照常開會。旅館不需要把一對女同志伴侶當成醜聞，也不必把她們變成宣傳象徵。以澄希望每位客人都能自在說出自己的名字，並感受到歡迎。當志工提議宣布燈屋已徹底擺脫歧視時，莉娜搖頭。「招牌可以承諾尊重，」她說，「但行為才是證明。」

The next morning Iris discovered that two cleaners had been working far beyond their agreed hours. They feared that a complaint would count against them when the hotel reopened. She called a staff meeting and said no one should overwork to rescue the owner's reputation. Mr. Qiu accused her of running a dictatorship whenever she overruled his cost-cutting ideas. “A dictatorship would prevent disagreement,” Iris answered. “I am putting yours in the minutes.” May helped revise the shifts, and the cleaners received back pay. The delay would be inconvenient, but exploitation disguised as devotion was a price Iris refused to charge anyone.

隔天早晨，以澄發現兩名清潔人員的工時遠超過原本約定。他們擔心抱怨會影響重開後的工作機會。她召開員工會議，表明沒有人應該為挽救老闆名聲而過勞。邱先生每次削減成本的提議被否決，就指責她搞獨裁。「獨裁會禁止異議，」以澄回答，「我正在把你的異議記進紀錄。」美姨協助調整班表，清潔人員也拿到補發薪資。這會造成一些延誤，但以澄拒絕讓任何人為披著奉獻外衣的剝削付錢。

Jules organized a handicraft afternoon to raise funds for the damaged stairs. Children made clay lanterns, while retired farmers described how a former sugar plantation had changed the local shoreline. One man brought a photograph of an old plow beside a drainage ditch. The picture did not establish ownership, but it suggested the ditch had existed before the hotel was built. That discovery could spur further research. Iris insisted that donated artworks remain the property of their makers unless sold with explicit consent. Even a trifle such as a painted shell represented someone's time. She wanted the fundraiser to demonstrate the fairness the hotel claimed to defend.

朱爾辦了一場手工藝下午活動，替受損階梯募款。孩子們做黏土燈籠，退休農夫則描述早年甘蔗種植園如何改變海岸線。一位老人帶來照片，畫面中舊犁具停在排水溝旁。照片不能證明所有權，卻顯示那條溝可能比旅館更早存在，足以促使大家繼續調查。以澄堅持，除非創作者明確同意出售，捐來展示的作品仍屬於本人。哪怕只是上色貝殼這種小東西，也代表某個人的時間。她希望募款活動能真正實踐旅館聲稱要守護的公平。

After the event, Noah invited Iris for a stroll along the upper road. The setting sun left a warm tan on the fishermen's faces as they pulled in their nets. From a hillside radio came an old ballad; Noah mentioned that the musician's family still received royalty payments for its recordings. Iris thought about the way value survived beyond the person who first created it. Yun had left behind more than walls and debts. She had also left relationships, obligations, and the expectation that a house by the sea should offer safety. Iris was not certain she could preserve all of them. Noah did not interrupt her while she said so.

活動結束後，柏舟邀以澄沿著上方道路散步。夕陽把收網漁民的臉曬成溫暖的褐色。山坡收音機傳來一首老歌，柏舟提到那位音樂人的家屬至今仍收到錄音版稅。以澄想到，一個人創造的價值竟能在他離去後繼續存在。雲奶奶留下的，不只是牆與債務，還有人際關係、責任，以及海邊房子應提供安全的期待。以澄不確定自己能保住全部。她說這些話時，柏舟沒有打斷。

By the end of the week, the surveyor submitted his preliminary report. The boundaries were pending review and remained unresolved, and the municipal archive had not yet answered Iris's request for the flood ledger. Still, volunteers had raised enough money to repair the first section of railing, and the staff had agreed to a sustainable work schedule. Iris marked another square on her ninety-day calendar. The progress was neither lofty nor dramatic. It was measurable. Before leaving the office, she found Noah's unsigned note beneath the survey: “You never needed me to decide for you. I should have learned that sooner.” She folded it without answering, allowing herself time to decide what the apology meant.

週末，測量師送來初步報告。界線仍待審查，尚未釐清，市立檔案館也尚未回覆洪水帳冊的申請。不過，志工募得的錢已足以修好第一段欄杆，員工也同意新的可持續班表。以澄在九十天日曆上又畫掉一格。進展並不高遠，也不戲劇化，卻可以測量。離開辦公室前，她在測量報告下發現柏舟未署名的便條：「妳從來不需要我替妳決定，我早該學會。」她沒有回覆，只把紙條折起來，讓自己有時間決定這句道歉的意義。
