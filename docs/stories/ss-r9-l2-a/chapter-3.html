空獸欄

The Empty Stall

聽不見咒語的靈獸醫師 · 第 3 / 25 章 · 1000–2000 · 英中對照

用互動版查詞、朗讀與收藏

An alarm rang before breakfast. A young lamb had escaped its pen, and its gate stood open. The chief keeper said an angry beast must have struck the lock. Yue entered the empty stall and asked everyone to remain calm. A small object lay beside the door: a broken piece of wood from a cleaning tool. The latch had been pressed from the outside. This did not prove who opened it. Chen closed the road to the training field and asked workers to search without shouting. A frightened animal could run farther if people rushed toward it.

早餐前警報響起。一頭小羊逃出欄舍，欄門大開。總飼育員說一定是發怒的靈獸撞壞門鎖。月走進空欄舍，請大家保持冷靜。門旁躺著一小塊斷木，是清潔工具的一部分。門閂似乎從外側被壓下，但這不能證明是誰打開了它。辰封閉通往訓練場的道路，請員工安靜搜尋。若人們大聲追趕，受驚的動物可能跑得更遠。

A visiting cowboy from the western farms offered to ride after the lamb. Yue refused the idea but thanked him for wanting to help. She had seen that lamb stop near moving water, and a horse might frighten it into the stream. The visitor showed her a classic trick for guiding sheep with food. This was more useful. Yue asked a cook for dry grain and a soft blanket. Another worker arrived with a hair dryer, thinking its warm air might comfort the animal. Yue left the dryer unplugged. It could make an unfamiliar noise and startle a small creature.

一位來自西部牧場的牛仔訪客提議騎馬追羊。月拒絕了，卻感謝他的好意。她曾看見那頭羊在流動的水邊停下，馬可能把牠嚇進溪中。訪客示範用食物引導羊群的傳統方法，這比較有用。月向廚師要來乾穀物和柔軟毛毯。另一位員工拿著吹風機趕來，認為暖風能安撫牠。月沒有插電，因為陌生聲響可能嚇到小動物。

Under the old rules, failing a duty could bring punishment. One keeper said he had seen a boy near the gate and wanted to punish him immediately. Yue asked him to wait. The boy worked for the school press and had come to take a picture for a farm tour. His camera had caught one shot of the open road, but not the moment the gate moved. The boy was embarrassed by the question. Yue said a false charge would not bring back the lamb. They could examine the photograph after the animal was safe. The boy showed them a spare battery cell from his camera bag, but that object could not direct the search. A witness with a fresh haircut confirmed only the time he had passed the gate.

依照舊規定，未盡職責可能遭受懲罰。一名飼育員說他看見男孩靠近欄門，想立刻處罰他。月請他等一下。男孩替校園媒體工作，原本是來替牧場導覽拍照。他的相機拍到一張開放道路的照片，卻沒有拍到欄門打開的瞬間。男孩因被詢問而尷尬。月說，錯誤指控不會把小羊帶回來。先確保動物安全，再檢查照片也不遲。 男孩拿出相機袋中的備用電池芯，但那個物件無法指引搜尋。一名剛剪完頭髮的證人，只能證實自己經過大門的時間。

Chen found the lamb beside a boat pond, where a wooden sail moved in the wind. The animal had not crossed the water. He placed grain on the ground and backed away. Yue asked everyone else to stop at the fence. The lamb followed the food until Chen could guide it into a quiet lane. When it was back in a safe pen, the workers cheered. Yue checked its breathing and feet before allowing a celebration. The lamb was tired but unharmed. The open gate, however, remained a question no one could honestly answer yet.

辰在小船池塘旁找到小羊，池邊有一片木帆迎風擺動。牠沒有涉水。辰把穀物放在地上，往後退。月請其他人停在圍籬旁。小羊跟著食物前進，直到辰能把牠引進安靜小道。回到安全欄舍後，員工們歡呼。月先檢查牠的呼吸和腳，才允許大家慶祝。小羊疲倦但沒有受傷。不過，欄門為何打開，仍是誰也無法誠實回答的問題。

They examined the broken tool with the cleaner who owned it. He explained that he had used it to lift a heavy bucket beside the stall. A piece could have fallen into the latch by accident. Yue tested that idea with an empty model gate, never with a living animal nearby. When Chen pushed the broken piece into the gap, the latch fell open. It was possible, but the team could not tell whether it had happened that way that morning. They repaired the latch and wrote the uncertainty in the report instead of inventing a neat ending.

他們與工具主人——那名清潔員——一起檢查斷掉的木柄。清潔員說自己曾用它在欄舍旁抬起重桶，一小塊木頭可能意外落進門閂。月用沒有動物的模型欄門測試這個想法。當辰把斷木塞進縫隙，門閂就落下了。這種情況確實可能發生，但小隊仍無法確認早上是否如此。他們修好門閂，在報告中寫下不確定性，而不是編造漂亮的結局。

Later the chief keeper offered a trade: he would cancel the charge against the boy if Yue stopped asking questions about the saddles. Yue did not accept. The lamb's escape and Moss's pain were separate matters, and both deserved proper records. She placed the repaired gate beside her notes and prepared to speak with the director. Chen worried that the keeper could strike their names from the trial list. Yue was frightened too. But she had seen a small animal return safely because people asked useful questions. She wanted the same care for every beast in the academy.

稍後總飼育員提出交換條件：只要月停止追問鞍具，他便取消對男孩的指控。月沒有接受。小羊逃脫與苔的疼痛是不同事件，都值得有正確紀錄。她把修好的門閂照片放進筆記，準備和主任談。辰擔心總飼育員會把他們從試煉名單中除名。月也害怕，但她親眼看見一頭小動物因為人們提出有用的問題而安全回來。她希望學院裡每頭靈獸都能得到同樣的照顧。
