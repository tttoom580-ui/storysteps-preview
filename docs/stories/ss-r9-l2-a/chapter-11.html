沒有騎士的測試

The Test Without a Rider

聽不見咒語的靈獸醫師 · 第 11 / 25 章 · 1000–2000 · 英中對照 · 校訂中

用互動版查詞、朗讀與收藏

An iron frame stood in the workshop, ready for the independent test. Chen had built it with a beginner from the city college. Instead of putting a saddle on an animal, they would charge the frame with bags of sand and measure where the pressure went. The goal was simple: find a design that did not press sharply into a shoulder. Yue asked the team to emphasize what the test could and could not show. A model could reveal a dangerous shape, but it could not describe an animal's fear. The nurse would still have to judge comfort before anyone returned to work.

工作室立著一個鐵製架台，準備接受獨立測試。辰和城市學院的一名初學者共同製作它。他們不把鞍具裝在動物身上，而是用沙袋對架台施加負重，測量壓力落在哪裡。目標簡單：找出不會尖銳壓住肩膀的設計。月請團隊強調測試能與不能說明什麼。模型能指出危險形狀，卻無法描述動物的恐懼。任何動物復工前，護理師仍須評估舒適程度。

A visiting craft worker brought a gold-colored ring for comparison. It looked beautiful, but the metal bent under a heavy load. "A fine color is not proof of strength," she said. The head keeper claimed that the workshop was wasting money by testing too many shapes. Yue offered him a mug of tea and invited him to watch the readings. He refused the tea but stayed. The first ring pressed hard into a narrow point. The second spread its force over a wider surface. A small change in shape made a clear difference on the model.

來訪的工藝師帶來一個金色環件作比較。它很漂亮，卻在重負下彎曲。「漂亮顏色不是堅固的證明。」她說。總飼育員認為工作室測試太多形狀是在浪費錢。月遞給他一杯茶，邀他看數據。他拒絕喝茶，卻留下來。第一個環把壓力集中在狹窄位置；第二個環把力量分散得更廣。形狀的小變化在模型上造成了明顯差異。

At noon, Su brought bread with grape jam for the workers. A child outside began to chase a bird, and Chen stepped out to stop him before he reached the stable. The child said he wanted to shoot a photograph of the bird in flight. Chen showed him a safer place to stand and explained why quick movement upset animals under care. In the workshop, the metalworker pointed to a small trap in the old design: once a strap tightened, the ring could not move with the shoulder. The problem had been hidden in plain sight.

中午，蘇帶來塗葡萄果醬的麵包。一個孩子在外頭追鳥，辰趕在他走到獸舍前攔下。孩子說他只想拍一張飛鳥的照片。辰指給他安全的站位，解釋突然移動會打擾正在接受照護的動物。工作室裡，金工師指出舊設計中的陷阱：皮帶一旦拉緊，環件就無法隨肩膀移動。問題一直明明白白地藏在眼前。

A student asked Yue why she trusted a metalworker more than a master of spirit magic. She answered that trust came from evidence and willingness to correct mistakes, not from a person's title. Religion and magic had their places in the mountain community, but the pressure test was a physical question. The master was welcome to inspect the numbers. The student listened quietly. Yue knew her words might sound disrespectful, so she added that nobody's knowledge was complete. Good work was social work: different people brought different skills, and each needed room to speak. She said the importance of the test lay in its clear limits, not in pretending one frame could answer every question about life.

一名學生問月為何比起靈術大師更相信金工師。月回答，信任來自證據及修正錯誤的意願，不是頭銜。宗教與法術在山區社群有自己的位置，但壓力測試是物理問題。大師同樣可以來檢查數據。學生安靜聽著。月知道自己的話可能被視為不敬，便補充說沒有人的知識是完整的。良好工作是社會性的合作：不同的人有不同技能，每個人都需要發言的空間。 她說測試的重要性在於界線清楚，而不是假裝一座架台能回答所有生命問題。

By late afternoon, the team could simply say one true thing: the older clasp shape produced less sharp pressure in their model. They could not yet promise success with a living animal. The usual plan was to announce a winner as soon as a test ended, but Yue asked them to wait for a second reading. Chen checked the scale and found a small error in its starting position. They repeated the test, and the same pattern remained. This did not make them heroes. It gave the nurse a safer design to examine next, which was enough for that day.

午後晚些時候，團隊終於能簡單說出一件真實的事：在模型裡，舊式扣環造成的尖銳壓力較小。他們仍不能保證真實動物穿戴後會成功。慣常做法是測試一結束便宣布優勝者，但月請大家等第二次讀數。辰檢查秤，發現起始位置有小誤差。他們重做試驗，仍得到相同模式。這沒有讓他們變成英雄，卻給護理師一個更安全的下一步選擇。今天這樣就夠了。

When the metalworker packed her tools, the head keeper approached her privately. He asked whether the new ring could be made before the spring display. She said yes, but only if the team paid for careful work and allowed time for testing. The keeper looked unhappy. He had hoped for a quick answer with no cost. Yue walked beside him to the door and said that they could adjust the display rather than the animals. He did not reply. Yet he left the iron frame standing, and he did not order Moss into the air the next morning.

金工師收拾工具時，總飼育員私下走近她，詢問春季展示前能否製作新環。她說可以，條件是支付仔細製作所需的費用，並留出測試時間。總飼育員看起來不高興。他原本希望不花代價就得到快速答案。月陪他走到門邊，說他們可以調整展示，而不是強迫動物配合。他沒有回答，卻讓鐵架留在原位，也沒有在隔天早上命令苔起飛。
