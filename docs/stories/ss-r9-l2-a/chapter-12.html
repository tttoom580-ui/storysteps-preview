山外的診所

A Clinic Beyond the Mountain

聽不見咒語的靈獸醫師 · 第 12 / 25 章 · 1000–2000 · 英中對照 · 校訂中

用互動版查詞、朗讀與收藏

A message came from a small countryside settlement. Several working animals had begun to refuse their carts, and their owners feared losing customers. The local clinic had no specialist. Yue and Chen received permission to visit with the nurse. On the way, the driver asked which direction to take after the capital road ended. Yue read the signs and kept a copy of the route beside the medical kit. When they arrived, one owner blamed a young worker for the animals' fear. Yue asked to see the harnesses before deciding whether anyone had acted carelessly. A worried customer wanted to blame the driver at once, but Yue asked them to examine the cart instead.

山外一處鄉間聚落傳來消息：幾頭工作動物開始拒絕拉車，主人們擔心失去顧客。當地診所沒有專科人員。月和辰獲准隨護理師前往。路上，司機問離開通往首都的道路後該往哪個方向走。月讀著路牌，把路線副本放在醫療箱旁。到達後，一名主人責怪年輕員工讓動物害怕。月請他先拿出挽具，別急著判定是誰疏忽。 一位擔憂的顧客想立刻責怪司機，月卻請大家先檢查推車。

The village office had a blank inspection form. Its ink had dried out, and the last record was several months old. Yue measured each cart and found that one wooden bar was too low for the taller animals. She marked the measurement and showed the owner how the bar touched their backs. The danger was greater on uneven roads. The owner looked scared. He had thought a heavy cart was safe as long as it moved. Yue said they could settle the question by testing an empty cart first, without making a tired animal pull anything.

村辦公室的檢查表是空白的，墨水也乾了；上一筆紀錄是幾個月前的事。月量測每台車，發現其中一根木橫桿對身形較高的動物而言太低。她記下尺寸，讓主人看木桿如何碰到背部。路面不平時危險更大。主人嚇壞了。他原以為重車只要能動就是安全。月說可先測試空車，不必讓疲倦動物拉任何東西。

The nurse also saw a man whose face was swollen from a painful tooth. She directed him to the traveling dentist rather than trying to treat his problem with an animal medicine kit. At the food stall, children ate fries and sweet pudding while adults talked about the clinic visit. A woman in slippers carried a teapot to the workers. She said that they hardly ever received clear explanations from visiting officials. Yue thanked her and asked which part of the care plan seemed confusing. The woman wanted to know what to do when an animal stopped on the road.

護理師也看見一名男子因牙痛而臉部腫脹，便請他去找巡迴牙醫，而不是用動物藥箱處理。在食物攤旁，孩子吃著薯條和甜布丁，大人則談論診所來訪。穿拖鞋的婦人端茶壺給工作者，說他們幾乎從未從外來官員得到清楚說明。月向她道謝，詢問照護計畫哪部分令人困惑。婦人想知道動物在路上停下時該怎麼辦。

Yue showed the village group a simple response: stop the cart, check for injury, move other traffic away, and call for help. There was no need to drag the animal or treat its fear as disobedience. A sudden stop could have many causes. Chen invited the owners to try the procedure with an empty model cart. One farmer admitted that he had never considered the animal's view. Another asked whether the new rules showed enough respect for people who depended on cart work. Yue said respect was not a choice between workers and animals. Both needed a safer system. She asked everyone to regard a refusal as useful information until a proper check showed otherwise.

月向村民示範簡單處理順序：停車、檢查傷勢、讓其他交通離開，再呼叫協助。不必拖拽動物，也不必把恐懼當作不服從。突然停下可能有很多原因。辰邀主人用空模型車練習。一名農夫承認自己從未考慮動物的角度。另一人問新規則是否尊重依靠運輸工作過活的人。月說尊重不是在員工與動物之間二選一，雙方都需要更安全的制度。 她請大家在正式檢查證明相反情況前，把拒絕視為有用資訊。

Before leaving, the nurse examined the tired animals and wrote clear rest plans. The team could not fix every cart or repay every lost sale in one day. Yue copied the owners' questions into the clinic record and promised to send practical answers after the repairs were reviewed. She refused to write a success report while a dangerous bar remained in use. Instead, she asked the local leader to close that cart until it was changed. The leader agreed on the condition that the academy help find a temporary replacement. Chen offered to bring one by the next supply run.

離開前，護理師檢查疲累動物，寫下清楚的休息計畫。小隊不可能一天內修好所有車，也無法賠償每筆損失。月把主人們的問題抄進診所紀錄，答應在維修審查後送來實用答覆。危險橫桿仍在使用時，她拒絕寫成成功報告，而是請地方負責人暫停那台車。負責人同意，條件是學院協助尋找臨時替代車。辰答應在下一趟補給時送來。

On the return journey, the driver asked Yue whether the trip had changed her mind about the mountain academy. She said it had widened her view. The rules they created would affect people far beyond their own stable, so the rules had to be understandable outside a classroom. She checked the sealed records once more and rested her head against the window. There was still a long road home. Yet she now knew that a good healer listened not only to the patient, but also to the people whose lives were tied to its care.

回程時，司機問月這趟旅程是否改變了她對山門的看法。她說這拓寬了自己的視野。他們訂出的規則會影響遠在獸舍以外的人，因此規則不能只有教室裡的人聽得懂。她再次確認封存的紀錄，然後靠著窗休息。回家的路還很長，但她如今明白，好醫者不只傾聽病患，也要聽見與照護息息相關的人們。
