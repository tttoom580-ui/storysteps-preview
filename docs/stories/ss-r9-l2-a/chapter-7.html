休息日曆

The Calendar of Rest

聽不見咒語的靈獸醫師 · 第 7 / 25 章 · 1000–2000 · 英中對照 · 校訂中

用互動版查詞、朗讀與收藏

The old flight calendar showed work days but almost no rest days. Chen wanted to advance the safety review before the next trial. Yue agreed and asked the nurse to introduce a new method to the keepers. Each animal would have a card showing food, sleep, work, and medical checks. The principle was humble: a living being was not a machine. Its needs changed with age, health, and climate. A sunny morning did not make an exhausted deer ready to fly. They could make progress only if they stopped guessing and began to record what actually happened. The cards would contain space for an animal's refusal as well as its achievements, so neither kind of information could be quietly removed.

舊飛行日曆列出工作日，幾乎沒有休息日。辰想趕在下一次試煉前推進安全審查。月同意，請護理師向飼育員介紹新方法：每頭動物都有一張記錄飲食、睡眠、工作和醫療檢查的卡片。原則很謙卑：活著的生命不是機器，需求會隨年齡、健康與氣候改變。晴朗早晨不代表疲倦的鹿就能飛。只有停止猜測，開始記錄實際情況，事情才會進步。 照護卡會同時包含動物的拒絕與成就，避免任何一類資訊被悄悄刪除。

The team cleaned a rocky path behind the stables before the first test. A trainee took a mop from the supply room, but Yue asked him not to wash the stones while the animals walked there. A wet surface might cause a fall. Another trainee wanted to jog beside Moss to encourage it. The nurse said a quiet walk was enough. Their ideal result was not a victory in a race. It was a deer that could move without fear or pain. Each small step would be written down, even if the animal chose to stop.

第一次測試前，小隊清理獸舍後的石路。見習生拿來拖把，月卻請他別在動物行走時把石頭弄濕，因為濕滑可能造成跌倒。另一名見習生想在苔旁慢跑，鼓勵牠前進。護理師說安靜散步便夠了。他們理想的結果不是比賽勝利，而是一頭能在沒有恐懼與疼痛下移動的鹿。每一小步都要記錄，即使動物選擇停下也一樣。

A dusty box in the storeroom contained old records from a desert station. In a dry region, the animals had worked for shorter periods because heat made their breathing difficult. Yue compared those notes with the mountain academy's schedule. The mountain air was cooler, but steep hills and heavy loads created other risks. The records showed that rest was not a single number for all places. Chen copied a chart, then marked the conditions that did not match their own. He said they should learn from other stations without pretending the situations were identical.

儲藏室裡一只滿是灰塵的箱子，裝著沙漠驛站的舊紀錄。乾燥地區因炎熱會讓動物呼吸困難，所以工作時段較短。月把資料和山上學院的時程比較。山間空氣較涼，但陡坡與重物造成另一種風險。紀錄顯示，休息不能對所有地方使用同一個數字。辰抄下表格，標明哪些條件不適用於本院。他說應該向別的驛站學習，卻不能假裝情況完全相同。

Near the pond, a turtle crossed the path at its own pace. A young student laughed and called it the slowest creature in the world. Yue asked him to watch it reach the water without help. The student fell quiet. Moss was standing a few steps away, alert but unafraid. The nurse invited it to follow a line of food, then stopped before it looked tired. Chen recorded the period of movement and the time of rest. Thus the first new care card held two useful facts: Moss could walk farther without the strap, and it still needed recovery.

池塘旁，一隻烏龜以自己的速度穿過小路。年輕學生笑牠是世界上最慢的動物。月請他看著牠不靠幫助抵達水邊，學生便安靜下來。苔站在幾步外，保持警覺卻不害怕。護理師用食物引導牠往前，再在牠疲倦前停止。辰記下行走時間與休息時段。因此第一張新照護卡記錄了兩件有用的事：苔卸下鞍帶後能走得更遠，但仍需要復原。

After dinner, Yue found a toothbrush in the wrong supply drawer. Someone had used it to clean a metal joint, then put it beside animal feeding tools. She moved it to a marked repair box and explained why tools needed separate places. Chen laughed at how a tiny brush could become part of a large safety plan. Yue answered that a plan was made of small habits repeated every day. The head keeper happened to hear her. He accused her of creating too much paperwork. Instead of arguing, Yue showed him how the card could be filled in less than a minute.

晚餐後，月發現一支牙刷被放錯抽屜。有人拿它清洗金屬接頭，卻又放回動物餵食工具旁。她把牙刷移到標示清楚的維修盒，解釋工具為何需要分開存放。辰笑說小牙刷竟會成為大安全計畫的一部分。月回答，計畫是由每天重複的小習慣構成的。總飼育員正好聽見，指責她製造太多文書。月沒有爭吵，只示範一分鐘內就能填完卡片。

Before leaving, Chen placed Moss's card where every keeper could see it. It showed one day of safe walking, not permission to return to heavy flights. He knew some workers still disliked the change. They preferred the old schedule because it gave clear commands and easy scores. But the new card gave something the old calendar had never offered: a place to say that an animal was not ready. Yue looked at the row marked "rest" and felt the smallest kind of victory. Nothing had been forced. Someone had finally written down what the animal needed.

離開前，辰把苔的卡片放在所有飼育員都看得到的地方。上面顯示一天安全步行的紀錄，而不是恢復重飛的許可。他知道有些員工仍不喜歡改變。他們偏好舊時程，因為指令清楚、分數好算。但新卡片提供了舊日曆從未提供的空間：可以寫下「動物尚未準備好」。月看著標有「休息」的那一列，感到一種極小的勝利。沒有人被強迫，終於有人寫下動物真正需要什麼。
