古老的靈獸書

The Old Animal Book

聽不見咒語的靈獸醫師 · 第 8 / 25 章 · 1000–2000 · 英中對照 · 校訂中

用互動版查詞、朗讀與收藏

An elder keeper visited the clinic with a book more than a century old. Its pages held drawings of ancient mountain deer and notes about the way they lived before people trained them to carry riders. Yue was surprised by one instruction: young animals should remain with their families for a long period. The academy had separated them much earlier. Chen asked whether the book was still useful. The elder said old knowledge could help, but nobody should follow it without checking. People once believed many things that later proved wrong. A careful healer must ask questions of both the past and the present. The elder's notes also described how to give basic aid when a calf showed signs of fever, while waiting for a qualified nurse.

一位資深飼育員帶著一本超過百年的書來到診所。書頁畫著古代山鹿，並記錄牠們在被訓練載人之前的生活。月對一項指示特別驚訝：幼獸應該長時間和家族待在一起。學院卻早早把牠們分開。辰問古書是否仍有用。長者說舊知識可以參考，卻不能不經查證就照做。人們過去相信過許多後來證明錯誤的事。細心醫者必須同時向過去與現在提問。 長者筆記也描述幼獸有發燒跡象時，等待合格護理師到來前如何提供基本協助。

The book explained how an adult deer carried a young one across a shallow stream during the flood season. It also showed how a calf rested on the ground near its mother's lap. One drawing seemed to show a wild animal walking backward to protect its young, while another showed two deer swimming. Yue copied the pictures but did not claim they described every animal. She wanted to maintain the identity of each patient in the clinic. An injured old deer was not the same as a healthy young one. Their records needed names, ages, and individual needs. Yue asked whether an injured animal should swim at all; the nurse said such a decision required an individual examination first.

書上描述成年鹿在洪水季節如何帶幼鹿穿過淺溪，也畫出幼鹿在母鹿身旁蜷伏休息的姿勢。一幅畫似乎是野鹿倒退保護幼崽，另一幅則畫了兩頭鹿游泳。月抄下圖畫，卻沒有聲稱每頭鹿都如此。她想維持診所裡每位動物病患各自的身分。受傷老鹿和健康幼鹿不同，紀錄必須有名字、年齡及個別需求。 月問受傷動物是否適合游泳；護理師說這必須先個別檢查才能決定。

For the midday break, the elder brought a peach and a small bag of grain. He asked Yue to help design a feeding plan that children could understand. She placed a used bowl beside a new one and asked whether color alone could tell them which was clean. Chen said no; they had to inspect both. The elder smiled at the typical mistake. A stranger might see the same bowl and make the wrong guess. Yue wrote that every feeding tool should carry a simple cleaning mark. People who were new to the clinic deserved clear information rather than hidden rules.

午間休息時，長者帶來一顆桃子和一小袋穀物。他請月協助設計孩子也能理解的餵食計畫。月把用過的碗放在新碗旁，問光看顏色能否知道哪個乾淨。辰說不能，兩個都要檢查。長者笑說這是典型錯誤。陌生人看見相同的碗也可能猜錯。月寫下每件餵食器具都應有簡單清潔標記。新來診所的人值得清楚資訊，不該被隱藏規則困住。

A young trainee wanted to pose beside Moss for a photograph. Yue explained that an animal recovering from pain did not exist to decorate a picture. The trainee looked embarrassed and offered to take a photograph of the care book instead. They discussed how to keep the old pages safe. A single tear near the binding could damage an important drawing. Chen built a flat support from spare wood, and the elder thanked him. The book would remain in the library; anyone using it would have to write down which page had been consulted.

一名見習生想在苔旁擺姿勢拍照。月解釋正在疼痛中復原的動物不是照片裝飾品。見習生有些尷尬，改提議拍照護書。他們討論如何保護舊書。裝訂處的一道裂痕可能毀掉重要圖畫。辰用木料做了平坦支架，長者向他道謝。書會留在圖書室，借閱者必須記下查看過哪一頁。

That afternoon, the nurse compared the old drawings with the care cards. The book suggested that some deer preferred quiet groups while others needed more space. Yue proposed a small change to the stable design: two resting areas with an open gate between them. The animals could choose where to stand. This was not a magical cure for pain, and it would not replace medical treatment. But it might lower stress. The director approved a temporary test, provided that staff watched both entrances and kept the animals from reaching the road.

當天下午，護理師比較古書圖畫與照護卡。書中提到有些鹿喜歡安靜群聚，另一些需要更大的空間。月提議稍微改造獸舍：設兩個休息區，中間以開放的門相連，讓動物自行選擇。這不是治療疼痛的魔法，也不能代替醫療，但可能降低壓力。主任批准暫時試行，條件是有人守住兩個入口，避免動物走到道路上。

At sunset, Moss entered the quieter area and lay down without a command. Yue did not rush to call the test a success. One evening could not show what would happen after a week. She wrote the date and asked the night staff to continue the record. Chen looked at the ancient book on its new stand. He said he had once thought old knowledge was only a decoration for ceremonies. Now a careful question from the past had helped them make a better room. Yue replied that the deer had given them the most important answer by choosing a place to rest.

日落時，苔沒有接到命令便走進較安靜的區域躺下。月沒有急著宣布成功。一個晚上還不足以知道一週後會如何。她寫下日期，請夜班員工接著記錄。辰望著新書架上的古書，說自己以前以為古知識只是典禮裝飾。現在過去提出的一個細心問題竟幫助他們改善獸舍。月說最重要的回答仍是苔自己給的——牠選了想休息的地方。
