安靜的聽證

The Silent Hearing

聽不見咒語的靈獸醫師 · 第 10 / 25 章 · 1000–2000 · 英中對照 · 校訂中

用互動版查詞、朗讀與收藏

The academy held a public meeting beside the valley station. A long track led from the animal clinic to a flat stone platform. Yue arrived with her notebook and a folder of photographs. Chen placed a small flag by the entrance to indicate that the clinic's animals would not join the demonstration. Some visitors were concerned that the spring trial might be canceled. Yue listened to their questions without promising an easy answer. They had gained useful information, but the distance between knowing a problem and solving it was still large. She would rather move forward slowly than rush a sick animal into flight. Her central concern was the cycle of injury and rushed return to work; a fine ceremony could not break it.

學院在山谷車站旁舉行公開會議。一條長軌道從靈獸診所延伸到平坦的石造月臺。月帶著筆記本和照片資料夾抵達。辰在入口放了小旗子，標示診所動物不參加示範。有些遊客擔心春季試煉取消。月聽取他們的問題，沒有許下簡單的答案。他們已獲得有用資訊，但知道問題與解決問題之間仍有很長距離。她寧願緩慢前進，也不願急著逼病獸起飛。 她最在意的是受傷後倉促復工的循環；漂亮典禮無法打破它。

The meeting followed a city law about animal welfare. The chair explained that a single report could cause a review but not an automatic punishment. Yue's first task was to describe what she had seen. The nurse explained that different bodies might need different saddle sizes. She used the word sex to describe one biological factor, but said age, injuries, and individual shape also mattered. Yue appreciated that the rules did not reduce an animal to one label. The chair asked the keepers to remain silent while each witness spoke. For a few minutes, the room was truly quiet. The silence in the room gave witnesses time to think, and even the strongest trainer could gain from listening.

會議依城市的動物福利法律辦理。主席說明，單份報告可以啟動審查，卻不會自動導致懲罰。月的第一項任務是描述親眼所見。護理師解釋不同身形可能需要不同鞍具尺寸。她以「性別」描述一項生物因素，但指出年齡、傷病與個體體型也同樣重要。月很欣慰規則沒有用單一標籤定義動物。主席請飼育員在每名證人發言時保持沉默。有幾分鐘，房間真正安靜下來。 房間裡的沉默讓證人有時間思考，即使最強的教練也能從傾聽中受益。

A wealthy visitor stood to speak about the riches that flying races brought to the town. He wore a fine suit and showed a wallet full of event tickets. Yue did not make fun of him. She knew local shops needed customers, and some families depended on trial work. But she asked if economic benefit should be counted together with the cost of care. The visitor said that he had never considered that question. His answer was not agreement, yet it opened a space for discussion. The chair wrote the question on the public board rather than pushing it aside.

一位富裕訪客站起來談飛行競賽帶給城鎮的財富。他穿著漂亮西裝，拿出裝滿活動票券的皮夾。月沒有嘲笑他。她知道本地商店需要顧客，有些家庭依賴試煉工作。但她問，經濟收益是否應該與照護成本一起計算。訪客說自己從未考慮過這點。這不是同意，卻替討論打開了空間。主席把問題寫在公開白板上，沒有把它推到一旁。

When the head keeper spoke, he denied that the new straps were harmful. He said no problem had occurred during the first week. Chen opened the old report and showed that similar refusals had been recorded months earlier. The keeper looked surprised, then complained that the paper had never reached his office. Yue said the records did not prove that he had seen it. They proved something else: the academy needed a system in which safety reports could not disappear in a drawer. The chair agreed to review the reporting route before the next trial.

總飼育員發言時，否認新鞍具會造成傷害。他說第一週沒有發生問題。辰翻開舊報告，指出幾個月前就有人記錄相似的拒飛情況。總飼育員驚訝，接著抱怨那張紙從未送到他辦公室。月說紀錄不能證明他讀過報告，它證明的是另一件事：學院需要不會讓安全報告消失在抽屜裡的制度。主席同意在下次試煉前檢討通報流程。

A merchant asked when flights would occur again. Yue explained that the clinic could continue active rehabilitation, but a return to work required medical approval and a safe equipment test. An observer asked whether the staff had measured the pressure on each animal. Chen showed the results and indicated where their method had limits. The next task was to compare designs without placing animals at risk. The chair thanked them for refusing to hide those limits. Outside, children watched empty carts roll along the track, discovering that even a quiet service could be useful.

一名商人問何時能恢復飛行。月解釋診所可以持續積極復健，但復工必須有醫療核准及安全裝備測試。旁聽者問員工是否量測每頭動物受到的壓力。辰展示結果，並指出方法的限制。下一項任務是在不讓動物冒險的前提下比較設計。主席感謝他們沒有隱藏限制。屋外，孩子們看著空推車沿軌道滑行，發現安靜的服務也可能有用。

At the close of the hearing, the chair ordered an independent equipment test and a temporary limit on heavy flights. It was not everything Yue had requested, but it was a real change. Chen packed their papers and asked whether they should celebrate. Yue said they could share a meal, then return to the animals. Moss would not know that a meeting had taken place. It would know only whether tomorrow brought pain or rest. They walked back to the clinic together, carrying a public decision that still had to become daily practice.

聽證結束時，主席下令進行獨立裝備測試，並暫時限制重負飛行。這並非月要求的全部，卻是真正的改變。辰收好文件，問是否該慶祝。月說可以一起吃飯，然後回去照顧動物。苔不會知道今天開了什麼會，只會知道明天迎來的是疼痛還是休息。他們一起走回診所，帶著一項仍須落實為日常習慣的公開決議。
