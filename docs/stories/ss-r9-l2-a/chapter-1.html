不肯飛的鹿

The Deer Who Refused to Fly

聽不見咒語的靈獸醫師 · 第 1 / 25 章 · 1000–2000 · 英中對照

用互動版查詞、朗讀與收藏

Yue reached the mountain animal house before sunrise. A windy balcony looked over the training field, where a white deer stood beside a wooden base. The deer was named Moss. Yesterday it had carried three riders, but this morning it would not take a single step. Chen, a young keeper, tried a gentle approach. Moss moved away, and the rope shook in his hand. Yue had no spirit power. She could only watch closely. When the head trainer called the animal lazy, she asked whether anyone had checked its feet. He told her that young cleaners should stay out of medical work.

月在日出前抵達山上的靈獸院。多風的陽臺俯瞰訓練場，一頭名叫苔的白鹿站在木製底座旁。昨天牠還載了三名騎士，今早卻連一步也不肯走。年輕飼育員辰試著溫和接近，苔卻退後，繩索在他手裡晃動。月沒有靈力，只能仔細觀察。總教練說這頭鹿懶惰，她便問是否有人檢查過牠的腳。對方要她這個年輕清潔工別插手醫療工作。

At the station office, a male operator watched the speed of small cargo cars on a screen. The mountain subway ran underground to the city, while a secondary passage led to the animal house. Yue used that passage every day. She had developed a belief that careful work could be as useful as magic. The academy's old tradition said only students with spirit roots could treat sacred beasts. Yue did not deny that spells sometimes saved lives. She simply knew that a wound would not disappear because a powerful person refused to see it. She asked Chen to discuss what he had noticed. Yue hoped to develop a care rule that would be simple enough for every worker to follow.

車站辦公室裡，一名男性操作員盯著螢幕上的貨車速度。山區地下鐵通往城市，另一條次要通道則通向靈獸院。月每天都走這條路。她逐漸相信細心工作也能和法術一樣有用。學院的古老傳統說，只有具有靈根的學生才能治療聖獸。月不否認法術有時能救命，只是明白傷口不會因權威者拒絕看見就消失。她請辰一起討論他觀察到的事。 月希望發展一條每名員工都能遵守的簡單照護規則。

Chen remembered one small instance from yesterday. When a heavy saddle came off, Moss had moved normally for a moment. But the challenge was not merely to remove equipment. The spring trial would begin in ten days, and the academy hoped to impress visitors from across the nation. Its director wanted every animal ready. Yue noticed a metal seesaw near the feed shed. A young keeper placed a bag on one end to demonstrate balance. The bag was lighter than Moss's saddle. Yue asked for a scale, but the keeper laughed and told her not to trouble the adults.

辰想起昨天的一個小例子：沉重的鞍具卸下後，苔曾短暫正常走動。但真正的挑戰不只是拆除裝備。春季試煉十天後就要開始，學院希望讓全國各地的訪客留下好印象。主任要求每頭靈獸都準備就緒。月注意到飼料棚旁一座金屬翹翹板。一名年輕飼育員把袋子放在一端，示範平衡；那袋東西比苔的鞍具還輕。月要求拿秤來，對方卻笑她不要麻煩大人。

A cleaner named Lin brought a notebook from the storage room. It listed the hours that Moss had worked, but not the weight carried on each flight. Yue wrote down the missing detail without pretending she knew its answer. Chen wondered if she was making an enemy of the trainers. She said she wanted a better question, not a fight. If they could record the animal's actions without forcing it to move, they might learn something. Chen agreed to ask the supervisor for permission. For the first time that morning, he stood beside Yue rather than behind the trainer.

清潔員林從儲藏室拿來一本簿子，上面記著苔的工作時數，卻沒有每趟飛行的負重。月記下缺少的細節，沒有假裝知道答案。辰擔心她會得罪教練。她說自己想提出更好的問題，不是想吵架。只要不強迫苔移動，也許就能記錄牠的反應並找出線索。辰答應向主管請示。那天早上，他第一次站在月身旁，而不是跟在教練後面。

They waited until Moss settled under a tree. Yue placed a bowl of water within reach and watched which leg the deer used first. It stepped forward with its right foot, then stopped when the saddle strap touched its left side. This was not proof of the cause, but it was a pattern worth testing. Chen sketched the strap and marked the exact point where it rested. Yue photographed the mark so that no one would later have to trust her memory alone. Neither of them touched the sore area, and they wrote that the examination was incomplete.

他們等到苔在樹下安定下來。月把一碗水放在牠伸得到的地方，觀察牠先用哪隻腳移動。苔以右前腳踏出一步，但鞍帶碰到左側身體時便停了。這還不能證明原因，卻是一個值得測試的模式。辰畫下鞍帶，標出它接觸身體的確切位置。月拍下痕跡，免得日後只能憑她的記憶。兩人都沒有碰疼痛處，並記下檢查尚未完成。

In the evening, the head trainer sent a message: Moss would return to training at dawn. Yue looked at the empty space beside the line for its weight. An order could fill a timetable, but it could not fill that gap. She and Chen wrote a request for a proper examination and signed their names. It might be rejected. It might even cost them their jobs. Yet Moss had refused a task that no one had explained to it, and Yue decided that its refusal deserved an answer before another command. Outside, the wind passed over the closed stable doors.

傍晚，總教練傳來通知：苔明天黎明必須恢復訓練。月看著負重欄旁的空白。命令能填滿時程表，卻填不上這個缺漏。她和辰寫了一份正式檢查申請，簽上姓名。申請可能被駁回，兩人甚至可能失去工作。但苔拒絕的是沒有人向牠解釋過的任務。月決定，在下一道命令之前，牠的拒絕值得被認真回答。屋外，風掠過緊閉的獸舍門。
