鞍帶的角度

The Angle of the Strap

聽不見咒語的靈獸醫師 · 第 2 / 25 章 · 1000–2000 · 英中對照

用互動版查詞、朗讀與收藏

The director gave Yue and Chen permission to examine the saddle, but only while Moss rested. They spread the leather on a wide board outside the stable. Chen measured the angle of its metal clasp. It was different from the drawing in the current textbook. Yue found an earlier edition in a dusty cabinet. The older drawing showed a softer bend. She did not know why the maker had changed it, so she took photographs of both pages. She wanted to avoid blaming a person before they had examined the object. A slow investigation, she told Chen, could still save time later.

主任准許月和辰檢查鞍具，但限定苔休息時才能進行。他們在獸舍外一塊寬木板上攤開皮帶。辰量了金屬扣環的角度，發現它和現行教科書的圖不同。月在滿是灰塵的櫃子裡找到較早版本，舊圖上的彎角較柔和。她不知道製造者為何改動，便拍下兩頁作對照。她不想在檢查物件前就責怪任何人。她告訴辰，緩慢調查仍可能替未來節省時間。

Across the yard, a barber was cutting a keeper's hair beneath a paper lantern. A dove landed beside his chair, and two young deer watched from the shade. The scene looked peaceful, but Moss stood far from the others. Yue appreciated that the animals chose different places to rest. The academy treated them as a single group, even though a huge difference could exist between one body and another. She used a small piece of chalk to mark a spot on the board where the clasp pressed too hard. Chen asked whether the mark alone could explain the painful movement.

院子另一頭，理髮師在紙燈籠下替一名飼育員剪頭髮。一隻鴿子落在椅子旁，兩頭幼鹿從陰影裡看著。景象看似平和，但苔站得離其他動物很遠。月很珍惜動物能自己選擇休息位置這件事。學院把牠們當作同一群，即使個體的身形可能相差很大。她用粉筆在木板上標出扣環施壓過重的位置。辰問，這個記號是否足以解釋疼痛的動作。

The animal care organization had a president who visited only on trial days. Its committee included very few workers from the lower sheds. Those workers were a minority in every important meeting, although they handled the animals most often. Yue wanted that to change, but she knew a particular injury had to be treated before a larger debate. She asked a nurse to check Moss. The nurse found no open wound, only heat under the strap. She recorded the result. "The truth may be smaller than a grand curse," she said, "and still important."

靈獸照護組織有一位只在試煉日現身的主席。委員會裡很少有下層獸舍的員工。這些員工雖然最常接觸動物，在重要會議中卻始終是少數。月希望改變這種情形，但她知道必須先治療眼前的傷。她請護理師檢查苔。護理師沒有發現破皮，只摸到鞍帶下方異常發熱，便記錄下來。「真相也許不像巨大詛咒那麼驚人，」她說，「但一樣重要。」

At lunch, a cook served sour fruit beside rice. Chen admitted that he had helped order the new straps because the seller promised faster flights. He felt ashamed and offered to return every one of them. Yue shook her head. They had not yet examined the other sizes, and some might be safe. Removing everything without a plan could leave the clinic unable to move injured animals. She proposed sorting the straps by length and comparing them with each animal. Chen looked relieved, not because she excused his decision, but because she offered him useful work. Chen said he had learned to appreciate a careful question more than a quick apology. Neither would remove the pain without action.

午餐時，廚師端出米飯和酸水果。辰承認自己曾參與訂購新鞍帶，因為賣家保證能飛得更快。他覺得羞愧，想把所有鞍帶退回。月搖頭。其他尺寸還沒檢查，其中有些或許安全。若毫無計畫地全部移走，診所可能連運送傷獸都做不到。她建議依長度分類，再和每頭動物的身形比較。辰鬆了口氣，不是因為月替他開脫，而是她給了他可以補救的工作。 辰說自己學會欣賞仔細提問，勝過迅速道歉；若沒有行動，兩者都不能消除疼痛。

Near sunset, an older stable worker said that the problem did not exist in his youth. Yue asked him what equipment he remembered. He drew a curved ring, then spoke about a traveling metalworker who used to make each saddle by hand. The new factory produced them in large numbers, which lowered their price. That fact mattered, but price was not the only measure of good care. They agreed to invite the metalworker for a second opinion. Before leaving, Yue placed the old and new drawings in separate covers so neither could be changed without leaving a trace.

接近日落時，一位資深獸舍員工說年輕時沒有這個問題。月問他記得什麼裝備。他畫出一個彎環，提到以前有位流動金工師會為每副鞍具手工打造零件。新工廠能大量生產，價格因此降低。這是重要事實，但價格不是良好照護的唯一標準。他們決定請那名金工師提供第二意見。離開前，月將新舊圖紙分別裝封，避免日後被改動卻毫無痕跡。

Moss allowed the nurse to touch its shoulder after the heavy strap was removed. It still refused to fly. Yue wrote that both facts were true. A small improvement did not mean an animal was ready for a dangerous task. Chen crossed out the morning flight on the schedule and asked another team to carry the medicine by cart. The director agreed to one day of rest, but warned that the spring trial was approaching. Yue knew the hardest part was ahead: they had to show not only what was wrong, but what a safe choice might look like.

拆下沉重鞍帶後，苔允許護理師觸摸肩膀，但仍拒絕飛行。月記下這兩件都是真的事實。小小改善不代表動物已準備好承擔危險任務。辰取消隔天早上的飛行，請另一組用推車運藥。主任同意休息一天，卻提醒春季試煉將近。月明白最難的事還在後頭：他們不只要指出錯誤，還必須提出安全的替代方式。
