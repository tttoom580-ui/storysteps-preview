沒有名字的瓶子

The Bottle Without a Name

聽不見咒語的靈獸醫師 · 第 9 / 25 章 · 1000–2000 · 英中對照 · 校訂中

用互動版查詞、朗讀與收藏

A cleaner found a glass tube beside the feeding room. Its lid was loose, and a little blue liquid remained inside. Yue asked everyone not to touch it until the nurse arrived. A picture of a fish was printed on the side, but there was no title or maker's name. The head keeper wanted to throw it away. Yue said that a careless move could destroy useful evidence or hurt someone. They placed the tube in a safe box and marked its position on a drawing of the room. Nothing about the bottle yet proved it had harmed the deer.

清潔員在飼料房旁找到一支玻璃管，蓋子鬆了，裡面還有一點藍色液體。月請大家在護理師抵達前不要碰它。瓶身印著一幅魚的圖像，卻沒有標題或製造商名稱。總飼育員想把它丟掉。月說莽撞處理可能破壞證據或傷人。他們把玻璃管放進安全盒，在房間圖上標出發現位置。此刻還沒有任何證據能證明瓶子傷害了鹿。

The nurse used a test from the clinic to check the liquid. It was a harmless cleaning mixture, not medicine, and it had been brought by a worker from the aquarium. Yue felt silly for imagining a dangerous substance, but the nurse told her that caution was not foolish when it was clearly recorded. The important result was that they could dismiss one possibility. Chen looked at the bright blue symbol on the tube and asked why the maker had chosen a fish. The nurse said the mark belonged to a company that cleaned water tanks. The fish image was a friendly company character, not a medical sign. Yue asked the nurse to express that difference clearly in the report.

護理師用診所的檢測方式檢查液體。那是無害的清潔混合液，不是藥物，由一名水族館員工帶來。月因曾想像它可能危險而覺得自己有些傻，護理師卻說，只要記錄清楚，謹慎就不是愚蠢。重要結果是他們能排除一個可能性。辰望著玻璃管上明亮的藍色符號，問製造商為何選魚當標記。護理師說那是替水槽清潔的公司商標。 魚的圖像只是公司的親切吉祥角色，不是醫療標誌。月請護理師在報告裡清楚表達這項差異。

At lunch the cook served delicious rice and vegetables. A student read the safety report aloud, making sure the younger cleaners understood why the bottle had been saved. The report described what happened, what was checked, and what remained uncertain. One trainee asked whether they could forgive the worker for leaving it beside the feed. Yue said the worker should correct the mistake and learn how to store supplies. Forgiveness was not the same as pretending nothing had happened. The next step was to improve the storage system, so the same error would be less likely to occur.

午餐時，廚師端出美味的米飯與蔬菜。一名學生大聲朗讀安全報告，確保較年輕的清潔員明白瓶子為何被保存。報告說明事件經過、查驗內容與仍不確定的部分。一名見習生問能否原諒把瓶子留在飼料旁的人。月說那名員工應該改正錯誤，並學會妥善收納物資。原諒不等於假裝沒事。下一步是改善儲藏制度，降低同樣錯誤再發生的機會。

In the afternoon, a sudden burst of noise came from the visitor exit. A child had seen a painted shark on an aquarium poster and screamed. The sound frightened the birds in the courtyard. Chen closed the door between the visitor hall and the stable until the birds were calm. Yue realized that sound might be another cause of stress. She asked the staff to record moments when an animal changed its behavior after a loud noise. The question did not replace the evidence about straps. It added another factor that might matter during a busy trial.

下午，遊客出口突然爆出一聲尖叫。一個孩子看見水族館海報上的鯊魚圖案，嚇得大叫，院裡的鳥群也受驚。辰關上遊客廳與獸舍之間的門，直到鳥安靜。月想到聲音也可能造成壓力。她請員工記下動物在巨大聲響後改變行為的時刻。這個問題沒有取代鞍帶的證據，而是增加另一項在繁忙試煉期間可能重要的因素。

When the director came, Yue expressed her concern carefully. The storage problem had a clear solution, but animal stress required more observation. Chen showed him the written report and the proposed changes. The director approved separate cleaning shelves, better labels, and a quiet waiting area for animals before treatment. He did not approve any claim that the bottle had caused the refusals. Yue accepted that limit. If a case was built on facts, it had to include inconvenient facts as well as useful ones. The director left with a copy of the report and no dramatic confession to announce.

主任到來後，月謹慎表達憂慮。儲藏問題有明確解法，但動物壓力仍需更多觀察。辰給他看書面報告與改善建議。主任批准清潔用品分架存放、改進標籤，以及治療前讓動物待在安靜等候區。他沒有同意瓶子造成拒飛的說法。月接受這個界線。以事實建立的調查，必須包含不方便的事實，不能只挑有利的。主任帶著一份報告離開，沒有任何戲劇性的供詞可以宣布。

That night Yue returned to Moss and watched the animal breathe in the quiet stall. It was alive and more willing to move, but its shoulder still needed time. She wrote one new line in the care record: "Noise may affect rest; test without forcing flight." Chen read it and asked whether they had finally found the answer. Yue shook her head. Their work was not a story in which every clue pointed to a single hidden villain. Real care meant accepting several small causes, then changing the world around an animal one safe step at a time.

那晚月回到苔身旁，看著牠在安靜欄舍裡呼吸。苔活力漸增，也更願意走動，但肩膀仍需要時間。她在照護紀錄加上一行：「噪音可能影響休息；測試時不得強迫飛行。」辰問是否終於找到答案。月搖頭。他們的工作不是每條線索都指向同一個隱藏惡人的故事。真正照護是接受幾個小原因可能同時存在，再以安全的步驟，一點一點改變動物周遭的世界。
