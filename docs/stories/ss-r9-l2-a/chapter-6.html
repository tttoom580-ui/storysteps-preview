壞掉的救援車

The Broken Rescue Cart

聽不見咒語的靈獸醫師 · 第 6 / 25 章 · 1000–2000 · 英中對照 · 校訂中

用互動版查詞、朗讀與收藏

The spring market opened with music, food, and a badminton match in the central yard. Yue had come to speak with a neighbor who repaired carts. Instead, she found the clinic in a small crisis. Its rescue cart would not start. The engine made a rough sound, and a wheel moved only a little before stopping. The driver wanted to bring in a flying deer, but Moss was still resting. Yue asked for the repair worker and wrote the time of the failure in her journal. She would not let one broken machine become an excuse to use an injured animal.

春季市集在音樂、食物與中央庭院的羽球比賽中開幕。月原本要找一位會修車的鄰居談話，卻發現診所陷入小危機：救援車發不動了。引擎發出粗糙聲響，輪子只動了一下便停住。司機想改用飛鹿，但苔還在休養。月請人找修車師傅，並在日誌記下故障時間。她不願一台壞機器成為使受傷動物工作的藉口。

An excited child ran forward with a basket of cabbage leaves, hoping to feed the deer. Chen caught the basket before the child reached the stable. He explained that each animal had its own diet, and even a kind gift could cause harm. A clever nurse suggested moving the medicine in smaller boxes with two handcarts. Yue asked who could participate without leaving the clinic short of staff. A human problem needed a human plan. They could not govern animals through orders alone while ignoring the workers who cared for them every hour.

一名興奮的孩子抱著高麗菜葉跑來，想餵鹿。辰在孩子走到獸舍前接住籃子，解釋每頭動物都有自己的飲食，即使出於好意的禮物也可能造成傷害。聰明的護理師提議把藥品分成小箱，用兩台手推車運送。月詢問哪些人能參與，又不會讓診所缺人。人類的問題需要人類自己計畫。不能只向動物下命令，卻忽略整天照顧牠們的員工。

A firewoman from the city rescue station came to inspect the broken cart. She said the fault was not magical: the chain had slipped because the wheel had not been cleaned. She set forth a simple repair order and invited the trainees to watch. In the local culture, some people saw mechanical work as less noble than spells. Yue held the lamp while Chen cleaned the chain. Their hands became dirty, but the cart moved again. The firewoman asked the director to give every individual keeper basic equipment training, not just the strongest students. The repair affair did not look exciting, but it brought unexpected excitement to the trainees. They finally saw a rescue plan work without asking a tired beast to carry its cost.

城市救援站的一名女消防員來檢查壞車。她說故障與魔法無關：輪子長期沒清潔，導致鏈條滑脫。她提出簡單的維修順序，邀請見習生旁觀。當地文化中，有些人認為機械工作不如法術高貴。月替辰拿燈，辰清理鏈條。兩人的手弄髒了，但車終於又動起來。女消防員請主任讓每一位飼育員都學習基本設備操作，而不只是最強的學生。 維修這件事看起來不刺激，卻讓見習生意外興奮。他們終於看到一項救援計畫成功運作，不必由疲倦靈獸承擔代價。

During the repair, an old man joked that the broken cart belonged in a prison for bad machines. Nobody laughed much. The nurse had placed a soft pillow beneath Moss's head, and Yue was more concerned with that simple comfort than with jokes. A sock had caught on a nail near the clinic door, showing how easily small things were left in dangerous places. Yue picked it up and checked the floor for other hazards. Small daily work, she thought, held the whole place together. It rarely appeared in the records of grand competitions.

修理時，一位老人開玩笑說壞車應該關進「壞機器監獄」。大家笑得不多。護理師在苔頭下墊了柔軟枕頭，月比起笑話更在意這份簡單的舒適。診所門邊有隻襪子勾在釘子上，顯示小物件多容易留在危險處。月撿起襪子，檢查地面是否還有其他風險。她想，正是這些不起眼的日常工作支撐整個地方，只是它們很少出現在盛大的競賽紀錄裡。

At dusk, the two handcarts carried medicine to the clinic across the shore road without using a flying beast. The director watched the delivery return on time. It was slower than a flight in good weather, but the goods arrived safely. Yue gave the nurse the repair record, then asked her to examine Moss again the next day. She did not want the cart to become another device pushed beyond its limits. Chen agreed that every tool, like every animal, had a proper use and a limit. The head keeper had no answer when they showed him the completed delivery.

黃昏時，兩台手推車沿著岸邊道路把藥送到另一所診所，完全沒有動用飛行靈獸。主任親眼看見車隊準時回來。天氣良好時推車比飛行慢，但貨物安全抵達。月把維修紀錄交給護理師，再請她隔天重新檢查苔。她不希望推車又成為被迫超限使用的工具。辰同意，每項工具和每頭動物都有合適用途及界線。當他們展示完成的配送時，總飼育員一時無話可說。

That night Yue wrote one sentence at the bottom of her journal: a good rescue system should have choices. She added the names of everyone who had helped, including the driver, the firewoman, and the worker who lent the second cart. None of them had performed a great spell. Their work had still prevented a difficult choice between needed medicine and an injured deer. Chen asked if their small success would change the academy. Yue said she did not know. But tomorrow, when someone asked for a quick flight, they could offer a working cart instead of a silent animal.

那晚月在日誌末尾寫下一句：良好的救援制度應該有選擇。她記下所有幫忙的人，包括司機、女消防員與借出第二台車的員工。沒有人施展偉大法術，他們的工作卻避免了「需要藥物」和「逼受傷鹿飛行」之間的兩難。辰問這點小小成功是否能改變學院。月說她不知道。但明天若有人急著要求飛行，他們至少能提出一台正常運作的推車，而不是讓無聲的動物承擔一切。
