休息的價格

The Price of Rest

聽不見咒語的靈獸醫師 · 第 5 / 25 章 · 1000–2000 · 英中對照

用互動版查詞、朗讀與收藏

A letter arrived from the academy college, where students were preparing the spring display. The production team wanted twice as many deer flights because the event had become popular. Chen called the request a burden that no keeper should accept without checking the animals. He listed every new expense caused by rest: extra feed, care hours, and the cost of carts. Yue asked him to add the expense of injuries too. A budget could not be perfect, but leaving harm out of the calculation made it dishonest. They needed a plan that could function even if the main flight show changed.

學院附設的修習學院寄來信，學生正在準備春季展示。製作團隊因活動受歡迎，要求鹿群飛行次數加倍。辰說，沒檢查動物前，飼育員不該接受這種負擔。他列出休息造成的各項新費用：額外飼料、照護工時與推車成本。月請他也把受傷的代價算進去。預算不可能完美，但若故意不計傷害便不誠實。他們需要一套即使主飛行秀改變也能運作的方案。

A fellow student named Su had designed the event's best feature: visitors could learn how to care for different animals. The display was seldom crowded, yet many children remembered it. Yue asked Su to expand that part instead of adding flights. She proposed a selection of short talks about rest, food, and safe handling. Chen would explain the equipment tests, and the nurse would answer health questions. The plan had a different spirit from the old race. It invited people to observe the animals rather than demand that they perform for applause. Su smiled and began drawing new signs.

同學蘇設計了活動最受歡迎的特色：參觀者可以學習照護不同動物。那個展示區很少擁擠，許多孩子卻記得它。月請蘇擴大這一區，而不是增加飛行。她提議安排關於休息、飲食和安全接觸的短講。辰負責解釋裝備測試，護理師回答健康問題。這個計畫的精神和舊比賽不同：讓人觀察動物，而不是要求牠們為掌聲表演。蘇笑了，開始畫新告示。

At the food stand, an older lady watched a swallow circle above a soccer field. She wore a thick sweater although the weather was warm. She told Yue that workers used to sweep the yard before dawn and keep small pieces of tissue ready for cleaning wounds. Now the busy show schedule left little time for ordinary care. Yue listened while the woman described a worker who had tried to upload a safety report last winter. The file had failed because the office computer was broken. It was still on paper, forgotten inside a drawer.

餐攤旁，一位年長女士看著燕子繞著足球場飛。天氣暖和，她卻穿著厚毛衣。她告訴月，以前員工天亮前會掃院子，並備好少量紙巾清理小傷口。如今繁忙的表演行程讓日常照護幾乎沒有時間。月聽她說到，去年冬天有員工試圖上傳安全報告，但辦公室電腦壞了，檔案沒有傳出去。紙本仍被遺忘在某個抽屜裡。

The missing report mentioned a threatening message sent to the clinic. It used a word often seen in warnings about terrorist attacks, but its sender had never been identified. The director had treated the message as a reason to restrict access to the records. Yue said fear should not make every cleaner a suspect. She and Chen asked the records officer to preserve the original message, then requested supervised access to the safety papers. They did not copy personal details or publish accusations. Their goal was to learn whether the same strap problem had appeared before the spring trial.

遺失的報告提到診所收到一則恐嚇訊息，內文使用了常見於恐怖攻擊警告的字眼，卻始終未查出寄件者。主任因此限制員工查看紀錄。月說恐懼不應讓每名清潔員都變成嫌疑人。她和辰請檔案員保存原訊息，再申請在監督下查閱安全文件。他們不複製個資，也不公開指控。目的是確認相同鞍帶問題是否在春季試煉前便已出現。

In a quiet room, they found last winter's report behind a stack of event posters. It listed five animals that had refused heavy saddles after a new shipment arrived. The writer had asked for more careful fitting, but nobody had answered. Chen read the page twice. He had chosen the supplier this year because he thought it was reliable. Yue did not ask him to blame himself forever. She asked whether he was willing to bring the report to the director with her. He said yes, though his hands shook when he folded the paper. She had once believed a full schedule meant good management. Now she understood that an honest empty space could be a necessary form of care.

在安靜的房間裡，他們從一疊活動海報後找到去年冬天的報告。上面列出五頭動物在新裝備到貨後拒絕穿重鞍。撰寫者要求更仔細地調整尺寸，卻沒有收到回覆。辰把那頁讀了兩遍。今年正是他選了這家供應商，因為以為它可靠。月沒有要他永遠自責，只問他願不願和她一起把報告交給主任。辰說願意，儘管摺紙時雙手仍在發抖。 她曾相信排滿的時程就是良好管理，如今明白，誠實留下的空白也可能是必要照護。

The director studied both the old report and the new measurements. He canceled the plan to double the flights, but kept the care display and one safe demonstration under review. Yue felt the change mattered. Visitors might be disappointed by fewer flying animals, yet they could leave with a useful understanding of how living creatures were treated. Outside, Su painted a new sign: "Rest is part of training." Moss stood beneath the sign, eating slowly. For now, that quiet sight was enough to make Yue believe their work could continue.

主任仔細閱讀舊報告與新測量結果。他取消飛行加倍計畫，保留照護展示，並將一項安全示範列為待審。月覺得這個改變很重要。遊客也許會因飛行表演減少而失望，卻可能因此理解如何對待生命。屋外，蘇畫了一塊新標語：「休息也是訓練的一部分。」苔站在牌下慢慢進食。至少此刻，這幅平靜景象已足夠讓月相信他們能繼續做下去。
