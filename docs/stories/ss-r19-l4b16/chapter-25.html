Months later Wen returned home at nine, turned on a lamp, and found only the dinner she had prepared that morning. Something had changed, but not everybody noticed it in the same order. Wen Yi listened to the account of the nearest worker, then to someone standing farther away. Their differences were not necessarily lies. Each person had been given a different view and a different reason to be afraid of admitting uncertainty. Han Lu preserved the original file and its metadata before discussing theories. A changed picture could frighten people; it could not change when a cable had been connected. One witness used the word broke; Han Lu noted that a description was not a measurement. A handwritten label read championship, but no one could say who had written it there.

數月後聞怡九點回家，開了燈，房裡只有早上準備好的晚餐。某件事改變了，但人們發現它的順序不同。聞怡先聽近處工作人員的描述，再聽遠處旁觀者說法。差異不一定是謊言：每個人看到的角度不同，承認不確定時所要承擔的風險也不同。韓律在討論理論前先封存原始檔與中繼資料。被修改的畫面能嚇人，卻不能改變線纜接通的時間。一位證人用了「身無分文的」一詞，韓律指出形容並不等於測量。手寫標籤寫著「錦標賽」，卻沒有人知道是誰留下的。

The case proceeded on evidence rather than audience votes, while victims sought privacy and compensation. Wen Yi refused to pretend the previous decision had solved everything. The practical result was still there, useful but narrow. For each thing the team knew, there were two things it had not measured. Wen Yi drew a line through the claims that depended on speculation and asked Han Lu to keep the original record intact. The term concerning appeared in the revised file; Wen Yi checked its meaning against the original page. Han Lu noticed the word electronics in a note that someone had added after the first inspection. A handwritten label read ethnic, but no one could say who had written it there.

案件依證據進行，不依觀眾投票；受害者則爭取隱私與補償。聞怡不願假裝前一次決定已解決所有問題。成果確實存在，卻有明確界限。每個已知事項旁都還有未測量的部分。聞怡劃掉靠猜測成立的說法，請韓律保留原始紀錄。修訂檔案出現「關於」一詞，聞怡拿原始頁面核對它的意思。韓律注意到初次檢查後有人在筆記裡加上「電子設備」一詞。手寫標籤寫著「族群文化的」，卻沒有人知道是誰留下的。

A small disagreement changed the schedule. Wen Yi wanted to proceed before evidence disappeared; Han Lu wanted another check because a wrong conclusion might hurt people who could not easily defend themselves. They did not settle the matter by choosing a leader. They wrote down what a short, reversible test could actually establish. An official memo described the plan as fantastic, but Wen Yi refused to treat an adjective as evidence. The term feast appeared in the revised file; Wen Yi checked its meaning against the original page. Han Lu noticed the word finance in a note that someone had added after the first inspection.

一場小爭執改變了時程。聞怡想趁證據消失前行動；韓律要求再核對一次，因為錯誤結論可能傷害難以自辯的人。他們沒有靠選領袖解決爭議，而是列出短暫、可逆測試真正能證明什麼。官方備忘錄形容計畫「奇幻的」，聞怡不願把形容詞當作證據。修訂檔案出現「盛宴」一詞，聞怡拿原始頁面核對它的意思。韓律注意到初次檢查後有人在筆記裡加上「財務」一詞。

Han sent a message that another case had been solved without livestreams, and she finally let the ordinary evening remain ordinary. The plan had been amended three times, and each change left a trace. Wen Yi valued those corrections more than a perfect-looking first draft. If the outcome proved disappointing, the group would still be able to identify which assumption needed repair. Han Lu watched for the quiet participant who had not yet been invited to speak. One witness used the word guilty; Han Lu noted that a description was not a measurement. Han Lu repeated the instruction to impose, then asked whether anyone had the right to refuse. The term offense appeared in the revised file; Wen Yi checked its meaning against the original page.

韓律傳訊說另案也不靠直播破案了，她終於讓平凡夜晚只是平凡夜晚。計畫改了三次，每次修改都留下痕跡。聞怡認為這些修正比看似完美的初稿更有價值。就算結果不理想，團隊仍能知道該修正哪一項假設。韓律也注意到那名一直沒有發言機會的人。一位證人用了「有罪的」一詞，韓律指出形容並不等於測量。韓律重述「強加」的指示，再問當事人是否有權拒絕。修訂檔案出現「罪行」一詞，聞怡拿原始頁面核對它的意思。

An official version reached the room before the practical work was finished. Wen Yi read it once, then compared it with what they had actually measured. The difference was not always malicious, but it could still hurt somebody. Han Lu asked that the correction be written in language the affected people could understand. 實際工作還沒完成，官方版本就傳到現場。聞怡讀了一次，再比對真正測得的結果。差異未必都是惡意，卻仍可能傷害人。韓律要求把更正寫成受影響者看得懂的語言。韓律在討論理論前先封存原始檔與中繼資料。被修改的畫面能嚇人，卻不能改變線纜接通的時間。修訂檔案出現「審查小組」一詞，聞怡拿原始頁面核對它的意思。一位證人用了「有毒的」一詞，韓律指出形容並不等於測量。韓律重述「保存」的指示，再問當事人是否有權拒絕。

A witness asked whether the team was taking sides. Wen Yi answered that refusing to invent a fact was not the same as refusing to care. What mattered was whether the procedure protected someone whose story was less convenient to tell. Han Lu listened without interrupting and wrote down the question exactly as it had been asked. A handwritten label read profession, but no one could say who had written it there. The term publication appeared in the revised file; Wen Yi checked its meaning against the original page. Han Lu noticed the word register in a note that someone had added after the first inspection.

一名見證人問團隊是否偏袒。聞怡回答，不捏造事實不代表冷漠。真正重要的是程序能否保護那個故事不方便被說出口的人。韓律沒有打斷，逐字記下原問題。手寫標籤寫著「職業」，卻沒有人知道是誰留下的。修訂檔案出現「出版品」一詞，聞怡拿原始頁面核對它的意思。韓律注意到初次檢查後有人在筆記裡加上「登記」一詞。

At nine o'clock Wen Yi turned on her kitchen lamp. The apartment held no corpse, no threatening script, only the dinner she had prepared that morning. In the courtyard a neighbor was gathering tools after rain, his spade leaning against the wall of a small shelter. A pet tortoise had escaped from its pen and was examining the pavement with grave interest; the neighbor's daughter picked it up while the washing on the line began to sway. Wen watched for a moment, then closed the curtain. Han Lu's message arrived: the last scheduled upload had been removed under a documented order, and the new evidence register was intact. Wen put the phone face down. The next case would come with its own facts. Tonight nobody had permission to turn an ordinary room into a prediction, and she intended to eat while the food was warm.

九點鐘，聞怡打開廚房燈。公寓裡沒有屍體，沒有恐嚇劇本，只有她早上準備的晚餐。院子裡鄰居雨後收工具，鏟子靠在小遮雨棚牆邊。一隻寵物陸龜溜出圍欄，認真研究地板；鄰居的女兒抱起牠，曬衣繩上的衣服在風中搖動。聞怡看了一會兒，拉上窗簾。韓律傳來訊息：最後一段排程上傳已依正式命令移除，新證據登記簿也完整保留。聞怡把手機翻過來放。下一件案子會有自己的事實。今晚沒有人有權把普通房間變成預言，她只打算趁飯菜還熱時好好吃飯。
