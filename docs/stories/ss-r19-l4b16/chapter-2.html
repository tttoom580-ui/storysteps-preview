Wen and digital examiner Han Lu documented the apartment's furniture without telling the residents which detail the forecast video showed. Something had changed, but not everybody noticed it in the same order. Wen Yi listened to the account of the nearest worker, then to someone standing farther away. Their differences were not necessarily lies. Each person had been given a different view and a different reason to be afraid of admitting uncertainty. A label marked TOMORROW on a clip did not make the event inevitable. Wen Yi checked the date on each physical door and each receipt instead. The term atom appeared in the revised file; Wen Yi checked its meaning against the original page. One witness used the word atomic; Han Lu noted that a description was not a measurement.

聞怡與數位鑑識員韓律記錄公寓傢俱，沒有告訴住戶預告影片拍到什麼細節。某件事改變了，但人們發現它的順序不同。聞怡先聽近處工作人員的描述，再聽遠處旁觀者說法。差異不一定是謊言：每個人看到的角度不同，承認不確定時所要承擔的風險也不同。影片標上「明天」不代表事情必然發生。聞怡改去核對實體門禁和收據上的每一個日期。修訂檔案出現「原子」一詞，聞怡拿原始頁面核對它的意思。一位證人用了「原子能的」一詞，韓律指出形容並不等於測量。

She sealed the recording before visiting the address, where the supposed victim was still alive and denied knowing the camera. Wen Yi refused to pretend the previous decision had solved everything. The practical result was still there, useful but narrow. For each thing the team knew, there were two things it had not measured. Wen Yi drew a line through the claims that depended on speculation and asked Han Lu to keep the original record intact. A handwritten label read combination, but no one could say who had written it there. The summary called the situation delicate; Wen Yi asked which observation justified that description. Han Lu noticed the word extent in a note that someone had added after the first inspection.

她先封存影片才前往地址，片中死者仍活著，也不認識那支攝影機。聞怡不願假裝前一次決定已解決所有問題。成果確實存在，卻有明確界限。每個已知事項旁都還有未測量的部分。聞怡劃掉靠猜測成立的說法，請韓律保留原始紀錄。手寫標籤寫著「組合」，卻沒有人知道是誰留下的。摘要以「微弱的」形容情況，聞怡追問這種說法有何觀察根據。韓律注意到初次檢查後有人在筆記裡加上「程度」一詞。

A small disagreement changed the schedule. Wen Yi wanted to proceed before evidence disappeared; Han Lu wanted another check because a wrong conclusion might hurt people who could not easily defend themselves. They did not settle the matter by choosing a leader. They wrote down what a short, reversible test could actually establish. Han Lu pointed to the fort in the inventory and asked why its location had changed. The earlier photograph clearly showed a fossil; the newer version did not, so Wen Yi kept both. The term gaze appeared in the revised file; Wen Yi checked its meaning against the original page.

一場小爭執改變了時程。聞怡想趁證據消失前行動；韓律要求再核對一次，因為錯誤結論可能傷害難以自辯的人。他們沒有靠選領袖解決爭議，而是列出短暫、可逆測試真正能證明什麼。韓律指向清單上的「堡壘」，追問其位置為何改變。較早的照片清楚顯示一項「化石」，新版卻沒有，聞怡因此保留兩張。修訂檔案出現「凝視」一詞，聞怡拿原始頁面核對它的意思。

The real room lacked a distinctive red chair from the livestream, suggesting the footage was staged or altered. The plan had been amended three times, and each change left a trace. Wen Yi valued those corrections more than a perfect-looking first draft. If the outcome proved disappointing, the group would still be able to identify which assumption needed repair. Han Lu watched for the quiet participant who had not yet been invited to speak. The term guarantee appeared in the revised file; Wen Yi checked its meaning against the original page. Han Lu noticed the word immigration in a note that someone had added after the first inspection. A handwritten label read intimate, but no one could say who had written it there.

實際房間沒有影片裡醒目的紅椅子，暗示畫面被布置或修改。計畫改了三次，每次修改都留下痕跡。聞怡認為這些修正比看似完美的初稿更有價值。就算結果不理想，團隊仍能知道該修正哪一項假設。韓律也注意到那名一直沒有發言機會的人。修訂檔案出現「保證」一詞，聞怡拿原始頁面核對它的意思。韓律注意到初次檢查後有人在筆記裡加上「入出境」一詞。手寫標籤寫著「私密的」，卻沒有人知道是誰留下的。

An official version reached the room before the practical work was finished. Wen Yi read it once, then compared it with what they had actually measured. The difference was not always malicious, but it could still hurt somebody. Han Lu asked that the correction be written in language the affected people could understand. A label marked TOMORROW on a clip did not make the event inevitable. Wen Yi checked the date on each physical door and each receipt instead. The earlier photograph clearly showed a landscape; the newer version did not, so Wen Yi kept both. A note about the messenger lay beside the old register, with a date that Wen Yi could not verify. Han Lu pointed to the mill in the inventory and asked why its location had changed.

實際工作還沒完成，官方版本就傳到現場。聞怡讀了一次，再比對真正測得的結果。差異未必都是惡意，卻仍可能傷害人。韓律要求把更正寫成受影響者看得懂的語言。影片標上「明天」不代表事情必然發生。聞怡改去核對實體門禁和收據上的每一個日期。較早的照片清楚顯示一項「風景布景」，新版卻沒有，聞怡因此保留兩張。一張談到「送件員」的便條放在舊登記簿旁，日期讓聞怡無法核實。韓律指向清單上的「磨坊」，追問其位置為何改變。

A witness asked whether the team was taking sides. Wen Yi answered that refusing to invent a fact was not the same as refusing to care. What mattered was whether the procedure protected someone whose story was less convenient to tell. Han Lu listened without interrupting and wrote down the question exactly as it had been asked. On the next form, the action field read murmur, but the witness had never approved the operation. An official memo described the plan as productive, but Wen Yi refused to treat an adjective as evidence. The summary called the situation psychological; Wen Yi asked which observation justified that description.

一名見證人問團隊是否偏袒。聞怡回答，不捏造事實不代表冷漠。真正重要的是程序能否保護那個故事不方便被說出口的人。韓律沒有打斷，逐字記下原問題。下一張表單把動作寫成「低語」，見證人卻從未同意這項操作。官方備忘錄形容計畫「有成果的」，聞怡不願把形容詞當作證據。摘要以「心理上的」形容情況，聞怡追問這種說法有何觀察根據。

Even after the group agreed on the immediate step, another question remained. The answer would not arrive through a dramatic announcement. Someone would have to return to the place, compare the earlier notes, and accept the possibility that the next piece of evidence might spoil an attractive theory. Wen Yi prepared to do exactly that. Han Lu preserved the original file and its metadata before discussing theories. A changed picture could frighten people; it could not change when a cable had been connected. The instructions used the verb retain without naming who would carry the risk; Wen Yi circled that gap. On the next form, the action field read revise, but the witness had never approved the operation. A handwritten label read voyage, but no one could say who had written it there.

即使大家同意眼前步驟，另一個問題仍在。答案不會靠戲劇性公告降臨；有人得返回現場、比對舊筆記，並接受新證據可能推翻漂亮理論。聞怡準備做的正是這件事。韓律在討論理論前先封存原始檔與中繼資料。被修改的畫面能嚇人，卻不能改變線纜接通的時間。指示裡用了「保留」這個動詞，卻沒說誰承擔風險；聞怡圈出這項缺漏。下一張表單把動作寫成「修改」，見證人卻從未同意這項操作。手寫標籤寫著「航程」，卻沒有人知道是誰留下的。
