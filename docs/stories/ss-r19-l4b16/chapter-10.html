At nine, the preview appeared to show a body in Wen's apartment. She confirmed her actual home was empty and secured the entry logs. Something had changed, but not everybody noticed it in the same order. Wen Yi listened to the account of the nearest worker, then to someone standing farther away. Their differences were not necessarily lies. Each person had been given a different view and a different reason to be afraid of admitting uncertainty. A label marked TOMORROW on a clip did not make the event inevitable. Wen Yi checked the date on each physical door and each receipt instead. Han Lu noticed the word apology in a note that someone had added after the first inspection. The earlier photograph clearly showed a bandage; the newer version did not, so Wen Yi kept both.

九點預告似乎顯示聞怡家裡有屍體，她確認住處當下沒人並封存出入紀錄。某件事改變了，但人們發現它的順序不同。聞怡先聽近處工作人員的描述，再聽遠處旁觀者說法。差異不一定是謊言：每個人看到的角度不同，承認不確定時所要承擔的風險也不同。影片標上「明天」不代表事情必然發生。聞怡改去核對實體門禁和收據上的每一個日期。韓律注意到初次檢查後有人在筆記裡加上「道歉」一詞。較早的照片清楚顯示一項「繃帶」，新版卻沒有，聞怡因此保留兩張。

She transferred public communication to a colleague so she could investigate without answering strangers' demands for spectacle. Wen Yi refused to pretend the previous decision had solved everything. The practical result was still there, useful but narrow. For each thing the team knew, there were two things it had not measured. Wen Yi drew a line through the claims that depended on speculation and asked Han Lu to keep the original record intact. The term bargain appeared in the revised file; Wen Yi checked its meaning against the original page. Han Lu noticed the word civilization in a note that someone had added after the first inspection. Han Lu repeated the instruction to congratulate, then asked whether anyone had the right to refuse.

她將對外溝通交給同事，避免被迫回應陌生人對戲劇化場面的要求。聞怡不願假裝前一次決定已解決所有問題。成果確實存在，卻有明確界限。每個已知事項旁都還有未測量的部分。聞怡劃掉靠猜測成立的說法，請韓律保留原始紀錄。修訂檔案出現「交易」一詞，聞怡拿原始頁面核對它的意思。韓律注意到初次檢查後有人在筆記裡加上「文明」一詞。韓律重述「恭賀」的指示，再問當事人是否有權拒絕。

A small disagreement changed the schedule. Wen Yi wanted to proceed before evidence disappeared; Han Lu wanted another check because a wrong conclusion might hurt people who could not easily defend themselves. They did not settle the matter by choosing a leader. They wrote down what a short, reversible test could actually establish. An official memo described the plan as critical, but Wen Yi refused to treat an adjective as evidence. The term explosion appeared in the revised file; Wen Yi checked its meaning against the original page. On the next form, the action field read forbid, but the witness had never approved the operation.

一場小爭執改變了時程。聞怡想趁證據消失前行動；韓律要求再核對一次，因為錯誤結論可能傷害難以自辯的人。他們沒有靠選領袖解決爭議，而是列出短暫、可逆測試真正能證明什麼。官方備忘錄形容計畫「關鍵的」，聞怡不願把形容詞當作證據。修訂檔案出現「爆炸」一詞，聞怡拿原始頁面核對它的意思。下一張表單把動作寫成「禁止」，見證人卻從未同意這項操作。

The feed contained a detail from a police-only evidence room, proving someone with privileged access was supplying the production. The plan had been amended three times, and each change left a trace. Wen Yi valued those corrections more than a perfect-looking first draft. If the outcome proved disappointing, the group would still be able to identify which assumption needed repair. Han Lu watched for the quiet participant who had not yet been invited to speak. On the next form, the action field read illustrate, but the witness had never approved the operation. Han Lu repeated the instruction to install, then asked whether anyone had the right to refuse. The summary called the situation mere; Wen Yi asked which observation justified that description.

畫面出現僅警方證物室才有的細節，證明有人利用特殊權限供應素材。計畫改了三次，每次修改都留下痕跡。聞怡認為這些修正比看似完美的初稿更有價值。就算結果不理想，團隊仍能知道該修正哪一項假設。韓律也注意到那名一直沒有發言機會的人。下一張表單把動作寫成「圖解說明」，見證人卻從未同意這項操作。韓律重述「安裝」的指示，再問當事人是否有權拒絕。摘要以「僅僅的」形容情況，聞怡追問這種說法有何觀察根據。

An official version reached the room before the practical work was finished. Wen Yi read it once, then compared it with what they had actually measured. The difference was not always malicious, but it could still hurt somebody. Han Lu asked that the correction be written in language the affected people could understand. A label marked TOMORROW on a clip did not make the event inevitable. Wen Yi checked the date on each physical door and each receipt instead. The summary called the situation pregnant; Wen Yi asked which observation justified that description. One witness used the word prosperous; Han Lu noted that a description was not a measurement. A handwritten label read resolution, but no one could say who had written it there.

實際工作還沒完成，官方版本就傳到現場。聞怡讀了一次，再比對真正測得的結果。差異未必都是惡意，卻仍可能傷害人。韓律要求把更正寫成受影響者看得懂的語言。影片標上「明天」不代表事情必然發生。聞怡改去核對實體門禁和收據上的每一個日期。摘要以「懷孕的」形容情況，聞怡追問這種說法有何觀察根據。一位證人用了「繁榮的」一詞，韓律指出形容並不等於測量。手寫標籤寫著「決議」，卻沒有人知道是誰留下的。

A witness asked whether the team was taking sides. Wen Yi answered that refusing to invent a fact was not the same as refusing to care. What mattered was whether the procedure protected someone whose story was less convenient to tell. Han Lu listened without interrupting and wrote down the question exactly as it had been asked. A handwritten label read revolution, but no one could say who had written it there. The term settler appeared in the revised file; Wen Yi checked its meaning against the original page. Han Lu noticed the word surgeon in a note that someone had added after the first inspection.

一名見證人問團隊是否偏袒。聞怡回答，不捏造事實不代表冷漠。真正重要的是程序能否保護那個故事不方便被說出口的人。韓律沒有打斷，逐字記下原問題。手寫標籤寫著「革命」，卻沒有人知道是誰留下的。修訂檔案出現「遷居者」一詞，聞怡拿原始頁面核對它的意思。韓律注意到初次檢查後有人在筆記裡加上「外科醫師」一詞。

A suspicious update appeared on the prediction website while Wen Yi stood in her own apartment, checking every window against the live image. The stream included a model tomb from a movie set, a prop she had never kept at home. That error should have been comforting; instead she understood the danger. Whoever made the footage possessed a copy of police evidence and knew enough about her address to stage the room convincingly. Han Lu sent a message: the prop had been checked out by a production company using a stolen technician's card. Wen opened her hallway camera record, preserving the file before watching it. A stranger had stood at her door that afternoon, holding a flat equipment box. The box was stamped with the same red logo as the prediction channel.

聞怡站在自己家裡，逐一比對窗戶與直播畫面時，預言網站跳出可疑更新。直播裡有一座電影布景用的模型古墓，她家從來沒放過這道具。錯誤本該讓人安心，她卻因此更明白危險：製作影片的人握有警方案件資料，也知道她的地址，足以把房間布置得像真的。韓律傳來訊息：那件道具被某製作公司用失竊技師卡借走。聞怡先封存自家走廊攝影機原檔才點開。下午，一名陌生人拿著扁平器材箱站在她門外。箱子上印著預言頻道相同的紅色標誌。
