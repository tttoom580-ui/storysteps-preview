A staged stream described a street robbery before one occurred, but evidence showed a hired accomplice had been sent to make the clip appear prophetic. Something had changed, but not everybody noticed it in the same order. Wen Yi listened to the account of the nearest worker, then to someone standing farther away. Their differences were not necessarily lies. Each person had been given a different view and a different reason to be afraid of admitting uncertainty. Han Lu preserved the original file and its metadata before discussing theories. A changed picture could frighten people; it could not change when a cable had been connected. A handwritten label read analysis, but no one could say who had written it there. The summary called the situation appropriate; Wen Yi asked which observation justified that description.

一段造假預告先描述街頭搶劫，之後果然發生，證據卻顯示有人雇共犯製造預言成真。某件事改變了，但人們發現它的順序不同。聞怡先聽近處工作人員的描述，再聽遠處旁觀者說法。差異不一定是謊言：每個人看到的角度不同，承認不確定時所要承擔的風險也不同。韓律在討論理論前先封存原始檔與中繼資料。被修改的畫面能嚇人，卻不能改變線纜接通的時間。手寫標籤寫著「分析」，卻沒有人知道是誰留下的。摘要以「適當的」形容情況，聞怡追問這種說法有何觀察根據。

A carbon copy in the equipment warehouse supplied the missing times and identified two crew members at the studio. Wen Yi refused to pretend the previous decision had solved everything. The practical result was still there, useful but narrow. For each thing the team knew, there were two things it had not measured. Wen Yi drew a line through the claims that depended on speculation and asked Han Lu to keep the original record intact. A small disagreement changed the schedule. Wen Yi wanted to proceed before evidence disappeared; Han Lu wanted another check because a wrong conclusion might hurt people who could not easily defend themselves. They did not settle the matter by choosing a leader. They wrote down what a short, reversible test could actually establish. A handwritten label read dispute, but no one could say who had written it there.

一場小爭執改變了時程。聞怡想趁證據消失前行動；韓律要求再核對一次，因為錯誤結論可能傷害難以自辯的人。他們沒有靠選領袖解決爭議，而是列出短暫、可逆測試真正能證明什麼。修訂檔案出現「對比」一詞，聞怡拿原始頁面核對它的意思。韓律注意到初次檢查後有人在筆記裡加上「裂縫」一詞。手寫標籤寫著「爭議」，卻沒有人知道是誰留下的。

Wen Yi valued those corrections more than a perfect-looking first draft. If the outcome proved disappointing, the group would still be able to identify which assumption needed repair. Han Lu watched for the quiet participant who had not yet been invited to speak. Han Lu repeated the instruction to endure, then asked whether anyone had the right to refuse. An official version reached the room before the practical work was finished. Wen Yi read it once, then compared it with what they had actually measured. The difference was not always malicious, but it could still hurt somebody. Han Lu asked that the correction be written in language the affected people could understand. 實際工作還沒完成，官方版本就傳到現場。聞怡讀了一次，再比對真正測得的結果。差異未必都是惡意，卻仍可能傷害人。韓律要求把更正寫成受影響者看得懂的語言。韓律在討論理論前先封存原始檔與中繼資料。被修改的畫面能嚇人，卻不能改變線纜接通的時間。韓律注意到初次檢查後有人在筆記裡加上「測量」一詞。手寫標籤寫著「最低限度」，卻沒有人知道是誰留下的。修訂檔案出現「監測」一詞，聞怡拿原始頁面核對它的意思。

A witness asked whether the team was taking sides. Wen Yi answered that refusing to invent a fact was not the same as refusing to care. What mattered was whether the procedure protected someone whose story was less convenient to tell. Han Lu listened without interrupting and wrote down the question exactly as it had been asked. The term noble appeared in the revised file; Wen Yi checked its meaning against the original page. On the next form, the action field read overlook, but the witness had never approved the operation. People disagreed about participation; Wen Yi separated the feeling from the facts they could test.

一名見證人問團隊是否偏袒。聞怡回答，不捏造事實不代表冷漠。真正重要的是程序能否保護那個故事不方便被說出口的人。韓律沒有打斷，逐字記下原問題。修訂檔案出現「高尚的」一詞，聞怡拿原始頁面核對它的意思。下一張表單把動作寫成「忽略」，見證人卻從未同意這項操作。大家對「參與」意見不同，聞怡把感受與可檢驗的事實分開。

Even after the group agreed on the immediate step, another question remained. The answer would not arrive through a dramatic announcement. Someone would have to return to the place, compare the earlier notes, and accept the possibility that the next piece of evidence might spoil an attractive theory. Wen Yi prepared to do exactly that. A label marked TOMORROW on a clip did not make the event inevitable. Wen Yi checked the date on each physical door and each receipt instead. A handwritten label read potential, but no one could say who had written it there. The instructions used the verb tolerate without naming who would carry the risk; Wen Yi circled that gap. One witness used the word vain; Han Lu noted that a description was not a measurement.

即使大家同意眼前步驟，另一個問題仍在。答案不會靠戲劇性公告降臨；有人得返回現場、比對舊筆記，並接受新證據可能推翻漂亮理論。聞怡準備做的正是這件事。影片標上「明天」不代表事情必然發生。聞怡改去核對實體門禁和收據上的每一個日期。手寫標籤寫著「可能的」，卻沒有人知道是誰留下的。指示裡用了「容忍」這個動詞，卻沒說誰承擔風險；聞怡圈出這項缺漏。一位證人用了「徒勞的」一詞，韓律指出形容並不等於測量。
