Wen Yi listened to the account of the nearest worker, then to someone standing farther away. Their differences were not necessarily lies. Each person had been given a different view and a different reason to be afraid of admitting uncertainty. Han Lu preserved the original file and its metadata before discussing theories. A changed picture could frighten people; it could not change when a cable had been connected. The instructions used the verb abandon without naming who would carry the risk; Wen Yi circled that gap. Even after the group agreed on the immediate step, another question remained. The answer would not arrive through a dramatic announcement.
