Investigators found that the studio used a physical lock and a separate electronic control, both with independent logs. Something had changed, but not everybody noticed it in the same order. Wen Yi listened to the account of the nearest worker, then to someone standing farther away. Their differences were not necessarily lies. Each person had been given a different view and a different reason to be afraid of admitting uncertainty. A label marked TOMORROW on a clip did not make the event inevitable. Wen Yi checked the date on each physical door and each receipt instead. The summary called the situation abstract; Wen Yi asked which observation justified that description. On the next form, the action field read acquire, but the witness had never approved the operation.

調查員發現攝影棚同時使用實體鎖與電子控制，兩者各有獨立日誌。某件事改變了，但人們發現它的順序不同。聞怡先聽近處工作人員的描述，再聽遠處旁觀者說法。差異不一定是謊言：每個人看到的角度不同，承認不確定時所要承擔的風險也不同。影片標上「明天」不代表事情必然發生。聞怡改去核對實體門禁和收據上的每一個日期。摘要以「抽象的」形容情況，聞怡追問這種說法有何觀察根據。下一張表單把動作寫成「取得」，見證人卻從未同意這項操作。

The designer's death involved a real injury, but the video had hidden who entered the studio before it occurred. Wen Yi refused to pretend the previous decision had solved everything. The practical result was still there, useful but narrow. For each thing the team knew, there were two things it had not measured. Wen Yi drew a line through the claims that depended on speculation and asked Han Lu to keep the original record intact. A handwritten label read bridegroom, but no one could say who had written it there. The term bulletin appeared in the revised file; Wen Yi checked its meaning against the original page. Han Lu noticed the word civilian in a note that someone had added after the first inspection.

設計師確實受致命傷，影片卻藏住案發前誰進入工作室。聞怡不願假裝前一次決定已解決所有問題。成果確實存在，卻有明確界限。每個已知事項旁都還有未測量的部分。聞怡劃掉靠猜測成立的說法，請韓律保留原始紀錄。手寫標籤寫著「新郎」，卻沒有人知道是誰留下的。修訂檔案出現「公告」一詞，聞怡拿原始頁面核對它的意思。韓律注意到初次檢查後有人在筆記裡加上「平民」一詞。

A small disagreement changed the schedule. Wen Yi wanted to proceed before evidence disappeared; Han Lu wanted another check because a wrong conclusion might hurt people who could not easily defend themselves. They did not settle the matter by choosing a leader. They wrote down what a short, reversible test could actually establish. Han Lu noticed the word composer in a note that someone had added after the first inspection. Han Lu repeated the instruction to constitute, then asked whether anyone had the right to refuse. The term cue appeared in the revised file; Wen Yi checked its meaning against the original page.

一場小爭執改變了時程。聞怡想趁證據消失前行動；韓律要求再核對一次，因為錯誤結論可能傷害難以自辯的人。他們沒有靠選領袖解決爭議，而是列出短暫、可逆測試真正能證明什麼。韓律注意到初次檢查後有人在筆記裡加上「作曲家」一詞。韓律重述「構成」的指示，再問當事人是否有權拒絕。修訂檔案出現「提示音」一詞，聞怡拿原始頁面核對它的意思。

A mismatch in the two records placed a contractor inside when the victim supposedly stood alone. The plan had been amended three times, and each change left a trace. Wen Yi valued those corrections more than a perfect-looking first draft. If the outcome proved disappointing, the group would still be able to identify which assumption needed repair. Han Lu watched for the quiet participant who had not yet been invited to speak. The instructions used the verb dominate without naming who would carry the risk; Wen Yi circled that gap. Han Lu noticed the word enthusiasm in a note that someone had added after the first inspection. A handwritten label read estimate, but no one could say who had written it there.

兩組紀錄不一致，顯示死者據稱獨處時有承包商在場。計畫改了三次，每次修改都留下痕跡。聞怡認為這些修正比看似完美的初稿更有價值。就算結果不理想，團隊仍能知道該修正哪一項假設。韓律也注意到那名一直沒有發言機會的人。指示裡用了「主導」這個動詞，卻沒說誰承擔風險；聞怡圈出這項缺漏。韓律注意到初次檢查後有人在筆記裡加上「熱情」一詞。手寫標籤寫著「估計」，卻沒有人知道是誰留下的。

An official version reached the room before the practical work was finished. Wen Yi read it once, then compared it with what they had actually measured. The difference was not always malicious, but it could still hurt somebody. Han Lu asked that the correction be written in language the affected people could understand. A label marked TOMORROW on a clip did not make the event inevitable. Wen Yi checked the date on each physical door and each receipt instead. An official memo described the plan as fertile, but Wen Yi refused to treat an adjective as evidence. The term gender appeared in the revised file; Wen Yi checked its meaning against the original page. Han Lu noticed the word gulf in a note that someone had added after the first inspection.

實際工作還沒完成，官方版本就傳到現場。聞怡讀了一次，再比對真正測得的結果。差異未必都是惡意，卻仍可能傷害人。韓律要求把更正寫成受影響者看得懂的語言。影片標上「明天」不代表事情必然發生。聞怡改去核對實體門禁和收據上的每一個日期。官方備忘錄形容計畫「適合滋生的」，聞怡不願把形容詞當作證據。修訂檔案出現「性別」一詞，聞怡拿原始頁面核對它的意思。韓律注意到初次檢查後有人在筆記裡加上「海灣」一詞。

A witness asked whether the team was taking sides. Wen Yi answered that refusing to invent a fact was not the same as refusing to care. What mattered was whether the procedure protected someone whose story was less convenient to tell. Han Lu listened without interrupting and wrote down the question exactly as it had been asked. Han Lu noticed the word intensity in a note that someone had added after the first inspection. Han Lu repeated the instruction to invade, then asked whether anyone had the right to refuse. The term jealousy appeared in the revised file; Wen Yi checked its meaning against the original page.

一名見證人問團隊是否偏袒。聞怡回答，不捏造事實不代表冷漠。真正重要的是程序能否保護那個故事不方便被說出口的人。韓律沒有打斷，逐字記下原問題。韓律注意到初次檢查後有人在筆記裡加上「強度」一詞。韓律重述「入侵」的指示，再問當事人是否有權拒絕。修訂檔案出現「嫉妒」一詞，聞怡拿原始頁面核對它的意思。

Even after the group agreed on the immediate step, another question remained. The answer would not arrive through a dramatic announcement. Someone would have to return to the place, compare the earlier notes, and accept the possibility that the next piece of evidence might spoil an attractive theory. Wen Yi prepared to do exactly that. Han Lu preserved the original file and its metadata before discussing theories. A changed picture could frighten people; it could not change when a cable had been connected. The summary called the situation learned; Wen Yi asked which observation justified that description. Han Lu noticed the word software in a note that someone had added after the first inspection. An official memo described the plan as systematic, but Wen Yi refused to treat an adjective as evidence.

即使大家同意眼前步驟，另一個問題仍在。答案不會靠戲劇性公告降臨；有人得返回現場、比對舊筆記，並接受新證據可能推翻漂亮理論。聞怡準備做的正是這件事。韓律在討論理論前先封存原始檔與中繼資料。被修改的畫面能嚇人，卻不能改變線纜接通的時間。摘要以「學養深厚的」形容情況，聞怡追問這種說法有何觀察根據。韓律注意到初次檢查後有人在筆記裡加上「軟體」一詞。官方備忘錄形容計畫「有系統的」，聞怡不願把形容詞當作證據。
