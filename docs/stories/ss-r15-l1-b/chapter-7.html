The Rabbit's List

鈴果村的百變遊戲日 · 第 7 / 25 章 · 1000以下 · 英中對照 · 校訂中

At the west side of the village, a small rabbit named Cora sat near the flower beds. She had heard that the new game course would be popular, but she did not want to join. Her class had a running race every week, and she always came last. Nela did not tell her to be brave or try harder. She sat beside her for a full hour and asked what kind of game Cora liked at home. Cora showed them a list of colors and shapes that she had collected from old picture books. “This is my real game,” she said. “I look for things other people miss.”

村子西邊的花圃旁，坐著一隻名叫可拉的小兔子。她聽說新遊戲活動會很受歡迎，卻不想參加。她的班級每週都有賽跑，而她每次都跑最後。妮拉沒有叫她勇敢一點或更加努力，而是在她身旁坐了整整一小時，問她平常在家喜歡什麼遊戲。可拉拿出從舊圖畫書收集的顏色和形狀清單。「這才是我真正喜歡的遊戲，」她說，「我會找別人沒注意到的東西。」

Cora's mother was a housewife who helped many families watch their younger children. She said Cora often listened closely but disliked noise. The team asked whether they could start a large picture-search table away from the main path. Cora agreed to help draw it, but only if nobody made her speak in front of a crowd. “Of course,” Nela answered. “Let us write that in the plan.” Milo made a note: children may show answers with a hand, a card, or words. They would collect more ideas before choosing the final form of each station.

可拉的媽媽是家庭主婦，也常幫許多家庭照看年幼的孩子。她說可拉聽得很仔細，但不喜歡吵鬧。小隊詢問，能不能在主通道旁較安靜的地方設立大型找圖桌。可拉答應幫忙畫圖，但有個條件：不要要求她站在人群面前說話。「當然可以，」妮拉回答，「我們把這點寫進計畫。」米洛記下新規則：孩子可以用手勢、卡片或說話來表達答案。他們還會繼續蒐集意見，再決定各站最後的形式。

Cora invited the group into her kitchen for warm chocolate after her mother agreed. On the table, she spread out her picture list and asked the visitors to find two drawings that were almost the same. Filo pointed at one quickly and was wrong. He wanted to leave, but Cora said the game did not end because of one mistake. Milo took longer and found a tiny difference in the window. The group saw that even quiet games could be challenging. Cora's mother said she would help at the table, provided Cora could choose when she needed a break. That was part of their plan too.

得到媽媽同意後，可拉請大家到廚房喝溫熱巧克力。她把找圖清單攤在桌上，請客人找出兩張幾乎一模一樣的畫。費洛很快指了一張，卻答錯了。他想離開，可拉說一次錯誤不代表遊戲結束。米洛花了較長時間，終於發現窗戶上的小差別。大家看見安靜的遊戲也能具有挑戰性。可拉的媽媽說她可以協助看顧遊戲桌，但可拉必須能自己決定何時休息。這也成了計畫的一部分。

Filo asked Cora why she wanted her name printed so small. “I am proud of my drawings,” she answered, “but I do not enjoy being the center of a crowd.” Filo considered the difference. He liked cheering and big races, but Cora did not have to like them too. They made a small promise to one another: Filo would help keep the picture table clear, and Cora would test the marks on the running path. Nela watched the two friends work side by side. Their different wishes had not become a wall between them.

費洛問可拉，為什麼希望名字印得那麼小。「我為自己的畫感到驕傲，」她回答，「但我不喜歡成為人群的中心。」費洛想了想其中的差別。他喜歡歡呼和大賽跑，可拉卻不必跟他一樣。他們互相答應一件小事：費洛幫忙保持找圖桌通暢，可拉則測試跑步路線上的記號。妮拉看著兩位朋友並肩工作。不同的願望，沒有變成隔在他們中間的牆。

For another short test, Nela put a blank sheet beside the notes about Cora’s picture search. She asked Milo to describe what a new visitor could see with no help. The group noticed that the quiet challenge was harder than Filo had expected. Filo was ready to make a quick choice, but Cora asked him to wait until everybody had spoken. He listened, even when the answer was not the one he hoped to hear. Mrs. Lark reminded the children that a game could be exciting and still leave room for someone who needed more time. In the end they decided to let Cora choose how her name and work were shown. They placed that decision where the next helper would find it. A rule only worked when people knew it and were willing to follow it.

為了再做一次簡短測試，妮拉在可拉的找圖遊戲的筆記旁放了張空白紙，請米洛說明新訪客在沒人幫忙時能看見什麼。小隊注意到：安靜的挑戰比費洛預期的還難。費洛急著做決定，可拉請他等每個人都說完。即使答案不是他希望的，他仍仔細聽。雲雀太太提醒孩子，遊戲可以刺激好玩，同時替需要更多時間的人留出空間。最後大家決定讓可拉自己決定姓名與作品怎麼呈現，並把這項決定放在下一位助手找得到的地方。規則只有在大家知道並願意遵守時，才能真正發揮作用。

The team stayed there until the sun moved behind the trees. Cora brought them three drawings: a house, a rabbit, and a flower with one leaf different from the others. It took Milo several minutes to find the different leaf. Cora smiled for the first time that day. Nela saw that this was not an easy game made for children who could not run. It was a real challenge with its own skill. She asked Cora whether her name could appear on the game board. Cora chose a smaller line beneath the picture: “Made with care by Cora.”

小隊一直待到太陽移到樹後。可拉拿來三張畫：一棟房子、一隻兔子，以及有片葉子與其他葉片不同的花。米洛花了好幾分鐘才找到那片不一樣的葉子。可拉這天第一次笑了。妮拉看出，這不是為了跑不快的孩子而做的簡單遊戲，而是一種需要獨特本領的真正挑戰。她問可拉是否願意把名字寫在遊戲板上。可拉選擇只在圖畫下方留一行小字：「可拉用心製作。」
