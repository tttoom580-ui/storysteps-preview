白風箏測試

The White Kite Test

鈴果村的百變遊戲日 · 第 11 / 25 章 · 1000以下 · 英中對照 · 校訂中

Mako made a white kite with a picture of each game station. He wanted to hang it over the library door so visitors would know where to go. In the basket beside him were thin pieces of wood, a belt, and colored paper. Cora asked if the kite could be useful to people who could not read small words. Mako checked the picture and saw his mistake: the letters were far too small. He had worked hard, but the sign needed to change. Nela did not make a joke about it. She offered to draw a larger picture with him.

馬可做了一只白風箏，上面畫著各個遊戲站。他想把風箏掛在圖書館門上，讓訪客知道方向。身旁籃子裡裝著薄木片、皮帶和彩色紙。可拉問，不容易看懂小字的人能不能使用這只風箏。馬可仔細檢查圖畫，發現錯誤：字實在太小了。他已經很努力，但標示仍需要修改。妮拉沒有拿這件事開玩笑，而是提議一起畫更大的圖。

Mrs. Lark asked a player with poor eyesight to test the picture at a safe distance. He could see the colors, but he could not tell which station was which. Mako disliked hearing that his work was unclear. For a moment he said he hated the whole project. Milo waited until he was ready to listen, then pointed to the larger symbols Cora had drawn. “If we send this picture to families, they should be able to use it,” Milo said. Mako took a breath and began again. The visitor thanked him for asking rather than guessing.

雲雀太太請一位視力不好的玩家，在安全距離外測試圖畫。他能看見顏色，卻無法分辨每個站點。馬可不喜歡聽到自己的作品不清楚，一時氣憤地說他討厭整個計畫。米洛等他願意聽了，才指向可拉畫的較大圖示。「如果我們把這張圖送到各個家庭，他們應該能看得懂，」米洛說。馬可深吸一口氣，重新動手。那位訪客謝謝他願意詢問，而不是憑空猜測。

“Dear visitors,” Mako wrote at the top of the new sign. Mrs. Lark asked him to think about people's health as well as their ability to see the words. Some visitors often needed to sit after walking, while others wanted a place away from noise. The group drew a chair beside the sign and kept the letters large. They tried placing the sign high, then low, and discovered that the lower position was easier for more people. Milo said that a useful picture should not make anyone feel foolish for needing it. Mako agreed, took out his pencil, and made the change himself.

馬可在新標示最上方寫著「親愛的訪客」。雲雀太太請他除了考慮文字是否看得清楚，也想想人們的健康需求。有些訪客走一段路就常需要坐下，另一些想遠離噪音。小隊在牌子旁畫了椅子，也把字保持大大的。他們試著把標示掛高，再掛低，發現低一點更容易被更多人看見。米洛說，有用的圖畫不該讓需要它的人覺得自己很傻。馬可同意，親手拿出鉛筆修改。

Cora asked whether a sign should show only happy pictures. She wanted to include a picture for asking an adult for help, too. Mako agreed, although the new sign looked less like a decoration. They asked the nurse from the hospital to test it and found that the picture of a hand was easy to understand. The nurse said visitors sometimes needed help even when nothing was wrong with the game. A tired child might need water or a parent. Nela added a clear place for adults to stand, and the group practiced pointing to it without stopping the rest of the players.

可拉問，標示是不是只能放快樂的圖案。她也想加上一張向大人求助的圖。馬可同意，雖然新標示看起來比較不像裝飾品。他們請醫院的護理師幫忙測試，發現手掌圖案很容易懂。護理師說，有時候即使遊戲本身沒有問題，訪客仍需要幫忙。疲倦的孩子可能想喝水或找家長。妮拉加上成人助手的明確位置，大家練習在不打斷其他玩家的情況下，指引人前往。

Later, the group sat around a small table to talk about the new sign above the library door. Nela asked every helper to give one clear example instead of simply calling the work good or bad. They remembered that the small letters were hard to see from the path. Milo wrote the example on a clean card and put a small question mark beside the parts they still did not know. They talked about what would happen if a stranger arrived without knowing any of their plans. A person might be shy, tired, or ready to ask a question. Nela said that a friendly game needed a clear answer, not a long speech. The group agreed to test the sign from where visitors would actually stand. Before moving on, they read the card once more and gave each other time to ask for a change.

稍後，小隊圍著小桌子討論圖書館門上的新標示。妮拉請每位助手提出一個清楚例子，而不只是說作品好或不好。他們記得：從小路上很難看清細小文字。米洛把例子寫在乾淨卡片上，還在尚未確定的地方畫個小問號。大家討論，如果陌生訪客什麼計畫都不知道就走進來，會發生什麼事。那個人可能害羞、疲倦，或正準備提問。妮拉說，友善的遊戲需要清楚的回答，而不是長篇演說。小隊同意站在訪客真正會站的位置測試標示。接著大家再次閱讀卡片，也給彼此時間提出修改意見。

Later, Milo visited the hospital with his mother to deliver supplies, and he saw a sign showing a simple picture of a chair. He asked a nurse why the sign was so clear. She explained that a good sign was made for people who were tired, worried, or new to a place, not only for people who knew the building. Back at the library, Milo drew the chair on their rest-area sign. Nela placed it beside the white kite. Those two pictures marked an important lesson: a sign was not finished when its maker liked it; it was finished when visitors could understand it.

稍後，米洛陪媽媽到醫院送用品，看見一塊畫著簡單椅子的標示。他問護理師，為什麼標示畫得這麼清楚。護理師說，好的標示是做給疲倦、擔心或初次來訪的人看的，而不只是給熟悉建築的人看的。回到圖書館，米洛在休息區的牌子上畫了一張椅子。妮拉把它放在白風箏旁。兩張圖提醒大家一件重要的事：標示不是作者喜歡就算完成，而是訪客真的看得懂才算完成。
