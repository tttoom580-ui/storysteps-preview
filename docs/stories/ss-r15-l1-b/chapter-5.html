免費遊戲的成本

The Price of Free Games

鈴果村的百變遊戲日 · 第 5 / 25 章 · 1000以下 · 英中對照 · 校訂中

用互動版查詞、朗讀與收藏

At the farm outside Bellberry Village, a teenager named Filo offered old wooden blocks for the building table. The pieces had once been used to mark rows of vegetables. Nela wanted to take many of them, but the farmer asked her to think about what the animals would still need on the farm. They agreed to borrow only the blocks that were no longer in use. Filo tied them with yellow string and showed the children a straight path through the field. “Go slowly,” he warned. “There is a strange hole where a cart wheel sank.”

在鈴果村外的農場裡，名叫費洛的少年願意借出舊木塊，給積木遊戲桌使用。那些木塊以前用來標記菜田。妮拉原本想拿很多，但農夫請她想想農場裡的動物還需要哪些東西。最後他們只借了已經不用的木塊。費洛用黃繩把木塊綁好，帶孩子走一條穿越田地的直路。「慢慢走，」他提醒，「那裡有個車輪陷下去留下的奇怪坑洞。」

The village bank gave no money for toys, but the clerk helped them make a list of costs. Mrs. Lark explained that a free day still needed care, time, and work. They would not sell the games or ask anyone to buy a ticket. Nela wrote a letter to the farm family thanking them for the loan. At dinner, they watched a short movie about games played by children in another village. Milo saw a game made with a bowl and beans. “We can make something good from simple things,” he said. “We do not need a machine or oil.”

村裡的銀行不會為玩具提供錢，但櫃員幫他們列出成本表。雲雀太太說明，免費活動仍然需要照顧、時間和勞力。他們不會販售遊戲，也不會要求任何人買票。妮拉寫了一封信給農場家庭，謝謝他們借東西。晚餐時，他們看了一部短片，介紹另一個村子的兒童遊戲。米洛看到一個只用碗和豆子就能玩的遊戲。「簡單的東西也能做出好遊戲，」他說，「不需要機器，也不需要機油。」

Before the team left the farm, the farmer showed the children how to feed the chickens slowly. He asked them to hold their hands still and wait for the birds to come near. Filo was used to moving fast, but he found he could enjoy a quiet task too. Milo pointed to a chicken that chose to stay away. “We do not need to chase it,” he said. Nela noticed how much that sounded like the game they hoped to make. Everyone could be invited, but an invitation was not a command. The borrowed blocks went into a box, and the farmer checked that every tool was back in its place.

小隊離開農場前，農夫教孩子慢慢餵雞。他請大家把手穩穩放著，等雞自己靠近。費洛習慣快速行動，卻發現安靜的工作也能讓他開心。米洛指著一隻選擇待遠一點的雞。「我們不必追牠，」他說。妮拉發現這和他們想辦的遊戲很像。每個人都可以受到邀請，但邀請不等於命令。借來的木塊放進箱子，農夫也確認每樣工具都回到原來的位置。

When they returned to the village, Filo asked Nela whether a slow game could still feel exciting. Nela held up one of the farm blocks and challenged him to balance it on two other blocks without letting it fall. Filo tried three times before he found the right place. His face grew bright when the little tower stayed up. Milo said there was no rule against being proud of success. The important thing was not using one person's success to make another person feel small. They put the blocks in storage and wrote down the farmer's name so they would remember to return them.

回村後，費洛問妮拉，慢速遊戲也能讓人興奮嗎？妮拉舉起一塊農場木塊，挑戰他把它平衡地放在另外兩塊上面，不讓它掉下來。費洛試了三次，才找到正確的位置。小塔站穩時，他的臉亮了起來。米洛說，為自己的成功感到驕傲並沒有錯；重要的是，不要拿一個人的成功去讓別人覺得渺小。大家把木塊收進儲藏室，記下農夫的名字，好記得之後歸還。

At the end of the test, Nela asked the others what they had learned about the wooden pieces borrowed from the farm. The first answers were short, but she waited for more. Soon Milo explained that a small tower was more fun when players could try again. Cora added a drawing, and Filo showed what he meant by moving one game piece slowly across the table. Nobody was trying to make another person look silly. They were looking for a way that more children could use. Mrs. Lark asked them to think about the next visitor, not only about the friends who already knew the game. The group chose to write down each loan and return date. Nela wrote it in plain words and asked everyone to check the page. They were getting better at making decisions without rushing one another.

測試結束後，妮拉問大家從向農場借的木塊學到什麼。最初的回答很短，但她耐心等待。接著米洛解釋：允許玩家重試時，小小的積木塔也變得更有趣。可拉補上一張圖，費洛則慢慢移動桌上的棋子，示範他的意思。沒有人想讓別人看起來笨拙；他們正在尋找更多孩子都能使用的方法。雲雀太太請大家想想下一位訪客，而不只是已熟悉遊戲的朋友。小隊選擇記下每筆借用與歸還日期。妮拉用簡單文字記下來，請所有人核對。他們逐漸學會不催促彼此，也能做出決定。

When the moon appeared, the group sat beside the farm fence and fed the chickens with the farmer's permission. Filo pointed to his sore toe and laughed about the time he had run through a field without looking. “I was in too much of a hurry,” he said. Nela was glad he could laugh now, but she did not want a child to get hurt at Game Day. They chose safety as the topic of their next meeting. Milo looked at the wooden blocks and wondered where everyone would place them. His question would lead to the most difficult choice yet.

月亮升起時，小隊坐在農場圍籬旁，在農夫同意下餵雞。費洛指著自己曾經疼痛的腳趾，笑說以前有一次跑過田地卻沒看路。「我那時太急了，」他說。妮拉很高興他如今能笑著說起這件事，但不希望遊戲日有孩子受傷。他們選擇把安全當成下次會議的主題。米洛看著那些木塊，開始想每個人該把它們放在哪裡。這個問題將帶來至今最困難的選擇。
