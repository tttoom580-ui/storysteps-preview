A Picture Is Not Permission

鈴果村的百變遊戲日 · 第 9 / 25 章 · 1000以下 · 英中對照 · 校訂中

用互動版查詞、朗讀與收藏

A visiting bear named Mako arrived from a foreign country with his farmer father. Mako loved taking a photograph of everything, but he seemed lonely in a place where he knew no one. He took a photo of Cora's picture board and planned to put it on the village wall. Nela asked him to keep the camera down until Cora could choose. Mako thought the request meant his picture was bad, and he looked sad. Milo explained that a good photograph could still belong to a person who had not agreed to share it. Nobody was calling Mako unkind.

一隻從外國來訪的熊，名叫馬可，和農夫爸爸一起抵達鈴果村。馬可喜歡拍下每樣東西，卻因為在這裡誰都不認識而感到孤單。他拍了可拉的找圖板，打算把照片貼上村裡的牆。妮拉請他先放下相機，讓可拉自己決定。馬可以為大家是在說他的照片不好，露出難過的神情。米洛解釋，好照片仍然需要得到創作者同意才能公開，沒有人因此說馬可不友善。

Cora joined them in the warm library. She said the board could be photographed after she finished the last drawing, but she did not want her face in the picture. Mako thanked her and asked whose name should go beside it. “Cora's,” Nela answered. Mako offered to make a small sign that told visitors how to ask before taking a photo. In the corner, a singer was practicing quietly for the opening day. She removed a metal ring from her scarf because it made a sharp sound against the table. Everybody listened carefully to the sound and to the person making it.

可拉在溫暖的圖書館和大家會合。她說，等最後一張圖畫完成後可以拍遊戲板，但不希望自己的臉被拍進去。馬可道謝，問照片旁該寫誰的名字。「可拉的，」妮拉回答。馬可提出要做一塊小標示，提醒訪客拍照前先詢問。角落裡，一位歌手正輕聲練習開幕日的歌曲。她把圍巾上的金屬戒環拿下來，因為那東西碰到桌子會發出刺耳聲響。大家不只留心聲音，也留心發出聲音的人。

Mako tried to be brave and speak about the photo problem himself. “I am a foreigner here, and I wanted people to know me,” he said. “But I forgot that Cora should decide what happens to her work.” Nobody laughed at him. Mrs. Lark offered him warm bread, but he asked if there was meat in it, because his father could not eat certain foods. She checked the ingredients and said there was nothing of that kind inside. The small exchange helped Mako feel well again. Nela saw a lesson in it: asking first could protect both a picture and a person's needs.

馬可鼓起勇氣，自己說明拍照的問題。「我是這裡的外國訪客，希望大家認識我，」他說，「但我忘了可拉應該能決定自己的作品要怎麼使用。」沒有人取笑他。雲雀太太拿溫麵包給他，但他問裡面有沒有肉，因為爸爸有些食物不能吃。她核對成分，說裡面完全沒有那一類食材。這段小小的對話讓馬可安心許多。妮拉也學到一課：先詢問，既能保護圖畫，也能照顧人的需求。

Mako asked Cora if she wanted him to remove the first photograph completely. She thought for a moment and said he could keep it for himself, but not show it to other people. Mako agreed and placed a note beside the picture in his own folder. Later, he showed his father the new sign about asking for permission. His father said that being new to a place could make rules harder to learn, but it did not remove the need to respect them. Mako nodded. He was beginning to feel that friends could correct one another without ending a friendship.

馬可問可拉，是否希望他把第一張照片完全刪掉。可拉想了一會兒，說他可以自己留著看，但不要公開給其他人。馬可同意，在自己的資料夾裡替照片加上註記。稍後，他把新的拍照前先詢問標示給爸爸看。爸爸說，初到新地方可能比較難理解規則，卻不代表可以不尊重規則。馬可點點頭。他開始覺得，朋友之間可以互相糾正，而不必因此結束友誼。

On the side of the table was a small notebook for ideas about the use of photographs. Everybody could add a drawing or a few words, but nobody had to write if they did not want to. Nela read the notes aloud to the group and checked whether she had understood them. One note showed that Cora wanted her drawings shown but not her face. Milo pointed out that an idea and a rule were different things. A rule needed to be clear enough for a new helper to follow, even on a busy day. The group agreed to ask the person involved before sharing a picture. Filo drew a small mark beside that choice so he would remember it. Nela closed the notebook carefully. A decision made together was easier to carry into the next day.

桌邊放著一本小筆記本，用來收集關於照片的使用方式的想法。每個人都能畫圖或寫幾個字，不想寫的人也不用勉強。妮拉把筆記念給小隊聽，確認自己沒有理解錯誤。其中一項紀錄顯示：可拉想展示圖畫，卻不想公開臉部照片。米洛提醒，點子和規則是不同的。規則必須清楚到新助手即使在忙碌時，也能照著做。大家同意分享照片前先詢問當事人。費洛在這項選擇旁畫了小記號，以免忘記。妮拉小心闔上筆記本。一起做出的決定，才比較容易帶到下一天。

Before leaving, Mako asked where the toilet was, and Nela showed him the way rather than laughing at his question. Milo offered him a cup of milk, but Mako preferred water and said so. The group learned that being friendly did not mean deciding what another person should want. Mako chose to help mark the picture station, and he promised not to lose Cora's note about photographs. On the way back, he said this was the first time he had felt like a member of the village. Nela answered that a visitor should be able to join without giving up a choice about their own picture.

離開前，馬可問洗手間在哪裡，妮拉帶他去，沒有取笑他的問題。米洛請他喝一杯牛奶，但馬可更想喝水，也直接說出來。小隊學到：友善不等於替別人決定想要什麼。馬可選擇幫忙標示圖畫遊戲站，並答應不會弄丟可拉寫的拍照說明。回去的路上，他說這是第一次覺得自己像村子的一分子。妮拉回答，訪客應該能加入活動，也能保有決定自己照片的權利。
