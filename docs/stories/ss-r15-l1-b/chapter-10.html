少了一顆鈕扣

The Missing Button

鈴果村的百變遊戲日 · 第 10 / 25 章 · 1000以下 · 英中對照 · 校訂中

One evening, Filo brought a box of colored buttons to the building table. He had made a menu of four challenges, from easy to hard. The first one was to put a rose-shaped button beside a potato-shaped piece. The next was to follow a line around the table without letting anything fall. But when everyone was ready, a large button was missing. Filo began to worry that someone had taken it. Nela asked him to watch what happened before he blamed anyone. Milo looked under the table and found the button caught in the edge of a cloth.

某天傍晚，費洛帶來一盒彩色鈕扣，要給積木桌使用。他設計了四種從簡單到困難的挑戰清單。第一關是把玫瑰形鈕扣放在馬鈴薯形積木旁，下一關則是沿桌邊移動物件而不讓任何東西掉下來。可是大家準備好後，卻發現少了一顆大鈕扣。費洛擔心有人偷拿。妮拉請他先仔細看清楚，不要急著責怪別人。米洛往桌下看，找到那顆卡在桌布邊緣的鈕扣。

Filo was usually the fastest player in every game, and he loved hearing the sound of a bell when he won. At first, he did not see why the new rules mattered. “If there is no first place, what makes a game interesting?” he asked. Nela smiled and invited him to try Cora's picture challenge. He could not find a small difference between two flowers. He stayed at the table, tried again, and finally found it. “That was trouble,” he said with a laugh. “But I liked it. I wanted to keep trying even when I was not the best.”

費洛平常幾乎在每種遊戲裡都是最快的玩家，也喜歡聽見獲勝時響起的鈴聲。一開始，他不明白新規則有什麼用。「如果沒有第一名，遊戲哪裡有趣？」他問。妮拉微笑，請他試試可拉的找圖挑戰。他找不到兩朵花之間微小的差異，便繼續坐著試，最後終於找到了。「這可真麻煩，」他笑著說，「但我喜歡。就算不是最厲害，我也想再試。」

A student named Lio stayed after the trial to help clean the table. The room was growing dark, so Nela asked an adult to turn on the lights. Lio found another button to the left of a wooden box. He offered a sweet from his pocket to celebrate, but the teacher reminded everyone to keep food away from the game pieces. They washed their hands and ate in the food area instead. Filo took a small note home about the missing button, so the group would remember how quickly a guess could become an unfair story. He wanted to be more careful next time.

一位名叫里歐的學生在試玩後留下來整理桌子。房間漸漸變暗，妮拉請大人開燈。里歐在木箱左側又找到一顆鈕扣。他從口袋拿出一顆糖想慶祝，老師卻提醒大家食物應該遠離遊戲零件。大家洗手後，改到用餐區吃。費洛把一張關於鈕扣事件的小紙條帶回家，提醒自己：一個猜測很容易變成不公平的故事。他希望下次更小心。

The teacher gave Filo a small bell and asked him to lead one round of the building game. He rang the bell before everyone was ready, and a younger child looked upset. Filo stopped, apologized, and asked when the child wanted to begin. The child took a deep breath and gave a signal. This time Filo waited. The round went well. Nela noticed how much his behavior had changed since the first race. He was still eager to win, but he was learning that leading a game meant paying attention to the players, not only to the clock.

老師給費洛一顆小鈴鐺，請他帶領一回合積木遊戲。他在大家準備好前就搖鈴，一位年幼孩子顯得不安。費洛停下來道歉，問孩子想什麼時候開始。孩子深呼吸，給出訊號，這次費洛等了。這回合進行得很順利。妮拉注意到，費洛自第一次賽跑以來改變了很多。他仍渴望贏，但正學習帶領遊戲需要注意玩家，不只是注意時鐘。

At the end of the test, Nela asked the others what they had learned about the missing button at the table. The first answers were short, but she waited for more. Soon Milo explained that the button was under a cloth and not in anyone’s pocket. Cora added a drawing, and Filo showed what he meant by moving one game piece slowly across the table. Nobody was trying to make another person look silly. They were looking for a way that more children could use. Mrs. Lark asked them to think about the next visitor, not only about the friends who already knew the game. The group chose to look carefully before saying a person has taken something. Nela wrote it in plain words and asked everyone to check the page. They were getting better at making decisions without rushing one another.

測試結束後，妮拉問大家從桌上少掉的鈕扣學到什麼。最初的回答很短，但她耐心等待。接著米洛解釋：鈕扣在布底下，並不在任何人的口袋裡。可拉補上一張圖，費洛則慢慢移動桌上的棋子，示範他的意思。沒有人想讓別人看起來笨拙；他們正在尋找更多孩子都能使用的方法。雲雀太太請大家想想下一位訪客，而不只是已熟悉遊戲的朋友。小隊選擇說別人拿走東西前，先仔細檢查。妮拉用簡單文字記下來，請所有人核對。他們逐漸學會不催促彼此，也能做出決定。

The group decided that some games could still have a winner, but nobody would be called a bad player for needing more time. They would offer a second try and let people choose when to stop. Filo said he could help watch the fast path and make sure it stayed clear. He also asked to learn the picture game before Game Day. On their way home, Nela heard the town clock and thought about what had changed. They had not removed excitement from play. They had made room for a different kind of excitement, one that did not leave Milo or Cora standing alone.

小隊決定有些遊戲仍可以分出勝負，但沒有人會因為需要比較多時間就被叫作差勁的玩家。他們會提供第二次機會，也讓人自己決定什麼時候停止。費洛說他可以幫忙照看快速路線，確保道路保持暢通。他還想在遊戲日前學會找圖遊戲。回家路上，妮拉聽見鎮上的鐘聲，想著發生的改變。他們並沒有把遊戲的興奮感拿掉，而是為不同的興奮感留出空間，讓米洛和可拉不再被孤單地留在原地。
