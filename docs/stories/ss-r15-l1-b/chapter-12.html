沒有價格的入場券

A Ticket With No Price

鈴果村的百變遊戲日 · 第 12 / 25 章 · 1000以下 · 英中對照 · 校訂中

用互動版查詞、朗讀與收藏

The village band offered to play at the opening. An actor and an actress from the school club offered to act out the rules. They met on a warm afternoon at the supermarket corner, where the owner had put a small table beside the door. Nela wanted every visitor to have a paper ticket with the names of the game stations. Filo asked how much each ticket would cost. “Nothing,” Nela said. “It is not a sale. A ticket is just a guide.” Milo held up a package of blank cards and asked if they could use those instead of buying new paper.

村裡的樂團願意在開幕時演奏，學校社團的一男一女兩位演員也願意用表演示範遊戲規則。他們在一個溫暖的下午碰面，地點是超市轉角；店主在門邊放了一張小桌子。妮拉希望每位訪客都有一張紙入場券，上面列出各個遊戲站。費洛問每張票要花多少錢。「不用錢，」妮拉說，「這不是買賣。入場券只是導覽。」米洛舉起一包空白卡片，問能不能使用這些，而不另外買紙。

A local media worker came to take a picture of the practice. She asked Milo to stand at the front and show his face, but he was tired and did not want to. Nela explained that he could choose to play or rest. The worker accepted the answer and photographed the game boards instead. At five o'clock, the actors did a short play about a child who missed a turn. In the story, nobody laughed at the child; a friend helped him find a new place in the line. When the play ended, more people asked about joining than Nela had expected.

一位地方媒體工作者來拍練習的照片。她請米洛站到前面露臉，但米洛累了，不想照做。妮拉解釋，米洛可以自己選擇繼續玩或休息。工作者接受了，改拍遊戲板。五點整，兩位演員演了一段關於小孩錯過輪次的短劇。劇中的人沒有取笑他，而是由朋友幫忙在隊伍中找到新位置。演出結束時，詢問如何參加的人比妮拉預期的還要多。

The band wanted to celebrate with a song before the play. One young actor joked that he might die of fear on stage, but the teacher reminded him that words about death could frighten younger guests. He changed his line to “I feel very nervous.” Nela said, “I like that better.” She put a key on the desk so the adult keeper would remember which room held the cards. A poor family could still enter without paying, and that was written plainly on every ticket. At six o'clock, the team closed the practice room and left together, tired but pleased.

樂團想在短劇前唱首歌慶祝。一位年輕演員開玩笑說自己可能會緊張到死掉，但老師提醒他，談到死亡的話可能嚇到較小的客人。他把台詞改成「我非常緊張」。妮拉說：「我比較喜歡這樣。」她把鑰匙放在桌上，提醒成人管理員哪個房間收著卡片。經濟困難的家庭也能免費入場，這點清楚寫在每張票上。六點整，小隊關閉排練室，一起離開，雖然疲倦卻很滿足。

The actors asked the children to act like visitors who had never heard the rules before. One child walked to the wrong station, another wanted to rest, and a third could not find the cards. The actors showed how a friendly helper could answer each question without getting angry. Nela noticed that the play taught more than a long written list. But she also saw that the actors would not be on stage all day. The team needed signs and real helpers to continue the lesson after the short performance ended. They checked the cards once again before packing them away.

演員請孩子扮演從沒聽過規則的訪客。一個走錯遊戲站，另一個想休息，第三個找不到卡片。演員示範友善助手可以怎麼回答每個問題，而不生氣。妮拉注意到，短劇教會大家的事比一長串文字還多。但她也明白，演員不會整天都在舞台上。短劇結束後，仍要靠標示和真正的助手延續這些做法。他們把卡片再次檢查，才收起來。

For another short test, Nela put a blank sheet beside the notes about the paper tickets for Game Day. She asked Milo to describe what a new visitor could see with no help. The group noticed that the old print hid important words about leaving and resting. Filo was ready to make a quick choice, but Cora asked him to wait until everybody had spoken. He listened, even when the answer was not the one he hoped to hear. Mrs. Lark reminded the children that a game could be exciting and still leave room for someone who needed more time. In the end they decided to make every choice clear on the free cards. They placed that decision where the next helper would find it. A rule only worked when people knew it and were willing to follow it.

為了再做一次簡短測試，妮拉在遊戲日的紙入場券的筆記旁放了張空白紙，請米洛說明新訪客在沒人幫忙時能看見什麼。小隊注意到：舊版小字藏著離開與休息的重要說明。費洛急著做決定，可拉請他等每個人都說完。即使答案不是他希望的，他仍仔細聽。雲雀太太提醒孩子，遊戲可以刺激好玩，同時替需要更多時間的人留出空間。最後大家決定在免費卡片上清楚寫出所有選擇，並把這項決定放在下一位助手找得到的地方。規則只有在大家知道並願意遵守時，才能真正發揮作用。

Nela's grandfather brought his old glasses and read the ticket carefully. “There is one last problem,” he said. “The smallest words tell people when they may leave. Those should be large too.” The team changed the print. They also added two choices: visitors could play more games, or they could stop at any time. Nobody had to earn a prize before going home. Nela felt pleased with the new cards, though she knew a printed promise had to become a real practice. On Game Day, helpers would need to follow what the cards said.

妮拉的爺爺戴上舊眼鏡，仔細閱讀入場券。「還有最後一個問題，」他說，「最小的那行字寫著人們什麼時候可以離開。這也應該放大。」小隊修改了字體，也加上兩種選擇：訪客可以繼續多玩幾場，也可以隨時停下。沒有人必須拿到獎品才能回家。妮拉對新卡片感到滿意，但她知道，印在紙上的承諾也得在現場做到。遊戲日時，工作人員必須遵守卡片寫的規則。
