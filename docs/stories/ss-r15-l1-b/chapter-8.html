裝滿遊戲的博物館

A Museum Full of Games

鈴果村的百變遊戲日 · 第 8 / 25 章 · 1000以下 · 英中對照 · 校訂中

用互動版查詞、朗讀與收藏

The village museum had a room full of old games. Its guide showed Nela and Milo a book with drawings of a train, a queen, and a rolling wheel. One game asked each player to roll a wooden disk across a line. Another used a fork-shaped tool to lift small blocks. The guide told the children not to try either game without checking the pieces first. A sharp edge could be dangerous. “An idea can be good even when an old tool is not safe,” she said. Milo wrote the thought in a file for their project.

村立博物館有一個收藏舊遊戲的房間。導覽員給妮拉和米洛看一本書，裡面畫著火車、女王和滾動的輪子。一種遊戲要玩家把木圓片滾過線，另一種則使用叉形工具拿起小積木。導覽員提醒孩子，不檢查零件就不要試玩。尖銳邊緣可能有危險。「舊工具不安全，不代表好點子就不能留下來，」她說。米洛把這句話記進他們的計畫檔案。

At noon, the museum was full of visitors. Each child received a paper dollar to use in a pretend old-time shop, but nobody had to give real money. Nela listened to an older visitor explain how her family used to play games after work. She had never found those evenings boring, even when they used the same few pieces again and again. “People tell stories while they play,” the woman said. “The game is not the only reason to come.” Nela began to understand that the tables could be places for meeting friends, not just places for collecting points.

中午時，博物館擠滿訪客。每個孩子都拿到一張紙做的一元鈔票，用來玩老式商店遊戲，但不必付出真錢。妮拉聽一位年長訪客說起家人過去工作後一起玩遊戲的時光。即使反覆使用少數幾種道具，她也從不覺得無聊。「人們玩遊戲時也會說故事，」那位女士說，「遊戲不是大家相聚的唯一原因。」妮拉開始明白，遊戲桌除了拿分數，也能是交朋友的地方。

The museum guide asked whether anybody else could think of a game that needed no expensive materials. Nela suggested moving a wooden ring between marked places. Milo added that players might work together to make a pattern. The guide placed a flat board on the table, then told the children to try their ideas. One pattern was too hard, another was almost too easy, and a third made the group laugh. Nothing was wrong with changing a game after trying it. Before they left, the guide wrote a short note allowing the children to copy the idea, but not to take the museum's old pieces home.

博物館導覽員問，還有沒有人想得出不需要昂貴材料的遊戲。妮拉提議把木環移到不同的記號之間，米洛則補充，玩家可以一起排出圖案。導覽員把平板放上桌，請孩子們試試自己的想法。一種圖案太難，另一種幾乎太簡單，第三種讓大家笑了起來。測試後修改遊戲並沒有錯。離開前，導覽員寫下一張簡短的同意紙，允許孩子使用遊戲點子，但不能把博物館的舊零件帶回家。

The guide let Nela touch a new copy of an old wooden puzzle. It was made for visitors, not part of the museum's protected collection. Nela pushed one block too hard, and it slipped out of place. She apologized and put it back. Milo noticed that the puzzle had a place where pieces could rest while players considered their next move. He suggested adding such a space to their building table. The guide smiled and said a small resting space could make a game much easier to share, especially when players took turns slowly.

導覽員讓妮拉摸一套舊木拼圖的新製複本，那是給訪客操作的，不屬於博物館受保護的藏品。妮拉把一塊推得太用力，木塊滑離位置。她道歉後放回原位。米洛注意到，拼圖留了一個暫放零件的區域，讓玩家思考下一步。他建議在自家的積木桌也設一個這樣的空間。導覽員微笑，說小小的暫放區能讓遊戲更容易分享，特別適合玩家慢慢輪流。

Before putting the materials away, the children looked once more at the museum’s old game ideas. Nela wanted to understand what had changed since their first try. Milo said that a copied puzzle could be tried without touching the old collection. This was a small thing to write in a notebook, but it could mean a great deal to someone standing in front of a game for the first time. Cora suggested checking the work with a child who had not helped to design it. Filo agreed and offered to explain the rules using only simple words. When the child asked a question, they answered without laughing. The group decided to write down permission before making their own copy. They did not call the work perfect. They called it ready for another honest test, and that was enough for the moment.

收起材料之前，孩子們再次查看博物館的舊遊戲點子。妮拉想知道第一次試玩後改變了什麼。米洛說：不用碰舊藏品也能試玩複製的拼圖。寫進筆記本只要短短一行，對第一次站在遊戲前的人卻可能非常重要。可拉建議請沒參與設計的孩子再檢查一次。費洛同意，並用簡單的話解釋規則。孩子提問時，他們認真回答，沒有取笑。小隊決定製作自己的複本前先把使用同意記下來。他們沒有說作品已經完美，只說可以接受下一次誠實測試，而目前這樣就夠了。

The guide gave the team a mistake to find in a model board: the steps were too close for large feet. The children moved the pieces farther apart and made room for smaller players as well. “You have made a useful change,” she said. “But do not call it successful until people test it.” The team took their notes and went home. They did not carry away any museum object. As Nela closed the book, she thought of all the people who had played before them. Their new Game Day could be part of a much longer story, if they treated old ideas and other people's things with respect.

導覽員請小隊找出模型遊戲板裡的一個錯誤：踏板間隔太近，腳大的人不好移動。孩子們把踏板拉開，也讓腳小的玩家容易使用。「你們做了有用的修改，」導覽員說，「但要等人真的試過，才能說成功。」小隊帶著筆記回家，沒有拿走任何博物館的物品。妮拉闔上書，想到以前曾玩過這些遊戲的人。如果尊重舊點子和他人的物品，他們的新遊戲日也能成為這段悠長故事的一部分。
