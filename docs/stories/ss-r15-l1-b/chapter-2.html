給每個人的邀請

An Invitation for Anyone

鈴果村的百變遊戲日 · 第 2 / 25 章 · 1000以下 · 英中對照 · 校訂中

用互動版查詞、朗讀與收藏

The next morning, Nela carried a piece of paper to the third apartment above the village bakery. Her aunt lived there on a soft sofa and could not walk far. “May anybody come to Game Day?” her aunt asked. Nela was going to guess, but stopped herself. The question was serious. She wrote it with a pen instead of making a promise. At lunch, Milo came late from the pond, where he had helped a young duck. He looked at the question and said, “If we invite anyone, we must make the games easy to reach.”

隔天早上，妮拉拿著一張紙，到村裡麵包店樓上的第三間公寓探望阿姨。阿姨坐在柔軟的沙發上，沒辦法走太遠。「任何人都能參加遊戲日嗎？」阿姨問。妮拉本來想猜個答案，卻停了下來，因為這是嚴肅的問題。她用筆把問題寫下來，而不是隨便答應。午餐時，米洛從池塘那裡晚到了；他剛幫過一隻小鴨。米洛看到問題便說：「如果我們邀請所有人，遊戲就得讓大家容易走到。」

They visited the village hall beside the old church. A reporter from the local paper was asking about the coming celebration. He wanted a clear answer about the cost. The village leader said no one would have to pay to enter, and every activity would be free. Nela used the hall telephone to call her aunt and tell her the news. She almost forgot to ask about the seats, but Milo touched her sleeve. They added a new line to the invitation: “Here is a place to sit, and here is a place to rest.”

他們來到舊教堂旁的村民會堂。地方報紙的記者正詢問即將舉辦的活動，想知道是否要收費。村長明確回答：不必付錢就能進場，所有活動都免費。妮拉用會堂的電話打給阿姨，告訴她這個消息。她差點忘記問座位，幸好米洛輕碰她的袖子。他們在邀請函上加了一行：「這裡有座位，也有休息的地方。」

Before they finished the invitation, Nela asked her aunt if she might forget a rule after reading it once. Her aunt laughed. “Of course. I forget things like anybody else.” Milo suggested putting the most important information on the front: the place, the time, and the fact that visitors could leave whenever they wished. Nela asked a younger cousin to read it aloud. The cousin found the words easy but wanted a picture of the entrance. They added one without covering the rest of the text. The invitation was getting clearer because the children had stopped writing only for themselves.

完成邀請函前，妮拉問阿姨會不會讀過一次就忘記規則。阿姨笑了。「當然會，我跟任何人一樣都會忘記事情。」米洛建議把最重要的資訊放在正面：地點、時間，以及訪客想離開時可以自行離開。妮拉請年幼的表親大聲讀一遍。表親覺得文字容易懂，卻希望有入口的圖。他們加上一張圖，也沒有遮住其餘文字。邀請函越來越清楚，是因為孩子們不再只替自己寫。

The bakery owner offered to leave the invitation on the counter, but Nela first asked whether it would block the place where customers put their bread. They moved the paper to a small board by the door. A parent came in and read the notice twice, then asked if there would be somewhere to wait with a young child. Milo drew a chair beside the invitation. The parent thanked him but pointed out that a picture was not enough; an actual chair had to be there. Nela added that question to the list for the hall keeper.

麵包店主願意把邀請函放在櫃台，妮拉卻先問是否會擋住顧客放麵包的位置。他們把紙移到門邊的小板上。一位家長進來，把通知讀了兩遍，詢問能否帶年幼孩子在某處等待。米洛在邀請函旁畫上椅子。家長謝謝他，也提醒光有圖還不夠，現場真的得有椅子。妮拉把這個問題加入準備向會堂管理員詢問的清單。

For another short test, Nela put a blank sheet beside the notes about the public invitation. She asked Milo to describe what a new visitor could see with no help. The group noticed that the older guests read the time but asked about places to sit. Filo was ready to make a quick choice, but Cora asked him to wait until everybody had spoken. He listened, even when the answer was not the one he hoped to hear. Mrs. Lark reminded the children that a game could be exciting and still leave room for someone who needed more time. In the end they decided to ask the hall keeper for real chairs before printing more copies. They placed that decision where the next helper would find it. A rule only worked when people knew it and were willing to follow it.

為了再做一次簡短測試，妮拉在公開邀請函的筆記旁放了張空白紙，請米洛說明新訪客在沒人幫忙時能看見什麼。小隊注意到：年長訪客讀懂時間後，仍詢問座位在哪裡。費洛急著做決定，可拉請他等每個人都說完。即使答案不是他希望的，他仍仔細聽。雲雀太太提醒孩子，遊戲可以刺激好玩，同時替需要更多時間的人留出空間。最後大家決定在多印邀請函前先向會堂管理員確認真正的椅子，並把這項決定放在下一位助手找得到的地方。規則只有在大家知道並願意遵守時，才能真正發揮作用。

The reporter offered to print a notice, but he asked whether the event was a sports race. “Not exactly,” Milo answered. “We are still finding out what it can become.” Nela liked that honest answer. She asked the leader for permission to talk with families across the country road and at the small pond. The leader agreed, provided they stayed with an adult. At the end of the day, Nela found three different needs on her list: a slower path, larger signs, and a quiet corner. None of them could be solved by calling a single child the winner.

記者願意刊登通知，但他問這是否是一場運動賽跑。「不完全是，」米洛回答，「我們還在找出它能變成什麼樣子。」妮拉喜歡這個誠實的回答。她向村長提出，希望在大人陪同下，到鄉間道路另一邊和小池塘旁詢問家庭的意見。村長答應了。一天結束時，妮拉的清單已有三種不同需求：較慢的路線、更大的標示，以及安靜的角落。這些需求都不是選出一個冠軍就能解決的。
