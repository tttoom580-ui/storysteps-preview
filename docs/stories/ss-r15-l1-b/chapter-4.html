穿過草地的兩條路

Two Ways Across the Field

鈴果村的百變遊戲日 · 第 4 / 25 章 · 1000以下 · 英中對照 · 校訂中

用互動版查詞、朗讀與收藏

The east side of the village field had one narrow path and one wide path. Children usually ran along the narrow one because it was shorter. Milo looked at the green grass between them and asked whether everyone must take the same road. Mrs. Lark brought a medium bowl filled with smooth stones. Each stone could mark a turn. Nela made a choice: both paths would reach the same table, and neither path would give more points. A cute young mouse knocked on the wooden block at the entrance and asked to test the plan.

村子草地東側有一條窄路和一條寬路。孩子們通常跑窄路，因為距離比較短。米洛看著兩條路之間的綠草，問大家是否一定要走同一條路。雲雀太太帶來一只中碗，裡面裝著光滑的小石頭，每一顆都能用來標示轉彎處。妮拉做了選擇：兩條路都能通向同一張桌子，而且不因路線不同而增加分數。一隻可愛的小老鼠敲了敲入口的木塊，表示想試試看。

At first, Nela did not notice a cart standing across the wide path. Someone had left it after washing it in a bath of soapy water. The cart blocked the way, and a child almost bumped into it. Mrs. Lark moved it to a safe place and reminded the team that a clear path must stay clear during the whole game. They asked the young mouse to cross the field again, this time while they watched from the side. “Quiet feet are useful,” said Milo. “They help us see things that fast feet miss.”

一開始，妮拉沒注意到寬路上橫著一台手推車。有人用一盆肥皂水洗過車子後，把它留在那裡。車子擋住道路，一個孩子差點撞上去。雲雀太太把車移到安全的地方，提醒小隊：一條暢通的路必須整場遊戲都保持暢通。他們請小老鼠再走一次，這回大家站在旁邊仔細觀察。「安靜的腳步也有用，」米洛說，「它們能讓我們看見跑太快時錯過的東西。」

The older guest tried both entrances once more. She asked the children to stand where she could see them, not where they thought they looked important. Mrs. Lark explained that a path had to remain wide after signs, chairs, and game boxes were placed beside it. Nela moved a box away and found that the route became easier at once. Milo wrote down the change so nobody would put the box back there later. A good decision on a quiet afternoon might be forgotten during a busy event. They needed simple rules that helpers could follow even when many people arrived together.

年長客人又試了兩個入口。她請孩子們站在她看得見的位置，而不是站在他們自以為重要的位置。雲雀太太解釋，路線放上標示、椅子和遊戲箱後，仍必須夠寬。妮拉移走一個箱子，通道立刻更容易通行。米洛把改動寫下來，免得之後有人又把箱子放回去。安靜午後做的好決定，到了忙碌活動時可能會被忘記。他們需要簡單到即使大家同時到場，助手也能遵守的規則。

A young visitor asked who should remove a box if it fell in front of the wide path. Nela began to answer that a child could move it, but Mrs. Lark interrupted gently. Not every box was light, and children should ask the adult helpers first. They placed a small picture of a hand beside the route, showing where to call for help. Milo checked that the sign did not narrow the path. The guest walked from one end to the other again and nodded. This time the way was clear, and the rule about moving objects was clear too.

一位小訪客問，如果箱子倒在寬路前面，應該由誰移開。妮拉正要回答孩子也能搬，雲雀太太便溫和地打斷她。不是每個箱子都輕，小孩應先請成年助手幫忙。大家在路線旁放了一張手掌圖案，標示遇到問題時去哪裡求助。米洛確認標示不會讓路變窄。客人又從頭走到尾，點了點頭。這次道路暢通，搬移物品的規則也很清楚。

On the side of the table was a small notebook for ideas about the two paths across the field. Everybody could add a drawing or a few words, but nobody had to write if they did not want to. Nela read the notes aloud to the group and checked whether she had understood them. One note showed that the wider way helped more visitors pass without stopping. Milo pointed out that an idea and a rule were different things. A rule needed to be clear enough for a new helper to follow, even on a busy day. The group agreed to keep the wide route free of boxes and chairs. Filo drew a small mark beside that choice so he would remember it. Nela closed the notebook carefully. A decision made together was easier to carry into the next day.

桌邊放著一本小筆記本，用來收集關於橫越草地的兩條路的想法。每個人都能畫圖或寫幾個字，不想寫的人也不用勉強。妮拉把筆記念給小隊聽，確認自己沒有理解錯誤。其中一項紀錄顯示：較寬的路讓更多訪客不用停下就能通行。米洛提醒，點子和規則是不同的。規則必須清楚到新助手即使在忙碌時，也能照著做。大家同意讓寬路維持沒有箱子和椅子的狀態。費洛在這項選擇旁畫了小記號，以免忘記。妮拉小心闔上筆記本。一起做出的決定，才比較容易帶到下一天。

A taxicab stopped near the gate to let an older guest get out. Nela was surprised that the guest could use the wide path without help, while the short path was too steep. “Look,” the guest said, pointing at her feet. “This is not a special path for one person. It is a good path for many people.” Nela lifted her head and added that thought to the board. The new design would let visitors pass one another without being pushed. It would also leave space for people who preferred not to join a loud group. The team kept the two paths.

一輛計程車在大門旁停下，讓一位年長的客人下車。妮拉驚訝地發現，那位客人不需要協助就能走寬路，但窄路的坡度對她太陡。「看，」客人指著自己的腳說，「這不是只為一個人設計的特別道路，而是讓很多人都方便的好路。」妮拉抬起頭，把這句話寫上板子。新的設計可以讓訪客互相通過，不必推擠，也會留給不喜歡吵鬧的人足夠空間。小隊決定保留兩條路。
