怎樣才算贏？

What Counts as Winning?

鈴果村的百變遊戲日 · 第 3 / 25 章 · 1000以下 · 英中對照 · 校訂中

用互動版查詞、朗讀與收藏

Nela and Milo held their first meeting in the school play area. A basketball had rolled beneath an orange bench, and a few children were looking for it. Milo found the ball but asked everyone to wait before the next game began. “Let us define winning,” he said. “Does it mean coming first, or does it mean finishing something together?” Nela wrote all the ideas on a large page. Some children wanted fast races. Others wanted puzzles. One wanted to make a picture without any clock. They agreed that the day should have several games, not only one.

妮拉和米洛在學校遊戲區開了第一次會。籃球滾到橘色長椅下面，幾個孩子正四處尋找。米洛找到球，卻請大家在下一場遊戲開始前先等一下。「我們先說清楚什麼叫作贏，」他說，「是第一個到終點，還是一起完成一件事？」妮拉把所有意見寫在大紙上。有些孩子想要快跑，有人想玩解謎，也有人想不計時地畫畫。最後大家同意，遊戲日應該有好幾種遊戲，而不只是同一種。

They chose three areas: a movement path, a building table, and a listening game. The modern school hall had a bright lamp and enough room for chairs. At a quarter past three in the afternoon, the teacher sent an email to parents. The message said the trial would begin at four pm. She would also mail a paper copy to homes without a computer. Nela took an eraser and changed a line saying “the fastest wins” to “every player can try.” It was a small change, but it made Milo breathe more easily.

他們選了三個區域：動作小徑、積木桌和聽聲音的遊戲。新的學校會堂有明亮的燈，也有足夠空間放椅子。下午三點十五分，老師寄電子郵件通知家長，說試玩將在下午四點開始。沒有電腦的家庭也會收到紙本郵件。妮拉拿起橡皮擦，把「最快的人獲勝」改成「每個玩家都能嘗試」。這只是小小的修改，米洛卻覺得肩膀輕鬆了些。

A teacher placed three cards on a desk and asked each child to choose one. The first card showed a ball; the second showed a picture; the third showed a group holding hands. The children moved to different corners of the room. Nela counted them and found that no single choice had all the votes. Milo said this did not mean the group was divided in a bad way. It meant the players liked different kinds of fun. The teacher asked them to explain one reason for their choice. They listened without trying to change anyone's mind, then placed the cards together on the plan.

老師在桌上放了三張卡，請每個孩子挑一張。第一張畫球，第二張畫圖，第三張畫大家牽手。孩子們移向教室不同角落。妮拉清點後發現，沒有任何一個選項拿到全部票數。米洛說，這不代表團體發生了不好的分裂，而是玩家喜歡不同種類的樂趣。老師請他們各說一個理由。大家傾聽，沒有急著改變別人的想法，再把三張卡一起放進計畫裡。

Filo raised his hand and said he did not want to lose the thrill of running. Nela listened instead of telling him he was wrong. The teacher suggested a fair test: let people try two games, then ask which they liked and why. When Filo tried the picture game, he found it much harder than expected. When Milo tried a short running path, he enjoyed moving at his own speed. The group discovered that a person could like more than one game, and that nobody had to be defined by a single result. Their plan could include both movement and quiet thinking.

費洛舉手說，他不想失去跑步時的刺激感。妮拉沒有說他錯了，而是仔細傾聽。老師提議公平測試：讓大家玩兩種遊戲，再問喜歡哪一種、為什麼。費洛試了找圖遊戲，發現比想像中困難得多。米洛試了短短的跑步路線，也很享受按照自己的速度前進。小隊發現，一個人可以同時喜歡不只一種遊戲，也不該被一次結果定義。計畫可以同時有動作與安靜思考。

Before putting the materials away, the children looked once more at the three proposed game stations. Nela wanted to understand what had changed since their first try. Milo said that each child wanted to try a different kind of game. This was a small thing to write in a notebook, but it could mean a great deal to someone standing in front of a game for the first time. Cora suggested checking the work with a child who had not helped to design it. Filo agreed and offered to explain the rules using only simple words. When the child asked a question, they answered without laughing. The group decided to leave three choices on the school plan. They did not call the work perfect. They called it ready for another honest test, and that was enough for the moment.

收起材料之前，孩子們再次查看三種預計設立的遊戲站。妮拉想知道第一次試玩後改變了什麼。米洛說：每個孩子都想玩不同種類的遊戲。寫進筆記本只要短短一行，對第一次站在遊戲前的人卻可能非常重要。可拉建議請沒參與設計的孩子再檢查一次。費洛同意，並用簡單的話解釋規則。孩子提問時，他們認真回答，沒有取笑。小隊決定在學校計畫上保留三種選擇。他們沒有說作品已經完美，只說可以接受下一次誠實測試，而目前這樣就夠了。

When evening traffic grew busy, the teacher asked the children to leave by the safe gate. Milo stayed to water a plant near the door, because the leaves looked dry. “A game day is like this plant,” he said. “It will not grow well if we only care for one side.” Nela knew his words were not a rule yet. They would need to test every station and change what did not work. That night she looked over their page by a lamp and saw that less competition did not mean less excitement. It could mean more ways to take part.

傍晚校門外交通變得忙碌，老師請孩子們從安全的大門離開。米洛留下來替門邊的植物澆水，因為葉子看起來有點乾。「遊戲日就像這盆植物，」他說，「如果只照顧一邊，它就長不好。」妮拉知道那還不是一條真正的規則。他們得試玩各站，再修改不合適的地方。當晚，她在燈下重看寫滿意見的紙，發現競爭少一些，並不代表興奮會變少，而是可能有更多參與的方法。
