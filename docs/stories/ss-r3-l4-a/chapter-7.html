留給最後一個人的路

A Route for the Last Person

零段位情報員：城市不該只聽強者說話 · 第 7 / 25 章 · 3000–4000 · 英中對照 · 校訂中

Cold frost covered the upper station when Zhiwei arrived. An emergency helicopter hovered above the platform, bringing supplies to residents isolated by closed roads. Below it, a farmer tried to lead a herd of goats and an elderly mule toward a shelter. A volunteer called the animals a slight concern beside the city's main dangers. Zhiwei disagreed. If a route worked only for strong people without burdens, it was not a safe route for a community. She asked the team to analyze the full surroundings, including the steps, narrow turns, and icy ground. The decision required mutual trust between fighters, drivers, and those who could barely walk. None of them could succeed by refusing to understand the others.

知微抵達時，上方車站覆著一層寒霜。緊急直升機盤旋在月台上方，替道路封閉而受困的居民運送物資。下方，一名農夫試圖領著一群山羊和一頭年老的騾子前往收容所。一名志工說，和城市主要危機相比，動物只是小問題。知微不同意。如果路線只適合沒有負擔的強壯者，就不算是適合社區的安全路線。她請團隊分析周圍全部環境，包括階梯、窄彎和結冰地面。這個決定需要武者、司機和幾乎走不動路的人互相信任。若拒絕理解彼此，沒有人能獨自成功。

The station supervisor displayed a remarkable diploma in emergency planning. He had trained officers to move quickly through dangerous areas, and his record showed impressive accuracy in tests. Still, the trial courses did not include wheelchairs or livestock. Zhiwei asked whether they could adapt his methods. He thought she was a competitor trying to embarrass him. “You want to oppose my plan before the cameras,” he said. “No,” she replied. “I want the last person to arrive, not just the first.” Luo An offered to test both routes without announcing which one she preferred.

車站主管展示了一張引人注目的緊急規劃文憑。他訓練警員快速穿越危險地帶，考試紀錄的準確率也很高。然而測試路線並沒有輪椅或牲畜。知微詢問是否能調整方法。主管以為她是想讓他難堪的競爭對手。「妳想在鏡頭前反對我的計畫。」他說。「不是，」她回答，「我希望最後一個人也能抵達，而不只第一個。」羅安提議測試兩條路線，不事先宣布偏好。

A plumber from the lower ward agreed to inspect the drainage channel beside the stairs. He found a broken cover that could injure anyone who slipped. Workers repaired it while the team marked alternative passages. The cold grew intense, but the farmer refused to abandon the mule. Zhiwei arranged a longer ground-level path that avoided the broken steps. It required twelve more minutes, a reduction in speed but an improvement in safety. The volunteers measured each journey, counting the slowest walker as carefully as the fastest runner.

一名來自下區的水管工同意檢查樓梯旁的排水道。他找到一塊破裂蓋板，任何滑倒的人都可能受傷。工人修好它時，團隊標出了替代通道。寒冷越來越刺骨，農夫卻拒絕拋下騾子。知微安排一條較長的平面道路，避開受損階梯。這要多花十二分鐘，速度降低，安全性卻提高。志工測量每次通行，對走得最慢的人和跑得最快的人一樣仔細計時。

By noon, the supervisor admitted that his old plan would have left several people behind. He offered an unusually remarkable correction: every future evaluation should include the slowest expected traveler. A local welfare officer supported the change because emergency transport had always served injured residents badly. Zhiwei asked them to publish the failure as well as the new standard. Hiding the first result would make the improvement difficult to trust. Their revised estimate showed that the safer route could carry ninety percent of the people in the same period, despite its extra length. That figure was worth publishing alongside the failure. The supervisor hesitated, then signed the revised report. He had lost no authority by admitting that a lesson had been learned.

中午時，主管承認舊方案會留下好幾個人。他提出一項出乎意料的顯著修正：往後所有評估都必須納入預期最慢的通行者。當地福利官員支持這項改變，因為緊急運輸一直不善於照顧傷者。知微請他們把測試失敗和新標準一併公開。隱藏初次結果，反而讓改進難以獲得信任。修訂後的估計顯示，儘管路程較長，新路線仍能在相同時間內運送百分之九十的人。這個數字值得與失敗一同公布。主管猶豫片刻，簽下修訂報告。承認學到教訓，並沒有讓他失去權威。

During the final test, a new warning appeared over the station. The arrow pointed down a steep service road that had no shelter at the end. Luo An heard the genuine pulse approaching from the east, but the arrow directed everyone west. Zhiwei checked the station timetable against the public clock; both agreed. The warning source, however, identified a different tower. “That tower is beyond the ridge,” Dao said through the radio. “It cannot see this platform.” The team stopped the test, used the verified ground route, and reached the shelter before the pulse passed.

最後一次測試期間，車站上空出現新警報。箭頭指向陡峭的工作道路，盡頭卻沒有收容設施。羅安聽見真實波動從東邊接近，箭頭卻把所有人往西引。知微核對車站時刻表與公共時鐘，兩者一致。然而警報來源顯示的是另一座訊號塔。「那座塔在山脊另一邊，」刀先生透過無線電說，「它根本看不到這個月台。」團隊停止測試，改走已確認的地面路線，在波動經過前抵達收容所。

The farmer returned after dark to thank the volunteers. His mule was resting, and every family member had reached safety. Zhiwei copied the tower number into her notebook. Two apparently separate districts were now receiving warnings from locations that did not match their real observation points. Such errors could no longer be explained by one damaged clock. But she still lacked the record showing who had chosen those tower assignments. She would need it before the five-day trial expired, or the ministry could dismiss the mountain test as an unusual local problem.

入夜後，農夫回來感謝志工。他的騾子已經休息，所有家人也都平安抵達。知微把訊號塔號碼抄進筆記本。如今已有兩個看似無關的街區，收到來自與真正觀測點不符位置的警報。這些錯誤不可能再只用一座壞鐘解釋。但她仍欠缺誰指定訊號塔分派方式的紀錄。她必須在五天試行期結束前取得，否則防衛部可以把山區測試當成罕見的地方問題而不予理會。
