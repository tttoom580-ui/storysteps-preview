The Handwritten Riddle

The railway office had become a public craft center, where families repaired household objects and preserved photographs of each ancestor. Dao introduced Zhiwei to a retired technician who had designed the first warning clocks. The man was distinguished for his work, but he called the new system a lousy copy of an older idea. On the wall hung two nearly identical charts. One showed train departures; the other showed waves passing the river. “People misunderstand these charts,” he said. “They think matching lines mean matching causes. Sometimes they only mean one person copied the other.”

鐵路辦公室如今成了公共手工藝中心，居民在這裡修理家用品，也保存每位祖先的照片。刀先生介紹知微認識一名退休技師，他曾設計最早的警報時鐘。此人在業界備受尊敬，卻把新系統稱為舊理念的拙劣仿製品。牆上掛著兩張幾乎一模一樣的圖表，一張記錄列車發車，另一張記錄波動經過河岸。「人們誤解了這些圖表，」他說，「以為線條一致就代表原因一致。有時只是有人抄了別人的資料。」

The technician laid out a notebook filled with tiny handwriting. A riddle stood at the top of one page: “When two clocks agree, which one is lying?” Zhiwei smiled at the trick. The answer could be neither, one, or both; agreement was not independent proof. She suggested that they examine where the clock signals had originally been sent. The technician approved her suggestion but warned that any defensible answer required records from both the transmitting tower and the receiving station. “A clever guess may be useful,” he said, “but a public decision needs more.” He offered to show them the old sending equipment after the day’s emergency drill, as long as the building manager permitted the inspection and a second technician witnessed it.

技師攤開一本字跡細小的筆記簿。一頁頂端寫著謎題：「兩座時鐘一致時，哪一座在說謊？」知微笑了。答案可能是都沒說謊、其中一座說謊，或兩座都說謊；彼此一致不等於獨立證明。她建議檢查時鐘訊號最初從哪裡送出。技師認可這個建議，卻提醒，要提出站得住腳的答案，必須同時取得發射塔與接收站的紀錄。「聰明的猜測或許有幫助，」他說，「公共決策卻需要更多。」他答應在當天緊急演練後展示舊發射設備，但前提是建築管理員准許檢查，並有第二名技師在場見證。

A call interrupted them. At the nearby maternity shelter, a woman in the late months of pregnancy needed transport, but a warning had closed the shortest bridge. The woman was not in immediate labor; nevertheless, the clinic could not risk waiting until night. Luo An wanted to launch a fast rescue by carrying her across the bridge. The nurse disagreed. A fall could crush the equipment needed for monitoring and hurt the patient. Zhiwei offered another route along the riverside. Its distance was greater, but the last confirmed wave had moved in the opposite direction.

一通電話打斷了他們。附近孕婦收容所裡，一名懷孕後期的婦女需要轉送，但警報關閉了最近的橋。她還沒有立刻臨盆，診所卻不能冒險等到夜裡。羅安想發動快速救援，抱著她穿過橋。護理師不同意；跌倒可能壓壞監測設備，也會傷害病人。知微提出沿河岸繞路。路程較長，但最後一次確認的波動正往相反方向移動。

The emergency team checked the route together. One worker unrolled a hose to clear water from a low tunnel; another equipped the vehicle with extra blankets and a battery lamp. Dao arranged a driver who knew the road's uneven surface. The pregnant woman asked to hear their plan before agreeing. Zhiwei explained the evidence, the remaining risks, and the option of staying in the clinic. Nobody treated her as a silent parcel. She chose to go, accompanied by the nurse. After forty careful minutes, the vehicle reached the hospital without encountering a surge.

緊急小隊共同核對路線。一名工人拉出水管，清除低矮隧道中的積水；另一人替車輛配備額外毛毯和電池燈。刀先生找來熟悉坑窪路面的司機。孕婦要求聽完計畫才做決定。知微說明證據、仍存在的風險，以及繼續留在診所的選項。沒有人把她當成沉默的包裹。她選擇由護理師陪同出發。四十分鐘後，車輛平安抵達醫院，途中沒有遇到回響潮。

On their return, Luo An confessed her guilt for having suggested the bridge. “I felt one weak pulse and thought my strength could handle it,” she said. Zhiwei disagreed with her conclusion but respected the admission. “Your ability to hear the wave matters,” she answered. “We just cannot let that single sense decide everything.” They walked beside a narrow canal where children practiced writing with sticks. One child made a long stroke in the dirt, then another beside it. Zhiwei suddenly remembered the two matching charts and understood what to ask the technician next.

回程時，羅安承認自己因提議走橋而感到內疚。「我只感應到一股微弱波動，就以為自己的力量應付得了。」她說。知微不同意她原本的結論，卻尊重這份坦白。「妳聽見波動的能力很重要，」她回答，「我們只是不能讓單一感官決定一切。」兩人沿著窄運河走，孩子們正用樹枝練字。一個孩子在泥地劃出長長一筆，又在旁邊劃出另一筆。知微突然想起那兩張相同圖表，明白接下來該問技師什麼。

The technician agreed to look for the original signal drawings. He warned them that his former employer might regard his involvement as disloyal. “That is the contrary of what public service should mean,” Dao said. The man laughed without happiness. Zhiwei promised not to publish his name without consent. The evidence had to survive even if a witness needed privacy. She went home with a copied riddle, a safe hospital route, a careful promise to protect the nurse’s report, and the suspicion that the arrow network was borrowing its timings from an unrelated system. The next question was why anyone would want that to happen.

技師同意尋找原始訊號圖，但提醒他們，前雇主可能把他的參與視為不忠。「這恰恰違反公共服務應有的意義，」刀先生說。技師苦笑了一下。知微答應未經同意不公開他的名字。即使證人需要隱私，證據也必須能夠留存。她帶著抄下的謎題、一條已驗證的醫院路線、一項妥善保護護理師報告的承諾，以及一項懷疑回家：箭頭網路可能借用了不相干系統的時間資料。接下來的問題是，誰會希望事情變成這樣。
