列車長的訊號

The Conductor's Signal

The five-day trial began on the anniversary of the city's first elevated railway. Its last surviving conductor, Mr. Dao, wore a coarse wool coat and carried a whistle polished by forty years of work. He had joined the safety campaign because his daughter could no longer reach the factory where she earned her living. “I can assure you that trains leave when a clock says they leave,” he told Zhiwei, “but I cannot assure you that the clock is right.” His simple distinction pleased her. It described the whole investigation better than any official speech. For once, she felt that an older worker understood why exact details mattered more than a bright uniform or a celebrated rank.

五天試行期開始時，正好是城市第一條高架鐵路通車的週年紀念日。最後一位仍在世的老列車長刀先生穿著粗呢外套，帶著一只經四十年工作磨得發亮的哨子。他參加安全改革運動，是因為女兒已無法抵達賴以維生的工廠。「列車會照鐘上的時間發車，這點我可以向妳保證，」他對知微說，「但我無法保證那座鐘是準的。」知微喜歡這個簡單的區別，比任何官方演說都更能說明整場調查。她第一次覺得，年長的工人真正理解精確細節為何比耀眼制服或受人讚頌的段位更重要。

Their task was to devise a way for people without martial power to confirm an alert. Luo An listened for the waves; Zhiwei read the tower clock; and Dao used the whistle to mark their shared time. A diligent apprentice recorded each result. The first evaluation found that the alarm frequency matched the waves, but the route arrows appeared to move independently. “That will complicate our report,” Taro said. Zhiwei agreed. The team could not claim that the whole network was false. They could only show where it told the truth and where it did not.

他們的任務，是設計一套沒有武者力量也能確認警報的方法。羅安負責聆聽波動，知微讀取訊號塔時鐘，刀先生則吹哨同步大家的時間。一名勤勞的學徒記錄每次結果。首次評估顯示警報頻率與波動吻合，但路線箭頭似乎會自行改變。「這會讓報告更複雜，」太郎說。知微同意。團隊不能宣稱整個系統都是假的，只能指出它在哪裡說了真話，又在哪裡說錯了。

At midday they visited the mineral works beside the river. Generations of workers had called it the Iron Dynasty, although no family had ruled it for years. A leak sent white foam across the yard, where a frayed cord hung from an old measuring device. Workers worried that the echo frequency had risen. Zhiwei checked the timestamps and discovered that the foam appeared five minutes before the warning. It came from an ordinary pressure release. She did not dismiss the workers' fear; she showed them the difference between an unfamiliar machine sound and the low pulse Luo An could detect.

正午，他們參訪河邊的礦物工廠。好幾代工人稱它為「鋼鐵王朝」，雖然早已沒有任何家族統治工廠。洩漏的白色泡沫漫過庭院，一條磨損的繩線垂在舊測量器旁。工人擔心回響潮頻率又升高了。知微核對時間，發現泡沫比警報早五分鐘出現，來源只是普通的壓力排放。她沒有否定工人的恐懼，而是讓他們看見陌生機械聲與羅安感應到的低頻脈動有何不同。

An argument began over who should provide the additional labor. Management wanted unpaid volunteers; the workers said extra inspection deserved wages. Zhiwei refused to call their disagreement selfish. The safety program had to protect the needy, not take more from them. A local teacher trained in psychology offered to help people manage the stress of repeated alarms, but she warned that calm words could not replace reliable information. “If you want people to trust the system,” she said, “make its decisions visible.” Dao nodded. He had spent a lifetime telling passengers why a train was late rather than telling them simply to be quiet.

現場開始爭論，額外勞力應由誰提供。管理層希望志工無償協助，工人則認為額外檢查應該有工資。知微拒絕把這場爭議說成自私。安全計畫應保護有需要的人，而非從他們身上拿走更多。一名受過心理學訓練的教師願意協助民眾處理反覆警報帶來的壓力，但提醒大家，安撫人心不能取代可靠資訊。「想讓人信任系統，」她說，「就把決策過程公開。」刀先生點頭。他花了一輩子告訴乘客列車為何晚點，而不是只叫他們安靜。

Near evening, the apprentice admitted that his first recording was partly an imitation of Dao's handwriting. He had been frightened of making a mistake and had copied the older man's figures. Zhiwei thanked him for telling the truth, then removed that entry from the evidence. “A courageous report is not one that looks perfect,” she said. “It is one that admits what we do not know.” The apprentice blushed but continued working. Their next independent measurement confirmed a three-minute gap between the real wave and one district's route change.

傍晚，學徒承認自己第一筆紀錄有一部分是在模仿刀先生的筆跡。他害怕記錯，便抄了前輩的數字。知微感謝他坦白，隨即把那一筆從證據中剔除。「勇敢的報告不是看起來完美的報告，」她說，「而是承認我們不知道什麼的報告。」學徒臉紅了，卻繼續工作。下一次獨立測量確認，真實波動和某區路線變更之間相差三分鐘。

When the whistle sounded at the end of the day, Zhiwei stood beside the empty tracks and tried to reflect on the costs of their method. It required time, skilled people, and permission to make mistakes in public. The ministry's single bright arrow was easier to follow. But an easy answer could be expensive if it sent people the wrong way. Dao invited her to inspect the station office the next morning. His staff had preserved old route books in case electronic boards failed. Zhiwei hoped the books might show when the suspicious corrections first began.

一天結束，哨聲再度響起，知微站在空鐵軌旁反省他們方法的代價。它需要時間、受訓人員，還需要讓人可以在公共場合犯錯的空間。防衛部那道亮眼的單一箭頭容易遵從得多；但若把人送往錯誤方向，簡單的答案也可能付出巨大代價。刀先生邀她隔天查看車站辦公室。員工保留了舊路線簿，以防電子看板故障。知微希望那些簿子能顯示可疑的校正究竟何時開始。
