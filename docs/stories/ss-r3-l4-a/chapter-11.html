The Fourth Input

零段位情報員：城市不該只聽強者說話 · 第 11 / 25 章 · 3000–4000 · 英中對照 · 校訂中

The unsigned note led Zhiwei to a commercial records office beneath the city's tallest skyscraper. Its entrance displayed a bright message about public commerce and the recovery of damaged neighborhoods. The clerk offered an assurance that every contract was available for inspection, but his desk held a separate list of exceptions. Zhiwei asked for the definitions behind those exceptions. “Don't make an accusation before you read them,” the clerk warned. She agreed. She had no desire to boast about catching a criminal before she even knew what the fourth input meant. “I will not accuse anyone without proof,” she told the clerk. He looked relieved that she was taking the distinction seriously.

沒有署名的紙條把知微引向全市最高摩天大樓地下的商業紀錄室。入口掛著關於公共商業活動及受損社區復原的明亮標語。職員保證每份合約都可查閱，但他的桌上另有一張例外清單。知微詢問那些例外的定義。「還沒讀就別先指控，」職員警告。她同意。連第四項輸入是什麼都不知道，她不想急著誇耀自己抓到了罪犯。「沒有證據，我不會指控任何人。」她告訴職員。職員看見她認真對待這個區別，似乎鬆了口氣。

The first contract allowed Meridian Data to manufacture warning equipment for the public network. The second was more troublesome. It allowed the company to rank properties by expected resistance to damage and to recommend which streets should remain open. A map of insured buildings appeared beside each warning district. The placement was remarkably consistent: expensive blocks had been marked safe more often than poorer ones, even when their wave measurements were similar. This did not prove deliberate discrimination. A wealthy district might simply have stronger walls. Zhiwei copied the data and wrote down that alternative explanation too.

第一份合約允許經線數據為公共網路製造警報設備。第二份更棘手：它允許公司依照建築物預期承受損害的能力為房地產排序，並建議哪些街道應保持開放。每個警報區旁都附著受保險保障的建築地圖。分布模式驚人地一致：即使波動測量相近，昂貴街區仍更常被標成安全。這還不能證明蓄意歧視；富裕地區可能只是牆更堅固。知微抄下資料，也寫下這個替代解釋。

At the office's annual convention, a Meridian engineer had given an informative talk about protecting city assets. His slides included a quiet sentence: “Route priority may reflect future insured loss.” The wording might expose the missing input. Zhiwei asked whether the phrase referred to buildings rather than human lives. The clerk said the ministry had never made the formula public. “You are loyal to your employer,” she told him, “but public loyalty also means keeping accurate records.” He paused before giving her the file number of the original presentation, which remained in the city archive.

在紀錄室年度大會上，一名經線數據工程師曾做過資訊豐富的簡報，主題是保護城市資產。投影片裡有一句不顯眼的話：「路線優先順序可能反映未來受保損失。」這可能揭露缺少的那項輸入。知微詢問這句話是否指建築物，而不是人的生命。職員說，防衛部從未公開公式。「你對雇主很忠誠，」她對他說，「但對公眾的忠誠也包括保存正確紀錄。」他沉默片刻，交給她那份簡報原件在市檔案館的編號。

That afternoon a hurricane warning reached the coast, followed by a notice about a hydrogen storage facility near the port. The two dangers were unrelated to the echo surges, but public screens combined them into one red area. The confusion delayed trucks carrying food. The workers' association asked everyone to contribute verified information rather than repeat rumors. A nurse explained which streets needed emergency access; Dao confirmed that the northern railway had power. Zhiwei marked each source separately. A shared map could be honest only if its readers could tell which warning came from which cause.

下午，沿海地區收到颶風警報，接著港口附近一處氫氣儲存設施也發出通知。這兩種危險與回響潮無關，公共螢幕卻把它們合成一個紅色區域。混亂延誤了運送糧食的貨車。工人協會請大家提供查證過的資訊，而不是散播謠言。一名護理師說明哪些道路需要供救護車使用；刀先生確認北方鐵路仍有電。知微分別標記來源。一張共享地圖要誠實，讀圖者就必須分得清每項警報由什麼原因造成。

Later, a frightened guard complained that civilians showed ignorance of how hard emergency work had become. Luo An answered without anger. “My team is tired too,” she said. “But our misfortune does not give us permission to conceal mistakes.” She proposed a short period of relaxation for the volunteers after the next shift, because exhausted recorders were beginning to copy one another's notes. Zhiwei supported her. Independent observations were valuable only when the observers were awake enough to make them. They set a rest schedule and returned to the fourth-input file when the storm passed.

後來，一名害怕的守衛抱怨平民無知，不懂緊急工作變得多辛苦。羅安沒有生氣地回答：「我的隊伍也累了。但我們遭遇不幸，不代表可以隱瞞錯誤。」她提議下一班結束後讓志工短暫休息，因為疲倦的紀錄員開始互相抄寫筆記。知微支持。只有觀測者清醒到足以親自測量，獨立觀測才有價值。他們安排休息表，等風暴過去再回頭追查第四項輸入。

At dusk, the storm cleared enough for a final check. Taro compared the harbor warnings with the property map. The direction changes followed insurance priorities more closely than they followed observed waves. That connection was strong but still incomplete. Zhiwei needed the formula itself, not merely a pattern. She wrote three questions for the coming audit: who chose the property weights, when they were applied, and whether any official had approved them in public. The answers would decide whether the company had made an engineering error or had quietly changed the meaning of public safety. Both possibilities deserved a careful hearing, with documents that every side could inspect.

黃昏時，風暴稍歇，足以讓他們做最後一次檢查。太郎把港口警報與房產地圖相比，發現箭頭變向更接近保險優先排序，而非實際波動方向。這個關聯很強，卻還不完整。知微需要公式本身，不能只有模式。她為即將到來的稽核寫下三個問題：誰決定房產權重？何時套用？是否有官員公開核准？答案將決定公司究竟犯了工程錯誤，還是悄悄改寫了公共安全的意義。兩種可能都值得審慎聽取，而且文件應讓各方都能查閱。
