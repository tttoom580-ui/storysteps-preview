第五天的選擇

The Fifth-Day Choice

On the fifth day, the trial ended at a public transit facility filled with an uneasy atmosphere. Residents gathered to decide whether the double-check system deserved acceptance. A newspaper publisher had printed both the official claims and the volunteers' results. Some called Zhiwei ambitious, implying that she wanted a government promotion. Others offered sincere thanks. She found both reactions uncomfortable. The work had not made her stronger, and she had no desire to become an official who could silence questions with a title. She wanted a map that an ordinary passenger could test.

第五天，試行計畫在一座公共轉運設施舉行成果會議，現場氣氛不安。居民聚集，決定是否接受雙重核對制度。一家報社已印出官方說法與志工測試結果。有些人稱知微很有野心，暗示她想靠此升官；另一些人真誠道謝。兩種反應都讓她不自在。這份工作沒有讓她變強，她也不想當上只靠頭銜就能讓人閉嘴的官員。她只希望有一張普通乘客也能核對的地圖。

Banu arrived with a group of displaced families. A young refugee carried a box of winter stockings donated by the shelter. The child asked whether the people in his new district would be punished for taking the wrong road. His question was more difficult than the technical report. Zhiwei told him the trial had produced a second option for checking dangerous routes, but it had not ended the threat. The city's map would have to transform from an order people obeyed into a public explanation people could examine. Such a change required law as well as software.

巴努帶著一群流離失所的家庭前來。一名年輕難民抱著收容所捐贈的冬襪。他問，來到新街區的人若走錯路，還會不會受罰？這問題比技術報告更難回答。知微告訴他，試行已產生第二種確認危險路線的方法，但威脅還沒結束。城市地圖必須從「人們應服從的命令」轉變成「公眾能檢查的說明」。這種改變需要的不只是軟體，還需要法律。

The main demonstration used three signals: an authentic tower reading, a deliberately delayed copy, and a false location selected by the researchers. Volunteers were asked to classify each result before seeing the answer. They learned that genuine waves and manipulated records could look almost identical on a public screen. When Qiao accused them of using offensive language about public servants, Zhiwei showed the exact words in her report. She had criticized procedures, not named people as criminals. “Facts can be unpleasant,” she said, “but a report should still protect the dignity of everyone involved.”

主要展示使用三組訊號：真正的訊號塔讀數、刻意延遲的副本，以及研究人員指定的假位置。志工在看到答案前，必須先分類每一組結果。他們發現真正的波動和遭操縱的紀錄，在公共畫面上幾乎一模一樣。喬指控他們用冒犯性語言談論公務員，知微便展示報告中的原句。她批評的是程序，沒有把具名人士稱為罪犯。「事實可能令人不快，」她說，「但報告仍應保護所有人的尊嚴。」

In the next test, a fighter and a civilian observer recorded the same event, then independently wrote their times on sealed cards. The observers explained that readings might differ by less than a second even when both were correct. The control room's arrow, however, changed direction six minutes later. Taro explained that the result was genuine evidence of a processing delay, though it did not prove who was responsible. Qiao refused to answer questions about Meridian Data's private calculations. The refusal caused visible embarrassment among several officials. One put down her pen and asked to see the contract rather than hear another statement of confidence.

下一項測試裡，一名武者與一名普通觀測員記錄同一事件，各自把時間寫在封好的卡片上。觀測者說，即使雙方都正確，讀數也可能相差不到一秒；控制室的箭頭卻在六分鐘後才改變方向。太郎說明，這是真實顯示處理延遲的證據，但還不能證明責任歸屬。喬拒絕回答經線數據私人計算方式的問題。這樣的拒絕讓好幾名官員露出尷尬神情。其中一人放下筆，要求直接看合約，而不是再聽一次充滿信心的聲明。

A sharp scent of perfume drifted through the hall as a company representative entered carrying a sealed folder. She announced that the contractor's engineers had offered their resignation if the city disclosed the code. Zhiwei considered the threat carefully. She had no wish to ruin anyone's career, but public safety could not depend on private secrets. She proposed protecting personal names while publishing the logic used to change routes. The representative left without answering. Luo An, now used to public meetings, remained by the door to make sure nobody blocked the exit during the argument.

一名公司代表帶著封口文件夾走進會場，香水味隨之飄過。她宣布，若城市公開程式碼，承包商的工程師將提出辭職。知微仔細思考這項威脅。她無意毀掉任何人的職涯，但公共安全不能依賴私人祕密。她提議保護個人姓名，同時公開變更路線所使用的邏輯。代表沒有回答便離開。如今已習慣公開會議的羅安守在門邊，確保爭辯期間沒有人堵住出口。

Before the vote, an alert struck the eastern entrance. A crowd began to run, but the new verification team checked the incoming pulse and immediately noticed that the suggested route led under a damaged roof. They opened the tested ground passage instead. Two officers helped rescue a man who had fallen near the steps. Nobody needed to overpower a great enemy. The crowd moved slowly, passed through the safe gate, and reached the courtyard without injury. The demonstration had become a real emergency, and the plan had survived a test no one had scheduled.

投票前，東入口突然響起警報。人群開始奔跑，新驗證小隊卻先核對傳來的波動，立刻發現建議路線通往一處損壞屋頂下方。他們改開啟測試過的平面通道。兩名警員協助救起跌倒在台階旁的男子。沒有人需要擊敗強大的敵人。人群緩慢前進，穿過安全大門，最後毫髮無傷地抵達庭院。展示變成了真正的緊急事件，而計畫經受住一項誰也沒有安排的測試。

The council voted to extend independent verification for thirty days and ordered an audit of the route-selection contract. Qiao retained command of martial emergency responses, but he could no longer close streets without showing the supporting record. Zhiwei welcomed the decision cautiously. Their first ten days had proved that some errors were real and that cooperative methods could protect people. They had not proved why the errors appeared. When she returned to the opera house, she found an unsigned note beneath the ticket counter: “Look for the fourth input. Trains, waves, and people are only three.”

議會投票將獨立核對制度延長三十天，並下令稽核路線選擇合約。喬繼續指揮武者緊急應變，但再也不能在不公布支持紀錄的情況下封路。知微謹慎歡迎這項決議。最初十天證明部分錯誤確實存在，也證明合作方法能保護人。他們卻仍不知道錯誤為何出現。回到歌劇院時，知微在售票櫃檯下發現一張沒有署名的紙條：「找出第四項輸入。列車、波動和人，只是其中三項。」
