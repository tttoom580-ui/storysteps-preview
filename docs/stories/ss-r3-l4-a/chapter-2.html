油漆下的時刻表

The Timetable Under the Paint

By morning, the opera house had become a temporary shelter. Mr. Han offered Zhiwei the convenience of a basement key, although the defense office had ordered staff to stay upstairs. His first remark was practical: “If you want the old records, bring a dry cloth. That room floods.” A faded poster on the stairway advertised a prominent fighter's autograph session from ten years earlier. Someone had tried to carve the fighter's name into the wooden frame. Zhiwei stepped around a sleeping dog whose injured paw was held in place by an elastic bandage.

隔天早上，歌劇院已成為臨時收容所。雖然防衛辦公室命令工作人員留在樓上，韓先生仍方便知微借用地下室鑰匙。他的第一句提醒十分實際：「想拿舊紀錄，就帶塊乾布來。那間房會淹水。」樓梯間褪色的海報，宣傳著十年前一位知名武者的簽名活動。有人試著把那名武者的名字刻在木框上。知微繞過一隻睡著的狗；牠受傷的腳掌纏著彈性繃帶。

At the basement door, they met Banu, an immigrant clerk who had managed the building's lost-property desk for twelve years. She had been on the night shift and had watched officers carry three boxes out just after the bell. “They said the boxes were dangerous,” she explained. “That could be true. But they never showed me the initial inspection.” Inside, the storage room smelled of wet paper. A damaged timetable hung on the wall, and the departure times of every eastern bus had been changed in red ink. Someone had tried to disguise the change with fresh paint.

地下室門口，他們遇見巴努。她是一名移居本市的職員，管理這棟建築的失物招領櫃臺已十二年。昨晚她值夜班，看到官員在鐘響後不久搬走三個箱子。「他們說箱子很危險，」她解釋，「這也許是真的，但他們始終沒讓我看最初的檢查結果。」儲藏室裡瀰漫著濕紙味。牆上掛著損壞的時刻表，所有開往東區的公車出發時間都被紅筆改過。有人還試著用新油漆掩飾這項改動。

Zhiwei photographed the board with an old camera and took one careful rubbing of the dates. The red paint had covered an earlier route order. Under the top layer, she could still read “North Gate, eight forty.” The official alarm had sent everyone south at eight forty-two. That did not prove mischief or cruelty, she reminded herself. A dispatcher might simply have made a mistake. But the same office was now claiming that people who ignored the southbound arrows would lose their travel privilege for a month. Such a punishment could ruin a family's ability to reach work, school, or medicine.

知微用舊相機拍下告示板，再小心拓印日期。紅色油漆蓋住了更早的路線命令。上層底下，她仍讀得出「北門，八點四十分」。官方警報卻在八點四十二分把所有人引向南方。她提醒自己，這還不能證明有人惡作劇或存心殘酷；調度員也可能只是犯錯。但同一間辦公室如今宣稱，不遵從南向箭頭的人，將失去一個月的通行特權。這種處罰可能毀掉一個家庭就醫、上學或工作的機會。

A sudden whistle echoed above them. Banu pointed to a storage label: EXPLOSIVE PRESSURE RISK. It referred to an old heating tank, not a weapon. The pressure gauge had climbed while a broken valve trapped steam. Mr. Han reached for it, but Zhiwei stopped him. “We cannot know whether that metal is safe to touch,” she said. They cleared the room, notified the maintenance crew, and set a barrier at the stairs. The crew cut the pressure from a remote control before opening the door. Prevention, Zhiwei thought, was much less dramatic than a rescue, but it protected more people.

一聲尖銳的哨音突然從上方傳來。巴努指著儲物標籤：「爆裂壓力風險」。那指的是一只老舊加熱槽，不是武器。損壞的閥門把蒸氣困住，壓力表正在升高。韓先生伸手想處理，知微阻止他。「我們根本不知道那塊金屬能不能安全碰觸。」她說。眾人撤離房間，通知維修人員，並在樓梯口設障。維修隊先從遠端釋放壓力，才重新開門。知微心想，預防遠不如救援戲劇化，卻能保護更多人。

During the delay, Luo An arrived with an unofficial request. Her captain wanted feedback on the courtyard evacuation, especially the moment when her team stopped trusting the arrows. Zhiwei answered every question but refused to sign a statement saying the system was broken. “It might be inaccurate,” she said. “Or somebody might have supplied false locations. We should not confuse the two.” Luo An nodded. She was starting to understand why the ministry disliked map readers: a map could question decisions without raising a fist. Still, the ministry could close doors, and Zhiwei had no badge to open them.

等待期間，羅安帶來一項非正式請求。隊長想聽庭院疏散的回饋，尤其想知道他們何時不再相信那些箭頭。知微回答了每個問題，卻拒絕在聲明上簽署「系統已經故障」。她說：「也許是系統不準，也可能是有人輸入假位置。兩件事不能混為一談。」羅安點點頭。她開始理解防衛部為何討厭讀圖的人：地圖不需要揮拳，也能質疑決策。但防衛部有權關門，知微卻沒有徽章能把門打開。

They returned to the wall. Banu discovered that the painted timetable had two layers of dates, yet both carried the same administrative code. In the older layer, a note predicted that the North District would prosper if a new transit line opened. In the later layer, the north was labeled unfit for residence. A political change could explain the difference, but not why the exact same code covered both documents. Zhiwei copied it into her notebook. She also marked the place where the paint had peeled back, in case another inspector wished to check her work rather than trust her word.

他們回到牆邊。巴努發現塗改的時刻表有兩層日期，兩者卻使用同一個行政編碼。較舊的一層寫著，新交通線開通後北區將繁榮；較新的一層則把北區標示成不適合居住。政策改變或許能解釋差異，卻無法解釋為何兩份文件使用完全相同的編碼。知微把編碼抄進筆記本，也標記了油漆剝落處，方便其他檢查員親自核對，而不必只信她的說法。

That afternoon, a child asked whether the dog could stay in the shelter. Qiao's staff said animals were forbidden because they might cause trouble. Banu asked them to show the rule in writing. No such rule appeared on the public notice. The dog stayed. Zhiwei found this tiny victory almost embarrassing compared with the damaged bridge. Yet it told her something important. If a clerk without rank could challenge an invented rule by asking for its source, perhaps the city did not need a stronger fighter first. Perhaps it needed people willing to ask exactly what the warning said, when it was written, and by whom.

下午，一個孩子問受傷的狗能否留在收容所。喬的職員說動物禁止入內，因為牠們可能惹麻煩。巴努請他們拿出白紙黑字的規定。公開公告上根本沒有這一條。狗留了下來。和被毀的橋相比，知微甚至覺得這場小小勝利有點不好意思。然而它告訴她一件重要的事：如果一名沒有段位的職員，只靠追問規則來源就能挑戰憑空捏造的禁令，也許這座城市首先需要的不是更強的武者，而是願意追問警報到底寫了什麼、何時寫下、又是誰寫的人。
