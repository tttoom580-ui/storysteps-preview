船上的示意圖

The Diagram on the Vessel

The audit team boarded a river vessel that carried emergency supplies between districts. Its captain, a former miner, had kept an oval metal case containing old tower hardware. A small drawing on its lid showed how to insert a timing card and fasten the cover. “This is not a secret weapon,” he told Zhiwei. “It's the simplest clock test we ever had.” His gracious manner made the nervous volunteers relax. They had expected a dangerous machine, but the case contained only paper rolls, a mechanical switch, and a battery connection anyone could inspect.

稽核小隊登上一艘往返各區運送緊急物資的河船。船長是退休礦工，留著一只橢圓形金屬箱，裡面放的是舊訊號塔硬體。箱蓋上的小示意圖顯示如何插入計時卡並扣牢蓋子。「這不是祕密武器，」他告訴知微，「只是我們曾用過最簡單的時鐘檢驗器。」他親切有禮的態度讓緊張的志工放鬆下來。他們原以為會見到危險機械，箱裡卻只有紙捲、機械開關，以及任何人都能檢查的電池接頭。

Taro spread an illustration of the modern system beside the old case. Both measured pulse arrivals, but the newer software included a separate line for property-loss estimates. Zhiwei felt a familiar dread. The line was real. The question was whether its use was absolute or limited by other safeguards. She asked the team to keep their discipline and avoid making public claims until they had tested a full record. “Perfection is not our goal,” she said. “A repeatable finding is.” Their first task was to compare readings from the river tower with independent paper measurements. To keep the test fair, they numbered every roll before opening it and invited the captain to select the first sample. No one on the team would know which result came from which device until the measurements were finished.

太郎把現代系統的圖解攤在舊箱旁。兩套設備都測量脈動抵達時間，但新軟體另有一條房產損失估值的資料線。知微感到熟悉的恐懼。那條資料線確實存在，問題是它有沒有絕對優先權，還是受到其他保護措施限制。她請團隊遵守紀律，在完成全套紀錄測試前不要公開下結論。「我們的目標不是完美，」她說，「而是能重複驗證的發現。」第一項工作是把河岸訊號塔讀數與獨立紙本測量相比。為求公正，他們在拆封前先替每卷紙編號，請船長選第一份樣本。測量完成前，小隊不會知道各組結果出自哪一台設備。

Above them, a hawk circled the shoreline. The captain pointed to a low bridge and explained why the river distribution route had changed. When waters rose, boats used a longer passage that kept them away from the old stone supports. Zhiwei saw the same principle in the map problem. Safe routing meant balancing risks, not simply taking the shortest distance. But the calculation had to put people first. A city could not call a route safe merely because it reduced the price of damaged buildings. She asked the captain for his written bridge safety rules.

一隻鷹在河岸上空盤旋。船長指著低矮的橋，解釋河運配送路線為何改變：水位升高時，船隻會改走較長的通道，遠離老舊石墩。知微在地圖問題裡看見相同原則。安全路線應平衡風險，而不只是走最短距離；但計算必須以人為優先。城市不能只因某條路減少建築物的損失價格，就稱它安全。她向船長索取書面的橋梁安全規則。

The old device recorded three signals correctly. The modern display recorded them too, then moved the recommended route toward the insured warehouse district. This time there was no strong wave to explain the change. Taro calculated the result twice to improve efficiency without sacrificing accuracy. He showed that the property-loss line could outweigh the location of a wave under certain conditions. “This is a realistic problem,” he said. “It may have begun as a reasonable attempt to protect equipment, but it can now redirect people away from a physically safer road.”

舊設備正確記錄了三次訊號。現代螢幕也記錄了它們，卻把建議路線移往已投保的倉庫區。這次沒有強烈波動足以解釋變更。太郎計算兩次，兼顧效率與準確。他證明在某些條件下，房產損失資料線的權重可以超過波動位置。「這是實際存在的問題，」他說，「它最初也許只是想保護設備的合理嘗試，如今卻能把人引離物理上更安全的道路。」

The captain spoke of his brother, who had died in a warehouse collapse years earlier. His grief was quiet, but he remembered every detail of the evacuation. “The alarm saved the machines first,” he said. “We called it misfortune, but maybe it was a choice.” Zhiwei could not tell him what had happened in that older disaster. She offered only the current evidence. He thanked her for not pretending to know. When they returned to shore, he allowed the team to keep a certified copy of the paper test, so another group could examine it without borrowing his device.

船長提起多年前死於倉庫坍塌的哥哥。他的悲痛平靜，卻記得疏散時每個細節。「警報先保住機器，」他說，「我們當時叫它不幸，也許那其實是選擇。」知微無法告訴他舊災難究竟發生什麼，只能提供眼前的證據。他感謝她沒有假裝知道。回到岸上時，船長允許小隊保留紙本測試的認證副本，讓其他人不用借他的設備也能檢查。

At the close of the inspection, Ms. Song called with news: the council had agreed to renew access to the protected contract files. The team might soon obtain legal recognition as independent observers. Zhiwei did not celebrate yet. The contract had not answered who chose the weights, and changing the formula would require public authority. Still, she felt a new resolve. The problem was no longer an unclear rumor, and the evidence did not rest on one person's word. It rested on a device, a record, and an experiment that could be repeated.

檢查結束時，宋女士打電話來，說議會同意重新開放受保護合約檔案的查閱權。小隊也可能很快取得獨立觀測者的合法認可。知微還沒有慶祝。合約仍未說明是誰選擇權重，修改公式也需要公共授權。然而她有了新的決心。問題不再只是含糊謠言，證據也不再只靠某個人的說法，而是設備、紀錄，以及能夠重做的實驗。
