食堂裡的預報

The Forecast at the Canteen

零段位情報員：城市不該只聽強者說話 · 第 9 / 25 章 · 3000–4000 · 英中對照 · 校訂中

A workers' association invited Zhiwei to the harbor canteen after hearing about the station test. The room smelled of boiled lobster and cheap pasta. A bald cook kept a little fireplace burning although the weather was warm; he said it dried his rusty cooking tools. On the wall hung a newspaper quotation from the former transport minister: “Our warnings belong to the people.” Zhiwei wondered whether the minister had meant those words or simply liked the sound of them. Her curiosity drew her to a folder on the dining table.

港區工人協會聽說車站測試後，邀請知微到食堂。屋裡飄著水煮龍蝦和便宜義大利麵的味道。一名禿頭廚師燒著小壁爐，儘管天氣並不冷；他說那能把生鏽的廚具烘乾。牆上貼著前交通部長的報紙引言：「我們的警報屬於人民。」知微不知道部長真心相信這些話，還是只覺得它們好聽。她的好奇心被餐桌上的資料夾吸引。

Inside was the autobiography of a harbor engineer, along with the city's first official surge forecast. The engineer had helped install tower machinery years before the current emergency. His handwritten note said each tower's original purpose was to measure the arrival of waves, not to choose evacuation routes. The mapping service was added later. When Zhiwei asked whether that addition had been tested, the association leader shook his head. “We don't know. But the contractor claimed it could restore order in twenty seconds.” A complicated service had been presented as if its speed made it trustworthy.

資料夾裡是一名港口工程師的自傳，以及城市第一份正式回響潮預報。這名工程師多年前曾參與安裝訊號塔機械。他的手寫註記說，塔的原始用途是測量波動何時抵達，不是選擇疏散路線。地圖服務是後來新增的。知微詢問新增部分是否通過測試，協會代表搖頭。「我們不知道。但承包商宣稱它能在二十秒內恢復秩序。」一項複雜服務被包裝成只要夠快就值得信任。

At noon, a delivery wagon stopped outside. The driver had been told to halt at the harbor gate because his district showed a dangerous warning. Yet the physical tower logs showed no new wave. He feared the consequent loss of his day's wages if he waited. Zhiwei refused to declare the road safe based on old information alone. She sent Luo An to check the current pulse while Dao measured the tower time. Their readings agreed: the harbor road was clear for the next forty minutes, provided the warning did not change. The driver left with a written route and a contact number.

中午，一輛運貨車停在門外。司機被告知港口大門有危險警報，必須停車；然而實體訊號塔紀錄沒有任何新波動。他擔心等下去會失去當日工資。知微拒絕只依舊資料就宣告道路安全。她派羅安檢查現場脈動，刀先生則測量訊號塔時間。兩者讀數一致：若警報沒有改變，港口道路在接下來四十分鐘可通行。司機拿著書面路線與聯絡電話離開。

The association leader then showed Zhiwei a list of vacant positions in the ministry's records office. One vacancy had remained open for months, although the workload had doubled. A former employee had warned that every route change was now dependent on a contractor's private file. “That would violate the published procedure,” Zhiwei said. The leader replied that the worker had resigned and no longer wanted attention. Zhiwei asked whether the union could request records without naming him. The leader agreed. Their case should not force a frightened employee to become a public hero.

協會代表接著給知微看防衛部紀錄室的職缺清單。儘管工作量增加一倍，其中一個缺額已空了好幾個月。一名離職員工曾警告，現在每次更改路線，都依賴承包商的私人檔案。「這會違反公開程序。」知微說。代表回答，該員工已離職，不想再受關注。知微詢問工會能否不提姓名，直接要求公開紀錄。代表同意了。他們不該逼迫害怕的員工成為公開英雄。

As evening approached, a problem began to arise in several districts at once. Their arrows shifted west, then east, then west again. None of the shifts matched the waves that Luo An and other fighters reported. Zhiwei laid the harbor forecast beside the old railway book. The movement of the arrows looked familiar: it resembled the scheduling adjustments made when trains shared a crowded track. Somebody might have taken software meant to prevent railway delays and applied it to the movement of people. That choice could create an empty road on a screen while leaving a crowded street in reality.

接近傍晚時，數個街區同時出現問題。箭頭先往西移，再往東移，最後又轉回西方。沒有任何變更符合羅安和其他武者回報的波動。知微把港口預報放在舊鐵路簿旁。箭頭的移動方式很眼熟：它像列車共用繁忙鐵軌時的排程調整。有人可能把原本避免列車延誤的軟體套用到人群移動上。這個選擇會讓螢幕上的道路看似空無一人，現實街道卻仍然擁擠。

The cook brought tea when he noticed the maps spreading over his tables. “If you have found the cause,” he asked, “why not tell everyone tonight?” Zhiwei answered that a resemblance was not proof of a connection. They needed the software contract, a description of the inputs, and a test showing the same failure under controlled conditions. The cook sighed. He preferred a simple story with one villain. Zhiwei understood him. She preferred a simple story too, but a true one mattered more than a satisfying ending. She asked Dao to keep the railway book safe until morning. He placed it in a locked cabinet and wrote down the time. If the next day brought an accusation that the document had been changed, they would at least know who had been responsible for it overnight.

廚師發現地圖攤滿桌子，端來了茶。「既然找到原因，」他問，「何不今晚就告訴大家？」知微回答，相似不等於存在關聯。他們還需要軟體合約、輸入資料說明，以及在受控情況下重現相同故障的測試。廚師嘆氣。他寧可聽一個只有壞人的簡單故事。知微理解他；她也偏愛簡單故事，但真實比令人滿意的結局更重要。她請刀先生妥善保管鐵路簿，直到隔天早上。他把簿子鎖進櫃子，寫下時間。若隔天有人指控文件遭到改動，他們至少知道是誰在夜裡負責保管。
