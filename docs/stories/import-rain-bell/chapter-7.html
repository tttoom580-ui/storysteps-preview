停住的鐘

The Clock That Stopped

雨來之前的鐘聲 · 第 7 / 25 章 · 1000以下 · 英中對照 · 校訂完成

The clock in River House had stopped at six ten. Mia saw it through the window when Officer Su opened the outside yard for a short visit. No one was allowed into the old building. “I believe someone wants us to remember that time,” Mia said. Leo stood close beside her. “A stopped clock may be an error, not a message.” Jay laughed at the idea of a secret clock, but stopped when he saw Mia's face. He knew she had not slept well. Every day she heard the bell in her dream, even when the morning was quiet. The sound seemed to follow her home.

河屋裡的時鐘停在六點十分。蘇小姐短暫開放外院參觀時，米婭透過窗戶看見了它。沒有人可以進入舊屋。「我相信有人想要我們記住這個時間，」米婭說。李奧站在她身旁：「停住的時鐘可能只是故障，不是訊息。」杰覺得祕密時鐘很好笑，卻在看見米婭的表情後不再笑。他知道她最近睡得不好。即使早晨安靜，她每天夢裡還是聽見鐘響，那聲音彷彿一路跟她回家。

Leo asked the others to meet in his yard after school. He had put a bowl of water on a table, with a piece of wood, a rope, and a small toy bell above it. “Today we learn about nature and machines,” he said in a serious voice. Jay rolled his eyes. “You sound like a teacher.” Leo smiled. “Fine. When the water rises, the wood rises. As it goes up, the rope moves. Then the bell rings.” He added water to the bowl, and the bell made a small clear noise. It was simple, but Mia felt the idea settle in her mind. Nobody had touched the little bell.

李奧請大家放學後到他家院子集合。他在桌上放一碗水、一塊木頭、一條繩子，上方掛著小玩具鐘。「今天我們來研究自然和機械。」他一本正經地說。杰翻白眼：「你聽起來像老師。」李奧笑了：「好吧。水上升時，木頭也升起，繩子跟著移動，於是鐘就響了。」他往碗裡加水，小鐘發出清脆的聲音。原理很簡單，但米婭心裡有了想法。剛剛沒有人碰那口小鐘。

“So the water is talking?” Ruby joked. “In a way,” Leo said. “The bell is its voice.” He pointed to the rope. “But we still don't know whether that is how the real bell works. We need the old plan, or someone who saw the machine.” Jay said, “Maybe the ghost is a rich ghost who paid for a very good machine.” Ruby laughed, but Mia did not. “Please don't say ghost when we have no proof,” she said. Jay looked awkward. “Sorry. I thought a joke would make everyone feel better.” Mia could see he meant it, and her anger slowly passed.

「所以是水在說話？」露比開玩笑。「某種程度上是，」李奧說，「鐘就是水的聲音。」他指著繩子：「但我們還不知道真正的鐘是不是這樣運作。我們需要舊圖，或親眼看過機器的人。」杰說：「也許那隻鬼很有錢，花大錢買了好機器。」露比笑了，米婭沒有。「沒有證據時，拜託別再說鬼了。」她說。杰露出尷尬表情：「對不起，我以為開玩笑會讓大家好過一點。」米婭看得出他是真心的，怒氣慢慢消失。

After dinner Mia looked out at the sky. A rainbow had appeared above the last light rain, the first they had seen for many days. Her father came into her bedroom and noticed the open book. “You're still studying that bell?” he asked. “Just trying to learn the truth,” she said. “Dad, why did you sign a paper about the rope?” His hand stopped on the door. “It was my job,” he said. “The work was not finished the way we hoped.” “Did someone make a mistake?” Mia asked. He did not answer. Instead he said, “You can be proud of asking questions. But be careful with answers.”

晚餐後，米婭望向天空。久違的小雨過後，晚霞上方出現一道彩虹。父親走進她的臥室，看見打開的筆記本。「妳還在研究那口鐘？」他問。「只是想知道真相，」她說，「爸，你為什麼在繩子的文件上簽名？」父親的手停在門邊。「那是我的工作，」他說，「事情沒有像我們希望的那樣完成。」「有人犯錯了嗎？」米婭問。父親沒有回答，只說：「妳願意問問題很好，但給答案時要小心。」

Earlier that afternoon, before Mia spoke with her father, Leo had shown his model to a younger class. The children asked whether a storm could make the little bell ring louder. He said the model could not give a sure answer about the real house, because his yard was not the river. Mia watched him use the same careful words he had asked her to use. Later they made a new rule for their group: each guess must have a reason, and each reason must be checked. Jay wrote the rule on a piece of paper with a bright rainbow at the top. “Now we sound like a school club,” he said. This time Mia laughed with him.

那天下午較早時，米婭還沒與父親談話，李奧就已把模型展示給低年級學生看。孩子們問暴風雨會不會讓小鐘響得更大聲。他說模型無法肯定回答真實河屋會怎樣，因為自家院子不是河流。米婭看著他用自己要求別人使用的謹慎語言。後來他們為團隊訂新規則：每個猜測都得有理由，每個理由都得經過查證。杰在紙張上方畫了亮彩虹，把規則寫在下面。「現在我們像學校社團了。」他說。這次米婭和他一起笑。

The next morning Ruby listened again to the missing part of her film. She had saved a copy on her computer. The noise before the bell had a steady beat, like the little machine Leo had made. “Do you hear it?” she asked Mia. “Yes,” Mia said. “And it always comes from the same side.” They walked around the safe outer yard, watching the gate beyond the fence. The sound seemed to come from below the north wall. Mia wrote this in her notebook, then turned to Leo. “If the clock is only a clock, why does it show the exact time the bell first rang?” Leo looked toward the house. “That,” he said, “is a better question.”

隔天早上，露比又聽了一次影片裡缺失片段附近的聲音。她在電腦裡留了備份。鐘聲前的噪音有固定節奏，像李奧做的小機器。「你聽見了嗎？」她問米婭。「聽見了，」米婭說，「而且總是從同一邊來。」他們在安全的外院繞行，看著圍欄另一側的閘門。聲音似乎來自北牆下方。米婭把這些寫進筆記本，接著轉向李奧：「如果時鐘只是時鐘，為什麼剛好停在我們第一次聽到鐘聲的時間？」李奧望向屋子：「這才是個好問題。」
