高處的課桌

Desks on High Ground

The rain did not stop. Families came to the school by the new high route, carrying bags, food, and dry clothes. Officer Su asked the older students to help count people, not to go near the river. Jay led a group of children to a room with benches and bright pictures on the wall. A toy robot from the school program sat beside a paper tiger. “The tiger is our guard,” Jay told a weak little boy. The boy smiled for the first time that night. “Can it climb?” he asked. Jay lifted the paper tiger onto a desk. “Only if you help it.”

雨一直沒有停。家庭們沿著新的高處路線到學校，帶著袋子、食物和乾衣服。蘇小姐請年紀較大的學生協助清點人數，不要靠近河邊。杰帶一群孩子去有長椅、牆上貼著彩色圖片的教室。學校節目用的玩具機器人坐在紙老虎旁。「老虎是我們的守衛，」杰對一名虛弱的小男孩說。男孩那晚第一次笑了：「它會爬嗎？」杰把紙老虎舉上桌子：「只要你幫它。」

Mia and Ruby worked at the door with a list of names. They checked the grade of each student and wrote where their family would wait. “I don't understand this number,” Ruby said, pointing to a mark on the list. Mia explained that it was an old class code, not a room number. Near them a teacher showed a child how to spell her name. Another teacher moved plates of food onto a dry table and gave a baby a little water with help from its parent. “We are near the high road,” Mia told a worried family. “You're somewhere safe for now. Please stay inside.”

米婭和露比拿著名單守在門口，核對學生年級，記下家人等待的位置。「我不懂這個號碼。」露比指著名單上的記號。米婭解釋那是舊班級代碼，不是教室號碼。旁邊一位老師教小孩拼自己的名字。另一名老師把食物盤搬到乾燥桌上，在家長協助下給嬰兒喝一點水。「我們就在高處道路附近，」米婭告訴擔心的家庭，「目前這裡是安全的，請留在室內。」

A message came from Officer Su: the low road near the north gate could no longer be used. Mia read it twice and changed the arrow on the big map. Leo placed a new sign beside the stairs so no one would walk down by mistake. “The water is rising by an inch at a time,” he said, showing Mia the report. “That sounds small,” she answered. “It isn't small if it keeps going,” he said. She looked around the crowded room. A few days ago they had argued about a building. Tonight there were new people arriving who had never seen the River House at all.

蘇小姐傳來訊息：北閘門旁的低路已不能使用。米婭看了兩遍，改掉大地圖上的箭頭。李奧在樓梯旁放新標示，免得有人誤往下走。「水位一吋一吋升高，」他把報告拿給米婭看。「聽起來好像不多，」她說。「一直升就不少了。」李奧回答。她環顧擁擠的教室。幾天前，大家還為一棟房子爭論。今晚卻不斷有人抵達，他們甚至從未見過河屋。

When the last group was checked, one name had no mark beside it. It belonged to an older woman who lived alone near the market. Jay wanted to run down the road to look for her. “I know where her home is,” he said. “No,” Mia answered firmly. “We give the name to Officer Su.” The officer checked the information and called a trained adult team to follow the safe route. “You did the right thing,” she told Jay. “Finding a missing person starts with good information.” Jay looked down at the little tiger in his hands. He had wanted to be a hero. Instead he would wait and keep the other children calm.

最後一組核對完時，有個名字旁沒有記號。那是一位獨居市場附近的老婦人。杰想衝下坡去找她：「我知道她住哪裡。」米婭堅定回答：「不行。把名字交給蘇小姐。」蘇小姐確認資料，請受過訓練的成人隊伍沿安全路線尋找。「你做得對，」她對杰說，「尋找失聯者，要從正確資訊開始。」杰低頭看手裡的小老虎。他原本想當英雄，現在明白自己應該等待，安撫其他孩子。

During the next check Jay found a paper bat from the school show under a desk. He placed it beside the tiger and robot, then offered the children a game. “Can you make an animal name with these letters?” he asked. A boy with a sore leg spelled bat slowly. A girl asked for some sugar in her tea, and a teacher brought a little, after checking with her parent. The room was noisy but calmer than before. Jay looked at the attendance list. Names were still the most important things on the page. The toys helped the children wait, but the list helped the adults know who was safe.

下一次核對時，杰在桌下找到學校表演用的紙蝙蝠。他把它放在老虎與機器人旁，帶孩子玩遊戲。「能用這些字母拼出動物名稱嗎？」他問。一名腿痛的男孩慢慢拼出 bat。一個女孩想在茶裡加點糖，老師先詢問家長，才給她少量。房間仍吵鬧，但比先前平靜。杰看了出席名單。頁面上最重要的仍是名字。玩具幫孩子等待，名單則幫大人確認誰安全。

Before dawn the adult team found the woman at a neighbor's house on high ground. She was tired but safe. Jay smiled when he heard the news and told the little boy with the tiger. “Our guard did a good job,” he said. Yet a new message came almost at once. The north gate was still closed, and rain was falling harder on the hills. The town could not open the gate until the workers had checked the way to reach it. Mia looked at the sign she had changed on the map. The next choice belonged to adults, but she knew the documents they had found might help them make it.

天亮前，成人隊伍在高地的鄰居家找到那位老婦人。她很累，但平安。杰聽見消息笑了，還告訴抱著紙老虎的男孩：「我們的守衛做得很好。」可是新的消息幾乎立刻傳來：北閘門仍關著，山區的雨更大了。工人確認抵達閘門的路線之前，公所不能開閘。米婭看著地圖上自己更改的箭頭。下一個選擇必須由大人做出，但她知道他們找到的文件或許能幫忙決定。
