鐘聲之後

After the Bell

When the rain finally stopped, the town was tired, dirty, and still standing. The north gate was open, and the old water path was carrying water away from the houses. There had been damage near the lower road, but the school was safe and every person on the list had been found. Mia saw Officer Su in the town square and asked what would happen now. “First we check the roads and the buildings,” the officer said. “Then we make a new plan that people can trust.” Mia was glad no one called it a miracle. Many people had worked hard to make the ending possible. One man alone could never have done it.

雨終於停了，整座小鎮疲憊、髒亂，卻還好好站著。北閘門已經打開，舊水路正把水從住家旁引走。低處道路受了損，但學校安全，名單上的每個人也都找到了。米婭在廣場遇見蘇小姐，問接下來會怎樣。「先檢查道路和建築，」蘇小姐說，「再做一份大家可以信任的新計畫。」米婭很高興沒有人把這稱為奇蹟。是許多人努力工作，才讓這個結局成為可能，單靠一個男人絕對辦不到。

The River House sale was stopped until the town could study the whole water system. Mr. Han agreed to change the factory plan, though it would take time and money. His workers would still have jobs while the next steps were decided. Leo's mother was relieved. “We are lucky,” she said, “but we also have work to do.” Mia's father helped prepare a clear public report about the old test. He did not cut out his own mistake. When Ruby asked if she could film him, he said yes. “I want people to see what I knew and what I failed to check.” Mr. Han, Leo’s mother’s boss, agreed that this record was necessary.

在鎮上完整研究水路系統前，河屋出售案先停止。韓先生同意更改工廠計畫，雖然需要時間和費用。下一步決定前，工人仍保有工作。李奧的母親鬆了口氣：「我們很幸運，但還有事得做。」米婭的父親幫忙準備公開報告，說明舊測試經過。他沒有把自己的失誤刪掉。露比問能不能拍攝他，他說可以。「我希望大家知道我當年知道什麼，又漏查了什麼。」李奧母親的老闆韓先生也同意，這份紀錄很必要。

On a bright day two weeks later, Jay went to the airport with his uncle to meet his mother, who had been working abroad. She was a small woman with a noisy suitcase and a big smile. Jay ran to catch her hand. “I was useful,” he told her at once. “I counted children and helped them stay calm.” His mother laughed and pulled him close. “That sounds like much more than fun.” “It was scary too,” Jay admitted. “I wanted to run down the road, but Mia stopped me. That was a good thing.” When they came home, he showed her the little paper tiger that had helped a child smile.

兩週後的晴天，杰和叔叔到機場接回在海外工作的母親。她個子不高，拖著輪子很吵的行李箱，笑容很大。杰跑去抓住她的手。「我有幫上忙！」他立刻說，「我清點小孩的人數，幫他們保持冷靜。」媽媽笑著抱住他：「那聽起來不只是好玩。」「也很可怕，」杰承認，「我本來想跑下坡，米婭阻止了我。那是對的。」回家後，他給媽媽看那隻曾逗孩子微笑的紙老虎。

The town held a small party at the school theater to thank the people who had helped. Ruby made a short film with pictures of the river, the warning bell, the new safe route, and the volunteers. She did not show crying children or make Mr. Han into a monster. “I used to think a good story had to be loud,” she said. “Now I like a story that is true.” Leo prepared a small model of the water gate for the show. When he pushed a wooden part, the water changed direction. People clapped. Mia looked at the sight of her friends working together and felt something warm settle inside her.

鎮上在學校劇場舉辦小小的感謝活動。露比製作短片，放進河流、警鐘、新安全路線和志工的照片。她沒有展示哭泣的孩子，也沒有把韓先生拍成怪物。「我以前以為好故事一定要很大聲，」她說，「現在我喜歡真實的故事。」李奧為活動準備一個小水閘模型。推動木製零件後，水便改變方向。大家鼓掌。米婭看著朋友合作的畫面，心裡慢慢暖了起來。

Grandma Chen invited the children back to the River House yard. The old wooden root of a tree had grown beside the wall, but it had not broken the water path. A repair team would soon check the building and decide which rooms could be used again. “Can we make this place a home for both stories and safety?” Grandma asked. “We can try,” Mia said. “The town can hold a small sale of old books, but the first money must go to the work that keeps people safe.” Her grandmother smiled. The bell above them was quiet. It no longer felt like a question that nobody could answer.

陳奶奶請孩子們再回河屋外院。老樹根長在牆邊，卻沒有破壞水路。維修隊很快會再檢查建築，決定哪些房間能重新使用。「我們能把這裡變成同時承載故事與安全的地方嗎？」奶奶問。「可以試試，」米婭說，「鎮上能辦一場舊書義賣，但第一筆錢得先用來維護保護大家的設施。」奶奶微笑。頭頂的鐘安靜無聲。它不再像個沒有人能回答的問題。

That evening Mia sat with her father beside the river. “When I first heard the bell, I wanted one person to blame,” she said. “Then I wanted one person to save us.” Her father looked at the open water path. “And now?” “Now I know a town is made of many choices. Some were wrong. Some were brave. We have to remember both.” Her father put his hand over hers. The river moved past the stones without hiding under the road. Mia opened her notebook to a fresh page. She wrote a new title: What We Will Check Before the Next Rain. Then she began a list, and this time she knew she would not write it alone.

那天傍晚，米婭和父親坐在河邊。「第一次聽到鐘聲時，我想找一個人來責怪，」她說，「後來，我又想找一個人來救我們。」父親望著敞開的水路：「現在呢？」「現在我知道，一座小鎮是很多選擇組成的。有些是錯的，有些很勇敢。我們都得記得。」父親的手覆在她手上。河水流過石頭，不再被困在路面底下。米婭翻開筆記本新的一頁，寫下新標題：下次下雨前，我們要檢查什麼。然後她開始列清單。這一次，她知道自己不會獨自書寫。
