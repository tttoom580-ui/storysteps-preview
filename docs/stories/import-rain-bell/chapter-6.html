叔叔的盒子

The Receipt in Uncle's Box

雨來之前的鐘聲 · 第 6 / 25 章 · 1000以下 · 英中對照 · 校訂完成

Mia's uncle asked her to bring old photographs to a family friend downriver. They would travel by boat, then drive the last part of the way. “We have to arrive before lunch,” he told her. “The river can be slow on weekends.” Mia brought the small metal box, hoping he might know the mark drawn on it. On the pier a lion smiled down from a painted shop sign. Inside, the shop sold coffee and sandwiches. “That lion looks stupid,” Jay said, making Uncle Lin laugh. Mia said nothing. She had spent the night looking at the wave shape on the box and remembering her father's face.

叔叔請米婭替他把幾張老照片送給下游的家族朋友。他們會先搭船，再開車走最後一段路。「午餐前得抵達，」叔叔說，「週末的河上交通可能很慢。」米婭帶著小鐵盒，希望叔叔認得上面的記號。碼頭上，一塊店家招牌畫著微笑的獅子。店裡賣咖啡和三明治。「那獅子看起來好笨，」杰說，逗得叔叔笑了。米婭沒說話。昨夜她一直看著鐵盒上的波浪圖案，想起父親的臉。

On the boat the water was calm, except for small waves near the back. Uncle Lin pointed to an old white house high on the bank. “People went there when the river was terrible,” he said. “Not every flood reaches the same place.” Mia showed him the half map. He saw the wave sign and turned it around twice. “I made that sign with your grandfather,” he said finally. “It shows a place where water could move away from the town.” Jay leaned over to see, but his uncle held the paper high. “Keep it dry,” he said. “Paper can tell us things only while we can still read it.”

船上水面平靜，只有船尾附近有小小的波浪。叔叔指著岸上高處的一棟白色老屋。「河水很可怕時，大家就去那裡，」他說，「不是每次洪水都會淹到同一個地方。」米婭拿出半張地圖。叔叔看見波浪記號，把紙轉了兩次。「那是我和妳爺爺一起畫的，」他終於說，「表示水能從那裡離開小鎮。」杰湊過來看，叔叔卻把紙舉高。「別弄濕，」他說，「紙張還能讀時，才能告訴我們事情。」

They reached the friend's home after a short drive. Uncle Lin found a large box under a bed and began to look for old town papers. There were photographs, letters, and a newspaper about the last great storm. Mia saw people standing on the top floor of the school, waiting for help. Her uncle placed one picture back in the box quickly. “It was a terrible weekend,” he said. “Your father was trying to make the river safer. Many things went wrong.” Mia asked why the bell had been repaired. Uncle Lin looked surprised. “Who told you about that?” “No one. I heard it ring.”

短程車程後，他們抵達朋友家。叔叔在床底找到大盒子，開始翻找舊公所資料。裡面有照片、信件，以及報導上次大風雨的報紙。米婭看見人們站在學校頂樓，等著救援。叔叔很快把一張照片放回盒子。「那是可怕的一個週末，」他說，「妳爸爸那時想讓河道更安全，很多事卻出了錯。」米婭問警鐘為什麼曾被修理。叔叔吃了一驚：「誰跟妳說的？」「沒有人，我自己聽到鐘響。」

At last he took out a small receipt. It had the shape of a bell at the top and a date from twelve years ago. Under the date were three short words: rope, wood, and test. Mia could see a name written across the bottom. It was her father's. “So the bell was not broken?” she asked. “No. The old men fixed it, but they were not free to open the water gate. They could only do a test.” Uncle Lin sighed. “A bell does not save anyone by itself. People have to see the sign and choose what to do.” Mia put the receipt beside the map.

最後，叔叔拿出一張小收據。上方畫著鐘的形狀，日期是十二年前。日期下只寫了三個短詞：繩子、木頭、測試。米婭看見底下有個簽名，是父親的。「所以鐘當時沒有壞？」她問。「沒有，老工匠把它修好了，但他們沒有權限打開水閘，只能測試。」叔叔嘆口氣：「一口鐘不會自己救人。人必須看見警示，再選擇怎麼做。」米婭把收據放到地圖旁。

On the way to the boat, Uncle Lin stopped at a quiet place where a small stone seat faced the river. “Your grandfather liked to sit here,” he told the children. “He would talk about the shape of the land and make us look carefully before we spoke.” Jay sat down and looked over the water. “He sounds like Mia,” he said. Uncle Lin smiled. “Or perhaps Mia sounds like him.” Mia felt glad to hear a kind memory instead of another secret. She did not want to turn her grandfather into only a sad part of the past. She wanted to know the person he had been.

去搭船的路上，叔叔停在河邊一個安靜處，石椅面向河流。「你們爺爺喜歡坐這裡，」他說，「他會談土地的形狀，叫我們說話前先看清楚。」杰坐下望著水面。「他聽起來很像米婭。」叔叔笑了：「也可能是米婭像他。」米婭很高興聽到溫暖回憶，而不是另一個祕密。她不想讓爺爺只成為悲傷往事的一部分，也想知道他原本是怎樣的人。

On the return boat Mia looked at the moving water. The sun made little lights on each wave, and the town seemed peaceful. She thought of the wet line under the locked door. “Can you tell me why my father never spoke about the repair?” she asked. Her uncle gave her a sad smile. “You should ask him. But first ask why the bell was tested on a day with no rain. That question has a better answer.” Mia wrote it down. She had another sign now, and this one was on paper. As the boat turned toward the River House, she saw that the old bell had not moved all morning. That silence felt almost as strange as its ringing.

回程船上，米婭望著流動的河水。陽光在波浪上閃出小亮點，小鎮看起來一片安寧。她想起上鎖門下的濕痕。「你可以告訴我爸爸為什麼從不談修鐘的事嗎？」她問。叔叔難過地笑了：「妳該問他。但先問問為什麼要在沒下雨的日子測試鐘。那個問題比較容易找到答案。」米婭記下來。她現在又多了一個線索，而且寫在紙上。船轉向河屋時，她看見老鐘整個早上都沒動。這份寂靜幾乎和鐘聲一樣奇怪。
