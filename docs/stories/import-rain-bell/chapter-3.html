橋下的白線

The White Line Under the Bridge

雨來之前的鐘聲 · 第 3 / 25 章 · 1000以下 · 英中對照

用互動版查詞、朗讀與收藏

The next day, Mia began to follow the road from River House toward the old bridge. She had heard about a white line on the ground from a street worker, but she did not know where it started. Jay came in shoes that were too large for him. Ruby carried her camera, and Leo brought a map. They passed the station, then turned toward the beach. A bird rose from the river wall as they walked near it. Far away, a cow stood in a small field. “You were born here,” Jay told Mia. “How can there still be places you don't know?” “Because knowing a town is not the same as seeing every part,” Mia said.

隔天，米婭開始沿著河屋通往舊橋的路尋找線索。她從道路清潔員口中聽過地上有一條白線，卻不知道它從哪裡開始。杰穿著一雙太大的鞋子來了。露比帶著相機，李奧帶著地圖。他們經過車站，接著往海灘方向走。一隻鳥從河堤上飛起。很遠的地方，一頭牛站在小農地裡。「妳生在這裡耶，」杰對米婭說，「怎麼還有不知道的地方？」米婭回答：「認識一座小鎮，和看過它的每個角落，不是一回事。」

Near the bridge they found a line of clean stone between two areas of dark road. It ran along the side of the wall, turned a corner, and seemed to disappear under an old metal cover. Leo did not touch it. “We are not going into that opening,” he said before Jay could ask. Mia knelt a safe distance away. The clean stones formed a narrow path. Water had washed away dirt there while leaving it on both sides. “Something must pull water through this place,” Ruby said. “Or push it,” Leo answered. He marked the point on the map and drew an arrow into his book.

靠近橋時，他們在兩片深色路面間找到一線乾淨的石頭。它沿著牆邊延伸，轉過一角，彷彿消失在舊金屬蓋下。李奧沒有碰它，還在杰開口前先說：「我們不會進那個洞。」米婭在安全距離外蹲下。乾淨的石頭形成一條狹窄的路徑，像是水沖走了這裡的泥，卻留下兩旁的泥。「有東西一定把水拉過了這裡，」露比說。「或者把它推過來，」李奧回答。他在地圖上標了位置，又在筆記本裡畫了箭頭。

A road worker was eating lunch at a table beside the station. His name was Mr. Bao. He listened while Mia explained what had happened at the River House. “That mark comes back after some tests,” he said. “Someone cleans this road, and then the line appears again. I have seen it more than once.” “Who makes the tests?” Ruby asked. Mr. Bao looked toward the factory road and took a drink of water. “Ask the office. I only clean roads.” His hands shook a little as he put the cup down. Mia wanted to push him for an answer, but Leo moved his head. They thanked the man and stepped away.

道路清潔員包先生正在車站旁的桌子吃午餐。他聽米婭說明河屋發生的事，便說：「那記號每次測試後都會再出現。有人把路清乾淨，線又會回來。我不只看過一次。」「誰在測試？」露比問。包先生望向工廠方向，喝了一口水。「去問辦公室。我只是掃馬路的。」他放下杯子時，手有點發抖。米婭很想繼續追問，李奧卻輕輕搖頭。他們向包先生道謝，走到一旁。

They stood on the bridge and looked down. A small stream of water moved along the old wall although the main river was low. On Leo's map, the stream should have stopped near a closed gate years ago. “What will happen if heavy rain comes?” Mia asked. “I can't answer from this map,” Leo said. “It may not show what is under the street today.” Ruby took a picture from the bridge. Jay held her bag so it would not fall. The camera showed the clean line, the gate, and a dark square where the wall met the ground. It showed nothing about the water behind the wall.

他們站在橋上往下看。主要河道的水位雖低，一股細流卻沿著舊牆流動。在李奧的地圖上，這股水多年前就該在封閉閘門附近停止。「如果下大雨，會發生什麼事？」米婭問。「光靠這張圖，我答不出來，」李奧說，「它不一定反映今天街道下方的情況。」露比從橋上拍照，杰替她抓住背包，以免掉下去。照片拍到乾淨的白線、閘門，以及牆根的一個黑色方塊；卻完全拍不到牆後的水。

On the way back they passed the River House from the other side. The place where the white line pointed lay close to the floor under the warning bell. Mia understood why the first morning's dark mark mattered. The river, the road, and the old house might all be part of one path for water. “Should we tell Officer Su?” she asked. “Yes,” Leo said. “And show her the picture. Not a story about ghosts.” At home Mia made a new page. She used one color for things they had seen and another for questions. She did not write that the factory was at fault, because they still had no proof.

回程時，他們從另一側經過河屋。白線指向的位置，正好靠近警鐘下方的地板。米婭終於明白第一天早晨的深色痕跡為何重要：河、道路和舊屋可能都屬於同一條水路。「要告訴蘇小姐嗎？」她問。「當然，」李奧說，「給她看照片，不要講鬼故事。」回家後，米婭另開一頁，用一種顏色記錄親眼所見，另一種顏色寫問題。她沒有寫工廠有錯，因為還沒有證據。

That evening Mia heard a car stop outside. She looked through the window and saw her father's work truck on the other side of the old bridge. He got out with a light and walked to the locked gate. The night air was still. He did not seem to see Mia at the corner of the street. For a moment she thought of calling to him. Then she saw him bend down and look at the same white line they had found. He knew exactly where it was. Mia took one step forward, then stopped. Why had her father never told her about the path under the road?

那天傍晚，米婭聽到外頭有車停下。她從窗內看見父親的工作車停在舊橋另一端。父親拿著燈下車，走向上鎖的閘門。夜裡沒有風，他似乎沒看見街角的米婭。她一度想喊他，卻看見父親彎腰，查看他們剛找到的同一條白線。他顯然很清楚那條線在哪裡。米婭向前踏了一步，又停住。父親為什麼從未告訴過她路面下的那條水路？
