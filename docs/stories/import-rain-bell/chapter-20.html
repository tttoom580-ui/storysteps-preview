地板下的路

The Road Under the Floor

Grandma Chen asked Mia and Ruby to come to her kitchen in the morning. She had found the other part of her husband's old notebook. It had been hidden below a wooden board in a low cupboard, because she could not bear to read it after his death. “I should have shown this to Officer Su sooner,” she said. Ruby began to cry. “Grandma, you don't have to do this alone.” Her grandmother held her hand. “I know. That is why I called you.” On the page was a drawing of the machine under the River House floor. A wooden tail rose with the water level and pulled a rope attached to the bell.

早上，陳奶奶請米婭與露比到她的廚房。她找到了丈夫舊筆記本的另一部分。筆記藏在低矮櫥櫃裡的一塊木板下，因為丈夫過世後她一直不忍再讀。「我應該更早把它給蘇小姐看，」她說。露比哭了：「奶奶，妳不用一個人做這件事。」奶奶握住她的手：「我知道，所以才叫你們來。」頁面上畫著河屋地板下的機器。一根木尾隨水位升起，拉動連著警鐘的繩索。

Mia looked at the drawing for a long time. “So that was the knock we heard below the floor,” she said. Grandma nodded. “Part of the machine can hit the wood when water goes down again.” On the next page were numbers showing how high the water had been during different tests. One number was almost the same as the water mark Leo had seen under the bridge. Mia could now see the whole road from the low channel to the house. “It wasn't magic,” she said. “It was built to tell people that water was coming.” Grandma Chen touched the page. “And it was built by people who wanted everyone to live.”

米婭看著圖很久。「所以我們聽到地板下的敲擊就是它。」她說。奶奶點頭：「水退時，機器的一部分會敲到木板。」下一頁有不同測試的水位數字，其中一個幾乎和李奧在橋下看到的水痕一致。米婭終於能想像從低水道到房屋的完整路徑。「那不是魔法，」她說，「是人們做來告訴大家水要來了。」陳奶奶輕碰紙頁：「也是希望每個人都活下來的人造的。」

Officer Su brought trained town workers to look from a safe part of the yard. Mia's father came with them. Nobody asked the children to go inside or crawl below the floor. The workers used their own lights and tools to check the old machine, while the friends waited behind the line. After an hour the officer returned. “The drawing is right,” she said. “We can see how the wooden part works. The water has been high enough to move it during recent tests.” Mia showed her the copy of the receipt. The date, the machine, and the sound from Ruby's phone finally told one story.

蘇小姐帶受過訓練的鎮上人員，從外院的安全區域查看。米婭的父親也來了。沒有人要孩子進屋或鑽到地板下。工作人員用自己的燈與工具檢查舊機器，朋友們站在界線後等待。一小時後，蘇小姐回來說：「圖是對的。我們看得出木製零件如何運作。最近的測試已讓水位升到足以推動它。」米婭給她看收據副本。日期、機器和露比手機裡的聲音終於拼成同一個故事。

At home Mia had to finish her homework, but she could not stop thinking about the people who had used the bell before. Her grandfather had been one of them. The machine was hard and simple at the same time. It could not tell people where the water came from; it could only warn them that it had reached a certain level. “A doctor can't help a hundred people by looking at one sign,” her father said when she told him this thought. “The sign tells us where to begin.” Mia smiled. “So the bell was the first question.” “Yes,” he said. “And now we have to give it a good answer.”

回家後米婭得完成作業，卻不停想著以前使用警鐘的人，爺爺也是其中之一。機器既堅硬又簡單。它不能告訴人水從哪裡來，只能警告大家水已到某個高度。「醫生不能只看一個徵兆，就治好一百個病人，」父親聽完她的想法說，「徵兆只是開始。」米婭笑了：「所以鐘聲是第一個問題。」「對，」父親說，「現在我們得好好回答。」

Grandma Chen made eggs and bread with butter for the visitors. Her husband had liked that simple breakfast, she said. On the front of the old notebook he had written, “Don't hide anything that could help a neighbor.” Ruby read the words aloud. “Did he ever go abroad?” Jay asked. “Only once,” Grandma answered, “and he wrote home every day because he missed this river.” She looked out at the water. “My hope is that the town will remember him for helping, not only for the storm.” Mia put the last page carefully into the folder. She could not change the past, but she could help keep the next warning clear.

陳奶奶替訪客煎蛋，準備抹了奶油的麵包。她說丈夫以前喜歡這樣簡單的早餐。舊筆記本封面寫著：「任何可能幫助鄰居的事，都不要藏起來。」露比朗讀出來。杰問：「爺爺出過國嗎？」「只有一次，」奶奶說，「他天天寫信回家，因為想念這條河。」她望向河水：「我希望小鎮記得他幫人的事，不要只記得暴風雨。」米婭把最後一頁小心放進資料夾。她無法改變過去，卻能幫下一次警示清楚傳達。

Before evening Officer Su called another meeting. The town engineers had checked the old route, and their message was clear: if heavy rain came before the north gate was opened, water might rise too quickly below the bridge. The workers would need time to check the gate and move the right machines into place. “What can we do?” Mia asked. “You can help with the safety list,” Officer Su said. “You know the new high route from the school practice. Keep people informed, and stay out of danger.” Mia looked at the blue glove in her bag. Her father's old work had led her here, but her own work was different. She would help people know where to go.

傍晚前，蘇小姐又開了會。鎮上工程人員已檢查舊路線，訊息很明確：如果北閘門開啟前先下大雨，橋下水位可能升得太快。工人需要時間檢查閘門，把合適機具移到定位。「我們能做什麼？」米婭問。「幫忙整理安全名單，」蘇小姐回答，「妳們知道演練過的高地新路線。讓大家收到正確資訊，遠離危險。」米婭看向包裡的藍手套。父親過去的工作把她帶到這裡，但她自己的工作不同：她要幫大家知道往哪裡去。
