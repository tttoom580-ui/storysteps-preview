The Ghost in the Video

雨來之前的鐘聲 · 第 8 / 25 章 · 1000以下 · 英中對照 · 校訂完成

用互動版查詞、朗讀與收藏

Ruby posted a new video on Sunday night. In it a white shape seemed to fly behind a broken window at River House. She added the words GHOST AT THE RIVER and sent it to her school group. By morning everyone was talking about it. A girl in a red dress said her brother had seen the same ghost from the park. A boy from the baseball team said the house was dangerous. Ruby liked the sudden attention, but her heart began to beat fast when Officer Su called. “Why did you write that it was a ghost?” the officer asked. “Because people wanted to know why the bell rang,” Ruby answered. “That is not the same thing.”

星期日晚上，露比上傳了一段新影片。畫面裡，一個白影似乎飛過河屋破窗後方。她加上「河邊有鬼」的字樣，傳給學校群組。隔天早上，大家都在討論。一個穿紅裙的女孩說她哥哥從公園也看過鬼影。棒球隊男孩說那房子很危險。露比喜歡突如其來的關注，但蘇小姐打電話來時，她心跳加快。「妳為什麼說那是鬼？」蘇小姐問。「因為大家想知道鐘為什麼響。」露比回答。「那不是同一回事。」

The officer closed the yard to visitors until the building could be checked. She did not believe in ghosts, but she had seen children walking too close to the old wall. “A story can make people do things,” she told Ruby. “Please think about that.” Leo was angry when he found out. “We worked to find facts, and now everyone thinks we just make up stories.” Ruby wanted to defend herself. “I was trying to save the house,” she said. “Your nice little science film got ten views. Mine got ten thousand.” “Ten thousand people can be wrong,” Mia said. Ruby turned away from them before she began to cry.

蘇小姐暫時關閉外院，不讓訪客進入，直到建築檢查完畢。她不相信鬼，但她看過孩子走得太靠近老牆。「故事會影響人做事，」她對露比說，「請記住。」李奧知道後很生氣：「我們努力查事實，現在大家卻覺得我們只是在編故事。」露比想替自己辯護：「我想救那棟屋子啊。你們那段漂亮的科學影片才十次觀看，我這段有一萬次。」「一萬個人也可能錯，」米婭說。露比轉身走開，才沒當場哭出來。

After school Mia met Leo and Jay outside the park. They could see the River House from a safe walk behind the playground. A metal sheet beside the roof moved whenever wind passed. A bright street light shone through it. Jay stood in a different place and shouted, “Look!” The shadow behind the window became long and pale. When the metal moved, the shadow seemed to fly. “That's our ghost,” Leo said. “A moving shadow. Nothing more.” Mia made a short film from the same place, then moved the camera to show the light. The result was easy to understand. They would give it to Ruby first, not post it over her head.

放學後，米婭在公園外找到李奧和杰。他們站在遊戲場後方的安全步道上，可以看見河屋。屋頂旁一片鐵板在風來時移動，明亮的街燈穿過鐵板照射。杰換了位置大喊：「看！」窗後的影子變得細長而蒼白。鐵板一動，影子彷彿飛起來。「那就是我們的鬼，」李奧說，「只是移動的影子。」米婭從同一位置錄影，再移動相機拍到街燈。結果非常清楚。他們決定先把影片交給露比，而不是搶在她前面上傳。

Ruby watched the film with both hands around her phone. She was quiet for a long time. “I didn't lie about seeing a shape,” she said at last. “But I gave it the wrong name.” “You can change the words,” Mia said. Ruby shook her head. “People have already shared it. I can't make it disappear.” “Then tell them what you learned,” Leo answered. His voice was softer now. Ruby made a new video, showing the light, the sheet of metal, and the shadow. She said clearly that the ghost story had been wrong. Some people laughed at her. Others thanked her. Neither response felt good, but she left the correction online.

露比雙手握著手機，看完了影片。她沉默很久才說：「我沒有說謊，我真的看到一個影子。但我給它取了錯誤的名字。」「妳可以改文字，」米婭說。露比搖頭：「大家早就分享出去了，我不能讓它消失。」「那就告訴他們妳學到了什麼。」李奧的聲音柔和許多。露比做了新影片，拍出街燈、鐵片和影子。她清楚說明鬼故事是錯的。有些人笑她，有些人感謝她。兩種反應都不好受，但她還是讓更正影片留在線上。

At Grandma Chen's restaurant Ruby tried to eat her dinner but had no taste for the food. She wanted to break her phone, or at least hide it so she would not see more comments. Grandma sat across from her. “One wrong picture doesn't make you a bad person,” she said, “but what you do next matters.” Ruby looked at the soup. “People believed me. That's the worst part.” “Then give them something better to believe,” Grandma answered. She did not tell Ruby that everyone would forgive her. She simply sat beside Ruby while the girl reviewed the correction she had already posted and read the replies without trying to erase her mistake.

在陳奶奶的餐館裡，露比想吃晚飯，卻食不知味。她甚至想摔壞手機，或至少把它藏起來，免得繼續看留言。奶奶坐在對面。「一張錯的照片不會讓妳變成壞人，」她說，「但妳接下來怎麼做很重要。」露比盯著湯：「大家相信了我，這才最糟。」「那就給大家更值得相信的內容。」奶奶回答。她沒有保證所有人都會原諒露比，只是陪露比坐著，直到女孩願意重新看自己已上傳的更正影片，讀過那些回覆，而不急著抹掉曾經犯下的錯。

That evening the four friends stood outside the locked yard, eating bread with beef from Grandma Chen's shop. Their group was different now. Ruby did not talk as much, and Leo was trying not to sound like a teacher. “Why did the shadow matter so much?” Jay asked. “Because we were afraid of the wrong thing,” Mia said. “We have to look behind every picture.” Officer Su came by and told them they had done a good thing by correcting the film. Then she pointed to the old door. “No one has been allowed inside,” she said. As if to answer her, three quick knocks came from behind the locked door. Every person turned toward it. No one moved.

那天傍晚，四個朋友站在上鎖外院旁，吃陳奶奶店裡做的牛肉麵包。他們的團隊已經不太一樣。露比話少了，李奧則努力不再像老師說教。「那個影子為什麼那麼重要？」杰問。「因為我們害怕了錯的東西，」米婭說，「每張畫面都要看它背後的原因。」蘇小姐走過來，肯定他們更正影片的做法，接著指著舊門說：「裡面不准有人進去。」話才說完，鎖住的門後傳來急促的三下敲擊。所有人都轉頭望過去，卻沒有人移動。
