The Missing Half of the Picture

雨來之前的鐘聲 · 第 10 / 25 章 · 1000以下 · 英中對照 · 校訂完成

用互動版查詞、朗讀與收藏

On Monday a picture from twelve years ago appeared online. It showed Mr. Lin near the north gate at night. He was holding a key, and a large metal door was behind him. Someone had written, “This is the man who closed the gate.” Mia saw it between classes and felt a hard hit in her chest. For half a minute she could not move. She knew her father had worked at the gate, but she had never seen him like this. At lunch she showed the picture to Ruby. “How can I still love him if he hurt the town?” she whispered. Ruby put a hand on her arm. “We don't know if he did.”

星期一，一張十二年前的照片出現在網路上。照片裡，林先生夜裡站在北閘門旁，手裡拿著鑰匙，背後是一扇大鐵門。有人寫著：「就是這個人關了閘門。」米婭在兩堂課之間看到照片，胸口像被重擊。半分鐘內，她幾乎動不了。她知道父親當年在閘門工作，卻從沒看過這樣的他。午餐時，她把照片給露比看。「如果他害了小鎮，我還怎麼愛他？」她低聲說。露比握住她的手臂：「我們還不知道是不是他。」

Their teacher saw that Mia was upset and asked her to stay after class. Mia showed the picture. “Look at the right side,” the teacher said. “It is clear. But the left side is missing. Why?” “Maybe someone cut the picture to make him look bad,” Mia answered. “Or perhaps that was just the photo they had,” the teacher replied. “Either way, we cannot see what is outside it. A blind guess is still a guess.” Mia took out a pencil and drew an empty square beside the picture. It looked simple, but the empty part was almost as large as the part she could see.

老師看出米婭很難過，請她下課後留下。米婭拿出照片。「看看右半邊，」老師說，「很清楚。但左半邊不見了。為什麼？」「也許有人裁掉它，想害爸爸看起來很壞。」米婭說。「也可能拍照的人原本就只有這一部分，」老師回答，「不管如何，我們看不到畫面之外的事。盲目猜測還是猜測。」米婭拿出鉛筆，在照片旁畫了一個空白方框。那圖很簡單，但空白部分幾乎和看得見的一樣大。

At the basketball center Leo laid a small net across a table and used a ball to show how two paths could cross. “One worker can stand at a gate while another is opening a different one,” he said. Jay gave the ball a kick, and it rolled under a chair. “So the person with the key is not always the person who used it,” he said. Leo nodded. “Right. We need the full record.” Mia looked at her friends. They had been trying to help her while she had spent most of the day angry. “Thank you,” she said. “I thought I was ready for the truth. Maybe I'm only ready for the answer I want.”

在籃球中心，李奧把小球網攤在桌上，用球示範兩條路線如何交叉。「一個工人可能站在某道閘門旁，另一個人卻正在開另一道，」他說。杰踢了球一下，球滾到椅子下。「所以拿鑰匙的人不一定就是用鑰匙的人。」他說。李奧點頭：「對。我們需要完整紀錄。」米婭望向朋友。他們一直在幫她，而她大半天只顧著生氣。「謝謝，」她說，「我以為自己準備好接受真相，可能其實只準備好聽到想聽的答案。」

That night Mia sat with her father at the kitchen table. An apple lay between them. “I saw a picture of you,” she said. Her father put down his spoon. “Where did you find it?” “Online. You are standing at the north gate with a key.” He grew quiet. “That was one way we checked the gate. There were other people there.” “Then why have you spent years saying nothing?” Mia asked. His voice was low. “Because the whole night was worse than one photograph. I made a choice I still think about.” Mia tried to say something kind, but the words did not come. She left the apple untouched.

那天晚上，米婭和父親坐在餐桌旁，中間放著一顆蘋果。「我看到一張你的照片，」她說。父親放下湯匙：「妳在哪裡找到的？」「網路上。你拿著鑰匙站在北閘門。」父親沉默下來。「那是我們檢查閘門的一種方法。現場還有其他人。」「那你為什麼多年來什麼都不說？」米婭問。父親的聲音很低：「因為那一整夜遠比一張照片複雜。我做過一個選擇，到今天還在想。」米婭想說些溫柔的話，卻說不出來。她連蘋果都沒碰。

Mia's younger cousin Jay, who felt like a brother to her, found her staring at the photograph. “You look like someone ate your ice cream,” he said. “It's not funny,” she answered. He sat down beside her anyway. “I know your dad can be quiet. But he spent all night helping me when I was sick last month. That is also your dad.” Mia remembered how she had spent the whole day seeing him as only the man in the picture. Her brother did not know the history of the gate. His words did not prove her father innocent. They simply helped her remember that a person could be more than one moment.

像弟弟一樣親近的表弟杰，發現米婭一直盯著照片。「妳看起來像冰淇淋被人吃掉，」他說。「一點都不好笑。」她回答。弟弟仍坐到旁邊：「我知道妳爸爸不愛說話，可上個月我生病時，他整晚陪我。那也是妳爸爸。」米婭想起自己整天只把父親看成照片裡那個人。弟弟不了解閘門的過去，這番話也無法證明父親清白；卻提醒她，一個人不只是一個瞬間。

Before bed a message arrived from Ruby. Her grandmother had found an old school picture taken on the same night. Only the corner was clear, but it showed two pairs of boots beside the gate and the end of another person's coat. “There was someone next to your dad,” Ruby wrote. Mia looked at the two pictures together. The old picture had not solved anything. It had only shown how much she still could not see. She wrote one sentence in her notebook: I must not call a half picture a whole story. Then a second message arrived. It was from her father: “Tomorrow, I will take you to the town records.”

睡前，露比傳來訊息。她奶奶找到同一晚拍的一張老學校照片。只有角落清楚，但能看見閘門旁有兩雙靴子，還有另一個人的外套下擺。「妳爸爸旁邊還有人。」露比寫道。米婭把兩張照片並排看。舊照片沒有解開問題，只顯示她還有多少看不見。她在筆記本寫下一句：不能把半張照片當成整個故事。接著父親傳來第二則訊息：「明天，我帶妳去查公所紀錄。」
