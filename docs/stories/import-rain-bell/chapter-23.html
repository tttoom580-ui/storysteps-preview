The Last Page in the Office

雨來之前的鐘聲 · 第 23 / 25 章 · 1000以下 · 英中對照 · 校訂完成

用互動版查詞、朗讀與收藏

By morning the rain had become a steady shower. Officer Su asked Mia and her father to come to the high school office, which had become the town's safe work room. Mr. Han was there with a copy of the factory plan. His wife had brought a pot of hot soup for the workers. Everyone spoke politely, though no one felt fine. Mia had never seen so much paper in one space. “Hello, Mia,” Mr. Han said. “I understand that you have the old test records.” “Yes,” she answered. “And we need to know which rule the factory used when it kept the gate closed.”

早上，雨仍不停地下。蘇小姐請米婭和父親到高中的辦公室，現在那裡成了鎮上的安全指揮室。韓先生帶著工廠計畫副本也在場。他的妻子為工作人員送來一鍋熱湯。大家說話都很客氣，卻沒有人真正輕鬆。米婭從沒見過這麼多紙擠在同一個空間。「你好，米婭，」韓先生說，「我知道妳有舊測試紀錄。」「有，」她回答，「我們還需要知道工廠一直關閘時依據哪一條規定。」

Ms. Kao had sent the final page from the city records room by official message. It belonged to the same document Mia had seen with her father. The bottom part named the two conditions: the gate could be closed only for a short dry-weather test, and it had to be opened again before heavy rain. Mr. Han read the page slowly. “I was told the approval never ended,” he said. “The new plan was made from that idea.” Officer Su looked at him. “The papers do not support it. We must stop the sale process and protect the water route first.” Mr. Han did not argue.

高小姐已透過正式訊息，從市區檔案室傳來文件最後一頁。它和米婭、父親先前看到的是同一份文件。頁尾寫了兩個條件：只能在乾天短時間關閘測試，而且必須在大雨前重新開啟。韓先生慢慢讀著。「有人告訴我核准永遠有效，」他說，「新計畫就是照那個說法做的。」蘇小姐看著他：「文件並不支持這點。我們必須先停止出售程序，保護水路。」韓先生沒有爭辯。

Mia's father placed his old blue glove on the table. “My name is on the test check,” he said. “I never made sure the gate opened again. I belong to the story too.” Mia felt a pain in her throat. She wanted to kiss him on the cheek, but there were too many people watching. “Dad,” she said, “we can talk about that when people are safe.” He nodded. Officer Su began to give instructions to the trained teams. Mr. Han offered the factory's high-road machines and workers. “I can get them here,” he said. “They know how to move heavy parts.”

米婭的父親把舊藍手套放在桌上。「我的名字在測試檢查單上，」他說，「但我沒有再確認閘門重新開啟。我也是這個故事的一部分。」米婭喉嚨一緊。她想親親父親的臉頰，但現場太多人。「爸，」她說，「等大家安全了，再談這件事。」父親點頭。蘇小姐開始向專業隊伍下達指示。韓先生提供工廠能走高路的機具和工人：「我能把他們帶來。他們知道怎麼移動重型零件。」

A town worker came in with another problem. The low road to the north gate had become too wet for a truck. “That's crazy,” Jay said quietly from the door. “We have the right paper at last, and we still can't get there.” Officer Su heard him. “Paper tells us what should be done. People still need a safe way to do it.” She asked the teams to check the high road used during the school practice. Leo knew that route and brought the map. He did not go with the workers. He showed them the safe turn and the place where the road went down.

一名公所人員進來，帶來另一個問題：通往北閘門的低路太濕，卡車已經過不去。「太瘋狂了，」杰在門邊小聲說，「終於找到正確文件，卻還到不了那裡。」蘇小姐聽見了：「文件告訴我們該做什麼，人們仍要找到安全做法。」她請隊伍檢查學校演練走過的高處道路。李奧熟悉那條路，帶來地圖。他沒有跟著工人走，只指出安全轉彎處和道路開始下坡的位置。

A volunteer brought warm apple pie into the office. Nobody felt very hungry, but Mr. Han asked the worker to leave it for the night team. “They may need food when they return,” he said. Mia noticed how polite he was to the volunteer, even while everyone was afraid. It did not change the record or take away his mistake. It did remind her that people are not made of one act alone. She took a small piece of pie to her father. He thanked her and gave half of it to a tired worker beside him. Then they all turned back to the map. The high road was their only safe option.

志工把溫熱蘋果派送進辦公室。沒有人很餓，但韓先生請對方留下，給夜班隊伍回來吃。「他們回來可能需要食物。」他說。米婭注意到，即使大家都害怕，他對志工仍很有禮貌。這不能改變紀錄，也無法抹去錯誤，卻提醒她一個人不只由單一行為組成。她拿一小塊派給父親，父親道謝，又分一半給旁邊疲憊的工人。然後大家重新看向地圖。高處道路是唯一安全的選擇。

Before the teams left, Mr. Han turned to Mia. “I never meant to harm the town,” he said. “But that isn't enough. I should have asked what the old rule meant.” Mia thought about her own mistake with the half picture. “I know how easy it is to see only what you want,” she replied. “Now please help them keep everyone safe.” “I will,” he said. There would be no sale that day, no celebration, and no easy ending. Outside, rain touched the dark windows. Officer Su spoke into the radio: “Use the high road. Check each turn. No one goes forward until the team says it's safe.”

隊伍離開前，韓先生對米婭說：「我從沒有想傷害小鎮，但光這樣不夠。我早該問清楚舊規定的意思。」米婭想起自己看半張照片時的錯誤：「我知道只看想看的東西有多容易，現在請幫忙保護大家。」「我會。」韓先生說。今天不會出售、不會慶祝，也不會有簡單結局。窗外的雨敲著黑色玻璃。蘇小姐對著無線電說：「走高路。每個轉彎都要檢查。隊伍說安全之前，任何人不得繼續前進。」
