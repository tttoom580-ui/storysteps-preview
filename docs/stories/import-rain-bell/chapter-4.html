便宜的承諾

The Cheap Promise

雨來之前的鐘聲 · 第 4 / 25 章 · 1000以下 · 英中對照

On Friday the town held a meeting in the square. A tall board stood beside a small stage, with a drawing of the new factory and a new play area. Mr. Han, the factory manager, said the company could give the town more jobs and a health service room. “The old house is beautiful,” he said, “but the new plan will help living people.” A worker near Mia clapped. A boy beside him called out, “My father needs that job!” Mia looked at Leo. His face said more than words. She had come ready to fight the sale, but now she knew the matter was not so simple.

星期五，鎮上在廣場舉行說明會。小舞臺旁立著一塊高大的展示板，上面畫著新工廠和新的遊戲區。工廠經理韓先生說，公司能帶來更多工作和一間健康服務室。「舊屋很美，」他說，「但新計畫能幫助還生活在這裡的人。」米婭附近一名工人鼓掌，旁邊的男孩喊：「我爸爸需要那份工作！」米婭望向李奧，他的表情勝過千言萬語。她原本準備反對出售，此刻才知道事情並不簡單。

Ruby had brought tomato sandwiches to sell for the school group. A butterfly rested on one of the paper bags. For a moment the square felt like a normal happy place. Then a dark cloud covered the sun, and a strong wind began to blow. The corner of the large board came loose and shook. One part of the drawing dropped to the ground. Under it Mia saw an older picture of a water path. The path ran straight under the River House. “Did you see that?” she asked Leo. “Yes,” he said. “That old line is not in the new picture.” A town worker came to put the board back.

露比帶了番茄三明治，替學校社團義賣。一隻蝴蝶停在紙袋上。廣場一時像個普通而快樂的地方。接著烏雲遮住陽光，強風開始吹。一大塊展示板的角落鬆開，不停晃動，其中一張圖紙掉到地上。米婭看見底下有張較舊的水路圖，水路筆直穿過河屋下方。「你看見了嗎？」她問李奧。「看見了，」他說，「新圖上沒有那條舊線。」一名鎮公所人員趕來扶正展示板。

“Sir, can we look at the older picture?” Mia asked. The worker shook his head. “The office has a copy. You can ask there.” Ruby took a photo before he put the new sheet over it. The wind carried a little dust across the square. Grandma Chen held her hat as if she feared it might fly off. “That line was there before this factory was built,” she said. “It led water away when the river was high.” Mr. Han heard her. “Our experts say the new plan is safe,” he answered. “We do not want anyone to be hurt.” Grandma Chen looked at him for a long moment.

「先生，我們可以看看舊圖嗎？」米婭問。工作人員搖頭：「公所裡有副本，你們可以去問。」露比在他把新紙蓋回去之前拍下照片。風吹著細塵越過廣場。陳奶奶按住帽子，怕它飛走。「那條線在工廠蓋起來前就有了，」她說，「河水高時，它能把水帶走。」韓先生聽見了：「專家說新計畫很安全。我們不希望任何人受傷。」陳奶奶看了他很久。

Mia wanted to tell the whole town about the wet floor and the white line, but she remembered the short film with its missing seconds. She stepped onto the low stage only when Officer Su invited questions. “I have a story,” Mia began, then corrected herself. “I have three things I saw. The old bell rang without rain. The ground below its door was wet. We found a clean line under the bridge.” People stopped talking. “Does the new plan cover that old water path?” she asked. Mr. Han answered that the company would check. A woman in a factory uniform said, “Please check before you make us choose between a home and a job.”

米婭想把濕地板和白線的事告訴全鎮，但她記得那段缺少十七秒的影片。直到蘇小姐開放提問，她才走上矮舞臺。「我有一個故事，」米婭開口，隨即改正，「不，我有三件親眼看到的事。老鐘在無雨時響起，門下的地面是濕的，而我們在橋下找到一條乾淨的線。」人們不再交談。「新計畫會不會蓋住那條舊水路？」她問。韓先生回答，公司會查。一名穿工廠制服的婦女說：「請先查清楚，不要逼我們在家園和工作之間選。」

Before the meeting ended, a teacher asked Mr. Han a question about the new health room. “Will it be open to every family, including people who don't work at the factory?” she asked. “Yes,” he said. “Healthy children make a stronger town.” One of the pictures began to shake in the wind again. A small piece of paper dropped from its corner, and Jay bent to pick it up. It showed the old river wall, with a blue line running behind the proposed square. Mia passed the paper to Officer Su instead of keeping it. “This needs to go with the record,” she said. The officer thanked her and placed it in a folder.

會議結束前，一位老師問韓先生關於新健康服務室的事。「所有家庭都能使用嗎？包括沒有在工廠上班的人？」她問。「可以，」韓先生說，「健康的孩子能讓小鎮更好。」一張圖又在風中搖晃，小紙片從角落掉下來，杰彎腰撿起。上面畫著舊河牆，藍線從預定廣場後方穿過。米婭沒有自己留著，而是把紙交給蘇小姐。「這應該放進正式紀錄。」她說。蘇小姐道謝，把紙收進資料夾。

After the meeting Leo walked beside Mia without speaking. She felt thankful that he had not turned away. “I thought you were against my mother,” he said at last. “I'm not,” Mia replied. “I want her to keep working, and I want the town to stay safe.” Leo gave a small smile. “Then we need more than a photo.” They watched the workers put the board inside. The cheap promise of a new square looked good on paper, but no one had explained where the water would go. At the office door, a notice said that only written records could change the sale decision. They had twenty days, and the paper they needed was still missing.

會後，李奧默默走在米婭身旁。她很感激他沒有離開。「我以為妳在反對我媽媽，」他終於說。米婭回答：「不是。我希望她能繼續工作，也希望鎮上安全。」李奧微微一笑：「那我們需要的不只是一張照片。」他們看著工作人員把展示板搬進屋內。新廣場的便宜承諾在紙上很漂亮，卻沒人解釋水將流向哪裡。公所門口的公告說，只有正式書面紀錄能改變出售決定。他們只剩二十天，需要的那張紙卻還找不到。
