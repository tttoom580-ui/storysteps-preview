草地上的演練

The Practice on the Grass

雨來之前的鐘聲 · 第 18 / 25 章 · 1000以下 · 英中對照 · 校訂完成

Officer Su asked the school to practice the safety plan that day. “Yesterday we had a paper map,” she told the students. “Today we need to know if it works for a real child, an older person, and a family with bags.” The team gathered on the grass near the sports field. A giant ball from a dance program stood in the middle of the path. “That must move,” Mia said. “Someone could fall while trying to go around it.” Jay helped put the ball in a safe place. Mr. Chen, the school leader, opened a notebook and began to set a time for each group.

蘇小姐請學校當天就練習避難計畫。「昨天我們只有紙上地圖，」她對學生說，「今天要知道真正的孩子、老人、提行李的家庭能不能走。」隊伍在運動場旁的草地集合。舞蹈節目用的巨大充氣球擋在步道中間。「它得移走，」米婭說，「有人繞過去時可能跌倒。」杰幫忙把球搬到安全處。學校負責人陳先生打開筆記本，開始記錄各隊時間。

At the first turn a little girl jumped over a low step and slipped on the wet grass. Her father caught her before she hit the ground. “Don't jump,” he said, trying to sound calm. “We need to walk together.” Mia hurried toward them, but Officer Su touched her shoulder. “Wait. The teacher is already there.” Mia stopped. She had wanted to be useful, yet she had almost run into the path and made it harder for people to pass. She went back to her group. “What should I do?” she asked. “Count the people who arrive, and tell us if anyone is missing,” Officer Su said.

第一個轉彎處，小女孩跳過矮階，滑倒在濕草上。她父親在她撞到地面前接住她。「不要跳，」他努力冷靜地說，「要一起走。」米婭急忙跑過去，蘇小姐卻碰了碰她肩膀：「等一下，老師已經在那裡。」米婭停住。她本來想幫忙，差點衝進通道，讓其他人更難通行。她回到自己的小組：「我該做什麼？」「清點抵達的人，告訴我們是否少人。」蘇小姐說。

Jay carried a towel and a bottle of water. “I can help the next group,” he offered. Leo studied the route and pointed to a road near the north gate. “That path is low. We need to test another one.” Officer Su agreed. The school leader changed the plan so everyone would end at the high school yard, not at the River House. “People know that house,” he said, “but a familiar place isn't always the right place today.” Mia wrote the new meeting point in large letters. She felt proud not because she had led the group, but because the group had learned something useful.

杰帶著毛巾和水瓶：「我可以幫下一組。」李奧研究路線，指向北閘門旁的道路：「那裡很低，我們得測另一條。」蘇小姐同意。學校負責人更改計畫，讓大家最後到高處的校園，而不是河屋。「大家熟悉河屋，」他說，「但熟悉的地方今天不一定安全。」米婭把新集合點寫得很大。她感到自豪，不是因為自己領隊，而是整個團隊確實學到有用的事。

Near the end of the practice, Leo noticed water touching the edge of the ground beside the north gate. It was only a thin wet place, almost too small to see. “This wasn't here this morning,” he said. Officer Su came to look from a safe distance. She took a photograph and asked a town worker to check the area. “No one goes close,” she told the children. “The old ground may not be strong.” Mia remembered the first wet mark beneath the River House. This one did not need a bell to frighten her. It showed that the water path was already changing.

練習快結束時，李奧注意到北閘門旁的地面邊緣有水。那只是一小片濕痕，幾乎看不見。「今天早上還沒有，」他說。蘇小姐站在安全距離外查看，拍了照，請鎮上的維修人員檢查。「孩子們誰都不准靠近，」她說，「舊地面可能不穩。」米婭想起河屋門下的第一道濕痕。這一次不用鐘響，她也感到害怕。水路已經在改變了。

Mr. Chen asked the students what they had learned before he sent them home. A boy rubbed his nose and said he was certain he could run faster than the group. “Maybe you can,” the leader answered, “but a safe path isn't a race. If your friend has a hurt leg, will you leave him behind?” The boy shook his head. “No, Mr. Chen.” “Then we choose a path people can use together.” Mia remembered how she had almost run toward the fallen girl. She wrote the lesson at the bottom of the practice sheet: the best route is the one the whole group can take safely.

放大家回家前，陳先生問學生學到了什麼。一名男孩揉著鼻子，說他確定自己跑得比全隊快。「也許可以，」負責人回答，「但安全道路不是比賽。如果朋友的腿受傷，你會丟下他嗎？」男孩搖頭：「不會，陳先生。」「那我們要選大家能一起走的路。」米婭想起自己差點衝向跌倒女孩。她在演練單底下記下：最好的路線，是整個團隊都能安全走的路。

That night Mia's father asked what she had learned. She told him about the fall, the giant ball, the new path, and the wet place by the gate. “So the practice was not perfect,” he said. “No,” Mia answered. “That's why we needed it.” He smiled. “Your grandfather would have liked that.” She looked at the blue glove on her desk and thought of the promise he had made. Today they had not saved anyone from a real flood. But when rain came, they would know where to go. Before bed her phone rang. Officer Su said the ground near the north gate was wetter than expected.

那晚，父親問米婭學到什麼。她說起跌倒、巨大充氣球、新路線以及閘門旁的濕地。「所以練習不完美，」父親說。「對，」米婭回答，「這就是為什麼需要演練。」父親笑了：「妳爺爺一定會喜歡。」她看向桌上的藍手套，想起父親的承諾。今天他們沒有真的從洪水中救出任何人。但下雨時，大家會知道往哪走。睡前手機響了，蘇小姐說北閘門附近地面比預期更濕。
