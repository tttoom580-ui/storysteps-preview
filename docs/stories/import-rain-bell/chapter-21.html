The Night the Lights Went Out

雨來之前的鐘聲 · 第 21 / 25 章 · 1000以下 · 英中對照 · 校訂完成

The last show at River House was meant to be a warm goodbye. Children had brought a ball, a toy airplane, and pictures for a small school program. Jay played piano near the open door of the room next to the house. He had been busy all day, but now he looked bored. “Only one more song,” he told Ruby. “Then we can get cheese bread from the kitchen.” A little boy laughed. He had enjoyed the whole experience, even though the old place smelled of wet wood. Mia stood by the door, watching the people who had come to see the house one more time before the sale meeting.

河屋最後一場活動原本是溫暖的告別。孩子們帶著球、玩具飛機和學校節目的圖片。杰在河屋旁活動室敞開的門口彈鋼琴。他忙了一整天，現在有點無聊。「只剩最後一首歌，」他對露比說，「然後就能去廚房拿起司麵包。」一個小男孩笑了。他很喜歡這場活動，雖然老屋有股濕木頭的味道。米婭站在門邊，看著居民在出售公聽會前再來看這棟屋子一次。

Just as the song ended, the light went out. The room fell dark and someone shouted from the bathroom. A strange hot smell reached Mia's nose. “Everyone stay calm,” an adult called. The workers cut the power to the room and checked the outside box. Officer Su, who had come for the event, told the children to use the practiced high path to the school. “No one goes back for a bag,” she said. Ruby held up her phone light, and Jay led the younger children toward the door. Mia took the attendance list and counted each person as they passed. The practice on the grass was no longer a game.

最後一首歌剛結束，燈就熄了。房間陷入黑暗，有人從浴室方向喊叫。一股奇怪的焦味飄進米婭鼻中。「大家保持冷靜，」一名大人喊。工作人員切斷活動室電源，檢查外側電箱。參加活動的蘇小姐請孩子們沿演練過的高處路線往學校移動。「誰也不准回去拿包包。」她說。露比打開手機燈，杰帶著年紀小的孩子往門口走。米婭拿著出席名單，逐一數過經過的人。草地上的練習不再只是遊戲。

Outside, rain had begun. It came down harder than the weather report had said. Water moved over the lowest step at the edge of the road. Leo pointed toward the River House bell. The rope inside its high frame was moving. One slow ring sounded above the wind. Then another. “It's the water again,” Mia said. Her mouth felt dry. She wanted to run and tell her father, but Officer Su was already speaking on the radio. The adults were checking the power, the river, and the gate. The children's job was to keep walking to high ground. Mia held the list tightly and moved with the others.

外頭開始下雨，而且比天氣預報說的更大。水流過道路邊緣最低的一級臺階。李奧指著河屋警鐘。高鐘架裡的繩子正在移動。一聲慢鐘越過風聲響起，然後又一聲。「又是水，」米婭說，嘴裡發乾。她想跑去告訴父親，但蘇小姐已經在用無線電聯絡。大人們在檢查電力、河流和閘門。孩子們的工作是繼續往高處走。米婭緊抓名單，跟著大家前進。

At the school a teacher gave the children hot water and a little food. Some were frightened; some still thought the power failure had been part of the show. Jay sat on the floor beside a little girl and showed her how to fold a paper plane. “When the rain stops, it can fly,” he said. Ruby did not film the crying children. She helped the teacher call their families instead. Leo checked the route map with Officer Su, who had returned after the last group reached the yard. “We need the other street,” she said. “The first white line near the river is already under water.”

在學校，老師給孩子們熱水和少量食物。有些孩子害怕，有些還以為停電是表演的一部分。杰坐在小女孩旁邊的地板上，教她摺紙飛機。「雨停後，它就能飛。」他說。露比沒有拍哭泣的孩子，而是幫老師打電話給家人。李奧和蘇小姐再次核對路線圖，蘇小姐在最後一組抵達校園後也回來了。「要改走另一條街，」她說，「河邊第一道白線已被水淹過。」

In the safe school room a teacher showed the younger children three types of emergency signs. One meant stay inside, one meant follow a safe road, and one meant ask a worker for help. “Which type do we use tonight?” Jay asked. “The one on the door,” the teacher answered, pointing to the high-route sign. A small girl held Mia's hand. “Will we save the old house?” she asked. Mia thought before speaking. “I don't know yet. Tonight we keep people safe. The house can wait.” The girl nodded and went to sit with the others. Mia was glad she had given an answer she could keep.

在安全的學校教室，老師向年幼孩子介紹三種緊急標誌：一種表示留在室內，一種表示沿安全道路前進，另一種表示向工作人員求助。「今晚用哪一種？」杰問。「門上的那種。」老師指著高處路線標誌回答。一名小女孩握住米婭的手：「我們會救下老屋嗎？」米婭想了一下才說：「我還不知道。今晚先讓大家安全，房子可以等。」女孩點頭，回到其他人身邊。米婭很高興自己給的是能守住的回答。

Mia's father arrived with a wet coat and the old blue gloves on his hands. “Are you all safe?” he asked. Mia showed him the checked list. “Everyone from our group is here.” He nodded, proud and worried at the same time. “Good. Stay here. The town has asked trained workers to check the north gate.” Mia looked through the window. Across the dark river she could see a small light moving near the high road. She remembered the knock under the floor, the missing pages, the half photograph. All of them had led to this wet night. Then the school radio spoke again: the water had crossed the first white mark.

父親穿著濕外套、戴著舊藍手套趕來。「你們都安全嗎？」他問。米婭把核對過的名單給他看：「我們這組的人都到了。」他點頭，神情既驕傲又擔心。「很好，待在這裡。鎮上已請受過訓練的人員檢查北閘門。」米婭望向窗外。黑色河面另一端，高處道路旁有一道小燈光在移動。她想起地板下的敲擊、缺頁文件和半張照片。所有線索都把大家帶到這個雨夜。接著學校廣播又響了：水已漫過第一道白色記號。
