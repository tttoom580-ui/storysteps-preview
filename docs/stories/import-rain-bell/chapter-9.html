The Card by the Lake

雨來之前的鐘聲 · 第 9 / 25 章 · 1000以下 · 英中對照 · 校訂完成

Grandma Chen took the friends to the lake on the next holiday. A little festival was held there every year, and she kept a box of cards from its early days. “You want to know the past,” she told Mia. “Then visit the people who remember it.” At a food stand, an old woman was serving rice and banana cakes. She knew Grandma Chen and gave the children a warm welcome. “Are you ready for a long story?” she asked. Jay was quite ready, especially when the woman put a sweet dish in front of him. Ruby whispered, “He would visit a history class every day if there were cake.”

下一個假日，陳奶奶帶朋友們去湖邊。那裡每年都有一場小節慶，她收藏了早年活動的賀卡。「你們想知道過去，」她對米婭說，「就去拜訪記得過去的人。」一個食物攤位上，老婦人正在盛飯和香蕉蛋糕。她認識陳奶奶，熱情迎接孩子們。「準備好聽一個長故事了嗎？」她問。杰非常樂意，尤其老婦人還放了一道甜點在他面前。露比小聲說：「如果歷史課有蛋糕，他會天天去上。」

The woman brought out a large blue card with a picture of the River House. On its back was a path drawn in pencil. It began at the lake, turned toward an old street, and ended beside the north gate. “We used this card to tell people where to go during the river festival,” she said. “Grandma Chen's husband helped draw it.” Grandma Chen held the card and looked at it as if it might speak. Her husband had died many years ago. He had loved the River House, and he had wanted every family to know a safe road to high ground. Mia did not ask about his death at once.

老婦人拿出一張大藍卡，正面畫著河屋，背面用鉛筆畫了一條小徑。小徑從湖邊出發，轉向老街，最後到達北閘門旁。「以前河祭時，我們用這張卡告訴大家怎麼走，」她說，「陳奶奶的丈夫也幫忙畫過。」陳奶奶拿著卡片，像在等它開口說話。她丈夫多年前過世。他愛河屋，希望每個家庭都知道通往高地的安全道路。米婭沒有立刻追問他的死因。

Later they walked beside the water. Grandma Chen told them that the last great storm had changed many lives. “Some people were sure the rain would stop,” she said. “Some stayed by the river to help others. Some left too late. We did not all make the right choice.” Mia could hear joy from the festival behind them, and the difference hurt. “Did Grandpa know the bell worked?” Jay asked. “Yes,” Grandma said. “He helped test it. He told me the town must never forget why we kept it.” Ruby reached for her grandmother's hand. Mia understood that some memories were hard to give away.

後來他們沿湖散步。陳奶奶說，上次大風雨改變了很多人的生命。「有些人確定雨很快就會停，」她說，「有人留在河邊幫助別人，有人太晚離開。我們不是每次都做了正確的選擇。」身後傳來節慶的歡樂聲，這樣的對比讓米婭難受。「爺爺知道鐘會響嗎？」杰問。「知道，」奶奶說，「他幫忙測試過，告訴我小鎮絕不能忘記為什麼保留它。」露比握住奶奶的手。米婭明白有些記憶很難說出口。

When they went back to the stand, the old woman handed Mia another card. “I almost forgot this one. It came back to me yesterday.” Mia turned it over and saw three names. One was her father's. “O.K., now I'm really worried,” she said. The woman gave her a kind look. “Don't be. A name on a card doesn't tell the whole story.” “Are you sure he was there?” Mia asked. “Yes, but I can't tell you what he did. I wasn't there when the gate was closed.” Mia put the card back on the table as if it had grown too hot to hold.

回到攤位時，老婦人又拿給米婭一張卡。「我差點忘了這張，昨天才找到。」米婭翻過來，看見三個名字，其中一個是父親的。「好吧，我真的有點擔心了。」她說。老婦人溫和地看著她：「先別擔心。卡上的名字不代表整個故事。」「妳確定他在場嗎？」米婭問。「確定，但我不知道他做了什麼。關閘時我不在那裡。」米婭把卡放回桌上，像它突然燙得拿不住。

Before leaving the lake, Mia asked her grandmother whether keeping the old cards had been hard. Grandma Chen smiled. “These cards held a part of our life,” she said. “Some days I could not even open the box.” Jay tried to lift everyone's spirits by making a loud shout from the far end of the path. “Look at me!” he called. “I'm the star of the lake!” Ruby rolled her eyes. Mia laughed and told him to lower his voice. The grandmother pointed to the tip of a narrow path near the shore. “That is where the old road began,” she said. “Okay, now we can go home.”

離開湖邊前，米婭問陳奶奶，收藏這些舊卡片是否很難受。奶奶微笑：「它們保存了我們生活的一部分。有些日子，我甚至打不開盒子。」杰想讓大家打起精神，跑到小徑遠端大喊：「看我！我是湖之王！」露比翻白眼。米婭笑著要他小聲點。陳奶奶指向岸邊窄路的盡頭：「以前的道路從那裡開始。」她說。「好了，現在可以回家了。」

At home Mia wrote the name and the date in her book. She had not told her father about this second card. She felt that she was moving back through a family story whose end she could not see. Ruby sent her a message: Grandma says the card is important, but please don't be angry with your dad yet. Mia read it twice. She wanted to treat her father fairly; she also wanted him to stop hiding things. The card lay beside the receipt on her desk. Under her father's name there was a small blue mark, the same shape as the one on the old box. Mia turned on her lamp and looked again.

回家後，米婭把名字和日期寫進筆記本。她還沒告訴父親第二張卡的事。她覺得自己正一步步走進一個看不見結尾的家族故事。露比傳訊息來：奶奶說那張卡很重要，但請先不要生爸爸的氣。米婭讀了兩遍。她想公平對待父親，也希望他別再隱瞞。卡片和收據並排放在書桌上。父親名字下方有個小藍記號，和舊盒上的圖形一模一樣。米婭打開檯燈，再仔細看了一次。
