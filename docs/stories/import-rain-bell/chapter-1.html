凌晨六點十分

The Bell at Six Ten

雨來之前的鐘聲 · 第 1 / 25 章 · 1000以下 · 英中對照

At 6:10 a.m., the old warning bell rang above the locked River House. Mia Lin stopped so quickly that a box of breakfast food almost fell from her arms. There had been no rain for days. Why did the bell sound as if the river were rising? The street was empty. Beside Mia, Ruby Chen held a cake in one hand and a bag of fruit in the other. “Did you hear that?” Ruby asked. “Everyone heard that,” Mia said. But no one came to the windows. The sound came again: three slow beats. Then it stopped. The early sun lit the roof, and the whole house became quiet.

上午六點十分，已上鎖的河屋上方響起老舊的警鐘。米婭·林猛然停步，手裡的一盒早餐差點掉下來。好幾天都沒有下雨。為什麼警鐘卻像河水正在上漲般響著？街上空無一人。身旁的露比·陳一手拿著蛋糕，另一手提著水果。「妳聽見了嗎？」露比問。「大家都聽見了吧。」米婭說。可是，沒有一個人來到窗邊。鐘聲再次響起：緩慢的三下。接著停住。清晨的陽光照亮屋頂，整間屋子恢復安靜。

The girls had come to help Grandma Chen prepare food for a school event. She was waiting inside the small room next to the River House, where an old couch sat under a white cover. Bowls of salad filled the table. The school would welcome foreign visitors on Friday, and the students planned to show them how people in Riverbend cared for the river. “A strange way to begin a good day,” Grandma Chen said when she opened the door. Her smile did not last. She looked toward the bell, then at the dry street. “That bell should not ring today.” Mia set the food down. “Who can make it ring?” “I don't know,” Grandma said too fast.

兩個女孩是來幫陳奶奶準備學校活動餐點的。奶奶在河屋旁的小房間等候，房內有一張蓋著白布的舊沙發。桌上放滿一碗碗沙拉。學校星期五將迎接外國訪客，學生準備介紹河灣鎮居民如何照顧河流。「這可不是好日子的正常開頭。」陳奶奶開門時說。她的笑容很快消失，先看向鐘，再看看乾燥的街道。「那口鐘今天不該響。」米婭放下食物。「誰能讓它響？」奶奶回答得太快：「我不知道。」

Mia had a hobby: she liked to write down things that other people passed without seeing. Her father used to teach her to keep three lists in a little book: what she saw, what she heard, and what she only guessed. Today she opened the book to a clean page. Ruby pointed at a heavy wooden door. A small dark mark ran across the bottom. At first Mia thought it was dirt. Then she touched the ground nearby. It was wet. “The floor is dry on this side,” she said. “Why is the ground wet under the door?” Grandma Chen took a long breath. “Please do not try to open it.”

米婭有個嗜好：她喜歡記下別人走過時沒注意到的小事。父親以前教她在小筆記本裡分成三欄：看見的、聽見的，以及純粹猜測的。今天她翻到空白的一頁。露比指著一扇沉重的木門，門底橫著一條深色痕跡。米婭起初以為是泥土，摸了旁邊的地面才發現是濕的。「這一側的地板是乾的，」她說，「為什麼門底下會濕？」陳奶奶深吸一口氣。「拜託妳們不要想打開它。」

A boy ran up the street, carrying a school bag as large as himself. It was Jay, Mia's younger cousin, with a paper monkey from the school show. One white tooth had fallen out of the monkey's open mouth. “I was doing my morning exercise,” he said proudly. Ruby laughed. “Running here with a paper monkey is exercise now?” Jay made the monkey bow. But Mia was looking over his shoulder at the old bell frame. On the wood beneath the bell was a little white shape, like the missing tooth. “Jay, who made that mark?” she asked. “No idea. Mine is only paper.” Mia drew its shape in her book.

一個男孩沿街跑來，背著幾乎和自己一樣大的書包。他是米婭年紀較小的表弟杰，手裡拿著學校表演要用的紙猴子。猴子張開的嘴裡掉了一顆白牙。「我剛才在做晨間運動！」杰得意地說。露比笑了：「帶著紙猴子跑來這裡，也算運動？」杰讓猴子鞠躬。但米婭正看著他肩膀後方的舊鐘架。鐘下方的木頭上有個小小的白色圖案，像猴子缺掉的牙齒。「杰，那個記號是誰畫的？」她問。「不知道，我這隻只是紙做的。」米婭把圖案畫進筆記本。

Grandma Chen came outside with a paper from the town office. Someone had put it on her door during the night. The paper said the River House would be sold in twenty days. The room where families met, played music, and shared food would become part of a new project. Ruby stared at the notice. “Then we have twenty days to save it.” “Shall we ask the town why?” Jay said, trying to sound grown up. “We can ask,” Mia answered, “but we need facts.” She told them about the water and the white mark. Grandma Chen seemed ready to speak, then changed her mind. “Go to school,” she said softly. “You can help here after class.”

陳奶奶拿著一張公所通知走出來。有人昨夜把它貼在門上。通知說河屋將在二十天後出售。這個讓居民聚會、演奏音樂、分享食物的地方，將成為新計畫的一部分。露比盯著通知：「那我們只剩二十天可以救它。」杰努力裝出大人的口氣說：「我們去問鎮公所為什麼，好嗎？」米婭回答：「可以問，但我們需要事實。」她把水痕和白色記號的事告訴大家。陳奶奶似乎想開口，最後卻改變心意。「先去上學吧，」她輕聲說，「下課再來幫忙。」

At school Mia could not listen to the first lesson. A drawing of the River House filled half her page. During lunch she found Leo Wu by the bicycle stand and showed him the drawing. Leo knew old machines better than anyone Mia knew. He looked at the mark and rubbed his chin. “A bell can ring without a person touching it,” he said. “Water can move wood. Wood can move a rope. But I need to see more.” Mia remembered her father's three lists and wrote Leo's idea under GUESS. When the last class ended, the friends returned to River House, keeping well away from its locked door.

到了學校，米婭完全聽不進第一堂課。她在筆記本半頁畫滿河屋。午餐時間，她在腳踏車停放處找到李奧·吳，把圖給他看。李奧比米婭認識的任何人都更懂老機械。他看著記號，摸摸下巴。「不一定要有人碰鐘，它才會響，」他說，「水能推木頭，木頭能拉繩子。但我還得看更多資料。」米婭想起父親教她的三欄筆記，把李奧的說法記在「猜測」欄。最後一堂課結束，朋友們回到河屋，小心地遠離上鎖的門。

The house stood silent in the warm afternoon. Jay wanted to play a joke by shouting “Ghost!” through the window, but Ruby stopped him. “I don't want Grandma to worry.” Leo pointed to a thin line under the door. The dark mark Mia had seen was still there, and a small drop of water moved slowly across the ground. “Water has been here recently,” he said. “That much is real.” They did not touch the door. Instead, Mia wrote the date, the time, and what each friend had noticed. As they turned to leave, something knocked once from inside the empty house. It was not the bell. It came from below the floor.

午後溫暖，河屋靜悄悄地站著。杰想對著窗戶喊「有鬼！」開個玩笑，卻被露比阻止。「我不想讓奶奶擔心。」李奧指著門下的一條細線。米婭早上看到的深色痕跡還在，一小滴水正慢慢流過地面。「最近這裡真的有水，」他說，「這點可以確定。」他們沒有碰門，而是由米婭記下日期、時間和每個朋友看到的事。轉身準備離開時，空屋裡忽然傳出一下敲擊聲。那不是鐘聲，而是從地板下傳來的。
