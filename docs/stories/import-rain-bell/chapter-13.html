The Second Map

雨來之前的鐘聲 · 第 13 / 25 章 · 1000以下 · 英中對照 · 校訂完成

Mia went to the city records room with her father and Officer Su. She had never been there before, though the city was only a short train ride away. “What is the age of this building?” she asked while they waited outside. “More than a hundred years,” her father said. “People have used it for records for a long time.” Mia felt small beside the high stone gate. It was difficult to imagine so many years of paper waiting behind one door. Leo and Ruby were at school; everyone had agreed that too many visitors might slow the search. Mia promised to call as soon as she learned anything.

米婭和父親、蘇小姐一起去市區檔案室。雖然搭火車不遠，她從沒去過。「這棟建築的屋齡是多少？」他們在外頭等候時，她問。「超過一百年，」父親說，「人們很久以前就用它存檔了。」高大的石門讓米婭感覺自己很渺小。她很難想像那麼多年留下的紙都藏在同一扇門後。李奧和露比留在學校；大家都同意訪客太多可能拖慢查詢。米婭答應一有發現就打電話。

Inside, a worker asked them to stand behind a rope while she found the old files. The room was quiet except for a soft song from a radio near the front desk. A family from another town was looking up a name, and a man in a brown coat waited beside them. Mia wanted to borrow a pen but found one in her bag. She opened her notebook to the page with the number from the town office. “Same number, same date,” she told the worker. “We need the pages after the first one.” The worker nodded. “That makes the search easier. Thank you for writing it clearly.”

進屋後，職員請他們站在繩子後方，等她尋找舊檔。室內很安靜，只有前臺收音機播出輕柔歌曲。外地一家人正在查姓名，一名穿棕外套的男子在旁等候。米婭想借筆，卻發現包裡就有。她把筆記本翻到公所文件編號那頁：「編號和日期都一樣，我們需要首頁後面的頁面。」職員點頭：「這樣比較容易找。謝謝妳寫得很清楚。」

An hour passed before the worker came back. She carried an old map and three pages of writing. “These were kept in a box from the old hotel,” she said. “During the move, some town papers came here by mistake.” Mia's father bent toward the page. There was the picture of the north gate, open in one drawing and closed in another. A note said it could be closed for a short test in dry weather. “That's what I remember,” he said. “We did not plan to leave it closed.” Mia searched his face. She could feel how hard it was for him to speak, yet he was speaking.

過了一小時，職員才回來，拿著舊地圖和三頁文件。「這些紙原本裝在舊旅館搬出的箱子裡，」她說，「搬遷時有些公所檔案誤送到這裡。」米婭的父親俯身看文件。圖上畫著北閘門，一張是開著的，一張是關著的。註記寫著只可在乾天進行短時間關閘測試。「這和我記得的一樣，」父親說，「我們並沒有打算一直關著。」米婭看著他的臉。她感覺得出開口有多難，但他終於說了。

Officer Su read the rule again, slowly. “This does not tell us what happened later,” she said. “It tells us what was allowed at the start.” Mia asked about the people who signed the paper. Her father touched his own name. “I checked that the machine moved as it should during the test. The person in charge said they would open the gate the next day.” “Why didn't you go back?” Mia asked. “I was sent to a different job. I thought the people after me would do what the plan said.” He stood up and walked to the window. “That was a mistake in my thinking.”

蘇小姐慢慢再讀了一次規定。「這份文件不能告訴我們後來發生什麼，」她說，「只能說明最初允許做什麼。」米婭問到簽名的人。父親摸著自己的名字：「測試時，我確認機械照要求移動。主管說隔天會重新開閘。」「你為什麼沒有回去？」米婭問。「我被調到別的工作，以為接手的人會照計畫做。」他站起來走向窗邊。「那是我想錯了。」

At the station Mia noticed a poster asking people to give money to care for old city buildings. “How much does it cost to keep all these records?” she asked the worker who had helped them. “More than most people think,” the woman replied. “Paper grows old, rooms need care, and people need time to find things. But we keep them because they can help tomorrow.” Mia thought about her own notebook. A year ago she had filled it with school news. Now it held questions about safety and her family. She did not mind the work. She felt that learning the past could change what people did next.

在車站，米婭看到一張海報，請人捐錢維護老舊市政建築。「保存這些紀錄需要多少錢？」她問協助他們的職員。「比大多數人想的多，」職員說，「紙會老、房間要保養、找資料需要時間。但我們保留它們，是因為可能幫助明天。」米婭想到自己的筆記本。一年前，她只用來寫校園新聞，現在裝滿安全和家人的問題。她不介意花時間。她覺得理解過去能改變人們接下來的行動。

They made copies with the worker's help and left the city before evening. On the train Mia did not ask another question for some time. She looked at the river far below the tracks and thought about promises. Her father took a small rock from his pocket, smooth and gray. “Your grandfather gave me this before my first day at work,” he said. “He told me to remember that one small thing can change a large road.” He put the stone in her hand. “Tomorrow we should show the copy to the town and the factory.” Mia nodded. But one question stayed with her: why had nobody checked that the gate was open again?

他們在職員協助下影印，傍晚前離開市區。火車上，米婭好一陣子沒有再問問題。她看著鐵軌下遠遠的河流，想著承諾。父親從口袋拿出一顆平滑的灰石頭。「這是妳爺爺在我第一天上班前送的，」他說，「他要我記得，一件小事也可能改變一條大路。」他把石頭放進米婭手中。「明天該把副本給公所和工廠看。」米婭點頭。但她心裡仍有一個問題：為什麼從沒有人再確認閘門已經打開？
