乾天的水聲

Seventeen Missing Seconds

雨來之前的鐘聲 · 第 2 / 25 章 · 1000以下 · 英中對照

On the next sunny morning, Leo brought a tool box to the River House, but he did not enter the building. Officer Su had asked everyone to keep out until the town checked the old floor. Leo stood beside the outside power box and showed Mia two wires that were no longer connected. “There is no power going to the bell,” he said. “That means someone pulled the rope, or something else moved it.” Ruby held her phone up. “I have a video of yesterday. Perhaps we can see the rope.” Jay was also there. Mia's cousin had decided that their small group needed a name, but no one agreed on one.

第二天陽光燦爛，李奧帶著工具箱來到河屋，卻沒有進屋。蘇小姐已要求大家在公所檢查舊地板前不要進去。李奧站在外面的電源箱旁，指給米婭看兩條早已斷開的電線。「鐘沒有接電，」他說，「所以不是有人拉了繩，就是別的東西推動了它。」露比舉起手機：「我有昨天的影片，也許能看到繩子。」杰也在旁邊。米婭的表弟已決定替這個小團體取名字，只是沒有人贊成他的提議。

They watched Ruby's video on a bench by the river. In the picture, the morning light lay across the south bank. Behind the water stood a field where a farmer kept six sheep. The sheep did not seem afraid, yet the water rose against the stones for a short moment. “Can you add that to our notes?” Mia asked. Jay opened his book. “I can also draw the river.” Ruby smiled. “That would be helpful. Your lines are better than mine.” Leo looked past them toward the factory. “The man running that place is famous for giving jobs to local families,” he said. “We should be careful about what we say.”

他們坐在河邊長椅上看露比的影片。畫面裡，晨光照著南岸；水後方是一片農田，農夫養著六隻羊。羊看起來並不害怕，但水曾短暫漫上石頭。「可以把這件事加進筆記嗎？」米婭問。杰打開筆記本：「我也能畫出河道。」露比笑了：「那會很有幫助，你畫的線比我的好。」李奧越過他們看向工廠。「經營那裡的人以提供當地家庭工作而出名，」他說，「我們說話得小心。」

“My mother works there,” Leo continued. His lip grew tight. “If the factory cannot build a new place for its machines, it may move away. People think a closed building is more important than my family.” Mia felt the heat in his words. “I don't think that,” she said. “I want to know why water rose on a dry day.” “Both things matter,” Ruby added. Jay tried to make peace. “If the river eats the town, nobody will have a job. If the factory leaves, people will still be sad. So let's learn what is happening.” They all looked at him. “What?” he asked. “A guy can have a good idea sometimes.”

「我媽媽在那裡工作，」李奧接著說，嘴唇繃得很緊。「要是工廠不能擴建機器廠房，可能就會搬走。大家把一棟關閉的舊屋看得比我家還重要。」米婭聽出他的激動。「我沒有那樣想，」她說，「我只是想知道乾天為什麼漲水。」「兩件事都重要，」露比補充。杰試著緩和氣氛：「如果河水淹了小鎮，誰都沒工作。如果工廠搬走，大家也會難過。所以先弄清楚發生什麼事吧。」三人都看著他。「怎樣？」杰問，「男生偶爾也會有好點子啊。」

At school, their science teacher let them use a screen to play the short film. She warned them that pictures can show one thing while hiding another. “You need the time, the place, and the full picture,” she said. Ruby opened her phone again, and Jay placed a ruler against the screen. They compared the place where the water began to rise with the place where it fell. A small movement had happened just before the bell rang. “Was the water coming from the sea?” Ruby asked. Leo shook his head. “Maybe. Maybe not. Look at the direction first.” Mia put the words MAYBE and FACT in different boxes.

到了學校，科學老師讓他們用螢幕播放那段短片。老師提醒他們，照片能顯示一件事，也能隱藏另一件事。「你需要時間、地點和完整畫面。」她說。露比再次打開手機，杰把尺放到螢幕旁。大家比對水開始上升及下降的位置。鐘響之前，水面有過一次小小的動靜。「水是從海裡來的嗎？」露比問。李奧搖頭：「可能是，也可能不是。先看方向。」米婭把「也許」和「事實」寫進不同的框裡。

After class they went to the bridge, where the south road turned toward the factory. Ruby filmed the river again. This time they had a clear view of the bank, the sheep, and the place where the water had reached the stones. Nothing moved. “Perhaps the first video is just strange because my hand moved,” she said. Mia did not answer. She had noticed something about the sound. A low noise began before the bell and ended after it. “Can you fill the screen with the sound lines?” she asked. Ruby found the right page on her phone. The line was long, then suddenly flat, then long again.

下課後，他們到橋邊，南路在那裡轉向工廠。露比再次拍攝河面。這一次，他們能清楚看見河岸、羊群和水曾到達的石頭。什麼都沒動。「也許第一段影片看起來奇怪，只是因為我的手晃了。」露比說。米婭沒有回答，她注意到了聲音：一陣低沉的噪音在鐘響之前開始，直到鐘聲停止後才結束。「可以把聲音波形放大嗎？」她問。露比在手機裡找到那個畫面。線條很長，突然平掉，之後又接起來。

“Did you stop the recording?” Leo asked. Ruby frowned. “I don't remember. I was walking, and Grandma called me. I may have touched the screen.” Jay counted the marks on the time bar. “We have a little problem,” he said. “Look here. The picture moves from six ten and two seconds to six ten and nineteen seconds.” Ruby checked it herself. Seventeen seconds were missing. The group stared at the phone, and the friendly morning seemed less friendly. Mia wrote three questions beneath the drawing of the river: What made the water rise? What made the bell ring? What happened during those seventeen seconds?

「妳那時停了錄影嗎？」李奧問。露比皺眉：「我不記得了。當時我在走路，奶奶叫我，我可能碰到了螢幕。」杰數著時間軸上的刻度。「有個小問題，」他說，「看這裡，畫面直接從六點十分零二秒跳到六點十分十九秒。」露比自己確認了一遍：少了十七秒。大家盯著手機，原本親切的早晨似乎也不那麼親切了。米婭在河道圖下寫了三個問題：是什麼讓水上升？是什麼讓鐘響？那十七秒究竟發生了什麼？
