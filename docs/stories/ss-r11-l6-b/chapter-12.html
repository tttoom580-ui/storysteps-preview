讓每個人安全的成本

The Cost of Keeping Everyone Safe

水道不為誰停留 · 第 12 / 25 章 · 5000–6000 · 英中對照 · 校訂中

The independent engineering report arrived with three options: repair the existing ventilation system, replace it entirely, or continue restricted operation while gathering additional data. Each option had different costs and risks. Mo invited the engineer to explain not only the recommendation but also what the tests could not establish. A councilor demanded a single definitive answer. The engineer refused to invent one. A complete replacement would be expensive and disruptive; continued restrictions would leave some users without access; immediate repair might not address every hidden fault. Jia asked whether the figures included accessible temporary facilities during construction. They did not. The engineer agreed to revise the comparison. An attendant helped the engineer locate the accessible demonstration room. The school fundraiser sold pancake batter mixes to cover printing costs. The latest district census helped estimate how many residents might need a public lane. A chemist explained why the pool test had to be repeated under ordinary conditions. Birds began to chirp outside the hall as the budget workshop ended.

獨立工程報告提出三個選項：修復既有通風系統、全面更換，或在蒐集更多數據期間繼續限制營運。每個選項的成本與風險都不同。沐禾請工程師不只說明建議，也說明測試無法證實什麼。一名議員要求唯一而確定的答案，工程師拒絕編造。全面更換昂貴又影響營運；繼續限制會讓部分人無法使用；立即修理也可能漏掉隱藏故障。嘉寧問數字是否包含施工期間的無障礙臨時設施，答案是否定的。工程師同意修正比較。 工作人員協助工程師找到無障礙示範室。 學校義賣煎餅麵糊材料包，用來支付印刷費。 最新地區人口普查有助估算多少居民可能需要公共水道。 一名化學師解釋泳池測試為何必須在正常使用條件下重做。 預算工作坊結束時，鳥兒開始在禮堂外啁啾。

At the school hall, Nia led a budget workshop where anyone could challenge an assumption. A woman who cleaned the pool pointed out that changing chemicals and washing equipment took longer than the previous schedules allowed. A teenage athlete asked why the city spent more on promotional events than lifeguard training. Nia wrote both questions on the board. Wei's representative attended and suggested that a private operator would work more efficiently. Ying asked for documented examples rather than a slogan. The resulting discussion was slow, awkward, and more informative than the glossy presentation the company had prepared. Nobody left with a perfect plan, but nobody could say the costs were unknowable. The city could not dispense emergency supplies without tracking where they went. A tired cleaner gave a quiet groan when another meeting ran beyond closing. The cooling system's iceberg diagram showed only the visible portion of its costs. Ying's knowledge of forgotten maintenance routines proved invaluable during the review. A lavish renovation sketch looked impressive but lacked a credible operating budget.

在學校禮堂，妮亞主持預算工作坊，任何人都能質疑假設。一名泳池清潔員指出，更換清潔用品與清洗設備的時間，遠比舊班表預留的長。青少年運動員問市府為何花在宣傳活動上的錢比救生訓練更多。妮亞把兩個問題都寫上白板。魏昊的代表出席，說私人營運會更有效率。宋穎要求拿出有紀錄的例子，而非口號。討論緩慢、尷尬，卻比公司準備的精美簡報更有資訊。沒人帶著完美方案離開，但也沒人能再說成本不可理解。 市府發放緊急用品時，必須追蹤流向。 另一場會議超過打烊時間，疲憊的清潔員低聲呻吟。 冷卻系統的冰山圖只呈現成本的可見部分。 宋穎熟悉被遺忘的保養流程，審查時顯得極有價值。 豪華整修草圖令人驚艷，卻缺乏可信的營運預算。

Mo received an email from the other city's director. The flexible contract she had requested was possible for one year, on condition that she spend a fixed week on site every month. She read it twice and felt an unexpected sense of possibility. The work would allow her to learn from several centers and bring that expertise back, if the town still wanted her. She told her mother first. Lan asked practical questions about travel, medical appointments, and who would manage the local project when Mo was away. Mo was grateful for questions instead of a demand that she remain close. She began sketching a shared leadership proposal with Ying and Jia. The city's new logo had to appear on the public inspection notices. A loudspeaker failed during the workshop, so organizers used printed questions. The martial arts club offered to share its accessible training hall during repairs. The inspector said a disease outbreak would require a separate public-health response. A local café served oyster soup at the fundraising dinner for adult volunteers.

沐禾收到另一座城市主管的電子郵件。她提出的彈性合約可以先試行一年，條件是每月固定一週到當地工作。她讀了兩遍，意外感到未來仍有許多可能。這份工作能讓她從幾座中心學習，再把專業帶回來，只要小鎮仍需要她。她先告訴母親。婉如問起交通、就醫安排，以及沐禾不在時誰管理本地計畫。她很感謝母親問的是實際問題，而非要求她留下。她開始和宋穎、嘉寧草擬共同領導方案。 公開檢查公告必須使用市府新標誌。 工作坊擴音器故障，主辦方改用印好的問題。 武術俱樂部願在修繕期間分享其無障礙訓練館。 檢查員說若發生疾病群聚，需另外啟動公共衛生應變。 當地咖啡館在成年志工募款晚餐供應牡蠣湯。

Eli helped assemble a model of the proposed access route, then withdrew from its evaluation because he had designed part of it. Watching him step aside gave Mo a quiet satisfaction: he was finally treating accountability as a practice, not a declaration. Later he asked whether they might take a walk somewhere unrelated to the project. Mo agreed, on Saturday after her planning meeting. She did not ask him to wait for an answer about the future; she had not found one yet. He accepted the uncertainty without making it a wound. The model remained on the table between their two chairs, useful but no longer a substitute for speaking honestly. Nia asked how many users would populate the temporary schedules in winter. The missing cost estimate was a setback, not permission to invent a number. Mo stayed skeptical of any promise that excluded repair and replacement costs. Residents could subscribe to text updates without giving away health information. The contractor was not permitted to withhold the safety diagrams from inspectors.

之庭協助組裝新無障礙路線模型，但因自己參與設計而退出評估。看見他主動退開，沐禾有一種安靜的滿足：他終於把負責當成日常做法，而不是口頭宣言。後來他問兩人能否到與計畫無關的地方散步。沐禾答應，約在週六企畫會議之後。她沒有要求他等一個關於未來的答案，因為自己也還沒有。他接受不確定，沒有把它當成傷害。模型留在兩張椅子之間的桌上，很有用，卻不再取代誠實交談。 妮亞詢問冬季臨時時刻表預計有多少使用者。 缺少費用估算是挫折，卻不代表可以捏造數字。 沐禾對排除維修與更新成本的承諾仍抱持懷疑。 居民可訂閱簡訊更新，不必交出健康資料。 承包商不得向檢查員扣留安全圖面。
