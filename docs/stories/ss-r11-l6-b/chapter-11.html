舊報告遺漏的事

What the Old Report Omitted

水道不為誰停留 · 第 11 / 25 章 · 5000–6000 · 英中對照 · 校訂中

Eli arrived at Mo's office with a copy of the report from the year she left competitive swimming. It carried his signature as a junior facilities adviser, attached to a committee summary that described her injury as 'noncompliance with training recommendations.' He had not written the phrase, but he had seen it and allowed his signature to remain. Mo read the page without speaking. The words were familiar enough to make her hands shake; they had once followed her from one job interview to another. Eli did not ask her to appreciate the difficulty of his position. He asked whether she wanted an independent correction submitted with the original medical restrictions attached. She said yes. The old team had given Mo acclaim only when she won races without complaint. Mo asked the committee to remove ambiguity from the injury summary. The report ignored bodily limits that had been stated in the original medical note. A cool draught entered the meeting room through the open upper window. The old pool thermometer displayed Fahrenheit while the revised chart used Celsius.

之庭帶著一份沐禾退出競技那年的報告來到她辦公室。文件有他以初階設施顧問身分留下的簽名，附在一份把她的傷勢描述為「不遵守訓練建議」的委員會摘要上。那句話不是他寫的，但他看見了，卻任由簽名留著。沐禾默默讀完，熟悉的字句使雙手發抖；它曾從一場面試跟到另一場面試。之庭沒有要求她體諒他的難處，而是問她是否希望附上當年的醫療限制，提出獨立更正。她說要。 舊隊伍只在沐禾不抱怨地贏得比賽時才給她讚譽。 沐禾要求委員會去除傷勢摘要中的模糊說法。 報告忽略原始醫療紀錄中已明列的身體限制。 一股涼風從會議室打開的高窗吹進來。 舊泳池溫度計顯示華氏，新圖表則使用攝氏。

The revision process was slower than they wanted. The old committee had dissolved, and some members had moved or retired. Its successor could correct a factual record but could not declare what every person had privately believed. Mo gathered the original training restriction, an attendance schedule, and correspondence showing that she had requested a second assessment before her final race. She allowed an advocate to represent her rather than negotiating directly with people who had humiliated her. Eli submitted a statement describing exactly what he had signed and what he had failed to check. It named his omission without asking anyone to praise his courage for finally acknowledging it. The archive's fireproof cabinet held a copy of the disputed training record. A geographical map showed how many districts depended on the public pool. The committee found no evidence that a hacker had changed the archived document. The legal explanation was rewritten so a layman could follow the correction procedure. Mo did not need Eli's apology to liberate her from the false description.

更正程序比想像中慢。舊委員會已解散，部分成員搬離或退休。繼任機關可以更正事實紀錄，不能宣告每個人當時心裡相信什麼。沐禾蒐集原始訓練限制、出席表，以及證明她最後一場比賽前曾請求複查的通信。她請代理人出面，而不親自與曾羞辱她的人談判。之庭提交陳述，準確寫下他簽過什麼、漏查什麼。文件承認疏忽，沒有要求任何人稱讚他如今終於鼓起勇氣。 檔案室防火櫃保存著爭議訓練紀錄的複本。 一張地理圖顯示多少行政區依賴公共泳池。 委員會沒有發現駭客修改封存文件的證據。 法律說明經改寫，讓一般人也能理解更正程序。 沐禾不需要之庭道歉，才能從不實描述中解放自己。

At the pool, Jia asked why Mo had not told the community about the accusation sooner. Mo replied that privacy was not dishonesty, and that a person could choose when a painful history became public. Jia apologized for pressing, then said she hoped the corrected record would help other athletes. Mo thanked her but refused to make that a condition of her own healing. The young swimmer understood. Together they returned to the pilot survey, where a few users complained that evening lessons conflicted with shift work. Their task was to solve that problem, not turn one woman's old injury into a symbol for everything the town needed. A faded mermaid was painted beside the old children's changing room. The missing file was found among miscellaneous boxes from the dissolved committee. The clerk recorded each occurrence of the misleading phrase across archived reports. After the formal hearing Mo took a quiet outing beside the river with her mother. The correction process required Mo to persevere without surrendering her privacy.

在泳池，嘉寧問沐禾為何不早點向社區說明昔日指控。沐禾回答，隱私不等於不誠實；人可以選擇何時公開痛苦經驗。嘉寧為追問道歉，接著說希望更正紀錄能幫助其他運動員。沐禾感謝她，卻拒絕把這當成自己療癒的條件。嘉寧明白了。兩人回到試辦問卷，有些使用者抱怨晚間課程與輪班工作衝突。她們的任務是解決那個問題，而不是讓一名女性的舊傷代表小鎮所有需求。 舊兒童更衣室旁畫著一條褪色美人魚。 缺失檔案在已解散委員會的雜項箱子裡找到。 書記記下誤導字句在舊報告中每次出現的位置。 正式聽證後，沐禾和母親到河邊安靜散步。 更正程序要求沐禾堅持，也不必放棄隱私。

On his way out, Eli asked if he could call Mo the next day. She said he could, but not to seek reassurance about the correction. 'You are doing something necessary,' she told him. 'It is not a test I have designed for you to pass.' He nodded and left. Mo stayed after closing to review the schedule, then took a quiet walk home by the river. For the first time, she imagined a future in which the false summary was corrected but did not become the most important fact about her. There would still be lessons to design, a pool to evaluate, and choices that belonged to her. She paused to ponder whether she wanted the past discussed at every future meeting. The old fans would revolve above the records table during summer hearings. Ying put a clean utensil beside the tea, keeping food away from documents. Mo refused to name one former coach the sole villain of a system that others enabled. A volunteer brought plain yogurt to the late-running review session.

離開前，之庭問隔天能否打電話給她。沐禾說可以，但不要為了從她口中得到關於更正的安慰。「你正在做必要的事，」她說，「這不是我為你設計、等你通過的考試。」他點頭離去。沐禾留下來審查時刻表，打烊後沿河慢慢走回家。第一次，她想像即使不實摘要獲得更正，它也不必成為自己人生最重要的事。她仍有課程要設計、泳池要評估，以及屬於自己的選擇。 她停下思考，是否願意往後每場會議都再談過去。 夏季聽證時，舊風扇會在檔案桌上方旋轉。 宋穎把乾淨餐具放在茶旁，讓食物遠離文件。 沐禾拒絕把單一前教練當成整套制度唯一的壞人。 志工為拖延的審查會送來原味優格。
