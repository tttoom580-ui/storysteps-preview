沒有新鮮空氣的房間

A Room Without Fresh Air

水道不為誰停留 · 第 8 / 25 章 · 5000–6000 · 英中對照 · 校訂中

用互動版查詞、朗讀與收藏

A parent reported an irritating smell near the indoor teaching pool. The operator said the building's ventilation had passed inspection three months earlier, but the test had occurred when most equipment was turned off. Mo requested measurements during ordinary lessons and compared them with the previous record. The second test found an uneven airflow pattern near the changing rooms. It did not prove a dangerous exposure; the independent hygienist recommended restricting occupancy until the system could be balanced and monitored. Some families accused Mo of exaggeration. Others wanted the whole center shut forever. She explained the narrower finding, line by line, until several listeners began asking better questions. A clinic poster reminded families that an antibiotic treated bacteria, not every cough. The group did not applaud until the inspector had finished explaining the limitations. An astronomer from the local college donated a star-map print to the children's room. Nia hoped to computerize the daily logs without removing staff access to paper copies. A power fault could disable the automatic doors, so a manual release was necessary.

一名家長通報室內教學池旁有刺激性氣味。營運方說通風系統三個月前通過檢查，但那次測試時多數設備沒有運轉。沐禾要求在正常上課期間量測，並與舊紀錄比較。第二次測試發現更衣室附近氣流不均。這還不能證明已造成危險暴露；獨立衛生專家建議限制人數，直到系統完成平衡調整並持續監測。有些家長指責沐禾誇大，有些人則想永久關閉泳池。她逐項說明更精確的結論，直到聽眾開始提出更好的問題。 診所海報提醒家庭，抗生素用來治療細菌感染，不是所有咳嗽。 團隊等檢查員說完測試限制，才鼓掌。 當地大學天文學家捐了一幅星圖給兒童休息室。 妮亞希望把日誌電腦化，同時保留員工索取紙本的權利。 電力故障可能使自動門失效，因此必須設置手動開啟機制。

Jia organized a walk-through with swimmers who used different mobility supports. They found a door that needed too much force, a sign visible only from one angle, and a bench that blocked access when it was moved for cleaning. None of these details appeared in the company's glossy brochure. The test group described what they experienced and what they preferred, not what an architect assumed they needed. Eli recorded every comment with its author's permission. He also noted places where people wanted different solutions. The building could not be called accessible simply because everyone had been offered the same arrangement. Fairness required more attentive work than uniformity. Ying rehearsed how to evacuate the room when the main corridor became crowded. Every new expenditure needed an identified source of funding. A tired parent became irritable during the delayed briefing, then apologized. Mo noted that kin should not be appointed to contracts without disclosing relationships. A quiet meditation class used the hall after the swimmers had departed.

嘉寧安排不同輔具使用者共同走查。大家發現一扇門開啟太費力、一張標誌只能從某個角度看見，以及一張清潔時移位就堵住通道的長椅。這些細節都沒出現在公司精美手冊裡。測試者描述實際經驗與偏好，而非建築師想像他們需要什麼。之庭在獲得同意後逐項記錄，也標出大家希望採不同解法的地方。不能只因每個人都被提供同一種安排，就宣稱建築無障礙。公平需要比整齊劃一更細緻的工作。 宋穎演練主走廊擁擠時如何撤離房間。 每筆新支出都必須標明資金來源。 一名疲憊家長在延遲簡報時變得易怒，後來道歉。 沐禾指出，親屬承接合約時必須揭露關係。 泳客離開後，安靜的冥想課使用大廳。

By Friday the city released a preliminary ventilation budget. The repairs were possible, but they would consume money reserved for the deep pool. Mo called a meeting to explain the conflict rather than hide it inside a revised schedule. One parent preferred air quality work; a competitive swimmer argued for restoring the deep lanes. Both had a legitimate stake, and neither could decide for the other. The council agreed to reserve part of the funds for independent monitoring and postpone the final division of repair money until more bids arrived. Mo disliked the delay, but the record showed exactly why it had happened. An honest postponement was better than a concealed priority. The repair invoice included a nickel washer that cost less than a bus fare. Jia asked the guide to orient the safety map for seated visitors. An oriental carpet from the old lounge was moved away from the wet entrance. The health team reviewed the pneumonia prevention notice for the community clinic. The rented lift was still a prototype under limited supervised evaluation.

週五，市府公布通風改善初步預算。修繕可行，卻會用掉原本留給深水池的經費。沐禾召開會議說明衝突，不把它藏在新時程裡。一名家長主張先改善空氣，競技泳者則要求先修深水道。兩邊都有正當權益，誰也不能替另一方決定。議會同意保留部分款項用於獨立監測，待更多報價到齊後再決定維修資金分配。沐禾不喜歡延誤，但紀錄清楚說明了原因。誠實延後，比隱藏優先順序更好。 維修發票列了一個鎳製墊圈，價格不到一趟車資。 嘉寧請導覽員調整安全地圖方向，方便坐姿訪客閱讀。 舊休息室的東方風格地毯被移離潮濕入口。 醫療團隊審閱社區診所的肺炎預防公告。 租來的升降設備仍是只准在有限監督下評估的原型。

That evening Eli found Mo sitting on the steps outside the emptied pool. He told her that his overseas interview had gone well and that a decision was expected soon. She asked what he actually wanted, not what he thought she wanted to hear. He admitted he was excited by the work and afraid that leaving might destroy the fragile trust between them. Mo had no easy reassurance. 'If trust depends on one of us giving up a real opportunity,' she said, 'it is not the kind I want.' They shared a sandwich under the streetlamp. Neither made a promise. Their unfinished conversation felt more honest than the declarations they had once expected from adults. The school's psychiatry adviser urged staff not to treat every anxious swimmer alike. The temporary handrail looked robust but still required a load test. Jia insisted that every swimmer remained sovereign over their own participation. A spokesperson read the committee's findings without hiding the unanswered questions. Mo kept a spare wardrobe rack to separate dry clothing from wet equipment.

當晚，之庭在清空的泳池外階梯找到沐禾。他說海外面試順利，很快就會有決定。她問他真正想要什麼，而不是他以為她想聽什麼。他坦言自己對工作很期待，也怕離開會破壞兩人剛建立的脆弱信任。沐禾無法輕易保證。「如果信任必須靠其中一人放棄真正的機會維持，」她說，「那不是我想要的信任。」兩人在路燈下分食三明治，沒有做出承諾。未完的對話，比他們從前期待大人該說的豪語更誠實。 學校精神醫學顧問提醒員工，不要把所有焦慮泳者視為相同。 臨時扶手看起來堅固，仍須做負載測試。 嘉寧堅持每位泳者都對自己的參與保有自主權。 發言人宣讀委員會結論，沒有隱藏尚未解答的問題。 沐禾保留一個備用衣架，把乾衣物與濕器材分開。
