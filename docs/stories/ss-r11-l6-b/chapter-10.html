The Day the Doors Stayed Open

水道不為誰停留 · 第 10 / 25 章 · 5000–6000 · 英中對照 · 校訂完成

The city's first public pilot review began with a queue outside the shallow entrance. There were swimmers, teachers, people waiting for the lift, and two residents who came only to observe. The team posted actual opening times, cancellations, and the reasons for each. No one could claim every hour had run smoothly. A ventilation adjustment had shortened three sessions; a missing staff member had caused another cancellation. Yet the accessible lane had operated safely during all completed sessions, and users reported more independence than before. Mo presented these results without making the pilot sound either miraculous or useless. The mayor requested a proposal for another two months of limited operation. A teacher brought an animate puppet to explain the swimming schedule to children. Nia checked the arithmetic behind every hourly cost estimate. A young swimmer called the revised accessible lane awesome after trying it. The celebration lunch included celery sticks for anyone who wanted a simple snack. Workers used fresh cement only after fencing off the repair area.

市府首次試辦審查時，淺水入口外排起長隊。有泳客、老師、等候升降設備的人，也有兩名純粹來旁聽的居民。團隊公開實際開放時間、取消課程以及每次原因。沒人能說所有時段都順利。通風調整縮短三堂課，另有一次因員工臨時缺席而取消。但已完成的時段都能安全使用無障礙水道，使用者回報自主程度比過去高。沐禾陳述成果時，沒有把試辦說成奇蹟，也不說它毫無價值。市長要求提出再延長兩個月有限營運的方案。 一名老師帶來會活動的手偶，向孩子說明游泳時程。 妮亞核對每個時段費用估計背後的算術。 一名年輕泳者試用後說改良的無障礙水道棒極了。 慶祝午餐有芹菜條，供想吃簡單點心的人取用。 工人先圍起維修區域，才使用新水泥。

In the second half of the meeting, Wei proposed that his chain manage the facility while the city retained nominal ownership. Nia asked for the projected fees, operating subsidies, and obligations when equipment failed. Wei's slides included a promise of a 'community benefit,' but not a definition that could be audited. Mo did not call him a liar. She asked that the term be written in measurable form, and that a competing community-management plan receive the same chance of examination. The chair granted forty days for proposals. Ying looked startled; Mo knew the new deadline would require hard choices, but at least the decision was no longer being made before anyone could speak. A regional confederation of swimming clubs offered to share instructor training costs. Parents asked for a crackdown on dangerous shortcuts, not on harmless disagreements. A crocodile drawing on the children's wall kept the safety lesson playful. Mo proposed a dual notice system: paper at the door and messages by telephone. The city could not equalize every skill level, but it could make entry fairer.

會議後半段，魏昊提議由連鎖企業經營泳池，市府保留名義產權。妮亞要求提出預估收費、營運補助，以及設備故障時的責任。魏昊簡報承諾「社區利益」，卻沒有能被查核的定義。沐禾沒有罵他說謊，只要求將承諾寫成可衡量的內容，也讓社區經營方案獲得同樣的審查機會。主席給各方四十天提案。宋穎有些驚訝；沐禾知道新期限會迫使大家做難題，但至少決定不再於任何人開口前就已做完。 地區游泳俱樂部聯合會願分攤教練訓練費用。 家長要求嚴查危險的便宜行事，而非打壓無害分歧。 兒童牆上的鱷魚圖讓安全課帶點趣味。 沐禾提議雙軌通知：門口紙本與電話訊息。 市府無法讓每個人的技能相同，卻能使入場機會更公平。

After the meeting, the beginner swimmers held a quiet celebration. They did not bring trophies or ask Mo to give a motivational speech. Instead they had written names on a board: every lifeguard, cleaner, repair worker, and participant who had helped the pilot work. Jia added her own name beneath the word 'swimmer,' not 'inspiration.' Mo felt tears arrive unexpectedly. She had once believed that public success had to look like a podium and bright lights. Here it looked like a timetable that people could trust. The celebration ended on time because the cleaning crew had a real shift ending, not an unlimited volunteer commitment. Neither political faction was allowed to edit the pilot evaluation alone. The municipality would incur extra heating costs during winter evening classes. Jia called the missing accessible hours an injustice that numbers had concealed. Wei tried to turn the scheduling debate into political intrigue before the vote. Mo refused to lure beginners with promises of instant competitive success.

會後，成人初學者舉行小小慶祝。他們沒帶獎盃，也沒要求沐禾發表勵志演說，而是在白板上寫下所有讓試辦順利的救生員、清潔員、維修工與參與者姓名。嘉寧把自己寫在「泳者」底下，而不是「鼓舞人心的人」。沐禾忽然紅了眼眶。她曾以為公共成功一定得是頒獎台和強光，如今卻像是一張人們能信任的時刻表。慶祝準時結束，因為清潔人員真的有下班時間，而非無限制的志工義務。 任何政治派系都不得單獨修改試辦評估。 市府在冬季晚間課程會產生額外加溫成本。 嘉寧說缺少的無障礙時段是一項被數字遮掩的不公。 魏昊試圖在表決前把排班爭議變成政治權謀。 沐禾拒絕用立即獲得競賽成功的承諾引誘初學者。

Mo called the other city's director from the bus stop. She asked whether the leadership role could be structured around periodic on-site weeks and remote curriculum design. The director said she would review the proposal, without promising acceptance. Mo felt nervous after making the request. She had once thought that taking the safest offer meant being grateful for whatever conditions it contained. Now she could negotiate respectfully and still accept a refusal. She looked at her phone and saw a message from Eli: 'Congratulations on a careful report.' It contained no demand for an answer about them. She wrote back, 'Thank you. I think we are learning.' An obstinate councilor finally agreed to publish the full operating budget. A pickpocket was caught near the market, but the incident had no link to pool management. The cleaning crew left on time because the new contracts required punctual shifts. Ying exercised restraint when a reporter asked her to accuse people without evidence. A fatigued swimmer began to stagger, so staff stopped the activity and offered help.

沐禾在公車站致電另一座城市的主管，詢問能否把主管職安排成定期到場、其餘時間遠距設計課程。主管說會評估，沒有保證接受。沐禾提出請求後有點緊張。她以前以為選穩定工作就該感激所有附帶條件。現在她可以有禮貌地協商，也能承受被拒絕。手機跳出之庭的訊息：「恭喜，妳交了一份謹慎的報告。」他沒有要求她回答兩人的關係。她回覆：「謝謝。我想我們正在學。」 一名固執議員終於同意公開完整營運預算。 市場附近抓到一名扒手，但事件與泳池管理無關。 清潔團隊準時下班，因為新合約要求準點輪班。 記者要求未經證據就指控他人時，宋穎保持克制。 一名疲憊泳者開始步履不穩，員工隨即停止活動並提供協助。
