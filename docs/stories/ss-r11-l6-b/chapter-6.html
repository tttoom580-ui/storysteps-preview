一個公共時段的價格

The Price of a Public Hour

水道不為誰停留 · 第 6 / 25 章 · 5000–6000 · 英中對照 · 校訂中

When the pilot began, the city assigned a financial analyst to explain why free access hours appeared to cost more than private lessons. Mo did not dispute the cost of heating, cleaning, or staff. She disputed the assumption that a class serving people with modest incomes counted as a failure if it did not make a profit. The analyst, Nia, agreed to separate operating expenses from the question of public value. Together they calculated the cost per open hour, including the hours previously omitted from the operator's spreadsheet. The number was higher than Mo expected. She published it anyway, with the assumptions clearly displayed beside it. The storage room needed an airtight container for archived water-test strips. Mo hoped to attain a workable agreement, not a ceremonial promise. A visiting congresswoman asked how the pilot protected access for low-income families. The lunch volunteers served vegetable curry while residents studied the budget. Ying said she did not despise private clubs; she opposed hiding public costs.

試辦開始後，市府派財務分析員妮亞說明：為何免費開放時段的成本，看起來比私人課程更高。沐禾不否認加溫、清潔和人事成本。她反對的是另一個假設：服務收入較低者的課程，只因不賺錢就算失敗。妮亞同意將營運開支與公共價值分開討論。她們一起計算每個開放時段的成本，也把過去被漏算的時數納入。結果比沐禾預期更高。她仍公開數字，並在旁邊清楚列出假設。 儲藏室需要密封容器保存舊水質試紙。 沐禾希望達成可執行的協議，而非儀式性的承諾。 來訪的女性國會議員詢問試辦如何保障低收入家庭使用權。 午餐志工端出蔬菜咖哩，居民一面研究預算。 宋穎說她不鄙視私人俱樂部，只反對隱瞞公共成本。

Wei sent a letter offering to fund the accessible lift in exchange for exclusive advertising and first choice of evening lanes. The offer was legal to propose but had not been approved. Jia asked why a company could choose whose exercise time was worth more. Mo invited Wei to explain the conditions at the next open meeting instead of rejecting the letter in a private exchange. That decision frustrated Ying, who felt the company had already been given too much room. They argued in the equipment store, then wrote down the exact disagreement. Mo wanted a fair record of every proposal; Ying feared that repeated discussion would exhaust the people with the least spare time. Both concerns had weight. An eclipse diagram in the science hall reminded Mo that appearances could mislead observers. The event permit required every donated snack to be clearly labeled edible. Mo held Eli in high esteem for correcting his technical record publicly. An evergreen beside the entrance offered shade where the old benches once stood. Jia painted a fish fin on the sign marking the beginner lane.

魏昊寄來一封信，願意出資購買無障礙升降設備，條件是取得獨家廣告權及晚間水道優先選擇權。提出這種方案並不違法，但尚未得到核准。嘉寧問，為什麼公司能選擇誰的運動時段更值得保留。沐禾沒有私下拒絕，而是請魏昊在下次公開會議說明條件。這讓宋穎很不滿，認為公司已有太多發言空間。兩人在器材室爭論，最後把分歧寫下來。沐禾希望每個提案都留下公平紀錄；宋穎擔心無止境討論會耗盡最缺時間的居民。兩人的憂慮都有道理。 科學館的日食圖提醒沐禾，外觀可能誤導觀察者。 活動許可要求所有捐贈點心清楚標示可食用。 沐禾很敬重之庭公開更正技術紀錄的舉動。 入口一棵常綠樹在舊長椅的位置投下樹蔭。 嘉寧在初學水道告示上畫了一片魚鰭。

Eli reviewed the lift specifications and identified a model that could be rented for a season. It offered less prestige than a permanent installation but might help gather reliable usage data while the building's ownership remained undecided. Mo insisted that the rental include maintenance and a clear responsibility for failures. She had seen temporary equipment described as a gift, then abandoned when the ceremony ended. The trial contract was revised three times before Jia signed off on the user instructions. She crossed out a phrase suggesting that someone needed another person's permission to enter the water. The company representative apologized for the wording and replaced it with a choice-based instruction. A flake of old paint fell from the ceiling, revealing a damp patch. The operator offered branded merchandise instead of the requested maintenance records. Mo feared the monotony of repeating the same budget argument every week. Swimming had begun as a pastime for many adults who now depended on the classes. Nia marked redundancy in the proposed staffing plan without cutting necessary supervision.

之庭查閱升降設備規格，找到可按季租用的機型。它沒有永久設施那樣亮眼，卻能在產權未定時蒐集可靠使用數據。沐禾堅持租約必須涵蓋維修，並明定故障責任。她看過臨時設備被稱為禮物，典禮一結束卻遭棄置。試用契約修了三次，嘉寧才同意使用指引。她刪掉一句暗示使用者入水需取得別人允許的文字。公司代表為措辭道歉，改成尊重個人選擇的說明。 一片舊漆剝落，露出天花板上的潮濕痕跡。 營運商拿出品牌商品，卻沒有提供要求的維修紀錄。 沐禾擔心每週重複同一場預算爭論的單調。 對許多成年人而言，游泳原是消遣，如今課程已成生活所需。 妮亞標出人力計畫的重複配置，但沒有刪減必要監督。

On the way home, Mo and Eli stopped for tea beneath the street trees. He told her he had been invited to join a prominent architecture firm abroad. His contract would begin in the autumn if he accepted. Mo congratulated him without asking him to stay; she still had her own offer unanswered. 'That is the strangest part of talking to you now,' he said. 'You never bargain for a future we have not promised.' Mo watched the bus lights cross the road. 'I spent too long letting other people decide what my time was worth,' she replied. 'I will not ask you to do the same.' A child was not allowed to roam through the machinery corridor during inspection. The cleaning team asked visitors to put rubbish in the marked bins. Ying joked that she could slay the paperwork monster only with a larger stapler. A sly clause would have transferred public evening hours to private lessons. Warm vapor rose from the practice pool as the evening air cooled.

回家途中，沐禾與之庭在行道樹下停下喝茶。他說自己獲邀加入海外一家知名建築事務所，若接受，秋天便得赴任。沐禾向他道賀，沒有要求他留下；她自己的聘書也還沒回覆。「現在和妳談話最奇怪的一點，」他說，「是妳從不拿尚未承諾的未來來交換。」沐禾看著公車燈光掠過馬路。「我花太久讓別人決定我的時間值多少，」她回答，「我不會要求你也這樣做。」 檢查時不准孩子在機房走廊四處亂逛。 清潔團隊請訪客將垃圾投入標示清楚的桶子。 宋穎開玩笑說，要消滅文書怪獸恐怕得靠更大的釘書機。 一條狡猾條款原本會把公共晚間時段轉給私人課程。 傍晚氣溫下降，溫暖水氣從練習池上升。
