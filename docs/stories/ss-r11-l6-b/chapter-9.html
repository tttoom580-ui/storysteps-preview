另一座城市的來信

A Letter from the Other City

水道不為誰停留 · 第 9 / 25 章 · 5000–6000 · 英中對照 · 校訂中

The coaching center in the other city sent Mo a revised offer. It proposed a leadership role designing beginner programs across several districts, not merely teaching one team. The salary was secure, the schedule predictable, and the director respected her refusal to push athletes beyond medical restrictions. Mo felt the pull of work she had earned. She asked for a week to compare the offer with the local project's actual prospects. She would not tell the town she was staying out of loyalty, then secretly resent everyone for what she had given up. She placed both contracts on her table and added a third page headed 'What I need to know.' A school nurse offered advice about acne without making teenagers feel ashamed. The new program director would administer the budget but not make every lesson decision. The brochure promised equal access, although its timetable showed fewer public hours. Mo rejected the notion that a careful contract could become emotional captivity. Students were told not to cram for the lifeguard exam instead of practicing skills.

另一座城市的教練中心寄來更新聘書，提供沐禾跨區設計初學課程的主管職，不再只帶一支隊伍。薪資穩定、班表可預期，主管也尊重她不強迫運動員違反醫療限制的原則。沐禾真切感到這是自己努力爭取來的工作。她請求一週時間，比較新職與本地計畫真正的前景。她不想口頭說為了忠誠留下，心裡卻偷偷怨恨所有讓她犧牲的人。她把兩份合約放在桌上，添一張標題為「我需要知道什麼」的紙。 校護提供青春痘相關建議，不讓青少年難堪。 新任計畫主管負責管理預算，卻不會決定每堂課的全部細節。 宣傳冊承諾平等使用，時刻表卻顯示公共時段減少。 沐禾拒絕讓謹慎簽訂的合約變成情感囚禁。 學生被提醒不要只臨時死背救生考試，而忽略練習技能。

A journalist asked whether her connection to Eli compromised the renovation inquiry. The question was uncomfortable but fair. Mo disclosed their past acquaintance and current collaboration, then recused herself from reviewing drawings for which Eli would be paid. A second architect was appointed to evaluate those designs. Wei's representative tried to turn the disclosure into a scandal; the committee chair reminded him that a disclosed conflict could be managed, while a concealed one could not. Mo left the meeting feeling both exposed and relieved. She had not built a life free of difficult appearances. She had helped create a process in which they could be discussed without guessing. The instructor could detach the floating strap without requiring another person's assistance. One swimmer described mild discomfort after the new bench was installed. Mo explained that the forthcoming job decision belonged to her, not the committee. The side windows helped illuminate the entrance without harsh overhead lighting. A small jade pendant hung from Mo's mother's key ring.

一名記者問她和之庭的關係是否影響整修調查。這問題令人不舒服，卻合理。沐禾揭露兩人的舊識與現今合作，並退出審查會支付之庭費用的圖面。委員會另聘建築師評估。魏昊的代表想把揭露炒成醜聞，主席提醒他：公開的利益衝突可以管理，隱藏的才無法處理。沐禾離開時感到暴露，也感到鬆一口氣。她沒有打造一個毫無難堪外觀的人生，而是協助建立能不靠猜測討論問題的程序。 教練可以拆下浮具繫帶，不必仰賴他人協助。 一名泳者描述新長椅安裝後造成輕微不適。 沐禾說明即將作出的職涯決定屬於她自己，不屬於委員會。 側窗有助照亮入口，又不必依賴刺眼頂燈。 沐禾母親的鑰匙圈上掛著一枚小玉墜。

Jia came to Mo with her own opportunity: a regional para-swimming meet whose schedule conflicted with the public consultation. She feared being treated as a symbol if she chose to compete rather than attend every meeting. Mo told her that participation included the right to leave the room. They prepared written feedback she could submit in advance, and Ying arranged a substitute user representative selected by the group. Jia laughed at the idea that speaking for herself meant being permanently available. 'I want to swim,' she said. Mo helped her check the travel plan, not the choice itself. The next day Jia entered the meet on her own terms. Jia was joyous after entering the race, whatever result the timing board would show. The program included water-safety literacy for adults unfamiliar with warning signs. The video narrator explained each accessible route without pretending to speak for all users. A child's nostril flared as she concentrated on her breathing before a first lesson. A peninsula sports club sent a letter supporting the public-lane proposal.

嘉寧也帶來自己的機會：一場區域身障游泳賽，時間和公眾諮詢重疊。她擔心若選擇比賽而不每場開會，就會被當成只會做象徵的人。沐禾說，參與也包含離開會議室的權利。她們先準備書面意見，宋穎則安排由使用者團體選出的替代代表。嘉寧笑說，為自己發聲不代表永遠隨傳隨到。「我想游泳。」她說。沐禾協助確認交通安排，卻不替她決定選擇。隔天嘉寧依自己的意願報名參賽。 嘉寧報名比賽後滿心喜悅，不論計時板最後顯示什麼。 課程加入水域安全識讀，供不熟悉警示標誌的成人學習。 影片旁白說明每條無障礙路線，卻不假裝代表所有使用者。 孩子第一次上課前專注調整呼吸，鼻孔微微張開。 半島上的運動俱樂部來信支持公共水道提案。

Eli's official offer arrived that evening. He walked to Mo's apartment with the unopened envelope, then stopped himself before asking her to decide with him. Instead they took separate chairs and read their contracts in silence. When he finally spoke, he said he wanted to accept the overseas position. Mo said she was genuinely glad, even though the prospect of distance hurt. He asked whether there might be a relationship beyond the renovation. 'There might,' she replied, 'but I need us to be capable of the truth first.' For once an uncertain answer was not a punishment. They agreed to talk after the public pilot evaluation, with neither promise treated as a debt. Mo knew a potent rumor could spread faster than a complete written correction. The community sought a revival of affordable lessons rather than an exact return to the past. The city promised a selective audit of contracts with the highest financial risk. Mo's stepchild from a former partnership sent congratulations on her new leadership offer. The nearby river still held trout that children observed on school field trips.

之庭的正式聘書當晚送達。他拿著未拆的信封走到沐禾家，臨開口前卻制止自己，沒有請她一起替他決定。兩人各坐一張椅子，安靜閱讀自己的合約。最後他說想接受海外工作。沐禾真心為他高興，雖然遠距離的想像讓她難過。他問整修工作結束後，兩人是否可能發展關係。「也許，」她回答，「但我們得先學會說真話。」這一次，不確定不是懲罰。他們約好在公共試辦評估後再談，不把任何承諾當成債務。 沐禾知道強烈的謠言可能比完整書面更正傳得更快。 社區希望平價課程復甦，不是完全回到過去。 市府承諾針對財務風險最高的合約進行選擇性稽核。 沐禾與前伴侶家庭中的繼子女，為她的新主管職送來祝福。 附近河川仍有鱒魚，孩子們校外教學時會觀察。
