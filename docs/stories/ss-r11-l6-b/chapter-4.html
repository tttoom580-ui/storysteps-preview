危險的水沒有獎盃

No Trophy for Unsafe Water

The engineer found a cracked fastening beneath the drain cover. The damage could be repaired, but the old procedure allowed staff to reset the cover without recording why it had moved. Wei wanted the defect fixed quietly before a press tour. Mo opposed the secrecy, not the repair. She requested a temporary restriction, a public repair entry, and a second inspection before the lane reopened. The city's safety officer agreed with all three. A group of children who had trained there for years complained that they would miss a weekend meet. Mo let them be disappointed. A competition was worth losing if the alternative taught them to treat invisible danger as the price of winning. Mo warned that gambling addiction and sports pressure were different problems requiring different responses. The proposed tiles had aesthetic appeal, but their wet-surface grip had not been tested. The engineer gave an approximate repair cost and marked it as an estimate. Students from the local archaeology club helped photograph the building's older foundations. The safety officer displayed her badge before entering the restricted pump room.

工程師在排水蓋下找到龜裂固定件。損壞可以修復，但舊規程允許員工重新放好蓋子，卻不必記下移位原因。魏昊想在媒體參觀前低調修好。沐禾反對的是隱瞞，不是維修。她要求暫時限制使用、公開維修紀錄，並在重新開道前二次檢查。市府安全員同意三項條件。多年在此訓練的孩子抱怨週末比賽將泡湯，沐禾容許他們失望。若另一個選擇是在無形風險中教孩子追求勝利，那麼失去一場比賽也值得。 沐禾提醒眾人，賭博成癮與競技壓力是不同問題，需要不同處理方式。 新磁磚的美感很好，卻尚未測試濕面止滑效果。 工程師提出約略維修費，明確標示為估算。 當地考古社學生協助拍攝建物較早期的地基。 安全官進入管制幫浦室前出示識別證。

Her mother, Lan, visited the center carrying a packed lunch and a separate question: was Mo planning to remain in town? Lan needed occasional help with paperwork, but she had already arranged her own transport and did not want to become an excuse for her daughter's decisions. Mo listened. 'I may stay for this project, or I may leave again,' she said. Lan nodded with an expression almost stern. 'Then make a plan I can understand. Do not call it a sacrifice for me.' In that exchange Mo recognized where she had learned to resist kindness disguised as control. Her mother had practiced independence long before it acquired a fashionable name. A tiny electrical blaze in an old motor had been recorded as a minor incident. One young coach had called Mo his disciple's greatest inspiration, which embarrassed her. The public meeting could enlighten residents only if the technical drawings were legible. Mo refused to make a fuss over a typo while ignoring a faulty emergency exit. The contract's implicit promise of free access conflicted with its actual fee schedule.

母親婉如帶著午餐來訪，還帶著另一個問題：沐禾打算留在鎮上嗎？婉如偶爾需要協助處理文件，但交通早已自己安排妥當，不想成為女兒決定人生的藉口。沐禾聽完說：「我可能為這個計畫留下，也可能再離開。」婉如神情近乎嚴厲地點頭：「那就訂一份我看得懂的計畫，不要說是為我犧牲。」沐禾在這段對話裡想起，自己為何學會抵抗包著善意的控制。母親早在「自主」成為時髦詞彙之前，就一直這樣生活。 舊馬達曾發生小型電氣火焰，紀錄卻只寫成輕微事件。 一名年輕教練說沐禾是他學生最重要的啟發，讓她有些尷尬。 技術圖面必須看得懂，公開會議才能讓居民理解情況。 沐禾拒絕為筆誤大驚小怪，卻忽略故障的緊急出口。 契約暗示免費使用的承諾，與實際收費表相矛盾。

The next meeting turned bitter when the contractor accused staff of spreading rumors to preserve their jobs. Ying replied with her own employment records and offered to correct any inaccuracy. She asked only that the company provide the same transparency about its maintenance subcontractors. Eli proposed a wall-mounted diagram showing what had been checked, what remained untested, and who was responsible for the next action. Jia insisted that the diagram appear at seated eye level, not only where standing adults could read it. The room reached agreement on a practical design. Nobody had to win an argument about virtue to make a sign people could actually use. An independent chair would preside over the next technical hearing. Wei relied on his company's prestige instead of answering the cost questions. A council member wanted to privatize the entire pool before evaluating public alternatives. Late sunlight seemed to radiate from the pale tiles across the empty lane. The lifeguard rotation had to cover every permitted training hour.

下一次會議很快轉為尖銳。承包商指控員工為保住工作而散播傳言。宋穎交出自己的雇用紀錄，願意更正任何錯誤，並要求公司對維修分包商提供同等透明度。之庭建議在牆上設一張圖，分別標明已檢查項目、尚未測試項目，以及下一步負責人。嘉寧堅持圖表也要貼在坐姿視線可及的高度，不只方便站著的成人看見。眾人對實際設計達成共識。沒有人需要贏得道德辯論，才能做出真正可用的告示。 下一場技術聽證將由獨立主席主持。 魏昊仰賴公司的聲望，卻未回答費用問題。 一名議員想先將整座泳池私有化，再考量公共替代方案。 傍晚陽光彷彿從空水道的淺色磁磚散射開來。 救生員輪班必須涵蓋每個核准訓練時段。

When the repaired cover passed the first mechanical test, Mo still delayed the reopening until a second inspector signed the result. Wei called her stubborn. A younger coach whispered that athletes had once used the same word about Mo when she refused to train through pain. She did not tell him that being right had once cost her the only career she had imagined. Instead she watched the water settle after the test and considered the long future beyond one meet. Eli stood beside her without asking whether she had forgiven him. At last she said, 'Tomorrow, we can talk about the shoulder.' His relief was quiet enough that she could accept it without feeling responsible for it. A forgotten electric shaver was found in a locker during the safety sweep. Ying led a stray dog away from the service gate before opening it. A thin veil of mist hung above the outdoor practice tank at dawn. The community fundraiser included a nearby vineyard that donated juice, not ownership rights. The contractor had promised a two-year warranty for the repaired platform.

修好的排水蓋通過第一次機械測試後，沐禾仍等第二名檢查員簽認，才同意開放。魏昊說她固執。一名年輕教練低聲說，當年她不願帶傷訓練時，其他運動員也這樣叫她。沐禾沒有告訴他，堅持正確曾讓她失去唯一想像過的職涯。她只是看著測試後平靜下來的水面，想著一場比賽之外漫長的未來。之庭站在身旁，沒有問她是否原諒了他。她終於說：「明天，我們可以談談肩傷。」他的鬆一口氣十分安靜，安靜到她不必為此負責。 安全清查時，在置物櫃找到一把被遺忘的電動刮鬍刀。 宋穎先把服務門旁的流浪狗引走，才開門。 黎明時，戶外練習池上方罩著一層薄霧。 社區募款有附近葡萄園捐贈果汁，卻沒有交換經營權。 承包商曾承諾修復升降平台提供兩年保固。
