公告底下的水聲

The Water Beneath the Notice

水道不為誰停留 · 第 1 / 25 章 · 5000–6000 · 英中對照

用互動版查詞、朗讀與收藏

On the morning Mo Chen returned to Green Channel Pool, she heard water striking tile before she saw the locked entrance. She had spent sixteen years measuring seconds in competition and another seven teaching people to enter water without fear. Now the city intended to lease the public building to a luxury fitness chain. A paper notice promised improvement but said nothing about the older swimmers who came before sunrise or the wheelchair users who needed the shallow ramp. Mo, thirty-nine, had not come home to rescue her childhood. She had come because her former teammate Ying asked her to inspect an unsafe practice schedule. The difference mattered. Mo checked the broken clasp on the changing-room gate before allowing any inspection. Ying asked the committee to convene while witnesses could still attend. A painted otter cub smiled from a faded children's swimming poster. A dental outreach team had used the lobby for a free clinic. One angry resident called Wei a dictator; Mo requested specific complaints instead.

陳沐禾回到青渠泳池的早晨，還沒看見上鎖的大門，便先聽見水拍打磁磚的聲音。她曾有十六年用秒數衡量競賽成績，之後又花了七年教人如何不帶恐懼地入水。現在，市府打算把公共泳池租給豪華健身連鎖企業。公告保證會改善設施，卻沒提及清晨來游泳的長者，或需要淺水坡道的輪椅使用者。三十九歲的沐禾不是為了拯救童年而回來。她是因為昔日隊友宋穎請她查看一份不安全的訓練時程。這個差別很重要。 沐禾先檢查更衣室門上損壞的門扣，才允許進行查驗。 宋穎請委員會趁證人還能到場時召開會議。 褪色的兒童游泳海報上畫著一隻微笑的水獺幼獸。 牙科巡迴團隊曾借用大廳舉辦免費門診。 一名憤怒居民罵魏昊是獨裁者；沐禾則要求提出具體問題。

The first person she met was Eli Ho, forty-three, an accessibility architect carrying rolled plans under one arm. Once, after Mo injured her shoulder, he had heard a coach call her lazy and had said nothing. They had not spoken properly in eleven years. He offered to show her the building drawings. She refused a private tour and asked that the lifeguards and users be invited as well. Eli did not protest. He placed his plans on the dry steps and said the consultation period ended in thirty days. Inside, an empty lane reflected the fluorescent ceiling, and Mo understood how easy it would be to mistake clean-looking water for a safe public space. The closure directive was posted without a record of the public consultation. An elderly swimmer's dwelling stood opposite the pool, a five-minute walk away. Children used to gallop across the grass after lessons, laughing at one another. Unverified rumors could heighten fear before the actual inspection results arrived. The janitor handed over an old maintenance key and recorded the exchange.

她遇見的第一個人是四十三歲的無障礙建築師賀之庭，手臂下夾著一卷圖紙。十一年前，沐禾肩傷後，教練當著他面說她懶惰，他卻沒有出聲。兩人多年來不曾好好交談。他提出帶她看建築圖，沐禾拒絕私下參觀，要求也邀請救生員和使用者。之庭沒有爭辯，把圖紙攤在乾燥階梯上，告訴她公眾諮詢只剩三十天。館內空蕩的水道映出螢光燈，沐禾忽然明白：看起來乾淨的水，很容易被誤認成安全的公共空間。 停用指令張貼時沒有附上公眾諮詢紀錄。 一名高齡泳客的住所就在泳池對面，步行五分鐘可達。 孩子過去上課後總在草地上奔跑嬉笑。 未經證實的傳言可能在檢驗結果出爐前加劇恐懼。 清潔員交出一把舊維修鑰匙，並登記交接。

At noon they opened the doors for a short inspection. The ramp railing moved when an adult swimmer leaned against it; the nearest emergency phone had no connection. Ying, now head lifeguard, showed Mo a log with two inspection dates written in the same ink but by different hands. Mo took photographs with the caretaker present and signed a receipt for each copy. She would not accuse anyone on a feeling. In the lobby an eighteen-year-old swimmer named Jia waited with her own list of questions. Jia used a wheelchair on land and was tired of being spoken about rather than spoken to. She asked Mo whether the proposed facility would keep an independent accessible lane. Mo answered, 'We have to make them answer that in writing.' The pool's faded motto promised that everyone belonged in the water. A pilgrim visiting the nearby shrine asked if the public showers would remain open. The old earthquake agreement provided a precedent for an independent safety review. Wei expected to reap membership profits after converting the facility into a private club. The lift platform stayed stationary when Ying pressed its emergency control.

中午，眾人短暫開門檢查。成年泳客靠上坡道扶手時，扶手竟晃動；最近的緊急電話也無法接通。如今擔任救生主管的宋穎拿出紀錄，兩個檢查日期雖用相同墨色，筆跡卻不同。沐禾在管理員見證下拍照，並為每份複本簽收。她不願憑感覺指控任何人。大廳裡，十八歲的游泳者嘉寧帶著自己的問題清單等待。嘉寧在陸地使用輪椅，早已厭倦別人只談論她、不直接和她說話。她問新設施是否會保留獨立的無障礙水道。沐禾回答：「我們得讓他們用白紙黑字回答。」 泳池褪色的座右銘寫著：每個人都能在水中找到位置。 一名到附近神社參拜的朝聖者問公共淋浴間是否仍開放。 過去的地震協議留下了獨立安全審查的先例。 魏昊期待將泳池轉為私人俱樂部後取得會員收益。 宋穎按下緊急控制鍵時，升降平台仍保持靜止。

That evening Mo considered returning to the coaching job she had temporarily left in another city. Its director had given her two weeks to decide whether she wanted a permanent contract. The pool had no promise to offer in return: only damaged equipment, incomplete records, and people who had learned to lower their expectations. Eli waited by the gate without approaching until she finished a call. 'I owe you an apology,' he said. 'Perhaps,' Mo replied, 'but tonight I need the original maintenance register.' He gave her the document request number, not a confession or a romantic speech. For the first time that day, the work seemed possible without surrendering her boundaries. Mo refused to treat expensive as a synonym for accessible. Gardeners planned to transplant three trees before repairing the outdoor drain. A neighborhood trustee requested the lease terms before any vote. Mo demanded an unconditional stop to the hazardous demonstration, not a permanent ban on swimming. She would not utter an accusation until she had checked the signatures.

當晚，沐禾想起另一座城市暫時離開的教練工作。主管給她兩週時間，決定是否接受正式合約。泳池卻無法給她任何承諾，只有壞掉的設備、不完整的紀錄，以及早已學會降低期待的人。之庭在門旁等她講完電話，才走近說：「我欠妳一個道歉。」沐禾回答：「也許。但今晚我需要原始維護登記簿。」他遞來申請文件的編號，沒有辯解，也沒有浪漫宣言。那天第一次，沐禾覺得自己也許能完成工作，而不必放棄界線。 沐禾拒絕把昂貴視為無障礙的同義詞。 園丁打算在修理戶外排水溝前移植三棵樹。 一名社區受託人要求表決前先公開租賃條款。 沐禾要求無條件停止危險示範，並非永遠禁止游泳。 在核對簽名之前，她不願說出指控。
