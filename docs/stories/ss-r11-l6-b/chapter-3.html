藍墨水的簽名

The Signatures in Blue Ink

水道不為誰停留 · 第 3 / 25 章 · 5000–6000 · 英中對照

The original register arrived with missing pages and a handwritten explanation that they had been destroyed in a flood. The building had not flooded in the year listed. Rather than announce a conspiracy, Mo asked the archive officer for a weather report and the insurance record. Both showed an ordinary dry season. Ying found the corresponding entries in a separate lifeguard notebook, including a report about a drain cover that had shifted during a children's lesson. The child had not been injured, but the class had been moved without any formal repair order. This was the first piece of evidence Mo could connect to a specific unresolved hazard. Her anger became less spectacular and more useful. The cashier showed Mo that family passes had been counted as one visitor. A free health checkup had once brought dozens of older residents to the pool lobby. Mo asked the city to curb speculation until the missing records were found. The echo of a maintenance drill could deafen people standing near the pump room. Ying refused to call an honest mistake a disgrace merely because it was public.

原始登記簿送來時缺了幾頁，旁邊手寫說明稱那些紙在洪水中毀損。但記載的年份，泳池根本沒有淹水。沐禾沒有立刻宣布陰謀，而是向檔案員索取天氣報告及保險紀錄，兩者都顯示那年是平常的乾季。宋穎在另一本救生日誌中找到相對應的記載：一堂兒童課時排水蓋曾移位。孩子沒有受傷，課程卻被移走，也沒有正式修繕命令。這是沐禾第一次把具體危險與未解決的紀錄連接起來。她的憤怒不再戲劇化，反而更有用。 收銀員告訴沐禾，家庭票被當成一位訪客計算。 一次免費健康檢查曾吸引數十位長者來到泳池大廳。 沐禾請市府在找到缺失紀錄前遏止猜測。 維修電鑽的回音可能使站在幫浦室附近的人震耳欲聾。 宋穎拒絕只因失誤公開，就將誠實的錯誤稱為恥辱。

The city's preliminary hearing opened in a library room too small for all the people who had come. Wei Hao, director of the prospective fitness chain, described Mo as a bitter former athlete who feared progress. Mo refused to defend her personality. She presented the two notebooks, named the differences, and asked for a physical inspection of the drain. When Wei said no child had been hurt, Jia replied that protection should not depend on waiting for a victim. The chair ordered an independent engineer to examine the mechanism. It was a small procedural victory, not proof that the company had forged anything. Mo said so plainly to the reporters outside. The disposal report listed worn drain parts without identifying who had received them. Jia's dissent was entered in the minutes instead of being politely ignored. One instructor planned to emigrate next spring and feared losing her unpaid wages. Mo wanted the center to enrich the neighborhood, not just sell expensive memberships. A lengthy inspection was frustrating, yet an unfinished one would protect nobody.

市府初步聽證在圖書館一間小會議室舉行，來的人多到擠不進去。未來健身連鎖企業負責人魏昊，把沐禾說成害怕進步的失意前運動員。沐禾沒有為性格辯解，而是交出兩本日誌、指出差異，要求實際檢查排水設備。魏昊說從沒孩子受傷，嘉寧回答，保護人不該建立在等到有人受傷之後。主席命獨立工程師檢查機件。這是小小的程序勝利，還不能證明公司偽造資料。沐禾在門外也向記者清楚說明。 廢棄處理報告列出磨損排水零件，卻未記錄收件人。 嘉寧的不同意見被列入會議紀錄，而非遭到禮貌性忽視。 一名教練打算明春移居海外，擔心未領薪資落空。 沐禾希望中心豐富社區生活，而非只販售昂貴會員證。 漫長檢查令人挫折，但未完成的檢查保護不了任何人。

Back at the pool, Eli showed Mo the original architecture of the ramp. Its steepest section had been approved as temporary during a renovation and never reassessed. He admitted that he had signed a later drawing without asking why the temporary label remained. His signature was on a technical review, not on the missing maintenance pages; the distinction mattered, though it did not make him innocent of neglect. Mo asked him to correct his report publicly before he asked her to believe his private regrets. He agreed. She was not interested in awarding forgiveness as payment for useful work. A friendship, if it returned, would have to survive truthful records. An older swimmer described the pool as a lifelong place of friendship. The clerk addressed the elderly witness as madam and waited for her complete answer. A melancholy song drifted from the school hall while the hearing continued. The inspector explained which temporary repairs were permissible under existing rules. Mo had no wish to preach about courage to people whose jobs were at risk.

回到泳池後，之庭拿出坡道最初的建築圖。最陡的一段在整建時被暫時核准，之後卻從未複查。他承認自己曾簽下一份更新圖，卻沒追問「臨時」標示為何還在。他的簽名出現在技術審查，並非缺頁的維護資料；兩者有差別，但也不代表他沒有疏失。沐禾請他先公開更正報告，再談私人悔意。之庭答應了。她不願用原諒交換他做好工作。若友誼要回來，必須經得起真實紀錄。 一名高齡泳者說，這座泳池承載了她終生的友誼。 書記尊稱那位年長證人為女士，並等她說完。 聽證進行時，憂傷的歌聲從學校禮堂飄來。 檢查員說明現行規定允許哪些臨時修繕。 沐禾不想向生計受威脅的人說教勇氣。

In the evening the neighborhood volunteers began counting the people who would lose an affordable place to exercise. They sorted attendance without names, separated school groups from paid classes, and marked every unknown number rather than inventing one. Mo watched Jia lead the discussion. The young swimmer challenged two assumptions in the plan, and no one asked her to be grateful merely for being invited. It was the kind of public room Mo had wanted when she was eighteen herself. She went home exhausted, carrying no dramatic revelation, only three verified dates and one urgent request for a drain inspection. For that night, it was enough. The cracked tiles were reminiscent of the pool in Mo's first junior competition. Old rivalry between two clubs made joint planning harder than it needed to be. The lease proposal came under public scrutiny for the first time. The waiting room was full of suspense before the safety officer spoke. The chain's trademark appeared on advertisements before the public lease was approved.

傍晚，社區志工開始計算將失去平價運動場地的人數。他們整理不含姓名的出席資料，區分學校團體與付費課程，對未知數據一律標示，不自行捏造。沐禾看著嘉寧主持討論，年輕泳者質疑計畫中的兩個假設，沒人要求她只因獲邀參與就該感恩。這正是沐禾十八歲時渴望的公共空間。她疲憊地回家，沒有戲劇性的爆料，只有三個核實日期與一項緊急排水檢查申請。今晚，這已經足夠。 龜裂磁磚讓沐禾想起初次青少年比賽的泳池。 兩個俱樂部多年的競爭讓共同規劃更加困難。 租約提案第一次受到公開檢視。 安全官發言前，等候室充滿懸念。 租約尚未核准，連鎖企業商標卻已出現在廣告上。
