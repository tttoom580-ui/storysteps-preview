志工也有工作時間

Volunteers Have Working Hours

水道不為誰停留 · 第 7 / 25 章 · 5000–6000 · 英中對照 · 校訂中

The pilot's second week exposed a problem that no water test could detect. Young assistants had been listed as volunteers while completing duties that the pool required for paid operation. Some had worked until closing, then returned before dawn to prepare the lanes. Ying raised the issue with the municipal labor office; Mo supplied attendance records but removed personal details until the workers consented to being identified. Wei said many had offered to help out of loyalty. One nineteen-year-old replied that willingness to learn was not agreement to work without wages. The officer ordered the operator to review all shifts and explain how hours would be paid or reduced. The bulky cleaning machine could not pass through the narrow storage doorway. The labor officer would deem required opening duties paid work regardless of their label. Ying warned that unpaid obedience could resemble a small workplace dictatorship. Jia watched a novice swimmer glide across the shallow water with new confidence. Children sold handicraft bookmarks to raise money for accessible signage.

試辦第二週暴露了水質檢測找不到的問題。年輕助理被列為志工，實際卻負責泳池營運不可缺的付費工作。有些人做到打烊，隔天黎明前又回來準備水道。宋穎向市府勞工單位反映，沐禾提供出勤紀錄，在員工同意公開身分前先移除個資。魏昊說很多人是出於忠誠而自願幫忙。一名十九歲助理回答，願意學習不等於同意不領薪。勞工官要求營運方檢查全部班表，說明如何付薪或減少工時。 笨重的清潔機器無法通過狹窄的儲藏室門。 勞工官會將營運必要工作認定為有薪勞動，不受名稱影響。 宋穎警告，無償服從可能讓職場變成小型獨裁。 嘉寧看著新手泳者更有信心地滑過淺水面。 孩子販售手作書籤，為無障礙標誌募款。

Mo found that she had benefited from the arrangement, too. She had accepted an extra pair of hands during crowded lessons without asking whether those hands belonged to a paid employee. In the public meeting she stated that omission and offered to amend her pilot report. Her admission was not welcomed by everyone. A councilor claimed it proved she was unfit to lead. Jia asked whether admitting a mistake was worse than concealing one. The room divided. Afterward Mo sat alone in the empty spectators' gallery, remembering how old coaches had demanded loyalty without showing their own decisions. She would not copy that pattern in the name of a good cause. An interpreter helped visiting residents who did not understand the meeting language. A lesbian coach asked that the staff policy protect partners of every gender. One worker described getting paid on time as a practical form of liberation. The council's lofty promises meant little without a funded staff roster. Mo taught the new assistants how to navigate the building without blocking exit routes.

沐禾發現自己也從這種安排中受益。課堂擁擠時她接受額外人手，卻沒有問對方是否為有薪員工。公開會議上，她承認疏忽並主動修正試辦報告。不是每個人都歡迎她坦白。一名議員說這證明她不適合領導。嘉寧反問，承認錯誤難道比隱瞞更糟？會場意見分裂。散會後，沐禾獨自坐在空觀眾席，想起往日教練要求忠誠，卻從不公開自己的決策。她不願以善意之名重演那套方式。 口譯員協助不熟悉會議語言的外地居民。 一名女同性戀教練要求員工政策保障不同性別的伴侶。 一名員工說，準時領薪就是一種實際的解放。 沒有經費支撐的排班表，議會崇高承諾便毫無意義。 沐禾教新助理如何在館內通行而不堵住逃生動線。

The revised roster cost money. To meet it, Mo canceled a promotional event and reduced her own consulting hours, leaving time for trained staff to carry out the work. She explained the trade-off to swimmers who had hoped to meet a famous athlete. An eleven-year-old asked if the cancelled visit meant nobody thought their little pool mattered. Mo knelt beside the dry lane marker and said the workers mattered more than a photograph with a champion. She asked the child to help paint a notice thanking the lifeguards by name. It became the most widely shared picture of the week, for reasons no publicity manager could have planned. The committee prohibited overwork even when volunteers insisted they felt energetic. A pebble caught in the entrance mat created a small but preventable hazard. The assistant's wage claim remained pending until the payroll records were reviewed. A plantation owner offered a donation but asked for no voting privilege. The gardener used a small plow to clear soil beside the outdoor drain.

新班表增加了成本。為了支應，沐禾取消宣傳活動，減少自己的顧問時數，把工作留給受訓員工。她向期待見到知名運動員的泳客解釋取捨。一名十一歲孩子問，取消訪問是否代表沒人重視這座小泳池。沐禾蹲在乾水道標線旁說，工作人員比與冠軍合照更重要。她請孩子幫忙畫張公告，逐一感謝救生員。這張照片反而成了當週最廣為分享的畫面，原因是任何公關都無法預先設計的。 即使志工自認精神充沛，委員會仍禁止過度工作。 入口地墊卡住一顆小石子，形成可預防的危險。 助理的工資申請仍待薪資紀錄審核。 一位種植園主提供捐款，沒有要求表決特權。 園丁用小犁清理戶外排水溝旁的泥土。

Eli arrived after his site visit and listened as Mo described the criticism. He offered practical help reviewing the new duty descriptions, but she declined the idea that he should solve her conflict. Instead she asked him to show the staff how his drawings recorded labor requirements: cleaning time, equipment movement, assisted evacuation. He agreed to prepare a workshop open to everyone. At the end of the evening, Mo took his hand for a moment. 'This does not mean I have decided everything,' she said. 'I know,' he answered. They stood without further explanation, allowing the gesture to mean exactly what it was. A retired athlete refused royalty payments for the use of her old training photograph. A fair contract could spur staff to report mistakes early rather than conceal them. Mo took a short stroll with Ying after they had settled their disagreement. The summer sun could tan exposed shoulders during the outdoor water-safety classes. Ying refused to call missing wages a trifle, however small the amount.

之庭結束工地訪查後來聽沐禾說起批評。他提出協助審閱新職務說明，沐禾卻拒絕讓他替自己解決衝突。她改請他向員工示範，如何把清潔時間、器材移動及協助撤離等勞動需求標在圖面上。他答應舉辦所有人都可參加的工作坊。那晚結束時，沐禾短暫握住他的手。「這不代表我已經決定好一切。」她說。「我知道。」他答。兩人沒有再解釋，讓那個動作只代表它本身的意思。 一名退役運動員拒收舊訓練照片的授權金。 公平契約能鼓勵員工及早通報錯誤，而非隱瞞。 沐禾與宋穎化解分歧後，出門散了一小段步。 夏日陽光可能使戶外水域安全課的裸露肩膀曬黑。 宋穎拒絕把欠薪稱作小事，不管金額多小。
