不能跨越的界線

A Line That Cannot Be Crossed

水道不為誰停留 · 第 2 / 25 章 · 5000–6000 · 英中對照

用互動版查詞、朗讀與收藏

The city granted Mo and Ying a forty-eight-hour examination slot, but only in the dry sections of the building. A private contractor insisted that the water system was operational because a green lamp glowed on its control cabinet. Mo asked whether the lamp had been tested under load. No one could produce the result. Meanwhile, the adult beginners' class stood outside with wet towels in their bags, disappointed at another cancellation. Mo explained that a temporary closure of the deep lane protected them better than a reassuring photograph. Her words earned no applause. They did earn a second inspection, witnessed by a municipal technician who did not work for the future tenant. Every contractor had to abide by the same electrical isolation rules. Eli asked the technician to affirm which outlet had actually been repaired. An alligator mascot on the old lesson chart reminded children to keep their distance. Deep bass notes from a school choir reached the inspection team through the windows. Jia rewrote the warning in colloquial language so more residents could understand it.

市府准許沐禾和宋穎在四十八小時內檢查，但範圍只限乾燥區域。私人承包商堅稱水循環設備正常，理由是控制箱亮著綠燈。沐禾問燈號是否在實際負載下測試過，卻沒人能拿出結果。成人初學班帶著泳巾站在門外，再次停課讓大家失望。沐禾解釋，暫時關閉深水道，比拍一張讓人安心的照片更能保護他們。她沒有得到掌聲，卻爭取到第二次檢查，並由不隸屬未來承租者的市府技師見證。 所有承包商都必須遵守相同的斷電隔離規範。 之庭請技師確認究竟修好了哪個插座。 舊課程圖表上的鱷魚吉祥物提醒孩子保持距離。 學校合唱團的低音透過窗戶傳進檢查隊耳中。 嘉寧將警告改寫成口語，讓更多居民看得懂。

Eli came with revised diagrams, and the group compared them with the real building. One staircase was labeled as a secondary exit even though its door opened onto an enclosed storage yard. The contractor called it an insignificant discrepancy. Jia asked him how a person using a chair was supposed to negotiate those steps during smoke or a power failure. He looked at Eli rather than answering her. Eli turned the question back: 'Jia is the user. Please answer Jia.' Mo noticed that changed behavior more than his earlier apology. It did not erase the past, but it altered what could happen next. She wrote the discrepancy in the public log and insisted that neither company nor city remove the entry. Minor scheduling disputes were commonplace, but a locked emergency exit was not. The temporary testing permit would expire unless the city received the second report. A burned fuse explained the dark control panel but not the missing maintenance signature. The school offered hospitality to stranded visiting inspectors without requesting favors. Ying's infectious laughter briefly softened the tense discussion about access.

之庭帶來修訂圖面，大家將圖面和實際建築逐項比較。有一道樓梯被標為第二逃生出口，門外卻是封閉的儲物院。承包商稱這只是無關緊要的小差異。嘉寧問他，停電或失火時，輪椅使用者該如何走那些階梯。對方看向之庭，沒有回答嘉寧。之庭把問題轉回去：「使用者是嘉寧，請回答嘉寧。」沐禾比起他的道歉，更注意這個行為上的改變。它無法抹去過去，卻改變了接下來可能發生的事。她把差異記進公開紀錄，要求公司與市府都不得刪除。 小型排班爭議很常見，但鎖住緊急出口絕非小事。 若市府收不到第二份報告，臨時測試許可便會到期。 燒壞的保險絲能解釋控制板為何不亮，卻解釋不了缺失的維修簽名。 學校款待滯留的外地檢查員，沒有要求任何回報。 宋穎富有感染力的笑聲讓緊張的無障礙討論稍微緩和。

At a meeting in the neighboring school gym, residents argued about whether public money should support a place that some rarely used. Mo let the uncomfortable question stand. She showed the facility's actual annual membership figures, but admitted that the figures excluded free rehabilitation hours and school sessions. A parent asked whether a better way of counting could be invented. Ying offered to bring attendance sheets from three years of programs, provided personal names were removed. Mo accepted the condition. A useful figure would explain need without making vulnerable participants surrender their privacy. The room quieted when Jia announced she would help design the access survey herself. The technician used one liter of clean water in a separate demonstration tank. A novice volunteer was assigned to write labels, never to touch live wiring. Mo said it would be rash to reopen the deep lane after one promising test. The accountant tried to reconcile two invoices bearing different dates. The shabby map had not been updated since the last renovation.

在隔壁學校的體育館會議上，居民爭論公帑是否該支持部分人很少使用的設施。沐禾沒有迴避難題。她展示泳池年度會員數，並承認數字漏掉免費復健時段與學校課程。有家長問能否改用更好的計算方式。宋穎提出可提供三年出席紀錄，條件是移除個人姓名。沐禾同意了。有用的數字應能說明需求，而不迫使處境脆弱的參與者放棄隱私。嘉寧宣布要親自參與無障礙調查設計，會場這才安靜下來。 技師在獨立的示範槽中使用了一公升清水。 新手志工只能負責寫標籤，不得碰觸通電線路。 沐禾說僅憑一次看似成功的測試便重開深水道太草率。 會計試圖核對日期不同的兩張發票。 破舊地圖自上次整修後就沒有更新。

Before leaving, Eli asked whether Mo could review his accessible changing-room plan. She said yes, as a paid consultant under an agreement that would also let Jia and Ying review it. He smiled at the insistence on a contract. 'I used to think agreement meant everyone was silent,' he said. Mo folded her notes. 'Silence is sometimes just a sign that nobody has been given room to disagree.' They walked to the bus stop together without touching. It was early enough for either to change their mind, and Mo found the simple freedom of that distance unexpectedly comforting. A borrowed spotlight made the presentation easier to see but obscured an exit sign. A volunteer brought ginger syrup for workers who had spent the afternoon in the cold. The handrail began to tilt when the tester leaned against its outer edge. At twilight the team photographed the door before sealing the inspection area. An adult donor offered whiskey at dinner, but the workers refused alcohol during their shift.

離開前，之庭問沐禾能否審閱無障礙更衣室設計。她答應，但必須依付費顧問契約進行，也讓嘉寧和宋穎共同審閱。他對她堅持簽約露出微笑。「我以前以為大家都安靜，就代表同意了。」他說。沐禾收好筆記：「沉默有時只是因為沒人被允許反對。」兩人一起走到公車站，沒有碰觸。彼此都還能改變心意，而這段簡單的距離讓沐禾意外安心。 借來的聚光燈讓簡報更清楚，卻遮住了逃生標誌。 志工帶來薑糖漿，慰勞在寒風中工作一下午的人。 測試者靠在外側時，扶手開始傾斜。 黃昏時，團隊先拍攝門況，再封閉檢查區。 一名成年捐助者在晚餐時提供威士忌，工作人員值班期間婉拒飲酒。
