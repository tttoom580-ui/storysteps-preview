重複的課程

A Lesson in Repetition

無罪辯護：消失的第七位證人 · 第 6 / 25 章 · 4000–5000 · 英中對照 · 校訂中

The interview contractor called its program "Clear Voices," a genre of public training that mixed emergency education with speaking practice. Its agenda included a bizarre metaphor about a baseball inning: all witnesses should "take their turn at the plate" but repeat the same example. The municipal office had paid for the course. Lin asked for the invoices rather than treating the trainer's religious theology or political nominee as relevant. A rigid administrator warned her to beware of a rebellion among angry witnesses if she questioned their honesty. She replied that the court needed the origin of a sentence, not a campaign against six ordinary citizens.

訪談承包商把課程稱為「清楚發聲」，是一種把緊急教育與口語練習混合的公共訓練類型。議程有個奇怪的棒球局數比喻：每個證人都要「輪到上場打擊」，卻要重複同一個範例。市府已為課程付款。林悠要求查看發票，而不是把講師的神學信仰或政治提名當作案情。僵化的行政主管警告她，若質疑證人誠實，可能激起憤怒證人的反抗。林悠回答，法院要尋找句子來源，不是向六位普通市民發動運動。

The trainer's notebook displayed a drawing of a skeleton, a poisonous organism in a biology lesson, and a recipe using vinegar to clean a stain. Another lesson said to shield frightened witnesses from hostile questions. In a corner someone had sketched a broken rib and written "do not cling to a first memory." An assistant had begun to sob when she realized the model sentence was not labeled fictional. Zeng read those pages with care. They were evidence of poor training, possibly toxic pressure, but not evidence that the instructor had planned murder. The word "witness" was being used for both classroom students and people in an active criminal case.

講師筆記本裡有骷髏圖、用在生物課的有毒生物圖示，以及使用醋清潔污漬的配方。另一堂課教人保護受驚證人，避免敵意問題。角落有人畫著一根斷肋骨，寫下「別緊抓第一次記憶」。助理發現示範句沒有標明是虛構案例時，竟開始啜泣。曾序小心地讀著這些頁面。它們能證明訓練品質差，甚至可能有有害壓力，卻不能證明講師策畫了謀殺。「證人」一詞同時指課堂學員及現實刑案中的證人，兩種意義被混在一起。

A federal training handbook appeared in the contractor's reading list, although the city itself used a different emergency procedure. The handbook taught instructors to distinguish invented classroom examples from real witness statements. Zeng wondered why the company had ignored such a basic warning. The instructor replied that his assistants had been rushing to meet a deadline. He offered to show them the lesson files, but only through the court's supervised process. Lin accepted. An explanation of carelessness might be true, partly true, or an excuse; they needed a record that could test it. She asked the judge to preserve the original lesson plan and its revision history.

承包商閱讀清單上有一本聯邦訓練手冊，雖然本市使用的緊急程序不同。手冊教講師區分虛構課堂例句與真實證人陳述。曾序不明白公司為何忽略如此基本的警告。講師回答，助理為趕截止日而倉促工作。他願意提供教材，但必須經法院監督程序。林悠接受。疏忽的解釋可能真實、部分真實，也可能是藉口；需要能夠驗證的紀錄。她請法官保全原始教案與修訂歷程。

The records showed an unusual timetable. Ke's alleged bridge crossing was said to occur at nine seventeen on Monday. The first training slide containing that exact line had been uploaded on Tuesday morning. Yet six interviews were officially timestamped late Monday night. Zeng wanted to overtake the clerk's review and announce fraud. Lin stopped him. They needed to determine whether timestamps referred to interviews, transcription, or later file imports. She asked the court to preserve every version, including the file that the contractor had quietly replaced. The judge granted the request and required a written explanation from the trainer.

紀錄呈現一份不尋常的時程。柯駿被指在星期一九點十七分過橋；含有那句完全相同文字的第一張訓練投影片，卻是星期二早晨才上傳。六份訪談的官方時間戳記都在星期一深夜。曾序想跳過書記員審核，直接宣布有造假。林悠阻止他：必須先判定時間戳記代表訪談、逐字稿製作，還是之後匯入系統。她請法院保全所有版本，包括承包商悄悄替換的檔案。法官准許，並要求講師提出書面說明。

During a supervised interview, the instructor admitted that the exercise had borrowed language from an incident description sent by City Relief. He said he believed the description was anonymous and fictional. He could not identify the author of the source email because the account was shared. Lin requested the sender logs and asked the instructor not to guess. He looked relieved by that instruction. Nobody had previously told him it was acceptable to answer, "I don't know." Outside the room, Zeng noticed how pressure could move in both directions: a witness might repeat what the authorities wanted to hear, while an investigator might prefer a dramatic accusation over an incomplete truth.

在受監督訪談裡，講師承認練習借用了救濟處送來的事件描述。他說自己以為那是匿名、虛構的故事。由於寄件帳號共用，他無法辨認來源郵件作者。林悠要求寄件日誌，請講師不要猜。他聽了反而鬆口氣，因為先前沒有人告訴他，「我不知道」也可以是答案。走出房間時，曾序注意到壓力可能來自雙方：證人可能重複當局想聽的話，調查員也可能寧可聽到戲劇性指控，而非不完整的真實。

That evening, Lin and Zeng went to eat noodles across from the courthouse. They sat without speaking until a woman from the training company approached and placed a business card on the table. "I arranged the room, not the script," she whispered. "If you want to understand the script, find the person who made the captions." Before Lin could ask a question, the woman left. Zeng wanted to follow. Lin shook her head. Voluntary contact was one thing; frightening a possible witness in the street would repeat the very pressure they were trying to expose.

傍晚，林悠與曾序到法院對面吃麵，沉默地坐著。一名訓練公司女員工走來，把名片放到桌上。「我安排教室，不負責腳本，」她低聲說，「想知道腳本來源，就去找做字幕的人。」林悠還來不及問，她便離開了。曾序想追上去，林悠搖頭。自願聯繫是一回事，在街頭嚇到可能的證人，卻是在複製他們正努力揭露的壓迫。
