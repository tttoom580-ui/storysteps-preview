The next morning, a local channel aired an episode about the murder, placing Ke's photograph beside the word "greed." Its host claimed that only a guilty man would hire a defense lawyer. Lin refused to answer an exaggeration with another exaggeration. A constitutional right to counsel was not a confession; neither a democrat's speech nor a party's incentive could change that principle. Ke's family lived above a retail shop that had struggled through the recession. Reporters filmed the door while a small fleet of vans blocked the road, making his daughter afraid to leave for school.

隔天早上，地方電視臺播出一集謀殺專題，把柯駿的照片放在「貪婪」兩字旁。主持人宣稱只有有罪的人才會聘請辯護律師。林悠拒絕用另一個誇張說法回擊。獲得辯護是憲制權利，不是認罪；無論民主派人士如何演說、政黨如何提供誘因，都不能改變這個原則。柯駿家住在一家零售小店樓上，經濟衰退時生意艱難。記者把門口拍個不停，一小隊採訪車還堵住道路，嚇得他的女兒不敢上學。

A charity kitchen provided a massive pot of rice and herb soup to families outside the courthouse. Its volunteers included a peasant farmer whose fields had been taken for the project. He said the land deal had crossed a threshold of fairness years ago, when compensation offers were made without accessible documents. The anger was widespread and substantial, but Lin would not recruit it as a tactic to smash the prosecution's reputation. She wanted a verdict based on admissible facts, not a contest over which crowd could shout loudest. Even a correct conclusion reached through intimidation would leave another injustice behind.

一家慈善廚房在法院外供應一大鍋飯和香草湯給家屬，志工之中有一位耕地被開發案徵收的農民。他說數年前的土地交易便越過公平底線：補償開價時，居民根本拿不到易讀的文件。憤怒廣泛而強烈，但林悠不會把它招募成摧毀檢方名聲的手段。她要的是根據可採證事實作出的判決，而非比賽哪一群人叫得更大聲。即使以威嚇得到正確結論，也只會留下另一種不公。

Ke's daughter brought a toy train to the family meeting room. When a delivery truck passed, its wheels began to rattle against the windowsill. The girl said she disliked the noise because it sounded like the night police took her father away. Lin asked permission before speaking to her, then kept the conversation short. She did not ask the child to become a witness or to remember details she could not know. Afterward, Lin explained to Ke that the public campaign around his case might continue even if the defense obtained favorable evidence. They would need a separate plan to protect his family's privacy and his daughter's school routine.

柯駿女兒把玩具火車帶進家庭會面室。送貨卡車經過時，車輪在窗臺上喀啦作響。女孩說她討厭那個聲音，因為它讓她想起警察帶走父親的夜晚。林悠徵求同意後才跟她說話，並保持簡短；她沒有要孩子當證人，也沒有逼她回想不可能知道的細節。之後林悠向柯駿解釋，即使辯方取得有利證據，外界輿論仍可能持續。他們需要另訂計畫，保護家人隱私與女兒正常上學。

Inside the court, a clerk showed Lin an official notice stating that all six witnesses had been "independently interviewed." That phrase mattered. If the interviews occurred separately, why had the resulting sentences become identical? Prosecutor Zhou agreed to seek the original interview schedule and the name of the trainer who had arranged the sessions. She would not admit wrongdoing without proof. Lin would not demand that she do so. For the first time the two women worked on the same narrow task from opposite sides of the courtroom, each aware that a careless disclosure could damage vulnerable people.

法院內，一名書記員讓林悠看一張正式通知，宣稱六名證人「均接受獨立訪談」。這個詞很關鍵。如果訪談彼此分開，為何最後出現完全相同的句子？周敏同意尋找原始訪談時程及安排訓練的人員姓名。她不會在沒有證據時承認違失，林悠也不要求她如此。兩名女性第一次從法庭的對立兩側處理同一項狹窄任務，都知道草率揭露可能傷害脆弱的當事人。

At the office, Zeng read the training sheet again and found that its print code matched an ordinary educational order. He had hoped it would immediately reveal a secret mastermind, but printing records rarely worked that way. Lin asked him to trace the order through receiving, editing, and distribution rather than leap from the code to a person's guilt. They agreed to divide the work: he would check the physical paper and she would request system logs. Their disagreement over speed had become a useful division of labor. Both knew that a single false allegation could undo the trust they were beginning to earn.

回到辦公室，曾序再讀訓練單，發現印刷代碼與一般教育訂單一致。他原本希望它立刻揭露幕後黑手，但印刷紀錄很少如此簡單。林悠請他沿著收件、編輯、發放程序追查，不要從代碼直接跳到個人有罪。他們同意分工：曾序檢查紙本，她申請系統日誌。兩人對速度的分歧，反而成為有用的工作分配。他們都知道，一次無根據指控就可能摧毀剛建立的信任。

In a sealed envelope later that day, the court received a photocopy of a training agenda. It included an instruction to "repeat the model sentence as written." The sender had removed the page identifying the instructor. Lin felt triumph for an instant, then checked herself. A photocopy without a source could expose a lead, not settle a trial. She asked the judge for a preservation order before the interview office could destroy its remaining material. Outside, Ke's daughter waited beside a shop window where a toy boat shook whenever traffic passed. Lin told the girl only what she could truthfully say: the defense had found a question worth asking.

當天稍晚，法院收到一封密封信，裡面是一份訓練議程影本。其中一項指示寫著：「依照文字重複示範句。」寄件人拿掉了標出講師的那一頁。林悠一瞬間很想歡呼，接著提醒自己：來源不明的影本能提供線索，不能直接決定審判。她請法官下令保全訪談辦公室尚存的資料。門外，柯駿的女兒站在商店櫥窗旁，玩具船隨著車流經過而輕輕震動。林悠只對孩子說自己能確定的話：辯方找到了一個值得追問的問題。
