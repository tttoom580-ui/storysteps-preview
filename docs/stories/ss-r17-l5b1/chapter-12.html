無罪辯護：消失的第七位證人 · 第 12 / 25 章 · 4000–5000 · 英中對照 · 校訂中

A celebrity commentator arrived before dawn, turning the courthouse steps into an arena. The judge issued a ban on broadcasting witnesses' private addresses but allowed reporters to discuss public filings. Lin opened with a simple distinction: the prosecution had to prove criminal intent, not merely a suspicious presence. The city's fraud inquiry and a pending civil lawsuit might reveal financial wrongdoing, but neither replaced proof in this case. Ke sat with a thin portfolio of repair certificates. His daughter had brought him a paper crystal, a small gift he kept beneath the table. He looked depressed yet alert when the clerk called his name.

一名名人評論員天未亮便到場，使法院台階像競技場一樣熱鬧。法官禁止播出證人的私人住址，但容許記者討論公開文件。林悠開場先區分一件簡單的事：檢方必須證明犯罪意圖，不能只證明可疑的在場。市府詐欺調查及待審民事訴訟或許揭露財務違失，都不能取代本案證明。柯駿坐在座位上，帶著薄薄一冊維修證書。女兒送他一顆紙折水晶，他放在桌下。他看來沮喪，但書記員叫名時仍保持清醒。

Witness One, a bus dispatcher, remembered seeing a red object through a residential window. The prosecutor asked an open question, and the witness described a rack of coats in the entry hall. Only after seeing the training transcript did he use the phrase about a red case. He admitted that the word "crossed" had not been his own. Lin did not poke fun at him or shove him toward a conclusion. She asked whether his memory came from the night itself or a later session. "A little of both," he said. The answer was uncomfortable, but the judge allowed it to stand without forcing an ultimate interpretation.

第一名證人是公車調度員。他記得透過住宅窗戶看見紅色物品。檢察官先提出開放式問題，證人說到玄關衣架。直到他看見訓練逐字稿，才用了紅箱那句話。他承認「走過」不是自己原本說的。林悠沒有取笑他，也沒有強推結論，只問記憶來自當晚還是後來的課程。「兩者都有一點，」他說。答案令人不自在，法官卻容許保留，不強迫它變成最後定論。

Before Witness Three entered, Judge Tang explained that cross-examination was not a contest in humiliation. Questions must focus on what a witness observed, remembered, or later learned. Lin listened carefully, since her first drafts contained several questions that assumed the answer. She rewrote them while Zeng watched from the back row. The new sequence began with the witness's location, proceeded to the lighting, and ended with the source of the disputed sentence. It was slower but fairer. The prosecutor nodded once when Lin removed a question that had described the witness as "careless" before she had even spoken.

第三名證人進庭前，唐法官說明交叉詰問不是羞辱比賽。問題應集中在證人觀察、記得或後來學到的內容。林悠仔細聽，因為她初稿裡有幾題先假定了答案。她在曾序從後排看著時重寫問題。新順序先問證人位置，再問照明，最後問爭議句子來源。這樣比較慢，卻更公平。林悠刪掉一題在證人開口前便稱她「粗心」的問題時，周敏微微點頭。

Witness Two worked in a nutrition clinic and carried a prescription log with entries in plural columns, one for each patient's session. She had been taking medicine to a colleague when the lights failed. Her own notes put her at the clinic at nine twelve, several streets away from the bridge. She could not explain the words in her police statement. Lin requested permission to reverse the order of questioning so the witness could first read her notes without seeing the prepared sentence. The judge approved a controlled procedure. The prosecutor objected to one random page that lacked a date, and Lin accepted the objection.

第二名證人在營養診所工作，帶來一份處方登記簿，表格用複數欄位記錄每位病人的每次看診。停電時她正在替同事送藥。她自己的筆記把她放在九點十二分的診所，距離人行橋好幾條街。她無法解釋警詢筆錄中的措辭。林悠請求調換提問順序，讓證人先讀自己的筆記，別先看到示範句。法官批准受控程序。檢方反對一頁沒有日期的隨機文件，林悠接受異議。

At lunch, a court volunteer offered water to both families waiting in separate rooms. Fang's brother thanked the volunteer but refused media interviews. Ke's wife asked whether the court could arrange a private entrance for their daughter the next day. The clerk agreed to check the rules. Lin found it unexpectedly moving that two families affected by the same death could ask for the same ordinary courtesy without sharing the same opinion about the case. The law could not erase grief or fear. It could, at least, stop making those feelings into public entertainment while the evidence was examined.

午餐時，法院志工分別向兩間等候室的家屬送水。方芮弟弟道謝，但拒絕媒體訪談。柯駿妻子則問隔天能否為女兒安排私人入口，書記員答應查規定。林悠意外感動：同一死亡事件影響的兩個家庭，即使對案件看法不同，也能要求相同的基本尊重。法律無法抹除悲傷或恐懼，至少能在查證期間，不把這些情緒變成公眾娛樂。

By afternoon the courtroom was quiet. No surprise confession had arrived, no dramatic witness had burst through the doors. Instead, two people had separated their direct memories from words learned afterward. It was less satisfying to spectators than a sudden reversal, and more valuable to the case. Ke whispered that he had feared nobody would listen to ordinary details. Lin told him that the ordinary details were precisely why they were there. At recess the judge ordered the original training recordings played at the next session, with technical witnesses available to answer questions from both sides.

午後，法庭逐漸安靜。沒有人突然認罪，也沒有戲劇性的證人破門而入。只有兩個人把親身記憶與後來學到的句子分開。對旁觀者來說，這比不上突然逆轉精彩，對案件卻更有價值。柯駿低聲說，他本來怕沒人會聽平凡細節。林悠告訴他，平凡細節正是他們來這裡的原因。休庭時，法官命令下次開庭播放原始訓練錄音，雙方都可向技術證人發問。
