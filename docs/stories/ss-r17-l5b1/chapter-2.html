漲水的人行橋

The Bridge at High Water

無罪辯護：消失的第七位證人 · 第 2 / 25 章 · 4000–5000 · 英中對照 · 校訂中

用互動版查詞、朗讀與收藏

Rain drove Lin and Zeng toward the river before dawn. Their destination was the blue footbridge, a narrow crossing between the archive annex and the municipal ridge road. A corporate contractor had installed a sensor there after the last epidemic had disrupted emergency transport. The contractor promised that its device would be beneficial during floods, yet maintenance logs showed no reliable reading on the night of Fang's death. Lin asked a technician to convert the old handwritten gauge readings into a timeline. He warned that the conversion could be useful without being extraordinary proof of any individual's movements.

天未亮，雨便催著林悠與曾序前往河邊。他們的目的地是藍色人行橋，一道連接檔案館附樓與市府山脊道路的狹窄通路。前次疫情打亂緊急運送時，一家企業承包商曾在此安裝感測器。廠商承諾設備在洪水期間會帶來助益，但維護紀錄顯示，方芮死亡當晚並沒有可靠讀數。林悠請技師將舊式手寫水尺數值換算成時間線。技師提醒她，換算可能有幫助，卻不能神奇地證明某個人的行蹤。

To reach the bank, they helped a road worker haul a heavy barrier aside. Water threatened to plunge into the walkway. A torch revealed marks that the current had left above the lowest rail, while the technician used a spare sensor to demonstrate its limits. One survey respondent remembered that the bridge had been closed before nine, but his reminder came from an errand to buy stew for his father, not from a clock. He was vocal about what he did not know. Lin appreciated the honesty more than a dramatic claim. There was a temptation to treat every high-water mark as a timestamp; they resisted it.

為了到達岸邊，他們幫道路工人拖開沉重的柵欄。水流幾乎要猛衝上步道。手電筒照出水流在最低欄杆上方留下的痕跡，技師則用備用感測器說明設備的限制。一名受訪者記得橋在九點前已封閉，但他的記憶來自替父親跑腿買燉菜的經過，而不是看了時鐘。他坦率地說明自己不知道什麼。比起戲劇性的斷言，林悠更重視這份誠實。大家很容易把每一道高水痕都當成時間標記；他們克制了這種誘惑。

At the road office, a mechanic showed them how the river could force a rapid transformation of the crossing. The crew had to trim vegetation along the approach so that warning lamps stayed visible, but they were forbidden to cut the reeds that stabilized the bank. Lin checked the work order against photographs made that same afternoon. One image contained a date written on a chalkboard; the date referred to the inspection, not the night of the murder. She asked the mechanic to explain that distinction in writing. The case had already suffered from confusing the day a record was made with the day an event occurred.

道路辦公室裡，技師向兩人說明河水如何迅速改變橋梁通行狀態。工班需要修剪入口植被，確保警告燈可見，但不得砍掉穩固河岸的蘆葦。林悠把工單與同日下午拍的照片核對。一張照片的黑板寫著日期，卻是檢查日，不是命案當夜。她請技師把差別寫清楚。這宗案件已經因混淆「紀錄製作日」與「事件發生日」付出代價。

The technician trimmed a copy of the diagram so that the bridge entrance, warning sign, and drainage channel fitted on one page. A small change in the water level could transform a safe passage into a lethal one, but the site's legal liability would depend on the precise sequence of warnings. Lin requested the original closure order. The first copy she received carried a signature without a recorded time. The second showed an electronic entry at eight forty-six. Neither established who, if anyone, was on the bridge at nine seventeen. Zeng wrote both limitations in his notebook rather than hiding them from the defense file.

技師裁整一張示意圖，讓橋口、警告標誌和排水溝都能印在同一頁。水位只要微微變動，就可能使安全通道變成致命險境，但法律責任仍須依警告的確切先後判定。林悠申請調閱原始封橋命令。第一份副本有簽名卻沒有時間，第二份電子紀錄顯示八點四十六分。兩份資料都不能證明九點十七分是否有人在橋上。曾序把這些限制同樣寫進筆記，而不是從辯方卷宗中藏起來。

Zeng returned to the bridge at the hour specified in the witness accounts. They had permission from the maintenance supervisor and stayed behind the barriers. Cars passed on the ridge road, their lights flashing across the water, while the footbridge itself remained dark. Lin realized how easily a viewer on the far bank might mistake the road for the bridge. She drew both lines separately on a map and marked the observation points of the six witnesses. If two people had stood in different places, their apparent agreement deserved testing, not admiration. By the time they left, the rain had stopped, but the bridge still could not be crossed.

曾序在證詞所指的時段再訪橋邊。他們已得到維護主管許可，始終待在柵欄後方。車輛沿山脊公路通行，車燈映在水面，人行橋本身卻一片黑暗。林悠發現，對岸觀察者很可能把公路誤認為橋。她在圖上分別畫出兩條路線，標記六位證人的觀察位置。如果兩個人站在不同地方，表面一致更應檢驗，而非讚嘆。離開時雨停了，橋依舊無法通行。

When they returned, prosecutor Zhou Min met Lin near the courthouse steps. Zhou had also requested the raw closure data, a fact Lin had not expected. "If the bridge was shut, I need to know," Zhou said. Lin answered that a closed bridge did not establish an alibi by itself; people sometimes ignored barriers. For the first time, they agreed on a narrow question to test, although their roles remained opposed. Back at her desk, Lin noticed that the city had purchased a replacement camera the day after the blackout. On its receipt, beside the serial number, someone had written the initials W7.

兩人返回法院時，檢察官周敏在台階附近遇見林悠。周敏也申請了原始封橋資料，這令林悠意外。「如果橋封了，我必須知道，」周敏說。林悠回答，封橋本身不能證明不在場，因為有人仍可能越過柵欄。兩人第一次就一個需要檢驗的狹窄問題達成共識，雖然各自的法庭立場仍然對立。回到辦公桌前，林悠注意到市府在停電隔天買了一臺替換攝影機。收據上的序號旁，有人寫了 W7。
