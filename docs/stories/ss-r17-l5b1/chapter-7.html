The Red Case Inventory

無罪辯護：消失的第七位證人 · 第 7 / 25 章 · 4000–5000 · 英中對照 · 校訂中

用互動版查詞、朗讀與收藏

A purchase record from the training company's acquisition department named six identical red document cases. Each came with a bonus stack of plastic sleeves and was delivered to a warehouse in the colonial quarter. The cases were spacious enough for recording devices, but the invoice said they held paper forms. Zeng, who had been a sophomore when the company first opened, knew one of its retired repairers. The man disapproved of their wasteful habit of throwing away equipment. He had kept a scrap of packaging showing the original serial numbers. Lin asked him to explain where he had found it before treating the numbers as evidence.

訓練公司的採購紀錄列出六只完全相同的紅色文件箱，每只附贈一疊塑膠護套，全部送到殖民時代舊城區的倉庫。箱子寬敞到能裝錄音設備，發票卻說裡面放的是紙表單。曾序在那家公司開幕時還是大二生，認識一名退休維修工。老人不贊成公司浪費器材、動不動就丟掉的習慣，因此留下了一小片印有原始序號的包裝紙。林悠先請他說明紙片來源，才考慮把序號當成證據。

An attached surveillance plan described the building as a symbolic public gateway. It specified where to stack supply boxes, how to reinforce a cracked spine of concrete, and which doors could be penetrated by service keys. The implication was clear: a person could carry a red case through the building without using the bridge. But the plan was old, and Lin would not claim that every passage remained open. A contractor measured one passage that afternoon. It was risky and narrow, with a damaged handrail, yet its lower exit faced the annex directly. This gave them another route to test, not another person to accuse.

附帶的監視規畫把建築形容成象徵性的公共入口。文件列出補給箱堆疊位置、如何加固有裂痕的混凝土主樑，以及哪些門能被勤務鑰匙打開。含意很清楚：有人可以提著紅箱穿過大樓，不必走人行橋。但圖是舊的，林悠不會宣稱所有通道仍能使用。承包商當天下午測量一條通道，發現路徑狹窄又危險，扶手也損壞，但下方出口正對檔案館附樓。這是另一條待測路線，不是另一名可以隨便指控的人。

A transit worker showed them a chunk of concrete removed from the warehouse wall during repairs. An old conduit had been built to penetrate the lower level, allowing cables to pass toward the annex. The worker did not disapprove of investigators entering the building; he simply insisted that everyone use protective gear and respect the structural limits. Lin asked for a safe inspection rather than a dramatic break-in. The team found a service hatch with a new lock, but its records showed the lock had been installed after Fang's death. That fact mattered more than the appearance of secrecy. It could not prove that the passage was open on the critical night.

一名運輸工人給他們看倉庫修繕拆下的一大塊混凝土。舊管道原本穿過下層牆體，使電纜可通往附樓。工人並不反對調查員進入建築，但堅持大家穿戴護具、尊重結構限制。林悠要求安全勘查，不要戲劇性的闖入。團隊找到一個加裝新鎖的勤務孔口，但紀錄顯示鎖是在方芮死後才裝的。這比神祕外觀重要得多，也不能因此證明關鍵夜晚通道必定開著。

A city council member who described himself as republican argued that workers should comprehend the importance of private ownership. At the same meeting, a volunteer spoke about calcium supplements for elderly residents and a backyard clinic that had recently closed. Lin listened because the case had become linked to questions of development, but she kept the debate separate from the murder allegation. A public meeting could reveal motive, not replace evidence. By evening the repairer produced a photograph of the six cases lined up under the workshop lights. Each one had the same bright strap and a numbered seal.

一名自稱共和派的市議員主張，工人應理解私有產權的重要。同一場會議裡，志工談到老年居民的鈣片需求，以及最近關門的後院診所。林悠聽著，因為案件已牽涉開發爭議，但她仍把政策辯論與殺人指控分開。公開會議能揭露動機，不能替代證據。傍晚，退休維修工找到一張照片：六只紅箱在工坊燈下排成一列，每只都有同樣鮮明的背帶與編號封條。

Zeng placed the photographs beside the original plans and marked which parts had been verified. One stairwell matched; another had been filled with cement; a third ended in a storage room. The warehouse manager offered to demonstrate a different path, but Lin delayed the visit until both parties could observe it. She had learned that even harmless reconstruction could create suspicion if only one side controlled the process. That evening Ke called to ask whether the red cases were proof that somebody had framed him. "They are proof the cases existed," Lin answered. "The rest is what we are still trying to discover."

曾序把照片放在原始設計圖旁，標出哪些部分已驗證。一處樓梯相符，一處已灌水泥，第三處通往儲藏室。倉庫經理願示範另一條路，林悠卻等到雙方能共同觀察才安排。她明白，即使無害的重建，只要由單方控制也可能引起懷疑。當晚柯駿打電話問，紅箱是否證明有人陷害他。「它們證明箱子存在，」林悠回答，「其餘才是我們仍在查的事。」

The sixth case was marked "training room," not "field kit." The other five bore ordinary equipment labels. Zeng wondered whether the prosecution had mistaken an object from a rehearsal for one carried at the crime scene. Lin answered that they still lacked proof anyone had seen a red case near the annex at all. She requested the distribution receipts and asked the police to photograph the remaining boxes in place. The repairer smiled at her caution. "I kept that paper because I hate waste," he said. "I didn't know it might keep a man from prison." Lin told him that deciding the man's fate would be the court's responsibility, not his.

第六只箱子標示「訓練室」，不是「外勤工具組」；其他五只貼著一般設備標籤。曾序懷疑檢方把排練使用的物件，誤當成犯罪現場的箱子。林悠回答，他們甚至還沒有證明真有人在附樓附近見到紅箱。她申請配送簽收單，並請警方在原處拍攝剩餘箱子的照片。退休維修工欣賞她的謹慎。「我留下那張紙是因為討厭浪費，」他說，「沒想到它可能救人免於坐牢。」林悠說，決定那個人命運的是法院，不是這張紙的主人。
