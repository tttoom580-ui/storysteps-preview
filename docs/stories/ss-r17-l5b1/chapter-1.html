The Sentence No One Remembered

無罪辯護：消失的第七位證人 · 第 1 / 25 章 · 4000–5000 · 英中對照 · 校訂中

用互動版查詞、朗讀與收藏

Six people told the police exactly the same thing: "At nine seventeen, Ke Jun crossed the blue footbridge with a red case." Not approximately the same thing. The same thirteen English words appeared in six signed statements, down to the unusual word "crossed." Twenty-eight-year-old defense lawyer Lin You read the sentence three times in the metropolitan courthouse. Her client, a maintenance electrician, had been charged with killing archivist Fang Rui during a September blackout. The prosecutor expected a quick conviction. Lin saw no certainty in the perfect agreement. Memories were rarely so tidy, especially on a night when the streetlights had failed.

六個人向警方說了完全相同的一句話：「九點十七分，柯駿提著紅色箱子走過藍色人行橋。」不是大致相同，而是六份簽名筆錄裡的十三個英文詞，連少見的「走過」一詞都一模一樣。二十八歲的辯護律師林悠在灣陵都會法院讀了三遍。她的當事人是維修電工柯駿，被控在九月停電夜殺害檔案員方芮。檢方預期很快定罪，林悠卻不認為這種整齊劃一能帶來確信。回憶很少如此工整，尤其是在街燈全滅的夜晚。

The scene photographs showed a cluster of people outside an old chapel, a portable generator, and a neglected riverside habitat whose reeds had begun to wither. Fang had been collecting evidence that a housing project threatened the district's heritage. At a public forum she had demanded that the city disclose every transaction involving compensation for displaced families. An immense developer had called her envious of metropolitan progress. Her supervisor replied that the city could make an optional provision for independent review. None of these disputes proved murder, but they explained why Fang had enemies and why witnesses might feel pressure to agree.

現場照片拍到一群人站在舊禮拜堂外，旁邊有一臺攜帶式發電機，河岸棲地因長期疏於照料，蘆葦開始枯萎。方芮生前蒐集資料，認為住宅開發案威脅當地的歷史資產。她在公開論壇要求市府揭露所有涉及搬遷補償的交易。一家規模龐大的開發商曾指責她嫉妒都會進步；她的主管則主張加入可供選擇的獨立審查條款。這些爭端無法證明謀殺，卻說明方芮可能與誰結怨，也說明證人為何可能感到必須一致表態的壓力。

Before their first application, Lin visited the courthouse archive counter to learn the rules for preserving evidence. The woman at the counter explained that public access and personal safety could coexist if requests were narrow. Lin had wanted the entire police database, but she changed the request to the seven-folder inventory and the original interview recordings. Zeng checked the dates against the charge sheet, finding that the witness count had changed after the first case conference. Neither lawyer nor investigator knew whether the change was deliberate. What they did know was that a numbered folder had to have a recorded history. They could ask for that history without inventing the contents of the folder.

提出第一份聲請前，林悠去法院檔案櫃臺了解證據保全規則。櫃臺女職員解釋，只要申請範圍夠明確，公開查閱與人身安全可以兼顧。林悠原本想要全部警方資料庫，後來縮小成七號資料夾清冊與訪談原始錄音。曾序把日期與起訴書對照，發現證人數目在第一次案情會議後改變。兩人都不知道改動是否蓄意，但知道有編號的資料夾理當有處理紀錄。他們可以索取這段紀錄，不必虛構資料夾裡的內容。

Lin's first impulse was to telephone every witness immediately. Her investigator, thirty-three-year-old Zeng Xu, stopped her before she could stumble into improper contact. "We request the original recordings first," he said. His insight was simple: a written statement did not tell them whether the witness had spoken freely, answered a leading question, or signed a prepared paragraph. Their client sat across the table with his hands folded. "I went to repair the annex lights," Ke said. "I never went onto that bridge." Lin did not promise to save him. She promised to check his account as carefully as she checked everyone else's.

林悠第一個衝動是立刻打電話給每位證人。三十三歲的調查員曾序攔住她，以免她貿然接觸不該私下聯絡的人。「先申請原始錄音，」他說。他的洞見很簡單：書面筆錄無法說明證人是否自由陳述、是否回答了誘導問題，或者是否簽下一段預先寫好的文字。柯駿雙手交握，坐在桌子另一端。「我去修檔案館的燈，」他說，「我根本沒有上那座橋。」林悠沒有保證救他，只承諾用檢查其他人說法的同一標準檢查他的說法。

The apartment where Lin worked was small enough that her evidence boxes occupied the kitchen table. She wrote the known facts on white cards and the open questions on blue ones. Ke's account went on a white card only as a record of what he had said, not as a fact proved true. The bridge's condition, the exact time of the blackout, and the seventh appointment remained blue. Zeng brought tea and asked whether they should contact Fang's family. Lin said not yet. Bereaved people should not be approached merely to fill the silence in a lawyer's notebook. First they would earn a reason to ask, then offer the family the choice to answer.

林悠工作的公寓很小，證據箱占滿廚房桌。她用白卡片寫已知資料，藍卡片寫尚待回答的問題。柯駿的說法只以「他曾如此陳述」的形式放在白卡，不代表已經證實。橋況、停電確切時間與第七次約談仍在藍卡。曾序帶茶來，問是否該聯絡方芮家人。林悠說還不要。不能只為填滿律師筆記裡的空白，就去打擾悲傷的人。他們應先有合理理由提出問題，再讓家屬自行決定是否回答。

At the end of the file, Lin found an inventory page with seven numbered witness folders. The seventh was missing. A handwritten note said "transfer authorized," but gave no destination, date, or signature. The clerk suggested it was a harmless clerical error. Lin photographed the inventory with permission, then requested preservation of the underlying record. In the hallway, Zeng asked whether the absent folder belonged to another witness or merely an abandoned number. "Either is possible," she replied. "What matters is that somebody counted seven before the court was told there were six." That small discrepancy changed the assignment from routine defense into a dangerous search.

卷宗末尾有一頁清冊，列出七個編號的證人資料夾，第七個卻不見了。手寫註記只寫「准予移轉」，沒有目的地、日期或簽名。書記員認為只是無傷大雅的行政疏失。林悠獲准拍下清冊，接著申請保全原始紀錄。走廊上，曾序問空缺究竟代表另一名證人，還是廢棄的編號。「都有可能，」她回答，「重要的是，法院被告知只有六人以前，就有人已經數到七。」這個小小的矛盾，把例行辯護變成一場危險的尋找。

What was unusual about the six written statements?
