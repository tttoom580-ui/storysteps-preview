The Room That Could Be Locked Twice

無罪辯護：消失的第七位證人 · 第 11 / 25 章 · 4000–5000 · 英中對照 · 校訂中

用互動版查詞、朗讀與收藏

The annex workshop had a door that could be locked from the corridor and a second latch that engaged when the power failed. An upgrade installed after a theft at a nearby casino had created the arrangement. Zeng found the mechanism fascinating, but Lin would not characterize it as a perfect locked-room mystery. A scar on the frame suggested repeated repairs, and an intact maintenance tag showed that technicians had opened the latch before. A clinical engineer recommended testing the door with the same emergency battery used on the night of Fang's death. The test showed that somebody could leave through the service passage without touching the front lock.

附樓工坊的門可從走廊上鎖，停電時第二道閂也會自動扣上。附近賭場曾發生竊盜，後來的設備升級才造成這種設計。曾序對機關著迷，林悠卻不會把它描述成完美密室謎案。門框上的疤痕顯示多次修補，完整的維修標籤也證明技師曾打開門閂。臨床工程師建議用案發夜同型號的緊急電池測試。結果顯示，有人可以經勤務通道離開，不必碰正門的鎖。

In the storage room they found an old rifle prop from an historical exhibition. An anxious spectator once mistook it for a real weapon and gave a gasp; the museum report recorded the misunderstanding. The object had no connection to Fang's injuries. A framed photograph showed Fang with an heir to the old property and a child using a wheelchair at a community refuge. The photograph's copyright belonged to a local artist who agreed to provide an authenticated copy. Lin considered the images apt reminders of Fang's work, not clues to a killer. The sheer number of objects in the annex made selective storytelling dangerously easy.

儲藏室裡有一把歷史展覽用的步槍道具。曾有緊張觀眾把它誤當真槍，嚇得倒抽一口氣；博物館報告記下這場誤會。道具與方芮的傷勢無關。一張裝框照片拍到方芮、舊產權繼承人，以及在社區避難所使用輪椅的小孩。照片版權屬於地方藝術家，他同意提供經驗證的複本。林悠認為這些畫面適合提醒人們方芮做過什麼工作，卻不是指認殺手的線索。附樓的物品數量實在太多，任意挑選來說故事會很危險。

The engineer said that mechanical locks continued to fascinate him because their behavior depended on small details. A dry spring, a swollen wooden panel, or a delayed battery response could change the way a door closed. Lin asked him to demonstrate each condition separately rather than combine them into a theatrical demonstration. He tested the normal latch first, then the emergency latch, and finally the service exit. The observations showed how someone might leave without appearing on the front camera. They did not establish that anyone actually did. The distinction mattered because the original police report had described the room as impossible to exit, giving the jury an exaggerated sense of certainty.

工程師說機械鎖一直令他著迷，因為運作取決於細微條件。乾掉的彈簧、膨脹的木板或延遲的電池反應，都能改變關門方式。林悠請他分別示範，不要把所有條件拼成戲劇化表演。他先測普通門閂，再測緊急門閂，最後測勤務出口。觀察顯示，有人可能不經正門攝影機便離開，卻沒有證明真的有人如此做。這個區別很重要，因為警方原報告形容房間不可能逃出，使陪審團產生過度確信。

Outside, a rescue dog began to howl as rain struck the metal roof. Ke, who had been brought to the site under supervision, quietly pointed to the electrical panel. "I replaced that fuse," he said. He remembered an inspection sticker with the wrong month printed on it. The sticker was still there, and the installation diagram supported his account. It did not prove he was absent from the victim's room, but it gave the defense an ordinary reason for him to be in the building. Prosecutor Zhou noted the detail herself. She had expected a dramatic trick and found instead a repair record that needed verification.

屋外，雨點打在金屬屋頂，一隻救援犬開始長嚎。受到監督帶到現場的柯駿安靜地指著配電盤。「我換過那個保險絲，」他說，還記得檢查貼紙印錯月份。貼紙仍在，安裝圖也支持他的說法。這不能證明他不在死者房內，卻給了辯方一個他在大樓裡的普通理由。周敏親自記下細節。她原以為會看到戲劇性花招，得到的卻是一份需要驗證的維修紀錄。

Ke described the repair work in the kind of detail only a technician might notice: the fuse manufacturer's mark, the smell of warm dust, and the spare screw kept in his pocket. Lin did not assume specialized detail guaranteed honesty. She asked the engineer to compare Ke's account with the original work order. The order mentioned the same fuse number, but its signature was partly blurred. Prosecutor Zhou requested the carbon copy from the maintenance office. Ke seemed disappointed when Lin said the result was still provisional. She told him that an alibi that survived honest challenge would be stronger than one everyone was afraid to examine.

柯駿描述維修細節：保險絲製造商標記、溫熱灰塵的氣味，以及口袋裡的備用螺絲，都是技師才可能注意的事。林悠並未因細節專業就預設他誠實，而是請工程師對照原始工單。工單寫了相同保險絲編號，簽名卻有些模糊。周敏申請調閱維修辦公室的複寫聯。林悠說結果仍屬暫定時，柯駿露出失望。她告訴他，能承受誠實質疑的不在場證明，比人人不敢檢查的證明更堅固。

Before leaving, Lin stood where Fang had last been photographed, beside a table holding flood maps and a broken recorder. The room smelled of old paper and damp wood. She imagined the archivist trying to decide which file to save first when the lights went out. That image was sympathy, not evidence, and she knew it. A camera in the service passage might once have answered their questions, but its storage drive had been removed for maintenance. Lin requested the receipt identifying who had taken it. The signature belonged to the same contractor that trained the six witnesses.

離開前，林悠站在方芮最後一張照片的拍攝位置：桌邊擺著洪災地圖和故障錄音機，房間聞得到舊紙與潮木頭的味道。她想像檔案員在停電時，試圖決定先保護哪份文件。那是同情，不是證據；她知道。勤務通道的攝影機原本可能回答問題，但儲存硬碟被取走維修了。林悠要求調閱領走硬碟的簽收單，簽名竟來自訓練六位證人的同一家承包商。
