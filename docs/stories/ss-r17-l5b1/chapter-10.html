沒有聲音的三分鐘

Three Minutes Without Sound

無罪辯護：消失的第七位證人 · 第 10 / 25 章 · 4000–5000 · 英中對照 · 校訂中

用互動版查詞、朗讀與收藏

Independent audio specialists from a public institute examined the emergency announcement archive. They began with a preliminary report rather than a grand claim. A short drought of recordings appeared between nine twelve and nine fifteen; the gap was a known technical feature of the terminal, not automatically evidence of deletion. The team cited maintenance logs, a descriptive signal chart, and a recording from a restaurant grill whose electrical hum matched the broadcast interference. Their collaboration required slow comparison. Zeng was overwhelmed by the number of variables, but Lin insisted on a rational account of what each result could and could not establish.

公立研究所的獨立聲音專家檢查緊急廣播檔案，先提出初步報告，而非誇大的結論。九點十二分到十五分之間有一小段錄音缺口；那是終端設備已知的技術現象，不自動等於有人刪除。小組引用維護紀錄、描述訊號的圖表，以及餐廳烤架旁的一段錄音，其電流嗡聲與廣播干擾相符。這項合作必須緩慢比較。曾序被大量變數弄得不知所措，林悠卻堅持理性說明每個結果能證明什麼、不能證明什麼。

A technician with a serious food allergy asked to leave when somebody brought peanut biscuits into the laboratory. The interruption reminded Lin that technical work happened among ordinary human needs. Another analyst explained that digestion noises could enter an open microphone; a sound that resembled a cry might be nothing more than a chair scraping. When a young assistant exclaimed that a dramatic burst sounded like a gunshot in a thriller, the supervisor requested a blind test. The sound proved to be a metal drawer. The team would not compel an interpretation merely because the defense hoped to hear a crime.

一名有嚴重食物過敏的技師在有人把花生餅乾帶進實驗室時提出離開。這個插曲提醒林悠，技術工作也發生在有日常需求的人群裡。另一名分析員解釋，消化聲響可能被開著的麥克風錄進去，像尖叫的聲音也可能只是一張椅子刮過地面。年輕助理驚呼某段強烈音效像驚悚片槍聲，主管要求盲測；結果證明那是金屬抽屜。小組不會只因辯方希望聽到犯罪，就強迫資料接受那個解釋。

The laboratory received a bulk delivery of backup drives. An engineer said that even a radical theory about deliberate editing had to cite measurable evidence, not just anger at the city. One drive came from a wilderness ranger station that had recorded the same regional announcement; its clock had been synchronized by a different service. The recording gave the team a second reference for the timing gap. They kept its chain of custody separate so that a failure in one system would not contaminate the other. Lin asked for the engineer's assumptions in plain English, knowing that a jury would need to understand the comparison without becoming technicians themselves.

實驗室收到一大批備份硬碟。工程師說，即使提出有人蓄意編輯的激進理論，也得引用可測證據，而非只表達對市府的憤怒。其中一臺來自荒野巡護站，曾錄到同一區域公告；它的時鐘由不同服務同步，於是提供了第二個時間缺口參考。團隊分別保管其證物鏈，以免一套系統故障污染另一套。林悠請工程師用白話寫出假設，因為陪審團必須在不成為技師的情況下理解比較方法。

Then they located a counselor's voice in a training-room file. It said, "Repeat the sentence exactly, including the time, for the caption example." The file had been generated on Tuesday, not Monday. A second clip contained six different voices reading the same words one after another. The discovery did not establish that every later police interview was false, but it explained the identical phrasing. Lin asked Zhou to join a formal playback session and requested that the equipment's clock offset be measured by an independent technician. In court, truth would require more than a satisfying sound bite.

接著，他們在訓練室檔案找到一名輔導員的聲音：「請為字幕範例逐字重複句子，包括時間。」檔案產生於星期二，不是星期一。另一段有六種不同聲音依序唸出同樣文字。這項發現不能證明所有後來的警方訪談都不實，卻能解釋措辭為何相同。林悠請周敏參加正式播放程序，並要求獨立技師測量設備時鐘偏差。在法庭上，真相需要的不只是一段令人滿意的聲音片段。

Zeng listened to both recordings through ordinary headphones and thought he heard a whisper under the static. The specialist amplified the segment and asked three colleagues to describe what they heard independently. Their answers differed. One heard a voice, another a ventilation fan, and the third nothing identifiable. The team left the segment marked unresolved. Zeng felt embarrassed, but Lin said his first impression was not the problem. The danger would have been treating that impression as a fact and using it to accuse someone. Honest investigation could contain questions that stayed unanswered, even after hours of careful work.

曾序用普通耳機聽兩份錄音，覺得雜訊底下有耳語。專家放大片段，請三位同事獨立描述。答案不同：一人聽見人聲，一人聽見通風機，第三人聽不出任何可辨認聲音。團隊把片段標記為未解。曾序有些尷尬，林悠說最初印象不是問題；危險的是把印象當事實，拿去指控某人。誠實調查可以保留問題，即使辛苦工作數小時後仍未解答。

As the session ended, the specialist handed Lin a sheet listing the original recording contributors. After the six names came one role rather than a name: "caption coordinator, protected." The software had preserved an annotation on the deleted interval: "Do not synchronize this draft with witness interviews." No one in the laboratory knew who had written it. The annotation could be a warning from a conscientious employee or a later attempt to shift blame. Lin requested the author history. Zeng stared at the page. "The seventh witness may have been the person trying to prevent the script from becoming evidence," he said.

程序結束時，專家交給林悠一張原始錄音參與者清單。六個名字之後，只有一項職務而非姓名：「字幕協調員，受保護」。軟體保留了一段在缺口上的註解：「不要將此草稿與證人訪談同步。」實驗室裡沒人知道作者。它可能是認真員工留下的警告，也可能是後來有人推卸責任。林悠要求查詢作者歷程。曾序盯著紙說：「第七位證人，也許就是試圖阻止腳本變成證據的人。」
