A Name Behind the Label

無罪辯護：消失的第七位證人 · 第 8 / 25 章 · 4000–5000 · 英中對照 · 校訂中

用互動版查詞、朗讀與收藏

The court's records officer had never seen such chaos in an ordinary filing dispute. The seventh name appeared in one database as "alien contact," in another as "external service," and in a third as nothing at all. An old myth in the records department claimed that deleting a line made an unwanted problem disappear. Lin asked for comprehension of the system's actual rules, not a legend. An assistant explained that a protected worker could be entitled to a separate contact procedure. Flexibility in labels, however, did not authorize destroying the underlying identity record. They asked the officer to suspend the scheduled data purge.

法院的紀錄官從沒在普通歸檔爭議中看過如此混亂的情況。第七個名字在一套資料庫顯示成「外來聯絡」，另一套寫「外部服務」，第三套則完全空白。紀錄部門有個老迷思，認為刪掉一行就能讓麻煩消失。林悠要求了解系統實際規則，不要傳說。助理解釋，受保護工作者有權使用獨立聯繫程序；標籤有彈性，卻不代表可以銷毀原始身分紀錄。他們請紀錄官暫停預定的資料清除作業。

The missing worker had once helped at a gathering for displaced families. A ragged poster from that event offered a free salon service, medical advice, and a short theatre performance. Funding shortage had forced the organizers to stall several sessions. On the poster's back, a handwritten note described the spectrum of ways people could report danger, from anonymous messages to a formal complaint. Lin saw a strategic difference between a person choosing silence and an institution making that person invisible. There was no reason whatsoever to drag a protected name into public simply to satisfy curiosity.

失蹤的工作者曾協助一場搬遷家庭聚會。活動一張破舊海報提供免費美髮服務、醫療諮詢與短劇演出，但經費短缺使幾場活動被迫停辦。海報背面有手寫筆記，列出通報危險的各種方式，從匿名訊息到正式申訴。林悠看出一個重要的策略差別：一個人自行選擇沉默，與機構把那個人抹成不存在，是兩回事。無論如何都沒有理由只為滿足好奇，就把受保護者姓名拖到公眾眼前。

A pale blue vein ran through the old marble counter in the record office. The clerk traced it while explaining that a protected witness could be entitled to private contact, but not to deletion from the original ledger. The city had tried to minimize paperwork by merging several record types under one code. That convenience had made mistakes easier to conceal. Lin proposed restoring separate entries for interviews, caption work, and safety requests. The clerk said the change would take money and training, not merely an order from a judge. They recorded the weakness as a possible institutional failure rather than treating the office's marble walls as evidence of corruption.

紀錄室舊大理石櫃臺有一道淡藍色紋脈。書記員沿著紋路比劃，解釋受保護證人可以享有私下聯繫權，卻不能從原始登記簿消失。市府試圖合併數種紀錄代碼以減少文書量，這種方便反而讓錯誤更容易被掩藏。林悠建議將訪談、字幕工作與安全申請重新分開登錄。書記員說，改變需要經費與訓練，不是一紙法令就行。他們把弱點記為可能的制度失靈，而不是把大理石牆當成腐敗證據。

An academic adviser proposed a theoretical model showing how aliases could spread through a database. The judge accepted the explanation as background, not as proof of a crime. A former clerk offered a tribute to the office's retired supervisor and remembered that workers needing a visa had received temporary identification numbers. There was nothing shameful about that procedure. The problem was that the seventh number had been reused after the protected interview request. Lin obtained a sealed comparison sheet linking the altered records, and Zhou Min agreed to have an independent technician review it.

一名學術顧問提出理論模型，說明別名如何在資料庫之間流轉。法官接受它作為背景解釋，而非犯罪證據。一名前書記員向退休主管致意，並回憶需要簽證的工作人員曾領取臨時識別號碼。那項程序本身並無可恥之處。問題是第七號識別碼在受保護訪談申請之後又被重複使用。林悠取得密封對照表，將變動的紀錄連結起來，周敏也同意交給獨立技師複查。

At home, Zeng tried to make a diagram of every system that had touched the seventh appointment. His paper soon filled with arrows, and he asked whether they were making the problem harder than it was. Lin answered that a complicated map could still represent a simple event: someone hired a caption worker, and a later office imported the worker's training material into a different file. The important questions were whether the import was authorized and whether anyone noticed the harm. She reminded him not to confuse a person who requested protection with a person hiding guilt. They sent the court a diagram with every uncertain arrow marked differently.

回家後，曾序試著畫出所有接觸第七次約談資料的系統。紙張很快布滿箭頭，他問是否把問題弄得太複雜。林悠回答，複雜的圖也能描繪簡單事件：有人聘請字幕員，後來另一個辦公室把訓練素材匯入不同檔案。重要的是匯入是否獲准、是否有人察覺傷害。她提醒他，不要把要求保護的人與躲避罪責的人混為一談。兩人送交法院的圖表，為每條不確定連線都做了不同標記。

That night Zeng found a message in the court's authorized contact inbox. The sender did not provide a name. "Please stop looking for my home," it said. "Look for the caption log. I marked the original sentence because it was delivered to us, not spoken to us." Lin read the words twice. They could be genuine, forged, or sent by somebody who knew what the defense wanted to hear. She saved the message's metadata through the court's preservation process and did not reply directly. If the seventh person wished to be heard, safety had to come before revelation.

當晚，曾序在法院授權聯絡信箱找到一則訊息，寄件者沒有留下姓名。「請別再找我的住處，」訊息寫道，「去找字幕紀錄。我標記那句原文，是因為有人把它發給我們，不是有人親口向我們說出來。」林悠讀了兩遍。訊息可能是真的、偽造的，也可能出自知道辯方想聽什麼的人。她透過法院保全程序保存訊息附帶資訊，沒有私下回信。如果第七個人願意發聲，安全必須先於揭露。
