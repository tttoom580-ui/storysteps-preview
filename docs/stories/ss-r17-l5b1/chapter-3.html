The Seventh Column

無罪辯護：消失的第七位證人 · 第 3 / 25 章 · 4000–5000 · 英中對照 · 校訂中

用互動版查詞、朗讀與收藏

The city archive permitted a supervised consultation in a windowless room. Lin was warned not to photograph personal addresses. Zeng accepted the restriction; the possible abuse of a witness's privacy would not help their client. A deputy clerk brought out the original paper register. It listed six police interviews and a seventh appointment under "Civic Wave, correspondence unit." The name had been covered with a removable label. A diplomatic explanation would have called this a filing mistake, but the clerk admitted that he had been instructed to leave the seventh line out of the court copy.

市立檔案館允許他們在沒有窗戶的房間接受監督查閱，並提醒林悠不得拍攝私人住址。曾序接受限制；濫用證人隱私無助於當事人。副書記員拿出紙本原始登記簿，上面列有六次警方訪談，另有一次屬於「啟脈公司通訊部門」的第七號約談。姓名被可移除的標籤蓋住。客氣的說法會把這稱為歸檔失誤，但書記員承認，有人指示他在送法院的副本省略第七行。

On the reverse side lay a map of the creek that fed the old pier and an environmental study of its ecosystem. The report warned that a rare marsh plant might become extinct if the bank were sealed. Fang had written in the margin that the development plan would gut the preservation district. Those words explained her dispute with City Relief, but did not identify her killer. Zeng calculated the odds of the seventh appointment being random and then crossed out his guess. Lin preferred a deliberate search to a clever number with no basis. They needed to notify the court of a missing item, not accuse the clerk of a conspiracy.

紙張背面有一張流入舊碼頭的小溪地圖，還附了當地生態系研究。報告警告，如果河岸被封住，稀有沼澤植物可能滅絕。方芮在頁邊寫道，開發計畫將掏空保存區。這些話說明她與市府救濟處的爭端，卻無法指出兇手。曾序估算第七次約談只是偶然的可能性，隨後劃掉毫無基礎的猜測。林悠寧可慎重地查找，也不願提出漂亮卻無根據的數字。他們需要通知法院卷宗缺件，而不是指控書記員參與陰謀。

A junior archivist privately admitted that her superior had shown contempt for citizens who challenged filing decisions. She was willing to describe that attitude, but Lin advised her not to speculate about the superior's role in the murder. The archivist then identified an ordinary office stamp on the canceled appointment: it belonged to the caption service, not the criminal investigations desk. The difference suggested the seventh person had been present because of work, not because police had formally summoned a new eyewitness. Lin wrote down both possibilities. A careful inquiry could protect someone who had been mislabeled as much as it could expose a document removed for improper reasons.

一位年輕檔案員私下坦承，上司曾輕蔑提出歸檔異議的市民。她願意描述這種態度，但林悠建議她不要猜測上司是否涉入謀殺。檔案員接著辨認出取消約談文件上的普通印章：那屬於字幕服務單位，不是刑事偵查部門。這項差異意味著第七人可能因工作而到場，而不是警方正式傳喚的新目擊者。林悠把兩種可能都寫下。審慎調查既可能保護被誤標的人，也能揭露因不當理由被移走的文件。

A news correspondent who once covered the pier recalled the first phase of the redevelopment. A public trophy for "sustainable growth" had been presented to the project director, who later chose a successor without open competition. The correspondent had seen workers swap labels on storage boxes as the files were moved. He could not say whether the seventh folder was among them. "I will not sneak a name into your story merely because it fits," he told Lin. She thanked him, requested his photographs, and asked him to separate firsthand observations from things he had heard from others.

一名曾報導舊碼頭的新聞特派記者回憶開發案第一階段。計畫主任曾獲頒「永續成長」獎盃，後來卻沒有公開遴選就指定繼任者。記者看過工人在搬檔時交換箱子標籤，但無法確定第七號資料夾是否在其中。「我不會只因為一個名字符合故事，就偷偷把它塞進去，」他告訴林悠。她道謝後申請調閱照片，也請他區分親眼所見與道聽塗說。

The clerk asked whether publishing the missing line would make the city more trustworthy. Lin said publication alone could never repair a bad record. A responsible correction must identify the original entry, preserve the version that contained the error, and explain who was authorized to change it. Zeng drafted a request with those three steps and sent it to the judge. Their target was not an employee's embarrassment but an accurate history of what the institution had done. That night Lin imagined how frightening it would be to discover one's work file had become a criminal clue without being asked. She resolved to use a neutral officer for any contact.

書記員問，把缺失那一行公開是否能增加市府公信力。林悠說，單純公開無法修好錯誤紀錄。負責任的更正必須指出原始條目、保存含錯版本，並交代誰有權更改。曾序依這三步起草聲請交給法官。他們的目標不是讓職員難堪，而是正確記錄機構做過什麼。當晚林悠想像，如果一個人的工作檔突然成了刑案線索，卻沒人徵詢本人，那會多麼可怕。她決定所有聯繫都透過中立人員。

At noon the clerk found a second ledger in a locked cabinet. It recorded that the seventh appointment had been canceled at the request of a "protected service worker." There was no evidence that the worker had died or vanished by force. Lin understood that the language might cover an entirely reasonable safety measure. Still, a person who had been prepared to speak to investigators could matter in a murder trial. She filed a sealed request for the appointment records and asked the court to contact the person through a neutral officer. The answer came the next morning: the address was no longer active.

中午時，書記員在上鎖的櫃子找到第二本登記簿。記錄顯示，第七次約談應一名「受保護的服務人員」要求取消。沒有證據顯示那人已死亡或被強迫帶走。林悠明白，這種措辭可能代表完全合理的安全措施。但曾經準備向調查員說話的人，在謀殺審判中可能極為重要。她提出密封申請，要求由中立人員聯繫此人。隔天早晨，法院回覆：登記地址已失效。
