Minutes That Do Not Match

無罪辯護：消失的第七位證人 · 第 4 / 25 章 · 4000–5000 · 英中對照 · 校訂中

用互動版查詞、朗讀與收藏

A reconstruction team visited the courthouse annex with Lin's permission and prosecutor Zhou's observer present. They expected to measure how long a commuter would need to walk from the bus stop to the archive door. Instead, they discovered that the alley narrowed around a stone column with an unusual diameter. A person carrying a case had to turn sideways. The route was distinctive enough that the police photographs should have shown it. Lin did not anticipate how much bureaucracy a simple walk-through would require: three signatures, a safety permit, and a separate approval for every camera.

重建小組在林悠同意、周敏派員在場的情況下，走訪法院旁的檔案館附樓。他們原本要測量通勤者從公車站走到檔案館大門需要多久，卻發現巷子被一根直徑特殊的石柱縮窄。提著箱子的人必須側身才能通過。這條路很有辨識度，警方現場照片理應拍到。林悠沒料到，一次簡單的實地行走竟需要這麼多行政程序：三個簽名、一張安全許可，以及每臺攝影機的單獨核准。

The medical faculty at Baylin University supplied a practitioner to discuss the victim's injuries, but she refused to estimate a minute of death from a photograph. Her report distinguished a marine weather hazard, a possible fall, and deliberate violence. An unusual hormone reading in an old laboratory sheet was not, she explained, a clock. Nor could a mortality table prove an individual's cause of death. Zeng compared parallel routes and found one street with a toll gate, one with a closed stairwell, and another blocked by delivery carts. A fraction of the apparent journey could be made in the time claimed by the witnesses.

灣陵大學醫學院派了一名執業醫師討論死者傷勢，但她拒絕僅憑照片推算死亡的確切分鐘。她的報告區分海洋天候危害、可能的跌落與蓄意暴力。她也解釋，舊化驗表上一項不尋常的荷爾蒙讀數並不是時鐘；死亡率表同樣無法證明個人的死因。曾序比較平行路線，找到一條有收費閘門、一條樓梯封閉，另一條被送貨車堵住的街道。依證人所稱的時間，人最多只能走完表面路程的一小部分。

The fiscal officer supervising the reconstruction said the city could not pay for endless experiments. Lin reassured him that they needed only three timed walks and one independent review of the clock. By coincidence, an apprentice technician had filmed that same route during an unrelated equipment inspection two weeks earlier. His video was useful because it showed a blocked stairway before anyone had become interested in Ke's trial. But the lighting differed from the blackout night, so no one would use the footage as a perfect reconstruction. They compared measurements openly, including the fastest attempt, rather than selecting only results that supported the defense. The fiscal officer signed the revised sheet in front of both legal teams.

監督重建經費的財務官說，市府無法無止境支付實驗費用。林悠向他保證，他們只需要三次計時步行，以及一次獨立時鐘檢查。碰巧一名見習技師兩週前因無關設備檢查，拍過同一條路線。影像有幫助，因為它證明樓梯在任何人關心柯駿審判前就已封閉；然而光線不同於停電夜，不能當成完美重建。團隊公開比較所有測量，包括最快一次，而非只挑對辯方有利的結果。財務官在雙方法律團隊面前簽署了修訂後的紀錄。

At the hearing, City Relief issued a denial of any involvement in the witness interviews. A legal scholar from the province said the defense's plea for original video was reasonable, but the court must protect unrelated residents. Lin reassured the witnesses that no one was asking them to become suspects merely because their memories differed. The judge granted limited access to the recording system and directed both sides to uncover the missing maintenance entries. The decision pleased neither side entirely. It did, however, make the next experiment possible without sacrificing personal information.

聽證會上，市府救濟處否認介入證人訪談。來自外省的一名法律學者認為辯方要求原始錄影合理，但法院也必須保護無關居民。林悠向證人保證，不會只因記憶不同就把他們當成嫌疑犯。法官准許有限度查閱錄影系統，並指示雙方尋找遺失的維護紀錄。這項決定沒有完全討好任何一方，卻讓下一次實驗能在不犧牲個資的情況下進行。

The observer appointed by Zhou Min challenged Lin's stopwatch procedure. Lin initially felt offended, but then saw that the observer was right: their volunteers had started moving before the timing signal. They repeated the test with a visible light cue and a second recorder. The new results were slower, not faster, yet Lin included both versions in the report. Zeng laughed that correcting an error had made their position stronger. Lin reminded him that sometimes correction would make it weaker, and they had to be prepared for that too. If they demanded honest memories from witnesses, they owed the court honest experiments.

周敏指派的觀察員質疑林悠的碼錶程序。林悠起初有些不快，隨後發現對方說得對：志工在計時信號前便已移動。他們改用看得見的燈號與第二臺記錄器重新測試。新結果更慢，不是更快，但林悠把兩版都寫進報告。曾序笑說，更正錯誤反而讓他們更有利。林悠提醒，有些更正也會使立場更弱，他們也得接受。如果要求證人誠實回憶，他們同樣欠法院誠實的實驗。

The team then repeated the route three times under the same lighting arrangement. Nobody could reach the bridge, cross it, and enter the annex in the stated interval. That result challenged the prosecution timeline but did not establish where Ke had been. As the observers packed their equipment, Lin saw an old clock above the service door. Its hands stopped at nine seventeen, although the maintenance record said it had failed at eight fifty-one. She asked that the clock be secured, not repaired. If an entire case rested on one minute, they first had to discover who had supplied that minute.

小組在相同照明條件下重走三次路線，沒有人能在所述時段內抵達人行橋、通過它再進入附樓。結果動搖檢方時間線，卻不能證明柯駿真正在哪裡。觀察員收拾器材時，林悠看見服務門上方一座舊鐘，指針停在九點十七分，維修紀錄卻說它八點五十一分就故障了。她要求封存時鐘，而不是修好它。如果整個案件建立在某一分鐘上，他們得先找出那分鐘是誰提供的。
