有兩扇門的房子

The House with Two Doors

無罪辯護：消失的第七位證人 · 第 9 / 25 章 · 4000–5000 · 英中對照 · 校訂中

用互動版查詞、朗讀與收藏

Lin's investigator approached an authorized neighborhood liaison, not the protected worker's residence. The liaison explained that the district's boundary had shifted after a housing project, leaving some tenants ineligible for ordinary assistance despite valid leases. A vulnerable family living beside the station had been forced to patch their roof with old advertising cloth. Their therapist had asked the city not to expose their addresses. Lin agreed. She needed an investigator who could distinguish a lead from an invitation to discriminate. A public record might establish that a caption worker had once lived nearby; it would not give them permission to turn that person's home into a spectacle.

曾序去找經授權的社區聯絡員，而不是闖去受保護者住處。聯絡員解釋，住宅開發後行政邊界改變，使部分房客即使有有效租約，仍不符合一般補助資格。車站旁一戶脆弱家庭只好用舊廣告布補屋頂，他們的治療師曾請市府不要公開住址。林悠同意。她需要能區分線索與歧視藉口的調查員。公開紀錄或許能證明某位字幕工作者曾住附近，卻不代表可以把她的住處變成展覽。

The liaison's office contained an odd cluster of objects: a sandal left by a child, a souvenir from a pyramid exhibition, and a cracked plastic bodyguard badge from a theatre show. Those items helped explain the building's past, but none identified the missing witness. A housing booklet described how the residence complex comprised four wings and incorporated a small clinic. Another version of the plan had quietly removed the clinic entrance. Zeng asked who had authorized that change. The answer led to the same contractor that had supplied the training cases, though the connection could still have an innocent explanation.

聯絡員辦公室堆著古怪物件：小孩遺落的涼鞋、金字塔展覽紀念品、以及舞臺劇留下的破塑膠保鑣證章。它們說明建築過往，卻無法辨認失蹤證人。住宅手冊提到社區由四翼組成，還納入一間小診所；另一版本的圖紙卻悄悄拿掉了診所入口。曾序問誰核准變更，答案指向供應訓練用紅箱的同一家承包商，但這個連結仍可能有無辜的解釋。

Behind the community hall, a small rescue farm kept an elderly ass named Clover. Its owner explained that the shelter's residents comprised several families who were not eligible for the new relocation grants. She hoped a future plan would incorporate accessible transport, but no one had asked the residents what they needed. Lin listened because the gap in community services helped explain why a caption worker would take extra shifts during an emergency. It did not prove where that worker had gone. When Zeng asked for the farm's visitor book, the owner declined to reveal private visitors without a court order. Lin thanked her for knowing the difference between helpfulness and consent.

社區會堂後方有一座小型救援農場，養著一頭叫幸運草的老驢。主人解釋，收容所住民由幾個家庭組成，卻不符合新搬遷補助資格。她希望未來計畫納入無障礙運輸，但從沒人問住民需要什麼。林悠聆聽，因為社區服務缺口有助說明字幕工作者為何會在緊急事件時加班，卻不能證明此人後來去了哪裡。曾序要求看農場訪客簿，主人說沒有法院命令就不揭露私人訪客。林悠感謝她分得清幫忙與同意。

By noon the liaison brought out a map of a second doorway leading from the community hall toward the archive annex. A fluid line on the diagram represented an old drainage tunnel, not a pedestrian route. Zeng initially hoped they could undo the prosecution's entire timeline with the map. Lin reminded him that drawings did not prove a passage was usable on the night of the death. They would need inspection, photographs, and testimony from someone who had maintained the door. The most passionate residents wanted immediate public accusations. Lin said the defense must remain responsible even when urgency made shortcuts attractive.

中午，聯絡員拿出一張地圖，顯示社區會堂有第二道門通往檔案館附樓。圖上一條流動狀線條代表舊排水隧道，而非步行通道。曾序起初希望這張圖能一舉推翻檢方時間線。林悠提醒他，圖面不能證明死者遇害當晚通道仍可使用。還需要勘查、照片與真正維護過那道門的人的證詞。最激動的居民希望立即公開指控；林悠說，即使情勢急迫，辯方也有責任不走捷徑。

A second volunteer showed them photographs taken during an evacuation exercise at the hall. In one image, several people carried folded chairs through the side door, demonstrating that it could be opened from the inside. Zeng wanted to test the lock immediately. Lin checked the date first: the exercise occurred six months before the murder, and the door had since been repaired twice. She requested those repair orders and asked a local safety officer to supervise a future test. Their investigation was beginning to resemble an excavation, revealing layers of ordinary changes rather than one buried secret. Lin preferred that honest complexity to an elegant but unsupported theory.

另一名志工拿出會堂撤離演習的照片。其中一張拍到幾個人從側門搬出折疊椅，證明門可從內側開啟。曾序想立刻測試門鎖，林悠先核日期：演習在命案六個月前，而門後來修了兩次。她申請維修單，並請地方安全官監督未來測試。調查逐漸像考古，揭開層層平凡變動，而不是單一埋藏祕密。比起漂亮卻無根據的理論，林悠寧可接受這種真實的複雜。

At the end of the visit, an elderly woman showed Zeng a paper receipt from the caption service. It carried no address and no private name, only a service code and the date of the blackout. The woman had kept it because the worker had helped her read an evacuation notice. Zeng accepted a copy after obtaining her permission. Back at the office, Lin compared the code with the sealed database sheet. It matched the seventh entry. For the first time, "W7" referred not merely to an omitted line but to a real service appointment at the time of the incident.

訪視結束時，一名老婦讓曾序看一張字幕服務收據，上面沒有住址與私人姓名，只有服務代碼及停電日期。她留下收據，是因為那位工作者曾幫她讀懂撤離通知。曾序獲得許可後才收下影本。回辦公室後，林悠將代碼與密封資料庫對照表比較，發現兩者一致。第一次，W7 不再只是清冊上被刪除的一行，而是案發當時確實存在的一次服務預約。
