The trust's storage annex had no windows, only shelves of boxes and an access book that recorded visitors by date. Mara's assistant offered Mira a private interview with a former technician, but he demanded anonymity and insisted his words never reach Nia. Mira refused to promise secrecy on someone else's behalf. She said the technician could explain what he remembered off the record, while any public claim would need separate permission. He agreed, then described a donor visit in which staff had been urged to make the memorial recording sound more immediate. He had heard the request; he had not seen an actual edit. As he spoke, Mira noticed an invoice number scribbled on his old badge. She copied it with his consent and asked whether that invoice might identify the company that prepared the exhibition file.

基金會的附屬儲藏室沒有窗，只有一排排紙箱，以及按日期記錄訪客的出入簿。瑪拉的助理安排米拉私下訪問一位前技師，但技師要求匿名，也堅持不讓妮亞得知他的話。米拉拒絕代替別人保證保密；她說可以先不具名聽他回憶，任何公開主張仍需另外取得同意。技師答應後描述，一次捐助者來訪時，有人希望員工把追思錄音做得更像現場。他聽見了要求，卻未親眼看見剪輯。談話間米拉注意到他舊識別證上寫著一組發票號碼。經他同意後，她抄下號碼，詢問那張發票是否能辨認製作展覽音檔的公司。

To establish the sound of an ordinary station day, Mira sampled short phrases from unrelated local broadcasts. The five fragments on this comparison reel concerned “an abrupt interruption in the signal”, “an activist seeking open records”, “a briefcase containing receipt copies”, “contemplate resigning from the station”, and “disrupt the scheduled program”. None was offered as evidence of the disputed emergency warning. Their purpose was practical: normal recordings carried room noise and small changes of breath between phrases, details she could examine without replaying anyone's private distress.

為了弄清普通電臺節目應有的聲音，米拉從互不相關的地方廣播抽取短句。這卷比對音軌的五段內容分別涉及「訊號突然中斷」、「爭取公開紀錄的行動者」、「裝著收據副本的公事包」、「思索是否辭去電臺工作」、「打亂排定節目」。它們都不是爭議緊急警報的證據。這項比對有實際用途：一般錄音在語句之間會留下室內底噪與呼吸變化，米拉不必重播任何人的私人痛苦，也能研究這些特徵。

At the catalog table the researchers examined five more pieces from a routine training reel. Their topics included “a legal entity managing the archive”, “exile from a familiar neighborhood”, “the frontier between rumor and proof”, “hostile questions from a sponsor”, and “an injection prepared at the clinic”. They recorded the labels exactly as found, even when a detail seemed unimportant. The listening exercise reminded them that a later researcher needed to distinguish a genuine fragment, a description supplied by an archivist, and an interpretation added years afterward.

在編目桌旁，研究者又檢查了普通訓練帶的五段內容，主題包括「管理檔案的法律實體」、「被迫離開熟悉街區」、「謠言與證據之間的界線」、「贊助者充滿敵意的提問」、「診所備妥的一劑注射藥物」。他們照原樣記下標籤，即使某個細節看似無關緊要也不省略。聆聽練習提醒眾人，日後的研究者必須區分真正的片段、檔案員加上的描述，以及多年後才出現的詮釋。

The former technician gripped his briefcase as he considered his obligation to former colleagues. An abrupt public accusation, he feared, could turn an ordinary error into a hostile investigation. Mira explained that the inquiry was meant to supervise the handling of records, not to exile anyone from their work. The man showed her a receipt for surplus batteries and a seminar attendance note from the same month. Neither established wrongdoing. Still, each document gave the team a date to compare against the promotional schedule. Mira thanked him for choosing accuracy over a more exciting story.

前技師握著公事包，思考自己對舊同事的義務。他害怕突然公開指控，把普通失誤變成充滿敵意的調查。米拉解釋，此事是為監督紀錄處理，而不是把誰放逐出工作。那人拿出一張多餘電池的收據，以及同月研討會的出席記錄，兩者都不能證明不當行為，卻提供了可與宣傳時程比對的日期。米拉感謝他選擇準確，而非更刺激的故事。

The team replayed samples that were clearly labeled as ordinary broadcasts rather than emergency testimony. The surviving phrases mentioned “a masculine voice on an old tape”, “a naive belief in perfect memory”, “an obligation to protect callers”, “an outlet for public complaints”, and “a pedestrian crossing near the tower”. Across these different subjects, the physical tape carried the same quiet hiss. The contrast made a useful technical example, although it could not by itself explain who had assembled the later anniversary film or what the listeners heard during the storm.

團隊重聽那些明確標示為普通節目、不是緊急證言的樣本。留下的短句提及「舊帶上一道男性聲音」、「對完美記憶的天真信念」、「保護來電者的義務」、「讓民眾申訴的管道」、「電塔附近的行人穿越道」。不論主題為何，實體磁帶都有相同的細微沙沙聲。這形成有用的技術對照，但無法單憑此回答誰組合多年後的周年影片，或災夜聽眾究竟聽到什麼。

To establish the sound of an ordinary station day, Mira sampled short phrases from unrelated local broadcasts. The five fragments on this comparison reel concerned “repay a borrowed emergency fund”, “a seminar on audio ethics”, “supervise the transfer of tapes”, “surplus blankets in storage”, and “a wound high on the thigh”. None was offered as evidence of the disputed emergency warning. Their purpose was practical: normal recordings carried room noise and small changes of breath between phrases, details she could examine without replaying anyone's private distress.

為了弄清普通電臺節目應有的聲音，米拉從互不相關的地方廣播抽取短句。這卷比對音軌的五段內容分別涉及「償還借用的緊急基金」、「聲音倫理研討會」、「監督錄音帶移交」、「倉庫裡多餘的毛毯」、「大腿上方的傷口」。它們都不是爭議緊急警報的證據。這項比對有實際用途：一般錄音在語句之間會留下室內底噪與呼吸變化，米拉不必重播任何人的私人痛苦，也能研究這些特徵。

Nia was angry when she learned there had been a meeting she could not attend, but she accepted that a reluctant witness owned the choice to speak. Together they checked the public procurement registry, which showed that a media company had billed the trust for narration and sound cleaning in 2023. The entry proved a payment, not misconduct. Still, the job description mentioned a promotional film, a product never listed in the early memorial catalog. Mira requested its version history. The company replied that its files were archived and would require a formal authorization letter. Ada warned that asking for records might cause the company to destroy them if it feared blame. Mira answered that an accusation would be weaker than a careful preservation request. They sent a neutral notice asking the firm to retain relevant files while the trust reviewed source descriptions.

妮亞得知有一場自己無法參與的訪談時很生氣，但仍接受不願露面的證人有權選擇是否說話。兩人一起查公眾採購登記，發現一家媒體公司在二〇二三年向基金會請款，項目是旁白與音訊清理。這只能證明有付款，不能證明不當行為；但工作說明提到一部宣傳影片，早期追思目錄根本沒有這項作品。米拉要求調閱版本歷史，公司回覆檔案已封存，必須有正式授權函。艾達擔心，一旦公司怕被怪罪，反而可能銷毀資料。米拉說，嚴謹的保存要求比直接指控更有力。她們於是發出中立通知，請公司在基金會審查來源描述期間保留相關檔案。
