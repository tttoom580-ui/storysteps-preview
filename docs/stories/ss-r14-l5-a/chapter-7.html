渡輪的第二聲鐘

The Ferry's Second Bell

最後一聲警報：被剪掉的八秒 · 第 7 / 25 章 · 4000–5000 · 英中對照 · 校訂中

用互動版查詞、朗讀與收藏

A ferry captain named Rafi kept a notebook full of weather, docking delays, and the times his crew rang the bell. Nia found him repairing a narrow boat behind the market. The captain remembered two distinct bell patterns on the flood night: three slow strokes from the harbor tower and, later, five quick strokes from an island rescue boat. The anniversary audio placed five quick strokes directly beneath the central announcer. Mira asked to hear any surviving recordings of routine departures. The captain had none, but his daughter had captured a training exercise on a cheap recorder the year before the storm. Its bell had the same high metal ring as the disputed clip. The match was suggestive; identical bells could sound alike, especially on worn tape. The crew agreed to repeat the pattern under controlled conditions.

渡輪船長拉菲的筆記本記滿天氣、靠岸延誤與船員敲鐘的時間。妮亞在市場後方找到正在修窄船的他。船長記得洪災夜裡有兩種不同的鐘聲：港塔先敲三下慢鐘，之後島上救援船敲五下快鐘。周年音檔卻把五下快鐘接在中央播報員聲音下方。米拉問是否留有平常出航的錄音。船長沒有，但他的女兒曾在災前一年用廉價錄音機錄下一次演習。裡面的鐘聲與爭議片段一樣，帶著高而薄的金屬聲。這只是提示，因為相同的鐘可能發出相近聲音，尤其經過破舊磁帶。船員同意在可控制的環境下重敲一次。

The catalog team read the next five captions beside their audio sources, making sure listeners would not mistake a quotation for a statement about the flood. They referred to “arouse suspicion with a missing signature”, “an array of mismatched plugs”, “controversy over the ferry schedule”, “a corridor behind the broadcast booth”, and “corrupt payments to a technician”. These modest excerpts showed how broad the station's everyday programming had been. A convincing voice was not automatically an eyewitness, and a polished edit was not automatically a recording made at the moment of an event.

編目團隊把接下來五段音訊的字幕與來源並列，避免聽眾誤把引文當成洪災事實。它們談到「缺失簽名引發懷疑」、「一排規格不合的插頭」、「渡輪時刻表的爭議」、「播音室後方的走廊」、「付給技師的不當款項」。這些平凡片段顯示電臺日常節目有多麼廣泛。令人信服的人聲不必然是目擊證言，修飾完善的剪輯也不一定是在事件當下錄成。

The final set of comparison clips preserved another five ordinary expressions from the station's history: “the cuisine of the fishing district”, “custody of the master recording”, “derive a date from the paper stock”, “disconnect the backup microphone”, and “expertise in tape restoration”. Mira kept them in their original sequence and attached the speakers' permissions where these were available. Their variety mattered because the archive was larger than one famous warning; it contained many lives, none of which should be reduced to a convenient supporting sound.

最後一組比對片段保存了電臺歷史中另五種普通表達：「漁港街區的料理」、「母帶的保管權」、「從紙張材質推算日期」、「拔除備援麥克風連線」、「修復磁帶的專業知識」。米拉按照原始順序保存，若有說話者授權也一併附上。它們的多樣性很重要，因為檔案庫不只是一則著名警報；裡面有許多人的生活，不該把任何人的聲音縮減成方便使用的陪襯。

Rafi's sturdy boat had carried passengers through storms, but he was wary of treating a remembered sound as indispensable proof. He handed over the mailing list without surrendering custody of his daughter's original recording. Nia agreed that the recipient addresses would be checked privately. Meanwhile, Mira's technical expertise helped the team derive a possible route by which the clip had reached the station. She said the discovery was worthwhile only if the final public description kept the same limits as the evidence. A rumor about corrupt payments, already spreading through the corridor, remained unverified.

拉菲的堅固渡輪曾載人穿過風暴，但他很謹慎，不願把記得的聲音當成不可或缺的確證。他交出寄件名單，卻沒有交出女兒原始錄音的保管權。妮亞同意私下核對收件地址。另一方面，米拉的技術專業協助團隊推算聲音可能進入電臺的路徑。她說發現只有在公開描述保持同樣限制時才值得。走廊裡已流傳有人收取不當款項的傳聞，仍無從查證。

Mira placed source cards in front of five short excerpts from the public collections. Their language included “an indispensable witness at the ferry”, “infect a rumor with false details”, “damaged infrastructure beside the pier”, “a league of volunteer boat crews”, and “a pirate broadcast from a fishing boat”. Every card had a separate date and purpose, which stopped visitors from imagining a single continuous event. She wanted the visitors to hear how easy it was for a smooth montage to conceal the boundaries between otherwise honest recordings.

米拉在公共典藏的五段短音訊前放上來源卡。它們的內容包含「渡輪上不可或缺的證人」、「以假細節污染傳聞」、「碼頭旁受損的基礎設施」、「志工船隊組成的聯盟」、「漁船上的非法廣播」。每張卡都有獨立日期與用途，防止訪客把不同內容想像成一件連續事件。她希望訪客能聽出，流暢的拼接多麼容易掩蓋本來誠實的各段錄音之間的界線。

The catalog team read the next five captions beside their audio sources, making sure listeners would not mistake a quotation for a statement about the flood. They referred to “the recipient of an unsigned letter”, “removal of a damaged antenna”, “a sturdy case for the original spool”, “a tournament announced on local radio”, and “a worthwhile risk for the public”. These modest excerpts showed how broad the station's everyday programming had been. A convincing voice was not automatically an eyewitness, and a polished edit was not automatically a recording made at the moment of an event.

編目團隊把接下來五段音訊的字幕與來源並列，避免聽眾誤把引文當成洪災事實。它們談到「一封匿名信的收件人」、「拆除受損天線」、「裝原帶的堅固盒子」、「地方廣播宣傳的競賽」、「對公眾而言值得承擔的風險」。這些平凡片段顯示電臺日常節目有多麼廣泛。令人信服的人聲不必然是目擊證言，修飾完善的剪輯也不一定是在事件當下錄成。

They arranged the test in daylight, recorded the bell at three distances, and invited a university technician to observe. The recordings established that the quick strokes could plausibly have come from the island boat; they could not establish that the anniversary editor had used that exact source. Rafi then revealed a more useful fact. His daughter had mailed copies of the training exercise to several community stations, including Harbor Voice. The mailing list survived because the cooperative had used it to request reimbursement for postage. Nia photographed the list with permission. For the first time they had a documented path by which an old bell could have entered the memorial studio. Mira marked it as a possible source, not the source. Rafi laughed at her cautious wording, then said the difference was why he trusted her.

她們安排白天測試，在三種距離錄下鐘聲，並邀一位大學技師旁觀。錄音證明快鐘有可能來自島船，不能證明周年剪輯者一定使用那份來源。拉菲接著說出更有用的事：女兒曾把演習錄音寄給幾個社區電臺，包括港聲。寄件名單因合作社曾申報郵費補助而留存。妮亞經同意拍下名單。她們第一次掌握一條有文件證明的路徑，說明舊鐘聲如何可能進入追思錄音室。米拉把它標成「可能來源」，不是「確定來源」。拉菲笑她用字小心，卻也說正是這種區別使他信任她。
