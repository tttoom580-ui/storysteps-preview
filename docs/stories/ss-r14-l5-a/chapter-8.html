The Switching Machine

最後一聲警報：被剪掉的八秒 · 第 8 / 25 章 · 4000–5000 · 英中對照 · 校訂中

The small island station had been dismantled after the storm. Only a cracked switching device remained in the school science room, beside a stack of children's weather drawings. Mira recognized its manufacturer but not its model. A patent number on the casing led to an instruction manual that explained how the device could insert a prerecorded announcement when the main signal failed. The museum's film described the island station as a powerless receiver, yet the machine showed that it had once been able to broadcast independently. Nia was excited enough to announce the discovery immediately. Mira insisted on an electrical test first. The school teacher agreed to let a qualified technician inspect it, provided nobody tried to run the corroded machine without proper protection.

小島電臺在風災後已遭拆除，僅存一台龜裂的切換裝置，放在學校科學教室，旁邊堆著孩子們畫的氣象圖。米拉認得製造商，卻不認得型號。機殼上的專利號碼讓她找到說明書，裡面寫著主訊號失效時，裝置可插入預錄公告。博物館影片把島上電臺描述成毫無供電能力的接收站，但這台機器顯示它曾能獨立播音。妮亞興奮得想立刻報導；米拉堅持先做電氣檢查。教師同意讓合格技師檢視，條件是沒有人在缺乏防護的情況下啟動腐蝕的機器。

At the catalog table the researchers examined five more pieces from a routine training reel. Their topics included “caution before releasing a name”, “a chore repeated each morning”, “clarity in the witness statement”, “fuel treated as a scarce commodity”, and “dedicate a channel to emergencies”. They recorded the labels exactly as found, even when a detail seemed unimportant. The listening exercise reminded them that a later researcher needed to distinguish a genuine fragment, a description supplied by an archivist, and an interpretation added years afterward.

在編目桌旁，研究者又檢查了普通訓練帶的五段內容，主題包括「公布姓名前的謹慎」、「每天清晨重複的雜務」、「證人陳述的清楚程度」、「被視為稀缺商品的燃料」、「專設緊急通訊頻道」。他們照原樣記下標籤，即使某個細節看似無關緊要也不省略。聆聽練習提醒眾人，日後的研究者必須區分真正的片段、檔案員加上的描述，以及多年後才出現的詮釋。

The team replayed samples that were clearly labeled as ordinary broadcasts rather than emergency testimony. The surviving phrases mentioned “a delegation from the island council”, “dreadful feedback from the loudspeaker”, “ethical limits on public playback”, “exploration of a deserted studio”, and “fluency in two regional languages”. Across these different subjects, the physical tape carried the same quiet hiss. The contrast made a useful technical example, although it could not by itself explain who had assembled the later anniversary film or what the listeners heard during the storm.

團隊重聽那些明確標示為普通節目、不是緊急證言的樣本。留下的短句提及「島上議會來的代表團」、「喇叭傳來刺耳的回授聲」、「公開播放的倫理界線」、「探索荒廢的錄音室」、「熟悉兩種地方語言」。不論主題為何，實體磁帶都有相同的細微沙沙聲。這形成有用的技術對照，但無法單憑此回答誰組合多年後的周年影片，或災夜聽眾究竟聽到什麼。

The undergraduate volunteers wanted to generate a dramatic demonstration for the public. Mira urged caution. The device's patent could explain its design, but a functioning copy would not prove the damaged island unit had worked throughout the storm. They agreed on an ethical demonstration that began with a written warning about these limits. A delegation from the island council would observe, and the venue would provide accessible captions in both languages. The aim was clarity, not applause. When the receiver switched to its backup recording, the room was quiet enough to hear the relay click.

大學部志工想做一場很戲劇化的公開示範，米拉卻要求謹慎。專利能解釋裝置設計，但正常的同型機器不能證明破損的島上原件在整場風暴中都運作。眾人同意做合乎倫理的展示，開頭先書面說明限制。島上議會派代表旁觀，場地也備有兩種語言的無障礙字幕。目標是清晰，不是掌聲。接收器切到備援錄音時，房間安靜得聽得見繼電器作響。

To establish the sound of an ordinary station day, Mira sampled short phrases from unrelated local broadcasts. The five fragments on this comparison reel concerned “generate a new comparison signal”, “the interior of an empty control room”, “a patent on the old switching device”, “a premium paid for extra coverage”, and “shed light on a missing hour”. None was offered as evidence of the disputed emergency warning. Their purpose was practical: normal recordings carried room noise and small changes of breath between phrases, details she could examine without replaying anyone's private distress.

為了弄清普通電臺節目應有的聲音，米拉從互不相關的地方廣播抽取短句。這卷比對音軌的五段內容分別涉及「產生新的比對訊號」、「空蕩控制室的內部」、「舊切換裝置的專利」、「額外訊號涵蓋所支付的溢價」、「釐清失蹤的一小時」。它們都不是爭議緊急警報的證據。這項比對有實際用途：一般錄音在語句之間會留下室內底噪與呼吸變化，米拉不必重播任何人的私人痛苦，也能研究這些特徵。

At the catalog table the researchers examined five more pieces from a routine training reel. Their topics included “the sole surviving copy”, “steer the discussion toward evidence”, “superstition about the sea bell”, “an undergraduate volunteering on weekends”, and “a venue for a careful listening session”. They recorded the labels exactly as found, even when a detail seemed unimportant. The listening exercise reminded them that a later researcher needed to distinguish a genuine fragment, a description supplied by an archivist, and an interpretation added years afterward.

在編目桌旁，研究者又檢查了普通訓練帶的五段內容，主題包括「唯一存世的副本」、「把討論導向證據」、「關於海鐘的迷信」、「週末服務的大學生」、「舉辦審慎聆聽會的場地」。他們照原樣記下標籤，即使某個細節看似無關緊要也不省略。聆聽練習提醒眾人，日後的研究者必須區分真正的片段、檔案員加上的描述，以及多年後才出現的詮釋。

The technician compared the device with another functioning unit borrowed from a training center. Under the same input, the working unit automatically switched to a recorded warning when a simulated failure occurred. They documented the test, then returned the borrowed machine. The broken island unit remained untouched. It was now plausible that an early warning had traveled through a route omitted from the museum story, but there was still no proof of its exact wording or audience. Nia wrote a balanced article that included a diagram of what the device could do and a paragraph stating what nobody could yet establish. The island teacher read the draft before publication and asked that the children not be photographed. Nia removed the images. The article reached the trust's board before the next planning meeting.

技師借來訓練中心一台仍能運作的同型裝置。在相同輸入下，正常機器遇到模擬故障，就自動切到預錄警報。眾人記錄測試，再把借來的機器歸還；島上破損裝置則保持原狀。如今，早期警報可能走過一條博物館故事省略的路線，但其確切措辭與接收人數仍無證據。妮亞寫了一篇均衡報導，附上裝置功能示意，也用一段文字說明尚無法證明的部分。島上教師在發稿前讀過草稿，要求不要拍攝學生。妮亞刪掉照片。報導在下一次策展會議前就送達基金會董事手中。
