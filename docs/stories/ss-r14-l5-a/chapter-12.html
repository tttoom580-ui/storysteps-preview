The Script That Arrived Late

最後一聲警報：被剪掉的八秒 · 第 12 / 25 章 · 4000–5000 · 英中對照 · 校訂中

用互動版查詞、朗讀與收藏

The media company's preserved drafts arrived with an audit record from its independent archivist. An early storyboard displayed a large caption: RECREATED FROM PUBLIC ANNOUNCEMENTS. A later storyboard removed it. Mira compared the narration script with a photocopy from Ada's memorial folder and found nearly identical language, including a grammatical error that no broadcaster would have been likely to repeat by chance. The script had been written for the memorial, years after the flood. That was strong evidence of the film's source, though not of the precise sounds underneath. Nia suggested that the public correction should state this clearly. Mara's lawyer asked them to avoid the word fraud until they could determine who ordered the alteration and what disclosures appeared in the donation materials. Mira agreed, irritated that careful language could also be used to delay accountability.

媒體公司保存的草稿，連同獨立檔案員的稽核紀錄送到了。早期分鏡有一行很大的說明：「依公開公告重建」；後期分鏡則將它刪去。米拉把旁白稿與艾達追思資料夾裡的影本比較，發現文字幾乎相同，甚至保留一處播報員不太可能偶然重複的文法錯誤。稿本明明是在洪災多年後才寫成的追思用稿。這強烈支持影片的來源判斷，卻不能直接證明底下每一道聲音取自何處。妮亞主張公開更正時把這點寫清楚。瑪拉的律師要求，在未確定誰下令變更及募款材料如何揭露前，避免使用「詐欺」一詞。米拉雖同意，卻也不滿謹慎措辭可能被拿來拖延問責。

To establish the sound of an ordinary station day, Mira sampled short phrases from unrelated local broadcasts. The five fragments on this comparison reel concerned “an abnormal interval of absolute silence”, “acquaint a new volunteer with the rules”, “a bolt missing from the rack”, “compatible connectors for the recorder”, and “confront a witness without accusation”. None was offered as evidence of the disputed emergency warning. Their purpose was practical: normal recordings carried room noise and small changes of breath between phrases, details she could examine without replaying anyone's private distress.

為了弄清普通電臺節目應有的聲音，米拉從互不相關的地方廣播抽取短句。這卷比對音軌的五段內容分別涉及「異常的一段全然寂靜」、「使新志工熟悉規則」、「機架上少了一顆螺栓」、「與錄音機相容的接頭」、「不帶指控地當面詢問證人」。它們都不是爭議緊急警報的證據。這項比對有實際用途：一般錄音在語句之間會留下室內底噪與呼吸變化，米拉不必重播任何人的私人痛苦，也能研究這些特徵。

At the catalog table the researchers examined five more pieces from a routine training reel. Their topics included “continental weather patterns in the news”, “discrimination against migrant callers”, “a hostage crisis mentioned in an unrelated program”, “kidnap rumors denied by police”, and “a missionary speaking at the hospital”. They recorded the labels exactly as found, even when a detail seemed unimportant. The listening exercise reminded them that a later researcher needed to distinguish a genuine fragment, a description supplied by an archivist, and an interpretation added years afterward.

在編目桌旁，研究者又檢查了普通訓練帶的五段內容，主題包括「新聞中的大陸性天氣型態」、「對移工來電者的歧視」、「另一節目提過的人質危機」、「警方否認的綁架傳言」、「在醫院說話的傳教士」。他們照原樣記下標籤，即使某個細節看似無關緊要也不省略。聆聽練習提醒眾人，日後的研究者必須區分真正的片段、檔案員加上的描述，以及多年後才出現的詮釋。

The script's late date forced Mira to confront a long-standing norm: dramatic memorials often blend real events and staged speech. That approach can be viable when viewers know they are watching a reconstruction. It becomes misleading when the label disappears. A university observer confirmed the matching grammatical error, but warned that even a distinctive match did not prove who had requested the change. Nia revised her headline to remove the word traitor. She preferred a report that could survive scrutiny to one that merely created a sensation for a day.

晚到的稿本使米拉面對一種長期存在的慣例：戲劇化追思常把真實事件和舞臺人聲混在一起。只要觀眾知道那是重建，這種做法可以成立；標籤一旦消失就會誤導。大學觀察員確認特殊文法錯誤一致，卻提醒即使有這種吻合，也不能證明誰要求修改。妮亞把標題裡的「叛徒」刪掉。她寧可寫一篇經得起查核的報導，也不要只轟動一天。

The team replayed samples that were clearly labeled as ordinary broadcasts rather than emergency testimony. The surviving phrases mentioned “mumble into the microphone”, “a social norm that discouraged complaints”, “an observer from the university”, “a script revised after the storm”, and “a sensation created by misleading headlines”. Across these different subjects, the physical tape carried the same quiet hiss. The contrast made a useful technical example, although it could not by itself explain who had assembled the later anniversary film or what the listeners heard during the storm.

團隊重聽那些明確標示為普通節目、不是緊急證言的樣本。留下的短句提及「對著麥克風含糊低語」、「壓抑申訴的社會規範」、「大學派出的觀察員」、「風暴後修訂的稿本」、「誤導標題造成的轟動」。不論主題為何，實體磁帶都有相同的細微沙沙聲。這形成有用的技術對照，但無法單憑此回答誰組合多年後的周年影片，或災夜聽眾究竟聽到什麼。

To establish the sound of an ordinary station day, Mira sampled short phrases from unrelated local broadcasts. The five fragments on this comparison reel concerned “a history lesson about slavery”, “stock stored beside the transmitter”, “a traitor in an invented radio play”, “a viable backup transmission plan”, and “a viewer who watched the public replay”. None was offered as evidence of the disputed emergency warning. Their purpose was practical: normal recordings carried room noise and small changes of breath between phrases, details she could examine without replaying anyone's private distress.

為了弄清普通電臺節目應有的聲音，米拉從互不相關的地方廣播抽取短句。這卷比對音軌的五段內容分別涉及「談論奴隸制度的歷史課」、「發射機旁存放的庫存」、「虛構廣播劇裡的叛徒」、「可行的備援傳輸方案」、「觀看公開重播的觀眾」。它們都不是爭議緊急警報的證據。這項比對有實際用途：一般錄音在語句之間會留下室內底噪與呼吸變化，米拉不必重播任何人的私人痛苦，也能研究這些特徵。

That night Mira discovered that the promotional film had used a fragment of Sol's voice to introduce the reconstructed warning. He had allowed the memorial team to record a public interview long ago, but the consent sheet permitted educational display only. The fundraising film fell outside that purpose. Mira stopped the preview distribution and asked Sol what he wanted. He listened for half a minute, then chose to withdraw the clip from future public screenings while allowing the institution to preserve a restricted evidence copy. Nia reported the decision without naming the company employee who had placed the audio. Responsibility for permission belonged to the project, not to a single junior editor. The investigation had found a clear misuse, but the remedy had to honor the speaker's present choice.

當晚米拉發現，宣傳影片還用了索爾一小段人聲來引入重建警報。他很多年前允許追思團隊錄一段公開訪談，授權書卻只准教育展示；募款影片超出了用途。米拉停止放映檔流通，詢問索爾想怎麼處理。索爾聽了半分鐘，決定未來的公開放映都撤下他的聲音，但允許機構保留一份受限制的證據副本。妮亞報導這項決定，沒有點名放入音訊的公司基層剪輯員。取得授權是整個專案的責任，不該隨意推給一名新人。調查找到了明確的不當使用，補救方式也必須尊重說話者現在的選擇。
