The First Listening Room

最後一聲警報：被剪掉的八秒 · 第 5 / 25 章 · 4000–5000 · 英中對照 · 校訂中

用互動版查詞、朗讀與收藏

The trust announced a preview in the school hall. Its director, Mara Vale, wanted a celebration; Mira requested a listening session that could also hold disagreement. Nia arranged three tables. One displayed the exhibition audio as the station currently described it. Another showed the earlier catalog calling it a reconstruction. The third held the clinic's route timeline with personal names covered. Visitors could listen through headphones and mark where they believed a claim exceeded the evidence. Mara worried the display would destroy confidence in the anniversary fundraiser. Mira answered that confidence built on an incorrect label was already fragile. Before the doors opened, Ada asked to sit beside the original catalog. She did not want to be treated as a villain for a reconstruction she had openly marked as such.

基金會宣布在學校禮堂舉辦預展。主任瑪拉・維爾想要慶祝；米拉要求把它設成容許分歧的聆聽會。妮亞安排了三張桌子：一張陳列依目前說法標示的展覽音檔，一張展示把它稱為重建版的早期目錄，第三張放遮住姓名的診所路線時間線。來訪者可以戴耳機聽，標記哪些宣稱超出了證據。瑪拉擔心活動會毀掉周年募款的信心。米拉說，建在錯誤標籤上的信心本來就脆弱。開門前，艾達要求坐在原始目錄旁；她不願因一份自己明明標示為重建版的作品，就被當成惡人。

Mira placed source cards in front of five short excerpts from the public collections. Their language included “attendance recorded by hand”, “a bruise on the courier's arm”, “contemporary stories about past storms”, “contend with two conflicting versions”, and “a controversial fundraising poster”. Every card had a separate date and purpose, which stopped visitors from imagining a single continuous event. She wanted the visitors to hear how easy it was for a smooth montage to conceal the boundaries between otherwise honest recordings.

米拉在公共典藏的五段短音訊前放上來源卡。它們的內容包含「手寫記錄的出席情況」、「信差手臂上的瘀傷」、「當代人講述的舊風暴故事」、「應付兩種互相矛盾的版本」、「引發爭議的募款海報」。每張卡都有獨立日期與用途，防止訪客把不同內容想像成一件連續事件。她希望訪客能聽出，流暢的拼接多麼容易掩蓋本來誠實的各段錄音之間的界線。

The catalog team read the next five captions beside their audio sources, making sure listeners would not mistake a quotation for a statement about the flood. They referred to “a corporation named as sponsor”, “defy an order to destroy notes”, “endorse the independent review”, “the essence of community broadcasting”, and “facilitate public access to records”. These modest excerpts showed how broad the station's everyday programming had been. A convincing voice was not automatically an eyewitness, and a polished edit was not automatically a recording made at the moment of an event.

編目團隊把接下來五段音訊的字幕與來源並列，避免聽眾誤把引文當成洪災事實。它們談到「列為贊助者的公司」、「違抗銷毀筆記的命令」、「支持獨立審查」、「社區廣播的核心精神」、「便利大眾查閱紀錄」。這些平凡片段顯示電臺日常節目有多麼廣泛。令人信服的人聲不必然是目擊證言，修飾完善的剪輯也不一定是在事件當下錄成。

The attendance sheets showed a surprising transition. At first residents had come hoping to praise the station; by the end they were comparing records and asking whose perspective had been excluded. A corporation's representative called the change controversial. Nia answered that disagreement was not the opposite of civic trust. The essence of the meeting was that ordinary people could examine the same segment of sound and reach different provisional conclusions. Mira avoided turning the overall vote into a statistical claim about the whole town. Those who attended had spoken; people who stayed home deserved a chance to respond later.

出席表顯示一種令人意外的轉變。居民起初是來讚美電臺，後來卻開始比對紀錄，詢問誰的觀點遭排除。一名公司代表稱此為具爭議的變化。妮亞回應，意見分歧並不是公民信任的反面。會議的核心精神，是讓普通人查看同一段聲音，也能得出不同的暫時結論。米拉沒有把投票結果當成代表全鎮的統計主張；出席的人已經發言，留在家裡的人日後也應有回應機會。

The final set of comparison clips preserved another five ordinary expressions from the station's history: “the irony of a silent radio station”, “an offering of dry blankets”, “an outfit kept for the ceremony”, “the overall pattern of missing minutes”, and “a different perspective from the hill”. Mira kept them in their original sequence and attached the speakers' permissions where these were available. Their variety mattered because the archive was larger than one famous warning; it contained many lives, none of which should be reduced to a convenient supporting sound.

最後一組比對片段保存了電臺歷史中另五種普通表達：「無聲電臺的諷刺」、「捐出乾燥毛毯的善意」、「為典禮準備的一套服裝」、「失蹤分鐘的整體模式」、「來自山坡區的另一種觀點」。米拉按照原始順序保存，若有說話者授權也一併附上。它們的多樣性很重要，因為檔案庫不只是一則著名警報；裡面有許多人的生活，不該把任何人的聲音縮減成方便使用的陪襯。

Mira placed source cards in front of five short excerpts from the public collections. Their language included “a resident who carried the injured”, “a ritual of reading names aloud”, “a segment of the original broadcast”, “a statistical error in the report”, and “a transition from rumor to evidence”. Every card had a separate date and purpose, which stopped visitors from imagining a single continuous event. She wanted the visitors to hear how easy it was for a smooth montage to conceal the boundaries between otherwise honest recordings.

米拉在公共典藏的五段短音訊前放上來源卡。它們的內容包含「搬運傷者的居民」、「大聲唸出名字的儀式」、「原始廣播的一段」、「報告中的統計錯誤」、「從謠言走向證據的轉變」。每張卡都有獨立日期與用途，防止訪客把不同內容想像成一件連續事件。她希望訪客能聽出，流暢的拼接多麼容易掩蓋本來誠實的各段錄音之間的界線。

The hall filled slowly. A man accused the islanders of trying to steal credit; a teenager asked why the poster mentioned neither the clinic nor the bus drivers. Instead of voting on who was heroic, the visitors voted on a smaller proposal: preserve every surviving source and suspend the claim that the disputed reel was a live transmission until an independent review. The proposal passed with a clear majority. Mara accepted the resolution on condition that no unapproved private voices be published. Mira accepted the condition because it matched what she had wanted from the start. By evening, the poster was covered with a plain paper notice: DATE AND SOURCE UNDER REVIEW. It was not a verdict about the storm. It was permission to keep asking truthful questions.

禮堂漸漸坐滿人。一名男子指責島民搶功；一名青少年問，海報為什麼既沒有提到診所，也沒有提到巴士司機。參加者沒有投票決定誰是英雄，而是表決一項範圍更小的提案：保護所有現存來源，並在獨立審查結束前，暫停將爭議錄音標為現場播出的說法。提案以明顯多數通過。瑪拉同意，條件是不得公開未經許可的私人聲音。米拉接受，因為這原本就是她的原則。傍晚，海報被一張素紙蓋住，上面寫著「日期與來源審查中」。這不是對洪災的判決，而是繼續提出誠實問題的許可。
