山頂的接收機

The Hilltop Receiver

最後一聲警報：被剪掉的八秒 · 第 9 / 25 章 · 4000–5000 · 英中對照 · 校訂中

On the northern hill, the old cemetery's caretaker still kept a battery radio under his desk. During the flood he had carried it between families gathering at the stone chapel. He remembered an early instruction to move inland, but the speaker had been a young man, not the woman on the famous recording. The caretaker's granddaughter, Elin, had written the words in a school notebook because she feared forgetting them. She produced the notebook, complete with an ink stain crossing the date. The wording did not match the memorial clip. That did not make one account false. Mira suspected they had captured two different warnings from two different routes. Nia asked whether Elin would allow the notebook to be photographed; Elin agreed to the lines about evacuation but asked them to exclude a private message about her brother.

北坡墓園的看守仍在桌下保存一台電池收音機。洪災時，他曾把它搬到聚集於石造小禮拜堂的家庭之間。他記得很早就有一則往內陸移動的指示，但說話的是年輕男性，不是著名錄音中的女子。看守的孫女艾琳害怕自己忘記，便把內容抄在學校筆記本上。她拿出那本筆記，日期上還橫著一道墨漬。措辭與追思片段不同，卻不代表其中一方說謊。米拉懷疑她們找到的是兩條不同路線、兩種不同警報。妮亞詢問能否拍下筆記；艾琳同意拍撤離部分，但要求遮掉一段關於弟弟的私人留言。

The final set of comparison clips preserved another five ordinary expressions from the station's history: “an ally within the old station”, “an assault falsely blamed on volunteers”, “a bid for the broadcasting contract”, “a cemetery on the northern hill”, and “citizenship documents kept in a drawer”. Mira kept them in their original sequence and attached the speakers' permissions where these were available. Their variety mattered because the archive was larger than one famous warning; it contained many lives, none of which should be reduced to a convenient supporting sound.

最後一組比對片段保存了電臺歷史中另五種普通表達：「舊電臺內部的一名盟友」、「被誣指為志工所為的襲擊」、「競標廣播合約」、「北坡上的墓園」、「收在抽屜裡的公民身分文件」。米拉按照原始順序保存，若有說話者授權也一併附上。它們的多樣性很重要，因為檔案庫不只是一則著名警報；裡面有許多人的生活，不該把任何人的聲音縮減成方便使用的陪襯。

Mira placed source cards in front of five short excerpts from the public collections. Their language included “compulsory training for new operators”, “distract listeners with cheerful music”, “embrace a grieving grandmother”, “an expedition to the upper reservoir”, and “the exterior wall of the tower”. Every card had a separate date and purpose, which stopped visitors from imagining a single continuous event. She wanted the visitors to hear how easy it was for a smooth montage to conceal the boundaries between otherwise honest recordings.

米拉在公共典藏的五段短音訊前放上來源卡。它們的內容包含「新操作員的強制訓練」、「用歡快音樂分散聽眾注意」、「擁抱悲傷的祖母」、「前往上游水庫的探勘」、「電塔的外牆」。每張卡都有獨立日期與用途，防止訪客把不同內容想像成一件連續事件。她希望訪客能聽出，流暢的拼接多麼容易掩蓋本來誠實的各段錄音之間的界線。

Elin's notebook lay beside the hill receiver like a map drawn from memory. The exterior of the radio case was scratched, and a sharp stink rose when the caretaker opened its old battery compartment. Mira chose not to power it on. An expedition up the hill could document the receiving location without damaging the object. Nia photographed the rear of the case, then helped Elin cover the private line before copying the page. Even a friendly ally could accidentally snatch a person's privacy away by publishing a picture too quickly. They left the cemetery before nightfall.

艾琳的筆記本放在山坡接收機旁，像一張由記憶畫成的地圖。收音機外殼有刮傷，看守打開舊電池室時冒出刺鼻臭味。米拉決定不通電。上山探勘能記錄收訊位置，不必破壞物件。妮亞拍下機殼後側，再協助艾琳遮住私人文字才複製頁面。即使友善的盟友，也可能因為太快發布照片而奪走人的隱私。她們在入夜前離開墓園。

The catalog team read the next five captions beside their audio sources, making sure listeners would not mistake a quotation for a statement about the flood. They referred to “a galaxy visible above the dark shore”, “a lounge used by relief volunteers”, “a manifest for the supply boat”, “mint leaves in hot water”, and “a premier performance by the town orchestra”. These modest excerpts showed how broad the station's everyday programming had been. A convincing voice was not automatically an eyewitness, and a polished edit was not automatically a recording made at the moment of an event.

編目團隊把接下來五段音訊的字幕與來源並列，避免聽眾誤把引文當成洪災事實。它們談到「暗岸上方可見的銀河」、「救援志工使用的休息室」、「補給船的貨物清單」、「熱水裡的薄荷葉」、「鎮上樂團的首演」。這些平凡片段顯示電臺日常節目有多麼廣泛。令人信服的人聲不必然是目擊證言，修飾完善的剪輯也不一定是在事件當下錄成。

The final set of comparison clips preserved another five ordinary expressions from the station's history: “a rear entrance to the archive”, “snatch a paper from the desk”, “squat beside a damaged radio”, “the stink of wet wiring”, and “tuna brought by the harbor cooks”. Mira kept them in their original sequence and attached the speakers' permissions where these were available. Their variety mattered because the archive was larger than one famous warning; it contained many lives, none of which should be reduced to a convenient supporting sound.

最後一組比對片段保存了電臺歷史中另五種普通表達：「檔案室的後門」、「從桌上搶走一張紙」、「蹲在壞掉的收音機旁」、「濕電線散發的臭味」、「港口廚師送來的鮪魚」。米拉按照原始順序保存，若有說話者授權也一併附上。它們的多樣性很重要，因為檔案庫不只是一則著名警報；裡面有許多人的生活，不該把任何人的聲音縮減成方便使用的陪襯。

A weather clock at the cemetery had stopped when lightning struck a wire. The caretaker knew the stop time from a repair receipt written the next morning. Mira used that receipt to define a broad time window, then compared it with the clinic's arrival entries. She refused to claim accuracy to the minute. The most striking result was not a perfect schedule, but a new possibility: volunteers at the hill and island might have communicated before the central station could resume service. When they returned to the harbor, Mara called Nia's article misleading because it described a functioning backup that no one had formally approved. Mira replied that unauthorized equipment could still have helped people, but it also needed independent safety review. Neither issue erased the other.

墓園的氣象鐘在雷擊電線時停擺。看守有次日開立的修繕收據，因此知道它大概何時停。米拉用這張收據界定一段較寬的時間範圍，再與診所到達登記比對，拒絕宣稱精確到分鐘。最驚人的不是完美時刻表，而是另一種可能：山坡與島上的志工也許在中央電臺恢復服務前就已經互通消息。回港後瑪拉批評妮亞的文章誤導讀者，因為它描述了一套未獲正式核准的備援設備。米拉回答，未經核准的設備仍可能救人，但也同樣需要獨立安全審查；兩件事不能互相抹消。
