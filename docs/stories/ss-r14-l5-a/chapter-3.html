Dust Under the Label

最後一聲警報：被剪掉的八秒 · 第 3 / 25 章 · 4000–5000 · 英中對照 · 校訂中

用互動版查詞、朗讀與收藏

The storage room smelled of paper glue and old wood. Mira placed the disputed reel beside the island recording under a soft lamp. The first box carried a new white label, but a narrow strip of brown paper remained beneath it. By lifting only a loose corner, she exposed a faded word: RECONSTRUCTION. The handwriting resembled Ada's. Mira photographed the layers without removing them, because the order of labels might later matter more than the words. Then she examined the reel hubs. The anniversary audio ran on tape manufactured several years after the flood, according to the code stamped inside the plastic. That made it an exhibition copy, not a master from the storm. It still did not prove who had removed the older label or why the new catalog called it a live broadcast.

儲藏室有紙膠與舊木頭的氣味。米拉在柔和燈光下，把爭議錄音帶與島上錄音並排。第一只盒子貼著新的白標籤，下方卻露出一小條褐色紙邊。她只掀開原本就翹起的角落，看到褪色的「重建版」字樣，筆跡很像艾達。米拉拍下標籤層次，沒有撕開，因為日後也許標籤的先後順序比文字更重要。她接著查看捲盤軸心。塑膠內側壓印的製造碼顯示，這捲周年音訊所用磁帶比洪災晚了好幾年才生產。它因此是展覽副本，而非風暴當晚的母帶；但仍不能證明誰換掉舊標籤，或為何新目錄把它稱為現場播出。

The final set of comparison clips preserved another five ordinary expressions from the station's history: “aggression directed at witnesses”, “an attorney advising the families”, “a beloved teacher's recorded message”, “biological samples from the marsh”, and “a promise bound by trust”. Mira kept them in their original sequence and attached the speakers' permissions where these were available. Their variety mattered because the archive was larger than one famous warning; it contained many lives, none of which should be reduced to a convenient supporting sound.

最後一組比對片段保存了電臺歷史中另五種普通表達：「針對證人的攻擊行為」、「為家屬提供意見的律師」、「一位受愛戴教師的錄音留言」、「沼澤採集的生物樣本」、「由信任維繫的承諾」。米拉按照原始順序保存，若有說話者授權也一併附上。它們的多樣性很重要，因為檔案庫不只是一則著名警報；裡面有許多人的生活，不該把任何人的聲音縮減成方便使用的陪襯。

Mira placed source cards in front of five short excerpts from the public collections. Their language included “a cautious review of the claims”, “diminish the volume without cutting words”, “an executive who denied responsibility”, “a layer of magnetic dust”, and “birds that prey on small fish”. Every card had a separate date and purpose, which stopped visitors from imagining a single continuous event. She wanted the visitors to hear how easy it was for a smooth montage to conceal the boundaries between otherwise honest recordings.

米拉在公共典藏的五段短音訊前放上來源卡。它們的內容包含「對說法的審慎審查」、「不刪字而降低音量」、「否認責任的主管」、「一層磁性粉塵」、「捕食小魚的鳥類」。每張卡都有獨立日期與用途，防止訪客把不同內容想像成一件連續事件。她希望訪客能聽出，流暢的拼接多麼容易掩蓋本來誠實的各段錄音之間的界線。

The executive office asked for a virtual reconstruction of the original control room. Mira was cautious. The scope of that project could expand until a polished image concealed gaps in the evidence. She preferred an honest photograph of the actual strap, scratched hub, and paper layer. Ada shrugged when a museum designer called the approach dull. Realism mattered, she said, especially when the public had been invited to believe a staged scene was a recording of real people. Mira promised to keep the physical object and the interpretation apart, even if the finished display looked less dramatic.

主管辦公室要求製作原控制室的虛擬重建。米拉十分謹慎，因為專案範圍可能一路擴大，直到精美畫面遮掩證據缺口。她寧可誠實拍下實物上的束帶、刮傷軸心與紙張層次。博物館設計師嫌這做法無聊時，艾達聳了聳肩。她說寫實很重要，尤其公眾已被要求相信一場表演是現實人聲的錄音。米拉答應把實物和詮釋分開，哪怕展覽因此不那麼戲劇化。

The catalog team read the next five captions beside their audio sources, making sure listeners would not mistake a quotation for a statement about the flood. They referred to “realism in a radio drama”, “the limited scope of the archive”, “shrug at an unanswered question”, “a shuttle between the clinic and port”, and “specialize in repairing old tape”. These modest excerpts showed how broad the station's everyday programming had been. A convincing voice was not automatically an eyewitness, and a polished edit was not automatically a recording made at the moment of an event.

編目團隊把接下來五段音訊的字幕與來源並列，避免聽眾誤把引文當成洪災事實。它們談到「廣播劇的寫實風格」、「檔案庫有限的收錄範圍」、「對未解問題聳肩」、「往返診所與港口的接駁車」、「專門修復舊錄音帶」。這些平凡片段顯示電臺日常節目有多麼廣泛。令人信服的人聲不必然是目擊證言，修飾完善的剪輯也不一定是在事件當下錄成。

The final set of comparison clips preserved another five ordinary expressions from the station's history: “straighten a curled paper label”, “a strap holding the case closed”, “the tick of a wall clock”, “a survivor's viewpoint on the flood”, and “a virtual reconstruction of the route”. Mira kept them in their original sequence and attached the speakers' permissions where these were available. Their variety mattered because the archive was larger than one famous warning; it contained many lives, none of which should be reduced to a convenient supporting sound.

最後一組比對片段保存了電臺歷史中另五種普通表達：「攤平捲曲的紙標籤」、「固定盒蓋的帶子」、「掛鐘的滴答聲」、「倖存者對洪水的觀點」、「路線的虛擬重建」。米拉按照原始順序保存，若有說話者授權也一併附上。它們的多樣性很重要，因為檔案庫不只是一則著名警報；裡面有許多人的生活，不該把任何人的聲音縮減成方便使用的陪襯。

Nia found the original catalog in a cabinet at the local library. It listed the same reel as a dramatized reconstruction prepared for a memorial gathering, with permission to use only a few public announcements. The catalog included no private calls. That distinction made Mira uneasy: the current exhibition audio appeared to contain a person's sob beneath the music. Perhaps it was an actor. Perhaps it was a caller. They could not tell by listening alone. Ada confirmed that she had supervised a memorial edit, but she insisted the old label had remained in place when she delivered it. Mira chose not to confront the trust's executive immediately. She and Nia agreed to request the chain of transfers in writing, keeping every intermediate copy separate. Someone had changed the description of the reel. The next question was when.

妮亞在地方圖書館的櫃子裡找到原始目錄。上面把同一捲帶子列為追思會製作的戲劇化重建，只准使用少數公開公告，並未授權私人來電。這個差別令米拉不安：現行展覽音檔的音樂下似乎有人的啜泣。也許是演員，也許是來電者，光聽無從分辨。艾達承認曾監督追思剪輯，卻堅稱交件時舊標籤還在。米拉選擇不立即質問基金會主管。她和妮亞同意以書面要求所有移交紀錄，並將各個中間副本分開保管。有人改變了錄音的描述；下一個問題是何時改的。
