沒有署名的收據

The Unnamed Receipt

A media company's archive manager finally answered Mira's request. She could not release private client files, but she could confirm that the original promotional project had a delivery schedule and several numbered revisions. The trust's board authorized a limited comparison of file metadata, with no access to unrelated work. One revision was delivered days before the anniversary fundraising campaign, years after the flood. Its notes instructed the editor to remove a visible RECONSTRUCTION card at the opening of the film. No document showed who had written that instruction. The manager said the request had arrived through a shared project account. Mira copied the file hashes and timestamps under observation. The words on the screen were altered; the identity of the person who ordered the change remained open.

媒體公司的檔案經理終於回覆米拉。她不能交出私人客戶檔案，卻能確認最初宣傳案有交件時程與數個編號版本。基金會董事授權有限度比對檔案中繼資料，不得碰觸無關案件。其中一版是在周年募款前幾天送交，距洪災已有多年。編輯備註要求移除影片開頭看得見的「重建版」字卡。沒有文件顯示誰寫了指示。經理表示要求來自共用的專案帳號。米拉在旁人見證下記錄檔案雜湊與時間戳。畫面上的文字確實被改了；誰下令改動，仍無定論。

The team replayed samples that were clearly labeled as ordinary broadcasts rather than emergency testimony. The surviving phrases mentioned “an abortion debate on an old discussion show”, “an anonymous message to the board”, “an arrogant claim of certainty”, “betray a caller's trust”, and “a bronze plate on the studio door”. Across these different subjects, the physical tape carried the same quiet hiss. The contrast made a useful technical example, although it could not by itself explain who had assembled the later anniversary film or what the listeners heard during the storm.

團隊重聽那些明確標示為普通節目、不是緊急證言的樣本。留下的短句提及「舊談話節目中的墮胎辯論」、「寄給董事會的匿名訊息」、「自大地宣稱毫無疑問」、「背叛來電者的信任」、「錄音室門上的銅牌」。不論主題為何，實體磁帶都有相同的細微沙沙聲。這形成有用的技術對照，但無法單憑此回答誰組合多年後的周年影片，或災夜聽眾究竟聽到什麼。

To establish the sound of an ordinary station day, Mira sampled short phrases from unrelated local broadcasts. The five fragments on this comparison reel concerned “a butcher delivering meat to shelters”, “credibility earned through open methods”, “a dome above the civic hall”, “an entrepreneur funding a mobile tower”, and “genetics discussed in a science broadcast”. None was offered as evidence of the disputed emergency warning. Their purpose was practical: normal recordings carried room noise and small changes of breath between phrases, details she could examine without replaying anyone's private distress.

為了弄清普通電臺節目應有的聲音，米拉從互不相關的地方廣播抽取短句。這卷比對音軌的五段內容分別涉及「送肉到避難所的肉販」、「靠公開方法建立的可信度」、「市政廳上方的圓頂」、「出資設立行動電塔的創業者」、「科學廣播談論的遺傳學」。它們都不是爭議緊急警報的證據。這項比對有實際用途：一般錄音在語句之間會留下室內底噪與呼吸變化，米拉不必重播任何人的私人痛苦，也能研究這些特徵。

The anonymous project account was a poor basis for naming a culprit. Mara's arrogant certainty had already damaged trust, but Nia did not want to betray the same principle by making a ridiculous accusation. The company's credibility rested on the version timestamps, while the trust's responsibility lay in how it chose to use the film. Mira proposed an initiative to integrate the preserved files into a restricted audit collection. The board could then testify about its publication decisions without pretending to know the editor's private intentions. A vertical scratch on one case remained a physical observation, not a confession.

匿名專案帳號不足以用來指認犯錯的人。瑪拉先前自信過頭，已傷害信任，但妮亞也不願背叛同一原則，提出荒謬指控。公司的可信度要看版本時間戳，基金會的責任則在它如何使用影片。米拉提議把保存的檔案整合進受限制稽核典藏。董事會便能就出版決策作證，而不必假裝知道剪輯者的私人動機。一只盒子上的垂直刮痕仍只是物理觀察，不是供詞。

At the catalog table the researchers examined five more pieces from a routine training reel. Their topics included “an initiative led by neighborhood women”, “integrate two damaged tape tracks”, “a molecule explained by a schoolteacher”, “a mortgage that threatened the radio building”, and “a ridiculous conspiracy about sea monsters”. They recorded the labels exactly as found, even when a detail seemed unimportant. The listening exercise reminded them that a later researcher needed to distinguish a genuine fragment, a description supplied by an archivist, and an interpretation added years afterward.

在編目桌旁，研究者又檢查了普通訓練帶的五段內容，主題包括「街區女性帶領的倡議」、「整合兩段損壞的聲軌」、「教師講解的一個分子」、「威脅電臺大樓的抵押貸款」、「關於海怪的荒謬陰謀論」。他們照原樣記下標籤，即使某個細節看似無關緊要也不省略。聆聽練習提醒眾人，日後的研究者必須區分真正的片段、檔案員加上的描述，以及多年後才出現的詮釋。

The team replayed samples that were clearly labeled as ordinary broadcasts rather than emergency testimony. The surviving phrases mentioned “the private sector's donation pledge”, “sustain broadcasting through the blackout”, “testify about the night shift”, “a vertical scratch on the spool case”, and “wildlife rescued from the marsh”. Across these different subjects, the physical tape carried the same quiet hiss. The contrast made a useful technical example, although it could not by itself explain who had assembled the later anniversary film or what the listeners heard during the storm.

團隊重聽那些明確標示為普通節目、不是緊急證言的樣本。留下的短句提及「民間部門的捐款承諾」、「在停電期間維持廣播」、「就夜班情況作證」、「帶盒上的垂直刮痕」、「從濕地救出的野生動物」。不論主題為何，實體磁帶都有相同的細微沙沙聲。這形成有用的技術對照，但無法單憑此回答誰組合多年後的周年影片，或災夜聽眾究竟聽到什麼。

That evening the media company asked the trust to correct its public description of the file before naming any individual editor. Mara worried the request would be seen as admitting deliberate fraud. Nia replied that correcting a demonstrably inaccurate label did not require deciding intent. At an emergency board session, Ada explained the difference between the original memorial montage, which had always been a reconstruction, and the later film presented as a live record. Mara accepted a temporary correction but opposed refunding donors until the financial records could be reviewed. Mira considered the position defensible as an interim step. Then she received an email from a family whose father's voice seemed to appear in the montage. They had never given permission for a fundraising film. The question was no longer only about the clock.

當晚媒體公司要求基金會在點名任何剪輯人員前，先更正對檔案的公開描述。瑪拉擔心這樣看起來像承認蓄意造假。妮亞回應，更正一項已證實不準確的標籤，並不等於先判定動機。臨時董事會上，艾達解釋原始追思拼接——從一開始就標為重建——與後來被當成現場紀錄的影片有何不同。瑪拉同意暫時更正，但反對在查清財務紀錄前就退款。米拉認為這可以作為過渡措施。接著，一個家庭寄來電郵，表示影片裡似乎有他們父親的聲音；他們從未授權把聲音用於募款。問題不再只有時鐘。
