值夜的代價

The Cost of Staying Awake

最後一聲警報：被剪掉的八秒 · 第 11 / 25 章 · 4000–5000 · 英中對照 · 校訂中

用互動版查詞、朗讀與收藏

Mira and Nia interviewed retired operator Sol Brenner at a public library, where he had chosen a table near the door. Sol had kept the station running through three consecutive shifts during the disaster. He remembered growing so tired that he once announced the wrong hour, immediately corrected himself, and wrote the mistake in his log. He hated the way later speeches turned the staff into tireless heroes. Exhaustion had been real; so had errors. When Mira asked about the missing eight seconds, Sol did not recognize them. He said the live recorder would have carried constant mechanical noise, even with a dead microphone. More importantly, the control desk's light had gone out before the famous announcement supposedly began. He could describe the sequence, but he no longer trusted the precise time in his memory.

米拉與妮亞在公共圖書館訪問退休操作員索爾・布倫納，他自己選了靠近門口的桌子。災難期間索爾曾連續值守三班，累到有一次播錯時間，立刻更正並在日誌記下失誤。他討厭後來的演說把員工塑造成不知疲倦的英雄。疲勞是真的，錯誤也是真的。米拉問起失蹤的八秒，索爾說不認得。他表示即使麥克風失效，現場錄音機仍會留下持續的機械底噪。更重要的是，在著名公告所稱的開始時間之前，控制台的燈就已熄滅。他能描述先後順序，卻不再信任自己記憶中的精確時刻。

Mira placed source cards in front of five short excerpts from the public collections. Their language included “accelerate the duplication process”, “compliment a volunteer's patience”, “a component inside the old mixer”, “deploy a portable receiver”, and “fatigue after three sleepless nights”. Every card had a separate date and purpose, which stopped visitors from imagining a single continuous event. She wanted the visitors to hear how easy it was for a smooth montage to conceal the boundaries between otherwise honest recordings.

米拉在公共典藏的五段短音訊前放上來源卡。它們的內容包含「加快複製流程」、「稱讚志工的耐心」、「舊混音器中的零件」、「部署可攜式接收機」、「三夜未眠後的疲勞」。每張卡都有獨立日期與用途，防止訪客把不同內容想像成一件連續事件。她希望訪客能聽出，流暢的拼接多麼容易掩蓋本來誠實的各段錄音之間的界線。

The catalog team read the next five captions beside their audio sources, making sure listeners would not mistake a quotation for a statement about the flood. They referred to “a grim report from the clinic”, “an institution expected to keep records”, “minimal sound treatment for the tape”, “a neutral person overseeing access”, and “a petition signed by residents”. These modest excerpts showed how broad the station's everyday programming had been. A convincing voice was not automatically an eyewitness, and a polished edit was not automatically a recording made at the moment of an event.

編目團隊把接下來五段音訊的字幕與來源並列，避免聽眾誤把引文當成洪災事實。它們談到「診所傳來的嚴峻報告」、「本應保存紀錄的機構」、「對錄音帶做最低限度處理」、「監督取用的中立人士」、「居民連署的請願書」。這些平凡片段顯示電臺日常節目有多麼廣泛。令人信服的人聲不必然是目擊證言，修飾完善的剪輯也不一定是在事件當下錄成。

The station's veteran engineer described a component that could fail when the generator was asked to carry too much load. He recommended a minimal test using a preserved training model, with a neutral observer present. Sol understood the recommendation but quivered when the room began to replay familiar static. Mira stopped at once and offered a written transcript instead. No technical breakthrough was worth making a witness undergo needless distress. The station had been an institution built by imperfect people; recognizing fatigue as part of its history was a form of respect.

電臺資深工程師解釋，有個零件在發電機負載過高時可能失效。他建議用保留的訓練同型機做最低限度測試，並安排中立觀察者。索爾明白建議，但房間開始重播熟悉雜訊時，他卻顫抖了。米拉立刻停下，改提供書面逐字稿。沒有任何技術突破值得讓證人承受不必要的痛苦。電臺本就是不完美的人建立的機構；承認疲勞也是歷史一部分，才是尊重。

The final set of comparison clips preserved another five ordinary expressions from the station's history: “quiver at the first voice”, “the realm of verified facts”, “a recommendation to protect originals”, “a specialty in radio engineering”, and “a specimen kept for laboratory comparison”. Mira kept them in their original sequence and attached the speakers' permissions where these were available. Their variety mattered because the archive was larger than one famous warning; it contained many lives, none of which should be reduced to a convenient supporting sound.

最後一組比對片段保存了電臺歷史中另五種普通表達：「聽到第一道人聲時顫抖」、「已驗證事實的範圍」、「保護原件的建議」、「無線電工程的專長」、「留作實驗室比對的樣本」。米拉按照原始順序保存，若有說話者授權也一併附上。它們的多樣性很重要，因為檔案庫不只是一則著名警報；裡面有許多人的生活，不該把任何人的聲音縮減成方便使用的陪襯。

Mira placed source cards in front of five short excerpts from the public collections. Their language included “a stake in the station's survival for local families”, “thrive after the station reopened”, “a trivial scratch that meant nothing”, “undergo another calibration test”, and “a veteran operator who remembered the bell”. Every card had a separate date and purpose, which stopped visitors from imagining a single continuous event. She wanted the visitors to hear how easy it was for a smooth montage to conceal the boundaries between otherwise honest recordings.

米拉在公共典藏的五段短音訊前放上來源卡。它們的內容包含「地方家庭對電臺存續的利害關係」、「電臺重啟後蓬勃發展」、「一條無關緊要的細刮痕」、「再接受一次校準測試」、「記得鐘聲的資深操作員」。每張卡都有獨立日期與用途，防止訪客把不同內容想像成一件連續事件。她希望訪客能聽出，流暢的拼接多麼容易掩蓋本來誠實的各段錄音之間的界線。

Sol offered a folded maintenance sheet. It showed an emergency generator test on the week before the flood, with a note that the automatic transfer sometimes failed. The sheet did not prove what happened on the disaster night, but it gave investigators a mechanism to examine. Mira asked the present station engineer to test the same model from a training collection rather than risk the old equipment. The engineer agreed. Nia suggested that the staff's fatigue should be part of the exhibition, not an embarrassing footnote. Mira pictured visitors hearing Sol's ordinary admission of error beside the official hero narrative. She asked him whether his words could be used publicly. He allowed a written summary, not the recording of his voice. For once, his refusal was treated as part of the record rather than an obstacle to it.

索爾拿出一張摺起的維修單。它記載洪災前一週的緊急發電機測試，並註明自動切換偶爾失效。這不能證明災難當夜發生什麼，卻提供值得檢查的機制。米拉請現任工程師用訓練館收藏的同型機器測試，不拿老設備冒險。工程師同意。妮亞提議把員工的疲憊寫入展覽，而不是當成難堪的註腳。米拉想像訪客在英雄式官方敘事旁，聽見索爾平實承認失誤。她問他是否准許公開；他同意書面摘要，不同意播放本人的聲音。這次他的拒絕被當成紀錄的一部分，而不是調查的障礙。
