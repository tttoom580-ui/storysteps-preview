By noon, rumors had outrun the facts. Some people wanted a villain; others wanted a hero who could end the argument without asking another question. Mira organizes a door-to-door census recording who lives in Haven without requiring any network identifier. Mira had worked long enough to distrust both shortcuts. In the offline city of Haven, the first task was to protect the evidence before anyone could turn it into a weapon.

到了中午，謠言早已跑得比事實快。有人想找反派，也有人想找英雄，好在不再追問的情況下結束爭論。米拉組織挨家挨戶的人口普查，不要求任何網路識別碼也能記錄居民。米拉工作多年，已經不相信這兩種捷徑。在離線城市港灣，當務之急是先保護證據，免得任何人將它變成武器。

Mira unlocked a cabinet with a metal key and checked the handwritten list of borrowers. The paper did not know whether the people named on it were fashionable, connected, or profitable. Theo could still recall the smell of the old library, though the cloud index insisted that the building had never stood there.

米拉用金屬鑰匙打開櫃子，核對手寫借書名單。紙張不在乎名單上的人是否時髦、連線或有利可圖。西奧仍記得舊圖書館的氣味，雖然雲端索引堅稱那棟樓從未存在。

A witness asked whether the inquiry would make tomorrow safer. Mira could not promise that. The truth could establish responsibility, but it would not repair a broken machine, feed a hungry child, or return lost time on its own. Theo suggested a temporary protection that could be checked after sunrise. They wrote down its limits in plain language and invited the people affected to challenge them.

一位證人問調查是否能讓明天更安全，米拉無法保證。真相能確立責任，卻不會自行修好機器、餵飽孩子或歸還失去的時間。西奧提出一項日出後可以檢驗的暫時保護措施。他們用白話寫清限制，邀請受影響的人提出異議。

The overlooked details in the witness file made the question harder, not easier: The family archive had won international acclaim before the payment questions emerged. Mira acknowledged the ambiguity of dream imagery instead of pretending that every vision was accurate. The report ignored bodily limits that had been stated in the original medical note. A cold draught crossed the hearing room when someone opened the ventilation hatch. The archived machine log measured heat in Fahrenheit, unlike the city's present metric settings. Nothing in these records excused the people who had made the dangerous decision.

證人檔案裡被忽視的細節使問題更加複雜，而不是更簡單： 付款疑雲浮現前，家族檔案曾享有國際讚譽。 米拉承認夢境影像的歧義，不假裝每個幻象都準確。 報告忽略原始醫療紀錄中已明列的身體限制。 有人打開通風口時，一股冷風穿過聽證室。 封存機械紀錄用華氏溫度量測熱能，與現行公制設定不同。 紀錄中沒有任何一項能替做出危險決定的人開脫。

For once the numbers did not merely decorate a report; they contradicted the order that had been issued in their name. A young man refuses to share his full history; his right to privacy is respected. Theo asked whether a mistake could explain the result. Mira agreed to test that possibility too. They checked an independent source, marked every assumption, and made copies before the originals could be transferred to an office beyond public reach.

這一次數字不只是報告裝飾，而是直接反駁以數字名義發布的命令。一名年輕人拒絕提供全部經歷，普查員尊重他的隱私。西奧詢問是否可能只是出錯，米拉也同意檢查這種可能。他們核對獨立來源、標記每項假設，在原件被移往公眾無法接觸的辦公室前留下副本。

A second bundle of observations brought the consequences closer to ordinary life: The archive's fireproof cabinet held a copy of the disputed training record. A geographical discrepancy placed the old station west of a wall the official map said had always existed. The committee found no evidence that a hacker had changed the archived document. Even a layman could see that the contract granted indefinite data rights. A court could liberate the records from secrecy, but it could not decide what every resident must remember. Taken alone, no detail settled the case; together they showed why a public answer mattered.

第二疊觀察紀錄使後果貼近普通人的生活： 檔案室防火櫃保存著爭議訓練紀錄的複本。 地理上的矛盾，把舊車站定位在官方宣稱一直存在的城牆西側。 委員會沒有發現駭客修改封存文件的證據。 即使外行人也看得出契約授予無限期資料使用權。 法院可以讓紀錄擺脫保密，卻不能決定每位居民必須記得什麼。 單獨看來沒有一項細節能結案，合在一起卻說明了公開答覆為何重要。

The debate sharpened when Director Vale demanded a decision before the evidence could be copied. Mira replied that urgency and secrecy were not the same thing. They established a temporary limit, published the reason for it, and scheduled an independent review. The result satisfied nobody completely. That was acceptable; the purpose of the process was to make abuse harder, not to produce applause.

當維爾總監要求在證據複製前就做決定，辯論變得尖銳。米拉回應說緊急和保密不是同一回事。團隊訂立暫時限制、公開理由，並排定獨立審查。結果沒有讓任何人完全滿意，但程序的目的本來就不是得到掌聲，而是使濫權更困難。

The team kept the concrete observations alongside the disputed theory: A mermaid mural from the lost railway appeared on two photographs taken decades apart. The seized archive included miscellaneous receipts that proved surprisingly important to the timeline. Each occurrence of an unusual login had to be recorded independently. A family outing ticket listed a train route that the government had later erased from transit maps. Mira chose to persevere through testimony rather than make an unsupported accusation for dramatic effect. Mira asked the clerk to preserve the original wording rather than improve the story.

團隊把具體觀察與有爭議的理論並列保存： 失蹤鐵路上的美人魚壁畫，出現在相隔幾十年拍攝的兩張照片裡。 查扣檔案裡有各種雜項收據，對重建時間線竟出乎意料重要。 每次異常登入事件都必須獨立記錄。 一張家庭出遊車票印有後來遭政府從運輸圖抹除的列車路線。 米拉選擇持續提出證詞，而非為了戲劇效果做出無根據的指控。 米拉要求書記保存原本措辭，而不是把故事修飾得更好聽。

Theo could still recall the smell of the old library, though the cloud index insisted that the building had never stood there.

米拉用金屬鑰匙打開櫃子，核對手寫借書名單。紙張不在乎名單上的人是否時髦、連線或有利可圖。西奧仍記得舊圖書館的氣味，雖然雲端索引堅稱那棟樓從未存在。

The record continued with small facts that would be lost in a confident speech: The judge asked the court to ponder how a government could collect taxes from an officially nonexistent district. The hearing began to revolve around verifiable records instead of arguments about whose dreams felt convincing. Tessa put a clean utensil beside the tea, keeping food away from documents. Ning warned that labeling Ning a villain before the inquiry ended would not replace evidence. A market yogurt container carried a printed delivery address from the supposedly destroyed station district. Theo marked what could be verified and left the rest open to challenge.

紀錄接著列出那些在自信演說中容易消失的小事實： 法官請法庭思考：政府怎能向官方不存在的行政區收稅？ 聽證逐漸圍繞可查的紀錄，而不是爭論誰的夢感覺更真實。 宋穎把乾淨餐具放在茶旁，讓食物遠離文件。 寧澄提醒，調查尚未結束前先把寧澄叫做惡人，不能取代證據。 一個市場優格容器印著據稱已毀車站區的送貨地址。 西奧標記能查證的部分，其餘保留給人質疑。

A message arrived after the public doors had closed. It carried no grand declaration, just a name and a time that did not match the official schedule. Mira resisted the urge to call it proof. Theo opened a fresh page in the journal and recorded the discrepancy exactly. They had won time, not certainty, and there were still people who might lose everything if they hurried.

公共大門關閉後，一則訊息送到。它沒有豪言壯語，只有一個名字和一個對不上官方時程的時間。米拉克制住把它當成證明的衝動。西奧翻開日誌新的一頁，逐字記錄差異。他們贏得的是時間，不是確定性；若急於求成，仍有人可能失去一切。
