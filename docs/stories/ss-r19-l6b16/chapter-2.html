A City Without an Account

最後一座離線城市，拒絕上傳人類 · 第 2 / 25 章 · 5000–6000 · 英中對照 · 校訂中

A messenger arrived before the bells had finished ringing. The news sounded impossible only because everyone had been trained not to question the institution that delivered it. Cloud authorities give Haven seven days to upload its citizens or lose all automated supply routes. Mira asked the messenger to repeat the part that had been omitted from the written notice. It was there, in the omission, that the day's real story began.

鐘聲尚未結束，信使已經抵達。消息之所以聽來不可能，只因每個人都習慣不質疑傳遞消息的制度。雲端當局給港灣七天上傳居民意識，否則切斷全部自動配送路線。米拉請信使重述書面公告遺漏的部分。這一天真正的故事，就從那處缺漏開始。

The cloud index controlled navigation, eligibility, and search results; it did not control whether an offline person physically existed. Paper records and direct local radio remained trustworthy alternatives, though neither could solve every problem alone.

雲端索引控制導航、服務資格與搜尋結果，卻不能決定離線的人是否真實存在。紙本紀錄與直接地方無線電仍是可信的替代方式，雖然各自都無法獨力解決所有問題。

Mira unlocked a cabinet with a metal key and checked the handwritten list of borrowers. The paper did not know whether the people named on it were fashionable, connected, or profitable. Theo could still recall the smell of the old library, though the cloud index insisted that the building had never stood there.

米拉用金屬鑰匙打開櫃子，核對手寫借書名單。紙張不在乎名單上的人是否時髦、連線或有利可圖。西奧仍記得舊圖書館的氣味，雖然雲端索引堅稱那棟樓從未存在。

The evidence did not arrive as a confession. It came through a date that did not fit, a measurement that had been repeated, and a witness who knew exactly what the official account had left out. Mira asked Theo not to describe suspicion as proof. If the accusation was wrong, an innocent person would suffer. If it was right, careless language could still give the guilty a way to escape.

證據不是以自白形式出現，而是一個對不上的日期、一項重複測量的結果，以及一位知道官方說法遺漏何事的證人。米拉請西奧不要把懷疑說成證明。若指控錯了，無辜者會受害；若指控正確，草率措辭仍可能讓有罪者脫身。

The overlooked details in the witness file made the question harder, not easier: Every store manager had to abide by the published repair policy. The director would not affirm the official opening date without seeing the original ledger. An alligator mascot on the old lesson chart reminded children to keep their distance. An old recording played a low bass note that made Jun remember the arrival of a train. The children's colloquial word for the forbidden street survived in a playground counting rhyme. Theo marked what could be verified and left the rest open to challenge.

證人檔案裡被忽視的細節使問題更加複雜，而不是更簡單： 每位店長都必須遵守公開的維修政策。 主任沒看過原始帳冊，不願確認官方啟用日期。 舊課程圖表上的鱷魚吉祥物提醒孩子保持距離。 舊錄音響起低沉的低音，讓小鈞想起列車進站。 孩子口語中稱呼禁忌街道的詞，仍留在遊戲數數歌裡。 西奧標記能查證的部分，其餘保留給人質疑。

A small act of honesty unsettled the room more than a shouted threat could have done. Mira learns the warehouses still hold food; only the address permissions have been removed. Mira watched a witness decide not to change a difficult detail merely because it complicated the preferred story. Theo recorded the correction openly. Their inquiry would be slower now, but its shape belonged less to the powerful and more to the people who had lived through the event.

一個小小的誠實舉動，帶來比喊叫威脅更強的震動。米拉發現倉庫仍有食物，遭刪的只是地址配送權限。米拉看見證人沒有因為細節讓主流說法變複雜就改口。西奧公開記錄修正。調查會因此變慢，卻能較少受權貴支配，也更貼近親歷者。

A second bundle of observations brought the consequences closer to ordinary life: Minor scheduling disputes were commonplace, but a locked emergency exit was not. The hospital's exemption would expire at midnight, giving them only hours to examine the records. Ivo discovered that an old fuse had protected the basement archive from the memory relay network. Mira offered quiet hospitality to worried parents, never asking for their children's forbidden dream details. The authorities called the visions infectious, although the clinic could provide no evidence of transmission. Nothing in these records excused the people who had made the dangerous decision.

第二疊觀察紀錄使後果貼近普通人的生活： 小型排班爭議很常見，但鎖住緊急出口絕非小事。 醫院的特別許可將在午夜到期，留給他們查閱紀錄的時間只剩數小時。 伊沃發現一枚舊保險絲，讓地下檔案室沒有接上記憶中繼網路。 米拉溫和接待焦慮的家長，從不逼問孩子的禁忌夢境內容。 當局稱那些幻象有傳染性，但診所拿不出任何傳播證據。 紀錄中沒有任何一項能替做出危險決定的人開脫。

No one in the room was permitted to declare that a good intention canceled a harmful action. Mira insisted on recording the error, identifying who had authorized it, and protecting those affected before assigning wider blame. Theo drew a simple boundary around what they could do tonight. The line looked disappointingly modest, yet it was the first promise that did not require another person to surrender a right.

室內沒有人能宣稱善意可以抵銷傷害。米拉堅持記錄錯誤、確認授權者，並在追究更大責任前保護受害者。西奧畫出今晚能做到的清楚界線。這條線看似微不足道，卻是第一個不要求別人放棄權利的承諾。

The team kept the concrete observations alongside the disputed theory: A liter of cleaning fluid had spilled over the paper archive, obscuring several names but not the stamps. A novice technician had left the storage sensor disconnected, accidentally preserving the original poster. Mira considered it rash to accuse the clinic staff before she understood who controlled their systems. The old invoice and the official date could not reconcile without an independent explanation. The shabby ledger cabinet looked worthless, which was probably why nobody had removed it. Taken alone, no detail settled the case; together they showed why a public answer mattered.

團隊把具體觀察與有爭議的理論並列保存： 一公升清潔液灑在紙本檔案上，模糊了幾個姓名，印章卻還清楚。 一名新手技師曾讓儲藏室感測器保持斷線，意外保存了原始海報。 米拉認為尚未查清誰控制系統，就指責診所員工太過魯莽。 沒有獨立說明，舊發票與官方日期便無法相互調和。 破舊的帳冊櫃看起來毫無價值，可能因此沒人搬走。 單獨看來沒有一項細節能結案，合在一起卻說明了公開答覆為何重要。

Theo could still recall the smell of the old library, though the cloud index insisted that the building had never stood there.

米拉用金屬鑰匙打開櫃子，核對手寫借書名單。紙張不在乎名單上的人是否時髦、連線或有利可圖。西奧仍記得舊圖書館的氣味，雖然雲端索引堅稱那棟樓從未存在。

The record continued with small facts that would be lost in a confident speech: A borrowed spotlight made the presentation easier to see but obscured an exit sign. A volunteer brought ginger syrup for workers who had spent the afternoon in the cold. The handrail began to tilt when the tester leaned against its outer edge. The artificial twilight outside the clinic concealed the fact that no real sun had reached the street. An old label marked whiskey lay in the storeroom beside the railway invoices, dated before the city's founding. Mira asked the clerk to preserve the original wording rather than improve the story.

紀錄接著列出那些在自信演說中容易消失的小事實： 借來的聚光燈讓簡報更清楚，卻遮住了逃生標誌。 志工帶來薑糖漿，慰勞在寒風中工作一下午的人。 測試者靠在外側時，扶手開始傾斜。 診所外的人造暮光掩蓋了真實太陽根本照不到街道的事實。 一張印著威士忌的舊標籤躺在儲藏室鐵路發票旁，日期早於官方建城日。 米拉要求書記保存原本措辭，而不是把故事修飾得更好聽。

On the way out, Mira passed people waiting for a result that would affect their meals, their homes, or their ability to say no. None of them had the luxury of treating the dispute as an intellectual puzzle. Theo paused to answer a child's practical question before they left. The answer was incomplete, but unlike the old official notice, it admitted the uncertainty out loud.

離開時，米拉經過等待結果的人群，這項結果將影響他們的餐食、家園或說不的能力。他們不能奢侈地把爭論當成智力謎題。西奧停下回答一名孩子實際的問題。答案並不完整，卻與舊公告不同：它坦白承認不確定之處。
