The Library Card

最後一座離線城市，拒絕上傳人類 · 第 4 / 25 章 · 5000–6000 · 英中對照 · 校訂中

Morning brought a decision that had already been made for everyone. There was a signature at the bottom of the order, but no space for those most affected to answer. A child brings Mira a library card that still records his grandmother’s name in ink. Mira knew that refusing a bad choice was not the same as knowing the correct one. Before making a promise, the team needed something they could test.

清晨帶來一項替所有人做好的決定。命令底下有簽名，卻沒有讓受影響最深者回答的地方。孩子拿給米拉一張借書證，上面仍用墨水記著祖母的姓名。米拉知道拒絕壞選項不等於已經知道正確答案。作出承諾前，團隊需要能夠驗證的事物。

Mira unlocked a cabinet with a metal key and checked the handwritten list of borrowers. The paper did not know whether the people named on it were fashionable, connected, or profitable. Theo could still recall the smell of the old library, though the cloud index insisted that the building had never stood there.

米拉用金屬鑰匙打開櫃子，核對手寫借書名單。紙張不在乎名單上的人是否時髦、連線或有利可圖。西奧仍記得舊圖書館的氣味，雖然雲端索引堅稱那棟樓從未存在。

Theo arrived with a second account that neither confirmed nor dismissed the first. They compared the routes by which each record had reached the room, then placed the uncertainties beside the facts. Mira could feel the pressure to announce a solution before anyone became frightened. That pressure was precisely why a second witness mattered: a conclusion that could not survive an independent question would be dangerous, even if it happened to be right.

西奧帶來第二份說法，既未證實也未推翻第一份。兩人比對紀錄抵達房間的途徑，再把不確定之處和事實並列。米拉感受到眾人希望立即宣布解方，以免恐懼擴大的壓力。但也正因這種壓力，第二位證人才重要：無法通過獨立質問的結論，即使碰巧正確，也可能很危險。

The overlooked details in the witness file made the question harder, not easier: The librarian posted a book about digital addiction beside a guide to balanced screen time. The clean aesthetic of the new galleries concealed the violence of their missing captions. The planner could give only an approximate count of attendees. The curator called his unauthorized work urban archaeology because ordinary history departments refused the materials. Ning wore a temporary keeper's badge, taking care not to mistake the emblem for unlimited authority. Taken alone, no detail settled the case; together they showed why a public answer mattered.

證人檔案裡被忽視的細節使問題更加複雜，而不是更簡單： 館員把數位成癮的書和健康使用螢幕指南並排展示。 新展廳整潔的美學風格，掩蓋了遭刪除說明所代表的暴力。 規劃者只能提供來賓人數的大約估計。 館長稱自己的未授權工作是都市考古學，因為正規歷史部門拒收這些資料。 寧澄佩戴臨時守護員徽章，謹慎提醒自己這個標誌不等於無限權力。 單獨看來沒有一項細節能結案，合在一起卻說明了公開答覆為何重要。

The contradiction reached the table just as the meeting was about to end. The same name is absent from every cloud family tree, even though the woman sits beside them. For a long moment no one spoke. Mira understood why silence felt comfortable; each answer threatened someone's settled account of how the world worked. Theo broke the stillness by asking who would be harmed if they simply filed the discovery away. No one could answer without naming a real person.

會議即將結束時，矛盾證據送上桌面。雲端家譜完全查無此人，儘管老婦人就坐在他們身旁。一時之間無人開口。米拉明白沉默為何讓人舒服：任何答案都可能動搖某人對世界的既定理解。西奧打破沉默，問若把發現歸檔，究竟誰會受害。沒人能在不指名真實人物的情況下回答。

A second bundle of observations brought the consequences closer to ordinary life: A tiny electrical blaze in an old motor had been recorded as a minor incident. One young disciple of the state historian copied his claims without checking a single photograph. Mira hoped the exhibition might enlighten visitors, but she refused to replace facts with another comforting myth. The guard made a public fuss about camera rules to distract an officer from Ning's interview. The map carried an implicit assumption that everyone could climb stairs. Mira asked the clerk to preserve the original wording rather than improve the story.

第二疊觀察紀錄使後果貼近普通人的生活： 一名年輕教練說泰莎是他學生最重要的啟發，讓她有些尷尬。 一名國家史官的年輕門生照抄他的說法，連一張照片都沒核對。 米拉希望展覽能啟發參觀者，卻拒絕以另一套安慰人心的神話取代事實。 警衛故意大聲為攝影規定爭論，好分散警員對莎拉訪談的注意。 地圖隱含一個假設：人人都能爬樓梯。 米拉要求書記保存原本措辭，而不是把故事修飾得更好聽。

Mira had expected the hardest part to be exposing the secret. Instead, it was choosing what to do after a partial truth became public. The people who had been ignored did not all want the same remedy. Theo proposed that each affected group could submit a written objection and receive a specific answer. The method would take time, but it recognized them as participants rather than scenery.

米拉原以為最難的是揭露祕密，後來才發現，部分真相公開後如何行動才是難題。曾被忽略的人並不全想要相同補救。西奧提議每個受影響群體都能提出書面異議並取得具體答覆。方法需要時間，但終於把他們當成參與者，而不是背景。

The team kept the concrete observations alongside the disputed theory: A government historian would preside over the reopening ceremony, though he had never inspected the archive. The museum's prestige depended on public trust, not simply on royal-looking seals and elegant lighting. The council planned to privatize the old station land before anyone could establish its rightful owners. Cold white lights appeared to radiate purity, but their brightness made the erased faces harder to see. The volunteer rotation gave everyone time for dinner. Theo marked what could be verified and left the rest open to challenge.

團隊把具體觀察與有爭議的理論並列保存： 一名從未檢查檔案的政府史官，卻將主持重新開幕典禮。 博物館的聲望來自公眾信任，不是華麗印章與燈光。 議會計畫在確定土地合法所有人之前，把舊車站用地私有化。 冷白燈光似乎散發純淨感，亮度卻讓被擦掉的面孔更難辨認。 志工輪班安排讓每個人都有時間吃晚餐。 西奧標記能查證的部分，其餘保留給人質疑。

Mira unlocked a cabinet with a metal key and checked the handwritten list of borrowers. The paper did not know whether the people named on it were fashionable, connected, or profitable. Theo could still recall the smell of the old library, though the cloud index insisted that the building had never stood there.

米拉用金屬鑰匙打開櫃子，核對手寫借書名單。紙張不在乎名單上的人是否時髦、連線或有利可圖。西奧仍記得舊圖書館的氣味，雖然雲端索引堅稱那棟樓從未存在。

The record continued with small facts that would be lost in a confident speech: A forgotten electric shaver was found in a locker during the safety sweep. A stray note beneath the glass included the route number of the missing passenger train. Official captions placed a veil of respectable language over the fact that whole families had vanished. A photograph of a vineyard outside the wall contradicted the government's claim that no crops survived there. The contractor's warranty still covered the sealed valve, proving the tunnel had been serviced recently. Nothing in these records excused the people who had made the dangerous decision.

紀錄接著列出那些在自信演說中容易消失的小事實： 安全清查時，在置物櫃找到一把被遺忘的電動刮鬍刀。 玻璃展示櫃下夾著一張零散紙條，上面寫著失蹤客車的路線編號。 官方說明以體面措辭掩蓋了整個家庭失蹤的事實。 城牆外葡萄園的照片，反駁了政府宣稱外面沒有作物存活的說法。 承包商的保固仍涵蓋封閉閥門，證明隧道不久前有人維修。 紀錄中沒有任何一項能替做出危險決定的人開脫。

When the lamps were lowered, Mira returned to a library card with no network ID. It was neither proof of innocence nor a shortcut to justice. It was a reminder that the next decision would be witnessed by people who no longer trusted easy promises. Theo remained at the door while the final record was sealed. Outside, the offline city of Haven waited for news that had to be more precise than a victory speech.

燈光調暗時，米拉再次看向一張沒有網路識別碼的借書證。它既不是清白證明，也不是通往正義的捷徑，而是在提醒眾人：下一個決定會由不再相信輕易承諾的人見證。西奧守在門邊，最後一份紀錄被封存。外頭的離線城市港灣等待的消息，必須比勝利演說更精確。
