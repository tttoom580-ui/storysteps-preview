哥哥不記得我的名字

My Brother Doesn't Know My Name

The first sign of trouble was not an explosion or a scream. It was a familiar procedure producing a result nobody could defend. Mira calls her uploaded brother Theo, but his cloud profile cannot retrieve her name or their childhood street. Mira checked the time, the witnesses, and the rule that authorized the action. The three did not agree. Outside, ordinary life continued, which made the error more frightening rather than less.

麻煩的第一個徵兆不是爆炸或尖叫，而是熟悉程序得出了誰也無法辯護的結果。米拉致電已上傳的哥哥西奧，他的雲端檔案卻找不到妹妹姓名或童年住址。米拉核對時間、證人與授權規則，三者卻互相矛盾。外頭的平凡生活照常進行，反而讓這個錯誤更加可怕。

The cloud index controlled navigation, eligibility, and search results; it did not control whether an offline person physically existed. Paper records and direct local radio remained trustworthy alternatives, though neither could solve every problem alone.

雲端索引控制導航、服務資格與搜尋結果，卻不能決定離線的人是否真實存在。紙本紀錄與直接地方無線電仍是可信的替代方式，雖然各自都無法獨力解決所有問題。

Mira unlocked a cabinet with a metal key and checked the handwritten list of borrowers. The paper did not know whether the people named on it were fashionable, connected, or profitable. Theo could still recall the smell of the old library, though the cloud index insisted that the building had never stood there.

米拉用金屬鑰匙打開櫃子，核對手寫借書名單。紙張不在乎名單上的人是否時髦、連線或有利可圖。西奧仍記得舊圖書館的氣味，雖然雲端索引堅稱那棟樓從未存在。

Beyond the meeting room, people were already finding ways to survive. They traded tools, shared the location of safe passages, and argued over fair turns without waiting for permission. Mira listened longer than the schedule allowed. Theo reminded the officials that no policy was complete until it made sense to the people expected to live with it. Their notes began with those ordinary acts of cooperation.

會議室外，人們早已開始尋求生存方法：交換工具、分享安全路線的位置，無須等候許可便爭論公平輪班。米拉聽得比議程允許的更久。西奧提醒官員，政策若不能讓實際生活其中的人理解，就不算完整。他們從這些平凡合作開始記錄。

The overlooked details in the witness file made the question harder, not easier: The cashier's handwritten signature on the toy receipt survived two rounds of official records cleaning. A routine checkup form showed that the children were healthy before the forced relocation. Police claimed the midnight searches would curb public panic, but the raids only frightened residents. The railway alarm could deafen passengers standing too close to the old tunnel speakers. The Ministry labeled the market vendor a disgrace for keeping receipts beyond the retention period. Nothing in these records excused the people who had made the dangerous decision.

證人檔案裡被忽視的細節使問題更加複雜，而不是更簡單： 玩具收據上收銀員的親筆簽名，熬過了兩輪官方檔案清理。 例行健康檢查表顯示，孩子們在強制搬遷前身體健康。 警方宣稱午夜搜查能遏止恐慌，突襲卻只讓居民更加害怕。 鐵路警報足以震聾站在舊隧道喇叭旁的乘客。 部會把超過保存期限還留著收據的攤販視為恥辱。 紀錄中沒有任何一項能替做出危險決定的人開脫。

Then the next piece of evidence changed the shape of the case. He recognizes a song she hums, proving memory survives somewhere beneath the indexing system. Mira read the statement twice, searching for the sentence that would make the contradiction disappear. There was none. Theo did not reach for a dramatic accusation; instead, they listed three people who could verify the finding separately. The delay would be unpopular, but a dramatic discovery deserved more care, not less.

接著出現的新證據改變了整起案件的輪廓。她哼起歌曲時他卻認得，證明記憶仍存在於索引系統之下。米拉把陳述讀了兩遍，試圖找出能消除矛盾的句子，卻沒有找到。西奧沒有立刻提出轟動指控，而是列出三位可分別驗證發現的人。延遲會不受歡迎，但重大發現更需要謹慎。

A second bundle of observations brought the consequences closer to ordinary life: The crew documented the safe disposal of contaminated fuel instead of pouring it into the tidewater behind the station. Mira recorded the vendor's dissent when officials tried to seize her original account book. The public register claimed the families had chosen to emigrate, yet none had signed a departure form. The false relocation policy did nothing to enrich the children whose school materials were confiscated. A lengthy transport invoice listed train journeys that were absent from the official timetable. Taken alone, no detail settled the case; together they showed why a public answer mattered.

第二疊觀察紀錄使後果貼近普通人的生活： 船員記錄受污染燃料的安全處置，沒有把它倒進站後海水裡。 官員試圖扣押原始帳本時，米拉記下了攤販的異議。 公共戶籍聲稱這些家庭自願移居他處，卻沒有一戶簽過離境表。 錯誤的搬遷政策沒有改善孩子生活，反而沒收了他們的學習用品。 一張冗長運輸發票列出官方時刻表上不存在的列車旅程。 單獨看來沒有一項細節能結案，合在一起卻說明了公開答覆為何重要。

The debate sharpened when Director Vale demanded a decision before the evidence could be copied. Mira replied that urgency and secrecy were not the same thing. They established a temporary limit, published the reason for it, and scheduled an independent review. The result satisfied nobody completely. That was acceptable; the purpose of the process was to make abuse harder, not to produce applause.

當維爾總監要求在證據複製前就做決定，辯論變得尖銳。米拉回應說緊急和保密不是同一回事。團隊訂立暫時限制、公開理由，並排定獨立審查。結果沒有讓任何人完全滿意，但程序的目的本來就不是得到掌聲，而是使濫權更困難。

The team kept the concrete observations alongside the disputed theory: For a lifelong resident, the changed light was more than a technical issue; it altered familiar night routes. The clerk addressed the elderly witness as madam and waited for her complete answer. A note of melancholy entered the room when the last school route was mentioned. The city's rules made it permissible to erase receipts if they contradicted an approved history. Ning had no wish to preach about courage to people whose jobs were at risk. Mira asked the clerk to preserve the original wording rather than improve the story.

團隊把具體觀察與有爭議的理論並列保存： 對一名終生住在島上的居民而言，變動的燈光不只是技術問題，也改變熟悉的夜間路線。 書記尊稱那位年長證人為女士，並等她說完。 」 提到最後一條校車路線時，屋裡浮現一絲憂傷。 城市規定允許銷毀與核准歷史矛盾的收據。 寧澄不想向生計受威脅的人說教勇氣。 米拉要求書記保存原本措辭，而不是把故事修飾得更好聽。

Mira unlocked a cabinet with a metal key and checked the handwritten list of borrowers. The paper did not know whether the people named on it were fashionable, connected, or profitable. Theo could still recall the smell of the old library, though the cloud index insisted that the building had never stood there.

米拉用金屬鑰匙打開櫃子，核對手寫借書名單。紙張不在乎名單上的人是否時髦、連線或有利可圖。西奧仍記得舊圖書館的氣味，雖然雲端索引堅稱那棟樓從未存在。

The record continued with small facts that would be lost in a confident speech: The smell of wet stone was reminiscent of a street Mira had been ordered to forget. Old rivalry between two clubs made joint planning harder than it needed to be. The suspicious transport account deserved scrutiny from an independent auditor, not an instant accusation. The suspense became unbearable when the command log revealed a search order for the witnesses. The old railway trademark on a receipt matched the symbol painted beneath the clinic's animals. Theo marked what could be verified and left the rest open to challenge.

紀錄接著列出那些在自信演說中容易消失的小事實： 濕石頭的氣味讓米拉想起一條被命令忘記的街道。 兩個俱樂部多年的競爭讓共同規劃更加困難。 可疑的運輸帳戶需要獨立稽核員仔細審查，而不是立刻定罪。 命令紀錄揭露搜捕證人的指示時，懸疑與緊張幾乎令人難以承受。 收據上的舊鐵路商標，與診所動物壁畫底下的符號吻合。 西奧標記能查證的部分，其餘保留給人質疑。

A message arrived after the public doors had closed. It carried no grand declaration, just a name and a time that did not match the official schedule. Mira resisted the urge to call it proof. Theo opened a fresh page in the journal and recorded the discrepancy exactly. They had won time, not certainty, and there were still people who might lose everything if they hurried.

公共大門關閉後，一則訊息送到。它沒有豪言壯語，只有一個名字和一個對不上官方時程的時間。米拉克制住把它當成證明的衝動。西奧翻開日誌新的一頁，逐字記錄差異。他們贏得的是時間，不是確定性；若急於求成，仍有人可能失去一切。
