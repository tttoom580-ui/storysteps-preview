A Message from Within

最後一座離線城市，拒絕上傳人類 · 第 7 / 25 章 · 5000–6000 · 英中對照 · 校訂中

Nobody had called it a crisis at first. A clerk had used a harmless word, a guard had followed an ordinary instruction, and by evening somebody else was carrying the cost. Mira refused to begin with the official conclusion. Theo sends a cryptic message using a childhood spelling mistake only he and Mira would recognize. In the offline city of Haven, even an accurate number could become a lie when it left out the person whose life it counted.

起初沒有人稱這是危機。書記使用看似無害的詞，守衛照普通命令行事，到了晚上代價卻落在別人身上。米拉拒絕從官方結論開始。西奧以童年時只有他與米拉知道的拼字錯誤傳來神祕訊息。在離線城市港灣，精確的數字若遺漏真正承受後果的人，也可能成為謊言。

Mira unlocked a cabinet with a metal key and checked the handwritten list of borrowers. The paper did not know whether the people named on it were fashionable, connected, or profitable. Theo could still recall the smell of the old library, though the cloud index insisted that the building had never stood there.

米拉用金屬鑰匙打開櫃子，核對手寫借書名單。紙張不在乎名單上的人是否時髦、連線或有利可圖。西奧仍記得舊圖書館的氣味，雖然雲端索引堅稱那棟樓從未存在。

The evidence did not arrive as a confession. It came through a date that did not fit, a measurement that had been repeated, and a witness who knew exactly what the official account had left out. Mira asked Theo not to describe suspicion as proof. If the accusation was wrong, an innocent person would suffer. If it was right, careless language could still give the guilty a way to escape.

證據不是以自白形式出現，而是一個對不上的日期、一項重複測量的結果，以及一位知道官方說法遺漏何事的證人。米拉請西奧不要把懷疑說成證明。若指控錯了，無辜者會受害；若指控正確，草率措辭仍可能讓有罪者脫身。

The overlooked details in the witness file made the question harder, not easier: The archived wedding costume was too bulky for the narrow display case. Officials could deem a resident nonexistent by deleting an address, even while collecting that person's taxes. The elder called the city's memory policy a quiet dictatorship built from forms rather than soldiers. An unregistered delivery cart could glide along the lower rails without triggering street cameras. Children sold small handicraft birds to purchase paper that city printers refused to supply them. Nothing in these records excused the people who had made the dangerous decision.

證人檔案裡被忽視的細節使問題更加複雜，而不是更簡單： 檔案中的婚禮服裝太笨重，放不進狹窄展示櫃。 官員可以刪除地址就認定居民不存在，卻同時向那人收稅。 長者稱城市的記憶政策是一種靠表格而非士兵建立的無聲獨裁。 一輛未登記的送貨車能沿下層軌道滑行，不觸發街道攝影機。 孩子們販售手工小鳥，換取城市印刷廠拒絕供應的紙張。 紀錄中沒有任何一項能替做出危險決定的人開脫。

A small act of honesty unsettled the room more than a shouted threat could have done. He warns that global memory searches now treat offline identities as dangerous false matches. Mira watched a witness decide not to change a difficult detail merely because it complicated the preferred story. Theo recorded the correction openly. Their inquiry would be slower now, but its shape belonged less to the powerful and more to the people who had lived through the event.

一個小小的誠實舉動，帶來比喊叫威脅更強的震動。他警告全球記憶搜尋已把離線身分當成危險的錯誤配對。米拉看見證人沒有因為細節讓主流說法變複雜就改口。西奧公開記錄修正。調查會因此變慢，卻能較少受權貴支配，也更貼近親歷者。

A second bundle of observations brought the consequences closer to ordinary life: A community interpreter translated the inspectors' questions for elders who still used the old district dialect. The neighborhood's lesbian teacher said she feared losing her students, not the disclosure of her identity. For residents, liberation meant an address and protection from raids, not a speech from aboveground heroes. Mira rejected the lofty promise that everyone would be saved if they simply trusted her. Ivo used a paper utility map to navigate the tunnels without sending a digital location signal. Taken alone, no detail settled the case; together they showed why a public answer mattered.

第二疊觀察紀錄使後果貼近普通人的生活： 社區口譯員替仍說舊區方言的長者翻譯檢查員的問題。 下層社區的女同志教師說，她害怕的是失去學生，而不是身分被揭露。 對居民而言，解放是擁有地址與免於突襲的保障，不是地面英雄的演說。 米拉拒絕那種空泛崇高的承諾：只要信她，所有人就會得救。 伊沃用紙本管線圖穿越隧道，沒有發出數位定位訊號。 單獨看來沒有一項細節能結案，合在一起卻說明了公開答覆為何重要。

The debate sharpened when Director Vale demanded a decision before the evidence could be copied. Mira replied that urgency and secrecy were not the same thing. They established a temporary limit, published the reason for it, and scheduled an independent review. The result satisfied nobody completely. That was acceptable; the purpose of the process was to make abuse harder, not to produce applause.

當維爾總監要求在證據複製前就做決定，辯論變得尖銳。米拉回應說緊急和保密不是同一回事。團隊訂立暫時限制、公開理由，並排定獨立審查。結果沒有讓任何人完全滿意，但程序的目的本來就不是得到掌聲，而是使濫權更困難。

The team kept the concrete observations alongside the disputed theory: The lower district's volunteer medics warned that overwork would leave them unable to handle another raid. The elder placed a smooth pebble beside every confirmed name on the handwritten community register. The application for registered street addresses remained pending despite decades of tax payments. An abandoned plantation above the tunnels supplied seeds to gardens the city denied existed. The community used a hand plow to cultivate strips of soil beside the old service tracks. Mira asked the clerk to preserve the original wording rather than improve the story.

團隊把具體觀察與有爭議的理論並列保存： 下層社區志工醫護警告，過勞會使他們無力應付下一次突襲。 長者在手寫社區名冊每個核實姓名旁放一顆光滑小石頭。 儘管居民繳了幾十年稅，街道地址的登記申請仍在待審。 隧道上方一座廢棄種植園提供種子，供給城市否認存在的菜園。 社區用手推犁在舊維修軌道旁耕作狹長土地。 米拉要求書記保存原本措辭，而不是把故事修飾得更好聽。

Mira unlocked a cabinet with a metal key and checked the handwritten list of borrowers. The paper did not know whether the people named on it were fashionable, connected, or profitable. Theo could still recall the smell of the old library, though the cloud index insisted that the building had never stood there.

米拉用金屬鑰匙打開櫃子，核對手寫借書名單。紙張不在乎名單上的人是否時髦、連線或有利可圖。西奧仍記得舊圖書館的氣味，雖然雲端索引堅稱那棟樓從未存在。

The record continued with small facts that would be lost in a confident speech: No member of any royalty had visited the lower district, though official portraits hung in its old school. The threat of another correction pulse could spur residents to hide rather than testify. Mira watched an elderly couple stroll down a street that did not appear on any official map. A boy with a deep tan from the garden lamps asked whether the real sun was warmer. The elder warned Mira not to treat the residents' missing citizenship as a trifle. Theo marked what could be verified and left the rest open to challenge.

紀錄接著列出那些在自信演說中容易消失的小事實： 沒有任何王室成員來過下層社區，舊學校卻還掛著官方肖像。 新一輪修正脈衝的威脅，可能促使居民躲藏而不是作證。 米拉看見一對老夫婦漫步在任何官方地圖都沒有的街道。 一名被菜園燈曬黑的男孩問，真正的太陽會不會更暖。 長者警告米拉，別把居民失去公民身分當成小事。 西奧標記能查證的部分，其餘保留給人質疑。

A message arrived after the public doors had closed. It carried no grand declaration, just a name and a time that did not match the official schedule. Mira resisted the urge to call it proof. Theo opened a fresh page in the journal and recorded the discrepancy exactly. They had won time, not certainty, and there were still people who might lose everything if they hurried.

公共大門關閉後，一則訊息送到。它沒有豪言壯語，只有一個名字和一個對不上官方時程的時間。米拉克制住把它當成證明的衝動。西奧翻開日誌新的一頁，逐字記錄差異。他們贏得的是時間，不是確定性；若急於求成，仍有人可能失去一切。
