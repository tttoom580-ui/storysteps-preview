抹除是一條規則

The Erasure Was a Rule

最後一座離線城市，拒絕上傳人類 · 第 10 / 25 章 · 5000–6000 · 英中對照 · 校訂中

用互動版查詞、朗讀與收藏

Morning brought a decision that had already been made for everyone. There was a signature at the bottom of the order, but no space for those most affected to answer. Theo finds a policy that removes unindexed people from search, transit, and medical eligibility simultaneously. Mira knew that refusing a bad choice was not the same as knowing the correct one. Before making a promise, the team needed something they could test.

清晨帶來一項替所有人做好的決定。命令底下有簽名，卻沒有讓受影響最深者回答的地方。西奧查到一條規則，同時將無索引者從搜尋、交通及醫療資格中排除。米拉知道拒絕壞選項不等於已經知道正確答案。作出承諾前，團隊需要能夠驗證的事物。

Theo guided Mira through a direct voice connection that the cloud index could not classify. On his screen, a single policy line appeared between medical eligibility and map search: unindexed individuals shall be treated as invalid references. It sounded technical, almost boring. It meant that a hospital would not call their names, vehicles would not stop at their addresses, and family searches would return empty results. The rule had been signed by Director Vale after Haven voted against compulsory uploading. It was neither an error nor an accident. Mira recorded the code and asked Theo if he understood what his office had done. He was silent for a long time. Then he answered, 'We made forgetting people cheaper than remembering them.'

西奧透過雲端索引無法分類的直接語音連線引導米拉。螢幕上，醫療資格與地圖搜尋之間出現一條政策文字：「沒有索引的個人應視為無效參照。」聽來技術性十足，甚至無聊，實際上卻代表醫院不會叫出他們的姓名、車輛不會停在他們的地址，家人搜尋只會得到空白。這條規則在港灣投票反對強制上傳後，由維爾總監簽署。不是錯誤，也不是意外。米拉記下程式碼，問西奧是否明白他所在辦公室做了什麼。他沉默許久，終於回答：「我們讓遺忘人變得比記住人更便宜。」

The overlooked details in the witness file made the question harder, not easier: An old training film used an animate cartoon train to teach children the route beyond the western gate. Simple arithmetic showed that reported train repairs continued long after the official closure date. The students called the corrected graph awesome, then asked how it was made. The celebration lunch included celery sticks for anyone who wanted a simple snack. Fresh cement around the old gate contradicted claims that no one had maintained it for decades. Theo marked what could be verified and left the rest open to challenge.

證人檔案裡被忽視的細節使問題更加複雜，而不是更簡單： 一部舊教學影片用栩栩如生的卡通列車教孩子認識西門外路線。 簡單的算術就能看出，列車維修紀錄在官方封閉日期之後仍持續多年。 學生稱更正圖表很棒，接著追問它如何製作。 慶祝午餐有芹菜條，供想吃簡單點心的人取用。 舊城門周圍的新水泥，與數十年無人維護的說法矛盾。 西奧標記能查證的部分，其餘保留給人質疑。

What changed the discussion was a fact that should have been obvious from the start. Director Vale approved it knowingly, calling the exclusion an incentive for digital unity. Mira felt the old temptation to turn that fact into an immediate victory. Instead, the team separated what the new evidence established from what it merely suggested. That distinction frustrated the loudest spectators, but it helped the witnesses remain willing to speak again.

改變討論的是一件本來就該看得見的事實。維爾總監明知後果仍核准，稱這種排除能鼓勵數位團結。米拉一瞬間也想把它當成立刻勝利的證明，團隊卻把新證據真正確立的部分與僅能推測的部分分開。這讓最喧鬧的旁觀者失望，卻使證人願意再次出面。

A second bundle of observations brought the consequences closer to ordinary life: A regional confederation of village councils demanded equal access to the records. A public crackdown on unauthorized boats could not substitute for fixing confusing instructions, Ning argued at the hearing. A crocodile drawing on the children's wall kept the safety lesson playful. The old railway had a dual power system, allowing trains to operate even when city electricity failed. The city could not equalize every skill level, but it could make entry fairer. Nothing in these records excused the people who had made the dangerous decision.

第二疊觀察紀錄使後果貼近普通人的生活： 村議會聯盟要求平等取得紀錄。 寧澄在聽證時主張，嚴厲取締未經許可的船隻不能取代修正令人困惑的指示。 兒童牆上的鱷魚圖讓安全課帶點趣味。 舊鐵路有雙重電力系統，即使城市停電，列車仍能運行。 市府無法讓每個人的技能相同，卻能使入場機會更公平。 紀錄中沒有任何一項能替做出危險決定的人開脫。

No one in the room was permitted to declare that a good intention canceled a harmful action. Mira insisted on recording the error, identifying who had authorized it, and protecting those affected before assigning wider blame. Theo drew a simple boundary around what they could do tonight. The line looked disappointingly modest, yet it was the first promise that did not require another person to surrender a right.

室內沒有人能宣稱善意可以抵銷傷害。米拉堅持記錄錯誤、確認授權者，並在追究更大責任前保護受害者。西奧畫出今晚能做到的清楚界線。這條線看似微不足道，卻是第一個不要求別人放棄權利的承諾。

The team kept the concrete observations alongside the disputed theory: A council faction argued that opening the gate would endanger the city's fragile social order. The gate engineer feared he would incur criminal penalties merely for preserving an obsolete switch. Residents called unequal access to safety information an injustice, especially when only wealthy boat owners received private warnings. A rumor of corporate intrigue attracted reporters, but the board examined invoices rather than treating gossip as evidence. A flashy app could lure officials away from the needs of paper-card residents. Taken alone, no detail settled the case; together they showed why a public answer mattered.

團隊把具體觀察與有爭議的理論並列保存： 一個議會派系主張開城門會危及城市脆弱的社會秩序。 城門工程師害怕只因保存淘汰轉轍器，就會受到刑事處罰。 居民認為安全資訊分配不均是一種不公，尤其只有富有船主能得到私人警示。 企業陰謀的傳言吸引記者，委員會卻審查發票，而不是把八卦當證據。 華麗應用程式可能引誘官員忽略紙本求援急救手冊證居民。 單獨看來沒有一項細節能結案，合在一起卻說明了公開答覆為何重要。

Mira unlocked a cabinet with a metal key and checked the handwritten list of borrowers. The paper did not know whether the people named on it were fashionable, connected, or profitable. Theo could still recall the smell of the old library, though the cloud index insisted that the building had never stood there.

米拉用金屬鑰匙打開櫃子，核對手寫借書名單。紙張不在乎名單上的人是否時髦、連線或有利可圖。西奧仍記得舊圖書館的氣味，雖然雲端索引堅稱那棟樓從未存在。

The record continued with small facts that would be lost in a confident speech: An obstinate councilor finally agreed to publish the full operating budget. A pickpocket had once stolen a train ticket and kept it as a souvenir after the railway disappeared. The cleaning crew left on time because the new contracts required punctual shifts. The chair showed restraint by allowing critics to finish their comments. After twelve hours of testimony, the exhausted witness began to stagger toward the exit, and the chair called a break. Mira asked the clerk to preserve the original wording rather than improve the story.

紀錄接著列出那些在自信演說中容易消失的小事實： 」 一名固執議員終於同意公開完整營運預算。 一名扒手曾偷過火車票，鐵路消失後卻把票當作紀念品保存。 清潔團隊準時下班，因為新合約要求準點輪班。 主席克制自己，讓批評者把話說完。 證人作證十二小時後，疲憊得往出口踉蹌，主席因此宣布休息。 米拉要求書記保存原本措辭，而不是把故事修飾得更好聽。

On the way out, Mira passed people waiting for a result that would affect their meals, their homes, or their ability to say no. None of them had the luxury of treating the dispute as an intellectual puzzle. Theo paused to answer a child's practical question before they left. The answer was incomplete, but unlike the old official notice, it admitted the uncertainty out loud.

離開時，米拉經過等待結果的人群，這項結果將影響他們的餐食、家園或說不的能力。他們不能奢侈地把爭論當成智力謎題。西奧停下回答一名孩子實際的問題。答案並不完整，卻與舊公告不同：它坦白承認不確定之處。
