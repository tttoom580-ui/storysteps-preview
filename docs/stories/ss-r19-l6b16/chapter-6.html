消失的生日

The Missing Birthdays

最後一座離線城市，拒絕上傳人類 · 第 6 / 25 章 · 5000–6000 · 英中對照 · 校訂中

The room smelled of dust, hot metal, and rain carried in on people's coats. Someone had left an ordinary object on the central table, yet no one wanted to touch it. Mira compares paper birthday books with online family memories and finds entire decades omitted. Mira saw that the object mattered less for what it was than for the decision it proved had been made. There would be no honest way back to ignorance.

室內有灰塵、熱金屬與人們外套帶進的雨味。有人把平凡物件放在中央桌上，卻沒有人願意碰它。米拉比較紙本生日冊與線上家庭記憶，發現整整幾十年的記錄遭跳過。米拉明白重要的不是物件本身，而是它證明了何種決定已經發生。如今已不可能誠實地假裝無知。

Mira unlocked a cabinet with a metal key and checked the handwritten list of borrowers. The paper did not know whether the people named on it were fashionable, connected, or profitable. Theo could still recall the smell of the old library, though the cloud index insisted that the building had never stood there.

米拉用金屬鑰匙打開櫃子，核對手寫借書名單。紙張不在乎名單上的人是否時髦、連線或有利可圖。西奧仍記得舊圖書館的氣味，雖然雲端索引堅稱那棟樓從未存在。

A witness asked whether the inquiry would make tomorrow safer. Mira could not promise that. The truth could establish responsibility, but it would not repair a broken machine, feed a hungry child, or return lost time on its own. Theo suggested a temporary protection that could be checked after sunrise. They wrote down its limits in plain language and invited the people affected to challenge them.

一位證人問調查是否能讓明天更安全，米拉無法保證。真相能確立責任，卻不會自行修好機器、餵飽孩子或歸還失去的時間。西奧提出一項日出後可以檢驗的暫時保護措施。他們用白話寫清限制，邀請受影響的人提出異議。

The overlooked details in the witness file made the question harder, not easier: The witness tapes survived because Ning's mother had stored them in airtight metal cylinders. The engineering team could not attain a safe restoration protocol without testing volunteers' reactions first. A visiting congresswoman asked whether public grants could support a safer battery station without transferring ownership to a developer. One sealed ration crate still contained curry packets labeled for the train station's night workers. Mira had begun to despise the lie, but she refused to treat every frightened employee as an enemy. Theo marked what could be verified and left the rest open to challenge.

證人檔案裡被忽視的細節使問題更加複雜，而不是更簡單： 證人錄音帶能留存，是因為寧澄母親把它們裝在氣密金屬筒裡。 工程團隊若不先測試自願者反應，就無法達成安全的記憶恢復程序。 一名來訪女國會議員詢問，公共補助能否支援較安全的電池站，而不把所有權轉給開發商。 一箱密封口糧裡仍有咖哩包，標示是給車站夜班工人的。 米拉開始厭惡謊言，卻拒絕把每位害怕的員工都視為敵人。 西奧標記能查證的部分，其餘保留給人質疑。

For once the numbers did not merely decorate a report; they contradicted the order that had been issued in their name. The omissions follow the date when Haven rejected compulsory mind uploading. Theo asked whether a mistake could explain the result. Mira agreed to test that possibility too. They checked an independent source, marked every assumption, and made copies before the originals could be transferred to an office beyond public reach.

這一次數字不只是報告裝飾，而是直接反駁以數字名義發布的命令。遺漏從港灣拒絕強制意識上傳那天開始。西奧詢問是否可能只是出錯，米拉也同意檢查這種可能。他們核對獨立來源、標記每項假設，在原件被移往公眾無法接觸的辦公室前留下副本。

A second bundle of observations brought the consequences closer to ordinary life: The archived sky schedule described an eclipse that public lessons insisted had never occurred. Nari checked whether the old ration packs were still edible before placing them with the archive. Ivo's esteem for Mira grew when she chose to protect the witness names instead of seeking fame. A painted evergreen tree marked the maintenance tunnel that led away from the live network. A child asked whether a fish fin worked like the flap on the book box. Nothing in these records excused the people who had made the dangerous decision.

第二疊觀察紀錄使後果貼近普通人的生活： 封存的天空時刻表記載了一次日食，公共課程卻堅稱那從未發生。 娜莉把舊口糧一起封存前，先確認它們是否仍可食用。 米拉選擇保護證人姓名而非追求名聲，讓伊沃對她更為尊重。 一棵彩繪常綠樹標記著通往現行網路之外的維修隧道。 孩子問魚鰭是否像書箱的活門那樣運作。 紀錄中沒有任何一項能替做出危險決定的人開脫。

No one in the room was permitted to declare that a good intention canceled a harmful action. Mira insisted on recording the error, identifying who had authorized it, and protecting those affected before assigning wider blame. Theo drew a simple boundary around what they could do tonight. The line looked disappointingly modest, yet it was the first promise that did not require another person to surrender a right.

室內沒有人能宣稱善意可以抵銷傷害。米拉堅持記錄錯誤、確認授權者，並在追究更大責任前保護受害者。西奧畫出今晚能做到的清楚界線。這條線看似微不足道，卻是第一個不要求別人放棄權利的承諾。

The team kept the concrete observations alongside the disputed theory: A flake of snow settled on the driver's route sheet. The operator offered branded merchandise instead of the requested maintenance records. The monotony of the relay's steady hum made its sudden silence in the chamber feel frightening. Keeping paper travel diaries had once been a common pastime before the Authority discouraged writing. The original design required redundancy: two independent archives in case one system failed or was compromised. Taken alone, no detail settled the case; together they showed why a public answer mattered.

團隊把具體觀察與有爭議的理論並列保存： 一片雪花落在司機的路線表上。 營運商拿出品牌商品，卻沒有提供要求的維修紀錄。 中繼器單調不變的嗡鳴，讓維修室裡突如其來的寂靜格外可怕。 在管理局不鼓勵書寫以前，紙本旅行日記曾是常見消遣。 原始設計要求備援機制：兩套獨立檔案，防止其中一套故障或遭滲透。 單獨看來沒有一項細節能結案，合在一起卻說明了公開答覆為何重要。

Mira unlocked a cabinet with a metal key and checked the handwritten list of borrowers. The paper did not know whether the people named on it were fashionable, connected, or profitable. Theo could still recall the smell of the old library, though the cloud index insisted that the building had never stood there.

米拉用金屬鑰匙打開櫃子，核對手寫借書名單。紙張不在乎名單上的人是否時髦、連線或有利可圖。西奧仍記得舊圖書館的氣味，雖然雲端索引堅稱那棟樓從未存在。

The record continued with small facts that would be lost in a confident speech: Ning wanted to roam the lower tunnels, but the court order authorized only one chamber. The cleaning team asked visitors to put rubbish in the marked bins. An official poster promised to slay the 'monster of dreams,' turning ordinary sleep into an imagined enemy. A sly alteration in the tape label hid the passengers' true destination under a routine maintenance code. Cold vapor rose from the old cooling pipes as the preservation team opened the storage cabinet. Mira asked the clerk to preserve the original wording rather than improve the story.

紀錄接著列出那些在自信演說中容易消失的小事實： 寧澄想在下層隧道四處探查，但法院命令只允許進入一間維修室。 清潔團隊請訪客將垃圾投入標示清楚的桶子。 官方海報宣稱要殺死「夢的怪物」，把普通睡眠塑造成虛構敵人。 錄音帶標籤上的狡猾修改，把乘客真正目的地藏在例行維護代碼下。 保全小組打開儲存櫃時，舊冷卻管冒出冰冷水霧。 米拉要求書記保存原本措辭，而不是把故事修飾得更好聽。

On the way out, Mira passed people waiting for a result that would affect their meals, their homes, or their ability to say no. None of them had the luxury of treating the dispute as an intellectual puzzle. Theo paused to answer a child's practical question before they left. The answer was incomplete, but unlike the old official notice, it admitted the uncertainty out loud.

離開時，米拉經過等待結果的人群，這項結果將影響他們的餐食、家園或說不的能力。他們不能奢侈地把爭論當成智力謎題。西奧停下回答一名孩子實際的問題。答案並不完整，卻與舊公告不同：它坦白承認不確定之處。
