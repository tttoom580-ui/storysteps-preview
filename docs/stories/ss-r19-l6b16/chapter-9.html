沒有臉的訪客

A Visitor With No Face

最後一座離線城市，拒絕上傳人類 · 第 9 / 25 章 · 5000–6000 · 英中對照 · 校訂中

用互動版查詞、朗讀與收藏

The first sign of trouble was not an explosion or a scream. It was a familiar procedure producing a result nobody could defend. A cloud representative arrives through a projection that cannot display the faces of anyone standing in Haven. Mira checked the time, the witnesses, and the rule that authorized the action. The three did not agree. Outside, ordinary life continued, which made the error more frightening rather than less.

麻煩的第一個徵兆不是爆炸或尖叫，而是熟悉程序得出了誰也無法辯護的結果。雲端代表透過投影抵達，畫面卻無法顯示港灣任何人的臉。米拉核對時間、證人與授權規則，三者卻互相矛盾。外頭的平凡生活照常進行，反而讓這個錯誤更加可怕。

Mira unlocked a cabinet with a metal key and checked the handwritten list of borrowers. The paper did not know whether the people named on it were fashionable, connected, or profitable. Theo could still recall the smell of the old library, though the cloud index insisted that the building had never stood there.

米拉用金屬鑰匙打開櫃子，核對手寫借書名單。紙張不在乎名單上的人是否時髦、連線或有利可圖。西奧仍記得舊圖書館的氣味，雖然雲端索引堅稱那棟樓從未存在。

Theo arrived with a second account that neither confirmed nor dismissed the first. They compared the routes by which each record had reached the room, then placed the uncertainties beside the facts. Mira could feel the pressure to announce a solution before anyone became frightened. That pressure was precisely why a second witness mattered: a conclusion that could not survive an independent question would be dangerous, even if it happened to be right.

西奧帶來第二份說法，既未證實也未推翻第一份。兩人比對紀錄抵達房間的途徑，再把不確定之處和事實並列。米拉感受到眾人希望立即宣布解方，以免恐懼擴大的壓力。但也正因這種壓力，第二位證人才重要：無法通過獨立質問的結論，即使碰巧正確，也可能很危險。

The overlooked details in the witness file made the question harder, not easier: A teenager with acne said the clinic treated his ordinary discomfort as proof that dreams were dangerous. Only licensed staff could administer the sleep medication, yet automated records named absent employees. The Authority's brochure cited a medical study that no independent researcher could find. No private diary should remain in captivity inside an institution's archive. Officials tried to cram decades of different histories into one approved textbook chapter. Mira asked the clerk to preserve the original wording rather than improve the story.

證人檔案裡被忽視的細節使問題更加複雜，而不是更簡單： 一名長青春痘的少年說，診所把他的普通不適當成夢境有害的證據。 只有持照人員能施用安眠藥，系統紀錄卻列出當天缺席的員工。 管理局的宣傳手冊引用一項獨立研究人員誰也找不到的醫學研究。 私人日記不該被機構檔案室永久囚禁。 官員試圖把幾十年的不同歷史硬塞進一章核准教科書。 米拉要求書記保存原本措辭，而不是把故事修飾得更好聽。

The contradiction reached the table just as the meeting was about to end. Mira insists on a paper transcript of every promise before allowing negotiations. For a long moment no one spoke. Mira understood why silence felt comfortable; each answer threatened someone's settled account of how the world worked. Theo broke the stillness by asking who would be harmed if they simply filed the discovery away. No one could answer without naming a real person.

會議即將結束時，矛盾證據送上桌面。米拉要求每項承諾都有紙本逐字紀錄，才同意開始談判。一時之間無人開口。米拉明白沉默為何讓人舒服：任何答案都可能動搖某人對世界的既定理解。西奧打破沉默，問若把發現歸檔，究竟誰會受害。沒人能在不指名真實人物的情況下回答。

A second bundle of observations brought the consequences closer to ordinary life: The instructor could detach the floating strap without requiring another person's assistance. One swimmer described mild discomfort after the new bench was installed. The forthcoming parent meeting would examine school transfer records rather than children's private dreams. The side windows helped illuminate the entrance without harsh overhead lighting. The school kept a small jade token donated by a parent from the missing eastern district. Theo marked what could be verified and left the rest open to challenge.

第二疊觀察紀錄使後果貼近普通人的生活： 教練可以拆下浮具繫帶，不必仰賴他人協助。 一名泳者描述新長椅安裝後造成輕微不適。 即將舉行的家長會將檢查學校轉學紀錄，而不是孩子的私人夢境。 側窗有助照亮入口，又不必依賴刺眼頂燈。 學校保存著一枚小玉牌，是失蹤東區一名家長捐贈的。 西奧標記能查證的部分，其餘保留給人質疑。

There were two tempting shortcuts. One would protect the reputation of Director Vale and leave the public guessing. The other would expose every private detail at once and call that transparency. Mira rejected both. The issue was whether people have the right to remain real without joining a cloud, not whether the team could win an argument in a single afternoon. A smaller step, supported by independent observers, could protect people while the wider dispute remained open.

有兩種誘人的捷徑：一種保住維爾總監的名聲，讓大眾自行猜測；另一種把所有私人細節一次揭露，並稱之為透明。米拉兩者都拒絕。核心是人們能否不加入雲端也保有真實身分，不是團隊能否在一個下午贏得爭論。由獨立觀察者支持的一小步，即使爭議未結，也能先保護民眾。

The team kept the concrete observations alongside the disputed theory: Ning was joyous after entering the race, whatever result the timing board would show. The teacher taught media literacy by asking pupils how to tell a drawing from a verified historical record. An anonymous narrator on an old school tape described the blue door in the same words as the drawings. A child's nostril flared as she concentrated on her breathing before a first lesson. A map in the teacher's collection placed the missing railway on a peninsula outside the city wall. Nothing in these records excused the people who had made the dangerous decision.

團隊把具體觀察與有爭議的理論並列保存： 嘉寧報名比賽後滿心喜悅，不論計時板最後顯示什麼。 老師透過辨別圖畫與經驗證史料的差異，教授媒體識讀能力。 一卷舊學校錄音帶裡，匿名旁白用和畫作一樣的詞描述藍門。 孩子第一次上課前專注調整呼吸，鼻孔微微張開。 老師收藏的地圖把失蹤鐵路畫在城牆外一座半島上。 紀錄中沒有任何一項能替做出危險決定的人開脫。

Mira unlocked a cabinet with a metal key and checked the handwritten list of borrowers. The paper did not know whether the people named on it were fashionable, connected, or profitable. Theo could still recall the smell of the old library, though the cloud index insisted that the building had never stood there.

米拉用金屬鑰匙打開櫃子，核對手寫借書名單。紙張不在乎名單上的人是否時髦、連線或有利可圖。西奧仍記得舊圖書館的氣味，雖然雲端索引堅稱那棟樓從未存在。

The record continued with small facts that would be lost in a confident speech: The potent combination of censorship and repeated correction had distorted what many residents thought possible. The revival of local history did not require exposing private notebooks. The city promised a selective audit of contracts with the highest financial risk. One parent searched for a stepchild whose name appeared on the same vanished train list. The nearby river still held trout that children observed on school field trips. Taken alone, no detail settled the case; together they showed why a public answer mattered.

紀錄接著列出那些在自信演說中容易消失的小事實： 審查與反覆修正的強大組合，扭曲了許多居民對可能性的認知。 地方史的復興不必以公開私人筆記為代價。 市府承諾針對財務風險最高的合約進行選擇性稽核。 一名家長尋找自己的繼子女，名字出現在同一份消失列車名單上。 附近河川仍有鱒魚，孩子們校外教學時會觀察。 單獨看來沒有一項細節能結案，合在一起卻說明了公開答覆為何重要。

The chapter ended without an easy reassurance. Mira had learned one thing that could be checked and another that still needed an answer. Theo wrote both on the same page so that relief would not erase doubt. Their decision had moved the crisis forward, but it had also drawn the attention of Director Vale. By morning, the next question would reach people far beyond this room.

這一天沒有簡單的安慰。米拉知道了一件能查證的事，也留下另一件尚待回答的事。西奧把兩者寫在同一頁，免得鬆一口氣就抹掉疑問。他們的決定使危機向前，也引起維爾總監注意。明早下一個問題將傳到房間外更遠的地方。
