消失的街道

The Street That Disappeared

最後一座離線城市，拒絕上傳人類 · 第 1 / 25 章 · 5000–6000 · 英中對照 · 校訂中

用互動版查詞、朗讀與收藏

The library still stood on Orchard Street. Its books still smelled of glue and rain. But at 8:05 that morning, an ambulance passed the entrance three times because its navigation system insisted the street did not exist. Mira had been the building’s archivist for twenty years; she could trace every address by hand. She pressed the clasp of her old library card until it clicked against the reader. The card worked. The global map did not. Across the road, a small boy holding a toy wolf cub watched the ambulance circle. “If the computer cannot see my grandmother,” he asked, “can a doctor?”

圖書館仍站在果園街上。書本還散發著膠水與雨水的氣味。但早上八點零五分，救護車三次開過入口，因為導航堅持街道不存在。米拉擔任這棟建築的檔案員已二十年，徒手就能查出每個地址。她按緊舊借書證的扣環，直到讀卡器喀一聲作響。卡片仍能使用，全球地圖卻不行。馬路另一側，一名抱著狼幼獸玩偶的男孩看著救護車兜圈。「如果電腦看不見奶奶，」他問，「醫生還看得見她嗎？」

Director Vale sent a directive to every dwelling in Haven. Within seven days residents must upload their minds to the cloud, or the city would be removed from all automated routes. He offered to convene a consultation meeting only after the deadline. The company motto claimed, “No One Will Ever Be Forgotten.” Mira turned the phrase over in her mind. Was forgotten now a synonym for not profitable? A janitor from the nearby school brought her a paper list of missing road signs. The same company had removed the signs at night, then claimed the roads were impossible to verify.

維爾總監把指令送往港灣每一處住所。七天內居民必須把心智上傳雲端，否則城市將從所有自動路線中移除。總監提出的諮詢會議卻排在期限之後。公司座右銘聲稱：「永遠不會有人被遺忘。」米拉反覆思考這句話。現在的「被遺忘」是否等於「不會獲利」？附近學校的清潔員拿來紙本道路標誌失蹤清單。竟是同家公司夜間拆掉標誌，白天再宣稱道路無法驗證。

Mira walked to the hospital, where a dental clinic still accepted patients through a paper appointment book. A wheelchair remained stationary beside the door, waiting for a woman whose cloud insurance had been canceled. The clinic’s trustee showed Mira the denial message. It cited no legal precedent, only a new eligibility algorithm. Outside, a young pilgrim from a cloud settlement asked whether staying offline meant rejecting modern medicine. “No,” Mira answered. “It means asking whether we can receive medicine without giving someone every memory we own.” The pilgrim took a photograph of the paper appointment schedule.

米拉走到醫院，牙科診所仍用紙本預約簿接待病人。一張輪椅停在門口，等待雲端保險遭取消的婦女。診所受託人把拒保訊息給她看。裡頭沒有任何法律先例，只寫一套新的資格演算法。外頭，一名來自雲端聚落的年輕朝聖者問，維持離線是否等於拒絕現代醫療。「不是，」米拉回答，「而是問能否不必交出自己全部記憶也得到醫療。」朝聖者拍下紙本預約表。

From a childhood audio record, the library speakers released the sound of horses at a gallop. Mira had kept the recording because it made her brother Theo laugh when they were children. Theo had uploaded his mind fifteen years earlier. When she called him, he recognized the tune but not her name. The loss was enough to heighten her fear, yet she forced herself to record precisely what he remembered. His voice paused, searched, and then said that somewhere inside the cloud a forbidden file still held the names of offline relatives. The memory had not vanished; something was preventing access to it.

圖書館喇叭播放童年錄音，傳出馬匹奔馳聲。米拉一直留著錄音，因為兒時哥哥西奧聽見就會笑。西奧十五年前已上傳心智。如今她打電話給他，他認得曲子，卻想不起妹妹姓名。這件事加劇她的恐懼，她仍逼自己精確記下哥哥還記得什麼。他停頓、搜尋，然後說雲端深處仍有一個被禁止的檔案，存著離線親人的名字。記憶並未消失，只是有東西擋住存取。

A group of gardeners were trying to transplant fruit trees from a plot scheduled to disappear from the municipal register. The trees would still reap their share of sunlight and bear fruit next spring, but no truck could legally deliver the harvest if the location remained unrecognized. A council trustee promised unconditional help with the move, then admitted she could not restore the cloud route. Mira listened to both parts of the offer. In her work she had learned that goodwill was not a substitute for a functioning address. She asked the gardeners for their old planting maps and promised to preserve them, without asking anyone to surrender a private memory.

園丁們正試圖把果樹從一塊即將被市政登錄刪除的地移植出去。明年春天樹木仍會收穫自己的陽光並結出果實，但若位置不受承認，貨車就無法合法載走收成。議會受託人承諾無條件協助搬遷，後來卻承認無法恢復雲端路線。米拉把兩部分都聽進去。多年工作使她明白，善意無法取代真正有效的地址。她向園丁索取舊種植地圖，承諾妥善保存，卻沒有要求任何人交出私人記憶。

That evening, a news commentator described Haven as a city ruled by dictators because it still allowed people to live without accounts. Mira refused to utter a rehearsed denial. Instead she placed three items on the library desk: a paper birth record, a street map, and a receipt from the dental clinic. Each belonged to a person the cloud said did not exist. She opened a public notebook and invited residents to write only what they wished to share. Before the first name was entered, the wall screen flickered with a message from Theo: “The erasure is not an error. Someone wrote it as a rule.” Below the sentence appeared seven digits: the time remaining before the city was cut off.

當晚，新聞評論員稱港灣是由獨裁者統治的城市，理由是它竟允許居民不用帳戶生活。米拉拒絕說出事先排練的否認詞，反而在圖書館桌上放了三樣東西：紙本出生紀錄、街道地圖和牙科診所收據。每一件都屬於雲端聲稱不存在的人。她打開公共筆記，邀請居民只寫自己願意分享的內容。第一個名字還沒寫上去，牆上螢幕就閃出西奧的訊息：「抹除不是錯誤，是有人把它寫成規則。」下方出現七位數字，代表城市遭切斷前的剩餘時間。
