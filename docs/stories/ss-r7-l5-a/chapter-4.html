A storm forced the troupe to stop beside a warehouse with a broken driveway. Their freight truck could not enter, so everyone carried costumes across wet gravel. The delay gave the actors an unexpected hour of boredom, but Lan used it to ask about the old traveling stage. Zhu remembered when the company had provided temporary housing for performers who owned no home. Back then, its civic duty had seemed more important than selling tickets. He spoke with an eloquent anger that made even the younger actors listen. The missing agreement was not merely a piece of paper; it threatened the promises that had held the troupe together.

一場暴風雨迫使劇團停在一座倉庫旁，破損的車道讓貨運卡車無法進入，大家只好踏著濕石子搬運戲服。演員意外得到一小時無所事事的空檔，葉嵐則趁機詢問舊巡迴舞台的往事。朱緒記得，劇團從前會替沒有住所的演員安排臨時住處。那時候，大家認為公共責任比售票更重要。他帶著雄辯而憤怒的語氣說話，連年輕演員都仔細聽著。失蹤的協議不只是一張紙，它威脅著讓劇團團結至今的承諾。

The youngest actor, Pao, had taken medication for travel sickness but still felt dizzy. He went outside to vomit beside the tire tracks and returned pale with despair. Lan did not ask him to perform. She found water and asked Nara to check whether he needed medical attention. The producer complained that illness would intensify their financial problems, then stopped when Zhu showed her the written health guideline. It required rest when symptoms might make stage work unsafe. The producer reluctantly agreed to prolong rehearsal instead of forcing Pao through the scene. Protecting people, Lan thought, should not be treated as an innovative experiment.

最年輕的演員阿寶吃了暈車藥，還是頭暈。他走到車轍旁嘔吐，回來時臉色慘白，滿心絕望。葉嵐沒有叫他上台，而是找來水，請娜拉確認他是否需要就醫。製作人抱怨疾病會加劇財務困難，直到朱緒拿出書面健康指引。上面規定，當症狀可能影響舞台安全時，工作者必須休息。製作人不情願地同意延長排練，而不是強迫阿寶演出。葉嵐心想，保護人不該被當作新奇的實驗。

That evening the cast opened a tin box containing thirty years of photographs. A noticeable trait appeared in every picture: Mo was never placed in the center, although her stories had drawn the crowds. She stood at the edge, holding a lamp or helping a child step onto the platform. Nara said the placement reflected modesty; Lan wondered whether it reflected the troupe's old habit of hiding women's work. A disagreement about morality began to stimulate a more difficult conversation. Who decided which labor was important enough to name? The pictures could not settle that question, but they made it impossible to ignore.

當晚演員打開一只鐵盒，裡頭收藏了三十年的照片。每張照片都有個明顯特徵：雖然莫蓮的故事吸引觀眾，她卻從不站在中央，只站在邊緣提燈，或扶孩子上台。娜拉說那是她謙虛的表現，葉嵐卻懷疑，這也反映劇團長久以來隱藏女性勞動的習慣。關於道德的爭論激發了更艱難的對話：誰有權決定哪些勞動值得署名？照片無法回答，卻讓人再也不能忽視這問題。

The old notice also included a sketch of the theater door and a warning about the weak hinge. Zhu recognized it as Mo's handwriting. He remembered that she often noticed practical hazards before anyone in management. Lan added the drawing to the evidence log, careful to separate its safety message from the unresolved question about recording rights.

舊紙條還畫著劇院門的草圖，提醒門鉸鏈薄弱。朱緒認出那是莫蓮的筆跡。他記得莫蓮常比管理層更早注意實際危險。葉嵐把圖畫記入證據清單，注意區分它的安全提醒與尚未解決的錄音權利問題。

At the truck, Lan inspected the recording cases for water damage. The windshield was cracked, and a narrow strip of rain had entered through an unsecured window. Beneath the wet floor covering she found a small paper envelope marked with the title of Mo's first show. It contained a handwritten instruction: do not combine the workshop tapes with commercial music. The envelope was old, but the ink had not run. Lan photographed the placement before touching it. She also called the driver and the costume manager to witness the discovery. Finding a warning was important; finding who had put it there would be another question.

葉嵐到卡車旁檢查錄音箱是否進水。擋風玻璃有裂縫，一扇沒扣好的窗讓一道雨水滲入。她在潮濕的地板覆蓋物下找到一只小信封，寫著莫蓮第一場演出的名稱。裡頭有手寫指示：不要把工作坊錄音和商業音樂合併。信封雖舊，墨水卻沒有暈開。葉嵐先拍下位置才動手，也找司機和服裝管理員見證。找到警告很重要，但究竟是誰放在那裡，仍是另一個問題。

Zhu wanted to plead with the sponsor to return every recording. Lan refused to build the case on a plea alone. They needed to know whether the instruction was authentic, what contract followed it, and which files had actually traveled to the remote server. The sponsor might argue that the letter was outdated; the troupe might contend that its meaning had never changed. Neither side could win by shouting from opposite sides of a damaged truck. Lan made three copies of the photographed note and kept the original dry. Tomorrow the road would reopen, and their search would move with the stage.

朱緒想懇求贊助方歸還每一份錄音。葉嵐拒絕只靠請求建立案件。她們得知道指示是否真實、後來簽了什麼合約，以及哪些檔案實際送上遠端伺服器。贊助方可能主張信件過時，劇團則可能認為它的意思從未改變。站在破卡車兩側叫嚷，誰也贏不了。葉嵐把紙條照片複製三份，妥善保存原件。明天道路重開，調查也會隨舞台繼續前進。

Before turning out the lights, Lan walked once around the sleeping trucks. Rain had washed the gravel clean, but it had not restored the hours they had lost. Pao was resting safely, the costumes were dry, and the uncertain letter sat sealed beside her notebook. Those were modest accomplishments. She had once believed a good sound engineer should remain invisible, making everyone else audible. Now she was learning that invisible work could become dangerous when nobody recorded who had done it. She returned to her bunk with a sharper idea of what she needed to change.

熄燈前，葉嵐繞著沉睡的卡車走了一圈。雨水把石子沖乾淨，卻沒有還給他們失去的時間。阿寶已安全休息，戲服保持乾燥，那封未解的信也封在筆記本旁。這些都是微小的成果。她從前相信，好音效師應該隱身幕後，讓別人的聲音被聽見。如今她開始明白，當沒有人記錄工作者身分，隱形的勞動可能變得危險。她帶著更清楚的改變方向回到床鋪。
