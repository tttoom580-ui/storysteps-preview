A Petition with Room for Doubt

After the broadcast, the sponsor tried to accelerate negotiations. Its representatives complimented the troupe for a successful live performance, then demanded that all criticism stop. Lan suggested a minimal alternative: appoint a neutral examiner and identify each component of the disputed sound track. The company could deploy its own technician as an observer but not as the only authority. At the town hall, a veteran teacher helped collect a petition from audience members who wanted the original artists credited. The teacher insisted that names be voluntary and that nobody sign on behalf of a child. Lan was grateful for the discipline of that small rule.

轉播結束後，贊助方試圖加速協商。代表先稱讚劇團的現場表演成功，隨後要求停止一切批評。葉嵐提出最低限度的替代方案：任命中立檢查人，逐項辨識爭議音軌的組成。公司可以派技師旁聽，但不能成為唯一裁決者。市民會堂裡，一位資深教師協助收集觀眾請願書，要求正確標示原始藝術家的姓名。教師堅持簽名必須自願，也不可代兒童簽署。葉嵐感激這條小規則所展現的紀律。

The troupe's medical adviser saw signs of fatigue among several performers and recommended two days without rehearsal. The recommendation seemed inconvenient because the next city had already sold tickets. Zhu's hands began to quiver as he calculated the possible loss. He had built the company with his own hands and feared that it would not thrive without the sponsor. Lan explained that the institution was more than one contract, even if its finances were grim. Its strength came from people who could work safely and make informed decisions. A schedule that broke those people would save nothing in the long run.

劇團醫療顧問看到幾名演員出現疲勞跡象，建議停止排練兩天。下一座城市已賣票，這項建議顯得不方便。朱緒計算可能損失時，雙手開始顫抖。他親手建立劇團，害怕沒有贊助方就難以繁榮。葉嵐解釋，這個機構不只是一份合約，即使財務情況嚴峻也是如此。真正的力量來自能夠安全工作、做出知情選擇的人。長期而言，摧毀這些人的行程表根本救不了劇團。

Qing prepared a short technical specimen from a permitted twenty-second recording. Her specialty was identifying how different machines left distinct traces. She showed the examiner a faint click at the start and an unnatural cut just before the final breath. The sample was small, but the method could be repeated without exposing private conversations. The examiner warned that an audio signature was not a confession. Lan accepted the warning because the facts at stake were too important for an easy story. She wanted findings that could undergo serious criticism and still remain useful.

蘇青從獲准使用的二十秒錄音中製作短樣本。她的專長是辨識不同設備留下的獨特痕跡。她向檢查人展示開頭一聲輕微喀響，以及最後呼吸前不自然的剪接。樣本很短，方法卻可以重複，不必公開私人談話。檢查人提醒她，聲音特徵不等於認罪。葉嵐接受提醒，因為牽涉的事實太重要，不能講成過於容易的故事。她希望調查結論即使接受嚴厲批判，也仍有價值。

The examiner accepted the troupe's plan but added one condition: neither side could edit the sample after the comparison began. All copies would be made in the presence of witnesses, and the unused materials would be returned. Lan considered the condition reasonable. An investigation about permission should not quietly create another unauthorized recording.

檢查人接受劇團計畫，另加一項條件：比對開始後，任何一方都不得編輯樣本。所有副本須在見證人面前製作，未使用材料必須歸還。葉嵐認為合理。調查許可的過程，不應悄悄製造另一份未經授權的錄音。

During a lunch break, the performers debated whether the issue belonged to law, art, or ethics. One actor called the technical arguments trivial. Another said every disagreement was now a threat to friendship. Lan disagreed with both. Art lived in the realm of feeling, but a recording also had dates, owners, and permissions. Those details were not enemies of creativity. They helped someone decide whether their work could travel beyond the stage. The conversation ended without consensus, yet the participants agreed on one thing: the next meeting would be public, and its agenda would be circulated beforehand.

午餐休息時，演員爭論這件事究竟屬於法律、藝術還是倫理。有位演員認為技術爭議無足輕重，另一位則說每次意見不同都在威脅友誼。葉嵐兩者都不同意。藝術存在於情感領域，錄音卻同時有日期、權利人與許可範圍。這些細節不是創造力的敵人，而是幫助人決定作品能否離開舞台。大家沒有達成共識，但同意下次會議公開舉行，並事先發送議程。

That evening Mo sent a message to the entire troupe. She did not ask them to worship her past or punish everyone who had benefited from the tour. She wanted to know how the recording had been made and who could prevent its future misuse. Her words were plain and carried no threat. Lan read them twice. The public dispute had made Mo an image to many people, but the message returned her to the center as a living person with practical questions. Lan began drafting a careful request for independent technical access, with Mo named as a participant rather than as an object of study.

當晚莫蓮傳訊息給全劇團。她沒有要求大家崇拜她的過去，也沒要求懲罰所有曾從巡演受益的人。她只想知道錄音如何製作，以及誰能阻止未來濫用。她的話樸實，沒有威脅。葉嵐讀了兩遍。公開爭議讓許多人把莫蓮當成象徵，但這則訊息讓她重新成為有實際問題的活人。葉嵐開始草擬獨立技術查核申請，把莫蓮列為參與者，而不是研究對象。

Before sleep, Lan laid the evidence across a table: the damaged agreement, the access log, the butcher's receipt, and Mo's statement. She could see the outline of an explanation but not its final shape. There was still a missing decision between the archival recording and the broadcast track. Whoever made that decision might have believed they were saving the company. Belief, however, did not remove the need for permission. Lan switched off the lamp and resolved not to let the desire for a satisfying ending outrun the slow work of proof.

睡前，葉嵐把證據鋪在桌上：破損合約、進出紀錄、肉店收據與莫蓮聲明。她能看見解釋的輪廓，卻看不清最後形狀。檔案錄音與轉播音軌之間，仍缺少一項決策紀錄。做決定的人或許真以為自己在拯救劇團，但信念不能取代許可。葉嵐關燈，決心不讓自己對完滿結局的渴望超越緩慢的查證工作。
