Names on the Program

紅色母帶：誰有權借走她的聲音 · 第 5 / 25 章 · 4000–5000 · 英中對照

In the old market theater, Lan compared the cast attendance sheets with the program printed for the sponsor's gala. Mo's name appeared beside a contemporary arrangement she had never performed. The corporation had described the track as an artistic offering, but the wording implied that Mo had actively endorsed the project. Nara pointed out that a written endorsement, if one existed, would need to identify exactly what was approved. Lan underlined the troubling sentence and asked the manager for the underlying document. He called the issue controversial and refused to share it until the weekend, after the gala.

在老市場劇院裡，葉嵐比對演員出席表和贊助商晚會的節目單。莫蓮的名字出現在一段她從未演唱的當代編曲旁。公司把音軌稱作藝術獻禮，措辭卻暗示莫蓮曾親自背書。娜拉指出，就算有書面背書，也必須明確說明批准的內容。葉嵐畫線標記可疑句子，要求經理提供原始文件。他稱問題很有爭議，說要等到週末晚會結束後才願意分享。

At the rehearsal, a dancer showed Lan a purple bruise from hitting a poorly secured platform. The incident was not serious, but it changed the mood. Zhu admitted that he had ignored a warning because the sponsor demanded an impressive visual transition. He had begun to defy his own standards in order to keep the tour alive. Lan saw the irony: a company selling the essence of human performance had made its performers less safe. She asked him to stop the moving platform until a carpenter could test it. He agreed without insisting that his pride mattered more than the dancer's knee.

排練時，一名舞者讓葉嵐看她撞上未固定平台留下的紫色瘀青。傷勢不重，卻改變了氣氛。朱緒承認自己忽略過警告，因為贊助方要求令人驚豔的視覺轉場。他為了讓巡演繼續，竟開始違背自己的標準。葉嵐感到諷刺：一家公司販售人類表演的精髓，卻讓表演者更加不安全。她請朱緒停用移動平台，直到木工測試完成。他同意了，沒有讓自尊凌駕舞者的膝蓋。

The next meeting included a representative elected by each work segment: actors, drivers, musicians, technicians, and costume makers. This system could facilitate discussion, but it also revealed different perspectives. Some residents of the touring community depended on the sponsor for their first steady income. Others feared the loss of their work more than any lawsuit. The manager offered new outfits and a bonus for everyone who signed the revised agreement. Nara asked whether the bonus was available to those who wanted time to read it. He answered with a smile rather than a policy. Lan recorded both the question and the nonanswer.

下一場會議由各工作部門選派一名代表參加：演員、司機、樂手、技師和服裝師。這種制度有助討論，也揭露不同立場。巡迴社群裡有些人第一次有穩定收入，全靠贊助商；另一些人比起官司，更害怕失去工作。經理提出新戲服和獎金，條件是大家簽署修訂版合約。娜拉問，需要時間閱讀的人是否也能拿到獎金。經理回以微笑，卻沒有說明政策。葉嵐把問題與沒有回答這件事都記下來。

Some performers continued to contend that the company deserved wider permission because it paid the bills. Lan asked them to explain which exact recordings that argument would include. The room gradually moved from a contest of slogans to a comparison of particular uses. Even those who disagreed with her began to see why the wording of a license mattered.

部分表演者仍主張，公司既然負擔費用，就應取得更廣泛的許可。葉嵐請他們說明，這論點究竟涵蓋哪些錄音。會議逐漸從口號對抗，轉成比較具體使用情境。即使不同意她的人，也開始理解授權措辭為何重要。

Di Wen asked whether the troupe could produce a statistical summary of who owned which recordings. No one could. Their archive was a collection of boxes, receipts, and handwritten agreements scattered across decades. Lan proposed a simple table: recording date, creator, performers, permissions, and current storage. Zhu worried that such administration would destroy the ritual of passing songs from person to person. Lan replied that respect for a tradition did not mean leaving it unprotected. The transition from trust to documented consent could be difficult, but both might coexist. A recorded answer should strengthen a relationship, not replace it.

狄雯詢問劇團能否製作統計摘要，說明各段錄音的權利歸屬。沒有人辦得到。檔案只是數十年來散落的箱子、收據與手寫協議。葉嵐提議建立簡單表格：錄製日期、創作者、表演者、許可範圍和保存位置。朱緒擔心行政作業會毀掉口傳歌曲的儀式。葉嵐回答，尊重傳統不代表讓它毫無保護。從互相信任過渡到書面同意或許困難，但兩者可以並存。記錄答案應該加強關係，而非取代關係。

That night the cast watched a rough cut of the gala film. It opened with Mo's voice but ended on the sponsor's logo, leaving no space for the people who had written the words. Lan stopped the video and asked whether this overall presentation matched what the troupe wanted to offer. The room grew quiet. Pao, still recovering from his illness, said the screen made their work look like a product without workers. Zhu, for once, did not try to defend the old arrangement. He asked Lan to draft a proposal that would put the artists' names back in view, with a clear choice about future use.

當晚，演員一起看了晚會影片的粗剪版。影片以莫蓮的聲音開始，卻只在贊助商標誌上結束，完全沒有留下詞作者的名字。葉嵐停住畫面，詢問這種整體呈現是否真是劇團想提供的作品。房間安靜下來。仍在康復的阿寶說，螢幕把他們的工作變成沒有工作者的產品。朱緒這回沒有替舊安排辯護。他請葉嵐草擬提案，讓藝術家的名字重新出現，也讓每個人能明確選擇未來的使用方式。

Lan wrote until dawn. She resisted the temptation to make a sweeping accusation that the sponsor was stealing everything. Her evidence was still partial: a suspicious sound file, incompatible approval dates, and a torn agreement. She could state those facts and request an independent review. The first five days had changed the troupe. People who rarely spoke in meetings had begun asking to see contracts; people who loved the old director still questioned his decisions. The caravan would continue its journey, but not with the easy silence that had once passed for harmony.

葉嵐寫到天亮。她克制自己，不用「贊助商偷走一切」這類全面指控。證據仍不完整：一份可疑音檔、互相衝突的核准日期，以及撕裂的協議。她能陳述事實，要求獨立審查。前五天已改變劇團：過去鮮少在會議發言的人開始要求看合約；敬愛老團長的人也開始質疑他的決策。巡迴隊伍仍將上路，卻不會再用從前那種輕易的沉默假裝和諧。
