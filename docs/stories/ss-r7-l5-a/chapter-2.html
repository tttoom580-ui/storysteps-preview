A Contract Behind the Curtain

The next morning, Lan found the sponsor's proposed agreement in a filing box beneath the stage. It was absurdly short for a project involving voices, images, and public performances. One sentence granted the donor the right to use any rehearsal material without a time limit. That could have adverse consequences for performers whose work survived long after the tour ended. Lan highlighted the sentence and drew a diagram of the recording architecture: microphones, mixing desk, archive drive, and the sponsor's remote server. The final connection was new. Nobody in the troupe recalled approving it.

隔天早上，葉嵐在舞台下的文件箱裡找到了贊助商提出的合約。這份涉及聲音、影像與公開演出的文件短得荒謬。其中一句話允許捐助方無限期使用所有排練素材，對巡演結束後仍仰賴作品生活的表演者可能有不利影響。葉嵐畫線標出那句話，再畫出錄音架構：麥克風、混音桌、檔案硬碟，以及贊助商的遠端伺服器。最後一條連線是新加的，劇團裡沒有人記得批准過。

At breakfast, actor Nara complained that the sponsor's ideology reduced every play to a product. She had spent years learning songs from indigenous storytellers who allowed the troupe to perform particular verses but not to sell the recordings. Lan said the distinction mattered even when an outsider could not hear it. A local journalist named Di Wen arrived to cover the tour for a weekly paper. She practiced careful journalism and would not print an accusation based on rumor. She asked to see the original terms, and Lan showed her only the page that the troupe was authorized to share.

早餐時，演員娜拉抱怨贊助方的理念把每齣戲都當成商品。她曾向原住民族說書人學歌，對方只允許劇團演唱特定段落，不同意販售錄音。葉嵐說，即使外人聽不出差異，這項區別也很重要。本地記者狄雯來替週報採訪巡演。她奉行審慎的新聞工作，不肯只憑傳聞刊登指控。她要求看原始條款，葉嵐只展示劇團有權公開的那一頁。

Zhu rubbed his shoulders as though trying to massage away the anxiety. The tour had brought excessive work for an aging crew, and a broken contract could leave them stranded. Their quest was therefore larger than finding the person who had edited the recording. They needed a way to protect artists and still keep the lights on. Di pointed out a rip in the corner of the agreement. The missing section appeared in a different photograph taken six months earlier, but the handwriting at the edge was barely visible. That variation suggested at least two physical versions of the terms.

朱緒揉著肩膀，像想按摩掉焦慮。年邁的工作人員已承受過量工作，合約若破裂，整團人可能受困異地。他們的追查因此不只要找出編輯音檔的人，還得讓藝術家受到保障、舞台繼續亮燈。狄雯指出合約角落有一道撕裂痕。六個月前拍攝的另一張照片上，缺失部分仍在，但邊緣手寫字模糊不清。這項差異表示條款可能至少有兩份實體版本。

Lan found the sponsor's claim absurd for another reason: it treated a person's silence as a signature. She asked Di how long such a misunderstanding could persist when a contract traveled through many offices. Di said even careful editors could repeat a false description if nobody checked the source. They agreed to compare the oldest available copy before describing anyone's intentions.

葉嵐還覺得贊助方說法荒謬，因為它把人的沉默當作簽名。她問狄雯，一份合約在多個辦公室流轉時，這種誤解能持續多久。狄雯說，若無人查證來源，即使認真的編輯也可能重複錯誤描述。兩人同意先比較最舊副本，再談任何人的意圖。

When Lan played an old series of workshop recordings, Mo's real voice sounded rougher than the commercial track. In one story she described a salmon swimming upstream past a mill. The fish was not a symbol of endless success; it was tired, and sometimes it rested. The sponsor's edit removed every rest, making the tale render Mo as a cheerful machine. Lan wondered why such small changes troubled her so deeply. They did more than improve the sound. They altered the character of a living person while keeping her name attached.

葉嵐播放一系列舊工作坊錄音時，莫蓮真正的聲音比商業音軌粗糙得多。她在一篇故事中描述鮭魚逆流游過磨坊。那條魚不是永遠成功的象徵；牠會疲倦，有時也需要休息。贊助方剪掉了所有停頓，使故事把莫蓮呈現得像一部快樂的機器。葉嵐不明白微小修改為何如此刺痛自己。那些改動不只改善聲音，也在保留本人姓名的同時，改變一個活人的形象。

The sponsor's legal officer warned Lan that public complaints might invite prosecution for damaging the company's reputation. She replied that she had made no public allegation. She wanted a copy of the approval record and a schedule of where the edited tracks were used. The officer would not provide either, yet he promised the company had acted properly. Di wrote down his exact words instead of describing them as proof. By sunset she had found three different dates for the supposed approval. A contradiction was not a verdict, but it gave the inquiry a clear direction.

贊助商的法務人員警告葉嵐，公開抱怨可能招來損害商譽的刑事追訴。葉嵐回答自己尚未公開指控，只想索取核准紀錄與改編音軌的使用清單。法務人員不願提供，卻保證公司行事正當。狄雯逐字記下他的說法，沒有把保證當作證據。傍晚前，她找到三個不同的所謂核准日期。矛盾不是判決，卻為調查指明方向。

Lan stayed behind to close the hall. Nara brought her tea and asked why she would risk her job for an old recording. Lan answered that she was not defending nostalgia. A voice could carry labor, memory, language, and the right to refuse. If the company wanted to sell a new performance, it could commission one. It could not assume that silence meant permission. Outside, the tour buses started their engines. The next city was already expecting a show, and Lan had twenty-four hours to decide what she could safely tell the cast.

葉嵐留下來關閉會場。娜拉端茶給她，問她為何為一段舊錄音冒失業風險。葉嵐說，她守護的不是懷舊，而是聲音所承載的勞動、記憶、語言，以及拒絕的權利。公司若想販售新演出，可以另行委託製作，卻不能假定沉默等於許可。外頭巡演巴士發動了引擎，下一座城市已在等演出。葉嵐只剩二十四小時，決定哪些資訊能安全地告訴演員。
