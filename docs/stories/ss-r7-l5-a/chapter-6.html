The sponsor delivered an abrupt notice at the next station: any performer who refused the revised agreement would lose the following month's work. Lan carried the paper in her briefcase to a meeting with a theater workers' activist named Mara. Mara had spent years helping small companies along the cultural frontier between rural towns and prosperous cities. She said employment terms were not an act of exile, and neither should they be a tool for driving dissenters away. But she warned Lan not to be naive. The sponsor was a powerful commercial entity, and any response would need witnesses, lawful procedures, and a plan for people who could not afford to lose wages.

下一站，贊助商突然發出通知：拒絕修訂合約的表演者，下個月將失去工作。葉嵐把通知放進公事包，去見劇場工作者倡議人瑪拉。瑪拉多年來協助鄉鎮與富裕城市交界的獨立劇團。她說，聘僱條款不該成為放逐人的手段，更不能拿來驅逐異議者。但她提醒葉嵐別天真。贊助商是強大的商業實體，任何回應都需要見證人、合法程序，以及保護無法失去薪水者的計畫。

During the afternoon seminar, Mara asked participants to contemplate their obligations to each other. A lighting technician described an outlet that sparked whenever rain reached the old wall. A driver spoke of a pedestrian injured while helping unload a truck. The room was hostile at first, as if each complaint were an attack on the troupe. Mara explained that safety reports were not personal insults. An honest report could disrupt a dangerous routine and prevent a worse loss. She promised to supervise a shared record in which workers could describe incidents without revealing private medical details.

下午的研討會上，瑪拉請大家思考彼此的義務。燈光技師提到，雨水滲進舊牆時某個插座就會冒火花。司機談到一名行人幫忙卸貨時受傷。起初會議充滿敵意，大家把每件抱怨都當成對劇團的攻擊。瑪拉解釋，安全通報不是人身侮辱；誠實紀錄可以打斷危險慣例，避免更嚴重的損失。她承諾監督共同紀錄，讓工作者敘述事故，同時不暴露私人病歷。

An older actor brought a photograph from his first season. He had once played a masculine hero with a sword, though he was the least forceful person in the company. He laughed at the memory, then pointed to an empty place in the photograph. Mo had stood there before the image was cropped for publicity. The actor said she had no obligation to surrender her work merely because her face had been removed. Lan wrote down the observation but would not turn it into an allegation about the new recording. A cropped picture demonstrated a pattern of invisibility, not the identity of a thief.

一位年長演員帶來他第一季的照片。他曾演過持劍的陽剛英雄，實際上卻是團裡最溫柔的人。他笑著回憶，然後指出照片裡一個空位。宣傳照裁切以前，莫蓮原本站在那裡。老演員說，不能因為她的臉被剪掉，就認為她有義務交出作品。葉嵐記下這個觀察，卻不把照片直接當成新錄音案件的證據。被裁切的影像能說明被忽視的模式，不能證明誰偷走了檔案。

The health adviser checked Pao's injury and said no injection was needed. Rest and a simple dressing would be enough unless the symptoms changed. Lan asked that the note stay in the medical file rather than the sponsor's public report. A worker could receive care without having his private health information turned into an argument about his employment.

醫療顧問檢查阿寶的傷，說不需要注射，休息與簡單包紮即可，除非症狀改變。葉嵐請求把記錄放在醫療檔案，不放進贊助方公開報告。工作者應能接受照護，不必讓私人健康資訊成為僱用爭論的材料。

The sponsor offered to repay the cost of canceled tickets if the cast signed immediately. A manager explained that the organization had surplus funds for promotions but none for direct compensation. His numbers looked oddly selective. Lan asked for an independent accountant rather than demanding access to the whole company. The manager refused, then proposed a compromise involving a short-term contract and an outside observer. Mara advised the troupe to consider it only after everyone had seen the same version. The problem had grown beyond one sound file: it was about whether decisions could be made without hidden pressure.

贊助方表示，只要演員立刻簽約，就願意償還取消門票的費用。一名經理解釋，公司宣傳預算有盈餘，卻沒有直接補償的款項。他列出的數字看來很片面。葉嵐要求獨立會計師查核，而不是要求看公司所有帳。經理拒絕，接著提出短期合約和外部觀察員的折衷方案。瑪拉建議劇團先確保每個人都看到同一版本，再考慮接受。問題已超出一份音檔，變成決策能否免於隱藏壓力。

At dusk Pao tripped on a cable and bruised his thigh. The wound was small, but the old emergency cabinet contained only expired dressings. Zhu remembered when he had personally checked every box before a performance. Now he felt overwhelmed by tasks assigned from distant offices. Lan bought fresh supplies and posted a short inspection schedule. It was a practical act, not an answer to the contract dispute. Yet the players noticed. For the first time in weeks, someone had responded to their needs without asking them to sign anything. Their next rehearsal began with a quiet, collective sense of relief.

黃昏時，阿寶被電線絆倒，撞傷大腿。傷口不大，但舊急救箱裡的敷料早已過期。朱緒想起從前每場演出前都親自檢查器材，如今遠方辦公室交付的任務讓他喘不過氣。葉嵐買來新用品，貼上一張簡短檢查表。這是務實行動，並非合約爭議的答案，演員卻都看在眼裡。好幾週來，終於有人回應他們的需要，而不要求先簽任何東西。下一場排練在安靜的集體鬆口氣中開始。

Lan ended the week with two commitments. She would not pretend that the sponsor had no legitimate interests, and she would not treat the troupe's fear of unemployment as consent. She asked Mara to teach them how to keep records that another person could verify. A quick victory in a loud meeting would be comforting, but it could collapse at the first serious question. The people she hoped to protect needed more than a speech. They needed options, time, and enough evidence to decide for themselves which risks they were willing to take.

這一週結束時，葉嵐為自己立下兩項承諾：她不會假裝贊助方毫無合法利益，也不會把劇團害怕失業當成同意。她請瑪拉教大家如何留下可由他人查核的紀錄。在喧鬧會議裡迅速獲勝或許令人安心，卻可能被第一個嚴肅問題擊垮。她想保護的人需要的不只是一場演說，還需要選項、時間，以及足夠證據，讓他們自己決定願意承擔哪些風險。
