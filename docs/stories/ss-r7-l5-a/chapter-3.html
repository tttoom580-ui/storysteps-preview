In the next town, Lan met sound editor Su Qing, a cautious technician who had once worked for the sponsor. Qing carried a worn strap across her shoulder and a small recorder in her coat. She warned Lan that the sound files were bound by several agreements, none of which could be understood from a single signature. Before opening any private material, they consulted an attorney who specialized in performance contracts. The attorney advised them to separate the original audio from whatever an executive later commissioned. They could investigate technical differences without pretending to decide the legal outcome themselves.

抵達下一座城後，葉嵐見到謹慎的聲音剪輯師蘇青。蘇青曾替贊助商工作，肩上背著磨舊的帶子，外套裡放著小錄音機。她提醒葉嵐，音檔受到多份協議約束，不能只憑一個簽名判斷權利。她們先向專門處理表演合約的律師請教，再打開私人資料。律師建議區分原始錄音與主管後來委託的製作版本。她們可以調查技術差異，卻不應自行宣稱已得出法律結論。

Qing's first comparison was surprisingly simple. The old tape had three layers: Mo's beloved voice, the quiet breathing of an actor beside her, and a faint mechanical hum from the theater. The new file contained a biological rhythm that was too regular to be Mo's breathing. It could be an ordinary filter, Qing said, or an artificial reconstruction. A virtual model might imitate the surface of speech while missing the movement of a real body. Lan watched the waveform and tried not to mistake suspicion for certainty. They marked each difference for later testing.

蘇青的第一項比對出奇簡單。舊錄音有三層聲響：莫蓮深受喜愛的聲音、身旁演員輕微的呼吸，以及劇院隱約的機械嗡鳴。新音檔的生理節奏規律得不像莫蓮的呼吸。蘇青說，那可能只是普通濾鏡，也可能是人工重建。虛擬模型可以模仿語音表面，卻未必保留真實身體的動作。葉嵐盯著波形，努力不把懷疑當作確定。兩人標記每個差異，留待後續測試。

A rehearsal shuttle brought the cast to a municipal hall, where a director spoke with open aggression about delays. He treated the actors as prey to be moved around a schedule. Nara refused to hurry through a scene in which her character asked for safety. The director shrugged and accused her of narrowing the scope of the tour. Lan supported Nara. Realism in a story did not mean forcing a worker to surrender her own viewpoint. They agreed to rehearse without the sponsor's track, even though doing so might diminish the spectacular sound of the finale.

排練接駁車把演員送到市民禮堂，導演帶著明顯的敵意談論延誤。他把演員當成排程裡任人搬動的獵物。娜拉拒絕匆促演完角色尋求安全的場景。導演聳肩，指責她限縮巡演範圍。葉嵐支持娜拉：故事的寫實不代表工作者必須放棄自己的觀點。他們同意不用贊助方的音軌排練，即使結尾的華麗音效可能因此減弱。

When Qing asked what Lan expected to find, Lan could only shrug. The sound might have been changed with a simple editing tool, or the sponsor might have used a more complicated process. Qing reminded her that a test could eliminate possibilities without revealing the entire truth. They made room in the report for results that did not match their fears.

蘇青問葉嵐預期會找到什麼，葉嵐只能聳肩。聲音可能只經過簡單剪輯，也可能使用了更複雜的製作流程。蘇青提醒她，測試可以排除某些可能，不一定立刻揭露全部真相。她們在報告裡保留不符合原先恐懼的結果。

At lunch, Qing used two sheets of paper to explain the practical problem. An unedited voice recording contained a source and a date. A reconstructed voice added decisions about pitch, pace, and emotion, often by people who had never met the speaker. One could straighten a bent wire, she said, without claiming to have created the wire itself. Lan heard a clock tick behind the kitchen wall. Each tick reminded her that the team had only a few days before a nationwide broadcast. They needed proof of where the substitute track came from, not merely a convincing impression.

午餐時，蘇青用兩張紙解釋實際問題。未剪輯的人聲錄音有來源與日期；重建聲音則加上音高、速度與情緒的決策，而做決策的人可能從未見過說話者。她說，就像把彎掉的電線拉直，不代表創造了電線。葉嵐聽見廚房牆後的時鐘滴答作響。每一下都提醒她，全國轉播只剩幾天。她們需要的是替代音軌來源的證據，而不只是令人信服的感覺。

That afternoon Qing found an odd marker in the left channel, a soft click at precisely the same point in three different recordings. The click belonged to a reel machine retired years ago. Mo had used that machine at her earliest performances, but the sponsor said its new track had been recorded last month. Lan felt a brief rush of excitement, then forced herself to be cautious. The click proved that old material had entered the production process. It did not show whether Mo had allowed it. The attorney told them to preserve the full file and refrain from making a public conclusion.

當天下午，蘇青在左聲道找到一個奇怪標記：三份不同錄音在完全相同的位置出現柔和的喀聲。那是多年前停用的盤式錄音機所留下的聲響。莫蓮早期演出曾使用那台機器，贊助商卻聲稱新音軌是上個月才錄製的。葉嵐一時興奮，隨即提醒自己保持謹慎。那聲喀響只能證明製作過程用到了舊素材，不能證明莫蓮是否同意。律師請她們保留完整檔案，暫不公開結論。

When the night show ended, the performers formed a circle around Lan and Qing. Nobody demanded that the sponsor be punished immediately. Instead, they asked for a narrow rule: no new voice replacement until the person whose voice was used could review it. Zhu recorded the proposal in the rehearsal notebook, alongside the names of people who agreed and those who wanted more information. The disagreement remained visible. Lan was grateful for that. A record of honest uncertainty, she thought, was more useful than a unanimous statement produced under pressure.

晚場結束後，演員們圍在葉嵐和蘇青身邊。沒有人要求立刻懲罰贊助商，他們只是提出一項有限規則：在聲音本人審閱前，不得再替換新音軌。朱緒把提案寫進排練本，也記下同意者與希望知道更多資訊者。分歧被如實保留。葉嵐很感激：誠實記錄不確定性，比在壓力下製造全體一致的聲明更有用。
