聆聽室

The Room for Listening

紅色母帶：誰有權借走她的聲音 · 第 8 / 25 章 · 4000–5000 · 英中對照 · 校訂中

The archive established a small listening booth for the independent review. Before entering, each participant received a written caution about the ethical treatment of private recordings. The sole purpose was to compare permitted samples, not to generate a new voice. Su Qing dedicated the first hour to checking the machines. A technician adjusted the interior panels to improve clarity while Lan recorded every setting. The review delegation included a performer, a sound engineer, a community observer, and a lawyer. They had different responsibilities, but none had the authority to make a secret copy.

檔案館為獨立審查設置一間小聆聽室。每位參與者入內前都收到關於私人錄音倫理處理的書面提醒。唯一目的是比對獲准使用的樣本，不是生成新的人聲。蘇青用第一個小時檢查設備。技師調整室內板材提高音質清晰度，葉嵐則記下每項設定。審查代表團包括表演者、音效師、社區觀察員及律師。眾人職責不同，沒有任何人有權秘密複製檔案。

The sponsor's representative argued that a voice had become a commodity, just like a costume design or lighting patent. The lawyer disagreed with that broad claim. Permission depended on specific rights, and the existence of a technical patent did not automatically transfer the work of a performer. She asked the representative to identify the document on which he relied. He produced a rental invoice for the recording venue instead of a license to use Mo's voice. Lan took care not to ridicule him. Confusing a room with its contents was a mistake; it was not yet proof that anyone had acted dishonestly.

贊助商代表主張，聲音如今已成為商品，就像戲服設計或燈光專利。律師不同意這種廣泛說法。許可取決於具體權利，技術專利也不會自動轉移表演者的作品。她請代表指出依據的文件。他拿出的是錄音場地租用發票，而非使用莫蓮聲音的授權。葉嵐小心不嘲笑他。把房間和房間內的內容混淆是一種錯誤，尚不足以證明有人故意不誠實。

During the next test, a dreadful scraping sound rose from the right speaker. The technician turned the machine off rather than trying to steer through the noise. She discovered a damaged connector that could shed metal dust into the socket. The repair was an ordinary chore, yet it delayed the inspection for an hour. The sponsor accused the team of wasting a premium facility. Qing calmly explained that poor equipment could generate false differences between tracks. If they wished to explore the truth, they had to resist the superstition that expensive machines were always accurate.

下一次測試時，右側喇叭發出刺耳可怕的刮擦聲。技師沒有硬撐著繼續，而是關掉機器，發現一個損壞接頭可能掉落金屬粉塵到插座。修理只是普通雜務，卻讓檢查延後一小時。贊助商批評團隊浪費昂貴設施。蘇青平靜解釋，故障設備可能製造音軌間的假差異。若要探索真相，就不能迷信昂貴機器一定準確。

The student's exploration of the waveform became a useful teaching exercise. She could explain how one visible mark corresponded to a breath or a cut, but she also learned what the diagram could not reveal. The room discussed how measurements should support testimony rather than replace it, and why everyone needed access to the same test conditions.

學生對波形的探索成了有用的教學練習。她能說明圖上的標記如何對應呼吸或剪接，也學會哪些事無法從圖表看出。大家討論測量應如何支持證詞而非取代證詞，以及為何每位參與者都應取得相同的測試條件。

An undergraduate from the local music school volunteered to prepare a listening chart. She wrote down where the performance contained breaths, pauses, and changes of dialect. Lan appreciated her fluency with the software but asked her not to add emotional labels that listeners might mistake for facts. The chart showed sound patterns, not intentions. A listener might find one voice gorgeous and another ordinary, yet beauty could not determine ownership. The student revised her report with that distinction in mind. By afternoon, the team had an objective list of differences that another examiner could repeat.

本地音樂學院的一位大學生自願製作聆聽圖表。她標出表演中的呼吸、停頓和方言變化。葉嵐欣賞她熟練操作軟體，卻請她不要添加容易被誤認為事實的情緒標籤。圖表顯示的是聲學模式，不是意圖。聽眾可以覺得一種聲音華麗動人，另一種平凡，卻不能以美感判定權利。學生依此修訂報告。下午，團隊已整理出一份可讓其他檢查者重複驗證的客觀差異清單。

At closing time the custodian brought a small card from a storage drawer. It listed an unpaid utility fee, a rehearsal time, and the name of a forgotten studio. On the back, somebody had written, 'Ask her before opening the red reel.' Lan recognized the handwriting as Zhu's, though she did not yet know why he had left it there. She showed him the card without accusation. His face changed. He said he had been afraid of that reel for years, not because it contained a scandal but because he had failed to keep a promise.

閉館時，保管人從抽屜取出一張小卡。上面記著未付的水電費、排練時間與一間被遺忘的工作室名稱。背面有人寫道：「打開紅色母帶前，先問她。」葉嵐認得那是朱緒的筆跡，卻不知道他為何把卡片留在那裡。她沒有指控，只把卡片交給他看。朱緒臉色一變。他說自己多年來一直害怕那卷母帶，不是因為裡面藏著醜聞，而是因為他曾失信於人。

Lan did not press him in front of the others. She closed the listening room, checked its seal, and offered Zhu a quiet conversation outside. He declined for the moment and asked to speak with Mo first. It was an uncomfortable answer, but Lan respected it. The investigation could not become an excuse to force private memories into public view. She wrote one sentence in her notebook: evidence can be urgent without making another person's dignity disposable. Then she prepared to seek Mo's own account, knowing that it might change everything they had assumed.

葉嵐沒有當著眾人逼問。她關好聆聽室、檢查封條，邀朱緒到外面私下談談。他暫時拒絕，說想先和莫蓮談。這答案令人不舒服，葉嵐仍予尊重。調查不能成為強迫私人記憶公開的藉口。她在筆記本寫下一句話：證據即使急迫，也不能使他人的尊嚴變得可以拋棄。接著她準備親自尋找莫蓮，知道這可能改變所有既有假設。
