The national broadcast was scheduled beneath the dome of the restored municipal theater. An entrepreneur from the sponsor's technology sector unveiled a bronze microphone as a symbol of the initiative. He claimed his team's advances in signal processing could sustain the voices of artists for generations. Lan did not oppose conservation. She opposed presenting an invented speech as if the speaker had chosen every word. The company's demonstration integrated old syllables into new sentences, some of them ridiculous enough to make the audience laugh. The laughter faded when Mo's name appeared beneath a promise she had never made.

全國轉播安排在修復後市立劇院的圓頂下。贊助方科技產業的創業者揭開一支青銅麥克風，作為計畫象徵。他宣稱團隊的訊號處理技術能讓藝術家的聲音延續數代。葉嵐不反對保存，她反對把發明出來的話說成發聲者自行選擇的每一字。公司的示範把舊音節拼進新句，有些荒謬得讓觀眾發笑。當莫蓮的名字出現在她從未作出的承諾下方，笑聲便停了。

In the lobby, an anonymous note appeared beside a vase: 'The key was not stolen. Ask the butcher.' Lan resisted the temptation to treat this as a revelation. The note might be a joke, a warning, or a deliberate attempt to betray someone. Di Wen advised that credibility depended on testing a lead, not on the drama of receiving one. They visited the butcher shop that had once rented a storage room to the troupe. The owner remembered a delivery of reels and a box of cables. He had kept a receipt because the sponsor paid late. It bore a location code matching the archive's temporary entry.

大廳裡一只花瓶旁出現匿名紙條：「鑰匙不是偷的。去問肉店老闆。」葉嵐克制自己，不把它當成重大揭露。紙條可能是玩笑、警告，也可能是刻意陷害別人。狄雯說，可信度來自驗證線索，不來自收到線索時的戲劇性。她們前往以前租儲藏室給劇團的肉店。老闆記得有人送來母帶與電線箱，因贊助商付款太晚，他保留了收據。上面的地點代碼與檔案館臨時進出紀錄吻合。

The receipt did not identify the person who transported the files. The butcher remembered a small truck but could not describe the driver. Lan asked whether he would testify only about what he personally saw. He agreed. Meanwhile the company's legal team circulated an arrogant message claiming that anyone questioning the project was hostile to science. Qing replied that a machine's ability to imitate speech was not a question of genetics or molecules; consent belonged to people. Even if the model were technically remarkable, that would not make every use of it legitimate. They prepared a technical report with clear distinctions between capability, permission, and purpose.

收據沒有寫誰運送檔案。肉店老闆記得一輛小卡車，卻記不清司機模樣。葉嵐問他是否願意只就親眼所見作證，他同意了。同時，公司法務團隊發布傲慢聲明，稱質疑計畫的人都反對科學。蘇青回答，機器模仿語音的能力不是基因或分子的問題，同意權屬於人。即使模型技術卓越，也不代表任何用途都正當。她們準備技術報告，清楚區分能力、許可與目的。

The old theater once hosted a documentary about abortion rights and a lecture on protecting wildlife. Lan used those programs to illustrate how performers might authorize one recording without agreeing to every future use. A vertical chart in the lobby showed how different audiences could reach the same work through live shows, schools, and broadcasts. Each route required its own questions about context.

老劇院曾放映關於墮胎權的紀錄片，也辦過野生動物保護講座。葉嵐用那些節目說明，表演者可以同意某次錄音，卻不必同意未來所有使用。大廳裡一張縱向圖表顯示，同一作品可經現場演出、學校與轉播接觸不同觀眾。每條途徑都有各自的情境問題。

A producer worried about the mortgage on his family home and begged Lan to withdraw the complaint. She did not dismiss his fear. She asked him what income the gala had guaranteed and which payments depended on Mo's voice. He could not answer, because the contract listed only a large general fee. Lan proposed that the troupe separate the live performance from the disputed recording. The actors could still earn wages while the audio question remained open. The sponsor called the suggestion inefficient. Lan thought it was more reasonable than forcing a choice between complete surrender and total cancellation.

一名製作人擔心家裡的房貸，懇求葉嵐撤回申訴。她沒有輕視他的恐懼，而是問晚會保證多少收入、哪些款項依賴莫蓮的聲音。他答不出來，因為合約只列一筆籠統費用。葉嵐提議把現場演出與爭議錄音分開。演員仍可領薪，聲音問題則繼續調查。贊助方認為這樣沒效率，葉嵐卻覺得，比迫使人們在完全屈服與全部取消間選擇，更為合理。

On the morning of the broadcast, the sponsor unexpectedly canceled the demonstration of the synthetic voice. The decision did not mean the company admitted wrongdoing. It meant that Mo's written objection, the receipt, and the incomplete approval timeline had made immediate use too risky. Lan watched technicians remove the bronze microphone from its stand. She felt relief, then recognized how much work remained. Files still existed on remote machines; performers still needed wages; and Zhu still had not explained the warning on the red reel. The first urgent crisis was contained, but the larger inquiry had only begun.

轉播當天早晨，贊助方突然取消合成聲音示範。這不代表公司承認違法，而是莫蓮的書面異議、收據與不完整的核准時間線，使立即使用的風險太大。葉嵐看著技師把青銅麥克風從支架拆下。她先感到放心，隨即知道還有多少事未完成。遠端機器裡仍有檔案；演員仍需要薪水；朱緒也還沒解釋紅色母帶上的警告。第一場急迫危機暫時止住，真正的大調查才剛開始。

That evening the troupe performed with living voices. They made mistakes, laughed at a missed cue, and took a breath whenever a story needed silence. The show lacked the sponsor's flawless effect, but the children listened closely. Lan stood at the mixing desk and heard the small signs of presence she had almost forgotten to value. Nobody onstage made a speech about technology. Instead, a performer asked the audience to remember that every voice has a person behind it. When the curtain fell, Lan finally allowed herself to smile without confusing one successful evening with a complete victory.

當晚，劇團使用真人聲音演出。他們會出錯、會笑場，也會在故事需要沉默時停下來呼吸。演出少了贊助方無瑕的效果，孩子們卻聽得入神。葉嵐站在混音桌旁，聽著那些自己差點忘記珍惜的生命痕跡。台上沒有人對科技發表長篇演說，只請觀眾記得每個聲音背後都有一個人。落幕時，葉嵐終於笑了，卻沒有把一晚順利當成整場戰役的勝利。
