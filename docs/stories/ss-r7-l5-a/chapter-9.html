墓園裡的女演員

The Actress at the Cemetery

Mo Lian was not hiding in a distant city. She was tending flowers at a cemetery outside the old theater district. Lan found her kneeling beside a stone, using a small mint-green watering can. For a moment Lan considered approaching from the rear, then chose to stand where Mo could see her. She explained her role and asked for ten minutes. Mo said she had stopped answering anonymous calls after reporters made her life unbearable. She would speak as a citizen, not as a monument to an earlier age of theater. Lan accepted that condition before mentioning the disputed audio.

莫蓮並沒有躲在遙遠城市。她正在舊劇院區外的墓園澆花，跪在墓石旁，手裡拿著薄荷綠的小水壺。葉嵐一度想從後方走近，最後仍選擇站在莫蓮看得見的地方。她說明身分，請求十分鐘時間。莫蓮說，記者曾讓她生活不堪負荷，所以不再接匿名電話。她願意以公民身分談話，而不是作為舊時代劇場的紀念碑。葉嵐先接受條件，才談到有爭議的錄音。

Mo listened to the first thirty seconds. She did not snatch the headphones away or embrace Lan with gratitude. Instead, she asked for the recording manifest, including the dates and the people allowed to access it. Her response surprised Lan. Mo had once studied citizenship law while working as a traveling actress; she knew that anger and documentation were not opposites. She had also joined an expedition to collect disappearing regional songs, and she understood how easily a familiar sound could be taken out of its original setting. The new recording sounded like her, she said, but it did not feel like a choice she had made.

莫蓮聽了前三十秒。她沒有搶下耳機，也沒有感激地擁抱葉嵐，而是要求查看錄音清冊，包括日期與有權接觸的人。這反應令葉嵐吃驚。莫蓮巡演期間曾研讀公民法，明白憤怒與文件並不衝突。她也參加過採集即將失傳地方歌曲的遠征，知道熟悉的聲響多容易被脫離原本情境。她說新錄音確實像她，卻不像是她自己做出的選擇。

They sat on a bench near the cemetery gate. Mo described a rehearsal twenty years earlier when a sponsor had requested a compulsory recording session. The session was canceled after she objected. She remembered a performer who tried to distract her with jokes while another person offered a new contract. She had refused it. Lan asked whether Mo had any surviving copy. Mo pointed to a battered metal box at her feet, then explained that she kept the papers for sentimental reasons rather than for a lawsuit. She was wary of reopening old arguments, but she did not want her silence to be mistaken for approval.

她們坐在墓園大門旁的長椅上。莫蓮談起二十年前一次排練，當時贊助者要求她參加強制錄音，她反對後活動取消。她記得一名表演者用笑話分散她的注意力，另一人遞上新合約，她拒絕了。葉嵐問是否留下副本。莫蓮指向腳邊破舊金屬盒，說她保存文件是出於感情，不是為了打官司。她不願再開啟舊爭執，也不希望沉默被誤解為贊同。

A former stage ally called to warn Lan that someone had described her questions as a personal assault on the sponsor. She asked him to read the message exactly. Its exterior envelope bore no signature, and the letter made no verifiable allegation. Lan placed it in her case and went to a nearby hotel lounge to call Mara. She wanted advice, not another rumor to repeat.

一位昔日舞台盟友來電警告葉嵐，有人把她的提問描述成對贊助商的人身攻擊。她請對方逐字讀出訊息。信封外部沒有署名，內文也沒有可查證的指控。葉嵐把信收進資料夾，到附近旅館休息廳打電話請教瑪拉。她要的是建議，不是又一則準備轉述的流言。

The box contained programs from a premier performance, letters with dried flowers, and a photograph of the troupe gathered beneath a painted galaxy. In the picture, Mo stood beside Zhu with a microphone. She smiled faintly at the memory. Their friendship had been strong, she said, and its collapse had left a stink of bitterness that lasted longer than the original quarrel. Lan did not ask her to name a villain. Instead, she requested permission to photograph only the relevant pages and to keep the rest private. Mo nodded after reading the proposed limits carefully.

盒子裡有首演節目單、夾著乾燥花的信，以及劇團站在彩繪銀河布景下的合照。照片中莫蓮拿著麥克風，站在朱緒身旁。她回想往事，微微一笑。她說兩人友誼曾很堅固，破裂後留下的苦味比原本的爭執還長久。葉嵐沒有請她指出壞人，只請求拍攝相關頁面，其他內容則繼續保密。莫蓮仔細看過限制後點頭同意。

On the walk back, Lan stopped at a food stall selling tuna sandwiches. She was too tired to squat beside the street boxes as the other workers did, so she ate standing up. An eager producer called to say the sponsor had made the highest bid for an exclusive broadcast. The offer sounded generous, but its deadline was that evening. Lan told him the troupe could not responsibly agree before reviewing the rights. He warned that a rival company would seize the opportunity. Lan answered that speed was not the only measure of a worthwhile decision.

回程途中，葉嵐在賣鮪魚三明治的攤位停下。她累得無法像其他工人那樣蹲在街邊箱子旁，便站著吃。製作人來電說，贊助商已為獨家轉播提出最高出價，條件看似優厚，期限卻是當晚。葉嵐告訴他，在檢查權利之前，劇團不能負責任地同意。他警告競爭公司會搶走機會。葉嵐回答，速度不是判斷決策是否值得的唯一標準。

Back at the theater, Mo's limited statement arrived by courier. She had never approved the replacement voice, but she was open to hearing a proposal for a new performance if she retained the right to refuse. The statement was narrow, direct, and written in her own hand. Lan stored it separately from the old letters. For the first time the team had a living participant's account to compare with the sponsor's timeline. It did not solve the entire question of ownership, yet it changed the next action: the gala could no longer proceed as though Mo had freely agreed.

回到劇院後，快遞送來莫蓮有限範圍的聲明。她從未批准替代人聲，但願意聽取新演出的提案，前提是保有拒絕權。聲明簡短直接，由她親筆書寫。葉嵐把它與舊信分開保存。團隊第一次有了當事人的現身說法，可以對照贊助商時間線。這仍無法解決全部權利問題，卻改變了下一步：晚會不能再假裝莫蓮早已自由同意。
