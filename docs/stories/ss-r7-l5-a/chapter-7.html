Who Keeps the Reels?

紅色母帶：誰有權借走她的聲音 · 第 7 / 25 章 · 4000–5000 · 英中對照 · 校訂中

The caravan reached a city famous for its underground theaters and excellent cuisine. Lan went first to the storage facility rather than the stage. An employee displayed an array of sealed reels behind a glass wall, each marked with a date and recipient. The collection seemed indispensable to the troupe's history, but no one could explain how the sponsor had obtained custody of the oldest tapes. The facility's director said the documents would be released only if the company approved their removal. Lan asked him to identify which company owned the physical reels and which held permission to reproduce the voices. He could answer neither question.

巡迴隊伍抵達一座以地下劇場和精緻料理聞名的城市。葉嵐先去倉儲設施，而不是舞台。員工隔著玻璃牆展示一排封存母帶，每卷都有日期和收件者。這些收藏看來是劇團歷史不可或缺的一部分，卻沒有人能解釋贊助商如何取得最舊錄音的保管權。主管說，只有公司批准移除，文件才能釋出。葉嵐要求他說明誰擁有實體母帶、誰獲准複製人聲。他兩個問題都答不出來。

The manager accused Lan of trying to pirate valuable recordings. She answered that wanting to inspect a record was not the same as copying or selling it. Su Qing offered her technical expertise to conduct a supervised comparison without disconnecting any storage device. The director objected that the equipment formed part of the theater's infrastructure and could not be touched. Mara proposed a narrower method: an independent custodian could open one container while witnesses observed from the corridor. Such an arrangement would not settle the controversy, but it could prevent the handling process itself from becoming another dispute.

經理指責葉嵐企圖盜錄有價值的錄音。她回答，要求查看紀錄不等於複製或販售。蘇青提出技術專長，願意在監督下比對而不拔除儲存設備。主管反對，說設備屬於劇場基礎設施，不可觸碰。瑪拉提出更有限的方式：由獨立保管人開啟一個容器，見證者在走廊觀察。這種安排無法解決爭議，至少可以避免保管過程成為新的爭端。

Inside the first box, they discovered an invoice for a sturdy shipping case and an insurance declaration. The sponsor had paid for transportation, not necessarily for artistic rights. Lan derived a simple timeline from the dates. The troupe had stored the tapes there long before the sponsor entered the project; the new contract came years later. The evidence did not prove corrupt behavior, but it weakened the assumption that storage fees granted control over every use. Di Wen agreed to describe the distinction carefully, even though a sensational story would attract more readers.

第一個箱子裡有堅固運輸箱的發票與保險聲明。贊助商付的是運送費，不必然是藝術權利。葉嵐依日期推導出簡單時間線：劇團早在贊助商加入計畫前多年，就把母帶存放於此；新合約是後來的事。證據不能證明有人貪腐，卻削弱了「付保管費就能控制所有用途」的假設。狄雯同意仔細說明這項區別，即使聳動新聞更能吸引讀者。

Qing invited Lan to walk through the archive once more before the boxes were sealed. The shelves held instruments, notes, and letters from people the troupe had long forgotten. Preserving them was not only about old songs. It was a way to let future performers understand how decisions had been made, including the decisions that turned out badly.

封箱前，蘇青邀葉嵐再走一次檔案庫。架上放著被劇團遺忘許久的人留下的樂器、筆記與信。保存它們不只為了舊歌，也讓未來表演者理解當初如何做決定，包括那些後來證明錯誤的決定。

A box of costumes beside the archive smelled of damp wool. An employee warned that mold might infect the paper labels, so Zhu volunteered to move only those containers that were not evidence. He and the custodian made a separate list of anything relocated. Lan was surprised by how much ordinary work an inquiry required. Recording dates, cleaning shelves, checking humidity, and answering questions were not glamorous tasks, but they kept evidence reliable. After a long afternoon she understood why custody was more than a legal word. It described the practical care that allows a stranger to trust what a box contains.

檔案旁一箱戲服散發潮濕羊毛味。員工警告黴菌可能感染紙質標籤，朱緒於是自願只搬動非證物的箱子。他和保管人另外列出所有移動物品。葉嵐驚訝於調查竟需要這麼多日常工作：記日期、清理架子、檢查濕度、回答問題。這些工作並不耀眼，卻能讓證據可靠。一整個下午後，她理解保管權不只是法律用語，更是讓陌生人信任箱內物品的實際照護。

That evening a local theater league hosted a friendly tournament of short plays. The troupe had promised to participate, and Nara argued that keeping their promise would be worthwhile. Their scene aroused laughter, then discomfort, as a young character asked whether a recorded apology could replace a real one. The audience heard the difference between a borrowed phrase and an answer spoken freely. After the show, a retired teacher offered to help organize the archive. Lan accepted the offer but made clear that volunteers would not receive private recordings without consent. Good intentions still needed boundaries.

當晚，本地劇場聯盟舉辦短劇友誼競賽。劇團曾答應參加，娜拉認為守信值得。她們的短劇先引起笑聲，再讓觀眾不安：一個年輕角色問，預錄的道歉能否取代真正的道歉。觀眾聽出了借來的話與自願回答之間的差別。演出後，一位退休教師願意幫忙整理檔案。葉嵐接受，但說清楚志工不得在未經同意下取得私人錄音。良好的動機仍需要界線。

On the bus, Qing showed Lan the facility's access log. It contained an entry made at dawn on the very day the substitute voice was first delivered. The entry did not list a person, only a temporary code. Lan placed it beside the three conflicting approval dates. The pattern was growing stronger, but still incomplete. She asked Qing what kind of result would convince her that they were wrong. Qing answered without hesitation: a verifiable recording session with Mo's participation and a matching consent record. They wrote that possibility down too, leaving room for an explanation they had not yet found.

回程巴士上，蘇青向葉嵐展示倉庫進出紀錄。替代人聲首次送達的那天清晨，正好有一筆進出紀錄，卻只列臨時代碼，沒有姓名。葉嵐把它放在三個互相矛盾的核准日期旁。模式愈來愈明顯，卻仍不完整。她問蘇青，怎樣的結果會使她承認自己的懷疑錯了。蘇青毫不遲疑：可查證的錄製場次，證明莫蓮參與，而且有對應的同意紀錄。她們也把這種可能性寫下，為尚未發現的解釋留下空間。
