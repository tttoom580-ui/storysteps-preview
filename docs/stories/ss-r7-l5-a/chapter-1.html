消失的聲音

The Voice That Wasn't There

紅色母帶：誰有權借走她的聲音 · 第 1 / 25 章 · 4000–5000 · 英中對照

On the first evening of the Lantern Caravan's winter tour, Ye Lan stood beside a rack of microphones and listened to the opening song. A batch of schoolchildren waited beneath the red tent, expecting to hear the beloved storyteller Mo Lian. Instead, the loudspeakers carried a smooth, unfamiliar voice that sounded almost like hers. Lan checked the recording circuit and found a decent signal. Nothing had failed technically. Yet the voice seemed to dissolve between two phrases, with none of Mo's usual hesitation before the last word. The audience applauded, unaware that someone had changed the performance.

燈篷劇團冬季巡演的第一晚，葉嵐站在一排麥克風旁，聽著開場曲。一批學童坐在紅色帳篷下，期待聽見深受喜愛的說書人莫蓮。擴音器傳出的卻是一個光滑、陌生而近似她的聲音。葉嵐檢查錄音電路，發現訊號正常。設備沒有故障，但那聲音在兩句話之間彷彿消散了，少了莫蓮在最後一字前慣有的停頓。觀眾熱烈鼓掌，毫不知情有人換掉了演出。

Lan's former mentor, stage carpenter Zhu Xu, came running from the wings. Their small nonprofit troupe could barely afford its fuel, and the sponsor had demanded a new sound projection system for the tour. Zhu said the placement of the speakers had changed that afternoon. He wanted to implement a quick repair, but Lan asked for an inquiry before anyone touched the console. A sponsor's assistant laughed and called her an idiot. Regardless of his opinion, she copied the audio file, noted its creation time, and sealed the original memory card in a paper envelope.

葉嵐從前的導師、舞台木工朱緒從側台跑來。這支非營利劇團連燃料費都快付不起，贊助者還要求巡演使用新的聲音投射系統。朱緒說，下午有人改過喇叭的位置。他想趕快修理，但葉嵐要求先展開調查，不讓任何人碰控制台。贊助商助理笑她是笨蛋。葉嵐不理會，把音檔複製下來，記下建立時間，再把原始記憶卡封進紙袋。

At the edge of the tent, a retired horse handler tightened a leather saddle on the troupe's old pony. The animal had carried sets before the roads became wide enough for trucks. Its back now showed the strain of too many winters. Lan remembered Mo once saying that a performance could terrify a listener or give them courage, and that this power required care. She could not allow a new commercial regime to treat every familiar human voice as free material. She asked the driver to leave the recording equipment in place until three witnesses had photographed it.

帳篷邊，一名退休馬伕正替劇團的老馬繫緊皮鞍。道路拓寬到能行駛卡車以前，都是牠搬運布景，如今牠的背已承受太多寒冬。葉嵐想起莫蓮曾說，演出可以使觀眾恐懼，也可以給人勇氣，因此必須慎用這種力量。她不能讓新商業制度把每個熟悉的人聲都當成免費素材。她請司機不要移動錄音設備，直到三名見證人完成拍照。

During the interval, the ticket clerk asked whether the audience should be told about the replacement. Lan refused to invent a comforting explanation. She prepared a short notice for the cast first, identifying what the equipment showed and what remained uncertain. The clerk agreed to keep the announcement factual, even though the sponsor preferred a cheerful story about modernization.

中場休息時，售票員問是否該告訴觀眾聲音遭替換。葉嵐不願編造安撫性的解釋。她先替演員準備簡短通知，區分設備顯示的情況與仍未知的事實。售票員同意公告保持客觀，雖然贊助商更喜歡講述現代化的快樂故事。

The sponsor's director arrived with a printed schedule and a smile. He suggested that the company could triple the number of shows if it replaced live narration with automated tracks. He claimed this was a breakthrough that would reinforce the troupe's future. Lan asked whether the company had written permission from Mo. The director tapped the metal rim of his briefcase, then changed the subject to ticket sales. Zhu, who had spent forty years building stages, recognized the silence. The claim about efficiency might be true, he said, but it did not answer the question about consent.

贊助方的主管帶著印好的行程表和笑容走來。他說，只要用自動音軌取代現場口述，演出場次就能增為三倍，這項突破也將強化劇團的未來。葉嵐問公司是否取得莫蓮的書面許可。主管敲著公事包的金屬邊緣，卻把話題轉向票房。做了四十年舞台的朱緒立刻察覺這段沉默。他說效率提升也許是真的，但那沒有回答同意權的問題。

When the audience left, Lan projected both versions of the opening line on a screen. The old recording contained a breath, a chair creak, and a tiny pause where Mo remembered her mother. The new file erased all three. Zhu proposed that they dissolve their arrangement with the sponsor immediately, but Lan warned that a sudden break could leave the actors without wages in the middle of the tour. They agreed on one limited step: keep performing live, preserve every file, and ask Mo herself before making an accusation. It was not victory. It was a defensible beginning.

觀眾離去後，葉嵐把兩個版本的開場句投到螢幕上。舊錄音裡有一聲呼吸、一聲椅響，還有莫蓮想起母親時的短暫停頓。新檔案把三者全抹掉了。朱緒提議立刻解除與贊助商的合作，葉嵐卻提醒他，巡演途中突然斷約可能使演員領不到薪水。他們決定先走有限的一步：繼續現場表演、保全所有檔案，並在指控任何人之前親自詢問莫蓮。這不是勝利，卻是站得住腳的起點。

Long after midnight, Lan locked the console cabinet and sat alone beneath the dark canvas roof. A drop of rain struck the tent pole, then another, until the sound resembled fingers on a table. She made a second list of questions for the morning: who supplied the replacement recording, when the first copy arrived, and why the contract contained no mention of the new voice. She could imagine several innocent explanations, though none fit every detail. The small clock beside her continued ticking. For the first time in years, she was afraid not of a poor performance but of a perfect one.

過了午夜很久，葉嵐鎖好控制台，獨坐在漆黑的帳篷屋頂下。雨滴一滴接著一滴敲在帳篷柱上，像手指輕叩桌面。她列出隔天要問的問題：替換錄音是誰提供的、第一份副本何時送到、合約為何沒有提到新聲音。她能想像幾種無辜的解釋，卻沒有一種能符合全部細節。身旁小鐘持續滴答作響。多年來，她第一次害怕的不是演出失敗，而是演出太完美。
