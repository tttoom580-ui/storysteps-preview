The Script Nobody Rehearsed

紅色母帶：誰有權借走她的聲音 · 第 12 / 25 章 · 4000–5000 · 英中對照 · 校訂中

用互動版查詞、朗讀與收藏

The public meeting began with an abnormal interruption: an unfamiliar actor walked onto the platform carrying a script titled The Captive Voice. He introduced himself as a viewer who had followed the broadcast controversy. The script depicted a musician held hostage by a ruler who wanted to collect every song. In its second scene, guards threatened to kidnap the musician's family. Lan asked the actor to stop. She was willing to discuss a fictional story about slavery, but the present inquiry did not contain evidence of a kidnapping. Dramatic images should not be allowed to replace the actual events under review.

公開會議一開始就出現異常插曲：一位陌生演員拿著《被囚的聲音》劇本走上台，自稱是關注轉播爭議的觀眾。劇本描繪音樂家被統治者扣為人質，因為統治者想收藏所有歌曲。第二幕的守衛還威脅要綁架音樂家家人。葉嵐請他停下。她願意討論描寫奴役的虛構故事，但目前調查並無綁架證據。戲劇性的影像不該取代正在審查的真實事件。

The actor mumbled that someone had paid him to read the piece. He was afraid of being labeled a traitor if he named the person. Mara introduced him to an observer who could record his account privately, without promising any result. They did not detain him or confront him before the audience. The incident reminded Lan how easily a room could become hostile when people believed they were defending a cause. The meeting resumed only after the chair explained the basic norm: witnesses spoke voluntarily, and allegations required identifiable facts that could be checked.

那名演員低聲說有人付錢請他朗讀，卻害怕說出姓名後被當成叛徒。瑪拉帶他去見一位觀察員，讓他私下記錄經過，不保證任何結果。眾人沒有拘留他，也沒有當眾逼問。這件事提醒葉嵐，當人們自認在保護某種理念，會場很容易變得充滿敵意。主席說明基本規範後，會議才恢復：證人自願發言，指控必須有可以查核的具體事實。

A technician then opened a metal case secured with a brass bolt. It contained two incompatible editions of the contract, one with the old studio's seal and one with the sponsor's new mark. The text seemed almost identical until Lan noticed a clause added after a handwritten signature. The alteration was small enough that an exhausted person could miss it. Qing photographed the page and asked the technician to acquaint the audience with the process used to preserve it. He described the chain of custody without claiming to know who inserted the sentence. The audience listened more carefully than Lan had expected.

接著技師打開一只以黃銅門閂固定的金屬箱。裡面有兩份互不相容的合約版本，一份蓋舊工作室印章，另一份蓋贊助商新印記。兩份文字看似相同，直到葉嵐注意到手寫簽名後新增的一條款。改動很小，疲憊的人很容易漏看。蘇青拍下頁面，請技師向觀眾介紹保存程序。他說明證據保管鏈，沒有宣稱知道誰加入了句子。觀眾比葉嵐預期得更加專心。

The observer explained that a continental distribution agreement was not necessarily compatible with a local workshop license. Different regions, dates, and audiences could require new permissions. Lan asked the lawyer to put the distinction in ordinary language. If the performers could not understand the answer, she said, the form would not protect them.

觀察員解釋，跨大陸發行協議未必與本地工作坊授權相容。不同地區、日期及觀眾可能需要新的許可。葉嵐請律師用一般人聽得懂的語言說明差別。她說，如果表演者聽不懂答案，表格就保護不了他們。

A missionary organization from across the continent offered to sponsor a free performance about workers' rights. Nara welcomed the idea but asked whether the troupe could control the language and casting. She would not trade one form of pressure for another. The offer included a large stock of printed posters, yet it did not include travel wages. Lan worked through the budget and found that the plan was not viable without further support. She explained her conclusion without accusing the organization of discrimination. A generous intention and a workable arrangement were different things, and the troupe could accept neither blindly.

一個來自大陸另一端的宣教組織提出贊助免費勞權演出。娜拉歡迎構想，卻詢問劇團是否能控制語言與選角。她不願用一種壓力交換另一種壓力。提案附上一大批印好的海報，卻未包含巡演薪資。葉嵐仔細算過預算，發現若沒有額外支持就不可行。她說明結論，沒有指控該組織歧視。慷慨的意圖與可執行的安排是兩回事，劇團不能盲目接受任何一方。

The meeting's final witness was Zhu. He admitted that he had moved the red reel to the archive years earlier, but said he had done so at Mo's request. He had refused to open it again because she asked him not to. The claim could be tested against the label and Mo's memory. Lan did not press him beyond that. A younger worker whispered that the room had finally produced a sensation, but Lan felt less excited than relieved. The statement was specific enough to check, and it left space for Mo to speak in her own time.

會議最後一位證人是朱緒。他承認多年前把紅色母帶移到檔案館，但說是莫蓮要求的。他一直不願再打開，是因為莫蓮請他別這麼做。這說法可以和標籤、莫蓮的回憶比對。葉嵐沒有再逼問。年輕工作者低聲說會場終於有了轟動消息，葉嵐卻感到釋然多於興奮。朱緒提供了可查證的具體說法，也為莫蓮保留了自行發言的時間。

After the audience left, Lan collected the printed copies of the fictional script so they would not be mistaken for testimony. She kept one clearly marked as an unrelated performance text and returned the rest to their author. Then she arranged a private meeting between Mo, Zhu, and the independent observer. The goal was neither reunion nor punishment. It was to clarify the history of one reel and to find out whether a current company had the right to use its contents. For the first time, the investigation had a path that did not depend on anyone becoming a villain.

觀眾散去後，葉嵐收起虛構劇本的印本，以免有人誤把它當作證詞。她留一份清楚標記為無關表演文字，其餘歸還作者。接著她安排莫蓮、朱緒與獨立觀察員私下會面。目的不是讓兩人重修舊好，也不是懲罰誰，而是釐清一卷母帶的歷史，以及現今公司有沒有使用權。這是調查第一次有了一條不需要把誰塑造成反派的道路。
