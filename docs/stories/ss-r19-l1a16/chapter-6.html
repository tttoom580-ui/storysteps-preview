The village guard sent a map showing both the high trail and the river road. A small event can change the meaning of an ordinary day. Toto had been thinking about simple things in the mountain shelter when a detail refused to fit the old picture. For a moment, everyone around seemed busy with something else. Toto tried not to rush, because a wrong guess could make the problem harder for someone nearby. The trail map was close at hand, ready for the facts that would be easy to forget once people began talking. In the corner of the trail map, Toto found the word “coffee” beside an old drawing and stopped to ask what it meant. “lion,” Kes read from the practice page, then tried to connect the word to something they could actually see. A little note used “top” as its key word; Toto kept the note because the rest of the line was still useful.

There was no great speech at the end of the day. Toto and Kes shared a look that said they had both noticed the change.
