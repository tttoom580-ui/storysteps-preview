A deer offered Toto a basket of food for the long walk. Some days begin with a plan; others begin with a question no one had expected. This one gave Toto both. At the mountain shelter, people were coming and going, each carrying a different idea about what was important. The trail map made it possible to hold on to a few clear details while the rest of the world kept moving. A little note used “close” as its key word; Toto kept the note because the rest of the line was still useful. Toto circled “nature” on a reading sheet, not to win a test but to remember the meaning of the whole sentence. The word “yard” appeared in an example someone had written earlier, and Kes asked if the example fit their situation.

一隻鹿送給托托一籃食物，幫他走完漫長路程。有些日子從計畫開始，有些日子卻從意料之外的問題開始。這一天，托托兩者都遇上了。在山間避雨棚，人們來來往往，每個人都覺得不同的事最重要。步道地圖讓托托在世界不停變動時，仍能抓住幾個清楚的細節。一張小筆記以「close」為關鍵字；托托留下它，因為剩下的句子仍然有用。托托在閱讀單上圈出「nature」，不是為了考高分，而是為了理解整句話。「yard」出現在前人留下的例句裡，凱斯問這個例子是否適合現在的情況。

Kes listened without offering an easy answer. That was unusual enough for Toto to notice. They named the part they understood, then left space for the part that still felt unclear. Toto circled “whether” on a reading sheet, not to win a test but to remember the meaning of the whole sentence.

The basket was too heavy for his small shell, and he had to choose what mattered. Toto felt a quick wish to fix everything at once. Then the faces nearby made the choice more complicated. It was not only about the urgent red letter. Other people had time, hopes, and worries of their own. Kes drew a line across the trail map: facts on one side, guesses on the other. That simple mark changed how they spoke. A little note used “sky” as its key word; Toto kept the note because the rest of the line was still useful.

籃子對他的小龜殼來說太重，他必須決定最重要的物品。托托一瞬間很想把所有事一次解決，但附近每張臉都讓選擇變得複雜。這不只是紅色急件的問題。其他人也有自己的時間、希望與憂慮。凱斯在步道地圖上畫一條線，一側寫事實，另一側寫猜測。這個簡單記號改變了他們說話的方式。在步道地圖的角落，托托看見一幅舊畫旁寫著「believe」，於是停下來詢問意思。「just。」凱斯讀出練習頁上的字，接著試著把它和眼前真正看得到的東西連起來。一張小筆記以「sky」為關鍵字；托托留下它，因為剩下的句子仍然有用。

A fresh page in the trail map gave the group a way to start again without pretending the past had not happened. Toto wrote down what could happen next, while Kes named the one thing they still needed to ask. They were not making a perfect picture. He kept water and one apple, then thanked the deer for her care. Once the surprise passed, Toto wanted to know what the new fact would mean tomorrow. Kes turned back to the trail map and crossed out a guess that no longer fitted what they knew. Neither called the mistake foolish. Changing your mind after new evidence was part of learning. Toto could not make his feet faster by wishing. He could keep his eyes open and choose where those feet should go. 他留下水與一顆蘋果，向關心他的鹿道謝。驚喜過後，托托開始想知道這個新事實明天會帶來什麼改變。凱斯重新翻開步道地圖，劃掉一個已不符合事實的猜測。兩人都沒有說那是愚蠢的錯。依新證據改變想法，本來就是學習的一部分。托托不可能靠希望就讓腳變快，但可以睜大眼睛，選擇應該往哪裡走。「as」出現在前人留下的例句裡，凱斯問這個例子是否適合現在的情況。「把『error』留在詞彙筆記裡吧，」托托說，「有需要時再確認意思。」在步道地圖的角落，托托看見一幅舊畫旁寫著「rainbow」，於是停下來詢問意思。

Later, when the noise of the day grew quiet, Toto remembered one face more clearly than any sign or number. The work had not been easy, and the result did not belong to one person. Still, something had moved forward. Before putting away the trail map, Toto wrote one question for the next day. It was not the end of the story yet. 後來，白天的吵鬧安靜下來時，托托記得最清楚的是一張臉，而不是任何標誌或數字。工作並不容易，成果也不屬於單一個人。然而，有些事情確實向前走了。收起步道地圖前，托托寫下一個留給明天的問題。故事還沒有結束。托托在閱讀單上圈出「dream」，不是為了考高分，而是為了理解整句話。「proud」出現在前人留下的例句裡，凱斯問這個例子是否適合現在的情況。
