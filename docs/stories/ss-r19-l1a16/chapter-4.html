A trail sign had fallen across the path, hiding the safe turn to the forest. Before anyone called the day strange, Toto had noticed how quiet the stone trail could become when people were worried. The smallest sounds seemed clear, and the smell of pine and wet stone made the place feel both familiar and new. Rather than act as if every answer was obvious, Toto opened the trail map and began with what had really happened. A little note used “boy” as its key word; Toto kept the note because the rest of the line was still useful. Toto circled “healthy” on a reading sheet, not to win a test but to remember the meaning of the whole sentence. The word “square” appeared in an example someone had written earlier, and Kes asked if the example fit their situation.

一面路標倒在路上，擋住通往森林的安全岔路。在人們說這一天奇怪之前，托托就注意到，當大家擔心時，石頭步道會變得多麼安靜。細微的聲音變得清楚，松樹與濕石頭的氣味也讓這地方既熟悉又陌生。托托沒有裝作一切都很明白，而是打開步道地圖，從真正發生的事情開始。一張小筆記以「boy」為關鍵字；托托留下它，因為剩下的句子仍然有用。托托在閱讀單上圈出「healthy」，不是為了考高分，而是為了理解整句話。「square」出現在前人留下的例句裡，凱斯問這個例子是否適合現在的情況。

Nobody could say yet how the story would end. Toto found this uncomfortable, especially with the urgent red letter still waiting for an answer. Kes suggested they take turns speaking, because repeating the same fear would not create a new solution. A little order returned to the conversation, and that made the next difficult step possible. Kes could see far from the sky; Toto could read the marks close to the ground. Their different views made the trail clearer. “blow,” Kes read from the practice page, then tried to connect the word to something they could actually see. A little note used “factory” as its key word; Toto kept the note because the rest of the line was still useful. Toto circled “sir” on a reading sheet, not to win a test but to remember the meaning of the whole sentence. The word “worker” appeared in an example someone had written earlier, and Kes asked if the example fit their situation.

Toto did not guess which way to go while dark water flowed nearby. This was the point where a simple wish met the world as it really was. Toto wanted to help, but wanting did not make every road safe or every rule fair. Kes asked what the other people involved might lose if the wrong choice were made. The answer took longer than either had expected. They agreed not to build the rest of their plan on a fact they had never checked. A little note used “without” as its key word; Toto kept the note because the rest of the line was still useful.

黑色溪水流過附近，托托不敢隨便猜路。這正是簡單願望遇上真實世界的時刻。托托想幫忙，但只靠想法不會讓所有路變安全，也不會讓所有規則公平。凱斯問，如果選錯了，牽涉其中的人可能失去什麼。兩人花了比預期更長的時間才回答。他們同意，不把整個計畫建立在尚未核實的事實上。在步道地圖的角落，托托看見一幅舊畫旁寫著「drop」，於是停下來詢問意思。「she。」凱斯讀出練習頁上的字，接著試著把它和眼前真正看得到的東西連起來。一張小筆記以「without」為關鍵字；托托留下它，因為剩下的句子仍然有用。

The group returned to the trail map and looked at the first line again. It now seemed to ask a different question. Toto asked Kes to explain the idea in plain words, as if telling it to someone who had just arrived. When the idea sounded confusing, they changed the plan rather than blaming the listener. “Let us keep ‘cloud’ in our word notes,” Toto said. “We can check its meaning again if we need it.” In the corner of the trail map, Toto found the word “shake” beside an old drawing and stopped to ask what it meant. “town,” Kes read from the practice page, then tried to connect the word to something they could actually see.

大家重新看著步道地圖的第一行，發現它彷彿提出了另一個問題。托托請凱斯用簡單的話解釋，就像對剛來的人說明。說明若讓人困惑，他們就修改計畫，而不是責怪聽的人。「把『cloud』留在詞彙筆記裡吧，」托托說，「有需要時再確認意思。」在步道地圖的角落，托托看見一幅舊畫旁寫著「shake」，於是停下來詢問意思。「town。」凱斯讀出練習頁上的字，接著試著把它和眼前真正看得到的東西連起來。

An old snail showed him a small mark on the stone that pointed to the forest trail. The answer brought relief, but it also carried a new responsibility. Toto did not look away from that part. Kes asked who should hear the news next, and whether it belonged to them to share it. They chose the careful answer over the exciting one, though excitement was still in the air. 一隻老蝸牛指出石頭上的小記號，指向森林小徑。答案帶來安心，也帶來新的責任。托托沒有迴避這一部分。凱斯問下一個該讓誰知道消息，以及他們是否有權公開。他們選擇審慎的回答，而非最刺激的那個，雖然空氣中仍充滿興奮。「cheap」出現在前人留下的例句裡，凱斯問這個例子是否適合現在的情況。「把『service』留在詞彙筆記裡吧，」托托說，「有需要時再確認意思。」在步道地圖的角落，托托看見一幅舊畫旁寫著「tomato」，於是停下來詢問意思。

The clock moved on. Toto felt tired, yet there was a good kind of energy left after honest work. Nobody had to say that the day was perfect. They only had to remember what had worked and what still needed care. Toto closed the trail map gently, already curious about the next choice. Toto circled “butterfly” on a reading sheet, not to win a test but to remember the meaning of the whole sentence. The word “off” appeared in an example someone had written earlier, and Kes asked if the example fit their situation. “Let us keep ‘tall’ in our word notes,” Toto said. “We can check its meaning again if we need it.”
