Toto heard someone calling from below the safe path and stopped to listen. Before anyone called the day strange, Toto had noticed how quiet the mountain shelter could become when people were worried. The smallest sounds seemed clear, and the smell of pine and wet stone made the place feel both familiar and new. Rather than act as if every answer was obvious, Toto opened the trail map and began with what had really happened. The word “easy” appeared in an example someone had written earlier, and Kes asked if the example fit their situation. “Let us keep ‘nice’ in our word notes,” Toto said. “We can check its meaning again if we need it.” In the corner of the trail map, Toto found the word “why” beside an old drawing and stopped to ask what it meant.

A young goat stood on a rock surrounded by rising water. This was the point where a simple wish met the world as it really was. Toto wanted to help, but wanting did not make every road safe or every rule fair. Kes asked what the other people involved might lose if the wrong choice were made. The answer took longer than either had expected. They agreed not to build the rest of their plan on a fact they had never checked.
