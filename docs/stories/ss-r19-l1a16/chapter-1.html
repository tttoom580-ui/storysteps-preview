最快的信

The Fastest Letter

全村最慢的郵差，收到最快的求救信 · 第 1 / 25 章 · 1000以下 · 英中對照 · 校訂中

At six a.m., the bell over the village mail house rang so hard that Toto almost dropped his breakfast fruit. The sun was behind a cloud, and the air smelled of wet stone. A red letter had arrived from the old mill above Pine Hill. Its paper was damp, its cover was torn, and the word HELP was written across the front. Toto, the smallest turtle in the village, took it with both hands. He was also the slowest mail carrier, and everyone knew it. He had planned a gentle morning of short roads and simple letters. The red envelope changed everything before he had finished eating.

早上六點，村莊郵務屋的鈴響得太大，讓托托差點把早餐水果掉到地上。太陽藏在雲後，空氣裡有濕石頭的氣味。一封紅色信件從松丘上方的舊磨坊送來。信紙受了潮，封皮破損，正面寫著大大的「救命」。村裡最小的烏龜托托雙手接過信。他也是全村最慢的郵差，大家都知道。托托原本打算用平靜早晨送短程普通信件，卻連早餐都沒吃完就遇上變故。

The village guard opened the letter while Toto watched. It said that workers at the old mill needed the hill lookout before heavy rain reached the valley. “We must send this at once,” the guard said. The fast rabbits were away, and the birds had been told to wait because the wind was growing dangerous. Toto looked at the road on the wall map. It was long, with a bridge and a steep side path. “I can carry it,” he said. Someone laughed, but the guard did not. “Shall we check the safe way first?” the guard asked. Toto nodded.

托托看著村莊守衛打開信。信上說，舊磨坊的工人需要山頂守望站在大雨抵達山谷前提供幫助。「必須立刻送達，」守衛說。跑得快的兔子已不在村裡；風愈來愈危險，鳥兒也被通知暫時不要飛。托托望向牆上的路線圖。那是一條長路，經過橋和陡峭的側道。「我可以送，」他說。有人笑了，守衛卻沒有。「我們先確認安全路線好嗎？」守衛問。托托點頭。

Toto went home for his dry bag. On his couch lay the little book where he liked to write stories about places he had visited. His hobby was drawing maps, and he used to teach young animals how to follow a trail line. He knew one monkey who could climb fast but often missed signs on the ground. Toto smiled at the memory, then folded his book away. The red letter was more important. He put a clean cover around it, tied the bag to his shell, and checked that he could still turn his head. A heavy bag could make a small mistake dangerous on wet rocks.

托托先回家拿防水袋。他的小沙發上放著一本喜歡記錄旅途的書。畫地圖是他的興趣，他也常教年幼動物如何沿著步道線行走。他認識一隻爬得很快的猴子，卻常看漏地面標誌。想到這件事，托托微微一笑，隨即把書收好。紅色急件更加重要。他用乾淨防水套包住信，將袋子綁上龜殼，檢查自己是否還能自由轉頭。濕滑石頭上，太重的袋子會讓小錯誤變得危險。

At the first road sign, Toto met Kes the sparrow. Kes was friendly and wanted to help. “I can fly straight to the hill,” he offered. Toto looked at the cloud and the bending tree. “You can look from here,” he answered, “but please do not fly into that wind.” Kes agreed to watch the road from a low branch. Down in the village, someone was preparing salad and cake for a school picnic; a painted poster showed a cow with a very large tooth. Toto noticed such ordinary things because they reminded him what the storm could threaten. Many homes stood below the hill.

在第一個路標旁，托托遇見麻雀凱斯。凱斯十分友善，也想幫忙。「我能直接飛到山上，」他說。托托看看烏雲和被風吹彎的樹。「你可以從這裡觀察，」托托回答，「但請不要飛進強風裡。」凱斯同意待在低樹枝上看路。村裡有人為學校野餐準備沙拉與蛋糕，一張彩繪海報還畫著一頭長著超大牙齒的牛。托托注意到這些平凡小事，因為它們提醒他暴風雨可能威脅什麼。山腳下有許多家庭。

The stone road climbed early into the trees. Toto did not hurry past the first marker. He read the trail guide and looked for the mark that showed which side stayed safe after rain. A foreign visitor had once called it a good exercise in patience. Today it felt more like an exercise in care. Toto checked the river below the bridge; the water was already rising. He chose the high road instead of the shorter one. Kes flapped from branch to branch, staying low. The two could not see the old mill yet, but at least they knew they had not taken the wrong turn. For a few minutes, the place held only ordinary sounds. Toto used that quiet time to think about the next person who would need help understanding the plan. A good answer should be clear even to someone who had not been there.

石頭路很快往樹林裡爬升。托托沒有匆忙經過第一個標誌。他讀著步道指南，尋找雨後表示安全一側的記號。一位外國訪客曾說，這是耐心的好練習。今天托托覺得，它更像照顧別人的練習。他查看橋下的河，水位已開始上升。於是他選擇高路，而非較短的那條。凱斯沿著樹枝低低飛行。兩人還看不見舊磨坊，但至少確定沒轉錯彎。有幾分鐘，周圍只剩平常的聲音。托托趁安靜時想著下一位需要理解計畫的人。好答案應該連不在現場的人都能看懂。

Behind them, the village bell rang again. Toto stopped and read the red letter one more time. He had feared he was too slow to be helpful, but the first careful choice had already saved him from the flood road. “We can do the next part,” Kes said. Toto nodded. From far above came a sound like a tree falling. He could not tell where it was, and he would not invent an answer. He marked the sound on his map so that the lookout would know. Then the turtle began his long climb, carrying the fastest warning his village had ever needed.

身後村莊的鈴聲又響起。托托停下，再讀一次紅色信件。他本來擔心自己慢得派不上用場，但第一個審慎選擇已讓他避開淹水道路。「下一段我們也做得到，」凱斯說。托托點頭。高處忽然傳來像大樹倒下的聲音。他不知道聲音從哪裡來，也不會捏造答案。他把聲音記在地圖上，好讓守望員知道。接著烏龜開始漫長的爬坡，帶著村莊有史以來最需要迅速傳達的警告。
