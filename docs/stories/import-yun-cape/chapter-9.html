Boundary Stones and Souvenirs

The new witness called herself Noor. Before showing her notebook, she asked to speak with a therapist from the island clinic. She had spent months fearing that every knock on the door was a threat. The therapist explained that the investigation could pause whenever she felt vulnerable, and that the choice to testify was hers. Yu respected the boundary, though waiting felt unbearable. Outside the clinic, a passionate group of residents demanded immediate arrests. An official investigator warned that the evidence had to be handled under a valid legal procedure. The families were tired of procedures, but Yu knew a broken process could give the company a path to escape responsibility.

新證人自稱努兒。出示筆記前，她要求先見島上診所的心理治療師。幾個月來，她一直怕每次敲門都是威脅。治療師說明，只要感到脆弱或不安，隨時可以暫停調查，是否作證由她決定。林雨尊重這道界線，儘管等待煎熬。診所外，熱情激動的居民要求立即逮捕涉案者。官方調查員提醒證據必須依有效合法程序處理。家屬已受夠程序，但林雨知道程序出錯反而可能使公司脫罪。

When Noor finally spoke, she described the project's original version. It had begun as a plan to move cool water toward overheated reefs, using a pump built into an artificial stone pyramid below the bay. The plan was never completed because a new owner had chosen to incorporate industrial waste disposal into the design. Noor had objected and been transferred to guard an empty residence near the old customs office. She showed Yu a photograph of the pump's control panel. A cracked patch of paint hid the manufacturer's mark. Beneath it, faint letters matched the souvenir badge Yu's father had brought home years ago.

努兒終於開始說話。計畫的最初版本，是在海灣下方人工石造金字塔內裝設幫浦，替過熱的礁體輸送冷水。然而計畫未完成，後來的新業主便把工業廢料處理設備納入設計。努兒曾反對，結果被調去看守舊海關旁的空屋。她拿出控制板照片，破裂的補漆遮住製造商標誌，底下淡淡字母卻與林雨父親多年前帶回的紀念徽章相同。

The mayor announced that the island's administrative boundary excluded the offshore machinery. To prove his point, he displayed a newer map in which the line stopped short of the trench. Yu compared it with the historical map and discovered that someone had altered the coordinates after the company's arrival. An old bodyguard who had once protected the mayor confirmed that he had accompanied him to a closed meeting about the change. He wore worn leather sandals and spoke without ceremony. “They said a boundary was only ink,” he recalled. “But I watched that ink decide which fishermen were eligible for compensation and which were not.”

鎮長宣稱島行政界線不包含外海機器，展示的新地圖竟把界線畫在海溝之前。林雨與舊圖比較，發現公司進駐後有人修改座標。曾保護鎮長的老保鑣證實，他陪同出席過討論界線的閉門會議。老人穿著磨損的皮涼鞋，說話毫不修飾：「他們說界線只是墨水，但我親眼看那道墨水決定哪些漁民有資格領賠償，哪些沒有。」

Noor led the team to a warehouse where documents had been stored beside tourist gifts. A carved wooden ass stood among miniature lighthouses and postcards, its head tilted toward a hidden shelf. Behind it were receipts for chemical fluid, imported pipes, and private security contracts. The list appeared to comprise more than a hundred entries, but many repeated the same shipment under different names. Rae began to undo the pattern by matching dates to ship movements. The mayor's accountant had used three sets of accounts, each presenting a different story. Yu reminded everyone that a contradiction in records was evidence of a problem, not yet proof of who had created it.

努兒帶眾人到一座倉庫，文件竟與遊客商品混放。木雕驢子站在迷你燈塔和明信片之間，驢頭歪向暗架。背後藏有化學流體、進口管件及私人保全合約的收據。帳面看似有上百筆，許多卻是同批貨物換名重複登記。蕾比對船舶進出日期，逐漸拆解花樣。鎮長會計有三套帳，各自說著不同故事。林雨提醒大家，紀錄矛盾說明問題存在，尚不能證明誰製造了問題。

The accountant's ledger also showed that island families with foreign surnames had been denied jobs despite equivalent qualifications. This was not merely an accounting error; the hiring committee had chosen to discriminate against people whose grandparents arrived from elsewhere. Noor's anger grew as she read the names. Her parents had been among those denied housing after their boats sank. Yu promised to include the discrimination in the public report, even if it complicated the environmental case. A truthful story might be harder to present than a simple accusation, but its strength came from refusing to erase inconvenient people.

會計帳冊還顯示，帶外來姓氏的島民即使資格相同，也被拒絕工作。這不是單純會計錯誤，招聘委員會刻意歧視祖輩來自外地的人。努兒看見名單越讀越生氣，她父母船沉之後也被拒絕安置。林雨答應把歧視問題列入報告，即使這讓環境案件更加複雜。真相或許比單一指控難以表達，但正因不抹去麻煩的人，才值得相信。

At sunset Noor placed her notebook in a secure cabinet and gave each witness a signed copy of the inventory. On the last page she had drawn three channels meeting under the thirteenth light. The symbol in the middle matched a mark carved inside Yu's father's lighthouse. Yu finally understood that the lighthouse was not merely a family home or a navigation tower. Its signals had been designed to warn of changes in the underwater system. If the next cycle began in four days, someone needed to learn what the flashing pattern meant before it was too late.

日落時，努兒把筆記本放入安全櫃，並給每位證人一份簽過名的清點表。最後一頁畫著三條水道在第十三道光下交會，中央符號與父親燈塔內牆上的刻痕一致。林雨終於明白，燈塔不只是祖產或導航塔，它的光號原本用來警告海底系統變化。若四天後就要再次啟動，他們必須盡快破解閃光模式。
