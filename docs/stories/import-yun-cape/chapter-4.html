The provincial health office claimed it could not anticipate every illness on a remote island. Its director blamed bureaucracy for the delay in releasing test results, but Yu suspected the timing was no coincidence. A daily commuter ferry had carried sick divers to the mainland for months, yet the official register contained only a fraction of their names. When Yu asked why, the director offered a firm denial: no organized concealment had occurred. Rae showed him copies of boarding passes. The director studied the paper so long that Yu could hear the wall clock ticking between their breaths. The case was no longer a local quarrel: families across the entire province were now asking whether health statistics had been presented honestly.

省衛生局聲稱無法預料偏遠島嶼上的每一種疾病。局長把公布檢測結果的拖延怪罪於官僚程序，林雨卻懷疑時間上的吻合不是巧合。每日往返本島的通勤渡輪已載走好幾個月的病患潛水員，官方名冊卻只列出其中一小部分。局長堅決否認有系統性隱瞞。蕾拿出渡輪登船紀錄，他盯著紙張許久，久到林雨能聽見牆上時鐘的每次滴答。這已不只是地方爭執，整個省的家庭都開始追問：健康統計是否被誠實呈現。

At the university's marine science faculty, a researcher measured the diameter of small lesions on fish caught near the trench. The marks had a distinctive ring, almost perfectly round, unlike the irregular wounds caused by hooks or predators. A physician asked whether a foreign hormone might explain the strange growth, while another practitioner warned that no diagnosis should be announced without further tests. Their caution did not comfort Yu, but it helped her understand the difference between a frightening pattern and a proven cause. Scientists owed frightened families more than confidence; they owed them careful work.

大學海洋科學院裡，研究員測量了海溝附近魚群身上病灶的直徑。那些痕跡呈現獨特的近乎正圓環狀，與魚鉤或掠食者造成的不規則傷口不同。一名醫師懷疑是否有外來荷爾蒙影響生長，另一位執業醫療人員提醒，沒有進一步檢測就不能宣布診斷。這份謹慎沒有讓林雨放心，卻讓她明白：可怕的規律與已證實的原因之間仍有距離。科學家欠受驚家庭的，不只是自信，更是紮實的工作。

The fiscal officer arrived with a chart claiming that the project created jobs, reduced poverty, and presented no major hazard. Yu drew a parallel chart beside it, counting medical visits, empty fishing days, and households that had borrowed money for food. The first chart looked impressive because its scale hid the loss. The second made that loss impossible to overlook. A grandmother then made a quiet plea: her grandson had stopped walking to school because his legs ached after every swim. “You may measure the cost any way you like,” she said, “but please count him.”

財政官員帶來一張漂亮圖表，宣稱計畫創造工作、降低貧窮，沒有重大危害。林雨在旁邊畫了另一張平行對照圖，統計就醫次數、無法出海的日數，以及借錢買食物的家庭。第一張圖看似亮眼，是因為比例尺掩蓋了損失；第二張圖使代價無從忽視。一位祖母輕聲懇求：孫子每次游泳後雙腿就疼痛，如今連走去學校都不敢。「你們愛怎麼計算都可以，」她說，「但請把他算進去。」

Later the doctor found a regional report on mortality among deep-sea workers. Its figures seemed normal until she noticed that temporary employees had been excluded. The administrator said the figures were correct according to the selected definition. Rae answered that a definition which erased the dead could not be the only useful one. The dean of the faculty agreed to form an independent review group. He tried to reassure Yu that the findings would be released even if they embarrassed powerful donors. Yet he added, almost in a whisper, that the university's newest building had been paid for by Ocean Reach.

稍後醫師找到一份深海工人死亡率的區域報告，數字看似正常，直到她發現臨時雇員全部被排除。行政人員說，只要按照選定的定義，數據就是正確的。蕾回應：把死者抹去的定義，不能成為唯一有用的定義。院長同意成立獨立審查小組，安慰林雨說，即使結果令重要捐助人難堪，也會公開。但他幾乎用耳語補上一句：大學新大樓是遠洋之境捐建的。

In an archive cabinet, Yu discovered an old plan for the dry dock. An underground pipe connected it to the offshore trench through a sealed tunnel. The plan had been filed in the wrong year and stamped “irregular,” a bureaucratic word that suddenly seemed useful. Yu copied every line and sent the scan to Bo. The tunnel offered a way to uncover what the company had placed below the bay without sending divers into the dangerous open sea. The thought was promising, but the plan also showed three emergency doors. No one knew whether they had rusted shut or been locked from the other side.

檔案櫃裡，林雨找到乾船塢舊設計圖。一條地下管線透過密封隧道通往外海海溝。圖紙竟被歸在錯誤年份，蓋著「異常」印記；這個原本煩人的官僚用詞忽然成了線索。她影印每條線，傳給阿博。隧道可能讓他們不必潛入危險海域，就能揭露公司藏在海灣下的設備。然而圖上還有三道緊急門，沒人知道它們是鏽死了，還是被另一端的人鎖住。

They returned on the late ferry, sharing a seat with a boy carrying a hospital bag for his father. The boy asked whether people in the mainland city cared about Yun Cape. Yu wanted to promise that they did, but she thought of the director's silence and the university's dependence on corporate money. “Some care,” she said. “More will, if we tell the story clearly.” She showed him how to read a tide table. For the rest of the journey they watched the sea rise and fall against the ferry window, measuring something that no official could declare nonexistent.

返程晚班渡輪上，他們與一名替父親提醫療袋的少年同座。少年問，本島城市的人是否在乎雲岬。林雨想說當然在乎，卻想起局長的沉默與大學對企業資金的依賴。「有些人在乎，」她回答，「只要我們把事情說清楚，就會有更多人。」她教少年讀潮汐表。剩下的航程中，他們一起看海水在船窗外升降，測量著任何官員都無法否認存在的東西。
