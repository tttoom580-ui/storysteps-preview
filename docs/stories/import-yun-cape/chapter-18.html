果園中的集會

The Orchard Rally

雲岬十三光 · 第 18 / 25 章 · 4000–5000 · 英中對照 · 校訂中

The town's newest administration announced another plan to postpone the inspection, arguing that public hostility made the port unsafe. Mina organized a peaceful rally in her grandmother's orchard, where the trees had grown weak after years of salty groundwater. Neighbors brought antique lamps, maps, and photographs that showed how the island had changed. An artist hung a strip of blue fabric between the branches, turning it into an icon of the sea they hoped to recover. Yu admired the display but reminded everyone that symbols should support the evidence, not replace it. The goal was a safe inspection, and the people needed a clear path to achieve it.

新一屆鎮行政團隊宣布再次延後稽查，理由是公眾敵意讓港口不安全。米娜便在祖母果園舉辦和平集會，果樹多年受鹹地下水侵襲而衰弱。鄰居帶來古董油燈、地圖和照片，展示島嶼變遷。一名藝術家在樹間掛藍布，變成盼望海洋復原的象徵。林雨喜歡這個畫面，卻提醒象徵應支援證據，不可取代證據。真正目標是安全稽查，必須有清楚方法實現。

A candidate for mayor asked the crowd to nominate her as their representative. Her speech attacked government corruption, but she offered no plan for water testing or medical care. Yu asked her what framework she would use to protect both workers and the reef. The candidate accused Yu of trying to undermine the protest. Rae recorded the exchange and later refused to edit out the question. A rival newspaper called the rally a political stunt. Yu worried that legitimate grievances could become tools for every ambitious rival seeking office. The people had not gathered to hand their pain to another powerful figure; they wanted institutions that would answer to them. A student’s mother said rising tuition had forced her daughter to leave college. The short-lived thrill of political speeches meant little to families trying to pay for education and food.

一名鎮長候選人要群眾提名她當代表，演說猛烈攻擊政府貪腐，卻沒有提出水質監測或醫療計畫。林雨問她用什麼制度框架同時保護勞工和礁群，候選人反指林雨破壞抗議。蕾完整錄下，拒絕剪掉問題。另一家報紙稱集會是政治作秀。林雨擔心正當訴求成為每名競爭者奪權的工具；大家不是為了把痛苦交給新的權勢人物，而是要求能向人民負責的制度。一名學生母親說學費不斷上漲，女兒被迫離開大學。對還在煩惱教育與糧食費用的家庭，政治演說轉瞬即逝的刺激毫無幫助。

In the orchard shed, the retired collector produced an old subsidy ledger. The town had received a subsidy to maintain the lighthouse and public drains, yet almost none had reached either. The entries showed gross inconsistencies between the budget and the actual work. Yu began to conceal the pages from the wind beneath a glass sheet while Rae photographed them. One receipt referred to an offshore venture jointly owned by Ocean Reach and several local officials. Sela recognized the company name and confirmed it was a subsidiary used to purchase equipment. The paper trail connected private profit with missing public repairs, though investigators would still have to prove each transaction.

果園工具棚裡，退休驗票員拿出舊補助帳冊。鎮上曾收到維護燈塔和公共排水的補貼，實際上兩邊幾乎沒有收到錢。預算與施工之間存在嚴重差異。林雨用玻璃壓住頁面防風吹，蕾逐頁拍照。一張收據指向遠洋之境和地方官員共同持有的外海事業。瑟拉認出公司名稱，證實它是採購設備的子公司。資金流向連結了私人獲利與公共修繕缺失，但每筆交易仍須由調查員證明。

The mayor's supporters arrived carrying banners, turning the rally into a confrontation of competing songs. For a moment Yu feared that a bizarre accident might spark violence. A child fell between two groups, and Mina rushed to lift him. Both sides stopped shouting while the boy checked his scraped knee. The silence gave the organizers time to establish a passage for emergency vehicles and agree on a rule against threats. Even people who disagreed could cooperate long enough to protect a child. Yu wished the same principle governed the entire island. She did not expect harmony, only room for evidence and human life to matter more than winning an argument.

鎮長支持者舉著旗幟趕來，雙方以不同歌聲互相壓過，林雨擔心一場怪異意外就會引爆衝突。孩子在兩群人間跌倒，米娜衝過去扶起。眾人停止喊叫，看他確認擦傷的膝蓋。安靜片刻讓主辦者劃出救護車通道，訂下禁止威脅的規則。意見相左的人至少能合作保護孩子。林雨希望全島制度也如此；她不期待人人和諧，只盼證據與人命比爭辯勝負重要。

By evening the rally had become a place for practical decisions. Teachers offered rooms for witnesses; doctors posted clinic hours; mechanics drew a map of routes that would remain open during the next pump cycle. The movement began to prevail not because it had defeated every opponent, but because it gave frightened people useful work to do. At the end, the artist removed the blue cloth from the orchard trees and brought it to the lighthouse. “Keep this where anyone can see it,” she said. Yu hung it beside her father's old warning map, and the cloth moved gently whenever the sea wind entered the room.

傍晚集會轉成實務討論：教師提供證人房間，醫師公告門診時段，機械師畫出下次幫浦運轉時的開放通道。這場運動開始取得優勢，不是因擊敗所有對手，而是給害怕的人一件有用的事做。最後藝術家把藍布從果樹拆下，送到燈塔。「掛在每個人都看得到的地方。」林雨把它掛在父親舊警示圖旁，海風吹進時便輕輕飄動。

Later Rae received a call from a reporter she had once considered an enemy. He had found records suggesting that the company's venture had also paid for the fake visitors at the memorial. Instead of competing for a headline, they agreed to share verified documents while publishing separate stories. Yu watched them sign a simple agreement and smiled for the first time that week. Journalism could be a contest, but the truth did not have to belong to a single reporter. Beyond the orchard, the sea remained dark. The next cycle was approaching, and their preparation was finally beginning to resemble a community rather than a crowd.

稍晚，蕾接到昔日視為敵人的記者來電，對方找到資料，顯示那家外海事業也出錢雇了追悼會的假證人。兩人沒有搶獨家，反而同意共享經驗證文件、各自發稿。林雨看他們簽下簡單協議，第一次在那週笑了。新聞工作可以有競爭，但真相不必屬於某個記者。果園外海色依然漆黑，下一次啟動將近，而島上的準備終於更像一個社群，不再只是一群人。
