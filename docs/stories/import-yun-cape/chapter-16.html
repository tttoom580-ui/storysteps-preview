帆布下的課

Lessons Under Canvas

雲岬十三光 · 第 16 / 25 章 · 4000–5000 · 英中對照 · 校訂中

The school roof leaked during another storm, so teachers moved classes beneath a wide canvas shelter near the harbor. Yu helped design a temporary curriculum about the island's water: how tides worked, what conservation meant, and why children should never enter an unknown tunnel. A boy asked whether adults had lied when they described the sea as endlessly generous. Yu said the sea was generous but not beyond harm. Overhead, a blue banner announced a science fair funded by Ocean Reach. The words “Our Sea, Our Future” seemed almost cruel beside the dead coral on the beach. Yu suggested the students draw what they themselves hoped to protect instead of copying the company's slogan.

另一場暴風雨使學校屋頂漏水，老師把課堂搬到港邊寬大的帆布棚下。林雨協助設計臨時課程，教孩子潮汐、保育，以及不該進陌生隧道的理由。一名男孩問，大人過去說海洋永遠慷慨，是不是在說謊。林雨回答，海很慷慨，卻不是永遠不會受傷。頭頂藍色布條寫著遠洋之境贊助的科展口號「我們的海，我們的未來」，在死珊瑚旁幾乎顯得殘忍。林雨請孩子畫出自己想保護的事物，不要照抄公司標語。

At the clinic, a recovering alcoholic mechanic asked to speak with Yu. He had once worked in the company enterprise and admitted he had helped manipulate maintenance data to make the pumps appear more efficient. He was ashamed of what he had done while drinking heavily, yet the illness did not erase his responsibility. The clinic counselor encouraged him to give his account in a confidential setting first. Yu listened without promising that his statement would remain private forever. If the information revealed immediate danger, officials might need to act. She explained those limits before turning on the recorder, letting him decide whether he was ready.

診所裡，一名正在戒酒康復的機械師要求見林雨。他過去任職公司，承認曾協助操縱維護數據，讓幫浦效率看起來更高。他為酗酒期間所做的事羞愧，疾病卻不會抹去責任。輔導員建議先在保密環境談話。林雨沒有保證證詞永遠不公開；若資訊顯示有人面臨立即危險，主管機關可能必須處置。她先把保密範圍說清楚，再開錄音，讓他自己選擇是否準備好了。

The mechanic described a control sequence that opened valves beneath the reef before every blue flash. If one valve failed, pressure would rise against a stone pillar under the town's old mansion. A miniature model showed how the structure might crack. Bo studied it and said that the equipment was designed to fail safely only when all warning signals reached the lighthouse. Someone had disabled one of those signals. The discovery startled Yu. She had assumed the thirteenth light meant the machine was healthy enough to operate. Instead, it might be the last surviving alarm from a system that no longer knew when to stop.

機械師說明控制程序：每次藍光前，礁底閥門就會依序開啟。若其中一個失效，壓力便壓向老宅地下的石柱。縮小模型顯示支柱可能如何裂開。阿博研究後指出，設備本來要在警示訊號完整傳到燈塔時才具備安全停機功能，但其中一條訊號已被停用。林雨大吃一驚。她原以為第十三道光代表機器仍健康，現在卻可能是失去停機能力的系統所剩最後一道警報。

That afternoon Elias invited the team to the founder's mansion, which had become a museum of expensive gifts. An old hockey stick hung above a fireplace, a reminder that the founder had loved games imported from the mainland. The display also contained a small shrine where workers used to worship the sea before their first voyage. Elias said his father considered himself an honorable inventor. Yu replied that an honorable intention was not proof of an honorable result. In a drawer beneath the shrine they found technical letters showing that the founder had urged the board to keep the emergency gates under public authority. His advice had been ignored.

下午伊萊亞斯邀團隊進創辦人的豪宅，如今裡面成了昂貴禮品的陳列館。壁爐上掛著一支曲棍球桿，提醒人們創辦人喜愛本島傳入的運動；角落還有工人出海前祭拜海洋的小神龕。伊萊亞斯說父親自認是高尚的發明家。林雨回答，高尚的動機不等於高尚的結果。神龕下抽屜的技術信件顯示，創辦人曾力勸董事會將緊急水門交給公眾管理，卻無人採納。

The island council faced a dilemma. It could allocate emergency money to strengthen the old pillar, or spend the same funds on clean water for families already harmed. Yu argued that both were necessary, but she knew that repeating a demand did not create new resources. A teacher suggested asking nearby islands to lend equipment rather than money. Bo prepared a list of what was needed and what could be returned after the emergency. The council approved a limited plan with public accounts and independent inspection. This was not a perfect answer, only the beginning of a collective one. Yu felt a strong conviction that public oversight was necessary, but she insisted that personal certainty should never replace inspection or debate.

島議會面對兩難：要分配緊急預算加固老石柱，還是拿同一筆錢供應已受害家庭乾淨飲水。林雨說兩者都必要，卻知道重複要求不會憑空變出資源。一名教師建議向鄰島借設備，不直接借錢。阿博列出所需及日後可歸還物品，議會批准帳目公開、受獨立稽查的有限計畫。這不是完美答案，只是大家共同尋找答案的起點。林雨深信公共監督不可或缺，卻堅持個人信念不能取代稽查或辯論。

Near midnight Yu stood beside the mansion pillar and put her hand on the stone. She thought she could perceive a faint vibration, though Bo's instrument showed no measurable movement. The difference mattered. A sensation was a reason to look again, not a measurement. She had begun this investigation determined to expose a corporation; now she was determined to prevent one more family from losing a home. Outside, the blue signal flashed twice. Bo checked the schedule and found that the next pump cycle had been advanced by six hours. Whatever time the island thought it had left was already shrinking.

接近午夜，林雨站在豪宅的石柱旁，手心似乎感到微弱震動，阿博的儀器卻沒有測到位移。兩者的差別很重要：感覺足以讓人再查，不能當成數值。她原本只想揭發公司，如今更想阻止任何家庭再次失去住所。屋外藍光閃了兩次，阿博核對排程發現下一次幫浦運轉被提前六小時。島民以為剩下的時間正在縮短。
