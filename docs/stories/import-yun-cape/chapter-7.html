The Bone in the Backyard

雲岬十三光 · 第 7 / 25 章 · 4000–5000 · 英中對照 · 校訂中

用互動版查詞、朗讀與收藏

The missing sailor's sister, Mina, lived in a small house behind the former colonial customs office. Her backyard was crowded with nets, broken buckets, and a stack of white stones collected from the shore. She refused to let Yu enter until Rae explained that they had not come to accuse her brother. On a wooden bench lay a chunk of coral with an odd mark running along its spine. Mina said her brother had kept it because the company offered a secret bonus for samples taken from a forbidden trench. “I disapprove of what he did,” she said, “but I am more afraid of what happened after he tried to return it.”

失蹤船員的妹妹米娜住在舊殖民地海關署後方的小屋。後院堆滿漁網、破桶和從海邊撿回的一疊白石。她起初不肯讓林雨進門，直到蕾說明她們不是來指控哥哥。長椅上放著一大塊珊瑚，中央骨架留有奇怪切痕。米娜說公司暗中發獎金，鼓勵員工從禁入海溝取得樣本，哥哥才把它留著。「我不贊成他的做法，」她說，「但他試著歸還之後發生的事，更讓我害怕。」

Yu carried the sample to a spacious community classroom, where a university sophomore named Ivo was testing the mineral content of the stones. He used a simple calcium reaction to distinguish coral from construction waste. He could not comprehend why someone would drill such a precise hole through a living reef. The implication was troubling: a device had been planted there, rather than a sample accidentally broken off. Ivo asked whether studying the evidence could put his scholarship at risk. Yu told him it was a risky question for a young student, but silence carried its own risks. They agreed to keep the original sample sealed and work from a small replica.

林雨把樣本帶到寬敞的社區教室，大二學生伊沃正在分析石塊的礦物成分。他以簡單的鈣反應區分珊瑚和建築廢料，卻不明白為何有人在活珊瑚上鑽出精準孔洞。這暗示某種設備曾被植入，而非採樣時不慎折斷。伊沃問研究證據會不會危及獎學金。林雨承認這對年輕學生確實有風險，但沉默也有代價。他們決定封存原物，只研究複製模型。

The island's independent investigator reviewed records of the company's acquisition of the customs building. Attached to the sale was a map showing that the private property line seemed to penetrate the sea itself. It crossed the public transit route used by ferries and reached almost to the protected reef. An old political association, calling itself republican because it opposed hereditary privileges, had filed an objection years earlier. Nobody had answered it. Yu recognized the handwriting on that objection: it was her father's. The document also carried the mayor's signature as a witness, making his present refusal to discuss the boundary far harder to explain.

獨立調查員重新查看公司收購舊海關樓的紀錄，發現買賣附件的地圖把私人土地界線延伸到海裡，穿過渡輪的公共通行水道，幾乎抵達保育礁。一個反對世襲特權、以共和派自稱的政治團體，多年前曾提出異議，卻始終沒人答覆。林雨認出異議書是父親的筆跡，而喬鎮長的簽名就在證人欄。他現在拒談產權界線，顯得更加可疑。

At dusk the investigators found a rusted scrap of machinery behind the customs building. It bore the same serial number as the hidden pumps below the dock. Rae wanted to post the discovery immediately, but Mina insisted on checking whether her brother had signed for the equipment. Their argument ended when a small drone rose over the wall. Someone had been conducting surveillance of the backyard. Yu covered the evidence with an old fishing basket and waved to the camera, hoping its operator would believe that they had noticed nothing. Ivo, pale and breathing quickly, quietly copied the serial number onto the inside of his sleeve.

傍晚，調查員在舊海關樓後找到一片生鏽機械廢料，序號和乾船塢隱藏幫浦相同。蕾想立即公開，米娜卻堅持先確認哥哥是否簽收過。爭論被突然飛越圍牆的小型無人機打斷。有人一直監視後院。林雨拿舊魚籃蓋住證物，朝鏡頭揮手，想讓操作者以為眾人沒察覺。伊沃臉色蒼白，悄悄把序號抄在袖子內側。

Later they gathered by the old customs flagpole. Mina proposed a symbolic act: leave one piece of clean coral on the public steps for each missing worker. Yu agreed only after they checked the names with relatives. She had seen what happened when grief became an opportunity for display. Mina's brother had been one of the people who installed the underwater cable. He might also be the only one who knew how to stop it. As the first stones were laid on the steps, the mayor's driver delivered an invitation. Mayor Qiao wanted a private meeting in the morning, with no journalist present. Yu wrote back that any meeting about public waters required a public record.

稍後眾人在舊海關旗桿旁聚集。米娜提議做象徵行動：每位失蹤工人留一塊潔淨珊瑚在公共階梯。林雨答應了，但必須先跟家屬核對姓名。她看過悲傷被拿來表演的後果。米娜的哥哥正是安裝海底電纜的工人之一，也可能是唯一懂得停機的人。大家放下第一批珊瑚石時，鎮長司機送來邀請，希望明早私下會面，且不准記者參加。林雨回覆：涉及公共水域的會議就必須留下公開紀錄。

In her father's notebook, Yu found a sketch of the coral's pierced spine and a note dated ten years before the company arrived. The hole was older than Ocean Reach. Perhaps another organization had begun the experiment, or perhaps the coral had been used to disguise a harmless scientific instrument. Yu hated having to reconsider a theory she was beginning to believe, but the drawing gave her no choice. She made two columns in her notebook, one for evidence that supported her suspicion and one for evidence that challenged it. If she wanted others to question powerful stories, she would first have to question her own.

父親的筆記裡竟有一幅相同珊瑚骨架切孔的草圖，日期比公司進駐早十年。孔洞可能由更早的組織鑽出，也可能只是無害儀器留下的痕跡。林雨不喜歡自己剛成形的推論被動搖，但圖畫迫使她重新思考。她在筆記本畫出兩欄，一欄放支持猜測的證據，一欄放反證。若她希望別人質疑強勢的說法，就得先學會質疑自己。
