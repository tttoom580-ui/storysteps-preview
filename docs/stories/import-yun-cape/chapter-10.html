The Station in the Wilderness

雲岬十三光 · 第 10 / 25 章 · 4000–5000 · 英中對照 · 校訂中

Yu followed Noor's coordinates into the island wilderness, where abandoned farms bordered a long drought-cracked reservoir. The path ended at a weather institute built decades earlier to study rare storms. Its guard treated the visit like a thriller unfolding outside his sleepy office. He led them to a control terminal and explained that the old equipment measured every environmental variable separately. Yu asked to see the preliminary logs from the week the reef first turned white. The guard hesitated: Ocean Reach had funded the station's renovations. Bo pointed out that public measurements belonged to the community regardless of who had painted the walls.

林雨按努兒座標走入島上荒野，荒廢農地圍繞著乾旱龜裂的水庫。小徑通向數十年前為研究罕見風暴而建的氣象研究所。守衛把這次訪查看成發生在無聊值班室外的驚悚故事，領眾人到控制終端，說明老儀器如何分開測量各項環境變數。林雨要求查看珊瑚初白那週的初步紀錄。守衛猶豫了：遠洋之境出資整修過觀測站。阿博提醒他，公共測量不因誰替牆壁油漆而改變歸屬。

Inside the station kitchen, a scientist was trying to grill vegetables on a broken stove. He warned that the cafeteria was a dangerous place for anyone with a serious allergy. He had once conducted research into digestion and marine nutrition before moving to climate data. His office was packed with descriptive field journals, including detailed accounts of wind direction and water temperature. “Numbers can compel us to look closer,” he said, “but they cannot tell us whom to blame without context.” He taught Ivo to cite the original date, instrument, and method for every observation. It was slow work, and its slowness made the findings stronger.

研究所廚房裡，一位科學家正用故障爐子烤蔬菜。他提醒，嚴重過敏的人別輕忽食堂風險。他過去研究消化與海洋營養，後來轉做氣候資料，辦公室堆滿描述詳盡的野外紀錄，寫著風向、水溫等細節。「數字能迫使我們仔細看，」他說，「但缺少背景，不能直接指責誰。」他教伊沃每筆觀測都要引用原始日期、儀器和方法。工作很慢，卻因為慢而更扎實。

The station's counselor told Yu that several young researchers had left after being asked to hide unfavorable results. One had become so overwhelmed that she stopped answering calls from her family. Yu suggested contacting her only through a trusted colleague. Meanwhile, Bo discovered that a company technician had removed data during routine maintenance. The missing files represented the bulk of measurements from the crucial week. Rae exclaimed when she found an older paper record in a storage drawer: the original readings had been printed on continuous rolls. The removal of digital records had not erased everything after all.

研究所輔導員告訴林雨，幾名年輕研究員曾被要求藏起不利結果，因而離職；其中一人壓力大到不再接家人的電話。林雨建議只透過她信任的同事聯絡。另一邊阿博發現，公司技師在例行保養時移走數據，最關鍵那一週的大部分測量都消失了。蕾卻從儲藏抽屜找到舊紙卷，驚呼出聲：原始讀數竟有紙本列印。移除電子資料並未抹去一切。

They compared the printouts with the weather log through a modest collaboration between the institute and the island clinic. At first the results suggested an exciting radical explanation: perhaps an unknown current had carried heat into the bay. But the temperature increase occurred at regular mechanical intervals, regardless of storms. The scientist favored a more rational possibility: an artificial pump was responsible. He refused to declare the conclusion final until they examined the machinery. Yu appreciated his restraint. It was tempting to confuse a persuasive explanation with a proven one, especially when a frightened island needed answers quickly.

研究所和診所以務實合作方式對照紙卷與天氣紀錄。最初結果似乎支持激進新說法：未知洋流把熱水帶入海灣。但升溫總以規律機械間隔發生，與風暴無關。科學家認為較合理的解釋是人工幫浦作祟，卻堅持見過機器前不做定論。林雨很感謝這份節制。尤其全島迫切需要答案時，人很容易把說得通的解釋誤當成已證實的事實。

Night fell as they prepared to leave. The guard offered tea and asked whether the company might shut down the station if the report became public. Yu could not promise otherwise. Instead, she explained the importance of an open archive and asked if he wanted his name withheld for safety. He agreed to testify under protected conditions. On the road back, distant lightning appeared above the bay without thunder. Bo measured the timing of the flashes and realized that each matched a pulse in the station's old temperature record. The thirteenth light was not a ghost signal. It was part of a machine—and someone had built the entire coast into its warning system.

夜色降下時，守衛端茶來問：報告公開之後，公司會不會關掉研究所？林雨無法保證不會，只解釋公開檔案的重要，並問是否需要隱匿身分保障安全。守衛同意受保護條件下作證。返程路上，遠方海灣有閃電般的亮光，卻沒有雷聲。阿博量出閃光間隔，發現與舊水溫紀錄上的脈衝一致。第十三道光不是鬼影，它是機器的一部分；有人把整條海岸變成了預警系統。

On the last page of the paper roll, Yu found a note from the station’s first director. He had once proposed putting spare warning equipment in the care of local schools so that no official could silence every alarm. The proposal had been rejected as too expensive. Now the schools were among the few places with enough volunteers to maintain a public monitoring system. Yu copied the note and shared it with the teachers. She wanted the coming emergency plan to include their voices, not merely borrow their buildings. The children who would inherit the coastline deserved more than a story about adults correcting an old mistake.

紙卷最後一頁，林雨找到觀測站首任主任的註記。他曾提議讓地方學校保管備援警示設備，避免任何官員能關掉所有警報，當年卻因預算太高遭拒。如今學校反而是少數有足夠志工維護公共監測的地方。林雨抄下註記交給老師，希望緊急計畫不只借用學校建築，也真正納入教師與學生的聲音。將繼承海岸線的孩子，值得得到的不該只是大人修正舊錯誤的故事。
