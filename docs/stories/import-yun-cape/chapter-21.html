失而復得的名字

Names Returned by the Tide

雲岬十三光 · 第 21 / 25 章 · 4000–5000 · 英中對照 · 校訂中

用互動版查詞、朗讀與收藏

Two weeks after the pump stopped, the island held its annual carnival without fireworks or motorboats. Instead, families carried paper lanterns along the beach to honor the missing. Mina held a small jug of seawater collected before the contamination. She had saved it for her brother, believing he would someday return to see the island's old colors. Yu walked alongside her and spoke of the affection that had kept people searching. No official announcement could restore lost years, but a gathering could remind neighbors they did not face the future alone. Across the shore, a new alliance of island clinics, fishing groups, and scientists prepared to protect the water together. A stall owner handed out bread with mustard, and for a moment Mina laughed at the familiar smell of a festival she had almost forgotten.

主幫浦停機兩週後，小島照常舉行年度嘉年華，卻沒有煙火和機動船。家屬改提紙燈沿海灘行走，紀念失蹤者。米娜抱著一小壺污染前保存的海水，原想等哥哥回家時讓他看見海的舊顏色。林雨陪她並肩前行，談起支撐眾人搜尋的深厚情感。任何公告都無法還回失去的年月，但相聚能提醒鄰居，未來不必獨自面對。岸上由診所、漁團與科學家組成的新聯盟，正準備共同守護海水。攤販分送抹了芥末的麵包，米娜聞到幾乎忘記的節慶味道，短暫笑了起來。

The investigator reported that one missing worker had been found alive in a distant rehabilitation center. He was Mina's brother. He had survived an accident in the trench, then been moved without proper documentation while suffering memory loss. The company's misleading records made his location difficult to trace. Mina began to cry, then demanded to know who had authorized the transfer. The investigator answered that several officials had signed the papers and that the law would determine responsibility. He refused to proclaim a verdict before trial. Yu welcomed the news with relief, yet could not forget the other families whose questions remained unanswered.

調查員宣布，在遠方復健中心找到一名仍生存的失蹤工人——米娜的哥哥。他海溝事故後失憶，被人未依程序轉院，公司的不實紀錄讓所在地難以追查。米娜先哭，接著追問誰授權轉送。調查員說有幾名官員簽字，責任將依法釐清，判決前不會先行宣布定罪。林雨為消息鬆口氣，卻不敢忘了其他仍不知道家人下落的家庭。

At the clinic Bo demonstrated a new emergency transmission network linking the harbor to neighboring islands. Its operational mechanism relied on ordinary radio towers and backup batteries rather than the secret beacon. An engineer checked its stability during heavy rain, while volunteers practiced sending clear messages. The old system had failed because warnings depended on a few people who could not always be reached. This new design was less elegant but easier to repair. Yu watched teenagers exchange test calls and thought that trustworthy infrastructure should be almost boring: it should work when fear and celebration made people forget it existed.

診所裡，阿博展示連結港口與鄰島的緊急通訊網。運作機制依靠普通無線電塔與備用電池，而非祕密燈號。工程師在豪雨下檢查穩定性，志工練習發出清楚訊息。舊系統失敗，是因警示太依賴少數不一定能聯絡上的人；新設計沒那麼優雅，卻容易維修。林雨看青少年互相試呼，想到可信基礎設施應近乎無聊：即使人們因恐懼或慶典忘了它，也必須照常運作。

The courtroom received evidence concerning managers who had helped convict innocent workers of stealing supplies. Their accusations were used as propaganda to portray union organizers as criminals. A legal aid group now sought to overturn those decisions. Yu had once assumed environmental work involved only animals and water; the investigation had shown her how a damaged ecosystem could be tied to damaged rights. A volunteer proposed that the town donate unused office space for legal assistance. The council approved the proposal after debating limits and oversight. The slow work of correcting old records had finally begun.

法院收到證據，顯示公司主管曾誣指無辜工人偷取物資，致使他們被定罪，再拿指控當宣傳，把工會組織者塑造成罪犯。法律扶助團體開始申請平反。林雨曾以為環境工作只涉及動物與海水，如今明白受傷的生態系也可能與權利受損相連。志工提議捐出閒置辦公空間供法律協助，議會討論監督與限制後通過。修改舊紀錄的緩慢工作終於開始。

A rescue squad returned from the outer islands with news of a burial site near an abandoned work camp. The discovery brought painful hope to families who had been waiting for years. The investigator asked everyone to avoid guesses while forensic specialists worked. Mina held Yu's hand and said that even the worst answer could be kinder than endless uncertainty. At the carnival, musicians played quietly and children offered lanterns to the tide. On the hillside beyond the shore, a barren field waited for new trees. The town planned a public memorial there, with names added only after families had verified them.

搜救小隊從外島返航，帶來廢棄工營附近發現墓葬地點的消息，讓等待多年的家庭得到痛苦的希望。調查員要求在法醫鑑定前別亂猜。米娜握住林雨的手說，即使最壞的答案，也可能比無窮的不確定仁慈。嘉年華上樂聲很輕，孩子把紙燈獻給潮水。海岸外的荒地等待種上新樹，鎮上計畫在那裡立公共紀念碑，只有家屬確認後才刻名字。

That evening Yu found a vague note among her father's letters: “No warning is eternal; build a system that people can question.” She read it twice. Her father had known the danger of relying on one inventor, one governor, or one perfect machine, even if he had not managed to prevent it. She brought the letter to the public archive, where anyone could compare it with the technical records. The island was still struggling with sickness, uncertain consumption of contaminated fish, and the cost of recovery. But it had begun to replace secrecy with shared knowledge, a foundation on which a more durable future might stand.

那晚林雨在父親信件間找到一段模糊註記：「沒有警報能永恆有效；要建立人們可以質疑的制度。」她讀了兩遍。父親早知道把希望寄於單一發明家、官員或完美機器有多危險，只是沒能阻止災難。她把信放進公開檔案，讓任何人能與技術紀錄對照。島上仍面臨疾病、受污染魚類食用安全的不確定性，還有高昂復原費，但開始用共享知識取代保密，這或許是更持久未來的基礎。
