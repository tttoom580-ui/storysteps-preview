海底排水試驗

The Underwater Trial

雲岬十三光 · 第 19 / 25 章 · 4000–5000 · 英中對照 · 校訂中

用互動版查詞、朗讀與收藏

The engineer invited Yu to observe the first public test of the emergency valve. His team had to coordinate the clinic, harbor office, and a group of volunteer divers. Every person received a specific task and a clear way to stop the trial. The lead engineer said competence mattered more than dramatic bravery. A strong, muscular diver demonstrated how to secure a line, while a quiet vendor from the fish market showed everyone a faster method of organizing the tools. The vendor had learned knots during twenty years at sea. Yu understood that professional knowledge and lived experience could work on behalf of the same goal without pretending they were identical.

工程師邀林雨旁觀緊急水閥首次公開測試，團隊得協調診所、港務處和志願潛水員。每人都有明確任務和停止試驗的方法。首席工程師說，能力比戲劇性勇敢重要。強壯的潛水員示範固定繩索，一名魚販卻展示更快整理工具的方法；她在海上二十年學會各種繩結。林雨明白，專業知識與生活經驗可以為同一目標合作，而不必假裝兩者完全相同。

A marine biologist measured the seasonal migration of fish through the channel. He believed the altered current might explain why some species had vanished even before the recent contamination. A small compact camera recorded the water without disturbing the animals. Bo set out tile markers to indicate safe positions for the divers, and Ivo lowered a yellow sponge to absorb a sample of suspended particles. The laboratory would attribute any unusual chemicals to a source only after comparing them with control samples. Yu repeated that instruction to the journalists gathered on the pier. A striking image could attract attention, but the method behind it would determine whether people should believe the claim.

海洋生物學家量測魚群季節性遷徙，認為改變的洋流可能解釋一些物種在最近污染前就已消失。小型相機不干擾生物地記錄水下影像。阿博用磁磚標記潛水員安全位置，伊沃放下黃色海綿吸附懸浮微粒。實驗室必須對照控制樣本後才能把異常化學物歸因到某來源。林雨向碼頭上的記者重申：醒目的影像能吸引注意，但背後的方法才決定說法是否可信。

The test was the team's public debut. Spectators lined the seawall, hoping for a spectacular success. Instead, the first command produced no movement. A loose connection had reduced the equipment's output, and several technicians began to scramble for spare parts. The engineer stopped them. He would rather delay than execute a rushed repair under pressure. He explained the problem over the loudspeaker and asked observers to note that an honest failed test was still a useful result. Yu saw several children copying his words into notebooks. They were learning a different kind of courage: the willingness to stop when a plan was not safe.

這是團隊的公開首次試驗，海堤上站滿觀眾，期待壯觀的成功。第一道指令下去，設備卻沒有動；接頭鬆脫使輸出下降，技術員匆忙找零件。工程師阻止他們，寧可延遲也不在壓力下草率執行修理。他透過擴音器說明失敗原因，提醒旁觀者，誠實的失敗仍有價值。林雨看見幾名孩子把話抄入筆記。他們學的是另一種勇氣：計畫不安全時願意喊停。

During the pause, Elias announced that the founder's family would make a donation to the public monitoring trust, with no control over how the money was spent. An opponent asked whether the gift would purchase forgiveness. Elias said it would not, and signed a document separating the funds from all pending investigations. A doctor added that no company executive was immune from legal questions because of charitable activity. Yu approved of the distinction. The trust needed resources, but it needed independence more. Money could repair an instrument; it could not buy the right to decide what that instrument was allowed to measure. Neither the company’s private domain nor a wealthy family’s idea of destiny could be allowed to determine who was protected. The rules had to apply to everyone.

暫停期間，伊萊亞斯宣布創辦人家族將向公共監測信託捐款，不附帶資金使用控制權。一名反對者問捐款是否想買原諒。伊萊亞斯說不是，並簽署將捐款與所有未決調查切割的文件。醫師補充：慈善行為不會讓企業主管免於法律追問。林雨認同這條界線。信託需要資源，更需要獨立；錢能修儀器，買不到決定儀器該量什麼的權力。不能再讓公司私人領域或富裕家族自認注定掌權的命運觀決定誰受保護。規則必須一體適用。

By dusk the replacement connection was ready. The team checked every step twice before beginning again. This time the valve moved a few centimeters and released a stream of cooler water into the test channel. The crowd cheered, but the engineer raised a hand to quiet them. Success at low pressure did not guarantee safety during a full pump cycle. He scheduled additional tests and kept the original machine sealed. Yu looked at the little current curling around a tile marker. It was not a rescued sea, nor a final victory, only a workable beginning. Sometimes, she thought, progress should be measured in centimeters before anyone attempted to measure it in promises.

黃昏時接頭換好，團隊每一步檢查兩遍才重啟。這次水閥移動幾公分，較冷的水流入測試通道。群眾歡呼，工程師卻舉手示意安靜：低壓成功不能保證正式運轉安全。他安排更多測試，原機仍封存。林雨望著繞過磁磚標記的小水流。這不是救回大海，也不是最後勝利，只是可行的開始。進步有時應先以公分量測，再用承諾丈量。

At night Bo delivered a troubling message. Company technicians planned to activate the main pump despite the council's freeze, claiming an emergency maintenance exception. The independent inspector disputed the claim but could not reach a judge until morning. Yu asked the engineer what might happen if both systems operated at once. He answered that no reliable model existed for that condition. They began preparing a temporary barrier and sent notices to low-lying homes. The test had offered hope, but the next few hours could erase it unless the island made a decision together.

夜裡阿博帶來壞消息：公司技師打算援引緊急維護例外，不顧議會凍結令啟動主幫浦。獨立稽查員反對，卻要到早上才能請法官處理。林雨問工程師兩套系統同時運轉會如何，他說沒有可靠模型可預測。眾人開始備臨時擋水設施，通知低窪住戶。試驗帶來希望，但若全島不能及時共同決定，接下來幾小時就可能讓希望消失。
