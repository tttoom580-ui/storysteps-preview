真相委員會

The Commission of Truth

雲岬十三光 · 第 22 / 25 章 · 4000–5000 · 英中對照 · 校訂中

A national commission arrived to assess the damage and design a judicial process for the victims. Its chair refused to begin with a speech. Instead, she invited families to describe what had happened in their own words. Some accounts were painfully profound, while others contained small practical details: the cost of a ferry ticket, the loss of a fishing day, the smell of a worker's clothes after a shift. Yu understood that no single story could contain the whole disaster. The assessment would combine testimony, medical records, water samples, and financial documents, with each source clearly marked. A public report was promised, but the chair warned that accuracy would require time.

國家調查委員會抵島，要評估損害並設計受害者的司法處理程序。主席沒有先演說，而邀家屬用自己的話陳述。有些證詞深刻而痛苦，有些只是實際細節：一張船票的價錢、一天無法捕魚、工人下班衣服上的氣味。林雨知道單一故事無法容納整場災難。評估將結合口述、病歷、水樣與財務文件，並清楚標示來源。主席承諾公開報告，也提醒準確需要時間。

At the burial site, specialists found a damaged coffin that belonged to an earlier generation, not the missing workers. Several people had already shared rumors online, and some mocked families for hoping it held answers. Mina asked the commission to ban the release of unverified names. The chair agreed to protect identities while allowing the evidence itself to be reviewed under appropriate rules. A young man spoke of his sibling, who had worked on the company ship and disappeared five years ago. He said the cruelest part was how strangers seemed to stereotype every worker as either a criminal or a helpless victim. His sister, he insisted, had been a complicated and capable person.

墓葬地點的專家找到一具受損棺木，卻屬更早世代，並非失蹤工人。網上早已有流言，有人還嘲笑懷抱希望的家屬。米娜請委員會禁止公布未經核實的姓名，主席同意保護身分，同時在適當規則下允許檢視證據。一名青年談起五年前失蹤的姊妹，說最殘酷的是陌生人總把工人刻板化成罪犯或無助受害者。他堅持，姊妹是一個有能力也很複雜的人。

The commission reviewed the company's claim that high productivity justified delaying expensive environmental repairs. A scientist explained how lower nutrient levels and increased suspended particle loads affected coral growth. The decline had begun gradually, then accelerated after the new pumps were installed. She did not claim every dying branch had one cause. Instead, she described which observations supported the company's role and which remained uncertain. The chief commissioner called this distinction a milestone in the investigation. It replaced a battle between slogans with a set of questions the public could test, and it revealed where more research was still needed.

委員會審查公司聲稱高生產力可以合理延後昂貴環保修繕的說法。科學家解釋營養物質下降與懸浮微粒增加如何影響珊瑚生長，衰退原先緩慢，新幫浦上線後才加速。她沒說每根枯枝都只有一個原因，而區分哪些觀測支持公司責任、哪些仍不確定。主席稱此為調查里程碑：口號之爭終於轉為公眾能檢驗的問題，也顯示哪些研究還不足。

A former engineer testified solo before the commission. He described a moment when supervisors ordered him to thrust a metal rod into a jammed valve while the system was pressurized. He refused, lost his contract, and later saw a younger colleague injured performing the same task. The engineer's report had been filed but never acted upon. Subsequent records showed that the warning had been copied and ignored three times. Yu felt anger rise, but she waited until the witness finished. His courage deserved careful attention rather than a dramatic interruption. The commission asked who had received his report and when, tracing responsibility through each signature. Some officials could not conceive of a hearing that welcomed a worker who had rejected an unsafe order; the commission answered that such objections were precisely what a safety system needed.

一名前工程師獨自向委員會作證，說主管曾命他在系統仍有壓力時把金屬桿猛推進卡住的閥門。他拒絕而失去合約，後來卻見年輕同事因同一工作受傷。工程師當年的報告雖已登錄，卻從未被處理；後續紀錄顯示警告被複製三次，也被忽略三次。林雨氣得想打斷，終究等他說完。他的勇氣值得細聽，而非戲劇化插話。委員會逐一查每份報告的收件人和日期。一些官員無法想像聽證會竟歡迎拒絕危險命令的工人；委員會回應，安全制度正需要這種異議。

The commission proposed a treaty among the island, neighboring communities, and national agencies to maintain shared water monitoring. It also recommended a small pension fund for permanently injured workers, financed separately from any criminal penalties. Yu worried that a treaty might sound grand while producing little. The chair invited her to help envision the safeguards: public budgets, independent testing, accessible complaint channels, and annual review meetings. The proposal remained unfinished, but each provision had an owner and a deadline. It was more useful than a promise that everyone would simply do better next time.

委員會提出島嶼、鄰近社區與國家機關之間的水質共同監測協定，也建議另設永久傷殘勞工年金基金，不與刑事罰則混為一談。林雨擔心協定只剩漂亮名稱。主席邀她構想保障措施：公開預算、獨立檢測、便利申訴管道及年度檢討。提案仍未完成，但每項條文都有負責者與期限，比「下次大家做得更好」的空話有用。

After the hearing, Yu met Mina beneath the newly planted trees. The light was fading, and the hill looked smaller than it had during the carnival. Mina said she was learning to live with two truths: her brother had survived, and the company had done terrible things to many others. Neither fact canceled the other. They walked toward the clinic without speaking for a while. Behind them the commission staff carried boxes of evidence to a secure van, preparing for subsequent interviews. There would be more difficult days, but the work no longer depended on one frightened researcher holding a secret map.

散會後林雨在新植樹旁遇見米娜，夕色中山坡看起來比嘉年華時小。米娜說，她正在學著與兩個事實共存：哥哥活著，公司也曾對許多人做了可怕的事；兩者互不抵消。她們安靜走向診所，身後委員會人員把證物箱搬上安全車，準備後續訪談。未來還有難日子，但工作終於不再取決於一位害怕的研究員獨守祕密地圖。
