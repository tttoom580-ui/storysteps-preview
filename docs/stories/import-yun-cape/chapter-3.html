溪口的使者

The Messenger at the Creek

雲岬十三光 · 第 3 / 25 章 · 4000–5000 · 英中對照

A freelance correspondent named Rae arrived by ferry, carrying a newspaper from the capital. She had come after receiving a message about abuse of diving workers. Yu invited her to a consultation with the island doctor, Bo, and an elder from the eastern creek. The group feared that the bay's fragile ecosystem was being turned into a testing ground. A local shellfish species had almost vanished, and a bird once common on the cliffs might soon be extinct. Rae warned them that a dramatic headline could bring attention, but careless reporting would also give the company an excuse to dismiss every true complaint.

自由撰稿記者蕾搭渡輪抵達，隨身帶著首都的報紙。她收到潛水工遭虐待的消息，才趕來調查。林雨邀她與島醫、阿博、東溪長者共同商議。眾人擔心脆弱的海灣生態系正在被當成試驗場。某種貝類幾乎消失，曾在海崖上常見的鳥兒也可能瀕臨滅絕。蕾提醒大家，聳動標題雖能吸引目光，草率報導卻會讓公司有藉口否認每一項真實指控。

At the pier, the mayor's deputy met them with a smile and unmistakable contempt. He claimed the tests were protected by a diplomatic arrangement with neighboring islands. Yu asked to see the agreement. The deputy refused, explaining that even the mayor had not read its final phase. Rae made a deliberate choice not to argue. She recorded his words exactly and asked who would notify the affected families if another diver fell ill. He stared at the water rather than answer. Behind him, a boy dragged a broken fish trap ashore, its bamboo slats coated in the same blue substance as the sample in Yu's bag.

碼頭上，鎮長副手帶著笑容迎接，眼神卻掩不住輕蔑。他宣稱實驗受到與鄰島的外交協議保護。林雨要求看協議，副手拒絕，說連鎮長都還沒讀到最後階段。蕾刻意不爭吵，只逐字記錄他的說法，接著問若再有潛水員病倒，誰負責通知家屬。副手看向海面，沒有回答。他身後，一名男孩把破魚籠拖上岸，竹片上覆著與林雨樣本相同的藍色物質。

Their next witness was a diver who had been ordered to sneak into a restricted trench at midnight. He said workers were asked to swap identity cards so that injuries could be attributed to nonexistent employees. The odds of proving this sounded poor until he unfolded a photograph showing six crew members beside an underwater machine. Its outer housing resembled the silver trophy displayed at the company's annual celebration. Rae understood the symbolism immediately: a prize presented as proof of invention might actually be a scaled model of equipment hidden in the sea. The diver's voice shook, but he refused to withdraw his account.

下一名證人是位潛水員。他曾被命令半夜偷偷進入禁航海溝，工人還得交換識別證，讓工傷紀錄落在不存在的員工名下。要證明這一切看似機會渺茫，直到他攤開照片：六名船員站在一部水下機器旁。機器外殼與公司年度慶典展示的銀色獎盃極為相似。蕾立刻看出意義：被當成發明成果炫耀的獎品，可能正是海底祕密設備的縮小模型。潛水員聲音顫抖，仍拒絕撤回證詞。

Yu's first instinct was to rush to the trench, but Bo urged patience. “Listen to your gut,” he said, “then test what it tells you.” The elder explained that a sustainable fishing culture depended on leaving breeding grounds untouched. Ocean Reach had promised to restore those grounds while secretly installing machinery beneath them. The company director was aging, and rumors named the deputy as his possible successor. That possibility made the deputy's loyalty easier to understand. He was not merely defending a policy; he was defending the future position he expected to inherit.

林雨第一個念頭是立刻前往海溝，阿博卻勸她先冷靜。「直覺可以聽，但要驗證。」東溪長者說，永續漁業的根本是讓繁殖區不受干擾。遠洋之境口頭答應復育，卻暗中在其下安裝機器。公司董事長年事已高，傳言鎮長副手可能成為他的接班人。這讓副手的忠誠不難理解：他守護的不只是一項政策，更是自己期待繼承的位置。

That night a sealed letter arrived at the chapel. It contained a timetable for the next test, marked in red ink with the words “Full Power.” There was no signature. Rae wanted to publish it immediately, but Yu insisted on checking its source. A false timetable could send protesters into dangerous waters at the very moment the company needed a public confrontation. They hid the letter in the lighthouse and arranged a meeting with the unnamed diver at dawn. The wind changed direction after midnight. By the sound of waves against the rocks, Yu knew something large had begun moving offshore.

那夜，一封密信送到教堂，裡面是下一次實驗的時程，紅筆圈著「全功率」三字，卻沒有署名。蕾想立即公開，林雨堅持查核來源。假的時程表可能引誘抗議者闖進危險海域，恰好成為公司想要的衝突。她們把信藏在燈塔，約無名潛水員天亮見面。午夜後風向轉了，海浪擊石的聲音也改變。林雨聽得出來，有個龐然大物正從外海移動。

At sunrise, the diver did not appear. Instead they found his boots, carefully placed at the end of the pier. A note inside one boot said, “Look at the dry dock, not the sea.” Rae photographed the scene without touching anything. The elder promised to keep watch while Yu and Bo checked the shipyard records. Their investigation had gained witnesses, documents, and a clear danger, yet it still lacked the single link that could connect the company's experiments to the dying reef. Yu looked at the empty boots and wondered how many people had already paid for that missing link.

日出時，潛水員沒有現身。眾人只在碼頭盡頭找到他的靴子，擺放得整整齊齊。其中一隻靴子塞著紙條：「查乾船塢，不要只查海。」蕾不碰現場，只先拍照。長者答應守望，林雨和阿博則去查造船廠紀錄。他們已有證人、文件與明確的威脅，卻仍缺少串起實驗與珊瑚死亡的關鍵證據。林雨望著那雙空靴，想著在證據出現前，究竟還有多少人已經付出代價。
