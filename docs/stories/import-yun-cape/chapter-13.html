水庫裡的影像

Images Beneath the Reservoir

雲岬十三光 · 第 13 / 25 章 · 4000–5000 · 英中對照 · 校訂中

The clinic reported an acute illness among technicians who had entered the tunnel, but doctors could not yet say whether it was contagious. The hospital administrator arranged separate rooms until the cause could be studied. Some families accused her of bias because company managers received private treatment. She acknowledged the unequal conditions and promised to publish the allocation of medical resources. Rae began filming a documentary about how different kinds of evidence were gathered. She avoided showing patients' faces without permission. Yu helped measure the tunnel's length, width, and depth, writing each dimension on a sheet that would later be checked by a second team.

診所通報進入隧道的技術員出現急性病症，醫師尚無法判斷是否具傳染性。醫院行政主管先安排分房觀察，部分家庭卻指責她偏袒接受私人治療的公司主管。她承認條件不平等，答應公開醫療資源分配。蕾開始拍攝紀錄片，說明不同證據如何取得；未經同意，她不拍攝病人面孔。林雨協助測量隧道長寬深，把每個尺寸寫下，供第二組人員複核。

The engineer placed a metal sphere on a tilted board and showed how gravity could move it without a motor. A similar principle operated an old safety valve near the island reservoir. If the valve were made to flip at the correct pressure, clean water could flow into the lower channel and dilute the contaminated discharge. The engineer emphasized that this was only a premise for planning, not authorization to alter a live system. Yu appreciated the caution. The thirteenth light had told her that machinery moved on a schedule; understanding how it moved was the first step toward stopping it safely. They would need drawings, permissions, and people trained to use them.

工程師把金屬球放在斜板上，示範重力如何不靠馬達使它移動。島上水庫旁的老式安全閥便用類似原理；若在適當壓力讓閥門翻轉，乾淨水便能流入下層水道，稀釋污染排放。工程師強調這只是規劃前提，還不是擅自更動運轉設備的許可。林雨欣賞他的謹慎。第十三道光已告訴她機器按時運動，理解運動方式才可能安全停機。他們需要圖紙、許可和受過訓練的人。

At a hearing, the company's defendant representative claimed the project had always followed local legislation. A government officer who had been assigned to oversee inspections admitted that several required visits had never occurred. He had accepted photographs in place of entry because the tunnels were difficult to reach. The public reacted angrily. The council chair reminded them that an officer's failure did not automatically prove the corporation guilty of every accusation. New evidence, however, could overturn earlier findings that described the site as safe. The officer promised to resign once the facts were recorded. Yu did not want a convenient resignation to become a substitute for repairing the damage.

聽證會上，公司被告代表宣稱計畫始終遵守地方法規。負責監督稽查的官員卻承認，幾次必要巡檢根本沒有執行；因隧道難進，他只收照片當作到場。群眾憤怒。議長提醒，官員失職不等於自動證明公司每項指控都成立，但新證據確實可能推翻過去安全的結論。官員答應完成紀錄後辭職。林雨不希望一場便利的辭職就取代實際修復。

The doctor invited several patients into a supervised therapy program and explained that treatment might take months. An elderly diver carried an olive-colored bag filled with tools he could no longer hold firmly. “The toll is not only what happened to my lungs,” he said. “It is the work I cannot do for my family.” Rae let the camera rest at his hands. When he finally looked up, he asked them to record something hopeful as well: his granddaughter had begun studying engineering because she wanted to build a safer port. His smile changed the room more than any official statement could have done.

醫師邀請幾名病患參與受監督的復健治療，提醒復原可能耗時數月。一名老潛水員提著橄欖綠工具袋，裡面的器具他已握不穩。「代價不只是肺受傷，」他說，「還有我再也不能為家人做的工作。」蕾讓鏡頭停在他的手上。他抬頭請她也錄下一件好事：孫女因為他的遭遇開始學工程，想造更安全的港。那抹笑容比任何官話更能改變房間氣氛。

That night Bo compared the new diagrams with an old map of the reservoir and noticed a weird detail. A tunnel shown in the oldest drawing had disappeared from every later version. Its entrance lay under a stone monument erected after a cyclone. Yu guessed the monument might conceal the original safety valve. The engineer proposed a noninvasive scan before anyone touched the stones. When the scan showed a hollow chamber, Yu felt the investigation shift. They were no longer only tracking what the company had built; they were discovering what previous generations had constructed to keep the island safe.

夜裡阿博把新圖和舊水庫圖比較，發現怪事：最早圖紙上有一條水道，在後來版本全都消失。入口位於颱風後豎立的石碑下。林雨猜測石碑掩著原始安全閥，工程師提議先做非侵入式掃描，不准直接敲石。掃描顯示下方果真有中空房間，林雨感到調查方向改變了。他們不只在追查公司建造了什麼，也在重新發現前人如何保護小島。

From the lighthouse roof Yu watched distant workers install temporary barriers beside the reservoir. The island's most vulnerable neighborhoods lay downhill, making the plan dangerous if calculations were wrong. She decided to request an independent inspection before moving any gate. Her father had once believed he could protect everyone by working secretly with one brilliant inventor. Now she understood the weakness of that approach. A safety system known only to a few people was easy to capture, neglect, or erase. This time, the map would be copied into the public archive before any machine was touched.

林雨從燈塔屋頂看遠處工人在水庫旁架臨時護欄。最脆弱的住區位於下坡，計算若有差錯就十分危險。她決定在動任何水門前要求獨立檢查。父親曾以為與一位天才發明家私下合作便能守護所有人，如今她看見這方法的弱點：只有少數人知道的安全系統，很容易被奪走、忽略或抹去。這一次，在碰機器前，她要先把地圖存入公開檔案。
