賭場裡的繼承人

The Heir at the Casino

雲岬十三光 · 第 11 / 25 章 · 4000–5000 · 英中對照 · 校訂中

用互動版查詞、朗讀與收藏

The old casino above the harbor had closed when tourism declined, but its glass windows still shone at night. Yu went there with Rae to meet Elias, the rumored heir to Ocean Reach's founder. The building's grand stairs were intact, while the workers' quarters behind it had collapsed. A former card dealer rolled forward in a wheelchair and warned them that security guards watched every guest. His old scar crossed his forehead like a pale thread. “This place used to fascinate visitors,” he said. “Now people come because the truth is hidden in a locked room.” Yu asked him whether Elias could be trusted. The man answered that family names were not evidence of character.

港灣山坡上的老賭場在觀光衰退後早已歇業，夜裡玻璃窗卻仍亮著。林雨和蕾前來會見伊萊亞斯，傳聞他是遠洋之境創辦人的繼承人。氣派樓梯完好無缺，後方員工宿舍卻坍塌。一名前發牌員坐著輪椅迎上來，提醒保全監視每位訪客。他額上舊疤像淡色絲線。「以前這裡讓遊客著迷，」他說，「現在人們只為了鎖在某房間的真相而來。」林雨問伊萊亞斯能否信任。老人說，姓氏並不能證明人的品格。

Elias waited in a former gaming workshop, surrounded by machines that had been stripped of their screens. He was young, polite, and apt to speak in precise legal phrases. Yu suspected he had rehearsed every answer. He claimed that the original underwater system had been designed as a place of refuge for coral during warming seasons. His father later sold the patents, and the new directors secretly changed the pumps. Elias wanted to recommend an independent inspection, but he feared the board would accuse him of industrial theft if he revealed company records. Rae asked why he had summoned journalists instead of going to the authorities. His silence lasted long enough to become an answer of its own.

伊萊亞斯在昔日機具工作間等候，四周機臺的螢幕都被拆走。他年輕有禮，說話卻習慣用精確法律用語，像每個回答都演練過。他說海底系統原意是在海水變暖時為珊瑚提供避難場所，父親後來賣掉專利，新董事私下改造幫浦。他想建議獨立勘查，卻怕公開公司檔案會被董事會控告竊取營業資料。蕾問他為何找記者而非主管機關，他沉默得太久，沉默本身便成了答案。

A sudden howl rose from the parking lot. Security officers were chasing a young mechanic who had tried to carry out a sealed box. One guard carried a rifle, though he kept its barrel toward the ground. Yu heard Elias gasp and saw his face lose color. The box contained replacement filters, each stamped with the same serial number found on the rusted scrap. Elias admitted that he had arranged their removal. “I thought I could stop the next cycle without anyone noticing,” he said. Yu reminded him that a damaged filter could cause new harm. Courage without planning might leave the reef worse than before. The mechanic agreed to put everything back under supervised conditions.

停車場突然傳來嚎叫聲。保全正追逐一名試圖帶走密封箱的年輕機械師，一名警衛帶著步槍，槍口朝地。林雨聽見伊萊亞斯倒抽氣，看到他臉色刷白。箱內是備用濾芯，序號和生鏽廢料相同。伊萊亞斯承認是自己安排取出。「我想不動聲色阻止下一次運轉。」林雨提醒他，損壞濾芯可能造成更大傷害。缺少計畫的勇氣，也許只會把珊瑚害得更慘。機械師答應在監督下放回原位。

The old dealer explained that the building's security cameras had once been upgraded to record every transaction. The casino's former manager had preserved copies of the footage on a disconnected drive. She would release it only if witnesses were protected. A packed corridor outside the archive was full of spectators, invited by the company to watch a public explanation. But there was no explanation, just a recorded speech meant to characterize the protesters as reckless. Rae recognized the trick. She began asking people what they themselves had seen instead of what the recording told them to believe. Their answers did not all agree, and that difference made them more credible. A clinical report on the injured dealer lay beside the drive, reminding everyone that people, not recordings, were at the center of the case. The manager also explained that the film had copyright restrictions, so the investigators would obtain lawful access before releasing copies.

老發牌員說，賭場攝影機早年升級，能記錄每筆交易，前經理把影片備份留在離線硬碟，願意在證人受到保護後交出。檔案室外走廊擠滿被公司邀來聽「公開說明」的旁觀者，但所謂說明只有一段錄好的演說，意圖把抗議者塑造成魯莽的人。蕾看穿手法，改問大家親眼看過什麼，而不是影片要他們相信什麼。答案並不全一致，反倒更可信。硬碟旁放著受傷發牌員的臨床報告，提醒眾人案件核心是人，不是錄影。經理也說影片涉及著作權限制，調查員必須先取得合法使用權才能公開副本。

At midnight Elias unlocked a drawer and handed Yu a letter written by his father before his death. It granted the inventor no permanent rights over the island's water; any license depended on public safety. The language was unusually clear. Elias confessed that he had spent years pretending not to know what the board was doing. He had told himself that an heir could not change a corporation by acting alone. Yu did not offer forgiveness. She asked him to appear before the council in daylight, bring authenticated records, and accept difficult questions. Outside, the harbor bell rang once. The sound echoed through the enormous empty rooms, sheer and lonely.

午夜，伊萊亞斯打開抽屜，交出父親生前寫的信。信中清楚規定，發明者不得永久占有島上水資源，任何授權都以公共安全為前提。伊萊亞斯承認多年來假裝不知道董事會在做什麼，總說繼承人獨自也改變不了公司。林雨沒有原諒他，只要求他白天到議會、帶可驗證的紀錄並接受質問。外頭港鐘響了一聲，單純而孤寂的回音穿過空蕩蕩的房間。

When Yu returned to the lighthouse, she found that someone had opened her father's tool chest. Nothing obvious was missing, but the dust had been disturbed around a small compass. Under its base was a note recommending a particular time to check the beacon: thirteen minutes after high tide. The note bore the founder's signature, not her father's. A strange partnership had existed between the two men long before their families became enemies. Yu began to wonder whether the original project had failed because its inventors disagreed—or because someone else wanted their collaboration to be forgotten.

林雨回到燈塔，發現有人打開過父親的工具箱。表面沒有東西失竊，但小羅盤周圍的灰塵被動過。羅盤底下有紙條，建議在滿潮後十三分鐘觀察燈號，署名竟是公司創辦人，不是父親。兩家變成敵人之前，兩名男子原來曾合作。林雨開始懷疑，當年的計畫究竟因發明者意見不合而失敗，還是有人刻意抹去了他們的合作。
