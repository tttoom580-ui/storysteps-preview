暗潮問卷

The Undertow Questionnaire

雲岬十三光 · 第 15 / 25 章 · 4000–5000 · 英中對照 · 校訂中

用互動版查詞、朗讀與收藏

The town's new questionnaire asked families how they experienced the damaged sea. Some questions measured lost yield from fishing, while others explored the perception of danger among parents. Yu insisted that the survey be accessible to people with poor eyesight and those who could not read the official language. A retired ticket collector offered to visit homes and record oral answers. He had once worked at the old port, where every departure depended on a single brass rod connecting the signal bell to the gate. His memories were ordinary, but they revealed which safety routines had quietly disappeared after the company's compound expanded.

鎮上新問卷詢問居民如何承受受損海域的影響，有的量測漁獲產量下降，有的探討家長對危險的感知。林雨堅持問卷必須讓視力不好或不識官方語言的人也能使用。退休驗票員願意登門記錄口述回答。他曾在老碼頭工作，那時每次出航都仰賴一根黃銅桿連結訊號鐘與水門。看似平凡的回憶，卻揭露公司廠區擴張後哪些安全程序悄悄消失。

A local comedian attended the meeting and joked that every new committee needed its own committee. The crowd laughed, but he soon grew serious. His foster brother had worked in the trench and come home coughing after every shift. Nobody had dared to question the company because the town sheriff routinely joined its private patrol. The comedian agreed to distribute survey forms at his performances if researchers would underline the promise of confidentiality. Yu accepted the help but made clear that participation was voluntary. People who had lost trust in institutions needed more than cheerful slogans; they needed control over their own stories.

一名本地喜劇演員在會上笑說，每成立一個委員會，總得再成立委員會來管理它，觀眾大笑；他接著正色說，寄養兄弟曾在海溝工作，每次下班都咳嗽。無人敢質問公司，因鎮警長常與私人巡邏隊同行。喜劇演員願在演出時發問卷，條件是把保密承諾畫線強調。林雨接受幫助，卻重申填答自願。失去制度信任的人，需要的不只是快樂標語，更要能掌控自己的故事。

Behind the school, an old canal began to decay because maintenance funds had been diverted for years. The drainage failure revealed a subtle pattern: neighborhoods with little political influence had received fewer repairs. A new conception of public safety emerged from the survey. Protecting coral was inseparable from protecting drinking water, housing, and the dignity of workers. Yu showed residents a map of the island with damaged infrastructure marked in different colors. She did not use the map to assign blame; she asked people to correct it. Their corrections produced a more superb record than any official report prepared from an office far away.

學校後方舊排水渠因多年經費挪用而逐漸腐朽。故障揭露微妙規律：政治影響力較小的社區，得到的修繕也較少。問卷於是形成對公共安全的新理解：保護珊瑚離不開飲水、住宅與勞工尊嚴。林雨展示不同顏色標出受損設施的島嶼地圖，不用它急著歸咎，而請居民糾正內容。共同修正後的紀錄，比遠方辦公室寫出的任何報告都更出色。

At the lighthouse, an estate lawyer explained that Yu might inherit her father's property while still being required to maintain a public beacon. She felt uneasy about becoming a private owner of something the town depended on. Elias offered a guest suite at the casino while repairs took place, but she declined. She preferred the small attic room above the workshop, where she could hear the waves and keep the documents close. At regular intervals, the thirteenth light flashed. Bo recorded the timing while Yu compared it with the pump schedule. Each new observation reduced the number of possible explanations, but none gave them a safe way to stop the machine yet.

燈塔裡，遺產律師說林雨或許能繼承父親房產，卻同時承擔維護公共燈號的責任。她對私人擁有全鎮仰賴的設施感到不安。伊萊亞斯提供賭場套房讓她整修時住，她拒絕了，寧願住在工作間樓上的小閣樓，聽得到浪聲，也能守著文件。第十三道光以規律間隔閃爍，阿博記下時間，林雨對照幫浦排程。每次觀察都縮小可能性，但還沒有安全停機的方法。

The survey's early results angered the mayor, who accused Yu of turning public opinion against him. She answered that the survey was a way to listen, not an election. Residents could disagree with its conclusions and still deserve to be heard. After the meeting, the retired collector handed her an envelope full of old shift schedules. One list showed maintenance crews sent into the underwater chamber three days before the first coral deaths. A note beside the shift said, “Do not resume ordinary operation until the third warning light clears.” Nobody on the present staff remembered the warning. The sentence sat on the page like a message from someone trying to reach the future.

問卷初步結果讓鎮長大怒，指控林雨操縱民意反對自己。她回答，問卷是為了傾聽，不是選舉；居民就算意見不同也值得聽。會後退休驗票員交給她一封舊輪班表，其中一組人員在珊瑚最初死亡前三天曾進海底機房，旁邊註記：「第三警示燈解除前，不得恢復正常運轉。」現在員工竟無人記得這條警告。那句話像某人設法跨越時間傳來的訊息。

Yu brought the schedules to the clinic and asked Noor to compare them with her notebook. Three names matched the missing workers, including Mina's brother. The sheriff arrived unexpectedly, not to arrest anyone, but to deliver a box of records he had kept at home. He admitted that years of friendship with company managers had clouded his judgment. Yu thanked him for the records without excusing the delay. Trust could be rebuilt through actions and accountability, not a single confession. Above the clinic entrance, the island flag moved in the night wind, and the rain began to fall.

林雨把班表送到診所，請努兒與筆記本比對，三個名字正是失蹤工人，包括米娜哥哥。警長意外現身，不是抓人，而是送來一箱多年藏在家中的紀錄。他承認與公司主管的多年交情蒙蔽了判斷。林雨感謝資料，卻不因此免除他拖延的責任。信任得靠行動與問責重建，不是一句認錯就夠。診所門上的島旗在夜風中飄動，雨終於落下。
