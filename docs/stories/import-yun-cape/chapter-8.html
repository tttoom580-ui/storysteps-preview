沒有簽證的訪客

The Visitor Without a Visa

雲岬十三光 · 第 8 / 25 章 · 4000–5000 · 英中對照 · 校訂中

用互動版查詞、朗讀與收藏

The island's largest gathering began as a memorial for missing workers, but by morning it had turned into chaos. Several strangers in ragged raincoats arrived claiming to be foreign divers trapped by the company. They had no visa or employment papers. A customs officer announced that they were not entitled to speak at the public meeting, but Mina challenged him: a person's right to describe an injury did not vanish with a missing stamp. Yu listened carefully, knowing that sympathy alone would not establish the visitors' identities. One stranger described the dry dock accurately, even naming a broken handrail that Yu had seen. Another could not remember the color of his own work boat.

島上最大一場集會原是悼念失蹤工人，到了早上卻陷入混亂。幾名穿破雨衣的陌生人出現，自稱遭公司困住的外籍潛水員，卻沒有簽證和雇用文件。海關人員宣稱他們無權在公聽會發言，米娜反問：難道少一枚印章，人就失去陳述傷害的權利？林雨仔細聽證詞，明白同情不能替代身分查核。一人能準確描述乾船塢破損扶手，另一人卻想不起自己工作船的顏色。

Rae invited the visitors into a quiet salon behind her aunt's bookstore. With more flexibility than the officials had shown, she let each person tell his story without interruption. Their accounts formed a spectrum from convincing detail to obvious contradiction. One described a “sea vein” carrying warm water through the rock, a phrase from an island myth about a sleeping whale. Another spoke of a mechanical pump that could stall if sand entered its intake. Yu noticed that technical descriptions appeared only after Rae placed her photographs on the table. She covered the pictures and asked the group to draw what they remembered instead.

蕾把訪客帶到姑媽書店後的小沙龍。她比官員更有彈性，讓每個人不受打斷地說完。證詞從細節可信到前後矛盾，呈現完整的差異光譜。一人說岩石裡有運送暖水的「海洋血脈」，那是沉睡巨鯨的本地神話用語；另一人說機械幫浦吸入沙子就會停轉。林雨發現他們只在看到蕾展示照片後才開始使用技術細節，便把照片蓋住，請眾人自己畫出記憶。

The bookstore owner announced a shortage of drinking water. Trucks had been delayed at the port, and the company had offered to deliver supplies only if the council agreed to suspend its investigation. This was a strategic threat disguised as charity. Yu and Bo organized neighbors to minimize the damage: they collected rainwater, brought sealed bottles from the clinic, and arranged a fair distribution list. An angry man claimed there was no evidence whatsoever that the corporation had blocked the trucks. Rae agreed that the claim required proof. She then produced a recording of a manager instructing drivers to wait for a political decision before unloading.

書店主人宣布飲水短缺。貨車被拖在港口，公司承諾只要議會暫停調查就立刻送物資。這是披著慈善外衣的策略性威脅。林雨和阿博動員鄰居降低損害：集雨水、從診所運密封瓶裝水、制定公平分配表。一名憤怒男子堅稱完全沒有證據證明企業攔車。蕾承認指控必須有證據，隨即播放經理指示司機等待政治決定才能卸貨的錄音。

At the memorial, the mayor offered an elaborate tribute to the workers but avoided naming those still missing. Mina interrupted him with a list checked by their families. The crowd grew quieter with each name. A visiting scientist explained a theoretical model showing how contaminated groundwater could reach coral without leaving a visible surface trail. The explanation was dense, and people struggled with comprehension until Ivo poured colored water through layers of sand in a glass jar. The demonstration did not prove the company's guilt, but it gave everyone a way to understand the question. For the first time, villagers began asking for specific tests rather than repeating rumors.

追悼會上，鎮長發表冗長致敬詞，卻避開仍失蹤者的姓名。米娜拿出家屬核對過的名單打斷他，名字一個個念出來，人群越來越安靜。來訪科學家解釋受污染地下水可不經表面流動便抵達珊瑚的理論模型，但眾人難以理解。伊沃便用玻璃罐的分層沙土示範染色水如何滲透。這不證明公司的罪責，卻使大家學會問對問題。村民第一次開始要求具體檢測，不再只傳謠言。

That night Yu found a photograph of the strange visitors in an old news archive. They were actors hired for a promotional film about the harbor, and one had once played an alien in a low-budget production. Their arrival had been timed to discredit genuine witnesses. Rae published the finding with copies of the contracts, while emphasizing that fraudulent visitors did not erase the testimony of real workers. Yu stared at the harbor from the salon window. Someone had tried to turn the island's pain into a performance, and nearly succeeded. At least the people now knew to separate the drama from the evidence—and to keep listening when the next witness came forward.

當晚，林雨在舊新聞檔案發現幾名訪客的照片：他們竟是受雇拍港口宣傳片的演員，其中一人曾在低成本作品裡扮演外星人。他們到來的時間被刻意安排，目的是讓真證人也失去信用。蕾附上合約公開此事，同時強調假訪客不能抹去真工人的證詞。林雨從沙龍窗口望向港灣。有人差點把島民的痛苦變成一齣表演；幸好，大家開始學會區分戲劇效果和證據，並願意聽下一個真正的證人說話。

Before midnight, the first real witness arrived at the bookstore back door: an exhausted woman with a diving scar and a sealed notebook. She asked for no reward, only a place where her account could be taken without interruption. Yu brought her water, Rae set down the camera, and Mina closed the curtains. The woman opened her notebook to a page marked “Thirteen.” It contained the location of an underground machine that had never appeared on a public map. Whatever name the company gave it, the device was still operating, and its next scheduled cycle would begin in four days.

午夜前，第一位真正的證人來到書店後門：一名滿身疲憊、帶著潛水疤痕、緊抱密封筆記本的女子。她不要酬勞，只求一個能完整陳述的地方。林雨端來水，蕾放下相機，米娜拉上窗簾。女人翻開標著「十三」的一頁，上面是從未出現在公開地圖上的地下機器位置。無論公司替它取了什麼名字，它都還在運轉，下一次啟動就在四天後。
