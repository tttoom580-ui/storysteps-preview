The Last Confrontation

雲岬十三光 · 第 24 / 25 章 · 4000–5000 · 英中對照 · 校訂中

The final public hearing began with a bitter confrontation between the mayor and the independent prosecutor. The mayor argued that his scheme had been designed to preserve jobs, not hurt the reef. The prosecutor displayed an extensive index of records showing that officials repeatedly ignored warnings. The evidence formed a coherent account: private contracts shaped public decisions, monitoring was obstructed, and emergency systems were left unsafe. Yu sat beside Mina, trying to soften the tension with a reassuring touch. Across the hall, victims' families held photographs rather than banners. Nobody needed to invent a villain; the documents already described a chain of choices that had caused harm.

最後一場公開聽證會從鎮長與獨立檢察官的激烈對質開始。鎮長辯稱自己的計畫是為保工作，不是傷害礁體。檢察官展示大量紀錄的索引，證明官員一再無視警告。證據構成連貫敘述：私人合約影響公共決定，監測受阻，緊急系統被放任不安全。林雨坐在米娜身旁，輕握她的手緩和緊繃。家屬只舉照片，沒有旗幟。根本不需要虛構壞人，文件已揭露導致傷害的一連串選擇。

The mayor's lawyer called the proceedings a violation of the town's development doctrine and warned of a possible riot outside. Officials had prepared a safe route through the rear entrance, but the crowd remained peaceful. A witness described the agony of waiting for a husband who never returned, then asked that anger not be used to threaten other families. The judge allowed her to finish without interruption. Yu felt the past years blur as the woman spoke. She could remember the first white coral branch, the last blue flash, and the countless small acts of kindness that had kept the investigation alive. Justice would be incomplete if it forgot those acts.

鎮長律師稱程序違反地方發展信條，警告外頭可能暴動。官員準備好後門安全通道，但群眾始終和平。一位證人說明等待永遠不會回家的丈夫有多痛苦，接著請大家別用怒火威脅其他家庭。法官讓她說完。林雨聽著，過去幾年的記憶像模糊在一起：第一根白珊瑚、最後一道藍光，還有無數支撐調查的小小善意。如果正義忘了那些善意，就仍不完整。

A prosecutor asked about the planned execution of the emergency shutdown order, meaning how the operation had been carried out, not a punishment. The mayor tried to turn the legal term into a sensational claim. The judge corrected him and brought the discussion back to the records. A technician described a nasty instruction to disable an alarm; his supervisor had even slapped the warning label off the panel during an argument. Independent photographs supported the testimony. The court did not decide all criminal charges that day, but it confirmed that public safety orders had been disobeyed. The finding could not restore lost lives, yet it established an important part of responsibility.

檢察官問緊急停機命令如何「執行」，此處指作業施行，不是刑罰。鎮長故意把法律用語扭成聳動指控，法官立即糾正，讓討論回到紀錄。技師描述主管下過惡劣命令，還曾在爭執時用手拍掉控制板上的警告標籤。獨立照片支持證詞。法院當天沒判完所有刑案，卻確認公共安全命令遭違反。這結論不能恢復生命，但確立了重要責任。

During the lunch break, Yu went to the archive to browse the papers her father had left. She found a sheet of music marked with one simple chord, used long ago to teach lighthouse keepers the rhythm of warning signals. On the back, her father had written that every system should welcome correction from the people who relied on it. Yu had spent years speculating about his disappearance and fearing that a single revelation would change everything. Now she understood that no one page could explain a life. What mattered was what the living chose to do with imperfect knowledge.

午休時林雨到檔案室瀏覽父親留下的紙張，找到一張標著單一和弦的樂譜，早年守塔人用它練習警報節奏。背面父親寫著，每套系統都該容許仰賴它的人提出修正。林雨多年猜測父親失蹤經過，總怕單一揭露就顛覆一切，如今明白沒有哪一頁紙能說明整個人生。真正重要的是活著的人如何使用不完美的知識。

The ruling required the operator to fund cleanup under independent supervision and prohibited the mayor from making further decisions about the disputed contracts. Further trials would determine personal liability. Elias accepted that his family's company must comply regardless of his private intentions. Mina asked the judge to ensure that affected spouses and children could review the compensation system without expensive lawyers. The judge directed the commission to publish simple explanations and an appeal process. Yu was surprised by the quietness of the moment. After so much noise, a few carefully worded orders began to change the rules governing the island. The written accord between the cleanup agency and local representatives would be published in full. As evening descended, the document was read aloud to the waiting families.

裁定要求營運商在獨立監督下出資清理，禁止鎮長繼續處理爭議合約；個人責任待後續審判。伊萊亞斯承認無論自己動機如何，家族公司都得服從。米娜請法官確保受害者的配偶與子女不必花高額律師費就能理解補償制度。法官命委員會公布淺白說明與申訴程序。林雨驚訝這一刻竟如此安靜；在漫長喧鬧後，幾句謹慎措辭的命令開始改變小島規則。清理機關與地方代表簽訂的書面協議將全文公開。暮色降臨時，文件也向等待的家屬朗讀。

At sunset the prosecutor asked Yu whether the years of work had been worthy of the sacrifice. She thought of families still grieving, patients still in treatment, and coral that might take decades to recover. No outcome could balance such losses neatly. She said the effort had been necessary, not glorious. Then she returned to the lighthouse, where the restored public beacon shone a steady white light. It no longer flashed secret instructions to a closed control room. From every side of the harbor, sailors could see it and understand its purpose. That change, small in appearance, was enormous in meaning.

日落時檢察官問林雨，這些年的努力是否值得犧牲。她想到仍悲傷的家庭、接受治療的病患、可能要數十年才恢復的珊瑚，沒有成果能把損失整齊抵銷。她說努力是必要的，不是光榮的。回燈塔時，修好的公共燈號正放出穩定白光，不再向封閉控制室發出祕密指令。港口各處的水手都能看見並理解用途。看似小小變化，意義卻巨大。
