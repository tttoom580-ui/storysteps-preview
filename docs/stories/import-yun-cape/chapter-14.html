燈塔拍賣會

The Lighthouse Auction

雲岬十三光 · 第 14 / 25 章 · 4000–5000 · 英中對照 · 校訂中

An unexpected auction notice appeared on the lighthouse door. The company claimed that Yu's father had pledged the building as collateral, and it intended to sell the land to pay an old debt. Yu went to the town hall to combat the claim. A clerk showed her a document supposedly granting the company full ownership, but the signature had been copied from a much older letter. Whoever prepared it had tried to deceive the court. Yu asked a lawyer to articulate the difference between a family debt and a public navigation duty. The lighthouse served every boat in the harbor. It could not be treated as an ordinary house without addressing that responsibility.

燈塔門上忽然貼出拍賣公告。公司宣稱林雨父親曾拿建物抵押，如今要賣地抵還舊債。林雨到鎮公所抗辯，書記出示的產權文件看似合法，簽名卻是從更早的信件複製而來。有人試圖欺騙法院。林雨請律師清楚說明家族債務與公共導航義務不同；燈塔服務港裡每艘船，不能像普通住宅般買賣而忽視責任。

The public hearing took place in the old cathedral, where cool air moved between stone columns. A compassionate nun opened the porch to families waiting outside in the rain. She remembered Yu's father bringing stranded sailors to shelter there. In the front row sat an enthusiastic group of young volunteers who wanted to abolish the town's private-water licensing scheme. A legal adviser recommended that they first amend the relevant ordinance, then require transparent compliance reports from every operator. The volunteers found that painfully slow, but the nun reminded them that enduring repairs were often less exciting than righteous speeches.

公聽會在老大教堂舉行，冷風穿過石柱。富有同情心的修女打開門廊，讓雨中的家屬進來。她記得林雨父親曾在此安置受困船員。前排坐著熱情的青年志工，希望廢除私人水域許可制度。法律顧問建議先修訂相關條例，再要求各營運商提出透明合規報告。志工嫌程序太慢，修女提醒他們，能長久的修復常比正義演說不夠刺激。

During the inspection, Bo found an old storage room containing a small animal skull, children's toys, and a bundle of engineering drawings. The skull belonged to a shark, once used in lessons about the difference between a feared predator and a damaged ecosystem. A drawing marked several underwater vents where heated emission from industrial machinery might enter the bay. The map included a note from the founder proposing a public grant to monitor the water independently. The proposal had never been funded. Instead, the money had been redirected to expand private tourism facilities. Yu wondered how many accidents were born from decisions that appeared harmless on paper.

稽查時阿博在舊儲藏室找到小動物頭骨、兒童玩具及一束工程圖。頭骨是鯊魚的，曾用於教孩子分辨可怕掠食者與受損生態系。圖上標出海底數個排氣口，工業機械的熱排放可能由此進灣。創辦人還留下一項補助提案，要資助獨立水質監測，最後卻沒有編列經費，錢反而挪去擴張私人觀光設施。林雨想知道多少事故源自文件上看似無害的選擇。

A banker arrived with the final sale papers. Yu's lawyer pointed out that the town had no legislative authority to privatize the navigation signal while ships depended on it. The banker was embarrassed, then admitted he had received instructions from a consultant rather than checking the law himself. Across the aisle, a child wearing a theatrical wig whispered that the mayor looked like a king who had misplaced his crown. The remark made several exhausted witnesses laugh. Rae resisted the urge to print it as a headline. The case was serious, and a clever insult could easily distract readers from the documents that mattered.

銀行代表帶來最終出售文件。林雨的律師指出，只要船隻依賴導航燈，鎮公所便沒有立法權能將它私有化。銀行代表尷尬承認，他只聽顧問指示，自己未查法律。走道另一側，戴戲劇假髮的孩子偷偷說鎮長像弄丟王冠的國王，惹得疲憊證人都笑了。蕾忍住不用這句話當標題的衝動。案子很嚴肅，聰明嘲諷很容易搶走真正重要文件的注意力。

When the hearing ended, Elias proposed a new prospect for the building: make it a public laboratory and navigation museum supported by a legally protected trust. The lawyer said such a trust might be possible after title disputes were settled. Yu agreed to discuss it but refused to trade away evidence in return for a promise. At sunset, Mina brought a group of juvenile divers who had been training in shallow water. They placed flowers beneath the lighthouse window for those who never came home. Yu stood with them, aware that saving a building mattered only if it helped protect living people.

散會後伊萊亞斯提出新的可能：把燈塔改成公共實驗室兼導航博物館，由法律保護的信託支撐。律師說須先解決產權爭議。林雨願意討論，卻拒絕用證據交換承諾。日落時，米娜帶著一群在淺水受訓的年少潛水員前來，在窗下獻花悼念沒有回家的人。林雨與他們站在一起，明白保住建築只有在能保護活人時才真正有意義。

Late that evening Yu found a legal appendix folded inside the founder's grant proposal. It specified that the inventor's equipment would revert to public control if its operation endangered the reef. The clause was narrow, and the company's lawyers would contest every word. Still, it provided the first documentary route toward stopping the machine without entering the forbidden trench. Yu copied the appendix, photographed its seals, and gave duplicate copies to three independent custodians. The lighthouse remained under threat of auction, but for the first time its history offered a path forward rather than another unanswered question.

深夜，林雨在創辦人的補助提案裡找到一份折起的法律附錄，規定設備若危及礁體，控制權須回歸公眾。條文很狹窄，公司律師勢必逐字爭辯，但它首次提供不闖入禁區也能合法停機的文件途徑。林雨影印、拍攝封印，將副本交給三名獨立保管者。燈塔仍面臨拍賣威脅，然而它的歷史終於指出一條前路，而非更多疑問。
