珊瑚復育方程式

The Coral Equation

雲岬十三光 · 第 23 / 25 章 · 4000–5000 · 英中對照 · 校訂中

The recovery team began with an ecological survey rather than a ceremony. Researchers measured dissolved carbon, acidity, and the amount of sediment crossing the reef. Ivo drew an equation to compare locations, then wrote an explicit warning beneath it: the formula estimated risk but could not predict every organism's fate. A donor wanted a sophisticated machine installed immediately, while a fisherwoman proposed restoring the small tidal canal that once flushed sediment away. Yu asked the team to test both possibilities. The island had already suffered from people who treated impressive technology as a substitute for careful observation. Their new work would begin from conditions in the water, not photographs in a brochure.

復育團隊從生態調查開始，而非先辦典禮。研究員測量溶解碳、酸鹼度和穿過礁體的泥沙量。伊沃畫出比較各地的方程式，並在下方清楚註明：公式能估計風險，不能預測每種生物命運。捐助者想立刻裝精密機器，一名漁婦則主張修復能沖走泥沙的舊潮汐水渠。林雨請團隊都先測試。小島已為「令人驚艷的科技能取代細心觀察」吃過苦，新工作應從水中條件而非宣傳照片出發。

The first public planning rehearsal was held at the fish market. Residents were asked to imagine a worst-case scenario in which repairs caused new flooding during a storm. A bakery patron brought sweet pastry, and a chubby child spent the meeting drawing fish with enormous smiles. The scene felt ordinary, which comforted Yu. Yet discussion grew tense when an official asked everyone to conform to the proposed schedule without debate. Mina objected that public discourse was the purpose of the meeting. The official apologized and invited written criticism. A plan that could survive honest questions would be stronger than one protected from them. Beside the pastries, farmers offered roasted squash from inland gardens, reminding planners that the health of the bay and the health of the farms were connected.

首次公開規劃預演在魚市場舉行，居民要想像最壞情境：修繕遇暴風雨導致新淹水。麵包店常客帶來甜糕點，一個胖嘟嘟的孩子整場畫著笑得誇張的魚，平凡場景讓林雨安心。但官員要求大家不必爭論、直接配合時程時氣氛轉緊。米娜反對，說公開討論正是會議目的。官員道歉，邀請書面批評。能承受誠實問題的計畫，比不許被質疑的計畫更強。甜糕點旁，農民也帶來內陸菜園烤的南瓜，提醒規劃者海灣與農地的健康緊密相關。

At the damaged trench, technicians prepared to inject a harmless tracer into a sealed test pipe to modify their model of water movement. They explained every step to observers and had a backup valve ready. Bo showed Yu the old cable that had formed a dangerous tangle around broken coral. The line could not simply be cut; fragments might drift across the reef. Divers would remove it in sections while the current remained weak. The company offered to provide workers, but the independent inspector required that all instructions come from the public team. Yu approved. Cooperation was possible, but control of the evidence and safety decisions could not quietly return to the party under investigation.

受損海溝旁，技師準備向密封試驗管注入無害追蹤劑，用來修正水流模型，過程完全對觀察者公開並備好備援閥門。阿博帶林雨看一條舊電纜，它纏成危險亂結，繞住破珊瑚；不能直接剪斷，否則碎片可能漂過礁區。潛水員要趁水流微弱時分段移除。公司主動提供工人，獨立稽查員卻規定安全指令必須來自公共團隊。林雨贊同：可以合作，但證據與安全的控制權不能偷偷回到被調查者手中。

The recovery office also faced rising unemployment. Fishing restrictions protected the surviving reef but left many families without income. Yu proposed paid monitoring jobs for experienced fishermen, with training and fair selection. A council member began to whine that science had become an excuse to spend public money. Rae asked him to compare those costs with the funds previously spent hiding pollution. He did not answer. At the same meeting, a planner showed how coastal smog from visiting ships might further harm air quality during repairs. The investigation was expanding, but Yu did not want it to become an endless list without priorities. Clean water and safe livelihoods remained at the center.

復育辦公室還面對失業上升。捕魚限制保護存活礁體，卻使許多家庭失去收入。林雨提議讓有經驗的漁民接受訓練，受薪參與監測，並以公平方式招募。一名議員開始抱怨科學只是亂花公帑的藉口，蕾請他對比過去掩蓋污染的支出，他不回答。同場規劃人員指出，維修期間來往船隻形成的煙霾也可能傷害空氣。議題愈來愈廣，林雨卻不希望變成沒優先順序的清單。乾淨的水和安全生計仍是核心。

At sunset a new sensor sent readings from the reef to a public display near the harbor. Some numbers were encouraging; others showed continuing harm. Yu refused to label the day a triumph. She had learned that recovery could be real without being complete. The child with the smiling fish came to show her a picture of the thirteenth light, now drawn as a window rather than an eye. “So everyone can look through it,” he said. Yu pinned the drawing beside the latest measurements. A public window, she thought, was a better image for knowledge than a hidden lamp that only a few people could interpret.

日落時，新感測器把礁區讀值傳到港口公開看板。有些數字令人振奮，有些仍顯示損害。林雨拒絕把今天稱為勝利，因為復原可以真實存在，卻尚未完成。畫笑臉魚的孩子拿來一張第十三道光的畫，這次畫得像窗戶，不再像眼睛。「這樣人人都能看進去。」他說。林雨把畫貼在最新數據旁。知識應像公共窗口，而非只有少數人能解讀的隱藏燈號。

Before leaving, Ivo prepared a short report summarizing the day's measurements and the concerns residents had raised. He included mistakes his own team had made and invited others to correct them. Yu watched the young researcher submit the document to the open archive. His confidence did not come from claiming certainty. It came from knowing what had been measured, what remained unknown, and who would be able to check the work. On the reef beyond the harbor, a few small coral branches still held their color. They were not proof of a full recovery, but they were worth protecting.

離開前，伊沃整理當日測量和居民疑慮成短報告，也列出團隊自身犯的錯，邀他人修正。林雨看年輕研究員把文件提交公開檔案。他的自信不是來自宣稱自己絕對正確，而是知道測了什麼、還不知道什麼，以及誰能檢驗成果。港外礁區少數珊瑚枝仍保有顏色。那不是全面復原的證據，卻值得好好保護。
