水晶證物

The Crystal Evidence

The council moved its hearing to the school arena, where an outside celebrity scientist would testify about the strange underwater light. Crowds filled the seats as though the event were entertainment. The scientist held up a transparent crystal and explained how minerals could glow under pressure. She was intent on avoiding fraud: the demonstration showed what was physically possible, not what had actually happened below the reef. A company lawyer tried to poke holes in her account, demanding that she declare the system safe. She refused. The council chair announced that the next session would focus on evidence rather than reputation. Yu silently thanked her for drawing that line.

議會把聽證會移到學校體育場，一名頗有名氣的外地科學家將說明海底異光。人群像來看表演般擠滿座位。科學家舉起透明水晶，解釋礦物如何在壓力下發光；她一心避免科學詐欺，強調示範只證明物理上可能，並未證實礁底的實況。公司律師試圖挑出漏洞，逼她宣布系統安全，她拒絕了。議長宣布下一場會議應以證據而非名望為中心。林雨暗自感謝她畫出這道界線。

Mina introduced the families' lawsuit against the operator and displayed a careful portfolio of photographs, medical records, and samples. Each document had an index and a chain of custody. A doctor explained the difference between a prescription intended to reduce pain and a cure that removed its cause. He also warned that poor nutrition could worsen the divers' recovery. Some families looked depressed by the long list of uncertainties. Yu saw their shoulders sag and wished there were a sentence that could make the years of suffering smaller. There was none, so she remained beside them while the doctor finished speaking.

米娜介紹家屬對營運商提出的訴訟，展示經過整理的照片、醫療紀錄與樣本資料集，每件都有編號和證物保管鏈。醫師說明止痛處方與真正消除病因的治療不同，也提醒營養不良可能拖慢潛水員復原。漫長的不確定性讓部分家屬神色消沉。林雨看見他們垂下肩，恨不得有一句話能減輕多年痛苦；但那樣的話不存在，她只好安靜站在旁邊陪他們聽完。

The company presented a random set of samples collected from clean beaches far from the damaged reef. Its spokesman insisted that a plural interpretation of the evidence was necessary: there were many seas and many possible causes. Yu agreed with the principle but questioned the sampling design. The chosen beaches were all on the opposite side of the island. She used a large map to reverse the order in which the results were displayed. Once the locations came first and the measurements second, the misleading pattern became clear. A girl in the front row whispered to her mother that numbers could tell the truth while pictures around them told a lie.

公司提出一組從遠離受損礁體的乾淨海灘隨機採集的樣本。發言人強調應保留多種解讀：海域很多，可能原因也很多。林雨同意原則，卻質疑採樣設計：所有海灘都在島的另一側。她用大地圖把展示順序反轉，先看地點再看數值，誤導就清楚了。前排女孩悄聲對母親說，數字也許是真的，包裝數字的圖片卻可能說謊。

During the break, an angry guard tried to shove Rae away from a storage rack where sealed samples were kept. Yu stepped between them and called the clerk. The guard claimed he was preventing theft, but the official order only banned unauthorized handling of evidence, not observation through a glass door. The clerk confirmed this distinction and documented the incident. Outside, residents from the residential hillside had brought food for witnesses who could not afford to miss work. Sharing a meal did not make anyone's testimony more accurate, but it made the burden of speaking a little easier to carry.

休息時，憤怒的警衛試圖把蕾從放置密封樣本的架子旁推開。林雨擋在中間，叫來書記官。警衛聲稱防止偷竊，但正式命令只禁止未經許可觸碰證物，沒有禁止隔玻璃觀察。書記官確認界線並記錄事件。外頭住宅區居民帶來食物，供請假出庭的證人充飢。一起吃飯不會使證詞更準確，卻讓開口的負擔稍微輕一些。

That evening the scientist asked Yu what the ultimate goal of the investigation should be. “A conviction?” she suggested. Yu answered that ending harm came first, with compensation and accountability following through lawful procedures. The scientist warned that public anger might fade before any judgment arrived. Yu knew she was right. It was easier to fill an arena with outrage than to maintain a laboratory, protect witnesses, and monitor water every month for years. She wrote this warning on a slip of paper and pinned it above her desk. The battle for the reef would not end with a dramatic announcement.

當晚科學家問林雨，調查的終極目標是什麼，「定罪嗎？」林雨說先停止傷害，再循法律程序追求補償與責任。科學家提醒，判決來前群眾怒火可能就會散去。林雨知道她說得對：用憤怒坐滿體育場，比長年維護實驗室、保護證人、每月監測水質容易得多。她把警告寫在紙上貼到書桌前。保衛礁群的工作不會因一次戲劇性的宣告就結束。

After everyone left, a janitor discovered a transparent fragment beneath the witness stand. It was not ordinary glass but the same synthetic crystal used in the underwater sensor housing. Someone had carried a damaged part into the arena, perhaps as a warning or perhaps hoping an expert would identify it. Yu asked the janitor to leave it untouched until an evidence officer arrived. Across the dark harbor, the thirteenth light flashed in a new pattern: once, twice, then a long silence. She copied the rhythm into her notebook and set her alarm for the next high tide.

人群散去後，清潔工在證人席下找到透明碎片。那不是普通玻璃，而是海底感測器外殼使用的合成晶材。有人把破損零件帶進場館，也許為了警告，也許盼望專家認出來。林雨請清潔工別碰，等證物官到場。黑暗港外，第十三道光出現新節奏：一下、兩下，然後長長的沉默。她抄下閃光規律，為下一次滿潮設好鬧鐘。
