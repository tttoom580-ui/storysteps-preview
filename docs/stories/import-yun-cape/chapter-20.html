黑潮啟動之夜

The Night of the Black Current

雲岬十三光 · 第 20 / 25 章 · 4000–5000 · 英中對照 · 校訂中

The emergency began with a dull vibration beneath the seawall. A pump had activated, sending warm water toward the reef. Engineers faced a severe constraint: the emergency valve was only partially tested, while the company's main system was already operating. A single wrong move could create a deadly pressure surge. Yu listened as the chief engineer elaborated on three possible responses. None was safe without conditions. The most cautious option was to evacuate low-lying streets, protect the clinic's power, and wait for pressure to fall naturally. The mayor called the plan cowardly. Yu asked him which homes he was willing to risk in order to appear brave.

海堤下方沉悶震動揭開緊急事件，幫浦已開始把熱水送向礁群。工程師受嚴格限制：緊急水閥只完成部分測試，公司主系統卻已運轉。一步錯誤可能引發致命壓力波。林雨聽首席工程師詳細解釋三種應對，沒有一種能在無條件下保證安全。最保守方案是撤離低窪街區、保護診所電力，等待壓力自然下降。鎮長斥之怯懦，林雨反問他願犧牲哪些人家，只為顯得勇敢。

A council senator arrived by helicopter with an emergency order authorizing independent intervention if the machine threatened public safety. Company lawyers challenged his interpretation of the law. Rae displayed a graphic chart of pressure readings, showing an unprecedented rise near the old mansion pillar. The senator asked who would qualify to lead the operation. The answer was not a politician or a famous scientist, but the engineer who had designed the test procedure and demonstrated its limits. He accepted the role only after insisting on the right to halt the work at any point. Yu recognized the importance of that condition. Authority without the ability to say no was not responsibility. The mainstream broadcasters had already sent crews to the harbor, but the senator asked them to report the limits of the evidence as carefully as the most dramatic measurements.

議會參議員搭直升機帶來緊急命令，准許獨立團隊在機器威脅公共安全時介入。公司律師爭執法律解釋。蕾展示壓力圖，指出老宅石柱附近出現前所未有的上升。參議員問誰有資格領導行動。答案不是政治人物或名科學家，而是親自設計測試並說明限制的工程師。他要求任何時刻都能下令停止，才願意承擔。林雨明白這條件的重要：沒有說不的權利，權力就不是真正的責任。主流媒體已派採訪隊到港口，參議員要求他們報導證據限制時，也要像報導最戲劇性數據那樣謹慎。

Inside the control room, a red warning light appeared beside a label marked radiation. The scientist immediately clarified that the sensor recorded weak background energy from a calibration source, not proof of a dangerous radioactive leak. The actual emergency concerned heat and pressure. The company spokesperson tried to tempt journalists into reporting the red light as the sole problem, hoping to distract from the pumps. Yu insisted they compare the ratio of incoming and outgoing water. A rising mismatch revealed that more fluid entered the trench than the old channel could release. The energy reading mattered, but it was not the cause of the mechanical crisis.

控制室裡，一盞標示「輻射」的紅燈亮起。科學家立即澄清，感測器記錄的是校準源微弱背景能量，不能當作危險輻射洩漏證據。真正緊急的是熱與壓力。公司發言人試圖引誘記者只追紅燈，轉移對幫浦的注意。林雨堅持比較入水與出水比率，發現流入海溝的水愈來愈多，舊水道卻排不出去。能量讀值值得查，但並非機械危機的起因。

As the crisis began to unfold, a hidden chamber ruptured beneath the pier. Fragments of stone flew across the water, and a cloud of steam seemed to erupt from the crack. Workers used a large vacuum pump to remove water from the electrical room while volunteers carried patients uphill. The clinic director reported a new complication: several people depended on oxygen machines and could not be moved without power. Bo organized portable batteries and asked boat owners to provide quiet transportation. The island's revenue from tourism had once dominated every public meeting; tonight nobody asked what the rescue would do to business. They asked where the remaining patients were.

危機持續展開，碼頭下隱藏房間突然破裂，石片飛過水面，蒸氣像爆發般從縫中噴出。工人用大型真空抽水設備移走電力室積水，志工將病人搬上山。診所主任報告新的併發問題：幾名病患依賴供氧機，沒有電就無法安全移動。阿博調度行動電池，請船主提供安靜運送。過去所有公聽會總談觀光收入，今夜沒人再問救援影響生意，只問還有哪些病人需要幫助。

The engineer decided to release a small fragment of the channel's retaining barrier, under controlled conditions, to reduce pressure. He made clear that the action could flood a deserted warehouse, and the council approved the trade-off publicly. A diver known as the harbor's warrior for his decades of rescues volunteered to inspect the release path. The engineer refused to let him dive alone. Two trained partners accompanied him while observers watched the water level from higher ground. The gate moved, the pressure fell, and the warehouse flooded exactly as predicted. For the first time that night, the screaming alarm slowed to a steady pulse.

工程師決定在受控條件下移除排水通道擋牆的一小部分，以降低壓力，但可能淹沒無人的倉庫；議會公開同意取捨。一名因多年救援而被稱為港口戰士的老潛水員自願檢查放水路徑，工程師拒絕讓他獨行，由兩名受訓夥伴同行，高處還有觀測人員。水門移動，壓力下降，倉庫按預測被淹。當夜第一次，尖銳警報漸漸變成平穩脈衝。

Near dawn the main pump stopped. Yu stood beside the seawall, watching gray water swirl around the empty warehouse. The emergency order had prevented a worse disaster, but it had not cleaned the reef or restored lost health. The mayor called the outcome proof that the system could be controlled. Yu replied that emergency success was no reason to resume the process that caused the emergency. The senator promised a public inquiry and independent monitoring. As the sun rose, the thirteenth light flashed once and went dark. For the first time in years, the island no longer depended on an unexplained signal to tell it when danger had passed.

天亮前主幫浦終於停下。林雨站在海堤看灰水繞過被淹的空倉庫。緊急命令阻止更大災難，卻沒有清理礁體或治好病人。鎮長說結果證明系統可以控制，林雨回說成功處理緊急事件，不是恢復造成事故程序的理由。參議員承諾公開調查和獨立監測。日出時，第十三道光最後閃一次便熄滅。多年來第一次，島民不再需要靠不明燈號判斷危險是否過去。
