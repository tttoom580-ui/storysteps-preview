北門之下

Beneath the North Gate

雲岬十三光 · 第 6 / 25 章 · 4000–5000 · 英中對照 · 校訂中

At low tide, the dry dock looked less like a harbor than a stage abandoned after a disaster. Yu unfolded her father's map while Rae read the council's agenda over the radio. A municipal inspector had agreed to join them, but a federal officer ordered everyone to stay outside the fenced zone. “Beware of unstable ground,” the officer shouted, as though safety were the only reason for the order. Bo pointed toward a narrow crack where seaweed continued to cling to the concrete. Fresh water seeped out, carrying a sharp smell. The same smell had filled the glass tube handed over by the vanished sailor. Yu slipped on gloves, collected a sample, and marked the place with a piece of orange cloth.

退潮時，乾船塢不像港口，倒像災難過後被遺棄的舞臺。林雨攤開父親的地圖，蕾透過無線電念出議會議程。一名市政稽查員同意陪同，聯邦官員卻命所有人留在圍欄外。「小心地面不穩！」他高喊，彷彿安全是唯一理由。阿博指出混凝土裂縫，海藻仍緊緊附著其上。滲出的淡水帶著刺鼻氣味，和失蹤船員交出的玻璃管一模一樣。林雨戴上手套取樣，綁上橘布作記號。

The inspector carried a camera and a little bottle of vinegar, useful for checking whether a white deposit might be ordinary limestone. When the liquid touched the powder on the gate, it fizzed, but a nearby yellow stain did not react. Under a portable microscope, the stain was crowded with an unfamiliar organism. Its delicate structure suggested life, yet the sample contained a toxic compound the clinic had already detected near the reef. Rae tried to make a metaphor of it for her article: an invisible army wearing the uniform of harmless sea life. Yu asked her not to publish that image. A metaphor could clarify fear, she said, but it could also lead readers to mistake possibility for proof.

稽查員帶著相機和一小瓶醋，用來判斷白色沉積物是否只是石灰岩。醋滴在水門白粉上立即起泡，一旁的黃色污漬卻毫無反應。攜帶式顯微鏡顯示，污漬中擠滿陌生微生物；它們像活物，樣本卻含有診所曾在礁區測出的有毒化合物。蕾想用「披著無害海洋生物外衣的隱形軍隊」作文章隱喻，林雨請她暫時不要發表。隱喻能說明恐懼，也可能使讀者把可能性錯當成證據。

A loud crack sounded beneath the pier. The inspector pulled a small shield from his pack and held it over Rae as stones fell. They entered the tunnel one at a time, finding a skeleton of rusted pipes arranged around a central chamber. Something struck Yu in the rib, and she cried out, but it was only a loose metal bar swinging in the current. Bo tried to rib her about being frightened by an old gate, then saw her trembling hands and apologized. Ahead, a rigid door stood open. Behind it, the concrete floor was marked with the tracks of recently moved equipment. Whatever lay there had not been abandoned with the old dock.

碼頭下傳來裂響。稽查員從背包取出小型防護盾，替蕾擋住落石。眾人依序進入隧道，見到中央房間四周排列著骨架般的鏽管。一根被水流帶動的鐵桿撞上林雨肋骨，她痛得驚叫。阿博本想取笑她被舊水門嚇到，見她雙手發抖便道歉。前方一扇僵硬沉重的門竟敞開著，水泥地留著新近搬動設備的輪痕。這裡並沒有隨舊船塢一同荒廢。

The chamber contained a locked cabinet and a child's painted picture of a baseball game. On its back someone had written, “Last inning, then home.” A worker had apparently sheltered here during long shifts. Yu felt an unexpected grief at the ordinary message. Rae photographed a ledger listing weekly chemical deliveries under the heading “research genre,” an absurd attempt to make inventory sound like literature. Beside the cabinet lay a letter from a workers' rebellion years earlier. The letter demanded protective clothing, clean water, and the right to leave the platform without punishment. It was addressed to Vale's predecessor. History had arrived in the same room as the present, and neither had been properly heard.

房間裡有上鎖櫃子，還有孩子畫的棒球比賽，背面寫著：「最後一局，然後回家。」顯然曾有工人在漫長輪班間躲到這裡。平凡的字句讓林雨突然難過。蕾拍下一本帳冊，化學品週送貨量竟被標成「研究文類」，像是荒唐地把庫存寫成文學。櫃旁另有多年以前工人反抗的公開信，要求防護衣、乾淨飲水及不受處罰的離職權；收件人是維爾的前任。過去和現在聚在同一房間，卻都沒有被好好傾聽。

Outside, the sound of engines grew louder. The patrol boats were about to overtake the inspector's small launch and cut off the exit. Rae fought back a sob as she sent photographs through the weak signal. Yu remembered an old passage from her grandmother's theology book: protection was not the same as obedience. She left a copy of the workers' letter where the approaching officers would see it, then led her friends through the low emergency passage toward the shore. The tide began to rise, filling their footprints. In her pocket, the glass sample clicked against her keys. They had found evidence of a concealed operation, but not yet a reason to trust any authority to protect it. The mayor had announced a new nominee for the port authority, but the crowd demanded that appointments be examined in daylight rather than traded behind closed doors.

外頭引擎聲逼近，巡邏艇即將超過稽查員的小艇，切斷出口。蕾含著啜泣，把照片從微弱訊號送出去。林雨想起祖母神學書裡的一段話：保護不等於服從。她把工人公開信的影本留在進來的警員會看見的地方，帶朋友沿著低矮逃生道向岸上走。漲潮開始吞沒腳印，口袋裡的樣本瓶碰著鑰匙發出輕響。他們找到秘密作業的證據，卻還不知道哪個掌權者值得託付。鎮長宣布新的港務主管提名人，群眾卻要求人事任命在陽光下接受檢視，而非在關門後交換。

When they emerged beneath the clinic, Bo turned back to see a faint blue light moving behind the gate. It stopped whenever he stopped, as if someone were carrying a lamp at precisely the same distance. Yu refused to chase it. The day's evidence needed witnesses and sealed containers, not another reckless adventure. She asked the inspector to sign every label in front of Rae's camera. Then she sat on the steps, rubbing her bruised side, while the first fishing boats returned empty to harbor. The north gate had opened, but the mystery behind the thirteenth light had only become deeper.

他們從診所下方出口爬出時，阿博回頭看見水門後一抹藍光移動。他停下，光也停下，像有人始終與他保持相同距離。林雨拒絕追趕。今天的證據需要證人和密封容器，而不是更多魯莽冒險。她請稽查員在蕾的鏡頭前逐一簽署封條，自己坐在石階上揉著瘀傷的側腹，看第一批漁船空手返港。北門已經打開，第十三道光的謎卻更深了。
