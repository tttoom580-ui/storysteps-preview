山脊上的急件

The Errand on the Ridge

雲岬十三光 · 第 2 / 25 章 · 4000–5000 · 英中對照

Before dawn Bo asked Yu to accompany him on an urgent errand to the northern ridge. A storm had damaged the island's old warning sensor, and the clinic needed readings before the next fishing fleet departed. They climbed through wet grass while a truck struggled to haul a replacement battery behind them. From the ridge Yu saw their destination: an observation hut beside a narrow gorge. The sea looked peaceful, but the monitor showed an extraordinary fall in oxygen levels. Bo said the change might be beneficial for certain microscopic species, yet disastrous for the fish that kept the village alive.

天亮前，阿博請林雨陪他跑一趟緊急差事，前往北方山脊。暴風雨損壞了舊式預警感測器，診所必須在漁船出海前取得讀數。兩人穿過濕草地，後方卡車拖著沉重的備用電池艱難上坡。從高處望去，目的地是一座建在峽谷旁的觀測小屋。海面看似平靜，螢幕卻顯示含氧量急遽下降。阿博說，這種變化或許對某些微生物有益，對仰賴魚群生存的村落卻可能是災難。

The island doctor arrived with a pot of fish stew wrapped in cloth. She was vocal about an unexplained fever spreading among divers, although officials refused to call it an epidemic. An interview respondent in her medical survey had reported seeing a glowing liquid released from the corporate vessel. Yu wrote down his description. A faded reminder on the hut wall warned workers never to enter the water without a partner. Yet the ship's diving team had been working in pairs of one, an impossible phrase that appeared repeatedly in their reports. Someone had altered the records, or someone had decided that a worker did not count as a person.

島上醫師帶來一鍋用布包好的魚燉湯。她毫不避諱地談起潛水員之間流傳的不明熱症，儘管官員拒絕稱它為流行病。醫療調查的一名受訪者曾看見企業船隻排出發光液體，林雨記下細節。小屋牆上貼著褪色的提醒：不可單獨下水。但公司報告卻反覆寫著「每組一人」的潛水編制。不是有人修改紀錄，就是有人認為工人不算真正的人。

A corporate lawyer called the station and warned them about legal liability if they published unverified numbers. He offered to convert the hut into a private laboratory, complete with new equipment, if the island would stop its independent tests. The offer held a powerful temptation: Bo had spent years repairing machines with wire and patience. But he refused. “New walls do not guarantee honest readings,” he said. Yu used a small knife to trim seaweed from the sensor casing. Beneath it she found scratches that looked deliberate. The machine had not simply broken in the storm; someone had opened it with a tool.

企業律師打電話到觀測站，警告他們若公布未經證實的數字，恐須承擔法律責任。他提出將小屋改建為私人實驗室、提供全新設備，交換條件是島方停止獨立檢測。這提議極具誘惑力：阿博多年來只能靠鐵絲和耐心修理機器。但他拒絕了。「新牆壁不能保證誠實的讀數。」林雨用小刀修剪感測器外殼上的海藻，意外發現刻意留下的刮痕。機器不只是被風雨破壞，有人拿工具打開過它。

At noon a siren rose from the bay. A company launch had lost power near the rocks, and its crew prepared to plunge into the cold water rather than wait for rescue. Bo lit a bright torch and signaled them toward a sheltered channel. The doctor organized blankets on the shore. Yu climbed down the ridge carrying the damaged sensor in her bag. She could not decide whether the sailors were victims or participants in the concealment, but she knew that leaving them to drown would settle nothing. When the boat reached safety, one sailor slipped a sealed glass tube into her coat pocket without a word.

正午時，海灣傳來警報。一艘公司快艇在礁石附近失去動力，船員準備跳進冰冷海水，不願等候救援。阿博舉起強光手電筒，引導他們朝避風水道前進。醫師在岸邊安排毛毯。林雨背著損壞的感測器走下山脊。她不知道船員是受害者還是隱瞞真相的一分子，但見死不救絕不會帶來答案。快艇安全靠岸時，一名船員默默把密封玻璃管塞進她的外套口袋。

Back at the clinic, Yu compared the tube's contents with seawater taken beyond the reef. The fluid gave off a faint blue glow only when shaken. She asked Bo whether a mineral could cause such a transformation. He described several possibilities but insisted that guesses were not evidence. The island had once trusted a promise that industrial fishing would bring prosperity; broken boats and empty nets had followed instead. This time they would preserve the chain of evidence, including the date, the temperature, and the name of the sailor who had handed her the sample. Without those details, the most remarkable discovery could become an ordinary rumor.

回到診所，林雨把玻璃管的液體與礁外海水比較。管中液體只有晃動時才透出淡藍光。她問阿博礦物是否會造成這種轉變。阿博列出幾種可能，卻堅持猜測不是證據。這座島曾相信工業捕魚能帶來繁榮，最後只換來破船和空網。這一次，他們必須完整保存證據來源，包括日期、溫度，以及交給她樣本的船員姓名。否則，再驚人的發現也會淪為普通謠言。

That evening the doctor returned with news: the sailor had vanished from the clinic's waiting area. On his chair lay a receipt for fuel bought by a shell company three weeks before the first reef deaths. The handwriting matched the signature on the generator service log. Yu pinned both pages to her father's old map and drew a line between the ridge station and the offshore lights. “This is becoming more than an environmental investigation,” Bo said. Outside, children watched a fishing boat limp home without its catch. The island was changing, and the people who had caused that change were still close enough to hear the waves.

傍晚醫師帶來消息：那名船員從候診區失蹤了。他座位上留著一張三週前空殼公司購油的收據，時間正是珊瑚開始死亡之前。筆跡竟與發電機維修紀錄上的簽名相同。林雨把兩張紙釘在父親的舊海圖上，在山脊觀測站與外海燈光之間畫線。「這已經不只是環境調查了，」阿博說。窗外，孩子們看著一艘沒有漁獲的船緩緩返港。島嶼正在變化，而引起變化的人仍近得可以聽見海浪。
