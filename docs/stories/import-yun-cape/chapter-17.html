閣樓裡的供詞

Confession in the Attic

雲岬十三光 · 第 17 / 25 章 · 4000–5000 · 英中對照 · 校訂中

用互動版查詞、朗讀與收藏

The former company accountant requested a meeting in the lighthouse attic. She carried two boxes of accounting records and a handwritten confession. Yu would not let her begin until the investigator had explained her rights. The woman, Sela, said she had learned to adore the island as a child but had spent her career helping a company that damaged it. She had worked to conduct internal audits, yet her reports were changed before reaching the board. The documents were comparable to the ledgers found in the warehouse, except for one crucial addition: the names of those who ordered the changes. Yu asked why Sela had waited so long. She replied, “Because I was afraid of being ordinary and guilty.”

公司前會計要求在燈塔閣樓會面，帶著兩箱帳務紀錄和手寫供詞。林雨先請調查員說明她的權利才讓她開口。女子名叫瑟拉，童年深愛這座島，職涯卻協助傷害海洋的公司。她負責內部稽核，但報告送董事會前總被修改。文件可與倉庫帳冊比對，差別在於新增了下令改帳者的名字。林雨問她為何現在才說。瑟拉回答：「因為我害怕承認自己只是個普通而有罪的人。」

Sela explained the scheme's motive: the company needed to show investors a low likelihood of environmental damage before selling its shares. A director ordered staff to initiate new accounting categories that hid chemical disposal as ordinary maintenance. When the hidden pumps malfunctioned, the company hired contractors to make the site look unused. The repairs were destructive because the workers had to remove natural stone from the reef. Sela had signed invoices knowing they were false. Rae asked whether she expected the confession to erase those choices. She said no. She only hoped the truth might keep others from repeating them.

瑟拉說明陰謀動機：公司出售股份前必須讓投資人相信環境損害機率很低。董事命員工建立新會計科目，把化學廢料處理藏在普通維修裡。幫浦故障後，公司雇包商把場地偽裝成未使用，但為了施工，他們拆除天然礁石，造成破壞。瑟拉明知發票不實仍簽署。蕾問坦承是否能抹去選擇，她說不能，只希望真相讓後人不再重犯。

The confession mentioned a delegate sent from the supreme regulatory council, a powerful official who had approved exceptions without visiting the site. His signature was on a page covered in smudged ink. Bo lifted the paper toward the light and saw the strange texture of an altered seal. The signature might have been forged, which could protect the official or implicate someone else. Yu called a document expert rather than deciding the issue herself. Through the open doorway, the harbor was visible beneath a sky turning the color of steel. Time was short, but an accusation supported by a fake signature could destroy their case.

供詞提到最高監管委員會派來的一名代表，這位高官從未到現場就批准例外。簽名頁墨跡模糊，阿博迎光一照，發現印章表面紋理異常，可能遭偽造；那既可能保護官員，也可能牽連他人。林雨請文件專家鑑定，不自行下結論。敞開門口外，港灣上方天空轉成鋼鐵色。時間有限，但倚靠假簽名提出指控，足以毀掉整個案子。

Below them, an old woman climbed the stairs carrying warm soup. She remembered Yu's father's devotion to the lighthouse, and she had heard the legendary story of how he once crossed the bay during a typhoon to repair its lamp. The woman said that hero stories could be a kind of torment for families left behind: people praised a brave man but seldom helped his grieving daughter. Yu felt herself shiver at the kindness and discomfort of the remark. For years she had imagined her father as either a hero or a victim. The possibility that he had also made mistakes was harder to hold, yet more human.

一位老婆婆端著熱湯上樓。她記得林雨父親對燈塔的奉獻，也聽過他颱風中越海修燈的傳奇。婆婆說，英雄故事有時會折磨留下的家人：大家讚美勇者，卻很少真正幫助悲傷的女兒。這句帶善意的話讓林雨微微發抖。多年來她只把父親想成英雄或受害者，如今要承認他也可能犯錯，雖然更困難，卻也更真實。

A bitter smell made Bo sniff the air. Water had begun entering the lower archive through a cracked filter pipe. They moved the documents to higher shelves and photographed every label before removing the boxes. Sela helped without being asked. When the emergency workers arrived, she handed the lead investigator a sealed copy of her confession. She knew she might face prosecution. Yu watched her walk down the wet stairs and understood that accountability was neither revenge nor instant redemption. It was a long, uncertain process that began when a person stopped hiding behind excuses.

阿博嗅到苦味，檔案室下方一根裂開的濾水管開始漏水。眾人先拍攝標籤，再把文件搬到高處。瑟拉主動幫忙，救援人員到場時，她把密封供詞交給主調查員，明白自己可能遭起訴。林雨看著她走下濕階，終於懂得問責既不是復仇，也不是立刻洗白，而是漫長不確定的過程，從一個人停止拿藉口躲藏開始。

At dawn the document examiner called. Several signatures were genuine, including the mayor's. The supreme council delegate's name, however, had been written using a stamp without authorization. Someone inside the company had claimed authority they did not possess. Yu placed the authenticated records into three separate boxes bound for independent custodians. The next hearing would be difficult, but at last they had a map of responsibility rather than a fog of suspicion. Outside the lighthouse, a solitary fishing boat departed toward the reef, following the old safe channel that her father had marked years ago.

天亮時文件鑑定師來電，多枚簽名為真，包括鎮長的，但最高委員會代表的名字竟是未經授權蓋章。公司內部有人冒用根本沒有的權力。林雨把鑑定完的紀錄分成三箱，送不同獨立保管者。下一場聽證不會容易，但他們終於有了責任的脈絡圖，而不再只有懷疑迷霧。燈塔外，一艘孤單漁船循父親多年前標出的安全水道，向礁群出發。
