枯萎的海灣

The Withering Bay

雲岬十三光 · 第 1 / 25 章 · 4000–5000 · 英中對照

When marine archivist Lin Yu returned to Yun Cape Island, she expected an argument over her father's abandoned lighthouse, not an immense ring of dead coral. The white branches lay under clear water like the bones of an ancient city. Beside the harbor chapel, fishing families formed a cluster around a notice promising compensation for damaged nets. The notice said nothing about the reef. Yu had spent ten years in a metropolitan laboratory, where problems arrived as numbers on screens. Here each number had a face: a child who could no longer swim, a fisherman afraid to take his boat out, a mother watching the tide with folded arms.

海洋檔案研究員林雨回到雲岬島時，以為等著她的只是父親遺留燈塔的產權爭執，沒想到先看見大片死去的珊瑚。潔白的枝椏躺在清澈海水裡，像古城的骸骨。港邊小教堂旁，一群漁家圍著賠償告示，政府承諾補償受損漁網，卻隻字不提礁群。林雨在大都會的實驗室待了十年，習慣把問題化為螢幕上的數字；但在故鄉，每個數字都是一張真實的臉：不能再下海的孩子、不敢出航的漁夫，以及望著潮汐發愁的母親。

Her old friend Bo had become the electrical supervisor at the island clinic. He showed her a battered generator that had started failing whenever the research vessel Aurora entered the bay. “That may be a transaction between two unrelated events,” he joked, then admitted he was not certain. An optional maintenance report listed a cable laid across a protected underwater habitat. The company called the cable a gift to the island. Bo called it a warning. When Yu asked who had approved it, he pointed to an empty signature box and changed the subject. Someone had made a decision without accepting responsibility.

舊友阿博如今是島上診所的電力主管。他帶林雨看一部老舊發電機：每逢「曙光號」研究船進港，它就會故障。「也可能是兩件無關事情碰巧同時發生，」他先開玩笑，接著承認自己也不確定。某份非必要填寫的維修報告卻記著一條穿過保育棲地的海底電纜。公司說這是送給島民的禮物，阿博卻視為警訊。林雨問誰批准施工，阿博指著空白簽名欄，默默轉開話題。有人做了決定，卻不肯負責。

At the evening forum, Mayor Qiao praised the island's heritage and introduced Vale, director of the Ocean Reach corporation. Vale described a generous provision for future schools, then refused to disclose where the company's experiments were taking place. A young guide, clearly envious of Vale's elegant suit and mainland fame, applauded first. Yu felt an impulse to interrupt, but she remembered her father's advice: watch what people avoid saying. Instead she asked why the coral had begun to wither in the same week that the ship arrived. The room grew so quiet that rain on the roof sounded like falling stones.

晚間公聽會上，喬鎮長歌頌島嶼的文化遺產，接著介紹「遠洋之境」公司的負責人維爾。維爾提到將為未來學校提供慷慨資源，卻拒絕揭露實驗地點。一名年輕導覽員顯然羨慕他的西裝與本島名聲，帶頭鼓掌。林雨衝動地想打斷對方，但想起父親教她要留意人們刻意不說的話。她於是問：為什麼研究船到港的同一週，珊瑚就開始枯死？全場驟然安靜，屋頂的雨聲像碎石落地。

Vale answered with practiced certainty. The sea was changing everywhere, he said, and no single business could be blamed. Then an elderly woman rose from the last row. She had kept tide records for forty years and knew every current around Yun Cape. When she tried to reach the front, she caught her foot on a loose board and began to stumble. Yu crossed the aisle to help her. The woman pressed a folded chart into Yu's palm. Three dark marks showed places where fishermen had seen unnatural flashes below the waves. “Your father drew the same marks,” she whispered. “Before he vanished, he said the thirteenth lighthouse was not built on land.”

維爾以熟練而篤定的語氣回答：全球海洋都在變化，不能單獨責怪任何企業。最後一排卻有位老婦站起。她記錄潮汐四十年，熟悉雲岬附近每股洋流。走到前方時，她被鬆動木板絆得踉蹌，林雨趕忙上前攙扶。老婦把摺好的海圖塞進她掌心，上頭有三處黑點，標示漁民看見海底異光的位置。「妳父親也畫過同樣的記號，」她低聲說，「失蹤前，他說第十三座燈塔不是蓋在陸地上。」

After the meeting Bo accompanied Yu up the hill. The chapel bell rang behind them, though no one was inside. At the lighthouse gate, Yu found her father's key still hidden beneath a stone carved with a gull. The lock resisted, then turned. Dust covered the stairs, but a fresh muddy footprint crossed the floor. Someone had visited recently. On a desk lay an envelope addressed to Yu in her father's handwriting. Inside was a single page of measurements and one sentence: “Trust the people who live with the water, not those who own the charts.” She stared at the page until the wind shook the window glass.

散會後，阿博陪林雨上山。身後的教堂鐘聲忽然響起，然而教堂裡沒有人。她在燈塔門前找到父親藏在海鷗雕石下的鑰匙，費力轉開鏽鎖。樓梯全是灰塵，地板卻留著一道新鮮泥腳印——最近有人來過。書桌上有封信，收件人是她，字跡屬於父親。信裡只有一頁測量數據和一句話：「相信與海共生的人，不要只相信擁有海圖的人。」她看著那句話，直到海風震動窗玻璃。

Yu did not tell Bo about the letter immediately. She needed time to distinguish fear from insight. If her father had discovered a secret under the bay, perhaps his disappearance had not been an accident. But she also knew how easily grief could transform coincidences into a story. She copied the measurements into her notebook, photographed the footprint, and locked the door behind them. Tomorrow she would visit the reef herself. Tonight she sat on the hillside watching the harbor lights flicker one by one. Far offshore, a thirteenth light blinked twice, paused, and then disappeared into the mist.

林雨沒有立刻把信告訴阿博。她需要分辨恐懼與真正的洞察。若父親發現海灣下的祕密，他的失蹤也許不是意外；但悲傷也可能讓人把巧合拼成故事。她把數據抄進筆記本，拍下腳印，再鎖好門。明天，她要親自下海查看珊瑚。今夜，她坐在山坡上，看港口燈火一盞盞閃動。遙遠外海，第十三道燈光閃了兩次，停了停，便消失在霧中。
