最冷的田地

The Coldest Field

遊戲降臨後，我只想開一家修理店 · 第 7 / 25 章 · 3000–4000 · 英中對照 · 校訂中

The northern farms were covered in white frost, although autumn had only just begun. The game's weather did not follow the season. A farmer named Bao met Lin beside a broken water pump. Beyond him, a herd of goats stood close together, and an old mule refused to leave the shelter. "If the pipes freeze tonight, the animals will have no water," Bao said. "We also have families here who depend on the farms." Lin looked at the icy surroundings and understood that this district needed more than another temporary lamp.

北方農場覆蓋著白色霜雪，雖然秋天才剛開始。遊戲裡的天氣並不遵循季節。農夫包先生在壞掉的抽水機旁迎接林澄。他身後一群山羊緊緊擠在一起，一頭老騾子則不肯離開棚舍。「如果今晚水管結冰，動物就沒有水喝了。」包先生說，「這裡還有靠農場生活的家庭。」林澄看著冰冷的四周，明白這個街區需要的不只是另一盞臨時照明燈。

Bao had already hired a plumber, but the man could not repair the pump without a missing seal. A former rescue helicopter sat in a nearby field. Its rubber fuel-line seal was almost the same size. Niko wanted to remove it at once. Lin insisted on accuracy before action. The helicopter might be needed for emergencies, and stripping a useful machine could injure someone later. She measured both parts and found a slight difference. "We will make a replacement from spare rubber," she said. "Borrowing a shape is not the same as stealing a safety part."

包先生已經請過水管工，但因為缺少密封圈，對方無法修好抽水機。附近田裡停著一架退役救援直升機，它的橡膠燃油管密封圈尺寸十分接近。尼可想立刻拆下來，林澄卻堅持先精確測量。直升機將來可能還有救援用途，拆走重要零件可能讓別人受傷。她量了兩邊的尺寸，發現仍有一點點差異。「我們用備用橡膠做替代品。」她說，「借用形狀，不等於偷走安全零件。」

Their competitor arrived before the new seal was ready. Vale offered a heated warehouse and asked the farmers to sign over their fields. His plan looked faster and less risky. Bao admitted that many neighbors would support it. Lin did not oppose the offer simply because it came from Vale. She asked what the contract would cost and how long the protection would last. The answer was hidden in a page of small writing: the company would own the fields even if the game ended. "That is an intense price for one night of warmth," Bao said.

替代密封圈尚未完成，競爭者維爾就到了。他提出提供溫暖倉庫的方案，要求農民簽字交出土地。計畫看起來又快又安全。包先生承認，很多鄰居會支持。林澄沒有只因提案來自維爾就反對，而是詢問契約成本與保護期限。答案藏在小字條款裡：即使遊戲結束，公司仍擁有農地。「只換一夜溫暖，這代價太沉重了。」包先生說。

Lin and the plumber worked beside the pump while Imani checked the animals. Bao's daughter, who had earned a technical diploma, helped them analyze the pressure readings. They had to adapt the rubber seal to an older machine, and the first attempt leaked. Lin changed the angle of a metal ring and tried again. This time the water moved steadily. The system showed a twelve percent reduction in energy waste. It awarded six repair points, enough to help light the farm beacon if the residents completed one more small job.

林澄與水管工在抽水機旁工作，伊曼妮則檢查動物。包先生的女兒擁有技術文憑，協助他們分析壓力數據。他們必須調整橡膠密封圈，讓它適用於舊式機器。第一次嘗試漏水了；林澄改變金屬環的角度，再試一次。這次水流終於穩定。系統顯示能源浪費減少了百分之十二，並獎勵六點維修分數。只要居民再完成一件小修理，就有機會點亮農場的信標。

The final repair came from an unexpected place. The old mule's gate had a broken hinge, and the animal kept striking it with its head. Bao fixed the hinge himself while Lin explained how to register the work. His repair earned two points. "That is remarkable," he said, laughing with relief. "I thought only engineers could help." Lin shook her head. "The rules reward necessary work, not impressive titles." The farmers voted to share spare parts and make mutual checks of their machines. For the first time, the district's welfare depended on a plan they controlled.

最後一件修理出現在意料之外的地方。老騾子的柵門鉸鏈壞了，牠不斷用頭去撞。包先生親手修好鉸鏈，林澄則教他如何登記工作。這次修理得到兩分。「真不可思議！」他鬆了一口氣，笑著說，「我以為只有工程師能幫忙。」林澄搖頭。「規則獎勵必要的工作，不是響亮的頭銜。」農民們投票決定共享備用零件，並互相檢查機器。這個街區的生活保障第一次建立在居民自己控制的計畫上。

The restored pump also changed the evening meal. Before the repair, families had planned to melt frost for cooking and give the cleanest water to the youngest children. Now they could wash vegetables, fill animal troughs, and make tea for people who had worked outside all day. Imani reminded them not to waste the supply. A working pump was not an endless source, and the protected road might close again tomorrow. Bao wrote a schedule so that everyone would have a fair turn at the tap.

抽水機修好後，連晚餐也改變了。原本居民打算融化霜雪來煮飯，把最乾淨的水留給年紀最小的孩子。現在他們能清洗蔬菜、替動物裝滿水槽，也能替整天在外工作的人泡茶。伊曼妮提醒大家不要浪費。會運作的抽水機不代表水源無窮無盡，明天受保護的道路也可能再次關閉。包先生寫下一張輪流用水的時間表，確保每個人都有公平的機會。

After sunset, Bao handed Lin a weathered supplier receipt. His grandfather had sold water-pump parts to the Common Maintenance Office, and the family had kept the duplicate with its farm accounts. The signature on the receipt matched the name in Guo's ledger. More importantly, the delivery address was the private warehouse Vale now controlled. Lin copied the number and thanked Bao. A second district had chosen cooperation over surrender, but the evidence also pointed to a larger operation. Someone had redirected public supplies long before anyone heard the first game announcement.

日落後，包先生交給林澄一張泛黃的供應商收據。他的祖父曾把抽水機零件賣給公共維護辦公室，家裡一直把副本留在農場帳目中。收據上的簽名和郭先生帳本的名字相同。更重要的是，送貨地址正是維爾如今控制的私人倉庫。林澄抄下編號，向包先生道謝。第二個街區選擇合作，而不是交出資產；但證據同時也指向一項更大的行動。早在任何人聽見第一則遊戲公告之前，就有人把公共物資轉往別處。
