清點名字的博物館

The Museum That Counted Names

遊戲降臨後，我只想開一家修理店 · 第 4 / 25 章 · 3000–4000 · 英中對照

The railway museum stood beside a station that had not carried passengers for twenty years. Its front hall contained model trains and a painted planet whose small moon moved in a slow orbit. Children used to watch the moon bounce on its hidden spring. Now the building was filled with people seeking shelter, and a sign above the door called it the Northern Embassy. "An embassy for whom?" Niko asked. "For anyone outside their home district," answered Rina, the museum's librarian. She carried a lamp, a heavy ring of keys, and a notebook full of names.

鐵道博物館坐落在一座二十年沒載過乘客的車站旁。前廳陳列著模型火車，以及一顆塗上顏色的行星；它的小衛星沿著緩慢的軌道轉動。孩子們以前喜歡看那顆衛星在隱藏的彈簧上彈跳。如今，建築裡擠滿尋求庇護的人，門上的牌子卻稱這裡為「北方大使館」。「這是誰的大使館？」尼可問。「任何離開原居區的人。」博物館圖書管理員莉娜回答。她提著燈、沉重的鑰匙圈，以及一本寫滿姓名的筆記本。

Rina had an earnest reason for keeping the notebook. The new system counted shelter requests, but people without digital records were marked "unconfirmed." A visiting translator helped families explain their needs in several languages. Beside him, a researcher compared the city's old maps with the new game zones. The work was slow because the screens changed without warning. "We need an official approval to open the underground archive," Rina told Lin. "Three residents must confirm a public purpose, and the doors must remain visible while we enter."

莉娜認真保存這本筆記，因為她有充分的理由。新系統會統計尋求庇護的人數，但沒有數位紀錄的人會被標成「尚未確認」。一名來訪的翻譯員正協助幾個家庭用不同語言說明需求。他身旁的研究員則把城市舊地圖和新遊戲區域互相比較。畫面毫無預警就會改變，工作因而進行得很慢。「要打開地下檔案室，我們需要正式核准。」莉娜告訴林澄，「必須有三位居民確認公共用途，而且進去時大門必須保持可見。」

Before they could apply, a frightened man pointed at the researcher. The screen above his head displayed one word: MURDERER. People stepped back. The researcher said the label was nonsense; he had never harmed anyone. Lin looked at the smaller words in the screen's margin and read them aloud: MURDER OF A PUBLIC SERVICE, NOT A PERSON. The accusation concerned an old decision to close the maintenance office. "The emphasis matters," she said. "A frightening title is not the same as an explanation, and an accusation is not proof."

他們還沒來得及申請，一名受驚的男子就指著研究員。研究員頭頂上方的畫面顯示出一個字：「殺人者」。人群往後退。研究員說這個標籤根本荒謬，他從未傷害過任何人。林澄看向畫面邊緣較小的字，當眾讀出：「謀殺的是一項公共服務，並非一個人。」原來指控涉及多年前關閉維修辦公室的決定。「文字重點很重要。」她說，「嚇人的標題不等於完整解釋，指控也不等於證據。」

The researcher admitted he had helped write a report supporting the closure. He had been passive when senior officials changed its conclusions. "I believed that waiting would protect my job," he said. "Instead, my silence helped them." Rina did not offer easy amusement or comfort. She asked him to surrender his copy of the report. He did, though his hands shook with disappointment at himself. "An apology will not restore the office," Lin said, "but an honest record may help us accomplish something useful now." Together they signed the application for archive access.

研究員承認，自己曾協助撰寫支持關閉辦公室的報告。當高層官員修改報告結論時，他只是被動接受。「我以為只要等待，就能保住工作。」他說，「結果我的沉默反而幫了他們。」莉娜沒有用玩笑或簡單的安慰帶過，而是請他交出報告副本。他照做了，雙手卻因對自己的失望而發抖。「道歉不會讓辦公室恢復。」林澄說，「但誠實的紀錄，也許能幫我們現在完成一件有用的事。」眾人一起簽署了進入檔案室的申請。

The archive door opened after three names were verified. Inside, a machine printed the same sentence with continual regularity: PUBLIC REPAIR REQUIRES PUBLIC POSSESSION. Rina explained that old equipment belonged to the people who used and maintained it together. The game's ownership system was supposed to recognize this shared history. Yet many records had disappeared. Lin found an index of repair stations, including one beneath the museum and several near the harbor. Each record had a number, an owner category, and the amount of energy its repair could contribute to a district.

三個名字通過核對後，檔案室的大門打開了。裡面有台機器不斷規律地印出同一句話：「公共維修需要公共持有權。」莉娜解釋，舊設備原本屬於共同使用並維護它們的居民。遊戲的所有權制度理應承認這段共享歷史，然而許多紀錄都失蹤了。林澄找到一份維修站索引，其中包括博物館地下的站點，以及港邊幾處設施。每份紀錄都有編號、所有權類別，還有維修後能替街區增加的能量。

The index was not a simple list of machines. Every entry recorded the names of people who had changed filters, cleaned engines, or checked the doors during ordinary years. Lin recognized her father's careful handwriting beside the theater's old generator. He had died before the game appeared, but the page showed his work was still part of the city's history. The discovery changed her idea of the repair points. Perhaps the system was not rewarding powerful equipment alone. Perhaps it was also trying to count the care that people had already given one another.

索引不只是一份機器清單。每筆資料都記錄了那些年替換濾網、清理引擎、檢查門鎖的人。林澄在劇院舊發電機旁，看見父親工整的筆跡。父親在遊戲出現以前就過世了，但這一頁證明，他的工作仍是城市歷史的一部分。這個發現改變了她對維修分數的理解。也許系統獎勵的不只是強大的設備；也許它還試著計算，人們過去曾如何互相照顧。

Niko found a child's poem taped beneath the index. It used a simple rhyme to remember the safety rule: "Share the key, keep the light; check the name before the night." Lin smiled despite herself. Then an urgent alarm sounded. Someone outside had challenged the museum's public ownership claim. The screen offered two choices: surrender the records to a private claimant, or produce evidence before dusk. Rina raised her notebook. "I can prove who lived here," she said. "Can you prove who kept the machines alive?" Lin opened the maintenance index to its first page.

尼可發現索引下方貼著一首兒童詩，押韻的句子用來幫人記住安全規則：「共享鑰匙留住光，入夜之前查名章。」林澄忍不住笑了。然而，緊急警報突然響起。有人在外頭挑戰博物館的公共所有權。畫面提供兩個選擇：把紀錄交給私人申請人，或在黃昏之前拿出證據。莉娜舉起自己的筆記本。「我能證明誰住在這裡。」她說，「你能證明是誰讓機器繼續運轉嗎？」林澄翻開維修索引的第一頁。
