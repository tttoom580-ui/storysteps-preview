油漆下的名字

The Name Beneath the Paint

遊戲降臨後，我只想開一家修理店 · 第 2 / 25 章 · 3000–4000 · 英中對照

By morning, the black glass had become an ordinary road again. Everyone remembered what they had seen, so nobody called it a dream. Lin opened the repair shop before sunrise. An initial report appeared above her workbench: DISTRICT TWELVE HAS SEVENTEEN HOURS OF PROTECTION REMAINING. The previous night's success had not solved the problem; it had only bought time. Outside, Niko was checking an old bus timetable against the strange new map. Several streets had shifted, and the shortest road to the water station was now closed.

到了早上，黑色玻璃又變回普通道路。每個人都記得昨晚看見的景象，因此沒有人把它當成夢。林澄在日出前就打開了修理店。工作檯上方浮現一份初始報告：「第十二區剩餘保護時間：十七小時。」前一晚的成功並沒有真正解決問題，只是替他們爭取到一些時間。屋外，尼可正拿著舊公車時刻表對照神祕的新地圖。幾條街道已經改變位置，前往供水站最近的道路也封閉了。

A woman named Samira arrived with a broken printing machine and a small dog. Samira was an immigrant who had opened a sign shop years before the disaster. Her dog, Kite, raised one muddy paw toward Lin as if asking for help. "My shop has the old district ownership records," Samira explained. "Vale's men tried to take them last night. They called it a necessary convenience for everyone." She showed Lin a public notice. It offered a special privilege to whichever company first registered the station as private property.

一名叫薩米拉的女子帶著壞掉的印刷機和一隻小狗來到店裡。薩米拉是移民，災變前幾年便在城裡開了一家招牌店。她的小狗凱特抬起一隻沾滿泥巴的爪子，像是在求助。「我的店裡保存著舊區域的產權紀錄。」薩米拉解釋，「維爾的人昨晚想搶走它們，還說那只是為了大家方便。」她拿出一張公告，上面寫著：第一家把供水站登記為私人財產的公司，可以獲得特殊權利。

The notice carried the printed signature of a prominent rescue official, but the ink looked too fresh. Samira had met the official at a charity event and kept his autograph on a program. The two signatures did not match. "Maybe someone copied his name badly," Niko said. "Or maybe they put on a disguise and pretended to speak for him," Lin replied. She did not yet know the truth. But she knew that a suspicious document was not enough reason to surrender a public water station.

公告上印著一位知名救援官員的簽名，但墨水看起來新得可疑。薩米拉曾在慈善活動上見過那名官員，還保存著他親筆簽名的節目單。兩個簽名並不相同。「也許有人把他的名字模仿得很糟。」尼可說。「也可能有人偽裝成他的代理人，假裝替他發言。」林澄回答。她還不知道真相，但她確定，不能因為一張有疑點的文件，就把公共供水站拱手讓人。

Lin examined the printing machine. Someone had removed its elastic drive belt and left a metal pin in the gears. The pin was small enough to look like childish mischief, but it could ruin the motor if the machine started. Samira's eyes hardened. "This is not an accident," she said. "It is cruelty dressed as business." Lin agreed that the damage was deliberate, yet she asked Samira not to accuse anyone without proof. "We can record the marks first," she said. "Then we can repair what was damaged."

林澄檢查印刷機，發現有人取走了具有彈性的傳動皮帶，還在齒輪裡留下一根金屬針。那根針小得像孩子惡作劇留下的東西，但機器一啟動，就可能毀掉馬達。薩米拉眼神一沉。「這不是意外。」她說，「這是把殘酷行為包裝成生意。」林澄也認為破壞是蓄意的，但她請薩米拉先別在沒有證據的情況下指控任何人。「先記錄痕跡，」她說，「再修好壞掉的部分。」

The machine's original belt had been cut, so Lin offered to carve a replacement wheel from a spare piece of rubber. Samira held the wheel steady while Niko measured the distance between the gears. They were beginning to work as a team when the system warned them of an explosive battery failure at the nearby water station. The display showed a red countdown. Lin stopped the machine immediately. "Our first repair will have to wait," she said. "A working printer cannot help people who have no water."

原本的皮帶已經被割斷，林澄提議用備用橡膠削製一個替代轉輪。薩米拉扶穩轉輪，尼可則測量齒輪間的距離。三人逐漸建立起合作默契時，系統突然警告：附近供水站的電池可能發生爆炸性故障，畫面上出現紅色倒數。林澄立刻停下機器。「我們的第一項修理得延後了。」她說，「如果大家沒有水，一台修好的印刷機也幫不上忙。」

At the station, Vale's guards stood behind a locked gate. Their shift had begun at dawn, and they refused to admit ordinary residents. "Repair work is restricted to our company," one guard said. Lin pointed to the public rules: no company owned the station yet. As an act of prevention, she asked the crowd to stay away from the damaged battery. Then she offered the guards a clear choice: let her isolate the dangerous cell, or sign a written statement that they had refused a safety repair. The gate opened.

到了供水站，維爾的守衛站在上鎖的門後。他們清晨就開始輪班，卻不讓一般居民入內。「只有我們公司能在這裡維修。」一名守衛說。林澄指著公開規則：目前還沒有任何公司擁有供水站。為了預防危險，她先請群眾遠離受損電池，再給守衛兩個明確選擇：讓她隔離危險電池，或簽下一份書面聲明，承認自己拒絕安全維修。大門終於打開。

Lin removed the overheated cell and connected the safe parts of the circuit. Water began to flow, but only through the public tap. A system window requested feedback from the people who had watched the repair. Niko selected "successful" and Samira recorded the result on paper. The station granted the district eight energy points and rejected Vale's ownership application because the machine was now listed as public. "That is an interesting remark from a machine," Samira said. Lin smiled. "It seems the rules can protect us if we read them carefully."

林澄拆下過熱的電池，重新接好安全的線路。水開始流動，但只從公共水龍頭流出。系統視窗要求見證維修的居民提供回饋。尼可選了「成功」，薩米拉把結果記錄在紙上。供水站替區域增加了八點能量，並以設備已登記為公共資產為由，拒絕維爾的所有權申請。「機器給出這樣的評語，還真有趣。」薩米拉說。林澄笑了笑。「只要仔細讀規則，它們似乎也能保護我們。」

Back at the shop, Samira made a new belt and printed twenty copies of the rules. Lin placed one copy beside every repair request. Their list of customers grew, but Lin refused to promise that the district would prosper overnight. She knew that one good day could not replace a reliable water system. At the bottom of a damaged metal plate, Niko found a name scratched beneath several layers of paint: COMMON MAINTENANCE OFFICE. It was the first clue to the missing record. The three friends looked at one another. Someone had tried very hard to hide that name.

回到修理店後，薩米拉做出新的皮帶，還印了二十份規則。林澄在每張維修申請旁都放上一份。顧客名單越來越長，但林澄拒絕保證社區能一夜繁榮。她知道，一天的順利無法代替可靠的供水系統。尼可在一塊受損金屬牌的底部，發現好幾層油漆下刻著一個名字：「公共維護辦公室。」這是他們找到的第一條遺失紀錄線索。三個朋友彼此對望。顯然，有人費盡心思想把這個名字藏起來。
