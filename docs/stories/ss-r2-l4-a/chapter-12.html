橋下的船

The Boat Under the Bridge

遊戲降臨後，我只想開一家修理店 · 第 12 / 25 章 · 3000–4000 · 英中對照 · 校訂中

用互動版查詞、朗讀與收藏

The payment list led Lin to a river vessel that had been tied beneath an old bridge for years. It carried crates labeled as public hardware, yet no district had received them. A retired miner named Sun guarded the boat. He had lost his brother during an earlier accident and treated safety with almost absolute seriousness. "I won't let strangers take anything without a clear record," he said. Lin respected his discipline and showed him copies of the invoices. A hawk circled above the river while they compared shipment numbers in the cold morning air.

付款清單帶林澄來到一艘多年來繫在舊橋下的河船。船上載著標示為公共五金器材的木箱，但沒有一個街區收到貨物。退役礦工孫先生守著船。他以前在事故中失去兄弟，因此對安全幾乎抱持絕對嚴肅的態度。「沒有清楚紀錄，我不會讓陌生人拿走任何東西。」他說。林澄尊重他的紀律，拿出發票副本。寒冷的晨風中，一隻老鷹盤旋在河上，他們逐一核對貨運編號。

The crates were secured with oval locks and wire that someone had tried to fasten too tightly. Sun explained that the locking system had been designed to prevent theft during public distribution. Now it prevented the public from receiving its own supplies. Niko wanted to cut the wire, but Lin asked for the original manifest. Sun produced an illustration of the cargo arrangement and a signed order stating that the goods belonged to the Common Maintenance Office. The paper gave them a path forward. They could open the crates with two witnesses and record every item removed.

木箱用橢圓形鎖具固定，鐵絲還被人綁得太緊。孫先生解釋，這套鎖定制度本來是為了在公共物資分配時防止偷竊，如今卻讓公眾無法拿到自己的補給。尼可想剪斷鐵絲，林澄則要求查看原始貨單。孫先生拿出一張貨物排列圖，以及一份簽名命令，顯示物資屬於公共維護辦公室。這張紙提供了正當做法：他們可以在兩位見證人在場時打開箱子，逐件記錄取出的物品。

Inside one crate was a damaged pump controller. Its design was simple, but years of moisture had reduced its efficiency. Lin tried to insert a new connector, then stopped when the circuit failed a safety test. "We are not chasing perfection today," she said, "but the machine must be safe." Niko felt dread because the boat had begun to shift with the river current. Sun suggested moving the work to the bridge platform rather than rushing. Their first realistic goal was to secure the supplies; repairing every machine could wait until they reached dry ground.

其中一個木箱裡放著受損的抽水機控制器。設計雖然簡單，多年潮濕卻降低了它的效率。林澄試著插入新接頭，安全測試沒通過時便停下。「我們今天不是在追求完美，」她說，「但機器必須安全。」河水帶動船身搖晃，尼可開始感到恐懼。孫先生建議先把工作移到橋上的平台，而不是急著完成。眼下較務實的目標，是先確保物資安全；每台機器都能等到搬上乾地再修。

Sun's wife arrived with food and spoke about her years of grief after the mine accident. She said the most painful part had been the absence of honest records. Managers had promised help but could not tell the families which equipment had failed. Lin understood why Sun guarded the boat so firmly. The couple offered a gracious welcome once they knew the team wanted to protect the supplies. Rina read each crate number aloud while Samira wrote it down. The process was slow, but no one could later claim that a box had vanished without explanation.

孫先生的妻子帶著食物來了，談起礦難後多年難以消散的悲傷。她說，最痛苦的部分是缺乏誠實紀錄：管理者承諾幫忙，卻說不清到底是哪件設備壞了。林澄終於明白孫先生為什麼那麼堅決地看守這艘船。知道大家想保護物資後，夫妻倆熱情地招待了他們。莉娜逐一朗讀木箱編號，薩米拉負責寫下。程序雖慢，卻能避免以後有人說某個箱子無故消失。

They recovered twenty-four public controllers, twelve bundles of cable, and a folder of delivery notes. The game granted recognition to the witnesses and allowed the group to renew the area's temporary protection using three repaired machines. Lin checked that the new energy had not been credited to a private account. It had not. "This is how resolve becomes useful," Sun said. "Not by making the loudest promise, but by doing the next careful thing." The sentence reminded Lin of her father's work records, where patience had seemed more important than victory.

他們找回二十四個公共控制器、十二捆電纜，以及一個裝著送貨紀錄的資料夾。遊戲承認見證人的身分，並允許他們用三台修好的機器更新當地的臨時保護。林澄檢查新能量是否被記到私人帳戶，結果沒有。「這才是把決心變成有用的東西。」孫先生說，「不是做出最大聲的承諾，而是謹慎完成眼前下一件事。」這句話讓林澄想起父親的工作紀錄：在那些紀錄裡，耐心似乎永遠比勝利更重要。

Before leaving the bridge, Lin wrote a rule on a sheet of cardboard: "A delivery ends when the receiver confirms it." She posted it beside the recovered crates, where workers from every district could read it. The rule was not yet official, but it would guide their volunteers. Sun asked whether ordinary people would really remember to sign for every item. "Some will forget," Lin admitted. "Then we will remind them. A system can be improved by people who notice its weak points instead of pretending it cannot fail."

離開橋之前，林澄在紙板上寫下一條規則：「收件者確認後，交貨才算完成。」她把紙板貼在找回的木箱旁，讓來自各區的工人都能看見。這條規則尚未正式生效，但能先指引志工。孫先生問，一般人真的會記得每件物品都簽收嗎？「有些人會忘記。」林澄承認，「那我們就提醒他們。系統可以靠看見弱點的人改進，而不是靠假裝系統永遠不會出錯。」

The last folder contained a delivery route ending at a place called the East Reservoir Foundation. The name did not appear in any modern city directory, but Mara remembered it as a public safety center that had been closed with the maintenance office. The recovered supplies could help many districts, yet no amount of equipment would matter if the same false delivery rules remained. Lin divided the work. Niko and Samira would bring the controllers home; she and Rina would seek the old foundation's original rules. That night, every crate had a witness, and every witness knew where the crate was going.

最後一個資料夾裡，有條送貨路線通往「東水庫基金中心」。現代城市名錄查不到這個名稱，但瑪拉記得，那原本是一處公共安全中心，與維護辦公室同時關閉。找回的物資能幫助很多街區，但如果錯誤的交貨規則仍存在，再多設備也不夠。林澄分配工作：尼可和薩米拉把控制器送回家，她和莉娜則去尋找舊中心原本的規則。那晚，每個箱子都有見證人，每位見證人也都知道箱子將送往何處。
