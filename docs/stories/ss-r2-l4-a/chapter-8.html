A Message Written by Hand

遊戲降臨後，我只想開一家修理店 · 第 8 / 25 章 · 3000–4000 · 英中對照 · 校訂中

Lin carried Bao's receipt to the old craft hospital, where an experienced technician had once checked public equipment. The building's history reached back to her ancestors, who had worked there as metalworkers. In a hall decorated with handmade tools, she met Mr. Qiu, a distinguished engineer with careful handwriting. He compared the receipt with his own records. "The numbers are identical," he said, "but the delivery destination has been changed." Lin had expected an answer. Instead, she found a new question: who could change a public order without changing its number?

林澄帶著包先生的收據來到舊工藝醫院。這裡曾有一名經驗豐富的技術員負責檢查公共設備。建築的歷史可追溯到她的祖先，他們曾在這裡擔任金工師傅。在展示手工工具的廳堂裡，她見到邱先生，一位字跡工整、受人敬重的工程師。他把收據和自己的紀錄比對。「編號完全相同，」他說，「但送貨目的地被改過了。」林澄原以為會得到答案，卻又遇上新的問題：誰能在不改編號的情況下更動公共訂單？

An alarm interrupted them. The hospital's water hose had split near the emergency room, and several patients were being moved away from the flooding. One woman was in the final weeks of pregnancy; another patient had recently suffered a stroke and could not walk without help. Imani joined the staff while Lin inspected the pipe. The damage was serious but not mysterious. Years of pressure had weakened the material. "A lousy repair would last one hour," Lin said. "We need something that can survive a night of heavy use." Mr. Qiu led her toward the workshop.

警報打斷了談話。醫院急診室附近的一條水管裂開，工作人員正把病人撤離積水區。一名女子已經到了懷孕末期，另一名病人最近中風，沒有協助便無法行走。伊曼妮去幫醫護人員，林澄則檢查水管。損壞嚴重，原因卻不神祕：多年水壓讓材料逐漸老化。「隨便修一修可能只撐一小時。」林澄說，「我們需要能承受整夜大量使用的辦法。」邱先生帶她走向工作間。

Mr. Qiu showed her how to craft a metal support and equip the pipe with a stronger joint. The task required more work credits than Lin had left that day, so two hospital workers volunteered. Niko worried that Vale would use the delay to crush the hospital's claim to independence. "That fear is understandable," Lin said, "but keeping people alive is a defensible choice." The repaired pipe held. The system recorded the three workers' separate contributions, confirming that a team could finish one repair without anyone transferring personal credits.

邱先生示範如何製作金屬支架，並為水管裝上更堅固的接頭。這件工作需要的額度超過林澄當天剩餘的數量，於是兩名醫院工作者主動參與。尼可擔心，維爾會利用拖延摧毀醫院維持獨立的機會。「這種擔心可以理解，」林澄說，「但先救人是有充分理由可以辯護的選擇。」修好的水管撐住了。系統分別記錄三位工作者的貢獻，證實團隊能共同完成修理，而不必轉移任何人的個人額度。

When the floor was dry, Mr. Qiu returned to the old orders. His first suggestion was to study the paper itself. Private warehouses used a blue stamp, while public offices used green. The changed destination bore both colors. Lin felt a sharp sense of guilt because she had almost accused the hospital staff of helping Vale. "We should not misunderstand a mistake as a crime," she said. Mr. Qiu nodded. "Quite the contrary. A false stamp can show that someone used a correct form for a dishonest purpose. That is why we preserve the originals."

地板乾燥後，邱先生重新拿出舊訂單。他的第一個建議，是先研究紙張本身。私人倉庫習慣用藍色印章，公共辦公室則用綠色。被修改的目的地上竟然出現兩種顏色。林澄感到一陣愧疚，因為她剛才差點指控醫院員工在幫維爾。「不能把錯誤誤會成犯罪。」她說。邱先生點頭。「正好相反。假的印章可以證明，有人用真正的表格做不誠實的事。這就是我們要保留原件的原因。」

In the records room, a locked cabinet displayed a small riddle: "What belongs to everyone yet disappears when no one remembers it?" Niko guessed "water," and Rina, who had joined by radio, guessed "history." Lin tried "shared responsibility." The cabinet opened, revealing a folder that listed everyone with authorized involvement in the old supply network. The file contained her father's name, Guo's, and an administrator called Mara Venn. Vale's name did not appear. Lin photographed each page, careful not to launch a public accusation before she could speak with Venn.

檔案室裡有個上鎖的櫃子，門上顯示一道謎題：「什麼東西屬於所有人，卻在無人記得時消失？」尼可猜「水」，透過無線電加入討論的莉娜猜「歷史」。林澄試著回答「共同責任」，櫃門便打開了。裡面有個資料夾，記載了所有獲准參與舊供應網路的人。名單上有父親、郭先生，以及一位叫瑪拉・文恩的行政人員，卻沒有維爾的名字。林澄拍下每一頁，小心不在與文恩對話之前就公開指控任何人。

Niko was disappointed that the cabinet had not shown the missing warehouse contract. Lin reminded him that evidence rarely came in the order people wanted. The hospital records told them who had been allowed to handle supplies; the receipts told them where those supplies had traveled. Neither piece alone proved that Vale had ordered the changes. Together they suggested which questions to ask. She made three copies of the new list and gave one each to Rina and Mr. Qiu. If a single screen failed, the paper would still remain.

尼可因櫃子裡沒有失蹤的倉庫合約而失望。林澄提醒他，證據很少按照人們期待的順序出現。醫院紀錄告訴他們誰有權處理物資，收據則顯示物資送往何處。任何一項單獨來看，都無法證明維爾下令修改資料。兩者放在一起，卻能告訴他們該問哪些問題。她把新名單影印成三份，分別交給莉娜和邱先生各一份。如果有某個螢幕失效，紙本仍然存在。

At the hospital entrance, Imani reported that both vulnerable patients were safe. The woman expecting a child had water and a quiet place to rest, while the patient recovering from the stroke had been returned to a dry room. Lin looked back at the repaired hose. The supply records mattered, but they mattered because people depended on real machines. She left Mr. Qiu copies of the receipt and asked him to keep the originals locked away. The next step was to locate Mara Venn, whose name linked the missing public supplies to the closed maintenance office.

在醫院門口，伊曼妮告訴大家，兩名脆弱的病人都安全了。即將生產的女子有乾淨的水和安靜的休息處，中風後復原中的病人也回到乾燥的病房。林澄回頭望向修好的水管。供應紀錄很重要，但重要的原因是人們真的依賴那些機器生活。她留下收據副本給邱先生，請他把原件鎖好保存。下一步就是尋找瑪拉・文恩；她的名字把失蹤的公共物資和已關閉的維護辦公室連在了一起。
