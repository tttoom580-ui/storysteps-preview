空著的辦公室

The Empty Office

遊戲降臨後，我只想開一家修理店 · 第 9 / 25 章 · 3000–4000 · 英中對照 · 校訂中

The address for Mara Venn led to an empty office above the harbor market. A sign advertised a vacancy, but the windows were covered from inside. Lin asked a nearby shopkeeper whether Mara still lived there. "She used to keep the harbor association records," he said. "Now she helps at the community kitchen." He pointed toward a building with a broken fireplace and an outdoor stove. The smell of pasta came from a large pot, and a cook was saving a single lobster for a patient who needed a special meal.

瑪拉・文恩的地址指向港口市場樓上一間空辦公室。門上貼著「空屋出租」，窗戶卻從裡面被遮住。林澄向附近店主打聽瑪拉是否仍住在那裡。「她以前負責保管港口協會的紀錄。」店主說，「現在到社區廚房幫忙。」他指向一棟壁爐壞掉、改用室外爐子的建築。大鍋裡傳來義大利麵的香味，廚師還特地留下一隻龍蝦，準備給需要特殊餐點的病人。

Mara was a bald woman with a gentle voice and a careful way of answering questions. She was writing an autobiography because she feared the city's public history might disappear. Lin showed her the receipts and asked why the old office had closed. Mara did not immediately reply. "Many problems arise when one small group controls every report," she finally said. "The former harbor minister signed an order moving equipment to a private warehouse. I was told the change was temporary." Her face tightened. "Then the public records vanished, and the office was shut."

瑪拉是一名光頭女子，聲音溫和，回答問題十分謹慎。她正在寫自傳，因為擔心城市的公共歷史會消失。林澄拿出收據，詢問舊辦公室為何關閉。瑪拉沒有立刻回答。「當一小群人掌控所有報告，很多問題就會出現。」她終於說，「當時的港務部長簽署命令，把設備移往私人倉庫。我被告知那只是暫時措施。」她表情一緊。「後來公共紀錄消失，辦公室也關閉了。」

Niko's curiosity made him ask whether Mara had kept a copy of the order. She showed them a notebook with a short quotation from the minister's public speech: "No citizen should be dependent on a single supplier for essential water and power." The statement seemed to violate the later transfer order. Mara explained that she had objected in writing but received no answer. "If you want me to declare someone guilty," she warned, "I will refuse. I know what the paper says. I do not know who changed the destination after it left my desk."

尼可好奇地問瑪拉是否保存了命令副本。瑪拉拿出一本筆記，上面摘錄著港務部長公開演說的一句話：「沒有任何市民應該在基本水電上依賴單一供應商。」這項聲明似乎與後來的轉移命令互相矛盾。瑪拉解釋，她當時曾提出書面異議，卻沒有收到回覆。「如果你們想要我宣布某人有罪，」她提醒，「我會拒絕。我知道紙上寫了什麼，卻不知道文件離開我桌面後，是誰改了目的地。」

Before they could talk further, a severe weather forecast appeared on every window. A game storm would reach the harbor at dusk. The harbor pumps began to shake, and workers called for an immediate halt to boat traffic. Lin followed them into a service tunnel, where an old rusty valve had become stuck. The machinery still worked, but pressure was rising behind the valve. If it broke, the kitchen and the market would flood. Mara rolled up her sleeves. "This is why I kept the repair records," she said. "Let's restore the valve before we lose the whole waterfront."

眾人還沒談完，所有窗戶便出現嚴重天氣預報：遊戲風暴將於黃昏抵達港口。港口抽水機開始震動，工作人員要求船隻立刻停止通行。林澄跟著進入維修隧道，發現一個生鏽的舊閥門卡死了。機器仍在運轉，但閥門後方的水壓越來越高。一旦破裂，廚房和市場都會淹水。瑪拉捲起袖子。「這就是我保存維修紀錄的理由。」她說，「先恢復閥門的功能，別讓整個岸邊都泡水。」

Lin asked workers to release pressure in stages while she cleaned the valve's threads. Mara read the old instructions aloud. Each adjustment was small, and the system awarded no points until the valve turned freely. Then the pump registered nine points for the district. A new warning followed: HARBOR PROTECTION NEEDS THREE CONNECTED BEACONS BEFORE THE STORM. The consequent problem was clear. Saving one market was no longer enough; their existing protected roads had to become a working network. Lin thought of the theater, museum, clinic, and farms. Every place had already repaired something useful. Now they needed to share a plan.

林澄請工人分階段釋放水壓，自己則清理閥門螺紋。瑪拉在旁朗讀舊說明。每個調整都很小，直到閥門終於轉動順暢，系統才一次給出九點維修分數。接著顯示新的警告：「風暴來臨前，港區保護需要三個相連的信標。」隨之而來的問題很清楚：只救一座市場已不夠，現有的受保護道路必須變成能共同運作的網路。林澄想起劇院、博物館、診所和農場。每個地方都已經修復過有用的設備，現在需要的是共享計畫。

Back at the kitchen, the cook divided the remaining meal among volunteers from several districts. People had traveled through dangerous streets because a harbor flood would affect their homes as well as the market. Lin noticed that nobody asked which block the workers came from. The practical need to protect water had already brought strangers together. She wrote this in her notebook beside the relay-station numbers: repairs were local tasks, but safety was shared. A single bright square could not protect a city when rain fell everywhere.

回到廚房後，廚師把剩下的食物分給來自不同街區的志工。大家冒險走過危險道路，因為港口淹水不只影響市場，也會影響他們自己的家。林澄注意到，沒有人追問工人來自哪一區。保護水源的實際需求已經讓陌生人聚在一起。她在筆記本的轉接站編號旁寫下：維修雖然發生在各個地方，安全卻必須共享。當大雨落在整座城市，單一明亮的廣場根本無法保護所有人。

That night, Mara handed Lin the first pages of her autobiography, not because she wanted praise, but because she wanted a witness who would question her story. She also gave Rina the harbor's old signal schedule. The pages named three public relay stations that had once shared power during storms. Two were still standing, though nobody knew whether they worked. Lin drew them on the museum map. Vale controlled the third. The approaching storm gave her group less than a day to bring enough of the old network back to life.

當晚，瑪拉把自傳前幾頁交給林澄。她不是想得到讚美，而是希望有一位願意質疑她說法的見證人。她也把港口舊訊號時刻表交給莉娜。資料指出三座在風暴期間曾共享電力的公共轉接站，其中兩座還在，卻沒人知道是否能運作。林澄把它們畫在博物館地圖上。第三座由維爾控制。逼近的風暴，讓他們只剩不到一天能恢復足夠的舊網路。
