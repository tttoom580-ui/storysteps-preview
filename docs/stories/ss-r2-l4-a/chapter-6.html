列車長的帳本

The Conductor's Ledger

遊戲降臨後，我只想開一家修理店 · 第 6 / 25 章 · 3000–4000 · 英中對照 · 校訂中

On the morning after the vote, Rina brought Lin a worn leather book from the museum. It had belonged to a railway conductor named Guo, who recorded repairs along every line. The first page was dated on the anniversary of the Common Maintenance Office's closure. Lin found her father's name beside a list of missing supplies. "I cannot assure you that he knew what was happening," Rina said, "but Guo may have been the last person to speak with him." Lin turned the page carefully. Half the entries had been covered by thick black ink.

投票後的隔天早上，莉娜從博物館帶來一本破舊的皮革帳本。那是列車長郭先生留下的，裡面記錄了各條路線的維修情況。第一頁的日期，正好是公共維護辦公室關閉的周年紀念日。林澄在一份失蹤物資清單旁看見父親的名字。「我不能向你保證他當時知道發生了什麼事，」莉娜說，「但郭先生也許是最後一個和他談過話的人。」林澄小心翻頁，發現一半資料都被厚厚的黑墨蓋住了。

The old station's speaker crackled at a strange frequency. A recorded voice asked residents to join a repair campaign and restore the public announcement system. Niko found a broken cord in the control room and a cabinet filled with coarse dust. The job looked simple until a line of yellow foam leaked from a pipe. The foam carried a sharp mineral smell and covered the control switches. Lin did not touch it. She called Imani, who checked the label and confirmed that the material was safe only after it had dried.

舊車站的廣播器在奇怪的頻率上發出沙沙聲。錄音聲音要求居民參加維修活動，恢復公共廣播系統。尼可在控制室找到一條斷掉的電線，還有一個積滿粗糙灰塵的櫃子。工作看來很簡單，直到一條管子漏出黃色泡沫。泡沫散發出刺鼻的礦物味道，覆蓋住控制開關。林澄沒有碰它，而是請伊曼妮來檢查。伊曼妮讀過標籤，確認這種材料必須乾燥後才算安全。

While they waited, Niko tried to devise a plan for reading the damaged ledger. He suggested making an imitation of the old pages and placing the copy under strong light. Lin explained that copying the letters would not reveal what black ink had hidden. "Our problem is not the paper," she said. "It is the order of events." Rina, whose diligent work had saved many records, sorted the entries by date. Each missing part had been delivered to the same private warehouse, one that later became Vale's company headquarters. The pattern was too regular to ignore.

等待泡沫乾燥時，尼可試著想出讀懂受損帳本的辦法。他建議仿製舊頁面，把副本放在強光下。林澄解釋，抄寫文字不可能顯示被黑墨遮住的內容。「問題不在紙張，」她說，「而在事件發生的順序。」莉娜一向勤勉，正因如此才保存了不少紀錄。她依日期整理帳目，發現每件失蹤零件都被送往同一座私人倉庫，而那裡後來成了維爾公司的總部。這種規律實在令人難以忽視。

Doctor Han joined them after finishing his clinic work. He had studied psychology, and he warned that fear could make witnesses remember details incorrectly. "We should not build a case from anger," he said. "The people of this city have watched one powerful dynasty of business families replace another. That history may shape our judgment, even when the records are real." His warning did not weaken Lin's purpose. Instead, it forced her to separate facts from guesses. She wrote two columns: what the ledger proved and what it only suggested.

韓醫師結束診所工作後也來幫忙。他研究過心理學，提醒大家恐懼可能讓證人的記憶出現偏差。「我們不能只憑怒氣建立指控。」他說，「這座城市的人看著一個又一個有權勢的商業家族接替掌權。這段歷史可能影響我們的判斷，即使紀錄是真的。」這番提醒沒有削弱林澄的決心，反而讓她更認真區分事實與猜測。她畫出兩欄，一欄寫帳本能證明的事情，另一欄寫它只能暗示的事情。

The dried foam came away with a brush. Lin replaced the broken cord, and the station speaker returned to life. A system evaluation credited the repair with five points, but it also displayed a warning: INCOMPLETE PUBLIC LABOR RECORD. The missing warehouse receipts had to be found before the station could contribute more energy. That made the work harder and could complicate their protection plan. Still, the loudspeaker now reached the needy families sleeping beyond the museum. Rina used it to announce clean-water locations and safe paths without asking anyone to pay.

泡沫乾了之後，林澄用刷子將它清除，再換上斷掉的電線，車站廣播終於重新響起。系統評估這次修理值五分，但同時顯示警告：「公共勞動紀錄不完整。」除非找回失蹤的倉庫收據，否則車站無法繼續提供更多能量。這使工作變得困難，也讓保護街區的計畫更複雜。不過，廣播已經能傳到博物館外需要幫助的家庭。莉娜開始公布乾淨水源的位置和安全路線，而且不向任何人收費。

Guo's final entry appeared beneath a strip of loose paper. He described a courageous mechanic who had refused to sign a false delivery form. Lin recognized her father from the description of his burned left hand. The page did not call him a hero. It recorded his hours of labor, the tools he returned, and the date he tried to report the missing parts. Lin stared at the ordinary details until they seemed to reflect a different truth about him. He had not been chasing a secret. He had simply been doing his job with care.

一條鬆開的紙片下露出郭先生最後一筆紀錄。上面寫著一名勇敢的技師拒絕在假的送貨單上簽名。林澄從「左手曾經燒傷」的描述認出了父親。那頁沒有稱他為英雄，只記錄了他的工時、歸還的工具，以及他試著通報失蹤零件的日期。林澄看著這些平凡細節，直到它們彷彿映照出父親不同的一面。父親並不是在追逐祕密；他只是在謹慎地做好自己的工作。

Before dusk, Lin called the group together. She did not want to break into the private warehouse, even though Niko was eager to try. Instead, they would search for the duplicate receipts that public suppliers were required to keep. "Our advantage is patience," she said. "We can prove a delivery without stealing a file." The others agreed, and they divided the city's old supplier list. A repair that had begun with a broken loudspeaker had opened a path toward the truth. Beyond the station, however, the protected road still ended sharply at the northern farms.

黃昏之前，林澄召集夥伴。雖然尼可很想試試看，她仍不願闖入私人倉庫。大家將改為尋找公共供應商依規定保留的送貨收據副本。「我們的優勢是耐心。」她說，「不必偷走文件，也能證明貨物曾被送出。」其他人同意，於是分頭調查城市的舊供應商名單。原本從一台壞掉的廣播器開始的維修工作，竟為他們打開了通往真相的道路。然而在車站外，受到保護的道路依舊突然中斷在北方農場前。
