風暴中的三盞燈

Three Lights in the Storm

The harbor's morning atmosphere was tense. Rain gathered over the water while volunteers prepared to repair three relay beacons. Lin wanted an ambitious result: not merely to save the market, but to protect the road to the clinic as well. She and Rina used the old signal schedule to classify each station by condition. The museum relay worked, the market relay needed a new switch, and the third stood behind Vale's fence. Their plans differed in cost and risk, but there was no time to build a completely new station before the storm.

港口早晨的氣氛十分緊張。雨雲在海面上聚集，志工正準備修復三座轉接信標。林澄希望達成一個更有企圖心的目標：不只保護市場，還要讓通往診所的道路安全。她和莉娜依據舊訊號時刻表，把每座站點按狀況分類。博物館的轉接站能運作，市場站需要新開關，第三座則位於維爾的圍欄後。各種方案的成本和風險都不同，但風暴前已經沒有足夠時間建造一座全新的站點。

At the public shelter, a refugee family offered blankets and dry stockings to the workers. A woman who once sold perfume had turned her store into a free supply room. "I don't want a promotion for doing this," she told Lin. "I want everyone to make it through the night." A local newspaper publisher printed repair schedules on paper because the digital screens could disappear without warning. The volunteers checked every notice with a witness before posting it. Lin found their care more valuable than the official badges that Vale's guards wore.

在公共避難所，一戶逃離危險地區的家庭送給工作者毯子和乾襪子。一名以前賣香水的女子，把店鋪改成免費物資室。「我不要因為這件事獲得升遷或宣傳。」她對林澄說，「我只希望所有人能撐過今晚。」一位地方報紙出版人把維修時刻表印成紙本，因為數位螢幕可能毫無預警地消失。志工張貼每份公告前，都請見證人再次確認。林澄覺得，這種細心比維爾守衛身上的官方徽章更有價值。

A shelter manager offered her resignation after discovering that she had miscounted the medical supplies. Lin refused to make an example of her. "An honest mistake deserves correction," she said, "not public embarrassment." The manager agreed to check the list with another volunteer and report the genuine shortage. That small act protected patients from false promises. The manager's quiet dignity reminded Lin why they were fighting for public equipment in the first place. The machines mattered only because people needed them, and people should not be treated as numbers on a screen.

一名避難所管理員發現自己算錯醫療物資後，提出辭職。林澄沒有拿她當作警告別人的例子。「誠實的失誤應該被改正，」她說，「不是公開羞辱。」管理員同意和另一名志工重新清點，報告真正短缺的數量。這個小小舉動讓病人不會收到虛假的承諾。管理員安靜而有尊嚴的態度，再次提醒林澄，他們究竟為何要爭取公共設備。機器的重要性，在於人需要它們；人不能被當成螢幕上的數字。

When Lin reached Vale's fence, she presented an authentic maintenance record proving that the third relay had once been public. Vale called her argument offensive, but he did not deny the document's date. He offered one option: his company would operate the station if the district accepted his permanent authority. Lin countered with a written agreement for one night's emergency operation and a public record of every work credit spent. "You may repair it as a resident," she told him, "but you cannot own it because you repaired it." Vale considered the approaching clouds.

林澄到達維爾的圍欄時，提出一份真實的維修紀錄，證明第三座轉接站以前屬於公眾。維爾說她的論點令人冒犯，卻沒有否認文件日期。他提出一個選項：只要街區承認他的永久管理權，公司就操作那座站。林澄反提出一份只限當晚緊急使用的書面協議，並要求公開記錄每筆工作額度。「你可以用居民的身分維修，」她告訴他，「但不能因為修好了就把它變成你的。」維爾望向越來越近的烏雲，開始考慮。

At last he signed. His guards opened the gate, and Vale himself replaced a damaged switch. The system recorded his four credits without giving him ownership. Lin watched as the market relay received power, and Rina called from the museum to confirm the second signal. With all three stations active, the game asked for the residents' acceptance of a shared route. Volunteers in each district confirmed their positions. The three separate lights joined into one long line. Lin saw the harbor's dark windows begin to glow, then heard the first thunder strike the water beyond the wall.

維爾終於簽字。守衛打開大門，維爾親手換掉損壞的開關。系統記錄他投入的四點額度，卻沒有將所有權交給他。林澄看著市場站收到電力，莉娜也從博物館回報第二個訊號正常。三座站點都啟動後，遊戲要求居民確認接受共享路線。各區志工分別確認所在位置。三盞分散的燈連成一條長長的光線。林澄看見港口黑暗的窗戶開始發亮，接著聽見第一聲雷擊中防護牆外的海水。

Wind battered the market throughout the night. The repaired pumps kept running, and people used the protected road to rescue two families from a flooded building. The district did not become a perfect facility overnight. Roofs still leaked, food remained limited, and several homes were damaged. But nobody had to cross the black-glass streets to reach a clinic. By dawn, the system displayed a new message: EMERGENCY NETWORK VERIFIED. Lin realized that the storm had transformed scattered repairs into something the city could rely on. The first network had survived its test.

狂風整夜拍打市場。修復的抽水機持續運作，人們沿著受保護的道路救出兩戶受困於積水建築的家庭。街區並沒有一夜之間變成完美設施。屋頂仍會漏雨，食物依然有限，還有幾棟房子受損。但沒有人再需要穿越黑色玻璃街道才能到達診所。天亮時，系統顯示：「緊急網路已驗證。」林澄終於明白，這場風暴把零散的維修工作轉變成城市能依靠的網路。第一條網路通過了考驗。

Rina's final message came just as Lin returned to the shop. The network had unlocked a list of public maintenance accounts that were supposed to exist beneath the old city hall. One account had received payments for parts that never reached the districts. Its owner was not Vale, but a company registered under another name. Lin put down her wet coat and opened a fresh page in her notebook. The storm was over, but the question of who had taken the city's supplies had only become more serious.

林澄回到修理店時，莉娜傳來最後一則訊息。新網路解鎖了一份公共維修帳戶清單，那些帳戶原本應保存在舊市政廳地下。其中一個帳戶曾收到零件款項，但零件從未到達街區。帳戶所有者不是維爾，而是一家以其他名稱登記的公司。林澄放下濕外套，在筆記本翻開新的一頁。風暴已經結束，但究竟是誰拿走了城市的物資，這個問題反而變得更加嚴重。
