沒有面孔的公司

The Company Without a Face

遊戲降臨後，我只想開一家修理店 · 第 11 / 25 章 · 3000–4000 · 英中對照 · 校訂中

The account belonged to Aster Infrastructure, a company with an office high inside a glass skyscraper. Its documents described the manufacture of emergency power cells and the transport of hydrogen containers. Lin and Mara went there with Rina, carrying copies of the public accounts. Aster's director welcomed them with a polished smile and began to boast about how much his company had done for the city. He claimed that private commerce was the only force strong enough to support long-term recovery. Lin listened, then placed the missing-parts invoices on his desk.

帳戶屬於「星辰基建」，一家在玻璃摩天大樓裡設有辦公室的公司。文件顯示，它生產緊急電力電池，並運輸氫氣容器。林澄和瑪拉、莉娜一起帶著公共帳戶副本前往。星辰公司的主管露出精緻的笑容迎接，開始吹噓公司對城市做過多少事。他宣稱私人商業是唯一足以支持長期復原的力量。林澄耐心聽完，再把失蹤零件的發票放到他桌上。

The director offered assurance that every delivery had been legal. He also called the museum records unreliable and suggested that Mara's memory was damaged by fear. Lin refused to accuse him without evidence, but she asked why several public purchases appeared as private stock. His answers were not consistent. First he said the goods were temporary loans. Then he said the government had transferred them permanently. Mara asked to see the signed transfer orders. "We cannot expose a mistake if you will not show us where the decision was made," she said. The director finally agreed to a review.

主管保證每筆運送都合法，又稱博物館紀錄不可靠，暗示瑪拉的記憶受到恐懼影響。林澄拒絕在缺乏證據時指控他，但詢問為什麼幾筆公共採購會出現在私人庫存中。主管的回答前後不一致：先說貨品只是暫借，後來又說政府已永久移交。瑪拉要求查看簽署的轉移命令。「如果你不讓我們知道決定在哪裡做出，我們就無法揭露錯誤。」她說。主管終於同意接受查核。

The review took place during an emergency industry convention in the building's lower hall. Engineers, traders, and repair crews had gathered to discuss the damage caused by the recent storm, which the system classified as a small hurricane. Lin discovered that Aster's forms used the word "delivered" for goods that had merely reached a warehouse. The explanation seemed informative, but it ignored whether the public districts had ever received their equipment. "That is not only a matter of ignorance," Rina said. "Someone designed the form so that an incomplete journey could look like a finished delivery."

查核會在大樓低層的緊急產業會議期間舉行。工程師、商人和維修人員聚集討論最近風暴造成的損害，系統把那場風暴歸類為小型颶風。林澄發現，星辰公司的表格把「抵達倉庫」也算成「已交貨」，完全沒有確認公共街區是否收到設備。說明看似提供了資訊，卻忽略了最後一段運送。「這不只是無知的問題。」莉娜說，「有人把表格設計成這樣，讓尚未完成的運送看起來像已經交貨。」

A young accountant asked to contribute his records to the inquiry. He had remained loyal to the company's ordinary workers, many of whom had repaired equipment in dangerous streets. Their work was real, he explained, even if the accounting system was not. He showed Lin a row of troublesome payment codes that appeared only on goods intended for public stations. One code matched the erased row beside her father's name. The accountant feared resistance from senior managers, so Mara offered to record his statement in front of independent witnesses rather than leave him alone with the director.

一名年輕會計師主動提出提供紀錄協助調查。他仍忠於公司裡的普通工作者；許多人確實在危險街道上修過設備。他解釋，工人的勞動是真的，即使帳務系統並不可靠。他向林澄展示一排棘手的付款代碼，這些代碼只出現在原本要送往公共站點的貨物上。其中一個與父親名字旁被擦除的紀錄吻合。會計師擔心高層阻撓，因此瑪拉提議由獨立見證人在場記錄證詞，避免讓他單獨面對主管。

The building's air system failed during the meeting. A warning said the cooling unit needed repair, but its power supply held a damaged hydrogen cell. Lin insisted on turning the unit off before anyone touched it. Some executives complained that the delay was a misfortune for their important meeting. The workers disagreed. They opened windows, guided visitors downstairs, and fixed the safer outer circuit with ordinary parts. No one attempted to repair the dangerous cell without proper equipment. The scene gave the accountant a chance to speak plainly: technical care was valuable, but so was the freedom to stop unsafe work.

會議進行時，大樓空調系統突然故障。警告顯示冷卻設備需要維修，但電源裡有一個受損的氫氣電池。林澄堅持關閉設備後才讓人接觸。有些主管抱怨，這種延誤對重要會議實在不幸。工人們卻不同意。他們打開窗戶、帶訪客下樓，並用普通零件修復較安全的外部線路。沒有人在沒有適當設備的情況下碰危險電池。這個場面讓會計師有機會直白地說明：技術上的細心很重要，但停止不安全工作的自由同樣重要。

Mara also discovered a note attached to the earliest payment. The note said that the old office had refused to close a delivery until residents confirmed receiving the parts. Its author was Lin's father. Someone had later crossed out that requirement and replaced it with a warehouse stamp. Lin photographed the two versions together. The difference mattered because it showed the false process had not always been accepted. There had once been a clear way to prevent it, and someone had made a decision to remove that protection.

瑪拉還發現最早一筆款項附著的註記。註記指出，舊辦公室原本要求居民確認收到零件，才能結束交貨流程。作者正是林澄的父親。後來有人劃掉這項要求，改用倉庫印章代替。林澄把兩個版本放在一起拍照。這項差異很重要，因為它證明不合理的流程並非一直受到接受。過去明明有清楚的方法可以防止這件事，卻有人決定移除那層保障。

After the hall reopened, Lin called for a short period of relaxation before the inquiry continued. People drank water and compared their notes. The director looked less confident, yet Lin did not enjoy his discomfort. She wanted a corrected record and a usable supply network, not a public humiliation. The review found that Aster had received payments for thirty-two shipments marked complete at the warehouse. Whether those shipments had been stolen, delayed, or redirected still required proof. Lin left with a copy of the payment list and a new question: who had approved the false definition of delivery?

大廳恢復開放後，林澄要求短暫休息，再繼續查核。大家喝水、核對筆記。主管看來沒有先前自信，林澄卻不以他的難堪為樂。她想要的是更正紀錄與可用的供應網路，而不是公開羞辱。查核發現，星辰公司有三十二筆貨運在抵達倉庫時就被標為完成，並已收到款項。那些貨物究竟遭竊、延誤還是被轉移，仍需要進一步證明。林澄帶著付款清單離開，心裡多了新問題：到底是誰核准了這種假的交貨定義？
