The Sponsor Contract

失蹤主播留下二十四小時影片 · 第 6 / 25 章 · 4000–5000 · 英中對照 · 校訂中

用互動版查詞、朗讀與收藏

The stream's sponsor threatens to sue anyone who reveals production details. The studio smelled of dust and overheated plastic, not the bright life shown on screen. Lin Huo had come to the Riverside Streaming District looking for one reliable fact, not a miracle. By midday even the smallest object on the table looked capable of changing someone’s life. Bea Wu asked whether they should tell the others immediately. Instead, Lin Huo wrote down what could be proved, what remained uncertain, and who might be hurt by a careless answer. An attractive theory was still only a theory, however badly everyone wanted it to be true.

直播贊助商威脅提告公開製作細節的人。 攝影棚聞起來是灰塵與過熱塑膠味，和螢幕上明亮人生完全不同。 林火來到河岸直播區，為的是尋找一項可靠事實，而不是奇蹟。到了中午，桌上最小的物品似乎都能改變某人的人生。吳貝雅問是否該立刻告訴其他人。林火卻先寫下哪些能證明、哪些仍不確定，以及草率回答可能傷害誰。再吸引人的理論仍然只是理論，即使所有人都迫切希望它成真。

The contract grants unlimited access to viewer locations. The first reading made the result seem clear. The second raised questions that neither Lin Huo nor Bea Wu could answer alone. They drew a line through every conclusion that depended on an unnamed witness, an unverified device, or a missing date. It was painful to lose so much apparent progress, but an empty space in a record was not permission to invent what belonged there. They would rather move slowly than build their next decision on a false certainty.

合約竟准許無限制存取觀眾位置。 第一遍讀時結果似乎很明確。第二遍卻提出林火與吳貝雅都無法獨自回答的問題。他們把依賴匿名證人、未驗證儀器或缺失日期的結論逐一刪去。失去這麼多看似已有的進展令人難受，但紀錄有空白，不代表能隨意編造。他們寧可走慢一點，也不願讓下一個決策建立在虛假的確定性上。

Lin Huo left the table to speak with moderator Niko. Public stories rarely mentioned the people who waited, cleaned, repaired, or carried messages while celebrated experts argued. Those people knew which door was normally locked and which official arrived only after an emergency. Bea Wu wrote down their observations without turning them into accusations. By evening the investigation had gained no spectacular revelation, but it had acquired something harder to buy: several people willing to correct the investigators when they were mistaken.

林火離開桌旁去和聊天室管理員尼可談話。公開故事很少提到名家辯論時，那些負責等待、清潔、修理或送訊息的人。這些人知道哪扇門平時上鎖，也知道哪位官員總在事故後才到場。吳貝雅記下觀察，不把它們直接變成控訴。到了晚上，調查沒有驚人的揭露，卻得到更難買到的東西：幾個願意在調查者犯錯時糾正他們的人。

The message from Producer Kade arrived before the afternoon meeting. It sounded polite and left no room for refusal. Lin Huo felt the old temptation to answer an insult with a louder insult, but Bea Wu asked to see the exact wording. They copied it into the record, checked whether it carried legal authority, and responded only to the part that required an answer. Behind the polished language lay a simple threat: stop asking questions or lose the little access they still possessed.

來自凱德製作人的訊息在下午會議前送達。語氣禮貌，實際上卻不容拒絕。林火一度想用更激烈的言語反擊侮辱，吳貝雅卻請她先看清楚原文。他們把訊息複製進紀錄，檢查是否有合法權限，只回答必須回應的部分。光鮮詞句背後是簡單的威脅：別再問，否則連目前僅存的查閱權也會失去。

The people outside had started measuring time in a different way. They counted missed meals, quiet nights, hospital visits, or the hours spent waiting for a message. Lin Huo carried those numbers into the meeting so that nobody could call the problem merely technical. Bea Wu described what could be repaired immediately and what required another witness. No one applauded. Yet several faces relaxed when they heard a plan that did not demand blind faith or a heroic sacrifice.

外頭的人開始用另一種方式計算時間。他們數漏掉的飯、安靜的夜、就診次數，以及等待訊息的時數。林火把那些數字帶進會議，讓沒人能把問題說成純技術。吳貝雅說明哪些能立刻修復、哪些還需要另一名見證者。沒有人鼓掌，但聽見不要求盲目信任或英雄犧牲的方案時，幾張臉終於放鬆。

On a second sheet, Lin Huo found an old cross-reference index. Its headings were not proof of any theory; they were search terms left by previous workers. Entry 1 listed “agenda”, “beware”, “cling”, “federal”, “genre”. Entry 2 listed “inning”, “metaphor”, “municipal”, “nominee”, “organism”. Entry 3 listed “overtake”, “rebellion”, “rib”, “rigid”, “shield”. Entry 4 listed “skeleton”, “sob”, “theology”, “toxic”, “vinegar”. Bea Wu insisted that a label could not replace a witness, an observed result, or a decision that people had actually approved. The index remained in the archive as a way to retrace the team's work, not as a shortcut to a verdict.

另一張紙上，林火找到舊交叉索引。標題並不能證明任何理論，只是前人留下的搜尋詞。第1欄列著“agenda”, “beware”, “cling”, “federal”, “genre”。第2欄列著“inning”, “metaphor”, “municipal”, “nominee”, “organism”。第3欄列著“overtake”, “rebellion”, “rib”, “rigid”, “shield”。第4欄列著“skeleton”, “sob”, “theology”, “toxic”, “vinegar”。吳貝雅堅持，標籤不能取代證人、實際觀察的結果，或當事人真正同意的決定。這份索引留在檔案中，方便後人回查工作，而非作為跳到結論的捷徑。

Each hour of the apparent live show contained stored answers to predictable fan reactions. Niko recovered a subtitle map with branch numbers, and Bea compared it with independent viewer captures. Where the branches diverged, the studio clock jumped; where they met, the host smiled at exactly the same frame. The illusion was impressively made. That only increased Lin’s concern for the man whose name and face were being used.

看似直播的每個小時，都藏著對常見粉絲反應的預存答案。尼可找回帶有分支編號的字幕圖，貝雅拿它比對獨立觀眾截圖。分支分岔處，棚內時鐘會跳動；重合處，主播總在完全相同的一格微笑。幻象做得精緻，反而讓林火更擔心那名姓名與面容被利用的人。

The investigation had entered its second stretch, when repeating the first discovery would no longer be enough. Lin Huo organized the new evidence by date and asked Bea Wu to identify what could be tested elsewhere. The most useful records came from ordinary workers, not from a dramatic confession. Every small correction narrowed the possibilities. For the first time, they could explain their doubts to strangers without asking those strangers to accept a hero on faith.

調查進入第二階段，重述第一次發現已經不夠。林火依日期整理新證據，請吳貝雅指出哪些可在別處複驗。最有用的紀錄來自普通工作者，而非戲劇性的自白。每項小修正都讓可能性縮小。兩人第一次能對陌生人說清疑點，而不用要求對方盲信英雄。

The test did not answer every question. It established one limit, exposed one assumption, and forced the group to revise an earlier prediction. Lin Huo found that more reassuring than an impossible perfect result. Bea Wu called the observers back to the table and asked them to describe what they had seen in their own words. The descriptions differed slightly, as honest recollections often do. They kept those differences. A truthful record was allowed to contain uncertainty and disagreement.

測試沒有回答所有問題，卻確認一項界線、揭露一個假設，並迫使大家修正先前的預測。林火反而覺得這比不可能的完美結果更令人安心。吳貝雅把觀察者叫回桌前，請他們用自己的話說出所見。描述略有差異，正如誠實回憶常有的情況。他們保留那些差異。真實紀錄可以包含不確定和不同意見。

The final visitor departed after signing the updated log. Lin Huo listened to the sounds of the Riverside Streaming District and wondered how many similar stories had ended without anyone checking the dates. Bea Wu closed the case box but did not lock away the remaining questions. They agreed to carry the uncertainty openly into the next stage. It was not a promise of success. It was a promise that success, if it came, would not require them to pretend somebody else had never existed.

最後一名訪客簽完更新的登記冊後離開。林火聽著河岸直播區的聲音，想著有多少類似故事在沒人核對日期的情況下結束。吳貝雅合上案件箱，卻沒有把剩餘問題鎖起來。兩人同意坦白帶著不確定進入下一階段。這不是成功的保證，而是承諾若成功來臨，不需要假裝某個人從未存在。
