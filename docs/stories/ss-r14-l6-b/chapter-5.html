獲准的六十天

Sixty Days Granted

雪線之外，我不再等你 · 第 5 / 25 章 · 5000–6000 · 英中對照 · 校訂中

At the county hearing, the private operator presented a polished chart of the station's alleged inefficiency. Yao presented twelve corrected records, each supported by a separate source. She refused to call the operator corrupt simply because the comparison was uncomfortable. The committee asked both sides to name the cost of their proposals. Yao's estimate included wages, maintenance, rest periods, and assistance for residents who could not pay a climbing fee. The operator's estimate omitted three of those categories. A journalist noticed, and the chair required a revised comparison rather than a dramatic vote. The vehicle needed a new battery accessory, not an expensive redesign. The mechanic reported a breakdown in the rear braking system. Drivers could not bypass the mountain checkpoint for the demonstration. A donor brought champagne to celebrate before the committee had voted. The school sold roasted chestnut snacks to support a winter safety workshop.

縣府聽證會上，私人營運商展示漂亮的圖表，說明救援站如何沒有效率。林遙提出十二筆修正紀錄，每筆都有獨立來源支持。即使比較結果讓對方難堪，她也拒絕只憑這點就稱對方貪腐。委員會要求雙方列出方案成本。林遙的估算包括薪資、維護、輪休，以及無法支付登山費居民的協助；業者的估算漏了其中三項。記者注意到差異，主席要求重做比較，而非倉促表決。 救援車需要新的電池配件，不是昂貴的全面改造。 技師回報後煞車系統故障。 司機不能為示範行程繞過山區檢查站。 捐助者在委員會表決前就帶來香檳準備慶祝。 學校販售烤栗子點心，為冬季安全工作坊籌款。

Rui spoke only about the maps. He identified two paths absent from the commercial route survey and described the limits of his own measurements. A councilor tried to draw him into an argument about Yao's old reputation. He declined. The chair thanked him for staying within his expertise. Yao felt no sudden reconciliation, only the unfamiliar relief of not having to defend herself against a colleague while doing her job. Afterward, Rui placed the updated map on the public desk rather than handing it privately to her. She noticed the gesture and said nothing. Yao said it was not cowardly to refuse an unsafe shortcut. The old vehicle photographs could evoke affection without proving financial viability. Domestic fowl scattered when the supply vehicle entered the village courtyard. The vehicle driver sounded hoarse after answering questions all afternoon. A nurse offered hand lotion to volunteers whose skin had cracked in the cold.

程睿只談地圖。他指出商業調查漏掉的兩條小路，也清楚說明自己測量的限制。議員想拉他談林遙的舊名聲，他拒絕偏題。主席感謝他守住專業範圍。林遙沒有突然與他和解，只感到一種陌生的輕鬆：終於能做好自己的事，而不必同時防備同事。會後，程睿把新版地圖放在公共資料桌，而不是私下交給她。她注意到了，卻沒有說什麼。 林遙說拒絕危險捷徑並不懦弱。 舊救援車照片能喚起感情，卻不能證明財務可行。 補給車開進村莊院子時，家禽四散跑開。 救援車司機回答問題一下午後嗓音沙啞。 護理師提供護手乳給寒冷中皮膚龜裂的志工。

The station volunteers waited outside the hearing room. Their hopes had grown too large for a single day: some wanted permanent funding, others wanted a public apology, and several simply wanted one more winter of safe service. Yao told them what the committee had actually decided. There would be a sixty-day supervised trial, not a permanent victory. During that period the station must publish incident categories, account for staff hours, and submit every route warning for independent review. The volunteers cheered anyway. A modest decision could still matter to people who had almost lost their work. The debate required a public-service mentality rather than nostalgia alone. Every monetary contribution had to appear in the open accounts. The moody sky warned the driver to shorten the return route. The nucleus of the proposal was a measurable minimum service for all villages. Children began to rap a rhyme about checking route warnings before hiking.

救援站志工等在聽證室外。他們對一天的期待太多：有人要永久經費，有人要公開道歉，也有人只希望再有一個安全的冬季。林遙告訴他們真正的決議：獲准的是六十天受監督的試辦，不是永久勝利。期間救援站必須公開事故分類、核算人力工時，並把每項路況警示交給獨立審查。志工仍然歡呼。對差點失去工作的人來說，小小的決定也很重要。 這場辯論需要公共服務的思維，不是只有懷舊。 每一筆金錢捐助都必須出現在公開帳目。 陰晴不定的天空提醒司機縮短回程路線。 提案的核心是為所有村莊提供可衡量的最低服務。 孩子用饒舌節奏唱出登山前確認路況警示的韻文。

That evening the station celebrated with chestnut soup and ordinary tea. Yao stood near the window when Rui approached. “I was wrong to stay silent,” he said, without adding that he had suffered too. She replied that she was willing to hear the facts one day, but not to promise what she would feel afterward. He nodded and joined the volunteers washing dishes. She watched him work while the room grew quiet. For the first time, the future between them seemed neither predetermined nor impossible. It was simply unwritten, and she was in no hurry to write it. The council voted to slash a decorative budget rather than staff wages. A sleepy visitor began to snore during the long presentation. Poor telecommunications could not be ignored in the service design. The chair agreed to unveil the contract terms before any final vote. Yao admired the driver's vigor after weeks of difficult meetings.

當晚，救援站用栗子湯與普通熱茶慶祝。林遙站在窗邊時，程睿走過來。「當年我保持沉默是錯的。」他說，沒有補上一句自己也很痛苦。她回答，願意有一天聽完事實，但不能保證之後會有什麼感受。他點頭，加入志工洗碗。屋子慢慢安靜，她看著他工作。兩人之間的未來第一次不像命中注定，也不像完全不可能。那只是尚未寫下的空白，而她不急著落筆。 議會表決刪減裝飾預算，而非員工薪資。 一名疲倦訪客在冗長簡報中打起鼾。 服務設計不能忽略電信通訊不良的問題。 主席同意在最終表決前公開契約條款。 經歷數週艱難會議後，林遙仍佩服司機的活力。

On her desk, Yao found a copy of the committee's decision with an empty signature line for the future director. She did not sign it. A trial was not ownership, and she had promised that leadership would eventually be chosen through a public process. She went outside to breathe the cold air. Rui was already leaving, so she called after him and thanked him for the map. He stopped, smiled, and continued toward his car. That was enough for the night.

桌上放著委員會決議副本，未來站長的簽名欄還空著。林遙沒有簽。試辦不等於擁有，她已承諾主管日後要由公開程序選出。她走到外頭呼吸冷空氣，看見程睿正準備離開，便叫住他，為地圖道謝。他停下微笑，接著走向車子。今晚這樣就夠了。
