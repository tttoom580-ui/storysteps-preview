被遺漏的名字

A Name Left Out

雪線之外，我不再等你 · 第 3 / 25 章 · 5000–6000 · 英中對照 · 校訂中

用互動版查詞、朗讀與收藏

A local cashier brought Yao three receipts for fuel sold during a winter search. The town account showed a routine delivery; the volunteer notebook described an emergency trip to find a missing shepherd. The discrepancy mattered because the county claimed that the station had handled only tourist rescues. Yao invited the cashier to explain the dates to an independent clerk, rather than taking the receipts out of the woman's hands. When the clerk compared them with the bank record, a new incident could finally be verified. The first corrected number appeared on the noticeboard before lunch. The school cashier recognized a number on an old fuel receipt from a rescue call. A nurse offered a checkup to an elderly resident who felt dizzy. The vehicle pulled beside the curb rather than blocking the classroom entrance. The factory siren could deafen anyone standing too near its horn. Yao insisted that a mistake was not a lifelong disgrace.

鎮上收銀員帶來三張冬季搜救時的燃料收據。商店帳目寫的是一般配送，志工筆記卻記錄著尋找失蹤牧羊人的緊急行程。這個差異很重要，因為縣府宣稱救援站只處理觀光客的求援。林遙請收銀員對獨立書記員說明日期，而不是把收據直接拿走。書記員與銀行資料比對後，終於確認另一件被漏掉的事故。午餐前，第一筆修正數字已貼上公告板。 學校出納認出一張舊搜救燃料收據上的編號。 護理師替感到暈眩的高齡居民做健康檢查。 救援車停在路緣旁，沒有擋住教室入口。 工廠警報器的聲音可能震聾站得太近的人。 林遙堅持，一次錯誤不該成為一生的恥辱。

The correction did not satisfy everyone. One council member demanded that Yao announce fraud immediately. Another said the station should remain silent until every entry was checked. Yao chose a third path: publish the verified correction with an explanation of what remained uncertain. Fen had taught her that careful speech was sometimes more courageous than a dramatic accusation. Rui helped trace an access road to the shepherd's hut, but declined to sign the witness account because he had not been there. His refusal surprised Yao. Years earlier, his silence had blurred a truth; today he was making its limits explicit. The old receipts were sorted for secure disposal after digital verification. A resident's dissent was recorded beside the proposed route change. One family planned to emigrate, yet wanted station access until departure. Public training could enrich a child's understanding of mountain safety without an app. The lengthy paper trail showed five separate delivery dates.

更正數字沒有讓所有人滿意。一位議員要求林遙立刻宣布有人詐欺；另一位主張所有項目查完前都不能說話。林遙選了第三條路：公開已驗證的更正，同時說明尚未確定的部分。芬姨教過她，謹慎發言有時比激烈指控更需要勇氣。程睿協助追查通往牧羊小屋的道路，卻拒絕簽署目擊證詞，因為他本人不在現場。這讓林遙意外。七年前，他的沉默模糊了真相；今天他反而主動說清楚自己不知道什麼。 舊收據完成數位核對後才進行安全銷毀分類。 一名居民對路線變更的異議被記在旁邊。 一家人打算移民，仍希望離開前能繼續申領急救手冊。 不靠應用程式，公共訓練也能豐富孩子對山區安全的理解。 冗長的紙本紀錄顯示五個不同的配送補給日期。

During an evening first-aid workshop, a teenager asked why local residents had never appeared in the official rescue maps. Yao admitted she could not answer yet. A former technician described a software change made after the new ticketing system arrived. Calls without a tourism code had been filed as general assistance rather than mountain incidents. It might be bad design, careless management, or an intentional choice. Yao photographed the settings with permission, preserving the original machine for inspection. She refused to let the student's anger turn into a rumor about a particular employee. The retired teacher described caring for neighbors as a lifelong habit. A child politely asked, 'Madam, may I keep this atlas until Friday?' A note of melancholy entered the room when the old evacuation was mentioned. It was permissible to photograph receipts only with the owners' consent. Yao did not preach about civic duty; she displayed the actual numbers.

晚上的急救課，一名少年問為什麼本地居民始終不在官方救援地圖裡。林遙坦承還無法回答。前任技師描述了新票務系統啟用後的一次軟體修改：沒有觀光代碼的來電被歸為一般協助，而非山區事故。這可能是設計不良、管理疏失，也可能有人刻意安排。她得到允許後才拍下設定，保留原機器供檢查。她不讓少年的憤怒變成對特定員工的流言。 退休教師形容照顧鄰居是終身的習慣。 孩子禮貌問道：「女士，我能把這本地圖集借到星期五嗎？ 提到舊撤離事件時，屋裡浮現一絲憂傷。 只有取得持有人同意後，拍攝收據才是被允許的。 林遙沒有說教公民義務，而是展示實際數字。

At night, Yao opened the box containing her old command notebook. A page from the failed evacuation carried the same kind of anonymous category code. She remembered signing the final summary while exhausted, telling herself that someone else had checked the numbers. That decision belonged to her. She wrote a question on fresh paper: which errors can a leader correct, and which harms must a leader acknowledge even after the facts change? She did not send the question to Rui. For the first time, she wanted to answer it without waiting for anyone's forgiveness. A faded timetable was reminiscent of the first vehicle that served the valley. Old rivalry between villages could not decide the new schedule. The corrected counts remained open to independent scrutiny. The room fell into suspense as the clerk checked the final duplicate. A manufacturer's trademark on the battery pack helped identify its production batch.

夜裡，林遙打開裝著舊指揮筆記的盒子。七年前撤離失敗的其中一頁，也寫著類似的匿名分類代碼。她記得自己當時精疲力竭，簽下最終摘要，還告訴自己別人已經查過數字。那是她自己的決定。她在新紙上寫下問題：領導者能修正哪些錯誤，又有哪些傷害，即使真相改變也必須承認？她沒有把問題傳給程睿。她第一次想不靠別人的原諒，自己找到答案。 褪色時刻表令人想起第一輛服務山谷的救援車。 村莊之間舊日的競爭不能決定新班表。 更正後的數字仍接受獨立審查。 書記員核對最後一張副本時，全場屏息等待。 電池組上的製造商商標協助辨認生產批次。

At the bottom of the corrected ledger, the clerk left an empty space for objections. A farmer wrote that his call had taken longer than the timeline suggested. Another resident remembered the time differently. Yao asked them to keep the disagreement visible until the phone record could be recovered. A truthful archive did not need everyone to remember the same minute. It needed a way for conflicting recollections to be examined without punishing the people who offered them.

更正帳冊的頁底，書記員留下申訴空格。農夫寫道，當年電話耗時比紀錄顯示的長；另一名居民卻記得不同時間。林遙請他們保留分歧，等電話紀錄找回再查。一份誠實的檔案不需要每個人都記得同一分鐘，而需要讓互相衝突的記憶能被檢驗，又不懲罰願意說出記憶的人。
