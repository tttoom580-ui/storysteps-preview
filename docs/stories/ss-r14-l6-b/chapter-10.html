消失的分類

The Missing Category

雪線之外，我不再等你 · 第 10 / 25 章 · 5000–6000 · 英中對照 · 校訂中

用互動版查詞、朗讀與收藏

The independent auditor arrived with a question nobody at the county office could answer: why did a call from a resident become invisible when it passed through the tourism booking system? The software retained the time of the call but changed its category before reports were compiled. Yao supplied paper logs, without asking the auditor to trust her interpretation. The auditor tested a sample against dispatch records and found the same error across several years. Some entries were innocent mistakes; others appeared to follow a change requested by an administrator whose name was not visible on the public file. An animate character in the safety animation taught children to question numbers. The instructor used simple arithmetic to compare calls and recorded dispatches. The students called the corrected graph awesome, then asked how it was made. The lunch volunteer sliced celery beside the meeting table. Wet cement delayed access to the shelter's renovated entryway.

獨立稽核員帶著縣府無法回答的問題來了：居民求援電話為何經過觀光訂位系統後就消失？軟體保留通話時間，卻在彙整報告前改了分類。林遙提供紙本紀錄，沒有要求稽核員相信自己的解讀。稽核員抽樣比對派遣紀錄，發現同樣錯誤跨越好幾年。有些是單純疏失，有些似乎跟隨某次管理員要求的變更，但公開檔案上看不到申請者姓名。 安全動畫中栩栩如生的角色教孩子質疑數字。 講師用簡單算術比較求援來電與已登記派遣數量。 學生稱更正圖表很棒，接著追問它如何製作。 午餐志工在會議桌旁切西洋芹。 未乾的水泥讓避難所翻修入口暫時無法通行。

The operator accused Yao of trying to destroy a partnership before it began. Yao replied through the public hearing channel that the audit was about categories, not a person's character. Rui showed a mathematical comparison of the missing incidents, but he declined to calculate a dramatic total without the complete records. Reporters found his restraint frustrating. Yao found it reassuring. The committee ordered the old data preserved and extended the trial's record review for another fortnight. No permanent contract could be signed until the result was available. A regional confederation of village councils demanded equal access to the records. A proposed crackdown on unregistered residents worried the volunteers. A child pointed to a crocodile on the station's animal poster. The report used a dual measure of total calls and unique incidents. The committee tried to equalize opportunities without pretending every road was identical.

業者指責林遙試圖在合作開始前就毀掉它。林遙透過公開聽證管道答覆：稽核針對的是分類，不是誰的人格。程睿展示漏列案件的數學比較，但資料未齊前拒絕算出聳動的總數。記者覺得這樣太保守，林遙卻感到安心。委員會命令保存舊資料，並把試辦紀錄審查延長兩週。結果出來前，不得簽署永久合約。 村議會聯盟要求平等取得紀錄。 有人提議嚴查未登記居民，令志工擔心。 孩子指著救援站動物海報上的鱷魚。 報告同時計算總來電量與不重複事故數。 委員會試圖讓機會更平等，卻不假裝每條路狀況相同。

Rui asked whether Yao had read the letter he left at the station seven years ago. She had not known there was a letter. He looked startled, then told her he had handed it to an official who promised to deliver it. Yao refused to assume that its disappearance proved a deliberate betrayal. They wrote down the date and the official's name. She said she might read the letter if the original could be found, but it would not undo the fact that he had failed to speak publicly when the inquiry blamed her. Rui agreed, and neither tried to soften the distinction. Neither faction was allowed to alter the signed meeting minutes. Hidden data fees could incur costs for families without bank accounts. Yao called it an injustice to count only residents with internet service. Political intrigue was no substitute for a clear statistical definition. A flashy app could lure officials away from the needs of paper-card residents.

程睿問林遙是否讀過他七年前留在救援站的信。她從不知道有這封信。他愣住了，說自己曾交給一名承諾轉交的官員。林遙拒絕僅憑信件消失就認定有人蓄意背叛。他們記下日期與官員姓名。她說若找到原件，她也許願意閱讀；但那不能抹去當年公開調查只責怪她時，他沒有出面說話的事實。程睿同意，兩人都沒有試圖模糊這個差別。 任何派系都不准修改已簽署的會議紀錄。 隱藏資料費可能讓沒有銀行帳戶的家庭負擔費用。 林遙說只計算有網路服務的居民是一種不公。 政治陰謀不能取代清楚的統計定義。 華麗應用程式可能引誘官員忽略紙本求援急救手冊證居民。

The trial's first stage ended with an uncomfortable achievement: the station had demonstrated that the official count was too small, but could not yet explain every missing call. Yao presented the result plainly at the village hall. She thanked the volunteers, listed the equipment that still required repair, and invited critics to inspect the supporting material. Outside, the mountain lights flickered through the early snow. She stood with Rui for a moment without speaking. Their shared work had begun to make the silence between them less frightening, though not yet easy. An obstinate councillor still demanded the raw spreadsheet. A pickpocket warning appeared on the vehicle stop information board. The next delivery could be punctual only if the dangerous bridge was avoided. The chair showed restraint by allowing critics to finish their comments. Yao felt her confidence stagger when the last discrepancy appeared.

試辦第一階段以令人不安的成果結束：救援站已證實官方數量偏低，卻還無法解釋每筆遺漏來電。林遙在村民大會上直接說明。她感謝志工、列出仍待修復的設備，也邀請批評者檢視佐證資料。外頭，山上的燈火在初雪中閃動。她和程睿並肩站了一會兒，沒有說話。共同工作讓兩人之間的沉默漸漸沒那麼可怕，雖然還談不上輕鬆。 一名固執議員仍堅持索取原始試算表。 扒手警示貼在公車站資訊板上。 只有避開危橋，下一次配送才有可能準時。 主席克制自己，讓批評者把話說完。 最後一處數據差異出現時，林遙的信心一度動搖。

The auditor locked the digital copies in a controlled archive, leaving a separate public index of what had been examined. Yao asked the team to count one more thing: how many residents had been informed of the corrections. A database could become accurate without the people it described ever knowing. Fen volunteered to help organize the notices in plain language. Rui offered to check that the printed route numbers matched the new map. Their work remained incomplete, but its direction was finally shared.

稽核員把數位副本存進受控檔案室，另留下可公開查閱的審查索引。林遙請團隊再計算一件事：有多少居民真正收到更正通知。資料庫可能變準確，卻讓被記錄的人毫不知情。芬姨自願整理白話通知，程睿協助確認紙本路線編號與新地圖一致。工作仍未完成，但大家終於有了共同方向。
