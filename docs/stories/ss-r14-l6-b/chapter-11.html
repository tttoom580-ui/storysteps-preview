The Cost of One Hour

雪線之外，我不再等你 · 第 11 / 25 章 · 5000–6000 · 英中對照 · 校訂中

Payroll records revealed that volunteers had spent hundreds of unpaid hours maintaining the station's emergency channel. The county had classified the work as a community hobby, even when a dispatcher relied on it during a medical call. Yao asked the workers what compensation they considered fair before drafting a budget. One proposed a fixed wage; another preferred scheduled training credit. She wrote down both and invited an employment adviser to explain the consequences. The private operator promised speed, but its contract did not say who would cover a shift when a rescuer became ill. The station's modest acclaim did not justify surrendering resident privacy. Yao asked the lawyer to remove ambiguity from the data clause. The privacy rule protected bodily autonomy as well as medical information. A cold draught blew through the old station trailer's cracked window. The school thermometer displayed seventy degrees Fahrenheit in the morning.

薪資資料顯示，志工曾花數百個沒有報酬的工時維持救援站的緊急頻道。縣府把這種工作列為社區興趣，即使調度員曾在醫療求助時依賴它。林遙先問工作者認為怎樣的補償合理，才開始編列預算。有人希望固定薪資，有人偏好列入訓練時數。她都寫下來，並邀請勞動顧問說明後果。私人業者承諾速度，合約卻沒交代救援員生病時由誰補班。 救援站獲得一些讚譽，卻不足以成為交出居民隱私的理由。 林遙要求律師消除資料條款中的模糊之處。 隱私規則不只保護醫療資訊，也保障身體自主權。 冷風從救援站舊拖車裂開的窗戶灌進來。 學校溫度計早晨顯示華氏七十度。

Rui helped calculate the number of hours lost to the outdated route system. The table showed that correcting a map could be less expensive than sending vehicles down the wrong valley. When a council member asked for an immediate financial verdict, Yao resisted. The audit would need to separate confirmed costs from projections. Fen thanked her after the meeting, but looked tired. She had carried the station for years, and the promise of a fair budget did not remove yesterday's exhaustion. Yao arranged an additional rest day rather than treating Fen's devotion as an endless resource. A fireproof cabinet held consent forms during the repair. A geographical survey confirmed the distance between remote stops. The security expert explained how a hacker could exploit weak passwords. Even a layman could see that the contract granted indefinite data rights. The new policy would liberate residents from compulsory app registration.

程睿協助計算過時路線系統耗掉的工時。表格顯示，修正一張地圖可能比派車走錯山谷便宜。議員要求立即作出財務結論，林遙拒絕了；稽核必須區分已確認的成本與預測。芬姨會後向她道謝，臉上卻充滿疲倦。她支撐救援站多年，公平預算的承諾不能消除昨日的勞累。林遙安排額外休息日，沒有把芬姨的奉獻當作無窮資源。 維修期間，防火櫃收納同意書。 地理測量確認偏遠停靠站之間的距離。 資安專家說明駭客可能如何利用薄弱密碼。 即使外行人也看得出契約授予無限期資料使用權。 新政策將讓居民擺脫強制應用程式登記。

A newspaper published a photograph from seven years ago, showing Yao beside the evacuation vehicles. The caption claimed she had ordered every route closed. She knew the caption was incomplete, but so was her own memory. Rui offered a copy of the model's update history. She asked him to send it to the independent reviewer, not to her privately. A fair inquiry could not be rebuilt as a conversation between two people who wanted to be believed. They needed records strong enough to survive disappointment, including her own. Children listened to a mermaid story while adults reviewed legal clauses. The miscellaneous charges included unexplained network maintenance fees. Each occurrence of an unusual login had to be recorded independently. A station outing could not require parents to disclose private medical data. The staff promised to persevere through the difficult budget review.

報紙刊出七年前的照片，林遙站在撤離車輛旁。圖片說明稱她命令封閉所有道路。她知道那句話不完整，卻也明白自己的記憶同樣不完整。程睿提出提供模型更新歷史，她請他直接交給獨立審查員，而不是私下給她。公平調查不能只靠兩個渴望被相信的人對談。證據必須足以承受失望，包括她自己的失望。 成人審閱法律條款時，孩子聽著人魚故事。 雜項費用含有未經說明的網路維護費。 每次異常登入事件都必須獨立記錄。 救援站戶外活動不能要求家長公開私人醫療資料。 員工承諾堅持完成艱難的預算審查。

The workers approved a provisional schedule and a transparent method for reporting extra hours. Nobody called it a permanent solution. That night, Yao found Fen asleep over a cup of tea and placed a blanket over her shoulders. The gesture felt more useful than another speech about gratitude. Outside, Rui waited with a message from the reviewer: the old route files had been located. He did not open them first. Yao thanked him for that restraint. The past was approaching, and for once they would meet it with other witnesses present. Rui needed time to ponder his undisclosed past advice. The meeting began to revolve around ownership rather than convenience. Every shared kitchen utensil was returned after the volunteer meal. Yao refused to turn the contractor into a cartoon villain without evidence. The children ate yogurt while the lawyers compared contract versions.

工作者通過暫行輪班表，以及申報額外工時的透明方法。沒有人稱這是永久解方。夜裡，林遙發現芬姨伏在茶杯旁睡著，替她披上毯子。這比再講一段感謝的話有用。屋外，程睿帶來審查員訊息：舊路線檔案找到了。他沒有自行開啟。林遙謝謝他的克制。過去正一步步走近，而這次他們會在其他見證人在場時共同面對。 程睿需要時間思索自己過去未揭露的建議。 會議開始圍繞所有權，而非便利性打轉。 志工餐後，每件共用廚具都歸回原位。 沒有證據前，林遙拒絕把承包商塑造成漫畫式惡棍。 律師比對合約版本時，孩子吃著優格。

Before the volunteers went home, Yao read the draft schedule aloud. A young dispatcher noticed that a night shift left no adequate rest before the next morning's training. She thanked him and changed the plan on the spot. In the old station, people might have accepted the schedule to avoid seeming uncommitted. Now a correction could be made without making anyone feel disloyal. Fen watched the exchange and looked proud in a way that made Yao briefly forget how tired she was.

志工回家前，林遙大聲讀出輪班草案。年輕調度員發現有個夜班之後接著清晨訓練，休息時間不夠。她道謝，當場修改。過去人們可能為免看起來不夠投入就照單全收。現在更正不必讓任何人被指責不忠誠。芬姨看著這一幕，神情驕傲，讓林遙短暫忘了自己的疲憊。
