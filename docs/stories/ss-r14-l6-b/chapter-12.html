冬夜的房間

A Room for Winter

雪線之外，我不再等你 · 第 12 / 25 章 · 5000–6000 · 英中對照 · 校訂中

用互動版查詞、朗讀與收藏

The lower village offered its unused community hall as a winter shelter. The plan sounded generous until a nurse asked where people with breathing difficulties would sleep and who would supervise the heating system. Yao added those questions to the official plan. She refused to treat an empty building as a functioning refuge without checking its water, exits, and accessible rooms. A local carpenter repaired a damaged door while volunteers counted blankets. Their work was ordinary, careful, and far less photogenic than a rescue helicopter. An attendant kept the temporary shelter staffed while the path remained closed. The school cook mixed pancake batter for the delayed visitors. The latest census showed households omitted from the original service map. A retired chemist helped explain why the old bridge paint was peeling. Birds began to chirp beyond the temporary barriers.

山下村落提供閒置活動中心作為冬季避難所。這個提案聽來慷慨，直到護理師問呼吸困難者該睡哪裡、暖氣由誰管理。林遙把問題寫進正式計畫。她拒絕把空房子直接當成可以使用的避難所，沒有檢查水、出口和無障礙房間前不算完成。木匠修好破門，志工清點毯子。這些工作平凡仔細，遠不如救援直升機適合拍照。 步道封閉期間，一名服務員持續留守臨時避難所。 學校廚師為滯留訪客調製煎餅麵糊。 最新人口普查顯示部分家庭被原始服務地圖遺漏。 退休化學師協助解釋舊橋油漆剝落的原因。 臨時圍欄外，鳥兒開始啁啾。

The county finance office offered a small emergency grant. It required the station to report every purchase, including food and medicine delivered by volunteers. Some members complained about the paperwork; others feared that strict forms would discourage donations. Yao designed a simplified receipt sheet with the accountant and asked independent staff to audit a sample. She would rather correct a clumsy form than allow money to vanish into good intentions. Rui tested the shelter's printed directions against its actual entrances. Two arrows had been placed backward. The volunteer desk could dispense blankets only after the inventory was recorded. The driver's tired groan made Yao call for a rest break. A children's encyclopedia described an iceberg drifting far from shore. The fire service gave invaluable advice on the safe collection point. A lavish charity dinner would not repair the damaged path.

縣府財務室提供一筆小額緊急補助，要求救援站申報每筆採購，包括志工送來的食物和藥品。有人抱怨文書繁瑣，有人怕嚴格表單嚇跑捐款。林遙與會計設計簡化收據，再請獨立人員抽查。她寧願修正難用的表單，也不願讓金錢消失在善意裡。程睿拿避難所的印刷指引對照實際入口，發現兩支箭頭標反了。 志工服務台必須先完成庫存登記，才能發放毯子。 駕駛疲憊的呻吟讓林遙要求休息。 兒童百科描述一座漂離海岸的冰山。 消防單位對安全領書點提供極有價值的建議。 豪華慈善晚宴修不好受損步道。

While they corrected the signs, Rui told Yao that his mother had recently moved into assisted housing. He had been afraid she would think he had abandoned her. Yao spoke about returning to White Ridge only after years of refusing invitations. Both had spent too long measuring duty by how much of themselves they could surrender. She said she was considering the city fellowship seriously. Rui did not ask whether there was a place for him in that plan. Instead, he asked what part of the work made her feel most alive. The new public logo included a mountain ridge and an open hand. A loudspeaker announcement directed visitors away from the closed bridge. A martial arts instructor offered a gentle mobility class in the shelter hall. The clinic monitored a minor flu outbreak in the neighboring village. A travel guide featured oyster farming along the distant coast.

更正標示時，程睿說母親最近搬進有照護服務的住所，他曾怕母親覺得被遺棄。林遙則談起自己多年來拒絕返鄉邀請，直到最近才回來。他們都花太久，用犧牲多少自己來衡量責任。她說自己正認真考慮外地的研究職缺。程睿沒有問那份計畫裡是否有他的位置，而是問哪一部分工作最讓她感覺活著。 新的公共標誌包含山脊與一隻張開的手。 擴音器公告引導訪客遠離封閉橋梁。 一名武術教練在避難所大廳提供溫和的活動課。 診所監測鄰村的小型流感疫情。 旅遊指南介紹遠方海岸的牡蠣養殖。

The shelter passed its first limited inspection. Only two rooms could open, and the hall could not replace a hospital or a permanent home. Yao posted those limitations as clearly as the address. Several residents were disappointed, but a nurse thanked her for not promising more than the building could offer. At dusk, the volunteers practiced guiding neighbors to the correct entrance. A child asked whether they would have to sleep there forever. “Only if there is a real emergency,” Fen said gently. “And only until we can make a better plan.” The council agreed to populate the route map with verified household counts. The fallen tree was a setback, not a reason to abandon the service. Some residents remained skeptical of the proposed licensed detour. Yao refused to subscribe to the idea that delay meant failure. The operator could not withhold public safety notices from residents.

避難所通過第一階段有限檢查，只有兩間房可開放；活動中心不能取代醫院或永久住所。林遙把這些限制和地址一樣清楚地張貼。幾名居民失望，護理師卻感謝她沒有承諾建築做不到的事。黃昏，志工練習引導居民走正確入口。孩子問是否要永遠睡在這裡。「只有真正發生緊急情況才需要，」芬姨溫柔回答，「而且只是暫時，直到我們有更好的安排。」 議會同意以查證過的家戶數填入路線圖。 倒木是挫折，不是放棄服務的理由。 部分居民仍懷疑提議中的合格繞道路線。 林遙拒絕接受延誤就等於失敗的想法。 營運者不能向居民扣留公共安全通知。

The nurse asked for a practice night before opening the shelter to the public. Yao agreed, even though the extra inspection would delay the announcement. A building could pass on paper and still be difficult for a frightened person to navigate in darkness. Volunteers tested the directions without revealing the answers first. They found one confusing turn and moved the sign. By midnight, the most valuable result was not a photograph of a ready shelter, but a list of small problems that could still be fixed.

護理師要求避難所公開啟用前先進行夜間試走。林遙答應，即使多一次檢查會延遲公告。建築可能在紙面上合格，驚慌的人在黑暗裡卻未必找得到路。志工不先公布答案，實際測試指引，找到一個容易走錯的轉角，移動了標示。午夜時最有價值的不是準備完成的照片，而是一份仍能修好的小問題清單。
