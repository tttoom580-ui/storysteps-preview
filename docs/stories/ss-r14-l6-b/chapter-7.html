每個人都能走的路

A Route for Everyone

雪線之外，我不再等你 · 第 7 / 25 章 · 5000–6000 · 英中對照 · 校訂中

The trial route crossed an old hillside plantation and then reached a narrow lookout. County maps marked it accessible, yet a volunteer who used a cane found the lower gate difficult to open. The station had been designing routes for healthy adults while calling the result public service. Yao invited residents with different mobility needs to review the plan and paid them for their time. Rui changed the map legend, but Yao insisted that a label alone could not remove a physical barrier. The gate would remain closed until its owner agreed to a safe modification. A bulky equipment case would not fit through the narrow gate. Yao would not deem the student guilty without checking the receipt. A history club discussed how dictatorship can silence dissent. A paper airplane seemed to glide across the quiet training room. Students displayed handicraft trail markers at the village safety fair.

試辦路線穿過山坡上的舊種植園，通向一處狹窄觀景台。縣府地圖將它標為無障礙，但一名拄杖志工連下方的門都很難打開。救援站一直以健康成年人的條件設計路線，卻稱那是公共服務。林遙邀請行動需求不同的居民審查計畫，並支付參與費。程睿修改圖例，林遙仍強調更改標籤不能消除實際障礙。地主同意安全修改前，通道保持封閉。 一只笨重的裝備箱無法通過狹窄大門。 林遙不會未查收據就認定學生有罪。 歷史社團討論獨裁統治如何壓制異議。 紙飛機彷彿滑過安靜的訓練室。 學生在村莊安全市集展示手工製作的步道標誌。

The review brought a difficult argument. An adventure company wanted the hillside reserved for paying guests. A village elder said locals had crossed it for generations. Neither statement alone settled the land agreement. Yao asked for the original documents and recorded who had custody of each copy. A translator helped the committee understand testimony from a migrant worker. His account described a gate that had been locked during an earlier medical call. Yao thanked him and kept his address private because he had requested it. An interpreter helped a visiting family understand the lending policy. A lesbian volunteer asked that the training policy protect every family equally. The station's liberation from rumors began with a written correction. The lofty shelves made several emergency kits impossible for some volunteers to reach. Young volunteers learned to navigate the route database without exposing private calls.

審查引出一場艱難爭論。探險公司希望山坡只供付費遊客使用；村中長者說居民已走了好幾代。任何一方的說法都不足以決定土地權利。林遙索取原始文件，記錄每份副本由誰保管。一名口譯員協助委員會聽懂移工的證詞；他說以前有次醫療求助時，門曾被鎖住。林遙向他道謝，並依他的要求保密住址。 口譯員協助來訪家庭理解申領規定。 一名女同志志工要求訓練政策平等保障每種家庭。 救援站擺脫謠言束縛的第一步是書面更正。 高聳架子讓部分志工拿不到幾組緊急套件。 年輕志工學會操作路線資料庫，同時不公開私人求援紀錄。

Rui asked Yao to walk beside him after the meeting, along the level path near the stream. She agreed because the workday had ended and she wanted to. He said he had once mistaken being useful for being trustworthy. She told him usefulness could be measured in reports, while trust took time and could not be commanded. They spoke about ordinary things after that: the shape of the clouds, Fen's terrible singing, the taste of tea after a cold shift. The conversation ended without a declaration, and she felt grateful for its simplicity. No volunteer should overwork to compensate for a weak public budget. A pebble trapped under the wheel had caused the equipment cart to shake. The disciplinary decision remained pending until the evidence was reviewed. The old plantation records prompted careful discussion of past forced labor. Farmers asked when they could plow the field beside the repair lane.

會後程睿問林遙，是否願意沿溪邊平緩小徑走一段。她答應了，因為工作已結束，也因為她自己想去。他說自己過去錯把「派得上用場」當成「值得信任」。林遙說，是否有用能寫進報告，信任卻需要時間，也不能命令別人給予。之後他們聊起普通小事：雲的形狀、芬姨難聽的歌聲、寒冷值勤後一杯茶的味道。談話沒有告白就結束了，她反而珍惜那份簡單。 不該讓志工過勞來彌補不足的公共預算。 輪子下卡住的小石子讓裝備推車晃動。 處分決定在證據審查完畢前保持待決。 舊種植園紀錄引發對過去強迫勞動的審慎討論。 農民詢問何時能犁整維修車道旁的田地。

The committee approved a revised lower route, not the inaccessible summit path. It also required the company to provide a written response to the worker's account and to address the gate before any new permit. Yao knew the decision would disappoint hikers who wanted the famous view. Still, a public route should not become a test of whose body was considered normal. She left the meeting with the documents in a sealed folder and a rare feeling that the community had changed the question itself. A children's tale about royalty did not change the rules for public grants. An unfair accusation could spur trainees to hide safety concerns. Yao invited the student to stroll through the reopened collection. An old guide explained how mountain sunlight could tan exposed skin. The chairman called privacy a trifle; the students challenged him immediately.

委員會核准的是修訂後的低處路線，不是無法讓所有人通行的山頂路徑。委員會也要求公司書面回應移工證詞，並在核發新許可前處理那道門。林遙知道渴望登頂的人會失望，但公共路線不能變成誰的身體才算正常的測驗。她把文件裝進封口資料夾離開，難得感覺社區不只是選了答案，更改變了問題本身。 兒童書中的王族故事不會改變公共補助規則。 不公平的指控可能促使受訓者隱瞞安全疑慮。 林遙邀那名學生走進重新開放的藏書區散步。 舊指南說明山區日照如何曬黑外露皮膚。 主席把隱私說成小事，學生立刻提出反駁。

Yao took a final look at the gate. The new route would be less spectacular, but it had places to rest and a way to ask for help. She imagined her younger self treating a successful summit photograph as proof of good planning. Now she knew that the quieter route often taught more. Rui had written the objections on the map in a different color, leaving space for future changes. She appreciated that he had not erased the line that showed where disagreement began.

林遙最後看了一眼那道門。新路線不那麼壯觀，但設有休息處，也能方便求助。她想起年輕時的自己，可能會把登頂照片當成計畫良好的證明。現在她明白，較安靜的道路往往教會人更多。程睿用另一種顏色在地圖上記下反對意見，留出未來修改空間。她欣賞他沒有擦去分歧從何開始的線。
