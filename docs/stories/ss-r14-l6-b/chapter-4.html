The Badge in the Snow

雪線之外，我不再等你 · 第 4 / 25 章 · 5000–6000 · 英中對照 · 校訂中

A child's winter badge turned up beneath a bench at the western trailhead. It belonged to a school group that had taken an unregistered shortcut during a class outing. The teacher insisted nobody had been in danger, but a volunteer found boot marks near a closed slope. Yao did not announce a rescue where none had occurred. She invited the teacher, the volunteer, and the trail manager to reconstruct the route separately. Their accounts agreed about the time but not the turn they had taken. The difference would matter when the next storm covered the markers. A nurse posted a guide about digital addiction beside the station's volunteer schedule. The new ramp had an elegant aesthetic but failed its first weight test. The engineer gave an approximate measurement until the instruments arrived. An archaeology survey described a lost mountain settlement near the closed path. Lian wore a volunteer badge and insisted on speaking for himself.

西側步道入口的長椅下，出現一枚孩子的冬季徽章。它屬於一群曾在校外教學時走未登記捷徑的學生。老師堅稱沒有人遇險，志工卻在封閉坡道附近發現鞋印。林遙沒有把沒有發生的事故誇大成搜救。她分別請老師、志工與步道管理人還原路線。三人的時間說法一致，轉彎位置卻不相同。下一場暴風雪會蓋住路標，這個差異因此格外重要。 護理師把數位成癮的衛教指南貼在救援站志工班表旁。 新坡道外觀美觀，卻未通過首次載重測試。 儀器到達前，工程師先提供近似測量值。 考古調查介紹封閉步道附近曾有一處消失的山中聚落。 連安佩戴志工識別證，堅持親自發言。

Rui returned with a map that showed an old archaeological site beneath the shortcut. In summer, hikers visited it for a view of the valley; in winter, broken stones made the crossing unpredictable. Yao asked the county to close that spur until a qualified inspection. A tour operator called her decision excessive and claimed she was destroying the area's image. She invited him to submit his evidence at the public hearing. Nobody would be excluded for disagreeing. The station's new rule was that disagreement belonged on the record, not in whispered attacks after the meeting. A small blaze in the previous winter had changed the school evacuation route. Yao said she wanted colleagues, not a disciple who never disagreed. A patient explanation could enlighten officials about everyday wheelchair use. There was no need to fuss over the paint color while the rail was loose. The map carried an implicit assumption that everyone could climb stairs.

程睿帶來的地圖顯示，捷徑下方有一處舊考古遺址。夏天遊客會來欣賞山谷，冬天鬆動的石塊卻讓通行難以預測。林遙要求縣府暫時封閉支線，直到合格檢查完成。觀光業者說她小題大作，破壞地方形象。她請對方在公開聽證中提出證據。任何人都不會因不同意見而被排除。救援站的新原則是把分歧寫進紀錄，而不是會後私下攻擊。 前一個冬季的小火災改變了學校疏散路線。 林遙說她需要同事，不需要永不反對的門徒。 耐心的說明能讓官員理解輪椅使用者的日常處境。 扶手還沒固定時，沒必要為油漆顏色大驚小怪。 地圖隱含一個假設：人人都能爬樓梯。

The training hall held an exhibition of photographs from earlier winters. Some showed the team celebrating successful searches; others showed empty chairs after long nights. Fen asked Yao which pictures should remain. Yao said all of them, provided the families had agreed. A proud organization could remember failure without turning suffering into decoration. When Rui looked at an image from seven years ago, he asked permission before discussing it. She did not grant it there, in front of everyone. That refusal was not cruelty. It was the boundary she needed to keep. The council asked a neutral chairperson to preside over the route hearing. Prestige could not substitute for a documented safety test. Some officials wanted to privatize the stopping bay without consulting residents. Morning sunlight began to radiate through the school windows. A fair rotation ensured Lian did not carry every consultation alone.

訓練大廳展出歷年冬季照片。有些記錄救援成功後的慶祝，有些則拍下漫長夜晚裡的空椅子。芬姨問林遙哪些照片該留下。她說只要家屬同意，就全部保留。值得驕傲的機構也能記得失敗，不必把別人的痛苦當裝飾。程睿看到七年前的一張照片，先問能不能談。林遙沒有在眾人面前同意。這不是殘酷，而是她需要維持的界線。 議會請中立主席主持路線聽證。 聲望不能取代有文件的安全測試。 部分官員想未諮詢居民就把停靠區私有化。 晨光開始透過學校窗戶向四周照射。 公平輪值確保連安不必獨自承擔所有諮詢。

The county accepted the short closure and agreed to review the school's walking plan. Yao marked the decision as temporary, with a date for reconsideration and an address for objections. She had learned that safety rules lose legitimacy when they become permanent commands without an appeal. Later, she returned the badge to its owner, who asked whether the ridge would ever be open again. “I hope so,” she answered. “But hope is not the certificate. We will tell you what the inspector finds.” An old shaver appeared among the personal items recovered from the training cabin. The group guided a stray dog away from the vehicle's wheels. Yao refused to put a veil of optimism over the failed ramp test. A vineyard owner offered land for a safe pullout but required a written agreement. The manufacturer confirmed that the folding lift still carried a warranty.

縣府同意短期封閉，並重新檢查學校的行走計畫。林遙在決定上寫明期限、重新審議日期與申訴窗口。她知道安全規則若變成沒有上訴途徑的永久命令，就會失去正當性。之後她把徽章還給孩子。孩子問山脊會不會再開放。「我希望會，」她回答，「但希望不是檢查證明。我們會告訴你檢查員的結果。」 訓練小屋整理出的個人物品裡，有一把老式刮鬍刀。 大家引導一隻流浪狗遠離車輪。 林遙拒絕用樂觀的薄紗遮掩坡道測試失敗。 葡萄園主人願提供安全停車用地，但要求書面協議。 製造商確認折疊升降設備仍在保固期內。

Later, the teacher returned with a revised field-trip plan. It included two trained adults, a clear turnaround point, and a promise to cancel the trip if the inspection remained incomplete. Yao accepted the plan for review, not as automatic permission. The child who had lost the badge brought a handwritten thank-you note. She pinned it near the doorway, where it could remind the team that safety sometimes meant disappointing people kindly instead of granting every request.

稍後老師帶著修改後的校外教學計畫回來，內容有兩名受訓成年人、清楚的折返點，也承諾檢查未完成就取消行程。林遙收下供審查，不代表直接核准。遺失徽章的孩子帶來手寫謝卡。她把卡片別在門口，提醒團隊：安全有時是溫柔地讓人失望，而不是滿足每項要求。
