沒有電池的訊號器

The Beacon Without a Battery

雪線之外，我不再等你 · 第 6 / 25 章 · 5000–6000 · 英中對照 · 校訂中

The inventory audit began with an empty slot. A rescue beacon had been marked ready although its battery had been removed months earlier. The staff had not concealed the removal; the paper repair slip hung beneath the workbench. Only the digital count had failed to change. Yao assigned a second person to check each device and required the volunteers to sign the result together. Rui offered to build an automatic inventory tool. She accepted the prototype only on the condition that the paper record would remain accessible when the power failed. An airtight storage box protected the rare maps from damp weather. The team hoped to attain a fair schedule before winter arrived. A visiting congresswoman asked residents about the road rather than lecturing them. Meiling made vegetable curry for the exhausted station volunteers. Yao did not despise neighbors who disagreed about funding.

設備盤點從一個空格開始。一具救援訊號器被標為待命，電池卻在數月前已拆下。工作人員沒有隱瞞，維修紙條就掛在工作檯下方；只有數位數量一直沒改。林遙安排第二人複查每一具設備，要求志工共同簽署結果。程睿提出製作自動盤點工具，她同意測試原型，但條件是停電時仍須能查閱紙本紀錄。 密封儲物箱保護珍稀地圖不受潮氣侵害。 團隊希望在冬季前達成公平班表。 來訪的國會女議員向居民詢問道路情況，而非訓話。 美玲為疲憊的救援站志工煮了蔬菜咖哩。 林遙不鄙視對經費有不同意見的鄰居。

A representative from the operator accused the station of inventing defects to extend its trial. Yao showed him the dated repair slip and invited him to observe the retest. He refused to attend but requested a copy of the result. She sent it through the same public channel used for everyone else. Fen later asked whether Yao regretted being so open with an opponent. “Facts do not belong to us because we found them,” Yao answered. “If they stand up to inspection, they can stand up to criticism.” An astronomy instructor explained the next lunar eclipse during the volunteer camp. The pantry marked which donated plants were edible. Meiling said she valued Yao's esteem but would make her own decisions. An evergreen tree beside the stop offered shelter from the wind. A child asked whether a fish fin worked like the stabilizer on a rescue boat.

私人業者的代表指責救援站捏造缺陷以延長試辦。林遙出示有日期的維修單，邀請對方旁觀複測。他拒絕出席，卻要求結果副本。她仍透過與其他人相同的公開管道寄出。芬姨後來問她是否後悔對反對者如此透明。「事實不會因為是我們找到的就屬於我們，」林遙回答，「經得起檢查，就應該經得起批評。」 志工營隊裡，天文老師解釋下一次月食。 餐食櫃標示哪些捐贈植物可以食用。 美玲說她珍惜林遙的敬重，但仍要自己做決定。 停靠站旁的常綠樹提供避風處。 孩子問魚鰭是否像救援船的穩定裝置一樣運作。

In the evening, Rui explained why his old map model had missed a narrow winter channel. The software relied on a summer photograph, and he had not marked the uncertainty clearly enough. Yao listened without interrupting. It was not the whole account of the evacuation, and he did not present it as such. She opened a separate page for questions that would need independent records. When he looked disappointed, she reminded him that asking careful questions was not the same as refusing to hear him. He said he understood. A flake of snow settled on the driver's route sheet. The school sold handmade merchandise to fund mountain safety classes. A rotating schedule could prevent monotony and volunteer burnout. Reading detective stories was Fen's favorite pastime after a long shift. The council removed redundancy from three overlapping reporting forms.

晚上，程睿解釋七年前的地圖模型為何漏掉一條狹窄的冬季通道。軟體依賴夏天照片，而他沒有把不確定性標清楚。林遙不打斷他。這不是那場撤離的全部經過，他也沒有假裝已解釋完。她另開一頁，列下必須靠獨立紀錄回答的問題。見他有些失望，她提醒他：謹慎提問不代表拒絕傾聽。他說自己明白。 一片雪花落在司機的路線表上。 學校販售手作商品，為山區安全課程籌款。 輪值班表能避免單調與志工倦怠。 長班結束後，芬姨最喜歡的消遣是讀偵探小說。 議會刪除三份重複報表中的冗餘內容。

The audit ended with four items available, three under repair, and one awaiting independent certification. The numbers were smaller than the station had reported, but they were real. Yao had learned to value a true limitation more than a beautiful promise. She posted the inventory near the entrance and asked visitors not to mistake it for a guarantee of assistance in every condition. Winter made guarantees dangerous. Preparation, honest warnings, and permission to stop were more dependable than confidence alone. Children were free to roam the schoolyard, not the closed bridge. Volunteers collected rubbish around the temporary rescue stop. The fantasy novel described a knight who would slay a dragon. Yao recognized a sly attempt to hide a data fee in the contract. Warm vapor rose from the bowls on the community dinner table.

盤點結束時，四具裝備可以使用、三具正在維修，一具等待獨立認證。數字比原先回報的小，卻是真實的。林遙已學會，真正的限制比漂亮的承諾更值得信賴。她把清單貼在入口，提醒訪客不要誤以為這代表任何天候都能得到救援。冬天讓保證變得危險。準備、誠實警示，以及必要時停止的權利，比單純的自信可靠。 孩子可以在校園遊走，不能走上封閉橋梁。 志工清理臨時救援停靠點附近的垃圾。 奇幻小說描述一名將屠龍的騎士。 林遙看出有人狡猾地想把資料費藏進合約。 社區晚餐桌上的熱湯碗升起水蒸氣。

Fen attached a yellow tag to every device awaiting repair. On the wall she hung a chart showing who would confirm each completed test. When a visitor asked why the station displayed so many faults, Yao pointed to the dates beside them. Problems that could be seen could also be addressed. She would have preferred a clean wall, but she trusted this honest one more. For the first time the equipment room looked less like a display and more like a working service.

芬姨替每具待修裝備掛上黃色標籤，牆上則張貼完工測試由誰確認的表格。訪客問為何要展示那麼多故障，林遙指著旁邊日期說明：能被看見的問題，才有機會被處理。她當然希望牆面整齊，卻更信任這面誠實的牆。設備室第一次不像展示間，而像真正運作中的服務場所。
