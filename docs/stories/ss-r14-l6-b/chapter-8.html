第一次警報演練

A Trial Alarm

雪線之外，我不再等你 · 第 8 / 25 章 · 5000–6000 · 英中對照 · 校訂中

The morning alarm was a scheduled drill, but Yao did not tell the team exactly which exercise would come first. They knew the boundaries: no one would enter a hazardous slope, and any member could stop the exercise for safety. The simulation involved a missing family at the trail junction and a visitor who needed urgent medical assessment. Fen watched the communications desk while Rui observed the map display. The signal reached most volunteers, but one shelter received it late. Yao marked the delay in red rather than rounding it away. A school nurse explained why antibiotic medicine should never be shared. Residents began to applaud after the revised road map was displayed. An astronomer visiting the school read aloud about winter constellations. The new team chose to computerize counts without eliminating paper receipts. A faulty ramp could disable access even when every rescue kit was ready.

早晨警報是預先安排的演練，但林遙沒有透露每個環節的確切順序。隊員知道界線：不得進入危險坡面，任何人都能因安全疑慮停止演練。模擬情境包括步道交叉口的失聯家庭，以及一名需要緊急醫療評估的訪客。芬姨監督通訊桌，程睿觀察地圖顯示。訊號送達多數志工，但一處避難所晚了幾分鐘才收到。林遙把延遲用紅字記下，沒有用四捨五入掩蓋。 學校護理師解釋為何抗生素藥品不該與人共用。 修訂道路地圖展示後，居民開始鼓掌。 到校的天文學家朗讀冬季星座的故事。 新團隊選擇電腦化統計，但不取消紙本收據。 即使救援套件都備妥，故障坡道仍會妨礙出入。

After the drill, the county spokesperson asked whether the station could declare its system fully reliable. Yao said no. They had proved some functions and found a failure that needed retesting. A journalist frowned at the answer, expecting a better headline. Fen smiled and said the public had suffered enough from impressive headlines. Rui traced the delay to an interface between old and new equipment, but he would not certify the repair himself. A different technician had to verify the changes, and the station needed to repeat the test before expanding service. The practice team would evacuate the stop if the river reached the warning line. Every expenditure appeared in the temporary grant ledger. An irritable official complained about the extra testing time. Yao said kin should not make decisions for a resident without consent. The school offered a meditation class while the repair crew worked.

演練後，縣府發言人問救援站能否宣布系統全面可靠。林遙說不能。他們證實部分功能正常，也找到一項必須複測的失效。記者皺眉，原以為能有更響亮的標題。芬姨笑說，公眾已經受夠漂亮的標題。程睿追出延遲原因是新舊設備的介面，但他不會自己簽署維修驗證，必須由另一名技師確認，再重新測試後才能擴大服務。 若河水達警戒線，演練團隊就會撤離停靠站。 每筆支出都列在臨時補助帳冊。 一名暴躁官員抱怨額外測試耗時。 林遙說親屬不能未經同意替居民做決定。 維修團隊工作時，學校開辦冥想課。

The medical officer used the drill to discuss fatigue and consent with younger volunteers. She reminded them that kindness did not permit them to treat someone who had refused nonessential help. Yao watched a new trainee ask a patient actor what assistance was wanted instead of assuming. It was a small moment, easy to overlook among screens and radios. She thought of how many decisions in her old career had been made for people who were absent from the room. This time, she would count whether the people affected had actually been heard. A nickel coin fell from the cracked box of donated objects. Rui helped volunteers orient the ramp toward the level path. The school exhibit explained oriental textile patterns with historical context. A resident recovering from pneumonia requested home delivery. The manufacturer brought a prototype of an accessible emergency-call button.

醫護人員利用演練向年輕志工談疲勞與同意。她提醒大家，善意不等於可以替拒絕非必要協助的人做決定。林遙看見一名新隊員先問扮演患者的人需要什麼，而非自行假設。那是螢幕與無線電間容易忽略的小片刻。她想起自己過去有多少決定，是在當事人不在場時替他們做的。這次她要計算的，也包括受影響的人究竟有沒有被聽見。 一枚五分硬幣從破裂的捐贈物箱中掉出。 程睿協助志工把坡道朝向平坦步道。 學校展覽以歷史脈絡介紹東方織物圖樣。 一名正從肺炎康復的居民請求到府配送補給。 製造商帶來無障礙緊急呼叫按鈕的原型。

By nightfall the delayed shelter had a temporary backup channel. The trial would continue under limits until the independent retest. Rui offered to walk Yao home, then changed his wording: “Would you like company?” She did. At her gate she thanked him for staying when the work had become unglamorous. He answered that the quiet repair work mattered as much as any emergency. Their hands brushed while she took back her notebook, but neither pretended the touch made a promise. She went inside smiling at a choice that remained her own. A medical reference on psychiatry stayed available without exposing residents' names. The second ramp was more robust under an independent load test. No sovereign authority could ignore the published accessibility requirements. The student spokesperson read the group's questions without interruption. Fen placed spare blankets in a wardrobe beside the shelter's entrance.

入夜前，延遲收到警報的避難所取得臨時備援頻道。獨立複測完成前，試辦只能在限制下繼續。程睿想說要送林遙回家，隨即改口：「妳想有人陪嗎？」她願意。到了門口，她謝謝他願意留下做那些沒有光環的事。他說安靜的修復工作和危急救援一樣重要。她拿回筆記本時，兩人的手輕輕碰了一下，卻沒有誰把那一觸當成承諾。她帶著微笑進屋，因為選擇仍屬於自己。 精神醫學參考書照常提供，且不公開居民姓名。 第二座坡道在獨立載重測試中更堅固。 即使是最高主權機關也不能忽視已公布的無障礙要求。 學生發言人不受打斷地讀出小組問題。 芬姨把備用毯子放進避難所入口旁的衣櫃。

Before sleeping, Yao replayed the drill in her mind. She remembered the delayed warning more clearly than the successful messages, and she wondered whether that was fair to the people who had worked hard. Then she corrected herself: recording a fault did not erase the successes surrounding it. It showed where the next hour of effort should go. She wrote a thank-you note to the volunteers, along with a precise plan for the retest. Both messages belonged together.

睡前林遙在心裡重播演練。她記得延遲警告比成功傳遞的訊息更清楚，擔心這是否對努力的隊員不公平。隨後她修正想法：記錄失效不會抹去其他成功，而是指出下一小時的努力該用在哪裡。她寫下給志工的感謝信，也附上精確的複測計畫。兩種訊息原本就該放在一起。
