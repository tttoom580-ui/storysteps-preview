門上的接管令

The Order on the Door

雪線之外，我不再等你 · 第 1 / 25 章 · 5000–6000 · 英中對照 · 校訂中

The closure notice had been nailed to the rescue station door so recently that a curl of pale wood still hung from the frame. Lin Yao read it twice. In sixty days, the county would award a single company the authority to coordinate mountain assistance. The document called this an efficiency measure. It said nothing about shepherds, schoolchildren, or the people who crossed the ridge without buying an adventure pass. A thirteen-year-old boy stood behind her with a broken radio. He had used it the previous night when his grandfather failed to return from the upper pasture. No incident number had been issued. The metal clasp on the equipment crate snapped in Yao's hand. Residents asked the county office to convene an open meeting. A picture of a bear cub marked the children's shelf. The mobile dental clinic used the same village square. One angry caller called the mayor a dictator, but Yao demanded evidence.

救援站門上的接管公告才釘上去，木框邊還掛著一小片新削下的木屑。林遙讀了兩遍。六十天後，縣府將把山區救援協調權交給一家得標公司。公告稱這是提升效率，卻完全沒提到牧羊人、學童，以及那些沒有買登山票證卻需要翻越山脊的居民。十三歲的男孩拿著壞掉的無線電站在她身後。前一晚祖父遲遲沒有從高處牧場回來，他用過這支無線電，卻沒有得到任何事故編號。 裝備箱的金屬扣環在林遙手中斷裂。 居民要求縣府召開公開會議。 兒童資料架上貼著小熊幼獸的圖片。 巡迴牙科診所也使用同一座村莊廣場。 一名憤怒來電者罵鎮長是獨裁者，林遙則要求提出證據。

Yao had been away from White Ridge for seven years. Her old locker was empty, but the wooden table still carried the mark of a hot kettle. Station caretaker Aunt Fen placed the boy's radio beside the closure letter and showed Yao a paper notebook. The missing grandfather had returned safely at dawn with help from three neighbors. None of them appeared in the county statistics. “If the work isn't counted,” Fen said, “someone can pretend we never did it.” Yao refused to turn one notebook into a public accusation. She asked the boy and his family before copying a single line. The closure directive offered no explanation for the uncounted emergency calls. A remote dwelling needed a way to contact rescuers without internet service. The shepherd's horse began to gallop across the pasture as clouds thickened. The misleading figures could heighten distrust between neighboring villages. The school janitor offered a dry room for storing emergency supplies.

林遙離開白嶺已經七年。舊置物櫃空空的，木桌上卻還留著熱水壺燙出的圓印。看守救援站的芬姨把男孩的無線電放在公告旁，拿出一本紙本紀錄。祖父在三名鄰居協助下於清晨平安返家，但這件事沒有出現在縣府數字中。「沒有被算進去，」芬姨說，「別人就能假裝我們什麼都沒做。」林遙沒有拿一本筆記就公開指控誰。她先徵得男孩與家人同意，才抄錄其中一行。 接管指令沒有說明為何漏算部分緊急求援。 偏遠住所即使沒有網路，也需要聯絡救援人員的方法。 雲層變厚時，牧羊人的馬開始奔馳穿過牧場。 誤導性的數字可能加深鄰近村落的不信任。 學校清潔員提供乾燥房間存放緊急物資。

Cheng Rui arrived before sunset carrying a roll of old contour maps. He had once supervised the forecasting model that Yao trusted during the disastrous winter evacuation. When the inquiry blamed her alone, he had not corrected the record. “I can check which routes the company plans to close,” he offered. Yao answered that his qualifications were useful, but his presence was not a personal favor. Any map he produced would be available to the volunteer committee and the county inspector at the same time. He accepted the condition without asking her to welcome him back. Their unresolved history stayed in the doorway between them. The old station motto said that every resident deserved a place. A pilgrim crossing the ridge asked where to report a missing traveler. A previous flood agreement offered a precedent for temporary deliveries. The contractor hoped to reap profits from an exclusive mountain assistance subscription. The rescue vehicle remained stationary until the driver completed safety checks.

日落前，程睿抱著一捲舊等高線地圖抵達。他曾負責七年前冬季撤離時林遙信賴的預報模型；事後調查只責怪她，他卻沒有更正紀錄。「我可以查新公司準備封閉哪些路線。」他說。林遙回答，他的專業有用，但這不是私人幫忙。他製作的地圖必須同時交給志工委員會與縣府檢查員。他接受條件，沒有要求她因此歡迎他回來。兩人尚未解開的往事，仍像一道門檻隔在中間。 救援站舊座右銘寫著每位居民都值得有自己的位置。 一名越過山脊的朝聖者詢問該向何處通報旅人失聯。 過去的洪水協議提供了臨時配送的先例。 承包商希望透過獨家山區協助訂閱制度獲利。 駕駛完成安全檢查前，救援車保持靜止。

At the first evening meeting, Yao wrote three promises on the board: every incident would receive a number, every route warning would show its source, and nobody would be sent into danger to make a performance look heroic. The room was crowded with residents whose names had never appeared on tourist charts. Some wanted the station preserved, while others worried about the taxes required to maintain it. Yao recorded both positions. She had come home believing she would stay only long enough to hand over a few boxes. Instead, she found herself asking for sixty days to make the records honest. Yao said an app download was not a synonym for an answered emergency call. The school planned to transplant two trees away from the turning area. A community trustee demanded the complete service contract before voting. Residents needed unconditional access to emergency assistance during the review. Yao refused to utter an accusation before checking the paper register.

晚間第一次會議上，林遙在黑板寫下三項承諾：每件求援都要有編號、每項路況警示都要註明來源、不能為了表演英勇而把任何人送進危險。屋裡坐滿了從未出現在觀光圖表上的居民。有些人想保留救援站，有些人擔心維持運作所需的稅金。她把兩種意見都記錄下來。她原本以為自己只會返鄉交接幾箱資料，如今卻提出六十天的期限，要求把紀錄重新做對。 林遙說，應用程式下載量不等於求援電話獲得接聽。 學校計畫把兩棵樹移植到迴車區以外。 一位社區受託人要求表決前公開完整服務契約。 審查期間，居民仍需要不附帶條件的緊急協助。 核對紙本登記冊之前，林遙拒絕說出指控。

Before turning out the lights, Yao placed the boy's broken radio in a labeled evidence bag. It was not evidence of misconduct; it was evidence that someone had tried to ask for help. She wrote the family's contact number in a separate private register, locked the drawer, and left the public version without their names. The difference mattered. The county might one day change its counting method, but Yao wanted the new habit of asking permission to begin tonight.

熄燈前，林遙把男孩的壞無線電放進標記清楚的證物袋。它不是違規的證據，而是有人曾努力求助的證據。她把家人的聯絡方式寫在另一份保密冊中，鎖進抽屜；公開版本則不列姓名。這個區分很重要。縣府也許有天會修改統計方式，但林遙希望「先取得同意」的習慣從今晚就開始。
