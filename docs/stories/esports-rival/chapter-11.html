Fees and Favors

死對頭竟然是我的電競搭檔 · 第 11 / 25 章 · 1000–2000 · 英中對照 · 校訂中

用互動版查詞、朗讀與收藏

A sponsor offered money for the university's gaming room if the club would display its logo on every guide. Mei worried that students might mistake advertising for a rule. Kai wanted a clear budget before anyone rejected help. They asked the council to publish the cost, the purpose, and a way for students to refuse promotional messages. The proposal was changed instead of secretly accepted. Outside the building, people hurried to their next classes. Inside, the work moved more slowly, because every rule would affect someone who was not present.

一名贊助商願意資助大學遊戲室，條件是所有指南都印上商標。梅擔心學生把廣告誤當規則；凱則希望在拒絕前先列出清楚預算。他們要求學生會公開費用、用途，以及拒收宣傳訊息的方法。提案最後被修改，而不是私下通過。大樓外，人們趕著上下一堂課；裡面的工作卻慢得多，因為每條規則都會影響不在場的人。

The author of the safety guide asked to remain unnamed. Kai measured a gap of one centimeter beside the damaged door. A crow called loudly from the tree outside the practice room. Mei wrote down their disagreement before trying another plan. The teacher raised an eyebrow when the sponsor refused clear terms. The next round would bring a different map and a new difficulty. They could not control that change, but they could control how carefully they prepared. At the end of the evening, they chose to ask one more question instead of making one more guess. It was slower, but it kept their work honest.

安全指南的作者希望不公開姓名。凱測量受損門旁一公分的縫隙。練習室外的樹上有隻烏鴉大聲叫。梅先記下兩人的分歧，才嘗試另一個方案。當贊助商拒絕明確條款，老師挑起眉毛。下一輪會有不同地圖與新困難。他們無法控制變化，卻能控制準備有多仔細。傍晚結束時，他們選擇多問一個問題，而不是多猜一次。這樣較慢，卻讓工作保持誠實。

Quiet Signal entered the regional bracket. The map contained a narrow forest route watched by a guard who reacted to sound. Moth proposed moving slowly; Beacon pointed out an opening after every third warning. They combined the ideas and crossed without losing a player. A coach in the public chat praised their different skills, not knowing how little they had shared about their lives. The room was noisy, but their notes were clear. They could return to those notes later instead of depending on memory or the loudest person.

靜默訊號進入區域賽。地圖有條狹窄森林路，守衛會對聲音作出反應。飛蛾提議放慢速度；燈塔指出每三次警告後會有空檔。他們結合兩個方法，全員安全通過。公開聊天室的一位教練稱讚他們能力互補，卻不知道彼此幾乎沒談過現實生活。教室雖然吵，筆記卻很清楚。他們之後可以回頭核對，而不是只靠記憶或聲音最大的人。

The club would pay no extra fee without a public vote. A narrow forest path appeared in the regional game map. The digital guard turned toward every loud movement on the map. Mei asked the report to include every known equipment cost. The narrow forest gate opened only after the third warning signal. Their signals were short because the important part had been practiced earlier. Good timing came from patient work that the audience could not always see.

沒有公開表決，社團不會支付額外費用。區域賽地圖出現一條狹窄森林小徑。地圖裡的數位守衛會朝每次大聲動靜轉身。梅要求報告列出所有已知設備成本。狹窄的森林門只會在第三次警告訊號後開啟。訊號很短，因為重要的部分早已練過。好的時機來自觀眾未必看見的耐心努力。

When Moth received a request for an interview, she declined to reveal personal details. Beacon did the same. They prepared a short statement together about teamwork and patience. On campus, Mei and Kai also signed a public notice about the sponsor, both names appearing on the same line. Neither noticed how their cooperation had begun to echo in two places. Mei listened to a student whose question sounded simple at first. She remembered that most difficult skills had begun with one simple question of her own. Before stopping for the day, they reviewed the next step and who would be responsible for it. That simple habit prevented an unfinished task from becoming a new argument.

飛蛾收到訪談邀請，拒絕揭露個人資料。燈塔也做了相同選擇。他們共同準備一份關於合作與耐心的簡短聲明。校園裡，梅與凱也在贊助案公告上聯名簽署。兩個名字出現在同一行，兩人卻沒察覺自己的合作已在兩個地方互相呼應。梅聆聽一名學生看似簡單的問題。她記得自己學到的許多困難技巧，也都是從一個簡單問題開始。一天結束前，他們核對下一步與負責的人。這個簡單習慣避免未完成任務變成新爭執。

Kai would neither accept hidden advertising nor ignore useful support. Mei looked pale after the long regional match. A game character dressed as a priest offered a map clue. Moth solved the tower puzzle without entering the dangerous gate. Their relationship as teammates remained private during the qualifier. They paused before the next step and checked the same information from two directions. Neither wanted to build another plan on an answer that had never been tested.

凱既不接受隱藏廣告，也不忽視有用的資助。漫長區域賽後，梅臉色蒼白。一名扮成祭司的遊戲角色提供地圖線索。飛蛾沒有走進危險的門，也解開了高塔謎題。資格賽期間，他們的隊友關係仍保密。他們在下一步前停下，從兩個方向核對相同資料。誰都不想再把計畫建在未經測試的答案上。

A rude comment appeared in the public chat after their win. The skilled players chose the safer crossing when the path narrowed. An organizer directed winners toward the small interview stage. A waiter carried water into the venue after the late match. A wedding party passed the hotel while Mei studied the schedule. A practice result was never the whole story. The team saved both the successful move and the mistake that had made the next attempt necessary.

贏球後，公開聊天室出現一則無禮留言。熟練的玩家在路變窄時選擇較安全的通道。主辦人引導贏家前往小採訪舞臺。深夜賽事後，服務生把水送進會場。梅看賽程時，一支婚禮隊伍經過飯店。一次練習結果從來不是全部。隊伍保存成功的招式，也留下讓下一次嘗試變得必要的錯誤。
