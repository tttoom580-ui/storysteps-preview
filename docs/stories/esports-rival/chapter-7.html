午夜之後的訊息

A Message After Midnight

死對頭竟然是我的電競搭檔 · 第 7 / 25 章 · 1000–2000 · 英中對照 · 校訂中

用互動版查詞、朗讀與收藏

Mei discovered that Kai had missed a morning committee meeting. She first called him careless. Later she learned only that he had requested a private family day; the details were none of her business. She covered his equipment shift without demanding an explanation. When he returned, he thanked her and asked how to repay the hour fairly. Outside the building, people hurried to their next classes. Inside, the work moved more slowly, because every rule would affect someone who was not present.

梅發現凱缺席了早上的委員會。她一開始說他不負責任，後來只得知他申請了家庭事假；細節不該由她追問。她替他完成設備值班，沒有要求解釋。他回來後向她道謝，還問該如何公平補回那一小時。大樓外，人們趕著上下一堂課；裡面的工作卻慢得多，因為每條規則都會影響不在場的人。

Kai gave Mei the actual repair times rather than an estimate. Mei wrote the correct address on the repair delivery form. A volunteer offered advice about keeping student reports anonymous. Quiet Signal hoped to beat its last score without rushing. The cafeteria displayed old blue china cups behind the counter. It was a long day, and some notes looked dull at first. By evening they could see how those notes protected people who had not been in the room. At the end of the evening, they chose to ask one more question instead of making one more guess. It was slower, but it kept their work honest.

凱提供給梅實際維修時段，而非估算。梅在維修送貨單寫下正確地址。一名志工提供如何匿名保存學生回報的建議。靜默訊號希望不靠急躁就打敗上次成績。餐廳櫃台後展示著舊藍瓷杯。那是漫長的一天，有些筆記一開始看來很無聊。到了晚上，他們才看出這些筆記如何保護當時不在場的人。傍晚結束時，他們選擇多問一個問題，而不是多猜一次。這樣較慢，卻讓工作保持誠實。

In the game, Beacon told Moth he would be late because someone at home needed help. He did not offer names or a story. Moth remembered how bad it felt when people demanded reasons for every absence. She wrote, 'Take the time you need. We can change our training plan.' Together they moved the session without losing their place in the tournament. The room was noisy, but their notes were clear. They could return to those notes later instead of depending on memory or the loudest person.

遊戲裡，燈塔告訴飛蛾，因為家裡有人需要幫忙，他會晚到。他沒有提供姓名或故事。飛蛾想起被人逼問每次缺席原因的感受，打字說：「你需要多久就休息多久。我們可以改練習計畫。」兩人調整時段，沒有失去比賽資格。教室雖然吵，筆記卻很清楚。他們之後可以回頭核對，而不是只靠記憶或聲音最大的人。

An expert checked the security of the club's public forms. Mei created a form for reporting equipment trouble after hours. A camera lens caught the sunset across the university garden. Beacon remained a patient listener even when Moth was upset. At midnight, the team sent its final practice confirmation. Nobody could solve every problem in one evening. The fair choice was to leave clear instructions so that another person could continue the work safely.

一位專家檢查社團公開表單的安全性。梅製作下班後回報設備問題的表單。鏡頭拍下校園花園另一端的夕陽。即使飛蛾心情不好，燈塔仍耐心傾聽。午夜時，隊伍送出最後練習確認。沒有人能在一晚解決所有問題。公平的做法是留下清楚指示，讓另一個人能安全接手。

Kai returned to campus carrying a small bag of food. He and Mei shared it over a list of repairs, each keeping a respectful distance. She learned that he could listen when she spoke slowly. He learned that she checked details he would have missed. They were still rivals in meetings, but the argument had stopped being a reason to hurt each other. Mei listened to a student whose question sounded simple at first. She remembered that most difficult skills had begun with one simple question of her own. Before stopping for the day, they reviewed the next step and who would be responsible for it. That simple habit prevented an unfinished task from becoming a new argument.

凱帶著一小袋食物回到校園。他和梅一邊吃，一邊核對維修清單，兩人維持自在的距離。梅發現只要自己慢慢說，凱就願意聽；凱也發現梅會注意到他忽略的細節。開會時他們仍是對手，但爭論不再成為傷害對方的理由。梅聆聽一名學生看似簡單的問題。她記得自己學到的許多困難技巧，也都是從一個簡單問題開始。一天結束前，他們核對下一步與負責的人。這個簡單習慣避免未完成任務變成新爭執。

A naughty kitten stole food from the open club lunch bag. Mei touched the silver necklace her mother had given her. None of the volunteers demanded a reason for Kai's absence. The origin of the equipment mistake was an old label. Their conversation became pleasant once they stopped asking for blame. When the meeting ended, they left enough space for another student to add a note. Shared work felt different once it stopped belonging to the loudest voice.

一隻調皮小貓從打開的社團午餐袋偷食物。梅摸著母親送她的銀項鍊。沒有志工要求凱解釋缺席原因。設備錯誤的起源是一張舊標籤。當兩人不再追究責怪，談話變得愉快。會議結束時，他們留下空白供其他學生加註。當共同工作不再屬於聲音最大的人，感覺便不一樣了。

The game described a prisoner trapped in a moving stone tower. Mei asked Kai about his role in checking the evening computers. Kai helped sort the repair forms by date rather than by name. They shared warm toast over the equipment list after class. The whole club agreed to protect private family information. One student asked why the details mattered. Mei answered that large plans failed when people ignored small needs. Kai agreed, then asked them to test the idea instead of merely praising it.

遊戲描述一名被困在移動石塔裡的囚犯。梅問凱在晚間電腦檢查裡擔任什麼角色。凱幫忙按日期而非姓名整理維修表。下課後，他們一邊核對設備清單，一邊分享熱吐司。整個社團都同意保護私人家庭資訊。有學生問這些細節為何重要。梅回答，大計畫常因忽略小需求而失敗。凱同意，接著要大家實際測試，而不只稱讚點子。
