The Road Map

死對頭竟然是我的電競搭檔 · 第 8 / 25 章 · 1000–2000 · 英中對照 · 校訂中

The club built a public guide to game strategy for new players. Mei designed simple pictures; Kai checked that each instruction could actually be followed on older machines. They fought over the word easy, which made one beginner feel foolish. When they replaced it with first step, the students began to ask more questions. Their work no longer depended on which person had been right first. Mei kept reminding herself that an argument had two sides, even when she liked only one of them. She wanted a useful result, not an easy victory.

社團製作公開的新手遊戲攻略。梅設計簡單圖片，凱檢查每項操作在舊電腦上是否做得到。他們為「簡單」這個詞爭執，因為一名新手看了覺得自己很笨。把它改成「第一步」後，學生開始更願意提問。工作終於不再取決於誰先說對。梅一直提醒自己，爭執總有兩面，即使她只喜歡其中一面。她想要的是有用的結果，而不是輕鬆取勝。

The average beginner needed more time than the guide had allowed. A barbecue smell drifted from the student festival across the road. Mei remembered her childhood fear of making mistakes in public. Kai set down his chopsticks before explaining the new map. Beacon waited for Moth's command before using his shield. The next round would bring a different map and a new difficulty. They could not control that change, but they could control how carefully they prepared. Before stopping for the day, they reviewed the next step and who would be responsible for it. That simple habit prevented an unfinished task from becoming a new argument.

一般新手需要的時間比指南預留的更久。道路另一端學生節傳來烤肉香。梅想起童年害怕在眾人面前犯錯的心情。凱放下筷子才開始解釋新地圖。燈塔在使用護盾前等待飛蛾的指令。下一輪會有不同地圖與新困難。他們無法控制變化，卻能控制準備有多仔細。一天結束前，他們核對下一步與負責的人。這個簡單習慣避免未完成任務變成新爭執。

Quiet Signal studied a tournament map with three roads. The shortest road carried too much weight for Beacon's shield. The safest road took almost all their time. Moth invented a middle route that used two small pauses, and Beacon practiced each pause until the timing felt natural. They won a narrow round without using a surprise trick. Kai checked the time once more before answering. A quick reply might sound impressive, but a correct answer would still be useful when the next problem arrived.

靜默訊號研究有三條路的賽事地圖。最短的路對燈塔護盾而言負擔太重，最安全的路又幾乎耗盡時間。飛蛾設計了中間路線，途中停兩次；燈塔反覆練習直到時機自然。他們沒有靠突然出現的招數，只以微小差距贏得一輪。凱回答前又核對一次時間。快速答覆或許很厲害，但正確答案才能在下一個問題來臨時繼續有用。

The safe path could disappear when both towers lost energy. The players refused to call the difficult route a matter of fate. Moth took one careful step forward after the red warning faded. Kai gave a gentle answer when a beginner asked for help. The shield could not carry the full load across the shortest bridge. Their signals were short because the important part had been practiced earlier. Good timing came from patient work that the audience could not always see.

兩座塔失去能量時，安全道路便可能消失。玩家拒絕把困難路線歸咎於命運。紅色警告消失後，飛蛾小心往前踏一步。一名新手求助時，凱溫和地回答。護盾無法承受最短橋上的全部負荷。訊號很短，因為重要的部分早已練過。好的時機來自觀眾未必看見的耐心努力。

The organizers announced that future matches would remain online until the national final. Each registered player had already sent a legal identity form through a private service. Those records were locked away from other players and spectators. Moth liked the rule because she wanted her ability judged before her face. Beacon agreed. They promised not to search for each other's personal accounts. The tournament rewarded speed, but their team had learned to value patience as well. A missed second could be repaired; a careless choice could cost the whole match.

主辦方宣布之後的比賽都在線上進行，直到全國決賽。每位註冊選手都已透過私人管道提交合法身分資料，其他選手與觀眾無法查閱。飛蛾喜歡這規定，因為她希望別人先評價能力，再看她的臉。燈塔同意，兩人約定不搜尋對方的私人帳號。賽事獎勵速度，但他們的隊伍也學會重視耐心。錯過一秒還能補救，粗心決定卻可能賠掉整場。

Mei changed the meaning of easy to first step in the guide. A map signal was their main means of communication in ranked play. The reality of older computers changed their perfect training plan. The map included a servant who carried supplies between two towers. A broken book shelf blocked the back corner of the club room. They paused before the next step and checked the same information from two directions. Neither wanted to build another plan on an answer that had never been tested.

梅把指南裡「簡單」的意思改寫成「第一步」。排名賽時，地圖訊號是他們主要的溝通方式。舊電腦的現實限制改變了完美的練習計畫。地圖裡有位僕人負責在兩座塔間運送補給。一個壞書架擋住社團教室後方角落。他們在下一步前停下，從兩個方向核對相同資料。誰都不想再把計畫建在未經測試的答案上。

Kai checked the source of each number in the equipment report. Mei used a wooden stick to point at the unsafe floor mark. A small stone marked the left turn on the game road. Moth said she would accept whatever result came from honest play. The adviser asked whom the new guide was meant to help. A practice result was never the whole story. The team saved both the successful move and the mistake that had made the next attempt necessary.

凱核對設備報告裡每個數字的來源。梅用木棒指出地上不安全的標記。一塊小石頭標示遊戲道路的左轉處。飛蛾說只要公平比賽，無論結果如何她都接受。指導老師問這份新指南究竟要幫助誰。一次練習結果從來不是全部。隊伍保存成功的招式，也留下讓下一次嘗試變得必要的錯誤。
