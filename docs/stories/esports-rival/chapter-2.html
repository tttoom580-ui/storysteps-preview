The university club announced a small exhibition on online teamwork. Mei wanted a place where beginners could learn without being laughed at. Kai wanted every public computer to have clear maintenance hours. Their arguments filled the notice board, but neither would take down the other's notes. Their adviser, Professor Wu, asked them to bring one workable proposal instead of another complaint. The players did not always win, and they did not pretend that they did. A fair record helped them decide what to practice the following day.

大學社團宣布舉辦線上合作展。梅希望新手能在不被嘲笑的環境學習，凱則希望公用電腦有明確維護時段。他們的爭論貼滿公告板，卻都沒有撕掉對方的留言。社團指導老師吳教授要求他們提出可行方案，而不是再抱怨一次。玩家並非場場都贏，也不假裝自己會贏。公平紀錄能幫助他們決定隔天該練什麼。

Mei saw beauty in a plan that welcomed slower players. The adviser could cancel the exhibition if safety rules were ignored. A chain secured the storage room but did not block the exit. Kai compared their tower plan to a careful game of chess. The current game update had changed the river's speed. The next round would bring a different map and a new difficulty. They could not control that change, but they could control how carefully they prepared. The next test still mattered. A single good result showed what might work; another careful test would show whether the plan could be trusted again.

梅在願意接納慢速玩家的計畫裡看見美。若忽視安全規則，指導老師可以取消展覽。一道鐵鍊鎖住儲藏室，卻沒有擋住出口。凱把高塔計畫比作一盤謹慎的西洋棋。目前的遊戲更新改變了河流速度。下一輪會有不同地圖與新困難。他們無法控制變化，卻能控制準備有多仔細。下一次測試仍重要。一次好結果只表示方法可能可行；再一次仔細測試才能看出它是否值得持續信任。

Online, Moth and Beacon made a new team called Quiet Signal. They practiced on a map where energy could pass from tower to tower only when both players agreed on a path. Moth saw the distant route; Beacon could protect only one tower at a time. They lost their first trial because neither expected the river gate to close so early. Instead of blaming each other, they watched the replay. Even when the answer seemed obvious, Mei wanted to know how it had been found. A good explanation could be shared with students who joined later.

在線上，飛蛾與燈塔成立了「靜默訊號」隊。他們在一張地圖練習：能量只能在雙方同意路線後由塔傳到塔。飛蛾能看見遠方道路，燈塔一次只能保護一座塔。他們第一次試跑失敗，因為都沒料到河門那麼早關閉。兩人沒有互相責怪，而是一起重看錄影。即使答案似乎很明顯，梅仍想知道答案怎麼來的。好的解釋可以分享給後來加入的學生。

Mei built a clear display of the club's shared timetable. The team measured its growth by better decisions, not louder applause. Professor Wu offered to guide the students through their disagreement. Moth did not hunt for private information about her teammate. Their first loss taught them to save every match replay. Their signals were short because the important part had been practiced earlier. Good timing came from patient work that the audience could not always see.

梅製作清楚的展示板，列出社團共用時程。隊伍用更好的判斷衡量成長，而非歡呼聲大小。吳教授願意引導學生處理分歧。飛蛾沒有搜尋隊友的私人資料。第一次落敗教會他們保存每場回放。訊號很短，因為重要的部分早已練過。好的時機來自觀眾未必看見的耐心努力。

At midnight, Beacon typed, 'A strong team does not need to think alike.' Moth saved the line. The following day Kai said almost the opposite in public: a shared room needed shared rules. Mei heard it as an order. She did not know that the person who frustrated her in the daylight had just given her the most useful lesson of the night. Everyone had a different reason for joining the club. Mei tried to remember that the shared room belonged to those quiet students too, not just the confident ones. At the end of the evening, they chose to ask one more question instead of making one more guess. It was slower, but it kept their work honest.

午夜時，燈塔打字說：「好的隊伍不必想得一模一樣。」飛蛾存下這句話。隔天，凱卻在公開場合說了一句幾乎相反的話：共用教室需要共用規則。梅把它聽成命令。她不知道白天讓她惱火的人，正是昨夜給她最有用建議的隊友。每個人加入社團的理由都不同。梅努力記住，共用教室也屬於那些安靜的學生，而不只屬於有自信的人。傍晚結束時，他們選擇多問一個問題，而不是多猜一次。這樣較慢，卻讓工作保持誠實。

Club membership gave students access to the public training room. Beacon sent a short message before the next practice round. A pear rolled out of Mei's lunch bag onto the floor. Mei chose to respond with a question instead of an insult. Kai wanted his better self to hear the entire proposal before voting. They paused before the next step and checked the same information from two directions. Neither wanted to build another plan on an answer that had never been tested.

社團會員資格讓學生可以使用公共練習室。燈塔在下一輪練習前送出簡短訊息。一顆梨子從梅的午餐袋滾到地板上。梅選擇用提問回應，而不是辱罵。凱希望自己更好的一面能在投票前聽完完整提案。他們在下一步前停下，從兩個方向核對相同資料。誰都不想再把計畫建在未經測試的答案上。

Neither player wanted to sound selfish when asking for rest. A broken speaker made the school demonstration strangely quiet. The crowd called the last save super, but Beacon stayed calm. The first trial ended when the river gate closed too soon. Mei said their worst mistake would be refusing to learn. A practice result was never the whole story. The team saved both the successful move and the mistake that had made the next attempt necessary.

兩名玩家都不想在要求休息時顯得自私。壞掉的喇叭讓校園展示異常安靜。觀眾說最後一救超厲害，燈塔卻保持冷靜。第一次測試因河門太早關閉而失敗。梅說他們最糟的錯誤將是拒絕學習。一次練習結果從來不是全部。隊伍保存成功的招式，也留下讓下一次嘗試變得必要的錯誤。
