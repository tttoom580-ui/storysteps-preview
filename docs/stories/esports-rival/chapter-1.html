靠窗的座位

The Seat by the Window

死對頭竟然是我的電競搭檔 · 第 1 / 25 章 · 1000–2000 · 英中對照 · 校訂中

At Haiwen University, twenty-one-year-old Mei Lin arrived early to prepare a game-design project. The last computer beside the window was already reserved by Kai Chen, a twenty-two-year-old engineering student. He had left a note saying that the machine needed a safety check. Mei believed he simply wanted to control the room. Kai believed she ignored the equipment rules. Neither asked what the other had been trying to finish. On the game screen, a small change often decided the whole route. That was why the players practiced the ordinary moves as seriously as the exciting ones.

在海文大學，二十一歲的林梅提早來準備遊戲設計作業。靠窗的最後一台電腦已被二十二歲的工程系學生陳凱預約。他留了紙條，表示設備需要安全檢查。梅認為他只是想控制教室；凱則認為她忽略設備規則。兩人都沒有問對方究竟急著完成什麼。遊戲螢幕上，微小改變往往決定整條路。因此玩家對普通招式與刺激招式一樣認真練習。

The guest coach stroked his beard while reading the room schedule. A small cafe across campus offered cheap tea after late practice. The cleaning bottle carried a chemical warning beside the gaming computers. Mei said taking another student's password would be a serious crime. The club needed to divide its hours between design and engineering students. It was a long day, and some notes looked dull at first. By evening they could see how those notes protected people who had not been in the room. No one called the result perfect. They left the notes open for correction because trust was stronger when everyone could see how a choice had been made.

客座教練讀教室時程時摸了摸鬍子。校園對面的小咖啡館在深夜練習後提供便宜的茶。遊戲電腦旁的清潔瓶上貼著化學品警告。梅說偷拿別人的密碼是嚴重的犯罪行為。社團需要把時段分給設計系與工程系學生。那是漫長的一天，有些筆記一開始看來很無聊。到了晚上，他們才看出這些筆記如何保護當時不在場的人。沒有人稱結果完美。他們讓紀錄能被修正，因為當所有人看得見決策過程，信任才更牢固。

That evening, Mei opened the competitive game Skyline from her small room. There she was Moth, a quiet route planner. Her teammate Beacon protected her with careful shields and never asked for a photo or a real name. The game allowed only typed messages and map signals during ranked play. That rule had helped them become friends without explaining anything about their ordinary lives. Their adviser did not expect a perfect plan on the first try. What mattered was whether the students could explain their choices and correct them openly.

那晚，梅在小房間打開競技遊戲《天際線》。在遊戲裡，她是擅長規劃路線、沉默寡言的「飛蛾」。她的隊友「燈塔」總以精準護盾保護她，也從不索取照片或真名。排名賽只允許文字與地圖訊號溝通。這項規則讓他們不必談現實生活也能建立友誼。指導老師不期待計畫一次就完美，重要的是學生能否解釋自己的選擇，並公開修正。

Kai refused to ask for a favor when the schedule was unfair. Mei could not explain the sharp feeling behind her angry words. The gardener outside the window was planting flowers before sunrise. A wet leaf stuck to the handle of the classroom door. Mei wore soft pajamas during her late online matches at home. Nobody could solve every problem in one evening. The fair choice was to leave clear instructions so that another person could continue the work safely.

當時程不公平時，凱不願靠人情取得好處。梅無法解釋自己氣話背後那股強烈的感受。窗外的園丁天亮前就在種花。一片濕葉黏在教室門把上。梅在家深夜打線上比賽時穿著柔軟的睡衣。沒有人能在一晚解決所有問題。公平的做法是留下清楚指示，讓另一個人能安全接手。

A national doubles tournament appeared on the screen. Each player would submit a private identity check to the organizers, but opponents and even teammates could continue using game names until the in-person final. Moth asked Beacon if he wanted to enter. He answered with a single green signal. At school the next morning, Kai moved his bag away from the window, but Mei was already too angry to thank him. Kai was careful with details because mistakes could affect other students. He knew that being careful did not give him the right to speak over anyone else.

螢幕上出現全國雙人賽公告。參賽者須私下向主辦單位驗證身分，但對手乃至隊友都能一直使用遊戲名稱，直到實體決賽。飛蛾問燈塔想不想報名，他回了一個綠色訊號。隔天早上，凱把背包從窗邊移開，梅卻還在氣頭上，沒向他道謝。凱重視細節，因為錯誤可能影響其他學生。他也知道，謹慎不代表自己有權蓋過別人的聲音。

Beacon's gentle personality made difficult practice feel less frightening. The tournament rule was plain enough for every new player to read. Mei could relate to players who had no safe room for practice. A rubber cover protected the cable beside the practice desk. Such a simple map signal saved their team from a mistake. When the meeting ended, they left enough space for another student to add a note. Shared work felt different once it stopped belonging to the loudest voice.

燈塔溫和的個性讓困難練習沒那麼可怕。賽事規則寫得很清楚，新手都能讀懂。梅能理解沒有安全練習場所的玩家。練習桌旁的電線有橡膠保護套。這樣簡單的地圖訊號讓他們免於犯錯。會議結束時，他們留下空白供其他學生加註。當共同工作不再屬於聲音最大的人，感覺便不一樣了。

The club listed tax in the budget instead of hiding the cost. Kai checked the computer's temperature before allowing another match. A tiny green light showed that Moth's message had arrived. Mei sliced a watermelon for herself after the long practice. Kai used a scale to weigh the heavy equipment box. One student asked why the details mattered. Mei answered that large plans failed when people ignored small needs. Kai agreed, then asked them to test the idea instead of merely praising it.

社團把稅金列進預算，沒有藏起費用。凱確認電腦溫度後才准許繼續比賽。一盞小小的綠燈表示飛蛾的訊息已送達。長時間練習後，梅切了一顆西瓜來吃。凱用磅秤測量沉重設備箱的重量。有學生問這些細節為何重要。梅回答，大計畫常因忽略小需求而失敗。凱同意，接著要大家實際測試，而不只稱讚點子。
