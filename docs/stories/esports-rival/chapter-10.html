死對頭竟然是我的電競搭檔 · 第 10 / 25 章 · 1000–2000 · 英中對照 · 校訂中

A tournament rule change moved Quiet Signal into an extra qualifying round. The notice was brief, and many teams misunderstood it. Moth read every line, while Beacon checked the updated schedule against their practice time. They disagreed about whether to enter an optional warm-up match. In the end they chose rest and a short review over more hours of tired play. The players did not always win, and they did not pretend that they did. A fair record helped them decide what to practice the following day.

賽事規則變動，使靜默訊號必須參加額外資格賽。公告很短，許多隊伍誤解了它。飛蛾逐行閱讀，燈塔把新賽程與練習時段比對。兩人對是否參加可選的暖身賽意見不同，最後選擇休息與簡短複盤，而不是疲憊地多打幾小時。玩家並非場場都贏，也不假裝自己會贏。公平紀錄能幫助他們決定隔天該練什麼。

The club made a quiet practice room available to new players. Mei took one biscuit from the shared snack box before practice. The team received congratulations after its narrow qualifying win. Beacon said courage could mean resting instead of playing another round. A red fox appeared beside the next tower on the game map. It was a long day, and some notes looked dull at first. By evening they could see how those notes protected people who had not been in the room. The next test still mattered. A single good result showed what might work; another careful test would show whether the plan could be trusted again.

社團提供安靜練習室給新手使用。練習前，梅從共用點心盒拿了一片餅乾。險勝資格賽後，隊伍收到祝賀。燈塔說勇氣也可能是休息，而非再打一輪。遊戲地圖下一座塔旁出現一隻紅狐狸。那是漫長的一天，有些筆記一開始看來很無聊。到了晚上，他們才看出這些筆記如何保護當時不在場的人。下一次測試仍重要。一次好結果只表示方法可能可行；再一次仔細測試才能看出它是否值得持續信任。

At school, Mei and Kai helped new club members repair the practice guide. A student had mixed up two symbols, making a safe route look dangerous. Mei redrew the pictures. Kai tested the controls with a damaged keyboard. They agreed to keep both the original mistake and the corrected note in the archive, so others could learn without repeating the same problem. Even when the answer seemed obvious, Mei wanted to know how it had been found. A good explanation could be shared with students who joined later.

在學校，梅和凱協助新社員修正練習指南。一名學生把兩個符號弄反，讓安全路線看來很危險。梅重畫圖片，凱用損壞的鍵盤測試操作。他們決定保留原錯誤與修正說明，讓別人能學習而不重蹈覆轍。即使答案似乎很明顯，梅仍想知道答案怎麼來的。好的解釋可以分享給後來加入的學生。

The volunteers would gather the broken keyboards for repair. Mei used glue to repair a torn paper guide. A cartoon goose on the wall pointed toward the student exit. Their gradual improvement mattered more than a perfect single match. Kai refused to ignore the broken key simply because it sometimes worked. Nobody could solve every problem in one evening. The fair choice was to leave clear instructions so that another person could continue the work safely.

志工將收集壞鍵盤送修。梅用膠水修補撕裂的紙本指南。牆上的卡通鵝指向學生出口。他們逐步進步，比單場完美比賽更重要。凱不願因按鍵偶爾能用，就忽視故障。沒有人能在一晚解決所有問題。公平的做法是留下清楚指示，讓另一個人能安全接手。

Quiet Signal survived the extra round by one point. Beacon sent no victory speech, only the replay time where Moth had trusted him with a difficult turn. She watched it twice. In the next campus meeting, she invited Kai to speak before offering her own plan. He noticed the change and returned the courtesy. Neither understood why their separate worlds now seemed a little less lonely. Everyone had a different reason for joining the club. Mei tried to remember that the shared room belonged to those quiet students too, not just the confident ones. At the end of the evening, they chose to ask one more question instead of making one more guess. It was slower, but it kept their work honest.

靜默訊號以一分之差通過加賽。燈塔沒發表勝利感言，只送出一個回放時間點：飛蛾在困難轉彎時選擇相信他。她看了兩遍。下一次校園會議，她先請凱發言，才提出自己的方案。凱注意到這個變化，也以同樣方式回應。兩人都不明白，為什麼各自的世界似乎沒那麼孤單了。每個人加入社團的理由都不同。梅努力記住，共用教室也屬於那些安靜的學生，而不只屬於有自信的人。傍晚結束時，他們選擇多問一個問題，而不是多猜一次。這樣較慢，卻讓工作保持誠實。

Mei checked the length of the cable before moving the monitor. A meter on the screen showed the remaining shield energy. A musical signal played when a tower reached full power. Each player had two minutes per turn in the practice drill. Kai insisted on proper labels for every repaired computer. When the meeting ended, they left enough space for another student to add a note. Shared work felt different once it stopped belonging to the loudest voice.

移動螢幕前，梅確認電線長度。螢幕上的計量表顯示剩餘護盾能量。高塔充滿能量時會響起音樂訊號。演練時每位玩家每回合有兩分鐘。凱堅持每台修好的電腦都要貼妥標籤。會議結束時，他們留下空白供其他學生加註。當共同工作不再屬於聲音最大的人，感覺便不一樣了。

A snail on the rainy window moved more slowly than the clock. Moth liked Beacon's calm style when the match became difficult. The storage unit kept extra headphones for silent-play students. Mei planned a short vacation after the tournament ended. The cafeteria served baked yam with tea during the evening meeting. One student asked why the details mattered. Mei answered that large plans failed when people ignored small needs. Kai agreed, then asked them to test the idea instead of merely praising it.

雨窗上的蝸牛爬得比時鐘走得還慢。比賽變難時，飛蛾很喜歡燈塔冷靜的風格。儲物櫃替無語音玩家保存備用耳機。梅打算在比賽結束後安排短假。餐廳在晚間會議提供烤地瓜和茶。有學生問這些細節為何重要。梅回答，大計畫常因忽略小需求而失敗。凱同意，接著要大家實際測試，而不只稱讚點子。
