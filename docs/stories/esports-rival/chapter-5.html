The Practice Log

死對頭竟然是我的電競搭檔 · 第 5 / 25 章 · 1000–2000 · 英中對照 · 校訂中

用互動版查詞、朗讀與收藏

The new evening practice began with trouble. A reservation disappeared, a keyboard stuck, and an angry student blamed the volunteers. Mei created a public log so people could see changes without exposing private information. Kai tested it on an old computer and pointed out two problems. She expected mockery, but he offered to fix the first one if she checked the second. On the game screen, a small change often decided the whole route. That was why the players practiced the ordinary moves as seriously as the exciting ones.

新的晚間練習時段一開始就遇上麻煩：預約消失、鍵盤卡住，一名生氣的學生責怪志工。梅建立公開紀錄，讓大家能看見異動，又不揭露私人資料。凱用舊電腦測試，指出兩個問題。她以為會被嘲笑，他卻提議自己修第一個，讓她處理第二個。遊戲螢幕上，微小改變往往決定整條路。因此玩家對普通招式與刺激招式一樣認真練習。

Mei had no appetite after the reservation system failed again. The angry student wanted attention, but the volunteers needed facts. Kai called the new public log a brilliant practical idea. Their conflict changed when both agreed to test the program. A weak internet connection delayed the club's evening session. The next round would bring a different map and a new difficulty. They could not control that change, but they could control how carefully they prepared. No one called the result perfect. They left the notes open for correction because trust was stronger when everyone could see how a choice had been made.

預約系統再次故障後，梅完全沒有胃口。生氣的學生想引起注意，志工卻需要事實。凱稱新的公開紀錄簿是絕妙又實用的點子。當兩人同意一起測試程式，衝突便出現轉變。不穩定的網路連線延誤了社團晚間活動。下一輪會有不同地圖與新困難。他們無法控制變化，卻能控制準備有多仔細。沒有人稱結果完美。他們讓紀錄能被修正，因為當所有人看得見決策過程，信任才更牢固。

Quiet Signal entered its first online qualifier. The map showed a busy town of moving platforms. If Beacon used his shield too soon, Moth would lose her route. If she waited too long, he would be unable to protect the final bridge. They chose a slower path and finished with only one second left. Neither called the win easy; they reviewed the moments that had nearly failed. Their adviser did not expect a perfect plan on the first try. What mattered was whether the students could explain their choices and correct them openly.

靜默訊號參加第一場線上資格賽。地圖是由移動平台組成的繁忙小鎮。燈塔若太早用護盾，飛蛾便會失去路線；她若等太久，他就保不住最後一座橋。他們選了較慢的道路，以僅剩一秒的差距過關。兩人都沒說勝利很輕鬆，而是回看那些差點失敗的瞬間。指導老師不期待計畫一次就完美，重要的是學生能否解釋自己的選擇，並公開修正。

Mei hoped to discover why one reservation kept disappearing. The school offered domestic travel support for qualified national players. An electrical problem kept one practice screen dark for an hour. The team crossed a mass of moving blocks in the busy map. Beacon's method required one shield and two carefully timed pauses. Their signals were short because the important part had been practiced earlier. Good timing came from patient work that the audience could not always see.

梅希望查出為何有筆預約一直消失。學校提供符合資格的全國賽選手國內交通補助。一項電力故障讓練習螢幕黑了一小時。隊伍穿過繁忙地圖裡一大堆移動方塊。燈塔的方法需要一個護盾和兩次精準停頓。訊號很短，因為重要的部分早已練過。好的時機來自觀眾未必看見的耐心努力。

After the match Moth wrote that someone at school had helped her with a difficult problem. Beacon replied that he, too, had finally understood a person he often argued with. Their messages arrived almost together. Neither mentioned a name. Both felt the quiet comfort of being understood by a teammate who seemed far away. Kai was careful with details because mistakes could affect other students. He knew that being careful did not give him the right to speak over anyone else. The next test still mattered. A single good result showed what might work; another careful test would show whether the plan could be trusted again.

賽後飛蛾說，學校裡有個人幫她解決了難題。燈塔回答，他也終於稍微理解了那個常和自己爭論的人。兩則訊息幾乎同時送出。誰都沒說名字。兩人都感到安心，因為似乎身在遠方的隊友理解了自己。凱重視細節，因為錯誤可能影響其他學生。他也知道，謹慎不代表自己有權蓋過別人的聲音。下一次測試仍重要。一次好結果只表示方法可能可行；再一次仔細測試才能看出它是否值得持續信任。

Mei ate a bowl of noodle soup after their narrow victory. The referee gave praise for their safe crossing rather than speed. A rooster painted on the map marked the next checkpoint. Cold rain touched the skin on Mei's hands outside the lab. A snowy mountain appeared in the game lobby's new background. They paused before the next step and checked the same information from two directions. Neither wanted to build another plan on an answer that had never been tested.

險勝後，梅吃了一碗麵湯。裁判稱讚的是安全通過，而不是速度。地圖上畫著公雞，標示下一個檢查點。實驗室外，冰雨打在梅手上的皮膚。遊戲大廳的新背景出現一座雪山。他們在下一步前停下，從兩個方向核對相同資料。誰都不想再把計畫建在未經測試的答案上。

Mei helped spread the corrected reservation notice to other students. Kai offered support when the damaged keyboard refused to work. A loud thunder sound came from the map's storm effect. Moth asked Beacon to signal whenever the shield needed more time. A wool scarf hung from the chair beside Mei's desk. A practice result was never the whole story. The team saved both the successful move and the mistake that had made the next attempt necessary.

梅協助把修正後的預約公告傳給其他學生。鍵盤壞到不能用時，凱提供協助。地圖的暴風特效傳來響亮雷聲。飛蛾要燈塔每當護盾需要更多時間就發訊號。一條羊毛圍巾掛在梅桌旁的椅背上。一次練習結果從來不是全部。隊伍保存成功的招式，也留下讓下一次嘗試變得必要的錯誤。
