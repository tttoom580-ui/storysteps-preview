The Hidden Page

死對頭竟然是我的電競搭檔 · 第 12 / 25 章 · 1000–2000 · 英中對照 · 校訂中

用互動版查詞、朗讀與收藏

During a cleaning day, Mei found a loose page from the club's accessibility guide. It explained how players who could not use voice chat might still take part in team matches. Someone had placed it behind an old cupboard, not destroyed it. Kai helped her find the original version, and together they restored it to the public handbook. Mei kept reminding herself that an argument had two sides, even when she liked only one of them. She wanted a useful result, not an easy victory.

大掃除時，梅發現社團無障礙指南散落的一頁。那頁解釋了不能使用語音聊天的玩家如何參加團隊賽。有人把它放在舊櫥櫃後方，並非蓄意銷毀。凱協助她找到原始版本，兩人把那頁恢復到公開手冊裡。梅一直提醒自己，爭執總有兩面，即使她只喜歡其中一面。她想要的是有用的結果，而不是輕鬆取勝。

Kai twisted his ankle while moving a chair and stopped immediately. A dog gave a loud bark outside the old cupboard during cleaning. One click on the public guide opened the missing page. The cultural center offered a quiet hall for club training. A short delay gave the players time to check the route. They put the small facts beside the larger problem and checked what each one really meant. The work took time, but a careful record was easier to trust than a rumor passed from person to person. Before stopping for the day, they reviewed the next step and who would be responsible for it. That simple habit prevented an unfinished task from becoming a new argument.

凱搬椅子時扭到腳踝，立刻停下。大掃除時，舊櫥櫃外有狗大聲吠叫了一聲。在公開指南點一下，就能打開失落的一頁。文化中心提供安靜的大廳給社團訓練。短暫延誤讓玩家有時間核對路線。他們把小事實放在大問題旁，確認每件事真正代表什麼。工作花時間，但仔細紀錄比人傳人的傳聞更值得信任。一天結束前，他們核對下一步與負責的人。這個簡單習慣避免未完成任務變成新爭執。

Online, Moth and Beacon used only the map signals they had always known. A new opponent sent rude messages about their silence. Instead of replying with an insult, Moth asked the referee to apply the tournament rule. Beacon stayed focused on the map. They learned that a calm report could protect a team better than a clever argument. Kai checked the time once more before answering. A quick reply might sound impressive, but a correct answer would still be useful when the next problem arrived.

在線上，飛蛾與燈塔仍只使用熟悉的地圖訊號。新對手對他們的沉默發出無禮訊息。飛蛾沒有回罵，而是請裁判依賽事規則處理；燈塔繼續專注地圖。他們明白冷靜的申訴有時比漂亮的爭辯更能保護隊伍。凱回答前又核對一次時間。快速答覆或許很厲害，但正確答案才能在下一個問題來臨時繼續有用。

Mei added a clear description of the silent-play signals. The rude player called their careful rule evil without understanding it. The report gave an exact time for each warning message. Mei bought a guava from the small fruit stall outside school. A science-fiction novel rested under the old club handbook. The game rewarded the team for reaching the goal together. It gave no extra points for arguing, and that made the correct next signal more important than pride.

梅補上無語音訊號的清楚描述。無禮玩家還沒理解，就把謹慎的規則稱為邪惡。報告列出每則警告訊息的確切時間。梅在校外小水果攤買了一顆芭樂。一本科幻小說壓在舊社團手冊下面。遊戲獎勵的是隊伍一起達標，不會因為爭吵加分。這讓下一個正確訊號變得比自尊重要。

Mei wrote a short explanation of why silent play mattered. Kai checked that the guide gave players several choices rather than a single required method. When they finished, they were sitting closer than before, though neither had planned it. Mei moved her chair back a little to make room; Kai smiled and asked whether the next page needed another review. The tournament rewarded speed, but their team had learned to value patience as well. A missed second could be repaired; a careless choice could cost the whole match. No one called the result perfect. They left the notes open for correction because trust was stronger when everyone could see how a choice had been made.

梅寫下一段說明，解釋為什麼無語音玩法很重要。凱確認指南提供多種選擇，而非強迫大家使用同一方法。完成時，兩人的椅子不知不覺靠得比以前近。梅稍微把椅子往後移，留出空間；凱笑著問下一頁是否也需要再檢查。賽事獎勵速度，但他們的隊伍也學會重視耐心。錯過一秒還能補救，粗心決定卻可能賠掉整場。沒有人稱結果完美。他們讓紀錄能被修正，因為當所有人看得見決策過程，信任才更牢固。

Kai wanted a protective cover for the new public tablet. Moth sent no angry reply to the rude opponent. Rain tapped the roof while they restored the missing page. The adviser asked the club to select two guide testers. A spoon fell from Mei's lunch box during the meeting. Mei wrote down one question for the next meeting, then crossed out a sentence that sounded too certain. She wanted the record to show what they actually knew.

凱希望新公用平板有保護套。飛蛾沒有回覆那個無禮對手的怒氣訊息。他們補回失落書頁時，雨點敲著屋頂。指導老師要社團選出兩名指南測試者。會議中，一支湯匙從梅的便當盒掉出來。梅為下次會議記下一個問題，又刪去一句聽起來過於肯定的話。她希望紀錄只呈現他們真正知道的事情。

The door began to swing when the evening wind entered. Mei hoped to travel to the final but kept her identity private. Upon reading the old guide, Kai noticed a missing safety step. Mei felt upset when students laughed at players who typed slowly. The project invited youth teams to test the new silent-play signs. There was no prize for sounding certain when the answer was unknown. They wrote a question mark in the notes and promised to return after another test.

晚風吹進來時，門開始擺動。梅希望前往決賽，但仍保密身分。讀完舊指南，凱注意到缺少一項安全步驟。有人嘲笑打字較慢的玩家，梅很難過。專題邀請青年隊伍測試新的無語音標誌。不知道答案時，裝得很肯定也不會有獎品。他們在筆記留下問號，答應再測試後回來確認。
