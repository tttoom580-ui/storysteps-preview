教室的規則

Rules of the Room

死對頭竟然是我的電競搭檔 · 第 4 / 25 章 · 1000–2000 · 英中對照 · 校訂中

用互動版查詞、朗讀與收藏

The student council held an open meeting about the shared practice room. Some students wanted longer hours; others were worried about broken cables and crowded aisles. Mei spoke for students who could practice only after work. Kai brought repair records showing why one machine had to be closed. Both arguments were reasonable, but their tone made cooperation look impossible. Mei kept reminding herself that an argument had two sides, even when she liked only one of them. She wanted a useful result, not an easy victory.

學生會針對共用練習室召開公開會議。有些學生希望延長時間，有些人擔心電線損壞與走道擁擠。梅替打工後才能練習的同學發聲；凱拿出維修紀錄，說明某台電腦為何必須停用。雙方都有道理，語氣卻讓合作看似不可能。梅一直提醒自己，爭執總有兩面，即使她只喜歡其中一面。她想要的是有用的結果，而不是輕鬆取勝。

Every student could attend the council meeting without joining the club. A wire cage protected the old cooling fan near the door. Kai used a comb to fix his untidy hair before speaking. The engineering department sent a clear list of broken equipment. A poster warned students not to leave any drug near shared desks. It was a long day, and some notes looked dull at first. By evening they could see how those notes protected people who had not been in the room. Before stopping for the day, they reviewed the next step and who would be responsible for it. That simple habit prevented an unfinished task from becoming a new argument.

每位學生都能參加學生會會議，不必加入社團。門邊舊散熱扇外有鐵網罩。凱發言前用梳子整理亂髮。工程系送來清楚的損壞設備清單。海報提醒學生不要把任何藥品留在共用桌上。那是漫長的一天，有些筆記一開始看來很無聊。到了晚上，他們才看出這些筆記如何保護當時不在場的人。一天結束前，他們核對下一步與負責的人。這個簡單習慣避免未完成任務變成新爭執。

That night, Quiet Signal met an enemy in Skyline that could copy a player's last movement. Moth attempted to outrun the copy. Beacon signaled that they should stop moving for one second, then turn together. The strange method worked. Moth laughed at herself for rejecting the plan before she understood it. They wrote a clear rule for the next match: ask before assuming. Kai checked the time once more before answering. A quick reply might sound impressive, but a correct answer would still be useful when the next problem arrived.

那晚，靜默訊號在《天際線》遇到能複製玩家上一步動作的敵人。飛蛾試著跑贏複製體，燈塔則示意先靜止一秒，再一起轉向。奇怪的方法竟然奏效。飛蛾笑自己還沒理解就否定提案。他們為下一場寫下明確規則：先問，再做假設。凱回答前又核對一次時間。快速答覆或許很厲害，但正確答案才能在下一個問題來臨時繼續有用。

The eastern window brought warm light into the meeting room. Mei tried to encourage a nervous student to speak for herself. Kai kept a firm hold on the safety rules during the debate. The cafeteria cook began to fry eggs before the evening session. Students waited in the gym for a larger meeting space. Nobody could solve every problem in one evening. The fair choice was to leave clear instructions so that another person could continue the work safely.

東側窗戶讓溫暖陽光灑進會議室。梅試著鼓勵緊張的學生為自己發言。辯論時，凱仍堅守安全規則。晚間時段前，餐廳廚師開始煎蛋。學生在體育館等待較大的會議場地。沒有人能在一晚解決所有問題。公平的做法是留下清楚指示，讓另一個人能安全接手。

Mei returned to the council with a new suggestion: keep the safety closure, but open a supervised evening period on working machines. Kai accepted it and offered to train volunteers. Neither used the word friendship. When the meeting ended, however, they stayed behind to move chairs together. It was their first useful conversation without an audience. The tournament rewarded speed, but their team had learned to value patience as well. A missed second could be repaired; a careless choice could cost the whole match. No one called the result perfect. They left the notes open for correction because trust was stronger when everyone could see how a choice had been made.

梅帶著新提案回到學生會：危險設備繼續關閉，但其他能使用的電腦開放有人值班的晚間時段。凱接受提案，並願意訓練志工。兩人仍不會說彼此是朋友。可是會議結束後，他們留下來一起搬椅子。那是第一次沒有旁觀者、卻有實際成果的對話。賽事獎勵速度，但他們的隊伍也學會重視耐心。錯過一秒還能補救，粗心決定卻可能賠掉整場。沒有人稱結果完美。他們讓紀錄能被修正，因為當所有人看得見決策過程，信任才更牢固。

Their first disagreement became minor when the broken cable was found. A hand motion from Moth told Beacon when to stop. A warm pan rested behind the cafeteria service window. The club promised to keep each player's private account information safe. Mei explained the purpose of her proposal without attacking Kai. When the meeting ended, they left enough space for another student to add a note. Shared work felt different once it stopped belonging to the loudest voice.

找到斷線後，他們最初的爭執變成小事。飛蛾的一個手勢訊號告訴燈塔何時停下。一只熱平底鍋放在餐廳服務窗口後。社團承諾保護每位玩家的私人帳號資訊。梅說明提案目的，沒有攻擊凱。會議結束時，他們留下空白供其他學生加註。當共同工作不再屬於聲音最大的人，感覺便不一樣了。

Players came from every region of the university campus. The standard safety check applied to every public computer. An umbrella leaned beside the wet entrance to the building. In Skyline, a mechanical wing opened above the river gate. A gray wolf appeared on the game map as a harmless guide. One student asked why the details mattered. Mei answered that large plans failed when people ignored small needs. Kai agreed, then asked them to test the idea instead of merely praising it.

玩家來自校園各個區域。標準安全檢查適用於每一台公用電腦。一把雨傘靠在大樓濕漉漉的入口旁。《天際線》裡，河門上方展開一片機械翅膀。遊戲地圖裡出現一隻灰狼，作為無害的嚮導。有學生問這些細節為何重要。梅回答，大計畫常因忽略小需求而失敗。凱同意，接著要大家實際測試，而不只稱讚點子。
