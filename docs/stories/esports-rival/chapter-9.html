One Missing Frame

死對頭竟然是我的電競搭檔 · 第 9 / 25 章 · 1000–2000 · 英中對照 · 校訂中

用互動版查詞、朗讀與收藏

A short clip from Mei's demonstration spread through the school. Someone had removed the part where she warned students not to copy an unsafe move. Kai found the complete recording in the club archive. He asked Mei before releasing anything, because the video included private faces. Together they published only the necessary frames and an explanation of the change. On the game screen, a small change often decided the whole route. That was why the players practiced the ordinary moves as seriously as the exciting ones.

一段梅示範操作的短影片在校園流傳。有人剪掉她提醒學生不要模仿危險動作的部分。凱在社團檔案裡找到完整錄影。由於影片拍到私人面孔，他在公開前先徵詢梅的意見。兩人只發布必要畫格與剪輯說明。遊戲螢幕上，微小改變往往決定整條路。因此玩家對普通招式與刺激招式一樣認真練習。

In addition to the clip, the club saved its original project notes. Mei wrote an article about safe ways to learn team games. The final chapter of the guide explained how to report mistakes. A student used a red crayon to mark the dangerous corner. Kai added a daily check to the room's equipment log. They put the small facts beside the larger problem and checked what each one really meant. The work took time, but a careful record was easier to trust than a rumor passed from person to person. No one called the result perfect. They left the notes open for correction because trust was stronger when everyone could see how a choice had been made.

除了短片，社團也保存了原始專題筆記。梅寫了一篇教人安全學習團隊遊戲的文章。指南最後一章說明如何回報錯誤。一名學生用紅色蠟筆標出危險轉角。凱在設備紀錄增加每日檢查欄。他們把小事實放在大問題旁，確認每件事真正代表什麼。工作花時間，但仔細紀錄比人傳人的傳聞更值得信任。沒有人稱結果完美。他們讓紀錄能被修正，因為當所有人看得見決策過程，信任才更牢固。

Meanwhile, Quiet Signal faced a team that replayed Moth's movements after every match. Beacon believed the opponents had simply studied the public data. Moth was angry but did not accuse them of stealing. She changed one route openly and let the result speak. Their success came from practice and trust, not from hiding a new rule in the game. Their adviser did not expect a perfect plan on the first try. What mattered was whether the students could explain their choices and correct them openly.

同時，靜默訊號遇到一隊會重複飛蛾走位的對手。燈塔認為對方只是研究公開資料。飛蛾很生氣，卻沒有指控對手偷竊。她公開改變一條路線，讓結果說話。他們靠練習與信任獲勝，不靠遊戲裡突然冒出的新規則。指導老師不期待計畫一次就完美，重要的是學生能否解釋自己的選擇，並公開修正。

Mei's fear rose when the edited clip spread across campus. An old historical poster hung beside the game-design classroom. They checked whether sharing the video would be legal under club rules. A loose nail on the notice board had scratched a paper sign. Mei moved a painting aside to read the full project notice. The game rewarded the team for reaching the goal together. It gave no extra points for arguing, and that made the correct next signal more important than pride.

剪輯影片在校園傳開時，梅感到害怕。遊戲設計教室旁掛著一張舊歷史海報。他們確認依社團規定分享影片是否合法。公告板上一枚鬆動的釘子刮破了紙牌。梅移開一幅畫，閱讀完整的專題公告。遊戲獎勵的是隊伍一起達標，不會因為爭吵加分。這讓下一個正確訊號變得比自尊重要。

After the clip was corrected, Mei thanked Kai outside the empty room. He looked surprised that she knew how hard it had been to protect the students in the video. She admitted that she had often judged his tone before hearing his reasons. They did not suddenly become close. But on her way home, she stopped referring to him as the enemy. Kai was careful with details because mistakes could affect other students. He knew that being careful did not give him the right to speak over anyone else.

影片更正後，梅在空教室外向凱道謝。凱很驚訝她明白保護影片中學生的隱私有多難。她承認自己過去常常只聽他的語氣，沒聽理由。他們並沒有突然變親密。但回家路上，她不再把他稱作敵人。凱重視細節，因為錯誤可能影響其他學生。他也知道，謹慎不代表自己有權蓋過別人的聲音。

Kai became a fair partner in fixing the damaged public report. The volunteers used paste to place the corrected notices on cardboard. A lost puppy waited quietly beside the campus security office. Mei highlighted the section that had been removed from the clip. Morning light began to shine through the empty practice room. Mei wrote down one question for the next meeting, then crossed out a sentence that sounded too certain. She wanted the record to show what they actually knew.

凱成為修正受損公開報告時公平的合作夥伴。志工用漿糊把更正公告貼在厚紙板上。一隻走失的小狗安靜地待在校園警衛室旁。梅標出影片中被刪除的段落。晨光開始照進空蕩蕩的練習室。梅為下次會議記下一個問題，又刪去一句聽起來過於肯定的話。她希望紀錄只呈現他們真正知道的事情。

The shopkeeper nearby lent the students a box for their posters. The guide would succeed only if beginners could follow each step. Kai asked who had changed the system log before the alarm. They would release no video unless the students had given permission. A false story could be worse than an honest mistake. There was no prize for sounding certain when the answer was unknown. They wrote a question mark in the notes and promised to return after another test.

附近店主借學生一個裝海報的箱子。只有新手能照步驟操作，指南才算成功。凱詢問警報前是誰修改了系統紀錄。沒有得到學生同意，他們就不會公開影片。虛假的故事可能比誠實的錯誤更糟。不知道答案時，裝得很肯定也不會有獎品。他們在筆記留下問號，答應再測試後回來確認。
