第一場假警報

The First False Alarm

死對頭竟然是我的電競搭檔 · 第 3 / 25 章 · 1000–2000 · 英中對照 · 校訂中

用互動版查詞、朗讀與收藏

A rumor spread that someone had used a forbidden program in the student practice room. Mei's project file was named in the complaint because it ran shortly before the alarm. Kai, who managed the equipment logs, was asked to investigate. Mei thought he would enjoy proving her wrong. Instead, he marked the times and refused to call anyone guilty until he had checked the evidence. Outside the building, people hurried to their next classes. Inside, the work moved more slowly, because every rule would affect someone who was not present.

校園流傳有人在練習室使用違規程式。因為警報前剛執行過梅的作業檔，申訴書便指名她。管理設備紀錄的凱受託調查。梅以為他一定很樂意證明她有錯；他卻先標註時間，堅持查完證據才判斷誰有責任。大樓外，人們趕著上下一堂課；裡面的工作卻慢得多，因為每條規則都會影響不在場的人。

Mei made an attempt to explain the alarm before people blamed her. A broken driver was the cause of the strange computer noise. She ate cereal while reading the first complaint at breakfast. The referee found no evidence that Quiet Signal had tried to cheat. A classmate asked Kai whether he really believed Mei was guilty. They put the small facts beside the larger problem and checked what each one really meant. The work took time, but a careful record was easier to trust than a rumor passed from person to person. At the end of the evening, they chose to ask one more question instead of making one more guess. It was slower, but it kept their work honest.

梅試著在眾人責怪她之前解釋警報。故障的驅動程式才是電腦怪聲的原因。她早餐吃著麥片，同時閱讀第一份申訴。裁判沒有發現靜默訊號企圖作弊的證據。一名同學問凱是否真的認為梅有罪。他們把小事實放在大問題旁，確認每件事真正代表什麼。工作花時間，但仔細紀錄比人傳人的傳聞更值得信任。傍晚結束時，他們選擇多問一個問題，而不是多猜一次。這樣較慢，卻讓工作保持誠實。

In Skyline, a warning also appeared. Quiet Signal had crossed a checkpoint faster than the system expected. Moth wanted to rush into another match to prove their skill. Beacon suggested saving the replay and asking a referee to review the route. The warning turned out to be a timing error in a public test map. Moth sent a brief apology for losing her temper. The room was noisy, but their notes were clear. They could return to those notes later instead of depending on memory or the loudest person.

《天際線》也跳出一則警告：靜默訊號穿越檢查點的速度比系統預期快。飛蛾想立刻再打一場證明實力，燈塔則建議先保存錄影，請裁判審查路線。結果只是公開測試地圖的計時錯誤。飛蛾為自己發脾氣簡短道歉。教室雖然吵，筆記卻很清楚。他們之後可以回頭核對，而不是只靠記憶或聲音最大的人。

The sky turned cloudy as the review meeting began. Kai asked everyone to continue working until the evidence was checked. Mei's silver earring caught the light when she turned toward the screen. Students waited beside the entrance instead of blocking the hallway. The final report named the old driver as the technical problem. The game rewarded the team for reaching the goal together. It gave no extra points for arguing, and that made the correct next signal more important than pride.

審查會開始時，天空變得多雲。凱要大家先繼續工作，等證據查清。梅轉向螢幕時，銀色耳環閃了一下。學生在入口旁等待，沒有堵住走廊。最後報告指出技術問題來自舊驅動程式。遊戲獎勵的是隊伍一起達標，不會因為爭吵加分。這讓下一個正確訊號變得比自尊重要。

The campus review reached a similar answer. An old driver, not Mei's project, had caused the alarm. Kai handed her the report without claiming credit. Mei noticed the absence of blame, yet she was embarrassed to say thank you. At home, she told Beacon that she disliked a certain person who always sounded correct. Beacon wrote, 'Perhaps correct is not the same as kind. But perhaps he is trying.' Mei listened to a student whose question sounded simple at first. She remembered that most difficult skills had begun with one simple question of her own.

校園調查也得到類似結論：造成警報的是舊驅動程式，而不是梅的作業。凱把報告交給她，沒有搶功。梅注意到他沒有指責自己，卻不好意思說謝謝。回家後，她告訴燈塔，自己討厭一個總是說得很有道理的人。燈塔回覆：「正確不一定等於體貼，但他也許正在努力。」梅聆聽一名學生看似簡單的問題。她記得自己學到的許多困難技巧，也都是從一個簡單問題開始。

No one could force Mei to accept an apology she had not heard. The adviser gave a general warning about judging too quickly. A government safety notice hung near the public computer room. The club had a liberal policy that welcomed beginners from other departments. Kai sent Mei a link to the complete equipment log. Mei wrote down one question for the next meeting, then crossed out a sentence that sounded too certain. She wanted the record to show what they actually knew.

沒有人能強迫梅接受她尚未聽見的道歉。指導老師提醒大家，不要太快下定論。公用電腦教室旁貼著政府安全公告。社團採取開放政策，歡迎其他系的新手。凱傳給梅完整設備紀錄的連結。梅為下次會議記下一個問題，又刪去一句聽起來過於肯定的話。她希望紀錄只呈現他們真正知道的事情。

A small nod from Mei showed she had read the report. Only trained volunteers could operate the damaged test machine. A runner arrived with the missing copy of the inspection sheet. Outside, someone's scooter rang its horn near the front gate. The lab would supply replacement cables before the next meeting. There was no prize for sounding certain when the answer was unknown. They wrote a question mark in the notes and promised to return after another test.

梅輕輕點頭，表示已看過報告。只有受訓志工能操作那台受損的測試機。一名跑步送件的學生帶來失蹤的檢查表影本。外面有輛機車在大門旁按喇叭。實驗室會在下次會議前提供替換電線。不知道答案時，裝得很肯定也不會有獎品。他們在筆記留下問號，答應再測試後回來確認。
