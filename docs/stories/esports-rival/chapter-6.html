橋上的雨

Rain on the Bridge

死對頭竟然是我的電競搭檔 · 第 6 / 25 章 · 1000–2000 · 英中對照 · 校訂中

用互動版查詞、朗讀與收藏

Heavy rain closed the university's north path. Mei helped students find a dry entrance while Kai checked whether the power room was safe. They argued over the best sign to place near a flooded step. Then Mei drew a clear arrow, and Kai fixed it above the water line. Students passed safely. Working together had taken less time than arguing about who should lead. The players did not always win, and they did not pretend that they did. A fair record helped them decide what to practice the following day.

大雨封閉了大學北側通道。梅協助同學尋找乾燥入口，凱確認配電室是否安全。兩人又為淹水階梯旁該放哪種標誌爭執。最後梅畫出清楚箭頭，凱把它固定在水位以上。學生安全通過。合作所花的時間，遠少於爭論誰該領導。玩家並非場場都贏，也不假裝自己會贏。公平紀錄能幫助他們決定隔天該練什麼。

Mei chose to accept help from the safety volunteers during the rain. Water ran down the side of the university building after sunset. A crowd gathered near the closed bridge but kept the passage clear. On the eve of the qualifier, the weather became unusually cold. Kai asked a worker to examine the damaged power cabinet. They put the small facts beside the larger problem and checked what each one really meant. The work took time, but a careful record was easier to trust than a rumor passed from person to person. The next test still mattered. A single good result showed what might work; another careful test would show whether the plan could be trusted again.

下雨時，梅決定接受安全志工的協助。日落後，雨水沿著大學建築外牆流下。人群聚集在封閉橋旁，仍讓通道保持暢通。資格賽前夕，天氣異常寒冷。凱請工作人員檢查受損的配電櫃。他們把小事實放在大問題旁，確認每件事真正代表什麼。工作花時間，但仔細紀錄比人傳人的傳聞更值得信任。下一次測試仍重要。一次好結果只表示方法可能可行；再一次仔細測試才能看出它是否值得持續信任。

Online, a storm map gave Quiet Signal a similar problem. Bridges appeared for short periods and disappeared with each wave of rain. Beacon could hold one crossing; Moth had to decide which groups to send first. They saved the slow team rather than chase a higher score. The referee praised their safety choice even though they placed second that night. Even when the answer seemed obvious, Mei wanted to know how it had been found. A good explanation could be shared with students who joined later.

在線上，暴風雨地圖給靜默訊號類似的難題。橋梁短暫出現，又隨雨浪消失。燈塔只能維持一處通道，飛蛾得決定讓哪一組先通過。他們選擇拯救速度較慢的隊伍，而不是追高分。儘管當晚只得第二名，裁判仍稱讚他們的安全判斷。即使答案似乎很明顯，梅仍想知道答案怎麼來的。好的解釋可以分享給後來加入的學生。

Moth said the river map was her favorite difficult training ground. A painted giraffe on the wall helped visitors find the safe door. A street musician played softly while students waited under the roof. The official notice said the north path would stay closed overnight. Mei remembered a short poetry line about rain and patience. The game rewarded the team for reaching the goal together. It gave no extra points for arguing, and that made the correct next signal more important than pride.

飛蛾說河流地圖是她最喜歡的困難練習場。牆上的長頸鹿圖畫幫訪客找到安全門。學生在屋簷下等候時，街頭音樂家輕柔演奏。正式公告指出北側通道整夜封閉。梅想起一行談雨與耐心的短詩。遊戲獎勵的是隊伍一起達標，不會因為爭吵加分。這讓下一個正確訊號變得比自尊重要。

Mei caught a cold from standing outside too long. At school Kai left a sealed warm drink on the public table, with a note stating that anyone could take it. At night Beacon told Moth to rest if she was ill. The two gestures felt strangely alike, but she had no reason to connect them. One was from an annoying classmate; the other was from a patient friend. Everyone had a different reason for joining the club. Mei tried to remember that the shared room belonged to those quiet students too, not just the confident ones.

梅在外面站太久而感冒。學校裡，凱在公共桌上留下密封的熱飲，紙條寫著任何人都能拿取。夜裡，燈塔告訴飛蛾如果生病就該休息。兩個舉動有種奇怪的相似感，她卻沒有理由聯想。一個來自惱人的同學，一個來自有耐心的朋友。每個人加入社團的理由都不同。梅努力記住，共用教室也屬於那些安靜的學生，而不只屬於有自信的人。

Kai offered a spare raincoat to a student without one. The maintenance team would repair the bridge lights after the storm. Beacon was sensitive to the timing of the short river crossings. Students walked along the sidewalk instead of crossing the flooded path. Mei wrote that the soul of their team was shared responsibility. Mei wrote down one question for the next meeting, then crossed out a sentence that sounded too certain. She wanted the record to show what they actually knew.

凱把備用雨衣借給沒有雨衣的學生。維修隊會在暴風雨後修理橋燈。燈塔對短暫河道通行的時機很敏銳。學生沿人行道走，沒有穿越淹水通道。梅寫道，隊伍的靈魂是共同承擔責任。梅為下次會議記下一個問題，又刪去一句聽起來過於肯定的話。她希望紀錄只呈現他們真正知道的事情。

The storm effect hid a warning marker near the second tower. A new term in the guide explained how long shields lasted. Moth sent an arrow toward the bridge when the water fell. A banner mentioned the old war that shaped Skyline's fictional world. The western route remained dry enough for visitors to use. There was no prize for sounding certain when the answer was unknown. They wrote a question mark in the notes and promised to return after another test.

暴風效果遮住第二座塔附近的警告標記。指南裡的新術語解釋護盾能持續多久。水位下降時，飛蛾朝橋梁發出箭頭訊號。布條提到《天際線》虛構世界裡一場古老戰爭。西側路線仍夠乾燥，能讓訪客通行。不知道答案時，裝得很肯定也不會有獎品。他們在筆記留下問號，答應再測試後回來確認。
