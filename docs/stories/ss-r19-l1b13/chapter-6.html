Before anyone called the day strange, Nadia had noticed how quiet the college library could become when people were worried. The smallest sounds seemed clear, and the smell of ink and tea made the place feel both familiar and new. Rather than act as if every answer was obvious, Nadia opened the letter notebook and began with what had really happened. A little note used “do” as its key word; Nadia kept the note because the rest of the line was still useful.
