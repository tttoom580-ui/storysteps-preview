At the college library, people were coming and going, each carrying a different idea about what was important. The letter notebook made it possible to hold on to a few clear details while the rest of the world kept moving. In the corner of the letter notebook, Nadia found the word “excited” beside an old drawing and stopped to ask what it meant. “mother,” Eli read from the practice page, then tried to connect the word to something they could actually see.
