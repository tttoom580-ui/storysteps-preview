The college found that someone had copied Nadia's best-known letter and changed its name to make two friends quarrel. Before anyone called the day strange, Nadia had noticed how quiet the college library could become when people were worried. The smallest sounds seemed clear, and the smell of ink and tea made the place feel both familiar and new. Rather than act as if every answer was obvious, Nadia opened the letter notebook and began with what had really happened. The word “dark” appeared in an example someone had written earlier, and Eli asked if the example fit their situation. “Let us keep ‘potato’ in our word notes,” Nadia said. “We can check its meaning again if we need it.” In the corner of the letter notebook, Nadia found the word “sweet” beside an old drawing and stopped to ask what it meant.
