A blue envelope vanished from the letter desk before its sender had approved the final words. The college library had its own familiar rhythm. Nadia knew the sound of footsteps there, yet something about this moment called for a closer look. It was tempting to make a quick story out of a small surprise. Instead, Nadia looked for one thing that could be checked and one person who deserved to be heard. There was room in the letter notebook for questions as well as answers, and today the questions were beginning to matter. The word “flower” appeared in an example someone had written earlier, and Eli asked if the example fit their situation. “Let us keep ‘rabbit’ in our word notes,” Nadia said. “We can check its meaning again if we need it.”

寄件人尚未確認最終內容，藍色信封就從代筆桌不見了。大學圖書館有自己熟悉的節奏。娜迪亞熟悉那裡的腳步聲，但此刻某件事值得更仔細地看。一個小驚喜很容易讓人立刻編出解釋。娜迪亞卻先尋找一件可核對的事，以及一位需要被傾聽的人。情書筆記本裡不只可以寫答案，也能記下問題；今天，那些問題開始變得重要。「flower」出現在前人留下的例句裡，艾利問這個例子是否適合現在的情況。「把『rabbit』留在詞彙筆記裡吧，」娜迪亞說，「有需要時再確認意思。」

The first reaction in the college library was a mixture of surprise and hope. Nadia felt both, and did not pretend otherwise.
