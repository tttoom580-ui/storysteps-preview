寫過千封情書的女孩

The Girl with a Thousand Letters

全校最會寫情書的人，從沒談過戀愛 · 第 1 / 25 章 · 1000以下 · 英中對照 · 校訂中

Nineteen-year-old Nadia had a red box in the middle of the college hall. Above it, a sign read, NEED HELP WITH A LOVE LETTER? She had written so many letters for other people that students called her the queen of kind words. That Friday, the box was nearly full. One envelope had a small star on it. Another came with a picture of a pet cat. A friendly woman from the college office had taken a camera photo for the school page. Nadia liked the work, but she had never gone on a date herself. She had begun to wonder whether that was a strange fact about her life.

十九歲的娜迪亞在大學走廊中央放了一個紅色箱子，箱子上寫著：「需要幫忙寫情書嗎？」她替別人寫過太多封信，同學都稱她為溫柔文字女王。那個星期五，箱子幾乎滿了。一個信封貼著小星星，另一個附著寵物貓的照片。校方辦公室有位友善的女士用相機拍了照片，準備放在學校頁面。娜迪亞喜歡這份工作，但自己從沒約會過。她開始懷疑，這是不是人生裡很奇怪的一件事。

“You use too many flowers,” said a voice behind her. Nadia turned. The new student was holding her sample letter as if it were a question on a test. “I am Eli,” he said. “You write as if every person should feel the same way.” Nadia felt her face grow warm. “People want help,” she answered. “They tell me what they mean, and I give them a better way to say it.” Eli placed the page down. “Then why did your sample say forever before anyone had even said hello?” He did not sound mean. He sounded curious, which was somehow harder to answer.

「你寫了太多花，」身後有個聲音說。娜迪亞轉身。新同學拿著她的示範信，彷彿那是考卷上的問題。「我叫艾利，」他說，「你寫得好像每個人的感情都一樣。」娜迪亞臉上發熱。「大家需要幫忙，」她回答，「他們告訴我心意，我幫他們說得更好。」艾利放下紙張。「可是你的範例連招呼都還沒打，就說永遠，為什麼？」他的口氣不壞，只有好奇，這反而更難回答。

A bug landed on the box, and Nadia brushed it gently to the window. Outside, students were making plans for a picnic in the north garden. Someone carried juice, and someone else was trying to reach a friend before the group left. Nadia watched them for a moment. Love, she thought, might be no more than the choice to notice another person. Eli noticed where she was looking. “That would make a better letter,” he said. “You saw a small thing and cared about it.” Nadia almost laughed. “Are you asking me to write a love letter about a bug?” “Why not?” he replied.

一隻小蟲停在箱子上，娜迪亞輕輕把牠送到窗邊。外頭的學生正準備去北花園野餐。有人提著果汁，另有人急著在隊伍出發前追上朋友。娜迪亞看了一會兒。她想，也許愛情不過是選擇留意另一個人。艾利發現她在看哪裡。「那樣寫出來的信會更好，」他說，「你看見一件小事，還真正在意。」娜迪亞差點笑出來。「你是要我寫一封關於小蟲的情書嗎？」「為什麼不行？」他反問。

“Show me one line you mean,” Eli said. Nadia picked up a pen, then put it down. She could invent a lovely line for someone else. For herself, every word seemed too large. “Maybe I don't know,” she admitted. Eli nodded without smiling as if he had won. “I don't know either,” he said. “I just want to be able to say what I know.” There was no lie in his face that she could notice. For a few seconds, the busy hall felt like a quiet room. Nadia looked at the empty page and understood how little practice she had had speaking in her own voice.

「給我看一句你自己真心相信的話，」艾利說。娜迪亞拿起筆，又放下。她能替別人編出漂亮句子，輪到自己卻覺得每個字都太大了。「也許我不知道，」她承認。艾利點頭，沒有露出贏了的笑容。「我也不知道，」他說，「我只想能說清自己知道的事。」娜迪亞看不出他臉上有說謊的痕跡。有幾秒鐘，忙碌的走廊像安靜的房間。她看著空白紙張，才知道自己多麼少練習用自己的聲音說話。

Eli took out a small card. On one side he had written, I like talking to you even when we disagree. On the other he had drawn an uneven star. “This is not a letter to you,” he said quickly. “It is an example of one true sentence.” Nadia looked at the card. It had no flowers, no promise about the future, and no perfect ending. Yet she did not want to use a different word. “Do you really think this is better than my sample?” she asked. “I think it sounds like somebody living,” Eli answered. “That is a start.”

艾利拿出一張小卡片。一面寫著：「即使意見不同，我也喜歡和你說話。」另一面畫著不太整齊的星星。「這不是寫給你的信，」他急忙說，「只是示範一句真話。」娜迪亞看著卡片。沒有花，沒有關於未來的承諾，也沒有完美結尾。可是她竟不想改掉任何一個字。「你真的覺得這比我的範例好嗎？」她問。「我覺得這像活生生的人會說的話，」艾利回答，「那就是開始。」

Then Eli pointed at the long line by her box. “If you write for all these people, who writes for you?” Nadia opened her mouth but found no answer ready. This was not a test she could pass with a pretty line. She placed a new paper under the red box and wrote, One letter I will never send.* She did not know who the letter was for. Eli walked toward the door without waiting for an answer, but he turned back once. “You don't have to show it to me,” he said. Nadia watched him leave and felt the strange wish to try. No one had asked Nadia to be perfect. The real request was harder: keep learning when the first answer was wrong, and keep treating other people as people even when the day became strange.

接著艾利指向箱子前長長的人龍。「如果你替這麼多人寫信，那誰替你寫？」娜迪亞張開嘴，卻找不到現成答案。這不是寫漂亮句子就能通過的考試。她在紅箱子下放一張新紙，寫上：「一封我永遠不會寄出的信。」她還不知道要寫給誰。艾利走向門口，沒有等她回答，但回頭說了一句：「你不必讓我看。」娜迪亞望著他離開，心裡竟生出想試試看的奇怪願望。沒有人要求娜迪亞完美。真正的要求更難：第一次答錯時仍要學習，日子變得奇怪時仍要把別人當成值得尊重的人。
