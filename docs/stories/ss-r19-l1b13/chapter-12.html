Nadia tried not to rush, because a wrong guess could make the problem harder for someone nearby. The letter notebook was close at hand, ready for the facts that would be easy to forget once people began talking. Nadia circled “media” on a reading sheet, not to win a test but to remember the meaning of the whole sentence.
