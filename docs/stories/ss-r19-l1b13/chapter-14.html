A college group planned a public wall where people could leave anonymous kind notes. Before anyone called the day strange, Nadia had noticed how quiet the college library could become when people were worried. The smallest sounds seemed clear, and the smell of ink and tea made the place feel both familiar and new. Rather than act as if every answer was obvious, Nadia opened the letter notebook and began with what had really happened. Eli did not treat silence as an empty space that needed a clever answer. That made Nadia want to speak in her own words. In the corner of the letter notebook, Nadia found the word “ant” beside an old drawing and stopped to ask what it meant. “hurt,” Eli read from the practice page, then tried to connect the word to something they could actually see. A little note used “sick” as its key word; Nadia kept the note because the rest of the line was still useful.

校內社團計畫設置匿名善意留言牆。在人們說這一天奇怪之前，娜迪亞就注意到，當大家擔心時，大學圖書館會變得多麼安靜。細微的聲音變得清楚，墨水與茶的香氣也讓這地方既熟悉又陌生。娜迪亞沒有裝作一切都很明白，而是打開情書筆記本，從真正發生的事情開始。艾利不把沉默當成必須用聰明答案填滿的空白。這讓娜迪亞想用自己的話說話。在情書筆記本的角落，娜迪亞看見一幅舊畫旁寫著「ant」，於是停下來詢問意思。「hurt。」艾利讀出練習頁上的字，接著試著把它和眼前真正看得到的東西連起來。一張小筆記以「sick」為關鍵字；娜迪亞留下它，因為剩下的句子仍然有用。

Nobody could say yet how the story would end. Nadia found this uncomfortable, especially with the honest letter still waiting for an answer. Eli suggested they take turns speaking, because repeating the same fear would not create a new solution. Nadia asked them to keep the wall free of names, guesses, and unwanted confessions. This was the point where a simple wish met the world as it really was. Nadia wanted to help, but wanting did not make every road safe or every rule fair. Eli asked what the other people involved might lose if the wrong choice were made. The answer took longer than either had expected. They agreed not to build the rest of their plan on a fact they had never checked. The word “clerk” appeared in an example someone had written earlier, and Eli asked if the example fit their situation. “Let us keep ‘several’ in our word notes,” Nadia said. “We can check its meaning again if we need it.”

娜迪亞請他們不要在牆上寫人名、猜測或不受歡迎的告白。這正是簡單願望遇上真實世界的時刻。娜迪亞想幫忙，但只靠想法不會讓所有路變安全，也不會讓所有規則公平。艾利問，如果選錯了，牽涉其中的人可能失去什麼。兩人花了比預期更長的時間才回答。他們同意，不把整個計畫建立在尚未核實的事實上。「clerk」出現在前人留下的例句裡，艾利問這個例子是否適合現在的情況。「把『several』留在詞彙筆記裡吧，」娜迪亞說，「有需要時再確認意思。」

The group returned to the letter notebook and looked at the first line again. It now seemed to ask a different question. Nadia asked Eli to explain the idea in plain words, as if telling it to someone who had just arrived. When the idea sounded confusing, they changed the plan rather than blaming the listener. Eli asked who should hear the news next, and whether it belonged to them to share it. They chose the careful answer over the exciting one, though excitement was still in the air. They only had to remember what had worked and what still needed care.
