An Unfinished Report

第二聲鐘響前，離開白沙灘 · 第 8 / 25 章 · 2000–3000 · 英中對照 · 校訂中

用互動版查詞、朗讀與收藏

A locked bookcase in the school's office finally opened when Omar adjusted its bent hinge. Behind the ordinary lesson books lay a report about an injury at the island's former visitor center. The report named the same blue triangle they had seen in the maintenance register. It was not a signature, Mei realized, but a mark for reports that required further investigation. The symbol had been copied onto later pages without its explanation. Jun read the account aloud while Mei compared dates. One witness described a broken step, another mentioned a locked emergency door, and both gave reasons the visitors had been unable to leave quickly.

奧馬調整彎曲鉸鏈後，學校辦公室上鎖的書櫃終於打開。普通課本後方是一份舊遊客中心事故報告，提到他們在維修登記冊看過的藍色三角形。梅明白，那不是簽名，而是標示「需進一步調查」的符號。後來的文件抄了符號，卻沒附說明。君大聲念證言，梅則比對日期。一名證人描述壞掉的階梯，另一名提到上鎖的緊急門，兩人都說明旅客為何無法迅速離開。

The three resisted the easy idea that one hidden villain explained every problem. A careless repair, an incomplete map, and an unsafe business decision could exist together. Only the recent changes to the beacon and water records suggested deliberate action after the station closed. Mei opened a page in her notebook called “Unknown.” She put the blue triangle there, followed by the fresh sand, the repaired pipe joints, and the radio tones. Then she wrote what would prove each theory wrong. Omar called it a strange way to search for an answer. Mei replied that a useful theory must allow the world to correct it.

三人沒有接受「一個藏起來的壞人解釋所有問題」的簡單想法。粗心維修、不完整地圖與不安全的商業決定可能同時存在。只有燈標和水務紀錄的近期修改，暗示站關閉後有人故意行動。梅在筆記本開了一頁「未知」，依序寫下藍三角、乾淨新沙、換過的管件與無線電短聲，並記下什麼證據會推翻各種假說。奧馬說，這是很奇怪的找答案方法。梅回答，有用的理論就必須允許現實來糾正它。

Jun's ankle began to ache, and he filed a complaint about having to climb again. Mei kept him conscious with conversation, then searched a bookcase for medical instructions. A discount notice for a former leisure tour lay behind a poster of a grasshopper. It had been a hasty business decision to bring visitors here, an old letter admitted. Omar tried to inform Mei where he had located the last first-aid box. A child's portrait showed a friendly monster beside a rocket and a swan; the picture was unique, and its frame was tight against the wall. When they pulled it free, they found an order to organize the release of station records after an incident of violence. Whoever had hidden the letter had known that those records mattered.

君的腳踝開始疼痛，抱怨又要爬樓梯。梅和他說話，確認他保持清醒，接著在書架上找醫療指示。一張昔日休閒旅遊的折價通知躺在蚱蜢海報後方。舊信承認，把旅客帶來這裡是倉促的商業決定。奧馬想告知梅最後一只急救箱的位置。孩子的畫像畫著友善怪物、火箭與天鵝；畫很獨特，畫框又緊緊貼著牆。三人把畫框拉開，找到一份命令，要求整理並公開一宗暴力事件後的氣象站紀錄。藏信的人顯然知道這些記錄的重要性。

As evening approached, a low boat passed beyond the western reef. It did not head toward them. Jun raised a bright cloth and waved, but the boat kept its distance. He was angry until he noticed that no one could have seen a small cloth through the mist. Their failure had a practical cause. They needed a higher, clearer place to send a light signal. The tower was high, but the third rule made it a dangerous choice. The old eastern stone platform on the school map offered another possibility. They marked the route and decided to inspect it during daylight.

黃昏前，一艘低矮小船從西側礁石外經過，沒有朝他們駛來。君舉起亮布揮動，船仍保持距離。他很生氣，直到想到霧裡根本沒人能看到那麼小的布。失敗有實際原因：他們需要更高、更清楚的地方發光訊號。燈塔雖高，第三條規則卻使它成為危險選項。校舍地圖上的東側石平台提供另一個可能。他們標好路線，決定白天再去查看。

The report in the bookcase made Mei think about the difference between a story and a record. A story can bring a reader close to a frightened person, but it can also place blame where no evidence exists. The injured visitors might have seen only a door that would not open. The workers might have seen a repair request that nobody had approved. The manager might have seen figures promising income and ignored the cost of safety. None of those views was complete. Mei asked Jun to read the witnesses' words exactly and to mark places where the paper was torn. If they later told others about the island, they would need to say what remained missing. Outside, the daylight faded behind clouds. The far boat did not return. Still, the eastern platform looked more promising than it had in the morning because their next action rested on checked maps, not hope alone. The three compared the observation with the earlier map before making a new decision. They left enough time to return safely.

書櫃裡的報告讓梅思考「故事」和「記錄」的差別。故事能讓讀者靠近害怕的人，也可能在沒有證據時怪罪別人。受傷旅客也許只看到打不開的門；工人也許只看到遲遲未被核准的維修要求；經理也許只看見收入數字，忽略安全代價。每種角度都不完整。梅請君照原文讀證人說法，並標出紙張破損之處。若未來向別人講述小島，他們得說清仍缺少什麼。屋外日光沉入雲後，遠方小船沒有回來。但東平台比早晨更有希望，因為下一步建立在核對過的地圖上，不再只靠盼望。三人把觀察與先前地圖比較後才作新決定，也預留足夠時間安全返回。
