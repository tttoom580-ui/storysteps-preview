雨中的藍光

Light in the Rain

第二聲鐘響前，離開白沙灘 · 第 7 / 25 章 · 2000–3000 · 英中對照 · 校訂中

用互動版查詞、朗讀與收藏

The coast was quiet until a sudden storm crossed the island. Lightning flashed above the radio mast, and the blue lamp continued to blink even while the main building lost power. Omar said that the tower must have another source of electricity. Mei suspected a separate battery or a small solar panel. Jun, restless after two days of injury, asked to go toward the light and find its switch. He argued that stopping the flashing might bring rescue faster. Mei showed him the map of the reef and asked what would happen if a ship misunderstood the signal. The possible harm to strangers was part of their decision.

海岸原本寧靜，直到突如其來的風暴穿過小島。閃電劃過無線電桅杆，主建築停電時，藍燈仍持續閃爍。奧馬認為燈塔一定有獨立電源。梅猜是另外的電池或小型太陽能板。受傷休息兩天後，君坐立難安，要求前往藍光處找開關。他認為關掉燈可能讓救援更快。梅把礁石地圖給他看，問如果船誤解訊號會怎樣。陌生人可能受到的傷害，也是他們必須考慮的事。

Mei proposed a different test. They watched the light from a protected window and recorded when it appeared between the rain clouds. It always pointed toward the deep gap in the rocks, yet the gap ended at a shelf of stone. A captain unfamiliar with the local current might believe it was a clear entrance. The warning “Never follow the blue light” was sensible for walkers and vessels alike. It did not tell them who had turned the lamp on, but it gave them a reason not to copy its pattern in their own distress signal. Jun accepted the explanation and helped mark the dangerous direction on their map.

梅提出另一個測試。他們從有遮蔽的窗邊觀察藍光，記錄它何時從雨雲間露出。光線總指向岩石間看似深的缺口，但缺口盡頭是一片淺石棚。不熟悉海流的船長可能以為那是通道。「不要跟隨藍光」對步行者和船隻都合理。它仍不能解釋誰打開燈，但足以告訴他們，發求救訊號時不能模仿藍燈模式。君接受了說明，幫忙把危險方向標到地圖上。

The station once had an automatic gate and a small automobile shed. Its committee kept instructions about how to clothe rescued people and stop them from drowning. A rusty faucet faced a broken fence, where a flock of birds had built a nest. The damaged microwave and a faded dumpling recipe told Jun that students had once eaten warmly here. He picked a juicy fruit, then put it down when lightning flashed. After that frightening sound, he felt naked without proper protection, and his mental picture of a safe island changed. Mei used a shovel to clear a drain so rain would not pollute the tank. She found a dried egg yolk inside a nest box. “It would be a sin to pretend this place was always empty,” she said. The evidence showed years of ordinary work, not a sudden curse.

氣象站從前有自動大門和小型汽車棚。委員會保存著替獲救者提供衣物、防止溺水的指示。生鏽水龍頭面對破損圍欄，鳥群在附近築巢。壞掉的微波爐和褪色的水餃食譜，讓君知道以前的學生曾在此吃過熱食。他摘下一顆多汁的果子，卻在閃電出現時放下。那一聲巨響後，他覺得沒有適當防護的自己彷彿赤裸，心中安全島嶼的印象也改變了。梅用鏟子清理排水溝，避免雨水污染儲水槽，並在巢箱裡找到乾掉的蛋黃。她說：「若假裝這裡一直沒有人，就太不應該了。」證據顯示這裡曾有多年日常工作，而不是突然出現的詛咒。

In the school yard, the storm tore down a loose gate. Omar used its fallen wooden bar to protect the water pipe from flying branches. Mei found evidence that birds had nested behind the electrical shed, so the team did not place a hot lamp there. They moved all batteries onto dry stone. By nightfall the rain had slowed, and Jun was able to stand with help. Nobody had reached the tower, but they had learned something important: the blue light could continue without the station's normal power, and the choice to obey the third rule had kept them out of a dangerous path.

學校院子裡，風暴吹倒一扇鬆動的門。奧馬用倒下的木橫條擋住飛來枝條，保護水管。梅發現鳥曾在電器棚後築巢，三人便沒有在那裡架設發熱燈。他們把電池全移到乾石上。夜幕降臨時雨勢變小，君在攙扶下已能站起來。沒有人走到燈塔，卻學到重要的一件事：藍光不靠氣象站的普通電源也能運作，遵守第三條規則使他們避開了危險道路。

Jun looked toward the blue light one last time before helping close the shutters. It was beautiful in a narrow way: a small blue mark against a wall of black sea. He admitted that beauty had almost persuaded him to walk toward it. Omar answered that danger often did not look dangerous from a distance. A cliff could appear to be an easy slope, a damaged wire could resemble a harmless string, and the wrong channel could look wider than the safe one. Mei wanted to remember this moment when they returned home. She had always believed maps were mostly made of distances. Now she saw that a good map also needed warnings about appearances. Under the table Jun carefully drew the tower, the false entrance, and the safer route in three colors. He added a note beside his own mistake: I wanted a quick answer; I needed a careful one.

君在幫忙關窗板前又看了藍燈最後一眼。它有一種狹窄的美：黑色大海前一個小小藍點。他承認，美麗幾乎說服自己走向那裡。奧馬說危險從遠處看常常不像危險。懸崖可能像容易攀爬的斜坡，壞電線可能像無害細繩，錯誤水道可能看來比安全水道更寬。梅想在回家後記住此刻。她一直以為地圖主要是距離，如今明白好地圖也要警告人別只相信外觀。君在桌下用三種顏色仔細畫出燈塔、假入口與安全路線，在自己的錯誤旁註記：「我想要快答案；我需要的是仔細答案。」
