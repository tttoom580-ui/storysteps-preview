地板下的聲音

Voices Under the Floor

第二聲鐘響前，離開白沙灘 · 第 4 / 25 章 · 2000–3000 · 英中對照

用互動版查詞、朗讀與收藏

A tapping sound beneath the classroom floor woke Mei before sunrise. She checked whether it returned at regular times and found that it came only when wind pressed against the sea wall. Omar lifted a loose board without breaking it. Underneath lay a hollow pipe carrying air from the beach to the building. The pipe could explain some of the voices they had heard during the night. Jun said the discovery made him feel foolish for being frightened. Mei disagreed. Fear had pointed them toward a question; careful testing had given them the answer. Neither feeling nor evidence was shameful.

日出前，教室地板下的敲擊聲吵醒梅。她檢查聲音是否定時出現，發現只有海風壓向防波牆時才聽得到。奧馬掀起一塊鬆動木板，沒有破壞它。下方是從海灘送風進校舍的中空管線，可以解釋昨夜聽見的部分聲音。君說自己曾因此害怕，現在感到愚蠢。梅不同意。恐懼引導他們提出問題，仔細測試才給了答案。感覺和證據本身都不丟臉。

Another section of the pipe led toward a closed room beside the old kitchen. There was no sign of an occupant, but the floor contained fresh sand. Mei could not tell whether wind or a person had brought it there. She traced the pipe and noticed that its metal joints had been replaced recently. Omar drew their positions on the back of a food label. Jun compared the drawing with the route marked by the blue triangle in the register. The two lines met beneath the bell tower. It was their first strong reason to believe that the strange sounds, the changed inventory, and the dangerous light belonged to one system.

管線的另一段通往舊廚房旁的封閉房間。沒有住人的跡象，但地板上有新鮮沙粒。梅無法判斷是風吹進來，還是有人帶來。她追蹤管線，注意到金屬接頭近期被更換過。奧馬把接頭位置畫在食品標籤背面。君拿圖與登記冊上藍三角標示的路線比較，兩條線都在鐘塔下方交會。這是他們第一次有較強理由相信，怪聲、遭修改的設備清單與危險藍光屬於同一套系統。

An old teacher's ambition had been to build a coastal classroom beside a barn. A dog's collar still hung by the gate. The children had planned a concert, but a dim storm had forced them inside. A handwritten joke about a squirrel showed that someone had kept a sense of humor, even when water began to freeze during a classroom experiment. In a box, a junior novelist had stored a story about an onion, a painter, and a precious lantern. The teacher's response was written on the back: “A traveler needs vision, but also facts.” Omar found a cooking pot for roast vegetables and a spray bottle beside it. When Mei gave the door three light taps, an empty metal pipe answered from below the floor. The sound confused them, then suggested a hidden space.

一位舊教師的志向，是在穀倉旁建立海岸教室。大門邊還掛著狗項圈。孩子們原本計畫舉行音樂會，卻被昏暗的暴風天氣趕進屋內。一則關於松鼠的手寫笑話，顯示即使課堂實驗的水開始結冰，仍有人保有幽默感。箱子裡，一位年輕小說家放著描寫洋蔥、畫家和珍貴燈籠的故事。教師在背面回應：「旅行者需要遠見，也需要事實。」奧馬找到一只用來烤蔬菜的鍋，旁邊還有噴瓶。梅輕敲門板三下，地板下的空金屬管傳來回音。聲響先讓他們困惑，接著暗示地板下有隱藏空間。

They did not rush into the sealed room. First they planned a return path, tied a guiding rope to the stable stairs, and chose an exact time to stop work before the shore became dangerous. The rope had to stay above the floor so a person could follow it in darkness. Mei gave Jun the notebook and asked him to record every movement from a safe seat. He objected that he wanted to help with more than writing. She answered that a reliable record might save all three of them. Jun accepted the job, but added his own condition: she must answer honestly whenever the risk changed.

他們沒有衝進密室，而是先規劃退路，把引導繩綁在穩固樓梯上，訂好在海岸危險前停止工作的時間。繩索必須高於地面，方便人在黑暗中摸著走。梅把筆記交給君，請他坐在安全處記下每次動作。君抗議自己想做的不只寫字。梅回答，可靠記錄可能救三個人的命。君接受工作，但加上一個條件：每當風險改變，她必須誠實告訴他。

Before sleeping, they tested the hollow pipe again. Omar covered one end with his palm while Mei listened beside the floor. The sound stopped at once. When he uncovered it, a low note returned, following the rise and fall of wind. Jun listened with his eyes closed and tried to describe what changed. He heard no footsteps and no separate voice, only air moving through metal. Mei wrote the result below her earlier account rather than replacing the original impression. Both mattered: what they had feared and what they had since tested. The small experiment also taught them not to break the pipe. It might be part of the old alarm system and could still give a useful warning. They placed a piece of cloth over the sharp opening, marked the area with stones, and left the room as they had found it. The mystery grew clearer when they stopped destroying its evidence.

睡前，他們又測試一次中空管線。奧馬用手掌蓋住一端，梅貼近地板聽，聲音立刻消失；他鬆手，低音便隨風的強弱回來。君閉眼聽，試著描述變化。他聽不出腳步或獨立人聲，只有空氣在金屬裡移動。梅把結果寫在早先印象下方，沒有覆蓋原先記述。兩者都重要：他們曾害怕什麼，以及後來測試出什麼。小實驗也教他們別把管線拆壞。它可能屬於舊警報系統，仍能提供有用警告。他們拿布覆蓋尖銳洞口，以石頭標記，再按原狀離開。停止破壞證據後，謎團反而更清楚。
