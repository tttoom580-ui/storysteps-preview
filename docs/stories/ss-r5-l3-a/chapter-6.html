綠色儲水槽

The Green Tank

第二聲鐘響前，離開白沙灘 · 第 6 / 25 章 · 2000–3000 · 英中對照 · 校訂中

用互動版查詞、朗讀與收藏

The morning count showed that the green tank held less water than they had expected. A slow leak darkened the ground beside its base. Omar located a cracked joint but could not repair it without a fitting. The old inn contained boxes of mixed hardware. Mei worried that taking the wrong piece from another part of the station would cause a second failure. They spread every object on a clean cloth and compared its size with the damaged joint. Jun read the labels aloud and crossed off items that could not carry water. The work was not exciting, but it kept their drinking supply from disappearing without anyone noticing.

早上清點發現綠色儲水槽的水比預期少。槽底旁有一處慢漏，把地面染暗。奧馬找到裂開的接頭，但沒有合適零件就修不了。舊旅舍有幾箱混雜五金。梅擔心從其他設備拿錯零件會造成第二次故障。他們把所有物件鋪在乾淨布上，逐一比對大小。君大聲讀標籤，刪掉不能用於水路的物件。工作不刺激，卻防止飲水在沒人察覺時逐漸消失。

A suitable metal ring lay beneath a row of photographs. Omar cleaned it, wrapped the joint, and tested with only a small quantity of water first. The first attempt leaked. The second held for a minute. They watched a full five minutes before deciding that the repair was good enough to use temporarily. Mei wrote “temporary” in large letters beside the date. Repairs that work today can fail tomorrow, especially when the weather changes. She also noted the tank level with a mark in charcoal, so they could measure any new loss. Jun smiled when the line stayed steady through the afternoon.

一排照片下方有個尺寸合適的金屬環。奧馬先洗淨、包緊接頭，再只用少量水測試。第一次仍漏，第二次撐了一分鐘。他們連續觀察五分鐘，才認定修補可以暫時使用。梅在日期旁大字寫下「暫時」。今天能用的修補，明天未必可靠，尤其天氣改變時。她還用木炭標出水位，之後可以量出任何新損失。下午水位維持不變，君終於露出笑容。

Their first achievement was finding a dry cupboard in the former inn. An old menu offered a fruit cocktail and jelly, but the jars were empty. A map showed the governor's summer visit to a nearby suburb, while a cartoon devil danced beside a sign advertising desirable views. Jun called the cheerful promises doubtful. Somebody had tried to dump a pile of waste near the water; a broken pole still marked the spot. Omar recovered a razor and ranked it below rope on their list of useful tools. “A reliable trail is better than a superior-looking shortcut,” he told his pal Jun. One photograph showed a wedding: two teachers had chosen to wed beside the bell tower, with their friends cheering for unity. Mei noticed that the wedding crowd stood above the present flood line.

他們第一項成就，是在舊旅舍找到乾燥的櫥櫃。舊菜單寫著水果雞尾酒與果凍，瓶罐卻全空了。地圖標示州長曾到附近郊區避暑，另一幅漫畫畫著魔鬼在「美景勝地」廣告旁跳舞。君不相信那些愉快的承諾。有人曾把一堆廢物倒在水邊，斷裂的柱子還標示著地點。奧馬撿到一把刮鬍刀，卻認為它不如繩子實用。他對好朋友君說：「可靠的小徑比看似更好的捷徑重要。」一張婚禮照片顯示兩名教師選擇在鐘塔旁結婚，朋友們為團結歡呼。梅注意到婚禮人群站的位置高於現在的淹水線。

The water shortage taught them to separate useful evidence from comforting stories. The school's bright visitor advertisements had promised that the island could welcome hundreds of people. Its emergency tank could barely support three for a week without new rain. Mei suspected the photographs and public signs described a dream, not the capacity of the real buildings. They stopped treating every printed page as an equal source. Engineering records with dates and measurements were stronger than cheerful pictures. At sunset, the tower sent two blue flashes into the mist. No vessel answered, but the light had returned on schedule, another fact they could test later.

缺水教會他們分辨有用證據與安慰人的故事。學校明亮的遊客廣告曾宣稱島上可容納數百人；實際緊急儲水槽若沒有新雨，連三人維持一週都很吃力。梅懷疑照片與公共標語描述的是願景，不是真實建築容量。他們不再把每張印刷紙都視為同等可靠；有日期和測量數字的工程紀錄，比愉快圖片更值得信任。日落時，塔向薄霧發出兩次藍色閃光。沒有船回應，但燈按預定時間再現，成為日後可測試的另一項事實。

As Omar worked on the water joint, Jun kept asking whether they could collect water from the well if the repair failed. Mei explained that their senses could not prove water safe. A clear liquid might contain substances they could not smell or taste. They had only simple tools and no laboratory, so a clever-looking test might create false confidence. They would instead collect rain, reduce unnecessary washing, and keep a clear count of what remained. The old visitors had treated the island as a place where supplies appeared by magic. Three tired people could no longer afford that belief. When the tank held its new level, Jun wanted to shout in relief. Mei smiled but asked him to mark the line again at dawn. A working repair should be trusted because repeated checks supported it, not because the person who made it deserved praise. Even a small detail mattered when the sea could change the path between one hour and the next.

奧馬修水管時，君不停問如果修補失敗，能不能改取井水。梅解釋，感官不能證明水安全。看來清澈的液體仍可能含有聞不到、嚐不出的物質。他們只有簡單工具，沒有實驗室，看似聰明的檢測反而可能帶來虛假信心。因此他們選擇收集雨水、減少不必要清洗，並清楚計算剩餘水量。舊遊客曾把島當成物資會像魔法般出現的地方；疲憊的三個人再也承擔不起這樣的信念。水槽水位穩住時，君想大聲歡呼。梅微笑，卻請他黎明再量一次。可信任的修補應由重複檢查支持，而不是因為修理者值得稱讚。當大海可能在一小時內改變道路時，再小的細節也很重要。
