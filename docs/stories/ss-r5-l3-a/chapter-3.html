A Door of Broken Glass

第二聲鐘響前，離開白沙灘 · 第 3 / 25 章 · 2000–3000 · 英中對照

The school's emergency room was locked, but its glass door had cracked in the storm. Omar wrapped his hand before reaching through the opening to turn the handle from inside. They needed dry bandages, medicine instructions, and a way to keep Jun's ankle still. Mei insisted that the broken entrance be marked, so a future visitor would not cut a hand by accident. Inside, a cupboard held rain covers and a small medical kit. None of the bottles was clearly dated. They decided not to take medicine they could not identify, even though Jun had begun to shiver with pain.

學校的緊急室上了鎖，但玻璃門在暴風雨中裂開。奧馬先包住手，才從裂口伸進去，從內側轉開門把。他們需要乾繃帶、用藥指示，還有固定君腳踝的方法。梅堅持標出破門位置，免得未來有人不小心割傷。櫥櫃裡有雨衣與小急救包，但藥瓶都沒有清楚日期。他們決定不吃無法確認的藥，儘管君已因疼痛開始發抖。

Mei found a room register with three different signatures for the same maintenance inspection. One belonged to the former station director, another to a school volunteer, and the third was only a triangle drawn in blue. Below the names, a line described a sealed storage pit beside the greenhouse. Omar wanted to open it at once. Mei asked him first to find out what had been stored there. Old equipment could be useful, but buried chemicals could be dangerous. She placed a flat rock over the damaged door threshold and copied the register numbers. The page made the mystery larger: the island had been closed, yet its equipment list showed recent changes.

梅找到一份房間登記冊，同一次維修檢查竟有三種簽名。一個屬於前站長，一個屬於學校志工，第三個只是一枚藍色三角形。名字下方寫著溫室旁有個封存的儲藏坑。奧馬想立即打開，梅卻要他先查清裡面裝了什麼。舊設備也許有用，埋藏的化學品卻可能危險。她在破損門檻上放平石頭，抄下登記號碼。這一頁讓謎團更大：氣象站早已關閉，設備清單卻出現近期變動。

They could not afford to waste daylight. Mei tied an apron around Jun's hurt leg, and Omar carried a bucket to the roof. A shadow cast by the chimney gave them a way to judge the sun. A faded menu promised dessert to the former students; now there was only dirt on its pages. The broken greenhouse looked dramatic against the clouds. Jun was still energetic enough to search the entry hall, where a warning had begun to fade. An inspector had signed it beside a child's drawing of a lollipop, an ox, and a penguin. The ongoing repair list mentioned a deep pit and a missing plug. A senior worker had once written, “A person who watches carefully is not a spy.” Mei wondered why someone had scratched through that line, and whether the damage was recent.

他們不能浪費白天的時間。梅把圍裙綁在君受傷的小腿上，奧馬則提著水桶走上屋頂。煙囪投下的影子讓他們能判斷太陽的位置。褪色的菜單曾向學生們承諾甜點，如今頁面只沾滿泥土。破損的溫室映著雲，景象十分驚人。君仍有精神搜尋入口大廳，看到一則正在褪色的警告。檢查員曾在警告旁簽名，旁邊有孩子畫的棒棒糖、牛和企鵝。持續維修的清單提到深坑與遺失的塞子。一位資深員工曾寫道：「仔細觀察的人不等於間諜。」梅不明白為什麼有人把這行字刮掉，也不確定破壞是否最近才發生。

In the afternoon the three walked to the roof and collected rain from a clean section of metal. They compared its smell with the water in the green tank. The tank water looked clear, and its cover was intact; the well water below had a sharp odor. Mei could not test every substance with the tools they carried. She could, however, follow the safe instruction while they searched for stronger evidence. They sealed the bucket and agreed to keep a daily count of water, food, and working batteries. This record would protect them from another danger: the fear that makes people remember only what they hope is true.

下午三人走上屋頂，從乾淨的金屬面收集雨水，與綠槽裡的水比較氣味。水槽的水清澈，蓋子完整；井水卻有刺鼻氣味。梅無法用現有工具檢驗所有成分，但在找到更強證據前，她可以先遵守安全指示。他們密封水桶，約定每天清點飲水、食物和可用電池。這份紀錄也能防止另一種危險：恐懼會讓人只記得自己想相信的事。

After checking the supplies, Mei used a clean stick to make a simple schedule on the floor. The first hour belonged to water collection; the second, to a rest and an ankle check; the third, to studying the register. She did not demand that anyone obey her without questions. Omar suggested moving the inspection until the sun was high enough to enter the greenhouse safely. Jun suggested keeping a spare dry cloth for the radio, even if the medical bag needed one too. They discussed both concerns and adjusted the schedule together. Such small choices were not exciting, but they kept the group from confusing activity with progress. By late afternoon the rain began again, and their collecting bucket filled more quickly than expected. Mei measured the new water instead of celebrating at once. The roof might hold leaves or salt near its edge, so they took water only from the cleaned inner section and kept it covered.

清點完物資後，梅用乾淨木棍在地板畫出簡單時間表。第一小時收集雨水，第二小時休息並檢查腳踝，第三小時讀登記冊。她不要求同伴不准提問地服從。奧馬建議等太陽夠高、進溫室更安全時才檢查；君提議替無線電留一塊乾布，即使醫療包也需要。三人討論後共同調整時間。他們的選擇不刺激，卻避免把忙碌誤當進展。傍晚又下雨，收集桶比預期更快裝滿。梅沒有立即慶祝，而是先測量新水。屋頂邊緣可能有葉子或鹽分，因此只從清潔過的內側取水，並保持遮蓋。
