會說話的機器

The Machine That Spoke

第二聲鐘響前，離開白沙灘 · 第 9 / 25 章 · 2000–3000 · 英中對照 · 校訂中

用互動版查詞、朗讀與收藏

The stone platform stood above a steep inlet and offered a view of the open sea. Mei climbed it first, testing every handhold before asking Omar to follow. From there they could see the blue tower and the shallow reef it faced. The signal was aimed across the false entrance, not across the safe western passage. Omar found a steel box beneath the steps. It contained a recorder that had been connected to the metal pipes underground. Whenever the bell system moved, a switch played a warning voice through the pipes. The frightening sounds had a mechanical source and could be turned off without entering the dangerous channel.

石平台位在陡峭海灣上方，可以看到外海。梅先爬上去，每個抓握點都測試過，才讓奧馬跟上。從那裡能看見藍塔與其正對的淺礁。訊號朝向假入口，而非西側安全通道。奧馬在階梯下找到鋼箱，裡面是一部連上地下金屬管的錄音機。每當鐘聲裝置動作，開關便透過管線播放警告聲。那些嚇人的聲音原來有機械來源，可以不進入危險水道就關掉。

They examined the box without taking the wires apart. Its old seal had been removed and replaced with a newer strip of tape. A maintenance date was written on the inside in blue ink. Mei copied the number and photographed the wire positions. She could not know who had done the work, but the recent date contradicted the public claim that the station had remained untouched for years. Omar said a person maintaining a warning system might have good reasons. Mei agreed. Their job was to find out what it did, not to decide guilt from the color of ink or tape.

他們沒有拆線，先檢查箱體。舊封條被去掉，換成較新的膠帶，內側用藍墨水寫著維修日期。梅抄下數字，拍攝線路位置。她不知道誰動過手，但近期日期與「多年無人碰觸」的公開說法相矛盾。奧馬說，維護警報系統的人也可能有正當理由。梅同意。他們的任務是查明設備做了什麼，而不是憑墨水或膠帶的顏色判人有罪。

A brass handle lay beside a buffet table in the former visitor hall. The station designer had described the new warning system as costly but necessary. Omar found a hammer, a porcelain cup, and a recorder in a box marked technical supplies. A humorous cartoon showed a kangaroo skipping through a stadium beneath icy mist, while a tangerine rolled across the track. Jun laughed despite himself. Mei tried to scrub a blue mark from the box, but the real seal had already been scraped away. Mei refused to skip the inspection even though the cold mist made the work unpleasant. She tightened a loose screw and heard the recorder click. A recorded voice seemed to threaten anyone who touched the tower, but Omar pointed out that the sound began only when a metal switch moved. It was equipment, not a ghost from hell.

黃銅把手躺在舊訪客大廳的自助餐桌旁。設計師曾把新警報系統形容為昂貴但必要。奧馬在標著技術物資的箱中找到鐵鎚、瓷杯與錄音機。一幅幽默漫畫畫著袋鼠在冰冷薄霧下跳過體育場，橘子在跑道上滾動。君忍不住笑了。梅試著刷掉箱子上的藍色記號，但真正的封條早已被刮除。儘管寒霧使工作不舒服，梅仍不肯略過檢查。她鎖緊一顆螺絲，聽見錄音機喀地一響。錄音中的聲音彷彿在威脅觸碰鐘塔的人，但奧馬指出聲音只在金屬開關移動時才出現。那是設備，不是來自地獄的鬼魂。

At the platform's edge, Jun took a clear photograph of the safer western channel. His ankle still hurt, so he remained seated and used the camera instead of climbing. Mei noticed that the three pale rocks at the channel entrance formed a triangle of their own. The shape on the old survey map matched their positions exactly. Perhaps the blue triangle had also been a geographic mark at first, later adopted for another purpose. By evening they had two channels marked on the same drawing: one that looked inviting and ended in rock, and one that looked narrow but led into deeper water.

在平台邊，君拍下安全西水道的清楚照片。腳踝仍痛，他便坐著使用相機，不再往高處爬。梅注意到水道入口的三塊淺色岩石也形成三角形，其位置恰好與舊測量圖相符。也許藍三角最初還是地理標記，後來才被轉用作別的意思。傍晚，他們在同一張圖上標出兩條水道：一條看似吸引人，終點卻是岩石；另一條看似狹窄，卻通向深水。

Omar found a second set of screw holes behind the recorder. They were too close together for the present mounting bracket. Someone had moved the equipment at least once. Mei measured the distance and asked whether the original bracket might have failed in the storm. Omar said that was possible, but the newer screws suggested a separate repair. He pointed out how easy it would be to put the recorder back in a different position without changing its sound. The distinction mattered. A recent repair might be ordinary maintenance; a changed signal direction could endanger ships. Jun took a photograph with the ruler visible so they could compare it later. When a gull crossed the inlet, its shadow briefly covered the false channel. From above, the rocks looked like the closed fingers of a hand. Mei was relieved they had never attempted the shortcut to the blue tower.

奧馬在錄音機後找到第二組螺絲孔，間距太窄，不適合現在的固定架。至少有人移動過設備一次。梅量了孔距，問原本支架是否可能在風暴中壞掉。奧馬說有可能，但較新的螺絲顯示另一次修補。他指出，把錄音機裝回不同位置而不改變聲音很容易。區別很重要：近期修理可能是普通保養；改變燈光方向卻可能危及船隻。君拍下有尺的照片，方便日後比較。海鷗飛越海灣時，影子短暫遮住假水道。從高處看，岩石像一隻手緊閉的手指。梅慶幸他們從未走捷徑靠近藍塔。
