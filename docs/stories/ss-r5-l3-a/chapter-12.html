地下檔案室

The Basement Archive

第二聲鐘響前，離開白沙灘 · 第 12 / 25 章 · 2000–3000 · 英中對照 · 校訂中

用互動版查詞、朗讀與收藏

The locked storage building had a narrow lower window, hidden by a pile of stones. When Omar removed the loose stones, they discovered a second door leading to the school's basement. This entrance was above the flood line shown on the chart. Mei sent Omar first with a flashlight and kept Jun at the top where he could call for help. They did not know what the room held, so they moved slowly and made no sudden changes to the air or power. A smell of dry paper greeted them. The basement was not a dangerous machine room after all; it was an archive containing school records and maintenance correspondence.

上鎖儲藏建築下方有扇狹窄小窗，被石頭堆擋住。奧馬移開鬆石後，發現第二道門通向學校地下室。這個入口高於海圖上的淹水線。梅讓奧馬拿手電筒先走，君留在上方可以呼救的位置。他們不知道房內有什麼，所以動作放慢，不突然改變空氣流通或供電。迎面而來的是乾紙味。地下室原來不是危險機房，而是保存學校紀錄與維修往來信件的檔案室。

Mei arranged the papers by date, from the oldest school letters to the newest equipment records. The first pages showed ordinary lessons and festival plans. Then came reports about a crowded tourist season, a broken well cover, and water that tasted strange after heavy rain. A dated inspection asked the station to separate the well from the green rain tank. That request was marked completed. Yet the later diagram showed the pipes connected again. Mei photographed both versions with their dates visible. It became possible that the second rule had been written after someone discovered the repeated connection.

梅按日期整理文件，從最早的學校信件排到最新設備紀錄。前幾頁是日常課程與節慶計畫，接著出現旅遊旺季人潮、井蓋損壞，以及大雨後井水味道異常的報告。有日期的檢查要求把井水與綠色雨水槽分開，並標成「已完成」。但較後來的圖又把兩路管線接在一起。梅拍下兩個版本，讓日期清楚入鏡。現在可以推測，第二條規則是在有人發現管線再度接錯後寫下的。

The basement classroom held an alphabet chart, a collection of shells, and a metal clip above a cigarette burn. A drawing of a cock stood beside a lesson about honesty. In foggy weather, the teacher had to hire extra helpers from a kindergarten on the mainland. Their goal was learning rather than profit, and the class called clean water a matter of liberty. Mei considered the notes meaningful because the previous inspector had numbered each page. A queer symbol in red powder marked a place where the soil had been disturbed. Jun found a bottle of shampoo inside a woven basket. Its label did not reveal the owner's name, but the way the students had learned to weave matched the knots on the beacon cable. Mei respected the practical wisdom behind the small lesson.

地下教室裡有字母表、貝殼收藏，以及夾在菸頭燒痕上方的金屬夾子。公雞的畫旁邊是一堂關於誠實的課。濃霧天氣時，教師得從本島的幼兒園聘請額外幫手。他們的目標是學習而非獲利，孩子們把乾淨飲水稱為自由的一部分。梅認為筆記很有意義，因為前任檢查員替每一頁都編了號。紅色粉末形成的奇怪符號標示土壤曾被挖動之處。君在編織籃中找到一瓶洗髮精。標籤沒有揭露主人的姓名，但學生學會的編織法，和燈標纜線上的繩結完全相同。梅敬重這堂小課蘊含的實用智慧。

One page explained the blue triangle. It marked a report that needed an independent safety review, and it did not identify a person. Mei felt relief: the symbol was not a secret code for an enemy, as she had briefly imagined. Instead it was proof that the island's own system had tried to warn of unresolved problems. She put a copy with their emergency notes. Jun found an older paragraph about how the blue beacon had been moved after several near misses on the reef. They had uncovered the reason for two rules, but not why the light still operated after the station closed.

有一頁清楚解釋藍色三角形：它表示報告需要獨立安全複查，並不指向任何人。梅鬆了一口氣。這符號不是她曾短暫想像的敵人祕密代碼，而是島上制度曾試圖提醒未解決問題的證據。她把影本收進緊急筆記。君找到更舊段落，記錄藍色燈標曾在多次船隻險些撞礁後被移動。他們查出兩條規則的來源，卻仍不知道氣象站關閉後藍燈為何繼續運作。

The archive had a quiet order that the empty station above no longer possessed. The papers were filed by year, each envelope marked with the room that had produced it. Mei had spent much of her working life correcting maps that mixed old and new information. Here she saw the same mistake made with records about water, repairs, and visitors. Someone reading one page alone could believe the well had been fixed. Someone reading a later page alone could believe it had never been a problem. Only the dates placed side by side showed the repeated failure. Jun asked whether the teacher had known about the later changes. Mei pointed out that the earliest warning had been written years before the newest connection. They could honor the teacher's caution without pretending she knew the future. When they climbed out, Mei left the paper stacks in their original order and carried only photographs and a carefully labeled copy.

檔案室的安靜秩序，是上方空蕩氣象站早已失去的。文件依年份存放，每個信封標明出自哪個房間。梅大半工作時間都在修正把新舊資訊混在一起的地圖；在這裡她看見同樣錯誤發生於水務、修理和旅客紀錄。只讀一頁的人可能相信井已修好；只讀後頁的人可能相信井從未出問題。只有把日期並排才能看見故障重複出現。君問教師是否知道後來改動。梅指出最早警告寫於最新接線多年以前。他們可以尊重教師的謹慎，不必假裝她能預知未來。離開地下室時，梅把紙疊放回原序，只帶走照片和標示清楚的影本。
