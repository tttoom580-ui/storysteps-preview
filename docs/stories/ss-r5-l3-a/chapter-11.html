無人領取的包裹

A Package Without a Name

第二聲鐘響前，離開白沙灘 · 第 11 / 25 章 · 2000–3000 · 英中對照 · 校訂中

用互動版查詞、朗讀與收藏

On the next clear morning, Mei found a newer parcel in the storage room. The paper was still stiff, and the writing was not blurred by years of damp air. It had been sent to a maintenance union on the mainland and never collected. Inside was a complaint about supplies delivered without proper safety records. Mei recognized the same shipment code that appeared on the altered water register. She held both papers side by side and checked every number. The code was too specific to be a coincidence. Someone had continued to move goods through the closed station, and the people who once worked here had tried to question that practice.

下一個晴朗早晨，梅在儲藏室找到一個比較新的包裹。紙張仍硬挺，字跡沒有被多年潮氣弄模糊。它原本寄給本島的維修工會，卻一直沒人領。裡面是一封投訴，說有物資缺少安全紀錄就被運來。梅認出同一個運貨代碼出現在遭修改的供水登記冊上。她把兩份紙並排，逐一核對數字。代碼太具體，不可能只是巧合。有人一直透過已關閉的氣象站運送物品，而過去的工人曾試著質疑這件事。

Omar wanted to accuse the island's former manager immediately. Jun pointed out that the name on the package might belong to a different person or simply be out of date. Mei wrote a careful note: evidence of recent deliveries, but no proof of who ordered them. Then she studied the shore from the hill and saw a low boat moving beyond the reef. It paused near the blue tower before heading east. The boat was too far away for a clear description. Their only safe chance was to photograph its position and time. Mei would not send a frightened accusation over an uncertain radio connection.

奧馬想立刻指控前任經理。君提醒，包裹上的名字可能屬於另一個人，也可能早已過時。梅小心記下：「有近期運送證據，但尚未證明由誰下令。」接著她從山坡觀察海岸，看見低矮船隻在礁石外航行，靠近藍塔停了一下才往東開。距離太遠，無法清楚描述船隻。他們唯一安全的做法，是拍下位置並記錄時間。梅不願把驚慌的指控透過不穩定無線電傳出去。

A bloody mark on an old bandage alarmed Jun, but the breeze soon carried away the smell of salt. The former supply manager had bought a loaf of bread, a parcel of pepper, and fishing line on credit. A criminal complaint later accused him of hiding a fortune. His emotional letter described hunger during the island's last winter. A dog had tried to lick his hand while he repaired the roof at a sharp pitch. He refused to pretend that the shortages were imaginary, and he asked the workers to resist an unfair order. Mei noticed a slender branch caught in a jacket sleeve on the steep slope. She gave the rope a careful twist around Omar's wrist. The last page was signed by the workers' union, which had demanded that the emergency stores remain open for everyone.

舊繃帶上的血跡讓君吃了一驚，海風很快又吹散鹽味。前任物資主管曾賒帳購買一條麵包、一包胡椒和釣魚線。後來一項刑事投訴指控他藏了一筆財富。他情緒激動的信描述了島上最後一個冬天的飢餓。一隻狗在他修理斜屋頂時，試著舔他的手。他拒絕假裝物資不足只是想像，並要求工人抵抗不公平命令。梅在陡坡上看見細長樹枝勾住一件外套的袖子。她把繩子小心纏繞在奧馬手腕上。最後一頁由工會簽署，要求緊急物資庫必須向所有人開放。

The afternoon brought strong wind and hunger. They had to share the last dry bread before walking to the storage building on the western side. The route crossed a slope covered in sharp grass. Omar tied the rope around the tree rather than around a person, reducing the chance of a sudden fall pulling everyone down. Jun stayed above the difficult part and called out time marks. By sunset they had reached the building, but it was locked. Mei decided to leave the door intact until they had planned a safe way in. A broken lock could destroy evidence as well as protect it.

下午風很強，三人也餓了。他們分完最後乾麵包，往西側儲藏建築走去。路線穿過長著尖草的斜坡。奧馬把繩索綁在樹上，而不是人身上，降低一人跌落時把所有人帶下去的危險。君留在困難區上方報時間。日落前他們到達建築，但門鎖住了。梅決定先保持門完整，等規劃安全進入方法後再行動。破壞門鎖可能毀掉證據，也可能破壞防護。

That evening Omar asked Mei whether it was wrong to hope that someone had acted badly. If there was a single guilty person, he said, perhaps the whole situation would finally make sense. Mei understood the feeling. She also remembered the earlier visitor report, where several careless decisions had combined into danger. The package could prove that deliveries continued; it did not prove why. The low boat could have carried equipment, fuel, or people, but they had not been close enough to see. Jun suggested drawing a table with one column for direct observations and another for possible explanations. They did so by the last light of day. The act of writing slowed their anger. Omar placed the package inside dry cloth, away from the food, and Mei noted where it had been found. If an investigator ever used it, those details might matter more than their first assumptions. For now they still needed shelter before night.

那晚奧馬問梅，希望有人真做了壞事是不是不對。如果只有一個犯人，他說，整件事也許終於能說得通。梅懂那種心情，但也想起較早的旅客報告：幾個粗心決定可能合在一起釀成危險。包裹能證明運送持續，卻不能證明原因。低矮船可能載設備、燃料或人，他們距離太遠，看不清。君提議畫表，一欄寫直接觀察，一欄寫可能解釋。他們借著最後日光完成，寫字讓憤怒慢下來。奧馬用乾布包好包裹，與食物分開；梅記下發現位置。未來調查人員可能更重視這些細節，而非三人的第一個猜想。眼前他們仍得在天黑前找到安全住處。
