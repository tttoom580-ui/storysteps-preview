三塊岩石的西方

West of the Three Rocks

第二聲鐘響前，離開白沙灘 · 第 10 / 25 章 · 2000–3000 · 英中對照 · 校訂中

用互動版查詞、朗讀與收藏

Mei measured the safe channel by watching driftwood move with the outgoing tide. Omar used a floating bottle tied to a cord to estimate the current near the edge. They did not throw anything where it could endanger another vessel. Their measurements were rough, but they agreed with the older chart. Jun used the radio from the high platform and finally heard a brief answer, too broken to understand. He resisted the wish to invent words between the sounds. Instead he repeated his call, gave the island's position in clear numbers, and asked for a confirmation. No confirmation came, yet the receiver had proved that the distant signal was not simply a recording inside their building.

梅觀察退潮時漂木的移動，估計安全水道的水勢。奧馬用繫繩的浮瓶量近岸海流，避免把物品丟在可能危及船隻的地方。測量雖粗略，卻與舊圖相符。君在高平台使用無線電，終於聽到短暫回覆，只是斷續得聽不清。他克制自己不要在雜音間填入想像的字句，而是重複呼叫，用清楚數字報上位置並要求確認。沒有確認傳回來，但接收器已證明遠方訊號不只是建築內的錄音。

Only a few days had passed, but the group felt as if months had gone by. Their second major milestone was now written on one page: the tide bell worked through water pressure; the green tank was fed by rain; the blue light faced a false channel; and an eastern platform could reach open sea. They still lacked a strong battery. Omar opened the radio case and found a corroded connection. He cleaned it with a dry cloth and tested the machine only when the sky was clear. The next message lasted longer than the first. It ended with a request that sounded like “repeat west,” but Jun refused to treat an uncertain phrase as an order.

其實只過了幾天，三人卻覺得已過了幾個月。第二個階段成果寫在同一頁：潮汐鐘靠水壓運作；綠槽由雨水供應；藍光面向假的水道；東平台能朝向外海。他們仍缺大電池。奧馬打開無線電外殼，找到鏽蝕接點，用乾布清理後只在天晴時測試。下一則訊息比前一次更長，末尾聽起來像「重複西方」，但君拒絕把不確定的字句當命令。

A banker had once paid for repairs around the bay, but the payment record was incomplete. The old beam above the transmitter showed a champion sailor holding a creative model of the channel. Mei used its shape to explain how a leak could decrease the power of a signal. Her voice returned as an echo from the historic stone wall. Jun was fearful of a crowded rescue boat, although no mob had ever reached the island. A holder for the receiver was missing, and an empty palace-shaped donation box contained only one penny. It was not normal to ignore an unsafe cable for years. The letter asking to postpone repairs referred to a scholarship fund, not a shortage of parts. Someone had tried to steal the money by moving one page behind a broken zipper. Mei put the page in a sealed envelope.

一位銀行家曾出錢修繕海灣周圍的設施，但付款紀錄不完整。發報器上方舊橫梁的圖畫裡，一名冠軍水手拿著富有創意的航道模型。梅用模型說明漏水會如何降低訊號強度。她的聲音從歷史悠久的石牆反射回音。君害怕救援船會擠滿人，雖然從沒有人群湧上小島。接收器的支架不見了，宮殿形捐款箱裡只剩一分錢。讓一條不安全的電纜多年不修，並不正常。請求延期維修的信提到獎學金，而不是缺少零件。有人把一頁帳目塞到壞拉鍊後面，企圖偷走款項。梅把那頁紙收進密封信封。

That night the blue lamp changed its rhythm. Two flashes became three, then stopped. Mei checked the recorder's mechanical timer and found that its schedule had not changed. The tower lamp was controlled by a different mechanism. Someone might still be working near it, or a damaged circuit might be producing irregular signals. They chose not to cross the beach after dark to find out. Jun wrote down each flash and the wind direction. The decision to wait was difficult, yet the alternative was to break the clearest warning on the island with no reliable plan to return.

當晚藍燈改變了節奏，兩次閃爍變成三次，接著停止。梅檢查錄音機的機械定時器，發現其時程沒有改變。塔燈由不同機制控制。也許仍有人在附近操作，也可能是壞電路造成不規則訊號。他們選擇不在夜裡穿越海灘查明。君記下每次閃光與風向。等待很困難，但另一選擇是在沒有可靠退路下，違反島上最清楚的警告。

It was tempting to believe the voice on the receiver belonged to a rescuer. Jun had spent long hours calling into silence and was exhausted by uncertainty. Mei could see the wish in his face before he said a word. She offered to read the numbers back with him, slowly and in the same order each time. Omar checked the power connection, because a fading battery might turn an ordinary sentence into meaningless sound. They worked together until they could separate a real reply from the tower's mechanical warning. In the process Mei learned something about trust. Trust was not the promise that a friend could never be wrong. It was the knowledge that a friend would tell you when the evidence was too weak to support a decision. Even after the blue light changed its rhythm, the three could disagree without forcing anyone to take a dangerous step.

他們很容易想相信接收器裡的聲音屬於救援者。君長時間對著沉默呼叫，已被不確定感磨得疲憊。梅還沒聽他說話，就從臉上看出他的盼望。她提議和他一起逐字複誦數字，每次都慢慢照相同順序。奧馬檢查供電，因為快沒電的電池也能把正常句子變成無意義雜音。他們合作，直到能把真實回覆與塔的機械警告分開。過程中梅更理解信任：信任不是保證朋友永不犯錯，而是知道當證據太弱，不足以支持決策時，朋友會坦白告知。即使藍燈節奏改變，三人仍能有不同意見，而不強迫任何人冒險。
