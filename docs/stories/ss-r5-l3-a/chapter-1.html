木板上的三條規則

Three Rules on Wood

第二聲鐘響前，離開白沙灘 · 第 1 / 25 章 · 2000–3000 · 英中對照

用互動版查詞、朗讀與收藏

The Morning Gull did not sink at once. It struck a reef shortly before dawn, rolled onto its side, and waited there while the waves climbed over its deck. Mei Lin climbed out with Omar, the boat's mechanic, and Jun, their radio operator. All three were adults. Jun had hurt his ankle, but he could still speak clearly. They carried a waterproof bag, a little food, a compass, and one damaged radio to a low island without houses. Mei had worked on coast maps for years. She knew the frightening difference between seeing land and finding safety. Before anyone wandered inland, she counted the people, checked their injuries, and marked their landing place with stones.

「晨鷗號」沒有立刻沉沒。它在黎明前撞上暗礁，側翻後卡住，海浪一波波爬過甲板。海岸測繪員梅琳與船隻技師奧馬、無線電員君一起爬了出來。三人都是成年人。君扭傷了腳踝，但還能清楚說話。他們把防水袋、少量食物、指南針和一台故障無線電搬上沒有房屋的低矮小島。梅多年從事海岸地圖工作，知道「看到陸地」與「找到安全地點」是兩回事。任何人進入島內前，她先清點人數、檢查傷勢，用石頭標示登岸位置。

Under a broken roof they found a board with three rules. “Leave the white shore before the second bell. Drink only rainwater from the green tank. Never follow the blue light.” None explained who had written them. Omar pointed to a bronze bell hanging from a wooden frame near the beach. A first clang came with the rising surf, and a second answered almost a minute later. Water poured across the white sand in a wide sheet. The group reached higher ground just in time. The rule had predicted something real. Mei wrote the words exactly, including their order, so memory would not change them into advice they merely wished to hear.

他們在破屋頂下找到一塊寫有三條規則的木板：「第二聲鐘響前離開白色海岸。只飲用綠色水槽的雨水。絕不可跟隨藍光。」木板沒有說明作者是誰。奧馬指向海灘旁木架上的銅鐘。第一聲鐘響隨著漲潮傳來，不到一分鐘又響起第二聲。海水立刻大片淹過白沙。三人及時跑上高地。第一條規則預測了真實危險。梅照原樣抄下文字，包括規則的順序，以免記憶把它們改成自己想聽的建議。

Mei found an acceptable shelter behind the station and tried to advise her friends calmly. Omar rested his chin on a coconut while they worked to connect a broken drainpipe. It was a cruel place for anyone who lacked tools: the last dose of medicine was wet, and a drip from the roof fell straight into the sleeping area. Jun admitted he felt envy when he saw Mei explore without fear, but she said that extreme heat could make anyone gamble with poor judgment. One glance at the tide persuaded her to move the camp. In a storage room, old hay lay beside abandoned laundry, a sincere letter, a cracked stool, and a tin that smelled of tobacco. A photograph showed twin brothers in fishing coats. Beneath it someone had written, “Three short blasts of a whistle mean return.” Mei copied that sentence, although she still did not know who had left the warning.

梅在氣象站後方找到一處可以接受的遮蔽地，試著冷靜地勸告同伴。奧馬把下巴靠在椰子上，一面幫忙接合斷裂的排水管。這裡對沒有工具的人相當殘酷：最後一劑藥已經潮濕，屋頂滴下的水正好落在睡覺的地方。君坦言，看到梅毫不畏懼地探索時，他有點嫉妒；梅則說，極端高溫會讓任何人做出賭運氣的錯誤判斷。她瞥了一眼潮水，便決定移動營地。儲藏室裡，舊乾草旁堆著沒人收的衣物、一封真誠的信、一張裂開的板凳，以及有菸草氣味的鐵罐。一張照片裡有穿著漁衣的雙胞胎兄弟；照片下方寫著：「三聲短促的哨音表示返回。」梅抄下這句話，卻仍不知道是誰留下了警告。

The green tank stood beside a weather building on the hill. Mei checked that rain had entered through a roof pipe, not from the dirty well below. She gave Jun a measured share and kept the rest covered. At dusk, a blue point began to blink beyond the northern rocks. It looked like a welcoming guide, especially to people who wanted a way home. Omar wanted to climb toward it and see who operated the lamp. Mei would not risk Jun's ankle or their only daylight path on a guess. They made camp behind a wall and agreed to watch the light without approaching it. Their first success was not escape; it was reaching night alive.

綠色水槽立在山坡的氣象建築旁。梅檢查雨水確實從屋頂管線流入，而不是從下方骯髒的井抽上來。她量好份量分給君，其餘水蓋起來保存。傍晚，北方礁石外開始閃爍一點藍光。對渴望回家的人來說，它看起來像歡迎的指引。奧馬想爬過去查看誰在操作燈，但梅不肯只憑猜測就冒著君的腳傷與唯一白天路線的風險。他們在牆後紮營，約定只觀察藍光，不向它靠近。第一晚的勝利不是逃走，而是三人都活著迎來黑夜。

For an hour nobody spoke about the possibility of dying. Mei had seen accidents turn small disagreements into serious mistakes, and she wanted their first decisions to be simple enough to check. They would move only while there was light, never separate without telling the others, and record each change in food and water. Those were their own working habits, not new laws of the island. The three rules on the board remained the only rules whose purpose they had not yet fully tested. Omar examined the damaged radio and said he could probably repair its antenna, but not before the equipment had dried. Jun placed the bag in a sheltered corner and agreed to leave it untouched overnight. When the blue lamp appeared again, it was easy to imagine a person waving from the far rocks. Mei watched until she could see the regular rhythm. Human hope could make a machine look like a friend. She did not want hope to become the reason they ignored what they already knew.

有整整一個小時，沒有人談起可能會死這件事。梅看過事故如何把小爭執變成嚴重錯誤，因此希望最初的決定簡單得能逐一核對。他們只在有光時移動，若未先告知絕不分開，每次食物和水有變化都記錄。這些是他們自己的工作習慣，不是小島的新法則。木板上的三條規則，仍是用途尚未完全驗證的唯一規則。奧馬檢查壞無線電，說也許能修好天線，但設備乾燥前不能動手。君把防水袋放在遮蔽角落，同意整晚不碰。藍燈再次出現時，很容易想像遠處岩石有人揮手。梅一直看，直到辨認出規律的閃光。人的希望會讓機器看起來像朋友。她不願讓希望變成忽略既有知識的理由。
