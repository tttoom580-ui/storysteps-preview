白色海岸

The White Shore

第二聲鐘響前，離開白沙灘 · 第 2 / 25 章 · 2000–3000 · 英中對照

By sunrise, Mei had made a rough sketch of the island. The white beach curved around a hidden channel, and a line of dark rock separated it from the station. A path of broken shell led toward a school building. Omar argued that the school might offer shelter, tools, and a better position for their radio. Jun worried that climbing would hurt his ankle, so they placed a stick under his arm and moved at his pace. No one had to prove courage by hiding pain. They marked every safe turn with three small stones, a sign that could be followed even if rain washed their footprints away.

日出時，梅已畫出小島草圖。白沙灘繞著一條隱蔽水道彎曲，一列黑岩把海灘和氣象站隔開。碎貝殼鋪成的小徑通往校舍。奧馬認為學校也許有遮蔽、工具，還有更適合架設無線電的高處。君擔心爬坡會讓腳踝更痛，另外兩人便找木棍讓他撐著，按照他的速度前進。沒有人需要藉隱瞞痛苦證明勇敢。他們在每個安全轉彎處堆三顆小石頭，即使雨水沖掉腳印，也有人能跟著標記回去。

Inside the school, the walls were covered with maps for visiting classes. One map showed the island with a narrow service road; another omitted the road entirely. Mei photographed both pages with the last power of her camera, then drew the differences in her notebook. The larger map had been printed twenty years before the station closed. It showed the green tank connected only to rain gutters. A later drawing had added a line from the well, then crossed it out. The crossing mattered more than the printed title. Someone had changed the water system, and someone else had tried to warn future visitors not to trust it.

校舍裡，牆上貼滿給參訪班級看的地圖。一張畫出狹窄的維修道路，另一張卻完全沒有畫那條路。梅用相機剩餘電力拍下兩頁，再把差異畫進筆記本。較大的地圖印於氣象站關閉二十年前，綠色水槽僅連接雨水溝。後來的圖添了一條通往井的線，卻又被劃掉。這道刪除線比印刷標題更重要：有人改過供水系統，另一個人則試圖警告後來者不要信任它。

A narrow avenue of shells led toward the old school, where a model cradle hung in the empty nursery. Someone had tried to decorate the room with paper stars. The elevator was useless, but a small exhibition in its lobby explained that every gallon of fresh water mattered. The station had once been the headquarters for a tourist survey, not a magical prison. Children's drawings showed a leopard, a firework, a boat with an odd ribbon, and mountains made of organic soil. Imagination had made the walls cheerful. Outside, the path turned steep; Omar had to spit out salt water after falling. Jun called the silent shore a threat to every careless tourist. Mei used her sleeve to wipe sand from a map and found that the sea reached farther inland than the old survey claimed.

一條滿是貝殼的小道通往舊學校，空蕩的育嬰室還掛著一座模型搖籃。有人曾用紙星星裝飾房間。電梯早已不能使用，但大廳的小型展覽說明每一加侖淡水都十分重要。這座氣象站從前是旅客調查的總部，並不是魔法監獄。孩子們畫了豹、煙火、一艘繫著奇怪緞帶的船，以及由有機土壤堆成的山。想像力曾讓牆面顯得快樂。外面的路開始陡峭；奧馬跌倒後吐出嘴裡的鹹水。君說寂靜的海岸對粗心的遊客是一種威脅。梅用袖子擦去地圖上的沙，發現海水已比舊測量報告所說的更深入內陸。

Near noon they returned to the shore to test the bell rather than guess its schedule. Mei watched the waves move through a slit in the rock; Omar timed the sound on his watch. The first bell rang when water entered the slit. The second came when the trapped air forced a metal plate upward. The period between them was short, but it changed with the strength of the tide. They chose a safer inland route that stayed above both water marks. That evening Jun heard no answering radio call. He still wrote the time of each attempt, because a failed signal was information too.

接近中午時，他們回到海岸測試鐘聲，而不是猜它何時響。梅觀察海浪如何穿過岩石裂口，奧馬用手錶計時。第一聲來自水進入裂口，第二聲則是受困空氣把金屬片往上推。兩聲間隔短，但會隨潮勢改變。三人選了一條高於兩道水痕的內陸路線。那天晚上，君沒有聽到無線電回應，但仍記下每次呼叫的時間，因為失敗的訊號也是資訊。

Mei had learned to draw coastlines by asking which details stayed the same when the tide changed. A bright beach might disappear, but the high rocks remained. A washed-out path could return after rain, but a roof built on solid stone offered a more reliable mark. She taught Omar and Jun to record both the position of an object and the conditions under which they saw it. A mark on dry sand meant little if the mark would be under water in an hour. Jun enjoyed looking for patterns until he realized the maps did not agree; then he grew worried again. Mei told him that disagreement between maps was not failure. It gave them a question they could answer by looking carefully. By evening their notebook held three sketches of the same place at different tide levels. The sketches were not beautiful. Their value was that another person could use them to avoid the sudden river of salt water beneath the second bell.

梅學海岸測繪時，習慣問潮汐改變後哪些細節仍固定。明亮海灘可能消失，高岩石卻留下。被水沖掉的小徑或許雨後再現，但建在實心石上的屋頂是更可靠的標記。她教奧馬和君同時記下物件的位置，以及看見時的環境。若一小時後標記就淹沒，乾沙上的記號意義有限。君原本很享受找規律，直到發現地圖不一致，又開始焦慮。梅告訴他，地圖意見不合不是失敗，而是提供可仔細觀察回答的問題。傍晚，他們筆記已有同一處在三種潮位下的草圖。草圖並不漂亮，價值在於其他人可以拿它避開第二聲鐘下突然而來的鹹水急流。
