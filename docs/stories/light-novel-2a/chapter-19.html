故意不求通過的測試

The Test That Failed on Purpose

轉生後只有一格道具欄！？——我在迷宮裡當上了最弱勇者 · 第 19 / 25 章 · 1000–2000 · 英中對照 · 校訂中

The new safety team began work in a hall behind the town library. It had a big screen, a table of old data, and a model of the moving hall. Ren made an extra copy of every report. "We do not need the Internet to make a good record," he said, "but I miss having a search box." The engineers laughed. Mira gave a short speech about the need to admit past errors. Argo stood nearby, looking uncomfortable but willing to answer questions. The first test used a low amount of pressure on a small metal wheel. It failed at once. Ren felt his stomach drop, but the engineer smiled. "That is why we test before we send people inside," she said.

新的安全小組在鎮立圖書館後方大廳開始工作。裡面有大螢幕、舊資料桌與移動大廳模型。任把每份報告都多複印一份。「做好紀錄不一定要靠網路，」他說，「但我真的懷念搜尋欄。」工程師們笑了。米拉簡短發言，說明承認過去錯誤的必要。阿爾戈站在一旁，看起來不自在，卻願意回答問題。第一次測試只讓小金屬輪承受低壓，它立刻失敗。任心一沉，工程師卻微笑：「所以我們才在送人進去之前測試。」

A second trial showed that the problem came from the narrow support, not from the wheel itself. Argo admitted that he had approved the change after a shortage of coal and metal. He had tried to save money quickly and had ignored a warning from a pupil in the training school. Mira asked for the student's original note. The team found it in a box marked "Personal Papers." The note had been written clearly, with a small drawing and a measured result. Ren looked at Argo. "You could have listened," he said. Argo nodded. There was no useful reply to that. The important thing now was to make sure the note could not be hidden again.

第二次測試顯示問題來自狹窄支架，不是輪子本身。阿爾戈承認，煤與金屬短缺時，是他核准了改動。他想快速省錢，卻忽略訓練學校一位學生的警告。米拉要求找出學生原始筆記。小組在標著「私人文件」的箱子裡找到它。字寫得很清楚，還有小圖與測量結果。任望著阿爾戈：「你當時可以聽他的。」阿爾戈點頭。那句話沒有什麼好辯解的。現在重要的是確保這張紙不會再被藏起來。

Workers gathered ahead of the public report. Some wanted to hurry the repair; others insisted on a longer test. Mira explained that both concerns were real. The team could not increase the pressure until the safety limits were known. They used a tiny weight to see how the new support moved, then compared it with the old design. The difference was small but important. Ren helped hold the ruler, counted each bit of change, and wrote down the result. He had no powerful spell to offer. He was simply making the work easier for the people who knew more than he did. That felt like honest effort.

公開報告前，工人們聚在一起。有人希望趕快修復，也有人堅持更長測試。米拉說明雙方擔憂都是真的。在知道安全限制前，團隊不能提高壓力。他們先用小重量觀察新支架如何移動，再與舊設計比較。差異不大，卻非常重要。任幫忙扶尺、數每一點變化、記下結果。他沒有強大魔法，只是在讓懂得更多的人工作更容易。這讓他感到自己確實付出了誠實努力。

At the end of the meeting, the team announced that the main hall would remain shut. The decision disappointed many people, but the report explained why. It also set a clear date for another test and named the people responsible. Ren noticed that Argo stayed to help put away the chairs. Mira's mother spoke to him briefly, then left without shaking hands. No one called that a happy ending. It was a beginning with hard work ahead. Pebble, however, believed any day with chalk was a success. He ate a broken piece from the floor and gave a loud happy squeak. Ren wished his own problems could be solved with such simple food.

會議最後，小組宣布主大廳繼續封閉。許多人失望，但報告清楚解釋原因，也訂出下次測試日期與負責人。任注意到阿爾戈留下幫忙收椅子。米拉的母親與他簡短說了幾句，就沒握手地離開。沒有人把這叫作快樂結局；這是需要辛苦努力的開始。然而小石認為只要有粉筆就是成功的一天。牠吃掉地板上一塊碎粉筆，快樂地尖叫。任真希望自己的問題也能用這種簡單食物解決。

At the public test, a student asked whether the wheel's sound could affect the brain or make people feel ill. Iko said that was a fair question and asked the medical team to check the noise level. Ren was confident enough to admit that he did not know the answer. He helped dial the number of the clinic and arranged a quiet restroom for visitors who needed a break. A cook arrived with soybean soup for the workers and asked them not to hurry their meal. Mira said, "I insist that the test wait until everyone is ready." The engineers agreed and changed the schedule. The machine had waited years; another hour would not hurt it.

公開測試時，一名學生問輪子的聲音會不會影響大腦，讓人不舒服。伊可說問題合理，請醫療團隊測試音量。任已有信心承認自己不知道答案。他幫忙撥號聯絡診所，也替需要休息的訪客安排安靜洗手間。一名廚師送來黃豆湯，請工人別匆忙吃飯。米拉說：「我堅持等大家準備好再開始。」工程師同意調整時程。機器已等了多年，多一小時不會傷到它。

When work began again, Ren watched the warning light with a new sense of purpose. The first red flash meant stop, and everyone followed it. He wrote the time beside the result and compared it with the previous trial. The numbers were not dramatic, but they made the problem visible. A child standing behind the barrier asked whether the hero could make the light turn green by shouting a spell. Ren shook his head. "No," he said, "but I can help people understand why it is red." The child nodded and returned to her mother. Ren was happy that his most useful answer could be an honest no.

工作重新開始後，任帶著新的目的看警示燈。第一道紅光表示停止，所有人照做。他在結果旁記下時間，與前次測試比較。數字不戲劇化，卻讓問題變得看得見。圍欄後的一個孩子問，勇者能不能念咒把燈變綠。任搖頭：「不能，但我可以幫大家懂它為何是紅的。」孩子點頭回到母親身邊。任很高興自己最有用的回答，竟可以是一句誠實的「不行」。
