The Pool of Old Names

轉生後只有一格道具欄！？——我在迷宮裡當上了最弱勇者 · 第 8 / 25 章 · 1000–2000 · 英中對照 · 校訂中

Mira hired an adult swimmer named Sena to guide the next visit. Sena carried a rope and a bag of first aid supplies. She explained that an ancient stone room lay below the pool, built a century before the town had its present name. The elders had left clear instructions: keep a rope on the surface, never swim alone, and stop when anyone felt a fever. Ren wrote the rules down. He had been used to fantasy stories where brave people dived into danger without thinking. Here, the adults argued about safety before anyone got wet. It was less exciting to watch, but it made him feel far less helpless.

米拉聘請成年游泳嚮導賽娜帶領下一次探查。賽娜帶著繩子與急救袋，說水池下方有間古老石室，建於小鎮改名之前的一個世紀。長者留下清楚指示：水面上必須留繩索、不能單獨游泳、有人發燒就停止。任把規則記下。他習慣的奇幻故事裡，勇敢人物總是不加思索地跳進危險；這裡的大人卻會先為安全爭論，再讓任何人下水。看著比較不刺激，卻讓他安心許多。

In the stone room, a figure carved on the wall held a peach and a bag of grain. Its face was partly broken, and no one knew its identity. Mira followed a line of marks toward a small door. Ren tried to help by lifting a heavy stone, but his arms were no match for it. Sena showed him a tool used by builders: a simple lever could move the stone if its support was placed correctly. They worked slowly and maintained the rope line. "The past was made by ordinary hands," Mira said. "It did not need heroes for every task." Ren thought of his old classroom and wondered whether anyone there was looking for him.

石室牆上刻著一個人物，手裡拿著桃子與穀袋。臉部已有部分破損，沒人知道他的身分。米拉沿著刻痕走到一扇小門。任想抬起重石幫忙，但他力氣不夠。賽娜示範工人使用的簡單槓桿：只要支點正確，就能移動石頭。他們慢慢工作，一直保持繩索連接。「過去也是普通人的雙手建立的，」米拉說，「不是每件事都需要勇者。」任想到原本的教室，不知道那裡是否有人在找他。

Behind the door stood a dry table with a page of directions. The ink had faded, but the first sentence was still clear: "Do not use this room to judge a stranger." Mira regarded those words with respect. A drawing showed how a worker could pose a question about the water and then record its answer. It was a typical lesson in careful observation, not a magic test of a person's soul. The stone figure was merely a sign for food storage. Ren felt foolish for expecting a secret king. Pebble, who had been resting in Sena's lap, woke up and tried to chew the page. Sena gently moved him away.

門後有一張乾燥桌子，上面放著一頁方向指示。墨水已褪色，但第一句仍清楚：「不要用這間房評判陌生人。」米拉很尊重這句話。圖上說明工人如何提出水流問題，再記錄答案。那是典型的細心觀察課程，並非測試靈魂的魔法。石像只是食物儲藏區的標誌。任對自己期待祕密國王感到好笑。一直待在賽娜腿上的小石醒來，想去咬那張紙，賽娜輕輕把牠抱開。

They found no treasure, but they did find a stone key shaped like a wave. Sena said it belonged to the old water gate and should stay under the town's care. Mira took a drawing rather than removing the key. Ren looked back at the quiet room. It had survived so long because earlier people followed rules that were easy to understand and teach. When they reached the surface, Bell asked whether the trip had been worth the trouble. "Yes," Mira answered. "Now we know the pool is a record room, not a shortcut." Ren smiled. A hero with one slot might never win a great sword. He could still help people understand what was already in front of them.

他們沒有發現寶藏，卻找到一把波浪形的石鑰匙。賽娜說那是舊水門的鑰匙，應留在小鎮共同保管。米拉只畫圖，沒有帶走鑰匙。任回頭望著安靜石室。它能保存那麼久，是因為早先的人遵循容易理解與傳授的規則。回到水面後，貝爾問這趟是否值得。「值得，」米拉回答，「我們現在知道水池是紀錄室，不是捷徑。」任微笑。只有一格的勇者或許永遠拿不到神劍，卻能幫大家理解眼前已經存在的事物。

On the return trip, Sena taught Ren how to move backward along the rope without turning his back on the water. The elder guide explained that the design of the old room was clever but not enough to remove every danger. "We maintain a path by checking it again and again," she said. Ren wrote her instruction beside the new drawing. He did not want one small tear in a rope to become the reason someone was hurt. Pebble climbed onto a dry rock and watched the swimmers with great interest. Ren suspected the dragon was learning something, although the only skill he had shown so far was eating the team's chalk.

回程時，賽娜教任沿繩子向後退，不要把背轉向水面。這位年長嚮導說，古房間設計雖然聰明，仍無法消除所有危險。「靠一次又一次檢查，才能維持道路安全。」她說。任把她的指示寫在新圖旁，不想讓繩子的一道小裂口成為別人受傷的原因。小石爬上乾岩，好奇地看著游泳的人。任猜牠也在學習，雖然牠至今唯一展示過的技能只有吃掉整隊的粉筆。

That evening, Mira placed the old key drawing beside the new map. She said the wave shape might connect to the moving hall, but she had no proof yet. Ren was tempted to make a grand guess. He imagined a secret king waiting beneath the town with a crown of sea stars. Mira listened politely, then asked for the first small question they could actually answer. Ren laughed at himself and pointed to the water gate. "We can find out whether the key shape appears on any other door," he said. She smiled and wrote down the task. The mystery remained large, but their next step was wonderfully small.

當晚米拉把舊鑰匙圖放在新地圖旁。她說波浪形狀可能與移動大廳有關，卻還沒有證據。任很想提出華麗猜測，想像鎮下有個戴海星王冠的祕密國王。米拉禮貌聽完，只問第一個真正能回答的小問題是什麼。任笑自己想太多，指向水門：「可以先查其他門上有沒有同樣的鑰匙形狀。」她微笑記下任務。謎團仍很大，但下一步小得令人安心。
