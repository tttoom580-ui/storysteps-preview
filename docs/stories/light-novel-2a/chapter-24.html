移動大廳的最終考試

The Exam of the Moving Hall

轉生後只有一格道具欄！？——我在迷宮裡當上了最弱勇者 · 第 24 / 25 章 · 1000–2000 · 英中對照 · 校訂中

The final examination began at sunrise. The schedule allowed three hours for the engineers to test the moving hall before the public arrived. Ren stood behind a curtain with a sandwich in his hand. "The hero eats while others work," he told himself, then put the food away. Mira reminded him that his task was to help users understand the signs, not to touch the main machine. A panda painted on the wall marked the first stop, and a flag showed the safe waiting area. Iko checked the highest load and told everyone that an increase would require another full test. The rules were clear, but Ren still felt his heart hop with worry.

最終驗收在日出時開始。時程表給工程師三小時，要求在民眾到來前完成移動大廳測試。任拿著三明治站在窗簾後。「勇者吃飯，其他人工作。」他自嘲，接著把食物收起。米拉提醒他的任務是幫使用者理解標誌，不是碰主機。牆上的熊貓畫標記第一站，旗子顯示安全等候區。伊可確認最高負載，告訴大家若要增加重量就得重新完整測試。規則很清楚，任的心卻仍因擔心而亂跳。

At the first turn, the wheel made a low tone. Ren thought it sounded similar to the old song, and he began to smile. Then the warning screen showed an unexpected number. The team stopped immediately. Iko examined the machine and found a bent support near its peak. A commercial company had offered to replace it quickly with a cheaper part, but she refused until the part could be checked. "A trick may make the numbers look better," she said, "but it cannot make the machine safe." Ren nodded. He had learned enough to know that passing an exam meant meeting the real conditions, not hiding a failure from the examiner.

第一個轉彎處，輪子發出低沉聲調。任覺得很像老歌，正要微笑，警示螢幕卻出現意外數字。隊伍立刻停下。伊可檢查後，發現最高處附近有支架彎曲。商業公司提出用較便宜零件快速更換，但她拒絕在未查驗前使用。「把戲或許能讓數字好看，」她說，「卻不能讓機器安全。」任點頭。他已學到，通過考試是符合真正條件，而非瞞過考官。

The engineers checked the spare parts stored in an old gun safe, a heavy box that had been used as secure storage after the town ended its military years. A label warned that careless work could create fear like the old days of terrorism, when public spaces had been closed without clear reason. Mira asked the team to keep the safety message practical and not use such history to frighten people. They selected a tested part and replaced the bend under Iko's direction. Then they restarted at low pressure. The wheel moved smoothly and stopped at the exact mark. Toma recorded the result, and two independent engineers signed it.

工程師檢查放在舊槍械保險櫃裡的備品；鎮上結束軍事時期後，那只沉重箱子改作安全儲物櫃。標籤提醒粗心工作可能重演恐怖主義年代的恐懼：當時公共場所曾在理由不明的情況下封閉。米拉請大家把警示寫得實際，不要用歷史嚇人。團隊選用已測試的零件，在伊可指導下換掉彎曲支架，再以低壓啟動。輪子平順轉動，在精確標記處停止。托馬記下結果，兩名獨立工程師簽署驗收。

At last the hall moved through its full safe route, from the market gate to the northern platform. No one called it a miracle. The engineers checked the brakes, doors, warning lights, and emergency path, then approved limited public use. Mira posted the rules where everyone could read them. Ren stood beside her, holding Pebble, who looked disappointed that no monster had appeared. "We did it," Ren whispered. "We passed an exam without a single heroic attack." Mira looked at him and smiled. "You helped us keep our promise," she said. The words warmed him more than any magic reward could have. Outside, the festival bells began to ring.

最後大廳跑完完整安全路線，從市場門到北方月臺。沒有人稱那是奇蹟。工程師逐一查驗煞車、門、警示燈與緊急通道，才核准有限度開放。米拉把規則貼在人人都能閱讀的地方。任抱著小石站在她身旁，小龍似乎因為沒有怪物出現而失望。「我們做到了，」任低聲說，「沒使出任何英雄攻擊就通過考試了。」米拉微笑看他：「你幫我們守住承諾。」這句話比任何魔法獎勵都讓他溫暖。外頭，祭典鐘聲開始響起。

A traveler from an international repair group arrived with a case of tools and a packet of cash to pay for meals. She had been working on railroads in several countries and wanted to observe the examination, not take command. Ren welcomed her and gave her a copy of the route rules. She said a swimsuit was hardly useful for a dry machine room, despite the ocean outside, and everyone laughed. Then she grew serious and asked whether every user of the hall could see the warning signs. Mira showed her the large pictures and the written notices. The visitor checked that the meaning remained clear even without special magic or local knowledge.

一名來自國際維修團隊的旅人帶著工具箱與用來付餐費的現金抵達。她曾在數個國家的鐵路工作，只想觀察驗收，不打算接管。任歡迎她，交給她一份路線規則。她開玩笑說，雖然外面有大海，但泳衣在乾燥機房幾乎派不上用場，大家都笑了。接著她認真詢問大廳每位使用者是否都能看見警告標示。米拉展示大圖與文字告示。訪客確認即使不懂特殊魔法或地方知識，也能清楚理解意思。

During the last part of the exam, Ren waited till the engineers completed their checks before asking a question. He had once rushed to speak whenever a room looked mysterious. Now he knew when to listen. He asked what would happen if the machine failed again after public opening. Iko showed him the emergency plan, the phone line, and the person responsible for closing the route. The answers were not glamorous, but they were ready. Ren wrote them in the guide for visitors and asked Mira to check the wording. She smiled and said his work would help people long after the festival lights went out.

驗收最後階段，任一直等到工程師檢查完才提問。從前房間一神祕，他就急著說話；現在他知道何時傾聽。他問開放後機器若再度失敗，該怎麼辦。伊可展示緊急計畫、聯絡線路，以及負責封閉道路的人員。答案不華麗，卻都已準備妥當。任把它們寫進訪客指南，請米拉核對文字。她微笑說，祭典燈光熄滅後，他的工作仍會長久幫到大家。
