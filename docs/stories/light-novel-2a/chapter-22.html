安靜之門的守門人

The Keeper of the Quiet Door

轉生後只有一格道具欄！？——我在迷宮裡當上了最弱勇者 · 第 22 / 25 章 · 1000–2000 · 英中對照 · 校訂中

The following morning, Toma took the team to the final test room. A cloth curtain hung before a wide screen that showed the moving hall's position. The keeper of the room was a shy older man who had worked there throughout his teens. He was nervous about the visitors, but Mira asked him to describe the old custom in his own words. "We lift the bell cover, check the limit marks, and return the tools before we leave," he said. Ren wrote each step down. The man had kept a diary for years, recording small changes after every shift. That careful habit had saved more information than the official records.

隔天早晨，托馬帶小隊到最終測試室。布製窗簾垂在寬大的螢幕前，螢幕顯示移動大廳的位置。守門人是一位害羞的年長男子，十幾歲時就一直在此工作。他對訪客很緊張，但米拉請他用自己的話描述舊習慣。「先抬開鐘罩、確認限制標線，離開前把工具放回去。」他說。任把每一步記下。男子多年來都寫日記，每次輪班後記錄小變化。這份細心保留的資訊，比官方紀錄還完整。

The engineers examined a complex gear behind the curtain and found a short piece of cream-colored paper caught in it. The paper carried a note from the day of the accident. It had been shut inside the machine, not hidden by a person. Mira read it aloud: "The following test must be done with the north door open." The line was missing from the public plan. Argo covered his face for a moment. "I did not see that page," he said. The keeper confirmed that the machine could not run properly with the north door closed. Ren felt a chill. The problem was larger than one man's choice, but the team had finally found the missing instruction.

工程師檢查窗簾後的複雜齒輪，發現一張奶油色短紙卡在裡面。紙上記著事故當天的訊息。它是被機器夾住，而非有人藏起來。米拉念道：「下一次測試，必須保持北門開啟。」這一行在公開計畫裡不見了。阿爾戈一時掩住臉。「我沒看過那頁。」他說。守門人證實北門關閉時，機器無法正常運作。任感到背脊發涼。問題比單一人的選擇更複雜，但團隊終於找到失落指示。

A loud bell rang before the team could begin. The safety screen showed a red line, and everyone stopped at once. Iko traced the cause to a loose cover, not to the main gear. She asked the keeper to replace it and checked his work. They then tested the machine at the lowest setting. It moved a little and stopped safely when the warning line appeared. There was no great flash, no shower of treasure, and no monster to fight. Ren almost laughed at how ordinary the success looked. Mira only smiled. "We made the warning work," she said. "That is more important than making the machine look strong."

隊伍還沒開始，大鐘便響了。安全螢幕亮出紅線，所有人立刻停下。伊可追查原因，發現是罩子鬆動，不是主齒輪故障。她請守門人換好罩子，再檢查工作。接著大家用最低設定試機。機器動了一小段，警告線出現時便安全停止。沒有華麗閃光、沒有灑下寶藏，也沒有怪物可打。任差點笑出聲，因為成功看起來竟如此普通。米拉只是微笑：「我們讓警告系統正常工作了。這比讓機器看起來強大更重要。」

When they returned to town, the keeper handed Mira a copy of his diary. He said the notes belonged to the people who would use the hall next. Mira thanked him and promised to keep the book available at the guide house. Ren watched him walk home slowly with Toma. The old man looked less shy now. Pebble found a piece of dry chalk on the floor and raised it like a tiny sword. "That's right," Ren told him. "The final enemy was poor record keeping." The dragon did not understand, but he looked very pleased to be part of the victory.

回鎮時，守門人把日記副本交給米拉，說這些紀錄屬於未來使用大廳的人。米拉道謝，承諾會讓大家在嚮導屋查閱。任看著他和托馬慢慢走回家，老人現在沒那麼害羞了。小石在地上找到一塊乾粉筆，像小劍般高舉。「沒錯，」任對牠說，「最終敵人是不良的紀錄習慣。」小龍沒聽懂，但對自己參與勝利顯得非常滿意。

A worker from the cloth industry visited the room before the final test. She explained that companies could rent the space to store fabric, and she had rented it in the past, and she remembered a battle with cold air that had spoiled several rolls. She showed the team a small stain made by ketchup from a worker's lunch, still visible on a piece of cloth. Ren thought it an odd clue, but the woman explained that the stain marked where the air had first changed direction. The team compared the location with the keeper's diary and found that it matched a note about the north door. Mira said the little memory was useful, although it did not prove the whole machine was safe.

最終測試前，一名布料產業的工人來訪。她解釋公司可以租用這房間儲存布匹，而她過去也曾承租，記得曾和冷空氣打了一場仗，害幾卷布受損。她拿出一片布，上面還留著工人午餐番茄醬的小污漬。任覺得是奇怪線索，女性解釋污點正好標示冷空氣第一次轉向的位置。團隊把位置與守門人日記比較，發現與北門的一段記錄相符。米拉說這份小記憶有用，卻不能單靠它證明整台機器安全。

On the way out, Ren shared a thought with Mira. Perhaps the maze had seemed mysterious for so long because its workers' everyday stories had been separated from the formal plans. The keeper knew the door, the cook knew the song, and the guide knew the paths. None had the whole answer alone. Mira said the next training class should bring these people together. She also invited the worker to tell her story to the students if she wished. The woman agreed, asking only that the details about her former customers remain private. Ren promised to respect that request.

離開時，任向米拉說出一個想法：迷宮之所以長久顯得神祕，也許因為工人日常故事與正式圖面被分開了。守門人懂門、廚師懂歌、嚮導懂道路，單靠一個人都不會有完整答案。米拉說下一期訓練課應讓這些人一起參與。她也邀請工人願意的話向學生說故事。那名女性答應，只希望舊顧客的細節維持隱私。任承諾尊重。
