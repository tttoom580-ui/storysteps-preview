The First Real Rescue

轉生後只有一格道具欄！？——我在迷宮裡當上了最弱勇者 · 第 4 / 25 章 · 1000–2000 · 英中對照 · 校訂中

Rain arrived with a loud crack of thunder. A branch fell across the lower path, and a traveler hurt his arm while trying to escape. Mira checked that he was awake and could speak. There was a little blood on his sleeve, but she did not guess at the cause. "We need a healer," she said. The group could combine their simple tools to carry him safely. Ren used a long piece of wood as a support while Bell called the clinic. The injured traveler was generous enough to thank them even when he was in pain. "I only came for a hamburger," he joked. Ren had never imagined that an adventure would begin with someone wanting lunch.

雷聲大作，雨勢突然變強。一根樹枝倒在下方小路上，一名旅人試著逃走時傷了手臂。米拉先確認他還清醒、能說話。袖子上沾了少許血，但她沒有擅自猜測傷勢。「我們需要醫療師。」她說。大家合用簡單工具，安全地搬運傷者。任拿長木板做支撐，貝爾則去聯絡診所。受傷的旅人雖然疼，卻還很大方地道謝。「我只是來吃漢堡的啊。」他開玩笑。任完全沒想過冒險會從一個人想吃午餐開始。

At the clinic, its director explained that the maze also supplied materials for the town's old water network. If a disease spread through dirty water, the whole population could be in danger. That made the missing road signs more serious than a simple game. Mira placed their evidence on a table: the old map, the changed notice, and the page about the two alarm bells. "We have a target now," she said. "Find the workers who changed the system." Ren had a sense that the answer might be close to the town itself. The director warned them not to accuse anyone until the facts were checked.

到了診所，主任解釋迷宮同時為小鎮舊水道提供建材。如果髒水傳播疾病，整個人口都可能受到威脅。這讓失去的路標不只是遊戲謎題。米拉把證據放到桌上：舊地圖、被改過的告示，以及雙警報鐘的手冊頁。「我們有目標了，」她說，「找出改動系統的工人。」任隱約覺得答案可能就在城裡。主任提醒他們，查清事實前不可以隨意指控任何人。

A message arrived from the harbor. The network of tunnels had stopped moving goods, and one group had been trapped by a fallen gate. A boat owner offered papaya and water for the rescue team. Ren asked if his skill would be worth anything this time. "We do not need a fast hero," Mira replied. "We need careful eyes." The team went to the harbor, where they found wet ropes and damaged wheels. The score on an old safety card showed that the gate had failed two checks. Therefore the group did not try to lift it at once. They first moved people away from the weak roof.

港口傳來消息：地下貨運網路停擺，一群人被落下的鐵門困住。一名船主送來木瓜與飲水給救援隊。任問自己的能力這次是否有用。「我們不需要跑得快的勇者，」米拉回答，「我們需要仔細的眼睛。」隊伍趕到港口，看見濕繩和壞掉的輪子。舊安全卡的分數顯示那扇門已有兩次檢查不合格。因此大家沒有立刻抬門，而是先把人移出脆弱的屋頂下。

Under Mira's direction, the workers fixed a strong rope to a stone post. Ren carried a tiny hook through a narrow slot, using his skill only after the rope was ready. The hook held the last knot in place. The workers pulled together, and the gate rose just enough for two children to crawl out. A healer checked them before anyone celebrated. Mira then closed the path, so no other traveler could follow the same dangerous route. Ren had wanted to show that his ability was powerful. Instead he learned that its value came from the plan around it. On the way home, he told Pebble, "Today I was a useful tool." The dragon made a happy sound and stole his papaya.

米拉指揮工人把粗繩固定在石柱上。等繩索準備妥當，任才用能力帶著小鉤子穿過窄縫，固定最後的繩結。工人一起拉，鐵門只抬高到足以讓兩個孩子爬出來。醫療師先檢查孩子，大家才敢慶祝。米拉隨後封閉道路，不讓其他旅人走同一條危險路線。任本想證明自己能力強大，現在卻懂得它的價值來自完整計畫。回家路上，他對小石說：「今天我至少算一件有用的工具。」小龍快樂地叫了一聲，然後偷走他的木瓜。

When the children were safe, Ren helped the harbor workers check the entire gate area. The wood beneath the wheels was wet, and the material covering the roof had several weak places. He tried to imagine how the scene would look during the next storm. The answer made him uneasy. "We have rescued people today," he told Mira, "but we have not repaired the road." She agreed and asked the workers to put up a clear barrier. A traveler complained that the closed path would add an hour to his trip. Mira listened and explained the danger without hiding it. She also marked an alternate route on a public board, so people were not left with only a warning.

孩子安全後，任協助港口工人檢查整片鐵門區域。輪下的木頭濕透，屋頂覆蓋材料也有幾處脆弱。他試著想像下次暴風雨時的景象，答案讓他不安。「今天救出了人，」他對米拉說，「但路還沒修好。」米拉同意，請工人設下清楚的圍欄。一名旅人抱怨封路讓他多走一小時。米拉聽完，沒有隱瞞危險，也在公共板上畫出替代路線，讓大家不會只得到警告。

At the clinic, the injured traveler asked Ren whether he would become a famous knight. Ren answered that his greatest attack so far had been dropping a stone on his own foot. The traveler laughed, then became serious. "The children followed the bright sign because I told them it was safe," he admitted. "I should have checked." Ren did not blame him. He asked whether the man would help share the new route with others after he healed. The traveler agreed. That promise could not fix a broken gate by itself, but it might keep another family from entering at the wrong time. Ren began to think that useful courage could sound very quiet.

診所裡，受傷旅人問任會不會成為著名騎士。任回答自己目前最強的攻擊，是把石頭砸到自己的腳。旅人笑了，接著認真起來：「孩子跟著亮亮的標誌走，是因為我告訴他們安全。我本該先確認。」任沒有責怪他，只問他傷好後願不願意協助告知新路線。旅人答應。這個承諾不能直接修好鐵門，卻可能阻止另一家人在錯誤時機進入。任漸漸覺得，有用的勇氣有時聽起來非常安靜。
