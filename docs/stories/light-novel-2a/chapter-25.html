The One Slot I Choose

轉生後只有一格道具欄！？——我在迷宮裡當上了最弱勇者 · 第 25 / 25 章 · 1000–2000 · 英中對照 · 校訂中

On festival morning, Ren was absent from the hero parade. He was standing among the workers at the station, helping them apply the new safety rules. A gentle melody played across the square. The principal of the guide school held a small court ceremony to thank everyone who had helped. Ren felt foolish when the crowd called his name. He had not found a magic sword or defeated a dark king. He had carried a small pin, held a ruler, and written records. His most useful skill was still just one slot. Yet when he looked at the flowing crowd, he could figure out why the work mattered. People were moving freely between homes again, and the road belonged to them.

祭典早晨，任缺席勇者遊行。他和站務工人站在一起，幫忙套用新安全規則。溫柔的旋律飄過廣場。嚮導學校校長主持簡單的公開表揚儀式，感謝每位協助的人。人群叫出任的名字，他有點傻氣地不好意思。他沒找到魔法劍，也沒擊敗黑暗國王。他只搬過小插銷、扶過尺、寫過紀錄。最有用的技能仍只有一格。但看著流動的人群，他終於想明白工作為何重要。大家能自由往來自己的家，這條路也重新屬於他們。

The engineers opened their final data book at the hall entrance. Every figure and record was available for anyone to read, from the smallest test to the final judgment. The team had also written what still needed repair. Bell explained that freedom was not the same as ignoring danger. It meant people could make choices with honest information. Argo stood beside the book and answered questions from visitors. Mira's mother listened nearby. She did not forgive him in public, but she agreed to help teach new guides how to keep records. Ren thought that was more than enough for one morning. Some endings did not need everyone to smile in the same way.

工程師在大廳入口打開最終資料冊。每個數字與紀錄都供任何人查閱，從最小測試到最後判定，連尚待修復的部分也清楚寫出。貝爾說明，自由不等於忽視危險，而是能在誠實資訊下做選擇。阿爾戈站在資料冊旁回答訪客問題，米拉的母親在附近聆聽。她沒有當眾原諒他，卻同意幫忙教新嚮導如何保存紀錄。任覺得這個早晨已經很好。不是每一種結局都需要所有人以同樣方式微笑。

A boy offered Ren a coin with a pumpkin on one side and a little bone on the other. "It's from the festival game," the boy said. "A hero should have a prize." Ren thanked him and placed the coin in his one slot. The blue window appeared, counting down ten minutes. Pebble watched with great interest, clearly hoping the coin would become food. Instead Ren released it into his palm and gave it back to the boy. "Keep it as proof that you were here," he said. The boy laughed and ran toward a seafood stall, where a cook was washing fresh fish. Ren looked at the empty square again. Nothing was stored, but it no longer felt like a failure.

一個男孩送給任一枚硬幣，一面畫南瓜，另一面畫小骨頭。「這是祭典遊戲贏來的，」男孩說，「勇者應該有獎品。」任道謝，把硬幣放進唯一道具欄。藍色視窗浮現，開始倒數十分鐘。小石興致勃勃地看著，顯然希望硬幣變成食物。任只是讓硬幣回到掌心，還給男孩。「留著吧，證明你今天來過。」男孩笑著跑向海鮮攤，那裡有廚師正在洗鮮魚。任再次望著空方格。裡面什麼都沒有，他卻不再覺得失敗。

Mira found Ren at the edge of the platform, where soft waves touched the sand. "The guide school has offered me a new job," she said. "I will teach the next group how to read maps and ask questions." Ren smiled. "That sounds right for you." She looked at him. "What about you? Will you apply to become a guide?" Ren considered his old life, the strange world, and the people who had taken him in. "I want to learn first," he answered. "I would like to stay, if that is my choice to make." Mira nodded. She did not ask him to become a hero or to stay for her. She just said, "Then I can show you the first lesson."

米拉在月臺邊找到任，柔軟海浪正輕拍沙地。「嚮導學校給了我新工作，」她說，「我要教下一批學生看地圖、提出問題。」任微笑：「很適合妳。」米拉看向他：「你呢？要申請當嚮導嗎？」任想著原本的生活、奇怪的世界，以及收留他的人。「我想先學習，」他回答，「如果這是我能自己決定的事，我想留下。」米拉點頭。她沒有要求他成為勇者，也沒有要求他為她留下，只說：「那我可以帶你上第一堂課。」

From the nearby shore came a pop as the festival fireworks began. Children called to each other, and someone started to surf on the gentle evening waves. Pebble climbed onto Ren's head, covered his face with a soft wing, and fell asleep. Ren looked at the moving hall, now full of ordinary passengers. He had arrived wanting a special power that would make him important. Instead he had learned to keep a small space free until the right moment came. The true treasure was not a sword, a title, or even the strange blue window. It was the chance to help without owning anyone else's future. When Mira called him toward the station, he followed with a smile. His next adventure would begin with a lesson, not a battle.

附近海岸傳來煙火開始時的啪聲。孩子互相呼喊，有人在溫和的傍晚海浪上衝浪。小石爬上任的頭，用柔軟翅膀蓋住自己的臉，就這樣睡著了。任望著已坐滿普通乘客的移動大廳。他初到時只想要讓自己變重要的特殊力量，現在卻學會把小小的空格留著，等真正需要的時刻到來。寶藏不是劍、稱號，甚至不是神奇的藍色視窗，而是能幫助他人、卻不占有別人未來的機會。米拉從車站呼喚他，任微笑著走去。下一場冒險會從一堂課開始，而不是一場戰鬥。

That night, Ren returned to the station for one last walk. He watched people flow through the doors without fear. A worker was wiping the windows, and a child helped wash the little signs by the entrance. Ren asked whether they needed help. The worker handed him a clean cloth, and he joined them. It was a task no one would record in a history book. Yet the clear glass allowed people to see the warning lights inside. Ren smiled at the simple connection between a clean window and a safe road. He looked at his empty blue slot and decided to leave it empty until someone truly needed it.

那天晚上，任回到車站做最後一次巡走。他看見人們毫無恐懼地通過門口。一名工人在擦窗，孩子幫忙清洗入口的小告示。任問需不需要協助。工人交給他乾淨的布，他便加入。這任務不會被寫進史書，但清澈玻璃讓人們能看見裡面的警示燈。任微笑，體會到乾淨窗戶與安全道路之間簡單卻重要的連結。他看向空空的藍色道具欄，決定等真正有人需要時，再把東西放進去。
