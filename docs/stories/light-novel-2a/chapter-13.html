天空地圖上的洞

A Hole in the Sky Map

轉生後只有一格道具欄！？——我在迷宮裡當上了最弱勇者 · 第 13 / 25 章 · 1000–2000 · 英中對照 · 校訂中

The third bell was supposed to stand near an old stream, but the map showed only a dark hole. "Anyway, we have to consider the whole route," Mira said. She opened a brief cartoon guide made by former workers. The pictures showed a sailor riding a motorcycle across an empty bridge. Ren laughed at the ugly drawing. Then he noticed the important detail: the road was painted in two colors. The lower path carried clean water; the upper path carried waste from the machine rooms. The streams were separate for a reason. If they were joined, dirty water could reach the town. "We should not test this by drinking it," Ren said. Mira agreed without smiling.

第三個鐘應該在舊溪流旁，但地圖上只畫著一個黑洞。「總之，得考慮整條路線。」米拉說。她打開昔日工人畫的簡短漫畫指南。圖上有個水手騎摩托車過空橋，醜陋畫風讓任笑出聲。但他發現重要細節：道路被塗成兩種顏色。下層運送乾淨的水，上層帶走機房的廢水。兩條溪流分開是有原因的；若接在一起，髒水就可能流進小鎮。「這種事別靠喝水測試。」任說。米拉毫無笑意地表示同意。

They asked an environmental worker to inspect the place before opening anything. She brought simple technology for testing soil and water. The first results showed no danger near the walking path, but the deeper hole was still unknown. Mira asked for a view from above rather than a jump into darkness. Toma led them to a high balcony with a narrow window. From there Ren could see old pipes following the walls. One pipe was lower than the others and had fresh marks around its base. Someone had worked on it recently. A worker who had felt sick with flu said he had seen the same marks two nights earlier, before he went home with a stomachache.

他們請環境工作者在任何開啟動作前檢查現場。她帶著測試土壤和水的簡單科技工具。第一批結果顯示步道附近沒有危險，但深洞內部仍未知。米拉決定先從高處觀察，而不是跳進黑暗。托馬領他們走上有窄窗的高陽臺。任從那裡看見舊管沿著牆延伸，其中一根比其他管更低，底座周圍還有新痕跡。顯然最近有人在那裡工作。曾得流感的工人表示，兩晚前他回家肚子痛前，也看過同樣痕跡。

A note tied to the pipe said, "Thief of the third bell, go to heaven and never return." Ren felt angry until Mira asked him to read it again. The words were written in new ink, but the rope was old. The message could be a trick to make people fear the room. "Do not conclude that a person is guilty because an ugly note says so," Mira reminded him. The environmental worker collected a sample without touching the unknown liquid. They would compare it with records from the machine shop. Pebble stayed safely outside, where he found a dry stone to chew. Ren was glad at least one member of the party had such simple needs.

管上繫著一張紙條：「偷走第三個鐘的賊，滾去天堂別再回來。」任一時氣憤，米拉卻請他再看一遍。文字用的是新墨，繩子卻很舊。這可能只是讓人害怕房間的把戲。「不能因為一張醜紙條，就斷定某人有罪。」米拉提醒。環境工作者在不碰未知液體的情況下採集樣本，準備和機房紀錄比較。小石安全地待在外面，找到一塊乾石頭啃。任很高興至少有一位隊員的需求如此簡單。

At the end of the day, Mira drew a new map showing both water routes and the uncertain hole. She did not erase the danger mark, even though they had learned more. Ren wrote the date and added the names of everyone who had checked the path. A good map, he realized, could hold questions as well as answers. On the way back they passed a small school where children were reading the old cartoon guide. One child called the silly sailor a hero. Ren looked at his own dusty shoes and smiled. Perhaps the next generation would remember the warning better because the drawing was funny, not in spite of it.

那天結束時，米拉畫出新地圖，標明兩條水路與未知深洞。雖然知道得更多，她仍保留危險記號。任寫上日期，補齊每位查驗者的姓名。他明白好地圖不只容納答案，也能保留問題。回程經過小學，孩子們正讀著舊漫畫指南。一個孩子把滑稽水手叫作勇者。任看看自己沾灰的鞋，笑了。也許下一代會更牢記警告，正是因為圖畫很好笑，而非儘管圖畫好笑。

The environmental worker asked the team to show consideration for the families who used water from the lower stream. They should not announce that the water was safe until the full test was complete, but they also should not leave people without an alternative. Mira called the kitchen and arranged a temporary delivery of clean water. Ren helped carry the containers through the market. Pebble tried to climb into one and was gently moved away. The families thanked the team, but Ren said there was no reason to celebrate yet. Their job was not finished until the source of the marks was known.

環境工作者提醒小隊，要體諒使用下層溪水的家庭。完整測試前，不該宣布水安全，但也不能讓人沒有替代方案。米拉聯絡廚房，安排暫時運送乾淨飲水。任幫忙搬容器穿過市場。小石想爬進其中一個，被溫柔地抱開。家庭們向小隊道謝，任卻說現在還不能慶祝，得查清痕跡來源工作才算完成。

After sunset Mira placed the two water samples beside their matching records. They had similar color, yet one had been collected above the machine room and the other below. She asked the worker to label them clearly and keep them in separate boxes. Ren offered to help carry the boxes, but he did not touch the samples without gloves. It was another small rule that would never appear on a hero's poster. He thought about how easy it was to praise courage and forget the ordinary care behind it. Then Pebble sneezed and knocked over an empty cup, reminding him that even quiet work could become exciting with the wrong dragon nearby.

日落後，米拉把兩份水樣和對應紀錄放在一起。顏色相近，但一份採自機房上游，一份採自下游。她請工作人員清楚標記，放進不同箱子。任自願搬箱，卻沒有在未戴手套時碰樣本。又是一條永遠不會印在勇者海報上的小規則。他想到人們多容易稱讚勇氣，卻忘了背後日常的照顧。接著小石打噴嚏，把空杯子撞翻，提醒他有不對的小龍在旁，安靜工作也可能變刺激。
