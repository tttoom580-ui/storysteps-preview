The Door That Rang Twice

轉生後只有一格道具欄！？——我在迷宮裡當上了最弱勇者 · 第 3 / 25 章 · 1000–2000 · 英中對照 · 校訂中

The door below the lantern made an alarm sound as soon as Mira touched its frame. Ren stepped back. A red light moved across the wall, then stopped on an empty cell with bars. The corridor looked like a classic prison from an adventure game. "Stay calm," Mira said. "An alarm is a warning, not a reason to run into danger." Her duty as a guide was to keep everyone alive. Ren nodded, although his knees were less interested in being calm than his face was. Pebble climbed onto his shoulder and struck a proud pose, as if a tiny dragon could direct the entire rescue. Then the dragon sneezed on Ren's ear.

米拉一碰燈籠下方的門框，警報立刻響起。任往後退了一步。紅光掃過牆面，停在一間帶鐵欄的空牢房上。走廊看起來像冒險遊戲裡的經典監獄。「保持冷靜。」米拉說，「警報是提醒，不是叫你衝進危險。」嚮導的責任是讓大家平安活著。任點頭，雖然他的膝蓋顯然比表情更難保持冷靜。小石爬上他的肩膀，擺出驕傲姿勢，彷彿小龍就能指揮整場救援。然後牠對任的耳朵打了個噴嚏。

A poster on the wall showed a cowboy with a strange haircut sailing a small boat. It was an old joke used to teach workers not to trust every picture. Below it someone had written a false order: "Press the blue button for a safe tour." Mira examined the board and found that the button was connected to a metal arm. If they pressed it, the arm would strike the wooden bridge behind them. Ren tried to object when she moved closer, but she showed him a line of chalk on the floor. "I am not going to touch the button," she said. "I am checking who changed its direction." Ren was glad his partner explained her plan instead of acting like a mysterious hero.

牆上的海報畫著一名剪了怪髮型的牛仔，正駕著小船航行。這是教工人不要相信所有圖片的老笑話。下面有人寫了假的指示：「按藍色按鈕，即可安全參觀。」米拉仔細看板子，發現按鈕接著一支金屬臂。要是按下去，金屬臂就會敲壞身後的木橋。她靠近時，任正想反對，她卻指給他看地上的粉筆線。「我不會碰按鈕，」她說，「我要查誰改變了它的方向。」任很高興夥伴願意解釋計畫，而不是故作神祕的勇者。

A worker came running from the upper room with a dryer in his arms. "The hall is wet," he said. "I was ordered to clear it before morning." He had no idea that the corridor was closed. He also carried a basket with a lamb puppet, used to show children how to trade tickets fairly. The odd objects made Ren smile, but Mira was serious. She asked the worker to stop and brought him back to the entrance. A single shot of steam escaped from the wall and made the lamp shake. The wall was still working; someone had simply used it as a trap. They could not punish a tired worker for following a false notice.

一名工人抱著乾燥機從上層房間跑來。「大廳積水了，」他說，「有人命令我明早前清乾淨。」他完全不知道走廊已經封閉。籃子裡還有一隻小羊布偶，是用來教孩子公平交換入場券的。奇怪的物品讓任笑了一下，但米拉很認真。她請工人停下，把他帶回入口。一股蒸氣從牆裡噴出，燈也跟著搖晃。牆上的設備還能運作，只是有人把它當成陷阱。他們不該因工人遵照假指示，就對疲倦的他施加處罰。

Ren used his one slot to carry a loose metal key through the narrow bars. It was small enough, and he had rested an hour since the gate rescue. He could not send the key through the air; he still had to reach through the cell himself. While he worked, Mira held a rope to keep him from falling. The key turned in the lock, and a hidden door opened behind the empty cell. On its floor lay a dry page from a maintenance book. The page showed a second alarm bell that should ring only during a real fire. Someone had joined the wires so both bells rang together. "That is our first proof," Mira said.

任用一格道具欄帶著鬆動的小金屬鑰匙穿過鐵欄。鑰匙夠輕，而且距離上次救貨車已過一小時。他不能隔空傳送鑰匙，仍得自己把手伸過欄杆。米拉拉著繩子，免得他跌倒。鑰匙一轉，空牢房後面就開了暗門。地上躺著一頁乾燥的維修手冊，畫著第二個警報鐘，說明那個鐘只有真正失火時才應該響。有人把線路接在一起，讓兩個鐘同時叫。米拉說：「這是我們拿到的第一份證據。」

Outside, Bell asked whether they had found the person behind the trick. Mira answered, "No. We only know the notice was changed." Ren had almost called the case solved, but her honest answer stopped him. A good investigation was not a race to make someone look guilty. The group marked the dangerous door and returned to town before sunset. On the way home, Pebble tried to eat the maintenance page. Ren held it above his head. "We have to keep this safe," he told the dragon. Pebble looked at him, thought for a moment, and ate the chalk from Ren's fingers instead. Ren wondered whether all adventures would end with him covered in white dust.

到了外面，貝爾問他們有沒有找到幕後的人。米拉回答：「沒有。我們只知道告示被改過。」任差點就說案子解決了，但她誠實的答案讓他停下。好的調查不是比誰更快讓別人看起來有罪。大家標好危險的門，趕在日落前回城。回程時小石想吃掉維修手冊那頁紙。任把紙舉過頭頂。「這個得好好保存。」他對小龍說。小石望著他想了想，改吃任手指上的粉筆灰。任開始懷疑，自己是不是每次冒險都會全身白粉地回家。

The worker with the dryer asked whether he should return to his job the following day. Mira did not tell him to sail away from his problems or act as if nothing had happened. She walked him to the safety desk and helped him make a report. Ren stayed with the broken notice and sketched its wires from a safe distance. When the team met again, the worker thanked them for treating him like a person rather than a suspect. Ren wrote that sentence on the back of his map. It was easy to remember a trap; he did not want to forget the person who had almost walked into it.

拿著乾燥機的工人問隔天是否該回去工作。米拉沒有叫他像航行離港般逃離問題，也沒有要求他當作沒事發生。她陪他走到安全櫃檯，協助填寫報告。任留在壞告示旁，從安全距離畫下線路。大家再次碰面時，工人感謝他們把自己當人看，而不是當嫌疑犯。任把那句話寫在地圖背面。陷阱容易記住，他卻不想忘記那個差點走進陷阱的人。
