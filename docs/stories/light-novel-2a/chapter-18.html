鏡子後的信

The Letter Behind the Mirror

轉生後只有一格道具欄！？——我在迷宮裡當上了最弱勇者 · 第 18 / 25 章 · 1000–2000 · 英中對照 · 校訂中

The old archive held more than anyone expected. There were maps, models, broken furniture, and a large mirror covered by cloth. Mira checked each item against the account book before opening boxes. Ren carried a backpack of pencils and labels. "We are here to learn, not to control the room," Mira reminded him. A letter appeared behind the mirror after they moved it with the help of two workers. It was from Mira's mother, written just after the accident. She had asked Argo to keep a record of every injured worker and every safety test. Ren could see why the missing pages mattered so much. The letter had been there for years, waiting to be read.

舊檔案室裡的東西多得出乎意料：地圖、模型、壞家具，還有一面蓋布的大鏡子。米拉開箱前先對照帳冊，任背著裝有鉛筆和標籤的背包。「我們來這裡是學習，不是要控制整個房間。」米拉提醒。兩名工人一起搬開鏡子後，後面露出一封信。那是米拉母親在事故後不久寫的，請阿爾戈保存每名受傷工人與每次安全測試的紀錄。任明白那些失落頁面為何重要。信在那裡等了多年，直到有人終於讀到。

The letter was not an attack on Argo. It said he had worked hard, but had made the situation worse by hiding information. "Further damage is likely if fear becomes the only reason for a decision," Mira read aloud. She paused, feeling the pressure of words from someone she loved. Ren suggested they hurry to show the council, but Mira shook her head. "This is personal as well as public," she said. "I want to ask my mother before sharing her words." Ren accepted the delay. He could help carry a page, but he could not make another person's choice for her. Pebble sat quietly on Mira's bag, as if he understood.

信並不是攻擊阿爾戈，而是說他確實努力工作，卻因隱瞞資訊使情況更糟。米拉念出一句：「若恐懼成為決策的唯一原因，進一步的損害很可能發生。」她停了下來，感到摯愛之人的文字帶來沉重壓力。任建議趕快拿給議會，米拉卻搖頭。「這不只是公共資料，也是私人信件。我想先問母親。」任接受延後。他可以幫忙拿紙，卻不能替別人做人生選擇。小石安靜坐在米拉的袋子上，彷彿也聽懂了。

They found another page showing the quantity of supplies used during the accident: rope, nuts, metal bars, and one powerful spring. The numbers did not match the official report. An engineer proposed testing the spring model under controlled conditions. Ren noticed an obvious difference between the old and new diagrams. The older machine had a wide surface to spread its weight; the new one placed everything on a narrow point. Mira wrote a question beside the drawing: who had approved the change, and why? It was a small line of ink, but it might lead to the real cause of the failure. Ren felt more confident now that they were asking exact questions.

他們又找到一頁記著事故時使用物資數量：繩索、螺帽、金屬棒，以及一個強力彈簧。數字和正式報告不符。工程師提議在受控條件下測試彈簧模型。任發現新舊圖面有明顯差異：舊機器用寬廣表面分散重量，新機器卻把重量集中在很窄的點上。米拉在圖旁寫下問題：誰核准了改動？為什麼？那只是一小行墨水，卻可能引向故障真正原因。任發現自己現在更有信心，因為大家開始提出精確問題。

That evening Mira spoke with her mother through a speaking crystal. Ren waited outside, giving them privacy. When Mira returned, her eyes were wet but her voice was steady. Her mother had agreed to share the letter with the safety team and wanted to attend the next meeting herself. "She said she is not ready to forgive everyone," Mira explained. "But she is ready to help keep the next worker safe." Ren thought that was a brave choice. He walked home with her beneath a quiet sky, while Pebble slept in the backpack. For the first time since arriving in the other world, Ren did not feel like a visitor waiting to be sent away. He felt like part of a place that needed him.

當晚，米拉用傳話水晶與母親談話，任在外頭等待，留給她們私人空間。米拉回來時眼眶濕潤，聲音卻穩定。母親同意把信交給安全小組，並想親自參加下次會議。「她說自己還沒準備好原諒所有人，」米拉解釋，「但已準備好幫助下一位工人安全工作。」任覺得這是勇敢的選擇。他與米拉在安靜夜空下走回家，小石睡在背包裡。自從來到異世界後，任第一次不覺得自己只是等待離開的訪客。他感到自己屬於這個需要他的地方。

Before leaving the archive, Iko weighed the old spring in pounds and checked the number against the book. One factor in the accident was the energy stored in the spring when it was pulled too far. A small note said one pound of force was enough to start the old test. Ren could see that a small nut had been placed in the wrong position. "A machine cannot marry two parts that do not fit," he joked, then stopped when no one laughed. Mira said the idea was clear even if the joke was strange. She asked him to propose a better way to describe the problem to new workers. Ren drew two simple boxes with arrows between them and explained where the pressure should go.

離開檔案室前，伊可用磅秤稱舊彈簧重量，再與帳冊核對。事故的一個因素，是彈簧被拉得太遠時儲存了過多能量。小字註明舊測試只需一磅的力量便能啟動。任看見一顆小螺帽裝錯位置。「機器也不能把不合的兩件零件硬結婚啊。」他開玩笑，發現沒人笑便停住。米拉說想法清楚，笑話雖怪。她請任提出讓新工人更容易理解的解說方式。任畫出兩個簡單方框和中間箭頭，說明壓力應往哪裡走。

That night the town was at peace, but Ren knew quiet streets did not mean every problem was solved. He wrote a short note about Mira's mother's letter and the questions it raised, without copying her private words. He wanted to remember the lesson: people could offer help and still keep something for themselves. Pebble lay beside the notebook, warming the desk with his small stone body. Ren looked at the dragon and said, "You are a terrible student, but a very good friend." Pebble opened one eye, took a bite of chalk, and went back to sleep. Ren laughed softly before turning out the light.

那晚小鎮十分平靜，但任知道街道安靜不代表所有問題已解決。他寫下一小段關於米拉母親來信引出的問題，沒有抄錄她的私人文字。他想記得這個教訓：人可以願意幫忙，同時仍保留屬於自己的東西。小石躺在筆記旁，石頭身體把桌子烘得暖暖的。任對牠說：「你是糟糕學生，卻是非常好的朋友。」小石睜一隻眼、咬一口粉筆，又睡回去。任輕輕笑了，才熄燈。
