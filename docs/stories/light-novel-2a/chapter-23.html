遊樂場旁的舊契約

The Old Contract at the Playground

轉生後只有一格道具欄！？——我在迷宮裡當上了最弱勇者 · 第 23 / 25 章 · 1000–2000 · 英中對照 · 校訂中

Two days before the festival, a broad path near the children's playground was closed. Workers had found a broken pipe carrying a small amount of poison from an old medical store. The safety team marked the area and called for a specialist. Ren wanted to search the pipe himself, but Mira stopped him. "We do not risk survival for a story with a good ending," she said. The store's former owner had signed a contract with the maze company many years earlier. According to that paper, the company had to keep the pipe within its own walls. The pipe had been moved during a repair, then forgotten. Ren felt shame that no one had checked the place before children played there.

祭典前兩天，兒童遊樂場旁的寬步道被封閉。工人發現一根破管，裡面殘留舊醫療倉庫的少量毒性物質。安全小組標示區域，找來專家。任想親自搜尋管內，米拉阻止他：「不能為了好看的故事結局，拿生存安全冒險。」倉庫前主人多年前和迷宮公司簽過契約。契約規定公司必須把管線留在自己牆內。管子卻在一次修理時被移動，之後就被忘了。任對從前竟沒人在孩子遊玩前檢查這裡感到羞愧。

The university team helped examine the old railroad plans. The drawings were complex, but one simple value was clear: the pipe had to remain a safe distance from public ground. Iko found a mark where the line had been changed. It was not made by an enemy or a thief; it had been recorded as a temporary solution that lasted far too long. Bell asked the company to clean the site and pay for proper testing. The former owner agreed to help find the original parts. Ren was glad no one used fear to scare the children into silence. Instead the adults gave a clear explanation and a date when the playground might reopen.

大學團隊協助研究舊鐵道設計圖。圖面複雜，但有個簡單數值很清楚：管線必須和公共地面保持安全距離。伊可找到改線的記號。那不是敵人或小偷留下的，而是一項被記為臨時解法、卻用了太久的措施。貝爾要求公司清理現場並支付正式測試費用。前主人也同意協助尋找原零件。任慶幸沒有人用恐懼嚇孩子閉嘴。相反地，大人清楚解釋狀況，也說明遊樂場可能重新開放的日期。

At the guide house, Mira's mother came to visit. She looked through the notes and said she was pleased with her daughter's careful work. Mira did not ask for praise, but her eyes brightened. Ren preferred to stay quiet, giving them time together. Pebble, however, rolled out from behind a chair with a bottle cap on his head. The two women laughed. Mira's mother asked whether Ren planned to return to his own world if a way was ever found. He said he had not decided. For now he wanted to make sure the hall could work without one special hero. "Then you have learned the best part of the job," she said.

在嚮導屋，米拉的母親來訪。她翻看筆記，說女兒做事仔細，讓她很欣慰。米拉沒有要求稱讚，眼睛卻亮了。任安靜留給母女相處時間。然而小石戴著瓶蓋，從椅子後滾出來，逗得兩人笑。米拉母親問，如果找到回到原本世界的方法，任會不會回去。他說還沒決定，眼下只希望大廳不用靠一位特殊勇者也能運作。「那你已學到這份工作最重要的部分。」她說。

That evening Ren wrote a page for new visitors. It explained the useful and dangerous parts of the maze in clear language. He avoided promises he could not keep and marked uncertain places honestly. Mira checked every line. They posted the guide next to the town map. Some visitors asked why the book did not include stories of great heroes. Ren pointed to the names of workers who had helped. "Because this place was not made by one person," he said. Pebble sneezed in agreement. Ren did not tell anyone that the dragon had probably only smelled chalk dust. It was a good moment, and a little mystery seemed harmless.

那晚任為新訪客寫了一頁指南，用清楚的語言說明迷宮有用與危險之處。他避免無法保證的承諾，如實標記未知地點。米拉逐行檢查，兩人才把指南貼在鎮地圖旁。有些訪客問，為何書裡沒有偉大勇者的故事。任指向協助過的工人姓名。「因為這地方不是一個人做出來的。」他說。小石打了個噴嚏表示贊同。任沒告訴別人，小龍多半只是聞到粉筆灰。那是一個美好的時刻，留點無害的謎也不錯。

A historian from the university brought an album of photographs taken before the old machine was changed. The pictures showed children playing safely near the market, workers resting by the doors, and passengers meeting family members at the station. Mira asked the historian to focus on pictures with a clear date and place. Some images had notes on the back, while others had nothing. "I prefer the ones we can check," Ren said. The historian agreed and placed uncertain pictures in a separate folder. They wanted to understand the past, not invent a perfect version of it. Mira proposed that the town should found a small history group to keep these records open. Pebble watched the pages turn, then grew bored and began to examine Ren's shoelace.

大學歷史學者帶來一本相簿，裡面是舊機器改動前拍的照片。照片上有孩子安全地在市場附近玩耍、工人在門邊休息，以及乘客在車站和家人團聚。米拉請學者先專注有清楚日期、地點的照片。有些背面留有註記，有些什麼都沒有。「我比較喜歡能核對的。」任說。學者同意，把不確定的照片另放資料夾。他們想理解過去，不是憑空編出完美版本。米拉提議小鎮應成立小型歷史小組，讓這些紀錄保持公開。小石看了幾頁便失去興趣，轉而研究任的鞋帶。

At the end of the visit Argo asked to speak. He said the pictures reminded him of the people who had trusted his work. "I ask your pardon for hiding the records," he told Mira's mother when she arrived, "but I know words alone cannot repair what happened." She listened and answered that she would judge his future actions by whether he kept the new rules. Ren respected both of them for speaking plainly. No one was forced to forgive, and no one was told that one good day erased years of fear. When they returned to the guide house, Mira placed the album copies beside the old safety plans, so both kinds of evidence could be studied together.

參觀結束，阿爾戈請求發言。他說照片讓他想起曾信任自己工作的人。米拉母親到場時，他對她說：「我為藏起紀錄請求原諒，但我知道話語不能修好已發生的事。」她聽完，回答會看他之後是否遵守新規則，再判斷他的行動。任很尊重兩人如此坦白。沒有人被迫原諒，也沒人被告知一天的好事能抹去多年恐懼。回到嚮導屋，米拉把相簿副本放在舊安全圖旁，讓兩種證據都能被研究。
