Soup, Socks, and a Crisis

轉生後只有一格道具欄！？——我在迷宮裡當上了最弱勇者 · 第 6 / 25 章 · 1000–2000 · 英中對照 · 校訂中

The cold room stood beside an old engine, which made a noise like a giant with a stomachache. Ren had expected a great magical crisis to begin with a dragon in the sky. Instead, he found two sacks of cabbage and a wet sock stuck behind a fan. "The sock is not the problem," Mira said. "It is a sign that someone entered the machine room without a guide." She opened her journal and wrote down the time. A neighbor who repaired toys offered to help, but he was not trained to work near the engine. Mira thanked him and asked him to stay outside. Ren realized that a clever answer was not always a brave action; sometimes it was simply knowing when not to touch a machine.

冷藏室就在一台舊引擎旁，機器的聲音像個肚子痛的巨人。任原以為偉大的魔法危機會從天上的巨龍開始，沒想到眼前只有兩袋高麗菜，以及卡在風扇後的一隻濕襪子。「襪子不是問題，」米拉說，「問題是有人沒找嚮導就進過機房。」她打開日誌，記下時間。一名擅長修理玩具的鄰居自願幫忙，但他沒有受過在引擎旁工作的訓練。米拉道謝，請他留在外頭。任明白聰明的答案不一定是英勇行動，有時只是知道何時不能動手。

An argument began outside. The firewoman wanted to close the whole building until its condition was known. A merchant said the kitchen could not stop its work for an entire day. Bell asked both sides to participate in a short meeting. "We cannot govern by fear," she said, "but we cannot pretend that fear is foolish." The group studied the old plans. The room had once been a small prison store during a war, and its shore entrance had later become a public path. The facts were uncomfortable, yet useful. A pipe could bring cold air through the hill, but only if the damaged joint was replaced. Ren's slot could carry the tiny screw into its place; it could not repair a whole engine by itself.

外面開始有人爭論。女消防員希望在確認狀況前，先把整棟建築封閉；商人則說廚房不能停工一整天。貝爾請雙方參加簡短會議。「我們不能只靠恐懼治理，」她說，「但也不能把恐懼當作愚蠢。」大家查看舊設計圖。原來這房間在戰爭時曾是小型監牢倉庫，靠岸的入口後來才成為公共通道。這些事實令人不舒服，卻很有用。管線可以把冷空氣引入山丘，但必須先換掉損壞的接頭。任的一格道具欄能把小螺絲帶到定位處，不能獨力修好整台引擎。

For a while, the repair seemed to work. Then the warning light changed from green to purple. Pebble made a sad noise and crawled under a pillow beside the doorway. Mira checked the guide and found a note about the machine's culture of care: every repair required a second person to watch the pressure. "We have not done that," she admitted. Ren felt his excitement fade. It was easy to imagine winning a battle with one brilliant move; it was harder to accept an incomplete repair. They stopped the test, wrote down the failure, and waited for the trained mechanic. By evening, the mechanic found a blocked air pipe behind the engine.

修復一度看似成功，警示燈卻突然從綠色轉成紫色。小石難過地叫了一聲，鑽到門邊的枕頭下面。米拉查閱說明，發現舊維護文化有個規定：每次修理都要有第二個人監看壓力。「我們剛才沒做到。」她承認。任的興奮慢慢退去。在想像中，一個漂亮招式就能打贏戰鬥；在現實裡，承認修復不完整比較難。大家停止測試、記下失敗，等專業技師到場。直到傍晚，技師才發現引擎後有條堵住的空氣管。

When the cold room finally held a safe temperature, the kitchen prepared soup for every worker. A child asked why Ren did not wear the bright clothing of a hero. "My hero suit is still in the wash," Ren said. The child looked impressed for the wrong reason. Mira laughed softly and handed him a clean pair of socks. "You can be a hero tomorrow," she said. "Tonight, be a person who rests." Ren appreciated that answer. He had been trying to act like someone from a story, but the real people around him were not waiting for a grand speech. They needed warm food, honest notes, and a door that would open safely in the morning.

等冷藏室終於維持安全溫度，廚房為每位工人準備了湯。一個孩子問任為何不穿勇者的閃亮服裝。「我的勇者服還在洗。」任回答。孩子因為完全不同的原因露出崇拜表情。米拉輕輕笑了，遞給他一雙乾淨襪子。「明天再當勇者吧，」她說，「今晚先當個會休息的人。」任很喜歡這個回答。他一直試著表現得像故事人物，但身邊真正的人並不是在等華麗演講。他們需要熱食、誠實紀錄，以及早上能安全打開的門。

The affair of the wet sock became a joke around the kitchen, but Mira wanted to know who had entered the room. A worker remembered that a group had played badminton in the empty hall during a break. One player had gone forth to fetch a ball and had opened the wrong door. The story sounded simple and human, yet it showed how one individual's mistake could affect a whole machine. Mira arranged a short training session with signs at every doorway. Ren helped by holding examples of safe and unsafe work. "If we only laugh at the sock," he said, "we will miss why it got there." The workers agreed to use the new door marks.

濕襪子事件成了廚房笑話，但米拉想知道是誰進過房間。一名工人回想，休息時有人在空廳打羽毛球，其中一名球員走上前撿球，誤開了門。故事簡單而有人情味，卻顯示一個人的失誤如何影響整台機器。米拉安排簡短訓練，在每扇門標上記號。任拿著安全與不安全操作的範例協助。「如果只笑襪子，」他說，「就會漏掉它為何會在那裡。」工人同意使用新的門標。

That night Ren wrote in his journal about the difference between a mistake and a secret. A mistake could be corrected when someone was willing to speak. A secret became more dangerous when people were afraid of being blamed. He was still learning how the town worked, and the number of small rules sometimes made his head ache. Mira brought him tea and asked what he had written. Ren read his note aloud. She nodded and said the best teams made it possible to report a problem before it became a disaster. Pebble slept through the whole conversation. He already seemed to believe that a good team existed mainly to provide a safe pillow.

那晚任在日誌寫下錯誤與祕密的不同：有人願意說出來，錯誤就能修正；人人害怕被責怪，祕密就會變得更危險。他還在學小鎮的運作方式，一大堆細小規則有時讓他頭痛。米拉端茶來問他寫了什麼。任念出筆記，米拉點頭，說最好的隊伍會讓人在問題變災難之前就有機會報告。小石睡過整段對話。牠似乎早已相信，團隊存在的主要目的就是提供安全枕頭。
