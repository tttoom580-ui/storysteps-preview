A Trial Without a Villain

轉生後只有一格道具欄！？——我在迷宮裡當上了最弱勇者 · 第 17 / 25 章 · 1000–2000 · 英中對照 · 校訂中

The chief of the city guard called a public meeting after the damaged papers were found. Ren expected a battle, but the room had no swords. There were chairs, water, and a long table. Argo sat at one end, looking tired. He admitted that his team had changed some signs after an accident years earlier. "We thought the old path would never be safe," he said. "I should have told people the reason." Mira did not clap or shout. She asked about the condition of the bridge and the records that had been taken away. Argo said he had kept the pages in a locked room because he feared another accident. His answer explained his emotion, but it did not make the missing records right.

發現受損文件後，城衛隊長召開公開會議。任以為會有戰鬥，會場卻連一把劍都沒有。那裡只有椅子、飲水與長桌。阿爾戈坐在一端，神情疲倦。他承認自己的隊伍多年前事故後改動過部分標誌。「我們以為舊路永遠不會安全，」他說，「我應該把理由告訴大家。」米拉沒有鼓掌，也沒有大喊，而是詢問橋梁狀況與被拿走的紀錄。阿爾戈說他因害怕再次出事，才把紙頁鎖進房間。這回答能解釋他的情緒，卻不能證明藏匿紀錄是對的。

A worker described how an accident had hurt a colleague when an iron wire broke. Another said the army had been called to guard the place, although the problem was a machine failure, not an enemy attack. A woman showed a receipt for corn sold to the work crew that day. The small account helped confirm the date. Ren listened carefully. He wanted to judge Argo at once, but Mira reminded him that a judge needed more than anger. The group compared the papers and found a lack of inspection notes for the week after the accident. They could not know the full depth of the problem until those pages were found.

一名工人描述鐵線斷裂、同伴受傷的意外。另一人說當時軍隊被叫來守衛現場，明明問題是機械故障，不是敵人攻擊。一名女性出示當天賣玉米給工班的收據，小小帳目幫助確認日期。任仔細聽著。他很想立刻判斷阿爾戈的對錯，米拉卻提醒他，裁判不能只靠怒氣。大家比對紙張，發現事故後那週缺少檢查紀錄。不找回那些頁面，就無法知道問題有多深。

When Argo was asked about his family, he mentioned that his partner had left him after the accident. Some people began to speak about marriage, saying a husband should never let work damage a home. Bell stopped them. "This meeting is about public safety, not private blame," she said. Ren was grateful. He had heard enough stories where a person's pain became an excuse for every bad decision. Mira asked whether Argo would be willing to share the locked pages with an independent team. He hesitated, then agreed, but insisted that the originals should not leave the town archive. The group accepted that condition and arranged for two witnesses to be present.

有人問阿爾戈家人的事，他提到事故後伴侶離開了他。幾個人開始議論婚姻，說丈夫不應讓工作傷害家庭。貝爾立刻制止：「這場會議談的是公共安全，不是私生活的指責。」任很感激。他聽過太多故事，把個人的痛苦當成所有錯誤的藉口。米拉問阿爾戈是否願意讓獨立團隊查閱鎖住的紙頁。他猶豫後答應，但堅持原件不能離開鎮檔案室。大家接受這個條件，安排兩名見證人在場。

The meeting ended without applause. Ren had expected a villain to be defeated and the story to move on. Instead the town had a list of questions, a date for checking the archive, and a promise to protect the people involved. Mira said that was a positive result, even if it felt small. Pebble, who had slept under the table, woke and gave a loud sneeze. Every person in the room turned to stare. Ren bowed and said, "Our dragon has made his closing speech." For the first time, even Argo smiled a little. A laugh did not solve the past, but it made the next difficult conversation possible.

會議在沒有掌聲的情況下結束。任原本期待擊敗反派、故事就能繼續，結果小鎮得到的是一份問題清單、一個查檔日期，以及保護當事人的承諾。米拉說那仍是正面的結果，即使看起來不大。一直睡在桌下的小石醒來，打了個驚天動地的噴嚏，全場都看向牠。任鞠躬說：「本隊的小龍發表結案演說。」連阿爾戈也第一次露出些許笑意。笑聲解決不了過去，卻可能讓下一場困難對話有機會開始。

After the meeting, Toma brought a small bottle of beer for the adult guards who had finished their shift. Ren declined a drink and stayed with Mira to put the chairs away. "Does it bother you that Argo is still working here?" he asked. Mira said it did, but she was willing to work under clear rules. She wanted a dialogue about responsibility, not a loud show of anger. Ren looked around the empty hall, where the light had become soft and warm. He could understand her feelings without claiming that every problem had a simple answer. That was a new skill he had not expected to learn in another world.

會議結束後，托馬替下班的成年守衛帶來一小瓶啤酒。任婉拒飲用，留下陪米拉收椅子。「阿爾戈還在這裡工作，妳會不會覺得不舒服？」他問。米拉說會，但只要規則清楚，她願意繼續合作。她要的是關於責任的對話，不是吵鬧的憤怒表演。任望著空廳，燈光已變得柔和溫暖。他可以理解她的感受，而不宣稱每個問題都有簡單答案。這是他沒想到會在異世界學到的新技能。

Outside, a child had mixed white chalk and black dust into a small drawing of the maze. She said she would pray for the workers to come home safely. She had learned to mix the two colors without making a mess. Ren thanked her and asked what she thought the picture needed. "A door with a handle," she replied. "So everyone can leave if they are afraid." Ren found the answer more wise than many grand speeches. He took the picture to the guide house and asked permission to place a copy near the safety notice. Mira agreed, but asked him to keep the child's name private. The drawing became a quiet reminder of why they were doing the work.

外頭，一個孩子把白粉筆與黑灰混在一起，畫了迷宮。她說要為工人平安返家祈禱。她學會把兩種顏色混合，而且不會弄得到處都是。任道謝，問她覺得畫裡還少了什麼。「有把手的門，」她回答，「這樣害怕的人就能離開。」任覺得這回答比許多華麗演說更有智慧。他把畫帶去嚮導屋，詢問能否在安全告示旁放副本。米拉同意，但請他不要公開孩子姓名。畫成了安靜的提醒，讓大家記得工作的理由。
