The Map That Walked Away

A week later, the council let Mira and Ren advance to the next maze floor. Their calendar showed only three safe mornings before a seasonal change in the underground climate. The new room looked like a desert, although the city above was wet with rain. Pebble jogged along the rocky path, following a line of cool air. "A maze is a being only in old songs," Mira said. "The walls move, but they do not make choices like people." Ren liked that principle. It meant they could study the floor instead of trying to guess its feelings. They took one step at a time, and Mira marked every turn in chalk. Their progress was slow but real.

一週後，議會同意米拉與任前進到迷宮下一層。行事曆顯示，在地下氣候進入季節變化前，只有三個安全的早晨。新房間像沙漠，雖然地上的城市正在下雨。小石沿著石路小跑，追著一股涼風。「只有古歌才把迷宮當成有意志的生物，」米拉說，「牆會移動，但不會像人一樣做選擇。」任很喜歡這個原則，因為這表示他們可以研究地板，不必猜測迷宮的心情。兩人一步一步走，米拉用粉筆標記每個轉角。他們進度緩慢，卻確實前進。

At noon they found a turtle resting beside a dry pool. Its shell held a strange pattern that matched an old map. Ren thought the animal might introduce them to a hidden path, but Mira asked him not to chase it. A humble guide from another town had once reported the same pattern during a brief period of heat. His report said that the pool would fill when the underground wind changed. "That is our ideal place to wait," Mira decided. They set a small tent behind a stone and watched the water return. Ren wanted to celebrate a victory at once, but the dry pool filled only halfway. Something was still blocking the stream.

中午，他們在乾水池旁找到一隻休息的烏龜。龜殼上的奇怪圖案竟與舊地圖相同。任以為動物會帶他們找出祕密通道，米拉卻請他不要追趕。另一座城的謙虛嚮導曾在短暫熱季記錄同樣的圖形，並說地下風改變時，池子會重新注水。「這裡最適合等候。」米拉決定。他們在石頭後搭小帳篷，等著水回流。任想立刻慶祝勝利，池水卻只回到一半。顯然仍有東西堵住溪流。

Near the pool they found a mop, a broken toothbrush, and a small wooden box. Ren first thought the items were trash left by careless visitors. Then he noticed that each one lay beside a blue line on the floor. The pattern was too regular to be random. Mira compared it with her notes and concluded that the objects marked places where workers had checked for water. She asked Ren to contain his excitement until they could confirm it. "If we rush, we may destroy the very clue we need," she said. Ren nodded and drew the positions. Pebble offered to carry the box; instead, he sat inside it like a king and refused to leave.

池邊有一支拖把、壞牙刷和小木箱。任起初以為那是粗心旅人留下的垃圾，接著卻發現每件物品都放在地上的藍線旁。排列太整齊，不像偶然。米拉比對筆記，推測這些是工人從前測水的位置。她請任先壓住興奮，等確認後再說。「一急著動手，就可能毀掉自己需要的線索。」她說。任點頭，畫下各物品的位置。小石自願搬箱子，結果是坐進箱子裡像國王一樣，怎樣都不肯出來。

At sunset the pool filled all the way. Mira found a safe ledge beneath the new waterline, and Ren placed a marker there. They did not enter the deep passage without a trained swimmer. Instead they returned to town with measurements and a clearer map. Bell praised the result because they had learned something useful without putting anyone at risk. Ren was a little disappointed; he had expected a secret door and a shining reward. Mira pointed to the drawing in his hand. "This will keep the next team from walking into water," she said. Ren looked again. A map was not exciting by itself, but it could bring people home. That was enough for one day.

日落時，水池終於滿了。米拉在新水位下找到安全的石緣，任在那裡做了標記。他們沒有在缺少合格游泳者的情況下進入深水通道，而是帶著測量結果與更清楚的地圖回城。貝爾稱讚他們，因為兩人沒有冒險也得到有用資訊。任有點失望；他本來期待祕密大門和閃亮獎品。米拉指著他手上的圖。「它能避免下一隊走進水裡。」她說。任重新看了一眼。地圖本身雖然不刺激，卻能讓人平安回家。這樣就夠了。

On the way back from the pool, Ren tried to jog beside Mira and almost slipped on loose stones. She caught his sleeve before he fell and asked him to slow down. "You do not have to prove you are useful by moving first," she said. Ren looked at the map and found that their careful work had saved the next group a dangerous trip. Thus a slow day could still move everyone forward. He drew a small star beside the place where they had waited. Not every mark had to mean a victory over something. Some marks could mean they had chosen the safer road together.

從水池回程時，任想沿著米拉身旁慢跑，差點踩到鬆石滑倒。米拉抓住他的袖子，請他放慢。「你不必靠搶先行動證明有用。」她說。任看著地圖，發現這天的細心工作已幫下一隊省下一趟危險路程。因此緩慢的一天也能讓眾人前進。他在等候位置旁畫了小星星。不是每個標記都得代表擊敗了什麼；有些標記能代表大家一起選了更安全的路。

The following morning Bell asked Ren to explain the new route to a group of younger students. He feared they would laugh at the hero who carried no sword. Instead they listened closely when he described the turtle and the rising water. A student asked why he had not jumped into the pool to find the treasure. Ren answered, "Because a map you can use tomorrow is better than a story about someone who never came home." The child thought about that for a moment and then asked to learn how to draw the water marks. Ren gave her a pencil. He had never expected teaching to feel like an adventure.

隔天早晨，貝爾請任向一群較年輕的學生解說新路線。他擔心大家會笑沒有劍的勇者，孩子們卻仔細聽他說烏龜與上升的池水。一個學生問他為何不跳進池裡找寶物。任回答：「明天還能用的地圖，比一個再也沒回家的人留下的故事好。」孩子想了一會兒，接著問能不能學畫水位記號。任遞給她鉛筆。他從沒想過教學也能像冒險。
