新手專用黃金陷阱

The Golden Trap for Beginners

轉生後只有一格道具欄！？——我在迷宮裡當上了最弱勇者 · 第 11 / 25 章 · 1000–2000 · 英中對照 · 校訂中

The first lesson on the lower floor began with a sign reading "Beginner Path." Ren was relieved until a gold coin rolled out of the wall. Pebble tried to chase it. Mira caught the little dragon before he stepped on a plate in the floor. "A trap should not become safe simply because the sign is friendly," she said. She emphasized the importance of looking for wires and marks before moving. Ren saw a thin iron line beside the coin. It ran into a box filled with old jam jars. The whole scene looked ridiculous, but the box was connected to a heavy door above them. Ren realized the danger was real even if the decoration looked like a joke.

下層的第一堂課從一塊寫著「新手路線」的牌子開始。任正鬆一口氣，牆裡就滾出一枚金幣。小石想追，米拉在牠踩上地板機關前抓住牠。「招牌寫得友善，不代表陷阱安全。」她說，並強調移動前先看線路和痕跡的重要性。任看見金幣旁有一條細鐵線，連到裝著舊果醬罐的箱子。整個景象荒唐得像玩笑，但箱子連著上方的沉重門板。任明白裝飾可笑，危險卻是真的。

A guard called Toma met them beside a table holding a mug of grape juice. He was in charge of the lower hall, but he did not behave like a soldier from a game. He asked for the permit, checked the names, and listened while Mira described their goal. "The usual route is closed," Toma said. "I can guide you to a social room where workers once met." He pointed to a small religious picture near the door. "Some people treated the maze as a holy place. Others used it for work. Both groups left records." Ren appreciated that he did not make fun of either belief. Their different memories could help the team understand how the hall had worked.

守衛托馬在桌邊等他們，桌上放著一杯葡萄汁。他負責下層大廳，卻不像遊戲裡的士兵。他先查許可和姓名，再聽米拉說明目標。「常用路線封閉了，」托馬說，「我可以帶你們去工人從前開會的交誼室。」他指向門旁的小宗教圖畫。「有人把迷宮當聖地，也有人把它當工作場所。兩邊都留下紀錄。」任很欣賞他沒有嘲笑任何一方的信念。不同記憶都可能幫助大家理解大廳曾如何運作。

In the meeting room, Mira found a damaged page about the moving hall. It said the hall could open only when three bells received a true signal at the same time. The old song was not magic by itself. It was a rhythm workers used to remember the safe order. "So our success depends on a signal, not a wish," Ren said. Toma nodded. A loud bang made everyone duck, but it was only a loose pipe that had shot steam into a metal bucket. Pebble looked disappointed that there was no monster to fight. Mira tested the door with a safe line and wrote down the result. They had learned the first rule without touching the main machine.

交誼室裡，米拉找到一頁受損的移動大廳說明。上面寫著大廳只有在三個鐘同時收到真正訊號時才會開啟。古歌本身不是魔法，而是工人記住安全次序的方法。「所以成功要靠訊號，不是許願。」任說。托馬點頭。突然一聲巨響，大家趕緊低頭，結果只是鬆管把蒸氣噴進金屬桶。小石因為沒有怪物可打，露出失望表情。米拉用安全繩測試門，記下結果。小隊沒碰主機，就先弄懂第一條規則。

Before leaving, Ren asked Toma whether the room's old rules could be trusted. The guard answered that trust came from checking them, not from the age of the paper. Mira agreed. She asked to keep a copy of the page, and Toma signed the record. Ren thought about how many stories told heroes to take treasure and run. Here he had found no shining sword, only a careful person who would not let him skip a line on a form. Oddly, he felt safer for it. Pebble finally got a reward: a piece of chalk. He ate it so quickly that Ren wondered if the dragon had a second mouth hidden somewhere.

離開前，任問托馬能不能相信舊規則。守衛說，信任來自重新檢查，而不是紙張有多年老。米拉表示同意，請求留一份影本，托馬也在紀錄上簽名。任想到自己讀過多少故事，都是勇者拿了寶藏便跑。他這次沒有得到發光神劍，只遇見一個不讓他跳過表格任何一行的細心人。奇怪的是，這反而讓他安心。小石終於得到獎勵：一塊粉筆。牠吃得太快，任開始懷疑牠是不是藏著第二張嘴。

Toma took them to a small training range beside the old hall. A painted target stood against the wall, and a wooden device was designed to shoot a harmless cork toward it. He explained that the range was once used to teach beginners how to recognize pressure before handling real tools. "I emphasize this because mistakes here must be safe mistakes," he said. Ren tried the device and missed badly. Mira corrected his stance without laughing. Pebble picked up the cork and brought it back like a dog. Ren thanked him, then wondered whether a baby stone dragon would be offended by that comparison. The creature only wanted another chance to chase the cork.

托馬帶他們去舊大廳旁的小訓練場。牆邊有畫好的靶，木製裝置能發射無害軟木塞。托馬說那裡從前用來讓新手在接觸真正工具前，學會認識壓力。「我要特別強調，因為這裡的錯誤必須是安全的錯誤。」他說。任試了裝置，偏得離譜。米拉幫他調整姿勢，沒有嘲笑。小石像狗一樣撿回軟木塞。任道謝，接著擔心把小龍和狗相比會不會冒犯牠。小石只想再追一次。

Later Toma showed them the room's old records about religion and public work. Some families had come there to pray before long journeys, while others had come to repair wheels. The same place had held different kinds of hope. Ren asked whether that was a problem. "Not when people respect each other's space," Toma answered. Mira marked the old prayer corner on the map so visitors would understand why it should not be used for storage. Ren put away his pencil and looked at the quiet hall. The floor was not a dungeon level waiting to be beaten. It was a place where generations had worked and worried, just as people did now.

後來托馬讓他們看宗教與公共工作的舊紀錄。有些家庭在長途旅行前來這裡祈禱，另一些人則來修理車輪。同一地方容納了不同的希望。任問這樣會不會有問題。「只要大家尊重彼此空間就不會。」托馬回答。米拉在地圖上標出舊祈禱角落，讓訪客知道那裡為何不該改作倉庫。任收好鉛筆，看著安靜大廳。這不是等待被擊敗的地下城關卡，而是一個數代人都曾工作與憂慮的地方，和今天的人一樣。
