理髮師與假地圖

The Barber and the False Map

轉生後只有一格道具欄！？——我在迷宮裡當上了最弱勇者 · 第 2 / 25 章 · 1000–2000 · 英中對照 · 校訂中

Ren woke in a room above a barber shop, because the town had no budget for a hero's palace. The barber, Old Len, cut his hair without asking for a reward. "Your haircut is not a magic spell," Len said, "but you can see better now." Mira arrived carrying a wooden board and an older edition of the maze map. She placed them at an angle and showed Ren a strange fact. On the new map, a huge lantern stood at the north crossing. On the older one, the same spot was marked with a deer and a dove. "The maps should not disagree about a fixed sign," Mira said. Ren appreciated that she had checked both versions before bringing him the problem.

任在理髮店樓上的房間醒來，因為小鎮根本沒錢為勇者準備宮殿。老理髮師倫免費幫他剪髮。「髮型不是魔法，」倫說，「但至少現在看得清楚了。」米拉帶來一塊木板，以及較舊版本的迷宮地圖。她把兩張圖斜放在桌上，讓任看一個怪事。新圖上，北方路口畫著一盞巨大燈籠；舊圖的同一處卻畫著鹿與鴿子。「固定的路標不應該在兩張圖上完全不同。」米拉說。任很欣賞她先比對版本，才把問題帶來。

At the market they asked an organization of local guides for help. Its president, a serious woman named Bell, worried that opening the maze would hurt the minority of families living near the old tunnel. "I want the truth," she said, "not another beautiful promise." The new lantern had been placed by an unknown worker the previous week. Beneath it was a sour smell, as if old fruit had been left in a box. Pebble found a black spot on the wall and began to scratch it. Mira stopped him. "Avoid breaking a wall before we know what is behind it," she warned. Ren thought she sounded exactly like the kind of teacher he had needed in his old world.

兩人在市場向當地嚮導組織求助。主席貝爾是一位嚴肅的女性，她擔心打開迷宮會傷害住在舊隧道旁的少數居民家庭。「我要真相，」她說，「不要另一個漂亮承諾。」新燈籠是一週前由不明工人放上去的。燈下有股酸味，像把壞水果留在箱子裡。小石發現牆上的黑點，開始用爪子刮。米拉立刻阻止牠。「不知道牆後有什麼，就別先把牆打破。」她提醒。任覺得她很像自己在原本世界裡需要的那種老師。

Len joined them with a small textbook about old road signs. The deer meant that a path was wide enough for animals; the dove meant a safe place above water. A lantern had a different meaning: it was a warning about smoke. The symbols still existed in the guide's records, even if most people had forgotten them. "A particular shape can change the whole message," Len said. Mira asked Ren to hold the book while she made a sketch. He watched her hands, careful and steady. For a moment he forgot that he was supposed to be a chosen hero. He was simply a helpful member of a team. That felt much less painful than pretending to know everything.

倫帶著一本介紹舊路標的教科書加入他們。鹿代表路寬得能讓動物通過；鴿子代表水面上方的安全休息處。燈籠的意思不同，是煙霧警告。這些符號仍存在嚮導紀錄中，只是多數人已忘記。「特定的圖形會改變整段訊息。」倫說。米拉請任拿著書，自己動手畫草圖。任看著她穩定而細心的雙手，一時忘記自己被叫作選中勇者。他只是隊伍裡一個能幫上忙的人。這感覺比假裝無所不知舒服多了。

The group decided not to enter the crossing yet. Instead Bell invited residents to share what they had seen. An elderly woman remembered a cart of lanterns arriving at night. A boy said he had watched a worker cover the original sign with fresh paint. Each story was small, but together they formed a clear picture. Ren was excited until Mira reminded him that a memory was not final proof. They would have to look for the cart records. Pebble offered to help by eating the corner of the map. "No," three people said at once. The dragon looked offended, then fell asleep on Ren's shoe.

大家決定暫時不進入路口，而是請貝爾邀居民分享見聞。一位老婦記得夜裡曾有一車燈籠送進來；一個男孩說他看見工人用新漆蓋掉原本的標誌。每段記憶都不大，卻拼出清楚的輪廓。任正要興奮起來，米拉提醒他：記憶不是最後的證據。他們還需要查車輛紀錄。小石自告奮勇，方法是啃掉地圖角落。「不行！」三個人同時喊。小龍一臉受傷，接著在任的鞋子上睡著了。

That evening Len showed Ren a page from the old edition. It described a service door below the sign and a narrow path behind it. Ren understood why the lantern had been placed so carefully. Someone wanted travelers to believe the path was dangerous, even when it might be safe. He looked out at the moon, wondering who would gain from such a lie. Mira came to tell him that she would lead tomorrow's walk. "If you would rather stay here, I won't blame you," she said. Ren raised his only weapon, a spoon from dinner. "I have an empty slot and a brave spoon. How could I miss it?" Mira laughed, and Ren decided that laughter was better than any heroic title.

當晚，倫拿出舊版手冊的一頁，描述標記下方有扇工作門，門後還有一條窄路。任終於懂得新燈籠為何被放得那麼仔細。有人想讓旅人相信那條路很危險，哪怕它可能是安全的。他望著月亮，想知道誰會從謊言中得利。米拉來告訴他，隔天由她帶路。「如果你想留在這裡，我不會怪你。」她說。任舉起晚餐用的湯匙，當作唯一武器。「我有一格空道具欄和勇敢湯匙，怎麼能不去？」米拉笑了，任也覺得這笑聲比任何勇者稱號都好。

The next morning, Ren asked Len whether old signs could really help them more than a bright spell. Len pointed at the hands of a clock above the shop. "I appreciate a good question," the barber replied. "Look at the clock. Its face tells us the time only because someone keeps the parts moving." Mira joined them, holding a fresh copy of the map. She reminded Ren that danger and care could exist in the same place. "We should not tell people there is no risk," she said. "We should show them where the risk is." Ren put a mark beside the lantern and promised to check it before any visitor followed them.

隔天早晨，任問倫，舊標誌真能比耀眼法術更有幫助嗎？理髮師指著店上的時鐘。「我很欣賞好問題，」倫回答，「看那時鐘，它能告訴我們時間，是因為有人維持零件運作。」米拉拿著新版地圖加入，提醒任危險與照顧可能同時存在於一個地方。「不能告訴人們完全沒有風險，」她說，「而是要指出風險在哪裡。」任在燈籠旁加上記號，答應在訪客跟隨前先確認。
