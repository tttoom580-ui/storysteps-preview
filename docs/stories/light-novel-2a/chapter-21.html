The Vote Before the Festival

轉生後只有一格道具欄！？——我在迷宮裡當上了最弱勇者 · 第 21 / 25 章 · 1000–2000 · 英中對照 · 校訂中

The town prepared for its spring festival while the safety team wrote its final plan. At a small bookstore, Ren watched children paint signs with chalk and bright brushes. Pebble tried to eat the chalk before the paint was dry. "That is not how artists earn income," Ren told him. The shopkeeper laughed and asked him to serve supper at the festival if he wanted a real job. Ren said he might agree if the soup did not boil over again. Across the street, a small drama group practiced a play about the old moving hall. A female actor played a worker who had refused an unsafe order. Ren was glad the story gave ordinary people an important role.

小鎮一邊準備春季祭典，安全小組一邊寫最後方案。任在小書店看孩子們用粉筆與亮色刷子做指示牌。小石想趁顏料還沒乾就把粉筆吃掉。「藝術家不是這樣賺收入的。」任對牠說。店主笑著問，若任想找真正工作，祭典能否幫忙供應晚餐。任說只要湯不再煮到溢出，自己也許願意。街對面的小劇團正排演移動大廳的故事，一名女性演員扮演拒絕不安全命令的工人。任很高興普通人在這故事裡也有重要角色。

Bell called a public vote on the repair plan. Some people wanted to remove the old machine completely. Others wanted to protect it as part of the town's wealth. The engineers explained the internal parts and the limits of the new support. A former military worker showed a postcard from his nephew, who had used the hall to reach school during winter. "This is not a question of one person's comfort," he said. "It is a question of who can travel." Mira asked that whoever had a concern be allowed to speak. Even those who disliked the project were given time. Ren had expected angry shouting, but the discussion was careful and surprisingly kind.

貝爾召開公開表決，討論修復方案。有些人想把舊機器完全拆除；另一些人想把它當成城鎮的共同財產保護。工程師說明內部零件與新支架的限制。一名退役軍人拿出外甥寄的明信片，孩子冬天曾靠大廳通學。「這不是一個人舒不舒服的問題，」他說，「而是誰能夠出行。」米拉要求任何有疑慮的人都能發言，即使反對者也分得到時間。任原以為會聽見憤怒吼叫，結果討論卻慎重且出乎意料地友善。

Before the vote, a rat ran under the table and sent everyone reaching for their bags. Ren held up a needle from the repair kit and said he could use it to fix a loose sign, but not to chase an animal. Toma caught the rat in a safe box and carried it outside. The silly interruption broke the tension. Then Bell read the choices: repair the hall under independent checks, close it permanently, or delay for another season. The repair plan received the most votes, but its approval came with conditions. No passenger could enter until the engineers signed the full inspection, and every future warning had to be made public.

投票前，一隻老鼠竄到桌下，嚇得大家抓緊行李。任舉起工具包裡的針，說它能修鬆動告示，卻不適合拿來追動物。托馬用安全箱抓住老鼠，帶到外頭。這段滑稽插曲讓氣氛稍微放鬆。貝爾宣讀三種選項：在獨立查驗下修好大廳、永久封閉，或再延一季。修復方案獲得最多票，但核准附帶條件：工程師完成全部驗收簽署之前，不得載客；未來任何警告都必須公開。

Ren helped clean the meeting room while the people went home. Argo offered to stay and work. Mira accepted his help with the chairs, though she did not act as if nothing had happened. "You can do the next right thing," she told him, "but it does not remove the last wrong one." Argo nodded. Outside, Pebble sat on a festival flag and looked like a very small ruler. Ren picked him up before he could fall. The dragon sneezed glitter into his hair. "Wonderful," Ren said. "I have been crowned by the kingdom's most expensive rock." Mira laughed all the way back to the guide house.

民眾回家後，任幫忙清理會議室。阿爾戈主動留下工作。米拉接受他幫忙搬椅子，卻沒有裝作以前的事沒發生。「你能把下一件事做對，」她告訴他，「但不能因此消除上一次的錯。」阿爾戈點頭。外頭，小石坐在祭典旗子上，像個很小的統治者。任趕快抱起牠，免得摔下來。小龍對他頭髮打出亮粉噴嚏。「太好了，」任說，「我被全王國最貴的石頭加冕了。」米拉一路笑回嚮導屋。

In a quiet corner of the bookstore, Ren found an old brush with a wooden handle. The shopkeeper said the tool belonged to the woman who had painted the first maze signs. She had used bright colors so workers could find their way even in weak light. Ren asked whether the shop could place a small note beside the brush. He wanted visitors to know that simple objects had a history. The shopkeeper agreed, and Mira helped write the note. Pebble climbed onto the counter and stared at the brush, hoping it was a snack. Ren moved it safely away before the little dragon could test the taste.

書店安靜角落裡，任找到一把木柄舊刷子。店主說，那是繪製第一批迷宮標誌的女性留下的工具。她用鮮明顏色，好讓工人在暗光下也能找到方向。任問能不能在刷子旁放一張小卡，讓訪客知道普通物品也有歷史。店主答應，米拉協助寫說明。小石爬上櫃檯，盯著刷子，希望它是點心。任趕緊移開工具，免得小龍實際試吃。

The next day children visited the store in groups and asked questions about the old maze. Ren showed them a simple drawing of the safety route and asked what they would do if a warning light turned red. Every child answered, "Stop and ask for help." He was surprised at how good it felt to hear that answer. A small lesson would not repair a broken machine, yet it could keep a curious visitor from turning a mistake into an accident. Mira stood in the doorway and watched. She did not interrupt the class, but afterward she told Ren he should think about teaching when the repair was finished.

隔天孩子們分組來店裡，詢問舊迷宮的事。任給他們看簡單安全路線圖，問警示燈變紅該怎麼辦。每個孩子都回答：「停下來，找人幫忙。」這個答案讓他意外開心。一堂小課修不好壞機器，卻可能阻止好奇訪客把錯誤變意外。米拉在門口看著，沒有打斷。課後她告訴任，等修復結束，他可以考慮教學工作。
