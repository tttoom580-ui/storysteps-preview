A Blank Page Can Speak

轉生後只有一格道具欄！？——我在迷宮裡當上了最弱勇者 · 第 12 / 25 章 · 1000–2000 · 英中對照 · 校訂中

The next stop was the capital storehouse, a large room where old customers had once paid for food. Most shelves were blank and dusty. Mira found a notebook with pages torn out. "We cannot blame anyone yet," she said. "But a missing record can show where to look." Ren measured the gaps between the remaining pages. Several had been removed at the same time, and the ink marks on the next sheet showed the direction of the writer's hand. It was not enough to know the name, but it told them that the book had been used recently. Pebble sat on the desk and looked proud of helping, although his only action had been to sit still.

下一站是首都舊倉庫，從前顧客會在這裡付錢領食物。如今架子幾乎全空，積滿灰塵。米拉找到一本被撕掉數頁的筆記簿。「現在不能責怪任何人，」她說，「但消失的紀錄能告訴我們該往哪裡找。」任測量剩餘頁面之間的空隙。好幾頁顯然在同一時間被取走，而下一頁的墨痕顯示寫字者運筆的方向。還不足以辨識人名，卻證明這本簿子最近仍有人使用。小石坐在桌上，一臉自己幫了大忙的樣子，雖然牠唯一的行動就是沒亂跑。

A countryside customer arrived with a basket of fries and pudding for the workers. She said an old dentist had once used the storehouse as a clinic during a dangerous winter. "He did not have many tools," she recalled, "but he knew how to keep people calm." She showed the team a teapot with a small drawing on its side. The drawing was a simple measurement guide: fill to the first mark for one cup, to the second mark for two. Mira regarded the object with respect. It was a reminder that careful measurements were part of ordinary life. Ren felt less scared of the giant machine when he imagined its workers doing simple tasks like making tea.

一名鄉下顧客提著薯條和布丁來慰勞工人。她說危險寒冬時，一位老牙醫曾把倉庫當臨時診所。「工具不多，」她回憶，「但他懂得安撫病人。」她拿出一把側面畫著小圖的茶壺。圖畫是簡單的量測指引：水到第一線是一杯，到第二線是兩杯。米拉十分尊重這個物品。它提醒大家，精確測量也是普通生活的一部分。任想像大機器的工人也會做泡茶這樣的簡單事，心裡便沒那麼害怕了。

A sudden sound came from the back door. Toma ran in wearing one slipper and one boot. He had been sent to settle an argument between two groups of cleaners. One wanted to remove all the old tables; the other wanted to keep them for study. Mira asked everyone to stop moving furniture until the records were checked. "Respect is not the same as agreeing," she said. "We can listen and still choose a careful plan." Ren drew a simple map of the tables and their marks. Behind one table they found a brass bell with a paper tag. Its number matched the second line of the song they had copied from the kitchen.

後門突然傳來聲響，托馬一腳穿拖鞋、一腳穿靴子跑進來。他剛被派去調停兩群清潔工的爭論。一方想把舊桌子全部搬走，另一方想留下研究。米拉請大家停止移動家具，先查紀錄。「尊重不等於同意，」她說，「我們可以聽完再謹慎決定。」任畫下桌子與標記的簡單位置圖。他們在一張桌子後找到黃銅鐘，鐘上掛著紙牌，數字正好對應廚房抄來的歌曲第二行。

The bell was too heavy for Ren's slot, so the team covered it and left it in place. Toma asked the storehouse workers to guard the room until an engineer could arrive. Ren wondered if the old dentist had ever imagined that his teapot would help a group understand a giant road machine. Mira smiled and said history was full of small links like that. They copied every mark before leaving. On the walk back, Pebble tripped over Toma's loose slipper. The guard looked at the dragon, looked at his own feet, and finally laughed. For the first time that day, everyone felt the work was moving toward an answer.

黃銅鐘超過任的道具欄重量限制，大家只把它蓋好，留在原處。托馬請倉庫工人守著房間，等待工程師到場。任好奇那位老牙醫有沒有想過，自己的茶壺會幫助一群人理解巨大的道路機器。米拉笑著說，歷史本來就充滿這種小連結。離開前，他們抄下所有標記。回程時，小石被托馬鬆掉的拖鞋絆倒。守衛看看小龍，又看看自己的腳，終於笑了。那天第一次，大家覺得事情正在朝答案前進。

Ren hardly slept that night. The danger of missing records kept circling in his mind, but Mira told him to regard the case as a puzzle with people inside it, not a contest with a hidden villain. She asked him to list what they knew and what they did not know. The missing pages might show an accident, a repair, or a simple mistake. Until they saw them, every explanation was only a guess. Ren wrote two columns in his notebook and found the work calming. The next morning he brought the list to Toma, who checked the dates without making fun of the hero's messy handwriting.

那晚任幾乎沒睡。消失紀錄的危險一直在腦中打轉，米拉卻請他把事情看成有人牽涉其中的謎題，而不是和隱藏反派比輸贏。她要他列出已知與未知。失落頁面可能記著事故、修復，或單純錯誤；親眼看見前，任何解釋都只是猜測。任在筆記本畫出兩欄，工作竟讓他平靜下來。隔天早晨他把清單交給托馬，守衛核對日期，沒有嘲笑勇者凌亂的字。

The archive keeper later allowed them to examine an old ink bottle and a torn cover. The color of the ink matched the newest lines on the notebook, but it was a common kind sold in town. Mira wrote down that limit too. "A clue is useful because of what it shows," she said, "not because it allows us to make any story we want." Ren looked from the ink to the quiet workers by the door. He understood that careful thinking was a kind of kindness. It kept people from being blamed for things they had not done. He had come expecting adventure; he was learning patience instead.

檔案管理員後來允許他們檢查舊墨水瓶與撕裂封皮。墨色和筆記本最新幾行相符，但那是鎮上常見的墨水。米拉也記下這個限制。「線索之所以有用，在於它真正證明什麼，」她說，「不是讓我們隨便編想聽的故事。」任看看墨水，又看看門邊安靜的工人，明白細心思考也是一種善意。它能讓人免於被指控自己沒做過的事。他來這裡原本期待冒險，學到的卻是耐心。
