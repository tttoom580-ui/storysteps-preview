No Hero Above the Law

轉生後只有一格道具欄！？——我在迷宮裡當上了最弱勇者 · 第 10 / 25 章 · 1000–2000 · 英中對照 · 校訂中

Ren expected the council to congratulate the team after the kitchen rescue. Instead a new law appeared on the wall of town hall: no one could enter the maze without a written permit. The order concerned Mira because her guide papers had only been approved for the first floor. "We must not hide this," she said. "We ask for permission and show our records." Ren was active in helping her arrange the notebooks, but he also worried about the distance between the town and the trapped workers farther below. At the meeting he spoke of the single voice they had heard through the repaired tube. "One person is enough to matter," he told the council. The room fell into silence.

任以為救出廚房後，議會會稱讚他們。沒想到市政廳牆上貼出新法律：沒有書面許可，任何人不得進入迷宮。米拉很在意，因為她的嚮導證只核准第一層。「我們不能隱瞞，」她說，「應該提出申請，拿出紀錄。」任積極幫她整理筆記本，卻也擔心鎮上與下層受困工人之間的距離。會議上，他提到大家曾從修好的傳話管聽見的一個聲音。「只要有一個人，就值得在意。」他對議員說。房間陷入安靜。

Bell invited the engineers to compare the old and new maps on a flat table. Each flag showed a tested path. An arrow pointed forward to the kitchen, while a second line indicated the water room. The new rules were not a punishment for success; they were meant to prevent accidents when more people entered. Ren began to understand the cycle: a new rescue brought new interest, and new interest brought new risk. He asked whether the town could gain more safety by training additional guides. Bell agreed, but warned that training could not occur in a single afternoon. People who wanted riches from the maze would have to wait with everyone else.

貝爾請工程師把新舊地圖放在平桌上比較。每面小旗都代表已測試路線。一支箭頭指向廚房，第二條線標示水室。新規則不是懲罰成功，而是避免更多人進入時發生意外。任開始理解這個循環：新救援帶來更多關注，更多關注又帶來新風險。他問能否訓練更多嚮導來提高安全。貝爾同意，卻提醒訓練不可能在一下午完成。想從迷宮獲得財富的人，也必須和大家一起等待。

A young merchant in a fine suit offered Ren a wallet full of coins if he would take a private group through a secret track. Ren refused. "My skill can hold one small thing," he said, "but it cannot hold your whole promise." Mira tried not to smile. The merchant left angry. Later a council clerk asked for information about the missing plates and the old song. Ren handed over Mira's copies, not the original pages. It was a small decision, but one they had learned from previous mistakes. When they returned to the street, Pebble waved his tail at a pair of children. They bowed to the "hero dragon." Ren wondered whether the little creature would become more famous than him.

一名穿精緻西裝的年輕商人拿著裝滿硬幣的錢包，想請任私下帶人走祕密路線。任拒絕：「我的能力只能放一件小東西，可放不下你整份承諾。」米拉努力忍住笑。商人氣憤地走了。後來，議會書記詢問歌板與老歌的資訊。任交出米拉準備的副本，而非原始頁面。這是小小的決定，卻是他們從過往錯誤學來的做法。回到街上，小石對兩個孩子搖尾巴。孩子向「勇者小龍」鞠躬。任開始懷疑這個小傢伙會不會比自己更有名。

At dusk a messenger arrived from the valley, carrying a request from workers who lived near the moving hall. The hall had stopped changing rooms, and families on both sides could no longer visit each other. The letter did not ask for a great hero. It asked for safe passage and clear signs. Bell read it twice, then granted Mira a limited permit for a survey team. Ren felt proud, but he knew the work would be harder from here on. Mira looked at him over the table. "Will you keep going?" she asked. He touched the empty square floating beside his hand. "Yes," he said. "I finally know what my one slot is for. It gives me a reason to work with other people."

黃昏時，一名信使從山谷送來移動大廳附近工人的請求。大廳已停止改變房間，兩側家庭再也無法互訪。信裡沒有要求偉大的勇者，而是請求安全通道與清楚標示。貝爾讀了兩遍，核准米拉組成小型勘查隊的限期許可。任很驕傲，也知道工作只會更困難。米拉隔著桌子看他。「你還願意繼續嗎？」任碰了碰手邊浮現的空方格。「願意，」他說，「我終於知道這一格的用處了。它提醒我要和別人一起做事。」

Mira opened her notebook after the meeting and wrote down every concern the workers had raised. One woman asked whether the rule would treat people differently because of their sex. Bell answered that permits depended on training and safety, not whether a person was male or female. That answer made Ren feel more confident about the town. A clear flag at the entrance would indicate when the route was open. Everyone could read the sign, and visitors would have a way to ask questions. Ren helped the clerk make copies, then returned to the empty street to think. The city had not become safer because it found a hero. It was becoming safer because people had begun to listen.

會議後米拉打開筆記本，寫下工人提出的每個疑慮。一名女性問，規則會不會因人的性別而差別待遇。貝爾回答，許可看的是訓練與安全，不是男性或女性。這讓任對小鎮更有信心。入口的明顯旗號會指出路線是否開放，人人都能看懂，也能提出問題。任幫書記影印資料，接著回到安靜街道思考。城市不是因為找到勇者才變安全，而是因為人們開始願意聆聽。

On the way home he saw a family reading the new notice together. The father explained the symbols to his child, while the mother checked the time on her watch. They did not need to know Ren's name. They only needed instructions that could be used without special power. Ren stood nearby, holding his empty lunch box, and felt unexpectedly happy. Pebble climbed onto his shoulder, perhaps hoping someone would offer a snack. Mira came from the office and asked why he was smiling. "I think we did something good," he answered. She looked at the family, then gave a small nod. For a moment neither of them needed any more words.

回家路上，任看見一家人一起閱讀新告示。父親向孩子解釋符號，母親則核對手錶時間。他們不必知道任叫什麼，只需要不用特殊力量也能遵循的指示。任抱著空午餐盒站在旁邊，忽然很快樂。小石爬上肩膀，大概希望有人送點心。米拉從辦公室走來，問他為何笑。「我想我們做了一件好事。」任回答。她看看那家人，輕輕點頭。那一刻兩人都不需要更多話。
