絕不出售的小龍

The Dragon Who Wouldn't Be Sold

轉生後只有一格道具欄！？——我在迷宮裡當上了最弱勇者 · 第 16 / 25 章 · 1000–2000 · 英中對照 · 校訂中

A wealthy host invited the team to a dinner in the northern hall. The room was decorated with comic pictures of brave dragons and rich hunters. Ren thought it looked like the final scene of an old game. The host offered to pay the town's debt if Ren would give him Pebble as a pet. "He is not furniture," Ren answered. "He is our friend, and he has his own choices." Mira supported Ren, but she added that they should not speak for the dragon without watching what he wanted. Pebble made his feelings clear by climbing up Ren's coat and hiding his face. The host looked disappointed. Ren had never been so proud of winning an argument over a creature that ate chalk.

一位富有的主人邀請小隊到北方大廳吃晚餐。房裡掛滿勇敢巨龍與富豪獵人的漫畫圖。任覺得這像舊遊戲的最終場景。主人提出替小鎮償還債務，條件是把小石賣給他當寵物。「牠不是家具，」任回答，「牠是朋友，也有自己的選擇。」米拉支持任，但提醒大家不能只代替小龍說話，也要看牠自己想要什麼。小石立刻爬上任的外套，把臉藏起來，態度十分明確。主人滿臉失望。任第一次因贏了一場關於愛吃粉筆生物的爭論而如此驕傲。

After dinner, a woman from the coast brought news of trash floating into the underground river. An eagle had been seen carrying bits of paper toward the cave. "This environment is part of our home," she said. "You cannot call it a waste place just because it is below our feet." Mira asked how the rubbish reached the river and learned that a slide from an old market had been left open. A shell caught in the gate showed the direction of the water. The team went to inspect the site with the town guard. Pebble sniffed at a strawberry box, then ignored the river, which made Ren glad. A dragon could be curious without being useful at every moment.

晚餐後，一名海岸女性帶來消息：垃圾漂進了地下河，有人看見老鷹把紙片叼向洞穴。「這環境也是我們的家，」她說，「不能因為它在腳下，就叫它廢棄區。」米拉問垃圾如何進河，得知舊市場的滑道一直敞開。一枚卡在閘門的貝殼顯示水流方向。小隊與鎮衛兵一起查看現場。小石聞了聞草莓箱，接著完全不理會河水，讓任鬆了口氣。龍可以好奇，但不必每時每刻都派上用場。

At the slide, Ren found a box of papers marked "Society of Maze Workers." The pages described an effort to win independence from an old merchant group. The writing was highly formal, yet one line was easy to understand: every worker could refuse an unsafe task. Mira rubbed dust from the cover and read it twice. The promise mattered because several recent workers had said they were told to keep silent. The town policeman copied the document and asked everyone to leave the original in a sealed case. Ren felt the weight of the decision. They were no longer just looking for a song. They were finding a history people had tried not to see.

在滑道旁，任找到一盒標著「迷宮工人協會」的紙張。內容寫著工人努力爭取不受舊商團控制的自主權。文字非常正式，卻有一句容易理解：每位工人都可以拒絕不安全工作。米拉抹掉封面的灰，讀了兩遍。這份承諾很重要，因為最近已有幾名工人說自己被要求保持沉默。鎮警察抄下文件，請大家把原件封進保管盒。任感到這個決定的分量。他們不只是在找一首歌，而是在找一段人們不願看見的歷史。

That night Mira told Ren she had joined the guide school because her mother once worked in the moving hall. Her mother had left after an accident and never wanted to speak about the place again. "I thought I could fix the story for her," Mira admitted. "But I cannot decide how she should remember it." Ren said he did not know the right answer. He only offered to sit beside her while she wrote a letter. Pebble curled between them, warm like a small stone stove. Ren looked at the sky and wondered whether another universe had a version of him who could give better advice. For now, being present was all he could do.

當晚米拉告訴任，她之所以念嚮導學校，是因為母親曾在移動大廳工作。母親在一次事故後離職，此後不願再提那裡。「我以為能替她修好那段故事，」米拉承認，「但我不能決定她該如何記得它。」任說自己不知道正確答案，只願意陪她坐著寫信。小石縮在兩人中間，暖得像一個小石爐。任望著天，心想另一個宇宙會不會有更懂得安慰人的自己。眼下，他只能好好陪伴。

The host had offered the team a smooth road and a warm room for the night, but Mira knew a gift could come with requirements that had not been discussed. She laid the invitation on the table and asked Ren what he thought. He wanted to rub the dust from his hands. He rubbed his forehead and said the offer sounded useful, yet it would be wrong to make Pebble part of a private bargain. The host did not require a written reply, but Mira chose to send one. Mira agreed. She added that the team should not lay all its worries on the little dragon simply because he could not explain himself in words. They decided to thank the host for the room and refuse any deal involving the animal. Their independence mattered more than a rich dinner.

主人提議讓小隊使用平順道路，還能在暖房過夜，但米拉知道禮物可能附帶未說清楚的要求。她把邀請放在桌上，問任的看法。任想把手上的灰擦掉，又揉了揉額頭，說提議雖然有用，卻不應把小石變成私人交易的一部分。主人沒有要求書面回覆，但米拉仍決定寄上一封。米拉同意，並補充不能只因小龍不會用人話解釋，就把所有煩惱壓到牠身上。兩人決定感謝房間的好意，拒絕任何涉及動物買賣的條件。自主權比昂貴晚餐更重要。

The next day the town guard checked the market slide with the engineers. It was designed to carry waste in sealed boxes, but the cover had been left open for many weeks. Ren helped place signs and kept visitors away while the workers cleaned the area. Mira asked the company to keep a public record of the repair so the same mistake would not be repeated. "It is not enough to promise we will remember," she said. "We need a way for the next shift to see what has changed." Ren thought of the old song, which also carried a practical memory across years. Stories, notes, and songs could all help, if people used them honestly.

隔天鎮衛兵與工程師一起檢查市場滑道。原設計是用封閉箱子載運廢物，但蓋子已經敞開好幾週。工人清掃時，任幫忙設標示，讓訪客遠離。米拉要求公司公開維修紀錄，避免再犯同樣錯誤。「光承諾記得不夠，」她說，「要讓下一班人看得出來變動了什麼。」任想到老歌同樣讓實用記憶跨越多年。故事、筆記和歌曲都能幫忙，只要人們誠實使用。
