小石的第一次真正飛行

Pebble's First True Flight

轉生後只有一格道具欄！？——我在迷宮裡當上了最弱勇者 · 第 14 / 25 章 · 1000–2000 · 英中對照 · 校訂中

A dragon appeared on the edge of the upper hall, spreading wide stone wings. Ren felt his heart stop. Then the creature turned, and he saw that it was a statue. Pebble tried to greet the statue like a long-lost relative. He rubbed his head against its foot and sneezed. The sound woke a small light inside the wall. "That is not proof of magic," Mira said, "but it gives us a place to look." She checked the wiring while Ren held a lamp. A faded print on the wall described the existence of a bridge used by flying animals. It had been closed because the edge was unsafe. They could not cross it, even if Pebble wished to fly.

上層大廳邊緣出現一隻巨龍，張著寬大的石翼。任心臟幾乎停了一拍。等牠轉過來——不，是任換了角度——他才看出那只是雕像。小石卻像見到失散親人般上前問好，拿頭磨蹭雕像腳趾，還打了個噴嚏。牆裡的小燈因此亮起。「這還不能證明是魔法，」米拉說，「但告訴我們該看哪裡。」她檢查線路，任舉燈照明。牆上褪色的印刷文字提到飛行動物曾使用的橋，但橋因邊緣不安全而關閉。就算小石想飛，他們也不能過去。

A young engineer named Iko climbed upstairs with a roll of paper. She had been asked to provide a brief examination of the bridge. "It is childish to call every high place a secret level," she said when Ren suggested a hidden prize. Her words stung, but she was right about the danger. Iko showed them a diagram with a diamond-shaped support. The support had shifted after years of rain. They needed to ease the load before anyone could work there. Ren offered to hold the lamp, carry tools, and keep the path clear. He could not help by pretending to be stronger than he was.

年輕工程師伊可拿著一捲紙上樓，她受託簡短檢查這座橋。任提議可能藏著獎品，她直說：「把每個高處都當祕密關卡太幼稚了。」話有點刺耳，但她指出的危險是真的。伊可展示圖上菱形的支架，支架受多年雨水影響，已經偏移。任何人上去工作之前，都得先減輕負重。任自願舉燈、搬工具、保持通道暢通。他知道假裝比自己強並沒有幫助。

While they worked, a deaf visitor arrived with a note asking whether the highway above the hall was open. Mira wrote a clear reply rather than shouting. Ren watched and learned how to greet someone without assuming they could hear. The visitor smiled and gave them garlic bread and tofu from a food stall. "This is a better reward than a flying sword," Ren whispered. Pebble heard the word "bread" and tried to climb into the bag. Iko caught him gently. She said that work teams had to prove a bridge was safe by testing it under known conditions. A dramatic flight would not count as evidence.

工作時，一名聽障訪客拿紙條來問上方公路是否開放。米拉用清楚文字回答，而不是提高音量。任看著她，學到不該假設每個人都聽得見。訪客微笑，送給他們攤位買來的蒜香麵包與豆腐。「這比飛劍更像獎賞。」任低聲說。小石聽見「麵包」就想爬進袋子，伊可溫柔地抓住牠。她解釋工作隊要在已知條件下測試，才能證明橋梁安全；一場戲劇化飛行不能當證據。

When the inspection ended, Iko approved only a short section of the bridge for workers. Mira took the note and placed it on the public board. Ren looked at the dragon statue one more time. The light inside the wall followed a pattern: one long flash, two short flashes, then silence. It matched the third line of the old song. Their next clue was not behind the bridge at all. It had been in the wall beside them. Pebble lifted his little wings and jumped from a low step. He landed safely on Ren's bag, having flown about the length of a spoon. "Amazing," Ren said. "Our party now has air travel. Very short air travel."

檢查結束，伊可只核准工作人員使用橋的一小段。米拉把通知貼上公開板。任又看了一眼龍雕像。牆內燈光出現固定節奏：一長、兩短，然後安靜。那正好符合舊歌的第三行。下一個線索根本不在橋後，就在他們身旁的牆裡。小石張開小翅膀，從低矮階梯跳下，安全落在任的袋子上，飛了大約一把湯匙的距離。「太厲害了，」任說，「我們隊伍現在有空中交通了，只是航程非常短。」

On the way down from the statue, Ren felt a sharp pain in his side. He wondered whether the liver was in that place, then realized he knew far less about his own body than a hero should. Mira asked him to rest and drink water. The day's activity had been tiring, and she refused to let pride become another injury. Iko checked that he could walk normally before the group continued. Ren tried to whisper a joke about a brave warrior defeated by stairs. Mira heard it and replied, "Even a brave warrior needs lunch." He was grateful she did not turn every small weakness into a grand speech.

從雕像下樓時，任側腹突然刺痛。他懷疑肝臟是不是在那個位置，隨即發現自己對身體的了解比勇者應有的還少。米拉請他休息喝水。當天活動很累，她不願讓逞強變成另一種傷害。伊可確認他行走正常，隊伍才繼續。任低聲開玩笑，說勇敢戰士被樓梯打敗。米拉聽見了，回答：「再勇敢的戰士也需要午餐。」任很感謝她不會把每個小缺點都變成華麗演說。

Later the engineer showed them a narrow stairway used to reach the signal lamp. It had a locked gate and a clear notice about the weight limit. Ren asked whether Pebble could be carried up. Iko said the little dragon was light enough, but no one needed him on the dangerous stairs. The correct choice was to test the lamp from the lower station, where trained workers could see it clearly. Ren nodded and helped place a mirror there. When the signal flashed, its light crossed the room like a tiny message from the past. Mira wrote the rhythm down before it faded.

稍後工程師帶他們看通往訊號燈的窄樓梯。那裡有鎖門，還貼著清楚重量限制。任問能不能抱小石上去。伊可說小龍雖然夠輕，卻沒有必要把牠帶上危險樓梯。正確做法是在下方工作站測試燈光，由受訓工人清楚觀察。任點頭幫忙架鏡子。訊號亮起時，光穿過房間，像過去送來的小訊息。米拉趕在它消失前記下節奏。
