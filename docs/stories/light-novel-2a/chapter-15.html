霧裡的歌

The Song in the Fog

Fog rolled through the path before sunrise, hiding even the nearest door. Mira arranged the team into pairs and set a regular meeting point. "The primary rule is simple," she said. "No one leaves the marked path alone." Ren wanted to argue that his skill could help them move faster, but the argument died when Pebble disappeared behind a stone. The dragon returned a moment later with a bun in his mouth. "Wherever there is food, you can find him," Ren said. The owner of a nearby shop arrived, looking angry, so Ren paid for the bun and apologized. Mira wrote down the contact details in case anyone else became lost in the fog.

天還沒亮，濃霧便滾過小路，連最近的門都遮住了。米拉把隊員分成兩人一組，安排固定集合點。「首要規則很簡單，」她說，「任何人都不能獨自離開標記路線。」任本想爭辯自己的技能可以加快速度，小石卻突然消失在石後，讓他話說不下去。小龍很快叼著麵包回來。「有食物就找得到牠。」任說。附近店主氣沖沖地趕到，任便付錢道歉。米拉記下聯絡方式，免得其他人也在霧裡走失。

The team found a quiet room where an old poet had written a poem about the moving hall. The lines described a path with seven turns and a bell at its end. A drawing showed how workers used a repeated pattern of light to deliver signals through fog. Ren was surprised that a poem could work like a map. "It is peaceful here," he said, looking around. Mira reminded him that beauty did not make a room safe. They checked the floor and the door before taking a rest. Outside, a zebra-striped cloth marked the end of a dangerous range of broken stones. The sign was simple enough to see even when the mist grew thick.

隊伍找到一間安靜房間，昔日一位詩人在此寫下移動大廳的詩。詩句描述有七個轉彎的路，盡頭有一個鐘。插圖顯示工人如何利用重複的光線圖樣，在濃霧中傳遞訊號。任很驚訝詩也能像地圖。「這裡真平靜。」他環顧四周。米拉提醒他，美麗不代表安全。他們先查地板與門，才坐下休息。外頭有塊斑馬紋布，標示碎石危險區的終點。即使霧變濃，這種標記仍容易看見。

A small light appeared beyond the door. Ren wanted to follow it, but Mira asked him to wait while she checked the pattern. It flashed in the same order as the third line of the song. She copied the timing, then called to Toma over the speaking tube. He confirmed that the signal came from the old work station, not from a strange creature. The team moved along the marked path until they reached a locked box. Inside was a paper record with the names of workers who had last used the third bell. One name had been crossed out in fresh ink: Argo, the town's present maze curator.

門外出現一道小燈光。任想追，米拉卻請他先等她確認節奏。燈光的閃爍次序與歌曲第三行相同。她抄下時間，再用傳話管呼叫托馬。他確認訊號來自舊工作站，不是什麼奇怪生物。隊伍沿標記路線前進，到達一只上鎖的箱子。裡面有張紙本紀錄，列著最後使用第三個鐘的工人姓名。其中一個名字被新墨劃掉：阿爾戈，現在的迷宮管理員。

Ren's stomach tightened. He had seen Argo in council meetings, always sitting at the back with calm eyes. Mira did not accuse him. She took a copy and asked the team to return before the fog grew worse. Their discovery was important, but it did not explain why his name had been removed. On the way home, Ren asked whether she was afraid. "Of course," Mira answered. "I care about this place. I also care about the people who may be hurt if we are wrong." Ren held the record inside a dry case and walked beside her. For once, he had no joke ready. The silence between them felt like trust, not fear.

任的胃一緊。他在議會見過阿爾戈，總是安靜地坐在後排。米拉沒有立刻指控，而是抄好副本，請隊伍在霧更濃前回程。發現很重要，卻無法解釋為何阿爾戈的名字會被劃掉。回家路上，任問她會不會怕。「當然怕，」米拉回答，「我很在乎這地方，也在乎如果我們弄錯，可能受傷的人。」任把紀錄收進乾燥盒，走在她身旁。這次他沒說笑話。兩人間的沉默讓他感到的是信任，不是恐懼。

The workers' old arrangement for foggy days had a simple purpose: keep every person within sight of a partner. Mira asked Ren to help arrange the new markers along the route. The strange part was that the markers followed a fashion of bright stripes used in the town many years ago. A local healer said the colors were easy to see even from a distance. She had come to cure a worker's cough but stayed to show the team how people with poor sight might use the signs. Ren was pleased that the old design had survived because it worked, not because it looked grand.

工人過去在霧天的安排很簡單：每個人都得有能互相看見的夥伴。米拉請任協助沿路排列新標記。有趣的是，這些標記沿用多年前鎮上流行的鮮明條紋風格。當地醫療師說，那些顏色遠遠也容易看見。她原本來治療一名工人的咳嗽，卻留下教小隊如何讓視力較差的人使用標誌。任很高興舊設計被留下，不是因為華麗，而是因為實用。

On the following weekday the team returned to test the signs with volunteers. Mira asked people to walk the approved route at an ordinary pace and to stop whenever they lost sight of a mark. Ren watched quietly and wrote down where they hesitated. The first test showed that one sign was hidden behind a low wall. They moved it and repeated the walk. The second result was better. "So even a good pattern needs a real test," Ren said. Mira nodded. Pebble followed the group, paused at every marker, and looked as though he were the captain of a very serious army. No one had the heart to tell him otherwise.

下一個平日，小隊帶志工回來測試標記。米拉請大家用正常速度走已核准路線，一旦看不到下一個記號就停下。任安靜觀察，記下大家猶豫之處。第一次測試發現一塊牌被矮牆擋住。他們移動位置，再走一次，結果更好。「所以好圖樣也得實測。」任說。米拉點頭。小石跟著隊伍走，每到一塊牌就停住，看起來像一支嚴肅軍隊的隊長。沒有人忍心告訴牠不是。
