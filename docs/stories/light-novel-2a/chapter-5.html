勇者的第一張帳單

The Hero's First Bill

轉生後只有一格道具欄！？——我在迷宮裡當上了最弱勇者 · 第 5 / 25 章 · 1000–2000 · 英中對照 · 校訂中

The town office sent Ren a bill for his first week of rescue work. Food, beds, rope, and lanterns were listed as an expense, but there was no pay. "Being chosen is a great honor," the clerk said. Ren was not sure whether honor could buy dinner. The burden of being a hero suddenly felt heavier than any sword. Mira visited from her college training room with a double set of notes. "We have to show the council what rescue work actually costs," she said. "If they do not understand the function of each tool, they will call everything waste." Ren opened his notebook. He had never expected to use school skills in a dungeon, but perhaps his old life was not entirely useless.

鎮公所寄來任第一週救援工作的帳單。食物、床位、繩索、燈籠都被列為支出，卻沒有任何報酬。「被選中是莫大的榮譽。」職員說。任不確定榮譽能不能買晚餐。勇者的負擔突然比任何劍都重。米拉從她的大學訓練室帶著兩套筆記來找他。「我們得讓議會知道救援到底花了多少，」她說，「他們不懂每件工具的功能，就會把所有東西都叫浪費。」任打開筆記本。他沒想過在迷宮還會用到學校知識，但原本的人生或許沒有完全白費。

They held a small meeting with fellow guides and the lady who managed the town kitchen. The kitchen produced soup for travelers and workers, and its production had doubled since the tunnel closed. A feature of the old maze was a room that could hold food in cold air, but the door was broken. "You cannot buy a perfect solution," the lady said. "You have to choose what helps the most people." She asked Ren to make a selection from three possible tasks: repair the cold room, help the clinic, or search the next maze floor. He hated choosing because each task mattered. Mira reminded him that he was allowed to listen before deciding.

兩人和其他嚮導，以及管理鎮上廚房的女士開了小會。廚房為旅人與工人煮湯，自隧道封閉後，供餐量已經翻倍。舊迷宮有個能用冷空氣保存食物的房間，但門壞了。「你買不到完美的解法，」女士說，「你只能選最能幫助大家的事。」她要任從三個任務中選一個：修冷藏室、幫診所，或搜尋迷宮下一層。任很討厭選擇，因為每件事都重要。米拉提醒他，決定前可以先聽別人的看法。

During the meeting, Pebble ran across a field where students were playing soccer. He chased the ball, slipped on a sweater, and rolled into a basket of tissue paper. Everyone laughed except the dragon. A young worker helped Ren sweep up the mess. He said his sister could not swallow ordinary food after an illness, so the kitchen soup was important to her. Another worker had lost his home to a terrorist attack years ago and was scared of closed doors. Ren understood that people carried stories he could not see. Their needs were not numbers in a game. He finally told the group that the cold room should come first, because it could help both the kitchen and clinic.

開會途中，小石跑過學生踢足球的空地。牠追著球跑，踩到一件毛衣滑倒，直接滾進一籃面紙裡。除了小龍，大家都笑了。一名年輕工人幫任打掃。他說妹妹生病後很難吞下普通食物，因此廚房的湯對她很重要。另一名工人多年前因恐怖攻擊失去家園，至今害怕關上的門。任明白每個人都有他看不見的故事，他們的需求不是遊戲裡的數字。最後他向大家表示，應先修冷藏室，因為廚房和診所都能受益。

Mira uploaded a copy of their plan to the council's message crystal, then brought a paper copy to the kitchen. "A crystal can fail," she said. "A second record is safer." Ren smiled at her careful habit. The council did not approve all the funds, but it agreed to let the team inspect the cold room. That was a small victory. When the meeting ended, the kitchen lady handed Ren a warm bun. "This is not hero pay," she said. "It is dinner." Ren ate it in silence for a moment. Then he raised the bun like a trophy and said, "My first real reward has no magic at all." Pebble tried to jump for it, and Ren quickly took another bite.

米拉把計畫副本上傳到議會的訊息水晶，接著又拿紙本送去廚房。「水晶也可能故障，」她說，「有第二份紀錄更安全。」任喜歡她細心的習慣。議會沒有核准全部經費，卻同意讓隊伍檢查冷藏室。這是小小的勝利。會議結束，廚房女士遞給任一個溫熱的麵包。「這不是勇者薪水，」她說，「是晚餐。」任安靜吃了一會，然後把麵包像獎盃般舉起：「我第一次真正的獎賞竟然毫無魔法！」小石跳起來想搶，任趕緊又咬了一口。

After the meeting Ren walked past the school garden, where a tall stone statue held a bowl. It was meant to honor the spirit of working together, not a real god. Students seldom noticed it because they ran by on their way to class. Ren stopped and read the name of the artist, a former kitchen worker. A small note said the statue had been made from pieces saved after a flood. Ren liked the idea that broken things could become something new without pretending they had never been broken. He returned to the office and helped Mira upload the updated cost sheet. They left both a digital copy and a paper copy in the public file.

會議後，任經過學校花園。高高的石像手裡捧著碗，象徵合作精神，不是真正神明。學生上課時總匆匆經過，很少注意它。任停下來讀雕刻者姓名，原來那人曾在廚房工作。旁邊的小字說，雕像用了洪水後留下的石塊製成。任喜歡這個想法：破損的東西也能成為新的作品，而不必假裝從沒壞過。他回到辦公室，幫米拉上傳更新的成本表，同時留存電子與紙本副本供大家查閱。

In the evening Mira asked Ren whether he regretted choosing the cold room over the next maze floor. "A little," he admitted. "I wanted the exciting part first." Mira leaned against the wall and explained that the rooms below would not disappear while they took care of the people above. Ren understood, though he still pictured a great door opening beneath his feet. Pebble scratched at the wall, making a sound like a very small drum. Then the dragon fell asleep before anyone could praise him. Ren decided the perfect adventure party would probably require a better alarm clock and fewer snacks.

傍晚米拉問任，選擇先修冷藏室而不是進迷宮下一層，會不會後悔。「有一點，」任承認，「我本來想先玩刺激的部分。」米拉靠著牆解釋，他們照顧地上居民時，下面的房間不會因此消失。任懂了，雖然仍忍不住想像腳下有扇巨大門開啟。小石抓抓牆，發出像小鼓的聲音，然後還沒人稱讚就睡著了。任決定完美冒險隊伍大概需要一個比較好的鬧鐘，以及比較少的點心。
