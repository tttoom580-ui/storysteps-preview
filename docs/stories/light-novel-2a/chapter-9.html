不會游泳的鯊魚

The Shark That Wasn't Alive

轉生後只有一格道具欄！？——我在迷宮裡當上了最弱勇者 · 第 9 / 25 章 · 1000–2000 · 英中對照 · 校訂中

The next room opened beneath a painting of a shark. Ren stepped back at once, but Mira pointed to the picture's flat edges. "It is only an image," she said. Pebble disagreed with a loud squeak and hid inside Ren's coat. The corridor smelled delicious, as if someone had been cooking soup on the other side. Ren lifted the lid of a nearby pot and found only clear liquid. "Our enemy is a silly kitchen?" he asked. Mira laughed but checked for a safe exit before entering. A wooden tube carried warm air to the room, and a painted symbol on its wall showed an old food route. The answer to the smell was probably ordinary cooking, not a sea monster.

下一個房間的入口上畫著鯊魚。任立刻往後退，米拉卻指著畫像平平的邊緣。「只是圖像而已。」她說。小石用大叫表達不同意見，躲進任外套裡。走廊傳來可口的香氣，像有人在另一側煮湯。任掀開附近鍋子的蓋子，只見清澈液體。「我們的敵人是笨蛋廚房嗎？」他問。米拉笑了，但進門前仍先確認安全出口。木管把溫暖空氣送進房間，牆上畫的符號指出舊時運送食物的路線。香味多半來自普通烹飪，而非海怪。

A cook named Nia came out of a side door. Her character was lively, and she spoke aloud even when no one asked a question. The kitchen had been trapped behind a broken wall for two days. "We tried to express our problem through the speaking tube," she said, "but the message did not get out." Mira examined the tube's position and found a bent joint. The result was simple: people outside had heard only a strange sound and never understood the words. Ren asked Nia to forgive the rescue team for taking so long. Nia waved a spoon. "Feed us first, then apologize," she said. Ren decided that was a fair solution.

名叫妮亞的廚師從側門走出來。她性格活潑，就算沒人問話，也會大聲說個不停。廚房已被壞牆困住兩天。「我們試過透過傳話管表達困境，」她說，「可是訊息傳不出去。」米拉檢查傳話管的位置，發現接頭彎了。結果很簡單：外頭的人只聽見怪聲，從沒聽懂話語。任請妮亞原諒救援隊來得太慢。妮亞揮揮湯匙：「先讓我們吃飽，再來道歉。」任認為這解法很公平。

While the cooks rested, Mira used chalk to mark every safe door. Ren suggested an improvement to the speaking system: two short taps before any message, so listeners would know when words were coming. Nia agreed to test it. Pebble was still scared of the shark painting, so Ren placed a cloth over the image. The dragon slowly came out, looked at the cloth, and made a proud noise. "You are alive, and the shark is not," Ren told him. Nia laughed so hard that soup nearly burst from her spoon. Ren then noticed a small plate under the floor. It carried the first part of an old song, marked with the title "Wake the Road."

廚師休息時，米拉用粉筆標出安全的門。任建議改良傳話系統：每次說話前先敲兩下，讓聽者知道訊息即將開始。妮亞同意測試。小石仍害怕鯊魚畫，任便拿布把畫蓋起來。小龍慢慢走出，看著布發出得意聲音。「你是活的，鯊魚不是。」任告訴牠。妮亞笑得湯匙裡的湯差點噴出去。這時任注意到地板下藏著一塊小板，上面記著一首老歌的第一段，標題是〈喚醒道路〉。

Mira did not remove the plate immediately. She checked the floor and asked Nia whether anyone knew its purpose. The cook said older workers once sang the tune when they opened the large moving hall. Mira copied the marks and left the plate where it was. If it belonged to the machine, pulling it out could make the road fail. Ren accepted the choice, even though a glowing prize would have made a perfect chapter ending in his favorite books. Instead he helped the cooks carry warm bowls into the street. Pebble tried to sit in one bowl and was removed by Nia, who said she had enough trouble with her real customers.

米拉沒有立刻拿走小板，而是先檢查地板，問妮亞知不知道用途。妮亞說，早年的工人打開大型移動大廳時，總會唱這首歌。米拉抄下記號，把小板留在原位。如果它原本屬於機器，硬拔可能讓道路故障。任接受這個決定，雖然發光的獎品更像他愛看的小說章末畫面。他改去幫廚師把熱碗送上街。小石想坐進其中一個碗裡，被妮亞抱起來趕走；她說自己已經有夠多真正的客人要照顧了。

Before leaving the kitchen, Ren asked Nia why the old song mattered to the cooks. She said the moving hall used to bring fresh food from distant farms before winter closed the roads. A short line of music told workers when the large door had reached its safe position. It was not a spell that could make a door obey; it was a signal that everyone could remember, even when they were tired. Ren liked the difference. He could not control the whole system with one lucky action, but he could learn to listen. Nia let him practice the rhythm on an empty pot. He was terrible at it. Pebble, however, hit the pot with his tail at exactly the right moment.

離開廚房前，任問妮亞老歌為何對廚師如此重要。她說移動大廳從前會在冬季道路封閉前，把遠方農場的新鮮食物運來。短短的音樂能告訴工人，大門何時到達安全位置。那不是令門聽話的法術，而是人人都能記住的訊號，就算累了也記得。任很喜歡這種區別：他無法靠一次幸運動作控制整個系統，卻能學著傾聽。妮亞讓他拿空鍋練節奏，他敲得一塌糊塗。小石反倒用尾巴準確地敲中該有的拍子。

Mira wrote the beats in her notebook, then asked Nia to check them. They agreed to keep a copy at the public kitchen. Ren wanted to carry the old plate home, but he remembered how many people depended on the machine. Removing a part for his own adventure would be the very kind of careless choice they were trying to stop. He stepped away from the floor and helped a young cook wash the dishes. The task was simple and quiet, yet it felt connected to everything they had discovered. A working road meant more than a moving door. It meant tomorrow's breakfast could reach people who needed it.

米拉把節拍記進筆記本，請妮亞核對，兩人同意在公共廚房保存副本。任原想把舊歌板帶回家，卻想起有多少人仰賴這部機器。為個人冒險而拆掉零件，正是他們一直想阻止的粗心行為。他離開地板，去幫年輕廚師洗碗。工作簡單又安靜，卻與整個調查有了連結。能運作的道路不只是會移動的門，也是讓明天早餐送到需要的人手中。
