退潮時的月臺

The Platform at Low Tide

轉生後只有一格道具欄！？——我在迷宮裡當上了最弱勇者 · 第 20 / 25 章 · 1000–2000 · 英中對照 · 校訂中

The next test took place near a quiet coast, where an old platform reached over the sand. The engineers wanted to compare the moving hall's wheel with a wheel from a small port machine. A dancer from the town festival offered to help mark the timing with music. Ren thought the plan sounded strange, but Mira explained that a steady rhythm could make it easier to notice a change. They measured the distance in miles, then checked the load in kilograms. A patient worker waited while every number was confirmed. "Luck is not a safety plan," Mira said. Ren wrote that sentence at the top of his notes. He suspected he would need it again.

下一次測試在安靜海岸舉行，舊月臺從沙地延伸出去。工程師要把移動大廳的輪子和港口小機器的輪子比較。參加鎮上節慶的舞者自願用音樂幫忙標記時間。任覺得計畫奇怪，米拉解釋穩定節奏能讓人更容易察覺變化。他們用英里測量距離，再用公斤確認負重。一名有耐心的工人等著大家核對每個數字。「運氣不是安全計畫。」米拉說。任把這句話寫在筆記最上面，預感以後還會用到。

The first test was impressive but not enough. The wheel turned smoothly under a small load, then slowed when the sand became wet. The joint between two pieces had been too stiff. Iko explained how the water could influence the movement. "We cannot satisfy ourselves with one good result," she said. "We need to know the conditions under which it fails." Ren nodded. He had once thought an adventure was won by a brave attack at the right moment. Here the most useful action was to stop, compare, and begin again. Mira looked at his notes and smiled. "You are getting good at being boring," she said. Ren pretended to be hurt.

第一次測試令人驚艷，卻還不夠。輪子在輕負重下順暢轉動，但沙子濕了就變慢。兩個零件間的接頭太硬。伊可解釋水分如何影響動作。「不能只因一次結果良好就滿足，」她說，「必須知道它在什麼條件下失敗。」任點頭。他以前以為冒險是在正確時機使出勇敢一擊。如今最有用的行動卻是停下、比較、重來。米拉看著他的筆記微笑：「你越來越擅長無聊了。」任假裝受到傷害。

Later a child became ill from the cold wind. The team moved her away from the testing area and asked the clinic for help. Ren offered his coat while her parents took her to a warm room. A couple from the town helped carry the equipment. They said the moving hall had once allowed their families to meet even during winter storms. "That is why we want it back," the woman explained. "But we want it safe for children like her." Ren understood the weight of the request. The hall was not a prize to show off; it was a link between lives. Its success would be measured by people arriving home.

後來，一名孩子被冷風吹得不舒服。團隊把她帶離測試區，請診所幫忙。父母帶她去暖房時，任借出外套。鎮上一對伴侶幫忙搬設備，他們說移動大廳從前讓兩邊家庭即使遇到冬季暴風雪，也能見面。「這就是我們想讓它回來的原因，」女性解釋，「但我們希望它對像她這樣的孩子安全。」任明白請求的重量。大廳不是拿來炫耀的獎品，而是連結不同生活的路。成功應由人們是否平安到家來衡量。

The engineers changed the joint and repeated the test at lower pressure. This time the wheel passed both dry and wet conditions, though the main hall still needed a full check. Everyone agreed that the result was promising, not final. Ren helped put the tools back into their cases. Pebble rolled across the empty platform and stared at the sea. "Don't even think about learning to surf," Ren warned. The dragon turned his head and sneezed. Mira laughed and said the little creature would probably choose a safer sport, such as sleeping. Ren watched the sunlight on the water. For the first time, the town had a path forward that did not depend on pretending everything was fine.

工程師更換接頭，再以較低壓力重測。這回輪子在乾、濕兩種條件下都通過，雖然主大廳仍需要完整檢查。大家同意結果有希望，卻不是最終結論。任幫忙把工具收回箱子。小石滾過空月臺，盯著海面。「你別想學衝浪喔。」任警告。小龍轉頭打了個噴嚏。米拉笑說牠大概會選更安全的運動，例如睡覺。任望著水上的陽光。小鎮終於有了一條不靠假裝一切正常的前進路線。

Before the seaside test, Ren helped bake small rolls for the workers. He had nearly burned the first tray, but Nia showed him how to turn it in the oven. The cook weighed every kilogram of flour and taught him why a recipe needed exact amounts. At the coast the engineers checked the path, which measured a little more than a mile from the market gate. Ren carried the food to the waiting area, where each worker signed for a meal. He felt proud of this simple task. It would not appear in the final report as a heroic act, but people tested machines better when they were fed and rested.

海邊測試前，任幫工人烤小麵包。第一盤差點烤焦，幸好妮亞教他在烤箱裡轉方向。廚師用公斤量好每份麵粉，說明食譜為何需要精確份量。到了海岸，工程師確認從市場門到月臺的路，略長於一英里。任把食物送到等候區，每名工人都簽收餐點。他為這簡單工作感到驕傲。它不會被寫進最終報告當英雄事蹟，卻能讓大家在吃飽休息後更好地測試機器。

After the test, Mira asked Ren whether he still wanted the grand adventure he had imagined on his first day. He thought about the shark painting, the old song, and the frightening accident records. "I do," he admitted, "but I want the people in it to come home." Mira smiled and told him that was a good reason to continue. Pebble crawled into the empty bread basket and began to snore. Ren looked at the little dragon. "Our party may never be famous," he said, "but at least we will never be hungry for chalk." Mira laughed, and the sound mingled with the gentle evening waves.

測試後米拉問任，還想不想要初到那天想像的偉大冒險。他想起鯊魚畫、老歌與可怕的事故紀錄。「想，」他承認，「但我希望故事裡的人都能回家。」米拉微笑，說這是繼續前進的好理由。小石爬進空麵包籃開始打呼。任看著小龍：「我們隊伍可能永遠不會有名，但至少永遠不缺想吃粉筆的人。」米拉笑出聲，笑聲與柔和晚浪混在一起。
