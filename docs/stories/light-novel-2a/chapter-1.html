只有一格的道具欄

One Slot, No Sword

轉生後只有一格道具欄！？——我在迷宮裡當上了最弱勇者 · 第 1 / 25 章 · 1000–2000 · 英中對照 · 校訂中

用互動版查詞、朗讀與收藏

The first thing Ren saw after waking in another world was a blue window floating above his nose. It said, "Welcome, chosen hero. Your special skill is ONE SLOT." Below the words was the picture of a tiny empty box. Ren, who had been a twenty-year-old student in Japan yesterday, waited for a second page. None appeared. From the balcony of the town hall, the people watched him with great belief. Their president had promised that a hero would save the nation from the moving maze below the city. "Can my skill stop a monster?" Ren asked. The operator of the magic gate shook his head. "It can hold one small object for ten minutes. That's all." Ren decided this was a very poor approach to becoming a legend.

任在異世界醒來，第一眼看見的是漂浮在鼻尖上方的藍色視窗。「歡迎，被選中的勇者。你的特殊技能是『一格道具欄』。」文字下面只有一個空小格。昨天還是日本二十歲大學生的任，等著第二頁出現，卻什麼也沒有。市政廳的陽臺上，民眾滿懷信念地望著他。他們的議長曾保證勇者會拯救國家，阻止城下不斷移動的迷宮。「這技能能擋怪物嗎？」任問。魔法門操作員搖頭：「只能放一件小東西，維持十分鐘，就這樣。」任覺得這實在不是成為傳說的好方法。

The official placed a stone on a table to test the skill. Ren touched it, and the stone disappeared. A clock began to count down in the air. Then a guard put a heavy sword before him. Ren tried again, but the blue window showed a red cross. The sword was too heavy, and he could not store a secondary item while the stone remained inside. "You can deny the challenge," the guard said kindly. "Our city needs workers, too." Ren wanted to answer that he had not asked to be summoned in the first place. Instead he asked how to return the stone. He opened his hand. It appeared so quickly that he dropped it on his foot. The guard looked away, perhaps to hide a laugh.

官員把一塊石頭放上桌，讓任測試能力。任碰到石頭，它便消失了，空中開始出現倒數時鐘。接著衛兵放來一把沉重的劍。任又試一次，藍色視窗卻跳出紅叉。劍太重，而且石頭還在裡面時，不能再放第二件物品。「你可以拒絕挑戰，」衛兵好心說，「城裡也需要普通工人。」任很想說，自己根本沒有要求被召喚。但他只問要如何取回石頭。他張開手，石頭立刻出現，快得讓他直接砸到腳。衛兵轉過頭，八成是在忍笑。

A girl with a green cloak was waiting at the base of the steps. "My name is Mira. I guide people through the first field of the maze," she said. Her voice was better than the official's grand speech because she told Ren the truth. The maze changed its rooms at sunrise, but its main doors followed a fixed tradition: every door had to be opened from the safe side. For instance, a wooden seesaw beside the entrance showed whether the floor was level. Mira had tried to discuss this problem with the council, yet they wanted a hero with speed and bright magic. "I can develop a map," she said. "I cannot use it alone." Ren noticed the confident look in her eyes. She wanted a partner, not a boss.

披著綠斗篷的女孩正在階梯底部等他。「我叫米拉，是迷宮第一區的嚮導。」她的聲音比官員華麗的演說更讓任安心，因為她說的是實話。迷宮會在日出時改變房間，但主要門仍遵循固定傳統：每扇門都得從安全的一側開啟。例如，入口旁的木製翹翹板會顯示地板是否水平。米拉試過與議會討論，議員卻只想要速度快、魔法耀眼的勇者。「我能繪製地圖，」她說，「但我一個人用不了。」任看見她眼裡的自信。她要的是夥伴，不是上司。

Before Ren could reply, a round creature rolled out of a drain. It looked like a stone with a tail. A male guard shouted, "Don't touch that thing!" The creature sneezed glitter onto his boots, then hid behind Ren. "Pebble is a baby stone dragon," Mira explained. "He follows the underground wind. He also eats chalk." Pebble bit Ren's empty lunch bag to prove her point. On the other side of the road, a subway tunnel carried food toward the market, but the windy air coming from the maze had stopped the carts. People were trapped behind a wooden gate. Ren looked at his one empty slot, Mira's map, and the worried faces. The city did not need a perfect hero. It needed one useful action now.

任還來不及回答，一團圓滾滾的生物就從排水口滾了出來。它像長了尾巴的石頭。一名男性衛兵大喊：「別碰那東西！」生物對他的靴子打出一個亮粉噴嚏，接著躲到任身後。「那是小石龍小石，」米拉解釋，「牠會追著地下風走，還會吃粉筆。」小石咬了一口任空空的午餐袋，證明米拉所言不假。路另一邊，地下鐵貨運隧道原本送食物去市集，但從迷宮吹出的怪風已讓貨車停下。有人受困在木門後。任看看自己唯一的空格、米拉的地圖和居民焦急的臉。城裡現在不需要完美勇者，而是需要一個真正有用的行動。

Mira handed Ren a little metal pin. "The gate has a hole on the far side. This pin can release the lock, but no human arm can reach it." The hole was smaller than a finger. Ren could not send his skill through a wall; he could only store something he touched and release it into his own hand. He studied the narrow gap below the gate. There was just enough space for his arm if he lay flat. Pebble pushed dust away with his nose. Ren stored the pin so it would not catch on the gap, slid his arm through, and brought it back into his palm. The pin clicked into place. The gate opened a little. Mira pulled it wide, and the first cart rolled free. The crowd cheered. Ren checked his window: one hour until he could use the slot again. "Great," he said. "I saved one cart and lost my lunch."

米拉交給任一根小金屬插銷。「門的另一側有個洞，插入這根銷子就能解鎖，但人的手臂伸不到。」孔洞比手指還小。任不能把道具隔空傳過牆；他只能收起親手碰到的東西，再讓它回到自己的掌心。他研究門下方的縫隙，發現只要整個人趴平，手臂剛好能伸過去。小石用鼻子把灰塵推開。任先收起插銷，免得伸手時卡住，再把手臂探進去，讓插銷回到掌中。金屬輕響一聲，門鬆開了。米拉用力拉開門，第一輛貨車終於通過。人群歡呼。任查看視窗：道具欄得等一小時才能再用。「太好了，」他說，「救了一輛車，午餐卻沒了。」
