賭場後面的工坊

The Workshop Behind the Casino

不販售的黎明：夜幕劇團 · 第 11 / 25 章 · 4000–5000 · 英中對照 · 校訂中

用互動版查詞、朗讀與收藏

The old mirror drawings led to a workshop hidden behind a noisy casino. Tourists were fascinated by the fake-sun display in its lobby, yet most never saw the workers who kept its gears turning. Tao asked to examine a large bronze shaft the institute had listed as a possible spare. The workshop manager said it had been sold to the owner of the Glass Arena. He showed a receipt with an empty signature box. Yi photographed the page but refused to call it theft before hearing from the person who had arranged the sale.

舊鏡陣圖紙把他們帶到熱鬧賭場後方的工坊。遊客對大廳假太陽展示著迷，卻幾乎看不見維持齒輪運轉的工人。陶岑要求查看研究所列為備用零件的巨大銅軸。工坊經理說零件已賣給玻璃競技場主人，並拿出簽名欄空白的收據。易尋拍下紙頁，卻拒絕在聽到經手人的說法前，就斷言那是偷竊。

The manager was an apt guide to old machinery but a poor speaker. He would gasp whenever the visitors asked about safety, then characterize every fault as a minor defect. A clinical worker from the nearby refuge warned that spectators had been hurt by falling scenery. The venue's wealthy heir wanted a copyright claim on the old model; the repairer's daughter said her family had built it. Outside, a wolf began to howl and a guard leaned on an unloaded rifle. The spectacle remained intact, but its sheer weight could overwhelm the temporary stage if they attempted an upgrade without testing the beams.

經理很懂舊機器，卻不善說話。訪客問起安全，他便倒抽一口氣，把每項故障都形容成小問題。附近避難所的醫療工作者警告，曾有觀眾被掉落布景傷到。場地富有的繼承人想對舊模型主張著作權，修復師的女兒則說那是家族打造。屋外狼開始嚎叫，一名守衛靠著沒有上彈的步槍。展示機關仍完整，但重量極大；不測試梁柱就升級，可能壓垮臨時舞臺。

Yi kept his notebook beside the lantern rather than beneath it. He had once hidden uncomfortable facts behind beautiful lines, believing that an elegant story could make a hard decision easier to bear. Now he placed the plain description first and the poetry second. Tao respected the change but did not pretend that trust had been restored in a single conversation. When Yi offered to hand over the notebook, Tao said, 'Keep it. Read it to me when something changes.' The request sounded modest. It also meant they would continue speaking to one another after the audience went home.

易尋把筆記放在燈旁，而不是藏在燈下。他曾把不舒服的事實藏在漂亮台詞後面，以為優美故事能讓艱難決定比較容易接受。如今他先寫平實描述，再寫詩句。陶岑尊重這份改變，但不假裝一次談話就能恢復信任。易尋想交出筆記，陶岑說：「你留著。事情有變就讀給我聽。」這要求聽來很小，卻代表觀眾回家後，他們仍會繼續對彼此說話。

Tao found a pale mark beneath the shaft housing, shaped like two small wings. His father had used the same mark on hinges built for travelling theatres. The discovery was personal, but it did not prove his father had supplied the missing shaft. Yi suggested that they compare the metal composition and the old workshop register. While waiting, the actors helped install a low workbench so a carpenter who used a wheelchair could participate without lifting heavy parts. The carpenter noticed a second mark that everyone standing above him had missed.

陶岑在轉軸座下發現兩枚小翅膀形的淡色印記。父親曾在巡迴劇院的鉸鏈上使用同樣標記。這項發現對他很私人，卻不能證明父親提供了失蹤轉軸。易尋提議比對金屬成分與工坊舊登記簿。等待時，演員幫忙裝設低工作臺，讓使用輪椅的木匠無需舉起重物也能參與。那位木匠發現了站著的人全都沒注意到的第二枚記號。

The mark lay beneath a scar in the copper, near a place where a loose wire could cause damage. The workers did not recommend turning the wheel until its bearings had been cleaned. A teenager offered to run a free workshop on safe tools for younger apprentices. Yi noted the date of a recent repair and found that it matched the arena's opening night. The manager still insisted he knew nothing about the sale, but his account could now be checked against other evidence. The company thanked him for the records without declaring him innocent or guilty.

記號位於銅面一道傷痕下，靠近鬆線可能造成損傷的位置。工人在軸承清潔前不建議轉輪。一名少年主動提出替年幼學徒開辦免費安全工具工作坊。易尋記下最近一次修理日期，發現與競技場開幕夜相同。經理仍堅稱不知道交易，但他的說法如今可拿其他證據比對。劇團感謝他提供紀錄，沒有宣布他無罪或有罪。

The road carried more than the stage wagon. It carried unfinished apologies, borrowed tools, and promises whose meaning changed with the people who heard them. The actors could pack a scene into a chest, but they could not pack away its consequences. When the wheels stopped, Tao asked what the nearest household needed before asking what the theatre needed. Yi learned to make the same distinction in his scripts: a moving speech was not proof that a plan would work, and a quiet objection deserved as much attention as a cheer.

路上運送的不只是舞臺車，還有未完成的道歉、借來的工具，以及會隨聽者改變意義的承諾。演員能把場景收進箱子，卻不能把後果也收起來。車輪停下時，陶岑先問最近的人家需要什麼，再問劇團需要什麼。易尋也學會在劇本中區分兩者：動人的演說不等於方案可行，輕聲提出的異議和歡呼同樣值得傾聽。

At supper, Tao admitted that he had avoided the workshop for years because his father had died there during an accident. Yi did not ask for a dramatic confession. He listened as Tao described the sound of the machinery and the smell of hot metal. The carpenter said the hardest part of returning was recognizing how ordinary the place had become without his father. Yi told him that an ordinary workshop could still be worthy of care. They planned to visit the Glass Arena, but Tao asked for one quiet evening before walking toward another machine bearing his family's mark.

晚餐時，陶岑承認多年避開工坊，是因父親當年在那裡的事故中去世。易尋沒有要求戲劇性自白，只聽他描述機器的聲音與熱金屬氣味。木匠說最難的是發現父親不在後，那裡竟已變得如此平凡。易尋告訴他，平凡工坊仍值得好好照顧。他們計畫前往玻璃競技場，但陶岑希望在走向另一部刻著家族記號的機器前，先擁有一個安靜的晚上。

The old gears could still fascinate visitors, Tao admitted, provided the workshop did not conceal their dangers. Curiosity was welcome; unsafe handling was not.

陶岑承認舊齒輪依然能使訪客著迷，只要工坊不掩蓋危險。好奇值得歡迎，不安全的操作則不行。
