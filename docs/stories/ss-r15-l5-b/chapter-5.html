五條街的一束光

One Beam for Five Streets

不販售的黎明：夜幕劇團 · 第 5 / 25 章 · 4000–5000 · 英中對照 · 校訂中

Copperside Market had prepared a festival for the first true beam of morning. Retail stalls offered herbs, painted shutters, and sweets shaped like little suns. Tao found the enthusiasm dangerous precisely because he shared it. He remembered the smell of bread in daylight before the valley darkened, and he wanted the children to know that ordinary pleasure. Yi, however, noticed that the market plan showed only the wealthiest street. The next street's small homes had been left blank. Together they made a larger model with five blocks of houses, each carrying a name chosen by its residents.

銅邊市集為第一束真正晨光準備了節日。零售攤位賣藥草、彩繪百葉窗和小太陽形狀的糖果。陶岑覺得這股熱情危險，正因為他也一樣渴望。他記得山谷變暗前，陽光下麵包的味道，希望孩子也能知道這種平凡的快樂。易尋卻注意到市場計畫只畫出最富裕的街道，下一條街的小屋全被留白。於是他們製作包含五個住宅區的大模型，每一區都寫著居民自己選的名稱。

A constitutional adviser argued with a young democrat about who could approve the experiment. The latest episode of the city's radio play had exaggerated the mirror's power, turning public fear into a profitable spectacle. A fleet of hired carriages blocked the road while merchants accused one another of greed. The market manager offered an incentive to the first shop that received the beam, but a peasant from the lower lane asked who would pay if his vegetables froze. The wooden model began to rattle as the crowd pressed closer, and a child shouted that someone would smash it.

一位憲制顧問與年輕民主派人士爭論誰有權批准實驗。城裡最新一集廣播劇誇大鏡子的力量，把大眾恐懼變成賺錢的表演。一隊租來的車擋住道路，商販互罵貪婪。市場經理提出獎勵，讓先得到光束的商店獲益；低地小農卻問，如果他的菜凍壞了誰賠。人潮擠近時木模型嘎嘎作響，一名孩子喊有人會把它砸爛。

For a while they worked without speech. Tao listened for the change in a gear's rhythm, and Yi marked each small result on a sheet that anyone could inspect. No single successful movement made the whole mechanism safe. One wheel could turn freely while another carried too much weight. Every test therefore ended with the same question: who stood beyond the wall, where the operators could not see? They invited those people inside before trying the next adjustment. A witness was not merely a person asked to applaud; a witness could say that the experiment must stop.

有一陣子，他們沒有交談。陶岑聽著齒輪節奏的變化，易尋把每一個細小結果記在人人可檢視的紙上。某個零件成功轉動，不代表整套機關就安全；一個輪子可能運轉順暢，另一個卻承受太多重量。因此每次測試結束，他們都問：牆外還站著誰，是操作員看不見的？他們先邀請那些人進來，才繼續調整。見證人不只是被請來鼓掌的人，也能說出實驗必須停止。

Madam Song asked for silence and did something the council had not expected: she invited the people who had most to lose to stand closest to the controls. The lower-lane farmer named three practical concerns, and Tao repeated them without mocking his accent. A nurse explained why even a brief period of darkness could matter to a patient travelling home. A baker offered to close his business for the length of the test. With those conditions written down, the group accepted a reversible adjustment lasting fifteen minutes. Nobody was compelled to join the crowd or declare the plan a victory.

宋夫人請大家安靜，做了議事會意想不到的事：讓可能損失最多的人站得離控制臺最近。低地農民說出三項實際擔憂，陶岑毫不嘲笑他的口音，逐一覆述。護理師解釋即使短暫變暗，也可能影響返家的病人。一名麵包師願意為測試暫時停業。條件寫妥後，大家同意做十五分鐘、能復原的調整。沒有人被迫加入人群，或把計畫說成勝利。

The public verdict was provisional: they would recruit observers and test a substantial range of angles before making a final decision. Several shop owners feared a widespread recession, while others supported a massive redesign. Yi asked them to distinguish a tactic from a long-term rule. The current proposal crossed a clear threshold of risk if the beam remained on one block too long. The meeting produced no cheerful slogan. It produced a schedule, an emergency stop signal, and a place where every household could read the observations.

公開裁定是暫時性的：他們將招募觀察員，測試相當大的角度範圍，再作最後決定。有些店主擔心普遍經濟衰退，另一些支持大規模重設計。易尋請大家區分短期策略與長期規則。如果光束在同一區停留太久，現行方案就會跨過明確風險門檻。會議沒有產生歡快口號，卻留下測試時程、緊急停止訊號，以及每戶都能閱讀觀察紀錄的地方。

In the travelling theatre, every prop had a history. A cracked bowl had survived a mountain winter; a blue curtain had been patched by people who disagreed about almost everything except the need for shelter. Yi wanted to preserve those marks rather than paint over them. Tao sometimes preferred a neat surface, because a repairer likes to see a job finished. They argued, then found that the scars of use helped newcomers understand what had broken and why. By night, their quarrel had become another practical lesson for the apprentices who carried the lamps.

巡迴劇團裡，每件道具都有歷史。裂了的碗熬過山區冬天；藍色幕布由彼此幾乎什麼都談不攏、卻都認為需要避難處所的人縫補。易尋想保留那些痕跡，不願把它們塗平。陶岑有時偏好整齊表面，因為修復師喜歡看見工作完成。他們爭過之後才發現，使用過的傷痕能讓新來的人明白哪裡壞了、為何會壞。入夜時，他們的爭論已成為提燈學徒的一堂實用課。

The mirror moved only a finger's width. For fourteen minutes the northern pavement held a pale strip of warmth, and a blind musician named Bo Mei felt the change on his hands. He smiled without needing to see the light itself. Then a bell sounded from the lower lane, indicating that a shaded lane had become unsafe. Tao stopped the test one minute early. The crowd groaned, but the scheduled observer recorded the decision and its reason. Yi looked at Tao with new respect. The first beam mattered, but the promise to stop mattered more.

鏡子只移動了一指寬。十四分鐘裡，北側人行道得到一條淡淡暖光，失明音樂家薄梅用雙手感受到溫度變化。他微笑了，不需要親眼看見光。此時低地巷道的鐘響起，表示背光小巷已不安全。陶岑提早一分鐘停止試驗。人群發出失望聲，見證員卻記下決定及原因。易尋以新的尊重看著陶岑。第一束光很重要，遵守停機承諾更重要。

Yi warned that exaggeration could turn a limited danger into panic. He asked the announcer to describe the fifteen-minute test exactly as it was, neither a disaster nor a permanent cure.

易尋警告，誇張會把有限危險變成恐慌。他請播報員如實描述十五分鐘試驗：既不是災難，也不是永久解方。
