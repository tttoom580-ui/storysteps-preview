渡口的一段記憶

A Memory at the Pier

The company reached Willow Pier after two days of false evening. Fishermen had tied bright strips of cloth around every post because the quay remained difficult to see. An elderly ferryman named Su agreed to lend one memory to the lantern, but only if he could stop the show whenever he wished. The others accepted that condition without bargaining. When the lantern opened, it projected the pier on the day before the permanent twilight: five clean stripes of morning light ran across the water, and the last stripe pointed not toward the capital but toward an island observatory.

劇團在兩天虛假的傍晚後來到柳樹渡口。碼頭始終難以看清，漁人便在每根柱上綁上鮮明布條。年老船夫蘇願意借出一段記憶給燈，條件是他可以隨時叫停演出。眾人不討價還價就接受了。記憶燈打開時，投出永恆暮色降臨前一天的渡口：五條清楚晨光橫過水面，最後一條不是指向京城，而是指向海島觀測臺。

A correspondent from the regional newspaper arrived with questions about the abuse of public equipment. Her consultation with the deputy harbor master had ended in contempt; he called every witness sentimental. Yi proposed a deliberate and diplomatic response. He would show only what Su had agreed to share, then notify the harbor office of the result. A narrow creek fed the local ecosystem, and the old ferry path protected an island nesting ground. If the channel changed, a species already close to extinct might have even worse odds of survival.

一名地方報紙記者前來詢問公共設備被濫用的問題。她先前向副港務長諮詢，卻只得到輕蔑的態度；他把每個證人都稱為感情用事。易尋提出謹慎而圓融的回應：只展示蘇先生同意公開的內容，再通知港務處。狹窄溪流維繫當地生態系，而舊渡線保護島上築巢地。若航道更動，已接近滅絕的物種可能更難存活。

Tao was not frightened by complicated machinery. He was frightened by the moment when somebody grew certain that machinery would forgive any mistake. He checked the simple parts first: doors, handles, brakes, and the path by which a tired person could escape. Yi stood beside him with the list, correcting a number whenever it was wrong. They had little patience for grand declarations that omitted a missing screw. The work seemed ordinary from a distance, yet every safe step gave their extraordinary journey a reason to continue.

陶岑不怕複雜的機關。他害怕的是有人確信機器能原諒任何錯誤的那一刻。他先檢查簡單的部分：門、把手、煞車，以及疲憊的人可以逃生的路。易尋拿著清單站在旁邊，數字錯了便立即修正。他們對忽略一顆螺絲的大話沒有耐性。遠看這些工作十分平凡，每一步安全的進展卻都給了這場不平凡旅程繼續走下去的理由。

At the first showing, Su raised his hand when his younger face appeared near the end of the memory. Yi extinguished the projection immediately. A few people protested because the image had almost reached the important moment. Tao reminded them that a person's consent mattered more than their desire for a dramatic ending. Su smiled at the carpenter and offered a shorter section instead. It showed a wooden sign by the observatory: 'Every mirror must be named from both sides.' The sign was strange but precise. They sketched its position without asking for the portion of the memory Su wished to keep private.

第一次播放時，蘇先生看到記憶裡年輕的自己快出現在片尾，便舉起手。易尋立刻熄掉投影。有些人抗議，因為畫面差點進入關鍵時刻。陶岑提醒大家，一個人的同意比觀眾想要戲劇性結局更重要。蘇先生朝木匠笑了，另外同意提供較短的一段。那一段出現觀測臺旁的木牌：「每面鏡子都得從兩側命名。」標語古怪卻明確。他們描下位置，沒有再索取蘇先生想保留的私人記憶。

An investigator from the lighthouse called the account an early phase of a wider inquiry. A successor to the former keeper had sold the old pier trophy and tried to sneak away with the map. His excuse had no gut-level honesty: he said the object was worthless, although he had arranged a profitable swap. The crew returned the trophy and asked who could maintain a sustainable ferry service. The ferryman answered that every journey required a safe arrival, not merely a heroic departure. Tao wrote those words inside his tool chest.

燈塔調查員把這項紀錄視為更大調查的早期階段。前燈守的繼任者賣掉了舊渡口獎盃，還想偷偷帶走地圖。他的藉口毫無真誠可言：明明安排了有利可圖的交換，卻說物件一文不值。劇團歸還獎盃，並問誰能維持永續的渡輪服務。船夫答道，每段旅程都必須有安全的抵達，而不只是英雄式的出發。陶岑把這句話寫進工具箱內側。

At sunset—although the sky no longer offered a true sunset—the cast gathered around a lamp whose flame was deliberately small. No one was asked to surrender a private memory to make it brighter. Their memory lanterns worked only with permission, played a scene once, and then returned the recording to its owner. The lanterns could reveal how a road had looked; they could not move a stone or turn a mirror. Keeping that difference clear was the first rule of the journey. The second was to stop an experiment the moment a witness withdrew consent.

到了傍晚——儘管天空已不再有真正的落日——演員們圍著一盞刻意調小火焰的燈。沒有人被要求交出私人記憶來讓燈更亮。記憶燈只能在得到同意時使用，播放一次場景後便把紀錄交還主人。它能顯示過去道路的模樣，卻不能移動石頭或轉動反光鏡。分清兩者是旅程的第一條規則；第二條是見證人撤回同意時立刻停止實驗。

That evening Yi asked Tao whether a memory could ever count as a map. Tao pointed to the compass bearings they had taken from the pier. 'A memory tells us where to look,' he replied. 'A measurement tells us where to put our feet.' Yi copied the distinction into the revised play. He had spent years hoping that the right words would settle an argument. On the pier he learned that words were most useful when they opened a question that other people were free to test. The island observatory became their next stop, though the ferryman warned them about the tide.

那天晚上，易尋問陶岑：記憶能否算作地圖？陶岑指著他們在碼頭測得的羅盤方位說：「記憶告訴我們該往哪裡找，測量則告訴我們腳該踏在哪裡。」易尋把差別寫進修訂劇本。他多年來一直希望有句對的話就能平息爭論。在渡口，他明白文字最有用的時候，是提出人人都能測試的問題。海島觀測臺成為下一站，儘管船夫警告他們要小心潮水。
