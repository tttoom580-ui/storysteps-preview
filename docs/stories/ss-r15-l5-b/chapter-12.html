玻璃競技場

The Glass Arena

A crowd filled the Glass Arena beneath an enormous crystal canopy. Tickets promised the ultimate sunrise, complete with music and a shower of golden ribbons. Tao recognized his father's bronze shaft turning above the stage, although the machine carried a newer manufacturer's label. Yi recognized something more painful: the opening speech was a shortened version of a script he had written before the valley lost its mornings. The impresario Sel welcomed him as a returning celebrity and offered a private box. Yi refused the favor and asked to watch from the public seats.

人潮擠滿巨大的水晶頂棚下的玻璃競技場。票券承諾最終極的日出，還搭配音樂與金色彩帶雨。陶岑認出父親做的銅軸在舞臺上方轉動，雖然機器已貼上新製造商的標籤。易尋認出更讓他痛苦的東西：開幕演說是他在山谷失去早晨前寫過劇本的刪節版。主辦人賽爾以歡迎回歸的名人稱呼他，提供私人包廂。易尋婉拒，只想坐在一般觀眾席。

The arena's promotional portfolio showed a crystal mirror that could reverse the night. An attorney said a ban on false claims might follow if the owners had committed fraud, but a lawsuit was not yet proof of wrongdoing. In the public session, a nutrition expert warned that the spectacle was not a prescription for prolonged exhaustion. Several residential families had bought tickets at random, hoping for a cure. An usher shoved a rack of costumes into the exit, then hurried to move it when Tao pointed out the danger. Someone poked at the machinery, and a guard ordered the crowd back.

競技場的宣傳作品集展示一面號稱能逆轉黑夜的水晶鏡。律師說若經營者有詐欺情事，可能依法禁止不實宣傳，但訴訟本身尚不能證明有罪。公開場次中，營養專家警告這場奇觀不是治療長期疲憊的處方。數戶住宅區家庭隨機買票，希望獲得療效。引座員把服裝架推進出口，陶岑指出危險後，他才急忙移走。有人伸手戳機器，守衛立即叫人退後。

Tao was not frightened by complicated machinery. He was frightened by the moment when somebody grew certain that machinery would forgive any mistake. He checked the simple parts first: doors, handles, brakes, and the path by which a tired person could escape. Yi stood beside him with the list, correcting a number whenever it was wrong. They had little patience for grand declarations that omitted a missing screw. The work seemed ordinary from a distance, yet every safe step gave their extraordinary journey a reason to continue.

陶岑不怕複雜的機關。他害怕的是有人確信機器能原諒任何錯誤的那一刻。他先檢查簡單的部分：門、把手、煞車，以及疲憊的人可以逃生的路。易尋拿著清單站在旁邊，數字錯了便立即修正。他們對忽略一顆螺絲的大話沒有耐性。遠看這些工作十分平凡，每一步安全的進展卻都給了這場不平凡旅程繼續走下去的理由。

When the show began, the crystal canopy blazed with a scene from a beautiful morning. People wept. Tao felt warmth on his face but noticed that the arena's outer windows remained dark. He lifted a harmless metal strip into the light; it cast no steady shadow. The spectacle came from a sequence of memory projections and heated air, not from the eastern ridge. Sel had never promised in writing that the real sun would appear. The audience, however, had been encouraged to believe exactly that. Yi stood and explained the difference without calling the spectators foolish for wanting hope.

演出開始時，水晶頂棚燃起美麗早晨的景象，人們哭了。陶岑感到臉上一陣暖意，卻發現競技場外窗仍是一片黑。他把無危險的金屬條伸進光裡，卻沒有投出穩定陰影。奇觀來自一連串記憶投影與熱空氣，而非東山。賽爾從未在書面上承諾真太陽會出現，但觀眾顯然被引導相信這件事。易尋站起來解釋差別，沒有嘲笑觀眾希望得到光明的心情。

The impresario offered to purchase Tao's diagnostic equipment to settle the dispute. Tao declined because it would leave the mountain communities without tools. Yi asked to see the original contract, and Sel allowed a copy to be inspected in the presence of witnesses. Its language was carefully plural: it promised experiences of dawn, not daylight itself. Yi admitted he had once helped write that ambiguity. He apologized to the audience for his part in it, then announced that he would revise the script before accepting another commission. Sel gave no answer, but the listeners had heard the distinction.

主辦人提議買下陶岑的診斷器材，以平息爭議。陶岑拒絕，因為那會讓山區失去工具。易尋要求查看原始合約，賽爾准許在見證人面前檢查副本。文字刻意使用複數與模糊說法：保證體驗黎明，不保證真正日光。易尋承認自己當年協助寫出這種歧義。他為此向觀眾道歉，並表示在接受其他委託前會先修訂劇本。賽爾沒有回答，但聽眾已聽到差別。

At sunset—although the sky no longer offered a true sunset—the cast gathered around a lamp whose flame was deliberately small. No one was asked to surrender a private memory to make it brighter. Their memory lanterns worked only with permission, played a scene once, and then returned the recording to its owner. The lanterns could reveal how a road had looked; they could not move a stone or turn a mirror. Keeping that difference clear was the first rule of the journey. The second was to stop an experiment the moment a witness withdrew consent.

到了傍晚——儘管天空已不再有真正的落日——演員們圍著一盞刻意調小火焰的燈。沒有人被要求交出私人記憶來讓燈更亮。記憶燈只能在得到同意時使用，播放一次場景後便把紀錄交還主人。它能顯示過去道路的模樣，卻不能移動石頭或轉動反光鏡。分清兩者是旅程的第一條規則；第二條是見證人撤回同意時立刻停止實驗。

Outside the arena, Tao asked why Yi had kept his old work secret. Yi answered that shame had made silence seem easier than correction. Tao said that trusting him would take more than a public apology. It was not punishment; a person who had once hidden the truth could not demand immediate belief. Yi accepted the answer. For the first time, Tao touched his shoulder without needing to steady a ladder. They agreed to travel together to the mountain control room, where the real mirrors still waited. Their partnership was damaged, but it had not ended.

在競技場外，陶岑問易尋為何一直隱瞞舊工作。易尋答道，羞恥讓沉默看起來比改正容易。陶岑說信任他需要的不只是公開道歉。這不是懲罰；曾隱藏真相的人不能要求他人立刻相信。易尋接受了回答。陶岑第一次不是為了扶住梯子，而是主動碰了碰他的肩。他們同意一起去山上控制室，那裡仍有真正的鏡子等待修復。夥伴關係受了傷，卻沒有結束。

Tao warned an attendant not to depress the emergency lever as a joke. The attendant's intent was harmless, but even a playful poke could trigger confusion, and nobody was allowed to shove through the narrow aisle.

陶岑警告工作人員不要把緊急拉桿當笑話按下。那人的意圖沒有惡意，但玩笑式一戳也可能造成混亂，任何人都不可在狹窄走道推擠。
