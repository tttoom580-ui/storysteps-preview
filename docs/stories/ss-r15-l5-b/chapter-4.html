The School of Two Angles

不販售的黎明：夜幕劇團 · 第 4 / 25 章 · 4000–5000 · 英中對照 · 校訂中

用互動版查詞、朗讀與收藏

Stoneleaf School stood on a road used by commuters from three provinces. Its science teacher had painted a bright sun on the ceiling to comfort pupils who had never known an ordinary morning. She welcomed Tao's instruments but worried that a public experiment would frighten children. Yi proposed a rehearsal in which every pupil could make a prediction, measure the result, and change an answer without punishment. The teacher agreed. Their model used two parallel screens and a narrow mirror, allowing a beam from the same lamp to reach either wall but never both at full strength.

石葉學校坐落在三個省份通勤者共同使用的路旁。科學教師在天花板畫了一顆明亮太陽，安慰從未經歷真正早晨的學生。她歡迎陶岑的儀器，卻擔心公開試驗會嚇到孩子。易尋提議讓每位學生都能預測、測量結果，再不受懲罰地修改答案。老師同意了。他們的模型使用兩面平行屏幕與一面窄鏡，使同一盞燈的光可以照向任何一面牆，卻無法同時讓兩面都獲得最大亮度。

The school faculty had to anticipate objections from a dense bureaucracy. One administrator called the lesson a coincidence and issued a denial that its old diagrams were wrong. Tao showed the class how to measure the diameter of the reflected circle. Its distinctive outline changed with the angle, not with the wishes of the audience. The experiment required only a fraction of the school's fiscal budget, yet it revealed a serious hazard: the official image ignored a second district behind the screen. A visiting marine practitioner offered a plea to include fishing families in the demonstration.

學校教師團隊必須預先考慮繁複官僚體系的反對。一位行政人員說試驗只是巧合，否認舊圖有錯。陶岑教學生量測反射圓面的直徑；它獨特的形狀會隨角度改變，不會因觀眾的願望而改變。實驗只用掉學校財政預算的一小部分，卻揭露嚴重風險：官方圖忽略了屏幕後的另一個街區。一名研究海洋健康的實務工作者懇請他們把漁家納入示範。

The silence between Tao and Yi had changed. At first it had been filled with accusations neither dared to speak. Later it became the quiet required to hear a tiny fault inside a moving mechanism. They still disagreed. Tao trusted what his hands could measure, while Yi could imagine how a choice might affect people not yet in the room. Together they tested both kinds of knowledge against reality. The company followed neither man blindly, and that, more than the repaired hinges, was why the stage continued to travel.

陶岑與易尋之間的沉默已經改變。最初，那裡充滿兩人都不敢說出口的責難；後來變成聽見運轉機關裡細小故障所需的安靜。他們仍會意見不同。陶岑相信雙手測量得到的東西，易尋則能想像選擇如何影響尚未進房的人。他們一起把兩種知識放到現實裡檢驗。劇團沒有盲從任何一人；比起修好的鉸鏈，這才是舞臺得以繼續旅行的原因。

A quiet girl challenged the model by placing her lunch box behind the left screen. Tao did not correct her immediately. He asked the class to observe what happened when the box interrupted the light. The shadow shifted in a way neither of the older charts predicted. A debate began, but it did not become a contest in which the loudest child won. The teacher moved the students around the room so that each could see from a different position. By the end, the children were explaining the limitation of the experiment more clearly than the visiting officials.

一名安靜的女孩把便當盒放到左側屏幕後，挑戰原有模型。陶岑沒有立刻糾正她，而是請全班觀察盒子阻斷光線的情形。陰影的變化是兩張舊圖都沒預測到的。討論開始了，卻沒有變成聲音最大的人獲勝的比賽。老師讓學生繞教室移動，從不同位置看見結果。課程結束時，孩子們對實驗限制的解釋甚至比來訪官員還清楚。

A physician explained that the hormone systems of exhausted workers could not be healed by one dramatic flash of light. She spoke carefully about mortality without using fear to silence questions. She asked the teacher to reassure families that the show was not a treatment, and she offered to uncover reliable information about prolonged darkness. Yi removed a scene in which a single beam cured every illness. He replaced it with a modest moment: a boy noticing that his mother's hands were finally warm enough to hold his own. The change made the performance quieter and more truthful.

一名醫師解釋，疲憊工人的荷爾蒙系統不會被一瞬華麗光芒治好。她謹慎談及死亡率，卻不以恐懼壓住提問。她請老師讓家長放心：演出不是治療，並願意協助揭露長期黑暗的可靠資訊。易尋刪掉劇中一束光就能治百病的橋段，換成樸素場景：男孩發現母親的手終於暖得足以握住他的手。這個改動讓演出更安靜，也更真實。

The company did not call a disagreement a betrayal. Tao drew the problem twice, once as the people in the room described it and again as the equipment would behave. The drawings did not always agree. Yi read both accounts aloud, including the inconvenient parts. They had learned that an audience could understand a difficult choice when it was shown the cost as well as the benefit. If someone wished to leave before the decision, the exit remained clear; the theatre had once been used to hold people in place, and they refused to repeat that mistake.

劇團不把意見相左當作背叛。陶岑把問題畫兩次：一次依照房內人們的說法，一次依照機關實際運作的方式。兩張圖未必一致。易尋將兩種說法連同不方便的部分都念出來。他們發現，只要把代價與好處一同展示，觀眾就能理解艱難的選擇。想在決定前離開的人始終有出口；劇場曾被用來困住人，他們拒絕重蹈覆轍。

The students offered their drawings to the travelling theatre. Yi asked whether they wanted their names printed beside the pictures, and some said yes while others preferred a symbol. Tao packed the screens carefully so the class could borrow them again. Before leaving, he left instructions on how to repeat the experiment with a household lamp. The teacher promised to teach both successful results and failed guesses. Later on the road, Tao admitted that he had feared the lesson might destroy the children's hope. Yi answered that hope built on a question could last longer than hope built on a perfect drawing.

學生把畫送給巡迴劇團。易尋問他們要不要把姓名印在作品旁，有人願意，有人更想用符號。陶岑細心打包屏幕，方便班級再借用。離開前，他留下用家用燈重做實驗的步驟。老師答應同時教授成功的結果與錯誤的猜測。後來在路上，陶岑承認自己原先擔心課程會毀掉孩子的希望。易尋回答：建立在提問上的希望，可能比建立在完美圖畫上的希望更長久。
