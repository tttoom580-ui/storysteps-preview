布做的邊界

A Boundary Made of Cloth

不販售的黎明：夜幕劇團 · 第 9 / 25 章 · 4000–5000 · 英中對照 · 校訂中

用互動版查詞、朗讀與收藏

Sandstep Crossing lay between a green valley and a region of bare, wind-shaped stone. A high cloth fence separated permanent houses from a camp used by seasonal performers. Tao noticed that the fence followed the line of an old stream rather than any defensible boundary. Yi spoke with a family who had stayed there for seven winters, paying for each season yet never gaining permission to use the main well. The eldest child wore one repaired sandal and carried a small souvenir of the summer sun. He asked not for a miracle, but for a way to reach school without being stopped.

沙階關口位在翠綠山谷與風蝕裸石之間。一道高布籬將固定住宅和季節性表演者的營地隔開。陶岑發現布籬沿著舊溪線，而非任何合理界線。易尋與一戶已在此過了七個冬天的家庭談話：他們每季付費，卻始終沒有權使用主井。長子穿著一隻補過的涼鞋，帶著夏日陽光的小紀念品。他所求的不是奇蹟，只是上學不用再被攔下的路。

A stubborn ass carried the props behind a hired bodyguard. At the boundary office, a form claimed that the household would comprise only people eligible for permanent residence. A clerk began to discriminate against the traveling family, although their valid receipts showed years of legal work. The investigator assigned to the crossing examined the current version of the form and found that an outdated rule had been incorporated without approval. A passionate argument followed. Tao patched the torn fence rather than undo it at once, because removing the barrier without a traffic plan could expose vulnerable children to the carts.

一頭倔強的驢子在雇來的保鑣後方馱著道具。關口表格聲稱，家庭成員只能包含符合永久居留資格的人。職員歧視流動家庭，儘管有效收據證明他們多年合法工作。負責此關的調查員檢查現行表格版本，發現過時規則未經核准便被納入。激烈爭論隨之而起。陶岑先補好破布籬，沒有立刻拆掉，因為沒有交通方案就拆除障礙，會讓脆弱的孩子暴露在車流前。

The company did not call a disagreement a betrayal. Tao drew the problem twice, once as the people in the room described it and again as the equipment would behave. The drawings did not always agree. Yi read both accounts aloud, including the inconvenient parts. They had learned that an audience could understand a difficult choice when it was shown the cost as well as the benefit. If someone wished to leave before the decision, the exit remained clear; the theatre had once been used to hold people in place, and they refused to repeat that mistake.

劇團不把意見相左當作背叛。陶岑把問題畫兩次：一次依照房內人們的說法，一次依照機關實際運作的方式。兩張圖未必一致。易尋將兩種說法連同不方便的部分都念出來。他們發現，只要把代價與好處一同展示，觀眾就能理解艱難的選擇。想在決定前離開的人始終有出口；劇場曾被用來困住人，他們拒絕重蹈覆轍。

Yi interviewed both sides without asking the children to repeat details that made them uncomfortable. A school counselor offered a private room for anyone who needed support. The older boy drew the route as he remembered it: a pyramid-shaped rock, a shallow channel, and three lamps beside the school entrance. Tao walked the path and measured its slope. The crossing could be made safer with a raised wooden strip and two signs. The real difficulty was not technical. Officials had learned to treat a family that moved as if it had no history.

易尋訪問兩邊居民，沒有要求孩子重述令他們不舒服的細節。學校輔導員提供私人房間，讓需要支持的人使用。長子憑記憶畫出路線：金字塔形岩石、淺水道，以及校門旁三盞燈。陶岑實際走一遍，量測坡度。只需加設一條架高木道和兩面標誌，就能讓通道安全些。真正的難題不是技術，而是官員習慣把會移動的家庭當作沒有歷史。

A local therapist warned that the children were tired of proving their residence to strangers. At the camp, a pot of soup stayed fluid over a small fire, and the family invited the troupe to eat without asking it to take sides in every dispute. Yi explained that he wanted to incorporate their route into the public map only with their agreement. Tao insisted that their new patch of road must remain useful after the theatre left. The investigator wrote the change in a formal record rather than promising an informal exception that could disappear next week.

當地治療師提醒，孩子早已厭倦向陌生人一再證明居住事實。營地裡一鍋湯在小火上保持流動，家庭邀劇團用餐，卻沒要求他們在每件爭議中都表態。易尋解釋，只有取得同意才會把他們的路線納入公共地圖。陶岑堅持新的補修道路必須在劇團離開後仍能使用。調查員把變更寫進正式紀錄，而非只答應下週可能消失的口頭特例。

For a while they worked without speech. Tao listened for the change in a gear's rhythm, and Yi marked each small result on a sheet that anyone could inspect. No single successful movement made the whole mechanism safe. One wheel could turn freely while another carried too much weight. Every test therefore ended with the same question: who stood beyond the wall, where the operators could not see? They invited those people inside before trying the next adjustment. A witness was not merely a person asked to applaud; a witness could say that the experiment must stop.

有一陣子，他們沒有交談。陶岑聽著齒輪節奏的變化，易尋把每一個細小結果記在人人可檢視的紙上。某個零件成功轉動，不代表整套機關就安全；一個輪子可能運轉順暢，另一個卻承受太多重量。因此每次測試結束，他們都問：牆外還站著誰，是操作員看不見的？他們先邀請那些人進來，才繼續調整。見證人不只是被請來鼓掌的人，也能說出實驗必須停止。

They opened the safe crossing at the next shift change. The cloth fence remained on either side of the ramp, but a gate now recognized both groups' right to pass. The boy crossed with his sister and stopped to look back at the lanterns. He did not thank the theatre as if it had given him a home; the home had been his all along. Yi read the revised route to the clerk, and Tao checked the last bolt. Their next road led toward the desert archives, where the missing eastern mirror had once been built.

下次換班時，安全通道啟用了。斜道兩旁仍有布籬，但一道新門承認兩群人都有通行權。男孩陪妹妹跨過去，停下來回看燈光。他沒有像被劇團賜予家園那樣道謝，因為家原本就是他的。易尋把修訂路線讀給職員聽，陶岑檢查最後一顆螺栓。下一條路通往沙漠檔案庫，失落的東方鏡子曾在那裡建造。
