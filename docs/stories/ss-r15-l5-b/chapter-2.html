計算陰影的山脊

The Ridge That Counted Shadows

不販售的黎明：夜幕劇團 · 第 2 / 25 章 · 4000–5000 · 英中對照 · 校訂中

用互動版查詞、朗讀與收藏

The road to Cedar Ridge climbed above the last trees. Tao carried a brass angle gauge, while Yi walked beside the wagon reading letters from residents who could no longer judge when to open their shops. The company found an old maintenance hut surrounded by instruments. One sensor still flashed green, although its protective cover was cracked. Tao suspected the display had been painted to reassure inspectors. He asked the apprentices to haul the heavy mirror frame onto a level platform, but forbade them to turn the mountain's working mechanism until the villages below had been warned.

通往雪松山脊的路爬過最後一排樹。陶岑攜著黃銅角度規，易尋走在車旁，讀著居民來信：他們如今連何時開店都拿不準。劇團找到一間滿是儀器的舊維修屋。一枚光感器還閃著綠燈，防護罩卻已裂開。陶岑懷疑有人把顯示器塗成讓檢查員安心的顏色。他請學徒把沉重的鏡框搬上平臺，但在通知山下村莊前，嚴禁任何人轉動仍在運作的山機。

A corporate survey team arrived with an extraordinary device that could convert reflected light into a printed graph. Its leader claimed their destination was a prosperous ridge resort, not a public repair site. She spoke of a seasonal epidemic of exhaustion among workers who had lived without real daylight. It was a beneficial reason to hurry, but haste would not cancel liability. A local respondent described the temptation to plunge the entire valley into darkness for one spectacular show. Yi wrote the warning down before running an errand to the nearest village with a reminder of the proposed test.

一家企業測量隊帶來一件非凡儀器，能把反射光轉換成印出的圖表。領隊聲稱她們的目的地是興旺的山脊度假地，而非公共修繕場所。她談到長年缺乏真正日照的工人出現一波疲憊問題。這確實是加快行動的有益理由，卻不能因此免除責任。當地一名受訪者說起為了華麗演出而讓整個山谷陷入黑暗的誘惑。易尋先記下警告，才跑腿去最近的村子，送達測試提醒。

Yi kept his notebook beside the lantern rather than beneath it. He had once hidden uncomfortable facts behind beautiful lines, believing that an elegant story could make a hard decision easier to bear. Now he placed the plain description first and the poetry second. Tao respected the change but did not pretend that trust had been restored in a single conversation. When Yi offered to hand over the notebook, Tao said, 'Keep it. Read it to me when something changes.' The request sounded modest. It also meant they would continue speaking to one another after the audience went home.

易尋把筆記放在燈旁，而不是藏在燈下。他曾把不舒服的事實藏在漂亮台詞後面，以為優美故事能讓艱難決定比較容易接受。如今他先寫平實描述，再寫詩句。陶岑尊重這份改變，但不假裝一次談話就能恢復信任。易尋想交出筆記，陶岑說：「你留著。事情有變就讀給我聽。」這要求聽來很小，卻代表觀眾回家後，他們仍會繼續對彼此說話。

At noon, or what the clock called noon, Tao built a screen from linen and wooden pegs. Every half-hour he compared a real shadow with the false clock on the sensor. Yi drew the results in large letters so that anyone standing farther back could read them. A shepherd noticed a narrow beam arriving three minutes later than the schedule allowed. That was the first piece of evidence that the eastern mirrors were not simply broken: someone had left the system balanced for a different arrangement of towns. The discovery changed their destination from the resort to a disused control tower.

到了鐘面所稱的正午，陶岑用亞麻布與木樁搭起屏幕。每半小時，他都把真實陰影和感器的假時鐘比較。易尋以大字記錄結果，讓站得遠的人也能看清。一名牧人發現一道窄光束比時程晚了三分鐘到達。這是第一項證據，顯示東方鏡陣不只是損壞，而是有人讓系統停留在另一種城鎮分配狀態。於是目的地從度假村改成廢棄控制塔。

The guard offered a pot of stew while the workers trimmed the torn canvas. A torch cast uneven lines across the stone floor, and Yi insisted that the team remain vocal about every doubtful reading. 'Call it a transformation if you like,' Tao told the survey leader, 'but the mirror has not transformed the amount of sunlight. It has only changed who receives it.' The leader folded her graph. She had expected a publicity opportunity and instead received a record that could be independently checked. No one was asked to surrender the equipment in order to disagree.

工人修剪破帆布時，守衛端上一鍋燉菜。一支火把在石地上投出不規則線條，易尋堅持每個人都要把可疑讀數大聲說出來。「你可以稱它為轉變，」陶岑對領隊說，「但鏡子並沒有改變陽光總量，只改變誰得到它。」領隊折起圖紙。她原本期待宣傳機會，得到的卻是一份可以獨立核對的紀錄。沒有人因為提出異議就被要求交出儀器。

The road carried more than the stage wagon. It carried unfinished apologies, borrowed tools, and promises whose meaning changed with the people who heard them. The actors could pack a scene into a chest, but they could not pack away its consequences. When the wheels stopped, Tao asked what the nearest household needed before asking what the theatre needed. Yi learned to make the same distinction in his scripts: a moving speech was not proof that a plan would work, and a quiet objection deserved as much attention as a cheer.

路上運送的不只是舞臺車，還有未完成的道歉、借來的工具，以及會隨聽者改變意義的承諾。演員能把場景收進箱子，卻不能把後果也收起來。車輪停下時，陶岑先問最近的人家需要什麼，再問劇團需要什麼。易尋也學會在劇本中區分兩者：動人的演說不等於方案可行，輕聲提出的異議和歡呼同樣值得傾聽。

When the party descended, a child asked whether the sun had moved farther away. Tao showed him the angle gauge and let him turn its harmless wooden pointer. The child measured an ordinary cloud as it passed the ridge, then understood the difference between a distant light and a local obstruction. Yi watched Tao explain without showing off. He wanted to ask why the carpenter had left his successful theatre career, but the question could wait. For now, the ridge had given them something better than applause: a test anyone could repeat with simple tools.

隊伍下山時，一名孩子問太陽是不是飛得更遠了。陶岑給他看角度規，讓他轉動無危險的木製指針。孩子測量一朵平凡的雲飄過山脊，便明白遙遠的光和當地遮蔽物有何不同。易尋看著陶岑毫不炫耀地解說，很想問他為何離開成功的劇場生涯，但那問題可以稍後再談。如今山脊給他們的比掌聲更好：一套用簡單工具就能重做的測試。

A carpenter asked Tao to trim the rough ends of the screen before anyone stood beside it. The small correction prevented cloth from catching on the frame during the next test.

木匠請陶岑先修剪屏幕粗糙邊緣，再讓任何人靠近。小小修正避免下一次測試時布料勾住框架。
