The Market of Other Names

不販售的黎明：夜幕劇團 · 第 8 / 25 章 · 4000–5000 · 英中對照 · 校訂中

The Lantern Bazaar had once welcomed traders from every side of the valley. Now its central stage was locked after dark, and newcomers could rent only the narrow space behind the stalls. Yi learned that the rule had been imposed to prevent chaos during the first nights of permanent twilight. It was supposed to be temporary, but nobody had reviewed it. Tao watched a family rehearse a dance in a passage too small for their instruments. He asked Madam Song to lend them the theatre's portable floor, provided everyone could leave safely if the passage became crowded.

燈籠市集曾歡迎山谷各方商人，如今中心舞臺入夜就鎖上，新來的人只能租攤位後方的窄小空間。易尋得知規則是在永恆暮色剛開始、為防混亂而設的，原應只是暫時措施，卻一直沒人檢討。陶岑看見一家人在小得放不下樂器的通道裡排舞，便請宋夫人借出可攜式舞臺地板，前提是人群擁擠時人人仍能安全離開。

An alien traveler looked frightened by the gathering, but the market teacher insisted that comprehension mattered more than perfect accents. Residents were entitled to ask questions, she said, while performers needed flexibility in their language. The organizers tried to minimize noise and rejected a myth that outsiders had caused the failing sun. A ragged performer from the old beauty salon described a shortage of seats, not a shortage of talent. The light spectrum cast different colors on their painted faces, and one musician warned that the procession might stall unless the exit remained clear.

一位外地旅人被聚會嚇得不安，但市集教師堅持理解比完美口音更重要。她說居民有權發問，表演者則需要語言上的彈性。主辦方努力減少噪音，否定外來者造成太陽失靈的神話。一名從舊美容沙龍來、衣衫襤褸的表演者說，缺少的是座位，不是人才。光的光譜在他們彩繪的臉上投出不同顏色，一名樂手提醒：若出口不夠順暢，遊行就可能停滯。

At sunset—although the sky no longer offered a true sunset—the cast gathered around a lamp whose flame was deliberately small. No one was asked to surrender a private memory to make it brighter. Their memory lanterns worked only with permission, played a scene once, and then returned the recording to its owner. The lanterns could reveal how a road had looked; they could not move a stone or turn a mirror. Keeping that difference clear was the first rule of the journey. The second was to stop an experiment the moment a witness withdrew consent.

到了傍晚——儘管天空已不再有真正的落日——演員們圍著一盞刻意調小火焰的燈。沒有人被要求交出私人記憶來讓燈更亮。記憶燈只能在得到同意時使用，播放一次場景後便把紀錄交還主人。它能顯示過去道路的模樣，卻不能移動石頭或轉動反光鏡。分清兩者是旅程的第一條規則；第二條是見證人撤回同意時立刻停止實驗。

Yi asked each group to describe one thing they wanted the audience to understand. A baker wanted to speak about labor; a dancer wanted to show the memory of an evening river; a child wanted to perform in the language her grandmother used at home. The result was not a single grand history but a sequence of small scenes with separate owners. Tao arranged the lamps to avoid casting shadows on the steps. No lantern was allowed to take a memory without the donor's clear permission. When the stage opened, the audience heard several accents without being told which one was correct.

易尋請每個團體說一件希望觀眾理解的事。麵包師想談勞動，舞者想展示傍晚河流的記憶，孩子想用祖母在家說的語言演出。結果不是一部宏大歷史，而是一連串有不同主人、各自完整的小場景。陶岑調好燈，避免階梯落入陰影。沒有明確同意，任何記憶燈都不得擷取內容。舞臺打開時，觀眾聽見多種口音，卻沒人被告知哪一種才是正確的。

The town's strategic committee voted to suspend the old restriction for a supervised trial. A scholar offered a theoretical argument about citizenship, while a musician paid tribute to the market's first migrant performers. One guard demanded a visa from a child whose family had lived there for years. The teacher replied that the stage application required no such document whatsoever. A narrow blue vein of light appeared above the stalls when Tao adjusted a screen, but he refused to let the striking sight distract from the decision that had made the evening possible.

市鎮策略委員會表決暫停舊禁令，進行有人監督的試辦。一位學者提出關於公民身分的理論論點，音樂家則向市集最初的移民表演者致敬。一名守衛向已在此住了多年的孩子索取簽證。教師回答：舞臺申請根本不需要這類文件。陶岑調整屏幕時，攤位上方出現一道細藍色光脈，但他拒絕讓這番醒目景象分散大家對真正促成演出的決議的注意。

The silence between Tao and Yi had changed. At first it had been filled with accusations neither dared to speak. Later it became the quiet required to hear a tiny fault inside a moving mechanism. They still disagreed. Tao trusted what his hands could measure, while Yi could imagine how a choice might affect people not yet in the room. Together they tested both kinds of knowledge against reality. The company followed neither man blindly, and that, more than the repaired hinges, was why the stage continued to travel.

陶岑與易尋之間的沉默已經改變。最初，那裡充滿兩人都不敢說出口的責難；後來變成聽見運轉機關裡細小故障所需的安靜。他們仍會意見不同。陶岑相信雙手測量得到的東西，易尋則能想像選擇如何影響尚未進房的人。他們一起把兩種知識放到現實裡檢驗。劇團沒有盲從任何一人；比起修好的鉸鏈，這才是舞臺得以繼續旅行的原因。

By the final bow, a woman who had never entered the central square asked to help organize the next gathering. Madam Song welcomed her without promising that every future performance would be easy. Yi gave her the draft rules and invited corrections. Tao rolled up the portable floor so the market workers could use the square again before dawn that never came. The night's success did not repair a single mountain mirror. It did show the company how much a community could change without demanding that everyone become alike. Yi wrote that lesson beside the route to the old observatory.

謝幕時，一位從未進過中心廣場的女士主動要求協助籌辦下一次聚會。宋夫人歡迎她，卻沒有保證未來每場演出都輕鬆。易尋交出規則草稿，歡迎修改。陶岑捲起可攜地板，讓市集工人在那永遠不會到來的黎明前重新使用廣場。這一夜沒有修好任何山上的鏡子，卻讓劇團看見社區不要求人人變得相同，也能產生多大改變。易尋把這一課記在前往舊觀測臺的路線旁。

'No ticket can entitle its holder to silence another performer,' Madam Song told the crowd. The actors repeated the rule in several languages, allowing everyone to hear the same condition before the show began.

「沒有任何票券能讓持有人有權叫其他表演者閉嘴，」宋夫人對人群說。演員用多種語言覆述規則，讓所有人在開演前聽到相同條件。
