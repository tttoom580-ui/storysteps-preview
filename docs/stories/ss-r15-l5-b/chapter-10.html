旱地研究所

The Drought Institute

不販售的黎明：夜幕劇團 · 第 10 / 25 章 · 4000–5000 · 英中對照 · 校訂中

Dryglass Institute had a garden of stones rather than flowers. Its director welcomed the travellers with a cup of bitter tea and an apology for the lack of fresh vegetables. The surrounding wilderness had been drying since the mirror failure, although no one knew how much of the drought came from reduced sunlight and how much from changing wind. Tao did not accept a neat explanation merely because it matched his hopes. He asked to inspect the old cooling vents, while Yi met the researchers who had once measured the weather before the false twilight began.

乾鏡研究所的園子裡是石頭而非花。所長以一杯苦茶迎接旅人，為沒有新鮮蔬菜致歉。自鏡陣故障以來，周邊荒野逐漸乾燥，但沒人知道旱象有多少源於日光減少，又有多少來自風向改變。陶岑不會因為某種整齊的解釋符合希望就照單全收。他要求檢查舊冷卻風口，易尋則去見在虛假暮色到來前量過天氣的研究者。

The laboratory's preliminary report cited collaboration between an agricultural counselor and a mechanical institute. A teacher with a wheat allergy could not join the outdoor grill, but she offered precise descriptive notes on plant digestion and growth. The bulk of the data was stored at a terminal that had lost power. 'Don't compel a conclusion,' she said when a student began to exclaim that the mirrors caused every drought. A rational method required considering each variable, even a radical one that the research team disliked. Yi was nearly overwhelmed by the contradictions but kept taking notes.

研究室的初步報告引用了農業顧問與機械研究所的合作成果。一名對小麥過敏的老師無法參加戶外烤食，卻提供精確的植物消化與生長描述。大部分數據存在失去電力的終端機裡。學生大喊鏡子造成所有旱災時，她說：「不要逼出結論。」理性方法得考慮每個變數，即使是研究團隊不喜歡的激進假說。易尋差點被互相矛盾的資料壓垮，卻仍持續記錄。

In the travelling theatre, every prop had a history. A cracked bowl had survived a mountain winter; a blue curtain had been patched by people who disagreed about almost everything except the need for shelter. Yi wanted to preserve those marks rather than paint over them. Tao sometimes preferred a neat surface, because a repairer likes to see a job finished. They argued, then found that the scars of use helped newcomers understand what had broken and why. By night, their quarrel had become another practical lesson for the apprentices who carried the lamps.

巡迴劇團裡，每件道具都有歷史。裂了的碗熬過山區冬天；藍色幕布由彼此幾乎什麼都談不攏、卻都認為需要避難處所的人縫補。易尋想保留那些痕跡，不願把它們塗平。陶岑有時偏好整齊表面，因為修復師喜歡看見工作完成。他們爭過之後才發現，使用過的傷痕能讓新來的人明白哪裡壞了、為何會壞。入夜時，他們的爭論已成為提燈學徒的一堂實用課。

The director found a paper copy of an early wind chart. It showed a narrow channel behind the observatory where daylight could be redirected into the valley without starving the riverside crops. That did not mean the channel was currently safe. A storm had damaged the retaining wall years earlier, and nobody had checked it since. Tao asked for an inspection instead of permission to operate. The director agreed to share the chart with the five towns and made two duplicate copies. She would not let a theatrical performance substitute for the missing maintenance work.

所長找出一張早年的風向紙圖，顯示觀測臺後有道窄谷，可把日光引入山谷而不奪走河邊農田的照射。但這不代表窄谷如今安全。多年前暴風損壞擋土牆，此後沒人再檢查。陶岑要求勘查，而不是要求立即操作。所長同意把圖交給五鎮，另做兩份複本。她不准用戲劇表演取代失落的維護工作。

One apprentice wanted to turn the findings into a thriller: a hidden room, a villain, and a perfect confession. Yi refused. The real story contained cautious people who had done some things well and neglected others. He could make the material moving without pretending that every scientist was either a savior or a criminal. When the team presented its observations, Tao displayed the broken retaining wall in a simple scale model. Anyone looking at it could see why the next journey would have to include engineers as well as actors.

一名學徒想把發現改成驚悚劇：密室、惡人、完美自白。易尋拒絕了。真實故事裡有人謹慎、有些事做得好、有些事卻忽略。他能讓故事動人，而不必把每個科學家都寫成救世主或罪犯。團隊展示觀察時，陶岑用簡單縮尺模型呈現損壞擋土牆。任何人一看便知道，下一段旅程除了演員，還必須有工程師。

At the shared meal, there was no seat reserved for a hero. The youngest apprentice served soup to the oldest carpenter, and the carpenter passed bread to a visitor who had spoken against their plan. Tao noticed that Yi remembered people's names even when he forgot the exact date of a performance. It made the journey feel less like a competition for praise. What mattered was whether the next town would have enough information to make its own decision after the actors had gone. They prepared instructions that could be followed without them, and tested those instructions on someone who had never seen the equipment.

共餐時沒有保留給英雄的座位。最年輕的學徒替最老的木匠盛湯，木匠又把麵包遞給曾反對計畫的訪客。陶岑注意到，易尋即使忘了演出的確切日期，也記得人們的姓名。這讓旅程不再像爭奪讚美的比賽。真正重要的是：演員離開後，下一座城是否仍有足夠資訊自行決定。他們寫下不需要兩人在場也能操作的說明，並請一位從未見過機器的人實際照著做。

They left the institute with more questions than answers, which disappointed the younger actors. Madam Song told them that uncertainty could be useful if it had clear boundaries. The chart showed where an alternative route might lie; it did not prove the route would carry enough light. The samples suggested drought stress but did not identify one cause. Yi asked Tao whether their play could end with an unanswered question. Tao smiled and said, 'Only if the audience knows what we will test next.' The following morning they set out for the mountain relay where the old charts ended.

他們帶著比答案更多的問題離開研究所，年輕演員十分失望。宋夫人告訴他們，只要界線清楚，不確定也有用。圖紙顯示替代路線可能在哪裡，卻沒證明它能送來足夠陽光。樣本顯示作物承受乾旱壓力，卻無法指出單一原因。易尋問陶岑戲能不能以未解之謎結尾。陶岑笑著說：「只要觀眾知道下一步要測什麼。」隔天清晨，他們前往舊圖紙終止的山上接力站。

The director asked each researcher to cite the source of every figure, including the rainfall data that seemed to support the most popular theory. Yi copied the references instead of choosing only those that made a dramatic plot.

所長要求每位研究員引用每筆數字的來源，包括看似支持熱門理論的降雨資料。易尋抄下參考資料，而非只挑能寫出戲劇性情節的部分。
