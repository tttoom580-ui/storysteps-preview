夜裡的廚房

The Night Kitchen

不販售的黎明：夜幕劇團 · 第 6 / 25 章 · 4000–5000 · 英中對照 · 校訂中

Bracken Kitchen was the first place that asked the travelling theatre for food instead of entertainment. The cooks had lost the daylight needed to dry grain, and several workers were ill. Tao set aside the stage wagon and helped repair the ovens. Yi carried water from an approved spring, careful not to pretend that a play could fill an empty stomach. The kitchen was also a rest station for night workers who maintained the old mirror tunnels. When an apprentice found a narrow door beneath the pantry, the owner warned them not to open it until its rusted frame had been examined.

蕨葉食堂是第一個向巡迴劇團索求食物而不是演出的地方。廚師失去曬乾穀物所需的日光，幾名工人也病倒了。陶岑先放下舞臺車，幫忙修理烤爐。易尋從核准的泉水取水，小心不假裝一齣戲能填飽空肚。食堂也供維護舊鏡道的夜班工人歇腳。學徒在儲藏室下發現一道窄門，老闆警告：檢查生鏽門框之前不可打開。

The municipal nurse placed food safety first on the meeting agenda. 'Beware of the well,' she said, as a child tried to cling to its rope. A federal health notice used the genre of a dry report, but Yi turned its central warning into a clear metaphor for the stage. During a children's baseball inning, a young nominee for the town committee called the contaminated water an isolated accident. The nurse showed that the same organism had appeared in samples upstream. No one could overtake the problem by speaking faster or louder than the evidence.

市鎮護理師把食品安全列在議程第一項。孩子想抓住井繩，她便說：「小心那口井。」聯邦衛生公告寫得像枯燥報告，易尋卻把核心警告轉成舞臺上清楚的比喻。孩童棒球賽進行到一局時，一名市鎮委員候選人把污染說成偶發事故。護理師拿出上游也驗出相同微生物的樣本。沒有人能靠說得比證據更快、更大聲就超越問題。

At the shared meal, there was no seat reserved for a hero. The youngest apprentice served soup to the oldest carpenter, and the carpenter passed bread to a visitor who had spoken against their plan. Tao noticed that Yi remembered people's names even when he forgot the exact date of a performance. It made the journey feel less like a competition for praise. What mattered was whether the next town would have enough information to make its own decision after the actors had gone. They prepared instructions that could be followed without them, and tested those instructions on someone who had never seen the equipment.

共餐時沒有保留給英雄的座位。最年輕的學徒替最老的木匠盛湯，木匠又把麵包遞給曾反對計畫的訪客。陶岑注意到，易尋即使忘了演出的確切日期，也記得人們的姓名。這讓旅程不再像爭奪讚美的比賽。真正重要的是：演員離開後，下一座城是否仍有足夠資訊自行決定。他們寫下不需要兩人在場也能操作的說明，並請一位從未見過機器的人實際照著做。

Tao inspected the door without opening it. Its hinges were covered in white mineral residue, and a faint vibration travelled through the floor. He placed a small ball against the wall to see whether the ground was moving. The ball rolled toward the kitchen's western corner, where the old floor sagged. The cook remembered that a maintenance corridor had once run beneath the building, carrying cold air past the ovens. An accident there might have harmed people even before the mirrors failed. The company postponed its performance and prepared a safe route to the courtyard.

陶岑不開門，只檢查門框。鉸鏈覆有白色礦物殘留，地板傳來微弱震動。他把小球放在牆邊，測試地面是否傾斜。球滾向食堂西角，那裡的舊地板下陷。廚師想起，以前有條維修走廊從建築下穿過，讓冷空氣流過烤爐。鏡陣故障之前，那裡的事故就可能傷人。劇團延後演出，先準備通往院子的安全路線。

An angry baker compared the repair to a rebellion against the old guild, but Tao told him that a rigid plan could be as dangerous as a loose one. They made a shield from boards, then found the skeleton of a broken support behind the plaster. A laborer gave a frightened sob when he saw a cracked rib in the wooden frame. The village priest spoke about theology but did not use faith to excuse toxic water. A jar of vinegar helped the cooks clean their tools, though the nurse insisted it was no substitute for a proper safety test.

一位生氣的麵包師把修繕比成反抗舊公會的叛亂，陶岑卻說僵硬計畫也可能和鬆散計畫一樣危險。他們用木板做出防護屏，從灰泥後找到斷裂支柱的骨架。工人看到木框一根裂開的肋條，嚇得啜泣。村裡神父談起神學，卻沒有用信仰替有毒井水開脫。一罐醋可用來清潔廚具，但護理師堅持這不能取代正式的安全檢驗。

Yi kept his notebook beside the lantern rather than beneath it. He had once hidden uncomfortable facts behind beautiful lines, believing that an elegant story could make a hard decision easier to bear. Now he placed the plain description first and the poetry second. Tao respected the change but did not pretend that trust had been restored in a single conversation. When Yi offered to hand over the notebook, Tao said, 'Keep it. Read it to me when something changes.' The request sounded modest. It also meant they would continue speaking to one another after the audience went home.

易尋把筆記放在燈旁，而不是藏在燈下。他曾把不舒服的事實藏在漂亮台詞後面，以為優美故事能讓艱難決定比較容易接受。如今他先寫平實描述，再寫詩句。陶岑尊重這份改變，但不假裝一次談話就能恢復信任。易尋想交出筆記，陶岑說：「你留著。事情有變就讀給我聽。」這要求聽來很小，卻代表觀眾回家後，他們仍會繼續對彼此說話。

That night the kitchen fed the entire crew with ingredients brought from an unaffected farm. Yi drafted a scene about a locked door, but Madam Song asked him to leave its ending open. The tunnel might lead to the mirror machinery, or it might hold only debris. Tao admired her refusal to turn a possibility into a certainty. He sat beside Yi on the kitchen steps while the dishes dried. 'You write the questions,' he said. 'I will try to make them safe to ask.' Yi answered by sliding half his bread across the step, a small gesture neither needed to explain.

當晚食堂用未受污染農場送來的食材餵飽劇團。易尋寫了一段關於鎖門的戲，宋夫人卻請他保留開放式結局。隧道可能通往鏡陣，也可能只剩碎石。陶岑欣賞她不把可能性說成確定事實。他坐在食堂階梯上陪易尋等碗盤晾乾。「你負責寫問題，」他說，「我試著讓人能安全地問出來。」易尋把半塊麵包推到他那側，這個小動作不需要解釋。
