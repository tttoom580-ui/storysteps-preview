走向查核的路

The Audit Road

我的修仙天賦，是看懂使用說明 · 第 10 / 25 章 · 1000–2000 · 英中對照

用互動版查詞、朗讀與收藏

The inspection group met beside a flat stone at the valley entrance. A small red flag marked the path, and a narrow track led toward the fourth passage. Mei placed her notebook on the stone and counted the people. There were two council members, Director Lin, Captain Su, Han, Rui, and three village witnesses. The clerk asked everyone to walk at a safe distance from the pipes. Han wore a dark suit and kept looking at his wallet, where a sealed letter could be seen. Rui wondered what it contained, but he did not ask. An audit had to follow facts, not guesses about pockets.

查核小組在山谷入口的一塊平石旁集合。小紅旗標出路線，狹窄小徑通往第四條暗道。梅把筆記本放在石上，清點人數。來的有兩名議員、林主任、蘇隊長、韓、睿，以及三名村民證人。書記請大家與水管保持安全距離。韓穿著深色西裝，不時看著錢包，裡面露出一封封好的信。睿好奇信的內容，卻沒有發問。查核必須依靠事實，而不是猜測別人口袋裡有什麼。

Rui explained that the hidden pump worked in a cycle. First it took water from the source, then raised it into a storage tank, and finally sent it toward the farms. If the machine ran for extra hours, the village would gain less water. The inspector asked how Rui knew the order of the cycle. He showed her the pipes and the marks on the old wall. “These arrows indicate the direction,” he said. “If we are wrong, the test will show it.” Han answered that the water might flow in several ways. The inspector said they would compare both ideas by observing the valves.

睿解釋暗藏的水泵有固定循環：先從水源抽水，送上蓄水槽，最後流往農場。機器多運轉幾小時，村莊得到的水就會減少。查核員問他如何確定循環順序。他展示管線與老牆上的記號。「這些箭頭指出流向，」他說，「若我們錯了，測試會顯示。」韓反駁水可能有數種流法。查核員表示，會藉由觀察閥門，比較兩種說法。

At the upper station, Lan brought the school logs. She had compared the original files with the versions that students could see on the Internet. Some numbers had changed after being posted. Han said such changes often occurred during normal office work. “That may be true,” Lan replied. “But each change should have a name and a reason.” She showed the dates without calling anyone a liar. Rui admired her silence after making that point. The records were clear enough to speak for themselves, and an angry speech would only give Han a chance to move attention away from them.

到了上方站舍，蘭帶來山門紀錄。她把原始檔案與學生在網路上看見的版本比較，發現一些數字在公布後改變了。韓說辦公時常會出現正常修訂。「那可能是真的，」蘭回答，「但每一次修改都應有負責人和理由。」她展示日期，沒有直接指控誰說謊。睿欣賞她說完後的沉默。紀錄已足以說明問題，憤怒的演說只會給韓轉移焦點的機會。

The clerk then produced a form used by the school to study water use. It asked for the age and sex of every family member, although neither fact helped measure how much water a field needed. Mei raised a concern about privacy. “We need water numbers, not details about every person,” she said. A council member agreed to remove those questions from the public copy. Rui felt relief. He had almost forgotten that a search for truth could hurt people if the wrong information was shared. The law mattered, but so did the care with which people used it.

接著書記拿出山門研究用水的表格。表格要求填每位家庭成員的年齡和生理性別，但這兩項都無法幫助測量田地用水需求。梅對隱私提出疑慮。「我們需要水量數字，不需要每個人的私人細節，」她說。一名議員同意從公開版本刪除那些欄位。睿鬆了口氣。他差點忘了，追查真相若分享不該公開的資料，也可能傷人。法律很重要，使用法律時的謹慎同樣重要。

The group went farther into the valley. At the fourth passage, they saw that the pump was still active. Its light showed power moving through the lines. The inspector measured the flow for a single hour, then compared it with the school record. The actual amount was higher. Han insisted that an extra test had been arranged for that morning. Captain Su asked who had approved it. Nobody answered. A council member moved forward, opened the control book, and found that the test had no name beside it. The group decided to seal the book until the records could be checked.

小組繼續深入山谷。在第四條水道旁，他們看見水泵仍在運作，指示燈顯示電力通過線路。查核員測量一小時的流量，再與山門紀錄比較。實際水量更高。韓堅持說當天早上安排了額外測試。蘇隊長問是誰核准的，沒人回答。一名議員上前翻開控制簿，發現那項測試旁沒有負責人姓名。小組決定先封存控制簿，等待進一步核對。

On their way back, Bo said the village did not need riches or special favors. People wanted a working system and a fair share. Rui thought about that as he watched the wheels turn. He had once believed that gaining power would solve his problems. Now he saw that power could also hide problems, especially when one person controlled the records. A council member asked him to help design a public track for future checks: dates, water levels, repair names, and reasons for changes. Rui accepted, but said others should be able to read and question it too.

回程時，柏說村民不需要財富或特別恩惠，只想要運作良好的系統與公平的份額。睿看著水輪，想起自己曾相信只要獲得力量，就能解決問題。如今他明白力量也可能用來隱藏問題，尤其當紀錄掌握在一個人手裡。一名議員請他協助設計未來公開查核流程，記下日期、水位、維修人員和修改原因。睿答應，但強調其他人也應能讀取與質疑紀錄。

That night the council announced a temporary order. The private farms could not take more than their listed share until the full review was complete. A measuring mark would be placed on every main pipe. Rui and Mei stood beside the first field as water moved along its edge. The stream was still narrow, and the next dry week might bring trouble again. Yet the order was a real change, not a promise made in a dark room. Rui looked toward the mountain and thought of the missing page in his father's manual. The more he learned, the more he wanted to find it.

那晚，議會發布臨時命令。完整審查結束前，私人農場不得抽取超過登記份額的水。每條主水管都將設置測量標記。睿和梅站在第一塊田邊，看著水沿田埂流動。水流仍窄，下週若天氣乾燥，問題可能再來。但這是實際改變，不是黑暗房間裡的空口承諾。睿望向山，想到父親說明書消失的最後一頁。知道得越多，他越想把那頁找回來。
