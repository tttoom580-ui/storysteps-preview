Words at the Open Door

我的修仙天賦，是看懂使用說明 · 第 9 / 25 章 · 1000–2000 · 英中對照

The council room was full when Rui arrived. An image of the mountain hung above the central table, and a small wooden shark stood on the shelf as a gift from a sea town. Mei thought the shark was silly, but Rui smiled at it. He was glad to see something ordinary before a serious meeting. A small Chinese character for courage was carved under the shark, though it offered no answer to the council. The clerk read the meeting's title aloud: “Water Access and Public Safety.” Rui held his notes against his chest. He felt alive with fear and hope. If the council allowed him to speak, he would need to express a careful story, not a long list of angry claims.

睿到達時，議會室已坐滿了人。中央桌上方掛著一張山景圖，架上擺著海邊小鎮送的木雕鯊魚。梅覺得那隻鯊魚有點傻，睿卻對它笑了笑。他很高興在嚴肅會議前看到一件普通的小物。鯊魚底下刻著一個代表勇氣的中文字，但那並不能替議會回答問題。書記大聲讀出會議標題：「水源使用與公共安全」。睿把筆記貼在胸前，既害怕又充滿希望。如果議會准許他發言，他得講一個謹慎的故事，而不是宣洩憤怒。

Han spoke first. He said the village machine was too old and that the school had done its best to improve the system. “We cannot forgive careless people who open gates without permission,” he declared. Rui waited until the clerk called his name. He showed the council the photograph of the hidden pipe and the image of Fen's old model. “We repaired only the part that had failed,” he said. “The result was a small safe flow. We did not break the machine.” He placed the reports side by side and pointed to the missing instruction. Several members leaned forward to see it.

韓先發言。他說村子的機器太老，山門已盡力改善系統。「我們不能原諒未經允許就開水閘的粗心人，」他宣稱。睿等書記叫到自己的名字，便展示暗管照片與芬的舊模型圖片。「我們只修復故障部分，」他說，「結果得到一股安全的小水流。我們沒有破壞機器。」他把兩份報告並排，指出消失的操作指示。幾名議員向前傾身細看。

Mei then explained the position of the fourth pipe. She used a clear tube full of colored liquid to show how pressure could move water through a hidden line. She put a lid on the tube and raised one end. The liquid changed level, just as they had seen near the reservoir. “This is a model,” she said. “It does not prove who used the real pipe. But it shows why our map is possible.” The council's record keeper asked her to repeat the test. She did so without changing the equipment. The simple action made the strange drawing easier for everyone to understand.

接著梅解釋第四條暗管的位置。她用裝著有色液體的透明管，示範壓力如何讓水穿過隱藏管線。她蓋上管蓋，抬高一端，液面便改變，正如他們在蓄水池附近看見的現象。「這只是模型，」她說，「它不能證明是誰用了真正的管子，但能說明我們的地圖為何合理。」議會紀錄員請她重做，她沒有改動設備就重複一次。這個簡單動作，讓每個人更容易理解陌生的圖。

Captain Su asked to speak next. She held up a sheet marked with the town's safety symbol and explained that the hidden pump had no public warning sign. If its pipe burst, hot water could injure people on the walking path. Director Lin confirmed the numbers from the private journal. Han burst into an angry speech and accused Lin of lying. But the clerk reminded him that everyone had to answer questions in turn. Lin spoke in a quiet voice. She said she had signed some reports without checking them personally. She regretted that choice and was ready to help correct it.

接著蘇隊長要求發言。她舉起印有鎮上安全標誌的紙，解釋暗藏的水泵沒有公開警示牌。萬一管子破裂，熱水可能傷到路上的行人。林主任確認了私人日誌的數字。韓突然憤怒地發言，指控林說謊，但書記提醒他每個人都必須輪流回答問題。林用平靜的聲音說，她曾在沒親自查驗的報告上簽字。她後悔那個選擇，現在願意協助改正。

During a short break, villagers shared tea and delicious cakes in the hall. Rui stood near the exit and tried to slow his breath. Bo told him that the old farmers were proud of the way he had spoken. “You did not promise a solution to every problem,” Bo said. “You only showed what you knew.” Rui looked at his hands, still rough from work. He had believed that people would listen only to a powerful master. Now he saw another way. A plain answer, supported by careful records, could sometimes carry more weight than a loud command.

短暫休息時，村民在大廳分享茶和好吃的蛋糕。睿站在出口附近，試著放慢呼吸。柏告訴他，老農們很欣賞他的發言方式。「你沒有答應解決所有問題，」柏說，「你只說出了自己知道的事。」睿看著因工作變粗糙的雙手。他過去以為人們只會聽強大師傅的話。現在他看見另一條路：有仔細紀錄支持的平實回答，有時比大聲命令更有分量。

When the meeting began again, the chair asked Han to explain the two water figures. Han said they came from different machines, but could not name them or show a second engine record. The room grew quiet. Rui noticed a small detail in Han's answer: he had changed the date of the private report by one day. Mei wrote it down. The chair did not make a final judgment. Instead, the council ordered an independent check of the water system. Two members would visit the source with both sides present. Rui thought this was only a partial result, but an important one.

會議重新開始後，主席請韓解釋兩組用水數字。韓說數字來自不同機器，卻無法說出那些機器名稱，也拿不出第二台引擎的紀錄。房間安靜下來。睿發現韓的回答有個小細節：他把私人報告日期改了一天。梅立刻記下。主席沒有立刻做最終裁定，而是命令獨立檢查供水系統。兩名議員將在雙方在場時到水源地查看。睿覺得這只是部分成果，但已相當重要。

As they left, a council member thanked Rui for a useful presentation. She added that mistakes could still be found in his own drawings. “If that happens, will you accept them?” she asked. Rui answered yes. He knew that truth was not a prize to keep for oneself. It was something they had to test together. Mei turned the key in their workroom door and placed the reports inside. The council had not opened the river yet. But it had opened a door that Han had tried to keep shut, and Rui meant to walk through it with care.

離開時，一名議員感謝睿做了有用的說明，也提醒他自己的圖可能仍有錯。「如果真的找到錯誤，你願意接受嗎？」她問。睿回答願意。他明白真相不是拿來獨占的獎品，而是需要大家共同檢驗的東西。梅鎖上工作室，把報告放好。議會尚未完全打開河道，卻打開了韓想一直關著的一扇門。睿決定謹慎地走過去。
