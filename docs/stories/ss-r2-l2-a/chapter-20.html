The First Full Test

A week later, the replacement parts arrived from a port on the distant coast. Lan had ordered a new joint, a wheel cover, and a small pressure alarm. The boxes weighed more than twenty kilograms together, and the driver had traveled many miles to bring them. Rui met him at the train platform with Mei and Jun. He checked the parts against the list before signing for them. The driver said the work sounded important. “We are only trying to keep water moving safely,” Rui answered. The words seemed simple after so much trouble, but he had never felt their meaning more strongly.

一週後，更換零件從遙遠海岸的港口送來。蘭訂了新接頭、輪子護蓋和小型壓力警報器。箱子合計重二十多公斤，司機走了許多英里的路才送達。睿和梅、俊到火車月臺接貨。他在簽收前依清單核對零件。司機說這項工作聽起來很重要。「我們只想讓水安全地流動，」睿回答。經歷這麼多麻煩後，這句話聽起來很簡單，他卻從未如此深刻理解它。

At the workshop, a couple who owned the village bakery offered to bake bread for the repair team. Their daughter had been ill during the dry season but was now well enough to help pack the food. Rui thanked them and asked them not to stand near the high-pressure work area. The mother smiled. “You are patient with our questions, so we can be patient with your rules.” Rui felt encouraged. No single repair could satisfy every need at once, but people were beginning to accept that careful work took time. That change in attitude mattered as much as any new tool.

在工坊，村裡經營麵包店的一對夫妻願意為維修隊烤麵包。他們的女兒在乾季曾生病，如今康復到能協助裝食物。睿向他們道謝，請他們別靠近高壓施工區。母親笑說：「你耐心回答我們的問題，我們也能耐心遵守你的規則。」睿受到鼓舞。單次維修無法立刻滿足所有需求，但人們逐漸接受謹慎工作需要時間。這種態度改變和新工具同樣重要。

The team compared the new parts with the old ones and cleaned sand from the lower chamber. Lan fitted the joint while Rui checked the marks. Mei recorded each step, and Gao confirmed the safety order. The wheel moved slowly at first. Then the alarm rang during a test, exactly when the pressure reached the high line. Lan closed the valve and reset the equipment. The result was impressive, but Rui said they still needed a second test. They had nearly lost control the week before, and he refused to trust one lucky result. One good result could be luck. They repeated the process with the same tools and conditions. The alarm worked again.

隊伍比較新舊零件，清掉下方水室的沙。蘭裝接頭，睿核對標記。梅記錄每一步，高確認安全程序。輪子起初慢慢轉動。一次測試中，壓力到達高線時警報正好響起。蘭關閉閥門，重新設定設備。結果令人印象深刻，但睿說還需要第二次測試。上週他們幾乎失去控制，所以他不願只相信一次幸運的結果。一次成功可能只是運氣。他們在相同工具與條件下重做，警報再次正常運作。

On the third test, the council allowed a larger flow. Water traveled through the repaired network and reached the second village field before sunset. The workers stood in silence for a moment. A child began to cheer, and the sound spread across the bank. Bo wiped his eyes. He had been asking for this day for years. Rui knew the flow was not yet perfect, but it was stable enough for a careful start. The upper farms also received their listed share. The system was working for both sides without anyone secretly taking more than the agreement allowed.

第三次測試時，議會准許增加水流。水穿過修好的管網，在日落前到達村裡第二塊田。工人們先安靜站了一會兒，接著一個孩子歡呼，聲音沿河岸傳開。柏擦了擦眼睛。多年來，他一直在等這天。睿知道水流還不算完美，但已穩定到能謹慎啟動。上游農場也得到登記份額。系統開始同時服務雙方，不再有人暗中拿走超過協議的水。

A small celebration took place near the field. A dancer from the local school performed, and children played a simple sport on the grass. Rui enjoyed watching, but did not forget the work still ahead. He explained to the council that they needed a plan for dry weeks, clear repair records, and a public alarm test every month. The success could influence future decisions only if people understood why it had worked. Lan suggested training more workers, and Mei offered to teach them how to read the water charts. The village agreed to begin classes the following week.

田邊舉辦了一場小慶祝。當地學校的一位舞者表演，孩子則在草地玩簡單的運動。睿看得開心，卻沒有忘記還有工作。他向議會說明，必須有乾週應變計畫、清楚維修紀錄，以及每月一次公開警報測試。成功若要影響未來決策，大家就得理解成功原因。蘭建議訓練更多工人，梅願意教大家讀水量圖。村民同意下週開始上課。

During the celebration, Han came to the river. He stayed at the edge of the crowd, watching the new wheel. Rui saw him but did not approach at once. Han had given the council a written statement and was awaiting its decision. The repair team had not been asked to judge him. Bo invited Han to take a cup of tea, but Han refused and walked away. Rui wondered whether the man felt regret, fear, or anger. He could not know. He only knew that the work had to remain fair even when the person who had caused trouble was standing nearby.

慶祝時，韓來到河邊。他站在人群外側，看著新水輪。睿看見他，卻沒有立刻走近。韓已向議會交出書面陳述，正等待裁定。維修隊並未受命審判他。柏邀韓喝杯茶，韓拒絕後走開。睿不知道他感到後悔、害怕還是憤怒；那無法猜測。睿只知道，即使造成麻煩的人就在附近，工作仍必須公平。

Late that night, Rui returned to the mill and listened to the river move. The sound was stronger than on the first day, but not wild. It was the sound of a machine being used within its limits. He opened his father's manual and read the first sentence again: “Power is not the first question. Balance is.” The words had once been a strange lesson on a seesaw drawing. Now he understood them in the fields, the council room, and the workshop. The river had passed its first full test. The next challenge was to make the rules strong enough to last.

深夜，睿回到磨坊，聽河水流動。聲音比第一天更強，卻不狂亂；那是一部在限制內運作的機器發出的聲音。他打開父親說明書，再讀第一句：「力量不是第一個問題，平衡才是。」這句話曾只是翹翹板圖下奇怪的教訓。如今，他在田野、議會和工坊裡都明白它的意思。河流通過了第一次完整測試，下一個挑戰是讓規則也足夠堅固，能維持下去。
