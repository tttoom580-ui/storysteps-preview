石頭上的日曆

The Stone Calendar

我的修仙天賦，是看懂使用說明 · 第 7 / 25 章 · 1000–2000 · 英中對照

The old path to the northern source was rocky, and Rui had to watch every step. Mei led the way while Bo carried a small pack of food. They passed a dry valley that people called the Little Desert. It was not a true desert, but the grass had become thin after three long summers without enough rain. Mei had copied a calendar of past storms and compared it with the village's water notes. “The climate is changing,” she said, “but that cannot explain why one farm gets more water while the rest get less.” Rui agreed. One problem should not be used to hide another.

通往北方水源的舊路滿是石頭，睿得注意每一步。梅帶路，柏背著一小袋食物。他們經過一處乾谷，大家叫它「小沙漠」。那並非真正的沙漠，但連續三個夏天雨量不足，草已變得稀疏。梅抄下過去風雨的日曆，並與村民用水紀錄比較。「氣候確實在變，」她說，「但這不能解釋為什麼一個農場水變多，其他地方卻變少。」睿同意。不能拿一個問題掩蓋另一個問題。

At the source they found an ancient stone wall with marks for each period of the year. The marks had been made by the people who built the first channels. Beside the wall was a shallow pool, and a turtle sat on a wet rock. Bo gave a quiet laugh. “At least it still has a home.” Rui knelt beside the pool and looked at the pipe beneath it. Its opening was too small to contain the amount of water shown in Han's records. That made him think there must be another route, one not on the school map.

在水源處，他們找到一面古老石牆，上面按一年各段時間刻著記號。那些記號是最早築渠的人留下的。牆旁有座淺水池，一隻烏龜坐在濕石上。柏輕笑：「至少牠還有家。」睿在池邊跪下，看著下方的管口。管口太小，無法容納韓的帳冊記載的水量。這讓他懷疑另有一條未畫在山門地圖上的路線。

Mei introduced an idea from her map class. “When two roads meet, there must be a place where they join,” she explained. “The same principle should work for water.” Rui searched the stones while Bo checked the old marks. After an hour Rui found a metal ring beneath moss. The ring was fixed to a flat plate. He tried to lift it, but the plate did not move. The manual told him to test whether pressure remained behind a cover before opening it. Thus, he first cleaned the small holes along the edge. He used an old toothbrush because its soft head would not cut the metal.

梅介紹她在地圖課學到的想法。「兩條路會合，就一定有交會點，」她解釋，「水也應該一樣。」睿搜索石頭，柏則查看舊刻痕。一個小時後，睿在青苔下找到金屬環，環固定在一塊平板上。他試著提起，但板子不動。說明書提醒，打開蓋板前必須測試後面是否還有壓力。因此他先清理邊緣的小孔。他用一支舊牙刷，因為軟毛不會刮傷金屬。

Water rose through the cleaned holes, not quickly but steadily. Rui stopped and waited for the level to settle. He had learned that progress did not always look like a great victory. Sometimes it was a quiet change that made the next action safe. When the pressure fell, Bo helped lift the plate. Beneath it was a narrow passage running toward the school. They could not follow it that day, but Mei measured its direction and drew it on her map. Their three known routes had become four. The hidden route explained how a small gate could feed a large farm.

水從清好的孔慢慢升起，流得不快，卻很穩定。睿停手等待水位安定。他學到進展不一定像偉大勝利，有時只是讓下一步變安全的安靜改變。壓力下降後，柏幫忙抬起板子。下面是一條通往山門的窄通道。他們當天無法沿路前進，但梅測量方向，畫進地圖。原本知道的三條水道成了四條。這條暗道能解釋小水閘為何養得起大片農場。

On the way back, Rui saw a small station building near the path. A student passed them on her morning jog and waved before continuing uphill. Someone had left a mop beside a bucket, and the windows were clean. A humble keeper came out and offered them water. He said he had worked there for thirty years, keeping the place ready even when no official visitors came. “My ideal is simple,” he said. “If one traveler needs help, the door should open.” He told them the fourth passage had once been used only during serious drought. The school had promised never to take it for private use. Mei wrote down his name and asked permission to record his words.

回程時，睿看見路旁一間小站舍。一名學生晨跑經過，揮手後繼續往山上慢跑。門邊放著拖把和水桶，窗子擦得乾淨。一位謙和的管理人走出來請他們喝水。他說自己在此工作三十年，即使沒有正式訪客，也一直讓站舍保持可用。「我的理想很簡單，」他說，「只要有旅人需要幫忙，門就該打開。」他告訴三人，第四條通道原本只在嚴重乾旱時使用。山門承諾過，絕不把它拿來私人使用。梅記下他的姓名，徵求同意後記錄他的話。

Rui asked why the keeper had remained silent for so long. The old man looked at his hands. “I thought I was only one being,” he answered. “Who would listen?” Bo said that the farmers had felt the same way. They had each kept their trouble inside their own homes. Now their notes could be read together. Rui began to see that his work was changing. He was not simply trying to repair a machine. He was helping people learn that they could ask for answers and act together. The thought made him stand a little straighter on the long road home.

睿問管理人為何沉默這麼久。老人看著自己的手。「我以為自己只是單獨一個人，」他回答，「誰會聽？」柏說農民以前也有同樣感覺。他們各自在家裡忍受困難。現在，大家的紀錄能放在一起看。睿開始發現自己的工作在改變。他不只是修理機器，也是在幫人們明白，可以一起提問、一起行動。想到這裡，他走在漫長的回家路上，背也挺得更直了。

At home, Rui copied the new route onto a large sheet. He checked the direction three times, marked the source, and placed the keeper's words beside the drawing. The next council meeting was only four days away. He wanted to advance their case before Han had time to hide the fourth pipe. Mei warned him that moving too fast might risk the keeper's job. They decided to ask the old man whether his name could be shared in public. That decision was a small step in another kind of learning: protecting the people who helped them was as important as winning.

到家後，睿把新路線抄到大紙上。他核對方向三次，標出水源，並把管理人的話放在圖旁。下一次議會只剩四天。睿想在韓藏起第四條管道前推進調查。梅提醒，行動太快可能害管理人失去工作。他們決定先詢問老人，是否同意公開姓名。這是另一種學習的小步：保護幫助他們的人，和贏得勝利同樣重要。
