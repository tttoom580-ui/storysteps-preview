The Nut Beneath the Mirror

我的修仙天賦，是看懂使用說明 · 第 18 / 25 章 · 1000–2000 · 英中對照

Lan brought a new model of the upper-valve control to the workshop. She carried it in a backpack because its parts were small and easy to lose. On the table she laid a metal nut, a mirror, a spring, and a short tube. Rui asked how the model would use energy. “Only a little,” Lan said. “The greater factor is how fast the valve moves.” She showed him that a slow change in pressure gave the pipes time to settle. The idea was obvious once he saw it, but he had never considered it before. He wrote the lesson in the group account so everyone could learn it.

蘭帶來上方閥門控制器的新模型。零件小又容易弄丟，她把它們裝在背包裡。桌上擺著金屬螺帽、鏡子、彈簧和短管。睿問模型會耗多少能量。「只要一點，」蘭說，「更大的因素是閥門移動速度。」她示範，慢慢改變壓力能讓管線有時間穩定。看過後，這道理變得很明顯，但睿以前從未想到。他把這堂課寫進團隊紀錄，讓所有人都能學到。

The next morning, they returned to the high station. The old control lay behind heavy furniture that had been pushed against a wall. Rui moved the chairs away and used the mirror to see the back of the box. One nut had become loose, making the lever turn farther than its mark. Lan thought the problem was likely a result of years of strong movement. Rui asked everyone not to hurry. A powerful machine could do great harm if a small part failed. They tested the lever by hand before adding pressure, then stopped when the spring made an unusual sound.

第二天早上，他們返回高站。舊控制器藏在靠牆堆放的沉重家具後方。睿移開椅子，用鏡子觀察箱子背面。一顆螺帽鬆了，使控制桿轉得比標記更遠。蘭認為這可能是多年劇烈運轉造成的。睿請大家不要急。強大的機器一旦小零件故障，也可能造成重大傷害。他們先用手測試控制桿，再加壓；聽見彈簧有異聲時就停下。

Rui wanted to propose replacing the whole box, but Lan showed him that its metal frame was sound. The broken parts could be changed separately. That would save money and make future repairs easier. They compared the old and new springs, then measured how much force each needed. Mei recorded the quantity of water that passed when the lever moved a little. Her notes showed that the difference was larger than Rui expected. The machine could be controlled, but only if people respected its limits. He began to see repair as a way of making power answer to care.

睿原本想提議整個箱子換新，蘭卻指出金屬框架仍然牢固，可以分別替換壞零件。那樣省錢，也方便日後維修。他們比較新舊彈簧，測量各自所需力量。梅記下控制桿小幅移動時的通水量。筆記顯示差異比睿預期更大。機器可以被控制，但前提是人們尊重它的限制。他開始把修理看成一種讓力量接受照顧與約束的方法。

A young worker named Jun arrived from the village. He was planning to marry his partner in the summer and hoped the river would be steady before their wedding. “We want to plant trees by our home,” he said. “Not just buy food every year.” Rui showed him the plans and invited him to watch the safety check. Jun said he did not understand the machine, so he would carry tools instead. Rui respected that choice. People could help in many ways, and nobody should be pushed into work beyond their training. The thought brought him a little peace after the dangerous test of the previous day.

一名叫俊的年輕工人從村裡來。他計畫夏天和伴侶結婚，希望婚禮前河流能穩定。「我們想在家旁種樹，」他說，「不只是每年買食物。」睿讓他看計畫，也邀請他觀看安全檢查。俊表示自己不懂機器，所以願意搬工具。睿尊重他的選擇。人們可以用許多方式幫忙，沒有人應被逼著做超出訓練範圍的工作。這想法讓睿在前一天危險測試後，稍稍平靜下來。

At noon, a hard sound came from behind the wall. Someone had begun to pound on an old pipe outside. Rui hurried to the window and saw a school worker trying to open a blocked side gate with a heavy bar. The bar weighed about a pound, but it could still damage the thin cover. Rui called to him to stop. The man explained that Han had asked him to make water move faster. Rui did not accuse the worker. He invited him inside and showed him the new measurements. The worker looked alarmed and said he had not been told about the pressure risk.

中午，牆後傳來重重敲擊聲。有人在外頭敲打一根舊管。睿走到窗邊，看見山門工人正用沉重鐵棒試著打開堵住的側閘。鐵棒大約一磅重，但仍可能弄壞薄蓋板。睿喊他停下。工人說是韓要他讓水流更快。睿沒有指控工人，只請他進來看新測量資料。工人露出驚慌神情，說沒有人告訴他壓力風險。

The worker handed them a written order. It called for more water to be sent to the private farms before the evening. The order had no reference to the council's temporary limit. Mei copied it and asked the worker if he would explain how he received it. He agreed. Rui felt the case move further forward, but he was also worried about the pipe. If Han continued to call for more water, an argument over numbers could become a dangerous failure. They had to protect the channel first and discuss blame later. That was what the old manual had taught him from the beginning.

工人交出一份書面命令，要求傍晚前把更多水送往私人農場，卻沒有提到議會的臨時限制。梅複製命令，詢問工人是否願意說明收到命令的過程，他同意了。睿感到調查又往前一步，但更擔心管線。若韓持續要求加大水量，數字上的爭執可能變成危險故障。他們得先保護水道，再談究責。這正是舊說明書從一開始教他的事。

By sunset, the team had replaced the nut and spring, but did not turn the full system on. They covered the new parts and marked them for a longer test under council supervision. Rui stood outside and watched the light touch the water's surface. It looked calm from above, hiding the strong force within the pipe. He thought of all the people who would be affected if the upper line failed. His work now demanded more than a quick repair. It demanded a plan for how much power the valley could safely use, and a rule that no one could secretly change.

日落時，團隊換好了螺帽和彈簧，卻沒有立刻啟動完整系統。他們蓋好新零件，留下標記，準備在議會監督下做更長時間的測試。睿站在外頭，看陽光落在水面上。從上方看很平靜，卻藏著管內強大的力量。他想著若上方管線故障，會有多少人受影響。現在他的工作需要的不只是快速修理，而是一份整個山谷能安全使用多少力量的計畫，以及任何人都不能暗改的規則。
