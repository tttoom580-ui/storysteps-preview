The Promise in the Fog

我的修仙天賦，是看懂使用說明 · 第 15 / 25 章 · 1000–2000 · 英中對照

A heavy fog covered the village on a regular weekday morning. Rui had arranged a meeting at the mill before the council's next session. Farmers walked along the narrow path carrying notes, baskets, and small tools. One owner of an orchard brought fresh buns, and an old poet came with a poem about the river. Rui did not plan a grand show. He wanted a peaceful discussion of what the group could prove. “We are not here to argue about who is strongest,” he said. “We are here to decide what the system should do and how everyone can check it.”

一個平常工作日的早上，濃霧蓋住村莊。睿安排大家在磨坊集合，時間就在下次議會前。農民沿小路走來，帶著筆記、籃子與小工具。一名果園主人帶來新鮮麵包，老詩人則帶了一首關於河流的詩。睿不想辦盛大表演，只想和平地討論哪些事能證明。「我們不是來爭誰最強，」他說，「而是要決定系統該做什麼，以及每個人怎麼檢查。」

Lan placed a chart on the wall. Its primary line showed the safe range of water for the lower fields. A second line showed how much the private farms had taken. The difference was clear. Mei used a picture of a zebra from a children's book to explain why stripes could help people read the marks. “When you see a dark line beside a light one, changes are easier to notice,” she said. The villagers laughed at the simple example, then asked serious questions. Some wondered if another dry summer would make every rule fail. Rui agreed that the system needed a plan for that too.

蘭把圖表貼在牆上。第一條主線表示下游田地的安全供水範圍，第二條線表示私人農場實際取走多少。差異很清楚。梅拿兒童書裡的斑馬圖解釋，為什麼條紋有助於讀取刻度。「深淺線放在一起，變化比較容易看出來，」她說。村民笑了笑這個簡單例子，接著提出嚴肅問題。有些人擔心再來一個乾夏，所有規則仍會失效。睿同意系統也需要那種情況的應對計畫。

Fen arrived with news from another old worker. She had made contact with a man who had delivered machine parts to the school years before. He remembered seeing a sealed box moved from the laboratory to a room near Han's office. The box carried the dragon mark. Rui asked the man whether he would tell the council. The worker agreed, but wanted the documents checked first. “Memories change,” he said. “I do not want a pattern in my head to become a fact on paper.” Mei thanked him. The group made an arrangement to visit the storage room with a clerk present.

芬帶來另一名老工人的消息。她聯絡上一位多年前替山門送機械零件的男子。他記得有人把一個封好的箱子從實驗室搬到韓辦公室旁的房間，箱上有龍形記號。睿問他願不願意向議會說明。工人答應，但希望先核對文件。「記憶會變，」他說，「我不希望腦中的印象直接變成紙上的事實。」梅向他道謝。大家安排由書記在場，一同前往儲藏室。

At the storage room, the clerk found a record of the box. It had been opened twice, once on the day of the old accident and once only a month ago. Inside was a thin manual page with two drawings. The first showed the upper valve. The second showed a safe test that had to be completed before any extra water could be moved to private farms. Beneath the drawings stood Rui's father's name. Rui touched the edge of the paper without lifting it. He had searched for this page for weeks. Now it was in front of him, quiet and ordinary.

在儲藏室，書記找到箱子的登記紀錄。它被開過兩次，一次是在當年事故那天，另一次是上個月。箱內有張薄薄的說明書頁，畫著兩幅圖。第一幅是上方閥門，第二幅則是把多餘的水送往私人農場前，必須完成的安全測試。圖下寫著睿父親的名字。睿只觸碰紙張邊緣，沒有把它拿起。他找了這一頁好幾週，現在它就在面前，安靜而平凡。

Rui wanted to shout, but he thought of the lessons that had brought him there. He asked the clerk to photograph the page and record where it had been found. Lan checked that the printed numbers matched the old metal plate. Mei compared the handwriting with the earlier manual. The parts fit. Rui finally understood that the page was not a cure for every problem. It could not make a machine safe on its own. But it proved that the safety test was part of the original design. Anyone who had removed that instruction had taken away an important warning.

睿想大喊，卻想起一路學到的事。他請書記拍照，記錄發現位置。蘭核對印刷數字與舊金屬板是否吻合，梅則把筆跡和早期說明書比較。各項都接得上。睿終於明白，這頁不是包治百病的藥方；光靠紙張不能讓機器安全。但它證明安全測試原本就是設計的一部分。任何移除這項指示的人，都拿掉了重要的警告。

On the way out, someone asked whether the workers would follow the old rules in every detail. Rui said no. Tools could improve, and some old customs might be unfair. “But when we change a safety rule, we must say why,” he explained. “We must show the change to the people it affects.” A young student in blue clothes, interested in fashion and graphic design, raised her hand. She offered to help make public instructions that could be read on a phone or posted on a wall. Rui welcomed the idea. Their path forward would use both old knowledge and new ways of sharing it.

離開時，有人問工人是否會原封不動遵守所有舊規。睿說不會。工具能進步，一些舊習俗也可能不公平。「但改安全規則時，必須說明原因，」他解釋，「也要讓受影響的人知道。」一名穿著藍衣、對時尚與平面設計感興趣的年輕學生舉手，表示願意協助製作能在手機上讀或貼在牆上的公開操作指示。睿很歡迎。他們接下來的道路將同時使用舊知識和新的分享方式。

That evening, the poet read his poem as the fog lifted. He wrote about a river that belonged to no single hand and a road that stayed open wherever people needed to walk. Rui listened beside the mill. For the first time since finding the manual, he was not thinking only of what Han had hidden. He was thinking of what the village might build next: a repair group, clear water numbers, and a system that could be checked by ordinary people. The missing page had returned. It did not end the struggle, but it gave the next stage a firm beginning.

當晚霧散時，老詩人朗讀他的詩。詩說河流不屬於任何一雙獨占的手，道路應在任何有人需要走的地方保持開放。睿站在磨坊旁聽。自從找到說明書以來，他第一次不只想韓藏了什麼，也開始想村子接下來能建什麼：維修小組、清楚的水量數字，以及普通人也能檢查的系統。遺失的頁面找回來了。它尚未結束爭鬥，卻讓下一階段有了穩固起點。
