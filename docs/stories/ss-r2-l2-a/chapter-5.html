我的修仙天賦，是看懂使用說明 · 第 5 / 25 章 · 1000–2000 · 英中對照

The news spread through the village by morning. Some people were excited that a hidden pipe had been found. Others feared that Han would punish anyone who spoke about it. A young woman from the mountain college arrived with her fellow students to ask questions. She wore a thick sweater and carried a small camera. Her name was Lan, and she studied machine production. “A machine's feature is not the same as its function,” she told Rui. “It may look perfect in a picture and still fail the people who use it.” Rui liked the way she spoke, because she seemed more interested in facts than in the school's pride.

到了早上，消息已傳遍村莊。有人為找到暗管感到興奮，也有人怕韓會處罰談論此事的人。一名來自山門學院的年輕女子帶著同學來採訪。她穿著厚毛衣，拿著小相機。她叫蘭，研究機器製造。「機器的外觀特色不等於真正功能，」她對睿說，「照片裡看起來再完美，也可能辜負使用的人。」睿喜歡她說話的方式，因為她似乎更在意事實，而不是山門的面子。

Lan showed them an online notice from Han's office. It said that the villagers were working with a dangerous terrorist group that wanted to destroy the water system. There was no evidence, only a frightening word. Bo felt the burden of the charge at once. His organization had spent years trying to make peaceful requests. “Our only plan was to open a gate,” he said. Lan offered to upload the repair notes and the original drawings to the public record, where the dates could be checked by everyone. Mei agreed, but insisted that they hide private information about families from the files.

蘭讓他們看韓辦公室發布的網路公告。公告說村民與危險的恐怖組織合作，想破壞供水系統。裡面沒有證據，只有一個令人害怕的詞。柏立刻感受到指控帶來的重擔。他的組織多年來一直和平提出請求。「我們唯一的計畫就是打開水閘，」他說。蘭提議把維修紀錄與原始圖紙上傳公開檔案，讓每個人都能查對日期。梅同意，但堅持必須隱藏文件中各戶人家的私人資料。

At noon, the village held a meeting beside the school field. Children played soccer nearby while their parents discussed the repair expense. One lady offered to bring food for the workers; another said she could lend a cart. The village could not afford to buy a new pump, much less pay double fees. Rui explained the selection of materials they would need: a new wooden handle, strong rope, two iron pins, and clean cloth for the old seal. No one could promise a perfect result. He made that clear before asking anyone to help. A few people left, but many stayed.

中午，村民在山門田地旁開會。孩子在附近踢足球，父母們則討論維修費用。一位女士願意替工人準備食物，另一位說能借出推車。村子負擔不起新水泵，更不可能付雙倍費用。睿說明所需材料的選擇：一個新木把手、結實的繩索、兩根鐵釘，以及替舊密封處使用的乾淨布料。他無法保證結果完美，也在徵求幫忙前說清楚了這點。有些人離開，但許多人留下。

They began by sweeping leaves away from the second gate. A narrow passage had to be cleaned before anyone could reach the handle. Lan checked the measurements and compared them with the notes from the college workshop. Mei watched the pressure mark. Bo carried tools and reminded the workers to rest. Rui seldom worked with so many people, and at first their different opinions made him nervous. But when he asked everyone to speak in turn, they found a useful rhythm. Each person had one job, and no one needed to pretend to have the strongest spirit in the group.

大家先掃除第二道水閘旁的落葉。通往把手的窄道必須清乾淨，才有人能靠近。蘭檢查尺寸，並和學院工坊的紀錄比較。梅監看壓力標記。柏搬運工具，提醒工人休息。睿很少和這麼多人合作，一開始各種意見令他緊張。但他請每個人輪流說話後，大家逐漸找到節奏。每個人有自己的工作，不必假裝擁有全隊最強的靈力。

When the cover finally came free, a dry seal fell into Rui's hand. It broke like an old piece of tissue. The missing seal had allowed sand to enter the lower pipe. Rui set a new cloth seal in place and fixed the handle with the pins. Before testing it, he asked everyone to stand back. He opened the gate a little, then stopped to check the reading. The pipe trembled. A man held his breath, and a child tried not to swallow too loudly. For a moment there was only a low sound behind the stones. Then water crossed the line that Rui had marked.

蓋板終於拆下來時，一塊乾掉的密封布落進睿手中，像舊面紙一樣碎裂。損壞的密封處使沙子進入下方水管。睿裝上新的布封，並用鐵釘固定把手。測試前，他請所有人退後。他把水閘開一點，就停下查看數值。管子微微震動。一名男子屏住呼吸，一個孩子連吞口水都怕太大聲。石後先傳來低沉聲響，接著水終於越過睿做的標記。

A thin stream reached the first village field by sunset. Some villagers cheered; others stood quietly, unable to believe it. Rui knew that the opening was small. The old network might not carry enough water for every home, and the hidden pump could still take much of the flow. But this result had changed something important. People could see that the gate was not dead. Han's office had called the repair impossible and the workers dangerous. Now the village had a working channel, dated photographs, and a shared record of how the work had been done.

日落時，一股細水流進村裡第一塊田。有些村民歡呼，另一些人靜靜站著，不敢相信。睿知道開口還很小。舊水網未必能供應所有家庭，暗藏的水泵也可能繼續抽走大部分水量。但結果已改變一件重要的事：人們親眼看到水閘並沒有死。韓的辦公室說維修不可能，還說工人危險。現在村子有運作中的水道、標記日期的照片，以及大家共同保存的施工紀錄。

That night, a boy delivered a note to Mei. It was from Director Lin. She had checked the main records and found that the water sent to the private school farms was nearly double the amount listed in the public report. She wanted to meet them at the college library before the next school council meeting. Rui read the note twice. “A little water is back,” he said, “but this is only the first part.” Mei nodded. Their next challenge would not be a broken handle. It would be a school office full of people who had reasons to keep the real numbers hidden.

當晚，一名男孩送來一張紙條給梅。那是林主任寫的。她查過主紀錄後發現，送往山門私人農場的水量幾乎是公開報告記載的兩倍。她希望在下一次山門議會之前，於學院圖書館與他們會面。睿把紙條讀了兩遍。「一點水已經回來了，」他說，「但這只是第一步。」梅點頭。下一場挑戰不是壞掉的把手，而是整間辦公室裡那些有理由把真實數字藏起來的人。
