龍形記號

The Dragon Mark

我的修仙天賦，是看懂使用說明 · 第 14 / 25 章 · 1000–2000 · 英中對照

用互動版查詞、朗讀與收藏

The old laboratory had once been a busy place, but now only a few rooms were used. Lan led Rui and Mei upstairs to an office where a deaf engineer named Teacher Wen kept the older machine drawings. Wen greeted them with a wave and asked them to write their questions on a large board. He could read lips in good light, but preferred clear written words when details mattered. Rui showed him the metal plate. Wen studied the mark near its edge and drew a small dragon beside it. “The dragon mark belongs to the first safety team,” he wrote. “Your father worked with us.”

舊實驗室以前很忙，現在只剩少數房間還在使用。蘭帶睿和梅上樓，拜訪保存早期機器圖紙的失聰工程師溫老師。溫揮手迎接，請他們把問題寫在大白板上。他在光線充足時能讀唇，但牽涉細節時，更喜歡清楚的文字。睿拿出金屬板。溫研究邊緣記號，畫下一條小龍。「龍形記號屬於第一支安全小組，」他寫道，「你父親跟我們一起工作過。」

Wen opened a drawer and showed them a small diamond-shaped key. It was not a valuable jewel but a piece of metal cut to fit an old cabinet. “The key makes the door easier to open,” he wrote. “It does not prove what is inside.” Mei smiled at this careful answer. They followed Wen into a room lined with shelves. The air smelled of old paper, garlic from the lunch kitchen, and machine oil. A worker carried plates of tofu and cooked liver past the door. Rui had not eaten since morning, but he waited. This work was more important than lunch.

溫打開抽屜，拿出一把小小的菱形鑰匙。那不是昂貴寶石，而是為舊櫃子切製的金屬片。「鑰匙讓門比較容易打開，」他寫道，「卻不能證明裡面有什麼。」梅欣賞這樣謹慎的回答。他們跟著溫進入一間排滿書架的房間。空氣混著舊紙、午餐廚房飄來的大蒜味，以及機油味。一名工人端著豆腐和炒肝臟經過門口。睿從早上起沒吃東西，仍決定先工作。

Inside the cabinet lay the first printed rules for the water system. Rui read them slowly. The page did not mention magic power or the rank of an operator. It listed safe levels, repair duties, and the right of lower villages to know the water numbers. One sentence had been printed twice for emphasis: “No private need may remove the primary safety flow.” Wen pointed to an empty space where another page should have been. Its absence did not erase the existence of the test; the older printed rule still referred to it. He wrote that the missing paper described how to test the upper valve before changing the water supply. Rui felt the old question come back with new force.

櫃子裡放著第一版供水系統印刷規則。睿慢慢讀著。頁面沒有談法術力量或操作員的階級，而是列出安全水位、維修責任，以及下游村子知悉用水數字的權利。其中一句為了強調印了兩次：「任何私人需求不得取走主要安全水流。」溫指向本應放另一頁的空位。少了那頁，不代表測試從未存在；更早的印刷規則仍提到它。他寫下那頁描述如何在改變供水前測試上方閥門。睿再次強烈想起一直未解的問題。

A child from the school activity club entered with a flying model she had built. She wanted to show Wen how it could record a short flight above the roof. Rui asked whether the model could take an image of the upper pipe from a safe distance. Wen checked the plan, then nodded. The child was pleased to provide help. Han later called the idea childish, but the council had approved the inspection. From the model's camera, they saw an extra valve on the far side of the roof. Its position matched the missing line on the old map.

一名參加學校活動社團的孩子帶著自製飛行模型進門，想向溫展示它如何在屋頂上方短暫飛行並拍攝。睿問能否從安全距離拍到上方管線。溫看過計畫後點頭。孩子很高興能幫忙。韓後來說這想法幼稚，但議會已批准檢查。從模型鏡頭裡，他們看到屋頂遠側多了一個閥門。位置正好對應舊地圖消失的線。

Rui made a brief note of what the image could and could not prove. It showed that the valve existed and where it stood. It did not show who had used it. Mei asked Wen whether he remembered the last test before Rui's father died. Wen's hands paused. He wrote that the old team had reported a pressure problem. They had asked for time to repair it, but their request was refused. “We should have spoken louder,” he wrote. Rui shook his head. “You are speaking now,” he answered. Wen looked at the words and gave a small, sad smile.

睿簡短記下影像能證明和不能證明的部分。它證明閥門存在以及所在位置，卻不能證明誰操作過。梅問溫是否記得睿父親去世前最後一次測試。溫的手停住。他寫道，舊團隊曾回報壓力問題，要求時間維修，卻被拒絕。「我們當年應該說得更大聲，」他寫。睿搖頭回答：「你現在就在說了。」溫看著字句，露出微微悲傷的笑。

Before they left, Wen printed the pages he was allowed to share and signed a note saying where the originals were kept. Rui thanked him and promised to return the diamond key. At the stairway, Han appeared and whispered to a guard. Rui could not hear the words, but he saw the guard move toward the document room. He did not rush after them. Instead, he asked Lan to contact the council clerk and report the official copies. Mei said they needed to keep the process open. A secret chase might feel brave, but it could make their evidence harder to trust.

離開前，溫列印可公開的頁面，並簽下一張註明原件保存地的紙。睿向他道謝，承諾歸還菱形鑰匙。到了樓梯口，韓出現並對守衛耳語。睿聽不清內容，只看見守衛朝文件室走去。他沒有衝上前，而是請蘭聯絡議會書記，通報正式副本。梅說他們得讓程序保持公開。祕密追逐也許看似勇敢，卻可能讓證據更難受信任。

On the highway home, Rui studied the copies again. Wen had given them a place to search and a way to understand the dragon mark. Their next task was to find the missing page and prove how the upper valve had been used. Rui felt a strange ease in knowing that he did not have to do everything alone. People with different lives and skills were now part of the work. Some used cameras, some read old records, and some repaired metal. The river was becoming a shared project, just as the first builders had intended.

回家的公路上，睿再次研究副本。溫讓他們知道該到哪裡查，也解開了龍形記號的意義。下一步是找回遺失的頁面，查明上方閥門如何被使用。睿忽然感到一種輕鬆，因為他不必獨自做完所有事。不同生活和技能的人已投入工作：有人用相機，有人讀舊紀錄，有人修金屬。河流正成為共同的計畫，正如最早建造它的人所希望的。
