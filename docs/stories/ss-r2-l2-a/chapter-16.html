山谷的共同工坊

A Workshop for the Valley

我的修仙天賦，是看懂使用說明 · 第 16 / 25 章 · 1000–2000 · 英中對照

用互動版查詞、朗讀與收藏

The village turned an empty storehouse into a shared workshop. Rui hoped this small change would help build a fairer society, not just a better repair shop. A cheerful hostess from the farmers' group welcomed every visitor and offered strawberry water. On the wall hung a comic about safe repairs, drawn by children from the northern school. Its hero was an eagle who checked every bridge before flying over it. Rui laughed at the pictures but praised the clear message. He wanted the village to gain independence from one powerful operator, not independence from learning. “Anyone can make a mistake,” he told the group. “What matters is that the system helps us find and correct it.”

村民把空倉庫改成共同工坊。睿希望這項小改變不只帶來更好的修理店，也能讓社會更加公平。農民組織一位熱情的女接待員迎接訪客，請大家喝草莓水。牆上掛著北方學校孩子畫的安全修理漫畫，主角是一隻每次飛越橋梁前都先檢查的老鷹。睿覺得畫很有趣，也讚美訊息清楚。他希望村子不再依賴單一強大的操作員，但不是不再學習。「任何人都可能犯錯，」他對大家說，「重要的是制度能幫我們找到並改正錯誤。」

Mei spread a large plan over the table and asked each person to lay a small stone beside the work they could do. Some chose cleaning, some chose measuring, and others chose carrying parts. One young man offered to remove trash from the old tunnel. Another promised to check waste water near the farms so it would not harm the environment. Lan explained the requirements for a repair record: date, place, problem, action, and person responsible. The rules were not highly difficult, but they had to be followed each time. Rui was pleased when villagers began asking questions without waiting for him.

梅把大計畫圖鋪在桌上，請每個人在自己能做的工作旁放一顆小石頭。有人選清潔，有人選測量，有人選搬零件。一名年輕人願意清掉舊隧道的垃圾，另一人承諾檢查農場附近的廢水，避免破壞環境。蘭解釋維修紀錄的必要欄位：日期、地點、問題、處理方式與負責人。規則並不特別困難，但每次都得遵守。當村民開始不等他發話就主動提問，睿很高興。

During the first test, a stone cover would not move. Rui rubbed dirt from its edge and saw a thin shell of hard material around the opening. He asked the group to loosen the cover a little at a time. When it began to slide, two people held it with ropes so it could not fall into the channel. Underneath was a smooth metal path for water. The path had been built well, but its entrance was full of old leaves. Nobody needed a great spell to clean it. The workers used brushes, small tools, and patience until water passed freely again.

第一次測試時，一塊石蓋怎麼也不動。睿擦去邊緣泥土，看見開口周圍結了一層硬殼。他請大家每次只鬆開一點。蓋子開始滑動時，兩人用繩索拉住，避免落進水道。下面有一條光滑的金屬水路。水路建得很好，只是入口塞滿舊葉。要清理它並不需要偉大法術。工人用刷子、小工具和耐心，把水道清到再次暢通。

A local policeman stopped by after hearing that people were opening a school pipe. He asked whether they had permission. Mei showed him the council order and the map of the allowed work area. He read both and said the team could continue, but only within those lines. Rui thanked him for checking. In the past, a uniform had often made the villagers afraid. Today it reminded Rui that public work needed clear limits for everyone. The officer agreed to help keep the path open if a crowd gathered. He did not join the repair or try to direct the workers.

一位當地警察聽說有人打開山門水管，特地過來詢問是否有許可。梅拿出議會命令與允許施工範圍圖。警察讀過後說可以繼續，但不能超出標線。睿感謝他查驗。過去制服常讓村民害怕，今天卻提醒他，公共工作需要每個人都明白的限制。警察答應若人群聚集，會協助維持小路暢通。他沒有加入維修，也沒有試圖指揮工人。

At noon, Bo opened the workshop accounts. The group had collected enough money for common tools, but still carried a debt for the first replacement parts. One person suggested charging rich farms extra without asking them. Rui said the group should not repeat the school's old mistake. “A rule made without listening can become unfair,” he said. Instead, they agreed to publish all costs and invite every water user to discuss a fair share. A farmer from the upper fields came forward and offered tools. She was surprised that the meeting let her speak even though her farm had used the private pipe.

中午，柏公開工坊帳目。大家籌到足夠購買共用工具的錢，但第一批更換零件仍有欠款。有人提議不必詢問就向富裕農場多收費。睿說他們不能重蹈山門舊錯。「不聽別人說話就訂規則，可能變得不公平，」他說。最後大家同意公布所有費用，邀請每位用水者討論合理分攤。上游一名農民走上前，表示願意借工具。她很驚訝，自己農場曾用私人暗管，會議卻仍讓她說話。

In the afternoon, Lan gave a short lesson about the wider water cycle. She drew the sun, mountains, sea, and clouds, then called them a small part of the universe that kept giving water back in different forms. The children listened. Rui added that water in nature did not mean a village had endless clean water. A dirty channel could make people sick, and a closed gate could leave fields dry. Good care meant knowing both the natural world and the machines people built inside it. The group decided to check water quality as well as water quantity each week.

下午，蘭簡短講解更大的水循環。她畫了太陽、山、海與雲，說它們是宇宙裡讓水以不同形式返回的小小部分。孩子聽得很認真。睿補充，自然界有水，不代表村子永遠有乾淨水。髒水道可能讓人生病，關上的水閘可能讓田地乾枯。好的照顧需要同時理解自然世界和人們在其中建造的機器。大家決定每週同時檢查水質與水量。

By evening, the old storehouse looked like a real workshop. Tools had places, records had copies, and visitors knew whom to ask for help. Rui felt proud, but he did not call it a victory. A shared system would face problems that a single person could never see coming. Mei reminded him of the upper valve they still had not tested. Its pressure might be rising while they worked below. Tomorrow, they would climb to the high station with a proper team. If the community wanted lasting freedom, it would have to protect the whole river, not only the part close to home.

傍晚，舊倉庫已經像真正的工坊。工具各有位置，紀錄有副本，訪客知道該向誰求助。睿感到驕傲，卻不把它叫作勝利。共同制度將面對許多單人想不到的問題。梅提醒，他們還沒測試上方閥門，他們在下游工作時，上游壓力可能持續升高。明天他們會帶合適的隊伍爬到高站。若社區想要長久自主，就必須保護整條河，而不只是家門前的一段。
