河流守住了承諾

The River Keeps Its Promise

我的修仙天賦，是看懂使用說明 · 第 25 / 25 章 · 1000–2000 · 英中對照

The first summer after the repairs began with a soft rain. Rui stood beside the mill and listened to the steady flow of water. Among the villagers, the new rules had become ordinary: check the marks, keep a record, and call for help when something seemed wrong. Nobody called this foolish work anymore. The principal of the mountain school had asked Rui to apply for a teaching position, and he was still thinking about it. His mother brought him a bowl of pumpkin soup, made with a bone broth she had cooked all morning. “You can choose your own road,” she told him.

修復後的第一個夏天，以柔和的雨開始。睿站在磨坊旁，聽穩定的水流。在村民之間，新規則已成日常：檢查刻度、保存紀錄，發現異常就求助。再也沒有人把這些事叫作愚蠢工作。山門校長請睿申請教職，他還在考慮。母親端來一碗南瓜湯，用的是她燉了一整個早上的骨湯。「你可以選自己的路，」她告訴他。

At the workshop, Mei showed him the newest data. The water level had remained safe during three weeks of dry weather, and the alarm had worked correctly in every public test. Rui used a simple figure to explain the changes to the village children. One small line showed the old flow, another showed the new flow, and a third showed the safe limit. A boy asked whether more water always meant more freedom. Rui said no. “Freedom means that we can understand our choices and share the water fairly. It does not mean taking as much as we want.”

在工坊，梅拿出最新數據。連續三週乾燥天氣裡，水位仍在安全範圍；公開測試時警報每次都正確運作。睿用簡單圖形向村裡孩子解釋變化。一條線表示舊水流，一條表示新水流，第三條表示安全上限。一個男孩問水越多是不是就越自由。睿說不是。「自由是我們能理解選擇，並公平分享水，不是想拿多少就拿多少。」

The final judgment from the court arrived that afternoon. It confirmed the public water agreement and the shared safety board. It also required the school to keep the changed records available for review. The council would continue a separate inquiry into who had hidden the old page. Han was absent from the celebration, and Rui did not ask why. He believed a fair system should not depend on his feelings about one person. The important part was that villagers could now challenge a decision, see the reason for it, and ask for a correction when facts changed.

下午，法院的最終裁定送到了。它確認公開用水協議與共同安全委員會，也要求山門保留遭修改的紀錄供查閱。至於誰藏起舊頁面，議會仍將另案調查。韓沒有出席慶祝，睿也沒追問。他相信公平制度不應取決於自己對某個人的感受。重要的是村民現在能質疑決定、看見理由，並在事實改變時要求修正。

On the village holiday, the market filled with music. A small band played a gentle melody, and children made paper wheels that gave a happy pop when the wind caught them. Bo placed a single coin in the repair fund box to mark the first year of public work. Everyone laughed because the box was already full of tools and donations. Nearby, a cook washed vegetables while another prepared seafood from the coast. Mei asked Rui whether he wanted to visit the sea after harvest. “Yes,” he said. “I want to hear the surf and learn how that water moves too.”

村子節日時，市場充滿音樂。小樂隊奏出溫柔旋律，孩子製作紙風輪，風一吹便快樂地啪一聲轉動。柏往維修基金箱放入一枚硬幣，紀念公開工作的第一年。大家笑了，因為箱子旁已堆滿工具和捐物。附近，廚師正在清洗蔬菜，另一人準備來自海岸的海鮮。梅問睿收成後想不想去看海。「想，」他說，「我也想聽海浪，學學那裡的水怎麼動。」

Rui returned to the mill in the evening and opened the old manual. He placed the recovered page back where it belonged, between the drawings of the seesaw and the upper valve. He did not hide the book. Instead, he made a copy for the workshop and put the original in a safe public box with a clear label. Anyone who needed it could ask to read it. The book held his father's careful work, but it no longer belonged only to his family. It had become part of the valley's record, one more way for people to protect one another.

傍晚，睿回到磨坊，翻開舊說明書。他把找回的那頁放回翹翹板與上方閥門圖之間的原位。他沒有把書藏起來，而是為工坊複製一份，把原件放進有清楚標籤的公共保管箱。需要的人都能申請閱讀。書裡有父親細心的工作，但已不只屬於他的家族。它成為山谷紀錄的一部分，是人們保護彼此的另一種方式。

As the sky grew dark, Rui heard a small click from the wheel. He checked the lower pipe and found a loose cover. It took only a moment to fix, yet he smiled at how ordinary the action felt. Years before, he had thought the world belonged to people with powerful magic. He had been wrong. A broken machine could still need a strong hand, but it also needed a patient eye, an open book, and people willing to work together. The river moved past the mill, and he let the sound fill the evening. Tomorrow there would be more to learn.

天色漸暗時，睿聽見輪子輕輕喀噠一聲。他查看下方水管，發現蓋板鬆了。修好只花片刻，他卻因這份日常感而笑了。多年前，他以為世界屬於擁有強大法術的人。原來他錯了。壞掉的機器有時需要有力的手，也需要耐心的眼睛、一本開放的書，以及願意合作的人。河水流過磨坊，他讓聲音填滿傍晚。明天還有更多事情等著他學習。

Before bed, he wrote the final line of his own journal: “I did not discover a secret power. I learned that understanding is work, and that work can be shared.” He closed the book and listened to the night. The village had water, the school had new rules, and the people had a way to question the next mistake before it grew too large. Rui would make more mistakes himself. He was not afraid of that truth now. The promise of the river was not that nothing would ever break. It was that no one would have to face a broken world alone.

睡前，他在自己的日誌寫下最後一行：「我沒有發現祕密力量。我學到理解需要工作，而工作可以與人分享。」他合上書，聽著夜色。村子有了水，山門有了新規則，人們也能在下一次錯誤變得太大之前提出質疑。睿自己也會再犯錯。他如今不害怕這個事實。河流的承諾不是永遠不會有東西壞掉，而是當世界壞了，沒有人必須獨自面對。
