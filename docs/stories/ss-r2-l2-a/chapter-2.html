隧道裡的燈

The Light in the Tunnel

我的修仙天賦，是看懂使用說明 · 第 2 / 25 章 · 1000–2000 · 英中對照

Mei arrived before sunrise with bread, two small lanterns, and a map of the old subway. Rui asked where she had found the map. “The barber keeps old papers beside his shop,” she said. “I helped him sort them. He gave me this one.” The tunnel entrance stood at a strange angle beside a high stone wall. Fresh boards covered half the door, even though the mine had closed many years ago. Rui tried to avoid making noise as he moved one board. They found a huge passage with cold air coming from deep inside. Someone had been here recently.

天亮前，梅帶著麵包、兩盞小燈和一張舊地下鐵地圖來了。睿問她從哪裡找來的。她說：「理髮師把舊文件放在店旁，我幫他整理，他就把這張送給我。」隧道入口以奇怪的角度靠著高高的石牆。門的一半被新木板封住，儘管礦場早在多年前就關閉了。睿盡量不發出聲音，移開一塊木板。他們發現一條巨大的通道，深處有冷空氣吹來。最近有人來過這裡。

A deer ran across the far end of the passage, followed by a white dove that flew toward an opening in the roof. Mei laughed softly. “At least something can get in and out.” Rui noticed wet marks on the floor. He touched one and saw that the water was clear. Nearby lay a red lantern with the mountain school's sign. He found a spot of blue paint on its handle, the same paint Han used on the main gate. The mark was small, but it was a particular detail worth remembering. Rui copied its shape in his notebook instead of taking the lantern away.

一隻鹿跑過通道遠端，接著一隻白鴿朝屋頂的缺口飛去。梅輕聲笑了：「至少還有東西進得來、出得去。」睿注意到地上有水痕。他摸了摸，水很清澈。旁邊倒著一盞紅燈，上面有山門標記。燈柄上沾著一點藍漆，和韓塗在主水閘上的顏色相同。痕跡雖小，卻是值得記住的特殊細節。睿把形狀抄進筆記本，沒有把燈拿走。

Farther in, they met old Bo, who carried baskets of sour fruit to the market. He had used the tunnel as a short road since he was young. “People think this place is empty,” he said, “but the second water route still exists below us.” Bo was the president of a small farmers' organization. Most members owned very little land, and their voices were often a minority at school meetings. He had asked for repairs three times, yet the school always answered that the water was gone. “If that is the truth,” Bo said, “why do I hear it every night?”

走得更深時，他們遇見挑著酸果去市場的老柏。從年輕時起，他就把隧道當成捷徑。「大家以為這裡空了，」他說，「但第二條水道還在我們腳下。」柏是小農組織的會長。大部分會員的土地都很少，在山門會議裡，他們的聲音往往是少數。他申請維修三次，山門卻總回答說水已經沒有了。「如果那是真相，」柏問，「那我每晚聽到的是什麼？」

Bo led them to an old service door. The lock had been replaced, but the stone beside it was loose. Rui took a careful look at the wall. The stones near the top had been cut at a different angle from those below. This was not damage from time. Someone had built a new wall over an older one. Mei opened her notebook and drew both layers. The work was slow and painful because the passage was narrow and their knees pressed against sharp rock. Rui wanted to break the wall, but Mei stopped him. “First we need evidence,” she said. “A broken wall can hide a true story.”

柏領他們來到一扇舊維修門前。鎖已經換過，但旁邊的石頭鬆動了。睿仔細看著牆。上方石塊的切割角度和下方不同。這不是年代久遠造成的損壞，而是有人在舊牆外又砌了一層。梅打開筆記本，畫下兩層石牆。通道狹窄，膝蓋頂著尖石，這份工作又慢又痛。睿想敲開牆，梅卻攔住他。「我們先要證據，」她說，「打壞的牆，也可能把真相一起藏起來。」

In a dry corner of the station stood a shelf of school books. Rui pulled out a textbook about water machines. It was the fifth edition, published only two years earlier. The book claimed that the second route had never worked well. But its diagram showed a wide channel beneath the wall, exactly where Bo heard water. Rui could appreciate the clear drawings, even when he questioned the written claim. A loose page fell out. It contained the minutes of a meeting. The school had approved money for a repair, and Han had signed to say the work was complete.

車站乾燥的角落有一排山門書籍。睿抽出一本水力機械教科書。那是兩年前出版的第五版。書上說第二條水道從來不好用，但圖解卻畫出一道寬闊的水渠，位置正好在柏聽到水聲的牆下。睿欣賞清楚的圖，卻對文字說法感到懷疑。一張散頁從書中掉出來，記載著一次會議紀錄。山門曾核准一筆維修費，而韓簽名表示工程已完成。

Mei read the date twice. On that same day, the village had been told that repairs were impossible. “Perhaps the money was spent in another way,” she said. “Or perhaps this paper is not real. We must not decide too early.” Rui agreed. Their plan was no longer only about opening a gate. They needed to understand who paid for work, who checked it, and who gained from a closed river. On the way out, a bell rang in the dark. Bo's face changed. “That is the school watch,” he whispered. “They are early. Take the back stairs.”

梅把日期讀了兩遍。就在同一天，村民被告知維修不可能。「也許錢被用在別的地方，」她說，「也許這張紙不是真的。我們不能太早下結論。」睿同意。他們的計畫已不只是打開水閘，還要了解誰付了工程費、誰驗收、誰因河道關閉而得利。出去時，黑暗中響起一聲鐘。柏臉色變了。「那是山門的巡查鐘，」他低聲說，「他們提早來了，走後面的樓梯。」

The back steps led through a garden behind the station. Rui saw a second document stuck under a stone. It was a list of families that would pay for the new pump. The poorest homes were marked with the largest fees. He put the list beside the meeting record and felt the problem grow in size. This was not a mistake that affected one old machine. It touched a whole village. Mei asked whether he still wished to continue. “Yes,” Rui said. “But I will not make a charge without proof.” They covered the opening again and walked home with their evidence kept dry beneath Mei's coat.

後樓梯通到車站後方的花園。睿看見石頭下壓著另一張文件，是新水泵的家庭繳費名單。最窮的住戶反而被標上最高費額。他把名單與會議紀錄放在一起，感覺問題越來越大。這不是只影響一台舊機器的錯誤，而是牽連整個村莊。梅問他還要不要繼續。「要，」睿說，「但沒有證據，我不會隨便指控。」他們重新遮好入口，把證據放在梅的外套下防潮，步行回家。
