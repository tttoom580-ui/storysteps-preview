前往都城

A Visit to the Capital

我的修仙天賦，是看懂使用說明 · 第 12 / 25 章 · 1000–2000 · 英中對照

The council approved Rui's archive request, but the most important copy had been moved to the capital. Mei read the letter twice. “We will have to travel,” she said. Rui had hardly left the countryside before. He worried about the expense, but Bo offered to pay for their bus tickets from the farmers' common fund. “The money is for solving water problems,” he said. “This is one.” Rui promised to measure every cost and return what was not used. They left at dawn, carrying a blank notebook for new facts and a folder of copies that they did not want to lose.

議會批准睿的檔案申請，但最重要的副本已移到都城。梅把信讀了兩遍。「我們得出門一趟，」她說。睿幾乎沒離開過鄉間。他擔心旅費，柏卻願意從農民共同基金支付公車票。「這筆錢是拿來解決用水問題的，」他說，「這就是其中一件。」睿答應記下每筆花費，未用的錢會退回。他們天亮出發，帶著一本準備記新事實的空白筆記本，以及不願遺失的文件副本。

The road curved through hills and reached a large town before noon. At a food stall, Mei bought fries while Rui chose a small pudding. They sat beside a dentist's office and checked the direction to the public records hall. An old woman was waiting outside the dentist. When she saw Rui's father's mark on the folder, she asked where he had found it. She had once been a customer at his workshop. “He repaired my water pot without charge when I had no money,” she said. Rui thanked her for the memory and asked if she knew anything about the old river.

公路彎過群山，中午前到達大城。梅在小吃攤買了薯條，睿選了一份小布丁。兩人坐在牙醫診所旁，核對前往公共檔案館的方向。一名等候看牙的老婦人看見資料夾上的父親記號，問他從哪裡來。她以前是父親工坊的顧客。「我沒錢時，他免費修好我的水壺，」她說。睿感謝她分享回憶，並問她是否知道舊河道的事情。

The woman remembered a sudden argument at the school years earlier. Rui's father had asked the council to regard the water agreement as a promise, not simply an old piece of paper. Han had blamed him for a failed test. “Your father looked scared,” she said, “but he did not stop speaking.” She warned Rui to respect the dangers of this work. “You can be brave and still be careful.” Mei wrote down the woman's name and asked permission to contact her later. They did not treat one memory as proof, but they knew it could help them search the records.

老婦人記得多年前山門有過一場突如其來的爭論。睿的父親要求議會把水道協議視為承諾，而不只是一張舊紙。韓卻把一次失敗測試歸咎於他。「你父親看起來很害怕，」她說，「但他沒有停止說話。」她提醒睿要尊重這份工作的危險。「勇敢，也可以很小心。」梅記下婦人的名字，徵得同意後表示日後可能聯絡。他們沒有把單一回憶當成證明，卻知道那能指引後續查檔。

The records hall was tall and quiet. A clerk brought them a teapot and explained the rules. No ink pens were allowed near old paper, and pages had to remain flat. The first record described a settlement between the school and the village. It said the village would keep its right to the lower channel. A later form carried Rui's father's signature, but the space for the date was blank. Mei noticed that the paper was newer than the one before it. “Could the signature have been added later?” she wondered. The clerk said that question needed an expert, not a guess.

檔案館高大而安靜。一名職員端來茶壺，說明規則：舊紙旁不能用墨水筆，文件必須平放。第一份紀錄描述山門與村子之間的協議，寫明村民保有下游水道的使用權。較晚的一份表格有睿父親的簽名，但日期欄空白。梅注意到紙張比前一份新。「簽名會不會是後來加上的？」她問。職員說這問題需要專家鑑定，不能靠猜。

While the clerk made copies, Rui watched people pass through the hall. A child dragged a pair of slippers across the floor until his mother picked him up. A worker carried a heavy box with great care. Ordinary life went on around the papers that had caused so much trouble. Rui began to understand why public records mattered. They were not only for leaders or judges. They helped an ordinary customer or farmer ask what had happened to a promise. Without them, the loudest person might tell the story and everyone else would have to accept it.

職員影印時，睿看著人們來來往往。一個孩子拖著拖鞋走過地板，直到母親把他抱起。工人小心搬著沉重箱子。在那些造成巨大紛爭的文件周圍，平凡生活仍持續。睿開始明白公共檔案為何重要。它們不只供領導者或法官使用，也幫助普通顧客和農民追問承諾發生了什麼事。沒有紀錄，聲音最大的人就可能決定故事，其他人只能接受。

Before leaving, Rui asked for an official copy of the first agreement and a note about the missing date. The clerk stamped the pages and recorded the request number. Mei put the copies beside their measurements from the river. The two kinds of evidence supported different parts of the story. The water marks showed what the machine did. The old agreement showed what the school was supposed to do. Rui had once thought a repair was only a matter of metal and wood. Now he knew that a repair could also mean keeping a promise that others had forgotten.

離開前，睿申請第一份協議的正式副本，以及日期缺漏的記錄。職員蓋章，記下申請編號。梅把副本放在河道測量資料旁。兩種證據支持故事不同的部分：水位標記說明機器做了什麼，舊協議則說明山門應該做什麼。睿以前以為修理只關乎金屬和木頭；現在他知道，修理也可能是守住別人忘記的承諾。

That night they stayed in a small guesthouse. Rui could hear buses moving outside and was too tired to speak. He checked the money one last time and placed the account beside his bed. The trip had cost less than he expected, though the answers were less simple than he had hoped. Mei asked if he regretted coming. “No,” he said. “I want to know exactly what happened, even if it makes our case harder.” He closed the folder and slept. Tomorrow they would return to the village with more questions, but also with a clear direction for the next stage.

當晚，他們住進一間小旅店。睿聽見窗外公車行駛的聲音，疲倦得不想說話。他最後核對旅費，把帳目放在床邊。這趟旅程花得比預期少，但答案比想像複雜。梅問他後不後悔來。「不，」他說，「我想知道事情究竟怎麼發生，即使真相讓我們的主張更難成立。」他收好資料夾，睡了。明天兩人將帶著更多問題回村，但也知道下一階段該往哪裡查。
