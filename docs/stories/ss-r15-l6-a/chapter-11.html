孔雀後面的門

A Door Behind the Peacock

惡女館長，不肯把歷史賣給王室 · 第 11 / 25 章 · 5000–6000 · 英中對照 · 校訂中

Mara's new audit of museum building plans uncovered a sealed room behind the peacock gallery. The room was absent from current visitor maps but present in an engineer's drawing from forty years earlier. Jorin feared that opening it carelessly could damage the shared wall and the paintings beside it. Viola obtained a safety survey instead of ordering workers to break through. A retired pianist who had played at royal ceremonies remembered musicians entering through a narrow door near the gallery. Her memory suggested a route, not the use to which it had been put. The team checked the old hinges and found recent oil.

瑪拉稽核館舍建築圖時，在孔雀展區後方發現一間封閉房。現行導覽圖沒有，四十年前的工程圖卻畫得清楚。喬林擔心莽撞破牆會傷及共用牆和隔壁畫作。薇奧拉先取得安全勘查，而不是命工人鑿開。一位曾在王室典禮演奏的退休鋼琴家記得樂師會走一扇側門。她的記憶只能指出路線，不能證明房間用途。團隊檢查舊鉸鏈，找到新近的潤滑油。

The hidden chamber remained twenty degrees Celsius despite the winter air outside. Fresh friction marks on the hinge showed that the sealed door had moved recently. A watchdog began to growl when strangers approached the peacock gallery after dark. Viola refused to induce a witness to change her account with promises of employment. Bats inhabit the unused upper passage, so the inspector checked it before opening the door.

隱藏房間即使外面寒冷，仍維持攝氏二十度。 鉸鏈上的新摩擦痕顯示封閉門最近曾被打開。 陌生人夜裡靠近孔雀展區時，看門狗開始低吼。 薇奧拉拒絕用工作承諾誘使證人修改說法。 蝙蝠棲息在閒置上層通道，因此檢查員開門前先勘查。

When a structural inspector cleared the opening, the group entered a low chamber lined with shelves. It contained empty crates, auction catalogues, and a collection of numbered wax tokens. Several tokens corresponded to lots the tribunal had frozen. None identified the Tide Star by name. A careful comparison of shelf dust suggested the heavy crates had been removed recently. Mara photographed the gaps and invited the contractor's observer to check the numbering. Viola wanted to believe she had caught Renn, but a storage room established only that somebody had prepared objects for shipment. Intent and authorization still required separate proof.

結構檢查通過後，眾人進入低矮貨房，看到空木箱、拍賣目錄和編號蠟牌。幾枚蠟牌對應法庭已凍結的拍賣品，卻沒有一枚直接寫潮星儀。架上灰塵顯示重箱近期被移走。瑪拉拍攝空位，請承包商觀察員核對編號。薇奧拉很想相信自己已抓到雷恩，但房間只能證明有人準備運貨；意圖與授權仍須另證。

It seemed ironic that the museum's secret room was hidden behind a display about openness. A mound of dust concealed a small brass token below the storage shelf. A painted peacock covered the narrow doorway that musicians had once used. The retired pianist identified the corridor from memories of royal winter concerts. Mara asked the engineer to probe the wall without damaging the adjacent painting.

密室竟藏在宣揚開放的展覽後面，顯得十分諷刺。 一堆灰塵掩住庫架下的小黃銅牌。 一幅孔雀壁畫遮住樂師曾使用的窄門。 退休鋼琴家憑王室冬季音樂會的記憶辨認走廊。 瑪拉請工程師探查牆壁，但不要傷及旁邊的畫。

The inspector separated structural safety from evidentiary value. Even an incriminating room would not justify a collapsing wall. Mara recorded who entered, what was moved, and which observations remained uncertain. The new floor plan was checked by people who used the building at night, including cleaners and musicians, not solely senior officials. That wider consultation exposed doors the architects had forgotten and made the revised record more useful to anyone working in an emergency.

檢查員區分建築安全與證據價值。即使房間可疑，也不值得讓牆倒塌。瑪拉記錄誰進出、移動了什麼、哪些觀察仍有疑問。新平面圖讓夜間清潔員和樂師一起核對，而非只問高階主管。廣泛諮詢找出建築師遺忘的門，也讓緊急應變紀錄更實用。

The workers and donors each appointed observers from their respective committees. The old director's shortsighted repairs had concealed water damage behind decorative panels. The guide tried to simplify the floor plan without deleting important emergency exits. A shipping map showed the narrow strait through which the missing crates might have traveled. A superficial inspection missed the fresh oil beneath the supposedly unused hinge.

工人與捐助者各自從所屬委員會指派觀察員。 舊館長短視的修理把水損藏在裝飾板後。 導覽員試著簡化平面圖，但不刪掉重要逃生出口。 運輸圖標示失蹤木箱可能經過的狹窄海峽。 表面檢查漏看了據稱未使用鉸鏈下的新油。

One crate retained a torn blue thread of wrapping cloth. Halda said the weave resembled work from a marsh workshop, but she did not claim to recognize its maker. Jorin preserved the fiber for comparative testing. The tribunal ordered the hidden room sealed and required the museum to publish a corrected floor plan, except for security details about vulnerable storage. Renn denounced the procedure as hostile to donors. Viola replied that donors deserved accurate records too. The question had changed: not merely who stole an astrolabe, but who had created an invisible route through an institution that called itself public.

一只木箱還黏著藍色包布纖維。哈爾妲說織法像沼地工坊的作品，卻不敢指認製作者。喬林保存纖維供比較。法庭封存密室，命館方公布修正後的平面圖，但保留易受竊庫房的安全細節。雷恩斥責程序敵視捐助者；薇奧拉說捐助者同樣應得到正確紀錄。問題已不只是誰偷了星盤，而是誰在自稱公共的機構內創造了一條看不見的運送通道。

No official could suppress a valid correction merely because it embarrassed the palace. The brass tokens displayed an unusual symmetry absent from authentic accession seals. A numbered token fell from a crate bound for the private loading bay. Mara hoped the revised register could unify the evidence without erasing disagreement. The urgency of the search did not cancel the requirement for an independent safety inspection.

官員不能只因更正令王室難堪便壓下有效資料。 黃銅牌呈現異常對稱，真入藏印章卻沒有。 一枚編號牌從準備送往私人卸貨區的木箱掉出。 瑪拉希望新登記冊能整合證據，卻不抹除不同意見。 搜索再急迫，也不能取消獨立安全檢查的要求。
