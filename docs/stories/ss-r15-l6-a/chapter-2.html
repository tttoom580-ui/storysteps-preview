從未出售的村落

The Village That Never Sold

惡女館長，不肯把歷史賣給王室 · 第 2 / 25 章 · 5000–6000 · 英中對照 · 校訂中

用互動版查詞、朗讀與收藏

A marsh delegate named Halda arrived before sunrise carrying a box lined with reeds. She had not come to recover an antique for herself. Her village's council had authorized her to challenge the museum's claim that the Tide Star had been sold eighty years earlier. The instrument had guided boats through seasonal channels, and its markings also recorded traditional fishing grounds. Without the original measurements, wealthy traders could pretend the waterways were private. Viola initially expected Halda to rejoice at the discovery of a forged seal. Instead Halda asked why the museum had displayed the object for decades without asking anyone from her community to describe it.

沼地代表哈爾妲在日出前抵達，手裡抱著鋪有蘆葦的盒子。她不是為自己討回古董，而是受村議會授權，質疑博物館宣稱八十年前已買下潮星儀的說法。這件儀器原本用來引導季節性水道上的船隻，刻度也記錄傳統漁場。失去原始測量紀錄，富商便能把公共水域說成私人領地。薇奧拉以為哈爾妲會因發現假印章而歡喜，對方卻問：博物館展出這件物品數十年，為何從未請原社群解說？

The aboriginal navigation exhibit had been labeled as a royal invention without consulting its makers. Reed boats abound in the marsh drawings, although none appear in the palace map. Halda asked the mapmakers to align their measurements with the older shore markers. The old chart recorded the altitude of a hill used to sight the winter stars. Renn could not hide his annoyance when the villagers requested independent interpreters.

原住民航行展被標成王室發明，卻從未徵詢製作者。 沼地圖畫裡蘆葦船隨處可見，王宮地圖卻一艘也沒有。 哈爾妲請製圖師把測量結果與舊岸邊標記對齊。 舊圖記錄一座冬季觀星用山丘的海拔。 村民要求獨立口譯時，雷恩掩飾不住不悅。

Halda opened her box and presented a woven tracing rather than a written deed. Its pattern matched the crescent nick in the old seal. Mara checked whether the tracing had been made recently; its fibers were old, but age alone could not establish ownership. The council could provide witnesses who remembered how elders passed the pattern to apprentices. They would testify publicly if their travel and interpretation expenses were covered without conditions. Viola offered transport from the museum budget and insisted that Halda choose the interpreters. Halda accepted travel support but declined the museum's proposal to speak only through a royal scholar.

哈爾妲打開盒子，拿出的不是書面地契，而是一塊編織描樣，圖案與舊印章的新月缺口相符。瑪拉檢查描樣是否近年製作；纖維雖老，年代本身仍不足以證明所有權。村議會能提供記得長者如何傳承圖案的證人，只要旅費與口譯費沒有附加條件，就願意公開作證。薇奧拉提出由館方支付交通費，並堅持口譯由哈爾妲自行選擇。哈爾妲接受資助，卻拒絕只能透過王室學者發言的安排。

A blunt chisel had left broad marks on the crate, unlike Jorin's fine tools. The disputed water crossing appeared on three village maps but vanished from the sale contract. Mara refused to discard an outdated map while its original annotations remained unexamined. The harbor discharge record showed when the packed cases had left the boat. Recovering the community's history would be a long endeavor rather than a single courtroom victory.

鈍鑿在木箱上留下寬痕，與喬林的精細工具不同。 爭議渡口出現在三份村圖，卻從出售契約消失。 原始註解還沒查清，瑪拉拒絕丟棄舊地圖。 港口卸貨紀錄寫明包裝展箱何時離船。 找回社群歷史是漫長努力，不是一場法庭勝利就能完成。

The investigating panel began distinguishing possession from lawful title. A museum might hold a delicate object for decades without acquiring the right to sell it. Even a valid purchase of the metal would not automatically transfer a community's shared water knowledge. Viola listed these questions separately on the public board and invited written objections. That procedure irritated donors who wanted a single quick answer, but it also prevented Halda from being treated as a symbol instead of a person capable of refusing the museum's offers.

調查小組開始區分「實際保管」與「合法所有權」。博物館即使收藏脆弱文物數十年，也不一定取得出售權。即使金屬本體曾合法購得，社群共有的水道知識也不會自動轉移。薇奧拉把問題分開列在公告板上，開放書面異議。這個程序讓想要迅速結案的捐贈者不滿，卻也避免哈爾妲被當成象徵，而不是能拒絕館方提議的人。

The first hurdle was proving that the old seal belonged to an actual village council. Halda changed her posture when Renn described her grandfather as an unreliable storyteller. The metalworker measured the crescent-shaped defect with remarkable precision. The court demanded a rigorous comparison before it would accept the tracing as evidence. An elder identified the stone terrace where apprentices once studied the night sky.

第一道障礙是證明舊印章確實屬於村議會。 雷恩稱她祖父是不可靠的說書人時，哈爾妲坐姿一變。 金工以驚人的精確度測量新月形缺口。 法庭要求嚴謹比對後才願採納描樣為證據。 長者指認學徒從前學觀星的石砌平台。

That afternoon a courier brought a directive from Councilor Renn ordering all community drawings dismissed as folklore. Viola knew Renn from the novel as the man who testified against her. This time she did not try to expose him by shouting his future actions. She asked him to cite the clause that made an oral tradition inadmissible. He could not find one, because no such clause existed. The clerk recorded the exchange. The evidence was still incomplete, but the hearing could no longer exclude the marsh witnesses merely for lacking palace stationery. Halda promised to return with two elders; Viola promised only that they would be heard.

下午，一名信差送來議員雷恩的命令，要求把社群描樣全部視為民間故事而排除。薇奧拉知道原著中正是雷恩出庭指控她，這次卻沒有喊出他未來的罪行。她只請他指出哪一條法律禁止口述傳統作證。他找不到，因為根本沒有那條規定。書記把問答記錄下來。證據仍不完整，但聽證不再能只因沼地證人沒有王室用紙就把人排除。哈爾妲答應帶兩位長者回來；薇奧拉只承諾他們一定有發言機會。

The palace catalogue called the village protest an uprising, though witnesses described a peaceful gathering. A volunteer offered to usher the elderly witnesses to the hearing entrance. The Tide Star was a versatile instrument for navigation, weather observation, and seasonal teaching. The marsh council retained a veto over any public use of its sacred recordings. A fisherman's widow brought a receipt proving that her family had only lent the compass.

王宮目錄把村民抗議稱作起義，證人卻說那是和平集會。 志工主動引領年長證人到聽證入口。 潮星儀是兼具導航、觀天氣與季節教學用途的多功能儀器。 沼地議會保留否決公開使用神聖錄音的權利。 漁夫的遺孀帶來收據，證明家人當年只是出借羅盤。
