河下密庫

The River Vault

惡女館長，不肯把歷史賣給王室 · 第 12 / 25 章 · 5000–6000 · 英中對照 · 校訂中

The numbered tokens matched a storage lease for a chamber beneath the river embankment. Its legal owner was a charitable society that had rented space to the auction contractor. Viola resisted the temptation to treat the society as a criminal front. Its directors had separate responsibilities, and the lease might have been lawful when signed. The tribunal sent a notice demanding preservation of listed crates and invited the society to appoint a representative. Halda accompanied the inspection party because the flood gates near the chamber could endanger nearby homes. Jorin brought a moisture meter; he feared water damage more than dramatic revelations.

編號蠟牌對上河堤下貨房的一份租約，法律所有人是一個曾出租空間給拍賣承包商的慈善團體。薇奧拉沒有直接把慈善團體當成罪犯掩護；董事各有職責，簽約當時也可能合法。法庭發出特定木箱保全通知，邀團體指派代表。哈爾妲因密庫旁的水閘可能危及住戶而同行，喬林則帶濕度計，比起戲劇性發現，他更害怕文物受潮。

A clear brook crossed the path beside the river's old stone embankment. The vault displayed a coral ornament that had never been included in its lease. A secret auction would deprive families of the chance to challenge the object's ownership. The safety officer asked porters to elevate the fragile crates above the rising water. Mara wrote the permit's expiration date in red so nobody could treat it as permanent.

一條清澈小溪流過舊石堤旁的小路。 密庫展示一件從未列入租約的珊瑚飾物。 祕密拍賣會剝奪家庭質疑文物所有權的機會。 安全員請搬運員把脆弱木箱墊高，避開上漲水位。 瑪拉用紅字寫出許可到期日，避免有人把它當成永久。

The vault held paintings, fishing instruments, and crates of ceramic fragments, but no complete astrolabe. One painting had a label naming an owner who had died before the claimed donation date. The society's chair showed the original lease and admitted that she had never inspected the inventory. She had trusted Renn's assurance that the contents were harmless exhibition supplies. Viola asked whether any charitable funds had paid for moving them. The chair found two disbursements labeled as flood repairs. Those expenses could be errors; an auditor would need invoices and bank records before determining whether money had been diverted.

密庫裡有畫、漁具和陶瓷碎片箱，卻沒有完整星盤。一幅畫的標籤標示某人捐贈，日期卻在那人過世後。慈善團體主席拿出原租約，承認自己從未檢查貨物清單，只信了雷恩稱它們是無害展覽用品的話。薇奧拉問是否曾用慈善款支付搬運，主席找到兩筆標成防洪修繕的支出。也許只是誤列，仍需稽核發票與銀行紀錄才能知道是否挪用。

An elder with poor eyesight requested a large-print copy of the recovered letter. The charity's volunteers served a hearty breakfast before the riverside inspection. A rescue bird seemed to hover above the water gate as the current rose. The emergency route led inland to higher ground beyond the flood wall. Halda's insistence on stopping the search prevented a dangerous entry into the vault.

一位視力不佳的長者要求尋回信件的大字影本。 慈善團體志工在河邊檢查前供應豐盛早餐。 水位上漲時，一隻救援鳥似乎在水閘上方盤旋。 緊急路線通往堤牆後方地勢較高的內陸。 哈爾妲堅持停止搜索，避免危險進入密庫。

A legal lease could explain access without explaining a false expense. The inspectors separated their observations into ownership, safety, and finances rather than forcing a single story to fit all three. Mara recorded the measured water level and the exact time the group left. The withdrawal would let critics argue that the team was cautious, but it would also make the eventual evidence credible. Viola began to understand that restraint was not the absence of courage; sometimes it was the condition for continuing the inquiry.

合法租約可以解釋進出權，卻無法解釋虛假的支出名稱。調查員把所有權、安全與財務分開處理，不強迫三條線索拼成同一故事。瑪拉記錄水位與撤離時間。批評者可以說團隊太保守，但這也讓後續證據更可信。薇奧拉漸漸理解，克制不是缺乏勇氣，有時正是調查得以繼續的條件。

The lease authorized storage space but did not transfer ownership of the museum objects. A local legislator requested an independent audit of the society's flood-repair expenses. The majestic river painting concealed a modest note written by the original marsh owner. The case gained momentum when the bank confirmed two unexplained transfers. The monarch's seal appeared on the contract, but nobody could locate her signed approval.

租約只授權使用倉儲空間，沒有轉移館藏所有權。 地方立法者要求獨立稽核慈善團體防洪修繕支出。 雄偉河景畫下藏著原沼地所有者寫的簡短註記。 銀行確認兩筆無法解釋的轉帳後，案件調查加速。 契約上有君主印章，卻無人找到她親筆核准。

At the river steps, Halda noticed a rising current and insisted that the inspection stop. Renn's agent accused her of hiding evidence. Jorin supported the pause: the river gauge had reached the safety mark stated in the permit. The team evacuated and secured only what the order specifically named. Viola later watched rain strike the museum windows, frustrated that the vault could not be searched again until water levels fell. She decided that preventing an avoidable injury mattered more than completing a triumphant chapter in a story. The tribunal scheduled a separate hearing on the charity's accounting rather than assuming its chair was a conspirator.

到河階時，哈爾妲看見水流上升，堅持停止檢查。雷恩的代理人反指她藏證據。喬林支持暫停，因為水位已達許可證標示的安全線。團隊撤離，只保全命令明列的物品。薇奧拉看著雨打館窗，為無法立刻再查密庫而懊惱，卻決定避免傷人比完成英雄式破案更重要。法庭另外安排慈善帳務聽證，沒有先把主席當共犯。

The artist's offspring asked why their mother's portrait had been labeled a palace gift. The inspectors smelled petrol near the vault entrance and checked for a fuel leak. Workers planned to rotate the crates only after the lifting frame was tested. Viola warned that a private monopoly could strangle community access to its own history. The medical box contained a surgical instrument whose owner had never consented to its sale.

藝術家的子女問母親肖像為何被標成王宮贈禮。 檢查員在密庫入口聞到汽油味，於是查看是否漏油。 工人打算在吊架測試後才旋轉木箱。 薇奧拉警告私人壟斷可能扼殺社群接觸自身歷史的機會。 醫療箱中有一件手術器械，原主人從未同意出售。
