The Donor's Ledger

惡女館長，不肯把歷史賣給王室 · 第 6 / 25 章 · 5000–6000 · 英中對照 · 校訂中

用互動版查詞、朗讀與收藏

At dawn Mara located an abandoned donor ledger beneath the accounting office floor. Its margins carried letters assigning false values to objects from marsh settlements, and the ink had been added in two distinct hands. Viola recognized one signature as her own. She remembered that the original curator had accepted a formula for estimating collection values, but now saw how it allowed the private auction company to mark a disputed artifact as an ordinary gift. Renn arrived with a lawyer who demanded the ledger be returned to him as confidential property. Viola ordered nobody to touch it until the tribunal could appoint an independent examiner.

清晨，瑪拉在會計室地板下找到一本廢棄的捐贈帳簿。邊欄用字母替沼地藏品標上虛假價值，墨跡分屬兩種筆跡。薇奧拉認出其中一個簽名是自己的。原著館長曾批准估價公式，如今她才看出那套公式如何使私人拍賣商把爭議文物登記成普通禮物。雷恩帶著律師趕來，要求以機密財產為由取回帳本。薇奧拉下令，在法庭指定獨立鑑定人之前，任何人不得碰觸。

Renn's lawyer could allege misconduct, but the court required specific actions and dates. The council refused to beautify the entrance while the staff toilets remained unsafe. Mara corrected a caption that wrongly described the marsh canoe as royal property. The first hearing reached its climax when the donor ledger was placed beside the auction list. Jorin would extract a small fiber sample only after the owners approved testing.

雷恩的律師可以指控不當行為，但法庭要求具體行動和日期。 員工廁所尚不安全，委員會拒絕先美化入口。 瑪拉更正一則錯把沼地獨木舟稱為王室財產的說明。 首次聽證在捐贈帳本與拍賣清單並列時達到高潮。 喬林只有取得所有者同意後才會抽取少量纖維樣本。

The examiner compared the ledger with tax receipts, donor correspondence, and a register of objects that had been repacked after a traveling exhibition. A missing line connected the museum to a company on the city's outskirts. Jorin explained why this was not proof that Renn had stolen the Tide Star: the invoice described a crate without naming its contents. The team nevertheless identified a worker who had signed for its delivery. She agreed to speak only if her supervisor could not attend. Viola approved the private interview, with a second neutral recorder and a copy for the worker to correct.

鑑定人把帳簿與稅收據、捐助者信函、巡迴展覽後重新包裝的清冊比對。缺失的一行帳目連向城郊公司。喬林解釋，發票只寫木箱，沒有記載內容，還不能證明雷恩偷走潮星儀。不過團隊找到了簽收貨物的工人。她願意陳述，條件是主管不能在場。薇奧拉同意在中立記錄員陪同下私下訪談，並讓工人取得可更正的逐字稿。

Students joined an extracurricular history club to help catalogue local fishing tools. The quarry families remained hospitable to investigators despite their anger at the palace. The usually jolly driver grew quiet when he recognized the private auction seal. Workers offered to mow the neglected path so elderly witnesses could reach the hall. The warehouse manager with a gray mustache denied authorizing the unlisted shipment.

學生參加課外歷史社，協助整理在地漁具目錄。 採石場家庭雖對王宮憤怒，仍熱情接待調查員。 平日開朗的車夫認出私人拍賣印章時沉默了。 工人提議修剪荒廢小徑，方便年長證人到會堂。 留著灰鬍子的倉庫經理否認批准未登記的貨運。

Viola required the investigators to name every limit on their authority before they entered the yard. A useful search would preserve an object's position, original packing, and chain of custody; a theatrical search could damage the same evidence it sought. She invited a representative chosen by the workers and one by the owners to observe. Both sides found this cumbersome. Neither side could later claim that an unseen official had silently rewritten the result.

薇奧拉要求調查員進貨場前列明權限界線。有效搜索要保存物品位置、原包裝和保管鏈；做戲式搜索反而可能毀掉證據。她邀請工人與業主各推選一名觀察員。雙方都嫌麻煩，但往後也都不能指控有看不見的官員偷偷改寫結果。

Viola chose not to nag the porter for an answer he could not honestly remember. A painted oasis in the desert gallery belonged to a caravan family, not the crown. The metalworker weighed every ounce of the recovered pin before testing it. A repair invoice directed the workers to a warehouse on the city's outskirts. A parliamentary clerk confirmed that parliament had never voted to authorize the private sale.

搬運員確實記不得，薇奧拉決定不反覆催問。 沙漠展廳畫中的綠洲屬於商隊家族，而非王室。 金工在檢測前稱量尋回插銷的每一盎司重量。 修復發票指示工人到城郊倉庫。 議會書記確認，國會從未表決批准私人出售。

The worker recalled a crate heavy enough to require two porters, carried through the night gate after the museum was closed. She had seen a painted crescent on its side. It matched the symbol on the old seal, but it might have marked any shipment from the marsh. Viola asked for the gate book instead of celebrating. Renn's lawyer threatened a complaint for disrupting trade, and the tribunal responded by permitting a one-hour inspection of the company yard. No search of private homes was authorized. The ledger stayed sealed, while the first physical route from the vanished case came into focus.

工人記得一只重得需要兩名搬運員的箱子，在閉館後從夜門送出，箱側畫著新月。圖案與舊印章相似，但也可能標示任何來自沼地的貨物。薇奧拉沒有慶祝，而是要求查閱大門簿。雷恩的律師威脅控告妨害交易，法庭則只批准檢查公司貨場一小時，沒有准許搜索私人住宅。帳簿繼續封存，失蹤展品的第一條實際運送路線卻漸漸清楚。

A ruthless collector threatened to withdraw school funding unless the village abandoned its claim. A sculptor identified her own repair mark beneath a frame sold as an ancient original. The new notice gained clarity through simplicity, not by hiding the disputed evidence. Simultaneous inspections at both gates revealed that one crate could not have traveled as claimed. Donations began to slump after the museum published the disputed accession figures.

一名冷酷收藏家威脅撤回學校捐款，除非村落放棄申索。 雕刻家在當作古代真品出售的框架下辨認出自己的修復記號。 新公告因簡潔而清楚，不是靠隱瞞爭議證據。 兩處大門同時檢查，顯示一只木箱不可能按所稱路線運送。 博物館公布爭議入藏數字後，捐款開始下滑。
