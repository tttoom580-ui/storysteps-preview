The Public Inventory

惡女館長，不肯把歷史賣給王室 · 第 10 / 25 章 · 5000–6000 · 英中對照 · 校訂中

On the tenth day, the museum opened a supervised reading room for accession records. The first visitors were not scholars. A fisherman searched for the compass his grandfather had lent to the palace after a storm. A retired seamstress asked why her mother's wedding cloth appeared as a royal gift. An apprentice claimed the museum had never paid for the carved wooden frame he repaired. Viola realized that the Tide Star case had exposed a wider system of missing consent, unpaid labor, and confused titles. The records were incomplete, but allowing people to inspect them would reveal errors the professional staff had overlooked.

第十天，博物館開放有監督的入藏紀錄閱覽室。最先到來的不是學者。漁夫尋找祖父在暴風雨後借給王宮的羅盤；退休裁縫問母親的婚禮布料為何成了王室贈禮；學徒則說自己修復木雕框架卻從未領到工錢。薇奧拉發現潮星儀失竊案揭開更廣的問題：缺失的同意、未付的勞務與混亂所有權。紀錄雖不完整，讓當事人查閱仍能發現專業人員漏看的錯誤。

Renn offered one concession on visiting hours but refused to disclose the buyer's identity. A cynical collector called the public inventory merely an attempt to win applause. The contract would default to a private sale if nobody challenged it before the deadline. Repairs could deplete the maintenance fund unless the museum separated emergency costs. A faint fragrance of jasmine lingered in the reading room beside the returned map.

雷恩在參觀時間上讓步一點，卻拒絕公開買家身分。 一名憤世嫉俗的收藏家說公開清冊只是博掌聲。 若期限前無人提出異議，契約將依預設條款轉為私人出售。 若不把緊急成本分開，修復可能耗盡維護基金。 歸還地圖旁的閱覽室仍有淡淡茉莉香。

Renn tried to close the reading room by citing the privacy of former donors. Mara proposed a practical distinction: private addresses and medical details would be protected, while accession numbers, transfer dates, and public contracts remained open. The tribunal approved that limited rule and appointed a privacy assessor from outside the museum. Halda agreed to the arrangement but insisted that the communities themselves decide which sacred descriptions should not be displayed. Viola publicly supported the request, then faced angry donors who called her disloyal. She answered that transparency did not mean making every person's intimate history a spectacle.

雷恩以過去捐贈者隱私為由企圖關閉閱覽室。瑪拉提出區分：私人地址和醫療資訊應受保護，入藏編號、交接日期和公共契約仍可開放。法庭批准有限規則，並任命館外的隱私鑑定員。哈爾妲同意，但堅持由社群自行決定哪些神聖敘事不可展示。薇奧拉公開支持，惹得捐助者怒斥不忠。她回答，透明不代表把每個人的親密歷史當成表演。

A donor attempted to intrude upon a confidential interview with an elderly claimant. The visitor was a retired jockey who remembered transporting exhibition horses during royal festivals. The clerk recorded the literal wording of the ownership claim without adding her opinion. Staff morale improved when wages could be paid from an independent account. The court could prosecute fraud only after evidence met the required standard.

一名捐助者企圖闖入年長申請人的保密訪談。 訪客是退休賽馬騎師，記得王室節慶時曾載運展演馬匹。 書記逐字記下所有權主張，沒有加入個人意見。 薪資得以由獨立帳戶支付後，員工士氣有所改善。 證據達到法定標準後，法庭才能起訴詐欺。

The reading room kept an index of corrections rather than silently replacing flawed pages. Each claimant received a written acknowledgement, a response deadline, and a route to independent review. Staff were trained to say 'not established' when evidence was missing, rather than inventing certainty. Viola also requested an audit of the fast-track rule she had once approved. The museum's fate now depended less on a single heroic discovery than on whether ordinary people could continue checking its records after the headlines faded.

閱覽室保留更正索引，而非悄悄換掉錯頁。每位申請人都收到書面收件證明、回覆期限和獨立複查途徑。員工被訓練在證據不足時說「尚未證實」，而非裝出確定。薇奧拉也要求稽核她曾核准的快速入藏規則。館舍命運越來越不取決於一個英雄式發現，而在於新聞熱潮過後，普通人能否持續查核紀錄。

A radioactive mineral sample required special handling unrelated to the ordinary art inventory. Renn prepared a sharp retort, but Mara asked him to answer the question first. Jorin used a small screwdriver to remove the damaged support without touching the bronze. The volunteers offered a serving of soup to each person waiting for the hearing. The palace's former splendor could not establish a lawful title to the marsh collection.

一件放射性礦物標本需特殊處理，不同於一般藝術藏品。 雷恩準備尖刻回嘴，瑪拉卻請他先回答問題。 喬林用小螺絲起子拆除損壞支架，避免碰觸青銅。 志工給每名等候聽證的人一份湯。 王宮往昔的輝煌，無法證明它合法擁有沼地收藏。

The tenth-day report froze twenty disputed objects, released eleven clearly owned items for educational display, and created a procedure for requesting corrections. The Tide Star was not among the recovered pieces. Viola accepted that the case remained open. During the public briefing, Mara announced that the museum's former curator had approved an unsafe fast-track registration rule. The audience went silent when Viola acknowledged the signature. Renn expected that admission to destroy her support; instead it made the coming ethics hearing unavoidable. She could not ask the institution to reveal its mistakes while concealing her own.

第十天報告凍結二十件爭議文物，准許十一件所有權明確的物品繼續教育展示，並建立更正申請程序。潮星儀仍未尋回。薇奧拉接受調查未結束。公開簡報中，瑪拉宣布前館長曾核准不安全的快速入藏規則。薇奧拉承認簽名時全場安靜。雷恩期待這會毀掉她的支持，結果只是讓倫理聽證再也無法迴避。她不能要求機構揭露錯誤，卻隱藏自己的過失。

A spontaneous round of applause followed the announcement that workers could read the records. One claimant's stepfather held a receipt showing the family's tapestry had been borrowed. A frightened clerk began to stutter when the minister demanded immediate answers. The city agreed to subsidize essential repairs without purchasing any disputed object. The laboratory identified synthetic dye in a label described as centuries old.

宣布工人可以查閱紀錄後，現場自發響起掌聲。 一名申請人的繼父持有收據，證明家中掛毯只是借出。 部長要求立刻回答時，害怕的書記開始口吃。 市府同意補助必要修繕，但不因此購買任何爭議文物。 實驗室在號稱數百年的標籤上驗出合成染料。
