商人的地圖

The Merchant's Map

惡女館長，不肯把歷史賣給王室 · 第 9 / 25 章 · 5000–6000 · 英中對照 · 校訂中

A map brought to the museum by a merchant placed the Tide Star's former home on an uninhabited island, far from Halda's village. If the map was accepted, Renn could claim that no living community held rights to the object. Mara found that its ink differed between the island name and the rest of the text. The mapmaker admitted that the label had been added on a client's instruction, but declined to name the client without a legal order. Viola asked the tribunal for a narrow disclosure order rather than publicly threatening the man. His silence was not yet evidence of guilt.

一名商人送來地圖，把潮星儀的舊故鄉畫在無人居住的小島，遠離哈爾妲的村落。若地圖獲採信，雷恩便能主張沒有現存社群擁有文物。瑪拉發現島名墨水與其餘文字不同。製圖師承認標籤是依客戶指示後加，卻拒絕未經法令便透露姓名。薇奧拉向法庭申請範圍有限的揭露命令，沒有公開恐嚇他；沉默尚不等於犯罪。

The board feared that opening the records would alienate wealthy donors. Mara noticed a purple blot over the island name on the merchant's map. The island lodge served chili soup to the boat crew during the cold crossing. Halda wanted continuity between the old waterway teachings and the new public maps. The cumulative weight of the shipping dates made the merchant's explanation difficult to sustain.

董事會擔心公開紀錄會疏遠富裕捐贈者。 瑪拉注意到商人地圖的島名上有一塊紫色墨漬。 島上旅舍在寒冷航程後供船員喝辣椒湯。 哈爾妲希望舊水道教學與新公開地圖保持連續性。 各次出貨日期累積起來，使商人的說法越來越難成立。

Halda organized a walking survey with two cartographers and village elders. They visited an old maple grove, a stone landing, and a seasonal channel whose boundaries shifted during heavy rain. The new map omitted these features even though older records showed them clearly. The omission could have been careless, but the client had paid for revisions after receiving the earlier charts. Meanwhile the museum's gardeners reported a truck delivering boxes to a supposedly abandoned lodge on the island. Viola connected the accounts only on the investigation board, labeling the route a lead rather than a fact.

哈爾妲與兩名製圖師及村中長者安排步行勘測，走訪老楓樹林、石造登岸處和雨季改道的水路。新地圖省略這些舊紀錄清楚標出的地貌。或許是疏忽，但委託人確實在收到前稿後付費要求修改。館內園丁也回報，一輛貨車正向島上據稱廢棄的旅舍送箱。薇奧拉只在調查板把兩件事連成待查線索，沒有寫成已確立的事實。

A decisive conclusion would require the original commission letter, not only a suspicious ink mark. The contractor treated local navigators as dispensable even when his captains relied on them. A jasmine hedge marked the old entrance to the island's public landing. The supposedly empty lodge had received fresh crates twice in the previous week. The missing maple grove still appeared in the marsh council's century-old drawing.

要作決定性結論，必須取得原委託信，不能只憑可疑墨痕。 承包商把在地引航者當成可有可無，船長卻一直依賴他們。 茉莉花籬標示島上公共登岸處的舊入口。 據稱空置的旅舍上週卻收過兩次新木箱。 消失的楓樹林仍在沼地議會百年前的描圖中。

The team documented where public access ended and private property began. Their notes distinguished the map's original drawing, the altered label, and every copy distributed afterward. A false location could misdirect a title hearing even when the object itself was real. Viola scheduled the next session for the village, not the palace, so witnesses who could not afford travel still had a chance to challenge the evidence. That procedural choice angered Renn more than the footprints did.

團隊記下公共通行和私人土地的分界，將原圖、後加的標籤與每份流通影本分別保管。地圖即使描繪真實物件，假地點仍會誤導所有權聽證。薇奧拉把下一次會議排在村裡而非王宮，讓負擔不起旅費的證人也能質疑證據。這個程序安排比腳印更令雷恩憤怒。

An old deed called the island's female leaseholder the mistress of the northern house. Halda helped mobilize elders to check the map before the next hearing. Viola had no time to muse about the novel's ending while a witness awaited protection. The schoolteacher's persistence persuaded the court to examine the overlooked landing records. A socialist workers' newspaper questioned why public objects could be sold without public review.

舊契約把島上女性承租者稱為北屋女主人。 哈爾妲協助動員長者，在下次聽證前核對地圖。 證人還在等保護時，薇奧拉沒時間沉思原著結局。 教師的堅持使法庭願意檢查被忽視的登岸紀錄。 一份社會主義工人報質疑，公共文物為何能不經公眾審查便出售。

The tribunal ordered the merchant to preserve his commission letters and permitted a visit to the island's public landing. It did not authorize entry into the private lodge. Halda insisted on joining the team; the marsh community had known the island's waters for generations, and she refused to be treated as a decorative guide. When Viola agreed, the minister's allies accused her of yielding control to outsiders. She answered that expertise could come from those who lived with a landscape, not only from officials who owned the maps. At the pier they found fresh footprints, but no missing astrolabe.

法庭命令商人保存委託信，准許調查隊前往島上公共碼頭，卻不准進入私人旅舍。哈爾妲堅持同行，沼地社群世代熟悉這片水域，她拒絕只當裝飾性的嚮導。薇奧拉同意，部長盟友便指責她讓外人控制調查。她回答，專業不只來自持有地圖的官員，也來自長期生活在地景中的人。調查隊在碼頭發現新腳印，卻沒有找到星盤。

After the survey, villagers could socialize without being treated as witnesses every minute. The abandoned footbridge had a narrow span too weak for the loaded wagon. The team began a long trek from the marsh village to the island's northern shore. A vigorous debate arose over whether the corrected map should be released immediately. The courier wrapped the tracing in waterproof cloth before boarding the ferry.

勘查後，村民終於能自在交際，而非每分鐘都被當作證人。 廢棄步橋的跨距狹窄，無法承受滿載貨車。 隊伍從沼地村落長途跋涉到島北岸。 對於是否立刻公開更正地圖，現場展開激烈辯論。 信差上渡船前用防水布包好描樣。
