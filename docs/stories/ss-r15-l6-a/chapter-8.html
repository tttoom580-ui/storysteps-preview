The Hidden Seal

惡女館長，不肯把歷史賣給王室 · 第 8 / 25 章 · 5000–6000 · 英中對照 · 校訂中

用互動版查詞、朗讀與收藏

The quarry copies led Mara to the museum's northern storehouse, where rejected display frames and broken transport cases had been stacked for years. Under a layer of dust, Jorin found the missing central pin of the Tide Star. It had been removed with care, not broken by accident. A certificate attached to its box claimed the pin was a modern replacement, but its measurements matched an older community drawing. Viola asked an independent metalworker to test the alloy without damaging it. She wanted to establish whether the pin belonged to the astrolabe before naming anyone who had handled the box.

採石場抄本帶瑪拉來到博物館北倉，裡面多年堆放著淘汰展架與破運輸箱。喬林在灰塵下找到潮星儀失蹤的中央插銷。它是被小心取下，而非意外折斷。盒上的證明寫它是現代替件，尺寸卻與古老社群描樣一致。薇奧拉請獨立金工在不傷害物件的前提下測試合金，先確認插銷是否屬於潮星儀，再談經手者責任。

The accumulation of dust showed which storage shelves had recently been disturbed. Mara offered a warm beverage to the witness who had waited through the night. A blond assistant remembered delivering the sealed replacement certificates to Renn's office. Jorin would not certify the recovered pin before an independent metal test. The contractor lacked the necessary clearance to enter the northern storehouse after closing.

灰塵堆積情況顯示哪些庫架最近被碰過。 瑪拉替徹夜等候的證人準備熱飲。 一位金髮助理記得曾把密封替件證明送往雷恩辦公室。 獨立金屬檢測完成前，喬林不會正式認證尋回插銷。 承包商沒有閉館後進入北倉所需的通行許可。

The metalworker found a tiny notch under the decorative ridge, in the same position marked in Halda's tracing. The alloy itself was common, so the notch mattered more than the chemical composition. He wrote a qualified report: the pin was consistent with the Tide Star, not conclusively identical. Mara checked the certificate's numbering against the paper register and discovered an administrative gap. Renn's office had authorized three replacement certificates on the day the central pin disappeared. Only two could be found. The third had been issued under the curator's stamp, a fact Viola could neither ignore nor explain away.

金工在裝飾脊線下找到一處小缺口，位置與哈爾妲的描樣相同。合金很常見，因此缺口比化學成分更有價值。他在報告寫「與潮星儀相符」，而非「確定相同」。瑪拉比對證明編號與紙本冊，發現行政空缺：中央插銷消失當天，雷恩辦公室批准三份替件證明，現在只有兩份。第三份使用館長印章簽發，薇奧拉無法忽視或輕易辯解。

The auditor refused to deduct unexplained transport costs from workers' unpaid wages. The fragile astrolabe was not destined for a private cabinet merely because its label said so. The lifting crew did not exert pressure on the damaged bronze rim. The porter rejected the heroic story that he had single-handedly saved the entire collection. A palace lieutenant signed the gate log but denied seeing the crates' contents.

稽核員拒絕從工人欠薪中扣除無法解釋的運輸費。 脆弱星盤不會只因標籤這樣寫，就注定進入私人收藏櫃。 吊掛團隊沒有向受損青銅外緣施加壓力。 搬運員拒絕那個把他說成單槍匹馬救出全部收藏的英雄故事。 王宮中尉簽過大門簿，卻否認看見箱內物品。

The metal report gave the investigators a physical comparison, not a final answer. Mara marked the conclusion with its exact confidence level and kept the raw measurements available to both sides. The revised certification process also had an expiration date so a temporary emergency rule would not become permanent government by accident. Viola accepted the resulting loss of status. Whatever her memory knew about the novel's villains, the living people around her deserved procedures that did not depend on her private certainty.

金工報告提供實體比較，不是最終答案。瑪拉標示推論的可信程度，保留兩造都可查閱的原始測量值。新證明程序也設期限，以免臨時緊急規則意外變成永久統治。薇奧拉接受地位受損。即使她記得原著反派的名字，眼前活著的人也值得一套不依賴她個人確信的程序。

A literate apprentice helped older workers review the certificates without speaking for them. A lotus pattern on the bronze pin matched the drawing preserved by the marsh council. A marginal note in the catalogue redirected two objects to the private warehouse. The delivery times overlap, so one driver could not have handled both wagons. A provincial museum offered to store the disputed frame while the title hearing proceeded.

識字的學徒協助年長工人閱讀證明，但沒有代替他們發言。 青銅插銷上的蓮花圖案與沼地議會保存的描圖相符。 目錄邊緣註記把兩件文物轉往私人倉庫。 兩趟送貨時間重疊，因此同一司機不可能同時駕兩車。 一所地方博物館願在所有權聽證期間保管爭議框架。

Viola voluntarily surrendered control of the curator's seal. The tribunal assigned Mara and a community observer to supervise future certifications, and Jorin moved the pin to a neutral cabinet. Some loyal staff accused Viola of weakening her own defense. She told them that a fair investigation could not rely on a suspect's exclusive access to evidence. That night a messenger carried a sealed complaint from the auction contractor. It accused Halda of inventing the village pattern. Halda demanded to read it herself before the panel answered. She would defend her history in her own words, not through Viola's voice.

薇奧拉主動交出館長印章管理權。法庭指定瑪拉及社群觀察員監督往後的證明，喬林把插銷移入中立保管櫃。幾名忠心員工指責她削弱自身辯護。她回答，公平調查不能讓嫌疑人獨占證據。當夜，拍賣承包商送來密封申訴，指控哈爾妲捏造村落圖案。哈爾妲要求先親自讀完再讓小組答覆，她要用自己的話維護歷史，而不是透過薇奧拉代言。

The examiner disclosed her qualification in metal conservation and her previous work for the crown. Volunteers brought refreshments for the elders attending the long certification meeting. The team needed a neutral order to retrieve the box from the restricted cabinet. The original owner had learned navigation in the swamp beyond the museum's mapped territory. Jorin called conservation a vocation that required humility before uncertain evidence.

鑑定人公開自己的金屬修復資格及曾替王室工作的經歷。 志工替參加漫長認證會的長者送來茶點。 團隊要有中立命令才能取回限制櫃中的箱子。 原所有者曾在博物館地圖之外的沼澤學習導航。 喬林說修復是一種志業，面對不確定證據必須謙遜。
