病房展區

The Sickroom Exhibit

惡女館長，不肯把歷史賣給王室 · 第 3 / 25 章 · 5000–6000 · 英中對照 · 校訂中

用互動版查詞、朗讀與收藏

A junior attendant reported that the museum's medical gallery had been cleared before the opening, although its inventory still listed hundreds of donated objects. The absent items included instruments, vaccination registers, and a child's diary from a former orphanage. Viola walked through the silent room with Jorin and found fresh scratches on the floor beneath an empty cabinet. A salesman claimed the display had been moved for repairs. Yet the work order described a broken shelf in another wing and bore no conservator's signature. Viola suspected a coordinated removal, but she instructed Mara to ask the workers what they had actually seen before filing a criminal allegation.

一名年輕勤務員報告，醫療展區在開幕前已被清空，清冊卻仍列有數百件捐贈物，包括醫療器具、疫苗登記冊與舊孤兒院兒童的日記。薇奧拉和喬林走過安靜的展廳，看見空櫃下方地板有新刮痕。商人聲稱展品只是搬去維修，但工作單記載的是另一翼的破架子，也沒有修復師簽名。她懷疑有人協調搬運，卻先請瑪拉詢問工人親眼看見的情況，再提出刑事指控。

An advisory notice warned visitors that ownership of the medical exhibit remained disputed. A mural celebrated royal conquest, but the community diary described families driven from their homes. Viola refused to declare a conspiracy until the missing crates could be traced independently. Volunteers sliced cucumber for the tired children waiting outside the archive. The oldest survivor spoke in a feeble voice but corrected the royal dates clearly.

告示提醒訪客，醫療展區的所有權仍有爭議。 壁畫歌頌王室征服，社群日記卻記錄被迫離家的家庭。 木箱尚未獨立查明前，薇奧拉拒絕斷言存在陰謀。 志工替在檔案室外等候的疲憊孩子切小黃瓜。 年紀最長的倖存者聲音微弱，卻清楚糾正王室年代。

The medical collection had been assembled after an epidemic that the royal court once called a rebellion. Its papers showed that healers from the marsh had treated both soldiers and displaced children. A grandmother remembered carrying supplies to the orphanage, but she could not identify the present removal crew. The workers supplied a cart number and a gate time instead. Those mundane details mattered. The museum had only one freight ramp wide enough for the large iron cabinet, and its wheels had left a distinctive black streak on the stone. Jorin preserved the streak before anyone scrubbed the floor.

這批醫療藏品源於一場瘟疫，王庭當年卻把它稱作叛亂。文書記錄沼地醫者曾同時救治士兵和失去家園的兒童。一名祖母記得自己曾把物資送往孤兒院，卻無法辨認現今搬運者。工人提供了貨車編號和通過大門的時間。這些平凡細節十分重要：館內只有一處貨坡道夠寬，能搬出大型鐵櫃，而櫃輪在石地上留下獨特黑痕。喬林在任何人擦洗前先保存痕跡。

A firecracker exploded outside the hearing, startling witnesses but causing no injury. A damaged storage jar began to fume when workers opened its leaking lid. Jorin traced a line of fresh grease left by the wheels beneath the missing cabinet. The registrar hoped clear interview rules would lessen the children's fear of the court. The wooden crate had a blue cloth liner unlike anything used by the museum.

聽證外一枚鞭炮爆響，嚇到證人卻沒有人受傷。 工人打開破損罐蓋後，漏液的保存罐開始冒出煙霧。 喬林追蹤失蹤展櫃底輪留下的新鮮油脂。 登記員希望明確的訪談規則能減輕孩子對法庭的恐懼。 木箱內有藍布襯裡，與館方常用材質不同。

The panel's notes used three headings: object, custody, and consent. A physician's tool could be genuine and still be displayed unlawfully; a diary could be historically valuable and still require the family's permission. Viola called the distinction basic, though she had ignored it when she first served as curator. She ordered that each former owner be contacted privately before public names appeared. No witness would lose access to medical care or food support for refusing an interview, and the clerk recorded that assurance in writing.

小組筆記分成三欄：物品、保管、同意。醫療工具可以是真的，展出方式卻可能不合法；日記可以具有史料價值，公開仍需家屬許可。薇奧拉稱這是基本原則，卻承認過去任館長時未遵守。她要求公開姓名前先私下聯繫原主人，並由書記寫明：任何人拒絕訪談，都不會因此失去醫療或糧食補助。

A diary from the old orphanage listed children who had helped during the epidemic. The medical gallery's account of the plague omitted the marsh nurses who saved patients. The label incorrectly called the community's ceremonial boat a recreational vessel. The royal guidebook used the word savage for people who had supplied the first medicines. A dried shrub sample was still tied to the herbal healer's original note.

舊孤兒院日記列出疫病期間曾協助的孩子。 醫療展對瘟疫的敘述漏掉救人的沼地護理員。 標籤錯把社群祭儀船稱為休閒用船。 王室導覽冊把最早提供藥物的人稱為野蠻人。 乾燥灌木標本仍繫著草藥醫者的原始便條。

Renn offered to return the diary if Viola publicly withdrew Halda's title challenge. She refused the trade. The diary belonged to a surviving family, not to her political strategy, and the family had never permitted its private pages to be exhibited. Mara arranged for the diary to be sealed and returned through a neutral custodian. During that transfer, she noticed a wax mark from the museum's auction contractor on the wrapping. It did not prove who removed the collection, but it gave the investigators a supplier to question. The panel authorized a narrow review of that contractor's deliveries, not a raid on every warehouse in the district.

雷恩表示，只要薇奧拉公開撤回哈爾妲的所有權質疑，就歸還那本日記。她拒絕交易。日記屬於仍在世的家人，並不是她的政治籌碼；家屬也從未同意展示私密內容。瑪拉安排由中立保管人封存並歸還日記。交接時，她發現包裝上有博物館拍賣承包商的蠟印。這不足以證明誰搬走展品，卻提供可調查的供應商。小組只批准查核該承包商特定貨運，沒有任意搜索整區倉庫。

An apprentice explained that the workers' slang on the crate meant 'return after winter.' The village's detailed nursing records could surpass the palace summary in factual accuracy. For the survivors, the empty crib came to symbolize children missing from the royal account. The textile collection preserved a nurse's armband with its original stitched initials. The vaccine register contradicted the palace's claim that only royal doctors had treated patients.

學徒解釋木箱上的工人俚語意為「冬後歸還」。 村落詳盡的護理紀錄，在事實準確度上可能超越王宮摘要。 對倖存者而言，空搖籃象徵被王室敘述漏掉的孩子。 紡織藏品保存一條護士臂章，上面仍有原先縫製的縮寫。 疫苗登記冊反駁了只有王室醫師曾救治病人的說法。
