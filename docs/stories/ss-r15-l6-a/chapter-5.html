拍賣邀請函

The Auction Invitations

惡女館長，不肯把歷史賣給王室 · 第 5 / 25 章 · 5000–6000 · 英中對照 · 校訂中

用互動版查詞、朗讀與收藏

The palace sent invitations for a ceremonial banquet that would introduce prospective buyers to the disputed collection. Councilor Renn claimed the sale was the only way to repair the museum and fund public schools. Viola obtained the budget and discovered that the estimated income included objects not yet legally cleared for sale. She presented the arithmetic to the council rather than calling the buyers thieves. Several donors threatened to withdraw support, and workers feared their wages would vanish. Halda asked whether the villages would receive anything if the auction proceeded. Viola answered that the collection could not be traded before the question of ownership had been heard.

王宮寄出儀式宴會邀請函，要把爭議藏品介紹給潛在買家。雷恩宣稱拍賣是修繕博物館、資助學校的唯一辦法。薇奧拉取得預算，發現收入估算包含尚未確認具備出售資格的物品。她把數字提交議會，而不是直接稱買家為竊賊。幾名捐助者威脅撤資，工人擔心薪資落空。哈爾妲問若拍賣進行，村落能得到什麼。薇奧拉回答：所有權問題尚未釐清前，藏品不能先被交易。

A graduate from the royal academy volunteered to authenticate the old photograph under supervision. Viola demanded a documented basis for every accusation made against the quarry workers. The palace banquet invitation pictured an astrolabe that officials claimed was already missing. A farmer disputed a label that called his grandfather's fertilizer recipe a royal discovery. The fishery exhibit included a disputed net borrowed from the marsh council.

王立學院畢業生自願在監督下鑑定舊照片。 薇奧拉要求對採石場工人的每項指控都附有文件根據。 王宮宴會邀請函畫著官方聲稱早已失蹤的星盤。 農夫質疑標籤把祖父的肥料配方說成王室發現。 漁業展含有一張從沼地議會借來、所有權有爭議的網。

Jorin noticed that the invitation pictured the Tide Star with its missing central pin intact. The photograph had been taken recently because the mounting cloth was new. Yet the official inventory said the instrument was already missing when the cloth arrived. The contradiction gave Mara a narrow line of inquiry. She asked the photographer for the dated glass negative and the invoice for its development. The photographer supplied both but could not identify the person who delivered the astrolabe. Meanwhile the banquet cook disclosed that the private auction company, not the palace, was paying several ministers' travel bills. The panel preserved those invoices for a financial review.

喬林發現邀請函上的潮星儀照片，中央原本失去的插銷竟完整可見。照片背景的墊布是新換的，因此應是近期拍攝；官方清冊卻寫道，墊布送到之前儀器便已失蹤。矛盾帶來明確的查詢方向。瑪拉要求攝影師交出附日期的玻璃底片和沖洗發票。攝影師都交了，卻不認得把星盤送來的人。宴會廚師則透露，幾名部長的旅費由私人拍賣公司而非王宮支付。小組把相關發票留待財務審查。

A hairline fracture near the instrument's pin made careless transport especially dangerous. Porters tested a lifting harness before moving the heavy display frame. Visitors used headphones to hear oral histories without broadcasting private voices across the hall. The museum's donation intake form lacked a space for the original owner's consent. The label preserved a young sculptor's maiden name instead of replacing it with her husband's.

儀器插銷旁的細裂縫使粗心運送格外危險。 搬運員在移動重型展框前測試吊掛用具。 訪客戴耳機聽口述歷史，避免私密聲音傳遍展廳。 博物館捐贈受理表沒有原所有者同意欄位。 標籤保留年輕雕刻家的婚前姓氏，未以丈夫姓氏取代。

The panel required three separate accounts for daily maintenance, evidence preservation, and restitution claims. Mixing those funds would let a donor buy silence by paying a bill at the right moment. Mara also opened a confidential channel for staff who feared retaliation, with no promise that every allegation would be true. Viola read the notice twice before signing it. The hall might flourish one day, but surviving its first week without selling disputed history was already a measurable victory.

小組要求日常維護、證據保全與返還補償分成三個帳戶。若混用資金，捐助者只要在關鍵時刻替館方付帳，就能換取沉默。瑪拉也開設員工匿名申訴管道，但不保證每件指控都是真的。薇奧拉讀了兩遍才簽名。七潮館或許終有一天能再次興盛，但第一週沒有賣掉爭議歷史，已是可衡量的勝利。

An offshore buyer had offered to purchase the Tide Star before the investigation began. Prospective buyers were told that the collection had no unresolved ownership claims. The marsh council considered a local referendum before approving any future loan. Jorin wanted to refine the display supports before anyone handled the fragile frame. The workers could not rejoice over a frozen auction until their overdue wages were addressed.

一名海外買家在調查開始前便出價購買潮星儀。 潛在買家被告知這批收藏不存在未決所有權爭議。 沼地議會考慮在批准未來借展前舉辦地方公投。 喬林想先改良展架支撐，再讓任何人碰觸脆弱框架。 工人無法只因拍賣凍結而歡喜，積欠工資仍未解決。

The fifth-day hearing brought the first formal result: the tribunal froze the disputed lots and allowed the museum to pay essential staff from a separate maintenance account. It did not acquit Viola, and it did not decide ownership. Renn smiled in the gallery because his political allies still controlled the museum board. Viola met the workers afterward and told them exactly what the order could and could not guarantee. One cleaner asked why she had approved the auction brochure last winter. She answered without blaming the woman from the original novel: the signature was on her office record, and she would submit it to an independent ethics review.

第五天聽證出現首項正式結果：法庭凍結爭議拍賣品，並准許博物館從獨立維護帳戶支付必要員工薪資。這不是宣告薇奧拉無罪，也沒有裁定所有權。雷恩仍在旁聽席微笑，因為盟友依舊控制董事會。薇奧拉向工人說清命令能保障什麼、不能保障什麼。一名清潔員問她去年冬天為何批准拍賣手冊。她沒有把責任推給「原著中的那個女人」：簽名就在館長職務紀錄上，她願意接受獨立倫理審查。

Some donors began to resent the curator for revealing errors she had once ignored. The banquet hall looked serene, but anxious staff waited outside its locked doors. A minister delivered a sermon on charity while refusing to discuss unpaid museum labor. The contractor tried to shuffle disputed crates into lots marked as ordinary furniture. A harbor tenant provided the clearest record of shipments made after midnight.

部分捐贈者開始怨恨館長揭露她從前忽視的錯誤。 宴會廳看似寧靜，焦慮的員工卻等在緊鎖門外。 一名部長高談慈善，卻拒絕討論博物館欠薪。 承包商想把爭議木箱混進標示普通家具的拍賣批次。 港口租客提供最清楚的午夜後出貨紀錄。
