採石場證人

Witnesses at the Quarry

惡女館長，不肯把歷史賣給王室 · 第 7 / 25 章 · 5000–6000 · 英中對照 · 校訂中

The marked crate had passed through a stone quarry before reaching the private yard. Jorin and Halda traveled there with a tribunal observer and discovered an old workers' hostel converted into a temporary packing station. The quarry manager claimed he had never agreed to store museum objects. Yet several workers recognized the crescent mark as a symbol used when marsh boats brought food to their camp. Viola understood that a mark could connect places without proving ownership. She needed the invoices, the workers' accounts, and a reason for the detour before drawing any conclusion about the Tide Star.

標記木箱在抵達私人貨場前，曾經過一處採石場。喬林和哈爾妲在法庭觀察員陪同下前往，發現舊工人宿舍被改成臨時包裝站。採石場經理否認同意儲放博物館文物，幾名工人卻認得新月記號，那原本是沼地船送食物到營地時的標誌。薇奧拉明白，標記能連結地點，不等於證明所有權；在斷定潮星儀去向前，她需要發票、工人說法和繞路原因。

A quarry worker showed the council the acre of land taken for a royal storage shed. An elderly bachelor kept the only surviving copy of the quarry's first employment agreement. The hostel displayed boxing gloves once used by miners during their days off. A disciplinary notice punished the worker for refusing to move an unsealed crate. An emigrant returned with a letter proving that his family had never sold the instrument.

採石工人向議會指出被徵作王室倉棚的一英畝土地。 一位年長單身漢保存採石場首份僱傭協議的唯一副本。 宿舍展示礦工休假時使用的拳擊手套。 一份紀律處分通知懲罰工人拒搬未封箱子。 一名移民帶信返鄉，證明家人從未出售儀器。

One worker had been disciplined for refusing to move an unsealed chest. Another had kept notes about a landslide that blocked the direct road, giving a possible innocent explanation for the detour. The notes were dated, but they did not explain why the chest remained at the quarry for eleven days after the road reopened. Halda found a copied list of object numbers on the back of a food invoice. Two numbers belonged to the medical gallery, not the astrolabe. The problem was larger than a single stolen instrument, and the team had to resist making that realization into an exaggerated accusation.

一名工人因拒搬未封箱子而遭處分；另一人記下曾有坍方堵住直路，或許能解釋繞道，但道路重新開放後箱子仍停留十一天，紀錄無法解釋。哈爾妲在食物發票背面找到物件編號抄本，其中兩號屬於醫療展區而非潮星儀。問題比單件失竊更大，但團隊不能因此誇大指控。

Viola warned the court not to equate a worker's protest with theft. The manager's confident account began to falter when Mara produced the dated gate book. The marsh records linked seasonal navigation with the fertility of riverside fields. Halda refused to forsake the elderly workers merely to recover the Tide Star sooner. A gay couple from the quarry asked that both partners be recognized in the housing register.

薇奧拉提醒法庭，不要把工人抗議等同偷竊。 瑪拉拿出附日期的大門簿後，經理自信的說法開始動搖。 沼地紀錄把季節航行與河岸田地肥沃度連在一起。 哈爾妲拒絕為了早點找回星盤而拋棄年長工人。 採石場的一對男同志伴侶要求住房登記承認雙方身分。

A witness could be brave and still mistaken about a crate's contents. Viola had the records compared by dates, weights, and handlers instead of praise for the loudest speaker. The panel drew a distinction between the museum's disputed title to objects and the quarry's responsibility for wages. Both problems required action, but neither could be solved by trading away the other. In her notebook Viola wrote a question she had never asked in the old life of the villainess: who would keep watching once she was no longer in charge?

證人可以勇敢，仍可能看錯箱中物。薇奧拉要求用日期、重量與經手人比對，而不是獎勵聲音最大的人。小組區分文物所有權爭議和採石場欠薪責任；兩者都須解決，不能拿其中一項交換另一項。她在筆記寫下原著惡女從未問過的問題：當她不再掌權，還有誰會繼續監督？

An unnatural gloom filled the hostel after another worker received a dismissal threat. At the road intersection, two witnesses saw the same blue-marked wagon turn west. A landslide had closed the direct quarry road, giving the driver one legitimate reason to detour. The quarry families grew vegetables to nourish children during the wage dispute. An orthodox historian doubted oral testimony, but admitted the charter allowed it.

又一名工人收到解僱威脅後，宿舍瀰漫陰鬱氣氛。 兩名證人在道路交叉口都看見藍色標記貨車向西轉。 山崩曾封閉採石場直路，讓司機有合理繞道原因。 採石場家庭種蔬菜，在欠薪爭議期間滋養孩子。 一名守舊史家懷疑口述證言，卻承認章程容許採納。

The quarry workers voted unanimously to release copies of their own shift records, on condition that their wages and future jobs were discussed openly. Renn called the vote a rebellion. Viola answered that giving testimony was not an uprising, and asked the panel to protect witnesses from retaliation while allowing the company to respond. The panel ordered the employer to preserve payroll records and set a date for a labor hearing. Halda left without promising loyalty to Viola. She told the curator that trust would depend on what happened after the grand investigation ended.

採石場工人一致投票，願意提供自己的輪班紀錄，條件是公開討論工資與工作保障。雷恩把投票稱為叛亂。薇奧拉回答，提供證詞不是起義，並要求保護證人免受報復，同時保障公司答辯權。小組命令雇主保存薪資資料，另訂勞資聽證日期。哈爾妲沒有宣誓效忠薇奧拉，她說信任要看這場盛大調查結束後會發生什麼。

The worker cited the prohibition against moving unstable loads without a trained supervisor. The court granted provisional protection to witnesses until the labor hearing concluded. The workers reached a unanimous decision to submit their shift records under supervision. Renn's vanity made him display an enormous portrait above the donors' entrance. A woodpecker tapped the hostel roof while the workers discussed their missing wages.

工人引用禁止在沒有受訓主管時搬運不穩貨物的規定。 法庭給予證人臨時保護，直到勞資聽證結束。 工人一致決定在監督下提交輪班紀錄。 雷恩的虛榮使他在捐助者入口掛上巨幅肖像。 工人討論積欠薪資時，一隻啄木鳥敲著宿舍屋頂。
