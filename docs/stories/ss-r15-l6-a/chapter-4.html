A Cabinet of Salt

惡女館長，不肯把歷史賣給王室 · 第 4 / 25 章 · 5000–6000 · 英中對照 · 校訂中

The fourth morning brought a strong coastal wind through the museum's loading bay. Rainwater had reached a cabinet of navigation charts, and the building superintendent wanted the entire room sealed until repairs were complete. Jorin agreed the wood needed protection, but the charts included the oldest surviving map of the tidal crossings. A permanent closure would make the public unable to inspect the same lines that investors claimed to own. Viola proposed a monitored drying station and limited viewing of certified photographs. Halda disliked the compromise, fearing copies could be altered, so Mara arranged for two community observers to watch every image being made.

第四天早晨，海風穿過博物館卸貨口，雨水已滲入存放航海圖的木櫃。管理員希望整個房間封閉到修復完成。喬林同意必須保護木材，卻指出其中有最古老的潮汐渡口地圖；若永久封閉，民眾將無法核對投資者聲稱擁有的水路。薇奧拉建議設立受監督的乾燥站，並限量開放經核驗的照片。哈爾妲擔心影本會被修改，瑪拉便安排兩名社群觀察員全程觀看拍攝。

A cleaner warned that bleach might destroy the salt marks on the recovered chart. The board requested a comprehensive survey of the damaged coastal storage wing. The tenant's dated receipts made her account of midnight crates more credible. Jorin praised the porters' dedication without asking them to risk entering a flooded corridor. An old dresser concealed repair notes that the modern inventory had never listed.

清潔員提醒漂白劑可能毀掉尋回海圖上的鹽痕。 董事會要求全面勘查受損的沿海庫房翼廊。 房客附日期的收據使午夜木箱的證詞更可信。 喬林肯定搬運員的奉獻，卻不要求他們冒險走進淹水走廊。 一座舊五斗櫃藏著現代清冊從未列出的修復便條。

While moving the cabinet, the team found an earlier repair note pinned behind a drawer. It described salt deposits on the charts before the museum's roof had begun to leak. Someone had therefore exposed the documents to seawater elsewhere. The note named a restoration subcontractor whose company had recently been absorbed by Renn's associates. That connection justified further questions, but not a verdict. Viola resisted the urge to announce a grand conspiracy. The workers photographed the nail holes, recorded the humidity, and placed the fragile note in a neutral envelope. The original chart remained where its condition could be monitored.

搬動木櫃時，團隊在抽屜後方發現一張早年的修復註記，寫明屋頂尚未漏水之前，海圖上就已有鹽分沉積。文件因此可能曾在別處接觸海水。註記提到一家修復分包商，而該公司最近被雷恩的關係企業併購。這足以提出新問題，卻還不能定罪。薇奧拉忍住宣布「巨大陰謀」的衝動。工人拍照記錄釘孔、量測濕度，把脆弱註記裝入中立封套；原始海圖仍留在可監測的地方。

Halda's familiarity with tidal channels helped the team avoid an unsafe landing. The museum hierarchy had discouraged junior cleaners from questioning senior curators. The harbor landlady brought her rent books to verify the noisy nights. The old map showed a lush island where the current survey found bare rocks. The family asked to mourn their lost daughter without displaying her private diary.

哈爾妲熟悉潮汐水道，協助隊伍避開危險登岸處。 館內階級制度曾使基層清潔員不敢質疑高階館長。 港口女房東帶來租帳，核實有噪音的夜晚。 舊圖畫著草木繁茂的島，如今勘查卻只有裸岩。 家屬希望能悼念亡女，而不必公開展示她的私人日記。

Preservation was not the same as possession. The museum could dry a damaged chart while admitting that a coastal family might own it, and a merchant could contest a temporary hold without controlling the investigation. Mara entered each decision, the reason for it, and its expiration time on the public board. The visible limits protected the residents as much as the accused contractors. Viola remembered how the novel's old curator had preferred secret orders; she now discovered that transparent ones could still protect fragile evidence.

保全不等於所有權。博物館可以修乾海圖，同時承認沿岸家族可能擁有它；商人可以反對臨時扣留，但不能因此操控調查。瑪拉在公告板寫上每項決定、理由和期限。可看見的界線同時保護居民與受調查承包商。薇奧拉記起原著館長偏愛祕密命令，如今才發現公開程序也能守住脆弱證據。

Viola promised to nurture local conservation skills rather than claim every repair belonged to the crown. Water threatened to overflow the basin beside the navigation charts. A night guard said he did not mean to overhear the contractors arguing about shipment dates. An engineer's measurements could refute the claim that the wall had never leaked. A seagull perched on the warehouse roof while investigators photographed the pier.

薇奧拉承諾培養在地文物修復能力，不再把所有修復成果歸王室。 水幾乎要溢出航海圖旁的集水盆。 夜間衛兵表示自己並非故意偷聽承包商爭論出貨日期。 工程師的測量可以反駁牆壁從未漏水的說法。 調查員拍攝碼頭時，一隻海鷗停在倉庫屋頂。

That evening, a woman who rented rooms above the old harbor offered her testimony. She had heard large crates dragged across the pier on nights when no museum shipments were scheduled. She did not know their contents. Renn's newspaper called her an unreliable gossip, but she brought rent ledgers showing when the noise had awakened her. The panel compared those dates with the contractor's records and found two unexplained journeys. Viola thanked the woman without asking her to embellish the account. The first preservation order expired at midnight; the panel voted to renew it for two days, with a written right of appeal for the contractor.

傍晚，一名在老港口樓上出租房間的婦人前來作證。她曾在沒有館方出貨的夜晚，聽到大型木箱拖過碼頭，但不知道箱中裝什麼。雷恩的報紙罵她愛說閒話，她卻帶來房租帳冊，標出噪音吵醒她的日期。小組比對承包商紀錄，找到兩次無法解釋的航程。薇奧拉道謝，卻不要求她把故事說得更誇張。首份保全令午夜到期，小組投票延長兩日，同時給承包商書面申訴權。

The building superintendent documented the damaged window before authorizing repairs. A concert program showed that the symphony had been rehearsed near the hidden loading door. Jorin placed a thermometer beside the damp maps to monitor their condition. The sudden closure caused turmoil among workers whose wages were already overdue. The volunteers served a wholesome meal to families attending the long hearing.

建築管理員在核准修繕前先記錄受損窗戶。 音樂會節目單顯示交響樂團曾在隱藏卸貨門附近排練。 喬林在潮濕地圖旁放置溫度計監測狀態。 突然關館讓薪資已遲發的工人陷入混亂。 志工為參加漫長聽證的家庭提供營養均衡的餐點。
