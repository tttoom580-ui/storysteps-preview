空置的展座

The Empty Cradle

惡女館長，不肯把歷史賣給王室 · 第 1 / 25 章 · 5000–6000 · 英中對照 · 校訂中

用互動版查詞、朗讀與收藏

Su Tang woke behind a glass case with a silver key pressing into her palm. She recognized the Hall of Seven Tides from a novel she had edited, and recognized her new face in the reflection: Viola Aster, the royal curator condemned for stealing the Tide Star astrolabe. A crowd had gathered for the opening exhibition, but the instrument's velvet cradle was empty. In the book, guards arrested Viola before noon and burned the old inventory before her trial. She had one advantage, knowledge of that ending, and one dangerous temptation, to treat it as proof. Instead she locked the doors without trapping visitors and asked the guards to record who had entered the room.

蘇棠在玻璃展櫃後醒來，掌心壓著一把銀鑰匙。她認出眼前是自己編輯過的小說中的七潮館，也在倒影裡認出新身分：因偷走「潮星儀」而被判罪的王室館長薇奧拉・艾斯特。開幕觀眾已經聚集，天文儀器的絨布展座卻空無一物。原著裡，衛兵會在中午前逮捕她，舊清冊則在審判前被燒毀。她雖知道結局，卻不能把記憶當成證據。於是她沒有囚禁訪客，只要求衛兵記錄每個進出展廳的人。

Mara would not abbreviate the donor's name because two families shared the same initials. A small anchor in the maritime case still carried the marsh community's painted mark. The preservation rule was applicable to royal collectors and village borrowers alike. Viola found the old archive locked, although its door was listed as publicly accessible. Halda held her grandmother's woven tracing against her bosom while the guards questioned her.

瑪拉不肯縮寫捐贈者姓名，因為兩個家族有相同的姓名縮寫。 航海展櫃裡的小船錨仍留著沼地社群畫上的記號。 保全規則同時適用於王室收藏家與村落借用者。 薇奧拉發現舊檔案室上了鎖，儘管清冊寫著可供公眾進入。 衛兵質問哈爾妲時，她把祖母的編織描樣緊貼胸前。

The registrar Mara showed her a paper inventory bearing two accession seals. One belonged to the palace; the other should have been impressed by the marsh community that had once owned the astrolabe. Under a lamp, the supposed community seal had smooth edges, while the authentic seal displayed in an older drawing had a small nick shaped like a crescent. Jorin, the conservator, refused to identify the forger on appearance alone. The impressions could have been made at different times, or a worn tool could have produced the change. He asked for controlled photographs, not an accusation. Viola agreed, even when a minister demanded a name.

登記員瑪拉拿來一份蓋有兩枚入藏印章的清冊。一枚屬於王宮，另一枚理應由原藏品所屬的沼地社群蓋印。燈光下，所謂社群印章邊緣平整，舊圖裡的真印卻有一道新月形缺口。修復師喬林拒絕只憑外觀指認偽造者；印痕可能來自不同時期，也可能是工具磨損所致。他要求先拍攝可比對照片，而不是先提出指控。即使部長催促她說出嫌疑人，薇奧拉仍同意。

The confiscated belongings included a bra sewn with the missing worker's name. An auction agent offered a bribe for the nighttime loading schedule, but the porter reported him. Kitchen volunteers began to broil river fish for witnesses who had traveled overnight. Jorin prepared a comparative drawing of the authentic seal and its suspicious replacement. Viola's eyelid twitched when she recognized her signature on the auction permit.

扣押私人物品裡有一件胸罩，縫著失蹤女工的名字。 一名拍賣代理人為取得夜間裝貨表提出賄賂，搬運員卻舉報了他。 廚房志工開始炙烤河魚，供連夜趕來的證人食用。 喬林把真印章和可疑替件畫成比較圖。 薇奧拉認出拍賣許可上的簽名時，眼皮微微抽動。

Under the hall's charter, an object entered the royal collection only after both the donor and the original owners approved the entry; a correction had to remain visible in the register. The charter also allowed a temporary hold when evidence might disappear, but never a permanent seizure without review. Viola repeated these limits to everyone present. An official who feared inconvenient facts might prefer absolute power, yet that was exactly why the rules needed witnesses, dates, and an appeal. She wrote down what was observed, what was inferred, and what was still unknown in separate columns.

按照七潮館章程，文物必須獲捐贈方與原所有者共同同意才能入藏，任何更正都須保留在登記冊中。若證據可能消失，可以暫時保全，但不能未經複查便永久扣押。薇奧拉向在場者重申界線。害怕事實的官員或許更喜歡絕對權力，這正是規則必須有證人、日期與申訴途徑的原因。她把觀察所得、合理推論和未明事項分成三欄。

The number of surviving original catalogues was finite, so Mara numbered every handling. Halda hoped local conservation workshops could flourish without royal control over their designs. A hired gangster threatened the glassworker who had repaired the Tide Star's case. The cart disappeared after the junction where the museum road met the quay. Despite his limp, the old porter insisted on identifying the crate he had carried.

存世的原始目錄數量有限，因此瑪拉每次經手都編號。 哈爾妲希望在地保存工坊不受王室掌控設計，仍能蓬勃發展。 受雇的黑幫分子威脅修過潮星儀展櫃的玻璃工。 貨車在博物館道路與碼頭交會的路口之後消失。 老搬運員雖跛行，仍堅持辨認自己搬過的木箱。

The minister offered to end the crisis if Viola signed a statement admitting careless custody. She found a second page underneath, already prepared to authorize a private auction of the remaining collection. If she signed, the missing object would become her personal crime while every disputed object could be sold. She refused, although the refusal left her under suspicion. The tribunal clerk granted a one-day preservation order only after three named witnesses signed it. At sunset the exhibit remained closed, the star remained missing, and the old accession records were still safe. For the first time, the plot in her memory had failed to dictate the next morning.

部長表示，只要薇奧拉簽署疏於保管的認罪聲明，就能平息風波。她卻發現聲明底下還壓著另一頁，已預先寫好其餘爭議藏品的私人拍賣許可。若她簽名，失竊只會算作個人罪行，其他藏品就能被賣掉。她拒絕了，即使這讓自己持續受到懷疑。三名具名證人簽署後，法庭書記才核發一天的證據保全令。日落時展廳仍關閉，潮星儀仍失蹤，但舊入藏紀錄保住了。原著中的命運第一次沒有照劇本推進。

The judge asked Halda to narrate what her elders had actually taught her. An orderly guided anxious witnesses into separate rooms before the first hearing. A bank teller confirmed which account had received the auction company's advance payment. The suspect transfer certificate was printed on virgin paper, unlike the stained original register. The court issued a narrow warrant to preserve records, not to arrest every museum employee.

法官請哈爾妲敘述長者真正教過她的內容。 勤務員在首場聽證前引導緊張的證人進入不同房間。 銀行出納確認哪個帳戶收到拍賣公司的預付款。 可疑的轉讓證明印在全新紙上，不同於有污漬的原始登記冊。 法庭核發範圍有限的調閱令，只保全紀錄，不拘捕所有館員。
