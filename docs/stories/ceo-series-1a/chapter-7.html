有兩座時鐘的房間

The Room with Two Clocks

The next day, Nora went back to Silver House as the sky turned gray. She had slept badly. In her bedroom, she had dreamed of a room full of clocks, and every clock showed a different time. At work she found two real clocks above the factory schedule board. One was ten minutes slow. The error made the morning line look shorter than it was. Nora asked May to check the old papers. As they stood close to the clocks, they could hear the low noise of the machines behind a wall. “That little change can make a big difference at the end of a day,” May said. Nora wrote the wrong time in her book but did not try to change the old page.

隔天，天空轉灰，諾拉回到銀屋公司。她沒睡好，夢見臥室裡有一排時鐘，每座都顯示不同時間。到了工廠，她真的看到排班板上掛著兩座鐘，其中一座慢了十分鐘。這個錯誤讓早班看起來比實際短。諾拉請梅一起核對舊紀錄。她們站在鐘旁，聽見牆後機器的低鳴。「每天差這麼一點，最後就差很多。」梅說。諾拉把錯誤時間記在本子裡，卻沒有直接改動舊表。

Adrian came to see the problem. Victor said the workers were making noise because they wanted more money. Nora asked whether he believed that a person could work without being paid for the full time. Victor gave a short laugh. “You make everything into a big story.” Adrian looked from one clock to the other. “I want the truth, not the loudest voice,” he said. He asked an outside checker to look at the time sheets and the clocks. Nora felt proud of the workers for speaking. She also knew that being proud was not enough. The people needed a clear result and a date when they would hear it.

艾德里安來看這個問題。維克多說工人只是為了多拿錢才吵鬧。諾拉問他，難道一個人工作了，不該得到全部時間的報酬嗎？維克多短笑一聲：「妳什麼都能講成大故事。」艾德里安在兩座鐘間看了看。「我要真相，不要最大聲的意見。」他請外部查核員檢查工時表和時鐘。諾拉為願意說話的工人感到驕傲，也知道光是驕傲不夠；大家需要明確的結果，以及知道何時能得到結果。

At lunch, the workers ate in a small yard. Some talked about nature outside the city. One had seen a rainbow after the last rain. Another spoke of a bright bird in the sky. Nora listened. She was not rich, and she did not have a large house, but she had people who cared for her. Adrian arrived with a lunch bag, which made everyone stop for a second. He asked May if he could sit there. May said yes. He did not tell anyone a long speech about being kind. He ate with them and listened to a worker explain how a missing break changed the day. Nora saw his hand stop moving when he understood.

午餐時，工人坐在小院子裡吃飯。有人聊城外的自然景色，有人說上次下雨後看見彩虹，也有人談天空裡色彩鮮明的鳥。諾拉安靜聽著。她不富有，沒有大房子，卻有關心自己的人。艾德里安拿著午餐袋走來，大家一時都停了動作。他問梅能不能坐這裡，梅說可以。他沒有長篇演說自己多善良，而是一起吃飯，聽工人解釋少算休息時間怎麼影響一天的工作。諾拉看到他理解時，手上的動作也停了。

Later Adrian asked Nora whether she wanted to go to dinner with his family after the public event. Nora thought for a moment. “No,” she said. “One dinner is enough for our agreement. I do not want your family to decide what we are.” Adrian accepted the answer. He looked sad, and Nora felt a little sad too, but she did not change her mind only to make his face bright again. “We can talk as two people after the work is done,” she said. “Not because anyone paid for a seat.” Adrian nodded. “Then I will ask again another day, with no company paper between us.” Nora smiled. She liked that idea more than any rich table.

晚些時候，艾德里安問諾拉，在公開晚宴之外是否還願意和他家人吃飯。諾拉想了一下。「不，」她說，「我們的約定只有一場。我不想讓你家人決定我們是什麼關係。」艾德里安接受了。雖然他看起來有點難過，她也有點難過，但她不會只為讓他開心就改變決定。「等工作結束，我們可以當成兩個人聊聊，」她說，「不是因為任何人付了座位的錢。」艾德里安點頭：「那我改天再問一次，中間不放公司文件。」諾拉微笑。這個想法比任何豪華餐桌更吸引她。

May later put one clock on the table and left the other on the wall. She did not want to break either one; she wanted the workers to see what had gone wrong. A young worker asked whether the new clock would make the old pay right. Nora explained that the clock was only one part of the story. They still needed the records and the people who knew the work. Adrian heard her from the door. He did not step in to make a long speech. He let the worker ask another question. Nora was proud of that small moment. The room had made space for a question, and no one laughed at the person who asked it.

後來梅把一座時鐘放在桌上，另一座留在牆上。她不是要打破它們，而是想讓工人看懂哪裡出了錯。新工人問換鐘是否就能補回舊工資。諾拉解釋，時鐘只是故事的一部分，還需要紀錄與懂工作的人。艾德里安站在門邊聽，沒有進來長篇演講，而是讓工人繼續問。諾拉為這個小時刻感到驕傲：房間留出了提問空間，沒有人嘲笑提問者。

Before going home, Nora stood by a window and watched the city turn gold under the late sun. She thought about the two clocks. People had used one clock as if it were right, just because it stood high on a wall. Adrian had been treated in the same way because he was the boss. Perhaps it was time for everyone to check the time for themselves. She drew two clocks on a page and wrote, “A clear answer needs more than one eye.” Then she added the real times May had shown her. She would bring the page to the outside checker in the morning. She wanted a result built from facts, not from any one person's dream.

回家前，諾拉站在窗邊，看夕陽把城市染成金色。她想到那兩座鐘。人們把掛得高的鐘當作正確，只因它在高處；艾德里安也因為是老闆而被人這樣看待。也許大家該自己核對時間了。她在紙上畫兩座鐘，寫下：「清楚的答案，需要不只一雙眼睛。」接著把梅提供的真實時間填上。明早她會把紙交給外部查核員。她想要的是由事實支持的結果，不是某一個人的夢想。
