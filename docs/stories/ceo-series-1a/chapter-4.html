漂亮衣服的代價

The Price of a Pretty Dress

冷面總裁，請先問我 · 第 4 / 25 章 · 1000以下 · 英中對照 · 校訂中

A strong wind began to blow above the factory. Gray clouds covered the sky, and a little boy ran after a yellow butterfly near the gate. Nora asked him to stay on the safe path. Inside, a tall worker named Ben brought a cheap dress from the last show. The color was pretty, but one side had already come apart. Ben showed her how the wrong thread could drop loose under a small pull. “This was meant to be a fine dress,” he said. “It is not healthy for a worker to rush through every line.” Nora put the dress on a square table and took photos of the weak places. She wanted to know who had made the choices, not who could be blamed most easily.

強風吹過工廠上方，灰雲遮住天空。一個小男孩在門邊追著黃色蝴蝶，諾拉請他留在安全的小路上。屋內，身材高大的工人班拿出上次服裝秀的一件便宜洋裝。顏色漂亮，側邊卻已經鬆開。班示範用錯線時，輕輕一拉就會脫線。「它原本應該是好衣服，」他說，「逼工人每一步都趕時間，也不健康。」諾拉把洋裝放到方桌上，拍下薄弱處。她想查清楚誰做了決策，而不是急著找最容易責怪的人。

The factory service book showed that several machines needed work. Victor had marked all of them as ready, yet two had been turned off for weeks. Nora asked why. May said she had sent a story about the problem to the main office, but no one had written back. Adrian listened without speaking. Then he asked his own office to find the old messages. “Sir,” Ben said, “the workers have asked before.” Adrian answered, “Then I should have heard sooner.” He did not promise to fix a machine with a smile or a strong voice. He promised a clear date for a safety check and time for the workers to give their account.

工廠的維修紀錄顯示好幾台機器需要處理。維克多卻把它們都標成可用，儘管其中兩台已停了數週。諾拉問原因。梅說她曾把問題寫給總公司，卻沒收到回覆。艾德里安安靜聽完，請自己的辦公室找出舊訊息。「先生，」班說，「我們以前就反映過。」艾德里安回答：「那我應該更早知道。」他沒有用笑容或強硬的口氣保證立刻修好機器，而是承諾明確的安全檢查日期，讓工人有時間說明經過。

At lunch, Ben ate a tomato with bread. Nora had a little soup without meat. From the window they could see the town square and the cars on the road. The workers talked about their children, their homes, and the things they liked to do when the day was over. One woman said she made paper flowers, and another liked to tell funny stories. Nora realized that people did not become only workers when they walked into a factory. They were whole people. She wrote this thought in her book, but she did not use their private words in the public report. She wanted to show the real cost of a dress without taking the workers' lives for free.

午餐時，班吃著番茄配麵包，諾拉喝了一點沒有肉的湯。窗外能看到鎮上的廣場和來往汽車。工人聊孩子、住家，以及下班後喜歡做的事。一名女子會做紙花，另一個人愛說笑話。諾拉突然明白，走進工廠的人並不只有「工人」這個身分；他們也有完整的生活。她把這個想法記在本子裡，卻沒有把私下談話放進公開報告。她想呈現洋裝的真實成本，而不免費取用他人的人生。

Adrian took Nora aside after the meeting. “You told me things I did not want to hear,” he said. “But you were right to say them.” Nora looked at the empty hall. “I did not come to fight you. I came to make good clothes.” Adrian gave a short nod. “Then we both have more work.” She almost smiled. He was a tall man, and people moved quickly when he spoke, but she no longer thought that a big office made every word right. A worker with a small voice could hold an important truth. That thought stayed with her as rain began to fall outside the window.

會後艾德里安請諾拉到一旁談話。「妳告訴我很多我不想聽的事，」他說，「但妳說得對。」諾拉望向空蕩走廊。「我不是來和你吵架的。我是來做好衣服的。」艾德里安簡短點頭。「那我們都有更多事要做。」她差點笑出來。他很高，說話時大家都動作很快，但她不再認為大辦公室裡說的每句話都必然正確。聲音很小的工人，也可能握著重要真相。窗外開始下雨時，她還在想這件事。

When the rain stopped, Ben asked the team to look at one loose button again. He held the cloth in both hands and gave it a small shake. The button moved. Nora watched carefully. It was not enough for the dress to look good in a picture; it had to stay together when someone wore it. Adrian asked how long a good repair would take. Ben gave a clear number. No one told him to make the answer smaller. Victor turned away, but the other workers began to ask their own questions. Nora felt the room change. The point was no longer to make someone look powerful. It was to make a dress that would last.

雨停後，班請大家再看一顆鬆動鈕扣。他雙手握布，輕輕搖了一下，鈕扣就動了。諾拉仔細看。衣服不能只在照片裡好看，穿在人身上也要牢固。艾德里安問做好修理要多久，班給出明確數字，沒人叫他把時間說短。維克多轉開臉，其他工人卻開始自己提問。諾拉感覺房間氣氛變了。重點不再是誰看起來最有權力，而是把衣服做得耐穿。

Back at Silver House, Adrian saw Victor laughing with two visitors. They were talking about the video again. He asked them to stop and said that company work was not a place to make guesses about a woman. Victor called him too serious. Adrian did not answer that. Instead he wrote a short note to the team: no worker or visitor could be filmed at work without permission. The rule would apply to him as well. Nora read the note on her phone before going home. She was pleased, but not ready to trust a promise just because it looked fine on paper. She would watch what the company did next.

回到銀屋公司，艾德里安看見維克多和兩名訪客笑著聊天。他們又在談那段影片。艾德里安叫他們停下，說公司工作不是任人猜測女性私事的場所。維克多說他太嚴肅，艾德里安沒有接話，只寫了一份簡短通知：任何人不得在工作場所未經同意拍攝員工或訪客，這條規定也適用於他自己。諾拉回家前在手機上讀到通知。她很欣慰，卻不會只因承諾寫在紙上就完全相信；她還要看公司接下來怎麼做。
