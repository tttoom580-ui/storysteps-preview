正中央的她的名字

Her Name in the Center

冷面總裁，請先問我 · 第 10 / 25 章 · 1000以下 · 英中對照 · 校訂中

用互動版查詞、朗讀與收藏

The next week Nora saw an online picture of her blue dress with a different name under it. The name was Victor's new brand. She felt as if someone had hit her with a stone. She went to the center of the Silver House office and asked to speak with Adrian. She did not shout. She put the picture on his desk with the first pencil drawing she had made at home. “My name is not here,” she said. “This is not right.” Adrian looked at both pages. He knew the public picture was wrong, but he did not ask her to wait until the next show. He called the team that had put the page online and told them to stop using it until the true record was checked.

隔週，諾拉在網路上看到自己的藍洋裝，照片下方卻寫著另一個名字：維克多的新品牌。她覺得像被石頭打中。她來到銀屋公司辦公室中央，要求與艾德里安談話。她沒有大吼，只把照片和自己在家畫的第一張鉛筆草圖放到他桌上。「這裡沒有我的名字，」她說，「這不對。」艾德里安看了兩張紙。他知道公開照片有問題，沒有叫她等到下次秀。他立刻聯絡發布照片的團隊，請他們在核對真實紀錄前停止使用。

Nora's brother Tom, a school teacher, came to help her sort the old files. He was good at making hard things simple. “Put the dates in one line,” he said. “The first drawing was made in your room. The second was sent to Silver House. The final dress was made with May.” Tom drew a net of lines between the pictures and the people. Nora added the email times and a short note from the woman who had worn the dress. She wanted to spend enough time on the record so it would be strong. She was still angry, but she did not want anger to make her careless. This was her work, and she would speak for it in her own voice.

諾拉的哥哥湯姆是一名學校老師，他來幫忙整理舊檔案。他擅長把難事說簡單。「把日期排成一條線，」他說，「第一張草圖在妳房間完成，第二張寄給銀屋，最後成衣由梅協助製作。」湯姆在圖片與人物間畫出連線網。諾拉加入郵件時間，以及穿過洋裝的女士提供的短說明。她願意多花時間把紀錄整理紮實。她仍很生氣，卻不想因怒氣而粗心。這是她的作品，她要用自己的聲音為它說話。

Adrian met with Victor between two long meetings. Victor said a rich company had a right to use any idea that came into its building. Adrian answered that a company only had the rights written in a fair agreement. “She owns her first drawings,” he said. “Our deal says so.” Victor asked whether Adrian was doing this for love. Adrian did not give him a story to use. “I am doing it because we have a rule,” he said. “It is a rule for every person who works here.” He asked an outside person to check the records, not because he doubted Nora, but because the same simple check should be used for everyone.

艾德里安在兩場會議間見維克多。維克多說，大公司有權使用進到公司裡的任何點子。艾德里安回答，公司只擁有公平合約裡明確寫出的權利。「最初的草圖屬於她，」他說，「條款寫得很清楚。」維克多問他是不是為了愛情才這樣做。艾德里安不給他能拿去說嘴的話。「我是因為規則才做，」他說，「這條規則適用於每個在這裡工作的人。」他請外部人士核對紀錄，不是懷疑諾拉，而是因為每個人都應接受同樣簡單的檢查。

At the end of the day, Nora and Tom met Adrian in a quiet room. The outside checker had found the first date on Nora's drawing, the date of the factory work, and the later date on Victor's page. The order was clear. The team made a public correction and put Nora's name in the center of the picture, with May and the other makers named in the notes. Nora looked at the new page for a long time. She had once wanted a rich boss to choose her work. Now she understood that the first choice had been hers: to make the work, to keep the record, and to ask for a fair name.

一天結束時，諾拉和湯姆在安靜房間與艾德里安見面。外部查核員找到諾拉草圖的最早日期、工廠製作日期，與維克多頁面較晚的日期。先後次序很清楚。團隊公開更正，把諾拉的名字放在圖片中央，也在說明欄列出梅和其他製作者。諾拉看著新頁面好久。她以前希望富有老闆選中她的作品，現在知道最初的選擇一直屬於自己：做出作品、保留紀錄，並要求公平署名。

Before the public correction, Tom and Nora met a blind customer who had asked for a clear label inside a coat. Nora gave her an apple and a cup of water while they talked. The woman said half the words on some labels were too small to read with her tools. Nora listened and wrote down another way to give the information. A boy playing outside kicked a ball near the door, and an ice drink fell from a table. Tom helped clean it up. “There is more than one way to make a place easy to use,” he said. Nora agreed. The same was true for clear names and honest work papers.

公開更正前，湯姆和諾拉見了一位視障顧客，她希望外套裡的標示能更容易讀懂。談話時，諾拉給她蘋果和水。女士說有些標籤一半的字都太小，輔具不容易讀。諾拉聆聽並記下另一種提供資訊的方式。外頭玩球的男孩踢到一顆球，讓桌上的冰飲翻倒，湯姆幫忙清理。「讓地方好使用，不只有一種辦法。」他說。諾拉同意，名字清楚與工作紀錄誠實，也一樣。

When they left the room, Adrian asked Nora if she wanted dinner. She said she had already promised to eat with her brother. “You can ask me another day,” she said. Adrian smiled. “I will.” Tom waited by the door and watched them with a quiet face. After Adrian went away, Tom asked whether Nora loved him. Nora looked at her brother with surprise. “I do not know yet,” she said. “I like talking to him. I do not always like what his company does. Both things can be true.” Tom nodded. As they walked home, Nora felt lighter. She could care for a man and still stand up for herself. She would not give up one for the other.

離開房間時，艾德里安問諾拉想不想一起吃晚餐。諾拉說已答應和哥哥吃飯。「你可以改天再問。」她說。艾德里安微笑：「我會的。」湯姆在門邊安靜看著兩人。艾德里安走後，湯姆問她是不是愛上了他。諾拉驚訝地看哥哥。「我還不知道，」她說，「我喜歡和他說話，但不一定喜歡他的公司做的每一件事。兩件事都可能是真的。」湯姆點頭。回家路上，諾拉覺得輕鬆些。她能在乎一個男人，同時仍為自己站出來，不必二選一。
