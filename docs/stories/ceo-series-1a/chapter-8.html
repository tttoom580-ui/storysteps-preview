公園裡的藍洋裝

The Blue Dress in the Park

冷面總裁，請先問我 · 第 8 / 25 章 · 1000以下 · 英中對照 · 校訂中

用互動版查詞、朗讀與收藏

On Saturday, Silver House held a small show in a city park. A group of children played baseball behind the stage, and a man sold warm beef rolls near the gate. Nora carried a blue dress in a box. She had made it for a woman who wanted something easy to wear while she walked. The dress was different from the heavy gowns Victor liked. It was nice, light, and had pockets. Adrian waited outside the tent with an officer who checked the path for guests. The officer said people must leave a clear way through the park. Nora agreed. No beautiful dress was worth making a path hard to use. The path would remain open during the event.

星期六，銀屋公司在市立公園辦小型展示。舞台後有孩子打棒球，門邊有人賣熱牛肉捲。諾拉帶著盒裝藍洋裝來到現場。那件衣服是為一位想要穿著舒服、方便走路的女士做的。它和維克多喜歡的厚重禮服很不一樣，輕巧、好看，還有口袋。艾德里安和檢查通道的管理員等在帳篷外。管理員說公園必須留出通道，諾拉同意。再美的衣服也不值得讓路變得難走；活動期間通道會保持暢通。

A young girl stopped near the dress. “It looks like something a bird could fly in,” she said. Nora thanked her. The girl pointed behind the cloth. “Why is there a little ghost?” Nora laughed. It was only a white dress shape on the paper behind the blue one. “That is the first drawing,” she explained. “Every dress starts with a picture that may not be right.” The girl wanted to know why Nora had changed it. Nora showed her how the first shape made walking difficult. She liked talking about the work, not only hearing that a dress was pretty. Adrian watched from a short way away, and for once he did not try to guide the talk.

一名小女孩停在洋裝旁。「看起來像小鳥能穿著飛。」她說。諾拉向她道謝。女孩指著布後面：「為什麼那裡有隻小鬼？」諾拉笑了，那其實只是藍洋裝後面紙上畫的白色衣形。「那是第一張草圖，」她說，「每件衣服一開始都可能畫得不對。」女孩問她為什麼修改。諾拉示範第一個版型如何讓人走路不方便。她喜歡談設計過程，而不只是聽讚美。艾德里安站在不遠處看著，難得沒有試圖主導談話。

Victor came with two guests and asked why Nora had not put on a more expensive dress. “A boss needs someone who looks fine beside him,” he said. Nora felt a small pain in her heart, but she kept her voice calm. “I am here as a designer,” she answered. “My work is on the stage. That is what I want people to taste with their eyes.” One guest smiled at the strange words. Adrian stepped beside Nora. “She chose the dress for this show, and I chose to support her work,” he said. He did not call her his girl or his special friend. Nora was glad. The words left her free to speak for herself.

維克多帶著兩名客人走來，問諾拉為何不穿更貴的洋裝。「老闆身邊的人總要看起來體面。」他說。諾拉心裡微微刺痛，仍平靜回答：「我是以設計師身分來的。我的作品就在舞台上，那才是我希望大家親眼品味的。」一位客人對這個說法笑了。艾德里安站到諾拉身旁：「這次展示的衣服由她選擇，而我選擇支持她的作品。」他沒有稱她是自己的女孩或特別朋友。諾拉很高興，這些話保留了她為自己說話的自由。

When the small show began, a woman walked across the stage in Nora's dress. She moved her arms, turned, and put a hand in each pocket. People clapped. A child called out that her mother needed a pocket too. Nora gave the woman a small card with her design name, so people could find her later. Adrian did not take the card from her hand or put the Silver House name over it. He gave her space. After the show he asked what she thought of the work. “The pockets were right,” she said, “but the side is still too long. I can make it better.” Adrian looked at her and said, “Then make it better.”

小秀開始後，一名女士穿著諾拉的洋裝走過舞台。她抬手、轉身，把雙手放進口袋。觀眾鼓掌，一個孩子喊媽媽也需要口袋。諾拉給模特兒一張印著自己設計名字的小卡，讓有興趣的人能找到她。艾德里安沒有抽走卡片，也沒有把銀屋公司的名字蓋在上面。他給了她空間。秀後他問她覺得如何。「口袋做對了，」她說，「但側邊還是太長，我可以改得更好。」艾德里安看著她說：「那就改得更好。」

Behind the stage, a small table began to shake when someone moved a box of clothes. Nora asked the team to stop for a break. The officer checked the table and found that one leg was not even. It was an easy problem to fix, but it mattered. A dress could look fine while the table beneath it was not safe. The team moved the box to a strong place. Nora thanked the officer. Adrian watched her choose a short delay over a quick picture. He had learned to value a calm answer, even when many eyes were on him. The show began only after the stage team said the path was clear.

後台有人搬衣箱時，小桌子突然搖晃。諾拉請團隊先暫停休息。管理員檢查後發現一支桌腳不平，問題不難修，卻很重要。洋裝可能看起來漂亮，底下的桌子卻不安全。團隊把箱子移到穩固的地方。諾拉向管理員道謝。艾德里安看她選擇短暫延遲而不是急著拍照，也開始懂得在人群注視下仍選平穩的答案。直到舞台團隊確認通道暢通，活動才開始。

They walked through the park after the crowd left. Nora bought two cups of water and offered him one. Adrian asked if she had always wanted to make dresses. “Not always,” she said. “When I was young, I wanted to be a teacher. Then I learned that clothes could help people feel at home in their own bodies.” He told her that he had once wanted to be a baseball player, but he could never hit the ball well. Nora laughed so hard she had to stop walking. Adrian laughed too. For a few minutes, there was no boss and no designer being watched by strangers. There were only two adults telling small stories in a quiet park.

人群離去後，他們走在公園裡。諾拉買兩杯水，分一杯給他。艾德里安問她是不是從小就想做衣服。「不是，」她說，「小時候想當老師，後來才發現衣服能讓人自在地生活。」他說自己以前想當棒球選手，卻怎樣都打不好球。諾拉笑到停下腳步，艾德里安也笑了。短短幾分鐘，沒有被人圍觀的總裁或設計師，只有兩個成年人在安靜公園裡分享小故事。
