藍車與紅色檔案

The Blue Car and the Red File

冷面總裁，請先問我 · 第 11 / 25 章 · 1000以下 · 英中對照 · 校訂中

用互動版查詞、朗讀與收藏

It was a cold morning when Adrian's secretary, Mira, called Nora. A blue car had taken May to a clinic after she hurt her hand at home. The nurse said May would be fine, but she needed to rest. Nora wanted to visit, so she called first. May agreed. Nora brought warm soup that she had helped cook, along with a small book of easy words for the new workers. The book was a dictionary with pictures. May smiled when she saw it. “You always come with paper,” she said. Nora laughed. “Paper can be useful. But I came to see you, not just the work.” They sat together and talked about the weather outside the window.

一個寒冷早晨，艾德里安的祕書蜜拉打電話給諾拉。一輛藍車把在家弄傷手的梅送到診所。護理師說她會康復，但需要休息。諾拉想去探望，先打電話徵得梅的同意。她帶了自己幫忙煮的熱湯和一本給新工人看的簡單圖文辭典。梅一看到就笑了：「妳每次來都帶紙。」諾拉笑道：「紙很有用，但我今天是來看妳，不只是看工作。」她們坐在一起，談窗外的天氣。

At Silver House, Mira opened a red file that had been missing from the usual list. The file held copies of messages sent in the past month. Several were from May. Some were about broken machines, and some were about pay. Mira showed Adrian that she had sent them to Victor's office, as the old rule required. “Maybe he did not read them,” she said, “but they reached that office.” Adrian looked at the dates and asked for the full record. He did not want to be a boss who could say, “I did not know,” forever. The company would have to be able to answer for what happened under its own roof.

回到銀屋公司，蜜拉打開一份原本不在清單中的紅色檔案。裡面是上個月寄出的訊息副本，好幾封來自梅，有的談機器故障，有的談薪資。蜜拉給艾德里安看，自己依舊規定轉送到了維克多辦公室。「也許他沒讀，」她說，「但文件確實送到了。」艾德里安查看日期，要求完整紀錄。他不想永遠當一個只會說「我不知道」的老闆。公司必須能對自己屋簷下發生的事負責。

Victor said the red file was old business. He spoke of a temple his family had helped build and a tree they had planted many years ago. He thought good things in the past could make up for trouble in the present. Adrian did not agree. “We can be thankful for the past,” he said, “and still change a bad rule now.” He asked Mira to write one clear sentence about each step of the new plan. A worker would be able to send a question directly to the outside checker, not only through a family office. Adrian knew it would probably make some people unhappy. That was not a reason to leave the old problem alone.

維克多說紅檔案只是舊事。他談到家族曾資助建造一座寺廟，還在多年前種了一棵樹，彷彿過去的善行就能抵掉現在的問題。艾德里安不同意。「我們可以感謝過去，」他說，「同時仍要改掉現在不好的規則。」他請蜜拉把新流程每一步寫成明確句子。工人可以直接向外部查核員提問，不必只經過家族辦公室。他知道這可能讓一些人不高興，但那不是放任老問題的理由。

That afternoon, Nora returned to the design room in plain pants and a warm coat. A small fan moved air around the table because the new cloth had to stay dry. She looked at the blue dress and thought about May's hand. Some people loved a dress because of its color. Nora loved it because she knew the hands that made it. She made a small change to the arm so a person could move easily. She took a photo and sent it to May, asking whether she wanted to give an opinion later. May replied with one word: “Yes.” Nora put the phone down. She wanted May to heal before doing any more work.

下午，諾拉穿著簡單長褲和暖外套回到設計室。因為新布料要保持乾燥，桌旁的小風扇正吹動空氣。她看著藍洋裝，想起梅受傷的手。有些人愛洋裝是因為顏色，諾拉愛它，是因為她認識製作它的雙手。她稍微修改袖口，讓穿的人更容易活動。她拍照傳給梅，問梅以後願不願意給意見。梅只回一個字：「願意。」諾拉放下手機。她希望梅先好好康復，再做其他工作。

Mira told Nora that the company made more than a thousand dresses in a busy year. Nora was surprised. She had always counted her work one dress at a time. “That is why each small error matters,” Mira said. “A little mistake can happen many times.” Nora understood. She asked Mira to put the new work rules where people could find them, not only in a book the boss kept. Mira agreed to make a short page for the workers and a longer page for the office. The same facts would be clear in both. Nora liked that. A big number did not make people less important. It made the need for a fair rule even greater.

蜜拉告訴諾拉，繁忙的一年裡公司會製作超過一千件洋裝。諾拉很驚訝，她向來一次只數一件。「所以每個小錯誤都重要，」蜜拉說，「一點錯可能重複很多次。」諾拉明白了。她請蜜拉把新規則放在人人找得到的地方，而不是只收在老闆的書裡。蜜拉答應做一份給工人的簡表、一份給辦公室的詳表，兩份事實一致。諾拉很喜歡。數量大不代表人比較不重要，而是更需要公平規則。

At the end of the day, Adrian walked Nora to the elevator. He asked whether he could visit May too. Nora said, “Ask May. She is the one who decides who comes.” Adrian nodded and took out his phone. The cold boss was learning that care was not the same as taking control. When the elevator doors opened, Nora saw her own face in the glass. She was tired, but she felt sure of her work. The red file had made the truth easier to see, and it had also made the next steps harder. Still, they had a plan. A good plan could begin with a single honest sentence, and a person could begin again with a simple choice.

一天結束時，艾德里安陪諾拉走到電梯。他問自己能不能也去看梅。諾拉說：「問梅呀，誰能來由她決定。」艾德里安點頭拿出手機。這位冷面總裁正在學習，關心不等於替別人掌控一切。電梯門開時，諾拉在玻璃裡看見自己的臉。她很累，卻對作品有信心。紅色檔案讓真相更清楚，也讓下一步變得更難，但他們有計畫。好計畫可以從一句誠實的話開始，人也能因一個簡單的選擇而重新出發。
