The Card at the Festival

冷面總裁，請先問我 · 第 9 / 25 章 · 1000以下 · 英中對照 · 校訂中

用互動版查詞、朗讀與收藏

Silver House held a food festival beside the lake to thank the people who made its clothes. Nora came with her grandmother, who had taught her to sew as a child. They walked down a busy street where families bought rice dishes, banana cakes, and warm tea. Nora felt joy when she saw May and Ben at a table with their names on the small signs. They were not hidden behind the company name. Adrian stood near the entrance, greeting each person who came to visit. He looked quite serious in his dark coat, but he had put the guest cards in clear boxes so no one would lose them. Nora's grandmother said, “That man seems ready to listen. Let us see if he keeps doing it.”

銀屋公司在湖邊辦美食節，感謝製作衣服的人們。諾拉帶著教她縫紉的外婆一起來。她們走過忙碌街道，看家庭買飯食、香蕉蛋糕和熱茶。看到梅和班坐在標有自己名字的桌邊，而不是被公司名稱遮住，諾拉很開心。艾德里安站在入口迎接訪客。他穿深色外套，看起來仍很嚴肅，但把來賓卡片分放透明盒，免得弄丟。外婆說：「那男人好像願意聽人說話。再看看他能不能一直做到。」

At lunch, Nora's grandmother took out a small card with an old sewing tip. It said to count the cloth twice before making a cut. “I have used this for most of my life,” she told Adrian. He thanked her and asked if he could keep a copy. She said yes but kept the old card herself. The festival staff wanted to put her face on a large sign, and she said no. Nora saw Adrian tell them to respect that answer. No one shouted at the old woman or tried to change her mind. This was a simple thing, but Nora knew how hard it could be when a company cared more about a happy picture than a person's real wish.

午餐時，外婆拿出一張舊縫紉小卡，上面寫著「裁剪前先量兩次」。她告訴艾德里安，自己大半輩子都靠這個小提醒。艾德里安道謝，問能否留一份副本。外婆答應，但自己保留原卡。節慶工作人員想把外婆的臉放上大招牌，她說不願意。諾拉看到艾德里安請工作人員尊重她的回答。沒人向老人喊叫，也沒人試圖改變她的意思。這件事看似簡單，諾拉卻知道，當公司更在乎開心照片而非真實意願時，做到有多難。

Victor walked by with a dish of rice and asked Nora why she had brought her grandmother to a business event. “Are you trying to make him feel sorry for your family?” he said. Nora answered, “No. She taught me my work. She belongs here.” Adrian heard the words and came over. Victor tried to turn the talk into a joke, but Nora did not let him. She said that she would not stay at any place where guests were treated as less important because they had less money. Her grandmother touched her arm. “I am okay,” she said softly. “I have seen men like him before. We can go if we want.” Nora stayed only because her grandmother chose to stay.

維克多端著飯走過來，問諾拉為什麼帶外婆參加商業活動。「想讓他可憐妳家嗎？」他說。諾拉回答：「不是。她教我這份手藝，她當然有資格來。」艾德里安聽見便走近。維克多想把話當玩笑帶過，諾拉沒有讓他逃避。她說自己不會留在因為賓客比較沒錢就看輕他們的地方。外婆輕碰她手臂。「我沒事，」她輕聲說，「我見過這種男人。想走就走。」諾拉留下，是因為外婆自己選擇留下。

Later, Adrian apologized to Nora's grandmother. “I should have stopped him sooner,” he said. The old woman asked him whether his power was only for people who knew his name. Adrian was quiet for a long time. “It should not be,” he answered. The old woman smiled. She had not come to make a rich man small. She had come to see who was ready to learn. At the end of the festival, May gave Nora a simple thank-you card from the workers. There was no expensive gift inside, only their names and a little drawing of a needle. Nora held it as if it were the finest thing at the festival.

後來艾德里安向外婆道歉。「我應該更早阻止他。」他說。老人問，他的權力是不是只用來照顧知道他名字的人。艾德里安沉默良久，才回答：「不應該是。」外婆笑了。她不是來讓富人丟臉，而是來看誰願意學習。節慶結束時，梅把工人們寫的感謝卡送給諾拉。裡面沒有昂貴禮物，只有姓名和一張小針線圖。諾拉珍惜地握著，覺得那是整個節慶最好的東西。

On the way back from the lake, Nora found that she had left her small card at the food table. She went back to get it. The worker had placed the card in a safe box with the other things people had left behind. Nora thanked her. She read the names on the card again and thought about the festival. There had been a great deal of food and many fine things, but the best part had been seeing people proud of their work. She was sure she wanted that feeling in her own shop one day. The card was small enough to fit in her pocket. Its meaning felt much larger.

離開湖邊時，諾拉發現小卡留在食物桌上，便回去找。工作人員把它和其他遺落物放在安全盒裡。諾拉道謝，又讀了一遍卡上的名字。節慶有很多食物與漂亮事物，但最好的是看到人們為自己的工作感到驕傲。她確定將來想在自己的店裡保有那種感覺。卡片小得能放入口袋，意義卻大得多。

That evening, Nora and Adrian walked beside the lake, with her grandmother a short way ahead. Adrian asked if he could say something without making it part of a deal. Nora nodded. “I like how you speak even when a room wants you to stay quiet,” he said. Nora looked at the water. She was not sure what the words meant, but she was sure he had meant them. “And I like that you are learning to listen,” she answered. “Do not stop.” A light wind moved over the lake. Adrian did not try to take her hand. The day had been full of people, but the little space between them felt kind and warm.

那天傍晚，外婆走在稍前方，諾拉與艾德里安沿湖散步。艾德里安問，能不能說一件與交易無關的話。諾拉點頭。「就算整個房間都要妳安靜，我也喜歡妳敢開口。」他說。諾拉望向湖面，不太確定這句話有什麼含意，卻相信他是認真的。「我也喜歡你開始學會傾聽，」她回答，「別停下來。」微風吹過湖面。艾德里安沒有試圖牽她的手。這一天有很多人，而兩人之間那小小的距離，卻顯得溫柔又暖和。
