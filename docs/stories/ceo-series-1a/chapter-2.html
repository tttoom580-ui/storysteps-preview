拿著相機的表哥

The Cousin with the Camera

冷面總裁，請先問我 · 第 2 / 25 章 · 1000以下 · 英中對照 · 校訂中

用互動版查詞、朗讀與收藏

Nora took the offer home to her small family apartment in the south of the city. Her sister Kira read it with her. Kira was helpful, but she did not tell Nora what to do. “The deal is better than your last job,” she said. “But why does the company need you for a month?” Nora did not know. They added three questions to a page: Who checks the pay? Who owns the new drawings? Can Nora leave if the work becomes wrong? Nora also wanted to bring her own tools to the factory. She knew cloth and color well, but she was not a science expert. She would need people who knew the machines and could show her real facts.

諾拉把聘約帶回城南的小公寓。妹妹琪拉陪她讀條款。琪拉很願意幫忙，卻不替姊姊決定。「比妳上一份工作的條件好，」她說，「但這間公司為什麼只找妳一個月？」諾拉也不知道。她們在紙上加了三個問題：誰查工資？新設計屬於誰？如果工作不對勁，諾拉能否離開？諾拉也想帶自己的工具去工廠。她懂布料和顏色，但不是科學專家；她需要懂機器的人用真正的資料告訴她答案。

At Silver House, Adrian's cousin Victor watched a video on his phone. It showed Adrian speaking with Nora in the bright office. Victor had filmed them without asking. He was a famous guy in the city because his family had money and power. “Our cold boss has a new friend,” Victor said to the others. Adrian came into the room and asked him to stop the video. Victor laughed. “It is only a little fun.” Adrian did not laugh. “Her work is not a story for you to sell.” Victor closed the screen, but his lip moved into a thin smile. He was not ready to give up the idea.

在銀屋公司，艾德里安的表哥維克多正看著手機影片。影片裡，艾德里安和諾拉在明亮的辦公室談話。維克多沒有經過同意就拍了下來。他家有錢有勢，因此在城裡是個出名人物。「我們的冰山老闆交了新朋友。」他對其他人說。艾德里安走進來，叫他停止播放。維克多笑道：「只是好玩而已。」艾德里安沒有笑。「她的工作不是給你拿去賣的故事。」維克多關上螢幕，嘴角卻微微上揚；他還不想放棄這個主意。

That afternoon, Nora and Adrian went to the factory by the river. The building was old, and its front room was full of boxes. A worker named May showed them how a machine could fill a bag with soft sheep wool. One tool had a broken handle. May put it to one side and used another, safer tool. She told Adrian that the workers wanted a full written record of their hours. Nora asked what part of the work was hard to count. May pointed to small jobs done after the main line stopped. Nora wrote down each one. The workers did not ask the boss for a gift. They asked to be paid for all the work they had done.

下午，諾拉和艾德里安來到河邊工廠。建築很舊，前廳堆滿箱子。工人梅帶他們看機器如何把柔軟的羊毛裝進袋裡。一把工具的柄壞了，梅把它放在一旁，換了比較安全的工具。她告訴艾德里安，工人想要完整的書面工時紀錄。諾拉問，哪些工作最難計算。梅指向主線停工後仍要做的小工作。諾拉一項一項記下。工人沒有向老闆討禮物，他們要的是每一分付出都得到工資。

The river outside looked sunny and calm. Yet inside the factory, the question was not simple. Victor said the workers must have made a mistake. He told Adrian that the south factory cost too much and should close. “That is one view,” Nora said, “but we need to see the records.” Victor stared at her. “You are a dress designer. You are not part of this family.” Nora felt the words hit, but she stayed quiet long enough to think. “That is true,” she answered. “And I am not asking for family power. I am asking for facts.” Adrian turned to May. “Please bring the time sheets. We will check them together.”

外頭的河水在晴天裡顯得平靜，工廠裡的問題卻不簡單。維克多說一定是工人算錯，還告訴艾德里安城南工廠太花錢，應該關閉。「那是一種說法，」諾拉回應，「但我們要看紀錄。」維克多盯著她。「妳是服裝設計師，又不是我們家的人。」諾拉被這句話刺了一下，仍先靜下來思考。「是的，」她回答，「我沒有要家族的權力。我只要事實。」艾德里安轉向梅：「請拿工時表，我們一起核對。」

May later showed Nora how the factory kept its old work book. It was not a perfect tool, but it could help the science of checking time: read the number, ask when it was written, and compare it with another page. Victor wanted everyone to trust his word, but Nora wanted to fill each empty place with a real answer. Adrian stood nearby and listened. He knew his family had power, but power could not make a wrong number right. They made a plan for a second visit. They would bring the books together in one room and let the workers see what was checked. That was helpful to everyone, not only the boss.

梅後來教諾拉看工廠舊工作簿。它不是完美工具，卻能幫助用科學方法核對時間：讀數字、問記錄時間、再和另一頁比對。維克多希望大家只信他的話，諾拉卻想用真實答案填上每個空白。艾德里安在旁聽著。他的家族有權力，但權力不會讓錯誤數字變正確。他們訂下第二次訪查，讓書面紀錄放在同一間房，工人也能看到查了什麼。這對每個人都有幫助，不只是總裁。

On the train home, Nora got a message from her cousin Jo, who lived in another town. Jo saw the video online and asked if Nora had met the rich boss for love. Nora wrote back that she had met him for work. She wanted a day when people would know her designs, not just the man who stood near them. Adrian sent one short message too. He told her that Victor had no right to share the video and that the company would ask for its removal. Nora thanked him. Then she added, “Tomorrow we look at the numbers, not at the camera.” He answered, “Agreed.” It was the first time their two names stood on the same side of a hard question.

在回家的火車上，諾拉收到住在別鎮的表妹喬傳來的訊息。喬在網路上看見那段影片，問諾拉是不是為了愛情去見有錢總裁。諾拉回覆，她是為了工作。她希望有一天，人們知道的是自己的設計，而不是站在設計旁邊的男人。艾德里安也傳來簡短訊息，說維克多沒有權利散播影片，公司會要求下架。諾拉道了謝，又寫：「明天看數字，不看鏡頭。」他回答：「同意。」那是兩人第一次為同一個難題站在同一邊。
