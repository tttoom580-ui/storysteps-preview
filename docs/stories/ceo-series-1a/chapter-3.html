通往舊工坊的路

The Road to the Old Mill

冷面總裁，請先問我 · 第 3 / 25 章 · 1000以下 · 英中對照 · 校訂中

The next morning, Nora met May at the station beside the road. May did not want the factory visit to become a show for the boss. She wanted every worker to be able to speak. Nora agreed. They made a simple line on a page: name of job, start time, end time, and pay. May showed her where a bridge joined the city road to the old mill. Far beyond it was a small beach where children played. A bird sat on the bridge rail. Nora watched the water move under it. She was born in this city, but she had never followed this road before. Now she saw how many hands helped make one coat.

隔天早上，諾拉在路旁車站見到梅。梅不希望工廠參訪變成做給老闆看的表演，她要每位工人都有說話的機會。諾拉同意。她們在紙上畫出簡單欄位：工作名稱、開始時間、結束時間和工資。梅指給她看，哪座橋把市區道路接到舊工坊。遠處有孩子玩耍的小海灘，一隻鳥停在橋欄上。諾拉看著下方水流。她在這座城市出生，卻從沒走過這條路；現在她才知道，一件外套需要多少雙手幫忙。

Inside the mill, every table had a different task. One worker cut cloth. Another worker put buttons in a box. A third worker tried to clean the floor so no one would fall. A long row of shoes stood near the door, including one pair with a bright cow picture. May laughed. “Those are mine. My little son drew the cow.” The joke made the room less cold. Nora looked at both sides of the work table. On one side were finished pieces. On the other side were papers that had not been signed. She did not pull the papers away. She asked May who could show them to her. “The office can,” May said, “if Adrian gives us permission.”

工坊裡，每張桌子做不同的工作。有人裁布，有人把鈕扣放進盒子，也有人努力把地板清乾淨，避免大家跌倒。門邊排著一列鞋，其中一雙印著明亮的牛圖案。梅笑說：「那是我的，我兒子畫的牛。」這個玩笑讓房間不再那麼冷。諾拉查看工作桌兩側：一側是完成的衣片，另一側是尚未簽名的紙。她沒有把紙抽走，而是問梅誰能讓她看。「辦公室可以，」梅說，「只要艾德里安允許。」

Adrian arrived with a small team. He did not know that a broken road had made the delivery truck late twice that week. He looked about the room and asked what had happened. May explained that the truck had to turn into a narrow road after the bridge. Workers had to carry heavy pieces the last short way to the station. Nora showed him the written times. “The road and the extra work must be part of the cost,” she said. Adrian took a photo of the road from a safe place. “We will ask a road officer about the truck route,” he said. “I will not tell a driver to use a road that is not safe.”

艾德里安帶著一小隊人到場。他不知道那週送貨車因道路損壞而晚到兩次。他看了看四周，問發生什麼事。梅解釋，卡車過橋後得轉進狹窄道路，最後一小段還要工人搬運沉重衣片到裝貨站。諾拉把時間紀錄給他看。「這條路和額外工作都要算入成本。」她說。艾德里安站在安全處拍下道路照片。「我們請道路管理人員確認貨車路線，」他說，「我不會叫司機走不安全的路。」

Victor appeared after lunch and asked Nora to give him the papers. “The company paid for them,” he said. Nora held them close to her side. “These copies belong to May until she gives them to the right person.” Victor did not like that answer. He pointed to the big square room where the machines stood. “If this place shuts, what will happen to all these people? Do you want that?” Nora answered that she wanted the numbers checked first. Adrian stepped between them but did not speak for her. He told Victor to speak to the factory manager about company papers. Nora noticed the difference. The CEO could protect a fair process without turning her into a person who could not speak.

午餐後維克多出現，叫諾拉把紙交給他。「公司付了錢。」他說。諾拉把紙靠近身側。「這些副本暫時由梅保管，直到她交給適當的人。」維克多不喜歡這個答案。他指向放機器的大方形房間：「要是這裡關了，這些人怎麼辦？妳要那樣嗎？」諾拉回答，她要先核對數字。艾德里安站到兩人中間，卻沒有代替她說話。他告訴維克多，公司的文件應向工廠經理洽詢。諾拉注意到這個差別：總裁能守護公平程序，不必把她當成沒有發言能力的人。

May walked Nora to the station after the visit. She said she had tried to speak about the extra work before, but she had not known whom to ask. Nora pointed to the new contact name on the wall. The name was not a promise that every problem would go away. It was a place to start. Nora followed the same road back to the city, and she saw the bridge from a different side. What would happen to the mill if the company could not make its work fair? She did not know. But she knew that every person who worked there deserved an honest answer before anyone made a final choice.

訪查後梅陪諾拉走向車站。她說以前也試著提過額外工作，卻不知道該找誰。諾拉指著牆上新公布的聯絡人。那名字不能保證問題都消失，但至少是開始。諾拉沿同一條路回城，從不同方向再看那座橋。如果公司無法讓工作公平，工坊會發生什麼事？她不知道。但她知道，做最後決定前，每位工人都值得一個誠實答案。

Before leaving, Nora put a cup of water on the table for May. The day had been hot, and they had walked a long way. May said that one new worker was afraid to speak because he did not know what would happen. Nora told her that the team would not put names on a public page. The workers could choose whether to take part. May smiled and said that was a good start. Outside, a bird flew over the river, and the light touched the old bridge. Nora looked back at the mill. The hard work behind a beautiful dress was real. It would be wrong to leave that work out of the story.

離開前，諾拉在桌上放了一杯水給梅。天氣炎熱，她們走了好長一段路。梅說有位新工人因為不知道後果而不敢開口。諾拉告訴她，團隊不會把姓名公開，工人可以自行選擇是否參與。梅微笑，說這是好開始。外頭一隻鳥飛過河，陽光照在舊橋上。諾拉回望工坊。漂亮衣服背後的辛苦勞動是真實存在的；把它們排除在故事之外，是不對的。
