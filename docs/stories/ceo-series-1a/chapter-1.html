七點鐘的冷面男人

The Man at Seven

冷面總裁，請先問我 · 第 1 / 25 章 · 1000以下 · 英中對照 · 校訂中

At seven a.m., Nora Lin came early to the top floor of Silver House. She was not there for breakfast with a famous man. She was there to show her dress designs. A little bell rang at the front desk. Through a large window, the morning sun made the city look gold. On one side of the room stood a heavy table with a white cover. On the other side was a gray couch. Nora sat down with a bag of fruit, a small cake, and a salad. She did not eat. Her hands were busy holding the paper that could change her work life. She was twenty-seven, and she wanted to start her own clothing name. She had already worked too many nights for other people to place their names on her work.

上午七點，諾拉・林提早來到銀屋公司的頂樓。她不是來和某位有名的男人吃早餐，而是來展示自己的服裝設計。櫃檯的小鈴鐺響了。透過大窗，晨光把城市照得金亮。房間一側有張鋪著白布的沉重大桌，另一側則是灰色沙發。諾拉坐下，袋裡有水果、小蛋糕和沙拉，卻一口也沒吃。她忙著握緊那些可能改變職涯的圖紙。二十七歲的她想創立自己的服裝品牌；她已經為別人熬了太多夜，不願再讓別人的名字蓋過自己的作品。

A man in a dark suit entered without a sound. He was Adrian Vale, the thirty-two-year-old boss of Silver House. People said he never smiled at work. He looked at the drawings, not at Nora's shoes or hair. “These are yours?” he asked. “Yes. I made every line.” Adrian pointed to a picture of a blue coat. Nora had put a small monkey on the inside pocket as a joke for children. The monkey had one big tooth. “Why a monkey?” he asked. “It is my hobby to make one small happy thing in each dress,” she said. Adrian did not laugh, but he turned the page with care. For the first time that morning, Nora did not feel small.

一名穿深色西裝的男人無聲走進來。他就是銀屋公司三十二歲的總裁艾德里安・維爾。大家都說他工作時從不笑。他看著設計圖，而不是諾拉的鞋或髮型。「這些是妳畫的？」他問。「是，每條線都是我畫的。」艾德里安指著一張藍外套的圖。諾拉在內袋畫了一隻小猴子，當作送給孩子的小玩笑，猴子有顆大牙。「為什麼是猴子？」他問。「我的興趣，是在每件衣服裡藏一件讓人開心的小事。」她說。艾德里安沒有笑，但翻頁的動作很輕。那個早晨，她第一次不覺得自己渺小。

Nora began to teach him how the coat was made. It needed two kinds of cloth. One came from a foreign country, but the other came from a small factory near the city. She could write down the full cost of each piece. Adrian took out a pen. “Our last show made money, but some workers said they did not get their full pay. I need to know what is true.” Nora stopped. No one had told her about that problem before she came. She had hoped for a simple yes or no about her drawings. Now she saw that the beautiful room could cover a hard question. “I can help you look,” she said, “but I will not hide what I find.”

諾拉開始教他這件外套怎麼製作。它需要兩種布，一種來自國外，另一種來自城邊的小工廠。她能寫下每一部分的真實成本。艾德里安拿出筆。「上次服裝秀賺了錢，但有工人說沒有領到全部薪水。我想知道真相。」諾拉停住。沒有人在她來之前告訴過她這個問題。她原本只希望自己的圖紙得到一個簡單的同意或拒絕。現在，她明白美麗的房間可能蓋住難題。「我可以幫忙查，」她說，「但我不會藏起發現的事。」

He asked her to stay for a meeting later that day. “Shall I send a car?” he said. Nora shook her head. “No. I know how to take the train.” Adrian looked surprised. She saw it in his eyes, though he kept the same calm face. He offered her a job for one month, with money for her time even if he did not use her designs. Nora asked for the words on paper. Adrian nodded and asked his secretary to write a clear offer. Nora felt her heart move fast. He was a rich man who could buy almost any coat, but he could not buy her answer before she chose it.

他請諾拉當天稍晚留下開會。「要我派車嗎？」他問。諾拉搖頭。「不用，我會搭火車。」艾德里安有些驚訝，雖然表情仍然平靜，她卻看得出來。他提出一個月的工作邀約，就算最後不用她的設計，也會支付她的時間。諾拉要求把條件寫在紙上。艾德里安點頭，請祕書寫成清楚的聘約。諾拉的心跳快了起來。這個有錢男人幾乎能買到任何外套，卻不能在她做決定前買下她的答案。

At home that night, Nora spread the offer across her small kitchen table. She drew a line under the sentence about pay and another under the sentence about her drawings. She was excited, but she also felt afraid. A big company could make her work easy to see, yet it could also make her own name hard to find. Kira called and asked whether she needed help reading the paper. Nora said yes. She was not ashamed to ask. Before bed, she put the offer beside the first little dress she had ever made. Tomorrow she would choose what to do, not because the boss was famous, but because the plan was fair.

那晚諾拉在小廚房桌上攤開聘約，把報酬條款和設計歸屬各畫上一道線。她很興奮，也有點害怕。大公司能讓作品被看見，也可能讓自己的名字不見。琪拉打來問要不要一起讀文件，諾拉說好，她不覺得求助丟臉。睡前，她把聘約放在自己做的第一件小洋裝旁。明天要選擇什麼，不是因為總裁有名，而是因為計畫是否公平。

As Nora waited, she walked past a room where staff did morning exercise. A new worker smiled and showed her a drawing of a sheep. Nora smiled back. She remembered what her mother used to say: a good teacher gives a child room to ask questions. Perhaps a good boss should do the same for grown people. Adrian returned with the offer. “Read it at home,” he said. “You do not need to answer now.” Nora put the paper in her bag. She would read each line before she signed. When she left, the bell sounded again. This time its bright sound did not make her afraid. A door had opened, but the next step belonged to her.

等待時，諾拉走過一間員工正在做晨間運動的房間。一名新人向她微笑，拿出一張羊的圖畫。諾拉也笑了。她想起母親常說，好老師會留空間讓孩子提問。也許好的老闆也應該讓大人這麼做。艾德里安帶著聘約回來。「帶回家看，」他說，「不必現在回答。」諾拉把紙放進袋子。她會先讀懂每一行才簽名。離開時鈴聲又響了。這一次，清亮的聲音沒有讓她害怕。門已經打開，但下一步由她自己決定。
