櫥窗裡的小船

A Boat in the Window

冷面總裁，請先問我 · 第 6 / 25 章 · 1000以下 · 英中對照 · 校訂中

On the weekend, Nora arrived at Adrian's family house for a short meeting about the dinner. A black car stopped near the gate, but Nora had chosen to drive her own small car. The house was by a lake, and a boat moved slowly over the water. Adrian stood at the top of the front steps with a box of old pictures. He showed her one of his uncle, who had made clothes by hand before the family opened a big company. “I want the new show to remember him,” Adrian said. In the picture, his uncle held a paper lion and waved at the camera. Nora could see why a simple shape could mean more to Adrian than a fine coat.

週末，諾拉來到艾德里安家的大宅，短暫商量晚宴事宜。一輛黑車停在門邊，諾拉卻選擇自己開小車來。宅子靠湖，一艘小船在水面慢慢移動。艾德里安站在前階頂端，手裡有盒舊照片。他拿出一張叔叔的照片：叔叔從前親手縫衣，家族後來才開成大公司。「我想讓新秀記得他。」艾德里安說。照片裡，叔叔拿著紙做的獅子向鏡頭揮手。諾拉看得出來，對他而言，一個簡單圖形可能比昂貴外套更有意義。

They sat in a bright room with coffee and bread. Adrian said the family dinner would be free for guests, but its gifts would cost a great deal of money. Nora wanted to see the list before she gave her name to any gift. “I do not want my work to be used to cover an unfair act,” she explained. Adrian looked at the paper and made a sign beside the gifts that were not yet checked. His face became dark when he found Victor's name next to a large order from the factory. “This looks terrible,” he said. Nora answered, “It may be wrong, but we should see the real paper before we call anyone stupid.” Adrian nodded, even though he clearly wanted to act at once.

兩人坐在明亮房間裡喝咖啡、吃麵包。艾德里安說家族晚宴讓賓客免費入場，但伴手禮要花很多錢。諾拉希望在同意署名前看過禮物清單。「我不希望自己的作品被拿來掩蓋不公平的事。」她解釋。艾德里安在尚未核對的禮物旁做記號。他看到工廠一筆大訂單旁寫著維克多的名字時，臉色沉了下來。「看起來很糟。」他說。諾拉回答：「可能有問題，但先看真實文件再下結論，不要急著罵人笨。」艾德里安點頭，雖然他顯然很想立刻行動。

After coffee, Nora asked to see the sewing room in the old house. It had a long table and a window with a view of the lake. A small wooden boat stood on the shelf. Adrian told her his uncle had made it to teach him patience. “You cannot make a good shape by pulling the wood too hard,” he said. Nora held the boat in her hands. The work was careful and slow. “That is also true of a coat,” she answered. “If you ask for speed every minute, the sewing line will suffer.” Adrian asked what she needed to make her first dress. She said she needed a clear budget, a safe workshop, and the right to put her name beside the people who sewed it.

喝完咖啡，諾拉請他帶她看老宅的縫紉房。裡面有長桌，窗外是湖景，架上放著一艘木船。艾德里安說那是叔叔為了教他耐心而做的。「木頭不能一直用力拉，不然做不出好形狀。」他說。諾拉把小船握在手裡，感受到緩慢細緻的手工。「衣服也一樣，」她說，「如果每分鐘都催快，縫線就會出問題。」艾德里安問她要做第一件洋裝需要什麼。她說，需要清楚的預算、安全的工坊，以及讓自己和縫衣工人的名字一起出現的權利。

Adrian offered to show her the garden, but she saw dark clouds and chose to leave before the rain. “I can send someone,” he said. “No, thank you. I know the road,” Nora replied. He did not ask again. At the door, he gave her a folded copy of his uncle's old work plan. “You may read this if it helps,” he said. “There is no secret price.” Nora smiled. The paper was old, but the lines were clear. On the way home she thought about how unusual their talk had become. She had expected a rich man's order. Instead, she was learning the shape of a man who did not always know how to ask for help.

艾德里安想帶她看花園，諾拉看到烏雲便決定趁雨前離開。「我可以派人送妳。」他說。「謝謝，不用。我認得路。」她回答。他沒有再問。門口，他交給她一份叔叔留下的工作計畫副本。「有幫助的話就讀讀看，沒有什麼秘密價格。」諾拉微笑。紙雖然舊，字跡仍很清楚。回家路上，她想著這場對話多麼出乎意料。她原以為會聽到富人的命令，卻慢慢看見一個不太會開口求助的男人。

Before Nora left the house, she walked once more into the old sewing room. Most things had been taken away, except a wooden ruler and the little boat. Adrian followed her to the door and told her about the day his uncle had finally finished the first coat for the family shop. It had taken many weeks. The coat was simple, but it had lasted for years. Nora understood why Adrian kept the picture. He wanted to build something that would last too. She wondered whether he could learn to do it without asking everyone around him to move as fast as he did. Perhaps that would be the hardest part.

離開前，諾拉又走進舊縫紉房。大多數東西已移走，除了木尺和小木船。艾德里安跟到門口，說叔叔當年花了好幾週，才終於完成家族店裡的第一件外套。外套很簡單，卻穿了很多年。諾拉明白他為何珍惜照片。他也想建立能長久存在的東西。她想，他能不能學著不再要求身旁每個人都和自己一樣快？或許那才是最難的部分。

That night Victor sent a short message to Nora. He said the dinner would be a terrible place for someone without an important family name. Nora took a deep breath. She did not answer him. Instead she wrote to the dinner office and asked where her designs would be shown, who would receive the gifts, and whether the workers would be paid for that extra work. A clear answer was more useful than an angry fight. She put the questions in one box on her desk. The little wooden boat in Adrian's story stayed in her mind. A good plan had to move like that boat: steady, open to the waves, and strong enough to reach the other side.

那晚維克多傳訊息給諾拉，說那種晚宴不適合沒有顯赫家世的人。諾拉深呼吸，沒有回他。她改寫信給晚宴辦公室，詢問作品展示位置、禮物會給誰、工人加班是否有薪水。清楚的回答比生氣爭吵更有用。她把問題收進桌上的盒子。艾德里安故事裡的小木船仍留在她心裡。好計畫得像那條船，走得穩，能迎著浪，也要有力量到達彼岸。
