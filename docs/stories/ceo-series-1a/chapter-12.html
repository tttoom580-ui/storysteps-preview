寫著自己名字的洋裝

A Dress with Her Own Name

冷面總裁，請先問我 · 第 12 / 25 章 · 1000以下 · 英中對照 · 校訂中

用互動版查詞、朗讀與收藏

Nora wanted an answer before the company made more dresses with her drawings. She rode her bicycle to a small workroom near her home. She wore jeans and carried a bag of cloth, paper, and a little salt for lunch. Her friend Bea worked there and liked to make hats with wide bows. Bea asked, “Are those drawings yours or the company's?” Nora smiled. “They are mine. That is what the first paper says.” She opened her bag and showed the signed agreement. It said she owned the original designs, while Silver House could use certain finished pieces for the show if it paid her. Bea read it twice. She was happy Nora had learned to pick words with care, as carefully as cloth.

諾拉想在公司用她的設計再做洋裝前，得到明確答案。她騎腳踏車到住家附近的小工作室，穿牛仔褲，袋裡裝著布、紙和午餐用的一點鹽。朋友碧雅在那裡工作，喜歡做有大蝴蝶結的帽子。碧雅問：「這些圖屬於妳，還是公司？」諾拉微笑：「是我的，第一份文件寫得清清楚楚。」她拿出簽好的合約。上面寫著原始設計歸她所有；銀屋公司若付費，可在服裝秀使用部分成品。碧雅讀了兩遍，為諾拉像挑布一樣仔細挑字而高興。

They talked about what kind of clothes they would make when they had their own shop. Nora wished for dresses that people could use every day, not only on one special night. Bea wanted bright colors, small pockets, and soft cloth for spring. She said a dress should move in the wind without making a person feel afraid to move. Nora drew a second dress, this one with a low back and long arms. Bea added a bow that could come off if someone did not like it. They worked for two hours without anyone giving them an order. It felt good to make choices for themselves, even though they still had a great deal to learn.

她們聊起將來如果有自己的店，想做什麼衣服。諾拉希望洋裝能每天穿，而不是只在特別的夜晚穿一次。碧雅想要亮色、口袋，以及適合春天的柔軟布料。她說衣服應該能隨風擺動，讓人不害怕伸展。諾拉畫了第二件洋裝，背部比較低、袖子較長。碧雅加上一個可以拆下的蝴蝶結，讓不喜歡的人有選擇。兩人工作兩小時，沒有任何人下命令。能自己決定很開心，雖然她們還有許多要學。

Adrian called while Nora was cleaning up. “I have good news,” he said. “The outside checker has found the missing hours, and the company will pay them.” Nora asked whether the workers had seen the numbers. He said not yet. “Then please show them before you make a public speech,” she answered. Adrian was quiet for a second. “You are right.” Nora did not want to borrow his power and call it her own. She wanted to do work that could stand when he was not in the room. They spoke for a few more minutes about the show. Before they ended the call, Adrian asked if she was free on Sunday to see a planet show at the city hall. Nora said she would think about it.

收拾時，艾德里安打電話來。「有好消息，」他說，「外部查核員找到漏算工時，公司會補發薪水。」諾拉問工人看過數字沒。他說還沒有。「那請先給他們看，再公開發言。」她回答。艾德里安靜了一秒：「妳說得對。」諾拉不想借他的權力當成自己的。她要做的是即使他不在房裡，也站得住腳的工作。兩人又聊了一會兒服裝秀。掛電話前，艾德里安問她星期天有沒有空去看市民中心的行星展。諾拉說會考慮。

On Sunday morning, Nora sent a message. “I can go for one hour,” she wrote. “But this is not a work meeting.” Adrian answered, “Understood.” At the show, they saw pictures of the moon, stars, and the earth. The guide said every planet moved in its own way. Nora liked that idea. She turned to Adrian. “I do not want to be a second part of your name,” she said. “I want my own path.” Adrian looked at her without speaking for a moment. Then he answered, “I would like to walk beside you, not in front of you.” Nora felt warm. She did not need to answer a question he had not asked yet.

星期天早上，諾拉傳訊息：「我可以去一小時，但這不是工作會議。」艾德里安回答：「明白。」展場裡有月亮、星星和地球的圖片。導覽員說，每顆行星都有自己的運行方式。諾拉喜歡這個想法。她轉向艾德里安：「我不想變成你名字的附屬品。我想走自己的路。」艾德里安看著她，沉默片刻後回答：「我想走在妳身邊，不是走在前面。」諾拉心裡一暖。對方還沒真正問出口的問題，她不需要急著回答。

Bea and Nora laid the pieces of the new dress across the table. “The body needs room to move,” Bea said. “Clothes are not pictures.” They looked at a short note written in two languages so the whole team could read it. Nora added some simple drawings to make the steps easier. “We need to send this soon,” Bea said. “So let us check it one more time.” The girls read the page out loud and found one missing word. They fixed it before sending it to the sewing team. Nora liked how their work had changed. It was no longer only about making one beautiful thing. It was also about making the work clear to other people.

碧雅與諾拉把洋裝各片排在桌上。「身體要有活動空間，」碧雅說，「衣服不是圖片。」她們查看用兩種語言寫的短說明，讓所有團隊成員都能讀懂。諾拉加了一些簡單圖片。「要快點寄出，」碧雅說，「所以再檢查一次。」兩人朗讀後找到漏掉的一個字，寄給縫紉團隊前先改好。諾拉喜歡工作方式的改變：不只是做漂亮的東西，也要讓其他人清楚理解怎麼做。

After the show, they walked out into the spring wind. Adrian did not try to hold Nora's arm. He asked before he took a picture of her looking at a bright model of the moon. Nora said yes and asked him to send it only to her. He agreed. She looked at the picture on her phone. She was smiling in it. It was strange to see herself happy beside a man who was once only a cold name on a company door. She still had many questions about him and about the future. But she did not wish for a perfect answer today. She wished only to keep the choice open until she knew her own heart.

看完展，他們走進春風裡。艾德里安沒有擅自拉諾拉的手臂。他想拍她看月亮模型的照片，先開口詢問。諾拉答應，並請他只傳給自己；他同意。她在手機裡看見照片中的自己正微笑。從前，那個男人只是公司大門上的冷冰冰名字；如今能在他身旁笑，實在奇妙。她仍有許多關於他和未來的問題，但今天不想要完美答案，只希望保有選擇，直到自己真正了解自己的心。
