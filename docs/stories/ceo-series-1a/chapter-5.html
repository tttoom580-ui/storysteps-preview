可以隨時離開的約定

A Deal with an Open Door

冷面總裁，請先問我 · 第 5 / 25 章 · 1000以下 · 英中對照 · 校訂中

By Friday, Nora knew the factory story was larger than one dress. She had been asked to work during a busy month, yet no one could show her all the pay records. Adrian brought her a new deal. The company would pay for her design time, and an outside team would check the missing hours. Nora read the page for a long minute. “Although this is better, one thing is wrong,” she said. “I can stop this job, but the workers cannot simply walk away from their bills.” Adrian listened. He agreed that the workers needed a safe way to report a problem. They wrote that into the new plan, with a clear date and a person to contact.

到了星期五，諾拉知道工廠的事遠比一件洋裝更大。她受聘在忙碌的月份工作，但沒有人能讓她看到完整工時紀錄。艾德里安拿來新條款：公司支付她的設計時間，並由外部團隊查核漏算工時。諾拉花了好一會兒讀完。「雖然好一些，還有一點不對，」她說，「我可以停止這份工作，工人卻不能說走就走，不管帳單。」艾德里安聽進去了。他同意工人需要安全的申訴方式。兩人在新計畫寫明聯絡窗口與處理日期。

Then Adrian asked a different question. His family wanted him to go to three public dinners with a woman they had chosen. He did not want to make a promise of love to someone he hardly knew. “Would you come to one dinner as my guest?” he asked Nora. “Not as my girlfriend. As a designer with work to show.” Nora looked up. His eyes were serious, but his hands rested still on the table. She could say no. “Why me?” she asked. “Because you speak to me as if my money is not the most important thing in the room,” he said. Nora laughed once. It was a strange answer from a man whose office was almost as high as the clouds.

接著艾德里安問了另一件事。家人希望他陪指定的女子出席三場公開晚宴，但他不想向幾乎不認識的人許下愛情承諾。「妳願不願意以我的客人身分出席一場？」他問諾拉，「不是當我的女朋友，而是能展示作品的設計師。」諾拉抬頭。他神情認真，雙手卻安靜放在桌上。她可以拒絕。「為什麼找我？」她問。「因為妳跟我說話時，不把我的錢當成房間裡最重要的事。」他說。諾拉笑了一聲。從一個辦公室幾乎高到雲端的男人口中聽到這句話，實在奇妙。

Nora asked for time to think. She went to lunch with her sister Kira and ordered beans and rice. At the next table, a family had a small picture of a frog and a hen beside a bowl of flowers. A child wanted to win a race to the door, but her mother asked her to be safe. Nora watched them and thought about the dinner. Going could help people see her work. It could also make them speak about her as if she were only a rich man's pretty guest. She did not want that. Kira said, “At least ask for what you need.” Nora wrote three rules on a napkin before she finished eating.

諾拉說要考慮。她和妹妹琪拉吃午餐，點了豆子和飯。隔壁桌的家庭把一張畫著青蛙與母雞的小圖放在花碗旁。孩子想和人賽跑到門口，媽媽提醒她注意安全。諾拉看著他們，想到晚宴。出席可能讓大家看見自己的作品，也可能讓人只把她當作富有男人身邊的漂亮客人。她不願意那樣。琪拉說：「至少說清楚妳需要什麼。」飯還沒吃完，諾拉就在餐巾紙上寫了三條規則。

She returned and put the napkin in front of Adrian. First, her work name would be on the dinner program. Second, no one would say they were in love or going to be married. Third, she could leave the dinner at any time without losing her job or pay. Adrian read it. “I am sorry that I did not say these things first,” he told her. “They are fair.” Nora asked him to put the words into a proper message so there could be no mistake. He did. Then he asked, “Does this mean yes?” Nora shook her head. “It means I can now decide.” For the first time, Adrian smiled. It lasted only a second, but it changed his face.

她回公司，把餐巾紙放到艾德里安面前。第一，晚宴節目單要印上她的設計師姓名。第二，任何人不得說他們相愛或準備結婚。第三，她可以隨時離席，且不影響工作或報酬。艾德里安讀完說：「抱歉，我沒有先想到這些，這些條件很公平。」諾拉請他把話寫成正式訊息，避免誤解，他照做了。然後他問：「這樣算答應嗎？」諾拉搖頭：「這樣我才有條件決定。」艾德里安第一次笑了，雖然只有一秒，卻讓整張臉都變了樣。

At the factory the next morning, Ms. May was checking a new sign beside the sewing line. A worker had hurt her neck from looking down at a table that was too low. Nora asked May to point to the place where the problem began. May showed her a table leg that could not be changed. They wrote the height on a page and asked a trained person to check it. No one needed to win a race against the clock. The work had to be safe first. Nora thought about her dinner rules. A good agreement was much like a good work table: it had to fit the people who used it.

隔天早上，梅女士正在檢查縫紉線旁的新標示。一名工人因長時間低頭看太矮的桌面，脖子不舒服。諾拉請梅指出問題從哪裡開始。梅帶她看一支無法調整的桌腳。他們把高度記下，請受過訓練的人檢查。沒有人需要和時鐘賽跑；工作應先安全。諾拉想到自己的晚宴條款。好約定就像好工作桌，必須適合真正使用它的人。

On the way home, Nora passed a shop window with a dress made for a very tall woman. Beside it was a small sign about the dress's height and length. She thought about how many choices could fit into one piece of cloth. She thought about the factory too. Someone had put a little sign showing where not to dig because a water pipe ran below the floor. Workers needed signs and rules that were clear, just as she did. Nora did not want to rush a choice like a race. She waited until the snow-white moon was high, then sent Adrian a short message: “I will go to one dinner. We will follow the rules.” He wrote back, “Thank you. Your choice matters.”

回家途中，諾拉經過一扇商店櫥窗，裡面有件給高個子女人穿的洋裝，旁邊小牌寫著衣服的高度和長度。她想，一塊布能容納多少選擇。她又想到工廠：地板下有水管的地方，工人立了一塊小牌提醒不要挖掘。工人需要清楚的標示和規則，她也一樣。她不想把決定當成賽跑。等到雪白月亮升高，她才傳訊息給艾德里安：「我會出席一場晚宴。我們照規則做。」他回覆：「謝謝。妳的選擇很重要。」
