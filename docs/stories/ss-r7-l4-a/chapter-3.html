山丘下的通道

A Path Beneath the Hill

失物檔案館：城市遺忘的那一天 · 第 3 / 25 章 · 3000–4000 · 英中對照

The bakery entrance was connected to a forgotten tunnel beneath a mountainous park. Mei brought Tao, Han, and a young surveyor named Lin to the locked barrier. Lin called the closure accidental; a clerk had entered the wrong number after last year's repair. But the lock was new, and its paint had not faded. Mei asked him to evaluate that difference before writing his report. They had seen how a simple claim could spread from an object to the place around it. This time they needed to know whether the tunnel had ever truly stopped serving the public.

麵包店入口連著一條被遺忘的山丘地下通道。陳玫帶著陶陶、韓叔和年輕測量員林遠來到鎖住的柵欄。林遠說封閉只是意外：去年修繕後，職員輸錯了編號。但門鎖很新，油漆也沒有褪色。陳玫請他先評估這個差異再寫報告。他們已看見一筆普通申領能如何蔓延至物品所在的地方；這次得查明通道是否真的停止供公眾使用。

Inside, a mild wind carried dust and the smell of wet stone. A fallen twig lay beside a line of emergency lights that blink at irregular intervals. Han remembered a brutal winter when the tunnel had sheltered families during a storm. Their gratitude was recorded in a wall painting, a composition of umbrellas and faces made by children. The painter had left a message beneath it: "Do not neglect the door just because the sun has returned." Mei copied the sentence, not as proof of legal ownership, but as evidence of what people once needed here. The team relied on shared intelligence, not on a secret warning that only one person could hear.

裡面有一陣微弱的風，夾著灰塵和潮石氣味。掉落的細枝旁，一排緊急燈不規律地閃爍。韓叔想起某個嚴酷的冬天，這條通道曾收容受暴風雨影響的家庭。孩子們畫了一幅由雨傘和人臉構成的壁畫，留下居民感激的記憶。畫家在下方寫道：「別因太陽回來，就忽略這扇門。」陳玫把句子抄下，但不是當作合法所有權證明，而是當成人們曾需要這裡的證據。 團隊仰賴共同蒐集的情報，不依賴只有一人能聽見的祕密警告。

An old speaker crackled. A voice announced that anyone who questioned the closure would be punished for offending the system. Tao looked frightened enough to choke on his words. Lin, who liked to quote a philosopher whenever he felt uncertain, began explaining why obedience might be safer. Mei stopped him gently. "There is a difference between a grave warning and an empty threat," she said. "Can the speaker tell us which written rule we broke?" The voice repeated its demand, but supplied no rule number. The threat was noise, not a verdict.

一具老舊擴音器響起雜音。有個聲音宣布，質疑封路的人都會因冒犯系統而受罰。陶陶害怕得幾乎說不出話。林遠一不安就喜歡引用哲學家的話，此時開始解釋服從也許更安全。陳玫溫和打斷他：「嚴重的警告和空洞的威脅不一樣。它能說出我們違反哪條明文規則嗎？」聲音不斷重複命令，卻報不出條號。那是噪音，不是判決。

They located a cabinet containing old repair cards. One card showed a design problem that had a tendency to recur: rainwater could collect near the lower steps. Another listed a proposed reform that would put the tunnel under shared maintenance. The documents did not support revenge against the officer who had closed it, and Mei did not want revenge. She wanted a working exit. She urged Lin to photograph the date stamps before anyone moved the cards. The fresh lock had been installed after the public repair request, not before it.

他們找到一個裝著舊維修卡的櫃子。有張卡記著容易反覆發生的設計問題：雨水會聚集在較低的階梯旁。另一張提出改革方案，要讓通道由多方共同維護。文件不能支持對封路官員的報復，陳玫也不想報復。她要的是能使用的出口。她催促林遠先拍下日期印章，再移動卡片。新鎖的安裝時間竟晚於公共修繕申請，而不是早於它。

Lin knelt beside the lock with a ruler. The unbroken paint on its bracket overlapped an older patch of repaired stone. Han held the lamp while Mei copied the repair card's date. Together these details gave Lin reason to withdraw his first explanation of an accidental closure. He crossed it out, signed the change, and left the old words readable. Tao asked whether they should force the barrier open. Lin checked the lower steps first and found water beneath a loose board. They would need a safe route before inviting the bakery's customers inside. Mei sent Han back to ask recent users for separate accounts; if none came forward, the tunnel might remain closed despite everything they had found. While they waited, Lin marked the wet step with a strip of cloth and began measuring a narrow path along the wall.

林跪在鎖旁，用尺測量。固定架上未破損的新漆蓋住一片較早修補過的石面。韓叔拿燈，陳玫抄下修繕卡日期。這些細節使林撤回最初意外封閉的解釋。他劃掉原文，簽名更正，舊字仍看得見。陶陶問是否該撬開障礙，林先檢查下方階梯，發現鬆木板下有水。他們得先找出安全路線，才能讓麵包店顧客進來。陳玫請韓叔回去尋找近期使用者，各自提供說法；若無人出面，即使有這些發現，通道也可能繼續封閉。等候時，林以布條標出濕階，開始測量牆邊的狹窄路線。

At the end of the tunnel, Lin discovered a narrow side gate with an old maintenance tag. A silver claim appeared immediately: "Gate abandoned." Mei asked Han and Lin to describe its last known use separately. Han remembered carrying supplies through it in the storm. Lin found a stamped delivery schedule from three months ago. Their accounts disagreed about the year but agreed that people had continued using the gate. Mei entered both dates, including the disagreement, and submitted the review. The claim froze. Through the gate, they saw the back wall of the municipal record office.

通道盡頭，林遠發現一扇窄側門，門上還掛著舊維護牌。銀色申領單立刻浮現：「側門已棄置。」陳玫請韓叔和林遠分開說明最近一次使用。韓叔記得風暴時曾搬物資經過；林遠則找到三個月前蓋章的送貨時刻表。兩人的年份說法不同，卻都證明門仍有人使用。陳玫連同分歧一起登錄、提出複核。申領暫停了。從側門望出去，他們看到市立檔案室的後牆。
