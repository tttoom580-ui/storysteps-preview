沒有贏家的聽證會

A Hearing Without a Winner

失物檔案館：城市遺忘的那一天 · 第 10 / 25 章 · 3000–4000 · 英中對照 · 校訂中

The municipal hall opened its first formal hearing on the Inventory. The atmosphere was tense, but Mei was determined to give each person a fair option to speak. An ambitious official wanted acceptance of every automatic claim as the default. Mei brought authentic copies of receipts and asked reviewers to classify objects by current use rather than the age of their labels. Two workers described the same facility differently. Their accounts could differ without either witness lying, she argued. A genuine investigation had to preserve that difference instead of turning it into an insult.

市政廳召開清冊遊戲的首次正式聽證會。氣氛緊張，陳玫仍堅持讓每個人都有公平發言的選項。一名野心勃勃的官員要求預設接受所有自動申領。陳玫帶來真正的收據副本，請審查者依現有用途而非標籤年代分類物品。兩名工人對同一設施的描述不同，她說這不代表其中一人必定說謊。真正的調查應保留差異，而非把差異變成侮辱。

A publisher testified that his promotion campaign had been interrupted when books were wrongly taken. A refugee family described how a donated coat had disappeared from their room. The mother remembered its perfume, while her daughter remembered the bright stocking tucked into a pocket. Neither detail was enough alone, but together they explained the coat's history. Mei was embarrassed when a clerk read the family's private address aloud. She stopped him and requested that addresses be sealed. Human dignity did not become less important simply because the game demanded evidence. They hoped to transform the hearing into a place where ordinary witnesses could be heard.

出版社代表作證說，書籍遭錯誤取走後，原定的宣傳活動被迫中斷。一戶難民家庭描述有人從住處拿走捐贈的大衣。母親記得衣上的香水味，女兒則記得口袋裡塞著鮮亮的長襪。單一細節不足以證明全部，合起來卻能說出大衣的來歷。職員當眾唸出家庭地址時，陳玫感到難堪，立刻要求密封住址。遊戲要求證據，也不能因此降低人的尊嚴。 大家希望把聽證會轉變成平凡見證人也能被聽見的地方。

The official called the public archive offensive and asked for Mei's resignation. She refused to make the hearing about her job. Instead she read the three printed review rules and explained why every silver ticket had to be tested against them. The hall had been designed for speeches, not the rescue of ordinary things, but neighbors brought their own dated records. Even officials who disliked Mei could see that her argument relied on checkable facts. The review board granted the archive thirty days to submit independent evidence on all disputed transfers.

那名官員稱公共檔案館令人反感，還要求陳玫辭職。她拒絕讓聽證會變成自己的職位爭議，轉而朗讀三條明文複核規則，說明每張銀色領物單都得受其檢驗。這座議事廳原本供人演說，而不是拯救平凡物品，但居民紛紛帶來有日期的紀錄。即使不喜歡陳玫的官員，也能看出她的論點有可查驗的事實。審查會給檔案館三十天，整理所有爭議移轉的獨立證據。

The clerk brought the printed tickets to the witnesses' table. Mei placed the family's donation receipt beside the coat notice and the publisher's delivery slips beside the warehouse claim. In each pair, a dated transaction contradicted the label 'unused.' The official asked to circulate the originals through the gallery. Mei pointed to the address still visible on the coat receipt and requested a covered copy instead. The chair ordered the clerk to record that request before continuing. A woman in the audience asked whether she could submit evidence without speaking her address into the hall. The clerk prepared a sealed envelope for her identifying details, then read only the transaction date aloud. Mei watched the process rather than the official's expression. If the thirty-day extension depended on exposing every household, many of the people who needed it would never come forward. The public evidence could travel around the room while the private information stayed with its authorized keeper.

書記把印出的申領單送到證人桌。陳玫把家庭的捐贈收據放在外套公告旁，把出版社的送貨單放在倉庫申領旁。每一組都有附日期交易，反駁「閒置」標籤。官員要求把原件傳給旁聽席，陳玫指著外套收據上仍露出的地址，要求改傳遮蓋副本。議長命書記先記下要求，再繼續。觀眾席一名女子問，能否不公開唸出住址也提交證據。書記準備密封信封保存身分資料，只朗讀交易日期。陳玫看著程序，沒看官員表情。若三十天延期得靠每家揭露私事，很多需要的人根本不會出面。公開證據可以在室內傳閱，私人資料則留在授權保管者手裡。

Outside the hall, Tao asked why a victory could feel like a loss. Mei said the hearing had not returned every object. It had only forced the game to wait while people checked its claims. Still, that waiting mattered. The archive now had a legal place to keep evidence, and the families had time to speak for themselves. Mei wrote down the deadline with a trembling hand. She had traded the comfort of a quick answer for a harder promise: before the next midnight, she would learn who had taught the city to mistake a missing label for an abandoned life.

議事廳外，陶陶問為何勝利還能讓人覺得失落。陳玫說，聽證沒有歸還所有東西，只是迫使遊戲等待大家核對。但等待本身很重要。檔案館現在有合法地方保存證據，家庭也有時間替自己說話。陳玫手微微發抖，寫下期限。她用簡單答案換了一個艱難承諾：下個午夜前，她要查出是誰教城市把失去標籤的東西誤認為遭人放棄的生活。
