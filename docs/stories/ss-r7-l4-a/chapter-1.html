沒有遺失的領物單

The Ticket That Was Not Lost

失物檔案館：城市遺忘的那一天 · 第 1 / 25 章 · 3000–4000 · 英中對照

On the first morning of the Inventory, Mei Chen found a bottle of acid beside the museum's copper counter. Someone had spilled a little of it on an old assembly notice, leaving a pale hole in the paper. She set down her broom and examined the damage. Outside, a silver square hovered above the street, announcing that every object without a registered owner would become public game property at midnight. Mei was thirty-one and worked with museum records, not weapons. She had never imagined that a missing label could turn an ordinary street into a battlefield.

「清冊遊戲」開始的第一個早晨，陳玫在博物館的銅製櫃檯旁發現一瓶酸液。有人將少量酸液灑在舊集會公告上，紙張因此破了一個泛白的小洞。她放下掃帚檢查損傷。街上浮著一塊銀色方框，宣布午夜前沒有登記主人的物品，都會變成遊戲的公共財產。三十一歲的陳玫負責博物館檔案，不會打鬥。她從未想過，少了一張標籤竟能把普通街道變成戰場。

The ministry called the new phenomenon a way to entertain the city while testing ownership. A bright slogan promised, "Find it first, keep it forever." A youthful volunteer repeated the words with a smile, but his uneasy facial expression suggested that he did not believe them. Behind him, workers tried to construct a route between two shaded doors. They would cooperate until a man wearing a shady gray badge ordered them to stop. His instructions sounded persuasive, yet they contained no explanation of who had authority to take the things people already used.

主管部門稱這個新現象是一場有趣的所有權測驗。醒目的標語寫著：「先找到，就永遠擁有。」一名年輕志工微笑著重複標語，但不安的臉部表情顯示他自己也不相信。身後的工人正努力在兩道陰暗的門之間建出一條通道。他們原本願意合作，直到一名戴著可疑灰色徽章的男子命令停工。他說得頗有說服力，卻始終沒有解釋誰有權拿走別人原本正在使用的東西。

Mei followed the noise to the old opera room. Onstage a comic actor pretended to be a coward who ran away whenever a prop fell. The audience tried to laugh, but the actor stopped when a silver ticket appeared on the curtain. It identified the room's grand piano as "unclaimed," although Mei had checked its usage only yesterday. A woman from the neighborhood still taught music there every Thursday. If the badge man could interfere with that lesson simply by naming the piano first, the game's rules were not a harmless performance.

陳玫循著聲音走進舊歌劇廳。台上一名喜劇演員假扮懦夫，每當道具掉落便轉身逃跑。觀眾勉強發笑，直到布幕上出現一張銀色領物單，演員才停下來。單上把三角鋼琴標成「無主」，但陳玫昨天才核對過它的使用紀錄。附近仍有一名女士每週四來這裡教音樂。若戴徽章的人能靠搶先報名就干涉那堂課，這場遊戲絕不是無害的表演。

She read the smaller print beneath the silver ticket. There were three rules. First, a claim needed evidence of the object's last real use. Second, two independent witnesses could freeze a transfer until review. Third, a correction entered in the public archive could be challenged but never erased. The same rules applied to every ticket, even one issued by the game itself. Mei photographed the text, wrote down the clock time, and asked the music teacher to describe her last lesson. She refused to invent details the teacher could not remember.

她讀了銀色領物單下方的細字。規則有三條。第一，領物申請必須提出物品最近一次真實使用的證據。第二，兩名互相獨立的見證人可以暫停移轉，等待複核。第三，登進公共檔案的更正可以遭到質疑，卻不能被抹除。這些規則適用於每張領物單，包括遊戲自己發出的單子。陳玫拍下文字，記下時間，請音樂老師描述最後一次授課；對方記不起的細節，她沒有擅自補上。

An elderly cleaner named Han offered to be the second witness. Mei hesitated. A signature from an employee might be dismissed as a favor, even though Han had independently seen the same lesson. She asked him to describe the sound he remembered before showing him the teacher's account. Han recalled one stubborn note that refused to stay in tune. The teacher had written exactly that complaint on a repair request. Their two descriptions agreed without being copied. The silver ticket changed from red to amber, and the piano remained in the hall.

年長的清潔員韓叔願意成為第二名見證人。陳玫有些猶豫：員工簽名容易被當成私下幫忙，儘管韓叔確實獨立目睹了同一堂課。她先請韓叔描述記得的聲音，沒有讓他看老師的說明。韓叔記起一個老是走音的音符；老師也在維修申請上寫過完全相同的抱怨。兩份敘述相符，卻不是互相抄寫。銀色領物單從紅變琥珀色，鋼琴暫時留在原處。

The teacher unlocked a drawer and spread out the Thursday lesson cards. Six families had places in that free class. Mei compared the dates with her museum register while Han pressed the worn pedal and listened to the same uneven note. She asked the teacher to keep the cards beside the piano until a reviewer arrived. Han would watch the door; Mei would copy the rule for the other teachers. The gray badge could take the room from them only if nobody answered its claim.

老師打開抽屜，攤開週四的上課卡。六個家庭的孩子在這堂免費課都有名額。陳玫把日期與博物館登記簿比對，韓叔則踩下磨損的踏板，聽見同樣不準的音。她請老師把卡片留在鋼琴旁，等候審查者。韓叔看守大門，陳玫則替其他老師抄寫規則。灰徽章的人要拿走教室，得趁無人回應申領才辦得到。

The man with the gray badge returned and called Han a coward for hiding behind paperwork. Mei did not argue about courage. She pointed to the printed rule and asked the man to give his name for the review record. He walked away without answering. No one celebrated. The teacher still had to prove what had happened to the other instruments before midnight, and many neighbors did not even know the review rule existed. Mei cleared a corner of the counter and put up a sign: PUBLIC LOST-AND-FOUND RECORDS. The first ticket in her new archive was for something that had never been lost.

戴灰色徽章的男子回來，罵韓叔是躲在文件後面的懦夫。陳玫沒有和他爭論勇氣，只指著印好的規則，請對方留下姓名以供複核。男子沒有回答便離開了。沒有人慶祝。音樂老師仍得在午夜前證明其他樂器的去向，而且許多鄰居根本不知道複核條款存在。陳玫清出櫃檯一角，掛上「公共失物檔案」的牌子。她的新檔案館收到的第一張領物單，屬於一件從來不曾遺失的物品。
