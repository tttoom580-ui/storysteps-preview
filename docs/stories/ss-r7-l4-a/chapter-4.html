不肯消失的信

The Letter That Refused to Vanish

失物檔案館：城市遺忘的那一天 · 第 4 / 25 章 · 3000–4000 · 英中對照

The municipal record office smelled of paper and cold tea. A librarian named Nila welcomed Mei with earnest concern, not applause. She said the game had started taking possession of files whenever a shelf label faded. The staff had tried to accomplish the impossible task of checking every box by hand. One young researcher had made a joke to amuse the others: perhaps the records were learning to orbit the moon. Nobody laughed after the labels on three empty drawers began to bounce like balls in a machine.

市立檔案室有紙張與冷茶的味道。圖書館員妮拉帶著真切的憂慮迎接陳玫，沒有鼓掌。她說只要架上的標籤褪色，遊戲就開始取得檔案的占有權。職員努力靠人工完成逐箱清點這項幾乎不可能的任務。某位年輕研究員曾開玩笑逗大家開心，說檔案大概正學著繞月球運行；當三個空抽屜的標籤像球一樣彈跳時，再也沒人笑得出來。

Nila showed them a letter translated from another language, written by Mei's mother twenty years earlier. It concerned a missing shipment sent through an embassy. The translator had preserved the original margin notes rather than smoothing them into a neat story. One note said, "My approval is not consent to surrender the archive." Another contained an urgent request for two independent copies. Mei felt a continual tightening in her chest. Her mother had vanished from the municipal register last winter, yet this letter sounded more like a warning than a farewell.

妮拉拿出一封二十年前由陳玫母親寫成、後來翻譯成另一種語言的信。內容與經由大使館寄送卻遺失的貨物有關。譯者保留了原文的頁邊註記，沒有把它修整成整齊的故事。其中一段寫著：「我的核准不等於同意交出檔案。」另一段緊急要求保留兩份互相獨立的副本。陳玫胸口持續收緊。母親去年冬天從市民登記簿上消失了，可這封信聽起來更像警告，不像告別。

A city officer announced that all unregistered letters might belong to a murderer who had once worked in the building. Nila called the claim nonsense. Mei was disappointed by the officer's careless language, but she did not answer with anger. She asked whether there was a specific case number. There was none. The officer was trying to make the workers passive by frightening them with a crime that no one could verify. A careful record had more value than a dramatic accusation, especially when the person accused could not answer.

一名市府職員宣布，所有未登記的信件可能屬於曾在此工作的某名殺人犯。妮拉說這根本是一派胡言。陳玫對職員草率的說法很失望，卻沒有以憤怒回應；她問是否有具體案號，得到的答案是沒有。對方想用無法查證的罪名恐嚇大家，讓人被動服從。仔細保存的紀錄比戲劇性的指控更有價值，尤其當被指控的人無法答辯。

Mei placed the letter under a clear sheet. The emphasis in her mother's handwriting fell on the word "independent." The lower margin held a tiny rhyme used by archivists to remember which copy went where. Nila had kept a record of the receiving desk, including the day the letter arrived. The clerk on duty then had been a visiting researcher, not the officer's supposed criminal. They requested a formal review of the letter's status. The game displayed "pending," which was not victory, but it prevented immediate removal.

陳玫把信放在透明保護套下。母親筆跡中最強調的是「獨立」二字。頁腳還有一段押韻短句，是檔案員用來記憶副本流向的。妮拉保留了收件櫃檯的紀錄，連信送達的日期都記著。當年值班的是一名來訪研究員，根本不是職員口中的罪犯。大家正式申請複核信件的狀態。遊戲顯示「待處理」；這不是勝利，卻能阻止信件立刻遭到移走。

Nila brought the receiving book to the table and found the letter's arrival stamp. Mei laid her photograph beside it, leaving the original beneath its clear cover. The two copy instructions matched the entries in different columns of the book. One clerk reached for a box of letters, afraid the officer would return to take it. Nila stopped him at the shelf and wrote a receipt for the box instead. No one had authorized its removal. Mei wanted to ask whether her mother had stood at this same table, but the receiving book recorded a delivery, not a visit. She copied that distinction onto the back of her photograph before helping Nila check the next drawer.

妮拉把收件簿拿到桌上，找到信件的到件印記。陳玫把照片放在旁邊，原件仍留在透明護套下。兩份副本指示與簿上不同欄位的紀錄相符。一名職員怕官員回來取走信件，伸手想搬走一箱。妮拉在架旁攔住他，改替箱子開立保管收據，因為沒人核准移動。陳玫想問母親是否也站過這張桌前，但收件簿只記錄送達，不代表本人來訪。她在照片背面寫下這個區別，再幫妮拉檢查下一格抽屜。

That evening Mei resisted the wish to carry the letter home. If she treated it as a private treasure, she might repeat the very mistake she hoped to expose. She asked Nila to store the original and permitted only a dated photograph for her own investigation. A small group began checking the other drawers, one by one. They did not recover the whole archive. They did recover a method: protect the source, record who touched it, and let different witnesses speak before deciding what their words meant.

那晚陳玫忍住把信帶回家的衝動。若她把它當成私人物件，可能正好重犯自己想揭露的錯誤。她請妮拉妥善存放原件，只為調查拍一張有日期的照片。幾名職員開始逐一核對其他抽屜。他們沒有救回整座檔案室，卻找回一套方法：保護原件、記錄經手的人，讓不同見證人先各自說話，再判斷證詞代表什麼。
