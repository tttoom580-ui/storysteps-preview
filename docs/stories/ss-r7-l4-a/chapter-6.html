走快兩次的鐘

The Clock That Counted Twice

失物檔案館：城市遺忘的那一天 · 第 6 / 25 章 · 3000–4000 · 英中對照 · 校訂中

On the anniversary of the old station's opening, a campaign arrived to collect objects labeled "unused." Mei went to examine a clock that still guided evening workers. The station conductor assured her that it rang at a regular frequency, yet its hands showed an hour that did not exist on the public schedule. The cord behind its case was coarse and had been tied recently. Someone had devised a way to make an old clock appear abandoned at the precise moment a silver claim would be issued.

舊車站開站周年那天，一場徵收「閒置物品」的活動進入站內。陳玫去檢查一座仍供晚班工人看時間的鐘。列車長向她保證，鐘聲以固定頻率響起，但指針顯示的時間卻不在公開班表上。鐘殼後的繩索粗糙，最近才被人綁過。有人設計了方法，讓一座老鐘在銀色申領單發出時看起來像已被棄置。

A diligent apprentice named Sami brought the repair records. One evaluation described a mineral coating on the gears and foam around a cracked window. Another spoke of a clock supplied to the station during the last dynasty, when workers kept time by hand. Mei warned that the dates could complicate the case, because a historic object could still have modern users. Sami showed her an imitation gear on display. It resembled the original, but its teeth were too wide to fit inside the working clock. Sami was courageous enough to admit that his first calculation had been wrong. The psychology of trust interested Mei more than the clock’s decorative case.

勤奮的學徒薩米帶來維修紀錄。其中一份評估寫著齒輪上的礦物沉積及裂窗周圍的泡棉；另一份提到時鐘是上個王朝供給車站的，當時工人仍靠手動計時。陳玫提醒大家，年代可能讓案件更複雜，因為歷史物品也可能繼續為現代人服務。薩米指著展示櫃中的仿製齒輪：它和原件相似，齒距卻太寬，無法裝進仍在運作的鐘裡。 薩米勇敢承認首次計算錯誤。陳玫對信任心理學的興趣，甚至超過時鐘外殼的裝飾。

The station union worried about lost labor if the clock disappeared. A needy porter might miss the last train, and cleaners could be blamed for leaving early. Mei asked a psychologist who worked nearby to explain why people kept trusting a clock they knew was sometimes wrong. The answer was not stupidity. People built habits around familiar sounds, particularly when the night shift left them exhausted. Mei began to reflect on a danger the game exploited: a false record could feel true when it repeated an ordinary routine.

車站工會擔心，鐘若消失，勞動排班便會受到影響。需要這份工作的搬運員可能錯過末班車，清潔員也可能被誤會提早離開。陳玫請附近一位心理師解釋，為何人們明知鐘偶爾不準仍會信它。答案不是愚蠢。人們會依熟悉的聲音建立習慣，尤其夜班結束時已經疲累。陳玫開始反思遊戲利用的一種危險：錯誤紀錄只要重複日常慣例，也會讓人覺得真實。

Sami spread the timetable across a bench and put an empty square beside every scheduled ring. Mei asked the conductor to cover the silver display so that it could not guide their count. A cleaner listened from the platform; Han waited beside the clock case. They would compare their notes only after the last train. While they prepared, a porter showed Mei the departure mark on his work card. If the false extra ring remained in the record, his ordinary departure could look late. Mei copied the card with his permission and asked the union to preserve the whole shift list. Sami then followed the new cord behind the case without pulling it. One end disappeared toward the service passage, where staff could reach it after the station closed. He marked the connection on his drawing. The clock's old gears had not suddenly forgotten the timetable; somebody had given the bell a second way to sound.

薩米把時刻表攤在長椅上，每次預定鐘響旁都畫一格。陳玫請列車長遮住銀色顯示，免得影響計數。一名清潔員在月台聽，韓叔守在鐘殼旁；末班車離站後才比對筆記。準備時，一名搬運員給陳玫看工作卡上的離站記號。若多出的假鐘響留在紀錄裡，他正常離站也可能被說成太晚。陳玫徵得同意後抄下卡片，請工會保存整份班表。薩米沿鐘殼後的新繩索查看，沒有拉動。繩子一端通往員工關站後仍能進入的勤務通道。他在圖上標出連接處。老齒輪並非忽然忘記班表，而是有人另設了敲鐘的方法。

Han volunteered to compare the bell with the recorded station timetable, while Sami counted the actual chimes. Their two accounts did not match the public display. A worker had added an extra ring after closing, which made the clock seem to have missed its final duty. Mei entered both sequences and requested a suspension. The reviewing screen turned amber. For now the clock stayed on the wall, although someone behind the silver system was still arranging errors as carefully as another person might arrange flowers.

韓叔自願把鐘聲和車站紀錄的時刻表比對，薩米則實際計數。兩份結果都和公開顯示不同。有人在關站後多加了一次鐘響，使時鐘看似沒有完成最後的任務。陳玫記下兩組順序，申請暫停移轉；複核畫面變成琥珀色。時鐘暫時留在牆上，但銀色系統背後的人仍像插花一樣仔細安排各種錯誤。
