落在錯邊的雪

Snow on the Wrong Side

失物檔案館：城市遺忘的那一天 · 第 7 / 25 章 · 3000–4000 · 英中對照 · 校訂中

A sudden frost covered the animal shelter, although the surrounding streets remained warm. The game marked the building as a competitor in its own contest, promising points to whoever removed its occupants fastest. Mei asked a veterinarian to analyze the temperature readings and demanded accuracy rather than a dramatic rescue story. A helicopter circled overhead without landing. Its crew warned that the shelter had an intense energy signature. The animals, however, were simply cold and frightened; the rule on the screen did not describe their needs.

動物收容所忽然結霜，四周街道卻依然溫暖。遊戲把建築列為自身競賽的對手，承諾誰最快移走裡面的生命，誰就能得分。陳玫請獸醫分析溫度紀錄，要求準確，而非戲劇化的救援故事。一架直升機在上空盤旋卻未降落，機組員警告收容所有強烈能量反應。然而動物只是又冷又怕；螢幕上的規則根本沒有描述牠們的需求。

The shelter manager showed Mei a diploma from her training and an old agreement promising mutual care between the city and its volunteers. She opposed handing the animals to a private collector. A frightened herd of goats pressed against the gate, while a patient mule stood apart. Tao wanted to open every pen at once, but the manager warned that a rush could injure the smaller animals. Mei asked him to adapt his plan: carry water first, then move one group through the rear corridor after counting them. A second walk around the surroundings confirmed that the ice ended at the shelter wall.

收容所長拿出受訓文憑，以及市府與志工承諾共同照護的舊協議。她反對把動物交給私人收藏者。一群受驚的山羊擠在門邊，一頭耐心的騾子獨自站著。陶陶想一次打開所有欄舍，所長卻說混亂會傷到較小的動物。陳玫請他調整方法：先送水，再清點後讓各群依序從後走廊離開。 再次檢查周遭環境後，他們確認冰層只延伸到收容所外牆。

The plumber who maintained the shelter found a slight leak in a warm-water pipe. Fixing it produced a remarkable reduction in the ice near the cages. That evidence mattered more than the glowing game score. A volunteer estimated that twenty percent of the animals had already been moved safely. Mei made her repeat the count and name the cages, because percentages could hide a missing life. The shelter's welfare depended on patience, not winning a race designed by an unknown observer.

負責維護收容所的水管工找到溫水管一處輕微漏水。修好後，籠旁的結冰顯著減少。這項證據比發亮的遊戲分數更重要。某位志工估計已有百分之二十的動物安全移走，陳玫請她重新計數、逐一列出籠號，因為百分比可能掩蓋一條失蹤的生命。收容所的福祉仰賴耐心，不是贏得一場由未知觀察者設計的競賽。

Tao carried a water pail toward the rear corridor, then stopped when two volunteers began opening pens ahead of him. He asked them to close the second gate until the first group was through. The manager counted the goats against her feeding book while the veterinarian examined the smallest animals. One volunteer held up a glowing score and urged everyone to hurry. Mei turned the screen toward the empty pen: it awarded points even before the manager confirmed that pen's occupant was safe. Tao put his pail down and helped check the pen numbers instead. At the pipe, the plumber marked the wet joint before repairing it. Mei photographed the ice line against the wall, waited, and photographed it again from the same place. The line drew back after the repair. She attached both pictures to the faulty temperature chart so the difference would remain visible when the ice itself was gone. The screen's score could not explain that change.

陶陶提著水桶往後走廊走，看見兩名志工搶先開欄門，便停下來。他請他們等第一群通過，再開第二道門。所長對著餵食簿清點山羊，獸醫檢查最小的動物。一名志工舉起發光分數，催大家加快。陳玫把螢幕轉向空欄：所長尚未確認裡面的動物安全，系統已加分。陶陶放下水桶，改幫忙核對欄號。水管工先標出潮濕接頭，才動手修理。陳玫拍下牆邊冰線，等候後又從同一處再拍。修理之後，冰線退了。她把兩張照片附在錯誤溫度圖旁，讓冰消失後仍看得到差異。螢幕分數無法解釋這個變化。

Two independent records showed that the shelter had never been abandoned: a feeding ledger and the plumber's repair receipt. The silver claim froze at sunset. Tao watched the mule settle in a dry pen and understood why the slowest animal could sometimes reveal the safest plan. Mei took no prize from the game. She took a copy of the faulty temperature chart, for its false reading seemed connected to the clock at the station. In both places someone had changed a measurement before claiming the thing it described.

兩份獨立紀錄證明收容所從未被棄置：一份餵食簿、一張水管工的維修收據。銀色申領單在日落時暫停。陶陶看著騾子安穩待在乾燥欄舍，明白有時最慢的動物反而能揭露最安全的做法。陳玫沒有領取遊戲獎品，只帶走一份錯誤溫度圖的副本。那張圖和車站時鐘似乎有關：兩處都有人先改動測量，再申領測量所描述的物品。
