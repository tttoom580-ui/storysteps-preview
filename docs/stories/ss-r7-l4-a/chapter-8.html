A distinguished craft worker named Mrs. Wu asked Mei to inspect a sewing room claimed by the game. The room had belonged to her ancestor, but Mei warned that family history alone was not defensible evidence of current ownership. Mrs. Wu accepted the point. She showed them the workshop's handwriting book, where customers had signed for alterations every week. The most recent entry was only two days old. Contrary to the silver notice, the room was neither forgotten nor empty; its tables still supported neighbors who needed their clothes repaired.

知名手工藝師吳太太請陳玫檢查被遊戲申領的裁縫室。這間屋子曾屬於她的祖先，陳玫提醒她，家族歷史本身還不足以作為現今所有權的可靠證據。吳太太接受了。她拿出顧客每週簽名取衣的筆跡簿，最新一筆就在兩天前。與銀色公告相反，房間並沒有被遺忘，也不是空的；這裡仍替鄰居修補需要使用的衣服。

The game had used identical pictures to suggest that two different sewing machines were one abandoned object. Mei asked a technician to equip both machines with numbered tags. The older machine had a scratch beneath its wheel and a hose connected to a steam press. The newer one lacked that mark. Wu felt guilt for not noticing earlier, but the technician said the error was designed to be confusing. A claim that involved two machines should never be treated as a single item merely because their photographs looked alike. A heavy crate nearly fell and could have crushed the newer machine. The technician called the labeling lousy, while Wu admitted she had misunderstood the photographs.

遊戲用了完全相同的照片，把兩台不同的縫紉機說成同一件棄置物。陳玫請技師替兩台機器裝上編號標籤。舊機器輪子下方有一道刮痕，並連著蒸氣熨燙用的軟管；新機器沒有那個痕跡。吳太太因沒早點發現而自責，技師說這個錯誤本來就是為了讓人混淆。涉及兩台機器的申領，不能只因照片相似就當成一件物品。 沉重木箱差點掉下來壓壞新機器。技師說標記做得很糟，吳太太則承認自己誤解了照片。

A customer in late pregnancy arrived for a fitting. Her visit gave the room's use a human meaning, but Mei would not treat a private medical fact as public proof. The woman offered a suggestion instead: use her dated order slip without revealing why the dress needed changes. Wu misunderstood at first and thought the woman wanted her name removed entirely. After a quiet talk, they agreed that a customer could confirm the transaction while keeping personal details hidden. Evidence did not require surrendering every secret.

一位懷孕後期的顧客來試穿衣服。她的來訪說明了房間的實際用途，但陳玫不願把私人健康狀況當作公開證據。女子改提建議：只使用有日期的訂單，不說明衣服為何需要修改。吳太太起初誤解，以為她連姓名也要完全刪除。談過之後，大家同意顧客能證明交易，卻保留個人細節。提供證據不等於交出所有秘密。

Wu brought the customer book to each numbered machine in turn. She could match an order with the older machine's scratched wheel, while a separate repair card identified the newer one. Mei copied the numbers onto different forms. The customer watched from the fitting screen and asked to see her own slip before it went into the public file. Wu covered the measurements and the note about pregnancy with plain paper. The date, service, and machine number remained visible. Mei invited her to check the copy and kept the covered original in Wu's locked drawer. Across the room, the technician held the disputed picture against the older machine. The scratch seemed to be on the wrong side. Rather than move either numbered tag, he walked around the table to inspect the reflecting surface behind it. Wu kept the machine still, and Mei cleared enough space for him to follow the belt's movement safely.

吳太太依序把顧客簿拿到兩台編號機器旁。她把一張訂單對上舊機器的刮痕輪子，另有維修卡能辨認新機器。陳玫把編號抄到不同表格。顧客在試衣屏風旁觀看，要求公開歸檔前先看自己的單據。吳太太用白紙蓋住尺寸與懷孕註記，日期、服務項目及機號仍看得見。陳玫請她核對副本，遮蓋的原件則鎖在吳太太抽屜裡。房間另一邊，技師拿爭議照片比對舊機器，刮痕似乎在錯誤的一側。他沒移動編號牌，而是繞到桌後檢查反光面。吳太太固定機器，陳玫清出空間，讓他安全追查皮帶動作。

When the machine moved, its belt gave a dull stroke against the frame. The technician followed the sound and discovered the false photograph had been taken from a mirror. The apparent riddle was less magical than it seemed: the image had been reversed before the silver ticket was issued. Mei prepared a written objection with both machine numbers. The claim paused, and Wu could launch her next class. The room would stay open while reviewers decided which copy had been changed and by whom.

機器轉動時，皮帶輕拍機架，發出沉悶的聲響。技師循聲檢查，發現錯誤照片其實是對著鏡子拍的。看似神祕的謎題並不那麼玄：銀色領物單發出之前，有人把影像左右翻轉。陳玫以兩台機器各自的編號提出書面異議。申領暫停了，吳太太得以開辦下一堂課。檔案審查者查明影像由誰更改之前，工作室可以繼續開門。
