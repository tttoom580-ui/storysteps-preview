消失的頁碼

The Missing Page Number

失物檔案館：城市遺忘的那一天 · 第 9 / 25 章 · 3000–4000 · 英中對照 · 校訂中

用互動版查詞、朗讀與收藏

Mei visited a readers' association after a silver ticket appeared on a publisher's warehouse. A bald editor gave her an autobiography written by the company's founder, who had once operated a printing press beside a fireplace. The book included a quotation from the first city archive charter. Mei's curiosity rose when she noticed that one page number jumped forward by two. The editor said a vacant shelf had appeared in the warehouse at the same moment. No one could declare that an empty shelf proved a book had never existed.

出版社倉庫出現銀色申領單後，陳玫去拜訪讀者協會。一名禿頭編輯交給她出版社創辦人所寫的自傳；當年創辦人曾在壁爐旁操作印刷機。書中引用了最早的城市檔案章程。陳玫發現頁碼突然跳過兩頁，頓時好奇起來。編輯說倉庫也在同一時刻出現空架。不能因書架空了，就宣稱那本書從未存在。

The city's weather forecast predicted rain, so the warehouse manager ordered a halt to moving cartons outside. A rusty piece of machinery stood near a delivery door, beside a box of pasta donated to night workers. Mei spotted an invoice for a lobster-shaped paperweight, a foolish purchase that nevertheless had a complete date and receipt. The less remarkable books had no such trail. Someone had removed the shipment list and left only objects unlikely to be claimed. The consequence was plain: missing records made useful things appear unused. New problems could arise if the publisher became dependent on a single missing ledger.

天氣預報說即將下雨，倉庫經理下令暫停把紙箱搬到室外。送貨門旁放著生鏽的機械，還有捐給夜班工人的義大利麵箱。陳玫注意到一張龍蝦造型紙鎮的發票；雖是古怪的採購，日期和收據倒很齊全。那些普通書籍反而沒有相同紀錄。有人移除了出貨清單，只留下不太會遭申領的東西。結果很明顯：紀錄一缺，仍有用途的物件便看似閒置。 如果出版社過度依賴唯一一本失蹤帳簿，就可能出現新問題。

A minister's portrait hung above the door, and a notice warned employees not to violate storage regulations. Mei found a different problem: the public review board had a vacancy where the readers' representative should sit. The editor thought the consequent delay would allow the game to take the whole warehouse at midnight. Mei requested that the association appoint a temporary witness and restore the missing page by comparing two legal copies. They could prove a page had been removed without pretending to know what it said.

門上掛著部長肖像，旁邊的公告警告員工不得違反倉儲規定。陳玫發現另一個問題：公共複核委員會原本屬於讀者代表的席次空缺了。編輯擔心隨之而來的拖延，會讓遊戲在午夜收走整座倉庫。陳玫請協會推派臨時見證人，再用兩份合法副本比對，復原缺頁的順序。他們可以證明頁面遭到取走，卻不能假裝知道上面原本寫什麼。

The editor took down two older public copies and opened them beside the founder's book. The same pair of numbers was absent from each printed sequence. Mei held the pages toward the window to check for torn edges, but the paper was intact. Whatever had happened to the text had entered the copies before these books were bound. She asked the editor to mark which editions they had examined and leave the missing words blank. Outside the office, a worker was already tying string around books that the game might collect. Mei asked him to keep the cartons under the roof until the warehouse review was decided. Readers had ordered those books; a vanished shipment entry did not cancel their requests. The editor fetched the current order slips, and the temporary witness counted the waiting cartons against them. They could defend the warehouse's present work while searching separately for the older damage to its records.

編輯拿下兩份較早的公共副本，與創辦人的書並排翻開。每份印刷頁碼都缺同一對數字。陳玫把紙朝窗舉起，檢查撕裂邊緣，但紙張完整。文字無論如何受損，都在這些書裝訂前就進了副本。她請編輯標出查閱版本，缺字維持空白。辦公室外，一名工人正把遊戲可能收走的書綁好。陳玫請他在倉庫複核完成前，把紙箱留在屋簷下。讀者訂了這些書，出貨登記不見，不代表訂單取消。編輯取來現行訂單，臨時見證人據此清點待運紙箱。他們能維護倉庫目前的工作，同時另查較早的檔案損害。

The editor found a carbon copy with the missing numbers, but the text had been blacked out years before the game appeared. Mei entered the finding as a limit, not a solution. The silver transfer was suspended because the warehouse's current use was clear. Yet the blank pages suggested that someone had been preparing the method for a long time. As rain struck the roof, Mei wondered whether her mother's missing registration had been handled in the same way: not by making a person vanish, but by making every official path to her records lead to a blank space.

編輯找出帶有缺失頁碼的複寫本，但文字早在遊戲出現前多年就被塗黑。陳玫把這項發現登記為限制，而不是答案。倉庫目前的使用情況明確，銀色移轉因而暫停；可空白頁顯示，或許有人很久以前就在準備這套做法。雨滴敲著屋頂，陳玫不禁想，母親的登記失蹤是否也是如此：不是讓人消失，而是讓每條通往她紀錄的官方路徑都指向空白。

Which finding mattered to the investigation?

  1. The postage record carried a name missing from the official list.
  2. The final rejection message conflicted with the two-witness rule on every ticket.
  3. The same page numbers were missing from older printed copies.
