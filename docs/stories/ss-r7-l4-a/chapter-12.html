Two Copies and a Bird

失物檔案館：城市遺忘的那一天 · 第 12 / 25 章 · 3000–4000 · 英中對照 · 校訂中

The archive's old hardware began to fail after the storm. A hawk perched outside the window while Mei and Nila checked the distribution of their backup files. The board demanded absolute accuracy, but the pair understood that perfection was unrealistic. They fastened paper labels to every storage box and practiced a simple discipline: enter each correction once, then ask another person to read it aloud. Mei inserted a damaged memory card into a reader and found a faded illustration of an oval ship carrying a public collection across the river.

風暴後，檔案館的舊硬體開始故障。一隻鷹停在窗外，陳玫和妮拉則檢查備份檔案的分布。系統要求絕對準確，但兩人明白完美並不現實。她們在每個儲存箱上固定紙標籤，練習簡單的紀律：每項更正只輸入一次，再由另一人讀出核對。陳玫把受損記憶卡插進讀卡機，找到一幅褪色插圖，畫著橢圓形的船載著公共館藏渡河。

The vessel in the picture had belonged to a miner's cooperative. A gracious elderly boatman still remembered its final crossing. He had carried boxes of books to the municipal archive, not sold them. His story offered recognition to workers whose names had been left out of the official transfer list. Mei felt grief when she saw her mother's initials in the margin. She had dreaded finding evidence of betrayal, but the initials showed only that her mother had received the boxes. They did not explain where she had gone. A realistic plan allowed for failed devices and forgotten passwords.

圖中的船屬於礦工合作社。一位和善的老船夫仍記得最後一次渡河。他載的是要送進市立檔案室的書箱，並沒有出售。這段敘述讓那些未被列在官方移轉清單上的工人重新獲得辨認。看到母親在頁邊留下的姓名縮寫時，陳玫感到悲傷。她一直害怕找到背叛的證據，但縮寫只能證明母親收過箱子，無法說明她後來去了哪裡。 現實可行的計畫必須考慮設備故障與密碼遭遺忘的情況。

Nila proposed that they renew the independent copy system rather than abandon it for a faster device. Speed was useful, but efficiency meant little if one failure could erase both copies at once. They resolved to keep one set at the museum and another at the riverside school. Mei drew a clear chart of the route, then invited the boatman to correct her assumptions. He changed two dates and refused to guess at a third. That refusal made the evidence stronger, because every blank space was visible instead of hidden.

妮拉主張更新雙份獨立副本制度，而不是為了更快的設備把它丟掉。速度有用，但若一次故障便能抹去兩份資料，效率也失去意義。兩人決定一份放博物館，另一份存河邊學校。陳玫畫出清楚的路線圖，請船夫修正她的假設。船夫改了兩個日期，第三個則拒絕猜測。這種拒絕讓證據更有力量，因為空白被保留下來，而不是遭到掩蓋。

The reader made a clicking sound and the ship picture broke into gray squares. Mei took her hand off the card at once. Nila wrote down the error message, then asked the schoolteacher to bring the paper transfer list instead of trying the card repeatedly. The boatman leaned over that list and recognized the names of two workers who had carried boxes beside him. Mei recorded his corrections on a separate sheet, keeping the damaged image unchanged. They could not risk losing the workers a second time to a failed device. Han took the first paper copy to the museum while the teacher prepared a dry shelf at the school. Before they separated, Nila read both destination labels aloud and Mei compared them with the route chart. The eagle outside lifted away as the boatman pointed across the river toward the landing. His account now had a place in two buildings, where neither a broken reader nor a missing password could erase it alone.

讀卡機喀喀響，船的圖像裂成灰色方格。陳玫立刻把手移開。妮拉記下錯誤訊息，請學校老師帶來紙本移轉清單，不再反覆測試記憶卡。船夫俯身看清單，認出兩名曾和他一起搬箱的工人。陳玫把他的更正記在另一張紙上，受損影像保持原狀。他們不能讓故障設備再使工人失去一次姓名。韓叔把第一份紙本送去博物館，老師在學校準備乾燥書架。分開前，妮拉唸出兩份目的地標籤，陳玫與路線圖比對。窗外的鷹飛走，船夫指向河對岸碼頭。他的說法如今存於兩棟建築，單靠讀卡機損壞或密碼遺忘，無法一併抹去。

That night a silver notice tried to claim the damaged memory card as electronic waste. The schoolteacher and Nila filed separate statements about its use. The transfer paused. More important, Mei now had evidence that her mother had once designed the very safeguard the Inventory was struggling to defeat. The memory card contained no hidden weapon, only a list of ordinary people who had agreed to keep one another's records safe. Mei copied their names carefully. They might be the missing witnesses she needed later.

當晚，銀色公告試圖把受損記憶卡當成電子廢棄物申領。學校教師與妮拉各自提交使用證明，移轉暫停。更重要的是，陳玫終於有證據顯示，母親曾參與設計清冊遊戲正企圖突破的保護措施。記憶卡沒有藏武器，只有一串平凡人的姓名；他們曾答應互相保存紀錄。陳玫仔細抄下名字。未來需要見證人時，也許就得靠這些人。
