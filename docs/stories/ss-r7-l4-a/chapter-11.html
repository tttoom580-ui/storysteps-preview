暴風中的收據

The Storm Receipt

失物檔案館：城市遺忘的那一天 · 第 11 / 25 章 · 3000–4000 · 英中對照 · 校訂中

用互動版查詞、朗讀與收藏

A hurricane struck the outer harbor while the city debated the Inventory. An official used the chaos to accuse the public archive of blocking relief commerce. He boasted that a private company could manufacture supplies faster than volunteers. Mei asked for assurance that donated materials would reach the families named on the shipping lists. The company offered a polished convention of signatures but no consistent delivery records. Without those records, the game could declare a shipment unused the moment it crossed a warehouse door.

颶風襲擊外港時，城裡還在辯論清冊遊戲。一名官員趁混亂指控公共檔案館阻礙救災物資交易，誇耀私人公司比志工更能快速生產用品。陳玫要求保證：捐贈物資必須送到出貨清單上列出的家庭。公司拿出一套漂亮的簽名慣例，卻沒有一致的交貨紀錄。沒有交貨證明，遊戲就能在貨物通過倉庫門口時把它判作無人使用。

A scientist delivered an informative note about a damaged hydrogen tank on the pier. Mei did not pretend to understand its engineering, so she sent it to the emergency crew. The crew called the risk troublesome but manageable if nobody touched the equipment. Han carried drinking water to a shelter beneath a skyscraper. A loyal shopkeeper offered to contribute blankets. The disaster was real; it did not need a false game notice to become frightening. Mei wanted to expose the paperwork trick without delaying help to people who were already cold.

一位科學家送來說明碼頭氫氣槽受損的資料。陳玫沒有假裝理解工程細節，而是把資訊交給緊急救援隊。隊員說風險麻煩，但只要無人碰設備就能管理。韓叔把飲用水送往摩天大樓下的避難所，忠實的店主也願意捐出毛毯。災害本來就真實，不必靠假的遊戲公告製造恐懼。陳玫想揭露文件手法，卻不能因此延誤受寒者的救援。

Ignorance would have been easy to hide behind official titles. Instead Mei asked the shelter nurses to keep their own receipts. The supplies arrived in four loads, and each person receiving a box wrote the date and quantity. The records could not prevent every misfortune, but they allowed recovery to be checked later. The company could not simply claim its trucks had delivered everything. The nurses showed resistance when a manager tried to collect all the records. They handed him copies and kept the originals in a dry cupboard.

官職很容易成為掩蓋無知的盾牌。陳玫反而請避難所護理師自行保存收據。物資分四批送達，每位收箱的人都記下日期與數量。紀錄不能阻止所有不幸，卻能讓之後的復原工作有跡可查。公司不能只說貨車已送完一切。經理要收走所有文件時，護理師提出抵抗：她們只交副本，把原件保存在乾燥櫃子裡。

At the shelter door, Han found a nurse comparing her receipt with the driver's list. The numbers differed. They counted the stacked water boxes again without consulting each other's totals, then called the second receiving team. That team had also signed for fewer boxes than the shipping office claimed to have sent. Mei made a copy of each count and left the drinking water where families could reach it. She would not hold the supplies while the company argued over its ledger. A manager tried to place a silver collection ticket on the blankets, pointing to the missing delivery record. The nurse opened the dry cupboard and produced her own original. Han kept the cupboard door clear while Mei asked the manager to name the loads he had personally seen. He could not account for the missing boxes. The new discrepancy went into the relief record with the driver's statement attached, so emergency crews could trace it without interrupting the shelter's next distribution.

收容站門口，韓叔看見護士正拿收據與司機清單比對，數量不同。他們不看彼此總數，重新清點堆好的飲用水箱，再聯絡第二個收貨小組。那組簽收的箱數，也比運送辦公室宣稱出貨的少。陳玫各留一份副本，飲用水仍放在家庭取用得到的地方；公司爭論帳簿時，她不會扣住物資。經理指著缺失送貨紀錄，想把銀色收取單貼到毛毯上。護士打開乾燥櫥櫃，拿出自己的原件。韓叔保持櫃門暢通，陳玫則請經理說出親眼見過哪幾車。他無法交代缺少的箱子。新的數量差異連同司機證詞一起歸入救濟紀錄，讓緊急人員追查時，不致妨礙下一次發放。

At midnight the game tried to transfer the remaining blankets to the company. Mei presented two independent counts from the shelter and the delivery crew. The transfer froze. She went home without the relaxation she had promised herself, but the public record now showed which boxes had arrived and which were still missing. Tao asked whether the storm had made their work more important. Mei answered that it had made their mistakes more costly. A careful archive should protect people during an emergency, not turn their fear into an opportunity for someone else.

午夜時，遊戲試圖把剩餘毛毯移轉給公司。陳玫提出避難所和送貨隊各自製作的清點紀錄，移轉便暫停了。她回家時沒能得到原本答應自己的休息，但公共紀錄已能指出哪些箱子送達、哪些仍不見蹤影。陶陶問風暴是否讓工作更重要。陳玫說，風暴讓錯誤的代價更大。謹慎的檔案館應在緊急時保護人，而非把人們的恐懼變成他人的機會。
