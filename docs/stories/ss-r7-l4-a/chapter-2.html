The Name Under the Collar

The next claim concerned a dog's collar. A child named Tao brought it to Mei with an elastic band tied around the buckle and a tiny paw carved into the metal. The game listed the animal as "property without an owner." Tao said his grandmother, an immigrant who ran a small bakery, had adopted the dog before he was born. She even kept an autograph from a famous dog trainer beside the old vaccination card, but the silver screen would accept only a current digital initial. Mei wondered who had decided that a signature written by hand was less trustworthy than a letter entered by a stranger.

第二件遭申領的東西是一條狗項圈。名叫陶陶的孩子把項圈拿來，上面扣著彈性帶，金屬片還刻有小小的爪印。遊戲把那隻狗列作「無主財產」。陶陶說，祖母是經營麵包店的移民，早在他出生前就領養了牠。她甚至在舊疫苗卡旁夾著一位知名訓犬師的親筆簽名，但銀色螢幕只肯接受目前的數位姓名縮寫。陳玫想知道，究竟是誰規定手寫簽名比陌生人輸入的字母更不可信。

A prominent merchant from the upper district arrived with two assistants. He said the dog would prosper in his garden and offered Tao money for the convenience of ending the dispute. His polished manner could not disguise the cruelty of treating a living animal like a prize. When Mei asked for evidence of his last contact with the dog, he made a rude remark about poor families. Tao's grandmother worked the evening shift and was not there to answer. Mei told the merchant that being busy was not proof of abandonment. Mei added a prevention note: a frozen claim did not permit anyone to move an animal without its caretaker.

上城區一位知名商人帶著兩名助手到場，說狗住進他的花園會過得更好，還提出給陶陶一筆錢，方便結束爭議。他舉止體面，卻無法掩飾把生命當作獎品的殘酷。陳玫請他提出最近接觸那隻狗的證據，他反而對貧困家庭說了難聽的話。陶陶的祖母值晚班，無法立刻到場。陳玫提醒商人，忙於工作不代表拋棄寵物。 陳玫補上預防說明：申領暫停期間，任何人都不得未經照護者同意移動動物。

Han showed Tao the printed timetable of the bakery deliveries. The dog walked with his grandmother before her first shift and slept by the back door afterward. A small camera had captured the routine. Mei warned that the recording might prevent a wrong claim, but it could not by itself prove the grandmother's identity. The child suggested asking a neighbor who always left water outside. That neighbor had no reason to favor the merchant or the family. Her careful feedback named the dog's favorite corner and the missing bell on its collar.

韓叔拿出麵包店送貨的時刻表。每天第一班前，狗都陪祖母散步，之後睡在後門邊。一台小相機拍過這段日常。陳玫提醒大家，影像有助預防錯誤申領，卻不能單憑它證明祖母的身分。陶陶建議找一位總在門外留水的鄰居。那位鄰居沒有理由偏袒商人或陶家。她仔細回饋，說出狗最愛待的角落，還提到項圈上少了一顆鈴鐺。

The merchant grew explosive when the review changed the ticket to amber. He claimed that challenging his privilege would ruin the neighborhood economy. Mei did not accuse him of mischief; she simply asked whether either assistant had seen the dog before this morning. Neither had. For the first time, Tao noticed the difference between people who repeated the same story and witnesses who could describe separate details. The game was not generous or cruel on its own. People could use its gaps for either purpose, and those choices needed names.

領物單轉為琥珀色時，商人勃然大怒，聲稱挑戰他的特權會毀掉地方經濟。陳玫沒有指控他惡作劇，只問兩名助手是否在今天早上之前看過那隻狗。兩人都沒有。陶陶第一次看懂：同聲複述故事的人，和能各自描述不同細節的見證人，並不相同。遊戲本身不會慷慨或殘酷；人們能利用它的漏洞做出不同選擇，而選擇應當留下姓名。

Tao turned the collar over on the counter. Its damaged bell loop had a rough edge which his grandmother had covered with the elastic band. The neighbor remembered bringing the band from her kitchen. Mei drew the broken loop and attached the drawing to the statement; the family photographs could stay in Tao's pocket. He chose one picture that showed the collar alone and refused to copy the others. Han put a bowl of water beneath the counter, where the dog could wait without becoming a display for the merchant's assistants.

陶陶把項圈翻到背面，放在櫃檯上。破損的鈴鐺環邊緣粗糙，祖母才用橡皮筋包住。鄰居記得那條橡皮筋是她從廚房拿來的。陳玫畫下斷環，附在證詞旁；家庭照片仍可留在陶陶口袋裡。他挑了一張只拍到項圈的照片，同意複印，其他的都不願公開。韓叔在櫃檯下放了一碗水，讓狗能等候，不必成為商人助手圍觀的展品。

When the grandmother arrived, the dog ran straight into her arms. Tao smiled, then stopped. A new silver notice appeared above the bakery: "Building entrance under review." The merchant's assistants had filed another claim while everyone watched the collar. Mei saw that winning a single case had not ended the threat; it had merely exposed a method. She asked Tao and his grandmother whether she could keep copies of their records. Both agreed, but only after Mei promised that the images would remain private unless they chose otherwise.

祖母趕到後，狗立刻撲進她懷裡。陶陶笑了，卻又停住：麵包店上方浮出新的銀色公告，寫著「建築入口待複核」。大家看項圈時，商人的助手已替店門另提申請。陳玫明白，贏下一案沒有消除威脅，只是揭露對方的方法。她徵求陶陶與祖母的同意，想保存紀錄副本。兩人同意，但陳玫必須承諾，除非他們自行決定，影像不會對外公開。
