Seven Bells at Six

魔法便利商店，今天正式營業 · 第 1 / 25 章 · 1000–2000 · 英中對照 · 校訂中

用互動版查詞、朗讀與收藏

At six in the morning, Nina lifted the blue shutter of the little shop her grandmother had left her. The brass bell over the door rang seven times, although she had only touched it once. Mochi, the paper cat who kept the accounts, stretched beside the register and reminded her of the rule: one completed and checked order meant one star, and five stars allowed one useful upgrade. Today the first guest was a cloud-field messenger with wet feathers. The guest asked for a sealed jar of sunrise sugar. Nina took the request seriously. Each visitor carried a whole world beyond the door, and a wrong purchase could follow that person home.

早上六點，妮娜拉起奶奶留給她的小店藍色鐵門。門上的黃銅鈴明明只被碰了一下，卻連響七聲。負責記帳的紙貓麻糬在收銀機旁伸懶腰，提醒她一條規則：完成且經確認的一筆訂單得到一顆星；集滿五顆才可解鎖一項實用升級。今天第一位客人是一名羽毛濕透的雲野信使。客人想買一罐封好的日出糖。妮娜認真看待這份委託。每位客人都把門外的一整個世界帶進店裡，買錯東西也可能把問題帶回家。

The receipt turned pale yellow when Nina asked about the real difficulty. The answer was clear: the sugar would lose its light in ordinary rain. A small line of writing appeared under the total. It named the materials, the time required, and the person who would take responsibility if the order failed. Nina had once believed that magic made trade easy. Her grandmother’s rules taught the opposite. Magic could open a door or cool a box; it could not make a stranger agree, replace lost time, or hide an unpaid cost. “We can refuse this order,” she told the guest. “Or we can change the plan until it is safe and fair.”

妮娜問起真正的困難時，收據變成淡黃色。答案很清楚：普通雨水會讓糖失去光芒。總額下面浮現一行小字，列出材料、所需時間，以及訂單失敗時由誰負責。妮娜曾以為魔法會讓交易變簡單，奶奶的規則卻教她相反的道理。魔法能打開門、讓箱子降溫；卻不能強迫陌生人同意、補回失去的時間，或藏起沒付的成本。「這筆訂單我們可以拒絕，」她對客人說，「也可以改變計畫，直到它安全又公平。」

Behind the counter stood three shelves that had not changed since Grandma Ada’s last winter. One held simple food, one held tools, and the last held empty boxes waiting for new use. Nina could not order a miracle by shouting at them. She had to choose a real action: build a covered cart from discarded boxes. Kai asked whether the visitor agreed with that approach. After a short pause, the guest nodded and helped list the materials already available at home. Mochi marked borrowed things with a return date. That little mark mattered. It kept help from turning into a debt nobody had wanted to accept.

櫃臺後的三座貨架，從艾達奶奶最後一個冬天起就沒變過。一架放簡單食物，一架放工具，最後一架放等待再利用的空箱子。妮娜不能對它們大喊就憑空叫出奇蹟。她必須採取真正的行動：用回收紙箱做一輛有頂的小推車。凱問客人是否同意這個方法。短暫停頓後，客人點頭，協助列出家裡已有的材料。麻糬替借用物品標上歸還日期。這個小記號很重要，能避免幫助變成沒人願意承擔的債。

Nina took a careful approach to her first delivery. From the balcony she could see a windy field beyond the street, although the shop stood at the base of an ordinary apartment block. Her grandmother’s belief was that small help could make a nation better, one customer at a time. This was a challenge Nina would not deny. She wanted to develop a fair way to discuss costs with every visitor. For instance, the male gate operator explained that a secondary road ran beside an old subway station; using it might save speed, but it crossed a playground with a seesaw. The traditional route was slower. “Tradition is not always right,” Kai said, “but a fast cart near children is not right either.” Nina chose the safe road and told the messenger why. A bright order was useless if somebody got hurt delivering it.

妮娜小心思考第一次送貨的方法。從陽臺望去，她看見街道之外多風的田野，雖然商店就位於普通公寓大樓的底層。奶奶相信，小小的幫助能讓一個國家逐漸變得更好；妮娜不願拒絕這項挑戰。她想建立公平的方法，和每位客人討論成本。例如，男性傳送門操作員解釋，舊地下鐵車站旁有條次要道路，走那裡速度較快，卻會經過設有翹翹板的遊樂場。傳統路線雖慢，卻比較安全。「傳統不一定永遠正確，」凱說，「但讓快車從孩子旁邊衝過去也不對。」妮娜選擇安全路線，並向信使說明原因。如果運送途中有人受傷，再明亮的訂單也失去意義。

The strange part of the work began when the world door changed color. It did not mean the order was ready; it only meant that a route could be opened for a short time. Nina refused to hurry just because the light looked beautiful. Instead, she asked Kai to check the materials and followed the plan: build a covered cart from discarded boxes. Mochi added the return date and counted the money aloud. The guest could see exactly what was being offered. A child outside pressed a nose against the window, but Nina kept the delivery path clear. The spell would end on time; a careless mistake might last for years.

工作最奇怪的時刻，是世界之門忽然變色。但那不代表訂單已準備好，只表示路線可短暫打開。妮娜沒有因光芒美麗就急著動手，而是請凱核對材料，依計畫進行：用回收紙箱做一輛有頂的小推車。麻糬補上歸還日期，大聲算清金額。客人看得見自己究竟得到什麼。外面一個孩子把鼻子貼在玻璃上，妮娜仍保持配送路線暢通。法術會準時結束；粗心留下的錯誤卻可能延續多年。

Before agreeing that the work was done, Nina asked the guest one final question: would this solution still help after the bright door disappeared? The visitor thought carefully and confirmed that the messenger carried the bright sugar home before dusk. The answer mattered more than the shine on the receipt. The star appeared only after the customer signed and the return materials were counted. Mochi made a delighted sound like a page turning. Kai carried the unused boxes back to the shelf. Nina enjoyed the moment, but she also felt tired. Running a shop meant giving attention to many small duties, even on a day when magic seemed to do most of the work.

在同意工作完成前，妮娜最後問客人一個問題：當發亮的大門消失後，這個方法還有用嗎？客人仔細思考，確認：信使在黃昏前把發亮的糖帶回家。答案比收據的光更重要。客人簽名、退回材料也清點完成後，星星才浮現。麻糬發出像翻頁般的高興聲音。凱把沒用完的箱子搬回貨架。妮娜享受這一刻，也感到疲累。經營商店意味著照顧許多小責任，即使那天看起來大部分工作都由魔法完成。

The last customer waved from beyond the world door. Nina waved back, then shut the register and wrote down one lesson she did not want to forget. A useful store was built from repeated choices, not a single wonderful spell. As she closed her notebook, she noticed a missing page marked Order Zero. Kai said the mark might be important, but they should compare it with other evidence before making an accusation. Nina liked that answer. They were learning to work together without guessing at other people’s motives. Outside, the ordinary streetlights came on. Inside, the shop kept its small promise: the doors would open again at six, ready for another visitor.

最後一名客人從世界之門另一邊揮手，妮娜也揮手回應。接著她關上收銀機，記下今天不想忘記的一個教訓：有用的商店靠每天反覆做出的選擇，而不是單一驚天法術。合上筆記本時，她發現一張標示「零號訂單」的缺頁。凱說這個記號也許重要，但指控任何人以前，應先和其他證據比對。妮娜喜歡這個答案。兩人正在學習不亂猜別人的動機，也能一起工作。外頭亮起普通路燈。店內則繼續守著小小承諾：明早六點再次開門，迎接下一名旅人。
