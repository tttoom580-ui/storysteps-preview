酸酸的燈籠

The Lantern that Tasted Sour

魔法便利商店，今天正式營業 · 第 2 / 25 章 · 1000–2000 · 英中對照 · 校訂中

用互動版查詞、朗讀與收藏

The shop opened at the same hour every day, even when the sky outside belonged to a different season. Nina liked that small piece of order. She was nineteen, and the shop deed had seemed much too big for her when she first held it. Now the door gave a gentle knock, and a deer keeper from the Lantern Wood entered. The visitor wanted a lantern filled with sweet moon oil. Mochi rolled an empty receipt toward Nina with one paw. No price appeared until both sides explained what was being bought. Nina pulled out a chair, offered water, and asked the guest to start from the beginning instead of rushing to the end.

商店每天都在同一時間開門，即使門外天空屬於不同季節也一樣。妮娜喜歡這一點小小的秩序。她十九歲，第一次拿到店契時，還覺得責任大得難以承受。如今門輕輕響了，一名來自燈籠森林的養鹿人走進來。客人想要一盞裝滿甜月油的燈籠。麻糬用爪子推來一張空白收據。雙方說清楚交易內容之前，上面不會出現價格。妮娜拉開椅子，送上水，請客人從頭說起，不要急著跳到結論。

Nina placed the request under a small glass weight so a sudden breeze would not blow it away. The problem was not hidden: a batch of oil had turned sour and scared the forest birds. Mochi wrote a cost beside each possible answer, then crossed out the choices that would take another customer’s goods without permission. “You cannot pay for one family with another family’s loss,” the cat said. Kai nodded. Nina repeated the request in her own words and asked the visitor to correct her if she was wrong. Only after receiving a clear answer did she move to the shelf where the old tools were kept.

妮娜用小玻璃紙鎮壓住訂單，以免忽然起風把它吹走。問題一點也不隱晦：一批油變酸，嚇壞了森林裡的鳥。麻糬在每個可能的解法旁寫下成本，劃掉那些未經同意就要拿走其他客人物品的方案。「不能用另一個家庭的損失，替這個家庭付錢，」紙貓說。凱點頭。妮娜用自己的話重新說一次委託，請客人糾正任何錯誤。得到清楚回答後，她才走到保存舊工具的貨架前。

Nina opened her grandmother’s notebook to a page headed “Try Small First.” The note was written in plain ink, not magic letters. It warned shopkeepers never to test a dangerous idea on a customer without permission. Nina read it aloud, then suggested this plan: compare the old and new recipes before buying supplies. Kai checked the door and made sure there would be a clear way back if the attempt failed. The guest added one useful fact that had not been on the original receipt. Nina wrote it down. Even after many successful days, she still believed a customer’s knowledge of home could be more important than a shopkeeper’s special tools.

妮娜打開奶奶的筆記本，翻到寫著「先小規模試做」的一頁。那是普通墨水寫的字，不是魔法文字。筆記警告店員，未經同意不能拿顧客測試危險點子。妮娜大聲讀完，提出計畫：先比對新舊配方，再採購材料。凱檢查門，確認若失敗也有清楚的返回路線。客人補充了原收據沒有的一項有用事實，妮娜立刻記下。即使已成功工作許多天，她仍相信客人對家鄉的了解，有時比店裡的特殊工具更重要。

The keeper held his lantern at an angle so Nina could spot a dark mark under its glass. “I appreciate your care,” he said, “but please avoid the wrong oil.” A barber from his village had once bought an old edition of the forest guide. On its first board were pictures of a deer and a dove. Those animals still exist in the wood, yet a huge new sign seemed to erase them. A minority of local families had asked the transport organization to keep their path open. Its president promised to listen, but the sour oil gave children painful headaches. Nina opened a particular textbook and learned the truth: the oil needed cool storage, not stronger fire. The keeper sat quietly, then admitted that he had trusted the expensive label without asking what was inside.

養鹿人把燈籠斜拿著，讓妮娜看清玻璃下方的暗色斑點。「謝謝妳這麼仔細，」他說，「但請避免用錯油。」村裡一位理髮師曾買過舊版森林指南。第一塊指示板上畫著鹿和鴿子。那些動物仍存在於森林中，新的巨大標誌卻似乎抹去了牠們。少數當地家庭曾要求交通組織保留通道；主席答應傾聽，但變酸的燈油已讓孩子頭痛難受。妮娜打開一本特定的教科書，終於找到真相：油需要低溫保存，而不是更強的火。養鹿人安靜坐下，承認自己只相信昂貴標籤，卻沒問裡頭是什麼。

The first test did not look impressive. A box stood on a chair, Kai counted the minutes, and Mochi watched the doorway as if it were a sleeping animal. Nina followed the agreed plan: compare the old and new recipes before buying supplies. Something went a little wrong, as things often did in a real store. She stopped and asked what had changed before trying again. The customer offered an idea from home, and Nina was willing to use it after one more safe check. They did not need to pretend the first attempt had been perfect. The written record would be more useful tomorrow if it included the mistake as well as the answer.

第一次測試一點也不華麗。椅子上放著箱子，凱計時，麻糬像盯著睡著的動物那樣守著門口。妮娜照約定計畫做：先比對新舊配方，再採購材料。和真實商店的工作一樣，途中出了點小錯。她停下來問清楚改變了什麼，再試第二次。客人提出家鄉的方法，妮娜在追加一次安全檢查後願意採用。大家不必假裝首次嘗試很完美。紀錄若同時寫下錯誤與答案，明天才會更有用。

By the time the bell announced the late afternoon, the store smelled of warm paper and fresh tea. Nina did not count the order as finished merely because it looked good from her side of the counter. The customer checked the result: a smaller lantern burned safely beside the deer path. Kai waited for a real answer, and Mochi held the receipt still until the visitor said the work met the agreed conditions. Only then did a tiny star appear on its corner. Nina placed the star in the ledger beside the cost and the time spent. It was not an endless reward. Five checked stars could buy only one upgrade, and that upgrade would still need care and supplies.

下午較晚時門鈴再次響起，店裡已有暖紙和新茶的香氣。妮娜不會只因櫃臺這邊看起來不錯，就算訂單完成。客人親自確認結果：較小的燈籠安全地照亮了鹿徑。凱等待真正的答覆，麻糬穩穩壓著收據，直到客人說成果符合約定條件。這時收據角落才浮現小星星。妮娜把星星記入帳簿，旁邊寫上成本和所花時間。它不是無限獎勵。五顆通過驗收的星只能換一項升級，而且升級後仍需要維護和材料。

After closing, Nina opened the old ledger to leave a note for the next morning. Between two ordinary receipts she noticed something that had not been there before: the oil invoice carried a private company seal. She showed it to Kai instead of hiding it. Together they decided to copy the page, check its date, and ask the next visitor whether anyone else had seen the same mark. Nina switched off the window light, but one of the seven bells rang softly in the dark. She remembered Grandma Ada saying that the store was not meant to make one person rich. It existed so travelers could return home with what they truly needed.

打烊後，妮娜打開舊帳簿，想替明早留下備忘。在兩張普通收據之間，她注意到一項以前沒有的線索：油品發票蓋著私人公司的印章。她沒有藏起來，而是拿給凱看。兩人決定先影印頁面、核對日期，再問下一位旅人是否見過相同記號。妮娜關掉櫥窗燈，七個鈴鐺中卻有一個在黑暗中輕輕響起。她想起艾達奶奶說過，商店不是為了讓某個人致富，而是讓旅人能帶著真正需要的東西回家。
