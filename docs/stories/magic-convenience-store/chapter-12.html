空白收據

The Blank Receipt

魔法便利商店，今天正式營業 · 第 12 / 25 章 · 1000–2000 · 英中對照 · 校訂中

用互動版查詞、朗讀與收藏

Nina had learned that a magical shop needed ordinary habits. Before touching the glowing register, she washed her hands, opened the account book, and checked the return shelf. Then the bell announced a countryside tea maker whose hands shook. The request sounded simple: a measured set of teapots for a festival. But customers from other worlds often used a familiar word for a very unfamiliar thing. Kai, the young courier who helped with deliveries, waited near the door with his boots still wet from the morning streets. Nina asked him to stay for the explanation. She had promised herself never to make a decision about somebody else’s home without first hearing that person speak.

妮娜已學會魔法商店也需要普通習慣。碰發亮的收銀機前，她先洗手、打開帳冊，再檢查退貨架。接著門鈴宣布一名雙手發抖的鄉間製茶人到來。委託聽起來很簡單：供節慶使用的一組定量茶壺。但其他世界的客人，常用熟悉的詞指陌生的東西。幫忙配送的年輕信使凱，還穿著被早晨街道弄濕的靴子，在門邊等候。妮娜請他留下聽解釋。她已答應自己，在沒聽當事人說話前，絕不替別人的家鄉做決定。

The customer did not hide the most difficult part of the request: the customer had paid a deposit but received a blank bill. Nina was grateful for that honesty. The store could promise a careful effort, but never an impossible result. She checked the receipt twice, once for the visible price and once for a small silver mark that meant a hidden condition. The mark appeared whenever somebody tried to make a decision for another person. Kai moved a stool closer to the counter so the guest could watch every step. “If we are going to solve this,” Nina said, “you will know what we do and why.”

客人沒有隱瞞委託中最棘手的部分：客人付了訂金，拿到的卻是空白帳單。妮娜很感謝這份坦誠。商店可以保證認真努力，卻不能保證不可能的結果。她把收據看了兩次，一次查明面上的價格，一次尋找代表隱藏條件的小銀印。只要有人替別人做決定，這個標記就會出現。凱把凳子拉近櫃臺，讓客人能看清每一步。「我們若要解決它，」妮娜說，「你會知道我們做了什麼，以及原因。」

The shop had no team of famous wizards. It had Nina, Kai, a paper cat, and a drawer full of pencil ends. That was enough to begin a good question. They drew the route of the order on brown paper, placing a dot where somebody might wait or need help. Next to the drawing Nina wrote the proposed action: weigh the teapots, rebuild the account, and give the buyer a copy. They checked how much could be done with materials they already owned, and which part needed a fresh purchase. Nobody was allowed to add a surprise fee after the guest had agreed. When the list was ready, Nina put it where everyone could read it.

商店沒有一隊知名巫師，只有妮娜、凱、一隻紙貓，和一抽屜快用完的鉛筆。這樣也足夠提出好問題。他們在牛皮紙上畫出訂單路線，把可能有人等待或需要幫助的地方點出來。圖旁寫上計畫：秤好茶壺、重建帳目，再給買家一份副本。大家核對哪些材料已有、哪些需要新買。客人同意後，任何人都不能再突然加收費用。清單準備好，妮娜就把它放在人人都看得到的地方。

Nina refused to blame the nervous customer for the blank bill. The tea maker came from the countryside near the capital, where every customer needed a clear warning about danger. A dentist who had brought a teapot for repair gave them the right direction to the old records office. Kai had bought fries for lunch but could hardly eat while they checked every ink line. They asked for a measurement of the pots and showed regard for the maker’s work. “I respect that you are scared,” Nina said, “but we can settle the payment in public.” A sudden knock came from the door. A visitor wearing one slipper held a note proving that the deposit had already been paid. The tiny pudding on the counter was not part of the order; it was a thank-you gift, and Nina put it aside until the matter was settled. Nina asked Kai to measure each pot again.

妮娜不願把空白帳單的錯怪給緊張的客人。製茶人來自首都附近的鄉村，那裡每位客人都需要清楚的危險提醒。一名帶茶壺來維修的牙醫指出前往舊紀錄室的正確方向。凱買了薯條當午餐，卻因核對每條墨水紀錄而幾乎吃不下。大家測量茶壺，尊重製茶人的勞動。「我知道妳害怕，也尊重妳的感受，」妮娜說，「但我們可以公開結清付款。」突然有人敲門。穿著一隻拖鞋的訪客帶來紙條，證明訂金早已付清。櫃臺上的小布丁不是訂單的一部分，而是謝禮；妮娜先把它放好，直到事情處理完。妮娜請凱再次測量每個茶壺。

Kai prepared a simple test area while Nina handled the item itself. Mochi checked the receipt against the supply shelf. For a moment there was only the sound of paper moving and feet crossing the wooden floor. Then they carried out the plan: weigh the teapots, rebuild the account, and give the buyer a copy. The guest watched closely and stopped Nina once to explain a custom from home. Nina thanked the visitor and adjusted her work. A magical order was not a contest in which the shopkeeper always knew best. If a change saved effort and remained safe, it belonged in the record. By afternoon they had enough evidence to finish the delivery, although they still needed a final check.

凱準備簡單的測試區，妮娜負責商品本身。麻糬把收據與供貨架核對。那一刻，只聽得到紙張移動和腳步踏過木地板的聲音。接著大家執行計畫：秤好茶壺、重建帳目，再給買家一份副本。客人仔細觀看，中途請妮娜停下來，解釋家鄉的一項習俗。妮娜道謝並調整工作。魔法訂單不是店員永遠最懂的比賽。只要改變能省力又安全，就該寫進紀錄。到了下午，他們已有足夠證據完成配送，但仍需要最後確認。

No star appeared at first. Nina felt a chill of fear, but Mochi pointed to a small part of the receipt they had not checked. They read it together and asked the visitor to look at the completed order once more. The customer confirmed that the maker recovered the missing deposit after a public check. A silver line turned gold, and the star finally arrived. Nina laughed in relief. “The register is strict,” Kai said. “That is better than a machine that rewards us for promises we never keep.” Nina wrote the final cost under the order number and returned the unused materials to their proper places. She was beginning to understand why Grandma Ada had trusted such a slow method.

一開始沒有星星出現，妮娜不禁有點害怕。麻糬指出收據上還有個細項未檢查。大家一起閱讀，再請客人看一次成品。客人確認：製茶人經公開核對取回失蹤的訂金。銀線轉成金色，星星終於出現。妮娜鬆了一口氣笑出來。「收銀機很嚴格，」凱說，「總比獎勵空話的機器好。」妮娜把最後成本記在訂單編號下，將沒用到的材料放回正確位置。她開始理解艾達奶奶為什麼相信這種緩慢的方法。

When the door finally locked, Nina counted the day’s supplies again. The shop had earned one honest star, but it had also used time, paper, and careful attention. That balance felt real. As she placed the receipt beside Grandma Ada’s handwriting, a new detail caught her eye: the blank bill used the same ink as Order Zero. Mochi jumped onto the book and tried to cover the page with his tail. “You know something,” Nina told him. The cat looked away. Kai suggested they keep the evidence safe and ask questions tomorrow. Nina agreed. The bell above the door was silent now, but the question in the ledger would not let her sleep easily.

門終於上鎖時，妮娜又清點一次當天的材料。商店賺到一顆誠實的星，卻也花掉時間、紙張和心力。這樣的平衡很真實。她把收據放在奶奶的字跡旁，忽然發現新的細節：那張空白帳單使用的墨水與零號訂單相同。麻糬跳上書，用尾巴想遮住那頁。「你知道些什麼，」妮娜對牠說。紙貓別過頭。凱建議把證據保管好，明天再追問。妮娜同意。門上的鈴此刻安靜了，帳簿裡的疑問卻讓她難以入睡。
