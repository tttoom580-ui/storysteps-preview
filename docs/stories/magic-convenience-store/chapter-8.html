長者與鏡桃

The Elder and the Mirror Peach

魔法便利商店，今天正式營業 · 第 8 / 25 章 · 1000–2000 · 英中對照 · 校訂中

用互動版查詞、朗讀與收藏

The shop opened at the same hour every day, even when the sky outside belonged to a different season. Nina liked that small piece of order. She was nineteen, and the shop deed had seemed much too big for her when she first held it. Now the door gave a gentle knock, and an elder from the Mirror Orchard entered. The visitor wanted soft peaches for children recovering from fever. Mochi rolled an empty receipt toward Nina with one paw. No price appeared until both sides explained what was being bought. Nina pulled out a chair, offered water, and asked the guest to start from the beginning instead of rushing to the end.

商店每天都在同一時間開門，即使門外天空屬於不同季節也一樣。妮娜喜歡這一點小小的秩序。她十九歲，第一次拿到店契時，還覺得責任大得難以承受。如今門輕輕響了，一名來自鏡桃園的長者走進來。客人想要給退燒孩子吃的柔軟桃子。麻糬用爪子推來一張空白收據。雙方說清楚交易內容之前，上面不會出現價格。妮娜拉開椅子，送上水，請客人從頭說起，不要急著跳到結論。

Nina placed the request under a small glass weight so a sudden breeze would not blow it away. The problem was not hidden: some crates were marked with the wrong delivery identity. Mochi wrote a cost beside each possible answer, then crossed out the choices that would take another customer’s goods without permission. “You cannot pay for one family with another family’s loss,” the cat said. Kai nodded. Nina repeated the request in her own words and asked the visitor to correct her if she was wrong. Only after receiving a clear answer did she move to the shelf where the old tools were kept.

妮娜用小玻璃紙鎮壓住訂單，以免忽然起風把它吹走。問題一點也不隱晦：有些木箱貼錯了收件身分。麻糬在每個可能的解法旁寫下成本，劃掉那些未經同意就要拿走其他客人物品的方案。「不能用另一個家庭的損失，替這個家庭付錢，」紙貓說。凱點頭。妮娜用自己的話重新說一次委託，請客人糾正任何錯誤。得到清楚回答後，她才走到保存舊工具的貨架前。

Nina opened her grandmother’s notebook to a page headed “Try Small First.” The note was written in plain ink, not magic letters. It warned shopkeepers never to test a dangerous idea on a customer without permission. Nina read it aloud, then suggested this plan: ask the elder to confirm every mark instead of guessing from appearances. Kai checked the door and made sure there would be a clear way back if the attempt failed. The guest added one useful fact that had not been on the original receipt. Nina wrote it down. Even after many successful days, she still believed a customer’s knowledge of home could be more important than a shopkeeper’s special tools.

妮娜打開奶奶的筆記本，翻到寫著「先小規模試做」的一頁。那是普通墨水寫的字，不是魔法文字。筆記警告店員，未經同意不能拿顧客測試危險點子。妮娜大聲讀完，提出計畫：請長者逐一確認標記，不憑外觀猜測。凱檢查門，確認若失敗也有清楚的返回路線。客人補充了原收據沒有的一項有用事實，妮娜立刻記下。即使已成功工作許多天，她仍相信客人對家鄉的了解，有時比店裡的特殊工具更重要。

The elder was an adult with a lifetime of work behind her, yet she asked for aid without shame. Her orchard was ancient; some peach trees were a century old. Nina read the old design backward, starting from the final address, and listened closely. An elder assistant had suffered fever, so each grain of sweet food mattered. The wrong identity on a crate could send the fruit to a stranger. “Follow every instruction,” Kai said. The elder sat with the list in her lap while Nina tried to maintain a clear record. One peach had a mark shaped like a child’s face, and its strange pose made everyone laugh. A traveler who wanted to swim in the mirror pond almost tore a label from the box. “That is typical,” the elder sighed. “People get used to guessing. Please ask before you move anything.” The elder warned that one small tear in a label could confuse everyone. The elder preferred a used wooden box rather than a new one.

長者已是閱歷深厚的大人，卻仍坦然求助。她的果園古老，有些桃樹已有一百年。妮娜從最後一個地址往回讀舊設計，仔細傾聽。年老的助手正在發燒，因此每一粒甜食都很重要。箱子上的錯誤身分標示可能把水果送給陌生人。「遵守每項指示，」凱說。長者把清單放在腿上，妮娜則維持清楚紀錄。一顆桃子帶著像孩子臉的記號，姿態奇怪，惹得大家發笑。想去鏡池游泳的旅人差點撕掉箱標。「真典型，」長者嘆氣，「大家習慣用猜的。移動東西前請先詢問。」長者提醒，一張標籤即使只有一道小裂口，也可能讓所有人搞混。長者寧願用二手木箱，而不是買新箱子。

The first test did not look impressive. A box stood on a chair, Kai counted the minutes, and Mochi watched the doorway as if it were a sleeping animal. Nina followed the agreed plan: ask the elder to confirm every mark instead of guessing from appearances. Something went a little wrong, as things often did in a real store. She stopped and asked what had changed before trying again. The customer offered an idea from home, and Nina was willing to use it after one more safe check. They did not need to pretend the first attempt had been perfect. The written record would be more useful tomorrow if it included the mistake as well as the answer.

第一次測試一點也不華麗。椅子上放著箱子，凱計時，麻糬像盯著睡著的動物那樣守著門口。妮娜照約定計畫做：請長者逐一確認標記，不憑外觀猜測。和真實商店的工作一樣，途中出了點小錯。她停下來問清楚改變了什麼，再試第二次。客人提出家鄉的方法，妮娜在追加一次安全檢查後願意採用。大家不必假裝首次嘗試很完美。紀錄若同時寫下錯誤與答案，明天才會更有用。

By the time the bell announced the late afternoon, the store smelled of warm paper and fresh tea. Nina did not count the order as finished merely because it looked good from her side of the counter. The customer checked the result: the right children received the fruit and the mistaken boxes went back. Kai waited for a real answer, and Mochi held the receipt still until the visitor said the work met the agreed conditions. Only then did a tiny star appear on its corner. Nina placed the star in the ledger beside the cost and the time spent. It was not an endless reward. Five checked stars could buy only one upgrade, and that upgrade would still need care and supplies.

下午較晚時門鈴再次響起，店裡已有暖紙和新茶的香氣。妮娜不會只因櫃臺這邊看起來不錯，就算訂單完成。客人親自確認結果：正確的孩子收到水果，誤送的箱子也退回。凱等待真正的答覆，麻糬穩穩壓著收據，直到客人說成果符合約定條件。這時收據角落才浮現小星星。妮娜把星星記入帳簿，旁邊寫上成本和所花時間。它不是無限獎勵。五顆通過驗收的星只能換一項升級，而且升級後仍需要維護和材料。

After closing, Nina opened the old ledger to leave a note for the next morning. Between two ordinary receipts she noticed something that had not been there before: the false addresses pointed to a company warehouse. She showed it to Kai instead of hiding it. Together they decided to copy the page, check its date, and ask the next visitor whether anyone else had seen the same mark. Nina switched off the window light, but one of the seven bells rang softly in the dark. She remembered Grandma Ada saying that the store was not meant to make one person rich. It existed so travelers could return home with what they truly needed.

打烊後，妮娜打開舊帳簿，想替明早留下備忘。在兩張普通收據之間，她注意到一項以前沒有的線索：那些錯誤地址指向一家公司的倉庫。她沒有藏起來，而是拿給凱看。兩人決定先影印頁面、核對日期，再問下一位旅人是否見過相同記號。妮娜關掉櫥窗燈，七個鈴鐺中卻有一個在黑暗中輕輕響起。她想起艾達奶奶說過，商店不是為了讓某個人致富，而是讓旅人能帶著真正需要的東西回家。
