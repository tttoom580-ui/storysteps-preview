The Empty Stop

紅色公車的第二張時刻表 · 第 2 / 25 章 · 1000以下 · 英中對照 · 校訂中

用互動版查詞、朗讀與收藏

Nina brought a blank form to the south stop. Her cousin Milo came too. Milo's family lived near the river, where tall grass grew beside the road. He wanted to add drawings to the form so people would understand it quickly. Nina said drawings were helpful, but the words had to be clear. They wrote three questions: Do you use Route Eight? What time would help you? What place do you need to reach? They did not ask for names or addresses. Milo also brought pencils and a little box in which people could put answers without showing them to everyone.

妮娜帶著空白表格來到南邊站牌，她的表弟米洛也來了。米洛的家住在河邊，路旁長著高草。他想在表格上加圖畫，讓人一看就懂。妮娜說，圖畫很有幫助，但文字也要清楚。他們寫下三個問題：你會搭八號公車嗎？什麼時間最方便？你需要到哪裡？他們沒有問姓名或住址。米洛還帶來鉛筆和一個小盒子，讓大家能把答案投入其中，不必給所有人看。

The first person was a man carrying a tool bag. He worked at the factory and started at six in the morning. The red bus came after he was already at work. “The bus is useful,” he said, “but its time is not useful for me.” A woman who kept sheep on a farm told them a similar story. She needed to visit the town in the afternoon, not at ten in the morning. Milo filled his page with small pictures: a clock, a factory, a farm. Nina began to understand why an empty stop did not always mean an empty need.

第一個受訪的人背著工具袋。他在工廠上班，早上六點就要開工。紅色公車來的時候，他早已在工作。「公車很有用，」他說，「但它的時間對我沒用。」一位在農場養羊的婦人也說了相似的事。她需要下午進城，而不是上午十點。米洛在紙上畫了小圖：時鐘、工廠、農場。妮娜漸漸明白，沒人在站牌等車，不一定代表沒有人需要公車。

They went to the community center for a short science class about power and travel. A student showed a video of a famous city bus with doors on both sides. Another student wanted to bring the same kind of bus to Willow Bend. “That bus is a good idea for a large city,” their teacher said, “but our streets are small. First learn what our town needs.” Nina liked that answer. She knew a new bus would cost a great deal. Perhaps moving one trip to a better hour would help more than buying a large bus.

他們到社區中心上了一堂關於動力和交通的科學短課。一位學生播放影片，介紹一輛兩側都有車門的知名城市公車。另一位學生希望柳灣鎮也能有同樣的車。老師說：「對大城市來說，那是好主意，但我們的街道很小。先了解自己鎮上的需要。」妮娜很喜歡這個回答。她知道新公車要花很多錢。或許把其中一班移到更合適的時間，比買大車更有用。

At lunch, Milo ate a sandwich and wiped his lip with a cloth. “That guy at the factory was right,” he said. “Why have a stop if no one can use it?” Nina thought about the question. A stop could be in the right place but have the wrong time. The bus could also be at the right time but travel to the wrong place. She wrote the two ideas on separate cards. Sol later helped them put the cards on a map. He made a mark for every answer, but he did not count the same answer twice.

午餐時，米洛吃著三明治，用布擦了擦嘴唇。「工廠那個人說得對，」他說，「如果沒人能用，站牌有什麼意義？」妮娜思考這個問題。站牌位置可能正確，時間卻不對；公車的時間也可能正確，路線卻不到需要的地方。她把兩種情況分別寫在卡片上。後來索爾幫忙把卡片放到地圖上。他每收到一份答案就做記號，但不把同一份答案算兩次。

A little girl asked whether they could promise a bus at every hour. Nina shook her head. She could not promise what the town had not approved. “We are collecting ideas, not selling tickets,” she said. The girl nodded and drew a sun beside the afternoon box. Soon other children drew pictures too. They used their hands to tell the story of their days: school, work, the clinic, and the market. Nina saw that a map of travel was also a map of family life. It showed when people needed to meet one another.

一個小女孩問，他們能不能保證每個鐘頭都有公車。妮娜搖頭。鎮公所還沒核准的事，她不能承諾。「我們是在收集意見，不是在賣車票。」她說。女孩點點頭，在下午的格子旁畫了一個太陽。不久，其他孩子也畫起圖來。他們用圖畫說明自己的日常：學校、工作、診所、市場。妮娜看出交通地圖也是家庭生活的地圖，記下人們何時需要相見。

Before they left, Milo wanted to fill the box with answers from his friends. Nina asked him to give the forms only to people who wished to take part. A form made for one person should not be filled by another person. They sat together and checked the questions again. Milo noticed that the third question could be hard for someone who wanted to visit more than one place. Nina added a little space for a second place. Then they tested the form on themselves. They both found it easier to use. Nina thanked Milo for seeing a problem she had missed. Good work did not require the oldest person to have every idea, and a kind cousin could help by asking a clear question.

離開前，米洛想把盒子塞滿朋友們的回答。妮娜請他只把表格交給願意參加的人。給某個人填的表格，不該由別人代填。他們坐下再次檢查問題。米洛發現第三題對想去不只一個地方的人來說不容易回答。妮娜加了一小格，讓人填第二個地點，然後兩人親自試填，發現的確更容易用了。妮娜謝謝米洛注意到她漏看的問題。做好工作不代表年紀最大的就得想出一切；好心的表弟也能靠清楚提問幫忙。

By the end of the sunny day, they had eighteen forms. Six people used the bus now; twelve said that they needed a different hour or stop. Nina did not say that all twelve would become riders. She wrote the numbers as answers, not as proof of future trips. Sol wanted to show them on the town website, but Nina said they should first check that the forms were easy to read. They took them home in the box. Tomorrow they would ask Ada what the present route could and could not change.

晴朗的一天結束時，他們收到了十八張表。六人現在會搭車；十二人表示需要不同的時間或站牌。妮娜沒有說那十二人一定會成為乘客。她把數字記成「回答」，而不是未來搭車次數的證明。索爾想把結果放到鎮公所網站，妮娜說先確認表格內容容易閱讀。他們把表格裝進盒子帶回家。明天要去問艾達，現有路線哪些部分能改、哪些不能改。
