有兩根指針的時鐘

The Clock with Two Hands

Sol woke from a dream about a bus with wings. He heard the station clock outside his bedroom and knew it was time to get up. In his dream the red bus could fly over every road, but real life was different. He laughed at the picture in his mind. As he walked to school, he looked at the sky and saw a rainbow over the hills. The rainbow was beautiful, yet it could not tell a driver when a road was closed. He wanted to draw a map that was useful even on a day with no colors in the sky.

索爾做了一個有翅膀的公車夢。他聽到臥室外車站時鐘的聲音，知道該起床了。夢裡的紅色公車能飛過每條路，現實卻不是那樣。他想到那幅畫面，自己笑了。走去學校時，他望向天空，看見山丘上方有一道彩虹。彩虹很美，卻不能告訴司機哪條道路封閉了。他想畫出一張實用的地圖，即使天空沒有漂亮的顏色，也能幫上忙。

At school, Nina and Sol found an error in the old time sheet. One clock in the station was five minutes slow. A second clock at the office showed the right time. That small difference had made it hard for passengers to know whether the bus had left early. “I just thought the bus never waited,” Milo said. Ada explained that drivers used the official clock on the bus, not the one on the wall. Nina wrote the rule down. She would not ask anyone to believe a guess when they could check the real time.

到了學校，妮娜和索爾在舊時刻表旁找到一個問題。車站裡的一個時鐘慢了五分鐘，辦公室的第二個時鐘才是正確時間。這點差異讓乘客難以判斷公車是否提早離站。「我還以為公車從不等人。」米洛說。艾達解釋，司機依照車上的正式時鐘行駛，不看牆上的時鐘。妮娜把規定寫下。既然能查真正的時間，就不該要求別人相信猜測。

The station stood beside a yard full of trees. In the morning, the yard was quiet except for birds and soft wind. In the afternoon, loud noise came from a truck that delivered food to a shop. The bus had to turn carefully then. “Nature gives us space, but our town also has work to do,” Ada told them. Nina felt proud of Sol when he marked both the quiet hours and the busy hours on his map. A place did not stay the same all day. A good plan had to respect its changes.

車站旁有個長滿樹的院子。早晨很安靜，只有鳥聲和微風；下午則有一輛運送食品的卡車發出很大的聲響，公車必須小心轉彎。「大自然給我們空間，但鎮上的人也得工作。」艾達告訴他們。索爾在地圖上標出安靜時段和忙碌時段，妮娜為他感到驕傲。同一個地方一天之中不會永遠一樣。好的計畫必須尊重那些變化。

The children decided to ask the shop owner when the truck usually came. She was not rich, she said, and losing the truck space could hurt her small business. The bus did not need to stop at her door. It could use the library corner, where no truck needed to turn. Nina realized that keeping Route Eight did not mean giving the bus every bit of the road. It meant finding a place for the bus among other kinds of work. She thanked the owner for telling her something that the old map could not show.

孩子們決定去問店主，貨車通常何時到。店主說自己並不富有，如果失去貨車停靠處，小生意就會受影響。公車不需要停在店門口，可以利用圖書館的轉角，那裡沒有貨車要轉彎。妮娜明白了，保住八號路線不表示公車能占用每一寸道路，而是要讓它在各種工作之間找到合適的位置。她謝謝店主說出舊地圖看不見的事情。

In class, their teacher asked each student to tell a story about a time someone had listened to them. Sol told the class about the man who thought he was stupid at the south stop. “He was not wrong to ask,” Sol said. “We were wrong to think the sign was easy.” Several children nodded. Nina did not need to be the only speaker. When Milo stood and spoke about his farm pictures, she listened without adding words. Their little group was learning that making a plan also meant making room for many voices.

課堂上，老師請每位學生說一個自己曾被傾聽的故事。索爾說起南邊站牌那位覺得自己很笨的先生。「他問問題沒有錯，」索爾說，「是我們誤以為標示很容易懂。」幾個孩子點頭。妮娜不必永遠當唯一的發言者。米洛站起來講農場圖畫時，她沒有插話，只認真聽。這個小團隊漸漸學會，做計畫也意味著給許多聲音留位置。

At the library, Nina helped Sol close a window before rain came. From inside they could hear the station bell. A boy asked Sol why his new map did not show every tree along the road. Sol began to laugh, then explained that the map was for finding stops, not for drawing everything in Willow Bend. He could make a second picture for people who liked the trees. Nina thought that was a good answer. One page did not need to do every job. A simple map could show stops and times, while another page could tell stories about the town. The children checked that the official times stayed easy to see. They wanted people to enjoy the picture without missing their bus.

在圖書館，妮娜幫索爾在下雨前關上窗戶。從室內能聽見車站的鈴聲。一個男孩問索爾，為什麼新地圖沒有畫出路邊每棵樹。索爾笑了，接著解釋，這張地圖用來找站牌，不是畫出柳灣鎮的全部東西。他可以另外為喜歡樹的人畫一張圖。妮娜覺得這回答很好。一頁紙不必包辦每件事。簡單地圖可以顯示站牌和時間，另一張圖則能說鎮子的故事。他們確認正式時刻仍然清楚可見，希望大家欣賞圖畫時，也不會錯過公車。

Before leaving school, Nina checked the times once more. She had wanted an easy answer, but the answer was growing into several small steps. Fix the clock. Check the stop. Listen to the shop owner. Keep the official ride count. She could not finish all of them in one day. That did not make the work less important. She took her book home under her arm and felt calm. A bus followed a road one stop at a time. Perhaps a town could find a better way one careful choice at a time too.

放學前，妮娜又核對了一次時間。她原本想得到簡單答案，如今答案卻變成好幾個小步驟：校正時鐘、檢查站牌、聽店主說話、保留正式乘車數字。她無法一天做完，但這不代表事情不重要。她夾著筆記本回家，心裡平靜。公車沿路一站一站前進，也許小鎮也能靠一個又一個謹慎的選擇，找到更好的方法。
