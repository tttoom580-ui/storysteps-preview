五分鐘的說明

Five Minutes to Talk

紅色公車的第二張時刻表 · 第 5 / 25 章 · 1000以下 · 英中對照 · 校訂中

The first small meeting was held during lunch. Ms. Quinn, the school librarian, gave Nina five minutes to speak. Nina put a picture of a red bus on the wall and pointed to two times: seven in the morning and ten at night. “That second time is not real,” Sol said. Nina laughed, then said she was sorry. She had written ten without the words “in the morning.” “At least we found our own mistake first,” she told the group. They changed the sign before anyone took it home. She felt safer with a checked page than with a beautiful page.

第一次小型說明會在午餐時間舉行。學校圖書管理員奎恩女士給妮娜五分鐘發言。妮娜把一張紅色公車圖貼到牆上，指著兩個時間：早上七點和晚上十點。「第二個時間不是真的吧，」索爾說。妮娜笑了，接著道歉。她只寫了十點，忘了標明「上午」。「至少我們先找到了自己的錯。」她告訴大家。眾人修改海報後，才讓人帶回家。比起漂亮的頁面，她更喜歡檢查過、讓人放心的頁面。

Milo had made a picture of a frog, a hen, and a bean plant to show the farms north of town. A student asked why a frog was on a bus plan. Milo said it was only to help people find the farm stop. The group decided to use a plain circle as well, so that the map would still make sense without the pictures. Someone asked about the height of the bus step. Ada had measured it. She explained that some people found the step difficult, and a new stop needed a safe place to stand.

米洛畫了青蛙、母雞和豆苗，代表鎮北邊的農場。一名學生問，為什麼公車計畫上會有青蛙。米洛說，那只是為了幫大家找到農場站。大家決定同時使用簡單的圓圈，這樣即使看不懂圖畫，地圖仍然清楚。有人問公車踏階的高度。艾達已量過。她解釋，有些人踏上去比較吃力，新站點也必須有安全的站立空間。

Nina showed a long line on the map and asked how many minutes the bus would need to travel it. One boy said they should race the bus on bikes and see who would win. Ada said that would not be safe. A bus must stop for people, and a bike should never race in traffic. They could look at the time on the bus company's old sheets instead. Nina put those minutes on the board. The route was not a game, but it could be studied carefully. The children agreed to let adults handle driving and road checks.

妮娜指著地圖上的長路線，問公車走完要幾分鐘。一個男孩提議騎腳踏車和公車比賽，看誰會贏。艾達說那樣不安全。公車必須停下來載客，腳踏車也不該在車流中競速。他們可以查看公司舊紀錄的行駛分鐘數。妮娜把數字寫到白板上。路線不是遊戲，但可以認真研究。孩子們同意讓成年人負責駕駛和道路檢查。

Someone asked whether the red bus could carry people when it snowed. Nina said Willow Bend almost never had snow, but the company already had a bad-weather rule. Ada showed them the page. In rain or snow, the driver had to check the road and could say a trip was not safe. A boy at the back looked unhappy. “What if my father is waiting?” he asked. Ada did not promise more than she could do. “We will tell people when the road is closed,” she said, “and we will not drive into danger.”

有人問，下雪時紅色公車能不能載客。妮娜說柳灣鎮幾乎不下雪，但公司早已有惡劣天氣規定。艾達把那頁拿出來。下雨或下雪時，司機必須檢查路況，必要時可判定班次不安全。一個坐在後面的男孩不太高興。「如果我爸爸在等車呢？」他問。艾達沒有做出超出能力的承諾。「路封閉時我們會通知大家，」她說，「也不會把車開進危險的地方。」

In the last minute, Nina asked everyone to put one point on the map where they wished a bus could stop. Some points were near homes; others were near work. A few were too far away from the road. She did not hide those points. Instead, she marked them with a question to ask the town planner. “Although we want many stops, we may not get them all,” she said. Milo felt sad, but he understood. A fair map showed what people wanted even when their wishes could not all become rules.

最後一分鐘，妮娜請每個人在地圖上標出一處希望公車能停的地方。有些點在住家旁，有些靠近工作地點，也有幾個離現有道路太遠。她沒有藏起那些點，而是加上問號，準備詢問鎮上規劃人員。「雖然我們希望有很多站，但不一定能全部得到。」她說。米洛有點失望，卻能理解。公平的地圖應呈現人們真正想要的，即使每個願望都無法成為規則。

As the children put away the maps, Milo touched the back of his neck and said he felt tired from listening so hard. Nina told him to take a break. They made a deal: no one would work on the bus plan during every minute of the day. School, meals, family, and play still mattered. Ms. Quinn agreed and asked them to choose one afternoon each week for their notes. “You can do a good thing without letting it take every hour,” she said. Nina had been afraid that a rest would mean losing time. Now she understood that a tired team might make new mistakes. She put the notebook in her bag and went outside to play with her friends.

孩子們收起地圖時，米洛摸著後頸，說自己太用力聽大家說話，已經累了。妮娜請他休息。他們約定：不能把一天的每分鐘都花在公車計畫上。學校、吃飯、家人和遊戲仍然重要。奎恩女士同意，請他們每週選一個下午整理筆記。「做好事，不代表得把每個鐘頭都交出去。」她說。妮娜原本怕休息會浪費時間，如今明白疲倦的團隊反而容易犯新錯。她把筆記本放進書包，出門和朋友玩。

After the talk, Ms. Quinn gave Nina a small notebook to keep the questions in. It had a drawing of a mountain on its cover. Nina wrote that no one should dig up the road or move a sign on their own. She also wrote that no change could begin before the town gave permission. Sol read the page and nodded. Their first report was ready: old signs needed correction, the town needed a better time survey, and possible stops needed safety checks. They had not won anything yet. But they had learned how to ask for change without making false promises.

說明會後，奎恩女士給妮娜一本小筆記本，用來保存問題。封面畫著一座山。妮娜寫下：任何人都不該擅自挖路或移動站牌；鎮公所核准之前，也不能開始更改。索爾讀完後點頭。他們的第一份報告準備好了：舊時刻表需要更正、鎮上需要更好的時間調查、可能的新站點需要安全檢查。他們還沒有贏得任何事，但已學會如何在不亂做承諾的情況下要求改變。
