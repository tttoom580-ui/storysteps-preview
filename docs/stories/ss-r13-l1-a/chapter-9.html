湖邊的節日

The Lake Festival

紅色公車的第二張時刻表 · 第 9 / 25 章 · 1000以下 · 英中對照 · 校訂中

A festival was coming to the lake near Willow Bend. Nina's grandmother helped cook rice for the visitors. She told Nina that the street beside the lake became quite busy during the festival. Buses had once been useful there, but the last festival had few bus riders. Nina asked why. Her grandmother said the bus had gone back to the station before the music ended. “People wanted to visit, but they could not get home on it,” she explained. Nina wrote this on a card. It was another example of a bus time that did not match people's lives.

柳灣鎮附近的湖邊快要舉辦節日活動。妮娜的奶奶幫忙替遊客煮飯。她告訴妮娜，節日期間湖旁街道會很忙。公車以前很有用，但上次節慶搭車的人很少。妮娜問原因。奶奶說，音樂還沒結束，公車就已經回站。「人們想來玩，卻沒辦法搭車回家。」她解釋。妮娜把這件事記在卡片上。這又是一個公車時間跟不上生活的例子。

At the festival table, volunteers put a dish of banana pieces beside the rice. A man shouted that there was not enough food, but he had only seen one small table. The kitchen had more food ready in the back. Nina's grandmother smiled and showed him where to go. “A little information can change how people feel,” she told Nina. “But you must be sure it is correct.” Nina thought of the old bus sign. She was beginning to see the same lesson in many places: do not take one small view and call it the whole story.

節慶桌上，志工把一盤香蕉片放在米飯旁。一個男人大喊食物不夠，但他只看見一張小桌子。後方廚房其實還有更多餐點。奶奶微笑著指給他看。「多一點資訊，就可能改變人的感受，」她對妮娜說，「但妳得確定資訊正確。」妮娜想起舊公車時刻表。她在許多地方看到相同的道理：不能只看見一小部分，就說那是全部。

Sol arrived with a sign that said “Are you ready to talk about the bus?” He asked people if it was okay to record their answers. Some said yes; others did not want to be recorded. Nina gave them paper forms instead. She wanted every person to feel sure that saying no was allowed. One visitor gave them a useful tip: ask about the trip home, not only the trip out. Sol added that question to the forms. Nina felt joy because the best idea of the day had come from someone outside their group.

索爾帶著牌子來了，上面寫著「準備好談談公車了嗎？」他問人們能不能錄下回答。有些人同意，有些人不願被錄音。妮娜改給他們紙本表格。她希望每個人都確信可以拒絕。一位遊客提供實用的提醒：不只要問去程，也要問回程。索爾把這題加進表格。妮娜很開心，因為今天最好的點子來自他們團隊以外的人。

At noon, a woman said her family had visited the lake every year. She wanted to treat her children to the festival, but she could not stay until evening without a way home. Nina asked if a later return bus might help. The woman said it might, but she did not promise to ride. Another man said he would still walk. Nina put both answers on the page. Her grandmother looked over her shoulder and nodded. “You are listening even when an answer is not the one you wanted,” she said.

中午，一位婦人說她家每年都會來湖邊。她想帶孩子到節日裡享受一下，但如果晚上沒有回家的交通方式，就不能待到那麼晚。妮娜問，較晚的返程公車會不會有幫助。婦人說可能會，但不保證搭乘。另一個男人說他還是會走路。妮娜把兩種答案都寫下。奶奶從肩後看了看，點點頭。「即使答案不是妳想聽的，妳也有在認真聽。」她說。

In the afternoon, the bus passed along the lake street with only three people inside. People on the road saw it and waved, but some were eating, and others were waiting for the music. Nina did not shout that the bus was empty because nobody liked it. She knew there were several possible reasons. On the card for the town meeting, she wrote, “Festival day: bus time ended before the event. Need to study a later return.” She marked the note as a question, not an order. The festival could help them learn, but it could not decide a whole year of service.

下午，公車沿著湖邊街道經過，車內只有三位乘客。路邊的人看到了都揮手，但有些人正在吃東西，有些人等著聽音樂。妮娜沒有大喊說車上沒人是因為大家不喜歡公車。她知道可能有好幾種原因。她在鎮民會議用的卡片上寫：「節日當天：公車結束時間早於活動結束。需研究較晚的回程班次。」她標示成問題，而不是命令。節慶能讓他們學習，卻不能決定一整年的公車服務。

On the way home from the festival, Sol asked whether they should ask the town to add a festival-only bus. Nina said that might be worth discussing later, but it was not the same question as the usual Route Eight service. A bus for one busy day could be useful even if it did not run all year. A daily bus had to be judged in a different way. They put the festival answers in a separate part of the folder. Nina wanted the council to know why those answers mattered, but she did not want one happy event to hide the needs of people going to work and the clinic on ordinary days.

從節日回家的路上，索爾問是否該請鎮上新增節慶專車。妮娜說那可能值得以後討論，但它和八號路線平日服務不是同一個問題。只在熱鬧日子行駛的公車也許很有用，即使不需全年運行；每天行駛的公車則需要不同的評估方式。他們把節慶問卷放在資料夾的獨立部分。妮娜希望議會明白節慶意見的重要，卻不願讓某個快樂活動掩蓋平常上班、看診的人們的需要。

At the end of the day, the family sat together beside the lake. Nina's grandmother said that life in a small town was not always simple. People needed to work, rest, care for children, and see friends. A bus could not solve everything, but it could help people meet. Nina looked at the lights on the water and thought about the last four weeks of questions. She was almost ready to speak to the town council. She would carry both good stories and hard numbers, because each could tell the council something the other could not.

一天結束時，一家人坐在湖邊。奶奶說，小鎮生活並不總是簡單。人們需要工作、休息、照顧孩子、探望朋友。公車無法解決所有問題，卻能幫人相聚。妮娜望著水上的燈光，回想這幾週提出的問題。她差不多準備好向鎮議會發言了。她要帶著暖心的故事，也帶著扎實的數字，因為兩者各能告訴議員一些另一方無法說清的事。
