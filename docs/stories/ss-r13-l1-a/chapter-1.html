A Notice at Seven

紅色公車的第二張時刻表 · 第 1 / 25 章 · 1000以下 · 英中對照 · 校訂中

At seven a.m., Nina came early to the town bus stop. The sun was bright, and the little bell above the station door made a soft sound. She had eaten fruit and a small cake for breakfast. Now she wanted to take the red bus to school. But a heavy board stood beside the door. A white paper covered half of it. The paper said, “Route Eight may end next month.” Nina read the words twice. The bus had been part of her mornings for years. She did not know how a town could lose a bus in just one month.

上午七點，妮娜提早到了鎮上的公車站。陽光明亮，站門上方的小鈴鐺輕輕作響。她早餐吃過水果和一小塊蛋糕，現在要搭紅色公車去學校。然而門邊立著一塊沉重的公告板，上面有一半被白紙蓋住。紙上寫著：「八號路線可能在下個月停駛。」妮娜把字讀了兩遍。多年來，公車一直是她早晨生活的一部分。她不明白一個小鎮怎麼會在短短一個月內失去公車。

A boy named Sol was waiting on the couch inside the station. He was Nina's friend, and drawing funny street maps was his hobby. On his book was a monkey with one large tooth. “That is not a real road,” Nina said, trying to smile. Sol closed the book. He saw the notice and did not laugh. His grandfather used the bus to go to the doctor. “If the bus stops, how will he go?” Sol asked. Nina could not give him an answer. She felt that a line on a map could mean much more than a line on paper.

一個叫索爾的男孩坐在站內沙發上等車。他是妮娜的朋友，畫有趣的街道地圖是他的興趣。他的本子上畫了一隻有著一顆大牙齒的猴子。「那不是真正的道路啦。」妮娜試著笑說。索爾闔上本子。他看到公告，就笑不出來了。他的祖父要搭公車去看醫生。「如果公車沒了，爺爺要怎麼去？」索爾問。妮娜答不出來。她感覺地圖上的一條線，意義可能遠比紙上的線條大。

The driver, Ada, came out with a blue cover for the front seat. She put it away before opening the bus. “We have fewer riders than before,” she explained. “The town must decide how to spend its money.” Nina asked whether the town had heard from everyone who needed the bus. Ada shook her head. “I drive. I cannot make that choice for the town.” She did not sound angry, only tired. Nina respected that answer. If she wanted to help, she would need to listen before she asked anyone to change a rule.

司機艾達拿著前座的藍色椅套走出來。她先收好椅套，才打開公車門。「搭車的人比以前少了，」她解釋，「鎮上必須決定怎樣花錢。」妮娜問，鎮公所是否聽過每個需要公車的人的意見。艾達搖頭。「我負責開車，不能替全鎮做決定。」她聽起來不是生氣，只是疲倦。妮娜尊重這個答案。她想幫忙，就得先傾聽，才能請別人改變規則。

On the ride, Nina saw an old woman carrying a salad and a bag of bread. She stood because the seats near the door were full. Sol offered her his place. The woman sat and thanked him. Nina looked at the people around her. Some were on their way to work; others were going to school or to the clinic. She wanted to write down their stories, but she knew that their names belonged to them. She asked Ada whether she could teach the children how to count riders without asking private questions. Ada said yes, after school, at the station.

車上，妮娜看到一位老奶奶拿著沙拉和一袋麵包。靠近車門的座位坐滿了，她只好站著。索爾讓出座位，老奶奶坐下並向他道謝。妮娜看看四周的人。有些人要上班，有些人去上學或診所。她想把他們的故事寫下來，但也知道姓名是每個人自己的事。她問艾達能不能教孩子們不問私人問題也能計算搭車人數。艾達答應放學後在車站教他們。

At school, Nina told her teacher about the notice. “Shall we hold a short meeting after class?” she asked. The teacher said they could use the room if the students did their schoolwork first. During exercise time, Nina watched a bus go past the yard. One new student from a foreign country pointed at it and said his family took that bus to the market. Nina had never asked him about it. She began to see that her own morning trip was only one small part of Route Eight. She took out a clean page and wrote a title: “Who uses the red bus?”

到了學校，妮娜把公告的事告訴老師。「我們放學後可以開個短會嗎？」她問。老師說，只要大家先完成課業，就能使用教室。運動時間，妮娜看到公車開過校園旁的院子。一位來自國外的新同學指著公車，說他家會搭那班車去市場。妮娜以前從沒問過他。她開始明白，自己的早晨旅程只是八號路線的一小部分。她拿出空白紙，寫上標題：「誰在搭紅色公車？」

The teacher asked whether Nina wanted to make a large red poster that said “Save Our Bus.” Nina thought about it for a moment. A poster could make people look, but it could not explain the cost or the travel times. She chose a smaller page with room for answers. Sol gave her two blue pencils and drew a little bus at the top. The other children helped make the questions short. They checked that every question allowed a person to say no. Nina felt that the page was becoming more useful with each change. She would still make a bright poster later, but first she wanted a true picture of the town, not only a loud message about what she hoped would happen.

老師問妮娜，是否想做一張大紅海報，上面寫「救救我們的公車」。妮娜想了一下。海報能吸引目光，卻無法說清成本和搭乘時間。她選了一張較小的紙，留下讓人回答的空間。索爾給她兩枝藍色鉛筆，在頂端畫上小公車。其他孩子幫忙把問題寫短，並確認每個問題都允許對方說不。妮娜覺得這張紙每改一次就更有用了。以後她仍可以做醒目的海報，但首先她想看到鎮子的真實樣貌，而不是只喊出自己的希望。

After school, Ada showed them an old passenger sheet. It had numbers, not names. The bus company counted each person who got on, but the sheet could not tell who had wanted to come at a different time. Nina drew two boxes on her page: “People who ride” and “People who cannot ride at this time.” Sol wanted to fill both boxes at once. Nina stopped him. “We do not know the second number yet,” she said. They agreed to begin with questions. Their first act would not be a race to win. It would be a careful exercise in listening.

放學後，艾達給他們看一張舊的乘客統計表。上面只有數字，沒有姓名。公車公司會計算上車的人數，但表格無法顯示有多少人想在其他時間搭車。妮娜在紙上畫了兩個框：「現在有搭車的人」和「因時間不合而無法搭車的人」。索爾想立刻把兩格填滿，妮娜制止了他。「第二個數字我們還不知道。」她說。他們同意先從提問開始。他們要做的第一件事，不是爭著取勝，而是認真學習聆聽。
