公園裡的一段路

A Walk in the Park

紅色公車的第二張時刻表 · 第 8 / 25 章 · 1000以下 · 英中對照 · 校訂中

用互動版查詞、朗讀與收藏

On Saturday, Nina joined a group of children for a walk in the park. A girl in a yellow dress carried a baseball, and a boy wore a shirt with a ghost on it. They were not there to play a game. They wanted to see whether a new bus stop near the park would be easy to reach. An officer from the town road team walked with them. He said the children could look and ask questions, but they could not move signs or walk into traffic. Nina was glad to have someone who knew the road rules.

星期六，妮娜跟一群孩子到公園走路。一個穿黃洋裝的女孩帶著棒球，另一個男孩的衣服上印著鬼怪圖案。他們不是去玩，而是要看看公園旁的新站點是否容易抵達。鎮上道路小組的一位工作員陪著走。他說孩子們可以觀察和提問，但不能移動路牌或走進車流。妮娜很高興有熟悉道路規則的人同行。

The park had two paths. One went behind the trees and was short but narrow. The other went outside the play area and was longer but wide. “The short path is easy,” Sol said. A small child tried it with a large bag and had to turn sideways. Nina said, “Easy for whom?” The group looked again. They did not need a person to be blind or hurt before they could see that a clear, wide path helped many people. The officer measured both paths and wrote the numbers on his own paper.

公園裡有兩條路。一條繞到樹後，距離短卻狹窄；另一條在遊戲區外，較長但寬敞。「短的比較容易走。」索爾說。一個小孩背著大袋子試走，卻必須側身才能通過。妮娜問：「對誰來說容易？」大家重新看了看。他們不必等到有人看不見或受傷，才明白清楚寬敞的小徑可以幫助很多人。道路工作員量了兩條路的尺寸，記在自己的紙上。

Near the park gate, a family shared a lunch of beef sandwiches. They gave the children water and asked why they were taking notes. Nina showed the two paths on her map. “We want to know which one more people can use safely,” she said. The father offered to move a heavy stone. The officer thanked him but said the town must approve any work on public land. They did not want a nice act to create another problem. The family understood. They promised to send comments to the town office instead.

公園門口附近，一家人分吃牛肉三明治。他們拿水給孩子們，問大家為何記筆記。妮娜在地圖上指給他們看兩條路。「我們想知道哪條路能讓更多人安全使用。」她說。父親提議搬走一塊大石頭。工作員謝過他，卻說公有土地上的改動必須經過鎮上核准。他們不希望善意反而造成另一個問題。這家人理解了，答應改向鎮公所提出意見。

At noon, the girl in the yellow dress asked why the new stop was not just in front of her house. Nina gave her the same answer she had given the little girl at the south stop. One home could not decide the route for everyone. The park was a place many people used, so it was worth studying. The girl smiled and said that was a nice reason. She gave Nina a card with the park name on it. Sol drew a clear picture of the wide path on the back.

中午，穿黃洋裝的女孩問，新站牌為什麼不能直接設在她家門口。妮娜給了她和南邊那位小女孩一樣的回答：單一家庭不能替所有人決定路線。公園有很多人使用，所以值得研究。女孩笑了，說這是個好理由，然後送妮娜一張印著公園名稱的卡片。索爾在卡片背面畫上寬敞小徑的清楚圖案。

After the walk, the officer told them about the road review. There were three things to check: space for the bus, a safe place for people to wait, and a safe way to cross. A stop could look fine on a map and still fail one of those checks. Nina asked him why so many rules were needed. “Because we want people to arrive safely, not only quickly,” he answered. His words stayed in her heart. She had been thinking about the number of riders, but the way people reached the bus mattered just as much.

散步結束後，工作員說明道路審查有三項：公車停靠空間、乘客安全等候區，以及安全穿越道路的方法。站點在地圖上看起來沒問題，仍可能過不了其中一項。妮娜問為何需要這麼多規定。「因為我們希望大家平安抵達，不只是到得快。」他回答。這句話留在她心裡。她一直想著乘客數字，現在也明白人們怎麼走到公車旁，同樣重要。

The children took a short break under a tree after the walk. A small bird tried to fly from one low branch to another. Milo watched it and said he wished he could move as freely. Nina gave him a piece of apple and asked whether he liked the taste. He nodded and said he would rather stay on the safe path than fly over the road. The officer joined them for a moment and explained that the park road would need another check if it changed in the future. No one could give an answer for all time. Nina put a little note beside the park on her map: “Check again before changing.” She liked that sentence because it was short, clear, and true.

走完路後，孩子們在樹下短暫休息。一隻小鳥試著從低枝飛到另一枝。米洛望著牠，說希望自己也能自由移動。妮娜給他一塊蘋果，問他喜不喜歡這味道。他點頭，說自己寧可留在安全小路上，也不要飛越馬路。道路工作員過來坐了一會兒，解釋公園道路如果日後改動，就要重新檢查。沒有人能給出永遠適用的答案。妮娜在地圖的公園旁寫了小字：「變更前再次檢查。」她喜歡這句話，因為簡短、清楚又真實。

That evening, Nina tasted the soup her mother had made and told her about the two park paths. Her mother asked whether they had picked the wider one. “Not yet,” Nina said. “We sent the facts to the road team.” Then she laughed at herself. A month ago she would have thought that a good idea was enough. Now she knew a good idea needed checking and sometimes a different answer. She walked to the window and watched the red bus pass the park. The bus was still there. For the moment, that was enough to make her smile.

傍晚，妮娜喝了一口母親煮的湯，說起公園的兩條小路。母親問是不是選了較寬的那條。「還沒有，」妮娜說，「我們把事實送去給道路小組了。」接著她笑了笑。一個月前，她會以為好主意就夠了。如今她知道好主意需要檢查，有時還得接受不同的答案。她走到窗邊，看紅色公車經過公園。公車還在。此刻，這已足以讓她微笑。
