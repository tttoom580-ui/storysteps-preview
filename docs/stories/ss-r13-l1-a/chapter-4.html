A Sign in the Wind

紅色公車的第二張時刻表 · 第 4 / 25 章 · 1000以下 · 英中對照 · 校訂中

用互動版查詞、朗讀與收藏

A strong wind began to blow on Thursday. A boy ran to Nina with a paper sign in his hands. It had fallen off the wall near the factory gate. The paper showed the old bus times. A cloud moved across the sun, and a butterfly flew down to the grass. Nina took the sign to Ada, who shook her head. “This time is wrong now,” she said. “The factory changed its work hours two years ago.” The town still had a sign with the old hours. Nina felt as if the paper had been waiting to tell its story.

星期四刮起了強風。一個男孩拿著紙站牌跑來找妮娜。紙張是從工廠大門附近的牆上掉下來的，上面印著舊公車時間。雲飄過太陽，一隻蝴蝶飛到草地上。妮娜把站牌拿給艾達，艾達搖頭。「現在這時間不對了，」她說，「工廠兩年前就改了上班時段。」鎮上竟還留著舊時刻表。妮娜覺得，這張紙像是等了很久，終於有人聽它說話。

At the town square, the public service worker gave them a cheap copy of the current route. He was tall and wore a blue jacket. “Do not put up your own signs yet,” he said. “People may think they are official.” Nina agreed. She wanted a new bus time, but she did not want anyone to miss a bus because of her words. A woman from the factory brought them a tomato from her lunch. She said that many workers would use an earlier bus, but some still drove. Nina wrote “may,” not “will,” next to the idea.

在鎮廣場，公共服務人員給了他們一份便宜的現行路線影本。他個子很高，穿著藍外套。「暫時不要自己張貼時刻表，」他說，「別人可能以為那是正式公告。」妮娜同意。她希望公車改時間，卻不願任何人因為她的話而錯過公車。一位工廠女工從午餐中拿了一顆番茄給他們。她說很多工人可能搭更早的公車，但也有人仍會自己開車。妮娜在想法旁寫下「可能」，而不是「一定」。

Sol wanted to drop a note into every house in town. Nina asked him to stop. “Without a clear plan, we might give people a false story,” she said. They sat on a bench and made a square chart. One side was for time; the other side was for places. They could show the chart at the library and ask for comments. The town worker offered to check the words before they shared it. Nina felt good about that. A fair question was useful only if people understood what they were answering.

索爾想在全鎮每一家投一張通知。妮娜請他先停下。「沒有清楚的計畫，我們可能讓人聽到不正確的說法。」她說。他們坐在長椅上，畫了一張方格表，一邊是時間，一邊是地點。他們可以在圖書館展示，請大家提出意見。鎮上工作人員願意先檢查文字再發布。妮娜覺得這樣很好。公平的提問，必須讓回答者明白自己在回答什麼，才有用。

An older man came by and said, “Sir, who is in charge here?” He was speaking to the town worker. Nina waited for the answer. The worker explained that the council made the final choice. The driver could speak about safety, and the bus company could speak about costs. Students could share what they heard from the town. No one person held all the facts. Nina thought that the story of Route Eight had many voices, like different people reading one long book together.

一位長者經過，問公共服務人員：「先生，這裡誰負責？」妮娜等著聽答案。工作人員說，最終由鎮議會決定。司機能提供安全意見，公車公司能說明成本，學生則能分享他們在鎮上聽到的事。沒有任何一個人掌握全部事實。妮娜想，八號路線的故事有許多聲音，就像不同的人共同朗讀一本長書。

That evening, the wind made the window shake. Nina stood beside her mother, who was cutting vegetables. Her mother asked whether she wanted the bus to stay just because she loved its red color. Nina said no. She wanted it to be useful and safe. She also wanted people to know what would happen before the town made a choice. Her mother smiled. “That is a better reason than the color,” she said. Nina went to her room and wrote the date of the next town meeting at the top of her page.

那天傍晚，風吹得窗戶作響。妮娜站在正切菜的母親旁。母親問她，是不是只因為喜歡公車的紅色，就希望它留下。妮娜說不是。她希望公車有用又安全，也希望鎮上的人能在決定前知道會發生什麼事。母親笑了。「這理由比顏色好多了。」她說。妮娜回房，在紙張最上方寫下下一次鎮民會議的日期。

At the clinic, Nina spoke to a woman who carried a bag of medicine for her father. The woman said she wanted the bus to be healthy for the town in more than one way. It should help people reach places they needed, but it should not block the factory road or make drivers work without rest. Nina liked her choice of words. She asked the woman whether that thought could be shared at the public meeting without a name. The woman agreed. Nina wrote it beside the chart and put a small mark next to the time she had asked for. She was learning that people sometimes wanted several good things at once, and those good things needed to fit together.

在診所，妮娜和一位替父親提藥的婦人談話。婦人說，她希望公車能以不只一種方式讓小鎮更健康。它應該幫人到達需要去的地方，但不該擋住工廠道路，也不該讓司機沒有休息時間。妮娜喜歡這樣的說法，問能否在不提姓名的情況下於公開會議分享。婦人同意。妮娜把話寫在方格表旁，也在婦人希望的班次時間旁做記號。她正在學習，人有時會同時希望好幾件事發生，而那些好事也必須能彼此配合。

The next morning the clouds were gone. The old paper sign lay flat on Ada's desk, with “old hours” written across its top. Beside it was the square chart, marked “questions only.” Nina checked both pages before going to school. She no longer thought she could fix the bus by finding one mistake. The sign had shown an error, but the town would still have to choose how to pay for each trip. Her new job was to keep asking clear questions and to bring the right answers to the right people.

隔天早晨，雲散了。舊紙站牌平放在艾達桌上，頂端寫著「舊時段」。旁邊是方格表，標明「僅供提問」。妮娜上學前檢查了兩張紙。她不再認為只要找到一個錯誤，就能修好公車問題。站牌證明資訊出了錯，但鎮上仍得決定每一班的經費。她現在的工作，是持續提出清楚問題，並把正確答案帶給需要的人。
