The Witness Table

兩個不能飛升的人，接下了送信任務 · 第 11 / 25 章 · 4000–5000 · 英中對照 · 校訂中

At the northern crossing, a deserted casino had been converted into a workshop for repairing chairs and travel equipment. A painted sign still showed a smiling celebrity, but inside the tables were covered with tools. Ji's brother, Master Ren, worked there in a wheelchair after an accident at the old terminal. His scar ran from his wrist to his elbow, and he spoke about it without embarrassment. When Wen delivered the professor's letter, Ren read it twice and gave a sharp gasp. His sister had found proof that the terminal's last upgrade had been performed without a public inspection. “The official story is apt to characterize every objection as fear of progress,” he said. “But a machine that affects whole valleys should not be protected from questions.” Yu was fascinated by the worn wooden table near the door. Its edge carried three small circles, the mark printed on their black envelope. The original witness table had not disappeared after all; it had become an ordinary workbench.

北方關口有座荒廢賭場，如今改成修理椅子與旅具的工坊。招牌仍畫著微笑的名人，室內桌面卻擺滿工具。紀教授的兄長任師傅在此工作；舊終端事故後，他需要使用輪椅。從手腕延伸到手肘的疤痕，他談起來並不羞於見人。李聞送達妹妹的信後，任師傅讀了兩遍，猛地倒吸一口氣。妹妹找到證據：終端最後一次升級未經公開檢查。他說：「官方故事總愛把反對意見描寫成害怕進步。但影響整片山谷的機器，不該免於被質問。」沈嶼則被門邊磨舊的木桌吸引。桌緣刻著三個小圓，和黑信封標誌一樣。原來見證桌沒有消失，只是成了一張普通工作桌。

Ren explained that the First Post once kept a public ledger here, signed by senders, couriers, and independent witnesses. A courier could not claim ownership of a letter merely because it passed through his hands. The rule protected ordinary people from theft and from powerful heirs who wanted family messages removed. After the sky network failed, a director ordered the ledgers transferred to the capital. Ren kept the table because he believed its existence mattered. “I cannot recommend opening your black envelope,” he said. “The seal looks intact, and the sender's wishes come first.” Wen wondered whether the envelope contained a confession or simply a request for help. He hated how the uncertainty worked on him, but he refused to mistake curiosity for permission. Outside, a dog began to howl at the wind. A guard carrying an old unloaded rifle passed the street, watched by nervous spectators. The workshop shutters closed early.

任師傅解釋，初郵站曾在此保存公開見證簿，由寄件人、信差及獨立見證者共同簽署。信件從誰手上經過，誰也不能因此宣稱擁有它。這規定防止普通人的信遭偷竊，也防止有權勢的繼承人刪除家族訊息。天空網路失效後，一名主管命人把見證簿移往都城。任師傅留下桌子，因為他相信這張桌子的存在很重要。「我不能建議你們拆黑信封，」他說，「封印完整，寄件人的意願應優先。」李聞猜不透裡面是自白還是求援。他討厭這種不確定，卻不願把好奇心當成許可。外面有狗對風嚎叫，一名帶著卸彈舊步槍的守衛走過街道，旁觀者都很緊張。工坊提早關上了百葉門。

Ren took the men into a small back room where drawings of the terminal hung beside clinical reports from injured workers. He explained the difference between observing harm and proving its cause. Several people had become ill after shifts near the power core, but the exact mechanism remained uncertain. Wen recognized Ji's careful approach in her brother's words. Ren also showed them a dispute over copyright: the capital had republished an engineer's drawings without his name. The engineer had not sought fame. He wanted later repairs to refer to the correct instructions so that mistakes would not be repeated. Yu understood the feeling immediately. His own map training had taught him that a mark without a reliable source could mislead travelers for generations. They spent the afternoon copying the workshop's public measurements and recording who had given permission. The work was slow and unglamorous, but every careful note made their next question more precise.

任師傅帶兩人進入後室，牆上掛著終端圖紙，旁邊是受傷工人的臨床報告。他解釋，觀察到傷害與證明成因是兩回事。數名工人曾在能量核心附近輪班後生病，但確切機制尚不清楚。李聞從任師傅的話裡聽出紀教授那份謹慎。任師傅還給他們看一場著作權爭議：都城重新發表一名工程師的圖紙，卻沒有署名。工程師不是要出名，只希望後人維修時能找到正確指示，不再重犯錯誤。沈嶼立刻明白那種心情。繪圖訓練曾教他，缺乏可靠來源的標記可能誤導旅人好幾代。整個下午，他們抄錄工坊的公開測量資料，記下授權者。工作緩慢且不耀眼，但每一則謹慎記錄都讓下一個問題更準確。

Before dawn, Ren led them to the road behind the workshop. There stood a refuge for travelers who could not cross the mountains alone. He asked Wen to carry a message to a former apprentice living at the next station. The apprentice might know why the public ledger had been divided into three parts. Yu asked whether Ren regretted staying behind when the others pursued ascension. Ren considered the question, then shook his head. “I have seen sheer suffering caused by people who believe they have escaped ordinary responsibility,” he answered. “I would rather repair one chair correctly than build a throne above the clouds.” Wen thought of the child on the Stair and felt his old guilt begin to loosen. He had called his decision a failure because others had judged it so. Ren treated the same choice as the beginning of a useful life. When Wen and Yu left, the workshop lamps were still glowing, and the three circles on the table seemed less like a mystery than a direction.

天亮前，任師傅領他們到工坊後的路，那裡有間收容無法獨自越山旅人的避難所。他請李聞送信給住在下一站的舊學徒；對方也許知道公開見證簿為何分成三部分。沈嶼問任師傅，別人追求飛升時自己留下，是否後悔。任師傅想了想，搖頭：「我看過太多苦難，都是那些自認已逃脫凡人責任的人造成的。我寧願修好一張椅子，也不要在雲上築王座。」李聞想起雲梯上的孩子，心裡多年未散的愧疚開始鬆動。他把自己的決定稱為失敗，是因為別人這麼判斷；任師傅卻把它看成有用人生的起點。離開時工坊仍亮著燈，桌上的三個小圓似乎不再只是謎，而是一個方向。
