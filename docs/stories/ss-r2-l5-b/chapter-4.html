The Tide Office

兩個不能飛升的人，接下了送信任務 · 第 4 / 25 章 · 4000–5000 · 英中對照

Yu could anticipate a storm by watching the birds, but he had never learned to predict bureaucracy. At Salt Province's coastal office, three clerks directed the travelers to three different windows, each demanding a separate stamp. Their denial of a simple travel pass seemed almost comic until Wen noticed the line of commuters waiting behind them. A sea wall had narrowed the road, and every delay exposed the crowd to the rising tide. A distinctive blue mark appeared on the official map beside the nearest harbor. Yu measured the printed circle's diameter and compared it with an old survey. The mark covered the precise location of a vanished bell station. “That cannot be coincidence,” he whispered. Wen reminded him that similarity was not proof. They copied the public map and asked the clerk for the office's fiscal records instead. If money had been spent to close the station, there would be a record of it somewhere, even if the stones had been moved.

沈嶼看鳥就能預料暴風雨，卻始終無法預測官僚程序。在鹽州沿海署裡，三個書記把旅人指向三個不同窗口，每個窗口都要不同印章。他們拒發簡單通行證的作法原本近乎可笑，直到李聞注意到身後排隊的通勤者。海堤使道路變窄，耽擱越久，眾人越容易遇上漲潮。官方地圖上，最近的港口旁有個特別的藍色標記。沈嶼量了印刷圓圈的直徑，與舊測量圖比對，發現標記正覆蓋一座消失的鐘驛。「這不可能是巧合。」他低語。李聞提醒他，相似不是證據。他們抄下公開地圖，轉而索取官署財政紀錄。若有人花錢關閉驛站，即使石頭搬走，總會留下文件。

A marine practitioner named Madam Luo helped them understand the danger outside. She treated fishermen, studied the local tide, and kept careful notes about mortality during the last great flood. The sea wall forced waves into a narrow channel, making a small hazard into a serious one. Some workers had reported dizziness after handling mineral lamps; Luo suspected a plant hormone used in nearby farms, but she admitted that she could not yet identify the cause. Her faculty of observation was stronger than her desire to sound certain. She showed Wen a separate road that ran parallel to the harbor. A fraction of the distance was flooded, but the rest remained safe for walkers. Yu asked why the official map did not show it. Luo answered with a tired smile: “Because maps tell you what offices approve. My patients tell me where their feet can actually go.”

一名叫羅夫人的海岸醫療工作者幫他們了解外面的危險。她替漁夫看病、研究當地潮汐，並仔細記錄上次洪水中的死亡情況。海堤把浪集中到狹窄水道，使原本不大的風險變得嚴重。有些工人碰過礦燈後頭暈；羅夫人懷疑附近農場使用的植物激素，但坦承目前還不能確認原因。她的觀察力比想表現得很確定的欲望更強。她指給李聞一條與港口平行的小路，只有一小段積水，其餘適合步行。沈嶼問官方地圖為何不標。羅夫人疲倦地笑道：「因為地圖告訴你官署批准什麼；病人的腳卻告訴我，路到底能不能走。」

At the far window, a young clerk listened to Wen's plea and began to tremble. She had been ordered to reject all travelers who carried unauthorized letters. The order was from the capital, not from Salt Province, and she could not explain its purpose. Wen tried to reassure her that he did not want her to lose her job. He asked whether a public route inspection could be recorded without revealing private addresses. She agreed and led them to an unlocked storage room where old notices were displayed for citizens. There they uncovered a document authorizing the removal of three bell stations, signed in the name of the First Post. Yu touched the seal but did not take the paper. “If this is genuine,” he said, “the keeper we seek helped close the road.” Wen studied the ink. The signature looked hurried, as if the signer had been interrupted.

最遠窗口的一名年輕書記聽到李聞的請求，手開始發抖。她奉命拒絕所有攜帶未授權信件的旅人。命令來自都城，不是鹽州官署，她也不知道原因。李聞安慰她，他不想害她失去工作。他問能否留下公開路線查核紀錄，而不洩露私人地址。她答應，帶兩人進入一間沒上鎖的儲藏室，裡面展示著可供民眾查閱的舊公告。他們在那裡發現一份核准拆除三座鐘驛的公文，署名竟是「初郵站」。沈嶼碰了碰印章，卻沒有拿走紙張。「如果是真的，」他說，「我們在找的守站人也參與了封路。」李聞細看墨跡，簽名似乎寫得很急，像有人突然打斷書寫。

That night they delivered two letters to families living above the harbor. One asked a daughter to return for her mother's birthday; the other contained a recipe for preserving fish. Neither message would alter a province, but each changed one person's evening. Yu wanted to travel directly to the capital and confront the office responsible for the seal. Wen proposed another course: follow the parallel road, inspect the remaining stations, and gather testimony without forcing anyone to speak. “You are always looking for a path that takes longer,” Yu complained. “And you are always choosing the path that sounds braver,” Wen replied. The argument ended when Luo placed a lantern between them. “Both of you are right about different dangers,” she said. They agreed to carry the document's reference number, not an accusation, to the next station. In the morning the tide was lower, and the road beyond the sea wall was visible again.

當晚兩人把兩封信送到港口上方的家庭。一封請女兒回來替母親過生日，另一封附著保存魚肉的方法。這些話不會改變整個州，卻改變了收信者的夜晚。沈嶼想直奔都城，質問簽署封路命令的官署。李聞提出另一條路：沿平行道路查訪剩餘驛站，蒐集證言，但不強迫任何人說話。「你總挑要走更久的路。」沈嶼抱怨。「你總挑聽起來比較勇敢的路。」李聞回嘴。羅夫人把燈放在兩人中間，終止爭執。「你們都看見了不同的危險。」她說。兩人最後決定帶走公文編號，而不是未經證實的指控。隔天潮水退去，海堤外的小路又露了出來。
