信任的邊界

A Boundary of Trust

兩個不能飛升的人，接下了送信任務 · 第 9 / 25 章 · 4000–5000 · 英中對照 · 校訂中

At the stone pass a uniformed bodyguard stopped them beside a donkey, which the local farmers stubbornly called an ass. The guard explained that the boundary comprised three checkpoints and that only residents with a valid travel version of the new pass were eligible to continue. Wen presented their papers, but the official dismissed the handwritten receipts. Yu asked for an investigator rather than arguing with an armed man. A quiet woman arrived wearing worn sandals and carrying a patched bag. Her name was Inspector Rui. She examined the documents, then asked why the guards had been told to discriminate between local messengers and official couriers. The chief could not answer. Rui said a rule was not valid merely because a uniformed person repeated it. She let the two men wait in a sheltered residence while she checked the order. Wen noticed several vulnerable travelers still standing in the wind and asked whether they, too, could use the shelter.

在石隘，一名穿制服的保鑣在一頭驢旁攔住兩人。當地農夫總固執地稱牠為 ass。守衛說邊界共有三道檢查哨，只有持新版有效通行證的居民才有資格前進。李聞拿出文件，但官員不承認手寫收據。沈嶼要求找調查員，而不是與帶武器的人爭吵。一名穿著舊涼鞋、背著補丁袋的安靜女子來了，她叫芮調查員。她檢查文件，接著問守衛為何接到命令，要歧視地方信使、只放行官方信差。隊長回答不出。芮說，一條規則不會因穿制服的人反覆說就自動有效。她讓兩人在避風的住屋等待查證，李聞看見幾名弱勢旅人仍站在寒風裡，便問他們是否也能進屋。

Rui agreed and soon discovered that the checkpoint order had been issued using an outdated form. It was not the first time someone had tried to undo a public protection by changing a few words. She carried copies of several versions and showed the guards the important difference: the current regulation allowed any traveler with a recorded reason for crossing. A souvenir shop owner volunteered to provide hot tea while the papers were corrected. Wen thanked her, and she told him her husband had once been a courier before losing his hearing. “He was passionate about every delivery,” she said. “A person waiting for a letter isn't a small matter.” Her words stayed with Wen. Yu helped Rui incorporate the handwritten receipts into the official list without exposing the names inside private letters. When the crossing finally opened, the other travelers passed first. Rui offered the couriers no special favor, only the same protection she owed everyone else.

芮同意讓旅人進屋，很快查出檢查哨的命令用的是過時表格。這不是第一次有人只改幾個字，就企圖撤掉公共保障。她帶著幾份不同版本的副本，向守衛展示重要差異：現行規定允許任何有登記過境理由的旅人通過。一位紀念品店老闆娘自願提供熱茶，等文件更正。李聞道謝，她說丈夫曾是信差，後來失去聽力。「他對每次投遞都熱情得不得了，」她說，「等信的人不是小事。」這話留在李聞心裡。沈嶼協助芮把手寫收據納入正式名單，沒有暴露私人信件裡的人名。關口終於開放時，其他旅人先行通過。芮沒有給兩名信差特權，只給了所有人都應享有的保護。

A storm trapped the party overnight in a mountain rest house. A therapist traveling to the southern clinics noticed Wen rubbing his shoulder and offered a simple exercise for the long journey. Wen explained that the pain came from carrying the bag, not from an injury in the sky. Yu listened from the next table, where he was studying an old stone pyramid marked with the three-circle symbol. The therapist asked what the men planned to do after their journey. Wen had no answer. He had been so focused on reaching the First Post that he had not imagined a life afterward. Yu said he hoped to draw maps that admitted uncertainty rather than covering blank spaces with invented certainty. “That sounds like a residence for questions,” the therapist joked. Wen laughed, then realized how rarely he and Yu had talked about a future beyond the next delivery. The thought made the room feel unexpectedly warm.

暴風雪迫使眾人在山間驛舍過夜。一位前往南方診所的治療師注意到李聞揉肩，教他幾個適合長途旅人的伸展動作。李聞解釋疼痛來自背郵袋，不是雲梯上的傷。沈嶼在隔桌研究一座畫有三圓標誌的舊石金字塔，邊聽他們說話。治療師問兩人旅程結束後想做什麼。李聞答不出來。他一直只想抵達初郵站，根本沒想過之後的生活。沈嶼說，希望畫出承認未知的地圖，不要用虛構的確定性填滿空白。「那聽起來像是給問題住的房子。」治療師開玩笑。李聞笑了，接著發現自己和沈嶼很少談到下次投遞之外的未來。這念頭使屋內意外地溫暖。

When the storm cleared, Rui returned with one final question. A private messenger had been seen carrying the same black envelope mark toward the northern mountains. She wanted to know whether the men had met him. Wen said truthfully that they had not. He could not disclose the contents of his own envelope, but he agreed to report any public sign of a threat to travelers. Rui accepted the answer. At the last checkpoint she gave them a sealed notice from the border office, recorded it in their ledger, and watched them leave. Wen touched the packet at his chest. His duty did not make him above questioning; it made honest answers more necessary. Ahead the path entered a narrow grove, and Yu slowed until their shoulders nearly met. “You didn't answer the therapist,” he said. “What happens after this?” Wen looked at him. “I might like to keep walking,” he answered, “if you're the one drawing the map.”

風雪停後，芮帶來最後一個問題。有人看到另一名私人信使帶著同樣黑信封標誌往北山走，她想知道兩人是否碰過。李聞如實回答沒有。他不能透露自己信封內容，但答應若發現威脅旅人的公開跡象便會通報。芮接受這個回答。在最後一道關口，她交給兩人一份封好的邊境公告，記入見證簿，目送他們離開。李聞摸了摸胸前的信。責任不使他免於被質問，反而讓誠實回答更加重要。前方小路進入狹窄樹林，沈嶼放慢腳步，兩人肩膀幾乎碰到。「你還沒回答治療師。」沈嶼說，「這趟結束後呢？」李聞看著他：「如果畫地圖的人是你，我也許願意一直走下去。」

At the grove's edge, melting snow turned the earth into fluid mud. Yu tested each foothold with a branch before Wen followed. Neither man mistook caution for a lack of courage. Their trust was becoming a set of ordinary habits, built one careful crossing at a time.

樹林邊緣，融雪把泥土變成流動的泥漿。沈嶼先用樹枝試探每一步，李聞再跟上。兩人都不把謹慎誤認為缺乏勇氣。他們的信任正變成普通習慣，一次小心渡越就累積一分。
