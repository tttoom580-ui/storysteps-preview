Passage Through the Market

The road ended at a market so crowded that its lanes looked like moving rivers. People in ragged clothes sold vegetables beside a polished salon, while a traveling singer performed a tribute to the sky. Wen and Yu needed a visa for the mountain border, but the issuing office had suspended new applications because of a shortage of paper. The market was in chaos. A trader shouted that a mysterious alien traveler had stolen official stamps, and bystanders repeated the myth without asking for evidence. Yu suggested finding the actual officer before believing the rumor. Wen approached a gathering of porters and asked how long the shortage had lasted. One man said two days, another said three weeks. The wide spectrum of answers made Wen cautious. He did not want to minimize their frustration, but he also refused to turn confusion into a charge against a stranger nobody could identify.

道路在一座擁擠得像流動河水的市場盡頭消失。穿破衣的人在精緻美容院旁賣蔬菜，流浪歌手唱著獻給天空的頌歌。李聞和沈嶼需要山境簽證，但發證所因紙張短缺而暫停受理。市場亂成一團。一名商販大喊某個神祕外來旅人偷走官印，旁人不求證便把傳聞當神話重複。沈嶼建議先找真正的辦事員，不要相信謠言。李聞問聚在一處的挑夫，紙張究竟短缺多久。有人說兩天，有人說三週。答案範圍太廣，使他更加謹慎。他不想輕忽眾人的挫折，也不願把混亂變成對一個無法辨認的陌生人的指控。

At the office a tired clerk explained that the stamps had not been stolen. A cart had stalled on the mountain road, leaving the new paper trapped beneath sacks of grain. The clerk had tried to preserve flexibility by issuing temporary passes, but a supervisor insisted on theoretical perfection: every document must use the newest form, regardless of the circumstances. Yu asked whether the couriers were entitled to a handwritten receipt under the published travel rules. The clerk checked the manual and found that they were. No one had mentioned the exception because the notice had fallen behind a shelf. Wen helped hang a clear copy near the entrance. The small act reduced the crowd's anger more effectively than an argument about the stranger. A porter offered to guide them to the broken cart, though he warned that the road was rough and the daylight nearly gone.

在辦事處，疲倦書記解釋印章根本沒被偷。貨車卡在山路上，新紙張被壓在一袋袋穀物下面。書記曾想靈活處理，發給臨時通行證，但主管堅持理論上的完美：不管實際情況，每份文件都得用最新表格。沈嶼詢問，依公開旅規，信差是否有權領取手寫收據。書記翻查手冊，發現確實可以。沒人提過這項例外，是因為公告掉到了架子後面。李聞幫忙把清楚的副本貼在入口。這小動作比爭論陌生人更有效地平息眾怒。一名挑夫自願帶他們去看壞掉的貨車，但提醒山路崎嶇，天光所剩不多。

Beyond the market, the cart lay sideways in a ditch. No one had been injured, but a wheel had sunk into loose earth. Yu inspected the axle and planned a repair using rope, stones, and several strong arms. The porter proposed an easier solution: burn the cart and claim the cargo had been destroyed. Wen refused. “There is no need to manufacture a disaster,” he said. Together they moved the sacks onto dry ground and raised the wheel. The repair required patience, not power. When the cart finally rolled forward, the porter laughed with relief. A child watching nearby asked whether the men were famous cultivators. “I have no powers whatsoever,” Wen answered, making the child grin at his awkward formality. Yu teased him for sounding like a very serious judge. Their laughter followed them down the hillside, a rare light moment on a journey that had begun with loss.

市場外，貨車側翻在溝裡。沒人受傷，但一只車輪陷入鬆土。沈嶼檢查車軸，打算用繩索、石頭和幾雙有力的手修復。挑夫提出更簡單的法子：燒掉貨車，謊稱貨物已毀。李聞拒絕：「沒必要製造災難。」眾人把袋子搬到乾地，合力抬起車輪。修理靠的是耐心，不是法力。車子終於再次滾動，挑夫鬆一口氣笑了。旁邊小孩問兩人是不是有名的修行者。「我完全沒有法力。」李聞說得過分正式，引得孩子咧嘴笑。沈嶼取笑他說話像一本正經的法官。笑聲一路跟著兩人下山，成了這趟從失落開始的旅程裡少見的輕快時刻。

The office reopened the next morning, and the waiting crowd received temporary passes. A singer offered a short performance in thanks, but Wen asked him to dedicate it to the workers who had moved the cart. He did not want their names to become another myth about heroes. Yu inspected their papers twice before they departed. One page contained a handwritten warning: travelers proceeding north might be required to attend an official hearing about unauthorized mail. “Now we have a choice,” Yu said. “Go back or keep going.” Wen looked at the ledger, where each delivered letter had a name and a witness mark. “We chose when we accepted the first envelope,” he replied. They left the market with supplies bought at fair prices and no new debt. Ahead, the valley narrowed into a stone pass. The cold wind carried the sound of an unseen bell, faint enough that either man might have imagined it.

隔天辦事處重開，等待的人領到臨時通行證。歌手想演一曲致謝，李聞請他把歌獻給搬貨車的工人。他不願讓他們的名字變成另一則英雄神話。出發前沈嶼把文件查了兩遍，其中一頁有手寫警告：北行旅人可能被要求出席非法郵件的官方聽證。「現在我們可以選，」沈嶼說，「折返或繼續。」李聞看著見證簿，每封送達的信旁都有姓名與見證記號。「接下第一封信時，我們就選過了。」他答。他們以合理價格補充物資，沒有欠新債，隨後離開市場。前方谷地收窄成石隘，寒風送來看不見的鐘聲，細微得讓兩人都不確定是否只是錯覺。

The porter later described his strategic choice to travel before sunrise, when a thin vein of shade lay across the steepest path. Wen checked his own comprehension of the directions by drawing the route in the dirt and asking the porter to correct it. That small exchange kept their next journey from depending on a misunderstood word.

挑夫後來解釋他的策略性選擇：日出前出發，最陡的道路上仍有一道細長陰影。李聞把路線畫在土上，請挑夫更正，以確認自己理解無誤。這個小小確認避免下一段旅程因誤聽一個字而出錯。
