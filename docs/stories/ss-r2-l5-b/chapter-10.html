乾旱研究所

The Drought Institute

兩個不能飛升的人，接下了送信任務 · 第 10 / 25 章 · 4000–5000 · 英中對照 · 校訂中

North of the pass stood a research institute built beside a wilderness that had once been covered by forest. The drought had made its gardens brittle, and every plant seemed to carry a different kind of warning. Yu and Wen arrived seeking records of the old bell generator, but the director required a preliminary interview. A counselor asked about their training, health, and purpose. Wen mentioned a mild dust allergy because the workroom was full of old paper; he did not want the staff to mistake a cough for an illness. The director, a rational woman named Professor Ji, was interested in collaboration but wary of sudden claims. “If you cite a document,” she said, “show me the page. If you make an interpretation, tell me why.” Wen appreciated the rule. It would keep their investigation from becoming a thriller in which every shadow concealed an enemy. They handed over the public reference numbers and requested access to a catalog, not to locked personal files.

山隘北方有座研究所，旁邊是過去曾被森林覆蓋的荒野。乾旱使庭園植物脆弱，每株都像用不同方式警告世人。沈嶼與李聞來找舊鐘發生器的紀錄，但所長要求先接受初步訪談。一名輔導員詢問他們的受訓背景、健康與目的。李聞提到自己對粉塵有輕微過敏，因為工作室堆滿舊紙，他不想讓人把咳嗽誤會成疾病。所長紀教授是位理性女子，願意合作卻警惕突如其來的指控。「引用文件就拿出頁碼，」她說，「提出解釋就說清理由。」李聞喜歡這規則，它能避免調查變成每道陰影都有敵人的驚悚故事。他們交出公開公文編號，申請查閱目錄，而非上鎖的私人檔案。

Professor Ji displayed a descriptive model of the mountain's water cycle. Its variables included rainfall, population, and the power drawn by an old terminal beneath the capital. The energy system had originally been designed to move messages across great distances, but it had begun drawing moisture from the lower valleys after repeated upgrades. The institute's first report had been ignored, then buried under a bulk delivery of unrelated documents. A junior researcher wanted to exclaim that the court had caused every drought in the region. Ji stopped him. “We can show a connection,” she said, “but we cannot yet compel anyone to accept one explanation.” Wen felt overwhelmed by the scale of the problem. Letters, water, and the sky ladder seemed linked, yet the evidence remained incomplete. Yu helped him grill the numbers, asking how many tests had been repeated and which conditions had changed. Their questions made the researchers more willing to share methods rather than conclusions.

紀教授展示山區水循環的描述模型。變數包含雨量、人口，以及都城下方一個舊終端設備抽取的能量。那套系統原本用來遠距離傳訊，但多次升級後開始抽走低谷的水分。研究所第一份報告被忽視，後來又埋在一大批無關文件之下。一名年輕研究員想大喊是王庭造成整區乾旱，紀教授制止他。「我們能指出關聯，」她說，「還不能強迫所有人接受單一解釋。」李聞被問題的規模壓得喘不過氣。信件、水與天梯似乎連在一起，證據卻還不完整。沈嶼幫忙仔細盤問數字：做過幾次重複測試？條件改變了哪些？這些提問反而使研究員更願意分享方法，而不只是結論。

During lunch the scientists served vegetables cooked on a small outdoor grill. A researcher explained that the institute had created a new system for recording public observations because old reports disappeared too easily. Each village could submit a dated measurement and receive a copy in return. The idea sounded radical to some officials, who preferred records to flow only toward the capital. Ji thought the approach was practical: local people were better placed to notice a broken spring than distant administrators. Wen asked whether their courier ledger could help carry measurements between villages. “Only with permission,” he added quickly. Ji smiled at his caution and agreed. She prepared a packet of public charts for the next station and asked them not to present the figures as a final answer. Yu took notes while Wen practiced explaining the difference between an observed fact and a proposed cause. By evening the lesson felt as important as any spell he had learned before the failed ascent.

午餐時，科學家用小型戶外烤爐烹調蔬菜。一名研究員說，舊報告太容易消失，因此研究所設計了新的公開觀察紀錄制度：各村可送交有日期的測量資料，也能拿回一份副本。有些官員認為這主意過於激進，偏好所有紀錄只往都城集中。紀教授覺得很實際：當地人比遙遠行政官更容易發現乾涸的泉水。李聞問信差見證簿是否能幫忙在村間傳遞測量結果，隨即補充：「當然要取得同意。」紀教授笑著同意，準備一包公開圖表，請他們不要把數字說成最終答案。沈嶼做筆記，李聞則練習區分觀察到的事實與推測的原因。到了晚上，這堂課的重要性已不亞於他失敗飛升前學過的任何法術。

Before they left, Ji warned them that the northern terminal might be under military control. A guard could mistake their charts for forbidden documents, and a frightened official might try to take them by force. The professor advised them to keep the institute's public stamp visible and to call her if anyone questioned the measurements. She also gave Wen a sealed personal letter for her brother, who worked at the next crossing. The two couriers accepted it under their usual rules. On the steps, Yu looked toward the dry horizon and admitted that he had once admired the power of the old sky system. He had dreamed of mastering it and escaping ordinary limits. Now the broken landscape showed what such power could cost when no one questioned its operation. Wen said he did not regret failing to ascend, though he still sometimes regretted the loneliness that followed. Yu put a hand on his shoulder. “You are not traveling alone now,” he said.

出發前，紀教授提醒他們，北方終端可能由軍方控制。守衛可能把公開圖表誤認為禁件，害怕的官員也可能想強行扣留。她建議把研究所公章放在明顯處，若有人質疑測量資料就請對方聯絡她。她還交給李聞一封私人密封信，要寄給在下一個關口工作的兄長。兩人照慣例登記收件。走到台階上，沈嶼望向乾燥的天際線，承認自己以前很崇拜舊天空系統的力量，夢想掌握它、擺脫凡人的限制。如今破敗地貌卻讓他看見，當沒有人質疑運作方式時，力量可能付出什麼代價。李聞說，他不後悔沒有飛升，只是有時仍懊悔失敗後的孤獨。沈嶼把手放在他肩上：「你現在不是一個人走了。」

At the institute's gate, a cook handed them dried fruit and warned that hurried eating could trouble digestion on the steep road. Wen thanked her and shared the food with Yu. The advice was small beside the terminal research, but it belonged to the same principle: a journey depended on care for the bodies that made it possible.

在研究所門口，廚娘交給他們乾果，提醒爬陡路時吃太急會影響消化。李聞道謝，與沈嶼分食。和終端研究相比，這建議很小，卻出自同樣道理：旅程要走得下去，也得照顧讓旅程成真的身體。
