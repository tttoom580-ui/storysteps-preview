The Archive in the Schoolyard

兩個不能飛升的人，接下了送信任務 · 第 7 / 25 章 · 4000–5000 · 英中對照 · 校訂中

The old bell station stood behind a spacious school, now used as a storehouse for colonial records. A sophomore student named Han was sorting books in the backyard when the couriers arrived. His task was to stack damaged papers in dry boxes, but he had found a page that did not belong. It described the acquisition of a mountain valley by an ancient governor, a transaction said to have brought every family a generous bonus. Han did not comprehend how the claim could be true. His grandmother still kept a scrap of cloth showing the boundaries of her family's land, and no one had paid them anything. Yu examined both records and explained that two documents could describe the same place from different positions. The old charter called the valley an empty resource; the cloth marked fields, graves, and meeting trees. Wen saw the implication at once: official silence was sometimes produced by a choice of words, not the absence of people.

舊鐘驛立在一所寬敞學校後方，如今被當成殖民時代檔案的倉庫。兩名信差抵達時，二年級學生阿翰正在後院整理書籍。他得把受損文件疊進乾燥箱，卻發現一頁不屬於那裡的紀錄。紙上說古代總督收購一處山谷，還宣稱每戶都得到豐厚補償。阿翰不明白這怎會是真的；祖母至今留著一塊布，標著家族土地邊界，家人從未領過錢。沈嶼檢查兩份紀錄，解釋同一個地方可能因書寫者立場不同而被描寫成不同樣貌。舊憲章把山谷當成空地資源，布上卻畫著田地、墳墓與議事樹。李聞立刻看出其中含意：官方沉默有時不是因為沒有人，而是因為有人選擇了特定字眼。

Han's teacher wanted to incorporate the cloth into a public history exhibit, but an administrator disapproved. The school was under surveillance because someone had stolen old land certificates. The administrator feared that another display would encourage risky arguments about property. Wen asked whether the teacher had permission to copy the page. She did, but she refused to reveal the cloth's owner without consent. Yu used a pencil to trace the old river line, then compared it with a current map. The line passed beneath the sealed bell station and appeared to penetrate the mountain at a point no one now visited. Han brought them a block of chalk and drew a symbolic version of the river on the yard wall. “It looks like a spine,” he said. Wen watched the pattern form and suddenly remembered the same curve on the black envelope. The sign of three circles might not represent three heavenly gates. It might represent three river crossings.

阿翰的老師想把那塊布納入公開歷史展覽，但校方行政人員反對。最近有人偷走土地憑證，學校正受監控；行政人員擔心再次展示會激起危險的財產爭論。李聞詢問老師是否獲准複印那頁文件。她有許可，卻拒絕在未經同意時公開布主人的身分。沈嶼用鉛筆描出舊河線，再和現代地圖比較。河線從封閉鐘驛下方流過，似乎在一個如今無人去的地方穿入山腹。阿翰拿來粉筆，在院牆上畫出象徵性的河流。「看起來像脊椎。」他說。李聞看著圖案浮現，忽然想起黑信封上也有相同彎曲。三個圓圈的標誌也許不是三重天門，而是三處渡河點。

The teacher invited the travelers to speak at a meeting about historical transit routes. A former republican councilor argued that every village should govern its own roads. Others feared that a private company would acquire the restored line and charge high fares. Yu explained their modest proposal: allow the community to inspect the route, keep letters in a witness ledger, and let local people decide who maintained the path. Wen did not mention the black envelope. A public discussion was not permission to expose a private message. When a parent questioned the value of old records, Han held up his grandmother's cloth. “If a map leaves our home blank,” he said, “our home does not disappear.” The room fell silent. After the meeting, the administrator offered a cautious agreement: the school could preserve copies of the records while protecting the originals. It was not a victory over anyone, only a workable next step.

老師邀請兩位旅人參加歷史交通路線座談。一位前共和派議員主張各村應自己管理道路，其他人卻擔心私人公司買下修復的路線後收取高額通行費。沈嶼解釋他們務實的提議：讓社區查核路線、在見證簿記錄信件，再由當地居民決定誰維護道路。李聞沒有提黑信封；公開討論不代表可以曝光私人訊息。一名家長質疑舊紀錄有何價值，阿翰便舉起祖母的布：「地圖把我們家畫成空白，不代表我們家就不存在。」屋內頓時安靜。會後，行政人員謹慎同意讓學校保存副本，同時保護原件。這不是戰勝誰的勝利，而是可行的下一步。

When the couriers prepared to leave, the students carried boxes of scrap paper into the yard for recycling. Han gave them a carefully folded tracing of the ancient river, signed by his teacher and grandmother. He said it was not proof of ownership, only a record they were allowed to use. Yu thanked him for understanding the distinction. On the path beyond the school, Wen asked Yu whether his training had ever included the three crossings. “No,” Yu answered, “but the academy taught us to treat certain maps as sacred and others as disposable.” He folded the tracing beside the black letter. A faint mark on the stone walkway matched the first river bend, and beyond it stood a deserted milestone. They could now follow a route older than the official roads. As the school bell rang behind them, Wen realized that an archive was not merely a room full of old documents. It was a promise that someone in the future could ask better questions.

信差準備離開時，學生們正把廢紙箱搬到院子回收。阿翰交給他們一張仔細摺好的古河線描圖，上面有老師與祖母的簽名。他說這不是土地所有權證明，只是獲准使用的紀錄。沈嶼謝謝他理解差別。走出學校後，李聞問沈嶼，受訓時是否學過三渡口。「沒有，」沈嶼回答，「但學院教我們，把某些地圖當聖物，把另一些當垃圾。」他把描圖摺好，放在黑信旁。石道上有一道淡痕，與第一個河彎一致，再往前是荒廢的里程碑。他們終於能沿著比官道更古老的路走。身後校鐘響起時，李聞明白檔案庫不只是堆滿舊文件的房間，也是留給未來的人提出更好問題的一份承諾。

Before they left, Han handed Yu a small chunk of chalk and a note about calcium deposits on the old stone bridges. The deposits marked places where spring water had once flowed. Yu kept the note with the river tracing; even a school lesson could turn into a useful clue when someone cared enough to observe.

臨走前阿翰交給沈嶼一小塊粉筆，以及一張關於舊石橋鈣質沉積的筆記。沉積痕跡標記著泉水曾流經的地方。沈嶼把筆記與河線描圖放在一起；只要有人認真觀察，連學校課程也能成為有用線索。
