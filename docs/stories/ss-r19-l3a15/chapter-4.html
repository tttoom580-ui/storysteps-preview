The Registry Fee

世界只剩一格技能欄，我選了借用 · 第 4 / 25 章 · 2000–3000 · 英中對照 · 校訂中

用互動版查詞、朗讀與收藏

The Skill Registry charges poor players for permission to lend powers to family members. Ren felt the meaning of this new event before the full crowd understood it. In Slot City, each unusual sign could become a rumor by evening, and rumors were hard to pull back once they had spread. Sia stood beside the nearest witness and asked for the sequence of actions, not a dramatic conclusion. Some details contradicted what they had learned the day before. That was disappointing and useful at the same time. Ren opened a fresh page, wrote the date at the top, and decided the truth would need more than one voice.

技能登記局向窮人收費，連借技能給家人都需要許可。 群眾尚未完全理解時，任已感到這件新事的分量。在技能城，每個異常徵兆到了晚上都可能變成謠言，而謠言一旦傳開就很難收回。希雅站到最近的證人身邊，詢問行動的先後順序，而非戲劇性的結論。有些細節與昨天得到的資訊矛盾，既令人失望也很有用。任翻開新的一頁，在上方寫下日期，決定真相需要不止一個人的聲音。

While others argued over a fast solution, Sia asked who had the right to make the final decision. Ren could have answered with a name or a title. Instead, they made a list of everyone whose home, work, or safety would change. The list was longer than expected. Some people could come to a meeting; others needed a visit or a written invitation. There was no way to make a difficult choice painless, but a choice made without listening would be worse. The team planned another discussion and made sure the invitation did not require wealth or rank.

其他人爭論最快解法時，希雅問誰有權作最後決定。任本可回答一個姓名或頭銜，卻改列出家園、工作或安全會受到影響的所有人。清單比預期長。有些人能來開會，另一些需要拜訪或書面邀請。艱難選擇不可能毫無痛苦，但不傾聽就做決定更糟。團隊安排下一次討論，確保受邀不需要財富或階級。

There was a practical problem behind the dramatic story. A route could look short on paper and still be impossible for a tired person to use. Ren walked it at a normal pace while Sia counted the steps and listed obstacles. At the narrowest point they stopped because the safest answer was not the fastest one. Someone suggested using the Borrow token as proof that everything was under control. Ren refused the easy claim. An impressive object could not replace a careful test, especially when strangers might have to trust it without knowing the story behind it.

戲劇性的故事背後有個實際問題。路線在紙上看起來很短，對疲憊的人卻可能走不了。任以正常速度走一遍，希雅數著步數並列出障礙。他們在最窄處停下，因為最安全的答案未必最快。有人提議用借用令牌證明一切都在掌握中，任拒絕了這種輕率說法。再令人印象深刻的物件，也不能取代仔細測試，尤其當陌生人必須在不知道背景時相信它。

During a pause in the investigation, Sia unfolded an old language ledger. Five marked entries on the first line read "ambition", "barn", "collar", "concert", "confuse". The next line held "dim", "freeze", "humor", "junior", "novelist". None was a magic password or an answer by itself; each was a fragment of a larger history. Ren asked who had written the marks, why they had been kept, and whether they belonged to the same account. It would have been easy to declare the puzzle solved simply because the words were now visible. Instead, they copied the terms, recorded the source, and returned to the unresolved problem outside the room.

調查暫停時，希雅攤開一本舊語詞紀錄。第一行的五個註記寫著「ambition」、「barn」、「collar」、「concert」、「confuse」；下一行則有「dim」、「freeze」、「humor」、「junior」、「novelist」。它們都不是魔法密碼，也無法單獨解答問題；每個詞只是更大段歷史的片段。任詢問誰寫下這些字、為何保存，以及它們是否屬於同一份記錄。只因文字可見就宣稱解開謎題很容易，但他們只抄下詞條、標明來源，便回到室外仍未解決的問題。

News of the plan reached the Skill Registry before sunset. The official reply was quick, polished, and strangely certain. Ren read it twice, looking for the questions it refused to answer. Sia noticed that it described a public problem as a private disagreement, although many witnesses had been affected. They did not accuse anyone merely because the answer sounded unpleasant. Instead, they requested the original documents and the name of the person responsible for storing them. A rival deserved the chance to respond to a clear question, but not the right to hide evidence behind an impressive signature.

日落前，計畫的消息傳到技能登記局。官方回覆快速、漂亮，而且確定得古怪。任讀了兩次，尋找其中刻意避開的問題。希雅注意到它把影響多名證人的公共問題描述成私人爭執。他們沒有因回答難聽就隨意指控，而是要求原始文件與保管人的姓名。對手有機會回答清楚的問題，卻沒有權利躲在漂亮簽名後藏匿證據。

During a pause in the investigation, Sia unfolded an old language ledger. Five marked entries on the first line read "onion", "painter", "precious", "response", "roast". The next line held "spray", "squirrel", "tap", "traveler", "vision". None was a magic password or an answer by itself; each was a fragment of a larger history. Ren asked who had written the marks, why they had been kept, and whether they belonged to the same account. It would have been easy to declare the puzzle solved simply because the words were now visible. Instead, they copied the terms, recorded the source, and returned to the unresolved problem outside the room.

調查暫停時，希雅攤開一本舊語詞紀錄。第一行的五個註記寫著「onion」、「painter」、「precious」、「response」、「roast」；下一行則有「spray」、「squirrel」、「tap」、「traveler」、「vision」。它們都不是魔法密碼，也無法單獨解答問題；每個詞只是更大段歷史的片段。任詢問誰寫下這些字、為何保存，以及它們是否屬於同一份記錄。只因文字可見就宣稱解開謎題很容易，但他們只抄下詞條、標明來源，便回到室外仍未解決的問題。

After the meeting, Sia walked with Ren toward a quieter road. There was still a great deal to repair, and the group had not agreed on every detail. Ren admitted being afraid of failing the people who had offered their trust. Sia answered that the aim was not to become impossible to question. It was to make a plan that could be corrected before anybody's pride became everybody's danger. They checked the next appointment and promised to bring another pair of eyes. Friendship did not remove the risk. It made admitting mistakes possible, and that changed how they understood courage.

會議後，希雅陪任走向較安靜的道路。仍有許多事情待修，團隊也不是每項細節都同意。任坦承害怕辜負那些信任自己的人。希雅說，目標不是變成不容質疑的人，而是讓計畫能在某人的自尊危害大家之前被改正。他們確認下次約定，答應多找一雙眼睛來檢查。友情不會消除風險，但它讓認錯變得可能，也改變了他們對勇氣的理解。

Before leaving, Ren wrote one line at the bottom of the page: the day's answer could still be corrected. Sia checked that people affected by the decision could read it and reply. A messenger brought news that the next meeting would concern 'One Minute of Healing'. Neither knew precisely what this new matter would demand. They did know what should not be traded away to save a few hours: honest records, a safe path, and the right of someone weaker to disagree. Ren carried the unfinished notes toward Slot City, listening to the footsteps behind them and thinking of the question that morning would bring.

離開前，任在紙頁底下寫道：今天的答案仍然可以訂正。希雅確認受決定影響的人能讀到並回應。信使傳來消息，下次會議將處理〈一分鐘治療〉。兩人都不確定新事件要求什麼，卻知道哪些東西不能只為節省幾小時就被交換掉：誠實紀錄、安全道路，以及弱者提出異議的權利。任帶著未完成的筆記走向技能城，聽著背後腳步聲，想著明早會出現的問題。
