運河裡的人魚

The Mermaid in the Canal

逆鐘旅店 · 第 11 / 25 章 · 5000–6000 · 英中對照 · 校訂中

用互動版查詞、朗讀與收藏

Yuna's allies met in a converted warehouse beside the canal. A stone mermaid decorated its entrance, an absurd reminder of the city's attempt to beautify its industrial past. Inside, a young hacker named Tomas had connected stolen record readers to an old fireproof cabinet. He had won local acclaim for restoring missing school transcripts, but this work was different. "The problem is ambiguity," he said. "One number can refer to a patient's body, a donor's memory, or an administrative identity." He showed Yuna the geographical distribution of altered records. The pattern revolved around a small number of clinics owned by Corven's relatives. A layman might have missed it, but Tomas's software made the connections visible. Yuna asked whether he could liberate the original names. He answered that the records could be recovered, but not safely published until every affected patient had been consulted.

優娜的盟友在運河旁改建的倉庫聚會。入口有座石雕人魚，是城市企圖美化工業歷史留下的荒唐裝飾。屋內，年輕駭客托瑪斯把偷來的資料讀取器接到舊式防火櫃。他曾因修復失蹤的學校成績單受到地方讚揚，但這次工作不同。「問題在於歧義，」他說，「同一個編號可能代表病患的身體、捐贈者的記憶，或行政身分。」他展示遭修改紀錄的地理分布。所有異常都圍繞少數幾家由柯文親戚擁有的診所。外行人可能看不出來，托瑪斯的程式卻讓關聯清晰可見。優娜問能否恢復原本的姓名。托瑪斯說能找回，但在諮詢每名受影響病患前，不該直接公開。

As evening fell, a cold draught slipped beneath the warehouse door. The thermometer showed forty-eight degrees Fahrenheit, low enough that their breath became visible. Miro set out bowls of yogurt and bread, an odd supper assembled from miscellaneous donations. Yuna found a bent utensil beside the stove and used it to lift a hot lid. The small domestic task helped steady her hands. On the wall hung a photograph of workers enjoying a rare outing after the canal's completion. One child in the picture resembled Ren. Yuna stared until Tomas reminded her that resemblance was not identification. "A single occurrence can mislead us," he said. "A repeated pattern deserves investigation." She knew he was right, but it was painful to treat a beloved face as uncertain evidence. She returned to the records and asked him to keep searching.

傍晚，冷風從倉庫門縫鑽進。溫度計顯示華氏四十八度，已冷到人們呼出的氣都看得見。米羅端出優格與麵包，那是零星捐贈拼出的奇怪晚餐。優娜在爐邊找到彎曲的餐具，用它掀起滾燙鍋蓋。這個小小家務動作讓她的手平穩下來。牆上掛著照片，是運河完工後工人難得出遊的紀念。其中一個孩子很像任恩。優娜盯著看，直到托瑪斯提醒，長得相像不等於身分相同。「單一事件可能誤導我們，」他說，「重複出現的模式才值得追查。」她知道他說得對，卻仍因必須把深愛的面容當作不確定證據而痛苦。她回到檔案旁，請他繼續搜尋。

Tomas discovered that the city had duplicated the recording schedules. One official calendar showed ceremonies for public remembrance; another contained unscheduled procedures at the clinics. The two lists matched whenever a donor's legal status changed. "This is not a coincidence," he said. "It is an operating system." Hale warned that publishing the findings would prompt accusations of bodily theft and could endanger patients already frightened of their own memories. Yuna began to ponder whether truth could become another weapon if released without care. She did not want to cast the people who had accepted treatment as villains. Many had been unconscious, young, or desperate. Miro suggested asking the patients themselves what they wished to reveal. Tomas offered a secure method for anonymous testimony, though he could not guarantee that the government would never trace the messages. Their plan would require patience and protection, not merely a dramatic announcement.

托瑪斯發現城市複製了兩套儀式時程。一套官方行事曆列著公開追憶典禮，另一套則記錄診所未公開的手術。每當捐贈者法定身分變動，兩份時間表就會吻合。「這不是巧合，」他說，「這是一套運作系統。」海爾警告，公開發現會引起「偷走身體」的指控，可能危及已被自身記憶嚇壞的病患。優娜開始思考，若不加謹慎，真相是否也會成為武器。她不想把接受治療的人都當成壞人。許多人當時昏迷、年幼，或已走投無路。米羅建議讓病患自己決定願意揭露什麼。托瑪斯提出安全的匿名證詞方法，但不能保證政府永遠無法追查來源。這項計畫需要耐心和保護，不是一場戲劇性的宣布就能完成。

The first patient message arrived near midnight: I remember a mother singing to me, but my own mother cannot sing. Whose voice is it? A second described waking after surgery with a fear of water never experienced before. Soon messages multiplied. Tomas had to persevere as the machine slowed under their weight. Yuna read each account, refusing to decide which was more important. Hale offered to arrange legal assistance and asked volunteers to check the facts when possible. They would neither dismiss suffering because it was difficult to prove nor accept every memory as literal history. Outside, water moved through the canal beneath the stone mermaid. Yuna remembered the city's motto about order and mercy. For the first time she imagined a different definition of order: not a command from above, but the patient arrangement of evidence so that vulnerable people could speak without being crushed. The clerk offered to help make a second copy of the register, but Yuna asked him to leave out the addresses of patients still in hiding. They compared the two versions line by line. His first instinct was to preserve every detail, as though completeness alone guaranteed justice. She pointed to the frightened families waiting outside. Records should protect people, she said, not turn them into targets. He crossed out the addresses and signed his name beneath the correction.

第一則病患訊息在午夜前抵達：「我記得母親唱歌給我聽，可是我真正的母親不會唱歌。那是誰的聲音？」第二則寫著，自己手術醒來後突然怕水，然而生病前從未有這種經驗。訊息很快倍增，機器因負荷變重而變慢，托瑪斯仍持續努力。優娜一篇篇讀，不願替誰決定哪段更重要。海爾主動安排法律協助，並請志工盡可能核實事實。他們既不會因痛苦難以證明就否定，也不會把每段回憶都當成字面上的歷史。外頭，水流在石雕人魚下穿過運河。優娜想起城市那句「秩序即仁慈」的格言。她第一次想像另一種秩序：不是上位者下達命令，而是耐心整理證據，讓脆弱的人也能說話，不至於被壓垮。 職員主動協助複製第二份登記簿，優娜卻要求刪去仍在躲藏的病患地址。兩人逐行比較兩個版本。他原本認為保留所有細節就能保障公義。她指向外頭等候的恐懼家庭，說紀錄應當保護人，不能把人變成獵物。他刪掉地址，並在修正處簽上自己的名字。
