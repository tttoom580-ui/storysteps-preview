沒有主人的田地

Fields Without Owners

逆鐘旅店 · 第 7 / 25 章 · 5000–6000 · 英中對照 · 校訂中

Hale's route led them through an abandoned plantation once owned by the old royalty. Workers had planted vegetables where profitable crops used to grow, and a few elderly farmers still guided a horse-drawn plow. The former owner's mansion was now a handicraft school. Its director, Nessa, was a lesbian woman whose partner had spent years translating medical documents for families denied official assistance. Nessa did not present her identity as a challenge to visitors; she simply introduced her partner as family and asked whether anyone needed tea. A bulky cabinet stood near the entrance, filled with letters rescued during the dictatorship. "The council deems these papers dangerous," Nessa said. "I think the real danger lies in the people who want nobody to compare one document with another." Yuna recognized the same stamped forms she had found at the border market. The lies had spread across generations.

海爾指的路穿過一座廢棄種植園，過去曾由舊王室擁有。工人已在原先種植高價作物的土地上改種蔬菜，幾名老農仍用馬拉犁耕地。前園主的宅邸如今變成手工藝學校。校長妮莎是一名女同志，她的伴侶多年來替無法取得官方援助的家庭翻譯醫療文件。妮莎並未把自己的身分當成對旅人的挑戰，只是平靜地介紹伴侶是家人，隨即問大家要不要喝茶。入口附近擺著一座笨重的櫥櫃，收滿獨裁統治時期搶救出的信件。「議會認定這些紙張很危險，」妮莎說，「我倒覺得，真正危險的是那些不准人們把文件拿來比對的人。」優娜認出邊境市場那種雙重蓋章表格。謊言早已延伸好幾代。

Nessa's partner worked as an interpreter at hearings for families from different regions. She showed Yuna three testimonies about the same child, each giving a different birthday. The dates had been altered so that the child could be classified as a legal donor at the time of treatment. "We were told these were trifles," she said, "not worth delaying an operation." But those small changes had made consent meaningless. Yuna felt a sharp desire to expose every official involved. Nessa warned that anger alone would not navigate the legal maze. They needed the original register, proof of the alterations, and people who could testify without being forced to surrender more memories. At the edge of the garden, a pebble lay on a path cut by wagon wheels. Yuna picked it up and imagined how a small stone could spur a wheel to turn aside. Evidence, too, might redirect a much larger machine.

妮莎的伴侶在聽證會擔任口譯，協助不同地區的家庭。她拿出三份關於同一孩子的證詞，每份生日都不相同。日期遭到修改，好讓孩子在接受治療時被歸類為合法捐贈者。「他們說那只是小事，」她說，「不值得為此延誤手術。」但那些細小改動已讓同意權失去意義。優娜強烈地想揭發所有涉案官員。妮莎提醒，光憑憤怒無法走出法律迷宮。他們需要原始登記簿、修改證據，以及能在不被迫再次交出記憶的情況下作證的人。花園邊，一顆小石子躺在被車輪壓出的路上。優娜撿起石子，想像它如何讓大輪子偏離方向。證據或許也能改變一部龐大機器的走向。

At sunset they climbed a lofty ridge overlooking the eastern plain. Workers were returning from the fields, their skin dark with a summer tan. Hale asked whether Nessa would help organize a public meeting after the eclipse. Nessa said liberation was not a single day of celebration; it would require schools, courts, and records that ordinary citizens could inspect. Her partner added that many clinic workers had been driven by overwork and fear, not personal cruelty. That distinction mattered if the city hoped to build a future without repeating its past. Yuna watched two children glide down the grassy slope on homemade boards, laughing as they went. For the first time she felt that their mission might involve more than recovering one brother. It could create space for children whose lives had never been officially recorded to decide what they wished to become.

日落時，他們登上俯瞰東方平原的高聳山脊。工人正從田裡回家，皮膚曬出深色。海爾問妮莎，是否願意在日蝕後協助組織公開集會。妮莎說，解放不是某一天的慶祝，而需要學校、法院，以及普通公民可以查閱的紀錄。伴侶補充，許多診所員工只是被過勞與恐懼推著走，並非天性殘酷。假如城市想要不重蹈覆轍，這項區別便很重要。優娜看著兩個孩子踏著自製滑板，順著草坡滑下，笑聲一路飛揚。她第一次感覺，這趟任務不只是找回弟弟。它也可能為那些從未被正式記錄人生的孩子留出空間，讓他們自己決定想成為誰。

A courier arrived carrying a pending arrest order against Nessa. The charge was possession of documents belonging to the former royal court. Nessa read it and smiled wearily. "They're still afraid of paper," she said. Hale proposed hiding the registers at the handicraft school, where teachers could defend them as teaching materials. Yuna objected that this would put the children at risk. They agreed instead to divide the records among adult volunteers who understood the consequences. Nessa's partner translated the plan into three languages. As the group prepared to depart, Yuna took a slow stroll through the orchard and found Miro feeding the cub beneath a tree. "Do you think we can win?" he asked. Yuna looked toward the distant city, where the bell tower stood against the fading sky. "I think," she said, "we can make it harder for them to decide the answer without us." The fox cub stirred in Miro's coat, and for once the sound of an animal breathing seemed more certain than any promise written by the government.

信使帶來妮莎尚待執行的逮捕令，罪名是持有前王室法庭的文件。妮莎讀完，疲倦地微笑。「他們還是怕紙張。」她說。海爾提議把登記簿藏在手工藝學校，讓教師以教材名義保護。優娜反對，因為這會危及孩子。大家最後同意把文件分給清楚理解風險的成年志工。妮莎的伴侶將計畫翻成三種語言。眾人準備出發時，優娜緩緩走過果園，找到在樹下餵狐狸幼崽的米羅。「妳覺得我們能贏嗎？」他問。優娜望向遠方城市，鐘塔正映在逐漸暗去的天空前。「我想，」她說，「我們至少能讓他們沒辦法再替我們決定答案。」狐狸幼崽在米羅的大衣裡動了一下；優娜忽然覺得，動物呼吸的聲音，比政府寫下的任何承諾都可靠。
