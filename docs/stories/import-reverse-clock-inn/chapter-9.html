半島上的女孩

The Girl on the Peninsula

逆鐘旅店 · 第 9 / 25 章 · 5000–6000 · 英中對照 · 校訂中

用互動版查詞、朗讀與收藏

The clinic on the peninsula looked like a boarding school, with pale walls and a courtyard illuminated by lanterns. A brochure promised the revival of childhood after illness. Yuna read it while a nurse administered medicine to a boy with acne on his forehead. The child complained of discomfort but was too frightened to argue. The nurse explained that the clinic practiced selective restoration: specialists chose memories believed most useful for a patient's recovery. Yuna wondered who decided usefulness. Nearby, children had been asked to cram facts about the city into notebooks, as though literacy could be installed by repetition alone. She asked to speak to Liora. The receptionist hesitated, then said the young woman was a stepchild of the city's ruling family and could not receive visitors without permission. Yuna held up Isa's letter and waited until the receptionist agreed to carry it upstairs.

半島上的診所看起來像寄宿學校，淡色牆面與庭院燈籠相互映照。一份簡介承諾，能讓病後失落的童年復甦。優娜閱讀時，護理師正替一名額頭長著青春痘的男孩給藥。孩子抱怨不舒服，卻嚇得不敢多說。護理師解釋，診所提供選擇性記憶復原：專家挑選被認為最有利康復的回憶。優娜不明白究竟由誰決定何謂有利。附近的孩子被要求把城市常識硬背進筆記，彷彿識字能力能靠反覆背誦直接植入。她要求見莉奧拉。接待員猶豫後表示，年輕女子是統治家族的繼女，未經許可不能見客。優娜舉起伊莎的信，等到接待員終於同意送上樓。

Liora appeared on a balcony, a jade pendant hanging from her neck. Her face was young, but her eyes carried a tired attention that made Yuna stop. "I've heard about you," Liora said. "They say you're a dangerous narrator who invents stories about other people's memories." Yuna answered that she wanted to ask one simple question. Did Liora remember a boy named Ren? The girl's nostrils widened as she struggled to breathe. She described a winter room, a wooden horse, and a brother who promised to fix its broken leg. The scene belonged to Yuna's childhood, not hers. Liora began to cry. "I don't know which part of me is real," she admitted. "When I tell them that, they say I should feel joyous because I survived." Yuna stepped closer but kept her hands at her sides, afraid that even comfort might feel like another demand.

莉奧拉出現在陽臺，脖子上掛著翡翠墜飾。她的臉還很年輕，眼神卻有種疲倦的專注，讓優娜不由停住。「我聽過妳，」莉奧拉說，「他們說妳是個危險的說故事者，編造別人的記憶。」優娜回答，她只想問一個簡單的問題：莉奧拉記不記得名叫任恩的男孩？女孩鼻翼張開，呼吸突然變得艱難。她描述一間冬日房間、一匹木馬，以及答應修好斷腿木馬的哥哥。那是優娜的童年，不是她的。莉奧拉哭了。「我不知道自己哪部分是真的，」她承認，「我一說這種話，他們就說我應該因為活下來而感到快樂。」優娜靠近一步，卻把雙手留在身旁，害怕連安慰都會變成另一種要求。

A doctor entered carrying a small tray of trout soup. He detached the tubes from Liora's wrist and ordered her back to bed. Yuna asked whether the woman had consented to the procedures performed on her. The doctor said her guardian had approved them, and that further questions would have to await a forthcoming official review. Liora glanced at Yuna and whispered that she kept a secret notebook beneath her mattress. The pages recorded every memory she could identify as not her own. Some were potent enough to make her wake in terror; others were ordinary scenes of a family she had never met. "I don't want you to take them away," she said. "They may be all that remains of the people who gave them." Yuna promised not to erase anything. Instead she offered to help distinguish the memories, if Liora wished, while preserving the right of the original owners to decide what happened next.

一名醫師端著鱒魚湯走進來，拆下莉奧拉手腕上的管線，命她回床休息。優娜問年輕女子是否同意過那些療程。醫師說監護人已批准，其他問題得等即將召開的官方審查。莉奧拉看了優娜一眼，悄聲說床墊下藏著一本筆記，記下每段她能辨認出不屬於自己的回憶。有些回憶強烈得讓她驚恐醒來，有些只是陌生家庭的平凡日常。「我不想要妳把它們拿走，」她說，「那可能是捐出它們的人唯一剩下的東西。」優娜答應不抹去任何內容，而是在莉奧拉願意時幫忙辨識回憶，同時保留原主人決定後續如何處理的權利。

At the gate, Miro waited beside a guard who had been ordered to escort them out. The guard's calm face changed when he noticed the date on Isa's letter. It was the day his younger sister had entered the clinic and never returned. He offered them a route through the servants' corridor so they could copy the patient register. Yuna refused to place him in danger without explaining the cost. "I know," he said. "I've been in captivity to my own silence for six years." Together they photographed the records before leaving the peninsula. On the last page was a list of planned memory transfers, including another treatment scheduled for Liora during the eclipse. Yuna now had two people to protect: her missing brother and a young woman whose body had become the setting for other people's political ambitions. Neither could be saved by treating the other as an enemy. As they crossed the bridge, Yuna realized she needed both of them to have a say in any decision about their shared memories.

門口，米羅身旁站著一名奉命送他們離開的守衛。守衛看見伊莎信上的日期，平靜的臉色突然改變。那一天，妹妹走進診所後便再也沒回來。他主動提出走僕役通道，讓他們複製病患登記簿。優娜不願在不說明後果的情況下讓他冒險。「我知道，」他說，「六年來，我一直被自己的沉默囚禁。」眾人一同拍下紀錄，才離開半島。最後一頁列著預定進行的記憶移植，其中一項竟排在日蝕時替莉奧拉再次治療。優娜現在有兩個人要保護：失蹤的弟弟，以及身體被別人拿來實現政治野心的年輕女子。若把其中一人視為敵人，就救不了另一個。走過橋時，優娜明白，有關他們共同記憶的任何決定，都需要兩人有發言權。
