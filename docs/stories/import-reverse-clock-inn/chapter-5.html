酒會上的陌生人

The Guest Without a Face

逆鐘旅店 · 第 5 / 25 章 · 5000–6000 · 英中對照

用互動版查詞、朗讀與收藏

Yuna escaped through an irrigation bypass and emerged at a manor where the trustees were hosting a winter reception. A carriage of chestnut color waited outside, and servants carried trays of champagne between rooms. Ors had given her a silver accessory used by technicians at the memory clinics. It allowed her to pass as an equipment inspector, though her wet shoes ruined the disguise. In the kitchen, a frightened cook was plucking a fowl for the feast. "Everyone here looks confident," he whispered, "but most of them are cowardly when a powerful guest asks a difficult question." He offered her a little lotion to cover the scratches on her hands. His kindness evoked memories of Ren helping strangers with broken toys. Despite his small size, Ren had approached every repair with vigor, as though a cracked wheel or loose screw deserved as much attention as a wounded person. Yuna thanked him and entered the main hall, where officials discussed monetary policy while families waited beyond the gate for food coupons.

優娜從灌溉旁路逃走，在一座莊園附近現身。理事們正在這裡舉辦冬季招待酒會。外頭停著栗色馬車，僕役端著香檳在房間間穿梭。奧斯給過她一枚記憶診所技師使用的銀色配件，足以讓她假裝成設備檢查員，只是濕透的鞋子破壞了偽裝。廚房裡，一名緊張的廚子正替宴席拔禽鳥的毛。「這裡每個人看起來都很有自信，」他低聲說，「但當權貴問出難題，大部分人其實很膽小。」他還給她一點乳液，好遮住手上的刮傷。善意喚起她對任恩的回憶：弟弟過去總幫陌生人修理壞玩具。任恩雖然個子小，修理東西卻總充滿活力，彷彿裂開的車輪、鬆脫的螺絲都和受傷的人同樣值得用心照料。任恩雖然個子小，修理東西卻總充滿活力，彷彿裂開的車輪、鬆脫的螺絲都和受傷的人同樣值得用心照料。優娜向他道謝，走進主廳。官員在那裡討論貨幣政策，門外的家庭卻還在等糧票。

Lady Corven stood beside an enormous telecommunications panel, speaking to engineers about a recent system breakdown. Her voice was hoarse, and her normally calm expression had turned moody with exhaustion. Yuna pretended to inspect a circuit while listening. Corven said the central nucleus of the network must be replaced before the next bell ceremony. A failure during the ritual could expose the identities of every memory recipient in the city. An engineer suggested placing the backup controls behind an independent door; Corven refused. "Our mentality must be one of unified responsibility," she said. Yuna had heard those words before. It was the language powerful people used when they wanted everyone to share blame without sharing control. She waited until Corven left, then photographed the maintenance sheet with a small mirror device concealed under her sleeve.

柯文夫人站在龐大的電信控制板旁，正和工程師討論近期系統故障。她嗓音沙啞，平日鎮定的神情也因疲憊變得喜怒無常。優娜假裝檢查電路，一邊偷聽。柯文說，網路中央核心必須在下次鐘聲儀式前更換。若儀式中斷，整個城市的記憶受贈者身分就可能曝光。一名工程師建議把備用控制器放在獨立門後，柯文拒絕了。「我們必須抱持共同承擔責任的思維。」她說。優娜早聽過這套說法。權貴想讓所有人一起背負責任，卻不肯分享控制權時，總會這樣說。她等柯文離開，便用藏在袖中的小鏡面裝置拍下維修表。

At midnight, a thin rap sounded against the service door. Miro appeared, carrying the fox cub in his coat. They had been separated during the escape, and he looked close to collapse. "I hid beneath a table while a senior judge began to snore," he said. "I heard the conversation afterward." The judges had argued over whether to unveil the city's next ruler before the annual remembrance ceremony. Corven wanted the announcement delayed; another trustee had threatened to slash funding for her clinics unless she obeyed. Yuna wondered why a dispute about succession involved memory patients. Miro answered with a name: Liora, the dictator's granddaughter, who had been in a coma for years. The council claimed she had recovered, but nobody had seen her awake. "They are preparing the public to meet a person who may not know who she is," he said. The significance struck Yuna like a physical blow.

午夜時，服務門傳來幾下輕敲。米羅出現了，大衣裡抱著狐狸幼崽。逃亡時他們失散，如今他看起來快要倒下。「我躲在桌子底下，等一名資深法官開始打鼾，」他說，「後來聽到了談話。」法官們爭論，要不要在年度追憶儀式前揭曉城市的下一任統治者。柯文想延後公布，另一名理事威脅，若她不聽命，就刪減診所經費。優娜不明白，接班問題為何牽扯到記憶病患。米羅說出一個名字：莉奧拉，獨裁者的孫女。她昏迷多年，議會卻聲稱她已甦醒，從沒人真正看見她清醒。「他們正準備讓人民迎接一個可能連自己是誰都不知道的人。」米羅說。優娜瞬間明白這句話的可怕分量。

A guard entered, forcing them behind the banquet curtains. Yuna saw the central network flash red as Corven authorized a test. For one instant, the screen displayed the word REN beneath Liora's medical number. A warning followed: Donor integrity compromised. The room fell silent. Corven ordered the engineers to restore the previous record, and the screen returned to normal. Yuna finally understood the pattern behind the names. Her brother's memories might have been used to make Liora appear alert, educated, and fit to rule. But the transfer was unstable, and the bell ceremony was meant to conceal each breakdown by changing official identities before anyone could ask questions. Yuna pressed the stolen photograph against her wrist. She no longer needed another rumor. She needed the person behind the curtain, and a chance to ask her what she remembered. But she also needed to know whether Liora herself had agreed to the treatment, or whether a frightened child had become another instrument of the council. Neither answer would be easy to bear.

守衛闖進來，迫使兩人躲到宴會廳窗簾後。柯文授權進行測試，中央網路閃起紅光。有那麼一瞬間，螢幕在莉奧拉的醫療編號下顯示「任恩」的名字，隨後跳出警告：「捐贈者完整性受損。」全場安靜了。柯文命令工程師恢復前一筆紀錄，螢幕很快回復正常。優娜終於理解姓名變動背後的規律。弟弟的回憶可能被用來讓莉奧拉顯得清醒、有知識，而且適合統治。然而移植不穩定，鐘聲儀式便一次次在民眾提問前改動官方身分，以掩蓋故障。優娜把偷偷拍下的照片壓在手腕上。她不再需要更多傳聞。她要見到幕後的那個人，親口問她究竟記得什麼。但她也必須知道莉奧拉是否自願接受治療，抑或又是一個受驚的孩子被議會當作工具。無論答案是哪一個，都不容易承受。
