雙重身分

The Double Register

逆鐘旅店 · 第 10 / 25 章 · 5000–6000 · 英中對照 · 校訂完成

A government crackdown began two days before the eclipse. Soldiers blocked the old bridges with cement barriers while rival factions competed to control the clinic records. Yuna entered the capital disguised as a vegetable seller, carrying celery beneath a cloth and a copy of Liora's notebook in her coat. At a crowded square, a pickpocket attempted to steal her purse. She caught his hand and discovered that the boy carried a badge belonging to the court archive. "Why would you want this?" she asked. He told her the judges had ordered children to steal identification papers from strangers. The city claimed it was an attempt to reduce fraud, but the boy knew it was an easy way to identify people who traveled without permission. Yuna gave him food and asked him to stop. He laughed at her optimism, then warned her which street was being watched.

日蝕前兩天，政府開始全面鎮壓。士兵用水泥障礙封鎖舊橋，對立派系爭相控制診所紀錄。優娜扮成蔬菜販進城，布下藏著西芹，大衣裡放著莉奧拉筆記的複本。擁擠廣場上，一名扒手想偷她的錢袋。她抓住男孩的手，發現他身上竟有法庭檔案館的徽章。「你要這個做什麼？」她問。男孩說法官命孩子們從陌生人身上偷走身分證件。城市宣稱是為了減少詐騙，但他知道，這樣能輕易找出未經許可旅行的人。優娜給他食物，勸他別再做了。男孩嘲笑她太天真，卻還是提醒她哪條街有監視。

At a secret meeting of the reform confederation, Hale explained the arithmetic of the transfer program. The council had recorded each patient under two numbers: one for the body receiving treatment and one for the memories being stored. The dual register allowed officials to conceal cases where donors had been declared dead even though they were still alive. "They tried to equalize the numbers by moving people between categories," Hale said. "But a record cannot be repaired by changing the person it describes." Miro found a page marked with Ren's number and another for Liora. Both records used the same sequence of transfers, suggesting that their lives had become dangerously intertwined. Hale asked whether Yuna understood the injustice well enough to testify without exposing private medical details. Yuna admitted she did not. The answer disappointed her, but it kept them from rushing into a public accusation they could not yet defend.

改革者聯盟的秘密會議上，海爾解釋移植計畫的計算方式。議會把每名病患記在兩個編號下，一個代表接受治療的身體，另一個代表保存記憶的來源。雙重登記簿讓官員得以掩蓋某些情況：捐贈者被宣告死亡，實際上仍然活著。「他們藉著把人挪到不同類別來平衡數字，」海爾說，「可是改掉被記錄者，不能算修好紀錄。」米羅找到任恩和莉奧拉各自的頁面，兩人的轉移順序完全相同，顯示他們的人生已危險地糾纏在一起。海爾問優娜是否已充分理解這項不義，能在不暴露私人病歷的前提下作證。優娜承認還沒有。答案讓她失望，卻也阻止了他們倉促提出一項無法辯護的公開指控。

The meeting ended when a loudspeaker announced that the dictator would appear at the eclipse ceremony. The crowd outside responded with an awesome roar, half delight and half fear. A street entertainer tried to animate a crocodile puppet and lure children away from the soldiers. His timing was punctual, almost suspiciously so. Yuna followed him into an alley and discovered an old court messenger who supported the reformers. He had staged the performance to give them a safe path through the square. "Politics is full of intrigue," he said, "but not every trick is meant to hurt someone." Yuna asked whether the city's divided factions would help her. The messenger explained that some wanted genuine reform, while others merely wanted their own names on the doors of power. Neither could be trusted without checking what each had actually done.

會議因擴音器宣布獨裁者將在日蝕儀式露面而結束。外頭人群爆出驚人的吼聲，一半喜悅，一半恐懼。一名街頭藝人操縱鱷魚布偶，試圖把孩子引離士兵。他的演出準時得近乎可疑。優娜跟著走進小巷，發現他其實是支持改革者的前法庭信使。他安排演出，是為了替他們開出穿越廣場的安全路線。「政治充滿權謀，」他說，「但不是每個把戲都想傷害別人。」優娜問城市裡分裂的派系會不會協助她。信使說，有些真想改革，有些只是想把自己的名字換到權力門口。若不檢視實際作為，兩種人都不能輕信。

On their way to the archive, Yuna felt a sudden restraint on her arm. A guard had recognized her from the Hall of Names. He held her long enough for two others to arrive, and Miro staggered when he tried to intervene. The guard called her obstinate for refusing to surrender the copied records. Yuna answered that she would turn herself in if the officers agreed to preserve the originals and allow a lawyer to witness every page. They knew promising it might incur punishment from their superiors, and they had no authority to do so. Then Hale stepped from the crowd and named the precise law under which they could request an independent review. The guards hesitated, and the old messenger led the group away. They had escaped arrest, but the danger had become unmistakable: the council could no longer merely deny the story. It would have to decide whether to destroy the witnesses or face them in public. Yuna could hear the ordinary city beyond the barricades, children calling for supper and shopkeepers closing their doors, people whose lives depended on the result.

前往檔案館途中，優娜手臂突然被人用力拉住。一名守衛認出她是姓名大廳的人，拖住她直到兩名同伴趕到。米羅試圖阻止，反而踉蹌了一下。守衛罵她固執，拒不交出複製的紀錄。優娜回答，只要他們答應保存正本，並讓律師見證每一頁，她就願意配合拘留。他們知道如此承諾可能招來上級懲罰，也沒有權限這麼做。這時海爾從人群中走出，精確指出允許要求獨立審查的法條。守衛遲疑，前法庭信使趁機帶眾人離開。他們逃過逮捕，危險卻越來越明確：議會已不能只是否認整件事，勢必要決定是摧毀證人，還是在公眾面前面對他們。優娜聽見路障外的尋常城市：孩子喊著要吃晚餐，店家關上門扉。這些人的生活都將被結果左右。
