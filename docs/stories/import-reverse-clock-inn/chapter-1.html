鐘聲帶走的人

The Person the Bell Took

逆鐘旅店 · 第 1 / 25 章 · 5000–6000 · 英中對照

用互動版查詞、朗讀與收藏

The bell rang thirteen times, and the man beside Yuna vanished from the city's legal register. He did not disappear from the street; he simply became someone the guards were allowed to arrest without giving a reason. Yuna, a janitor at the Hall of Names, watched the guards clasp his wrists in iron cuffs. The man was a pilgrim who had arrived before dawn. All he carried was a wounded fox cub and a folder of dental records. "There must be a mistake," he said. The tallest guard answered with a government directive: every unregistered resident must be detained before sunset. It had been issued in the name of the dictator who ruled the city from behind a sealed window. Yuna knew the rule. She cleaned the office where names were crossed out. But she had never seen a man lose his rights while she was standing close enough to hear his heart beat.

鐘聲敲了十三下，站在優娜身旁的男人便從城市的法定戶籍上消失了。他並未從街道上憑空蒸發；只是從那一刻起，守衛可以不說理由就把他逮捕。身為「姓名大廳」清潔工的優娜，看著守衛用鐵銬扣住他的雙腕。男人是黎明前才抵達的朝聖旅人，全部行李只有一隻受傷的狐狸幼崽，和一夾牙科紀錄。「一定搞錯了，」他說。最高大的守衛唸出一項政府指令：所有未登記居民必須在日落前拘留。命令以那位隔著密封窗戶統治城市的獨裁者之名頒布。優娜知道規矩，她每天都清掃刪去姓名的辦公室。但過去，她從沒站得這麼近，親眼見到一個還有心跳的人瞬間失去權利。

The pilgrim called himself Miro. Before the guards dragged him away, he pushed the cub into Yuna's arms. "The boy's teeth," he whispered, pointing at the folder. "They are the only proof." A horse began to gallop across the inner square as an official courier arrived. The crowd became stationary, every face turned toward the black carriage. The courier announced that the city's trustees would convene at noon to consider a new law: no dwelling could shelter a person whose name the bell had rejected. The announcement seemed to heighten the silence rather than break it. Above the steps, the city's motto was carved into stone: Order is the synonym of mercy. Yuna had polished those words for seven years. Until that morning she had never asked why mercy needed a synonym, or why it sounded so much like obedience.

旅人自稱米羅。守衛拖走他之前，他把狐狸幼崽塞進優娜懷裡。「那男孩的牙齒，」他指著文件夾低聲說，「那是唯一的證據。」一匹馬在內廣場上疾馳，官府信使隨之抵達。人群像被釘住般停在原地，所有面孔都轉向黑色馬車。信使宣告，城市受託理事將在正午開會，審議一條新法：鐘聲拒絕其姓名之人，不得由任何住家庇護。宣告沒有打破寂靜，反而讓沉默更加沉重。階梯上方刻著城市格言：「秩序即仁慈的同義詞。」優娜七年來每天擦亮那幾個字，直到今天早上，她才第一次懷疑，仁慈為何需要同義詞，而且聽起來竟如此接近服從。

In the empty council chamber, Yuna opened the folder. The dental drawings belonged to a child identified as No. 417, a patient who had undergone a memory transplant after a fever. She had heard of the procedure. A witness donated a carefully chosen recollection, and a surgeon transferred its physical pattern into the patient's damaged memory tissue. The operation could preserve language or a parent's face, but it could not create a memory from nothing. Every successful transplant also cost the donor the original memory. Yuna turned the final page and saw her own signature as the witness. She had never signed such a document. Beside it was a photograph of her younger brother, Ren, who had disappeared three winters earlier. Under his image someone had written: Recipient alive. Donor informed. She held the page so tightly that her nails cut into the paper.

優娜躲進空蕩的議事廳，打開文件夾。牙科圖示屬於編號四一七的孩子；他曾在高燒後接受記憶移植。優娜聽過這項手術：證人捐出一段特別挑選的回憶，外科醫師把它的物理模式轉移到病患受損的記憶組織中。這能保住語言能力，或讓人記得父母的面容，卻無法憑空創造記憶。每次成功移植，也意味著捐贈者永遠失去原來那段回憶。優娜翻到最後一頁，看見自己的簽名被列為證人。她從來不記得簽過這份文件。簽名旁是一張弟弟任恩的照片。他在三個冬天前失蹤。照片下有人寫著：「受贈者存活。捐贈者已獲通知。」她把紙抓得太緊，指甲割進了紙面。

A trustee entered without knocking. Lady Corven, whose family controlled the memory clinics, had an expression of utter weariness. "The law has precedent," she said, noticing the open folder. "In an emergency, one person's recollection may be used to protect another." Yuna asked whether consent was required. "Unconditional consent is preferred," Corven replied, "but a guardian may authorize treatment for a child." Yuna felt the cub struggle against her coat. "My brother wasn't a child when he disappeared." For the first time, Corven hesitated. She admitted that a patient sometimes received a name from another person's file if both records had been damaged. Such errors were officially rare. Yuna thought about the bell, the hollow iron tower, and all the people she had watched disappear from the register. Perhaps the bell was not identifying strangers. Perhaps it was protecting mistakes that powerful families had made.

一名理事沒敲門就走了進來。掌控記憶診所的柯文夫人神情極度疲倦。「法律有先例可循，」她看見攤開的文件夾後說，「緊急情況下，可以用一個人的回憶保護另一個人。」優娜問是否需要當事人同意。「最好是無條件同意，」柯文回答，「但監護人也可以替孩子授權治療。」狐狸幼崽在優娜大衣裡掙動。「我弟弟失蹤時已經不是小孩子了。」柯文第一次遲疑。她承認，若兩份紀錄同時受損，病患偶爾會被賦予另一人的姓名。官方說法是這類錯誤極罕見。優娜想起鐘聲、空心鐵塔，以及她看過那麼多從戶籍上消失的人。或許鐘聲根本不是在辨認陌生人，而是在保護有權勢家族犯下的錯誤。

Corven reached for the folder, but Yuna stepped aside. The trustee could reap the obedience of hundreds of clerks, yet she did not know the building as a janitor did. Yuna had discovered a narrow passage behind the chimney where old pipes met the wall. She slipped through with the cub and waited until Corven's footsteps faded. In the passage she found a brass panel bearing twenty tiny switches. One had been marked with her brother's name. Another bore hers. A third carried a word she had never seen: REHEARSAL. Yuna wanted to pull the switch, but the cub cried out, and she realized that the panel was warm. Something on the other side was alive and working. She withdrew her hand. Instead of taking the easiest action, she copied each symbol onto her sleeve with cleaning chalk. Then she climbed toward the bell tower, where someone was already striking the metal for a fourteenth time.

柯文伸手要拿文件夾，優娜卻側身避開。理事能收割上百名職員的服從，卻沒有清潔工那樣熟悉建築內部。優娜曾發現煙囪後有條狹窄通路，老舊管線在那裡與牆面交會。她抱著狐狸幼崽鑽了進去，等柯文的腳步聲遠去。暗道裡有塊黃銅面板，裝著二十個細小開關。其中一個刻著弟弟的名字，另一個刻著她自己的。第三個標著從未見過的字：「預演」。優娜很想扳下開關，但狐狸幼崽突然哀叫，她才發現面板微微發熱。牆的另一邊，某種東西正活著、運作著。她收回手，沒有選擇最容易的動作，而是用清潔粉筆把每個符號抄在袖子上。接著她爬向鐘樓；樓上有人正準備敲響第十四聲。
