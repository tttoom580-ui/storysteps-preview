天文台裡的病歷

The Observatory's Patients

逆鐘旅店 · 第 8 / 25 章 · 5000–6000 · 英中對照 · 校訂完成

用互動版查詞、朗讀與收藏

The road toward the capital passed an observatory that had been converted into a private hospital. An astronomer still worked in its upper dome, tracking the eclipse with a battered telescope. Below, nurses treated patients with pneumonia and other illnesses that the council's specialized clinics refused to admit. Yuna and Miro arrived carrying the old recording drum. A robust nurse named Isa examined it and recognized the prototype used before memory transplants became common. "The council tried to computerize every operation," she said. "The new systems can disable a record with one command, but they cannot replace human judgment." Her hospital was short of antibiotics and food. Government expenditure on the bell ceremonies had risen while its medical budget declined. Yuna wondered how many lives could have been saved with even a small part of what the government spent making its authority seem eternal.

前往首都的道路經過一座改成私人醫院的天文台。天文學家仍在上層圓頂工作，用破舊望遠鏡追蹤日蝕。樓下護理師則照顧肺炎及其他病患；議會的專門診所拒絕收治他們。優娜和米羅帶著舊錄音滾筒抵達。一名體格健壯的護理師伊莎看過後，認出那是記憶移植普及之前使用的原型機。「議會試圖讓每項手術都電腦化，」她說，「新系統能一鍵停用紀錄，卻無法取代人的判斷。」醫院缺抗生素，也缺食物。政府在鐘聲儀式上的支出節節上升，醫療預算反而下降。優娜想知道，若把製造永恆權威幻象的經費挪一小部分，又能救回多少人。

Isa led them to the psychiatry department, a quiet ward where survivors received counseling after memory procedures. One woman had spent months in meditation, attempting to orient herself within a childhood partly borrowed from someone else. Another patient, an irritable carpenter, remembered making a nickel coin into a toy but could not identify the child who received it. The clinic had assured both patients that their confusion was temporary. Isa disagreed. "People need contact with their kin and an honest account of what happened," she said. "Without that, treatment becomes another method of control." Yuna asked whether the hospital had examined Liora. Isa showed her a record with the sovereign family's seal, then covered the name. The patient had arrived unconscious, been treated for a fever, and awakened with fluent speech but no coherent account of her own past. "She is a patient," Isa said. "Don't forget that because the council calls her an heir."

伊莎領他們走進精神醫學科，那裡有間安靜病房，術後倖存者在此接受諮商。一名女子花了好幾個月冥想，試著在部分借自他人的童年裡重新定位自己。另一名脾氣暴躁的木匠記得曾把五分鎳幣做成玩具，卻想不起是送給哪個孩子。診所保證兩人的混亂只是暫時的，伊莎不同意。「人們需要與親人接觸，也需要知道自己真正經歷了什麼，」她說，「否則治療只是另一種控制。」優娜問醫院是否檢查過莉奧拉。伊莎拿出一份蓋著主權家族印章的紀錄，遮住姓名。病患入院時昏迷，接受高燒治療後醒來，說話流利，卻無法說清自己過去的人生。「她是病患，」伊莎說，「不要因為議會稱她為繼承人，就忘了這點。」

At noon the government spokesperson arrived to announce an evacuation drill. Officially, an approaching storm threatened the observatory. In reality, guards were searching for patients whose files belonged to the original memory experiments. The guards ordered staff to evacuate the wing, but Isa asked where the patients would go. A junior doctor applauded when Isa refused to hand over the files without individual consent. The spokesperson did not appreciate the gesture. "The wards are not a theater," he said. Isa answered that neither were they a prison. Yuna helped move patients into a sheltered corridor while the astronomer followed the changing sky above. A nurse brought an oriental medicine manual used by the hospital's first director, not as proof of every treatment but as a reminder that useful knowledge came from many traditions. The old ward's wardrobe had been converted into a cabinet for blankets. Yuna found spare coats there and distributed them to patients waiting in the cold.

中午，政府發言人抵達，宣布進行疏散演練。官方理由是有暴風雨逼近天文台，實際上守衛正在搜索原始記憶實驗相關病患。守衛命令醫護人員撤離整個側翼，伊莎卻追問病患要安置在哪裡。一名年輕醫師見伊莎拒絕在未取得個別同意下交出檔案，不禁鼓掌。發言人對此很不高興。「病房不是劇院。」他說。伊莎回答，病房也不是監獄。優娜協助病患轉往遮蔽走廊，天文學家則在樓上觀測變化中的天空。一名護理師帶來醫院創辦主任留下的東方醫學手冊，不是說每種療法都必然正確，而是提醒人們實用知識來自許多傳統。舊病房的大衣櫃已改成毛毯儲藏室。優娜找到備用外套，分給在冷風中等待的病患。

Before leaving, Isa gave Yuna a sealed case containing Liora's early examinations. "I cannot tell you what to think," she said. "But the records show she was afraid, and fear should matter even when the patient is powerful." Yuna promised to keep the file private until Liora could decide how it should be used. The astronomer called from the roof that the eclipse would begin sooner than predicted. The observatory's clock had been altered by several minutes; its face was accurate, but the mechanism had been deliberately shifted. Miro examined the gears and found a new connector that led toward the capital's communication network. Someone had been manipulating not just memories but the timing of public events. Yuna realized that the next bell ceremony was designed to begin while the city believed the sky itself was giving a signal. The performance of power depended on one carefully chosen moment.

離開前，伊莎交給優娜一只封好的盒子，裡面是莉奧拉早期檢查資料。「我不能替妳決定想法，」她說，「但紀錄顯示她很害怕；即使病患擁有權勢，恐懼也應該受到重視。」優娜答應在莉奧拉自行決定之前，不公開病歷。天文學家從屋頂喊道，日蝕將比預測更早開始。天文台時鐘的指針看似正確，機械卻被故意調動了幾分鐘。米羅檢查齒輪，發現一個新接頭通往首都通訊網路。有人不只操縱記憶，還操縱公眾活動的時間。優娜明白，下次鐘聲儀式是刻意要在全城以為天空也發出徵兆的時刻開始。權力的表演，仰賴一個精心選定的瞬間。
