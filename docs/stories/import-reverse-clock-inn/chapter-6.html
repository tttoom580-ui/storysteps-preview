日蝕的備用鐘

The Bell Beneath the Eclipse

逆鐘旅店 · 第 6 / 25 章 · 5000–6000 · 英中對照 · 校訂中

用互動版查詞、朗讀與收藏

At the manor's rear gate, Yuna encountered a congresswoman named Hale who was carrying a crate of edible supplies to a nearby forest settlement. Hale had once introduced legislation to protect memory donors. The trustees had praised her speech while quietly blocking the law. "I used to believe esteem from powerful people meant I'd attained something," she told Yuna. "Now I measure my work by whether it survives their displeasure." She drove them through an evergreen grove where mist rose like vapor from the wet earth. The cub sniffed a stream, startling a fish whose silver fin broke the surface. Hale stopped beside a cabin filled with spare clocks. "During the eclipse next week, the city's main bell will be hidden from view," she explained. "That's when the trustees test their backup." Yuna wondered why a supposedly sacred ceremony required so much machinery.

莊園後門，一名叫海爾的女議員正在把一箱可食用物資送往附近林區聚落。海爾曾提出保護記憶捐贈者的法案。理事們表面讚美她的演說，私底下卻封殺提案。「我以前以為受到權貴敬重，就代表有所成就，」她告訴優娜，「現在我用另一種標準衡量工作：就算惹他們不高興，成果能不能留下來。」她開車載兩人穿過常綠樹林，濕地冒出蒸氣般的霧。狐狸幼崽嗅著溪流，驚動水中一條魚，銀亮的魚鰭劃破水面。海爾在堆滿備用時鐘的小屋前停下。「下週日蝕時，城市的主鐘會被遮住，」她解釋，「理事們就會測試備用鐘。」優娜不明白，據說神聖的儀式，為何需要那麼多機械。

Inside, an elderly mechanic served curry with rice and apologized for the smell of solder. He had worked on the bells for forty years, a pastime that became his profession and finally his prison. "Each bell contains an airtight compartment for recording dust," he explained. "The dust isn't magical. It carries a chemical marker that makes the clinics' implanted memories unstable." Yuna stared at him. "So the bell doesn't choose people?" "It announces the names chosen by the network," the mechanic replied. "The sound helps officials make the changes feel inevitable." On the workbench lay a handful of metal flakes and an ugly pile of rubbish from broken gears. The mechanic showed her a slim wheel whose teeth had been filed away. Without it, the bell would ring but could not transmit a signal. He had once made such a wheel and been ordered never to mention it.

屋內，年老技師端出咖哩飯，並為焊錫的氣味道歉。他修了四十年鐘，這項消遣成為職業，最終也成了牢籠。「每座鐘都有保存紀錄粉塵的密封艙，」他解釋，「粉塵沒有魔法，只是帶著一種化學標記，會讓診所植入的記憶變得不穩定。」優娜盯著他。「所以鐘聲沒有在選人？」「它只是宣告網路已挑選的人，」技師回答，「聲音能讓官員更容易把變更說成無可避免。」工作桌上散著金屬薄片和壞齒輪堆成的垃圾。技師展示一枚細齒輪，齒牙已被磨平。有了它，鐘仍能響，卻無法傳送訊號。他曾做過這樣的零件，之後卻被命令永遠不得提起。

The mechanic described the monotony of his former work: replace a fuse, inspect a gear, erase a fault report, repeat. Every year the same errors returned because the trustees refused to repair the network's central flaw. A backup system could create redundancy, but it could not make false records true. Hale asked him why he had stayed. He admitted he had been afraid the clinics would deny treatment to his granddaughter. "I despise the people who use children as bargaining tools," he said, "and I despise that I helped them." Yuna had begun to recognize such confessions. The system was held together not only by sly rulers but by frightened people who believed disobedience would hurt someone they loved. Hale offered him a chance to testify. He refused at first, then asked whether they could guarantee his family's safety. Neither woman promised what she could not deliver.

技師描述過往工作的單調：換保險絲、檢查齒輪、刪除故障報告，然後重來。每年同樣的錯誤都會發生，因為理事們拒絕修復網路最核心的缺陷。備援系統可以提供冗餘保護，卻無法把假的紀錄變成真的。海爾問他為何一直留下。技師承認，他害怕診所拒絕替孫女治療。「我鄙視拿孩子當籌碼的人，」他說，「也鄙視自己曾幫過他們。」優娜開始熟悉這種自白。體系不只靠狡猾的統治者維持，也靠那些害怕不服從會傷害所愛之人的普通人。海爾邀請他作證。技師起初拒絕，接著詢問能否保證家人安全。兩名女子都不願許下無法兌現的承諾。

Before dawn, a message arrived from the city: the government had approved a market in recorded memories. Their contents would be sold as merchandise to wealthy families seeking skills, languages, or comforting childhood scenes. The announcement made Yuna sick. People would be allowed to roam through other lives while their original owners struggled to remember their own. Hale read the document and laughed without humor. "They call it freedom of choice," she said, "though the poor are being asked to sell what they cannot replace." The mechanic showed Yuna a narrow service path that would bring them to the main bell controls during the eclipse. Yuna thanked him and packed the filed wheel. She did not intend to slay a villain in a tower, as the old children's stories promised. She intended to make the bell useless, then let the people whose names it had stolen speak for themselves. If she succeeded, the work of rebuilding justice would belong to those people, not to any new ruler claiming a better kind of absolute power.

天亮前，城裡傳來消息：政府批准了記錄記憶的交易市場。富裕家庭可以把其中的技能、語言，甚至溫暖童年片段當成商品購買。優娜感到噁心。人們將能在別人的人生裡漫遊，原本的主人卻可能想不起自己是誰。海爾讀完公文，毫無笑意地笑了。「他們稱這是選擇自由，」她說，「可是窮人賣掉的是永遠補不回來的東西。」技師指給優娜一條維修窄道，日蝕時能通往主鐘控制器。優娜向他道謝，收好磨平齒牙的輪片。她不打算像舊童話那樣，跑去塔裡斬殺某個惡人。她想讓鐘再也無法奪名，接著讓被奪走姓名的人親自發言。如果成功，重建正義的工作應該屬於那些人，而不是屬於聲稱自己擁有更好絕對權力的新統治者。
