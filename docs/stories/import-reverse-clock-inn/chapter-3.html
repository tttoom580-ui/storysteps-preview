The Stamp Beneath the Stamp

逆鐘旅店 · 第 3 / 25 章 · 5000–6000 · 英中對照

用互動版查詞、朗讀與收藏

The marsh passage ended beneath a border market where a cashier checked permits beside a row of medicine stalls. Yuna and Miro could not enter the next district without a medical checkup, a rule introduced after a fever years earlier. The queue was lengthy. An old woman addressed the inspector as "madam" and asked whether a birth certificate would be permissible in place of a travel card. The inspector pointed toward a disposal bin filled with rejected papers. Yuna recognized the printing on one card: it bore the trademark of the clinic that had treated Ren. She rescued it before the inspector noticed, but a guard told her to curb her curiosity. "This is an ordinary health station," he said. "We have no interest in private family matters." His tone sounded rehearsed. Yuna had cleaned too many government offices not to recognize a lie that had been repeated until everyone believed it.

沼澤通道終於通往邊境市場。收銀員在藥攤旁檢查通行許可。優娜和米羅若想進入下一區，就得先做健康檢查；這是多年前一場熱病後訂下的規矩。隊伍排得很長。一名老婦客氣地稱檢查員為「夫人」，詢問能否用出生證明代替通行卡。檢查員指向一個塞滿遭拒文件的廢紙桶。優娜認出其中一張卡片的印刷圖樣：那是替任恩治療的診所商標。她趁檢查員沒注意把卡片撿起，一名守衛卻命令她收斂好奇心。「這只是普通衛生站，」他說，「我們對私人家庭的事沒興趣。」他的語氣像背好的台詞。優娜清掃過太多政府辦公室，很清楚那種重複到眾人都相信的謊言。

At the edge of the market stood a small hall where refugees applied to emigrate to the northern coast. A woman named Elian ran its information desk. She had a lifelong interest in preserving family histories, and her work had enriched the settlement's records. Her brother had vanished after expressing dissent at a public hearing. "The official report called his questions a disgrace," she told Yuna. "I called them questions." Elian opened a drawer full of complaints and revealed a pattern: people transferred into memory clinics were later assigned new addresses, while their original families received notices of death. The envelopes were stamped twice, one seal laid directly over another. Under close scrutiny, the first seal could be read: Administrative Reassignment. The second said Medical Disposal. Miro went pale. "Disposal isn't a treatment," he said. "It's what you call something when you decide no one has to account for it."

市場邊有一座小廳，難民可在此申請移居北方海岸。資訊櫃檯由名叫艾蓮的女子管理。她畢生喜愛保存家族歷史，也因此充實了聚落紀錄。她的弟弟曾在公開聽證會表達異議，之後便消失了。「官方報告說他的提問使國家蒙羞，」她告訴優娜，「我卻覺得那只是問題。」艾蓮拉開抽屜，裡頭全是投訴信。她發現一個規律：被送進記憶診所的人，之後會被分配新住址，原本的家人卻收到死亡通知。信封上蓋了兩層章，一枚印章正好壓在另一枚上面。仔細檢視，可讀出底層寫著「行政重新分配」，上層則是「醫療除籍」。米羅臉色發白。「除籍不是治療，」他說，「而是你不想再向任何人交代時，替某人貼上的標籤。」

A bell from the market tower began to ring. Its sound was loud enough to deafen anyone standing directly underneath. The guards shouted that curfew was approaching. Elian offered shelter, but Miro insisted they had to leave before the bells were synchronized. He explained that the city's administrators treated doubt as an illness; if people repeated the approved account often enough, private memory would begin to feel shameful. He did not preach a heroic doctrine. His voice trembled when he admitted that he had once worked for the clinics himself, a novice technician who had accepted orders without understanding their purpose. Yuna's sympathy struggled with anger. Her brother might have been among those patients. "Why help me now?" she asked. "Because I remember the exact day I stopped asking questions," Miro answered, "and I don't want that to be the last true thing about me."

市場鐘樓開始敲鐘，聲音大到足以震聾正下方的人。守衛高喊宵禁將近。艾蓮提出留宿，但米羅堅持必須在各處鐘聲同步前離開。他解釋，城市管理者把懷疑視為疾病；只要人們反覆說出官方版本，私人記憶終究會令人覺得羞恥。他並非在宣講英雄信條；坦承自己曾替診所工作時，聲音還在顫抖。那時他只是個不懂命令用途、照單全收的新進技師。優娜的同情與憤怒彼此拉扯。弟弟也許曾是他的病患。「那你現在為什麼幫我？」她問。「因為我記得自己從哪天起不再提問，」米羅說，「而我不希望那成為我人生最後一件真實的事。」

They left by an old carriage road, following Elian's directions toward the northern hills. Rain fell with a soft melancholy that reminded Yuna of the winters she and Ren had spent indoors repairing broken toys. Miro's description of the clinics was reminiscent of those evenings: too many pieces, too little light, and nobody certain how the device had worked before it broke. Behind them, the rival guards of two districts argued over who should claim the travelers. Their rivalry gave Yuna and Miro time to escape. Yet suspense remained, because the stamped card showed a date two weeks in the future. If the clinic could prepare a disposal order before its patient arrived, then either the process was a fraud or the administrators were deciding in advance who would have a future. Yuna folded the card into her sleeve. At the next hearing, she intended to ask the question that had cost Elian's brother his freedom.

兩人循艾蓮指的方向，沿舊馬車道前往北方丘陵。細雨帶著淡淡的憂鬱，讓優娜想起過去與任恩待在室內修理壞玩具的冬天。米羅所描述的診所，竟有點像那些夜晚：零件太多、燈光太少，而且沒人確定裝置壞掉前究竟如何運作。身後，兩個行政區的守衛爭著要把旅人算作自己的逮捕對象。守衛間的競爭反而讓優娜和米羅得以脫身。然而懸念仍在：被蓋章的卡片日期竟是兩週之後。假如診所能在病患抵達前就開好除籍命令，那麼不是整個程序造假，就是管理者預先決定了誰可以擁有未來。優娜把卡片摺進袖子。下一次聽證會上，她打算問出那個害艾蓮弟弟失去自由的問題。
