A club member posted a group picture that showed Mina and Leo standing close together. A text message could be read again and again, yet it did not show the whole face of the person who wrote it. Mina was learning that words needed context, and feelings needed time. Leo did not answer every silence with another message. Sometimes he waited until they could speak at the library table, with books between them and no audience to please. The quiet gave Mina space to ask what she really felt. She was not ready to name it all at once, and for once that was perfectly all right. The paper was checked by Leo. Somebody had packed chocolate for a short break later. Someone had asked Leo a question about class. They needed to collect all the forms before the end of the day. The open book contained a short story about course.

社團成員貼出一張合照，照片裡米娜與里歐站得很近。 一則訊息能反覆閱讀，卻不能呈現寫信人的完整模樣。米娜學到言語需要脈絡，感情也需要時間。里歐沒有用更多訊息填滿每次沉默，有時等到兩人能坐在圖書館桌旁、書本隔在中間，也不用取悅觀眾時才談。這份安靜讓米娜有空間問自己真正的心意。她還不急著一次說清，而這樣也完全沒關係。 這張紙由里歐核對。有人為稍後的休息準備了巧克力。有人曾問里歐關於課堂的問題。他們必須在今天結束前收齊所有表格。翻開的書有一則談到課程的小故事。

Some friends tease them, and Mina felt uncomfortable with the attention. For several seconds, nobody spoke. Mina was thinking about the new fact and the way it touched earlier choices. Leo drew a short line under the important part of the notes. They agreed that a record was useful only if it was clear enough to correct. If a number, name, or time was uncertain, they marked it as uncertain. That extra care took a little longer, but it protected people who were not in the room to explain themselves. At last, Mina could see a practical question worth asking next. The class reading card used flower in an example sentence. A notice near the entrance mentioned hour. Someone had asked Leo a question about housewife. "What does large mean in this note?" Mina asked. "Let me check the last line," Leo said.

有朋友起鬨，米娜對這種關注感到不舒服。 好幾秒沒有人開口。米娜思考新事實與先前選擇的關係。里歐在筆記重要處畫了短線。他們同意紀錄要清楚，才方便日後更正。數字、姓名或時間若不確定，就標明不確定。這樣雖然多花時間，卻能保護不在場、無法替自己說話的人。最後米娜終於想到下一個值得問的實際問題。 課堂閱讀卡用花造了一個例句。入口附近的告示提到小時。有人曾問里歐關於家庭主婦的問題。「這張筆記裡的「大的」是什麼意思？」米娜問。「讓我檢查最後一行。」里歐說。

They politely ask that captions not invent a relationship and keep the photo as a club memory. Making a choice was harder than making a wish. Mina could imagine a quick solution, but a real plan needed to fit the time, the place, and the people taking part. Leo listened while Mina explained the steps aloud. They stopped at one unclear part, changed it, and started again. 'If somebody says no, we need another way,' Mina said. Leo agreed that helping was not the same as deciding for others. Together they wrote a short plan anyone involved could question, improve, or leave if it was not right for them. Someone had asked Leo a question about list. Mina remembered a drawing of main seen earlier that week. The open book contained a short story about note. The popular activity drew more visitors than expected. A drawing of a rabbit hung on the classroom notice board.

兩人禮貌請求不要捏造關係，讓照片單純當社團紀念。 做選擇比許願難。米娜可以想像快速解法，但真正的計畫必須配合時間、地點與參與者。里歐聽米娜逐步說明，在不清楚的地方停下修改，再重新開始。「如果有人說不，我們要有其他辦法。」米娜說。里歐同意，幫忙不等於替別人做決定。他們一起寫下簡明計畫，讓相關的人都能提問、改善或選擇退出。 有人曾問里歐關於清單的問題。米娜想起本週稍早看到的主要的圖畫。翻開的書有一則談到筆記的小故事。熱門活動吸引了超出預期的訪客。教室公告欄上掛著一張兔子的圖畫。

The member updates the caption, and the teasing slowly fades. A small mistake had brought them to the same table. What happened there was no longer an accident. Mina had chosen to ask questions, and Leo had chosen to answer without taking away her freedom. They did not agree about everything. Sometimes their tastes in books or plans for a weekend were completely different. That difference made their conversations more interesting, not less. When they said goodbye, Mina did not need to invent a perfect line. The simple words they had chosen felt true enough. "We need a real answer, not only a nice story," Mina said. The class reading card used start in an example sentence. A notice near the entrance mentioned there. They waited until the responsible person arrived. A notice near the entrance mentioned west.

成員修改文字，玩笑也漸漸停了。 一個小錯誤把兩人帶到同一張桌旁，但之後發生的事已不再是意外。米娜選擇提問，里歐選擇回答，也沒有奪走她的自由。他們並非每件事都同意，有時喜歡的書或週末計畫完全不同。差異讓對話更有趣，而不是更無趣。道別時，米娜不必想出完美台詞。自己選擇的簡單話語，就已足夠真實。 「需要真實答案，不只是好聽故事。」米娜說。課堂閱讀卡用開始造了一個例句。入口附近的告示提到那裡。他們等到負責的人來了才行動。入口附近的告示提到西方。

In the afternoon light, the campus looked ordinary again. That surprised Mina a little. A room, a street, or a simple object could stay the same while the people who used it learned something new. Leo checked that nothing needed to be carried away without permission. Mina checked the time and remembered an ordinary promise waiting outside the day's work. They had made progress, but other parts of life still deserved care. Neither of them wanted to turn an important project into a reason to forget everyone else.

午後光線下，校園又顯得很平凡，這讓米娜有些驚訝。房間、街道或物品可能沒有改變，使用它們的人卻已學到新事。里歐確認沒有物品被擅自帶走。米娜看了時間，想起工作以外還有平常的約定。事情有了進展，生活其他部分仍值得照顧。他們都不想因為一項重要計畫，就忘記身邊其他人。

On the way home, Mina kept thinking about one small moment from the day. It was not necessarily the loudest moment. Sometimes the most important change happened when somebody listened, stopped, or asked one more question. Leo waved goodbye at the corner. Mina waved back and felt ready to begin again when the next problem came. The story had moved forward, not because every difficulty was gone, but because they now knew something they had not known in the morning.

回家路上，米娜一直想著今天的一個小片刻。那不一定是最熱鬧的一刻。有時真正重要的改變，就發生在有人願意聽、停下來，或多問一句時。里歐在轉角揮手再見，米娜也揮手回應，覺得下次遇到問題時還能重新開始。故事之所以前進，不是因為困難全消失了，而是因為他們比早上多懂了一件事。
