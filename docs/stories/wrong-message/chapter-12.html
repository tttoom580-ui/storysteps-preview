Ethan invited Mina to watch his new play from a front-row seat. Campus life rarely gave people a perfect moment to say an important thing. There were classes to reach, books to return, and friends talking nearby. Mina had imagined that love would begin with a grand confession, not with a mistaken tap on a phone. Leo made room for her to be uncertain without turning that uncertainty into a joke. She noticed his kindness, but she also noticed the work he took seriously. A person could be more interesting when seen in an ordinary afternoon than when imagined from a distance. Someone had asked Leo a question about actor. The class reading card used afternoon in an example sentence. Mina remembered a drawing of band seen earlier that week. "We can celebrate when the others are ready," Mina suggested. A notice near the entrance mentioned corner.

伊森邀請米娜坐前排看他的新戲。 校園生活很少提供完美時刻讓人說重要的話。大家要趕課、還書，身邊還有朋友聊天。米娜以前想像愛情從盛大告白開始，而不是誤按手機。里歐讓她能安心不確定，沒有把那份不確定拿來開玩笑。她看見他的善意，也看見他認真對待的工作。從平常下午近距離認識一個人，有時比遠遠想像更有趣。 有人曾問里歐關於演員的問題。課堂閱讀卡用下午造了一個例句。米娜想起本週稍早看到的樂團圖畫。「等其他人準備好，我們就能慶祝。」米娜提議。入口附近的告示提到轉角。

Mina felt the old excitement but also noticed she knew him mainly as a performer. For several seconds, nobody spoke. Mina was thinking about the new fact and the way it touched earlier choices. Leo drew a short line under the important part of the notes. They agreed that a record was useful only if it was clear enough to correct. If a number, name, or time was uncertain, they marked it as uncertain. That extra care took a little longer, but it protected people who were not in the room to explain themselves. At last, Mina could see a practical question worth asking next. "We can die when the others are ready," Mina suggested. The class reading card used glasses in an example sentence. Mina said the word I in a careful answer. A small poster on the wall included the word key. A notice near the entrance mentioned last.

米娜再度有些心動，也發現自己主要認識的是舞台上的伊森。 好幾秒沒有人開口。米娜思考新事實與先前選擇的關係。里歐在筆記重要處畫了短線。他們同意紀錄要清楚，才方便日後更正。數字、姓名或時間若不確定，就標明不確定。這樣雖然多花時間，卻能保護不在場、無法替自己說話的人。最後米娜終於想到下一個值得問的實際問題。 「等其他人準備好，我們就能枯死。」米娜提議。課堂閱讀卡用眼鏡造了一個例句。米娜仔細回答時說出了「我」。牆上的小海報寫著鑰匙這個字。入口附近的告示提到最後的。

She accepted the invitation as a friend and did not promise anything more. Making a choice was harder than making a wish. Mina could imagine a quick solution, but a real plan needed to fit the time, the place, and the people taking part. Leo listened while Mina explained the steps aloud. They stopped at one unclear part, changed it, and started again. 'If somebody says no, we need another way,' Mina said. Leo agreed that helping was not the same as deciding for others. Together they wrote a short plan anyone involved could question, improve, or leave if it was not right for them. A small poster on the wall included the word media. They needed more time to check the last note. At three o’clock, the hall grew quiet. They could stop or try again after a short rest. Someone had asked Leo a question about package.

她以朋友身分接受邀請，沒有做更多承諾。 做選擇比許願難。米娜可以想像快速解法，但真正的計畫必須配合時間、地點與參與者。里歐聽米娜逐步說明，在不清楚的地方停下修改，再重新開始。「如果有人說不，我們要有其他辦法。」米娜說。里歐同意，幫忙不等於替別人做決定。他們一起寫下簡明計畫，讓相關的人都能提問、改善或選擇退出。 牆上的小海報寫著媒體這個字。他們需要更多時間核對最後一張紙。三點整時，大廳安靜下來。他們可以停止，或休息一下再試。有人曾問里歐關於包裹的問題。

After the play she enjoyed talking about the show without forcing a romance. Some stories end at the first confession. Mina was beginning to think the interesting part came afterward: whether people would listen on difficult days, keep their separate dreams, and say what they really meant. Leo had plans of his own, and she did too. Their growing friendship did not make those plans smaller. She put her phone away and met his eyes when he spoke. If another important message came, she wanted to understand the person behind it before deciding how to answer. Someone had asked Leo a question about play. The day felt poor to Mina, so Leo asked what was wrong. A notice near the entrance mentioned supermarket. The open book contained a short story about ticket. "What does tired mean in this note?" Mina asked.

看完戲，她開心討論演出，沒有勉強發展戀情。 有些故事在第一次告白就結束。米娜卻開始覺得有趣的部分在後面：難過的日子是否還願意聽、能否保有各自的夢想、會不會誠實說出心意。里歐有自己的計畫，她也一樣。友情成長沒有縮小那些計畫。她收起手機，對方說話時看著他的眼睛。若再收到重要訊息，她想先理解訊息背後的人，再決定如何回答。 有人曾問里歐關於演奏的問題。米娜覺得今天有些不足的，里歐便問怎麼了。入口附近的告示提到超市。翻開的書有一則談到門票的小故事。「這張筆記裡的「疲倦的」是什麼意思？」米娜問。

In the afternoon light, the campus looked ordinary again. That surprised Mina a little. A room, a street, or a simple object could stay the same while the people who used it learned something new. Leo checked that nothing needed to be carried away without permission. Mina checked the time and remembered an ordinary promise waiting outside the day's work. They had made progress, but other parts of life still deserved care. Neither of them wanted to turn an important project into a reason to forget everyone else.

午後光線下，校園又顯得很平凡，這讓米娜有些驚訝。房間、街道或物品可能沒有改變，使用它們的人卻已學到新事。里歐確認沒有物品被擅自帶走。米娜看了時間，想起工作以外還有平常的約定。事情有了進展，生活其他部分仍值得照顧。他們都不想因為一項重要計畫，就忘記身邊其他人。

On the way home, Mina kept thinking about one small moment from the day. It was not necessarily the loudest moment. Sometimes the most important change happened when somebody listened, stopped, or asked one more question. Leo waved goodbye at the corner. Mina waved back and felt ready to begin again when the next problem came. The story had moved forward, not because every difficulty was gone, but because they now knew something they had not known in the morning.

回家路上，米娜一直想著今天的一個小片刻。那不一定是最熱鬧的一刻。有時真正重要的改變，就發生在有人願意聽、停下來，或多問一句時。里歐在轉角揮手再見，米娜也揮手回應，覺得下次遇到問題時還能重新開始。故事之所以前進，不是因為困難全消失了，而是因為他們比早上多懂了一件事。
