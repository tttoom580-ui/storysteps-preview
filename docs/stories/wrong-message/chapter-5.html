She gave herself time to learn what she actually wanted. The new feeling did not arrive like a flash of light. It grew while the two of them carried books, corrected mistakes, and learned when to give each other room. Mina had feared that the wrong message would always stand between them. Instead it was becoming one ordinary part of a much longer conversation. Leo looked at her with the same calm respect he had shown on the first day. She understood that an answer given freely was worth more than one demanded for the sake of a romantic ending. Light came through the open window. 她留一些時間，弄清自己真正想要什麼。 新感情沒有像閃電一樣忽然落下，而是在兩人搬書、修正錯誤、學會彼此留空間時慢慢長出來。米娜曾怕錯傳訊息會永遠隔在中間，如今它逐漸變成一場長談裡平凡的一部分。里歐仍用第一天那種平靜尊重的眼神看她。她明白自願說出的回答，比為了浪漫結局而要求的答案更珍貴。 光線穿過敞開的窗戶。米娜想起本週稍早看到的腳趾圖畫。課堂閱讀卡用主題造了一個例句。「這張紙應該放在哪裡？」米娜問。入口附近的告示提到黃色。

Before leaving, Mina gave the day one last careful look. It had contained a surprise, a question, a decision, and a result. Those parts did not always arrive in the order people wanted. Leo asked whether they were ready for tomorrow. 'Ready to listen,' Mina answered, 'even if tomorrow changes the plan.' The reply was small, but it was true. 離開前，米娜最後仔細回顧這一天：有驚喜、問題、決定，也有結果。事情不一定照人希望的順序發生。里歐問明天準備好了嗎？「準備好傾聽了，」米娜回答，「即使明天又要改計畫。」這句話很簡單，卻是真的。他們收拾用品、謝謝附近的人，也確認沒有讓誰期待一個從未答應的承諾。

The small objects from the day were back in their places. The important words were written down, and the people involved had heard the result. Mina could not know every part of the future, but there was comfort in knowing what to do first: ask, check, and listen. Leo said goodbye with a tired smile. Mina returned the smile. Somewhere beyond the familiar campus, another ordinary day was waiting. It would bring its own surprises, but they did not have to meet them without friends or without a plan.

今天的小物件都回到了原位。重要的話已寫好，相關的人也知道結果。米娜不可能知道未來每件事，卻因知道第一步該怎麼做而安心：發問、查證、傾聽。里歐疲憊地笑著道別，米娜也回以微笑。在熟悉的校園之外，又有平凡的一天等待著，會帶來新的驚喜，但他們不必獨自、毫無計畫地面對。
