A Second Message

Leo sent Mina a note about a library event that needed two volunteers. Campus life rarely gave people a perfect moment to say an important thing. There were classes to reach, books to return, and friends talking nearby. Mina had imagined that love would begin with a grand confession, not with a mistaken tap on a phone. Leo made room for her to be uncertain without turning that uncertainty into a joke. She noticed his kindness, but she also noticed the work he took seriously. A person could be more interesting when seen in an ordinary afternoon than when imagined from a distance. Mina said the word all in a careful answer. A small poster on the wall included the word area. The class reading card used basketball in an example sentence. "We can begin when the others are ready," Mina suggested. "Can we define what success means here?" Mina asked.

里歐傳訊息告訴米娜，圖書館活動需要兩名志工。 校園生活很少提供完美時刻讓人說重要的話。大家要趕課、還書，身邊還有朋友聊天。米娜以前想像愛情從盛大告白開始，而不是誤按手機。里歐讓她能安心不確定，沒有把那份不確定拿來開玩笑。她看見他的善意，也看見他認真對待的工作。從平常下午近距離認識一個人，有時比遠遠想像更有趣。 米娜仔細回答時說出了「全部」。牆上的小海報寫著區域這個字。課堂閱讀卡用籃球造了一個例句。「等其他人準備好，我們就能開始。」米娜提議。「我們能先定義這裡的成功是什麼嗎？」米娜問。

She hesitates because another message might be read as a romantic sign. A question that had sounded easy in the morning now looked more complicated. Mina did not dislike that change; learning often made a simple picture less simple. Leo compared the new detail with what they had already seen, while Mina checked whether the two could really fit together. They discovered that looking carefully was different from looking for proof of a favorite idea. 'We can change our minds,' Leo said. Mina agreed. A good plan could grow as new facts arrived, and no one had to win an argument first. The class reading card used email in an example sentence. A small poster on the wall included the word eraser. Mina said the word few in a careful answer. A small poster on the wall included the word lamp. Mina remembered a drawing of leave seen earlier that week.

她猶豫著，怕任何訊息都被誤會成曖昧。 早上聽來簡單的問題，如今變得複雜。米娜並不討厭這種變化；學習常讓簡單畫面變得豐富。里歐比對新細節和已觀察的事，米娜確認兩者能否真的接上。他們發現仔細觀察和只找支持自己想法的證據不同。「我們可以改變想法。」里歐說。米娜同意。好計畫能隨新事實成長，不需要先爭個輸贏。 課堂閱讀卡用電子郵件造了一個例句。牆上的小海報寫著橡皮擦這個字。米娜仔細回答時說出了「少數」。牆上的小海報寫著燈這個字。米娜想起本週稍早看到的離開圖畫。

Mina chose to answer clearly about the work instead of avoiding him. The choice did not come with a promise of success. Mina knew it might take patience and that an honest attempt could still fail. Leo suggested checking the smallest part first, so they could stop safely if it did not work. They considered who should ask permission, who should keep the notes, and who would speak if a misunderstanding appeared. 'We do not have to do everything alone,' Mina said. Sharing the work made the decision less frightening, not because it was easier, but because everyone knew what they had agreed to do. There was less noise once everyone knew the plan. A small poster on the wall included the word mail. The day felt modern to Mina, so Leo asked what was wrong. Mina remembered a drawing of night seen earlier that week. Somebody had packed orange for a short break later.

米娜決定直接回覆工作內容，不再躲避他。 這個選擇沒有保證成功。米娜知道它需要耐心，認真嘗試也可能失敗。里歐建議先測試最小的部分，不合適就能安全停止。他們討論由誰詢問許可、誰保管紀錄、誤會發生時由誰說明。「不必什麼都自己做。」米娜說。分工讓決定不再那麼可怕，不是因為事情變簡單，而是每個人都清楚自己答應什麼。 每個人了解計畫後，吵鬧聲少了。牆上的小海報寫著郵件這個字。米娜覺得今天有些現代化的，里歐便問怎麼了。米娜想起本週稍早看到的夜晚圖畫。有人為稍後的休息準備了橘色。

They arrange to help visitors choose books on Saturday morning. Some stories end at the first confession. Mina was beginning to think the interesting part came afterward: whether people would listen on difficult days, keep their separate dreams, and say what they really meant. Leo had plans of his own, and she did too. Their growing friendship did not make those plans smaller. She put her phone away and met his eyes when he spoke. If another important message came, she wanted to understand the person behind it before deciding how to answer. A small poster on the wall included the word plant. The meeting was set for 3 p.m. on the notice. Someone had asked Leo a question about quarter. The open book contained a short story about traffic. A notice near the entrance mentioned will.

兩人約好星期六早上幫訪客挑書。 有些故事在第一次告白就結束。米娜卻開始覺得有趣的部分在後面：難過的日子是否還願意聽、能否保有各自的夢想、會不會誠實說出心意。里歐有自己的計畫，她也一樣。友情成長沒有縮小那些計畫。她收起手機，對方說話時看著他的眼睛。若再收到重要訊息，她想先理解訊息背後的人，再決定如何回答。 牆上的小海報寫著盆栽這個字。告示上寫著會議在下午三點舉行。有人曾問里歐關於一刻鐘的問題。翻開的書有一則談到往來人流的小故事。入口附近的告示提到將會。

Later, Mina looked back at the four parts of the day: what happened, what they learned, what they decided, and what followed. The list was simple enough to fit on a little page, but it held much more than a score or a rumor. Leo read the page and pointed to a place where the order could be clearer. Together they fixed it. They also left a line for the next person to write a question. That made the record useful even when the people who started it were not there.

稍後，米娜回想今天的四部分：發生了什麼、學到了什麼、做了什麼決定，以及結果如何。清單雖然只有小小一頁，卻比一個分數或流言包含更多內容。里歐讀完後指出一處順序不清楚，兩人一起修改。他們還留下一行，讓之後接手的人能寫下問題。這樣，即使最初參與的人不在場，紀錄也依然有用。

When Mina turned the last page of the notes, one line stood out: people deserve the chance to explain their own choices. It was easy to forget that in a busy day, especially when everyone wanted a quick ending. Leo had reminded Mina more than once to keep an open mind. The lesson was not that people should wait forever. It was that acting with care usually made the next action safer and fairer. Mina closed the book and made a quiet plan to remember that tomorrow.

米娜翻到筆記最後一頁，看見一行話特別醒目：每個人都應有機會解釋自己的選擇。忙碌時很容易忘記，尤其人人都想快點結束。里歐不只一次提醒米娜保持開放心態。這不是要大家永遠等待，而是說仔細行動通常會讓下一步更安全、更公平。米娜合上本子，默默決定明天也要記住。
