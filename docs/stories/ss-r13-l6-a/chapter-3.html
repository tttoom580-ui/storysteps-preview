帳冊裡的縫線

The Stitch in the Ledger

我不替王冠翻譯謊言 · 第 3 / 25 章 · 5000–6000 · 英中對照 · 校訂中

Nessa opened an archive register in the presence of two witnesses. The page numbering was continuous, but the thread binding had been replaced between the court translation and the authorizing signature. Maelin asked the witnesses to describe the physical damage before anyone proposed a culprit. A shipping note mentioned a school in the hills and a lost parcel of blank paper; neither detail proved forgery by itself. The team made copies, marked the original location, and refused to remove the register from public custody.

妮莎在兩名見證人面前打開檔案登記冊。頁碼連續，然而宮廷譯文與核准簽名之間的裝訂線被換過。梅琳要求證人先描述實際損傷，不要急著指認犯人。一張運單提到山區學校和一包遺失的空白紙；單靠這些都不能證明偽造。團隊製作副本、標記原件位置，並拒絕把登記冊私自帶離公家保管處。

Keir asked whether the decision would still seem reasonable to someone who had not attended the hearing. Nessa read the order aloud without using the officials' titles as shortcuts. Where a reason depended on a document, they named that document. Where no document existed, they said so.

凱爾問，沒參加聽證的人是否仍能理解這項決定。妮莎朗讀命令，沒有以官銜代替解釋。凡理由依賴某份文件，他們就寫出文件名稱；沒有文件的地方，也如實註明。

The advisory council had recorded the treaty signing, but its minutes mentioned no surrender of farmland. The hill envoy rejected the word conquest because the agreement described cooperation rather than military victory. Vask called the missing binding a conspiracy invented by people who wanted to embarrass the crown. The innkeeper served cucumber salad while both delegations waited for the witness room to open. An elderly scribe looked feeble, yet he remembered exactly where the archive chest had stood.

諮詢議會記錄過條約簽署，會議紀錄卻未提到交出農地。 山地使者反對使用「征服」，因為協議寫的是合作而非軍事勝利。 瓦斯克把遺失裝訂線稱為企圖羞辱王室的人編造的陰謀。 兩方代表等待證人室開門時，旅店主人端上小黃瓜沙拉。 一名年老書記看似虛弱，卻清楚記得檔案箱原本的位置。

Iva reminded the officials that a receipt was not merely a scrap of paper. It might represent a month's food, a ferry journey, or a family's decision to remain in its home. She asked the court to keep ordinary consequences beside its grand arguments about sovereignty.

伊娃提醒官員，收據不只是一小片紙；它可能代表一個月的食物、一次渡船，或一個家庭決定留在家鄉。她要求法庭在討論宏大的主權問題時，也記下普通人的實際後果。

A firecracker exploded outside the gate, and the officers checked whether it was a signal or prank. The printer began to fume when investigators asked for the invoice he had already surrendered. Keir wiped grease from a cargo wheel to read the scratched route number beneath it. The preservation order could lessen the risk of destruction but could not determine who had altered the text. A river liner had carried the chest to the island station before the last treaty ceremony.

門外一枚爆竹炸響，官員檢查究竟是暗號還是惡作劇。 調查員再次索取已上交的發票時，印刷工開始氣憤。 凱爾擦去貨輪上的油脂，讀取下面刻出的路線編號。 保全令能降低文件遭毀的風險，卻不能判定誰改過文字。 一艘河上客輪在最後一次條約儀式前把箱子送往島站。

Every version had a history: who held it, who copied it, what was left out, and why it reached one village before another. The team began to treat those histories as part of the evidence. A document could be authentic and still be misused by an official claiming more than it said.

每個版本都有歷史：誰保管、誰抄寫、刪掉什麼、為何先到某村。團隊開始把這些歷史視為證據的一部分。一份文件可能是真的，卻仍被官員利用來聲稱文件沒有授予的權力。

The orphanage kept a teaching copy of the river alphabet in its small reading room. An old plague notice proved that the village had used the crossing long before the disputed decree. The recreational sailing club had logged the unusual midnight delivery in its harbor notebook. An angry official called the river protesters savage, and Maelin demanded that the insult be removed from the record. Nessa found a small shrub growing beside the wall where the binding thread had been dropped.

孤兒院在小閱覽室保存了一份河語字母教學本。 一張舊瘟疫公告證明該村在爭議命令之前很久便使用渡口。 休閒帆船社在港口筆記本記下異常的午夜送貨。 一名憤怒官員稱河民抗議者野蠻，梅琳要求從紀錄中刪除侮辱用語。 妮莎在裝訂線掉落的牆邊發現一叢小灌木。

At the shared meal, people who had argued over language passed bowls across the same table. Nobody pretended that kindness settled legal ownership. Still, the ordinary act of feeding a stranger made it harder to describe the neighboring villages as enemies. The discussion resumed with fewer threats and more questions.

共餐時，曾為語言爭執的人們在同一張桌上互相遞碗。沒有人假裝善意能決定法律所有權，但餵飽陌生人的平凡行動讓人更難把鄰村說成敵人。討論重新開始，威脅少了，問題多了。

The interpreter explained that local slang could not be substituted for a defined legal term. The hill witnesses hoped the evidence would surpass the palace's confident but unsupported account. Three carved stones symbolize the agreement to share the river rather than divide it by force. A textile merchant produced a dated receipt for cloth delivered to the treaty ceremony. A vaccine shipment had passed the same checkpoint, showing that the route served ordinary medical needs.

譯員解釋，地方俚語不能取代定義明確的法律用語。 山地證人希望證據能勝過王宮自信卻缺乏支持的說法。 三塊雕石象徵共同使用河流，而不是武力瓜分。 一名紡織商拿出條約儀式時交貨的布匹日期收據。 一批疫苗也曾通過同一檢查站，顯示路線服務一般醫療需求。

That evening, an envoy from the Hill Council arrived with a complaint: his people had not signed away winter grazing routes. In the novel he had been portrayed as a dangerous conspirator, yet he brought dates, a witness list, and a willingness to answer questions. Maelin explained the binding discrepancy without claiming it proved his case. They agreed to compare the hill copy and the royal copy in an open room. For the first time, two governments prepared to examine the same page together.

當晚，山地議會使者帶著申訴抵達：他們從未簽字放棄冬季放牧路線。小說把他描繪成危險的陰謀家，但他帶來日期、證人名單，也願意接受提問。梅琳說明裝訂異常，卻不宣稱這已證明使者正確。雙方同意在公開房間比對山地本與王室本。兩個政府第一次準備一起查看同一張紙。
