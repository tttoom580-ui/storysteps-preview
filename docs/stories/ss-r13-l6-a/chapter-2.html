The Village That Could Read

The second morning took Maelin to Reed Crossing, where river families had kept their own handwritten copies of the agreement. An elder called Iva explained that the river word translated as “hold” could mean storing provisions on behalf of neighbors, not taking ownership of the land. The governor had applied the second meaning without consulting the community. Keir, an independent surveyor, measured the flood markers while Maelin listened to the people affected. She wrote their exact words beside her own interpretations.

第二天早晨，梅琳前往蘆葦渡口，當地居民保存著手抄的協議。長者伊娃解釋，河語的「持有」既可能指替鄰居保管物資，也可能被解釋成取得土地所有權。總督未徵詢居民便採用了後一種意思。獨立測量員凱爾測量洪水刻度，梅琳則傾聽受影響者，將原話與自己的解讀分開記錄。

Maelin kept a copy of her own earlier summary at the front of the case file. She hated seeing it there. Yet removing it would repeat the kind of convenient erasure she was trying to expose. Nessa watched her make the choice and said nothing, which was sometimes the most respectful help.

梅琳把自己先前的摘要放在卷宗最前面。她痛恨看見它，卻知道拿掉就會重演自己正在揭露的便利抹除。妮莎看著她做出選擇，什麼也沒說。有時不說話才是最尊重人的協助。

An aboriginal elder explained the old names of the river bends before any court translator offered an interpretation. Disputes abound when a public notice contains only the palace language and excludes the others. Nessa tried to align the three handwritten headings without altering the irregular spacing beneath them. Keir recorded the altitude of the ridge because spring floods could isolate the crossing below. The magistrate concealed his annoyance when Iva asked him to repeat a legal question slowly.

一位原住民族長者在宮廷譯員解說前，先說明河灣的舊地名。 公告只有宮廷語而排除其他語言時，爭端便大量出現。 妮莎試圖對齊三份手寫標題，但不改動其下不規則的間距。 凱爾記下山脊海拔，因為春洪可能切斷下方渡口。 伊娃請地方法官慢慢重述法律問題時，他掩飾住自己的不耐。

The judge asked everyone to distinguish an error from a lie, and a lie from a crime. The distinctions did not excuse harmful conduct. They allowed the court to say what had been proved, what remained doubtful, and what another hearing must examine. That discipline protected victims and defendants alike.

法官請大家區分錯誤、謊言與犯罪。這些區分不是替傷害開脫，而是讓法庭能說清楚哪些已證明、哪些仍可疑、哪些須另案審理。這項紀律同時保護受害者與被告。

Iva gave a blunt answer: her neighbors had agreed to store grain, never to lose their homes. The eastern crossing opened briefly after officials verified the court's temporary suspension of collection. Maelin would not discard a damaged draft until both sides had inspected its surviving lines. The river guard received a medical discharge note but still had to answer questions about his shift. Their endeavor was to restore a shared crossing, not to replace one ruler with another.

伊娃直截了當地回答：鄰居同意保管糧食，從未同意失去家園。 官員確認法院的暫停徵收令後，東側渡口短暫開放。 梅琳不願在雙方檢查殘存字句前丟棄破損草稿。 河岸衛兵拿到出院證明後，仍須回答有關值班的問題。 他們努力的是恢復共用渡口，而非以另一位統治者取代現任者。

The guards expected Maelin to demand immediate punishment. Instead she asked for a table, a lamp, and enough time for witnesses to check their own statements. Some officials called this weakness. The witnesses called it the first occasion on which their actual words had survived a meeting.

衛兵以為梅琳會要求立刻懲處。她卻只要一張桌、一盞燈，以及讓證人核對自己供詞的時間。有些官員稱此為軟弱；證人說，這是他們真正的話第一次能在會議後留存。

A missing customs number became the first hurdle for families seeking to recover their grain. Keir changed his posture when the old survey poles no longer matched the new boundary sketch. The interpreter demanded precision in the word for custody because a careless synonym could transfer ownership. Nessa insisted on a rigorous comparison of every stamped page before anyone announced fraud. Families waited on a stone terrace overlooking the water while the magistrate prepared his order.

遺失的海關編號成為家庭領回糧食的第一道障礙。 舊測量桿與新邊界圖不符時，凱爾改變站姿仔細查看。 譯員要求精確使用「保管」一詞，因為草率的同義詞可能轉移所有權。 妮莎堅持先嚴格比對每張蓋印頁面，任何人才能宣布有偽造嫌疑。 家人們在俯瞰河水的石露臺等候地方法官起草命令。

The translator who occupied this body in the original book had liked quick answers. Maelin remembered that too clearly. She had inherited the position and its privileges, but she could not inherit innocence simply by changing her intentions. The next signature she gave would have to mean more than regret.

原著裡這具身體的翻譯官喜歡迅速給答案，梅琳記得太清楚了。她繼承了職位與特權，卻不能只靠改變心意就得到清白。她下一次簽名，必須比後悔有更多意義。

The commander feared an uprising if hungry villagers were again told that their names did not matter. Volunteers offered to usher elderly witnesses toward the chamber without choosing their answers for them. The versatile survey cart carried measuring poles, winter blankets, and a box of public notices. The local council chose not to veto the temporary food route merely because the palace disliked it. A widow spoke about the fee she had paid twice for the same ferry passage.

指揮官擔心若再次告訴挨餓的村民其姓名無關緊要，將引發起義。 志工引導年長證人走向聽證室，但不替他們決定答案。 多用途測量車載著測量桿、冬毯與一箱公開公告。 地方議會沒有只因王宮不喜歡就否決臨時糧食路線。 一名寡婦談起她為同一趟渡船被收取兩次的費用。

The families did not ask Maelin to rescue them as though they had no voices. They demanded a public translation table, a review of seized grain, and safe access to the crossing while the court investigated. A local magistrate could not decide sovereignty, but he could suspend collection for forty-eight hours where proof was disputed. That limited order allowed the first food wagon to pass. Maelin saw Iva sign the receipt in her own language and realized the story's supposed villains had never been permitted to describe themselves.

居民並未要求梅琳像救助無聲者一般拯救他們。他們要求公開翻譯對照表、複查被徵收的糧食，並在調查期間保障通行安全。地方法官不能決定主權，卻能在證據有爭議時暫停徵收四十八小時。這道有限命令讓第一輛糧車得以通過。梅琳看見伊娃用自己的文字簽收，終於明白小說所稱的「惡民」從未獲准自己發聲。
