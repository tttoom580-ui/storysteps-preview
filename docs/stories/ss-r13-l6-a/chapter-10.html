讓步的代價

The Price of a Concession

我不替王冠翻譯謊言 · 第 10 / 25 章 · 5000–6000 · 英中對照 · 校訂中

At the first formal hearing, Vask admitted that the palace had distributed a shortened version for convenience. He called the changes harmless and offered a concession: families could keep half their grain if they accepted the crown's interpretation of the border. The proposal divided the room. Maelin argued that disputed ownership could not be settled by forcing hungry people to choose between food and legal rights. She requested an interim supply plan independent of the territorial question.

第一次正式聽證時，瓦斯克承認王宮為了方便而分發簡短版本，卻稱改動無害。他提出讓步：家庭若接受王室的邊界解釋，便可保留一半糧食。提案讓現場意見分裂。梅琳指出，不能逼挨餓的人在食物與法律權利之間二選一，藉此決定有爭議的所有權。她請求將臨時糧食安排與領土問題分開處理。

When a clerk faltered, Maelin did not finish the answer for him. She repeated the question in plain language and let him decide whether he knew. Silence was recorded as silence, not consent. The distinction irritated several ministers, which only convinced her that it was worth preserving.

書記猶豫時，梅琳沒有替他答完。她用白話重述問題，讓他自己判斷是否知道。沉默被記作沉默，而非同意。這使幾名大臣不耐，也更讓她相信必須保留這項區分。

The chancellor offered a concession that would feed the villages only if they accepted his border claim. Maelin heard a cynical laugh when she asked why hungry families had been given such a choice. The treasury threatened to default on the bridge contract unless disputed grain fees were collected. Winter hunger could deplete the villagers' food stores long before the judges finished their arguments. The fragrance of warm bread drifted into the hearing room from the public kitchen outside.

宰相提出讓步：村民只有接受他的邊界主張，才能獲得糧食。 梅琳詢問為何要逼挨餓家庭作這種選擇時，聽見一聲冷嘲。 國庫威脅，若不收取有爭議的糧食費，就會拖欠橋梁合約款項。 法官辯論結束前，冬季飢餓可能早已耗盡村民糧食儲備。 熱麵包的香氣從外面的公共廚房飄進聽證室。

Maelin kept a copy of her own earlier summary at the front of the case file. She hated seeing it there. Yet removing it would repeat the kind of convenient erasure she was trying to expose. Nessa watched her make the choice and said nothing, which was sometimes the most respectful help.

梅琳把自己先前的摘要放在卷宗最前面。她痛恨看見它，卻知道拿掉就會重演自己正在揭露的便利抹除。妮莎看著她做出選擇，什麼也沒說。有時不說話才是最尊重人的協助。

A palace agent tried to intrude on the confidential meeting between Iva and her legal adviser. A young jockey delivered a sealed message after taking the fastest road beside the racetrack. Anika demanded a literal reading of the disputed sentence before the palace offered its interpretation. The temporary food order raised morale among ferry workers who had feared losing their winter wages. The tribunal could prosecute deliberate fraud only after establishing who had authorized the replacement.

王宮代理人試圖闖入伊娃與法律顧問的保密會面。 一名年輕騎師沿賽馬場旁最快的路送來封口信件。 安妮卡要求先逐字朗讀爭議句，再聽王宮的解釋。 臨時配糧令提高了渡口工人的士氣，他們原本擔心失去冬季工資。 法庭必須確定誰核准替換文件，才能起訴故意詐欺。

The judge asked everyone to distinguish an error from a lie, and a lie from a crime. The distinctions did not excuse harmful conduct. They allowed the court to say what had been proved, what remained doubtful, and what another hearing must examine. That discipline protected victims and defendants alike.

法官請大家區分錯誤、謊言與犯罪。這些區分不是替傷害開脫，而是讓法庭能說清楚哪些已證明、哪些仍可疑、哪些須另案審理。這項紀律同時保護受害者與被告。

An old mining report mentioned radioactive stone, but Keir confirmed it had no connection to the treaty case. Vask delivered a sharp retort when Maelin said that a convenient translation was not necessarily a lawful one. A mechanic used a screwdriver to repair the hearing room's broken speaking stand before testimony resumed. Each serving of soup was recorded separately so food distribution could not be confused with land settlement. The splendor of the royal hall could not hide the hunger described by witnesses from the river villages.

舊礦業報告提及放射性石料，凱爾確認它與條約案件無關。 梅琳說方便的翻譯未必合法時，瓦斯克尖刻地反駁。 技師用螺絲起子修好聽證室損壞的發言台，證詞才得以繼續。 每份湯食單獨記錄，避免配糧與土地和解混為一談。 王室大廳的華麗掩蓋不了河岸村民證詞中的飢餓。

The guards expected Maelin to demand immediate punishment. Instead she asked for a table, a lamp, and enough time for witnesses to check their own statements. Some officials called this weakness. The witnesses called it the first occasion on which their actual words had survived a meeting.

衛兵以為梅琳會要求立刻懲處。她卻只要一張桌、一盞燈，以及讓證人核對自己供詞的時間。有些官員稱此為軟弱；證人說，這是他們真正的話第一次能在會議後留存。

A spontaneous offer from local bakers filled the temporary shelter with bread before nightfall. The witness's stepfather signed a statement confirming that the family had paid the disputed ferry fee. The young clerk began to stutter under pressure, so the judge gave him time to answer slowly. The government agreed to subsidize emergency grain transport without demanding a surrender of property rights. A synthetic ribbon on the false archive seal differed from the natural fiber used in the originals.

當地麵包師自發提供協助，夜幕前把麵包送滿臨時避難所。 證人的繼父簽署聲明，確認家人支付了有爭議的渡船費。 年輕書記在壓力下開始口吃，法官讓他慢慢回答。 政府同意補貼緊急運糧，不要求居民放棄財產權。 偽造檔案封印上的合成纖維帶與原件使用的天然纖維不同。

The judge approved emergency food distribution under independent accounting and continued the hearing. Maelin then acknowledged on record that her own careless summary had weakened the original safeguards. Some spectators jeered; others wanted her immediately punished. The judge reserved that question for a separate proceeding. Maelin accepted the separation because a translator's fault and a suspected forgery required different evidence. By sunset, no one had won the border dispute, but nobody had to sign away a home to receive a meal.

法官准許在獨立帳目監督下緊急配糧，並繼續審理。梅琳隨後在紀錄中承認，自己粗心的摘要削弱了原始保障。有些旁聽者嘲諷，有些要求立刻處罰她。法官將此問題另案處理。梅琳接受分案，因為翻譯官的疏失與疑似偽造需由不同證據判斷。日落時無人贏得邊界爭端，但也沒人必須放棄家園才能吃飯。
