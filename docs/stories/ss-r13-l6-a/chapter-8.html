A Copy Under the Classroom Floor

我不替王冠翻譯謊言 · 第 8 / 25 章 · 5000–6000 · 英中對照 · 校訂中

用互動版查詞、朗讀與收藏

A teacher from the inland school led Nessa to a storage box beneath a raised classroom floor. It contained a certified treaty copy, wrapped against dampness, with a disputed river phrase written more fully than in the palace edition. The school had been permitted to store teaching copies under the original agreement. Maelin requested laboratory comparison of the inks and seals before announcing what the discovery meant. Children watched the adults weigh the evidence, learning that authority was not the same as accuracy.

內陸學校的老師帶妮莎到高架教室地板下的儲物箱。裡面有一份經認證的條約副本，妥善防潮保存，爭議的河語句子比宮廷版完整。原協議准許學校保存教學副本。梅琳請求先化驗墨水與印章，再宣布發現的意義。孩子們看著大人衡量證據，學到權威不等於準確。

Nessa paused to ask who had not been invited into the room. The answer was rarely flattering: an absent ferryman, a younger clerk, a family speaking an unfamiliar dialect. The team changed its witness schedule rather than treating attendance as proof that every voice had already been heard.

妮莎停下來問，還有誰沒被邀進房間。答案通常不好聽：缺席的船夫、年輕書記、使用陌生方言的家庭。團隊調整證人時程，不再把有人出席當作所有聲音都已被聽見。

The storage log showed an accumulation of sealed bundles beneath the classroom floor over several years. A volunteer served a warm beverage to the children waiting through the document inspection. A blond teacher carefully unfolded the school copy without touching its inked surface. Two appointed experts had to certify the age of the paper before the court could rely on it. Nessa requested written clearance to photograph the school copy and leave the original in place.

儲藏簿顯示，數年間教室地板下累積了多捆封存文件。 志工替等待文件檢查的孩子端來熱飲。 一名金髮老師小心展開學校副本，沒有碰觸墨跡表面。 法院採信前，必須有兩名指定專家證明紙張年代。 妮莎申請書面許可拍攝學校副本，原件則留在原處。

Maelin divided the notebook into three spaces: the words preserved on paper, the memories people could verify, and the conclusions that remained uncertain. She knew how tempting it was to merge them into one elegant explanation. Yet the families at the border needed something sturdier than an elegant explanation.

梅琳把筆記分成三欄：紙上保存的文字、證人可以核實的記憶，以及仍不確定的結論。她知道把三者合成漂亮說法有多誘人；邊境家庭需要的卻是比漂亮說法更可靠的東西。

The clerk could deduct transport expenses from the budget only with receipts signed by both custodians. Maelin realized the copied warning was destined to matter more than the elegant ceremonial title. The survey team had to exert care when lifting the old storage chest from damp floorboards. The villagers called the teacher's quiet refusal to surrender the original heroic, though she asked for no praise. A retired lieutenant helped protect the school without interfering with the investigators' authority.

書記只有拿到雙方保管者簽署的收據，才能從預算扣除運費。 梅琳發現副本裡的警告注定比華麗的儀式標題更重要。 測量團隊從潮濕地板抬起舊儲藏箱時必須格外用力且小心。 村民稱老師拒絕交出原件的沉默堅持很英勇，她卻不求讚美。 一名退役中尉協助保護學校，沒有干涉調查權限。

Anika spoke slowly, leaving space after every translated sentence. She would not allow speed to become another form of power. When a listener asked for a phrase to be repeated, she did so without complaint. The pause made the meeting longer and the eventual agreement much clearer.

安妮卡說得很慢，每句翻譯後都留出空間。她不讓速度成為另一種權力。有人要求重述句子時，她毫無怨言地照做。停頓讓會議較長，最後的理解卻清楚得多。

Every literate child in the school could read the disputed river sentence more accurately than the official pamphlet. A lotus flower had been pressed between the pages as a marker for the river passage. The marginal note explained why the river verb described custody rather than ownership. The seals showed an overlap of ink from two different periods, prompting further laboratory tests. A provincial inspector confirmed that the classroom register had not left the district for ten years.

學校裡每個識字的孩子都能比官方小冊子更準確讀懂爭議河語句。 一朵蓮花被壓在書頁間，作為河流條款的書籤。 頁邊註記解釋為何河語動詞指保管而非所有權。 印章顯示兩個年代的墨跡重疊，因此需要進一步化驗。 一名地方督察確認，教室登記冊十年間未離開該區。

The river families disagreed among themselves about what result would be fair. Some wanted immediate compensation; others wanted land records corrected first. Maelin asked them to choose representatives without palace interference. A single sympathetic translator, however well intentioned, could not replace their right to decide.

河岸家庭對公平結果也有分歧。有人要立刻補償，有人要先更正土地紀錄。梅琳請他們不受王宮干預自行選代表。再善良的翻譯官，也不能取代他們決定的權利。

The interpreter's professional qualification did not excuse her from explaining an omitted warning. Parents offered refreshments after the experts completed their examination of the preserved copy. Nessa promised to retrieve a second copy only after receiving permission from the Hill Council. The school yard bordered a swamp that made unauthorized night travel difficult. The children learned that a translator's vocation involved responsibility to readers, not blind loyalty to rulers.

翻譯官的專業資格不能免除她解釋警語為何被省略的責任。 專家完成保存副本的檢查後，家長提供茶點。 妮莎承諾取得山地議會許可後才取回第二份副本。 校園旁是沼澤，讓未經許可的夜間通行變得困難。 孩子們學到翻譯家的職志是對讀者負責，而非盲目效忠統治者。

The ink tests showed that the classroom document was old enough to predate the current dispute. They could not establish who wrote every word. That evening Maelin received a note from the queen: tell the court everything, including the omission you approved. She understood the queen was offering no pardon. Still, the note made one duty clear. She would testify without asking to be spared the consequences of her own negligence.

墨水檢驗證實教室文件年代早於這場爭議，卻無法確定每個字是誰寫的。當晚梅琳收到女王便條：「告訴法庭全部事實，包括妳核准省略的內容。」她明白女王沒有提供赦免。然而便條使一項責任變得清楚：她會作證，不要求免除自己疏失的後果。
