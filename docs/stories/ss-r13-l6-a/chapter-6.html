地圖下方的說明

The Caption Beneath the Map

我不替王冠翻譯謊言 · 第 6 / 25 章 · 5000–6000 · 英中對照 · 校訂中

Keir found that the public map's caption called a temporary shelter a permanent border post. The words were small, but soldiers had used them to keep families from returning home. Maelin traced the caption to a committee meeting where no qualified river interpreter had been present. She insisted that the map and the meeting minutes be reviewed together. An argument about ink now involved real doors, roads, and livelihoods. The clerk who prepared the map agreed to testify only if her employer could not dismiss her immediately.

凱爾發現公開地圖的圖說把臨時避難站稱作永久邊境哨所。字很小，士兵卻據此阻止家人返鄉。梅琳查出圖說來自一場沒有合格河語口譯員出席的委員會議。她堅持必須把地圖和會議紀錄一起審查。一場關於墨字的爭執，如今涉及真實的門、路和生計。製圖書記同意作證，條件是雇主不能立刻解雇她。

The guards expected Maelin to demand immediate punishment. Instead she asked for a table, a lamp, and enough time for witnesses to check their own statements. Some officials called this weakness. The witnesses called it the first occasion on which their actual words had survived a meeting.

衛兵以為梅琳會要求立刻懲處。她卻只要一張桌、一盞燈，以及讓證人核對自己供詞的時間。有些官員稱此為軟弱；證人說，這是他們真正的話第一次能在會議後留存。

The city council could allege misconduct, but Maelin asked it to identify the specific page and signature. Officials promised to beautify the map room while refusing to correct the misleading border labels. The caption beneath the map described a temporary shelter as a permanent royal checkpoint. The hearing reached its climax when a young cartographer refused to repeat an unverified claim. Nessa used a special lamp to extract faint writing from beneath the later ink without damaging the sheet.

市議會可以指控不當行為，但梅琳要求指出具體頁碼與簽名。 官員承諾美化地圖室，卻拒絕更正誤導性的邊界標籤。 地圖下的圖說把臨時避難所描述成永久王室檢查站。 當年輕製圖員拒絕重複未經證實的說法時，聽證達到高潮。 妮莎利用特殊燈具辨認後加墨水下的淡字，沒有損壞紙張。

The translator who occupied this body in the original book had liked quick answers. Maelin remembered that too clearly. She had inherited the position and its privileges, but she could not inherit innocence simply by changing her intentions. The next signature she gave would have to mean more than regret.

原著裡這具身體的翻譯官喜歡迅速給答案，梅琳記得太清楚了。她繼承了職位與特權，卻不能只靠改變心意就得到清白。她下一次簽名，必須比後悔有更多意義。

An extracurricular mapmaking club had kept an earlier classroom sketch of the bridge. The village inn was hospitable to visiting observers even when they disagreed with its owners. A jolly old driver offered tea while the investigators compared delivery times. Farm workers had to mow a narrow path before surveyors could reach the buried marker. The clerk with a thick mustache remembered carrying the map tube into the committee room.

一個課外製圖社團保存了更早的教室橋梁草圖。 村莊旅店熱情接待訪客觀察員，即使他們與店主意見不合。 一位開朗的老車夫在調查員比對送貨時間時端來茶。 測量員到達埋沒的界標前，農工必須先割出一條狹窄小路。 留著濃鬍子的書記記得曾把地圖筒帶進委員會議室。

When a clerk faltered, Maelin did not finish the answer for him. She repeated the question in plain language and let him decide whether he knew. Silence was recorded as silence, not consent. The distinction irritated several ministers, which only convinced her that it was worth preserving.

書記猶豫時，梅琳沒有替他答完。她用白話重述問題，讓他自己判斷是否知道。沉默被記作沉默，而非同意。這使幾名大臣不耐，也更讓她相信必須保留這項區分。

Maelin would not nag the witness into an answer when he honestly could not recall the hour. The riverside shelter became an oasis for displaced families during the dispute. The sealing wax weighed less than an ounce, yet its broken edge offered a useful comparison. The printing contractor operated on the outskirts of the capital, beyond the usual patrol route. The parliament record showed the emergency border powers had never been approved in full.

證人真心想不起時間時，梅琳不願反覆催逼他回答。 爭端期間，河邊避難所成了流離失所家庭的綠洲。 封蠟不到一盎司重，破損邊緣卻提供有用的比對依據。 印刷承包商在首都郊區營業，位於一般巡邏路線之外。 議會紀錄顯示，緊急邊界權力從未獲得完整核准。

Maelin kept a copy of her own earlier summary at the front of the case file. She hated seeing it there. Yet removing it would repeat the kind of convenient erasure she was trying to expose. Nessa watched her make the choice and said nothing, which was sometimes the most respectful help.

梅琳把自己先前的摘要放在卷宗最前面。她痛恨看見它，卻知道拿掉就會重演自己正在揭露的便利抹除。妮莎看著她做出選擇，什麼也沒說。有時不說話才是最尊重人的協助。

A ruthless collector threatened to seize grain even after the suspension order arrived. A sculptor confirmed that the stone boundary marker had been moved only recently. Maelin preferred the simplicity of a dated original to the grandeur of an unsupported proclamation. Three simultaneous deliveries used the same unofficial route around the southern checkpoint. Public confidence fell into a slump when the palace refused to explain the disputed caption.

即使暫停令已送達，一名無情的徵收員仍威脅扣押糧食。 一名雕刻師確認，石製界標是最近才被移動的。 相較缺乏證據的華麗宣告，梅琳更重視附日期原件的簡明。 三批同時送達的貨物都使用繞過南方檢查站的非官方路線。 王宮拒絕解釋爭議圖說，公眾信心因此陷入低潮。

The independent tribunal provided temporary protection but refused to guarantee lifelong employment. Maelin explained that limit plainly. At night she found a second altered caption on a food distribution chart, printed before the alleged emergency began. That timing suggested preparation, not a spontaneous misunderstanding, yet an unknown clerk could still have made an innocent draft. She submitted the finding as a question for examination and resolved not to call a suspicion a verdict.

獨立法庭提供暫時保護，卻拒絕保證終身工作。梅琳坦白說明限制。夜裡，她在糧食配給圖上發現另一處改過的圖說，印製日期竟早於所謂緊急事件。這個時間差暗示事前準備，而非臨時誤會；但未知的書記也可能只是無心起草。她把發現提交審查，決定不把懷疑當判決。
