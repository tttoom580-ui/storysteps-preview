The Seal That Was Too Perfect

我不替王冠翻譯謊言 · 第 11 / 25 章 · 5000–6000 · 英中對照 · 校訂中

用互動版查詞、朗讀與收藏

Nessa laid three seals beneath a magnifying lens. The royal seal and hill seal showed ordinary wear, but the stamp on the public summary was unnaturally even. Keir found a craftsman's invoice for a new printing plate delivered after the original signing. That fact alone was not criminal; duplicate plates were sometimes permitted. The troubling detail was the invoice's coded destination, which did not match the registered archive. The tribunal authorized a narrow search.

妮莎把三枚印記放在放大鏡下。王室和山地印記有正常磨損，公開摘要的印章卻異常均勻。凱爾找到工匠發票，顯示原約簽署後曾送出新印版。複製印版有時合法，單憑此事不算犯罪。真正可疑的是發票的代碼目的地與登記檔案室不符。法庭准許有限範圍的搜索。

Maelin could remember the cruel ending written for her character, but the living world kept refusing to follow it exactly. Each witness made choices the novel had ignored. She began to think less about escaping the execution scene and more about leaving a procedure that might work after she was gone.

梅琳記得角色殘酷的原定結局，但活生生的世界不斷拒絕照本演出。每名證人都做出了小說忽略的選擇。她漸漸少想如何逃過處決場面，多想如何留下自己離開後仍有效的程序。

The temperature in the print shop rose above thirty degrees Celsius during the inspection. Friction had worn the old seal plate unevenly, unlike the smooth new impression. A guard dog began to growl when strangers approached the workshop's rear door. Maelin would not induce a confession by promising a pardon the tribunal had never authorized. Several families inhabit the alley beside the workshop, and their accounts of deliveries were recorded separately.

檢查期間，印刷坊的氣溫升到攝氏三十度以上。 摩擦使舊印版磨損不均，與光滑的新印痕不同。 陌生人靠近工坊後門時，警犬開始低吼。 梅琳不會藉承諾法庭未核准的赦免來誘導供認。 幾戶家庭住在工坊旁的巷子裡，他們對送貨的說法被分別記錄。

Keir asked whether the decision would still seem reasonable to someone who had not attended the hearing. Nessa read the order aloud without using the officials' titles as shortcuts. Where a reason depended on a document, they named that document. Where no document existed, they said so.

凱爾問，沒參加聽證的人是否仍能理解這項決定。妮莎朗讀命令，沒有以官銜代替解釋。凡理由依賴某份文件，他們就寫出文件名稱；沒有文件的地方，也如實註明。

It was ironic that the palace demanded perfect language while publishing a summary no one had checked. The workers found a mound of discarded paper behind a wall used for temporary storage. A peacock wandered across the old courtyard as investigators examined the sealed printing plates. The town pianist stopped practicing when the officers began their formal interviews. Nessa used a narrow probe to inspect the edge of the plate without touching the engraved letters.

王宮要求語言完美，自己卻發行未經核對的摘要，實在諷刺。 工人在臨時儲物牆後發現一堆丟棄的紙張。 調查員檢查封存印版時，一隻孔雀走過舊庭院。 官員開始正式訪談時，鎮上的鋼琴師停止練習。 妮莎用細探針檢查印版邊緣，沒有碰到刻字。

Iva reminded the officials that a receipt was not merely a scrap of paper. It might represent a month's food, a ferry journey, or a family's decision to remain in its home. She asked the court to keep ordinary consequences beside its grand arguments about sovereignty.

伊娃提醒官員，收據不只是一小片紙；它可能代表一個月的食物、一次渡船，或一個家庭決定留在家鄉。她要求法庭在討論宏大的主權問題時，也記下普通人的實際後果。

Each witness received a copy of the notes taken during their respective interviews. A shortsighted minister called the missing word unimportant because the map looked almost unchanged. Maelin tried to simplify the public explanation without removing the legal qualification from the original. Ships crossing the strait depended on accurate winter notices from the same office that printed the treaty. Nessa refused a superficial inspection and measured the spacing between engraved letters.

每名證人都收到自己那場訪談的筆記副本。 一名目光短淺的大臣稱遺失的字不重要，因為地圖看來幾乎沒變。 梅琳試圖簡化公開說明，但不刪去原文的法律限定語。 穿越海峽的船隻仰賴同一辦公室發布的正確冬季公告。 妮莎拒絕膚淺檢查，仔細測量刻字間距。

Every version had a history: who held it, who copied it, what was left out, and why it reached one village before another. The team began to treat those histories as part of the evidence. A document could be authentic and still be misused by an official claiming more than it said.

每個版本都有歷史：誰保管、誰抄寫、刪掉什麼、為何先到某村。團隊開始把這些歷史視為證據的一部分。一份文件可能是真的，卻仍被官員利用來聲稱文件沒有授予的權力。

No officer was allowed to suppress a witness statement merely because it contradicted the palace account. The perfect symmetry of the new seal suggested a different tool from the worn official stamp. A brass token bearing the shop number appeared in the carrier's payment purse. The delegates hoped the three authentic versions could unify the public record without erasing their differences. An urgency notice required investigators to preserve the plates before the workshop reopened.

任何官員都不得只因證詞與王宮說法相反就壓下證言。 新印記過於對稱，顯示可能使用不同於磨損官印的工具。 信差的付款袋裡出現一枚帶工坊編號的黃銅代幣。 代表希望三份真實版本能統一公開紀錄，同時不抹去差異。 一份緊急通知要求調查員在工坊重開前保全印版。

Searchers discovered a workshop that had printed several unauthorized summaries. The owner supplied his order book and identified the official who had paid him, but he could not say who wrote the words. Maelin refused to fill that gap with a name from the novel. Instead, she prepared a side-by-side sheet marking punctuation, word order, and dates. A judge remarked that it lacked drama. The families waiting outside did not want drama; they wanted a decision that would survive appeal.

搜索者發現一間印製多份未經授權摘要的工坊。店主交出訂單簿並指認付款官員，卻不知道文字由誰撰寫。梅琳拒絕拿小說裡的人名填補空白，而是準備並排比較表，標示標點、字序和日期。一名法官說這很不戲劇化。外面等待的家庭卻不需要戲劇，他們需要經得起上訴的決定。
