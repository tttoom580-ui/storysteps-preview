The Banquet of Three Seals

我不替王冠翻譯謊言 · 第 5 / 25 章 · 5000–6000 · 英中對照 · 校訂中

A peace banquet was announced before the investigation had reached any conclusion. Vask intended to display a single polished translation beneath the three national seals. Maelin refused to endorse it. She asked the foreign delegates to bring their own copies and proposed an independent table of unresolved phrases. Her request embarrassed the palace, but the Hill Council and River Assembly both accepted. The public ceremony became an unexpected opportunity to compare documents rather than an occasion to declare false harmony.

調查尚未得出結論，宮廷便宣布舉辦和平宴會。瓦斯克打算把一份精美的單一譯本展示在三國印章之下。梅琳拒絕背書，要求外國代表帶來各自副本，並提出獨立的未決詞語對照表。此舉使王宮難堪，山地議會和河民議會卻都接受了。公開儀式意外變成比對文件的機會，而非宣告虛假和諧的場合。

The magistrate warned that a temporary order could not decide the whole dispute. Maelin agreed to the limit even when villagers begged for a quicker result. She would not promise that one hearing could restore every lost wage or broken relationship. Honest limits were painful, but they kept the next appeal possible.

地方法官警告，暫時命令不能決定整場爭端。即使村民希望更快，她仍接受限制。她不承諾一次聽證能找回所有失去的工資與破裂關係。誠實的界線令人難受，卻讓下一次申訴仍有可能。

The royal academy sent two language scholars to observe the banquet without taking sides. Vask rejected the accusation that his office had changed the treaty for private gain. At the banquet, every guest received a copy of the disputed clause in three languages. A farmer explained that fertilizer purchased for spring planting was now locked inside a seized warehouse. The coastal fishery had lost buyers because the new checkpoint charged an unauthorized fee.

王立學院派了兩名語言學者觀察宴會，不預先選邊。 瓦斯克否認其辦公室為私利修改條約的指控。 宴會上每位賓客都拿到三種語言並列的爭議條款。 農民解釋，為春耕購買的肥料如今被鎖在遭扣押的倉庫。 沿岸漁業因新檢查站收取未授權費用而失去買家。

As evening fell, the public board displayed what the investigators had confirmed and what they still needed to check. People stopped to read, sometimes pointing out missing names or dates. The record grew more complicated with each correction, and more trustworthy because those corrections could be seen.

傍晚，公告板列出調查員已確認及仍須查核的事項。人們停下閱讀，不時指出缺少的姓名或日期。紀錄因每次更正而更複雜，也因更正公開可見而更可信。

Keir reported a fracture in the bridge beam that could endanger the next grain convoy. Dock workers used a safety harness while repairing the warehouse roof before the rain. An archivist wore headphones to review a preserved reading of the treaty ceremony. The emergency grain intake had doubled, but the market had no public record of distribution. The maiden voyage of a new ferry brought delegates who had never met the palace officers.

凱爾報告橋梁橫樑有裂縫，可能危及下一支運糧隊。 碼頭工人在雨前修理倉庫屋頂時使用安全吊帶。 檔案員戴著耳機聆聽保存的條約儀式宣讀錄音。 緊急糧食收取量倍增，市場卻沒有公開分配紀錄。 新渡船的首航載來從未見過宮廷官員的代表。

A single disputed sentence had traveled farther than any messenger: into receipts, school lessons, bridge notices, and family decisions. Maelin could not undo all of those consequences by changing one page. She nevertheless had to begin with the page, because every later decision had borrowed its supposed authority from it.

一句有爭議的文字傳得比任何信差都遠，進入收據、課本、橋梁公告和家庭決定。梅琳不能只改一頁就消除全部後果，但她必須從那一頁開始，因為往後的決定都借用了它表面上的權威。

An offshore storm forced the banquet's planned river parade to be cancelled. A prospective buyer was told that the grain's ownership remained under legal review. The Assembly demanded a referendum only after residents had received translations they could actually read. Nessa asked the clerks to refine the public table without concealing the original spelling. Families could rejoice at the temporary food order without pretending the land dispute was over.

近海風暴迫使宴會預定的河上遊行取消。 一名潛在買家被告知，糧食所有權仍在司法審理中。 議會要求先讓居民讀懂譯文，再舉行公民投票。 妮莎請書記改善公開對照表，但不可隱藏原始拼法。 家人可以為臨時配糧令欣喜，無須假裝土地爭議已結束。

Nessa numbered the pages before anyone was permitted to rearrange them. She asked each handler to sign the transfer book and note the condition of the seal. It was slow work, but the record would show precisely where uncertainty entered instead of allowing the powerful to hide it later.

妮莎在任何人重排頁面之前編好頁碼，要求經手者在交接簿簽名並記下封印狀態。工作很慢，紀錄卻能指出不確定從何而來，不讓有權者日後藏起來。

Some fishers began to resent the palace spokesman for describing their losses as trivial. A serene moment followed when the three delegations read the same sentence aloud together. The temple speaker gave a sermon about promises but refused to decide what the treaty legally meant. The guests had to shuffle between the hall and the archive to inspect the authenticated seals. A tenant farmer testified that the governor had collected payment on land he did not own.

部分漁民開始怨恨王宮發言人，因為他把損失說得微不足道。 三方代表一起朗讀同一句話後，現場出現短暫寧靜。 寺院講者發表關於承諾的講道，卻拒絕替條約的法律意思下定論。 賓客必須在宴會廳與檔案室間緩步移動，查看認證印章。 一名佃農作證說，總督在不屬於他的土地上收取費用。

During the meal, a fisherwoman showed how the disputed clause affected the price of her winter catch and the wages of dock workers. Her testimony made the legal wording concrete. The judges issued a limited prohibition against selling the seized grain until its ownership could be reviewed. Maelin accepted the result with relief, then signed an admission that her earlier summary had omitted a warning about ambiguity. Saving the grain was progress; repairing trust would take longer.

席間，一名漁婦說明爭議條款如何影響冬季魚獲價格和碼頭工資。她的證詞讓法律措辭有了實際意義。法官發出有限禁令：糧食所有權完成複查前不得出售被徵收的糧食。梅琳鬆了口氣，也簽署承認自己先前的摘要省略了語義不明的警告。保住糧食是進展，重建信任則需要更久。
