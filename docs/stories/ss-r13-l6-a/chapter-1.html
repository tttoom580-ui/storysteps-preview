改變邊界的那個字

The Word That Changed a Border

我不替王冠翻譯謊言 · 第 1 / 25 章 · 5000–6000 · 英中對照 · 校訂中

Maelin Vale woke inside a book she had once edited, with a royal interpreter's chain around her neck and a trial scheduled for sunrise. In the original story, Maelin would be executed for selling the river border to a foreign court. Now she faced a treaty with three authorized language versions and one troubling difference: the court copy said the villages must surrender grain, while a penciled river draft said they could share it voluntarily. Knowing the plot would not count as evidence, she asked the registrar to preserve every existing page.

梅琳・維爾在自己曾編輯過的小說裡醒來，頸上掛著王室翻譯官的徽鏈，而她的審判將在日出時開始。原著中的梅琳因出賣河岸邊境而被處死。如今她看見條約的三種正式語文版本存在重大差異：宮廷本要求村民交出糧食，河語草稿卻寫著可以自願分享。她明白記得劇情不能當證據，因此要求登記官保全所有現存頁面。

Nessa numbered the pages before anyone was permitted to rearrange them. She asked each handler to sign the transfer book and note the condition of the seal. It was slow work, but the record would show precisely where uncertainty entered instead of allowing the powerful to hide it later.

妮莎在任何人重排頁面之前編好頁碼，要求經手者在交接簿簽名並記下封印狀態。工作很慢，紀錄卻能指出不確定從何而來，不讓有權者日後藏起來。

Nessa refused to abbreviate the witness's village name, because the shorter form referred to another settlement. A loaded barge lowered its anchor while the guards argued over which treaty copy controlled the crossing. The emergency rule remained applicable to noble families as well as to farmers standing outside the gate. Maelin requested the public archive register instead of asking a private acquaintance to steal the sealed papers. Iva pressed the folded copy against her bosom as the royal guard approached the waiting villagers.

妮莎拒絕縮寫證人的村名，因為簡寫會指向另一個聚落。 載貨駁船放下船錨，衛兵仍在爭執該依哪份條約副本管理渡口。 緊急規則對貴族家庭與門外農民都同樣適用。 梅琳要求調閱公共檔案登記冊，而非請私人熟人偷取封存文件。 皇家衛兵靠近等候的村民時，伊娃把摺好的副本緊貼胸前。

Keir spread the map across the table and placed the witnesses' routes beside the official boundary. No individual memory could settle the matter, but matching dates and physical markers could expose errors. He refused to call the revised drawing final until people from every affected settlement had been invited to examine it.

凱爾在桌上攤開地圖，把證人的行走路線與官方邊界並列。單憑一個人的記憶不能定案，但相符的日期與實體界標可以揭露錯誤。他拒絕在每個受影響聚落都受邀檢查前稱新圖為定稿。

A confiscation inventory listed a bra and two coats belonging to a woman who had been detained. A merchant offered the driver a bribe to send the winter grain toward the palace warehouse. In the crowded kitchen, volunteers began to broil fish for families waiting through the hearing. Maelin made a comparative chart that placed the court wording beside the complete river-language sentence. Her eyelid trembled when she recognized the condemned interpreter's signature at the bottom of the summary.

扣押清單列有一名遭拘留女子的胸罩與兩件外套。 一名商人企圖賄賂車夫，把冬糧送往王宮倉庫。 擁擠的廚房裡，志工開始炙烤魚肉，供等候聽證的家庭食用。 梅琳製作比較表，將宮廷措辭與完整河語句子並列。 她看見摘要底部那位注定遭處決的翻譯官簽名時，眼皮微微顫抖。

The magistrate warned that a temporary order could not decide the whole dispute. Maelin agreed to the limit even when villagers begged for a quicker result. She would not promise that one hearing could restore every lost wage or broken relationship. Honest limits were painful, but they kept the next appeal possible.

地方法官警告，暫時命令不能決定整場爭端。即使村民希望更快，她仍接受限制。她不承諾一次聽證能找回所有失去的工資與破裂關係。誠實的界線令人難受，卻讓下一次申訴仍有可能。

The supply of preserved originals was finite, so each handling required a dated entry and witness. Nessa hoped public translation lessons could flourish after people stopped fearing the palace interpreters. A hired gangster tried to frighten the courier who had delivered an earlier version of the decree. Keir marked the southern junction where the grain carts had suddenly changed direction. The injured ferryman walked with a limp yet insisted on checking his own testimony before signing.

保留下來的原件數量有限，每次經手都必須留下日期與見證人。 妮莎希望人們不再害怕宮廷譯員後，公共翻譯課程能蓬勃發展。 一名受雇的黑幫分子企圖恐嚇送過早期命令版本的信差。 凱爾標記南方交叉路口，糧車正是在那裡突然改變方向。 受傷的渡船夫雖然跛行，仍堅持簽名前親自核對證詞。

As evening fell, the public board displayed what the investigators had confirmed and what they still needed to check. People stopped to read, sometimes pointing out missing names or dates. The record grew more complicated with each correction, and more trustworthy because those corrections could be seen.

傍晚，公告板列出調查員已確認及仍須查核的事項。人們停下閱讀，不時指出缺少的姓名或日期。紀錄因每次更正而更複雜，也因更正公開可見而更可信。

The judge asked Iva to narrate what she witnessed without speculating about the governor's motives. An orderly escorted the detained families to a separate room where their names could be checked. A bank teller preserved the receipt showing which office had paid the grain hauler in advance. The revised clause had been printed on virgin paper rather than inside the worn treaty book. The magistrate issued a limited warrant to preserve the versions, not to imprison everyone who had touched them.

法官請伊娃只敘述親眼所見，不猜測總督的動機。 一名勤務員帶遭拘留的家庭到另一間房核對姓名。 銀行出納保留了收據，顯示哪個辦公室預付糧車運費。 修改後的條款印在全新紙上，而非磨損的條約冊內。 法官核發有限調取令，只為保全版本，並非拘捕每個經手者。

The registrar Nessa agreed to seal the drafts, but reminded Maelin that she herself had approved a shortened public summary months earlier. Maelin did not deny it. Outside, carts carrying winter food waited for an order that nobody could translate with confidence. She requested a temporary delay, not an acquittal, and promised to identify who had seen each version before publication. The judge granted one day of preservation and rejected her request for unrestricted access to the palace. It was a small beginning, and the villages still faced hunger.

登記官妮莎同意封存草稿，卻提醒梅琳：幾個月前她本人曾核准縮寫的公開摘要。梅琳沒有否認。宮外載著冬糧的馬車正等待一項沒人能準確翻譯的命令。她申請的是暫緩執行而非無罪判決，並承諾查清每一版本公開前由誰經手。法官只准許一天的證據保全，拒絕她自由進出王宮。這只是微小的開始，村莊仍可能挨餓。
