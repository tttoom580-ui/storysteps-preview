越過楓樹隘口

Across the Maple Pass

我不替王冠翻譯謊言 · 第 9 / 25 章 · 5000–6000 · 英中對照 · 校訂中

用互動版查詞、朗讀與收藏

The Hill Council invited the investigators across Maple Pass to inspect its own treaty chest. Anika, the hill envoy, set one rule: the visitors could copy the documents but could not remove them without local consent. Maelin accepted. On the journey, Keir heard villagers describe a flood season when both sides had cooperated without claiming ownership of each other's roads. Oral memory was not identical to a signed treaty, yet it offered names and dates the official histories had neglected.

山地議會邀調查員越過楓樹隘口，檢查自己的條約箱。山地使者安妮卡提出一項規則：訪客可以抄錄，未經當地同意不得帶走原件。梅琳接受了。路上凱爾聽村民談起某次洪水時，兩方曾合作卻未宣稱擁有對方道路。口述記憶不等於簽署條約，但提供了官方歷史忽略的人名和日期。

A single disputed sentence had traveled farther than any messenger: into receipts, school lessons, bridge notices, and family decisions. Maelin could not undo all of those consequences by changing one page. She nevertheless had to begin with the page, because every later decision had borrowed its supposed authority from it.

一句有爭議的文字傳得比任何信差都遠，進入收據、課本、橋梁公告和家庭決定。梅琳不能只改一頁就消除全部後果，但她必須從那一頁開始，因為往後的決定都借用了它表面上的權威。

Maelin feared that careless accusations would alienate the Hill Council before the evidence could be examined. Nessa examined a blot of blue ink beside the disputed verb on the hill copy. The innkeeper added chili to the stew served after the long mountain journey. A witness list supplied continuity between the first treaty meeting and the later public reading. The cumulative weight of small dated records became harder for the palace to dismiss.

梅琳擔心草率指控會在查驗證據前疏遠山地議會。 妮莎檢查山地副本爭議動詞旁的一滴藍色墨漬。 旅店主人在長途山行後端上的燉菜裡加入辣椒。 證人名單使首次條約會議與後來公開宣讀之間的脈絡得以連續。 許多附日期的小紀錄累積起來的證據分量，使王宮更難忽視。

Nessa numbered the pages before anyone was permitted to rearrange them. She asked each handler to sign the transfer book and note the condition of the seal. It was slow work, but the record would show precisely where uncertainty entered instead of allowing the powerful to hide it later.

妮莎在任何人重排頁面之前編好頁碼，要求經手者在交接簿簽名並記下封印狀態。工作很慢，紀錄卻能指出不確定從何而來，不讓有權者日後藏起來。

Anika demanded a decisive answer about whether local interpreters had reviewed the printed summary. A witness's accent was not dispensable merely because the court clerk found it inconvenient. Jasmine grew near the inn window where the delegates reviewed their copied documents. The hill lodge offered both sides equal rooms and refused to charge the river guests more. A red maple leaf marked the treaty page the old translator had asked his daughter to remember.

安妮卡要求明確回答：地方譯員是否審查過印製摘要。 證人的口音不能只因法庭書記覺得麻煩就被當作可有可無。 代表審閱抄件的旅店窗邊長著茉莉花。 山區旅舍提供雙方同樣房間，拒絕向河民多收費。 一片紅楓葉標記著老翻譯員請女兒記住的條約頁面。

Keir spread the map across the table and placed the witnesses' routes beside the official boundary. No individual memory could settle the matter, but matching dates and physical markers could expose errors. He refused to call the revised drawing final until people from every affected settlement had been invited to examine it.

凱爾在桌上攤開地圖，把證人的行走路線與官方邊界並列。單憑一個人的記憶不能定案，但相符的日期與實體界標可以揭露錯誤。他拒絕在每個受影響聚落都受邀檢查前稱新圖為定稿。

The mistress of the house showed a dated guest book recording the translator's last visit. Local volunteers agreed to mobilize carts for a return journey before the mountain snow arrived. Maelin began to muse about how many borders had been changed by ordinary-looking words. Anika's persistence persuaded a reluctant clerk to release the original meeting attendance sheet. A socialist printer joined the public debate, arguing that agreements must be legible to working families.

女主人出示附日期的訪客簿，記錄老譯員最後一次來訪。 當地志工同意在山雪來臨前動員馬車協助返程。 梅琳開始沉思，究竟有多少邊界是被看似普通的字詞改變。 安妮卡的堅持讓猶豫的書記交出原始會議出席表。 一名社會主義印刷工加入公共辯論，認為協議必須讓勞動家庭讀得懂。

The magistrate warned that a temporary order could not decide the whole dispute. Maelin agreed to the limit even when villagers begged for a quicker result. She would not promise that one hearing could restore every lost wage or broken relationship. Honest limits were painful, but they kept the next appeal possible.

地方法官警告，暫時命令不能決定整場爭端。即使村民希望更快，她仍接受限制。她不承諾一次聽證能找回所有失去的工資與破裂關係。誠實的界線令人難受，卻讓下一次申訴仍有可能。

Delegates could socialize after the meeting, but they were forbidden to negotiate secret changes to the record. The mountain pass would span two valleys once the winter bridge was repaired. The final trek down the slope took longer because the team carried fragile copies in sealed boxes. A vigorous debate followed the discovery of the deceased translator's mark beside the clause. The waterproof wrapping had protected the hill copy through three seasons of heavy rain.

代表可在會後交際，卻禁止私下協商更改紀錄。 冬季橋修復後，山隘將連接兩座山谷。 下坡的最後一段跋涉較久，因為團隊以封箱搬運脆弱副本。 發現已故譯員在條款旁的記號後，現場展開激烈辯論。 防水包裝讓山地副本挺過三季豪雨。

The hill copy carried a tiny mark beside the disputed verb, identifying a translator who had died the previous spring. His daughter remembered that he always asked for the public reading to be recorded. No recording remained, but an attendance sheet confirmed a meeting had taken place. The team returned with copies and an expanded witness list. Vask accused them of seeking foreign influence. Maelin answered that foreign participation was not a defect in a treaty signed by three peoples.

山地副本在爭議動詞旁有微小記號，指向一名去年春天去世的翻譯員。他的女兒記得父親總要求留下公開宣讀紀錄。錄音沒有保存，出席表卻確認會議確曾舉行。團隊帶著副本和更完整的證人名單返回。瓦斯克指責他們尋求外國勢力。梅琳回答：三方簽署的條約，本來就需要三方參與。
