The Island Lease

我不替王冠翻譯謊言 · 第 12 / 25 章 · 5000–6000 · 英中對照 · 校訂中

The disputed treaty included a small island used for boat repairs and winter shelter. Its lease named the River Assembly as custodian, while the palace summary implied permanent royal possession. The island's mechanics described how a changed word had forced them to pay fees twice. Maelin and Keir crossed the strait to inspect the original lease, leaving the legal debate open while dangerous boats were repaired. Their task was to establish what was written, not who deserved to be celebrated.

爭議條約包括一座供修船與冬季避難的小島。租約指定河民議會為保管者，王宮摘要卻暗示王室永久持有。島上技師描述，一個字的改動使他們必須繳兩次費。梅琳和凱爾跨海檢查原租約，同時讓法律爭議保持開放，並先修好危險船隻。他們的工作是確認寫了什麼，不是決定誰值得受頌揚。

Anika spoke slowly, leaving space after every translated sentence. She would not allow speed to become another form of power. When a listener asked for a phrase to be repeated, she did so without complaint. The pause made the meeting longer and the eventual agreement much clearer.

安妮卡說得很慢，每句翻譯後都留出空間。她不讓速度成為另一種權力。有人要求重述句子時，她毫無怨言地照做。停頓讓會議較長，最後的理解卻清楚得多。

A narrow brook behind the island workshop supplied fresh water to the mechanics. Keir found coral fragments beneath a pier that the old treaty listed as shared shelter. The double fee could deprive boat owners of money needed for winter repairs. The harbor council voted to elevate safety inspection above the argument over ownership. The lease carried an expiration date that the palace summary had quietly omitted.

島上工坊後的一條小溪替技師提供淡水。 凱爾在一座舊條約列為共用避難所的碼頭下發現珊瑚碎片。 雙重收費可能剝奪船主冬季修船所需的資金。 港務議會投票決定把安全檢查置於所有權爭論之上。 租約有到期日期，王宮摘要卻悄悄省略了它。

The river families disagreed among themselves about what result would be fair. Some wanted immediate compensation; others wanted land records corrected first. Maelin asked them to choose representatives without palace interference. A single sympathetic translator, however well intentioned, could not replace their right to decide.

河岸家庭對公平結果也有分歧。有人要立刻補償，有人要先更正土地紀錄。梅琳請他們不受王宮干預自行選代表。再善良的翻譯官，也不能取代他們決定的權利。

The old boat builder's eyesight had weakened, so his apprentice read the dates aloud. A hearty meal was prepared for everyone stranded by the storm, including the visiting officials. A rescue bird seemed to hover above the harbor entrance before settling on the roof. The inland authorities had demanded fees for boats using an island landing they did not own. The witnesses' insistence on keeping the original lease prevented a second undocumented substitution.

老船匠視力衰退，因此由學徒朗讀日期。 為所有因暴風雨受困的人準備了豐盛餐點，包括外來官員。 一隻救援隊飼養的鳥似乎在港口入口上方盤旋，然後停在屋頂。 內陸當局曾向使用其無所有權島岸碼頭的船隻收費。 證人堅持保存原租約，防止第二次未記錄的替換。

The queen's seal frightened people because it seemed to end discussion. Maelin insisted that a seal proved an official act had occurred, not that every word beneath it was correct. If the act was mistaken, the record needed a visible correction rather than the quiet disappearance of the earlier version.

女王印璽使人害怕，彷彿它能結束所有討論。梅琳堅持，印章只能證明某次正式行為發生，不保證其下每個字都正確。若行為有誤，紀錄需要可見更正，而非讓舊版悄悄消失。

A short-term lease could not legally establish permanent ownership of the harbor. The legislator asked who had approved the new fee before any storm warning was issued. The majestic cliffs of the island looked unchanged despite the conflict written into the paperwork. Anika used the storm's momentum to persuade rival officers to open a shared shelter. The monarch had signed the original treaty but not the later pamphlet sent to the island.

短期租約依法不能建立港口的永久所有權。 議員詢問，誰在風暴警報發布前就核准新費用。 島上雄偉峭壁看來未變，紙上的爭議卻已延燒。 安妮卡利用風暴帶來的迫切感，說服對立官員開放共用避難所。 君主簽署過原始條約，卻未簽署送往島上的後來小冊子。

Nessa paused to ask who had not been invited into the room. The answer was rarely flattering: an absent ferryman, a younger clerk, a family speaking an unfamiliar dialect. The team changed its witness schedule rather than treating attendance as proof that every voice had already been heard.

妮莎停下來問，還有誰沒被邀進房間。答案通常不好聽：缺席的船夫、年輕書記、使用陌生方言的家庭。團隊調整證人時程，不再把有人出席當作所有聲音都已被聽見。

A boatman's offspring produced the family receipt that recorded the first legal landing fee. The ferry crew reserved petrol for medical trips instead of using it to carry ceremonial guests. The wind began to rotate the loose harbor sign until Keir tied it securely. Maelin warned that the false fee might strangle the island's small repair trade over time. The nurse carried a surgical kit to the storm shelter in case anyone was injured.

船夫的子女拿出家庭收據，記載最早的合法靠岸費。 渡船員預留汽油給醫療航次，而非運送典禮賓客。 風開始使鬆動的港口標誌旋轉，直到凱爾將它綁牢。 梅琳警告，不實收費長期可能扼殺島上小型修船業。 護士把外科急救工具帶到風暴避難所，以備有人受傷。

A storm warning arrived before the return crossing. The islanders offered shelter to both visiting officials and local workers, refusing to turn humanitarian help into a claim of allegiance. In a storage room, Nessa found a receipt noting that the archive chest had briefly passed through the island station. The receipt tied the chest to the hidden print shop's route. It narrowed the list of possible handlers without establishing which one altered the language.

返程前傳來暴風雨警報。島民為外來官員與本地工人提供避難，拒絕把人道協助變成效忠承諾。妮莎在儲藏室找到收據，顯示檔案箱曾短暫經過島上驛站。收據把箱子與祕密印刷坊的運輸路線連接起來。這縮小了可能經手者的名單，仍無法證明誰改動條文。
