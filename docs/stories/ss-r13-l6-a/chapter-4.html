證人的暫住屋

A House for Witnesses

我不替王冠翻譯謊言 · 第 4 / 25 章 · 5000–6000 · 英中對照 · 校訂中

The first witnesses refused to sleep in the palace barracks, where earlier statements had been corrected without their consent. Maelin arranged a neutral boardinghouse and asked a local caretaker to record every visitor. A messenger from Chancellor Vask protested that a translator had no authority to protect witnesses. Maelin answered by showing the preservation order and inviting a court officer to supervise. The arrangement was inconvenient, but the witnesses were finally free to leave without giving up their testimony.

第一批證人拒絕住進王宮營房，因為過去有人未經同意修改他們的證詞。梅琳安排中立的寄宿屋，請看守人記錄每一名訪客。瓦斯克宰相的使者抗議翻譯官無權保護證人。梅琳出示證據保全令，並邀法庭官員監督。安排雖不方便，證人終於能在不放棄證詞的情況下自由離開。

The river families disagreed among themselves about what result would be fair. Some wanted immediate compensation; others wanted land records corrected first. Maelin asked them to choose representatives without palace interference. A single sympathetic translator, however well intentioned, could not replace their right to decide.

河岸家庭對公平結果也有分歧。有人要立刻補償，有人要先更正土地紀錄。梅琳請他們不受王宮干預自行選代表。再善良的翻譯官，也不能取代他們決定的權利。

Workers used bleach to clean the boardinghouse corridor, but Nessa forbade cleaning the evidence cabinet. The judges demanded a comprehensive visitor list rather than a collection of rumors about the witnesses. The landlady appeared credible because her account matched the dated kitchen purchase book. Iva thanked the guards for their dedication to keeping witnesses safe without controlling what they said. A torn ribbon lay inside the dresser beside the room where the visiting scribe had slept.

工人用漂白劑清潔寄宿屋走廊，妮莎卻禁止清洗證物櫃。 法官要求完整訪客名單，而非收集關於證人的流言。 房東太太的說法與附日期的廚房採購簿相符，因此顯得可信。 伊娃感謝衛兵盡心保護證人安全，而不干涉證言內容。 那名訪客書記睡過的房間旁，梳妝檯內放著一段破緞帶。

The queen's seal frightened people because it seemed to end discussion. Maelin insisted that a seal proved an official act had occurred, not that every word beneath it was correct. If the act was mistaken, the record needed a visible correction rather than the quiet disappearance of the earlier version.

女王印璽使人害怕，彷彿它能結束所有討論。梅琳堅持，印章只能證明某次正式行為發生，不保證其下每個字都正確。若行為有誤，紀錄需要可見更正，而非讓舊版悄悄消失。

Maelin's familiarity with the novel made her suspicious, but she refused to present it as proof. The palace hierarchy placed interpreters below ministers, although their errors could affect entire villages. The landlady recorded the visitor's time of departure without inventing a reason for the late hour. A lush garden behind the house gave frightened children somewhere quiet to sit. The villagers still mourn the ferryman lost during last year's unsafe winter crossing.

梅琳熟悉原著而起疑，卻拒絕把這種熟悉當作證據。 王宮階層把譯員置於大臣之下，他們的錯誤卻可能影響整個村莊。 房東太太只記下訪客離開的時間，沒有猜造他深夜離去的理由。 屋後繁茂的花園讓受驚的孩子有安靜的地方可坐。 村民仍哀悼去年冬季危險渡口事故中失去的渡船夫。

Nessa paused to ask who had not been invited into the room. The answer was rarely flattering: an absent ferryman, a younger clerk, a family speaking an unfamiliar dialect. The team changed its witness schedule rather than treating attendance as proof that every voice had already been heard.

妮莎停下來問，還有誰沒被邀進房間。答案通常不好聽：缺席的船夫、年輕書記、使用陌生方言的家庭。團隊調整證人時程，不再把有人出席當作所有聲音都已被聽見。

The teacher hoped a fair treaty would nurture trust among children speaking different languages. After heavy rain, water began to overflow the stone basin outside the witness house. Maelin happened to overhear a clerk argue about an order to remove margin notes. She could refute the false date by showing a receipt signed two days earlier. A seagull circled above the harbor where the archive chest had been unloaded.

老師希望公平條約能培養使用不同語言的孩子間的信任。 大雨後，水開始漫出證人屋外的石盆。 梅琳偶然聽見一名書記爭論刪除頁邊註記的命令。 她能出示早兩天簽署的收據，反駁錯誤日期。 一隻海鷗在卸下檔案箱的港口上方盤旋。

Maelin divided the notebook into three spaces: the words preserved on paper, the memories people could verify, and the conclusions that remained uncertain. She knew how tempting it was to merge them into one elegant explanation. Yet the families at the border needed something sturdier than an elegant explanation.

梅琳把筆記分成三欄：紙上保存的文字、證人可以核實的記憶，以及仍不確定的結論。她知道把三者合成漂亮說法有多誘人；邊境家庭需要的卻是比漂亮說法更可靠的東西。

The school superintendent offered a neutral classroom for the next comparison meeting. A distant symphony drifted from the town hall while the witnesses checked their statements. The infirmary nurse placed a thermometer beside an ill courier and offered him rest before questioning. Public turmoil increased when officials announced that the border had already been decided. A wholesome supper helped the children settle before the adults returned to their difficult discussions.

學校督學提供一間中立教室供下次比對會議使用。 證人核對證詞時，遠處市政廳傳來交響樂聲。 醫務室護士把溫度計放在生病信差旁，讓他先休息再接受詢問。 官員宣稱邊界已經決定後，公共動盪加劇。 健康而營養的晚餐讓孩子安定下來，大人才繼續艱難討論。

At supper, a laundress described how a visiting scribe had asked for a particular kind of binding cord. Nessa recorded the account as a lead, not proof of guilt. The court's rules also required the witnesses to see their statements before signing. Maelin read each statement aloud twice, once literally and once in ordinary language, and asked what meaning the speaker intended. A frightened child corrected one word. Nobody scolded her; that correction would later change the case.

晚餐時，一名洗衣工說有位訪客書記曾要求特殊的裝訂線。妮莎把這記作線索，而非有罪證明。法庭規定證人簽名之前必須看過自己的證詞。梅琳逐份讀兩次，一次照字面，一次用白話，再詢問講者真正的意思。一名害怕的孩子更正了一個字。沒有人責備她；這項更正日後將改變整件案件。
