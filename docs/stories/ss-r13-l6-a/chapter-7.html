從未出售的土地

The Land That Was Not Sold

我不替王冠翻譯謊言 · 第 7 / 25 章 · 5000–6000 · 英中對照 · 校訂中

The land register showed that a meadow had been leased for grazing, not sold to the crown. Vask's office had used the word “transfer” in its public statement, although the old charter called the arrangement a seasonal permission. Keir photographed boundary stones, and the local farmers invited the team to check harvest records. Maelin knew the novel would have blamed the farmers for resisting an allegedly lawful order. She instead asked the tribunal to compare the legal verbs in all three languages.

土地登記冊顯示，草地是季節性放牧租地，並未賣給王室。瓦斯克辦公室在公告中使用「轉讓」，舊憲章卻稱之為季節性許可。凱爾拍下界石，農民邀團隊核對收成紀錄。梅琳知道原著會指控農民抗拒合法命令。她改請法庭比較三種語言的法律動詞。

Every version had a history: who held it, who copied it, what was left out, and why it reached one village before another. The team began to treat those histories as part of the evidence. A document could be authentic and still be misused by an official claiming more than it said.

每個版本都有歷史：誰保管、誰抄寫、刪掉什麼、為何先到某村。團隊開始把這些歷史視為證據的一部分。一份文件可能是真的，卻仍被官員利用來聲稱文件沒有授予的權力。

The survey covered an acre of wet meadow that the palace pamphlet called newly acquired crown land. A bachelor who had lived beside the meadow all his life showed Keir the oldest boundary stone. The village boxing club had stored its equipment in a barn now claimed by royal collectors. The council announced a disciplinary review for the officer who had ignored the suspension order. An emigrant returning home discovered that the revised map no longer showed her family's grazing route.

測量涵蓋一英畝潮濕草地，王宮小冊子稱之為新取得的王室土地。 一名終身住在草地旁的單身漢向凱爾指出最古老的界石。 村裡的拳擊社曾把器材存放在一間如今遭王室徵收員主張所有權的穀倉。 議會宣布對無視暫停令的官員進行紀律審查。 一名返鄉移民發現新版地圖不再顯示家族放牧路線。

At the shared meal, people who had argued over language passed bowls across the same table. Nobody pretended that kindness settled legal ownership. Still, the ordinary act of feeding a stranger made it harder to describe the neighboring villages as enemies. The discussion resumed with fewer threats and more questions.

共餐時，曾為語言爭執的人們在同一張桌上互相遞碗。沒有人假裝善意能決定法律所有權，但餵飽陌生人的平凡行動讓人更難把鄰村說成敵人。討論重新開始，威脅少了，問題多了。

Maelin refused to equate a temporary lease with permanent ownership simply because one English verb sounded convenient. The young witness began to falter when Vask's lawyer asked three questions at once. Farmers argued that the fertility of the floodplain depended on seasonal access to both banks. Iva would not forsake her community's claim merely to secure a private appointment with the governor. A gay teacher asked the committee to use the names families chose, not those assigned in outdated registers.

梅琳拒絕只因一個英語動詞方便，就把暫時租賃等同永久所有權。 瓦斯克律師一次提出三個問題時，年輕證人開始結巴猶豫。 農民指出，沖積平原的土壤肥力仰賴兩岸季節性通行。 伊娃不願只為得到總督私下接見就放棄社區權利主張。 一位男同志教師要求委員會使用家庭自己選的名字，而非過時登記所指定的名字。

Maelin could remember the cruel ending written for her character, but the living world kept refusing to follow it exactly. Each witness made choices the novel had ignored. She began to think less about escaping the execution scene and more about leaving a procedure that might work after she was gone.

梅琳記得角色殘酷的原定結局，但活生生的世界不斷拒絕照本演出。每名證人都做出了小說忽略的選擇。她漸漸少想如何逃過處決場面，多想如何留下自己離開後仍有效的程序。

Gloom spread through the room when the court announced that compensation would require another hearing. The old intersection of farm roads appeared on all three authenticated survey maps. A landslide delayed the inspection, so Keir refused to send inexperienced workers uphill. The local school proposed a food program to nourish children while grain ownership remained unresolved. The orthodox court scholars disliked the river dialect but admitted its grammar was legally relevant.

法庭宣布補償還需另開聽證時，陰鬱氣氛籠罩全場。 那處古老農路交叉口在三份經認證的測量圖上都存在。 山崩延誤檢查，凱爾因此拒絕派缺乏經驗的工人上山。 當糧食所有權仍未解決時，地方學校提出供餐計畫滋養孩童。 正統宮廷學者不喜歡河語方言，卻承認其文法具有法律相關性。

Keir asked whether the decision would still seem reasonable to someone who had not attended the hearing. Nessa read the order aloud without using the officials' titles as shortcuts. Where a reason depended on a document, they named that document. Where no document existed, they said so.

凱爾問，沒參加聽證的人是否仍能理解這項決定。妮莎朗讀命令，沒有以官銜代替解釋。凡理由依賴某份文件，他們就寫出文件名稱；沒有文件的地方，也如實註明。

A prohibition against night crossings remained in effect only where bridge damage had been independently confirmed. The provisional route notice would expire after seven days unless a new safety review justified renewal. The village council reached a unanimous decision to preserve the old boundary markers during investigation. The governor's vanity had led him to display a larger map than anyone could read closely. A woodpecker struck a hollow tree beside the stone that marked the meadow's western edge.

夜間禁渡只在橋梁損傷經獨立確認的地方繼續生效。 暫行路線公告七天後失效，除非新的安全審查證明需要延長。 村議會一致決定在調查期間保護舊界標。 總督的虛榮使他展示了一張大得無法近看細讀的地圖。 一隻啄木鳥敲擊草地西緣界石旁的空心樹。

The farmers agreed to suspend grazing for a week while a dangerous hillside was examined. They did not agree to surrender their title. The distinction mattered to families with no alternative land. A junior official confessed that he had copied a convenient formula because his supervisor demanded speed. The tribunal treated his admission as evidence of process failure, not automatic proof of a conspiracy. For Maelin, that restraint felt slower than the dramatic revenge promised by fiction, and far more useful.

農民同意危險山坡受檢的一週內暫停放牧，卻沒有同意放棄土地權利。對沒有其他田地的家庭來說，這個區別極重要。一名年輕官員承認，上司催促趕工，他便照抄方便的格式。法庭將供述視為流程失誤的證據，而非陰謀的直接證明。梅琳覺得這種克制比小說裡戲劇性的復仇慢得多，卻更有用。
