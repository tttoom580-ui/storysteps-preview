墓園信託

The Cemetery Trust

明燈基金：第三輛車的帳 · 第 9 / 25 章 · 4000–5000 · 英中對照 · 校訂中

The initials on the approval form belonged to a trustee named Alden Voss. He was also director of a memorial trust that maintained the city's oldest cemetery. Yu visited the trust with Samira, who came as a journalist rather than an ally in a private case. The trust's exterior was modest, but its lounge held a model galaxy under glass and a series of portraits of former city leaders. Alden welcomed them with a mint tea and a speech about citizenship. He said he had made a bid to support public health after an assault on an elderly neighbor shocked him. Yu did not doubt his concern. She wanted to know why he had approved private use of a public-service vehicle.

核准表上的縮寫屬於董事奧登．沃斯。他同時擔任一項維護城市古老墓園的紀念信託董事。林雨與莎米拉前往信託辦公室；莎米拉以記者身分同行，不是私人案件的盟友。建築外觀樸素，休息室卻有玻璃罩裡的銀河模型與一系列前市府領袖肖像。奧登用薄荷茶招待她們，談起公民身分。他說一名年長鄰居遭襲擊後，自己深受震撼，因而提出資助公共衛生的方案。林雨不懷疑他關心這件事，她要問的是，為何他會批准把公共服務車輛作私人用途。

Alden explained that the vehicle had been sent to a compulsory emergency exercise ordered by a premier regional official. He produced a manifest showing three vehicles assigned to the drill. But its final page listed the third bus under a company that did not appear in the fund's contracts. Samira asked why that entry was not reported in the annual statement. Alden grew impatient and tried to distract them with photographs from his expedition to the northern coast. Yu returned to the manifest. An unfamiliar company was not automatically evidence of wrongdoing, she said, but the difference required explanation. Alden agreed to let Tomas inspect the original under supervision.

奧登解釋，車輛被送往一項由區域首長下令的強制緊急演習。他出示清單，載明有三輛車參與。但清單最後一頁把第三輛車列在一家未出現在基金會合約裡的公司名下。莎米拉問為何年報沒有這筆紀錄。奧登不耐煩了，試圖用自己北方海岸遠征的照片轉移話題。林雨把問題拉回清單。她說陌生公司名稱並不自動表示不法，但差異必須解釋。奧登同意讓湯瑪斯在監督下查看原件。

Leaving the building, Yu and Samira walked past the rear gate of the cemetery. Groundskeepers were repairing a damaged wall, and one worker had to squat beside a broken drain to clear leaves. A strong stink rose from the water. Samira joked that the scene would never appear in a premier charity brochure. Yu asked her not to publish the joke beside a photograph of the worker without consent. "Fair," Samira said. "People who are doing necessary work shouldn't become decoration for our story." At a small market they shared tuna sandwiches and reviewed their notes. Their partnership was limited, but it had rules that each understood.

離開建築時，林雨與莎米拉經過墓園後門。園丁正修復損壞圍牆，一名工人蹲在壞掉的排水口旁清理樹葉，水裡飄出濃重臭味。莎米拉笑說，這種畫面永遠不會出現在高級慈善宣傳冊上。林雨請她別在未經同意時，把這玩笑與工人照片一起刊登。「有道理，」莎米拉說，「做必要工作的人不應淪為我們故事的裝飾。」她們在小市場吃鮪魚三明治，重新核對筆記。這份合作有界限，卻有雙方都理解的規則。

At noon a clerk hurried out of the trust with a folded envelope. A person on a scooter tried to snatch it, but the clerk stepped back and reached the security desk. The envelope contained a copy of a letter asking the trust to embrace a new joint procurement arrangement. Yu did not assume the attempted theft was connected to the bus. She asked the clerk to report it to the appropriate office and kept her own notes separate. The incident made her anxious, yet she knew that a frightening event could distort anyone's judgment. Samira offered to accompany the clerk until a colleague arrived. Yu called Dara and asked whether the fund's staff needed a safer method for carrying sensitive records.

中午，一名職員拿著摺好的信封匆忙走出信託辦公室。機車上的人想搶走它，職員退後一步躲到警衛櫃檯。信封裡是邀請信託加入新共同採購安排的信函影本。林雨沒有假定搶奪與避暑車有關。她請職員向主管單位報案，自己的筆記則分開保存。事件讓她緊張，但她知道恐怖的情景容易扭曲判斷。莎米拉主動陪職員等同事抵達。林雨打電話給達拉，詢問基金會是否需要更安全的敏感文件運送方式。

Tomas reported later that the manifest's company had been paid for training services by both the memorial trust and the Lantern Fund. One payment might have covered facilities while the other covered the vehicle; the contracts were not specific enough to tell. Yu added a proposed distinction to the audit: duplicate descriptions were not necessarily duplicate payments, but no one should accept them without cross-checking deliverables. Alden's records gave the team a useful route into the procurement chain. They also made the investigation more politically difficult. Rhea warned that a powerful trustee might withdraw all support. Yu replied that support which could survive only in silence was too fragile to carry the community's future. The register also showed a modest payment for a loading ramp, but the invoice named no installer. Yu marked it for later review, explaining to Samira that every unexplained line deserved the same ordinary test. Whether a payment concerned a powerful trustee or a small repair shop, the supporting evidence had to be comparable. Their investigation would fail if famous names received either harsher treatment or easier forgiveness simply because they were famous.

湯瑪斯稍後回報，清單中的公司分別向紀念信託和明燈基金會收取訓練服務費。一筆可能是場地費，另一筆可能是車輛費，但合約寫得不夠具體，無法確定。林雨在稽核中加入區別：重複描述不一定等於重複付款，卻也不能不交叉核對成果就接受。奧登的紀錄讓團隊有了追查採購鏈的路徑，也讓調查在政治上更加困難。瑞雅警告，有權勢的董事可能撤掉所有支持。林雨回答，只能靠沉默維持的支持太脆弱，承擔不起社區的未來。登記簿還列有一筆不大的裝卸坡道費，發票卻未列安裝者。林雨標記以便稍後核查，向莎米拉解釋每筆無法說明的項目都應接受同樣平常的檢驗。不論付款涉及有權勢的董事還是小修理店，支持證據都必須可比較。若有名望的人只因出名就受到更嚴苛對待或更容易被原諒，調查便失敗了。
