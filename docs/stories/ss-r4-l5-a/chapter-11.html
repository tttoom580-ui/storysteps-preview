避暑路線

The Cooling Route

明燈基金：第三輛車的帳 · 第 11 / 25 章 · 4000–5000 · 英中對照 · 校訂中

用互動版查詞、朗讀與收藏

The next heat wave forced the fund to accelerate its emergency plan before the final audit. Felix proposed that they deploy a smaller borrowed vehicle to cover the uncovered district. The battery was the weakest component, and the driver warned that a failure could leave passengers stranded. Rhea gave Felix a compliment for finding a practical option, but he refused to accept praise until the safety team signed off. Fatigue had made everyone eager for a simple answer. Yu insisted on a minimal test with the vehicle empty, followed by a supervised trip carrying only staff. Tomas suggested a neutral engineer from a local technical institution. The plan would cost money, but pretending that a free loan carried no risk could cost far more.

下一波熱浪迫使基金會在最終稽核前加快緊急計畫。費利克斯提議部署一輛借來的小型車，補上尚未服務的街區。電池是車上最弱的組件，司機警告故障可能讓乘客受困。瑞雅稱讚費利克斯找到務實選項，但他拒絕在安全團隊簽核前接受讚美。疲累讓所有人都渴望簡單答案。林雨堅持先做空車的最低限度測試，再由員工乘坐進行監督行程。湯瑪斯建議請本地技術機構的中立工程師協助。計畫得花錢，但假裝免費借車沒有風險，代價可能更高。

The test took place at a grim industrial lot behind the river. An engineer placed a temperature specimen inside the cabin and recorded its readings at five-minute intervals. A veteran driver checked the emergency exit while Felix watched the battery indicator quiver. On the second circuit the voltage fell below the permitted range. The engineer's recommendation was immediate: the vehicle could carry supplies but not vulnerable passengers until its battery underwent replacement. Felix looked defeated. Yu reminded him that a failed test was not a trivial waste. It had prevented an unsafe trip. He sighed and asked whether the fund could afford a battery. She said they first needed a quotation and a documented source of payment. Battery inspection was the engineer’s specialty, and any substitute vehicle would have to undergo the same independent safety test.

測試在河後方一處陰暗工業空地進行。工程師把溫度測試樣本放進車廂，每五分鐘記下讀數。資深司機檢查緊急出口，費利克斯則看著電池指示器顫動。第二圈時，電壓降到許可範圍以下。工程師立即提出建議：換電池前，車輛只能載物資，不能載脆弱乘客。費利克斯垂頭喪氣。林雨提醒他，失敗的測試不是無意義的浪費，而是阻止了一趟不安全的行程。他嘆氣問基金會是否買得起電池。她說得先拿報價，並確認有紀錄可循的付款來源。電池檢驗是這名工程師的專長，任何替代車輛都必須接受相同的獨立安全測試。

At the same time Mako carried a petition signed by residents who wanted an immediate cooling stop near the river. She did not demand that the borrowed vehicle be used. She demanded a safe service, with a date and a name attached to the promise. Yu felt the difference as Mako read the signatures aloud. In the realm of budgets, a delay appeared as a number in the next column. At a crowded bus stop it meant people waiting through another afternoon. Dara suggested turning the unused clinic classroom into a temporary refuge. Rhea agreed, provided the fire exits stayed clear. It would be less convenient than a bus but more dependable than a vehicle whose battery might fail.

同時，真子帶來居民連署書，希望河邊立即設置避暑站。她沒有要求一定使用借來的車，而是要求安全服務，並且承諾中要有日期和負責人。林雨聽真子念出署名，感受到兩者差別。在預算的世界裡，延誤只是下一欄的一個數字；在擁擠站牌下，它意味著人們再苦等一個下午。達拉提議把診所閒置教室改成臨時休息處。瑞雅同意，條件是消防出口必須暢通。它不如巴士方便，卻比可能沒電的車可靠。

Yu visited the classroom with a volunteer who had once worked in disability services. He measured doorways and explained why a narrow turn could keep a wheelchair from reaching the cooler corner. Felix carried chairs out and placed drinking water near the entrance. The clinic donated fans, and a neighborhood shop offered to repair a cracked window. None of these gifts solved the disputed contract. Yet together they showed how an institution might thrive without making its users depend on a single expensive device. Yu wrote a temporary spending order limited to accessibility improvements, electrical inspection, and drinking water. Dara countersigned because the recipients' needs had shaped the plan.

林雨與一位曾在身心障礙服務工作過的志工查看教室。志工測量門口，解釋狹窄轉角可能讓輪椅無法到達較涼的角落。費利克斯搬走椅子，把飲水放在入口附近。診所捐出風扇，附近商店願意修補破窗。這些捐贈沒有解決爭議合約，但它們顯示，機構可以不讓使用者全靠一部昂貴設備，也能成長。林雨簽發臨時支出命令，只限無障礙改善、電力檢查與飲水。達拉也簽名，因為這項計畫是由受助者需求形塑的。

That evening the vendor finally supplied the missing annex to the contract. Its first paragraph authorized temporary training use; its second required the company to offset any rental fees against the final purchase price. The fund's accounting file showed no such offset. Tomas did not declare the amount stolen. He calculated the potential credit and requested a reconciliation. Yu saw the practical stake: if the credit was real, it might pay for a safe replacement battery. Rhea asked if they could use it immediately. Yu shook her head. "We cannot spend an amount that exists only in an allegation," she said. She promised to report the claim and seek the vendor's written response.

傍晚供應商終於補交合約附件。第一段允許暫時訓練用途，第二段要求公司把租金從最終購價扣抵。但基金會帳務裡沒有這筆扣抵。湯瑪斯沒有宣布有人偷錢，只計算可能的應退金額，要求對帳。林雨看見實際利害：如果這筆抵扣確實存在，也許就能支付安全替代車的電池。瑞雅問能否立刻使用。林雨搖頭。「我們不能花一筆目前只存在於指控裡的錢，」她說。她承諾列出這項主張，並要求供應商書面答覆。

Before going home, Felix brought Yu a photograph of the borrowed van with a hand-painted sign reading "Temporary, Tested, Not Yet for Passengers." He had chosen honesty over a more flattering advertisement. Yu laughed for the first time that week. Their different methods were beginning to reinforce each other: he knew what broke in the field, and she knew how a missing line in a ledger could make that break invisible. The classroom refuge opened the next morning with a clear capacity limit. A resident waiting near the door told Yu that an accurate sign was kinder than a beautiful promise that could not be kept.

回家前，費利克斯拿來借車的照片，車上手繪標示寫著：「臨時使用、已測試、尚不可載客。」他選擇誠實，而不是更好看的廣告。林雨那週第一次笑出來。兩人的不同方法開始互相補強：他知道現場什麼會壞，她知道帳冊少一行怎會讓故障變得看不見。教室避暑所隔天早上開放，標明容量上限。門邊排隊的一名居民告訴林雨，準確的告示比無法履行的美麗承諾更體貼。
