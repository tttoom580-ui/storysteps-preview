星期五的食物隊伍

Friday at the Food Line

明燈基金：第三輛車的帳 · 第 4 / 25 章 · 4000–5000 · 英中對照

用互動版查詞、朗讀與收藏

Friday's distribution line curved from the clinic driveway into a public square. Volunteers were too busy for boredom, but their exhaustion carried the weight of despair. A civic association had sent a freight truck of drinking water and blankets under an emergency guideline, though the city had not yet decided which housing blocks qualified for support. Dara spoke with an eloquent retired teacher who wanted every family counted. Behind him, a child showed Yu a tin box of carefully saved bus tickets. The tickets proved that the family had taken three long journeys to find an open cooling stop. Yu recorded the dates, not the child's name, and asked whether the teacher would witness the summary.

星期五，物資領取隊伍從診所車道一路彎到公共廣場。志工忙得不會無聊，疲憊中卻帶著絕望的重量。一個公民團體依緊急指引送來一車飲水和毯子，但市府還沒決定哪些住宅區符合補助資格。達拉和一位口才出眾的退休教師交談，他希望每個家庭都被計入。一名孩子向林雨展示自己小心保存公車票的鐵盒。票根顯示，一家人為了找到開放的避暑站，長途奔波了三次。林雨只記日期，不記孩子姓名，並請教師見證摘要。

Near the tables, an innovative volunteer team tested a misting tent that could lower temperatures without the missing bus. Their idea was promising but could intensify heat stress if the water supply stopped halfway through operation. A nurse needed medication kept at a reliable temperature and insisted on a separate cooler. Yu understood that moral arguments became practical choices here: where to spend scarce money, whose needs received priority, and how to prolong a safe service without creating a new hazard. The nurse noticed a smell near the storage tank. A boy began to vomit. The team closed that tap immediately and offered sealed water while samples were collected. No one called the episode a minor inconvenience.

桌子附近，一組創新的志工團隊測試霧化帳篷，希望在沒有第三輛車的情況下降低溫度。構想很有希望，但如果供水中途停止，可能反而加劇熱壓力。一名護理師需要把藥物保存在穩定溫度，堅持另外準備冷藏箱。林雨明白，道德討論在這裡變成實際選擇：有限的錢要花在哪裡、誰的需求應優先、如何延長安全服務而不製造新危險。護理師發現儲水桶旁有異味，一名男孩開始嘔吐。團隊立刻關閉那個水龍頭，採樣時改發密封飲水。沒有人把它當成小小的不便。

Felix was in the van, staring through a windshield clouded by dust. A noticeable bruise colored his wrist after a box had slipped, and Dara begged him to take a break. He shook his head. "If we stop, the queue grows." Yu replied that exhaustion would only create more mistakes. She described the morality of stopping a service briefly to make it safe again. He was not persuaded until the teacher stepped forward and offered to supervise a rotation of volunteers. Families might plead for an extra bottle, but none wanted a driver too tired to see the road. Felix finally agreed to rest for twenty minutes. The line moved more slowly and, to everyone's surprise, more calmly.

費利克斯坐在貨車裡，透過滿是灰塵的擋風玻璃望向外頭。箱子滑落時砸傷他的手腕，留下明顯瘀青；達拉懇求他休息。他搖頭說：「我們一停，隊伍就更長。」林雨回答，過度疲累只會製造更多錯誤。她解釋，為了讓服務重新安全，暫停片刻也是一種責任。他仍不信服，直到退休教師上前提出協助監督志工輪班。家屬可能懇求多一瓶水，卻沒有人希望司機累得看不清路。費利克斯終於答應休息二十分鐘。隊伍前進得更慢，卻出乎意料地更平靜。

That afternoon a representative from the city said the fund should advertise its new tent as proof that the original plan had succeeded. Yu refused. "A substitute is a substitute," she said. Rhea watched the exchange and did not interrupt. For once the director saw the difference between celebrating volunteers and changing the story of a failed delivery. The civic association offered to pay for the misting system's independent safety check. It would not cover the third bus, but it could make the next week less dangerous. Yu asked for the donation to be restricted to that specific check. A small, clear agreement seemed preferable to a generous promise whose boundaries no one could explain.

下午，一名市府代表提議把新帳篷宣傳成原計畫成功的證明。林雨拒絕。「替代措施就是替代措施，」她說。瑞雅在旁看著，沒有插話。這一次主任終於看出，讚揚志工和改寫交車失敗的事實並不相同。公民團體提出支付霧化系統的獨立安全檢測費。這不能填補第三輛車的缺口，卻能讓下一週更安全。林雨請對方把捐款限定於這項檢測。比起界線不明的大方承諾，她更願意接受範圍狹小但清楚的協議。

As the sun went down, Yu and Dara collected the remaining tokens. A volunteer asked why a report about a vehicle should include the child's tickets and the nurse's spoiled water. Yu explained that the numbers meant something only beside the services they were supposed to buy. Without the families, an audit could become a contest between accountants. Without the invoices, their suffering could become a story that powerful people praised and then forgot. Dara wrote the explanation in plain language for the neighborhood noticeboard. Their earlier argument had not vanished, but they had found a way to work without demanding agreement about everything. The retired teacher said curiosity was a valuable trait in volunteers: good questions could stimulate safer work, even when everyone wanted to rush home.

太陽下山時，林雨與達拉收回剩餘的領物號碼牌。一名志工問，為何車輛報告要包含孩子的票根和護理師發現的問題飲水。林雨解釋，只有把數字放回原本應購買的服務中，帳目才有意義。少了家庭，稽核就可能淪為會計師的比賽；少了發票，民眾的痛苦則可能變成權貴稱讚後就忘掉的故事。達拉把這段話改寫成淺白文字貼在社區公告板上。她們先前的爭論沒有消失，卻找到不必事事同意也能合作的方法。退休教師說，好奇心是志工珍貴的特質：好的問題能促進更安全的工作，即使每個人都急著回家。

Before leaving, Yu received an electronic freight receipt for three cooling buses. The shipping company had marked the third delivery "returned to origin," but the fund's accounting system had translated that status as "received." An ordinary software rule might explain the contradiction; a deliberate change might explain it too. She printed both descriptions, highlighted the source fields, and sent the evidence to Tomas. The day had been long and painful, but the new record made one thing clear: the question was no longer simply where the third bus had gone. It was also why a return had been counted as a successful delivery, and who had accepted the report without asking.

離開前，林雨收到一份三輛避暑車的電子貨運收據。運輸公司將第三輛標示為「退回原處」，基金會的會計系統卻把該狀態轉成「已收貨」。普通的軟體規則或許能解釋，也可能是有人刻意修改。她列印兩種描述，標出來源欄位，將證據寄給湯瑪斯。這一天漫長又痛苦，但新紀錄讓事情更清楚：問題不再只是第三輛車去了哪裡，也包括為何退貨會被算成成功交付，以及誰在未追問下接受了報告。
