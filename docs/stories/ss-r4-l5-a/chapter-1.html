第三輛車

The Third Bus

明燈基金：第三輛車的帳 · 第 1 / 25 章 · 4000–5000 · 英中對照

用互動版查詞、朗讀與收藏

On the hottest morning of the year, Lin Yu counted a batch of receipts beside the Lantern Fund's window. The nonprofit had promised three mobile cooling buses to neighborhoods without reliable electricity. Only two buses stood outside. A loose circuit in the office fan clicked above her head, and she could hardly call the air decent. Her mentor, retired accountant Mrs. Ho, had taught her never to confuse a troubling number with a breakthrough. Yu opened an inquiry anyway. The third vehicle's payment had been recorded as complete, though the delivery schedule described its placement as pending. She copied the page and wrote down the time, resisting the easy pleasure of announcing a scandal before she understood the records.

一年中最炎熱的早晨，林雨在明燈基金會的窗邊清點一批收據。這個非營利組織承諾，向供電不穩的街區提供三輛流動避暑車。然而窗外只有兩輛。辦公室風扇的電路鬆脫，在她頭頂發出喀喀聲，室內空氣實在稱不上舒適。退休會計師何女士是她的導師，曾教她不要把一個令人不安的數字誤認為重大突破。林雨仍決定展開調查。第三輛車的款項記為已全數支付，交車時程卻註明尚待安置。她影印那頁並記下時間，克制住尚未讀懂紀錄就宣布醜聞的衝動。

Program coordinator Felix called from the parking area. The municipal heat-alert regime required every shelter to report capacity by noon, and he needed to implement an emergency route. He had tied a spare water container to his bicycle saddle because the buses were already full. A worker pointed to a crack along the rim of a drinking fountain; another asked whether their budget projection could cover a repair. Yu could feel the strain spreading through the staff. Regardless of the missing vehicle, people needed cool water today. She authorized only the repair covered by an existing maintenance line and left the disputed payment untouched. It was a modest decision, but it kept two different problems from dissolving into one accusation.

計畫協調員費利克斯從停車區打來電話。市府的高溫警戒制度要求各避暑站在中午前回報容量，他急著執行緊急行車路線。他把備用水桶綁在腳踏車坐墊後，因為兩輛車已經載滿。有人指著飲水台邊緣的裂縫，另一人詢問預算預估是否足夠修理。林雨感受到壓力正傳遍團隊。不論第三輛車是否失蹤，人們今天都需要涼水。她只批准既有維修項目可支付的費用，沒有碰有爭議的款項。這是個不起眼的決定，卻讓兩個不同的問題不至於混成一項指控。

When caseworker Dara arrived, she was carrying the attendance sheets of families who had waited in the sun. Her young nephew had drawn three blue buses on the cover, and the empty space where the third should have parked seemed suddenly enormous. Dara asked if the board intended to dissolve the project. "Don't terrify people with guesses," Yu said, more sharply than she meant. Dara's expression cooled. "I'm asking because I must answer them, not because I enjoy rumors." Yu apologized. They agreed to reinforce the public service with a temporary shaded stop while searching for the vehicle. Yu wrote a second note: fear made a poor substitute for information, even when it came from someone trying to help.

個案工作者達拉抵達時，帶來等在烈日下的家庭簽到表。她的小姪子在封面畫了三輛藍色巴士，第三輛本該停靠的空位突然顯得特別巨大。達拉問董事會是否打算解散計畫。「別用猜測嚇壞大家，」林雨回答得比預期尖銳。達拉的表情冷了下來。「我問，是因為我得回答他們，不是因為我喜歡傳言。」林雨道歉。兩人同意先加設臨時遮蔭站，維持公共服務，同時尋找車輛。林雨又記下一句話：恐懼無法取代資訊，即使是出於善意也一樣。

At lunchtime the fund's director, Rhea Vale, came down from the boardroom with a prepared statement. It said the organization might triple its reach before winter. Yu asked which document supported that forecast. Rhea tapped a chart and insisted the vendor had confirmed delivery. The chart, however, was a projection drafted before any contract amendment. Yu requested the delivery receipt rather than calling her director an idiot. Rhea looked offended, but promised to obtain the document. Outside, Felix had persuaded neighbors to share their shaded storefronts with elderly visitors. The help was real. So was the gap in the accounts. Yu decided that neither fact should be used to erase the other.

午餐時，基金會主任瑞雅帶著預先寫好的聲明走下董事會會議室。聲明宣稱，組織在冬季前服務範圍可能擴大三倍。林雨問那項預測由哪份文件支持。瑞雅指著圖表，堅持供應商已確認交車。然而圖表只是修約前編製的預估。林雨要求看交貨收據，沒有出言辱罵主任。瑞雅看似不悅，但答應取得文件。外頭，費利克斯說服鄰居開放有遮蔭的店面，讓長者休息。這份協助是真實的，帳目缺口也是真實的。林雨決定不讓其中一件事抹掉另一件事。

Mrs. Ho visited just before closing and listened without interrupting. She pointed out that the vehicle might have been rented under another name, or that a canceled invoice might have survived in the database. "Those explanations deserve testing," she said, "not belief." She showed Yu how to request a bank reference and a delivery confirmation from separate custodians. A trustworthy finding, she added, should survive being read by someone who hoped it was false. Yu placed the original ledger back in its locked drawer. Instead of taking photographs of private family records, she listed document identifiers that an independent reviewer could check. The office was quieter now, but the silence did not feel like relief.

打烊前，何女士前來探望，安靜聽完，沒有打斷。她指出車輛可能以其他名稱租用，也可能是已取消的發票仍留在資料庫。「這些解釋值得驗證，」她說，「但不值得盲信。」她教林雨向不同保管人分別索取銀行交易編號和交貨確認，並提醒她，可信的結論應該經得起希望它是錯誤的人查驗。林雨把原始帳冊放回上鎖抽屜。她不拍攝個案家庭的私人資料，只列出獨立審查者可核對的文件編號。辦公室終於安靜下來，但那份沉默不像解脫。

At dusk, the third bus appeared in a security photograph—parked outside a warehouse across the harbor three days earlier. The picture carried a date, not proof of delivery. Yu noticed a service label that differed from the number in the payment file. Dara wanted the photograph sent to every volunteer. Felix argued that a public allegation might endanger tomorrow's water supplies. Both had reasons. Yu chose to send a narrow preservation request to the warehouse manager first, with the photograph's source and date attached. She would tell the volunteers what could be verified at the next morning's briefing. For the first time, the empty parking space outside looked less like a mystery than a question that could be answered.

黃昏時，一張監視照片出現了第三輛車——三天前，它停在港口對岸的倉庫外。照片有日期，卻不能證明交車。林雨注意到車上的服務標籤與付款檔案中的編號不同。達拉希望把照片發給所有志工；費利克斯擔心公開指控會危及隔天的供水。雙方各有理由。林雨決定先向倉庫管理員送出範圍明確的資料保全要求，附上照片來源與日期，隔天早會再向志工說明已確認的事實。她第一次覺得，窗外那個空車位不是不可解的謎，而是一個有機會得到答案的問題。
