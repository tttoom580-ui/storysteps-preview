發票背後的名字

A Name Behind the Invoice

明燈基金：第三輛車的帳 · 第 6 / 25 章 · 4000–5000 · 英中對照 · 校訂中

用互動版查詞、朗讀與收藏

The audit's first week began with an abrupt visit from a neighborhood activist named Omar. He carried a briefcase packed with copies of earlier water-delivery complaints. He was angry that the fund had described his district as a distant frontier instead of part of the same city. Yu invited him to sit, but asked him not to place documents containing family medical details on the public counter. Omar called the request hostile. Dara stepped between them and explained the obligation to protect recipients even when the evidence was important. Omar agreed to keep the originals. He had once lived in exile after reporting workplace abuse, he said, and secrecy made him nervous. Yu thanked him for explaining rather than asking him to forget that history.

稽核第一週開始時，社區倡議者奧瑪突然到訪，手提公事包裡塞滿舊供水申訴的影本。他不滿基金會把他的街區稱為遙遠的邊緣地帶，彷彿那裡不屬於同一座城市。林雨請他坐下，但要求他別把含有家庭醫療資料的文件放在公共櫃檯。奧瑪認為這種要求充滿敵意。達拉上前解釋，即使證據重要，基金會仍有保護受助者的義務。奧瑪答應保留原件。他說自己過去揭露職場虐待後被迫流亡，因此對祕密特別敏感。林雨感謝他說明，而不是要他忘記那段歷史。

Omar had attended a seminar on public procurement and learned to check the legal identity of each entity receiving money. He showed Yu two names printed below the warehouse address. One belonged to the bus vendor; the other to a transport outlet created only six months earlier. Yu had been naive to assume that a similar address proved a shared owner. She asked Tomas to supervise a registry search instead of publishing a theory. The records revealed one director in common but no direct proof that funds had moved between the two firms. Tomas told Yu to contemplate both honest subcontracting and false billing. The possibilities were uncomfortable, but an audit should not choose its conclusion before the documents arrived.

奧瑪參加過公共採購研討會，學會查核每一個收款實體的法定身分。他讓林雨看倉庫地址下方印出的兩個名稱：一個屬於避暑車供應商，另一個屬於六個月前才成立的運輸營業點。林雨知道，光靠相同地址就推定共同所有人太天真。她請湯瑪斯監督登記查詢，沒有立即發表理論。紀錄顯示兩家公司有一名共同董事，卻不能直接證明資金在它們之間流動。湯瑪斯提醒她，同時考慮合法分包與虛假請款。這些可能性讓人不舒服，但稽核不能在文件到齊前先選定結論。

At the community clinic a nurse gave a scheduled injection to an elderly patient while Felix waited with a portable fan. The patient complained of pain in his thigh, then asked whether the buses would return. Felix promised to keep one route running; he could not promise the third vehicle. A pedestrian outside tripped on a loose cable, and the service team had to close the narrow entrance. Yu watched the interruption disrupt the day's carefully arranged schedule. The smallest piece of neglected infrastructure could threaten a large promise. She asked the clinic to submit an itemized safety request, separate from the missing-bus account, and stayed to help move chairs. The work was ordinary and necessary.

社區診所裡，護理師替一名長者施打預定注射，費利克斯在旁拿著手提風扇。病人說大腿疼痛，接著問避暑車是否還會來。費利克斯承諾保留一條路線，但無法保證第三輛車。一名行人在門外被鬆脫的電線絆倒，團隊不得不封閉狹窄入口。林雨看見這場意外打亂精心安排的行程。再小的公共設備疏失，都能威脅很大的承諾。她請診所另行提交安全費用明細，不要混入失蹤車輛帳目，自己留下來幫忙搬椅子。這是平凡卻必要的工作。

In the afternoon Rhea received an email from the donor's representative. The message asked the fund to repay an unrelated advance before the audit ended. Its tone was courteous, but the deadline would consume nearly all the cash surplus reserved for emergency water. Rhea argued that refusing might damage the relationship beyond repair. Yu asked to see the contract and found a clause allowing repayment over six months. "Why does the message demand six days?" she asked. Rhea could not explain. They agreed that the board, rather than a single frightened employee, should decide whether to accelerate payment. An organization might owe money without owing obedience to every demand made in a confident voice.

下午，瑞雅收到捐助者代表的電子郵件，要求基金會在稽核結束前償還一筆無關的預付款。語氣很客氣，但期限會耗盡幾乎所有為緊急供水保留的現金結餘。瑞雅擔心拒絕會破壞關係。林雨要求看合約，發現條款允許分六個月償還。「為何郵件卻要求六天？」她問。瑞雅無法解釋。兩人同意由董事會決定是否提前付款，而不是讓一名驚慌的員工獨自決定。組織可能欠錢，卻不代表必須服從每一個以自信語氣提出的要求。

Omar returned that evening with a more useful lead: the third bus's chassis number had appeared on a training invoice. He admitted that he had nearly posted a photograph of the invoice online. Dara pointed out that the picture also contained a patient number. They removed the sensitive field together and logged the unedited original under restricted access. Omar had been taught that masculine courage meant never showing doubt; the careful process made him feel, to his surprise, stronger rather than weaker. Yu thanked him for trusting the group with the source. Their first shared success did not establish guilt. It established a way to preserve evidence without making a vulnerable person pay for it.

傍晚奧瑪帶來更有用的線索：第三輛車的底盤號碼出現在一張訓練費發票上。他坦承自己差點把發票照片放上網。達拉指出照片還包含病患編號。大家一起遮去敏感欄位，並把未修改的原件登記為限制存取。奧瑪從小被教導，男子氣概就是永不懷疑；令他意外的是，這套謹慎程序讓他感覺更強大，而非更軟弱。林雨感謝他信任團隊保管來源。第一次合作沒有證明誰有罪，卻建立了一套保存證據、不讓弱勢者付出代價的方法。

Before locking the office, Yu added the new registry record to a timeline that everyone on the audit team could inspect. Tomas offered to check the transport outlet's statements independently. Felix asked if there was a practical task for him besides waiting. Yu invited him to compare the disputed bus number with photographs from the original drivers' training day. He accepted immediately. Rhea, who had heard the exchange, offered to locate the board's contract approvals. The team had not become harmonious; they still disagreed about publicity and risk. But each person now had a job that could answer a precise question. Yu took that as progress enough for one difficult day.

鎖門前，林雨把新查到的公司登記放進稽核團隊都能查看的時間線。湯瑪斯提出獨立核對運輸營業點的申報資料。費利克斯問，除了等待自己還能做什麼。林雨請他把爭議車輛編號與最初的司機訓練照片比對。他立刻答應。聽見對話的瑞雅也提出尋找董事會的合約核准紀錄。團隊仍不和諧，大家對公開與風險的看法依然不同。但每個人如今都有一項能回答具體問題的工作。林雨覺得，對這艱難的一天來說已足夠。
