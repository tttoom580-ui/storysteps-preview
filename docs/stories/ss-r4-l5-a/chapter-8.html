The Workshop Key

明燈基金：第三輛車的帳 · 第 8 / 25 章 · 4000–5000 · 英中對照 · 校訂中

用互動版查詞、朗讀與收藏

The depot's repair workshop smelled of metal dust and old rain. Yu entered with caution, accompanied by Tomas and a community delegation. Their first chore was to establish clarity about ownership: a green sticker marked the vehicle as private equipment, while a blue registration card named the Lantern Fund. The manager explained that the sticker referred to a temporary training venue, not a sale. Yu asked whether the bus had been treated as a commodity that could be loaned without permission. He bristled at the word. Tomas intervened, reminding everyone that the question was ethical as well as financial: who had the authority to expose donated equipment to further wear, and who would bear the risk?

倉庫維修工坊有金屬粉塵與舊雨水的氣味。林雨謹慎入內，同行的是湯瑪斯與社區代表團。他們第一項工作，是釐清所有權：綠色貼紙把車標作私人設備，藍色登記卡卻寫著明燈基金會。管理員解釋，綠貼紙指的是暫時的訓練場地，不代表出售。林雨問，車輛是否被當成可任意借出的商品，不需取得許可。管理員對這個字眼很不滿。湯瑪斯介入提醒，這不僅是財務問題，也涉及倫理：誰有權讓捐助設備承受額外耗損，風險又由誰承擔？

In the workshop's interior they discovered a replacement water filter marked with a patent number. The supplier had charged a premium for this particular part, claiming it alone could prevent contamination. Felix had warned that the claim might be superstition disguised as technology. The actual test record showed that two ordinary filters performed just as well under the same conditions. Yu did not call the patented part fraudulent; she asked why the specification had excluded less expensive alternatives. The manager admitted the design had been copied from an earlier contract without new testing. That explanation might account for excessive costs but not for an absent delivery. Each answer seemed to uncover another document the team needed.

工坊內部，他們發現一個替換濾芯，上頭標了專利號碼。供應商宣稱只有這款濾芯能防污染，因此收取較高價格。費利克斯曾提醒，那可能只是披著技術外衣的迷信。實際測試紀錄顯示，在相同條件下，兩款普通濾芯表現同樣好。林雨沒有說專利零件是假貨，而是問規格為何排除便宜的替代品。管理員承認設計沿用舊合約，沒有重新測試。這或許能解釋成本過高，卻不能解釋未交車。每個答案似乎又揭開團隊需要的另一份文件。

A local undergraduate named Sera had been employed to generate digital copies of maintenance books. She spoke with remarkable fluency about file formats but grew nervous when asked about the missing logbook. "I was told to shed old paper after scanning," she said. "I didn't know it was the sole original." Yu assured her that a student should not carry an institution's entire responsibility. Instead, Sera described the scanning workflow and the supervisor who approved disposal. The team dedicated the afternoon to recovering the archive from an old server. Sera volunteered to help, provided her university mentor could attend as an independent observer. Yu agreed immediately.

本地大學生瑟拉受雇製作維修簿的數位副本。她流暢解釋檔案格式，談到失蹤日誌卻開始緊張。「有人叫我掃描後丟掉舊紙，」她說，「我不知道那是唯一原件。」林雨向她保證，一名學生不應承擔整個機構的責任。瑟拉於是描述掃描流程及核准銷毀的主管。團隊把下午投入從舊伺服器復原檔案。瑟拉自願協助，條件是她的大學導師能以獨立觀察者身分在場。林雨立刻答應。

By evening the files were recovered, but their order differed from the paper ledger's index. Tomas refused to steer the evidence toward his favorite explanation. He noted every missing page and asked the team to repeat the search with a second tool. Yu respected the restraint. Investigation could become an exploration of one's own expectations as easily as of another person's actions. The manager, exhausted by questions, brought them tea and asked whether the fund would sue him. Yu said she had no authority to make that decision. "Our task today is to document what exists," she told him, "including things that may support your account." For the first time his hands stopped shaking.

傍晚時檔案復原了，順序卻和紙本帳簿索引不同。湯瑪斯拒絕把證據引向自己偏好的解釋。他列出每個缺頁，請團隊用第二套工具重做搜尋。林雨尊重這份克制。調查很容易從探索他人的行為，變成探索自己想相信的事。管理員被問題問得筋疲力盡，端茶來問基金會會不會告他。林雨說她沒有權限作決定。「我們今天的工作是記下存在的東西，」她告訴他，「也包括可能支持你說法的資料。」他的手第一次停止顫抖。

The recovered index revealed that the third bus had been assigned to a disaster-drill contract on the date of its alleged delivery. An attached approval form carried the initials of a fund trustee, not a depot employee. The team photographed the file in place and wrote a joint record before touching the keyboard again. Sera looked relieved that the process was slow and visible. Yu left with a more precise question for the board: why had an asset bought for heat shelters been used in a private exercise before any shelter received it? The workshop manager had given them a useful lead. He had not given them permission to call it a verdict.

復原的索引顯示，第三輛車在宣稱交付當天被分配到一份防災演習合約。附帶的核准表上有一名基金會董事的縮寫簽名，而不是倉庫員工。團隊在原位拍攝檔案，先共同記錄才再次碰鍵盤。瑟拉似乎因程序緩慢且公開而鬆了一口氣。林雨帶著更明確的問題離開：為避暑站買的資產，為什麼在任何避暑站收到它之前就被用於私人演習？管理員提供了有用線索，但沒有給他們把線索當判決的權利。

On the tram home Mako asked Yu why she had spoken so gently to a manager who had allowed the confusion to persist. Yu said respect was not the same as agreement. If she could ask a witness a question without humiliating him, he might answer more accurately, and the public would be better served. Mako was not fully persuaded. She reminded Yu that residents had waited weeks and were entitled to more than polite meetings. Yu accepted the criticism. Their different responsibilities would continue to pull them in different directions. But both women agreed that the recovered approval form should be disclosed to the independent reviewer the following morning. It would be dreadful to treat a missing document as proof that a nervous student had destroyed it on purpose.

搭電車回家時，真子問林雨，為何對放任混亂持續的管理員如此溫和。林雨說，尊重不代表同意。如果提問時不羞辱證人，他或許會回答得更準確，公眾也會得到更好的結果。真子並未完全被說服。她提醒林雨，居民已等了好幾週，應得到的不只是一場場有禮貌的會議。林雨接受批評。她們不同的責任仍會把彼此拉往不同方向，但兩人都同意，隔天一早就應把復原的核准表交給獨立審查者。把失蹤文件當成緊張學生故意銷毀的證明，將是很糟糕的做法。
