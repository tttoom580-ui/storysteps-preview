The independent review held its first formal evidence session beneath the library's glass dome. A bronze clock stood behind the witnesses' table, and Yu had to remind herself that ceremonial surroundings did not add credibility to testimony. Rhea opened the session by admitting that the fund's records were incomplete. Alden denied any attempt to betray its donors and called the questions ridiculous. An entrepreneur who owned the training company sat beside him. She said her business served the medical sector and had been invited to help sustain a public emergency initiative. Yu listened, then asked for the contract specifying where the third bus was supposed to be on the date of payment.

獨立審查在圖書館玻璃圓頂下舉行第一次正式證據會議。證人桌後立著一座青銅鐘，林雨提醒自己，莊嚴場地不會替證詞增加可信度。瑞雅開場承認基金會紀錄不完整。奧登否認背叛捐助者，稱質疑荒謬。訓練公司的一名女企業家坐在旁邊，說公司服務醫療部門，受邀協助維持公共緊急應變計畫。林雨聽完，請她出示合約，說明付款當天第三輛車本應停在哪裡。

A clinic representative described a separate program providing counseling after abortion and other reproductive-health treatment. She had come because a rumor falsely claimed that the missing vehicle's money had been spent on that service. The claim was both inaccurate and harmful, she said. The clinic's genetics laboratory purchased small supplies through a different budget, and not a single molecule of the disputed transport funding had been assigned to it. Yu thanked her for the correction and asked the session chair to record the unsupported allegation as false, without revealing any patient's identity. The room fell silent. Accurate accounting, Yu realized, could protect services from unfair attacks as well as expose bad decisions.

一名診所代表說明另有一項計畫，提供墮胎及其他生殖健康治療後的諮商。她前來是因為有謠言誣稱失蹤車輛的款項花在該服務上。她說，這種指控既不正確又會造成傷害。診所的遺傳學實驗室另有預算購買小型耗材，爭議運輸款項一分一毫都沒有撥給該部門。林雨感謝她澄清，並請主席把無根據的指控列為錯誤資訊，不揭露任何患者身分。全場安靜下來。林雨明白，精確帳務既能揭露錯誤決策，也能保護服務不受不公平攻擊。

The training company's accountant gave an anonymous written statement, later agreeing to testify behind a privacy screen. The statement described a mortgage on the company's depot and pressure to keep vehicles active so that the bank would not revise its terms. She did not accuse her employer of theft. Instead, she explained how a legitimate rental system had been stretched past the limits of its original authorization. The bus remained on the company's lot because a safety inspection had failed; someone then copied an old delivery code into the new invoice. Tomas checked the code against the shipping record and confirmed the match. The mechanism was becoming clearer, though the authority for the change remained disputed.

訓練公司會計先提交匿名書面陳述，後來同意隔著保護隱私的屏風作證。她說公司倉庫背負抵押貸款，銀行可能修改條件，因此主管面臨維持車輛運作的壓力。她沒有指控雇主偷竊，而是說明一套原本合法的租用制度如何超出最初授權。避暑車因安全檢查未通過而留在公司場地，之後有人把舊交貨代碼複製進新發票。湯瑪斯核對運輸紀錄，確認代碼相符。事情的運作方式逐漸清楚，但誰授權變更仍有爭議。

One trustee grew arrogant under questioning and suggested that the fund's critics were no better than a butcher cutting apart a useful institution for sport. Mako answered calmly that her neighbors were not trying to destroy the project. They wanted it to serve the people named in the grant. Yu felt grateful that Mako had chosen precision over anger. Felix presented a map of the two functioning bus routes and the neighborhoods they missed. He did not pretend those routes could serve everyone. The visual contrast, more than any insult, made the failure intelligible. The panel ordered a written clarification of the third bus's contractual status before any further payment.

一名董事被問到後顯得傲慢，說批評者像屠夫，只為娛樂就拆解有用的組織。真子冷靜回答，鄰居不是想摧毀計畫，只希望補助服務真正送達承諾中的人。林雨感謝真子用精確代替憤怒。費利克斯展示兩輛運行車的路線，以及仍未覆蓋的街區。他沒有假裝兩條路線能照顧所有人。這份視覺對比比辱罵更清楚說明了失敗。審查小組下令，在任何後續付款前，必須書面釐清第三輛車的契約地位。

After the session Samira asked whether Yu would give an on-camera interview. Yu agreed to describe the verified timeline, but declined to speculate about motive. Samira said the public might find such caution unsatisfying. "The people affected deserve an answer that lasts longer than a headline," Yu replied. Outside, a wildlife volunteer helped an injured bird away from the vertical glass wall. The small rescue reminded Yu that transparency was not merely a slogan; it required changing structures that made collisions likely. She walked back to the office with Dara, each carrying half the audit boxes. The team had reached a milestone, yet the most difficult question remained: who would accept responsibility for the wrong code?

會後莎米拉問林雨願不願上鏡受訪。林雨同意描述已確認的時間線，拒絕猜測動機。莎米拉說公眾可能不滿意如此謹慎的回答。「受影響的人值得一個比新聞標題更持久的答案，」林雨回答。外頭，一名野生動物志工把撞上垂直玻璃牆的受傷鳥兒移到安全處。小小的救援提醒林雨，透明不只是口號，還得改造容易造成碰撞的結構。她與達拉一起走回辦公室，各搬一半的稽核箱。團隊抵達一個里程碑，但最艱難的問題仍在：誰要為錯誤的代碼負責？

That evening Yu wrote the first public interim finding in language a resident could understand. The third bus had been purchased, but its delivery record was incorrectly marked complete; the bus had also been used under a separate training agreement while waiting for safety repairs. Neither statement alone established personal guilt. The panel would next review authorization, financial adjustments, and the effect on promised services. Yu emailed the draft to the company and the fund, inviting corrections tied to evidence. Rhea responded with two dates; the company promised a missing annex. The report was not dramatic, but it allowed people who disagreed to challenge the same facts rather than fight over different rumors. The team would integrate the shipping records, the bank ledger, and the safety documents only after checking each source independently.

當晚，林雨以居民能理解的語言寫下第一份公開階段性結論。第三輛車確實已採購，但交貨紀錄被錯誤標為完成；車輛等待安全維修時，也曾依另一份訓練協議使用。單憑這兩點無法認定個人有罪。審查小組接下來會查核授權、帳務調整，以及對承諾服務的影響。林雨把草稿寄給公司與基金會，邀請雙方提出有證據支持的更正。瑞雅回覆兩個日期，公司承諾補交缺漏附件。這份報告並不戲劇化，卻讓意見不同的人能挑戰同一組事實，而不是對著不同傳言爭吵。團隊將在獨立查核每個來源後，才整合運輸、銀行帳冊與安全文件。
