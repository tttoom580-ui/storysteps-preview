A Witness's Terms

明燈基金：第三輛車的帳 · 第 12 / 25 章 · 4000–5000 · 英中對照 · 校訂中

The training company's former payroll clerk, Bea, contacted Tomas through an observer she trusted. Her message was abnormal in one respect: she would speak only if the interview began with an explanation of data protection, not questions about money. Yu went to acquaint herself with the witness protocol before the meeting. Bea had kept a copy of a script used to train temporary workers on invoice codes. She believed the code for a returned vehicle was not compatible with the accounting system's "delivered" status. A missing software bolt, she said, could change a report without changing the original transaction. Yu asked whether Bea could demonstrate the error on a test database instead of exposing anyone's real pay records.

訓練公司前薪資職員碧雅透過信任的觀察員聯絡湯瑪斯。她提出一項不尋常的條件：訪談必須先解釋個資保護，才談金錢。林雨會前先熟悉證人訪談程序。碧雅保留一份教臨時工使用發票代碼的腳本，認為退回車輛的代碼不應與會計系統的「已交付」相容。她說，軟體裡少了一個像螺栓那樣小的關鍵設定，就可能改變報表而不改動原交易。林雨問她能否在測試資料庫示範，不暴露任何人的真實薪資紀錄。

Bea agreed, but wanted to confront a rumor first. She had been called a traitor after questioning the manager. Someone claimed she had tried to kidnap the company's documents and hold its staff hostage to force a settlement. "I copied the training script I wrote myself," she said. "I never took anyone's personnel file." The group recorded her account without endorsing every detail. An observer from a workers' rights organization explained that accusations of disloyalty often discourage people from reporting discrimination. Yu thanked Bea for setting her own conditions. The interview would be viable only if participation remained voluntary and its purpose was narrow.

碧雅答應，但先要面對一則傳言。她質疑主管後被罵成叛徒，有人說她想綁架公司文件、把員工當人質逼迫和解。「我只複製了自己寫的訓練腳本，」她說，「沒有拿任何人的人事檔。」團隊記錄她的說法，卻不替每個細節背書。一名勞工權益組織觀察員解釋，指控不忠常使人不敢舉報歧視。林雨感謝碧雅提出自己的條件。只有參與自願且目的明確，訪談才可能有效。

The group met in a room once used by a missionary school. A wall display traced the city's continental trade history, including an uncomfortable section on forced labor and slavery. Bea looked at the photographs and said that the story of workers being treated as replaceable stock was not entirely in the past. Yu did not compare an invoice dispute to slavery. She recognized instead that workplaces carried different degrees of power, and a junior clerk needed protection when challenging a superior. Outside the door a viewer watched a public education film with subtitles. Inside, the team agreed that no audio or image from Bea's interview would be released without her written approval.

訪談在一間過去屬於傳教學校的教室舉行。牆面展示城市的洲際貿易史，其中一段令人不安的展區談到強迫勞動與奴隸制度。碧雅看著照片，說把工人當成可替換庫存的故事並非完全過去。林雨沒有把發票爭議與奴隸制度相提並論，而是想到職場權力不對等，初階職員挑戰上司時需要保護。門外一名觀眾正看附字幕的公共教育影片；門內眾人則同意，未經碧雅書面同意，不得公開訪談音檔或影像。

Bea ran the demonstration. The system's normal process moved a returned shipment into a review queue, but a manual override copied its amount into the completed-delivery column. That override was not automatically a crime; accountants sometimes used it while preparing corrections. The important question was whether a correction had actually followed. Tomas asked Bea to stop before opening the administrator's logs. He wanted an independent technician to collect those records under formal permission. Bea mumbled that she had expected the team to demand everything. "That is what happens in the stories people tell about investigations," Yu answered. "It doesn't have to happen here." The witness relaxed enough to explain how the override had become a dangerous workplace norm.

碧雅開始示範。系統正常流程會把退貨送進待審清單，但人工覆寫會將金額複製到已交付欄位。這不必然是犯罪；會計師準備更正時有時也會使用。真正問題是後續是否完成更正。湯瑪斯在她打開管理員紀錄前請她停下，想由獨立技術員在正式授權下取得那些資料。碧雅低聲說，原以為大家會要她交出所有東西。「很多人講調查故事時確實是那樣，」林雨說，「但這裡不必如此。」證人稍微放鬆，開始說明人工覆寫如何變成危險的職場常態。

When the demonstration ended, Bea described the sensation of seeing the same warning disappear from every monthly report. She had thought she was mistaken until the third bus failed to arrive. Rhea, who had attended as a silent board observer, apologized that the fund had not asked the vendor for an independent reconciliation earlier. Bea said an apology was useful only if it changed the process. The director agreed. Yu drafted three safeguards: a separate return code, a second reviewer's signature, and a public report of unresolved adjustments. Bea checked the wording and removed a phrase that implied she had known the entire scheme. No one argued. Accurate limits made the testimony stronger.

示範結束後，碧雅描述每月報告裡同一則警告反覆消失時的感受。她一度以為自己弄錯，直到第三輛車沒來。以安靜董事會觀察者身分出席的瑞雅道歉，承認基金會沒有更早要求供應商獨立對帳。碧雅說，道歉只有改變程序才有用。主任同意。林雨起草三項防護：單獨的退貨代碼、第二名審查者簽名、以及尚未調整項目的公開報告。碧雅檢查措辭，刪除一段暗示她知道整個計畫的文字。沒有人爭執。界線準確，證詞反而更有力量。

Afterward Yu walked to the river with Tomas. The witness had shown them an error mechanism and a culture that allowed it to persist, but still not who had approved the particular transaction. Tomas warned that they might never know every person's motive. Yu said their task was not to write a perfect psychological account. They needed to identify a recordable chain of authorization, recover money where justified, and prevent repetition. On the opposite bank, the temporary cooling classroom was closing for the evening. Dara waved from the steps. The small scene reminded Yu why their technical patience mattered: people needed a service tomorrow, not a theatrical accusation tonight.

結束後林雨與湯瑪斯沿河散步。證人揭示了錯誤機制與縱容它延續的職場文化，卻仍無法證明誰批准了這筆特定交易。湯瑪斯提醒，他們可能永遠不知道每個人的動機。林雨說，他們不必寫出完美的心理分析，而是要確認可記錄的授權鏈、在有理由時追回款項並防止重演。對岸的臨時避暑教室正在關門，達拉在台階上揮手。小小畫面提醒林雨，技術上的耐心何以重要：居民明天需要服務，而不是今晚的戲劇化指控。
