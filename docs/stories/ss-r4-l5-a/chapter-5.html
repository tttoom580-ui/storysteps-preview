A Vote with Conditions

The first public review drew record attendance to the library hall. A contemporary art exhibition had been removed to make space for chairs, and a volunteer with a bruise on his cheek managed the microphone. Yu was asked to present the fund's overall financial picture, not just the controversial vehicle. A corporation had supplied most of the money; a local business had donated ice, and individual residents had offered hours of labor. Rhea wanted the presentation to endorse the project's successes. Dara argued that any honest account must also show who still lacked service. Their disagreement was not about whether the work mattered. It was about whose perspective should define success.

第一次公開審查吸引破紀錄的人數來到圖書館大廳。為了騰出椅子，館方移走了一場當代藝術展。一名臉上有瘀青的志工負責麥克風。林雨受邀介紹基金會的整體財務，而不只那輛有爭議的車。某家企業提供主要資金，本地商家捐贈冰塊，居民則付出勞力。瑞雅希望簡報能支持計畫成效；達拉主張，誠實的帳目也應呈現仍未得到服務的人。她們不是在爭論工作是否重要，而是在爭論由誰的觀點定義成功。

Yu divided the figures into three segments: confirmed purchases, emergency replacements, and disputed transactions. She displayed a statistical comparison of planned journeys against actual trips, then warned that numbers alone could not reveal the quality of a ride. A resident stood to describe how the buses had helped his mother visit a clinic. Another explained that her neighborhood had received nothing. The contrast showed the essence of the problem. The fund had achieved something valuable, and its records still required correction. Yu invited both speakers to verify the wording of their testimonies before publication. She would not turn either person's life into a convenient symbol for the board.

林雨把數字分成三個部分：已確認的採購、緊急替代措施和爭議交易。她展示規劃車次與實際趟次的統計比較，提醒大家數字本身無法顯示乘車品質。一名居民站起來說避暑車曾幫母親到診所；另一人說她的街區什麼也沒得到。這份對比揭示問題核心：基金會確實完成有價值的事，帳目仍有待更正。林雨請兩名發言者確認證詞文字後再公開。她不願把任何人的生活變成董事會方便使用的象徵。

A trustee tried to contend that public criticism would defy the wishes of donors. Mako answered that donors had funded a service, not a ritual of gratitude. Several people applauded. Rhea looked pale, yet she did not silence Mako. Tomas explained a proposal to facilitate a limited outside audit while allowing authorized emergency spending to continue. The plan required written controls: no new payments to the disputed vendor, weekly expense reports, and a public summary with personal data removed. It also required the board to acknowledge the gap without naming anyone guilty. Those restrictions were unpopular with trustees who preferred a simple endorsement or a complete rejection.

一名董事辯稱，公開批評會違背捐助者的心意。真子回答，捐助者資助的是服務，不是感恩儀式。有人鼓掌。瑞雅臉色蒼白，卻沒有阻止真子。湯瑪斯提出有限外部稽核方案，讓已獲授權的緊急支出同時持續。計畫要求書面控管：不得再向爭議供應商付款、每週公開費用報告、個資去識別後公布摘要。董事會也必須承認差異，卻不能先指控任何人有罪。部分董事只想全盤支持或全盤否定，這些限制令他們不滿。

Felix approached the microphone in his work outfit rather than a formal suit. He had spent the morning offering water to people waiting outside. "I don't think this is a fight between kindness and accounting," he said. "If a bus is missing, the next driver needs to know. If our volunteers are doing good work, they deserve records that prove it." His plain words shifted the room. Yu recognized the irony: the man most impatient with paperwork had given its clearest defense. She took the opportunity to explain that the next transition would depend on more than a vote. It would require people willing to write down what went wrong, even when their own decisions were part of the story.

費利克斯穿著工作服而非西裝走向麥克風。他早上一直為排隊者送水。「我不認為這是善良和會計之間的戰爭，」他說。「如果有車不見了，下一個司機需要知道。如果志工做了好事，他們值得有紀錄來證明。」平實的話改變了室內氣氛。林雨感受到其中的反諷：最不耐煩文書工作的人，竟替紀錄做了最清楚的辯護。她趁機解釋，下個階段不能只靠表決，還需要有人願意記下錯誤，即使其中牽涉自己的決策。

The trustees approved the audit proposal by four votes to one. Rhea voted in favor and asked that the decision include an obligation to keep the buses running wherever safety allowed. Mako requested a seat for a community observer, without access to patient names. Tomas agreed, provided the observer followed the same confidentiality rules as the auditors. Dara volunteered to prepare information sheets for families. Yu accepted responsibility for a transparent account of the vehicle discrepancy. The agreement did not repair the missing bus, but it created a common method for deciding what to do next. People left with fewer promises and more dates, names, and tasks.

董事以四比一通過稽核提案。瑞雅投下贊成票，並要求決議包含在安全容許範圍內持續車輛服務的義務。真子爭取一個社區觀察席位，但不得接觸患者姓名。湯瑪斯同意，條件是觀察員遵守與稽核人員相同的保密規則。達拉自願編寫給家庭的資訊單。林雨負責透明交代車輛差異。協議沒有修好失蹤的車，但建立了下一步共同決策的方法。人們帶走的承諾變少，日期、姓名和具體任務反而變多。

As the hall emptied, Rhea asked Yu whether the fund could survive another month of scrutiny. Yu could not answer. She had discovered that the organization was neither a flawless hero nor a hidden villain. It was a set of people, incentives, records, and habits that could fail in overlapping ways. The decision to investigate had already changed how they spoke to one another. Dara handed Yu a copy of the community's attendance list with addresses removed. At the bottom someone had written, "We came because we still want this to work." Yu folded the page carefully. That sentence would matter when the next set of invoices arrived.

大廳漸漸清空，瑞雅問林雨，基金會能否撐過下一個月的審查。她答不出來。她已經看見，組織既不是完美英雄，也不是藏在暗處的惡棍；它是由人、誘因、紀錄與習慣構成的系統，可能在彼此重疊的地方出錯。調查本身已改變大家說話的方式。達拉遞給她一份去除地址的社區簽到表。最下方有人寫：「我們來，是因為我們還希望它做得成。」林雨小心摺好紙。當下一批發票抵達時，這句話會變得重要。
