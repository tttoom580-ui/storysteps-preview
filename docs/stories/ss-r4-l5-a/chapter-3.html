空白簽名欄

The Empty Signature

明燈基金：第三輛車的帳 · 第 3 / 25 章 · 4000–5000 · 英中對照

用互動版查詞、朗讀與收藏

On Monday, the board met in a narrow conference room above the clinic. Rhea opened with a warning against aggression, though no one had raised a voice. An attorney hired by the fund sat beside her, carefully distinguishing legal risk from an accounting question. Felix brought the original delivery schedule, bound with a blue ribbon that had belonged to the fund's beloved founder. Yu remained cautious; the nostalgic detail could not diminish the importance of a blank signature line. The executive director insisted that the third vehicle had passed through the depot. "Then somebody should have signed for it," Yu said. The attorney requested that no one alter the file while the discrepancy was examined.

星期一，董事會在診所樓上一間狹窄會議室開會。沒人提高音量，瑞雅卻先警告大家避免攻擊性言論。基金會聘請的律師坐在她旁邊，謹慎區分法律風險與會計問題。費利克斯帶來原始交車時程，文件以一條藍緞帶綁著，那是已故創辦人珍愛的物品。林雨保持小心；懷舊的細節無法減輕空白簽名欄的重要性。執行主任堅持第三輛車曾通過倉庫。「那就應該有人簽收，」林雨說。律師要求，在查清差異前不得修改檔案。

The finance screen displayed a layer of administrative notes above the underlying invoices. An innocent-looking remark said the bus had been reassigned to a biological research center that monitored heat injuries. Dara worried that patients might become prey to public curiosity if their names were exposed. Yu agreed to limit the scope of her request to vehicle identifiers, dates, and authorizations. Their colleague Tomas, who specialized in independent accounting reviews, connected by a virtual meeting link. He did not claim the evidence was conclusive. Instead, he asked the team to straighten the distinction between a purchase order, proof of payment, and proof of physical delivery. Those were three separate facts, regardless of how conveniently the database displayed them together.

財務畫面在原始發票上方疊著一層行政註記。其中一則看似無害的備註說，車輛已重新配置到研究熱傷害的生物研究中心。達拉擔心，一旦患者姓名曝光，他們可能成為外界窺探的獵物。林雨同意把調閱範圍限於車輛識別碼、日期和授權。專做獨立帳務審查的同事湯瑪斯透過線上會議加入。他沒說證據已經確鑿，而是請團隊釐清採購單、付款證明及實物交付三者的區別。這是三項不同事實，不論資料庫把它們排在一起有多方便。

Outside the conference room, a shuttle waited to take patients to a temporary shelter. Dara fastened a safety strap around a child's wheelchair while counting the slow tick of the accessible lift. Felix offered to drive instead of attending the rest of the meeting. Yu saw him shrug when Rhea called that choice disloyal. He had a different viewpoint: people could not wait for auditors to establish a clean narrative. Yu admired his instinct and also feared that a rushed substitute vehicle would hide the original failure. She asked him to record each emergency journey separately. "That way," she explained, "the help you give today will never be mistaken for the vehicle we paid for last month."

會議室外，一輛接駁車等著送病人到臨時避暑所。達拉為輪椅上的孩子扣好安全帶，聽著無障礙升降台緩慢的滴答聲。費利克斯主動開車，放棄後半場會議。瑞雅說那是不忠誠，他聳聳肩。對他來說，人們不能等稽核人員整理好敘事才得到協助。林雨欣賞這種本能，也害怕急忙調來的替代車輛會遮掩原本的缺失。她請他把每趟緊急接送獨立記錄。「這樣，」她解釋，「你今天提供的幫助就不會被誤認成上個月已付款的那輛車。」

Tomas requested a copy of the bank record under a written confidentiality agreement. Rhea feared the request implied distrust. "It implies a question," Yu replied. She had learned from documentary realism that the smallest detail could carry an entire argument: a timestamp, a signature, a missing serial number. The attorney reviewed the agreement and suggested an audit window of ten working days. A volunteer at the door asked whether that meant no services would be cut. Yu said she could not promise the future. She could promise that the staff would not spend disputed funds without a documented reason. The answer disappointed some people, but no one could say she had invented certainty.

湯瑪斯依書面保密協議要求銀行紀錄副本。瑞雅擔心這表示彼此不信任。「這表示我們有問題要回答，」林雨說。她從寫實紀錄作品學到，一個最小的細節也可能承載整段論證：時間戳記、簽名或遺漏的序號。律師審閱協議，建議稽核期限為十個工作日。門口志工問這是否代表服務不會中斷。林雨說自己無法保證未來，但可以保證團隊不會在缺乏書面理由時動用有爭議的款項。答案讓部分人失望，卻沒有人能說她捏造了確定性。

When the meeting ended, Rhea stayed behind. She confessed that the fund's largest corporate donor had threatened to withdraw support if the missing bus became public. Yu asked whether the donor had seen the same blank signature. Rhea had not told them. For the first time Yu understood why her director seemed to regard questions as attacks. The organization had built a protective wall of goodwill around work it genuinely valued, and the wall now concealed a dangerous blind spot. Rhea said, "I don't want the foundation to die because I chose the wrong contractor." Yu answered, "Then help us learn exactly what was wrong before someone else decides for us."

會議結束後，瑞雅留下來，承認最大的企業捐助者威脅：若失蹤車輛的事公開，他們就撤回支持。林雨問捐助者是否看過那道空白簽名。瑞雅還沒告訴他們。林雨第一次理解，為何主任把提問當成攻擊。基金會用善意替真正重要的服務築起保護牆，卻也在牆後藏了一個危險的盲點。瑞雅說：「我不想因為選錯承包商，就讓基金會倒閉。」林雨回答：「那就幫我們弄清楚究竟錯在哪裡，別讓別人替我們決定。」

An hour later Tomas sent the first technical comparison. The delivery file had been generated twelve minutes after the bank transfer, but the supposed receipt bore a time three hours earlier. That inconsistency did not prove fraud; computers could export old data after an event. The decisive detail was the absent receiver's name. Yu wrote the discrepancy into the meeting record and sent copies to all five board members, including Rhea. She refused to create a private faction inside the staff. If the fund was to survive, its next decision had to be made with a common record, not competing rumors. By evening the board had agreed to preserve every version of the delivery schedule.

一小時後，湯瑪斯送來第一份技術比對。交貨檔案產生於銀行轉帳後十二分鐘，所謂收據卻標示三小時前的時間。這個矛盾不能證明詐欺，電腦也可能事後匯出舊資料。關鍵細節是沒有收貨人姓名。林雨把差異記進會議紀錄，寄給全部五名董事，包括瑞雅。她拒絕在員工間建立私下派系。若基金會想存續，下一個決定就必須建立在共同紀錄上，而不是各說各話的傳言。傍晚時，董事會同意保留交車時程的每一個版本。
