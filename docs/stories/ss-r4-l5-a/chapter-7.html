被偷走的標籤

The Stolen Label

明燈基金：第三輛車的帳 · 第 7 / 25 章 · 4000–5000 · 英中對照 · 校訂中

用互動版查詞、朗讀與收藏

An argument over a stolen service label threatened to arouse another round of controversy. Felix found an array of photographs from the opening ceremony, including one taken in a corridor behind the depot. In that picture the third bus wore a blue plate. In last week's warehouse photograph it wore a green plate. Dara suspected corrupt dealings, but Tomas cautioned that a vehicle could receive a new label after repair. The team needed the original registration, not a theory about the colors. Yu asked the warehouse to place its repair logs in neutral custody until the review ended. The manager agreed only after Rhea promised that the records would be returned with their contents unchanged.

一枚遭偷換的服務標籤引發爭論，也可能掀起新一波風波。費利克斯找到開幕典禮的一整組照片，其中一張拍於倉庫後方走廊。照片裡第三輛車掛著藍牌，上週倉庫照片裡卻是綠牌。達拉懷疑有人貪腐，湯瑪斯提醒車輛維修後可能換標籤。團隊需要原始登記，不是關於顏色的理論。林雨要求倉庫在審查結束前把維修日誌交由中立人士保管。經瑞雅承諾紀錄將原封不動歸還後，管理員才同意。

In a nearby kitchen volunteers prepared a local cuisine festival to raise money for the heat shelters. The neighborhood cooking league had planned a tournament between teams, and a retired sailor jokingly called his spicy fish soup "the pirate's prize." The event sounded cheerful, but the organizer feared that the missing vehicle might disconnect the festival from its promised recipient: the shelter network. Yu helped derive a budget from the actual cost of ingredients, the number of meals, and the rented equipment. She made a separate line for electricity and clean water, both indispensable to the event. The organizer thanked her for bringing expertise without trying to run the kitchen herself.

附近廚房裡，志工正準備地方料理節，為避暑站募款。社區烹飪聯盟安排隊伍錦標賽，一名退休水手開玩笑地把他的辣魚湯叫作「海盜獎品」。活動聽來歡樂，主辦者卻擔心失蹤車輛事件讓募款不再流向原定的受助對象——避暑站網絡。林雨依食材實際成本、餐點數量及租用設備推導預算，另外列出電力與潔淨用水，兩者都是活動不可或缺的條件。主辦人感謝她提供專業知識，卻沒有試圖接管廚房。

During an inspection, Felix discovered that the depot had removed a sturdy metal bracket from the third bus. The bracket held a filter that prevented contaminated water from entering the drinking system. Without it, bacteria might infect the supply even if the vehicle's engine worked perfectly. He asked for an immediate safety hold, regardless of the financial investigation. Rhea approved the hold and notified the contractor in writing. Yu recorded the distinction: one team was evaluating a disputed payment, another was checking physical safety. A failure in either area could harm people, but the evidence had to be kept separate. The decision cost them a possible replacement vehicle for the weekend.

檢查時，費利克斯發現倉庫把第三輛車上一個堅固的金屬支架拆走了。支架固定濾水器，能防止受污染的水進入飲水系統。少了它，即使引擎運作正常，細菌也可能感染供水。費利克斯要求立即停止使用，與財務調查無關。瑞雅核准停用並書面通知承包商。林雨記下這項區分：一組人查有爭議的付款，另一組人查實體安全。兩邊任何疏失都可能傷人，但證據必須分開。這項決定使團隊週末可能少一輛替代車。

Omar arrived with a volunteer who had worked at the depot before leaving for another job. The witness knew how its infrastructure was maintained and recognized the green label as part of a private training program. He did not know who authorized the transfer. Yu asked whether he would give a signed statement describing what he had directly seen, rather than what colleagues had told him. He agreed. The statement identified a supervisor, a date, and the location of a locked storage cabinet. It also revealed a gap: the original logbook had been removed for digital scanning but never returned. That missing book was now more important than either colored plate.

奧瑪帶來一名曾在倉庫工作、後來轉職的志工。證人熟悉設備維護方式，也認出綠色標籤屬於私人訓練計畫，卻不知道是誰授權轉移。林雨問他能否只就親眼所見簽署陳述，不混入同事轉述。他同意。證詞指出一名主管、一個日期，以及上鎖置物櫃的位置，也揭露另一項缺口：原始日誌曾被取走數位掃描，之後沒有歸還。那本書現在比兩種顏色的牌子都更重要。

At sunset the cooking festival opened. Children cheered for the teams, while a poster explained that every donation would be registered and the beneficiary chosen publicly. Yu stood aside and watched Mako count the first receipts with two witnesses. Dara said the procedure felt slow. "It is," Yu admitted, "but it makes the money traceable after the excitement is gone." A cook offered them soup; neither woman was ready to forgive all their disagreements, yet they shared the meal. Yu saw that community trust could be rebuilt in small, verifiable steps. A worthwhile tradition was not one that never changed. It was one willing to improve when people discovered a weakness.

日落時料理節開幕，孩子們替隊伍歡呼，海報說明每筆捐款都要登錄，受益對象也會公開決定。林雨站在一旁，看真子與兩名見證人清點第一批收據。達拉說程序感覺很慢。「確實慢，」林雨承認，「但熱鬧過後，錢還能追得回去。」廚師端來湯，兩名女子還沒準備原諒彼此所有分歧，卻一起吃了晚飯。林雨看見社區信任可以在微小、可驗證的步驟裡重建。值得保留的傳統，不是永遠不改變，而是發現弱點後願意改善。

That night Tomas confirmed that the private training program had charged the fund for using the disputed vehicle. He could not establish whether the charge was a second bill or a legitimate rental offset. Yu placed the document beside the other records and wrote two questions on a large sheet: who owned the bus on that date, and who had authorized its use? The answers might clear the supplier or expose a deeper problem. Either outcome would be better than an accusation built on a photograph alone. She asked Rhea to invite the depot manager to a recorded meeting. Rhea agreed, then warned that the manager might refuse. Yu answered that a documented refusal would also be a fact. The removal of the blue label was recorded separately from the repair, so a reader would not mistake a change in appearance for proof of a changed owner.

當晚湯瑪斯確認，私人訓練計畫曾向基金會收取使用爭議車輛的費用，但他無法確定那是重複請款，還是合法租用折抵。林雨把文件放在其他紀錄旁，在大紙上寫下兩個問題：當天誰擁有車輛？誰授權使用？答案可能替供應商洗清嫌疑，也可能揭露更深的問題。兩種結果都比只憑一張照片指控別人更好。她請瑞雅邀請倉庫管理員參加錄音會議。瑞雅答應，卻提醒對方可能拒絕。林雨說，經正式記錄的拒絕也是一項事實。藍色標籤的移除另行登記，免得讀者把外觀改變誤認為所有權改變的證據。
