捐助者的晚宴

The Donor's Dinner

明燈基金：第三輛車的帳 · 第 2 / 25 章 · 4000–5000 · 英中對照

The next evening the Lantern Fund held a donor dinner in a museum whose elegant architecture concealed a leaking roof. Yu disliked the absurd contrast between polished speeches and volunteers collecting water in buckets. An adverse weather warning had arrived, yet the catering staff kept opening the balcony doors for photographs. Rhea asked Yu to highlight the program's achievements and avoid mentioning the vehicle inquiry. "A successful donor meeting protects our work," she said. Yu agreed to report that two buses were operating. She refused to say that three had been delivered. Their disagreement was quiet, but its seriousness exceeded the sound of the music floating across the room.

隔晚，明燈基金會在一座建築優雅、屋頂卻漏水的博物館舉行捐助者晚宴。林雨不喜歡這種荒謬對比：台上致詞光鮮亮麗，志工卻拿水桶接漏水。惡劣天氣警報已發布，餐飲人員仍為了拍照不斷打開陽台門。瑞雅請林雨強調計畫成果，不要提車輛調查。「捐助者會議成功，才能保住工作，」她說。林雨同意報告已有兩輛車運行，卻拒絕說三輛都已交付。這場分歧很安靜，卻比飄過大廳的音樂更沉重。

A journalist named Samira attended as a guest of the neighborhood press. She had studied journalism because the city's official announcements often left small communities invisible. At her table sat an indigenous organizer, Mako, whose district had been promised a cooling stop but received none. Rhea introduced Yu as the finance expert. Mako was not impressed by titles. "Our families don't need an ideology," she said. "We need a vehicle that arrives." Samira asked whether an independent outsider could inspect the disputed schedule. Yu explained that a limited review might be possible if families' addresses were removed. Mako agreed to help identify the necessary information, but insisted that the community would control what personal details could be shared.

社區媒體記者莎米拉受邀參加晚宴。她選擇學新聞，是因為城市的官方公告常讓小社區變得隱形。同桌的原住民組織者真子所住的街區獲承諾設置避暑站，卻至今沒有。瑞雅向她介紹林雨是財務專家。真子對頭銜毫無興趣。「我們家人不需要一套意識形態，」她說，「需要的是會到站的車。」莎米拉問能否讓外部獨立人士查閱有爭議的時程。林雨解釋，只要移除家庭地址，也許能進行有限審查。真子願意協助界定必要資訊，但堅持社區有權決定哪些個資可以分享。

At the buffet a plate of salmon lay untouched beside a series of lavish desserts. Yu noticed how excessive hospitality could render an ordinary request for records impolite. One trustee offered her a neck massage after seeing her tense shoulders; she declined with a smile and repeated her question about the vendor. The trustee said all projects contained variation between forecasts and real deliveries. "Variation is not the issue," Yu replied. "It is whether a payment was approved for a vehicle no one can locate." He changed the subject to the museum's paintings. She felt the urge to rip the printed menu in half. Instead she wrote the names of the people who had heard the conversation.

自助餐桌上，一盤鮭魚躺在一排豪華甜點旁，幾乎沒人碰。林雨注意到，過度殷勤的招待竟能讓平常的查帳要求顯得失禮。一名董事見她肩膀緊繃，提議替她按摩脖子；她微笑婉拒，再次追問供應商的事。對方說每項計畫的預測與交付之間都有差異。「問題不在差異，」林雨回答，「而是是否有人批准付款給一輛無法找到的車。」他轉而談博物館的畫。林雨很想把印好的菜單撕成兩半，最後只記下聽見對話者的名字。

Samira met her near the stairwell and asked if she expected a prosecution. "I expect documents," Yu answered. She knew that public accusations could destroy the credibility of the fund before anyone established intent. Samira nodded and described her own quest: to explain how a public service could fail without treating every worker as dishonest. Their professions required different kinds of proof. A news report could quote a witness with permission; an audit required records that another examiner could reproduce. They agreed not to exchange confidential files in the stairwell. Instead, Samira would ask questions publicly, and Yu would provide only the verified status of deliveries.

莎米拉在樓梯間遇見她，問她是否預期有人遭到起訴。「我預期會找到文件，」林雨回答。她知道在查明意圖前公開指控，可能摧毀基金會的可信度。莎米拉點頭，說自己的目標是解釋公共服務為何失敗，而不是把每名工作者都當成壞人。兩種專業需要不同證據：新聞可以在獲准後引用證人，稽核則需要他人能重現的紀錄。她們約定不在樓梯間交換機密檔案。莎米拉將公開提問，林雨則只提供已確認的交付狀態。

Rain began before dessert. Mako led people away from the balcony while a waiter rescued the donor cards. One card carried a pledge for the very district whose residents were still waiting in the heat. Yu could see how that promise might persist in a report long after its practical meaning had faded. Rhea came to help close the doors. For a moment the three women worked together, pressing towels against the frame and directing visitors downstairs. They had different viewpoints about publicity but the same immediate concern for safety. Yu wondered if this cooperation could survive the questions she would ask on Monday. Mako, noticing her expression, said, "You can disagree with us. Just don't disappear when we ask for the truth."

甜點上桌前便下起雨。真子帶領人群離開陽台，服務生則搶救捐助者卡片。其中一張卡，承諾援助的正是仍在高溫中等待的街區。林雨看得出，一項承諾可能在報告裡存續多年，實際意義卻早已消失。瑞雅過來協助關門。片刻間三名女子一起用毛巾堵住窗框，指引賓客下樓。她們對公開程度看法不同，卻都關心眼前安全。林雨不知道這份合作能否承受星期一即將提出的問題。真子看見她的神色，說：「妳可以不同意我們，但別在我們問真相時突然消失。」

Yu returned home with an invitation to visit Mako's district and an address for an independent review office. On the bus she read the dinner program and found a donor's name repeated in two different sections, once as a foundation and once as a contractor. She did not know whether the overlap meant anything. She marked both entries, then closed the booklet. The city lights made islands on the wet windows, and she thought about the danger of collecting clues merely because they were dramatic. Her duty was narrower: find the third bus, trace its payment, and ask whether the people who had donated money were told the truth. She would begin with the simplest question the next morning.

林雨回家時，手上多了真子街區的拜訪邀請，以及一間獨立審查辦公室的地址。公車上，她閱讀晚宴手冊，發現同一名捐助者的名稱在兩處出現，一處標為基金會，另一處卻是承包商。她不知道這種重疊是否有意義，便把兩處標起來，合上手冊。城市燈光在濕玻璃上形成一座座小島。她想起蒐集戲劇性線索的危險，自己的責任其實更明確：找到第三輛車，追查付款，弄清楚捐款人是否得知實情。隔天她將從最簡單的問題開始。
