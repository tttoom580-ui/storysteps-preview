樓上吸血鬼的茶

Tea for the Vampire Upstairs

The night began quietly in Moonport City, although Luna knew that quiet did not mean safe. It was the early days, and the group had already learned that a confident guess could be worse than an honest question. Luna reviewed the route log and invited Eli and the night delivery crew to look at the same page. Nobody had to agree before hearing the whole problem. A shy vampire orders tea but does not invite Luna inside. She leaves the box at the agreed shelf and receives a signed receipt. Their task suddenly seemed larger than it had a moment before. Luna made one important choice: the next step would be written down where everyone could check it.

在月港城，這個夜晚起初很安靜，但露娜知道安靜不代表安全。現在已是最初幾天，大家明白，自信的猜測有時比誠實提問更危險。露娜翻閱配送紀錄簿，邀請伊萊與夜間快遞小隊一起查看相同頁面。所有人都可以先聽完整個問題，再決定是否同意。當天的第一份紀錄寫道：害羞的吸血鬼訂了茶，卻沒邀露娜進屋。她把盒子放在約定架上，取得簽收。那一刻，眼前任務突然顯得比剛才更加龐大。露娜做出重要決定：下一步要公開記下，讓每個人都能核對。

They examined the attempt and waited for a clearer answer. The group discussed the cause openly rather than allow a rumor to decide. The cereal became one more detail to check before the group moved on. Luna offered to cheat, but wanted the others to agree first.

他們檢查嘗試，等待更清楚的答案。 團隊公開討論原因，不讓傳聞替大家做決定。 在團隊繼續之前，麥片成了需要查核的另一項細節。 露娜願意作弊，但希望先取得其他人的同意。

Before acting, Eli asked which facts came from direct observation. The team drew two columns: what they knew and what they had only heard. Luna moved a doubtful claim into the second column because its source was missing. Nobody called the correction a defeat. They wanted the next person to understand the evidence, not to inherit an untested guess.

行動之前，伊萊問大家哪些事實來自直接觀察。團隊畫出兩欄：一欄是確知的事，一欄是聽來的話。露娜把找不到來源的說法移到第二欄。沒有人把這項更正當成失敗；大家希望後來的人能理解證據，而不是繼承未經驗證的猜測。

Eli asked how the classmate might change their plans. They did not treat the description cloudy as evidence by itself. They agreed not to continue until the facts were clearer. The earring became one more detail to check before the group moved on.

伊萊詢問同學可能如何改變大家的計畫。 他們沒有只憑「多雲的」這種描述就當成證據。 大家同意等事實更清楚後才繼續。 在團隊繼續之前，耳環成了需要查核的另一項細節。

They made a small test before changing the larger plan. Luna explained each step aloud while Eli watched for a mistake. A second person kept the old record so that the result could be compared later. At first the difference looked too small to matter. Then the night delivery crew noticed that one part of the plan would affect people who were not in the room. The group paused and changed the order of its work. They did not pretend a single success was enough. A good test was something another person could repeat, with clear limits and a way to stop safely.

他們先進行小型測試，再考慮改動大計畫。露娜逐步大聲說明操作，伊萊則注意可能的錯誤。另一個人保留舊紀錄，方便日後比較。起初差異小得像是不重要，後來夜間快遞小隊注意到其中一步會影響當時不在場的人。團隊停下來調整工作順序，沒有假裝一次成功就足夠。真正好的測試，是別人也能重複操作、限制清楚，而且知道如何安全停止的測試。

Another detail changed the mood of the group. The vampire left a small thank-you card beside his signature, making the first late delivery feel less lonely. The others had a chance to ask questions before the decision became final. A shared choice was not always easy, but it was easier to defend when everyone knew its cost.

另一個細節改變了團隊氣氛。吸血鬼在簽名旁留下小小感謝卡，讓第一趟深夜配送不再孤單。大家在決定正式生效前仍有提問的機會。共同選擇不一定容易，但當所有人都知道代價時，就更能為它負責。

In her bag, the parcels made small sounds when the road turned. Luna checked each seal without lifting the lid. One box smelled of oranges, another of wet stone, and a third hummed like a sleeping bee. She wanted to know their stories, but she knew that a customer's privacy was not a puzzle made for her entertainment.

道路轉彎時，露娜袋裡的包裹發出細微聲響。她不掀蓋，只檢查每道封條。一盒有橘子香，一盒像濕石頭，第三盒嗡嗡作響，彷彿熟睡的蜜蜂。她想知道故事，卻明白客人的隱私不是供自己娛樂的謎題。

Luna made a record of the entrance, so everyone could compare it later. Eli said the situation felt final, though he could still be wrong. They examined the force and waited for a clearer answer. Someone described the result as general, which the team needed to verify.

露娜記下了入口，讓所有人日後能夠比較。 伊萊說情況感覺最後的，但也承認自己可能判斷錯誤。 他們檢查強迫，等待更清楚的答案。 有人形容結果一般的，團隊仍需要確認。

Later, Luna and Eli spoke away from the others. Luna admitted to being afraid of making a decision that could not be repaired. Eli said that fear was useful when it encouraged care, but dangerous when it stopped people from listening. Neither offered an easy promise. They returned to the group and explained what had changed, inviting questions from every person affected. The time they spent together began to feel more personal, though neither owed the other a promise. They could still disagree the next morning, and they could still work on the same problem. That seemed more valuable than winning an argument in front of an audience.

稍後，露娜與伊萊離開人群談話。露娜坦承自己害怕做出無法挽回的決定。伊萊說，害怕若能提醒人謹慎就有用；若讓人不肯聆聽就會危險。兩人沒有輕易保證一切順利，而是回到團隊說明改動，邀請所有受影響的人提問。他們相處的時間逐漸有了更私人的意義，但任何一方都不欠對方承諾。隔天他們仍可能意見不同，也仍能為同一問題努力。這似乎比在眾人面前贏得爭論更有價值。

The government became one more detail to check before the group moved on. The latest report called the result liberal, and Luna asked why. Eli asked how the link might change their plans. They examined the nod and waited for a clearer answer.

在團隊繼續之前，政府成了需要查核的另一項細節。 最新報告稱結果開放的，露娜追問原因。 伊萊詢問連結可能如何改變大家的計畫。 他們檢查點頭，等待更清楚的答案。

By the end of the night shift the team had completed one useful piece of work, not the whole journey. Luna checked the route log one last time and wrote both the result and the remaining doubt. Eli read the entry and asked whether a newcomer could understand it without meeting the people who wrote it. The answer was not quite yes, so they added the missing detail. Their shared goal remained to finish the night routes while protecting every customer's choice. Tomorrow would bring a different test, and the group could not know its answer in advance. For tonight, however, everyone knew exactly what had been done, what had not been done, and who had agreed to the next step.

夜班結束時，團隊完成了一項有用的工作，卻還沒走完整段旅程。露娜最後檢查配送紀錄簿，同時寫下結果與尚未解決的疑問。伊萊讀完後問：如果新來的人沒見過作者，能看懂嗎？答案還不完全是肯定的，於是大家補上缺少的細節。他們共同的目標仍是在保障每位客人選擇權的前提下完成夜間路線。明天會有不同的考驗，誰也不能提前知道答案。不過今晚每個人都清楚：哪些事已完成、哪些尚未完成，以及下一步由誰共同同意。

They agreed not to operate until the facts were clearer. The runner became one more detail to check before the group moved on. Luna made a record of the scooter, so everyone could compare it later. Eli asked how the supply might change their plans.

大家同意等事實更清楚後才操作。 在團隊繼續之前，跑步送件者成了需要查核的另一項細節。 露娜記下了機車，讓所有人日後能夠比較。 伊萊詢問供應可能如何改變大家的計畫。
