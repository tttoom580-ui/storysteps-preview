小龍的藥品

The Baby Dragon's Medicine

夜班魔女的快遞清單 · 第 4 / 25 章 · 1000–2000 · 英中對照 · 校訂中

The night began quietly in Moonport City, although Luna knew that quiet did not mean safe. It was the early days, and the group had already learned that a confident guess could be worse than an honest question. Luna reviewed the route log and invited Eli and the night delivery crew to look at the same page. Nobody had to agree before hearing the whole problem. A tiny dragon needs a cool medicine pouch. Luna checks the package label and returns it when the seal is damaged. Their task suddenly seemed larger than it had a moment before. Luna made one important choice: the next step would be written down where everyone could check it.

在月港城，這個夜晚起初很安靜，但露娜知道安靜不代表安全。現在已是最初幾天，大家明白，自信的猜測有時比誠實提問更危險。露娜翻閱配送紀錄簿，邀請伊萊與夜間快遞小隊一起查看相同頁面。所有人都可以先聽完整個問題，再決定是否同意。當天的第一份紀錄寫道：小龍需要低溫保存的藥袋。露娜檢查包裝標籤，發現封條損壞便退回處理。那一刻，眼前任務突然顯得比剛才更加龐大。露娜做出重要決定：下一步要公開記下，讓每個人都能核對。

They agreed not to attend until the facts were clearer. The cage became one more detail to check before the group moved on. Luna made a record of the comb, so everyone could compare it later. Eli asked how the department might change their plans.

大家同意等事實更清楚後才參加。 在團隊繼續之前，鐵網罩成了需要查核的另一項細節。 露娜記下了梳子，讓所有人日後能夠比較。 伊萊詢問系所可能如何改變大家的計畫。

Before acting, Eli asked which facts came from direct observation. The team drew two columns: what they knew and what they had only heard. Luna moved a doubtful claim into the second column because its source was missing. Nobody called the correction a defeat. They wanted the next person to understand the evidence, not to inherit an untested guess.

行動之前，伊萊問大家哪些事實來自直接觀察。團隊畫出兩欄：一欄是確知的事，一欄是聽來的話。露娜把找不到來源的說法移到第二欄。沒有人把這項更正當成失敗；大家希望後來的人能理解證據，而不是繼承未經驗證的猜測。

They examined the drug and waited for a clearer answer. Someone described the result as eastern, which the team needed to verify. The team decided to encourage only after checking the risks. Luna made a record of the firm, so everyone could compare it later.

他們檢查藥品，等待更清楚的答案。 有人形容結果東邊的，團隊仍需要確認。 團隊決定先確認風險，再嘗試鼓勵。 露娜記下了堅定的，讓所有人日後能夠比較。

They made a small test before changing the larger plan. Luna explained each step aloud while Eli watched for a mistake. A second person kept the old record so that the result could be compared later. At first the difference looked too small to matter. Then the night delivery crew noticed that one part of the plan would affect people who were not in the room. The group paused and changed the order of its work. They did not pretend a single success was enough. A good test was something another person could repeat, with clear limits and a way to stop safely.

他們先進行小型測試，再考慮改動大計畫。露娜逐步大聲說明操作，伊萊則注意可能的錯誤。另一個人保留舊紀錄，方便日後比較。起初差異小得像是不重要，後來夜間快遞小隊注意到其中一步會影響當時不在場的人。團隊停下來調整工作順序，沒有假裝一次成功就足夠。真正好的測試，是別人也能重複操作、限制清楚，而且知道如何安全停止的測試。

Another detail changed the mood of the group. Luna returned the leaking medicine before it reached the baby dragon, accepting a late delivery over an unsafe one. The others had a chance to ask questions before the decision became final. A shared choice was not always easy, but it was easier to defend when everyone knew its cost.

另一個細節改變了團隊氣氛。露娜在藥品抵達小龍手中前退件，寧可遲到也不冒險配送。大家在決定正式生效前仍有提問的機會。共同選擇不一定容易，但當所有人都知道代價時，就更能為它負責。

Eli held the lantern under the awning while rain tapped the old metal roof. Its light was small, yet the address lines became easier to read. Luna thanked him without pretending she could not have done the task alone. He said he liked working beside someone who knew when to ask for help. Neither turned the moment into a promise they had not chosen.

雨敲著老金屬屋頂，伊萊在遮雨棚下提著燈。光不強，卻讓地址更容易讀。露娜道謝，卻不假裝自己無法獨立完成任務。他說喜歡和懂得何時求助的人並肩工作。兩人沒有把這一刻變成尚未共同選擇的承諾。

Eli asked whether they could fry without putting anyone in danger. They examined the gym and waited for a clearer answer. Someone described the result as minor, which the team needed to verify. The motion became one more detail to check before the group moved on.

伊萊問大家能否在不讓任何人陷入危險的情況下煎。 他們檢查體育館，等待更清楚的答案。 有人形容結果較小的，團隊仍需要確認。 在團隊繼續之前，動作成了需要查核的另一項細節。

Later, Luna and Eli spoke away from the others. Luna admitted to being afraid of making a decision that could not be repaired. Eli said that fear was useful when it encouraged care, but dangerous when it stopped people from listening. Neither offered an easy promise. They returned to the group and explained what had changed, inviting questions from every person affected. The time they spent together began to feel more personal, though neither owed the other a promise. They could still disagree the next morning, and they could still work on the same problem. That seemed more valuable than winning an argument in front of an audience.

稍後，露娜與伊萊離開人群談話。露娜坦承自己害怕做出無法挽回的決定。伊萊說，害怕若能提醒人謹慎就有用；若讓人不肯聆聽就會危險。兩人沒有輕易保證一切順利，而是回到團隊說明改動，邀請所有受影響的人提問。他們相處的時間逐漸有了更私人的意義，但任何一方都不欠對方承諾。隔天他們仍可能意見不同，也仍能為同一問題努力。這似乎比在眾人面前贏得爭論更有價值。

Luna made a record of the pan, so everyone could compare it later. Eli said the situation felt private, though he could still be wrong. They examined the purpose and waited for a clearer answer. The group discussed the region openly rather than allow a rumor to decide.

露娜記下了平底鍋，讓所有人日後能夠比較。 伊萊說情況感覺私人的，但也承認自己可能判斷錯誤。 他們檢查目的，等待更清楚的答案。 團隊公開討論區域，不讓傳聞替大家做決定。

By the end of the night shift the team had completed one useful piece of work, not the whole journey. Luna checked the route log one last time and wrote both the result and the remaining doubt. Eli read the entry and asked whether a newcomer could understand it without meeting the people who wrote it. The answer was not quite yes, so they added the missing detail. Their shared goal remained to finish the night routes while protecting every customer's choice. Tomorrow would bring a different test, and the group could not know its answer in advance. For tonight, however, everyone knew exactly what had been done, what had not been done, and who had agreed to the next step.

夜班結束時，團隊完成了一項有用的工作，卻還沒走完整段旅程。露娜最後檢查配送紀錄簿，同時寫下結果與尚未解決的疑問。伊萊讀完後問：如果新來的人沒見過作者，能看懂嗎？答案還不完全是肯定的，於是大家補上缺少的細節。他們共同的目標仍是在保障每位客人選擇權的前提下完成夜間路線。明天會有不同的考驗，誰也不能提前知道答案。不過今晚每個人都清楚：哪些事已完成、哪些尚未完成，以及下一步由誰共同同意。

At first the answer looked standard, but the team checked again. Luna made a record of the umbrella, so everyone could compare it later. Eli asked how the wing might change their plans. They examined the wolf and waited for a clearer answer.

答案起初看來標準的，但團隊再次核對。 露娜記下了雨傘，讓所有人日後能夠比較。 伊萊詢問翅膀可能如何改變大家的計畫。 他們檢查狼，等待更清楚的答案。
