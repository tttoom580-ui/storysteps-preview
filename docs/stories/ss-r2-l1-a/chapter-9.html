節慶卡片

Cards for the Festival

打烊後，這間店由我作主 · 第 9 / 25 章 · 1000以下 · 英中對照 · 校訂中

用互動版查詞、朗讀與收藏

The town festival was near. Morning Corner had a small table on the busy street, and Rina was ready to help. Her grandmother came to visit and brought a bag of bananas and apples. "Food is one joy in life," she said. "But good people are another." Rina made a card for each dish. The card had the food name, its price, and the time. She wanted every customer to know exactly what they were buying. Ben made the cards bright and easy to read. Leo placed them between the food and the money box.

小鎮節慶快到了。「晨光角落」在熱鬧街上有張小桌子，莉娜準備幫忙。她的祖母來訪，帶了一袋香蕉和蘋果。「食物是人生的一種快樂，」她說，「好人則是另一種。」莉娜為每道餐點做卡片，寫上食物名稱、價格和時間。她希望每位顧客都清楚知道自己買了什麼。班把卡片做得明亮又好讀。里歐將它們放在食物與錢箱之間。

The festival began beside the lake. People sang, played games, and walked from store to store. A child shouted, "Rice cakes!" Rina showed him the price card and asked if he was sure he wanted one. "Okay!" he said. His mother smiled. The child paid, and Rina gave him a small paper with the sale number. "Why do you write every little sale?" his mother asked. "So you can ask about it later if something is wrong," Rina said. The mother nodded and put the paper safely in her bag.

節慶在湖邊開始。人們唱歌、玩遊戲，穿梭在各家攤位間。一個孩子大喊：「米糕！」莉娜給他看價目卡，問他確定要不要買。「好啊！」孩子說。他的母親笑了。孩子付錢後，莉娜給他一張寫有交易編號的小紙。「為什麼連小生意都要記？」母親問。「這樣如果有問題，您之後可以詢問。」莉娜說。母親點頭，把紙妥善放進包裡。

Mark came to the festival and said the card system took too much time. He wanted to make every price a round number, without writing why. "That will be easier," he said. Rina answered, "It may be easy for us, but not fair to the people who pay." Mr. Park stood near the table and heard her. "Keep the cards," he said. "We will count the money at the end." Rina felt quite happy. It was the first time Mr. Park had spoken for her plan in front of Mark. She did not shout about her small win.

馬克來到節慶，說卡片制度太花時間。他想把所有價錢改成整數，卻不寫明原因。「這樣比較簡單。」他說。莉娜回答：「對我們可能簡單，對付錢的人卻不公平。」朴先生站在桌旁聽見了。「卡片留下，」他說，「收攤再數錢。」莉娜相當高興。這是朴先生第一次當著馬克的面支持她的計畫。她沒有為這小小勝利大喊。

Later, an older man came back with his sale paper. He said he had paid for two dishes but got only one. Rina looked at the number. She found the second dish on the table, still covered. "I am sorry," she said. "Here it is. Thank you for telling us." The man gave her a small tip. Rina put it in the tip jar and wrote down the amount. "That was easy," Ben said. "The paper helped us see the mistake." Rina agreed. A good record could protect the customer and the worker at the same time.

後來，一位年長男子拿著交易紙回來。他說付了兩道餐的錢，卻只拿到一道。莉娜查看編號，在桌上找到了仍蓋著的第二道餐。「真抱歉，」她說，「餐點在這裡。謝謝您告訴我們。」男子給她一點小費。莉娜把小費放進罐子，記下金額。「這很簡單，」班說，「紙讓我們看見錯誤。」莉娜同意。好的紀錄能同時保護顧客和員工。

When the festival ended, the group counted the money. Rina's cards matched the money in the box. Mark looked away. He had said the new way would fail, but it had worked. Rina did not ask him to feel bad. She asked Mr. Park whether they could keep the same system in the shop. "Yes," he said. "And tomorrow we will look at the old pages." Her grandmother took her hand. "You did not only sell food today," she said. "You gave people a way to trust what they saw." Rina carried the cards home like a prize.

節慶結束後，大家一起數錢。莉娜的卡片和錢箱裡的金額完全相符。馬克移開視線。他曾說新方法會失敗，結果卻成功了。莉娜沒有要他難堪，而是問朴先生能否在店裡保留同樣制度。「可以，」他說，「而且明天我們會看舊帳。」祖母牽起她的手。「妳今天不只是賣食物，」祖母說，「妳讓人們有方法信任眼前所見。」莉娜像抱著獎品一樣把卡片帶回家。

"We should treat every child with care," her grandmother said. She cut a banana into small pieces for a child who could not eat a large one. "A good snack is not always a big thing," she added. Rina watched the child's smile and understood. The festival had a great many people, but each person needed something a little different. The paper cards gave everyone the same clear price. The workers could then give help based on the person's real need. For Rina, that was a good reason to keep the new system.

「我們應該好好對待每個孩子。」祖母說。她把一根香蕉切成小塊，給吃不下一整根的孩子。「好的點心不一定要很大。」她又說。莉娜看著孩子的笑容，明白了。節慶人很多，每個人的需要卻稍有不同。紙卡提供每人相同的清楚價格，員工再依真正需求給予協助。對莉娜而言，這是保留新系統的好理由。

Rina slept well that night. In her dream, the busy street was quiet, and every shop had a small light. She walked by the lake and remembered the smile of the boy with the rice cake. The festival had not solved the old problem. Yet it showed that clear prices and clear words could work for real people on a real day. She put one festival card inside the first page of her notebook. It would remind her that the shop could change. Now she was ready to ask why it had once been different.

那晚莉娜睡得很好。夢裡熱鬧的街道安靜下來，每間店都有一盞小燈。她沿湖走著，想起買米糕男孩的笑容。節慶沒有解決舊問題，卻證明清楚的價格與說明，真的能在真實的一天幫助人們。她把一張節慶卡片夾進筆記本第一頁，提醒自己店裡能夠改變。現在她準備追問，過去為何會不一樣。
