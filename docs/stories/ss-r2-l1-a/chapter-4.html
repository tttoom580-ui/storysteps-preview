小鎮廣場

The Town Square

打烊後，這間店由我作主 · 第 4 / 25 章 · 1000以下 · 英中對照

A cold wind began to blow through the town square. Gray cloud covered the sky. Rina went to the market to learn about the price of tomato boxes. The fruit worker there was a tall woman named May. She knew how much food cost every week. "A cheap box is not always a good box," May said. "Look at the food, not only the price." She opened a box, and Rina saw many bad tomatoes under the good ones. "If a shop buys this, customers will be unhappy," May said. Rina took notes in her small book.

冷風吹過小鎮廣場，灰色雲朵蓋住天空。莉娜去市場了解番茄箱的價格。水果攤的員工是一位高個子女子，叫梅。她知道每週食物的價格。「便宜的箱子不一定是好箱子，」梅說，「要看食物，不只看價錢。」她打開一個箱子，莉娜看見好的番茄下面藏著許多壞的。「店家如果買這種，客人就會不高興。」梅說。莉娜把話記進小本子。

A small boy ran by with a paper butterfly. He let it fly, but it fell near a factory truck. A worker picked it up and gave it back. "Thank you, sir!" the boy said. The man smiled. "Take care," he answered. Rina watched them. It was such a small act, but it made the square feel safe. She thought of the service at Morning Corner. Good service meant more than fast food. It meant telling the truth about what people paid for. She wanted the shop to be healthy in that way too.

一個小男孩拿著紙蝴蝶跑過。他讓蝴蝶飛起來，紙蝴蝶卻掉在工廠貨車旁。一名工人撿起來還給他。「謝謝您，先生！」男孩說。男子微笑著回：「小心喔。」莉娜看著他們。這是件小事，卻讓廣場顯得安全。她想到「晨光角落」的服務。好服務不只是快速送上食物，也包括誠實告知顧客所付的錢買到了什麼。她希望店裡也能這樣健全。

May told Rina a story about her first job. Her old boss asked her to shake each box before a sale. If she heard bad fruit moving, she had to take the box off the shelf. "He said we must not make money without care," May explained. "We can lose a sale today, but keep a friend tomorrow." Rina liked that idea. She asked May if she would explain it to Mr. Park. "I can speak about my own work," May said. "I cannot speak about Mark. I do not know him." Rina said that was fair.

梅告訴莉娜她第一份工作的故事。以前的老闆要求她每次賣貨前搖一搖箱子；如果聽見壞水果在裡面滾動，就得把箱子拿下架。「他說我們不能只顧賺錢、不顧品質，」梅解釋，「今天可能少賣一箱，明天卻能保住一位朋友。」莉娜很喜歡這個想法。她問梅是否願意向朴先生說明。「我可以談自己的工作，」梅說，「但我不了解馬克，不能評論他。」莉娜認為這很公平。

When Rina got back, Mark asked why she had left the shop during her free hour. "I went to check food prices," she said. "Here is the town list." He looked at the paper, then at her. "You think you can run this place?" he asked. "No," she said. "I think the owner should know what he buys." Her voice did not shake. Mark turned to another worker and told her that Rina was telling a strange story. Rina did not answer him. The list on the table was more useful than a loud fight.

莉娜回到店裡時，馬克問她為何在休息時間離開。「我去查食物價格，」她說，「這是鎮上的價目表。」他看了紙，又看她。「妳以為自己能管理這裡？」他問。「不，」她回答，「我認為店主應該知道自己買了什麼。」她的聲音沒有顫抖。馬克轉向另一位員工，說莉娜在講怪故事。莉娜沒有回嘴。桌上的清單比大吵一架更有用。

In the evening, Mr. Park read May's price list. The shop had paid too much for some food. It had also bought tomatoes that were not fresh. "I gave Mark the job because I cannot stand here all day," he said. "I must see the work, even if I am not here." Rina agreed. She did not say that she could do everything without help. She asked for a second person to check large orders. Mr. Park said yes. For the first time, a small rule changed because Rina had brought a clear fact.

傍晚，朴先生讀了梅的價目表。店裡有些食物買得太貴，也進了不新鮮的番茄。「我不能整天站在這裡，所以把工作交給馬克，」他說，「但即使我不在，也必須看清店裡的事。」莉娜同意。她沒有說自己可以一個人包辦一切，而是提議大筆訂單要由第二個人核對。朴先生答應了。這是第一次，莉娜提出清楚事實後，店裡的一條小規則真的改變了。

May showed Rina another table with good vegetables and fair prices. She said her workers could tell her when food was old, even if she had already paid for it. "I would rather hear a hard truth from them," May said, "than see a customer leave forever." Rina thanked her. She could not make Mark speak honestly by force, but she could help build a place where honest words were welcome. That idea stayed with her as the wind moved through the empty square.

梅帶莉娜看另一張擺著新鮮蔬菜、公平標價的桌子。她說即使食品已付款，員工也能提醒她東西不新鮮。「我寧可聽員工說難聽的真話，」梅說，「也不願看客人永遠離開。」莉娜向她道謝。她不能強迫馬克說實話，卻可以協助建立一個歡迎誠實話語的地方。風穿過空廣場時，這想法仍留在她心中。

That night the rain began to drop from the clouds. Rina stood under the roof and watched it fall. Ben came with two cups of hot water. "You were brave today," he said. "I was afraid," Rina answered. "But I can be afraid and still do one good thing." They looked across the square as the last worker closed a door. Rina knew one rule would not fix everything. Still, she had made a safe place for a second pair of eyes. She went home ready for the next part of the work.

夜裡，雨滴開始從雲層落下。莉娜站在屋簷下看雨。班帶著兩杯熱水過來。「妳今天很勇敢。」他說。「我很害怕，」莉娜回答，「但我可以一邊害怕，一邊做好一件事。」兩人望向廣場，看著最後一名工人關上門。莉娜知道一條規則無法解決所有問題，但她已為第二雙核對的眼睛創造安全的位置。她準備好回家，迎接下一步。
