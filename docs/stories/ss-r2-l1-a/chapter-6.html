船上的箱子

A Box on the Boat

打烊後，這間店由我作主 · 第 6 / 25 章 · 1000以下 · 英中對照 · 校訂中

Rina's uncle Tom arrived on Saturday morning. He lived in a small house on the other side of the river. He could drive a boat and knew many people near the water. He came with a box of coffee from a friend. "Before you open it, look at the sign on the top," he said. The sign had a lion on it. "This is the right coffee, but the box is for another store." Rina looked again. The store name was different. "Then we cannot use it yet," she said. Tom smiled. "That is why I came to see you."

星期六早上，莉娜的叔叔湯姆來了。他住在河對岸的小房子裡，會開船，也認識許多水邊的人。他帶來朋友的一箱咖啡。「打開之前先看上面的標誌。」他說。標誌上有隻獅子。「咖啡是對的，但箱子是給另一間店的。」莉娜再看一次，發現店名不同。「那我們還不能用。」她說。湯姆微笑。「這就是我來找妳的原因。」

They took the boat to the small food house by the river. Rina did not want to make a terrible mistake by taking another shop's food. The owner there was kind. She looked inside the box, then pointed to the shape of a number on the paper. "That is not a one," she said. "It is a seven." The number was written badly. The same error might explain another box from last week. Rina thanked her. "I thought this would be stupid work," Tom said. "But a tiny line can change a whole order."

他們搭船到河邊的小食品屋。莉娜不想把別間店的貨拿走，犯下可怕的錯誤。那裡的店主人很親切。她看了箱內，又指向單據上數字的形狀。「這不是一，是七。」她說。那個數字寫得很糟。同樣的錯誤也許能解釋上週的另一箱貨。莉娜向她道謝。「我原以為這工作很笨，」湯姆說，「但一條細線就能改變整張訂單。」

The two shops checked their papers together. Each had one box meant for the other. They made a fair exchange and wrote the time. Rina could finally see why the numbers on those two boxes had not matched. "We found one answer," she said. "Not the whole answer," Tom reminded her. She agreed. They still had to learn why money from the shop was missing. The boat moved slowly across the water, and Tom gave a small wave to a man on the shore. That man waved back, free from any worry about papers.

兩間店一起核對單據，發現各自都有一箱原本屬於對方的貨。他們公平交換，並寫下時間。莉娜終於知道那兩箱的數字為何不合。「我們找到一個答案了。」她說。「還不是全部答案。」湯姆提醒。她同意。店裡的錢為何短少，仍待查清。小船慢慢划過水面，湯姆向岸邊一名男子揮手，那人也回揮，看起來完全不必擔心任何單據。

Back at the shop, Mark asked why she spent her weekend looking at boxes. "Because a box can go to the wrong store," Rina answered. He said the owners could fix it without her. "They did," she said. "I only helped them see the problem." Mr. Park read the note from both shops. He was happy they had not lost money on the coffee. He gave Rina a simple job: make a clear list of all deliveries that week. She accepted. It was not a big job, but it was work she could do well.

回到店裡，馬克問她為何週末都在查箱子。「因為箱子可能送錯店。」莉娜回答。他說老闆們自己會解決，不需要她。「他們的確解決了，」她說，「我只是幫忙看見問題。」朴先生讀過兩店的紀錄，很高興咖啡沒有造成損失。他交給莉娜一項簡單工作：列出那週全部的送貨紀錄。莉娜接受了。那不是大工作，卻是她可以做好的事。

At closing time, Ben helped Rina put the last boxes away. "Can you see the top shelf?" he asked. "Not well," she said. He got a chair and moved the box to a lower place. They talked about how hard it was to find a paper in the wrong box. "A clear place for every paper would help," Ben said. Rina drew three boxes on a page: new orders, checked orders, and orders with a problem. She could use those simple shapes every day. She smiled because order was beginning to feel possible.

打烊時，班幫莉娜收好最後幾個箱子。「妳看得到最高那層架子嗎？」他問。「看不太清。」她說。他搬了椅子，把箱子移到較低的位置。兩人談起單據放錯箱子就很難找。「每張紙都有固定位置會更好。」班說。莉娜在紙上畫出三格：新訂單、已核對訂單、有問題的訂單。這些簡單圖形每天都能使用。她微笑了，因為整齊的制度開始變得可能。

"If we arrive before the boats leave, we can do this safely," Tom said. When they reached the river dock, he asked everyone to wait except the workers who were moving boxes. "One person at a time," he said. Rina watched carefully. She was learning that a safe job had a good order. First they checked the name, then the number, and last they moved the food. Tom had done this for many years, but he still checked each step. Rina thanked him for showing her a quiet way to do important work.

「如果在船離開前抵達，就能安全地完成。」湯姆說。他們到了碼頭後，他請所有人等待，除了正在搬箱子的工人。「一次一個人。」他說。莉娜仔細看著，學習安全工作需要良好順序：先查名字，再查數字，最後搬食物。湯姆做這工作多年，仍一步步核對。莉娜謝謝他示範如何安靜地完成重要工作。

When Tom left, he gave Rina one last piece of advice. "Do not take every problem into your own hands," he said. "Ask the people who know that part of the work." Rina watched his boat move away. She knew she had needed Jo, May, Nora, and Tom. The little shop was not alone. It sat inside a whole town of people who could help one another. That night she wrote in her book, "One mistake is not the same as a lie. Find the difference." She closed the book and went to sleep.

湯姆離開時給了莉娜最後一個建議。「不要把每個問題都扛在自己身上，」他說，「去問熟悉那份工作的人。」莉娜看著他的小船遠去。她知道自己需要喬、梅、諾拉和湯姆。這間小店並不孤單，而是在一座能互相幫忙的小鎮裡。那晚她在本子裡寫道：「錯誤不等於謊言。要找出差別。」接著合上本子睡覺。
