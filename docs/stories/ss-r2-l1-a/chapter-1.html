七點的鈴聲

The Bell at Seven

打烊後，這間店由我作主 · 第 1 / 25 章 · 1000以下 · 英中對照

The bell above the shop door rang at seven a.m. Rina was early again. The sun was still low, and the street was quiet. She worked at a small food shop called Morning Corner. She liked to make breakfast for people who had no time to cook. She put fruit near the window and a fresh cake on the table. A man on the old couch held a bag against his chest. He was Mr. Park, the shop owner. He smiled, but his face looked tired. "Today, please watch the money carefully," he said. Rina nodded and opened her small book.

早上七點，店門上的鈴響了。莉娜又提早到了。太陽還很低，街道十分安靜。她在一間叫「晨光角落」的小食物店工作。她喜歡替沒時間煮飯的人準備早餐。她把水果放在窗邊，把新鮮蛋糕放在桌上。舊沙發上的一名男子抱著袋子坐著。他是店主朴先生。他微笑著，臉色卻很疲倦。「今天請仔細看管錢。」他說。莉娜點頭，打開自己的小本子。

The new manager, Mark, came in with a heavy box. He put it down with a loud sound. "We shall have more customers today," he said. "Put a cover over that salad. And move the cake. You can exercise later." Rina was not doing exercise. She was moving chairs so an older customer could walk safely. She did not argue. Mark was new, and she wanted to give him a fair chance. But when she looked at the money box, she saw that the paper with yesterday's total was gone. She had written that total herself before going home.

新經理馬克帶著一個沉重的箱子進門，把它重重放下。「我們今天應該會有更多客人。」他說。「把那份沙拉蓋起來，蛋糕也移開。運動可以晚點再做。」莉娜其實不是在運動，而是在移動椅子，好讓一位年長客人安全走過。她沒有爭辯。馬克是新來的，她想公平對待他。但她看向錢箱時，發現寫有昨天總額的紙不見了。那是她下班前親手寫的。

Her friend Ben arrived with a new drawing for the shop menu. Drawing animals was his hobby, and he had made a smiling monkey with one large tooth to welcome children. He wanted to teach a foreign classmate some simple English words to write on the menu. "This is a monkey," he said, showing Rina the paper. "And this is a tooth." He pointed to the animal's wide smile. Rina laughed. Then she remembered the missing paper. She asked Ben to wait while she looked under the table. There was nothing there. She looked near the fruit and under the couch. The paper was not there either.

她的朋友班帶來一張準備放在店裡菜單上的新圖畫。畫動物是他的興趣；他畫了一隻露出一顆大牙齒的笑臉猴子，希望孩子們看了會開心。他想教美術班的一位外國同學幾個簡單英文詞，替菜單上的圖畫加上標籤。「這是猴子，」他拿著畫對莉娜說，「這是牙齒。」他指向猴子大大的笑容。莉娜笑了，接著想起不見的紙。她請班等一下，自己查看桌子下面。那裡什麼也沒有。她又找了水果旁與沙發底下，仍找不到紙。

At nine, Mark counted the money and spoke in a hard voice. "The shop has less money than it should. Someone made a mistake." Rina felt cold, although the morning was warm. "May I see the numbers?" she asked. Mark shut the box. "I will take care of it." Mr. Park did not say anything. He had trusted his workers for many years. Now he looked from one face to another. Rina wanted to help him. She also knew that a kind person should not have to answer for a number that nobody could explain.

九點時，馬克數完錢，語氣強硬地說：「店裡的錢比應有的少，有人犯了錯。」早晨其實溫暖，莉娜卻感到一陣寒意。「我可以看看數字嗎？」她問。馬克關上錢箱。「我會處理。」朴先生沒說話。他多年來一直信任員工，現在卻看著大家的臉。莉娜想幫他，也知道善良的人不該為一個沒人能解釋的數字負責。

At lunch, Rina wrote every sale in her book. She wrote the time, the food, and the money. It was a simple plan, but it was her plan. Ben asked if she wanted him to speak to Mark. "Not yet," she said. "First I need to know what is true." They ate salad by the window. People went past in the light of the sun. The bell rang each time someone came in. Rina listened to that small sound. It reminded her that many people loved this shop. They came here for food, but also for a little peace.

午餐時，莉娜在本子裡寫下每筆交易，記下時間、食物和金額。計畫很簡單，卻是她自己的計畫。班問要不要由他去找馬克談。「先不要。」她說。「我得先知道真相。」兩人在窗邊吃沙拉。人們在陽光下走過。每有人進來，鈴聲就響一次。莉娜聽著那小小的聲音，想起很多人喜愛這間店。他們來這裡不只是買食物，也是尋找片刻安寧。

The next morning, Rina decided to keep one rule for herself. She would not change a number just because somebody spoke loudly. She would check it first, and she would write down what she found. She could be kind to Mark and still ask him questions. She could care for Mr. Park and still tell him when something was wrong. This little choice was her first real step. It did not make her a manager. It made her a worker who could trust her own careful work.

隔天早上，莉娜替自己訂了一條規則。她不會因有人大聲說話就改動數字，而是先核對、再記下發現。她可以友善對待馬克，也可以向他提問；她可以關心朴先生，也可以在有錯時告訴他。這個小小選擇是她真正的第一步。它沒有讓她成為經理，而是讓她成為能信任自己謹慎工作的員工。

Before closing, Mark told the other workers that Rina might have lost the paper. She wanted to cry, but she kept her voice calm. "You may look at my book," she told him. "I will write everything again tomorrow." Mark gave a short laugh and went away. Mr. Park stayed. "Can you really find the answer?" he asked. "I do not know," Rina said. "But I can try." She put her book in her bag and turned off the lights. Outside, the bell rang once in the wind. Tomorrow she would compare her notes with the shop records again. Facts, not fear, would guide her next step.

打烊前，馬克告訴其他員工，那張紙可能是莉娜弄丟的。她很想哭，卻仍平靜地說：「你可以看我的本子。我明天也會重新記下每件事。」馬克短促地笑了一聲便走開。朴先生留了下來。「妳真的找得到答案嗎？」他問。「我不知道，」莉娜說，「但我可以試試。」她把本子放進包裡，關掉燈。外頭的風讓鈴鐺又響了一下。明天，她會再把筆記和店裡的帳目逐一比對。她接下來要依靠的是事實，不是恐懼。
