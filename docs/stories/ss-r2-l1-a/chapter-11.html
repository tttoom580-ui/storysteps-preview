寒冷的廚房

The Cold Kitchen

打烊後，這間店由我作主 · 第 11 / 25 章 · 1000以下 · 英中對照 · 校訂中

The weather turned cold, and the shop kitchen felt like a room under a blue sky with no sun. The cook wore two warm coats and asked for a new fan heater. Rina called a nurse who often bought lunch there and asked whether the room was warm enough for workers. The nurse said people needed a safe place to work. "A cold room can make hands slow," she said. Rina looked at the old machine. Maybe the problem was not the whole machine. There was a loose part behind it, and it made a sound whenever someone turned the fan on.

天氣轉冷，店裡的廚房彷彿處在沒有陽光的藍天下。廚師穿著兩件厚外套，要求買一台新的暖風機。莉娜打電話給常來買午餐的護理師，詢問這個工作空間是否夠暖。護理師說員工需要安全的工作環境。「房間太冷，手就會變慢。」她說。莉娜看著舊機器。也許不必整台換掉。後面有個零件鬆了，每次有人打開風扇，都會發出聲響。

Mr. Park called a repair worker. The worker came by car and asked the cook to turn the machine off. "I will look first," he said. Rina waited in the room with the shop secretary, June. June had kept a dictionary near her desk for many years. She used it to find clear words for letters. "A good sentence should help people understand," she told Rina. "That is true for money pages too." Rina liked the idea. A number needed a name and a reason, just as a sentence needed words in the right place.

朴先生找來維修工。工人開車來，請廚師先關掉機器。「我要先檢查。」他說。莉娜和店裡的秘書君一起在房裡等。君的桌邊多年來一直放著一本字典，用來替信件找出清楚的用詞。「好的句子應該幫助別人理解。」她對莉娜說，「帳頁也一樣。」莉娜喜歡這個想法。數字需要名稱和原因，就像句子需要放在正確位置的字。

The repair worker found a broken wire. "I can fix this today," he said. "The fan is probably fine." Rina asked him to write down what he changed. He agreed. He also showed her the old part before he put it in a bag. "You should not touch this yourself," he warned. Rina said she understood. She was learning that being careful did not mean doing every job. Sometimes the right call was to ask a person with the right skill. When the warm air came back, the cook gave everyone a bowl of hot soup.

維修工找到一條壞掉的電線。「今天就能修好，」他說，「風扇本身大概沒問題。」莉娜請他寫下更換了什麼，他答應了，還在把舊零件放進袋子前給她看。「妳不能自己碰這個。」他提醒。莉娜說她明白。她正在學習，謹慎不代表每份工作都要自己做；有時正確的決定是找有專業能力的人。暖風重新吹出時，廚師替每個人盛了一碗熱湯。

At lunch, a woman from the temple came to buy food for older people. She said a thousand small acts could change a town. Rina did not know if that was true, but she liked the thought. June showed her an old page from the past year. It had a clear call number beside each order. "This was our old way," June said. "Then Mark wanted a faster system." Rina asked whether anyone had checked the new system before using it. June shook her head. "We were busy. We thought it would be fine."

午餐時，一位從寺廟來的女子替長者買食物。她說一千件小小的善舉能改變一座城鎮。莉娜不知道這是否為真，卻喜歡這個想法。君拿給她看去年的一張舊頁面，每筆訂單旁都有清楚的通話編號。「以前我們這樣做，」君說，「後來馬克想改成更快的系統。」莉娜問新系統使用前是否有人核對。君搖頭。「大家很忙，便以為不會有問題。」

Rina did not want to blame June. She said, "We can learn from the past without being angry at the people in it." June smiled. Together they made a new page that showed the order number, the food, and the money. The fan made a soft sound over their heads. Outside, a tree moved in the wind. Rina felt warmer than she had that morning. There was still a hard question about the missing money. But she now knew that one weak system had made it easy for a mistake to stay hidden.

莉娜不想責怪君。「我們可以從過去學習，不必對當時的人生氣。」她說。君微笑了。兩人一起做了一張新表，列出訂單號碼、食物與金額。頭上的暖風機發出輕聲。窗外一棵樹在風中搖動。莉娜覺得比早上溫暖許多。短少的錢仍是難題，但她知道有個脆弱的制度曾讓錯誤容易被藏起來。

June put her bag on a chair and pulled on a warm pair of pants. She had come from the cold street and wanted to be ready to work. Rina gave her some tea. "We will not finish every page today," she said. "We can do one careful page and then stop." June agreed. They checked the first order and found that the food and money matched. Rina made a small mark beside it, then asked June if she wanted a short rest before they went on.

君把包放在椅上，穿上溫暖的長褲。她剛從寒冷街道進來，想準備好工作。莉娜給她一杯茶。「今天不必做完每一頁，」她說，「仔細完成一頁就先停。」君同意。兩人核對第一筆訂單，發現食物和金額相符。莉娜在旁畫上小記號，再問君要不要休息一下再繼續。

As the shop closed, Mr. Park asked Rina to keep June's old page with the new one. "I want to see what changed," he said. Rina put both in a safe folder. She also asked June to teach the next worker how to read the two kinds of numbers. "You are good at that," Rina said. June laughed. "Maybe this is my new job," she answered. Rina went home thinking of the old fan. One broken part had made the kitchen cold. One bad rule could make a whole shop feel just as cold.

打烊時，朴先生請莉娜把君的舊頁和新頁放在一起。「我想看清楚改變了什麼。」他說。莉娜把兩張紙收進安全的資料夾，也請君教下一位員工看懂兩種編號。「妳很擅長這件事。」莉娜說。君笑了。「也許這就是我的新工作。」她回答。回家路上莉娜想到舊風扇。一個壞零件讓整個廚房變冷，一條壞規則也能讓整間店同樣冰冷。
