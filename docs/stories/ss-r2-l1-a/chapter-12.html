A Page of My Own

打烊後，這間店由我作主 · 第 12 / 25 章 · 1000以下 · 英中對照 · 校訂中

In spring, the wind carried a fresh smell through the shop door. Rina wanted to make a page of her own for the new orders. She took a pencil, a bicycle bell, and a small notebook to the school. Ben's art teacher had asked her to say a few words about shop work. She felt shy, so she made a little bow when she entered the room. The students wore jeans and sat beside a model of the planet. They asked many questions. "Is your shop big?" one child asked. "No," Rina answered. "But every small job can teach a big lesson."

春天裡，風把清新的氣味帶進店門。莉娜想替新訂單設計一張自己的表。她帶著鉛筆、腳踏車鈴鐺和小本子去學校。班的美術老師邀她談談店裡的工作。她有點害羞，進教室時微微鞠躬。學生穿著牛仔褲，坐在一個行星模型旁，問了很多問題。「妳的店很大嗎？」一個孩子問。「不大，」莉娜說，「但每件小工作都能教會人重要的道理。」

A boy asked to borrow her notebook. Rina said yes, but asked him to pick a clean page. "This page is mine," she said, "and that one can be yours." The children laughed. She explained that a customer should get a second copy of a sale number, so the shop and the customer could check the same order. Then a girl asked how to spell "receipt." Rina wrote the word slowly and said it aloud. "Language matters," she told them. "If we say what we mean, it is easier to help each other."

一個男孩想借她的本子。莉娜答應了，但請他挑一頁乾淨的。「這頁是我的，」她說，「那頁可以給你。」孩子們笑了。她解釋顧客應拿到第二份交易編號副本，店家和顧客才能核對同一筆訂單。接著一個女孩問「收據」的英文怎麼拼。莉娜慢慢寫下 receipt 並唸出來。「語言很重要，」她告訴孩子們，「我們說清楚意思，就更容易互相幫忙。」

After class, the teacher gave Rina some salt and small stones for a science game. The students were learning to sort things by shape. Rina held up two stones. "They look the same from far away," she said, "but one is round and one is not." She pointed to two old order numbers. "These look the same too, but they should not be." The teacher said this was a good example. A boy asked whether the shop could use a new machine. "Soon, perhaps," Rina said. "First we must know what the machine should do."

下課後，老師拿出一些鹽和小石頭，進行科學遊戲。學生正在學習依形狀分類。莉娜舉起兩顆石頭。「遠看很像，」她說，「但一顆是圓的，另一顆不是。」她又指著兩個舊訂單號碼。「這兩個也看起來一樣，但本來就不該一樣。」老師說這是好例子。一名男孩問店裡是否能用新機器。「也許很快可以，」莉娜說，「但我們得先知道機器應該做什麼。」

On her way home, Rina rode her bike past the river. She wished she could tell her grandmother every good thing that had happened. She stopped at a small bridge and called her. "I taught a class today," Rina said. Her grandmother was happy. "Then you have learned something too," she answered. Rina knew that was true. She had learned to say a difficult idea in a simple way. She had also learned that no one had to own the whole truth alone. A fair answer could come from many small voices.

回家路上，莉娜騎著腳踏車經過河邊。她希望能把所有好事都告訴祖母。她在小橋旁停下，打電話給祖母。「我今天教了一堂課。」莉娜說。祖母很高興。「那妳自己也學到了東西。」她回答。莉娜知道這是真的。她學會用簡單方式說明困難的想法，也學會沒有人必須獨自擁有全部真相。公平的答案可以由許多小小聲音一起找出。

That evening, June showed Rina how to make the new page part of the shop program. They typed three test orders and checked the numbers twice. The first was for a small salad, the second for some bread, and the third for a cake. Each got a different number. "This is our work," June said. "Not Mark's and not only mine." Rina agreed. She placed the test pages in the safe folder. They would show Mr. Park the next morning. For now, she let herself smile at the clean new list.

當晚，君教莉娜把新表加入店裡的程式。兩人輸入三筆測試訂單，並核對兩次。第一筆是小沙拉，第二筆是一些麵包，第三筆是蛋糕。每筆都得到不同編號。「這是我們的工作，」君說，「不是馬克一個人的，也不只是我的。」莉娜同意，把測試頁收進安全的資料夾。隔天早上她們會拿給朴先生看。現在，她允許自己對那張整齊的新清單微笑。

One student asked why Rina kept the old pages when she had a new plan. Rina held up both and said, "A shop is like a body. Each part has work to do. If one part is hurt, we want to know when it happened." The teacher said it was a clear answer. Rina was glad, but she also knew the example had limits. A real person needed more care than a paper page. She reminded the class that records were made to help people, not to make people afraid.

一名學生問莉娜既然有新方法，為何還留舊頁。莉娜舉起兩張紙說：「商店像身體，每個部分都有自己的工作。若某部分受傷，我們會想知道何時發生。」老師說這個回答很清楚。莉娜很高興，也明白比喻有限。真正的人比紙張更需要關心。她提醒全班，紀錄是用來幫助人，而不是讓人害怕。

Before bed, Rina wrote one more wish. She wanted people at the shop to be able to say, "This is my own work," and feel proud. She did not want anyone to take a page and change its name. She wanted old workers and new workers to have the same chance to learn. It was a simple wish, and she knew it might take time. She put her pencil down, looked through the window at the night sky, and said, "We can begin tomorrow." Then she turned off her light.

睡前，莉娜又寫下一個願望。她希望店裡的人都能說「這是我自己的工作」，並以此為榮。她不想看到任何人把別人的頁面拿去改名。她希望老員工與新員工都有同樣的學習機會。這願望很簡單，她也知道需要時間。她放下鉛筆，透過窗戶看著夜空，說：「明天可以開始。」然後關上燈。
