河邊的影片

A Video by the River

打烊後，這間店由我作主 · 第 2 / 25 章 · 1000以下 · 英中對照

用互動版查詞、朗讀與收藏

Rina's cousin Leo came to the shop with his family on a sunny morning. He was a helpful guy who liked science and small tools. He could bring a broken lamp back to life with one simple tool. Today he brought his camera and showed Rina a short video. "I made this for our family," he said. "Look at the sheep near the river." They were in a green field south of town. Rina smiled at the funny sheep. Then she saw something else behind them: Mark's blue car, beside a delivery truck, at a strange time.

一個晴朗的早晨，莉娜的表弟里歐和家人來到店裡。他是個樂於助人的傢伙，喜歡科學與小工具。他能用一個簡單工具讓壞掉的燈恢復運作。今天他帶來相機，給莉娜看一段短片。「我拍給家人看的，」他說，「看河邊的羊。」羊群站在城南的一片綠地上。莉娜對有趣的羊笑了。接著，她看見牠們後方有別的東西：馬克的藍色汽車在一個奇怪的時間停在送貨車旁。

"Can you play that part again?" she asked. Leo moved his finger over the screen. Mark stood by the truck and took a small box from the driver. The box was not from Morning Corner. It had a picture of a red apple on one side. Rina knew the shop had no apple order that day. "It may be nothing," Leo said. "But the time is on the video." Rina did not want to call anyone bad without proof. She asked Leo to keep the video safe and not send it to other people.

「可以再播一次那部分嗎？」她問。里歐在螢幕上滑動手指。馬克站在貨車旁，從司機手裡拿了一個小箱子。那不是「晨光角落」的箱子，側面畫著紅蘋果。莉娜知道店裡那天沒有訂蘋果。「可能沒什麼，」里歐說，「但影片上有時間。」莉娜不想沒有證據就說誰是壞人。她請里歐把影片保存好，不要傳給別人。

At the shop, Mark asked Rina to add new numbers to the sales page. "Also fill this empty line," he said. "The owner wants a better total." Rina put down her pen. "I can add a real sale, but I cannot add a sale that did not happen." Mark pressed his lip together. "Do not make this hard," he said. "I have the power to change work hours." Rina did not enjoy that talk. She had bills to pay. Still, a false number could hurt the shop and every worker in it.

回到店裡，馬克要求莉娜在銷售頁面加上新的數字。「也把這一空行填滿，」他說，「老闆想看到更好的總額。」莉娜放下筆。「我可以加進真正的交易，但不能加進沒發生的交易。」馬克緊抿嘴唇。「別把事情弄難看，」他說，「我有權改動班表。」莉娜不喜歡這場談話。她有帳單要付，但假數字也可能傷害店裡每位員工。

Ben came in and heard the last words. He did not shout. "The rule should be the same for they and for us," he said, then smiled at his own mistake. "For them and for us," he corrected himself. Leo laughed quietly. Ben was learning how to speak clearly in English. Rina was learning how to ask clear questions. She showed Mark the real page. "Please tell me which line is wrong," she asked. He pointed at the bottom, but gave no answer about the missing money.

班進門時聽到了最後幾句話。他沒有大吼。「規則對 they 和我們應該一樣，」他說完笑自己的文法錯誤，又改口：「對 them 和我們。」里歐輕聲笑了。班正在學著把英語說清楚；莉娜則在學著問出清楚的問題。她把真實紀錄拿給馬克。「請告訴我哪一行有錯。」她說。馬克指著頁面底部，卻沒解釋短少的錢。

That night, Leo offered to help. "I can compare the video time with the delivery time," he said. "We do not need to guess." Rina asked him to bring the camera next day. She also asked Ben to speak to the driver, if the driver agreed. "No tricks," she said. "We only ask what happened." The three sat with warm drinks and drew a small plan. It was not famous work. There were no bright lights or big prizes. But to Rina, a fair answer was worth more than a prize.

那晚，里歐主動幫忙。「我可以把影片時間和送貨時間比對，」他說，「我們不必猜。」莉娜請他隔天帶相機來，也請班在司機願意時和對方談談。「不耍花招，」她說，「我們只問發生了什麼。」三個人喝著熱飲，畫出一份簡單計畫。這不是什麼有名的工作，沒有聚光燈，也沒有大獎。但對莉娜而言，公平的答案比獎品更重要。

Rina asked Leo to write the date on a second page and keep the original video with him. She knew a picture could be copied, but a copy might not show every detail. They would tell Mr. Park when they had a clear reason to ask for help. Leo agreed. "I also want the truth," he said. "Not a story that makes someone look bad." Rina was glad he understood. Working with people who could say no to a bad idea made her feel safer.

莉娜請里歐在第二張紙上記下日期，並由他保存原始影片。她知道畫面可以複製，但副本可能沒有完整細節。他們會等到有清楚理由需要協助時再告訴朴先生。里歐同意。「我也想知道真相，」他說，「不是讓某個人看起來很壞的故事。」莉娜很高興他懂。和能對壞主意說不的人合作，讓她更安心。

In the morning, Mr. Park thanked Rina for keeping the real page. He said a customer had also asked why prices were changing. "People trust us," he said. "I do not want to lose that." Rina showed him only the numbers she could explain. She did not show the video yet, because it did not prove that Mark had taken shop money. Mr. Park nodded. "You are careful," he said. Rina felt a little stronger. They could take one good step at a time, and they did not need to hurt anyone to do it.

隔天早上，朴先生謝謝莉娜保留真實紀錄。他說有顧客也問起為什麼價格一直改變。「人們信任我們，」他說，「我不想失去信任。」莉娜只拿出自己能解釋的數字。她還沒拿出影片，因為影片尚不能證明馬克拿走店裡的錢。朴先生點頭。「妳很謹慎。」他說。莉娜感到更有力量了。他們可以一次走好一步，無須傷害任何人。
