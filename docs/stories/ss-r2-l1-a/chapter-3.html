通往海灘的路

The Road to the Beach

打烊後，這間店由我作主 · 第 3 / 25 章 · 1000以下 · 英中對照

The next delivery came on a clear morning. Rina asked the driver, Nora, about the boxes. Nora said she drove from the station, over the bridge, and along the road by the beach. "I make this trip every day," she said. "I stop at three shops." Rina asked if she could follow Nora's route after work. Nora agreed, but said Rina must keep out of the truck's work area. Ben came with her. They took a small bag of water and food. Rina wanted to see where the delivery papers came from, not look for a person to blame.

下一次送貨是在晴朗的早晨。莉娜向司機諾拉詢問箱子的事。諾拉說她從車站開出，越過橋，再沿著海灘旁的道路行駛。「我每天都走這條路，」她說，「會停三間店。」莉娜問下班後能否沿著同一路線走一趟。諾拉答應了，但提醒她不能進貨車的工作區。班陪她去。兩人帶了一小袋水和食物。莉娜想查明送貨單從何而來，不是尋找可以責怪的人。

A bird sang near the bridge. Far below, water moved around the rocks. They saw a cow in a field and a man with a clean white shirt. The man said he was born in the town. He knew the road well. "That small building on the other side is where drivers get their papers," he told them. He pointed at a low office near the beach. Ben took one step forward, then stopped. His shoe was caught in a long line of grass. Rina helped him pull it free. They laughed and walked on.

橋邊有隻鳥在唱歌。下方很深處，水流繞過石頭。他們看見田裡的一頭牛，以及一名穿乾淨白襯衫的男子。男子說自己出生在這座小鎮，很熟悉這條路。「另一邊那棟小房子，就是司機領送貨單的地方。」他指向海灘旁的一間低矮辦公室。班向前跨一步又停下來。他的鞋子被長草纏住了。莉娜幫他把草拉開。兩人笑著繼續走。

At the office, a woman named Jo showed them the rules. She could not give them papers for another shop. She could, however, tell Rina how a delivery worked. Every box had a shop name, a number, and a time. The driver put one paper into a book, and the shop got another paper. "What happens if one paper is lost?" Rina asked. "We can check our own book," Jo said. "But the shop owner must ask for it." That was useful news. Rina wrote the office phone number in her notebook.

在辦公室裡，一位叫喬的女子向他們解釋規則。她不能交出別間店的單據，不過可以告訴莉娜送貨的流程。每個箱子都有店名、數字與時間。司機把一份紙放進自己的冊子，店裡則得到另一份。「如果有張紙不見了呢？」莉娜問。「可以查我們自己的紀錄，」喬說，「但必須由店主提出要求。」這是有用的消息。莉娜把辦公室電話記在本子裡。

On their way back, they saw a young boy carry a table into a house. He asked where Morning Corner was. Rina told him to follow the road toward the station and turn left. "Are you about to buy a cake?" she asked. "No," he said. "My mother wants to work there." Rina felt a new worry. If false numbers made the shop look bad, more than the present workers could be hurt. She wanted the store to be a place where people could find safe work. She did not want fear to decide who got a job.

回程時，他們看見一個小男孩把桌子搬進房子。男孩問「晨光角落」在哪裡。莉娜告訴他沿著路往車站走，再左轉。「你是要去買蛋糕嗎？」她問。「不是，」男孩說，「我媽媽想去那裡工作。」莉娜又多了一份擔心。若假數字讓店的名聲受損，受傷的不只是現有員工。她希望那裡是人們能安心工作的地方，不願讓恐懼決定誰能得到工作。

When they returned, Rina asked Mr. Park to call Jo. He did it beside her, so they could hear the same answer. Jo explained the rule again and offered to check the shop's deliveries. She would need one day. Mr. Park thanked her. Mark heard the call and asked why they were making trouble. Rina said, "We are checking a number. It may be a small error." Mark turned away. Rina was glad she had used the word "error." That word left room for the truth, whatever the truth might be.

他們回來後，莉娜請朴先生打電話給喬。朴先生就在她身邊撥打，兩人聽到了相同的答案。喬再次解釋規則，表示可以查核店裡的送貨紀錄，但需要一天。朴先生向她道謝。馬克聽見通話後，問他們為何要找麻煩。莉娜說：「我們在查一個數字，也許只是小錯誤。」馬克轉身走開。莉娜很慶幸自己用了「錯誤」這個字，因為它留給真相足夠的空間，不管真相是什麼。

Ben wanted to take a picture of every paper he saw. Rina stopped him. "We do not have the right to copy every shop's work," she said. "We can ask for the information we need, and let the owner give us the rest." Ben put his camera away. He understood that a search for truth needed rules too. If they broke other people's trust while looking for an answer, the answer would not make the shop better. They walked back together without taking a single private paper.

班想把每張看到的單據都拍下來。莉娜阻止他。「我們沒有權利複製每家店的工作文件，」她說，「可以詢問需要的資訊，其他部分由店主提供。」班收起相機。他明白尋找真相也需要規則。若為了找答案而破壞別人的信任，就算找到答案，也不會讓店變得更好。兩人一起走回去，沒有帶走任何私人文件。

At home, she sat by the window and read her notes. She saw one thing happen again and again: the shop page gave a number, but a box had a different number. She drew a line between them. The line did not tell her who was wrong. It showed her where to ask next. She sent Ben a short message: "Tomorrow we look at the boxes, not at people's faces." He wrote back, "Good plan." The sea was far away now, but Rina could still hear the bridge water in her mind.

回家後，她坐在窗邊讀筆記。她看見同一件事重複發生：店裡的表單記著一個數字，箱子上的數字卻不同。她在兩者之間畫了一條線。這條線沒告訴她誰犯錯，卻指出下一步該問什麼。她傳訊息給班：「明天看箱子，不看人的臉色。」班回：「好計畫。」大海已經很遠，莉娜腦中卻仍聽得見橋下的水聲。
