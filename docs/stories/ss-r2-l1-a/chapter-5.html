和雪賽跑

A Race Against the Snow

打烊後，這間店由我作主 · 第 5 / 25 章 · 1000以下 · 英中對照

用互動版查詞、朗讀與收藏

Although it was still early in the year, snow fell on the hills above town. The road to the food farm was long, and the delivery car could not move fast. Rina had made a deal with a farmer named Ms. Hill: the shop would buy fresh beans and eggs from her. But the boxes had to arrive before lunch. Rina did not want to put anyone in danger. "A sale is not worth a life," she told Ben. They decided to drive only to the safe road and wait there for the farm workers.

雖然一年還沒過多久，小鎮上方的山丘竟下起雪來。通往農場的路很長，送貨車開不快。莉娜已和一位叫希爾女士的農夫談好交易：店裡會向她買新鮮豆子與雞蛋，但箱子必須在午餐前送達。莉娜不想讓任何人冒險。「一筆生意不值得拿生命去換，」她告訴班。他們決定只開到安全的路段，在那裡等待農場工人。

Near the road, a frog sat beside a wet stone. A hen walked past it, looking for food. Ben held up his phone and showed the height of the snow on a weather page. "At least it is not deep here," he said. Rina pointed to a mark on a road post. "If the snow reaches that point, we go back." She put a small red cloth on the post so everyone could see the line. "One minute at a time," she said. The cold air touched her neck, but she kept looking at the road.

路邊，一隻青蛙坐在濕石頭旁，一隻母雞經過尋找食物。班拿起手機，展示天氣頁面上的積雪高度。「至少這裡還不深。」他說。莉娜指著路柱上的一道記號。「如果雪到這裡，我們就回去。」她在柱上綁了一小片紅布，讓大家看得見那條線。「一次看一分鐘。」她說。冷空氣吹過她的脖子，她仍盯著路面。

After twenty minutes, a farm worker came with a small cart. She said the eggs were safe, but the road was getting worse. Rina asked her to put the boxes under a dry cover. They checked every box together. One had a long crack. "Sorry," the worker said. "That box is not safe for eggs." Rina thanked her for saying so. They moved the eggs into another box. The worker had not tried to hide the damage. Rina felt that this was how a good business should work: people helping one another before a problem became bigger.

二十分鐘後，一位農場工人推著小車來了。她說雞蛋很安全，但路況愈來愈差。莉娜請她把箱子放到乾燥的遮蔽物下。兩人一起逐箱檢查，其中一箱有很長的裂痕。「對不起，」工人說，「這箱裝雞蛋不安全。」莉娜謝謝她願意說出來。他們把雞蛋移進另一個箱子。工人沒有試圖藏起損壞。莉娜覺得這才是好生意的方式：問題變大前，大家先互相幫忙。

The farm worker asked if Rina wanted to race back to town. "No," Rina said. "We will move slowly." She had learned that winning did not always mean coming first. Sometimes it meant going home with everyone safe. Ben made a little joke about a snow race between a frog and a hen. Rina laughed so hard that she almost dropped her gloves. Then they put the boxes in the car. They checked the weight and the number once more. The farmer's page and Rina's page showed the same total.

農場工人問莉娜要不要和時間賽跑，快點回鎮上。「不要，」莉娜說，「我們慢慢走。」她學到勝利不一定是第一個抵達；有時是每個人都安全回家。班開玩笑說青蛙和母雞正在進行雪地賽跑。莉娜笑得差點掉了手套。接著他們把箱子放進車裡，再核對一次重量和數量。農夫的單子與莉娜的單子顯示相同總額。

When they reached Morning Corner, Mark looked at the clock. "You are late," he said. Rina showed him the road note and the two matching pages. "We did not take a risk," she answered. "And we have the food." Mr. Park came out from the back room. He read the pages slowly. "Good," he said. "I would rather lose a sale than hurt someone." Mark said nothing. Rina put the eggs away and made a warm meal for the team. The day had been hard, but it had given them a new way to check deliveries.

他們抵達「晨光角落」時，馬克看著時鐘。「你們遲到了，」他說。莉娜出示路況記錄和兩張相符的單子。「我們沒有冒險，」她回答，「而且食物送到了。」朴先生從後房走出，慢慢讀完那些紙。「很好，」他說，「我寧可少賣一筆，也不要有人受傷。」馬克一言不發。莉娜把雞蛋收好，替大家做了熱食。這一天很辛苦，卻讓他們得到新的送貨核對方法。

During the drive, they talked about the bean soup they could make for lunch. Ben said he hoped the little shop would win the town's food prize one day. Rina laughed. "First we must bring the beans home," she said. She kept both hands on her bag and watched the road. In her mind, a prize could wait. The people in the car and the boxes beside them were more important. When the road was safe, there would be time for games and prizes again.

開車途中，他們談起午餐可以做的豆子湯。班說希望小店有天能贏得小鎮美食獎。莉娜笑了。「先把豆子安全帶回家再說。」她說。她抱好袋子，看著前方道路。在她心中，獎項可以等；車裡的人和旁邊的箱子更重要。道路安全後，還有時間再玩遊戲、爭取獎項。

That evening, the snow stopped. Rina read her book again. She saw that last week's strange numbers came only from orders made while Mark was alone. That did not mean he had taken anything. It did mean the shop needed a better rule. She wrote a note for Mr. Park: "Two people check every large box. Both write the number. No one must dig through a bag for a lost paper." She put the note by the door, ready for the morning. Small steps, she thought, can make a long road safer.

傍晚雪停了。莉娜再次翻閱小本子。她發現上週奇怪的數字都來自馬克獨自處理的訂單。這不代表他拿了任何東西，卻表示店裡需要更好的規則。她寫給朴先生一張便條：「每個大箱子由兩人核對，兩人都記下數字。不要再讓誰為了遺失的紙而翻找整個袋子。」她把便條放在門邊，等隔天早上。她心想，小小的步伐能讓漫長的道路更安全。
