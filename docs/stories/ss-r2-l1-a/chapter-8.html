公園裡的遊戲

The Game in the Park

打烊後，這間店由我作主 · 第 8 / 25 章 · 1000以下 · 英中對照 · 校訂中

用互動版查詞、朗讀與收藏

On Sunday, Ben asked Rina to walk through the park with his art group. The group had a simple game: one person stood behind a tree, and another tried to find that person in a photo. A girl in a blue dress was easy to see. A boy behind a large sign was not. "A picture can look clear, but still miss something," Ben said. Rina thought of Leo's video. It showed Mark and a box, but not what was inside it. She felt better about not showing it to everyone in town.

星期天，班邀莉娜和他的美術小組一起走過公園。小組玩一個簡單遊戲：一個人站在樹後，另一人試著在照片中找出那人。穿藍色洋裝的女孩很容易被看見，但躲在大告示牌後的男孩就看不到。「照片看起來清楚，也可能漏掉東西。」班說。莉娜想起里歐的影片。影片拍到馬克和箱子，卻沒拍到裡面裝什麼。她慶幸自己沒有把影片給全鎮的人看。

They took a break on a bench. A man sold beef sandwiches near the park gate. The food had a nice taste, and Rina gave one sandwich to Leo. "Why did you come?" she asked him. "I want to help the group," he said. "And I want to see if a camera can tell the whole story." A small bird began to fly over the grass. Leo took a picture. The bird looked like a ghost in the photo because it moved too fast. "There is our answer," he said. They all laughed.

他們在長椅上休息。一名男子在公園門口賣牛肉三明治。食物味道不錯，莉娜分了一份給里歐。「你怎麼來了？」她問。「我想幫小組，」他說，「也想看看相機能不能說出全部故事。」一隻小鳥飛過草地。里歐拍下照片，但鳥飛太快，在照片裡看起來像幽靈。「這就是答案了。」他說。大家都笑了。

An officer walked through the park and asked whether they were having fun. Rina said yes. She did not ask the officer to take sides in the shop problem. She had no proof of a crime. The officer told the group to stay on the path and keep the park clean. "Different people see different parts of a day," he said. "That is why we ask questions." Rina liked his words. She wrote them in her book. She wanted to be careful with other people's names, because one unfair story could hurt a heart for a long time.

一名警員走過公園，問他們玩得開不開心。莉娜說很開心。她沒有請警員介入店裡的問題，因為還沒有犯罪的證據。警員提醒他們留在小路上，並維持公園整潔。「不同的人看見一天中不同的部分，」他說，「所以我們才需要問問題。」莉娜喜歡這句話，記進了本子。她想謹慎對待別人的名字，因為一個不公平的故事可能長久傷人。

After the game, Leo showed a second picture. It was taken outside the store on the same day as the video by the river. The delivery truck had another name on its side. It was not the company that usually brought food to Morning Corner. "Now we have two things to ask about," Rina said. "A different truck and a box with an apple." Ben asked if that meant Mark was bad. "No," Rina replied. "It means we should ask him where the box came from." She said it with a calm voice.

遊戲結束後，里歐又給大家看第二張照片。那張照片是在河邊影片同一天、店外拍的。送貨車側面有另一家公司的名字，並不是平常送食品到「晨光角落」的公司。「現在有兩件事可以問了，」莉娜說，「不同的貨車，以及畫蘋果的箱子。」班問這是否代表馬克是壞人。「不是，」莉娜回答，「代表我們應該問他箱子從哪裡來。」她語氣平穩。

The group walked outside the park as the light began to fall. Rina thanked everyone for the game. She had learned something easy to forget: a photo was one view, not the whole world. She would not break a friendship or a job with one view. At home she put the new picture with her notes. She wrote a question beside it, not an answer. Then she called Mr. Park and asked for a meeting with Mark and the delivery driver. Mr. Park agreed to ask both people when they were free.

光線漸暗時，小組走出公園。莉娜謝謝大家一起玩。她學到一件很容易忘記的事：一張照片只是一個角度，不是整個世界。她不會因一個角度就破壞友誼或工作。回家後，她把新照片放進筆記，在旁邊寫下問題，而非答案。接著她打電話給朴先生，希望和馬克、送貨司機開會。朴先生答應詢問兩人何時有空。

In the park, two boys play baseball for a few minutes. One ball goes near Rina, but she stops it with her foot before it reaches the path. She walks over to give the ball back. "Thank you," one boy says. Rina asks the boys to play away from the busy path. They agree. Ben says that the small moment is like shop work: when people see a problem early, they can help before it hurts someone. Rina says that is exactly what she hopes to do.

在公園裡，兩個男孩玩了幾分鐘棒球。一顆球滾向莉娜，她在球到小路前用腳擋住，再走過去把球還回去。「謝謝。」一個男孩說。莉娜請他們遠離繁忙小路玩球，他們答應了。班說這一刻就像店裡的工作：人們若早點發現問題，就能在傷人前幫忙。莉娜說這正是她想做的事。

That night, Rina looked through the first eight pages of her book. The pages were different, but together they told her where to go. She could see the river, the road, the town square, and the park in her mind. Many kind people had helped without asking for a prize. She made a little list of their names, because she wanted to thank them when the shop was safe again. She was not sure when that day would come. But she was sure she would keep looking for it.

那晚，莉娜翻看本子的前八頁。每頁都不同，合在一起卻告訴她下一步該去哪裡。她腦中浮現河流、道路、廣場與公園。許多善良的人幫了忙，從不要求獎賞。她列了一小張名單，想在店裡重新安定時謝謝大家。她不確定那一天何時來，但她確定會繼續尋找。
