慢了一分鐘

One Minute Late

打烊後，這間店由我作主 · 第 7 / 25 章 · 1000以下 · 英中對照 · 校訂中

Rina woke up in her bedroom before her clock rang. She had a dream about a room full of papers. In the dream, each paper had a mouth and made a loud noise. She laughed when she opened her eyes. Real paper did not speak. But a time on a paper could tell a story. She went to the shop and looked at the delivery times. One page said seven thirty. Another said seven thirty-one. There was only one minute between them. Was that a true error, or did it matter? She did not know yet.

莉娜在鬧鐘響前就從臥室醒來。她夢見一間滿是紙張的房間，夢裡每張紙都有嘴巴，還發出很大的聲音。她睜開眼時笑了。真的紙不會說話，但紙上的時間可以告訴人一個故事。她去店裡查看送貨時間。一張寫七點半，另一張寫七點三十一分，中間只差一分鐘。那真是錯誤嗎？或者這一分鐘很重要？她還不知道。

Ben came as the shop opened. "I believe the small clock in the kitchen is slow," he said. Rina closed the door so they could hear it clearly. It made a soft sound every minute. The front clock was right, but the kitchen clock was two minutes late. "We have to test the times," Rina said. She did not want to say Mark had done something wrong because of a bad clock. They set both clocks to the same time and wrote it down. When Mr. Park arrived, Rina showed him exactly what they had changed.

店門一開，班就到了。「我認為廚房的小鐘慢了。」他說。莉娜關上門，好讓兩人聽清鐘聲。每分鐘都有很輕的聲響。前面的時鐘是準的，但廚房那個慢兩分鐘。「我們得先測試時間。」莉娜說。她不想因一個壞時鐘就指控馬克做錯事。他們把兩個鐘調到同一時間並記錄下來。朴先生到店時，莉娜完整告訴他修改了什麼。

Outside, the sky was blue after rain. A rainbow stood above the yard behind the shop. Ben said the colors reminded him of nature class. "A rich person may buy a big clock," he joked, "but a good clock must still show the right time." Rina was proud of his joke. She was also glad they had found a real problem. It did not bring the missing money back, but it stopped one mistake from becoming another. They needed to know whether other times in the book had been written by the front clock or the kitchen clock.

外面雨後的天空很藍，店後院上方出現一道彩虹。班說那些顏色讓他想起自然課。「有錢人可以買大時鐘，」他開玩笑說，「但好時鐘還是得報對時間。」莉娜為他的玩笑感到驕傲，也很高興找到一個真正的問題。這不能讓失去的錢回來，卻能阻止一個錯誤造成另一個錯誤。他們還得知道本子裡其他時間，是看前面的鐘還是廚房的鐘記下的。

Mark heard them talking and said, "You are making too much noise about a little thing." Rina asked him whether he had used the kitchen clock. He did not answer at first. Then he said, "Sometimes." Rina wrote that answer. "Thank you. That helps," she said. Mark looked surprised. He had been ready for a fight. Instead, Rina asked where the clock stood when he wrote the numbers. She wanted a map of the work. She did not want an angry talk that ended with everyone saying a different thing.

馬克聽見他們談話，便說：「你們為小事吵得太大聲。」莉娜問他有沒有看廚房的鐘。他一開始不回答，後來說：「有時候。」莉娜把這答案記下。「謝謝，這很有幫助。」她說。馬克看起來很意外。他原本準備吵架，莉娜卻問他記數字時鐘放在哪裡。她想畫出工作的全貌，而不是在一場怒氣沖沖、人人各說各話的對話中收場。

At the end of the day, Rina and Ben made a new page with three columns: what they knew, what they did not know, and what they could check. They wrote the slow clock under the first column. They wrote the missing money under the second. Under the third, they wrote "the bank page" and "the food orders." "Do you think this will work?" Ben asked. "I cannot be sure," Rina said. "But it will help us ask better questions." The bell over the door rang once. The day was done; the work of finding out was not.

一天結束時，莉娜和班做了一張三欄表：已知道的事、還不知道的事、可以查核的事。他們把慢掉的鐘寫在第一欄，失蹤的錢寫在第二欄，「銀行紀錄」與「食品訂單」寫在第三欄。「妳覺得這樣有用嗎？」班問。「我不能確定，」莉娜說，「但能幫我們問更好的問題。」門鈴響了一聲。這一天結束了，找出真相的工作還沒結束。

Just before they close the shop, Ben asks Rina to look at the two clocks once more. She says she will, and he gives a little laugh. "I used to think a clock was always right," he says. "Now I know I must check." Rina writes the new time below the old time. She does not erase the old number, because it shows what changed. Then she puts the pen away. A good page should tell the story of a correction, not make it disappear.

店裡快打烊時，班請莉娜再看一次兩個時鐘。莉娜答應了，班輕笑一聲。「我以前以為時鐘永遠正確，」他說，「現在知道也得檢查。」莉娜把新時間寫在舊時間下面。她沒有擦掉舊數字，因為它能顯示改動了什麼。接著她收起筆。好的紀錄應該說明更正過程，而不是讓修改消失。

When she went home, Rina looked at the rainbow one last time. It was already growing pale. She knew not every beautiful thing could stay. A good rule, however, could last after the people who made it went away. Tomorrow they would look at the bank page together, if Mr. Park agreed. She did not need to win an argument tonight. She only needed a clear place to begin again. She turned toward home and felt ready to sleep without another dream of loud papers.

回家路上，莉娜最後看了一眼彩虹。它已漸漸變淡。她知道美好的東西不一定能一直留下；但好的規則，即使訂規則的人離開，也可能繼續存在。若朴先生同意，明天他們就一起看銀行紀錄。今晚她不需要在爭論中獲勝，只需要找到明天清楚的起點。她走向家，覺得今晚終於可以好好睡，不再夢見吵鬧的紙張。
