The First Honest Runway

Attendance at Yitang's repaired fashion show was modest, but every seat had been reserved by someone who cared about the work. A corporation offered to cover the full budget in exchange for a controversial endorsement: the guests would be told that Meridian had created the designs. Yitang refused. She would not defy her own values merely because an expensive company wished to appear generous. When Zhiyan learned what had happened, he told his marketing director to withdraw the offer. The director complained that the overall perspective was too emotional. “The essence of a partnership,” Zhiyan replied, “is that both partners remain visible.” Yitang heard the exchange and felt relief mixed with irritation that such a basic truth still required a fight.

以棠重新舉辦服裝秀，出席人數不多，但每張座位都留給在乎作品的人。有家公司願意全額贊助，條件卻很有爭議：所有來賓必須相信設計是明曜創作的。以棠拒絕了；她不會因大企業想顯得慷慨，就違背自己的原則。知衍得知後要求行銷主管撤案。主管抱怨整體考量過於情緒化。知衍回答：「合作的本質，是雙方都能被看見。」以棠既鬆一口氣又生氣，因為這麼基本的道理竟還得爭取。

Behind the curtains, a model revealed a bruise on her shoulder from a poorly fitted strap. Yitang replaced the piece rather than hiding the mark with makeup. Her collection mixed contemporary cuts with fabric methods taught by older residents of the district. A local artist had offered an embroidered ritual pattern but requested that its ceremonial meaning be explained, not used as decoration without context. Yitang invited her to speak before the show. A production manager worried that the extra segment would delay the schedule. “Then we will begin later,” Yitang said. She wanted fashion that could facilitate real conversation, not simply display expensive outfits under bright light.

後臺裡，一名模特兒肩上因肩帶不合而出現瘀青。以棠沒有用粉底蓋住，而是直接換掉衣服。她的系列結合當代剪裁與老社區居民傳授的布藝。一名在地藝術家提供儀式刺繡圖案，但要求介紹其文化意義，不能只拿來裝飾。以棠請她在秀前發言。製作經理擔心多一個環節會延遲時間。「那就晚一點開始。」以棠說。她要的是促進真實交流的時尚，而不只是強光下的昂貴服飾。

A board representative began to contend that the studio's small scale made its success irrelevant. Yitang asked him to define success before dismissing hers. When he could not, she invited the workers to describe what a stable wage and visible credit meant to them. Their answers were neither grand nor expensive, but they changed the mood of the room.

一位董事開始主張，工作室規模小，成功也不值得一提。以棠請他先定義成功，再否定她的成果。對方答不上來，她便請工人說明穩定工資與清楚署名的意義。答案既不宏大也不昂貴，卻改變了現場氣氛。

A young resident from the apprentice housing program came to watch the first walk. She carried statistical notes showing that unpaid training forced many talented workers to leave design. Another guest argued that the figures were too small to matter. Zhiyan surprised them by asking to see the underlying surveys. He did not praise Yitang as a miracle worker or pretend the data solved everything. He offered to fund a separate study, with results published even if they reflected badly on Meridian. The irony was almost funny: the man called a controlling billionaire was learning to fund an answer he could not control. Yitang watched him more closely than she watched the cameras.

一位參加學徒住宅計畫的年輕住戶來看首場走秀，帶著統計筆記，顯示無薪培訓迫使許多有才華的工人離開設計界。另一名來賓嫌樣本太小。知衍反而要求查看原始調查。他沒有把以棠吹捧成奇蹟，也沒假裝數據解決一切，而是願意資助獨立研究，即使結果對明曜不利也公開。諷刺的是，被稱為控制狂的富豪，正在學習資助自己不能控制的答案。以棠比起看鏡頭，更常看向他。

The final dress appeared under a single warm light. It had no hidden logo, only the names of everyone who had made it embroidered along an inside seam. The audience began to applaud before the model reached the end of the runway. Yitang turned toward Aunt Luo, who was wiping her eyes, and bowed to the workers. Zhiyan remained standing at the rear, refusing to take the place reserved for the designer. Afterward, he asked whether he could offer her a flower. She said yes, but when he tried to order a hundred roses, she stopped him. “One is an offering,” she said. “A hundred is a public relations campaign.” He returned with one small white flower.

最後一件洋裝在暖色單燈下登場。衣服上沒有隱藏商標，只有所有參與製作者的名字，繡在內側接縫。模特兒尚未走到盡頭，觀眾就已鼓掌。以棠轉身向擦眼淚的羅姨和工人鞠躬。知衍站在後方，不坐設計師的位置。散場後，他問能否送她一朵花。她同意，卻在他準備訂一百朵玫瑰時阻止：「一朵是心意，一百朵是公關活動。」他最後帶來一朵小白花。

The show caused a transition in the way the public saw them. Some still preferred the old fairy tale of a rich rescuer and a grateful woman. Others noticed that Yitang had begun something long before meeting him, and that Zhiyan's best contribution was sometimes to step aside. A guest said the collection was a declaration of independence. Yitang disagreed. “Independence is not the absence of help,” she said. “It is being able to accept help without losing your voice.” Zhiyan heard the sentence and repeated it to himself later, in his otherwise empty apartment. He wondered when he had last said anything equally brave.

這場秀讓公眾的看法開始轉變。有人仍偏愛有錢救星與感恩女孩的童話，更多人卻看出，以棠在遇見他以前就已開始努力，而知衍最好的貢獻有時是退一步。一名來賓說這場秀是獨立宣言。以棠不同意：「獨立不是不接受幫助，而是接受幫助時仍能保有自己的聲音。」知衍後來在空蕩的公寓裡反覆想著這句話。他不禁問自己，上一次說出同樣勇敢的話是什麼時候。
