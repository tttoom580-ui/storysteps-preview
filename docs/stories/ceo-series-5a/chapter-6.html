The Chairman's Old Photograph

An abrupt invitation brought Yitang to a seminar about young entrepreneurs. The guest list included an activist who accused Meridian of using fashionable charity campaigns to hide an unfair labor system. Zhiyan carried a black briefcase rather than sending a representative. When the activist confronted him, he did not turn hostile. He said he had come to hear the complaint and would not pretend that one speech could repay a lifetime of poor decisions. Yitang was struck by his unusual patience, but she also noticed that his assistants were taking notes faster than anyone could speak. Listening was valuable only if it led to action.

一封突然的邀請讓以棠參加青年創業論壇。名單上有位社運人士，指控明曜用時尚慈善活動掩蓋不公平的勞動制度。知衍親自提著黑色公事包出席，沒有派代表。面對質問，他沒有敵意，只說自己是來聽抱怨的，不會假裝一場演講就能償還多年錯誤。以棠欣賞他的耐心，卻發現助理記筆記的速度比大家發言還快。傾聽若不能帶來行動，就沒有多少價值。

Afterward, an old photograph appeared in the foyer. It showed Zhiyan at twenty, thin and exhausted, standing beside a hospital bed. The caption described a frontier medical program that Meridian had supported before he became chairman. Yitang asked about it. His younger sister had required an injection every week during a difficult illness, and the nearest clinic had been several hours away. The family possessed money, yet money could not always buy timely care. “That is why I fund rural services,” he said. She wondered whether the obligation came from compassion or guilt. He replied that he had spent years trying to separate the two.

論壇後，大廳出現一張舊照片，二十歲的知衍瘦削疲憊，站在病床旁。說明文字提到明曜早年的邊境醫療計畫。以棠問起往事：知衍的妹妹曾因生病每週需要注射一次藥物，最近的診所卻在幾小時外。家裡有錢，也不總能及時買到照護。「所以我資助偏鄉醫療。」他說。以棠想知道這是出於同情還是愧疚；他回答，自己花了很多年都分不清楚。

A municipal official introduced them to a new training entity for workers who had been pushed into exile from the city by rent increases. The project included a pedestrian market, small studios, and an outlet where apprentices could sell their work directly. Zhiyan wanted to supervise the budget, while Yitang insisted that residents should supervise it with him. A lecturer dismissed her as naive and said strong leadership required a masculine hand. “Hands do not have a gender when they sign a fair contract,” she answered. The room fell quiet. Zhiyan's smile was brief but unmistakable, and it left her oddly warm.

市府官員向兩人介紹新的勞工培訓組織，服務因租金上漲而被迫離開城市的人。計畫包括行人市集、小型工作室，以及讓學徒直接販售作品的店面。知衍想監督預算，以棠則堅持居民也必須共同監督。一位講師笑她天真，說強勢領導需要男人的手腕。「簽公平合約的手沒有性別。」她回答。全場安靜下來，知衍短暫卻明顯地笑了，讓她心頭微暖。

During the break, Yitang found a photograph of an ordinary school dance in Zhiyan's old papers. He had attended it in a cheap suit and looked as awkward as everyone else. He admitted that he used to contemplate leaving the family business to repair motorcycles. Once, he had fallen from a bike and injured his thigh; his sister teased him for being dramatic about it. He spoke of her without presenting grief as a heroic achievement. Yitang told him he could still learn to repair a motorcycle. He answered that he had forgotten what a hobby felt like. “Then you need a teacher,” she said, “but you cannot buy one who will flatter you.”

休息時，以棠從知衍舊文件裡看到一張普通舞會照片。他穿著便宜西裝，和其他人一樣尷尬。他曾想過離開家族企業，去修理摩托車；有次摔車傷到大腿，妹妹還笑他太誇張。他談起妹妹時，沒有把悲傷塑造成英雄事蹟。以棠說現在也可以去學修車。知衍承認自己已忘記興趣是什麼感覺。「那你需要老師。」她說，「但別買一個只會拍馬屁的。」

A power failure disrupted the evening reception. Someone called the event a disaster, but the organizers had spare lights and a surplus of food prepared for the neighborhood. Yitang helped distribute meals instead of posing for the press. Zhiyan joined her, rolling up his sleeves and asking how many people remained outside. Nobody applauded when he carried the final crate. That suited her. Later, he asked whether the photograph of his sister had made her pity him. “No,” Yitang said. “It made you less mysterious.” He held her gaze as if the answer were more generous than he deserved.

傍晚接待會突然停電。有人說活動完了，主辦方卻有備用燈與多準備的食物，能分給鄰里。以棠不去拍照，反而幫忙發餐。知衍捲起袖子加入，詢問外面還有多少人。當他搬完最後一箱時，沒有人鼓掌；以棠反而覺得這樣很好。後來，他問妹妹的照片是否讓她可憐自己。「沒有。」以棠說，「只是讓你沒那麼神祕。」他望著她，彷彿這答案比自己應得的還溫柔。

On the journey home, neither tried to turn uncertainty into an easy promise. She had learned that a beautiful announcement could hide an ordinary problem, and he had learned that silence was not always kindness. They compared what each person actually knew, then marked the questions that still needed answers. Their hands touched while they folded the paper, but she did not pull away. He smiled only after she looked at him first.

回程時，兩人都沒有把不確定變成輕易的承諾。她知道漂亮的公告可能掩蓋平凡的問題；他也明白沉默不一定是體貼。他們先核對彼此確實知道的事情，再標記仍待解答的疑問。摺紙時兩人的手碰在一起，她沒有抽開。他等她先抬眼，才露出微笑。

A new message arrived after midnight, asking for a decision before morning. She read it twice, then left it unanswered until she had enough information. He had the resources to make a call that would change the whole schedule, yet he asked her what would happen to the people whose names were not printed on the invitation. For once, the most powerful person in the building was the one willing to wait.

午夜過後，一則新訊息要求她天亮前決定。她讀了兩遍，仍等到資訊足夠才答覆。他有能力一通電話改變所有時程，卻先問起那些名字沒有印在邀請函上的人會如何受影響。那一刻，整棟樓裡最有權力的人，反而是願意等待的那個人。
