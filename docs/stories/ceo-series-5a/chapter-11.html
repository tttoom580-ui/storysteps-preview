他學會等待的夜晚

The Night He Learned to Wait

Meridian's annual research reception took place at an old medical institution overlooking the river. Zhiyan had been asked to accelerate a funding decision after a veteran surgeon warned that a machine would soon fail. The problem was grim, but Yitang disliked the suggestion that urgency justified ignoring proper checks. She studied the recommendation from an independent technician, including a component that needed replacing and the limited time available to deploy a safe alternative. The board considered the details trivial compared with its publicity schedule. Yitang asked who would carry the stake if the machine failed after a rushed purchase. The room fell quiet, and Zhiyan supported the technical review.

明曜年度研究酒會在河邊一棟舊醫療機構舉辦。資深外科醫師警告設備即將故障，要求知衍加快撥款。情況嚴峻，以棠卻不接受「緊急」就可以跳過檢查。她閱讀獨立技師建議，確認要替換的零件，以及安全替代方案的部署時間。董事會嫌這些細節無關緊要，只想趕上宣傳行程。以棠問，若採購倉促導致設備失效，後果由誰承擔。會議安靜了下來，知衍支持進行技術審查。

During dinner, a researcher displayed a specimen of sustainable fiber that had undergone years of testing. The invention could help small studios thrive, but its specialty applications were expensive. Yitang complimented the scientist for explaining limitations as clearly as achievements. The scientist laughed and said most donors only wanted to hear a miracle. A petition from patients asked the foundation to remain neutral in a dispute between two suppliers. Zhiyan did not make a dramatic promise. He asked for a minimal emergency purchase while the longer review continued. It was a small, responsible decision, and Yitang felt unexpectedly proud of him.

晚餐時，研究員展示經過多年測試的永續纖維樣本。發明可能幫助小工作室發展，但特殊用途成本很高。以棠稱讚科學家同樣清楚說明限制與成果。科學家笑說多數捐助者只想聽奇蹟。病患連署請基金會在兩家供應商的爭議中保持中立。知衍沒有發表戲劇性的承諾，只決定先採購最低必要的緊急用量，其餘交給完整審查。那是個平凡而負責的決定，以棠竟感到驕傲。

Before the guests left, the surgeon explained that the new device would still need to undergo months of testing. A donor protested that the announcement would sound less exciting. Zhiyan replied that an exciting claim would not help a patient harmed by faulty equipment. Yitang looked at him with an affection she no longer bothered to deny to herself.

賓客離開前，外科醫師說明新設備仍須經過數月測試。一名捐助者抗議，認為這樣的公告不夠吸引人。知衍回應，誇張的宣傳救不了因故障設備受傷的病患。以棠望著他，已不想再對自己否認那份喜歡。

After the reception, fatigue settled over them. They stood under the covered steps while rain touched the garden. Yitang noticed Zhiyan's fingers quiver as he opened his umbrella. He admitted that his sister had once undergone a painful treatment at this institution, and that the smell of the corridors still returned him to that night. “You do not need to explain every part of your grief,” she said. He looked relieved. The world he inhabited treated personal weakness like a flaw in a public asset. Beside her, he could be afraid without an audience. He asked whether she wanted to walk or wait for a car. She chose to walk.

酒會後，疲勞籠罩兩人。他們站在雨廊下，看雨滴落進庭園。以棠發現知衍撐傘時手指微微顫抖。他承認妹妹曾在這家醫院接受痛苦治療，走廊的氣味至今仍讓他想起那晚。「你的悲傷不需要每個部分都向我解釋。」她說。他鬆了口氣。在他的世界裡，私人脆弱像公開資產的缺陷；站在她身邊，他可以不用給觀眾看，也能害怕。他問想散步還是叫車。她選了散步。

They stopped at a small bridge where the lamps were reflected in black water. Zhiyan had prepared a speech about the realm of business and the burden of responsibility, but he abandoned it halfway through. “I like talking to you when you tell me I am wrong,” he said. “That sounds like a terrible reason for romance,” Yitang answered. “Perhaps. I am new to it.” She wanted to laugh but felt the seriousness beneath his awkward words. She told him that affection was not a negotiation in which the stronger party won. He did not offer to buy her a future. He asked whether he might see her again on a day with no official agreement.

他們停在小橋上，燈光映入黑水。知衍原本準備一段關於商業領域與責任負擔的演說，說到一半卻放棄了。「你說我錯的時候，我反而喜歡和你說話。」他說。「這聽起來是最糟的戀愛理由。」以棠回答。「也許吧，我沒什麼經驗。」她想笑，卻聽出笨拙字句下的認真。她告訴他，感情不是強者獲勝的談判。他沒有說要買給她一個未來，只問若沒有正式協議，還能不能再見她。

Yitang did not answer at once. She thought of the studio she had built, the exhaustion of defending it, and the ordinary happiness of sharing a quiet walk. Finally she said she would have dinner with him next Friday, if they both could leave their work on time. “No driver?” he asked. “You may have a driver,” she said. “I am arriving on the subway.” He smiled as if she had offered him a gift of immense value. Then he accompanied her to the station, carrying the umbrella over both heads without trying to decide their direction.

以棠沒有立刻回答。她想到自己建立的工作室、捍衛它的疲憊，以及安靜並肩散步的平凡幸福。最後她說，下週五若兩人都能準時下班，就一起吃飯。「不用司機嗎？」他問。「你可以有司機。」她說，「我坐捷運來。」他笑得彷彿收到非常珍貴的禮物。接著他陪她到車站，替兩人撐傘，卻沒有替她決定要走哪條路。

On the journey home, neither tried to turn uncertainty into an easy promise. She had learned that a beautiful announcement could hide an ordinary problem, and he had learned that silence was not always kindness. They compared what each person actually knew, then marked the questions that still needed answers. Their hands touched while they folded the paper, but she did not pull away. He smiled only after she looked at him first.

回程時，兩人都沒有把不確定變成輕易的承諾。她知道漂亮的公告可能掩蓋平凡的問題；他也明白沉默不一定是體貼。他們先核對彼此確實知道的事情，再標記仍待解答的疑問。摺紙時兩人的手碰在一起，她沒有抽開。他等她先抬眼，才露出微笑。
