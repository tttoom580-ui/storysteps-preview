A Question No One May Demand

The rumor grew uglier when a tabloid suggested that Yitang had secretly arranged an abortion to protect her career. A second anonymous post implied that Meridian's medical charity used genetics research to influence private relationships. Both claims were ridiculous, and neither contained evidence. Yitang told Lin Yue that questions about reproductive health belonged to the individual, not to a headline. Zhiyan's lawyer advised against repeating the accusation in full, because doing so might give it new life. Yitang agreed. A brief correction would say only that the claims were unsupported and that private medical information was not public property.

八卦越傳越難聽，小報竟暗示以棠為了事業秘密墮胎；另一則匿名貼文又說明曜醫療慈善利用遺傳學研究操控私密關係。兩項指控都荒謬，也沒有證據。以棠告訴林玥，生殖健康的問題屬於當事人，不能任由標題索取。知衍的律師建議不要逐字重複指控，以免讓謠言再次擴散。以棠同意簡短澄清：傳言毫無根據，私人醫療資訊不是公共財產。

At the foundation clinic, an entrepreneur donated equipment for a genetics education program, and a biologist displayed models showing how a molecule could carry information in living cells. The exhibition was designed for patients making informed choices, not for gossip. Yitang visited with Zhiyan and noticed a bronze plaque honoring the donors. She suggested adding the nurses and counselors who sustained the service every day. A director called the initiative unnecessary. Zhiyan surprised him by agreeing immediately, then asking Yitang to lead the discussion with the staff. He was learning that generosity was not only about money, but about who was allowed to speak.

基金會診所裡，一名企業家捐贈設備推動遺傳學教育，生物學家用模型說明分子如何在細胞中傳遞訊息。展覽是為病患的知情選擇而設，不是八卦素材。以棠和知衍參觀時，看見感謝捐款者的銅牌，提議加上天天維持服務的護理師與諮詢員。主管嫌多餘，知衍卻立刻同意，還請以棠與員工討論。他正在學會，慷慨不只是給錢，還包括誰能發聲。

The clinic's new building had a glass dome above a vertical garden. Yitang liked the design but asked whether patients in wheelchairs could reach the viewing platform. The architect showed her the lift plan. A nurse added that the center's credibility depended on everyday access and honest counseling, not on the beauty of its entrance. Zhiyan agreed to publish accessibility checks alongside the photographs.

診所新大樓有玻璃圓頂與垂直花園。以棠喜歡設計，但追問輪椅使用者能否抵達觀景區。建築師拿出電梯規畫。護理師補充，中心的可信度來自日常無障礙使用與誠實諮詢，不是入口多漂亮。知衍同意把無障礙檢查與照片一起公開。

Zhiyan's mother arrived without warning. She was graceful, severe, and convinced that reputation mattered more than comfort. She accused Yitang of using the romance to obtain a mortgage for her studio. The accusation came from an arrogant investor who had previously tried to purchase Yitang's patterns cheaply. Yitang showed the bank records but refused to hand over every personal account. “You may dislike me,” she told the older woman, “but you do not get to audit my entire life.” Zhiyan stood beside her and said the family would not use her as a shield against its own public problems. His mother's expression hardened, but she had heard the answer.

知衍的母親突然來訪。她優雅嚴厲，認為名聲比舒適更重要。她指控以棠利用戀情取得工作室抵押貸款；說法來自一位曾想低價買走她圖樣的傲慢投資人。以棠出示銀行資料，卻拒絕交出所有私人帳戶。「妳可以不喜歡我，但沒有權利稽核我的整個人生。」她說。知衍站在她身旁，表示家族不會拿她當自身公關問題的盾牌。母親神色更冷，卻已聽見答案。

Later, a butcher at the community market asked why the charity continued spending on public health while other sectors needed money. Yitang listened to him describe his shop's rising costs and said those needs deserved attention too. She did not want one vulnerable group used to silence another. A wildlife educator passing through the market spoke about how protected habitats depended on stable communities. The connection was imperfect but sincere. Zhiyan realized that Yitang could integrate different concerns without pretending they were identical. He could negotiate a billion-dollar contract before lunch, yet she was teaching him how to have a conversation without assigning a winner.

後來，社區市場的肉販問基金會為何一直投入公共衛生，其他產業也缺錢。以棠聽他說店鋪成本上漲，回應這些需求也值得重視；她不願讓一群弱勢者壓過另一群的聲音。路過的野生動物教育者提到，棲地保育也依賴穩定社區。類比未必完美，卻很真誠。知衍發現以棠能整合不同需求，不假裝它們毫無差異。他中午前就能談成十億合約，她卻在教他如何不必每次對話都選出贏家。

Before leaving, Yitang met a volunteer who wanted to testify about misleading advertising at the clinic. The woman feared retaliation, so Yitang recommended an independent channel rather than asking her to speak on camera. That evening, Zhiyan said he believed his mother had tried to betray his trust by spreading an unfounded accusation. Yitang said the family relationship was his to manage, not hers to repair. He looked tired, almost lonely. She touched his hand and said she did not need him to choose between every person he loved. She needed him to choose honest behavior when choices became difficult. He nodded, understanding the difference.

離開前，以棠遇到想揭露診所誤導性廣告的志工。對方害怕報復，以棠便建議使用獨立申訴管道，而不是立刻上鏡頭。當晚，知衍說母親散布無根據的指控，是背叛了他的信任。以棠回答，家庭關係由他自己處理，不該由她修復。他看起來疲憊而孤獨。她輕碰他的手說，她不需要他每次都在所愛的人之間選邊；她只需要他在艱難時選擇誠實行事。他點頭，聽懂了分別。

After the guests left, she stayed with the unfinished garment instead of following the cameras. The last pin was crooked, and the seam was slightly uneven, but no one had asked her to surrender her name to make the work appear perfect. She fixed the seam herself. Across the room, he put down his telephone and waited for her to decide whether she wanted company. This small courtesy unsettled her more than his expensive gifts could have done.

賓客離開後，她留在未完成的衣服旁，沒有追隨鏡頭。最後一根別針歪了，接縫也略不平整，但沒有人要她為了作品看似完美而交出姓名。她親手修好接縫。房間另一端，他放下電話，等她決定是否需要陪伴。這份小小的尊重，比昂貴禮物更令她心動。
