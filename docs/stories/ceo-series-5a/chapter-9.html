The Photograph at Midnight

An anonymous account posted a picture of Yitang embracing a former classmate outside an airport lounge. The caption suggested betrayal and claimed she had exchanged information during a private business bid. The photograph was real, but the story was not. The classmate, a young woman named Su Lan, had just returned from an expedition to study traditional dyes. Yitang had embraced her in relief after months apart. A commentator turned the gesture into a scandal, and another account tried to snatch a designer bag from the image as evidence of an extravagant gift. Yitang refused to answer insults about her friendships as though they were charges in a court.

匿名帳號貼出以棠在機場貴賓室外擁抱舊同學的照片，暗示她背叛知衍，還說她在私人競標時交換商業情報。照片是真的，故事卻是假的。對方是剛從傳統染料考察回來的女同學蘇蘭，兩人數月未見，見面就高興擁抱。評論者把動作炒成醜聞，另一個帳號甚至把照片裡的設計師包當成昂貴贈禮證據。以棠不願像在法庭上受審一樣，為自己的友誼辯解。

Zhiyan saw the rumor during a directors' dinner. His first instinct was to send his security team to the rear entrance of Yitang's studio and prevent reporters from approaching. His assistant warned that a compulsory escort could make her feel trapped. Zhiyan stopped. He phoned Yitang, who was sitting in a quiet corner of her shop, and asked if she felt safe. “I am angry, not helpless,” she answered. He apologized for assuming that fear would be her first reaction. She asked him to help verify the source of the picture rather than act like a jealous guard. He agreed and sent the technical records directly to her lawyer.

知衍在董事晚餐會上看見流言。第一個反應是派保全封住以棠工作室後門，阻擋記者。助理提醒，強制護送可能讓她覺得被囚禁，他便停手。他打電話給坐在店裡的以棠，問她安不安全。「我很生氣，但我不是無助。」她說。知衍為自己預設她會恐懼而道歉。她要求查照片來源，不要他扮成吃醋保鑣。他同意，並把技術紀錄直接交給她的律師。

Su Lan had always been Yitang's ally. The airport photograph showed an embrace outside the glass exterior of the lounge, not a secret transaction. A stranger had shouted an insult that felt like a verbal assault, and Su Lan had helped Yitang step away. When their suitcase fell, both women had to squat to gather scattered papers. The photograph omitted that moment, making the meeting appear more mysterious than it was.

蘇蘭一直是以棠的盟友。機場照片拍到的是貴賓室玻璃外牆旁的擁抱，不是秘密交易。當時陌生人的侮辱幾乎像言語攻擊，蘇蘭幫以棠離開。手提箱掉落後，兩人還蹲下來撿散落文件。照片剪掉那一刻，讓平凡會面看似神祕。

A security analyst traced the posting to a server that had also promoted a failed bid for Meridian's property near an old cemetery. The connection did not prove that the bidder ordered the rumor. Lin Yue, the journalist, cautioned against treating a pattern as a confession. She explained that a manifest of advertising purchases might show who had funded the messages, but that evidence would need independent review. Yitang felt as if she were staring at a galaxy of clues with no clear path between them. Zhiyan tried to make a joke about hiring a detective from a crime novel. It was a terrible joke, but she smiled at the effort.

資安分析師查出，發文伺服器也曾宣傳明曜一塊舊墓園旁土地的失敗競標。但這種關聯不能證明競標者下令造謠。林玥提醒，不要把模式當成自白。廣告購買清單或許能指出誰出資，但仍需獨立審核。以棠覺得自己面前像展開一片線索銀河，看不到清楚路徑。知衍試著開玩笑，說可以找推理小說裡的偵探。笑話很爛，她卻因他的努力笑了。

A charity banquet that evening offered tuna canapes, mint tea, and a premier auction of antique watches. Yitang went only because the funds supported citizenship classes for migrant workers. She wore a simple dress and refused to let gossip distract her from the event. Near the entrance, a man crouched to collect spilled coins; the smell of spoiled food made the corridor stink. Zhiyan squatted to help him and missed the photographers' first call. When he finally joined Yitang, he said that the world's richest rooms often needed the most ordinary kindness. She could tell he was trying not to sound like a speechwriter.

當晚的慈善宴會提供鮪魚點心、薄荷茶，還有高級古董錶拍賣。以棠只因款項用於移工公民課程才去。她穿著簡單洋裝，不讓八卦分散自己對活動的注意力。入口有人蹲下撿灑落的零錢，走廊還有腐敗食物的臭味。知衍跟著蹲下幫忙，因此錯過攝影師第一次叫他合照。走回以棠身邊時，他說最有錢的房間往往最需要普通的善意。她聽得出他正努力不像公關稿。

When the auction ended, Su Lan called by video. She offered to make a public statement, but Yitang asked whether she truly wished to invite attention into her own life. Su Lan said she was willing to state the simple facts, nothing more. They agreed on two sentences about the airport meeting and no details about her research. Zhiyan watched Yitang set the boundary and understood why he had begun to admire her. She protected other people even when her own reputation was being attacked. Later, alone in the elevator, he asked if he could hold her hand. This time she said yes.

拍賣結束後，蘇蘭視訊來電，表示願意公開澄清。以棠先問她是否真想把注意力引進自己生活。蘇蘭說只願陳述簡單事實，不提供研究細節。兩人決定用兩句話交代機場見面。知衍看著她設下界線，終於明白自己為何越來越欣賞她：即使名譽遭攻擊，她仍會保護他人。後來電梯裡只剩兩人，他問可不可以牽她的手。這次，她說可以。

At the end of the day, she wrote three sentences in her private notebook: what she had decided, what remained beyond her control, and whom she could trust to tell the truth when it hurt. The answers changed from chapter to chapter, but the questions remained useful. She put the notebook away before seeing him. Their next conversation would not be a performance for shareholders. It would belong to the two of them.

一天結束時，她在私人筆記本寫下三句話：今天作了什麼決定、哪些事情仍不由她控制、痛苦時誰還肯說真話。答案會隨經歷改變，問題卻始終重要。見他之前，她收起筆記本。接下來的談話不是演給股東看的表演，只屬於他們兩人。
