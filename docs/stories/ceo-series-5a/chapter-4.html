The Driveway Full of Reporters

On the morning of their first public appearance, reporters filled the driveway of Meridian House. A civic group had invited Yitang to speak about affordable housing for apprentices, an issue that mattered to her because her first workshop had been an unheated room over a bakery. Yet her stylist delivered an outfit that looked more appropriate for a royal wedding than a community forum. Yitang sent it back and chose her own jacket. Zhiyan watched her step out of the car with a noticeable smile. A reporter shouted that the chairman had made her rich. She answered that an innovative scholarship was worth more than a diamond necklace if it let young workers pay rent and keep their jobs.

第一次公開露面當天，明曜大樓車道擠滿記者。一個公民團體邀請以棠談設計學徒可負擔住宅；她自己第一間工作室就是麵包店樓上沒有暖氣的小房間，因此特別在意。造型師送來一套像皇室婚禮的服裝，她退回去，穿自己的外套。知衍看她下車時露出明顯笑意。記者大喊是董事長讓她變有錢。她回答，若創新獎學金能讓年輕工人付房租、保住工作，那比鑽石項鍊更有價值。

The forum began with boredom: long speeches, expensive slides, and a freight company promising generous transport that never arrived. Yitang spoke with a cook whose son needed medication and a seamstress who traveled two hours each way. Their accounts turned policy into something concrete. An eloquent director tried to plead that development must take time. Yitang agreed, then asked him why the housing guideline excluded workers with irregular schedules. He had no answer. Zhiyan stepped forward as if to rescue the discussion, but she touched his sleeve and shook her head. She wanted to ask the next question herself. He sat down without taking offense.

論壇起初令人無聊：冗長致詞、昂貴簡報，以及一家口頭承諾載運卻從未到場的貨運公司。以棠和兒子需要藥物的廚師、每天通勤四小時的裁縫交談，政策因此變得具體。一位能言善道的主管懇求大家理解開發需要時間。以棠同意，接著問為何住宅準則排除工時不固定的勞工。對方答不上來。知衍本要起身替她解圍，以棠輕碰他的袖口搖頭，她想自己問下一題。他沒有不悅，坐了回去。

After the student discussion, a teacher asked Yitang whether morality belonged in professional training. “Of course,” Yitang answered, “but it must be visible in schedules, wages, and safety rules, not only on inspirational posters.” The teacher wrote that sentence on the board. Zhiyan quietly photographed it, then asked the teacher's permission before sharing it with his staff.

學生討論後，一名老師問以棠，道德是否屬於專業訓練的一部分。「當然。」她說，「但它應該出現在班表、工資與安全規定裡，而不是只寫在勵志海報上。」老師把話寫在黑板上。知衍先拍下來，再詢問老師能否分享給公司員工。

At lunch, a chef served soup in a small tin bowl and warned them the food was still hot. A young volunteer became pale from a sudden smell and ran outside to vomit. Rumors spread immediately, some cruelly claiming she was pregnant. Yitang went after her with water, and the volunteer explained that she suffered from severe motion sickness. Zhiyan told his press secretary that neither the girl's health nor her private life belonged in the event summary. A complaint about the catering could wait until there was evidence. “You cannot prolong a crisis just to make yourself look protective,” Yitang told him. He accepted the correction without argument.

午餐時，廚師用小錫碗盛湯，提醒大家小心燙。一位年輕志工聞到氣味突然臉色發白，衝出去嘔吐。謠言立刻傳開，還有人惡意猜她懷孕。以棠拿水跟出去，志工說自己只是嚴重暈車。知衍告訴公關秘書，不可把她的健康或私生活寫進活動摘要；對餐點的抱怨要等有證據再處理。「你不能為了顯得會保護人，就延長一場危機。」以棠提醒他。他沒有反駁。

That afternoon, a design exhibition presented models of dormitories made from recycled material. One project proposed a narrow tower, but Yitang showed how its stairwell would intensify crowding and despair in an emergency. She argued that good design had a moral dimension: a beautiful building should not force residents to live without dignity. A student asked if her confidence was a natural trait. Yitang laughed. “I practiced speaking while washing dishes. I still get frightened.” The answer seemed to stimulate more questions than the polished advertisements had done. Zhiyan noticed the students listening to her rather than looking at him.

下午的展覽展示以回收材料製作的宿舍模型。一件作品提議蓋狹長高樓，以棠指出它的樓梯間在緊急時會加劇擁擠與絕望。她說好設計有道德面向，美麗建築不該剝奪住戶尊嚴。學生問她自信是不是天生特質。她笑說：「我洗碗時就練習演講，到現在還是會害怕。」這比精美廣告引出更多問題。知衍注意到，學生們都在聽她說話，而不是看他。

Rain began as the forum ended. A gust sent several display boards across the pavement, and a driver complained about scratches on the windshield. Zhiyan asked the staff to move the boards before anyone could be hurt. In the scramble, Yitang saw him holding the door for the same director who had dismissed her questions. That small act complicated the story she wanted to tell about him. He was ruthless in negotiation and surprisingly patient with embarrassment. She still did not know whether such a man could love without trying to lead. For the first time, she admitted she wanted to find out.

論壇結束時下起雨，強風吹倒幾塊展示板，司機抱怨擋風玻璃被刮傷。知衍先叫工作人員搬走木板，免得有人受傷。混亂中，以棠看到他替剛才輕視她問題的主管扶門。這個小動作讓她原本對他的評價變得複雜：他談判強硬，面對尷尬卻意外有耐心。她還不確定這種男人能否不控制別人地去愛。但她第一次承認，自己想知道答案。

At the end of the day, she wrote three sentences in her private notebook: what she had decided, what remained beyond her control, and whom she could trust to tell the truth when it hurt. The answers changed from chapter to chapter, but the questions remained useful. She put the notebook away before seeing him. Their next conversation would not be a performance for shareholders. It would belong to the two of them.

一天結束時，她在私人筆記本寫下三句話：今天作了什麼決定、哪些事情仍不由她控制、痛苦時誰還肯說真話。答案會隨經歷改變，問題卻始終重要。見他之前，她收起筆記本。接下來的談話不是演給股東看的表演，只屬於他們兩人。
