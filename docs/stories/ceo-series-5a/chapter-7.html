A Pattern That Was Not for Sale

總裁的合約，買不到我的名字 · 第 7 / 25 章 · 4000–5000 · 英中對照 · 校訂中

A controversy arose when Meridian's sports league announced uniforms bearing an embroidered flower that belonged to a rural craft community. The design had appeared in a public exhibition, but that did not grant the company custody of its meaning. An executive called it a harmless tribute. Yitang explained why it was not: the community's elders had declined commercial reproduction, and somebody had disconnected the refusal from the final approval file. She asked the league to stop production while the source was checked. Zhiyan agreed, even though thousands of garments had already been ordered and the delay would be expensive.

明曜旗下運動聯盟宣布新制服時引發爭議：服裝上有一朵刺繡花，圖樣屬於某個偏鄉工藝社群。它雖曾在公開展覽出現，卻不代表公司擁有其文化意義。主管稱那只是無害致敬。以棠解釋問題何在：長者早已拒絕商業複製，但這份拒絕意見被人從最後核准檔中刪掉了。她要求停產，直到查清來源。知衍同意，即使上千件服裝已下訂，延誤成本很高。

Their inquiry led to a corridor filled with archived contracts. A purchasing officer had derived permission from an old photograph rather than written consent. Another employee, trying to protect his job, had altered the record. A rumor accused him of being corrupt, but Yitang refused to make a judgment without hearing his account. She requested an independent specialist in community rights and a translator. The village representative arrived carrying a sturdy folder of letters. “Our expertise is not a decoration,” she said. “It is indispensable knowledge.” Zhiyan invited her to address the board directly, and the director who had dismissed the issue began to look uncomfortable.

調查把他們帶到堆滿舊合約的走廊。採購員把老照片當成授權證據，另一名員工為了保住工作，改動了紀錄。流言指他貪腐，以棠卻拒絕在沒聽當事人說明前下判斷。她要求獨立的社群權益專家與翻譯員。村落代表抱著厚實文件夾到場：「我們的專業不是裝飾，而是不可或缺的知識。」知衍讓她直接向董事會發言，原本輕視此事的董事開始不安。

The cancelled uniforms had been intended for a youth tournament with a food festival featuring village cuisine. The organizers feared that the dispute would arouse resentment between the athletes and the craftspeople. Yitang explained that a person could not derive permission from a photograph alone. A nurse at the event added that even a small wound could infect an athlete's skin if equipment was shared carelessly. The conversation reminded everyone that good events required preparation and consent, not simply cheerful posters.

遭取消的制服原本是青年錦標賽用服，活動還安排展示村落料理的美食節。主辦方擔心爭議會引起運動員與工藝家之間的不滿。以棠解釋，不能只憑照片推導出授權。活動護理師也提醒，共用裝備若不注意清潔，小傷口也可能受感染。這場談話讓大家理解，好活動需要準備與同意，不只是歡樂海報。

During the review, someone joked that a modern fashion business should behave like a pirate and seize attractive ideas before competitors could. The joke aroused anger in the room. Yitang answered that fashion could not thrive on the removal of other people's rights. She showed an array of sample fabrics and offered a genuine alternative: a new collection developed with paid artists who kept ownership of their original symbols. That would require different infrastructure, including a clear licensing process, fair payments to each recipient, and a way to withdraw permission. It would take longer, but she believed it was worthwhile.

審查時，有人開玩笑說現代時尚企業該像海盜，先搶走好點子。這句玩笑激怒了在場的人。以棠回答，時尚不能靠剝奪別人的權利繁榮。她展示一排布樣，提出真正的替代方式：付費和工藝家共同開發系列，原創符號的權利仍留在作者手中。這需要不同的授權制度、公平付款方式，以及撤回許可的機制。花的時間更多，但她認為值得。

That night, Zhiyan found her sitting alone on a stairwell. She was tired of explaining basic respect to people who profited from ignoring it. He offered to dismiss everyone involved. She shook her head. “If you turn every mistake into an enemy, no one will tell you the truth.” He admitted that his instinct was to act faster than he thought. She asked him to speak first with the affected artists and employees. He waited beside her without demanding forgiveness. As the cleaners passed, he pulled his coat from his shoulders and asked whether she wanted it. She said yes, surprised that such a small question could mean so much.

當晚，知衍在樓梯間找到獨坐的以棠。她厭倦了向靠忽視別人賺錢的人解釋基本尊重。他提議開除相關員工，她搖頭：「如果每個犯錯的人都是敵人，就沒人敢告訴你真相。」他承認自己習慣先行動再思考。她請他先與受影響的工藝家和員工談談。他沒有要求她原諒，只安靜陪著。清潔人員經過時，他脫下外套問她要不要。她說好，意外發現這麼小的詢問竟如此重要。

Two weeks later, the league released a revised uniform without the disputed symbol. The village artists chose whether to join the new project; some accepted, some declined. Both answers were recorded without punishment. Yitang stood beside the representative during the announcement, while Zhiyan remained in the back row. The episode did not repair every harm, yet it proved the company could learn. When Yitang thanked him for respecting the decision, he corrected her gently. “You made the decision possible. I only stopped standing in the way.” She almost took his hand, then remembered the cameras. He seemed to understand and did not reach for hers.

兩週後，聯盟發表不含爭議符號的新制服。村落藝術家可自行決定是否參與後續計畫；有人接受，有人拒絕，兩種答案都被尊重。以棠站在代表旁邊，知衍留在後排。這件事還不能彌補所有傷害，至少證明公司有能力學習。以棠感謝他尊重決定，他溫和糾正：「是你讓決定有可能，我只是停止擋路。」她差點牽住他的手，卻想起現場鏡頭。他似乎懂了，沒有主動伸手。

A new message arrived after midnight, asking for a decision before morning. She read it twice, then left it unanswered until she had enough information. He had the resources to make a call that would change the whole schedule, yet he asked her what would happen to the people whose names were not printed on the invitation. For once, the most powerful person in the building was the one willing to wait.

午夜過後，一則新訊息要求她天亮前決定。她讀了兩遍，仍等到資訊足夠才答覆。他有能力一通電話改變所有時程，卻先問起那些名字沒有印在邀請函上的人會如何受影響。那一刻，整棟樓裡最有權力的人，反而是願意等待的那個人。
