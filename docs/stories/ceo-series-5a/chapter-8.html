A Journey Without a Camera

Yitang traveled with a small delegation to inspect a textile workshop near the sea. The factory advertised ethical production and charged a premium for its cloth, but the paperwork was confusing. A contractor insisted that every chore had been completed, while workers described long hours in the interior rooms with poor ventilation. Yitang asked for clarity rather than attractive slogans. Zhiyan came as an observer, not as the delegation leader. When he began questioning a worker in rapid technical language, Yitang touched the table and asked him to slow down. “Fluency is not the same as understanding,” she said. He apologized and let the worker finish.

以棠跟著小型代表團去海邊檢查紡織工坊。工廠標榜道德生產，布料價格也較高，文件卻混亂。承包商堅稱所有作業都已完成，工人卻描述內部房間工時長、通風差。以棠要求清楚說明，不要漂亮口號。知衍這次只是觀察員，不是領隊。他用快速的技術術語追問工人時，以棠輕敲桌面請他放慢：「說得流利，不等於別人就懂。」他道歉，讓工人完整說完。

On the second day, the group conducted an exploration of a nearby dye facility. Its waste report claimed that a special appliance could generate clean water from the used liquid, but the equipment had no current patent and the demonstration was incomplete. Yitang refused to make the single successful test the sole basis for approval. The engineer seemed offended, then admitted that the trial had been performed under unusually favorable conditions. Zhiyan asked the engineer to shed his fear of disappointing investors and report the limits openly. Yitang was glad the chairman could protect an honest answer rather than purchase a more flattering one.

第二天，大家參觀附近染料處理廠。報告宣稱某種特殊設備能從廢液產生乾淨的水，但裝置沒有現行專利，示範也不完整。以棠拒絕只憑一次成功試驗核准。工程師起初不高興，後來承認測試條件特別理想。知衍請他放下讓投資人失望的恐懼，公開說明限制。以棠很高興看到董事長願意保護真實答案，而不是買一個比較好聽的。

Before approving the next supplier, Yitang wrote a note headed caution. She wanted workers to know how to report unsafe conditions without risking their jobs. The factory owner accepted the procedure reluctantly, but a union adviser explained that early reports could prevent costly accidents. Zhiyan said he had learned to regard a careful warning as useful information rather than personal criticism.

核准下一家供應商前，以棠在筆記上寫下「謹慎」。她希望工人能在不冒失業風險的情況下回報不安全環境。廠主勉強接受，工會顧問則解釋，早期通報可以預防昂貴事故。知衍說自己學會了把審慎提醒當成有用資訊，而不是人身批評。

That evening, a university undergraduate joined their dinner and spoke about a local superstition: if two strangers walked the shore at dawn, they would become lovers before winter. Zhiyan smiled at the story. Yitang accused him of trying to steer the conversation toward their false engagement. He denied it, then asked what she believed instead. “I believe people become close by watching how they behave when no one is applauding.” The student laughed, but Zhiyan seemed to take the answer seriously. He walked with Yitang along the beach while the others returned to the hotel. They kept enough distance for her to choose the path beside him freely.

晚餐時，一名大學生說起地方迷信：若兩個陌生人黎明時走在海邊，冬天前就會相愛。知衍聽了微笑，以棠說他想把話題帶向假訂婚。他否認，接著問她相信什麼。「我相信人是看見對方在沒人鼓掌時怎麼做，才慢慢靠近的。」學生笑了，知衍卻聽得認真。眾人回旅館後，他與以棠沿海散步，始終保留足夠距離，讓她自由選擇是否同行。

The inspection concluded with a document requiring the factory to dedicate part of its budget to safer ventilation. The owner called the requirement dreadful and argued it would hurt sales. Workers replied that ordinary safety should not be a luxury commodity. Yitang agreed to a temporary purchasing pause, not an instant permanent ban. The decision created discomfort for everyone, including her own studio. Yet she had asked other people to respect informed consent; now she had to follow the same principle when it cost her money. Zhiyan offered his firm's lawyers for free. She accepted their technical explanation but paid the fee from her own account.

檢查結果要求工坊撥出一部分預算改善通風。老闆抱怨條件可怕，會損失營收；工人則回應基本安全不應是奢侈商品。以棠決定暫停採購，而非立刻永久禁購。這讓所有人，包括她自己的工作室，都不方便。但既然她要求別人尊重知情同意，自己也必須在有成本時遵守同樣原則。知衍提出免費提供公司律師，她接受專業說明，卻堅持從自己的帳戶付費。

Their final stop was a small community venue where women displayed weaving techniques passed down through several generations. Yitang bought a scarf for Aunt Luo and asked permission before photographing the maker. Zhiyan stood beside a shelf of old instruments, looking more interested than he ever did during a board reception. “You look different when you do not have to win,” Yitang told him. “So do you,” he replied. Neither reached for the other's hand. The evening felt more intimate because no contract required them to pretend. When their train departed, the city lights grew smaller and the unfinished question between them grew larger.

最後一站是小型社區展場，女性工藝家展示數代相傳的織法。以棠買條圍巾送羅姨，拍攝作者前也先徵求同意。知衍站在舊工具架旁，比他在董事會酒會裡更有興致。「你不用贏的時候，看起來很不一樣。」以棠說。「你也是。」他回道。兩人沒有牽手。正因合約沒要求他們演戲，傍晚反而更親密。火車離站，城市燈光漸遠，兩人之間未說破的問題卻越來越大。

The following morning gave them no simple victory. A clerk still wanted another signature, and a rumor still crossed the city faster than a correction. She made copies of the relevant pages, signed only the portion she understood, and kept her own original. When he offered a car, she chose to walk the first block. He walked beside her without asking whether the choice was sensible. It was simply hers.

隔天早上沒有輕鬆的勝利。職員仍要求另一個簽名，謠言傳遍城市的速度仍比澄清快。她複印相關頁面，只簽下自己理解的部分，並保留正本。他提議派車，她選擇先走一個街口。他沒有質問這個決定是否明智，只是陪她走。那是她自己的選擇。
