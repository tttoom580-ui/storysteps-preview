假的緊急事件

The False Emergency

總裁的合約，買不到我的名字 · 第 12 / 25 章 · 4000–5000 · 英中對照 · 校訂中

A late-night call claimed that Yitang's assistant had been kidnapped and held hostage in a warehouse. The voice was distorted, the script sounded rehearsed, and the caller demanded access to Meridian's stock records. Yitang felt an abnormal sensation of fear, but she refused to follow instructions alone. She called the assistant's family, then the emergency service, and learned that her colleague was safe at home. The threat was a fraud. Zhiyan arrived as the police documented the call. He wanted to confront the caller immediately, but the officer asked everyone to preserve the original recording. Yitang understood that anger could not replace careful evidence.

深夜一通電話聲稱以棠的助理遭綁架，正被當成人質關在倉庫，並要求交出明曜股票紀錄。聲音經過變造，臺詞像背過，讓她感到異常恐懼。但她沒有獨自照辦，而是聯絡助理家人與緊急服務，確認同事其實平安在家。威脅是一場詐騙。警方紀錄電話時，知衍趕到現場，想立刻找出來電者。警員請大家先保存原始錄音。以棠明白，憤怒不能替代證據。

The next day, an observer from a consumer agency explained that similar schemes used digital voices and false documents. The attackers often pretended to belong to a continental security network. Their language borrowed words from international law, but their demands were incompatible with legitimate procedures. Yitang asked the agency to acquaint her staff with common warning signs. A worker mumbled that only careless people were deceived. The observer corrected him: a criminal could exploit anyone's fear, and blaming victims made reporting less likely. Zhiyan listened while the staff discussed their own experiences. He could not purchase a world without danger, but he could help make one safer.

隔天，消費者保護單位的觀察員解釋，類似手法會利用變造聲音和假文件，冒充跨洲安全網路。詐騙者借用國際法律術語，要求卻完全不符合法程序。以棠請機構教員工辨認警訊。一名工人咕噥說只有粗心的人會受騙。觀察員糾正他：犯罪者能利用任何人的恐懼，責怪受害者只會讓人不敢報案。知衍聽員工談自己的經驗。他買不到沒有危險的世界，至少能幫忙建立更安全的環境。

The officer clarified that a false claim about a kidnap was still a serious crime even when no one had been taken. The suspect had borrowed terms from a missionary relief organization to sound credible, but the language was not compatible with the agency's actual rules. A frightened intern began to mumble an apology for believing the call. Yitang told her that reporting suspicious messages quickly was more helpful than feeling ashamed.

警員澄清，即使沒有人真的被綁走，虛構綁架的威脅仍是嚴重犯罪。嫌犯借用傳教救援組織的術語增加可信度，內容卻與機構真正規定不相容。一名嚇壞的實習生低聲咕噥著道歉，因為自己差點相信電話。以棠告訴她，快速通報可疑訊息比自責更有用。

Zhiyan's chief of security suggested a temporary policy that would bolt every studio door and require guards for all visitors. Yitang rejected the proposal. The industry norm, she argued, should balance safety with freedom; a workshop was not a prison. She also noticed that migrant workers faced discrimination when asked to prove their identity far more often than local employees. “You cannot protect one person by turning everyone else into a suspect,” she said. Zhiyan asked the officer to design a viable alternative, including an emergency contact tree, visitor records, and the right to decline a private meeting. The team produced a practical plan by evening.

知衍的保全主管提議暫時用螺栓鎖住每個工作室出入口，所有訪客都要由保全陪同。以棠否決，認為業界常規應平衡安全與自由，工作室不是監獄。她也發現移工比本地員工更常被要求查驗身分，構成歧視。「不能為了保護一個人，把其他人都當嫌犯。」她說。知衍請主管提出可行替代方案，包含緊急聯絡網、訪客紀錄和拒絕單獨會面的權利。團隊當晚完成了實用計畫。

An unrelated exhibition at the city museum provided an uncomfortable reminder of the past. Its display described slavery in early shipping routes and the work of missionaries who had helped document the abuses. Yitang had come to choose fabric references, but the subject kept her there. Power without accountability had always found elegant words to disguise harm. Zhiyan stood beside her, silent. He said his company could never claim to be innocent merely because it used contemporary vocabulary. When she asked whether he believed himself a traitor to the business traditions of his family, he answered that refusing an injustice was not betrayal.

市立博物館的一場展覽提醒他們，權力濫用從不是新事。展品介紹早期航運中的奴役制度，以及記錄暴行的傳教士工作。以棠原本只是來看布料參考，卻為此停留許久。沒有問責的權力總能用好聽的話掩飾傷害。知衍沉默站在旁邊，說公司不能只因使用現代語彙就自認清白。以棠問他是否覺得自己背叛家族的經商傳統。他回答，拒絕不義不是背叛。

That evening, the police sent a short report: the false emergency had been traced to an internet account, but the person behind it remained unknown. Yitang was relieved and frustrated at once. A television viewer might expect a dramatic arrest, yet real investigations needed patience. Zhiyan offered to keep a car outside her home. She declined and asked him to walk her to the station instead. He accepted without arguing. At the entrance, she said she was grateful that he had come when she called. “I came because you asked,” he replied. “I will leave when you ask, too.” It was the promise she wanted to hear.

晚上警方送來簡短報告：假緊急電話來自某個網路帳號，背後身分仍未知。以棠既放心又挫折。電視觀眾或許期待戲劇性逮捕，真正調查卻需要耐心。知衍提議派車守在她家外，她拒絕，只請他陪走到車站。他沒有爭辯。入口處，她感謝他接到電話就趕來。「因為你找我，我才來。」他說，「你要我走時，我也會走。」這才是她想聽的承諾。

A new message arrived after midnight, asking for a decision before morning. She read it twice, then left it unanswered until she had enough information. He had the resources to make a call that would change the whole schedule, yet he asked her what would happen to the people whose names were not printed on the invitation. For once, the most powerful person in the building was the one willing to wait.

午夜過後，一則新訊息要求她天亮前決定。她讀了兩遍，仍等到資訊足夠才答覆。他有能力一通電話改變所有時程，卻先問起那些名字沒有印在邀請函上的人會如何受影響。那一刻，整棟樓裡最有權力的人，反而是願意等待的那個人。
