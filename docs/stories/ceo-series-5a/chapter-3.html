The first draft of their public partnership was written by Zhiyan's attorney, a cautious woman who had seen too many clients confuse a signature with affection. Yitang read every layer of the document and found a paragraph that would bind her studio to Meridian for five years. She crossed it out. “A contract cannot make you my beloved,” she said, looking at Zhiyan rather than the lawyer. He gave a small shrug and ordered the clause removed. Yitang was no longer willing to be prey for the next ambitious executive who wanted her designs. She wanted the scope of the agreement to be narrow: attend four public events, correct false statements together, and end the arrangement by mutual notice.

公開合作協議的第一份草案由知衍的律師起草。那是位謹慎的女性，見過太多客戶把簽名誤當成愛情。以棠逐層閱讀條文，發現其中一段竟要把她的工作室綁在明曜五年。她直接劃掉。「合約不能把你變成我的愛人。」她看著知衍說。他聳了聳肩，吩咐刪去條文。以棠不願再成為下一個野心主管的獵物。她要求合作範圍限於四場公開活動、共同澄清不實資訊，以及雙方都可通知終止。

Outside the office, a shuttle carried staff between Meridian towers. Yitang watched a man straighten his tie and a woman tighten the strap of her laptop bag before entering a meeting. Both moved as if every minute mattered. Zhiyan's assistant said the chairman specialized in acquiring troubled businesses; his negotiations could diminish a competitor's value within days. Yitang was not impressed. “That is an efficient way to buy a company,” she told Zhiyan, “not an argument for owning my choices.” He answered with unexpected realism: “I have been rewarded for control for a long time. I may be bad at giving it up.” She appreciated the admission more than another polished promise.

辦公室外，接駁車載著員工往返明曜大樓。以棠看著一名男子拉直領帶、一名女子拉緊筆電袋肩帶，人人都像分秒必爭。助理說，董事長擅長收購陷入困境的企業，幾天內就能讓競爭對手的價值下降。以棠不覺得厲害。「這是買公司的效率，不是擁有我選擇的理由。」她說。知衍意外坦率地回答：「我太久以來都因控制局面得到獎勵，可能不擅長放手。」比起漂亮承諾，她更珍惜這句承認。

A director spoke with open aggression when Yitang deleted the clause. “You are bound to regret refusing us,” he said. Zhiyan answered that no one was bound to accept an unfair offer. The attorney, who had chosen to specialize in contracts for creative workers, noted the remark in her minutes. Yitang was pleased she had brought her own counsel. A fair negotiation was not supposed to feel like a punishment for having an opinion.

以棠刪掉條款時，一名董事毫不掩飾攻擊性：「你一定會後悔拒絕我們。」知衍回應，沒有人注定要接受不公平的提議。那位專門研究創意工作者契約的律師把這句話記入會議紀錄。以棠慶幸自己也帶了顧問。公平談判不該讓人因為有意見就受到懲罰。

They reviewed the remaining rules at a quiet cafe. No surprise visits to her home. No interference in her employment decisions. No private medical information shared for publicity. No touch in public without consent, even if cameras were pointed at them. An independent observer would keep the contract on file. One section concerned an unusual biological fabric project that Meridian funded. Yitang insisted that the research team, not the marketing office, should decide whether data could be released. Zhiyan agreed. The pen stopped ticking against his watch when he realized how many rules protected him as well: he, too, could say no to a performance.

兩人在安靜的咖啡館檢視其餘條款：不得突然到她家、不得干預她聘用員工、不得把私人醫療資訊拿去宣傳；即使鏡頭在場，未經同意也不能觸碰。獨立見證人會保管合約。文件還提到明曜資助的一項生物布料研究，以棠堅持是否公開資料應由研究團隊決定，而非行銷部。知衍同意了。他的筆不再敲著腕錶，因為他突然發現這些規定也保護他：他同樣可以拒絕表演。

In a virtual meeting that afternoon, board members wanted to know whether their staged engagement would influence the share price. Yitang asked whether any of them knew the actual cost of her studio's work. Nobody answered. She presented her viewpoint in figures: wages, training, travel, and the value of original patterns. Then she said the contract was not a romance for sale. A director smiled without warmth, calling it a charming performance. Zhiyan closed the meeting early. When Yitang asked why, he said he had wanted to interrupt sooner but was learning to let her finish first. It was the most useful thing he had said all day.

下午的線上會議裡，董事只想知道這場假訂婚會不會影響股價。以棠反問，有誰知道她的工作室實際成本。沒有人回答。她列出工資、培訓、交通和原創版型的價值，再說合約不是待售的戀情。一名董事冷冷地笑，稱這是討喜的演出。知衍提早結束會議。以棠問原因，他說自己早想打斷那些人，但正在練習先讓她說完。這是他一整天最有用的一句話。

They signed at six thirty, with separate advisers present. The arrangement would end after ninety days unless both renewed it. There was no secret marriage, no penalty for ending the public story, and no transfer of studio shares. Yitang kept the original agreement in her own bag. The attorney shook her hand and said a cautious person sometimes looked difficult only because other people had grown accustomed to shortcuts. As Yitang left, Zhiyan asked if he might take her to dinner sometime with no photographers and no business agenda. She considered the question. “Ask me again when neither of us needs the answer for a headline,” she said.

傍晚六點半，兩人在各自顧問見證下簽字。協議九十天後到期，除非雙方同意續約；沒有祕密婚姻、沒有終止公開關係的罰金，也沒有工作室股權轉移。以棠把正本放進自己的包。律師握著她的手說，謹慎的人之所以顯得難相處，有時只是別人習慣走捷徑。離開時，知衍問能不能找一天不用攝影師、也沒有商業議題，單純一起吃晚餐。以棠想了想：「等我們都不需要用答案上頭條，再問一次。」
