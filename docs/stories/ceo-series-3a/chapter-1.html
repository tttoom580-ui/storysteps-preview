The Contract on the Table

總裁，這份合約我不簽 · 第 1 / 25 章 · 2000–3000 · 英中對照 · 校訂中

用互動版查詞、朗讀與收藏

The rain followed Avery Chen into the Old River Market. At twenty-nine, she had worked on enough old buildings to know that the expensive ones were not always the safest. A single drip of water landed on the empty signature line of the contract. Beside it stood an untouched glass of coconut water, ordered for a guest nobody had bothered to ask. At the end of the table, a vendor balanced on a stool so she could see the wall map. Julian Lin, the thirty-four-year-old president of the Lin Group, raised his chin when Avery entered. He wore a dark suit and an expression that suggested the meeting was already over. Avery laid her notebook on the table instead of sitting down.

雨水一路跟著陳艾薇進入舊河市場。二十九歲的她修復過不少老建築，早已明白造價高昂的建物不一定最安全。一滴水落在合約空白的簽名欄上。旁邊擺著一杯沒人碰過的椰子水，那是為某位貴賓點的，卻沒人問過對方想喝什麼。桌尾有位攤商站上矮凳，想看清牆上的地圖。三十四歲的林氏集團總裁林景衡見她進門，微微抬起下巴。他穿著深色西裝，表情彷彿會議已經結束。艾薇沒有坐下，而是把筆記本放上桌。

"Ten days," Avery said, reading the offer. "You want every family out in ten days?" The company lawyer answered that the payment was acceptable under the draft agreement. "A contract can be legal and still be cruel," Avery replied. The word made the room go still. She did not envy Julian's position, but she would not gamble with a hundred families' safety to make his schedule easier. Julian folded his hands. "You were hired to review a building, not to judge me." "Then give me the building report," she said. "An actual report, not this beautiful picture." A few directors looked toward the exit. Julian could have ended the meeting with a word. Instead, he asked her to continue.

「十天？」艾薇讀著提案說。「你們要所有家庭在十天內搬走？」公司律師回答，按照合約草案，這筆補償是可以接受的。「合約即使合法，也可能殘酷。」艾薇回答。房間頓時安靜。她並不羨慕景衡的處境，但也不願拿上百個家庭的安全，去賭他能否趕上進度。景衡交握雙手。「我請妳來審查建築，不是來評斷我。」「那就把建築報告給我。」她說。「我要真正的報告，不是這張漂亮圖片。」幾名董事望向出口。景衡只要一句話就能結束會議，卻請她繼續說下去。

A laundry shop owner stood first. The back entrance was her only way to move heavy carts, she explained, and the new plan covered it with a wall. Two twin sisters from the tea stall said that their parents had sold drinks there for forty years. On the notice board hung a black-and-white photograph of old carts loaded with hay. An early market record listed tobacco from the farms beyond the river. Avery listened because she wanted to know what the property had been before anyone called it a luxury address. She could not promise the vendors that the market would remain exactly as it was. She could promise to treat their memories as evidence about how the building had actually been used.

一位洗衣店老闆先站起來。她解釋，後門是搬運沉重推車的唯一出口，新圖面卻用一面牆封住它。茶攤的雙胞胎姊妹說，她們父母已在這裡賣飲料四十年。公告欄上有張黑白照片，拍著裝滿乾草的舊推車。市場早年的交易紀錄甚至記載了河對岸農場運來的菸草。艾薇仔細聽，因為她想知道，在任何人把這裡稱作豪華地段之前，這處空間究竟是什麼。她無法保證市場永遠維持原貌，但她可以承諾，把居民的記憶視為實際使用建築的證據。

Avery invited the market lawyer to advise the shopkeepers before they answered the company. Then she opened the first-aid cabinet by the service door. A printed guide showed the safe dose for each emergency medicine, but the inspection sticker on the cabinet had expired. "That is a small problem," a manager said. "Small problems are how large ones hide," she replied. The oldest flood plan warned of extreme rainfall. Avery used a red pencil to connect two marks that were far apart on the new drawing. One glance at the old plan showed a blue line between them. "This is a public passage," she said. "Where did it go?" Julian leaned forward for the first time.

艾薇先請市場法律顧問向攤商提供建議，再讓大家決定如何回應公司。接著她打開服務門旁的急救箱。裡面有張印著各種急救藥物安全劑量的指南，但箱上的檢查貼紙早已過期。「那是小問題。」一名主管說。「大問題就是這樣藏在小問題裡的。」她回答。最早的防洪圖警告過極端降雨。艾薇用紅筆把新圖面上相隔很遠的兩個標記連起來。只看一眼舊圖，就能發現兩者之間原本有一條藍線。「這是公共通道。」她說。「它去哪了？」景衡第一次往前傾身。

A maintenance whistle sounded behind the wall. Avery asked to explore the blocked corridor with an independent inspector, but only after the workers confirmed it was safe to enter. Julian looked toward his project director, Victor, whose smile had disappeared. "The corridor was removed years ago," Victor said. "Then show us the order," Avery answered. No one moved. Julian took the contract from her side of the table and placed it face down. "The demolition is on hold for forty-eight hours," he said. "No files are to be destroyed. I want the original drawings tonight." His voice was quiet, but everyone understood it was an order. Avery understood something else: he had just made an expensive decision without asking her to thank him.

牆後傳來維修用的警示哨聲。艾薇要求與獨立檢查員探索被封閉的走廊，但必須先確認工人能安全進入。景衡望向專案主管維克多；對方原本的微笑已經消失。「通道很多年前就拆掉了。」維克多說。「那就拿出拆除命令。」艾薇回答。沒有人動。景衡拿起她桌前的合約，翻面放下。「拆除工程暫停四十八小時。」他說。「任何文件都不准銷毀。今晚我要看到原始圖紙。」聲音不高，所有人卻都明白那是命令。艾薇也明白另一件事：他剛剛做了個代價高昂的決定，卻沒有要求她感謝。

After the meeting, Julian found her beneath the market clock. "I was sincere about stopping the work," he said. "I know," she answered. "But I still won't sign your contract." He looked at her for a long moment, then gave a short, unexpected laugh. "Most people say yes before I finish asking." "Then perhaps you should ask better questions." For a moment the distance between the powerful president and the independent architect seemed smaller. Avery still had no reason to trust the company, and she did not mistake interest for love. She opened her notebook again. On the last sheet she had drawn a red circle around the missing passage. Under it she wrote three words: Find the original.

散會後，景衡在市場大鐘下找到她。「我是真心要停工。」他說。「我知道。」她回答。「但我還是不會簽你的合約。」他看了她片刻，忽然短促地笑了一聲。「大多數人在我還沒問完時就答應了。」「那或許你該問更好的問題。」有一瞬間，強勢總裁與獨立建築師之間的距離似乎縮短了。但艾薇仍沒有理由相信整間公司，也不會把好感誤認為愛。她再次打開筆記本，在最後一頁用紅筆圈出消失的通道，下面寫著三個字：找原件。
