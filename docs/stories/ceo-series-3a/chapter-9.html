She kept her notebook open when the room grew quiet. A beautiful promise was not the same as a signed responsibility, and she would not let an expensive presentation hide an unanswered question. A night storm filled the market’s outer road with cold mist and fast-moving water. The site files also contained short accounts of the market’s roof garden, shopkeepers, and old community exhibitions. A brass switch was hidden beneath the humidity gauges. The old festival buffet had used this hall for serving food. Opening the blue door early could cause costly damage. The original designer had drawn both gauges on one plan.

房間安靜下來時，她仍攤開筆記本。美麗的承諾不等於簽名承擔的責任，她不會讓昂貴的簡報掩蓋尚未回答的問題。夜間暴風雨讓市場外側道路瀰漫冷霧，水勢湍急。現場檔案也收錄了市場屋頂花園、攤商及舊社區展覽的簡短記述。兩只濕度儀表下方藏著黃銅開關。過去的節慶自助餐曾在大廳供餐。太早打開藍門可能造成高昂損失。原設計師把兩只儀表畫在同一張圖上。

Avery had learned that a city could lose its memory one small decision at a time. The danger rarely arrived with a warning; sometimes it came inside a polite email or a neat drawing. A neglected metal seal failed, and water entered a storage room holding community objects. Avery checked the small details in the workers’ notes rather than ignoring them as unimportant. Owen set down his hammer before testing the lock. Victor muttered that the repeated bell was driving him to hell. Ms. Tang made a humorous remark about the bell's poor timing. The inner handle felt icy despite the warm hallway.

艾薇早已明白，城市的記憶可能在一個又一個小決定中消失。危險很少帶著警告出現；有時它藏在一封客氣的電子郵件或一張整齊的圖面裡。一處遭忽視的金屬封條失效，水滲入保存社區物件的倉庫。艾薇仔細核對工人筆記裡的小細節，不因它們看似不起眼就忽略。歐文測試門鎖前先放下鐵鎚。維克多嘀咕，重複鐘聲快把他逼到地獄。唐女士幽默地評論鐘聲總在不對時候響。儘管走廊溫暖，內側門把仍冰冷。

He watched Avery speak without looking to him for permission. Her confidence was not loud; it came from the fact that she could explain every step and accept questions about her own mistakes. Avery called trained staff, separated people from the hazard, and documented the leak before moving anything. The site files also contained short accounts of the market’s roof garden, shopkeepers, and old community exhibitions. An old children's sign showed a kangaroo carrying seeds. A thin mist appeared when the cleaning cycle changed pressure. A porcelain cup rattled beside the moving gauges. Avery used a digital recorder to capture the bell's timing.

他看著艾薇說話，卻不向他尋求允許。她的自信並不張揚，而是來自她能解釋每個步驟，也願意接受對自身錯誤的提問。艾薇找來受訓人員、把人員移離危險區域，並在搬動物品前記錄漏水情形。現場檔案也收錄了市場屋頂花園、攤商及舊社區展覽的簡短記述。舊兒童標牌畫著袋鼠揹種子。清潔循環改變氣壓時，出現一層薄霧。兩只移動的儀表旁有個瓷杯作響。艾薇用數位錄音機記錄鐘聲的時間。

The answer required more than courage. Avery checked dates, asked who owned each document, and wrote down what still needed outside review. She could not control the whole company, but she could control the care she gave her work. Julian opened an emergency budget, but accepted that money could not replace professional clearance. Avery checked the small details in the workers’ notes rather than ignoring them as unimportant. Owen began to scrub dirt from the old switch label. The blue door's rubber seal prevented air from escaping. Avery refused to skip the twelve-minute safety test. A former stadium technician had serviced the bell's motor.

答案需要的不只是勇氣。艾薇查核日期、追問文件所有權，也記下尚待外部審查的部分。她無法控制整間公司，卻能決定自己如何謹慎工作。景衡啟用緊急預算，也接受金錢不能取代專業核准。艾薇仔細核對工人筆記裡的小細節，不因它們看似不起眼就忽略。歐文開始刷洗舊開關標籤上的汙垢。藍門的橡膠密封條阻止空氣外洩。艾薇拒絕跳過十二分鐘安全測試。一位前體育場技師曾維修鐘聲馬達。

Outside, the street continued its ordinary life. Someone opened a shop, someone carried supper home, and a child asked whether the market would still be there next year. Neither of them knew the answer yet. At dawn, their hands met around a wet folder, and neither pretended the moment meant a promise. Avery checked the small details in the workers’ notes rather than ignoring them as unimportant. Maya peeled a tangerine while waiting for the needles to settle. The technical drawing showed the cleaning cycle clearly. An early door opening could threaten the stored seeds. Owen had to tighten one loose screw after the safe test.

街道仍過著平凡生活。有人開店，有人提晚餐回家，一名孩子問市場明年是否還在。兩人此刻都不知道答案。天亮時，兩人同時握住一份濕文件，誰也沒有假裝那瞬間代表了承諾。艾薇仔細核對工人筆記裡的小細節，不因它們看似不起眼就忽略。等待指針穩定時，瑪雅剝了一顆橘子。技術圖清楚標示清潔循環。提早開門可能威脅保存中的種子。安全測試後歐文得鎖緊一顆鬆動螺絲。

The old market had survived because its people were skilled at finding another way. They repaired tools, shared stories, and watched children grow. Avery wanted the new plan to protect that ordinary strength, not turn it into decoration. Julian understood that the cost would appear in his financial reports. He also understood that the cost of doing nothing had never appeared there at all.

舊市場能延續，是因為居民總能找到另一條路。他們修理工具、分享故事，看著孩子長大。艾薇希望新計畫守護這份平凡的力量，而不是把它變成裝飾。景衡知道這會增加財務報表上的成本，也明白什麼都不做的代價，從未出現在那些報表裡。

Before they left, Avery checked the route one more time and explained her decision to the people standing beside the gate. She did not want their agreement merely because a famous company president was present. She wanted questions that could be answered with dates, measurements, and names. Julian listened without reaching for his phone. The next step would take time, but this time everyone knew who was responsible for it.

離開前，艾薇又檢查一次路線，向站在門旁的人解釋自己的決定。她不希望大家只是因為知名集團總裁在場就點頭，而是希望每個問題都能以日期、測量與姓名回答。景衡沒有伸手拿手機，只是安靜聽著。下一步需要時間，但這次每個人都知道該由誰負責。
