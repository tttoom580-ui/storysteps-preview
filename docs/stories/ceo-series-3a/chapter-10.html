The building had once been a place where different lives met. Avery tried to hear those lives before deciding what any wall was worth. The company saw a development site; she saw homes, work, and an unfinished argument. The board called Julian back to headquarters and accused him of losing control of the project. The site files also contained short accounts of the market’s roof garden, shopkeepers, and old community exhibitions. A retired banker had signed the oldest donation receipt. The first shipment had arrived through the western bay. A narrow beam of light fell across the old ledger. Maya called Ms. Tang a champion of the local seed project.

這棟建築曾讓許多不同的人生在此交會。艾薇想先聽見這些人的故事，再決定一面牆值多少。公司看見的是開發基地；她看見的是家園、工作與一場尚未結束的爭論。董事會把景衡召回總部，指責他失去對專案的控制。現場檔案也收錄了市場屋頂花園、攤商及舊社區展覽的簡短記述。一位退休銀行家曾簽署最早的捐贈收據。第一批貨物從西側海灣抵達。一道細長光束落在舊帳冊上。瑪雅稱唐女士是地方種子計畫的捍衛者。

The people in the corridor waited for a quick answer. She knew a quick answer would please them today and perhaps hurt them tomorrow, so she pointed again to the missing evidence. A vice president offered Avery a senior position if she would postpone her report. Avery checked the small details in the workers’ notes rather than ignoring them as unimportant. The missing page required a creative but careful search. The number of disputed jars did not decrease after recounting. An echo from the hall made the night bell sound louder. The fearful workers asked for a clear explanation.

走廊裡的人等待一個簡單答案。她知道快速回答今天或許能讓人高興，明天卻可能傷害他們，因此她再次指向缺少的證據。一位副總裁答應給艾薇高階職位，條件是延後發布報告。艾薇仔細核對工人筆記裡的小細節，不因它們看似不起眼就忽略。尋找缺頁需要有創意又謹慎的方法。重新清點後，有爭議的罐子數量沒有減少。大廳回音讓夜間鐘聲聽來更響。害怕的員工要求清楚說明。

Julian was used to a room becoming still when he entered it. This time the quiet made him uncomfortable. He had built a company by giving orders, but orders could not make the past disappear. Avery walked out and sent her findings to the independent committee instead. The site files also contained short accounts of the market’s roof garden, shopkeepers, and old community exhibitions. The original donation was a historic event for the town. The badge holder recorded a late-night entrance. A small leak had damaged the edge of one receipt. Avery stopped an angry mob from entering the archive.

景衡習慣自己一走進房間，眾人就安靜下來。這次的寂靜卻讓他不自在。他靠下指令建立公司，但指令無法讓過去消失。艾薇離開會議，把發現提交給獨立委員會。現場檔案也收錄了市場屋頂花園、攤商及舊社區展覽的簡短記述。原始捐贈是小鎮的重要歷史事件。識別證持有人留下深夜進入紀錄。一處小漏水損壞了收據邊緣。艾薇阻止憤怒群眾闖入檔案室。

The market was not a picture to be saved for a brochure. People needed safe doors, affordable spaces, clear agreements, and a way to disagree without losing their place. Julian refused to ask her for a favor that would damage her professional name. Avery checked the small details in the workers’ notes rather than ignoring them as unimportant. The edited computer entry looked normal at first. A postcard of a distant palace was inside the receipt box. A single penny had been taped to a donor's old letter. Avery asked the board to postpone the sale pending an audit.

市場不是供宣傳手冊展示的圖片。人們需要安全的門、負擔得起的空間、清楚的協議，以及能提出反對卻不失去立足之地的方法。景衡拒絕請她做出會損害專業聲譽的人情。艾薇仔細核對工人筆記裡的小細節，不因它們看似不起眼就忽略。修改過的電腦條目起初看來正常。收據箱內有一張遠方宮殿明信片。一枚一分硬幣貼在捐贈者舊信上。艾薇要求董事會在稽核完成前延後出售。

When the meeting ended, the distance between them felt different. Avery could recognize Julian’s effort without forgiving every decision his company had made. He could admire her without expecting her work or her heart in return. The committee voted to keep the old market closed during further testing. Avery checked the small details in the workers’ notes rather than ignoring them as unimportant. The receiver beside the door had logged a strange call. An early founder had funded a farming scholarship. Nobody could prove that the consultant meant to steal the seeds. Owen closed the evidence bag with a strong zipper.

會議結束時，兩人之間的距離似乎改變了。艾薇可以肯定景衡的努力，卻不必因此原諒公司做過的一切。他可以欣賞她，卻不能期待她回報工作或感情。委員會表決，在進一步檢測期間維持舊市場封閉。艾薇仔細核對工人筆記裡的小細節，不因它們看似不起眼就忽略。門旁的接收器記錄了一通奇怪電話。早期創辦人曾資助農業獎學金。沒有人能證明顧問有意偷走種子。歐文以牢固拉鍊拉上證據袋。

Neither of them spoke about a perfect future. They had seen how quickly an easy promise could become a burden for someone else. Instead they agreed to ask the next question openly and give the people affected a chance to answer. Avery closed her notebook only after marking the unfinished tasks. Julian waited while she checked the lock on the office door. For once, he did not hurry her.

兩人都沒有談論完美的未來。他們見過輕率承諾如何很快成為別人的負擔。於是他們同意公開提出下一個問題，讓受影響的人有機會回答。艾薇把未完成事項標好後才闔上筆記本。景衡等她檢查辦公室門鎖，這一次他沒有催促她。

Before they left, Avery checked the route one more time and explained her decision to the people standing beside the gate. She did not want their agreement merely because a famous company president was present. She wanted questions that could be answered with dates, measurements, and names. Julian listened without reaching for his phone. The next step would take time, but this time everyone knew who was responsible for it.

離開前，艾薇又檢查一次路線，向站在門旁的人解釋自己的決定。她不希望大家只是因為知名集團總裁在場就點頭，而是希望每個問題都能以日期、測量與姓名回答。景衡沒有伸手拿手機，只是安靜聽著。下一步需要時間，但這次每個人都知道該由誰負責。
