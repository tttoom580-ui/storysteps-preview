The building had once been a place where different lives met. Avery tried to hear those lives before deciding what any wall was worth. The company saw a development site; she saw homes, work, and an unfinished argument. At the project exhibition, a shining model promised a beautiful new hotel above the river. The site files also contained short accounts of the market’s roof garden, shopkeepers, and old community exhibitions. A tree-lined avenue led visitors to the east exhibition. A wooden cradle held a model of the first seed vault. Volunteers had used paper leaves to decorate the entrance. The service elevator was locked after the final tour.

這棟建築曾讓許多不同的人生在此交會。艾薇想先聽見這些人的故事，再決定一面牆值多少。公司看見的是開發基地；她看見的是家園、工作與一場尚未結束的爭論。在專案展覽上，亮晶晶的模型描繪了一座建於河畔的美麗新飯店。現場檔案也收錄了市場屋頂花園、攤商及舊社區展覽的簡短記述。一條林蔭大道引導遊客前往東側展區。木製搖籃托著第一代市場種子檔案室的模型。志工曾用紙葉子裝飾入口。最後一場導覽後，貨梯便上鎖。

The people in the corridor waited for a quick answer. She knew a quick answer would please them today and perhaps hurt them tomorrow, so she pointed again to the missing evidence. The elevator in the model stood exactly where residents remembered an old flood route. Avery checked the small details in the workers’ notes rather than ignoring them as unimportant. The old exhibition projector made the green footprints. A photograph showed a firework above the garden festival. Owen measured one gallon of water from the leaking pipe. The owner's headquarters had approved the new visitor route.

走廊裡的人等待一個簡單答案。她知道快速回答今天或許能讓人高興，明天卻可能傷害他們，因此她再次指向缺少的證據。模型上的電梯位置，正是居民記憶中的舊防洪路線。艾薇仔細核對工人筆記裡的小細節，不因它們看似不起眼就忽略。舊展覽投影機製造了綠色腳印。照片記錄了花園節慶上空的煙火。歐文量出漏水管流出的一加侖水。業主總部曾核准新遊客動線。

Julian was used to a room becoming still when he entered it. This time the quiet made him uncomfortable. He had built a company by giving orders, but orders could not make the past disappear. Avery asked visitors to mark their everyday walking routes on a blank map. The site files also contained short accounts of the market’s roof garden, shopkeepers, and old community exhibitions. Avery said imagination could not replace a safety test. A painted leopard watched from a children's animal mural. In the dark, the projected path looked almost magical. It was odd that the bright path ended at a dangerous fan.

景衡習慣自己一走進房間，眾人就安靜下來。這次的寂靜卻讓他不自在。他靠下指令建立公司，但指令無法讓過去消失。艾薇請參觀者在空白地圖上標出平日走的路線。現場檔案也收錄了市場屋頂花園、攤商及舊社區展覽的簡短記述。艾薇說想像力不能代替安全測試。兒童動物壁畫上有隻彩繪豹。黑暗中，投影道路看起來幾乎像魔法。明亮道路竟通向危險風扇，這點很奇怪。

The market was not a picture to be saved for a brochure. People needed safe doors, affordable spaces, clear agreements, and a way to disagree without losing their place. Julian stopped the planned demolition for forty-eight hours and ordered his team to preserve the original drawings. Avery checked the small details in the workers’ notes rather than ignoring them as unimportant. The side shop sold organic vegetables from nearby farms. A red ribbon marked the page missing from the route book. The loose pipe could spit warm water across the passage. The white tiles avoided a steep ramp near the service door.

市場不是供宣傳手冊展示的圖片。人們需要安全的門、負擔得起的空間、清楚的協議，以及能提出反對卻不失去立足之地的方法。景衡暫停原定拆除工程四十八小時，命團隊保存原始圖紙。艾薇仔細核對工人筆記裡的小細節，不因它們看似不起眼就忽略。側邊商店販售附近農場的有機蔬菜。一條紅緞帶標出路線冊缺頁的位置。鬆動管線可能把溫水噴濺到通道上。白色地磚避開了維修門旁的陡坡。

When the meeting ended, the distance between them felt different. Avery could recognize Julian’s effort without forgiving every decision his company had made. He could admire her without expecting her work or her heart in return. A stranger left a note on Avery’s table: Stop asking whose land this is. Avery checked the small details in the workers’ notes rather than ignoring them as unimportant. Maya planned a survey of visitors who had used the route. The open fan was a real threat, unlike the false ghost story. A tourist had photographed the footprints the day before. Owen used a cloth to wipe the dust from the projector.

會議結束時，兩人之間的距離似乎改變了。艾薇可以肯定景衡的努力，卻不必因此原諒公司做過的一切。他可以欣賞她，卻不能期待她回報工作或感情。一名陌生人在艾薇桌上留下字條：別再追問這片土地屬於誰。艾薇仔細核對工人筆記裡的小細節，不因它們看似不起眼就忽略。瑪雅打算調查曾走過那條路線的遊客。裸露風扇是真正的威脅，不像虛構的鬼故事。一名遊客前一天拍下了那些腳印。歐文用布擦掉投影機上的灰塵。

At the window, Julian asked whether Avery thought people could change. She answered that change was not a speech made at the right moment; it was a series of difficult actions that other people could check. He did not reply at once. She found she appreciated that delay more than a clever compliment. When they turned back toward the street, they were still two people with different responsibilities, and both knew it.

在窗邊，景衡問艾薇是否相信人會改變。她說，改變不是在恰當時機說出的一篇演說，而是一連串能被他人檢驗的困難行動。他沒有立刻回答。她發現自己比起漂亮的恭維，更欣賞這份停頓。兩人轉身走向街道時，仍肩負不同責任，彼此都很清楚。

Before they left, Avery checked the route one more time and explained her decision to the people standing beside the gate. She did not want their agreement merely because a famous company president was present. She wanted questions that could be answered with dates, measurements, and names. Julian listened without reaching for his phone. The next step would take time, but this time everyone knew who was responsible for it.

離開前，艾薇又檢查一次路線，向站在門旁的人解釋自己的決定。她不希望大家只是因為知名集團總裁在場就點頭，而是希望每個問題都能以日期、測量與姓名回答。景衡沒有伸手拿手機，只是安靜聽著。下一步需要時間，但這次每個人都知道該由誰負責。
