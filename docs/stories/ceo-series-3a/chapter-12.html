總裁，這份合約我不簽 · 第 12 / 25 章 · 2000–3000 · 英中對照 · 校訂中

The building had once been a place where different lives met. Avery tried to hear those lives before deciding what any wall was worth. The company saw a development site; she saw homes, work, and an unfinished argument. The old basement held paper records that were older than the company’s digital system. The site files also contained short accounts of the market’s roof garden, shopkeepers, and old community exhibitions. The wooden alphabet board once taught children seed names. The basement held older files than the electronic archive. An empty cigarette box lay behind the wooden board. A rusty clip held three old pages together.

這棟建築曾讓許多不同的人生在此交會。艾薇想先聽見這些人的故事，再決定一面牆值多少。公司看見的是開發基地；她看見的是家園、工作與一場尚未結束的爭論。老地下室保存著比集團數位系統更早的紙本紀錄。現場檔案也收錄了市場屋頂花園、攤商及舊社區展覽的簡短記述。木製字母板過去用來教孩子讀種子名稱。地下室保存著比電子檔案更早的文件。木板後有一個空香菸盒。一枚生鏽的夾子把三張舊紙夾在一起。

The people in the corridor waited for a quick answer. She knew a quick answer would please them today and perhaps hurt them tomorrow, so she pointed again to the missing evidence. The pages included community rights, repair dates, and private information that did not belong in a news story. Avery checked the small details in the workers’ notes rather than ignoring them as unimportant. At dawn, a cock crowed from the farm beyond the road. The collection included more than two hundred seed varieties. The morning was foggy, making the outer garden hard to see. Victor had wanted to hire a private records consultant.

走廊裡的人等待一個簡單答案。她知道快速回答今天或許能讓人高興，明天卻可能傷害他們，因此她再次指向缺少的證據。紙頁記載社區權利、維修日期及不該出現在新聞中的私人資訊。艾薇仔細核對工人筆記裡的小細節，不因它們看似不起眼就忽略。黎明時，路對面農場的公雞啼叫。收藏包含兩百多種種子品種。早晨霧氣濃重，外園難以看清。維克多曾想聘請私人檔案顧問。

Julian was used to a room becoming still when he entered it. This time the quiet made him uncomfortable. He had built a company by giving orders, but orders could not make the past disappear. Avery made a public summary and placed sensitive records in a sealed archive for authorized review. The site files also contained short accounts of the market’s roof garden, shopkeepers, and old community exhibitions. Maya asked every witness for honesty about uncertain memories. A kindergarten class had donated drawings to the seed display. The old room had been designed for learning, not sales. The families wanted the liberty to request their own seeds.

景衡習慣自己一走進房間，眾人就安靜下來。這次的寂靜卻讓他不自在。他靠下指令建立公司，但指令無法讓過去消失。艾薇整理公開摘要，把敏感文件封存，交由獲授權的人審閱。現場檔案也收錄了市場屋頂花園、攤商及舊社區展覽的簡短記述。瑪雅請每位證人誠實說明不確定的記憶。一班幼稚園孩子曾捐畫給種子展。舊房間設計用途是學習，而不是販售。家戶希望有自由申請取回自家種子的權利。

The market was not a picture to be saved for a brochure. People needed safe doors, affordable spaces, clear agreements, and a way to disagree without losing their place. Julian stayed on the stairs because only the archivist had permission to enter. Avery checked the small details in the workers’ notes rather than ignoring them as unimportant. The matching dates gave the search meaningful support. A little white powder from an old label fell onto the table. The previous catalogue still named the original owners. A queer mark on the page proved to be an old number.

市場不是供宣傳手冊展示的圖片。人們需要安全的門、負擔得起的空間、清楚的協議，以及能提出反對卻不失去立足之地的方法。景衡留在樓梯上，因為只有檔案員獲准入內。艾薇仔細核對工人筆記裡的小細節，不因它們看似不起眼就忽略。相符的日期為調查提供有意義的支持。舊標籤上的少量白色粉末落到桌上。前一版清冊仍列出原始所有人。頁上一個奇特記號原來是舊數字。

When the meeting ended, the distance between them felt different. Avery could recognize Julian’s effort without forgiving every decision his company had made. He could admire her without expecting her work or her heart in return. Their growing trust began to feel more valuable than the expensive watch on his wrist. Avery checked the small details in the workers’ notes rather than ignoring them as unimportant. The faded ink could reveal a name beneath the crossing line. A forgotten bottle of shampoo sat in the staff washroom. Families used to weave baskets to carry harvested seeds. Ms. Tang said farming wisdom was stored in the letters too.

會議結束時，兩人之間的距離似乎改變了。艾薇可以肯定景衡的努力，卻不必因此原諒公司做過的一切。他可以欣賞她，卻不能期待她回報工作或感情。逐漸形成的信任，比他腕上的昂貴手錶更有價值。艾薇仔細核對工人筆記裡的小細節，不因它們看似不起眼就忽略。褪色墨水可能顯露刪除線下的姓名。員工盥洗室留著一瓶被忘記的洗髮精。家戶過去會編織籃子搬運採收種子。唐女士說信件也保存著農耕智慧。

At the window, Julian asked whether Avery thought people could change. She answered that change was not a speech made at the right moment; it was a series of difficult actions that other people could check. He did not reply at once. She found she appreciated that delay more than a clever compliment. When they turned back toward the street, they were still two people with different responsibilities, and both knew it.

在窗邊，景衡問艾薇是否相信人會改變。她說，改變不是在恰當時機說出的一篇演說，而是一連串能被他人檢驗的困難行動。他沒有立刻回答。她發現自己比起漂亮的恭維，更欣賞這份停頓。兩人轉身走向街道時，仍肩負不同責任，彼此都很清楚。

Before they left, Avery checked the route one more time and explained her decision to the people standing beside the gate. She did not want their agreement merely because a famous company president was present. She wanted questions that could be answered with dates, measurements, and names. Julian listened without reaching for his phone. The next step would take time, but this time everyone knew who was responsible for it.

離開前，艾薇又檢查一次路線，向站在門旁的人解釋自己的決定。她不希望大家只是因為知名集團總裁在場就點頭，而是希望每個問題都能以日期、測量與姓名回答。景衡沒有伸手拿手機，只是安靜聽著。下一步需要時間，但這次每個人都知道該由誰負責。
