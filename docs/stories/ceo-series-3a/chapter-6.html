The building had once been a place where different lives met. Avery tried to hear those lives before deciding what any wall was worth. The company saw a development site; she saw homes, work, and an unfinished argument. Julian invited Avery to a family gathering at the old hillside inn, where his father still influenced the company. The site files also contained short accounts of the market’s roof garden, shopkeepers, and old community exhibitions. Victor claimed the sale would achieve a full repair of the garden. A bright fruit cocktail remained untouched at the sponsor's reception. Maya agreed that a safe repair was desirable, but not any sale. Owen joked that the devil was hiding in the agreement's final page.

這棟建築曾讓許多不同的人生在此交會。艾薇想先聽見這些人的故事，再決定一面牆值多少。公司看見的是開發基地；她看見的是家園、工作與一場尚未結束的爭論。景衡邀艾薇到山坡上的老旅館參加家族聚會；他的父親仍影響集團決策。現場檔案也收錄了市場屋頂花園、攤商及舊社區展覽的簡短記述。維克多聲稱出售能達成溫室全面修復。贊助商招待會上的鮮豔水果調飲無人碰觸。瑪雅同意安全修繕值得追求，卻不代表能任意出售。歐文開玩笑說魔鬼藏在協議最後一頁。

The people in the corridor waited for a quick answer. She knew a quick answer would please them today and perhaps hurt them tomorrow, so she pointed again to the missing evidence. His father called the market a poor investment and demanded that Julian choose between family approval and the delay. Avery checked the small details in the workers’ notes rather than ignoring them as unimportant. Avery was doubtful that a photocopy proved complete ownership. The contractor wanted to dump broken cabinets behind the fence. The former governor had attended the roof garden's opening ceremony. A nearby inn offered rooms to the visiting auditors.

走廊裡的人等待一個簡單答案。她知道快速回答今天或許能讓人高興，明天卻可能傷害他們，因此她再次指向缺少的證據。父親稱市場是不良投資，要求景衡在家族支持與工程延期之間選邊。艾薇仔細核對工人筆記裡的小細節，不因它們看似不起眼就忽略。艾薇懷疑影本能否證明完整所有權。承包商想把壞掉的櫃子丟在圍籬後。前任州長曾參與溫室開幕典禮。附近旅店提供房間給來訪稽核員。

Julian was used to a room becoming still when he entered it. This time the quiet made him uncomfortable. He had built a company by giving orders, but orders could not make the past disappear. Avery left the private argument and wrote down only what could be tested in public. The site files also contained short accounts of the market’s roof garden, shopkeepers, and old community exhibitions. Owen brought a small jar of berry jelly for the long shift. Ms. Tang called the night guard an old pal from farming days. A pile of repair bills lay on Victor's desk. The survey map marked an iron pole at the old garden gate.

景衡習慣自己一走進房間，眾人就安靜下來。這次的寂靜卻讓他不自在。他靠下指令建立公司，但指令無法讓過去消失。艾薇離開私人爭執，只記錄能在公開場合驗證的事項。現場檔案也收錄了市場屋頂花園、攤商及舊社區展覽的簡短記述。歐文為長班帶了一罐莓果果凍。唐女士稱夜班警衛是耕作年代的老朋友。維克多桌上堆著一疊維修帳單。測繪圖在舊園門處標記一根鐵柱。

The market was not a picture to be saved for a brochure. People needed safe doors, affordable spaces, clear agreements, and a way to disagree without losing their place. Julian declined to make her his excuse; he told his father the decision belonged to him. Avery checked the small details in the workers’ notes rather than ignoring them as unimportant. Avery would not rank money above the market cooperative's rights. An old razor lay in a box of abandoned staff items. A reliable signature mattered more than an impressive speech. The sponsor owned a new office in the western suburb.

市場不是供宣傳手冊展示的圖片。人們需要安全的門、負擔得起的空間、清楚的協議，以及能提出反對卻不失去立足之地的方法。景衡不拿她當藉口，告訴父親那是他自己的決定。艾薇仔細核對工人筆記裡的小細節，不因它們看似不起眼就忽略。艾薇不願把金錢排在市場合作社權利之上。員工遺留物品盒裡有一把舊刮鬍刀。可信的簽名比動聽演說更重要。贊助商在西郊有一間新辦公室。

When the meeting ended, the distance between them felt different. Avery could recognize Julian’s effort without forgiving every decision his company had made. He could admire her without expecting her work or her heart in return. Outside, he admitted that people often obeyed his surname before hearing his ideas. Avery checked the small details in the workers’ notes rather than ignoring them as unimportant. Victor argued that the new facilities would be superior to the old. A muddy trail led toward the abandoned service gate. Maya said unity required a fair vote, not silent agreement. An old register noted that two early donors would wed in spring.

會議結束時，兩人之間的距離似乎改變了。艾薇可以肯定景衡的努力，卻不必因此原諒公司做過的一切。他可以欣賞她，卻不能期待她回報工作或感情。在屋外，他承認人們常在聽他說明前，就先服從他的姓氏。艾薇仔細核對工人筆記裡的小細節，不因它們看似不起眼就忽略。維克多主張新設施會比舊設施更優越。一道泥濘小徑通往廢棄維修門。瑪雅說團結需要公平投票，而非沉默同意。舊登記冊記載兩名早期捐贈者將在春天結婚。

Avery did not mistake his silence for agreement. She explained the difference between an intention and an action, then gave him the space to make his own choice. Julian looked at the papers again. For a man whose signature could move millions, the hardest thing was to admit that a signature could not give him every right. They left the room with a question to answer, rather than a false victory to announce.

艾薇沒有把他的沉默當作同意。她說明想法和行動的差別，再給他自行選擇的空間。景衡重新看向文件。對一個簽名就能調動巨款的人而言，最困難的事是承認：簽名也不能賦予他所有權利。他們離開會議室時，帶走的是必須回答的問題，而非準備宣布的虛假勝利。
