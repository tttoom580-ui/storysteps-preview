The Story in the Shadow

The building had once been a place where different lives met. Avery tried to hear those lives before deciding what any wall was worth. The company saw a development site; she saw homes, work, and an unfinished argument. A frightening video of the old market spread online before the repair committee could meet. The site files also contained short accounts of the market’s roof garden, shopkeepers, and old community exhibitions. Owen felt an ache in his shoulder after moving the projector. An empty bookcase stood against the far exhibition wall. The visitor's complaint described a shadow taller than the door. Avery stayed conscious of the difference between fear and evidence.

這棟建築曾讓許多不同的人生在此交會。艾薇想先聽見這些人的故事，再決定一面牆值多少。公司看見的是開發基地；她看見的是家園、工作與一場尚未結束的爭論。維修委員會開會前，一段舊市場的可怕影片就在網路上傳開。現場檔案也收錄了市場屋頂花園、攤商及舊社區展覽的簡短記述。歐文搬完投影機後肩膀隱隱作痛。展覽廳遠牆旁立著一座空書櫃。遊客抱怨信描述一個比門還高的影子。艾薇始終意識到恐懼與證據的不同。

The people in the corridor waited for a quick answer. She knew a quick answer would please them today and perhaps hurt them tomorrow, so she pointed again to the missing evidence. The clip showed a tall shadow and claimed the building was too dangerous to save. Avery checked the small details in the workers’ notes rather than ignoring them as unimportant. The gift shop offered a discount on last year's guidebooks. A grasshopper rested on the warm window ledge. A hasty explanation could make the team ignore real danger. Avery asked Owen to inform visitors that the route was closed.

走廊裡的人等待一個簡單答案。她知道快速回答今天或許能讓人高興，明天卻可能傷害他們，因此她再次指向缺少的證據。影片出現高大的影子，聲稱建築危險到無法保存。艾薇仔細核對工人筆記裡的小細節，不因它們看似不起眼就忽略。禮品店為去年的導覽書打折。一隻蚱蜢停在溫暖窗台。倉促的解釋可能使團隊忽視真正危險。艾薇請歐文通知遊客路線封閉。

Julian was used to a room becoming still when he entered it. This time the quiet made him uncomfortable. He had built a company by giving orders, but orders could not make the past disappear. Avery traced the light to a forgotten projector and released the full test with the owner’s permission. The site files also contained short accounts of the market’s roof garden, shopkeepers, and old community exhibitions. The projector had been installed for a leisure festival. They used the light beam to locate the hidden projector. A harmless shadow had become a monster in local stories. Maya helped organize the original photographs by date.

景衡習慣自己一走進房間，眾人就安靜下來。這次的寂靜卻讓他不自在。他靠下指令建立公司，但指令無法讓過去消失。艾薇追查光源，找到一台被遺忘的投影機，並經持有人同意公開完整測試。現場檔案也收錄了市場屋頂花園、攤商及舊社區展覽的簡短記述。投影機原本是為休閒節慶裝設。他們沿光束找出藏起來的投影機。無害影子在地方故事裡變成怪物。瑪雅協助依日期整理原始照片。

The market was not a picture to be saved for a brochure. People needed safe doors, affordable spaces, clear agreements, and a way to disagree without losing their place. Julian ordered his public relations team to correct their earlier statement without attacking the person who posted it. Avery checked the small details in the workers’ notes rather than ignoring them as unimportant. A portrait of the founder hung near the control switch. A faded festival poster covered the old switch diagram. Avery agreed to release the test video after checking privacy. A model rocket from a science fair stood in the hall.

市場不是供宣傳手冊展示的圖片。人們需要安全的門、負擔得起的空間、清楚的協議，以及能提出反對卻不失去立足之地的方法。景衡要求公關團隊更正先前說法，但不攻擊發布影片的人。艾薇仔細核對工人筆記裡的小細節，不因它們看似不起眼就忽略。創辦人肖像掛在控制開關旁。褪色節慶海報蓋住舊開關圖。艾薇同意檢查隱私後公開測試影片。大廳立著科展的模型火箭。

When the meeting ended, the distance between them felt different. Avery could recognize Julian’s effort without forgiving every decision his company had made. He could admire her without expecting her work or her heart in return. Someone inside the company had supplied the misleading recording. Avery checked the small details in the workers’ notes rather than ignoring them as unimportant. The display included a paper swan folded by a child. The projector cable was pulled tight behind the curtain. The bright shadow had a unique shape caused by two lenses. Avery asked people not to answer rumors with violence.

會議結束時，兩人之間的距離似乎改變了。艾薇可以肯定景衡的努力，卻不必因此原諒公司做過的一切。他可以欣賞她，卻不能期待她回報工作或感情。有人從公司內部提供了這段誤導性的影片。艾薇仔細核對工人筆記裡的小細節，不因它們看似不起眼就忽略。展示品包含一隻孩子摺的紙天鵝。投影機線材在布幕後被拉得很緊。明亮影子因兩片鏡片形成獨特形狀。艾薇請大家不要以暴力回應謠言。

Avery wrote down the day’s result without calling it success. The notes included what had worked, what had failed, and which witnesses wanted to remain private. Julian read the summary and returned it with one question, not an order. She answered only after checking the original page. A few months earlier she would have expected a fight. Now the argument felt like the beginning of useful work.

艾薇記下當天結果，沒有把它直接稱為成功。筆記包含成功與失敗之處，以及希望保密的證人。景衡讀完摘要後，帶著一個問題還給她，而不是下命令。她查過原始頁面才回答。幾個月前她會預料兩人爭吵，如今這樣的討論反而像是有效工作的開端。

Before they left, Avery checked the route one more time and explained her decision to the people standing beside the gate. She did not want their agreement merely because a famous company president was present. She wanted questions that could be answered with dates, measurements, and names. Julian listened without reaching for his phone. The next step would take time, but this time everyone knew who was responsible for it.

離開前，艾薇又檢查一次路線，向站在門旁的人解釋自己的決定。她不希望大家只是因為知名集團總裁在場就點頭，而是希望每個問題都能以日期、測量與姓名回答。景衡沒有伸手拿手機，只是安靜聽著。下一步需要時間，但這次每個人都知道該由誰負責。
