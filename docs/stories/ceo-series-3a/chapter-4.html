The building had once been a place where different lives met. Avery tried to hear those lives before deciding what any wall was worth. The company saw a development site; she saw homes, work, and an unfinished argument. A school concert brought children and older neighbors back to the market courtyard. The site files also contained short accounts of the market’s roof garden, shopkeepers, and old community exhibitions. Avery's ambition was to restore the archive without hiding its faults. An old barn appeared on the roof garden's first site map. Owen raised his coat collar against the cold morning air. The garden had once hosted a small outdoor concert.

這棟建築曾讓許多不同的人生在此交會。艾薇想先聽見這些人的故事，再決定一面牆值多少。公司看見的是開發基地；她看見的是家園、工作與一場尚未結束的爭論。一場校園音樂會讓孩子與年長鄰居重返市場庭院。現場檔案也收錄了市場屋頂花園、攤商及舊社區展覽的簡短記述。艾薇的志向是修復檔案，而非掩蓋缺失。園區第一張地圖上畫有一座舊穀倉。歐文豎起外套領子抵擋清晨寒意。溫室以前曾舉行一場小型戶外音樂會。

The people in the corridor waited for a quick answer. She knew a quick answer would please them today and perhaps hurt them tomorrow, so she pointed again to the missing evidence. A senior manager offered Avery an impressive private contract that would forbid her from sharing the report. Avery checked the small details in the workers’ notes rather than ignoring them as unimportant. Similar labels could confuse even an experienced volunteer. The lights grew dim when the east fan started. The team would freeze the electronic records before further edits. Maya used gentle humor to calm the worried staff.

走廊裡的人等待一個簡單答案。她知道快速回答今天或許能讓人高興，明天卻可能傷害他們，因此她再次指向缺少的證據。一名高階經理拿出優渥的私人合約，卻要求艾薇不得公開調查報告。艾薇仔細核對工人筆記裡的小細節，不因它們看似不起眼就忽略。相似標籤連有經驗的志工也可能搞混。東側風扇啟動時，燈光變得昏暗。團隊會先凍結電子紀錄，防止繼續修改。瑪雅以溫和的幽默讓焦慮員工冷靜。

Julian was used to a room becoming still when he entered it. This time the quiet made him uncomfortable. He had built a company by giving orders, but orders could not make the past disappear. Avery refused and said the public should not pay for safety with silence. The site files also contained short accounts of the market’s roof garden, shopkeepers, and old community exhibitions. A junior gardener wrote down every moisture reading. A visiting novelist had described the glowing glass years ago. An onion plant beside the window had wilted from heat. A local painter had drawn the old ventilation tunnel.

景衡習慣自己一走進房間，眾人就安靜下來。這次的寂靜卻讓他不自在。他靠下指令建立公司，但指令無法讓過去消失。艾薇拒絕了，表示公眾不該用沉默交換安全。現場檔案也收錄了市場屋頂花園、攤商及舊社區展覽的簡短記述。一名初級園丁記下每次濕度讀數。一位來訪小說家多年前曾描寫發光玻璃。窗旁的洋蔥植株因高溫而枯萎。一位在地畫家曾畫下舊通風隧道。

The market was not a picture to be saved for a brochure. People needed safe doors, affordable spaces, clear agreements, and a way to disagree without losing their place. Julian removed the silence clause after reading it, although the board would be angry. Avery checked the small details in the workers’ notes rather than ignoring them as unimportant. The cold room held precious seeds no shop could replace. The fan's strange response appeared on the test chart. The kitchen used the dry oven to roast nuts for a festival. A broken pipe could spray warm mist onto the glass.

市場不是供宣傳手冊展示的圖片。人們需要安全的門、負擔得起的空間、清楚的協議，以及能提出反對卻不失去立足之地的方法。景衡讀過後刪除了保密條款，即使知道董事會會生氣。艾薇仔細核對工人筆記裡的小細節，不因它們看似不起眼就忽略。低溫室保存著商店無法替代的珍貴種子。風扇異常反應顯示在測試圖表上。廚房曾用乾燥烤箱烘烤節慶用堅果。破裂管線可能把溫暖水霧噴向玻璃。

When the meeting ended, the distance between them felt different. Avery could recognize Julian’s effort without forgiving every decision his company had made. He could admire her without expecting her work or her heart in return. For the first time, Avery noticed that his quiet answer cost him something real. Avery checked the small details in the workers’ notes rather than ignoring them as unimportant. A squirrel crossed the roof while the team measured the air. Avery gave the cold metal pipe a light tap. A traveler had reported the white mist in a visitor book. Maya described her vision of a public seed library.

會議結束時，兩人之間的距離似乎改變了。艾薇可以肯定景衡的努力，卻不必因此原諒公司做過的一切。他可以欣賞她，卻不能期待她回報工作或感情。艾薇第一次發現，他平靜的回答其實讓他付出了真實代價。艾薇仔細核對工人筆記裡的小細節，不因它們看似不起眼就忽略。團隊測量空氣時，一隻松鼠越過屋頂。艾薇輕敲了冰冷金屬管。一名旅客曾在訪客簿記下白霧。瑪雅說明她對公共種子圖書館的願景。

The old market had survived because its people were skilled at finding another way. They repaired tools, shared stories, and watched children grow. Avery wanted the new plan to protect that ordinary strength, not turn it into decoration. Julian understood that the cost would appear in his financial reports. He also understood that the cost of doing nothing had never appeared there at all.

舊市場能延續，是因為居民總能找到另一條路。他們修理工具、分享故事，看著孩子長大。艾薇希望新計畫守護這份平凡的力量，而不是把它變成裝飾。景衡知道這會增加財務報表上的成本，也明白什麼都不做的代價，從未出現在那些報表裡。

Before they left, Avery checked the route one more time and explained her decision to the people standing beside the gate. She did not want their agreement merely because a famous company president was present. She wanted questions that could be answered with dates, measurements, and names. Julian listened without reaching for his phone. The next step would take time, but this time everyone knew who was responsible for it.

離開前，艾薇又檢查一次路線，向站在門旁的人解釋自己的決定。她不希望大家只是因為知名集團總裁在場就點頭，而是希望每個問題都能以日期、測量與姓名回答。景衡沒有伸手拿手機，只是安靜聽著。下一步需要時間，但這次每個人都知道該由誰負責。
