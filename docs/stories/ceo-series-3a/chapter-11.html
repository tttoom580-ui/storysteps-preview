Letters from the Workers

She kept her notebook open when the room grew quiet. A beautiful promise was not the same as a signed responsibility, and she would not let an expensive presentation hide an unanswered question. A retired worker delivered a box of letters about the market’s first flood. The site files also contained short accounts of the market’s roof garden, shopkeepers, and old community exhibitions. One bloody fingerprint on the parcel came from a paper cut. A light breeze moved the loose letter across the desk. The original families deserved credit for protecting the seeds. Avery refused to label a clerk criminal before the investigation.

房間安靜下來時，她仍攤開筆記本。美麗的承諾不等於簽名承擔的責任，她不會讓昂貴的簡報掩蓋尚未回答的問題。一位退休工人送來一盒談及市場首次淹水的信件。現場檔案也收錄了市場屋頂花園、攤商及舊社區展覽的簡短記述。包裹上一枚帶血指印來自紙張割傷。微風吹動桌上鬆散的信紙。原始家戶應因保護種子獲得肯定。調查完成前，艾薇拒絕稱職員為罪犯。

Avery had learned that a city could lose its memory one small decision at a time. The danger rarely arrived with a warning; sometimes it came inside a polite email or a neat drawing. Several letters described unsafe orders, but none alone proved who gave them. Avery checked the small details in the workers’ notes rather than ignoring them as unimportant. The family letters made Maya emotional but not careless. Victor had said the sale might bring the garden a fortune. Old letters described hunger after a year of failed harvests. Ms. Tang watched a cat lick water from a clean stone.

艾薇早已明白，城市的記憶可能在一個又一個小決定中消失。危險很少帶著警告出現；有時它藏在一封客氣的電子郵件或一張整齊的圖面裡。好幾封信提到不安全的指令，但沒有任何一封能單獨證明是誰下令。艾薇仔細核對工人筆記裡的小細節，不因它們看似不起眼就忽略。家族信件讓瑪雅動容，卻未使她失去謹慎。維克多曾說出售可能為園區帶來巨額財富。舊信描述歉收之年後的飢餓。唐女士看見貓從乾淨石頭上舔水。

He watched Avery speak without looking to him for permission. Her confidence was not loud; it came from the fact that she could explain every step and accept questions about her own mistakes. Avery asked the workers’ association to review the material before names were made public. The site files also contained short accounts of the market’s roof garden, shopkeepers, and old community exhibitions. The accountant had packed a loaf of bread for the journey. The parcel had been wrapped twice to protect the letters. A list of donated crops included several kinds of pepper. The warehouse floor had a slight pitch toward the drain.

他看著艾薇說話，卻不向他尋求允許。她的自信並不張揚，而是來自她能解釋每個步驟，也願意接受對自身錯誤的提問。艾薇請工人協會先審閱資料，再決定是否公開姓名。現場檔案也收錄了市場屋頂花園、攤商及舊社區展覽的簡短記述。前會計為路程帶了一條麵包。包裹被包了兩層，以保護信件。捐贈作物清單包含數種辣椒。倉庫地板稍微傾斜，朝向排水口。

The answer required more than courage. Avery checked dates, asked who owned each document, and wrote down what still needed outside review. She could not control the whole company, but she could control the care she gave her work. Julian read his grandfather’s signature on a separate file and did not ask her to hide it. Avery checked the small details in the workers’ notes rather than ignoring them as unimportant. Avery would not pretend the letters proved every detail. Maya had to resist the urge to publish names immediately. A torn sleeve caught on the edge of an old box. A slender green stem grew from one donated seed.

答案需要的不只是勇氣。艾薇查核日期、追問文件所有權，也記下尚待外部審查的部分。她無法控制整間公司，卻能決定自己如何謹慎工作。景衡在另一份文件上看到祖父的簽名，卻沒有要求她隱瞞。艾薇仔細核對工人筆記裡的小細節，不因它們看似不起眼就忽略。艾薇不會假裝這些信證明了所有細節。瑪雅必須克制立刻公開姓名的衝動。破裂袖口勾住舊箱子邊緣。一根纖細綠莖從捐贈的種子長出。

Outside, the street continued its ordinary life. Someone opened a shop, someone carried supper home, and a child asked whether the market would still be there next year. Neither of them knew the answer yet. He said that love for a family could not turn a false record into truth. Avery checked the small details in the workers’ notes rather than ignoring them as unimportant. The outer path followed a gentle slope to the storage room. There was a surprising twist in the dates on the letters. The workers' union asked to review the safety findings. Avery wore an identification band around her wrist.

街道仍過著平凡生活。有人開店，有人提晚餐回家，一名孩子問市場明年是否還在。兩人此刻都不知道答案。他說愛自己的家人，也不能讓虛假的紀錄變成事實。艾薇仔細核對工人筆記裡的小細節，不因它們看似不起眼就忽略。外側小路沿緩坡通向儲藏室。信件日期出現令人驚訝的轉折。工會要求檢視安全調查結果。艾薇手腕上戴著識別手環。

Avery did not mistake his silence for agreement. She explained the difference between an intention and an action, then gave him the space to make his own choice. Julian looked at the papers again. For a man whose signature could move millions, the hardest thing was to admit that a signature could not give him every right. They left the room with a question to answer, rather than a false victory to announce.

艾薇沒有把他的沉默當作同意。她說明想法和行動的差別，再給他自行選擇的空間。景衡重新看向文件。對一個簽名就能調動巨款的人而言，最困難的事是承認：簽名也不能賦予他所有權利。他們離開會議室時，帶走的是必須回答的問題，而非準備宣布的虛假勝利。

Before they left, Avery checked the route one more time and explained her decision to the people standing beside the gate. She did not want their agreement merely because a famous company president was present. She wanted questions that could be answered with dates, measurements, and names. Julian listened without reaching for his phone. The next step would take time, but this time everyone knew who was responsible for it.

離開前，艾薇又檢查一次路線，向站在門旁的人解釋自己的決定。她不希望大家只是因為知名集團總裁在場就點頭，而是希望每個問題都能以日期、測量與姓名回答。景衡沒有伸手拿手機，只是安靜聽著。下一步需要時間，但這次每個人都知道該由誰負責。
