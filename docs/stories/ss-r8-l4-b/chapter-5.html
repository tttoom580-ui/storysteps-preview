The Price of a Trial

1995，最後一班渡輪的午餐 · 第 5 / 25 章 · 3000–4000 · 英中對照

When the first electric cooler arrived, Jiahe discovered that its adjustment switch had been damaged in transit. The seller offered a smaller portable device as a temporary replacement, but its capacity was clearly different. She could not enlarge the cooler by wishing it bigger. Bojun suggested borrowing money for a new machine, while their mother asked how much allowance remained in the family savings account. Jiahe felt anxiety rising as the ferry office demanded a decision by noon. She considered abandoning the trial, then remembered that a short pause would be safer than a rushed purchase. She telephoned the supplier, recorded the broken part, and asked for a written refund policy. The supplier agreed to collect the machine and provide a replacement after inspection.

第一台電動保冷箱送到時，佳禾發現調節開關在運送途中損壞。賣方願意先借一台小型便攜設備代替，但容量明顯不同。她不可能靠希望就把箱子變大。伯鈞建議借錢買新的，母親卻問家中還剩多少可動用的生活預備金。渡輪辦公室要求中午前決定，佳禾因此焦慮。她一度想放棄試辦，接著想起短暫停下來總比倉促購買安全。她打電話給供應商，記錄損壞零件，並要求書面退款辦法。供應商同意收回設備，檢查後提供替換品。

That afternoon, the island school invited her to explain the service during its arts day. A student orchestra was practicing beside a display of handmade boats, including a canoe. The teacher asked Jiahe to avoid the old idiom that every problem had one clever answer. Children, she said, often treated successful adults as idols and missed the work behind their decisions. Jiahe prepared an imaginative presentation with three boxes: one for safe meals, one for meals that required rechecking, and one for rejected meals. She let pupils put paper pictures into the boxes and explain their choices. Some protested that good food should never be thrown away. Jiahe agreed that waste mattered, but asked whether a favorable smell could prove that a meal was safe. The children soon found the problem in their own argument.

下午，島上學校邀她在藝術日介紹服務。學生管弦樂團正在手工船展旁練習，展品中還有一艘獨木舟。老師請佳禾避開「每個問題都有一個聰明答案」的老套說法，因為孩子常把成功大人當偶像，卻看不到背後的工作。佳禾準備了富想像力的展示：三個箱子分別代表安全餐、需要再檢查的餐，以及必須拒收的餐。她讓學生把紙製圖片放進箱子，再說明理由。有孩子抗議好食物不該丟掉。佳禾同意浪費值得重視，卻反問聞起來正常就能證明安全嗎？孩子很快發現自己論點中的漏洞。

At the school meeting, a restaurant owner raised an objection. He believed the ferry kitchen was receiving unfair public support and that his own business would lose customers. Jiahe asked him to inspect her accounts. She had paid market rent, wages, water charges, and the temporary license fee. No public money covered her losses. Still, she understood the impact that a new service could have on established shops. She proposed that island restaurants could supply sealed meals under the same safety rules and receive a fair share of orders. This was not a promise of instant prosperity. It was a test of whether cooperation could provide more reliable food without allowing one operator to seize all the business. The owner asked for two days to consider the proposal.

學校會議上，一位餐館老闆提出異議。他認為渡輪廚房取得不公平的公共支持，會搶走自己的客人。佳禾請他查閱帳目，裡面已列入市場租金、薪資、水費與臨時許可費，沒有公共資金補貼虧損。她仍理解新服務對既有店家的衝擊，因此提議島上餐館可以依相同安全規則供應密封餐點，公平分配訂單。這不是立即致富的保證，而是測試合作能否讓供餐更可靠、又不讓單一業者搶占全部生意。老闆請她給兩天考慮。

The school event also revealed a distinct difference between public excitement and actual demand. Many visitors praised the lunch service, yet only fourteen paid for orders. Jiahe separated compliments from purchase records before making the next budget. Bojun found the distinction discouraging, but A-Ling said it was better to learn now than after buying an expensive machine. They drew a chart showing the minimum orders needed to pay wages, rent, and ingredients. If the number was not reached for four weeks, the team would reconsider the service rather than use family savings without discussion. Jiahe placed the chart beside the damaged cooler receipt. She wanted every hopeful speech to have a matching calculation. The children might remember the colorful boxes from arts day; the adults had to remember who paid for them.

學校活動也顯示，公眾的熱情和真實需求之間存在明顯差異。許多訪客稱讚供餐服務，真正付錢訂購的卻只有十四人。佳禾在做下一份預算前，先把稱讚與購買紀錄分開。伯鈞覺得掃興，阿苓卻說現在發現總比買下昂貴機器後才知道好。團隊畫出最低訂單量，計算必須賣出多少餐才能支付工資、租金和食材。若連續四週達不到，就重新考慮營運，而不擅自動用家中積蓄。佳禾把圖表放在壞保冷箱收據旁，希望每段充滿希望的發言都有一份對應計算。孩子或許只記得藝術日的彩色箱子，大人卻必須記得是誰付錢買了它們。

That night, A-Ling read the day's notes and laughed at a sentence Jiahe had written in a hurry. One syllable was missing from the name of a supplier, turning a useful record into nonsense. They corrected it together. The error reminded Jiahe that even a good device, a careful plan, and an attractive speech depended on people checking small things. Her mother brought tea and asked whether Jiahe wanted the family's kitchen forever. "No," Jiahe said. "I want work that can pay the people who do it, including you if you choose to help." Her mother smiled, relieved to hear the word choose. The trial would continue, but only after the damaged equipment was replaced and the finances were reviewed again. That was a smaller victory than an expansion, yet it was one they could afford.

當晚，阿苓讀完當天紀錄，發現佳禾匆忙寫的一句話少了一個音節，連供應商名稱都變得不知所云。兩人一起訂正。這提醒佳禾，再好的設備、謹慎的計畫與動人的演說，都需要有人檢查細節。母親端茶進來，問佳禾是不是打算永遠使用家裡的廚房。「不是，」佳禾說，「我想建立一份能付得起每個工作者薪水的工作；你願意幫忙，才算你的工時。」母親聽見「願意」兩字，露出放心的笑容。試辦會繼續，但必須先更換損壞設備，再重新核算財務。那是比擴張更小的勝利，卻是她們負擔得起的勝利。
