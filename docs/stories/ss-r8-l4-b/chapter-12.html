失蹤的帳本

The Missing Ledger

1995，最後一班渡輪的午餐 · 第 12 / 25 章 · 3000–4000 · 英中對照 · 校訂中

用互動版查詞、朗讀與收藏

One rainy morning, the week's purchase ledger disappeared from the locked cabinet. Bojun immediately spoke of a burglar, but Jiahe asked everyone to follow the written procedure before making an accusation. They checked the kitchen, storage room, and paper bin, recording who had last used the book. A-Ling remembered that a delivery agent had requested a copy of the rice bill. The agent had signed a visitor sheet but had not been left alone in the office. These details did not give them a definite answer. Jiahe told the workers not to spread rumors. A missing book could lead to financial hardship and damaged trust, yet claiming theft without proof would cause another kind of destruction. They kept the day's orders moving while they searched.

一個下雨早晨，本週採購帳本從上鎖櫃子裡消失。伯鈞立刻說是闖入的小偷所為，佳禾卻要求大家按書面程序調查，不要先指控任何人。他們檢查廚房、儲藏室和紙類回收桶，記下最後使用帳本的人。阿苓記得一名送貨代理曾要求影印米價帳單，但那人簽過訪客表，也沒有獨自在辦公室待過。這些細節還無法形成確定答案。佳禾請員工不要散播謠言。帳本不見可能造成財務困難與信任受損，但沒證據就宣稱遭竊，也會帶來另一種破壞。搜尋期間，團隊仍維持當天已確認的訂單。

The accountant arrived wearing a silver bracelet and offered a calm comment: every paper record should have a durable copy. She had suggested carbon paper weeks earlier, but the team had delayed buying it. "I can say I told you so," she said, "or I can help now." Jiahe asked her to reconstruct the purchases from receipts. The workers had been faithful about signing deliveries, although the receipts occupied several different boxes. Their habit had seemed unnecessarily slow during busy mornings; now it was the reason they could pursue the truth. They checked the date and amount on each slip, leaving spaces where information was missing. No one was allowed to fill a gap from memory and present it as fact. The accountant said that discipline was more useful than confidence.

會計師戴著銀手環來到廚房，平靜地評論：每份紙本紀錄都應有耐用副本。她幾週前就建議使用複寫紙，但團隊一直拖延採購。「我可以說我早告訴過你，」她說，「也可以現在幫忙。」佳禾請她根據收據重建採購紀錄。員工一直忠實簽收，只是收據散放在幾個不同箱子裡。忙碌時這習慣看似緩慢，如今卻成了追查真相的依據。他們逐張核對日期與金額，缺資料的地方就留空，不許任何人憑記憶補上後當作事實。會計師說，這種紀律比自信更有用。

At noon, Aunt Huang brought photographs from an earlier era of island trading. She had once worked with a tribal crafts group that sold baskets at the port, and she remembered how carefully its members documented orders. The visit was not an answer to the missing ledger, but it helped Jiahe think about whom records were meant to protect. A business should not use the language of loyalty to silence questions. During the conversation, A-Ling recalled moving a pile of files when rain entered the window. Everyone searched the upper shelf again. Behind an empty rice tin, the book began to emerge from beneath a cloth cover. It was damp but readable. No burglar had entered; the movement had simply never been recorded.

中午，黃阿姨帶來較早年代島上交易的照片。她曾和原住民族手工藝團體合作，在港口販售籃子，記得成員如何細心記錄訂單。這次來訪並沒有直接解開帳本失蹤之謎，卻讓佳禾思考紀錄究竟為了保護誰。企業不應用忠誠的名義阻止發問。聊天時阿苓想起下雨時曾為避開滲水窗戶移動一疊文件。大家再次搜索高層架子，終於在空米罐後、布套下找出帳本。紙頁潮濕但仍可讀，根本沒有小偷進來，只是有人移動時忘了登記。

While reviewing the recovered ledger, Jiahe decided to commit a portion of the week's income to better records. The accountant said a well-kept book had little fame compared with a busy shopfront, but it could prevent expensive mistakes. They reorganized the room so that signed receipts would occupy one dry cabinet and unfinished papers another. A-Ling chose a colored label for documents awaiting review, and Mei checked that every worker understood it. The change took one slow afternoon, during which the team prepared no extra meals. Bojun worried that the pause would make them look unproductive. Jiahe reminded him that they were paying for the time and that avoiding another search was itself valuable work. The new system would be tested by a staff member who had never used the old shelves.

重新檢查找回的帳本時，佳禾決定從本週收入撥一部分，投入更好的紀錄設備。會計師說，保管完善的帳本不會像熱鬧櫥窗那樣出名，卻能避免昂貴錯誤。她們重新安排房間，已簽收據占用一個乾燥櫃子，未完成文件另放一處。阿苓替待審資料選定彩色標籤，小梅確認每名員工都看得懂。改動花了一個緩慢下午，期間沒有增做餐點。伯鈞擔心停下來看起來沒效率。佳禾提醒他，這段時間照樣付薪，而避免下一次大搜尋本身就是有價值的工作。新制度將請從未使用舊架子的員工測試。

Jiahe was relieved, then disappointed with how quickly everyone had suspected an outsider. She gathered the workers and admitted that the missing-book system had been her responsibility. The town was experiencing inflation, making every purchase difficult to replace, but worry had not justified the rumor. She committed to buying a second ledger, keeping duplicate receipts, and placing moved files in a clearly labeled tray. A-Ling apologized for not recording the relocation; Bojun apologized for speaking of a burglar without evidence. Nobody demanded a tragic punishment. Instead, they made one practical change each. Jiahe wrote the lesson on the inside cover of the recovered book: a careful system should make errors visible before fear turns them into accusations. The sentence stayed there for years.

佳禾鬆了口氣，卻也失望於大家如此快就懷疑外來者。她召集員工，承認帳本管理制度是自己的責任。當時物價通膨，任何採購都不容易補回，但焦慮不能為謠言開脫。她承諾購買第二本帳、保留重複收據，並設置標示清楚的文件移動托盤。阿苓為沒有登記搬動道歉，伯鈞則為無證據便說有小偷致歉。沒有人要求悲劇性的懲罰；每個人各提出一項實際改善。佳禾在找回的帳本封面內寫下：謹慎的制度，應讓錯誤在恐懼將它變成指控前被看見。這句話之後留在那裡多年。
