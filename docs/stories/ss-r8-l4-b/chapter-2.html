冷水不能保證的事

What Cold Water Cannot Promise

1995，最後一班渡輪的午餐 · 第 2 / 25 章 · 3000–4000 · 英中對照

The morning after the first trial, Jiahe visited the island's maritime museum because the ferry engineer had mentioned an old ice mill. From the hill above the harbor, she could see a landscape of fishing roofs and narrow waterways. At the museum entrance, children were studying a fossil fish beside a model fort. A science poster explained that an atom was small and that atomic energy was not the same thing as the cooling effect of melting ice. Jiahe smiled at the unexpected lesson. What mattered to her was a combination of insulation, travel time, and honest temperature records. A delicate thermometer could break during a rough voyage. She needed a method that did not depend on one instrument and did not pretend to guarantee safety without evidence.

第一次試辦隔天早晨，佳禾前往島上的海事博物館，因為渡輪工程師提到過一座舊製冰廠。從港口上方的山丘望去，可以看見漁村屋頂與狹窄水道構成的風景。博物館門口，孩子們正研究化石魚和堡壘模型。一張科學海報說明原子很小，原子能與冰融化產生的冷卻效果並不是同一回事。佳禾因這堂意外的科學課笑了出來。她需要的是隔熱、航行時間及誠實測溫的組合。一支精細的溫度計可能在顛簸航程中摔壞，她得設計不依賴單一儀器、也不會無憑據保證安全的方法。

The museum curator, who had managed immigration papers in his youth, opened a drawer of shipping records. He showed her how the old mill had once delivered ice before dawn, and how a messenger carried written arrival times from the pier to the fish market. Jiahe studied the extent of delays recorded during winter storms. One storm had held a boat for three hours, far longer than the schedule allowed. The curator spoke in a quiet murmur, almost as if he feared the ink would fade if he raised his voice. He also showed her an intimate letter from a sailor to his wife. It described a box of fish that had spoiled despite looking perfectly clean. Appearance, Jiahe realized, could tell a buyer very little about what had happened during the journey.

館長年輕時曾處理移民文件，現在從抽屜裡拿出航運紀錄。他讓佳禾看舊製冰廠如何在天亮前送冰，以及信差如何把靠港時間以紙條送到魚市。佳禾研究冬季暴風造成延誤的程度，其中一次竟讓船停了三小時，遠超原定時刻。館長低聲說話，彷彿聲音太大就會讓墨跡消失。他又展示一封水手寫給妻子的親密家書，裡面提到一箱外觀看似乾淨、實際卻已腐壞的魚。佳禾明白，貨品的外觀並不能告訴買家它一路經歷了什麼。

Back at the pier, she asked captain Jiang to let her test two containers on an empty morning run. He agreed to retain control over loading: the containers could not block exits or occupy seats. Jiahe would revise her plan if either container leaked or became too warm. Her new partner, A-Ling, was twenty-six and had worked in a bakery. She worried that testing would waste a productive morning. Jiahe explained the psychological cost of one food-poisoning incident: passengers might stop trusting the service for years, and someone could become seriously ill. A-Ling agreed to measure the ice at departure, halfway across, and on arrival. They used two thermometers rather than one and wrote the readings on paper both of them signed.

回到碼頭後，佳禾請江船長允許她在清晨空船航次測試兩種容器。船長同意，但裝載仍由他決定：容器不能堵住出口，也不能占用座位。若漏水或溫度過高，佳禾便會修改計畫。新夥伴阿苓二十六歲，曾在麵包店工作，她擔心測試會浪費一個原本能賺錢的早晨。佳禾解釋，一次食物中毒的心理代價可能延續數年，乘客可能再也不信任服務，甚至有人會重病。阿苓同意在出發、航程中段與抵達時量冰溫。她們不用單一溫度計，而以兩支交叉核對，並共同簽署紙本紀錄。

Before leaving the museum, Jiahe paused to gaze at an enormous wall chart showing ice deliveries over forty years. The chart had gaps where no one had kept records. The curator was careful to say that an empty space did not mean a boat had never sailed. Jiahe asked to copy the chart's legend and the dates of its most complete entries. She later compared them with captain Jiang's present timetable. Some old delays had been caused by weather; others had resulted from loading too much cargo at once. The distinction helped her design the next test. She would measure not only the temperature inside a box but also the time it spent waiting at the gate. Safety depended on the entire journey, including the minutes during which a lunch was not yet on the boat.

離開博物館前，佳禾停下凝視一張巨大的牆面圖表，上面記錄四十年間的送冰情況。有些年份留白，因為當時沒有人保存紀錄。館長謹慎說明，空白不代表那一年沒有船航行。佳禾請他讓自己抄下圖例，以及資料最完整的日期，再拿來和江船長現在的時刻表比較。有些舊延誤由天氣造成，另一些則是一次裝載過多貨物。這項區分幫她設計下次測試：不只量箱內溫度，也要記錄箱子在閘口等候的時間。安全取決於整段旅程，包含午餐還沒上船的那些分鐘。

The first box held its temperature on a calm crossing, while the second warmed too quickly when the sun reached the upper deck. Jiahe did not call the result a failure. It was a useful limit. She and A-Ling moved the delivery position below the shaded stair, where passengers would not trip over it. They also kept a separate container for food that could be served without cooling. Later, the curator came down to watch them and said the old sailors would have approved of their care. Jiahe answered that approval was pleasant but not proof. She wanted the trial to end with a written rule simple enough for a new worker to follow. By dusk, they had one: every loaded box needed a checked departure time, a checked temperature, and a named person responsible for rejecting unsafe meals.

第一個箱子在平穩航程中能保持溫度，第二個卻在陽光照到上層甲板後迅速升溫。佳禾不把結果當成失敗，而視為有用的限制。她與阿苓將送餐位置移到陰涼的樓梯旁，又確認箱子不會讓乘客絆倒，另外留一個容器放不必冷藏的食物。後來館長到碼頭看她們，說老水手一定會欣賞這份謹慎。佳禾回答，欣賞固然好，卻不是證據。她希望測試結束時能寫出新進員工也能遵守的規則。傍晚，她們完成了：每個上船的箱子都須核對出發時間、溫度，並指定一個有權拒收不安全餐點的負責人。
