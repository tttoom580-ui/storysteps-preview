渡輪辦公室的電腦

The Computer at the Ferry Office

The ferry office acquired a secondhand computer to replace its crowded paper order board. A student who understood simple software volunteered to explain the machine, but Jiahe insisted that the kitchen retain a paper record during the trial. The new system could estimate orders, yet it knew nothing about sudden illness or a late passenger. Its numbers were abstract until someone checked them against real people. The student printed a bulletin showing the first week of results. On the wall nearby hung a wedding photograph of a bridegroom who had once worked on the boat; the groom now ran a shop across the gulf. Jiahe noticed how the old photograph invited affection, while the computer invited an assumption that newer information must always be better. She trusted neither without checking.

渡輪辦公室買了一台二手電腦，準備取代擁擠的紙本訂單板。一名懂簡單軟體的學生自願教大家使用，但佳禾堅持試辦期間保留紙本紀錄。新系統能估計訂單，卻不了解突發疾病或遲到乘客；若不拿來和真實人物核對，那些數字仍然抽象。學生印出第一週結果公告。附近牆上掛著一張新郎婚照，他以前曾在船上工作，如今在海灣另一側開店。佳禾發現，舊照片容易讓人產生感情，新電腦則容易讓人以為較新的資訊一定更好。她不會不經核對就相信任何一方。

A discussion began about whether the machine should constitute an official order record. The clerk Mingjuan argued that it would reduce errors. A civilian passenger representative disagreed because older customers could not always read the printed codes. Their enthusiasm was sincere, but the debate grew intense when Bojun began to dominate the room. Jiahe used a quiet cue agreed upon at the start of the meeting: she raised one finger, and each person was given two minutes to speak. The clerk was surprised that such a small gesture worked. A local composer attending the meeting compared it to conducting music. Strong voices, he said, must sometimes pause so that quieter instruments can be heard. Jiahe wrote the metaphor in her notebook because it described the kind of management she wanted.

眾人討論電腦紀錄是否應成為正式訂單依據。辦事員明娟認為這能降低錯誤，一名一般乘客代表卻反對，因為年長客人不一定看得懂列印代碼。雙方都很熱心，但伯鈞開始主導整場討論，讓氣氛變得激烈。佳禾做出會前約好的安靜手勢：舉起一根手指，每人接著有兩分鐘發言。明娟驚訝這樣簡單的方法竟然有效。參加會議的本地作曲家說，這就像指揮音樂；有時強勢樂器要停下來，才能聽見較安靜的聲部。佳禾把這個比喻記下，因為它正好形容她想建立的管理方式。

The next problem came from an argument about gender. Someone suggested that men should handle technology and women should handle the cooking. A-Ling asked whether that rule was written anywhere. The computer student, who was female, gave a patient demonstration and ended the discussion with her skill. Jiahe asked her to teach Mingjuan how to review the entries and correct mistakes without erasing the original paper forms. On a map beside the desk, fertile fields around the harbor appeared bright green. The students hoped improved ordering might reduce food waste from those farms. That would be useful, Jiahe said, but the software must not invade the privacy of customers by collecting addresses it did not need. Only order numbers and contact arrangements would be entered.

下一個問題來自性別分工的爭論。有人提議男人處理科技、女人負責烹調。阿苓問這條規則寫在哪裡。那名電腦學生其實是女性，她耐心示範操作，以能力結束了爭議。佳禾請她教明娟審核輸入內容，並在不擦除原始紙單的情況下更正錯誤。桌旁地圖上，港口周圍肥沃的農田一片青綠。學生希望改進訂單能減少農場食材浪費。佳禾說這很有用，但軟體不能收集不必要的住址、侵犯顧客隱私，因此只輸入訂單號碼和聯絡安排。

During the second computer trial, the intensity of the morning rush made the student operator skip a verification step. Mingjuan spotted a mismatch between three printed orders and the signed paper list. Rather than blame the student, she proposed a systematic routine: one person entered the data, another compared totals, and the originals stayed in a locked drawer. Jiahe approved the method but asked what would happen when only one trained clerk was on duty. They agreed to switch to paper-only orders rather than pretend a double check had occurred. A-Ling tested the rule during a quiet afternoon, and the result matched the written records. The student said she had expected criticism for the error. Jiahe replied that learning a reliable method mattered more than protecting anyone's pride.

第二次電腦試辦時，清晨繁忙的強度使學生操作員跳過了一道驗證程序。明娟發現三張列印訂單和已簽署紙本名單不符。她沒有責怪學生，而提出系統化流程：一人輸入，另一人核對總數，原件鎖入抽屜。佳禾贊成，但問如果值班時只有一名受訓辦事員怎麼辦。大家同意改用純紙本訂單，不假裝已完成雙人覆核。阿苓在安靜的下午測試，結果與書面紀錄一致。學生說原以為會因錯誤受責備。佳禾回答，學會可靠方法，比維護任何人的面子都重要。

At the end of the week, the system produced two different totals because one canceled order had been entered twice. Mingjuan found the mistake in three minutes using the paper sheet. Nobody needed to blame the student; the error showed what the trial was for. A rival restaurant owner admitted he felt jealousy when he saw the ferry kitchen attracting attention, but he also wanted his own orders handled accurately. Jiahe invited him to join a shared record test without giving up control of his shop. A learned fisherman at the back of the room asked whether they could still write a receipt by hand during a power failure. "Yes," Jiahe replied. "A machine should help us serve people, not make service impossible when it stops." That answer won the day's quietest applause.

週末，系統因一筆取消訂單被輸入兩次，產生了兩組不同的總數。明娟利用紙本資料，只花三分鐘就找到錯誤。沒有人需要責怪學生，因為測試本來就是用來發現問題。一位競爭餐館老闆坦承，看到渡輪廚房受關注時自己會嫉妒，但也希望訂單能準確處理。佳禾邀他參加共同紀錄試辦，並不要求交出店舖控制權。後排一位博學漁民問停電時能否繼續手寫收據。「可以，」佳禾回答，「機器應該協助我們服務人，不該讓它一停就完全無法服務。」這句話贏得當天最安靜卻真誠的掌聲。
