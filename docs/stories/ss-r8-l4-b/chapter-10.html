爐後的一道火光

A Flame Behind the Stove

1995，最後一班渡輪的午餐 · 第 10 / 25 章 · 3000–4000 · 英中對照 · 校訂中

用互動版查詞、朗讀與收藏

On the day of the island harvest festival, a faulty gas hose released a small flame behind the kitchen stove. A-Ling noticed the smell and immediately turned off the supply. There was a sharp pop, almost an explosion, but no fire spread beyond the stove. Bojun suffered a shallow cut while moving a loose tray and received a bandage at the clinic. A visiting surgeon checked the injury and said it was minor, yet he praised the team for shutting the kitchen instead of continuing to cook. Jiahe placed a sign on the door forbidding further use of the stove until a qualified mechanic had examined it. The loss of festival sales was critical, but food safety mattered more than a mere day's profit. She phoned every customer waiting for an order.

島上豐收節當天，廚房爐子後一段有問題的瓦斯管冒出一道小火光。阿苓聞到異味，立刻關掉供氣。現場傳出一聲尖響，幾乎像爆炸，但火沒有蔓延出爐台。伯鈞移動鬆動托盤時被割出淺傷，到診所包紮。一位來訪外科醫師檢查後說傷勢輕微，卻稱讚團隊沒有硬撐營業，而是關閉廚房。佳禾在門上貼公告，禁止在合格技師檢查前再使用爐具。失去節慶營收是重大打擊，但食品安全比單日利潤更重要。她逐一打電話通知等待訂單的顧客。

The repair bill led to a difficult bargain. The landlord wanted the kitchen to pay for every part of the old gas line. Jiahe brought the lease, inspection notes, and photographs to illustrate which section had been damaged before they moved in. The landlord looked suspicious at first, then agreed that a shared inspection was reasonable. They reached a resolution: he would replace the building's fixed pipe, while the kitchen would install its own approved stove connection and pay for routine checks. Jiahe wrote an apology to the families whose orders had been canceled. One customer, a pregnant teacher, thanked her for not taking the risk. Another said the loss had spoiled an important celebration. Jiahe listened to both without trying to congratulate herself on following the rules.

維修帳單引發艱難的費用談判。房東要廚房負擔老瓦斯管的每一個零件。佳禾拿出租約、檢查紀錄與照片，說明哪些管線在她們搬入之前就已損壞。房東起初懷疑，後來同意共同檢查合理。雙方達成協議：房東更換建物固定管線，廚房自行安裝合格爐具接頭，並負擔日常檢查。佳禾寫信向訂單被取消的家庭道歉。一位懷孕老師謝謝她沒有冒險，另一位則說這次停工毀了重要慶祝活動。佳禾都認真聽，沒有把守規矩當成值得自我祝賀的事。

While the repairs continued, Jiahe attended a college exhibition about local civilization. Students showed photographs of an early settler's house, a hillside tomb, and the first public kitchen in the district. Another booth introduced a simple website on a university computer connected by telephone line. It loaded slowly, and the professor warned that few local families had access to it. Jiahe wondered whether future announcements might appear there, but she refused to abandon the paper notice board. A display about a peaceful political revolution described how ordinary workers had demanded public records, and it made her think of the kitchen ledger. She could not know what the next decade would bring. She could only choose methods that the people she served could actually use in 1995.

維修期間，佳禾去參觀大學舉辦的地方文明展。學生展示早期移居者的房屋、山坡古墓，以及本區第一座公共廚房的照片。另一個攤位介紹大學電腦透過電話線連接的簡易網站，讀取速度很慢，教授也提醒在地家庭很少能使用。佳禾想，也許將來公告可以放在這裡，但她不會因此撤掉紙本公告欄。關於和平政治革命的展覽記錄了普通工人如何爭取公開檔案，讓她想起廚房帳本。她不知道下一個十年會如何變化，只能選擇一九九五年服務對象真正用得上的方法。

The mechanic asked Jiahe to forbid anyone from moving the stove until the gas connection had passed a pressure test. She put a bright tag on the handle and asked every worker to sign the temporary closure sheet. A-Ling proposed cooking in a neighbor's kitchen for the festival, but the health officer explained that the neighbor's premises were not covered by their approval. Jiahe refused the shortcut, although she offered to help direct customers to other licensed shops. The decision cost the team a day's sales and some angry telephone calls. That evening, Bojun asked whether saying no made Jiahe feel weak. She answered that refusing an unsafe job was not weakness; it was the same responsibility they expected from a captain who kept a boat in port during bad weather.

機械師請佳禾禁止任何人在瓦斯接頭通過壓力測試前移動爐具。她在把手掛上鮮明標籤，要求每名員工簽署臨時停用表。阿苓提議在鄰居廚房做節慶餐，但衛生員解釋，鄰居場地不在許可範圍內。佳禾拒絕走捷徑，卻願意協助顧客轉向其他持照店家。這項決定讓團隊損失一天營收，也接到幾通不滿電話。當晚伯鈞問，說不會不會讓佳禾覺得軟弱。她回答，拒絕不安全工作不是軟弱，而是與船長在天氣惡劣時把船留在港裡一樣的責任。

On Friday, the mechanic signed the repair record, the health officer returned, and the kitchen reopened with a reduced menu. The team still needed two more weeks of observation before increasing orders. Jiahe gathered the workers to review the ten chapters of their own journey, as she jokingly called the stack of daily reports. They had learned that an attractive promise could hide an expense, a small mistake could require a public correction, and an accident could reveal a weakness before anyone was seriously hurt. Bojun said the project was beginning to look prosperous. Jiahe shook her head and pointed to the repair reserve. "Not yet," she said. "We are learning to survive an ordinary bad week. That is enough progress for now." Even A-Ling, who had been the most impatient, agreed.

星期五，技師簽妥修復紀錄，衛生員複查後，廚房以縮減菜單重新營業。團隊仍得再觀察兩週才能增加訂單。佳禾召集員工回顧厚厚的每日報告，開玩笑說這就是她們旅程的十個篇章。她們學到動人的承諾可能隱藏成本，小錯誤也必須公開更正，一次事故則可能在真正傷人前暴露弱點。伯鈞說計畫看起來開始興旺了。佳禾搖頭，指向維修準備金：「還沒有。我們只是學著度過平凡的壞日子。現在做到這樣，已經算進步。」連向來最沒耐心的阿苓也同意。
