渡輪不是餐廳

The Ferry Is Not a Dining Room

1995，最後一班渡輪的午餐 · 第 3 / 25 章 · 3000–4000 · 英中對照

用互動版查詞、朗讀與收藏

By Monday, twenty families had asked for lunch tickets, and Jiahe had to decide who would receive the first deliveries. The ferry served patients, fishermen, students, and an intermediate school on the island. She chose not to assign meals by friendship. Instead, she announced a simple priority rule: paid orders placed by the previous evening would be filled in order, while a small number of emergency meals would remain for delayed patients. She calculated the calorie range of each box with help from a clinic nurse. A meal did not have to be luxurious, but it needed enough food for a long working day. A raisin bread roll was added to the children's option. A passenger called the idea incredible; another complained that rice alone should have been cheaper. Jiahe invited both to examine the actual ingredient costs.

星期一之前，已有二十戶家庭詢問便當票，佳禾必須決定先供應誰。渡輪服務病患、漁民、學生，以及島上一所中學。她不願按交情分配餐點，而公布簡單的優先規則：前晚完成訂購及付款者依順序供應，另留少量緊急餐給行程延誤的病患。她請診所護理師協助估算每盒熱量範圍。便當不必豪華，但得足以支持漫長工作日。兒童餐另加一個葡萄乾麵包捲。有乘客稱這主意不可思議，也有人抱怨只有飯應該更便宜；佳禾請雙方一起看真正的食材成本。

On the first full crossing, a lifeguard traveling to the island pool asked where the boxes were stored. He had recently been assigned an exercise about emergency evacuation and wanted the aisles clear. Jiahe showed him the storage diagram. Then a tourist took a photograph of the boxes beside a life jacket and claimed the company was turning the ferry into a restaurant. The picture lacked context: none of the boxes blocked the exit, and passengers were not eating near the engine room. Jiahe resisted the temptation to insult the tourist. She asked the captain to defend the safety arrangement in writing rather than through an angry argument. The captain agreed, but told her to concentrate on accurate facts, not on whether the tourist liked her business.

第一次完整航行時，一名準備去島上泳池的救生員問便當箱放在哪裡。他最近接受緊急疏散作業，特別在意走道暢通。佳禾拿出儲存位置圖。隨後一名遊客拍下便當箱和救生衣同框的照片，宣稱渡輪正在變成餐廳。但照片缺少脈絡：箱子沒有堵住出口，乘客也沒有在機房附近吃飯。佳禾忍住辱罵遊客的衝動，請船長以書面說明安全安排，而非用爭吵回應。船長同意，卻叮嚀她專注於正確事實，不必在意遊客喜不喜歡她的生意。

That afternoon, A-Ling stayed after closing to do the housework at her shared lodging, then returned to help clean the kitchen. Jiahe realized she had mistaken A-Ling's quiet pace for endless energy. The young cook explained that she was supporting two younger sisters and could not work without predictable hours. Her inspiration for joining the lunch project was not a dream of fame; she wanted a job with written wages. Jiahe revised the assignment sheet so closing duties would rotate and nobody would be expected to work for free. Their first five days had produced twelve percent waste, a percentage too high for a small kitchen. Reducing it would matter more than selling five extra meals. A-Ling proposed asking customers to select smaller portions in advance.

當天下午，阿苓收工後先回合租住處做家務，再回來協助打掃廚房。佳禾這才發現，自己把阿苓安靜穩定的工作步調誤以為用不完的精力。阿苓說她要扶養兩個妹妹，不能接受沒有固定工時的工作。她加入便當計畫不是為了成名，而是想要一份有書面薪資的工作。佳禾修改分工表，讓收尾工作輪流擔任，任何人都不必無薪勞動。前五天的浪費率達百分之十二，對小廚房而言太高。降低浪費，比多賣五盒更重要。阿苓提出讓客人提前選擇小份量的辦法。

The tourist's photograph appeared in a local newspaper two days later. A photography student noticed that the image had been cropped just beyond the safety line painted on the deck. She offered a second picture taken from a wider angle, with the exit and storage area both visible. Jiahe did not ask the newspaper to print a flattering story. She wrote a short letter giving the loading measurements and inviting the reporter to check them. The editor agreed to publish a correction, though only in a small column. The experience was uncomfortable. Jiahe had once believed a clear fact would speak for itself, but she was learning that evidence needed context and people willing to read it. The student kept ownership of her picture and gave permission for one specific use.

兩天後，遊客照片出現在地方報紙上。一名學攝影的學生注意到，照片剛好裁掉甲板上的安全線。她提供從更廣角度拍攝的第二張照片，讓出口與儲存區都看得見。佳禾沒有要求報紙寫讚美報導，只寫短函列出裝載尺寸，邀記者自行核對。編輯同意在小欄位刊登更正。這經驗讓人不舒服：佳禾原以為清楚事實能自己說話，如今才知道證據需要脈絡，也需要有人願意閱讀。學生保留照片著作權，只同意這一次指定用途。

The week ended with a meeting in the ferry waiting room. Some passengers wanted the service to proceed, while others feared it would slow boarding. Jiahe described its maximum daily capacity, the cleaning schedule, and the right of the captain to reject a load. She did not promise to solve every passenger's need. She promised that the evidence would be public and the price would not change without notice. When the manager asked why someone with hospital experience had returned to a ferry, Jiahe answered that a ferry was a moving link between homes and services, not merely a machine for carrying tickets. She wanted the people who crossed it to be able to trust one another. After the meeting, she watched the last boat leave at a careful pace and knew the real trial had only begun.

這一週在渡輪候船室的會議中結束。有些乘客希望服務繼續，也有人擔心影響登船速度。佳禾說明每日供餐上限、清潔時程，以及船長拒絕裝載的權利。她沒有承諾解決所有乘客需求，只承諾證據公開，價格變更前一定通知。經理問她，為何有醫院工作經驗的人要回來做渡輪便當。佳禾回答，渡輪是串連住家和各種服務的移動通道，不只是運送車票的機器；她希望每天渡海的人能彼此信任。會後她看著末班船以謹慎的速度離港，明白真正的考驗才剛開始。
