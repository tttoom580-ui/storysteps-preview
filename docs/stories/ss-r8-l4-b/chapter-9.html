The Refund Notice

1995，最後一班渡輪的午餐 · 第 9 / 25 章 · 3000–4000 · 英中對照 · 校訂中

A storm interrupted the morning crossing, and a handwritten notice appeared at the ferry gate claiming that all lunch payments were lost. The notice had a copied company seal as an attachment, but the writing carried the strange accent of a visitor rather than the usual office language. Jiahe frowned. Their policy offered a full refund when the boat could not sail. She asked Mingjuan whether the office had issued the paper. Mingjuan said no. A clumsy attempt to imitate an official notice could mislead dozens of customers and damage the kitchen's economic future. Jiahe was careful not to accuse a rival without proof. She took the notice down, photographed it with a film camera, and posted a clear correction beside the ticket window.

暴風中斷早晨航次，渡輪入口突然貼出一張手寫公告，聲稱所有便當費用都不退。紙上附了複製的公司印章，但用語帶著陌生人的口吻，不像辦公室慣用的寫法。佳禾皺眉。她們的政策明明規定停航須全額退款。佳禾問明娟是否由辦公室張貼，明娟回答沒有。這樣拙劣模仿官方告示，仍可能誤導數十名顧客，傷害廚房未來的經濟基礎。佳禾沒有證據就不指控競爭者，而是取下公告、用底片相機拍照，再在售票口旁貼出清楚的更正說明。

In the waiting room, a man leaned toward the counter to peep at a customer's order list. A-Ling asked him to step back. He said he only wanted to know whether a neighbor from his homeland was traveling that day. Jiahe explained that the list was private, whatever his reason. She gave him the public sailing information instead. A child wearing one earphone ate a crunchy biscuit and asked why adults were so upset about paper. Jiahe said an interruption in transport could become an interruption in a patient's appointment, a worker's pay, or a family's meal. The child thought about it, then asked whether people could choose to keep their credit for another day. Jiahe agreed to offer that choice alongside the refund, with written consent.

候船室裡，一個男人靠向櫃檯，想偷看顧客訂單名單。阿苓請他退後。他說只是想知道同鄉鄰居今天是否搭船。佳禾解釋，不論原因如何，名單都屬於私人資料，於是改提供公開航班資訊。一個戴著單邊耳機、咬著酥脆餅乾的孩子問，大人為什麼對一張紙這麼生氣。佳禾說，交通中斷可能連帶使病人錯過門診、工人少領薪水，或家庭吃不到飯。孩子想了想，問能不能自願把費用留到隔天使用。佳禾同意增加這種選擇，並要求留下書面同意。

By noon, the person who had posted the notice appeared. He was a temporary porter who believed refunds would cause the kitchen to collapse. He had not acted on orders from another shop. His intentions did not excuse him, and Jiahe instructed him to write an apology and explain exactly what he had done. She did not wink at the error or pretend that kindness erased consequences. The porter said he had little education and had thought the seal made the notice look important. Jiahe explained that modesty was better than false authority: he could have asked for help. They agreed that only Mingjuan and Jiahe could issue changes to payment terms, and every notice would carry a date, signature, and copy in the ledger.

中午，張貼公告的人出現了。他是臨時搬運工，以為一旦退款，廚房就會倒閉，並非受其他店家指使。動機不能替行為開脫，佳禾要他寫道歉信，說清楚自己做了什麼。她沒有對錯誤眨眼放過，也不假裝善意能抹除後果。搬運工說自己讀書不多，以為加個印章公告就會顯得重要。佳禾告訴他，謙遜比假裝有權更好，有疑問原可先詢問。大家決定往後只有明娟和佳禾能發布付款條款變更，每張公告都要有日期、簽名和留存副本。

A customer asked to read the full content of the false notice before it was destroyed. Jiahe provided a copy marked clearly as incorrect and explained the circumstance of its discovery. The customer wanted to know whether a porter should be allowed to work again. Jiahe said his duties would be reviewed, he would receive training, and he would not issue financial notices. Removing him without a fair process would not automatically repair the harm. They also tested the new refund form with older passengers, who found two questions confusing. Mingjuan rewrote them in ordinary language. The revised form asked whether the customer wanted money returned or credit held for a later sailing; neither option was selected in advance. Jiahe wanted consent to be a real choice, not a box checked by somebody else.

一名顧客想在假公告銷毀前閱讀全部內容。佳禾提供標明「錯誤」的副本，並解釋發現時的情況。顧客問，貼公告的搬運工是否應繼續工作。佳禾說會檢討他的職責、安排訓練，且不再讓他發布財務公告；若沒有公平程序就解僱，也不會自動修復傷害。她們還找年長乘客測試新退款表，發現兩道問題難懂，明娟便改用日常語言。新版表格讓顧客選擇退錢，或將金額保留到下一班船；沒有任何一項預先打勾。佳禾希望同意是真實選擇，不是由別人替顧客勾好的格子。

The incident reminded Jiahe of a revolutionary idea she had heard at a community lecture: public trust grows when mistakes can be corrected openly. The speaker had compared the spread of false notices with rumors about a nuclear power station miles away. In both cases, fear traveled faster than facts unless someone explained the evidence. Jiahe did not need a dramatic speech to act on the lesson. She refunded the canceled orders, counted every remaining coin, and placed the final balance where workers could see it. Their cash reserve was smaller, but their promises remained intact. When the rain stopped at sunset, Bojun asked whether she regretted refusing the false notice. Jiahe looked out at the empty pier and said, "If our first rule disappears in bad weather, it was never a rule at all."

這件事讓佳禾想起社區講座的一個革命性觀念：錯誤能公開更正，公共信任才會成長。講者曾把假公告的傳播，比作數里外核能電廠的謠言；兩者都可能讓恐懼跑得比事實快，除非有人解釋證據。佳禾不必發表激昂演說就能實踐這堂課。她退回取消訂單的費用，清點每一枚硬幣，把最後餘額放在員工看得見的地方。現金準備金變少了，但承諾仍然完整。傍晚雨停，伯鈞問她是否後悔不肯接受那張假公告。佳禾望向空蕩碼頭說：「如果我們的第一條規則一遇壞天氣就消失，那它從來不算一條規則。」
