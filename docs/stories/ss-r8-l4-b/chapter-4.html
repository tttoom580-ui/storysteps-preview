The Inspection Window

1995，最後一班渡輪的午餐 · 第 4 / 25 章 · 3000–4000 · 英中對照

The district health officer, Ms. Luo, came to accompany Jiahe through the temporary kitchen. She did not offer a friendly greeting until she had examined the handwashing station. On the wall hung a diagram of the preparation area and a draft cleaning manual. The arrangement was experimental: a rented storeroom had been divided by washable screens instead of permanent walls. Luo said the screens might be acceptable, but the team had to prove that a germ from raw ingredients could not reach cooked food. Jiahe asked whether the inspector would explain the essential requirements in writing. "Of course," Luo answered. "A rule hidden in my head would be useless to the next cook." She also warned that a good-looking room was no substitute for actual evidence.

區衛生員羅女士來陪佳禾檢查臨時廚房。她直到看完洗手設備才打招呼。牆上掛著備餐區配置圖，以及一份清潔手冊草稿。這種安排屬於試驗性質：租來的倉房用可清洗屏風隔開，而不是築起永久牆面。羅女士說屏風或許可行，但團隊必須證明生食的病菌不會接觸熟食。佳禾請稽查員把必要條件寫成書面。羅女士答應：「只藏在我腦袋裡的規則，對下一位廚師沒有用。」她也提醒，房間看起來漂亮並不代表有實際證據。

At the pier, the ferry mechanic was trying to loosen a rusted valve. The captain asked him to strengthen the emergency handrail before the coming holiday. A magnificent sunset made the sea appear calm, but a satellite weather bulletin showed a storm beyond the horizon. The mechanic had once served on a research vessel that carried diplomats and visiting scientists; he said even a diplomat could not negotiate with a storm. Jiahe laughed, then felt a brief hesitation. Her business depended on a timetable that the sea could cancel without warning. She asked the captain to identify the point after which a lunch order could no longer be loaded safely. He drew a red line on her form and said that once the loading gate shut, no late delivery would be accepted.

碼頭邊，渡輪機械師正設法鬆開生鏽的閥門。船長請他在假日前加固緊急扶手。壯麗的晚霞讓海面看來平靜，但衛星天氣通報顯示地平線外正有暴風。機械師曾在載運外交官及訪問學者的研究船工作，他說連外交官也無法和暴風談判。佳禾笑了，接著短暫遲疑：她的生意依靠一張隨時可能被海象取消的時刻表。她請船長指出便當最後能安全裝載的時間。船長在表格上畫紅線，說裝載門一旦關上，任何遲到的餐點都不能再上船。

The following day, students from a recreation club asked to use the kitchen yard for a small music event. Its main attraction would be a choir that had won an outstanding school prize. Jiahe wanted to help, but the event would bring visitors close to food storage. She offered a separate outdoor table and asked the club to take its rubbish away. When a student complained that she was being unfriendly, she explained that hygiene rules protected their event as well. A music festival could not become an excuse to flee responsibility for the kitchen. The student returned with a sketch showing a safer path between the stage and the washroom. Jiahe accepted it and included the drawing in the manual. Good planning, she thought, was not a gift from one expert but an exchange of observations.

隔天，休閒社團的學生要求借廚房外的院子辦小型音樂活動，最吸引人的節目是一支曾獲學校大獎的合唱團。佳禾願意幫忙，但活動會讓訪客接近食物儲存區。她提供另一張戶外桌子，並請社團自行清理垃圾。有學生抱怨她不友善，她解釋衛生規則同樣是在保護活動。音樂節不能成為逃避廚房責任的理由。那名學生之後帶來一張草圖，標出舞台與洗手間之間更安全的走道。佳禾接受，並把圖放進手冊。她想，好的規劃不是某位專家的恩賜，而是彼此分享觀察的結果。

During the second visit, the inspector opened the manual to a page on broken glass. The kitchen had a plan for cleaning floors but no detailed procedure for a shattered thermometer near ingredients. Jiahe did not argue that such an accident was unlikely. She asked the team to rehearse what they would do: stop preparation, remove all exposed food, protect their hands, and record the disposal. They carried out the practice with harmless paper pieces instead of glass. A-Ling found that the waste bin was too far from the preparation table. Moving it closer required checking that the new position would not block the exit. The inspector watched the entire exercise and asked them to write down the change. A manual, she said, was useful only when actual workers could follow it under pressure.

第二次訪查時，稽查員翻開手冊，找到玻璃破裂的處理頁。廚房雖有地板清潔計畫，卻沒有詳細規定：若溫度計在食材旁摔碎該怎麼做。佳禾不辯稱事故機率很低，而要求團隊演練：停止備餐、移走所有可能接觸碎片的食物、保護雙手，並記錄丟棄數量。她們用無害紙片代替碎玻璃演練。阿苓發現垃圾桶離工作桌太遠，移近時又得確認不會堵住出口。稽查員看完整個過程，要求把改動寫下。她說，真正有用的手冊，是員工在壓力下仍能照做的手冊。

By Friday, Luo approved a limited license for thirty lunches per crossing, subject to weekly temperature records and a second visit. Jiahe put the written conditions beside the cash box so every worker could read them. She also asked the mechanic to review the loading route once more. He found a corner where a delivery cart could strike a loose pipe, and they marked it with tape. The captain praised the team's attention, then reminded everyone that the license could be suspended if the rules were ignored. At home, Jiahe's mother said the project was becoming more complicated than making lunch. Jiahe agreed. Feeding people on land was hard enough. Feeding people while a boat moved demanded that the kitchen and the harbor respect each other's limits.

星期五，羅女士核准每航次三十份便當的有限許可，條件是每週提出溫度紀錄，並接受第二次訪查。佳禾把書面條件放在收銀箱旁，讓每個員工都能閱讀。她又請機械師複查裝載路線。機械師發現推車可能撞到一段鬆動水管，大家立即貼膠帶警示。船長稱讚團隊注意細節，也提醒忽略規則就可能被暫停許可。回家後，母親說這計畫已比做午餐複雜得多。佳禾同意。陸地上供餐已經不容易，船上供餐更需要廚房與港口彼此尊重限制。
