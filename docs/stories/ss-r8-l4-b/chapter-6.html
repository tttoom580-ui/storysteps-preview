一箱白色亞麻布

A Box of White Linen

After the cooler was repaired, Jiahe visited a wholesale goods shop to compare prices. Its owner was a conservative man who distrusted small partnerships, but he did not refuse to listen. A food consultant working nearby suggested that Jiahe invest in washable linen covers instead of buying disposable wrapping every day. The covers would cost more at first and require careful cleaning. Jiahe asked who would do that work and how the washing would be paid. She refused to turn a hidden household task into a free business service. At the shop, a bucket of blue dye had spilled beside the cloth. The owner moved the stained rolls away without being asked. "Food and dye do not belong together," he said. His practical response gave Jiahe more confidence than his speeches against cooperation.

保冷箱修好後，佳禾走訪批發雜貨店比較價格。店主是個保守的人，不太信任小型合作組織，但仍願意聽她說明。附近的一位食品顧問建議她投資可清洗的亞麻布套，而不是天天買拋棄式包材。布套起初成本較高，清洗也需要謹慎。佳禾問誰負責洗、洗滌工時如何計酬，不願把家務藏進企業的免費服務裡。店內一桶藍色染料剛好翻倒在布料旁，店主不待提醒便把沾染的布捲移開。「食物和染料不能放一起，」他說。這種實際行動，比他反對合作的演說更能贏得佳禾的信任。

On the ferry, an older consumer complained that the new lunch boxes had no sense of luxury. Another passenger said the plain rice and vegetables were a relief. Jiahe realized that neither opinion represented everyone. She offered a choice between two meals at the same price and promised to enclose a clear ingredient list with each order. A timid passenger asked whether the wrapping was safe for people with allergies. Jiahe said she was still studying that issue and would not guess. At the health office, a technician showed her photographs taken through a microscope to explain why a surface might look clean without being clean. The images made her tremble, not with fear of the technician, but with awareness of how much she still had to learn.

渡輪上一名年長消費者抱怨新便當毫無奢華感，另一位乘客卻覺得清淡的飯菜很舒服。佳禾明白，兩種意見都不能代表所有人。她提供兩款同價餐點，並承諾每筆訂單都附上清楚的食材清單。一名害羞乘客問，包材是否適合過敏者。佳禾坦言仍在研究，不願猜測。衛生所技術員用顯微鏡照片告訴她，表面看似乾淨並不一定真的乾淨。那些影像讓她發抖，不是怕技術員，而是意識到自己仍有許多必須學習的事。

Bojun had a habitual way of treating every disagreement as an attack on the family. He heard the consultant question their storage plan and burst into rage. Jiahe pulled him aside. "You are not defending us when you stop people from checking our work," she told him. He grew quiet. His old occupation had been selling radios, and he was proud of knowing how to fix a broken stereo. Yet a lunch service required a different orientation: the person who found a fault was often helping, not insulting. The consultant agreed to explain the inspection in ordinary language. She also admitted that her proposed covers would be too expensive until a reliable laundry partner was found. For once Bojun listened without interrupting, and Jiahe recognized the effort it took him.

伯鈞習慣把每次不同意見都當成對家人的攻擊。聽見顧問質疑儲藏計畫，他立刻大發脾氣。佳禾把他拉到旁邊：「你阻止別人檢查我們的工作，不是在保護我們。」伯鈞沉默了。他以前的職業是賣收音機，也很自豪自己會修壞掉的立體音響；但便當服務需要不同的思考方向：指出問題的人往往是在幫忙，而不是羞辱。顧問同意用淺白語言解釋檢查，也承認在找到可靠洗衣夥伴之前，自己建議的布套確實太貴。這次伯鈞沒有打斷，佳禾看見他努力改變的樣子。

When the laundry owner visited, she said it would be ignorant to assume that all cloth could be washed at the same temperature. She brought small samples and demonstrated how some materials shrank or lost their shape. Jiahe asked for a price that covered sorting, drying, inspection, and the time required to keep records. The woman offered a written quote and refused a request to work on Sundays. Jiahe accepted the refusal without bargaining. She then asked Bojun to check whether storing clean cloth near the radio repair bench might expose it to dust or oil. He moved his tools to a separate shelf. Their small kitchen had no grand equipment, but it was slowly learning to distinguish between a task that looked easy and a task that was safely complete.

洗衣店老闆來訪時說，以為所有布料都能用同樣溫度清洗，是很無知的想法。她帶來小樣本，示範某些材料洗後會縮水或變形。佳禾要求報價包含分類、烘乾、檢查和登記所需的時間。老闆娘提供書面估價，也拒絕星期日加班。佳禾接受這項拒絕，沒有討價還價。她接著請伯鈞檢查，若將乾淨布料放在收音機維修桌旁，是否會沾上灰塵或油污。伯鈞把工具移到另一個架子。小廚房沒有豪華設備，卻漸漸學會分清看起來容易的工作，和真正安全完成的工作。

That evening, A-Ling proposed a separate washing trial and a rule to isolate any cloth that touched raw food. Jiahe approved the test but not a full purchase. Her mother, who had cleaned ferry seats for twenty years, watched the discussion with unexpected grace. She said that careful cleaning was skilled labor, not a minor favor. The words corrected Jiahe's own ignorance. She had called the work "washing" without considering its knowledge, equipment, and time. They added a labor line to the budget and asked a nearby laundry owner for a quotation. No one was required to volunteer. The next morning, Jiahe placed two boxes on the counter: one for clean cloth and one for used cloth. The difference between them was simple, visible, and essential.

當晚，阿苓提議先做獨立洗滌測試，凡接觸生食的布套必須隔離處理。佳禾同意測試，但暫不大量採購。清潔渡輪座椅二十年的母親從旁觀察，態度出奇從容。她說，仔細清潔是有技術的勞動，不是順手幫忙。這句話也糾正了佳禾自己的無知：她過去只說「洗一洗」，沒考慮工作所需的知識、設備和時間。她們將清潔工資列入預算，向附近洗衣店詢價，不要求任何人義務幫忙。隔天，佳禾在櫃檯放了兩個箱子，一個裝乾淨布套，一個裝使用過的。兩者的差別簡單、清楚，卻非常重要。
