水盆測試

The Basin Test

1995，最後一班渡輪的午餐 · 第 7 / 25 章 · 3000–4000 · 英中對照 · 校訂中

用互動版查詞、朗讀與收藏

The school principal invited Jiahe to a meeting about serving lunches at an academic event on the island. An accountant from the parents' association would review the budget, and a chemistry teacher offered to test the cleaning water. The teacher carried samples in a covered basin and explained that the town's pipes sometimes changed color after heavy rain. Jiahe liked the plan, but asked which findings were relevant to the kitchen and which were only for classroom study. She did not want scientific language to replace practical decisions. The students had recently visited an aquarium and were eager to compare clear and cloudy water. Their admiration for the teacher was evident, yet he reminded them that clarity alone could not prove safety. They would need more than a beautiful demonstration.

島上學校校長邀佳禾討論學術活動供餐，家長會會計師將審查預算，化學老師則提出檢測清洗用水。他把樣本裝在有蓋水盆裡，解釋小鎮管線在大雨後有時會改變水色。佳禾喜歡這項計畫，但也問哪些發現真正與廚房相關，哪些只是課堂研究；她不願用科學名詞取代實務判斷。學生最近參觀過水族館，很想比較清水與混濁水。他們十分敬佩老師，老師卻提醒，清澈本身不能證明安全，漂亮的示範還不夠。

The accountant noticed a characteristic problem in Jiahe's books: income arrived daily, but some expenses were paid only at the end of the month. This made the first week look more fortunate than it had really been. Jiahe asked her to calculate the contribution of each type of lunch and the cost of the shared cooler. A-Ling worried the result would defeat their hopes. "It might," Jiahe admitted, "but a false profit will annoy us later in a much more expensive way." The accountant proposed setting aside a fixed amount from every order for repairs, holidays, and paid sick leave. They could not afford immediate expansion, but they could strive to make existing work stable. Jiahe copied the formula into the main ledger and explained it to everyone on the team.

會計師發現佳禾帳本的一項典型問題：收入每天入帳，部分支出卻到月底才支付，讓第一週看起來比實際上順利。佳禾請她計算每種便當的貢獻及共用保冷箱成本。阿苓擔心結果會擊敗大家的希望。佳禾承認可能如此，但虛假的獲利將來只會帶來更昂貴的麻煩。會計師建議從每筆訂單固定留一部分，支付維修、休假與有薪病假。她們還無法立即擴張，卻能努力讓現有工作穩定。佳禾把公式抄進總帳，逐一向團隊解釋。

On Saturday, the principal arranged a small market by the school gate. A band played a steady rhythm beneath a canvas shade. Jiahe sold only the number of meals already approved, even though a line of visitors formed. A student who spoke fluent Japanese translated the ingredient notices for visiting families. Jiahe thanked her and offered a paid translation task for the next event; the student would need her parent's permission and could decline. Across the yard, the school's prime basketball player tried to motivate classmates to carry extra boxes. Jiahe stopped him until the route was checked for steps and slippery corners. She wanted young helpers to learn that energy was not the same as safety. Their contribution mattered only if nobody was hurt doing it.

星期六，校長在校門旁安排小市集。樂隊在遮陽棚下奏出穩定節奏。即使排隊的人越來越多，佳禾仍只販售核准數量的餐點。一名日語流利的學生為來訪家庭翻譯食材告示，佳禾道謝，並提出下一次活動可以提供有薪翻譯工作；學生須取得家長同意，也有權拒絕。球隊明星則鼓勵同學搬更多箱子。佳禾先檢查路線上的台階和濕滑轉角，才允許協助。她希望年輕人明白，熱情不等於安全。只有不傷害人，貢獻才真正有價值。

Before the school market closed, a parent asked whether the lunch service could promise cheaper prices for an entire year. Jiahe showed the accountant's estimates and explained why seasonal vegetables, ferry closures, and repairs could change expenses. The parent said that a clear review date was more useful than a price promise nobody could keep. They agreed to post prices for one month at a time, with written notice before any increase. Jiahe then asked the chemistry teacher to keep a copy of the water test in the school library so other cooks could compare their own results. The teacher supported the idea but warned against treating one sample as a permanent guarantee. Learning, Jiahe thought, was not a staircase that ended at a certificate. It was a habit of returning to questions when conditions changed.

校園市集結束前，一名家長問便當服務能否保證全年降價。佳禾展示會計師估算，解釋季節蔬菜、渡輪停航和維修如何影響支出。家長說，清楚的檢討日期比無法守住的價格承諾更有用。雙方同意每次公布一個月的價格，漲價前要有書面通知。佳禾又請化學老師把水質檢測影本放進學校圖書館，讓其他廚師也能比較自己的結果。老師贊成，但提醒不可把單次樣本當成永久保證。佳禾想，學習不是走到證書就結束的樓梯，而是情況改變時仍願意重新發問的習慣。

That night, the accountant finished the revised figures. The kitchen had not earned much, but it had covered actual wages, ingredients, rent, and a modest repair reserve for three weeks. Jiahe felt relief, then immediately checked the assumptions. A single storm could change the result. Her mother placed the water-test record beside the ledger and told Jiahe not to let worry waken her in the middle of every night. Jiahe said the work was not a contest to prove herself right. She was building a service that could admit mistakes while there was still time to correct them. The accountant agreed to review the books again after one full month. Until then, expansion was paused, and everyone knew why.

當晚，會計師完成修正數字。廚房獲利不多，但連續三週都足以支付真實工資、食材、租金和少量維修準備金。佳禾鬆了一口氣，接著立刻檢查計算假設：一次暴風就可能改變結果。母親把水質檢測紀錄放在帳本旁，勸她別讓焦慮每晚半夜把自己叫醒。佳禾說，這不是要證明自己永遠正確的競賽；她想建造的是一種能承認錯誤、並在來得及時修正的服務。會計師同意等完整一個月後再次審查帳目。在那之前暫停擴張，每個人都明白理由。
