冬季談判

The Winter Negotiation

Winter winds began to change the harbor schedule. The captain warned that an eventual reduction in crossings could make the kitchen's present business model impossible. Jiahe asked the ferry company to circulate a draft winter timetable before anyone signed a new supply agreement. At a meeting, the company's dominant manager argued that competition was the natural code of capitalism: the strongest seller would survive. A-Ling replied that customers also deserved predictable refunds and safe meals. The manager called her a rebel. Jiahe said there was no need for insults and invited him to negotiate actual costs. If orders were reduced, they could shrink their cooking days rather than force workers to accept unpaid hours. The manager agreed to compare the figures, although he made no promise to support them.

冬季風向開始改變港口時刻表。船長警告，若航次最終減少，廚房目前的經營模式可能無法維持。佳禾請渡輪公司先傳閱冬季班表草案，再談新供應合約。會議上，態度強勢的經理主張競爭是資本主義的規則，最強的賣家才能存活。阿苓回應，顧客也有權獲得可預期的退款與安全餐點。經理稱她是叛逆者。佳禾說不必互相辱罵，並邀經理直接談實際成本。若訂單縮減，就減少烹調日，而不是逼員工無薪工作。經理同意比較數字，卻沒有保證支持她們。

The next morning, Jiahe joined the captain on a visit to the old coastal telegraph station, now used to store weather records. Dusty logbooks described a marathon effort during a storm decades earlier, when operators worked through the night to warn nearby islands. A solar panel recently installed above the door powered a small emergency lamp. Beside the station lay the wreck of a wooden boat, kept as a reminder that an incorrect warning had consequences beyond a missed delivery. The captain pointed to a log entry recording a vessel's last message. Jiahe could almost feel the fear behind its careful handwriting. She asked whether the ferry company still kept comparable records. It did, he said, but only certain staff could see them. She wondered how to request public safety information without exposing private passenger details.

隔天早晨，佳禾跟船長拜訪舊海岸電報站，現在那裡用來保存天氣紀錄。灰塵滿布的日誌記載數十年前一場漫長救災：操作員整夜發出警報，提醒鄰近島嶼。門上新裝的太陽能板替小型緊急燈供電。旁邊保留了一艘木船殘骸，提醒大家錯誤警報的後果遠超送餐延誤。船長指著一條記載船隻最後訊息的日誌，佳禾幾乎能感到謹慎筆跡背後的恐懼。她問渡輪公司現在是否保留類似紀錄。船長說有，但僅部分員工能查閱。佳禾開始思考，如何取得公共安全資訊，同時保護乘客隱私。

A retired cook named Aunt Huang visited the kitchen with a graceful suggestion. She wanted to grow herbs for the lunches in pots beside the back wall. Jiahe liked the idea but questioned whether the garden would expand the work beyond its capacity. Aunt Huang laughed when a leaf tickled her wrist. "I am not asking you to breed animals or plant an entire farm," she said. She proposed maintaining four pots as a test and keeping a record of what was harvested. Jiahe accepted on the condition that the plants would not replace bought ingredients in the budget until their yield could be measured. There was no indication that a tiny garden would save the company; its value might instead be helping neighbors feel welcome. They wrote that distinction down.

退休廚師黃阿姨來到廚房，優雅地提出一項建議：在後牆旁用盆栽種香草，供便當使用。佳禾喜歡，但擔心種植會讓工作超出負荷。葉子輕觸黃阿姨手腕，惹得她笑起來。「我又不是叫你養動物，或種出整座農場，」她說。她提議先維持四盆，記錄每次收成。佳禾同意，但在產量可以量測之前，預算裡不能直接把種植成果當成免付費食材。小花園沒有跡象能拯救企業，它的價值或許只是讓鄰居感覺受到歡迎。兩人也把這項區別寫了下來。

After the hearing, a student asked Jiahe whether capitalism always rewarded the person with the biggest boat or the most money. She said an economic system was more complicated than a slogan, and the ferry's public obligations still required clear decisions from its operators. The kitchen had no power to rewrite the country's economy. It could, however, negotiate a service contract that described safety, wages, prices, and how disputes would be handled. The student asked why Jiahe had not simply accepted the manager's offer for guaranteed sales. She showed him the clause that would have made the kitchen pay for meals on canceled sailings. A guaranteed number on paper could become a real loss in bad weather. The student took a copy and said he would study the details instead of repeating the manager's speech.

聽證後，一名學生問佳禾，資本主義是不是永遠獎勵擁有最大船或最多錢的人。她說經濟制度比口號複雜，而渡輪的公共責任仍需要營運者作出清楚決定。廚房無權改寫整個國家的經濟，卻可以談一份說明安全、工資、價格和爭議處理方式的服務合約。學生問她為何不直接接受經理保證銷量的條件。佳禾指出其中一條：若停航，廚房仍得支付原定餐量的成本。紙上的保證數字，在壞天氣裡可能變成真正虧損。學生拿走影本，說會仔細研究，而不是重複經理的演說。

At the winter timetable hearing, the manager asked whether Jiahe was trying to begin an invasion of the company's business. She answered that meals were not boats and that she had no interest in controlling departures. Her concern was the people who would be stranded between crossings. She offered to arrange collection points on each side of the water and to stop cooking when a sailing was canceled. The manager tried persuasion, pointing to the cost of extra staff. Jiahe presented the cost of doing nothing: missed medical appointments, wasted food, and passengers left without clear information. They agreed to test the plan during two weekends, with the captain free to stop loading whenever conditions changed. It was not a final contract. It was a measured opportunity to learn.

冬季班表聽證會上，經理問佳禾是不是企圖侵入公司的業務。她回答，餐點不是船，她無意控制發船時間；她在意的是可能困在兩個航次之間的人。她提出在海峽兩側設置取餐點，停航就停止烹調。經理試圖說服她，指出增聘人力的成本。佳禾則展示什麼也不做的代價：錯過門診、浪費食物、乘客得不到清楚資訊。雙方同意用兩個週末測試，海象變化時船長仍可停止裝載。這不是最終合約，而是一個經過衡量、可以學習的機會。
