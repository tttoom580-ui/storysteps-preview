長者與河流

The Elder and the River

星光送不到的第七封信 · 第 8 / 25 章 · 1000–2000 · 英中對照 · 校訂中

用互動版查詞、朗讀與收藏

An elder named Sora waited at the river crossing. She was an adult with a warm voice, but a fever had kept her inside for three days. She showed Mei an ancient chart, more than a century old, with a drawing of grain fields that no longer existed. The paper gave an instruction to new walkers: never travel backward through the river bend during heavy rain. Sora said the chart had belonged to her grandmother. Mei asked permission before touching it, because age had made its edges weak.

名叫索拉的長者在河道渡口等著。她是一位聲音溫暖的大人，但發燒讓她三天無法出門。她拿出一張超過一世紀的古老圖表，上面畫著如今已不存在的穀田。圖上有給新步行者的指示：大雨時不要從河彎逆向穿過。索拉說圖原本屬於她的祖母。紙張邊緣已因年代久遠而脆弱，梅先徵求同意才碰它。

The chart’s design was unusual. Instead of showing only roads, it used small signs for trees, wells, and places where someone could swim across in summer. An said they must not mistake a summer path for a winter one. A stranger standing nearby offered to lead them, but could not tell them his identity or why he knew the river. Mei thanked him and chose to ask Sora for a known local guide. The stranger looked disappointed but accepted the choice without a fight.

圖表的設計很特別。它不只標道路，也用小符號表示樹、水井和夏天可以游泳渡過的位置。安說他們不能把夏季路線誤當冬季道路。一位站在附近的陌生人主動說願意帶路，卻說不出自己的身分，也無法解釋為何熟悉河道。梅道謝後，選擇請索拉介紹認識的在地嚮導。陌生人雖然失望，仍平靜接受。

The guide explained that the river changed after each long rain. A road that followed its edge might disappear even when the map showed it clearly. Mei asked the guide to mark places where people had to stop and look at the water. Sora listened from her chair and corrected one mark: the lowest point was not the bridge but a bend beside a tree. The guide agreed after checking it. An was impressed that the old woman could remember the land so well, yet Sora reminded him that memory should still be tested against what stood before them.

嚮導解釋，每次長雨後河道都會改變。即使地圖畫得清楚，沿岸道路仍可能消失。梅請嚮導標出人們必須停下觀察水位的位置。索拉坐在椅上聽，糾正一個記號：最低處不是橋，而是樹旁的河彎。嚮導檢查後同意。安驚訝於老婦對土地記得如此清楚，索拉卻提醒他，記憶仍得拿眼前實景來驗證。

Sora gave them a peach and told them about a bridge that people used to maintain together. The bridge still stood, but its ropes had been left without care. She asked Mei to pose beside the old picture for a drawing. Mei refused at first because the river behind her was too close. Sora laughed and moved her chair to a safe place. Their conversation showed Mei what good care looked like: people listened when someone said a situation felt unsafe, even if no disaster had happened yet.

索拉給他們一顆桃子，並說起以前大家共同維護的橋。橋還在，但繩索已長期沒有人照料。她請梅在舊照片旁擺姿勢，好畫下對比。梅起初拒絕，因為身後河水太近。索拉笑著把椅子移到安全處。這段對話讓梅理解良好照顧的樣子：有人說情況不安全時，就該傾聽，即使災難還沒有發生。

They found that one rope had begun to tear. The damage was typical of wet winters, An said, and the rope had already been used beyond its safe life. Mei did not try to fix it with a knot she had never tested. She marked the bridge closed and asked a carpenter to examine it. It meant a longer journey for everyone that day. Still, no one was hurt, and the local children learned why an old bridge could look strong while its inside was weak.

他們發現一條繩子開始撕裂。安說這是潮濕冬季常見的損壞，那條繩索也早已使用超過安全年限。梅沒有用自己不曾測試過的繩結硬修。她標示橋梁封閉，請木匠檢查。這表示當天大家都要繞遠路，但沒有人受傷，當地孩子也學到：舊橋外觀看來堅固，裡面卻可能早已脆弱。

When Sora grew tired, Mei asked whether she wanted to end their visit. The old woman said yes, and nobody argued. She had given them more than enough for one day. Mei helped put the papers away and wrote down which drawing was a copy, so nobody would confuse it with the old original. On the walk back, An said he wished he could remember every detail the way Sora did. Mei said he did not need to. Keeping clear notes and inviting other people to check them was another way of caring for the past.

索拉累了，梅問她是否想結束訪談。老婦說想，沒有人爭辯。她今天已給出足夠多的幫助。梅幫她收好紙張，標出哪張是副本，免得有人與原圖搞混。返程時，安說希望自己也能像索拉一樣記得所有細節。梅說不必。寫清楚筆記、邀請別人核對，也是照顧過去的一種方式。

Sora showed them where first aid supplies were kept. An placed the paper chart across his lap, keeping it away from the wet ground, while Mei checked the date on each bandage and asked the nurse to replace anything too old.

索拉指給他們看急救用品的位置。安把紙圖攤在膝上，避免碰到濕地；梅則檢查每條繃帶的日期，請護理師更換過期物品。

That evening Sora let Mei copy the old river chart. In its corner was the same seven-star mark found behind the barber’s mirror. Mei held the two drawings side by side and discovered they matched. Two distant people had kept different parts of the same travel record. No one had planned this meeting, yet it brought the team closer to understanding the silent villages. Mei wrote Sora a letter of thanks and promised to return with news before the first snow.

傍晚索拉讓梅抄下舊河圖。圖角有與理髮師鏡後相同的七星記號。梅把兩張圖並排，發現它們能吻合。相距很遠的兩個人，保存著同一份旅行紀錄的不同部分。沒有人事先安排這場相遇，但它讓小隊更接近沉默村落的答案。梅寫信感謝索拉，答應在初雪之前帶回消息。
