新手舉起燈

A Beginner Holds the Light

星光送不到的第七封信 · 第 11 / 25 章 · 1000–2000 · 英中對照 · 校訂中

用互動版查詞、朗讀與收藏

A beginner named Fen came to the market watch post carrying a mug of warm grape juice. She wanted to help but did not know how to charge the hand lamps. Mei told her the goal was simple: learn one task well before learning another. Fen tried to chase a rolling battery down a slope. An stopped her and emphasized the importance of keeping power parts dry. They set the battery on an iron shelf and checked its case. It was old but still safe to use under the keeper’s guidance.

一位名叫芬的新手端著一杯溫葡萄汁來到市場守望站。她很想幫忙，卻不知道怎麼替手燈充電。梅告訴她，目標很簡單：先學好一件事，再學另一件。芬追著一顆沿坡滾動的電池跑，安攔住她，強調電力零件保持乾燥的重要。他們把電池放上鐵架，檢查外殼。在守塔人指導下，它雖舊，仍能安全使用。

The market had stalls selling gold rings, berry jam, and bread. Fen asked if a rich buyer might pay to rebuild the tower, but Mei said their success could not depend on one person’s riches. Nearby, a small social group from several faiths was sharing meals. One leader said religion taught many people to care for strangers, but no group should control who received a warning. Mei agreed. Whether someone prayed differently or not at all, a message about a storm had to reach them.

市場攤位賣著金戒指、莓果醬和麵包。芬問有沒有富有買家願意出錢重建高塔，但梅說他們的成功不能只靠一個人的財富。附近，不同信仰的人組成的小型社群正在分享餐點。一位領袖說宗教教導許多人關懷陌生人，但任何團體都不該控制誰能收到警告。梅同意。不論一個人如何祈禱，或根本不祈禱，風暴的訊息都必須送達。

Fen asked whether a messenger needed to be brave. Mei thought about the question before answering. A person did need courage to walk in bad weather, but courage also meant saying no when a route was unsafe. She told Fen about the northern bridge and how Lian had refused to open it before checking the supports. Fen nodded and wrote that story on the back of her card. It was not as exciting as a story about someone crossing a storm alone, yet it showed how a wise refusal could protect people who came later.

芬問信使是否一定要勇敢。梅想了一下才回答。人在壞天氣裡行走確實需要勇氣，但勇氣也包括道路不安全時敢說不。她告訴芬北橋的事，還有蓮如何在檢查支撐樑前拒絕開放。芬點頭，把故事寫在卡片背面。這不像孤身穿過風暴的故事那麼刺激，卻說明明智的拒絕如何保護後來的人。

An showed Fen how the lamp should shoot a clean line of light toward the next hill. He did not let her stand in the usual place, because a loose stone there could become a trap in the dark. She took three steps to the side and tried again. The beam reached the far post. “That is true,” Fen said, surprised. Mei told her that trust came from repeated checks, not from saying she was sure. Fen wrote the time and weather on a card and asked An to check it too.

安教芬讓燈朝下一座山丘射出一條清楚的光。他沒有讓她站在平常的位置，因為那裡一塊鬆石在黑暗中可能變成陷阱。她往旁邊走三步再試，光束便抵達遠方守望點。「真的成功了，」芬驚訝地說。梅告訴她，信任來自反覆確認，不是嘴上說很有把握。芬把時間和天氣寫在卡片上，也請安檢查。

Before closing the market, they held a short practice for anyone willing to join. Fen made one mistake and then corrected it herself. Nobody laughed. The keeper asked her to teach the next beginner, and she smiled for the first time that day. Mei saw how the work was growing: not by finding one great helper, but by teaching small skills to people who could teach others. The tower might have gone dark, yet new points of light were appearing around the valley.

收市前，他們替願意參加的人辦了簡短練習。芬犯了一次錯，隨即自己改正，沒有人嘲笑她。守塔人請她再教下一位新手，她露出那天第一個笑容。梅看見工作如何擴大：不是找到一位偉大幫手，而是讓學會小技能的人再教別人。觀星塔雖然熄滅，山谷四周卻開始出現新的光點。

Mei asked Fen to give the lesson a second time, with the keeper acting as a new traveler. Fen explained the signals clearly but forgot to mention that every message needed an answer. She paused, looked at her card, and corrected herself. The keeper thanked her. There was no prize for speaking without a mistake, and nobody was being judged for needing a note. Mei saw how much easier learning became when people were not afraid to admit what they had forgotten. That might be the most useful habit the market could share with the whole valley.

梅請芬再教一次，讓守塔人扮演新旅人。芬清楚說明訊號，卻忘了每則訊息都需要回覆。她停下來看卡片，自行修正。守塔人向她道謝。不犯錯沒有獎品，需要筆記的人也不會被評判。梅看見，當人不怕承認忘記了什麼，學習就容易許多。這也許是市場能分享給整座山谷最有用的習慣。

The keeper tried to emphasize one lesson: simply holding a bright lamp was not enough. A sender had to watch for the answer and know when to stop. Fen repeated that rule aloud until she could explain it without looking at the card.

守塔人試著強調一個道理：只拿著亮燈還不夠。發訊者必須等待回覆，也要知道何時停下。芬大聲重複規則，直到不用看卡片就能解釋。

When Mei returned to the tower, she found Fen’s first message waiting. It reported that the western market lamp was working, but its holder needed repair. Mei answered with two flashes for delay and sent a note to the school metal worker. She felt proud of the lesson Fen had learned. A reliable network was not a row of machines; it was a chain of people who knew when to stop, ask for help, and tell the next person what they had discovered.

梅回到高塔後，發現芬送來第一則訊息。內容說西市場的燈能運作，但燈架需要修理。梅用兩次閃光表示延誤，並寫信給學校金工師。她為芬學到的事感到驕傲。可靠的網路不是一整排機器，而是一群知道何時停下、求助，並把發現告訴下一個人的人。
