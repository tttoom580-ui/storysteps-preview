舊路上的警報

An Alarm on the Old Road

星光送不到的第七封信 · 第 3 / 25 章 · 1000–2000 · 英中對照 · 基礎校訂完成

At sunrise a small alarm sounded from the abandoned goods station. Mei ran there, but An asked her to stay calm. A power cell beside the door had been left open. The station was famous for a classic painting of a cowboy crossing a great plain. Now rain had fallen through the roof and made the colors run. Mei found a dryer in the corner, but it had no power. She did not touch the cell until An checked the wires. Their first duty was to keep the place safe, not to prove how brave they were.

日出時，廢棄貨運站傳來一陣警報。梅跑過去，安卻請她保持冷靜。門邊的電池盒被打開了。這座站以一幅牛仔穿越大平原的古典畫作聞名，如今雨水從屋頂落下，讓顏色流散。梅在角落發現烘乾機，但機器沒有電。安檢查電線之前，她沒有碰電池。他們最先的責任是確保安全，而不是證明自己多勇敢。

A farmer arrived with a lost lamb and an empty food basket. He had heard a false story that the tower planned to punish anyone who used the old road. Mei told him nobody had authority to do that. The farmer pointed to an object near the track: a metal plate marked with three circles. An pressed the plate gently and heard a bell from the roof. It was not a command to leave. It was an old signal to direct people toward the nearest shelter when the mountain fog grew thick.

一位農夫帶著迷路的小羊和空食籃趕來。他聽說一個不實傳言：高塔打算懲罰所有走舊路的人。梅告訴他，沒有人有這種權力。農夫指著軌道旁的物品：一片刻著三個圓圈的金屬牌。安輕輕按下金屬牌，屋頂便傳出鐘聲。這不是叫人離開的命令，而是舊時山霧變濃時，指引人們前往最近避難處的訊號。

An found a card inside the station desk. It had been written for people who visited only once a year, so the instructions used short words and simple pictures. Mei held the card beside the metal plate and checked whether the drawings matched. They did. She explained the shapes to the farmer, who repeated them without looking. He had never worked with tower lights before, yet he could understand the warning. Mei thought a system that could be taught to a stranger in a few minutes would be more useful than one that required years of special training.

安在車站桌內找到一張卡片。它是寫給一年只來一次的人看的，因此用字短、圖案簡單。梅把卡片放在金屬牌旁，確認兩邊圖形相符。它們確實一樣。她向農夫解釋，農夫不看卡片也能重複說明。他從沒用過高塔燈光，卻能懂得警告。梅心想，一套幾分鐘就能教會陌生人的系統，比需要多年特別訓練的系統更有用。

The farmer said he had once watched a small sail move across the lake below. In those days a tour boat carried goods for trade as well as visitors. When a storm struck, the captain would fire one warning shot into the air. But the boat had been sold, and the shore signal had fallen silent. The farmer was ready to strike the broken plate with a hammer. Mei stopped him. A damaged sign might still contain information, and a sudden blow could destroy it before they understood its use.

農夫說他曾看過一面小船帆在下方湖面移動。從前遊湖船同時載送交易貨物和遊客。遇上暴風雨時，船長會朝天鳴放一聲示警，但那艘船後來賣掉了，岸邊訊號也沉寂。農夫拿起鎚子，準備敲擊破損的金屬牌。梅阻止了他。壞掉的標誌也可能留有資訊，突然敲下去，會在看懂用途之前毀掉它。

An drew three circles in his notebook. One circle meant a traveler had arrived, two meant a delay, and three meant immediate shelter. This was what the station signs said, but the group still had to test it safely. They borrowed a hand lamp and stood far apart where they could see each other. Mei flashed it once. An answered once. Then they repeated the action twice. For the first time since the tower went dark, a message had traveled between two people without a working glass wheel.

安在筆記本畫下三個圓圈。一個圓圈代表旅人抵達，兩個代表延誤，三個代表立刻避難。這是站內標誌的說法，但他們還得安全地測試。他們借了一盞手燈，站到相距很遠卻能互相看見的位置。梅閃一次，安也回答一次，接著兩人又用兩次燈光重複測試。觀星塔熄燈以來，訊息第一次在沒有玻璃輪運作的情況下，從一個人傳給另一個人。

The practice was not exciting to watch. It was one flash, then a pause, then another answer. Mei resisted the wish to make the result seem bigger. The lamp could be seen across the open field, but the mountains were another matter. An drew a line from their station to the next post and marked a question where the forest began. A traveler coming from that direction would need a different test. They planned to ask someone who knew the forest before moving a lamp into it. A small success had given them work, not permission to guess.

練習看起來並不刺激。一次閃光，停頓，再收到回覆。梅忍住把成果說得更大的衝動。手燈能照過開闊田地，但山區是另一回事。安從車站畫線到下一個守望點，在森林起點畫了問號。從那個方向來的旅人需要另一種測試。他們打算先請教熟悉森林的人，再把燈移過去。小成功帶來更多工作，並沒有給他們亂猜的許可。

The farmer mentioned that he had missed a haircut because the station was closed. An told Mei to press the warning plate once more only after he had checked it. She wrote the result beside the first test, so nobody would mistake a working button for a repaired road.

農夫提到車站關閉，害他錯過一次理髮。安檢查後，才告訴梅再次按下警示牌，並把結果寫在第一次測試旁，避免有人把按鈕能用誤認為道路已修好。

The farmer offered them bread, and the lamb slept beside the dry wall. Mei wrote down the test, including what they had not checked: rain, distance, and fog. An smiled. “One small answer,” he said. “Not the end of the problem.” Mei knew he was right. They could now warn someone on the far side of a field, but seven villages lay beyond the mountains. Their next step was to find a person willing to carry the signal farther than a single lamp could reach.

農夫分給他們麵包，小羊則在乾燥的牆邊睡著。梅記下測試結果，也寫下尚未檢查的條件：雨、距離和濃霧。安笑著說：「這只是一個小答案，不是問題的終點。」梅知道他說得對。他們現在可以向田地另一端的人示警，但七座村莊還隔著群山。下一步是找到願意把訊息送得比單盞燈更遠的人。
