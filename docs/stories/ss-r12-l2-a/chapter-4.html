樹枝交錯的路

The Road of Branches

星光送不到的第七封信 · 第 4 / 25 章 · 1000–2000 · 英中對照 · 校訂中

用互動版查詞、朗讀與收藏

The first family Mei visited lived beyond a narrow forest bridge. A boy had cut his hand on a branch, and his mother had wrapped it to stop the blood. She feared disease more than the small wound, because the clinic was across the entire valley. The family could escape by the river path if the bridge failed, but a storm would make that path dangerous. Mei asked what supplies they needed. The mother wanted wood for repairs and clean material for bandages, not a grand promise about the future.

梅拜訪的第一戶人家住在狹窄森林木橋的另一端。一個男孩被樹枝割傷手，母親替他包紮止血。她擔心的不是小傷，而是感染疾病，因為診所在整個山谷的另一端。若橋斷了，一家人可沿河道逃離，但暴風雨會讓路變危險。梅問他們缺什麼物資。母親需要修橋用的木材和乾淨的繃帶材料，而不是關於未來的宏大承諾。

At lunch the mother shared a generous meal of rice, a small hamburger, and slices of papaya. She said the village population was falling because many families feared winter travel. An tried to imagine how the new signal network might help. He drew a simple score beside each road: one mark for a clear road, two for a road that needed care, and three for a closed road. The mother understood his meaning at once. “A light can tell us danger,” she said, “but somebody must also tell us which way is still open.”

午餐時母親慷慨分享米飯、一個小漢堡和幾片木瓜。她說村裡的人口愈來愈少，因為許多家庭害怕冬季交通。安試著想像新的訊號網路能怎麼幫忙。他在每條路旁畫簡單分數：一劃代表道路暢通，兩劃代表需要小心，三劃代表封路。母親立刻明白。「燈可以告訴我們有危險，」她說，「但還要有人說清楚哪一條路仍然能走。」

The family invited the travelers to sit while the mother checked the boy’s bandage. She said the bridge did not feel dangerous to people who lived on the safe side of it. To her, every loose board meant a missed school day or a longer walk for medicine. Mei asked the boy to draw his own path to school. He made a line past three trees and a small stone house. An compared the drawing with the public map and found two places where the main route was wrong. Children, it turned out, could be careful observers of their own roads.

母親檢查男孩的繃帶時，邀請旅人坐下。她說住在安全橋頭的人不覺得橋危險；對她而言，每塊鬆板都可能意味著孩子缺課，或拿藥要多走一段路。梅請男孩自己畫上學路線。他畫了一條經過三棵樹和一座石屋的線。安和公用地圖比較，發現主路線有兩處錯誤。原來孩子也能仔細觀察自己每天走的路。

Mei could sense that the family had been waiting to be heard. The tower committee had set a target of seven lamps, yet it had never counted the number of homes each lamp served. Therefore, a village with few homes seemed unimportant on paper. The mother showed them an old list of families that used the bridge every week. It was worth more than a beautiful drawing without names. Mei asked permission to copy it. The mother agreed only after Mei explained where the copy would go and who could read it.

梅感覺得出這家人等著被傾聽已經很久。守塔委員會把目標訂成七盞燈，卻從未計算每盞燈服務幾戶人家。因此，戶數少的村莊在紙上看起來就不重要。母親拿出一張舊名單，記著每週使用木橋的家庭。它比一張沒有姓名的漂亮圖畫更有價值。梅徵求影印的同意。母親在梅說明副本會送去哪裡、由誰閱讀後，才答應。

Before leaving, Mei and An helped the family replace two loose pieces of wood. They could not rebuild the whole bridge, and they did not pretend that they could. The mother showed them a safe place where a warning lamp could be seen from three houses. An measured the view while Mei made a sketch. By late afternoon the family could see the hand lamp from the other side of the bridge. A little cooperation had turned a note on a map into something that might protect people.

離開前，梅和安幫忙換掉兩塊鬆動的木板。他們無法重建整座橋，也沒有假裝可以。母親帶他們看一個三戶人家都能看見警示燈的位置。安測量視線，梅則畫草圖。到了下午，家人已能從橋的另一端看到手燈。一點點合作，讓地圖上的註記變成可能保護人的實際安排。

An wanted to put a lamp beside every house. The mother explained why that would not work: some families could not pay for fuel, and empty houses stood between the occupied ones. Instead she pointed to a bend where three families could see one shared light. The place was high enough to avoid floodwater and close enough for the adults to reach safely. Mei wrote down the advantages and the remaining problems. The mother did not want the group to do everything for her. She wanted a system her neighbors could learn to manage together.

安想在每戶屋旁裝一盞燈。母親說明不可行的原因：有些家庭付不起燃料，住人房屋之間還有空屋。她改指一個彎道，三戶都能看見同一盞燈。位置高得不會淹水，又近得讓大人安全到達。梅寫下優點和剩餘問題。母親不希望小隊把一切都替她做完，而想要一套鄰居能一起學著管理的系統。

The clinic director sent a short letter about the bridge. He wanted the team to combine the family list with the emergency travel plan, while keeping each patient's name private. Mei placed his advice beside the mother's sketch and marked it for the next meeting.

診所主任寄來一封關於木橋的短信。他希望小隊把家庭名單與緊急旅行計畫結合，同時保護病人姓名。梅把他的建議放在母親的草圖旁，標記為下次會議事項。

Mei carried the copied list back toward the tower. She thought about the children who still had to cross the bridge in the cold. A signal would not carry a child over the river, but it could give a parent time to choose another path. She put a new question beside the old one in her notebook: who decides which villages are too small to count? That question would follow her farther than the forest, all the way to the tower council.

梅帶著抄好的名單往高塔走。她想到那些天冷時仍得過橋的孩子。訊號不會替孩子跨過河流，卻能讓家長有時間選擇別條路。她在筆記裡舊問題旁添了一個新問題：究竟是誰決定哪些村莊小到不值得計算？這問題將跟著她走出森林，一直走進守塔議會。
