沒有星光的高塔

The Tower Without a Star

星光送不到的第七封信 · 第 1 / 25 章 · 1000–2000 · 英中對照 · 校訂中

Mei took the steep approach to Starwatch Tower before sunset. From its balcony she could see the field below, the stone base of an old seesaw, and the narrow valley beyond. A windy sky made her pull her coat tight. The tower operator had promised to light seven distant lamps for the winter travelers, yet tonight not one shone. Mei was twenty, a messenger rather than a magician. She had no special speed or power. She had only a bag of letters and a belief that a message should reach the person who needed it.

梅在日落前沿著陡路走向觀星塔。從塔的陽臺，她看得到下方的田地、一座舊翹翹板的石製底座，以及更遠處的狹長山谷。多風的天空讓她拉緊外套。高塔操作員原本答應為冬季旅人點亮七座遠方燈塔，今晚卻一盞都沒有亮。二十歲的梅是信使，不是魔法師。她沒有特別快的速度，也沒有神奇力量，只有一袋信，以及相信訊息應送到需要的人手中的信念。

Inside the tower, An was trying to fix a glass wheel. He was a young male student of the stars, but he did not pretend to know everything. The challenge was not simply to make the wheel turn. Light had to travel in the correct order, or a traveler might walk toward a closed pass. An could not deny that the old system was failing. He asked Mei to discuss a better way to carry warnings while he examined the glass. Together they decided to develop a written record of every lamp that still worked.

塔內，安正試著修好一面玻璃輪。他是年輕的男性觀星學徒，卻不假裝什麼都懂。他們的挑戰不只是讓輪子轉動。光必須依照正確順序傳遞，否則旅人可能走向封閉的山口。安不能否認舊系統正在失效。他請梅討論更好的警示傳送方式，自己則檢查玻璃。兩人決定共同建立紀錄，寫下每一盞仍能使用的燈。

Before opening the old map, Mei asked An to show her how the tower had worked in an ordinary winter. He pointed to a glass wheel and seven small marks around its edge. The wheel did not know whether anyone was safe; it only sent light. Each village had once kept a person near its own lamp, and those people had answered one another. Mei copied the order of the marks into her notebook. She was surprised that the most useful part of the system was not a clever machine but the quiet work of people who stayed awake to receive a message.

打開舊地圖前，梅請安示範高塔在平常冬季如何運作。他指著玻璃輪和邊緣的七個小記號。輪子不知道任何人是否安全，它只會送出光。從前每村都安排人在燈旁值守，彼此回覆。梅把記號順序抄進筆記，驚訝地發現最有用的部分不是巧妙機器，而是那些守夜接訊的人所做的安靜工作。

On a secondary shelf Mei found a map of the nation, marked with seven small stars. For instance, the western star stood for a village reached by an underground freight train. Children called the short line a subway, though it carried boxes rather than passengers. Under the map was a note about a tradition: each village must answer the next village before a warning is trusted. The tower had stopped receiving replies three days ago. This was not only a broken machine; it was a broken conversation between places.

梅在第二層書架上找到一張全國地圖，上面標著七顆小星星。例如，西邊的星星代表一座可以搭地下貨運列車抵達的村落。孩子們把那條短線叫作地下鐵，雖然它載的是箱子而不是乘客。地圖下方有張紙，寫著一項傳統：每個村子都必須回答下一個村子，警告才能被相信。三天前高塔就收不到回覆了。壞掉的不只是機器，而是各個地方之間的對話。

The tower keeper returned after dark, tired and embarrassed. He told them the last light had gone out while he was looking for a missing message bag. Mei offered to walk to the first village at dawn. An said she might find only empty roads. She answered that even an empty road could tell them something if they observed it carefully. They agreed on one rule before leaving: no one would announce that a path was safe without hearing from a person who had actually crossed it.

天黑後守塔人回來了，疲憊又難堪。他說最後一盞燈是在他尋找失蹤信袋時熄滅的。梅主動提出天亮就走到第一個村落。安說她可能只會遇見空蕩的道路。梅回答，即使道路空無一人，只要仔細觀察，也能帶回線索。出發前，他們同意一條原則：沒有實際走過那條路的人回報，就不能宣稱它是安全的。

They carried their notes to the balcony and tried to find all seven hills with their eyes. Two were easy to see; three were hidden behind trees, and the last two lay beyond a high ridge. An explained that a strong light could reach some places directly but never all of them. Mei asked how the last village had received messages in the past. An could not answer. That was important: the person who knew the tower best still had a gap in his knowledge. Mei wrote the question in large letters and left space below it for an answer.

兩人帶著筆記走到陽臺，想用眼睛找出七座山丘。兩座很清楚，三座藏在樹後，最後兩座則在高山脊之後。安解釋，強光可以直接照到某些地方，卻不可能照到全部。梅問最後一村過去如何收訊，安答不上來。這很重要：最熟悉高塔的人也有不知道的事。梅用大字寫下問題，下面留空等答案。

At the foot of the tower, a mother waited for news of her daughter across the pass. Mei could not promise a quick answer. She wrote down the daughter’s name and the last day anyone had seen her. The mother thanked her for not pretending. As Mei walked home, the dark tower looked less like a dead building and more like a question standing over the valley. For the first time she understood that delivering letters might also mean helping seven villages listen to one another again.

塔腳下，一位母親等著山口另一端女兒的消息。梅無法保證很快得到答案，只記下女兒的姓名，以及最後有人看見她的日期。母親感謝她沒有假裝知道一切。梅走回家時，漆黑的高塔看起來不像死去的建築，反而像一個立在山谷上方的問題。她第一次明白，送信也可能意味著幫助七個村莊重新聽見彼此。
