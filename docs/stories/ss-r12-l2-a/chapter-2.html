鏡子後面的地圖

The Map Behind the Mirror

星光送不到的第七封信 · 第 2 / 25 章 · 1000–2000 · 英中對照 · 校訂中

用互動版查詞、朗讀與收藏

The next morning Mei brought An to a barber who lived near the tower road. From a particular angle, his window reflected the western hill perfectly. The barber said his father had once helped the tower workers set their lamps. He still kept a wooden board with their marks. Mei thanked him and said she could appreciate a person who saved old things. He warned them to avoid the deer path after rain, because its stones moved under a traveler’s feet. Outside, a dove sat calmly on the roof.

隔天早晨，梅帶安到塔路附近一位理髮師的店裡。從某個特定角度看，他的窗戶能完整映出西邊山丘。理髮師說父親曾幫守塔人調整燈光，至今仍保留一塊刻著記號的木板。梅道謝，說她很欣賞會保存舊物的人。理髮師提醒他們雨後別走鹿群的小徑，因為腳下的石頭會滑動。屋外，一隻鴿子平靜地停在屋頂。

He showed them an old edition of the valley textbook. It said the seven lamps had been built by a workers’ organization, not by the tower alone. Its first president had insisted that even the smallest village receive a light. That village belonged to a minority community on the high slope. On the newest map, its star had become a tiny spot, easy to miss. An looked at the huge blank space around it and said, “A place does not stop existing because someone draws it smaller.”

他拿出一本舊版的山谷教科書。書上記載七座燈塔是工人組織建立的，而不是觀星塔獨自完成。第一任會長堅持連最小的村落也該得到燈光。那座村子位於高坡，是少數族群的聚居地。在新版地圖上，它的星星縮成一個極小、容易被忽略的點。安看著周圍大片空白，說：「不會因為有人把地方畫小，那個地方就不存在了。」

The barber’s wife joined them with a box of old photographs. Each picture had a date, a name, and sometimes a note about the weather. One photograph showed a crowded wagon standing below the missing western star. Mei asked whether the wagon had carried people away from a storm. The wife said it had carried medicine in. She remembered because her younger sister was ill that winter. An placed the picture beside the map. The tiny spot on the new page had once been a busy place where people came together when someone needed help.

理髮師的妻子帶著一盒舊照片加入談話。每張照片都有日期、名字，有時還記著天氣。一張照片裡，一輛擠滿物品的馬車停在被省去的西方星號下。梅問馬車是否曾載人躲避風暴。妻子說車是送藥來的，因為那年冬天她妹妹病了。安把照片放到地圖旁。新圖上一個極小的點，從前卻是人們在需要幫助時聚集的熱鬧地方。

The barber offered them tea made from a sour mountain fruit. While Mei drank, a painful memory returned: last winter she had carried a letter there and found the road gate locked. No one in the tower had explained why. Now she noticed a line across the old map that was missing from the new one. It led to a lantern station under a hanging rock. The map gave no secret door or wonderful spell, only the place where travelers had once stopped to exchange news.

理髮師端出用酸澀山果泡的茶。梅喝茶時想起一段難受的回憶：去年冬天她曾替那座村莊送信，卻發現道路大門上了鎖。塔裡沒有人解釋原因。如今她注意到舊地圖上有一條線，新地圖卻沒有。線通往懸岩下的一座提燈站。地圖沒有祕門或神奇法術，只標示旅人從前停下交換消息的地方。

They copied both maps and asked the barber whether he would speak at a public meeting. He said he needed time to think. Mei accepted his answer. An wanted to announce their discovery immediately, but Mei reminded him that a drawing alone did not prove a road was still open. They would visit it first. The barber put the old book back in its box, as carefully as if it were a living bird. Before they left, he gave them the name of a woman who once carried letters for the western village.

兩人抄下兩張地圖，並問理髮師願不願在公開會議發言。他說需要時間考慮，梅接受了。安想立刻宣布新發現，但梅提醒他，一張圖不足以證明路現在仍可通行。他們得親自走一趟。理髮師把舊書放回盒子，動作小心，彷彿那是活著的鳥。離開前，他給了他們一位曾替西村送信的婦人的名字。

Mei offered to return the photographs before sunset. The couple said she could borrow copies but asked that the family names remain private. An took time to draw the wagon and the old road without copying faces. They had learned the difference between evidence about a route and information about a person’s life. Mei liked being trusted with both. On the way to the hanging rock, she put the photographs in a dry case and checked the sky again. There would be no sense in discovering an old route if they made the journey unsafe by rushing.

梅答應日落前歸還照片。夫妻允許她借副本，卻請她保密家人姓名。安花時間畫下馬車與舊路，沒有描摹人臉。他們學到路線證據和私人生活資訊的差別。梅很珍惜這份信任。前往懸岩途中，她把照片放進乾燥盒，再次檢查天空。若因趕路而讓旅程變危險，找到舊路也就沒有意義了。

An turned the last page and asked if a path could still exist when nobody used it. The barber answered that a road was more than lines on paper. People had to remember where it led and who might need it again.

安翻到最後一頁，問路若沒人走，是否仍然存在。理髮師回答，道路不只是紙上的線，還必須有人記得它通往何處，以及未來誰可能再需要它。

By dusk they reached the hanging rock. Beneath it lay the broken lantern station, covered in leaves. Mei found an iron ring that could still hold a lamp, but no fuel and no sign of recent visitors. An pointed to seven marks on the wall; the seventh had been covered with gray paint. They did not know who had painted it or when. Mei added both questions to her notebook. She had learned that the truth often began with a small mark someone else had stopped looking at.

傍晚兩人來到懸岩。岩下有一座破舊的提燈站，滿是落葉。梅找到一個仍可掛燈的鐵環，卻沒有燃料，也沒有最近有人來過的痕跡。安指著牆上七道記號；第七道被灰漆遮住。他們不知道是誰塗的，也不知道何時塗的。梅把兩個問題寫入筆記。她開始懂得，真相常常從別人不再注意的小記號開始。
