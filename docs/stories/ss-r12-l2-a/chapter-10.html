谷口的旗

The Flag at the Valley Gate

星光送不到的第七封信 · 第 10 / 25 章 · 1000–2000 · 英中對照 · 校訂中

用互動版查詞、朗讀與收藏

The group walked an active route beside the valley and reached a flat place near the old track. A single red flag stood there, moving forward in the wind, though the road itself was closed. Mei felt concern for travelers who might follow it by mistake. She measured the distance to the next shelter and wrote it in her notebook. The flag did not indicate a safe road, she realized; it marked a track where workers had once moved goods. Its meaning had changed, but nobody had changed the sign.

小隊沿著仍可通行的山谷路線前進，來到舊軌道旁一塊平地。一面紅旗立在那裡，迎風朝前飄，雖然道路本身已封閉。梅擔心旅人會誤以為旗幟指示安全方向。她量了到下一處避難所的距離，寫進筆記。她發現旗不是標示安全道路，而是過去工人搬運貨物的軌道。意思已經改變，卻沒有人更換標誌。

An found a wallet under a stone. It held no money, only a card for a law school and a note about a bicycle repair cycle. Mei was careful not to read the private messages. She wrote down where the wallet had been found and asked the village office to contact its owner. A woman in a clean suit arrived and said she had lost it while helping to clear the trail. She was studying how roads could be shared without taking away the rights of people who lived nearby.

安在石頭下找到一只錢包。裡面沒有錢，只有一張法律學校證件，以及一張關於腳踏車維修週期的便條。梅小心地沒有閱讀私人訊息，只記下拾獲位置，請村公所聯絡失主。一位穿著整齊套裝的女士來了，說她是清理小路時遺失錢包的。她正研究道路該如何共用，才不會剝奪附近居民的權利。

The law student asked to walk the route with her eyes on the ground rather than on the signal pole. She found that the red flag had another problem: it was hidden from shorter travelers behind a pile of boxes. Mei helped move the boxes to a marked storage area. Then they tested the view from several heights. A sign that one adult could see easily was not always clear to a child. The work took time, but the student said a rule mattered only when it could be used by the people it claimed to protect.

法律系學生提議走路時看著地面，而不只盯著訊號桿。她發現紅旗還有另一個問題：個子較矮的旅人會被一堆箱子擋住視線。梅協助把箱子移到標示好的儲存區，接著從不同高度測試。大人很容易看到的標誌，孩子不一定看得清楚。雖然費時，學生卻說規則只有真正能被聲稱要保護的人使用，才有意義。

She told them that a new plan might gain support from rich merchants, but riches should not buy the right to ignore safety. A question about sex appeared on an old travel form, next to a box asking whether the traveler needed help. The woman said such private information should not be used to decide whose warning came first. Mei agreed. Silence followed as they considered how easily a rule could exclude somebody. The law student offered to help write clearer rules for the message network.

她說新計畫或許會得到富有商人的支持，但財富不該買到忽視安全的權利。舊旅行表上有一欄詢問性別，旁邊則是是否需要協助的勾選欄。女士說，這類私人資料不應決定誰先收到警報。梅同意。眾人安靜片刻，想到規則竟可能如此容易排除某些人。法律系學生主動提出協助撰寫更清楚的傳訊規則。

They spent an hour changing the flag and adding a sign with a picture of a blocked road. Mei made sure that someone who could not read the written words would still understand the picture. An tested the view from the bend. Then Mei asked every person there to describe what the sign meant. The answers matched. They would review the sign after the next storm, because even a good idea needed to be checked where people actually used it.

他們花了一小時換下旗子，並加上畫有封路圖案的標誌。梅確認即使看不懂文字的人，也能理解圖意。安從彎道測試視線，接著梅請在場每個人說出標誌代表什麼。答案一致。他們決定下次風暴後再檢查，因為再好的點子也得在實際使用處接受驗證。

An drew a clear picture of the new sign and gave a copy to the next village. Mei asked him to add the date and the name of the person who had checked it. They also placed an older picture beside it, with a line explaining why the meaning had changed. The two drawings made a useful pair: one showed what to do today, and the other showed what had caused confusion before. Mei hoped that leaving both would stop someone from restoring the old flag years later without knowing its history.

安畫了新標誌的清楚圖樣，把副本送往下一村。梅請他加上日期和檢查人的姓名。他們還在旁放了舊圖，附上一行說明意思為何改變。兩張圖很有用：一張告訴人們今天該怎麼做，一張說明從前為何混淆。梅希望兩張都留下，免得多年後有人不了解歷史，又把舊旗恢復原狀。

The law student asked what would occur if two warning lights flashed at different times. Mei answered that no one should guess which was right. Each receiver would report the difference and wait for a checked message before choosing a closed road.

法律系學生問，如果兩盞警示燈在不同時間閃爍，會發生什麼事。梅回答，沒有人應該猜哪個才對。接收者要回報差異，等查證訊息後再決定是否走封閉道路。

That evening the law student walked with them to the tower. Five of the seven villages had answered now. The tower itself still held no steady light, but a small network of hand lamps was forming below it. Mei looked at the open road and the new sign beside the closed one. She felt that their work had finally reached beyond repair. They were learning to give a warning a clear meaning, so each person could decide how to move.

當晚法律系學生和他們一起走回高塔。七個村子已有五個回覆。高塔仍沒有穩定光源，但下方的小型手燈網路正在成形。梅望著暢通的道路，以及封路處的新標誌。她覺得他們的工作終於超越了修理本身。他們正在學會替警告賦予清楚意義，讓每個人都能決定如何前進。
