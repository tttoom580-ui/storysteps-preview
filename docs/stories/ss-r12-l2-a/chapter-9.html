藍色玻璃

The Blue Glass

星光送不到的第七封信 · 第 9 / 25 章 · 1000–2000 · 英中對照 · 校訂中

用互動版查詞、朗讀與收藏

At the lake laboratory, an old glass tube burst while nobody was near it. Blue liquid ran across the table and beneath a loose lid. Mei called for help aloud instead of touching the spill. A student named Ivo was still alive with excitement about the tower, but he had been careless with the bottles. His teacher said a good character was shown not by never making mistakes, but by telling the truth after one. Ivo apologized and asked Mei to forgive him for keeping a damaged bottle on the shelf.

湖畔實驗室裡，一支舊玻璃管在無人靠近時突然破裂。藍色液體流過桌面，滲進鬆動的蓋子下方。梅大聲求助，沒有伸手碰液體。學生艾沃對高塔仍充滿熱情，卻對瓶罐太粗心。他的老師說，品格不是永不犯錯，而是犯錯後願意說實話。艾沃道歉，請梅原諒他把損壞瓶子留在架上的事。

Ivo showed them an image of a shark in a book about sea animals. Its strong body, he said, had helped him imagine the shape of a lamp cover that could stand against wind. Mei smiled at the silly connection. The idea might be useful, but it would need testing. An asked him to express the problem in simple words: light was escaping in the wrong direction. If they improved the position of the glass, more of the result might reach the next village, without making the lamp brighter or hotter.

艾沃拿出一本海洋動物書，指著鯊魚的圖像。他說鯊魚堅固的身形讓他想像出能抵抗風的燈罩形狀。梅對這有點傻氣的聯想笑了。點子或許有用，但仍需要測試。安請他用簡單的話說明問題：光正在向錯誤方向散出。如果改善玻璃位置，更多光就可能到達下一個村子，不必把燈弄得更亮或更熱。

The teacher asked everyone to draw what they had seen before they talked about a new design. Ivo drew a round light, An drew its shadow, and Mei drew the distance from the lamp to the next post. Their pictures were different, but none was useless. When they laid them together, they could explain why a light looked bright near the glass and weak from far away. The lesson was slow, yet Mei enjoyed it. She did not have to know the answers first. She only had to ask a clear question and record the test honestly.

老師請大家在討論新設計前，先畫出各自看到的東西。艾沃畫圓光，安畫陰影，梅畫燈到下一站的距離。圖畫不同，卻沒有哪張無用。並排之後，他們能解釋為何光在玻璃旁看來很亮，遠處卻微弱。課程進展很慢，梅卻很喜歡。她不必事先知道答案，只要提出清楚問題，誠實記下測試。

Their first solution was a small paper model. Ivo drew a symbol for each part and gave the project a short title, “Light for the Road.” Mei insisted that the model should be placed away from the spilled liquid. A teacher checked the room and said the blue material was a cleaning mixture, dangerous to drink but safe to remove with proper gloves. The students cleaned the table, then built a dry model beside an open window. Their work began again without hurry.

他們的第一個解法是小型紙模型。艾沃替各部件畫符號，並把計畫取名為「路上的光」。梅堅持模型應遠離濺出的液體。老師檢查後說，藍色物質是清潔混合液，喝下去有危險，但戴妥手套就能安全清理。學生清潔桌面，再在開著的窗旁製作乾燥模型。他們沒有急著趕工，就這樣重新開始。

At dusk, An carried the paper shape outside and held it before the lamp. Mei stood at a safe distance and checked whether the light became clearer. It did, but only when the wind came from one direction. They recorded the failure too. Mei refused to call the test a complete success. She said the new cover might help a traveler on a calm night and fail on the night that mattered most. Ivo agreed to spend the next day making another shape.

黃昏時，安把紙樣帶到戶外，放在燈前。梅站在安全距離，看光線是否變得清楚。的確變清楚了，但只在風從一個方向吹來時有效。他們連失敗都記錄下來。梅拒絕稱測試完全成功。她說新燈罩或許能在晴夜幫上忙，卻可能在最關鍵的夜裡失效。艾沃同意隔天再做另一種形狀。

After the spill, the laboratory changed one simple rule. Every bottle now had to carry its name, the date it was opened, and the person responsible for checking it. Ivo was asked to help make the labels, not as punishment, but because he knew exactly what had gone wrong. Mei watched him work with careful hands. She thought a mistake could teach people only if they were willing to face it. Hiding a broken bottle might have protected Ivo from embarrassment for one day, but it could have hurt a friend the next.

液體濺出後，實驗室改了一項簡單規則。每個瓶子都要標示名稱、開封日期，以及負責檢查的人。老師請艾沃協助製作標籤，不是懲罰他，而是因為他最清楚出了什麼錯。梅看他仔細動手，心想錯誤只有在願意面對時才能教人。藏起破瓶子也許能讓艾沃少丟臉一天，隔天卻可能傷害朋友。

At the laboratory exit, the teacher served delicious vegetable soup. Ivo said the meal made him feel better, but Mei reminded him that a warm lunch would not improve the glass without more tests. They laughed and returned to their notes.

在實驗室出口，老師端出美味蔬菜湯。艾沃說這餐讓他舒服多了，但梅提醒他，暖和的午餐不會在沒有額外測試下改善玻璃。他們笑了，回頭繼續記筆記。

When Mei left, the teacher gave her a clean drawing of the model but kept all chemicals at the school. She was glad to see the students treat each failure as information. The little star on the drawing reminded her of the seven marks at the hanging rock. Perhaps the missing tower light was not one mystery to be solved by one clever person. It was a series of small questions that many careful people could answer, one after another, until the valley could see again.

梅離開時，老師給她一張乾淨的模型圖，所有化學藥品則留在學校。她很高興學生把每次失敗都當成資訊。圖上的小星星讓她想起懸岩下的七道記號。也許高塔失光並不是一個聰明人就能解開的單一謎題，而是許多細心的人能輪流回答的一連串小問題，直到山谷再次看得見光。
