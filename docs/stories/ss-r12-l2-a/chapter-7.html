乾燥山口

The Dry Pass

星光送不到的第七封信 · 第 7 / 25 章 · 1000–2000 · 英中對照 · 校訂中

用互動版查詞、朗讀與收藏

Their next journey led to a rocky pass that people called the little desert. Its climate was dry, and the land could contain very little water after summer. Mei checked her calendar, then began to jog to catch a guide before he left. An asked her to slow down. Each step mattered more than speed on this narrow road. They met an old keeper cleaning his doorway with a mop. He had a humble home, but he knew every stone between the pass and the northern village.

下一段旅程通往一處多石山口，大家叫它小沙漠。那裡氣候乾燥，夏天後土地留不住多少水。梅看了日曆，開始慢跑，想在嚮導出發前追上他。安請她放慢。在這條狹路上，每一步都比速度重要。他們遇見一位用拖把清理門口的老守路人。他的家很樸素，卻認得山口到北村之間的每一塊石頭。

The keeper told them the best travel period came before the cold winds. He had learned one important principle from his father: never leave a traveler behind because the traveler walks slowly. An thought it was an ideal rule for their new network. He asked the keeper to introduce them to the people farther west. The keeper showed them a stone shaped like a turtle, a landmark from which the path turned sharply uphill. Thus the group could make a map that even a new messenger could follow.

守路人說最佳旅行期間在寒風來臨之前。他從父親身上學到一項重要原則：不能因為旅人走得慢，就把人丟下。安覺得這很適合當新網路的規則。他請守路人介紹西方居民。守路人指著一塊形似烏龜的石頭，那是道路急轉上坡的地標。如此一來，他們就能畫出連新信使也走得懂的地圖。

The old keeper had marked safe sleeping places with small piles of stones. He told Mei that a traveler should never move one just for fun. Another person might reach the pass in darkness and trust the marker. Mei added that rule to her route card and drew the shape beside it. They also counted how many people could fit into the nearest shelter without blocking its door. The keeper liked that question. He said a shelter was not safe merely because it had walls; people had to be able to enter and leave when the wind became strong.

老守路人用小堆石頭標示安全的歇宿處。他告訴梅，旅人不能只是好玩就搬走，因為另一個人可能在黑暗中抵達山口並信任標記。梅把規則寫進路線卡，也畫出石堆形狀。他們還清點最近避難屋能容納幾人，才不會堵住門口。守路人很欣賞這問題。他說，避難屋不是有牆就安全；起強風時，人們還得能進出。

At midday they stopped beside a well. The keeper brushed his teeth with a simple toothbrush and filled his bottle before talking again. He said the tower had once sent messages about both storms and road work, but the old signs had disappeared over time. “You call this progress?” he asked, pointing at a broken marker. Mei did not defend the tower. She opened her notebook and asked which warning signs had worked best. The keeper drew one shape for loose stones and another for a blocked road.

中午他們在井邊停下。守路人用普通牙刷刷完牙、裝滿水瓶後，才繼續說話。他說高塔從前會傳送風暴和道路施工的消息，但舊標記隨時間消失。「你們把這叫進步嗎？」他指著壞掉的路標問。梅沒有替高塔辯護，而是打開筆記，問什麼警示記號最有用。守路人畫了一種代表鬆動石頭的形狀，以及另一種代表道路封閉的形狀。

The walk grew harder near sunset. Mei had to choose between a short, steep path and a longer path that passed a safe shelter. She chose the longer road. An agreed, but only after they checked that the shelter was open. The keeper smiled when they returned before dark. He offered to carry a letter to the next village the following morning. Mei gave him a copy and kept the original in her bag, so no single loss would end the message.

夕陽將落時，路愈來愈難走。梅必須在又短又陡的道路，和經過安全避難處的長路之間選擇。她選了長路。安也同意，但先確認避難處確實開放。兩人在天黑前回來，守路人露出笑容。他願意隔天早晨替他們把信送到下一村。梅給他副本，原件留在自己的袋中，避免一次遺失就讓訊息中斷。

Mei imagined the seventh village waiting beyond the ridge. She had never been there, and for that reason she tried not to turn its people into a story she had invented. They might be angry, glad, busy, or unwilling to meet strangers. None of those feelings changed the need for a safe route. An wrote a short letter asking whether anyone there could send a reply by hand or by light. The keeper promised to carry it only as far as he knew the road. Another messenger would have to take the next part.

梅想像山脊後方等待的第七村。她從未到過，所以努力不把那裡的人編成自己想像的故事。他們可能生氣、高興、忙碌，或不願見陌生人；這些心情都不改變安全道路的重要。安寫了一封短信，詢問那裡的人能否用手遞或燈光回覆。守路人承諾只送到自己認識的路段，下一段必須交給其他信使。

The keeper said every living being on the pass needed water, even the birds that crossed it only once a year. Mei added a small water sign to the map but noted that travelers should never take water meant for another person's emergency supply.

守路人說山口每個活著的生命都需要水，連一年只飛過一次的鳥也是。梅在地圖加上小水源記號，也註明旅人不能取用別人留下的緊急用水。

Before they slept, An placed seven little stones on the table. Four villages had now answered, but three remained silent. Mei wanted to call the day a victory. Instead she said they had made one careful advance and still faced a long road. They would need the keeper’s help, and perhaps the help of people they had never met. Far above the pass, the dark star tower stood under a clear sky. Its darkness no longer meant that nobody was moving.

睡前，安在桌上放了七顆小石頭。四個村子已有回覆，另外三個仍沉默。梅想把這一天稱為勝利，最後卻說他們只是謹慎地向前推進一步，前方還有長路。他們需要守路人的幫助，也許還需要陌生人的幫助。高處的暗色觀星塔立在晴空下，但它的黑暗已不再代表所有人都停下腳步。
