一盞燈的價錢

The Price of One Lamp

星光送不到的第七封信 · 第 5 / 25 章 · 1000–2000 · 英中對照 · 校訂完成

用互動版查詞、朗讀與收藏

At the public school hall, Mei learned that carrying winter warnings was becoming a burden for families. A college student named Rui brought a double list of every expense: oil, glass, cloth, and food for the walkers. His fellow students had built a box that could hold a hand lamp on a bicycle. The box had one useful feature, a cover that moved when the wheel turned. The group wanted the box to function in rain, but they had not made a perfect one yet. Mei asked them to show what failed as well as what worked.

在公立學校禮堂，梅得知傳遞冬季警告愈來愈成為家庭負擔。大學生睿帶來兩份支出清單，列出燈油、玻璃、布料和步行信使的食物。他的同學做了一個能固定在腳踏車上的手燈盒。這個盒子有項實用特色：輪子轉動時，蓋子也會移動。他們希望它能在雨中正常運作，但還沒做出完美版本。梅請他們同時展示失敗與成功的部分。

A lady who ran the school kitchen talked about the production of glass covers. The factory could make only a small selection of sizes. Its workers seldom had enough time to repair old lamps as well as make new ones. Outside, a soccer game had filled the yard with happy noise. Mei liked that spirit, but she knew a good plan needed more than cheers. The kitchen worker offered soup to every volunteer, then asked how the cost would be shared. Nobody had a clear answer. The students had been working without asking who would pay later.

經營學校廚房的女士談到玻璃燈罩的生產。工廠只能製作少數尺寸，工人很少有足夠時間同時修舊燈和製作新燈。外面足球比賽讓院子充滿歡呼。梅喜歡這樣的精神，卻知道好計畫不只需要加油聲。廚房女士請每位志工喝湯，再問費用要如何分攤。沒有人能清楚回答。學生一直努力工作，卻沒先問之後由誰付錢。

The students invited Mei to inspect their work table. Each tool had a place, but many parts were borrowed from different homes. Rui said the group wanted to return everything with a clear list. He showed her a lamp with a cracked corner and another with a loose handle. Mei liked that he had kept the damaged examples instead of hiding them. The broken pieces would help new volunteers learn what to check. She suggested making a picture card with one safe example and one unsafe one, so even a child could see the difference.

學生請梅檢查工作桌。每件工具都有位置，但許多零件是向各戶借來的。睿說大家希望列好清單，日後全數歸還。他拿出一盞角落裂開的燈，以及另一盞把手鬆動的燈。梅喜歡他沒有藏起壞掉的樣品。破損零件能幫新志工學會檢查。她建議做圖片卡，一面是安全例子，一面是不安全例子，讓孩子也看得出差別。

A child tried to swallow a piece of bread too quickly and began to cough. Mei handed him a tissue, and the kitchen worker gave him water. A sweater hung beside the door to dry, while another student began to sweep the floor. Someone had spread a frightening story that a terrorist group wanted to destroy the lamps. Rui had checked the report and found no evidence for it; it was an old story from another nation, not news about their valley. He asked everyone to stop repeating it as if it were true.

一個孩子把麵包吞得太快，開始咳嗽。梅遞給他面紙，廚房女士給他水。門邊掛著一件正在晾乾的毛衣，另一位學生則開始掃地。有人散播可怕傳言，說恐怖組織打算破壞燈塔。睿核對後沒有發現相關證據；那是別國的舊故事，不是山谷的新聞。他請大家別再當成真事傳下去。

Rui wanted to upload the list of expenses for every family to see. Mei agreed, but asked him to remove private names and home addresses first. They would publish the prices, the work still needed, and the places where help was welcome. It was their first public cost sheet. Before leaving, Mei counted the lamps the students had tested: only two were ready. The result disappointed them, yet it was also a beginning. Two real lights were better than seven promises no one could keep.

睿想把支出清單上傳，讓每戶人家都能看見。梅同意，但請他先刪去私人姓名和住址。他們將公開價格、仍需完成的工作，以及歡迎協助的地點。這是他們第一張公開成本表。離開前，梅清點學生測試過的燈：只有兩盞準備好了。結果讓大家失望，卻也是開始。兩盞真正能亮的燈，比七個做不到的承諾更好。

The public cost sheet started an argument about fair work. Some students had paid for supplies from their own pockets, while others had offered time instead of money. Mei said both kinds of help mattered, but neither should be forced. The group agreed that anyone could say no to a new task and still belong to the project. Rui added a column for donated hours and another for borrowed goods. When the list was finished, they read it aloud. People were less afraid of a cost when they could see what it meant and who had chosen to pay it.

公開成本表引發關於公平工作的討論。有些學生自掏腰包買材料，其他人則捐出時間而不是金錢。梅說兩種幫助都重要，但都不能強迫。大家同意任何人都可以拒絕新工作，仍然是團隊一員。睿加了一欄捐出工時，另一欄記借用物品。清單完成後，大家朗讀一遍。當人們看得懂成本代表什麼、又是誰自願支付，就不那麼害怕它。

At night An carried one lamp to the school roof, and Mei took the other to the tower steps. They followed the old rule: one flash for arrival, two for delay, three for danger. It worked across the short distance. Mei wrote the time and asked Rui to keep a second record. He understood why. If the tower failed again, two sets of notes would help people see whether the mistake came from the lamp, the road, or the person sending the message.

夜裡安把一盞燈帶上學校屋頂，梅則拿另一盞到高塔階梯。他們依照舊規則測試：閃一次代表抵達，兩次代表延誤，三次代表危險。短距離傳訊成功了。梅記下時間，也請睿保留第二份紀錄。他明白原因。如果高塔再次失靈，兩份筆記將幫大家判斷錯誤來自燈、道路，或傳訊的人。
