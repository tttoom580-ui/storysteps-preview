湖邊的火光

Fire Beside the Lake

星光送不到的第七封信 · 第 6 / 25 章 · 1000–2000 · 英中對照 · 校訂中

用互動版查詞、朗讀與收藏

The first trouble arrived from the shore, where a storage shed had caught fire. A fireman named Bo had put out the flames, but the little boat engine was damaged. The affair seemed small compared with a mountain storm, yet it had left the village without a fast way to send warnings. A neighbor brought cabbage soup and dry socks for the tired workers. Mei found Bo sitting on a pillow beside the lake, writing everything in a journal. She asked whether he would let her copy his report instead of relying on guesses.

第一個麻煩來自湖岸，一間儲藏棚起火了。名叫博的消防員已經撲滅火焰，但小船引擎受損。與山區風暴相比，這件事似乎很小，卻讓村子失去快速傳送警告的方法。鄰居帶來高麗菜湯和乾襪子給疲憊的工人。梅發現博坐在湖邊的墊子上，正把經過寫進日誌。她問能不能抄錄他的報告，而不是只憑猜測。

The village held a badminton match each autumn, a simple part of its culture. This year the match had become an emergency meeting. Bo said the crisis showed why a clever plan should not depend on one engine. People spoke about how to govern the lake routes fairly. An reminded them that a signal was a human promise, not a magic light that made danger disappear. Every individual who received a warning needed to know what to do next. Mei asked the boat owners to participate in a short test from the opposite bank.

村莊每年秋天都舉辦羽毛球比賽，這是當地文化的一部分。今年比賽變成了緊急會議。博說這場危機證明，聰明的計畫不能只依賴一具引擎。大家討論如何公平管理湖上的路線。安提醒眾人，訊號是人與人的承諾，不是能讓危險消失的魔法光。每個接到警報的人都必須知道接下來怎麼做。梅邀請船主到對岸參與簡短測試。

Bo showed them a map of the lake when it was covered by winter ice. The old path across the water was drawn with a dotted line, and he explained that the line meant memory, not permission. Travelers had once crossed there during hard winters, but nobody checked its strength now. Mei drew a large warning mark beside the line on her copy. She liked old maps, yet she was learning to distrust any map that forgot to say when it had been made. The water outside looked peaceful, but a picture from another winter could not keep someone safe today.

博給他們看一張冬季結冰時的湖圖。舊時跨湖路線畫成虛線，博解釋那代表記憶，不代表准許通行。以前寒冬有人走過那裡，如今卻沒有人檢查冰面強度。梅在副本的虛線旁畫上大大的警告記號。她喜歡舊地圖，卻逐漸學會不信任忘記標示製作日期的地圖。外頭的水面雖平靜，別個冬天的圖畫卻不能保障今天的安全。

A child asked whether the old tower was a prison for the people who worked there. His grandmother laughed and told him no; it was meant to help free travelers move safely. The child ran forth and back with the hand lamp, eager to help. Mei stopped him before he reached the slippery stones. She explained the three signal patterns and chose an adult to hold the lamp. Across the water, Bo answered the first flash. The second reply came late, because a tree covered part of the view.

一個孩子問舊高塔是不是把工作的人關起來的監獄。祖母笑著說不是；它原本是要幫旅人安全地自由往來。孩子拿著手燈來回奔跑，很想幫忙。梅在他踏上濕滑石頭前攔住他，解釋三種燈光模式，並請一位大人拿燈。湖對岸，博回應了第一道閃光。第二次回覆卻晚了，因為一棵樹擋住了部分視線。

They moved the lamp several steps and tried again. This time both sides could see it without walking near the water. The group marked the safe place with white paint. Bo offered to teach three people to send the same warning, so the system would not stop when he was away. Mei liked that idea. A village should not have to wait for one hero when ordinary neighbors could learn a clear method and support one another.

他們把燈移動幾步後重新測試，這次兩岸都看得見，不必走到水邊。大家用白漆標出安全位置。博主動提出教三個人傳送相同警告，這樣他不在時系統也不會停擺。梅喜歡這個主意。村子不該只能等待一位英雄，平凡鄰居也可以學會清楚的方法，彼此支持。

A grandmother took Mei to the place where the old boat had once been tied. She pointed out a stone that could be seen from both banks, even when the water rose. Mei asked if the stone would also be visible in fog. The grandmother thought about it and said she did not know. That answer led to another test, one they would do on a foggy morning rather than pretend to complete that night. Mei thanked her. Uncertainty was easier to work with when people said it aloud, instead of hiding it behind a confident voice.

一位祖母帶梅到舊船曾綁住的位置，指著兩岸都看得到的一塊石頭，即使漲水也看得見。梅問濃霧時是否仍能看到。祖母想了想，說不知道。這讓他們多出一項測試，必須等起霧早晨做，而不是假裝今晚就能完成。梅向她道謝。只要人們把不確定說出口，而不是藏在自信口氣後面，問題就更容易處理。

The work did not excite everyone in the same way. One child was more interested in a dry sock that had lost its partner. Bo smiled and said that caring for people after a fire included small comforts as well as large machines.

這項工作並不會讓每個人同樣興奮。有個孩子更關心一隻找不到另一半的乾襪子。博笑著說，火災後照顧人不只靠大型機器，也包括這些微小安慰。

That night Mei slept in the school guest room. Outside, the sky was clear, but the smell of smoke remained on her coat. She wrote that the lake needed a second route and a second trained sender. An added a small drawing of the tree that had blocked their signal. The drawing looked ordinary, almost foolishly simple. Yet it explained a delay that might have frightened a whole town during a real storm. They had turned one day of trouble into a lesson that could travel farther than the boat.

當晚梅睡在學校客房。外面天空晴朗，外套上卻仍有煙味。她記下湖區需要第二條路，以及第二位受訓傳訊人。安加上一張擋住燈光的樹木小圖。那圖畫得平凡，簡單到幾乎可笑，卻說明了一種在真正風暴中可能嚇壞整座城的延誤。他們把一天的麻煩變成能比小船傳得更遠的教訓。
