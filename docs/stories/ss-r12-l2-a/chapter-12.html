來自首都的信

A Letter from the Capital

星光送不到的第七封信 · 第 12 / 25 章 · 1000–2000 · 英中對照 · 校訂中

用互動版查詞、朗讀與收藏

A letter arrived from the capital on a day of sudden rain. The form inside was nearly blank, except for a warning about danger on the northern road. Mei did not blame the writer for the missing information; she asked for a clearer direction. In the countryside, names changed from village to village, and an unclear sign could send a customer or a traveler to the wrong place. An measured the distance between two shelters and wrote the measurement in dark ink so the number would not be lost in the next copy.

一封來自首都的信在突然下雨的日子送到。裡面的表格幾乎空白，只有關於北方道路危險的警告。梅沒有責怪寫信的人漏填資訊，而是要求更清楚的方向。鄉間同一條路在不同村子可能有不同名字，模糊的標示會讓顧客或旅人走錯地方。安量了兩處避難所的距離，用深色墨水寫下測量數據，避免下一次抄寫時遺漏。

At the meeting, a dentist from the lower village spoke about people who could hardly travel for care during storms. One child had been scared by the last river crossing and now refused to leave home. The dentist asked everyone to show respect for such fears, not laugh at them. Mei agreed that a safe route must be judged by more than strong walkers. A person in soft slippers might need extra time to get to the gate. The group began to write travel times for people of different ages.

會議上，下村的一名牙醫談到風暴時幾乎無法外出就醫的居民。有個孩子上次過河被嚇壞，如今不肯離家。牙醫請大家尊重這種害怕，不要取笑。梅同意，安全路線不能只用體力強的人當標準。穿著柔軟拖鞋的人也可能需要更多時間到大門。小組開始記下不同年齡的人所需的步行時間。

Before the meeting, Mei and An walked part of the northern road with a retired teacher. The teacher moved more slowly than they did and stopped once to rest under a tree. The travel time was longer than the public chart claimed. Mei did not tell the teacher to hurry. She changed the chart instead. The teacher thanked her, then reminded her that some travelers would need a place to sit as well as a sign showing the way. An drew a small bench beside each suitable shelter. Their new plan began to include time for people, not only distance on a map.

會議前，梅和安陪一位退休教師走了一段北路。教師走得比他們慢，途中還在樹下休息一次。所花時間比公用圖表記載的長。梅沒有叫老師走快，而是修改圖表。教師道謝，並提醒有些旅人除了路標，還需要坐下休息的地方。安在合適避難所旁畫了小椅子。新計畫開始計入人的時間，而不只地圖上的距離。

Someone brought fries and pudding for lunch. As tea was poured from a heavy teapot, the village leader asked what Mei thought about a proposed settlement between the tower council and the northern villagers. Mei said she would regard the plan seriously, but only if the villagers could read and change it. The old plan had been made far away without speaking to the people who walked the paths. A new sheet would leave space for questions, corrections, and disagreement before any money was spent.

有人帶了薯條和布丁當午餐。大家從沉重茶壺倒茶時，村長問梅對守塔議會與北村提出的協議有什麼想法。梅說她會認真看待，但前提是村民能閱讀並修改。舊計畫是在遠方訂好的，沒有人和真正走路的人交談。新表格將留出提出問題、修正和表達不同意見的空間，然後才花錢。

The dentist offered to help test routes with patients who chose to volunteer. Mei thanked him but refused to use anyone’s medical details in public reports. They would record travel time and needed support without names. An placed the new form beside the old blank one. The difference was clear: one was a request to obey, the other was an invitation to take part. The meeting voted to send the second form to the capital with several examples from the valley.

牙醫願意協助，讓自願的病人測試路線。梅道謝，但拒絕把任何人的醫療細節放進公開報告。他們只記錄步行時間和所需協助，不記姓名。安把新表格放在舊空白表旁。差別很清楚：一份要人服從，另一份邀請人參與。會議投票通過，把第二份連同山谷實例送往首都。

A council clerk asked Mei why she had left several answers blank on the funding request. She replied that she could not give a number she had not checked. The clerk showed her where to write a question instead and promised to ask the capital office for a clear reply. Mei was grateful. The new process felt slower than making a quick guess, but it gave everyone a record of the same questions. She hoped the people reading the form far away would remember that each number stood for a person walking on a real mountain road.

議會書記問梅為何補助申請上有幾格沒填。她回答，未核對的數字不能亂寫。書記教她如何改寫成問題，答應向首都辦公室要求明確回覆。梅很感謝。新流程比猜個數字慢，卻讓每個人都有相同的問題紀錄。她希望遠方讀表格的人記得，每個數字都代表一個在真實山路上行走的人。

The team would measure the walking time again when the road dried. Mei asked everyone to settle questions about the form before approving it. A child wearing one slipper and one shoe watched the meeting and asked why the pictures were larger than before.

道路乾燥後，小隊會再測量步行時間。梅請大家在批准表格前先解決疑問。一個一腳穿拖鞋、一腳穿普通鞋的孩子看著會議，問為什麼圖畫變得比以前大。

On her way home, Mei passed a small bridge where water had begun to rise. She put up a temporary sign and told two nearby families what it meant. The rain had stopped, but the road was still wet. She was learning to act before a problem grew large, rather than wait for a disaster to prove her right. In her bag lay another letter, this one addressed to the sixth village. It was finally time to send a warning there and see whether anyone would answer.

返家途中，梅經過一座小橋，發現水位開始上升。她架起臨時標示，向附近兩戶人家解釋意思。雨已停，但路面仍濕。她正學著在問題變大之前採取行動，而不是等災難來證明自己正確。袋中還有另一封信，是寫給第六村的。他們終於該把警告送到那裡，看看是否有人回覆。
