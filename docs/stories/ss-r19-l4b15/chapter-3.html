The show aired footage of a lakeside date both partners insisted had never happened, including dialogue they would not normally use. Something had changed, but not everybody noticed it in the same order. Jia Mi listened to the account of the nearest worker, then to someone standing farther away. Their differences were not necessarily lies. Each person had been given a different view and a different reason to be afraid of admitting uncertainty. Lu Sheng read the consent form as a series of separate choices, not a single signature that could authorize everything forever. A handwritten label read assign, but no one could say who had written it there. The term calorie appeared in the revised file; Jia Mi checked its meaning against the original page.

節目播出湖畔約會影像，兩人都堅稱沒有發生，對話也不是平常說話方式。某件事改變了，但人們發現它的順序不同。賈米先聽近處工作人員的描述，再聽遠處旁觀者說法。差異不一定是謊言：每個人看到的角度不同，承認不確定時所要承擔的風險也不同。陸聲把同意書當成一連串分開的選擇，而不是一個簽名就能永遠授權一切。手寫標籤寫著「指派」，卻沒有人知道是誰留下的。修訂檔案出現「卡路里」一詞，賈米拿原始頁面核對它的意思。

Jia insisted on a complete version before the next voting window, while Lu documented their objections on paper. Jia Mi refused to pretend the previous decision had solved everything. The practical result was still there, useful but narrow. For each thing the team knew, there were two things it had not measured. Jia Mi drew a line through the claims that depended on speculation and asked Lu Sheng to keep the original record intact. On the next form, the action field read concentrate, but the witness had never approved the operation. People disagreed about context; Jia Mi separated the feeling from the facts they could test. The instructions used the verb defend without naming who would carry the risk; Jia Mi circled that gap.

A small disagreement changed the schedule. Jia Mi wanted to proceed before evidence disappeared; Lu Sheng wanted another check because a wrong conclusion might hurt people who could not easily defend themselves. They did not settle the matter by choosing a leader. They wrote down what a short, reversible test could actually establish. A note about the ferry lay beside the old register, with a date that Jia Mi could not verify. Lu Sheng pointed to the housework in the inventory and asked why its location had changed. An official memo described the plan as incredible, but Jia Mi refused to treat an adjective as evidence.

一場小爭執改變了時程。賈米想趁證據消失前行動；陸聲要求再核對一次，因為錯誤結論可能傷害難以自辯的人。他們沒有靠選領袖解決爭議，而是列出短暫、可逆測試真正能證明什麼。一張談到「渡輪」的便條放在舊登記簿旁，日期讓賈米無法核實。陸聲指向清單上的「家務」，追問其位置為何改變。官方備忘錄形容計畫「驚人的」，賈米不願把形容詞當作證據。

A camera operator admitted some scenes had been reconstructed with doubles after the couple refused a location change. The plan had been amended three times, and each change left a trace. Jia Mi valued those corrections more than a perfect-looking first draft. If the outcome proved disappointing, the group would still be able to identify which assumption needed repair. Lu Sheng watched for the quiet participant who had not yet been invited to speak. A handwritten label read inspiration, but no one could say who had written it there. The term insult appeared in the revised file; Jia Mi checked its meaning against the original page. Lu Sheng noticed the word intermediate in a note that someone had added after the first inspection.

The difference was not always malicious, but it could still hurt somebody. Lu Sheng asked that the correction be written in language the affected people could understand. A witness asked whether the team was taking sides. Jia Mi answered that refusing to invent a fact was not the same as refusing to care. What mattered was whether the procedure protected someone whose story was less convenient to tell. Lu Sheng listened without interrupting and wrote down the question exactly as it had been asked. 一名見證人問團隊是否偏袒。賈米回答，不捏造事實不代表冷漠。真正重要的是程序能否保護那個故事不方便被說出口的人。陸聲沒有打斷，逐字記下原問題。修訂檔案出現「步調」一詞，賈米拿原始頁面核對它的意思。陸聲注意到初次檢查後有人在筆記裡加上「百分比」一詞。手寫標籤寫著「攝影」，卻沒有人知道是誰留下的。

Even after the group agreed on the immediate step, another question remained. The answer would not arrive through a dramatic announcement. Someone would have to return to the place, compare the earlier notes, and accept the possibility that the next piece of evidence might spoil an attractive theory. Jia Mi prepared to do exactly that. A memory could be precious even when two people remembered it differently. Jia Mi would not let a network promise perfect love by deleting disagreement. 即使大家同意眼前步驟，另一個問題仍在。答案不會靠戲劇性公告降臨；有人得返回現場、比對舊筆記，並接受新證據可能推翻漂亮理論。賈米準備做的正是這件事。即使兩人記憶不一致，回憶仍然珍貴。賈米不允許電視台靠刪除分歧來承諾完美愛情。手寫標籤寫著「優先事項」，卻沒有人知道是誰留下的。指示裡用了「繼續進行」這個動詞，卻沒說誰承擔風險；賈米圈出這項缺漏。陸聲指向清單上的「葡萄乾」，追問其位置為何改變。
