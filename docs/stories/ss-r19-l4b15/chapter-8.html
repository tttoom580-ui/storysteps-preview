A Memory on a Flash Drive

Jia had recorded their first meeting in a private audio journal. A comparison showed her recollection after the vote was missing a specific sentence. Something had changed, but not everybody noticed it in the same order. Jia Mi listened to the account of the nearest worker, then to someone standing farther away. Their differences were not necessarily lies. Each person had been given a different view and a different reason to be afraid of admitting uncertainty. A memory could be precious even when two people remembered it differently. Jia Mi would not let a network promise perfect love by deleting disagreement. The summary called the situation abstract; Jia Mi asked which observation justified that description. On the next form, the action field read acquire, but the witness had never approved the operation.

賈米曾用私人音訊日記記下初次見面，比對後發現投票後她少記得一句話。某件事改變了，但人們發現它的順序不同。賈米先聽近處工作人員的描述，再聽遠處旁觀者說法。差異不一定是謊言：每個人看到的角度不同，承認不確定時所要承擔的風險也不同。即使兩人記憶不一致，回憶仍然珍貴。賈米不允許電視台靠刪除分歧來承諾完美愛情。摘要以「抽象的」形容情況，賈米追問這種說法有何觀察根據。下一張表單把動作寫成「取得」，見證人卻從未同意這項操作。

One contestant's signature appeared on a page she had never received, revealing a potential forged attachment. Jia Mi refused to pretend the previous decision had solved everything. The practical result was still there, useful but narrow. For each thing the team knew, there were two things it had not measured. Jia Mi drew a line through the claims that depended on speculation and asked Lu Sheng to keep the original record intact. A handwritten label read bridegroom, but no one could say who had written it there. The term bulletin appeared in the revised file; Jia Mi checked its meaning against the original page. Lu Sheng noticed the word civilian in a note that someone had added after the first inspection.

一名參賽者的簽名出現在沒見過的附件上，顯示可能有偽造文件。賈米不願假裝前一次決定已解決所有問題。成果確實存在，卻有明確界限。每個已知事項旁都還有未測量的部分。賈米劃掉靠猜測成立的說法，請陸聲保留原始紀錄。手寫標籤寫著「新郎」，卻沒有人知道是誰留下的。修訂檔案出現「公告」一詞，賈米拿原始頁面核對它的意思。陸聲注意到初次檢查後有人在筆記裡加上「平民」一詞。

A small disagreement changed the schedule. Jia Mi wanted to proceed before evidence disappeared; Lu Sheng wanted another check because a wrong conclusion might hurt people who could not easily defend themselves. They did not settle the matter by choosing a leader. They wrote down what a short, reversible test could actually establish. Lu Sheng noticed the word composer in a note that someone had added after the first inspection. Lu Sheng repeated the instruction to constitute, then asked whether anyone had the right to refuse. The term cue appeared in the revised file; Jia Mi checked its meaning against the original page.

一場小爭執改變了時程。賈米想趁證據消失前行動；陸聲要求再核對一次，因為錯誤結論可能傷害難以自辯的人。他們沒有靠選領袖解決爭議，而是列出短暫、可逆測試真正能證明什麼。陸聲注意到初次檢查後有人在筆記裡加上「作曲家」一詞。陸聲重述「構成」的指示，再問當事人是否有權拒絕。修訂檔案出現「提示音」一詞，賈米拿原始頁面核對它的意思。

The journal proved a discrepancy, not a machine that could perfectly restore memory, and Jia refused to let the show access it. The plan had been amended three times, and each change left a trace. Jia Mi valued those corrections more than a perfect-looking first draft. If the outcome proved disappointing, the group would still be able to identify which assumption needed repair. Lu Sheng watched for the quiet participant who had not yet been invited to speak. The instructions used the verb dominate without naming who would carry the risk; Jia Mi circled that gap. Lu Sheng noticed the word enthusiasm in a note that someone had added after the first inspection. A handwritten label read estimate, but no one could say who had written it there.

日記證明兩種記憶不同，並非可以完整恢復的機器，賈米拒絕交給節目組。計畫改了三次，每次修改都留下痕跡。賈米認為這些修正比看似完美的初稿更有價值。就算結果不理想，團隊仍能知道該修正哪一項假設。陸聲也注意到那名一直沒有發言機會的人。指示裡用了「主導」這個動詞，卻沒說誰承擔風險；賈米圈出這項缺漏。陸聲注意到初次檢查後有人在筆記裡加上「熱情」一詞。手寫標籤寫著「估計」，卻沒有人知道是誰留下的。

An official version reached the room before the practical work was finished. Jia Mi read it once, then compared it with what they had actually measured. The difference was not always malicious, but it could still hurt somebody. Lu Sheng asked that the correction be written in language the affected people could understand. A memory could be precious even when two people remembered it differently. Jia Mi would not let a network promise perfect love by deleting disagreement. An official memo described the plan as fertile, but Jia Mi refused to treat an adjective as evidence. The term gender appeared in the revised file; Jia Mi checked its meaning against the original page. Lu Sheng noticed the word gulf in a note that someone had added after the first inspection.

實際工作還沒完成，官方版本就傳到現場。賈米讀了一次，再比對真正測得的結果。差異未必都是惡意，卻仍可能傷害人。陸聲要求把更正寫成受影響者看得懂的語言。即使兩人記憶不一致，回憶仍然珍貴。賈米不允許電視台靠刪除分歧來承諾完美愛情。官方備忘錄形容計畫「適合滋生的」，賈米不願把形容詞當作證據。修訂檔案出現「性別」一詞，賈米拿原始頁面核對它的意思。陸聲注意到初次檢查後有人在筆記裡加上「海灣」一詞。

A witness asked whether the team was taking sides. Jia Mi answered that refusing to invent a fact was not the same as refusing to care. What mattered was whether the procedure protected someone whose story was less convenient to tell. Lu Sheng listened without interrupting and wrote down the question exactly as it had been asked. Lu Sheng noticed the word intensity in a note that someone had added after the first inspection. Lu Sheng repeated the instruction to invade, then asked whether anyone had the right to refuse. The term jealousy appeared in the revised file; Jia Mi checked its meaning against the original page.

一名見證人問團隊是否偏袒。賈米回答，不捏造事實不代表冷漠。真正重要的是程序能否保護那個故事不方便被說出口的人。陸聲沒有打斷，逐字記下原問題。陸聲注意到初次檢查後有人在筆記裡加上「強度」一詞。陸聲重述「入侵」的指示，再問當事人是否有權拒絕。修訂檔案出現「嫉妒」一詞，賈米拿原始頁面核對它的意思。

Even after the group agreed on the immediate step, another question remained. The answer would not arrive through a dramatic announcement. Someone would have to return to the place, compare the earlier notes, and accept the possibility that the next piece of evidence might spoil an attractive theory. Jia Mi prepared to do exactly that. Lu Sheng read the consent form as a series of separate choices, not a single signature that could authorize everything forever. The summary called the situation learned; Jia Mi asked which observation justified that description. Lu Sheng noticed the word software in a note that someone had added after the first inspection. An official memo described the plan as systematic, but Jia Mi refused to treat an adjective as evidence.

即使大家同意眼前步驟，另一個問題仍在。答案不會靠戲劇性公告降臨；有人得返回現場、比對舊筆記，並接受新證據可能推翻漂亮理論。賈米準備做的正是這件事。陸聲把同意書當成一連串分開的選擇，而不是一個簽名就能永遠授權一切。摘要以「學養深厚的」形容情況，賈米追問這種說法有何觀察根據。陸聲注意到初次檢查後有人在筆記裡加上「軟體」一詞。官方備忘錄形容計畫「有系統的」，賈米不願把形容詞當作證據。
