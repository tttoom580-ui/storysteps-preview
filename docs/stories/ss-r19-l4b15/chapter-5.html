Another couple tried to leave the set but found an exit fee larger than their entire participation payment. Something had changed, but not everybody noticed it in the same order. Jia Mi listened to the account of the nearest worker, then to someone standing farther away. Their differences were not necessarily lies. Each person had been given a different view and a different reason to be afraid of admitting uncertainty. Lu Sheng read the consent form as a series of separate choices, not a single signature that could authorize everything forever. The instructions used the verb abandon without naming who would carry the risk; Jia Mi circled that gap. Lu Sheng noticed the word adjust in a note that someone had added after the first inspection.

另一對情侶想離開攝影棚，卻發現退賽費比全部參加酬勞還高。某件事改變了，但人們發現它的順序不同。賈米先聽近處工作人員的描述，再聽遠處旁觀者說法。差異不一定是謊言：每個人看到的角度不同，承認不確定時所要承擔的風險也不同。陸聲把同意書當成一連串分開的選擇，而不是一個簽名就能永遠授權一切。指示裡用了「拋下」這個動詞，卻沒說誰承擔風險；賈米圈出這項缺漏。陸聲注意到初次檢查後有人在筆記裡加上「調整」一詞。

Lu declined the show's offer to sell the name back as a premium reveal and contacted an independent clinician. Jia Mi refused to pretend the previous decision had solved everything. The practical result was still there, useful but narrow. For each thing the team knew, there were two things it had not measured. Jia Mi drew a line through the claims that depended on speculation and asked Lu Sheng to keep the original record intact. A small disagreement changed the schedule. Jia Mi wanted to proceed before evidence disappeared; Lu Sheng wanted another check because a wrong conclusion might hurt people who could not easily defend themselves. They did not settle the matter by choosing a leader. They wrote down what a short, reversible test could actually establish. Jia collected testimonies while Lu identified a labor adviser willing to review all six participants' contracts. The plan had been amended three times, and each change left a trace. Jia Mi valued those corrections more than a perfect-looking first draft. If the outcome proved disappointing, the group would still be able to identify which assumption needed repair. Lu Sheng watched for the quiet participant who had not yet been invited to speak. The summary called the situation favorable; Jia Mi asked which observation justified that description. Lu Sheng noticed the word idiom in a note that someone had added after the first inspection. A handwritten label read idol, but no one could say who had written it there.

An official version reached the room before the practical work was finished. Jia Mi read it once, then compared it with what they had actually measured. The difference was not always malicious, but it could still hurt somebody. Lu Sheng asked that the correction be written in language the affected people could understand. Lu Sheng read the consent form as a series of separate choices, not a single signature that could authorize everything forever. 實際工作還沒完成，官方版本就傳到現場。賈米讀了一次，再比對真正測得的結果。差異未必都是惡意，卻仍可能傷害人。陸聲要求把更正寫成受影響者看得懂的語言。陸聲把同意書當成一連串分開的選擇，而不是一個簽名就能永遠授權一切。官方備忘錄形容計畫「有想像力的」，賈米不願把形容詞當作證據。修訂檔案出現「影響」一詞，賈米拿原始頁面核對它的意思。陸聲注意到初次檢查後有人在筆記裡加上「反對意見」一詞。

A witness asked whether the team was taking sides. Jia Mi answered that refusing to invent a fact was not the same as refusing to care. What mattered was whether the procedure protected someone whose story was less convenient to tell. Lu Sheng listened without interrupting and wrote down the question exactly as it had been asked. 一名見證人問團隊是否偏袒。賈米回答，不捏造事實不代表冷漠。真正重要的是程序能否保護那個故事不方便被說出口的人。陸聲沒有打斷，逐字記下原問題。陸聲注意到初次檢查後有人在筆記裡加上「管弦樂隊」一詞。官方備忘錄形容計畫「便攜的」，賈米不願把形容詞當作證據。修訂檔案出現「繁榮」一詞，賈米拿原始頁面核對它的意思。

Even after the group agreed on the immediate step, another question remained. The answer would not arrive through a dramatic announcement. Someone would have to return to the place, compare the earlier notes, and accept the possibility that the next piece of evidence might spoil an attractive theory. Jia Mi prepared to do exactly that. A memory could be precious even when two people remembered it differently. Jia Mi would not let a network promise perfect love by deleting disagreement. The term protest appeared in the revised file; Jia Mi checked its meaning against the original page. On the next form, the action field read seize, but the witness had never approved the operation. A handwritten label read syllable, but no one could say who had written it there.

即使大家同意眼前步驟，另一個問題仍在。答案不會靠戲劇性公告降臨；有人得返回現場、比對舊筆記，並接受新證據可能推翻漂亮理論。賈米準備做的正是這件事。即使兩人記憶不一致，回憶仍然珍貴。賈米不允許電視台靠刪除分歧來承諾完美愛情。修訂檔案出現「抗議」一詞，賈米拿原始頁面核對它的意思。下一張表單把動作寫成「奪取」，見證人卻從未同意這項操作。手寫標籤寫著「音節」，卻沒有人知道是誰留下的。
