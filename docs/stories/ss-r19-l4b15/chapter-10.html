投票要他們分手

The Vote to Break Them Up

The show announced its highest-turnout vote: Jia and Lu must separate for a week, and one memory of choosing each other would be deleted. Something had changed, but not everybody noticed it in the same order. Jia Mi listened to the account of the nearest worker, then to someone standing farther away. Their differences were not necessarily lies. Each person had been given a different view and a different reason to be afraid of admitting uncertainty. A memory could be precious even when two people remembered it differently. Jia Mi would not let a network promise perfect love by deleting disagreement. Lu Sheng noticed the word apology in a note that someone had added after the first inspection. The earlier photograph clearly showed a bandage; the newer version did not, so Jia Mi kept both.

節目宣布最高票數決議：賈米與陸聲必須分開一週，一段兩人選擇彼此的記憶會被刪除。某件事改變了，但人們發現它的順序不同。賈米先聽近處工作人員的描述，再聽遠處旁觀者說法。差異不一定是謊言：每個人看到的角度不同，承認不確定時所要承擔的風險也不同。即使兩人記憶不一致，回憶仍然珍貴。賈米不允許電視台靠刪除分歧來承諾完美愛情。陸聲注意到初次檢查後有人在筆記裡加上「道歉」一詞。較早的照片清楚顯示一項「繃帶」，新版卻沒有，賈米因此保留兩張。

A viewers' group questioned why the show offered only harmful options when viewers had never requested them. Jia Mi refused to pretend the previous decision had solved everything. The practical result was still there, useful but narrow. For each thing the team knew, there were two things it had not measured. Jia Mi drew a line through the claims that depended on speculation and asked Lu Sheng to keep the original record intact. The term bargain appeared in the revised file; Jia Mi checked its meaning against the original page. Lu Sheng noticed the word civilization in a note that someone had added after the first inspection. Lu Sheng repeated the instruction to congratulate, then asked whether anyone had the right to refuse.

觀眾團體質疑為何節目只提供傷害性選項，觀眾根本沒要求這樣投票。賈米不願假裝前一次決定已解決所有問題。成果確實存在，卻有明確界限。每個已知事項旁都還有未測量的部分。賈米劃掉靠猜測成立的說法，請陸聲保留原始紀錄。修訂檔案出現「交易」一詞，賈米拿原始頁面核對它的意思。陸聲注意到初次檢查後有人在筆記裡加上「文明」一詞。陸聲重述「恭賀」的指示，再問當事人是否有權拒絕。

A small disagreement changed the schedule. Jia Mi wanted to proceed before evidence disappeared; Lu Sheng wanted another check because a wrong conclusion might hurt people who could not easily defend themselves. They did not settle the matter by choosing a leader. They wrote down what a short, reversible test could actually establish. An official memo described the plan as critical, but Jia Mi refused to treat an adjective as evidence. The term explosion appeared in the revised file; Jia Mi checked its meaning against the original page. On the next form, the action field read forbid, but the witness had never approved the operation.

一場小爭執改變了時程。賈米想趁證據消失前行動；陸聲要求再核對一次，因為錯誤結論可能傷害難以自辯的人。他們沒有靠選領袖解決爭議，而是列出短暫、可逆測試真正能證明什麼。官方備忘錄形容計畫「關鍵的」，賈米不願把形容詞當作證據。修訂檔案出現「爆炸」一詞，賈米拿原始頁面核對它的意思。下一張表單把動作寫成「禁止」，見證人卻從未同意這項操作。

Instead of performing a tearful farewell, the couple publicly withdrew consent and demanded the devices be switched off under observation. The plan had been amended three times, and each change left a trace. Jia Mi valued those corrections more than a perfect-looking first draft. If the outcome proved disappointing, the group would still be able to identify which assumption needed repair. Lu Sheng watched for the quiet participant who had not yet been invited to speak. On the next form, the action field read illustrate, but the witness had never approved the operation. Lu Sheng repeated the instruction to install, then asked whether anyone had the right to refuse. The summary called the situation mere; Jia Mi asked which observation justified that description.

兩人沒有表演哭著道別，而是公開撤回同意，要求在見證下關閉設備。計畫改了三次，每次修改都留下痕跡。賈米認為這些修正比看似完美的初稿更有價值。就算結果不理想，團隊仍能知道該修正哪一項假設。陸聲也注意到那名一直沒有發言機會的人。下一張表單把動作寫成「圖解說明」，見證人卻從未同意這項操作。陸聲重述「安裝」的指示，再問當事人是否有權拒絕。摘要以「僅僅的」形容情況，賈米追問這種說法有何觀察根據。

An official version reached the room before the practical work was finished. Jia Mi read it once, then compared it with what they had actually measured. The difference was not always malicious, but it could still hurt somebody. Lu Sheng asked that the correction be written in language the affected people could understand. A memory could be precious even when two people remembered it differently. Jia Mi would not let a network promise perfect love by deleting disagreement. The summary called the situation pregnant; Jia Mi asked which observation justified that description. One witness used the word prosperous; Lu Sheng noted that a description was not a measurement. A handwritten label read resolution, but no one could say who had written it there.

實際工作還沒完成，官方版本就傳到現場。賈米讀了一次，再比對真正測得的結果。差異未必都是惡意，卻仍可能傷害人。陸聲要求把更正寫成受影響者看得懂的語言。即使兩人記憶不一致，回憶仍然珍貴。賈米不允許電視台靠刪除分歧來承諾完美愛情。摘要以「懷孕的」形容情況，賈米追問這種說法有何觀察根據。一位證人用了「繁榮的」一詞，陸聲指出形容並不等於測量。手寫標籤寫著「決議」，卻沒有人知道是誰留下的。

A witness asked whether the team was taking sides. Jia Mi answered that refusing to invent a fact was not the same as refusing to care. What mattered was whether the procedure protected someone whose story was less convenient to tell. Lu Sheng listened without interrupting and wrote down the question exactly as it had been asked. A handwritten label read revolution, but no one could say who had written it there. The term settler appeared in the revised file; Jia Mi checked its meaning against the original page. Lu Sheng noticed the word surgeon in a note that someone had added after the first inspection.

一名見證人問團隊是否偏袒。賈米回答，不捏造事實不代表冷漠。真正重要的是程序能否保護那個故事不方便被說出口的人。陸聲沒有打斷，逐字記下原問題。手寫標籤寫著「革命」，卻沒有人知道是誰留下的。修訂檔案出現「遷居者」一詞，賈米拿原始頁面核對它的意思。陸聲注意到初次檢查後有人在筆記裡加上「外科醫師」一詞。

The production website carried a suspicious announcement under photographs of the six contestants: THE PUBLIC HAS CHOSEN THEIR FUTURE. Jia thought of a tourist display about an ancient tomb they had seen on their first date, how people mistook an attractive reconstruction for the life of someone who had actually lived. The same trick was being used here, except the subjects were still alive. Lu pointed to the blank consent attachment and asked the camera operator to record it before the producer could take it away. Then he and Jia faced the audience without rehearsed tears. 'You don't own what we remember,' Jia said. The vote timer fell to zero. A light blinked on their sleep devices despite both having switched them off.

製作網站在六名參賽者照片下發布可疑公告：「公眾已選擇他們的未來。」賈米想到初次約會時看過的古墓展覽，人們常把漂亮的重建模型當成死者真實人生。節目正在做相同的事，只是當事人還活著。陸聲指著同意書的空白附件，請攝影師在製作人收走前錄下來。接著兩人面對觀眾，沒有排練眼淚。「我們的回憶不是你們的財產。」賈米說。投票倒數歸零。明明兩人已關掉睡眠裝置，裝置上的燈卻又亮了。
