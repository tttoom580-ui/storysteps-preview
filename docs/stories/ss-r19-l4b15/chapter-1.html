改變早餐的投票

The Vote That Changed Breakfast

當全網投票要我們分手 · 第 1 / 25 章 · 3000–4000 · 英中對照 · 校訂中

The show's first camera followed Jia Mi into a seaside cottage where six contestants had been promised ordinary romance and a generous fee. A producer asked them to convey one secret about a partner before the first dinner. Jia laughed, then stopped when a screen beside the dining table displayed the actual audience choices: CHANGE THEIR BEDROOM, CHANGE THEIR BREAKFAST, DELETE ONE SHARED MEMORY. The last option had no explanation. Lu Sheng reached for the contract in his coat, the version he had signed because Jia wanted a break from editing other people's love stories. 'That wasn't in the presentation,' he said. The producer smiled as though uncertainty were another part of the entertainment. Outside, a drone hovered above the water, waiting for tears.

節目第一台攝影機跟著賈米走進海邊小屋。六名參賽者原本被承諾可以談一場平凡戀愛，還能領到合理酬勞。製作人要求他們晚餐前各自說出關於伴侶的一個祕密。賈米笑了，直到餐桌旁螢幕亮出真正的觀眾選項：「改變他們的房間」、「改變他們的早餐」、「刪除一段共同記憶」。最後一項沒有任何說明。陸聲伸手去拿外套裡的合約；當初他簽約，是因為賈米想暫時離開替別人的愛情故事剪接的工作。「簡報裡沒有這個。」他說。製作人卻笑得彷彿不確定也是娛樂的一部分。屋外，無人機停在海面上，等著拍眼淚。

A pink cushion sat at each place around the dinner table, as though the house had been designed to protect everyone's feelings. The producer called the show's first rule a defense against loneliness: viewers would guide six couples through choices they could not make alone. Jia had edited enough reality television to recognize the language. 'Diversity of decisions,' said the host, pointing at the screen. 'A new kind of love.' Lu asked which decisions the participants could refuse. The host glanced toward the camera operator, who pretended to adjust a microphone. Jia reached under her chair for the contract they had been given during casting. Its appendix ended halfway through a sentence. She felt the first sharp separation between the pleasant set and the thing they had agreed to do.

晚餐桌旁每個座位都有粉紅軟墊，彷彿房子被設計成能保護所有人的情緒。製作人說第一項規則是對抗孤單的防線：觀眾將引導六對戀人在無法自行決定時作出選擇。賈米剪過夠多實境節目，認得這套說詞。「選擇的多元性，」主持人指向螢幕說，「全新的愛情。」陸聲問哪些決定是參賽者可以拒絕的。主持人望向假裝調整麥克風的攝影師。賈米伸手拿出選角時拿到的合約。附件竟在一句話中間就斷了。她第一次清楚感受到，愉快布景與他們真正同意參加的事存在尖銳落差。

The first argument grew fierce when a contestant demanded to know whether the vote could change her medication schedule. The producer tried to flatter her, calling her a genius for raising a question that would create a dramatic episode. Jia noticed that he never answered it. Lu, who worked with people whose private choices had often been taken away, moved closer to the contestant but did not speak for her. 'Ask it again,' he said. She did, slowly, looking into the nearest camera. The host announced a break. The screen behind him continued displaying smiling hearts and a timer that would not stop. Jia had thought the night's mystery would concern who liked whom. Now she wanted to know who controlled the clocks.

一名參賽者追問投票能否改變她的服藥時程，第一場爭執立刻激烈起來。製作人想奉承她，稱讚她提出能製造精彩節目的天才問題。賈米注意到他始終沒有回答。陸聲長期和私人選擇被奪走的人共事，他走近那名參賽者，卻沒有替她說話。「再問一次。」他說。她放慢速度，對著最近的鏡頭又問了一遍。主持人宣布休息，背後螢幕卻仍顯示微笑愛心與停不下來的倒數。賈米原以為今晚的謎團是誰喜歡誰，現在她只想知道時鐘掌握在誰手裡。

A contestant gave a nervous giggle when the director announced the evening's first game: dropping paper hearts from a parachute on the lawn. The crew called it a trust exercise. Jia watched the little parachute descend while Lu studied the bracelet on his wrist, a wearable the staff had described as a mood monitor. He was pessimistic about the terms, but not about the other contestants. They had begun sharing what each had actually been told before signing. Some had been promised a simple dating show; others had been warned of difficult emotional tasks. None remembered agreeing to the removal of a memory. By the time the paper hearts landed, the six participants had quietly decided to exchange copies of their contracts.

導演宣布晚間第一個遊戲是用降落傘在草地上空撒下紙愛心，一名參賽者緊張地笑了聲。製作組把它叫作信任練習。賈米看著小降落傘慢慢落下，陸聲則研究腕上的手環；工作人員說那只是心情監測器。他對條款很悲觀，但還沒對其他人失望。大家開始交換簽約前真正聽到的說法：有些人被承諾單純戀愛節目，另一些人被提醒會有困難的情緒任務，卻沒有任何人記得同意刪除記憶。紙愛心落地時，六名參賽者已悄悄決定交換合約副本。

The producer liked to speak as though viewers could possess the contestants' affection. He gave a presentation about 'participatory romance' and handed each couple a reference card describing the three votes allowed that night. Jia read the last card twice. It said a shared memory could be changed for 'narrative purposes' without specifying a medical procedure, a time limit, or a right of refusal. Lu asked whether the word changed meant editing the broadcast or altering what people remembered. 'The audience won't care about the distinction,' the producer said. 'We do,' Jia answered. She laid the card beside the contract and photographed both with the camera time visible, so nobody could later claim she had misunderstood the words.

製作人說話總彷彿觀眾可以擁有參賽者的愛。他簡報「參與式戀愛」，交給每對伴侶一張參考卡，列出當晚可投的三個選項。賈米把最後一項讀了兩次：共同記憶可為「敘事目的」改變，卻沒有說明醫療程序、時間限制或拒絕權。陸聲問「改變」究竟指剪輯節目，還是改變人腦記憶。「觀眾不在乎差別。」製作人說。「我們在乎。」賈米回答。她把卡片放在合約旁，拍下相機上清晰可見的時間，以免日後有人說她誤會了條款。

The host described the evening as a spiritual journey and promised tremendous emotional growth. Jia had been a producer long enough to recognize an advertising promise disguised as a therapeutic claim. To her, the real violation was simple: people who agreed to be filmed had not agreed to surrender control over their minds. She told Lu she was considering walking out. He did not urge her to stay because the money would help them pay rent. Instead, he asked whether she wanted to tell the others before deciding. Jia looked around the table. A frightened couple was counting their remaining minutes, while the host's smile remained perfectly fixed. She stood up and asked for a meeting without cameras.

主持人把夜晚形容成精神之旅，承諾巨大的情感成長。賈米做過製作人，認得披著治療外衣的廣告話術。真正的違規很簡單：同意被拍攝的人，並沒有同意交出對自己心智的控制權。她告訴陸聲自己考慮離開。陸聲沒有因為酬勞能幫忙付房租就勸她留下，而是問她要不要先告訴其他人再決定。賈米環視餐桌，一對害怕的伴侶正在數剩下的分鐘，主持人的微笑卻像固定好的面具。她站起來，要求一場沒有攝影機的會議。

The visual display made the memory vote look harmless: pastel colors, smiling hearts, and a countdown that invited a quick decision. Jia asked the nearest witness, a camera assistant, whether anyone had tested what the button would do. He glanced toward the producer instead of answering. On the terrace, contestants finished a workout challenge designed to make them look like affectionate rivals. Lu unfolded their contract and pointed to an unnumbered attachment bearing a blank signature line. 'I don't want the audience to vote on us,' he said quietly. 'I want to know whether we can leave.' Jia looked at the locked studio gate. The screen changed before the producer could reply: VIEWER POLL OPEN. MEMORY CHOICE AVAILABLE IN FIVE MINUTES.

視覺投票畫面把記憶選項設計得像無害遊戲：粉彩色、微笑愛心，還有催人快速決定的倒數。賈米問身旁作為見證人的攝影助理，有沒有人測試過按鈕究竟會做什麼。他望向製作人，沒有回答。露臺上，參賽者剛做完一場體能訓練挑戰，節目想拍出他們既親密又競爭的樣子。陸聲展開合約，指著沒有頁碼的附件及空白簽名欄。「我不想讓觀眾替我們投票。」他低聲說，「我想知道我們能不能離開。」賈米望向上鎖的攝影棚大門。製作人還沒回答，螢幕就跳出：「觀眾投票開啟。五分鐘後可選擇記憶項目。」
