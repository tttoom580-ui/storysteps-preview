The network displayed a summary saying all contestants had approved memory editing, though no independent witness had seen the signatures. Something had changed, but not everybody noticed it in the same order. Jia Mi listened to the account of the nearest worker, then to someone standing farther away. Their differences were not necessarily lies. Each person had been given a different view and a different reason to be afraid of admitting uncertainty. Lu Sheng read the consent form as a series of separate choices, not a single signature that could authorize everything forever. One witness used the word academic; Lu Sheng noted that a description was not a measurement. A handwritten label read accountant, but no one could say who had written it there.

電視台顯示所有參賽者同意記憶編輯的摘要，卻沒有獨立見證人看過簽名。某件事改變了，但人們發現它的順序不同。賈米先聽近處工作人員的描述，再聽遠處旁觀者說法。差異不一定是謊言：每個人看到的角度不同，承認不確定時所要承擔的風險也不同。陸聲把同意書當成一連串分開的選擇，而不是一個簽名就能永遠授權一切。一位證人用了「學術的」一詞，陸聲指出形容並不等於測量。手寫標籤寫著「會計師」，卻沒有人知道是誰留下的。

The couple disabled their own devices, but the producer threatened to classify that action as a contract violation. Jia Mi refused to pretend the previous decision had solved everything. The practical result was still there, useful but narrow. For each thing the team knew, there were two things it had not measured. Jia Mi drew a line through the claims that depended on speculation and asked Lu Sheng to keep the original record intact. The term admiration appeared in the revised file; Jia Mi checked its meaning against the original page. On the next form, the action field read annoy, but the witness had never approved the operation. The earlier photograph clearly showed a aquarium; the newer version did not, so Jia Mi kept both.

兩人關掉自己的裝置，製作人卻威脅把這認定為違約。賈米不願假裝前一次決定已解決所有問題。成果確實存在，卻有明確界限。每個已知事項旁都還有未測量的部分。賈米劃掉靠猜測成立的說法，請陸聲保留原始紀錄。修訂檔案出現「欣賞」一詞，賈米拿原始頁面核對它的意思。下一張表單把動作寫成「使困擾」，見證人卻從未同意這項操作。較早的照片清楚顯示一項「水族館」，新版卻沒有，賈米因此保留兩張。

A small disagreement changed the schedule. Jia Mi wanted to proceed before evidence disappeared; Lu Sheng wanted another check because a wrong conclusion might hurt people who could not easily defend themselves. They did not settle the matter by choosing a leader. They wrote down what a short, reversible test could actually establish. The earlier photograph clearly showed a basin; the newer version did not, so Jia Mi kept both. The term characteristic appeared in the revised file; Jia Mi checked its meaning against the original page. Lu Sheng noticed the word chemistry in a note that someone had added after the first inspection.

一場小爭執改變了時程。賈米想趁證據消失前行動；陸聲要求再核對一次，因為錯誤結論可能傷害難以自辯的人。他們沒有靠選領袖解決爭議，而是列出短暫、可逆測試真正能證明什麼。較早的照片清楚顯示一項「水池」，新版卻沒有，賈米因此保留兩張。修訂檔案出現「特徵」一詞，賈米拿原始頁面核對它的意思。陸聲注意到初次檢查後有人在筆記裡加上「化學反應」一詞。

One contestant's signature appeared on a page she had never received, revealing a potential forged attachment. The plan had been amended three times, and each change left a trace. Jia Mi valued those corrections more than a perfect-looking first draft. If the outcome proved disappointing, the group would still be able to identify which assumption needed repair. Lu Sheng watched for the quiet participant who had not yet been invited to speak. Lu Sheng noticed the word contribution in a note that someone had added after the first inspection. A handwritten label read defeat, but no one could say who had written it there. The term expansion appeared in the revised file; Jia Mi checked its meaning against the original page.

一名參賽者的簽名出現在沒見過的附件上，顯示可能有偽造文件。計畫改了三次，每次修改都留下痕跡。賈米認為這些修正比看似完美的初稿更有價值。就算結果不理想，團隊仍能知道該修正哪一項假設。陸聲也注意到那名一直沒有發言機會的人。陸聲注意到初次檢查後有人在筆記裡加上「捐贈」一詞。手寫標籤寫著「擊敗」，卻沒有人知道是誰留下的。修訂檔案出現「擴張」一詞，賈米拿原始頁面核對它的意思。

An official version reached the room before the practical work was finished. Jia Mi read it once, then compared it with what they had actually measured. The difference was not always malicious, but it could still hurt somebody. Lu Sheng asked that the correction be written in language the affected people could understand. Lu Sheng read the consent form as a series of separate choices, not a single signature that could authorize everything forever. The summary called the situation fluent; Jia Mi asked which observation justified that description. One witness used the word fortunate; Lu Sheng noted that a description was not a measurement. Lu Sheng repeated the instruction to motivate, then asked whether anyone had the right to refuse.

實際工作還沒完成，官方版本就傳到現場。賈米讀了一次，再比對真正測得的結果。差異未必都是惡意，卻仍可能傷害人。陸聲要求把更正寫成受影響者看得懂的語言。陸聲把同意書當成一連串分開的選擇，而不是一個簽名就能永遠授權一切。摘要以「流暢的」形容情況，賈米追問這種說法有何觀察根據。一位證人用了「幸運的」一詞，陸聲指出形容並不等於測量。陸聲重述「激勵；促使」的指示，再問當事人是否有權拒絕。

A witness asked whether the team was taking sides. Jia Mi answered that refusing to invent a fact was not the same as refusing to care. What mattered was whether the procedure protected someone whose story was less convenient to tell. Lu Sheng listened without interrupting and wrote down the question exactly as it had been asked. A handwritten label read prime, but no one could say who had written it there. The summary called the situation relevant; Jia Mi asked which observation justified that description. Lu Sheng noticed the word rhythm in a note that someone had added after the first inspection.

一名見證人問團隊是否偏袒。賈米回答，不捏造事實不代表冷漠。真正重要的是程序能否保護那個故事不方便被說出口的人。陸聲沒有打斷，逐字記下原問題。手寫標籤寫著「主開關」，卻沒有人知道是誰留下的。摘要以「相關的」形容情況，賈米追問這種說法有何觀察根據。陸聲注意到初次檢查後有人在筆記裡加上「節奏」一詞。

Even after the group agreed on the immediate step, another question remained. The answer would not arrive through a dramatic announcement. Someone would have to return to the place, compare the earlier notes, and accept the possibility that the next piece of evidence might spoil an attractive theory. Jia Mi prepared to do exactly that. A memory could be precious even when two people remembered it differently. Jia Mi would not let a network promise perfect love by deleting disagreement. Lu Sheng noticed the word shade in a note that someone had added after the first inspection. Lu Sheng repeated the instruction to strive, then asked whether anyone had the right to refuse. The instructions used the verb waken without naming who would carry the risk; Jia Mi circled that gap.

即使大家同意眼前步驟，另一個問題仍在。答案不會靠戲劇性公告降臨；有人得返回現場、比對舊筆記，並接受新證據可能推翻漂亮理論。賈米準備做的正是這件事。即使兩人記憶不一致，回憶仍然珍貴。賈米不允許電視台靠刪除分歧來承諾完美愛情。陸聲注意到初次檢查後有人在筆記裡加上「陰影」一詞。陸聲重述「努力」的指示，再問當事人是否有權拒絕。指示裡用了「喚醒」這個動詞，卻沒說誰承擔風險；賈米圈出這項缺漏。
