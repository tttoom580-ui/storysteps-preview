空掉的綠色架子

The Empty Green Shelf

At seven a.m., Ivy came early to the school garden. The sun was just above the roof. She ate fruit and a small cake for breakfast, then looked at the green shelf near the door. Yesterday, a heavy green box had stood there. It held seeds, signs, and a little book for the garden club. Now the shelf was empty. The bell rang for the first class, but Ivy did not move. Their garden day was only four weeks away. Without the box, they could not begin.

上午七點，艾薇提早來到學校花園。太陽剛升到屋頂上方。她早餐吃了水果和一塊小蛋糕，接著望向門邊的綠色架子。昨天，一個沉重的綠箱子還放在上面。裡頭有種子、標示牌和園藝社的小本子。如今架子空了。第一堂課的鐘響了，艾薇卻沒有移動。距離花園開放日只剩四週。沒有箱子，大家便無法開始。

There was one more reason Ivy cared about the box. Each student in the club had written one small message about a plant. A boy wanted to grow beans for his grandmother. A girl wanted flowers that would bring bees to the garden. Ivy had written that she wanted a place where a quiet student could ask a question without being laughed at. All those messages were in the notebook inside the box. Losing the box meant losing part of their promise to one another. She did not want to give up that promise simply because the search looked hard.

艾薇在意那個箱子，還有另一個原因。園藝社每位學生都寫過一段關於植物的小心願。一個男孩想替奶奶種豆子，一個女孩想種能吸引蜜蜂的花。艾薇寫的是：希望有個地方，讓安靜的學生能發問，而不會被取笑。所有訊息都在箱子裡的筆記本上。失去箱子，也像失去了大家對彼此的一部分承諾。艾薇不想只因為尋找很困難，就放棄那個承諾。

Jun arrived with a cover for the garden table. He was Ivy's friend, and fixing small things was his hobby. He had once painted a monkey with one large tooth on the side of the missing box, so everyone could tell which box belonged to the club. He still kept a small copy of his funny drawing in his notebook. “Shall we look behind the couch in the club room?” he asked. They looked, but the box was not there. Ivy asked him to help without moving anything else. Someone might need the room later.

俊帶著花園桌子的罩布來了。他是艾薇的朋友，修理小東西是他的興趣。失蹤箱子的側面原本畫著一隻露出大牙齒的猴子，那是俊親手畫的，好讓大家認出園藝社的箱子。他的筆記本裡還留著一張這幅逗趣圖畫的小副本。「我們要不要看看社團教室的沙發後面？」他問。他們找了，箱子卻不在那裡。艾薇請他幫忙時不要移動其他東西，因為稍後可能有人要使用教室。

Ms. Wen, their teacher, met them in the hall. She wanted to teach the club how to care for plants and how to care for people. “Searching is not a race,” she said. “First, write down what you know.” Ivy took out a notebook. She wrote: green box; seed cards; monkey picture; last seen on the shelf yesterday. She did not write that anyone had stolen it. Jun nodded. He said that making a fair list was better exercise for his mind than running from room to room.

社團老師溫老師在走廊遇見他們。她想教社團成員照顧植物，也學習照顧人。「找東西不是比賽，」她說，「先寫下你們確定的事。」艾薇拿出筆記本，寫上：綠箱子、種子卡、猴子圖畫、昨天最後一次看見的位置。她沒有寫任何人偷走了箱子。俊點點頭，說列出公平的清單，比在一個個房間之間奔跑更能訓練他的腦袋。

That day Ivy made a promise to herself as well. She would tell the truth about what she saw, and she would not make a person feel bad just because a thing was missing. The teacher helped her put a large sheet of paper on the wall. Children could draw what they remembered about the box, but they were not to write another person's name as a guess. When another class passed through the hall, several students stopped to add pictures of seeds and small leaves. The wall began to look like a garden before the real garden was ready.

那天艾薇也對自己許下承諾。她會誠實說出看見的事，不會只因東西不見，就讓某個人難受。老師幫她在牆上貼一張大紙。孩子可以畫出記得的箱子模樣，但不能把猜測對象的名字寫上去。後來另一個班級經過走廊時，好幾位學生停下來，畫了種子和小葉子。真正的花園還沒準備好，牆上卻已經有了花園的樣子。

At lunch the club shared salad under a tree. One new student came from a foreign country and asked what the green box looked like. Ivy explained slowly, and Jun showed him the copy of his monkey drawing. The student said he had seen a green shape near the school gate during the rain, but he was not sure it was their box. Ivy thanked him. A guess could be useful, she thought, as long as people did not turn it into a fact too soon. She added a question mark to her notes.

午餐時，社團成員在樹下分吃沙拉。一位來自國外的新同學問綠箱子長什麼樣。艾薇慢慢說明，俊則拿出筆記本裡那張猴子圖畫的副本給他看。新同學說，下雨時曾在校門附近看見一個綠色的東西，但不確定是不是他們的箱子。艾薇向他道謝。她想，猜測也許有用，但不能太快把它當成事實。她在筆記上加了一個問號。

In the afternoon they found a wet piece of paper under the shelf. Only the words “keep dry” could be read. Ivy put the paper between two clean pages without trying to pull it apart. Jun wanted to ask every student in the school, but Ms. Wen suggested a smaller first step. They would speak to the people who used the garden yesterday. Ivy closed her book. She still wanted the green box back, yet she also wanted everyone to feel safe when they answered her questions.

下午，他們在架子底下找到一小片濕紙。上面只看得清「保持乾燥」幾個字。艾薇把紙片夾在兩張乾淨的紙中間，沒有硬把它攤開。俊想詢問全校每一個學生，溫老師卻建議先從小範圍開始。他們要先問昨天使用花園的人。艾薇闔上筆記本。她仍想找回綠箱子，但也希望每個回答問題的人都能安心。
