花園開門的日子

The Garden Opens

雨後的綠色箱子 · 第 25 / 25 章 · 1000以下 · 英中對照

The rain stopped in the morning. Ivy could see the school garden from the gate, and the sight made her smile. Its new path was clean, the green box was safe, and small leaves had begun to grow in every bed. Jun had cut paper signs the night before, and they looked bright against the wet earth. A worker pushed a cart of chairs to the side, away from the path. “Now we are ready,” Ms. Wen said. Ivy felt lucky, but she knew the day had not come together by luck alone. Many people had made a plan and followed it.

早晨雨停了。艾薇從校門看見學校花園，那景象讓她微笑。新小徑乾淨，綠箱子安全，每塊花圃也都開始長出小葉子。俊前一晚剪好的紙標示，在潮濕的土地旁看起來格外鮮明。一位工人推著裝椅子的手推車到旁邊，沒有擋住通道。「現在準備好了，」溫老師說。艾薇覺得自己很幸運，但她知道今天不是只靠運氣才實現。許多人一起訂計畫，還認真照著做。

Before the first visitors arrived, Ivy helped prepare the tables. The rain had left the ground dirty in one corner, so a worker put a small sign there and asked visitors to use the clean path. Jun wanted to push the last table closer to the flowers. Kai explained that it would make the turn too narrow. Jun listened and moved it back. Ivy looked at the two friends and felt proud. They did not need a long argument to make a good choice. They could ask, listen, and change a small part of the plan.

第一批訪客到來前，艾薇幫忙準備桌子。雨讓角落的一塊地面變髒，工人就在那裡放了小牌子，請訪客走乾淨的小徑。俊想把最後一張桌子推近花圃。凱解釋，那樣會讓轉彎處太窄。俊聽了，把桌子移回原位。艾薇看著兩位朋友，感到自豪。他們不需要長時間爭論，也能做出好決定，只要提出問題、聽懂彼此，再調整計畫中的一小部分。

The garden party began with a short play from the school theater club. A child wore a paper crown and pretended to be a king who did not know how roots worked. The other children showed him a real root in a clear jar of water. The play was fun and a little noisy, but it helped the visitors understand the plants. Ivy watched a man from the town office laugh with the children. The school had invited him to see the new path. He said the workers had done careful work, and Kai agreed after trying the final turn.

花園慶祝活動從學校劇團的一齣短劇開始。一位孩子戴著紙皇冠，扮演不懂植物根部如何生長的國王。其他孩子拿出一個裝水的透明罐，讓他看真正的根。短劇好玩又有點吵，卻幫訪客理解植物。艾薇看到一位鎮公所的男子和孩子們一起笑。學校邀請他來看新小徑。他稱讚工人做得細心，凱試走最後一個轉彎後，也表示同意。

At one table, the club held a small sale of handmade plant cards. The money would buy seeds for next year. There were no hidden prices, and every card had a clear sign. Ivy's aunt came from the airport after visiting a relative and stopped by to see the garden. She did not need to catch an early flight again until next month. She bought a card, then told Ivy that working carefully was more important than being anyone's boss. Ivy liked that. She wanted to help, not give orders for the joy of hearing people obey.

一張桌上，社團舉辦了手作植物卡片的小義賣，所得要買明年的種子。價錢都公開清楚，每張卡片也有明確標示。艾薇的姑姑探訪親戚後從機場回來，順道來看花園。她到下個月才需要再趕早班飛機。姑姑買了一張卡片，接著告訴艾薇，細心工作比當任何人的老闆都重要。艾薇很喜歡這句話。她想幫助別人，而不是為了聽人服從才發號施令。

When the first family walked into the garden, a child asked which plant Ivy liked most. She could not choose only one. She liked the beans because they grew while everyone waited for the box. She liked the little flowers because they brought people together. Most of all, she liked the path, which let more friends come in and make their own choices. She pointed to the green box on its safe shelf. That box had once seemed like the end of their work. Today it was only one part of a garden that would keep growing.

第一個家庭走進花園時，一個孩子問艾薇最喜歡哪種植物。她無法只選一種。她喜歡豆子，因為大家等箱子的時候，豆苗仍慢慢生長；她喜歡小花，因為小花讓人聚在一起；她最喜歡的其實是小徑，因為它讓更多朋友能走進來，做出自己的選擇。她指向安全放在架上的綠箱子。那個箱子曾讓她以為所有工作都結束了，今天卻只是會繼續生長的花園裡的一部分。

Kai stood beside the plant signs and showed visitors how he liked to use them. Some people wanted to look, some wanted to touch, and some wanted a friend to read aloud. Ivy asked whether he would like to speak at the end of the party. “Yes,” he said, “but I will say what I think, not what you tell me to say.” Ivy laughed and agreed. When his turn came, he thanked the club for listening and said there was still more they could improve next year. The visitors clapped. A good garden could keep changing.

凱站在植物標示旁，向訪客示範自己喜歡怎麼使用它們。有些人想用眼睛看，有些人想觸摸，也有人希望朋友念給自己聽。艾薇問他是否願意在活動尾聲發言。「願意，」他說，「但我要說自己的想法，不是照你叫我說的話。」艾薇笑著答應。輪到凱時，他感謝社團願意聆聽，也說明年還有能改善的地方。訪客們鼓掌。好的花園可以一直變得更好。

When the party ended, Ivy carried the green box back to its shelf with Jun. They checked the name on the top and bottom, then wrote down where they had put it. The monkey with orange ears faced the room. Ivy remembered the day she had first seen the empty shelf. She had felt that everything was lost. Now she knew how many small good things could grow from a hard question. “Shall we come back tomorrow?” Jun asked. Ivy smiled. “Yes. Plants need care after the party too.” Together they closed the door and went home under the evening sun.

慶祝活動結束後，艾薇和俊把綠箱子搬回架上。他們核對箱子上面和底部的名字，再記下存放位置。橘耳朵的猴子朝向房間。艾薇想起最初看見空架子的那天，當時她覺得一切都失去了。現在她知道，一個困難的問題也能長出許多小小的好事。「明天還要回來嗎？」俊問。艾薇微笑。「要啊，活動結束以後，植物還是需要照顧。」兩人一起關上門，在傍晚的陽光下回家。
