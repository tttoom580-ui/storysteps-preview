好多箱子的屋子

The House of Many Boxes

On the weekend, Ivy went with her mother to her uncle Dan's house. He lived near the river and had a small boat, which he used only when the weather was good. Before they arrived, he put his coffee away from a table of tools. “Finally, my favorite visitors,” he said, waving from the door. Ivy smiled but told him about the terrible feeling of seeing the empty green shelf. Her uncle listened without laughing. “A missing box does not mean someone did a stupid thing,” he said. “There are many reasons people move things.”

週末，艾薇跟媽媽到丹叔叔家。他住在河邊，擁有一艘小船，只在天氣好的時候使用。她們抵達前，他先把咖啡移離工具桌。「終於等到我最喜歡的客人了！」他在門口揮手。艾薇笑了，卻也告訴他看見綠架子空了時有多難受。叔叔認真聽，沒有取笑她。「箱子不見，不代表一定有人做了蠢事，」他說，「人們移動東西有很多原因。」

When Ivy and her mother arrived at the house, a note on the door said, “Please arrive before lunch.” Uncle Dan had just come back from a short drive. He said he would not drive his boat anywhere; boats were for water, not the road, and people needed training before using them. Ivy laughed at the joke. Everyone in the family was there except her older cousin, who had a school game. From the kitchen window they could see neighbors wave to one another across the street. Ivy felt peaceful. A quiet visit gave her a chance to think about the lost box without feeling that she had to solve everything at once.

艾薇和媽媽抵達時，門上有張紙條寫著：「請在午餐前抵達。」丹叔叔剛短程開車回家。他開玩笑說自己不會把船開到馬路上；船應該在水裡走，而且操作船隻前需要訓練。艾薇笑了。家人都在，除了有學校比賽的年長表哥。從廚房窗外，能看見街對面的鄰居互相揮手。艾薇感到平靜。安靜的探訪，讓她能不急著一次解決所有問題，慢慢想想失蹤的箱子。

Uncle Dan repaired boxes for a living. He showed Ivy a large box with a picture of a lion on its top. A red sign told workers to keep it dry. “See this shape?” he asked. The box had a small cut beside one handle. “When we move a heavy box, we check its shape and its marks before we decide it is the right one.” Ivy remembered the monkey on the garden box. She could name that mark exactly. The word green alone was not enough. Many school boxes were green.

丹叔叔以修理箱子為工作。他拿出一個大箱子，上面畫著獅子，紅色標示牌提醒工人保持乾燥。「看到這個形狀嗎？」他問。箱子一側的把手旁有道小切口。「搬重箱子時，要先確認形狀和記號，才能確定是不是對的。」艾薇想起園藝箱上的猴子。那個記號她能清楚描述，但只有「綠色」兩個字還不夠，因為學校有很多綠箱子。

Ivy asked whether a box could travel on a boat by mistake. Her uncle said it was possible, but they had no reason to think this had happened. He drove no school truck, and his boat had not left the house that week. “Do not follow an exciting idea just because it sounds like a good story,” he said. Ivy laughed. She had imagined her seeds crossing the river under a high blue sky. It was a funny picture, not a clue. She drew a line through that guess in her notebook.

艾薇問箱子會不會不小心被運上船。叔叔說雖然有可能，但沒有理由認為事情真的如此。他沒有開學校的貨車，那星期小船也沒有離開住處。「不要只因一個想法聽起來像好故事，就追著它跑，」他說。艾薇笑了。她剛才還想像種子在藍天之下渡河。那是有趣的畫面，不是線索。她在筆記本中劃掉這個猜測。

Uncle Dan took them to the room where he kept his work notes. Every box repair had a date and a drawing. “People may forget a color or a number,” he said, “but a clear note can help the next person.” Ivy asked whether she could make the same kind of page for the garden. He gave her a blank sheet and a pencil. She drew slowly, checked the monkey's ears, and marked the place for the school name. When she finished, she felt ready to ask better questions on Monday.

丹叔叔帶她們到存放工作紀錄的房間。每次箱子維修都有日期和圖畫。「人可能忘記顏色或數字，」他說，「但清楚的紀錄能幫助下一個人。」艾薇問自己是否也能替花園做一張類似的表。他給她一張白紙和鉛筆。她慢慢畫，確認猴子的耳朵，再標出寫校名的位置。完成時，她覺得星期一能提出更好的問題了。

After lunch Uncle Dan gave Ivy a free sheet of strong paper. It showed how to draw a box from the front, back, and side. Ivy used it to make a clear picture of the garden box. She showed where the lid opened and where Jun had painted the monkey. Her mother said the new drawing would help adults look without asking children to enter work rooms. Ivy folded the paper and put it in her bag. She had not found the box, but she had found a better way to ask about it.

午餐後，丹叔叔免費送艾薇一張厚紙，上面教人從正面、背面和側面畫出箱子。艾薇照著畫出園藝箱的清楚圖像，標明蓋子從哪裡開，還有俊畫猴子的位置。媽媽說這張新圖能幫大人尋找，孩子便不必進入施工房間。艾薇把紙折好放進袋子。她沒有找到箱子，卻找到了更好的詢問方式。

On Sunday evening she called Jun. He had spent the day helping his family clean the kitchen and had almost forgotten the garden problem. Ivy said that was fine. A club could not ask friends to work all the time. She told him about the drawing and asked whether he could check the monkey's exact color. “Orange ears and one white tooth,” Jun said at once. Ivy wrote the answer. On Monday they would bring the picture to Ms. Wen and ask which storage rooms were safe for adults to check.

星期日晚間，艾薇打電話給俊。他那天都在幫家人打掃廚房，幾乎忘了花園的問題。艾薇說沒關係，社團不能要求朋友一直工作。她告訴俊箱子的圖畫，請他確認猴子的顏色。「橘色耳朵，還有一顆白牙齒！」俊立刻回答。艾薇記下答案。星期一，他們會把圖帶給溫老師，詢問哪些儲藏室可以請大人安全地查看。
