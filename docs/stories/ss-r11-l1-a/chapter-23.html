A Place for Every Box

雨後的綠色箱子 · 第 23 / 25 章 · 1000以下 · 英中對照

The school office held a short meeting about keeping things safe. Ms. Wen said the green box belonged to the garden club, but the school still needed a clear place for it. “Hello, everyone,” said the secretary as people came into the room. She put the box on a low table and showed its name on the bottom. A worker brought a new label and a small shelf. “Which room should this go in?” he asked. Ivy said the club wanted a room that students could use with permission, not a room where boxes could never be found again.

學校辦公室開了場短會，討論如何安全保管物品。溫老師說綠箱子屬於園藝社，但學校仍需替它安排清楚的位置。「大家好，」秘書迎接進房間的人。她把箱子放在矮桌上，展示底部的名稱。一位工人帶來新標籤和小架子。「這應該放在哪個房間？」他問。艾薇說，社團希望放在學生經許可後能使用的房間，而不是一個從此再也找不到箱子的地方。

Mr. Reed came into the office with his wife, who had helped choose plants for the garden. “The new shelf will belong to the club,” he said, “but we all need to keep the way to it clear. Please come to the shelf with a teacher.” His wife smiled and asked whether the children knew how to put things back after using them. Jun showed her the picture guide. He spoke in a polite voice and waited for her answer. She said the guide was easy to follow and thanked the club for making it. Ivy felt glad that a clear rule could feel welcoming instead of strict.

里德先生和妻子走進辦公室。里德太太曾幫忙為花園挑選植物。「新架子屬於社團，」里德先生說，「但大家都要讓通往架子的道路保持暢通。請和老師一起來架子這裡。」他的妻子微笑，問孩子們知不知道用品用完後該怎麼放回去。俊展示圖畫指南，禮貌地說明，然後等她回答。她說指南很容易照做，還感謝社團的用心。艾薇很高興，清楚的規則也能讓人感到受歡迎，而不是只覺得嚴厲。

The new system was simple. Each club would write its name on the top and bottom of a box. When a worker moved it, the office would note where it went. The list would give the time and the name of the room, but not any private information about children. Jun said that sounded fine. A boy joked that there would be a crazy number of labels around school. Everyone laughed. Ms. Wen said they should never need a large list if a small one could do the job clearly.

新制度很簡單。每個社團都要在箱子的上面和底部寫上名稱。工人移動箱子時，辦公室便記下它去了哪裡。紀錄會寫時間和房間名稱，但不會包含孩子的私人資料。俊說這樣很好。一個男孩開玩笑說，學校要變成到處都是標籤的瘋狂世界了，大家都笑了。溫老師說，如果一張小清單已能清楚記錄，就沒有必要做一張很大的清單。

At lunch the garden club shared pie and tea. An older woman named Mrs. Reed visited with her husband, the school gardener. She had brought a pot for a new flower and gave her husband a quick kiss on the cheek before he went back to work. A child laughed softly, but no one was unkind. Ivy asked Mrs. Reed whether the pot would fit on the new shelf. She said the pot should stay near the window instead. A little space around a living plant was more important than making every shelf look full.

午餐時，園藝社一起吃派、喝茶。一位叫里德太太的年長女士，和她的丈夫——學校園丁——一起來訪。她帶來一個新花盆，在丈夫回去工作前輕吻他的臉頰。一個孩子輕聲笑了，但沒有人失禮。艾薇問里德太太那個花盆是否能放上新架子。她說花盆應該留在窗邊。讓活植物周圍有一點空間，比把每層架子塞滿更重要。

Before the room closed, Ms. Wen asked the group to write a small welcome message for the next students. Ivy suggested, “If you cannot find something, ask.” Kai suggested, “If a place is hard to use, tell us.” Jun added, “If you have a new idea, we want to hear it.” They wrote all three. The message did not promise that nothing would ever go wrong. It promised that nobody would have to solve every problem alone. Ivy liked that better than a perfect-looking rule book.

房間關閉前，溫老師請社團替下一屆學生寫一小段歡迎訊息。艾薇提議：「找不到東西時，請提出來。」凱提議：「如果有地方不好使用，請告訴我們。」俊補充：「有新想法，我們想聽。」大家把三句都寫上去。這段話沒有保證永遠不出錯，而是承諾沒有人需要獨自解決所有問題。艾薇覺得，這比一本看似完美的規則手冊更好。

The teacher asked who would show new students how to use the shelf. Ivy said the group could take turns. “I do not want the garden to depend on one person,” she explained. “If I am away, someone else should understand where things go.” Jun offered to draw the shelf on a simple page. Kai asked for a clear spoken guide as well. Ivy said yes. One child had a sore throat and could not speak much that day, so the club let him help by checking labels quietly instead.

老師問誰要教新同學使用架子。艾薇說，大家可以輪流。「我不希望花園只依靠一個人，」她解釋，「如果我不在，別人也應該知道東西要放哪裡。」俊自願畫出架子的位置圖。凱也希望有清楚的口頭說明。艾薇答應了。一位孩子喉嚨痛，當天不太能說話，社團便讓他安靜地檢查標籤，不勉強他發言。

Before leaving, Ivy watched rain from a short shower run down the outside window. The green box sat dry on its new shelf. She never wanted to find it lost again, but she also knew accidents could happen. The important part was having a way to notice, ask, and correct a mistake. She looked at Jun and said, “This is a better home for our box.” “Yes,” he answered. The monkey with orange ears smiled from the shelf, ready for the next group of gardeners.

離開前，艾薇看著短暫陣雨的水沿窗外流下。綠箱子乾乾地坐在新架子上。她再也不想看見它失蹤，但也明白意外仍可能發生。重要的是有一種方法能發現問題、提出問題，並改正錯誤。她看看俊說：「這是箱子更好的家。」「對，」俊回答。橘耳朵的猴子從架上對大家微笑，準備迎接下一批園藝社成員。
