The False Accusation

A wallet vanished during a crowded special-service night, and a young ticket inspector accused a teenager who had been standing nearby. Cheng insisted that the train's security procedure be followed rather than allowing the crowd to invent a culprit. Gu Yu stayed beside the student but did not speak in his place. A classroom poster could animate a discussion of fairness without turning real passengers into examples. The inspector's arithmetic counted a twenty-minute safeguarding delay as a simple performance failure. One teenager called the recovered wallet awesome news, then apologized for his nervous joke. A lunch container held celery sticks, which the wrongly accused student offered to share.

特別加班車擁擠的夜裡，一只錢包不見了，年輕驗票員指控站在附近的少年。方澄堅持依列車安全程序處理，不讓人群自行創造犯人。顧嶼留在學生旁邊，卻不替他說話。教室海報可以活化公平討論，卻不應把真實乘客當成教材。驗票員的算術把二十分鐘的保護程序延誤，簡化成績效失敗。一名少年說找回錢包真是太棒了，隨後為緊張時的玩笑道歉。午餐盒裡裝著芹菜條，遭錯誤指控的學生還邀大家分享。

The incident delayed departure, exposing another problem in the agency's performance targets. Managers punished trains for being late even when staff complied with safeguarding rules. Cheng showed how an apparently simple punctuality measure could pressure workers into ignoring complaints. Fresh cement near the entrance had forced passengers through the crowded rear door. The regional confederation of unions asked for safeguards that protected both workers and passengers. A public crackdown on theft would be meaningless if officers ignored the difference between evidence and suspicion. A crocodile mascot on the child's backpack became a detail in the first witness account.

事件耽誤發車，也揭露機關績效目標的另一個問題。即使員工遵守保護程序，只要列車誤點，主管仍會處罰。方澄指出，看似單純的準點率會如何逼迫人忽略申訴。入口附近新鋪的水泥，迫使乘客擠向後門。區域工會聯盟要求同時保護員工與乘客的防護措施。如果人員不區分證據與懷疑，公開掃蕩竊盜便毫無意義。孩子背包上的鱷魚吉祥物，成了第一份目擊陳述的細節。

Security footage later showed the wallet had slipped between two seats before the teenager entered the carriage. The inspector apologized directly and submitted a correction. Cheng required the written record to distinguish a mistaken suspicion from an established theft. The railway needed dual measures: punctuality and proper treatment of safety complaints. Cheng asked the committee to equalize standards for people using ordinary and institutional passes. A political faction tried to turn the misunderstanding into proof that night trains encouraged crime. The agency would incur costs for proper review, but avoiding review could cost far more. The teenager asked whether the correction would reach the office that held his name. An apology in the carriage would not help if an accusation survived elsewhere. Cheng asked security to identify every copy of the incident form and record where the amendment was sent. Gu Yu sat down again when the student asked to read the wording himself. This time nobody filled the pause with a guess about what he needed.

後來監視畫面顯示，錢包在少年上車前就滑進兩個座位之間。驗票員直接道歉並提交更正。方澄要求書面紀錄明確區分錯誤懷疑與已證實的竊盜。鐵路需要雙重指標：準點率與安全申訴的妥善處理。方澄請委員會使一般票與機構通行證乘客適用相同標準。一個政治派系試圖把誤會說成夜車助長犯罪的證據。機關進行正式審查必須承擔費用，但逃避審查可能代價更高。少年問，更正是否會送到保存他姓名的辦公室。如果指控還留在別處，車廂裡的道歉就幫不上忙。方澄請安全人員找出事件表的每份副本，記下更正送往何處。學生要求自己讀內容時，顧嶼重新坐下。這次沒有人用猜測他需要什麼來填滿沉默。

Gu Yu was upset by the accusation, while Cheng was more worried about the system that rewarded quick answers. They argued briefly in the empty carriage, then returned to the shared facts. Neither tried to win the disagreement by questioning the other's character. The student said the greater injustice was being treated as guilty before anyone checked the seats. Cheng disliked the intrigue surrounding the missing wallet, especially the anonymous messages blaming outsiders. A reporter tried to lure the teenager into an interview by promising instant public sympathy. The inspector was obstinate at first, but changed his statement when the footage became available.

顧嶼對指控感到憤怒，方澄更憂心獎勵快速結案的制度。兩人在空車廂裡短暫爭論，之後回到共同事實。他們都沒有靠質疑對方的人格來贏得爭執。學生說，更大的不公是在有人檢查座位前，他就被當成有罪。方澄厭惡失錢包事件周圍的陰謀傳言，尤其是匿名訊息責怪外地人。一名記者以立即獲得公眾同情為誘餌，想引誘少年受訪。驗票員起初很固執，看到畫面後才更正說法。

At the tenth-day review, the committee accepted a revised incident measure alongside punctuality statistics. It was a modest improvement, not a decision to save the railway. Cheng went home tired but relieved. The first phase of the inquiry had created better questions, and better questions had already prevented one injustice. Security officials found no pickpocket; the wallet had simply slipped into the seat gap. Cheng still wanted the train to be punctual, but never at the cost of a rushed accusation. The teenager showed remarkable restraint when the inspector came to apologize. A passenger began to stagger when the delayed crowd surged, prompting a careful pause before departure.

第十天檢討時，委員會同意把修正後的事件指標與準點率並列。這只是小小進展，並非決議保留鐵路。方澄疲倦卻鬆了一口氣：調查第一階段提出了更好的問題，而那些問題已經阻止一件不公。安全人員沒有找到扒手；錢包只是滑進座椅縫隙。方澄仍希望列車準點，卻不願以倉促指控為代價。驗票員前來道歉時，少年展現令人欽佩的克制。延誤人潮湧動時，一名乘客踉蹌，促使列車在發車前謹慎暫停。
