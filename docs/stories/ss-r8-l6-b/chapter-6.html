A Trial Beneath the Eclipse

The committee granted a fourteen-day trial with stricter lighting and safety checks. Cheng welcomed the limited approval, although some supporters called it a victory. A trial could demonstrate a working solution or expose its failure; her job was to make both outcomes visible. Cheng wanted an airtight explanation of the costs, not a slogan that promised savings without evidence. The committee would attain better information only by comparing ordinary nights with the eclipse event. A visiting congresswoman attended the trial but refused to announce a decision before reading the results. Gu Yu brought vegetable curry to the station for volunteers who had missed dinner.

委員會核准十四天試行，附加更嚴格的照明與安全檢查。方澄歡迎這項有限批准，卻不認為這是支持者宣稱的勝利。試行可能證明方案有效，也可能揭露失敗；她的工作是讓兩種結果都被看見。方澄要的是嚴密的成本說明，不是毫無證據的節省口號。委員會只有把平常夜晚與月蝕活動相比，才能獲得更好的資訊。一名到訪的女國會議員參加試行，卻拒絕在閱讀結果前宣布立場。顧嶼帶蔬菜咖哩到車站，給錯過晚餐的志工。

Gu Yu organized evening students to observe pedestrian access without touching railway equipment. Each volunteer received a precise task, emergency contact, and the right to withdraw. Cheng appreciated that he made participation voluntary rather than using his students to make the project appear popular. Cheng did not despise angry critics; she asked which harm they believed the pilot concealed. During the eclipse, the platform remained properly lit even when the sky darkened. Only edible snacks were offered in the volunteer area, with allergens clearly labeled. A driver's professional esteem grew when passengers publicly thanked her for refusing an unsafe departure.

顧嶼安排夜校學生觀察行人通道，不讓他們碰觸鐵路設備。每位志工都得到清楚任務、緊急聯絡方式及退出權。方澄欣賞他沒有拿學生充人氣，而是讓參與保持自願。方澄沒有鄙視憤怒的批評者，而是詢問他們認為試行掩蓋了什麼傷害。月蝕期間，儘管天空變暗，月台照明仍然充足。志工區只提供可以食用的點心，並清楚標示過敏原。乘客公開感謝一名駕駛拒絕不安全的發車，她的專業自尊因此提升。

A partial lunar eclipse drew visitors to the coast. For one evening, ridership increased dramatically, but Cheng refused to use the unusual numbers as an ordinary forecast. She separated special-event journeys from weekday travel and explained the distinction at the station notice board. An evergreen hedge sheltered the new walkway from the strongest seaside wind. The visitor center displayed a fish fin beside the coastal ecology map for curious children. A single snow flake landed on Cheng's notebook, then disappeared before she could close it. Local shops prepared merchandise for eclipse visitors, creating a temporary spike in ticket sales. After the visitors left, Cheng counted the people still waiting for the regular connection. A cleaner sat with her dinner unopened; two students were comparing tomorrow's homework. She asked the volunteers to record that quieter departure with the same care they had given the crowded one. Gu Yu found fresh sheets rather than extending the festival tally. The station sounded suddenly large without the visitors, but the remaining journeys still had destinations.

一次月偏蝕吸引遊客到海岸，單晚搭乘量大增，但方澄拒絕把特殊數字當成一般預測。她區分活動旅次與平日出行，在車站公告板解釋差異。常綠樹籬擋住海邊最強的風，替新步道提供遮蔽。遊客中心在海岸生態地圖旁展示魚鰭，供好奇孩子觀看。一片雪花落在方澄的筆記本上，還來不及合起來就融化了。地方店家為賞蝕遊客準備商品，短暫推高了售票量。遊客離開後，方澄數了仍在等日常接駁的人。一名清潔員坐著，晚餐還未打開；兩名學生正在對明天的作業。她請志工像記錄擁擠班次一樣仔細記錄這次安靜的出發。顧嶼拿來新表，沒有延用活動統計。少了遊客，車站忽然顯得空曠，剩下的旅程卻仍各有目的地。

At a late meal, Mei-lin joined Cheng and Gu Yu. She asked the teacher about his plans outside Saltbay, not whether he intended to care for her daughter. He said he was applying for a fellowship in another province. Cheng was relieved: he had a future that did not need to orbit hers. The monotony of regular commuting remained more important than one spectacular festival night. For some students, watching the sky was a pastime that required affordable transport home. Cheng removed redundancy from the survey while retaining questions about actual travel barriers. A lost dog began to roam near the station, and staff guided it away from the tracks.

宵夜時，美玲加入方澄與顧嶼，問教師在鹽灣以外的規劃，而不是問他是否打算照顧她女兒。他說正在申請外縣市的研修。方澄鬆了口氣：他有自己的未來，不必繞著她轉。平日通勤的單調生活，仍比一次壯觀的節慶夜晚更能反映需求。對一些學生而言，觀星是休閒活動，也需要負擔得起的回家交通。方澄刪去問卷的重複題，保留真正涉及交通障礙的問題。一隻迷路的狗在車站附近徘徊，員工把牠引離鐵軌。

The trial's first report listed both welcome improvements and troubling gaps. The new lights worked, but the temporary footpath remained slippery. The committee would have to fund permanent access or admit that the trial had not tested the problem people actually faced. Gu Yu collected rubbish after the public event instead of assigning the work to janitors alone. The mayor joked that the eclipse might slay opposition, but Cheng said the evidence would decide. A sly advertisement claimed the trial had already guaranteed the train's permanent survival. Vapor rose from Gu Yu's curry while they reviewed the revised accessibility map.

試行首份報告列出值得肯定的改進，也指出令人不安的缺口。新燈有效，但臨時步道依舊濕滑。委員會必須資助永久通道，否則就得承認試行沒有真正測到居民面對的問題。公開活動結束後，顧嶼協助撿拾垃圾，而不是把工作全丟給清潔員。市長開玩笑說月蝕也許能擊敗反對聲浪，方澄說仍應由證據決定。一則狡猾的廣告宣稱試行已保證夜車永久存續。他們檢查修正後的無障礙地圖時，顧嶼的咖哩冒著熱氣。
