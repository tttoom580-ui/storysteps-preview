不確定冬季的車票

Tickets for an Uncertain Winter

最後一班夜車，我要自己決定終點 · 第 12 / 25 章 · 5000–6000 · 英中對照 · 校訂中

用互動版查詞、朗讀與收藏

A flu outbreak complicated the winter schedule. The public health office recommended reducing crowding, while hospital staff needed the last train for changing shifts. Cheng arranged for an independent review of ventilation and capacity. She refused a proposal to use the outbreak as an excuse for an immediate, permanent closure. A station attendant offered a chair to a passenger who had waited through two cancellations. Gu Yu laughed when the cafeteria served vegetable batter instead of the promised fried cakes. A recent census showed more hospital workers living along the coast than the old study assumed. The municipal chemist tested cleaning supplies for safe use in enclosed carriages.

流感爆發使冬季時刻表更複雜。衛生單位建議減少擁擠，醫院員工卻需要末班車換班。方澄安排獨立通風和容量審查，拒絕利用疫情作為立即永久停駛的藉口。一名車站服務員替等過兩次取消班次的旅客搬來椅子。餐廳端出蔬菜麵糊而不是承諾的炸餅，顧嶼笑了。最新人口普查顯示，居住海岸的醫院員工比舊研究假設的更多。市府化學師檢測清潔用品，確認它們能安全用在封閉車廂。

The review brought together cleaners, attendants, nurses, and engineers. They described different risks and sometimes contradicted each other. Cheng made the conflict visible on a shared chart. She knew a consensus built by deleting objections would collapse during the first difficult evening. Birds began to chirp outside the window while Cheng updated the first winter timetable. The clinic would dispense medication according to its own rules, not railway decisions. A tired nurse gave a quiet groan when another meeting ran past midnight. An iceberg photograph on the classroom wall reminded students how much evidence could remain unseen.

審查集合了清潔員、服務員、護理師與工程師。他們描述不同風險，有時互相矛盾。方澄把分歧列在共享圖表上。她知道靠刪掉反對意見建立的共識，第一個艱難夜晚就會崩潰。方澄更新第一份冬季時刻表時，窗外鳥兒開始啁啾。診所將依自身規範配藥，不由鐵路決定。又一次會議拖過午夜時，一名疲憊護理師低聲呻吟。教室牆上的冰山照片提醒學生，仍有大量證據可能未被看見。

Gu Yu suggested limited reservations during the highest-risk period, with emergency access protected for hospital workers. Cheng insisted that passengers without smartphones still needed a way to reserve. A local shop offered to help print free reservation slips. The small arrangement was both practical and imperfect. The cleaners' daily experience was invaluable to the ventilation review. The company refused a lavish promotional campaign while essential repairs were unfinished. A new logo appeared on reservation slips but did not replace the official permit number. The loudspeaker announcement repeated reservation rules slowly for passengers without mobile phones.

顧嶼建議高風險期間限制預約名額，同時保障醫院員工緊急通行。方澄堅持沒有智慧手機的人也要能預約。地方商店願免費列印預約單，這個小安排既實用也不完美。清潔員的日常經驗對通風審查非常寶貴。重要修繕未完成時，公司拒絕奢華的宣傳活動。預約單上印了新標誌，但沒有取代正式許可編號。擴音器慢速重複預約規則，照顧沒有手機的乘客。

At home, Mei-lin worried that Cheng was spending too many hours at the station. Cheng thanked her for noticing, then explained that a concern did not authorize her mother to set the timetable. Mei-lin laughed and promised to ask rather than instruct. Both had practiced a new kind of affection. A martial arts teacher volunteered to help direct queues, not to provide medical advice. The outbreak prompted cautious scheduling, but medical experts rejected assumptions that every cough was contagious. The market seller offered an oyster soup for staff working the late shift. A new housing project would populate the coast with residents whose travel needs had not been surveyed.

美玲擔心方澄在車站待得太久。方澄感謝她關心，隨即說明關心不代表母親可以替她排工作時間。美玲笑了，答應改用詢問而非命令。兩人都練習了新的親密方式。一名武術教師自願協助引導排隊，而不是提供醫療建議。疫情促使時刻安排更謹慎，但醫療專家反對把每次咳嗽都當成具傳染性。市場攤商為夜班員工提供牡蠣湯。新住宅計畫將讓更多居民移入海岸，但他們的交通需求尚未調查。

The revised plan passed for a seven-day trial, conditional on medical advice and transparent publication of passenger numbers. Cheng placed the document online and at each ticket window. She knew the plan might change, but people deserved to know exactly which version governed their journeys. The reservation system suffered a setback when its paper slips ran out on the first evening. Cheng remained skeptical of any claim that limited reservations would solve every problem. Passengers could subscribe to text alerts but had to retain an option for printed notices. The agency promised not to withhold safety results merely because they complicated the closure vote. At the ticket window, the attendant tore a blank sheet into temporary slips and immediately asked whether they would be recognized at the next stop. Cheng called ahead before another passenger left with one. By morning the shop had printed a numbered replacement batch. She kept one of the improvised slips in the trial folder beside the supply request, showing exactly where a plan for people without phones had nearly failed.

修正計畫獲准試行七天，條件是遵循醫療建議，並公開乘客數字。方澄把文件放上網，也張貼在每個售票窗。她知道方案可能再變，但旅客應清楚知道自己的旅程由哪一版規則管理。預約系統第一晚就因紙本預約單用完而遇到挫折。方澄仍懷疑限制預約能解決所有問題的說法。乘客可以訂閱簡訊提醒，也必須保留取得紙本通知的選項。機關承諾不會只因安全結果使停駛投票更複雜就隱瞞資料。售票窗的服務員把白紙撕成臨時預約單，立刻追問下一站是否會承認。另一名乘客帶著紙條離開前，方澄先打電話確認。到了早上，商店印好附編號的替代預約單。她把一張臨時紙條與補貨申請放進試行資料夾，清楚留下這個照顧無手機乘客的方案差點在哪裡失靈。
