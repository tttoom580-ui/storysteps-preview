A respiratory illness spread through one coastal village, and the local clinic asked for a reliable evening connection. Cheng resisted making public promises about medical matters she did not control. She arranged a meeting with health professionals and asked which journeys genuinely required the train. The clinic explained that an antibiotic could not treat every respiratory illness, despite common assumptions. Passengers began to applaud when the first wheelchair crossed the temporary ramp safely. An astronomer visiting the observatory offered volunteers a ride home after the training session. The agency planned to computerize its incident log without deleting older handwritten records.

一場呼吸道疾病在沿海村落蔓延，地方診所要求可靠的晚間交通。方澄不願對自己無法掌控的醫療事務作公開承諾，於是安排醫護會議，詢問哪些行程真正需要夜車。診所解釋，抗生素並非對每種呼吸道疾病都有效，儘管很多人這樣以為。第一部輪椅安全通過臨時坡道時，乘客開始鼓掌。一名造訪天文台的天文學家，提議訓練後載志工回家。機關打算把事故紀錄電腦化，但不刪除舊手寫紀錄。

The health team presented clear criteria: people who could use a daytime appointment should do so, while patients requiring specific treatment times needed a safe alternative. A temporary station ramp was inspected as part of the pilot, with a passenger testing the design by choice. Cheng asked whether a faulty ramp might disable independent travel for passengers who relied on it. The driver would evacuate passengers only under the authorized safety procedure. Every new expenditure appeared in a separate column for the public to review. An irritable patient asked for quiet, and the crew respected that need without complaint.

醫療團隊提出明確標準：可在日間看診者盡量改用日間時段，特定時段治療的病人則必須有安全替代方案。臨時月台坡道被列入試行檢查，一名乘客自願實測設計。方澄詢問，故障坡道是否會讓依賴它的乘客無法自行出行。駕駛只會依核准的安全程序疏散乘客。每項新增支出都列在單獨欄位，供公眾檢視。一名煩躁的病人要求安靜，工作人員沒有抱怨就尊重他的需求。

Gu Yu stayed late to help deliver educational materials, then told Cheng he had forgotten dinner. She bought him soup, and he offered to return the favor another night. Their conversation became less guarded. They were discovering that mutual care need not create a debt. Cheng explained that a patient's kin could help with travel only if the patient agreed. Gu Yu practiced meditation before difficult night classes, though he never prescribed it to others. A child handed Cheng a nickel found beside the ticket machine, and she returned it to lost property. A tactile marker helped visually impaired travelers orient themselves at the repaired entrance.

顧嶼忙到深夜協助送衛教資料，才告訴方澄忘了吃飯。她買湯給他，他提議改天回請。兩人的談話不再那麼防備。他們正發現相互關照不必變成欠債。方澄說，病人親屬可以協助交通，但應先取得病人同意。顧嶼在困難的夜課前練習冥想，但從不要求別人照做。一個孩子把售票機旁撿到的五分鎳幣交給方澄，她送去失物招領。觸覺標記幫助視障旅客在修復入口辨明方向。

Mei-lin chose a home-care provider and asked Cheng to attend only the final interview. Cheng agreed to that specific request while keeping the next morning for work. The arrangement seemed unremarkable, but it made both women less afraid of depending on each other. A museum brochure used the historical term oriental, and the editor explained why modern labels were preferred. The doctor said suspected pneumonia required clinical assessment rather than guesses made by rail staff. An engineer displayed the ramp prototype and invited criticism before installing a permanent version. A visiting lecturer described how psychiatry could support patients without replacing their decisions.

美玲選定居家照護機構，只請方澄出席最後面談。方澄同意這項具體請求，仍保留隔天早上的工作時間。安排看似平凡，卻讓母女更不怕彼此依靠。博物館手冊用了昔日的「東方」稱呼，編輯說明現代用語為何更合適。醫師說疑似肺炎需要臨床評估，不能由鐵路人員猜測。工程師展示坡道原型，邀請大家在正式安裝前提出批評。一名到訪講師說明精神醫學如何支持病人，而非取代其決定。

At the end of the week, the clinic reported that the new ramp helped but still required a handrail. Cheng added the deficiency to the public log. A pilot, she concluded, did not become successful by withholding its unfinished parts. The ramp looked robust, yet the inspector still demanded a proper weight test. No sovereign authority could make a medical promise on behalf of an individual patient. The clinic's spokesperson requested clear transport information rather than a publicity photograph. The volunteer left her wardrobe of event costumes at home to make room for supplies. The passenger who had tested the ramp returned to point out where her hand had searched for support. Cheng marked the position while the engineer held the drawing against the entrance wall. They agreed that a successful crossing did not settle the question of a return trip in rain. The clinic would receive the installation date directly, so its staff could give patients current information instead of relying on last week's demonstration.

一週末，診所回報新坡道有幫助，但仍需要扶手。方澄把缺失寫入公開紀錄。她認為試行不是靠隱藏未完工的部分才變成成功。坡道看起來很堅固，檢查員仍要求正式荷重測試。任何主權機關都不能代替個別病人作醫療承諾。診所發言人要求清楚的交通資訊，而不是宣傳照片。志工把一整套活動服裝留在家裡，好騰出空間放物資。曾測試坡道的乘客回來，指出自己的手曾在哪裡尋找支撐。工程師把圖貼著入口牆面，方澄標下位置。他們同意，一次成功通過，不代表雨中返程的問題也已解決。診所將直接收到安裝日期，讓員工向病人提供最新消息，而不是依賴上週的示範。
