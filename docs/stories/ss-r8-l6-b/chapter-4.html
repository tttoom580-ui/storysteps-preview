葡萄藤下的車站

The Station Under the Vines

最後一班夜車，我要自己決定終點 · 第 4 / 25 章 · 5000–6000 · 英中對照

Cheng visited the oldest station on the route, a small stone building beside a neglected vineyard. The municipality planned to sell it after closure. A local heritage group wanted to preserve its beauty, while a private company offered to turn the platforms into an exclusive event venue. Neither proposal explained how residents would get home at night. The mechanic compared reckless spending to an addiction that could damage public trust. The station's aesthetic value was real, but it could not settle the transport question. Cheng calculated the approximate cost of keeping one platform accessible through winter. A university archaeology team had documented the station's original foundations.

方澄造訪路線上最古老的車站：一棟坐落在荒廢葡萄園旁的小石屋。市府打算停駛後出售它。地方文資團體希望保存建築之美，私人公司則想把月台改成專屬活動場地。兩個方案都沒說明居民夜裡要怎麼回家。技工把不負責任的花費比作成癮，說那會傷害公共信任。車站的美學價值確實存在，卻不能替交通問題作決定。方澄估算了冬季維持一座無障礙月台的大約費用。大學考古團隊已記錄車站的原始地基。

An aging maintenance ledger showed that some safety work had been funded but repeatedly postponed. Cheng compared inspection dates and found no evidence of a completed handover. This mattered more than the station's charming appearance. Gu Yu introduced her to a former mechanic who had kept his own copies of repair orders. The mechanic showed his faded railway badge before opening the maintenance ledger. A previous electrical blaze had damaged the roof, though the official report listed no injuries. Gu Yu refused to become a disciple of either the preservation committee or its opponents. The ledger might enlighten the committee if the missing signatures could be explained. The mechanic spread his copies on a bench, holding the corners down with clean bolts. Two orders described the same staircase, but only one carried a completion date. Cheng photographed the numbers and asked the office to retrieve the corresponding invoice. Gu Yu wanted to ask who had failed; she wanted first to know whether anyone had actually accepted the work. The mechanic nodded and turned over the next page.

一本老舊維修簿顯示，部分安全工程早已編列預算，卻一再延期。方澄比對檢查日期，找不到完成交接的證據。這比車站迷人的外觀更重要。顧嶼介紹一名曾經任職的技工，對方保存了修繕單副本。技工打開維修簿前，先展示褪色的鐵路徽章。先前一次電氣火災曾燒壞屋頂，雖然官方報告記載無人受傷。顧嶼不願盲從文資委員會，也不願盲從反對者。若能說清楚缺失的簽名，這份簿冊或許能讓委員會看清事實。技工把副本鋪在長椅上，用乾淨螺栓壓住紙角。兩張工程單描述同一座樓梯，只有一張填了完工日期。方澄拍下編號，請辦公室調出對應發票。顧嶼想問誰失職，她則想先知道究竟有沒有人驗收。技工點點頭，翻到下一頁。

At lunch they disagreed about whether old stations deserved public funding. Gu Yu valued their role as meeting places; Cheng argued that access mattered more than nostalgia. Neither withdrew. Their disagreement ended with a shared question: could the building be reused without removing the transport service the community needed? Cheng did not make a fuss about the faded paint; she asked about the missing handover. The company's proposal carried an implicit assumption that private guests mattered more than local passengers. A retired judge would preside over the heritage hearing to avoid conflicts of interest. The investor promised prestige, but the villagers asked for reliable access first.

午餐時，兩人對舊站是否值得公共資金有不同看法。顧嶼重視車站作為聚會場所的功能；方澄認為可及性比懷舊重要。誰都沒有退讓。他們最後共同提出一個問題：能否保留建築的新用途，同時不取消社區需要的交通？方澄沒有為褪色油漆大驚小怪，而是詢問缺少的交接紀錄。公司的提案隱含一個假設：私人賓客比地方乘客更重要。一位退休法官將主持文資聽證，以避免利益衝突。投資人承諾帶來聲望，村民卻先要求可靠的通行服務。

That evening Cheng telephoned her mother instead of returning early out of guilt. Mei-lin reported that the nurse had arrived on time and that she had chosen her own dinner. Cheng understood how small, reliable services could protect dignity without demanding gratitude. She wanted the railway assessment to be equally specific. The council could not privatize the public platform without a separate legal procedure. At sunset, light seemed to radiate from the vineyard's wet leaves. A rotation of volunteers could monitor the old building without replacing qualified inspectors. The mechanic joked that his ancient shaver was more dependable than the station's electric heater.

當晚方澄打電話給母親，而不是因愧疚提早回家。美玲說護理師準時到訪，晚餐也由她自己選。方澄明白細小而可靠的服務如何保護尊嚴，卻不要求被照顧者感恩。她希望鐵路評估也如此具體。市議會若沒有另一套法定程序，就不能把公共月台私有化。夕陽下，濕潤的葡萄葉彷彿散發光芒。志工輪班可以看顧老建築，卻不能取代合格檢查員。技工開玩笑說，他那台老舊電動刮鬍刀比車站電暖器還可靠。

On the return ride, Gu Yu asked whether they could have dinner when the review was over. Cheng said she did not want her professional decision interpreted as a romantic favor. He agreed to wait until their work no longer involved shared testimony. It was the first invitation she could consider without feeling cornered. A stray dog slept beneath the bench, untroubled by the investors' arguments. Nobody wanted a decorative veil to hide structural damage behind the renovated facade. A vineyard worker asked whether the evening train would still take seasonal staff home. The contractor's warranty covered paint but excluded the very stairs passengers used.

返程車上，顧嶼問審查結束後能否一起吃晚餐。方澄說，她不希望專業決策被解讀成感情上的人情。他同意等到兩人的工作不再涉及共同證詞。這是第一個讓她不覺得被逼迫、可以認真考慮的邀約。一隻流浪狗睡在長椅下，絲毫不受投資人爭論影響。沒有人希望用裝飾布幕遮住翻修立面後面的結構損傷。一名葡萄園工人詢問晚班車是否仍能載季節工返家。承包商的保固涵蓋油漆，卻排除了乘客真正使用的樓梯。
