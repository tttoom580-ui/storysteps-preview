The ninth chapter of Cheng's field notes began at the night school. Its adult students had different reasons for taking the train: retraining, employment, a second chance at literacy, or simply a safe route home. Cheng asked permission before using any account in the assessment. A student worried that facial acne would distract from her upcoming job interview. The program director would administer the literacy test without charging additional fees. The new brochure included real bus departure times instead of vague promises. One speaker described the feeling of captivity that comes with being unable to leave a village at night.

方澄的第九份田野筆記從夜校開始。成年學生搭車的理由各異：再培訓、工作、重新學習閱讀，或只為了安全回家。她在把任何故事寫進評估前都先徵求同意。一名學生擔心臉上的青春痘會影響即將到來的求職面試。計畫主任將主持識字測驗，不另收費。新版手冊列出真正的公車發車時間，而不是空泛保證。一名發言者形容夜晚無法離村時那種被困住的感覺。

Gu Yu invited Cheng to observe a class about reading public notices. One student discovered that the closure leaflet listed travel alternatives without their final departure times. Another pointed out that the accessible bus was excluded on Sundays. Nobody needed an expert to recognize what the document concealed. Students were told not to cram facts but to practice locating information on a timetable. Gu Yu asked Cheng to detach her professional opinion from her personal fondness for the line. A commuter described the discomfort of waiting beside the unlit road in winter. The forthcoming council session would consider whether the pilot deserved another month. Gu Yu gave Cheng a spare leaflet and asked her to follow it as though she had never visited the station. She reached the Sunday line and stopped. There was a bus symbol, but no departure she could actually take. A student slid his own copy across the desk with that empty space circled. Cheng put away her prepared explanation and asked him how he would want the exception printed.

顧嶼邀請方澄旁聽閱讀公共公告的課。一名學生發現停駛傳單列了替代交通，卻沒寫末班時間；另一人指出無障礙公車星期日不開。誰都不需要專家資格，也能看出文件隱瞞了什麼。老師告訴學生不要死背資料，而要練習從時刻表找資訊。顧嶼請方澄把專業判斷與個人對路線的喜愛分開。一名通勤客說起冬天在無燈路旁候車的不舒服。即將舉行的議會會議將討論試行是否值得延長一個月。顧嶼給方澄一張備用傳單，請她假裝從未來過車站，照著安排行程。她看到星期日那行便停住：有公車圖示，卻沒有真正能搭的班次。一名學生把自己的傳單推過桌面，空白處已被圈起。方澄收起準備好的解說，問他希望如何印出這項例外。

At the break, Cheng spoke with a mother who had chosen to raise her stepchild while working night shifts. The woman did not ask for pity. She asked for a timetable she could trust on exam nights. Cheng added the request to the service criteria. A new sign could illuminate the crossing without shining directly into nearby bedrooms. The classroom kept a jade-colored paperweight beside the attendance book. The students' joyous reaction to the corrected leaflet made Cheng smile for the first time that day. Adult literacy was not merely a school achievement; it shaped people's ability to challenge official notices.

休息時，方澄與一名夜班工作、同時養育繼子的母親交談。那名女子不求同情，只希望考試夜晚有可信的時刻表。方澄把需求加入服務標準。新標誌可以照亮穿越處，同時不把光直射附近臥室。教室在點名簿旁放了一枚玉綠色鎮紙。學生看見修正傳單時的喜悅反應，讓方澄當天第一次露出笑容。成人識字不只是學校成就，也影響人們質疑官方公告的能力。

After class, Gu Yu admitted that he had once written confusing notices because he thought complicated words sounded professional. Cheng laughed. She told him that clear writing was not a lesser form of expertise. They shared an unexpectedly easy walk beneath the station lamps. Gu Yu asked one narrator to speak only about her own journeys, not those of absent neighbors. Cold air entered Cheng's nostril as she stepped onto the exposed platform. The peninsula had only two reliable roads, both vulnerable to heavy rain. A small change in the final bus time could have a potent effect on student attendance.

下課後，顧嶼承認自己以前寫過難懂的公告，因為以為複雜用詞比較專業。方澄笑了，說清楚的文字不是比較低級的專業。兩人在站燈下散步，氣氛出乎意料地自在。顧嶼請一名敘述者只談自己的旅程，不替未到場鄰居發言。方澄踏上沒有遮蔽的月台，冷空氣鑽進鼻孔。半島只有兩條可靠道路，兩條都容易受豪雨影響。末班公車時間的小改動，可能對學生出席率產生強大影響。

The next morning Cheng revised the official leaflet with actual departure times and alternatives. Her supervisor approved only the corrections supported by current operations. She accepted the limitation while attaching a list of unverified claims. Accuracy, she thought, could be generous without pretending to certainty. The village hoped for a revival of night classes, not merely a ceremonial train ride. Cheng insisted on selective use of quotations to protect students who requested anonymity. A stepchild needed the same safe route as any other child, the mother told the council aide. A fisherman gave the class a photograph of a trout caught upriver before the dam was built.

隔天早上，方澄修正官方傳單，補上真正的末班時間與替代方案。主管只批准有現行營運資料支持的更正。她接受限制，同時附上尚未查證的聲明清單。她認為準確可以很體貼，不必假裝無所不知。村民期盼夜間課程復甦，而不只是象徵性搭一趟火車。方澄堅持謹慎選用引文，保護要求匿名的學生。那位母親告訴議員助理，繼子與其他孩子一樣需要安全回家路線。一名漁夫送給班級一張照片，拍的是築壩前上游捕到的鱒魚。
