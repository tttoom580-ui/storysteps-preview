The Farm Stop

最後一班夜車，我要自己決定終點 · 第 7 / 25 章 · 5000–6000 · 英中對照 · 校訂中

The next station served farms rather than a town center. Its platform was uneven, and the replacement bus plan placed the nearest stop beyond an unlit field. Cheng walked the route with farm workers instead of measuring it from a satellite image. Two kilometers felt different in boots after a night shift. A bulky wheelbarrow blocked the path, showing why a route suitable for walkers might fail wheelchair users. Cheng refused to deem the unlit field safe merely because no accident had been reported. One worker compared the owner's absolute authority to a small dictatorship, drawing nervous laughter. The wheelchair's front wheels could not glide across the loose stones near the platform.

下一站服務的是農場而非市中心。月台不平，替代公車計畫把最近站牌設在沒有照明的田地之外。方澄與農工一起走這段路，而不是只看衛星影像。夜班後穿靴子走兩公里，感受截然不同。一輛笨重的手推車擋住步道，說明行人可走的路不一定適合輪椅。方澄拒絕只因沒有事故通報，就認定無燈的田地安全。一名工人把地主的絕對權威比作小型獨裁統治，引起緊張的笑聲。輪椅的前輪無法平順滑過月台附近鬆動的石子。

Gu Yu brought a group of adult learners, including a woman who used a wheelchair and an interpreter who supported a visiting farmhand. He asked each person which parts of the route they wanted to describe. Cheng did not treat any passenger as a symbol for an entire community. A local handicraft group offered reflective armbands, provided the council funded approved materials. The interpreter translated both the landowner's offer and the workers' objections without choosing sides. A lesbian farm worker asked whether the safety meeting could welcome her partner without awkward questions. For the youngest workers, liberation meant being able to change jobs without losing access to transport.

顧嶼帶來成年學員，其中包括使用輪椅的女子，以及協助外籍農工的口譯員。他逐一詢問想描述路段的部分。方澄沒有把任何人當成整個社群的象徵。地方手工藝團體願製作反光臂帶，前提是市府支付合格材料費。口譯員不偏袒任何一方，完整翻出地主提議與工人的反對意見。一名女同志農工詢問安全會議能否歡迎伴侶出席，而不被尷尬盤問。對最年輕的工人而言，解放意味著換工作後仍有交通可用。

A landowner offered a narrow strip for a temporary path, but only in exchange for a private advertisement at the station. Cheng asked the council to verify ownership and public access rights before accepting. She had learned that a generous offer could carry an invisible condition. A lofty promise of regional progress meant little beside the muddy entrance to the station. Gu Yu helped students navigate the lane, but he did not substitute his judgment for theirs. Cheng warned that volunteers could overwork themselves unless the municipality accepted long-term responsibility. A pebble caught beneath the wheelchair's small wheel until the path was cleared. The woman stopped her wheelchair where the proposed path narrowed and asked everyone to stand behind her. From there the advertisement would hide anyone approaching from the field. Cheng drew the obstruction on the sketch while the interpreter explained it to the owner. Gu Yu moved back without offering to push. Once the sightline was clear, the woman asked to try the turn again and described the space her wheels required.

一名地主願提供狹長地帶做臨時步道，條件是在車站放私人廣告。方澄請市府先核對土地權屬與公共通行權，不願貿然接受。她學會了慷慨提議也可能藏著看不見的條件。區域進步的崇高承諾，在泥濘的車站入口前顯得空洞。顧嶼協助學生辨識道路，卻沒有代替他們作判斷。方澄警告，如果市府不負長期責任，志工可能過度工作。一顆小石子卡在輪椅小輪下，直到步道清理才排除。那名女子把輪椅停在預定步道收窄處，請大家站到自己身後。從那裡看，廣告會擋住田間走來的人。方澄把遮擋畫進草圖，口譯員向地主說明。顧嶼退後，沒有主動推輪椅。視線清楚後，女子要求再試一次轉彎，說明車輪需要的空間。

In the afternoon, Gu Yu told Cheng that his fellowship interview was confirmed. He would be away for four months if selected. Cheng congratulated him without hiding the small sadness that followed. Nothing between them had been promised, and that was precisely why she could speak honestly. The owner's land offer remained pending while the title record underwent inspection. The old plantation road had become a public lane long before the current owners arrived. A farmer parked his plow well clear of the pedestrian crossing during the access test. The station's faded poster showed royal imagery, though no actual royalty funded its upkeep.

下午，顧嶼告訴方澄研修面試已確定。如果錄取，他會離開四個月。方澄真心道賀，也沒有隱藏隨之而來的一絲失落。兩人尚未許下承諾，也正因如此她能坦率說話。地主的土地提議仍待核准，產權紀錄正在檢查。舊種植園道路早在現任地主到來前，就已成為公共巷道。通行測試期間，一名農夫把犁具停在行人穿越處之外。車站褪色海報印著王室圖像，實際上卻沒有王室資金維護。

By dusk, the group had produced an access sketch that separated repairs requiring engineers from tasks residents could perform safely. The station was still inadequate, but the problems finally had owners, costs, and deadlines. That was a better beginning than pretending everyone could simply walk farther. The clear map might spur the council to repair rather than merely relocate the stop. Cheng and Gu Yu took a quiet stroll back instead of turning the day into a date. The farmer's summer tan remained visible even as cold weather arrived. The landowner called the missing path a trifle, but Cheng insisted on testing it in darkness.

傍晚時，團隊畫出通行草圖，清楚區分需要工程師修復的項目與居民能安全完成的工作。車站仍不合格，但問題終於有負責人、成本和期限。這比假裝人人都能走更遠好得多。清楚的地圖或許能促使市府修繕，而非只搬走站牌。方澄和顧嶼安靜散步回程，沒有把這一天硬變成約會。雖然天氣轉冷，農人夏天曬出的膚色仍看得出來。地主說缺少步道只是小事，方澄卻堅持夜間實測。
