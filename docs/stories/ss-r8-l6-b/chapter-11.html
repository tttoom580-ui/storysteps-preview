沒有火的煙

最後一班夜車，我要自己決定終點 · 第 11 / 25 章 · 5000–6000 · 英中對照 · 校訂中

A summer photograph from the rail depot showed a scorched electrical cabinet that had never been mentioned in the recent safety summary. Cheng asked for a controlled demonstration, not a frightening public spectacle. The depot manager agreed to let qualified technicians test the circuit under independent supervision. The depot manager received acclaim for cooperation, but Cheng reminded the committee that correction was still necessary. An ambiguity in the checklist allowed inspectors to test the wrong electrical circuit. A passenger's bodily safety mattered more than the railway's public image. The old platform door admitted a cold draught that made the electrical room damp.

鐵路機廠的一張夏日照片顯示電箱曾燒焦，但近期安全摘要從未提及。方澄要求受控示範，而不是嚇人的公開表演。機廠主管同意讓合格技師在獨立監督下測試電路。機廠主管因合作受到讚揚，方澄卻提醒委員會仍須改善。檢查表的模糊用字，讓檢查員測到了錯誤電路。乘客的人身安全，比鐵路的公眾形象更重要。舊月台門滲入冷風，使電氣室潮濕。

Gu Yu helped arrange observers from the night school, making sure they understood that watching a test did not qualify them to repair equipment. The group was quiet as a technician traced an overloaded wire. Cheng finally understood why previous inspections had missed the fault: they tested an unused circuit. An archived heat reading was recorded in Fahrenheit, confusing an inspector who expected Celsius. A fireproof partition had been promised but never installed behind the cabinet. A geographical map showed which coastal sections were most exposed to salt spray. An online rumor blamed a hacker, although no evidence showed unlawful access to the controls.

顧嶼協助安排夜校觀察員，並強調看過測試不代表有資格維修。技師追查過載電線時，眾人安靜下來。方澄終於明白先前檢查為何漏掉問題：他們測的是平常不用的電路。歸檔溫度以華氏記錄，讓預期攝氏資料的檢查員混淆。電箱後原本承諾安裝防火隔板，卻始終沒有裝上。地理圖顯示哪些海岸區段最容易受到鹽霧侵蝕。網路傳言責怪駭客，卻沒有證據顯示有人非法存取控制系統。

The manager admitted that he had signed an incomplete checklist under pressure to reopen the depot. His admission did not make him the sole cause of the failure. Cheng documented the approval chain and the exact maintenance orders rather than turning one weary worker into a villain. A layman could still recognize that a checklist describing an unused circuit was inadequate. The new safety procedure would liberate workers from pressure to approve a defective system. A painted mermaid on the old depot wall survived the repair work untouched. The technician sorted miscellaneous parts rather than guessing which replacement would fit. The manager's pen hovered above the new form until the technician showed him the correct circuit label. Cheng asked that the label appear in the inspection photograph as well as the written entry. At the doorway, the observers compared it with the older image and saw why the two tests could not be treated as equivalents. The manager signed only after the technician completed the measurement and read it back to him.

主管承認曾因復工壓力簽下不完整的檢查表，但這不代表他是唯一原因。方澄記下核准流程與維修命令，而非把一名疲倦工人塑造成惡人。即使外行人，也能看出只描述閒置電路的檢查表不充分。新安全程序將讓工人免於被迫核准有缺陷的系統。機廠舊牆上的美人魚彩繪在修繕中毫髮無傷。技師分類各式雜項零件，而不是猜哪個備品合用。主管的筆停在新表格上方，直到技師指出正確的電路標籤。方澄要求檢查照片和文字紀錄都保留那張標籤。門口的觀察員拿舊照片比對，明白兩次測試為何不能當成同一回事。技師完成測量並向主管讀回結果後，他才簽名。

Later, Gu Yu said that keeping an unpopular fact in the report required courage. Cheng disagreed. Courage would have been refusing the earlier reopening until the operating circuit had been tested. He accepted the correction, and they sat together in companionable silence until the station lights came on. The manager logged every occurrence of overheating, including those that caused no interruption. Gu Yu canceled the class outing to the depot until the inspection team declared it safe. Cheng would persevere with the audit even if the final recommendation remained unpopular. She began to ponder why senior managers had demanded a rapid reopening without a complete test.

後來顧嶼說，把不受歡迎的事實留在報告裡需要勇氣。方澄不同意；真正的勇氣原本應是拒絕先前那次復工，直到營運電路完成測試。他接受了她的修正，兩人在車站燈亮起前安靜相伴。主管記下每次過熱事件，包括沒有造成中斷的情況。顧嶼取消班級機廠參訪，直到檢查團隊確認安全。即使最後建議不受歡迎，方澄也會堅持完成查核。她開始思考，為何高層主管要求在測試不完整時迅速重啟。

The committee ordered repair and a new inspection. The line remained open under temporary restrictions, but Cheng resisted celebrating. They had discovered a serious defect because someone kept a photograph. A resilient system should not depend on the luck of finding an old image. The discussion continued to revolve around responsibility rather than simple blame. A technician set down a metal utensil before touching the insulated tool cabinet. The depot manager refused to name one exhausted employee as the villain of the incident. Gu Yu offered Cheng yogurt after the long inspection, and she finally admitted she was hungry.

委員會命令修繕並重新檢查。路線在臨時限制下維持營運，但方澄不打算慶祝。他們是靠有人留下照片才找到嚴重缺陷；堅韌的制度不該依賴偶然找到舊照片的運氣。討論仍圍繞責任，而非簡單究責。技師碰絕緣工具櫃之前，先放下手中的金屬餐具。機廠主管拒絕把一名疲憊員工指成整起事件的惡人。長時間檢查後，顧嶼給方澄優格，她終於承認自己餓了。
