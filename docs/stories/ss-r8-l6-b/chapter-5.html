A Stop Among Chestnut Trees

最後一班夜車，我要自己決定終點 · 第 5 / 25 章 · 5000–6000 · 英中對照

On the fifth night a signaling failure forced the train to stop among chestnut trees above the harbor. Rain threatened the road, and the driver refused to move without permission. Passengers could see the distant lights of their destination but could not safely reach them. For the first time, Cheng had to help manage the consequences of the system she was evaluating. A damaged signal lamp was only an accessory to a much larger communication failure. The official breakdown report failed to mention when the backup channel first stopped working. The driver refused to bypass the safety procedure, even as frustrated passengers argued. Someone had brought champagne for a wedding, but nobody suggested opening it during the emergency.

第五晚，號誌故障迫使列車停在港口上方的栗樹間。雨水威脅公路安全，駕駛沒有許可便不肯前進。乘客看得見遠方目的地的燈火，卻無法安全抵達。方澄第一次得親自協助處理她正在評估的系統所造成的後果。損壞的號誌燈，只是更大通訊故障中的附帶問題。官方故障報告沒有提到備援頻道何時開始失效。儘管乘客焦急爭論，駕駛仍拒絕繞過安全程序。有人為婚禮帶了香檳，但緊急時刻沒人提議開瓶。

Gu Yu organized a list of medicines and urgent needs, then passed it to the crew. He insisted that the conductor, not volunteers, determine whether an evacuation could proceed. Cheng called the control center and learned that its delay estimate relied on a communication channel that had failed two hours earlier. She recorded the discrepancy before requesting a backup. The train waited beneath a chestnut tree whose leaves shivered in the cold rain. Gu Yu said it would be cowardly to hide an avoidable system failure behind a cheerful announcement. A passenger's account of an earlier stranding seemed to evoke a similar communication problem. A farmer carried a crate of domestic fowl and worried more about ventilation than his luggage. Cheng asked the dispatcher to read the time printed beside the estimate, not simply repeat the predicted arrival. For a moment the line was quiet. Then he gave the old timestamp, and she heard a second voice begin checking the backup equipment. Inside the carriage, Gu Yu handed the conductor a revised count without announcing anyone's medication aloud. The waiting passengers could hear that somebody was finally checking the clock.

顧嶼整理乘客藥物和緊急需求清單，再交給列車人員。他堅持由列車長而非志工決定是否撤離。方澄致電調度中心，發現延誤估算竟依賴兩小時前就失效的通訊頻道。她先記下落差，再要求備援。列車停在一棵栗樹下，樹葉在冷雨中顫動。顧嶼說，用歡樂公告遮掩可避免的系統故障是怯懦的。一名乘客提起先前受困的經歷，似乎勾起類似的通訊問題。一名農人帶著一箱家禽，比起行李更擔心牠們的通風。方澄請調度員讀出估算旁的時間，而不是重複預計抵達時刻。電話那端安靜了一會兒，他才說出過時的時間標記；她隨後聽見另一人開始檢查備援設備。車廂裡，顧嶼把更新後的人數交給列車長，沒有當眾報出任何人的藥物。候車乘客聽得出來，終於有人真的在核對時間。

Mei-lin sent a message asking whether her daughter was safe. Cheng replied directly and asked her mother to call the nurse if she needed help tonight. She was surprised that setting a boundary felt neither cold nor selfish. The earlier version of herself would have apologized for a failure she had not caused. The conductor's voice grew hoarse after hours of giving calm safety instructions. A passenger offered hand lotion to an elderly woman whose skin had cracked in the cold. Cheng rejected the mentality that admitting one failure meant abandoning the whole route. The monetary cost of the delay would be measured separately from its impact on vulnerable travelers.

美玲傳訊詢問女兒是否平安。方澄直接回覆，並請母親今晚若需要幫助就聯絡護理師。她意外發現設下界線既不冷漠也不自私。從前的她可能會為一場並非自己造成的故障道歉。列車長數小時冷靜說明安全指示後，聲音變得沙啞。一名乘客把護手乳送給一位皮膚被寒冷凍裂的老太太。方澄反對那種「承認一次故障就等於放棄整條路線」的心態。延誤的金錢成本，將與弱勢旅客承受的影響分開評估。

The backup vehicle arrived only when a landslide inspection had cleared the access road. The passengers waited in a heated carriage, sharing snacks and stories. One woman remarked that a service deserved repair, not blind defense. Cheng wrote down the sentence. It sounded like a useful description of both the railway and the relationships she wanted. A moody teenager stopped complaining when he was invited to help distribute warm drinks. The crew became the nucleus of a small, orderly response rather than a collection of competing volunteers. A sudden rap on the carriage door signaled that the inspection team had arrived. The dispatcher feared officials would slash the maintenance budget after the incident.

備援車直到邊坡檢查確認道路可通行後才抵達。乘客在有暖氣的車廂裡等待，分享點心和故事。一名女子說，服務值得修理，而不是盲目辯護。方澄把這句話記下來。它彷彿同時描述了鐵路與她想要的關係。一名情緒不穩的少年受邀分發熱飲後，不再抱怨。列車人員成為有序應變的核心，而非讓志工各自為政。車門突然傳來敲擊聲，表示檢查小隊已抵達。調度員擔心事故後官員會大砍維修預算。

By dawn every passenger had reached a safe location. Cheng published a preliminary account listing delays, decisions, and unresolved questions. It included her own mistake: she had first trusted an outdated control-center estimate. A credible report, she decided, must make room for the author's errors as well as everyone else's. An exhausted traveler began to snore once the heat returned to the carriage. The telecommunications failure affected the crew's estimate but not the brakes or emergency lights. Cheng promised to unveil the corrected timeline after independent confirmation. The volunteers showed vigor, yet their effort could never replace trained railway staff.

天亮前，所有乘客都抵達安全地點。方澄公布初步記錄，列出延誤、決策及未解問題，其中也包括自己的錯：她一開始相信了過時的調度估算。她認為可信的報告，不只要容納別人的錯，也要容納作者自己的錯。暖氣恢復後，一名疲憊旅客開始打鼾。電信通訊故障影響了車組的估算，卻沒有影響煞車或緊急照明。方澄承諾，在獨立確認後公開修正後的時間線。志工展現旺盛活力，但他們的努力不能取代受訓鐵路人員。
