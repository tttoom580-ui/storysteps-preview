The Notice on Platform Four

最後一班夜車，我要自己決定終點 · 第 1 / 25 章 · 5000–6000 · 英中對照

Fang Cheng returned to Saltbay Station with a suitcase and a professional assignment she had not requested. Her employer wanted her to assess the coastal night train before voting on its closure. Her mother needed help after surgery, but that was a separate conversation, one neither of them had yet learned to conduct. Cheng felt her fingers clasp the cold rail, then opened her notebook instead of making a dramatic promise. The station committee would convene on Friday, before the first survey could possibly be complete. A rescued fox cub appeared on the wildlife poster beside the closure notice, advertising the coastal sanctuary. A dental nurse approached the window to ask whether her evening travel pass would still work.

方澄拖著行李回到鹽灣車站，帶著一項自己並未爭取的專業任務：評估海岸夜車是否應該停駛。母親術後確實需要協助，但那是另一場她們都還沒學會好好進行的談話。方澄感覺自己的手指握緊冰冷扶手，接著打開筆記本，沒有許下戲劇化的承諾。車站委員會將於星期五召開，屆時第一次調查根本還不可能完成。停駛公告旁的野生動物海報上，有一隻獲救的小狐狸幼獸，替海岸保護區宣傳。一名牙科護理師走近窗口，詢問晚間通行證是否仍然有效。

At the ticket window, a paper notice announced that the last departure might come in ninety days. Cheng had designed timetables for fifteen years; she knew a printed schedule could seem objective even when its assumptions were deeply personal. Outside, people were still arriving to ask about tomorrow's train. A passenger called the remote railway director a dictator, but Cheng declined to adopt the insult. The new directive counted only individually sold tickets, excluding institutional passes and emergency transfers. For a person without a car, the nearest alternative dwelling could be two villages away. Horses seemed to gallop across an old mural, reminding Cheng that this railway had once replaced a dangerous road.

售票窗貼著公告：最後一班夜車可能在九十天後開出。方澄設計時刻表已有十五年，知道印刷的表格看似客觀，背後的假設卻可能深深影響個人。窗外仍有人趕來詢問明天的班次。一名乘客罵遠方的鐵路主管是獨裁者，方澄卻不願照單全收這種指責。新指令只計入單獨售出的車票，排除了機構通行證與緊急轉乘。對沒有車的人而言，最近的替代住所可能遠在兩個村落之外。舊壁畫上的馬匹彷彿正在奔馳，提醒方澄這條鐵路曾取代危險道路。

A volunteer teacher named Gu Yu offered her a list of people who used the service without purchasing ordinary tickets: carers, evening students, and hospital workers with travel passes. He spoke without flirting and without assuming that a visiting expert would automatically believe him. Cheng asked for the original records, not his confidence. The threat of sudden closure could heighten anxiety for patients whose appointments ended after sunset. The janitor, Mr. Luo, kept a handwritten count of wheelchair users whom the electronic gate missed. His motto was simple: a traveler who passes quietly is still a traveler. A pilgrim heading to the seaside shrine described using the same train every winter. Gu Yu's list had coffee stains along one edge and different handwriting beside several names. Cheng asked him to leave those corrections visible. Tomorrow she would compare them with the gate totals, starting with the dental nurse's return journey. He put a finger beside the station office number, showing her whom to call before the morning shift ended.

志工教師顧嶼遞給她一份名單，列出許多不靠一般單程票搭車的人：照護者、夜校學生以及持通行證的醫院員工。他沒有調情，也不預設外來專家一定會相信他。方澄要求看原始紀錄，而不是只聽他的把握。突然停駛的威脅可能加劇日落後才看完診病人的焦慮。清潔員羅先生手寫記錄電子閘門漏算的輪椅乘客。他的座右銘很簡單：安靜通行的旅客，也仍然是旅客。一名要去海邊神社朝聖的人說，他每年冬天都搭這班車。顧嶼的名單邊緣留著咖啡漬，幾個姓名旁還有不同筆跡。方澄請他保留那些更正。明天她會先從牙科護理師的返程開始，拿名單與閘門總數比對。他指著站務室電話，告訴她早班結束前該聯絡誰。

Her mother, Mei-lin, met her in the station cafe rather than at home. She said she wanted help choosing a care arrangement, not a daughter who would abandon her career. Cheng was startled by how much relief could arrive in one ordinary sentence. Yet the train's future still seemed impossible to untangle from the household's. A previous flood had created a precedent for treating the night service as emergency infrastructure. Cheng knew that false savings could reap praise today while imposing invisible costs tomorrow. The carriage remained stationary while the driver checked a warning lamp near the rear door. In the policy draft, efficiency had become a synonym for removing anything difficult to measure.

母親美玲選在車站咖啡館見她，而不是直接回家。她說自己需要協助挑選照護方式，不需要女兒放棄職涯。方澄驚訝於這句平凡的話竟能帶來如此大的寬慰，但夜車的命運與家庭仍難以完全分開。先前的洪水曾留下前例，把夜車列為緊急公共設施。方澄知道，虛假的節省今天可能贏得讚許，明天卻會帶來看不見的成本。司機檢查後門附近的警示燈時，車廂保持靜止。政策草案把效率當成刪去難以衡量事物的同義詞。

Before midnight, Cheng stood on the platform with a notebook divided into observations, assumptions, and questions. She had no intention of saving a line merely because it sounded romantic. She had even less intention of allowing a careless calculation to erase other people's choices. The first task was to find out who was missing from the numbers. The clinic scheduled a kidney transplant patient for a journey that had no safe daytime connection. A local trustee said he would fund a temporary shuttle only if the evidence justified it. Mei-lin said love was unconditional, but her daughter's availability should never be assumed. Cheng would utter no promise about the train until she had heard the people affected.

午夜前，方澄站在月台，把筆記分成觀察、假設與待答問題。她不會只因夜車聽起來浪漫就決意拯救它，更不會容許草率的計算抹去他人的選擇。她首先得找出：數字裡少算了哪些人。診所替一名腎臟移植病人安排了沒有安全日間接駁的行程。一名地方受託人表示，只有證據支持，才會資助臨時接駁車。美玲說愛可以無條件，卻不代表女兒的時間可以被理所當然地占用。在聽見受影響者的聲音之前，方澄不會對夜車說出任何承諾。
