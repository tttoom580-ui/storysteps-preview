沒有爆炸的那一夜

The Night Without an Explosion

頂流偶像的失蹤倒數 · 第 10 / 25 章 · 3000–4000 · 英中對照 · 校訂中

用互動版查詞、朗讀與收藏

One day remained. An old television website listed the Studio B incident as an explosion that had destroyed the set. Yet when Yinghe visited the location with a licensed inspector, the walls showed no damage from an explosion. A small bandage wrapper lay beneath a cabinet, but that proved nothing about the past. The inspector illustrated the difference between water damage and sudden pressure on a simple diagram. Qiu arrived and congratulated her on finding a "mere technicality." She refused the bargain he offered: stop the inquiry, and the company would pay for a private hospital visit for Jingyao. "Medical care is not a reward for silence," she answered. He left without an apology.

倒數剩一天。舊電視網站把B棚事件寫成爆炸並摧毀布景，但映荷和合格檢查員走入現場時，牆面毫無爆炸痕跡。櫃子下雖有舊繃帶包裝，也不能證明過去發生什麼。檢查員用圖解釋水損和瞬間壓力的差異。邱董事前來，嘲諷她找到的只是小小技術問題，並提出交易：停止調查，公司就支付景遙私人醫療費。映荷拒絕：「醫療照護不是沉默的獎賞。」他沒有道歉便離開。

The inspector was critical of the company's preservation of the site. New locks had been installed over old entrances, and workers were forbidden to enter certain rooms without management approval. A surgeon who treated an injured crew member years earlier recalled bruises and water exposure, not burns. His patient had survived but transferred hospitals soon afterward. The account did not reveal Lan Cen's location. It did show that the official story of destruction was suspicious. A shelf held books on ancient civilization, a souvenir tomb model, and photographs of prosperous tours. These theatrical objects had been mistaken for evidence by previous investigators. Yinghe set them aside carefully.

檢查員批評公司保存現場的方式：新門鎖蓋住舊入口，工人未經管理層同意不得進入部分房間。當年診治傷者的外科醫師記得瘀傷與浸水，沒有燒傷；病人活下來後很快轉院。這不能指出藍岑去向，卻使「爆炸毀滅現場」的官方故事更加可疑。架上有古文明書籍、墓穴紀念模型及巡演照片。前人曾把這些戲劇道具誤當證據，映荷一件件放到旁邊。

Jingyao received the final note while eating breakfast: "Tomorrow at the encore, no one will be able to find you." The wording differed from the first message. It threatened a disappearance rather than predicting one. Yinghe asked him whether he wanted to cancel. He said he needed time to think, and she respected that. In the meantime she arranged two plans: a reduced concert with the moving stage disabled, and a cancellation with full refund information. She also called the venue's safety officer. The officer promised a written resolution before opening the doors. Yinghe had learned that a decision should be made in daylight, not under the audience's demand for a miracle.

早餐時，景遙收到最後一封信：「明天安可時，沒有人能找到你。」這和第一封語氣不同，是威脅失蹤，不只是預言。映荷問他想不想取消，他說需要時間，她尊重。她先安排兩套方案：停用升降台的簡化演唱會，或取消並提供完整退款資訊。她也聯絡場館安全主管，對方承諾開門前給出書面決議。映荷知道，重要決定應在白天完成，而非受觀眾期待奇蹟的情緒挾持。

A final interview with Chen exposed a strange detail. On the old night, he had heard a three-note whistle immediately before the water pumps stopped. A man with a wedding ring had walked away carrying the camera master. The name on the camera checklist belonged to Qiu, but the signature had been written by someone else. The inspector suggested seeking the original employee register rather than trusting the checklist. Yinghe agreed. Chen also remembered a girl saying, "Don't let them name my silence for me." The words sounded too precise for him to invent casually, yet Yinghe marked them as recollection. They still needed independent support.

陳碩最後一次受訪時提到一個細節：當晚抽水機停止前，他聽見三音口哨。一名戴婚戒的男子拿著攝影母帶離開。攝影名單寫邱董事姓名，簽名卻像別人代寫。檢查員建議尋找原始員工名冊，而不是只信這張單。陳還記得有個女孩說：「不要讓他們替我的沉默命名。」這句話精確得難以隨口編造，但映荷仍標成回憶，等待獨立證據。

The old neighborhood had once housed new settlers who worked at the television station. One former camera assistant was now pregnant and did not want reporters waiting outside her home. Yinghe asked the investigator to arrange an interview without cameras and to forbid unauthorized publication of her address. The assistant remembered being told to turn off a warning lamp, but she could not say who gave the order. She supplied a copy of her duty roster instead. The document placed her on the second floor when the pumps stopped. That fact did not solve the case, but it ruled out one false claim that she had operated the lower switch.

舊攝影棚附近曾住許多新遷入的電視台員工。一位前攝影助理如今懷孕，不願記者守在家門外。映荷請調查員安排無攝影的訪談，禁止未經授權公布住址。助理記得有人命令她關警示燈，卻無法確定發令者；她提供當年值班表，顯示抽水機停止時她人在二樓。這不能解決案件，卻排除她曾操作下方開關的錯誤指控。

That evening, Jingyao told Yinghe he would sing. He also agreed that the performance could stop at any moment if the safety officer required it. He wanted to speak honestly, not stage a revolution or perform a heroic sacrifice. "If I disappear," he said, "please remember that I wanted to come back." Yinghe answered that she would do more than remember. She would have staff stationed at both approved exits and a written list of everyone allowed backstage. Outside the window, workers erected a glossy banner with the words FOREVER PRESENT. Under the banner, someone had drawn four tiny waves in red paint. The countdown had reached zero, but its author was still in the building.

晚上，景遙告訴映荷他決定唱，也同意安全主管要求時可隨時停演。他想誠實開口，不是上演革命或英雄犧牲。「如果我消失，」他說，「請記得我本來想回來。」映荷回答，她不只會記得，還會讓人員守在兩個核准出口，列出所有可進後台的人。窗外工作人員掛起亮麗布條：「永遠在場」。布條下，有人用紅漆畫了四道小浪。倒數歸零，但寫信的人還在大樓裡。
