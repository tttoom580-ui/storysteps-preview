少了一個音節的歌

The Song With a Missing Syllable

頂流偶像的失蹤倒數 · 第 5 / 25 章 · 3000–4000 · 英中對照 · 校訂中

用互動版查詞、朗讀與收藏

With five days left, the company held a rehearsal beside a lake for a television special. A canoe floated behind the orchestra, and portable speakers played an imaginative arrangement of Jingyao's first hit. He had always been an idol of prosperity and good luck to sponsors. Now the countdown's impact was visible in his sleepless eyes. A technician enlarged a sheet of music so Yinghe could read the handwritten notes. In the middle of the chorus, the lyric sheet contained a distinct empty space. One syllable had been deliberately removed. Jingyao said the original word was "return," but the public version used "remain." The difference changed who was being told to leave.

倒數剩五天，公司在湖邊為電視特別節目排練。獨木舟漂在樂團後方，便攜式喇叭播著景遙出道曲的新編曲。他向來是贊助商眼中的繁榮吉祥偶像，如今失眠卻寫在臉上。技師放大樂譜，讓映荷看清手寫註記。副歌中央竟有一處刻意留下的空白，少了一個音節。景遙記得原詞是「回去」，公開版本卻改成「留下」。這一字之差，改變了命令誰離開。

The composer, an older woman named Luo Wen, arrived with a portable cassette recorder and a firm objection. She had not approved the revised chorus. In the first recording, a child sang "return to the water line" as a location cue, not as a spiritual idiom. Another voice said "do not abandon the last child." Luo explained that a studio assistant had requested the change after the accident. She offered the original tape, but only if the team would not seize it and broadcast it without permission. Yinghe agreed to create copies in her presence and return the master. An honest process mattered more than a favorable headline.

作曲家羅雯帶著便攜錄音機來到現場，堅決反對改動。她從未同意新版副歌。最初錄音裡，孩子唱的是「回到水線」，那是位置提示，不是抽象的精神成語。另一個聲音說：「別丟下最後一個孩子。」羅雯說事故後有助理要求改詞，願提供原帶，但不允許團隊扣押或未經同意播出。映荷同意當面複製並歸還母帶。公平程序比漂亮新聞更重要。

Near the lake, fans staged a small protest against the heavy security fences. Qiu said the crowd's anxiety made it necessary to protect the star from everyone. Jingyao listened and asked why the fences blocked the only route used by wheelchair visitors. Yinghe adjusted the design. The company offered her an extra allowance if she would approve the original layout, an offer she rejected. "This device is meant to protect people, not separate them," she said. A child in the audience giggled when Jingyao moved the microphones to the accessible platform. He looked relieved. The changed plan meant fewer dramatic camera angles, but nobody had to be carried down the stairs.

湖邊歌迷抗議厚重的隔離欄。邱董事說大家太焦慮，必須把偶像和所有人隔開。景遙卻問：為什麼欄杆堵住輪椅觀眾唯一能通過的路？映荷調整動線。公司甚至提出額外津貼，希望她批准原設計，她拒絕了：「這設備應該保護人，不是把人分開。」景遙把麥克風移到無障礙台，台下一名孩子開心笑了。少了戲劇化鏡頭，卻不必有人被抱下樓梯。

Mei found a second sheet of lyrics inside a costume pocket. Across the top someone had written "IDOL" in block letters. The missing syllable was marked with a red circle and a note: "Fourth child, not fourth verse." Yinghe compared the paper with the first threat. The circles were similar, but the handwriting was not. She recorded the difference rather than forcing them into one explanation. Then she asked Jingyao to describe what he remembered of Shen Yu. "He was imaginative," Jingyao said. "He invented a town where every door could open from either side." The boy's dream had left a strange echo in the real studio's design.

梅姨在戲服口袋找到第二張歌詞，紙頂用大寫字母寫著「偶像」。少掉的音節被紅圈標出，旁邊註明：「第四個孩子，不是第四節歌。」映荷比對第一封威脅，紅圈相似，筆跡卻不同。她如實記下差異，沒有勉強拼成單一解釋。然後她請景遙描述沈宇。「他很有想像力，」景遙說，「他畫了一座每扇門都能從兩邊打開的城。」那個孩子的夢，在真實攝影棚裡留下奇怪的回聲。

Luo added a small detail about the original score. The word "return" had not been selected for its sound alone; the children used it during rehearsal to remember where the safe passage began. The later replacement, "remain," sounded almost the same when sung quickly, making it easy for listeners to miss the difference. Jingyao practiced both versions softly and noticed that each changed his breathing. He asked to keep the old sheet music away from the press until they could confirm the children's names. Luo agreed, pleased that he was treating the song as someone's work rather than a secret message addressed only to himself.

羅雯補充一項細節：最初歌詞選「回去」，不只是為了好聽，而是孩子排練時用來記住安全通道的起點。後來改成「留下」，快唱時聽起來很接近，觀眾容易忽略差別。景遙輕聲練兩個版本，察覺連呼吸方式都不同。他要求確認孩子姓名前暫不讓媒體取得原譜。羅雯同意，也欣慰他終於把歌曲當成他人的勞動，而非專門寄給自己的暗號。

That evening, a stranger tried to buy Luo's master tape. He offered enough money for her to retire and promised to restore her reputation as a composer. She refused. When Yinghe heard about the attempt, she helped Luo change the storage location and document the approach. They still did not know which side the stranger represented. A public scandal could hurt the singer, but silence could also leave old damage unanswered. Jingyao watched dusk fall over the canoe and made a decision without asking Qiu: the next rehearsal would include the original line, sung once, for the team alone. It might not solve the mystery. It would be the first time in years he had chosen his own words.

當晚有陌生人想買羅雯的母帶，開價足以讓她退休，還保證恢復她作曲家的名聲。她拒絕了。映荷得知後協助更換保存地點，記錄接觸過程。她們仍不知道陌生人代表哪一方。公開醜聞會傷害景遙，沉默卻也會讓舊傷無人處理。景遙望著暮色中的獨木舟，未經邱董事同意便做了決定：下一場排練，他要為團隊唱一次最初的歌詞。這未必能解謎，卻是多年來第一次，他自己選擇要唱的字。
