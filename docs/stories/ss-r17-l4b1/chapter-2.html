海面下的聲音

A Voice Hidden Under the Sea

頂流偶像的失蹤倒數 · 第 2 / 25 章 · 3000–4000 · 英中對照 · 校訂中

用互動版查詞、朗讀與收藏

Eight days remained. Jingyao's second rehearsal took place in a studio designed like a coastal landscape. A painted fort stood beside a cardboard mill, and a fossil from a science museum sat behind glass. The set belonged to the old children's series, not his current concert. Qiu claimed the combination was a harmless nostalgic tribute. Yinghe was more interested in why the sound crew had been ordered to revise the original track. A delicate whisper survived beneath the music. A young voice said, "The fourth door is not a door." Jingyao fixed his gaze on the speakers. He had heard the song for years but had never recognized that sentence.

距離倒數還剩八天。第二次排練的舞台畫成海岸風景，紙板堡壘旁有磨坊，玻璃後還擺著科學博物館借來的化石。這不是新歌舞台，而是當年兒童節目的布景。邱董事說只是懷舊致敬。映荷更在意的是音控組為何被要求修改原始錄音。音樂底下藏著一段極輕的聲音：「第四扇門，不是門。」景遙注視喇叭。他聽了這首歌多年，竟從沒認出這句話。

The sound engineer showed her two tape boxes. One was marked ATOM, an absurd title for a children's sea song; the other said ATOMIC SUMMER. Both had traveled through an immigration inspection after a Hong Kong television event. The extent of the edits was unclear because the logs had vanished. "I can guarantee only that I did not make this cut," the engineer said. He did not want an intimate conversation with management recorded on camera. Yinghe switched off her recorder at his request and wrote his statement by hand. She promised not to identify him until they agreed on how it could be used. That agreement made him willing to show one more reel.

音控師拿出兩盒磁帶，一盒寫著「原子」，另一盒寫著「原子之夏」，都曾在香港電視活動後通過入境檢查。究竟被剪掉多少內容，因紀錄遺失而無法確定。「我只能保證不是我剪的。」音控師說。他不願私密的談話被管理層看見，映荷便依要求關掉錄音，改用手寫，並同意在確認用途前不公開姓名。他因此願意拿出另一捲錄音帶。

On that tape, a child was explaining a class project about an atom and its movement. Another asked why a so-called atomic machine would have a wooden emergency lever. The children laughed. Underneath, a woman's murmur gave a practical instruction: "If the lights fail, take the painted river to the low room." Yinghe suspected the sentence referred to the studio layout, not a fictional voyage. She walked the landscape with a flashlight and counted the exits. The fourth door on the program did not exist. Instead, four painted waves surrounded a narrow passage behind the mill. A productive afternoon of checking measurements told her more than Qiu's entire publicity speech.

另一卷帶子裡，小孩正解釋課堂上的原子運動模型。有人問，為什麼一台號稱原子能的機器會配木製緊急拉桿，大家笑了起來。笑聲底下有女人的低語：「燈熄時，沿著畫好的河走到低處房間。」映荷懷疑這是攝影棚的實際路線，而非虛構航程。她拿手電筒逐一檢查，節目單上的第四道門根本不存在，倒是磨坊後方的四道畫浪圍著一條窄走道。整個下午的測量，比邱董事的宣傳話術更有用。

Jingyao finally admitted that he had retained pieces of that day in dreams: a wet floor, a child crying, a red curtain pulled across an opening. His nightmares were psychological evidence of distress, not reliable proof of who had acted. Yinghe explained that difference gently. "Memory may guide a question," she said, "but it cannot answer every question." He watched the singer he had been at fourteen on the small screen and asked whether Lan Cen had returned from her voyage to another country. Qiu had told him her family emigrated. The immigration records did not confirm it. That was the first gap Jingyao wanted to investigate himself.

景遙終於承認，夢裡總反覆出現那天的片段：濕滑地板、孩子哭聲、紅幕遮住洞口。這些噩夢證明他受到驚嚇，卻不能可靠地指出誰做了什麼。映荷溫和地解釋這個差別：「記憶能引導問題，不能替所有問題作答。」他看著螢幕裡十四歲的自己，問藍岑是否真的遠赴他國。邱董事一直說藍家移民了，但出入境紀錄並無法證實。這成為景遙第一次主動想追查的缺口。

The engineer refused to play the entire hidden tape for the public. Several children had spoken on it, and their adult families had not approved its use. Yinghe agreed. She checked the labels of the two boxes, keeping the dates visible in photographs. A scratch on one reel suggested it had been handled after the supposed destruction of the studio. If the original had survived, the company's claim that every recording was lost required revision. The possibility gave Jingyao a practical goal: find people who had handled the tape, not enemies who might exist only in his nightmares.

音控師不願向公眾播放完整隱藏錄音，因為裡面有孩子說話，成年後的本人及家屬尚未同意。映荷接受，將兩盒帶子的日期拍得清清楚楚。一卷帶邊緣的刮痕顯示，它在攝影棚宣稱毀損後仍被操作過。原始錄音若還在，公司「全數遺失」的說法就得修正。這也讓景遙有了實際目標：找曾經接觸帶子的人，而不是尋找可能只存在噩夢裡的敵人。

At closing time, the engineer placed a small paper messenger envelope in Yinghe's hand. It had been enclosed in the tape case for six years. Inside was a map of the fort and a pencil note: "Do not trust the sea when it moves without wind." Its handwriting differed from the countdown threat. Yinghe could not yet know whether either writer had good intentions. She made copies with dates, locked the originals away, and kept the work schedule ordinary. The singer needed rest, not an audience for every fear. Outside, the river set was being folded for shipping. Behind the cardboard mill, someone had carved an arrow into the floorboards. It pointed down.

收工時，音控師給映荷一只小紙信封，說它在磁帶盒裡躺了六年。裡頭有堡壘地圖，以及鉛筆寫的話：「沒有風時自己移動的海，不可信。」筆跡與倒數威脅不同。映荷仍無法判斷哪個寫信人存有善意。她留下日期與複本、鎖好原件，並維持正常工作安排；景遙需要休息，而不是讓每個恐懼都成為表演。外面河流布景正在拆運。紙板磨坊後的地板，竟刻著一支向下的箭頭。
