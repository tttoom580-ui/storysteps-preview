空房間的節奏

The Rhythm of the Empty Room

頂流偶像的失蹤倒數 · 第 7 / 25 章 · 3000–4000 · 英中對照 · 校訂中

Jingyao's seventh rehearsal was held at an aquarium, a strange place for a famous pop concert. The glass tanks gave every sound a delayed echo. An academic expert had been hired to praise the chemistry between light and music, but the show's accountant could not explain the sudden expansion of equipment costs. Yinghe asked for relevant invoices, not applause. The manager of the aquarium was annoyed by the request until she showed him a damaged cable near a water basin. His staff moved visitors away, fortunate to have found the fault early. Jingyao watched the fish swim through shade and said he could remember a similar blue room from childhood.

第七次排練安排在水族館，對頂流演唱會而言格外奇特。玻璃魚缸讓每個聲音都帶延遲回音。公司請學術專家讚美燈光與音樂的化學反應，會計卻解釋不了設備費為何突然擴張。映荷要的是相關發票，不是掌聲。水族館經理原本對要求感到厭煩，直到她指出水池邊受損電線，他才趕緊疏散訪客。景遙看著魚群游進陰影，說自己童年似乎也見過相同的藍色房間。

Luo asked the musicians to play the old melody without words. The rhythm contained five beats where the official score showed four. Jingyao did not understand the discrepancy until a stage worker tapped the missing beat against a metal door. A second door answered with a hollow sound. "I used to strive to be fluent in every song," the singer said. "But I never learned to hear the spaces." Yinghe explained the difference between a characteristic of the composition and an accident of the room. They repeated the sequence three times. Every fifth beat made the hollow wall vibrate. The sound was not magical. A loose panel had been mounted over a service cavity.

羅雯請樂手不唱詞，只演奏舊旋律。節奏明明有五拍，正式樂譜卻只寫四拍。景遙直到看見舞台工人用缺少的那一拍敲金屬門，聽到另一扇門發出空洞回聲，才察覺差異。「我一直努力把每首歌練得流暢，卻沒學過聽空白。」映荷區分作品本身的節奏特徵和房間意外形成的聲響。他們重複三次，每第五拍都讓中空牆板震動。這不是魔法，只是維修空間上裝了一片鬆動面板。

The accountant returned with a contribution record from a retired engineer. The man had donated an inexpensive safety system to Studio B after a difficult fire drill. His note mentioned a prime switch that should waken two alarms at once. The company had recorded the donation but not its installation. "Someone paid for protection that nobody can find," Yinghe said. Jingyao's admiration for Qiu began to crack. He had always believed his director's stories about heroic sacrifices and glamorous opportunities. Now he wondered whether the company had defeated its own safety rules in pursuit of a smoother performance. He asked Yinghe to contact the engineer directly, without announcing the inquiry to the public.

會計帶來退休工程師的捐贈紀錄。那人曾在一次艱難消防演練後，送給B棚一套便宜的安全設備，備註寫著「主開關應同時喚醒兩個警報」。公司登記了捐贈，卻沒有安裝紀錄。「有人出錢買保護裝置，現在卻找不到它。」映荷說。景遙開始動搖對邱董事的崇拜。他過去相信導演口中的英勇犧牲與華麗機會，現在卻懷疑公司為了順暢演出，親手擊敗了安全規則。他請映荷私下聯絡工程師。

On a quiet bench, Jingyao admitted that the aquarium frightened him more than a crowded arena. Water made him remember a small hand gripping his wrist and then slipping free. His memory did not tell him whose hand it was. Yinghe resisted the temptation to motivate him with a dramatic speech. She suggested a pause, water to drink, and a choice: continue the search or leave. He chose to stay, but only until the aquarium closed. The distinction mattered. Each time he exercised control over his own time, the fear loosened a little. When the last visitors left, he found a five-beat mark scratched beneath the bench. It was the same symbol from the tape case.

坐在長椅上，景遙承認水族館比擠滿人的體育館更讓他害怕。水面讓他想起一隻小手緊抓他的手腕，後來又滑開。他記不起那是誰。映荷沒有用激昂演說逼他振作，只提出休息、喝水，以及繼續或離開的選擇。他決定留下，但只待到閉館。這個界線很重要。每次能自己決定時間，恐懼就稍微鬆動。最後一批訪客離開時，他發現長椅下刻著五拍記號，與磁帶盒上的一模一樣。

The aquarium's caretaker showed them an ordinary maintenance ledger. Each month, workers had written down the time when a pump started and stopped. The old studio had used similar equipment, yet its records contained an impossible gap during the water incident. Yinghe asked the retired engineer to compare those gaps with the sound logs. The caretaker warned that a missing entry could mean forgetfulness rather than a cover-up. Yinghe agreed. She had begun to value people who could tell her how their own evidence might be misunderstood. It made the eventual conclusion slower, but stronger.

水族館照護員拿出普通維修簿，每月都記下抽水機啟停時間。舊攝影棚使用類似設備，事故當日紀錄卻有不合理的空白。映荷請退休工程師對照音控紀錄。照護員提醒，漏填也可能只是忘記，不一定有人掩蓋。映荷同意。她愈來愈重視能主動說明自身證據限制的人。這讓結論更慢，卻更穩固。

The engineer answered the telephone that evening. He remembered designing the alarm and said he had never seen the final installation. When Yinghe mentioned the five-beat rhythm, he fell silent. "That was a test signal," he finally said. "It was never meant for an audience." He agreed to meet them the next day, but only if they brought the original contract and not Qiu's representatives. Yinghe thanked him, recorded the conditions, and hung up. Before leaving, she studied the aquarium map. A service door behind the blue tank opened toward a basement corridor. The route resembled the old painted waves so closely that she stopped thinking of the missing beat as a song at all.

工程師當晚接了電話。他記得自己設計警報，卻從未見過正式安裝。映荷提到五拍節奏時，他沉默很久。「那是測試訊號，」他終於說，「從來不是給觀眾聽的。」他願意隔天見面，條件是帶原始合約，不帶邱董事的人。映荷答應並記下要求。離開前，她查看水族館平面圖：藍色魚缸後有扇維修門通往地下走廊，路線和舊布景的四道浪太相似。她開始不再把那個缺拍當成一首歌。
