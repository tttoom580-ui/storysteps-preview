證物箱裡的手環

The Bracelet in the Evidence Bin

頂流偶像的失蹤倒數 · 第 12 / 25 章 · 3000–4000 · 英中對照 · 校訂中

用互動版查詞、朗讀與收藏

Concert doors would open at six. A former publicity agent approached Yinghe with a bin of objects removed from the old studio. Among them was a child's bracelet with four blue beads and one red bead. The agent said a burglar had stolen it years earlier, then returned it anonymously. Yinghe would not commit to that explanation without a report. She asked him to identify the person who had stored the bin. His comment was hesitant: the company's founder had ordered everything locked away after a television program threatened its reputation. Fame had made the event a public legend, but the physical objects remained ordinary things, each requiring a definite history.

演唱會六點開門。前宣傳代理人帶來一箱舊攝影棚物品，其中一只兒童手環串著四顆藍珠和一顆紅珠。代理人說多年前有小偷拿走它，後來匿名歸還。映荷沒有報案資料，不接受這說法是定論，反問誰負責存放。代理人猶豫地說，公司創辦人因某個電視節目威脅聲譽，下令把一切鎖起來。名聲讓事件變成公共傳說，但具體物品仍是普通物件，各自需要明確來源。

A young reporter pushed through the hallway and demanded to know why the singer continued pursuing a tragic memory. Yinghe described the hardship of the people involved without revealing their addresses. Some had lived through difficult economic inflation and did not want another media campaign to occupy their lives. The reporter apologized for the intrusion but asked whether Lan Cen was dead. "We have no verified information about her present location," Yinghe replied. "That is the only honest answer." She noticed the bracelet had been repaired with a durable knot different from the factory cord. Someone had cared enough to mend it after the supposed accident. That could mean its owner had survived, but it could not yet identify who.

年輕記者擠進走廊，追問景遙為何繼續追查悲劇回憶。映荷說明當事人承受的艱難，卻不揭露住址。有些人熬過經濟通膨與困頓，不想再被媒體占據生活。記者為闖入道歉，仍問藍岑是否已死。「我們沒有她目前所在地的可靠資料。」映荷回答。「這是唯一誠實的答案。」她發現手環曾用不同材質的結重新修好。有人在所謂事故後珍惜地修補它，可能表示主人還活著，卻無法指出是誰。

The agent mentioned a tribal dance festival where the children's show once performed. Lan Cen had taught the group a step based on a local cultural tradition, with permission from its instructor. The recording was eventually removed because management considered it inconvenient to credit the original teachers. Yinghe wrote down the instructor's name for later verification. She did not want the investigation to become another form of destruction, taking cultural work out of its context. The reporter offered to help pursue the old festival program and promised not to publish rumors. Yinghe accepted the offer but requested that all factual claims be checked with their subjects before release.

代理人提到，兒童節目曾參加地方部族舞蹈節。藍岑在取得老師許可後，教大家跳一段來自當地傳統的步伐，錄影後來被刪，因為管理層嫌註明原老師太麻煩。映荷記下老師姓名，準備再查。她不希望調查又變成另一種破壞，把文化勞動從原脈絡奪走。記者提出幫忙找舊節目單，也答應不刊登謠言。映荷同意，但要求發布前讓當事人查證事實主張。

The emergency procedure binder held a surprise. An employee had filed a report describing a young girl emerging from the lower studio after water had entered it. She was carrying another child's bracelet and requesting medical help. The report had never reached the official accident summary. A supervisor had added the words "no action necessary," though the form lacked a signature. Yinghe copied the page and asked the venue officer to certify its condition. Jingyao read the report twice. The girl had been alive after the water incident. The truth was still distant, but one central fear had finally lost its power: Lan Cen had not vanished into the flooded stage that night.

緊急程序資料夾裡出現驚人紀錄：一名員工回報，有年輕女孩在進水後從地下攝影棚走出，手裡拿著另一個孩子的手環，要求醫療協助。這份報告從未進入正式事故摘要。主管補上一句「無需處理」，卻沒有簽名。映荷複印文件，請場館主管認證原件狀態。景遙讀了兩遍。至少那天進水後，藍岑還活著。真相仍遠，但一個長久的恐懼終於失去力量：她沒有在當晚被淹的舞台裡消失。

The former publicity agent explained how memory of an era becomes a story. In the 1990s, the company had promoted every children's program as a perfect family dream. It was easy to remove a mistake from an advertisement, then later forget that the advertisement was not history. Yinghe asked him to identify which materials were publicity copies and which were original production records. He could distinguish several by their paper and stamps. The work was slow, but the difference mattered. One cheerful poster did not cancel a real child's distress, just as one frightening newspaper headline could not prove a death.

前宣傳代理人說明一個年代的記憶如何成為故事。九〇年代公司把每個兒童節目都宣傳成完美家庭夢。從廣告刪掉錯誤很容易，久而久之大家竟忘記廣告不是歷史。映荷請他辨別宣傳複本和原始製作紀錄，他從紙質與印章認出幾份。工作很慢，差異卻重要：一張快樂海報不能抹掉孩子的恐懼，一條可怕新聞標題也不能證明死亡。

At five, Jingyao stood by his dressing table and removed the old silver ring Qiu had told him to wear for luck. He replaced it with the repaired bracelet, borrowed with permission for the performance. Yinghe asked whether it would cause him pain to see the object onstage. He admitted it might, and she reminded him that he could change his mind. He said he wanted to carry a question, not display a trophy. Outside, the audience was gathering, faithful to the performer they believed they knew. Inside, he was beginning to understand that being faithful to an absent friend required listening to records that did not flatter him.

五點時，景遙摘下邱董事要求他戴的幸運銀戒，換上經同意借來的舊手環。映荷問看到它會不會痛苦，他承認可能會。她提醒他隨時能改變主意。他說自己想帶著一個問題上台，而不是展示勝利獎盃。外頭歌迷聚集，忠實支持他們以為認識的歌手。裡頭的他開始明白，忠於失聯朋友不是讓自己看起來更好，而是願意聆聽那些不會討好他的紀錄。
