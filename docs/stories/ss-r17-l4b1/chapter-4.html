搬不動的舞台

The Stage That Could Not Be Moved

頂流偶像的失蹤倒數 · 第 4 / 25 章 · 3000–4000 · 英中對照 · 校訂中

用互動版查詞、朗讀與收藏

Six days remained. At an international music reception, Jingyao was expected to accompany a diplomat and sing beneath a magnificent painted horizon. The stage attraction was an experimental moving platform, recently imported with its manual written in another language. Yinghe had a mechanic review the diagram. The technician immediately found that the emergency handle could not be reached when the platform rose. Qiu called it an outstanding design and ordered the rehearsal to continue. Yinghe felt a moment of hesitation. Then she asked the mechanic to loosen the safety cover, inspect the cable, and stop the machine. She had no interest in testing whether frightened performers could flee quickly enough.

還剩六天。國際音樂酒會上，景遙預計陪同一名外交官在壯麗的地平線布景下獻唱。舞台的賣點是一座新進口的實驗性升降台，操作手冊卻只有外文。映荷找機械技師看圖，技師立刻發現平台升高時摸不到緊急把手。邱董事稱這是傑出設計，命令繼續排練。映荷猶豫一瞬，隨即請技師鬆開安全罩、檢查鋼索並停止機器。她沒有興趣測試受驚表演者能跑多快。

Jingyao's greeting to the guests was brief. Instead of taking the stage, he invited the school orchestra to perform from the floor. The change strengthened the children’s confidence and gave the staff time to inspect the equipment. From a booth behind the reception hall, Yinghe discovered an old satellite video feed labeled STUDIO B. The images contained a curtain painted with four waves. A little girl was trying to explain a problem while an adult waved her away. The sound was missing, and the image ended before the children's performance. Yinghe resisted the attraction of inventing words for the silent girl. She asked for the uncut recording and kept the still frame as an essential reference.

景遙向來賓簡短問候，卻沒有站上升降台，而是邀請學生管弦樂隊在地板上演出。這個改動讓孩子們更有信心，也給工作人員檢修時間。映荷在酒會後方找到一捲標示「B棚」的舊衛星轉播影像，裡面有畫著四道浪的布幕。一名女孩急著向大人說明問題，卻被揮手趕走。影像沒有聲音，還沒到兒童表演就被切斷。映荷沒有替無聲女孩編造台詞，而是要求看完整原帶，把截圖當成重要參考。

A diplomat who had once visited the television station remembered that filming had stopped after a minor earthquake. A worker had reported germs growing in a flooded storage area, but management called it a recreation problem and reopened the site too soon. Another guest disagreed about the earthquake's timing. Yinghe wrote both accounts separately, without treating either as final. At the end of the night the mechanic showed her the new platform's draft approval sheet. The signature was identical to one on an old studio B document, though thirteen years separated them. Qiu said the engineer had stayed in the business. Perhaps that was true. The question was why the same unreachable handle kept appearing.

一位曾參觀電視台的外交官記得，當年拍攝因小地震停過工。有人回報淹水庫房裡滋生細菌，管理層卻當成休閒空間的小問題，太早開放場地。另一位賓客對地震時間有不同說法。映荷將證言分開記錄，不把任何一份當結論。夜末，技師拿來升降台的草案核准表，簽名竟與十三年前B棚文件相同。邱董事說那名工程師一直在業界工作。或許如此，但為什麼同樣摸不到的停止把手一次次出現？

Jingyao asked what would happen to the concert if every imported attraction failed inspection. Yinghe answered that they could sing without them. "Music does not need a trapdoor to be music," she said. He smiled for the first time that week, then looked toward the empty stage with concern. "When I was fourteen, I heard someone under a stage shouting my name. I thought it was part of the scene." She asked whether he could describe the voice without forcing a guess. It had been young, frightened, and oddly calm at the end. The description did not identify the speaker, but it suggested that someone had stayed behind after the audience left.

景遙問，如果所有進口舞台裝置都不合格，演唱會怎麼辦。映荷說可以不用它們唱歌。「音樂不需要陷阱門也能成立。」她說。他第一次露出笑容，隨即不安地望著空台。「十四歲那年，我聽到有人在舞台下叫我的名字。我以為是戲的一部分。」映荷請他描述聲音，不必猜是誰。那聲音年輕、驚恐，最後卻異常冷靜。這無法辨認說話者，卻顯示觀眾離去後，可能還有人留在地下。

During a brief break, Yinghe asked the mechanic to explain the original two-switch system to the youngest stage assistants. He used a model made of paper, a rubber band, and two wooden buttons. The model made clear why a beautiful machine could still be unsafe. One assistant had previously been told that only senior staff were allowed to ask questions about the platform. Yinghe changed that instruction on the crew notice. Any person could report a risk, and no report would be treated as an interruption to art. Jingyao signed the notice because it applied to him as well.

短暫休息時，映荷請技師向最年輕的舞台助理解釋雙開關，技師用紙、橡皮筋和兩顆木鈕做模型，讓大家看懂華麗機器也可能不安全。一名助理說以前被告知，只有資深人員能問平台問題。映荷立刻修改內部公告：任何人都可通報風險，不能把通報當成妨礙藝術。景遙也簽名，因為規則同樣適用於他。

Before dawn, a copy of the studio's original manual appeared beneath Yinghe's hotel door. Its pages were tied with red thread. A penciled diagram showed two switches, one above the platform and one below. Both had to be held to move the stage. Someone had crossed out the lower switch and written a single word: CHEAPER. In the margin was a small mark shaped like the number four. The envelope carried no threat and no return address. Yinghe did not know whether its sender wished to help or steer her toward a false story. She photographed each page and called the mechanic again. The deadline was approaching, but she would not sacrifice a test for an answer.

天亮前，有人把B棚原始操作手冊塞到映荷旅館門下，紙頁用紅線綁著。鉛筆圖示出上下兩個開關，必須同時按住才能移動平台。有人把下方開關劃掉，寫上「比較便宜」。頁邊還有一個形似四的記號。信封沒有威脅，也沒有回郵地址。映荷不確定寄件者是幫忙，還是故意導向錯誤故事。她逐頁拍照，再打給技師。期限逼近，她仍不願為了答案犧牲測試。
