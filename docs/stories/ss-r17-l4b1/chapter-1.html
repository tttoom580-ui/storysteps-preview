安可前的信封

The Envelope Before the Encore

頂流偶像的失蹤倒數 · 第 1 / 25 章 · 3000–4000 · 英中對照 · 校訂中

In October 1999, Su Yinghe arrived at the Taipei press presentation of Asia's most famous singer with a borrowed briefcase and a headache. At thirty-four, she had managed theater crews, not idols. Her new client, twenty-seven-year-old Lin Jingyao, had sold out six cities in three weeks. A tremendous screen showed his face above the reporters. Yet the fierce security guards looked less like a defense against fans than a wall against questions. His company director tried to flatter the crowd with a speech about diversity and spiritual connection. Yinghe opened her file. Its first page contained a written violation of fire rules from a previous show. Nobody had bothered to sign the correction.

一九九九年十月，三十四歲的蘇映荷拿著借來的公事包，帶著頭痛走進台北的記者發表會。她以前管理劇場工作人員，從沒帶過偶像。新接手的藝人林景遙二十七歲，三週內已在六座城市售罄門票。巨大螢幕投出他的臉，兇悍的保全卻不像防範歌迷，更像在阻止發問。公司董事用多元與精神連結的漂亮話討好媒體。映荷翻開文件，第一頁竟是上一場演出違反消防規定的報告，改善欄始終沒有人簽字。

A messenger brought a white envelope to the stage door. Inside lay a paper parachute, an old reference photograph, and a line in Jingyao's handwriting: "When the final encore begins, I will die." Someone had written NINE DAYS in red beneath it. Jingyao stared at the note without a giggle or a gasp. He said the parachute came from a children's television show filmed in a mountain cottage. "I don't possess that photograph anymore," he whispered. "And I never sent this." Yinghe noticed a cushion of fresh ink beneath the folded corner, as if the sender had pressed the page against a soft surface. She photographed both sides before anyone could take it away.

一名送件員把白信封送到後台。裡面有紙降落傘、一張舊參考照片，以及一行看似景遙筆跡的話：「最後一次安可開始時，我就會死去。」下方有人用紅筆寫著「九天」。景遙盯著紙張，沒有笑也沒有驚呼，只說那把降落傘出自一部在山間小屋拍的兒童節目。「那張照片早就不在我手上了。」他低聲說。「信也不是我寄的。」映荷發現折角下的墨痕像是在軟墊上書寫留下的壓印，立刻將正反兩面拍照存證。

Director Qiu called it a publicity genius's trick. He wanted to turn the threat into a visual countdown on the tour website. Yinghe refused. "A person's possible death is not a presentation theme," she said. Jingyao glanced at her, surprised that his new manager would oppose the man who paid her salary. Qiu was pessimistic about replacing the publicity plan. He ordered Jingyao through a workout and a full vocal rehearsal anyway. Yinghe watched him miss a step he had performed hundreds of times. His hands trembled whenever the band's clock reached eleven seventeen. She did not diagnose him. She simply marked the exact time and asked whether he wished to stop.

邱董事說那不過是宣傳天才的把戲，還想把威脅變成巡演網站上的視覺倒數。映荷拒絕：「一個人可能死去，不是發表會主題。」景遙訝異地看她，沒想到新經紀人敢頂撞付薪水的人。邱董事悲觀地表示不做倒數就無法宣傳，仍命令景遙完成體能訓練與整場排練。映荷發現他在熟悉的舞步上出錯，樂隊時鐘跳到十一點十七分時雙手就發抖。她沒有擅自診斷，只記下時間，問他是否想先停下來。

After the reporters left, a witness appeared: an elderly costume assistant named Aunt Mei. She recognized the paper parachute from a television sequence filmed when Jingyao was fourteen. "He used to run around that set with a girl called Lan Cen," she said. "One scene was cut after a terrible accident." Qiu immediately interrupted, but Mei had already conveyed one more detail. The original prop had been stitched with a blue thread. This one carried red. Yinghe examined it under the dressing room lamp and found a tiny number four on the knot. It was neither random decoration nor an artist's signature. The singer looked suddenly sick. "There were four children in that scene," he said. "Only three are in the photograph."

記者離開後，資深服裝助理梅姨認出了紙降落傘。那是景遙十四歲時拍攝的兒童節目道具，當年有個叫藍岑的女孩和他一起在攝影棚奔跑。「出事後，有一幕被剪掉了。」梅姨說。邱董事立刻打斷，但她已經說出另一個細節：原道具用藍線縫，這一個卻是紅線。映荷在化妝間燈下發現線結上有個小小的「四」，不像裝飾，也不是畫師簽名。景遙臉色發白：「那個場景裡原本有四個孩子。照片上只剩三個。」

Before the rehearsal ended, Yinghe examined the press-room seating chart and found that the visual countdown had already been purchased as an advertising package. It had been approved a week before she joined the company. Qiu's claim that it was a spontaneous response to the letter could not be true. She added the invoice to her notes and asked the accounts department to retain the original. Jingyao sat beside her with a paper cup of water. "Does that prove he sent the note?" he asked. "No," she replied. "It proves someone expected a countdown before you knew about one."

排練結束前，映荷檢查記者室座位圖，發現視覺倒數早在她入職前一週就被購買成廣告方案。邱董事聲稱收到威脅信才臨時想到宣傳，顯然不符時間。她把發票加入筆記，請會計留存原件。景遙坐在旁邊喝水，問：「這證明他寄信嗎？」映荷回答：「不。只證明有人在你知道倒數前就預料會有倒數。」

Yinghe made a simple choice before leaving. She would not announce a scandal, and she would not hand the note back to Qiu. She placed the original in a sealed bag, gave Jingyao a copy, and invited Mei to witness the record. The singer asked whether she believed him. "I believe this envelope exists," she replied. "That is where we start." Beyond the theater, fans were singing his newest love song beneath a tremendous billboard. Behind them a television truck replayed an old concert image: Jingyao opening his arms as a paper parachute fell. For one frame, a fourth shadow crossed the screen. Yinghe froze the tape and counted the shadows twice.

離開前，映荷先做了最簡單的決定：不公開尚未查明的醜聞，也不把原信交還邱董事。她將原物封袋，給景遙影本，請梅姨在紀錄上見證。景遙問她相不相信自己。「我相信這封信確實存在。」她回答。「我們從這裡開始。」劇院外，歌迷在巨型廣告牌下唱著景遙的新歌。旁邊電視車播放舊演唱會畫面：景遙張開雙臂，紙降落傘飄下。只有一格影像裡，螢幕上出現第四個人的影子。映荷倒帶，數了兩次。
