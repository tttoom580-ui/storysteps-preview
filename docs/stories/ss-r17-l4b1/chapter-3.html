缺席的第四個孩子

The Photograph With No Fourth Child

頂流偶像的失蹤倒數 · 第 3 / 25 章 · 3000–4000 · 英中對照 · 校訂中

Seven days before the concert, Yinghe was assigned to a ferry performance for charity sponsors. The location looked luxurious in photographs, but the narrow exit had a maximum capacity of forty people. She made safety the first priority. A lifeguard stood by the ramp, and a technician checked the equipment before they could proceed. Qiu called her pace insulting. "I have an incredible audience waiting," he complained. Yinghe answered with a percentage from the passenger list: almost one third were children. No photograph of a singer was worth putting them at risk. She reduced the guest number, then asked the deck captain to defend the decision in writing if management challenged him.

演唱會前七天，映荷被指派處理一場渡輪慈善演出。照片看起來豪華，狹窄出口卻最多只能容納四十人。她把安全列為首要，請救生員守在斜坡旁，設備檢查完成才讓活動繼續。邱董事抱怨她的步調是在侮辱貴賓：「我有多了不起的觀眾等著！」映荷拿出乘客資料：接近三分之一是兒童。沒有任何明星照片值得冒險。她先縮減人數，再請船長書面支持決定。

The charity program included photography from Jingyao's childhood, provided by the company's archive. A picture of four smiling children had been cropped until only three remained. The empty space at the edge was covered by a decorative raisin-brown border. Jingyao could not concentrate on his assigned song. He kept tracing that border with one finger. "We had to share the housework on set," he said abruptly. "Lan Cen was the one who made sure the youngest child ate his lunch." He remembered a packet of raisins she carried in her pocket. The detail seemed small, but it explained why she had been beside the younger actors when the alarm sounded.

慈善節目展示公司典藏的童年照片。原本四名孩子笑著合影，卻被裁成三人，空白邊緣覆上一條葡萄乾色的花框。景遙無法專注被分派的歌曲，一直摸著那條邊。「當時拍戲還要分工做家事。」他突然說。「藍岑會盯著最小的孩子吃午餐。」他記得她口袋裡總有一包葡萄乾。那不是大線索，卻能解釋警報響起時，她為什麼站在小演員身旁。

Yinghe requested the original negative. The archive supervisor claimed it was an intermediate print, with no value beyond publicity. A retired photographer disagreed. "Look at the context," she said. "The shadow falls across the missing child, not away from her." She found an old contact sheet and revealed that the fourth child was a boy named Shen Yu, who was never listed in the final credits. The girl Lan Cen stood behind him, one hand on his shoulder. In the corner, a sign showed the same four waves from the sea set. The photographer's inspiration had been a painting on the studio wall, not a planned camera trick. That wall, she believed, still existed.

映荷要求看原始底片，檔案主管卻說只是中間沖印版本，沒有宣傳以外的價值。退休攝影師不同意：「看照片的脈絡。影子是落在被刪掉的孩子身上，不是背向她。」她找出接觸印樣，證實第四個孩子是未列入片尾的沈宇；藍岑站在他身後，手扶他的肩。角落標誌正是海景布幕的四道浪。攝影師拍照的靈感來自棚內牆畫，不是刻意安排的鏡頭技巧，而那面牆也許還在。

During the return crossing, a reporter shouted that Jingyao had lied about the past. The insult made him go pale. Yinghe refused to argue on the boat. Instead she asked whether he wanted the public to know they were checking the record. He said yes, but not the children's names yet. She issued a short statement: the company was reviewing archival photography and would not speculate about anyone's whereabouts. She also canceled an unnecessary calorie-counting press challenge that a sponsor had suggested. Jingyao needed food, sleep, and control over his schedule. For the first time he chose a quiet seat over the camera's most favorable angle.

回航時，一名記者高喊景遙對往事說謊。這句侮辱讓他臉色發白。映荷不在船上爭吵，而是問他願不願公開正在查證。他同意，但暫時不要公布孩子姓名。於是她發布簡短聲明：公司正重查典藏照片，不會猜測任何人的去向。她也取消了贊助商提出的卡路里挑戰宣傳。景遙需要的是正常飲食、睡眠，以及對行程的控制。那一刻，他第一次選擇安靜座位，而非鏡頭最漂亮的角度。

On the ferry's upper deck, a young assistant admitted that she had been ordered to remove the fourth child's name from a display. She had never met that child, and she did not want to be blamed for the earlier accident. Yinghe thanked her for being clear about what she knew. They photographed the original instruction while leaving the assistant's private information out of the public record. Jingyao watched the dark water and asked why so many small edits had been made in different departments. "Because people followed parts of a story," Yinghe said, "without seeing who controlled the whole."

渡輪上層甲板，一名年輕助理坦承自己曾被命令刪除展板上的第四個孩子姓名。她沒見過那孩子，也不希望因多年前事故被怪罪。映荷感謝她清楚說出所知範圍，拍下原始指示，卻不在公開紀錄中放個資。景遙望著黑色水面，問為何不同部門各做了小修改。映荷說：「因為每個人只看見故事的一部分，沒看見是誰在控制全貌。」

The final clue arrived just before the ferry docked. Mei called to say she had found a costume label sewn in a child's coat: S. YU, STAGE B. Beneath it was a hand-drawn map of the four waves. It did not show the camera position; it showed a route to a staircase. Yinghe put the label beside the photograph and saw the pattern at last. Each concert prop, each missing sound, and each altered picture pointed toward the same room. They had an intermediate answer, not the truth. Somewhere in the city stood a wall that four children had once been ordered to walk through. Who had closed it, and why?

渡輪靠岸前，梅姨打電話說在童裝外套裡找到布標：「S. YU，B舞台。」布標背面畫著四道浪和一條通往樓梯的路線，不是攝影機位置。映荷把布標和照片放在一起，終於看出規律：每一件演唱會道具、每一段遺失聲音、每一張遭修改的照片，都指向同一間房。這只是中途答案，還不是真相。城市某處有面牆，四個孩子曾被要求走進去。是誰關上它，又為什麼？
