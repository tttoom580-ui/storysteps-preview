只有一人聽見的提示

The Cue Only One Person Heard

頂流偶像的失蹤倒數 · 第 8 / 25 章 · 3000–4000 · 英中對照 · 校訂中

用互動版查詞、朗讀與收藏

Three days remained. The retired engineer lived above a small music shop near the gulf-side road. He described how his experimental software had been built to prevent a moving stage from operating without two human confirmations. The composer had created an audible cue for workers who could not see the switches. In the company's version, the cue was buried under applause. The engineer estimated that the system had been changed before its first proper test. "Two signals constitute one approval," he said, tracing a curve in the air. "One person cannot dominate the machine." His explanation had the practical intensity of someone who had spent years fearing that the original design might fail.

倒數剩三天。退休工程師住在海灣公路旁的樂器店樓上。他解釋，當年實驗性軟體的目的，是讓升降舞台必須經過兩人確認才能運轉；作曲家設計了一個聲音提示，供看不到開關的工人使用。公司版本卻把提示埋進掌聲。他估計系統在正式測試前就遭修改。「兩個訊號才構成一次核准。」他在空中畫了一條弧線。「一個人不能主宰機器。」那口氣像多年來一直害怕自己設計失效的人。

Yinghe received a bulletin from the venue authority: the main concert was still scheduled, but a separate equipment review would begin at dawn. Qiu called the decision a rebellion against artistic freedom. He accused the engineer of jealousy toward a successful tour. The engineer did not answer. Instead he showed a learned article describing how an abstract warning can be mistaken for a decorative sound. The article discussed gender differences in who was permitted to operate controls, because only senior men had held the keys in the old studio. Lan Cen had once asked why the warning could not be heard by everyone. The question was recorded; the answer had been cut.

映荷接到場館管理機關公告：主演唱會仍照常排期，但清晨要另行審查設備。邱董事稱這是在反抗藝術自由，還指控工程師嫉妒巡演成功。工程師沒有反駁，拿出一篇學術文章，說明抽象警示聲如何被當成裝飾音效。文章也討論誰有權操作控制器的性別問題，因為舊攝影棚只有資深男性持有鑰匙。藍岑曾問警報為何不能讓所有人聽見。這個問題留下了紀錄，答案卻被剪掉。

In the engineer's cabinet lay an invitation to an old wedding. The bridegroom, who had worked as a camera operator, was Chen Shuo's older brother. On the back was a note that made Yinghe pause: "If the lights go dark, Chen knows the second switch." This did not mean Chen had used it. It meant somebody knew he could. The technician explained that the second switch was meant to protect civilians, not only performers. A systematic test required both channels to stay visible. When one channel was removed, a single press could move the platform while another worker was still beneath it. That was the danger the absent cue had concealed.

工程師櫃子裡放著舊婚禮邀請函，新郎曾是攝影師，也是陳碩的哥哥。背面一句話讓映荷停住：「燈暗時，陳知道第二個開關在哪裡。」這不代表陳曾操作，只代表有人知道他有能力操作。工程師說第二開關是用來保護所有在場平民，不只表演者。完整測試必須讓兩個通道都看得見。拿掉其中一個後，一人按鍵就能移動舞台，即使另一人仍在底下。失去的聲音提示掩蓋的正是這個危險。

Jingyao asked whether the rehearsal accident could have been caused by an invasion of the studio computer. The engineer said that software failure was possible, but they had not acquired logs that proved it. An even simpler explanation was a deliberate shortcut by someone managing the schedule. Yinghe was grateful for his refusal to claim certainty. Outside, a group of fans waited with homemade signs. Their enthusiasm was real, but a rumor had begun to spread that Lan Cen was secretly Jingyao's bride. Yinghe issued a correction: there was no evidence for such a story. The girl in the tape deserved a history, not an invented romance designed to sell tickets.

景遙問當年的事故是否可能有人入侵電腦。工程師說軟體故障確實可能，但目前沒有取得足以證明的紀錄；更簡單的解釋，也可能是排程人員故意走捷徑。映荷欣賞他不假裝確定。樓下有歌迷拿著自製標語等候，他們的熱情真實，卻有人開始散播藍岑其實是景遙祕密新娘的傳言。映荷立刻澄清：沒有證據支持。帶子裡的女孩值得有真實人生，而不是為了賣票被編造戀情。

A school music teacher joined the conversation after seeing the five-note drawing. She explained that the studio's old courtyard was fertile ground for rumors because its walls carried unusual echoes. Children had once imagined that ghosts could invade the building through the speakers. The engineer laughed softly, then reminded everyone that the alarm tones had been designed precisely because echoes were unreliable. Yinghe asked the teacher to demonstrate the difference with a piano and a handheld recorder. One tone bounced twice; the other stopped cleanly. The test gave the team a way to distinguish a recording from a nearby voice.

一名學校音樂老師看見五音圖也加入討論。她說舊攝影棚庭院回音奇特，是謠言滋生的肥沃土壤，當年孩子甚至想像鬼能透過喇叭入侵。工程師輕笑，提醒大家警示音就是因為回音不可靠才設計的。映荷請老師用鋼琴與手持錄音機示範：一個音反彈兩次，另一個乾淨停下。這個測試讓團隊有辦法區分錄音與附近真人的聲音。

The engineer finally handed Yinghe a drawing of the original sound sequence. It had five notes. The fifth was a short, high tone meant to identify the lower switch. He asked her to compare the drawing against every surviving performance recording. "Someone left a path through the edits," he said. At the music shop door, Jingyao received a second countdown note. This one said THREE DAYS and included a photograph of his empty dressing room. It had been taken that morning. Unlike the earlier letters, its paper smelled sharply of the company's new stage paint. Yinghe realized the threat writer had access to the current tour, not merely old memories.

工程師終於交給映荷原始聲響圖，有五個音，最後一個短促高音用來辨識下方開關。他要她比對所有留下的演出錄音。「有人在剪接裡保留了一條路。」走到樂器店門口，景遙收到第二封倒數信：「三天」，附上一張他今天早晨空化妝間的照片。與之前不同，紙張帶著公司新舞台油漆的刺鼻味。映荷意識到發威脅的人能進入現在的巡演，不只是掌握舊回憶。
