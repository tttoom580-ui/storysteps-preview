洗不掉的顏色

The Color That Would Not Wash Out

頂流偶像的失蹤倒數 · 第 6 / 25 章 · 3000–4000 · 英中對照 · 校訂中

Four days remained. The costume room smelled of warm linen and strong blue dye. Yinghe had asked to inspect the goods before the final television appearance. The company consultant called the examination conservative and unnecessary; in his view, a luxury costume needed only to look expensive. Mei disagreed. One sleeve had been cleaned with an unfamiliar chemical and left a mark that a microscope could reveal. She enclosed a fabric sample in a clean envelope. The singer's habitual habit of gripping his sleeve had rubbed the dye onto his fingers. Yinghe isolated the costume without accusing the wardrobe staff. "I won't punish an occupation for a management decision," she said. "I want to know who ordered the change."

還剩四天。服裝間瀰漫溫熱亞麻與深藍染料的味道。映荷要求檢查最後一次電視亮相的服裝。公司顧問說她太保守，奢華戲服只要看起來昂貴就夠了。梅姨不認同：一隻袖子曾被陌生藥劑清洗，顯微鏡下仍有痕跡。她把布樣封進乾淨信封。景遙習慣緊抓袖口，手指因而沾上藍色。映荷先隔離服裝，沒有責怪服裝組：「我不會為管理層的決定懲罰整個職業團隊。我要知道誰下令更換。」

Qiu arrived in a rage. He said the sponsor had invested heavily in the costume and the studio could not afford to replace it. "You are ignorant of consumer expectations," he told Yinghe. She requested the purchase order instead of answering the insult. No chemical name appeared on it. The document listed a new supplier but no orientation briefing for the cleaning team. Mei, usually timid in meetings, held up her stained gloves. Her hands began to tremble, yet her voice remained steady. "I cleaned the coat according to instructions," she said. "Someone changed those instructions after I signed." The revised sheet had been printed from the same computer used for the stage countdown.

邱董事怒氣沖沖地闖進來，說贊助商已投資巨款，不能重做戲服，還罵映荷不懂消費者期待。映荷沒有還嘴，只要求看採購單。上面沒有化學品名稱，倒是列出新供應商，清潔人員卻未接受任何職前說明。平常開會很膽小的梅姨舉起染色手套，雙手發抖，聲音仍穩定：「我照指示洗了衣服，但有人在我簽名後更動指示。」新版本竟由製作舞台倒數的同一部電腦列印。

The wardrobe team showed Yinghe a stereo rehearsal tape that had been stored with the supplier papers. On the left channel was the usual music. On the right, an adult voice quietly counted the children before each take: one, two, three, four. Then a second voice cut in: "Take four off the list." Jingyao leaned against a doorframe. He did not know whether the command referred to a child or a camera take. Yinghe would not let the ambiguity become a verdict. She checked the recording date and found that it came from two weeks before the alleged accident. Someone had wanted the fourth number removed before the incident everybody remembered.

服裝組拿來一捲與供應商文件放在一起的立體聲排練帶。左聲道是普通配樂，右聲道有人在每次拍攝前數孩子：一、二、三、四。另一個成人聲音突然插入：「把第四個從名單上拿掉。」景遙靠著門框，無法確定那是在說孩子還是第四次拍攝。映荷不允許模糊話語直接變成判決。她查出錄音日期竟比傳說中的事故早兩週：有人在事故前就想拿掉那個「四」。

Luo Wen visited with a stack of linen-backed music sheets. They had been purchased as an investment in the children's series, and each page carried an original production number. One number had been carefully erased. Yinghe's request for a replacement costume had unexpectedly opened an older record. She asked Mei whether she remembered a young worker named Chen Shuo. Mei said he had cleaned both the concert stage and the television studio. "He was graceful with frightened children," she recalled. "When the lights went out, he knew where the spare lamps were." The name was absent from every company history, though he might have witnessed the critical scene.

羅雯帶來一疊用亞麻裱襯的樂譜，那是公司當年投資兒童節目時購買的，每張都印有原始製作編號，其中一個卻被仔細擦去。換戲服的要求意外打開更舊的紀錄。映荷問梅姨記不記得清潔員陳碩。梅姨說他同時清理演唱會與電視棚，擅長安撫受驚孩子。「斷電時，他知道備用燈在哪裡。」然而公司歷史裡沒有這個人，儘管他可能親眼看見最重要的一幕。

Mei handled the disagreement with surprising grace. Rather than accuse the youngest wardrobe worker, she asked everyone to place their written instructions side by side. Three versions existed, each dated differently. The most recent told staff to use an unfamiliar cleaning solution, but the supplier had not provided a safety sheet. Yinghe thanked Mei for showing the differences without humiliating anyone. Jingyao realized he had never once asked who washed the costumes he wore. He asked to meet the team after the show, not to offer empty praise but to learn what support their work required.

梅姨用出乎意料的從容處理爭議，沒有指責最年輕的服裝員，而請大家把書面指示並排。居然有三個不同日期版本，最新一張要求使用陌生洗劑，供應商卻沒附安全說明。映荷感謝她指出差異而不羞辱人。景遙忽然發現，自己從未問過穿的戲服是誰洗的，便提出節目後和團隊見面，不是空泛讚美，而是了解他們需要何種支援。

That night, the team reached an agreement with the cleaning workers. Nobody would be asked to enter a dangerous area or reveal a private address for the investigation. They would collect payroll names and publicly available schedules first. Jingyao removed the dyed coat and chose a plain cotton shirt for the television show. The sponsor protested, but the audience responded to his voice rather than the cloth. When he sang "return" in place of "remain," the lights in the studio briefly changed from blue to white. Behind the control desk, a technician covered a small switch with his hand. Yinghe saw the motion. She had found another person who knew what the altered lyric could trigger.

當晚，團隊和清潔人員達成共識：任何人都不必為調查進入危險空間，也不會被要求公開私人住址，先收集薪資名冊與公開班表。景遙脫下染色戲服，改穿簡單棉衫上節目。贊助商抗議，觀眾卻仍為歌聲歡呼。他把「留下」唱成「回去」時，攝影棚燈光短暫由藍轉白。控制桌後一名技師用手蓋住小開關。映荷看見了：又有一個人知道歌詞改動會觸發什麼。
