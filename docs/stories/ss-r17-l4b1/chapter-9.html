幕後的口音

The Accent Behind the Curtain

頂流偶像的失蹤倒數 · 第 9 / 25 章 · 3000–4000 · 英中對照 · 校訂中

用互動版查詞、朗讀與收藏

Two days before the concert, Yinghe discovered a small attachment to a rehearsal report. It described a sound technician with an unfamiliar accent who had instructed staff to remove one earphone from their monitoring set. "The audience won't notice," the note said. Jingyao frowned. He could remember an interruption during an old broadcast, when someone spoke in the accent of his mother's homeland. He leaned toward the monitor to listen again. The voice was clumsy and nervous, not the confident order heard on the official track. Yinghe suspected that two people had been speaking at once. Mixing the channels might have misled everyone about which person had given the command.

演唱會前兩天，映荷在排練報告附件裡發現一句話：一名帶陌生口音的音控師指示大家拿掉監聽耳機的一側，理由是「觀眾不會發現」。景遙皺眉，想起舊轉播曾被打斷，有人用和母親故鄉相似的口音說話。他俯身聽聲音，發現那個人笨拙而緊張，並不是正式錄音裡自信下令的人。映荷懷疑兩人當時同時說話，混音可能讓大家誤會誰發出指示。

The economic pressure on the tour was obvious. Qiu showed a chart of ticket sales and warned that a refund would bankrupt the company. Yinghe insisted on modesty about what they knew. They had not established that the concert would fail, only that an important device remained untested. She requested an independent inspection and a clear refund policy if the show had to change. When Qiu peeped at her notebook, she closed it and asked him to request documents formally. He winked as if the matter were a game. She did not smile. The notes included private accounts from workers who had not agreed to speak publicly.

巡演承受的經濟壓力十分明顯。邱董事拿出票房圖表，警告退款會讓公司破產。映荷堅持誠實面對未知：目前沒有證明演唱會會失敗，只證明重要設備尚未測試。她要求獨立檢查與清楚退款方案。邱董事偷瞄她筆記本時，她合上本子，請他正式申請資料。他眨眼像在玩遊戲，她沒有笑。筆記裡有尚未同意公開身分的工作人員證言。

A radio host described the missing-child investigation as revolutionary, then asked if Jingyao intended to confess a crime. Yinghe corrected the false choice. Looking into an event did not mean confessing or accusing anyone. Their current content was a set of conflicting records, not a completed case. Jingyao took the microphone and said he remembered fear but not certainty. "Please don't make other people carry my guesses," he added. His quiet sentence surprised the audience more than a dramatic apology would have. Several fans later wrote that they had felt trusted rather than manipulated. Yinghe collected those reactions but refused to use them as a substitute for evidence.

電台主持人把調查形容成革命性揭露，接著問景遙是否準備認罪。映荷指出那是錯誤二選一：調查事件不等於承認或指控任何人。現在只有相互衝突的紀錄，還不是完整案件。景遙接過麥克風，說他記得害怕，卻不敢說自己確定。「請不要讓別人替我的猜測負責。」那句平靜的話比戲劇化道歉更讓人驚訝。許多歌迷說因此感到被信任。映荷收下回應，但不拿它代替證據。

Late that afternoon, a cleaning worker named Chen Shuo finally agreed to speak. He was not the villain of the old story. In 1993 he had been nineteen and frightened of losing his job. He had heard the warning cue but was told to keep a door shut because cameras were recording. His circumstances had left him little power, though he still regretted not calling for help sooner. He remembered Lan Cen leading Shen Yu through a service passage while the rest of the children were rushed toward the front. Then someone ordered the lower switch disabled. Chen could not identify the speaker by sight. He had heard only a voice and a distinctive three-note whistle.

午後，清潔員陳碩終於願意開口。他不是舊故事裡的壞人。那時他十九歲，害怕失業，聽到警示卻被命令關門，因為攝影機還在錄影。他當年處境弱勢，仍後悔沒有早點求救。他記得藍岑帶沈宇走進維修通道，其餘孩子則被帶往正門。接著有人下令停用下方開關。陳沒看見發令者的臉，只聽見聲音，以及特別的三音口哨。

Chen stopped at a small shop for crunchy rice crackers before the interview. The ordinary snack helped him feel less like a suspect. He said the old studio had staged a science special about nuclear energy, using painted warning signs that could easily mislead later viewers into thinking there had been an actual industrial accident. Yinghe checked the props list and agreed. The word NUCLEAR appeared only on a cardboard display. The evidence for the water failure came from maintenance records, not that dramatic label. Chen relaxed when he saw her refuse a sensational explanation. For once he felt able to describe what he had actually heard.

陳碩受訪前買了一包酥脆米餅，普通零食讓他比較不像嫌犯。他說舊攝影棚曾拍核能科學特輯，布景上的警告標誌很容易誤導後來的人，以為真發生工業事故。映荷查道具單後同意：核能字樣只出現在紙板上，進水故障證據來自維修紀錄，不是戲劇化標籤。看到她拒絕聳動解釋，陳放鬆下來，終於能講出自己真正聽到的事。

Yinghe played the recordings for Chen with his permission. He recognized the whistle in the right channel but said the words had been attached from another recording. The discovery changed the direction of the investigation. The threatening letters might also be assemblies of borrowed handwriting and altered images. Before leaving, Chen pointed to a photograph of a doorway with four painted waves. "That is where I last saw Lan," he said. "She was alive when I left." Yinghe wrote down his exact words. The room felt suddenly smaller, because finding the truth now meant locating a living person who might have spent years hiding from the company. She would not risk that person's safety for tomorrow's headlines.

陳碩同意聽錄音，辨認出右聲道裡的口哨，但表示那句指令是從其他錄音拼上去的。調查方向因此改變：威脅信也可能是借來筆跡與修改影像的組合。離開前，陳指著照片上畫四道浪的門：「那是我最後看到藍岑的地方。我離開時她還活著。」映荷逐字記下。房間突然顯得狹小，因為真相如今涉及一名可能多年躲著公司的活人。她不願為明天的新聞危及那人的安全。
