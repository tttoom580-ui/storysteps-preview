五音警報

The Five-Note Warning

On concert morning, Yinghe stood inside the dusty control room while an electrician tested the warning circuit. The diagram had survived an era of company expansion, but its code had been overwritten. A solar battery, installed after an earlier power failure, was connected to the wrong channel. The eventual consequence could have been serious: one operator would have been able to move the stage while workers were still below. Qiu wanted to circulate a statement calling the error harmless. Yinghe refused. "The stage's dominant appearance is not what matters," she said. "The machine must stop when a person asks it to stop." The electrician marked the dangerous connection with a bright paper tag.

演唱會當天早上，映荷站在滿是灰塵的控制室，看電工測試警報電路。設計圖經歷公司擴張的年代仍留存，控制程式卻被覆寫。早年停電後增設的太陽能電池，竟接到錯誤通道。後果可能嚴重：一名操作員就能在底下仍有工作人員時移動舞台。邱董事想發布「無害小錯誤」聲明，映荷拒絕：「重要的不是舞台外觀多有氣勢，而是有人要求停止時，機器必須停。」電工在錯接處貼上醒目的警示紙。

Jingyao waited in a separate room with a cold drink. The familiar song played through the wall, graceful and almost comforting. Yet he heard the fifth note now, a tiny sound that changed everything. He asked whether his fame had helped hide the old problem. Yinghe said it had made difficult questions easier for management to delay, but fame itself had not written the faulty instruction. "You are responsible for what you do after learning," she told him. He did not argue. A fan marathon would begin outside at noon, and a charity broadcast had promised coverage. He asked that its runners be given the same exit information as ticket holders. That small request was the first public consequence of his new knowledge.

景遙在另一間房喝冷飲，熟悉歌曲從牆外傳來，優雅得近乎安慰。然而他如今聽得見第五個短音，所有事都變了。他問自己的名氣是否幫忙遮掩舊問題。映荷說名氣確實讓管理層更容易拖延難題，卻不是名氣自己寫出錯誤指令。「知道以後怎麼做，才是你要負責的。」他沒有爭辯。中午外頭有歌迷馬拉松，慈善轉播也會報導，他要求跑者取得和持票觀眾相同的出口資訊。這是他理解新真相後第一個公開決定。

A retired telegraph operator arrived carrying a small wooden case. He had helped the studio create warning tones for deaf and hearing workers by pairing sound with flashing lamps. The original design had been a cooperative project, not one person's invention. His notes included a passage about an invasion of water under the old platform. The pumps had failed during a rehearsal, not during a grand explosion. He had negotiated with management for a second alarm but lost the argument. "They called my warning an act of rebellion," he said. "I was only asking for a switch that could not be ignored." Yinghe added his name to the list of people whose work deserved credit.

一位退休電報員帶著小木盒來訪。他曾協助攝影棚把警示音與閃燈配成一組，讓不同聽覺需求的工作人員都能收到警報。原設計是合作成果，不是某個天才的獨家發明。他的筆記寫著舊舞台底下曾進水，抽水機在排練中故障，並非盛大爆炸。他曾和管理層協商第二警報卻失敗。「他們說我在反叛，」他說，「我只想要一個不會被忽視的開關。」映荷把他的名字列入應受肯定的工作者名單。

During the test, a technician accidentally tickled the loose wire with a metal probe, producing a sharp five-note sequence. The platform began to rise, then stopped when the engineer pressed both safety buttons. Everyone was shaken. The test had exposed a weakness, not proved that the full system was safe. Qiu stormed into the room and said the demonstration could wreck the concert. Yinghe insisted the work continue with no performers present. She had no desire to persuade the director that he was a villain; she needed his authority to stop interfering. The venue officer signed a temporary order removing the moving platform from the show.

測試時，技師用金屬探針碰到鬆動電線，意外發出尖銳五音。平台開始上升，工程師同時按下兩個停止鍵才停住。大家都受驚。測試證明存在弱點，卻不能說整套系統安全。邱董事闖入說這會毀掉演唱會。映荷堅持在沒有表演者的情況下繼續檢測。她不需要說服邱董事承認自己是壞人，只需要他別再妨礙。場館主管簽下臨時命令，將升降台排除在節目之外。

Near the control desk, an electrician found an indication that the second alarm had once worked: a faded green light remained in its original housing. The design had been meant to expand protection to workers below the stage, including those too far away to hear instructions. When a representative of the company's capital investors suggested that such rules breed unnecessary fear, Yinghe answered with the maintenance dates. No skillful persuasion could remove those dates. A junior worker, tired of being called a rebel whenever he asked a question, quietly placed his own report beside hers. The growing stack of records was less dramatic than a public confession, but harder to dismiss.

控制桌旁，電工找到第二警報曾運作的跡象：原燈座留著褪色綠燈。設計本來要把保護擴展到台下工作人員，包括聽不清指示的人。公司資本投資代表說這種規則只會滋生恐懼，映荷便拿出維修日期。再高明的說服，也抹不掉日期。被稱為愛反叛的年輕工人默默把自己的報告放在旁邊。紀錄堆疊起來，雖不如公開認罪戲劇化，卻更難忽視。

At noon, Yinghe found a final addition to the old tape archive: a narrow receipt for the studio's first emergency repair. The purchaser had been the company, but the person who collected the parts was Lan Cen's mother. That connection did not prove wrongdoing; it suggested the family had kept trying to prevent another accident. Jingyao touched the receipt without lifting it. "I thought they disappeared because they hated me," he said. Yinghe told him that a story built around one guilty child could be easier for a company to sell than a story about adults making decisions. He asked to hear the five-note signal once more, slowly, so he could recognize it without fear.

中午，映荷在舊帶檔案裡找到一張緊急維修收據。付款的是公司，領走零件的人卻是藍岑母親。這不證明她有罪，反而顯示家人也許一直試圖避免事故重演。景遙輕碰收據：「我以為她們消失，是因為恨我。」映荷說，把過錯歸咎某個孩子的故事，可能比描述成人決策更容易讓公司宣傳。他請她再慢慢放一次五音訊號，好讓自己不再一聽就害怕。
