Students began giving statements. Their memories differed about the nighttime alarms, the staff they had seen, and what Cheng had told them. Administrators pointed to the contradictions as proof that nobody could be believed. Ye asked investigators to compare each claim with independent schedules and access logs. Some frightened recollections were wrong; others matched precisely. The college's most damaging error had been teaching students that imperfect memory meant dishonesty. A witness could be sincere and mistaken. Another could be confident and rehearsed. Ye learned to ask where the evidence came from rather than how convincingly someone spoke.

學生陸續陳述夜間警報、見過的員工與程露說過的話，記憶彼此有出入。管理層說矛盾證明無人可信。葉秋請調查員比對班表與登入紀錄。有些恐懼中的回憶確實錯了，有些卻精確吻合。學校最大的錯誤，是教學生把不完美記憶當成說謊。證人可以真誠卻記錯，也可以自信卻排練過。葉秋學著問證據來自何處，而非說話者有多像真話。
