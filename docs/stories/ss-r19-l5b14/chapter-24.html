Ye resigned from the leadership of the old program. He did not leave teaching; he accepted a supervised role designing courses on evidence, consent, and the limits of criminal profiling. Several students invited him to their workshop, then challenged his first lesson plan. He listened and revised it. Cheng enrolled in a different department and continued her studies without public appearances. The college offered to call the scandal a closed chapter. She rejected that phrase. The investigation had ended, she said, but safeguards had to stay open to revision. Ye found that answer more demanding than forgiveness and more useful.
