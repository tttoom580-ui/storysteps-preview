Ye compared the class reports. Every student had included the same eighth sentence: "A reliable witness is one who doubts their own memory." The sentence was not in the official assignment. Some students thought the professor had given it to them; others had copied it from an anonymous online hint. Ye checked his teaching records and found a scheduled message from an account that no longer existed. The message had been delivered during his absence. He asked the students to preserve their drafts and offered independent counseling. A classmate who had disliked Cheng admitted she was frightened. The mystery was no longer only where Cheng had gone, but who was teaching the students to mistrust each other.

葉秋比較全班報告，每篇第八句都一模一樣：「可靠的證人會懷疑自己的記憶。」那句不在正式作業裡。有學生以為是教授指定，有人從匿名提示複製。葉秋查到自己不在校時，一個後來消失的帳號曾排程發訊息。他請學生保存草稿，並提供獨立心理諮詢。原本討厭程露的同學也承認害怕。謎題不只是程露去了哪裡，還有誰在教學生彼此不信任。
