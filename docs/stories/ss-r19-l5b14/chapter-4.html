A dormitory supervisor admitted that the college had run a secret nighttime exercise designed to measure how students reacted to uncertainty. Participation had never been clearly disclosed. Some alarms and messages were staged. Ye demanded that the exercise stop while investigators checked whether anyone faced real danger. The college director insisted the secrecy was necessary for authentic research. Ye asked for the signed ethics approval. None could be produced. Students began reviewing the nights they had felt watched or manipulated, unsure which events were real. Ye told them that uncertainty itself was an injury when imposed without consent. He also worried that he had been too proud of the course to notice its shadow.

宿舍管理員承認學校曾進行祕密夜間心理測試，以觀察學生對不確定性的反應。參與者從未得到清楚告知，有些警報和訊息都是安排的。葉秋要求立即停辦，並查明是否有人真正遇險。校長說保密才有研究價值。葉秋索取倫理審核文件，對方拿不出來。學生開始回想那些被監視、被操弄的夜晚，不知道哪些是真的。葉秋說，被強加的不確定本身就可能造成傷害，也反省自己是否太驕傲於課程而忽視陰影。
