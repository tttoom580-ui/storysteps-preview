唯一的滿分

The Only Perfect Score

At Meridian College, the only student to earn a perfect score in Criminal Psychology disappeared before sunrise. Professor Ye Qiu learned this from the attendance system, not from the police. The student's name was Cheng Lu, age twenty-two. At midnight, the university had posted her grade: one hundred. At six that morning, her name vanished from the class list. Ye stared at the empty row. He felt no certainty about what had happened, but the speed of the change disturbed him. The college's metropolitan campus was proud of its security, and the student forum was already full of rumors. Someone had written, SHE PASSED THE FINAL TEST BY DISAPPEARING. Ye closed the page.

在子午學院，唯一拿到犯罪心理學滿分的學生，天亮前就失蹤了。葉秋教授是從點名系統而不是警方那裡得知此事。學生名叫程露，二十二歲。午夜時，學校公布她的成績：一百分。早上六點，她的名字卻從班級名單消失。葉秋盯著空白那一列。他無法確定發生了什麼，但變更速度令他不安。這所位於都會區的學院向來以保全嚴密自豪，學生討論區已充滿傳言。有人寫道：「她用失蹤通過了期末考。」葉秋關掉網頁。

His classroom occupied the former chapel, a stone building preserved for its architectural heritage. A cluster of student portraits hung beside the door, and a white flower had been placed beneath Cheng's photograph. By afternoon it would wither in the dry air. The supervisor of academic records arrived carrying a sealed folder. “The family has requested privacy,” she said. “You must not disclose her attendance history.” Ye agreed at once, then asked why the course record had been altered. The supervisor said the change was part of an automated transaction with the grading system. Yet the school's generator had been running during a network outage at dawn. Who had authorized the deletion?

他的教室設在舊禮拜堂裡，這棟石造建築因其建築文化遺產價值而保存。一組學生肖像掛在門邊，程露的照片下放著一朵白花。到了下午，花大概會在乾燥的空氣裡枯萎。學籍紀錄主管拿著密封資料夾走來。「家屬要求保護隱私，」她說，「你不得揭露她的出席紀錄。」葉秋立刻同意，接著問為什麼課程紀錄被改動。主管說，變更屬於與評分系統同步的自動交易程序。但學校在清晨網路中斷時，靠發電機維持運作。究竟是誰授權刪除名單？

The class had been advertised as an optional seminar on criminal reasoning, not a lesson in harming people. Its final assignment asked students to identify how fear could distort a witness's memory. The university had added a provision forbidding students to investigate real classmates without consent. Ye had supported it. He knew the danger of turning other people into examples. One student, Han Rui, appeared at the classroom door and seemed to stumble over his greeting. “Professor, Cheng's report wasn't like ours,” he said. “She didn't describe a crime. She described the school.” Ye asked whether Han had read the report himself. Han nodded, then hesitated. “I only saw the first page.”

這門課對外宣傳為選修的犯罪推理研討課，不是教人如何傷害別人。期末作業要求學生辨認恐懼如何扭曲證人的記憶。大學新增了一項規定，禁止學生未經同意調查真實的同學。葉秋支持這項條款；他知道把別人當作案例有多危險。學生韓瑞出現在教室門口，打招呼時說話有些結巴。「教授，程露的報告跟我們的不一樣，」他說，「她寫的不是犯罪，而是學校。」葉秋問他是否親眼讀過報告。韓瑞點頭，隨即猶豫。「我只看到第一頁。」

Ye invited him to sit near the window, where ivy formed a small habitat for sparrows outside. Han watched two birds on the ledge before speaking. “Everybody wanted the perfect score. I was envious of Cheng, but I didn't want her gone.” Ye gave him time. The young man explained that several students had received identical comments on their essays, though their work was different. He had assumed the marker was simply careless. Now he feared someone else had been reading their private submissions. Ye felt an impulse to promise that everything would be all right. Instead, he asked Han to save his own documents and contact student welfare if he felt unsafe.

葉秋請他坐在窗邊，外面的常春藤形成一小片麻雀棲地。韓瑞看著窗臺上的兩隻鳥，才開口。「每個人都想拿滿分。我很羨慕程露，可是我不希望她消失。」葉秋讓他慢慢說。韓瑞解釋，好幾個學生的報告明明不同，卻收到一模一樣的評語。他原以為閱卷者只是粗心，現在卻懷疑另有人讀過他們的私人作業。葉秋有股衝動，想保證一切都會沒事。他最後只請韓瑞保留自己的文件，若感到不安全，就與學生輔導單位聯絡。

A campus message soon announced that the school would provide compensation for any academic inconvenience caused by the missing grade. Ye read the sentence twice. The immense problem was not an inconvenient grade; a young woman was missing. He searched the legitimate course archive for Cheng's submission, but found only its title: THE PERSON WHO SET THE PERFECT SCORE. The file itself required an authorization that Ye did not possess. A new insight struck him: he had never set the final grading standard. The course had been handed to an external assessment company two weeks earlier. He had signed the administrative notice without reading the attached data policy carefully enough.

校方很快發出通知，表示將對成績異常造成的學務不便提供補償。葉秋把那句話讀了兩遍。真正巨大的問題不是分數不方便，而是一名年輕女子失蹤了。他在合法授權的課程檔案庫搜尋程露的作業，只找到標題：「決定滿分的人」。檔案本身需要葉秋沒有的權限才能開啟。這時，他突然領悟：自己從未制定最後的評分標準。兩週前，課程評量已交給外部公司。他當時簽了行政通知，卻沒有仔細閱讀附帶的資料政策。

Ye called the dean and demanded a record of the company's access. The dean replied that the investigation belonged to campus security. “Then tell me who can preserve the records before they're overwritten,” Ye said. He did not accuse the dean of any wrongdoing. He needed the files to remain available for proper review. The dean agreed to issue a preservation notice but warned Ye against discussing the case publicly. Ye accepted that limit for the student's sake, not for the institution's reputation. At lunch, he met Cheng's aunt in a quiet room. She asked only one question: “Does the school know where my niece is?” Ye told her the truth. He did not know.

葉秋打電話給院長，要求取得外部公司存取紀錄。院長回答，調查是校園保全的責任。「那就告訴我，誰能在紀錄被覆寫之前先保全它，」葉秋說。他沒有指控院長犯錯，只是要讓檔案得以留待適當查核。院長同意發出資料保全通知，但警告他不要公開談論此案。葉秋接受這項限制，是為了學生，而不是為了學校名聲。午餐時，他在安靜的房間見了程露的阿姨。她只問了一件事：「學校知道我姪女在哪裡嗎？」葉秋說了實話。他不知道。

That evening, six students sent Ye copies of their feedback. Every file contained the same last line: THE PERFECT STUDENT LEAVES NO WITNESSES. It was not part of the assignment, and none remembered writing it. Ye documented the messages and sent them to the authorized investigator. He would not turn the classroom into a private police unit. Still, the wording mattered. Someone had introduced a threat into the students' grades while hiding behind the university's systems. Ye walked back to the old chapel, where the white flower had begun to droop. Cheng's photograph still hung beside the door, though her name had disappeared from the computer.

傍晚，六名學生把評語副本寄給葉秋。每一份檔案的最後一行都是：「完美的學生不留下目擊者。」這句話不屬於作業內容，也沒有人記得自己寫過。葉秋記錄訊息，交給有權調查的人員。他不打算把教室變成私人警察單位。儘管如此，這句文字仍然重要。有人利用大學系統，把威脅放進學生的成績裡，並躲在系統背後。葉秋回到舊禮拜堂，白花已經垂下。程露的照片仍掛在門邊，雖然電腦裡已經找不到她的姓名。

On the lectern lay an envelope with no stamp. Inside was a single page from Cheng's report. Ye recognized her careful handwriting. It began: If I disappear, please do not ask which student committed the perfect crime. Ask who decides what perfect means. Beneath the sentence was a printed code from the external assessment company. Someone had circled a date three days in the future. Ye folded the page and sealed it in an evidence sleeve. He suddenly understood why a perfect score might be the most dangerous result in his class. But he still did not know whether Cheng had left by choice or whether someone had made that choice for her.

講臺上放著一個沒有郵票的信封。裡面是一頁程露的報告，葉秋認得她工整的字跡。開頭寫著：「如果我消失，請不要問哪個學生犯下完美犯罪。請問，是誰決定什麼叫完美。」句子下方印著外部評量公司的代碼，有人圈起三天後的日期。葉秋把紙摺好，封進證物套。他忽然明白，為什麼班上的滿分可能是最危險的結果。但他仍不知道程露是自願離開，還是有人替她作出那個選擇。
