The grading rubric shocked Ye. It did not measure knowledge of criminal psychology or ethical reasoning. It awarded points for how completely a student's plan could isolate a target from friends. Cheng's answer, however, had earned one hundred points. Ye requested the original scoring data. An encrypted note beside her grade read: "Resistance successful." He asked who had entered it; the administration said the scoring contractor owned the code. Ye refused to continue teaching under a system he could not inspect. He suspended grades, invited independent reviewers, and told the students not to interpret their scores as judgments of character. A perfect mark now seemed to mean Cheng had recognized the game.

葉秋看到評分標準大吃一驚。考試不測犯罪心理知識或倫理推理，而是計算學生能多徹底地讓目標失去朋友。程露卻得滿分。葉秋要求原始評分資料，看到加密註記：「抵抗成功。」校方說計分程式屬外包商，無法查看。葉秋拒絕繼續使用不可稽核的評分制度，暫停成績並請獨立委員審查，也告訴學生別把分數當人格判決。那個滿分，似乎代表程露看穿了遊戲。
