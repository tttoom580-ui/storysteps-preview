Professor Ye Qiu found Cheng Lu's desk empty and her examination paper missing from the university server. A clerk showed him an instruction to remove her name from the class list, signed by a department administrator rather than police. Ye kept a copy of the instruction. The official explanation was that Cheng had withdrawn voluntarily, but her phone still rang unanswered. Ye called the police and reported the facts without claiming she had been harmed. One student whispered that Cheng had been the only person to refuse the class's final exercise. Ye asked what that meant. The student said, "We were supposed to write how to make someone suspicious of everyone else." The perfect score began to look less like a prize.
