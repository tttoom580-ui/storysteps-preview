The new semester began without a perfect-score competition. Students entered the classroom carrying ordinary notebooks instead of secret instructions. Ye wrote a question on the board: "What do investigators owe the people they study?" The answers came slowly. One student spoke of consent; another of verification; a third of admitting uncertainty. Cheng was not in the room, and Ye did not invoke her name to make the lesson dramatic. After class he checked the oversight schedule and saw that students had added a review of anonymous reporting channels. He approved the time. Outside, the old campus bell rang, marking a day no one would later describe as perfect. It was an honest place to begin.

新學期沒有滿分競賽。學生拿著普通筆記本進教室，不再收到祕密指令。葉秋在黑板寫：「調查者對研究對象負有什麼責任？」答案慢慢出現：同意、查證、承認不確定。程露不在這堂課，他沒有用她的名字製造高潮。下課後他看到監督表上新增匿名申訴管道檢查，便排入時間。校鐘響起，這一天沒有人會叫它完美。正因如此，它是誠實的開始。
