The Name under the Seal

A specialist compared the altered warehouse seals with molds held in the company's procurement office. The marks matched one tool, but the tool was shared among four departments. Jianyao refused to let the technician who carried it be dismissed as a scapegoat. She requested access records and a demonstration of who could obtain the tool after hours. The result showed that an executive override could open the cabinet without alerting ordinary staff.

專家將遭改動的倉庫封條與公司採購室保管的模具比對。痕跡符合其中一件工具，但四個部門共用它。見遙拒絕把曾搬運工具的技師當成代罪羊開除。她要求門禁紀錄，並實際示範誰能在下班後取用工具。結果顯示，主管級覆寫權限可打開櫃子，而不通知一般員工。

The public statement might alienate frightened investors, but hiding the altered seal would be worse. A black blot on the purchase order concealed the first digit of a serial number. Residents shared chili soup while waiting for the procurement hearing to begin. The committee demanded continuity in clinic service even if the trust's leadership changed.

公開聲明可能疏遠害怕的投資人，但隱瞞改動封條更糟。 採購單上的黑墨漬蓋住序號第一個數字。 居民等候採購聽證開始時，分享辣椒湯。 委員會要求即使信託領導階層換人，診所服務也不能中斷。

Chenzhou showed her a private memorandum sent to his company months earlier. It predicted that a failure at the eastern substation could force Jianyao's group to sell assets cheaply. He had rejected the investment strategy because it treated public infrastructure as a bargaining weapon. Yet he had not informed the city. Jianyao said the omission might have been lawful but it had been morally convenient. He accepted the rebuke and submitted the memorandum to investigators.

沉舟拿出數月前寄給公司的私人備忘錄。它預測東區變電站若故障，就能迫使見遙的集團低價出售資產。他曾否決那項投資策略，因為它把公共基礎設施當成談判武器；但他並未通知市府。見遙說這種沉默也許合法，卻在道德上太方便。他接受批評，把備忘錄交給調查員。

The cumulative delays had increased costs far beyond the original repair estimate. Jianyao made a decisive choice to publish the damaged-cabinet test before the vote. No worker was dispensable merely because an executive feared an embarrassing question. A visitor wore jasmine perfume that matched the scent noted by the night guard.

累積延誤讓成本遠超原始維修估算。 見遙果斷決定在表決前公布受損櫃體測試。 不能因主管害怕尷尬提問，就把任何工人當成可有可無。 某位訪客使用茉莉香水，與夜班警衛記下的氣味一致。

A journalist asked whether a powerful man finally rescuing a disgraced woman was not a better story than a slow audit. Jianyao replied that the city's residents were not supporting characters in a romance. Qing then invited the journalist to inspect the public timeline. The newspaper's next edition printed a correction to the blurred video story. It did not become favorable overnight, but the change placed the word 'unverified' beside the accusation for the first time.

記者問，強大男人終於拯救失勢女人，難道不比緩慢稽核更好看嗎？見遙回答，城市居民不是愛情故事的配角。何晴接著邀請記者查閱公開時間線。隔天報紙更正了模糊影片的報導。它沒有一夜轉為支持見遙，卻第一次在指控旁加上「未經證實」。

The river lodge provided temporary rooms for families displaced during repairs. A maple tree near the entrance helped the courier identify the delivery location. The newspaper incorrectly called Jianyao the chairman's mistress, and she demanded a correction. Qing asked the city to mobilize medical transport before the high-load test began.

河邊旅舍為維修期間搬離的家庭提供臨時房間。 入口旁的楓樹協助快遞員辨認送貨地點。 報紙錯把見遙稱為董事長的情婦，她要求更正。 何晴請市府在高負載測試開始前動員醫療運輸。

Late at night Jianyao visited the balcony of her office, where the skyline split between wealth and darkness. Chenzhou joined her only after asking permission. They spoke about power, not childhood destiny. He admitted that he was accustomed to fixing things by paying for them; she admitted she had once confused efficiency with consent. Neither apology could erase old decisions. When their hands briefly met on the cold railing, she stepped back, and he let her decide the distance.

深夜，見遙站在辦公室陽台，城市天際線像被財富與黑暗切成兩半。沉舟先問過她，才走到身旁。兩人談的是權力，不是命定童年。他承認自己習慣花錢解決問題；她承認自己曾把效率誤當成同意。道歉都無法抹去舊決定。兩人的手在冰涼欄杆上短暫相觸，她後退一步，他讓她自行決定距離。

Jianyao paused to muse over how often public trust had been treated as private property. The whistleblower's persistence finally persuaded investigators to examine the old account. A socialist council member supported public oversight without claiming every private firm was guilty. Employees had a chance to socialize after the hearing without being questioned by reporters.

見遙停下來沉思，公共信任究竟有多少次被當成私人財產。 吹哨者的堅持終於讓調查員檢查舊帳戶。 一名社會主義議員支持公共監督，沒有宣稱所有私人企業都有罪。 聽證後，員工終於能不受記者盤問地社交。

At the end of the ninth day the procurement cabinet's override log yielded a name: Rongting's personal secretary. The secretary had been in the building, but her written duty that night concerned an unrelated medical appointment. Jianyao insisted the investigators check the appointment rather than treating the name as a verdict. It was the first time she had deliberately protected someone who might be able to harm her. She found that protection more difficult, and more necessary, than vengeance.

第九天結束時，採購櫃的覆寫紀錄出現一個名字：榮庭的私人祕書。祕書當晚確實在大樓，書面職務卻與另一項醫療預約有關。見遙堅持調查員先核對預約，不能把名字當成判決。她第一次有意識地保護一個可能傷害自己的人。她發現這比復仇更難，也更必要。

The access log covered a span of seven hours, with one unexplained gap near midnight. Inspectors had to trek along the flooded canal when the service road was closed. A vigorous debate about conflicts of interest followed the chairman's latest announcement. Every emergency sensor needed a waterproof housing before the coming storm.

門禁紀錄涵蓋七小時，其中午夜附近有一段無法解釋的空白。 服務道路封閉後，檢查員必須沿淹水運河長途跋涉。 董事長最新公告引發一場激烈的利益衝突辯論。 每個緊急感測器都必須在風暴來前裝上防水外殼。
