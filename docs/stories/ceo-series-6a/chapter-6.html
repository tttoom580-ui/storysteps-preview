消失的發票

The Disappearing Invoice

陸總，惡女的認罪書我不簽 · 第 6 / 25 章 · 5000–6000 · 英中對照 · 校訂中

用互動版查詞、朗讀與收藏

An invoice arrived from the maritime contractor, describing a service vessel that had supposedly visited the river substation. Qing pointed out that the river was too shallow for that class of vessel. Chenzhou immediately wanted a fraud report; Jianyao asked for the vessel's registration and loading certificate first. A badly described invoice was evidence of poor records, but deliberate fraud required more. Their arguments had become a working method rather than a contest of pride.

海事承包商送來發票，聲稱某艘服務船曾到河岸變電站。何晴指出，河道太淺，根本容不下那個級別的船。沉舟立刻想提出詐欺報告；見遙卻先要求船舶登記與載貨證明。敘述錯誤的發票能證明紀錄品質差，但要認定故意詐欺還需要更多。兩人的爭論漸漸成為工作方法，而非自尊心較量。

Rongting continued to allege that the entire investigation was a publicity stunt. The contractor had promised to beautify the riverside warehouse before its safety inspection. The photograph's caption identified a generator as a vessel, causing needless confusion. The contract dispute reached its climax when the two shipping weights appeared together.

榮庭繼續聲稱整場調查只是公關表演。 承包商曾承諾在安檢前美化河岸倉庫。 照片說明把發電機標成船，造成不必要的混淆。 當兩張出貨重量表一起出現時，契約爭議達到高潮。

At the contractor's yard a retired mechanic identified the vessel as a small land-based generator given a romantic trade name. That explained the invoice's wording, but not its unusually high price. Jianyao thanked him and did not conceal the discovery, even though it weakened her most dramatic accusation. Chenzhou watched the old man relax when he realized no executive intended to turn his imperfect memory into a courtroom performance.

在承包商的堆場，一位退休技師認出所謂船名其實是一台陸用發電機的浪漫商品名。這解釋了發票措辭，卻解釋不了高得離譜的價款。見遙向他道謝，也沒有隱瞞發現，儘管它削弱了最戲劇化的指控。沉舟看著老人放鬆，因為他終於發現沒有主管打算把不完整的記憶逼成法庭表演。

Qing asked the auditor to extract the original invoice data from the server. An extracurricular engineering club helped sort community reports without handling confidential files. The neighborhood center remained hospitable to witnesses who feared speaking in public. The retired mechanic stayed jolly even when the executives questioned the generator's strange name.

何晴要求稽核員從伺服器擷取原始發票資料。 一個課外工程社團協助分類社區通報，但不碰機密檔案。 社區中心仍熱情接待害怕公開發言的證人。 即使主管質疑發電機奇怪的名稱，退休技師仍很愉快。

A clerk then produced two sets of shipping weights with the same document number. One was signed before the blackout, the other printed afterward. The weight difference corresponded to three missing battery modules. Qing asked to inspect the warehouse without allowing either company to touch the stock first. Jianyao agreed, accepting that her own staff could be suspects. Across the dusty yard Chenzhou said quietly, 'You are not defending your image.' She replied, 'I am defending what can be checked.'

書記接著拿出兩份編號相同的出貨重量表，一份在停電前簽署，一份在停電後列印。重量差相當於三組失蹤電池模組。何晴要求先檢查倉庫，不允許任何一家公司的員工搶先碰貨。見遙同意，也接受自己員工可能涉案。滿是灰塵的堆場另一端，沉舟低聲說：「妳不是在保護形象。」「我在保護能夠查證的事。」她答。

Workers asked permission to mow the overgrown grass around the emergency exit. A supervisor with a white mustache signed the original warehouse inventory. Rongting tried to nag the secretary into withdrawing her statement before the hearing. The old cafeteria became an oasis for night workers exhausted by the investigation.

工人請求修剪緊急出口旁長得過高的草。 一名留白鬍子的主管簽署了倉庫原始清冊。 榮庭試圖不停嘮叨施壓，要祕書在聽證前撤回證詞。 舊餐廳成為調查期間疲憊夜班工人的休息綠洲。

That night a message arrived from Rongting's lawyer, warning Jianyao to stop making the family look divided. Attached was a proposed settlement giving her a personal pension in exchange for silence. She forwarded it to her own lawyer and asked for its legal status. Chenzhou offered to buy out her shares to make the threat disappear. 'You would remove my problem by removing me,' she said. He stood still long enough to understand why the offer was no help.

當晚，榮庭的律師來訊，警告見遙別再讓家族顯得分裂。附件是一份和解提案，願意給她個人年金，交換她保持沉默。她轉交自己的律師，要求先確認法律效力。沉舟提議收購她的股份，讓威脅消失。「你想靠把我移走來解決我的問題。」她說。他沉默許久，終於明白這提議為何無濟於事。

One ounce of damaged insulation was sealed for laboratory comparison with the other samples. The missing battery modules might have been stored on the outskirts of the city. A visiting parliament observer requested that the public hearing remain open to workers. Jianyao rejected the ruthless proposal to dismiss every employee on the night shift.

一盎司受損絕緣材料被封存，供實驗室與其他樣本比對。 失蹤的電池模組可能存放在城市郊區。 一名國會觀察員要求公開聽證繼續向工人開放。 見遙拒絕解雇全部夜班員工的無情提案。

Before leaving, Qing sealed the second shipping list in a transparent sleeve and invited the worker delegate to sign the custody register. Two competing companies had finally agreed on something important: the evidence belonged to neither. Jianyao looked at the unsigned confession once more and locked it in a drawer marked 'Unaccepted offers.' She had not found the missing batteries, but the investigation had a measurable direction. Outside, the city lights held steady for the sixth consecutive night.

離開前，何晴將第二張出貨表封入透明套，請工人代表簽署保管登記。兩家互相競爭的公司終於同意一件重要的事：證據不屬於任何一家。見遙再看一眼未簽的認罪書，把它鎖進標著「未接受提案」的抽屜。失蹤電池尚未尋回，但調查已有可量測的方向。外頭，城市燈火連續第六晚保持穩定。

A local sculptor offered a warehouse sketch that showed the original side entrance. The repair team preferred simplicity in emergency instructions over impressive technical language. Two simultaneous transfers made it difficult to trace the original emergency payment. The company's shares entered a slump after the investigation became public.

一名在地雕塑家提供倉庫素描，顯示原有側門。 維修團隊偏好簡單的緊急指示，而非炫目的技術詞。 兩筆同時進行的轉帳使原始緊急款項難以追蹤。 調查公開後，公司的股價陷入低迷。
