The Signature at Midnight

陸總，惡女的認罪書我不簽 · 第 1 / 25 章 · 5000–6000 · 英中對照 · 校訂中

Shen Yingzhen woke to the sound of applause and a pen striking glass. The woman in the window was Su Jianyao, the ruthless executive from a novel she had read while auditing a client's records. A blackout had struck the eastern district, and the board wanted her confession before an independent inquiry could begin. In the novel she signed it, lost her company, and died in prison. This time Yingzhen folded the paper and asked who had preserved the switching logs.

沈映真在掌聲與筆尖敲擊玻璃的聲音中醒來。窗戶倒影裡的女人是蘇見遙——她曾在替客戶稽核時讀過的一本小說中的冷酷執行長。東區剛經歷大停電，董事會卻急著要她在獨立調查開始前認罪。原著裡，她簽了字，失去公司，最後死在獄中。這次映真把文件折好，追問誰保管了切換設備的紀錄。

The legal clerk refused to abbreviate the witness's full name on a disputed record. A steel anchor kept the emergency service boat from drifting into the powerless pier. The preservation rule was applicable even to the chairman who proposed the emergency vote. The public archive stored a copy of every switching order issued that week.

法務書記拒絕在有爭議的紀錄上縮寫證人全名。 鋼錨讓緊急服務船不致漂向停電的碼頭。 證據保全規則即使對提出緊急表決的董事長也適用。 公共檔案室保存了那週每份切換命令的副本。

Across the table sat Lu Chenzhou, chief executive of the rival Meridian Group. His expression had the hard composure that made financial newspapers call him the Ice President. He had proposed an emergency rescue investment, but its conditions would give his group temporary voting power over her company. Jianyao read the price carefully. She told him that saving a district and buying control of its electricity supplier were separate decisions. He withdrew the contract without withdrawing his demand for facts.

桌子另一端坐著競爭對手辰衡集團的總裁陸沉舟。那張冷靜得近乎嚴厲的臉，使財經媒體稱他為「冰山總裁」。他提出緊急紓困投資，條件卻會讓辰衡暫時取得她公司的投票權。見遙仔細讀了價格與條款，告訴他拯救一個區域和收購供電商控制權是兩回事。他收回合約，卻沒有收回要求查明事實的立場。

An elderly resident pressed the emergency card against her bosom as she waited. A worker reported that her bra had been taken with her uniform during the inspection. A contractor had offered a bribe for access to the missing hour of footage. Volunteers used a small grill to broil vegetables for families at the center.

一位年長居民等待時，把緊急卡片緊貼胸前。 一名女工反映，檢查時她的胸罩與制服一同被拿走。 一名承包商曾為取得失蹤那一小時的影像提出賄賂。 志工用小烤架炙烤蔬菜，供服務中心的家庭食用。

Engineer He Qing arrived carrying a sealed drive and a torn maintenance sheet. In the original book she was the innocent heroine whom Jianyao was supposed to bully. Yingzhen recognized that expectation and refused it. She invited Qing to speak first. The drive contained a missing interval, while the sheet listed a manual override authorized forty minutes before the blackout. The authorization used Jianyao's initials; the authentication box beside them was empty. An initial was not a signature, and neither was an absence of evidence an acquittal.

工程師何晴帶著封存的儲存裝置與撕裂的維修單走進來。原著中，她是見遙注定要欺負的善良女主角。映真認得這個安排，卻拒絕照演。她讓何晴先發言。裝置裡少了一段時間紀錄，而維修單顯示停電前四十分鐘有人授權手動切換。授權欄使用見遙姓名縮寫，旁邊的驗證格卻是空白。縮寫不是簽名；證據不足也不等於已獲清白。

Qing requested a comparative chart of the manual switch and the automatic system. Jianyao's eyelid trembled when she saw her initials on the unsigned order. Their reserve power was finite, so the clinic had to prioritize essential equipment. The safety fund would let small neighborhood repair workshops flourish after the blackout.

何晴要求製作手動開關與自動系統的比較圖。 見遙看到未簽文件上的姓名縮寫時，眼皮微微顫抖。 備用電力有限，因此診所必須優先供應必要設備。 安全基金將讓社區小修理工坊在停電後蓬勃發展。

Her uncle Su Rongting, chairman of the family trust, offered a simpler escape. If she admitted negligence, he would protect her personal assets and leave her a ceremonial title. Jianyao asked for a copy of the trust rules. The chairman smiled as though a niece had forgotten her place. She placed a recorder on the table and stated that every board member would receive identical records. The first vote preserved the logs for forty-eight hours. It did not clear her name, but it kept the evidence alive.

掌管家族信託的叔父蘇榮庭提出比較簡單的退路：只要她承認疏失，他就保住她的個人財產，留給她一個沒有實權的頭銜。見遙要求一份信託章程。董事長笑得像在提醒姪女認清身分。她把錄音機放在桌上，表示每名董事都將收到相同紀錄。第一項表決通過保全紀錄四十八小時。她還沒洗清罪名，但證據暫時保住了。

A hired gangster threatened the courier who had delivered the original maintenance sheets. The freight van vanished at the junction between the riverside road and the tunnel. A porter with a limp identified the sealed box he had carried upstairs. The tribunal asked Qing to narrate the exact sequence of commands without guessing motives.

一名受雇黑幫分子威脅遞送原始維修表的快遞員。 貨車在河岸道路與隧道交會的路口消失。 一名跛行的搬運員辨認出自己搬上樓的封盒。 法庭請何晴敘述指令的精確順序，不要猜測動機。

Outside, the city glittered only on its western bank. Jianyao could have driven away with her old life in ruins; instead she walked into the emergency center where families waited for power to return. Chenzhou followed at a distance, not with flowers or a promise of rescue, but with a public copy of the offer he had just withdrawn. 'You should know exactly what I wanted,' he said. 'Yes,' she replied. 'And tomorrow you will learn what I want.' The novel had prepared a confession. She prepared a question.

室外的城市只有西岸還閃著燈。見遙本可以開車逃離這場崩毀，卻走進緊急服務中心，面對等候復電的家庭。沉舟隔著一段距離跟來，手裡沒有鮮花，也沒有救她的承諾，只有他剛撤回的投資提案公開副本。「妳應該知道我原本想要什麼。」他說。「是。」她答。「明天你也會知道我要什麼。」小說替她準備了認罪書，她則準備了一個問題。

An orderly guided exhausted families to separate waiting rooms in the emergency clinic. A bank teller confirmed that the suspicious transfer had occurred before the outage. The alleged contract was printed on virgin paper, unlike the stained original file. The judge issued a narrow warrant for records rather than permission to arrest everyone.

勤務員引導疲憊家庭前往緊急診所不同的候診室。 銀行出納確認可疑轉帳發生於停電之前。 所謂合約印在全新紙上，與有污漬的原檔不同。 法官核發有限範圍的調閱令，而非允許逮捕所有人。
