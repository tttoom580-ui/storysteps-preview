The Video with Two Shadows

陸總，惡女的認罪書我不簽 · 第 3 / 25 章 · 5000–6000 · 英中對照 · 校訂中

A television station released a blurred clip of Jianyao entering the substation minutes before the blackout. The camera showed someone in her gray coat; the face was hidden by steam. The board called an emergency meeting. Qing discovered that the clip had been exported twice and that its second version carried a new audio track. Nothing about the alteration proved Jianyao innocent. It did, however, make the clip unsuitable as a complete account of the night.

電視台播出一段模糊影片，畫面裡有人穿著見遙的灰色外套，在停電前進入變電站。蒸氣遮住了那張臉。董事會立刻召開緊急會議。何晴發現影片曾輸出兩次，第二個版本配上新的音軌。改動本身不能證明見遙清白，卻讓影片無法再作為完整的當晚紀錄。

An advisory panel warned against treating the blurred coat as proof of identity. Rongting called the contract a commercial conquest, but workers called it a threat to their jobs. The lawyer said a conspiracy required coordinated intent, not merely two suspicious shadows. Someone had left a cucumber sandwich beside the abandoned recording equipment.

顧問小組警告不可把模糊的外套畫面視為身分證明。 榮庭稱這份契約是商業征服，工人卻稱它威脅工作。 律師說共謀需要協同意圖，不是只有兩道可疑人影。 有人在廢置錄音設備旁留下一份小黃瓜三明治。

In a private elevator, Chenzhou asked her why she had not denied owning the coat. Jianyao told him she did own it, and had lent it to a driver on a cold night. Denying a true detail would make every later statement weaker. He admitted that his investment committee had assumed she was guilty before examining the evidence. He had not voted for punishment, but he had failed to challenge the assumption. She accepted the admission without granting him instant trust.

在私人電梯裡，沉舟問她為何不否認外套屬於自己。見遙告訴他，外套確實是她的，而且她曾在寒夜借給司機。否認真實細節只會削弱以後所有證詞。他承認投資委員會在檢視證據前就假定她有罪。雖然他沒有投票支持處分，卻也未曾質疑這項假設。她接受他的坦白，但沒有立刻交付信任。

The portable radio emitted a feeble signal from the edge of the substation. A box of firecracker decorations explained the flashes on one clip but not the missing minutes. The mechanic began to fume when his accurate warning was described as a rumor. The delivery map was stained with grease from the night-shift kitchen.

便攜無線電從變電站邊緣發出微弱訊號。 一盒爆竹裝飾解釋了某段影像的閃光，無法解釋失蹤時間。 技師見自己的準確警告被說成謠言，開始氣憤。 送貨地圖沾有夜班廚房的油脂。

They visited the night workers' lunchroom, where grease stains covered a discarded inspection map. A kitchen volunteer remembered someone leaving a box before the official evacuation. The box contained small fireworks for a staff celebration and a broken recording microphone; neither explained the blackout. Jianyao insisted that every object be catalogued without forcing it into a convenient story. Qing began to respect a leader who allowed uncertainty to remain on the page.

他們走進夜班休息室，廢棄檢查圖上滿是油漬。一名廚房志工記得，正式撤離前有人放下一只箱子。箱內有員工慶祝用的小煙火與損壞的錄音麥克風，兩者都不能解釋停電。見遙堅持所有物品必須列冊，不准硬塞進方便的故事。何晴開始尊重這個允許疑點留在紙上的主管。

Transparent records could lessen fear even before the investigators reached a final answer. An ocean liner appeared in the contractor's promotional brochure, though no ship reached the site. The old orphanage had been promised funding that was never entered in its bank account. Residents compared the recurring blackouts to a plague that never seemed to end.

透明紀錄即使在調查員得出答案前，也能減輕恐懼。 承包商宣傳冊上印著遠洋客輪，但沒有船抵達現場。 舊孤兒院被承諾的經費從未存入銀行帳戶。 居民把反覆停電比作似乎永不結束的災難。

The same afternoon, Rongting announced a scholarship in the name of an old orphanage and called critics ungrateful. Jianyao located a transfer voucher showing that the donation had been promised but not paid. Instead of humiliating him at a banquet, she asked the trustees to verify the bank receipt. Chenzhou watched her step away from an easy public victory. It was harder to build a case that survived review than to make an enemy look foolish for an evening.

同日下午，榮庭宣布以舊孤兒院名義設立獎學金，並稱批評者不知感恩。見遙找到一張轉帳憑單，顯示捐款只有承諾，沒有付款。她沒有在宴會上羞辱叔父，而是要求受託人核對銀行收據。沉舟看見她放棄一次輕易的公開勝利。建立能通過審查的案件，比讓敵人當晚出醜難多了。

The community requested recreational lighting for its sports field only after clinics were safe. The video editor warned that online debate had become savage toward the unknown driver. A shrub outside the camera room blocked one corner of the delivery truck's view. The technician explained that a local slang term had been mistaken for an equipment code.

社區表示診所安全後，再考慮運動場休閒照明。 影像編輯警告網路討論對不明司機已變得殘酷。 攝影室外一叢灌木遮住貨車鏡頭的一角。 技師解釋某個地方俚語被誤認成設備代碼。

By midnight Qing recovered a second camera angle from an independent delivery truck. It revealed two shadows moving toward the door, not one, but still no clear faces. Jianyao placed the footage under shared custody before informing the board. Chenzhou asked whether she would now accept his financial rescue. She shook her head. 'I will accept your testimony if it is accurate,' she said. 'Your money can wait.' For the first time he smiled, not because she had yielded, but because she had not.

午夜，何晴從獨立貨車上找回第二個攝影角度。畫面顯示有兩道人影朝門口移動，而非一人，臉部卻依然不清楚。見遙先將影像交由共同保管，才通知董事會。沉舟問她現在是否願意接受他的財務援助。她搖頭。「如果證詞準確，我會接受你的證詞，」她說，「你的錢可以等。」他第一次笑了，不是因為她屈服，而是因為她沒有。

The second camera could surpass the first in clarity without identifying the people shown. For workers the damaged seal came to symbolize years of ignored safety warnings. A textile pattern on the borrowed coat helped establish when it had been photographed. The clinic kept its vaccine refrigerator running while the citywide evidence review continued.

第二台攝影機雖然清晰度勝過第一台，仍無法辨認人物。 對工人而言，破損封條象徵多年被忽略的安全警告。 外借大衣的紡織圖案協助確認它何時被拍攝。 全市證據審查期間，診所仍維持疫苗冰箱運作。
