夜班聽見的事

What the Night Shift Heard

陸總，惡女的認罪書我不簽 · 第 4 / 25 章 · 5000–6000 · 英中對照 · 校訂中

用互動版查詞、朗讀與收藏

The workers' union arranged interviews with every employee on the affected shift. Jianyao entered as a witness, not the chair of the inquiry, because her own authorization remained under review. A cleaner described overhearing a supervisor order the corridor camera turned away from a storage door. Another worker recalled an unfamiliar company badge. Their memories could support a timeline, but the committee would need payroll records and access logs before naming anyone.

工會安排訪談受影響班次的所有員工。見遙以證人身分出席，而非主持調查，因為她自己的授權仍在受審。一名清潔員說，曾聽到主管命令把走廊攝影機轉離儲藏室；另一名員工記得一張陌生的公司識別證。這些記憶可以補足時間線，但委員會必須取得薪資與門禁紀錄，才能指認任何人。

The cleaning team used bleach only after investigators recorded the stains near the door. The union requested a comprehensive list of every visitor with access to the storage area. A credible witness described what she heard but refused to guess who issued the command. Qing praised the workers' dedication to preserving damaged equipment instead of concealing it.

調查員記錄門旁污漬後，清潔隊才使用漂白水。 工會要求完整列出所有能進入儲藏區的訪客。 可信的證人描述聽到的話，拒絕猜測誰下命令。 何晴讚賞工人保存損壞設備而非藏匿的投入。

Chenzhou suggested replacing the substation's entire security system. Qing objected that a rapid replacement could erase technical evidence and create a new danger before the old one was understood. The three of them argued over risk and cost in front of the workers instead of behind a locked executive door. They finally approved a narrow repair: protect the working switches, isolate the damaged recorder, and keep duplicate copies of every changed setting. The agreement felt ordinary, which made it important.

沉舟建議整套更換變電站保全系統。何晴反對，認為倉促更換可能抹去技術證據，且在釐清舊危險前製造新危險。三人在工人面前辯論風險與費用，而非躲進緊鎖的主管辦公室。最後他們通過限定修復：保護仍能運作的開關、隔離損壞記錄器，並替每次設定變更保留副本。協議看似平凡，卻十分重要。

A technician found an old badge inside a dresser in the abandoned guard room. The guard's familiarity with the corridor helped reconstruct the route taken that night. The office hierarchy did not give the chairman permission to disregard witness protection rules. The landlady of the nearby boarding house remembered an unusual delivery before dawn.

技師在廢置警衛室的梳妝櫃裡找到舊識別證。 警衛對走廊的熟悉協助重建當晚路徑。 辦公室階級制度不允許董事長無視證人保護規則。 附近寄宿屋的女房東記得黎明前一件異常送貨。

In the maintenance archive, a porter found a ledger of after-hours visitors. One entry was signed by Rongting's assistant, although the chairman had publicly said none of his office staff visited the site. Jianyao restrained the urge to announce a lie. The assistant could have had a legitimate errand. She requested the reason for the visit and the authorization record. Her lawyer reminded her that a contradiction deserved investigation, not a verdict.

在維修檔案庫，一名搬運員找到非上班時間訪客登記簿。其中一筆由榮庭的助理簽名，董事長卻公開宣稱辦公室沒有人到過現場。見遙克制直接宣布他說謊的衝動；助理可能有正當差事。她要求調閱來訪原因與授權紀錄。律師提醒她，矛盾值得調查，不等於已經有判決。

A lush garden surrounded the workers' entrance where the unknown visitor had waited. Families gathered to mourn an elderly patient who had died during an earlier outage. A fair hiring process would nurture younger engineers rather than reward personal loyalty. The team checked whether the canal could overflow during another heavy storm.

一座繁茂花園環繞工人入口，不明訪客曾在那裡等待。 家屬聚集悼念在先前停電期間過世的老病人。 公平聘任程序能培育年輕工程師，而非獎勵個人忠誠。 團隊檢查另一場暴風雨是否可能讓運河溢流。

That evening Jianyao received a message from the book's supposed heroine, He Qing. Qing knew people assumed she needed to be rescued from the villainess. In fact she had applied for the chief engineer position and wanted a transparent interview. Jianyao agreed to remove herself from the hiring panel. Chenzhou called it a costly choice. 'Only if I believe power is something I must hold with both hands,' she replied. He said nothing, but he copied the rule for his own company.

當晚，見遙收到小說中「原女主角」何晴的訊息。何晴知道大家以為她需要被惡女拯救，其實她早已申請總工程師職位，希望有透明的面試。見遙同意退出聘任委員會。沉舟稱這是代價高昂的選擇。「只有在我以為權力必須兩手抓牢時才是。」她回答。他沒有說話，卻把這條規則抄給自己的公司。

A cleaner had begun to overhear the order before the supervisor closed the door. The company's new report could refute a rumor only by presenting verifiable records. A seagull circled the river dock while technicians inspected the abandoned van. The site superintendent admitted that he had approved a broken gate's temporary use.

主管關門前，清潔員已無意間聽到那項命令。 公司新報告只能用可驗證紀錄反駁謠言。 技師檢查廢置廂車時，一隻海鷗繞著河港盤旋。 現場總監承認自己曾批准暫時使用損壞閘門。

The interview ended with a surprising witness: a retired gatekeeper whose old badge had been copied. He remembered returning it through the official disposal office, which should have destroyed the chip. The independent chair ordered that disposal records be preserved immediately. Jianyao walked home through quiet rain. She had once thought being the villain meant everyone would reject her. Instead, people were beginning to argue with her honestly. That was a more useful kind of company.

訪談最後出現意外的證人：一位退休門衛，他的舊識別證曾遭複製。他記得自己透過正式報廢窗口繳回識別證，晶片理應已銷毀。獨立主席立刻下令保全報廢紀錄。見遙沿著細雨步行回家。她曾以為當反派意味著所有人都會排斥她；如今，人們開始真誠地和她爭論。這比盲目擁護更有價值。

A recorded symphony played in the lobby, strangely calm beside the anxious witnesses. A thermometer showed that the overheated cabinet had not cooled to safe levels. Jianyao said transparent testimony mattered more than turning the hearing into public turmoil. Volunteers prepared wholesome meals for interviewees who had missed the last bus.

大廳播放錄製的交響樂，與焦慮證人形成奇特反差。 溫度計顯示過熱櫃體尚未降至安全溫度。 見遙說透明證詞比把聽證變成公眾騷動更重要。 志工為錯過末班車的受訪者準備營養均衡的餐點。
