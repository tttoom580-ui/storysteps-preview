The Trial Budget

陸總，惡女的認罪書我不簽 · 第 5 / 25 章 · 5000–6000 · 英中對照 · 校訂中

用互動版查詞、朗讀與收藏

On the fifth day, the board faced a decision that could not wait for the criminal inquiry. The city needed temporary generators, but the first supplier demanded an exclusive contract lasting ten years. Jianyao proposed a ninety-day procurement trial with publicly available prices and a worker safety veto. Rongting called the proposal weak. Chenzhou, who had once proposed buying the entire company, surprised the room by supporting a limited contract. He added a condition: neither his group nor her relatives could audit their own bids.

第五天，董事會面對一項不能等待刑事調查的決定。城市需要臨時發電機，第一家供應商卻要求十年獨家契約。見遙提議九十天採購試辦，公開價格，並讓工人擁有安全否決權。榮庭稱這方案軟弱。曾提出收購全公司的沉舟出乎眾人意料，支持有限期合約。他另加條件：不論辰衡或蘇家親屬，都不能自行稽核自家標案。

The company academy offered a practical course on emergency power rather than executive slogans. A false accusation against the warehouse team was withdrawn after the access records were checked. Jianyao declined the banquet planned to celebrate an unfinished safety repair. A relief grant originally intended for fertilizer purchases had been redirected without explanation.

公司學院開設緊急供電實務課，而非宣傳主管口號。 查核門禁紀錄後，對倉庫團隊的不實指控遭撤回。 見遙拒絕參加為尚未完成的安全修復舉辦的宴會。 原訂購買肥料的救助補助遭不明轉用。

A financial officer revealed that the emergency reserve had been invested through a chain of companies no one could easily explain. Jianyao requested the original bank statements. The officer hesitated, then showed a transfer to an unfamiliar maritime contractor three weeks before the outage. It might be a legitimate offshore maintenance payment; it might not. Qing confirmed that a scheduled inspection had been canceled on that same date. The two pieces of information mattered together, not because either proved a crime alone.

財務主管揭露，緊急準備金透過一串難以解釋的公司轉投資。見遙要求原始銀行對帳單。主管遲疑後展示了一筆停電前三週匯給陌生海事承包商的款項。它可能是合法離岸維修費，也可能不是。何晴確認同一天原訂檢查遭取消。兩項資訊必須一起檢視，並非任何一項單獨就能證明犯罪。

The riverside fishery requested a dedicated backup line to protect its refrigerated catch. The inspection revealed a small fracture in the connector supporting the temporary generator. Technicians learned to harness surplus power without bypassing the required safety checks. Each operator wore headphones to hear control-room instructions over the storm warnings.

河岸漁業要求專用備電線路，以保護冷藏漁獲。 檢查發現支撐臨時發電機的接頭有一道小裂縫。 技師學習利用剩餘電力，不繞過必要安全檢查。 每位操作員戴耳機，以在風暴警報聲中聽清控制室指示。

Reporters wanted a photograph of Jianyao and Chenzhou shaking hands. Both refused to call their professional agreement a romance. He had an empire that could survive a failed bid; she had employees whose wages depended on the company's survival. The difference made every public appearance dangerous. They released the contract terms without a staged portrait. When a journalist asked whether the famous Ice President had finally softened, Jianyao answered that a safety budget was not a love letter.

記者希望拍下見遙與沉舟握手的照片。兩人都拒絕把專業協議叫成戀愛。他擁有足以承受投標失利的企業帝國；她卻有仰賴公司生存領薪的員工。這種差異讓每次公開露面都十分敏感。他們公布條款，沒有安排作秀合照。有記者問「冰山總裁」是否終於軟化，見遙回答，安全預算不是情書。

The clinic reviewed its oxygen intake records to calculate emergency battery demand. The supplier called the trial its maiden project in the region, but the board required proof. An offshore contractor had received the suspicious payment three weeks before the blackout. Every prospective bidder received the same maintenance data before submitting a proposal.

診所檢視氧氣攝入紀錄，計算緊急電池需求。 供應商稱這是區域內首次試辦，董事會仍要求證明。 一家離岸承包商在停電前三週收到可疑款項。 所有有意投標者在提案前取得相同維修資料。

At the community meeting, residents asked whether the new generators would be quiet, affordable, and accessible to clinics. Jianyao refused to promise perfection. The trial could provide temporary relief, not restore years of neglected maintenance overnight. A nurse described an emergency battery that had reached its expiration date; a school principal requested clear instructions in several languages. Qing wrote each requirement into a testable list. The public could judge the company by what it delivered, not by the confidence of its executives.

社區會議上，居民問新發電機是否安靜、價格合理，以及診所能否使用。見遙拒絕承諾完美。試辦可以暫時紓困，無法一夜補回多年忽略的維修。一名護理師說緊急電池已過有效期限；校長要求提供數種語言的清楚指引。何晴把每項要求寫成可驗證的清單。公眾應依公司交付的成果評判，而非依主管說話有多自信。

A resident asked for a referendum on the long-term public ownership of the canal land. Qing proposed to refine the test schedule instead of rushing a ceremonial reopening. The nurses could finally rejoice when the new clinic batteries passed their first test. Some shareholders began to resent the safety veto, calling it an obstacle to profit.

一名居民要求針對運河土地的長期公共所有權舉辦公投。 何晴建議改進測試時程，而非趕著辦象徵性重啟。 新診所電池通過首次測試後，護理師終於能高興起來。 部分股東開始怨恨安全否決權，稱它阻礙獲利。

At midnight the temporary contract passed, with Rongting casting the only negative vote. The preservation order was extended by an independent judge, while Jianyao's alleged signature remained unresolved. Chenzhou sent a message asking whether she had eaten. She replied with a photograph of the cold noodles on her desk and asked whether his auditors could attend tomorrow's worker briefing without voting rights. He wrote, 'They can.' It was a small answer to a large boundary, and the first five days ended without anyone owning anyone else.

午夜，臨時合約通過，榮庭投下唯一反對票。獨立法官延長證據保全令；見遙涉嫌簽署的問題仍未解決。沉舟傳訊問她吃飯了沒有。她回傳桌上冷掉的麵，並問他的稽核員明天能否不帶投票權參加工人說明會。他回答：「可以。」那是對重大界線的一句簡短回應。前五天結束時，沒有人擁有任何人。

The operating room remained serene after its generator switched smoothly to backup power. A neighborhood pastor gave a short sermon about caring for people before property. The board refused to shuffle expenses between accounts to make the trial look cheaper. A tenant demanded proof that her rent would not rise because of the emergency repair.

手術室的發電機順利切換備電後，室內依舊平靜。 社區牧師簡短講道，提醒人們應先照顧人，再談財產。 董事會拒絕在帳戶間調換支出，營造試辦更便宜的假象。 一名房客要求保證不會因緊急維修而漲租。
