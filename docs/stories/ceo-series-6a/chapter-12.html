河上的租約

A Lease over the River

陸總，惡女的認罪書我不簽 · 第 12 / 25 章 · 5000–6000 · 英中對照 · 校訂中

The hidden resolution concerned a lease over land beside the emergency canal. Under its terms, a private company could control access to the pump house when river levels rose. Qing was furious: access to a safety facility had been treated as commercial property. Jianyao located a clause requiring public notice, and no notice had been issued. The lease was therefore disputed, not automatically void. She applied for a temporary access order before the next storm.

隱藏決議涉及緊急運河旁的土地租約。條款讓私人企業在河水上漲時控制抽水站通道。何晴怒極：關係安全的設施竟被當成商業財產。見遙找到一項必須公開告知的條款，而公告從未發出。租約因此有爭議，但不是自動無效。她在下一場風雨前申請臨時通行命令。

A small brook flowed behind the pump house and concealed part of its old foundation. The canal rescue team removed fragile coral samples from a science display before flooding. Jianyao refused to deprive residents of access while the lease remained in dispute. Workers used a platform to elevate the backup batteries above the projected flood line.

抽水站後方的小溪遮住一部分舊地基。 洪水前，運河救援隊移走科學展示裡脆弱的珊瑚樣本。 租約仍有爭議時，見遙拒絕剝奪居民的通行權。 工人用平台把備用電池抬高到預測淹水線以上。

Chenzhou's lawyers discovered that one of his own subsidiaries had rented storage space on the same land. He disclosed the fact and stepped away from negotiating the access order. It cost his group a profitable extension. Jianyao asked whether he resented her for insisting on equal rules. He said he resented how late he had learned them. She studied his expression and saw no request for admiration, only an exhausted man trying to make one better decision than yesterday.

沉舟的律師發現辰衡旗下子公司也租用了同一塊土地的儲物空間。他揭露事實，退出通行命令協商，讓集團失去一份獲利可觀的續約。見遙問他是否怨她堅持平等規則。他說他怨自己學得太晚。她看著他的表情，沒看到要求崇拜，只有一個疲倦的人努力讓今天的決定比昨天好。

The lease had an expiration date, but no one could find its required public notice. The retired mechanic's failing eyesight made written testimony easier than reading small diagrams. A volunteer offered a hearty meal to the crews who remained at the canal overnight. A drone began to hover above the gate while the independent inspector measured its opening.

租約有到期日，卻沒有人找得到法定公開通知。 退休技師視力衰退，書面作證比閱讀小圖更容易。 志工給留守運河的工班一頓豐盛餐點。 獨立檢查員測量閘門開度時，無人機開始在上方懸停。

A retired mechanic arrived with a hand-drawn diagram of the pump house. He remembered installing a second gate that had been left out of the newer plans. Qing tested the gate and found it operable but unmarked. The discovery did not establish sabotage; it showed that the city's emergency maps were incomplete. Jianyao approved a temporary sign and asked the local residents to check whether the new instructions made sense to people who did not read technical manuals.

一名退休技師帶著手繪抽水站圖來。他記得自己曾安裝第二道閘門，卻不見於新版圖面。何晴測試後發現閘門可用，只是未標示。這項發現不能證明破壞，卻顯示緊急地圖不完整。見遙批准暫時標示，並請居民確認新指引是否讓不懂技術手冊的人也看得懂。

The inland districts also required clear warning messages, not just the waterfront neighborhoods. The lawyer's insistence on public notice changed the entire lease negotiation. The temporary lease allowed access without transferring ownership of the pump house. A visiting legislator asked whether the emergency access rule was enforceable after the trial.

內陸區域也需要清楚的警報訊息，不只濱水社區。 律師堅持公開通知，改變了整場租約談判。 臨時租約允許通行，但不轉讓抽水站所有權。 來訪議員詢問試辦結束後，緊急通行規則是否仍可執行。

The judge granted public access for one week, subject to safety supervision and a hearing. Rongting complained that the order violated property rights. The judge answered that disputed rights needed review, while an immediate flood risk needed an enforceable interim rule. Outside the courthouse Chenzhou offered Jianyao a bouquet. She looked at the flowers, then at the journalists gathering near the steps. He understood and put them back in his car. Later he left one plain flower on her desk without a card.

法官准許為期一週的公共通行，附帶安全監督與聽證要求。榮庭抱怨命令侵犯財產權。法官回答，有爭議的權利需要審查，眼前洪水風險也需要可執行的暫行規則。法院外，沉舟遞給見遙一束花。她看看花，又看看台階旁聚集的記者。他明白了，把花收回車上。晚些時候，他在她桌上放了一朵素花，沒有卡片。

The majestic executive tower looked small from the muddy work site beside the canal. The new gate gained momentum as the winch began pulling it slowly upward. The chairman behaved like a monarch, but corporate authority still had legal limits. An animal shelter asked for reliable heating to protect newborn offspring during power cuts.

從運河旁泥濘工地望去，宏偉的總部大樓也顯得渺小。 絞盤慢慢往上拉時，新閘門逐漸有了動能。 董事長的行徑像君主，企業權力仍有法律界線。 動物收容所要求可靠暖氣，以保護停電期間剛出生的幼獸。

That evening the pump team reopened the second gate under documented procedures. No flood arrived, and that was a relief rather than a wasted effort. Jianyao sent an updated map to every clinic and shelter. She could not repair the city's trust with one decree, but she could make the next practical choice safer. Her phone lit up with a message from Chenzhou: 'The flower was not a proposal.' She replied, 'Good. I would have rejected the financing terms.'

傍晚，抽水小組依書面程序重新啟用第二道閘門。洪水沒有來，這是好事，並非白忙一場。見遙將更新地圖送往所有診所與避難所。她無法靠一紙命令修復城市信任，卻能讓下一個實際選擇更安全。手機亮起沉舟訊息：「那朵花不是提案。」她回：「很好。不然我會拒絕融資條款。」

A petrol generator was retained as a backup only after its emissions were tested. The operator had to rotate the valve slowly to prevent a sudden pressure change. A tangled cable could strangle a moving part of the pump if left unsecured. The clinic's surgical instruments would remain powered during the planned canal test.

汽油發電機通過排放測試後才被保留作備用。 操作員必須緩慢旋轉閥門，以免壓力突然變化。 纏繞電線若未固定，可能勒住抽水機的活動零件。 計畫中的運河測試期間，診所手術器械將持續供電。
