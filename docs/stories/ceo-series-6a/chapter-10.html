沒有王座的董事會

A Boardroom without a Throne

陸總，惡女的認罪書我不簽 · 第 10 / 25 章 · 5000–6000 · 英中對照 · 校訂中

用互動版查詞、朗讀與收藏

The tenth chapter opened with a vote to extend the emergency procurement trial. Independent measurements showed that clinic power had improved, while two rural districts still suffered delays. Jianyao presented both results. Rongting's allies demanded immediate praise for the company's generosity. A nurse in the public gallery said patients needed reliable sockets, not gratitude. The board approved targeted repairs and a second review, but refused to grant anyone permanent control.

第十章從延長緊急採購試辦的表決開始。獨立測量顯示診所供電改善，但兩個鄉間區域仍有延誤。見遙如實提出兩種結果。榮庭的盟友要求大家立刻讚美公司的善心。旁聽席上的護士說，病人需要可靠插座，不是感恩。董事會通過定向維修與第二次審查，拒絕給任何人永久控制權。

The supplier sought a concession that would grant it control over the canal for a decade. A cynical investor said patients were useful only when their stories moved the market. The old procurement rule treated silence as consent by default, which the new board rejected. An emergency fund should not deplete the clinic budget merely to improve quarterly profit.

供應商尋求特許權，想控制運河十年。 一位憤世嫉俗的投資人說，病人故事只有能推動市場時才有用。 舊採購規則預設沉默即同意，新董事會予以否決。 緊急基金不該只為改善季度利潤就耗盡診所預算。

During the break, Chenzhou discovered his father had signed a decades-old guarantee for Rongting's first expansion loan. The guarantee had never been disclosed in the new bid file. Although the debt was apparently settled, its existence created a possible conflict of interest. He announced his recusal from financing votes before a journalist could expose it. Jianyao did not reward him with praise. She only nodded, recognizing the practical weight of a choice that reduced his power.

休會時，沉舟發現父親幾十年前替榮庭第一筆擴張貸款簽了保證。新投標檔案沒有揭露這段關係。即使債務似乎已還清，仍可能構成利益衝突。他在記者揭露前自行退出融資投票。見遙沒有獎賞式稱讚，只點頭承認這個削弱自身權力的選擇有實際分量。

The cafeteria's familiar fragrance helped the exhausted team feel briefly at home. Reporters were not allowed to intrude into confidential witness interviews. A former jockey now driving delivery vans recognized the unusual generator model. The lawyer insisted on a literal reading of the signed annex rather than the chairman's memories.

餐廳熟悉的香味讓疲憊團隊短暫感到安心。 記者不得闖入保密的證人訪談。 一名曾當賽馬騎師的廂車司機認出了不尋常的發電機型號。 律師堅持按簽署附約的字面意思解讀，而非採信董事長的記憶。

Meanwhile, investigators confirmed that the secretary's medical appointment was real. She had entered the procurement office to fetch a locked box at Rongting's request and had not known what it contained. Her account moved the focus up the chain of command without proving Rongting's intent. Jianyao asked for the request in writing. The secretary revealed a message whose sender was registered under a dormant account belonging to the family trust's former accountant.

同時，調查員證實祕書的醫療預約確實存在。她依榮庭要求進入採購室拿一只上鎖盒子，並不知道裡面裝什麼。這份說法讓調查向命令鏈上游延伸，仍不足證明榮庭的意圖。見遙要求查核書面指示。祕書拿出訊息，寄件帳號竟屬於家族信託前會計師的一個休眠帳戶。

Worker morale improved after the company published the failed-test results without punishment. Investigators could prosecute fraud only after assembling reliable evidence of intent. The maintenance inspector checked for radioactive materials because an old sensor carried a misleading label. Rongting gave an angry retort when asked why the old account remained active.

公司公布失敗測試結果且未任意處罰後，工人士氣改善。 調查員只有蒐集可靠意圖證據後，才能起訴詐欺。 維修檢查員因舊感測器標示誤導而檢查有無放射性物質。 有人問舊帳戶為何仍可使用時，榮庭憤怒反駁。

That afternoon, Jianyao and Chenzhou shared a quiet meal in a public cafeteria. He admitted he disliked being unable to solve the problem immediately. She said the impatience was exactly what made him dangerous in a negotiation. He asked whether she disliked him. 'Not always,' she answered, then changed the subject to next week's inspection. His laugh surprised the nearby employees more than any stock-market announcement. The moment was warm because neither tried to own it.

下午，見遙與沉舟在公共餐廳安靜用餐。他承認自己討厭無法立刻解決問題的感覺。她說，這種急躁正是他在談判中危險的原因。他問她是不是討厭他。「不總是。」她答，隨即轉向下週的檢查。他的笑聲比任何股市公告都讓附近員工吃驚。這一刻之所以溫暖，是因為兩人都沒想占有它。

Qing used a screwdriver to remove the cover from the failed relay under supervision. Each serving of dinner was numbered to ensure night-shift operators received meals. The tower's splendor seemed irrelevant beside the dark apartments across the river. Chenzhou's spontaneous laugh surprised the staff when Jianyao rejected his elaborate slogan.

何晴在監督下用螺絲起子拆下故障繼電器外蓋。 每份晚餐都有編號，確保夜班操作員能拿到餐點。 相較河對岸漆黑的公寓，大樓的華麗顯得毫無意義。 見遙拒絕他華麗口號時，沉舟突如其來的笑聲讓員工驚訝。

The board's final act that day was to authorize an independent accounting examination, limited to the emergency fund and its subcontractors. The city retained the right to stop dangerous work. Rongting remained chairman while the investigation continued, and Jianyao remained under scrutiny for the old bypass policy. No one won the empire. Yet the tenth day ended with records preserved, a suspect order traced one step higher, and the public supplied by a system no longer controlled by a single signature.

董事會當天最後一項決議，是授權獨立會計檢查，範圍限定緊急基金與轉包商。市府仍保留停止危險工作的權利。調查期間榮庭繼續任董事長，見遙過去的繞過程序政策也繼續受審。沒有人贏得帝國，但第十天結束時，證據保住了，可疑命令追上了一層，市民也開始由不再受單一簽名控制的系統供電。

A witness explained that her stepfather had worked on the first version of the old substation. The nervous clerk began to stutter before the judge invited her to speak slowly. The city agreed to subsidize emergency batteries for low-income patients during the trial. A synthetic seal material had been used in the forged authorization package.

一名證人解釋繼父曾參與舊變電站的初版工程。 緊張的書記開始口吃，法官請她慢慢說。 市府同意在試辦期間補助低收入病人的緊急電池。 偽造授權封套使用了合成封條材料。
