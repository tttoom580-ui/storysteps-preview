上鎖的水道

The Locked Waterway

陸總，惡女的認罪書我不簽 · 第 8 / 25 章 · 5000–6000 · 英中對照 · 校訂中

用互動版查詞、朗讀與收藏

Three missing batteries were traced to an inland warehouse near the old canal. The route belonged to a logistics company partly owned by Rongting's trust. Jianyao arrived with an independent order to inspect sealed stock, but the warehouse manager refused entry until a city official appeared. She accepted the delay; she would rather lose an hour than corrupt the custody chain. Chenzhou used the waiting time to question his own freight division about possible subcontracting.

三組失蹤電池的運送線索指向舊運河旁的內陸倉庫。路線屬於一家由榮庭信託部分持股的物流公司。見遙帶著獨立簽發的檢查令抵達，倉庫經理卻堅持要等市府官員到場才開門。她接受延誤；寧可少一小時，也不願破壞證物保管鏈。沉舟利用等待時間，詢問自己的貨運部是否曾再轉包。

The accumulation of damaged batteries made the warehouse inspection increasingly urgent. A vendor offered each inspector a sealed beverage, but they declined gifts during the audit. The blond driver in the delivery photograph confirmed that he had used the wrong route. Only the independent laboratory could certify the chemical condition of the stored batteries.

損壞電池不斷堆積，使倉庫檢查越來越迫切。 供應商給每位檢查員密封飲料，但眾人在稽核期間拒絕收禮。 送貨照片裡的金髮司機證實自己走錯了路。 只有獨立實驗室能證明儲存電池的化學狀況。

Inside, the team found battery crates with altered numbers. The contents had been replaced with ordinary equipment. Qing photographed every cut seal and observed that one serial pattern belonged to an older generation of batteries. They could now demonstrate substitution, though they still could not identify the person responsible. Jianyao asked the staff to record which doors had remained locked and which had been opened under the inspection order. No one was allowed a private victory photograph.

倉庫裡的電池箱編號遭到改動，內容物換成一般設備。何晴逐一拍攝被割開的封條，發現其中一組序號屬於更舊的電池世代。他們現在能證明貨品遭調包，卻還不知道是誰動手。見遙要求人員記錄哪些門原本上鎖、哪些門依檢查令打開。不允許任何人拍攝私人勝利照片。

The warehouse manager demanded official clearance before the team opened the locked crates. Jianyao refused to deduct a worker's salary for using a badge that management had failed to replace. Qing said the battery unit was not destined for disposal until its test results were known. The inspection team could exert pressure through lawful requests, not threats of punishment.

倉庫經理要求團隊開啟鎖箱前先取得正式許可。 見遙拒絕因工人使用管理階層未換新的證件而扣薪。 何晴說測試結果出來前，不應認定電池裝置注定報廢。 檢查團隊可以依法提出要求施壓，而非恐嚇處罰。

At lunch a local organizer described the canal community's reliance on pumps and emergency lights. Jianyao admitted she had once approved a supply policy that favored central districts. She offered no excuse about market efficiency. Instead, she invited residents to help design the next test and stated that their choices would be recorded with dissenting opinions intact. Chenzhou asked how she could bear the public criticism. She said criticism was lighter than the cost of another blackout.

午餐時，地方組織者說起運河社區對抽水機與緊急照明的依賴。見遙承認自己曾批准偏向市中心的供應政策，沒有拿市場效率當藉口。她邀請居民一起設計下一次測試，並表示不同意見會完整保留。沉舟問她怎能承受公開批評。她說，批評總比再次停電的代價輕。

A heroic rescue story could not substitute for the full record of warehouse risks. A retired army lieutenant volunteered to organize transport without taking control of the inquiry. Literate workers could read the manual, but the team also prepared diagrams for everyone else. The canal's lotus flowers were untouched, though the adjacent storage fence was broken.

英雄式救援故事不能取代倉庫風險的完整紀錄。 一名退役陸軍中尉志願安排運輸，沒有接管調查。 識字的工人能閱讀手冊，團隊也替其他人準備圖解。 運河裡的蓮花完好，旁邊儲藏圍籬卻破損。

Rongting's legal team circulated an old family photograph claiming Chenzhou's father had funded Jianyao's first appointment. The rumor threatened to make their professional cooperation look like a family arrangement. Both executives disclosed their companies' past transactions, including a forgotten scholarship that had connected their parents. They did not pretend the connection had no meaning; they asked the independent chair to determine whether it required recusal. The answer was yes for one financing decision, no for the safety inquiry.

榮庭的律師團散布一張舊家族合照，聲稱沉舟的父親曾資助見遙第一次任命。謠言讓兩人的專業合作看起來像家族安排。兩位總裁公開雙方公司的舊交易，包括讓父母輩相識的一項獎學金。他們沒有假裝這段關係毫無意義，而是請獨立主席判斷是否必須迴避。結果是某項融資決策須迴避，安全調查則不必。

The marginal cost of a second inspection was small compared with another citywide failure. Two security routes overlap near the gate, creating a blind spot in the records. A provincial transport official reviewed the company's hazardous-cargo permit. The new technician's qualification was checked by an independent professional board.

第二次檢查的邊際成本與另一場全市故障相比微不足道。 兩條保全路線在大門附近重疊，造成紀錄盲點。 一名省級交通官員檢視公司的危險貨物許可。 新技師的資格由獨立專業委員會核對。

The inspection ended with an uncomfortable discovery: one warehouse worker had used an expired clearance badge because management never replaced it. That act violated policy but did not make the worker a thief. Jianyao ordered the safety system fixed and refused a collective punishment. The independent investigators kept the question of substitution open. When Chenzhou offered his umbrella, she took it without bargaining for what came next. There were still things they could share without signing them away.

檢查最後發現一件不舒服的事實：一名倉庫工人因管理階層從未換新證件，只好使用過期通行證。這違反規定，卻不代表工人偷竊。見遙下令修復安全系統，拒絕連坐懲罰。獨立調查員保留調包案的疑點。沉舟遞來傘時，她接了，沒有拿未來當交換條件。他們仍能分享某些東西，而不必簽字讓渡。

Volunteers brought refreshments to the residents waiting outside the warehouse. The team tried to retrieve a broken seal without disturbing the surrounding evidence. The warehouse entrance bordered a swamp that became unsafe after heavy rain. Qing reminded Jianyao that engineering was her vocation, not a favor granted by a president.

志工為倉庫外等待的居民送來茶點。 團隊試圖取回破損封條，同時不干擾周圍證物。 倉庫入口鄰近沼澤，豪雨後變得危險。 何晴提醒見遙，工程是她的志業，不是總裁賞賜的恩惠。
