變電站上方的山丘

The Hill Above the Substation

The second morning began above the city, where a hillside substation overlooked the river crossing. He Qing demonstrated the difference between a command being sent and a command being accepted. The corrupted file proved that a message had disappeared, not who deleted it. Jianyao marked three possible paths on a board: equipment failure, unauthorized intervention, or an authorized intervention concealed afterward. She resisted the desire to choose the third because it resembled the novel's villainous plot.

第二天清晨，眾人抵達俯瞰河道的山丘變電站。何晴示範了「指令送出」與「指令接受」的差別。損壞檔案只能證明一段訊息消失，無法證明是誰刪除。見遙在白板寫下三條路徑：設備故障、未授權介入，或獲授權介入後遭到掩飾。她壓下直接選第三項的衝動，因為那太像原著的陰謀。

An aboriginal community upstream requested access to the substation's emergency warning system. Reports of earlier power failures abound in the neighborhood's handwritten complaint register. Qing asked the technicians to align the two timestamps before comparing the footage. The substation's altitude made it safe from river water but difficult for older residents to reach.

上游原住民族社群要求能接收到變電站的緊急警報。 社區手寫申訴簿裡充滿過去停電的通報。 何晴請技師先對齊兩組時間戳，再比對影像。 變電站的海拔讓它不易淹水，卻使年長居民難以前往。

Chenzhou came with his own safety inspector, but Jianyao refused to let his employees control the evidence. Instead they agreed to appoint a technician chosen by the city and one representative of the night-shift workers. The inspector examined a physical switch that should have remained locked. Its casing was intact; its timestamp seal was not. That distinction mattered. Chenzhou had a reputation for delivering blunt judgments. Today he asked for a measurement before offering one, and Jianyao noticed.

沉舟帶來集團安全檢查員，但見遙拒絕讓他的員工獨占證據管理。最後雙方同意加上由市府選出的技師與夜班工人代表。檢查員查看理應上鎖的實體開關。外殼完好，時間戳封條卻破損。這個差異很重要。沉舟向來以直截了當的判斷聞名，今天卻先要求測量再下結論。見遙注意到了。

Chenzhou hid his annoyance when the independent inspector rejected his preferred testing method. The chairman gave a blunt answer without explaining how the missing seal had been replaced. A student described the dark pedestrian crossing where a cyclist had nearly struck him. No technician was allowed to discard the damaged seal before independent photographs were taken.

獨立檢查員否決他偏好的測試方法時，沉舟壓下惱怒。 董事長回答得直截了當，卻沒解釋封條如何遭替換。 學生描述漆黑的行人穿越道，說自己差點被自行車撞到。 完成獨立拍照前，技師不得丟棄受損封條。

At the community hall, residents described an earlier outage that had never entered the official statistics. A widow explained how her husband's oxygen machine had required a portable battery, and a student described a dangerous walk across an unlit road. Their experiences were not technical proof of sabotage, but they exposed the cost of the company's silence. Jianyao offered free safety inspections through an independent fund. She refused to describe compensation as charity; people had paid for a service that had failed.

社區活動中心裡，居民說起一次從未列入官方統計的舊停電。一名寡婦解釋丈夫生前的氧氣機必須靠備用電池；學生則描述走過沒有路燈的道路有多危險。這些經歷不能當成蓄意破壞的技術證明，卻揭露了公司沉默造成的代價。見遙提出設立獨立基金進行免費安全檢查。她拒絕把賠償說成施捨；人們付費購買的服務確實失效了。

The inspector recorded an unexpected electrical discharge near the damaged control panel. Their joint endeavor would be assessed through measurements, not a flattering press release. The first hurdle was getting both companies to accept a technician chosen by workers. Jianyao changed her posture when the resident began speaking, listening without interruption.

檢查員記錄損壞控制板附近一次意外放電。 雙方共同努力將以測量結果評估，而非以討好的新聞稿。 第一道障礙是讓兩家公司接受工人推選的技師。 居民開始發言時，見遙調整姿勢，沒有打斷對方。

Rongting appeared that afternoon with an opinion poll claiming that the public wanted one decisive ruler for the power network. Jianyao demanded its sample and methods. He laughed and said a company was not a parliament. 'Precisely,' she answered. 'That is why the company must not decide a city's rights without scrutiny.' When he attempted to remove Qing from the site, the worker representative invoked the agreed preservation order. For the first time, the chairman was forced to wait outside his own gate.

下午，榮庭拿著民意調查現身，聲稱市民希望供電網有一位果斷的掌權者。見遙要求查看樣本與方法。他笑說公司又不是國會。「正因如此，」她答，「公司才不能不受審查就決定一座城市的權利。」當他試圖把何晴趕出現場時，工人代表援引剛通過的證據保全命令。董事長第一次被迫在自己的大門外等待。

The team needed precision because a three-minute error could distort the entire timeline. A rigorous inspection revealed a damaged timestamp seal but an undamaged outer casing. Volunteers stood on the shaded terrace to distribute water while the hearing continued. A workers' uprising was not the issue; they were requesting the safety rights already promised.

團隊必須精確，因為三分鐘誤差可能扭曲整段時間線。 嚴格檢查發現時間戳封條損壞，外殼卻完好。 聽證進行時，志工站在遮蔭露臺上發水。 這不是工人起義；他們只是要求已被承諾的安全權利。

Sunset brought no confession, only a pair of photographs showing that the seal had been replaced after its last certified inspection. Chenzhou offered his car when the hillside road became unsafe. Jianyao accepted the ride and paid for the fuel from her own account; she would not let a practical favor become a debt of obedience. In the dark vehicle he asked whether she feared losing her company. 'Of course,' she said. 'But I fear keeping it by making everyone else lose theirs.'

日落沒有帶來認罪書，只帶來兩張照片，顯示封條在上一次合格檢查後被更換過。山路變得危險時，沉舟提供座車。見遙接受搭載，卻堅持自付油費；她不願讓實際幫助變成服從的債務。黑暗車廂裡，他問她是否害怕失去公司。「當然，」她說，「但我更怕為了保住公司，讓別人失去生活。」

A volunteer usher led witnesses through the side entrance to protect their privacy. The versatile backup unit could power either a clinic refrigerator or an emergency radio. The workers retained a safety veto over repairs that failed the written threshold. A widow asked the board to remember patients who depended on home oxygen machines.

志工引導員帶證人從側門進入，以保護隱私。 多功能備用裝置可供應診所冰箱或緊急無線電。 工人保有對不符合書面門檻之維修工作的安全否決權。 一名寡婦請董事會記住依賴家用氧氣機的病人。
