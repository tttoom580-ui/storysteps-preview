The Agreement No One Could Read

陸總，惡女的認罪書我不簽 · 第 7 / 25 章 · 5000–6000 · 英中對照 · 校訂中

The company lawyer discovered an annex written in deliberately obscure language. It transferred maintenance authority to a trust whenever an emergency lasted more than six hours. The blackout had lasted six hours and seven minutes. Jianyao asked why this clause had never been explained to the employees who would carry out its orders. Rongting insisted that sophisticated investors had approved it. Qing answered that complexity could not replace informed consent.

公司律師發現一份措辭刻意晦澀的附約：只要緊急事件超過六小時，維修權就轉給信託。那次停電長達六小時七分鐘。見遙問，為何從沒向負責執行命令的員工解釋這條款。榮庭堅稱成熟投資人已同意。何晴回答，複雜不代表知情同意。

The trust owned one acre of land beside the canal, but ownership did not erase public access. A wealthy bachelor on the board objected to being excluded from the safety committee. Employees organized a charity boxing exhibition to support families hurt by the outage. The disciplinary rules required an independent hearing before any worker could be fired.

信託持有運河旁一英畝土地，但所有權不會抹去公共通行權。 董事會上一位富有的單身漢反對自己被排除在安全委員會外。 員工舉辦慈善拳擊表演，支援停電受影響的家庭。 紀律規則要求解雇任何工人前先有獨立聽證。

Chenzhou brought a specialist in trust law but sat outside the drafting room because his company might benefit from the annex. His willingness to step aside cost him influence over the outcome. Jianyao recognized the discipline, though she did not romanticize it. The lawyer identified a provisional clause: any transfer triggered during a technical emergency required judicial confirmation within two days. No confirmation was on record. The missing document became a more useful question than the trust's expensive language.

沉舟請來信託法律師，自己卻坐在起草室外，因為他的公司可能從附約得利。退出讓他失去影響結果的機會。見遙看見這份自律，但沒有把它浪漫化。律師指出一項暫行條款：技術緊急事件觸發的權力轉移，兩日內必須經司法確認。卷宗裡卻沒有確認文件。缺失的文件比昂貴的法律詞藻更值得追問。

An emigrant engineer explained why the old contract translation was unclear to new workers. Chenzhou refused to equate a delayed audit with a failure of leadership. Rongting's confidence began to falter when the tribunal requested the missing confirmation. The clinic requested backup power for its fertility treatment laboratory.

一名移居海外的工程師解釋舊合約翻譯為何讓新工人難懂。 沉舟拒絕把稽核延誤等同領導失敗。 當法庭要求缺失的確認文件時，榮庭的信心開始動搖。 診所要求為生育治療實驗室提供備用電力。

At a worker forum a technician asked whether their safety veto could be canceled by the annex. Jianyao admitted that the answer was uncertain and requested an expedited interpretation from the independent tribunal. A veteran said executives usually preferred to speak as if every doubt had been settled. She replied that the workers were entitled to know when a decision remained unfinished. The room did not applaud; it began to offer concrete suggestions.

工人論壇上，一名技師問附約能否取消他們的安全否決權。見遙承認答案尚不明確，並向獨立法庭申請加速解釋。資深工人說主管通常喜歡說得像所有疑點都已解決。她回答，工人有權知道決策尚未完成。現場沒有鼓掌，卻開始提出具體建議。

Jianyao would not forsake her obligations merely to keep the board's favor. A gay employee asked whether the revised benefits would cover his husband equally. The workplace gloom lifted slightly after the tribunal protected the workers' safety veto. A survey found the dangerous intersection near the substation lacked proper lighting.

見遙不願為保住董事會好感而背棄自己的義務。 一名男同性戀員工詢問新福利是否同等保障他的丈夫。 法庭保護工人安全否決權後，工作場所的陰鬱稍稍散去。 調查發現變電站旁危險路口缺少適當照明。

That evening Chenzhou found Jianyao sitting alone on an empty stadium bench after a site visit. He offered to walk beside her, not to demand that she explain her childhood or her fear. She told him she had read a story in which everyone called this woman a villain. He took the remark as a metaphor rather than an impossible confession. 'A label is not a verdict,' he said. She replied, 'Then do not make me your next label.'

傍晚，沉舟在勘查後的空體育場長椅找到獨坐的見遙。他提出陪她走一段，沒有逼她交代童年或恐懼。她告訴他，自己讀過一個故事，裡面每個人都稱這個女人為惡女。他把話當成比喻，沒有當成不可思議的告白。「標籤不是判決。」他說。「那你也別替我貼下一張。」她回答。

The route had been damaged by a landslide years earlier and never fully repaired. The community kitchen tried to nourish patients waiting for delayed equipment deliveries. An orthodox interpretation of the old lease favored the trust, but the law required public notice. The prohibition on unsafe work could not be canceled through a private annex.

該路線多年前遭山崩破壞，從未完全修復。 社區廚房努力提供營養餐點給等候設備送達的病人。 按照傳統解讀，舊租約有利信託，但法律仍要求公開告知。 禁止危險作業的規定不能透過私人附約取消。

On the seventh night the tribunal issued an interim interpretation: the annex could not cancel safety duties, and the claimed transfer required additional proof. Rongting angrily denied that it changed anything. Jianyao did not claim victory. She asked the clerk to publish the reasoning in plain language and invited the affected workers to read it before tomorrow's shift. For now, one narrow right had survived a very broad contract.

第七夜，法庭做出暫時解釋：附約不能取消安全義務，主張權力轉移者還須提供更多證明。榮庭憤怒地否認有任何改變。見遙不宣稱勝利，只要求書記以白話公布理由，邀請受影響工人在隔天輪班前閱讀。眼下，一項狹窄卻重要的權利，挺過了一份龐大契約。

A provisional court order preserved public access while the disputed lease was reviewed. The safety committee reached a unanimous decision to postpone the untested repair. Jianyao recognized that her old vanity had made her dismiss criticism too quickly. A woodpecker struck a roadside pole while the technicians inspected the power lines.

暫時法院命令在爭議租約受審期間保住公共通行權。 安全委員會一致決定延後尚未測試的維修。 見遙承認過去的虛榮心讓自己太快忽略批評。 技師檢查電線時，一隻啄木鳥啄著路邊電線桿。
