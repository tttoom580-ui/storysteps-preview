沒有死去的帳戶

The Account That Kept Breathing

陸總，惡女的認罪書我不簽 · 第 11 / 25 章 · 5000–6000 · 英中對照 · 校訂中

用互動版查詞、朗讀與收藏

The dormant account had sent three messages after the former accountant's death. Investigators located its login token on a shared terminal at the trust's headquarters. That finding raised a disturbing possibility: someone had revived an old identity to issue instructions without taking responsibility. Jianyao refused to name the user without terminal logs and witness records. Chenzhou asked whether she ever became tired of uncertainty. She answered that certainty borrowed from a dead person was precisely what they had to resist.

那個休眠帳戶在前會計師過世後還寄過三封訊息。調查員在信託總部的共用終端找到登入憑證。這帶出令人不安的可能：有人借死者身分發布命令，卻不肯負責。見遙拒絕在取得終端紀錄與證人說法前指名。沉舟問她是否會厭倦不確定。她說，正因如此，他們才必須拒絕借死者名義得來的確定感。

The repair room felt hotter than thirty Celsius when the cooling fan stopped. Excessive friction inside the relay produced the unusual vibration recorded during the test. A stray dog began to growl when inspectors approached the abandoned loading dock. The lawyer said a forged instruction could induce workers to override a safe setting.

散熱風扇停轉時，維修室溫度超過攝氏三十度。 繼電器內過度摩擦造成測試中記錄的異常震動。 檢查員靠近廢置裝貨區時，一隻流浪狗開始低吼。 律師說偽造指令可能誘使工人覆寫安全設定。

The former accountant's daughter brought a notebook containing his private calculations. She wanted the family to stop presenting him as a thief, but she did not want his writings made public without context. Jianyao arranged for an independent examiner to compare the figures under a privacy order. The notebook recorded a reserve fund that had been moved repeatedly between firms. None of the entries identified who had approved the transfers. They would have to follow the money farther.

前會計師的女兒帶來一本私人計算筆記。她希望家族不要再把父親說成小偷，也不願不加說明就公開他的文字。見遙安排獨立審查員在隱私保護令下比對數字。筆記記錄一筆準備金在多家公司間反覆移轉，卻沒有標出誰批准。調查還得繼續追錢。

Many families still inhabit older buildings without reliable backup electricity. It was ironic that the trust's security account had been active long after its owner died. A mound of damaged cable insulation lay beside the repair bench awaiting disposal. A peacock from a nearby garden wandered close to the temporary generator fence.

許多家庭仍居住在沒有可靠備電的老建築裡。 諷刺的是，信託保全帳戶在原持有人死後很久仍活躍。 一堆損壞的電纜絕緣材料放在維修台旁等待處理。 附近花園的一隻孔雀走近臨時發電機圍欄。

At the same time, power demand rose with the heat. Qing proposed a test that could demonstrate whether the temporary generators were safe under stress. Jianyao wanted the test witnessed by the clinic and the night-shift workers. Rongting called the plan theatrical. The nurse replied that watching an emergency system operate was not theater when your patients depended on it. The first test passed at moderate load; the second was postponed when a cooling fan began to vibrate.

同時，炎熱天氣讓用電需求升高。何晴提出一項測試，檢驗臨時發電機在高負載下是否安全。見遙希望由診所與夜班工人見證。榮庭稱這是表演。護士回答，當病人仰賴系統時，觀察緊急設備運作就不是戲。第一次中等負載測試通過；第二次因散熱風扇開始震動而延期。

A pianist played for patients while the clinic's backup system was tested. Investigators used a thermal probe to identify the relay's overheating point. The respective duties of the city and company had been confused in the old emergency plan. Jianyao admitted that her previous shortsighted policy had made a second approval optional.

診所測試備電系統時，一位鋼琴家為病人演奏。 調查員使用熱探針找出繼電器過熱點。 舊緊急計畫混淆了市府與公司的各自責任。 見遙承認過去短視的政策使第二道核准變成可選。

Chenzhou stayed after the meeting to help pack measuring equipment. Jianyao asked why he had not returned to his tower. He admitted that he had once judged leadership by speed, and he was learning that a delayed test could be the responsible choice. She told him no single apology would absolve the companies they led. He agreed. They stood under the harsh fluorescent lights discussing spare parts, which felt unexpectedly intimate because neither was performing certainty for the other.

沉舟會後留下協助整理測量設備。見遙問他怎麼不回自己的總部大樓。他承認過去總以速度判斷領導能力，如今才學到延後測試也可能是負責任的選擇。她提醒他，一句道歉不能替兩家公司免責。他同意。兩人站在刺眼的日光燈下談備品，意外有種親近感，因為他們都不必在對方面前裝出確定。

The nurse asked the engineers to simplify the battery instructions for home patients. A vessel crossing the strait was delayed by the same storm approaching the city. The superficial inspection had missed heat damage deep inside the relay cabinet. No director could suppress a failed test merely to preserve the company's image.

護士請工程師簡化居家病人的電池使用說明。 一艘穿越海峽的船因同場暴風雨而延誤。 表面的檢查漏掉了繼電器櫃內部深處的熱損傷。 董事不得只為保護公司形象而壓下失敗測試。

Before dawn, the examiner reported that a fund transfer had been authorized through a board resolution missing from the public minute book. The resolution might have been misfiled, but it might also have been deliberately hidden. Jianyao asked for all numbered minute pages and the office printer's maintenance history. Chenzhou quietly canceled a planned media appearance so the examination would not be turned into a spectacle. A ledger entry had become a path, not a verdict.

天亮前，審查員報告一筆基金轉帳依董事會決議授權，但公開會議紀錄裡找不到那份決議。它可能歸檔錯誤，也可能遭刻意藏起。見遙要求全部編號會議頁與辦公室印表機維修史。沉舟悄悄取消原定媒體露面，避免調查變成表演。一筆帳目成為線索，而不是判決。

The diagram's symmetry made one missing sensor unusually easy to notice. An expired access token had been used to send the dormant account's last message. The public committee tried to unify the three separate emergency notification systems. The nurse emphasized the urgency of restoring the clinic's refrigerated medical supply.

圖面對稱的配置讓一個缺失的感測器格外明顯。 最後一封休眠帳戶訊息使用了一枚過期存取憑證。 公共委員會試圖整合三套分離的緊急通知系統。 護士強調恢復診所冷藏醫療用品供應的急迫性。
