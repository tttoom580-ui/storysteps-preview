沒有地址的人

The People Without Addresses

禁止做夢的最後一座城市 · 第 7 / 25 章 · 5000–6000 · 英中對照 · 校訂中

用互動版查詞、朗讀與收藏

The police questioned Lin for six hours and released her only after a judge confirmed the preservation order. During the interview she learned that the Authority had already announced a 'temporary network repair' in three residential blocks. Ivo explained what the phrase meant: a stronger correction pulse would make it harder for those residents to recall the old railway. Nari organized a lawful medical petition demanding that the pulse be delayed. She needed affected families to sign it, but the neighborhoods had been sealed as a precaution. Lin offered the witnesses a choice: anonymous sworn statements or no participation at all.

警方問了林澈六個小時，直到法官確認保全令才放人。訊問期間，她得知管理局已宣布三個住宅街區將進行「臨時網路維修」。伊沃解釋，那表示更強的修正脈衝會使居民更難想起舊鐵路。娜莉依法提出醫療請願，要求延後脈衝。她需要受影響家庭簽名，但這些社區已以預防名義封鎖。林澈讓證人選擇：匿名具結陳述，或完全不參與。

The bulky old tax ledgers could not fit through the school door without removing its damaged frame. Officials could deem a resident nonexistent by deleting an address, even while collecting that person's taxes. The elder called the city's memory policy a quiet dictatorship built from forms rather than soldiers. An unregistered delivery cart could glide along the lower rails without triggering street cameras.

厚重的舊稅務帳冊，不拆掉損壞門框就搬不進學校。 官員可以刪除地址就認定居民不存在，卻同時向那人收稅。 長者稱城市的記憶政策是一種靠表格而非士兵建立的無聲獨裁。 一輛未登記的送貨車能沿下層軌道滑行，不觸發街道攝影機。

An old utility map revealed an access corridor connecting the sealed blocks to a forgotten lower neighborhood. With court permission, Lin joined a sanitation inspection team. The lower streets were occupied, not abandoned. Families lived beneath the official floor plan, maintaining their own schools and markets without registered addresses. The residents knew that outsiders sometimes forgot them after a night aboveground. They had built routines around that terrifying fact: handwritten names on doors, physical meeting tokens, and shared accounts of who had visited whom. One community elder refused Lin's offer of public recognition. 'First tell us who gets to decide what is remembered,' she said.

一張舊管線圖揭露一條通道，連接封鎖街區與被遺忘的下層社區。林澈取得法院許可，加入衛生檢查隊。下層街道不是廢棄，而是有人居住。家庭住在官方樓層圖之下，維持自己的學校和市場，卻沒有合法地址。居民知道，外來者在地面睡一晚後，有時就會忘記他們。於是大家建立了一套令人心酸的生活規律：門上的手寫名字、實體會面信物，以及彼此記錄誰拜訪過誰。一名社區長者拒絕林澈提出的公開認可。「先告訴我們，誰有權決定大家該記得什麼。」她說。

Children sold small handicraft birds to purchase paper that city printers refused to supply them. A community interpreter translated the inspectors' questions for elders who still used the old district dialect. The neighborhood's lesbian teacher said she feared losing her students, not the disclosure of her identity. For residents, liberation meant an address and protection from raids, not a speech from aboveground heroes.

孩子們販售手工小鳥，換取城市印刷廠拒絕供應的紙張。 社區口譯員替仍說舊區方言的長者翻譯檢查員的問題。 下層社區的女同志教師說，她害怕的是失去學生，而不是身分被揭露。 對居民而言，解放是擁有地址與免於突襲的保障，不是地面英雄的演說。

Lin had no satisfactory answer. The city might make the residents visible by publishing their identities, but visibility could also expose them to arrests, eviction, and medical experiments. Ivo listened while the elder described a girl taken to a sleep laboratory years earlier. Her first name matched the younger sister Lin had been told died of fever. Lin's stomach tightened. She asked to see the source record, not the girl's present address. The elder said the record was kept in a school ledger and agreed to show it only if the girl's guardian approved. Lin accepted the condition, though every instinct urged her to run.

林澈沒有滿意的答案。公布居民身份或許能使他們重新被看見，也可能讓他們遭逮捕、驅逐或醫療試驗。伊沃靜靜聽長者說起多年前一個被帶往睡眠實驗室的小女孩。那女孩的名字，和林澈被告知死於熱病的妹妹相同。林澈胃部一緊。她要求先看紀錄來源，而不是追問女孩現址。長者說紀錄在學校帳冊中，必須得到監護人同意才可查閱。林澈答應，儘管她每個本能都催她快跑。

Lin rejected the lofty promise that everyone would be saved if they simply trusted her. Ivo used a paper utility map to navigate the tunnels without sending a digital location signal. The lower district's volunteer medics warned that overwork would leave them unable to handle another raid. The elder placed a smooth pebble beside every confirmed name on the handwritten community register.

林澈拒絕那種空泛崇高的承諾：只要信她，所有人就會得救。 伊沃用紙本管線圖穿越隧道，沒有發出數位定位訊號。 下層社區志工醫護警告，過勞會使他們無力應付下一次突襲。 長者在手寫社區名冊每個核實姓名旁放一顆光滑小石頭。

At the lower market, a group of workers showed Nari utility bills carrying valid city taxes despite their addresses being officially nonexistent. That contradiction mattered more than slogans. If the government had continued collecting revenue from the people it erased, its public explanation could be challenged through ordinary finance law. Nari requested certified copies and promised a community representative a seat in any future hearing. Ivo remained beside Lin, neither embracing her nor offering easy comfort. When she finally said that she was afraid of finding her sister alive, he answered, 'You are allowed to be afraid and still ask permission before opening her file.'

在下層市場，一群工人拿給娜莉看水電帳單，上面有合法市稅，地址卻在官方資料裡不存在。這個矛盾比口號更重要。如果政府持續向被抹除的人收稅，官方說法就能透過一般財政法受到挑戰。娜莉申請認證副本，承諾未來聽證會有社區代表席位。伊沃站在林澈身旁，沒有擁抱，也沒有廉價安慰。當她終於說害怕發現妹妹還活著時，他回答：「妳可以害怕，也仍然要在打開她的檔案前取得允許。」

The application for registered street addresses remained pending despite decades of tax payments. An abandoned plantation above the tunnels supplied seeds to gardens the city denied existed. The community used a hand plow to cultivate strips of soil beside the old service tracks. No member of any royalty had visited the lower district, though official portraits hung in its old school.

儘管居民繳了幾十年稅，街道地址的登記申請仍在待審。 隧道上方一座廢棄種植園提供種子，供給城市否認存在的菜園。 社區用手推犁在舊維修軌道旁耕作狹長土地。 沒有任何王室成員來過下層社區，舊學校卻還掛著官方肖像。

Before leaving, Lin saw children racing across a narrow courtyard. They shouted street names that existed nowhere on a digital map. Their school clock ran five minutes ahead because no one trusted the municipal time signal. The elder handed Lin a handwritten address and explained it would remain valid only as long as someone could find the door. Lin went back upstairs carrying a certified tax record, an unsigned consent form, and a question more urgent than her own memories. If the lower neighborhood had survived for decades, how many other districts had been made invisible?

離開前，林澈看見孩子們在狹窄院子裡奔跑，喊著數位地圖上不存在的街名。學校時鐘快了五分鐘，因為沒有人相信市政時間訊號。長者給她一個手寫地址，解釋只有還有人找得到那扇門，它才算有效。林澈帶著認證稅單、未簽的同意書，以及一個比自己記憶更急迫的問題回到地面。如果下層社區已存活幾十年，還有多少地方被刻意變成看不見？

The threat of another correction pulse could spur residents to hide rather than testify. Lin watched an elderly couple stroll down a street that did not appear on any official map. A boy with a deep tan from the garden lamps asked whether the real sun was warmer. The elder warned Lin not to treat the residents' missing citizenship as a trifle.

新一輪修正脈衝的威脅，可能促使居民躲藏而不是作證。 林澈看見一對老夫婦漫步在任何官方地圖都沒有的街道。 一名被菜園燈曬黑的男孩問，真正的太陽會不會更暖。 長者警告林澈，別把居民失去公民身分當成小事。
