夢見藍色的女人

The Woman Who Dreamed in Blue

At 3:17 a.m., Lin Che woke with a street name on her tongue: Glass Orchard Lane. According to every municipal map, the lane had never existed. The blue thread tied around her wrist was wet, though she had gone to sleep in a sealed apartment. Across the room, her mandated dream monitor flashed red. A cheerful recorded voice informed her that she had experienced an unauthorized narrative event. She was a memory engineer, trained to distrust the stories a sleeping brain invented. Yet the knot in the thread was one her mother had taught her when Lin was seven.

凌晨三點十七分，林澈醒來，嘴裡還唸著一條街名：玻璃果園巷。但市府所有地圖都顯示，這條巷子從未存在。綁在她手腕上的藍線濕了，明明她是在密閉公寓裡入睡。房間另一端，強制佩戴的夢境監測器亮起紅燈。愉快的錄音聲告知她經歷了一次未經許可的敘事事件。她是記憶工程師，受過訓練，不該相信睡眠中的故事。然而，那條線上的繩結，是母親在她七歲時親手教的。

Lin held the cracked clasp of her monitor in her palm; somebody had opened it with a narrow tool. Vale planned to convene an emergency ethics board before anyone could inspect the missing relay code. A faded children's poster showed a fox cub sleeping beside a blue orchard that maps denied existed. Lin found a dental clinic receipt bearing the erased district's code in her mother's notebook.

林澈把監測環裂開的扣環放在掌心；有人用細工具打開過它。 維爾打算召集緊急倫理委員會，趕在有人檢查失蹤的中繼代碼之前。 褪色的兒童海報畫著一隻小狐狸，睡在地圖否認存在的藍色果園旁。 林澈在母親的筆記裡找到一張牙科診所收據，上面印著被抹除行政區的代碼。

Morning in Everday City began without sunrise. The ceiling projected a pale artificial horizon, and the municipal broadcast repeated the old assurance that dreams transmitted a contagious form of historical confusion. The authorities claimed their overnight corrections were a kindness. Lin had repaired thousands of monitors without asking what they removed. This morning she opened her own device instead. Its sealed record contained a repair request issued two hours before she went to sleep, signed by a technician who had been dead for twenty-five years. Somebody had entered the system while its owner dreamed.

永晝市的早晨沒有日出。城市穹頂投影出蒼白的人造地平線，市政廣播又一次宣稱，夢會傳染一種使人混淆歷史的疾病。當局說每晚的「修正」是一種善意。林澈修過數千個監測環，從沒追問它們究竟刪掉什麼。今天早上，她拆開自己的裝置。密封紀錄裡有一張維修申請，時間竟在她入睡前兩小時，簽名者則已死去二十五年。有人趁佩戴者睡覺時進入系統。

The broadcast called the old governor a dictator, but Lin could find no source for that judgment. A sealed directive ordered engineers to treat every reference to Glass Orchard Lane as corrupted memory. Her childhood dwelling was listed as demolished, although the old utility bill still showed monthly charges. A children's recording made horse hooves gallop across the speakers before its final minute was erased.

廣播稱前任總督是獨裁者，林澈卻找不到任何可以證實這項評語的來源。 一份密封指令要求工程師將所有提到玻璃果園巷的內容視為記憶損壞。 她童年的住所被列為已拆除，舊水電帳單卻仍顯示每月收費。 一段兒童錄音傳出馬蹄奔馳聲，最後一分鐘卻被刪掉。

At the Memory Authority, her supervisor Director Vale demanded the original unit. Lin gave him a carefully labeled copy and kept the unbroken sensor core in her pocket. The machine did not store pictures of dreams, only patterns, body temperature, and commands sent by nearby relay towers. An edit required a remembered sensation, the right sleep phase, and an active relay. That was why the blue thread mattered: its dampness was physical, outside the reach of a memory command. Lin wrote the exact time in a paper notebook her mother had forbidden her to throw away.

在記憶管理局，主管維爾主任要求她交出原裝置。林澈遞上標示清楚的複製品，把未拆封的感測核心留在口袋裡。機器不會儲存清晰夢境，只保存神經模式、體溫與附近中繼塔傳來的命令。要修改記憶，必須有感官錨點、正確睡眠階段及啟動中的中繼器。因此藍線很重要：潮濕是真實物質狀態，記憶指令改不了。林澈把確切時間寫進母親要她永遠留著的紙本筆記。

Repeated warnings about forbidden dreams were designed to heighten fear before the nightly correction cycle. An elderly janitor remembered cleaning blue paint from the transit signs before the district vanished. The Authority's motto, 'Sleep Safely, Remember Correctly,' appeared above every workstation in silver letters. A pilgrim from the sealed southern tunnel had once described a city beyond the official walls.

關於禁夢的反覆警告，是為了在每晚修正程序前加劇民眾恐懼。 一名年老清潔員記得，那個行政區消失之前，他曾擦掉運輸站牌上的藍色油漆。 管理局的座右銘「安睡無虞，正確記憶」以銀色字體掛在每張工作桌上。 一名來自封閉南隧道的朝聖旅人，曾描述過官方城牆外的城市。

A service worker named Ivo caught her examining the relay schedule. He looked older than his thirty-six years, with a burn across one knuckle and the patient expression of someone accustomed to alarms. 'If the records are wrong, tell the Review Office,' he said. 'It reports to Vale,' she answered. Ivo glanced at the camera and then at her notebook. 'Paper doesn't report to anybody.' He did not offer friendship or demand trust. Instead, he showed her a blank inspection form with a missing district code: the same code she had glimpsed beside Glass Orchard Lane.

維修工伊沃撞見她檢查中繼器時刻表。他三十六歲，卻顯得比實際年齡滄桑，一根指節有燒傷疤痕，神情像是早已習慣警報的人。「紀錄有錯就找審查室。」他說。「審查室向維爾報告。」她回答。伊沃看向攝影機，又看向她的筆記本。「紙不向任何人報告。」他既沒急著交朋友，也不要求她信任，只拿出一張缺少行政區代碼的空白檢查單。那個代碼，正是她在玻璃果園巷旁看見的。

No legal precedent allowed the Authority to erase an engineer's private maintenance notes without notice. The agency promised citizens they would reap peace if they surrendered their dreams. The mechanical clock remained stationary during the city's three-second power interruption, preserving a crucial discrepancy. Vale used the word 'correction' as a synonym for deletion whenever journalists asked difficult questions.

沒有法律先例允許管理局在不通知的情況下，抹去工程師的私人維修筆記。 當局承諾人民，只要交出夢境，就能收穫和平。 城市停電三秒時，機械鐘保持靜止，留下了一項關鍵的時間差異。 記者提出尖銳問題時，維爾總把「修正」當成「刪除」的同義詞。

That evening Lin did not announce a conspiracy. She checked the device clock against an old mechanical clock, copied the relay route, and placed the wet thread in a clean glass jar. Then she called the public registry and asked whether Glass Orchard Lane had ever had a postal address. The operator hesitated long enough for Lin to hear someone whisper in the background. 'There is no record,' the woman finally said. Before the call ended, Lin heard a softer sentence, almost hidden by static: 'Don't search the street. Search who was told to forget it.'

當晚，林澈沒有急著宣稱發現陰謀。她用老式機械鐘核對裝置時間，抄下中繼路線，把濕線裝進乾淨玻璃罐。接著她打給公共戶籍室，詢問玻璃果園巷是否曾有郵寄地址。接線員猶豫許久，久到她聽見背景裡有人低語。「查無紀錄。」那女人終於說。電話掛斷前，雜訊裡又傳來更輕的一句：「別找那條街。去找被命令忘記它的人。」

Her mother had once tried to transplant a blue orchard tree into a public courtyard. A city trustee had authorized the transfer of children's records to an unlisted storage facility. The Authority demanded unconditional obedience, but its own equipment still required independent safety checks. Lin refused to utter the required confession that her dream had been a harmless technical malfunction.

她母親曾試圖把一棵藍色果園的樹移植到公共庭院。 一名市府受託管理人曾批准把兒童紀錄轉往未列名的儲存設施。 管理局要求無條件服從，自己的設備卻仍需要獨立安全檢查。 林澈拒絕說出那段規定的自白，否認自己的夢只是無害的技術故障。
