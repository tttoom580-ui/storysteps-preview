消失街道的收據

A Receipt for a Missing Street

禁止做夢的最後一座城市 · 第 3 / 25 章 · 5000–6000 · 英中對照 · 校訂中

用互動版查詞、朗讀與收藏

The basement ledger showed that every object in the children's clinic had once belonged to a neighborhood called Orchard East. A cashier had signed for twelve boxes of toys, while another office recorded their disposal on the same day. Lin checked the handwriting against copies retained by a retired market inspector. The signatures matched, but the second stamp came from the Memory Authority, not the sanitation department. Somebody had turned ordinary possessions into dangerous evidence. Under the final entry she found a handwritten note: 'Ask why the children were moved before anyone was sick.'

地下室帳冊顯示，兒童診所每件物品都曾屬於名叫東果園的社區。收銀員簽收過十二箱玩具，另一個單位卻在同一天登記銷毀。林澈將筆跡與退休市場稽查員留下的影本比對，簽名吻合，第二枚印章卻來自記憶管理局，而不是環保清潔部門。有人把普通財物變成了危險證物。最後一條紀錄下有手寫註記：「問問孩子們為何在任何人發病以前就被遷走。」

The cashier's handwritten signature on the toy receipt survived two rounds of official records cleaning. A routine checkup form showed that the children were healthy before the forced relocation. Police claimed the midnight searches would curb public panic, but the raids only frightened residents. The railway alarm could deafen passengers standing too close to the old tunnel speakers.

玩具收據上收銀員的親筆簽名，熬過了兩輪官方檔案清理。 例行健康檢查表顯示，孩子們在強制搬遷前身體健康。 警方宣稱午夜搜查能遏止恐慌，突襲卻只讓居民更加害怕。 鐵路警報足以震聾站在舊隧道喇叭旁的乘客。

Lin wanted to confront Vale, but Ivo persuaded her to follow the paper trail instead. They traveled to the city market, where most transactions had become entirely digital. One elderly vendor still kept handwritten receipts because her husband had designed the obsolete register. She recognized the missing district code at once. She did not remember living there, yet she could name the bakery, the tram stop, and the smell of rain against hot stone. When Lin asked how she knew, the woman looked frightened. 'I've always known,' she whispered, 'until somebody asks me to explain.'

林澈想當面質問維爾，伊沃卻勸她先循著紙本線索調查。他們去了城市市場，這裡大多數交易早已完全數位化。一名年老攤販還保留手寫收據，因為舊收銀機是她丈夫設計的。她立刻認出失蹤的行政區代碼。她不記得自己住過那裡，卻能說出麵包店、電車站，以及雨落在熱石頭上的氣味。林澈問她為什麼知道，她驚恐地望過來。「我一直都知道，」她低語，「直到有人要我解釋。」

The Ministry labeled the market vendor a disgrace for keeping receipts beyond the retention period. The disposal order listed books and toys as contaminated property although no test result was attached. Lin recorded the vendor's dissent when officials tried to seize her original account book. The public register claimed the families had chosen to emigrate, yet none had signed a departure form.

部會把超過保存期限還留著收據的攤販視為恥辱。 銷毀命令把書與玩具列為受污染財物，卻沒有附任何檢測結果。 官員試圖扣押原始帳本時，林澈記下了攤販的異議。 公共戶籍聲稱這些家庭自願移居他處，卻沒有一戶簽過離境表。

The vendor's description offered a clue, not proof. Lin bought bread and requested permission to photograph the book of receipts. The woman refused to surrender the original but allowed a witnessed copy. They discovered purchases dated after the supposed destruction of Orchard East: flour, school shoes, medicines, and a dozen travel passes. Each payment had been redirected through an account named Civil Adjustment. The name sounded harmless. The amounts did not. Thousands of residents appeared to have purchased tickets for trains the government insisted had never run.

攤販的描述提供線索，還不是證據。林澈買了麵包，並請求拍下收據簿。女人拒絕交出正本，卻同意在見證下製作副本。他們發現東果園據稱毀滅之後仍有人購物：麵粉、學童鞋、藥品，還有十多張通行證。所有款項都被轉入一個叫「民政調整」的帳戶。名稱聽起來無害，金額卻不然。數千名居民似乎買過某些列車的車票，而政府堅稱那些列車從未行駛。

The false relocation policy did nothing to enrich the children whose school materials were confiscated. A lengthy transport invoice listed train journeys that were absent from the official timetable. For Ivo, finding his parents was a lifelong question, not a puzzle Lin could solve with a guess. The vendor said, 'Madam, you can copy this page, but the original stays with me.'

錯誤的搬遷政策沒有改善孩子生活，反而沒收了他們的學習用品。 一張冗長運輸發票列出官方時刻表上不存在的列車旅程。 對伊沃而言，尋找父母是畢生的問題，不是林澈猜一猜就能解的謎。 攤販說：「女士，妳可以抄這一頁，但正本要留在我手裡。」

On the return trip Lin noticed a camera turning to follow Ivo rather than her. He admitted that his parents had left the city when he was nine, though the official register classified them as deceased. He had not seen them since. 'I don't know whether they are alive,' he said. 'Don't promise me they are.' Lin promised nothing. She understood then why he valued paper: he had spent twenty-seven years refusing to accept a bureaucratic sentence as the last word about the people he loved. Their investigation was no longer about her one strange dream.

回程路上，林澈注意到攝影機一直跟著伊沃轉動，而不是盯著她。他承認自己九歲時父母離開城市，官方戶籍卻把兩人列為死亡。他此後再沒見過他們。「我不知道他們是不是還活著，」他說，「別保證他們還活著。」林澈沒有作出保證。她終於明白伊沃為何重視紙張：二十七年來，他一直拒絕讓一道官僚文句成為所愛之人的最後定論。他們查的已不只是她的一場怪夢。

A melancholy railway tune returned to the vendor whenever rain struck the market roof. The city's rules made it permissible to erase receipts if they contradicted an approved history. The public speakers continued to preach obedience while officers confiscated the stalls' paper records. The smell of wet stone was reminiscent of a street Lin had been ordered to forget.

每當雨打在市場屋頂，攤販便想起一段憂傷的鐵路旋律。 城市規定允許銷毀與核准歷史矛盾的收據。 警員沒收攤商紙本紀錄時，公共喇叭仍不斷宣講服從的重要。 濕石頭的氣味讓林澈想起一條被命令忘記的街道。

Back at her apartment, Lin arranged the records on the floor without connecting a terminal. The clinic poster, the vendor's receipts, and the maintenance log all mentioned the same transportation number. She photographed them separately with an old optical camera, then locked the originals in different locations. Before sleeping she left a note where she would see it upon waking: 'The documents exist even if your memory changes.' At 3:17, the monitor flashed again. This time its command log held a clear instruction from headquarters: locate the witnesses before morning.

回到公寓，林澈把紀錄鋪在地上，沒有接上任何終端機。診所海報、攤販收據和維修紀錄都提到同一個運輸編號。她用老式光學相機分別拍下，再把正本鎖到不同地點。睡前，她留下一張醒來就能看見的字條：「即使你的記憶改變，文件仍然存在。」凌晨三點十七分，監測環再次閃光。這一次，命令紀錄清清楚楚寫著總部的指示：天亮前找出所有證人。

There was no rivalry between Lin and Ivo; they needed different skills to verify the same documents. The suspicious transport account deserved scrutiny from an independent auditor, not an instant accusation. The suspense became unbearable when the command log revealed a search order for the witnesses. The old railway trademark on a receipt matched the symbol painted beneath the clinic's animals.

林澈與伊沃之間沒有競爭，他們需要不同專長來核實同一批文件。 可疑的運輸帳戶需要獨立稽核員仔細審查，而不是立刻定罪。 命令紀錄揭露搜捕證人的指示時，懸疑與緊張幾乎令人難以承受。 收據上的舊鐵路商標，與診所動物壁畫底下的符號吻合。
