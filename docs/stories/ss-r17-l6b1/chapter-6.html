城市下方的空氣

The Air Beneath the City

禁止做夢的最後一座城市 · 第 6 / 25 章 · 5000–6000 · 英中對照 · 校訂中

The preservation order permitted a supervised visit to the reservoir chamber. Lin, Ivo, Nari, and a court clerk descended in a maintenance lift that smelled of mineral water and rust. After the final gate closed, their monitors lost contact with the city relay system. Lin felt a sudden pressure behind her eyes, then an unfamiliar lightness, as though a hand had stopped pressing against a bruise she had carried all her life. She did not suddenly remember everything. She remembered one detail: her mother humming while labeling glass jars in a room full of blue pipes. The memory was incomplete, but the pipes were now in front of her.

保全令准許在監督下進入水庫維修室。林澈、伊沃、娜莉和法院書記搭上有礦物水與鐵鏽味的維修電梯。最後一道閘門關上後，監測環失去與城市中繼系統的聯絡。林澈眼後突然一陣壓迫，接著感到難以形容的輕鬆，彷彿一隻長年按在瘀青上的手終於移開。她沒有一下子想起全部，只記起一件小事：母親在滿是藍色管線的房間裡哼歌、替玻璃罐貼標籤。而那些管線此刻就在眼前。

The witness tapes survived because Lin's mother had stored them in airtight metal cylinders. The engineering team could not attain a safe restoration protocol without testing volunteers' reactions first. An old congresswoman had signed the emergency evacuation review and disappeared from later records. One sealed ration crate still contained curry packets labeled for the train station's night workers.

證人錄音帶能留存，是因為林澈母親把它們裝在氣密金屬筒裡。 工程團隊若不先測試自願者反應，就無法達成安全的記憶恢復程序。 一名前任女性議員簽過緊急撤離審查，卻從後來紀錄中消失。 一箱密封口糧裡仍有咖哩包，標示是給車站夜班工人的。

The chamber contained old cooling equipment, unopened ration crates, and a set of tapes stored in airtight containers. Nari insisted that the tapes be listed before anybody played them. The clerk agreed, photographing seals and recording their condition. One label read 'Founding Emergency, Witness Copies.' Another bore a warning in Lin's mother's handwriting: 'Never restore without consent. A person has the right not to remember.' Lin stared at that sentence for a long time. Her mother had opposed the memory machine, but she had also feared what an uncontrolled restoration might do to survivors.

維修室裡有舊冷卻設備、未開封的口糧箱，以及存放於密閉盒中的錄音帶。娜莉堅持先列清冊再播放。書記同意，拍下封條並記錄狀態。其中一張標籤寫著「建城緊急事件：證人副本」，另一張有林澈母親的字跡：「未經同意，絕不可恢復記憶。人有不記得的權利。」林澈久久凝視這句話。母親反對記憶機器，卻也擔心毫無限制的恢復會對倖存者造成什麼傷害。

Lin had begun to despise the lie, but she refused to treat every frightened employee as an enemy. The archived sky schedule described an eclipse that public lessons insisted had never occurred. Nari checked whether the old ration packs were still edible before placing them with the archive. Ivo's esteem for Lin grew when she chose to protect the witness names instead of seeking fame.

林澈開始厭惡謊言，卻拒絕把每位害怕的員工都視為敵人。 封存的天空時刻表記載了一次日食，公共課程卻堅稱那從未發生。 娜莉把舊口糧一起封存前，先確認它們是否仍可食用。 林澈選擇保護證人姓名而非追求名聲，讓伊沃對她更為尊重。

They played only the tape authorized by the preservation order. The voice belonged to a civil engineer recording measurements of an evacuation train. The account described hundreds of people transferred to a lower district before any announced epidemic. It also described a network of doors leading outward beyond Everday's perimeter, though the route names had been deliberately removed. The engineer did not claim a conspiracy. He asked why the departure lists had no matching arrival lists. When the tape ended, Ivo covered his face. His parents' names were among the passengers, not among the dead.

他們只播放保全令允許的一卷錄音帶。聲音來自一名土木工程師，他記錄著撤離列車的測量數據。內容提到在疫情公布之前，就有數百人被送往下層行政區；也提到一套通往永晝邊界外的門，但路線名稱遭刻意移除。工程師沒有直接指控陰謀，只問為何出發名冊找不到對應的抵達名冊。錄音結束時，伊沃掩住臉。他父母的姓名在乘客名單裡，而不是死亡名冊。

A painted evergreen tree marked the maintenance tunnel that led away from the live network. A metal fin on the cooling unit bore the same blue paint as Lin's childhood thread box. A flake of old paint fell from the ceiling when the evacuation tape began to play. The inventory distinguished emergency supplies from merchandise once sold to the missing district.

一棵彩繪常綠樹標記著通往現行網路之外的維修隧道。 冷卻裝置上的金屬鰭片，帶著和林澈童年線盒相同的藍漆。 撤離錄音帶開始播放時，一片老油漆從天花板剝落。 清冊將緊急物資與昔日出售給失蹤行政區的商品分開。

Lin wanted to run upstairs and broadcast everything. Nari stopped her. The tape identified living families, children, and people whose whereabouts were still unknown. Publishing it without protection would give the Authority a list of witnesses to seize. They agreed to file a sealed evidence notice and request an independent medical team before allowing anyone to undergo memory restoration. The court clerk, previously silent, asked whether Lin trusted the judge. 'Not completely,' she said. 'That is why we need a copy in more than one lawful place.' The clerk nodded and signed the inventory.

林澈想立刻跑上樓，向全市播出所有發現。娜莉攔住她。錄音點出了可能仍在世的家庭、孩子，以及下落不明的人。若毫無保護地公開，就等於送給管理局一份可逮捕證人的名單。他們同意遞交密封證據通知，並在任何人接受記憶恢復前，先申請獨立醫療團隊。一直沉默的書記問林澈是否信任法官。「不完全信任。」她說，「所以我們需要把副本合法存放在不只一個地方。」書記點頭，在清冊上簽了名。

The monotony of the relay's steady hum made its sudden silence in the chamber feel frightening. Keeping paper travel diaries had once been a common pastime before the Authority discouraged writing. The original design required redundancy: two independent archives in case one system failed or was compromised. Lin wanted to roam the lower tunnels, but the court order authorized only one chamber.

中繼器單調不變的嗡鳴，讓維修室裡突如其來的寂靜格外可怕。 在管理局不鼓勵書寫以前，紙本旅行日記曾是常見消遣。 原始設計要求備援機制：兩套獨立檔案，防止其中一套故障或遭滲透。 林澈想在下層隧道四處探查，但法院命令只允許進入一間維修室。

When they returned aboveground, the artificial sky had turned black for its scheduled evening. The monitors resumed transmitting as soon as the lift crossed the relay boundary. Lin felt the pressure return behind her eyes. She wrote down what she had remembered before it faded. Beside the words 'blue pipes,' she added, 'Memory is not proof. The tapes are evidence that must be authenticated.' Outside the reservoir, a municipal car waited with its engine running. An officer stepped out and asked Lin to explain why the sealed chamber had been opened. She presented the court order and asked for counsel.

他們回到地面時，人造天空正按排程轉為黑夜。電梯一跨過中繼邊界，監測環便恢復傳訊，林澈眼後的壓力也回來了。她趕在記憶淡去前寫下所想起的事。在「藍色管線」旁，她加上一句：「記憶不是證據。錄音帶仍須鑑定。」水庫外，一輛市府車發動著引擎等待。警員下車，要求林澈解釋為何打開密封維修室。她出示法院命令，並要求律師在場。

Vale had dismissed the engineers' warnings as rubbish without ever reading the measurements. An official poster promised to slay the 'monster of dreams,' turning ordinary sleep into an imagined enemy. A sly alteration in the tape label hid the passengers' true destination under a routine maintenance code. Cold vapor rose from the old cooling pipes as the preservation team opened the storage cabinet.

維爾未曾讀過測量數據，就把工程師警告斥為垃圾話。 官方海報宣稱要殺死「夢的怪物」，把普通睡眠塑造成虛構敵人。 錄音帶標籤上的狡猾修改，把乘客真正目的地藏在例行維護代碼下。 保全小組打開儲存櫃時，舊冷卻管冒出冰冷水霧。
