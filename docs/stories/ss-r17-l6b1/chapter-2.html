雜訊裡的鱷魚

The Alligator in the Static

禁止做夢的最後一座城市 · 第 2 / 25 章 · 5000–6000 · 英中對照 · 校訂中

The next day's repair order sent Lin into a children's sleep clinic, where every wall bore smiling animals. The youngest patients had learned to describe dreams as 'noise,' because adults corrected them whenever they said 'story.' A little boy named Jun pointed at a painted alligator and insisted it wore a conductor's cap. The clinic psychologist called that impossible. Lin knew the picture came from a children's transit poster dated before the city's official founding. She asked for the original paper poster, not an image on a screen, and the nurse quietly led her to a locked storage room.

第二天的維修單讓林澈走進兒童睡眠診所，牆上到處是笑咪咪的動物。最小的病童學會把夢叫做「雜訊」，因為只要說成「故事」，大人就會糾正。小男孩小鈞指著畫上的鱷魚，堅持牠戴著列車長的帽子。診所心理師說那不可能。林澈卻認得那張圖，來自建城官方日期以前的兒童交通海報。她要的是紙本原件，不是螢幕照片。護理師悄悄領她走進上鎖的儲藏室。

Mira promised to abide by the clinic's consent rules even while helping Lin preserve the invoice. The director would not affirm the official opening date without seeing the original ledger. The painted alligator on the clinic wall wore a conductor's cap from an erased railway line. An old recording played a low bass note that made Jun remember the arrival of a train.

米拉承諾遵守診所的同意規則，同時協助林澈保存發票。 主任沒看過原始帳冊，不願確認官方啟用日期。 診所牆上的彩繪鱷魚戴著一頂來自被抹除鐵路的列車長帽。 舊錄音響起低沉的低音，讓小鈞想起列車進站。

The poster had been folded around an invoice with two versions of the same date. In the official calendar, the old railway had opened in 2167; in the invoice, it was already thirty years old. A transit stamp and three signatures remained visible under ordinary light. Lin recorded them by hand. A message from Vale arrived ordering her to stop 'distressing vulnerable patients.' She agreed not to interview the children again without parental consent, but she refused to abandon evidence that existed independently of their dreams. The nurse, Mira, asked whether the city had ever belonged to somebody else.

海報裡摺著一張發票，同一天竟有兩種日期。官方年表說舊鐵路於二一六七年啟用，但發票顯示那時鐵路已經有三十年歷史。普通燈光下仍能看見運輸印章和三個簽名。林澈把它們逐一抄下。維爾傳訊命令她停止「驚擾脆弱病患」。她同意未獲家長同意前不再訪問孩子，卻拒絕放棄獨立於夢境而存在的證據。護理師米拉問她，這座城以前是不是也屬於別人。

The children's colloquial word for the forbidden street survived in a playground counting rhyme. Treating forgotten dreams as commonplace noise was part of the clinic's training manual. The hospital's exemption would expire at midnight, giving them only hours to examine the records. Ivo discovered that an old fuse had protected the basement archive from the memory relay network.

孩子口語中稱呼禁忌街道的詞，仍留在遊戲數數歌裡。 把被遺忘的夢當成尋常雜訊，是診所訓練手冊的一部分。 醫院的特別許可將在午夜到期，留給他們查閱紀錄的時間只剩數小時。 伊沃發現一枚舊保險絲，讓地下檔案室沒有接上記憶中繼網路。

At lunch, Ivo met Lin beneath the hospital's maintenance stairs. He explained that the overnight signals passed through relays on every residential block. The monitors measured patterns but could not create memories from nothing; they reinforced particular gaps by interrupting the moment a person was about to recall a familiar smell, song, or face. The system required a match between the signal and an existing sensory anchor. That was why erased neighborhoods sometimes survived in songs, recipes, and badly repaired signs. Lin understood that she was not hunting a single hidden video. She was searching for traces that correction could not reach.

午餐時，伊沃在醫院維修樓梯下和林澈碰面。他解釋，夜間訊號透過每個住宅街區的中繼器傳送。監測環只量測神經模式，不能憑空製造記憶；系統會在人快要想起熟悉的氣味、歌曲或面孔時打斷回憶，藉此強化記憶缺口。它必須讓訊號對應到既有的感官錨點。因此被抹去的街區，有時仍留在歌曲、食譜和修壞的招牌裡。林澈終於明白，自己找的不是單一祕密影片，而是修正程式碰不到的痕跡。

Mira offered quiet hospitality to worried parents, never asking for their children's forbidden dream details. The authorities called the visions infectious, although the clinic could provide no evidence of transmission. A liter of cleaning fluid had spilled over the paper archive, obscuring several names but not the stamps. A novice technician had left the storage sensor disconnected, accidentally preserving the original poster.

米拉溫和接待焦慮的家長，從不逼問孩子的禁忌夢境內容。 當局稱那些幻象有傳染性，但診所拿不出任何傳播證據。 一公升清潔液灑在紙本檔案上，模糊了幾個姓名，印章卻還清楚。 一名新手技師曾讓儲藏室感測器保持斷線，意外保存了原始海報。

The clinic director summoned Lin for a disciplinary interview. She asked for the request in writing and brought Mira as a witness. Rather than accusing the director of a cover-up, Lin showed the contradictory dates and asked whether the archive permitted a physical audit. The director's hands shook. He said the order came from a committee that no longer appeared in the government's organizational chart. When he tried to retrieve its contact address, his terminal produced only a blank gray square. 'Don't use this machine,' he said at last. 'The basement has a paper ledger.'

診所主任召見林澈接受紀律訪談。她要求書面通知，也請米拉在場作證。林澈沒有直接控告主任掩蓋真相，而是拿出彼此矛盾的日期，問檔案庫能否接受實體查核。主任的手微微發抖。他說命令來自一個已從政府組織圖上消失的委員會。當他試著查聯絡地址，終端機只顯示空白灰框。「別用這台機器了。」他終於說，「地下室有紙本帳冊。」

Lin considered it rash to accuse the clinic staff before she understood who controlled their systems. The old invoice and the official date could not reconcile without an independent explanation. The shabby ledger cabinet looked worthless, which was probably why nobody had removed it. A ceiling spotlight illuminated the faded train route when Mira opened the basement door.

林澈認為尚未查清誰控制系統，就指責診所員工太過魯莽。 沒有獨立說明，舊發票與官方日期便無法相互調和。 破舊的帳冊櫃看起來毫無價值，可能因此沒人搬走。 米拉打開地下室門時，一盞聚光燈照亮了褪色的列車路線。

That night the public screens praised the city's perfect record of restful sleep. Lin watched the children leaving the clinic with their parents, their eyes drawn to the animal posters as if something there had survived. She would not use Jun as a witness or publish his name. Instead she copied the invoice number into her notebook, signed her service log accurately, and arranged with Mira to inspect the ledger under hospital policy. The next move was smaller than a revolution: finding out who had paid to repaint the alligator in a train conductor's cap.

那天晚上，公共螢幕稱讚全市完美的安眠紀錄。林澈看著孩子們跟父母離開診所，目光仍不由自主地投向動物海報，好像有什麼在那裡倖存。她不會拿小鈞當證人，也不會公開他的姓名。她只是把發票號碼抄進筆記，忠實簽署維修紀錄，再和米拉約好依醫院規定查閱帳冊。下一步還談不上革命，只是查清楚誰付錢重畫了那隻戴列車長帽子的鱷魚。

The clinic offered apple syrup before sleep tests, a smell several former patients remembered distinctly. Lin had to tilt the poster to see the original ink beneath the official repainting. The artificial twilight outside the clinic concealed the fact that no real sun had reached the street. An old label marked whiskey lay in the storeroom beside the railway invoices, dated before the city's founding.

診所做睡眠測試前會提供蘋果糖漿，那股氣味讓好幾名前病患記得格外清楚。 林澈必須傾斜海報，才看見官方重新上色底下的原始墨跡。 診所外的人造暮光掩蓋了真實太陽根本照不到街道的事實。 一張印著威士忌的舊標籤躺在儲藏室鐵路發票旁，日期早於官方建城日。
