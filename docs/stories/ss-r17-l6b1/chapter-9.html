畫出雨的孩子

The Children Who Drew Rain

禁止做夢的最後一座城市 · 第 9 / 25 章 · 5000–6000 · 英中對照 · 校訂中

用互動版查詞、朗讀與收藏

An elementary teacher asked Lin to look at drawings collected over twenty years. Many children had painted the same long yellow train passing through a field of blue trees. Their artwork differed in style, but the number on the front carriage appeared again and again. The teacher had kept the drawings in cardboard portfolios after digital files were quietly removed from the education portal. Lin did not assume the children shared a supernatural vision. She compared dates, lesson plans, and the old transit poster. The children were drawing a place their families had once described before the city prohibited those descriptions.

一名小學老師請林澈查看二十年來收集的畫作。許多孩子畫了相同的黃長列車，穿過一片藍樹林。畫風各異，車頭號碼卻一再出現。教育入口網站的數位檔案被悄悄移除後，老師把畫放進紙板作品夾保存。林澈沒有假設孩子們共享超自然幻象，而是比較日期、教案和舊運輸海報。孩子們畫的，是城市禁止家人描述以前、家庭裡曾經談過的地方。

A teenager with acne said the clinic treated his ordinary discomfort as proof that dreams were dangerous. Only licensed staff could administer the sleep medication, yet automated records named absent employees. The Authority's brochure cited a medical study that no independent researcher could find. The children had been told that confinement protected them, but many described the clinic as captivity.

一名長青春痘的少年說，診所把他的普通不適當成夢境有害的證據。 只有持照人員能施用安眠藥，系統紀錄卻列出當天缺席的員工。 管理局的宣傳手冊引用一項獨立研究人員誰也找不到的醫學研究。 孩子們被告知隔離是保護，許多人卻把診所形容為囚禁。

The school principal, fearing dismissal, demanded that the collection remain private. Lin agreed and asked whether the teacher would permit independent dating of the paper and ink. At an old laboratory, Nari arranged a non-destructive examination. Results showed the earliest drawings preceded the government's account of the railway's construction. They were not enough to establish where the train went, but they undermined the claim that the image had been invented recently by rebels. Lin wrote the distinction clearly. She had once built a system that rewarded certainty; now she was learning the value of admitting what remained unknown.

校長害怕被解職，要求這批畫作保密。林澈同意，並問老師是否願意讓獨立人員檢驗紙和墨水的年代。娜莉在舊實驗室安排非破壞性檢測。結果顯示最早的畫作，早於政府所稱的鐵路興建日期。它們還不足以證明列車的去向，卻動搖了叛軍最近捏造圖像的說法。林澈清楚記下這個差別。她曾建立一套獎勵確定答案的系統，如今正學習承認未知的價值。

Officials tried to cram decades of different histories into one approved textbook chapter. Lin asked the teacher to detach the drawing from a damaged frame without tearing its original edge. Dr. Asa warned that discomfort during sleep was not by itself evidence of an infectious disease. The forthcoming parent meeting would examine school transfer records rather than children's private dreams.

官員試圖把幾十年的不同歷史硬塞進一章核准教科書。 林澈請老師小心從壞掉的畫框拆下畫作，別撕裂原本邊緣。 阿莎醫師警告，睡眠不適本身不能證明有傳染疾病。 即將舉行的家長會將檢查學校轉學紀錄，而不是孩子的私人夢境。

The principal eventually disclosed a second archive: attendance lists showing several children disappearing from classrooms during the founding period. Their records had been reclassified as transfers, but the destination schools never received them. One listed student shared Lin's surname and her sister's birth date. Lin stopped reading. She asked Nari to contact the independent guardian assigned to the lower district and request permission before revealing more. The principal looked bewildered. He had assumed she would seize the file. 'It's her history,' Lin replied, 'even if I am part of it.'

校長最終拿出第二份檔案：出席名冊顯示建城時期有幾名孩子從班級消失。紀錄後來改列轉學，但目的地學校從未收到人。其中一個學生與林澈同姓，生日也與她妹妹相同。林澈停下閱讀，請娜莉聯絡下層社區的獨立監護人，先取得許可再揭露更多。校長困惑地看著她。他原以為林澈會直接拿走檔案。「那是她的歷史，」林澈回答，「即使我也是其中一部分。」

A side lamp helped illuminate an erased carriage number on the oldest train drawing. The school kept a small jade token donated by a parent from the missing eastern district. The children's joyous train drawings contrasted painfully with the city's official story of terror. The teacher taught media literacy by asking pupils how to tell a drawing from a verified historical record.

一盞側燈幫助照出最古老列車畫作上被擦掉的車廂編號。 學校保存著一枚小玉牌，是失蹤東區一名家長捐贈的。 孩子們快樂的列車畫作，與城市官方的恐怖敘述形成令人心痛的對比。 老師透過辨別圖畫與經驗證史料的差異，教授媒體識讀能力。

Meanwhile, the Authority released a brochure warning parents that old railway stories could 'reactivate inherited delusions.' The brochure quoted a medical study that nobody on the panel could locate. Dr. Asa publicly requested its methods and sample size. Vale refused, then accused the doctors of encouraging disobedience. Parents responded not by marching, but by asking practical questions at school meetings: Why had records been altered? Who had approved the nightly settings? What happened to children classified as transferred? Those questions could not be solved by silencing a single engineer.

與此同時，管理局發布手冊，警告家長舊鐵路故事可能「重新啟動遺傳性妄想」。手冊引用一項醫療研究，但小組裡沒人找得到。阿莎醫師公開要求方法與樣本數。維爾拒絕，反而指責醫師鼓勵不服從。家長沒有立即遊行，而是在學校會議提出實際問題：紀錄為何遭修改？誰批准夜間設定？被列為轉學的孩子去了哪裡？這些問題不是讓一名工程師閉嘴就能解決的。

An anonymous narrator on an old school tape described the blue door in the same words as the drawings. The smell of wet railway stone reached Lin's nostril when she opened a box of old school tiles. A map in the teacher's collection placed the missing railway on a peninsula outside the city wall. The potent combination of censorship and repeated correction had distorted what many residents thought possible.

一卷舊學校錄音帶裡，匿名旁白用和畫作一樣的詞描述藍門。 林澈打開一箱舊學校地磚，濕潤鐵路石頭的氣味鑽進她鼻孔。 老師收藏的地圖把失蹤鐵路畫在城牆外一座半島上。 審查與反覆修正的強大組合，扭曲了許多居民對可能性的認知。

As Lin left the laboratory, the teacher handed her one drawing whose reverse side showed a different scene: a blue door at the end of a railway platform. Beneath it a child had written, 'This is where my brother went, and someday I will find him.' Lin's hands trembled, but she did not show the words to a camera. She carried the paper back to the school vault. Her next discovery had to come from adults who could consent to testimony. The children had already carried more of the city's history than any child should have been asked to bear.

林澈離開實驗室時，老師交給她一幅畫，背面是不同場景：鐵路月台盡頭的一扇藍門。下面有孩子寫道：「哥哥去了這裡，有一天我會找到他。」林澈手發抖，卻沒把字拿給攝影機看。她將畫送回學校保險庫。下一個發現必須來自能同意作證的成年人。這些孩子已經承擔了太多城市歷史，遠超過任何孩子該承受的重量。

Some parents asked for the revival of handwritten school registers to prevent silent edits. The selective deletion of destination schools made ordinary transfers look like spontaneous disappearances. One parent searched for a stepchild whose name appeared on the same vanished train list. A child's picture of a trout beneath the railway bridge matched a fishing guide printed before the founding.

部分家長要求恢復手寫學校名冊，以防無聲修改。 選擇性刪除目的地學校，讓普通轉學看起來像突然失蹤。 一名家長尋找自己的繼子女，名字出現在同一份消失列車名單上。 孩子畫在鐵路橋下的鱒魚，與建城以前出版的釣魚指南吻合。
