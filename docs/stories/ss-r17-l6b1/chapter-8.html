醫療例外

A Medical Exception

禁止做夢的最後一座城市 · 第 8 / 25 章 · 5000–6000 · 英中對照 · 校訂中

用互動版查詞、朗讀與收藏

The city announced a public-health review after Nari submitted the lower district's tax records. Vale expected the review to prove that remembering forbidden places caused brain damage. Lin insisted on an independent medical panel and refused to hand over the residents' names. Her own supervisor placed her on paid leave, describing her caution as insubordination. Ivo escorted her to the hospital, but she did not allow him to speak for her. She presented the engineering facts herself: the monitors identified neural patterns, sent timed signals, and depended on sensory anchors. No controlled study had yet shown that ordinary dreams caused a contagious illness.

娜莉送交下層社區稅務紀錄後，城市宣布進行公共衛生審查。維爾想讓審查證明記得禁忌地點會造成腦損傷。林澈堅持成立獨立醫療小組，也拒絕交出居民姓名。主管讓她帶薪停職，稱她的小心謹慎是不服從命令。伊沃陪她去醫院，但她沒有讓他替自己發言。她親自說明工程事實：監測環辨識神經模式，按時間傳送訊號，還需要感官錨點。至今沒有受控研究證明，普通夢境會造成傳染疾病。

The hospital stocked every necessary antibiotic, but medicines could not reverse an improperly timed neural pulse. Families began to applaud when the court suspended the strongest correction settings in their district. An astronomer explained that the clinic's perfect sky was a projection, not evidence about the world outside. The Authority had tried to computerize every patient statement, leaving almost no independent paper copies.

醫院備有必要的抗生素，藥物卻無法逆轉時機錯誤的神經脈衝。 法院暫停該區最強的修正設定時，家屬開始鼓掌。 一名天文學家解釋，診所完美的天空是投影，不能證明外面的世界如何。 管理局試圖將每份病患陳述電腦化，幾乎不留下獨立的紙本副本。

A neurologist named Dr. Asa examined the correction equipment without turning it on. She discovered that the strongest pulses were associated with headaches, disorientation, and occasional breathing irregularities. The medical records had classified those side effects as symptoms of forbidden dreaming. This reversal shocked Lin: the treatment might be producing the illness used to justify it. Asa warned them not to draw conclusions from a small sample. She proposed a voluntary comparison between residents whose monitors had failed and those whose monitors remained active. The protocol would be reviewed by representatives from both groups.

神經科醫師阿莎沒有啟動設備，先檢查記憶修正裝置。她發現最強脈衝與頭痛、定向感混亂及偶發呼吸異常有關。醫療紀錄卻把這些副作用分類為禁忌夢境的症狀。這種因果顛倒令林澈震驚：所謂治療可能正製造被拿來證明它必要的疾病。阿莎警告不能以小樣本下定論。她提議在自願前提下，比較監測環故障與正常運作兩組居民，由雙方代表一起審查試驗規範。

Ivo helped disable the overheating relay before it could injure anyone on the hospital floor. Nurses had to evacuate patients according to the written plan rather than follow Vale's sudden instructions. The medical panel asked why expenditure on sleep control had tripled while ordinary clinics lost funding. One patient became irritable after a high-intensity pulse, a side effect hidden in the official statistics.

伊沃協助停用過熱中繼器，避免醫院樓層有人受傷。 護理師必須依書面計畫疏散病患，而非聽從維爾的臨時指示。 醫療小組詢問，為何睡眠控制支出增加三倍，一般診所預算卻遭削減。 一名病患接受高強度脈衝後變得易怒，這項副作用被官方統計隱藏。

As the panel met, a sudden alert ordered the evacuation of one hospital floor. An engineer had detected a faulty relay producing excessive heat. Ivo helped disable it while the hospital staff moved patients according to a written emergency plan. Lin saw children asleep without corrections for the first time. Nothing supernatural happened. A few cried; most simply slept. Jun woke and asked his mother whether he could draw the train he had seen. His mother said yes, then asked him to put the drawing away until they got home. Lin understood her caution. It was a protective choice made under fear, not consent to the system.

小組開會時，警報突然要求撤離醫院一層樓。工程師發現故障中繼器產生過多熱量。伊沃協助關閉設備，醫護依書面緊急計畫移動病患。林澈第一次看見沒有修正訊號的熟睡孩子。沒有發生任何超自然事件。少數孩子哭了，大多只是睡覺。小鈞醒來，問媽媽能不能畫夢裡的列車。媽媽說可以，但請他回家再拿出來。林澈理解這份謹慎：那是恐懼環境下保護孩子的選擇，不代表同意整套制度。

The lower district demanded that missing patients' kin be notified before any memory study began. Dr. Asa offered voluntary meditation sessions as a low-risk alternative for patients struggling with sleep. A nickel contact inside the old monitor showed corrosion inconsistent with the reported replacement date. Without the constant pulse, several patients could orient themselves by the windows and handwritten signs.

下層社區要求，任何記憶研究開始前，都必須通知失蹤病患的親屬。 阿莎醫師提供自願的冥想課程，作為睡眠困難病患的低風險選擇。 舊監測器內的鎳製接點有腐蝕痕跡，與申報的更換日期不符。 沒有持續脈衝後，幾名病患能依窗戶及手寫標示重新辨認方向。

The preliminary report did not declare the dream ban fraudulent. It stated that evidence for its medical necessity was insufficient and that the stronger pulses posed documented risks. Vale attacked the panel as irresponsible. Dr. Asa calmly requested the original safety trials, whose location the Authority refused to disclose. Nari asked the court to suspend the most intense settings until those records were inspected. The court granted a narrow order for the hospital district only. Residents celebrated quietly, aware that other neighborhoods remained exposed. Lin celebrated with tea, then returned to her paper notes. A single favorable ruling could be reversed; a reproducible finding could not be dismissed so easily.

初步報告沒有直接宣布禁夢令是騙局，而是指出支持其醫療必要性的證據不足，且強脈衝存在已記錄的風險。維爾指責小組不負責任。阿莎冷靜地要求查看原始安全試驗，但管理局拒絕透露位置。娜莉向法院申請，在紀錄查核前暫停最強設定。法院只針對醫院行政區發出有限命令。居民低調慶祝，也知道其他街區仍暴露於風險。林澈喝了杯茶，又回到紙本筆記。單一有利裁定可能被推翻，可重現的研究結果卻不容易被輕易否定。

The museum used the outdated label 'oriental' on a medical artifact; the curator agreed to replace it with a precise origin. A child treated for pneumonia had been wrongly classified as suffering from forbidden-dream sickness. The neurologist refused to activate the untested prototype until an ethics committee approved the trial. The psychiatry department confirmed that fear and loss of sleep could explain some patients' symptoms.

博物館用過時的「東方的」標籤描述醫療文物，館長同意改用精確產地。 一名肺炎孩童被錯誤分類為罹患禁忌夢境疾病。 在倫理委員會批准試驗前，神經科醫師拒絕啟動未測試的原型機。 精神醫學部確認，恐懼與睡眠不足能解釋部分病患症狀。

That evening Lin and Ivo walked beside the dark canal. He told her about his parents' last letter, which described a journey to the coast. She said her mother's safety warning had protected strangers even after her disappearance. Ivo reached toward her hand, then paused. 'May I?' he asked. She answered yes. The gesture did not repair their histories, and neither pretended it did. Above them, the projected stars were perfectly arranged. Somewhere beyond the dome, she thought, there might be stars that nobody had programmed. Their next task was to find the original safety trials and learn why they had been hidden.

那天傍晚，林澈與伊沃沿著黑暗運河散步。他說起父母最後一封信，裡面描述去海岸的旅程。她說母親留下的安全警告，即使在人消失後仍保護陌生人。伊沃伸手想牽她，又停住。「可以嗎？」他問。她說好。這個動作沒有修復兩人的過去，誰也沒假裝已修好。頭頂投影的星星排列得完美無缺。她想，穹頂外某處也許有沒有人編排過的星星。下一項任務，是找出原始安全試驗，以及它們為何被藏起來。

The panel demanded a robust study with independent reviewers, not a promotional chart from the Authority. Vale claimed sovereign authority over all dreams, but the court questioned whether any such power existed. A hospital spokesperson said the ruling covered only one district and did not settle the wider case. Behind the children's wardrobe, staff found an unused cable channel leading to the relay maintenance shaft.

小組要求有獨立審查員參與的嚴謹研究，而非管理局的宣傳圖表。 維爾宣稱政府對所有夢境擁有主權，法院卻質疑這種權力是否存在。 醫院發言人表示，裁定只涵蓋一個行政區，尚未解決整體案件。 兒童衣櫃後面，員工發現一條未使用的電纜通道，通往中繼器維修井。
