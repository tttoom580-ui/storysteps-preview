死去世界開來的列車

The Train from the Dead World

禁止做夢的最後一座城市 · 第 10 / 25 章 · 5000–6000 · 英中對照 · 校訂中

用互動版查詞、朗讀與收藏

The missing trains had not ended at the city border. A repair map from the school's archive showed a service line crossing a sealed western gate. Lin and Ivo traveled to the gate with Nari and an authorized inspector. The guards insisted the territory beyond was uninhabitable after an ancient catastrophe. Yet through a narrow ventilation grille came the smell of sea salt and a distant mechanical whistle. Lin asked the inspector to record both observations. They could not prove that people lived outside, but the air certainly did not smell like a dead world.

失蹤的列車並沒有在城市邊界終止。學校檔案裡的維修圖顯示，一條支線穿過封閉的西門。林澈、伊沃和娜莉帶著授權檢查員前往。警衛堅稱外面在古老災難後已無法居住。然而，狹窄通風柵欄裡傳來海鹽味和遠方機械汽笛聲。林澈請檢查員把兩者寫進紀錄。這還不能證明城外有人，卻至少表示空氣不像死寂世界。

An old training film used an animate cartoon train to teach children the route beyond the western gate. Simple arithmetic showed that reported train repairs continued long after the official closure date. Ivo called the live coastal signal an awesome discovery, then carefully noted its unverified source. The railway worker offered celery soup made from vegetables grown in a garden near the sealed gate.

一部舊教學影片用栩栩如生的卡通列車教孩子認識西門外路線。 簡單的算術就能看出，列車維修紀錄在官方封閉日期之後仍持續多年。 伊沃說即時海岸訊號是驚人的發現，隨後仍謹慎註明來源尚未驗證。 鐵路維修員拿出芹菜湯，食材種在封閉城門旁的菜園。

Nari found a railway maintenance worker who had kept an obsolete mechanical switch in his home. The switch's metal plate bore dates extending six years beyond the official closure. The man agreed to identify his own repairs but refused to speculate about passengers. His testimony was modest and therefore valuable. When Vale's guards arrived, the inspector reminded them that physical evidence under a court order could not be removed without a new warrant. The guards retreated, furious. For the first time, Lin watched the state obey a rule it had written for itself.

娜莉找到一名鐵路維修員，他家裡留著一個淘汰的機械轉轍器。金屬銘牌上的日期比官方封閉日期還晚六年。維修員願意確認自己做過的修理，卻拒絕猜測乘客身分。他的證詞克制，因而更有價值。維爾的警衛抵達時，檢查員提醒他們，法院命令保全的物證，沒有新令狀便不能移走。警衛氣急敗壞地退開。林澈第一次看見國家遵守它自己寫下的規則。

Fresh cement around the old gate contradicted claims that no one had maintained it for decades. The coastal radio mentioned a confederation of towns that the city's textbooks described as extinct. A threatened crackdown on unauthorized radio listening prompted the inspector to protect the receiver by court order. The gatehouse displayed a crocodile logo identical to the animal on Jun's childhood train poster.

舊城門周圍的新水泥，與數十年無人維護的說法矛盾。 海岸廣播提到一個城鎮邦聯，城市教科書卻稱其早已消亡。 針對未授權收音的鎮壓威脅，促使檢查員用法院命令保護接收器。 門衛室裡的鱷魚標誌，與小鈞童年列車海報上的動物相同。

Inside the gatehouse, a battered communications receiver picked up a signal from the coast. It identified a town named Harbor Nine, asked whether the supply train was delayed, and repeated the same request in three languages. The broadcast was too current to belong to the founding period. Ivo's eyes filled with tears. He did not know whether his parents had reached Harbor Nine, but the possibility of living communities beyond the city wall had become difficult to deny. Lin recorded the frequency, date, and exact wording. She did not answer immediately: doing so could expose the gatehouse staff to punishment.

門衛室裡，一台破舊接收器收到了海岸訊號。訊號自稱來自九號港，詢問補給列車是否延誤，還以三種語言重複提問。這顯然不是建城時期的舊廣播。伊沃眼裡泛起淚光。他不知道父母是否抵達九號港，但城牆外有活人社區的可能性已很難否認。林澈記下頻率、日期與完整語句，沒有立即回覆；回覆可能讓門衛室員工遭到處分。

The old railway had a dual power system, allowing trains to operate even when city electricity failed. Nari demanded policies that would equalize access to transport for registered and unregistered residents. A council faction argued that opening the gate would endanger the city's fragile social order. The gate engineer feared he would incur criminal penalties merely for preserving an obsolete switch.

舊鐵路有雙重電力系統，即使城市停電，列車仍能運行。 娜莉要求政策能讓有戶籍與無戶籍居民平等使用交通服務。 一個議會派系主張開城門會危及城市脆弱的社會秩序。 城門工程師害怕只因保存淘汰轉轍器，就會受到刑事處罰。

The inspector submitted an emergency finding that the city's 'dead world' claim required independent verification. The court ordered the western gate kept intact and forbidden any destruction of its receiver. It did not order the gate opened, a distinction that frustrated the younger activists. Lin explained why she supported the narrower ruling. Opening a sealed transport route without knowing its condition could kill passengers or provoke a violent response from the guards. To recover history, they needed to keep people alive long enough to hear it. Some activists accused her of being cautious to the point of cowardice. She accepted the criticism without changing her safety judgment.

檢查員提交緊急發現，要求對城市的「死寂世界」說法進行獨立查證。法院命令保留西門，不得銷毀接收器，卻沒有命令開門。年輕倡議者對此十分不滿。林澈解釋自己為何支持較窄的裁定：在不知道路況前打開封閉運輸線，可能害死乘客，還可能引來守衛的暴力反應。要恢復歷史，先得讓人活到能聽見真相的時候。有人批評她謹慎得近乎懦弱。她接受批評，卻不改變安全判斷。

Lin said the greatest injustice was forcing people to forget relatives who might still be alive. The unexplained coastal signal deepened the intrigue surrounding the city's false founding history. A false offer of coastal jobs might have been used to lure families onto the evacuation trains. Vale called the inspector obstinate for refusing to surrender the old receiver without a warrant.

林澈說，最大的冤屈是強迫人們忘記可能仍在世的親人。 無法解釋的海岸訊號，讓城市虛假建城史背後的陰謀更加撲朔迷離。 虛假的海岸工作機會，可能曾被用來引誘家庭登上撤離列車。 維爾說檢查員固執，只因他拒絕在沒有令狀時交出舊接收器。

On the journey home, Ivo sat beside Lin in silence. After a while he said, 'You could have told me my parents must be out there.' She shook her head. 'I could have lied to make you feel better.' He considered this, then gave a small, tired laugh. They had found another city, or at least a live transmitter. Their official world had already become impossible. Still, the most important questions remained: who had sealed the gate, why they had lied about the outside, and what had happened to the people sent away? Lin opened her notebook to a clean page and wrote three questions, not three conclusions.

回程車上，伊沃靜靜坐在林澈旁邊。過了一會兒他說：「妳本來可以告訴我，我爸媽一定在外面。」她搖頭。「我可以說謊讓你好受些。」他想了想，疲倦地笑了一聲。他們發現了另一座城市，或至少一個仍在運作的發射站。官方世界觀已經難以成立。可是最重要的問題仍在：誰封了城門？為何要對城外撒謊？被送走的人發生了什麼事？林澈翻開筆記的新頁，寫下三個問題，而不是三個結論。

A pickpocket had once stolen a train ticket and kept it as a souvenir after the railway disappeared. The coastal dispatcher sounded punctual and irritated, hardly like a ghost from a dead civilization. Lin urged restraint until qualified workers could inspect the old tracks beyond the gate. Ivo began to stagger when he realized the departure list might lead to his parents' destination.

一名扒手曾偷過火車票，鐵路消失後卻把票當作紀念品保存。 海岸調度員說話準時又不耐煩，完全不像死去文明的幽靈。 林澈呼籲克制，等合格工人檢查城門外舊軌道再行動。 伊沃意識到出發名冊可能指向父母去處時，腳步踉蹌。
