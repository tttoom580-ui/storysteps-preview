被允許存在的廢墟

The Museum of Acceptable Ruins

禁止做夢的最後一座城市 · 第 4 / 25 章 · 5000–6000 · 英中對照 · 校訂中

用互動版查詞、朗讀與收藏

Officially, Everday had no history before its founding. Unofficially, it had a museum filled with objects labeled 'unreliable artifacts.' Lin took a day of medical leave to visit it. Beneath a glass ceiling, broken telephones stood beside porcelain bowls and faded photographs. The captions claimed all the objects came from communities destroyed by a natural disaster. Yet one photograph showed a festival on Glass Orchard Lane, complete with a date after the supposed disaster. The museum did not have the original negative, but its curator knew who had donated the print.

官方說，永晝在建城以前沒有歷史。非官方地說，城裡卻有一間博物館，收藏的都是標成「不可靠文物」的東西。林澈請了病假去參觀。玻璃屋頂下，破舊電話和瓷碗、褪色照片並排展示。說明牌稱所有物件都來自遭天災摧毀的社區。但其中一張照片顯示玻璃果園巷正在舉行節慶，日期卻晚於那場據稱發生的災難。館方沒有底片正本，館長卻知道捐贈者是誰。

A museum display falsely described public interest in old photographs as an addiction to dangerous nostalgia. The clean aesthetic of the new galleries concealed the violence of their missing captions. Sera could approximate the station's dimensions even when she could not recall its official name. The curator called his unauthorized work urban archaeology because ordinary history departments refused the materials.

博物館一張展板竟把民眾對舊照片的興趣說成沉迷於危險懷舊。 新展廳整潔的美學風格，掩蓋了遭刪除說明所代表的暴力。 即使莎拉想不起車站正式名稱，仍能估算出它的尺寸。 館長稱自己的未授權工作是都市考古學，因為正規歷史部門拒收這些資料。

The donor was a former civil engineer named Sera, now living in a retirement residence. She remembered very little of her working life and apologized each time she lost the thread of a sentence. But she understood measurements. When Lin described the unusual dimensions of the old train station, Sera drew it precisely, down to the width of a maintenance shaft. She insisted the station had not burned. 'They emptied it,' she said, pressing the pencil until its point broke. 'And we were told not to ask where the passengers had gone.'

捐贈者是退休土木工程師莎拉，如今住在安養住宅。她幾乎不記得工作生涯，說話一斷線就連連道歉。但她還記得測量。林澈描述舊車站的特殊尺寸時，莎拉立刻畫出精準平面圖，甚至標出了維修井的寬度。她堅持車站沒有失火。「他們把人清空了。」她用力握筆，筆尖折斷。「然後我們被要求別問乘客去了哪裡。」

Lin concealed her Authority badge before entering the museum, unwilling to frighten elderly witnesses. The official account mentioned a great blaze, although the station's stone walls showed no fire damage. One young disciple of the state historian copied his claims without checking a single photograph. Lin hoped the exhibition might enlighten visitors, but she refused to replace facts with another comforting myth.

林澈進博物館前收起管理局識別證，不想嚇到年長證人。 官方記載提到一場大火，車站石牆卻沒有任何火損。 一名國家史官的年輕門生照抄他的說法，連一張照片都沒核對。 林澈希望展覽能啟發參觀者，卻拒絕以另一套安慰人心的神話取代事實。

In the afternoon the museum's public feed abruptly changed its exhibition description. It now blamed a former rebel movement for the disappearances and declared the photographs fabricated. Lin recognized the update code as belonging to Vale's office. She preserved the time, asked the curator for an authenticated printout of both versions, and avoided sharing Sera's name. Behind a partition, Ivo found a manual valve that could open a service route to the old station, but it was locked by a physical key. Someone had gone to considerable trouble to preserve a passage while pretending it no longer existed.

下午，博物館公共資訊突然更改展覽說明。新版指責一個舊叛亂組織造成失蹤，並宣稱照片遭偽造。林澈認出更新代碼屬於維爾辦公室。她保存變更時間，請館長為前後兩版製作認證影本，並避免散播莎拉的姓名。隔板後方，伊沃找到一道通往舊車站的維修閥，卻被實體鑰匙鎖住。有人大費周章地保留通道，同時假裝它不存在。

The guard made a public fuss about camera rules to distract an officer from Sera's interview. The new museum label carried an implicit threat: remembering the wrong street made someone suspicious. A government historian would preside over the reopening ceremony, though he had never inspected the archive. The museum's prestige depended on public trust, not simply on royal-looking seals and elegant lighting.

警衛故意大聲為攝影規定爭論，好分散警員對莎拉訪談的注意。 新館藏標籤暗含威脅：記得錯誤街道的人會遭到懷疑。 一名從未檢查檔案的政府史官，卻將主持重新開幕典禮。 博物館的聲望來自公眾信任，不是華麗印章與燈光。

Sera agreed to be interviewed if an independent advocate sat beside her. She could not prove where the trains had gone, but she could identify the valve's manufacturer and the municipal contractor responsible for sealing it. Those details belonged to the physical world. The advocate arranged for her testimony to be sealed until a credible inquiry existed. Lin felt the frustration of waiting, but she had begun to understand that taking memories from frightened people without safeguards would repeat the logic of the Authority. The investigation had to protect the people whose history it hoped to restore.

莎拉同意接受訪談，條件是要有獨立權益代理人在場。她不能證明列車去了哪裡，卻能認出閥門製造商和封閉它的市府承包商。那些是物質世界裡可查的事實。代理人安排證詞封存，直到可信的調查機制成立。林澈等得焦急，卻逐漸理解：若沒有保障就從害怕的人身上奪取記憶，那與管理局的邏輯並無不同。這場調查必須保護她想替其恢復歷史的人。

The council planned to privatize the old station land before anyone could establish its rightful owners. Cold white lights appeared to radiate purity, but their brightness made the erased faces harder to see. The guard changed his patrol rotation to give the advocate time to seal Sera's testimony. An old electric shaver displayed beside the photographs carried a manufacturer's address from Orchard East.

議會計畫在確定土地合法所有人之前，把舊車站用地私有化。 冷白燈光似乎散發純淨感，亮度卻讓被擦掉的面孔更難辨認。 警衛調整巡邏輪班，讓權益代理人有時間封存莎拉證詞。 照片旁展示的老式電鬍刀，標著東果園的製造商地址。

By evening, a museum guard had warned them that the next correction cycle included the retirement residence. Lin and Ivo faced a choice: break into the memory network now, risking a citywide alarm, or find the contractor's paper records first. They chose the slower path. Before leaving, Lin watched a school group walk past the photographs without stopping. One girl turned back to a painted tree. 'That looks like my grandmother's garden,' she told her teacher. The teacher hurried her along, but the girl's question stayed suspended in the quiet gallery.

傍晚，博物館警衛警告他們，下一輪記憶修正涵蓋安養住宅。林澈與伊沃面臨選擇：現在闖進記憶網路，冒著全城警報的風險，或先找承包商的紙本紀錄。他們選擇較慢的路。離開前，林澈看見一群學生走過照片，連停都沒停。只有一名女孩回頭看畫上的樹。「那好像我外婆的花園。」她告訴老師。老師匆匆帶她走開，那個問題卻一直停留在安靜的展廳裡。

A stray note beneath the glass included the route number of the missing passenger train. Official captions placed a veil of respectable language over the fact that whole families had vanished. A photograph of a vineyard outside the wall contradicted the government's claim that no crops survived there. The contractor's warranty still covered the sealed valve, proving the tunnel had been serviced recently.

玻璃展示櫃下夾著一張零散紙條，上面寫著失蹤客車的路線編號。 官方說明以體面措辭掩蓋了整個家庭失蹤的事實。 城牆外葡萄園的照片，反駁了政府宣稱外面沒有作物存活的說法。 承包商的保固仍涵蓋封閉閥門，證明隧道不久前有人維修。
