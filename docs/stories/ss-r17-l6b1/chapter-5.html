The Night of the First Breach

禁止做夢的最後一座城市 · 第 5 / 25 章 · 5000–6000 · 英中對照 · 校訂中

用互動版查詞、朗讀與收藏

The contractor's headquarters stood above the city's oldest water reservoir. Lin had expected a fortress. Instead, she found an almost empty building where the last employees were preparing to relocate. A receptionist offered to sell them the firm's maintenance archive, provided they signed an agreement preventing any public criticism. Lin declined the condition and asked to inspect the disposal schedule under an open-records statute. The receptionist frowned at the unfamiliar request. For the first time, Lin realized how few people remembered that the statute still existed.

承包商總部位於城裡最古老的水庫上方。林澈原以為會看見一座堡壘，沒想到是一棟幾乎空了的大樓，剩下的員工正準備搬走。接待員願意出售維護檔案，條件是兩人必須簽署不得公開批評的協議。林澈拒絕，改依資訊公開法申請查看銷毀排程。接待員對這個陌生要求皺起眉頭。林澈第一次意識到，已很少有人記得這條法律仍然存在。

The consultant treated the broken sensor as an accessory, though Lin knew it was vital evidence. The archived report described a breakdown in the relay safeguards during the first citywide memory trial. Ivo could bypass the shutter motor, but he refused to endanger the receptionist without asking. A photograph showed officials raising champagne glasses beside the first experimental sleep relay.

顧問把損壞感測器當成小配件，林澈卻知道它是重要證據。 封存報告描述第一次全城記憶測試時，中繼器的安全機制曾故障。 伊沃能繞過百葉窗馬達，卻拒絕未經詢問就使接待員陷入危險。 一張照片裡，官員在第一座實驗性睡眠中繼器旁舉起香檳杯。

The paper records had been divided into two groups: routine repairs and 'sleep infrastructure.' The second category carried the signature of a municipal financier who had overseen the city's founding. In one report, engineers warned that the neighborhood relays could overwrite sensory associations but not erase material traces. They recommended shutting the equipment down for safety. The recommendation was crossed out in red. Beneath it, a different hand had written: 'Historical continuity is a greater threat than side effects.' Lin photographed the page beside a ruler, preserving its size and orientation.

紙本紀錄分成兩類：例行修繕，以及「睡眠基礎設施」。後者有一名市府財務官的簽名，那人曾監督永晝建城。一份報告裡，工程師警告社區中繼器能覆寫感官聯想，卻無法抹除物質痕跡，並建議基於安全因素關閉設備。建議被紅筆劃掉。下面換了另一個筆跡寫道：「歷史連續性比副作用更危險。」林澈把頁面與尺一起拍下，保留尺寸和方向。

The paper archive smelled faintly of chestnut polish, a scent Lin associated with her mother's workbench. Vale called the safety engineers cowardly because they had refused to approve a dangerous expansion. A blue thread tied to the warning sheet could evoke Lin's childhood memory of her mother's hands. The contractor's abandoned cafeteria still displayed a menu for roast fowl from the founding banquet.

紙本檔案帶著淡淡栗木蠟氣味，讓林澈想起母親的工作桌。 維爾說安全工程師膽小，只因他們拒絕批准危險擴建。 警告紙上綁著的藍線，能喚起林澈對母親雙手的童年記憶。 承包商廢棄的餐廳仍展示建城宴席的烤禽類菜單。

An alarm sounded as the building's automated shutters descended. Ivo discovered that their visitor passes had been canceled remotely. He could open a maintenance door, but doing so would notify security and expose the receptionist. Instead, Lin called the public safety line and reported an obstructed emergency exit. Under the city's own regulations, an independent inspector had to attend. While they waited, Ivo copied the names of the engineers who had opposed the relay system. Among them was Lin's mother. She had not merely been a victim of correction. She had tried to prevent its expansion.

大樓自動百葉窗下降時，警報突然響起。伊沃發現訪客證被遠端取消。他能打開一道維修門，但會通知保全，也會暴露接待員。林澈沒有那樣做，而是撥打公共安全專線，通報緊急出口遭阻擋。依城市自己的規定，獨立檢查員必須到場。他們等待時，伊沃抄下反對中繼系統的工程師姓名。林澈的母親也在其中。她不只是記憶修正的受害者，還曾努力阻止系統擴張。

The receptionist's voice grew hoarse as she explained how many records were scheduled for destruction. Lin recognized the citrus lotion her mother had used from a stain inside the old engineer's glove. The records exposed a dangerous mentality: officials valued silence more than the health of residents. The monetary trail linked the contractor's expansion payments to an office outside normal public oversight.

接待員解釋即將銷毀多少紀錄時，聲音變得沙啞。 林澈從舊工程手套內側的污漬，認出母親常用的柑橘乳液。 紀錄揭露一種危險心態：官員重視安靜勝過居民健康。 金錢流向把承包商的擴建款項連到一個不受一般公共監督的辦公室。

The inspector arrived with a mechanical key and a woman in a gray civil-defense coat. She introduced herself as Nari, a former communications auditor now responsible for public shelters. After reading the crossed-out warning, Nari said the reservoir contained a maintenance chamber disconnected from the city's live network. Its door required two physical signatures, one from a safety officer and one from the contractor. The contractor's signer had disappeared years ago. The receptionist, trembling, admitted that an old signature key was stored in a safe, but taking it without authorization would be theft. Lin asked for a judicial preservation order rather than forcing the safe.

檢查員帶著機械鑰匙，以及一名穿灰色民防外套的女人抵達。她自稱娜莉，曾是通信稽核員，現在負責公共避難所。讀完那份被劃掉的警告，娜莉說水庫裡有一間維修室，與城市現行網路斷開。要開門，必須有兩個實體簽章，一個來自安全官，一個來自承包商。承包商的簽章人多年前就失蹤了。接待員顫抖地承認，保險櫃裡存著舊簽章鑰匙，但擅自拿走會構成竊盜。林澈沒有逼人開櫃，而是申請司法保全令。

The building's moody lighting made every empty corridor seem occupied by someone who had vanished. At the nucleus of the network stood a reservoir relay designed to synchronize an entire district. A sharp rap at the metal door announced the safety inspector's arrival. A red slash across the engineer's recommendation marked the moment the council ignored the warning.

大樓陰沉多變的燈光，使每條空走廊都像住著消失的人。 整套網路的核心是水庫中繼器，設計目的在同步整個行政區。 金屬門上傳來急促敲擊聲，宣告安全檢查員抵達。 工程師建議上的紅色劃線，標記了議會無視警告的那一刻。

At midnight the court granted temporary preservation, not unrestricted access. Lin had won a small but real victory: the archive could no longer be destroyed as scheduled. Nari volunteered to keep the room under two-person watch. Ivo handed Lin a copy of the engineers' warning. She stared at her mother's name until it blurred. The city had taught her that forgotten people were either sick or dangerous. Now she possessed a document showing that one of them had tried to keep people safe. The next step would take her beneath the reservoir, where the correction signal did not reach.

午夜，法院准許暫時保全，卻沒有開放無限制查閱。林澈得到一個小而真實的勝利：檔案不能按原排程銷毀。娜莉自願安排兩人輪流看守。伊沃遞給她一份工程師警告的影本。她凝視母親的名字，直到字跡變得模糊。城市教導她，被遺忘的人不是生病就是危險。現在她手裡有文件，證明其中一人曾試圖保護大家。下一步，她得走進水庫下方，那裡沒有記憶修正訊號。

A recording captured a sleeping worker's snore before an illegal correction signal interrupted it. Nari had audited telecommunications contracts before discovering the city's hidden sleep-control equipment. Lin refused to unveil the witnesses' names until a court could protect them. Despite exhaustion, Nari worked with surprising vigor to secure the reservoir archive.

錄音收進一名熟睡工人的鼾聲，接著被違法修正訊號打斷。 娜莉曾稽核電信契約，因而發現城市祕密睡眠控制設備。 林澈拒絕在法院能保護證人前揭露姓名。 雖然疲憊，娜莉仍充滿活力地保全水庫檔案。
