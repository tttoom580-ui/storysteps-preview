夢境不是證據

Evidence Is Not a Dream

禁止做夢的最後一座城市 · 第 11 / 25 章 · 5000–6000 · 英中對照 · 校訂中

用互動版查詞、朗讀與收藏

The first public hearing opened in a room decorated with the city's founding emblem: a golden eye above a sleeping child. Vale appeared with a prepared presentation showing images of frightened patients and declaring that dreams were inherently unstable. Lin took the opposite position but refused the easy claim that every dream revealed the truth. She explained how memory depended on association, suggestion, and prior experience. A dream could preserve a clue or invent one; neither possibility made it legal evidence by itself. She submitted the paper invoices, sensor logs, and independent medical observations as separate exhibits. The difference mattered to the judge.

第一場公開聽證在一間掛著建城徽章的房間舉行：金色眼睛俯視熟睡的孩子。維爾帶著準備好的簡報，展示受驚病患的照片，宣稱夢境本質上不穩定。林澈持相反立場，卻拒絕說每場夢都是真相。她解釋記憶如何依賴聯想、暗示與舊經驗。夢可以保存線索，也可能創造線索；兩者都不表示夢本身具有法律證據力。她分別提出紙本發票、感測紀錄與獨立醫療觀察。這個區分讓法官認真聽了下去。

Vale sought public acclaim for the dream ban, but the authenticated invoices weakened his heroic image. Lin acknowledged the ambiguity of dream imagery instead of pretending that every vision was accurate. The medical panel distinguished bodily harm from ordinary discomfort reported during sleep. A cold draught crossed the hearing room when someone opened the ventilation hatch.

維爾希望禁夢政策帶來公眾讚譽，認證發票卻動搖他的英雄形象。 林澈承認夢境影像的歧義，不假裝每個幻象都準確。 醫療小組區分身體傷害與睡眠時的一般不適。 有人打開通風口時，一股冷風穿過聽證室。

Vale accused her of encouraging a citywide delusion. Lin answered with a measurement from the western gate: electrical service had continued after the official railway closure, and the supplier had been paid with public funds. A financial auditor authenticated the original invoices. Next came the medical report documenting harmful high-intensity pulses. Finally, Nari described the lower district's tax payments under invalid addresses, taking care not to reveal any residents who had refused publicity. Vale had explanations for every individual document, but those explanations contradicted one another. The judge ordered the city to preserve the source databases for independent examination.

維爾指責她鼓勵全城陷入妄想。林澈拿出西門測量結果回應：官方鐵路關閉後，電力維修仍在持續，供應商也領了公款。財務稽核員認證了原始發票。接著是醫療報告，記錄高強度脈衝的危害。最後娜莉說明下層社區如何用無效地址繳稅，謹慎避開不願公開的居民。維爾能為每份文件個別辯解，那些說法卻彼此矛盾。法官命令市府保存原始資料庫，以供獨立查核。

The archived machine log measured heat in Fahrenheit, unlike the city's present metric settings. The original train invoices survived inside a fireproof document case beneath the market counter. A geographical discrepancy placed the old station west of a wall the official map said had always existed. Lin refused to call an anonymous hacker as her only witness when the documents could be verified lawfully.

封存機械紀錄用華氏溫度量測熱能，與現行公制設定不同。 原始列車發票存放在市場櫃檯下的防火文件箱，因此得以倖存。 地理上的矛盾，把舊車站定位在官方宣稱一直存在的城牆西側。 文件能依法核對時，林澈拒絕把匿名駭客當成唯一證人。

Outside, reporters demanded the names of the missing children. Lin refused. One journalist accused her of hiding the truth she claimed to defend. She responded that citizens could learn the scope of the disappearance without publishing private medical records. The independent panel could verify numbers and dates while protecting identities. Ivo watched her speak, impressed by how much she had changed since the first morning. She had come to the inquiry desperate to prove her own memory was real. Now she understood that one person's certainty was not worth sacrificing somebody else's privacy.

法庭外，記者要求提供失蹤兒童姓名，林澈拒絕。一名記者指責她掩蓋自己聲稱要捍衛的真相。她回答，市民可以知道失蹤事件規模，不必因此公開私人病歷。獨立小組能核實數字與日期，同時保護身分。伊沃看著她發言，對她從第一天至今的改變十分佩服。她最初急著證明自己的記憶是真的，現在卻理解，一個人的確定感不值得拿別人的隱私交換。

Nari explained the relay mechanism in terms a layman could understand without sacrificing accuracy. A court could liberate the records from secrecy, but it could not decide what every resident must remember. A mermaid mural from the lost railway appeared on two photographs taken decades apart. The seized archive included miscellaneous receipts that proved surprisingly important to the timeline.

娜莉用外行人能理解、又不失準確的方式解釋中繼器機制。 法院可以讓紀錄擺脫保密，卻不能決定每位居民必須記得什麼。 失蹤鐵路上的美人魚壁畫，出現在相隔幾十年拍攝的兩張照片裡。 查扣檔案裡有各種雜項收據，對重建時間線竟出乎意料重要。

That night the Authority released selected clips from her testimony, cutting out every qualification. The broadcast made her sound as if she believed dreams were perfect records. Nari preserved the complete hearing transcript, and a group of students compared both versions line by line. The discovery spread without anyone needing to hack a device. Ordinary people could see that the government's report had omitted important words. Lin felt a cautious excitement. The city still controlled the screens, but it could not compel every reader to misunderstand the same printed page.

那晚管理局剪輯林澈的證詞，刪去所有保留條件，讓她聽起來像相信夢境是完美紀錄。娜莉保存完整聽證逐字稿，一群學生逐行比對兩版差異。發現傳開了，根本不用入侵任何裝置。普通人就能看出政府報導省略了重要語句。林澈感到謹慎的興奮。城市仍掌控螢幕，卻無法強迫所有讀者誤解同一張印刷紙頁。

The repeated occurrence of identical correction codes pointed toward a centralized instruction. A family outing ticket listed a train route that the government had later erased from transit maps. Lin chose to persevere through testimony rather than make an unsupported accusation for dramatic effect. The judge asked the court to ponder how a government could collect taxes from an officially nonexistent district.

相同修正代碼一再出現，顯示可能有中央統一指示。 一張家庭出遊車票印有後來遭政府從運輸圖抹除的列車路線。 林澈選擇持續提出證詞，而非為了戲劇效果做出無根據的指控。 法官請法庭思考：政府怎能向官方不存在的行政區收稅？

When Ivo walked her home, he asked whether she had ever imagined leaving Everday. She said yes, but not while the records were in danger. He said he might search for his parents beyond the gate if it opened. Neither made a promise about what the other should do. At her door, Lin gave him a key to a box containing duplicate evidence, then asked him to use it only if she was arrested. The trust between them was practical before it was romantic. She went to bed with the blue thread on her wrist and a new warning in her notebook: protect the difference between a story and a fact.

伊沃送她回家時，問她有沒有想過離開永晝。她說有，但不是在證據仍受威脅的時候。他說如果城門開了，也許會去尋找父母。兩人都沒有要求對方作出承諾。到家門口，林澈交給他一把證據副本箱的鑰匙，請他只有在自己被捕時才使用。他們的信任先是實際的，然後才逐漸變成感情。林澈帶著手腕上的藍線入睡，在筆記裡又加一句警告：保護故事與事實之間的界線。

The hearing began to revolve around verifiable records instead of arguments about whose dreams felt convincing. A metal kitchen utensil bearing the erased district's address was admitted as a physical exhibit. Lin warned that labeling Vale a villain before the inquiry ended would not replace evidence. A market yogurt container carried a printed delivery address from the supposedly destroyed station district.

聽證逐漸圍繞可查的紀錄，而不是爭論誰的夢感覺更真實。 一件刻著被抹除行政區地址的金屬廚具，被列為實體證物。 林澈提醒，調查尚未結束前先把維爾叫做惡人，不能取代證據。 一個市場優格容器印著據稱已毀車站區的送貨地址。
