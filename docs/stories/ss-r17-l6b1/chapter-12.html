冰裡的名字

The Name in the Ice

禁止做夢的最後一座城市 · 第 12 / 25 章 · 5000–6000 · 英中對照 · 校訂中

用互動版查詞、朗讀與收藏

The independent review found a sealed medical inventory beneath the original sleep laboratory. Among boxes of obsolete sensors was a register of children moved during the founding period. One entry matched Lin's sister Mei: her age, date of birth, and a distinguishing scar on her elbow. But the status line was unreadable, and the current location field had been cut away. Lin felt the old temptation to complete the missing story with hope. Dr. Asa reminded her that a matching record established a historical transfer, not proof that Mei was still alive. Lin asked the panel to preserve the page without making it public.

獨立審查在原始睡眠實驗室下方找到一份密封醫療清冊。成箱的淘汰感測器之間，有一份建城時期被移送兒童的名冊。其中一項與林澈的妹妹梅吻合：年齡、生日，以及手肘上特殊的疤痕。但狀態欄無法辨識，現址欄則遭剪去。林澈感到熟悉的衝動，想用希望填補缺失的故事。阿莎提醒她，吻合紀錄只能證實過去的轉移，不能證明梅仍活著。林澈要求小組保全該頁，暫不公開。

A laboratory attendant identified the storage shelves where the children's original treatment registers had been kept. Several paper folders were battered by damp air, but the stamped dates remained legible. The old census counted hundreds of children absent from the city's approved founding population. A retired chemist explained how old ink could be dated without damaging the children's records.

一名實驗室服務員指出，兒童原始治療名冊存放在哪些架上。 好幾個紙本資料夾被濕氣損壞，蓋章日期仍能辨認。 舊人口普查列出數百名不在官方建城人口名單上的兒童。 退休化學家解釋如何不損傷兒童紀錄，就能測定舊墨水年代。

In another box, the reviewers discovered a set of experimental records showing that the original system had been designed for limited treatment of traumatic memories. Volunteers could choose a particular association to weaken, and they could withdraw consent. The later citywide system had replaced that careful design with compulsory nightly exposure. Lin recognized the changes in the software architecture. The same engineers who had warned against expansion were listed in the early trials. Their names vanished from subsequent reports. This history changed the moral shape of her work: a tool invented to relieve suffering had become a means of governing what entire communities could remember.

另一個箱子裡，審查員發現實驗紀錄，顯示原系統設計目的其實是有限度處理創傷記憶。志願者能選擇要減弱哪個聯想，也能撤回同意。後來的全城系統卻把審慎設計改成強制每夜曝露。林澈認出軟體架構裡的改動。早期試驗列有那些反對擴張的工程師，姓名卻從後續報告消失。這段歷史改變了她對工作的理解：原為減輕痛苦的工具，竟成為統治整個社群記憶的手段。

A soft chirp from the storage alarm warned the reviewers that the archive's humidity had risen. Only licensed nurses could dispense medication during the early voluntary memory trials. The technician let out a groan when he discovered that the latest digital copy lacked entire pages. The missing laboratory files were only the visible tip of an iceberg of altered civic records.

儲存警報輕輕鳴叫，提醒審查員檔案室濕度升高。 早期自願記憶試驗期間，只有持照護理師能配藥。 技師發現最新數位副本缺了整頁時，發出一聲呻吟。 失蹤的實驗室檔案，只是整批遭修改市政紀錄露出的冰山一角。

A technician offered to run the old sensor on Lin and attempt a personal restoration. She refused. Without safeguards, it might reactivate painful memories, create confusion, or overwrite details that she needed for the investigation. Instead, she contacted the lower district guardian and asked whether Mei's file could be checked privately. The guardian replied that a woman matching the record had once been treated under a different surname, but consent would be required before any family contact. Lin wrote back: 'Please tell her I exist. She can decide whether she wants to know me.' Sending those words hurt more than any confrontation with Vale.

一名技師提議用舊感測器替林澈嘗試個人記憶恢復。她拒絕。缺乏保障的操作可能重新引發痛苦記憶、造成混亂，甚至覆寫調查所需的細節。她改為聯絡下層社區監護人，請求私下核對梅的檔案。監護人回覆，有一名與紀錄吻合的女子曾以另一個姓氏接受治療，但家人聯絡仍需當事人同意。林澈寫道：「請告訴她我存在。她可以決定要不要認識我。」寄出這幾個字，比與維爾正面衝突更痛。

The undamaged consent forms proved invaluable for distinguishing voluntary treatment from compulsory correction. Vale had funded a lavish celebration of safe sleep while cutting staff assigned to monitor side effects. The original clinic logo showed an open hand, unlike the Authority's later symbol of a watchful eye. A loudspeaker ordered visitors to leave, but the judge's preservation warrant kept the examination team inside.

完好的同意書對區分自願治療與強制修正極其珍貴。 維爾砸錢舉辦奢華安眠慶典，卻削減追蹤副作用的人力。 原診所標誌是一隻張開的手，不同於管理局後來監視的眼睛符號。 擴音器命令訪客離開，但法官的保全令使檢查小組得以留下。

The city council reacted by proposing that all old medical files be placed under a new security office. Nari challenged the proposal as an attempt to move evidence away from the court. The independent panel asked the judge for a protective extension, and the judge granted it after reviewing the risk of destruction. Vale could no longer claim exclusive authority over the laboratory. Yet he retained access to the network controlling millions of sleepers. Lin knew the balance of power remained dangerous. The truth was moving into public view, but the machine that hid it was still operating every night.

城市議會隨即提出，要把所有舊病歷移交新的安全辦公室。娜莉質疑這是在把證據移出法院控制。獨立小組請求法官延長保全令，法官審查銷毀風險後予以准許。維爾不再對實驗室擁有排他控制權，但他仍能接觸每天控制數百萬睡眠者的網路。林澈知道權力仍不平衡。真相開始進入公眾視野，隱藏真相的機器卻還每晚運作。

New martial security regulations threatened to transfer the medical archive to an armed enforcement office. No confirmed outbreak preceded the children's transfers, despite what the founding textbooks claimed. A preserved oyster shell in Mei's childhood box suggested a visit to the coast before the city sealed its gates. The early engineers never intended the technology to populate every home with compulsory monitors.

新的軍事化安全規章威脅要把醫療檔案移交武裝執法辦公室。 儘管建城教科書如此宣稱，兒童轉移之前沒有任何確診疫情爆發。 梅童年盒子裡保存的牡蠣殼，顯示城門封鎖前她可能去過海岸。 早期工程師從未打算讓強制監測環遍布每戶家庭。

Late at night Lin found Ivo waiting outside the archive with two cups of hot tea. He did not ask whether she had found her sister. She told him only what she was permitted to share, and he listened without demanding more. Then he said the coastal authority had acknowledged receiving their inquiry and would send a delegation under independent supervision. His parents' case was no nearer to resolution, but they finally had a lawful channel to ask. Lin took his hand. Both of them were searching for people who might refuse to return. That possibility was painful, and it had to remain theirs to respect.

深夜，林澈發現伊沃拿著兩杯熱茶站在檔案室外。他沒有追問她是否找到妹妹。她只說自己有權透露的部分，他靜靜聽著，沒有要求更多。接著他告訴她，海岸當局已確認收到詢問，將在獨立監督下派遣代表。他父母的案件仍未有結果，但終於有合法管道能查詢。林澈牽起他的手。兩人都在尋找可能拒絕回來的人。那種可能令人痛苦，卻仍必須受到尊重。

The unreadable status line was a setback, not permission to announce that Mei had died or survived. Dr. Asa remained skeptical of any claim that a single restored memory could settle the entire case. The original patients had to subscribe voluntarily to treatment, with a right to withdraw at any time. Lin chose to withhold Mei's name from the press until Mei herself could consent to contact.

看不清的狀態欄是挫折，卻不能因此宣布梅死亡或存活。 阿莎醫師仍懷疑，單一恢復的記憶能否解決整個案件。 原始病患必須自願加入治療，而且隨時有退出權。 林澈選擇不向媒體透露梅的姓名，直到梅本人同意聯絡。
