The Wrong Lunch Box

交換午餐的三十天 · 第 1 / 25 章 · 1000以下 · 英中對照 · 校訂中

Quiet Min opened a lunch box full of bright rice balls that belonged to loud Lola. Nobody had planned for this moment. The room was full of ordinary sounds, yet Min felt that something important had changed. Lola waited beside the door and let the first surprise settle. It was tempting to find an easy answer before anyone knew the whole story. Instead, they looked carefully at what was truly in front of them. Min opened the lunch notebook and made a small note about the day.

安靜的小敏打開便當，裡面是熱鬧的樂樂做的彩色飯糰。 沒有人預料到這一刻。學校餐廳充滿平常的聲音，小敏卻感覺有件重要的事改變了。樂樂站在門旁，讓最初的驚訝慢慢沉澱。大家很容易在弄清事情之前就急著下結論，但他們決定先看清眼前真正發生的事。小敏翻開午餐筆記本，記下今天的小小發現。

They began by asking who might be affected by the next step. Lola thought about the people who had not spoken yet, and Min thought about the rule they had already promised to follow. One person offered a quick answer, but the group asked for a reason. A good question was not an attack. It was a way to make the answer safer and kinder. Ms. Chen reminded them that everyone could ask for a pause. Lola asked how to use a.m. in a sentence about their day. In the English practice book, bell appeared on the next line. Min read breakfast aloud, then checked the meaning with Lola. Lola drew a small picture of cake in the margin of the class story page. The word couch gave the group a new example to discuss.

大家先問，下一步會影響到誰。樂樂想到還沒發言的人，小敏則想到自己早已承諾遵守的規則。有人很快提出答案，其他人卻請他說明理由。好問題不是攻擊，而是讓答案更安全、更體貼的方法。陳老師提醒大家，每個人都可以要求暫停。 樂樂問起，如何用「a.m.」（上午（a.m.））寫一句與今天有關的話。 英文練習本的下一行出現「bell」（鐘聲；鈴鐺）。 小敏朗讀「breakfast」（早餐），再和樂樂確認意思。 樂樂在班級故事頁的空白處畫了「cake」（蛋糕）的圖。 「couch」（沙發）這個字讓小組多了一個可以討論的例子。

They checked their names and returned the food before eating anything. Min chose the part of the work that could be done carefully. Lola handled a different task and promised to report back before making any large change. A short question from Ms. Chen caught an error in their first version. Instead of hiding it, they made a neat correction on the page. That correction did not erase their effort. It showed that the group was learning how to be responsible for its own work. Lola put cover on the board without turning it into a test. They found early in a short reading exercise near the end of the page. Min drew a tiny sign beside exercise so it would be easier to remember. Lola chose foreign as a word to explain kindly to a new learner. Lola drew a small picture of fruit in the margin of the class story page.

她們先核對姓名，把食物還回去，沒有隨便吃。 小敏選擇自己能謹慎完成的工作，樂樂負責另一項任務，並答應在重大更改前先回報。陳老師的一個簡短問題，讓大家發現第一版中的錯誤。他們沒有掩飾，而是在頁面上整齊修正。這項修正沒有抹去先前的努力，反而證明小組正在學習對自己的工作負責。 樂樂把「cover」（外罩；覆蓋物）寫在板上，卻沒有把它變成考試。 他們在頁面末尾的短文練習裡找到「early」（提早）。 小敏在「exercise」（運動）旁畫上小記號，幫助記憶。 樂樂選了「foreign」（外文的），準備耐心向初學者說明。 樂樂在班級故事頁的空白處畫了「fruit」（水果）的圖。

Min was afraid that saying the wrong thing would make the day worse. Lola answered that staying silent could also leave a misunderstanding in place. They practiced one honest sentence and checked that it did not blame anyone unfairly. This took longer than making a joke, but the result was clearer. Near the table, Ms. Chen nodded and let the young people try their own words before offering advice. Lola asked how to use he in a sentence about their day. In the English practice book, heavy appeared on the next line. Min read hobby aloud, then checked the meaning with Lola. Another card carried monkey and a simple drawing by a classmate. The word salad gave the group a new example to discuss.

小敏害怕說錯話會讓事情變糟。樂樂回答，保持沉默有時也會讓誤會繼續存在。他們練習說出一句誠實的話，再確認沒有不公平地責怪任何人。這比開玩笑花時間，結果卻更清楚。桌旁的陳老師點頭，先讓年輕人用自己的話嘗試，再提供建議。 樂樂問起，如何用「he」（他；他的）寫一句與今天有關的話。 英文練習本的下一行出現「heavy」（沉重的）。 小敏朗讀「hobby」（興趣），再和樂樂確認意思。 另一張圖卡上有「monkey」（猴子），旁邊是同學的簡筆畫。 「salad」（沙拉）這個字讓小組多了一個可以討論的例子。

Lola joked that their boxes had become friends before they did. Ms. Chen asked the group to explain its choice in everyday words. Min tried once, paused, and then gave a shorter answer. Lola added an important detail that had been left out. The explanation became clear enough for another person to check. That mattered more than making their decision sound grand. The friendship at lunch could grow only if other people understood how it worked. Lola put shall on the board without turning it into a test. They found sun in a short reading exercise near the end of the page. Min drew a tiny sign beside teach so it would be easier to remember. Lola drew a small picture of tooth in the margin of the class story page. A small study card showed write, which Min copied beside today's notes.

樂樂開玩笑說，兩個便當比她們先成為朋友。 陳老師請小組用平常的話解釋選擇。小敏試了一次，停頓後改用更短的回答。樂樂補充了一個漏掉的重要細節。解釋變得足夠清楚，讓別人也能核對。這比把決定說得偉大更重要。只有其他人理解做法，午餐桌上的友情才有機會繼續成長。 樂樂把「shall」（要不要／應否）寫在板上，卻沒有把它變成考試。 他們在頁面末尾的短文練習裡找到「sun」（太陽）。 小敏在「teach」（教）旁畫上小記號，幫助記憶。 樂樂在班級故事頁的空白處畫了「tooth」（牙齒）的圖。 一張小小練習卡上寫著「write」（寫），小敏把它抄在今日筆記旁。

Near the end of the day, Min stayed beside Lola for one more minute. They remembered how strange the morning had felt. A problem that once seemed to fill the whole room had become several smaller questions. Each question could be handled in turn. They did not know what the following day would bring, and that was all right. What they had learned was not a magic answer but a habit: notice a change, ask with care, and let people choose for themselves. Min closed the lunch notebook and left with a lighter heart.

一天快結束時，小敏在樂樂身邊多待了一分鐘。他們想起早上的奇怪感受。原本像占滿整間房間的難題，現在分成幾個較小的問題，可以逐一處理。他們不知道明天會帶來什麼，但這沒有關係。今天學到的不是神奇答案，而是一種習慣：留意變化、仔細發問，也讓別人自己選擇。小敏闔上午餐筆記本，帶著較輕鬆的心情離開。
