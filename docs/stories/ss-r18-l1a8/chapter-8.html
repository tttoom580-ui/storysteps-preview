第七天：空椅子

Day Seven: An Empty Chair

Min was absent for a day, and Lola missed their quiet lunch talk. The light through the window looked ordinary, but the news did not. Min stopped for a moment, feeling both curious and unsure. Lola did not laugh at the question, even when it sounded strange. They knew from earlier mistakes that moving too fast could make a simple problem harder. The school cafeteria offered enough space for a calm talk. On the first page of the lunch notebook, Min wrote what was known and left a blank space for what remained uncertain.

小敏請假一天，樂樂很想念她們安靜的午餐聊天。 窗外的光線和平常一樣，消息卻一點也不平常。小敏停了一會兒，既好奇又不確定。就算問題聽來奇怪，樂樂也沒有取笑。他們從先前的錯誤學到，行動太快反而可能讓小問題變複雜。學校餐廳有足夠空間讓大家平靜討論。小敏在午餐筆記本第一頁寫下已知的事，也留下空白給尚未確定的部分。

The first idea sounded simple, and that was useful. Still, Min wanted to know whether it respected everyone's choice. Lola suggested changing only one part rather than throwing the whole plan away. They drew two small boxes on a page: what we can do and what we must ask about. The list made the daily lunch swap easier to discuss without turning a friend into an enemy. Min drew a tiny sign beside baseball so it would be easier to remember. Lola chose beef as a word to explain kindly to a new learner. A small study card showed behind, which Min copied beside today's notes. Lola asked how to use break in a sentence about their day. In the English practice book, different appeared on the next line.

第一個想法聽起來很簡單，這是好事。但小敏還想確認是否尊重每個人的選擇。樂樂建議只修改其中一部分，不必推翻整個計畫。他們在紙上畫兩個方框：我們能做的事，以及必須先詢問的事。有了這份清單，討論每日交換午餐便容易多了，也不會把朋友當成敵人。 小敏在「baseball」（棒球）旁畫上小記號，幫助記憶。 樂樂選了「beef」（牛肉），準備耐心向初學者說明。 一張小小練習卡上寫著「behind」（在後面），小敏把它抄在今日筆記旁。 樂樂問起，如何用「break」（斷掉；破裂）寫一句與今天有關的話。 英文練習本的下一行出現「different」（死亡）。

Lola sent a kind message but did not demand an answer. The plan became real only when someone took the first small action. Min checked the time, Lola checked the shared materials, and Ms. Chen confirmed which steps needed adult approval. The group left a clear record so that a person arriving later could understand the decision. They also agreed on what to do if something went wrong. It was not exciting in the way stories often promised, but it let everyone move forward with confidence. Min read dress aloud, then checked the meaning with Lola. Another card carried easy and a simple drawing by a classmate. The word fly gave the group a new example to discuss. Lola put ghost on the board without turning it into a test. They found girl in a short reading exercise near the end of the page.

樂樂傳去關心訊息，沒有要求立即回覆。 有人做出第一個小小行動時，計畫才真正開始。小敏確認時間，樂樂檢查共用材料，陳老師確認哪些步驟需要大人同意。小組留下清楚紀錄，讓稍後到的人也能理解決定。他們還約好若事情出錯該怎麼辦。這不像故事裡常見的刺激冒險，卻讓每個人都更有信心往前走。 小敏朗讀「dress」（裙子；洋裝），再和樂樂確認意思。 另一張圖卡上有「easy」（容易的），旁邊是同學的簡筆畫。 「fly」（飛）這個字讓小組多了一個可以討論的例子。 樂樂把「ghost」（幽靈）寫在板上，卻沒有把它變成考試。 他們在頁面末尾的短文練習裡找到「girl」（女孩）。

For a minute, the group spoke all at once. Min raised a hand and suggested taking turns. Lola repeated the question in simpler words, so that even a quiet student could follow the talk. Nobody needed to win an argument. They needed to learn which part of the problem could be handled now and which part needed more help. The daily lunch swap remained difficult, but it no longer felt impossible. Min drew a tiny sign beside give so it would be easier to remember. Lola chose group as a word to explain kindly to a new learner. A small study card showed heart, which Min copied beside today's notes. Lola asked how to use nice in a sentence about their day. In the English practice book, officer appeared on the next line.

有一分鐘，大家幾乎同時開口。小敏舉起手，提議輪流說話。樂樂用更簡單的詞重述問題，讓安靜的學生也能跟上。沒有人需要爭贏；重要的是分清哪些事現在能做、哪些事需要更多協助。每日交換午餐仍然困難，卻不再讓人覺得無法處理。 小敏在「give」（給予）旁畫上小記號，幫助記憶。 樂樂選了「group」（團體），準備耐心向初學者說明。 一張小小練習卡上寫著「heart」（心），小敏把它抄在今日筆記旁。 樂樂問起，如何用「nice」（好的）寫一句與今天有關的話。 英文練習本的下一行出現「officer」（管理員）。

Min replied the next morning with a drawing of a small soup bowl. They gathered the papers and put the shared things back in their places. Min could feel that the room had changed even though the desks had not moved. Lola pointed to the page where the group had written its first worry. The words were still there, but they no longer told the whole story. Now there was also an action, a result, and a reason to look forward to the next meeting. Min read outside aloud, then checked the meaning with Lola. Another card carried park and a simple drawing by a classmate. The word taste gave the group a new example to discuss. Lola put walk on the board without turning it into a test. They found why in a short reading exercise near the end of the page.

小敏隔天早上回傳一張小湯碗的畫。 他們整理紙張，把共用物品放回原處。雖然桌子沒有移動，小敏卻覺得房間已經不同。樂樂指出小組寫下第一個擔憂的那一頁。那些字還在，卻已無法概括整件事。現在多了行動、結果，以及期待下次見面的理由。 小敏朗讀「outside」（外面），再和樂樂確認意思。 另一張圖卡上有「park」（公園），旁邊是同學的簡筆畫。 「taste」（品嚐）這個字讓小組多了一個可以討論的例子。 樂樂把「walk」（步行）寫在板上，卻沒有把它變成考試。 他們在頁面末尾的短文練習裡找到「why」（為什麼）。

Outside, the air was cooler than it had been at lunch. Min looked back once and saw the school cafeteria in the evening light. It seemed like an ordinary place, but there was more to it now: a remembered question, a mistake corrected, and a choice made together. Lola waved from a few steps away. They still had their separate dreams and small worries, yet they also shared one clear memory. Whatever happened next, they could return to that memory without needing to pretend the day had been perfect.

外面的空氣比午餐時涼了些。小敏回頭望了一眼晚光中的學校餐廳。它看似平凡，現在卻多了值得記住的問題、改正的錯誤，以及共同做出的選擇。樂樂在幾步外揮手。他們仍有各自的夢想和小煩惱，也有一段共同的清晰回憶。無論接下來發生什麼，都不必假裝今天完美，就能想起它的價值。
