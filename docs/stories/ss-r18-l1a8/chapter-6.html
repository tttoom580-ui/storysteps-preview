第四天：小食譜

Day Four: The Small Recipe

交換午餐的三十天 · 第 6 / 25 章 · 1000以下 · 英中對照 · 校訂中

Min wrote a simple recipe card for a safe vegetable sandwich. The day began with a small question and soon became more interesting than anyone expected. Min could hear other students talking, but did not hurry to join their guesses. Lola pointed out a detail that had been easy to miss. They stood together in the school cafeteria, where the same familiar objects suddenly seemed to tell a different story. Min kept the lunch notebook close, not as a perfect answer, but as a place to begin.

小敏寫了一張簡單又安全的蔬菜三明治食譜。 這一天從一個小疑問開始，後來卻比大家預想的更有趣。小敏聽到其他同學交談，沒有急著加入猜測。樂樂指出了一個容易忽略的細節。他們站在學校餐廳裡，熟悉的東西忽然像在說另一段故事。小敏把午餐筆記本帶在身邊，那不是完美答案，而是一個開始。

Min was afraid that saying the wrong thing would make the day worse. Lola answered that staying silent could also leave a misunderstanding in place. They practiced one honest sentence and checked that it did not blame anyone unfairly. This took longer than making a joke, but the result was clearer. Near the table, Ms. Chen nodded and let the young people try their own words before offering advice. Lola put arrive on the board without turning it into a test. They found before in a short reading exercise near the end of the page. Min drew a tiny sign beside boat so it would be easier to remember. Lola drew a small picture of box in the margin of the class story page. A small study card showed coffee, which Min copied beside today's notes.

小敏害怕說錯話會讓事情變糟。樂樂回答，保持沉默有時也會讓誤會繼續存在。他們練習說出一句誠實的話，再確認沒有不公平地責怪任何人。這比開玩笑花時間，結果卻更清楚。桌旁的陳老師點頭，先讓年輕人用自己的話嘗試，再提供建議。 樂樂把「arrive」（抵達）寫在板上，卻沒有把它變成考試。 他們在頁面末尾的短文練習裡找到「before」（在……之前）。 小敏在「boat」（船）旁畫上小記號，幫助記憶。 樂樂在班級故事頁的空白處畫了「box」（箱子）的圖。 一張小小練習卡上寫著「coffee」（咖啡），小敏把它抄在今日筆記旁。

Lola read it aloud and asked permission to copy it. There was a quiet kind of courage in trying again. Min noticed that the last attempt had taught them something, even without a perfect result. Lola offered practical help and asked which part would be most useful. They worked together for a short time and then stopped to check whether they were still following the rule. Ms. Chen praised the careful check more than the speed of the work. Lola asked how to use drive in a sentence about their day. In the English practice book, except appeared on the next line. Min read finally aloud, then checked the meaning with Lola. Another card carried free and a simple drawing by a classmate. The word house gave the group a new example to discuss.

樂樂朗讀食譜，也先詢問能否抄寫。 願意再試一次，是一種安靜的勇氣。小敏發現，就算上次結果不完美，大家仍學到了一些事。樂樂提供實際協助，先問哪一部分最有用。他們一起工作一小段時間，再停下來確認是否仍遵守規則。陳老師稱讚的不是速度，而是這份細心。 樂樂問起，如何用「drive」（開車）寫一句與今天有關的話。 英文練習本的下一行出現「except」（除了）。 小敏朗讀「finally」（終於），再和樂樂確認意思。 另一張圖卡上有「free」（免費的），旁邊是同學的簡筆畫。 「house」（房屋）這個字讓小組多了一個可以討論的例子。

They began by asking who might be affected by the next step. Lola thought about the people who had not spoken yet, and Min thought about the rule they had already promised to follow. One person offered a quick answer, but the group asked for a reason. A good question was not an attack. It was a way to make the answer safer and kinder. Ms. Chen reminded them that everyone could ask for a pause. Lola put lion on the board without turning it into a test. They found see in a short reading exercise near the end of the page. Min drew a tiny sign beside shape so it would be easier to remember. Lola chose sign as a word to explain kindly to a new learner. A small study card showed stupid, which Min copied beside today's notes.

大家先問，下一步會影響到誰。樂樂想到還沒發言的人，小敏則想到自己早已承諾遵守的規則。有人很快提出答案，其他人卻請他說明理由。好問題不是攻擊，而是讓答案更安全、更體貼的方法。陳老師提醒大家，每個人都可以要求暫停。 樂樂把「lion」（獅子）寫在板上，卻沒有把它變成考試。 他們在頁面末尾的短文練習裡找到「see」（看見）。 小敏在「shape」（形狀）旁畫上小記號，幫助記憶。 樂樂選了「sign」（標誌），準備耐心向初學者說明。 一張小小練習卡上寫著「stupid」（笨的），小敏把它抄在今日筆記旁。

The recipe became the first page of their shared lunch notebook. The answer made someone smile, but Min still asked one final question: was everyone comfortable with what came next? Lola listened to the replies, including the quiet ones. They changed a small detail when one person needed something different. Nothing about the change made the story less meaningful. It helped the friendship at lunch belong to more than one person, which was why the work had begun. Lola asked how to use terrible in a sentence about their day. In the English practice book, top appeared on the next line. Min read uncle aloud, then checked the meaning with Lola. Another card carried wave and a simple drawing by a classmate. The word weekend gave the group a new example to discuss.

食譜成了兩人共用午餐筆記本的第一頁。 答案讓某個人笑了，但小敏仍問最後一個問題：大家都能接受下一步嗎？樂樂聽取回答，包括輕聲說出的意見。有人有不同需求，他們就修改一個小細節。這種修改沒有讓故事失去意義，反而讓午餐桌上的友情不只屬於一個人；這也正是大家當初開始的原因。 樂樂問起，如何用「terrible」（嚴重的；糟糕的）寫一句與今天有關的話。 英文練習本的下一行出現「top」（頂端）。 小敏朗讀「uncle」（舅舅），再和樂樂確認意思。 另一張圖卡上有「wave」（揮手），旁邊是同學的簡筆畫。 「weekend」（週末）這個字讓小組多了一個可以討論的例子。

When the last bell sounded, Min saw that Lola was waiting by the familiar door. The two had not said everything perfectly. They had even disagreed about a detail, yet the disagreement had made their plan clearer. The moment did not need a large speech. A small smile and one honest thank-you were enough. At home, Min would remember the most surprising part of the day and write it on the next page. Tomorrow the daily lunch swap might look different again. This time they would know how to begin.

最後一聲鈴響時，小敏看見樂樂在熟悉的門旁等待。兩人不是每句話都說得完美，甚至為某個細節意見不同，但那場分歧讓計畫更清楚。這一刻不需要長篇大論，一個微笑和真誠的道謝就夠了。回家後，小敏會把今天最驚訝的事寫在下一頁。明天每日交換午餐也許又有不同面貌，但這次他們知道如何開始。
