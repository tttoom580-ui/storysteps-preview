Day Five: The Photo Problem

交換午餐的三十天 · 第 7 / 25 章 · 1000以下 · 英中對照 · 校訂中

A classmate posted a lunch photo that showed Min's private note. At first, Min thought the problem could be solved before the next bell. A closer look showed that someone else's wishes mattered too. Lola listened without interrupting, while Ms. Chen watched the group from nearby. They did not have to agree on everything immediately. There was time to separate a real observation from a story someone had only imagined. Min took out the lunch notebook and asked what should be checked first.

一名同學上傳午餐照片，拍到了小敏的私人紙條。 起初，小敏以為下次鐘響前就能解決問題。但仔細看過後才發現，別人的願望也很重要。樂樂沒有打斷，靜靜傾聽，陳老師則在附近看著大家。眾人不必立刻同意每件事，還有時間分清真實觀察與想像中的說法。小敏拿出午餐筆記本，問大家應先確認什麼。

The first idea sounded simple, and that was useful. Still, Min wanted to know whether it respected everyone's choice. Lola suggested changing only one part rather than throwing the whole plan away. They drew two small boxes on a page: what we can do and what we must ask about. The list made the daily lunch swap easier to discuss without turning a friend into an enemy. They found as in a short reading exercise near the end of the page. Min drew a tiny sign beside bedroom so it would be easier to remember. Lola chose believe as a word to explain kindly to a new learner. Lola drew a small picture of clock in the margin of the class story page. Lola asked how to use close in a sentence about their day.

第一個想法聽起來很簡單，這是好事。但小敏還想確認是否尊重每個人的選擇。樂樂建議只修改其中一部分，不必推翻整個計畫。他們在紙上畫兩個方框：我們能做的事，以及必須先詢問的事。有了這份清單，討論每日交換午餐便容易多了，也不會把朋友當成敵人。 他們在頁面末尾的短文練習裡找到「as」（當……的時候）。 小敏在「bedroom」（臥室）旁畫上小記號，幫助記憶。 樂樂選了「believe」（相信），準備耐心向初學者說明。 樂樂在班級故事頁的空白處畫了「clock」（時鐘）的圖。 樂樂問起，如何用「close」（關閉）寫一句與今天有關的話。

Lola helped ask for the post to be removed without starting a fight. The plan became real only when someone took the first small action. Min checked the time, Lola checked the shared materials, and Ms. Chen confirmed which steps needed adult approval. The group left a clear record so that a person arriving later could understand the decision. They also agreed on what to do if something went wrong. It was not exciting in the way stories often promised, but it let everyone move forward with confidence. In the English practice book, day appeared on the next line. Min read dream aloud, then checked the meaning with Lola. Another card carried error and a simple drawing by a classmate. The word hear gave the group a new example to discuss. Lola put just on the board without turning it into a test.

樂樂幫忙請對方刪除貼文，沒有吵架。 有人做出第一個小小行動時，計畫才真正開始。小敏確認時間，樂樂檢查共用材料，陳老師確認哪些步驟需要大人同意。小組留下清楚紀錄，讓稍後到的人也能理解決定。他們還約好若事情出錯該怎麼辦。這不像故事裡常見的刺激冒險，卻讓每個人都更有信心往前走。 英文練習本的下一行出現「day」（一天）。 小敏朗讀「dream」（夢），再和樂樂確認意思。 另一張圖卡上有「error」（錯誤），旁邊是同學的簡筆畫。 「hear」（聽見）這個字讓小組多了一個可以討論的例子。 樂樂把「just」（只是、剛好）寫在板上，卻沒有把它變成考試。

For a minute, the group spoke all at once. Min raised a hand and suggested taking turns. Lola repeated the question in simpler words, so that even a quiet student could follow the talk. Nobody needed to win an argument. They needed to learn which part of the problem could be handled now and which part needed more help. The daily lunch swap remained difficult, but it no longer felt impossible. They found laugh in a short reading exercise near the end of the page. Min drew a tiny sign beside nature so it would be easier to remember. Lola chose noise as a word to explain kindly to a new learner. A small study card showed proud, which Min copied beside today's notes. Lola asked how to use rainbow in a sentence about their day.

有一分鐘，大家幾乎同時開口。小敏舉起手，提議輪流說話。樂樂用更簡單的詞重述問題，讓安靜的學生也能跟上。沒有人需要爭贏；重要的是分清哪些事現在能做、哪些事需要更多協助。每日交換午餐仍然困難，卻不再讓人覺得無法處理。 他們在頁面末尾的短文練習裡找到「laugh」（笑）。 小敏在「nature」（自然）旁畫上小記號，幫助記憶。 樂樂選了「noise」（噪音），準備耐心向初學者說明。 一張小小練習卡上寫著「proud」（驕傲的），小敏把它抄在今日筆記旁。 樂樂問起，如何用「rainbow」（彩虹）寫一句與今天有關的話。

Their class agreed to ask before posting other people's food or notes. They gathered the papers and put the shared things back in their places. Min could feel that the room had changed even though the desks had not moved. Lola pointed to the page where the group had written its first worry. The words were still there, but they no longer told the whole story. Now there was also an action, a result, and a reason to look forward to the next meeting. In the English practice book, rich appeared on the next line. Min read sky aloud, then checked the meaning with Lola. Another card carried time and a simple drawing by a classmate. The word whether gave the group a new example to discuss. Lola put yard on the board without turning it into a test.

班上同意，公開別人的食物或紙條前要先問。 他們整理紙張，把共用物品放回原處。雖然桌子沒有移動，小敏卻覺得房間已經不同。樂樂指出小組寫下第一個擔憂的那一頁。那些字還在，卻已無法概括整件事。現在多了行動、結果，以及期待下次見面的理由。 英文練習本的下一行出現「rich」（富有的）。 小敏朗讀「sky」（天空），再和樂樂確認意思。 另一張圖卡上有「time」（時間），旁邊是同學的簡筆畫。 「whether」（是否）這個字讓小組多了一個可以討論的例子。 樂樂把「yard」（院子）寫在板上，卻沒有把它變成考試。

The hallway grew quiet, and Min finally had time to think. A good friend had not supplied every answer. Instead, Lola had left space for another idea and had stayed when the first idea failed. That was harder to notice than a loud victory, but it was real. Min made a promise to remember the person as well as the task. The friendship at lunch was important; so were the people who helped shape it. With that thought, the next chapter of their ordinary and extraordinary days felt possible.

走廊逐漸安靜，小敏終於有時間思考。好朋友不是替自己提供每個答案；樂樂反而留下思考空間，也在第一個想法失敗時陪在身旁。這比熱鬧的勝利難以察覺，卻是真實的。小敏答應自己，不只記得任務，也要記得參與的人。午餐桌上的友情重要，幫忙塑造它的人也同樣重要。帶著這個念頭，他們平凡又不平凡的日子可以繼續寫下去。
