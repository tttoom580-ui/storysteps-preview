An Idea on a Napkin

交換午餐的三十天 · 第 2 / 25 章 · 1000以下 · 英中對照 · 校訂中

用互動版查詞、朗讀與收藏

Lola suggested swapping lunches on purpose for thirty school days. The day began with a small question and soon became more interesting than anyone expected. Min could hear other students talking, but did not hurry to join their guesses. Lola pointed out a detail that had been easy to miss. They stood together in the school cafeteria, where the same familiar objects suddenly seemed to tell a different story. Min kept the lunch notebook close, not as a perfect answer, but as a place to begin.

樂樂提議，兩人刻意交換三十個上學日的午餐。 這一天從一個小疑問開始，後來卻比大家預想的更有趣。小敏聽到其他同學交談，沒有急著加入猜測。樂樂指出了一個容易忽略的細節。他們站在學校餐廳裡，熟悉的東西忽然像在說另一段故事。小敏把午餐筆記本帶在身邊，那不是完美答案，而是一個開始。

They began by asking who might be affected by the next step. Lola thought about the people who had not spoken yet, and Min thought about the rule they had already promised to follow. One person offered a quick answer, but the group asked for a reason. A good question was not an attack. It was a way to make the answer safer and kinder. Ms. Chen reminded them that everyone could ask for a pause. In the English practice book, add appeared on the next line. Min read also aloud, then checked the meaning with Lola. Another card carried bring and a simple drawing by a classmate. The word but gave the group a new example to discuss. Lola put cousin on the board without turning it into a test.

大家先問，下一步會影響到誰。樂樂想到還沒發言的人，小敏則想到自己早已承諾遵守的規則。有人很快提出答案，其他人卻請他說明理由。好問題不是攻擊，而是讓答案更安全、更體貼的方法。陳老師提醒大家，每個人都可以要求暫停。 英文練習本的下一行出現「add」（增加、加入）。 小敏朗讀「also」（也），再和樂樂確認意思。 另一張圖卡上有「bring」（帶來），旁邊是同學的簡筆畫。 「but」（但是）這個字讓小組多了一個可以討論的例子。 樂樂把「cousin」（表親、表哥／表妹）寫在板上，卻沒有把它變成考試。

Min asked both families and the school about food allergies and permission. Min chose the part of the work that could be done carefully. Lola handled a different task and promised to report back before making any large change. A short question from Ms. Chen caught an error in their first version. Instead of hiding it, they made a neat correction on the page. That correction did not erase their effort. It showed that the group was learning how to be responsible for its own work. They found family in a short reading exercise near the end of the page. Min drew a tiny sign beside famous so it would be easier to remember. Lola chose fill as a word to explain kindly to a new learner. A small study card showed guy, which Min copied beside today's notes. Lola asked how to use helpful in a sentence about their day.

小敏先向雙方家庭和學校確認過敏問題及許可。 小敏選擇自己能謹慎完成的工作，樂樂負責另一項任務，並答應在重大更改前先回報。陳老師的一個簡短問題，讓大家發現第一版中的錯誤。他們沒有掩飾，而是在頁面上整齊修正。這項修正沒有抹去先前的努力，反而證明小組正在學習對自己的工作負責。 他們在頁面末尾的短文練習裡找到「family」（家人）。 小敏在「famous」（有名的）旁畫上小記號，幫助記憶。 樂樂選了「fill」（裝滿、填入），準備耐心向初學者說明。 一張小小練習卡上寫著「guy」（男子），小敏把它抄在今日筆記旁。 樂樂問起，如何用「helpful」（有幫助的）寫一句與今天有關的話。

Min was afraid that saying the wrong thing would make the day worse. Lola answered that staying silent could also leave a misunderstanding in place. They practiced one honest sentence and checked that it did not blame anyone unfairly. This took longer than making a joke, but the result was clearer. Near the table, Ms. Chen nodded and let the young people try their own words before offering advice. In the English practice book, lip appeared on the next line. Min read power aloud, then checked the meaning with Lola. Lola drew a small picture of river in the margin of the class story page. The word science gave the group a new example to discuss. Lola put sheep on the board without turning it into a test.

小敏害怕說錯話會讓事情變糟。樂樂回答，保持沉默有時也會讓誤會繼續存在。他們練習說出一句誠實的話，再確認沒有不公平地責怪任何人。這比開玩笑花時間，結果卻更清楚。桌旁的陳老師點頭，先讓年輕人用自己的話嘗試，再提供建議。 英文練習本的下一行出現「lip」（嘴唇）。 小敏朗讀「power」（權力；特權），再和樂樂確認意思。 樂樂在班級故事頁的空白處畫了「river」（河流）的圖。 「science」（科學）這個字讓小組多了一個可以討論的例子。 樂樂把「sheep」（綿羊）寫在板上，卻沒有把它變成考試。

They agreed on a food-safety card and a rule that either could say no. Ms. Chen asked the group to explain its choice in everyday words. Min tried once, paused, and then gave a shorter answer. Lola added an important detail that had been left out. The explanation became clear enough for another person to check. That mattered more than making their decision sound grand. The friendship at lunch could grow only if other people understood how it worked. They found south in a short reading exercise near the end of the page. Min drew a tiny sign beside sunny so it would be easier to remember. Lola chose they as a word to explain kindly to a new learner. A small study card showed tool, which Min copied beside today's notes. Lola asked how to use video in a sentence about their day.

她們訂出食物安全卡，也約定任何一方都能拒絕。 陳老師請小組用平常的話解釋選擇。小敏試了一次，停頓後改用更短的回答。樂樂補充了一個漏掉的重要細節。解釋變得足夠清楚，讓別人也能核對。這比把決定說得偉大更重要。只有其他人理解做法，午餐桌上的友情才有機會繼續成長。 他們在頁面末尾的短文練習裡找到「south」（南方）。 小敏在「sunny」（晴朗的）旁畫上小記號，幫助記憶。 樂樂選了「they」（他們；他們的），準備耐心向初學者說明。 一張小小練習卡上寫著「tool」（工具），小敏把它抄在今日筆記旁。 樂樂問起，如何用「video」（影片）寫一句與今天有關的話。

When the last bell sounded, Min saw that Lola was waiting by the familiar door. The two had not said everything perfectly. They had even disagreed about a detail, yet the disagreement had made their plan clearer. The moment did not need a large speech. A small smile and one honest thank-you were enough. At home, Min would remember the most surprising part of the day and write it on the next page. Tomorrow the daily lunch swap might look different again. This time they would know how to begin.

最後一聲鈴響時，小敏看見樂樂在熟悉的門旁等待。兩人不是每句話都說得完美，甚至為某個細節意見不同，但那場分歧讓計畫更清楚。這一刻不需要長篇大論，一個微笑和真誠的道謝就夠了。回家後，小敏會把今天最驚訝的事寫在下一頁。明天每日交換午餐也許又有不同面貌，但這次他們知道如何開始。
