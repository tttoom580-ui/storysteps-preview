第三天：胡椒加太多

Day Three: Too Much Pepper

Lola's family had packed spicy food that Min could not enjoy. Nobody had planned for this moment. The room was full of ordinary sounds, yet Min felt that something important had changed. Lola waited beside the door and let the first surprise settle. It was tempting to find an easy answer before anyone knew the whole story. Instead, they looked carefully at what was truly in front of them. Min opened the lunch notebook and made a small note about the day.

樂樂家準備的食物太辣，小敏吃不習慣。 沒有人預料到這一刻。學校餐廳充滿平常的聲音，小敏卻感覺有件重要的事改變了。樂樂站在門旁，讓最初的驚訝慢慢沉澱。大家很容易在弄清事情之前就急著下結論，但他們決定先看清眼前真正發生的事。小敏翻開午餐筆記本，記下今天的小小發現。

Min was afraid that saying the wrong thing would make the day worse. Lola answered that staying silent could also leave a misunderstanding in place. They practiced one honest sentence and checked that it did not blame anyone unfairly. This took longer than making a joke, but the result was clearer. Near the table, Ms. Chen nodded and let the young people try their own words before offering advice. The word although gave the group a new example to discuss. Lola put bean on the board without turning it into a test. They found deal in a short reading exercise near the end of the page. Min drew a tiny sign beside dig so it would be easier to remember. Lola chose during as a word to explain kindly to a new learner.

小敏害怕說錯話會讓事情變糟。樂樂回答，保持沉默有時也會讓誤會繼續存在。他們練習說出一句誠實的話，再確認沒有不公平地責怪任何人。這比開玩笑花時間，結果卻更清楚。桌旁的陳老師點頭，先讓年輕人用自己的話嘗試，再提供建議。 「although」（雖然）這個字讓小組多了一個可以討論的例子。 樂樂把「bean」（豆子）寫在板上，卻沒有把它變成考試。 他們在頁面末尾的短文練習裡找到「deal」（處理；作決定）。 小敏在「dig」（挖）旁畫上小記號，幫助記憶。 樂樂選了「during」（在……期間），準備耐心向初學者說明。

Min said so kindly, and Lola asked her family for a mild option next time. There was a quiet kind of courage in trying again. Min noticed that the last attempt had taught them something, even without a perfect result. Lola offered practical help and asked which part would be most useful. They worked together for a short time and then stopped to check whether they were still following the rule. Ms. Chen praised the careful check more than the speed of the work. A small study card showed frog, which Min copied beside today's notes. Lola asked how to use height in a sentence about their day. In the English practice book, hen appeared on the next line. Min read least aloud, then checked the meaning with Lola. Another card carried long and a simple drawing by a classmate.

小敏委婉說出感受，樂樂請家人下次做清淡一些。 願意再試一次，是一種安靜的勇氣。小敏發現，就算上次結果不完美，大家仍學到了一些事。樂樂提供實際協助，先問哪一部分最有用。他們一起工作一小段時間，再停下來確認是否仍遵守規則。陳老師稱讚的不是速度，而是這份細心。 一張小小練習卡上寫著「frog」（青蛙），小敏把它抄在今日筆記旁。 樂樂問起，如何用「height」（高度）寫一句與今天有關的話。 英文練習本的下一行出現「hen」（母雞）。 小敏朗讀「least」（至少），再和樂樂確認意思。 另一張圖卡上有「long」（長的），旁邊是同學的簡筆畫。

They began by asking who might be affected by the next step. Lola thought about the people who had not spoken yet, and Min thought about the rule they had already promised to follow. One person offered a quick answer, but the group asked for a reason. A good question was not an attack. It was a way to make the answer safer and kinder. Ms. Chen reminded them that everyone could ask for a pause. The word minute gave the group a new example to discuss. Lola put Ms. on the board without turning it into a test. They found neck in a short reading exercise near the end of the page. Min drew a tiny sign beside point so it would be easier to remember. Lola chose put as a word to explain kindly to a new learner.

大家先問，下一步會影響到誰。樂樂想到還沒發言的人，小敏則想到自己早已承諾遵守的規則。有人很快提出答案，其他人卻請他說明理由。好問題不是攻擊，而是讓答案更安全、更體貼的方法。陳老師提醒大家，每個人都可以要求暫停。 「minute」（分鐘）這個字讓小組多了一個可以討論的例子。 樂樂把「Ms.」（女士）寫在板上，卻沒有把它變成考試。 他們在頁面末尾的短文練習裡找到「neck」（脖子）。 小敏在「point」（指向；重點）旁畫上小記號，幫助記憶。 樂樂選了「put」（放置），準備耐心向初學者說明。

Neither girl pretended to like food just to keep the plan alive. The answer made someone smile, but Min still asked one final question: was everyone comfortable with what came next? Lola listened to the replies, including the quiet ones. They changed a small detail when one person needed something different. Nothing about the change made the story less meaningful. It helped the friendship at lunch belong to more than one person, which was why the work had begun. A small study card showed race, which Min copied beside today's notes. Lola asked how to use safe in a sentence about their day. In the English practice book, snow appeared on the next line. Min read sorry aloud, then checked the meaning with Lola. Another card carried win and a simple drawing by a classmate.

兩人都不為了維持交換計畫而假裝喜歡。 答案讓某個人笑了，但小敏仍問最後一個問題：大家都能接受下一步嗎？樂樂聽取回答，包括輕聲說出的意見。有人有不同需求，他們就修改一個小細節。這種修改沒有讓故事失去意義，反而讓午餐桌上的友情不只屬於一個人；這也正是大家當初開始的原因。 一張小小練習卡上寫著「race」（賽跑），小敏把它抄在今日筆記旁。 樂樂問起，如何用「safe」（安全的）寫一句與今天有關的話。 英文練習本的下一行出現「snow」（雪）。 小敏朗讀「sorry」（抱歉），再和樂樂確認意思。 另一張圖卡上有「win」（贏得），旁邊是同學的簡筆畫。

Near the end of the day, Min stayed beside Lola for one more minute. They remembered how strange the morning had felt. A problem that once seemed to fill the whole room had become several smaller questions. Each question could be handled in turn. They did not know what the following day would bring, and that was all right. What they had learned was not a magic answer but a habit: notice a change, ask with care, and let people choose for themselves. Min closed the lunch notebook and left with a lighter heart.

一天快結束時，小敏在樂樂身邊多待了一分鐘。他們想起早上的奇怪感受。原本像占滿整間房間的難題，現在分成幾個較小的問題，可以逐一處理。他們不知道明天會帶來什麼，但這沒有關係。今天學到的不是神奇答案，而是一種習慣：留意變化、仔細發問，也讓別人自己選擇。小敏闔上午餐筆記本，帶著較輕鬆的心情離開。
