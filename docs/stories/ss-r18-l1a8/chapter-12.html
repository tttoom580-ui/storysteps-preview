第十二天：橘子驚喜

Day Twelve: The Orange Surprise

Min hid a cheerful orange drawing under Lola's napkin. The light through the window looked ordinary, but the news did not. Min stopped for a moment, feeling both curious and unsure. Lola did not laugh at the question, even when it sounded strange. They knew from earlier mistakes that moving too fast could make a simple problem harder. The school cafeteria offered enough space for a calm talk. On the first page of the lunch notebook, Min wrote what was known and left a blank space for what remained uncertain.

小敏在樂樂的餐巾紙下放了一張快樂橘子的圖。 窗外的光線和平常一樣，消息卻一點也不平常。小敏停了一會兒，既好奇又不確定。就算問題聽來奇怪，樂樂也沒有取笑。他們從先前的錯誤學到，行動太快反而可能讓小問題變複雜。學校餐廳有足夠空間讓大家平靜討論。小敏在午餐筆記本第一頁寫下已知的事，也留下空白給尚未確定的部分。

For a minute, the group spoke all at once. Min raised a hand and suggested taking turns. Lola repeated the question in simpler words, so that even a quiet student could follow the talk. Nobody needed to win an argument. They needed to learn which part of the problem could be handled now and which part needed more help. The daily lunch swap remained difficult, but it no longer felt impossible. In the English practice book, answer appeared on the next line. Min read bicycle aloud, then checked the meaning with Lola. Another card carried body and a simple drawing by a classmate. The word bow gave the group a new example to discuss. Lola put jeans on the board without turning it into a test.

有一分鐘，大家幾乎同時開口。小敏舉起手，提議輪流說話。樂樂用更簡單的詞重述問題，讓安靜的學生也能跟上。沒有人需要爭贏；重要的是分清哪些事現在能做、哪些事需要更多協助。每日交換午餐仍然困難，卻不再讓人覺得無法處理。 英文練習本的下一行出現「answer」（答案；回答）。 小敏朗讀「bicycle」（自行車），再和樂樂確認意思。 另一張圖卡上有「body」（身體），旁邊是同學的簡筆畫。 「bow」（鞠躬）這個字讓小組多了一個可以討論的例子。 樂樂把「jeans」（牛仔褲）寫在板上，卻沒有把它變成考試。

Lola wrote a silly thank-you poem in return. Once everyone understood the choice, the next step was smaller than it had seemed. Min spoke first and waited for an answer. Lola watched the other person's face instead of trying to hurry the talk. There was room to say no, to ask for an explanation, or to think for another minute. The group used the lunch notebook as a guide, not as an order that could never be changed. They found language in a short reading exercise near the end of the page. Min drew a tiny sign beside mine so it would be easier to remember. Lola chose of as a word to explain kindly to a new learner. A small study card showed own, which Min copied beside today's notes. Lola asked how to use pick in a sentence about their day.

樂樂寫了一首逗趣的感謝小詩回禮。 當所有人都了解選擇後，下一步其實沒有想像中困難。小敏先開口，然後等待回答。樂樂注意對方的表情，沒有催促。大家可以說不、要求解釋，或多想一分鐘。小組把午餐筆記本當作指引，而不是絕不能改變的命令。 他們在頁面末尾的短文練習裡找到「language」（語言）。 小敏在「mine」（我的）旁畫上小記號，幫助記憶。 樂樂選了「of」（的、屬於），準備耐心向初學者說明。 一張小小練習卡上寫著「own」（自己的），小敏把它抄在今日筆記旁。 樂樂問起，如何用「pick」（挑選）寫一句與今天有關的話。

The first idea sounded simple, and that was useful. Still, Min wanted to know whether it respected everyone's choice. Lola suggested changing only one part rather than throwing the whole plan away. They drew two small boxes on a page: what we can do and what we must ask about. The list made the daily lunch swap easier to discuss without turning a friend into an enemy. In the English practice book, planet appeared on the next line. Min read salt aloud, then checked the meaning with Lola. Another card carried say and a simple drawing by a classmate. The word second gave the group a new example to discuss. Lola put so on the board without turning it into a test.

第一個想法聽起來很簡單，這是好事。但小敏還想確認是否尊重每個人的選擇。樂樂建議只修改其中一部分，不必推翻整個計畫。他們在紙上畫兩個方框：我們能做的事，以及必須先詢問的事。有了這份清單，討論每日交換午餐便容易多了，也不會把朋友當成敵人。 英文練習本的下一行出現「planet」（星球）。 小敏朗讀「salt」（鹽），再和樂樂確認意思。 另一張圖卡上有「say」（說），旁邊是同學的簡筆畫。 「second」（第二個）這個字讓小組多了一個可以討論的例子。 樂樂把「so」（所以）寫在板上，卻沒有把它變成考試。

The drawing and poem made their notebook feel like a friendship book. Before calling the task finished, Min went back over the facts. One detail had changed, and another still needed an answer. Lola wrote both down rather than hiding the uncertain part. They could be pleased with progress without pretending that every worry had disappeared. By comparing the new result with the first note, they could see what had actually improved. The friendship at lunch still mattered, but now the group knew one more way to protect it. They found some in a short reading exercise near the end of the page. Min drew a tiny sign beside soon so it would be easier to remember. Lola chose spring as a word to explain kindly to a new learner. A small study card showed wind, which Min copied beside today's notes. Lola asked how to use wish in a sentence about their day.

畫和詩讓筆記本像一本友情紀念冊。 在宣布完成前，小敏重新檢查事實。有一個細節改變了，另一個仍有待解答。樂樂把兩者都寫下，沒有隱藏不確定之處。大家可以為進展高興，卻不必假裝所有擔憂都消失。比對新結果與第一張筆記後，他們看出真正改善的部分。午餐桌上的友情依然重要，現在小組又多學會一種守護它的方法。 他們在頁面末尾的短文練習裡找到「some」（一些）。 小敏在「soon」（很快）旁畫上小記號，幫助記憶。 樂樂選了「spring」（春天），準備耐心向初學者說明。 一張小小練習卡上寫著「wind」（風），小敏把它抄在今日筆記旁。 樂樂問起，如何用「wish」（希望）寫一句與今天有關的話。

Outside, the air was cooler than it had been at lunch. Min looked back once and saw the school cafeteria in the evening light. It seemed like an ordinary place, but there was more to it now: a remembered question, a mistake corrected, and a choice made together. Lola waved from a few steps away. They still had their separate dreams and small worries, yet they also shared one clear memory. Whatever happened next, they could return to that memory without needing to pretend the day had been perfect.

外面的空氣比午餐時涼了些。小敏回頭望了一眼晚光中的學校餐廳。它看似平凡，現在卻多了值得記住的問題、改正的錯誤，以及共同做出的選擇。樂樂在幾步外揮手。他們仍有各自的夢想和小煩惱，也有一段共同的清晰回憶。無論接下來發生什麼，都不必假裝今天完美，就能想起它的價值。
