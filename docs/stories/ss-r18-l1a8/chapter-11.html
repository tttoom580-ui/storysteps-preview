第十一天：公平分量

Day Eleven: A Fair Share

交換午餐的三十天 · 第 11 / 25 章 · 1000以下 · 英中對照 · 校訂中

用互動版查詞、朗讀與收藏

Lola noticed that one lunch box always held more food than the other. At first, Min thought the problem could be solved before the next bell. A closer look showed that someone else's wishes mattered too. Lola listened without interrupting, while Ms. Chen watched the group from nearby. They did not have to agree on everything immediately. There was time to separate a real observation from a story someone had only imagined. Min took out the lunch notebook and asked what should be checked first.

樂樂發現兩個便當的分量一直不一樣。 起初，小敏以為下次鐘響前就能解決問題。但仔細看過後才發現，別人的願望也很重要。樂樂沒有打斷，靜靜傾聽，陳老師則在附近看著大家。眾人不必立刻同意每件事，還有時間分清真實觀察與想像中的說法。小敏拿出午餐筆記本，問大家應先確認什麼。

For a minute, the group spoke all at once. Min raised a hand and suggested taking turns. Lola repeated the question in simpler words, so that even a quiet student could follow the talk. Nobody needed to win an argument. They needed to learn which part of the problem could be handled now and which part needed more help. The daily lunch swap remained difficult, but it no longer felt impossible. Lola asked how to use be in a sentence about their day. In the English practice book, blue appeared on the next line. Min read call aloud, then checked the meaning with Lola. Lola drew a small picture of car in the margin of the class story page. The word cold gave the group a new example to discuss.

有一分鐘，大家幾乎同時開口。小敏舉起手，提議輪流說話。樂樂用更簡單的詞重述問題，讓安靜的學生也能跟上。沒有人需要爭贏；重要的是分清哪些事現在能做、哪些事需要更多協助。每日交換午餐仍然困難，卻不再讓人覺得無法處理。 樂樂問起，如何用「be」（是、成為）寫一句與今天有關的話。 英文練習本的下一行出現「blue」（藍色的）。 小敏朗讀「call」（叫；打電話），再和樂樂確認意思。 樂樂在班級故事頁的空白處畫了「car」（汽車）的圖。 「cold」（寒冷的）這個字讓小組多了一個可以討論的例子。

They talked with their families about eating enough for their own needs. Once everyone understood the choice, the next step was smaller than it had seemed. Min spoke first and waited for an answer. Lola watched the other person's face instead of trying to hurry the talk. There was room to say no, to ask for an explanation, or to think for another minute. The group used the lunch notebook as a guide, not as an order that could never be changed. Lola put cook on the board without turning it into a test. They found dictionary in a short reading exercise near the end of the page. Min drew a tiny sign beside fan so it would be easier to remember. Lola chose maybe as a word to explain kindly to a new learner. A small study card showed nurse, which Min copied beside today's notes.

她們和家人討論每個人需要的份量。 當所有人都了解選擇後，下一步其實沒有想像中困難。小敏先開口，然後等待回答。樂樂注意對方的表情，沒有催促。大家可以說不、要求解釋，或多想一分鐘。小組把午餐筆記本當作指引，而不是絕不能改變的命令。 樂樂把「cook」（烹煮）寫在板上，卻沒有把它變成考試。 他們在頁面末尾的短文練習裡找到「dictionary」（字典）。 小敏在「fan」（風扇）旁畫上小記號，幫助記憶。 樂樂選了「maybe」（也許），準備耐心向初學者說明。 一張小小練習卡上寫著「nurse」（護理師），小敏把它抄在今日筆記旁。

The first idea sounded simple, and that was useful. Still, Min wanted to know whether it respected everyone's choice. Lola suggested changing only one part rather than throwing the whole plan away. They drew two small boxes on a page: what we can do and what we must ask about. The list made the daily lunch swap easier to discuss without turning a friend into an enemy. Lola asked how to use pants in a sentence about their day. In the English practice book, past appeared on the next line. Min read probably aloud, then checked the meaning with Lola. Another card carried secretary and a simple drawing by a classmate. The word sentence gave the group a new example to discuss.

第一個想法聽起來很簡單，這是好事。但小敏還想確認是否尊重每個人的選擇。樂樂建議只修改其中一部分，不必推翻整個計畫。他們在紙上畫兩個方框：我們能做的事，以及必須先詢問的事。有了這份清單，討論每日交換午餐便容易多了，也不會把朋友當成敵人。 樂樂問起，如何用「pants」（長褲）寫一句與今天有關的話。 英文練習本的下一行出現「past」（過去的）。 小敏朗讀「probably」（可能），再和樂樂確認意思。 另一張圖卡上有「secretary」（秘書），旁邊是同學的簡筆畫。 「sentence」（句子）這個字讓小組多了一個可以討論的例子。

The girls stopped trying to make the lunches look identical. Before calling the task finished, Min went back over the facts. One detail had changed, and another still needed an answer. Lola wrote both down rather than hiding the uncertain part. They could be pleased with progress without pretending that every worry had disappeared. By comparing the new result with the first note, they could see what had actually improved. The friendship at lunch still mattered, but now the group knew one more way to protect it. Lola put temple on the board without turning it into a test. They found thousand in a short reading exercise near the end of the page. Min drew a tiny sign beside tree so it would be easier to remember. Lola chose under as a word to explain kindly to a new learner. A small study card showed weather, which Min copied beside today's notes.

兩人不再勉強讓便當看起來一模一樣。 在宣布完成前，小敏重新檢查事實。有一個細節改變了，另一個仍有待解答。樂樂把兩者都寫下，沒有隱藏不確定之處。大家可以為進展高興，卻不必假裝所有擔憂都消失。比對新結果與第一張筆記後，他們看出真正改善的部分。午餐桌上的友情依然重要，現在小組又多學會一種守護它的方法。 樂樂把「temple」（寺廟）寫在板上，卻沒有把它變成考試。 他們在頁面末尾的短文練習裡找到「thousand」（一千）。 小敏在「tree」（樹）旁畫上小記號，幫助記憶。 樂樂選了「under」（在……下方），準備耐心向初學者說明。 一張小小練習卡上寫著「weather」（天氣），小敏把它抄在今日筆記旁。

The hallway grew quiet, and Min finally had time to think. A good friend had not supplied every answer. Instead, Lola had left space for another idea and had stayed when the first idea failed. That was harder to notice than a loud victory, but it was real. Min made a promise to remember the person as well as the task. The friendship at lunch was important; so were the people who helped shape it. With that thought, the next chapter of their ordinary and extraordinary days felt possible.

走廊逐漸安靜，小敏終於有時間思考。好朋友不是替自己提供每個答案；樂樂反而留下思考空間，也在第一個想法失敗時陪在身旁。這比熱鬧的勝利難以察覺，卻是真實的。小敏答應自己，不只記得任務，也要記得參與的人。午餐桌上的友情重要，幫忙塑造它的人也同樣重要。帶著這個念頭，他們平凡又不平凡的日子可以繼續寫下去。
