科學家的第二個名字

The Scientist's Second Name

時間旅行者的殺人不在場證明 · 第 11 / 25 章 · 5000–6000 · 英中對照 · 校訂中

It began not with a battle but with an ordinary interruption in the transit laboratory. The victim used another identity while investigating illegal transfers of vulnerable patients. At first, no one considered the interruption important enough to preserve. Lin An thought otherwise. A forgotten detail could become a map, and a map could be used to control where frightened people were permitted to go. The station's modest acclaim did not justify surrendering resident privacy. The shared-account policy contained an ambiguity that allowed several technicians to use one password without recording their names. The medical panel distinguished bodily harm from ordinary discomfort reported during sleep. A cold draught crossed the hearing room when someone opened the ventilation hatch.

事情不是從戰鬥開始，而是從時間轉移實驗室裡一次平凡的中斷開始。死者調查弱勢病患非法轉移時，使用過另一個身分。起初沒人認為這件事值得留下紀錄，但林安想法不同。遭忽略的細節可能變成地圖，而地圖也可能被用來控制驚恐的人可以去哪裡。 救援站獲得一些讚譽，卻不足以成為交出居民隱私的理由。 共用帳號政策有一處模糊之處，容許多名技師共用密碼而不記姓名。 醫療小組區分身體傷害與睡眠時的一般不適。 有人打開通風口時，一股冷風穿過聽證室。

Ji Ren came back carrying a practical objection rather than an inspiring speech. A clinic register connects that name to a child who vanished during a closed trial. They had both seen people harmed when an assumption hardened into policy. A signed report could be wrong, and an emotional memory could be right, but neither became proof merely by being repeated. They agreed to seek a measurement from an independent observer. Other entries in the evidence ledger offered useful comparisons: The archived machine log measured heat in Fahrenheit, unlike the city's present metric settings. The original train invoices survived inside a fireproof document case beneath the market counter. A geographical survey confirmed the distance between remote stops.

紀任帶回的是實際的異議，而不是振奮人心的演說。診所紀錄把那個名字連到封閉試驗中失蹤的孩子。他們都見過假設硬化成政策後如何傷人。有簽名的報告可能是錯的，強烈的記憶也可能是真的；兩者都不會因為反覆提起就變成證據。他們決定找獨立觀察員再測量一次。證據帳冊裡還有可供比對的紀錄： 封存機械紀錄用華氏溫度量測熱能，與現行公制設定不同。 原始列車發票存放在市場櫃檯下的防火文件箱，因此得以倖存。 地理測量確認偏遠停靠站之間的距離。

“We can tell the truth without pretending we know everything,” Lin An said. Ji Ren replied that a partial truth was difficult to defend against people who controlled the doors. She answered that a perfect story with no verifiable source was more dangerous. They divided the work: one would protect the witnesses, while the other checked the physical records and the sequence of events. The security expert explained how a hacker could exploit weak passwords. Even a layman could see that the contract granted indefinite data rights. The new policy would liberate residents from compulsory app registration.

「我們能說實話，不需要假裝知道一切。」林安說。紀任回答，當對方掌控所有門口時，局部真相比較難捍衛。她則認為沒有可驗證來源的完美故事更危險。他們分配工作：一人保護證人，另一人核查實體紀錄和事件時序。 資安專家說明駭客可能如何利用薄弱密碼。 即使外行人也看得出契約授予無限期資料使用權。 新政策將讓居民擺脫強制應用程式登記。

The argument moved from a private corridor into a formal room, where every statement would be copied. A senior official from the chronology board demanded certainty before granting access. Lin An offered a less comfortable bargain: a limited preservation order now, followed by independent examination and a chance for every accused person to respond. That was enough to prevent an irreversible decision. Other entries in the evidence ledger offered useful comparisons: A mermaid mural from the lost railway appeared on two photographs taken decades apart. The seized archive included miscellaneous receipts that proved surprisingly important to the timeline. Each occurrence of an unusual login had to be recorded independently. A family outing ticket listed a train route that the government had later erased from transit maps.

爭論從私下走廊進入正式會議室，每句話都會留下副本。時間委員會的一名高級官員要求先確定真相才准查資料。林安提出比較不舒服的交換：先給有期限的保全命令，再獨立檢查，也讓所有被指控者有回應機會。這足以擋下一個不可逆的決定。證據帳冊裡還有可供比對的紀錄： 失蹤鐵路上的美人魚壁畫，出現在相隔幾十年拍攝的兩張照片裡。 查扣檔案裡有各種雜項收據，對重建時間線竟出乎意料重要。 每次異常登入事件都必須獨立記錄。 一張家庭出遊車票印有後來遭政府從運輸圖抹除的列車路線。

Lin tracks the people affected rather than hunting a sensational time paradox. Even those who agreed with the choice could see its cost. Time was running out, supplies were not unlimited, and a mistake might travel far beyond this room. Ji Ren asked whether they should conceal the uncertainty until the crisis passed. Lin An shook her head. People could not make free choices if the information reaching them had already been edited. The staff promised to persevere through the difficult budget review. The judge asked the court to ponder how a government could collect taxes from an officially nonexistent district. The hearing began to revolve around verifiable records instead of arguments about whose dreams felt convincing.

林安追查受影響的人，而不是只追逐轟動的時間悖論。即使同意這個選擇的人，也看得出代價。時間不多、資源有限，一次錯誤可能傳得很遠。紀任問是否該先隱瞞不確定性，等危機過後再說。林安搖頭。若資訊早被剪裁，人們便無法作出自由的選擇。 員工承諾堅持完成艱難的預算審查。 法官請法庭思考：政府怎能向官方不存在的行政區收稅？ 聽證逐漸圍繞可查的紀錄，而不是爭論誰的夢感覺更真實。

Later, under dim lights, Lin An reread the original question. The dispute was larger now, but its center remained unchanged: who had the right to make an irreversible choice for someone else? Ji Ren stayed until the last copy was sealed. Neither spoke about victory. They knew the next door would open only because today’s evidence had been preserved and today’s mistakes had been admitted. A metal kitchen utensil bearing the erased district's address was admitted as a physical exhibit. Lin An refused to turn the contractor into a cartoon villain without evidence. A market yogurt container carried a printed delivery address from the supposedly destroyed station district.

晚些時候，在微弱燈光下，林安重讀最初的問題。爭端已經擴大，但核心沒變：誰有權替別人作出不可逆的選擇？紀任留到最後一份副本封存。他們沒有談勝利，只知道下一扇門之所以能打開，是因為今天保住了證據，也承認了錯誤。 一件刻著被抹除行政區地址的金屬廚具，被列為實體證物。 沒有證據前，林安拒絕把承包商塑造成漫畫式惡棍。 一個市場優格容器印著據稱已毀車站區的送貨地址。
