另一個亞里

The Other Ari

時間旅行者的殺人不在場證明 · 第 8 / 25 章 · 5000–6000 · 英中對照 · 校訂中

用互動版查詞、朗讀與收藏

It began not with a battle but with an ordinary interruption in the transit laboratory. A transit record places Ari in two different years within the same hour according to local clocks. At first, no one considered the interruption important enough to preserve. Lin An thought otherwise. A forgotten detail could become a map, and a map could be used to control where frightened people were permitted to go. A school nurse explained why antibiotic medicine should never be shared. Residents began to applaud after the revised road map was displayed. An astronomer visiting the school read aloud about winter constellations. The new team chose to computerize counts without eliminating paper receipts.

事情不是從戰鬥開始，而是從時間轉移實驗室裡一次平凡的中斷開始。轉移紀錄顯示亞里在本地時鐘所計的同一小時，出現在兩個不同年份。起初沒人認為這件事值得留下紀錄，但林安想法不同。遭忽略的細節可能變成地圖，而地圖也可能被用來控制驚恐的人可以去哪裡。 學校護理師解釋為何抗生素藥品不該與人共用。 修訂道路地圖展示後，居民開始鼓掌。 到校的天文學家朗讀冬季星座的故事。 新團隊選擇電腦化統計，但不取消紙本收據。

Ji Ren came back carrying a practical objection rather than an inspiring speech. The apparent contradiction is possible only if one image was recorded, replayed, and misdated. They had both seen people harmed when an assumption hardened into policy. A signed report could be wrong, and an emotional memory could be right, but neither became proof merely by being repeated. They agreed to seek a measurement from an independent observer. Other entries in the evidence ledger offered useful comparisons: A faulty ramp could disable access even when every rescue kit was ready. The practice team would evacuate the stop if the river reached the warning line. The medical panel asked why expenditure on sleep control had tripled while ordinary clinics lost funding.

紀任帶回的是實際的異議，而不是振奮人心的演說。這個矛盾只有在其中一段影像遭錄製、重播並誤標日期時才可能成立。他們都見過假設硬化成政策後如何傷人。有簽名的報告可能是錯的，強烈的記憶也可能是真的；兩者都不會因為反覆提起就變成證據。他們決定找獨立觀察員再測量一次。證據帳冊裡還有可供比對的紀錄： 即使救援套件都備妥，故障坡道仍會妨礙出入。 若河水達警戒線，演練團隊就會撤離停靠站。 醫療小組詢問，為何睡眠控制支出增加三倍，一般診所預算卻遭削減。

“We can tell the truth without pretending we know everything,” Lin An said. Ji Ren replied that a partial truth was difficult to defend against people who controlled the doors. She answered that a perfect story with no verifiable source was more dangerous. They divided the work: one would protect the witnesses, while the other checked the physical records and the sequence of events. An irritable official complained about the extra testing time. The lower district demanded that missing patients' kin be notified before any memory study began. The school offered a meditation class while the repair crew worked.

「我們能說實話，不需要假裝知道一切。」林安說。紀任回答，當對方掌控所有門口時，局部真相比較難捍衛。她則認為沒有可驗證來源的完美故事更危險。他們分配工作：一人保護證人，另一人核查實體紀錄和事件時序。 一名暴躁官員抱怨額外測試耗時。 下層社區要求，任何記憶研究開始前，都必須通知失蹤病患的親屬。 維修團隊工作時，學校開辦冥想課。

The argument moved from a private corridor into a formal room, where every statement would be copied. A senior official from the chronology board demanded certainty before granting access. Lin An offered a less comfortable bargain: a limited preservation order now, followed by independent examination and a chance for every accused person to respond. That was enough to prevent an irreversible decision. Other entries in the evidence ledger offered useful comparisons: A nickel coin fell from the cracked box of donated objects. The investigator used the floor map to orient new witnesses. An oriental carpet from the old lounge was moved away from the wet entrance. A child treated for pneumonia had been wrongly classified as suffering from forbidden-dream sickness.

爭論從私下走廊進入正式會議室，每句話都會留下副本。時間委員會的一名高級官員要求先確定真相才准查資料。林安提出比較不舒服的交換：先給有期限的保全命令，再獨立檢查，也讓所有被指控者有回應機會。這足以擋下一個不可逆的決定。證據帳冊裡還有可供比對的紀錄： 一枚五分硬幣從破裂的捐贈物箱中掉出。 調查員用樓層圖協助新證人辨明方向。 舊休息室的東方風格地毯被移離潮濕入口。 一名肺炎孩童被錯誤分類為罹患禁忌夢境疾病。

Lin challenges the investigators to test the replay hypothesis before announcing a paradox. Even those who agreed with the choice could see its cost. Time was running out, supplies were not unlimited, and a mistake might travel far beyond this room. Ji Ren asked whether they should conceal the uncertainty until the crisis passed. Lin An shook her head. People could not make free choices if the information reaching them had already been edited. The neurologist refused to activate the untested prototype until an ethics committee approved the trial. The psychiatry department confirmed that fear and loss of sleep could explain some patients' symptoms. The second ramp was more robust under an independent load test.

林安要求調查員先驗證重播假設，不急著宣布時間悖論。即使同意這個選擇的人，也看得出代價。時間不多、資源有限，一次錯誤可能傳得很遠。紀任問是否該先隱瞞不確定性，等危機過後再說。林安搖頭。若資訊早被剪裁，人們便無法作出自由的選擇。 在倫理委員會批准試驗前，神經科醫師拒絕啟動未測試的原型機。 精神醫學部確認，恐懼與睡眠不足能解釋部分病患症狀。 第二座坡道在獨立載重測試中更堅固。

Later, under dim lights, Lin An reread the original question. The dispute was larger now, but its center remained unchanged: who had the right to make an irreversible choice for someone else? Ji Ren stayed until the last copy was sealed. Neither spoke about victory. They knew the next door would open only because today’s evidence had been preserved and today’s mistakes had been admitted. No sovereign authority could ignore the published accessibility requirements. A volunteer spokesperson read the revised warning in plain language, then invited residents to correct confusing phrases. Behind the children's wardrobe, staff found an unused cable channel leading to the relay maintenance shaft.

晚些時候，在微弱燈光下，林安重讀最初的問題。爭端已經擴大，但核心沒變：誰有權替別人作出不可逆的選擇？紀任留到最後一份副本封存。他們沒有談勝利，只知道下一扇門之所以能打開，是因為今天保住了證據，也承認了錯誤。 即使是最高主權機關也不能忽視已公布的無障礙要求。 一名志工發言人以白話朗讀修正警示，並邀請居民指出令人困惑的用語。 兒童衣櫃後面，員工發現一條未使用的電纜通道，通往中繼器維修井。
