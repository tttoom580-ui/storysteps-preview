The Clock That Ran Backward

時間旅行者的殺人不在場證明 · 第 2 / 25 章 · 5000–6000 · 英中對照 · 校訂中

It began not with a battle but with an ordinary interruption in the transit laboratory. A wall clock stopped at 18:42, but an independent mechanical timer shows the room lost eleven minutes. At first, no one considered the interruption important enough to preserve. Lin An thought otherwise. A forgotten detail could become a map, and a map could be used to control where frightened people were permitted to go. Every driver must abide by the bridge's posted weight limit. The director would not affirm the official opening date without seeing the original ledger. A child painted an alligator on the school safety mural, far from its mountain home. The music teacher played a quiet bass line during the waiting period.

事情不是從戰鬥開始，而是從時間轉移實驗室裡一次平凡的中斷開始。牆鐘停在十八點四十二分，獨立機械計時器卻顯示房間少了十一分鐘。起初沒人認為這件事值得留下紀錄，但林安想法不同。遭忽略的細節可能變成地圖，而地圖也可能被用來控制驚恐的人可以去哪裡。 每位駕駛都必須遵守橋梁公告的載重限制。 主任沒看過原始帳冊，不願確認官方啟用日期。 孩子在學校安全壁畫上畫了鱷魚，雖然牠不屬於高山生態。 等候時，音樂老師演奏一段低音旋律。

Ji Ren came back carrying a practical objection rather than an inspiring speech. Chronologist Ji Ren explains that the transit chamber moves bodies between years without rewriting the past. They had both seen people harmed when an assumption hardened into policy. A signed report could be wrong, and an emotional memory could be right, but neither became proof merely by being repeated. They agreed to seek a measurement from an independent observer. Other entries in the evidence ledger offered useful comparisons: The children's colloquial word for the forbidden street survived in a playground counting rhyme. Minor delays were commonplace, but a shaking bridge required special action. The hospital's exemption would expire at midnight, giving them only hours to examine the records.

紀任帶回的是實際的異議，而不是振奮人心的演說。時間學家紀任解釋，轉移艙能讓身體跨越年份，卻不能改寫過去。他們都見過假設硬化成政策後如何傷人。有簽名的報告可能是錯的，強烈的記憶也可能是真的；兩者都不會因為反覆提起就變成證據。他們決定找獨立觀察員再測量一次。證據帳冊裡還有可供比對的紀錄： 孩子口語中稱呼禁忌街道的詞，仍留在遊戲數數歌裡。 小幅誤點很常見，搖晃的橋卻需要特殊處理。 醫院的特別許可將在午夜到期，留給他們查閱紀錄的時間只剩數小時。

“We can tell the truth without pretending we know everything,” Lin An said. Ji Ren replied that a partial truth was difficult to defend against people who controlled the doors. She answered that a perfect story with no verifiable source was more dangerous. They divided the work: one would protect the witnesses, while the other checked the physical records and the sequence of events. A burned fuse had disabled the inspection trailer's light. The innkeeper offered hospitality to two stranded inspectors without asking them to alter their report in return. The school cook's infectious laughter eased the worried meeting.

「我們能說實話，不需要假裝知道一切。」林安說。紀任回答，當對方掌控所有門口時，局部真相比較難捍衛。她則認為沒有可驗證來源的完美故事更危險。他們分配工作：一人保護證人，另一人核查實體紀錄和事件時序。 一枚燒壞的保險絲使檢查拖車的燈失效。 旅店老闆招待兩名受困檢查員，並未要求他們以修改報告作為回報。 學校廚師富有感染力的笑聲緩和了焦慮的會議。

The argument moved from a private corridor into a formal room, where every statement would be copied. A senior official from the chronology board demanded certainty before granting access. Lin An offered a less comfortable bargain: a limited preservation order now, followed by independent examination and a chance for every accused person to respond. That was enough to prevent an irreversible decision. Other entries in the evidence ledger offered useful comparisons: The crew carried one liter of water for a controlled demonstration. A novice technician had left the storage sensor disconnected, accidentally preserving the original poster. It would be rash to send a full vehicle across before the load test. The accountant tried to reconcile the map dates with the road invoices.

爭論從私下走廊進入正式會議室，每句話都會留下副本。時間委員會的一名高級官員要求先確定真相才准查資料。林安提出比較不舒服的交換：先給有期限的保全命令，再獨立檢查，也讓所有被指控者有回應機會。這足以擋下一個不可逆的決定。證據帳冊裡還有可供比對的紀錄： 工作隊帶了一公升水進行受控示範。 一名新手技師曾讓儲藏室感測器保持斷線，意外保存了原始海報。 載重測試前就讓滿載救援車過橋太草率。 會計試圖核對地圖日期與道路工程發票。

Lin collects both clocks and prevents the laboratory owner from resetting them. Even those who agreed with the choice could see its cost. Time was running out, supplies were not unlimited, and a mistake might travel far beyond this room. Ji Ren asked whether they should conceal the uncertainty until the crisis passed. Lin An shook her head. People could not make free choices if the information reaching them had already been edited. The shabby ledger cabinet looked worthless, which was probably why nobody had removed it. A handheld spotlight showed the damaged joint under the bridge. The clinic offered apple syrup before sleep tests, a smell several former patients remembered distinctly.

林安蒐集兩只時鐘，阻止實驗室主人重新設定。即使同意這個選擇的人，也看得出代價。時間不多、資源有限，一次錯誤可能傳得很遠。紀任問是否該先隱瞞不確定性，等危機過後再說。林安搖頭。若資訊早被剪裁，人們便無法作出自由的選擇。 破舊的帳冊櫃看起來毫無價值，可能因此沒人搬走。 手持聚光燈照出橋下受損的接頭。 診所做睡眠測試前會提供蘋果糖漿，那股氣味讓好幾名前病患記得格外清楚。

Later, under dim lights, Lin An reread the original question. The dispute was larger now, but its center remained unchanged: who had the right to make an irreversible choice for someone else? Ji Ren stayed until the last copy was sealed. Neither spoke about victory. They knew the next door would open only because today’s evidence had been preserved and today’s mistakes had been admitted. A loaded truck could make the surface tilt dangerously. The artificial twilight outside the clinic concealed the fact that no real sun had reached the street. An illustrated memoir mentioned whiskey, but the crew remained sober on duty.

晚些時候，在微弱燈光下，林安重讀最初的問題。爭端已經擴大，但核心沒變：誰有權替別人作出不可逆的選擇？紀任留到最後一份副本封存。他們沒有談勝利，只知道下一扇門之所以能打開，是因為今天保住了證據，也承認了錯誤。 滿載貨車可能使路面危險地傾斜。 診所外的人造暮光掩蓋了真實太陽根本照不到街道的事實。 一本插畫回憶錄提到威士忌，工作人員值勤時仍保持清醒。
