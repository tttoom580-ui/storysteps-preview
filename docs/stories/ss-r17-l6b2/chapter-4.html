The morning had already acquired the look of an emergency before Lin An reached the scene. A rail terminal receives a passenger list naming three travelers who have not yet entered the city in official records. An official wanted a clean story, with a beginning that could be announced and an ending that would require no questions. Lin An had learned that reality rarely showed such courtesy. She made room for the people who had been ordered to wait outside. A nurse posted a guide about digital addiction beside the station's volunteer schedule. The clean aesthetic of the new galleries concealed the violence of their missing captions. The planner could give only an approximate count of attendees. An archaeology survey described a lost mountain settlement near the closed path.

在林安抵達現場前，早晨已經有了緊急事件的模樣。鐵路站收到乘客名單，列著三名官方紀錄顯示尚未入城的人。官員想要一套簡潔的故事，開頭能宣布，結尾又不用回答問題。她知道現實很少如此客氣，於是請那些被命令等在門外的人進來說話。 護理師把數位成癮的衛教指南貼在救援站志工班表旁。 新展廳整潔的美學風格，掩蓋了遭刪除說明所代表的暴力。 規劃者只能提供來賓人數的大約估計。 考古調查介紹封閉步道附近曾有一處消失的山中聚落。

Ji Ren examined the account twice, once as a friend and once as a witness who might have to defend every word. The list was printed on paper manufactured in the victim's own laboratory. The difference between what they suspected and what they could establish mattered. They placed the new fact beside the previous chapter’s unanswered question and looked for a second source that had not been copied from the first. Other entries in the evidence ledger offered useful comparisons: Lian wore a volunteer badge and insisted on speaking for himself. A small blaze in the previous winter had changed the school evacuation route. One young disciple of the state historian copied his claims without checking a single photograph.

紀任把紀錄看了兩次：第一次以朋友身分，第二次則像一位必須為每個字負責的證人。名單印在死者實驗室生產的紙上。懷疑和能確立的事實必須分清楚。他們把新線索放在前一章未解的問題旁，尋找不曾從同一份資料抄出的第二來源。證據帳冊裡還有可供比對的紀錄： 連安佩戴志工識別證，堅持親自發言。 前一個冬季的小火災改變了學校疏散路線。 一名國家史官的年輕門生照抄他的說法，連一張照片都沒核對。

A crowd gathered around the disputed notice. “You have to choose a side,” someone shouted. Lin An answered that she had chosen one already: the side of a claim that survived questions. Ji Ren did not smile, but moved the most vulnerable bystanders away from the growing argument. The pair could disagree privately without turning their disagreement into a public threat. A patient explanation could enlighten officials about everyday wheelchair use. There was no need to fuss over the paint color while the rail was loose. The map carried an implicit assumption that everyone could climb stairs.

人群圍住有爭議的公告。「妳必須選邊站！」有人喊。林安回答自己早已選擇：站在經得起提問的主張那一邊。紀任沒有微笑，只把最脆弱的旁觀者帶離爭執。他們可以私下不同意對方，卻不能把分歧變成公開威脅。 耐心的說明能讓官員理解輪椅使用者的日常處境。 扶手還沒固定時，沒必要為油漆顏色大驚小怪。 地圖隱含一個假設：人人都能爬樓梯。

The institution was not a single villain. It contained frightened clerks, dedicated workers, ambitious supervisors, and people who had learned to hide mistakes. the chronology board wanted the incident classified as routine. Lin An asked whose definition of routine was being used and whose losses were absent from the statistics. Nobody at the table could answer without consulting the sealed records. Other entries in the evidence ledger offered useful comparisons: The council asked a neutral chairperson to preside over the route hearing. The museum's prestige depended on public trust, not simply on royal-looking seals and elegant lighting. Some officials wanted to privatize the stopping bay without consulting residents. Cold white lights appeared to radiate purity, but their brightness made the erased faces harder to see.

這個機構不是單一惡棍。裡面有害怕的書記、盡責的工人、野心勃勃的主管，也有學會掩蓋錯誤的人。時間委員會想把事件歸類為例行事項。林安追問：是誰定義例行？又有誰的損失被統計漏掉？桌邊沒人能在不看封存紀錄的情況下回答。證據帳冊裡還有可供比對的紀錄： 議會請中立主席主持路線聽證。 博物館的聲望來自公眾信任，不是華麗印章與燈光。 部分官員想未諮詢居民就把停靠區私有化。 冷白燈光似乎散發純淨感，亮度卻讓被擦掉的面孔更難辨認。

Lin interviews the station workers separately before accepting the list as authentic. The decision did not repair everything. It altered one procedure, protected one person, or saved one piece of evidence; on a day when almost everyone demanded a miracle, that was worth recording. Lin An insisted that the result and its limits appear together in the official minutes. Anything less would turn a modest gain into tomorrow’s misleading legend. The lifeguard rotation had to cover every permitted training hour. A forgotten electric shaver was found in a locker during the safety sweep. A stray note beneath the glass included the route number of the missing passenger train.

林安先分別訪談站務員，再決定是否認定名單為真。這個決定沒有修好一切，只改變一道程序、保護一個人，或留下了一份證據；當幾乎所有人都要求奇蹟時，這仍值得記錄。林安堅持把成果和限制一起寫入正式紀錄。否則小小的進展又會變成明天誤導人的傳說。 救生員輪班必須涵蓋每個核准訓練時段。 安全清查時，在置物櫃找到一把被遺忘的電動刮鬍刀。 玻璃展示櫃下夾著一張零散紙條，上面寫著失蹤客車的路線編號。

The lights beyond the window did not offer comfort. They showed how many people were still waiting. Lin An understood that the investigation might eventually expose her own earlier errors, and she resolved not to erase them. Ji Ren suggested a short rest before the next hearing. She agreed, because exhaustion could damage judgment as thoroughly as deliberate deception. Official captions placed a veil of respectable language over the fact that whole families had vanished. A vineyard owner offered land for a safe pullout but required a written agreement. The manufacturer confirmed that the folding lift still carried a warranty.

窗外的燈光沒有帶來安慰，只照出還有多少人在等待。林安明白調查最終可能揭露自己過去的錯誤，決定不去抹除。紀任建議下一場聽證前短暫休息。她同意，因為疲憊和蓄意欺騙一樣可能損害判斷。 官方說明以體面措辭掩蓋了整個家庭失蹤的事實。 葡萄園主人願提供安全停車用地，但要求書面協議。 製造商確認折疊升降設備仍在保固期內。
