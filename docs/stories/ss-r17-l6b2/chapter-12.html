二一六○年的醫院

The Hospital in 2160

時間旅行者的殺人不在場證明 · 第 12 / 25 章 · 5000–6000 · 英中對照 · 校訂中

Lin An arrived with the unpleasant certainty that the simplest explanation was probably the one that had been prepared for public consumption. A legal jump lets Lin and Ji Ren visit the future hospital where Ari was born. The place was full of people who needed a quick answer, yet the evidence still belonged to those whose lives it could change. Instead of making a dramatic accusation, Lin An marked the hour, the witnesses, and the smallest observable detail in a paper notebook. An attendant kept the temporary shelter staffed while the path remained closed. The school cook mixed pancake batter for the delayed visitors. The old census counted hundreds of children absent from the city's approved founding population. A retired chemist helped explain why the old bridge paint was peeling.

林安抵達時，心裡很清楚：最簡單的解釋往往是事先準備好要給大眾聽的版本。一次合法跳躍讓林安和紀任來到亞里出生的未來醫院。現場的人都急需答案，但證據仍屬於那些生活可能被它改變的人。她沒有戲劇化地指控誰，而是先在紙本筆記上記下時刻、證人，以及最小卻可觀察的細節。 步道封閉期間，一名服務員持續留守臨時避難所。 學校廚師為滯留訪客調製煎餅麵糊。 舊人口普查列出數百名不在官方建城人口名單上的兒童。 退休化學師協助解釋舊橋油漆剝落的原因。

There was a moment of quiet when Ji Ren set the new material on the table. Staff confirm the certificate but discover that a donor's identity file was substituted during the birth registration. What mattered was not how surprising the discovery sounded, but whether anyone could repeat the test. Lin An copied the relevant names carefully. If one person later changed the account, the original would still be available for comparison. Other entries in the evidence ledger offered useful comparisons: Birds began to chirp beyond the temporary barriers. Only licensed nurses could dispense medication during the early voluntary memory trials. A tired engineer gave a quiet groan when the second clock revealed yet another inconsistent timestamp.

紀任把新資料放上桌時，四周安靜了一瞬。院方證實出生證明，卻發現當年的捐贈者身分檔被調包。重要的不是發現有多驚人，而是別人能不能重做同一項測試。林安仔細抄下相關姓名；如果日後有人更改說法，原件仍可供比對。證據帳冊裡還有可供比對的紀錄： 臨時圍欄外，鳥兒開始啁啾。 早期自願記憶試驗期間，只有持照護理師能配藥。 第二只時鐘又顯示矛盾時間戳時，疲倦的工程師輕輕呻吟。

“Will you trust me?” Ji Ren asked. “With the evidence, yes,” Lin An answered, “but not with a blank signature.” It was the sort of answer that could sound cold until someone noticed how carefully she had protected the other person’s right to disagree. Together they made a plan with a backup route, a deadline, and a record of who had accepted each responsibility. A children's encyclopedia described an iceberg drifting far from shore. The fire service gave invaluable advice on the safe collection point. A lavish charity dinner would not repair the damaged path.

「妳信任我嗎？」紀任問。「證據上可以，空白授權書不行。」林安回答。這聽起來很冷淡，直到人們注意到她也小心保護對方拒絕的權利。他們一起訂下有備用路線、期限，以及明確責任分工的計畫。 兒童百科描述一座漂離海岸的冰山。 消防單位對安全領書點提供極有價值的建議。 豪華慈善晚宴修不好受損步道。

A representative of the chronology board arrived with an offer to resolve the matter quietly. Quietly, in this case, meant without giving affected people access to the original. Lin An declined. She knew an office could be useful and still be dangerously wrong. Her objection was narrow and specific: the proposed decision relied on a document whose origin had not yet been independently verified. Other entries in the evidence ledger offered useful comparisons: The original clinic logo showed an open hand, unlike the Chronology Board's later symbol of a watchful eye. A loudspeaker ordered visitors to leave, but the judge's preservation warrant kept the examination team inside. A martial arts instructor offered a gentle mobility class in the shelter hall. No confirmed outbreak preceded the children's transfers, despite what the founding textbooks claimed.

時間委員會的代表前來，提議私下解決。所謂私下，就是不讓受影響的人看到原件。林安拒絕了。她知道一個機關可以有用，也可能犯下危險的錯。她的反對具體而有限：提議中的決定依賴一份來源還沒接受獨立驗證的文件。證據帳冊裡還有可供比對的紀錄： 原診所標誌是一隻張開的手，不同於時間委員會後來監視的眼睛符號。 擴音器命令訪客離開，但法官的保全令使檢查小組得以留下。 一名武術教練在避難所大廳提供溫和的活動課。 儘管建城教科書如此宣稱，兒童轉移之前沒有任何確診疫情爆發。

Lin obtains copies with patient consent and refuses access to unrelated medical histories. A few witnesses thanked Lin An; others accused her of doing too little. She accepted both reactions without offering a promise she could not keep. Ji Ren wrote down the complaints, including the ones directed at their own conduct. They were not building a monument to themselves. They were building a process that would still function after they left. A travel guide featured oyster farming along the distant coast. The early engineers never intended the technology to populate every home with compulsory monitors. The fallen tree was a setback, not a reason to abandon the service.

林安經病患同意取得副本，不查閱無關病史。幾名證人向林安道謝，另一些人指責她做得不夠。她接受兩種反應，不許下無法兌現的承諾。紀任寫下抱怨，包括針對他們自己行為的部分。他們不是為自己立紀念碑，而是建立自己離開後仍能運作的程序。 旅遊指南介紹遠方海岸的牡蠣養殖。 早期工程師從未打算讓強制監測環遍布每戶家庭。 倒木是挫折，不是放棄服務的理由。

By nightfall the newest report was signed by observers who were free to disagree. Lin An stood beside Ji Ren and looked at the distance between them and a verifiable explanation that does not punish someone for the year of birth. Progress felt less like triumph than like leaving a reliable mark on a difficult road. Someone would challenge that mark tomorrow. This time, there would be a public record with which to answer. Some residents remained skeptical of the proposed licensed detour. Residents could subscribe to the public warning bulletin without giving the operator access to private travel histories. The operator could not withhold public safety notices from residents.

夜幕降臨前，新的報告由可以提出異議的觀察者簽署。林安站在紀任身旁，看著他們與不因出生年份而定罪的可驗證真相之間的距離。進展不像勝利，比較像在艱難路上留下可靠的記號。明天有人會質疑，而這一次將有公開紀錄可以回答。 部分居民仍懷疑提議中的合格繞道路線。 居民可以訂閱公開警示通報，不必讓營運商取得私人旅行紀錄。 營運者不能向居民扣留公共安全通知。
