One Minute of Silence

The morning had already acquired the look of an emergency before Lin An reached the scene. A witness recalls the laboratory going silent exactly once while the power supply remained steady. An official wanted a clean story, with a beginning that could be announced and an ending that would require no questions. Lin An had learned that reality rarely showed such courtesy. She made room for the people who had been ordered to wait outside. A bulky equipment case would not fit through the narrow gate. Officials could deem a resident nonexistent by deleting an address, even while collecting that person's taxes. The elder called the city's memory policy a quiet dictatorship built from forms rather than soldiers. An unregistered delivery cart could glide along the lower rails without triggering street cameras.

在林安抵達現場前，早晨已經有了緊急事件的模樣。證人記得實驗室曾短暫一片寂靜，但電力供應始終穩定。官員想要一套簡潔的故事，開頭能宣布，結尾又不用回答問題。她知道現實很少如此客氣，於是請那些被命令等在門外的人進來說話。 一只笨重的裝備箱無法通過狹窄大門。 官員可以刪除地址就認定居民不存在，卻同時向那人收稅。 長者稱城市的記憶政策是一種靠表格而非士兵建立的無聲獨裁。 一輛未登記的送貨車能沿下層軌道滑行，不觸發街道攝影機。

Ji Ren examined the account twice, once as a friend and once as a witness who might have to defend every word. That minute matches a scheduled transit window that the facility claimed had been canceled. The difference between what they suspected and what they could establish mattered. They placed the new fact beside the previous chapter’s unanswered question and looked for a second source that had not been copied from the first. Other entries in the evidence ledger offered useful comparisons: Children sold small handicraft birds to purchase paper that city printers refused to supply them. An interpreter helped an overseas vessel captain explain the chart discrepancy without relying on uncertain gestures. The neighborhood's lesbian teacher said she feared losing her students, not the disclosure of her identity.

紀任把紀錄看了兩次：第一次以朋友身分，第二次則像一位必須為每個字負責的證人。那一分鐘符合一個設施聲稱已取消的轉移時段。懷疑和能確立的事實必須分清楚。他們把新線索放在前一章未解的問題旁，尋找不曾從同一份資料抄出的第二來源。證據帳冊裡還有可供比對的紀錄： 孩子們販售手工小鳥，換取城市印刷廠拒絕供應的紙張。 一名口譯員協助外國船長解釋海圖差異，不必依靠容易誤解的手勢。 下層社區的女同志教師說，她害怕的是失去學生，而不是身分被揭露。

A crowd gathered around the disputed notice. “You have to choose a side,” someone shouted. Lin An answered that she had chosen one already: the side of a claim that survived questions. Ji Ren did not smile, but moved the most vulnerable bystanders away from the growing argument. The pair could disagree privately without turning their disagreement into a public threat. For residents, liberation meant an address and protection from raids, not a speech from aboveground heroes. The council's lofty promises meant little without a funded staff roster. The survey crew needed to navigate between exposed rocks without confusing the proposed deeper channel with the existing route.

人群圍住有爭議的公告。「妳必須選邊站！」有人喊。林安回答自己早已選擇：站在經得起提問的主張那一邊。紀任沒有微笑，只把最脆弱的旁觀者帶離爭執。他們可以私下不同意對方，卻不能把分歧變成公開威脅。 對居民而言，解放是擁有地址與免於突襲的保障，不是地面英雄的演說。 沒有經費支撐的排班表，議會崇高承諾便毫無意義。 測量隊必須在露出的礁石間航行，不能把擬議深航道和現有航線混淆。

The institution was not a single villain. It contained frightened clerks, dedicated workers, ambitious supervisors, and people who had learned to hide mistakes. the chronology board wanted the incident classified as routine. Lin An asked whose definition of routine was being used and whose losses were absent from the statistics. Nobody at the table could answer without consulting the sealed records. Other entries in the evidence ledger offered useful comparisons: The committee prohibited overwork even when volunteers insisted they felt energetic. A pebble trapped under the wheel had caused the equipment cart to shake. The disciplinary decision remained pending until the evidence was reviewed. An abandoned plantation above the tunnels supplied seeds to gardens the city denied existed.

這個機構不是單一惡棍。裡面有害怕的書記、盡責的工人、野心勃勃的主管，也有學會掩蓋錯誤的人。時間委員會想把事件歸類為例行事項。林安追問：是誰定義例行？又有誰的損失被統計漏掉？桌邊沒人能在不看封存紀錄的情況下回答。證據帳冊裡還有可供比對的紀錄： 即使志工自認精神充沛，委員會仍禁止過度工作。 輪子下卡住的小石子讓裝備推車晃動。 處分決定在證據審查完畢前保持待決。 隧道上方一座廢棄種植園提供種子，供給城市否認存在的菜園。

Ji Ren tests the acoustic sensors while Lin protects the witness from public speculation. The decision did not repair everything. It altered one procedure, protected one person, or saved one piece of evidence; on a day when almost everyone demanded a miracle, that was worth recording. Lin An insisted that the result and its limits appear together in the official minutes. Anything less would turn a modest gain into tomorrow’s misleading legend. Farmers asked when they could plow the field beside the repair lane. No member of any royalty had visited the lower district, though official portraits hung in its old school. The threat of another correction pulse could spur residents to hide rather than testify.

紀任測試聲學感測器，林安則保護證人不受公眾猜測傷害。這個決定沒有修好一切，只改變一道程序、保護一個人，或留下了一份證據；當幾乎所有人都要求奇蹟時，這仍值得記錄。林安堅持把成果和限制一起寫入正式紀錄。否則小小的進展又會變成明天誤導人的傳說。 農民詢問何時能犁整維修車道旁的田地。 沒有任何王室成員來過下層社區，舊學校卻還掛著官方肖像。 新一輪修正脈衝的威脅，可能促使居民躲藏而不是作證。

The lights beyond the window did not offer comfort. They showed how many people were still waiting. Lin An understood that the investigation might eventually expose her own earlier errors, and she resolved not to erase them. Ji Ren suggested a short rest before the next hearing. She agreed, because exhaustion could damage judgment as thoroughly as deliberate deception. Lin An invited the student to stroll through the reopened collection. An old guide explained how mountain sunlight could tan exposed skin. The chairman called privacy a trifle; the students challenged him immediately.

窗外的燈光沒有帶來安慰，只照出還有多少人在等待。林安明白調查最終可能揭露自己過去的錯誤，決定不去抹除。紀任建議下一場聽證前短暫休息。她同意，因為疲憊和蓄意欺騙一樣可能損害判斷。 林安邀那名學生走進重新開放的藏書區散步。 舊指南說明山區日照如何曬黑外露皮膚。 主席把隱私說成小事，學生立刻提出反駁。
