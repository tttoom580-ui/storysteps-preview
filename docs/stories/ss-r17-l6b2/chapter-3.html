出生證明

The Birth Certificate

時間旅行者的殺人不在場證明 · 第 3 / 25 章 · 5000–6000 · 英中對照 · 校訂中

用互動版查詞、朗讀與收藏

Lin An arrived with the unpleasant certainty that the simplest explanation was probably the one that had been prepared for public consumption. The suspect Ari presents a genuine birth certificate issued decades after the murder. The place was full of people who needed a quick answer, yet the evidence still belonged to those whose lives it could change. Instead of making a dramatic accusation, Lin An marked the hour, the witnesses, and the smallest observable detail in a paper notebook. The cashier's handwritten signature on the toy receipt survived two rounds of official records cleaning. A routine checkup form showed that the children were healthy before the forced relocation. The vehicle pulled beside the curb rather than blocking the classroom entrance. The railway alarm could deafen passengers standing too close to the old tunnel speakers.

林安抵達時，心裡很清楚：最簡單的解釋往往是事先準備好要給大眾聽的版本。嫌疑人亞里出示真正的出生證明，簽發日期比命案晚了數十年。現場的人都急需答案，但證據仍屬於那些生活可能被它改變的人。她沒有戲劇化地指控誰，而是先在紙本筆記上記下時刻、證人，以及最小卻可觀察的細節。 玩具收據上收銀員的親筆簽名，熬過了兩輪官方檔案清理。 例行健康檢查表顯示，孩子們在強制搬遷前身體健康。 救援車停在路緣旁，沒有擋住教室入口。 鐵路警報足以震聾站在舊隧道喇叭旁的乘客。

There was a moment of quiet when Ji Ren set the new material on the table. His fingerprints nonetheless match traces left near the laboratory door. What mattered was not how surprising the discovery sounded, but whether anyone could repeat the test. Lin An copied the relevant names carefully. If one person later changed the account, the original would still be available for comparison. Other entries in the evidence ledger offered useful comparisons: The Ministry labeled the market vendor a disgrace for keeping receipts beyond the retention period. The crew documented the safe disposal of contaminated fuel instead of pouring it into the tidewater behind the station. A resident's dissent was recorded beside the proposed route change.

紀任把新資料放上桌時，四周安靜了一瞬。但他的指紋確實符合實驗室門口留下的痕跡。重要的不是發現有多驚人，而是別人能不能重做同一項測試。林安仔細抄下相關姓名；如果日後有人更改說法，原件仍可供比對。證據帳冊裡還有可供比對的紀錄： 部會把超過保存期限還留著收據的攤販視為恥辱。 船員記錄受污染燃料的安全處置，沒有把它倒進站後海水裡。 一名居民對路線變更的異議被記在旁邊。

“Will you trust me?” Ji Ren asked. “With the evidence, yes,” Lin An answered, “but not with a blank signature.” It was the sort of answer that could sound cold until someone noticed how carefully she had protected the other person’s right to disagree. Together they made a plan with a backup route, a deadline, and a record of who had accepted each responsibility. One family planned to emigrate, yet wanted station access until departure. The false relocation policy did nothing to enrich the children whose school materials were confiscated. A lengthy transport invoice listed train journeys that were absent from the official timetable.

「妳信任我嗎？」紀任問。「證據上可以，空白授權書不行。」林安回答。這聽起來很冷淡，直到人們注意到她也小心保護對方拒絕的權利。他們一起訂下有備用路線、期限，以及明確責任分工的計畫。 一家人打算移民，仍希望離開前能繼續申領急救手冊。 錯誤的搬遷政策沒有改善孩子生活，反而沒收了他們的學習用品。 一張冗長運輸發票列出官方時刻表上不存在的列車旅程。

A representative of the chronology board arrived with an offer to resolve the matter quietly. Quietly, in this case, meant without giving affected people access to the original. Lin An declined. She knew an office could be useful and still be dangerously wrong. Her objection was narrow and specific: the proposed decision relied on a document whose origin had not yet been independently verified. Other entries in the evidence ledger offered useful comparisons: For a lifelong resident, the changed light was more than a technical issue; it altered familiar night routes. The vendor said, 'Madam, you can copy this page, but the original stays with me.' A note of melancholy entered the room when the old evacuation was mentioned. The lawyer checked whether a private interview was permissible under the order.

時間委員會的代表前來，提議私下解決。所謂私下，就是不讓受影響的人看到原件。林安拒絕了。她知道一個機關可以有用，也可能犯下危險的錯。她的反對具體而有限：提議中的決定依賴一份來源還沒接受獨立驗證的文件。證據帳冊裡還有可供比對的紀錄： 對一名終生住在島上的居民而言，變動的燈光不只是技術問題，也改變熟悉的夜間路線。 攤販說：「女士，妳可以抄這一頁，但正本要留在我手裡。」 提到舊撤離事件時，屋裡浮現一絲憂傷。 律師查核依命令是否允許私下訪談。

Lin separates proof of presence from proof of killing and asks how the certificate reached the past. A few witnesses thanked Lin An; others accused her of doing too little. She accepted both reactions without offering a promise she could not keep. Ji Ren wrote down the complaints, including the ones directed at their own conduct. They were not building a monument to themselves. They were building a process that would still function after they left. The public speakers continued to preach obedience while officers confiscated the stalls' paper records. A faded timetable was reminiscent of the first vehicle that served the valley. Old rivalry between villages could not decide the new schedule.

林安把人在現場與是否殺人分開，追查出生證明如何到達過去。幾名證人向林安道謝，另一些人指責她做得不夠。她接受兩種反應，不許下無法兌現的承諾。紀任寫下抱怨，包括針對他們自己行為的部分。他們不是為自己立紀念碑，而是建立自己離開後仍能運作的程序。 警員沒收攤商紙本紀錄時，公共喇叭仍不斷宣講服從的重要。 褪色時刻表令人想起第一輛服務山谷的救援車。 村莊之間舊日的競爭不能決定新班表。

By nightfall the newest report was signed by observers who were free to disagree. Lin An stood beside Ji Ren and looked at the distance between them and a verifiable explanation that does not punish someone for the year of birth. Progress felt less like triumph than like leaving a reliable mark on a difficult road. Someone would challenge that mark tomorrow. This time, there would be a public record with which to answer. The suspicious transport account deserved scrutiny from an independent auditor, not an instant accusation. The waiting room was full of suspense before the safety officer spoke. A manufacturer's trademark on the battery pack helped identify its production batch.

夜幕降臨前，新的報告由可以提出異議的觀察者簽署。林安站在紀任身旁，看著他們與不因出生年份而定罪的可驗證真相之間的距離。進展不像勝利，比較像在艱難路上留下可靠的記號。明天有人會質疑，而這一次將有公開紀錄可以回答。 可疑的運輸帳戶需要獨立稽核員仔細審查，而不是立刻定罪。 安全官發言前，等候室充滿懸念。 電池組上的製造商商標協助辨認生產批次。
