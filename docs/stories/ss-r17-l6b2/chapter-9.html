Lin An arrived with the unpleasant certainty that the simplest explanation was probably the one that had been prepared for public consumption. A funeral invitation arrives for a person who is alive in the current year. The place was full of people who needed a quick answer, yet the evidence still belonged to those whose lives it could change. Instead of making a dramatic accusation, Lin An marked the hour, the witnesses, and the smallest observable detail in a paper notebook. The clinic offered a teenage visitor a guide explaining common causes of acne. Only licensed staff could administer the sleep medication, yet automated records named absent employees. The recall brochure explained refunds in plain language. No private diary should remain in captivity inside an institution's archive.

林安抵達時，心裡很清楚：最簡單的解釋往往是事先準備好要給大眾聽的版本。有人收到一張葬禮邀請，死者在當年其實還活著。現場的人都急需答案，但證據仍屬於那些生活可能被它改變的人。她沒有戲劇化地指控誰，而是先在紙本筆記上記下時刻、證人，以及最小卻可觀察的細節。 診所提供青少年訪客一份介紹青春痘常見成因的指南。 只有持照人員能施用安眠藥，系統紀錄卻列出當天缺席的員工。 召回手冊以簡明文字說明退款方法。 私人日記不該被機構檔案室永久囚禁。

There was a moment of quiet when Ji Ren set the new material on the table. The sender has used a commercial time courier to frighten witnesses into silence. What mattered was not how surprising the discovery sounded, but whether anyone could repeat the test. Lin An copied the relevant names carefully. If one person later changed the account, the original would still be available for comparison. Other entries in the evidence ledger offered useful comparisons: Officials tried to cram decades of different histories into one approved textbook chapter. The archivist would detach only the pages approved for copying. One swimmer described mild discomfort after the new bench was installed.

紀任把新資料放上桌時，四周安靜了一瞬。寄件者利用商業時間快遞恐嚇證人噤聲。重要的不是發現有多驚人，而是別人能不能重做同一項測試。林安仔細抄下相關姓名；如果日後有人更改說法，原件仍可供比對。證據帳冊裡還有可供比對的紀錄： 官員試圖把幾十年的不同歷史硬塞進一章核准教科書。 檔案員只會拆出獲准複印的頁面。 一名泳者描述新長椅安裝後造成輕微不適。

“Will you trust me?” Ji Ren asked. “With the evidence, yes,” Lin An answered, “but not with a blank signature.” It was the sort of answer that could sound cold until someone noticed how carefully she had protected the other person’s right to disagree. Together they made a plan with a backup route, a deadline, and a record of who had accepted each responsibility. The forthcoming parent meeting would examine school transfer records rather than children's private dreams. Soft lamps could illuminate the writing without damaging old ink. The school kept a small jade token donated by a parent from the missing eastern district.

「妳信任我嗎？」紀任問。「證據上可以，空白授權書不行。」林安回答。這聽起來很冷淡，直到人們注意到她也小心保護對方拒絕的權利。他們一起訂下有備用路線、期限，以及明確責任分工的計畫。 即將舉行的家長會將檢查學校轉學紀錄，而不是孩子的私人夢境。 柔和的燈能照亮文字而不傷害舊墨。 學校保存著一枚小玉牌，是失蹤東區一名家長捐贈的。

A representative of the chronology board arrived with an offer to resolve the matter quietly. Quietly, in this case, meant without giving affected people access to the original. Lin An declined. She knew an office could be useful and still be dangerously wrong. Her objection was narrow and specific: the proposed decision relied on a document whose origin had not yet been independently verified. Other entries in the evidence ledger offered useful comparisons: The children's joyous train drawings contrasted painfully with the city's official story of terror. The evening course connected family stories with practical literacy. The narrator of one memoir had deliberately hidden her own name. A child's nostril flared as she concentrated on her breathing before a first lesson.

時間委員會的代表前來，提議私下解決。所謂私下，就是不讓受影響的人看到原件。林安拒絕了。她知道一個機關可以有用，也可能犯下危險的錯。她的反對具體而有限：提議中的決定依賴一份來源還沒接受獨立驗證的文件。證據帳冊裡還有可供比對的紀錄： 孩子們快樂的列車畫作，與城市官方的恐怖敘述形成令人心痛的對比。 夜間課程把家族故事與實用識字能力結合。 一部回憶錄的敘述者刻意隱去自己的名字。 孩子第一次上課前專注調整呼吸，鼻孔微微張開。

Lin identifies the courier account and obtains a warrant without seizing unrelated private letters. A few witnesses thanked Lin An; others accused her of doing too little. She accepted both reactions without offering a promise she could not keep. Ji Ren wrote down the complaints, including the ones directed at their own conduct. They were not building a monument to themselves. They were building a process that would still function after they left. A map of the peninsula drew interest from children planning a school trip. The potent combination of censorship and repeated correction had distorted what many residents thought possible. The revival of local history did not require exposing private notebooks.

林安找出快遞帳戶，取得搜索令，卻不扣押無關私信。幾名證人向林安道謝，另一些人指責她做得不夠。她接受兩種反應，不許下無法兌現的承諾。紀任寫下抱怨，包括針對他們自己行為的部分。他們不是為自己立紀念碑，而是建立自己離開後仍能運作的程序。 半島地圖引起準備校外旅行的孩子興趣。 審查與反覆修正的強大組合，扭曲了許多居民對可能性的認知。 地方史的復興不必以公開私人筆記為代價。

By nightfall the newest report was signed by observers who were free to disagree. Lin An stood beside Ji Ren and looked at the distance between them and a verifiable explanation that does not punish someone for the year of birth. Progress felt less like triumph than like leaving a reliable mark on a difficult road. Someone would challenge that mark tomorrow. This time, there would be a public record with which to answer. The selective deletion of destination schools made ordinary transfers look like spontaneous disappearances. A stepchild asked whether family stories could include more than one household. The school aquarium held trout rescued from an abandoned study tank.

夜幕降臨前，新的報告由可以提出異議的觀察者簽署。林安站在紀任身旁，看著他們與不因出生年份而定罪的可驗證真相之間的距離。進展不像勝利，比較像在艱難路上留下可靠的記號。明天有人會質疑，而這一次將有公開紀錄可以回答。 選擇性刪除目的地學校，讓普通轉學看起來像突然失蹤。 一名繼子女問家族故事能否包含不只一個家庭。 學校水族箱養著從廢棄研究缸救出的鱒魚。
