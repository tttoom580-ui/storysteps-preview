The First Departure Ledger

時間旅行者的殺人不在場證明 · 第 5 / 25 章 · 5000–6000 · 英中對照 · 校訂中

It began not with a battle but with an ordinary interruption in the transit laboratory. Ji Ren finds a transit ledger describing each jump as a one-way voyage requiring a unique departure token. At first, no one considered the interruption important enough to preserve. Lin An thought otherwise. A forgotten detail could become a map, and a map could be used to control where frightened people were permitted to go. The vehicle needed a new battery accessory, not an expensive redesign. The archived report described a breakdown in the relay safeguards during the first citywide memory trial. Drivers could not bypass the mountain checkpoint for the demonstration. A photograph showed officials raising champagne glasses beside the first experimental sleep relay.

事情不是從戰鬥開始，而是從時間轉移實驗室裡一次平凡的中斷開始。紀任找到轉移帳冊，記載每次跳躍都是單程，且需獨立出發代幣。起初沒人認為這件事值得留下紀錄，但林安想法不同。遭忽略的細節可能變成地圖，而地圖也可能被用來控制驚恐的人可以去哪裡。 救援車需要新的電池配件，不是昂貴的全面改造。 封存報告描述第一次全城記憶測試時，中繼器的安全機制曾故障。 司機不能為示範行程繞過山區檢查站。 一張照片裡，官員在第一座實驗性睡眠中繼器旁舉起香檳杯。

Ji Ren came back carrying a practical objection rather than an inspiring speech. The ledger contains a token issued to the victim after the hour of his reported death. They had both seen people harmed when an assumption hardened into policy. A signed report could be wrong, and an emotional memory could be right, but neither became proof merely by being repeated. They agreed to seek a measurement from an independent observer. Other entries in the evidence ledger offered useful comparisons: The school sold roasted chestnut snacks to support a winter safety workshop. A resident called caution cowardly, but the ferry captain explained why a safe delay mattered more than bravado. The old vehicle photographs could evoke affection without proving financial viability.

紀任帶回的是實際的異議，而不是振奮人心的演說。帳冊裡有一枚代幣，發給死者的時間竟晚於公布的死亡時刻。他們都見過假設硬化成政策後如何傷人。有簽名的報告可能是錯的，強烈的記憶也可能是真的；兩者都不會因為反覆提起就變成證據。他們決定找獨立觀察員再測量一次。證據帳冊裡還有可供比對的紀錄： 學校販售烤栗子點心，為冬季安全工作坊籌款。 一名居民把謹慎說成懦弱，渡輪船長則解釋安全延誤為何比逞勇重要。 舊救援車照片能喚起感情，卻不能證明財務可行。

“We can tell the truth without pretending we know everything,” Lin An said. Ji Ren replied that a partial truth was difficult to defend against people who controlled the doors. She answered that a perfect story with no verifiable source was more dangerous. They divided the work: one would protect the witnesses, while the other checked the physical records and the sequence of events. Domestic fowl scattered when the supply vehicle entered the village courtyard. The receptionist's voice grew hoarse as she explained how many records were scheduled for destruction. The repair kit included lotion safe for handling rough synthetic material.

「我們能說實話，不需要假裝知道一切。」林安說。紀任回答，當對方掌控所有門口時，局部真相比較難捍衛。她則認為沒有可驗證來源的完美故事更危險。他們分配工作：一人保護證人，另一人核查實體紀錄和事件時序。 補給車開進村莊院子時，家禽四散跑開。 接待員解釋即將銷毀多少紀錄時，聲音變得沙啞。 維修包裡附有處理粗糙合成材料後可用的乳液。

The argument moved from a private corridor into a formal room, where every statement would be copied. A senior official from the chronology board demanded certainty before granting access. Lin An offered a less comfortable bargain: a limited preservation order now, followed by independent examination and a chance for every accused person to respond. That was enough to prevent an irreversible decision. Other entries in the evidence ledger offered useful comparisons: The records exposed a dangerous mentality: officials valued silence more than the health of residents. The monetary trail linked the contractor's expansion payments to an office outside normal public oversight. The moody sky warned the driver to shorten the return route. The nucleus of the proposal was a measurable minimum service for all villages.

爭論從私下走廊進入正式會議室，每句話都會留下副本。時間委員會的一名高級官員要求先確定真相才准查資料。林安提出比較不舒服的交換：先給有期限的保全命令，再獨立檢查，也讓所有被指控者有回應機會。這足以擋下一個不可逆的決定。證據帳冊裡還有可供比對的紀錄： 紀錄揭露一種危險心態：官員重視安靜勝過居民健康。 金錢流向把承包商的擴建款項連到一個不受一般公共監督的辦公室。 陰晴不定的天空提醒司機縮短回程路線。 提案的核心是為所有村莊提供可衡量的最低服務。

Lin orders an independent autopsy review instead of assuming the body is wrongly identified. Even those who agreed with the choice could see its cost. Time was running out, supplies were not unlimited, and a mistake might travel far beyond this room. Ji Ren asked whether they should conceal the uncertainty until the crisis passed. Lin An shook her head. People could not make free choices if the information reaching them had already been edited. A sharp rap at the metal door announced the safety inspector's arrival. A red slash across the engineer's recommendation marked the moment the council ignored the warning. An exhausted mechanic began to snore quietly during the break, having worked through the previous night's emergency checks.

林安要求獨立重驗遺體，不直接假設死者認錯。即使同意這個選擇的人，也看得出代價。時間不多、資源有限，一次錯誤可能傳得很遠。紀任問是否該先隱瞞不確定性，等危機過後再說。林安搖頭。若資訊早被剪裁，人們便無法作出自由的選擇。 金屬門上傳來急促敲擊聲，宣告安全檢查員抵達。 工程師建議上的紅色劃線，標記了議會無視警告的那一刻。 一名疲憊技師在休息時輕輕打鼾，因為前一晚他整夜處理緊急檢查。

Later, under dim lights, Lin An reread the original question. The dispute was larger now, but its center remained unchanged: who had the right to make an irreversible choice for someone else? Ji Ren stayed until the last copy was sealed. Neither spoke about victory. They knew the next door would open only because today’s evidence had been preserved and today’s mistakes had been admitted. Poor telecommunications could not be ignored in the service design. The chair agreed to unveil the contract terms before any final vote. An elderly boat captain defended the public path with surprising vigor, insisting that younger residents deserved safe access too.

晚些時候，在微弱燈光下，林安重讀最初的問題。爭端已經擴大，但核心沒變：誰有權替別人作出不可逆的選擇？紀任留到最後一份副本封存。他們沒有談勝利，只知道下一扇門之所以能打開，是因為今天保住了證據，也承認了錯誤。 服務設計不能忽略電信通訊不良的問題。 主席同意在最終表決前公開契約條款。 一名年長船長充滿活力地捍衛公共步道，堅稱年輕居民也應享有安全通行。
