尚未出生的男人

The Man Not Yet Born

時間旅行者的殺人不在場證明 · 第 1 / 25 章 · 5000–6000 · 英中對照 · 校訂中

The morning had already acquired the look of an emergency before Lin An reached the scene. Detective Lin An finds a scientist dead in a locked laboratory, while the suspect's legal birthdate lies twenty-three years in the future. An official wanted a clean story, with a beginning that could be announced and an ending that would require no questions. Lin An had learned that reality rarely showed such courtesy. She made room for the people who had been ordered to wait outside. Lin An checked the broken clasp on the changing-room gate before allowing any inspection. Readers asked the district office to convene an open meeting. A picture of a bear cub marked the children's shelf. The mobile dental clinic used the same village square.

在林安抵達現場前，早晨已經有了緊急事件的模樣。林安探員在上鎖實驗室發現一名科學家死亡，嫌疑人的法定出生日期竟在二十三年後。官員想要一套簡潔的故事，開頭能宣布，結尾又不用回答問題。她知道現實很少如此客氣，於是請那些被命令等在門外的人進來說話。 林安先檢查更衣室門上損壞的門扣，才允許進行查驗。 讀者要求區公所召開公開會議。 兒童資料架上貼著小熊幼獸的圖片。 巡迴牙科診所也使用同一座村莊廣場。

Ji Ren examined the account twice, once as a friend and once as a witness who might have to defend every word. Security cameras show the suspect entering the building before anyone in that year could have known his name. The difference between what they suspected and what they could establish mattered. They placed the new fact beside the previous chapter’s unanswered question and looked for a second source that had not been copied from the first. Other entries in the evidence ledger offered useful comparisons: The guild master behaved like a dictator when he threatened the tired porters. The closure directive offered no explanation for the uncounted emergency calls. A remote dwelling needed a way to contact rescuers without internet service.

紀任把紀錄看了兩次：第一次以朋友身分，第二次則像一位必須為每個字負責的證人。監視畫面顯示，嫌疑人在那一年還不可能被人認識時就進入大樓。懷疑和能確立的事實必須分清楚。他們把新線索放在前一章未解的問題旁，尋找不曾從同一份資料抄出的第二來源。證據帳冊裡還有可供比對的紀錄： 公會長威脅疲憊的搬運工時，行徑就像獨裁者。 接管指令沒有說明為何漏算部分緊急求援。 偏遠住所即使沒有網路，也需要聯絡救援人員的方法。

A crowd gathered around the disputed notice. “You have to choose a side,” someone shouted. Lin An answered that she had chosen one already: the side of a claim that survived questions. Ji Ren did not smile, but moved the most vulnerable bystanders away from the growing argument. The pair could disagree privately without turning their disagreement into a public threat. The shepherd's horse began to gallop across the pasture as clouds thickened. Repeated warnings about forbidden dreams were designed to heighten fear before the nightly correction cycle. The school janitor offered a dry room for storing emergency supplies.

人群圍住有爭議的公告。「妳必須選邊站！」有人喊。林安回答自己早已選擇：站在經得起提問的主張那一邊。紀任沒有微笑，只把最脆弱的旁觀者帶離爭執。他們可以私下不同意對方，卻不能把分歧變成公開威脅。 雲層變厚時，牧羊人的馬開始奔馳穿過牧場。 關於禁夢的反覆警告，是為了在每晚修正程序前加劇民眾恐懼。 學校清潔員提供乾燥房間存放緊急物資。

The institution was not a single villain. It contained frightened clerks, dedicated workers, ambitious supervisors, and people who had learned to hide mistakes. the chronology board wanted the incident classified as routine. Lin An asked whose definition of routine was being used and whose losses were absent from the statistics. Nobody at the table could answer without consulting the sealed records. Other entries in the evidence ledger offered useful comparisons: The old station motto said that every resident deserved a place. A pilgrim crossing the ridge asked where to report a missing traveler. No legal precedent allowed the Chronology Board to erase an engineer's private maintenance notes without notice. The agency promised citizens they would reap peace if they surrendered their dreams.

這個機構不是單一惡棍。裡面有害怕的書記、盡責的工人、野心勃勃的主管，也有學會掩蓋錯誤的人。時間委員會想把事件歸類為例行事項。林安追問：是誰定義例行？又有誰的損失被統計漏掉？桌邊沒人能在不看封存紀錄的情況下回答。證據帳冊裡還有可供比對的紀錄： 救援站舊座右銘寫著每位居民都值得有自己的位置。 一名越過山脊的朝聖者詢問該向何處通報旅人失聯。 沒有法律先例允許時間委員會在不通知的情況下，抹去工程師的私人維修筆記。 當局承諾人民，只要交出夢境，就能收穫和平。

Lin seals the clocks and refuses to call a date on paper a complete alibi. The decision did not repair everything. It altered one procedure, protected one person, or saved one piece of evidence; on a day when almost everyone demanded a miracle, that was worth recording. Lin An insisted that the result and its limits appear together in the official minutes. Anything less would turn a modest gain into tomorrow’s misleading legend. The mechanical clock remained stationary during the city's three-second power interruption, preserving a crucial discrepancy. Lin An An used the word 'correction' as a synonym for deletion whenever journalists asked difficult questions. The school planned to transplant two trees away from the turning area.

林安封存時鐘，不願把紙上的日期視為完整不在場證明。這個決定沒有修好一切，只改變一道程序、保護一個人，或留下了一份證據；當幾乎所有人都要求奇蹟時，這仍值得記錄。林安堅持把成果和限制一起寫入正式紀錄。否則小小的進展又會變成明天誤導人的傳說。 城市停電三秒時，機械鐘保持靜止，留下了一項關鍵的時間差異。 記者提出尖銳問題時，林安總把「修正」當成「刪除」的同義詞。 學校計畫把兩棵樹移植到迴車區以外。

The lights beyond the window did not offer comfort. They showed how many people were still waiting. Lin An understood that the investigation might eventually expose her own earlier errors, and she resolved not to erase them. Ji Ren suggested a short rest before the next hearing. She agreed, because exhaustion could damage judgment as thoroughly as deliberate deception. A city trustee had authorized the transfer of children's records to an unlisted storage facility. The Chronology Board demanded unconditional obedience, but its own equipment still required independent safety checks. She refused to utter a false confession just to keep her job.

窗外的燈光沒有帶來安慰，只照出還有多少人在等待。林安明白調查最終可能揭露自己過去的錯誤，決定不去抹除。紀任建議下一場聽證前短暫休息。她同意，因為疲憊和蓄意欺騙一樣可能損害判斷。 一名市府受託管理人曾批准把兒童紀錄轉往未列名的儲存設施。 時間委員會要求無條件服從，自己的設備卻仍需要獨立安全檢查。 她拒絕為了保住工作而說出虛假的認罪詞。
