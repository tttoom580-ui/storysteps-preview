The First Public Hearing

時間旅行者的殺人不在場證明 · 第 10 / 25 章 · 5000–6000 · 英中對照 · 校訂中

The morning had already acquired the look of an emergency before Lin An reached the scene. At the ten-day review the judge rules that future birth records alone neither convict nor clear the suspect. An official wanted a clean story, with a beginning that could be announced and an ending that would require no questions. Lin An had learned that reality rarely showed such courtesy. She made room for the people who had been ordered to wait outside. An old training film used an animate cartoon train to teach children the route beyond the western gate. Simple arithmetic showed that reported train repairs continued long after the official closure date. The students called the corrected graph awesome, then asked how it was made. The railway worker offered celery soup made from vegetables grown in a garden near the sealed gate.

在林安抵達現場前，早晨已經有了緊急事件的模樣。第十日審查時，法官裁定未來出生紀錄本身既不能定罪，也不能洗清嫌疑。官員想要一套簡潔的故事，開頭能宣布，結尾又不用回答問題。她知道現實很少如此客氣，於是請那些被命令等在門外的人進來說話。 一部舊教學影片用栩栩如生的卡通列車教孩子認識西門外路線。 簡單的算術就能看出，列車維修紀錄在官方封閉日期之後仍持續多年。 學生稱更正圖表很棒，接著追問它如何製作。 鐵路維修員拿出芹菜湯，食材種在封閉城門旁的菜園。

Ji Ren examined the account twice, once as a friend and once as a witness who might have to defend every word. The prosecution asks to ban all time travelers from testifying. The difference between what they suspected and what they could establish mattered. They placed the new fact beside the previous chapter’s unanswered question and looked for a second source that had not been copied from the first. Other entries in the evidence ledger offered useful comparisons: Fresh cement around the old gate contradicted claims that no one had maintained it for decades. A regional confederation of village councils demanded equal access to the records. A threatened crackdown on unauthorized radio listening prompted the inspector to protect the receiver by court order.

紀任把紀錄看了兩次：第一次以朋友身分，第二次則像一位必須為每個字負責的證人。檢方要求禁止所有時間旅人作證。懷疑和能確立的事實必須分清楚。他們把新線索放在前一章未解的問題旁，尋找不曾從同一份資料抄出的第二來源。證據帳冊裡還有可供比對的紀錄： 舊城門周圍的新水泥，與數十年無人維護的說法矛盾。 村議會聯盟要求平等取得紀錄。 針對未授權收音的鎮壓威脅，促使檢查員用法院命令保護接收器。

A crowd gathered around the disputed notice. “You have to choose a side,” someone shouted. Lin An answered that she had chosen one already: the side of a claim that survived questions. Ji Ren did not smile, but moved the most vulnerable bystanders away from the growing argument. The pair could disagree privately without turning their disagreement into a public threat. A child pointed to a crocodile on the station's animal poster. The old railway had a dual power system, allowing trains to operate even when city electricity failed. The city could not equalize every skill level, but it could make entry fairer.

人群圍住有爭議的公告。「妳必須選邊站！」有人喊。林安回答自己早已選擇：站在經得起提問的主張那一邊。紀任沒有微笑，只把最脆弱的旁觀者帶離爭執。他們可以私下不同意對方，卻不能把分歧變成公開威脅。 孩子指著救援站動物海報上的鱷魚。 舊鐵路有雙重電力系統，即使城市停電，列車仍能運行。 市府無法讓每個人的技能相同，卻能使入場機會更公平。

The institution was not a single villain. It contained frightened clerks, dedicated workers, ambitious supervisors, and people who had learned to hide mistakes. the chronology board wanted the incident classified as routine. Lin An asked whose definition of routine was being used and whose losses were absent from the statistics. Nobody at the table could answer without consulting the sealed records. Other entries in the evidence ledger offered useful comparisons: A council faction argued that opening the gate would endanger the city's fragile social order. An unsafe launch would incur costs beyond the initial recall. Residents called unequal access to safety information an injustice, especially when only wealthy boat owners received private warnings. The unexplained coastal signal deepened the intrigue surrounding the city's false founding history.

這個機構不是單一惡棍。裡面有害怕的書記、盡責的工人、野心勃勃的主管，也有學會掩蓋錯誤的人。時間委員會想把事件歸類為例行事項。林安追問：是誰定義例行？又有誰的損失被統計漏掉？桌邊沒人能在不看封存紀錄的情況下回答。證據帳冊裡還有可供比對的紀錄： 一個議會派系主張開城門會危及城市脆弱的社會秩序。 不安全上市會招致超過初次召回的成本。 居民認為安全資訊分配不均是一種不公，尤其只有富有船主能得到私人警示。 無法解釋的海岸訊號，讓城市虛假建城史背後的陰謀更加撲朔迷離。

Lin opposes the blanket ban and proposes verifying each travel history independently. The decision did not repair everything. It altered one procedure, protected one person, or saved one piece of evidence; on a day when almost everyone demanded a miracle, that was worth recording. Lin An insisted that the result and its limits appear together in the official minutes. Anything less would turn a modest gain into tomorrow’s misleading legend. A flashy app could lure officials away from the needs of paper-card residents. An obstinate councillor still demanded the raw spreadsheet. A pickpocket warning appeared on the vehicle stop information board.

林安反對一概禁止，主張逐一驗證旅程紀錄。這個決定沒有修好一切，只改變一道程序、保護一個人，或留下了一份證據；當幾乎所有人都要求奇蹟時，這仍值得記錄。林安堅持把成果和限制一起寫入正式紀錄。否則小小的進展又會變成明天誤導人的傳說。 華麗應用程式可能引誘官員忽略紙本求援急救手冊證居民。 一名固執議員仍堅持索取原始試算表。 扒手警示貼在公車站資訊板上。

The lights beyond the window did not offer comfort. They showed how many people were still waiting. Lin An understood that the investigation might eventually expose her own earlier errors, and she resolved not to erase them. Ji Ren suggested a short rest before the next hearing. She agreed, because exhaustion could damage judgment as thoroughly as deliberate deception. The next delivery could be punctual only if the dangerous bridge was avoided. The chair showed restraint by allowing critics to finish their comments. After twelve hours of testimony, the exhausted witness began to stagger toward the exit, and the chair called a break.

窗外的燈光沒有帶來安慰，只照出還有多少人在等待。林安明白調查最終可能揭露自己過去的錯誤，決定不去抹除。紀任建議下一場聽證前短暫休息。她同意，因為疲憊和蓄意欺騙一樣可能損害判斷。 只有避開危橋，下一次配送才有可能準時。 主席克制自己，讓批評者把話說完。 證人作證十二小時後，疲憊得往出口踉蹌，主席因此宣布休息。
