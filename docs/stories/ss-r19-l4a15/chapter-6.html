Yu qualified for a trophy after Han's family requested her results be frozen, but he refused to celebrate an unverified correction. Something had changed, but not everybody noticed it in the same order. Yu Cheng listened to the account of the nearest worker, then to someone standing farther away. Their differences were not necessarily lies. Each person had been given a different view and a different reason to be afraid of admitting uncertainty. The federation's seal looked official on the letter, but the attached calculation had no visible source. Yu Cheng asked for a reproducible result rather than a prestigious signature. A handwritten label read anniversary, but no one could say who had written it there. The instructions used the verb assure without naming who would carry the risk; Yu Cheng circled that gap.

韓霏家屬要求凍結成績後，余程取得獎盃資格，卻拒絕慶祝尚未查核的修正。某件事改變了，但人們發現它的順序不同。余程先聽近處工作人員的描述，再聽遠處旁觀者說法。差異不一定是謊言：每個人看到的角度不同，承認不確定時所要承擔的風險也不同。公文上的協會印章看來正式，附上的計算卻沒有來源。余程要的是可重現的結果，不是顯赫的簽名。手寫標籤寫著「周年紀念日」，卻沒有人知道是誰留下的。指示裡用了「保證」這個動詞，卻沒說誰承擔風險；余程圈出這項缺漏。

Han's posthumous rise was caused by a model still treating her body as training data, not by any real race. Yu Cheng refused to pretend the previous decision had solved everything. The practical result was still there, useful but narrow. For each thing the team knew, there were two things it had not measured. Yu Cheng drew a line through the claims that depended on speculation and asked Luo Qi to keep the original record intact. Luo Qi noticed the word campaign in a note that someone had added after the first inspection. An official memo described the plan as coarse, but Yu Cheng refused to treat an adjective as evidence. The instructions used the verb complicate without naming who would carry the risk; Yu Cheng circled that gap.

韓霏死後排名上升，是模型仍把她的身體視為訓練資料，不是她參加了比賽。余程不願假裝前一次決定已解決所有問題。成果確實存在，卻有明確界限。每個已知事項旁都還有未測量的部分。余程劃掉靠猜測成立的說法，請羅琪保留原始紀錄。羅琪注意到初次檢查後有人在筆記裡加上「活動」一詞。官方備忘錄形容計畫「粗糙的」，余程不願把形容詞當作證據。指示裡用了「使複雜化」這個動詞，卻沒說誰承擔風險；余程圈出這項缺漏。

A small disagreement changed the schedule. Yu Cheng wanted to proceed before evidence disappeared; Luo Qi wanted another check because a wrong conclusion might hurt people who could not easily defend themselves. They did not settle the matter by choosing a leader. They wrote down what a short, reversible test could actually establish. The term conductor appeared in the revised file; Yu Cheng checked its meaning against the original page. Luo Qi noticed the word cord in a note that someone had added after the first inspection. An official memo described the plan as courageous, but Yu Cheng refused to treat an adjective as evidence.

一場小爭執改變了時程。余程想趁證據消失前行動；羅琪要求再核對一次，因為錯誤結論可能傷害難以自辯的人。他們沒有靠選領袖解決爭議，而是列出短暫、可逆測試真正能證明什麼。修訂檔案出現「列車長」一詞，余程拿原始頁面核對它的意思。羅琪注意到初次檢查後有人在筆記裡加上「纜繩」一詞。官方備忘錄形容計畫「勇敢的」，余程不願把形容詞當作證據。

The federation threatened to suspend him for bringing the integrity of the sport into question. The plan had been amended three times, and each change left a trace. Yu Cheng valued those corrections more than a perfect-looking first draft. If the outcome proved disappointing, the group would still be able to identify which assumption needed repair. Luo Qi watched for the quiet participant who had not yet been invited to speak. Luo Qi repeated the instruction to devise, then asked whether anyone had the right to refuse. The summary called the situation diligent; Yu Cheng asked which observation justified that description. Luo Qi noticed the word dynasty in a note that someone had added after the first inspection.

協會威脅因他質疑競賽公正而停賽。計畫改了三次，每次修改都留下痕跡。余程認為這些修正比看似完美的初稿更有價值。就算結果不理想，團隊仍能知道該修正哪一項假設。羅琪也注意到那名一直沒有發言機會的人。羅琪重述「想出」的指示，再問當事人是否有權拒絕。摘要以「勤勉的」形容情況，余程追問這種說法有何觀察根據。羅琪注意到初次檢查後有人在筆記裡加上「世代；王朝」一詞。

An official version reached the room before the practical work was finished. Yu Cheng read it once, then compared it with what they had actually measured. The difference was not always malicious, but it could still hurt somebody. Luo Qi asked that the correction be written in language the affected people could understand. The federation's seal looked official on the letter, but the attached calculation had no visible source. Yu Cheng asked for a reproducible result rather than a prestigious signature. Luo Qi noticed the word evaluation in a note that someone had added after the first inspection. A handwritten label read foam, but no one could say who had written it there. The term frequency appeared in the revised file; Yu Cheng checked its meaning against the original page.

實際工作還沒完成，官方版本就傳到現場。余程讀了一次，再比對真正測得的結果。差異未必都是惡意，卻仍可能傷害人。羅琪要求把更正寫成受影響者看得懂的語言。公文上的協會印章看來正式，附上的計算卻沒有來源。余程要的是可重現的結果，不是顯赫的簽名。羅琪注意到初次檢查後有人在筆記裡加上「評估」一詞。手寫標籤寫著「泡沫」，卻沒有人知道是誰留下的。修訂檔案出現「頻率」一詞，余程拿原始頁面核對它的意思。

A witness asked whether the team was taking sides. Yu Cheng answered that refusing to invent a fact was not the same as refusing to care. What mattered was whether the procedure protected someone whose story was less convenient to tell. Luo Qi listened without interrupting and wrote down the question exactly as it had been asked. The term imitation appeared in the revised file; Yu Cheng checked its meaning against the original page. Luo Qi noticed the word labor in a note that someone had added after the first inspection. A handwritten label read mineral, but no one could say who had written it there.

一名見證人問團隊是否偏袒。余程回答，不捏造事實不代表冷漠。真正重要的是程序能否保護那個故事不方便被說出口的人。羅琪沒有打斷，逐字記下原問題。修訂檔案出現「模仿」一詞，余程拿原始頁面核對它的意思。羅琪注意到初次檢查後有人在筆記裡加上「體力勞動」一詞。手寫標籤寫著「礦物粉末中的礦物」，卻沒有人知道是誰留下的。

Even after the group agreed on the immediate step, another question remained. The answer would not arrive through a dramatic announcement. Someone would have to return to the place, compare the earlier notes, and accept the possibility that the next piece of evidence might spoil an attractive theory. Yu Cheng prepared to do exactly that. Luo Qi compared the sensor's raw timestamp with the official results. A predicted number was not a medal, no matter how brightly a sponsor displayed it. An official memo described the plan as needy, but Yu Cheng refused to treat an adjective as evidence. The report claimed psychology, but Yu Cheng wanted an example instead of a reassuring noun. On the next form, the action field read reflect, but the witness had never approved the operation.

即使大家同意眼前步驟，另一個問題仍在。答案不會靠戲劇性公告降臨；有人得返回現場、比對舊筆記，並接受新證據可能推翻漂亮理論。余程準備做的正是這件事。羅琪比對感測器原始時間與官方成績。無論贊助商展示得多亮眼，預測數字都不是獎牌。官方備忘錄形容計畫「需要幫助的」，余程不願把形容詞當作證據。報告聲稱有「心理學；心理」，余程要求具體例子，而不是安撫人的名詞。下一張表單把動作寫成「反射；反思」，見證人卻從未同意這項操作。
