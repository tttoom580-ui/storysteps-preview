Han's former coach withheld training notes, claiming they would hurt the family, while her parents demanded an independent review. Something had changed, but not everybody noticed it in the same order. Yu Cheng listened to the account of the nearest worker, then to someone standing farther away. Their differences were not necessarily lies. Each person had been given a different view and a different reason to be afraid of admitting uncertainty. The federation's seal looked official on the letter, but the attached calculation had no visible source. Yu Cheng asked for a reproducible result rather than a prestigious signature. Luo Qi noticed the word accomplish in a note that someone had added after the first inspection. A handwritten label read amuse, but no one could say who had written it there.

韓霏的前教練說資料會傷害家屬，拒絕交出訓練筆記，但父母要求獨立調查。某件事改變了，但人們發現它的順序不同。余程先聽近處工作人員的描述，再聽遠處旁觀者說法。差異不一定是謊言：每個人看到的角度不同，承認不確定時所要承擔的風險也不同。公文上的協會印章看來正式，附上的計算卻沒有來源。余程要的是可重現的結果，不是顯赫的簽名。羅琪注意到初次檢查後有人在筆記裡加上「成就；完成」一詞。手寫標籤寫著「逗樂；令人發笑」，卻沒有人知道是誰留下的。

The score jumped whenever the sensor passed an underground transmitter, proving at least part of the feed was manufactured. Yu Cheng refused to pretend the previous decision had solved everything. The practical result was still there, useful but narrow. For each thing the team knew, there were two things it had not measured. Yu Cheng drew a line through the claims that depended on speculation and asked Luo Qi to keep the original record intact. The report claimed approval, but Yu Cheng wanted an example instead of a reassuring noun. On the next form, the action field read bounce, but the witness had never approved the operation. An official memo described the plan as continual, but Yu Cheng refused to treat an adjective as evidence.

感測器經過地下發射器時分數就跳動，證明至少部分資料是人造的。余程不願假裝前一次決定已解決所有問題。成果確實存在，卻有明確界限。每個已知事項旁都還有未測量的部分。余程劃掉靠猜測成立的說法，請羅琪保留原始紀錄。報告聲稱有「核准」，余程要求具體例子，而不是安撫人的名詞。下一張表單把動作寫成「彈跳」，見證人卻從未同意這項操作。官方備忘錄形容計畫「持續的」，余程不願把形容詞當作證據。

A small disagreement changed the schedule. Yu Cheng wanted to proceed before evidence disappeared; Luo Qi wanted another check because a wrong conclusion might hurt people who could not easily defend themselves. They did not settle the matter by choosing a leader. They wrote down what a short, reversible test could actually establish. A handwritten label read disappoint, but no one could say who had written it there. The term earnest appeared in the revised file; Yu Cheng checked its meaning against the original page. Luo Qi pointed to the embassy in the inventory and asked why its location had changed.

一場小爭執改變了時程。余程想趁證據消失前行動；羅琪要求再核對一次，因為錯誤結論可能傷害難以自辯的人。他們沒有靠選領袖解決爭議，而是列出短暫、可逆測試真正能證明什麼。手寫標籤寫著「失望」，卻沒有人知道是誰留下的。修訂檔案出現「認真的」一詞，余程拿原始頁面核對它的意思。羅琪指向清單上的「大使館」，追問其位置為何改變。

A signed consent sheet showed Han had explicitly refused continuous monitoring after her last race. The plan had been amended three times, and each change left a trace. Yu Cheng valued those corrections more than a perfect-looking first draft. If the outcome proved disappointing, the group would still be able to identify which assumption needed repair. Luo Qi watched for the quiet participant who had not yet been invited to speak. Luo Qi noticed the word emphasis in a note that someone had added after the first inspection. The earlier photograph clearly showed a librarian; the newer version did not, so Yu Cheng kept both. A note about the margin lay beside the old register, with a date that Yu Cheng could not verify.

簽過名的同意書顯示韓霏在最後一場比賽後明確拒絕持續監測。計畫改了三次，每次修改都留下痕跡。余程認為這些修正比看似完美的初稿更有價值。就算結果不理想，團隊仍能知道該修正哪一項假設。羅琪也注意到那名一直沒有發言機會的人。羅琪注意到初次檢查後有人在筆記裡加上「重點」一詞。較早的照片清楚顯示一項「圖書管理員」，新版卻沒有，余程因此保留兩張。一張談到「頁邊」的便條放在舊登記簿旁，日期讓余程無法核實。

An official version reached the room before the practical work was finished. Yu Cheng read it once, then compared it with what they had actually measured. The difference was not always malicious, but it could still hurt somebody. Luo Qi asked that the correction be written in language the affected people could understand. The federation's seal looked official on the letter, but the attached calculation had no visible source. Yu Cheng asked for a reproducible result rather than a prestigious signature. The term murderer appeared in the revised file; Yu Cheng checked its meaning against the original page. Luo Qi noticed the word nonsense in a note that someone had added after the first inspection. The earlier photograph clearly showed a orbit; the newer version did not, so Yu Cheng kept both.

實際工作還沒完成，官方版本就傳到現場。余程讀了一次，再比對真正測得的結果。差異未必都是惡意，卻仍可能傷害人。羅琪要求把更正寫成受影響者看得懂的語言。公文上的協會印章看來正式，附上的計算卻沒有來源。余程要的是可重現的結果，不是顯赫的簽名。修訂檔案出現「殺人犯」一詞，余程拿原始頁面核對它的意思。羅琪注意到初次檢查後有人在筆記裡加上「荒謬的事」一詞。較早的照片清楚顯示一項「運行軌道」，新版卻沒有，余程因此保留兩張。

A witness asked whether the team was taking sides. Yu Cheng answered that refusing to invent a fact was not the same as refusing to care. What mattered was whether the procedure protected someone whose story was less convenient to tell. Luo Qi listened without interrupting and wrote down the question exactly as it had been asked. An official memo described the plan as passive, but Yu Cheng refused to treat an adjective as evidence. A note about the possession lay beside the old register, with a date that Yu Cheng could not verify. Luo Qi pointed to the researcher in the inventory and asked why its location had changed.

一名見證人問團隊是否偏袒。余程回答，不捏造事實不代表冷漠。真正重要的是程序能否保護那個故事不方便被說出口的人。羅琪沒有打斷，逐字記下原問題。官方備忘錄形容計畫「被動的」，余程不願把形容詞當作證據。一張談到「擁有權」的便條放在舊登記簿旁，日期讓余程無法核實。羅琪指向清單上的「研究員」，追問其位置為何改變。

Even after the group agreed on the immediate step, another question remained. The answer would not arrive through a dramatic announcement. Someone would have to return to the place, compare the earlier notes, and accept the possibility that the next piece of evidence might spoil an attractive theory. Yu Cheng prepared to do exactly that. Luo Qi compared the sensor's raw timestamp with the official results. A predicted number was not a medal, no matter how brightly a sponsor displayed it. Luo Qi noticed the word rhyme in a note that someone had added after the first inspection. A handwritten label read translator, but no one could say who had written it there. The summary called the situation urgent; Yu Cheng asked which observation justified that description.

即使大家同意眼前步驟，另一個問題仍在。答案不會靠戲劇性公告降臨；有人得返回現場、比對舊筆記，並接受新證據可能推翻漂亮理論。余程準備做的正是這件事。羅琪比對感測器原始時間與官方成績。無論贊助商展示得多亮眼，預測數字都不是獎牌。羅琪注意到初次檢查後有人在筆記裡加上「童謠」一詞。手寫標籤寫著「翻譯員」，卻沒有人知道是誰留下的。摘要以「緊急的」形容情況，余程追問這種說法有何觀察根據。
