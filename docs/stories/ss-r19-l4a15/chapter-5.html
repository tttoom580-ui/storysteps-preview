A Prediction Sold as Performance

Luo discovered the federation was mixing predicted future performance with completed results, without clearly separating the two columns. Something had changed, but not everybody noticed it in the same order. Yu Cheng listened to the account of the nearest worker, then to someone standing farther away. Their differences were not necessarily lies. Each person had been given a different view and a different reason to be afraid of admitting uncertainty. Luo Qi compared the sensor's raw timestamp with the official results. A predicted number was not a medal, no matter how brightly a sponsor displayed it. The instructions used the verb adopt without naming who would carry the risk; Yu Cheng circled that gap. Luo Qi noticed the word agency in a note that someone had added after the first inspection.

羅琪發現協會把未來預測和已完成成績混入同一欄，沒有明確區分。某件事改變了，但人們發現它的順序不同。余程先聽近處工作人員的描述，再聽遠處旁觀者說法。差異不一定是謊言：每個人看到的角度不同，承認不確定時所要承擔的風險也不同。羅琪比對感測器原始時間與官方成績。無論贊助商展示得多亮眼，預測數字都不是獎牌。指示裡用了「採用」這個動詞，卻沒說誰承擔風險；余程圈出這項缺漏。羅琪注意到初次檢查後有人在筆記裡加上「機構」一詞。

A signed consent sheet showed Han had explicitly refused continuous monitoring after her last race. Yu Cheng refused to pretend the previous decision had solved everything. The practical result was still there, useful but narrow. For each thing the team knew, there were two things it had not measured. Yu Cheng drew a line through the claims that depended on speculation and asked Luo Qi to keep the original record intact. People disagreed about behavior; Yu Cheng separated the feeling from the facts they could test. The instructions used the verb cherish without naming who would carry the risk; Yu Cheng circled that gap. Luo Qi noticed the word congress in a note that someone had added after the first inspection.

簽過名的同意書顯示韓霏在最後一場比賽後明確拒絕持續監測。余程不願假裝前一次決定已解決所有問題。成果確實存在，卻有明確界限。每個已知事項旁都還有未測量的部分。余程劃掉靠猜測成立的說法，請羅琪保留原始紀錄。大家對「行為」意見不同，余程把感受與可檢驗的事實分開。指示裡用了「珍惜」這個動詞，卻沒說誰承擔風險；余程圈出這項缺漏。羅琪注意到初次檢查後有人在筆記裡加上「議會」一詞。

A small disagreement changed the schedule. Yu Cheng wanted to proceed before evidence disappeared; Luo Qi wanted another check because a wrong conclusion might hurt people who could not easily defend themselves. They did not settle the matter by choosing a leader. They wrote down what a short, reversible test could actually establish. Luo Qi noticed the word conscience in a note that someone had added after the first inspection. A handwritten label read decoration, but no one could say who had written it there. The term discourage appeared in the revised file; Yu Cheng checked its meaning against the original page.

一場小爭執改變了時程。余程想趁證據消失前行動；羅琪要求再核對一次，因為錯誤結論可能傷害難以自辯的人。他們沒有靠選領袖解決爭議，而是列出短暫、可逆測試真正能證明什麼。羅琪注意到初次檢查後有人在筆記裡加上「良心」一詞。手寫標籤寫著「裝飾」，卻沒有人知道是誰留下的。修訂檔案出現「使卻步」一詞，余程拿原始頁面核對它的意思。

Han's posthumous rise was caused by a model still treating her body as training data, not by any real race. The plan had been amended three times, and each change left a trace. Yu Cheng valued those corrections more than a perfect-looking first draft. If the outcome proved disappointing, the group would still be able to identify which assumption needed repair. Luo Qi watched for the quiet participant who had not yet been invited to speak. The term fundamental appeared in the revised file; Yu Cheng checked its meaning against the original page. On the next form, the action field read horrify, but the witness had never approved the operation. An official memo described the plan as influential, but Yu Cheng refused to treat an adjective as evidence.

韓霏死後排名上升，是模型仍把她的身體視為訓練資料，不是她參加了比賽。計畫改了三次，每次修改都留下痕跡。余程認為這些修正比看似完美的初稿更有價值。就算結果不理想，團隊仍能知道該修正哪一項假設。羅琪也注意到那名一直沒有發言機會的人。修訂檔案出現「根本的；基本的」一詞，余程拿原始頁面核對它的意思。下一張表單把動作寫成「使驚恐」，見證人卻從未同意這項操作。官方備忘錄形容計畫「有影響力的」，余程不願把形容詞當作證據。

An official version reached the room before the practical work was finished. Yu Cheng read it once, then compared it with what they had actually measured. The difference was not always malicious, but it could still hurt somebody. Luo Qi asked that the correction be written in language the affected people could understand. Luo Qi compared the sensor's raw timestamp with the official results. A predicted number was not a medal, no matter how brightly a sponsor displayed it. A handwritten label read interaction, but no one could say who had written it there. The report claimed outcome, but Yu Cheng wanted an example instead of a reassuring noun. One witness used the word primitive; Luo Qi noted that a description was not a measurement.

實際工作還沒完成，官方版本就傳到現場。余程讀了一次，再比對真正測得的結果。差異未必都是惡意，卻仍可能傷害人。羅琪要求把更正寫成受影響者看得懂的語言。羅琪比對感測器原始時間與官方成績。無論贊助商展示得多亮眼，預測數字都不是獎牌。手寫標籤寫著「相互作用」，卻沒有人知道是誰留下的。報告聲稱有「結果」，余程要求具體例子，而不是安撫人的名詞。一位證人用了「古早的」一詞，羅琪指出形容並不等於測量。

A witness asked whether the team was taking sides. Yu Cheng answered that refusing to invent a fact was not the same as refusing to care. What mattered was whether the procedure protected someone whose story was less convenient to tell. Luo Qi listened without interrupting and wrote down the question exactly as it had been asked. Luo Qi noticed the word reception in a note that someone had added after the first inspection. A handwritten label read robber, but no one could say who had written it there. The term scenery appeared in the revised file; Yu Cheng checked its meaning against the original page.

一名見證人問團隊是否偏袒。余程回答，不捏造事實不代表冷漠。真正重要的是程序能否保護那個故事不方便被說出口的人。羅琪沒有打斷，逐字記下原問題。羅琪注意到初次檢查後有人在筆記裡加上「接待會」一詞。手寫標籤寫著「強盜」，卻沒有人知道是誰留下的。修訂檔案出現「舞臺布景」一詞，余程拿原始頁面核對它的意思。

Even after the group agreed on the immediate step, another question remained. The answer would not arrive through a dramatic announcement. Someone would have to return to the place, compare the earlier notes, and accept the possibility that the next piece of evidence might spoil an attractive theory. Yu Cheng prepared to do exactly that. The federation's seal looked official on the letter, but the attached calculation had no visible source. Yu Cheng asked for a reproducible result rather than a prestigious signature. The summary called the situation shameful; Yu Cheng asked which observation justified that description. Luo Qi noticed the word terror in a note that someone had added after the first inspection. A handwritten label read witch, but no one could say who had written it there.

即使大家同意眼前步驟，另一個問題仍在。答案不會靠戲劇性公告降臨；有人得返回現場、比對舊筆記，並接受新證據可能推翻漂亮理論。余程準備做的正是這件事。公文上的協會印章看來正式，附上的計算卻沒有來源。余程要的是可重現的結果，不是顯赫的簽名。摘要以「可恥的」形容情況，余程追問這種說法有何觀察根據。羅琪注意到初次檢查後有人在筆記裡加上「恐懼」一詞。手寫標籤寫著「巫師」，卻沒有人知道是誰留下的。
