Yu visited a remote stadium that supposedly hosted a midnight championship. Its track was flooded and its gate had been chained for weeks. Something had changed, but not everybody noticed it in the same order. Yu Cheng listened to the account of the nearest worker, then to someone standing farther away. Their differences were not necessarily lies. Each person had been given a different view and a different reason to be afraid of admitting uncertainty. Luo Qi compared the sensor's raw timestamp with the official results. A predicted number was not a medal, no matter how brightly a sponsor displayed it. Luo Qi repeated the instruction to arise, then asked whether anyone had the right to refuse. The term association appeared in the revised file; Yu Cheng checked its meaning against the original page.

余程走訪據稱辦過午夜冠軍賽的偏遠體育場，發現跑道淹水、鐵門已上鎖數週。某件事改變了，但人們發現它的順序不同。余程先聽近處工作人員的描述，再聽遠處旁觀者說法。差異不一定是謊言：每個人看到的角度不同，承認不確定時所要承擔的風險也不同。羅琪比對感測器原始時間與官方成績。無論贊助商展示得多亮眼，預測數字都不是獎牌。羅琪重述「產生」的指示，再問當事人是否有權拒絕。修訂檔案出現「協會」一詞，余程拿原始頁面核對它的意思。

The contract explained the pressure but did not establish who had altered Han Fei's feed after her death. Yu Cheng refused to pretend the previous decision had solved everything. The practical result was still there, useful but narrow. For each thing the team knew, there were two things it had not measured. Yu Cheng drew a line through the claims that depended on speculation and asked Luo Qi to keep the original record intact. Luo Qi noticed the word autobiography in a note that someone had added after the first inspection. An official memo described the plan as bald, but Yu Cheng refused to treat an adjective as evidence. The summary called the situation consequent; Yu Cheng asked which observation justified that description.

合約解釋壓力，卻尚未證明誰在韓霏死後修改資料。余程不願假裝前一次決定已解決所有問題。成果確實存在，卻有明確界限。每個已知事項旁都還有未測量的部分。余程劃掉靠猜測成立的說法，請羅琪保留原始紀錄。羅琪注意到初次檢查後有人在筆記裡加上「自傳」一詞。官方備忘錄形容計畫「光頭的」，余程不願把形容詞當作證據。摘要以「隨之而來的」形容情況，余程追問這種說法有何觀察根據。

A small disagreement changed the schedule. Yu Cheng wanted to proceed before evidence disappeared; Luo Qi wanted another check because a wrong conclusion might hurt people who could not easily defend themselves. They did not settle the matter by choosing a leader. They wrote down what a short, reversible test could actually establish. The term curiosity appeared in the revised file; Yu Cheng checked its meaning against the original page. On the next form, the action field read declare, but the witness had never approved the operation. A handwritten label read dependent, but no one could say who had written it there.

一場小爭執改變了時程。余程想趁證據消失前行動；羅琪要求再核對一次，因為錯誤結論可能傷害難以自辯的人。他們沒有靠選領袖解決爭議，而是列出短暫、可逆測試真正能證明什麼。修訂檔案出現「好奇心」一詞，余程拿原始頁面核對它的意思。下一張表單把動作寫成「宣布」，見證人卻從未同意這項操作。手寫標籤寫著「依賴的」，卻沒有人知道是誰留下的。

A security worker had kept the original gate log despite an order to discard it. The plan had been amended three times, and each change left a trace. Yu Cheng valued those corrections more than a perfect-looking first draft. If the outcome proved disappointing, the group would still be able to identify which assumption needed repair. Luo Qi watched for the quiet participant who had not yet been invited to speak. The earlier photograph clearly showed a fireplace; the newer version did not, so Yu Cheng kept both. The term forecast appeared in the revised file; Yu Cheng checked its meaning against the original page. On the next form, the action field read halt, but the witness had never approved the operation.

保全員不理會丟棄命令，保留了原始出入紀錄。計畫改了三次，每次修改都留下痕跡。余程認為這些修正比看似完美的初稿更有價值。就算結果不理想，團隊仍能知道該修正哪一項假設。羅琪也注意到那名一直沒有發言機會的人。較早的照片清楚顯示一項「壁爐」，新版卻沒有，余程因此保留兩張。修訂檔案出現「預測」一詞，余程拿原始頁面核對它的意思。下一張表單把動作寫成「停止」，見證人卻從未同意這項操作。

An official version reached the room before the practical work was finished. Yu Cheng read it once, then compared it with what they had actually measured. The difference was not always malicious, but it could still hurt somebody. Luo Qi asked that the correction be written in language the affected people could understand. Luo Qi compared the sensor's raw timestamp with the official results. A predicted number was not a medal, no matter how brightly a sponsor displayed it. Luo Qi pointed to the lobster in the inventory and asked why its location had changed. The earlier photograph clearly showed a machinery; the newer version did not, so Yu Cheng kept both. The term minister appeared in the revised file; Yu Cheng checked its meaning against the original page.

實際工作還沒完成，官方版本就傳到現場。余程讀了一次，再比對真正測得的結果。差異未必都是惡意，卻仍可能傷害人。羅琪要求把更正寫成受影響者看得懂的語言。羅琪比對感測器原始時間與官方成績。無論贊助商展示得多亮眼，預測數字都不是獎牌。羅琪指向清單上的「龍蝦」，追問其位置為何改變。較早的照片清楚顯示一項「機械設備」，新版卻沒有，余程因此保留兩張。修訂檔案出現「部長」一詞，余程拿原始頁面核對它的意思。

A witness asked whether the team was taking sides. Yu Cheng answered that refusing to invent a fact was not the same as refusing to care. What mattered was whether the procedure protected someone whose story was less convenient to tell. Luo Qi listened without interrupting and wrote down the question exactly as it had been asked. A note about the pasta lay beside the old register, with a date that Yu Cheng could not verify. Luo Qi noticed the word quotation in a note that someone had added after the first inspection. Luo Qi repeated the instruction to restore, then asked whether anyone had the right to refuse.

一名見證人問團隊是否偏袒。余程回答，不捏造事實不代表冷漠。真正重要的是程序能否保護那個故事不方便被說出口的人。羅琪沒有打斷，逐字記下原問題。一張談到「義大利麵」的便條放在舊登記簿旁，日期讓余程無法核實。羅琪注意到初次檢查後有人在筆記裡加上「報價單」一詞。羅琪重述「恢復」的指示，再問當事人是否有權拒絕。

Even after the group agreed on the immediate step, another question remained. The answer would not arrive through a dramatic announcement. Someone would have to return to the place, compare the earlier notes, and accept the possibility that the next piece of evidence might spoil an attractive theory. Yu Cheng prepared to do exactly that. The federation's seal looked official on the letter, but the attached calculation had no visible source. Yu Cheng asked for a reproducible result rather than a prestigious signature. An official memo described the plan as rusty, but Yu Cheng refused to treat an adjective as evidence. The term vacancy appeared in the revised file; Yu Cheng checked its meaning against the original page. On the next form, the action field read violate, but the witness had never approved the operation.

即使大家同意眼前步驟，另一個問題仍在。答案不會靠戲劇性公告降臨；有人得返回現場、比對舊筆記，並接受新證據可能推翻漂亮理論。余程準備做的正是這件事。公文上的協會印章看來正式，附上的計算卻沒有來源。余程要的是可重現的結果，不是顯赫的簽名。官方備忘錄形容計畫「生鏽的」，余程不願把形容詞當作證據。修訂檔案出現「職缺」一詞，余程拿原始頁面核對它的意思。下一張表單把動作寫成「違反」，見證人卻從未同意這項操作。
