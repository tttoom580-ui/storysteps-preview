During a live demonstration, another inactive athlete's record overtook Han's and then vanished when Luo cut the test transmitter. Something had changed, but not everybody noticed it in the same order. Yu Cheng listened to the account of the nearest worker, then to someone standing farther away. Their differences were not necessarily lies. Each person had been given a different view and a different reason to be afraid of admitting uncertainty. The federation's seal looked official on the letter, but the attached calculation had no visible source. Yu Cheng asked for a reproducible result rather than a prestigious signature. Luo Qi wrote acceptance in the margin and asked how the team would recognize it in practice. An official memo described the plan as ambitious, but Yu Cheng refused to treat an adjective as evidence.

現場示範時，另一位未參賽選手的紀錄超越韓霏，羅琪切斷測試發射器後才消失。某件事改變了，但人們發現它的順序不同。余程先聽近處工作人員的描述，再聽遠處旁觀者說法。差異不一定是謊言：每個人看到的角度不同，承認不確定時所要承擔的風險也不同。公文上的協會印章看來正式，附上的計算卻沒有來源。余程要的是可重現的結果，不是顯赫的簽名。羅琪在頁邊寫下「接受；同意」，追問團隊實際上如何辨認。官方備忘錄形容計畫「有企圖心的」，余程不願把形容詞當作證據。

A security worker had kept the original gate log despite an order to discard it. Yu Cheng refused to pretend the previous decision had solved everything. The practical result was still there, useful but narrow. For each thing the team knew, there were two things it had not measured. Yu Cheng drew a line through the claims that depended on speculation and asked Luo Qi to keep the original record intact. The term atmosphere appeared in the revised file; Yu Cheng checked its meaning against the original page. One witness used the word authentic; Luo Qi noted that a description was not a measurement. Luo Qi repeated the instruction to classify, then asked whether anyone had the right to refuse.

保全員不理會丟棄命令，保留了原始出入紀錄。余程不願假裝前一次決定已解決所有問題。成果確實存在，卻有明確界限。每個已知事項旁都還有未測量的部分。余程劃掉靠猜測成立的說法，請羅琪保留原始紀錄。修訂檔案出現「氣氛」一詞，余程拿原始頁面核對它的意思。一位證人用了「真實可靠的」一詞，羅琪指出形容並不等於測量。羅琪重述「分類」的指示，再問當事人是否有權拒絕。

A small disagreement changed the schedule. Yu Cheng wanted to proceed before evidence disappeared; Luo Qi wanted another check because a wrong conclusion might hurt people who could not easily defend themselves. They did not settle the matter by choosing a leader. They wrote down what a short, reversible test could actually establish. Luo Qi repeated the instruction to differ, then asked whether anyone had the right to refuse. The report claimed dignity, but Yu Cheng wanted an example instead of a reassuring noun. Luo Qi noticed the word embarrass in a note that someone had added after the first inspection.

一場小爭執改變了時程。余程想趁證據消失前行動；羅琪要求再核對一次，因為錯誤結論可能傷害難以自辯的人。他們沒有靠選領袖解決爭議，而是列出短暫、可逆測試真正能證明什麼。羅琪重述「有所不同」的指示，再問當事人是否有權拒絕。報告聲稱有「尊嚴」，余程要求具體例子，而不是安撫人的名詞。羅琪注意到初次檢查後有人在筆記裡加上「使人難堪」一詞。

The crowd saw that the ranking system could create a champion without an athlete, and the tournament was paused. The plan had been amended three times, and each change left a trace. Yu Cheng valued those corrections more than a perfect-looking first draft. If the outcome proved disappointing, the group would still be able to identify which assumption needed repair. Luo Qi watched for the quiet participant who had not yet been invited to speak. Luo Qi noticed the word facility in a note that someone had added after the first inspection. An official memo described the plan as genuine, but Yu Cheng refused to treat an adjective as evidence. The summary called the situation offensive; Yu Cheng asked which observation justified that description.

群眾看見系統能憑空創造冠軍，賽會因此暫停。計畫改了三次，每次修改都留下痕跡。余程認為這些修正比看似完美的初稿更有價值。就算結果不理想，團隊仍能知道該修正哪一項假設。羅琪也注意到那名一直沒有發言機會的人。羅琪注意到初次檢查後有人在筆記裡加上「設施」一詞。官方備忘錄形容計畫「真實的」，余程不願把形容詞當作證據。摘要以「惡意的」形容情況，余程追問這種說法有何觀察根據。

An official version reached the room before the practical work was finished. Yu Cheng read it once, then compared it with what they had actually measured. The difference was not always malicious, but it could still hurt somebody. Luo Qi asked that the correction be written in language the affected people could understand. The federation's seal looked official on the letter, but the attached calculation had no visible source. Yu Cheng asked for a reproducible result rather than a prestigious signature. The term option appeared in the revised file; Yu Cheng checked its meaning against the original page. Luo Qi noticed the word perfume in a note that someone had added after the first inspection. A handwritten label read promotion, but no one could say who had written it there.

實際工作還沒完成，官方版本就傳到現場。余程讀了一次，再比對真正測得的結果。差異未必都是惡意，卻仍可能傷害人。羅琪要求把更正寫成受影響者看得懂的語言。公文上的協會印章看來正式，附上的計算卻沒有來源。余程要的是可重現的結果，不是顯赫的簽名。修訂檔案出現「選項」一詞，余程拿原始頁面核對它的意思。羅琪注意到初次檢查後有人在筆記裡加上「香水」一詞。手寫標籤寫著「行銷」，卻沒有人知道是誰留下的。

A witness asked whether the team was taking sides. Yu Cheng answered that refusing to invent a fact was not the same as refusing to care. What mattered was whether the procedure protected someone whose story was less convenient to tell. Luo Qi listened without interrupting and wrote down the question exactly as it had been asked. A handwritten label read publisher, but no one could say who had written it there. The term refugee appeared in the revised file; Yu Cheng checked its meaning against the original page. Luo Qi noticed the word rescue in a note that someone had added after the first inspection.

一名見證人問團隊是否偏袒。余程回答，不捏造事實不代表冷漠。真正重要的是程序能否保護那個故事不方便被說出口的人。羅琪沒有打斷，逐字記下原問題。手寫標籤寫著「出版社」，卻沒有人知道是誰留下的。修訂檔案出現「難民」一詞，余程拿原始頁面核對它的意思。羅琪注意到初次檢查後有人在筆記裡加上「救援」一詞。

The demonstration ended with a formal resignation from the ranking committee's only independent adviser. She left behind a damaged stocking that had belonged to Han Fei, found among equipment marked for disposal, and refused to treat it as a publicity prop. Luo Qi had now watched a mannequin transform into the nation's top athlete without crossing any finish line. The crowd saw the trick. The federation could not call the score a human achievement, yet it still controlled the qualification list for the coming championship. Yu Cheng stepped onto the empty track and addressed the officials, not the cameras. 'Pause the ranking,' he said. 'Keep the genuine results. Show us every score you cannot verify.' Behind him the screen flickered and displayed a second ghost champion.

示範結束時，排名委員會唯一的獨立顧問正式辭職。她留下從待丟棄器材裡找到的一隻韓霏的受損長襪，拒絕讓它變成宣傳道具。羅琪親眼看著一個假人從未跨過終點線，卻被轉化成全國第一的選手。觀眾看到了手法。協會再不能稱那是人類成就，卻仍掌握即將到來的全國賽資格名單。余程站上空跑道，對著官員而非鏡頭說：「暫停排名，保留真實成績，把所有不能驗證的分數列出來。」他身後螢幕閃爍，又出現第二位幽靈冠軍。
