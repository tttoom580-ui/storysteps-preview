The station receives three mutually incompatible death forecasts. A faint tapping returned through the cable, delayed just enough to make everyone listen twice. Mira Chen watched the crowd move through Aster Station beneath the Pelagic Trench and noticed how quickly fear became a rumor. Jonas Reed wanted to act before the rumor hardened into policy. They spread the documents across a clean surface, placing dated facts on one side and guesses on the other. It was slower than making a bold announcement, but that small division gave them a way to ask questions without accusing innocent people. Nothing in the room could safely be ignored.

觀測站收到三段互相矛盾的死亡預測。 電纜傳回微弱敲擊聲，延遲得恰好讓每個人都想再聽一次。 米拉・陳看著人群穿過海溝下的星辰觀測站，注意到恐懼轉變成謠言有多快。喬納斯・里德想在謠言變成政策前行動。兩人把文件攤在乾淨桌面，一邊放有日期的事實，另一邊放推測。這比發表強硬聲明慢，卻能在不指控無辜者的情況下追問。房間裡沒有什麼能安全地被忽略。

Each forecast assumes a different action by Director Voss. The detail mattered because it could be checked without reading anyone’s private thoughts. Jonas Reed traced the ordinary steps that might have produced it, then asked what observation would prove their explanation wrong. Mira Chen made a separate copy and marked the time of collection. They had already learned that a convincing story and a reliable record were different things. One could persuade a room; only the other could endure a difficult question from someone outside it.

每段預測都假設沃斯主任會採取不同動作。 這個細節重要，是因為不用偷看任何人的私密想法也能查驗。喬納斯・里德追出可能造成它的普通步驟，再問什麼觀察會推翻這項解釋。米拉・陳另存一份副本，記下收集時間。兩人已明白，動聽的故事與可靠紀錄是兩回事。前者能說服滿屋子的人，後者才能承受局外人的艱難質問。

When diver Seo Min arrived, Jonas Reed offered a chair rather than a demand for testimony. The pause mattered. A witness could be useful without owing an investigator every detail of a private life. Mira Chen explained how the information would be stored, who could read it, and when it could be withdrawn. Once the rules were clear, the conversation became practical. They discussed routes, dates, signatures, and objects that could be examined by someone who had never met the people in the room.

潛水員徐敏抵達時，喬納斯・里德先遞上椅子，而不是要求作證。這份停頓很重要。證人即使提供線索，也不欠調查者自己全部的私生活。米拉・陳說明資訊會如何保存、誰能查閱、何時可以撤回。規則清楚後，談話變得務實。他們討論路線、日期、簽名與物件，這些都能由從沒見過當事人的人重新查驗。

Director Voss had a familiar argument: a large institution could not survive if every ordinary person questioned its decisions. Mira Chen disagreed, not because institutions were always wrong, but because power made errors expensive for those unable to challenge them. Jonas Reed refused to let the argument become personal. They requested a copy of the applicable rule and proposed a small independent test. If the official claim was true, the test should strengthen it. If it was false, the public deserved to know.

沃斯主任提出熟悉的說法：若每個普通人都質疑決策，大型機構便無法運作。米拉・陳不同意，並非因為機構永遠錯，而是權力會讓無法申訴的人為錯誤付出巨大代價。喬納斯・里德拒絕讓爭論私人化。他們要求適用規則的副本，並提出小型獨立測試。如果官方說法正確，測試理應使它更有力；若不正確，大眾就該知道。

By dusk the stakes had become visible beyond the papers. Someone had to keep the ordinary work of Aster Station beneath the Pelagic Trench going while others argued over what the records meant. Mira Chen divided the tasks, leaving a clear path for anyone who wanted to step away. Jonas Reed checked the safest route, and diver Seo Min reminded them that a clever plan could still fail people who had never agreed to be part of it. They changed the plan before asking anyone to take that risk.

黃昏時，紙張之外的風險已經可見。當其他人爭論紀錄意義時，仍有人得維持海溝下的星辰觀測站的日常工作。米拉・陳分配任務，也為想退出的人保留清楚道路。喬納斯・里德檢查最安全的路線，潛水員徐敏提醒大家：聰明計畫仍可能傷害從未同意參與的人。他們先修改計畫，再請別人承擔風險。

On a second sheet, Mira Chen found an old cross-reference index. Its headings were not proof of any theory; they were search terms left by previous workers. Entry 1 listed “abnormal”, “acquaint”, “bolt”, “compatible”, “confront”. Entry 2 listed “continental”, “discrimination”, “hostage”, “kidnap”, “missionary”. Entry 3 listed “mumble”, “norm”, “observer”, “script”, “sensation”. Entry 4 listed “slavery”, “stock”, “traitor”, “viable”, “viewer”. Jonas Reed insisted that a label could not replace a witness, an observed result, or a decision that people had actually approved. The index remained in the archive as a way to retrace the team's work, not as a shortcut to a verdict.

另一張紙上，米拉・陳找到舊交叉索引。標題並不能證明任何理論，只是前人留下的搜尋詞。第1欄列著“abnormal”, “acquaint”, “bolt”, “compatible”, “confront”。第2欄列著“continental”, “discrimination”, “hostage”, “kidnap”, “missionary”。第3欄列著“mumble”, “norm”, “observer”, “script”, “sensation”。第4欄列著“slavery”, “stock”, “traitor”, “viable”, “viewer”。喬納斯・里德堅持，標籤不能取代證人、實際觀察的結果，或當事人真正同意的決定。這份索引留在檔案中，方便後人回查工作，而非作為跳到結論的捷徑。

Inside the old tower, tiny fibers ran beneath the metal plates. The route of the signal was stranger than the shape of the building. Jonas connected it to the abandoned forecast model while Seo searched for a physical junction that could have survived the decades. Mira refused to treat every new image as a prophecy. What mattered was whether the same recording could be reproduced under known conditions.

舊高塔內，細小光纖在金屬板下延伸。訊號路徑比建築形狀更古怪。喬納斯把它連到廢棄預測模型，徐敏則尋找可能存活數十年的實體接點。米拉拒絕把每張新影像都當預言。重要的是，在已知條件下能否重現相同錄影。

At the midpoint, the argument was no longer about a single error. The larger institution had reasons to protect its reputation, and ordinary families had reasons to fear what exposure might cost them. Mira Chen and Jonas Reed gave both sides room to speak, but they did not agree that comfort justified hiding evidence. Their plan became slower and broader: independent checks, meaningful consent, and practical service before any public victory.

故事來到中段，爭論已不只關於單一錯誤。大型機構有理由保護名聲，普通家庭也害怕揭露帶來的代價。米拉・陳與喬納斯・里德讓雙方都有發言機會，卻不認同舒適可以合理化藏匿證據。他們的計畫變得更慢也更全面：獨立查核、真正同意，以及公開勝利之前先維持實際服務。

When the signal ended, nobody moved for several seconds. Then Jonas Reed checked the final reading and gave Mira Chen a tired nod. The result was smaller than the bold promises circulating outside, but it was real. diver Seo Min asked who would maintain the improvement after the investigators left. That question changed the room. They began dividing responsibility among people who would still be present next month, rather than awarding credit to whoever spoke most loudly today.

訊號停止後，有幾秒鐘誰也沒動。接著喬納斯・里德檢查最後讀數，疲憊地向米拉・陳點頭。結果比外頭流傳的豪言小，卻是真的。潛水員徐敏問調查者離開後誰來維護改善。這個問題改變了整間屋子。眾人開始把責任分給下個月仍會在場的人，而不是把功勞給今天喊得最大聲的人。

That night Mira Chen returned to the beginning of the chapter in her notebook. A few hours earlier the problem had seemed to demand an immediate victory; now she understood why the next decision would require patience. Jonas Reed brought tea and sat beside the window without asking for reassurance. They had not solved everything, but they had preserved a useful trail for the people who came after them. In the quiet, the work felt less like a contest and more like a promise.

那晚米拉・陳翻回筆記本本章最初的那一頁。數小時前，問題似乎要求立刻獲勝；此刻她才懂得下一個決定為何需要耐心。喬納斯・里德端茶坐到窗邊，沒有要求安慰。他們尚未解決一切，卻為後來的人保留了可用線索。在寂靜中，工作不像競賽，而更像承諾。
