港口空缺

A Vacancy at the Harbor

董事會副本：總裁只剩一票 · 第 9 / 25 章 · 3000–4000 · 英中對照 · 校訂中

用互動版查詞、朗讀與收藏

The ninth gate opened beside a rusty harbor crane. A bald dock supervisor introduced himself as the chairman of the local workers' association. He had kept the harbor machinery running through many storms, yet the company's new plan listed his position as a vacancy. Xingzhou asked how that could happen. The supervisor showed him a forecast that predicted every crane would be replaced within a month. The machines were still in use, and the new equipment had not arrived. If the staff left early, deliveries of medicine and food would halt. The game offered points for declaring the old workers unnecessary. Xingzhou recognized the temptation of a neat but untested forecast.

第九道門開在一臺生鏽的港口起重機旁。一名光頭碼頭主管自我介紹，說他是當地工人協會主席。多年來他在風暴中維持港口機械運作，新公司計畫卻把他的職位列為空缺。行舟問為何如此。主管拿出一份預測報告，聲稱一個月內所有起重機都將被更換。然而舊機器仍在使用，新設備也未到貨。若員工提早離職，藥品與食物的運送就會中斷。遊戲提出，只要宣布老員工已無必要，就能獲得積分。行舟看出漂亮卻未經驗證的預測有多麼誘人。

At a small canteen, a cook served pasta and lobster soup to the people trapped by the game. The food had been prepared for a community festival, not for executive guests. A rusty fireplace warmed the room. Xingzhou noticed a quotation above it: EVERY HARBOR DEPENDS ON HANDS IT DOES NOT ALWAYS SEE. The cook said the words came from her father's autobiography. She showed the book to Xia, who read the account with curiosity. The harbor had survived a past disaster because workers restored an abandoned pump before the government arrived. Nothing in that story suggested that those workers could be replaced by a forecast alone.

小餐館裡，廚師把義大利麵與龍蝦湯分給受困的人。食物原本是為社區慶典準備，不是為主管貴賓而做。生鏽的壁爐讓室內暖和。行舟注意到上方一句引言：「每座港口都依靠一些不總被看見的雙手。」廚師說這句話出自父親的自傳。她把書交給夏澄，夏澄好奇地讀起當年的紀錄。過去一次災難中，工人曾在政府抵達以前修復棄置水泵，才讓港口撐過危機。故事裡沒有任何證據表示，光靠預測就能取代那些工人。

A minister appeared on a public screen and requested an immediate production report. Xingzhou's assistant wanted to send the corporate forecast as if it described current reality. Xia objected: a prediction was not a measured result. The game warned that a delayed report might violate the minister's request. Xingzhou replied that an accurate delay was preferable to a false statement. The dock supervisor invited the team to inspect the machinery directly. They found one working crane, one needing repair, and a third already disconnected from power. The consequent plan would require fewer workers only after the new machines passed tests. Until then, safety depended on experienced hands.

一位部會首長出現在公共螢幕上，要求立即提交生產報告。行舟的助理想把公司預測當成現況發出去。夏澄反對：預測不是測量結果。遊戲警告，延遲報告可能違反首長要求。行舟回答，準確的延遲好過錯誤聲明。碼頭主管邀請大家直接檢查機械。他們找到一臺正常運作的起重機、一臺需要維修，還有一臺早已斷電。由此而來的計畫顯示，只有新機器通過測試後才可能減少人力；在那之前，安全仰賴有經驗的雙手。

A dispute arose when an investor demanded that the harbor declare the replacement finished immediately. He said the company needed to protect its market value. Guo asked who would unload the next shipment if the declaration became an order to dismiss staff. The investor had no answer. Xingzhou refused to sign the false completion form. Instead he signed a list of open tasks, including repairing the damaged crane and keeping the current crew on duty. The screen punished his refusal, but the harbor horn sounded a long note of approval from the stranded passengers. Nobody mistook that sound for a legal decision; it was simply gratitude from people who still needed supplies.

一名投資人要求港口立即宣布設備更換已完成，引發爭議。他說公司需要保護市值。郭黎問，如果聲明變成裁員命令，下一批貨物要由誰卸下。投資人無話可說。行舟拒絕在虛假的完工表上簽名，改而簽署未完成事項清單，包括修復損壞的起重機，以及讓現有班組繼續值勤。畫面因他的拒絕扣分，港口汽笛卻傳來長長一聲，受困乘客向他們表達支持。沒有人把聲響當成法律決定；那只是仍需要物資的人們表示感謝。

The dock supervisor let Xia copy the repair ledger, then asked her to compare it with the corporate summary. One number differed in a way that mattered. A column labeled DEPENDENT CONTRACTORS had been erased, making six workers disappear from the official count. Their pay and safety equipment had consequently vanished from the budget. Xingzhou had approved the summary in a crowded meeting without reviewing the source. He admitted that his signature did not make the data true. The team preserved both versions, and the screen granted the fourth verified failure. They were one careful finding away from the independent review specified in the original agreement.

碼頭主管讓夏澄影印維修帳冊，請她與公司摘要比較。有一項數字出現了重要差異：標為「依附承包工」的欄位被刪掉，讓六名工人從正式人數中消失。他們的薪資與安全設備因此也從預算裡消失。行舟過去曾在忙碌會議中批准摘要，卻未檢查來源。他承認自己的簽名不會讓資料自動成真。小隊保存兩個版本，畫面認定第四次經核實的失敗。他們距離原始協議要求的獨立覆核，只差最後一項仔細查證的事實。

Before leaving, the cook asked whether the team intended to save the harbor or merely win an argument. Xingzhou said he could not promise the old machines would remain forever. He could promise that nobody would be dismissed on the basis of an untested timetable. The dock supervisor said that distinction was enough to begin negotiations. The canteen door opened to an immense hall filled with people carrying luggage. A sign above it read RESCUE AND REASSIGNMENT. Xia closed the harbor ledger and warned everyone that the next trial might test whether their promises applied to people with no employment contract at all.

離開前，廚師問小隊究竟想救港口，還是只想贏一場爭論。行舟說，他無法承諾舊機器永遠不會被更換，但能承諾不根據未經測試的時程解雇任何人。碼頭主管說，這項區分足以成為談判起點。餐館大門打開，外面是一座擠滿提著行李人群的大廳，牌子上寫著「救援與重新安置」。夏澄合上港口帳冊，提醒大家下一場試煉可能會測試，他們的承諾是否也適用於完全沒有僱傭合約的人。
