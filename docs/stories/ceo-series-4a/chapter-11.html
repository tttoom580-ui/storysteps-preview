摩天樓示範室

The Skyscraper's Demonstration

董事會副本：總裁只剩一票 · 第 11 / 25 章 · 3000–4000 · 英中對照 · 校訂中

用互動版查詞、朗讀與收藏

The review request brought the team to a skyscraper whose windows showed a simulated hurricane. An enormous glass panel listed the city's commerce figures beside a model of the corporate Continuity Engine. A director in a silver jacket boasted that the device could protect every investor from misfortune. He offered an assurance that the remaining citizens would eventually benefit as well. Mei asked whether the engine had been tested during a real storm. The director accused her of ignorance. Xia said an informative answer would include the tests, their limits, and the people who reviewed them. A slogan about guaranteed protection was not the same thing as evidence.

覆核申請把小隊帶到一座摩天樓，窗外顯示模擬颶風。巨大的玻璃板把城市商業數字與企業「營運持續引擎」模型並列。一名穿銀色外套的董事誇口說，裝置能保護每名投資人免於災禍。他保證其他市民最終也會受益。阿梅問引擎是否曾在真實風暴中測試。董事指控她無知。夏澄說，有資訊價值的回答應包含測試、限制及審查人員名單。保證保護的標語不是證據。

The demonstration hall used hydrogen cells manufactured by a private supplier. A technician explained that the cells were safe only when air could circulate around them. The director wanted to lock every door to make the visual display look more dramatic. Guo warned that such a rule would create a troublesome risk during a power failure. He was loyal to the workers, not to any company's image. Xingzhou ordered the side doors kept clear. The game's score fell. He felt the old frustration of losing a competition he had not intended to enter. Xia reminded him that the purpose of a review was to expose a problem before people were hurt, not to earn praise.

示範廳使用私人供應商製造的氫能電池。技師解釋，電池周圍必須保持空氣流通才算安全。董事想鎖住所有門，讓視覺展示看起來更戲劇化。郭黎警告，停電時這種安排會造成棘手風險。他效忠的是工人，不是任何公司的形象。行舟命令側門保持暢通。遊戲分數下降。他又感到一種舊日挫折：像在輸掉自己原本不想參加的競賽。夏澄提醒他，覆核的目的是在有人受傷前揭露問題，不是贏得讚美。

The director produced a convention report that described the engine as consistent and ready for sale. Xia compared its graphs with the technician's log. Four tests had been repeated under identical indoor conditions, but the report described them as four different weather events. The distinction mattered. A machine could work four times in a laboratory and still fail in a storm. Xingzhou asked the director to contribute the original records rather than another summary. The director refused until the independent witnesses used their permanent marks. Only then did the wall reveal a hidden file showing that the trial had been expanded from a company exercise to a citywide system without public review.

董事拿出一份大型會議報告，宣稱引擎性能穩定，已可上市。夏澄把圖表和技師日誌比對。四次測試全在相同室內條件下重做，報告卻把它們寫成四種不同天氣事件。差異很重要：機器在實驗室成功四次，仍可能在風暴中失效。行舟要求董事交出原始紀錄，不要再提供摘要。董事拒絕，直到獨立見證者使用永久記號。牆面才顯示隱藏檔案，指出這場試煉曾在未經公眾審查的情況下，從企業演練擴展為全城系統。

Xingzhou recognized his predecessor's signature on the expansion order and his own name on a later budget renewal. He had never signed permission to trap a city. Yet his decision to renew the project had funded machinery whose risks he did not understand. The gap between intention and consequence felt larger than the office tower around him. The director demanded that he show resistance to the findings for the good of the company. Xingzhou answered that recovery required admitting the failure first. The technician added a practical request: release the safety data to the people who maintained the cells. It was a modest request, but nothing else could make their next inspection trustworthy.

行舟認出擴大計畫的命令上有前任總裁簽名，後來的預算續約則有自己的名字。他從未簽字准許困住一座城市，但續約確實資助了他不了解風險的機械。意圖與後果的距離，讓他覺得比這座辦公樓還巨大。董事要求他為公司利益抵抗調查結果。行舟回答，復原必須先承認失敗。技師提出實際要求：讓維護電池的人取得安全數據。那要求不大，卻是讓下一次檢查可信的必要條件。

During a short break, the game projected a relaxation room for executives only. It displayed soft music and a view of an empty beach, while workers waited beside the hot equipment. Xingzhou asked why the two spaces had different access rules. The system said high-ranking decision makers needed protected rest. Mei replied that people who carried fuel cells also needed rest. The team divided its shift and ensured that technicians could step away safely. The review board, still only a small illuminated window, registered the unequal rest arrangement as another concern. The skyscraper lights flickered; the tower was beginning to lose the perfect appearance its directors had worked so hard to maintain.

短暫休息時，遊戲投射出一間只供主管使用的放鬆室，裡面有柔和音樂與空蕩海灘景色，工人卻仍守在發熱設備旁。行舟問兩種空間為何有不同的使用規則。系統回答，高階決策者需要受到保護的休息。阿梅說，搬運燃料電池的人也需要休息。小隊重新分配輪班，確保技師能安全離開工作區。仍只是小小發光視窗的覆核委員會，把不平等休息安排記成另一項疑慮。摩天樓的燈開始閃爍；董事們苦心維持的完美外觀正在崩解。

The independent review did not immediately cancel the game. It established that the Continuity Engine had been expanded without the required checks and ordered a full technical inquiry. Xingzhou could not use that finding to declare himself innocent. He had approved the renewal money and ignored a request for public testing. Xia asked him to preserve the evidence rather than write a defense speech. He agreed and signed a transfer note allowing three independent witnesses to carry copies outside the tower. The next lift descended toward an industrial harbor where the original hardware had been assembled. For once, the route led from a boardroom promise to the place where people had actually built the machine.

獨立覆核沒有立刻取消遊戲。它認定營運持續引擎曾在未接受必要檢查下被擴大使用，並下令進行完整技術調查。行舟不能藉此宣稱自己無辜；續約預算是他批准的，公眾測試的要求也是他忽略的。夏澄請他保存證據，不要急著寫辯護演說。他同意，簽署移交單，讓三名獨立見證者將副本帶出大樓。下一部電梯下降至原始硬體組裝的工業港口。這次，路線終於從董事會的承諾，通往人們真正建造機器的地方。
