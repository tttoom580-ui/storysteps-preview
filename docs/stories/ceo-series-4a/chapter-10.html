沒有人認領的人

The Hall of Unclaimed People

董事會副本：總裁只剩一票 · 第 10 / 25 章 · 3000–4000 · 英中對照 · 校訂中

用互動版查詞、朗讀與收藏

The tenth trial resembled a refugee reception center, with beds arranged in a former sports facility. A woman in worn stockings stood beside the entrance, holding a child who had lost his shoes. The game demanded an acceptance certificate before they could enter. Nobody could provide one because their documents had been left in a flooded district. An ambitious manager offered a different option: they could sign a temporary employment contract with the company in exchange for rescue. Xingzhou recognized the proposal as a promotion strategy disguised as aid. Xia said a person's dignity should not depend on whether a corporation could classify them as useful labor.

第十場試煉像一座難民接待中心，床鋪排在改建的體育設施裡。一名穿著磨舊長襪的女子站在入口，抱著失去鞋子的孩子。遊戲要求他們提出接納證明才能進門，但文件全留在淹水地區，沒有人拿得出來。一名有野心的主管提供另一種選項：只要與公司簽臨時勞動合約，就能換得救援。行舟認出這是把行銷升遷策略偽裝成援助。夏澄說，一個人的尊嚴不該取決於企業能否把他們分類成有用勞力。

A publisher had placed a cheerful booklet near the registration desk. Its title promised to transform every newcomer into a loyal customer within one day. The atmosphere in the hall was too tense for such language. A genuine volunteer pointed out that people differed in age, ability, and immediate needs. Some required dry clothes; others wanted news of relatives. Mei asked each arrival what help was necessary and wrote the answer without inventing missing information. The manager found the method embarrassing because it was slower than his standardized form. Guo replied that a form could be efficient and still offensive if it refused to hear the person filling it out.

一名出版商在登記櫃臺放了本歡樂小冊子，標題承諾一天內把所有新來者轉變成忠誠顧客。大廳的氣氛太緊張，根本不適合這種話語。一位真誠的志工指出，人們年齡、能力與眼前需求都不同。有些人需要乾衣服，有些人只想知道家人消息。阿梅逐一詢問抵達者需要什麼，忠實記下，不捏造缺漏資訊。主管覺得這種方式太慢，很丟臉。郭黎回答，表格可以有效率，卻仍可能因為不聽填寫者說話而充滿冒犯。

The game's next command was RESIGNATION REQUIRED. It claimed that anyone receiving shelter must resign from all previous occupations and accept the company's protection. Xingzhou refused to enforce it. A woman who had worked as a perfume maker said she wanted to return to her own trade after the disaster. Another newcomer had been an architect and was willing to help inspect the building, but not to become anyone's property. Xia asked how the manager could distinguish a voluntary offer from forced service. He replied that both created the same output. Xingzhou surprised himself by saying that identical output did not make the choices identical.

遊戲下一項指令是「必須辭職」。它聲稱所有接受安置的人都得辭去原有工作，接受公司的保護。行舟拒絕執行。一名曾做香水的女子表示，災難後想回到自己的行業。另一名新來者是建築師，願意幫忙檢查建物，卻不願成為任何人的財產。夏澄問主管如何區分自願協助與強迫服務。主管回答，兩者都能產生相同成果。行舟意外地聽見自己說：成果相同，不代表選擇也相同。

A rumor spread that the shelter's food was inauthentic and that accepting it would cause illness. Guo inspected the kitchen with a nurse and a cook. The supplies had authentic labels and had been stored safely. The real problem was a broken refrigerator, which reduced the amount of food that could be kept overnight. The team arranged smaller deliveries and posted the findings in plain language. They did not pretend every risk was gone. A patient asked whether the company was now running the city. Xingzhou said the company was helping operate one facility, not owning the people inside it. The distinction mattered enough to put on the wall.

謠言傳開，聲稱安置中心的食物不正宗，吃了會生病。郭黎與護理師、廚師一起檢查廚房。物資標示真實可靠，保存也安全。真正的問題是一臺壞掉的冰箱，使過夜保存的食物量減少。小隊安排較小批次的補給，把調查結果以簡單語言公告，沒有假裝所有風險都消失。一名病患問，公司是否已經接管整座城市。行舟回答，公司只是協助營運一處設施，不是擁有裡面的人。這項差異重要到值得貼在牆上。

The manager submitted a list of arrivals who supposedly accepted the employment offer. Xia compared it with the actual signatures and found that several people had made no choice at all. One child had been listed as a full-time worker. The manager blamed an automated template. Xingzhou ordered the incorrect entries removed and asked the affected adults to review any future offer independently. A warning called this rejection of corporate efficiency a failure. The permanent witness marks said otherwise. The refugees had been given a real opportunity to refuse, and the shelter remained open without collecting their working lives as payment.

主管提交一份據稱已接受僱傭提案的入住者名單。夏澄和真實簽名比較，發現有些人根本沒有作出任何選擇，甚至有孩子被列成全職員工。主管怪罪自動套版。行舟下令刪除錯誤資料，讓受影響的成年人日後能獨立審閱任何工作提案。警告畫面把拒絕企業效率稱為失敗；永久見證記號卻顯示相反結果。受難者真正有了拒絕機會，安置中心也不必收走他們的工作人生當作代價，就能繼續開放。

The fifth verified failure appeared on the wall: SHELTER ACCESS CONDITIONED ON INVALID CONSENT. Xia checked the names of all five separate witnesses and compared the time stamps. This time the requirement from the library was satisfied. Xingzhou expected a victory announcement, but the game merely displayed an independent-review window that would open after a formal request. The review would require the records from every trial and three signatures from people who did not hold company stock. Mei, Guo, and the shelter architect volunteered to examine the material. Xingzhou did not offer to sign for them. Their marks were theirs, and that was exactly why the rule had survived.

牆上出現第五次經核實的失敗：「安置資格附帶無效同意條件。」夏澄檢查五名獨立見證者的名字與時間戳記。這次，圖書館裡看到的要求終於達成。行舟以為會出現勝利公告，遊戲卻只顯示一個必須正式申請才能開啟的獨立覆核視窗。覆核需要各試煉的完整紀錄，以及三名沒有公司股份者的簽名。阿梅、郭黎與安置中心的建築師自願審閱資料。行舟沒有提出代簽。他們的記號屬於自己，而規則能留存，正因如此。
