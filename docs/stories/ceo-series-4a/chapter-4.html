The Library of Unfinished Promises

董事會副本：總裁只剩一票 · 第 4 / 25 章 · 3000–4000 · 英中對照 · 校訂中

用互動版查詞、朗讀與收藏

The fourth floor was a library surrounded by glass windows that showed the earth as if the building were in orbit. A librarian stood at a desk beneath a sign reading URGENT APPROVAL REQUESTED. He explained that every corporation had submitted a city emergency agreement, but most executives had treated the document as a passive formality. Xingzhou's agreement lay in a locked cabinet marked PRIVATE POSSESSION. The librarian said only the chief executive could request it. Xia asked whether the public would be allowed to read what the agreement promised. He answered that public access was a separate question. Xingzhou felt the familiar temptation to approve a secret document and move on.

第四層是一間圖書館，四周的玻璃窗映出地球，彷彿大樓正在軌道上運行。圖書管理員站在寫著「緊急核准申請」的櫃臺下。他解釋每家公司都曾繳交城市緊急協議，但大多數主管只是被動地把文件當作例行手續。行舟公司的協議放在標著「私人財產」的上鎖櫃裡。館員說只有執行長能提出調閱。夏澄問，公眾能否閱讀協議承諾的內容；他回答，公開閱覽是另一個問題。行舟又感到那種熟悉的誘惑：批准一份機密文件，然後繼續往前走。

A researcher from the city university had left a margin note on the original agreement. Its emphasis fell on three things: keep witness marks permanent, protect ordinary exits during disputes, and allow an independent review after five verified failures. The text was earnest and remarkably clear. An embassy translator had checked foreign-language copies, because several international workers used the same building. Xingzhou had not known any of this. He remembered laughing at an old colleague who insisted that public contracts mattered more than impressive presentations. That colleague had been dismissed as a disappointment. Now her careful work was the only reason the team understood the rules.

城市大學一名研究員曾在原始協議頁邊留下註記，強調三件事：見證記號必須永久保留、爭議期間要保護普通出口、五次經核實的失敗後可啟動獨立覆核。文字真誠而清楚。一名使館譯員核對過外語版本，因為同一棟建築裡有許多國際勞工。行舟此前完全不知情。他記得自己曾嘲笑一位認為公共契約比漂亮簡報重要的同事，那人後來被當成令人失望的員工而遭解職。如今只有她當年的仔細工作，能讓小隊讀懂規則。

To open the cabinet, the game ordered them to solve a rhyme about a murderer who could never be caught. Mei called the puzzle nonsense. Xia agreed that the words were an amusement, not evidence of a real crime. The answer was TIME, which could destroy a promise without touching a person. The cabinet bounced open with a loud spring. Inside lay a paper record of continual changes to the corporate agreement. Each change had reduced the time allowed for objections. Xingzhou recognized his own initials beside three of them. None had been reviewed by the city's researchers. He could no longer claim that the problem had appeared from nowhere.

要打開櫃子，遊戲要求他們解一段押韻謎語，內容是一名永遠抓不到的殺人者。阿梅說這根本胡言亂語。夏澄同意，謎語只是逗人玩的文字，不是真實犯罪的證據。答案是「時間」——它不必碰觸任何人，也能毀掉承諾。櫃子彈簧一響，門彈開了。裡面放著公司協議持續修改的紙本紀錄，每次修改都縮短了提出異議的時間。行舟認出其中三份文件旁有自己的縮寫簽名，而城市研究員從未審閱過那些修改。他再也不能聲稱問題憑空出現。

The librarian offered a strange accomplishment badge if Xingzhou concealed the changes. He refused it and asked the translator to read the agreement aloud in two languages. The building shook when the last sentence was spoken. A warning claimed the new review process could prevent the company from reaching its profit target. Xia asked whether a profit target had ever been part of the emergency agreement. Nobody could find such a clause. A junior librarian brought a box of past complaints, many written by workers whose names had been removed from the annual report. Their requests were not dramatic. They asked for lights, wide doors, and time to disagree.

圖書管理員表示，只要行舟藏起修改紀錄，就能得到一枚奇特的成就徽章。行舟拒絕了，請譯員用兩種語言朗讀協議。最後一句讀完，大樓就震了一下。警告畫面聲稱新的覆核程序會讓公司無法達成獲利目標。夏澄問，獲利目標何時成為緊急協議的條款。沒有人找得到這樣的規定。一名年輕館員帶來一箱舊申訴，許多申訴者是名字已被年度報告刪除的工人。他們的要求並不戲劇化，只是照明、夠寬的門，以及可以提出不同意見的時間。

Xingzhou proposed publishing the original agreement with the changed pages beside it. The librarian warned that such an act required approval from every witness whose private details appeared there. Xia pointed out that they could hide addresses and personal numbers without hiding the clauses themselves. A researcher helped them prepare a public copy. Mei checked whether the doors remained free while they worked. They did, although the game tried to distract the group with bouncing colored lights. Xingzhou signed a notice admitting which changes he had approved. The screen did not forgive him. It merely recorded that the admission existed, and another condition for review had become visible.

行舟提議把原始協議與修改頁面並列公開。館員警告，若文件含有見證者的私人資料，就必須取得相關人的同意。夏澄指出，他們能遮住地址與個人號碼，卻不必隱藏條款本身。研究員協助製作公開版本，阿梅則確認工作期間各扇門仍可自由通行。門確實沒關，儘管遊戲不斷用跳動的彩光分散眾人注意。行舟在公告上簽名，承認哪些修改由他批准。畫面沒有原諒他，只記下承認確實存在，並顯示出另一項覆核條件。

At closing time, the librarian handed Xia a small folder labeled FIVE VERIFIED FAILURES. It did not contain five examples; it contained a blank form describing how each one must be documented by separate witnesses. The team had evidence from the lobby, the shopping floor, and the mountain terminal, but only two events had been formally verified. Xingzhou wanted to count everything. Xia insisted that exaggeration would weaken their case. He closed the folder and agreed to carry it forward. When a company demanded unquestioning loyalty, even a blank box could be dangerous. Here it was a promise that nobody's testimony would be invented simply to fill a page.

閉館時，管理員交給夏澄一個寫著「五次經核實的失敗」的小資料夾。裡面並沒有五個案例，只有一份空白表格，說明每次失敗必須由不同見證者如何記錄。小隊雖然有大廳、購物樓層與山區貨運站的證據，但真正完成正式核實的只有兩件。行舟想把所有事件都算進去，夏澄堅持誇大只會削弱他們的主張。他合上資料夾，同意帶著它前進。當公司要求無條件忠誠時，連一個空格都可能很危險；但這裡的空格代表一項承諾：不會為了填滿表格而捏造證詞。
