降落場上的雪

Snow on the Landing Field

董事會副本：總裁只剩一票 · 第 7 / 25 章 · 3000–4000 · 英中對照 · 校訂中

Frost covered the northern landing field, where a helicopter waited beside an emergency clinic. The cold was intense, and a herd of frightened goats blocked the road. A farmer led a stubborn mule toward a shed but could not move the animals safely through the crowd. The game offered Xingzhou an exclusive flight if he ordered everyone else away from the runway. He knew a fast choice could create a remarkable score. Xia asked him to analyze the surroundings first. The clinic's plumber had reported a broken heating pipe, and several patients were waiting for transport. Clearing the road for one executive might injure them more than the weather did.

北區降落場覆著霜雪，一架直升機停在緊急診所旁。寒意強烈，一群受驚的山羊堵住道路。一名農夫牽著固執的騾子走向棚屋，卻無法讓動物安全穿過人群。遊戲提議，只要行舟下令所有人離開跑道，就能獨享飛行服務。他知道快速決定可以得到漂亮分數。夏澄請他先分析周遭環境。診所水管工通報暖氣管線破裂，還有幾名病患等待運送。為了一名總裁清空道路，可能造成比寒冷更嚴重的傷害。

An ambitious competitor from another corporation challenged Xingzhou to a contest. Each company would earn points for moving the highest percent of people in ten minutes. The competitor displayed a diploma from a famous academy and promised that his method would provide a forty-percent reduction in waiting time. Guo asked whether the number included passengers who could not walk. The man admitted that it did not. Xia demanded accuracy, not a polished percentage. She counted each person, noting who required a stretcher, who needed a warm vehicle, and who could wait. A slight delay at the start prevented a serious mistake near the aircraft.

另一家公司的野心勃勃的競爭者向行舟挑戰。兩家公司要比賽，看誰能在十分鐘內運送最高比例的人。對方展示名校文憑，承諾方法能讓等待時間減少百分之四十。郭黎問，數字是否包含無法行走的乘客。那人承認沒有。夏澄要求準確，而不是好看的百分比。她逐一清點，記下誰需要擔架、誰需要暖車、誰還能等待。起步時稍微耽擱，反而避免了飛機旁的重大錯誤。

The clinic nurse opposed using the helicopter as a prize. She said mutual aid would move more patients if the runway remained clear for medical trips. The competitor mocked her as untrained. Guo answered that she knew the patients' actual needs better than either executive. Xingzhou asked the farmer to adapt his animal route, keeping the goats away from the aircraft. Two volunteers set up a quiet enclosure. The plumber repaired the heating pipe with parts from a maintenance box, and the clinic grew warmer. The game had treated the helicopter as the only valuable vehicle; the small ground ambulance now carried three patients safely before the contest clock ended.

診所護理師反對把直升機當獎品。她說只要跑道留給醫療運送，互助方式能幫助更多病患。競爭者嘲笑她沒有受過訓練。郭黎回答，她比兩位總裁都更了解病人的實際需求。行舟請農夫調整牲畜路線，別讓山羊靠近飛機。兩名志工設置安靜圍欄。水管工用維修箱裡的零件修好暖氣，診所逐漸暖和。遊戲原本把直升機當成唯一有價值的交通工具；比賽計時結束前，小型地面救護車已先安全載走三名病患。

The competitor complained that his speed advantage was being stolen. Xia showed him the counting sheet. His method excluded five people, including a woman whose leg was injured and a child with a fever. He had created a better score by removing the hardest cases. Xingzhou recognized the pattern from corporate welfare reports that celebrated reduced expenses while ignoring people who lost services. He announced that every transport comparison would use the same list of passengers and record anyone left behind. The screen called this rule an inefficient burden. The nurse called it a fair calculation. Xingzhou was beginning to prefer the nurse's words to the game's awards.

競爭者抱怨自己的速度優勢被偷走了。夏澄把統計表給他看。他的方法排除了五個人，包括腿受傷的女子與發燒的孩子。他藉由刪去最困難的個案，換得較好成績。行舟想起公司福利報告常慶祝成本下降，卻忽略因此失去服務的人。他宣布所有運送比較都必須使用同一份乘客名單，並記錄誰被留下。畫面說這項規則增加無效率的負擔。護理師則說這才是公平的計算。行舟開始更喜歡護理師的說法，而不是遊戲頒發的獎項。

When the helicopter finally departed with the most urgent patient, nobody cheered. The nurse remained at the landing field, checking the next departure time and asking for a reserve vehicle. The competitor had earned more points by the official measure, but Xingzhou's group had transported more people without abandoning anyone. Their reports were attached to a witness mark by the clinic door. Guo warned that the northern district archive was still locked. The nurse pointed them toward a workshop owned by families who had lived in the mountains before either company existed. That workshop, she said, had supplied the first emergency stretchers in the region.

直升機終於載著最緊急的病患起飛時，沒有人歡呼。護理師仍站在降落場，確認下一班時間並要求預備車輛。依遊戲官方算法，競爭者拿到較多分數；但行舟的小隊運送了更多人，沒有拋下任何個案。他們的報告被附在診所門邊的見證記號下。郭黎提醒大家，北區檔案仍鎖著。護理師指向一座由山區世居家族經營的工坊，說那裡曾提供全區第一批緊急擔架。

Xingzhou asked the competitor to keep his own results rather than surrender them. The man looked surprised. Xia explained that an inaccurate measurement could still become useful if its missing cases were identified. She wrote both calculations beside each other and labeled their limits. The lesson was not that competition always failed, but that a contest needed rules people could inspect. Snow began falling more heavily. The farmer drove the last goat into the shed, and the repaired heating pipe sent steam above the clinic roof. The new gate displayed a woodworking bench and an old family photograph. Xingzhou followed it, carrying the signed list of patients they had moved.

行舟請競爭者保留自己的結果，而不是交出它。對方很驚訝。夏澄解釋，只要清楚指出漏算的個案，不準確的測量仍能成為有用資料。她把兩套算法並列，標示各自限制。這一課不是說競賽必定失敗，而是競賽需要能接受檢查的規則。雪下得更大了。農夫把最後一隻山羊趕入棚屋，修好的暖氣管線讓診所屋頂冒出熱氣。新門上顯示木工桌與老家庭照片。行舟帶著已簽名的病患運送清單，跟著走了進去。
