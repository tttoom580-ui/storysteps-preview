接待廳裡的小偷

The Thief in the Reception Hall

董事會副本：總裁只剩一票 · 第 5 / 25 章 · 3000–4000 · 英中對照 · 校訂中

用互動版查詞、朗讀與收藏

The fifth trial began in a reception hall filled with cheap decoration. Visitors were invited to admire artificial scenery while a voice described a robber hiding among the service staff. A primitive security camera replayed a blurred image of someone carrying a wooden box. The game demanded that Xingzhou name a suspect. He recognized this tactic from an old corporate campaign: create enough terror, then offer a confident answer. His conscience resisted, but the influential directors beside him urged speed. A woman named Wen, who managed the reception desk, asked to see the original recording before anybody was accused. Xingzhou gave her the screen rather than deciding for her.

第五場試煉在佈滿廉價裝飾的接待廳展開。訪客被邀請欣賞人造景觀，廣播則宣稱服務人員中藏著一名搶匪。一部原始的保全攝影機反覆播放模糊畫面：某人抱著木箱走過。遊戲要求行舟指認嫌疑人。他想起某次舊公司的宣傳活動：先製造足夠的恐懼，再提供一個肯定答案。良心讓他猶豫，旁邊有影響力的董事卻催他快做決定。接待主任小文要求在任何人受指控前先看原始錄影。行舟把畫面交給她，而不是代替她判斷。

An agency representative said the company should adopt a simple rule: anyone without an executive badge could be searched. Guo Li objected that such behavior treated low-paid workers as suspects by default. Xia called the proposal a shameful shortcut. The representative tried to discourage further questions, claiming that the outcome of a public search would be too complicated. Wen opened the guest register and pointed to the time stamps. A box had been moved by a supplier in uniform, then collected by a technician whose badge had failed to print. The camera's poor angle could not show theft. It showed a delivery, and perhaps nothing more.

保全外包公司的代表說，瑞霄應採用一條簡單規則：凡是沒有主管識別證的人，都可以被搜查。郭黎反對，認為這種做法把低薪員工預設成嫌疑人。夏澄稱提案是可恥的捷徑。代表試圖阻止繼續提問，說公開調查的結果會太複雜。小文打開來賓登記簿，指著時間戳記。木箱先由穿制服的供應商搬入，再由一名識別證印不出來的技師領走。攝影機角度太差，無法證明偷竊；它只拍到一次運送，可能根本沒別的事。

The hall's walls suddenly changed into an old fantasy painting. A witch and a wizard appeared in the corner, offering to identify the robber by reading people's thoughts. Several visitors laughed, but one child was horrified by the threatening masks. Mei guided the child away from the display and reminded the crowd that nobody could inspect another person's mind. Xingzhou asked how a city congress might respond to such an accusation in ordinary life. Xia said a real process would begin with evidence, the chance to answer, and protection against humiliation. The game tried to make those fundamental steps seem slow. The team kept them anyway.

大廳牆面忽然換成古老的奇幻畫作，一名女巫與一名巫師出現在角落，表示能透過讀心找出搶匪。幾位訪客笑了，一名孩子卻被可怕的面具嚇壞。阿梅帶孩子遠離畫面，提醒大家沒有人能檢查別人的思想。行舟問，若事情發生在平常生活，城市議會會如何面對這種指控。夏澄說，真正的程序應從證據、答辯機會，以及避免羞辱開始。遊戲想把這些基本步驟說成拖延，但小隊仍堅持如此。

At the center of the hall stood a glass cabinet containing gifts from customers. One letter described how a family cherished an old worker who had once found their lost child. Another displayed the agency's campaign promise to treat everyone fairly. Wen noticed that the wooden box in the video was now inside the cabinet. Its label said REPAIRED LIGHTING EQUIPMENT. The supposed robber had been carrying lamps for a broken emergency passage. Xingzhou felt anger toward the directors who had praised suspicion as strong leadership. Yet he remembered how often he had rewarded that same attitude. Anger alone would not change the company's interaction with its workers.

大廳中央的玻璃櫃收藏著顧客送來的禮物。一封信寫著某戶人家如何珍惜一名老員工，因為他曾找回走失的孩子。另一件展品列著外包公司承諾公平對待每個人的宣傳文案。小文發現錄影中的木箱如今就在櫃子裡，標籤寫著「修復後的照明設備」。所謂的小偷，其實正在搬運故障逃生通道所需的燈具。行舟對那些把猜疑稱為強勢領導的董事感到憤怒，但他也記得自己過去常獎賞同一種態度。單靠生氣，不會改變公司與員工的互動方式。

Xia asked the technician whether he wanted the story made public. He said yes to the repair record but no to showing his face. Wen prepared a statement that corrected the accusation without exposing his identity. Xingzhou signed it and withdrew the search order. The screen displayed a third verified failure: A COMPANY RULE HAD RESTRICTED AN EXIT WITHOUT EVIDENCE. The capitalized sentence was not a victory speech. It was a finding, supported by a guest register, a repair label, and the technician's own witness mark. For the first time, a director asked if the company could change its security policy after the trial. Guo said that would be a useful beginning.

夏澄詢問技師是否願意公開整件事。他同意公開修繕紀錄，但不願露臉。小文寫出一份更正聲明，既澄清指控，也不暴露身分。行舟簽名，撤回搜查命令。畫面顯示第三次經核實的失敗：「公司規則在沒有證據的情況下限制出口。」這句大寫字不是勝利演說，而是有來賓登記簿、修繕標籤與技師見證記號支持的調查結果。第一次有董事問，遊戲結束後公司是否能改變保全政策。郭黎說，那才是有用的開始。

The hall's final gate opened only after the accused worker was allowed to review the statement. He asked for one sentence to be removed because it made his work sound heroic rather than routine. Xia removed it. Xingzhou realized that even praise could become another form of control if the person concerned had no say. The technician took his tools and returned to repair the lights. Mei placed the recovered box on a trolley and pushed it toward the stairs. The game awarded no public applause, only a small seal beside the third failure. Five were needed. Two more would have to be documented without guessing, and the tower above them was still full of people.

被誤指控的工人獲准審閱聲明後，大廳最後一道門才打開。他要求刪去一句話，因為那句話把普通的工作寫得像英雄事蹟。夏澄照做。行舟明白，即使是稱讚，只要當事人沒有發言權，也可能成為另一種控制。技師帶著工具回去修燈；阿梅把找回的木箱放上推車，推向樓梯。遊戲沒有給他們掌聲，只有第三次失敗旁的一枚小印章。要啟動覆核，仍需要五次。他們還得不靠猜測地記錄另外兩次，而上方的塔樓裡仍有許多人。
