船艙裡的硬體

The Vessel and the Hardware

董事會副本：總裁只剩一票 · 第 12 / 25 章 · 3000–4000 · 英中對照 · 校訂中

用互動版查詞、朗讀與收藏

The industrial harbor was dark except for a vessel loaded with heavy electronic hardware. A miner from the inland hills had helped deliver the raw metal used to build the Continuity Engine. He carried an oval badge showing the original production team. Above the dock, a hawk circled in the wind. The game's announcer demanded absolute efficiency and instructed the workers to move every container before dawn. Guo insisted on discipline instead of haste. He showed a distribution chart that placed more weight on one side of the vessel than the other. If they fastened the cargo without correcting that imbalance, the ship could tip as it left the pier.

工業港口一片昏暗，只有一艘裝滿沉重電子硬體的貨船亮著燈。一名來自內陸山區的礦工曾協助運送建造營運持續引擎所需的金屬原料。他帶著橢圓形徽章，上面有最初生產團隊的標記。碼頭上方，一隻鷹在風中盤旋。遊戲廣播要求絕對效率，命令工人在天亮前搬完所有貨櫃。郭黎堅持應有紀律，而非一味求快。他展示一張載重分配圖，指出船的一側比另一側重。若不先修正失衡就固定貨物，船離港時可能傾斜。

A gracious foreman welcomed the team but warned that the shipyard had lost its most experienced safety inspector the previous year. Her death had left the crew with grief as well as unfinished training. Xingzhou asked whether the company had sent a replacement. The foreman shook his head. He had received a framed illustration of the company's founder instead. The gift was expensive and useless for inspecting machinery. Xia asked the foreman to show them the actual inspection checklist. Several sections were blank. No amount of recognition for the founder could fill the spaces where a trained worker should have recorded measurements.

一名態度親切的工頭歡迎他們，卻提醒造船廠去年失去最資深的安全檢查員。她的離世使團隊承受悲傷，也留下尚未完成的訓練。行舟問公司是否派人接替。工頭搖頭，說自己只收到一幅裝框的創辦人插畫。禮物昂貴，對檢查機械卻毫無用處。夏澄請工頭拿出真正的檢查表，其中幾欄是空白的。再多對創辦人的表揚，也無法代替受過訓練的人測量並填寫數據。

A locked control box held the hardware specifications. The game told Xingzhou to insert his executive card and take the documents as company property. He hesitated, remembering the workshop ledger. Xia suggested that the foreman and the miner be present when the box was opened. Inside they found a schematic showing how an automatic decision module could override a physical door. Its designers had included a manual stop switch, but the latest installation guide had removed the instruction to connect it. Xingzhou felt dread when he saw the consequences. The game had not invented every unsafe feature; some had been built into the machine under ordinary contracts.

上鎖的控制箱裡放著硬體規格。遊戲要行舟插入主管卡片，將文件當成公司財產帶走。他想起工坊帳冊，猶豫了。夏澄建議由工頭與礦工一起見證開箱。裡面有張線路圖，顯示自動決策模組如何覆蓋實體門禁。設計師原本納入人工停止開關，最新安裝說明卻刪去了連接該開關的指示。行舟看見後果，心裡充滿恐懼。並非所有不安全功能都是遊戲憑空創造；有些早在普通商業合約裡就被建造出來。

Guo proposed a realistic test on an empty loading door, not a crowded exit. The foreman agreed and asked workers to secure the area. A technician reconnected the manual stop switch under supervision. When the automatic command closed the door, the switch interrupted it and renewed physical control. The result was not perfection. It was one repeatable way to prevent an order from trapping someone. Xingzhou wanted to test every door immediately. Xia resolved to start with the sites where people were waiting and where repairs could be made safely. The board accepted that sequence after the workers signed the test log.

郭黎提議在沒有人使用的貨運門上進行實際測試，而不是拿擁擠的出口冒險。工頭同意，請工人先封鎖測試區。一名技師在監督下重新接上人工停止開關。自動命令關門時，開關成功中斷指令，恢復實體控制。這不是完美解方，卻是一種可以重複驗證、防止人被困住的方法。行舟想立刻測試每一扇門。夏澄決定先處理有人等待、且能安全維修的地點。工人在測試日誌簽名後，覆核視窗才接受這項順序。

The miner asked if he could take a copy of the schematic back to the hills. His community operated an emergency storehouse connected to the same system. Xingzhou started to refuse because the drawing contained commercial details. Guo proposed redacting only the parts unrelated to safety. The foreman supported him. They prepared a public diagram showing where the stop switch belonged and who should verify its installation. The miner thanked them without promising to join the company. As the vessel's cargo was balanced, the game displayed a new notice: PHYSICAL OVERRIDE VERIFIED AT ONE SITE. One site was not enough, but the team finally possessed a method they could test elsewhere.

礦工問能否帶一份線路圖回山區，因為他的社區有一座使用同系統的緊急倉庫。行舟起初想拒絕，理由是圖面含有商業資訊。郭黎提議只遮掉與安全無關的部分，工頭支持他們。大家製作公開圖，說明人工停止開關應接在哪裡，以及誰應確認安裝。礦工道謝，卻沒有因此答應加入公司。貨船載重恢復平衡時，遊戲顯示新公告：「一處場址的實體覆蓋功能已驗證。」一處還不夠，但小隊終於有了能在其他地方重做的方法。

On the pier, the hawk flew toward a row of distant roofs. Xia followed it with her eyes and noticed emergency lights in several residential blocks. A new problem was waiting beyond the shipyard: even a working switch would be useless if nobody knew where it was. Xingzhou asked the foreman to appoint local instructors who could train other workers without requiring an executive invitation. The foreman said the company would have to pay them for that time. Xingzhou wrote the cost into the same public sheet as the repair parts. He had spent years treating labor as a line to be reduced. The game was teaching him how expensive that habit could become.

碼頭上，那隻鷹飛向遠方一排屋頂。夏澄循著牠的方向望去，看見數個住宅街區亮起緊急燈。造船廠外還有下一個問題：即使開關可用，沒人知道位置也毫無用處。行舟請工頭任命在地講師，讓工人不必得到主管邀請也能接受訓練。工頭表示，公司必須支付那段教學工時。行舟把費用和維修零件列在同一張公開表上。他多年來把勞動視為該削減的一行數字，如今遊戲讓他看見這種習慣有多昂貴。
