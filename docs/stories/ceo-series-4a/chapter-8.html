兩份複本的工坊

The Workshop of Two Copies

董事會副本：總裁只剩一票 · 第 8 / 25 章 · 3000–4000 · 英中對照 · 校訂中

The workshop smelled of wood and machine oil. A distinguished craft worker named Aunt Lin showed the team a photograph of her ancestor building rescue stretchers. The game had placed two identical repair certificates beside the picture. One said the company had supplied the workshop with equipment; the other said the workshop had supplied the company. Their handwriting appeared the same, yet the dates were ten years apart. Xingzhou asked which document was defensible. Aunt Lin answered that neither was complete without the original workshop ledger. A technician brought the ledger from a locked drawer and asked that nobody crush its fragile pages while turning them.

工坊充滿木頭與機油的氣味。傑出的手工師傅林姨展示一張祖先製作救援擔架的照片。遊戲在照片旁放了兩張完全相同的修繕證明，一張說公司提供設備給工坊，另一張卻說工坊提供設備給公司。筆跡看起來一模一樣，日期卻相差十年。行舟問哪一份文件能站得住腳。林姨回答，沒有原始工坊帳冊，兩份都不完整。一名技師從上鎖抽屜拿來帳冊，叮囑大家翻閱時別壓壞脆弱紙張。

Outside, a hose had burst and was spraying water toward stored timber. Guo and Mei shut the valve while Xia checked whether the workshop's emergency pump could start. It could not. The maintenance contractor had reported a lousy result the month before, but the warning had been filed under a less urgent heading. Xingzhou felt guilt when he saw his signature on the final budget reduction. He wanted to blame the contractor for failing to insist on repairs. Aunt Lin said a manager should not misunderstand silence as approval. She had written the problem down three times, and each letter had been returned without a useful answer.

工坊外一條水管爆裂，水正噴向存放的木料。郭黎與阿梅關掉閥門，夏澄檢查緊急水泵能否啟動，答案是否定的。維護承包商上個月就報告結果很差，警告卻被放進不急迫的分類。行舟看見預算削減的最終文件上有自己的簽名，感到愧疚。他想責怪承包商沒有堅持修理。林姨說，主管不該把沉默誤認成同意。她曾寫信三次，每封都被退回，沒有得到有用的答覆。

A young visitor who was in late pregnancy rested beside a window. She explained that narrow workshop stairs could make a small emergency dangerous, especially for someone carrying a child. Xia invited her to offer a suggestion without making her represent everyone in the same situation. The woman pointed to a wider side passage that had been used for moving heavy furniture. Aunt Lin said the passage was sound but needed its old latch repaired. The technician took responsibility for the latch while Guo checked the floor for slipping risks. Xingzhou noticed how different this discussion was from the game's riddle of two certificates: nobody was seeking a perfect slogan, only a usable exit.

一名懷孕後期的年輕訪客在窗邊休息。她解釋工坊狹窄的樓梯，可能讓小事故變成嚴重危險，尤其對懷著孩子的人。夏澄請她提出建議，但不要求她代表所有處境相同的人。女子指向較寬的側通道，過去人們用它搬運大型家具。林姨說通道結構穩固，只需修好舊門閂。技師負責門閂，郭黎檢查地面是否易滑。行舟發現這場討論與兩張證明的謎題截然不同：沒有人尋找完美口號，大家只想找到能用的出口。

The ledger revealed a neglected fact. The workshop had paid for the original stretchers, and a later company donation had replaced only their damaged straps. A corporate summary had turned that partial involvement into complete ownership. Xingzhou asked to correct the record. A warning said doing so would make the company's contribution appear smaller. Xia answered that a smaller true number was better than a larger false one. On the contrary, Aunt Lin did not ask for revenge or praise. She wanted the workshop's craft recognized and the repair contract honored. The game accepted the corrected ownership record only after she used her own witness mark to approve the wording.

帳冊揭露了被忽略的事實。最初的擔架由工坊自己出資，公司後來的捐贈只更換過損壞的綁帶。企業摘要卻把這種部分參與寫成完全擁有。行舟要求更正紀錄。警告說這會使公司貢獻看起來變少。夏澄回答，較小的真實數字勝過較大的虛假數字。相反地，林姨沒有要求報復或稱讚，她只希望工坊手藝被承認、維修契約被履行。直到她以自己的見證記號同意文字，遊戲才接受更正後的所有權紀錄。

The game then launched a test of the emergency passage. Everyone had to cross it with a piece of wood representing the weight of a stretcher. The test showed that the latch caught on the frame when a load was carried at shoulder height. Guo adjusted the hinge, and Xia repeated the test without rushing. After the third attempt, the passage worked. Aunt Lin recorded each stroke of the technician's file so the repair could be repeated. Xingzhou offered to fund a new pump, but the team reminded him that a promise was not the same as a completed purchase. He wrote the price and the date on a public board.

遊戲接著啟動緊急通道測試。每個人都得扛著一塊代表擔架重量的木板穿過通道。測試顯示，負重到肩膀高度時，門閂會卡在門框上。郭黎調整鉸鏈，夏澄不急著重做測試。第三次嘗試後，通道終於運作正常。林姨記下技師用銼刀每一次磨削的動作，讓修理方法可以重複。行舟答應出資購買新水泵，小隊提醒他，承諾不等於已完成採購。他便在公開板上寫下價格與日期。

At the exit Aunt Lin gave Xia a copy of the original workshop ledger. She kept the original herself. Xingzhou had once believed that collecting every document in the executive archive was a sign of efficient management. Now he understood why independent copies mattered. If one cabinet was locked, another person could still challenge its account of events. The next door was stained with salt water, and a distant horn sounded beyond it. A hand-painted notice pointed toward the northern harbor. The team carried the dry records in sealed sleeves and left the heavy timber where it belonged. They would need evidence more than souvenirs.

出口處，林姨交給夏澄一份原始帳冊的副本，原件由她自己保管。行舟過去相信，把所有文件收進主管檔案室代表管理有效率；現在他明白，獨立副本為何重要。若一個櫃子被鎖住，仍有人能對裡面的敘述提出質疑。下一扇門沾著鹽水，門外傳來遠遠的汽笛。手繪公告指向北方港口。小隊把乾燥的紀錄放入防水套，把沉重木料留在原處。他們需要的是證據，而不是紀念品。
