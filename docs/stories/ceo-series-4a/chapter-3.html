大樓背後的山

The Mountain Behind the Tower

董事會副本：總裁只剩一票 · 第 3 / 25 章 · 3000–4000 · 英中對照 · 校訂中

用互動版查詞、朗讀與收藏

The next gate opened onto a mountainous freight terminal that should have been two hours outside the city. It stood beneath the corporate tower as though distance had been folded into a single floor. Cold air crossed the loading yard. A worker named Guo Li raised a barrier to stop a truck whose brakes were making a brutal sound. The driver's eyes did not blink when the warning lights flashed. Xia Cheng ran beside the cab and saw that he could barely breathe. A loose strap had caught against his neck and made him choke. Xingzhou ordered the heavy gate opened, but Guo asked whether its path was clear. The chief executive had to admit he did not know.

下一道門通往一座本應距離城市兩小時車程的山區貨運站。它竟像距離被摺疊一樣，直接接在企業大樓底下。冷風掠過裝卸場。工人代表郭黎升起一道柵欄，攔住煞車發出刺耳巨響的卡車。警示燈閃爍時，司機連眼睛都沒有眨。夏澄跑到駕駛室旁，發現他幾乎無法呼吸。一條鬆脫的綁帶纏在他的頸邊，讓他喘不過氣。行舟命令打開重型大門，郭黎卻問門後是否暢通。總裁只能承認自己不知道。

The game declared an accidental delay and offered to erase it if Xingzhou blamed the driver for neglect. He had signed that kind of report before, after listening only to senior managers. This time Xia asked him to evaluate the actual evidence. The tire marks showed that the truck had tried to stop, and a broken sensor had failed to sound a warning. A mild wind carried fragments of insulation across the yard. Guo held up a tiny twig trapped inside the sensor housing. It had entered through a gap left by an unfinished repair. The fault was not a mystery, but neither was it proof that the driver had been careless.

遊戲把事故稱為意外延誤，並表示只要行舟指控司機疏忽，就能消除延誤紀錄。過去他曾只聽高階主管的一面之詞，便簽過類似報告。這一次，夏澄請他評估實際證據。輪胎痕跡顯示卡車曾試圖煞停，損壞的感應器卻沒有發出警報。微風吹過裝卸場，帶來一小片片隔熱材料。郭黎從感應器外殼裡取出一根小樹枝；它是從未修好的縫隙掉進去的。故障並非神祕事件，但也無法證明司機有錯。

A recorded message from the company intelligence office urged Xingzhou to punish someone quickly, explaining that people wanted revenge after a frightening event. Guo answered that workers wanted safe brakes, not a victim. He showed the composition of the repair team: one mechanic, two temporary loaders, and no trained supervisor on the night shift. Their reports contained a grave warning about missing parts. Xingzhou felt offended when Guo said the company had ignored it. The accusation was true. His own habit was to demand results without asking what resources had been supplied. He made the reports visible to everyone, even though the game called disclosure an act of weakness.

公司情報室的錄音催促行舟盡快懲罰某人，聲稱發生可怕事件後，人們需要報復。郭黎回答，工人需要的是安全的煞車，不是替罪羊。他列出維修隊伍的組成：一名技工、兩名臨時搬運工，夜班沒有受過訓練的主管。他們的報告對缺少零件提出嚴重警告。郭黎說公司一直忽略警告，行舟聽了感到受冒犯，但他知道指控屬實。自己過去總要求成果，卻不問提供了多少資源。他把報告公開，即使遊戲將此稱為軟弱。

The terminal's final challenge was a puzzle about a philosopher who had written, centuries earlier, that an order was not the same as a reason. Xingzhou recognized the quotation from a schoolbook, but Xia refused to let a famous name settle a practical question. She asked the team to reform the loading procedure: nobody would release a vehicle until a mechanic and a driver both checked the sensor. Guo asked whether the policy would survive the game. Xingzhou wrote it on a paper sheet and signed it in his own name. It would be embarrassing to reject it later, but that was precisely why the signature mattered. The driver offered his gratitude without pretending the injury had disappeared.

貨運站最後一道挑戰是一個哲學家的謎題。那位哲學家數百年前寫過，命令不等於理由。行舟在課本讀過引言，但夏澄不願讓名人之言取代現場判斷。她請大家改革裝卸程序：技工與司機共同檢查感應器前，不得放行車輛。郭黎問這項政策在遊戲結束後是否仍有效。行舟把規定寫在紙上，親自簽名。日後若想反悔，這會讓他難堪，而這正是簽名的重要性。受傷司機向他們表示感謝，卻沒有假裝傷勢已經消失。

When the signal changed from red to amber, the whole terminal was allowed to pause. No one received points for moving faster. Instead the screen asked whether the witnesses accepted the repair plan. The driver approved it only after adding a request for medical care. Guo approved it after the mechanic confirmed there would be replacement parts. Xingzhou tried to add a promise of immediate perfection, and Xia crossed it out. A tendency to make impossible promises had helped create the problem. The final vote was not unanimous, but the objections stayed visible, and the barrier rose without threatening anyone.

訊號從紅色轉成琥珀色時，整座貨運站獲准暫停。沒有人因為移動較快而得分。畫面反而詢問見證者是否接受維修方案。司機先提出醫療照護要求才同意；郭黎確認技工能取得替換零件後才同意。行舟想再寫上「立即達到完美」，夏澄卻把那句話劃掉。喜歡做出不可能承諾的傾向，正是問題的一部分。最後的表決並非全數贊成，但反對意見保留了下來，柵欄也在沒有威脅任何人的情況下升起。

Before they left, Guo asked Xingzhou to read one sentence from the company's forgotten safety manual. It said that a manager could not transfer the cost of an unsafe order to the person who obeyed it. Xingzhou read it twice, then asked to meet the mechanics once the city returned to normal. Guo did not offer friendship. He offered a meeting time and the names of the people who should attend. Xia collected the sensor housing and put the twig inside an evidence bag. The game announced a smaller reward than it had promised for punishment. Behind them, a new stairwell appeared. This one led toward a library, not another executive lounge.

離開前，郭黎請行舟讀出公司遺忘已久的安全手冊裡一句話：主管不能把不安全命令的代價轉嫁給服從者。行舟讀了兩遍，然後要求城市恢復正常後與維修技工見面。郭黎沒有表示願意做朋友，只提供會議時間和應到人員名單。夏澄收起感應器外殼，把那根樹枝放進證物袋。遊戲宣布的獎勵，比先前承諾給懲罰行為的獎勵少得多。他們身後出現新的樓梯，這次通往圖書館，而不是另一間主管休息室。
