The Price of a Name

董事會副本：總裁只剩一票 · 第 2 / 25 章 · 3000–4000 · 英中對照 · 校訂中

The next floor looked like a shopping center dressed for a famous actor's autograph session. A prominent poster claimed that privileged customers could leave first. A clock showed a new shift beginning in twelve minutes. Xingzhou wanted to test the claim before letting another crowd move. Xia Cheng suggested asking people what they had already seen. A shopkeeper carved a notch into a wooden counter whenever a gate changed color; his rough record was more useful than the cheerful timetable. An immigrant baker named Farid showed them that his witness mark had stayed active even after a game clerk erased his work permit from the screen. The company owned the counters, but not Farid's name.

下一層看起來像正在舉辦明星簽名會的購物中心。一張醒目的海報聲稱，享有特權的顧客能先離開。時鐘顯示十二分鐘後將開始新一輪輪班。行舟想先測試這句話，再讓另一群人移動。夏澄建議先問人們已經看見什麼。一名店主每次看見門變色，就在木櫃臺刻一道痕；粗糙的紀錄比歡樂的時刻表更有用。移民麵包師法里德指出，即使遊戲職員從畫面刪除他的工作許可，他的見證記號仍然有效。公司擁有櫃臺，卻不擁有法里德的名字。

The first challenge offered convenience in exchange for silence. A clerk in disguise said that anyone who surrendered an objection would receive an elastic wristband and a seat in a private lift. His initial announcement sounded kind, but the small print transferred future speaking rights to the company. Farid called it cruelty dressed as customer service. He remembered losing a small shop after a landlord raised the rent without warning. Xingzhou almost replied that contracts were contracts; he stopped when Xia asked whether a contract made under fear was a free choice. The clerk accused her of mischief. She asked for a written explanation, and the clerk could provide only an advertisement.

第一道挑戰以便利交換沉默。一名偽裝成服務員的遊戲職員宣稱，任何放棄異議的人都能得到彈性腕帶和私人電梯座位。最初的公告聽來親切，細則卻把未來的發言權交給公司。法里德說，這是披著顧客服務外衣的殘酷。他想起自己曾因房東突然漲租而失去小店。行舟差點說出「合約就是合約」，但夏澄問他，在恐懼下簽訂的合約算不算自由選擇，他便住了口。職員指控夏澄搗亂。她要求對方提出書面解釋，職員卻只拿出一張廣告。

Something with a mechanical paw scratched the shutter of a nearby pet shop. The game described the sound as an explosive threat. Mei, who had followed them through the stairs, checked from behind a protective barrier. It was a toy dog whose battery was trapped under the metal door. They disconnected its power and prevented a panic. The clerk's claim had been designed to ruin trust, not protect people. Mei requested feedback from those waiting in the queue. One elderly woman said the wristband cut into her skin. Another remarked that the private lift was too narrow for her chair. Their direct experience was stronger evidence than the bright sales pitch.

附近寵物店的鐵門後，有隻機械爪子不斷刮著門板。遊戲把聲音描述成爆炸威脅。跟著他們走樓梯上來的阿梅在防護欄後查看，發現那是一隻電池卡在鐵門下的玩具狗。他們切斷電源，防止了恐慌。職員的說法是要破壞信任，而不是保護人。阿梅請排隊的人提供回饋。一位老婦人說腕帶勒痛了她的皮膚，另一人則指出私人電梯窄得放不下輪椅。她們的直接經驗，比閃亮的銷售話術更有證據力。

A director from Xingzhou's company arrived with a polished suit and a false promise. If the team supported the privilege program, he said, the company would prosper after the trial. He showed an account listing the names of customers who had accepted the offer. Xingzhou recognized three people who were not in the building. The director insisted that an automated signature was acceptable for their convenience. Xia asked the absent customers to respond through their own witness marks. No response came. She refused to call the silence consent. The director tried to shift blame toward Farid, but Farid had kept the time and place of every conversation in a flour-stained notebook.

瑞霄集團的一名董事穿著筆挺西裝出現，帶來空泛的承諾。他說只要大家支持特權方案，公司就能在試煉後興盛。他展示一份接受提案的顧客名單。行舟認出其中三個人根本不在這棟大樓。董事堅持自動簽名是為了顧客方便。夏澄要求缺席者透過各自的見證記號回應，卻沒有得到任何回覆。她拒絕把沉默算成同意。董事試圖把責任轉到法里德身上，但法里德早已用沾著麵粉的筆記本記下每次交談的時間與地點。

Xingzhou made a public remark: there would be no privileged exit until every person affected could see the terms. The screen warned that such prevention rules would reduce his score. He approved them anyway. A cashier used chalk to divide the queue into small groups, allowing people to check each other's names without exposing private records. A child drew a door beside every witness mark, then gave the drawing to Mei. The shopping floor did not open completely; it created a slow shared stairwell instead. Xingzhou had expected to win through speed. He was beginning to understand that making a decision reversible could be more valuable than finishing first.

行舟公開表示，所有受影響的人都看得到條件以前，不會設立特權出口。畫面警告，這種預防規則會降低他的分數。他仍同意執行。一名收銀員用粉筆把隊伍分成小組，讓大家能彼此核對姓名，不必公布私人紀錄。一個孩子在每枚見證記號旁畫了一扇門，然後把畫交給阿梅。購物樓層沒有完全開放，但出現一條緩慢、人人可用的樓梯。行舟過去以為速度就是勝利，現在開始明白，能夠撤回決定，有時比搶先完成更重要。

At the landing, Xia asked the company director to sign the original offer beside its corrected version. He refused, but the recorded refusal stayed on the wall. Farid took the wooden counter with the carved marks as evidence. The small group's reward was not a better wristband. It was the ability to carry a reliable objection into the next floor. Xingzhou watched workers share information that no chief executive could have collected alone. For years he had paid consultants to tell him how people behaved. This evening a baker, a cleaner, and two customers had taught him more than a hundred reports. An unknown voice announced that the next trial would be held outside the city.

抵達樓梯平臺時，夏澄請那名董事在原始提案與更正版旁簽名。他拒絕了，但拒簽的紀錄留在牆上。法里德把刻有痕跡的木櫃臺帶走作為證據。小隊獲得的獎勵不是更好的腕帶，而是能把可靠的異議紀錄帶到下一層。行舟看著員工分享任何總裁都無法獨自蒐集的資訊。多年來，他花錢請顧問告訴他人們如何行動；今晚，一名麵包師、清潔員和兩名顧客教他的事，比上百份報告還多。陌生的聲音宣布，下一場試煉將在城外舉行。
