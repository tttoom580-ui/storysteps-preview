週年紀念列車

The Anniversary Train

董事會副本：總裁只剩一票 · 第 6 / 25 章 · 3000–4000 · 英中對照 · 校訂中

The sixth floor was a railway station celebrating the company's fortieth anniversary. A conductor stood beside a train made of painted steel, while a campaign poster called the old founder the beginning of a business dynasty. The image was an imitation of a real photograph; somebody had removed the workers who built the original station. Xingzhou knew the photograph from the company museum. He had never asked who was missing. When the game invited him to board a private carriage, he asked the conductor which workers would be allowed on the same train. The answer was that ordinary passengers must perform extra labor before a gate would open. Guo Li refused to call that a fair ticket.

第六層是一座慶祝公司成立四十週年的火車站。一名列車長站在彩繪鋼製列車旁，宣傳海報將老創辦人稱為商業王朝的起點。海報模仿了一張真實照片，卻刪掉了建造原車站的工人。行舟在公司博物館看過照片，從沒問誰被刪除了。遊戲邀請他登上私人車廂，他卻先問列車長，哪些工人能搭同一班車。答案是普通乘客必須額外勞動，才能打開閘門。郭黎拒絕把這稱為公平的車票。

A diligent mechanic named Nuo inspected the engine and found coarse mineral powder around a damaged cord. When she touched the control panel, white foam escaped through a narrow crack. The game's announcer assured everyone that the machine was safe, but the warning sound grew more frequent. Xia asked Nuo to measure its frequency with a pocket recorder. The pulses repeated every eleven seconds, even when the conductor turned the engine off. That meant a separate system was feeding power into the train. Xingzhou wanted to disconnect everything. Nuo said doing so without a plan might complicate the danger. The brave choice, she explained, was to make a careful test.

勤勞的技工小諾檢查引擎，在一條損壞的電纜旁發現粗糙的礦物粉末。她碰到控制面板時，白色泡沫從狹縫冒出。遊戲廣播保證機器安全，警示聲卻越來越頻繁。夏澄請小諾用口袋錄音器測量頻率。即使列車長關閉引擎，聲音仍每十一秒重複一次。這表示另有系統正在向列車供電。行舟想把所有電源都拔掉，小諾說，沒有計畫就這麼做可能讓危險更複雜。她解釋，勇敢的選擇是先仔細測試。

The station offered a test of psychology: choose whether needy passengers deserved seats if they could not earn enough points. Guo objected to the question itself. A seat was a safety measure, not a prize for good behavior. Xingzhou watched a courageous cleaner help an older traveler across the platform without waiting for permission. The traveler had worked for the railway before the company bought it. His account made Xingzhou reflect on the history behind the anniversary banner. The company celebrated forty years of ownership, while the station had served the community for twice that long. Its people were not decorations in a success story.

車站提出一道心理學測驗：無力取得足夠積分的弱勢乘客，是否值得獲得座位。郭黎反對這個問題本身。座位是安全措施，不是良好行為的獎品。行舟看見一名勇敢的清潔員不等許可，就扶著年長旅客穿過月臺。老人曾在公司收購車站以前，替鐵路工作。他的故事讓行舟反思週年布條背後的歷史。公司慶祝持有資產四十年，車站服務社區的時間卻是它的兩倍。這些人不是成功故事的裝飾。

Xia and Nuo devised an evaluation plan. They would isolate the extra power source, check the brake lines, and board the passengers in small groups. Guo asked the cleaners what assistance they needed; Xingzhou asked the directors to fund proper tools rather than offer speeches. One director said such a process lacked excitement. Mei replied that emergencies were not entertainment. By the third test, the warning pulse had stopped. Nuo confirmed the engine could move at reduced speed, but she would not authorize a full journey yet. The game tried to punish her caution. Xingzhou recorded her decision as the responsible technical judgment, not a failure to obey.

夏澄與小諾設計一套評估計畫：隔離額外電源、檢查煞車線，再分小組讓乘客上車。郭黎詢問清潔員需要什麼協助；行舟則要求董事出資購買正確工具，不要只發表演說。有董事抱怨這樣缺乏刺激感。阿梅回答，緊急事件不是娛樂。第三次測試時，警示脈衝終於停止。小諾確認引擎能低速移動，卻尚未同意整段行程。遊戲試圖懲罰她的謹慎。行舟把她的判斷記為負責任的技術決策，而不是不服從命令。

The train left with the doors unlocked and a clear return platform. Xingzhou could have claimed the successful departure as a corporate achievement. Instead he asked Guo to read the names of everyone who had repaired the engine, swept the platform, checked the passengers, and recorded the tests. The founder's picture remained above the carriage, but the missing workers' names appeared below it. The screen offered a small reward for shared credit. More important, the passengers had remained able to refuse boarding. Xingzhou kept that detail in his notes. In his old reports, success meant that the train departed on schedule. Tonight it also meant that people could choose not to board.

列車出發時，車門沒有上鎖，返回月臺也保持暢通。行舟本可以把順利發車寫成企業成就，卻請郭黎念出修理引擎、清掃月臺、照顧乘客與記錄測試的所有人名字。創辦人的照片仍掛在車廂上方，被刪除工人的名字則出現在下方。畫面提供少量共享功勞的獎勵。更重要的是，乘客仍能拒絕上車。行舟把這項細節記入筆記。在他過去的報告裡，成功意味著列車準時出發；今晚，成功也意味著人們可以選擇不上車。

When the last carriage entered the tunnel, Xia noticed the station's emergency log carried an unfamiliar seal: CONTINUITY ENGINE, PRIVATE DEMONSTRATION. Nuo recognized it from a discontinued research contract. The company had once paid for simulations that measured how quickly managers made decisions under pressure. The game might be using those records, although nobody knew how a simulation had become a physical city. Xingzhou asked for the old contract's location. Nuo said the archive had been moved to the northern district. Another gate appeared, showing snow and a distant helicopter landing field. Guo tightened his coat. Their next lesson would not happen in a comfortable boardroom.

最後一節車廂進入隧道時，夏澄發現車站緊急紀錄上有個陌生印章：「營運持續引擎，私人示範」。小諾認出那是已終止研究合約的名稱。公司過去曾資助模擬實驗，測量主管在壓力下多快能做出決策。遊戲可能使用了那些紀錄，雖然還沒有人知道模擬如何變成真實城市。行舟詢問舊合約存放何處，小諾說檔案已搬到北區。另一道門出現，門後是積雪與遠方的直升機降落場。郭黎把外套拉緊。他們下一課不會發生在舒適的董事會議室。
