董事長的出口

The Chairman's Exit

董事會副本：總裁只剩一票 · 第 1 / 25 章 · 3000–4000 · 英中對照 · 校訂中

At seven in the evening, He Xingzhou stood at the copper doors of the Celestial Opera Center, where his company was holding its annual shareholder assembly. A youthful host rehearsed a slogan about confidence while a counter above the entrance displayed the expected profit. He had approved both the entertainment and the speech without reading their details. Then every phone showed the same white sentence: BOARDROOM DUNGEON ACTIVATED. The windows became dark mirrors. An electronic voice announced that company shares would determine the route to safety. Xingzhou owned forty-two percent of the voting stock. He was not frightened by numbers. He was frightened when the crowd cheered them.

晚上七點，賀行舟站在天穹歌劇中心的銅門前。瑞霄控股正在此舉行年度股東大會。一名年輕主持人排練著充滿信心的標語，入口上方的計數器則顯示預期獲利。行舟先前批准了娛樂節目與演講，卻沒有讀過細節。突然，每支手機都出現同一句白字：「董事會副本已啟動。」窗戶變成深色鏡面。電子聲宣布，公司股份將決定通往安全地點的路線。行舟持有百分之四十二的投票權。他不害怕數字；讓他害怕的是人群竟然為那些數字歡呼。

A woman carrying a broom pushed through the lobby and asked him to stop the revolving door. She was Mei, a night cleaner whose name was absent from the guest list. A bottle of acid cleaner had broken near the ticket desk, and the fumes were spreading toward a child. Xingzhou reached for the controls, but the display called the cleaning staff NONSHAREHOLDERS and locked the service entrance. A security captain dismissed Mei as a coward for refusing to send the child through the smoke. Xingzhou saw the child's strained facial expression and told the captain to step aside. Ownership was already interfering with the most ordinary rescue.

一名提著掃帚的女子穿過大廳，請行舟停止旋轉門。她叫阿梅，是夜班清潔員，來賓名單上沒有她的名字。售票櫃臺附近有瓶酸性清潔劑破裂，刺鼻氣味正往一名孩子所在的位置飄去。行舟伸手操作門禁，畫面卻將清潔人員標成「非股東」，鎖住服務入口。一名保全隊長罵阿梅是膽小鬼，因為她不願讓孩子穿過煙霧。行舟看見孩子緊繃的臉部表情，要保全退開。股權制度已經開始妨礙最普通的救援。

A safety auditor named Xia Cheng approached with a folded evacuation map. She said the official screen was persuasive but wrong: its bright route crossed a maintenance hall blocked by fallen scenery. Xingzhou asked how she could be certain. Xia did not claim certainty. She showed him a handwritten record of door usage from that afternoon and a photograph of the blocked passage. The city's emergency ministry had stamped the original plan, yet the game had ignored a later repair notice. Xia called the transformation an unexplained phenomenon, not permission to surrender judgment. She asked Xingzhou to help construct a temporary barrier around the chemical spill.

職業安全稽核員夏澄帶著摺好的疏散圖靠近。她說官方畫面很有說服力，卻是錯的：發亮的路線會穿過被倒下布景堵住的維修廊道。行舟問她怎麼確定。夏澄沒有假裝自己能確定一切。她拿出當日下午的手寫門禁使用紀錄，還有受阻通道的照片。市府緊急應變部門曾在原圖蓋章，但遊戲忽略了後來的維修通知。夏澄稱這是無法解釋的現象，而不是放棄判斷的理由。她請行舟協助在化學液體旁搭建臨時圍欄。

The game offered Xingzhou an immediate vote: OPEN EXECUTIVE GATE FOR SHAREHOLDERS. His forty-two percent counted as hundreds of ordinary votes. Investors crowded near him and urged him to press YES. Instead he asked Mei whether the child could breathe. She said the air beside the orchestra stairs was clear. They used folding chairs and advertising boards to construct a safe channel. Several ushers agreed to cooperate. When Xingzhou delayed the vote, a warning declared that the executive gate would close in ninety seconds. A shady broker offered to buy other people's votes. Xia wrote the offer down rather than arguing with the broker.

遊戲給行舟一項立即表決：「為股東開啟主管出口。」他的百分之四十二股份被換算成數百張普通選票。投資人擠到他身邊，催促他按下贊成。行舟反而問阿梅孩子能不能呼吸。阿梅說管弦樂團樓梯旁的空氣比較乾淨。他們用折疊椅與廣告板搭出安全通道，幾名引導員也同意合作。行舟延後投票時，警告聲宣稱主管出口九十秒後就會關閉。一名可疑仲介提出要買其他人的票。夏澄沒有與對方爭吵，只把這項提議記了下來。

Xia noticed a second line beneath the warning, written in letters so small that most people missed it. Every person inside a trial area possessed one permanent witness mark. Shares could increase the weight of a proposal, but they could not erase a witness or remove a recorded objection. Xingzhou was used to treating the room as a table of assets. This rule made a cleaner's testimony impossible to purchase outright. He asked the system to display the individual marks. One appeared beside Mei's name, another beside the child's mother, and one beside every worker. The shareholders were suddenly less interested in a public count.

夏澄注意到警告下方還有一行極小的字，大多數人都沒有看見。試煉區裡每個人都擁有一枚永久見證記號。股份能增加提案的票重，卻不能抹除見證者，也不能刪掉已記錄的反對意見。行舟過去總習慣把會議室看成資產表，但這條規則讓清潔員的證詞無法被直接買斷。他要求系統顯示每個人的記號。阿梅的名字旁有一枚，孩子母親旁有一枚，每位員工也有一枚。股東們突然對公開計數失去了興趣。

By the time the doors shook again, the child had reached fresh air, and Mei had marked the danger with a red cloth. Xingzhou refused the executive vote. The screen took away his private exit and announced a loss of twenty points. He had never lost so much authority in a single minute. Yet the ordinary stairwell remained open because no vote had sealed it. Xia did not thank him for being noble. She asked him to copy the witness rule before the screen changed. He used the back of his prepared speech. For the first time that evening, the chief executive wrote down something he had not decided himself.

門再次震動時，孩子已走到空氣清新的地方，阿梅也用紅布標出危險區域。行舟拒絕了主管出口的提案。畫面取消他的私人出口，宣布扣除二十分。他從未在一分鐘內失去這麼多權力。但普通樓梯因為沒有被表決封閉，仍維持開放。夏澄沒有稱讚他高尚，而是請他趁畫面改變前抄下見證規則。他拿出準備好的演講稿，在背面寫字。那是他當晚第一次記錄一件不是由自己決定的事。
