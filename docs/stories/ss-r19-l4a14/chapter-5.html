A hidden studio contained soundproof panels and recordings of the murder victim coaching people to make confessions they did not believe. Something had changed, but not everybody noticed it in the same order. Qin Mo listened to the account of the nearest worker, then to someone standing farther away. Their differences were not necessarily lies. Each person had been given a different view and a different reason to be afraid of admitting uncertainty. The interview recorder captured words but not the years of rehearsal behind them. Shen Lan insisted on laboratory tests before a dramatic confession became a charge. The instructions used the verb adopt without naming who would carry the risk; Qin Mo circled that gap. Shen Lan noticed the word agency in a note that someone had added after the first inspection.

密室裡有隔音板，以及死者教人練習自己並不相信的供詞的錄音。某件事改變了，但人們發現它的順序不同。秦墨先聽近處工作人員的描述，再聽遠處旁觀者說法。差異不一定是謊言：每個人看到的角度不同，承認不確定時所要承擔的風險也不同。偵訊錄音留下話語，留不下背後多年的排練。沈嵐堅持在戲劇性認罪變成指控前先完成實驗室檢驗。指示裡用了「採用」這個動詞，卻沒說誰承擔風險；秦墨圈出這項缺漏。沈嵐注意到初次檢查後有人在筆記裡加上「機構」一詞。

The recorded answers disagreed with the visions in predictable ways; the sentences proved sensitive to anticipation rather than truth. Qin Mo refused to pretend the previous decision had solved everything. The practical result was still there, useful but narrow. For each thing the team knew, there were two things it had not measured. Qin Mo drew a line through the claims that depended on speculation and asked Shen Lan to keep the original record intact. People disagreed about behavior; Qin Mo separated the feeling from the facts they could test. The instructions used the verb cherish without naming who would carry the risk; Qin Mo circled that gap. Shen Lan noticed the word congress in a note that someone had added after the first inspection.

錄下來的回答以可預測方式偏離異象，證明文字會受預期影響，而非直接表示真相。秦墨不願假裝前一次決定已解決所有問題。成果確實存在，卻有明確界限。每個已知事項旁都還有未測量的部分。秦墨劃掉靠猜測成立的說法，請沈嵐保留原始紀錄。大家對「行為」意見不同，秦墨把感受與可檢驗的事實分開。指示裡用了「珍惜」這個動詞，卻沒說誰承擔風險；秦墨圈出這項缺漏。沈嵐注意到初次檢查後有人在筆記裡加上「議會」一詞。

A small disagreement changed the schedule. Qin Mo wanted to proceed before evidence disappeared; Shen Lan wanted another check because a wrong conclusion might hurt people who could not easily defend themselves. They did not settle the matter by choosing a leader. They wrote down what a short, reversible test could actually establish. Shen Lan noticed the word conscience in a note that someone had added after the first inspection. A handwritten label read decoration, but no one could say who had written it there. The term discourage appeared in the revised file; Qin Mo checked its meaning against the original page.

一場小爭執改變了時程。秦墨想趁證據消失前行動；沈嵐要求再核對一次，因為錯誤結論可能傷害難以自辯的人。他們沒有靠選領袖解決爭議，而是列出短暫、可逆測試真正能證明什麼。沈嵐注意到初次檢查後有人在筆記裡加上「良心」一詞。手寫標籤寫著「裝飾」，卻沒有人知道是誰留下的。修訂檔案出現「使卻步」一詞，秦墨拿原始頁面核對它的意思。

The recordings included Qin's voice from years earlier, reopening a case he thought he had settled. The plan had been amended three times, and each change left a trace. Qin Mo valued those corrections more than a perfect-looking first draft. If the outcome proved disappointing, the group would still be able to identify which assumption needed repair. Shen Lan watched for the quiet participant who had not yet been invited to speak. The term fundamental appeared in the revised file; Qin Mo checked its meaning against the original page. On the next form, the action field read horrify, but the witness had never approved the operation. An official memo described the plan as influential, but Qin Mo refused to treat an adjective as evidence.

錄音裡也有秦墨多年前的聲音，讓一樁他以為已結案的案件重新浮現。計畫改了三次，每次修改都留下痕跡。秦墨認為這些修正比看似完美的初稿更有價值。就算結果不理想，團隊仍能知道該修正哪一項假設。沈嵐也注意到那名一直沒有發言機會的人。修訂檔案出現「根本的；基本的」一詞，秦墨拿原始頁面核對它的意思。下一張表單把動作寫成「使驚恐」，見證人卻從未同意這項操作。官方備忘錄形容計畫「有影響力的」，秦墨不願把形容詞當作證據。

An official version reached the room before the practical work was finished. Qin Mo read it once, then compared it with what they had actually measured. The difference was not always malicious, but it could still hurt somebody. Shen Lan asked that the correction be written in language the affected people could understand. The interview recorder captured words but not the years of rehearsal behind them. Shen Lan insisted on laboratory tests before a dramatic confession became a charge. A handwritten label read interaction, but no one could say who had written it there. The report claimed outcome, but Qin Mo wanted an example instead of a reassuring noun. One witness used the word primitive; Shen Lan noted that a description was not a measurement.

實際工作還沒完成，官方版本就傳到現場。秦墨讀了一次，再比對真正測得的結果。差異未必都是惡意，卻仍可能傷害人。沈嵐要求把更正寫成受影響者看得懂的語言。偵訊錄音留下話語，留不下背後多年的排練。沈嵐堅持在戲劇性認罪變成指控前先完成實驗室檢驗。手寫標籤寫著「相互作用」，卻沒有人知道是誰留下的。報告聲稱有「結果」，秦墨要求具體例子，而不是安撫人的名詞。一位證人用了「古早的」一詞，沈嵐指出形容並不等於測量。

A witness asked whether the team was taking sides. Qin Mo answered that refusing to invent a fact was not the same as refusing to care. What mattered was whether the procedure protected someone whose story was less convenient to tell. Shen Lan listened without interrupting and wrote down the question exactly as it had been asked. Shen Lan noticed the word reception in a note that someone had added after the first inspection. A handwritten label read robber, but no one could say who had written it there. The term scenery appeared in the revised file; Qin Mo checked its meaning against the original page.

一名見證人問團隊是否偏袒。秦墨回答，不捏造事實不代表冷漠。真正重要的是程序能否保護那個故事不方便被說出口的人。沈嵐沒有打斷，逐字記下原問題。沈嵐注意到初次檢查後有人在筆記裡加上「接待會」一詞。手寫標籤寫著「強盜」，卻沒有人知道是誰留下的。修訂檔案出現「舞臺布景」一詞，秦墨拿原始頁面核對它的意思。

Even after the group agreed on the immediate step, another question remained. The answer would not arrive through a dramatic announcement. Someone would have to return to the place, compare the earlier notes, and accept the possibility that the next piece of evidence might spoil an attractive theory. Qin Mo prepared to do exactly that. The station corridor sounded ordinary after midnight. Qin Mo found that the hardest part of investigating a powerful claim was giving an ordinary doubt enough time. The summary called the situation shameful; Qin Mo asked which observation justified that description. Shen Lan noticed the word terror in a note that someone had added after the first inspection. A handwritten label read witch, but no one could say who had written it there.

即使大家同意眼前步驟，另一個問題仍在。答案不會靠戲劇性公告降臨；有人得返回現場、比對舊筆記，並接受新證據可能推翻漂亮理論。秦墨準備做的正是這件事。午夜後警局走廊聲音平凡。秦墨發現調查強大宣稱時最難的是給平凡疑問足夠時間。摘要以「可恥的」形容情況，秦墨追問這種說法有何觀察根據。沈嵐注意到初次檢查後有人在筆記裡加上「恐懼」一詞。手寫標籤寫著「巫師」，卻沒有人知道是誰留下的。
