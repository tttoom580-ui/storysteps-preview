The Script Beneath the Floor

Investigators recovered a carbon-copy script beneath a studio floor. Its page numbers showed that several people had rehearsed the same accusation. Something had changed, but not everybody noticed it in the same order. Qin Mo listened to the account of the nearest worker, then to someone standing farther away. Their differences were not necessarily lies. Each person had been given a different view and a different reason to be afraid of admitting uncertainty. The station corridor sounded ordinary after midnight. Qin Mo found that the hardest part of investigating a powerful claim was giving an ordinary doubt enough time. Shen Lan wrote acceptance in the margin and asked how the team would recognize it in practice. An official memo described the plan as ambitious, but Qin Mo refused to treat an adjective as evidence.

調查人員在攝影棚地板下找到複寫劇本，頁碼顯示多人練過同一句指控。某件事改變了，但人們發現它的順序不同。秦墨先聽近處工作人員的描述，再聽遠處旁觀者說法。差異不一定是謊言：每個人看到的角度不同，承認不確定時所要承擔的風險也不同。午夜後警局走廊聲音平凡。秦墨發現調查強大宣稱時最難的是給平凡疑問足夠時間。沈嵐在頁邊寫下「接受；同意」，追問團隊實際上如何辨認。官方備忘錄形容計畫「有企圖心的」，秦墨不願把形容詞當作證據。

She recognized the phrase THE KILLER IS YOU as a line from an unfinished rehearsal script. Qin Mo refused to pretend the previous decision had solved everything. The practical result was still there, useful but narrow. For each thing the team knew, there were two things it had not measured. Qin Mo drew a line through the claims that depended on speculation and asked Shen Lan to keep the original record intact. The term atmosphere appeared in the revised file; Qin Mo checked its meaning against the original page. One witness used the word authentic; Shen Lan noted that a description was not a measurement. Shen Lan repeated the instruction to classify, then asked whether anyone had the right to refuse.

她認出「兇手是你」出自一份尚未完成的排練劇本。秦墨不願假裝前一次決定已解決所有問題。成果確實存在，卻有明確界限。每個已知事項旁都還有未測量的部分。秦墨劃掉靠猜測成立的說法，請沈嵐保留原始紀錄。修訂檔案出現「氣氛」一詞，秦墨拿原始頁面核對它的意思。一位證人用了「真實可靠的」一詞，沈嵐指出形容並不等於測量。沈嵐重述「分類」的指示，再問當事人是否有權拒絕。

A small disagreement changed the schedule. Qin Mo wanted to proceed before evidence disappeared; Shen Lan wanted another check because a wrong conclusion might hurt people who could not easily defend themselves. They did not settle the matter by choosing a leader. They wrote down what a short, reversible test could actually establish. Shen Lan repeated the instruction to differ, then asked whether anyone had the right to refuse. The report claimed dignity, but Qin Mo wanted an example instead of a reassuring noun. Shen Lan noticed the word embarrass in a note that someone had added after the first inspection.

一場小爭執改變了時程。秦墨想趁證據消失前行動；沈嵐要求再核對一次，因為錯誤結論可能傷害難以自辯的人。他們沒有靠選領袖解決爭議，而是列出短暫、可逆測試真正能證明什麼。沈嵐重述「有所不同」的指示，再問當事人是否有權拒絕。報告聲稱有「尊嚴」，秦墨要求具體例子，而不是安撫人的名詞。沈嵐注意到初次檢查後有人在筆記裡加上「使人難堪」一詞。

The final page addressed Qin by name and asked him to investigate the innocent driver he had helped convict. The plan had been amended three times, and each change left a trace. Qin Mo valued those corrections more than a perfect-looking first draft. If the outcome proved disappointing, the group would still be able to identify which assumption needed repair. Shen Lan watched for the quiet participant who had not yet been invited to speak. Shen Lan noticed the word facility in a note that someone had added after the first inspection. An official memo described the plan as genuine, but Qin Mo refused to treat an adjective as evidence. The summary called the situation offensive; Qin Mo asked which observation justified that description.

最後一頁直接點名秦墨，要他重新調查自己曾協助定罪的無辜司機。計畫改了三次，每次修改都留下痕跡。秦墨認為這些修正比看似完美的初稿更有價值。就算結果不理想，團隊仍能知道該修正哪一項假設。沈嵐也注意到那名一直沒有發言機會的人。沈嵐注意到初次檢查後有人在筆記裡加上「設施」一詞。官方備忘錄形容計畫「真實的」，秦墨不願把形容詞當作證據。摘要以「惡意的」形容情況，秦墨追問這種說法有何觀察根據。

An official version reached the room before the practical work was finished. Qin Mo read it once, then compared it with what they had actually measured. The difference was not always malicious, but it could still hurt somebody. Shen Lan asked that the correction be written in language the affected people could understand. The station corridor sounded ordinary after midnight. Qin Mo found that the hardest part of investigating a powerful claim was giving an ordinary doubt enough time. The term option appeared in the revised file; Qin Mo checked its meaning against the original page. Shen Lan noticed the word perfume in a note that someone had added after the first inspection. A handwritten label read promotion, but no one could say who had written it there.

實際工作還沒完成，官方版本就傳到現場。秦墨讀了一次，再比對真正測得的結果。差異未必都是惡意，卻仍可能傷害人。沈嵐要求把更正寫成受影響者看得懂的語言。午夜後警局走廊聲音平凡。秦墨發現調查強大宣稱時最難的是給平凡疑問足夠時間。修訂檔案出現「選項」一詞，秦墨拿原始頁面核對它的意思。沈嵐注意到初次檢查後有人在筆記裡加上「香水」一詞。手寫標籤寫著「行銷」，卻沒有人知道是誰留下的。

A witness asked whether the team was taking sides. Qin Mo answered that refusing to invent a fact was not the same as refusing to care. What mattered was whether the procedure protected someone whose story was less convenient to tell. Shen Lan listened without interrupting and wrote down the question exactly as it had been asked. A handwritten label read publisher, but no one could say who had written it there. The term refugee appeared in the revised file; Qin Mo checked its meaning against the original page. Shen Lan noticed the word rescue in a note that someone had added after the first inspection.

一名見證人問團隊是否偏袒。秦墨回答，不捏造事實不代表冷漠。真正重要的是程序能否保護那個故事不方便被說出口的人。沈嵐沒有打斷，逐字記下原問題。手寫標籤寫著「出版社」，卻沒有人知道是誰留下的。修訂檔案出現「難民」一詞，秦墨拿原始頁面核對它的意思。沈嵐注意到初次檢查後有人在筆記裡加上「救援」一詞。

The last page of the script carried a resignation note from a studio assistant and a drawing of a Christmas stocking hung upside down over an interrogation chair. Qin Mo turned it slowly beneath the lamp. The page did not identify a killer. It did something worse: it reproduced a line he himself had written years earlier when a coerced confession helped convict an innocent driver. Whoever had assembled this file wanted to transform his old guilt into the next headline. Shen Lan touched the paper's lower corner. There, in a different hand, was a date one day before the speech coach's death. 'Someone prepared this long before the murder,' she said. Qin looked toward the dark recording booth. A red light blinked on, although the power had been cut.

劇本最後一頁附著攝影棚助理的辭職便條，還畫了倒掛在偵訊椅上的聖誕長襪。秦墨在燈下慢慢轉動紙張。它沒有指認兇手，反而做了更可怕的事：重現他多年前在一樁逼供案件中寫下的句子，當時無辜司機因此入獄。編造這份文件的人，想把他的舊罪咎轉化成下一則新聞標題。沈嵐摸了摸紙張下角，那裡用另一種筆跡寫著日期：語言教練死亡前一天。「有人在命案之前就準備好了。」她說。秦墨看向黑暗的錄音間。明明電源已切斷，紅燈卻亮了。
