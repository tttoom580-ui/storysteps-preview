The Words Above His Head

我看得見兇手頭上的最後一句台詞 · 第 1 / 25 章 · 3000–4000 · 英中對照 · 校訂中

THE KILLER IS YOU. Qin Mo had worked seventeen murder investigations without seeing words float above a suspect's head. Now the sentence hung in the interrogation room like an accusation written in acid, bright enough that he could not look away. The man across the table was a theatre usher who had attended the morning assembly at the opera house. His hands shook, but the new evidence did not prove murder: there was a receipt for two coffees and a badge recording one late exit. Qin blinked. The sentence vanished. 'You think I'm lying,' the man said. Qin knew what a frightened person sounded like, and he knew how often fear had been mistaken for guilt. He turned off his recorder before asking the question that mattered. 'Who taught you those words?'

「兇手是你。」秦墨辦過十七起命案，從沒看過句子浮在嫌疑人頭上。現在那行字像用酸液寫在偵訊室的指控，亮得讓他移不開眼。桌子對面的男人是歌劇院引座員，早上參加過員工集會。他的手在發抖，但新證據還不能證明殺人：只有兩杯咖啡的收據，以及一筆逾時離場的證件紀錄。秦墨眨眼，文字消失了。「你以為我在說謊。」男人說。秦墨聽得出恐懼，也知道恐懼有多常被誤認為有罪。他在問出真正重要的問題前關掉錄音機：「是誰教你那句話的？」

An officer arrived with a broom to sweep away the broken glass beneath the suspect's chair. Qin stopped him; the shards were evidence, not an inconvenience. He and Shen Lan could construct a timeline from the theatre's entrance logs, but the last twenty minutes were missing. The usher had no reason to cooperate after Qin stared at him as though an invisible accusation were proof. Qin tried again, this time offering the man a chance to explain the missing period without being told what answer the police expected. 'We rehearsed a line,' the usher whispered. 'The coach asked me to look straight at the person across the table and say it.' The sentence returned above his head, exactly the same, and Qin finally understood the danger of seeing it.

一名警員拿著掃帚進來，要掃掉嫌疑人椅子下的碎玻璃。秦墨阻止他：碎片是證據，不是礙事的垃圾。他和沈嵐可以從劇院進出紀錄建構時間線，但最後二十分鐘不見了。秦墨先前用看著有罪者的眼神盯著引座員，對方沒有理由合作。他重新問話，這次不預設警方想聽的答案。「我們排練一句台詞。」引座員低聲說，「教練叫我看著桌子對面的人，直接說出來。」同一句話再次浮在男人頭上。秦墨終於明白看見它有多危險。

A copper stage ticket lay on the counter with a hole punched in the wrong corner. The usher said it belonged to the dead speech coach, who always entered through the side passage. Qin asked whether the coach had been frightened. 'He called me a coward because I wouldn't repeat one sentence,' the usher replied. Qin looked for the words above the man's head. Nothing appeared. His ability seemed to have its own timing, and the absence of a vision offered no comfort. Shen Lan packed the ticket for fingerprint comparison, then pointed to the ticket machine's mechanical clock. Its time was four minutes behind the building's digital system. That difference placed the coach near the side door when the police thought he had already left.

櫃臺上有一張銅色舞台票，打孔位置錯了一角。引座員說票屬於死去的語言教練，對方總從側門出入。秦墨問教練是否害怕過。「他罵我是膽小鬼，因為我不肯重複一句話。」引座員回答。秦墨再看男人頭上，卻什麼字都沒有。這能力似乎有自己的時機，沒有異象並不代表安全。沈嵐把票裝袋準備比對指紋，又指向售票機的機械鐘：它比建築物的數位系統慢了四分鐘。這個差異顯示，警方原以為教練已離開時，他其實仍在側門附近。

The theatre's director, whose job was to entertain the public, insisted the rehearsal was ordinary work. Qin asked why the suspect's facial expression had changed each time the accusation was repeated. A rehearsal recording answered part of the question: the speech coach had trained him to pause, lower his eyes, and look suddenly at the listener. The director tried to interfere with the playback, saying the recording was proprietary. Shen Lan requested the original file under the proper warrant and kept the director's objection in the report. 'You can own a performance,' she told him. 'You cannot own the physical facts of a murder.' Qin noticed that the sentence above the usher appeared only when the director stepped close enough to be heard.

劇院娛樂部主管堅稱排練只是普通工作。秦墨問為何引座員每次重複指控時，臉部表情都會改變。排練錄音解答了一部分：語言教練教他停頓、垂眼，再突然望向聽眾。主管企圖干擾播放，說錄音屬於商業資產。沈嵐依合法程序申請原檔，並把他的異議記進報告。「表演可以屬於你，」她說，「謀殺的客觀事實不能。」秦墨注意到，主管走到足夠靠近的位置時，引座員頭上的句子才會出現。

A ministry official phoned during the search, nervous that the opera house might close before a visiting delegation arrived. The official's argument sounded persuasive: thousands of tickets had been sold, and the city could not afford another scandal. Qin said a closed room could be reopened after inspection, whereas contaminated evidence could not be made innocent again. Shen Lan took a photograph of the floor before the technicians moved the lights. In the reflection, a figure seemed to stand behind the speech coach's chair. Nobody had noticed that figure in the original photograph because the camera was angled toward the stage. Qin asked the officer to preserve every version rather than choose the most dramatic one.

搜索期間，部會官員打電話來，擔心歌劇院在外賓抵達前被關閉。他的論點聽來很有說服力：數千張票已賣出，城市受不了另一場醜聞。秦墨回答，封閉房間檢查後可以重開，遭污染的證據卻無法重新變乾淨。技師移動燈具前，沈嵐先拍下地板。反光裡，語言教練的椅子後似乎站著一道人影。原照片把鏡頭朝向舞台，所以誰也沒注意到。秦墨要求保留所有版本，而非只挑最戲劇化的一張。

The phenomenon repeated just once more. As the usher walked toward the holding room, he turned by the service route and looked directly at Qin. THE KILLER IS YOU returned above his head, bright as before. But an employee waiting near the staircase said the coach had taught the usher that line several weeks earlier, long before the killings. A shady acting school had paid for the rehearsals, promising students that a convincing confession would secure them roles in a crime drama. Qin wrote down the school's address without assuming it explained the murder. He had one real piece of evidence now: somebody had prepared a sentence designed to make him stop investigating and start defending himself.

這種現象又出現了一次。引座員沿服務動線走向留置室時，轉身直望秦墨，頭上再次浮出「兇手是你」，與先前一樣明亮。但樓梯旁的員工說，教練好幾週前就教過他這句台詞，遠在命案之前。一家可疑的演員訓練所支付了排練費，承諾學生只要認罪演得夠真就能接到犯罪劇角色。秦墨記下學校地址，卻不假定它就能解釋謀殺。現在他至少有一項真正證據：有人事先準備了這句話，要讓他停止調查，轉而替自己辯護。

The city police liked the slogan TRUST YOUR INSTINCTS. Qin had lived with that youthful confidence until one wrong confession cost a stranger six years. He now wrote down the precise usage of his impossible new ability: the words appeared before a person spoke, changed when the person expected a different question, and disappeared before anyone else could see them. Such observations could guide a search but could not replace a witness. Shen Lan arrived with the autopsy folder and found him staring at the blank wall. 'There are no words up there,' she said. 'There were,' he replied. She did not laugh. She opened the file to a photograph of the dead speech coach and pointed to a note beside his hand: FINAL LINE, REHEARSE TWICE.

市警局喜歡的口號是「相信直覺」。秦墨年輕時也曾充滿這種信心，直到一次錯誤供詞讓陌生人失去六年。他此刻寫下怪異能力的確切用法：文字在對方開口前出現，對方預期問題改變時也會變化，並且在其他人看見前消失。這些觀察可以指引搜尋，卻不能取代證人。沈嵐帶著驗屍卷宗進來，發現他盯著空牆。「牆上什麼都沒有。」她說。「剛才有。」他答。她沒有笑，而是翻到死去語言教練的照片，指著死者手邊的便條：「最後一句，排練兩次。」
