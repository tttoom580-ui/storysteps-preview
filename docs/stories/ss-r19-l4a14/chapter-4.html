Qin wrote questions on cards and let another officer conduct the interview without telling him which suspect had appeared in the first vision. Something had changed, but not everybody noticed it in the same order. Qin Mo listened to the account of the nearest worker, then to someone standing farther away. Their differences were not necessarily lies. Each person had been given a different view and a different reason to be afraid of admitting uncertainty. The station corridor sounded ordinary after midnight. Qin Mo found that the hardest part of investigating a powerful claim was giving an ordinary doubt enough time. Shen Lan noticed the word accomplish in a note that someone had added after the first inspection. A handwritten label read amuse, but no one could say who had written it there.

秦墨先寫下問題卡，再讓另一名警員主持問話，沒有透露首次異象來自誰。某件事改變了，但人們發現它的順序不同。秦墨先聽近處工作人員的描述，再聽遠處旁觀者說法。差異不一定是謊言：每個人看到的角度不同，承認不確定時所要承擔的風險也不同。午夜後警局走廊聲音平凡。秦墨發現調查強大宣稱時最難的是給平凡疑問足夠時間。沈嵐注意到初次檢查後有人在筆記裡加上「成就；完成」一詞。手寫標籤寫著「逗樂；令人發笑」，卻沒有人知道是誰留下的。

Shen Lan proposed blinded questioning so Qin's gift would not shape every interview and contaminate the evidence. Qin Mo refused to pretend the previous decision had solved everything. The practical result was still there, useful but narrow. For each thing the team knew, there were two things it had not measured. Qin Mo drew a line through the claims that depended on speculation and asked Shen Lan to keep the original record intact. The report claimed approval, but Qin Mo wanted an example instead of a reassuring noun. On the next form, the action field read bounce, but the witness had never approved the operation. An official memo described the plan as continual, but Qin Mo refused to treat an adjective as evidence.

沈嵐建議採盲測問話，以免秦墨的能力污染整個調查。秦墨不願假裝前一次決定已解決所有問題。成果確實存在，卻有明確界限。每個已知事項旁都還有未測量的部分。秦墨劃掉靠猜測成立的說法，請沈嵐保留原始紀錄。報告聲稱有「核准」，秦墨要求具體例子，而不是安撫人的名詞。下一張表單把動作寫成「彈跳」，見證人卻從未同意這項操作。官方備忘錄形容計畫「持續的」，秦墨不願把形容詞當作證據。

A small disagreement changed the schedule. Qin Mo wanted to proceed before evidence disappeared; Shen Lan wanted another check because a wrong conclusion might hurt people who could not easily defend themselves. They did not settle the matter by choosing a leader. They wrote down what a short, reversible test could actually establish. A handwritten label read disappoint, but no one could say who had written it there. The term earnest appeared in the revised file; Qin Mo checked its meaning against the original page. Shen Lan pointed to the embassy in the inventory and asked why its location had changed.

一場小爭執改變了時程。秦墨想趁證據消失前行動；沈嵐要求再核對一次，因為錯誤結論可能傷害難以自辯的人。他們沒有靠選領袖解決爭議，而是列出短暫、可逆測試真正能證明什麼。手寫標籤寫著「失望」，卻沒有人知道是誰留下的。修訂檔案出現「認真的」一詞，秦墨拿原始頁面核對它的意思。沈嵐指向清單上的「大使館」，追問其位置為何改變。

The recorded answers disagreed with the visions in predictable ways; the sentences proved sensitive to anticipation rather than truth. The plan had been amended three times, and each change left a trace. Qin Mo valued those corrections more than a perfect-looking first draft. If the outcome proved disappointing, the group would still be able to identify which assumption needed repair. Shen Lan watched for the quiet participant who had not yet been invited to speak. Shen Lan noticed the word emphasis in a note that someone had added after the first inspection. The earlier photograph clearly showed a librarian; the newer version did not, so Qin Mo kept both. A note about the margin lay beside the old register, with a date that Qin Mo could not verify.

錄下來的回答以可預測方式偏離異象，證明文字會受預期影響，而非直接表示真相。計畫改了三次，每次修改都留下痕跡。秦墨認為這些修正比看似完美的初稿更有價值。就算結果不理想，團隊仍能知道該修正哪一項假設。沈嵐也注意到那名一直沒有發言機會的人。沈嵐注意到初次檢查後有人在筆記裡加上「重點」一詞。較早的照片清楚顯示一項「圖書管理員」，新版卻沒有，秦墨因此保留兩張。一張談到「頁邊」的便條放在舊登記簿旁，日期讓秦墨無法核實。

An official version reached the room before the practical work was finished. Qin Mo read it once, then compared it with what they had actually measured. The difference was not always malicious, but it could still hurt somebody. Shen Lan asked that the correction be written in language the affected people could understand. The station corridor sounded ordinary after midnight. Qin Mo found that the hardest part of investigating a powerful claim was giving an ordinary doubt enough time. The term murderer appeared in the revised file; Qin Mo checked its meaning against the original page. Shen Lan noticed the word nonsense in a note that someone had added after the first inspection. The earlier photograph clearly showed a orbit; the newer version did not, so Qin Mo kept both.

實際工作還沒完成，官方版本就傳到現場。秦墨讀了一次，再比對真正測得的結果。差異未必都是惡意，卻仍可能傷害人。沈嵐要求把更正寫成受影響者看得懂的語言。午夜後警局走廊聲音平凡。秦墨發現調查強大宣稱時最難的是給平凡疑問足夠時間。修訂檔案出現「殺人犯」一詞，秦墨拿原始頁面核對它的意思。沈嵐注意到初次檢查後有人在筆記裡加上「荒謬的事」一詞。較早的照片清楚顯示一項「運行軌道」，新版卻沒有，秦墨因此保留兩張。

A witness asked whether the team was taking sides. Qin Mo answered that refusing to invent a fact was not the same as refusing to care. What mattered was whether the procedure protected someone whose story was less convenient to tell. Shen Lan listened without interrupting and wrote down the question exactly as it had been asked. An official memo described the plan as passive, but Qin Mo refused to treat an adjective as evidence. A note about the possession lay beside the old register, with a date that Qin Mo could not verify. Shen Lan pointed to the researcher in the inventory and asked why its location had changed.

一名見證人問團隊是否偏袒。秦墨回答，不捏造事實不代表冷漠。真正重要的是程序能否保護那個故事不方便被說出口的人。沈嵐沒有打斷，逐字記下原問題。官方備忘錄形容計畫「被動的」，秦墨不願把形容詞當作證據。一張談到「擁有權」的便條放在舊登記簿旁，日期讓秦墨無法核實。沈嵐指向清單上的「研究員」，追問其位置為何改變。

Even after the group agreed on the immediate step, another question remained. The answer would not arrive through a dramatic announcement. Someone would have to return to the place, compare the earlier notes, and accept the possibility that the next piece of evidence might spoil an attractive theory. Qin Mo prepared to do exactly that. The interview recorder captured words but not the years of rehearsal behind them. Shen Lan insisted on laboratory tests before a dramatic confession became a charge. Shen Lan noticed the word rhyme in a note that someone had added after the first inspection. A handwritten label read translator, but no one could say who had written it there. The summary called the situation urgent; Qin Mo asked which observation justified that description.

即使大家同意眼前步驟，另一個問題仍在。答案不會靠戲劇性公告降臨；有人得返回現場、比對舊筆記，並接受新證據可能推翻漂亮理論。秦墨準備做的正是這件事。偵訊錄音留下話語，留不下背後多年的排練。沈嵐堅持在戲劇性認罪變成指控前先完成實驗室檢驗。沈嵐注意到初次檢查後有人在筆記裡加上「童謠」一詞。手寫標籤寫著「翻譯員」，卻沒有人知道是誰留下的。摘要以「緊急的」形容情況，秦墨追問這種說法有何觀察根據。
