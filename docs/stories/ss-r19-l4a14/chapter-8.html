A former assistant of the speech coach died after a rehearsal. Her notebook contained corrections to the confession in the driver's old case. Something had changed, but not everybody noticed it in the same order. Qin Mo listened to the account of the nearest worker, then to someone standing farther away. Their differences were not necessarily lies. Each person had been given a different view and a different reason to be afraid of admitting uncertainty. The station corridor sounded ordinary after midnight. Qin Mo found that the hardest part of investigating a powerful claim was giving an ordinary doubt enough time. The term ancestor appeared in the revised file; Qin Mo checked its meaning against the original page. Shen Lan noticed the word contrary in a note that someone had added after the first inspection.

語言教練的前助理在排練後身亡，她的筆記本保留了司機舊案供詞的修改痕跡。某件事改變了，但人們發現它的順序不同。秦墨先聽近處工作人員的描述，再聽遠處旁觀者說法。差異不一定是謊言：每個人看到的角度不同，承認不確定時所要承擔的風險也不同。午夜後警局走廊聲音平凡。秦墨發現調查強大宣稱時最難的是給平凡疑問足夠時間。修訂檔案出現「祖先」一詞，秦墨拿原始頁面核對它的意思。沈嵐注意到初次檢查後有人在筆記裡加上「正好相反」一詞。

A suspect could deliberately feed a dramatic sentence into Qin's sight, making the gift a channel for deception. Qin Mo refused to pretend the previous decision had solved everything. The practical result was still there, useful but narrow. For each thing the team knew, there were two things it had not measured. Qin Mo drew a line through the claims that depended on speculation and asked Shen Lan to keep the original record intact. A handwritten label read craft, but no one could say who had written it there. The term crush appeared in the revised file; Qin Mo checked its meaning against the original page. One witness used the word defensible; Shen Lan noted that a description was not a measurement.

嫌疑人能故意把戲劇性的句子送入秦墨視野，使能力成為欺騙管道。秦墨不願假裝前一次決定已解決所有問題。成果確實存在，卻有明確界限。每個已知事項旁都還有未測量的部分。秦墨劃掉靠猜測成立的說法，請沈嵐保留原始紀錄。手寫標籤寫著「手工技藝」，卻沒有人知道是誰留下的。修訂檔案出現「擠壓；造成壓傷」一詞，秦墨拿原始頁面核對它的意思。一位證人用了「有充分理由的」一詞，沈嵐指出形容並不等於測量。

A small disagreement changed the schedule. Qin Mo wanted to proceed before evidence disappeared; Shen Lan wanted another check because a wrong conclusion might hurt people who could not easily defend themselves. They did not settle the matter by choosing a leader. They wrote down what a short, reversible test could actually establish. One witness used the word distinguished; Shen Lan noted that a description was not a measurement. A handwritten label read equip, but no one could say who had written it there. The term guilt appeared in the revised file; Qin Mo checked its meaning against the original page.

一場小爭執改變了時程。秦墨想趁證據消失前行動；沈嵐要求再核對一次，因為錯誤結論可能傷害難以自辯的人。他們沒有靠選領袖解決爭議，而是列出短暫、可逆測試真正能證明什麼。一位證人用了「傑出的；精湛的」一詞，沈嵐指出形容並不等於測量。手寫標籤寫著「器材」，卻沒有人知道是誰留下的。修訂檔案出現「愧疚」一詞，秦墨拿原始頁面核對它的意思。

The new death shared a chemical trace with the first, but the delivery mechanisms were different. The plan had been amended three times, and each change left a trace. Qin Mo valued those corrections more than a perfect-looking first draft. If the outcome proved disappointing, the group would still be able to identify which assumption needed repair. Shen Lan watched for the quiet participant who had not yet been invited to speak. A note about the handwriting lay beside the old register, with a date that Qin Mo could not verify. Shen Lan pointed to the hose in the inventory and asked why its location had changed. An official memo described the plan as identical, but Qin Mo refused to treat an adjective as evidence.

新案件與首案有相同化學痕跡，施用方式卻不同。計畫改了三次，每次修改都留下痕跡。秦墨認為這些修正比看似完美的初稿更有價值。就算結果不理想，團隊仍能知道該修正哪一項假設。沈嵐也注意到那名一直沒有發言機會的人。一張談到「字跡」的便條放在舊登記簿旁，日期讓秦墨無法核實。沈嵐指向清單上的「水管」，追問其位置為何改變。官方備忘錄形容計畫「完全相同的」，秦墨不願把形容詞當作證據。

An official version reached the room before the practical work was finished. Qin Mo read it once, then compared it with what they had actually measured. The difference was not always malicious, but it could still hurt somebody. Shen Lan asked that the correction be written in language the affected people could understand. The station corridor sounded ordinary after midnight. Qin Mo found that the hardest part of investigating a powerful claim was giving an ordinary doubt enough time. A handwritten label read involve, but no one could say who had written it there. The term launch appeared in the revised file; Qin Mo checked its meaning against the original page. One witness used the word lousy; Shen Lan noted that a description was not a measurement.

實際工作還沒完成，官方版本就傳到現場。秦墨讀了一次，再比對真正測得的結果。差異未必都是惡意，卻仍可能傷害人。沈嵐要求把更正寫成受影響者看得懂的語言。午夜後警局走廊聲音平凡。秦墨發現調查強大宣稱時最難的是給平凡疑問足夠時間。手寫標籤寫著「涉及；參與」，卻沒有人知道是誰留下的。修訂檔案出現「推出；啟動」一詞，秦墨拿原始頁面核對它的意思。一位證人用了「品質差的」一詞，沈嵐指出形容並不等於測量。

A witness asked whether the team was taking sides. Qin Mo answered that refusing to invent a fact was not the same as refusing to care. What mattered was whether the procedure protected someone whose story was less convenient to tell. Shen Lan listened without interrupting and wrote down the question exactly as it had been asked. On the next form, the action field read misunderstand, but the witness had never approved the operation. The earlier photograph clearly showed a pregnancy; the newer version did not, so Qin Mo kept both. The term riddle appeared in the revised file; Qin Mo checked its meaning against the original page.

一名見證人問團隊是否偏袒。秦墨回答，不捏造事實不代表冷漠。真正重要的是程序能否保護那個故事不方便被說出口的人。沈嵐沒有打斷，逐字記下原問題。下一張表單把動作寫成「誤會」，見證人卻從未同意這項操作。較早的照片清楚顯示一項「懷孕」，新版卻沒有，秦墨因此保留兩張。修訂檔案出現「謎題」一詞，秦墨拿原始頁面核對它的意思。

Even after the group agreed on the immediate step, another question remained. The answer would not arrive through a dramatic announcement. Someone would have to return to the place, compare the earlier notes, and accept the possibility that the next piece of evidence might spoil an attractive theory. Qin Mo prepared to do exactly that. The interview recorder captured words but not the years of rehearsal behind them. Shen Lan insisted on laboratory tests before a dramatic confession became a charge. The term stroke appeared in the revised file; Qin Mo checked its meaning against the original page. Shen Lan noticed the word suggestion in a note that someone had added after the first inspection. A handwritten label read technician, but no one could say who had written it there.

即使大家同意眼前步驟，另一個問題仍在。答案不會靠戲劇性公告降臨；有人得返回現場、比對舊筆記，並接受新證據可能推翻漂亮理論。秦墨準備做的正是這件事。偵訊錄音留下話語，留不下背後多年的排練。沈嵐堅持在戲劇性認罪變成指控前先完成實驗室檢驗。修訂檔案出現「一筆」一詞，秦墨拿原始頁面核對它的意思。沈嵐注意到初次檢查後有人在筆記裡加上「建議」一詞。手寫標籤寫著「技術員」，卻沒有人知道是誰留下的。
