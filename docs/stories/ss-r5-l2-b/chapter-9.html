The Missing Chapter

不必編造的約會 · 第 9 / 25 章 · 1000–2000 · 英中對照 · 校訂中

用互動版查詞、朗讀與收藏

The town archive finally sent the missing chapter of the garden report. Lian opened the file with fear as well as excitement. It described a public reading room and a small garden that had been built together. An addition at the end named the families who helped pay for the work. One name belonged to Jun's grandfather; another belonged to Mei's former neighbor. Lian wanted to write an article about the discovery, but Jun reminded her that some family details might be private. “We can tell the historical part first,” he said. “The people should decide whether we tell the rest.”

鎮公所檔案室終於寄來花園報告缺失的章節。蓮既興奮又害怕地打開文件。內容描述一座公共閱覽室和一座同時建造的小花園。最後的補充頁列出出錢協助的家庭，其中一個名字屬於俊的祖父，另一個是美以前的鄰居。蓮想寫文章介紹發現，俊提醒她某些家庭細節可能是隱私。「先講歷史部分，」他說。「其他內容讓當事人決定能否公開。」

The archive also included a painting of the reading room's opening day. In one corner a child held a bright crayon, while a puppy slept beneath a chair. A handwritten note said the shopkeeper from the next street had brought free tea. Lian checked every section of the picture against the old programs. Jun placed the copy under clear paper, using paste only on the new backing and a small nail to hang the frame. The team was finally learning a safe system for showing fragile things. They wanted the images to shine, not be damaged by a careless display.

檔案還附了一張閱覽室開幕日的畫。角落裡有孩子拿著鮮豔蠟筆，小狗睡在椅子下。手寫註記說隔壁街店主送來免費茶。蓮把畫面每個部分與舊節目單逐一核對。俊把影本放在透明紙下，只在新的背板上用漿糊，並以小釘子掛起畫框。團隊終於學會安全展示脆弱材料的方法。他們想讓影像發光，而不是被粗心的展示方式損壞。

Mei prepared a daily task list so that volunteers would know what to do without waiting for orders. The team had to ask permission for every photograph, record its source, and check the language on each sign. A partner from another club offered help with legal questions, but he warned them that he was only a student, not a lawyer. Lian appreciated that honest limit. She wanted the exhibition to succeed, yet success could not mean using material that belonged to other people. The first public draft would show only what they had permission to share. Everything else would wait.

美整理每天的工作表，讓志工不用等命令也知道該做什麼。團隊必須為每張照片取得許可，記下來源，並檢查標示文字。另一個社團的合作夥伴願意協助處理法律問題，但他提醒大家自己只是學生，不是律師。蓮欣賞這種誠實界線。她希望展覽成功，但成功不能代表擅用別人的作品。第一版公開稿只展示已取得授權的內容，其餘一律等待。

When Jun saw his grandfather's name, he grew quiet. He had always believed that the old man disliked public events. Now the record suggested that he had helped create a place for all kinds of people. Jun called his mother to ask about it. She said his grandfather had made benches for the reading room after work and refused payment. The news made Jun look at his own designs differently. He had wanted his signs to win a prize. Now he wanted to make something his grandfather would have been glad to see. Lian sat beside him and did not interrupt.

俊看見祖父名字時安靜下來。他一直以為祖父不喜歡公共活動，如今文件卻顯示祖父曾協助建造人人可用的空間。他打電話問母親。母親說祖父下班後替閱覽室做過長椅，拒絕收錢。這件事讓俊用不同的眼光看待自己的設計。他原本想讓標示得獎，現在想做出祖父看了會高興的東西。蓮坐在身旁，沒有打斷。

That evening someone posted a claim online: the two club leaders were using a fake romance to collect votes. The note included a photograph from their announced stage practice but left out the sign that said “Fictional Scene.” Mei became angry. Lian asked her not to attack the account. “Unless we know who wrote it, fighting strangers could make things worse,” she said. Instead, she prepared a calm reply explaining the performance, the consent agreement, and the real purpose of the route. Jun checked her wording. Their names were on the poster together, so the response belonged to both.

當晚有人在網路上發文，聲稱兩位社長利用假戀情蒐集選票。貼文附了公開彩排的照片，卻裁掉寫著「虛構場景」的標示。美氣得想回擊，蓮請她不要攻擊帳號。「除非知道作者是誰，否則和陌生人吵只會更糟，」她說。她準備了一則冷靜回覆，說明表演性質、雙方同意協議，以及路線真正目的。俊核對文字。兩個名字都印在海報上，回應就該由兩人共同負責。

After posting the reply, Lian worried that students would laugh at her. Jun suggested stepping away from the screens for an hour. They walked to the old market and bought warm bread from a shopkeeper who knew the gardener. The man had not seen the online claim. He simply wanted to know whether the route would be safe for his mother. Lian answered with the steps they had taken. It felt good to talk about the real work. Jun carried the bread bag as they walked back, not because she needed help, but because she had offered to carry the map.

發出回覆後，蓮仍擔心學生會嘲笑她。俊建議離開螢幕一小時。兩人走到老市場，向認識園丁的店主買熱麵包。那位先生沒看見網路指控，只關心母親能否安全走完路線。蓮說明已採取的措施，能談真正的工作讓她心情好些。回程俊拿麵包袋，並非認為她需要幫忙，而是因為她主動拿地圖。

Back at the club room, Mei pointed to the report's last page. A line stated that the reading room was never meant to become private property without a public hearing. Lian knew the words alone would not open the door, but they could help people ask a better question. She wrote a new chapter for the play. Its two characters would find the old rule and invite the audience to decide what fairness meant. No one in the story would arrive as a perfect hero. They would simply bring the evidence into the light and let the town answer.

回社辦後，美指著報告最後一頁，其中寫著未經公開聽證，閱覽室不得變成私人財產。蓮知道這句話不會自動打開門，卻能幫人問更好的問題。她替戲加了一章：兩個角色發現舊規則，邀請觀眾思考公平的意義。故事裡不會有完美英雄突然降臨。他們只把證據帶到光下，讓城鎮回答。
