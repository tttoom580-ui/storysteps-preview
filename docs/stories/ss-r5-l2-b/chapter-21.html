黑板上的清單

The List on the Blackboard

不必編造的約會 · 第 21 / 25 章 · 1000–2000 · 英中對照 · 校訂中

用互動版查詞、朗讀與收藏

The final week began with a long review meeting. Lian wrote every unfinished task on the blackboard: check signs, test seats, print maps, and answer permission questions. Jun brought a blanket for the old volunteer who often sat near the door in the evening cold. Neither expected praise for small acts, but the group had learned to notice them. A new member called the work dull and asked why they could not have a grand show. Lian did not make an excuse. “Because our visitors need a clear route more than a loud entrance,” she said. “A good ending begins with the basics.”

最後一週從漫長的檢討會開始。蓮把所有未完成任務寫在黑板上：檢查標示、測試座位、印地圖、回覆授權問題。俊替常在門邊坐到傍晚的年長志工帶來毛毯。他們都不期待小動作得到讚美，但團隊已學會看見它。一位新成員嫌工作乏味，問為何不能辦場盛大表演。蓮沒有找藉口：「因為訪客比起吵鬧入口，更需要清楚的路線。好的結局要從基礎做起。」

Mei prepared an introduction for the visiting judges. She would explain the two clubs, then lend the microphone to the volunteers who had made the route work. A music student offered an instrument for the closing scene, but the song had to be short so that people could still hear directions. Jun checked the sound levels. A salesperson from a nearby shop brought samples of soap for the public toilets, which had been running low. Lian thanked him and wrote his help on the list without putting his business name across the historical display. She was getting better at keeping gifts and stories separate.

美替來訪評審準備開場介紹：先說明兩個社團，再把麥克風借給真正讓路線運作的志工。音樂系學生提供一件樂器作為結尾配樂，但歌曲必須短，讓大家仍聽得見導引。俊檢查音量。附近商店的銷售員送來公共廁所快用完的肥皂樣品，蓮道謝，把幫助記在清單上，沒有因此把商家名字貼滿歷史展示。她愈來愈懂得把贈禮與故事分開。

An old magazine arrived from a distant town, sent by a former reader of the library. It contained a photograph of a goat eating leaves near the garden wall. The note beside it mentioned a lone gardener who had kept the paths clear during an unusually hard winter. Lian read the story aloud. Jun smiled, because the present gardener had once told them about that same man. The two accounts did not prove every detail, but they supported each other. Mei suggested adding the photograph to the memory table. Lian agreed after sending a permission request to the magazine's owner.

遠方小鎮寄來一本舊雜誌，寄件人以前曾使用閱覽室。裡面有一張山羊在花園牆旁吃葉子的照片，旁註提到一位孤身園丁曾在特別嚴寒的冬天維持小路暢通。蓮念出故事，俊笑了，因為現在的園丁也提過同一人。兩份說法不能證明每個細節，卻能相互支持。美建議把照片放進記憶桌，蓮在寄出授權申請後同意。

For lunch the volunteers shared a simple meal. A cook had prepared steak for one group and vegetables for those who preferred them. Lian checked that no one felt pushed to take food they did not want. A student read the latest text on the information sign and said it sounded somewhat formal. She suggested changing “Visitors are requested to proceed” to “Please follow the blue line.” Jun tried the new words aloud and agreed they were clearer. The team had once argued about every change. Now they could judge language by whether it helped people instead of whether it belonged to one leader.

午餐時志工分享簡單餐點。廚師為一組人準備牛排，也為想吃蔬菜的人準備其他料理。蓮確認沒有人被迫拿不想吃的食物。一位學生讀資訊牌最新文字，說語氣有點太正式，建議把「敬請訪客依規定前進」改成「請沿藍線走」。俊念出新句，認為確實比較清楚。過去團隊為每項修改爭論，如今能按是否幫助人來判斷，而不只看點子屬於哪位社長。

In the afternoon Jun noticed Lian looking at the first poster again. The red heart was still there, beside two people who had barely wanted to share a room. “I did not like that picture,” she said. “I know,” he replied. “I should have helped remove it sooner.” She did not ask him to feel guilty forever. She told him that their new signs and rules mattered more than an old mistake. They had corrected the misleading picture and learned from it. He looked relieved. The gentle exchange felt like a review of their whole semester.

下午俊看見蓮又看第一張海報。紅心仍在，旁邊是兩個當初連同處一室都不願意的人。「我不喜歡那張照片，」她說。「我知道，」他回答。「我該更早幫忙把它拿掉。」她沒有要求他永遠內疚，只說現在的標示和規則比舊錯更重要。他們已更正誤導的照片，也學到了教訓。他鬆了一口氣。這段溫柔對話像是回顧整個學期。

Before closing the office, Mei asked Lian what would happen to the club partnership after the contest. Lian said the two groups could keep working together if members chose to. There should be no automatic union and no permanent leader. Jun agreed and proposed a new vote next term. Mei then asked what would happen to the two people whose faces were on the posters. Lian smiled without answering. That question belonged to a different kind of decision, and she wanted to make it without a room full of listeners. For now she was proud of how the project had changed them.

關閉社辦前，美問蓮競賽結束後兩社團怎麼辦。蓮說只要社員選擇，兩團可以繼續合作；但不應自動合併，也不該設永久領導者。俊同意，提議下學期重新投票。美接著問海報上那兩個人會怎樣。蓮微笑不答。那是另一種決定，她想在沒有整間房聽眾時親自作出。現在她只為計畫如何改變大家感到驕傲。

A young helper asked who would handle the final room key after the festival. Jun said the school office would keep it, not either club leader. Lian felt pride in that answer. They had learned to leave clear rules for the people who came after them.

一名年輕助手問節慶結束後由誰處理房間鑰匙。俊說應由學校辦公室保管，而非任何一位社長。蓮為這個回答感到驕傲。他們已學會替後來的人留下清楚規則。

That night the weather service warned of heavy rain over the weekend. The festival was near, and the team had already printed the maps. Lian asked Jun to check the outdoor boards once more. He replied that he would, but also reminded her they might have to stop the event if conditions became unsafe. She looked at the blackboard photograph on her phone and knew he was right. After months of careful work, cancellation would hurt. Still, the rules they had written together could not become empty words when the moment came to use them.

那晚氣象單位警告週末可能有豪雨。節慶近在眼前，地圖都印好了。蓮請俊再檢查一次戶外展板。他答應，也提醒若天候不安全就可能必須停止活動。她看著手機裡黑板清單的照片，知道他說得對。幾個月的努力若取消，一定很難受。但他們共同寫下的規則，不能在真正需要遵守時變成空話。
