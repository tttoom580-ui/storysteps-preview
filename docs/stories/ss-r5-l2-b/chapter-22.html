颱風規則

The Typhoon Rule

不必編造的約會 · 第 22 / 25 章 · 1000–2000 · 英中對照 · 校訂中

用互動版查詞、朗讀與收藏

The typhoon arrived earlier than the forecast had suggested. Wind shook the trees, and rain crossed the square in wild sheets. The town canceled most outdoor events, but the university hall remained available as a temporary rest area. Lian called the club leaders and said the walking exhibition would not open that day. Jun did not doubt the decision. He checked whether any sign had caused damage and asked volunteers not to go anywhere near the old roof. The festival could wait. The safety of the people who had helped make it was worth more than the last chance at a prize.

颱風比預報更早抵達。風吹得樹木搖晃，雨水成片掃過廣場。鎮上取消多數戶外活動，但大學禮堂仍可作為臨時休息區。蓮打電話給社團幹部，宣布今天不開放步行展覽。俊沒有懷疑這個決定。他檢查是否有標示造成損壞，並請志工不要靠近舊屋頂。節慶可以等，協助完成活動的人的安全，比最後得獎機會更重要。

Inside the hall, a fisherman from the coast arrived with his family after their bus service stopped. He wore a silver ring and carried a small golden fish charm from his daughter. A volunteer offered cola, while another brought clean blankets. Mei checked the supply bill to see what had been used and what needed replacing. A hunter visiting from the hills helped move heavy chairs, but Jun asked him not to work alone. The group had no official rescue role. It could offer a safe place to sit, clear information, and help people contact the right services when needed.

禮堂裡，一位來自海岸的漁夫和家人因公車停駛而進來休息。他戴著銀戒指，帶著女兒送的金色小魚吊飾。志工遞上可樂，另一人拿來乾淨毛毯。美檢查物資帳單，記下用掉及需要補充的物品。一位從山區來的獵人幫忙搬重椅，俊請他不要獨自工作。團隊沒有正式救援職責，只能提供安全座位、清楚資訊，必要時協助聯絡相關服務。

A sudden leak appeared above the memory table. Jun asked everyone to move the paper copies before water reached them. Lian helped carry the frames to a dry corner. One volunteer slipped slightly, then caught the rail. The instant frightened them all. They put up a warning and closed that part of the hall until staff inspected it. “We have to solve the water problem, not hide it,” Lian said. Jun agreed, and the university worker arrived with a bucket. The documents survived because the team had kept copies and had practiced moving the displays safely.

記憶桌上方突然漏水。俊請大家在水滴到影本前搬走紙張，蓮把相框移到乾燥角落。一位志工腳下滑了一下，幸好抓住扶手，那一瞬間讓眾人害怕。他們立刻張貼警告，封閉該區直到校方檢查。「得解決漏水，不是藏起來，」蓮說。俊同意，校工帶著水桶到場。文件得以保存，因為團隊先前留下備份，也練習過安全搬移展品。

At midday the event committee sent two choices. The former plan was to cancel the public show entirely; the latter was to hold a short indoor version after the storm had passed. Lian asked for opinions instead of deciding alone. Several volunteers wanted the second possibility, but only if the hall passed inspection and visitors could travel safely. Jun read the conditions aloud. They agreed to wait for an official update before announcing anything. The contest mattered, but nobody wanted to turn a damaged town into a stage for their own success. Lian felt proud of their careful decision.

中午活動委員會提出兩個選擇：前者是完全取消公開演出，後者是暴風雨過後在室內舉行短版。蓮徵求意見，不獨自決定。幾位志工希望採第二方案，但條件是禮堂通過檢查、訪客交通安全。俊大聲念出條件。他們同意等正式更新再公告。競賽雖重要，沒人想把受災的城鎮變成自己成功的舞臺。蓮為團隊謹慎的決定感到驕傲。

During the afternoon Lian sat with Mei beside a window. The rain was still strong, and neither could help the workers outside. Mei said she had thought a good leader must always have an answer. “I am learning that sometimes we should wait,” she admitted. Lian agreed. She had wanted to win so badly at the start of term that waiting had felt like weakness. Now she understood it as care. Jun came with fresh tea and listened quietly. The three friends watched the storm without trying to make a lesson out of every drop.

下午蓮與美坐在窗邊。雨仍很大，她們也幫不上外頭工作人員。美說自己以前以為好領導者必須永遠有答案。「現在我學到有時該等，」她承認。蓮同意。學期初她那麼想贏，等待就像軟弱；如今她知道那也是關心。俊帶來新茶，安靜坐下聽。三個朋友看著風雨，沒有想把每滴雨都變成大道理。

By evening the wind weakened, but the roads were still wet. The school announced that the indoor event might be held the next day if the safety staff agreed. Jun offered to check the hall, and Lian asked him to go only with an authorized worker. He promised. They had learned that bravery did not mean taking every risk. Before leaving, he looked at her and said, “I am glad we made the safe choice together.” She answered, “Me too.” For a moment the quiet hall felt warmer than any crowded stage. Their trust had grown strong enough to survive a missed performance.

傍晚風勢轉弱，路面卻仍濕。學校宣布若安全人員同意，隔天可能舉辦室內版。俊願意檢查禮堂，蓮請他務必與授權工作人員同行。他答應了。他們學會勇敢不等於承擔每個風險。離開前他看著她說：「我很高興我們一起選擇安全。」她回答：「我也是。」一瞬間，安靜禮堂比擁擠舞臺更溫暖。他們的信任已能承受錯過一次演出。

A volunteer's mate arrived after his employment shift had ended. He offered to help stack dry chairs, but the floor was still wet enough for someone to slip. Lian thanked him and showed him a safe task at the information desk instead. Nobody needed to prove kindness by stepping into danger.

一名志工的夥伴在下班後趕來，願意幫忙堆放乾椅子，但地板仍濕滑，可能使人滑倒。蓮道謝，改安排他到資訊桌做安全的工作。沒有人需要用冒險來證明善意。

That night the team posted a clear notice: the outdoor walk was canceled, no one should enter the old building, and a new time would be announced only after inspection. Lian checked every line before posting. Jun added contact information for people who had registered. They had spent months preparing a beautiful story, but their best decision might have been to stop telling it for one day. The idea made Lian smile sadly. A real partnership was not proven by perfect weather or public applause. It was proven by how people protected one another when the plan fell apart.

那晚團隊發布清楚公告：戶外步行取消，任何人不得進入舊建築，重新舉辦時間須等檢查後才會公布。蓮逐行核對，俊補上已報名者的聯絡資訊。他們花幾個月準備漂亮故事，但最好的決定也許正是停止演出一天。蓮帶著一點難過微笑。真正的合作不是靠好天氣或掌聲證明，而是在計畫瓦解時，彼此如何保護對方。
