天亮前的決定

A Decision Before Dawn

不必編造的約會 · 第 20 / 25 章 · 1000–2000 · 英中對照 · 校訂中

用互動版查詞、朗讀與收藏

The grant committee met in the central hall to make its first decision. Lian had prepared a balance sheet showing costs, volunteer hours, and the supplies that remained. A businessman from a sponsoring company offered new lights for the show. He said his firm had sold products to a billion people, so their brand would attract visitors. Jun listened politely but noticed that the agreement required the company name on every historical sign. Lian refused that condition. “The people who built the reading room should be named first,” she said. “A sponsor cannot replace their stories.” The businessman asked for time to revise the offer.

補助委員會在中央大廳開會，準備做第一階段決定。蓮帶來收支平衡表，列出成本、志工工時與剩餘材料。一位贊助公司的商人願意提供新燈具。他說公司產品賣到十億人手中，品牌能吸引訪客。俊禮貌傾聽，卻發現協議要求每塊歷史標示都印公司名稱。蓮拒絕這個條件。「建造閱覽室的人應該先被記住，」她說。「贊助商不能取代他們的故事。」商人要求時間修改提案。

At the break, Mei served warm cocoa in small cups. She had drawn a dot beside each question on the committee's list, marking the ones that still needed answers. The major issue was not the festival itself; it was how the town might care for the room after the student clubs ended their project. Jun suggested a public group with seats for local residents, teachers, and students. Lian added that its decisions and costs should be available to read. “We can give the town a possible plan,” she said. “We cannot decide its future for everyone.” The committee asked them to write the idea clearly.

休息時，美用小杯分送熱可可。她在委員會問題清單旁點上小圓點，標出還需要答覆的項目。主要問題不在節慶本身，而是學生計畫結束後，城鎮如何照顧閱覽室。俊提議成立公共小組，讓居民、老師和學生都有席次。蓮補充決議和支出應供人閱讀。「我們能提出可能方案，」她說。「不能替所有人決定未來。」委員會請他們把構想寫清楚。

A health student interrupted the meeting to ask for a seat. She had pain in her knee after helping carry boxes, and standing was difficult. The room had many chairs, but all were being used for bags and coats. Lian asked volunteers to clear two chairs at once. Jun moved the bags and checked that the walkway stayed open. The student thanked them and said a project about access should notice such needs in its own meetings. Lian agreed. She had been looking at big numbers while a person beside her needed help. The reminder was uncomfortable and useful.

一位醫學系學生在會議中請求座位。她搬箱後膝蓋疼痛，不容易久站。房間裡其實有許多椅子，卻全放了包包和外套。蓮立刻請志工清出兩張，俊搬走物品並確認走道仍暢通。學生道謝，說一個講無障礙的計畫應該連自身會議都注意這些需要。蓮同意。她一直看著龐大的數字，身旁卻有人需要幫忙。這個提醒讓人不自在，卻很有用。

During the next discussion, the committee asked why the story showed a fictional couple instead of a family or two friends. Lian answered that the original design had been a story about a date with the town, but the project had since expanded to include many kinds of care. She did not use romance as a secret weapon. “There is no secret ending we must force on our visitors,” she said. Jun added that their stage characters would make a choice together at the final scene, while each visitor could choose a different response. The judges seemed interested in that difference.

下一輪討論，委員會問故事為何用虛構情侶，而不是家庭或朋友。蓮回答，原始設計是與城鎮約會的故事，但計畫後來擴展到多種關心方式。她沒有把戀愛當成秘密武器。「我們沒有必須強加給訪客的秘密結局，」她說。俊補充，舞臺角色會在最後一幕共同作出一個選擇，但每位參觀者可以有不同回應。評審似乎對這個差別很有興趣。

When the meeting finally ended, the group was sleepy but relieved. The committee had not chosen a winner yet. It gave the clubs one final chance to present a plan for the reading room's future use. Lian felt the weight of the task and wondered whether they could do it in time. Jun said they should not try to fix a whole town in one night. They could write a short proposal based on the work already done. Mei agreed to organize the photographs, while two volunteers checked the budget. The work began quietly before dawn, with no need for a dramatic speech.

會議終於結束時，大家睏倦卻鬆了一口氣。委員會還沒選出贏家，而是給社團最後一次機會，提出閱覽室未來使用方案。蓮感到任務沉重，不知道能否準時完成。俊說不必一夜修好整座城，只要根據已做的工作寫一份簡短提案。美整理照片，兩位志工核對預算。天亮前，他們安靜地開始工作，不需要戲劇性演說。

At five in the morning Lian found Jun resting his head on the desk. She offered him water and asked whether he wanted to sleep for an hour. He looked up with a tired expression and said yes. She covered the light near his face and took the next turn checking the pages. The work was finally balanced enough that one person could rest without letting the whole plan fall apart. She thought of the first day, when both had been ready to fight for control. Now she wanted him to be well, not simply useful. The difference surprised her.

清晨五點，蓮看見俊把頭靠在桌上休息。她遞水，問他是否想睡一小時。他帶著疲倦神情抬頭，答應了。她遮住照在他臉上的燈，接手檢查文件。工作終於平衡到有人休息也不會使整個計畫垮掉。她想起第一天兩人都準備爭奪主導權；現在她希望他平安健康，而不只是有用。這個差別讓她意外。

A volunteer suggested adding a card about the birth of the reading room and the organ once played at community music nights. That history was especially interesting to a visitor whose family had moved from a town by the ocean. Lian bit her tongue instead of promising the story immediately. She would first verify the instrument and its owner.

一位志工建議增添閱覽室誕生之初的介紹卡，提到社區音樂夜曾使用的管風琴。這段歷史讓一位家庭從海邊城鎮搬來的訪客特別感興趣。蓮忍住沒有立刻承諾，先表示要核對樂器及其所有人。

When dawn arrived, the revised proposal lay ready on the table. It did not claim a magic cure for every problem. It set out three possible steps: keep the outdoor exhibit open, complete a professional repair study, and ask the town to discuss the room's future in public. Lian read the pages once more and signed her name. Jun woke and added his. Their hands touched briefly as they passed the pen, but neither made a joke. They had reached a real decision together. What they might decide about each other would have to wait a little longer.

天亮時，修訂提案已放在桌上。它沒有聲稱能神奇解決所有問題，只列三個可能步驟：保留戶外展覽、完成專業修復評估、請城鎮公開討論閱覽室未來。蓮再次讀完，簽下自己的名字。俊醒來，也簽下名字。傳筆時手短暫碰觸，兩人都沒有開玩笑。他們一起作出了一項真正的決定。至於彼此之間會如何選擇，還得再等一下。
