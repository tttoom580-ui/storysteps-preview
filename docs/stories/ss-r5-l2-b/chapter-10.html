用腳步量出的路線

A Route Measured in Steps

不必編造的約會 · 第 10 / 25 章 · 1000–2000 · 英中對照 · 校訂中

用互動版查詞、朗讀與收藏

The route had grown too long for visitors who needed frequent rest, so Jun measured its length again. He marked every hundred meters with a small blue unit on the map and listed where benches were available. Mei brought biscuits for the volunteers and a bag of yams from her aunt's farm. Lian helped gather everyone before the test. She told them that one strong walker could not stand for the whole group. They needed a proper record from several people of different ages. Jun nodded. It took courage to show a design before it was finished, but he would rather learn the truth now than at the final event.

路線變得太長，常需要休息的參觀者可能走不完，於是俊重新量距離。他在地圖上每一百公尺做一個藍色單位標記，並列出可用長椅的位置。美帶來餅乾，還帶了阿姨農場的地瓜。蓮在測試前集合大家，說一位走得快的人不能代表所有參觀者，他們需要不同年齡的人留下正式紀錄。俊點頭。設計還沒完成就公開確實需要勇氣，但他寧願現在知道真相，也不要等到決賽。

A younger volunteer wore a fox hat while another carried a paper goose for the children's section. They were preparing a musical stop with animal sounds and short poems. The style looked playful, yet the team had to be careful not to block the narrow road. Jun used glue to attach a sign to a portable board rather than the town's old wall. A snail crossed the path near the garden, and the children asked the guide to pause. Lian did not ignore them. The pause showed that the new route allowed visitors to notice small living things, not only large buildings.

一位年輕志工戴著狐狸帽，另一位拿著兒童區要用的紙鵝。他們正準備一站有動物聲音與短詩的音樂活動。風格雖然活潑，卻必須避免擋住狹窄道路。俊用膠水把牌子固定在可移動展板，而不是貼上老牆。花園旁有一隻蝸牛穿過路面，孩子請導覽員停一下。蓮沒有忽略他們。這個暫停讓人看見新路線能使參觀者注意小生命，而不只是大建築。

At the second stop, a teacher offered congratulations on the progress but also gave difficult feedback. The signs were clear for adults, while children could not read the longer phrases. She suggested using pictures beside the words. Lian first felt defensive. She had spent nights choosing every sentence. Then she watched a little girl struggle to follow one card and saw the problem herself. Growth, she decided, could be gradual. She shortened the text and asked Jun to draw a picture of a door. Their changes were small, but they made the exhibition easier to enjoy.

第二站，一位老師向團隊的進步道賀，也提出尖銳意見。標示對成人很清楚，孩子卻看不懂長句。她建議把圖像放在文字旁。蓮起初很想辯解，因為每句話都是她花夜晚挑選的；但她看見一個女孩讀卡片讀得很吃力，才親眼明白問題。她決定成長可以是逐漸的。她縮短文字，請俊畫一扇門。改動不大，卻讓展覽更容易欣賞。

The third stop brought them to a small open square. It was on the schedule as a place for photographs, but a family had planned a picnic there. Lian did not tell them to move. Instead, she asked the group to use the opposite side and made a note to check public use before future events. Jun admitted that his earlier map had treated empty spaces as if nobody belonged to them. “A map has people in it, even when you cannot draw them,” Lian said. He wrote that line on his measuring book. She felt a surprising pride that he wanted to remember her words.

第三站是一座小廣場。行程表把它列為拍照地點，但已有一家人在那裡野餐。蓮沒有叫他們離開，而是請隊伍使用另一側，並記下未來活動前應先確認公共使用情況。俊承認自己以前畫圖時，把空地當成不屬於任何人的地方。「地圖裡有人，就算你沒把他們畫出來，」蓮說。他把這句話寫進測量筆記。她意外地為他願意記住自己的話感到高興。

Back in the office, the team compared each person's walking time per section. The results were different enough that they decided to offer two routes, a short one and a full one. Someone asked whether that would make judging harder. Lian said the contest should reward a useful public project, not a route that looked neat only on paper. Mei prepared clear signs for both choices. Jun checked that no bench blocked another visitor. The work felt slow, but it was now based on evidence. A plan that could change was better than a perfect-looking plan that would fail in the street.

回辦公室後，團隊比較每個人走完各段的時間。結果差異夠大，於是他們決定提供短路線和完整路線。有人問這樣會不會增加評分難度。蓮說競賽應獎勵實用的公共計畫，而不是紙上漂亮的路線。美替兩種選擇做清楚標示，俊檢查長椅沒有擋到其他訪客。進度看似慢，卻有證據作基礎。能修改的計畫，比只在紙上完美、實際卻失敗的計畫更好。

During the evening break, Lian told Jun she would take a short vacation after the contest, even if they lost. He said his plan was to visit his grandmother and ask more about the old reading room. “You do not have to come with me,” he added quickly. “I was not going to invite myself,” she replied, then laughed at how disappointed he looked. The joke surprised both of them. They had become comfortable enough to tease each other. Lian did not feel ready to explain what that meant. The promise of a real date should not be made only because the fake dates were going well.

傍晚休息時，蓮告訴俊比賽結束後會放個短假，無論輸贏都一樣。他說想回去探望祖母，問更多閱覽室往事。「妳不用跟我去，」他立刻補充。「我本來也沒打算自己跟去，」蓮回答，看他有點失落的神情，忍不住笑了。兩人都沒料到這個玩笑。他們已熟到能互相打趣。蓮還不準備解釋這意味著什麼。真正的約會不能只因為假約會演得順利就倉促承諾。

That night Lian opened the newest map and counted every place where a visitor could stop, sit, or turn back. More marks filled the page than there had been at the start. The route looked less simple, but far more honest. She sent Jun a photograph of the new plan with the words, “This is what care looks like.” He replied with a photograph of a repaired bench. Under it he wrote, “This is one way it feels.” Lian held the phone for a moment, smiling. A work partner could make a difficult day easier. She wondered whether that was how something real might begin.

那晚蓮打開最新地圖，數出每個可以停下、坐下或折返的地點。頁面上的記號比最初多了許多。路線看起來不再單純，卻誠實得多。她拍照傳給俊，寫著：「這就是關心的樣子。」他回傳修好的長椅照片，下面寫：「這是感受到它的一種方式。」蓮拿著手機微笑了一會兒。工作搭檔能讓艱難的日子容易一些。她好奇，真實的感情是不是就這樣開始的。
