給每位訪客的一堂課

A Lesson for Every Visitor

不必編造的約會 · 第 17 / 25 章 · 1000–2000 · 英中對照 · 校訂中

用互動版查詞、朗讀與收藏

The school gave the clubs one afternoon for a public education test. The task was basic: see whether visitors understood the route without a guide standing beside them. Jun put a wooden sign at the entrance and opened a drawer full of maps. Mei prepared a short quiz that asked where the rest areas and safe exits were. Lian observed quietly. Two visitors who looked alike chose different paths, which reminded her not to suppose that age or appearance told her what anyone needed. The plan would succeed only if people could make their own choices without depending on a leader every step of the way.

學校給社團一個下午進行公開教育測試。任務很基本：看訪客在沒有導覽員跟隨時，能否理解路線。俊在入口放木牌，拉開裝滿地圖的抽屜。美準備短測驗，問休息區和安全出口在哪裡。蓮安靜觀察。兩位長得很像的訪客選了不同路線，提醒她不能只憑年齡或外表猜測需求。計畫要成功，人們就必須能自行選擇，不必每一步依賴領隊。

A local teacher brought students from a nearby village. The children were excited by the small models but had difficulty understanding the division between the old garden and the reading room. Jun showed a sample map with a blue channel marking the waterway that once joined the two spaces. Lian explained how the town's project had changed over time. The teacher praised the idea but asked for simpler language. They rewrote three signs on the spot. Learning, Lian realized, was a shared act: the visitors asked questions, and the makers had to change their answers when those answers were unclear.

一位本地老師帶著附近村莊的學生來。孩子對小模型很興奮，卻不懂老花園和閱覽室為何分成兩區。俊拿出地圖樣本，以藍色水道標示過去連接兩處的地方。蓮解釋城鎮計畫如何隨時間改變。老師稱讚構想，但請他們改用簡單語言。他們當場重寫三塊標示。蓮體會到學習是共享的行動：訪客提問，製作者發現回答不清楚時就必須修改。

During the break a visitor offered a bottle of soda to the volunteers. Another brought bread baked by a family who had once managed the old room. The group sat together and talked about the grand plans they had made at the beginning of term. Some had expected a huge show with music and lights. Now the real project was quieter, but it helped more people. Jun said he had learned to manage his pride. Lian said she had learned that a strong story did not need to hide hard facts. Mei laughed and told them they sounded like people giving speeches at their own graduation.

休息時，一位訪客送來汽水，另一人帶來曾管理舊閱覽室的家庭烤的麵包。大家坐在一起，談學期初訂下的宏大計畫。有些人原本期待大型音樂燈光秀，如今真正的計畫安靜許多，卻能幫更多人。俊說他學會管理自己的驕傲，蓮說好故事不需要藏起難聽的事實。美笑說兩人講話像在自己的畢業典禮致詞。

A student from the business school asked whether the project could sell postcards to cover costs. Lian said the idea was possible if the owners of the photographs agreed and the income was recorded openly. Jun noted that selling items was not necessary for the test and should not delay the main work. The student accepted their answer and offered to help write a simple price list. Their club had learned to welcome useful ideas without accepting every offer. The team was not a small company with one boss. It was a group of volunteers whose choices mattered.

商學院學生問能否賣明信片補貼成本。蓮說如果照片權利人同意、收入公開記錄，就有可能。俊提醒販售不是測試的必要部分，不能拖慢主要工作。學生接受回答，願意幫忙做簡單價格表。社團學會歡迎有用想法，卻不必接受每項提議。他們不是由單一老闆管理的小公司，而是每個人選擇都重要的志工團體。

At the last stop, an older man asked to read a poem aloud. The words described a river that had watched several generations grow up. His voice was unsteady, but everyone listened. When he finished, he said he had once been a child in the reading room. Lian thanked him and asked whether the recording could become part of the exhibition. He agreed and gave his name. Jun carefully checked that the microphone had worked. The test had begun as a lesson in signs and routes. It ended as a lesson in why people wanted those signs and routes in the first place.

最後一站，一位長者請求朗讀詩。他念的是一條看著好幾代人成長的河，聲音有些不穩，大家卻都靜靜聽。念完後，他說自己小時候曾在閱覽室讀書。蓮道謝，詢問能否把錄音放進展覽。他同意並留下姓名。俊仔細確認麥克風錄音成功。這場測試原本是教人看標示和走路線，最後卻成了關於人們為何需要那些標示與路線的一堂課。

That night the team reviewed the quiz results. Most visitors understood the easy sections, but one exit sign still caused confusion. Jun corrected its angle, and Lian changed two words. They sent the revised file to the school safety office. Mei asked whether they could call the pilot a success. “A useful first step,” Lian said. “A success needs one more test.” Jun did not object. They were becoming alike in their care for exact words, even though their skills remained different. Lian liked that their differences still had room inside the same plan.

當晚團隊檢視測驗結果。大部分訪客懂簡單區域，但一塊出口標示仍引起混淆。俊調整角度，蓮換掉兩個詞，再把修改版送到學校安全辦公室。美問能否稱試辦成功。「有用的第一步，」蓮說。「要算成功還需要再測一次。」俊沒有反對。兩人在重視精確措辭上變得相似，儘管能力仍不同。蓮喜歡一個計畫能容納這些差異。

A history student said the cost of the old roof had been a shock to his family. His uncle had offered to donate some wine for a celebration, but Lian declined because the school event served children and had no plan for alcohol. She thanked the family for offering support and suggested a book donation instead.

一位歷史系學生說老屋頂費用讓家人很震驚。他的叔叔提出捐幾瓶酒慶祝，蓮因校園活動有兒童參與、且沒有酒精飲品安排而婉拒。她感謝那家人的好意，建議可以改捐書。

Before going home Jun showed Lian a drawing of the future reading room. He had added a table for children and a quiet corner for older people. It was not a promise that the room would open tomorrow. It was a possible plan the town could consider. Lian wrote a short caption and signed both their names as its makers. Jun looked at the page with a smile. For once he was happy to share credit without counting who had done more. The club contest was almost over. The part of their partnership that had started to matter most was not.

回家前，俊給蓮看未來閱覽室的圖。他加了孩子用的桌子，也加了長者的安靜角落。這不是保證明天就能開門，而是給城鎮參考的方案。蓮寫一小段說明，並把兩人的名字都署上。俊看著紙微笑。他第一次不用計較誰做比較多，就樂於共享功勞。社團競賽快結束了，但這段合作中最重要的部分並沒有結束。
