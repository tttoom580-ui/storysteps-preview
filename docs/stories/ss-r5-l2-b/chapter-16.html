改變計畫的演練

The Drill That Changed the Plan

不必編造的約會 · 第 16 / 25 章 · 1000–2000 · 英中對照 · 校訂中

用互動版查詞、朗讀與收藏

The campus announced an earthquake drill two weeks before the festival. A building manager asked every club to join. Lian had never led a large group during an emergency, so she began with the basics. The volunteers learned the safe exits and put an arrow above each doorway. Jun checked the upper hall where their exhibits would stand. He found that one tall shelf could fall if the ground shook. They moved it before the drill. “This is not the exciting work people photograph,” Jun said, “but it is valuable.” Lian agreed. A safe festival had to begin with people, not prizes.

節慶前兩週，校園宣布地震演練。一位大樓管理員請每個社團參加。蓮從未在緊急情況帶領大團體，因此先從基礎學起。志工認識安全出口，在每個門上方放箭頭。俊檢查展覽所在的樓上大廳，發現一座高架子遇到搖晃可能倒下。演練前大家先移走它。「這種工作不會有人覺得值得拍照，」俊說，「但它很有價值。」蓮同意。安全節慶必須先照顧人，再談獎項。

The younger visitors were invited to create simple safety cards. One boy drew a hippopotamus under a strong table. Another drew a worm hiding in the earth. Their pictures were funny, but they helped children remember what to do. Mei set up a printer so each visitor could take home a copy. A parent begged the team not to make the directions too long; her child found dense text frightening. Lian shortened the sentences and added pictures. Jun checked that the words were still correct. The cards were not part of the original contest plan, yet they made the event more useful.

年紀較小的訪客受邀製作簡單安全卡。一個男孩畫了躲在堅固桌下的河馬，另一個畫了藏進土裡的蟲子。畫面很好笑，卻能幫孩子記得動作。美架起印表機，讓每位訪客帶一份回家。一位家長懇求團隊不要寫太長的指令，因為孩子看到密密麻麻的文字會害怕。蓮縮短句子並加插圖，俊確認內容仍正確。安全卡不在最初的競賽計畫裡，卻讓活動更實用。

During the drill a visitor slipped near the staircase. She was not injured, but the incident changed the mood. The team stopped the rehearsal and checked the floor. A little water had come through the upper window. Jun asked the manager to fix the seal before the festival, and Lian recorded what had happened. “Do not turn this into a story about a hero saving someone,” she told Mei. “Our goal is to prevent the next slip.” The manager agreed to inspect the windows that afternoon. A careful report had done more good than a dramatic photograph.

演練時一位訪客在樓梯旁滑了一下，雖未受傷，氣氛卻立刻改變。團隊停止排練並查看地面，發現少量雨水從樓上窗戶滲入。俊請管理員在節慶前修好密封處，蓮把經過記錄下來。「不要寫成英雄救人的故事，」她對美說。「我們的目標是避免下一次滑倒。」管理員答應當天下午檢查窗戶。仔細的報告比戲劇性照片更有用。

When the drill ended, Jun suggested a short hike through the garden to relax. Lian accepted, and they walked slowly beneath the trees. He told her that his grandfather had taught him to stretch wood before cutting it, because a rushed hand could waste a rare piece. She said her father had taught her to pause before writing about someone else's life. “Our families were teaching the same lesson,” Jun said. “Maybe,” Lian replied. It was a quiet thought about the relation between patience and care. She was glad they could share it without pretending to be their stage couple.

演練結束，俊提議到花園走一小段路放鬆。蓮答應，兩人慢慢走在樹下。他說祖父以前教他切木頭前先檢查木紋和伸展狀況，因為匆忙可能浪費珍貴木料。蓮說父親教她寫別人的人生前先停下想一想。「我們家教的是同一件事，」俊說。「也許吧，」蓮回答。他們安靜地想到耐心與關心之間的關係。她很高興不必扮演舞臺情侶，也能分享這一刻。

At the last planning meeting, a student proposed a royal entrance with gold cloth and large lights. Mei liked the color, but the design would block the exit. Lian rejected it and offered a simpler banner. Jun helped explain why. The student looked disappointed at first, then asked whether the same cloth could make a small welcome table. “Indeed it can,” Jun said. They moved the idea to a safe place instead of throwing it away. Lian noticed that the group had become better at hearing no. Their teamwork had grown more important than any one person's favorite idea.

最後一次規劃會議，一位學生提出用金布和大燈做出皇家入口。美喜歡顏色，但設計會擋住出口。蓮拒絕，提議用簡單橫幅替代。俊幫忙解釋原因。學生起初失望，隨後問同一塊布能否做小迎賓桌。「的確可以，」俊說。他們不是把點子丟掉，而是放到安全位置。蓮發現團隊愈來愈能接受拒絕。合作已經比任何人的心愛點子更重要。

The next morning a message came from the manager. The window had been repaired, and the fire exits had passed inspection. Mei cheered, then asked whether the group could return to work. Lian answered yes but reminded everyone to take breaks. They had a long week ahead. Jun offered to handle the heavy boards while Lian checked the written signs. She suggested changing roles for an hour so each understood the other's work. He agreed. By evening they were tired, but the room was ready for the first public test. The victory was small and real.

隔天早上管理員傳來訊息：窗戶已修好，消防出口通過檢查。美高興歡呼，問大家能否重新工作。蓮說可以，但提醒每個人休息，因為前方還有漫長一週。俊提議搬重展板，蓮核對文字；她又建議兩人交換一小時，彼此了解對方工作。他答應。傍晚大家很累，但房間已可進行第一次公開測試。這是小而真實的勝利。

One volunteer began to beg the manager to reopen the upper room before the inspection. Lian asked him to stop. The team could recover from a delay, she explained, but not from a preventable injury. Protecting the quality of the event meant accepting a safety decision even when it was frustrating.

一位志工開始懇求管理員在檢查前重新開放樓上房間，蓮請他停下。她解釋，團隊可以從延誤中恢復，卻無法輕易挽回可避免的傷害。維持活動品質，就得在沮喪時仍接受安全決定。

On the way home Jun told Lian that he had almost asked her out for a real dinner. He had stopped because they were both tired and because the contest still needed their attention. She appreciated the honesty. “I am not saying no forever,” she told him. “I am saying I want to finish what we started without confusing anyone.” He nodded. “Then I will not ask again before the festival ends.” She smiled at his respect for her timing. The night air felt cool, and for the first time the distance between their hands seemed like a choice rather than a rule.

回家的路上，俊告訴蓮，他差點問她願不願意真正一起吃晚餐，但兩人都累了，競賽也還需要專注，所以停下來。蓮欣賞他的誠實。「我不是永遠說不，」她告訴他。「我只是想先完成我們開始的事，不要讓任何人混淆。」他點頭：「那節慶結束前我不再問。」她為他尊重自己的步調而微笑。夜裡很涼，兩人手之間的距離第一次像是選擇，而不是規則。
