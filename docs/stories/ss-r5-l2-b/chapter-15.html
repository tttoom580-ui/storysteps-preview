A Fair Share of the Work

不必編造的約會 · 第 15 / 25 章 · 1000–2000 · 英中對照 · 校訂中

用互動版查詞、朗讀與收藏

By midterm the project had grown beyond what two clubs could carry alone. The volunteer workbook listed every task, but the names were not spread equally. Lian counted the hours and made a discovery: a few quiet students had been doing most of the cleaning and carrying. That was not fair. She asked the whole team to meet, then showed a chart with the hours altogether. Nobody was blamed by name. Instead, each group had to suggest a better plan for the next week. Jun supported her, even though his own club had received more help than it had given.

期中時，計畫已超出兩個社團原本能獨力負擔的範圍。志工工作簿列著每項任務，但名字分配不平均。蓮計算工時，發現一件事：少數安靜的學生做了大多數清潔和搬運，這並不公平。她召集全隊，展示總工時圖表。她沒有點名責怪任何人，而是請每組提出下週更好的安排。俊支持她，即使他自己的社團接受的幫助比付出的多。

During the meeting Mei told a story about her aunt, who had recovered from cancer and now visited a community center every week. Her aunt needed a quiet seat near the door, not special praise. “People help in different ways,” Mei said. “Someone who cannot lift a box can still welcome visitors or write letters.” Lian realized that equal hours did not always mean equal effort. Jun suggested a task board with different kinds of work, including jobs that could be done from home. The group agreed to test it. Their rule would be clear: nobody had to explain private health matters to deserve a useful role.

會議中，美談起從癌症康復的阿姨，如今她每週都去社區中心。阿姨需要靠門的安靜座位，而不是特別讚美。「每個人幫忙的方式不同，」美說。「不能搬箱子的人仍能接待訪客、寫信。」蓮明白相同工時不一定代表相同付出。俊提議設置不同種類的任務板，也包括可在家完成的工作。大家同意試行。新規則很清楚：沒有人必須公開私人健康情況，才配得到有用的工作角色。

At lunch they shared mango slices and a plate of pork from a nearby shop. Mei showed the team a drawing of an old castle beside the river. A visitor had suggested adding it to the route, but the building had disappeared before the reading room was built. Lian gave a negative answer to the change, explaining that their story already had enough places to cover. Jun suggested keeping the castle in a separate page of possible future routes. The decision cheered Mei. An idea could be valuable without fitting this project today. The group was learning that saying no could be respectful.

午餐時，大家分享芒果片與附近店家送來的豬肉料理。美拿出河邊舊城堡的圖畫。一名訪客建議把它加進路線，但那座建築早在閱覽室出現前就已消失。蓮否決這個改動，解釋路線已有夠多地點。俊提議把城堡保留在未來可能的路線頁面。美因此高興起來：點子有價值，不表示今天就適合。團隊逐漸學會，拒絕也可以很尊重。

The makers repaired the meeting room's low ceiling panel and moved the refrigerator away from a door so the passage was clear. A student used a small saw under staff supervision to cut a display board. Another volunteer suggested protecting the sign files with a password that more than one leader knew. “No more secret single account,” Lian agreed. They also chose a simple phrase to mark which draft was ready for printing. The new plan kept documents easy to find and reduced the chance of a lost file causing another delay. Jun looked relieved to see others take responsibility for details he had once held alone.

創客社修好會議室低矮的天花板板片，並把冰箱移離門口，讓通道暢通。一位學生在教職員監督下使用小鋸子裁展板。另一名志工建議讓不只一位幹部知道標示檔案的密碼。「不要再由一個人獨占帳號，」蓮同意。他們也選定簡短詞組，標示哪些草稿已可印製。新方式讓文件好找，減少單一檔案遺失造成延誤。俊看見其他人開始承擔過去由他獨自處理的細節，顯得鬆了一口氣。

When the meeting ended, Jun gave Lian a small notebook. “For the things you notice that the rest of us miss,” he said. She thanked him but asked whether it came from project funds. “No,” he said. “I paid for it myself.” She liked the gift but did not want gifts to become a debt between them. She told him so. Jun answered that he expected nothing in return. Lian decided to accept it as a sign of friendship, leaving any other meaning for later. The honesty made the small notebook feel more important than an expensive present.

會議結束，俊送給蓮一本小筆記本。「記下那些我們沒看見、妳卻注意到的事，」他說。她道謝，但先問是不是用計畫經費買的。「不是，」他回答。「我自己付錢。」她喜歡禮物，卻不想讓禮物成為兩人之間的債，於是明說。俊表示不期待回報。蓮決定先把它當成友情的表示，其他含義留待以後。這份誠實讓小筆記本比昂貴禮物更有分量。

That night she used the notebook to write down the names of people who had supported the project quietly: the cleaner who found extra chairs, the cook who saved food, and the guard who opened the hall early. She showed Jun the list and suggested reading it at the final exhibition. He added the name of the student who had noticed the broken cable during the storm. Soon the page was full. Lian felt grateful that their work was not only theirs. An exhibition about shared places should be made by people who knew how to share credit.

那晚蓮用筆記本記下安靜支持計畫的人：找到備用椅子的清潔員、幫忙留餐點的廚師、提早開廳的警衛。她給俊看，建議在最終展覽念出名單。俊又補上雷雨時發現破線的學生姓名。很快整頁寫滿。蓮感謝這份工作不只屬於他們。談共享空間的展覽，也該由懂得共享功勞的人完成。

On the way home, Mei showed them a narrow lane behind the old market. It might make the route shorter, but Jun pointed out that delivery carts used the same space. They agreed not to add the lane until a proper safety test was possible.

回家路上，美帶他們看老市場後的一條窄巷。它或許能縮短路線，但俊指出送貨推車也走那裡。他們同意在正式安全測試之前，不把這條巷子加入路線。

Before sleep, Mei sent a new photograph. It showed Lian and Jun at the task board, arguing gently over a list of chairs. A red heart had been drawn above them again, but this time Mei sent it privately and asked before sharing. Lian replied, “Keep it for now.” She was not angry; she simply wanted the choice to be hers. Jun sent a separate message asking how the day had gone. She answered that she was tired but proud of the team. He replied that he was too. They did not need to call the day a date for it to feel close.

睡前，美傳來一張新照片：蓮與俊站在任務板前，溫和爭論椅子清單。上方又被畫了一顆紅心，但這次美先私下傳送，詢問能否分享。蓮回說：「先留著。」她並不生氣，只希望由自己決定。俊另外傳訊問她今天過得如何。她回答很累，卻為團隊驕傲，他說自己也是。這一天不必稱作約會，也能感覺親近。
