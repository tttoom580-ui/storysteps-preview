屋頂與故事

The Roof and the Story

不必編造的約會 · 第 12 / 25 章 · 1000–2000 · 英中對照 · 校訂中

用互動版查詞、朗讀與收藏

The next week began with a delay. The town could not give the clubs permission to enter the old reading room because the roof was unsafe. Lian felt upset, but Jun said the decision was protective, not evil. “They would be wrong to let us walk under loose tiles,” he said. An engineer sent an exact description of the damage. A beam needed support, and a corner over the door could fall in strong wind. The clubs would travel no farther than the garden until repairs were complete. Lian changed the script to show the building from outside instead of promising visitors a tour inside.

下週一開始就遇到延誤。鎮公所不能讓社團進入舊閱覽室，因為屋頂不安全。蓮很失望，俊卻說這是保護措施，不是惡意。「讓我們走進鬆動屋瓦底下才不對，」他說。工程師送來精確損壞說明：一根樑需要支撐，門上的角落遇到強風可能掉落。維修完成前，社團只能走到花園。蓮修改劇本，改從外部展示建築，不再向參觀者承諾室內導覽。

To cheer the young volunteers, Mei arranged a cultural afternoon under the covered market. A storyteller read from a novel about a village where children tried to repair a broken swing. A dog gave a loud bark whenever the storyteller changed voices. One child laughed so hard that she nearly twisted her ankle, and her father helped her sit down. Lian watched the event closely. Children stayed interested when a story gave them a choice, not only a lesson. She decided to let visitors select one of two endings for the stage play and explain why they chose it.

為了鼓舞年輕志工，美在有遮棚的市場辦了一個文化下午。說故事的人讀一部小說，內容是村中孩子修理壞秋千。每當講者換聲音，一隻狗就大聲吠叫。某個孩子笑得差點扭到腳踝，父親扶她坐下。蓮仔細觀察。故事若讓孩子有選擇，而不只是說教，就能吸引他們。她決定讓觀眾從兩個舞臺結尾中選一個，並說明原因。

A volunteer handed out slices of guava with small spoons and pointed to a sign that said “click here” for a spoken version of the story. The recorded voice was useful to visitors who found reading hard. Jun made the control large enough for older fingers and tested it with a visitor who wore thick glasses. Lian wrote a shorter reply for those who asked how the machine worked. They were learning that access could have many forms: a clear sign, a seat, an audio recording, or simply a patient person willing to answer a question.

一位志工用小湯匙分送芭樂片，指著標示說可以「點這裡」聽有聲故事。對閱讀吃力的訪客，聲音錄製很有幫助。俊把按鈕做得夠大，讓手指不靈活的長者也能操作，並找戴厚眼鏡的訪客測試。蓮替詢問機器用法的人寫了簡短回答。他們學到無障礙有許多形式：清楚標示、座位、錄音，或願意耐心回答的工作人員。

Later the club leaders held a planning meeting. Mei worried that the repair delay would ruin their chance at the grant. Jun opened the budget and showed that they could finish the outdoor exhibition without entering the building. Lian agreed to remove every scene that depended on a door opening. “We cannot build our success upon an unsafe promise,” she said. The group chose a smaller display and asked the town for a clear time to review the roof again. The choice cost them a dramatic scene, but it protected their visitors. Their adviser praised the change.

稍後社團幹部開計畫會議。美擔心維修延誤會讓補助落空。俊攤開預算，說即使不進屋內，戶外展覽仍能完成。蓮同意刪除所有必須開門的場景。「不能把成功建立在不安全的承諾上，」她說。小隊選擇縮小展區，並請鎮公所提出重新檢查屋頂的時間。這個選擇犧牲了戲劇性，卻保護了訪客。指導老師稱讚他們。

After the meeting, Jun noticed Lian looking tired. He did not ask her to smile. Instead, he offered to walk with her to the bus stop. She accepted. When they passed the old bridge, he said he had been upset too. He had imagined showing his grandmother the room where her friend once worked. “I wanted to make one big thing happen,” he said. “But maybe small safe steps are worth more.” Lian touched his sleeve lightly, then let her hand fall. He smiled but made no move to hold it. She appreciated that he let the moment be hers.

會後，俊看見蓮很疲倦，沒有要求她笑，而是問能否陪她走到公車站。她答應了。走過老橋時，他承認自己也很失落。他曾想帶祖母去看朋友工作過的閱覽室。「我想讓一件大事成功，」他說。「但也許安全的小步更有價值。」蓮輕輕碰了碰他的袖子，隨即放下手。他微笑，沒有伸手去握。她很感激他讓這一刻由自己決定。

That night a youth volunteer sent a drawing of the reading room as it might look after repairs. Lian loved it but replied that the picture must be marked as a hope, not a photograph of present reality. The volunteer agreed and added the words “One Possible Future.” Jun placed the drawing beside an exact picture of the damaged roof. Side by side, they told an honest story: here is what exists, and here is what people might build if they work together. Lian closed her laptop feeling less upset. A dream did not need a false promise to remain worth sharing.

當晚，一位青年志工畫了閱覽室修復後可能的樣子。蓮很喜歡，卻回覆說要標明這是希望，不是現在的實景照片。志工同意，添上「一種可能的未來」。俊把畫放在破損屋頂的真實照片旁。兩張圖並列，說出誠實的故事：這是現況；而如果大家合作，也可能建成另一個樣子。蓮闔上筆電時已不那麼失落。夢想不必靠虛假承諾才值得分享。

Before sleeping, Lian revised the final stage scene. In it, the fictional couple no longer opened the old door. Instead, they placed a table outside and invited neighbors to tell stories. The choice made the script quieter but more believable. She sent the change to Jun, who replied, “This ending is stronger.” Lian leaned back and thought about their own unfinished story. It was full of delays and choices, none of which had appeared in the first poster. That might be why she was beginning to care about it. Real life did not follow a script, and she was learning to accept that.

睡前，蓮修改最後一場戲。虛構情侶不再打開舊門，而是在外頭擺桌子，邀請鄰居講故事。新選擇讓劇本安靜些，卻更可信。她把修改傳給俊，他回說：「這個結尾更有力量。」蓮靠在椅背，想起兩人自己尚未寫完的故事。裡面有很多延誤與選擇，第一張海報一項也沒預告。也許正因如此，她才開始在乎它。真實生活不照劇本走，她正在學著接受。
