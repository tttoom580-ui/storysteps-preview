童年的地圖

The Map from Childhood

不必編造的約會 · 第 8 / 25 章 · 1000–2000 · 英中對照 · 校訂中

用互動版查詞、朗讀與收藏

At the next rehearsal, the team tested an average walking speed instead of asking everyone to hurry. Jun had drawn arrows on the ground, but rain made two of them disappear. Lian set a gentle pace and asked visitors to tell her when the route became hard. A grandfather carrying a load of shopping stopped by the old bridge. “I came here in my childhood,” he said. “We used to eat barbecue on that bank.” His story gave the place meaning beyond its stone walls. Jun recorded the distance and drew the safe path forward. A map, he was learning, was also a record of people's lives.

下一次彩排，團隊測試一般步行速度，不再要求所有人趕路。俊在地上畫了箭頭，但雨水讓兩個箭頭消失。蓮放慢腳步，請參觀者在路線難走時直接說。一位提著沉重採購袋的爺爺停在舊橋邊。「我小時候常來這裡，」他說。「以前會在岸邊烤肉。」他的故事讓這個地方有了石牆以外的意義。俊記下距離，畫出往前走的安全方向。他逐漸明白，地圖也是人們生活的紀錄。

A little boy wanted to carry the map and give the next command. His mother asked him to wait until the guide invited him. The boy obeyed and then carefully held the paper with both hands. At the market he pointed to a shelf full of chopsticks and asked why an old photograph showed people eating with wooden spoons. Lian explained that objects changed as shops changed. She did not know the source of the photograph, however, so she promised to check before writing that part of the script. The child seemed pleased that adults could say they did not know something.

一個小男孩想拿地圖發出下一個指令，母親請他等導覽員邀請。他照做，之後用雙手小心拿紙。到了市場，他指著放滿筷子的架子，問為什麼舊照片裡有人拿木湯匙吃飯。蓮解釋，店舖改變時物品也會跟著變；但她不知道照片來源，所以承諾查清再寫入劇本。孩子很高興大人也能坦承自己不知道。

Near the old wall, a sign used the word servant to describe a woman who had cleaned the reading room many years ago. Mei thought the word might hide the woman's actual work. The family called her a teacher, not a servant, because she had taught children to read. Lian asked whom they should contact to check the description. Jun said he would not stick the new label over the old one until the family replied. “Whatever we choose, we must not make her smaller,” Lian said. They took a picture of the stone sign and left it untouched.

老牆旁有一塊牌子，把多年以前清潔閱覽室的婦女稱作「僕人」。美覺得這個詞可能遮住她真正做過的事。家人稱她為老師，不是僕人，因為她曾教孩子識字。蓮詢問該聯絡誰核對說明。俊說，在家人回覆前，他不會把新標籤直接貼到舊牌上。「不管怎麼選，都不能把她的貢獻說小了，」蓮說。他們拍下石牌，沒有動它。

The following day, a visitor asked whether the old room would truly reopen. Lian wanted to answer yes, but the reality was uncertain. They had no key, no repair budget, and no agreement with the town. “There are several means of keeping the memory alive,” she said. “Opening the room is one possibility, not a promise yet.” Jun appreciated her answer. He had once believed a good design could force a result. Now he understood that many people had to agree before a public door could open. The route was making his thinking broader as well as his drawings better.

隔天，一位參觀者問閱覽室是不是真的會重新開放。蓮很想說會，但現實並不確定：他們沒有鑰匙、沒有維修預算，也沒有和城鎮達成協議。「保存記憶有好幾種方法，」她說。「重新開放是可能性之一，現在還不能承諾。」俊欣賞她的回答。他以前以為好的設計就能逼出成果，如今明白公共大門要打開，需要許多人同意。這條路讓他的思考和圖面一起變得更寬廣。

During a quiet moment at the bridge, Lian told Jun about her father. When she was small, he took her to the market each Sunday and asked her to remember one new thing. After he died, she kept doing it because it made the world feel close. Jun listened without telling her to cheer up. “Thank you for trusting me with that,” he said. She felt a warmth that had nothing to do with the planned romance on stage. They stood beside the water until a cyclist passed and reminded them that the group was waiting. Neither had to pretend the moment was less important than it was.

橋邊有一段安靜時間，蓮向俊談起父親。小時候，父親每週日帶她去市場，要她記住一樣新事物。父親過世後，她繼續這個習慣，因為它讓世界仍感覺很近。俊靜靜聽，沒有急著叫她振作。「謝謝妳願意告訴我，」他說。蓮心裡的溫暖，和舞臺上的安排無關。他們站在水邊，直到自行車騎士經過，才想起隊伍在等。兩人都不必假裝那一刻不重要。

At the end of the walk, Mei asked visitors to place a mark on a large map: green for places they could reach easily, yellow for places that needed care. The new marks showed a problem the club had missed. Many older visitors avoided the steep path beside the market. Jun redrew the route without complaint, even though it meant losing one of his favorite signs. Lian helped him move the paper. Their story was no longer simply about two characters falling in love. It was about two people learning to listen to the town around them.

步行結束，美請參觀者在大地圖上做記號：綠色代表容易抵達，黃色代表需要改善。新標記顯示社團忽略了一項問題：許多年長訪客會避開市場旁的陡坡。俊沒有抱怨就重新畫路線，即使這表示他最喜歡的一塊標示得撤掉。蓮幫他挪動圖紙。他們的故事已不再只是兩個角色相愛，而是兩個人學著傾聽周遭城鎮。

A boy said the garden's fate was surely to disappear, since it had been locked for years. Lian answered that nobody knew the future yet. The past could teach them to ask better questions, but they still had to make decisions in the present.

一位男孩說花園的命運一定是消失，因為它已鎖了多年。蓮回答，沒有人現在就知道未來。過去能教大家提出更好的問題，但眼前仍得自己作決定。

That night the group read the comments together. Some wanted more seats, some wanted less reading, and some asked for music at every stop. They could not obey every request, but they could explain each decision. Lian wrote a short note about the difference between a story's happy ending and a real public change. Jun placed it at the beginning of the route. The message was simple: a better town begins when people pay attention to what others need. The fake date had helped the visitors follow the play, yet the real lesson came from the people walking beside them.

當晚大家一起讀回饋。有人要更多座椅，有人要少一點文字，有人希望每站都有音樂。他們不可能照辦每個要求，但可以解釋每項決定。蓮寫了一段短文，說明故事的幸福結局與現實公共改變有何不同。俊把它放在路線起點。訊息很簡單：更好的城鎮，從注意別人的需要開始。假約會幫觀眾進入劇情，真正的課卻來自並肩走路的人。
