不必編造的約會 · 第 19 / 25 章 · 1000–2000 · 英中對照 · 校訂中

On Monday the local paper published an article about the exhibition. The headline described the fake dating scene but said little about the work behind it. Lian felt alone for a moment, even with a room full of friends. The latest photographs showed her standing close to Jun, and strangers began to discuss whether their relationship was real. Mei wanted to answer every comment. Lian asked for one careful discussion instead. “Our private lives are not the project,” she said. “We should describe the actual work, correct the public facts, and protect everyone's right to make personal choices.” Jun supported her immediately.

星期一，地方報紙刊出展覽文章。標題描述假約會表演，卻很少提幕後工作。即使身旁都是朋友，蓮一瞬間仍感到孤單。最新照片拍到她站在俊旁邊，陌生人開始討論兩人是否真的交往。美想回覆每一則留言，蓮則要求大家先好好討論。「我們的私生活不是計畫，」她說。「應說明實際工作、更正公共事實，並保護每個人做私人選擇的權利。」俊立刻支持她。

The paper also claimed that the clubs had promised to repair the reading room. That statement was not true. They had offered an outdoor display and a possible plan for the town to consider. Lian wrote a clear correction and included a link to the full project report. A delivery worker from the newspaper office came to collect the printed reply. He apologized for the mistake but explained that he was only an employee, not the editor. Lian thanked him and sent the correction through the proper channel. She did not make a stranger responsible for a decision he had not made.

報紙還聲稱社團承諾修好閱覽室，這並不正確。他們提出的是戶外展覽，以及可供城鎮考慮的未來方案。蓮寫了清楚的更正，附上完整計畫報告連結。一位報社送件員來收紙本回覆，為錯誤道歉，卻說自己只是員工，不是編輯。蓮謝謝他，透過正確管道送出更正。她不會要求陌生人為不是自己做的決定負責。

At the campus office, Jun explained the difficulty of keeping the old room closed while inviting people to care about it. Some guests thought the clubs were asking for money; others believed the doors would open next week. The team needed more effective signs. Mei changed the introduction to say, “The room is not open. The outdoor exhibition is.” Jun printed the line in large letters and placed a mat near the information desk so people could stand safely after rain. Lian liked the direct wording. An honest answer was often shorter than the rumor it corrected.

在校園辦公室，俊說明一個難題：他們希望大家關心舊閱覽室，卻又必須讓屋子維持關閉。有些訪客以為社團募款修屋，另一些以為下週就開門。團隊需要更有效的標示。美把引言改為：「閱覽室未開放；戶外展覽有開放。」俊用大字印出，並在資訊桌旁放止滑墊，以免下雨後有人滑倒。蓮喜歡直接的說法。誠實回答往往比需要更正的謠言短。

A visitor with a toothache asked where he could find a dentist. Lian told him the address of the campus clinic and did not try to offer treatment. Another guest asked whether the route included a place for private prayer. Mei showed him a quiet area that anyone could use respectfully. They had learned that small practical needs could change how people experienced a public event. A traditional performance could be beautiful, but it was not enough if visitors felt lost or unsafe. Every question helped the team see a different part of the same town.

一位牙痛訪客問附近哪裡有牙醫，蓮告訴他校園診所位置，沒有試著自己治療。另一位訪客問路線上是否有可安靜禱告的地方，美指給他一個任何人都能尊重使用的安靜區。他們學到小小的實際需求能改變人們感受公共活動的方式。傳統表演可以很美，但若訪客迷路或感到不安全，就仍不夠。每個提問都讓團隊看見城鎮的另一面。

That evening Jun brought pizza for the volunteers who had stayed to rewrite the signs. They sat on the floor and ate while reading the revised policy about public photographs. It said that visitors could ask to be left out of the club's posts. Lian insisted that the rule apply even if a picture was very good. Jun agreed. He admitted that he had once treated every successful design as something to show off. Now he understood that a beautiful picture could still cause trouble for someone who did not want to appear in it. The volunteers added a clear notice at the entrance.

晚上俊替留下改寫標示的志工帶來披薩。大家坐在地上吃，一邊讀修改後的公開照片政策。新規定指出，訪客可以要求不出現在社團貼文裡。蓮堅持再漂亮的照片也要適用。俊同意。他坦承以前常把每個成功設計都當作應該炫耀的東西，如今明白漂亮照片仍可能困擾不想入鏡的人。志工在入口加上一塊明確告示。

Later Lian told Jun she was tired of strangers deciding how she felt. He did not ask her to make a public romantic statement to silence them. “We can say what the play is,” he replied. “We do not have to explain ourselves.” She felt grateful. She also felt the fear of making their friendship harder if she admitted her growing affection. For months, the fake date had given them a safe distance. Now that distance was beginning to feel strange. She did not want to keep using a role to avoid a choice. She decided to speak after the final contest.

稍晚蓮告訴俊，自己厭倦陌生人替她決定感受。他沒有要求公開愛情宣言來堵住眾人嘴巴。「可以說清楚戲是什麼，」他回答。「不必解釋我們自己。」她很感激，也害怕承認逐漸滋長的好感會使友情變難。幾個月來，假約會給他們安全距離，現在那距離卻開始奇怪。她不想繼續用角色逃避選擇，決定等最終競賽後再說。

The newspaper offered a link where readers could download the corrected report. Lian checked that the file showed the necessary permissions and did not share private phone numbers. The correction had an effect: people began asking more useful questions. The team still faced a struggle against rumor, but the most effective answer was a clear public record.

報社提供連結，讓讀者下載修正後的報告。蓮確認檔案附有必要的授權資訊，且沒有公開私人電話。更正產生了效果，人們開始提出更有用的問題。團隊仍在努力對抗謠言，但最有效的回答是一份清楚的公開紀錄。

Before sleeping, Lian looked through the old photographs once more. The reading room had survived storms, changes in town policy, and long periods of silence. People had kept its memory alive without any contest. She hoped their project would add one more honest chapter, even if it did not win. Jun sent her a copy of the revised route and a simple question: “Are you okay?” She answered, “Tired, but okay. Thank you.” He replied, “Good night.” Nothing dramatic happened. The small exchange still helped her feel less alone.

睡前，蓮再次翻看老照片。閱覽室經歷暴風雨、城鎮政策變動和長久沉默，仍有人記得它，完全不靠競賽。她希望計畫能增加誠實的新一章，即使沒得獎也好。俊傳來更新路線，附一句簡單的問候：「妳還好嗎？」她回答：「累，但還好。謝謝你。」他回：「晚安。」沒有戲劇性事件，這個小小交流仍讓她不那麼孤單。
