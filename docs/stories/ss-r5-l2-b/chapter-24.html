我們選擇的那一幕

The Scene We Chose

不必編造的約會 · 第 24 / 25 章 · 1000–2000 · 英中對照 · 校訂中

用互動版查詞、朗讀與收藏

The hall looked different when Lian arrived on the final morning. Colorful balloons were tied to the entrance, but the path downstairs remained clear. A student had brought a drawing of an ape and a camel for the children's table, and the bakery across the street sent warm bread. Everyone seemed to behave with more care after the storm. Jun was checking the signs, while Mei was helping a volunteer carry the last box. Lian felt a sharp memory of their first meeting. Back then she had expected failure because she believed the two clubs could never work together. Now the room itself proved otherwise.

最後一天早上，蓮到場時禮堂已換了樣子。入口綁著彩色氣球，但通往樓下的路仍暢通。一位學生替兒童桌帶來猿猴與駱駝畫作，街對面麵包店送來熱麵包。暴風雨之後，大家做事似乎更留心。俊正在檢查標示，美幫志工搬最後一箱。蓮鮮明地想起第一次會議。那時她預期失敗，因為相信兩社團永遠無法合作。現在這間屋子本身就證明了不同答案。

The opening scene began with two fictional characters meeting on a street. They looked at a closed door and argued about what it meant. One wanted to break it open at once; the other asked who could be hurt and who had the right to decide. The actors did not promise to fix everything. They invited the audience to look at the records and choose the next step. The scene was simple, and the message was clear. A future had to be built on a basis of truth, safety, and public choice. The visitors listened without waiting for a romantic kiss.

開場戲是兩位虛構角色在街上相遇，看著關閉的門，爭論其意義。一個想立即打開，另一個問這樣可能傷害誰，以及誰有權決定。演員沒有承諾修好一切，而是邀請觀眾看紀錄、選下一步。這一幕簡單而清楚：未來應建立在真相、安全與公共選擇的基礎上。觀眾安靜傾聽，並沒有等著浪漫的吻。

A former librarian was invited to speak. She thanked the clubs for showing the old room without pretending it was ready to open. Her hands shook a little, but her voice was strong. She remembered children reading by the windows and neighbors repairing chairs together. “The room did not survive in our memory because the roof was perfect,” she said. “It survived because we cared for one another.” Lian listened with tears in her eyes. She had once thought the contest prize would make the work complete. Now she knew a prize was only one small part of a story people would continue telling.

一位前圖書館員受邀發言。她感謝社團展示老屋，卻沒有假裝它已能開放。她雙手微微發抖，聲音卻堅定。她記得孩子在窗邊讀書、鄰居一起修椅子。「這間房在我們記憶裡活下來，不是因為屋頂完美，」她說。「是因為我們曾互相關心。」蓮眼裡含淚。她以前以為得獎才能讓工作完整，如今知道獎只是人們繼續講述的故事裡一小部分。

At the memory table, visitors could leave a short note or draw a picture. A child wrote about her grandmother, while a young man thanked the volunteers for a safe place to sit during the storm. Some people wrote about pain and loss. Others left funny drawings. The team did not mix their voices into one perfect message. Each person had a different scene to remember. Jun helped an older visitor reach the pen without taking it from her hand. Lian saw the gesture and smiled. It showed the kind of respect she wanted the whole exhibition to offer.

記憶桌讓訪客留下短文字或畫圖。一個孩子寫祖母的事，一位年輕人感謝暴風雨時志工提供安全座位。有些人寫痛苦與失落，也有人留下好笑圖案。團隊沒有把所有聲音混成完美訊息，每個人記得的場景不同。俊幫一位年長訪客拿到筆，沒有直接替她寫。蓮看見這個動作微笑了。這正是她希望整個展覽提供的尊重。

Before the closing performance, Jun found Lian beside the old poster. He wore a simple jacket and looked handsome, but she liked the careful way he checked the stage more than his appearance. “Our last fake date,” he said quietly. “Our last stage date,” she corrected with a smile. They reviewed their lines and agreed to stop the scene if either needed to. She felt nervous, but not trapped. A promise made freely was different from a role forced on someone. She was ready to perform because she had chosen it. The audience's expectations no longer decided what her part would be.

閉幕演出前，俊在舊海報旁找到蓮。他穿著簡單外套，看起來很英俊，但蓮喜歡的是他仔細檢查舞臺的方式，而不只是外貌。「我們最後一次假約會，」他低聲說。「最後一次舞臺約會，」她笑著糾正。兩人核對臺詞，同意任何一方有需要都能停演。她緊張，卻不覺得受困。自由作出的承諾不同於被迫扮演角色。她準備好上臺，因為這是自己的選擇，不再由觀眾期待決定。

The final scene did not end with the door opening. The fictional pair placed their hands on a table full of memories and asked the visitors what they would carry into the next year. A child said kindness. An older woman said patience. A student said better signs. Laughter moved through the hall. Lian and Jun bowed together, then stepped back so the whole volunteer team could come forward. The applause belonged to everyone. Lian's fear of failure had not disappeared completely, but it no longer controlled her. They had made something useful, and they had made it together.

最後一幕沒有開門。虛構的一對把手放在滿是記憶的桌上，問觀眾想把什麼帶進下一年。孩子說善意，長者說耐心，一位學生說更好的標示。禮堂響起笑聲。蓮與俊一起鞠躬，然後退開，讓整個志工隊上前。掌聲屬於所有人。蓮對失敗的害怕沒有完全消失，但已不再控制她。他們共同做成了一件有用的事。

Lian noticed the absence of the first gardener, who had been ill that week. She sent him a photograph and a note of thanks instead of calling for him to attend. The strange mixture of joy and worry made her nervous, but she found the nerve to speak clearly on stage. The event was for people, including those who could not be there.

蓮注意到最早協助的園丁缺席了，因為他這週生病。她寄照片與謝卡給他，沒有要求他到場。喜悅與擔心混在一起，讓她緊張，但她仍鼓起勇氣在臺上清楚說話。活動是為人而辦，也包括無法親自來的人。

After the hall emptied, Mei handed Lian a small packet of salty crackers and told her to eat before cleaning. Jun carried the final boxes to storage with two volunteers. The team completed the safety list and returned every borrowed item. No one was too tired to remember the people who had helped. When the last light went off, Lian stood outside and saw the quiet street. The contest had once seemed like the whole world. Now she knew it was one chapter, not the entire book. Tomorrow would bring results. Tonight they could rest.

禮堂清空後，美遞給蓮一包鹹餅乾，叫她清掃前先吃。俊與兩位志工把最後的箱子搬去倉庫。團隊完成安全清單，歸還每一件借物。大家雖疲倦，仍沒有忘記協助過的人。最後一盞燈熄滅時，蓮站在外面看安靜的街道。競賽以前像整個世界，如今她知道它只是一章，不是整本書。明天會公布結果，今晚可以休息。
