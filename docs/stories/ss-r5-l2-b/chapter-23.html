我們欠觀眾的真話

What We Owe the Audience

不必編造的約會 · 第 23 / 25 章 · 1000–2000 · 英中對照 · 校訂中

用互動版查詞、朗讀與收藏

The morning after the storm, the school approved a small indoor exhibition for the following afternoon. Lian called a formal meeting to explain the change. She said the team could cancel its stage romance at any time, even on the day of the event, if either actor felt uncomfortable. Jun agreed. Their friendship had become important, but that did not give classmates the right to decide what it meant. A volunteer asked whether the fake date should be mentioned in the introduction. Lian answered yes. “We should say plainly what is fictional and what is real,” she said. “Our visitors deserve that much.”

暴風雨隔天早上，學校核准隔天下午辦小型室內展。蓮召開正式會議說明變動，表示即使到了活動當天，只要任一演員不舒服，都能取消臺上的戀愛演出。俊同意。他們的友情已很重要，但不代表同學有權替它下定義。一位志工問開場是否應提假約會，蓮回答應該。「要清楚說哪些是虛構、哪些是真實，」她說。「觀眾至少值得知道這些。」

Mei prepared a drawing of two stage characters beside a different picture of the actual club team. One showed a romantic tale; the other showed the many responsible people who had built the exhibition. Jun measured the width of the signs and added a triangle marking the safe path through the hall. A student offered to dress as a hero, but Lian thanked him and explained that their story needed no rescuer. They were telling how ordinary people worked together. The student's costume could be used for a children's tale later. He accepted the change with good manner and went to help move chairs.

美畫了兩張圖：一張是兩名舞臺角色，一張是真實社團隊伍。前者呈現浪漫故事，後者呈現許多共同完成展覽的負責任的人。俊量標示寬度，用三角形標記禮堂安全路線。一位學生提議打扮成英雄，蓮道謝，解釋他們的故事不需要救星。他們講的是普通人合作。學生的服裝可以留給未來兒童故事。他有禮貌地接受變更，轉身幫忙搬椅子。

At lunch Lian felt too thirsty to speak and drank two glasses of water. She had forgotten breakfast, and the stress of the last week was beginning to show. Jun brought a simple meal and asked if she wanted company. She said yes. They ate without discussing the contest for several minutes. Then she told him she had started to care about him beyond the role they played. She did not ask for an immediate answer. He put down his spoon and said softly, “I have feelings for you too. But I will not use the play to ask you for anything.” Lian felt both nervous and relieved.

午餐時蓮渴得幾乎說不出話，連喝兩杯水。她忘了吃早餐，上一週壓力已開始顯現。俊帶來簡單餐點，問她需不需要有人陪。她說好。兩人先安靜吃了幾分鐘，沒有談比賽。接著她告訴俊，自己已開始在意角色以外的他，但不要求立刻回答。他放下湯匙輕聲說：「我也對妳有感覺。但我不會利用戲來向妳要求什麼。」蓮既緊張又鬆了一口氣。

They agreed to leave the question of dating until after the final result, when there would be no audience waiting for a scene. Lian knew she could change her mind anytime. Jun said the same was true for him. They were both adults with plans outside the club: she wanted to write a book, and he hoped to build useful public spaces. A relationship, if they chose it, would have to make room for those goals. Lian liked hearing that. She did not want a romance that erased the life she already had. She wanted someone who could walk beside it.

他們同意等最終結果出來、沒有觀眾期待一幕戲時，再談真正交往。蓮知道自己隨時可以改變主意，俊說他也一樣。兩人都是有社團之外計畫的成年人：她想寫書，他想建造實用的公共空間。若選擇交往，關係就得容納那些目標。蓮喜歡這樣的想法。她不要抹去現有生活的愛情，而是希望有人能與這份生活並肩走。

That afternoon the club checked its final public notice. The text mentioned the fictional romance at the beginning and named the people who had given permission to use their stories. A small ladybug landed on the table while Mei read the list. She smiled and said it seemed like good luck. Lian did not need luck to be certain about the words. She had examined them carefully. At the same time she realized that she could not control how everyone would react. She could only offer the truth, be ready to listen, and correct mistakes she discovered later.

下午社團檢查最後的公開告示。文字在開頭註明虛構戀愛，也列出同意提供故事的人。美念名單時，一隻瓢蟲落在桌上。她笑說這像好運。蓮不需要運氣來確定文字正確，因為她已仔細檢查；同時她也明白無法控制每個人的反應。她能做的只有提供真相、願意傾聽，日後發現錯誤再更正。

In the evening Mei asked whether Lian was happy. Lian answered that she was, but the happiness had no single cause. She was proud of her team, hopeful about the reading room, and grateful that Jun had respected her choices. Mei hugged her and promised not to post personal photographs without permission. “You have grown up,” she teased. “We are all still learning,” Lian replied. The two friends laughed, then returned to work. There were still tables to move and chairs to test. A good friendship could celebrate an important moment without letting the practical tasks disappear.

晚上美問蓮開不開心。蓮說開心，但原因不只一個：她為團隊驕傲，對閱覽室抱著希望，也感謝俊尊重她的選擇。美抱抱她，承諾未經許可不再發私下照片。「妳長大了，」她打趣。「我們都還在學，」蓮回答。兩位朋友笑了，又回去工作。桌子要搬，椅子要測試。好友情可以慶祝重要時刻，同時不讓實際任務消失。

Before the public meeting, a volunteer told Lian that her employer had changed her work hours, so she needed to leave early. Lian thanked her for speaking up. Another helper asked about a special diet at the food table. Mei made the room silent for a moment to check the request, then used a steel ruler to measure space beside a chair so a visitor could rest without hurting her hip.

公開會議前，一位志工說雇主調整了工作時間，她必須早點離開。蓮謝謝她提前說明。另一位助手詢問餐桌能否照顧特殊飲食需求。美請房間短暫安靜，好核對需求，再用鋼尺量出椅旁空間，讓一位訪客能舒適休息，不會傷到髖部。

Before leaving, Lian found the original poster in a storage box. She looked at the red heart that had begun the rumor. Then she placed beside it the new sign that explained the stage play honestly. She would display both in the final exhibition to show how the project had changed. The first picture had tried to make people curious. The new sign asked them to think. Lian believed the second was more respectful. She turned off the light and went home without fear of tomorrow's questions. She did not know every answer, but she knew how she wanted to give them.

離開前，蓮在儲物箱找到第一張海報。她看著那顆開啟謠言的紅心，再把誠實說明舞臺戲的新版告示放在旁邊。她決定兩張都放進最後展覽，呈現計畫如何改變。第一張想引人好奇，新告示則邀請人思考。蓮認為後者更尊重觀眾。她關燈回家，不再害怕隔天的問題。她不知道所有答案，卻知道自己想用什麼方式回答。
