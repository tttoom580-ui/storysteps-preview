沒有承諾的合照

A Photograph Without a Promise

不必編造的約會 · 第 1 / 25 章 · 1000–2000 · 英中對照

用互動版查詞、朗讀與收藏

At the first meeting of the autumn term, Lian arrived at the university café carrying a plain folder and a clear plan. She was twenty-two and led the Paper Lantern Club. Across the room sat Jun, a twenty-four-year-old design student with a serious face and a short beard. His Makers Club had beaten hers in the spring contest. Now the school would give one grant to a joint team, and their advisers wanted the two clubs to work together. Lian did not like the idea. Jun raised one hand and said, “I do not want a fight either. I want a project that works.”

秋季學期第一次會議，二十二歲的蓮帶著素色資料夾和明確計畫走進大學咖啡館。她是「紙燈社」社長。房間另一頭坐著二十四歲的設計系學生俊，神情嚴肅，留著短鬍子。他的「創客社」曾在春季競賽中擊敗紙燈社。現在學校只會把一筆補助交給跨社團團隊，兩邊的指導老師希望他們合作。蓮不喜歡這個主意。俊舉起手說：「我也不想吵架。我只想做出真正能運作的作品。」

Their task was to build a walking exhibition called “A Date with Our Town.” Visitors would follow a story about two people learning to see the city together. The first poster showed Lian and Jun standing under a tree, holding a paper map. A friend had drawn a tiny red heart beside them as a joke. Within an hour, half the campus believed they were dating. “That feeling is not funny,” Lian told Jun. “People will judge our work by a rumor.” He agreed to divide the work fairly: she would write the stories, and he would build the signs, but both would approve every public message.

他們的任務是製作名為「和城市約會」的步行展覽。參觀者會跟著一段兩人重新認識城市的故事走。第一張海報裡，蓮與俊站在樹下，手拿紙地圖。有朋友開玩笑，在兩人旁邊畫了一顆小小的紅心。一個小時內，半個校園都以為他們在交往。「這種感覺一點也不好笑，」蓮對俊說。「大家會用謠言評判作品。」俊同意公平分工：她寫故事，他做標示，但所有公開訊息都要兩人共同確認。

The next morning a gardener brought them a dry leaf from the old public garden. The city planned to cut down the garden wall, and he hoped their exhibition would help people remember the place. He asked one favor: please tell the true history, not just a pretty love tale. Jun found a rubber model of the old gate in storage. Lian found a report about a chemical spill years earlier, but she refused to call it a crime without evidence. They could relate the garden story to a safe walking route. It was their first useful decision together, and neither needed to pretend to be in love to make it.

隔天早上，一位園丁送來公共老花園的一片乾葉。市府計畫拆除花園圍牆，他希望展覽能幫大家記起那個地方。他只拜託一件事：說出真實歷史，不要只有漂亮的愛情故事。俊從倉庫找到舊大門的橡膠模型。蓮則找到一份多年以前的化學物質外洩報告，但沒有證據前，她拒絕稱之為犯罪。他們決定把花園故事連成安全的步行路線。這是第一次有效合作，並不需要假裝相愛。

At lunch their adviser put two cards on the table. One asked visitors to weigh a bag of local seeds, while the other explained the old market tax that once paid for the garden. Lian liked the idea of touching real objects instead of reading long speeches. Jun checked the temperature of the display room because paper could bend in the heat. His personality was quiet but careful. Her friends, by contrast, wanted a romantic photograph for the opening. “A staged date could be part of our play,” said Lian, “but the visitors must know it is a play.” Jun answered, “And we both have the right to stop.”

午餐時，指導老師把兩張卡片放在桌上。一張請參觀者秤一袋本地種子，另一張說明過去支應花園費用的市場稅。蓮喜歡讓人動手接觸實物，而不是聽冗長演講。俊則檢查展示室溫度，因為紙張遇熱容易彎曲。他的個性安靜卻細心；相反地，蓮的朋友們想替開幕拍浪漫合照。「我們可以在戲裡演一場約會，」蓮說，「但觀眾必須知道那是戲。」俊回答：「而且我們兩人都可以隨時停止。」

They wrote a short agreement on the back of the map. They would act as a couple only during announced practice walks and public scenes. Outside those times, nobody could use their names to sell tickets or suggest a private relationship. Jun added that each of them could ask for a break without giving a reason. Lian smiled for the first time. In the corner, a student in pajamas ate watermelon after an overnight laboratory shift. She watched them sign and said, “You two look less like lovers and more like careful lawyers.” Lian laughed. That was exactly how she preferred to begin.

他們在地圖背面寫下一份簡短協議：只有公告過的彩排與公開演出，才會扮演情侶；其他時候，任何人都不能拿他們的名字賣票，或暗示兩人私下交往。俊又加上一條：任何一方都能不必解釋理由就要求休息。蓮第一次笑了。角落裡，一位剛結束通宵實驗、穿著睡衣的學生正在吃西瓜。她看見兩人簽字便說：「你們看起來比較像謹慎的律師，不像戀人。」蓮笑出聲來。她正喜歡這樣的開始。

That evening the clubs gathered for their first rehearsal. Lian walked several steps ahead to check whether a person with a cane could follow the path. Jun stayed behind to measure the slope. When he caught up, he did not take her hand for the photograph; he asked first. “Not today,” she said. “We can hold the map between us.” He nodded without looking hurt. A student took the new picture. There was no red heart in this one, only two people studying the same street. Lian kept a copy. She had entered the meeting expecting another rival. She went home with a partner and a boundary he had respected.

當晚，兩個社團集合第一次彩排。蓮走在前面檢查拿手杖的人能不能通行；俊落在後面測量斜坡。追上她之後，他沒有直接牽手拍照，而是先問。「今天不要，」她說。「我們可以一起拿地圖。」他點頭，沒有露出受傷的神情。另一位同學拍下新照片，這次沒有紅心，只有兩個人研究同一條街。蓮留了一張。她原以為會再次遇見競爭對手，回家時卻多了一個尊重界線的搭檔。

Before leaving, Lian noticed how such a small drawing had changed so many conversations. She asked Mei to keep the first poster in the project file, not on the public wall. It would remind them how easily a playful image could tell a story nobody had agreed to live.

離開前，蓮注意到如此小的一幅畫竟改變了那麼多對話。她請美把第一張海報存進專案檔案，不要繼續公開張貼。它會提醒大家，玩笑圖像多容易替當事人編出未曾同意的人生故事。

Before sleeping, Lian opened the old garden file. The report had a missing final page, and the date on its cover did not match the gardener's memory. She sent Jun a note: “Our story may have a real question inside it.” He answered with a drawing of the wall and three possible places where a record might be stored. Their agreement did not promise friendship, much less love. Yet it did promise to tell the truth. For two students who had spent a year trying to beat each other, that small promise already felt larger than a contest.

睡前，蓮打開老花園檔案。報告少了最後一頁，封面的日期也與園丁的記憶不同。她傳訊息給俊：「我們的故事裡，可能藏著真正的疑問。」他回覆一張圍牆草圖，標出三個可能保存紀錄的地方。他們的協議沒有承諾友情，更沒有承諾愛情；卻承諾要說真話。對兩個花了一年想擊敗彼此的學生而言，這個小承諾已經比競賽更重要。
