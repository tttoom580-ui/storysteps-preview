燈光下的辯論

The Debate Under the Lights

不必編造的約會 · 第 14 / 25 章 · 1000–2000 · 英中對照 · 校訂中

用互動版查詞、朗讀與收藏

The university debate took place in a lecture hall filled with electric lamps. Each team had ten minutes to explain its project, followed by questions from students. Lian began with a short conversation from the play, then stepped out of character to describe the real work. She showed a map of the southern gate and gave the detail that mattered most: older visitors could not pass until the narrow corner was changed. “A suitable project must work beyond a pretty photograph,” she said. Jun followed with his design and explained which parts the club could produce without heavy construction. Their voices were steady, but both were nervous.

大學辯論在滿是電燈的演講廳舉行。每隊有十分鐘說明計畫，接著接受學生提問。蓮先表演劇中一段短對話，再走出角色講真正的工作。她展示南側大門地圖，指出最重要的細節：狹窄轉角改善前，年長訪客無法通行。「適合大眾的計畫，不能只在漂亮照片裡有用，」她說。俊接著展示設計，說明社團能在不進行大型工程的前提下製作哪些部分。兩人語氣穩定，心裡卻都緊張。

A rival team asked whether their fictional romance was a trick to attract votes. Lian did not hide behind a mask or pretend to be hurt. She explained that the route was a stage story, marked clearly as fiction, and that the clubs had corrected the earlier misleading photograph. The student pressed for a different ending: why not make the two characters promise a wedding? Lian answered that a tale about public space did not need a romantic promise to matter. Jun added that their real partnership was about work and consent. Their answers did not make the room silent, but the question moved back to the exhibition.

競爭隊伍問他們的虛構戀情是否只是吸票把戲。蓮沒有戴上假面，也沒有假裝被傷害。她說明路線是清楚標示虛構的舞臺故事，社團也已更正先前易引起誤解的照片。那位學生又逼問：為何不讓角色在結局承諾結婚？蓮回答，公共空間的故事不需要靠戀愛承諾才重要。俊補充，兩人現實中的合作建立在工作與同意上。回答沒有立刻讓全場安靜，卻把問題拉回了展覽本身。

Another student asked about the difference between the two repair plans. Jun explained the weight of the old roof and the possible wound a falling tile could cause. He had brought no dangerous materials; only a small paper model was on the desk. Lian showed an essay by a former librarian asking the town to protect the reading room. The text had an official stamp but was not itself a law. “We seek a public answer, not a quick accusation,” she said. Several listeners took notes. They appeared more interested in the practical problem than in the rumor about the two leaders.

另一位學生問兩種維修方案差在哪裡。俊說明老屋頂的重量，以及落下瓦片可能造成的傷口。他沒有帶危險材料，桌上只有紙模型。蓮展示前圖書館員寫的一篇文章，要求城鎮保護閱覽室。文件蓋了正式印章，卻不是法律本身。「我們要尋求公開答覆，不是倉促指控，」她說。幾名聽眾開始記筆記，看起來對實際問題的興趣已超過兩位社長的緋聞。

After the debate, Lian found Jun near the football field. He was carrying the paper model by its base. “You were brave,” she said. “I was afraid,” he admitted. “I thought I would forget every number.” She told him she had felt the same. They walked along the track and discussed what they should change before the next meeting. Lian suggested more photographs of real steps and fewer charts. Jun proposed adding a short recorded explanation for people who disliked reading. Their ideas fit together. It was no longer surprising that the best parts of their work came from listening to one another.

辯論後，蓮在足球場旁找到俊。他正從底部托著紙模型。「你很勇敢，」她說。「我其實很怕，」他承認。「我以為會忘掉所有數字。」她說自己也一樣。他們沿跑道走，討論下次會議前要改什麼。蓮建議增加真實臺階照片、減少圖表，俊提議增加短語音說明，方便不喜歡讀字的訪客。點子彼此配合。兩人最好的成果來自互相傾聽，已不再讓人意外。

At the library, Mei offered them a new costume for the final scene. It had a wide belt that sat at the waist, and a plain scarf instead of bright flowers. Lian tried it on and turned in front of a mirror. “This looks like a person who can do the work,” she said. Mei smiled. “That was my plan.” Jun offered to carry the boxes downstairs, but Lian took half. “Equal partners,” she reminded him. He laughed and let her carry the lighter box without arguing. It was a small act, yet it showed how they had learned to share tasks rather than exchange dramatic favors.

圖書館裡，美替最後一幕拿來新服裝，腰間有寬皮帶，搭配素色圍巾而不是鮮豔花朵。蓮穿上在鏡前轉身。「看起來像真正能做事的人，」她說。美笑道：「這就是我的計畫。」俊提議把箱子搬下樓，蓮卻拿走一半。「平等搭檔，」她提醒。他笑了，沒有爭論便讓她搬較輕的箱子。這個小動作顯示他們已學會分擔，而非交換誇張的人情。

That evening the debate results arrived. Their team had not won the public speaking prize, but judges praised the care given to safety and clear records. The rivals had done better on stage lighting. Lian did not feel ashamed. She sent the judges' comments to every volunteer and asked them to choose one useful suggestion. Jun called and asked whether she wanted to take a walk without scripts or maps. She said yes, but only for thirty minutes because she needed to finish her work. He answered, “Thirty minutes sounds good.” For once a limit did not feel like a wall between them.

晚上辯論結果公布。他們沒有拿到口語表達獎，但評審稱讚安全考量與紀錄清楚。對手則在舞臺燈光表現更好。蓮沒有覺得丟臉，將評語傳給每位志工，請大家各選一條可用建議。俊打電話問她要不要出去散步，不帶劇本也不帶地圖。她答應，但因為還有工作，只能走三十分鐘。他說：「三十分鐘很好。」這次界線不再像隔在兩人之間的牆。

When the hall closed, the volunteers checked when their next report was due. Lian moved two tasks to the following day so the team could rest. Jun agreed that a clear deadline was useful only when the work stayed safe and the people doing it remained well.

演講廳關閉時，志工核對下次報告的到期時間。蓮把兩項工作延到隔天，讓團隊有休息空間。俊同意，明確期限只有在工作安全、做事的人保持健康時才有價值。

They walked beside the river in the cool air. Jun said he liked hearing Lian speak when she cared about something. She told him she liked his patience when he had to explain a hard design. The words hung between them. Neither turned them into a joke. Lian was aware that a real choice waited beyond the contest, but she did not want to make it while classmates watched. They returned to the dormitory gate before her time was up. Jun said good night, and she answered with a smile. Their stage story could wait for an ending. Their own story still had room to grow.

他們在微涼的空氣裡沿河散步。俊說喜歡聽蓮談自己在乎的事情，蓮說喜歡看他耐心解釋困難的設計。這些話停在兩人之間，沒有人用玩笑帶過。蓮知道比賽之後有真實的選擇等著她，但不想在同學注視下決定。他們在約定時間前回到宿舍門口。俊道晚安，她微笑回答。舞臺故事可以等待結局，自己的故事還有成長空間。
