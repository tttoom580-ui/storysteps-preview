The Underground Canteen

灰橋不低頭 · 第 6 / 25 章 · 4000–5000 · 英中對照 · 校訂中

Mira set the agenda before dawn: beware of anyone asking them to cling to a single theory. A federal inspector praised the story's genre as a mystery but ignored the actual deaths. Rain slid down the windows of the old municipal kitchens, turning the street outside into a wavering photograph. Mira arrived without a police escort. She carried the worn notebook in which she had written every promise made to the people of Graybridge, including the promises she had failed to keep. The building smelled of wet stone and strong tea. Nobody stood when she entered, and she was grateful for that. She had never wanted citizens to rise for her; she wanted them to have enough confidence to ask difficult questions. When the first witness spoke, she kept her pencil still until the person had finished. Listening was slower than issuing an order, but the city had already suffered enough orders issued too quickly.

米拉天亮前訂下議程：別固守單一假設。聯邦督察把事件當成懸疑故事，卻忽視真正的死者。 雨水沿著窗玻璃流下，街景像一張晃動的照片。米拉沒有帶警衛，只帶著記錄承諾的舊筆記本走進現場，其中也記著她未能履行的承諾。屋裡有濕石與濃茶氣味。沒人起立，她反而慶幸。她從不希望市民對她鞠躬，只盼他們敢問難題。第一位證人發言時，她等對方說完才動筆。傾聽比下令慢，但這座城早已承受太多倉促的命令。

The old cook had once umpired a school baseball inning. She used that metaphor while showing the municipal officers a tiny organism under a lens; the poison had begun to overtake the food tanks. Mira refused to sign the preliminary summary. The statements in it were technically tidy but emotionally dishonest: houses became units, injured people became cases, and missing wages became a minor adjustment. Cook Tessa showed her where a number had been rounded until an entire family disappeared from the total. She corrected the page in red ink. Then she asked a volunteer to read it aloud, because a sentence that seemed acceptable on paper could sound cruel when spoken before the people it described. The revised account took longer to prepare. For the first time, however, the workers recognized their own experience inside an official document. That recognition was not justice, but it was a beginning.

老廚師曾替校隊棒球賽主持一局比賽，她以此比喻向市政官員說明，微小生物已開始侵佔食物儲槽。 米拉拒絕簽初步摘要。它在技術上整齊，情感上卻不誠實：房子成了單位，傷者成了個案，欠薪成了微小調整。廚師泰莎指出某個數字四捨五入後，竟使一整家人從總數消失。米拉用紅筆訂正，又請志工朗讀，因為紙上看似可接受的句子，當著被描述的人說出口可能格外殘酷。修訂花了更久，但工人第一次在官方文件裡看見自己的經歷。這還不是正義，卻是開始。

The workers spoke of rebellion. A rigid plastic shield lay beside a broken rib and a skeleton recovered from an old storehouse; one woman began to sob. The pressure on Mira was not spectacular. Nobody offered her a crown or threatened her with a dramatic duel. Instead she received polite letters recommending rest, notices questioning her qualifications, and invitations to meetings where she would be the only person without legal counsel. She saved each letter. Cook Tessa asked whether keeping them made her angry. Mira answered that anger was useful only when it improved attention. She slept poorly, drank her tea cold, and returned to work the next morning. Her private discomfort could not become a reason to abandon the public problem, but neither would she glorify exhaustion as a measure of virtue. The nominee for the proposed citizens' board was an ordinary cook, not a wealthy official.

工人議論著反抗。堅硬塑膠護盾旁有一根斷肋骨，以及老倉庫發現的骸骨；一名女子忍不住哭泣。 施加在米拉身上的壓力並不戲劇化，沒有王冠或決鬥，只有勸她休息的禮貌信件、質疑資格的公告，以及只有她沒帶律師的會議邀請。她把信一封封留存。廚師泰莎問她是否生氣，她說怒氣只有提高注意力才有用。她睡得差、茶總喝到冷，隔天仍工作。私人不適不能成為拋棄公共責任的理由，但她也不把過勞歌頌成美德。 候選人是平凡廚師，而非富有官員。

An ancient theology text had been used to hide a list of toxic shipments. Iona poured vinegar on the sealed label and revealed the ink beneath it, without claiming the trick was scientific proof. At dusk the group reached a narrow landing where the lamps had begun to flicker. Mira noted the last names on her list, then handed a copy to someone who had disagreed with her all morning. She believed an honest record should be able to travel without its author's protection. The team had not yet solved poisoned food reserves, and she refused to call the day a victory. Yet one fact had changed: people who had once whispered behind closed doors were now speaking openly beside one another. Before leaving, she checked that the doors were unlocked and that the youngest volunteers had transport home. A new clue waited in the papers, but it could wait until everyone was safe.

一本古老神學書藏著有毒貨物清單。艾歐娜將醋滴在封籤上，使底層墨跡浮現，但她不把這種技巧誤認為科學證據。 傍晚，眾人走到燈光閃爍的平台。米拉記下最後幾個名字，把副本交給一位整個上午都反對她的人。她相信誠實紀錄不該只能靠作者保護。問題還未解決，她拒絕稱今天為勝利。但有件事已改變：曾在門後低語的人，如今敢站在一起公開說話。離開前，她確認門未上鎖、年輕志工有車回家。文件裡還有新線索，但眾人平安更重要。

Behind the kitchen wall, Tessa found a steel door whose hinges had been painted to resemble brick. Iona forced it open just enough for a draft of cold air to escape. On the floor beyond lay an identification tag and a uniform button. The tag did not belong to a kitchen employee. It belonged to a municipal surveyor missing since the first flood. Mira locked the passage again and called for witnesses before anyone entered.

食堂牆後，泰莎找到一扇被漆成磚色的鋼門。艾歐娜勉強推開一條縫，冷風立即竄出。門後地上有身分標牌與制服鈕扣，標牌主人不是廚工，而是初次洪災後失蹤的市政測量員。米拉先封住通道，召集證人後才准大家進入。
