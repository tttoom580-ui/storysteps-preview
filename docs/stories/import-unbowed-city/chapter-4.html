Mira knew to anticipate bureaucracy, but the coincidence of three matching records troubled her. Every commuter on the affected line had received the same denial of care. A gust pushed dust beneath the doorway of the provincial health office. For a moment Mira could taste the dry grit between her teeth. She watched the workers moving around the room, silently noticing who took up space and who stepped aside to make space for others. That distinction often told her more than a person's official title. She had been called stubborn, ambitious, and difficult; each word sounded different depending on whether it came from a frightened resident or a threatened official. She no longer spent her energy trying to control every description of herself. Her attention belonged to the situation in front of her, and she opened the meeting by asking who had not yet been heard. There was another task that no decree could complete: carrying information through a frightened neighborhood without turning it into rumor. Mira asked the volunteers to repeat the facts in their own words, and to admit whenever they did not know the answer. This slowed them down. It also prevented one confident mistake from multiplying into a dozen unnecessary emergencies. She watched an older woman correct a younger man's account without humiliating him. In that small exchange she saw the kind of public life the city had almost forgotten: people sharing knowledge without demanding obedience in return.

米拉早已預料官僚阻礙，但三份相同紀錄的巧合令她不安。受影響路線上的通勤者都遭拒絕醫療服務。 風把灰塵吹進門縫，米拉嘴裡彷彿嚐到砂粒。她觀察誰佔據空間、誰願意為旁人讓位；這往往比職稱更能說明人品。人們稱她頑固、野心勃勃、難以相處，話從害怕的居民或受威脅的官員口中說出，意義完全不同。她不再把力氣花在控制別人的形容詞上，而是將注意力交給現場，開口詢問還有誰尚未被聽見。 還有一項命令無法完成的工作：把消息傳遍害怕的社區，卻不使它變成謠言。米拉請志工用自己的話重述事實，不知道就坦承不知道。進度因此慢了，卻避免一個自信的錯誤擴大成十幾場多餘危機。一名老婦溫和地糾正年輕人的說法，沒有羞辱他。米拉在這小小交流裡，看見城市險些遺忘的公共生活：分享知識，不要求別人以服從交換。

The pipe's diameter was wrong by a distinctive margin. University faculty questioned the fiscal report: only a fraction of the spending had addressed the actual hazard. The first paper looked harmless. Its corners had been stamped, its numbers placed neatly into boxes, and its official language suggested that every question had already been answered. Mira had learned to distrust that feeling. She compared the paper with the marks residents had made on walls, packages and worn calendars. Those rough marks were less elegant than the forms, yet they preserved events that the city had chosen not to acknowledge. She invited Yara and the faculty doctors to challenge her interpretation. In her office, disagreement had once been treated as a personal attack. Here it became a method of protecting the people whose lives depended on their conclusion. Together they wrote down what they knew, what they guessed, and what still required proof.

管線直徑有明顯誤差。大學教師質疑財政報告：只有極小比例的經費真正用來排除危害。 第一張公文看似毫無問題：蓋章、編號、官樣措辭都很整齊，彷彿每個問題已有答案。米拉偏偏不信這種感覺。她拿紙張對照居民在牆壁、包裹與舊日曆留下的痕跡。這些痕跡不如表格美觀，卻保存了當局不肯承認的事情。她請雅拉與大學醫師挑戰自己的解讀。從前辦公室把異議視為人身攻擊，在此卻成了保護人命的方法。大家一同寫下已知、推測，以及尚待驗證之事。

A doctor tested hormone exposure among marine transport workers. Their mortality statistics formed a parallel pattern that no practitioner in the province could easily dismiss. A woman in the last row asked whether the team had come to replace one group of powerful people with another. The question landed harder than any accusation from Provincial Auditor Ren. Mira took it seriously. She explained how decisions would be documented, how residents could inspect them, and how she herself could be challenged if she misused authority. The woman remained doubtful but agreed to serve as an observer. Mira preferred that wary consent to effortless loyalty. She had seen too many institutions turn gratitude into obedience, and too many good intentions survive only until the day someone was frightened of losing a comfortable office.

醫師檢驗海運工人所受的荷爾蒙干擾，死亡率形成相同趨勢，省內任何專業醫療人員都難以忽略。 後排一名婦人問，團隊是否只是換另一群掌權者。這問題比省稽核官任的控訴更尖銳。米拉認真回答：如何記錄決策、居民如何查閱、若她濫權又如何被質疑。婦人仍存疑，卻願擔任觀察員。米拉寧可接受警惕的同意，也不要輕易的忠誠。她見過太多機構把感謝變成服從，好意也可能在害怕失去舒適官位時變質。

Yara made a plea for an independent inquiry, trying to reassure grieving families. Mira began to uncover how the city had counted patients as equipment instead of people. The last witness left by the side entrance and paused to look back at the building. Mira understood the expression: the suspicion that everything might return to the way it had been. She did not offer reassurance she could not guarantee. She promised only that copies would exist in more than one place and that the next meeting would welcome questions. It was a smaller promise than the magnificent one printed on official posters, but she could keep it. From the doorway, before returning to the figures, Mira noticed that the street had acquired a new sound: neighbors calling one another by name instead of waiting for instructions from a loudspeaker.

雅拉懇請展開獨立調查，並安撫罹難者家屬。米拉開始查出市府竟把病人當作設備統計。 最後一位證人從側門離開，回頭望著大樓。米拉讀懂他的表情：害怕一切回到老樣子。她不說無法保證的安慰話，只承諾證據將多處備份、下次會議仍可提問。這承諾比官方海報上的宏願小，卻是她做得到的。回頭整理數字前，她在門口發現街上多了一種新聲音：鄰居開始互相叫名字，而非等擴音器下令。

The most disturbing figure was not the death rate. It was a payment entered under the heading “family relocation” on a day when no buses had run. Mira copied the amount and noticed that it matched the floodgate maintenance budget exactly. One account had financed a lie while another account reported an empty emergency fund. She asked the doctors to preserve their original figures and prepared to bring both records before the council.

最駭人的數字不是死亡率，而是某日列在「家庭遷移」項下的支出；那天根本沒有公車行駛。米拉抄下金額，發現它與水閘維修預算一模一樣。一個帳戶資助了謊言，另一個帳戶卻說救急基金已空。她請醫師保留原始數據，準備把兩份紀錄帶進議會。
