Building the City Again

灰橋不低頭 · 第 23 / 25 章 · 4000–5000 · 英中對照 · 校訂中

Bea measured carbon in the smog and watched a chubby child carry his books. Every contractor had to conform to an ecological standard rather than dismiss public discourse as noise. Bea put the air readings beside the proposed construction timetable. Work could improve the water system while making the streets harder to breathe in. A contractor wanted the dust figures removed as a distraction. She asked him which days he intended children to use the road beside his site. The question moved the discussion from an abstract standard to a route people needed. Mira requested a revised timetable with the same work and a stated way to reduce exposure, rather than a promise that every inconvenience would disappear.

碧亞量測霧霾中的碳，望著圓潤孩子抱著書。承包商必須符合生態標準，不能把公開討論當作噪音。 碧亞把空氣讀數與施工表並排。修水網同時可能讓街道更難呼吸。包商要刪揚塵數字，說會分散注意，她問施工旁道路哪些日子要讓孩子走。問題把抽象標準變成真實路線。米拉要求同樣工程重排並說明減少暴露方法，不要保證一切不便消失。

Their equation was explicit: each system would inject only the required energy, and residents could modify the plan. Ada served pastry while an elderly patron studied the drawings. The patron asked where the energy estimate allowed for a pump used during an emergency. Dai showed a separate line and admitted that the first sketch had underestimated its duration. Ada brought another sheet rather than letting anyone write over the old figures. Residents could then see what the revision changed. Mira liked one feature of the slower meeting: an awkward question did not disappear into an office and return as an unexplained total. Its effect remained visible beside the pastry crumbs and the original drawing.

方程式說得明白：系統只能注入所需能源，居民可以修改方案。艾達端出糕點，老顧客細看圖紙。 老顧客問估算何處包含緊急水泵。戴指出另列的一行，承認初稿低估運行時間。艾達另拿紙，不讓人蓋寫舊數。居民能看見改了什麼。米拉喜歡慢會議的一點：難題不會消失進辦公室，再變成無解釋的總額；其影響就在糕點屑和原圖旁可見。

During rehearsal for a flood scenario, smog alarms startled the planners. Their sophisticated controls could not repair everything, and a crate of squash rolled across the floor. The rolling squash interrupted the rehearsal before the planners noticed that their evacuation path crossed a delivery route. Suri asked the delivery worker to move the empty crate along the line they had drawn. It blocked the turn exactly where a wheelchair would need room. No complex control could solve that physical obstruction. They moved the unloading point on the plan and ran the exercise again. The smog alarm remained a separate problem to address, not an excuse to abandon what the fallen vegetables had revealed.

洪災情境排演時，霧霾警報驚動規劃者。精密控制系統無法修好一切，一箱南瓜滾滿地板。 南瓜滾落打斷排演，規劃者才注意撤離路線跨過送貨線。蘇芮請工人沿圖推空箱，恰在輪椅需轉彎處卡住。精密控制解不了實體阻礙。他們改卸貨點，再演一次。霧霾警報仍須另處理，不是放棄蔬菜所揭問題的理由。

Mira asked citizens to submit designs despite the tangle of problems caused by unemployment. She let them whine about delays, because a city built with consent would endure better than one built in silence. A worker objected that the cheaper material would require repairs sooner. Another worried that rejecting it would leave her without wages this month. Mira asked the budget group to show both the immediate cost and the expected maintenance cost. She favored one proposal, but the neighborhood representatives preferred a different balance. Instead of extending the debate until they agreed with her, she asked them to state their reasons for the public vote. Rebuilding would mean accepting decisions she could inspect without necessarily controlling them.

米拉請市民提交設計，儘管失業造成一團糾結難題。她容許大家抱怨進度，因為經同意建成的城市比強迫沉默更堅固。 工人說便宜材料較快要修，另一人怕不用就失去本月工資。米拉請預算組同時列立即費用與預期維護費。她偏好一案，各區代表卻選另一種平衡。她不拖到大家同意自己，而請他們在公開投票前說明理由。重建意味她能檢查決定，卻未必控制決定。

The reconstruction vote took place in Ada's courtyard instead of a guarded mansion. Each neighborhood had its own representative, and the budget was projected on a sheet large enough for children to read. The winning plan preserved parks, used affordable materials and required a public test before any major pump was installed. Mira lost two arguments during the debate. She accepted the result, relieved that people were becoming skilled enough to disagree with her.

重建表決在艾達的校園舉行，而非戒備森嚴的豪宅。每區都有代表，預算投影在孩子也看得見的大布幕上。勝出的方案保留公園、採用可負擔材料，並要求大型水泵裝設前先公開測試。米拉在辯論中輸了兩項主張，但她接受結果，慶幸人們已有能力與她意見不同。
