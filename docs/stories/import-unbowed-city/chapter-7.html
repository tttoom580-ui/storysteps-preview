The acquisition record named a backyard nursery and a bonus paid to a contractor. Among a chunk of paper containing colonial maps, Mira found calcium stains on the paper and tried to comprehend their implication. Nell spread the damp sheets across the nursery's empty potting bench. Water had left pale crescents around the purchase signatures, but not around the older survey lines. Mira asked her to hold the pages toward the window. The different stains meant the papers had not always been stored together. Someone had assembled an apparently continuous history from documents kept in separate places. Nell numbered the sheets on loose slips so that their original order would remain visible even after they dried.

收購紀錄列出一座後院苗圃與付給包商的獎金。一疊殖民時代地圖上有鈣質污斑，米拉試著理解其中含意。 奈兒把濕紙攤在苗圃空著的工作檯上。收購簽名周圍有淺色水痕，舊測量線旁卻沒有。米拉請她對著窗光看；不同污痕表示文件原本並未存放一起。有人用來自不同地方的紙拼出一份看似連續的歷史。奈兒另用紙條編號，讓紙乾後仍能辨認原有順序。

Nell could disapprove without losing her temper. A rusty tool had been used to penetrate the vault, and an old republican newspaper called the project a risky experiment built on scrap metal. The newspaper had printed a drawing of the vault before the break-in. Its narrow lower hinge was visible in the picture, whereas the official inventory described a broad modern hinge. Nell wanted to write that the inventory was false. Mira suggested marking the disagreement first. A replacement could explain it; so could a false inventory. They needed a dated repair bill before choosing. Nell crossed out her accusation and wrote a question that the archive staff could actually answer.

奈兒能理性表達反對。有人用鏽工具鑿開金庫，舊共和派報紙形容那是建立在廢鐵上的冒險實驗。 報紙印著金庫遭闖入前的圖畫，圖中下方鉸鏈很窄，官方清單卻寫著寬大的新式鉸鏈。奈兒想直接寫清單造假，米拉建議先記下矛盾：可能換過鉸鏈，也可能清單不實。兩人需要有日期的修理收據才能判斷。奈兒劃掉指控，改寫成檔案人員能回答的問題。

The sophomore led them through a spacious reading room, her spine straight despite the threats. A stack of files detailed surveillance of teachers who opposed the deal. Nell stopped beside the reading room's catalogue and found her teacher's name filed under a building number. She had expected accusations; instead the file contained timetables, borrowed books, and descriptions of ordinary conversations. The smallness of those details frightened her. Mira asked whether she wanted someone else to read the remaining pages. Nell stayed, but asked that the names of pupils be covered before any copies went outside. Exposing surveillance did not require exposing its victims a second time.

這名大二學生領著眾人穿過寬敞閱覽室，受威脅仍挺直脊梁。一疊檔案記錄了反對交易的教師如何被監視。 奈兒在閱覽室目錄旁停下，發現老師姓名被歸在建築編號之下。她以為會看到指控，裡面卻是課表、借書紀錄與日常談話。細節如此瑣碎，反而令她害怕。米拉問是否要別人代讀其餘頁面。奈兒留下，但要求公開副本前遮住學生姓名；揭露監視，不該讓受害者再被暴露一次。

A symbolic key on an old transit map identified the water company headquarters. Mira understood that their next journey would have to begin underground. The transit key represented an entrance, not an instruction to force it. Iona traced the old service line with her finger and warned that several passages might have been sealed after the flood. Before planning a descent, they would need the company's access records. Nell searched the copied papers for the authority under which those records had been withheld. The answer was buried behind a nursery valuation. Reading a price for seedlings had led them to something no buyer should own: the power to decide whose name existed.

一張舊大眾運輸圖上的象徵性鑰匙指向自來水公司總部。米拉明白下一站必須深入地底。 交通圖上的鑰匙標示入口，不表示可以強行闖入。艾歐娜沿著舊維修線指點，提醒有些通道可能已在洪災後封閉。下去之前，她們需要公司的出入紀錄。奈兒在抄錄文件中尋找拒絕公開紀錄的法律依據，答案竟藏在苗圃估價之後。從幼苗價格追查下去，她們找到任何買家都不該擁有的權力：決定誰的姓名存在。

The original acquisition order appeared on the last page of Nell's copied files. It gave the board control not merely of land but of the city registry whenever an emergency was declared. The water crisis had therefore changed more than property lines: it had changed who counted as a resident. Nell looked at the signed clause and said quietly, “They didn't just move us. They made us disappear.” Mira asked her to lead the next hearing.

奈兒抄錄檔案最後一頁出現原始收購命令：一旦宣布緊急狀態，董事會不只控制土地，也控制全市戶籍。水患改變的不只是產權線，還有誰算市民。奈兒看著簽名條款低聲說：「他們不只搬走我們，還讓我們消失。」米拉請她主持下一場聽證。
