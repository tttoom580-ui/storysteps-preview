蓄水庫下的影子

Shadows Under the Reservoir

灰橋不低頭 · 第 13 / 25 章 · 4000–5000 · 英中對照 · 校訂中

An acute fever brought the administrator to the reservoir. He accused Yara of bias and insisted the illness was not contagious, though the defendant in another case had used the same excuse. Yara asked the administrator to describe the fever cases by onset time rather than political district. People supplied from the same branch had fallen ill on different streets. That did not settle the cause, and she said so before anyone could turn the pattern into a slogan. Mira requested a clean copy of the water distribution plan. The administrator wanted a statement denying danger. Yara gave him instructions for seeking care instead. A public reassurance unsupported by evidence could leave patients waiting until their symptoms became worse.

急性高燒讓行政官趕到水庫。他指責雅拉偏頗，堅稱疾病不具傳染性；另一案件被告也曾用同樣藉口。 雅拉請行政官按發病時間而非政治分區描述病例。同一支線供水、住不同街的人都病了。她先說明這仍不足以確定病因，免得模式被變成口號。米拉要求乾淨供水圖。行政官要一份否認危險的聲明，雅拉給的卻是就醫指示；無證據的保證可能讓病人等到更嚴重。

A documentary crew filmed each dimension of the new tower as workers tried to erect it. Mira watched Dai flip a heavy diagram and explain the pull of gravity. Dai weighted the diagram's corners before turning it over. On the back were small marks made when another drawing had been pressed against it. The film crew moved closer, but he asked them to wait until a scale lay beside the marks. A dramatic close view would show scratches; a measured view could preserve their relationship. Mira recognized the curve of the reservoir wall. The tower was new, yet its foundations touched a much older arrangement of tunnels that the public plan left blank.

紀錄片團隊拍下新塔的各項尺寸，工人正努力架設。米拉看著戴工程師翻開大圖，說明重力牽引。 戴先壓住四角再翻圖。背面留著另一張圖壓出的細痕。攝影隊靠近，他請他們等比例尺放好；戲劇化近拍只能看到刮痕，帶尺度的影像才能保存關係。米拉認出水庫牆曲線。塔雖新，基礎卻碰上舊得多的隧道配置，公開圖上那裡一片空白。

The proposed legislation would let the city oversee emergency gates, but a lobby wanted to overturn the premise. Their model showed a sphere trapped beneath the reservoir wall. The model's trapped sphere was a chamber, not a decorative support. Dai showed where pressure would build if its outlet remained shut. A lobby representative asked whether the model exaggerated the risk. Dai welcomed the question and invited him to indicate a different outlet on the original drawing. None was shown on the revised sheet. Mira asked for both versions to remain on the table. Debate about legal authority could continue, but the practical question was whether a hidden passage could carry water safely away.

新法案原擬讓市府監督緊急水閘，遊說團體卻想推翻前提。模型顯示蓄水牆下困著一個球形艙室。 模型中受困的球體是艙室，不是裝飾支柱。戴指出出口關閉後壓力堆積之處。遊說代表問模型是否誇大風險，戴歡迎質疑，請他在原圖指出另一出口。修改版沒有畫出任何出口。米拉要求兩版都留桌上。法律權限可以繼續辯論，但務實問題是隱藏通道能否安全排水。

Yara offered therapy to shaken workers as the council debated the toll. Mira called the diagram weird, not because it was impossible, but because someone had erased a whole neighborhood. One worker could point to the neighborhood on a childhood photograph but not on the diagram before him. Yara let him pause when his voice failed. Mira placed the photograph beside the plan and asked Dai to use surviving landmarks rather than memory alone. A stair, a retaining wall, and the reservoir curve began to align. They still lacked a safe route into the chamber. Searching beneath the revised plan for the missing connection, Dai lifted a loose corner and found the older emergency drawing. In the childhood photograph, an olive tree stood beside the stair; Dai treated it as a clue to the location, not as a precise survey point.

雅拉為受驚工人提供治療，議會則討論傷亡代價。米拉說圖面古怪，不是因為它不可能，而是有人抹去整個街區。 一名工人能在童年照片指出街區，卻無法在圖上找到。雅拉在他哽咽時讓他暫停。米拉把照片放在圖旁，請戴用現存地標而非只靠記憶比對。台階、擋土牆與水庫曲線逐漸吻合，但仍缺安全進艙路線。戴尋找遺漏連接時，掀起修改圖鬆動的一角，找到較早的緊急設計。 童年照片裡台階旁有棵橄欖樹；戴將它視為位置線索，不當成精確測量點。

Dai finally located the reservoir's original emergency drawing beneath a revised plan. A second valve had been designed to release pressure into an empty service tunnel. Someone had painted its handle the same color as the wall. Mira ordered the tunnel inspected and the neighboring residents warned before any test. The board demanded speed. She answered that the city had been injured by people who treated a warning as an obstacle to their schedules.

戴終於在修改後圖紙下面找到水庫最初的緊急設計。第二道閥門原可把壓力導入空的維修隧道，有人卻把把手漆成牆色。米拉要求測試前先檢查隧道、通知鄰居。董事會催促加快，她說城市正是被把警告視為行程障礙的人傷害。
