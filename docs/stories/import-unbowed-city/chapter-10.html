The Drought Institute

An allergy alert reached the warehouse as bulk grain arrived. Mira asked the institute to cite the study supporting the order; collaboration was impossible while executives could compel researchers to lie. The warehouse workers had been told both to unload the grain and to keep it away from families. No one could explain which instruction came first. Mira asked the institute to separate an actual food warning from a purchasing decision. Until the source was checked, workers could hold the disputed sacks apart without declaring the whole supply unsafe. Bea brought the labels to a clear table and began matching dates. A warning printed after a purchase might justify caution, but could not have caused that purchase.

大量穀物抵達倉庫時，過敏警示也傳來。米拉要求研究所引述禁令依據；若主管逼學者說謊，合作便無從談起。 倉庫工人同時奉命卸糧與禁止供糧，卻無人能說哪道指示先來。米拉要求研究所把食安警示與採購決定分開。來源未核實前，可隔開有爭議的袋子，不必宣布所有糧食不安全。碧亞在空桌上比對標籤日期。採購後才印的警示或能支持謹慎，卻不可能是當初採購的原因。

A counselor offered descriptive testimony about children's digestion during the drought. Mira could only exclaim at the evidence of meals cooked on a broken grill. The counselor had kept meal notes because parents asked why children were hungry again so soon. Her pages named portions and symptoms, not causes. Yara thanked her for preserving that distinction. A damaged grill could explain an interrupted meal; it could not by itself explain every illness. They copied the entries with permission and covered children's names. The institute had tables of numbers, but these notes showed what the numbers represented: a person waiting beside a stove for something safe to eat.

輔導員詳細描述旱災期間孩子的消化問題。米拉看見他們靠壞烤架煮飯的證據，忍不住驚呼。 輔導員記錄餐食，是因父母想知道孩子為何很快又餓了。她只記份量與症狀，沒有推斷病因，雅拉感謝她保留區別。壞烤架可以解釋餐食中斷，不能單獨解釋所有疾病。她們徵得同意後遮住姓名抄錄。研究所有數字表，筆記卻讓人看見數字所代表的、在爐旁等待安全食物的人。

The institute director tried to overwhelm them with preliminary figures. Bea proposed a radical but rational plan: publish the readings from every terminal with each variable explained. Bea drew three columns on the board: the reading, the instrument, and the time. The director kept returning to an average without identifying the stations included. She asked him to mark them on the institute map. Several were far from the farms named in the emergency order. That did not make the measurements false, but it changed what they could demonstrate. Mira watched Bea erase her own first calculation and begin again with the appropriate stations, even while the director smiled at her correction.

所長拿初步數字淹沒會議。碧亞提出激進但合理的計畫：公布各終端機讀數，逐一說明每個變數。 碧亞在板上畫讀數、儀器與時間三欄。所長反覆談平均值，卻不說包含哪些測站。她請他在地圖上標示，有些離緊急命令中的農地很遠。測量未必是假，能證明的事卻不同。即使所長因她改算而微笑，碧亞仍擦掉原式，用適當測站重新計算。

To the executives the affair was a thriller set in the wilderness. To Mira it was a test of whether the city would feed people before preserving a company's reputation. At the discarded terminal, Bea found the folders arranged by sponsor rather than season. She explained the search aloud so another researcher could repeat it. Mira stood back and left the keyboard to her. The useful discovery would not be that a clever visitor had found a hidden number; it would be that the institute's own records could be checked by people outside its management. When an old chart began to appear, Bea compared its date with the emergency grain contract before reading the rainfall totals.

高層把事件當成荒野驚悚片，米拉卻認為那是考驗城市究竟先養活人民，還是先維護企業聲譽。 廢棄終端機的資料夾依贊助者而非季節排列。碧亞朗聲說明搜尋步驟，讓其他研究者可以重做。米拉退後，把鍵盤留給她。發現的價值不在聰明訪客找到祕密數字，而在管理層之外的人也能核對研究所紀錄。舊圖出現時，碧亞先比對緊急糧食合約日期，再讀降雨總量。

Bea recovered an unpublished rainfall chart from a discarded terminal. It contradicted the official drought warning by more than half. Yet the council had used that warning to buy emergency grain at an inflated price. Mira asked Bea to speak at the public laboratory demonstration, even if the figures embarrassed her former teachers. “A good scientist doesn't owe certainty to a powerful sponsor,” Mira told her. “She owes people the conditions under which the result was found.”

碧亞從廢棄終端機救回未公開的降雨圖，與官方旱災警報差了超過一半。但議會正是以那個警報高價購買緊急糧食。米拉請她在公開實驗示範說明，哪怕讓昔日老師難堪。「好科學家不欠權勢者確信，」米拉說，「她欠人民的是研究結果如何得出的條件。」
