The Water Network Debut

Mira refused to attribute the malfunction to the workers. On their behalf, Dai showed a compact design, proven competence and a plan to coordinate the network's debut. Dai placed the school notebook beside the working diagram. The copied sequence was useful, but he would not ask a crew to trust an old classroom page without checking present equipment. Iona named each valve and another worker repeated its location. The exercise revealed places where a familiar nickname differed from the label on the wall. They wrote both names on the test sheet. During an alarm, a command could fail even when everyone understood the machinery if they did not understand one another.

米拉拒絕把故障怪罪工人。戴工程師代表大家展示緊湊設計、專業能力與首度啟用的協調計畫。 戴把學校筆記放在工作圖旁。抄出的順序有用，但未核對現有設備前，不能叫工班盲信舊教材。艾歐娜逐一說閥名，另一工人重述位置。他們發現俗名與牆上標示有別，便兩者都寫進測試表。警報時，即使人人懂機械，彼此用詞不通也會失敗。

The crew spoke of destiny, but the station was a practical domain where every donation had to be accounted for. Iona would execute the test only when all workers stood clear. The first test moved only a small amount of water while observers watched the adjoining gauges. Iona kept one hand away from the lever until the last worker answered. The delay made the visitors restless. Dai explained that the waiting was part of the design, because a test without a way to stop was simply another dangerous order. Mira noted the cost of the materials next to the donated parts. Shared labor deserved a clear account as much as money paid to a contractor.

團隊談著命運，但機房是務實的工作領域，每筆捐款都得交代清楚。艾歐娜只在全員退到安全區後才進行測試。 首次測試只放少量水，觀察員看相鄰壓力計。最後一人回報前，艾歐娜不碰拉桿。訪客等得焦躁，戴解釋等待是設計一部分：無法停止的測試只是另一道危險命令。米拉把材料費與捐贈零件並列；共同勞力同樣值得清楚記帳。

An immune child watched a strange insect migration from the window while a muscular opponent threatened to cut power. Mira insisted on checking the output before any public announcement. The threatened power cut forced a practical question that no speech could answer: which functions would survive without the master board? Dai asked the crew to identify them on the diagram before touching a switch. A worker pointed to a blank corner where an older feed ought to have been. Mira asked for a physical inspection rather than a guess. Outside the window, the child followed the insects with a finger, unaware that the adults were trying to find their own route through a changing system.

一名免疫力強的孩子從窗口看昆蟲遷徙，健壯的反對者卻威脅切電。米拉堅持先驗證輸出結果再向公眾公布。 切電威脅帶來演說回答不了的問題：主控制盤失效後哪些功能仍在？戴請工班先在圖上指出，再碰開關。工人指向應有舊供應線的空角落。米拉要求實地查看，不靠猜測。窗邊孩子用手指跟著昆蟲，不知道成人也在變動系統中找自己的路。

When the alarms sounded, crews had to scramble across a spectacular hall. A soaked sponge fell onto a cracked tile near a vendor's cart, revealing an old access hatch. The sponge had soaked up water seeping around a tile, making the outline of the hatch easier to see. Iona stopped the nearby work and checked whether the space below was safe to open. The vendor moved the cart away without being asked to abandon it. Dai compared the hatch's position with the missing corner on the drawing. They had not discovered a solution merely by finding a door, but they now had a place where the unexplained line might continue beyond the official plan.

警報響起時，工人衝過壯觀廳堂。濕海綿掉在攤販推車旁的裂磚上，露出老舊維修入口。 海綿吸起磚緣滲水，使入口輪廓更清楚。艾歐娜叫附近工人停下，先查下方能否安全開啟。攤販移開推車，不必被迫棄置。戴把入口位置對上圖中空角。找到門不等於找到答案，但未解線路可能在官方圖外繼續的地方，終於可查。

The old access hatch opened onto a second control room. Its walls held physical switches that could isolate each neighborhood from the central network without cutting its water supply. Dai demonstrated one switch, and the eastern clinic lights stayed on when the master board shut down. It was proof the city possessed another design. Mira ordered the procedure written in plain language and distributed before the company realized they had found it.

老維修口通往第二控制室，牆上有實體開關，能切斷中央網路對各社區的控制，卻不影響供水。戴試動一個開關，主控制盤關閉時，東診所燈仍亮著。城市確實擁有另一種設計。米拉要求用白話寫下程序，在公司察覺前送到各區。
