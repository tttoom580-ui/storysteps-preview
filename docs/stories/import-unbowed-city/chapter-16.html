The Canvas School

灰橋不低頭 · 第 16 / 25 章 · 4000–5000 · 英中對照 · 校訂中

A recovering alcoholic offered to allocate his spare wages to a school beneath a canvas banner. Ada promised confidential lessons without asking children about family status. Ada wrote the donation on the classroom board and left the donor's personal history out of the announcement. He wanted children to have books, not a public account of his recovery. The canvas lifted whenever wind crossed the courtyard, so the first lesson took place with stones holding the pages down. Mira watched a child copy an address carefully into a notebook. Reading an address was a small exercise in school; outside, it could become a way to challenge an official who claimed a street had never existed.

一位戒酒中的工人願捐出部分薪資，在帆布旗幟下辦學。艾達承諾保密教學，不追問孩子的家庭身分。 艾達把捐款記上教室黑板，沒有公布捐款者的私人經歷。他想給孩子書，不是公開自己的戒酒故事。風一過庭院，帆布就掀動，第一堂課用石頭壓住書頁。米拉看孩子仔細抄地址。在學校是小練習，在外面卻可能成為質疑官員「街道不存在」說法的方法。

The conservation course ended in a debate about conviction and curriculum. A private enterprise offered help, but its representative wanted to manipulate the children's test results. The representative proposed removing difficult answers before the results were reported. Ada asked whether the pupils would be allowed to learn from those answers first. He spoke of protecting the school's reputation. She replied that a class could not be taught through a record of errors that had been erased. Mira suggested keeping a public account of donations separate from individual test papers. The company could inspect how its money was used without being given control of what the children were said to know.

保育課最後演變成信念與課程的辯論。私人企業表示願幫忙，代表卻想操弄孩子的測驗成績。 企業代表提議報告成績前刪去難看的答案。艾達問學生能否先從錯誤學習，他卻談保護學校聲譽。她回答，若抹去錯誤，課也教不成。米拉建議捐款公開帳與個別試卷分開：公司可查錢如何使用，卻不能控制孩子被描述成懂了哪些事。

A hockey player donated sports gear, creating a dilemma for the honorable head teacher. She accepted the equipment but refused a mansion offered as a bribe. Ada asked the donor to put the equipment and the house offer on separate pages. The first carried no conditions; the second required a place on the school's governing committee. Once the offers were separated, the choice became easier to explain to parents. She could welcome sticks and balls without selling authority over lessons. A pupil carried the gear into the courtyard while Ada returned the second page unsigned. The school needed shelter, but not a shelter whose owner could decide which children deserved to remain.

曲棍球選手捐出器材，讓正直校長面臨兩難。她收下體育用品，卻拒絕以豪宅形式送來的賄賂。 艾達請捐贈者把器材和房屋分列兩頁。前者沒有條件，後者要求學校管理席位。拆開之後，她更容易向家長解釋選擇：可以歡迎球具，不必出售教學權。學生把用品搬進庭院，艾達退回未簽房屋提案。學校需要遮蔽，但不能由屋主決定哪些孩子值得留下。

Mira examined a miniature pillar and could perceive a sequence of carved symbols. The sudden bell did startle her; no community should have to worship wealth to earn the right to read. Ada had used the little pillar to teach copying shapes before anyone suspected the marks were instructions. Mira traced them onto paper without rubbing the fragile surface. A student asked why one symbol appeared twice. Rather than answer, Ada set the teaching notebook beside the tracing and invited the class to compare the order. The room grew quiet with concentration. Children who had been asked to repeat facts supplied by adults were now helping adults recognize a pattern in an older engineer's work.

米拉查看微型石柱，看出刻符排列順序。突來鐘聲嚇了她一跳；任何社區都不應為獲得讀書權而崇拜財富。 在沒人知道刻符是指令之前，艾達用小石柱教摹寫形狀。米拉另紙描下，避免摩擦脆弱表面。學生問為何一符號重複，艾達沒有直接答，拿教材筆記與描圖並排，邀全班比順序。房間因專注而安靜。原本總被要求重述成人事實的孩子，正幫成人辨認舊工程師留下的模式。

At the end of a night class, a child identified the carved symbol on the miniature pillar as the same mark on the chapel's floodgate ledger. Ada opened a teaching notebook left by the city's first engineer. Its final page contained a hand-drawn sequence for resetting the water network without central permission. Mira asked every student to copy it. Knowledge held by a single office had almost destroyed Graybridge; shared knowledge might yet keep the city alive.

夜課結束時，一名孩子辨出微型石柱刻符與聖堂水閘帳冊上的印記相同。艾達打開建城首位工程師留下的教材，最後一頁手繪了不經中央批准即可重設水網的步驟。米拉請每名學生抄一份。單一辦公室壟斷的知識幾乎毀掉灰橋，共享知識或許能救它。
