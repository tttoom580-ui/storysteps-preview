The Glass Evidence

At the arena, an order tried to ban public evidence from a celebrity's trial. Ora held a crystal against the light and described the fraud, reminding Mira that intent mattered. Ora turned the crystal slowly above a dark cloth, making its scratched edge visible to the court. The celebrity's lawyer called it an ornament. She answered by placing its impression beside the mark on a disputed record. A decoration could still have been used as a tool. Mira asked the recorder to distinguish what Ora demonstrated from what she inferred. The matching shape connected the objects; the sequence of dates would be needed to explain how that connection had been used.

在競技場，命令企圖禁止明星案件公開證據。歐拉迎光舉起水晶封印，解釋詐欺，提醒米拉動機同樣重要。 歐拉在深色布上慢轉水晶，讓法庭看見邊緣刮痕。明星的律師說它只是裝飾品，她把印痕放在爭議紀錄的標記旁。裝飾也可能被當作工具。米拉請書記區分示範與推論：形狀相符連結了物件，但要靠日期順序才能解釋如何使用。

The city's lawsuit included nutrition records copied in the plural from imaginary patients. One doctor had written a prescription while another stored envelopes in a broken rack. The nutrition sheets described identical meals for patients with incompatible addresses. Yara read a sample without identifying living patients. Some entries used a closed clinic's name, while others referred to a room that had never held beds. Mira made separate piles for demonstrably impossible entries and merely doubtful ones. An honest error still deserved correction, but accusing every doctor of invention would hide the pattern they were trying to establish. The envelopes in the rack might explain which office had supplied the false addresses. The repeated refusals could depress anyone trying to correct a record. Yara kept a separate portfolio of verified clinic documents so that a family's next application would not begin with an empty file.

市府訴訟附著虛構病人的多份營養紀錄。一名醫師開處方，另一人把信封藏進破架子。 營養表為地址互不相容的病人記錄相同餐食。雅拉讀出樣本，避開在世病人姓名。有些引用停業診所，有些寫著從未放病床的房間。米拉分開確定不可能與僅有疑點的紀錄。誠實錯誤也要更正，但把所有醫師都指為造假，會掩蓋真正模式。架上的信封或能解釋哪個辦公室提供了假地址。 一再遭拒會使任何想更正紀錄的人沮喪。雅拉另備一份已核實診所文件的資料集，讓家庭下次申請不必再從空白檔案開始。

By random inspection, Ora found a residential code used on industrial invoices. Mira tried to reverse the filing order in a private session before the lawyer could poke holes in the chain of custody. Ora wrote the code on a spare card and asked an archive clerk to find it without telling him her expectation. He returned with a housing index. She then showed him the industrial invoice. The clerk checked again, this time using the original ledger instead of the copied catalogue. His second answer was the same. Mira asked him to describe the search in his own words. A code might have been reused innocently, but the mismatch now had an independent witness and a reproducible route through the records.

歐拉隨機檢查，發現工業發票竟用住宅代碼。米拉趕在律師挑漏洞前，於閉門會議試著反轉歸檔順序。 歐拉把代碼另寫在卡上，請檔案員查找，未先告訴他預期答案。他拿回住宅索引後，她才出示工業發票。職員改用原帳簿而非抄本目錄再查，結果相同。米拉請他自行描述過程。代碼或許被無意重用，但矛盾已有獨立證人與可重做的查詢途徑。

A guard tried to shove them from the archive, but Mira refused to retreat. The ultimate evidence was not a jewel's value: it was the list of names stamped beneath the glass. The guard's hand reached across the catalogue before the court clerk stepped between them. Mira kept her hands visible and requested the order authorizing removal. Ora gathered no loose pages into her bag; doing so could turn a challenge to secrecy into an argument about theft. They waited for the clerk to inventory the glass and its supporting frame. Beneath the clear surface, letters could be seen where a bright reflection had concealed them. Opening it would require permission and an uninterrupted record of what lay inside.

警衛想把她們推出檔案室，米拉沒有後退。最終證據不是寶石價值，而是封印底下那些人名。 警衛伸手越過目錄，法庭書記擋在中間。米拉讓雙手保持可見，要求出示驅離命令。歐拉沒有把散頁收進包裡，否則反保密的抗議會變成竊盜爭論。她們等書記清點玻璃及支架。原被反光遮住的字母在透明表面下顯現；開啟之前，需要授權與連續紀錄。

Ora broke no seal herself. Before independent witnesses she photographed the glass, measured the scratch beside a maker's mark, and asked the court to authorize an opening. The hidden page listed names already removed from the city registry. Mira did not celebrate finding proof: it meant the erasure had been deliberate. She thought of the people who had spent years applying for services to which they were entitled, only to be told their addresses had never existed.

歐拉沒有自行破封。她在獨立證人面前拍下玻璃、量測製作者記號旁的刮痕，再請法院授權開啟。隱藏頁上列著早被戶籍抹掉的人名。米拉沒有為找到證據慶祝，因為那意味抹除是蓄意的。她想到多年申請原有權利卻被告知住址不存在的人們。
