Every complication exposed another constraint as a deadly surge approached. Voss offered an elaborate evacuation speech, but steam began to erupt from the lower chamber. Steam obscured the lower stair before the announcement on the television had finished. Mira sent a runner to check the upper exit instead of letting the speech determine the evacuation route. The runner returned with a report of rising water, and Suri marked it beside the latest street messages. The reports were imperfect, arriving at different times, but they named actual places. Mira asked for the oldest entries to be checked again so a route that had been safe half an hour earlier was not offered as safe now.

致命洪流逼近，每項併發問題都暴露新的限制。沃斯發表精巧的撤離演說，下層機房卻噴出蒸汽。 電視演說還沒完，蒸汽已遮住下樓梯。米拉派人查上方出口，不讓演說決定撤離路線。跑腿者回報漲水，蘇芮把它記在最新街報旁。消息時間不一、並不完美，卻指向真實地點。米拉要求重查最舊條目，免得把半小時前安全的路線當成現在仍安全。

A metal fragment struck the graphic display, ruining the official interpretation. Mira demanded intervention based on actual street reports rather than mainstream television claims. With the display dark, the console looked suddenly less authoritative. Dai used the paper network plan and asked each district to report its local gauge. Mira repeated the readings back before they were entered. A misplaced number could direct water toward the wrong street. Iona kept the local sequence visible beneath a lamp while another worker watched the lower door. They had practiced the words for the switches in daylight. Now, in the noise, the shared names made it possible to understand short instructions without guessing.

金屬碎片擊碎圖形顯示器，官方解釋再也站不住腳。米拉要求根據街道實況介入，而不是信奉主流電視說詞。 螢幕暗下，控制台忽然不再那麼威嚴。戴改用紙圖，請各區回報壓力計。米拉先複述再登記，錯置數字可能把水導往錯街。艾歐娜把區域順序放燈下，另一工人守下層門。白天練過的開關用語，此刻讓人能在噪音中理解短令，不必猜。

An engineer qualified to measure radiation calculated the ratio of safe pumps to failing pumps. Voss worried about lost revenue; a senator begged Mira not to tempt an armed response. The engineer marked an unusable pump with a broad cross rather than leaving a doubtful value in the total. Voss objected that stopping machinery would destroy the accounts. Mira asked which account could pay for a district flooded by a pump known to fail. The senator lowered his voice, but the water did not pause for the argument. Dai requested authority for local crews to halt independently. Mira understood the cost of dividing control and the greater danger of making every crew wait for her permission. He had needed specialist training to qualify for the radiation measurements, and he stated that limit before advising on the pumps.

合格工程師測量輻射，計算安全與故障水泵比例。沃斯只在乎收入，參議員則求米拉不要誘發武裝對抗。 工程師把不能用的水泵打大叉，不在總數中留下可疑值。沃斯說停機會毀帳，米拉問哪份帳能賠償明知故障還用泵而淹掉的街區。參議員壓低聲音，水卻不等爭論。戴要求區域工班有獨立停工權。米拉懂分散控制的代價，也懂讓人人等她許可更危險。 他曾受專業訓練，才具備測量輻射的資格；在提供水泵建議前，他先說明自己的專業界限。

The disaster began to unfold on an unprecedented scale. A vacuum seal broke, and even the youngest warrior in Mira's rescue team understood that saving strangers came before arresting enemies. A rescue volunteer saw someone trapped near the service doorway wearing a board employee's coat. Another volunteer recognized the badge and hesitated. Mira pointed toward the clear landing, not toward an arrest position. They brought the person out before deciding what questions should follow. Iona called from the switches that the channel was nearly ready. The separation of duties could no longer remain a suggestion on a sheet. Each coordinator needed enough authority to act while Mira stayed where the district calls could reach her.

災難以前所未有的規模展開，真空密封破裂。米拉救援隊最年輕的戰士也明白，救陌生人比逮捕敵人更急迫。 救援志工看見維修門邊有人受困，穿董事會員工外套。另一人認出徽章，猶豫了。米拉指向安全平台，而非逮捕位置。人先救出，問題之後再問。艾歐娜從開關處喊通道將就緒。紙上分工不能再只是建議；米拉守在能接各區回報的地方，各協調員必須有足夠權限自行行動。

When the central board failed, Mira did not order everyone into the dangerous tunnel. She put Yara in charge of shelters, Suri over routes, and Dai over the valves; each had authority to stop a procedure that endangered her team. At midnight Iona pulled the final local switch. Water roared through the empty service channel instead of the crowded East Ward. Mira heard people cheering above her, but she stayed at the console until every district answered the safety call.

中央控制盤失效時，米拉沒命所有人擠進危險隧道。她讓雅拉管收容、蘇芮管路線、戴管閥門，每人都有權停止危害團隊的程序。午夜，艾歐娜拉下最後一道區域開關。洪流奔入無人的維修道，而非擁擠東區。上方有人歡呼，米拉仍守在控制台直到每區回報平安。
