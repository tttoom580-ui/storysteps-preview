失去姓名的街區

The Ward Without Names

灰橋不低頭 · 第 21 / 25 章 · 4000–5000 · 英中對照 · 校訂中

Mira showed affection without promises she could not keep. She built an alliance and asked the court to authorize access to the barren site of an old burial ground. The night surge had left the burial ground wet but reachable. Mira did not let the urgency of discovery replace the court's permission. Families waited outside the marked area while the request was read aloud. Some wanted every surface opened immediately; others feared losing the last traces that might identify someone. Yara arranged a place where either group could step away from the work. Recovering a record was not the same as being ready to confront what that record might say about a missing relative.

米拉以真誠關懷代替空話。她組成聯盟，請法院授權調查荒蕪的舊墓地。 午夜洪流後，墓地仍濕，已可到達。米拉不讓發現急迫性取代法院授權。家屬在標示區外聽人朗讀申請，有人想立刻挖開一切，有人怕最後身分線索遭毀。雅拉安排可暫離工作的地方。找回紀錄，不等於已準備好面對它對失蹤親人可能說出的事。

The neighborhood once held a carnival, but rationed consumption had emptied its stalls. A wrongly convicted carpenter offered to donate tools in memory of his eternal friend. The carpenter offered tools, then asked to keep the smallest one beside him until he understood the work. Mira agreed. His earlier conviction had taught him how quickly an object could be turned into an accusation. Iona explained which tools would touch the lock and which should stay away from fragile paper. Ruth listed every borrowed item with its owner. People were contributing what they still possessed to recover a neighborhood that official records insisted had never possessed them at all. The decision to convict him had not made the missing neighborhood any less real.

社區過去舉辦嘉年華，物資配給卻使攤位空蕩。一名遭冤判的木匠願捐工具紀念已逝摯友。 木匠獻工具後，要求先留最小一件，等懂用途再交。米拉同意，他過去遭判刑的經驗使他知道物件多快能變成指控。艾歐娜解釋哪些可碰鎖、哪些須遠離脆紙。露絲記下每件借物與物主。人們拿剩下的財物，來找回官方紀錄聲稱從不曾擁有他們的街區。 對他作出的有罪判決，並沒有讓失落街區變得比較不真實。

A cracked jug beside a jar of mustard concealed part of the lock mechanism. Iona studied how to make it operational so that the original street signs could rise from the silt. Iona brushed the mechanism without turning it until Dai checked the connected channel. The jars and broken household objects had made the place look like abandoned storage. Beneath them lay fittings shaped for deliberate operation. A resident recognized the sound when the first part moved. It reminded her of the lock opening on market mornings. Mira asked that the memory be recorded separately from the mechanism's technical description. Both mattered, but one could not substitute for a safe inspection of the other. After the inspection, she operated the lock and watched the signs rise clear of the mud.

破水壺與芥末罐旁藏著水鎖機關的一部分。艾歐娜研究如何恢復機關功能，讓原本的街牌能從泥中升起。 戴查連接水道前，艾歐娜只清刷不轉機關。罐子和碎家用品使那裡像廢棄倉庫，底下卻是為操作而做的零件。第一部件動時，一位居民認出聲音，想起市場早晨開鎖。米拉請另記回憶，不與技術描述混寫。兩者重要，但記憶不能代替機械安全檢查。 檢查完成後，她操作水鎖，看著街牌從淤泥中升起。

Voss tried to proclaim victory through propaganda and a security squad. Mira answered with records of stability and interrupted transmission, refusing to accept his vague account. Voss's broadcast described the recovered area as a successful company clearance. Nell placed the interrupted messages beside the times the local crews had acted. His account omitted those crews entirely. Mira asked the temporary counters to display the sequence without adding praise for her. Residents arriving for corrections needed to know which documents had survived and where to appeal if a claim was refused. The company's version could be challenged with a public timeline; the people's names needed a process that would last beyond that argument.

沃斯派保全小隊，用宣傳宣告勝利。米拉提出穩定性與中斷傳輸紀錄，不接受他的模糊說法。 沃斯廣播把復原地區說成公司成功清理成果。奈兒把中斷訊息與區域工班行動時間並排，他的版本全漏了工班。米拉請臨時櫃台展示順序，不加對自己的讚美。前來更正的人需要知道何種文件尚存、被拒可去哪申訴。公開時間線能反駁公司，姓名卻需要比爭論更長久的程序。

The recovered street signs gave residents a place to begin reclaiming their names. A woman who had been listed as dead found the title to her bakery; a boy recognized the address written by his grandmother. Mira set up temporary counters staffed by volunteers and lawyers who could correct the record without charging fees. Then she stood aside and let the people speak for themselves. No leader could return all the lost years, but the city could stop pretending those years had never belonged to anyone.

找回的街牌讓居民開始追回姓名。被登記死亡的婦人找到麵包店地契，男孩認出祖母親筆地址。米拉設置臨時服務台，由志工和律師免費更正紀錄。接著她退到一旁，讓當事人自行發言。領袖不能歸還所有失去的歲月，但城市至少能停止假裝那些歲月不曾屬於任何人。
