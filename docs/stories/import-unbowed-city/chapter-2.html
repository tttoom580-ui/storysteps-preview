北坡禁區

The Quarantine Ridge

The council called the restriction beneficial, but Mira refused to convert a corporate warning into law. Her destination was the infirmary, where a rumored epidemic had begun with a simple errand. By the time Mira reached the North Ridge checkpoint, the crowd had divided itself into those who wanted an immediate answer and those who were tired of hearing answers at all. She did not pretend that an investigator possessed magic. Evidence might reveal who signed a document, but it could not bring back a lost house or a lost hour. A child offered her a paper bird, folded from a rejected notice. Mira accepted it and placed it beside her notebook. The gesture reminded her that every technical question had a human cost. When her companions asked whether the meeting should be postponed, she shook her head. The people who had waited the longest should not be required to wait again simply because their story was inconvenient.

議會聲稱禁令有益，米拉卻拒絕把企業警告當成法律。她前往診所，據說那場流行病最初只是一趟普通差事。 米拉抵達時，群眾分成急著要答案的人與早已不想再聽答覆的人。她沒有裝作調查員會魔法：證據能查出簽名的人，卻無法歸還失去的家園與時間。一名孩子用遭退回的公告摺紙鳥送她，她將它放在筆記本旁。這提醒她每個技術問題都有人的代價。同伴問會議是否延期，她搖頭。等待最久的人，不該只因故事讓官員難堪就被要求再等。

An extraordinary team had to haul medicine uphill. The company denied liability when two workers took a plunge from the icy ridge; a reminder of their names remained on a stone. The evidence arrived wrapped in ordinary brown cloth. It had passed through too many hands for anyone to be confident about its origin. Mira made the volunteers reconstruct its journey from memory, stopping whenever two accounts disagreed. Dr. Yara disliked the delay, but Mira reminded the group how an earlier investigation had collapsed because an impatient witness skipped a single hour. She knew the city wanted heroes who could announce a solution before supper. She preferred the unglamorous work of keeping a chain of custody intact. If the next hearing turned against them, their notes would have to survive scrutiny without the comfort of her presence in the room.

救援隊費力將藥物拖上坡。兩名工人從冰冷山脊墜落，公司卻否認責任；石上仍刻著他們的姓名。 證物包在普通棕布裡，經手人過多，來源已不明。米拉請志工從記憶重建它的旅程，兩種說法衝突就停下來核對。同伴不喜歡耽擱，但她提醒大家，過去有案子只因證人跳過一個鐘頭便全盤崩潰。城裡想要晚餐前就宣布解答的英雄，她卻寧可做枯燥的證物保全。下次聽證若不利，筆記也必須能獨立接受檢驗。

One respondent described a broken sensor beside the watch house. The nurse heated stew over coals, and Mira rejected the temptation to accept a convenient official explanation. That afternoon corporate counsel Pell demanded a private meeting. Mira arrived with two witnesses and announced that she would publish a record of the conversation. The demand was greeted as an insult, which told her something about the expectation of secrecy. She did not argue about anyone's character. She asked the practical questions instead: who had power to stop the dangerous procedure, who would pay for the harm already caused, and what would happen if the next warning came at night? The answers were evasive. Outside, someone had begun painting the damaged steps so visitors would not notice them. Mira thought of that paint as the most accurate portrait of the administration she had yet seen.

一名受訪者說瞭望站旁的感測器遭破壞。護士用炭火熱燉湯，米拉拒絕接受方便卻草率的官方說詞。 午後，對方要求密談。米拉帶兩名證人前往，聲明會公開會談紀錄。對方把這當成侮辱，足見他多麼依賴保密。她不評論人格，只問務實問題：誰有權停止危險程序、誰賠償既有傷害、半夜再響警報怎麼辦？回答全是閃躲。外頭有人正在粉刷破損台階，好讓訪客看不見傷痕。米拉覺得那層新漆，就是她見過最準確的政府肖像。

Using a torch, Iona found that someone had tried to trim the sensor wires. The discovery marked a transformation in their investigation, and Yara grew vocal about protecting patients. There was no single moment when fear disappeared. It loosened gradually, as workers tested the same switch twice and saw the same result, as parents recognized their children's names on a list, as a damaged door finally opened from the inside. Mira watched those small changes and resisted the urge to turn them into a grand tale about herself. Dr. Yara suggested placing her portrait beside the repaired machinery. She declined. The wall, she said, would be better used for instructions anyone could understand, including a visitor who had arrived that very morning. The next difficult question was already approaching, and a portrait could not answer it.

艾歐娜舉著手電筒，發現有人剪過感測器電線。這項發現改變了調查方向，雅拉也開始公開為病人發聲。 恐懼沒有一下子消失。工人兩次測試開關得到同樣結果，父母在名冊看見孩子名字，受損門終於能從裡面開啟，恐懼才逐漸鬆動。米拉看著小改變，不讓它變成歌頌自己的傳奇。同伴提議把她的肖像掛在修好的機器旁，她拒絕了。牆面應留給人人看得懂的說明，包括今早才來的訪客。下一道難題已在路上，肖像無法作答。

The damaged sensor held a strip of thin paper beneath its back plate. In careful handwriting someone had recorded temperatures, dates, and the word NORTH. It was a route, not a warning. Yara wanted to carry it to the police immediately, but Mira insisted on making a second copy. She had already seen too many unique originals vanish inside official drawers. That night they followed the route toward South Creek, where the first witness was waiting beside a closed pier.

感測器背板下藏著一條紙，上面仔細記著溫度、日期與「北方」二字。那是一條路線，不是警告。雅拉想立刻交給警方，米拉卻堅持先影印。她已看過太多孤本消失在官署抽屜。當晚，她們沿路前往南溪，第一位證人正在封閉的碼頭旁等待。
