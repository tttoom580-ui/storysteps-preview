The Final Testimony

灰橋不低頭 · 第 24 / 25 章 · 4000–5000 · 英中對照 · 校訂中

用互動版查詞、朗讀與收藏

The parties met in accord that no family should suffer another year of agony. Behind windows that rain had begun to blur, Mira began to browse the evidence while a single piano chord echoed in the hall. The piano belonged to a waiting room, where someone had struck a key while moving a bench. Its unfinished sound followed Mira into the evidence room. She opened her notebook to the first chapel entry, then checked the dates against the court index. The work now occupied boxes instead of a single damp ledger. She still needed to distinguish the things she had seen herself from the things others had established. On the witness stand, her reputation could not replace a clear answer about how each fact reached her.

各方至少同意，不該再讓家庭承受一年的痛苦。雨水使窗面漸趨模糊，米拉在窗後開始瀏覽證據，廳中響起單一鋼琴和弦。 鋼琴在等候室，有人搬椅碰了琴鍵，未完的聲音跟米拉進證物室。她翻回聖堂初筆，拿日期核對法庭索引。工作已由一冊濕帳擴成箱箱文件，她仍須分清親眼所見與他人證實之事。證人席上，名聲不能取代每項事實如何到她手中的清楚回答。

Her argument was coherent: the confrontation concerned deliberate orders, not bad weather. The judges would descend into the archive to examine the doctrine used to justify execution of the plan. A judge asked why a floodgate order could not be treated as an unfortunate response to bad weather. Mira laid out the approval, the concealed maintenance account, and the later map alteration in order. Weather explained the water arriving; it did not explain those decisions being hidden. She stopped there rather than claiming the documents revealed every thought in Voss's mind. In the archive, the judges would be able to inspect the same sequence, including the gaps the investigation had not resolved.

她論點清晰：對質涉及蓄意命令，而非壞天氣。法官下到檔案庫，檢查用來正當化執行計畫的教條。 法官問為何不能把水閘命令視為天氣惡劣下的不幸應變。米拉按序列核准、隱匿維修帳、後續改圖。天氣解釋水為何來，不能解釋決定為何被藏。她就此停下，不說文件能揭沃斯每個念頭。法官在檔案庫可查同樣順序，包括調查仍未解的缺口。

An extensive index listed every nasty scheme. Outside, a riot threatened to begin when a guard tried to slap a protester; Mira ordered her supporters to soften their voices. The commotion outside reached the hall as a confused roar. Ruth went to the entrance with an observer, and Mira asked her supporters to leave room for people who wanted to depart. Someone demanded that the guard be seized immediately. She requested that the attempted assault be witnessed and reported, while the hearing continued inside. A confrontation at the door could swallow the testimony everyone had waited years to hear. The index remained open on the table, its numbered references quieter and more durable than the shouts.

詳盡索引列著每個惡劣陰謀。外頭警衛想掌摑抗議者，暴動一觸即發；米拉要支持者放低聲量。 外面喧鬧傳入成混亂吼聲。露絲與觀察員到入口，米拉請支持者留空間給要離開的人。有人要立刻抓警衛，她要求見證並通報企圖攻擊，同時繼續聽證。門口衝突可能吞掉大家等多年的證詞。桌上索引仍攤開，編號參照比叫喊安靜，也更持久。

Lawyers could speculate about motive, but a spouse had signed as witness to the forged approval. Mira asked only for a worthy ruling that would protect people who had never held power. The spouse was asked to identify the signature without being made responsible for every act of the person married to them. Mira welcomed that distinction. She had followed family connections when they led to records, but had refused to treat kinship as a substitute for evidence. Now the court had an actual witness to the approval process. Before judgment, she reviewed the requested remedies with the families' lawyers. Removal from office would matter, but so would the practical means to recover money and challenge future emergency orders.

律師可以揣測動機，但有人的配偶曾簽名見證偽造核准。米拉只要求一項值得信任、能保障無權者的判決。 法庭請配偶辨認簽名，沒有要其為婚姻對象所有行為負責。米拉認同這區別。她曾循親屬關係找紀錄，卻不以血親代替證據。現在核准程序有真正證人。判決前，她和家屬律師重看救濟要求。免職重要，追回款項與質疑未來緊急命令的實際方法也重要。

The court ruled that Voss had knowingly concealed the emergency gate and diverted public money. He was removed from office and ordered to face further proceedings over the missing workers. The judgment did not erase every injury, and several families left the hall in silence. Mira refused the reporters' request for a triumphant pose. She read the ruling once more, checking the provisions for restitution and independent oversight. Then she returned to the city square, where a public charter still needed its final signatures.

法院認定沃斯明知祕密水閘存在，仍刻意隱瞞並挪用公款。他被免職，還須為失蹤工人接受後續審理。判決無法抹平所有傷害，有些家庭沉默離開。米拉拒絕記者要她做勝利姿勢的要求，而是重新讀判決，確認賠償與獨立監督條款。之後她返回廣場，公共憲章仍等著最後簽署。
