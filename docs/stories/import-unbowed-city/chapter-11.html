賭場的繼承文件

The Casino Inheritance

灰橋不低頭 · 第 11 / 25 章 · 4000–5000 · 英中對照 · 校訂中

用互動版查詞、朗讀與收藏

A casino seemed an apt hiding place for stolen public funds. Its murals characterize the past as glamorous, but clinical reports showed why a wheelchair refuge had been quietly shut down. The refuge appeared in the casino's old plans as a broad room opening onto the street. In the current drawing it had become a narrow service corridor. Mira measured neither with her eyes alone. She asked the attendant to bring the scale printed with each plan. Once the drawings were compared properly, the lost space was unmistakable. A place designed for people to rest had been absorbed into private rooms, while the public accounts still listed its upkeep as an expense.

賭場似乎正適合藏匿公款。華麗壁畫描繪舊時代，臨床報告卻揭露輪椅庇護站為何被悄悄關閉。 賭場舊圖將庇護站畫成面向街道的寬房間，新圖卻成了狹窄維修廊。米拉沒有只靠目測，她請人拿出每圖附帶的比例尺。正確比較後，失去的空間一目了然。原供人休息之處被私人房間吞併，公共帳目卻仍列著維護費。

The famous heir did not fascinate Mira. She heard him gasp when Judge Eun named a copyright theft, then watched him try to recommend a false witness. Eun asked the heir which documents he had personally seen. He answered with his family's reputation. The judge repeated the question without changing its wording. At last he named a man who could supposedly confirm the trust's wishes. Mira requested the man's connection to the estate before accepting his account. A witness could possess useful knowledge and still have an interest in its outcome. The heir's surprise at the copyright claim suggested a line of inquiry, not a verdict already reached.

知名繼承人無法使米拉著迷。恩法官提到著作權竊取時，他倒抽一口氣，隨即試圖推薦假證人。 恩法官問繼承人親眼看過哪些文件，他卻回答家族聲望。法官原句再問，最後他才說出一名據稱能證明信託意願的人。米拉要求先查此人與產業的關係。證人可能知道有用的事，也可能與結果有利益關係。繼承人對著作權指控的驚訝只是調查線索，不是既定判決。

From the balcony came a wolf's howl; the sealed files remained intact. A guard carried a rifle, and a thin scar across his wrist suggested he had witnessed the theft. The guard lowered the rifle only after Eun ordered the room cleared of unnecessary personnel. Mira asked about the wrist gently, allowing him to refuse. He covered the scar with his sleeve and pointed toward the basement instead. She did not treat the gesture as testimony. It was enough to ask whether the sealed inventory included the rooms below. The attendant brought a key list. One basement lock had no corresponding room in the new plan, though its key had been signed out repeatedly.

陽台傳來狼嚎，密封檔案仍完整。警衛扛著步槍，手腕的細疤顯示他可能目擊了竊案。 恩要求無關人員離開後，警衛才放低槍。米拉溫和詢問手腕，允許他拒答。他拉袖遮疤，指向地下室。她沒有將手勢視為證詞，只據此詢問密封清單是否包含樓下房間。管理員拿來鑰匙表，一把地下室鎖在新圖上沒有對應房間，鑰匙卻被多次借出。

A spectator asked for an upgrade to the public workshop rather than another costly statue. Mira showed the crowd that even sheer wealth could not replace accountability. The workshop request came from someone who had once used the refuge doorway. Mira asked that it be recorded beside the repair accounts, where it could be costed rather than applauded and forgotten. Eun then read the ownership clause to the gathering. The heir's family could possess objects inside the building without owning every wall around them. That distinction gave the judge a reason to examine the basement safe. Mira followed carrying the plans, alert to the difference between a family story and a deed.

一位旁觀者要求改善公共工坊，而非再建昂貴雕像。米拉讓群眾明白，光有驚人財富無法取代問責。 提出工坊要求的人曾使用庇護站門口。米拉請把要求記在修繕帳旁，讓人估價，而不是鼓掌後遺忘。恩接著向眾人朗讀產權條款；家族擁有室內物品，不等於擁有每面牆。這項區別使法官有理由查看地下保險櫃。米拉帶著圖紙跟下去，注意家族故事與地契之間的差異。

In the casino basement, Judge Eun located an old safe behind a broken painting. The heir protested that only his family had the right to open it, but the title deed named the public trust as owner. Inside were not coins or jewels, but insurance documents covering a secret reservoir chamber. One policy had been renewed the week before the flood. Mira copied the policy number. At last the money trail and the water trail had met.

恩法官在賭場地下室的破畫後找到保險櫃。繼承人抗議只有家族有權開啟，但地契上寫著公共信託才是所有者。櫃中沒有錢幣珠寶，只有祕密蓄水室的保險文件，其中一份在洪災前一週續保。米拉記下保單號碼，金流與水流的線索終於交會。
