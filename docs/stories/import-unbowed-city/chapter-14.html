The Day of Auction

灰橋不低頭 · 第 14 / 25 章 · 4000–5000 · 英中對照 · 校訂中

用互動版查詞、朗讀與收藏

Mira proposed to abolish an emergency sale clause and amend its language. She struggled to articulate her objection at the auction held inside a former cathedral. The auctioneer asked for objections to be brief. Mira held up the sale clause and asked whether the buyers would receive equipment or the emergency duties attached to it. He answered that duties were not part of the lot. That answer made her objection clearer: selling the tools while retaining the duty to rescue would leave the city responsible for work it could no longer perform. She asked the recorder to enter the exchange before the next item was announced from the old cathedral steps.

米拉提議廢除緊急出售條款並修正文字。她在改建自大教堂的拍賣場清楚表達反對理由。 拍賣官要求簡短反對。米拉舉起出售條款，問買家會接收設備，還是連緊急責任一起接收。他說責任不在拍品內。答案使她更清楚：賣掉工具卻保留救援義務，城市將負責自己已無法完成的工作。下一項在舊教堂台階宣布前，她要求記錄這段問答。

Voss claimed the sale would combat poverty. Leena asked for compassionate compliance rules instead, exposing how bidders tried to deceive citizens about each vehicle's emission level. Leena compared the bidders' transport promises with the maintenance cards hanging from the vehicles. Several claims concerned engines that were not in the room. She asked where those engines could be inspected. The bidders offered polished photographs instead. Mira requested the serial numbers beneath the images, since a picture of a clean vehicle did not identify the vehicle being sold. Leena copied each number onto the auction list and left a blank where the seller could not provide one.

沃斯聲稱拍賣能打擊貧窮。莉娜要求有人道的法規遵循標準，揭穿競標者如何欺騙市民有關各車輛排放量的事實。 莉娜拿競標者的運輸承諾比對車上維修卡。有些宣稱涉及不在場的引擎，她問去哪裡檢查，對方卻只給精美照片。米拉要求影像下的序號，因乾淨車輛照片並不能識別拍賣中的車。莉娜把序號抄進清單，無法提供的地方留白。

An enthusiastic volunteer discovered a grant meant for juvenile shelters. The legislative report placed it under decorative repairs to a mayoral porch. The shelter grant appeared to have crossed between budgets without ever reaching a shelter. The volunteer had nearly missed it because the decorative repairs used the same short project code. Mira asked her to follow the amount through each dated entry rather than assume the code was decisive. They found an approval signed before the porch estimate existed. Leena placed the dates in front of the council representative, who could not explain how an estimate had authorized a payment made earlier.

熱心志工找到一筆原供青少年庇護所的補助款。立法報告竟把它列成市長門廊裝飾工程費。 庇護所補助似乎只在預算間移動，從未抵達庇護所。志工差點錯過，因裝飾工程使用相同簡碼。米拉請她依日期追查金額，不把代碼當成定論。她們發現核准竟早於門廊估價。莉娜把日期放到議會代表面前，他無法解釋後來的估價如何授權先前付款。

One predator disguised as a bidder promised the prospect of riches; another had a skull tattoo, and a cheap wig hid his face. Mira ordered storage records opened before a single truck could leave. The disguises distracted the crowd, but Mira kept returning to the storage list. A buyer's appearance could not show where a rescue engine had gone. She asked volunteers to record which doors opened and which trucks moved, without blocking drivers or searching private bags. Leena checked the loading slips against the serial numbers copied earlier. Two entries had departure marks and no matching inspection. The auction had become a race between a legal pause and vehicles that might already be beyond the city gates.

一名掠奪者假裝買家，承諾發財前景；另一人有骷髏刺青，戴假髮遮臉。米拉要求貨車離場前先公開倉儲紀錄。 偽裝吸引人群目光，米拉卻不斷回到倉儲清單。買家外表不能說明救援引擎去向。她請志工記下開門與車輛移動，不阻擋司機、不搜私人包。莉娜用先前序號比對裝貨單，有兩筆記著出庫，卻沒有相符檢查。法律暫停令正與可能已出城的車輛競速。

By evening the auction was suspended, but two rescue engines had already vanished from storage. Leena found that their serial numbers were attached to a company controlled by Voss's sister. Mira refused to accuse the sister without questioning her. Family ties could reveal a conflict of interest, not prove an individual crime. She sent investigators to trace the vehicles and left volunteers to guard the remaining equipment. A fire broke out near the eastern school before sunrise.

傍晚拍賣被暫停，兩輛救援引擎卻已從倉庫消失。莉娜查到車輛序號連結沃斯姊妹控制的公司。米拉拒絕未詢問就定罪；親屬關係可揭露利益衝突，不能直接證明犯罪。她派調查員追車，留志工守住其餘設備。天亮前，東校附近起火。
