第一場議會戰

The First Council Battle

灰橋不低頭 · 第 5 / 25 章 · 4000–5000 · 英中對照

The constitutional committee included one lonely democrat willing to challenge the chairman. After a tense episode, Voss dismissed every complaint as exaggeration and called in a fleet of private guards. At the council's marble chamber, someone had opened every window despite the cold. The fresh air lifted loose pages on the table, and Mira pressed them down with smooth stones gathered from the roadside. She considered how easily a city could confuse speed with progress. A gate opened quickly might flood the next street; a form processed quickly might erase the name of the very person it was meant to protect. She asked for a few minutes of silence before the first decision. It was not a ceremony. It gave the tired volunteers time to check their work, and it gave her time to remember that confidence was not a substitute for knowing what was true.

憲政委員會裡只有一位民主派敢挑戰主席。漫長爭議後，沃斯說所有控訴都是誇大其詞，還調來大批私人警衛。 有人不顧寒冷打開全部窗戶，微風吹起文件，米拉用路邊撿來的圓石壓住。她想著城市多容易把速度誤認成進步：開水閘太快，下一條街可能被淹；處理表格太快，反可能抹掉要保護的名字。她要求決定前安靜幾分鐘，並非儀式，而是讓疲憊志工重新核對，也讓自己記住：自信不等於真相。

His greed had emptied the market of medicine and simple herb remedies. An incentive scheme favored wealthy contractors, while massive fees drove each peasant family from the outer farms. A clerk brought out three copies of the same announcement. They were almost identical, except for a word near the bottom of the second page. Mira asked to see the printing record and was told it had gone missing during a routine transfer. She did not raise her voice. She asked where the printer had stood, who had carried the box, and why the transfer had occurred before dawn. Each answer was small. Together they made a route through the confusion. Trade-union leader Ruth noticed that one witness was staring at the floor. During a recess, Mira waited until the side room emptied before asking whether the witness wished to speak privately. People frightened into silence sometimes needed privacy before they could offer courage.

他的貪婪耗盡藥物與草藥。獎勵制度偏袒富有承包商，高昂費用迫使城外農家離去。 書記拿來三份幾乎相同的公告，第二頁底部只有一個字不同。米拉索取印刷紀錄，卻被告知例行搬運時遺失。她沒有提高音量，只問印表機放在哪、誰搬箱子、為何凌晨搬運。每個答案都微不足道，串起來卻通往真相。工會領袖露絲發現一名證人一直盯著地板。休會時，米拉等側室人群散去才私下詢問。被恐嚇而沉默的人，有時需要隱私，才能重新鼓起勇氣。

The benches began to rattle when Mira described the recession. She wanted to recruit retail workers, not smash the council, because their records contained substantial evidence. The confrontation did not resemble the brave speeches Mira had imagined as a student. There was a leaking roof, a table with one short leg, and an exhausted clerk who kept misplacing his pen. Chairman Voss spoke confidently of order and necessity. Mira pointed instead to the costs omitted from the calculation: days without wages, nights without heat, and the quiet labor of people who kept a street alive. She would not allow those costs to be called insignificant. When the clerk finally found his pen, she waited until he had written down the entire answer. An argument entered into the record might outlive the person who made it.

米拉提到經濟衰退，座椅竟因鼓譟而震動。她要招募零售工人作證，而非破壞議會，因為他們手中有大量證據。 現實的對質不像米拉求學時想像的英勇演說：屋頂漏水，桌腳一短，書記累得一直找不到筆。主席沃斯侃侃而談秩序與必要，她卻指出計算漏掉的代價：沒薪水的日子、沒有暖氣的夜晚，以及支撐街道運轉的無聲勞動。她不容這些被稱作微不足道。書記終於找到筆，她等他完整記下回答。寫進紀錄的辯論，可能比辯論者活得更久。

Her tactic was to cross the threshold of the public gallery with witnesses beside her. The first verdict was delayed, but the recordings spread until the accusations became widespread. Before the workers dispersed, Mira unfolded a page she had kept apart from the official report. On it she had drawn the city's damaged bridges, not as a collection of failures but as a set of places where people might meet again. She knew repairs alone would not cure the habit of obedience that had allowed the disaster to spread. Someone would always prefer a short command to a difficult conversation. She told the group that they could begin again without pretending the past had been harmless. Then she folded the page and returned to the practical task of finding lanterns for the walk home.

她的策略是帶著證人跨入旁聽席。第一道裁決雖延後，錄音卻已廣泛傳播，指控席捲全城。 工人散開前，米拉攤開一張未放入官方報告的紙。上面畫著受損橋梁，她把它們視為人們能再次相遇的地方，而非單純失敗。她知道修繕不能治好讓災難擴大的盲從習慣，總有人寧聽簡短命令，不願艱難對話。她說，大家可以重新開始，但不必假裝過去沒有傷害。然後她收起圖紙，回頭找燈籠，好讓眾人安全回家。

As the guards moved to clear the room, Ruth placed an iron lunch box on the table. It contained the same meal served to the workers the night the first gate failed. A laboratory receipt had been pasted beneath the lid. Voss ordered the box removed, but too many spectators had already photographed it. Mira left the chamber with no vote and one better lead: the city's oldest kitchens had handled something more dangerous than spoiled food.

警衛準備清場時，露絲把鐵便當盒放在桌上。裡面裝著第一道水閘失效當晚供工人吃的食物，盒蓋下黏著實驗室收據。沃斯下令移走盒子，但太多人已拍照。米拉沒拿到表決，卻獲得更好的線索：城裡最古老的廚房處理過比腐敗食物更危險的東西。
