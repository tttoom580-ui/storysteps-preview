閣樓的自白

The Confession in the Attic

灰橋不低頭 · 第 17 / 25 章 · 4000–5000 · 英中對照 · 校訂中

用互動版查詞、朗讀與收藏

An accounting error had concealed names that Mara said she used to adore. In the attic, she showed Mira a comparable conduct report and prepared her confession. Mara had laid two sets of accounts beneath the attic window. One showed the entries as she had received them; the other showed what she submitted after the instruction arrived. She did not ask Mira to call the difference a mistake. Her finger shook over the changed column. Mira moved the lamp closer and asked permission before making a copy. The confession would need more than an admission of shame: it needed a sequence another person could follow without relying on Mara's memory alone.

會計錯誤掩蓋了瑪拉曾經敬重的人名。她在閣樓拿出可供比對的行為報告，準備坦白。 瑪拉在閣樓窗下放兩套帳，一套是收到時的原貌，一套是接命令後交出的版本。她沒有要求米拉把差異稱為失誤。手指在改動欄位上發抖，米拉移近燈，先徵求抄錄許可。自白不能只有羞愧，還要有旁人不靠她記憶也能追查的順序。

The council had sent a delegate to plan destructive repairs under the language of devotion. At the doorway, Mira refused to let the woman enter without a recording. The delegate objected to being recorded in a private house. Mira offered to continue at a neutral location with counsel present. The objection weakened when Mara said she wanted a record too. Until then the conversation had been framed as a favor to a distressed former employee. Mara asked whether the proposed repairs would destroy the attic wall behind her papers. The delegate did not answer directly. Mira wrote the unanswered question down, careful not to transform an evasive response into proof of a planned destruction.

議會派代表以奉獻之名安排破壞性工程。門口，米拉堅持必須錄音才能讓她進入。 代表反對在私人住宅錄音，米拉提議改到中立地點，讓律師在場。瑪拉也說想保留紀錄，反對便減弱了。原先這場談話被說成照顧失意前員工；瑪拉問，提議工程是否會拆掉文件後的閣樓牆。代表沒有直接回答。米拉記下未答問題，沒有把閃躲擅自當作蓄意毀物的證據。

A handmade filter lay on the table. Mara had been ordered to initiate a legendary pump, though the likelihood of flooding had been written into its manual. The filter was a reminder of the work Mara thought she had been helping. She had believed the pump would bring cleaner water to the district. The manual's warning used a diagram she had never been trained to read, yet her signature appeared beneath a claim that the risk was understood. Mira asked who had explained the page and who had required the signature. Mara named offices before naming people. Iona opened the manual flat and marked the warning's location for later technical examination.

桌上躺著手工濾器。瑪拉曾奉命啟動傳說中的幫浦，而說明書早記載洪災風險。 濾器提醒瑪拉，她曾以為自己在幫人。她相信幫浦會帶來乾淨水。說明書警告是她未受訓讀懂的圖，但她的簽名卻在「已了解風險」之下。米拉問誰解釋過、誰要求簽。瑪拉先說辦公室，再說人名。艾歐娜攤平說明書，標示警告位置，留待技術審查。

The motive made Mira shiver. She could sniff damp paper, feel its rough texture and understand the torment of someone who had obeyed a supreme order for years. The attic contained no explanation that could make the lost years harmless. Mira could acknowledge coercion without calling every choice inevitable. She asked Mara to distinguish the instruction she heard from the decision she made afterward. The answer took a long time. Outside, steps sounded on the stairs and stopped. Mara looked at the recording device, then at the two versions of her accounts. Before speaking again, she requested someone independent to explain what signing the statement might mean for her.

動機令米拉寒顫。她聞見潮濕紙味，摸到粗糙紙面，理解一個多年服從最高命令之人的煎熬。 閣樓裡沒有任何解釋能使失去的歲月變得無害。米拉可以承認脅迫存在，同時不把每個選擇都說成無可避免。她請瑪拉分清聽到的指示與自己隨後作的決定。回答花了很久。外面樓梯腳步停住。瑪拉看錄音機，再看兩套帳，重新開口前，要求獨立人士解釋簽署陳述的法律後果。

Mara's confession revealed that Voss had ordered the city's emergency map redrawn after a failed inspection. She had carried out the instruction and regretted it ever since. Mira did not promise immunity in return for truth. She explained the risks and offered independent counsel before recording the statement. When Mara signed, she asked if this could repair what she had done. “Not by itself,” Mira answered. “But it can stop the next person from being forced into the same silence.”

瑪拉自白，沃斯在一次失敗檢查後命人重畫全市緊急地圖。她服從後多年悔恨。米拉沒有以真相交換豁免，而是說明風險、提供獨立律師，再錄下陳述。瑪拉簽字後問能否彌補過錯。「光這樣不夠，」米拉答，「但能讓下一個人不再被迫沉默。」
