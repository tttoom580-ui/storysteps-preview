A Thousand Doorways

The collector insisted that public rooms be accessible. A comedian made children laugh while residents discussed a chemical compound responsible for paint decay. After the fire, people answered the doorway questions with unusual care. A narrow entrance was no longer an inconvenience to be endured in private; it could determine who escaped. Suri asked volunteers to measure the route a person would actually use, including the turn beyond each door. A broad opening followed by a tight corner could still trap a chair. Mira added a sketch box to the form so a resident could show an obstacle that the printed choices did not describe.

調查員堅持公共場所要無障礙。喜劇演員讓孩子們發笑，住戶則討論造成油漆腐蝕的化合物。 火災後，人們格外仔細回答門口問題。狹入口不再只是私下忍受的不便，還可能決定誰能逃出。蘇芮請志工測實際路線，包括門後轉角；寬門之後若接窄彎，輪椅仍會卡住。米拉在表上加繪圖格，讓住戶畫出選項沒有描述的障礙。

A foster mother questioned the conception of property as something only the rich could inherit. During an interval between patrol visits, she completed Mira's questionnaire without giving up her dignity. The mother asked whether an answer could cost the family its temporary room. Mira could not guarantee what every official would do with a name. She could explain what the survey would publish and let the woman withhold identifying details. Together they marked the dangerous step without marking the children's sleeping places. The distinction slowed the visit but made the result more trustworthy. A signature obtained through fear would tell the city less than an incomplete form given with a clear understanding of its purpose.

一名寄養母親質疑財產只能由富人繼承的觀念。巡邏間隙，她不卑不亢地填寫米拉的問卷。 母親問，回答會不會讓一家失去臨時住處。米拉不能保證每位官員如何使用姓名，卻能解釋公開範圍，讓她保留身分細節。她們標示危險台階，沒有標孩子睡處。這使訪查較慢，結果卻更可靠。恐懼中取得的簽名，不如清楚理解用途後自願交出的不完整表格。

Mira had to resume the survey after a sheriff seized a metal rod. His threat was subtle, yet the suite reserved for executives remained untouched and superb. The seized rod had been used to measure depth, and its loss stopped the volunteers at the next doorway. Suri borrowed a marked stick from a resident and checked it against the remaining tape. They could continue the measurements without turning the street into a confrontation. Mira recorded the seizure separately, with the officer's stated reason. Comparing the executive rooms with nearby homes would require consistent measurements, not an argument in which the missing tool became the only thing anyone remembered.

郡警扣走金屬棒後，米拉必須恢復調查。他的威脅含蓄，主管專用套房卻仍完美無缺。 被扣金屬棒原用來測深，失去後志工停在下一道門。蘇芮向住戶借有刻度的木條，拿剩下的捲尺核對，讓測量不必變成街頭衝突。米拉另記扣押與官員理由。要比較主管套房與鄰近住宅，需要一致尺度，不能只留下一場大家只記得工具被拿走的爭吵。

When an officer tried to underline selected answers, Suri refused to yield the original sheets. Public perception would not be rewritten by someone carrying a badge. Suri kept the originals in their numbered envelopes and offered copies with the same unanswered spaces. A resident then asked why the form did not include the alley behind her building. It was the path she used when the main street flooded. Others began describing similar paths. Mira stopped adding questions long enough to listen. The survey had been designed to discover defects in rooms; the residents were showing how those rooms connected to a city that the official plans could not see.

官員試圖替特定答案畫底線，蘇芮拒絕交出原卷。大眾觀感不能任由佩徽章的人改寫。 蘇芮把原卷放回編號信封，提供保留同樣空白的副本。一位住戶問，表格為何沒有她家後巷；主街淹水時她走那裡。其他人也說起類似路線。米拉暫停加問，開始聽。原本用來找房間缺陷的問卷，正讓居民展示這些房間如何連接成官方地圖看不見的城市。

The survey uncovered something the investigators had missed: most households knew a safe walking route that no official map showed. Suri marked the routes with colored string across a wall, connecting homes, clinics and the school courtyard. She had used a wheelchair for twenty years and knew which ramps could survive rain. Mira made Suri the evacuation coordinator, not a ceremonial adviser. The first successful rescue after the school fire belonged to the people who understood their own streets.

問卷發現調查員忽略的事：多數家庭知道官方地圖沒有的安全步道。蘇芮用彩線在牆上連結住宅、診所與校園。她使用輪椅二十年，熟悉哪道斜坡禁得起暴雨。米拉請她擔任撤離協調員，而非象徵性顧問。校火後第一場成功救援，屬於真正了解街道的人。
