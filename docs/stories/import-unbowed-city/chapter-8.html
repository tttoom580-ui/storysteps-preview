沒有名字的人

Those Without Names

灰橋不低頭 · 第 8 / 25 章 · 4000–5000 · 英中對照 · 校訂中

The word alien appeared on a form, causing chaos because no official offered comprehension of its meaning. Mira argued that rules should entitle every resident to help and allow flexibility. Nell asked the clerk to read the form aloud without skipping its headings. The first box asked for an address; the next rejected anyone whose address was no longer registered. A mother had filled both boxes correctly and still been refused. Mira placed her application beside the acquisition clause. The contradiction lay in the procedure, not in the woman's answers. She asked that the refusal be dated and signed, so the family would have something more useful than another instruction to return tomorrow.

表格上的「外來者」引發混亂，沒有官員能解釋其涵義。米拉主張，規則應賦予每個居民受助權，並容許彈性。 奈兒請職員連同標題朗讀表格。第一格要求地址，下一格卻拒收地址不在戶籍中的人。一位母親兩格都填對，仍被拒絕。米拉把申請書放在收購條款旁；矛盾在程序，不在婦人的答案。她要求拒絕決定註明日期並簽名，讓這家人拿到比「明天再來」更有用的東西。

At a gathering under a ragged roof, a retired salon owner offered space. Mira sought to minimize the food shortage while officials argued over the political spectrum. The salon still had mirrors along one wall. Volunteers turned the chairs toward the food table, leaving the mirrors uncovered so people could see who was approaching behind them. The owner knew many residents by their old appointments, though she had no official register. She offered to confirm who she remembered and to say plainly when she was unsure. Nell made two lists: people needing food now, and people seeking corrections later. Hunger could not wait for a completed investigation into every missing address.

破屋頂下舉辦集會，一位退休美容院老闆願意騰出店面。米拉努力減少糧食短缺，官員卻只爭辯政治立場。 美容院一面牆仍掛著鏡子。志工把椅子轉向食物桌，保留鏡面，讓人看見背後有誰接近。店主憑舊預約記得許多住戶，但沒有官方戶籍。她願證明記得的人，也坦承不確定之處。奈兒分列立即需要食物與稍後要更正身分的人；飢餓不能等待每個遺失地址都調查完畢。

The permit clerk tried to stall every strategic move and then suspend emergency transport. A theoretical model could not explain why a simple bus route had vanished from the records. The clerk pointed to an empty space on the transport chart as if emptiness settled the argument. A driver asked where the fuel payments for that route had gone. His question changed the discussion. A missing line on a map did not explain years of payments for a bus and its driver. Mira requested those payments without claiming they proved every passenger's identity. For the first time the clerk had to search for a document instead of asking a hungry person to produce one.

許可官拖延每項策略，又想暫停緊急運輸。理論模型無法解釋普通公車路線為何從紀錄中消失。 職員指著交通圖空白處，彷彿空白便能結案。司機追問那條路線的燃料費去了哪裡，問題改變了討論。圖上少一條線，無法解釋多年來支付的車資與薪資。米拉要求查看付款紀錄，並未聲稱那足以證明所有乘客身分。職員終於得自己找文件，而非叫飢餓者拿出證明。

A tribute to former workers hid a map of a water vein. One traveler had no visa and no papers whatsoever, yet she was the only witness who remembered the lost street. The traveler described a bend where buses slowed beside the workers' memorial. She could not supply a street number, but remembered the slope because her mother had needed help there. An elderly driver listening near the door lifted his head. Mira let the two compare their memories before showing either of them the disputed map. Agreement reached independently would mean more than a memory guided by her questions. When the traveler described the ticket punch, the driver reached slowly into the lining of his coat. The erased street was not a myth to the traveler who had lived there.

紀念舊工人的碑文藏著地下水脈地圖。一名旅客沒有簽證，也沒有任何文件，卻是唯一記得失落街道的目擊者。 旅客描述公車會在工人紀念碑旁減速的彎道。她說不出門牌，卻記得坡度，因母親在那裡需要攙扶。門邊老司機抬起頭。米拉先讓兩人比對記憶，沒有出示爭議地圖；獨立吻合比被她提問引導的回憶更有力。旅客說到車票打孔時，司機慢慢伸手進外套內襯。 對曾住在那裡的旅客來說，被抹去的街道並不是神話。

At the shelter, an elderly bus driver recognized a woman who had been listed as a stranger. He recalled driving her family along a street that no longer appeared on any map. For proof he produced a ticket punched with a pattern unique to that route. Mira held the scrap to the window and saw the outline of the erased district. A city could falsify its registry, she thought, but it could not so easily erase everyone's memory at once.

收容所裡，老公車司機認出一名被登記為陌生人的婦人。他記得曾載她一家走過如今從地圖消失的街道，還拿出有該線獨特打孔的車票。米拉迎窗舉起紙片，看見被抹去街區的輪廓。城市可以造假戶籍，卻很難同時抹掉所有人的記憶。
