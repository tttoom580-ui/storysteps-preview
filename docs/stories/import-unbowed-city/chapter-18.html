果園集會

The Orchard Rally

The administration called an antique document bizarre, then tried to conceal corruption behind decorative fabric. Mira used its legal framework to challenge the gross misuse of relief funds. The fabric covered a notice board where the old emergency powers had once been posted. Residents lifted it so Mira could read the document beside the new campaign promises. Its language was stiff, but its requirement was plain: temporary powers had to be reviewed. Mira asked a teacher to explain the clause in ordinary words. People who could not read the older script listened and challenged the explanation. The document would have little force if only an auditor could understand when it protected them.

行政部門稱古老文書荒謬，企圖以華美布幔掩蓋貪腐。米拉援引法律架構挑戰濫用救濟金的行為。 布幔遮著曾張貼緊急權限的公告板。居民掀開，讓米拉把舊文件與新競選承諾並讀。文字古板，要求卻明確：臨時權力必須複核。她請教師用日常語句解釋。不識舊字體的人也能聽、能質疑。若只有稽核員讀懂保護何時生效，文件的力量就很有限。

Hostility rose around the orchard, where an icon of the city's founder faced a new campaign poster. Citizens asked Mira to nominate a candidate, but she refused to become another unchallenged ruler. The crowd began calling Mira's name as though that settled the question of leadership. She asked who would check her decisions if she accepted an unchecked office. A mechanic answered from beside the founder's statue: the same residents who should check everyone else's. Mira invited the gathering to nominate several people and question them publicly. The mechanic looked alarmed when someone suggested her. That hesitation made the choice more real than the chant had been. A candidate would have to explain the work, not simply accept admiration.

果園旁敵意升高，建城者雕像面對新的競選海報。市民要米拉提名人選，她拒絕成為另一個不容質疑的統治者。 人群喊著米拉名字，彷彿領導問題已決定。她問，若接受不受制衡職位，誰檢查她？雕像旁技師答，同樣該檢查所有官員的居民。米拉邀大家提名多人並公開問話。有人提技師時，她吃了一驚。猶豫使選擇比口號更真實：候選人得解釋工作，不能只接受崇拜。

The rally might prevail if rival neighborhoods trusted one another. A hidden subsidy had paid agitators to turn their excitement and thrill into fear. An argument broke out between two groups near the orchard gate. Ruth asked them to name the claim they disputed before repeating accusations about one another. The claim came from identical leaflets handed out on opposite sides of town. Nell collected examples with the recipients' permission and compared their printing marks. They did not prove who had paid for every leaflet. They did show that supposedly separate neighborhood complaints had been prepared together. The crowd could question the message without turning on the neighbors who had received it.

只要敵對街區願意互信，集會便可能獲勝。有人暗中補助煽動者，將民眾的興奮轉成恐懼。 果園門邊兩群人爭吵。露絲請他們先說具體爭議，別再互相指控。說法出自城兩端發的相同傳單。奈兒徵得收件者同意收樣，比對印刷記號。這不能證明每張由誰出錢，卻顯示貌似各區獨立的抱怨是一起製作的。人群可以質疑訊息，不必攻擊收到訊息的鄰居。

The city could not charge tuition for speaking freely, Mira said. She would not undermine a neighbor to win; the venture mattered only if no one was left without a voice. The teacher proposed reading each candidate's answers twice, once before the vote and again after the pledge was written. That would make an omission easier to notice. A market porter asked who would keep the copies if the office refused them. Three observers from different streets volunteered. Mira gave them the same information rather than a private version for favored allies. By the time ballots were prepared, the gathering had begun practicing the checks it wanted its future representatives to accept.

米拉說，城市不能向自由發言收學費。她不會為勝選抹黑鄰居；只有人人都能發聲，這場行動才有價值。 教師提議候選人答案讀兩次，投票前一次，承諾寫成後再一次，讓遺漏容易發現。市場搬運工問，若辦公室拒收，誰保管副本？三條街的觀察員自願。米拉提供相同資訊，不給親近盟友私人版本。選票備好時，集會已在練習希望未來代表接受的監督。

At the orchard rally, residents chose three candidates instead of elevating Mira herself. One was a teacher, one a mechanic, and one a market porter. They signed a public pledge requiring every emergency order to carry a review date. Voss's agents tried to interrupt the vote, but the ballots had already been copied by independent observers. For the first time, Mira was not the only person who knew how to oppose him. That gave her room to prepare for the next storm.

果園集會上，居民選出三名候選人，而非擁戴米拉：教師、技師與市場搬運工。三人簽下公開承諾，所有緊急命令都必須有複核日期。沃斯的人想打斷投票，選票早由獨立觀察員複製。米拉第一次發現，反對他的人不再只有自己，她終於能騰出手準備下一場風暴。
