The Truth Commission

The commission promised to assess the damage, not merely count each coffin. Judge Eun asked citizens to conceive a future after years of decline and to envision something beyond punishment. Eun began by explaining what the commission could decide and what would require another court. Several families had expected a final sentence that morning. Their disappointment was visible, and she did not ask them to hide it. Mira brought the recovered registry entries with the earlier refusals attached. A corrected name alone would not show how long a person had been denied help. The two records together could preserve both the restoration and the injury, without pretending the second had vanished because the first was finally possible.

委員會承諾評估損失，不只是數每一口棺木。恩法官請市民思考多年衰退後的未來，想像超越懲罰的道路。 恩先解釋委員會能決定哪些事、哪些得交別的法院。家屬原盼今早就有終判，她沒要求掩飾失望。米拉把找回戶籍與過去拒絕書一同送來。更正姓名本身看不出被拒援助多久；兩種紀錄合存，才能既保留復原也保留傷害，不因終於可更正就假裝傷害消失。

The judicial hearing marked a milestone. Voss tried to mock witnesses while a laboratory expert traced a missing nutrient to a particle found in the drinking water. The laboratory expert was asked to explain the limits of the water sample before describing the particle. Voss laughed at the admission that not every past condition could be reconstructed. Eun stopped him and repeated the expert's distinction. Uncertainty about one measurement did not erase a documented order or payment. Yara listened from the witness seats, relieved that the hearing did not require her to claim knowledge she lacked. Mira marked the technical findings separately from the administrative decisions they might help explain.

司法聽證會成為里程碑。沃斯嘲弄證人，實驗室專家則追查飲用水微粒裡缺少的養分。 實驗室專家先說水樣限制，再描述微粒。沃斯笑他承認無法重建全部舊狀況，恩制止並重述區別：一項測量不確定，不會抹掉有紀錄的命令與付款。證人席的雅拉慶幸不必假裝知道自己不知道的事。米拉把技術結果與可能由此解釋的行政決定分列。

A pension ledger showed how productivity bonuses had concealed a profound theft. Two sibling witnesses appeared, one speaking alone about the stereotype that poor families always lied. The siblings disagreed over the date of a pension visit. Their lawyer tried to settle it quickly, but Eun allowed each to explain. One remembered the office appointment; the other remembered the later refusal at home. The disagreement had concealed two events rather than destroyed either account. Mira found the corresponding entries on separate ledger pages. A process designed only to catch contradiction could have silenced them. Here, a patient question made the pension trail more precise and gave both witnesses room to remain truthful. One had requested a solo appearance before the two accounts were compared.

退休金帳簿顯示生產力獎金如何掩護重大竊案。兩名手足出庭，其中一人單獨打破「窮人必說謊」的刻板印象。 手足對退休金訪查日期說法不同。律師想迅速解決，恩讓兩人各自解釋。一人記得辦公室預約，另一人記得後來到家拒絕。分歧藏著兩件事，未必推翻任何一方。米拉在不同帳頁找到對應。若程序只求抓矛盾，兩人可能被迫沉默；耐心追問讓金流更精確，也使證人有空間說真話。 其中一人要求先單獨出庭，再比較兩人的敘述。

In a subsequent hearing, Mira refused to thrust blame onto junior clerks. The city needed a treaty of responsibilities, not another list of convenient scapegoats. Mira described how orders passed through offices and where a clerk could reasonably have stopped them. She refused to give every signature the same weight. Some people had created the harmful policy; others had copied a form under threat; still others had tried to object. Eun asked her to identify evidence for each distinction. Mira removed one unverified claim from her statement. The commission would be stronger for recording that limit than for presenting an accusation so complete that no uncertainty was allowed to remain.

後續聽證中，米拉不把罪責推給低階職員。城市需要權責協議，而不是另一份方便的代罪羔羊名單。 米拉描述命令如何流經辦公室、職員何處有能力阻止，不讓每個簽名負相同責任。有人制定傷害政策，有人受威脅抄表，有人曾反對。恩要求逐項證據，米拉從陳述刪掉一項未核實主張。承認界限，比呈現一份不容任何疑問的完整指控，更能使委員會可靠。

The commission's final interim report named the officials who had approved the secret gate and the companies that had profited. It also recorded public agencies that had tried to help but lacked the authority to intervene. Judge Eun said the distinction mattered: a truthful history could not be built from heroes and villains alone. Mira signed only the sections she had personally verified. The complete report was placed in three public libraries, beyond the reach of any one minister's drawer.

委員會期中報告列出批准祕密水閘的官員與獲利公司，也記錄那些曾想幫忙卻無權介入的公共機構。恩法官說區分很重要，真實歷史不能只由英雄與惡人構成。米拉只簽署親自核實的部分。完整報告分存三座公共圖書館，不再落入任何部長的抽屜。
