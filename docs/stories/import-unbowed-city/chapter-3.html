溪邊的證人

The Witness at the Creek

灰橋不低頭 · 第 3 / 25 章 · 4000–5000 · 英中對照

用互動版查詞、朗讀與收藏

Mira documented abuse at the river clinic and demanded consultation with residents. The deputy answered with contempt, although a correspondent from the capital recorded his words. A thin band of sunlight crossed the floor at the South Creek pier. It fell on a map whose edges were worn where people had pointed to the same missing street for years. Mira remembered her mother's advice about mending a torn coat: first turn the fabric over and find out where the original seam lies. She approached the city's damaged records in much the same way. A recent signature was not necessarily the beginning of a decision. Sometimes one had to follow an old mistake through several administrations before seeing the hand that benefited from it. She let the witnesses arrange the documents in their own order, even when it differed from the order preferred by the office.

米拉記錄溪邊診所的濫權行為，要求與居民協商。副署長傲慢回應，沒發現首都記者正在錄音。 一道細陽光橫過地板，照亮邊緣被反覆指點而磨損的地圖。米拉記起母親修補衣服的叮嚀：先翻到背面找出原縫線。她也用這個方式研究受損的市府紀錄。近期簽名未必是決策的起點，有時得追溯幾屆政府的舊錯，才能看出誰從中得利。她任由證人依自己的方式排列文件，即使和辦公室習慣不同。

At the creek, a deliberate leak had damaged the diplomatic shipment. The deputy wanted to call the loss an accident, though the local ecosystem was already failing. An official map suggested a simple explanation, but Mira drew a second one based on footsteps, meals delivered and doors opened after dark. Ordinary labor left a geography of its own. Nell, a young clerk, marked the places where workers had waited for instructions; Mira marked the places where an order could have been changed without leaving a signature. When the two maps overlapped, a narrow gap appeared. It was not yet proof of guilt, and she warned everyone against treating it as such. It did, however, point to the one office that had been absent from every interview. Their next question was not what the office claimed. It was why its silence had been so carefully preserved.

溪流旁有人蓄意破壞外交貨物。副署長想將損失說成意外，當地生態系卻早已惡化。 官方地圖提供簡單說法，米拉卻根據腳印、送餐與夜裡開門的紀錄重畫另一張。平凡勞動也有自己的地理。同伴標出工人等候指示處，米拉標出可無簽名改令之處。兩圖重疊出現細縫，尚不足證明犯罪，她提醒大家不可過度解讀；但它指向唯一未受訪的辦公室。接下來要問的不是那裡宣稱什麼，而是為何沉默被如此小心保存。

A fish thought extinct washed onto the bank. Yara trusted her gut and told Mira to notify every household, even though the odds of finding the source seemed terrible. For the first time the team disagreed openly. One volunteer wanted the evidence released at once; another feared that doing so would alert the people who controlled the machines. Mira listened and then divided the problem into decisions that could be reversed and decisions that could not. They could revise a notice tomorrow. They could not restore a life lost because a warning was withheld. She asked Nell, a young clerk, to prepare the safer of two routes while she informed residents of the uncertainty. No one cheered for the compromise. Mira considered that a good sign. Difficult choices were not made honest by applause, and a leader who needed applause would become dangerous very quickly.

一種被認為滅絕的魚被沖上岸。雅拉相信直覺，請米拉通知每戶人家，儘管找到污染源的機率渺茫。 團隊第一次公開爭執：有人想立即公布證據，有人怕驚動操縱機器的人。米拉把問題分成可挽回與不可挽回的決定。通知明天可以修訂，隱瞞警告造成的死亡卻無法挽回。她請同伴準備較安全的路線，自己向居民說明不確定之處。沒人為折衷鼓掌，她反倒覺得是好事。艱難抉擇不會因掌聲而更誠實，需要掌聲的領袖也很快會變危險。

During the next phase, Nell waited at the pier while someone tried to sneak past security. The supposed successor offered to swap a sealed box for a trophy from the mayor's office; Mira insisted on sustainable proof. Night fell without bringing a clean conclusion. Mira marked the places where the witness accounts still contradicted one another and circled a date that appeared three times. She had learned to live with unfinished knowledge without becoming careless about it. As the others packed their tools, she asked them to choose a person outside the team who could check tomorrow's work. That request surprised the older volunteers; they were accustomed to leaders demanding trust rather than inviting inspection. Mira smiled only briefly. If they wished to build a different city, the difference had to begin in habits small enough to practice on an ordinary Tuesday.

下一階段，奈兒在碼頭守候，有人偷偷越過警戒。自稱繼任者的人提出用密盒交換市長辦公室的獎盃，米拉只接受可長久驗證的證據。 天黑了，事情仍沒有乾淨俐落的結論。米拉標出證詞互相矛盾之處，圈起重複三次的日期。她學會與未完成的知識共處，卻不因此馬虎。同伴收拾工具時，她請大家找一位團隊外的人來檢查明天工作。年長志工很驚訝，他們習慣領袖要求信任，而非邀請監督。米拉只是短暫微笑。若要建設不同的城市，差異必須從平常日也做得到的小習慣開始。

The witness handed Nell a tin box and asked that it be opened only before three people. Inside lay a railway pass, a child's drawing, and a page naming workers sent to inspect the hidden gate. Every name had been crossed out except one: MARA. Mira remembered an accountant who had resigned without explanation five years earlier. A new route appeared in the investigation, and this time the clue was a person still alive.

證人給奈兒一只鐵盒，要求至少三人在場才能開。盒裡有火車通行證、兒童畫，以及受派檢查祕密水閘的工人名冊。每個名字都被劃掉，只剩「瑪拉」。米拉想起五年前無故辭職的會計員。調查出現新路線，這回線索是個仍活著的人。
