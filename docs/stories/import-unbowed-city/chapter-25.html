無人能獨占的城

The City No One Can Own

灰橋不低頭 · 第 25 / 25 章 · 4000–5000 · 英中對照 · 校訂中

用互動版查詞、朗讀與收藏

Standing alongside children, Mira opened an alternate route beside the restored canal. The word communism appeared in a hostile leaflet, but she insisted that the crucial question was who could inspect the records. A child stopped at the canal notice and asked whether the new path could close during another emergency. Mira pointed to the place where a closure would have to be recorded with its reason and review date. The answer was less exciting than declaring that nothing bad could happen again. It was also something the child could check later. A resident carrying the hostile leaflet joined them. Mira offered the same explanation, leaving the person free to criticize the charter after reading its actual provisions.

米拉與孩子並肩站著，開啟修復運河旁的替代道路。敵意傳單寫著共產主義，她堅持真正關鍵是誰能檢查紀錄。 孩子在運河公告前問，再有緊急狀況，新路會不會關。米拉指給他看，關閉必須記理由與複核日期。答案沒有「壞事永不再來」動聽，卻能日後查證。持敵意傳單的居民走近，她同樣解釋，讓對方讀真條文後仍可批評憲章。

The city would evolve beyond one leader's grip. Her hypothesis was simple: optimism meant work, and originality meant refusing the old machine's false choices. The new board's table had no chair reserved permanently for her. Mira found the empty space unsettling before she found it welcome. For months, every unanswered question had seemed to arrive in her notebook. Now a clerk directed one question to a neighborhood representative and another to the inspection group. Neither looked at Mira for permission. She wrote down the date, recognizing a change that would not appear in a heroic portrait: useful work was proceeding in her presence without requiring her command.

城市要走出單一領袖的掌握。她的假設很簡單：樂觀必須付諸行動，原創就是拒絕舊機器設下的假選擇。 新董事會桌邊沒有永久留給她的椅子。米拉先覺不安，才覺歡迎。幾個月來，未答問題似乎全進她筆記。如今職員把一題交各區代表，另一題交檢查組，無人看她求許可。她記下日期，看見英雄肖像畫不出的改變：有用的工作在她面前進行，無需她下令。

A poetic speech from a street prophet followed the steady pulse of drums. When the council set a fair water quota, families recognized a resemblance to the promises they had once made themselves. The quota debate lasted longer than the celebration speeches. A household caring for sick relatives needed more water than the proposed allowance. Another feared that exceptions would reopen the old private privileges. Yara helped the board frame a public appeal process with reasons that could be reviewed without exposing medical details. Mira listened from the ordinary seats. The first version of fairness had been too simple. Being able to revise it openly was a better sign than pretending the celebration had already answered every difficult need.

街頭先知用詩般演說應和穩定鼓聲。議會訂下公平用水配額時，家庭認出它與當年彼此的承諾相似。 配額辯論比慶祝演說久。照顧病親家庭需較多水，有人怕例外重開特權。雅拉幫董事會設公開申訴程序，使理由可複核又不洩病情。米拉坐一般席聽。第一版公平過於簡單，能公開修正，比假裝慶祝已回答所有難題更好。

No shareholder could make the water price soar without review. The charter would specify every stimulus payment, and its most striking sentence declared that Mira herself could be removed by a public vote. Before signing, Mira asked a student to read the removal clause aloud. The student stumbled over one legal phrase, and the drafters agreed to replace it with ordinary words. No one should need her help to discover how to challenge her. The signatures filled the sheet slowly, with space left for objections to be recorded beside them. She put the pen down and walked past the canal without an escort. The next months would test the machinery and the charter alike, through work that belonged to the city rather than to her. A resident asked how to appeal if a relief voucher was refused. The drafters pointed to the same review process, rather than sending her to seek a private favor from a board member.

沒有股東能不經審查就任意調漲水價。憲章列明每筆刺激補助，最醒目的一句是：連米拉本人，也可以透過公開投票被撤換。 簽前，米拉請學生朗讀撤換條款。他卡在一處法律用語，起草者同意改成日常話。人們不該要她幫忙才知道如何質疑她。簽名慢慢填上，旁邊留記反對意見的空間。她放筆，無隨從走過運河。未來幾月，機械與憲章都要受檢驗；工作屬於城市，不屬她。 一位居民問救濟券若被拒收如何申訴。起草者指出相同複核程序，而非叫她私下求董事幫忙。

Six months later, the water gates had been tested in public three times. The city board now published its accounts and allowed residents to challenge any emergency order. Mira had declined a permanent executive seat. She taught a free evening class on reading contracts and kept her old notebook on a shelf where students could examine it. On the first page she wrote a final sentence: “A strong city does not need one woman who cannot be defeated. It needs people who no longer have to kneel.”

六個月後，水閘已公開測試三次。新的市民董事會公布帳目，居民也可質疑任何緊急命令。米拉婉拒永久行政席位，改教免費夜課，教大家讀合約，舊筆記本放在學生可查閱的架上。扉頁她寫下最後一句：「堅強的城市不需要一名永不敗的女人，需要的是再也不必下跪的人們。」
