第一次試燒

The First Firing

裂痕也能盛住光 · 第 10 / 25 章 · 2000–3000 · 英中對照 · 校訂中

The safety team finally gave permission for a limited test firing. An prepared one plain bowl and one bold sample with a pearl-colored surface. She wore a simple handkerchief around her hair, while Wei checked the kiln's structure and the distance to the nearest stove used for tea. A student called the glowing test pieces fashionable. An smiled but asked everyone to stand behind the marked line. She had once imagined a kiln opening like a fairy tale, with a perfect object appearing in a sudden flame. Real testing moved more slowly and required careful notes.

安全團隊終於允許進行有限度的試燒。林安準備一只素碗與一只帶有珍珠色釉面的醒目樣品，還把手帕綁在頭髮上。鍾維檢查窯爐結構，以及它和煮茶爐子的距離。一名學生說發亮的試作品很時尚。林安笑了，卻請每個人站在標示線後。她曾想像開窯像童話，完美作品會在火焰中突然出現。真正的測試慢得多，也需要仔細記錄。

At lunchtime a bride and her partner visited to ask about wedding gifts. The couple wanted small bowls with an angel design, but An refused to determine a price before the results were known. She offered them a drawing instead. A guest brought melon slices, and another described a silk dress she had worn to a family celebration. Everyone had a different idea of beauty. An found relief in admitting she could make only what her hands and equipment could safely support. The couple thanked her and said they would return after testing.

午餐時，一位準新娘和伴侶來詢問結婚禮物。兩人想訂製天使圖案的小碗，林安卻拒絕在結果出來前決定價格，只先提供草圖。訪客帶來切好的哈密瓜，另一人聊起家庭慶典穿過的絲質洋裝。每個人對美都有不同想法。林安發現承認自己的雙手與設備目前只能安全做到哪些事，反而讓她鬆一口氣。那對伴侶道謝，說測試完會再回來。

The kiln controller flashed a warning, and Wei raised a hand rather than rushing toward it. An felt an urge to stare at the screen, hoping the numbers would somehow improve. The team had planned what to do: stop heating, record the exact time, and wait for the equipment to cool. There was no dramatic punch or sudden rescue. The useful action was simply following the plan. Later, when the sensor was checked, the alarm turned out to reflect a real temperature difference between the upper and lower shelves.

窯爐控制器閃出警告，鍾維舉手示意大家停下，而不是衝向機器。林安忍不住盯著螢幕，希望數字不知怎地改善。團隊早已規劃好流程：停止加熱、記下確切時間，等待設備冷卻。沒有戲劇性的重擊，也沒有突然的英雄救援；真正有效的是遵守計畫。之後檢查感測器，發現警報反映上下層架之間真實的溫差。

An was optimistic that the problem could be solved without hiding it. Wei explained how the tide of hot air moved inside the kiln, rising along one wall before crossing the roof. An drew arrows on a sheet to describe the movement. Mei asked whether the company that had sold the kiln should be informed. “Yes,” An answered. “They may know something we don't, but we also need independent measurements.” The decision felt moral as well as technical. A customer should be able to trust a maker who knows when to ask for help.

林安樂觀地相信問題可以被解決，而不必藏起來。鍾維解釋窯內熱氣如何像潮水般移動，沿一面牆上升，再穿過頂部。林安在紙上畫箭頭表示流向。美怡問是否應通知販售窯爐的公司。「要，」林安回答，「他們可能知道我們不知道的事，但我們也需要獨立測量。」這不只是技術選擇，也有道德意義。顧客應能信任懂得在需要時求助的創作者。

An kept the two test bowls in different boxes so that nobody could confuse them. On the first she wrote “appearance only,” and on the second “further testing required.” A visitor asked why she did not throw the imperfect bowl away. An explained that its shape held information about heat distribution, and that a failure could be useful when properly recorded. The visitor, an older woman who had repaired clothing all her life, nodded. She told An about saving a damaged wedding dress by changing its shape rather than pretending the fabric was new. An found the comparison comforting. Repair did not erase the past. It made the past visible in a different form. She invited the woman to join a future class about mending and ceramics, but promised to check which activities the equipment could actually support.

林安把兩只試燒碗放在不同盒裡，避免混淆。一只寫著「僅供外觀觀察」，另一只寫「尚待進一步檢測」。訪客問她為何不丟掉不完美的碗。林安解釋，形狀保留了窯內熱度分布的資訊；只要記錄得當，失敗也能有用。那位一生都在修補衣物的年長女士點頭，說自己曾靠改造版型救回受損婚紗，而不是假裝布料仍全新。林安覺得這個比喻令人安心。修復不會抹除過去，而是讓過去以另一種形式被看見。她邀女士參加未來的修補與陶藝課，但答應先確認設備能安全支援哪些活動。

When the kiln cooled, An opened it under supervision. The pearl-colored bowl was beautiful, but its base was slightly uneven. She did not put it on the sale shelf. Instead she wrote a card listing the successful steps and the remaining problem. Wei said the notes would impress an inspector more than a picture of a flawless object with no history. An held the bowl under the afternoon light. It was neither a failure nor a triumph. It was the first honest answer her new workshop had produced, and it gave her a reason to continue.

窯爐冷卻後，林安在監督下開窯。珍珠色的碗很美，底部卻微微不平。她沒有把它放上商品架，而是寫了一張卡，列出成功的步驟與尚待解決的問題。鍾維說，對檢查員來說，這些筆記比一張沒有製程紀錄的完美照片更有說服力。林安把碗舉向午後陽光。它既不是失敗也不是勝利，而是新工坊給出的第一個誠實答案，讓她有理由繼續。
