A Fair Made of Choices

裂痕也能盛住光 · 第 8 / 25 章 · 2000–3000 · 英中對照 · 校訂中

用互動版查詞、朗讀與收藏

The town's craft fair opened beneath a long web of paper flags. An carried her baggage to a small table and arranged the first unfired tiles. A cheerful citizen offered to help, but she stopped him before he could grab a box without knowing how fragile it was. The market controller checked electrical outlets, while a cook tested a portable cooker for the food stalls. The smell of crispy bread made An hungry. She had not joined the fair to sell cups yet; she was there to explain how residents could give or withdraw permission for their stories to appear.

鎮上的手作市集在紙旗交織的長廊下開幕。林安提著行李到小攤位，排好第一批未燒的陶片。一名熱心市民想幫忙，她卻先攔住他，免得他不清楚箱子易碎就直接抓起。市集管理員檢查插座，廚師則試用攤位的可攜式炊具。酥脆麵包的香氣讓林安餓了。她這次還不是來賣杯子的，而是說明居民如何同意展出自己的故事，或撤回這份同意。

A volunteer dressed as a buffalo for the children's parade, and a toy maker placed a harmless wooden bullet on a display about old games. Some visitors laughed at the strange combination. An had learned lately that a public exhibition could include objects she did not personally like, provided they were safe and clearly explained. She placed a notice beside every tile, including who had made it and whether it could be photographed. A woman asked for her name to be hidden. An changed the card at once, without demanding a reason.

志工穿成水牛參加兒童遊行，一位玩具師傅則在古早遊戲展品旁放了一枚無害的木製子彈。奇怪的組合讓一些訪客大笑。林安最近學到，公共展覽可以容納她個人不喜歡的物件，前提是安全且說明清楚。她在每片陶片旁寫出製作者，以及是否允許拍照。有位女士希望隱藏姓名，林安立刻改掉標籤，沒有追問原因。

An noticed litter beneath the next stall and asked the organizer for a bin. The request led to a wider conversation about fair rules. A talkative seller said the wealthy sponsors should decide the schedule because they paid most of the bills. Mei disagreed. Money helped, but it did not replace consent. Another visitor worried that a personal story about sexual harassment might be displayed without the survivor's permission. An answered that such a story would stay private unless its owner clearly chose otherwise. She would not use pain as a shadow that made her own artwork look important.

林安注意到隔壁攤位下有垃圾，便向主辦單位要了垃圾桶。這個請求引出更廣泛的市集規則討論。一名健談的攤商認為，富有的贊助者付了大部分費用，就應決定時程。美怡不同意：錢能幫忙，卻不能取代同意。另一位訪客擔心涉及性騷擾的個人故事會在未獲當事人同意時展出。林安表示，除非故事主人明確選擇公開，否則一定保密。她不願利用他人的痛苦來襯托自己的藝術。

Wei came to the stall after finishing a safety inspection across town. An asked whether he thought the whole permission process was too slow. He shook his head. “You owe people a real choice, not merely a box they are afraid to refuse.” His answer stayed with her. At the edge of the fair someone began to yell over a disputed price, and the crowd turned toward the noise. Mei moved the delicate tiles away from the crush. They did not need to win every argument. They needed to keep the work and its makers safe.

鍾維完成鎮上另一處安全檢查後來到攤位。林安問他是否覺得整套同意程序太慢。他搖頭說：「妳欠大家的是能真正選擇的權利，不是一個讓人不敢拒絕的勾選框。」她把這句話記在心裡。市集另一端有人為價格爭吵，開始大喊，群眾轉頭看去。美怡將脆弱陶片移離擁擠處。他們不必贏得每場爭論，只需要保護作品與創作者。

Mei placed a feedback box at the edge of the stall and asked visitors to write a question even if they did not want to give their names. The box quickly filled with practical concerns. Could the studio be reached without climbing the steep path? Would an adult learner be required to show a personal story? What happened if someone withdrew a tile after the exhibition began? An wrote responses on a public sheet, leaving space for questions she could not yet answer. A child asked if the buffalo costume would return next year. Mei laughed and said that decision belonged to the festival team, not the ceramics group. The simple exchange reminded An that a community program could become confusing when one organizer claimed control over every part of it. She wanted to be responsible for her promises, not for matters outside her authority.

美怡在攤位邊放了意見箱，請訪客即使不署名也可以寫下問題。箱子很快裝滿實際的疑問：能否不爬陡坡就到工坊？成人學員一定要分享私人故事嗎？展出開始後撤回陶片該怎麼處理？林安把回覆寫在公開表格，也留下暫時無法回答的空格。一名孩子問水牛裝明年還會出現嗎？美怡笑說，那要由節慶團隊決定，不是陶藝小組。這個簡單對話提醒林安，若主辦者聲稱掌管每件事，社區計畫就容易混亂。她只想對自己做出的承諾負責，而不攬下權限以外的事情。

When the fair closed, An counted more questions than sales. She had given away information sheets, accepted corrections to labels, and met a teacher who wanted to bring an adult class for a visit. She felt tired but not defeated. In her notebook she wrote three results: people understood the difference between a safe display and a usable cup; some wished to keep their memories private; and one small market could make room for different kinds of participation. The unfinished tiles were still unfinished. The promises she had made about them were becoming clearer.

市集結束時，林安算出收到的問題比成交的商品還多。她發出說明單、接受標籤修正，還認識一位希望帶成人班來參觀的老師。她很累，卻沒有挫敗。筆記本上記下三項成果：人們理解安全展示品與可使用杯子的差別；有些人想保留記憶的隱私；一場小市集也能容納不同的參與方式。未燒的陶片仍然未完成，但她對它們做出的承諾愈來愈清楚。
