A Practice Alarm

裂痕也能盛住光 · 第 7 / 25 章 · 2000–3000 · 英中對照 · 校訂中

The emergency drill began with an awful sound from the alarm speaker. An felt awkward giving instructions in front of so many neighbors. She had built the studio to create beauty, not to practice leaving it. Mei reminded her that an appeal for volunteers would succeed only if people trusted the plan. Behind a brick wall, a narrow cupboard blocked part of the escape route. Wei asked two helpers to move it before anyone entered. The change was simple, but An wondered how many people had walked past the cupboard without noticing the danger.

緊急演練從警報器刺耳的聲音開始。林安在眾多鄰居面前下指令，覺得非常不自在。她建工坊是為了創作美，不是為了練習撤離。美怡提醒她，招募志工的呼籲只有在大家相信計畫時才會有效。磚牆後有一個狹窄櫥櫃擋住逃生通道，鍾維請兩位幫手先搬開。改動很簡單，但林安忍不住想：多少人曾走過櫥櫃，卻沒看見危險？

At the back table a child had made a clay lily in a vase, while another student shaped a jar meant for dry flowers. One visitor put a dime beside a tile to show how small the first community donations had been. An explained that even a terrific-looking vase needed a steady base. Clay could break into sharp pieces if dropped. She asked everyone to leave the displays behind during an evacuation. The works were important, but human flesh could not be replaced as easily as a piece of fired earth.

後桌有個孩子做了插在花瓶裡的陶百合，另一位學生捏了適合插乾燥花的罐子。一名訪客把一枚十美分硬幣放在陶片旁，說明最早的社區捐款有多麼微小。林安解釋，即使花瓶看來棒極了，也得有穩固的底部。陶器摔落會裂成尖片，因此緊急撤離時應放下展品。作品很重要，人身卻不像燒過的泥土那麼容易替換。

Mei turned the drill into a practical lesson. She asked the group to invent a clear message for visitors who did not know the building. One suggestion was “Run!” Another was “Follow the green signs to the yard.” They chose the second. Someone joked that the old electrical box might explode, but Wei stopped the rumor. There was no evidence of an explosion risk from that box. They did have a blocked exit and an outdated sign, both problems that could be solved now. An liked how the discussion replaced fear with action.

美怡把演練變成實際課程，請大家設計一則讓陌生訪客也聽得懂的訊息。有人建議「快跑！」，也有人說「沿著綠色標誌走到院子」。大家選了第二句。有人開玩笑說舊電箱可能爆炸，鍾維立刻制止傳言。他說沒有證據顯示電箱有爆炸風險；目前真正的問題是出口被擋住、標誌過時，而這兩件事現在就能修正。林安喜歡大家用行動取代恐懼。

The volunteers cleaned junk from the path and discussed the flavor of the tea they would serve after the drill. One complained about the wage offered to temporary helpers. An agreed that work deserved fair payment and changed her plan: no one would be asked to carry heavy furniture without training or proper pay. She also scheduled a shorter drill sometime next week, after the repairs. When the final person reached the yard, everyone cheered. There had been no emergency, but they had learned something they might need one day.

志工清走通道裡的雜物，還聊起演練結束後要提供哪一種口味的茶。有人抱怨臨時幫手的工資太低，林安同意勞動應得到合理報酬，於是修改計畫：未受訓或沒有適當薪資，就不要求任何人搬運重物。她也安排下週某個時間再做一次較短的演練，前提是先完成維修。最後一個人走到院子時，大家歡呼。那天並未發生災難，卻學到將來可能用得上的事。

The next morning Mei asked An to remind the children what they had learned. An did not quiz them about complicated regulations. She showed two drawings of the same corridor: one with the cupboard blocking the way and one with a clear exit. The children pointed to the safer picture immediately. A quiet boy asked whether adults also needed practice. “More than children sometimes,” An said, and several parents laughed. She explained that the purpose of a drill was not to make anyone feel foolish. It was to notice a problem while there was still time to correct it. The children helped draw small arrows for the visitor map, and Wei checked that the arrows matched the real route. Each arrow was an invitation to act rather than a promise that danger could never happen. They agreed to practice again after the repairs.

隔天早上，美怡請林安提醒孩子們昨天學到什麼。林安沒有考複雜法規，而是拿出兩張相同走廊的圖：一張被櫥櫃擋住，另一張出口暢通。孩子們立刻指出較安全的畫面。一個安靜男孩問大人是否也需要練習。「有時比孩子更需要。」林安說，引得幾位家長笑了。她解釋演練不是讓人出醜，而是在還來得及修正時發現問題。孩子們幫訪客地圖畫小箭頭，鍾維確認箭頭符合真正路線。每個箭頭都在邀請人採取行動，而不是保證危險永不發生。他們約好修繕完再練習一次。

As evening fell, Mei asked An what had surprised her most. “The speed,” An answered. “We spent weeks talking about beauty and ten minutes discovering where people could get hurt.” Mei said the discovery did not make their earlier work worthless; it made the next version better. They drew a new floor plan and left room for visitors to correct it. An placed the plan on the studio door, where nobody could miss it. The next day would bring more work, but tonight she could look at the clear pathway and see progress.

傍晚，美怡問林安最驚訝的是什麼。「速度，」她回答，「我們談了好幾週的美，卻只花十分鐘就發現人可能在哪裡受傷。」美怡說，這不代表之前的努力毫無價值，而是讓下一版更好。兩人畫了新的平面圖，並保留訪客提出修正的空間。林安把圖貼在工作室門上，讓誰都看得見。明天仍有許多事要做，但今晚她至少能看著暢通的動線，確認事情有在前進。
