The Missing Pages

裂痕也能盛住光 · 第 9 / 25 章 · 2000–3000 · 英中對照 · 校訂中

用互動版查詞、朗讀與收藏

An opened the workshop database and found a weekly inspection log with three missing pages. One entry said the fan had made a strange roar; another described a worker who had gone faint from heat. There was no record of anyone being injured, but the gaps gave her a fright. Wei reminded her that “missing” did not automatically mean stolen. He asked her to check the backup cabin beside the road, where older paper files had been kept. An disliked the narrow building, but she agreed that a sound conclusion would need more than a guess.

林安打開工坊資料庫，發現每週檢查紀錄少了三頁。一筆記載風扇發出奇怪的怒吼聲，另一筆說某位員工熱得快昏倒。紀錄沒有顯示有人受傷，但缺口仍讓她吃了一驚。鍾維提醒她：「缺頁不一定就是遭竊。」他請她去路旁的備用小屋查舊紙本檔案。林安不喜歡那座狹窄建築，卻同意可靠結論不能只靠猜測。

Inside, a skillful carpenter had once built shelves from local wood. A small horn hung above the door, used to announce deliveries before telephones became common. An found a folder that had begun to sink into a damp corner. She lifted it carefully to avoid making the pages bleed ink. At the back was a note about a children's summer camp, with a cheerful tune and laughter described by the teacher. The folder held ordinary life beside technical records. An wondered how much of the town's history could be lost through careless storage alone.

小屋內的木架由一位手藝高明的木匠用本地木材打造。門上掛著小號角，過去還沒普及電話時用來通知送貨。林安發現一份資料夾正陷進潮濕角落，小心取出，免得墨水滲開。後面夾著兒童夏令營便條，老師記下輕快曲調與孩子的笑聲。平凡生活與技術紀錄竟被放在一起。林安不禁想，光是保存不當，就可能遺失多少小鎮歷史。

A retired cleaner visited while they worked. He remembered a colleague who loved to wander through the hills on weekends and a former lover who had moved overseas. He spoke briefly, then pointed out a locked cabinet. Its key had been returned to the office years ago. An thanked him but did not ask him to open it without permission. “A cabinet is not a weapon,” she told Mei later. “We don't need to force it just because we want an answer.” They wrote down the cabinet number and requested access through the owner.

他們整理時，一位退休清潔員來訪。他想起一名同事週末愛在山裡閒逛，也提到多年以前搬到海外的舊情人。他只簡短說了幾句，便指向一個上鎖的櫃子，說鑰匙多年前已歸還辦公室。林安道謝，沒有要求他在未獲許可時開櫃。「櫃子不是武器，」她後來告訴美怡，「不能因為想知道答案就硬闖。」他們記下櫃號，正式向所有人申請查看。

Outside, a child stepped into a puddle and made a dramatic splash. The sound broke An's tension. Wei joked that if he listened long enough, every machine would seem to have its own song. But he also explained that sound alone could not identify a fault. He needed readings, dates, and the condition of the equipment. An promised to provide the pages that remained and not invent the missing ones. She had thought truth would arrive like a bright light. Instead it was coming slowly through damp paper, patient questions, and several people willing to say what they did not know.

屋外，一個孩子踩進水坑，濺出好大的水花，打散林安的緊張。鍾維開玩笑說，聽久了每台機器好像都有自己的歌。但他也解釋，光靠聲音不能確認故障，還需要讀數、日期和設備狀態。林安答應提供仍在的資料，不替缺頁編造內容。她以為真相會像一道亮光突然出現，沒想到它是透過潮濕紙張、耐心提問，以及願意承認不知道的人，慢慢靠近的。

The retired cleaner had once worked under a local manager who believed a loud voice could make people work faster. He said the manager's orders used to bore everyone, because they never explained the reason for a rule. An asked what had changed after the manager left. “We began talking,” he answered. “A workshop is not a democracy in every technical decision, but workers should be heard about the conditions they face.” He then remembered that the missing pages had been moved shortly before the roof was repaired. The memory gave An a useful date to check against the invoices. She thanked him and wrote it down without turning his statement into an accusation. By the time she closed the folder, the unknown history felt less like a shadow and more like a set of questions someone could investigate.

退休清潔員以前在一位相信大聲命令能讓人做快點的主管底下工作。他說那些命令總讓大家無聊，因為從不解釋規則的理由。林安問主管離開後發生什麼變化。「我們開始能說話。」他回答，「工坊不是每件技術決定都要投票的民主制度，但員工應能說出自己面對的工作條件。」他又想起，失蹤的文件是在修屋頂前不久被移走。這份回憶提供了可與發票交叉比對的日期。林安感謝他並記錄，沒有把他的話變成指控。闔上資料夾時，未知的歷史已不像陰影，更像一組能被查證的問題。

That night An received permission to examine the locked cabinet with the owner present. The door opened easily. Inside lay three pages wrapped in cloth, not hidden by a criminal but set aside during a roof repair. One page included a temperature graph that might change Wei's diagnosis. An photographed it and sent him a copy. He replied, “This is useful. It is not yet a complete answer.” An smiled at the familiar caution. The old studio was teaching her to trust careful work more than sudden discoveries.

當晚，林安獲准在所有人陪同下查看上鎖櫃子。門很容易就打開了。裡面有三張用布包好的紙，不是罪犯藏起來的，而是屋頂修理時被移開。其中一頁有溫度曲線圖，可能改變鍾維的判斷。林安拍照傳給他。他回覆：「這很有用，但還不是完整答案。」林安對這份熟悉的謹慎笑了。老工坊正教會她，比起突然出現的發現，更值得信任的是細緻的工作。
