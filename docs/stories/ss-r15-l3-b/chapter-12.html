The Contract on the Table

裂痕也能盛住光 · 第 12 / 25 章 · 2000–3000 · 英中對照 · 校訂中

用互動版查詞、朗讀與收藏

A client from a large design company arrived with a monthly order proposal. He put the contract aside while An showed him the unfinished story tiles. He praised the work but wanted her to edit out the names of makers and replace them with his company's mark. An said the tiles were not her property to sell without permission. “They represent people, not just patterns,” she explained. The client seemed surprised that she would refuse a profitable offer. An was nervous, but she had become capable of holding a boundary even while listening politely.

一位大型設計公司的客戶帶來每月訂單提案。他先把合約放在一旁，聽林安介紹未完成的故事陶片。他稱讚作品，卻希望刪掉創作者姓名，換上公司的標記。林安說這些陶片不是她能擅自出售的財產。「它們代表的是人，不只是圖案。」她解釋。客戶對她拒絕賺錢機會感到驚訝。林安雖然緊張，卻已能在禮貌聆聽的同時守住界線。

In the yard, an elderly sailor who had once served as a ship captain helped visitors make bowls. He described a canyon he had seen overseas and the way sunlight moved across its stone walls. A student shaped a tiny hatch into a model ship. Another made a sword for a fantasy story but agreed to keep the sharp edges soft. An felt hopeful seeing different lives meet around the same table. She did not need all the stories to sound alike. She needed their owners to know what would happen to the objects they made.

院子裡，一位曾任船長的年長水手協助訪客捏碗。他描述在海外見過的峽谷，以及陽光在石壁上移動的模樣。學生在模型船上捏出小艙口。另一人為奇幻故事做了一把劍，也同意把尖端處理圓滑。林安看見不同人生在同一張桌旁相遇，心裡充滿希望。她不需要所有故事都一模一樣，只希望創作者知道自己的作品接下來會被如何使用。

At lunchtime Wei checked an electricity cable that had produced a brief flash near the old wall. No one was hurt. An asked everyone to leave the room while the power was isolated. A little bubble formed in a bowl of wet clay and then disappeared. “That is not the same kind of warning,” Wei said, smiling at her anxious expression. They were learning to compare risks rather than call everything dangerous. An recorded the electrical problem and kept the repair notice visible until a qualified electrician finished the work.

午餐時，鍾維檢查一條在舊牆旁短暫閃光的電線。沒有人受傷，林安仍請大家先離開房間，等電源隔離。旁邊一只濕陶碗冒出小氣泡，隨即消失。「這不是同一種警訊。」鍾維看著她焦慮的表情笑說。他們開始學會比較風險，而不是把一切都叫作危險。林安記錄電路問題，並在合格電工完成維修前持續張貼告示。

Mei prepared information sheets about the contract. She asked the residents whose tiles might be used to meet without the company present. Some were interested in being paid; others wished to keep their work at home. One woman began to weep as she explained that her tile carried a family memory she did not want turned into advertising. An moved the business discussion to another room and asked whether the woman wanted to continue. She did not. Everyone respected the answer. A useful agreement, An thought, had to include the right not to agree.

美怡準備合約說明單，邀請作品可能被使用的居民在公司不在場時討論。有人想取得報酬，有人希望作品留在家中。一位女士說，陶片承載了不想被變成廣告的家庭記憶，說著便哭了。林安把商務討論移到別室，問她要不要繼續。她說不要，大家都尊重這個答案。林安想，一份有用的協議必須包含不同意的權利。

After the business client left, Mei suggested that An explain the situation in a letter to the cooperative. The client had offered a large order, but it depended on taking control of work made by people who had never agreed to sell it. An wrote down the benefits as well as the risks. She did not call the company evil or assume it knew the wishes of every maker. She simply described the proposed terms and why they could not be accepted. At dinner, Qiao added a little spice to the soup and asked whether a separate commercial collection might support free classes. An said that possibility deserved a fair discussion. If she developed new work under her own name, she could decide how to license it; she could not make that decision for someone else's memory. By the end of the evening, the team had questions to take to a new meeting, not an argument to repeat endlessly.

商務客戶離開後，美怡建議林安寫信向合作社說明情況。公司提出大型訂單，卻要求控制從未同意出售作品的人所製作的東西。林安同時記下好處與風險，沒有把公司說成邪惡，也沒有假設對方知道每位創作者的願望。她只清楚描述條件與無法接受的理由。晚餐時，巧巧在湯裡加了一點香料，問能否開發獨立商業系列來支持免費課。林安認為這值得公平討論。若她用自己的名字創作，就可以決定授權方式；但她不能替他人的記憶作決定。夜晚結束時，團隊帶著可供新會議討論的問題，而非一場無休止的爭吵。

That evening An wrote the company a reply. She offered to discuss a new line of her own designs, separate from the residents' tiles, after safety approval. She would not give away names, stories, or designs she did not own. The client might walk away; the possibility frightened her. Yet she felt calmer than she had at the start of the day. She had found a way to negotiate without pretending her workshop was bigger, richer, or more certain than it really was. She sent the message herself and closed the computer.

傍晚，林安寫信回覆公司。她願意在安全核准後，討論一系列由她獨立設計的新作品，並與居民陶片分開處理。她不會交出不屬於自己的姓名、故事或設計。客戶可能因此離開，這想法令她害怕；但她比早上平靜多了。她找到一種談判方式，不必假裝工坊比實際更大、更有錢或更確定。她親自寄出訊息，關上電腦。
