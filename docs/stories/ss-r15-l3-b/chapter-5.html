失真的訪談

The False Interview

裂痕也能盛住光 · 第 5 / 25 章 · 2000–3000 · 英中對照 · 校訂中

用互動版查詞、朗讀與收藏

An had prepared a small breakfast of bacon and ripe fruit before the first public interview. A young reporter arrived with a microphone and a camera crew. The studio smelled of steam from tea and warm clay. Behind the table, a faded carpet covered a patch of floor that still needed repair. An asked visitors to stay on the marked path. The reporter wanted a lively story about a brave artist saving an old business, but An wanted to discuss the responsibility that came with selling objects people would use every day.

第一次公開訪談前，林安準備了培根與熟透的水果當早餐。年輕記者帶著麥克風和攝影團隊到來。工坊瀰漫著茶的蒸氣和溫暖陶土的氣味。桌後一塊褪色地毯蓋著仍待修補的地面。林安請訪客走在標示好的動線上。記者想拍一個勇敢藝術家挽救老店的熱鬧故事，林安卻希望談談販售日用品必須承擔的責任。

In the garage Wei inspected a small motor that powered the ventilation fan. A flashlight revealed an old sack of clay beside a box of peanuts left from a festival. Someone had written “approved” on the fan's cover, but there was no test date. Wei worried that the word was fake. An felt her jaw tighten; she had already told the reporter she hoped to open soon. Still, she refused to complain about the delay until she understood it. They photographed the cover and switched the machine off.

鍾維在車庫檢查帶動通風扇的小馬達。手電筒照到一袋舊陶土，以及節慶留下的一箱花生。有人在風扇外殼寫著「已核准」，卻沒有測試日期。鍾維懷疑這是假的核准記號。林安咬緊下巴，因為她剛告訴記者希望早日開幕。但她決定先弄清楚原因，不急著抱怨。他們拍下外殼，關閉機器。

During the interview the reporter asked An to pronounce the word “engagement” for a video caption. An explained that public engagement meant listening before deciding, not merely collecting applause. The reporter seemed disappointed and asked about sorrow in her childhood. An answered that her aunt's death had brought grief, but she would not turn that loss into a performance for strangers. A cough interrupted her. She took a sip of water and asked to see the recorded questions before any further editing. The crew agreed, though their producer looked impatient.

訪談中，記者請林安為影片字幕唸出「公眾參與」這個英文詞。林安解釋，參與是做決定前先聆聽，不只是收集掌聲。記者看來有些失望，又問她童年有多悲傷。林安說阿姨去世確實帶來傷痛，但她不會把這份失去表演給陌生人看。她咳了一聲，喝口水後要求在進一步剪輯前先看錄下的提問。攝影團隊同意了，製作人卻顯得不耐煩。

The next day a short clip appeared online. It made An sound as if she had promised immediate sales, although she had clearly said testing came first. Mei showed her the full recording. The edit was misleading, not violent, but it could still hurt customers who trusted it. An sent the reporter a careful message with exact times and the full statement. She did not ask for an apology in private. She asked that the public correction be as easy to find as the original clip.

隔天，一支短片出現在網路上。影片讓林安聽起來像已承諾立刻出貨，儘管她明明說過要先完成檢測。美怡拿出完整錄影給她看。剪輯並不暴力，卻有誤導性，仍可能傷害信任影片的顧客。林安傳給記者一封仔細寫好的訊息，附上確切時間與完整發言。她沒有要求私下道歉，而是要求公開更正必須和原影片一樣容易被找到。

The reporter returned to check the correction request. An showed him a considerable difference between the first edited clip and the full conversation. The producer had selected a sentence about opening, then removed the condition about testing. An invited the reporter to engage with the complete evidence instead of making another dramatic cut. While they spoke, Qiao set a small bowl of peanut snacks on the table and offered water to everyone. No one pretended the mistake was harmless. The reporter explained how the short clip had been prepared and agreed to publish a correction with a link to the full interview. An asked him to keep the dates visible so readers could understand the sequence. She did not try to control his future reporting. She wanted the audience to have enough information to decide whether her workshop deserved their trust. She also gave the volunteer team a copy, so nobody would have to explain the correction from memory.

記者回來確認更正要求。林安展示首次剪輯與完整談話之間相當明顯的差異：製作人保留了她談開幕的一句話，卻剪掉必須先完成測試的條件。她邀請記者真正參與完整證據的討論，而不是再剪一段戲劇化片段。談話時，巧巧在桌上放了一小碗花生點心，也替大家倒水。沒有人假裝這個錯誤沒有影響。記者解釋短片的製作流程，同意發布更正，並連結完整訪談。林安請他保留日期，讓讀者理解事情先後。她不是要控制往後的報導，只希望觀眾有足夠資訊判斷工坊是否值得信任。

In the evening An read her revised business plan aloud. It listed every known problem, every promised repair, and the point at which she would request another inspection. Her voice was less exciting than a television story, but it belonged to her. Wei offered to review technical details; Mei offered to check whether residents could understand them. Neither tried to speak in her place. An kept the final words simple: “The studio will open when the work is safe, not when a camera says it is.”

晚上，林安大聲讀出修訂後的營運計畫。裡面列出所有已知問題、承諾完成的維修，以及下一次申請檢查的時點。她的聲音比不上電視故事刺激，卻真正屬於自己。鍾維願意核對技術細節，美怡則協助確認居民是否看得懂。沒有人試著代替她說話。林安最後用簡單的話總結：「工坊會在安全時開門，而不是在鏡頭說安全時開門。」
