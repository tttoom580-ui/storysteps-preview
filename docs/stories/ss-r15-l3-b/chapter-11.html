信任的刻度

A Measure of Trust

裂痕也能盛住光 · 第 11 / 25 章 · 2000–3000 · 英中對照 · 校訂中

The county inspector arrived with a notebook and a demand for accurate readings. Her name was Chen, and she wanted to inspect the kiln operation from beginning to end. She asked Wei to perform the safety checks while An described how she planned to use the equipment. Neither could answer for the other. Beneath a loose board near the back door, they found old plastic wrapping and a damaged gasoline can that had once been used for a generator. The can was empty, but it had no place beside a working kiln. An arranged for safe removal and recorded the decision.

縣府檢查員帶著筆記本到來，要求提供準確讀數。她姓陳，想從頭到尾了解窯爐的操作。她請鍾維執行安全檢查，林安則說明未來的使用計畫。兩人都不能代替對方回答。後門一片鬆動木板下有舊塑膠包材與破損的汽油罐，過去似乎用來供應發電機。罐子已空，卻不該留在窯旁。林安安排安全清除，並記錄決定。

Mei had placed a freezer in the meeting room to store ice for summer workshops. She offered drinks to the team, then listened as Chen explained the difference between a theory and a tested result. “A good theory can lead us to the right question,” the inspector said, “but we still need proof.” An remembered the strange heat pattern in the kiln. She had once wanted to dive into repairs without taking time to measure. Now she was beginning to understand that an intelligent solution often started by admitting exactly where knowledge ended.

美怡在會議室放了一台冷凍櫃，準備保存暑期課程的冰塊。她替大家倒飲料，聆聽陳檢查員解釋理論與已驗證結果的差別。「好理論能帶我們問對問題，」檢查員說，「但仍然需要證據。」林安想起窯內奇怪的溫度分布。她曾想不測量就一頭投入修理，如今開始懂得，聰明的解法往往始於承認知識的界線。

Outside, workers were repairing a ditch that kept rainwater away from the foundation. They moved wet timber with care, pausing when their muscle strength was not enough to lift a beam safely. An walked over to ask whether the repair would weaken the old wall. The foreman showed her a plan and explained the supports. It was roughly the same problem as pottery: a structure could look firm while carrying stress in the wrong place. An went back inside with a clearer understanding of why Wei refused to rely on appearances.

屋外工人正修理把雨水導離地基的水溝。他們小心搬動潮濕木材，當單靠肌肉力量不足以安全抬起梁柱時就停下來。林安走去問工程是否會削弱老牆。領班拿出圖面，解釋支撐方式。這和陶藝大致相同：結構看似穩固，內部壓力卻可能集中在錯誤位置。林安回到屋裡，更明白鍾維為何不肯只相信外觀。

A student carried in a violet tile and asked for a blessing before the inspection finished. An laughed softly. “You may bless it if that helps you,” she said, “but we will still check its strength.” Chen appreciated the answer. The inspector asked for information about each firing, including the temperature at the top and bottom shelves. An showed the records, then pointed out which readings were uncertain. For the first time she felt no need to hide a blank line. She could speak plainly about what was known and what still needed work.

一名學生捧著紫羅蘭色陶片來，問檢查結束前可不可以先祝福它。林安輕聲笑道：「妳可以祝福它，如果那讓妳安心，但我們還是會檢查強度。」陳檢查員欣賞這個回答。她要求每次燒製的資料，包括上下層架溫度。林安展示紀錄，也指出哪些讀數尚不確定。她第一次不再想遮掩空白欄位，能坦白說明已知與待查的事情。

During the inspection, Chen asked An to decide how the team would tell the public about its progress. An suggested a short notice in simple language, with a link to the full technical report. A local media worker offered to help make the report understandable without removing important warnings. Chen agreed that a medium chosen for communication should fit the audience, but the facts must remain the same. A volunteer wiped sweat from his forehead after carrying heavy boxes. An asked him to rest and reminded the group that tired people made mistakes, even when their intentions were good. She added a break schedule to the safety plan. Before the inspector left, she invited staff to question any instruction they found unclear. One worker asked why the generator can had to be removed if it was empty. Chen explained the remaining risk and showed the written disposal procedure. The question had improved the lesson for everyone.

檢查期間，陳檢查員問林安，團隊打算如何向公眾說明進度。林安建議用白話寫短公告，並附完整技術報告連結。一位地方媒體工作者願意協助讓內容更易理解，同時保留重要警告。陳檢查員同意，溝通媒介應適合讀者，但事實不能因媒介不同而改變。一名志工搬完重箱，擦掉額頭的汗。林安請他休息，提醒大家即使出於善意，疲勞也可能導致錯誤。她在安全計畫加入休息時段。檢查員離開前，林安請員工對不清楚的指示提問。有人問空汽油罐為何仍須移走，陳檢查員解釋剩餘風險，並展示書面處理程序。這個問題讓所有人學得更完整。

Chen issued a limited approval for continued tests, not permission to sell food containers. An read the document twice and asked her to explain one difficult sentence in ordinary language. The inspector did so without complaint. When she left, Wei offered to help plan the next firing. An thanked him, then asked for the schedule in writing because she would have to make her own commitments to customers. He smiled at her confidence. Their work together was becoming easier not because they always agreed, but because each knew where the other's responsibility began.

陳檢查員核准繼續進行有限測試，並沒有准許販售食品容器。林安讀了文件兩遍，請她用白話解釋一句難懂的文字。檢查員毫不介意地說明。她離開後，鍾維提出協助安排下一次試燒。林安道謝，並請他把時程寫下來，因為她也得對顧客作出承諾。他欣賞她的篤定。兩人合作愈來愈順，不是因為總能同意彼此，而是都清楚對方責任從哪裡開始。
