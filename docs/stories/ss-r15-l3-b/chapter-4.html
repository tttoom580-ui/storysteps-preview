The Red Label

裂痕也能盛住光 · 第 4 / 25 章 · 2000–3000 · 英中對照 · 校訂中

用互動版查詞、朗讀與收藏

A delivery van stopped in the narrow alley behind the studio. Its driver carried boxes of cherry-colored glaze, each with a bright red label. The supplier had promised a smooth, solid finish, but the label said the product was for decoration only. An felt dizzy when she realized the glaze had been listed for cups in her first catalog. She called Wei rather than pretending she knew the answer. “We need to investigate before we use a single jar,” she said. He photographed the boxes and asked her to keep the original receipt.

送貨廂型車停在工坊後方狹窄的小巷。司機搬下幾箱櫻桃紅釉料，每箱都有鮮紅標籤。供應商保證釉面光滑堅固，但標籤清楚寫著「僅供裝飾」。林安發現自己第一版商品目錄竟把它列為杯子用釉，頓時頭暈。她沒有裝懂，而是打電話給鍾維。「使用任何一罐之前，都得先調查。」她說。鍾維拍下箱子，請她保留原始收據。

An's neighbor, Mrs. Gao, was learning to knit and had made small covers for the studio's teapots. She offered a saucer to hold the labels while they worked. “A label is not a definition of every risk,” Wei explained. “It tells us where to begin checking.” A driver passing the gate heard their conversation and said a recent road crash had taught him not to ignore small warnings. An thanked him. She had once considered safety rules dull, but now she understood the bravery it could take to stop production when everyone expected quick sales.

鄰居高太太正在學編織，替工坊茶壺做了幾個小套子。她拿來一只茶碟，讓大家整理標籤。「標籤並沒有定義所有風險，」鍾維解釋，「它只告訴我們從哪裡開始查。」路過大門的司機聽見談話，說最近一次交通事故讓他學會不要忽略小警訊。林安向他道謝。她從前覺得安全規定乏味，如今才懂，在人人盼著快點出貨時停下生產，也可能需要勇氣。

A tired volunteer asked for a short nap while An checked the storage room. She found a cracked wall near the old tower-shaped chimney and recorded its position. Mei brought warm tea, and the room grew quiet with ordinary warmth rather than excitement. A child had drawn a mystery story on a tile in which someone tried to solve a murder. An explained that such a story could be displayed as fiction, but a real accident report required evidence. The distinction mattered: a dramatic title should never replace an honest account of what had happened.

一名疲倦的志工想先小睡片刻，林安趁機檢查儲藏室。她在塔形舊煙囪附近發現牆面裂縫，記下位置。美怡送來熱茶，房裡多了一種日常的溫暖，而非緊張的興奮。某個孩子在陶片上畫了謀殺推理故事。林安解釋，這種故事可以標示為虛構作品展示；真正的事故報告卻必須有證據。兩者不能混淆：吸引人的標題不該取代誠實的事件紀錄。

At the craft market, a seller called one of An's vases sexy and suggested using that word in an advertisement. An smiled awkwardly; she preferred to describe its shape and color. Another seller complained that safety testing would starve small makers of income. An understood the fear, but said customers had a right to know what touched their food. The market manager agreed to let her display a warning beside her unfinished cups. An was thankful for the chance, although she knew that transparency might bring fewer sales at first.

在手作市集，一位攤商說林安的花瓶很性感，建議拿這個詞做廣告。林安尷尬一笑；她更想介紹形狀與顏色。另一名攤商抱怨，安全檢測會讓小創作者餓肚子。林安了解這份擔憂，卻仍認為顧客有權知道哪些材料碰觸食物。市集管理員同意讓她在未完成的杯子旁放上警告。林安心存感謝，即使她知道透明說明起初可能減少銷量。

An also noticed an electrical cable running behind the shelf of glaze jars. The cable had been squeezed by heavy boxes, and its outer cover showed a small split. Wei asked her to leave the area untouched until an electrician could examine it. An wrote the finding on the same sheet as the red-glaze warning, but she made separate entries for each problem. One concerned material use; the other concerned electrical safety. A customer later asked whether the studio was about to close forever. An answered that no such decision had been made. She described what was closed now, who was checking it, and how people could receive updates. The customer thanked her for speaking plainly. An had feared that admitting trouble would end public support. Instead she was discovering that careful truth could be more reassuring than a bright sign with no explanation.

林安還注意到，釉料架後面有一條電纜。沉重箱子壓住電線，外皮出現一道小裂口。鍾維請她不要再碰，等電工檢查。林安把發現記在紅釉警告同一張表上，但兩項問題分開列示：一項關乎材料用途，另一項關乎用電安全。後來有顧客問，工坊是不是要永遠關閉。林安說尚未作出這種決定，並清楚說明目前哪些區域關閉、由誰檢查，以及怎樣取得更新。顧客感謝她說得明白。林安曾怕承認問題會失去支持，如今卻發現，謹慎的真話可能比沒有解釋的明亮招牌更令人安心。

That night An moved the red glaze into a clearly marked box and closed the door to the kiln room. She told her first customer why the order would be late and offered a full refund. The woman surprised her by asking to visit the studio when the tests were finished. “I would rather wait for a cup I can trust,” she said. An had not earned much money that day, but she had prevented a mistake from becoming a larger one. She placed the receipt beside the inspection form, two ordinary papers that now meant more than a beautiful advertisement.

當晚，林安把紅釉移進標示清楚的箱子，關上窯房。她告訴第一位顧客訂單為何延遲，並提出全額退款。顧客卻意外地說，等測試完成後想來參觀。「我寧可等一只可以信任的杯子。」她說。林安那天沒賺多少錢，卻阻止小錯變成大錯。她把收據與檢查表放在一起。這兩張平凡的紙，如今比漂亮的廣告更重要。
