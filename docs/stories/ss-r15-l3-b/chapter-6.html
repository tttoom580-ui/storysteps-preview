溫度筆記本

The Temperature Notebook

裂痕也能盛住光 · 第 6 / 25 章 · 2000–3000 · 英中對照 · 校訂中

用互動版查詞、朗讀與收藏

Wei opened the kiln's inner panel while An stood beyond the safety line. A row of old numbers had been covered with wax, as if someone had wanted to hide earlier readings. “We must not assume it was done to trick us,” he said. “It may have been someone's careless technique.” An was thankful for the distinction. The studio's creator, her aunt, had cared about excellence, but excellence was not a magic resource. It required maintenance, trained people, and records that could be understood after the person who wrote them was gone.

鍾維打開窯爐的內側面板，林安站在安全線外。舊讀數上覆著一層蠟，彷彿有人刻意遮住先前的紀錄。「先別假設有人想騙我們，」他說，「也可能只是某種粗心的做法。」林安很感激他願意區分。阿姨是這間工坊的創辦人，向來追求卓越，但卓越不是憑空就有的資源；它需要維護、受訓的人，以及在記錄者離開後仍看得懂的文件。

On the workbench sat a stale sandwich and an old notebook containing a surprising summary of kiln repairs. The writer had compared heat traveling across the oven to a small missile, a dangerous image that made An uneasy. Wei explained that the comparison described speed, not a real weapon. He showed her how several small errors could multiply into one serious problem. The necessity of testing was no longer a dull line in a rulebook. It was the difference between a crack that could be studied and a cup that might break in a customer's hand.

工作桌上放著一份不新鮮的三明治，以及一本舊維修筆記。筆記竟把窯內傳遞的熱比喻為小型飛彈，讓林安不太舒服。鍾維解釋，這只是形容速度，並不是真的武器。他示範好幾個小錯誤如何相乘，最後變成大問題。測試的必要性不再是規章裡無聊的一行字，而是能被研究的裂痕與可能在顧客手中破裂的杯子之間的差別。

Mei arrived with a teenage volunteer who had found photographs of the studio in its early years. One showed An's aunt teaching visitors how to shape tropical birds from clay. Another showed a local politician praising the studio while ignoring a broken window. The volunteer asked if the photographs proved the official had lied. “No,” An answered. “They show what was visible on that day. We need more records before naming a suspect.” Mei smiled. An was becoming careful not only with clay but also with what she claimed about other people.

美怡帶著一名青少年志工到來，他找到工坊早年的照片。一張拍著阿姨教訪客捏熱帶鳥類，另一張拍著地方政治人物在破窗旁讚美工坊。志工問照片是否證明官員說謊。「還不能，」林安回答，「照片只能呈現當天看得見的事。要把誰列為嫌疑人，還需要更多紀錄。」美怡笑了。林安如今不只對陶土謹慎，對於談論別人時所下的結論也更小心。

During a break, An felt a bump in the road as a delivery truck passed, and the studio shelves shook again. Her stomach tightened. Wei suggested checking the wall supports as well as the kiln. “Anyhow,” he added, “a workshop is more than one machine.” They traced a crack from a shelf toward the floor and marked it for an engineer. A jar of old clay carried a warning about bacteria, reminding An that stored materials had their own risks. She did not throw everything away; she separated, labeled, and documented what needed further inspection.

休息時，送貨卡車經過路面的凸起處，工坊架子又晃動了，林安胃部一緊。鍾維建議除了窯爐，也要檢查牆面支架。「總之，」他補充，「工坊不只有一台機器。」他們追蹤一條從架子延伸至地面的裂痕，標記後請結構工程師查看。一罐舊陶土上還有細菌警告，提醒林安保存材料同樣有風險。她沒有全部丟掉，而是分類、標記，記下待查項目。

An reviewed the kiln history with a retired technician who had helped install the machine. He remembered replacing one sensor after an earlier workshop complained about uneven heat. The paperwork had been moved during the building's closure, and nobody could say whether the replacement had been tested again. An wrote down his account as a memory, not as proof of the repair. The technician showed her how the sensor should have been recorded, with a date, location, name, and measured result. He said the missing information did not make him proud, but it could still help the new team ask the right questions. When he left, An placed a copy of the blank test form on the noticeboard. She wanted staff to see what a complete record looked like, and why an attractive piece of pottery could not take the place of one.

林安與一位參與安裝舊窯的退休技師重新查看設備歷史。技師記得曾有工坊抱怨熱度不均，後來更換過一顆感測器。建築停用時文件被移走，沒人能確定更換後是否重測。林安把他的說法記為回憶，而非已證實的維修結果。技師示範正確紀錄應包括日期、位置、姓名與測量結果。他說缺失資訊並不光彩，但仍可幫新團隊問對問題。離開後，林安把空白測試表影本貼到公告板，讓員工看見完整紀錄的模樣，知道再漂亮的陶器也不能取代它。

Before closing, An copied the numbers from the notebook and placed a clean sheet beside the old one. Both would remain in the archive. Wei insisted that an uncertain reading be marked uncertain. An disliked the empty spaces at first; she wanted a complete story and an opening date. But an honest blank could be more useful than a confident invention. She wrote a new rule for the staff: “If we cannot explain a reading, we stop and ask.” Then she locked the kiln room and sent the council an updated schedule without pretending the delay had disappeared.

關門前，林安把舊筆記的數字抄在乾淨的紙上，並將新舊兩份一同保存。鍾維堅持無法確定的讀數就應標示為不確定。林安起初不喜歡那些空白；她想要完整的故事與明確開幕日期。然而，誠實的空格也許比自信編造的答案更有價值。她為員工寫下一條新規則：「無法解釋讀數時，就停下來問。」然後鎖上窯房，把更新的時程寄給議會，不假裝延誤已消失。
