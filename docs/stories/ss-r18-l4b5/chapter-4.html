It was a pause in an otherwise predictable routine, long enough for a careful person to notice. Others kept moving, and their movement made it tempting to do the same, but the pause deserved an explanation. Yiqing checked whether her microphone was really off before answering. The script contained private arguments quoted from their earlier counseling sessions. They compared the new account with what could be measured independently. It was an unglamorous exercise, made of repeated readings and questions that did not impress an audience. The result mattered precisely because it might prove the people asking those questions were wrong. The six empty dinner plates looked more honest than the rehearsed smiles. Ruohe disliked the word essential in the summary because it hid a decision someone had made. A witness called the result experimental; Yiqing wrote the adjective beside the witness’s name.

劇本竟引用兩人先前諮商中的私人爭執。他們把新說法與能獨立測量的事情比較。這不光鮮，只是重複讀值與不會取悅觀眾的提問。結果重要，正因為它也可能證明提問者自己錯了。六個空餐盤比排練過的笑容還誠實。舊未剪輯錄影出現了「外交官」，若禾追問新版為何漏掉。沒人質疑「草案」，讓若禾困擾的是它的來歷沒有人提。若禾不喜歡摘要中的「必要的」，因為它掩藏了某個人的決定。見證人稱結果「實驗性的」，以晴把形容詞記在見證人姓名旁。

Ruohe crossed out the quotes and chose to discuss the practical reason for their separation. They argued quietly because shouting would only give the nearby cameras and strangers another story to tell. The crew had faces and exhausted feet, not just job titles in the credits. It might help to flee from the unsafe corridor, but Ruohe refused to mistake possibility for proof. The team considered the hesitation a separate detail, not proof that a forced public reveal was safe.

若禾劃掉引言，選擇談分居的實際原因。他們壓低聲音爭辯，因為大喊只會讓旁邊的鏡頭與陌生人又多一個故事。爭論底下是實際問題：自信的人若弄錯，誰承擔後果？沒有人願意代替別人回答。工作人員有臉孔與疲憊的腳，不只是片尾名單上的職稱。「逃離」或許有幫助，若禾卻拒絕把可能當成證明。舊未剪輯錄影出現了「病菌」，若禾追問新版為何漏掉。沒人質疑「問候」，讓若禾困擾的是它的來歷沒有人提。團隊把「猶豫」視為獨立細節，不當成強制公開感情安全的證據。

Chengan admitted he had believed the producer's promise to help them reconcile. The first result was neither victory nor defeat. It would have been tempting to call the day a success and leave. But one person could not yet go home, and another still carried a cost nobody had agreed to repay. The group sat down again, this time without pretending that good intentions guaranteed a good outcome. Ruohe glanced toward Yiqing and remembered the principle they had been arguing about: love is not a performance prize. It sounded simple until it demanded another hour of work. The camera light went red just as Ruohe wanted to say something in private. Nobody disputed the manual; what troubled Ruohe was the silence around its origin. 把今天稱為成功就離開，很誘人。但還有人不能回家，也有人承擔著無人答應補償的代價。大家再次坐下，這次不假裝好意必然帶來好結果。若禾望向以晴，想起兩人不斷爭論的原則：愛情不是表演的獎品。話雖簡單，真正實踐又需要多一小時工作。若禾剛想私下說話，攝影機紅燈就亮了。沒人質疑「操作手冊」，讓若禾困擾的是它的來歷沒有人提。團隊把「機械技師」視為獨立細節，不當成強制公開感情安全的證據。見證人稱結果「傑出的」，以晴把形容詞記在見證人姓名旁。

There would be easier stories to tell about this day. In those stories a brave person would find one clue, name one enemy and fix everything at once. The truth they carried was more difficult: a choice could be necessary and still leave work for the morning. Ruohe knew the next day would not begin from a clean slate. 關於今天，可以講得更簡單：勇敢的人找到一個線索，指出一個敵人，一切立刻解決。他們帶走的真相更難：一個選擇可以是必要的，也仍會留下明早要處理的工作。若禾知道明天不會從零開始。未剪輯錄影將保存又一個問題，以晴也會在場質疑第一個容易的答案。若禾希望海風能吹走製作人的下一個指令。他們在受損未剪輯錄影旁找到提及「休閒活動」的紀錄，日期卻看不清。舊未剪輯錄影出現了「衛星」，若禾追問新版為何漏掉。在能夠「加強」之前，團隊需要一位能自由提出異議的見證人。
