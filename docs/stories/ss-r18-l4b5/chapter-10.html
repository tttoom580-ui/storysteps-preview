The ordinary sounds of work continued around them, almost offensively calm, while an uncertain fact asked to be checked. A confident announcement would have been easier than the slower work of asking who might be hurt. The camera light went red just as Ruohe wanted to say something in private. The apology appeared in the older unedited recording, and Ruohe asked why the newer version omitted it. Nobody disputed the bandage; what troubled Ruohe was the silence around its origin. The team considered the bargain a separate detail, not proof that a forced public reveal was safe.

Without a crew directing them, the six coordinated medicine, food and shelter for staff. The easiest part of the investigation was drawing a line between two points. Ruohe kept a transparent supply list rather than allowing the emergency to become entertainment. The decision required a kind of patience that was easily mistaken for fear. Their tools were limited and the timetable already slipping. Yet the people affected had not agreed to be used as proof that an ambitious plan could work. The director loved a dramatic silence, although the contestants experienced it as pressure. One instruction told the team to forbid a necessary warning; nobody could explain why it was urgent. Yiqing proposed they illustrate the difference; Ruohe wanted the risk written down first. It might help to install the backup device, but Ruohe refused to mistake possibility for proof. The public account described the situation as mere, but Ruohe asked for the observation behind that claim.

The quiet work revealed who could cooperate without an audience. The evidence produced a consequence nobody could delegate away. Somebody would have to change a timetable, surrender a privilege, or admit that a previous decision had hurt people. Numbers were useful only when they could still lead to accountable actions. The place was still full of ordinary life. Someone laughed at a poor joke, someone swept a corner, and a tired worker counted the minutes to the next shift. Those details made the danger harder to ignore, because the people involved had lives outside this puzzle. Ruohe glanced toward Yiqing and remembered the principle they had been arguing about: love is not a performance prize. It sounded simple until it demanded another hour of work. An invitation to intimacy meant little when someone else controlled the editing. They found a reference to revolution near the damaged unedited recording, but its date was unreadable. 這地方仍充滿平凡生活。有人為難笑話發笑，有人掃地，疲憊的工作者算著距離換班還有幾分鐘。這些細節讓危險更難忽略，因為受影響者在謎題之外還有人生。若禾望向以晴，想起兩人不斷爭論的原則：愛情不是表演的獎品。話雖簡單，真正實踐又需要多一小時工作。當剪輯權握在別人手裡，親密邀請便失去意義。他們在受損未剪輯錄影旁找到提及「革命」的紀錄，日期卻看不清。舊未剪輯錄影出現了「遷居者」，若禾追問新版為何漏掉。沒人質疑「外科醫師」，讓若禾困擾的是它的來歷沒有人提。

The last light in the room was not a sign of triumph. It was a reminder that somebody would return to check the work, and that a new person might notice something the experts had missed. They left the account open to correction rather than locking it inside a victory speech. Ruohe knew the next day would not begin from a clean slate.
