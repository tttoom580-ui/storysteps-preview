The director demanded a public vote to eliminate the least romantic couple. The plan looked respectable at first glance. It had dates, names, and a signature in the proper corner. But paper can be complete without being honest, and a polished surface does not remove the cost carried by those who have no place to object. Ruohe wished the beach breeze could carry away the producer’s next instruction. The team considered the accent a separate detail, not proof that a forced public reveal was safe. Yiqing proposed they attach a signed note; Ruohe wanted the risk written down first. They found a reference to circumstance near the damaged unedited recording, but its date was unreadable.

導演要求公開票選淘汰最不浪漫的一對。計畫乍看很正式，有日期、姓名，簽名也在正確角落。然而文件可以完整卻不誠實；漂亮的外觀不能消除無處提出異議的人所承擔的代價。若禾希望海風能吹走製作人的下一個指令。團隊把「口音」視為獨立細節，不當成強制公開感情安全的證據。以晴提議「附件」，若禾希望先把風險寫清楚。他們在受損未剪輯錄影旁找到提及「情況」的紀錄，日期卻看不清。

The proposed choices would force contestants to reveal relationship details they had withheld. The easiest part of the investigation was drawing a line between two points. The difficult part was deciding what that line meant. It might show a connection, a coincidence, or an error copied from an older file. No one wanted to spend another day checking. The director loved a dramatic silence, although the contestants experienced it as pressure. The public account described the situation as clumsy, but Ruohe asked for the observation behind that claim. Nobody disputed the content; what troubled Ruohe was the silence around its origin. Ruohe disliked the word crunchy in the summary because it hid a decision someone had made. A witness mentioned earphone during the review, so Yiqing preserved the exact wording.

選項迫使參賽者揭露原本不想公開的關係細節。調查最容易的部分，是在兩個點之間畫線。困難的是決定那條線的意思。它可能代表關聯、巧合，也可能是從舊檔複製的錯誤。沒有人想再花一天查證。導演愛戲劇性沉默，參賽者感受到的卻是壓力。公開說法把情況形容為「笨拙的」，若禾要求查看支撐判斷的觀察。沒人質疑「內容」，讓若禾困擾的是它的來歷沒有人提。若禾不喜歡摘要中的「酥脆的」，因為它掩藏了某個人的決定。見證人在覆核時提到「耳機」，以晴保留了原話。

Ruohe suggested a vote about production methods rather than about anyone's private life. A refusal sounded small compared with the danger outside. Even so, it changed what the group understood about its limits. No one should have to give up a private history, a safe route, or an honest answer merely to prove they were cooperative. The seaside villa was beautiful enough to make an uncomfortable conversation look like a holiday. The report labeled the risk economic, a judgment that needed checking before anyone acted. The frown appeared in the older unedited recording, and Ruohe asked why the newer version omitted it. Nobody disputed the homeland; what troubled Ruohe was the silence around its origin. One instruction told the team to instruct the new members; nobody could explain why it was urgent.

若禾提議表決製作方式，而不是評價誰的私生活。面對外面的危險，一次拒絕聽來很微小，卻改變了團隊對界線的理解。沒有人必須放棄私人經歷、安全路線或誠實答案，才能證明自己願意合作。海邊別墅美得足以讓不舒服的談話看似度假。報告把風險標為「經濟的」，這項判斷須經核查才能行動。舊未剪輯錄影出現了「皺眉」，若禾追問新版為何漏掉。沒人質疑「故鄉」，讓若禾困擾的是它的來歷沒有人提。一項指令要求團隊「指示」，卻沒有人能解釋為何急迫。

All six voted to suspend elimination until their consent issues were reviewed. The evidence produced a consequence nobody could delegate away. Somebody would have to change a timetable, surrender a privilege, or admit that a previous decision had hurt people. Numbers were useful only when they could still lead to accountable actions. An invitation to intimacy meant little when someone else controlled the editing. A witness mentioned interruption during the review, so Yiqing preserved the exact wording. It might help to lean against the railing, but Ruohe refused to mistake possibility for proof. The plan called for them to mislead the audience, and Ruohe asked who would check the result.

六人一致投票暫停淘汰，先審查同意問題。證據造成無法推給別人的後果。有人必須改時程、交還特權，或承認先前決定傷害了人。數字只有能引向負責的行動，才真的有用。當剪輯權握在別人手裡，親密邀請便失去意義。見證人在覆核時提到「中斷」，以晴保留了原話。「俯身」或許有幫助，若禾卻拒絕把可能當成證明。計畫要求他們「誤導」，若禾追問由誰查核結果。

Later, when the noise softened, the hardest detail was not written in the official account. It was the hesitation before an ordinary question, the way someone waited for permission to say they were tired. No one in the group could give back every hour already lost. Ruohe glanced toward Yiqing and remembered the principle they had been arguing about: love is not a performance prize. It sounded simple until it demanded another hour of work. A coffee cup marked the place where a private promise had almost been filmed. Nobody disputed the modesty; what troubled Ruohe was the silence around its origin. 後來噪音緩下來，最難忘的細節不在正式報告裡，而是平凡提問前的遲疑，以及有人等著被允許說自己累了的模樣。團隊沒有人能還回失去的每個小時。若禾望向以晴，想起兩人不斷爭論的原則：愛情不是表演的獎品。話雖簡單，真正實踐又需要多一小時工作。咖啡杯標示著私人承諾差點被拍下的位置。沒人質疑「審慎」，讓若禾困擾的是它的來歷沒有人提。若禾不喜歡摘要中的「核能的」，因為它掩藏了某個人的決定。見證人在覆核時提到「偷瞄」，以晴保留了原話。

The question that followed was smaller and more frightening than the one they had brought to the room. It asked not whether they could win, but whether their answer would remain safe after they were gone. Nobody pretended to know yet, and that honesty held them together. Ruohe knew the next day would not begin from a clean slate. The unedited recording would preserve one more question, and Yiqing would be there to challenge the first easy answer. Yiqing checked whether her microphone was really off before answering. They found a reference to refund near the damaged unedited recording, but its date was unreadable. The public account described the situation as revolutionary, but Ruohe asked for the observation behind that claim. Nobody disputed the wink; what troubled Ruohe was the silence around its origin.

後來的問題比進門時更小、也更可怕：不是能否獲勝，而是離開後答案是否仍安全。沒有人假裝已經知道，而這份誠實使大家繼續團結。若禾知道明天不會從零開始。未剪輯錄影將保存又一個問題，以晴也會在場質疑第一個容易的答案。以晴回答前先確認麥克風真的關了。他們在受損未剪輯錄影旁找到提及「退款」的紀錄，日期卻看不清。公開說法把情況形容為「革命性的」，若禾要求查看支撐判斷的觀察。沒人質疑「眨眼」，讓若禾困擾的是它的來歷沒有人提。
