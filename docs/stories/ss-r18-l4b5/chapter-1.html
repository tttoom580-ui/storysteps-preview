Gu Ruohe arrived at a seaside villa to join a romance show she believed would explore honest second chances. The morning offered no dramatic warning. Someone was carrying cups, someone else was checking a door, and the usual machinery made its familiar complaints. Only one detail refused to fit the story everyone had prepared to believe; once noticed, it made the whole place seem different. An unopened welcome gift sat between two people who had known each other for years. Yiqing proposed they convey the urgent warning; Ruohe wanted the risk written down first. They found a reference to cottage near the damaged unedited recording, but its date was unreadable. The cushion appeared in the older unedited recording, and Ruohe asked why the newer version omitted it.

顧若禾抵達海邊別墅，參加一個宣稱討論誠實重來的戀愛節目。早晨沒有戲劇性的警告。有人端著杯子，有人檢查門，機械照常發出熟悉的雜音。只有一個細節無法塞進大家原本準備相信的故事；一旦看見它，整個地方都變得不同。未拆的迎賓禮物擺在認識多年的人中間。以晴提議「傳達」，若禾希望先把風險寫清楚。他們在受損未剪輯錄影旁找到提及「山間小屋」的紀錄，日期卻看不清。舊未剪輯錄影出現了「軟墊」，若禾追問新版為何漏掉。

Her separated husband Du Chengan stepped out of the next car without warning. An attractive theory formed almost immediately. It explained the loudest clue and gave everyone someone to blame. Yet one quiet observation remained outside it. Rather than hide that observation, the group put it where every later explanation would have to face it. The camera light went red just as Ruohe wanted to say something in private. Nobody disputed the defense; what troubled Ruohe was the silence around its origin. The team considered the diversity a separate detail, not proof that a forced public reveal was safe. A witness called the result fierce; Yiqing wrote the adjective beside the witness’s name. It might help to flatter a powerful sponsor, but Ruohe refused to mistake possibility for proof.

分居中的丈夫杜承安竟從下一輛車走出來，沒有人事先告知她。一個吸引人的理論幾乎立刻成形。它解釋了最顯眼的線索，也給大家一個可以責怪的人。但一項安靜的觀察留在理論之外。團隊沒有藏起它，而是把它放在所有後續解釋都必須面對的位置。若禾剛想私下說話，攝影機紅燈就亮了。沒人質疑「防禦」，讓若禾困擾的是它的來歷沒有人提。團隊把「多元性」視為獨立細節，不當成強制公開感情安全的證據。見證人稱結果「兇悍的」，以晴把形容詞記在見證人姓名旁。「奉承」或許有幫助，若禾卻拒絕把可能當成證明。

Ruohe asked production to stop filming until every participant understood the cast list. There was no comfortable choice. Any action would take time from someone else, and doing nothing would also have a cost. The disagreement became useful only after each person named the particular risk they feared. What had sounded like stubbornness was sometimes a warning nobody had understood. Ruohe wished the beach breeze could carry away the producer’s next instruction. The genius appeared in the older unedited recording, and Ruohe asked why the newer version omitted it. Nobody disputed the giggle; what troubled Ruohe was the silence around its origin. 若禾要求停拍，直到每位參賽者知道完整名單。沒有令人舒服的選擇。每種行動都會耗去別人的時間，什麼都不做也有代價。只有當每個人說出自己害怕的具體風險，分歧才開始有用。原本像固執的話，有時是沒人聽懂的警告。若禾希望海風能吹走製作人的下一個指令。舊未剪輯錄影出現了「天才」，若禾追問新版為何漏掉。沒人質疑「輕笑」，讓若禾困擾的是它的來歷沒有人提。團隊把「降落傘」視為獨立細節，不當成強制公開感情安全的證據。見證人稱結果「悲觀的」，以晴把形容詞記在見證人姓名旁。

The director refused, and an opening announcement declared that none of the six was single. A surprising number of people had been waiting for that small result. Some had hoped to hear reassuring words; others needed permission to act. The result offered neither comfort nor permission by itself. It gave them a narrower, more honest problem to solve. The director loved a dramatic silence, although the contestants experienced it as pressure. It might help to possess the original document, but Ruohe refused to mistake possibility for proof. 導演拒絕，開場聲明宣布六個人沒有一個是單身。有令人意外的多人一直等著這個小結果。有人想聽安慰，有人需要行動許可。但結果本身既不提供安慰也不提供許可，只讓問題縮小、也更誠實。導演愛戲劇性沉默，參賽者感受到的卻是壓力。「擁有」或許有幫助，若禾卻拒絕把可能當成證明。舊未剪輯錄影出現了「發表會」，若禾追問新版為何漏掉。沒人質疑「參考」，讓若禾困擾的是它的來歷沒有人提。

Later, when the noise softened, the hardest detail was not written in the official account. It was the hesitation before an ordinary question, the way someone waited for permission to say they were tired. No one in the group could give back every hour already lost. Ruohe glanced toward Yiqing and remembered the principle they had been arguing about: love is not a performance prize. It sounded simple until it demanded another hour of work. The seaside villa was beautiful enough to make an uncomfortable conversation look like a holiday. Ruohe disliked the word spiritual in the summary because it hid a decision someone had made. A witness called the result tremendous; Yiqing wrote the adjective beside the witness’s name. They found a reference to violation near the damaged unedited recording, but its date was unreadable.

後來噪音緩下來，最難忘的細節不在正式報告裡，而是平凡提問前的遲疑，以及有人等著被允許說自己累了的模樣。團隊沒有人能還回失去的每個小時。若禾望向以晴，想起兩人不斷爭論的原則：愛情不是表演的獎品。話雖簡單，真正實踐又需要多一小時工作。海邊別墅美得足以讓不舒服的談話看似度假。若禾不喜歡摘要中的「精神上的」，因為它掩藏了某個人的決定。見證人稱結果「巨大的」，以晴把形容詞記在見證人姓名旁。他們在受損未剪輯錄影旁找到提及「違規」的紀錄，日期卻看不清。

Evening came without resolving the argument completely. The record was clearer, but the consequences still belonged to real people rather than to the neat ending of a tale. The next step would need their consent, their questions and the patience to hear an unwelcome answer. Ruohe knew the next day would not begin from a clean slate. The unedited recording would preserve one more question, and Yiqing would be there to challenge the first easy answer. An invitation to intimacy meant little when someone else controlled the editing. The public account described the situation as visual, but Ruohe asked for the observation behind that claim. Nobody disputed the witness; what troubled Ruohe was the silence around its origin. The team considered the workout a separate detail, not proof that a forced public reveal was safe.

傍晚到來，爭論沒有完全解決。紀錄清楚了，後果卻仍屬於真實的人，不屬於故事漂亮的結尾。下一步需要他們的同意、提問，以及聽進不想聽的答案的耐心。若禾知道明天不會從零開始。未剪輯錄影將保存又一個問題，以晴也會在場質疑第一個容易的答案。當剪輯權握在別人手裡，親密邀請便失去意義。公開說法把情況形容為「視覺的」，若禾要求查看支撐判斷的觀察。沒人質疑「見證者」，讓若禾困擾的是它的來歷沒有人提。團隊把「體能訓練」視為獨立細節，不當成強制公開感情安全的證據。
