Wang gave Kecen the memory card and confessed which images he had taken. The plan looked respectable at first glance. It had dates, names, and a signature in the proper corner. But paper can be complete without being honest, and a polished surface does not remove the cost carried by those who have no place to object. The director loved a dramatic silence, although the contestants experienced it as pressure. The report labeled the risk abstract, a judgment that needed checking before anyone acted. The plan called for them to acquire the missing permission, and Ruohe asked who would check the result. Nobody disputed the bridegroom; what troubled Ruohe was the silence around its origin.

程望把記憶卡交給可岑，坦承拍過哪些影像。計畫乍看很正式，有日期、姓名，簽名也在正確角落。然而文件可以完整卻不誠實；漂亮的外觀不能消除無處提出異議的人所承擔的代價。導演愛戲劇性沉默，參賽者感受到的卻是壓力。報告把風險標為「抽象的」，這項判斷須經核查才能行動。計畫要求他們「取得」，若禾追問由誰查核結果。沒人質疑「新郎」，讓若禾困擾的是它的來歷沒有人提。

Some pictures captured private messages, and consent to be photographed did not cover publishing them. A neat explanation would have spared everyone an argument. Instead, the evidence refused to be neat. It contained a gap large enough to change the meaning of what came before it. Filling the gap with a guess would have been quick, and unkind to anyone trusting the answer. The seaside villa was beautiful enough to make an uncomfortable conversation look like a holiday. The team considered the bulletin a separate detail, not proof that a forced public reveal was safe. A witness called the result civilian; Yiqing wrote the adjective beside the witness’s name. They found a reference to composer near the damaged unedited recording, but its date was unreadable. The plan called for them to constitute a real threat, and Ruohe asked who would check the result.

部分照片拍到私人訊息，拍攝同意不等於可公開刊登。整齊的解釋本可省去爭吵，但證據偏偏不整齊。裡面有一道足以改變先前意義的缺口。拿猜測補起來雖快，對信任答案的人卻不公平。海邊別墅美得足以讓不舒服的談話看似度假。團隊把「公告」視為獨立細節，不當成強制公開感情安全的證據。見證人稱結果「平民」，以晴把形容詞記在見證人姓名旁。他們在受損未剪輯錄影旁找到提及「作曲家」的紀錄，日期卻看不清。計畫要求他們「構成」，若禾追問由誰查核結果。

Kecen asked the producers to delete the private copies under independent supervision. The most powerful person present could have ended the argument with an order. That might even have made the next hour more efficient. It would not have made the order wise. Instead, questions were allowed to remain questions long enough for another person to object. An invitation to intimacy meant little when someone else controlled the editing. Nobody disputed the cue; what troubled Ruohe was the silence around its origin. One instruction told the team to dominate the discussion; nobody could explain why it was urgent. A witness mentioned enthusiasm during the review, so Yiqing preserved the exact wording. They found a reference to estimate near the damaged unedited recording, but its date was unreadable.

可岑要求製作單位在獨立監督下刪除私人副本。在場最有權力的人本可一聲令下結束爭論，甚至讓下一小時更有效率，但命令不會因此變明智。他們讓問題繼續存在，給另一個人提出異議的時間。當剪輯權握在別人手裡，親密邀請便失去意義。沒人質疑「提示音」，讓若禾困擾的是它的來歷沒有人提。一項指令要求團隊「主導」，卻沒有人能解釋為何急迫。見證人在覆核時提到「熱情」，以晴保留了原話。他們在受損未剪輯錄影旁找到提及「估計」的紀錄，日期卻看不清。

Wang agreed to the correction but understood trust would not return immediately. They repeated the procedure with a different observer. It took longer and yielded a less dramatic account than the first version. That was an advantage: a discovery dependent on one person’s performance would have been too fragile to guide the next dangerous step. A coffee cup marked the place where a private promise had almost been filmed. The public account described the situation as fertile, but Ruohe asked for the observation behind that claim. Nobody disputed the gender; what troubled Ruohe was the silence around its origin. The team considered the gulf a separate detail, not proof that a forced public reveal was safe.

程望同意更正，也明白信任不會立刻回來。他們換一位觀察者重做程序。耗時更久，結果也不如第一版戲劇化。這反而有利：如果發現只依靠一個人的表現，就太脆弱，不足以指引下一個危險步驟。咖啡杯標示著私人承諾差點被拍下的位置。公開說法把情況形容為「適合滋生的」，若禾要求查看支撐判斷的觀察。沒人質疑「性別」，讓若禾困擾的是它的來歷沒有人提。團隊把「海灣」視為獨立細節，不當成強制公開感情安全的證據。

Later, when the noise softened, the hardest detail was not written in the official account. It was the hesitation before an ordinary question, the way someone waited for permission to say they were tired. No one in the group could give back every hour already lost. Ruohe glanced toward Yiqing and remembered the principle they had been arguing about: love is not a performance prize. It sounded simple until it demanded another hour of work. Yiqing checked whether her microphone was really off before answering. A witness mentioned intensity during the review, so Yiqing preserved the exact wording. It might help to invade the private records, but Ruohe refused to mistake possibility for proof. The jealousy appeared in the older unedited recording, and Ruohe asked why the newer version omitted it.

後來噪音緩下來，最難忘的細節不在正式報告裡，而是平凡提問前的遲疑，以及有人等著被允許說自己累了的模樣。團隊沒有人能還回失去的每個小時。若禾望向以晴，想起兩人不斷爭論的原則：愛情不是表演的獎品。話雖簡單，真正實踐又需要多一小時工作。以晴回答前先確認麥克風真的關了。見證人在覆核時提到「強度」，以晴保留了原話。「入侵」或許有幫助，若禾卻拒絕把可能當成證明。舊未剪輯錄影出現了「嫉妒」，若禾追問新版為何漏掉。

Before leaving, they checked the names once more. Names were easier to lose than equipment, especially when a system reduced people to numbers. The check took little time. It kept the next chapter of the problem from beginning with a convenient omission. Ruohe knew the next day would not begin from a clean slate. The unedited recording would preserve one more question, and Yiqing would be there to challenge the first easy answer. The six empty dinner plates looked more honest than the rehearsed smiles. The warning sounded learned to some listeners, although the actual measurements remained uncertain. The team considered the software a separate detail, not proof that a forced public reveal was safe. A witness called the result systematic; Yiqing wrote the adjective beside the witness’s name.

離開前，他們再次核對名字。名字比設備更容易消失，尤其當系統把人簡化成數字。檢查花不了多少時間，卻避免問題的下一章從方便的遺漏開始。若禾知道明天不會從零開始。未剪輯錄影將保存又一個問題，以晴也會在場質疑第一個容易的答案。六個空餐盤比排練過的笑容還誠實。對部分聽眾而言，警告聽起來「學養深厚的」，實際測量卻仍有疑點。團隊把「軟體」視為獨立細節，不當成強制公開感情安全的證據。見證人稱結果「有系統的」，以晴把形容詞記在見證人姓名旁。
