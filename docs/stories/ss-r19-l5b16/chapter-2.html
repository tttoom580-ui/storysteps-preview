Cheng Wei reviewed footage from three bank branches. In each case, a payment had been authorized at the same minute, yet no person matching the named recipient appeared at the counter. She resisted the easy conclusion that one invisible murderer was moving between cities. The branch records showed that some transfers were automatic. The account name had been generated by a settlement system, not typed by a clerk. Cheng asked the banks to preserve the original transaction codes before their routine archives expired. A supervisor suggested quietly reversing the payments to avoid scandal. She refused. If funds were returned without tracing how they had moved, the system could repeat the loss tomorrow.
