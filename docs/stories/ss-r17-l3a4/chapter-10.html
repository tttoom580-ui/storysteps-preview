Outside, small ordinary tasks continued. Someone needed a meal, a place to rest, and a truthful message about what was happening. A rumor traveled faster than the evidence. Some people praised Luka as a genius; others said the whole effort was a trick. Luka disliked both stories. Sen proposed a public explanation with the facts, the uncertainties, and the names of those who had done the work. Nobody's private information would be shown without permission.
