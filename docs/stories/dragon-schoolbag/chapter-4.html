窗邊的風

A Window Full of Wind

我的書包住著一隻迷你龍 · 第 4 / 25 章 · 1000以下 · 英中對照 · 校訂中

用互動版查詞、朗讀與收藏

Aki could not stop thinking about the circle with three lines. On Wednesday morning, he drew it at the top of his art page. A strong wind began to blow outside the classroom. The clouds moved quickly, and the tall trees shook. Their teacher, Ms. Ono, told every boy and girl to stay away from the open window while she closed it. Nono was inside the bag under Aki's chair. Aki checked that the bag would not fall. He could feel the dragon shake through the cloth. “You are safe here,” he whispered, though he knew he must still speak with an adult about the situation.

亞希一直想著那個圓和三條短線。星期三早上，他把圖案畫在美術紙的最上方。窗外忽然吹起大風，雲跑得很快，高樹也不停搖動。小野老師請所有男孩女孩離開打開的窗邊，自己去把窗關好。諾諾待在亞希椅子下的書包裡。亞希先確認書包不會倒，隔著布感覺到小龍在發抖。「你在這裡很安全。」他低聲說，但心裡也知道，還是得找大人商量。

The art lesson was about the town. Each child could paint one square picture. Yui drew a butterfly over a tomato plant. Another student drew the factory where his father worked. Aki chose the little park near the road. He used green, blue, and yellow, and left a white space near a bench. Nono pushed its nose into the open side of the bag to see. Aki moved the paper closer instead of taking the dragon out. “A picture does not need to be expensive to tell a story,” Ms. Ono said. “Even a cheap pencil can show us something true.” Aki thought of the mysterious circle.

美術課的主題是小鎮。每個孩子都能畫一張方形圖片。優衣畫的是番茄葉上飛舞的蝴蝶，另一位同學畫父親工作的工廠。亞希選了道路旁的小公園，用綠、藍、黃三色上色，還在長椅旁留下空白。諾諾從書包側口探出鼻子想看，亞希便把畫紙靠近，沒有把牠抱出來。小野老師說：「畫不需要昂貴才能說出故事。便宜的鉛筆也能畫出真實的事情。」亞希想起那個神祕圓圈。

A loose sheet suddenly flew off the table. Nono let out a surprised puff. White mist touched the paper, which dropped harmlessly near Aki's shoe. The dragon had not made the wind, but it seemed sure it had caused the whole problem. It hid its face. Aki knelt and picked up the page. “You did not break anything,” he whispered. “The window was already open.” A school worker came to check the window frame. The worker said the room needed better service on windy days. Ms. Ono thanked him politely. Aki liked that she could ask for help without making anyone feel foolish.

一張沒壓好的紙忽然從桌上飛走。諾諾嚇得吐出一小團白霧，紙張剛好輕輕落在亞希鞋旁。風不是小龍引起的，但牠以為整件事都是自己的錯，立刻藏起臉。亞希蹲下拾起紙。「你沒有弄壞東西，」他低聲說，「窗戶本來就是開的。」一位校務工作人員來檢查窗框，說刮風的日子需要更仔細保養。小野老師有禮貌地向他道謝。亞希很喜歡老師求助時不讓任何人覺得笨的方式。

At lunch, Yui noticed his drawing again. “That mark looks like the sign outside the old book shop,” she said. “Not the one in the town square. The small shop near the bus road.” Aki tried to remember it. The shop had a painting of a circle with lines on its front wall. Its owner was a tall man who always called children “sir” and “madam” in a joking voice. Aki had walked past it many times without really seeing the sign. Suddenly he wanted to go there. But he could not take Nono to a strange place without letting his mother know.

午餐時，優衣又注意到他的畫。「那記號很像舊書店外面的招牌，」她說，「不是廣場那間，是公車路旁的小店。」亞希努力回想。那家店的前牆確實畫著圓形和幾條線。店主是個高個子男人，常開玩笑地叫小孩「先生」和「女士」。亞希走過那裡好多次，卻從沒仔細看過招牌。現在他很想去看看，但不能不告訴媽媽就帶著諾諾跑去陌生地方。

After the art class, Aki opened his notebook to draw the circle again. A white cloud moved past the window, and a drop of rain touched the glass. Yui leaned over his page. “Why do you draw that mark so often?” she asked. Aki looked for an answer he could say honestly. “It may be a place I need to find,” he replied. Yui pointed out that a picture could have more than one meaning. A circle might be a pond, a place to meet, or a sign for a shop. Aki agreed. He had been ready to call it a home simply because he wanted it to be one. Yui had helped him slow down.

美術課後，亞希打開筆記本，又畫了一次圓圈。白雲飄過窗外，一滴雨落在玻璃上。優衣湊近問：「你怎麼常畫這記號？」亞希找了一個能誠實回答的說法：「也許是我需要找的地方。」優衣說一張圖可能有不只一種意思。圓圈可以是池塘、相會的地方，也可以是商店招牌。亞希同意。他先前因為希望那裡是家，就急著把它當作家的記號。優衣幫他把腳步放慢了。

That evening, Aki wrote a careful message to his mother: “I have found a small living creature in my old bag. It is healthy as far as I can see, but I need your help.” He did not send it at once. He was afraid she might tell him to leave Nono outside without a safe home. Nono climbed onto the desk and placed its tiny foot beside the message. Aki read his words again. They were true, and they were not angry words. He sent the message. In the little pocket, Nono curled up peacefully. It could live without fire, but Aki could not care for it well without help.

那晚，亞希小心寫訊息給媽媽：「我在舊書包裡發現一個小小的生物。就我看來牠很健康，但我需要你幫忙。」他遲遲沒有按下傳送，怕媽媽叫他把諾諾丟到沒有安全住處的外面。諾諾爬上書桌，把小腳輕放在訊息旁。亞希再讀一次。他寫的是真話，也沒有責怪任何人。他按下傳送。小龍在口袋裡安心蜷起。牠可以不噴火生活，但亞希不能只靠自己就把牠照顧好。
