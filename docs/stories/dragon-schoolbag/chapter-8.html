小得不能再小的學生

A Very Small Student

我的書包住著一隻迷你龍 · 第 8 / 25 章 · 1000以下 · 英中對照 · 校訂中

Yui brought three picture books to Aki's house. One was about a girl learning baseball. Another was about a park officer who helped animals. The third showed a ghost in a long white dress, but it was a funny ghost that only wanted friends. Nono walked behind the books and looked at each cover. It stopped at the park story. “Is that the one you want?” Yui asked. Two taps. She sat on the floor, opened it gently, and began to read. Aki had thought teaching a dragon would be difficult. For the first five minutes, it was as easy as turning a page slowly.

優衣帶了三本圖畫書到亞希家。一本是女孩學棒球，一本是公園管理員幫助動物，第三本畫著穿白裙子的鬼，但那是個只想交朋友的有趣小鬼。諾諾走到書後面，一一看封面，最後停在公園故事前。「你想看這本嗎？」優衣問。兩聲敲擊。她坐在地板上，小心翻開書讀起來。亞希原以為教小龍讀書很難，前五分鐘卻只需要慢慢翻頁。

Then Nono tried to fly over the open page. Its little wings lifted it one inch before it dropped onto a soft cloth. It was not hurt, but its heart was beating fast. “You do not have to fly to learn,” Aki told it. “You can walk, or I can give you a better place to sit.” He moved a low box near the book. Nono climbed up and looked pleased. Yui explained that children learned in different ways, too. Some liked pictures, some liked words, and some needed a break before trying again. Nono seemed to understand the part about taking a break.

接著，諾諾想從打開的書上方飛過去。小翅膀只把牠抬高一點，牠便掉到軟布上。牠沒有受傷，心卻跳得很快。「你不必會飛才能學，」亞希說，「可以走過去，或我幫你換個好坐的位置。」他把低矮的小盒子移近書邊。諾諾爬上去，露出滿意的神情。優衣說，孩子也有不同的學習方式：有人喜歡圖，有人喜歡字，也有人需要休息一下才繼續。諾諾似乎特別懂得休息那部分。

For lunch, Aki's mother brought a small dish of rice and vegetables for the children. She had cooked beef for herself, but she did not give any to Nono because they did not yet know what food was good for a dragon. Nono tasted a bit of plain cooked rice and seemed to like it. “Why does it want to attend school?” Yui asked quietly. Aki did not know. Nono pointed to the book, then to Aki's school bag hanging by the door. It made two taps on the floor. “Maybe it wants to go where the words are,” Yui said. That was a nice way to say it.

午餐時，媽媽端來孩子們吃的米飯和蔬菜。她替自己煮了牛肉，卻沒有餵給諾諾，因為大家還不清楚什麼食物適合小龍。諾諾嚐了一點原味熟飯，似乎很喜歡。優衣小聲問：「牠為什麼想去學校？」亞希也不知道。諾諾指向圖畫書，再指向門邊掛著的書包，敲了兩下。「也許牠想去有文字的地方，」優衣說。亞希覺得這個說法很美好。

When they came home, Yui invited Nono to choose a book for the next lesson. She held two covers side by side. One showed a kitchen, and one showed a forest after rain. Nono touched the forest. “That makes sense,” she said. “You seem to like green places.” Aki stopped her gently. “Maybe, but we have only seen one choice.” Yui laughed at herself. “Right. I need to ask again another day.” They let the dragon taste one small piece of plain soft fruit from its familiar food list. Nono ate it happily, then moved away from the dish. No one asked it to finish more.

回家後，優衣請諾諾挑選下一次要讀的書。她把兩個封面並排：一張是廚房，另一張是雨後森林。諾諾碰了森林。「這很合理，」優衣說，「你好像喜歡綠色的地方。」亞希輕聲提醒：「也許，但現在只看到這一次選擇。」優衣笑自己想太快：「對，下次再問。」他們從諾諾熟悉的食物清單裡拿出一小片柔軟水果讓牠品嚐。小龍吃得開心，接著離開小碟子，沒有人要求牠再吃。

The picture book had one page about a team of park workers saving a bird with a hurt wing. Yui pointed out that the workers did not simply pick up every bird they saw. First they checked whether it needed help. Then they called the right people. Aki said this sounded like the way they should look for Nono's family. If they found a nest, they should not take it apart. If they saw another little dragon, they should give it space. Nono looked up at the bird picture and tapped twice. Aki hoped that meant agreement, but he knew he must keep asking as the search went on.

圖畫書裡有一頁畫著公園工作團隊幫助受傷翅膀的小鳥。優衣指出，工作人員不是看見每隻鳥都抱起來，而是先判斷牠是否需要幫忙，再找對的人。亞希覺得尋找諾諾家人也該這樣：發現巢穴不能拆開，看到另一條小龍也要留空間。諾諾抬頭看看鳥的圖片，敲了兩下。亞希希望那是同意的意思，但也知道搜尋的每一步仍需要繼續詢問。

After lunch they took a short walk outside, with Aki's mother beside them. Nono rode in an open, shaded carrier and watched children playing in a group at the park. A park officer saw the carrier and reminded them that small animals must not be left alone in the sun. Aki thanked her. He did not try to show off his unusual friend. When Nono grew tired, they went home at once. On the way back, Yui said, “I thought a magic creature would always be brave. But Nono is brave in a different way. It tries a new thing, then asks for a rest.” Aki agreed. They had learned something without going to school.

午餐後，媽媽陪他們到戶外走一小段路。諾諾坐在有遮蔭、保持通風的小提籃裡，看公園裡一群孩子玩耍。公園管理員看到提籃，提醒他們不能把小動物獨自留在太陽下。亞希向她道謝，沒有藉機炫耀這位不尋常的朋友。諾諾一累，他們就立刻回家。路上，優衣說：「我以前以為魔法生物一定什麼都不怕。諾諾不一樣，牠會試新事物，也會要求休息。」亞希同意。今天不用到學校，大家一樣學到了東西。
