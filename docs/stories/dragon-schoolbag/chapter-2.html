兩下是願意

Two Taps Mean Yes

我的書包住著一隻迷你龍 · 第 2 / 25 章 · 1000以下 · 英中對照 · 校訂中

At school, Aki did not know what to do with a dragon in his bag. He could not bring Nono into a crowded room without a plan. He set the bag beside his chair, away from feet, and opened its side air window. His cousin Mina had made that window last summer when the bag felt too hot. Aki had never been so thankful for a small piece of cloth. He wanted to add a soft bed, a cup for water, and a place to hide. But he could not fill the bag with things until he knew what Nono wanted. He bent down and quietly asked, “Are you comfortable?” Two taps.

到了學校，亞希還不知道該如何照顧書包裡的小龍。他不能毫無準備就把諾諾帶進擁擠的教室。他把書包放在椅旁，避開來往的腳，打開側邊透氣窗。那扇窗是表姊米娜去年夏天縫的，當時她覺得書包太悶熱。亞希從沒像今天這麼感謝一小塊布。他想加上軟床、小水杯和藏身處，但在知道諾諾的心意以前，不能擅自塞滿東西。「你舒服嗎？」他彎下腰問。兩聲敲擊。

The first lesson was science. Their teacher showed a video about rivers that ran from hills in the north to fields in the south. On the screen, a famous scientist used a simple tool to measure water. Aki felt two taps against his shoe. Nono wanted to see, too. Aki moved his bag a little so the open side faced the screen, but he kept it safely under the table. He could not share the secret with everyone. A picture of a sheep appeared in the video, and the dragon made a tiny sound like a sneeze. Aki pressed his lip between his teeth to stop a laugh.

第一堂課是自然科學。老師播放影片，介紹河流如何從北方山丘流向南邊的田地。畫面裡，一位有名的科學家拿著簡單工具量水。亞希的鞋邊傳來兩下敲擊，諾諾也想看。他稍微移動書包，讓透氣窗面向螢幕，同時保持書包安穩地放在桌下。他還不能向所有人說明祕密。影片裡出現一隻綿羊時，小龍打了個像噴嚏的聲音。亞希咬住嘴唇，才忍住笑。

During the break, his friend Yui asked, “Why is your bag looking at the board?” Aki almost told her everything. Yui was a helpful person, and she always returned books on time. Still, this was not only his secret. Nono had a choice. “There is something living in there,” Aki said carefully. “It is not dangerous, but I need to talk to my family and a teacher before I make it everyone's problem.” Yui studied him. “A fish?” she asked. “No.” “A very small guy in a hat?” He laughed in spite of himself. “Not quite.” She did not force him to answer.

下課時，朋友優衣問：「你的書包為什麼像在看黑板？」亞希差點說出全部。優衣一向樂於幫忙，也總是準時還書。但這不只是亞希一個人的祕密，諾諾也有選擇權。「裡面有個活生生的小東西，」他小心地說，「牠沒有危險，但我想先和家人、老師商量，不能直接讓大家面對這件事。」優衣看著他。「是魚嗎？」「不是。」「那是戴帽子的小人嗎？」亞希忍不住笑了。「也不是。」她沒有逼他回答。

At lunch, Nono ate one slice of apple from Aki's box. The dragon also drank water from a clean bottle cap. It did not like the bread crust, and it tapped once when Aki offered more. Aki stopped at once. “No means no,” he said. “That is true even when I want to give you something nice.” He wrote the rule in his notebook. The school had rules about animals for good reasons: food, health, space, and other children's fears. He could not pretend those rules did not exist. Nono was not a normal pet, but this did not give Aki special power to ignore other people.

午餐時，諾諾吃了一小片蘋果，從乾淨的瓶蓋喝水。牠不喜歡麵包邊。亞希再遞過去時，牠敲了一下。亞希立刻停手。「不願意就是不願意，」他說，「即使我覺得那是好東西也一樣。」他把規則寫下來。學校對動物有規定，是為了食物衛生、空間和其他孩子的感受。諾諾雖然不是普通寵物，也不代表亞希有權不顧別人。

After lunch the class made a wall chart of useful places. Aki drew the river path because it ran near his house. Yui drew the public library, and their teacher drew a quiet room where children could go when the hall became too loud. Aki liked that idea. Nono had only a small pocket at school and could not ask a grown-up for help in the usual way. When the teacher said every student needed a safe place, Aki felt a little worried. He had been thinking mostly about how to keep Nono unseen. He had not thought enough about what would happen if the dragon needed more care than he could give.

午餐後，全班畫了一張「有用的地方」海報。亞希畫了住家附近的河邊步道，優衣畫公共圖書館，老師則畫了走廊太吵時可以休息的安靜房間。亞希很喜歡這個點子。諾諾在學校只有小小的口袋，也無法像普通學生那樣向大人求助。老師說每位學生都需要安全的地方時，亞希有點擔心。他一直只想著怎樣讓諾諾不被看見，還沒認真想過，若小龍需要超出自己能力的照顧，該怎麼辦。

After school the day was sunny and warm. Aki sat under a tree near the gate and let Nono look out at the open sky while he watched the path. “We need a better way,” he said. “A place where you can rest, a way to learn, and grown-ups who can help if you are sick.” The dragon reached up and touched his hand with one foot. Aki thought of his mother, who worked long hours but always listened when he was afraid. He would tell her soon. Nono looked back into the bag and tapped twice. They had made a small start. Neither one knew how large the next question would be.

放學後天氣晴朗又溫暖。亞希坐在校門附近的樹下，讓諾諾探頭看看天空，自己留意來往行人。「我們得找個更好的辦法，」他說，「讓你有地方休息、能學東西，生病時也有大人幫忙。」小龍伸出一隻腳碰他的手。亞希想起總是願意聽他說害怕的事、即使工作很忙的媽媽。他決定很快就告訴她。諾諾回頭看看書包，又敲了兩下。他們已經開始，但都還不知道下一個問題有多大。
