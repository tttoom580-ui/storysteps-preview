舊書店的招牌

The Sign at the Book Shop

我的書包住著一隻迷你龍 · 第 6 / 25 章 · 1000以下 · 英中對照 · 校訂中

By Friday, Aki and his mother had a plan. Nono would stay in its open basket at home during school hours. Aki's mother would ask the school what was possible before anyone brought a dragon into a classroom again. She did not want to drive a creature away just because it was new, but she needed to protect other children, too. “We can visit the old book shop together this weekend,” she said. “We will look at the sign, and Nono can decide whether to come.” The dragon stood on the top of its little box and gave two taps. Aki was glad they had asked instead of making the choice for it.

星期五，亞希和媽媽終於訂好計畫。上學時間，諾諾留在家裡開放的小籃子裡；媽媽會先向學校詢問，再決定能不能帶小龍進教室。她不願意因為生物陌生就把牠趕走，也必須顧及其他孩子。「這週末我們一起去舊書店看看招牌，」她說，「諾諾可以自己決定要不要同行。」小龍站上小盒子的頂端，敲了兩下。亞希很高興他們先詢問，而不是代替牠決定。

The book shop stood between a coffee shop and a narrow house. An uncle who lived nearby was carrying a boat model in a glass case. He stopped to wave at Aki's mother. The book-shop owner, Mr. Park, had put a paper lion in the front window. Below it was the sign Aki wanted to see: a blue circle with three short lines. It was the same shape as Nono's drawing, except that the shop sign was larger and painted on wood. “I finally found it,” Aki whispered. Nono's nose appeared at the open side of the carrier. It looked excited, but not frightened.

舊書店夾在咖啡店和一棟窄房子中間。附近一位叔叔抱著玻璃盒裡的小船模型，停下來向媽媽揮手。書店主人朴先生在櫥窗擺了一隻紙獅子，下面正是亞希想找的招牌：藍色圓圈和三條短線。它和諾諾畫的形狀一樣，只是木招牌比較大。「終於找到了。」亞希輕聲說。諾諾的鼻子從透氣的提袋側邊探出來，看起來很興奮，卻沒有害怕。

Mr. Park noticed Aki staring at the sign. “That is an old street mark,” he explained. “It shows the place where three small roads meet. There used to be a tree there. The tree is gone, but the mark remains in some old maps.” Aki asked if the shop had a copy. Mr. Park smiled. “I might. A map of our town is not a terrible secret.” He went into the back room and returned with a thin book. Its cover showed streets, roofs, and a river. “Please do not call yourself stupid if you cannot read it,” he said. “Old maps are hard even for grown-ups.”

朴先生注意到亞希看著招牌。「這是舊街道的記號，」他解釋，「代表三條小路相會的地方。以前那裡有棵樹，樹不在了，但一些舊地圖還留著記號。」亞希問店裡有沒有地圖，朴先生笑了：「也許有。小鎮地圖可不是什麼可怕的祕密。」他進後室拿出一本薄書，封面畫著道路、屋頂和河流。「看不懂也別罵自己笨，」他說，「舊地圖連大人都覺得難。」

They had planned to arrive before the shop became busy, and they did, so Mr. Park had time to answer another question. Aki asked why the old sign remained even though the tree was gone. “Some marks help people remember how a place used to look,” the owner said. “But they do not tell us everything that happened there.” Aki turned that sentence over in his mind. Nono had recognized the circle, yet no one knew whether it had seen the real tree. It might have learned the picture from the bag. Aki did not want to take away Nono's hope, but he also did not want to build their next journey on a story with no proof.

他們在書店變忙前就到了，朴先生還有時間回答另一個問題。亞希問老樹不在後，招牌為什麼還留著。「有些記號能幫人記住地方過去的模樣，」店主說，「卻不能告訴我們那裡發生過的每件事。」亞希反覆想著這句話。諾諾認得圓圈，但沒人知道牠是否見過真正的老樹，也許只是從書包學會那圖案。亞希不想奪走諾諾的希望，也不想讓接下來的旅程只靠沒證據的故事前進。

While they waited, Aki looked through a child's picture guide about streets. One page showed a boat moving under a bridge; another showed a home made inside a hollow tree. Nono touched the tree picture, paused, and then touched the book-shop sign again. “They look alike,” Aki said, “but they are not the same place.” His mother asked whether the dragon wanted to rest. One tap: no. Aki asked whether it wanted to see the map. Two taps: yes. He felt glad that a little word game could help them make a real choice. The dragon was not just the reason for the search. It was part of deciding how the search would happen.

等待時，亞希翻看一本兒童街道圖鑑。一頁畫著小船穿過橋下，另一頁畫著樹洞裡的家。諾諾碰了樹的圖片，停一下，又碰書店招牌。「它們看起來像，但不是同一個地方。」亞希說。媽媽問小龍想不想休息，一聲：不要。亞希再問要不要看地圖，兩聲：願意。他很高興小小的字卡遊戲，竟能幫大家做真正的決定。諾諾不只是被尋找的理由，也參與了尋找的方法。

Aki could see one blue circle near a small square marked “Three Paths.” But there were no pictures of dragons. The map did not tell them whether Nono had come from there, or whether Nono only knew the mark from a story. Mr. Park let them copy the page for free, provided they wrote the book's name below the picture. Aki thanked him and returned the book. Nono gave a tiny wave from inside the open pocket, unseen by the owner. Outside, Aki's mother asked, “What did we learn?” “We have a place to look,” Aki answered. “Not proof of a home.” His mother nodded. That answer was better than a guess.

亞希在地圖上找到一個藍圈，旁邊寫著「三路口」，卻沒有龍的圖片。地圖無法證明那裡就是諾諾的家，也可能牠只在故事裡看過這符號。朴先生讓他們免費影印那頁，條件是要在圖下寫明書名。亞希道謝後還書。諾諾在透氣口裡偷偷揮了一下腳，店主沒有看到。離開後，媽媽問：「我們學到了什麼？」「有一個可以找的地方，」亞希說，「但還不能證明是牠的家。」媽媽點頭，覺得這答案比猜測好得多。
