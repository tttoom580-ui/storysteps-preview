The Warm Pocket

At seven a.m., Aki was early for breakfast, but his blue school bag was late to the kitchen. It was still on the couch, where he had left it under a cover the night before. On Sunday afternoon he had set the clean bag beside the open yard door to dry, then moved it inside at night. His mother had made fruit salad and a little cake. Aki wanted to eat quickly because the school bell would ring in one hour. He did his morning exercise beside the table, then tried to lift his bag. It was much too heavy. He had only one notebook inside. “Did someone put a stone in here?” he asked. The bag answered with three tiny taps.

上午七點，亞希早早坐下吃早餐，可是他的藍色書包還留在客廳的沙發上，蓋著昨晚放上去的布。星期天下午，他曾把洗乾淨的書包放在敞開的院門旁晾乾，晚上才拿回屋裡。媽媽準備了水果沙拉和一小塊蛋糕。亞希想快點吃完，因為再過一小時學校就要響鐘了。他在桌邊做完晨間運動，試著提起書包，卻發現沉得奇怪。裡面明明只有一本筆記本。「有人放石頭進去嗎？」他問。書包竟回了三聲輕輕的敲擊。

Aki opened the front pocket by one finger. Two golden eyes looked out. A little green dragon sat on a pencil case, no longer than his hand. Its wings were smaller than leaves, and one white tooth showed when it yawned. Aki did not scream. He was too surprised to make a sound. “You are not a toy,” he whispered. The creature shook its head. It pointed at a picture of a monkey on Aki's key ring and shook its head again, as if to say, “Not that, either.” Aki had found a new, very strange hobby: understanding a dragon.

亞希只把前袋拉開一指寬。兩隻金色眼睛正望著他。一條綠色小龍坐在鉛筆盒上，身長還不到他的手掌，翅膀比葉子還小，打呵欠時露出一顆白牙。亞希驚訝得連尖叫也忘了。「你不是玩具。」他低聲說。小傢伙搖搖頭，又指著鑰匙圈上的猴子圖案搖頭，彷彿在說：「也不是那個。」亞希突然多了一個非常奇怪的興趣：學著理解一條龍。

“Can you breathe fire?” Aki asked. The dragon puffed with all its strength. A small white cloud came out and made the bag's clear name cover misty. There was no flame, no smoke, and no heat that could hurt him. The dragon looked down at its feet. Aki understood that it had tried hard. “That is okay,” he said. “I cannot jump as high as my friend Yui, but I can still play.” The dragon looked up. Its tail moved like a happy little flag. When Aki held out his palm, it climbed on carefully, as if he were a bridge.

「你會噴火嗎？」亞希問。小龍用盡力氣吐了一口氣，只吐出一團白霧，讓書包透明的姓名套起了霧。沒有火焰，也沒有會傷人的高溫。小龍低頭看著腳。亞希明白牠很努力了。「沒關係，」他說，「我跳得沒有朋友優衣高，還是能一起玩。」小龍抬起頭，尾巴像高興的小旗子輕輕搖動。亞希伸出手掌，牠小心爬上來，像在走一座橋。

His mother called from the kitchen. Aki quickly placed the dragon back on the soft cloth in the pocket, but he did not close the zipper. It needed air. “Are you ready?” she called. “Almost!” he said. He looked at a foreign word printed on an old tag inside the bag. It was a word he could not read. Perhaps the dragon knew it. “I shall teach you some school words,” he whispered, “if you want to learn.” The dragon gave two soft taps. Aki decided that two taps meant yes and one tap meant no, but only if the dragon agreed. It tapped twice again.

媽媽從廚房叫他。亞希趕緊讓小龍回到前袋柔軟的布上，卻沒有把拉鍊拉緊，因為牠需要空氣。「準備好了嗎？」媽媽問。「快了！」亞希看見書包內側舊標籤印著一個他不認識的外文單字。也許小龍看得懂。「如果你想學，我可以教你一些學校用語。」他說。小龍敲了兩下。亞希想把兩下當作「願意」、一下當作「不要」，但前提是小龍也同意。牠又敲了兩下。

For a moment Aki wondered if he should stay home. His bag had been ordinary yesterday. Today it held someone who had never seen his school. He sat on the floor, set a dry cloth beside the pocket, and listened for Nono's breathing. The little dragon moved into the light, looked at the front door, and tapped twice when Aki asked if it wanted to come. “You can change your mind,” he said. “I will listen.” He also put a tiny piece of paper beside the pocket. On it he drew a cup for water and a hand for help. Nono looked at both pictures and touched the cup. Aki smiled. Their first lesson was not about fire. It was about asking.

亞希有一瞬間想乾脆請假在家。昨天書包還很普通，今天卻住著一位沒看過學校的小客人。他坐到地板上，在口袋旁放好乾布，聽諾諾輕細的呼吸。小龍走到光線裡，看了看大門。亞希問牠想不想同行，牠敲兩下。「你隨時可以改變主意，」亞希說，「我會聽。」他還在袋旁放一張小紙，畫了水杯和求助的手。諾諾碰了水杯。亞希笑了。他們的第一堂課不是噴火，而是好好詢問。

Before leaving, Aki opened his notebook to write three things. First, the little dragon was real. Second, its breath was only mist. Third, the pocket had to stay open enough for air, and the dragon could leave whenever it wished. He drew a small sun above the list. “You need a name,” he said. The dragon pressed a tiny foot on the page. Four marks looked like the word Nono if Aki turned the book sideways. “Nono?” he asked. Two taps. Aki smiled. His bag was still heavy, but he no longer thought it held a stone. It held a new friend with a very small voice.

出門前，亞希在筆記本寫下三件事：第一，小龍是真的；第二，牠只能吐霧；第三，書包口必須留下足夠的空氣，牠想離開時也能離開。他在上方畫了一個小太陽。「你需要名字。」他說。小龍在紙上按下四個小腳印，亞希把本子轉向一旁看，竟有點像「Nono」。「諾諾？」他問。兩聲敲擊。亞希笑了。書包依舊很重，但他知道裡頭不是石頭，而是一位聲音很小的新朋友。
