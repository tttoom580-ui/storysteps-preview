願意等待的朋友

A Friend Who Waits

Aki kept the copied map in his bedroom, next to a small clock and a picture of a rainbow. On Monday he looked at it again and again. He wanted to believe the blue circle would lead to Nono's family, but he knew that hope was not the same as proof. He made an error while drawing the roads and had to start over. Nono sat in the yard under his mother's watch, looking at the sky. The dragon liked the morning air. Aki felt proud that it could move around freely at home, instead of spending every day in a bag. Then he looked toward school and wished it could learn there safely.

亞希把影印地圖放在臥室裡，旁邊是小時鐘和彩虹畫。星期一，他一看再看。他很想相信藍圈能帶自己找到諾諾的家，卻也知道希望不是證據。畫路線時他畫錯了一段，只好重畫。媽媽看顧著院子裡的諾諾，小龍抬頭望著天空，似乎很喜歡早晨的空氣。亞希為牠能在家自在活動感到高興，不必整天待在書包裡；但一想到學校，又希望牠有朝一日能安全地去學習。

At school, Yui looked at him as if she had been waiting for a long time. “Is the small living thing okay?” she asked. Aki could hear noise from the hall, so he moved close to the window before answering. “Yes. It is at home. My mother knows, and we are trying to find where it belongs.” Yui smiled but did not ask to see it. “I had a dream about a talking mouse,” she said, “but I don't think your secret is a mouse.” Aki began to laugh. His friend was funny, but the best thing about her was that she knew when to stop asking.

學校裡，優衣好像等了很久。「那個小東西還好嗎？」她問。走廊傳來雜音，亞希便走近窗邊才回答：「很好，現在在家裡。媽媽知道了，我們正在找牠的家。」優衣笑了，卻沒有要求看牠。「我昨天夢到會說話的老鼠，」她說，「不過我想你的祕密不是老鼠。」亞希笑出來。朋友很有趣，而她最好的地方是懂得什麼時候該停止追問。

That afternoon, Aki asked his mother whether Yui could meet Nono. “Only if Nono wants it,” his mother said. “And only here, with me present.” Nono heard Yui's name and tapped twice. When Yui arrived, she sat far from the basket and spoke gently. “Hello. I am Yui. I like books about nature.” At first the dragon stayed close to Aki. Then it stepped out, no bigger than a leaf beside the chair. Yui's eyes grew wide. “A dragon,” she breathed. She did not touch it. She simply waited as if she had all the time in the world.

下午，亞希問媽媽能不能讓優衣認識諾諾。「要先問諾諾，」媽媽說，「而且只能在家裡、由我在場。」諾諾聽到優衣的名字，敲了兩下。優衣來後，坐在離籃子稍遠的地方，輕聲說：「你好，我是優衣。我喜歡看自然的書。」小龍起初緊靠著亞希，後來才跨出來，身形像椅旁的小葉子。優衣瞪大眼睛：「是龍。」她沒有伸手摸，只安靜等待，彷彿有用不完的時間。

Yui asked whether Nono could understand their voices. Aki said it seemed to know simple words, but they had to check each new one. “I just want to be sure I am not putting my words in its mouth,” he said. Yui agreed. She had seen rich people in stories give orders to everyone because they thought money made them wise. Aki did not want to act like that just because he was bigger than Nono. The dragon tapped twice when Yui asked if she might turn the map. She turned it slowly. Nono pointed toward the edge, not the center. All three children—two human and one dragon—had something to teach the others.

優衣問諾諾是否聽得懂人類說話。亞希說牠好像懂簡單的詞，但每個新字都得慢慢確認。「我只想確定自己沒有把想說的話塞到牠嘴裡，」他說。優衣同意。她在故事裡看過有錢人因為有錢，就認為自己能命令所有人。亞希不願因為身體比諾諾大，也做出同樣的事。優衣問能不能轉動地圖，小龍敲了兩下。她慢慢轉過紙頁，諾諾指向邊緣而不是中心。三個孩子——兩個人類、一條龍——都能教會彼此一些事。

After the visit, Yui stayed for a cup of water and drew a small picture of the circle. Aki checked whether it was correct, then handed it to Nono. The dragon looked at the lines and moved one foot. Yui made a second picture with the lines farther apart. Nono chose the first one. “Good,” she said. “Now we know which drawing looks closer to what Nono means.” Aki wrote the result down. This did not tell them the location of the family, but it helped them agree on what to search for. He thanked Yui for asking questions without making Nono feel like a school test.

探訪後，優衣留下來喝水，順手畫了小圓圈。亞希確認畫法，再交給諾諾看。小龍看著線條，動了一隻腳。優衣又畫一張三條線距離較遠的圖，諾諾選了第一張。「很好，」她說，「現在知道哪張更接近你想表達的意思。」亞希記下結果。這還無法告訴他們家在哪裡，卻能讓大家對搜尋的圖案取得共識。他感謝優衣願意提問，卻不把諾諾當成參加考試的學生。

Nono walked onto the floor and drew its circle with a wet foot. Yui looked from the mark to the map. “The old sign,” she said. “Do you want to find it?” Two taps. “I can help read the street names, but I will not tell my whole class.” Nono gave another two taps. Aki felt that something important had happened. He no longer had to hide from his best friend, and Nono had helped choose whom to trust. As the day grew dark, the three watched the first stars appear. Aki did not know whether their search would succeed. For now, they had made room for one more friend.

諾諾走到地板上，用沾濕的小腳畫出圓圈。優衣看看記號，又看看地圖。「是舊招牌。你想找它嗎？」兩聲敲擊。「我可以幫忙讀街名，但不會告訴全班。」諾諾又敲了兩下。亞希覺得今天發生了重要的事：他不必再瞞著最好的朋友，諾諾也親自選擇了信任誰。天漸漸暗了，三人一起看最早的星星出現。亞希不知道尋找會不會成功，但此刻又多了一位能一起想辦法的朋友。
