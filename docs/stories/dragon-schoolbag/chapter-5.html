不是賽跑

Not a Race

我的書包住著一隻迷你龍 · 第 5 / 25 章 · 1000以下 · 英中對照 · 校訂中

Aki's mother answered the message before bed. She was not angry, but she asked him to keep the bag at home the next morning until they could talk. Aki was sorry that he had waited to tell her. She said, “I know you wanted to help, although a living creature needs more than kindness.” She would come home early after work. At school, Aki sat through a long morning without Nono. His desk felt too quiet. During the break, he found a green bean in his lunch box and almost saved it for the dragon. Then he remembered that Nono had its own food at home and did not need a surprise gift.

媽媽睡前回覆了訊息。她沒有生氣，只請亞希隔天先把書包留在家，等兩人談清楚。亞希很抱歉自己拖了這麼久才告訴她。媽媽說：「我知道你想幫忙，雖然善意很重要，活生生的生物還需要更多照顧。」她會提早下班。沒有諾諾的早上，亞希覺得桌邊安靜得不習慣。下課時，他在便當裡看到一顆綠豆，差點想留給小龍，隨後想起諾諾在家有自己的食物，不需要突如其來的禮物。

In physical education, Ms. Ono asked everyone to run a short race. Aki did not win. He had been looking at the height of the steps, thinking about how high they must seem to a dragon no longer than his hand. His friend Yui pointed at the finish line and said, “You can try again next time.” Aki laughed. “At least I did not fall.” The teacher gave them one minute to rest and drink water. They watched a frog hop near the edge of the field. A hen was pictured on a farm poster behind the gate. Aki wished Nono could ask questions about both animals.

體育課時，小野老師請大家短跑。亞希沒贏。他一直看著階梯的高度，想著對掌心大小的小龍來說，那會有多高。優衣指向終點說：「下次再試就好了。」亞希笑了：「至少我沒跌倒。」老師讓大家休息一分鐘並喝水。大家看到一隻青蛙在操場邊跳，校門旁的農場海報還印著母雞。亞希真希望諾諾也能問問這兩種動物的事。

At home, his mother had placed a shallow dish of water and a cloth beside the bag. Nono was sitting on the cloth, looking well. “I did not touch it,” she said. “I asked from a distance, and it came out on its own.” Aki introduced them. “This is Nono. Nono, this is my mother.” The dragon bowed its neck and tapped twice. His mother smiled in surprise. “So that is how you talk.” Then she grew serious. “We need a safe plan. No hiding it in a hot, closed bag. No school until we know more. And if it wants to leave, we must let it.” Aki agreed.

回家時，媽媽已在書包旁放好淺水碟和一塊布。諾諾坐在布上，精神很好。「我沒有碰牠，」媽媽說，「我只是隔著一段距離問牠，牠自己走出來。」亞希替雙方介紹：「這是諾諾。諾諾，這是我媽媽。」小龍彎下脖子，敲了兩下。媽媽驚喜地笑了：「原來你們這樣說話。」接著她認真說：「我們需要安全計畫。不能把牠關在悶熱書包裡，弄清楚以前不能再帶去學校。如果牠想走，也必須讓牠走。」亞希點頭同意。

They looked through books for one hour. One page told them how a small animal might dig a hole to stay warm. Another showed the shape of snow, though it was spring and no snow fell in their town. None showed a dragon that blew mist instead of fire. “We cannot make a deal with a guess,” his mother said. “We should learn by watching what Nono chooses.” Nono pointed to the little circle Aki had drawn. It put one foot on the three lines, then held its feet close to its chest. Aki thought it might mean family. He did not say it was certain. He only wrote the idea down.

他們一起查了一個小時的書。有一頁介紹小動物會挖洞保暖，另一頁畫著雪花，儘管小鎮正值春天，沒有下雪。沒有任何一本提到只吐霧、不噴火的龍。媽媽說：「不能拿猜測來做決定。我們先觀察諾諾自己怎麼選。」諾諾指向亞希畫的圓圈，把腳放在三條線上，再抱住自己的胸口。亞希猜那也許代表家人，但沒有說成確定的事，只把想法寫下來。

His mother also asked about the tiny dragon's sleep. Nono sometimes closed its eyes during the day, then woke when it heard a page turn. They did not know whether this was normal. Instead of waking it for a lesson, they made a simple record of when it chose to rest, eat, or play. Aki wanted to point at every line and ask a question, but his mother reminded him that observation could be quiet. They left space on the page for Nono's own marks. When the dragon woke, it touched the word rest, then looked toward its basket. Aki moved the notebook away so it could go there freely.

媽媽也問起小龍的睡眠。諾諾有時白天閉眼，翻書聲一響又醒來。他們不確定這是不是正常的，因此沒有叫醒牠上課，而是簡單記下牠何時自己選擇休息、吃飯或玩耍。亞希很想指著每一行發問，媽媽提醒他，觀察也可以安靜進行。他們在紙上留空白給諾諾留下記號。小龍醒來後碰了「休息」卡，又望向籃子。亞希把筆記本移開，讓牠自由走過去。

Before sleeping, Aki asked if Nono still wanted to learn school things. Two taps. “And do you want to look for the place in your picture?” Two taps again. “We can do both, slowly,” he said. “You do not have to race anyone or win a prize.” His mother set a small night light near the open basket where Nono could sleep freely. She promised to call the school the next day and ask how they might keep everyone safe. The dragon climbed into its basket, turned once, and put its head on the cloth. For the first time since Monday, Aki knew he was not carrying the whole secret alone.

睡前，亞希問諾諾還想不想學校裡的東西。兩聲敲擊。「那你想找圖案上的地方嗎？」又是兩聲。「我們可以慢慢做兩件事，」亞希說，「不用和任何人比快，也不用得獎。」媽媽在敞開的小籃子旁放了夜燈，諾諾可以自由進出。她答應隔天打電話給學校，詢問怎樣保障大家安全。小龍轉了一圈，把頭靠在布上睡了。從星期一以來，亞希第一次知道自己不用獨自扛著這個祕密。
