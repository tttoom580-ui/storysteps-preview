外婆的舊卡片

A Letter From Grandmother

On Saturday, Aki's grandmother came to visit. She lived on the other side of the lake and brought a dish of rice cakes and a banana for each child. Aki was happy to see her, but he was also nervous. His mother had told her about Nono, and she wanted to hear the whole story. Grandmother sat by the table and listened without a shout or a laugh. When she saw the dragon, her eyes filled with quiet joy. “Well,” she said, “life can still surprise an old woman.” Nono stepped forward, bowed, and tapped twice. “Okay,” Grandmother replied. “It is good to meet you, too.”

星期六，住在湖另一岸的外婆來訪，帶了米糕和每個孩子一根香蕉。亞希很高興見到她，也有些緊張。媽媽已經說過諾諾的事，外婆想聽完整經過。她坐在桌邊安靜聽完，沒有尖叫也沒有大笑。看到小龍時，眼裡浮出溫柔的喜悅。「哎呀，」她說，「原來活到這個年紀，生活還能給我驚喜。」諾諾走上前鞠躬，敲兩下。「好啊，」外婆說，「我也很高興認識你。」

Grandmother had given Aki the blue bag for the first day of school. She looked at the card that had come with it. It showed a street festival from many years ago. On the back was a drawing of three paths meeting at one tree. “That festival was near the old market,” she said. “My friend made bags for children there. Each bag had a small pocket for a lucky note.” Aki turned the card over again. The mark was almost the same as Nono's. He wanted to be sure, so he asked where the tree had stood. Grandmother pointed to the map from the book shop. “Right here.”

藍色書包是外婆在亞希開學時送的。她找出當時附上的卡片，上面畫著多年前的街頭節慶，背面是一棵樹旁三條路相會的圖。「節慶在舊市場附近辦，」她說，「我朋友那時為孩子做書包，每個書包都有裝幸運紙條的小口袋。」亞希反覆翻看卡片。記號幾乎和諾諾畫的一樣。他想確認地點，便問那棵樹曾經站在哪裡。外婆指著書店的地圖說：「就在這裡。」

Nono climbed onto the card with great care. It touched the tree picture and then the back of Aki's bag. Aki felt quite sure the dragon recognized something, but he did not know what. “Do you want to visit that street?” he asked. Two taps. Grandmother gave him a tip: “Ask before you go. Old places can change. You should not walk into a shop or a house because a map says it was once there.” Aki promised. He was ready to search, but he would bring his mother, and Nono could decide if the trip was too much. Nothing was more important than keeping everyone well.

諾諾小心爬上卡片，碰了碰樹的圖，又碰書包背面。亞希很確定牠認出了什麼，卻還不知道那是什麼。「你想去那條街看看嗎？」兩聲敲擊。外婆給了提示：「出門前先問清楚，舊地方會改變。不能因為地圖說以前有店或房子，就自己闖進去。」亞希答應。雖然準備開始尋找，他也會讓媽媽陪同，並讓諾諾決定這趟路是不是太累。沒有什麼比大家平安更重要。

Grandmother turned the old card over and told the children about the festival day. There had been a long line of little shops, music near the lake, and a small table where people could write notes for friends. The bag maker had always said a pocket should hold something kind, not something taken without asking. Aki liked that idea. Nono was not an object that belonged in a pocket, but the pocket had helped keep it warm on a rainy day. “A safe place can be useful without becoming a prison,” he said. Grandmother listened, then smiled as if he had understood a story she had meant to tell.

外婆把舊卡片翻過來，向孩子們說起那場節慶。當時湖邊有一長排小攤、音樂和一張能替朋友寫留言的小桌。做書包的朋友常說，口袋應該裝善意的東西，而不是沒問過就拿來的東西。亞希很喜歡這句話。諾諾不是屬於口袋的物品，但雨天裡，口袋確實給了牠溫暖。「安全的地方可以有用，卻不該變成監牢。」他說。外婆聽了微笑，像是他終於理解她一直想說的故事。

Later, Aki opened the old bag and looked at each stitch. He noticed that the front pocket was wider than a normal pencil pocket, with a loose cloth flap that let air pass. Grandmother said the maker had used that pattern for many small gifts. It was not proof that she knew dragons existed. Aki wrote that line in his notebook, because he had begun to notice how easily a wish could turn into a claim. Nono stepped into the bag for one moment, then climbed out again. It seemed to remember the place, but it also liked the open basket. Aki decided to keep both ready and let the dragon choose.

稍後，亞希打開舊書包，一針一線地看。前袋比普通鉛筆袋寬，有塊鬆布蓋，能讓空氣流通。外婆說製包人把這種版型用在許多小禮物上，不能因此證明她知道龍的存在。亞希把這句話記下，因為他開始察覺願望多容易變成斷言。諾諾走進袋子待了一會兒，又爬出來。牠似乎記得這地方，也喜歡敞開的小籃子。亞希決定兩個都留著，讓小龍自己選。

That night the children made a small plan. First they would visit the public street, then look at the old tree place from a safe distance. They would not enter private land. Yui drew a little red heart on the corner of the map, and Aki put the card inside a clean cover. Grandmother gave Nono a gentle treat: a soft new cloth for its basket. Nono liked the cloth more than the sweet food. Aki watched it turn three times before lying down. The journey had not begun, but the bag's first owner had given them a real clue. He went to bed feeling that the world was wider than yesterday.

那晚，孩子們訂下小計畫：先走到公開的街道，再從安全距離看看舊樹的位置，不進入私人土地。優衣在地圖角落畫上一顆小紅心，亞希把卡片裝進乾淨的保護套。外婆也送諾諾一塊柔軟的新布當禮物。小龍比起甜食更喜歡那塊布，轉了三圈才躺下。旅程還沒開始，書包的第一位主人卻提供了真線索。亞希上床時覺得，今天的世界比昨天大了很多。
