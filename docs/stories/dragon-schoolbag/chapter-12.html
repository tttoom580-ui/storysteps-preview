「家」這個字

The Word Home

我的書包住著一隻迷你龍 · 第 12 / 25 章 · 1000以下 · 英中對照 · 校訂中

用互動版查詞、朗讀與收藏

Aki took the little dictionary to Ms. Ono after class. He did not show her Nono yet, but his mother had already spoken with the school about the unusual creature. “I have an answer to one question,” he said. “It wants to learn, and it can pick words by touching pictures.” Ms. Ono looked at the pages. “That is a kind of language,” she said. “It is also a way to say no. A small body should not mean a person or animal has no choice.” Aki looked down at his own hands. He had always been able to speak for himself. Nono had to work much harder just to ask for water.

下課後，亞希把小字典拿給小野老師看。雖然還沒帶諾諾來，媽媽已告知學校有這隻特殊的小生物。「我找到一個問題的答案，」他說，「牠想學習，也能碰圖來選字。」小野老師看過紙頁後說：「這也是一種語言，而且能用來表達拒絕。身體很小，不代表一個人或動物沒有選擇權。」亞希看看自己的雙手。他一直能替自己說話，諾諾卻得努力很久，才能說出想喝水這麼簡單的需要。

The next lesson was about the planet Earth. Students drew spring winds, hills, rivers, and the paths a bicycle could take. Yui made a picture of a bike with a bright bell. Aki drew a map from the book shop to Three Paths. He added a second path so they could avoid a busy road. “That route is mine to check with Mom,” he wrote, “not Nono's to travel alone.” Ms. Ono told the class that a map was useful only when people understood its limits. Some roads were old; some were new; some were closed for work. Aki put a question mark beside the tree.

下一堂課談的是地球。學生畫出春風、山坡、河流和單車能行走的路。優衣畫了一輛有亮色車鈴的腳踏車。亞希則畫出書店到三路口的地圖，另加一條路，以避開車多的街道。他在旁註明：「路線要跟媽媽確認，不能讓諾諾自己走。」小野老師提醒全班，地圖只有在知道限制時才有用：有的道路老了，有的新建了，也有的正在施工。亞希在老樹旁畫了一個問號。

That night Nono learned another useful word. Aki put the cards “home,” “school,” and “rest” in a row. Nono touched home, then school, and finally home again. “You want two places?” Aki asked. Nono made two taps, then bowed its head. The dragon looked sad. Aki placed his own hand beside the cards, not over them. “I wish I knew exactly what you mean,” he said. “We can keep asking. You do not have to choose right now.” His mother listened from the doorway. “Soon we will visit Three Paths,” she promised, “but we still need good weather and permission to use the public path.”

那晚，諾諾又學會一個重要的字。亞希把「家」、「學校」、「休息」排成一列。小龍先碰家，再碰學校，最後又碰家。「你想要兩個地方嗎？」亞希問。兩聲敲擊，但諾諾低下了頭，看起來有些難過。亞希把手放在字卡旁，沒有擋住牠。「我希望能完全懂你的意思，」他說，「我們可以慢慢問，你不用立刻選。」媽媽在門邊聽著。「我們很快會去三路口，」她答應，「但還要看天氣，並確認能走公開通道。」

Yui came over wearing blue jeans and carrying a notebook full of pictures. She had drawn a bicycle, a house, and a small tree. When Nono appeared, she gave a polite bow rather than reaching toward it. Aki asked whether the dragon wanted to try the picture game again. It touched yes. They used two pages, then stopped to listen to the wind outside. Nono looked at the moving curtain as if it remembered another place. Yui did not ask it to explain before it was ready. She placed the home card near the window and let the dragon come to it when it wanted.

優衣穿著藍色牛仔褲來訪，手裡拿著畫滿圖片的筆記本。她畫了腳踏車、房子和小樹。諾諾出現時，她先禮貌地鞠躬，沒有伸手抓牠。亞希問小龍願不願意再玩圖卡遊戲，牠碰了「願意」。他們用了兩頁圖，就停下來聽外頭的風。諾諾望著擺動的窗簾，像是想起另一個地方。優衣沒有逼牠立刻解釋，只把「家」卡放在窗邊，讓牠想來時自己靠近。

After the lesson, Aki and Yui compared the old map with a new public street map. Some lines had changed, and a road that looked short on the old page was now closed to walkers. Aki marked it with a red cross. He was learning that even a correct picture could become wrong when the world changed. His mother checked their route again before agreeing to a visit. Nono touched a picture of a tree, but it did not touch the closed road. Aki smiled. “We can learn a new way without forgetting the old one,” he said. The dragon sat down beside the map and rested.

課後，亞希和優衣把舊地圖與新的公開街道地圖相比。幾條線已經改變，一條在舊圖上很短的小路，如今不准行人通行。亞希畫了紅叉。他發現即使圖畫原本正確，世界改變後也可能不再適用。媽媽又確認一次路線，才同意探訪。諾諾碰了樹的圖片，卻沒碰封閉道路。亞希笑說：「我們可以學新路，也不用忘記舊路。」小龍坐在地圖旁，開始休息。

At dinner Ryu reached for the salt, and Nono reached for a picture card at the same time. Aki nearly mixed them up and laughed. “One is for soup, one is for words,” he said. Ryu had not met the dragon, so Aki put the card away and asked his mother when it would be right to tell him. “When Nono agrees and we can keep both of them calm,” she said. “Your brother is young. We do not need a surprise today.” Aki nodded. He had learned that a good secret was not simply something hidden. Sometimes it was a piece of news that needed the right time and a careful, kind way to tell.

晚餐時，龍也伸手拿鹽罐，諾諾同時碰向字卡，亞希差點把兩個動作搞混，忍不住笑了。「一個給湯用，一個給語言用。」他說。弟弟還沒見過小龍，亞希先收好卡片，再問媽媽什麼時候可以說。「等諾諾同意，而且我們能讓兩邊都安心時，」媽媽說，「弟弟還小，今天不用突然讓他嚇一跳。」亞希點頭。他慢慢明白，好好保守祕密不是一味隱藏，有時是等到適合的時間，用溫柔、謹慎的方法說出真相。
