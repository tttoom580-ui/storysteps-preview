上學路上的第一課

The Road to School

我的書包住著一隻迷你龍 · 第 3 / 25 章 · 1000以下 · 英中對照 · 校訂中

用互動版查詞、朗讀與收藏

On Tuesday, rain fell before Aki left home. He put a clean cloth beside Nono and checked the open air window. “The road will be wet,” he said. “We must stay on the safe side.” Nono pointed at a picture in Aki's book. It showed a bird flying above a beach. Then it touched a drawing of a bridge. Aki wondered whether the dragon had been born near water, or whether it simply liked pictures of open places. He did not know enough to say. “I can tell you about the town,” he offered. “You can tell me about your home when you are ready.”

星期二早上，亞希還沒出門就下雨了。他替諾諾放上一塊乾淨的布，確認透氣窗打開。「道路會很濕，」他說，「我們得走安全的一側。」諾諾指向書裡的圖片：一隻鳥飛過海灘，旁邊還畫著一座橋。亞希不知道牠是不是生在水邊，還是單純喜歡空曠的地方。他沒有足夠證據，便不亂猜。「我可以先介紹這座小鎮，」他說，「你準備好時，再告訴我你的家。」

They walked past a bus station. A long line of people waited under a roof while water ran along the street. Aki kept the bag close and did not run. One shoe slipped a little on the road, so he stopped and held the rail. “This is why we follow the white line,” he whispered. “It keeps us away from cars.” Nono watched a woman pull a cart through a puddle. The dragon pressed against the cloth, trying to help, but there was nothing it could safely do. Aki understood that wanting to help and knowing how to help were different things.

他們走過公車站。一長排人在屋簷下等車，雨水沿著街道流。亞希把書包拿穩，不敢奔跑。一隻鞋在濕地上微微打滑，他便停下來扶住欄杆。「這就是我們要沿著白線走的原因，」他輕聲說，「可以離車子遠一點。」諾諾看到一位女士正拖著推車穿過水窪，立刻靠向袋口，像想幫忙。但牠沒有安全的辦法。亞希明白，想幫忙和知道怎樣幫忙是兩回事。

At the corner they met a man with a picture of a cow on his umbrella. Nono almost climbed out to look. Aki lowered the bag and touched the side pocket gently. “Not here,” he said. “There are too many feet.” The dragon moved back. Aki did not want every new sound to become a danger, so he began explaining each one. A bus door opened; a bicycle bell rang; someone laughed under a yellow raincoat. Nothing terrible happened. Nono became quiet. It was learning that a loud town did not always mean an angry town.

轉角處，有個男人拿著印了乳牛圖案的雨傘。諾諾差點爬出來看。亞希放低書包，輕碰側袋。「這裡不行，」他說，「腳步太多了。」小龍退了回去。亞希不想讓每一種新聲音都成為恐懼，於是逐個解釋：公車門打開、單車鈴響、有人在黃色雨衣下笑出聲。沒有可怕的事發生。諾諾慢慢安靜，開始明白吵鬧的城鎮不一定代表大家生氣。

In class, the teacher set a small table near the window for a map lesson. Aki had to draw how people got from their homes to school. He drew every turn he could remember: the station, the bridge, the market, and the side road beside the park. He made a small mark for places where people should be careful. Nono watched from behind his open notebook. When Aki drew a blue square for home, the dragon touched it. Then it touched the empty edge of the paper, far beyond the school. Aki had a question, but he waited. Some stories need time before they can be told.

課堂上，老師在窗邊的小桌安排地圖練習。亞希得畫出從家到學校的路。他畫出記得的每一個轉彎：車站、橋、市場，還有公園旁的小路。他也在需要小心的地方做記號。諾諾躲在打開的筆記本後面看。亞希畫出代表家的藍色方格時，小龍碰了一下，又碰向紙張遠離學校的空白角落。亞希很想問，但忍住了。有些故事需要等對方願意才說得出口。

At lunch Aki tried an easy map game. He drew a little square for the classroom and another for the library. Then he drew a line into the hall and asked Nono to point to the place with books. The dragon studied the page for a long time before touching the right square. Aki almost cheered, but he remembered they were still in a classroom. He only nodded. Nono repeated the choice without help. That tiny act made Aki feel proud, yet it also raised a question: Was the dragon learning the map, or simply following his finger? He decided to test that gently another day, not to claim too much from one answer.

午餐時，亞希試著玩簡單的地圖遊戲。他畫了一個代表教室的方格，又畫了圖書館的方格，再把線延伸進走廊，請諾諾指出有書的地方。小龍看了很久，最後碰對了。亞希差點歡呼，但想到還在教室，只輕輕點頭。諾諾不需要幫忙又選了一次。這個小動作讓亞希很驕傲，也讓他想到一個問題：小龍真的學會看地圖了，還是只是在跟著他的手指？他決定改天溫柔地再試一次，不用一個答案就宣稱太多。

At the end of the day, a teacher asked what might happen if rain covered the white road line. Students offered many answers. Aki said, “We should slow down and look. We should not guess where the cars are.” His teacher nodded. “Very good. A map helps, but your eyes and care help, too.” In the bag, Nono tapped twice. Aki smiled at the sound without looking down. On the way home, he showed the little dragon the river from a dry place under a roof. Its eyes followed the moving water. Then Nono made a mark in the dust: a circle with three short lines. The first clue about its home had arrived.

放學前，老師問：如果雨水蓋住路上的白線，該怎麼辦？同學們提出許多答案。亞希說：「應該放慢腳步、仔細看，不可以猜車在哪裡。」老師點頭：「地圖很有幫助，但眼睛和小心也很重要。」書包裡傳出兩下敲擊，亞希沒有低頭，卻笑了。回家路上，他站在乾燥的屋簷下讓諾諾看河水。小龍的眼睛追著水流，接著在灰塵上畫出一個圓和三條短線。第一個關於牠故鄉的線索出現了。
