諾諾的小字典

A Dictionary for Nono

我的書包住著一隻迷你龍 · 第 11 / 25 章 · 1000以下 · 英中對照 · 校訂中

On Monday, the weather turned cold again. Aki wore blue pants and a warm coat to school. At the gate, a car stopped so a nurse could help a child cross safely. Aki watched the nurse and thought about how many kinds of help a town needed. In class, Ms. Ono told them to make a small dictionary for someone who was learning new words. “Maybe a younger brother, maybe a friend,” she said. “Choose words that person can use.” Aki did not have to think for long. He knew a tiny learner who could point at pictures but had trouble telling people what it wanted.

星期一，天氣又冷了。亞希穿上藍色長褲和厚外套上學。校門口有輛車停下來，一位護理師協助孩子安全過馬路。亞希看著她，想到一座小鎮需要許多不同的幫助。課堂上，小野老師要大家替正在學新字的人做一本小字典。「可以是弟弟，也可以是朋友，」她說，「選對方用得上的字。」亞希不必想很久。他認識一位身形迷你、能指圖卻還不擅長表達需要的學習者。

He chose ten words: water, food, rest, home, yes, no, hot, cold, help, and stop. Yui drew clear pictures beside each word. Aki had wanted a thousand pages at first, but Yui shook her head. “Ten useful pages are better than a huge book nobody can carry,” she said. Ms. Ono came by and asked why the pictures were so large. “The reader has very small hands,” Aki replied. That was true, although not the whole answer. The teacher said large letters could help many people. She showed them how to write one simple sentence under each picture. Aki was pleased that the project could help others, too.

他選了十個字：水、食物、休息、家、願意、不要、熱、冷、幫忙、停止。優衣在每個字旁畫上清楚的圖。亞希起初想做一千頁，優衣卻搖頭：「十頁真正有用的，比沒人拿得動的大書好。」小野老師經過，問圖為什麼畫得這麼大。「讀的人手很小，」亞希回答。這是真話，雖然不是全部。老師說大字也能幫到其他人，還教他們在每張圖下寫一句簡單的句子。亞希很高興這份作品不只對諾諾有幫助。

At home, his mother was cooking soup while a small fan moved warm air through the kitchen. Nono sat under the table in its open basket and listened to the spoon hit the pot. Aki set the dictionary in front of it. “Call me if you need help,” he said, then stopped. Nono could not make a loud call. “No,” Aki corrected himself. “Tap the help picture.” Nono placed a foot on the picture, then another on the food page. Aki gave it a safe little piece of cooked vegetable. The dragon seemed very proud of its new way to ask.

回家時，媽媽正在煮湯，小風扇讓廚房的暖空氣慢慢流動。諾諾坐在桌下敞開的小籃子裡，聽著湯匙碰鍋子的聲音。亞希把小字典放到牠面前。「需要幫忙就叫我。」他說完又停住。諾諾沒辦法大聲叫。「不對，」他改口，「可以按『幫忙』那張圖。」諾諾先用腳碰了幫忙，再碰食物。亞希給牠一小片適合吃的熟蔬菜。小龍似乎對新學會的表達方式很自豪。

While they worked, Aki learned that a dictionary was not simply a list of hard words. It was a way to make a message clearer. He had once used the word “cook” when he meant “eat,” and Nono had pointed to the wrong card. He changed the picture and tried again. Yui suggested testing the pictures without reading the words aloud, to see whether Nono could use them on its own. Aki agreed. The dragon chose the water picture when it was thirsty, even when no one pointed at it first. This was a small success, but it mattered more to Aki than winning a game.

製作字典時，亞希發現它不只是難字的清單，而是讓訊息更清楚的工具。有一次他把「煮飯」當成「吃飯」使用，諾諾便指錯了圖。他修改圖片，再試一次。優衣建議不念出文字，看看諾諾能不能自己使用圖卡。亞希同意。小龍口渴時，沒有人先指圖，牠仍然自己選了「水」。這是小小的成功，對亞希來說卻比贏得遊戲更重要。

His mother asked Aki to help make labels for the kitchen shelves. The family sometimes put cups and bowls in the wrong place, and Ryu was still learning to read. Aki used the same clear style that he had chosen for Nono. Soon the shelves had pictures of cups, plates, and spoons. Ryu could find his own cup without asking. Aki was surprised that a lesson meant for a dragon could help his brother, too. He began to see that good ways of learning were not a special gift for one student. They could make the house easier for everyone who lived there.

媽媽請亞希替廚房架子做標籤。家人有時會把杯碗放錯地方，龍也也還在學認字。亞希用替諾諾挑選的清楚圖案，幫架子貼上杯、盤、湯匙的圖片。很快，龍也不用問就能找到自己的杯子。亞希很驚訝，原本替小龍做的學習工具，也能幫助弟弟。他逐漸明白好的學習方法不是只送給某一位學生的特別禮物，而是能讓同住一個家的人都更方便。

Grandmother called that evening with news from the past. She had found a photograph of the old tree near Three Paths. A small temple had once stood on the next street, and a school secretary used to keep a box of local pictures there. The temple was gone now, but the pictures might be in the town office. “Probably not a dragon picture,” Grandmother warned, “but it may show where the tree stood.” Aki wrote the idea in his notebook. Then Nono pushed the page marked home toward him and tapped twice. Aki smiled. He could not promise to find the dragon's family, but he could promise not to stop listening.

傍晚，外婆打電話帶來一個舊消息。她找到一張三路口老樹的照片。那附近以前有座小寺廟，一位學校祕書曾保管當地的老照片。寺廟現在不在了，但照片可能移到鎮公所。「大概不會有龍的照片，」外婆提醒，「但也許看得出樹原本在哪。」亞希把這消息記下。諾諾將標著「家」的字卡推到他面前，敲兩下。亞希笑了。他不能保證找到小龍家人，但能答應繼續好好聽牠說。
