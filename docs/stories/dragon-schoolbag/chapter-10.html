A Rule for Everyone

我的書包住著一隻迷你龍 · 第 10 / 25 章 · 1000以下 · 英中對照 · 校訂中

Aki's mother called Ms. Ono and spoke with her for half an hour. She did not send a photo online, and she did not ask the teacher to make a quick choice. She explained that Nono was small, alive, and able to answer with taps. Ms. Ono listened in a calm voice. “Our school needs rules that are right for every child,” she said. “A pet cannot simply come to class. We can still ask how to help this creature learn, without putting anyone at risk.” Aki heard only half the call from the hall, but he was glad his mother had begun the talk.

媽媽打電話給小野老師，談了半個小時。她沒有把照片傳到網路，也沒有要求老師立刻決定。她說明諾諾很小、是活生生的，而且能用敲擊回答。小野老師用平靜的聲音聽完。「學校的規則必須對每個孩子都公平，」她說，「不能隨便把寵物帶進教室，但我們可以一起想，怎樣既讓牠學習，也不讓任何人冒險。」亞希只在走廊聽到電話的一半，卻很高興媽媽已經展開對話。

At school the class made simple signs for the reading center. One sign showed an apple, another a pencil, and another a hand asking for help. Yui suggested a sign that a blind child could feel, with raised dots on a card. Ms. Ono liked the idea and said the school would ask an expert before making changes. “Good help begins by asking the person what works,” she said. Aki thought of Nono's two taps. Even when a friend was very small, it was better to ask than to guess. He wrote that sentence on his worksheet. It was the best thing he had learned that week.

學校裡，全班替閱讀角做簡單標示。有的畫蘋果，有的畫鉛筆，有的畫出求助的手。優衣建議做有凸點、讓失明的孩子也能摸讀的卡片。小野老師喜歡這個想法，並說學校會先請專家提供意見。「好的幫助要先問對方需要什麼，」老師說。亞希想到諾諾的兩聲敲擊。朋友再小，也應該先問而不是猜。他把這句話寫在學習單上，覺得這是整週最重要的一課。

On the playground, Aki's little brother Ryu came to meet him with their mother. Ryu was kicking a ball too hard and nearly hit a net beside the fence. Aki stopped him. “Not between the children,” he said. “Go to the open area.” Ryu looked upset, then saw the safe place and went there. Afterward Aki offered him a piece of ice from the water bottle to cool his warm hand. Ryu asked why Aki still loved the old blue bag so much. Aki smiled. “It has been with me a long time,” he answered. That much was true.

操場上，亞希的弟弟龍也跟媽媽來接他。龍也把球踢得太用力，差點打到圍欄旁的球網。亞希攔住他：「別往小朋友中間踢，去空的地方。」龍也有點不高興，但看到安全的空地便走過去。活動後，亞希從水瓶拿出一小塊冰，讓弟弟替熱熱的手降溫。龍也問哥哥為什麼這麼愛那個舊藍書包。亞希笑說：「它陪我很久了。」這倒是真話。

Aki and Yui spent their break making a second set of cards. They added a picture of a foot for “walk,” a ball for “play,” and a face with closed eyes for “sleep.” Ryu came into the room and wanted to kick the paper ball picture across the floor. Aki laughed and asked him not to. “It is a learning card,” he said, “not a real ball.” Ryu helped by drawing a sun on another card. “I love that my brother can help without knowing every part of the secret,” Aki thought. They decided to spend the next ten minutes checking the pictures, and Yui said the new set was easier to see.

亞希和優衣利用休息時間做第二套圖卡，加上代表「走路」的腳、代表「玩耍」的球，以及閉上眼睛的「睡覺」臉。龍也跑進房間，想把紙上的球圖踢過地板，亞希笑著請他別這麼做。「那是學習卡，不是真的球。」龍也幫忙在另一張卡畫太陽。亞希很喜歡弟弟即使不知道祕密的全部，也能提供幫助。他們又花了十分鐘確認圖片，優衣說這套更容易看懂。

That evening Aki took the cards home and placed them beside Nono. The dragon selected “play,” then “stop” after a few minutes. Aki obeyed at once. He had once believed teaching meant giving another person as much information as possible. Now he saw that a learner might need control over when a lesson began and ended. His mother asked what he had learned at school. “That a right choice can be simple,” Aki said. “I can stop when Nono says stop.” His mother nodded. The next step, she reminded him, was to find out what the school needed to make a safe visit possible.

傍晚，亞希把圖卡放在諾諾身旁。小龍先選「玩耍」，過幾分鐘又選「停止」，亞希立刻照做。他以前以為教學就是盡量把知識給別人，現在才知道學習者也需要決定何時開始和結束。媽媽問他今天在學校學到什麼。「對的選擇可以很簡單，」亞希說，「諾諾說停，我就停。」媽媽點頭，提醒他下一步是弄清楚學校需要哪些條件，才能安排安全探訪。

That evening Nono watched Aki draw letters at the table. The dragon could follow the line with one tiny finger, but it could not hold a normal pencil. Aki did not force it. He made a large letter card and let Nono point. For the first time, it chose the word “home” from three cards. Then it tapped on the map. “You want us to go there,” Aki said. Two taps. “We will, with Mom, when the weather is good.” Nono rested its head on the card. Aki knew the trip would not be simple, yet he also knew it mattered. Their way forward began with a word the dragon could finally show.

那晚，諾諾在桌邊看亞希寫字。牠能用小手指沿著線走，卻拿不動一般鉛筆，亞希沒有勉強，而是做了大字卡，讓牠用指的。第一次，諾諾從三張卡片裡選出「家」，接著敲敲地圖。「你想讓我們去那裡。」亞希說。兩聲敲擊。「天氣好時，我們會和媽媽一起去。」諾諾把頭靠在字卡上。亞希知道這趟旅程不一定簡單，但也知道它很重要。現在，小龍終於能指出自己最在意的那個字。
