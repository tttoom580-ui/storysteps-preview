The Place Between

Two days later, a music teacher named Ada came to see the festival cards. Ada was blind, and she used a white cane to find her way. Her brother walked beside her but let her choose where to go. "I love hearing the hill bell," Ada said. "I do not need to stand right next to the tower. I need a safe way to reach a good listening place." Erin showed her the map and the Little Lookout between the garden and the road. "The path is still rough," Erin admitted. "Would you like to help us think about it?" Ada said yes, and she asked to visit it in daylight.

兩天後，一位名叫艾達的音樂老師來看節慶卡片。艾達是視障者，使用白手杖辨認路況。她哥哥走在旁邊，仍讓她自己決定往哪裡走。「我喜歡聽山上的鈴聲，」艾達說。「我不需要站到塔旁，只需要一條安全的路，走到適合聽聲音的地方。」艾琳拿出地圖，指給她看菜園與道路中間的小小觀景點。「那條路還不平，」艾琳坦白說。「妳願意幫我們想想嗎？」艾達答應，請他們安排白天實地走一趟。

At the lookout, Ada stopped after only half the path. Her cane had hit a loose stone, and the ground changed under her feet. "This is not right yet," she said. Erin took a pencil and marked the place on the map. Ben offered to kick the stone away. "Please leave it," Ada told him. "First we need to know where people will walk. Moving one stone may not solve the problem." The teacher asked how much money was left for the path. "We have spent nothing on a new path yet," Erin said. "We are still learning what it needs."

到了觀景點，艾達走到一半就停下來。她的手杖碰到鬆動的石頭，腳下的地面也突然變了。她說：「這裡目前還不適合走。」艾琳拿鉛筆在地圖上標出位置。班說他可以把石頭踢開。「請先不要動，」艾達說。「我們要先確認人們會走哪裡。移開一塊石頭，不一定能解決問題。」艾達問整條路還有多少預算。「我們目前還沒有花錢鋪新路，」艾琳說。「我們還在了解需要做什麼。」

They sat at the lower half of the path, where the ground was level. Ada heard the bell from there. "The sound is clear," she said. "I think this could be the center of a listening space." Ben asked whether an online map would show the path. Erin said some maps might, but a real visit mattered more. An apple fell from a nearby tree, landing softly in the grass. "It is a simple place," Mrs. May said, "but we can make it welcoming." Ada smiled. "And please ask people who will use it before deciding what is best for them."

他們坐在小路下半段地勢平坦的地方，艾達從那裡聽見了鈴聲。「聲音很清楚，」她說。「我想這裡可以成為聆聽空間的中心。」班問網路地圖上會不會標出這條路。艾琳說可能會，但實際走過更重要。附近一棵樹掉下一顆蘋果，輕輕落在草地上。「這地方很簡單，」梅太太說，「但我們能讓它更歡迎大家。」艾達微笑著補充：「而且在決定什麼對使用者最好之前，請先問問他們。」

Ada then gave Ben a small exercise. "Close your eyes for half a minute," she said, "and tell me what you hear. Keep your feet still." Ben heard a bird, a far car, the moving leaves, and the bell. He also heard his own breath. "There are more sounds than I thought," he whispered. Ada told him he had used his ears in a different way. Erin felt that the project had changed shape. They had begun with a broken machine on a hill. Now they were thinking about how many kinds of people could enjoy the hill without going inside it.

接著艾達請班做一個小練習。「把眼睛閉上半分鐘，」她說，「告訴我你聽見什麼。腳不要動。」班聽到鳥叫、遠處的車聲、樹葉聲和鈴聲，也聽到自己的呼吸。「聲音比我想的還多，」他輕聲說。艾達告訴他，這次他用不同的方法運用了耳朵。艾琳感覺整個計畫有了新的方向。他們原本只是想處理山上一台壞掉的機器，如今卻開始思考，不同的人如何在不進塔的情況下享受山坡。

As the group rested, Ada spoke in a gentle voice about a winter visit she had made to a lake. Ice had covered the shore, and her brother had wanted to walk across it. She had asked him to stay on the marked path instead. "A lovely place can still have dangers we cannot see," she said. Ben pointed to the net around a nearby garden bed and asked whether it might help mark the lookout. Erin said they needed a proper rail, not a light net that could fall. She would rather spend money on a safe path than on pretty signs that did not protect anyone. Ada thanked her. "Good design begins with real people," she said. "Listen to what we need, and the place will tell you how it should work."

大家休息時，艾達用溫和的聲音說起一次冬日湖邊旅行。當時岸邊結了冰，她哥哥想走上冰面，她請他留在有標示的小路上。「美麗的地方，也可能藏著我們看不見的危險，」她說。班指向附近菜圃外的網子，問能不能用來標示觀景點。艾琳說，需要的是正式欄杆，而不是容易倒下的輕網。她寧願把錢花在安全道路，也不想只買看起來漂亮卻保護不了人的標誌。艾達向她道謝。「好的設計從真實的人開始，」她說。「聽懂我們的需求，才會知道地方應該怎麼使用。」

On the way back, Erin thanked Ada for telling them what did not work. "People sometimes tell me only nice things," she said. Ada shook her head. "Good friends can tell you a hard truth. That is one way to show love." Erin wrote this sentence beside the map. She did not know whether the town would pay for a whole new path, but she knew that a listening space could not be called ready while someone was stopped by a loose stone. They would bring the idea to the next meeting, with a clear picture and no easy promises.

回程時，艾琳謝謝艾達指出那些不適用的地方。「大家有時候只對我說好聽的話，」她說。艾達搖頭。「好朋友也會告訴妳難聽的事。那也是表達關心的方法。」艾琳把這句話寫在地圖旁。她不知道鎮上會不會願意出錢重鋪整條路，但她知道，只要有人會被鬆動的石頭擋住，就不能把聆聽空間稱作準備完成。他們要帶著清楚的圖，而不是輕率的承諾，去下一次會議。
