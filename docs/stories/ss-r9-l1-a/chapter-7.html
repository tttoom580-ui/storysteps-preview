等待的聲音

The Sound of Waiting

山頂上的藍色風車 · 第 7 / 25 章 · 1000以下 · 英中對照 · 校訂中

用互動版查詞、朗讀與收藏

The day after the meeting was quiet. Erin woke in her bedroom and looked at the clock. The windmill was still closed, and she could not make the inspection happen sooner. Mrs. May had left a note in the yard: Good work takes time. Erin read it twice. She believed that, but she did not like waiting. Ben came over with a drawing of a rainbow above the hill. "I had a dream that the arms were turning," he said. Erin laughed gently. "I have that dream too. But nature decides how much wind we get, and people must decide what is safe. Those are two different things."

會議隔天十分安靜。艾琳在臥室醒來，看了一眼時鐘。風車依然封閉，她沒辦法讓檢查提早發生。梅太太在院子裡留了一張紙條：「好的工作需要時間。」艾琳讀了兩次。她相信這句話，卻不喜歡等待。班帶著一張畫過來，畫上是山坡上方的彩虹。「我夢見風車葉片在轉，」他說。艾琳輕輕笑了。「我也有過這種夢。但自然決定有多少風，人們決定什麼是安全的。那是兩件不同的事。」

They went to see Mr. Han, who was repairing a clock in his yard. He did not open the windmill, and he did not have a secret key to its problems. Instead, he let Ben listen to a small clock on his table. "Can you hear the ticking?" he asked. "A little noise tells you the parts are moving. But the sound alone cannot tell you whether the clock is correct." Erin understood. A turning windmill might look wonderful, but a nice sight would not prove that its roof, arms, and brakes were safe. Mr. Han was proud of the old tower, and careful about it too.

他們去找韓爺爺。他正在院子裡修一個時鐘。他沒有打開風車，也沒有能解決問題的秘密鑰匙。相反地，他讓班聽桌上一個小時鐘的聲音。「聽得見滴答聲嗎？」他問。「一點點聲音表示零件在動，但只有聲音，不能證明時鐘走得準。」艾琳明白了。風車轉動時或許很美，卻不能因此證明屋頂、葉片和煞車都是安全的。韓爺爺以老風車為榮，同時也十分小心。

Mira called with an error she had found in the old book. One number for the water tank was too high. She had checked a second page and discovered that someone had written the same day twice. "It was just a mistake," she said. "But it changes the answer." Erin thanked her. She crossed out the wrong total and wrote the new one clearly, with a note showing why it had changed. Ben asked whether people would be angry when they saw an error. "Some may be," Erin answered. "But we cannot build a good plan on a number we know is wrong."

米拉打電話來，說她在舊紀錄簿裡發現一個錯誤。水槽的一項數字太高，她查了另一頁，才發現有人把同一天記了兩次。「這只是個失誤，」她說。「但它會影響答案。」艾琳向她道謝，把錯誤的總數劃掉，清楚寫上新數字，並註明修改原因。班問，大家看見錯誤會不會生氣。「有些人可能會，」艾琳回答。「但既然知道數字錯了，就不能繼續用它制定計畫。」

At lunch, they sat outside under a bright sky. A rich visitor from another town stopped to ask whether the windmill would soon open. He offered money if the town would put his name on the door. Erin said that was a question for the whole town, not for her. "We have not even decided what the door will lead to," she explained. The visitor nodded and said he would wait for a public plan. Ben looked pleased. "You did not let him buy the answer," he said. Erin shook her head. "He asked a fair question. We gave him the answer we have today."

午餐時，他們坐在明亮天空下的戶外。一位來自外鎮的富有訪客停下來，問風車是不是快開放了。他願意捐錢，條件是把他的名字放在門上。艾琳說，那應由全鎮共同討論，不能由她決定。「我們甚至還沒決定那扇門以後通往什麼地方，」她解釋。訪客點頭，說他會等公開計畫出來。班很高興。「妳沒有讓他用錢買走答案，」他說。艾琳搖頭。「他提出合理的問題，我們只是說出今天能確定的答案。」

As they cleaned up the clock parts, Ben asked Mr. Han whether he could close the box himself. Mr. Han showed him where each piece belonged, then let him try. "I believe you can do it if you take your time," he said. Ben put the pieces away carefully. One small spring slipped from his fingers, and the three of them began to laugh. It was not a serious error; they found the spring on the table and put it in its place. Erin realized that careful work did not require every person to look worried. People could be proud of good work and still enjoy doing it together. Later, she wrote a new note for her folder: Check the facts; leave room for joy. She wanted to remember that on difficult days.

收拾時鐘零件時，班問韓爺爺能不能自己把盒子關好。韓爺爺教他每個零件的位置，再讓他試。「我相信你慢慢做就能做好，」他說。班小心收好零件，但一個小彈簧從手指間滑走，三個人都笑了出來。這不是嚴重的錯誤；他們在桌上找到彈簧，放回正確位置。艾琳發現，仔細工作不代表每個人都得滿臉擔憂。大家可以為好工作感到驕傲，也能享受合作的過程。稍後她在資料夾寫下一句：「核對事實，也給快樂留點空間。」她希望在困難的日子記得這句話。

By evening, the quiet day felt useful after all. Erin had corrected a number, answered a visitor, and learned something from a small clock. She could not know whether tomorrow would bring rain or sunshine, or whether the inspector would find good news. She could choose how to use the time before then. She made a copy of her notes for Nora and left it in the town office. On the way back, the bell sounded over the yard. Erin looked at the dark hill and felt ready to hear whatever the inspection would say.

到了傍晚，這個安靜的日子竟也很有收穫。艾琳修正了一個數字，回答了一位訪客，還從小時鐘學到一件事。她無法確定明天會下雨還是放晴，也不知道檢查員會帶來好消息或壞消息。但她能決定現在如何運用時間。她把筆記影印一份送到鎮公所。回家路上，鈴聲傳過院子。艾琳望著黑暗中的山坡，覺得自己已經準備好聽取任何檢查結果。
