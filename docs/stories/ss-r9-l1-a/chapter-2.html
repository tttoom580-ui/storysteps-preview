小小數字的紀錄

The Book of Small Numbers

山頂上的藍色風車 · 第 2 / 25 章 · 1000以下 · 英中對照

The next morning Erin returned to Mrs. May's kitchen with a question: how much power had the windmill really made? Mrs. May said the answer was in a book at the village room. Ben offered to bring Erin there. His cousin Mira was also visiting that day. Mira worked at a science center in the south, where she showed children how simple machines worked. "A famous building can still have a small job," Mira said. "We must not make the old windmill seem stronger than it was." Erin liked that. The sky was sunny, and they walked along the river without hurrying. Ben carried a pencil and the little tool he used to open his drawing box.

隔天早晨，艾琳在梅太太的廚房提出一個問題：風車以前究竟能產生多少動力？梅太太說，村民活動室裡有本紀錄簿。班願意帶她去。他的表姊米拉也剛好來訪。米拉在南方的科學中心工作，常向孩子示範簡單機械。「有名的建築也可能只做小小的工作，」米拉說。「我們不能把舊風車說得比事實更厲害。」艾琳很認同。那天陽光明亮，他們沿著河邊慢慢走。班帶著鉛筆，還有打開畫具盒的小工具。

Inside the village room, Mr. Han showed them a thick blue book. He was Ben's grandfather, the man who rang the morning bell. He opened it with care. The first page showed his family standing beside a new windmill many years before. The next pages held numbers, dates, and a picture of a water tank. The windmill had once helped lift water to a high garden. Later, a small generator had made enough power for one lamp. It had never filled every house with light. "That is helpful to know," Erin said. "We can tell the truth when we ask the town for help." Mira copied the numbers onto clean paper.

在活動室裡，韓爺爺拿出一本厚厚的藍色紀錄簿。他是班的祖父，也是每天早上敲鈴的人。他小心地翻開書。第一頁是多年前他的家人站在新風車旁的照片。後面的幾頁寫著數字、日期，還畫了一座水槽。風車曾經協助把水送到高處的菜園；後來一部小發電機曾提供一盞燈所需的電力，從來沒有照亮每一戶人家。「知道真相很有幫助，」艾琳說。「我們向鎮上求助時，可以說實話。」米拉把數字抄在乾淨的紙上。

A little girl came in with her mother and asked about a video she had seen online. In it, a big windmill turned very fast and lit an entire street. Mira smiled. "That is a different kind of machine," she explained. "This village windmill is old, and its wooden parts need to be checked before anyone moves them." A guy at the next table said they should paint the arms and push them by hand. Erin bit her lip. She wanted to disagree without being rude. "But we do not know what holds those arms in place," she said. "A nice coat of paint will not make a weak part safe."

一位小女孩跟著媽媽走進來，問起她在網路上看過的影片。影片中的大風車轉得很快，能照亮整條街。米拉微笑著解釋：「那是不同的機器。村裡這座風車很老，木頭零件必須先檢查，才能讓它轉動。」隔壁桌的一名男子說，只要把葉片重新油漆，再用手推一推就好。艾琳抿了抿嘴唇，希望有禮貌地提出不同意見。「可是我們還不知道什麼東西固定著葉片，」她說。「漆得漂亮，也不代表脆弱的零件會變安全。」

Mira asked Ben to draw two boxes. In the first he drew a sheep drinking from a high garden trough. In the second he drew the lamp in the village room. "These were the two jobs," she said. "Water first, and a little light later." Ben used blue lines to show the water going up. Erin could not yet explain every wheel inside the tower, but she could see why the village had loved it. The work was small and clear. They decided to add one page to the book: a list of things they knew and things they did not know. They left space for new answers.

米拉請班畫兩個方框。第一個框裡，他畫了一隻羊在高處的菜園水槽旁喝水。第二個框裡，是活動室的一盞燈。「這就是兩項工作，」她說。「先是送水，後來提供一點照明。」班用藍線畫出水往上流的方向。艾琳還不能解釋塔裡每個輪子的用途，但她開始明白村民為何喜歡風車。它做的事情雖小，卻很實在。他們決定在紀錄簿裡加上一頁，分別列出已知和未知的事情，並留出空白，等待新的答案。

To make the new page easier to read, Mira drew a small chart. She told Ben to fill the empty spaces only after finding a number in the old book. The children had seen charts at school, but this one seemed different because it showed real work done by their grandparents. Mr. Han remembered carrying water up the hill when the machine needed repair. "Some days, people still had to use their own hands," he said. Erin wrote that memory beside the chart, marking it clearly as a personal story rather than a measured fact. She then placed the old book and the new page side by side. Anyone who visited the room could check where the numbers came from. That made the project feel less like a mystery and more like something the whole village could understand.

為了讓新頁面更容易閱讀，米拉畫了一張小圖表。她告訴班，只有在舊簿裡找到數字之後，才能填入空格。孩子們在學校也看過圖表，但這張不同，因為它記錄著祖父母那一代真實做過的工作。韓爺爺記得機器維修時，自己曾親手把水搬上山。「有些日子，大家還是得靠雙手工作，」他說。艾琳把這段回憶寫在圖表旁，清楚標記它是個人故事，不是測量數據。她把舊簿和新頁並排放好，讓任何訪客都能查到數字來源。這讓計畫不再像謎團，而是全村都能理解的事情。

Before they went home, Mrs. May brought tea and asked what they had found. Erin held up the new page instead of making a long speech. "We know what the windmill did," she said. "We do not know why it stopped, or what it would cost to make it safe. Those are our next questions." Mrs. May looked at the drawings. "Then we must not ask a family to pay for a promise," she said. "Let us learn the cost first." They agreed to visit the old river path the next day, but only from the public road. Erin wrote that rule in her notebook so nobody would forget it.

回家前，梅太太送來茶，問他們找到什麼。艾琳沒有長篇大論，而是舉起新寫的那一頁。「我們知道風車做過什麼，」她說。「但還不知道它為何停止，也不知道修到安全需要多少錢。這是接下來要回答的問題。」梅太太看著圖畫說：「那就不能請大家為一個承諾掏錢。我們要先知道花費。」他們約好隔天從公共道路查看舊河道，不走進封閉區。艾琳把這條規則寫進筆記本，免得有人忘記。
