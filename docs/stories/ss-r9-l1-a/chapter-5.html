No Race to the Top

山頂上的藍色風車 · 第 5 / 25 章 · 1000以下 · 英中對照

用互動版查詞、朗讀與收藏

A thin layer of snow fell during the night, even though it was almost spring. In the morning, Ben wanted to race Erin to the top of the hill. "At least let me win once," he said. His long scarf moved in the wind. Erin pointed to a wet sign near the path. "No race today. The ground is not safe." Ben looked sorry. Mr. Han came out of his house and showed them a better place to stand. The path near the bean garden was flat, and it gave a clear view of the tower. A hen walked past them, followed by a small frog that jumped away at once.

夜裡下了一層薄雪，雖然已經快到春天。早上，班想和艾琳比賽，看誰先跑上山頂。「至少讓我贏一次嘛，」他說，長圍巾在風中飄動。艾琳指向小路旁潮濕的告示。「今天不比賽。地面不安全。」班露出失望的表情。韓爺爺從家裡走出來，帶他們到一個比較適合站立的位置。豆田旁的小路很平坦，能清楚看見塔身。一隻母雞從旁邊走過，一隻小青蛙又立刻跳開。

Ms. Cho, Ben's teacher, joined them with a measuring stick. She asked the children to guess the height of a small post, not the windmill. "A minute of careful looking can teach more than a fast climb," she said. They put the stick on level ground and marked the post's shadow. Ben wanted to dig a hole for it, but Erin asked him not to. "We are guests on this land. We do not change it without permission." The school had made a deal with the garden keeper: they could take notes, but they must leave the soil as they found it.

班的老師周老師帶著量尺加入。她請孩子們猜一根小木柱的高度，而不是風車的高度。「仔細看一分鐘，學到的可能比快速爬山還多，」她說。他們把尺放在平坦的地面，標出木柱影子的位置。班想挖個洞固定木柱，艾琳請他不要這麼做。「我們是來到這片土地的客人，沒有得到允許，就不能隨意改變它。」學校已和菜園管理員約好：可以做紀錄，但必須保持土壤原樣。

The windmill looked very close from this point, but the broken roof was still above their heads. Erin put one hand on her neck and looked away before it began to hurt. "We have been here for twenty minutes," she said. "That is enough for today." Ben made a face. "But we did not fix anything." Mira, who had come to collect their notes, answered, "We found a good place to watch the wind. That is a real result." Ms. Cho smiled. She wrote the word safe at the top of the class page. Every child put one observation under it.

從這裡看，風車似乎很近，但破損的屋頂仍高高在上。艾琳扶著脖子，避免一直抬頭而痠痛。「我們已經站了二十分鐘，」她說。「今天這樣就夠了。」班皺起臉。「可是我們什麼也沒修好。」米拉來收筆記時回答：「我們找到一個能安全觀察風的地方。這就是實際的成果。」周老師笑了，在全班的紀錄頁最上方寫下「安全」，請每個孩子在下面記一件觀察。

As they walked back, the snow began to melt. Water ran beside the path, and a frog called from the garden. Ben said he understood why Erin had stopped the race. A fast runner could still fall. "I am sorry I pushed you," he said. Erin told him there was nothing wrong with asking a question, but everyone needed to listen to the answer. "Put your drawing in the book," she added. "It shows where we can stand." Ben drew a thick line around the safe viewing place. He called it the Little Lookout, and the name stayed.

他們走回去時，雪開始融化，水沿著小路旁流下，菜園裡傳來青蛙的叫聲。班說，他現在明白艾琳為什麼不讓他比賽。跑得快的人也可能跌倒。「對不起，我一直催妳，」他說。艾琳告訴他，提問沒有錯，但每個人也要聽別人的回答。「把你的畫放進紀錄簿吧，」她又說。「這張圖能告訴大家該站在哪裡。」班在安全的觀景處外畫了一條粗線，取名「小小觀景點」，這個名字就此留下。

Although Ben had wanted an exciting morning, he began to like the quiet test. He asked Ms. Cho why they had used the shadow of a short post instead of measuring the tower. She explained that the short post was safe to reach and easy to check. "If our first answer is wrong," she said, "we can do the test again without putting anyone in danger." Erin asked the children to write one question they still had. Ben wrote about the wind; another child wrote about the roof. Nobody knew every answer, and nobody had to pretend. The snow left small wet circles on the garden stones. Erin showed the class how to notice a change without touching it. They would carry their questions to the village room and look for answers in books.

雖然班原本期待一個刺激的早晨，現在卻慢慢喜歡上這種安靜的測試。他問周老師，為什麼要量矮木柱的影子，不直接量風車。老師解釋，矮柱容易安全接近，也方便核對。「如果第一次答錯，」她說，「我們還能重新做，不會讓任何人陷入危險。」艾琳請孩子們各寫下一個尚未解開的問題。班寫風，另一個孩子寫屋頂。沒有人知道所有答案，也不需要假裝知道。雪在菜園石頭上留下小小的濕圈。艾琳教大家如何觀察變化，不必動手碰觸。他們要把問題帶回活動室，再從書裡找答案。

At dinner, Mrs. May asked whether the children had enjoyed their lesson. Erin said they had learned something that could not be bought in a store: patience. "A long job must be done one piece at a time," she said. Mr. Han agreed and told them about a day many years ago when a man had tried to work in bad weather. Everyone had asked him to stop, and he had listened. That choice had kept the team safe. Ben wrote the story beneath his drawing. He did not feel like a loser anymore. He was glad his map might help someone else next week.

晚餐時，梅太太問孩子們喜不喜歡今天的課。艾琳說，他們學到了一件在商店裡買不到的事：耐心。「很長的工作要一件一件完成，」她說。韓爺爺點頭，講起多年前有人想在壞天氣工作，大家勸他停下來，而他也聽了。那個決定讓整個隊伍平安。班把故事寫在自己的畫下面，不再覺得自己輸了。他很高興自己的地圖也許能在下週幫上別人的忙。
