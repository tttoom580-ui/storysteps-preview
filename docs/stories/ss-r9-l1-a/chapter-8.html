檢查員看見的事

What the Inspector Saw

山頂上的藍色風車 · 第 8 / 25 章 · 1000以下 · 英中對照 · 校訂完成

用互動版查詞、朗讀與收藏

Ms. Patel arrived with another inspector on a clear morning. Erin and a small group waited outside the fence. The inspectors wore work clothes, strong shoes, and safety hats. They checked the outside first, then entered the windmill through a door kept closed to visitors. Ben could not see what happened behind the wall, and he asked why the children had to stay in the park below. "An officer who checks a building must be able to work without people walking under it," Erin said. A girl in a blue dress stood beside Ben. "That sounds easy," she said, "but waiting is hard." Erin agreed. She wanted answers as much as the children did.

帕特爾女士和另一位檢查員在晴朗的早晨抵達。艾琳和一小群人在圍欄外等待。兩位檢查員穿著工作服、結實的鞋子，戴上安全帽。他們先檢查外部，再從禁止訪客進入的門走進風車。班看不到牆後面的情況，便問為什麼孩子必須待在下方的公園。「檢查建築的工作人員需要一個沒有人在下方走動的環境，」艾琳說。一個穿藍色洋裝的女孩站在班身旁。「聽起來很容易，」她說，「但等人真的很難。」艾琳同意。她和孩子一樣想知道答案。

While they waited, the children played a gentle baseball game on the grass. Ben was told not to hit the ball toward the hill. The girl, whose name was June, asked if the windmill had a ghost. "My brother says he heard a voice from inside," she said. Mr. Han told them the old tower sometimes made a sound when the wind found a narrow opening. "Different sounds can have simple reasons," he said. "But we still need someone to check where they come from." Erin listened. She remembered hearing a low whistle by the roof. It was not proof of a ghost, or proof that the building was safe.

等待時，孩子們在草地上玩輕鬆的棒球遊戲。班被提醒不能把球打向山坡。那個叫小君的女孩問風車裡是不是有鬼。「我哥哥說他聽見裡面有人說話，」她說。韓爺爺告訴他們，風穿過塔身狹窄的縫隙時，有時會發出聲音。「不同聲音可能有很簡單的原因，」他說。「不過我們仍需要人查清來源。」艾琳聽著，想起自己在屋頂旁也聽過低低的哨聲。那既不能證明有鬼，也不能證明建築安全。

At noon the inspectors came out for a break. Ms. Patel gave no quick answer. She asked everyone to give her team time to write a full report. Mrs. May had brought a small lunch of beef and vegetables, with bread for people who did not eat meat. She offered the workers water. "That is nice of you," one inspector said. The group ate outside, where they could taste the fresh air as well as their food. Erin noticed dust on Ms. Patel's gloves. She wanted to ask a hundred questions, but the inspector needed to finish her work first. Erin gave her room to do that.

中午，兩位檢查員出來休息。帕特爾沒有立刻給答案，而是請大家等團隊寫完完整報告。梅太太帶來牛肉、蔬菜和麵包，沒有吃肉的人也有東西可吃。她把水遞給工作人員。「妳真貼心，」一位檢查員說。大家在戶外用餐，除了食物，也能感受清新的空氣。艾琳注意到帕特爾手套上的灰塵，心裡有一百個問題，但檢查員必須先完成工作。艾琳決定讓她專心。

Near sunset, Ms. Patel spoke to Nora and Erin. The good news was that the main stone walls looked strong enough to study further. The bad news was clear too: one wooden support was damaged, the roof let in water, and the old brake could not be trusted. "Nobody should try to make the arms fly today," she said. Erin felt her heart sink, but she had asked for the truth. The full report would explain what could be repaired, what must stay closed, and what might cost too much. Ben asked why it was not a happy day. Erin answered, "Because today we learned how to protect tomorrow."

接近日落時，帕特爾向諾拉和艾琳說明初步結果。好消息是，主要石牆看起來仍夠結實，可以再做進一步評估。壞消息也很明確：一根木支柱受損、屋頂漏水，而且舊煞車不可靠。「今天誰都不該讓葉片轉起來，」她說。艾琳心裡一沉，但這正是她要求得到的真相。完整報告將說明哪些地方能修、哪些必須繼續封閉，以及哪些費用可能過高。班問今天為什麼不能算是快樂的一天。艾琳回答：「因為我們今天學會怎麼保護明天。」

When the inspectors went back inside, June asked whether she could draw a picture of the safe waiting place. Erin said yes and gave her a large sheet of paper. June drew the park, the fence, and the road. She placed a little girl behind the fence, then drew a dotted line showing where adults had asked everyone to stand. Ben added the bell and a tree. Neither child drew an open door, because no one had been allowed to enter. The picture helped a younger boy understand why his parents would not let him walk toward the tower. Erin watched the children share their drawing and felt a small hope rise. Even on a day filled with uncertain news, they had made something useful, clear, and kind.

檢查員回到塔裡後，小君問能不能畫出安全的等候位置。艾琳答應，給她一大張紙。小君畫了公園、圍欄和道路，在圍欄後畫上一個小女孩，又用虛線標出大人請大家站的位置。班加上鈴鐺和樹。他們都沒有畫開著的塔門，因為今天沒有人被允許進入。這張圖讓一個更小的男孩明白，父母為什麼不讓他往塔那邊走。艾琳看著孩子們分享畫作，心裡升起小小的希望。即使這一天充滿不確定，他們仍然做出一件實用、清楚而溫柔的事。

On their walk home, June asked whether there was anything the group could do now. Erin pointed to the open park and the Little Lookout. "We can give people a safe place to learn, even while the tower remains closed," she said. June thought of making paper arms that would turn in a gentle breeze. Ben suggested a small stand to hold drawings. The children began talking about shapes and colors. Erin did not tell them to stop. An old building could not be made safe by wishing, but a good lesson could begin with paper, a pencil, and people willing to ask why.

走回家時，小君問大家現在還能做什麼。艾琳指向開放的公園和小小觀景點。「即使塔身繼續封閉，我們還是可以讓人們在安全的地方學習，」她說。小君想到可以做能隨微風轉動的紙葉片，班則建議放一個小架子展示圖畫。孩子們開始討論形狀與顏色。艾琳沒有阻止。許願不能讓老建築變安全，但一堂好課可以從紙、鉛筆，以及願意提問的人開始。
