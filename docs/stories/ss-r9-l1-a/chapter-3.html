河邊的那條線

The River Line

山頂上的藍色風車 · 第 3 / 25 章 · 1000以下 · 英中對照

用互動版查詞、朗讀與收藏

The old road followed the river before it turned toward the hill. Erin, Mira, and Ben stopped beside a small bridge. Below it, the water moved between round stones. There was a narrow beach of pale sand on one side. A black bird stood there, watching the water. "The old water line crossed this bridge," Mr. Han said. He had come to show them the way. "I was born here, so I remember when the pipe was in use." He pointed to a dark mark on the stone. Erin did not step down to the sand. The bridge was open to walkers, but the wet bank below was far too slippery.

舊道路沿著河流延伸，然後才轉向山坡。艾琳、米拉和班停在一座小橋旁。橋下的水在圓石之間流動，一側有片淺色沙灘，一隻黑鳥站在上面看水。「以前的水管從這座橋經過，」韓爺爺說。他特地來帶路。「我在這裡出生，還記得水管能用的時候。」他指著石頭上的深色痕跡。艾琳沒有下到沙地。雖然橋面開放行走，下面潮濕的河岸卻很容易滑倒。

A farmer walked past with a cow on a rope. She stopped to ask what they were doing. "Every spring, we clean the road near the garden," she said. "If you follow that line of grass, you can see where water used to go." Erin thanked her. The farmer explained that the water line went into a covered tank, not straight into people's homes. That detail mattered. Erin drew a map on her paper. On one side she wrote garden. On the other she wrote hill. She did not pull at any old pipe, even when she saw a small end sticking out of the ground.

一位農夫牽著一頭牛走過來，停下來問他們在做什麼。「每年春天，我們都會清理菜園旁的道路，」她說。「沿著那條草線看，就能知道以前的水往哪裡走。」艾琳向她道謝。農夫解釋，那條水管接到有蓋的水槽，並不是直接送進各家各戶。這個細節很重要。艾琳在紙上畫了地圖，一側寫「菜園」，另一側寫「山坡」。即使看見一截舊水管露出地面，她也沒有伸手去拉。

Ben noticed a pair of muddy shoes beside a public table at the rest station. A sign said the shoes were there for anyone who needed to change after working in the garden. "Do you think the old water line is still good?" he asked. Mira shook her head. "We cannot know by looking from here. It may be full of earth, or it may have broken long ago." Erin added another question to the map. "What will happen if we repair only the windmill and leave the pipe broken?" Nobody had an easy answer. Mr. Han said that was why they needed a real inspection, not just memories.

班在休息站的公共桌旁看到一雙沾滿泥土的鞋。告示說，給需要從菜園工作回來換鞋的人使用。「你們覺得舊水管還能用嗎？」他問。米拉搖頭。「只從這裡看不出來。裡面可能塞滿泥土，也可能很久以前就斷了。」艾琳在地圖上又加了一個問題：「如果只修風車，不修壞掉的水管，會發生什麼事？」沒有人能立刻回答。韓爺爺說，這正是為什麼他們需要正式檢查，而不能只靠回憶。

They sat on the bridge's wide stone wall for a moment, well away from the road. Mr. Han told them that the old windmill had made work easier, but the village no longer needed it for water. A modern pump now served the garden. "Then perhaps it should teach instead of work," Ben said. Erin looked into the clear river. A fish moved under the surface. "Perhaps," she answered, "but only if we can make the place safe, and only if people want that." They walked back together, with a cleaner map and a better question. The windmill's future did not have to be the same as its past.

他們在橋邊寬闊的石牆上休息了一會兒，與道路保持距離。韓爺爺說，舊風車以前讓工作變得容易，但村裡現在已經不需要靠它送水。如今有現代水泵替菜園工作。「那它也許可以變成教學的地方，而不是工作的機器，」班說。艾琳望著清澈的河水，一條魚從水面下游過。「也許可以，」她回答，「但前提是安全，而且村民真的希望如此。」他們一起走回村裡，帶著更清楚的地圖，也帶著更好的問題。風車的未來不一定要和過去一樣。

Back at the rest station, Ben asked about the small metal plate on the bridge. The plate held the year when the bridge had been repaired. Mr. Han explained that a road and a bridge needed care even when they looked strong. The farmer returned and told them the garden keeper kept a list of places where rainwater collected. Erin asked permission to copy that list, and the farmer offered to speak to the keeper. "We are learning about the hill," Erin said, "but we must learn about the ground below it too." Ben drew the bridge a second time, this time adding the safe walking side and the place where the old water line crossed. Erin thought his new drawing was better than his first because it showed people where not to go as well as where to look.

回到休息站，班問橋上的小金屬牌是做什麼的。牌上寫著橋梁整修的年份。韓爺爺說，道路和橋梁即使看起來牢固，也需要保養。農夫又走回來，告訴他們菜園管理員記著哪些地方容易積雨水。艾琳請問能不能影印那張清單，農夫答應替她詢問。「我們正在了解山坡，」艾琳說，「但也要了解山坡下面的土地。」班把橋又畫了一次，這次加上安全步行的一側，以及舊水管通過的位置。艾琳覺得新畫比第一張好，因為它不只告訴大家看哪裡，也標出不該去的地方。

That evening, Erin placed the map next to Mr. Han's old picture. The drawing made one fact easy to see: the river, the hill, and the garden were close, but they were not the same place. A plan for one would change work at the others. Mrs. May asked whether the walk had been useful. Erin said it had been useful because they had found things they should not touch. "A good worker knows when to stop," Mrs. May said. Erin smiled and put her pencil down. Tomorrow she would ask the town office who was responsible for the closed windmill, and who could inspect it.

當晚，艾琳把地圖放在韓爺爺的舊照片旁。圖上有一件事非常清楚：河流、山坡和菜園彼此靠近，卻是不同的地方。改變其中一處，也會影響其他地方的工作。梅太太問今天的走訪有沒有幫助。艾琳說，最大的收穫是知道哪些東西不能亂碰。「好的工人知道什麼時候該停手，」梅太太說。艾琳笑著放下鉛筆。明天，她要去鎮公所問清楚：誰負責封閉中的風車，又該由誰來檢查它。
