週末的會議

The Weekend Meeting

山頂上的藍色風車 · 第 6 / 25 章 · 1000以下 · 英中對照 · 校訂中

用互動版查詞、朗讀與收藏

By the weekend, news about the windmill had reached every house. Some people arrived at the village room by car; others came on foot. A man from the lake district had driven a small boat across the water before taking a bus into town. He carried a box of old photographs. Mrs. May offered coffee, and Erin put a paper sign on the door: Please speak one at a time. The meeting was free and open to everyone. The old windmill belonged to the town, not to Erin. She knew she could not choose its future alone, even if she had good ideas about how to repair wood.

到了週末，風車的消息已傳遍村裡。有人開車來活動室，也有人走路來。一名住在湖區的男子先搭小船過湖，再轉公車進鎮，帶來一箱舊照片。梅太太準備了咖啡，艾琳在門上貼著紙條：「請一次由一人發言。」會議免費，任何人都能參加。舊風車屬於鎮上，不是艾琳個人的。她很清楚，即使自己有修木頭的好點子，也不能獨自決定它的未來。

Nora asked the county inspector, Ms. Patel, to explain the next step. Ms. Patel had checked many old buildings, but she had not seen this windmill yet. "I cannot give it a safe sign today," she said. "First I must see the roof, the arms, and the machine inside, with the right people and equipment." Someone asked whether the town could open the top room for a single visit. "No," she answered. "Not before an inspection." Her answer was simple, and no one asked her to change it. Erin was glad the rule was clear. A beautiful view was not worth a terrible accident.

諾拉請郡裡的檢查員帕特爾女士說明下一步。帕特爾檢查過許多老建築，但還沒看過這座風車。「今天我不能替它掛上安全標誌，」她說。「我必須先和適當的專業人員、設備一起檢查屋頂、葉片和內部機械。」有人問能不能只開放最上層房間讓大家看一次。「不行，」她回答。「檢查完成前不行。」她的回答很明確，沒有人再要求她改變。艾琳很高興規則如此清楚。再美的景色，也不值得冒嚴重意外的風險。

Mr. Han showed a photograph of himself as a young man standing beside a painted lion on the festival gate. Ben laughed. "You looked like my uncle!" he said. His grandfather laughed too. Then Mr. Han turned serious. "The windmill mattered because we worked together," he said. "If the best answer is to keep it still, I will accept that." Nora asked people to wave a hand if they agreed to pay for a professional inspection from the town's public building fund. Almost every hand went up. The few who did not agree were allowed to explain their worries about cost.

韓爺爺展示一張照片，照片裡年輕時的他站在節慶門口的一隻彩繪獅子旁。班笑了。「你那時候好像我叔叔！」他說。韓爺爺也笑了，接著認真地說：「風車有意義，是因為我們一起工作。如果最好的答案是讓它保持停止，我也會接受。」諾拉請同意由鎮上公共建築基金支付專業檢查費的人舉手。幾乎每個人都舉了手，少數不同意的人也有機會說明對費用的擔憂。

The town secretary wrote down the decision before anyone went home. The inspection would happen the following week, except if the weather was unsafe. Erin promised to be there to answer questions about wood, but she would not enter the tower without the inspector's permission. "Finally, we have one true step," Ben said. Ms. Patel nodded. "A first step, not a last one." Outside, the wind moved across the square. Erin looked up at the hill. She could see the still arms from the village room, but for the first time she did not feel that they were asking her to hurry.

大家回家前，鎮務秘書把決議寫了下來。檢查訂在下週，除非天氣不安全。艾琳承諾到場回答木工方面的問題，但沒有檢查員允許，就不會進塔。「終於有真正的進展了，」班說。帕特爾點點頭。「是第一步，不是最後一步。」外面，風掠過廣場。艾琳抬頭望向山坡，從活動室還看得見靜止的葉片。但這是她第一次不覺得風車正在催促自己。

Before the meeting ended, a man asked whether a new sign should show the shape of the windmill. Erin said a picture could help visitors see which building was closed. Mr. Han showed Ben how to draw its four arms without making them look like a flower. The man then asked whether it was stupid to hope for a working windmill. Nora answered gently, "It is not stupid to hope. It would be foolish to ignore a safety report." People seemed glad that wanting something and checking it carefully could happen together. As new visitors began to arrive at the door, the secretary gave each one the same printed meeting paper. No one had to learn the rules from rumors. Erin watched the room grow quieter and understood that good information could change the shape of a discussion.

會議結束前，一位男子問新告示是否該畫上風車的形狀。艾琳說，圖畫能讓訪客明白哪一棟建築封閉。韓爺爺教班畫四片葉片，讓它們看起來不像花。男子又問，希望風車恢復運作是不是很愚蠢。諾拉溫和回答：「抱有希望並不愚蠢，無視安全報告才不明智。」大家似乎很高興，原來抱有期待和仔細確認可以同時存在。有新訪客抵達時，秘書都給他們相同的紙本會議資料。沒有人需要靠傳言理解規則。艾琳看著房間逐漸安靜，明白可靠資訊能改變討論的方式。

That night, Mrs. May asked Erin whether she wanted to drive home for a rest. Erin thought of her own small room, the quiet hill, and the people she had met. "I would rather stay and see what we learn," she answered. "But I can take a day off if I need one." Mrs. May smiled. "Good. A person is not a machine." Erin put the meeting paper in her notebook and closed it. She would wait for the inspection results instead of guessing. The bell rang once in the distance, a soft sound across the dark town.

當晚，梅太太問艾琳要不要開車回家休息。艾琳想起自己住的小房間、安靜的山坡和剛認識的人。「我比較想留下來，看看我們能學到什麼，」她回答。「但如果真的需要，我也會休息一天。」梅太太笑著說：「很好。人不是機器。」艾琳把會議紀錄放入筆記本，然後闔上。她決定等待檢查結果，不再自己猜測。遠方的鈴聲響了一下，輕輕傳過夜色中的小鎮。
