小小測風實驗

The Little Wind Test

山頂上的藍色風車 · 第 12 / 25 章 · 1000以下 · 英中對照 · 校訂中

用互動版查詞、朗讀與收藏

On the first warm day of spring, Mira came by bicycle with a paper wind wheel in her bag. She wore old jeans and carried a notebook of her own. "This is a model for the Little Lookout," she said. "It does not need anyone to touch the real windmill." Ben wanted to pick it up right away, but Mira asked him to wait. "First answer one question: what will make it turn?" "The wind," Ben said. Mira gave a small bow. "Correct. And where does the wind come from?" Ben thought of the whole planet and laughed. "I cannot choose that," he said. "So I will watch and write."

春天第一個暖和的日子，米拉騎腳踏車來了，袋子裡裝著紙製風輪。她穿著舊牛仔褲，帶著自己的筆記本。「這是給小小觀景點用的模型，」她說。「不必碰真正的風車就能使用。」班很想立刻拿起來，米拉請他等等。「先回答一個問題：什麼會讓它轉？」班說：「風。」米拉微微鞠躬。「答對了。那風從哪裡來？」班想到整個地球，笑了。「我沒辦法決定風從哪裡來，」他說。「所以我要觀察、記錄。」

They went to the lookout with Ada and Ms. Cho. Erin placed the model on a low table that was easy to reach. Mira made sure the pointed parts were covered. "This wheel is mine to lend to the class," she said, "but the notes will belong to everyone." Ada listened as the paper moved. "It makes a soft sound," she said. Ms. Cho asked the children to say the word wind in the language they used at home. Some children knew two words for it. No answer was better because it came from a different country. The class listened with interest.

他們和艾達、周老師一起前往觀景點。艾琳把模型放在一張容易伸手碰到的矮桌上，米拉確認尖角都已包好。「這個風輪是我借給班上的，」她說，「但觀察紀錄會屬於大家。」艾達聽著紙輪轉動。「它的聲音很輕，」她說。周老師請孩子們用家裡常說的語言說出「風」。有些孩子知道兩種說法。沒有哪種答案會因為來自別的國家就比較好。大家都很有興趣地聽著。

For the second test, Mira used a fan with three marked settings. She showed the class how to keep their bodies away from the spinning wheel and how to turn the fan off. "Say what you see, not what you wish," she told them. At the lowest setting, the paper wheel moved slowly. At the highest, it moved faster. Ben wanted to write that the old windmill would do the same. Erin stopped him gently. "A paper model can teach us about a shape," she said, "but it cannot tell us whether heavy wooden arms are safe. That answer belongs to the trained inspectors."

第二次測試時，米拉拿出一台有三段風速的電風扇，教大家讓身體遠離旋轉的風輪，也示範如何關掉電源。「寫下你看見的，不是你希望看見的，」她說。最低風速時，紙輪轉得很慢；最高風速時，轉得比較快。班想寫真正的風車也會如此，艾琳溫和地阻止他。「紙模型能教我們理解形狀，」她說，「但不能說明沉重的木葉片是否安全。那必須交給專業檢查員判斷。」

Before leaving, Ada asked whether the path would be ready soon. Erin told her the town needed prices and permission first. "I wish I could give you a date," she said. "But I would rather give you a true answer." Ada thanked her. Ms. Cho said the class could make some signs for the lookout, while adults planned the real path. The children wrote their names on the back of their drawings. Mira packed the model into her bicycle basket. Nothing on the hill had moved, but the children had learned how to ask better questions.

離開前，艾達問小路是不是快修好了。艾琳說，鎮上得先確認價格和取得許可。「我真希望能給妳一個日期，」她說。「但我更想給妳真實的答案。」艾達向她道謝。周老師說，孩子們可以幫觀景點製作標誌，真正的道路規劃則由大人負責。孩子們在畫作背面寫上名字。米拉把模型收進腳踏車籃。山上的風車沒有動，但孩子們學會了提出更好的問題。

After the test, Ms. Cho asked the children what each body had told them about the weather. Some felt wind on their hands; others noticed the change on their faces. One child had brought bread with a little salt for lunch and noticed that the strong breeze made the napkin move. Mira told the class that small observations could be useful if they wrote them honestly. "The paper wheel is not the whole story," she said. "Neither is one windy day." Ben added a new page to his science notebook. He drew the wheel, the table, and the place where everyone had stood. Erin liked how much space he left for notes. The picture did not claim to know everything. It invited the next person to look again and learn more.

測試後，周老師請孩子們說說身體感受到什麼天氣變化。有些人感覺風碰到手，有些人注意到臉上的風感。一個孩子午餐帶了加一點鹽的麵包，也看見強風把餐巾吹動。米拉告訴全班，小小的觀察只要誠實記錄，就很有用。「紙風輪不是全部的故事，」她說。「一天的風也不是。」班在科學筆記裡加了一頁，畫出風輪、桌子和大家站的位置。艾琳喜歡他留了許多空白給新筆記。這張圖沒有假裝知道所有答案，而是邀請下一個人再來觀察、學習。

That night, Erin looked at two pages in her notebook. One was a drawing of the old arms; the other was a list of ways people could learn without entering the tower. She was surprised that the second page now held more ideas than the first. There were ways to see, hear, touch safe models, and ask questions. She chose three ideas to bring to Nora. Then she closed the book. The wind outside the window was gentle. It had not promised anything, and Erin was finally learning not to ask it for a promise.

那晚，艾琳翻看筆記本的兩頁。一頁畫著老風車葉片，另一頁列出不進塔也能學習的方法。她驚訝地發現，第二頁的點子已經比第一頁多。大家可以看、聽、摸安全模型，還能提出問題。她選了三個點子，準備帶給諾拉。然後她闔上筆記本。窗外的風很溫和，並沒有向任何人許諾什麼。而艾琳終於慢慢學會，不再要求風向自己保證。
