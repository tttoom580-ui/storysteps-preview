小點子的節日

A Festival of Small Ideas

山頂上的藍色風車 · 第 9 / 25 章 · 1000以下 · 英中對照 · 校訂中

用互動版查詞、朗讀與收藏

The town was preparing for its spring festival near the lake. This year, Nora offered the village room one long table. "You may show what you have learned about the windmill," she said. "But no one may visit the tower yet." Erin agreed. Ben made a card with a picture of the old blue arms, and June brought a dish of rice to share at lunch. Her grandmother came with her and told everyone how she had visited the hill when she was young. "We were quite ready to work hard," she said, "but we did not always know enough to do it safely." Erin wrote her words on a card.

小鎮正在湖邊準備春日節慶。諾拉今年願意讓他們在活動室使用一張長桌。「你們可以展示學到的風車知識，」她說。「但現在誰都還不能進塔。」艾琳同意。班做了一張畫著舊藍色葉片的卡片，小君帶了一盤米飯來與大家分享午餐。她的祖母也跟著來，向眾人說起年輕時到山坡玩的回憶。「那時我們很願意努力，」她說，「但不一定知道怎樣做才安全。」艾琳把這句話寫在卡片上。

The festival street was bright with small flags. A man gave out bananas, and a woman showed children how to make paper birds. Ben wanted to shout for people to come to their table. Mrs. May offered a better tip. "Speak clearly and let people choose," she told him. "A friendly welcome is better than a loud call." Ben tried again. "Would you like to see our windmill drawings?" he asked. Several people stopped. One man said, "Okay, I have two minutes." He looked at the pictures and said he had never known the old windmill had helped a garden.

節慶街道掛滿小旗子。一名男子分送香蕉，一位婦人教孩子們做紙鳥。班想大喊，吸引大家來看展示。梅太太給他更好的建議：「清楚說話，讓別人自己選擇。友善的招呼比大聲吆喝好。」班改用溫和的聲音問：「想看看我們畫的風車嗎？」幾個人停下腳步。一名男子說：「好啊，我有兩分鐘。」他看了圖，才知道舊風車曾幫助菜園送水。

On another card, Erin wrote three choices for the future: leave the building closed and care for its outside, repair some safe parts for viewing, or make a separate learning model. People could place a mark beside the idea they liked. "Are we voting today?" a visitor asked. Erin shook her head. "No. This is not the town's final choice. We are learning what people hope for." Some visitors wanted the old arms to turn. Others were worried about money. A boy said the lower path needed a bench for his grandmother. That idea was new, and Erin added it to the page.

艾琳在另一張卡上寫下三個未來選項：保持建築封閉、照顧外觀；修理部分安全設施供人觀看；或另做一個學習模型。大家可以在喜歡的主意旁做記號。「今天是在投票嗎？」一位訪客問。艾琳搖頭。「不是。這不是鎮上的最終決定，我們只是想了解大家的希望。」有些人想看舊葉片轉動，有些人擔心花錢。一個男孩說，下面的小路需要長椅，讓祖母能坐下。這是新點子，艾琳也把它記下。

June's grandmother brought a photograph from the back of her bag. It showed the hill, the old bell, and a small garden party many years ago. Her face was full of joy as she told the story. "The windmill was part of our life," she said. "But life changes. We can keep the memory and choose a new use." Erin thanked her. She was sure the photograph would help explain why the hill mattered, even if the tower remained closed. They did not promise the visitors an answer. They invited them to return after the inspector's report was made public.

小君的祖母從袋子後面拿出一張舊照片，上面有山坡、老鈴鐺和多年前的小型菜園聚會。她講故事時滿臉喜悅。「風車是我們生活的一部分，」她說。「但生活會改變。我們可以保留回憶，也能選擇新用途。」艾琳向她道謝。她相信這張照片能說明山坡的重要，即使風車最後仍封閉，也一樣有意義。他們沒有向訪客保證結果，只邀請大家在檢查報告公開後再回來討論。

Near the end of the festival, a child offered Ben a banana and asked why his drawing had no bright lights on the windmill. Ben explained that the old lamp was part of history, not something the tower could safely make now. The child looked surprised, then pointed to the picture of the water tank. "I like this better," she said. "It helped plants grow." Ben gave her a blank card and asked what she would want to learn at the lookout. She wrote, How does wind move water? Erin added the question to the idea board. She was glad the display had made room for new questions instead of only asking people to choose between old answers. As the last flags came down, the table held more questions than it had when the festival began.

節日快結束時，一個孩子送給班一根香蕉，問他的圖上為什麼沒有風車發出的亮光。班解釋，舊燈是歷史的一部分，不代表塔現在能安全供電。孩子有點驚訝，接著指向水槽的圖片。「我比較喜歡這個，」她說。「它幫植物長大。」班遞給她一張空白卡，問她想在觀景點學什麼。她寫：「風怎麼讓水往上走？」艾琳把問題加到點子板上。她很高興展示能容納新的問題，而不是只讓大家從舊答案裡挑選。最後一面旗子收下時，桌上的問題已比節日開始時更多。

When the festival ended, Ben counted the cards while Erin watched. He was surprised to see that no idea had won everyone over. Mrs. May said that was normal. "A town is not one person," she told him. "There is no magic dish that everyone must like." Ben laughed and put the cards in three neat piles. The group would treat every note with care, even notes they did not agree with. On the walk home, Erin felt tired but hopeful. The festival had not fixed a single piece of wood. It had given the project something else: more voices and better questions.

節慶結束後，班在艾琳的陪伴下數卡片。他很意外，沒有任何一個想法獲得所有人的支持。梅太太說這很正常。「一個鎮不是只有一個人，」她說。「也沒有一道菜是所有人都非喜歡不可。」班笑了，把卡片分成三疊。大家決定仔細對待每張意見，就算內容不同意也一樣。回家路上，艾琳雖然疲倦，卻覺得有希望。節日沒有修好一根木頭，但帶來了別的東西：更多聲音與更好的問題。
