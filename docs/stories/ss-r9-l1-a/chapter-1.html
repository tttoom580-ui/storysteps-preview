山上的鈴聲

The Bell on the Hill

山頂上的藍色風車 · 第 1 / 25 章 · 1000以下 · 英中對照

At six a.m., Erin opened her eyes. She was twenty-four and had come to Hill Village only yesterday. The sun was not high yet, but a little bell rang outside her window. She got up early because she wanted to see the old blue windmill on the hill. It was her first day in the village, and she did not want to be late. Her room was small. There was a bed, a table, and a green couch. On the table was a plate with an apple and a note from Mrs. May, the woman who owned the house. The note said, “Please have breakfast before you go. The hill is a long walk.” Erin smiled. She ate the apple and a piece of cake. She put more fruit in her bag for later. Then she opened the window and looked at the hill. The windmill stood there, very still.

早上六點，艾琳睜開眼睛。她二十四歲，昨天才來到山村。太陽還沒有升得很高，但窗外響起一陣小鈴聲。她很早就起床，因為她想看看山上的舊藍色風車。這是她來到村子的第一天，她不想遲到。她的房間很小，裡面有一張床、一張桌子和一張綠色沙發。桌上放著一顆蘋果，以及屋主梅太太留下的紙條。紙條寫著：「出門前請先吃早餐。走到山上要走很長一段路。」艾琳笑了。她吃了蘋果和一塊蛋糕，又把一些水果放進袋子，準備晚點吃。接著她打開窗戶，望向山坡。風車靜靜地站在那裡，一動也不動。

Mrs. May was in the kitchen. She made a small salad for herself and asked Erin to sit down. “You are here to look at the windmill?” she asked. Erin said yes. “My father could make many things from wood. He taught me to work with my hands. Now I want to learn more.” Mrs. May pointed to a picture on the wall. In it, the windmill was turning, and children were playing under it. “That was many years ago,” she said. “The windmill did not make all our power, but it helped us. People came here to learn about wind and the land.” Erin looked at the picture for a long time. “Why did it stop?” she asked. Mrs. May did not know. “Some people say it is too old. Others say we should try to save it. You must look before you decide.” Erin liked that answer. She did not want to make a promise she could not keep.

梅太太正在廚房。她為自己做了一小盤沙拉，請艾琳坐下。「妳是來看風車的嗎？」她問。艾琳點頭。「我父親會用木頭做很多東西。他教我用雙手工作。現在我想學更多。」梅太太指著牆上的一張照片。照片裡，風車正在轉動，孩子們在下面玩耍。「那是很多年前的事了，」她說。「風車沒有供應我們全部的電力，但它幫了我們。人們會來這裡學習風和土地的知識。」艾琳看了照片很久。「它為什麼停了？」她問。梅太太不知道。「有些人說它太老了，另一些人說我們應該試著保留它。妳要先看清楚，再做決定。」艾琳喜歡這個答案。她不想做出自己無法實現的承諾。

On the road, Erin met a boy named Ben. He was carrying a heavy box. Erin asked if she could help, but Ben shook his head. “It is full of books,” he said. “I can carry it. I do this for exercise.” Then he stopped and put the box on a low wall. “Well, perhaps I need a short rest.” Erin laughed, and Ben laughed too. He said he was taking the books to the village room. There, children could read after school. “My hobby is drawing,” he told her. “I draw birds, houses, and sometimes a funny monkey. Would you like to see my pictures one day?” Erin said she would. She asked about the bell she had heard that morning. Ben pointed to the top of the hill. “It is near the windmill. My grandfather rings it every morning. He says a village should have a sound that tells everyone a new day has begun.”

在路上，艾琳遇見一個叫班的男孩。他正搬著一個很重的箱子。艾琳問需不需要幫忙，但班搖了搖頭。「裡面都是書，」他說。「我搬得動。這也是我的運動。」接著他停下來，把箱子放在矮牆上。「好吧，也許我需要休息一下。」艾琳笑了，班也笑了。他說自己正要把書送到村子的公共活動室，孩子們放學後可以在那裡看書。「我的嗜好是畫畫，」他告訴她。「我畫鳥、房子，有時也畫一隻好笑的猴子。改天妳想看看我的畫嗎？」艾琳說她很願意。她問起早上聽到的鈴聲。班指向山頂。「鈴就在風車附近。我祖父每天早上都會敲響它。他說村子應該有一個聲音，告訴大家新的一天開始了。」

They walked together for a while. At the foot of the hill, they saw a wooden sign. It said, “Please do not go inside the windmill.” Below the words was a drawing of a door with a red line across it. Erin took out a small book. She liked to write down what she saw. “The door may not be safe,” she said. “I will stay outside today.” Ben nodded. “My grandfather can teach you about the hill. He knows the old paths.” Just then, a man came down the road. His name was Mr. Lee, and he had a small bag over his shoulder. He was the village doctor. “Good morning,” he said. “Did you remember to brush every tooth?” Ben made a face. “Yes, Doctor!” Mr. Lee smiled. He asked Erin where she had come from. “My mother was born in another country,” Erin said, “so our family has some foreign books and songs. But I grew up in a town not far from here.” Mr. Lee welcomed her.

兩人一起走了一段路。到了山腳下，他們看見一塊木製告示牌，上面寫著：「請勿進入風車內部。」文字下面畫著一扇門，門上有一道紅線。艾琳拿出一本小筆記本。她喜歡把看到的事情寫下來。「門可能不安全，」她說。「今天我會待在外面。」班點點頭。「我祖父可以教妳這座山的事。他知道以前的小路。」這時，一名男子沿著道路走下來。他叫李先生，是村裡的醫生，肩上背著一個小袋子。「早安，」他說。「你記得把每顆牙齒都刷乾淨嗎？」班做了個鬼臉。「有啦，醫生！」李先生笑了，接著問艾琳從哪裡來。「我母親出生在另一個國家，」艾琳說，「所以我們家有一些外國書籍和歌曲。不過我是在離這裡不遠的小鎮長大的。」李先生歡迎她來到村裡。

At last, Erin stood near the windmill. Its blue paint was old, and one part of the roof had no cover. She could see a little rainwater on the ground below it. The wind was strong, but the great wooden arms did not move. A little bird sat on one arm and sang. Erin looked up, then stepped back. She would not touch the windmill today. A safe plan had to come first. Ben's grandfather was standing by the bell. He was a tall man with kind eyes. “So you are Erin,” he said. “I hear you want to help us.” Erin answered, “I want to learn what happened. Then we can talk about what to do.” The old man smiled. “Good. Shall we begin with the story of this hill?” He rang the bell once, softly. Erin opened her book to a clean page. Under the words First Day, she wrote one question: Why did the blue windmill stop? She did not know the answer yet, but now she knew where to begin.

最後，艾琳站在風車附近。風車的藍色油漆已經老舊，屋頂有一部分失去了遮蓋。她看見下面的地面上有一點積水。風很強，但巨大的木製葉片沒有轉動。一隻小鳥停在葉片上唱歌。艾琳抬頭看了看，接著往後退。今天她不會碰風車。安全的計畫必須擺在第一位。班的祖父站在鈴旁。他是個高個子、眼神親切的人。「所以妳就是艾琳，」他說。「我聽說妳想幫我們。」艾琳回答：「我想先了解發生了什麼事。然後我們再談該怎麼做。」老人笑了。「很好。我們就從這座山的故事開始，好嗎？」他輕輕敲了一下鈴。艾琳把筆記本翻到空白頁，在「第一天」下面寫下一個問題：「藍色風車為什麼停了？」她還不知道答案，但現在她知道該從哪裡開始。
