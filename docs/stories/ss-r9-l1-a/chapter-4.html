看似容易的提議

An Easy Offer

山頂上的藍色風車 · 第 4 / 25 章 · 1000以下 · 英中對照

On Monday, a tall worker from the old furniture factory came to the town office. His name was Paul. He carried a square box of blue paint and said the factory could give it to the village for a cheap price. "The wind will blow soon," he said. "Why not put on fresh paint and open the hill?" A boy near the door liked that idea. He told a story about a butterfly sitting on the windmill years ago. Erin listened, but she watched the dark cloud above the hill. Rain could drop through the roof where its cover was missing. Paint alone would not keep people safe.

星期一，一名高個子的舊家具工廠工人來到鎮公所。他叫保羅，帶著一個方形箱子，裡面裝著藍色油漆。他說工廠可以用便宜的價格提供油漆。「很快就會起風，」他說。「為什麼不漆得漂亮一點，就把山坡重新開放？」門旁的一名男孩很喜歡這個主意，還說起多年前蝴蝶停在風車上的故事。艾琳耐心聽著，卻注意到山頂上方的烏雲。屋頂失去遮蓋的地方會漏雨，光是上漆並不能讓人安全。

The town chair, Nora, asked Erin to speak. "Sir, I am thankful for the offer," Erin told Paul. "But we must turn off any old machine and have a trained person check it first. We do not know what will shake when the wind grows strong." Paul lowered the box. "I did not think about that," he said. "My service is paint, not machine repair." A second worker added that the factory had strong boards in storage. Nora said the town could look at those boards later, after an inspector made a list. They would not buy anything without a clear need.

鎮務主席諾拉請艾琳發言。「先生，謝謝您的提議，」艾琳對保羅說。「但是舊機器必須先保持停止，由受過訓練的人檢查。我們不知道風變強時，有哪些零件會搖晃。」保羅放低箱子。「我沒想到這一點，」他說。「我的服務是油漆，不是修機器。」另一名工人補充，工廠倉庫裡還有一些結實的木板。諾拉說，等檢查員列出需求後，鎮上再考慮那些木板；在此之前，不會買不確定需要的東西。

At lunch, Mrs. May brought a tomato sandwich and a small bowl of soup to the office. Erin had worked through the morning without eating and felt tired. "You cannot do healthy work when you forget your own meal," Mrs. May said. Ben, who had come with his grandfather, asked if the windmill would be open for his school visit next month. Erin would not give an easy yes. "She is trying to do this well," Mr. Han told him. "Being careful is not the same as doing nothing." They sat together while people from the town came and went, each with a different hope for the hill.

午餐時，梅太太把番茄三明治和一小碗湯帶到辦公室。艾琳忙了一上午，忘了吃東西，已經有些疲倦。「自己連飯都不吃，就沒辦法健康地工作，」梅太太說。跟祖父一起來的班問，下個月學校參訪時風車能不能開放。艾琳不願隨便答應。「她正在努力把事情做好，」韓爺爺告訴班。「小心謹慎，不等於什麼都不做。」大家一起坐著，看鎮民來來去去，每個人對山坡都有不同的期待。

Nora placed a sheet of paper on the wall. It showed three steps: check the building, make a public plan, and then decide whether to repair anything. "We cannot skip the first step," she said. Erin was pleased, although she knew the town might still vote to leave the tower closed. She asked Paul if he would keep the blue paint at the factory for now. He agreed. The paint was useful only if a safe place for it existed. Outside, the clouds moved away. The sun appeared above the square, and a butterfly passed between two windows. No one needed to chase it.

諾拉把一張紙貼到牆上，上面寫著三個步驟：檢查建築、提出公開計畫，再決定要不要修理。「第一步不能省略，」她說。艾琳很高興，但也明白鎮上最後可能決定繼續封閉風車。她請保羅先把藍色油漆留在工廠，保羅答應了。只有找到安全的使用方式，油漆才真正有用。外面的雲逐漸散開，陽光照進廣場，一隻蝴蝶飛過兩扇窗戶之間。沒有人需要追趕它。

Paul stayed behind after the others left the table. He looked a little embarrassed. "I wanted to do something helpful," he told Erin. "I did not mean to push the town into a bad choice." Erin said she understood. She asked him whether his factory kept records of the paint it sold. Paul said yes and offered to bring the product information next time. Nora thanked him. A clear record would help the town choose materials later, if the work reached that step. Erin noticed that Paul seemed more at ease once he had a useful job that did not involve touching the tower. She was learning how to say no to an unsafe idea without saying no to the person who had offered it. A town needed that skill as much as it needed tools.

其他人離開桌邊後，保羅留了下來，神情有點不好意思。「我只是想幫忙，」他對艾琳說。「我不是要逼鎮上做錯決定。」艾琳說她明白，接著問工廠是否保留出售油漆的資料。保羅說有，願意下次帶產品資訊過來。諾拉向他道謝。如果工程進到用漆的階段，清楚的紀錄能幫助選材料。艾琳注意到，保羅有了不必碰塔身的實際工作後，輕鬆了許多。她正在學習如何拒絕不安全的點子，卻不必拒絕提出點子的人。對一座小鎮而言，這種能力和工具一樣重要。

As Erin left, she heard a man say that the young carpenter was making a simple job hard. She turned back, ready to answer, then saw that Nora had heard him too. Nora pointed to the three steps on the wall. "We all want a good town," she said. "That means asking questions before we spend money." The man read the list again and said nothing more. Erin went outside with her notebook. It was not a win or a loss. It was one useful rule that people could read for themselves. Tomorrow, she would look for a safe way to measure the wind from the ground.

艾琳離開時，聽到有人說，這位年輕木匠把簡單的事弄得太複雜。她轉過身，準備回答，才發現諾拉也聽見了。諾拉指著牆上的三個步驟說：「大家都希望鎮上變好。這就表示，花錢之前要先問清楚。」那人又看了一遍清單，沒有再說話。艾琳帶著筆記本走出去。這不是勝利，也不是失敗，只是一條每個人都能看懂的好規則。明天，她打算尋找從地面安全測風的方法。
