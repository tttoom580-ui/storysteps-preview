The weather turned cold, and a hard wind moved through the trees. Nora called Erin from her car. "The inspector's full report is ready," she said. "Come to the town office before the snow starts." Erin put on warm pants and took the blue folder she had used for her notes. Mrs. May, who liked to cook soup on cold days, packed a small bowl for her. "You may be there for a long time," she said. In the office, the secretary placed the report under a desk lamp. Several pages showed cracks, wet wood, and the old brake. Erin knew the past could not be changed, but the next steps could.

天氣轉冷，強風穿過樹林。諾拉從車上打電話給艾琳：「完整檢查報告出來了，」她說。「下雪前來鎮公所吧。」艾琳穿上暖和的長褲，拿起裝筆記的藍色資料夾。梅太太喜歡在冷天煮湯，替她裝了一小碗。「妳可能會待很久，」她說。到了辦公室，秘書把報告放在檯燈下。好幾頁都記錄著裂縫、受潮木頭和舊煞車。艾琳知道過去無法更改，但接下來該怎麼做，是可以決定的。

Ms. Patel joined the call from the county office. "The main walls can probably stay," she explained, "but the arms must remain stopped until trained workers replace the weak pieces and test the brake. The roof needs repair, and the public must stay behind the fence." Ben had asked for a simple sentence he could understand, so Erin wrote one on the board: Do not turn the old arms before experts say it is safe. She checked a dictionary to make sure every word was easy for the children. Nora read it aloud. "That will be our first rule," she said.

帕特爾從郡辦公室加入通話。「主要牆體大概可以保留，」她解釋，「但在專業工人更換脆弱零件、測試煞車之前，葉片必須保持停止。屋頂需要修理，民眾必須留在圍欄外。」班先前希望能有一句自己看得懂的說明，於是艾琳在白板上寫道：「專家確認安全前，不要轉動舊葉片。」她還查了字典，確保孩子也看得懂。諾拉大聲讀出這句話。「這就是我們的第一條規則，」她說。

The report had one more message. The old windmill could become a teaching place, but not a source of power for the town. "Maybe a small model could show how it used to work," Ms. Patel suggested. "A fan may make the model turn, but the real arms are another matter." Mr. Han listened quietly. He remembered a time when the village had needed the windmill's help. "We must be honest about the thousand little changes since then," he said. "We have new machines now. We do not need to ask the tower to do its old job."

報告還有一項提醒：舊風車可以成為教學場所，卻不能再被當作鎮上的電力來源。「也許可以用小模型示範它以前怎麼運作，」帕特爾建議。「電風扇可以讓模型轉動，但真正的葉片完全是另一回事。」韓爺爺安靜地聽著，想起村子曾需要風車幫忙的年代。「這些年有上千件小小的改變，」他說。「我們現在有新的機器，不必再叫老風車做以前的工作。」

Mrs. May sent a message asking whether Erin would be home for dinner. Erin answered that she would. The wind outside had grown stronger, and Nora said the meeting should end early. On the way out, Erin met a nurse who lived near the temple. The nurse said the temple yard had sheltered people during many storms. "A good building is cared for before trouble comes," she said. Erin thought about the roof and the fence. The windmill had been left alone too long. Fixing it was not simply a matter of putting a new color on old wood.

梅太太傳訊息問艾琳會不會回家吃晚餐，艾琳回答會。外面的風越來越大，諾拉說會議應該提早結束。離開時，艾琳遇見一位住在寺廟附近的護理師。護理師說，寺廟院子曾在多次風雨中為居民提供遮蔽。「好的建築需要在出事之前就好好照顧，」她說。艾琳想到風車的屋頂與圍欄。老風車已經太久沒有人維護。修復它，絕不只是替舊木頭塗上新顏色。

The secretary showed Erin an older drawing of the hill. It marked a big tree near the tower that had fallen many years ago. "That is why the old road bends," she explained. Erin had wondered about that bend on her map. She now understood that even a path could hold pieces of history. She asked permission to place the drawing beside the new plan, making sure people could see which parts were old and which were proposed. Nora agreed. "We will label the dates so nobody gets confused," she said. On the way home, Erin noticed how the branches of living trees moved in the wind. They could bend and grow; the old tower needed help to remain strong. She felt more certain that respecting the past meant paying attention to what was true today.

秘書拿給艾琳一張更早的山坡圖，上面畫著塔旁一棵多年前倒下的大樹。「這就是舊道路會轉彎的原因，」她解釋。艾琳之前一直疑惑地圖上的彎道，現在明白連小路也藏著歷史。她請問能不能把舊圖放在新計畫旁，清楚標出哪些是過去存在的、哪些只是提案。諾拉同意。「日期也要標清楚，免得有人混淆，」她說。回家路上，艾琳看著仍活著的樹枝在風中搖動。樹能彎曲、生長；老塔卻需要幫助才能維持結實。她更確信，尊重過去也表示要正視今天的事實。

Back at Mrs. May's house, Erin spread the report across the kitchen table. She did not call it good news or bad news. It was a way to make a real plan. She drew three columns: work that must happen, work that might happen, and ideas that could wait. The roof and brake went into the first column. A learning model went into the second. A bright new coat of blue paint went into the third. Mrs. May brought warm soup. "A plan can be changed when we learn more," she said. Erin folded the pages and felt warmer already.

回到梅太太家，艾琳把報告攤在廚房桌上。她不把它稱為好消息或壞消息，而是當成制定真正計畫的工具。她畫了三欄：「必須做」「可以考慮」「可以等」。屋頂和煞車放在第一欄；教學模型放在第二欄；重新漆上鮮亮藍色則放在第三欄。梅太太端來熱湯。「知道更多之後，計畫也可以改變，」她說。艾琳收起紙張，心裡已經暖和了一些。
