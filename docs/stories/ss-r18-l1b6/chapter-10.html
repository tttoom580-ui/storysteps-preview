安靜角落

The Quiet Corner

The club planned a quieter corner for students who disliked loud sounds. The light through the window looked ordinary, but the news did not. May stopped for a moment, feeling both curious and unsure. Theo did not laugh at the question, even when it sounded strange. They knew from earlier mistakes that moving too fast could make a simple problem harder. The college campus offered enough space for a calm talk. On the first page of the song and drawing, May wrote what was known and left a blank space for what remained uncertain.

社團規劃一個給不喜歡吵鬧聲音的學生使用的安靜角落。 窗外的光線和平常一樣，消息卻一點也不平常。芽衣停了一會兒，既好奇又不確定。就算問題聽來奇怪，哲也也沒有取笑。他們從先前的錯誤學到，行動太快反而可能讓小問題變複雜。大學校園有足夠空間讓大家平靜討論。芽衣在歌曲和圖畫第一頁寫下已知的事，也留下空白給尚未確定的部分。

May was afraid that saying the wrong thing would make the day worse. Theo answered that staying silent could also leave a misunderstanding in place. They practiced one honest sentence and checked that it did not blame anyone unfairly. This took longer than making a joke, but the result was clearer. Near the table, Dr. Chen nodded and let the young people try their own words before offering advice. A small study card showed around, which May copied beside today's notes. Theo asked how to use button in a sentence about their day. In the English practice book, dark appeared on the next line. May read evening aloud, then checked the meaning with Theo. Another card carried fall and a simple drawing by a classmate.

芽衣害怕說錯話會讓事情變糟。哲也回答，保持沉默有時也會讓誤會繼續存在。他們練習說出一句誠實的話，再確認沒有不公平地責怪任何人。這比開玩笑花時間，結果卻更清楚。桌旁的陳博士點頭，先讓年輕人用自己的話嘗試，再提供建議。 一張小小練習卡上寫著「around」（周圍），芽衣把它抄在今日筆記旁。 哲也問起，如何用「button」（按鈕）寫一句與今天有關的話。 英文練習本的下一行出現「dark」（昏暗的）。 芽衣朗讀「evening」（傍晚），再和哲也確認意思。 另一張圖卡上有「fall」（落下），旁邊是同學的簡筆畫。

May designed a sign, and Theo tested how easy it was to find. There was a quiet kind of courage in trying again. May noticed that the last attempt had taught them something, even without a perfect result. Theo offered practical help and asked which part would be most useful. They worked together for a short time and then stopped to check whether they were still following the rule. Dr. Chen praised the careful check more than the speed of the work. The word have gave the group a new example to discuss. Theo put interesting on the board without turning it into a test. They found left in a short reading exercise near the end of the page. May drew a tiny sign beside menu so it would be easier to remember. Theo drew a small picture of potato in the margin of the class story page.

芽衣設計標示，哲也測試是否容易找到。 願意再試一次，是一種安靜的勇氣。芽衣發現，就算上次結果不完美，大家仍學到了一些事。哲也提供實際協助，先問哪一部分最有用。他們一起工作一小段時間，再停下來確認是否仍遵守規則。陳博士稱讚的不是速度，而是這份細心。 「have」（有）這個字讓小組多了一個可以討論的例子。 哲也把「interesting」（有趣的）寫在板上，卻沒有把它變成考試。 他們在頁面末尾的短文練習裡找到「left」（左側）。 芽衣在「menu」（菜單）旁畫上小記號，幫助記憶。 哲也在班級故事頁的空白處畫了「potato」（馬鈴薯）的圖。

They began by asking who might be affected by the next step. Theo thought about the people who had not spoken yet, and May thought about the rule they had already promised to follow. One person offered a quick answer, but the group asked for a reason. A good question was not an attack. It was a way to make the answer safer and kinder. Dr. Chen reminded them that everyone could ask for a pause. A small study card showed rose, which May copied beside today's notes. Theo asked how to use smile in a sentence about their day. In the English practice book, sound appeared on the next line. May read stay aloud, then checked the meaning with Theo. Another card carried student and a simple drawing by a classmate.

大家先問，下一步會影響到誰。哲也想到還沒發言的人，芽衣則想到自己早已承諾遵守的規則。有人很快提出答案，其他人卻請他說明理由。好問題不是攻擊，而是讓答案更安全、更體貼的方法。陳博士提醒大家，每個人都可以要求暫停。 一張小小練習卡上寫著「rose」（玫瑰），芽衣把它抄在今日筆記旁。 哲也問起，如何用「smile」（微笑）寫一句與今天有關的話。 英文練習本的下一行出現「sound」（聲音）。 芽衣朗讀「stay」（留下），再和哲也確認意思。 另一張圖卡上有「student」（學生），旁邊是同學的簡筆畫。

They worked well together without agreeing on everything. The answer made someone smile, but May still asked one final question: was everyone comfortable with what came next? Theo listened to the replies, including the quiet ones. They changed a small detail when one person needed something different. Nothing about the change made the story less meaningful. It helped the respect between classmates belong to more than one person, which was why the work had begun. The word sweet gave the group a new example to discuss. Theo put trouble on the board without turning it into a test. They found usually in a short reading exercise near the end of the page. May drew a tiny sign beside watch so it would be easier to remember. Theo chose worry as a word to explain kindly to a new learner.

即使不是每件事都同意，兩人合作得很好。 答案讓某個人笑了，但芽衣仍問最後一個問題：大家都能接受下一步嗎？哲也聽取回答，包括輕聲說出的意見。有人有不同需求，他們就修改一個小細節。這種修改沒有讓故事失去意義，反而讓同學之間的尊重不只屬於一個人；這也正是大家當初開始的原因。 「sweet」（甜蜜的）這個字讓小組多了一個可以討論的例子。 哲也把「trouble」（困擾）寫在板上，卻沒有把它變成考試。 他們在頁面末尾的短文練習裡找到「usually」（通常）。 芽衣在「watch」（觀看）旁畫上小記號，幫助記憶。 哲也選了「worry」（擔心），準備耐心向初學者說明。

Outside, the air was cooler than it had been at lunch. May looked back once and saw the college campus in the evening light. It seemed like an ordinary place, but there was more to it now: a remembered question, a mistake corrected, and a choice made together. Theo waved from a few steps away. They still had their separate dreams and small worries, yet they also shared one clear memory. Whatever happened next, they could return to that memory without needing to pretend the day had been perfect.

外面的空氣比午餐時涼了些。芽衣回頭望了一眼晚光中的大學校園。它看似平凡，現在卻多了值得記住的問題、改正的錯誤，以及共同做出的選擇。哲也在幾步外揮手。他們仍有各自的夢想和小煩惱，也有一段共同的清晰回憶。無論接下來發生什麼，都不必假裝今天完美，就能想起它的價值。
