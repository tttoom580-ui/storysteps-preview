A Page from the Song

我的網友坐在隔壁班 · 第 9 / 25 章 · 1000以下 · 英中對照 · 校訂中

用互動版查詞、朗讀與收藏

Theo accidentally left a music notebook on a public table. At first, May thought the problem could be solved before the next bell. A closer look showed that someone else's wishes mattered too. Theo listened without interrupting, while Dr. Chen watched the group from nearby. They did not have to agree on everything immediately. There was time to separate a real observation from a story someone had only imagined. May took out the song and drawing and asked what should be checked first.

哲也不小心把音樂筆記本留在公共桌上。 起初，芽衣以為下次鐘響前就能解決問題。但仔細看過後才發現，別人的願望也很重要。哲也沒有打斷，靜靜傾聽，陳博士則在附近看著大家。眾人不必立刻同意每件事，還有時間分清真實觀察與想像中的說法。芽衣拿出歌曲和圖畫，問大家應先確認什麼。

May was afraid that saying the wrong thing would make the day worse. Theo answered that staying silent could also leave a misunderstanding in place. They practiced one honest sentence and checked that it did not blame anyone unfairly. This took longer than making a joke, but the result was clearer. Near the table, Dr. Chen nodded and let the young people try their own words before offering advice. Theo chose bear as a word to explain kindly to a new learner. A small study card showed brave, which May copied beside today's notes. Theo asked how to use farmer in a sentence about their day. In the English practice book, foreigner appeared on the next line. May read keep aloud, then checked the meaning with Theo.

芽衣害怕說錯話會讓事情變糟。哲也回答，保持沉默有時也會讓誤會繼續存在。他們練習說出一句誠實的話，再確認沒有不公平地責怪任何人。這比開玩笑花時間，結果卻更清楚。桌旁的陳博士點頭，先讓年輕人用自己的話嘗試，再提供建議。 哲也選了「bear」（承受），準備耐心向初學者說明。 一張小小練習卡上寫著「brave」（勇敢），芽衣把它抄在今日筆記旁。 哲也問起，如何用「farmer」（農夫）寫一句與今天有關的話。 英文練習本的下一行出現「foreigner」（外國人）。 芽衣朗讀「keep」（保留），再和哲也確認意思。

May handed it back unopened, even though she saw a familiar blue cover. There was a quiet kind of courage in trying again. May noticed that the last attempt had taught them something, even without a perfect result. Theo offered practical help and asked which part would be most useful. They worked together for a short time and then stopped to check whether they were still following the rule. Dr. Chen praised the careful check more than the speed of the work. Another card carried lonely and a simple drawing by a classmate. The word lose gave the group a new example to discuss. Theo put meat on the board without turning it into a test. They found milk in a short reading exercise near the end of the page. May drew a tiny sign beside nothing so it would be easier to remember.

芽衣原封不動還給他，儘管她看見熟悉的藍色封面。 願意再試一次，是一種安靜的勇氣。芽衣發現，就算上次結果不完美，大家仍學到了一些事。哲也提供實際協助，先問哪一部分最有用。他們一起工作一小段時間，再停下來確認是否仍遵守規則。陳博士稱讚的不是速度，而是這份細心。 另一張圖卡上有「lonely」（孤單的），旁邊是同學的簡筆畫。 「lose」（失去）這個字讓小組多了一個可以討論的例子。 哲也把「meat」（肉）寫在板上，卻沒有把它變成考試。 他們在頁面末尾的短文練習裡找到「milk」（牛奶）。 芽衣在「nothing」（沒有任何東西）旁畫上小記號，幫助記憶。

They began by asking who might be affected by the next step. Theo thought about the people who had not spoken yet, and May thought about the rule they had already promised to follow. One person offered a quick answer, but the group asked for a reason. A good question was not an attack. It was a way to make the answer safer and kinder. Dr. Chen reminded them that everyone could ask for a pause. Theo chose person as a word to explain kindly to a new learner. A small study card showed photograph, which May copied beside today's notes. Theo asked how to use ring in a sentence about their day. In the English practice book, sad appeared on the next line. May read singer aloud, then checked the meaning with Theo.

大家先問，下一步會影響到誰。哲也想到還沒發言的人，芽衣則想到自己早已承諾遵守的規則。有人很快提出答案，其他人卻請他說明理由。好問題不是攻擊，而是讓答案更安全、更體貼的方法。陳博士提醒大家，每個人都可以要求暫停。 哲也選了「person」（人），準備耐心向初學者說明。 一張小小練習卡上寫著「photograph」（照片），芽衣把它抄在今日筆記旁。 哲也問起，如何用「ring」（環狀光圈）寫一句與今天有關的話。 英文練習本的下一行出現「sad」（難過的）。 芽衣朗讀「singer」（歌手），再和哲也確認意思。

Theo thanked her for respecting something private. The answer made someone smile, but May still asked one final question: was everyone comfortable with what came next? Theo listened to the replies, including the quiet ones. They changed a small detail when one person needed something different. Nothing about the change made the story less meaningful. It helped the respect between classmates belong to more than one person, which was why the work had begun. Another card carried toilet and a simple drawing by a classmate. The word wall gave the group a new example to discuss. Theo put warm on the board without turning it into a test. They found well in a short reading exercise near the end of the page. May drew a tiny sign beside whose so it would be easier to remember.

哲也感謝她尊重私人筆記。 答案讓某個人笑了，但芽衣仍問最後一個問題：大家都能接受下一步嗎？哲也聽取回答，包括輕聲說出的意見。有人有不同需求，他們就修改一個小細節。這種修改沒有讓故事失去意義，反而讓同學之間的尊重不只屬於一個人；這也正是大家當初開始的原因。 另一張圖卡上有「toilet」（洗手間），旁邊是同學的簡筆畫。 「wall」（牆）這個字讓小組多了一個可以討論的例子。 哲也把「warm」（溫暖的）寫在板上，卻沒有把它變成考試。 他們在頁面末尾的短文練習裡找到「well」（還好；健康）。 芽衣在「whose」（誰的）旁畫上小記號，幫助記憶。

The hallway grew quiet, and May finally had time to think. A good friend had not supplied every answer. Instead, Theo had left space for another idea and had stayed when the first idea failed. That was harder to notice than a loud victory, but it was real. May made a promise to remember the person as well as the task. The respect between classmates was important; so were the people who helped shape it. With that thought, the next chapter of their ordinary and extraordinary days felt possible.

走廊逐漸安靜，芽衣終於有時間思考。好朋友不是替自己提供每個答案；哲也反而留下思考空間，也在第一個想法失敗時陪在身旁。這比熱鬧的勝利難以察覺，卻是真實的。芽衣答應自己，不只記得任務，也要記得參與的人。同學之間的尊重重要，幫忙塑造它的人也同樣重要。帶著這個念頭，他們平凡又不平凡的日子可以繼續寫下去。
