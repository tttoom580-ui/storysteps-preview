Paperboat wrote that he feared losing a friend by saying the wrong thing. Nobody had planned for this moment. The room was full of ordinary sounds, yet May felt that something important had changed. Theo waited beside the door and let the first surprise settle. It was tempting to find an easy answer before anyone knew the whole story. Instead, they looked carefully at what was truly in front of them. May opened the song and drawing and made a small note about the day.

紙船寫道，他怕說錯話而失去朋友。 沒有人預料到這一刻。大學校園充滿平常的聲音，芽衣卻感覺有件重要的事改變了。哲也站在門旁，讓最初的驚訝慢慢沉澱。大家很容易在弄清事情之前就急著下結論，但他們決定先看清眼前真正發生的事。芽衣翻開歌曲和圖畫，記下今天的小小發現。

The first idea sounded simple, and that was useful. Still, May wanted to know whether it respected everyone's choice. Theo suggested changing only one part rather than throwing the whole plan away. They drew two small boxes on a page: what we can do and what we must ask about. The list made the classroom disagreement easier to discuss without turning a friend into an enemy. Theo asked how to use basket in a sentence about their day. In the English practice book, belt appeared on the next line. May read check aloud, then checked the meaning with Theo. Another card carried color and a simple drawing by a classmate. The word dear gave the group a new example to discuss.

第一個想法聽起來很簡單，這是好事。但芽衣還想確認是否尊重每個人的選擇。哲也建議只修改其中一部分，不必推翻整個計畫。他們在紙上畫兩個方框：我們能做的事，以及必須先詢問的事。有了這份清單，討論教室裡的意見不同便容易多了，也不會把朋友當成敵人。 哲也問起，如何用「basket」（籃子）寫一句與今天有關的話。 英文練習本的下一行出現「belt」（腰帶）。 芽衣朗讀「check」（檢查），再和哲也確認意思。 另一張圖卡上有「color」（顏色），旁邊是同學的簡筆畫。 「dear」（親愛的）這個字讓小組多了一個可以討論的例子。

May answered that a careful apology could be more useful than perfect words. The plan became real only when someone took the first small action. May checked the time, Theo checked the shared materials, and Dr. Chen confirmed which steps needed adult approval. The group left a clear record so that a person arriving later could understand the decision. They also agreed on what to do if something went wrong. It was not exciting in the way stories often promised, but it let everyone move forward with confidence. Theo put hate on the board without turning it into a test. They found health in a short reading exercise near the end of the page. May drew a tiny sign beside hospital so it would be easier to remember. Theo chose if as a word to explain kindly to a new learner. A small study card showed joke, which May copied beside today's notes.

芽衣回答，認真道歉比完美措辭更有用。 有人做出第一個小小行動時，計畫才真正開始。芽衣確認時間，哲也檢查共用材料，陳博士確認哪些步驟需要大人同意。小組留下清楚紀錄，讓稍後到的人也能理解決定。他們還約好若事情出錯該怎麼辦。這不像故事裡常見的刺激冒險，卻讓每個人都更有信心往前走。 哲也把「hate」（討厭）寫在板上，卻沒有把它變成考試。 他們在頁面末尾的短文練習裡找到「health」（健康）。 芽衣在「hospital」（醫院）旁畫上小記號，幫助記憶。 哲也選了「if」（如果），準備耐心向初學者說明。 一張小小練習卡上寫著「joke」（玩笑），芽衣把它抄在今日筆記旁。

For a minute, the group spoke all at once. May raised a hand and suggested taking turns. Theo repeated the question in simpler words, so that even a quiet student could follow the talk. Nobody needed to win an argument. They needed to learn which part of the problem could be handled now and which part needed more help. The classroom disagreement remained difficult, but it no longer felt impossible. Theo asked how to use kite in a sentence about their day. In the English practice book, library appeared on the next line. May read often aloud, then checked the meaning with Theo. Another card carried picture and a simple drawing by a classmate. The word player gave the group a new example to discuss.

有一分鐘，大家幾乎同時開口。芽衣舉起手，提議輪流說話。哲也用更簡單的詞重述問題，讓安靜的學生也能跟上。沒有人需要爭贏；重要的是分清哪些事現在能做、哪些事需要更多協助。教室裡的意見不同仍然困難，卻不再讓人覺得無法處理。 哲也問起，如何用「kite」（風箏）寫一句與今天有關的話。 英文練習本的下一行出現「library」（圖書館）。 芽衣朗讀「often」（經常），再和哲也確認意思。 另一張圖卡上有「picture」（圖畫），旁邊是同學的簡筆畫。 「player」（演奏者；參與者）這個字讓小組多了一個可以討論的例子。

The message helped her understand her own sharp words toward Theo. They gathered the papers and put the shared things back in their places. May could feel that the room had changed even though the desks had not moved. Theo pointed to the page where the group had written its first worry. The words were still there, but they no longer told the whole story. Now there was also an action, a result, and a reason to look forward to the next meeting. Theo put send on the board without turning it into a test. They found thin in a short reading exercise near the end of the page. May drew a tiny sign beside those so it would be easier to remember. Theo chose useful as a word to explain kindly to a new learner. A small study card showed white, which May copied beside today's notes.

這則訊息也讓她反思自己對哲也太尖銳的話。 他們整理紙張，把共用物品放回原處。雖然桌子沒有移動，芽衣卻覺得房間已經不同。哲也指出小組寫下第一個擔憂的那一頁。那些字還在，卻已無法概括整件事。現在多了行動、結果，以及期待下次見面的理由。 哲也把「send」（寄送）寫在板上，卻沒有把它變成考試。 他們在頁面末尾的短文練習裡找到「thin」（薄的）。 芽衣在「those」（那些）旁畫上小記號，幫助記憶。 哲也選了「useful」（有用），準備耐心向初學者說明。 一張小小練習卡上寫著「white」（白色的），芽衣把它抄在今日筆記旁。

Near the end of the day, May stayed beside Theo for one more minute. They remembered how strange the morning had felt. A problem that once seemed to fill the whole room had become several smaller questions. Each question could be handled in turn. They did not know what the following day would bring, and that was all right. What they had learned was not a magic answer but a habit: notice a change, ask with care, and let people choose for themselves. May closed the song and drawing and left with a lighter heart.

一天快結束時，芽衣在哲也身邊多待了一分鐘。他們想起早上的奇怪感受。原本像占滿整間房間的難題，現在分成幾個較小的問題，可以逐一處理。他們不知道明天會帶來什麼，但這沒有關係。今天學到的不是神奇答案，而是一種習慣：留意變化、仔細發問，也讓別人自己選擇。芽衣闔上歌曲和圖畫，帶著較輕鬆的心情離開。
