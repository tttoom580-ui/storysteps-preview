一樣的錯誤答案

The Same Wrong Answer

我的網友坐在隔壁班 · 第 8 / 25 章 · 1000以下 · 英中對照 · 校訂中

May and Theo made the same funny mistake on a club planning sheet. The day began with a small question and soon became more interesting than anyone expected. May could hear other students talking, but did not hurry to join their guesses. Theo pointed out a detail that had been easy to miss. They stood together in the college campus, where the same familiar objects suddenly seemed to tell a different story. May kept the song and drawing close, not as a perfect answer, but as a place to begin.

芽衣與哲也在社團計畫表上犯了一樣好笑的錯。 這一天從一個小疑問開始，後來卻比大家預想的更有趣。芽衣聽到其他同學交談，沒有急著加入猜測。哲也指出了一個容易忽略的細節。他們站在大學校園裡，熟悉的東西忽然像在說另一段故事。芽衣把歌曲和圖畫帶在身邊，那不是完美答案，而是一個開始。

For a minute, the group spoke all at once. May raised a hand and suggested taking turns. Theo repeated the question in simpler words, so that even a quiet student could follow the talk. Nobody needed to win an argument. They needed to learn which part of the problem could be handled now and which part needed more help. The classroom disagreement remained difficult, but it no longer felt impossible. Theo drew a small picture of book in the margin of the class story page. Theo chose boring as a word to explain kindly to a new learner. A small study card showed dangerous, which May copied beside today's notes. Theo asked how to use dollar in a sentence about their day. In the English practice book, each appeared on the next line.

有一分鐘，大家幾乎同時開口。芽衣舉起手，提議輪流說話。哲也用更簡單的詞重述問題，讓安靜的學生也能跟上。沒有人需要爭贏；重要的是分清哪些事現在能做、哪些事需要更多協助。教室裡的意見不同仍然困難，卻不再讓人覺得無法處理。 哲也在班級故事頁的空白處畫了「book」（書）的圖。 哲也選了「boring」（無聊的），準備耐心向初學者說明。 一張小小練習卡上寫著「dangerous」（危險的），芽衣把它抄在今日筆記旁。 哲也問起，如何用「dollar」（美元）寫一句與今天有關的話。 英文練習本的下一行出現「each」（每個）。

They corrected it together instead of deciding who was to blame. Once everyone understood the choice, the next step was smaller than it had seemed. May spoke first and waited for an answer. Theo watched the other person's face instead of trying to hurry the talk. There was room to say no, to ask for an explanation, or to think for another minute. The group used the song and drawing as a guide, not as an order that could never be changed. May read else aloud, then checked the meaning with Theo. Another card carried file and a simple drawing by a classmate. The word fork gave the group a new example to discuss. Theo put full on the board without turning it into a test. They found idea in a short reading exercise near the end of the page.

兩人一起改正，沒有爭誰該負責。 當所有人都了解選擇後，下一步其實沒有想像中困難。芽衣先開口，然後等待回答。哲也注意對方的表情，沒有催促。大家可以說不、要求解釋，或多想一分鐘。小組把歌曲和圖畫當作指引，而不是絕不能改變的命令。 芽衣朗讀「else」（其他），再和哲也確認意思。 另一張圖卡上有「file」（檔案），旁邊是同學的簡筆畫。 「fork」（叉子）這個字讓小組多了一個可以討論的例子。 哲也把「full」（滿的）寫在板上，卻沒有把它變成考試。 他們在頁面末尾的短文練習裡找到「idea」（點子）。

The first idea sounded simple, and that was useful. Still, May wanted to know whether it respected everyone's choice. Theo suggested changing only one part rather than throwing the whole plan away. They drew two small boxes on a page: what we can do and what we must ask about. The list made the classroom disagreement easier to discuss without turning a friend into an enemy. May drew a tiny sign beside listen so it would be easier to remember. Theo chose mistake as a word to explain kindly to a new learner. A small study card showed museum, which May copied beside today's notes. Theo asked how to use noon in a sentence about their day. In the English practice book, queen appeared on the next line.

第一個想法聽起來很簡單，這是好事。但芽衣還想確認是否尊重每個人的選擇。哲也建議只修改其中一部分，不必推翻整個計畫。他們在紙上畫兩個方框：我們能做的事，以及必須先詢問的事。有了這份清單，討論教室裡的意見不同便容易多了，也不會把朋友當成敵人。 芽衣在「listen」（聽）旁畫上小記號，幫助記憶。 哲也選了「mistake」（錯誤），準備耐心向初學者說明。 一張小小練習卡上寫著「museum」（博物館），芽衣把它抄在今日筆記旁。 哲也問起，如何用「noon」（中午）寫一句與今天有關的話。 英文練習本的下一行出現「queen」（女王）。

For the first time, May laughed with him in person. Before calling the task finished, May went back over the facts. One detail had changed, and another still needed an answer. Theo wrote both down rather than hiding the uncertain part. They could be pleased with progress without pretending that every worry had disappeared. By comparing the new result with the first note, they could see what had actually improved. The respect between classmates still mattered, but now the group knew one more way to protect it. May read roll aloud, then checked the meaning with Theo. Another card carried successful and a simple drawing by a classmate. The word tell gave the group a new example to discuss. Theo drew a small picture of train in the margin of the class story page. They found you in a short reading exercise near the end of the page.

芽衣第一次在現實裡和他一起笑。 在宣布完成前，芽衣重新檢查事實。有一個細節改變了，另一個仍有待解答。哲也把兩者都寫下，沒有隱藏不確定之處。大家可以為進展高興，卻不必假裝所有擔憂都消失。比對新結果與第一張筆記後，他們看出真正改善的部分。同學之間的尊重依然重要，現在小組又多學會一種守護它的方法。 芽衣朗讀「roll」（捲；滾動），再和哲也確認意思。 另一張圖卡上有「successful」（成功的），旁邊是同學的簡筆畫。 「tell」（告訴）這個字讓小組多了一個可以討論的例子。 哲也在班級故事頁的空白處畫了「train」（火車）的圖。 他們在頁面末尾的短文練習裡找到「you」（你；你的）。

When the last bell sounded, May saw that Theo was waiting by the familiar door. The two had not said everything perfectly. They had even disagreed about a detail, yet the disagreement had made their plan clearer. The moment did not need a large speech. A small smile and one honest thank-you were enough. At home, May would remember the most surprising part of the day and write it on the next page. Tomorrow the classroom disagreement might look different again. This time they would know how to begin.

最後一聲鈴響時，芽衣看見哲也在熟悉的門旁等待。兩人不是每句話都說得完美，甚至為某個細節意見不同，但那場分歧讓計畫更清楚。這一刻不需要長篇大論，一個微笑和真誠的道謝就夠了。回家後，芽衣會把今天最驚訝的事寫在下一頁。明天教室裡的意見不同也許又有不同面貌，但這次他們知道如何開始。
