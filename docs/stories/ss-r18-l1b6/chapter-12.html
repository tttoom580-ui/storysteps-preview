午餐時的道歉

The Apology at Lunch

我的網友坐在隔壁班 · 第 12 / 25 章 · 1000以下 · 英中對照 · 校訂中

用互動版查詞、朗讀與收藏

May told Theo she had judged his quiet manner too quickly. The day began with a small question and soon became more interesting than anyone expected. May could hear other students talking, but did not hurry to join their guesses. Theo pointed out a detail that had been easy to miss. They stood together in the college campus, where the same familiar objects suddenly seemed to tell a different story. May kept the song and drawing close, not as a perfect answer, but as a place to begin.

芽衣在午餐時向哲也坦承，自己太快評斷他的寡言。 這一天從一個小疑問開始，後來卻比大家預想的更有趣。芽衣聽到其他同學交談，沒有急著加入猜測。哲也指出了一個容易忽略的細節。他們站在大學校園裡，熟悉的東西忽然像在說另一段故事。芽衣把歌曲和圖畫帶在身邊，那不是完美答案，而是一個開始。

The first idea sounded simple, and that was useful. Still, May wanted to know whether it respected everyone's choice. Theo suggested changing only one part rather than throwing the whole plan away. They drew two small boxes on a page: what we can do and what we must ask about. The list made the classroom disagreement easier to discuss without turning a friend into an enemy. In the English practice book, actor appeared on the next line. May read afternoon aloud, then checked the meaning with Theo. Another card carried band and a simple drawing by a classmate. The word celebrate gave the group a new example to discuss. Theo put corner on the board without turning it into a test.

第一個想法聽起來很簡單，這是好事。但芽衣還想確認是否尊重每個人的選擇。哲也建議只修改其中一部分，不必推翻整個計畫。他們在紙上畫兩個方框：我們能做的事，以及必須先詢問的事。有了這份清單，討論教室裡的意見不同便容易多了，也不會把朋友當成敵人。 英文練習本的下一行出現「actor」（演員）。 芽衣朗讀「afternoon」（下午），再和哲也確認意思。 另一張圖卡上有「band」（樂團），旁邊是同學的簡筆畫。 「celebrate」（慶祝）這個字讓小組多了一個可以討論的例子。 哲也把「corner」（轉角）寫在板上，卻沒有把它變成考試。

Theo admitted that his criticism had sounded unkind. The plan became real only when someone took the first small action. May checked the time, Theo checked the shared materials, and Dr. Chen confirmed which steps needed adult approval. The group left a clear record so that a person arriving later could understand the decision. They also agreed on what to do if something went wrong. It was not exciting in the way stories often promised, but it let everyone move forward with confidence. They found die in a short reading exercise near the end of the page. May drew a tiny sign beside glasses so it would be easier to remember. Theo chose I as a word to explain kindly to a new learner. A small study card showed key, which May copied beside today's notes. Theo asked how to use last in a sentence about their day.

哲也也承認，自己的批評聽起來不夠友善。 有人做出第一個小小行動時，計畫才真正開始。芽衣確認時間，哲也檢查共用材料，陳博士確認哪些步驟需要大人同意。小組留下清楚紀錄，讓稍後到的人也能理解決定。他們還約好若事情出錯該怎麼辦。這不像故事裡常見的刺激冒險，卻讓每個人都更有信心往前走。 他們在頁面末尾的短文練習裡找到「die」（枯死）。 芽衣在「glasses」（眼鏡）旁畫上小記號，幫助記憶。 哲也選了「I」（我），準備耐心向初學者說明。 一張小小練習卡上寫著「key」（鑰匙），芽衣把它抄在今日筆記旁。 哲也問起，如何用「last」（最後的）寫一句與今天有關的話。

For a minute, the group spoke all at once. May raised a hand and suggested taking turns. Theo repeated the question in simpler words, so that even a quiet student could follow the talk. Nobody needed to win an argument. They needed to learn which part of the problem could be handled now and which part needed more help. The classroom disagreement remained difficult, but it no longer felt impossible. In the English practice book, media appeared on the next line. May read more aloud, then checked the meaning with Theo. Another card carried o’clock and a simple drawing by a classmate. The word or gave the group a new example to discuss. Theo put package on the board without turning it into a test.

有一分鐘，大家幾乎同時開口。芽衣舉起手，提議輪流說話。哲也用更簡單的詞重述問題，讓安靜的學生也能跟上。沒有人需要爭贏；重要的是分清哪些事現在能做、哪些事需要更多協助。教室裡的意見不同仍然困難，卻不再讓人覺得無法處理。 英文練習本的下一行出現「media」（媒體）。 芽衣朗讀「more」（更多），再和哲也確認意思。 另一張圖卡上有「o’clock」（整點鐘），旁邊是同學的簡筆畫。 「or」（或）這個字讓小組多了一個可以討論的例子。 哲也把「package」（包裹）寫在板上，卻沒有把它變成考試。

They agreed to ask questions before making hard comments. They gathered the papers and put the shared things back in their places. May could feel that the room had changed even though the desks had not moved. Theo pointed to the page where the group had written its first worry. The words were still there, but they no longer told the whole story. Now there was also an action, a result, and a reason to look forward to the next meeting. They found play in a short reading exercise near the end of the page. May drew a tiny sign beside poor so it would be easier to remember. Theo chose supermarket as a word to explain kindly to a new learner. A small study card showed ticket, which May copied beside today's notes. Theo asked how to use tired in a sentence about their day.

兩人同意，嚴厲評論之前先提出問題。 他們整理紙張，把共用物品放回原處。雖然桌子沒有移動，芽衣卻覺得房間已經不同。哲也指出小組寫下第一個擔憂的那一頁。那些字還在，卻已無法概括整件事。現在多了行動、結果，以及期待下次見面的理由。 他們在頁面末尾的短文練習裡找到「play」（演奏）。 芽衣在「poor」（不足的；昏暗的）旁畫上小記號，幫助記憶。 哲也選了「supermarket」（超市），準備耐心向初學者說明。 一張小小練習卡上寫著「ticket」（門票），芽衣把它抄在今日筆記旁。 哲也問起，如何用「tired」（疲倦的）寫一句與今天有關的話。

When the last bell sounded, May saw that Theo was waiting by the familiar door. The two had not said everything perfectly. They had even disagreed about a detail, yet the disagreement had made their plan clearer. The moment did not need a large speech. A small smile and one honest thank-you were enough. At home, May would remember the most surprising part of the day and write it on the next page. Tomorrow the classroom disagreement might look different again. This time they would know how to begin.

最後一聲鈴響時，芽衣看見哲也在熟悉的門旁等待。兩人不是每句話都說得完美，甚至為某個細節意見不同，但那場分歧讓計畫更清楚。這一刻不需要長篇大論，一個微笑和真誠的道謝就夠了。回家後，芽衣會把今天最驚訝的事寫在下一頁。明天教室裡的意見不同也許又有不同面貌，但這次他們知道如何開始。
