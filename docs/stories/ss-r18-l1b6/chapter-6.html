The campus event room was crowded during a rehearsal. The light through the window looked ordinary, but the news did not. May stopped for a moment, feeling both curious and unsure. Theo did not laugh at the question, even when it sounded strange. They knew from earlier mistakes that moving too fast could make a simple problem harder. The college campus offered enough space for a calm talk. On the first page of the song and drawing, May wrote what was known and left a blank space for what remained uncertain.

大學活動室排練時擠滿人。 窗外的光線和平常一樣，消息卻一點也不平常。芽衣停了一會兒，既好奇又不確定。就算問題聽來奇怪，哲也也沒有取笑。他們從先前的錯誤學到，行動太快反而可能讓小問題變複雜。大學校園有足夠空間讓大家平靜討論。芽衣在歌曲和圖畫第一頁寫下已知的事，也留下空白給尚未確定的部分。

They began by asking who might be affected by the next step. Theo thought about the people who had not spoken yet, and May thought about the rule they had already promised to follow. One person offered a quick answer, but the group asked for a reason. A good question was not an attack. It was a way to make the answer safer and kinder. Dr. Chen reminded them that everyone could ask for a pause. Theo put arm on the board without turning it into a test. They found convenient in a short reading exercise near the end of the page. May drew a tiny sign beside deep so it would be easier to remember. Theo drew a small picture of desk in the margin of the class story page. A small study card showed do, which May copied beside today's notes.

大家先問，下一步會影響到誰。哲也想到還沒發言的人，芽衣則想到自己早已承諾遵守的規則。有人很快提出答案，其他人卻請他說明理由。好問題不是攻擊，而是讓答案更安全、更體貼的方法。陳博士提醒大家，每個人都可以要求暫停。 哲也把「arm」（手臂）寫在板上，卻沒有把它變成考試。 他們在頁面末尾的短文練習裡找到「convenient」（方便的）。 芽衣在「deep」（深處的）旁畫上小記號，幫助記憶。 哲也在班級故事頁的空白處畫了「desk」（桌子）的圖。 一張小小練習卡上寫著「do」（做），芽衣把它抄在今日筆記旁。

Theo helped May move a display to a safer spot with the staff's approval. May chose the part of the work that could be done carefully. Theo handled a different task and promised to report back before making any large change. A short question from Dr. Chen caught an error in their first version. Instead of hiding it, they made a neat correction on the page. That correction did not erase their effort. It showed that the group was learning how to be responsible for its own work. Theo asked how to use envelope in a sentence about their day. In the English practice book, find appeared on the next line. May read fish aloud, then checked the meaning with Theo. Another card carried funny and a simple drawing by a classmate. The word history gave the group a new example to discuss.

哲也在工作人員同意下幫芽衣移動展示架。 芽衣選擇自己能謹慎完成的工作，哲也負責另一項任務，並答應在重大更改前先回報。陳博士的一個簡短問題，讓大家發現第一版中的錯誤。他們沒有掩飾，而是在頁面上整齊修正。這項修正沒有抹去先前的努力，反而證明小組正在學習對自己的工作負責。 哲也問起，如何用「envelope」（信封）寫一句與今天有關的話。 英文練習本的下一行出現「find」（找到）。 芽衣朗讀「fish」（魚），再和哲也確認意思。 另一張圖卡上有「funny」（有趣），旁邊是同學的簡筆畫。 「history」（歷史）這個字讓小組多了一個可以討論的例子。

May was afraid that saying the wrong thing would make the day worse. Theo answered that staying silent could also leave a misunderstanding in place. They practiced one honest sentence and checked that it did not blame anyone unfairly. This took longer than making a joke, but the result was clearer. Near the table, Dr. Chen nodded and let the young people try their own words before offering advice. Theo put however on the board without turning it into a test. They found may in a short reading exercise near the end of the page. May drew a tiny sign beside mud so it would be easier to remember. Theo chose news as a word to explain kindly to a new learner. A small study card showed rise, which May copied beside today's notes.

芽衣害怕說錯話會讓事情變糟。哲也回答，保持沉默有時也會讓誤會繼續存在。他們練習說出一句誠實的話，再確認沒有不公平地責怪任何人。這比開玩笑花時間，結果卻更清楚。桌旁的陳博士點頭，先讓年輕人用自己的話嘗試，再提供建議。 哲也把「however」（然而）寫在板上，卻沒有把它變成考試。 他們在頁面末尾的短文練習裡找到「may」（可能）。 芽衣在「mud」（泥巴）旁畫上小記號，幫助記憶。 哲也選了「news」（新聞），準備耐心向初學者說明。 一張小小練習卡上寫著「rise」（升起），芽衣把它抄在今日筆記旁。

May thanked him for a practical action she had not expected. Dr. Chen asked the group to explain its choice in everyday words. May tried once, paused, and then gave a shorter answer. Theo added an important detail that had been left out. The explanation became clear enough for another person to check. That mattered more than making their decision sound grand. The respect between classmates could grow only if other people understood how it worked. Theo asked how to use seat in a sentence about their day. In the English practice book, shop appeared on the next line. May read speak aloud, then checked the meaning with Theo. Another card carried surprised and a simple drawing by a classmate. The word welcome gave the group a new example to discuss.

芽衣感謝他的實際幫忙，這讓她很意外。 陳博士請小組用平常的話解釋選擇。芽衣試了一次，停頓後改用更短的回答。哲也補充了一個漏掉的重要細節。解釋變得足夠清楚，讓別人也能核對。這比把決定說得偉大更重要。只有其他人理解做法，同學之間的尊重才有機會繼續成長。 哲也問起，如何用「seat」（座位）寫一句與今天有關的話。 英文練習本的下一行出現「shop」（商店）。 芽衣朗讀「speak」（說話），再和哲也確認意思。 另一張圖卡上有「surprised」（驚訝），旁邊是同學的簡筆畫。 「welcome」（歡迎）這個字讓小組多了一個可以討論的例子。

Outside, the air was cooler than it had been at lunch. May looked back once and saw the college campus in the evening light. It seemed like an ordinary place, but there was more to it now: a remembered question, a mistake corrected, and a choice made together. Theo waved from a few steps away. They still had their separate dreams and small worries, yet they also shared one clear memory. Whatever happened next, they could return to that memory without needing to pretend the day had been perfect.

外面的空氣比午餐時涼了些。芽衣回頭望了一眼晚光中的大學校園。它看似平凡，現在卻多了值得記住的問題、改正的錯誤，以及共同做出的選擇。哲也在幾步外揮手。他們仍有各自的夢想和小煩惱，也有一段共同的清晰回憶。無論接下來發生什麼，都不必假裝今天完美，就能想起它的價值。
