An Online Promise

我的網友坐在隔壁班 · 第 5 / 25 章 · 1000以下 · 英中對照 · 校訂中

Moonbird and Paperboat agreed to avoid guessing each other's real identity. At first, May thought the problem could be solved before the next bell. A closer look showed that someone else's wishes mattered too. Theo listened without interrupting, while Dr. Chen watched the group from nearby. They did not have to agree on everything immediately. There was time to separate a real observation from a story someone had only imagined. May took out the song and drawing and asked what should be checked first.

月鳥與紙船約好，不隨意猜測彼此真實身分。 起初，芽衣以為下次鐘響前就能解決問題。但仔細看過後才發現，別人的願望也很重要。哲也沒有打斷，靜靜傾聽，陳博士則在附近看著大家。眾人不必立刻同意每件事，還有時間分清真實觀察與想像中的說法。芽衣拿出歌曲和圖畫，問大家應先確認什麼。

They began by asking who might be affected by the next step. Theo thought about the people who had not spoken yet, and May thought about the rule they had already promised to follow. One person offered a quick answer, but the group asked for a reason. A good question was not an attack. It was a way to make the answer safer and kinder. Dr. Chen reminded them that everyone could ask for a pause. The word bank gave the group a new example to discuss. Theo put farm on the board without turning it into a test. They found feed in a short reading exercise near the end of the page. May drew a tiny sign beside letter so it would be easier to remember. Theo chose many as a word to explain kindly to a new learner.

大家先問，下一步會影響到誰。哲也想到還沒發言的人，芽衣則想到自己早已承諾遵守的規則。有人很快提出答案，其他人卻請他說明理由。好問題不是攻擊，而是讓答案更安全、更體貼的方法。陳博士提醒大家，每個人都可以要求暫停。 「bank」（銀行）這個字讓小組多了一個可以討論的例子。 哲也把「farm」（農場）寫在板上，卻沒有把它變成考試。 他們在頁面末尾的短文練習裡找到「feed」（餵食）。 芽衣在「letter」（信）旁畫上小記號，幫助記憶。 哲也選了「many」（許多），準備耐心向初學者說明。

They decided to share creative work, not addresses or school schedules. May chose the part of the work that could be done carefully. Theo handled a different task and promised to report back before making any large change. A short question from Dr. Chen caught an error in their first version. Instead of hiding it, they made a neat correction on the page. That correction did not erase their effort. It showed that the group was learning how to be responsible for its own work. A small study card showed moon, which May copied beside today's notes. Theo asked how to use movie in a sentence about their day. In the English practice book, oil appeared on the next line. May read sell aloud, then checked the meaning with Theo. Another card carried slow and a simple drawing by a classmate.

兩人決定只分享作品，不傳住址或課表。 芽衣選擇自己能謹慎完成的工作，哲也負責另一項任務，並答應在重大更改前先回報。陳博士的一個簡短問題，讓大家發現第一版中的錯誤。他們沒有掩飾，而是在頁面上整齊修正。這項修正沒有抹去先前的努力，反而證明小組正在學習對自己的工作負責。 一張小小練習卡上寫著「moon」（月亮），芽衣把它抄在今日筆記旁。 哲也問起，如何用「movie」（電影）寫一句與今天有關的話。 英文練習本的下一行出現「oil」（油）。 芽衣朗讀「sell」（販賣），再和哲也確認意思。 另一張圖卡上有「slow」（緩慢地），旁邊是同學的簡筆畫。

May was afraid that saying the wrong thing would make the day worse. Theo answered that staying silent could also leave a misunderstanding in place. They practiced one honest sentence and checked that it did not blame anyone unfairly. This took longer than making a joke, but the result was clearer. Near the table, Dr. Chen nodded and let the young people try their own words before offering advice. The word straight gave the group a new example to discuss. Theo put strange on the board without turning it into a test. They found string in a short reading exercise near the end of the page. May drew a tiny sign beside teenager so it would be easier to remember. Theo chose think as a word to explain kindly to a new learner.

芽衣害怕說錯話會讓事情變糟。哲也回答，保持沉默有時也會讓誤會繼續存在。他們練習說出一句誠實的話，再確認沒有不公平地責怪任何人。這比開玩笑花時間，結果卻更清楚。桌旁的陳博士點頭，先讓年輕人用自己的話嘗試，再提供建議。 「straight」（直接地）這個字讓小組多了一個可以討論的例子。 哲也把「strange」（奇怪）寫在板上，卻沒有把它變成考試。 他們在頁面末尾的短文練習裡找到「string」（線）。 芽衣在「teenager」（青少年）旁畫上小記號，幫助記憶。 哲也選了「think」（思考），準備耐心向初學者說明。

Their messages became easier because the boundary was clear. Dr. Chen asked the group to explain its choice in everyday words. May tried once, paused, and then gave a shorter answer. Theo added an important detail that had been left out. The explanation became clear enough for another person to check. That mattered more than making their decision sound grand. The respect between classmates could grow only if other people understood how it worked. A small study card showed through, which May copied beside today's notes. Theo asked how to use toe in a sentence about their day. In the English practice book, topic appeared on the next line. May read where aloud, then checked the meaning with Theo. Another card carried yellow and a simple drawing by a classmate.

界線清楚後，兩人的訊息聊得更自在。 陳博士請小組用平常的話解釋選擇。芽衣試了一次，停頓後改用更短的回答。哲也補充了一個漏掉的重要細節。解釋變得足夠清楚，讓別人也能核對。這比把決定說得偉大更重要。只有其他人理解做法，同學之間的尊重才有機會繼續成長。 一張小小練習卡上寫著「through」（經過；透過），芽衣把它抄在今日筆記旁。 哲也問起，如何用「toe」（腳趾）寫一句與今天有關的話。 英文練習本的下一行出現「topic」（主題）。 芽衣朗讀「where」（哪裡），再和哲也確認意思。 另一張圖卡上有「yellow」（黃色），旁邊是同學的簡筆畫。

The hallway grew quiet, and May finally had time to think. A good friend had not supplied every answer. Instead, Theo had left space for another idea and had stayed when the first idea failed. That was harder to notice than a loud victory, but it was real. May made a promise to remember the person as well as the task. The respect between classmates was important; so were the people who helped shape it. With that thought, the next chapter of their ordinary and extraordinary days felt possible.

走廊逐漸安靜，芽衣終於有時間思考。好朋友不是替自己提供每個答案；哲也反而留下思考空間，也在第一個想法失敗時陪在身旁。這比熱鬧的勝利難以察覺，卻是真實的。芽衣答應自己，不只記得任務，也要記得參與的人。同學之間的尊重重要，幫忙塑造它的人也同樣重要。帶著這個念頭，他們平凡又不平凡的日子可以繼續寫下去。
