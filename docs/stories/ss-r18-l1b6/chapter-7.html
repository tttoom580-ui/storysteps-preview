沒有名字的歌

A Song with No Name

Paperboat sent a short original song about feeling lost in a large school. Nobody had planned for this moment. The room was full of ordinary sounds, yet May felt that something important had changed. Theo waited beside the door and let the first surprise settle. It was tempting to find an easy answer before anyone knew the whole story. Instead, they looked carefully at what was truly in front of them. May opened the song and drawing and made a small note about the day.

紙船傳來一首自己寫的短歌，說在大校園迷路的感覺。 沒有人預料到這一刻。大學校園充滿平常的聲音，芽衣卻感覺有件重要的事改變了。哲也站在門旁，讓最初的驚訝慢慢沉澱。大家很容易在弄清事情之前就急著下結論，但他們決定先看清眼前真正發生的事。芽衣翻開歌曲和圖畫，記下今天的小小發現。

For a minute, the group spoke all at once. May raised a hand and suggested taking turns. Theo repeated the question in simpler words, so that even a quiet student could follow the talk. Nobody needed to win an argument. They needed to learn which part of the problem could be handled now and which part needed more help. The classroom disagreement remained difficult, but it no longer felt impossible. They found by in a short reading exercise near the end of the page. May drew a tiny sign beside chocolate so it would be easier to remember. Theo chose class as a word to explain kindly to a new learner. A small study card showed collect, which May copied beside today's notes. Theo asked how to use course in a sentence about their day.

有一分鐘，大家幾乎同時開口。芽衣舉起手，提議輪流說話。哲也用更簡單的詞重述問題，讓安靜的學生也能跟上。沒有人需要爭贏；重要的是分清哪些事現在能做、哪些事需要更多協助。教室裡的意見不同仍然困難，卻不再讓人覺得無法處理。 他們在頁面末尾的短文練習裡找到「by」（經由；在旁）。 芽衣在「chocolate」（巧克力）旁畫上小記號，幫助記憶。 哲也選了「class」（課堂），準備耐心向初學者說明。 一張小小練習卡上寫著「collect」（收集），芽衣把它抄在今日筆記旁。 哲也問起，如何用「course」（課程）寫一句與今天有關的話。

May asked whether she could draw a picture inspired by the song. Once everyone understood the choice, the next step was smaller than it had seemed. May spoke first and waited for an answer. Theo watched the other person's face instead of trying to hurry the talk. There was room to say no, to ask for an explanation, or to think for another minute. The group used the song and drawing as a guide, not as an order that could never be changed. In the English practice book, flower appeared on the next line. May read hour aloud, then checked the meaning with Theo. Another card carried housewife and a simple drawing by a classmate. The word large gave the group a new example to discuss. Theo put let on the board without turning it into a test.

芽衣問是否可以為這首歌畫插圖。 當所有人都了解選擇後，下一步其實沒有想像中困難。芽衣先開口，然後等待回答。哲也注意對方的表情，沒有催促。大家可以說不、要求解釋，或多想一分鐘。小組把歌曲和圖畫當作指引，而不是絕不能改變的命令。 英文練習本的下一行出現「flower」（花）。 芽衣朗讀「hour」（小時），再和哲也確認意思。 另一張圖卡上有「housewife」（家庭主婦），旁邊是同學的簡筆畫。 「large」（大的）這個字讓小組多了一個可以討論的例子。 哲也把「let」（讓）寫在板上，卻沒有把它變成考試。

The first idea sounded simple, and that was useful. Still, May wanted to know whether it respected everyone's choice. Theo suggested changing only one part rather than throwing the whole plan away. They drew two small boxes on a page: what we can do and what we must ask about. The list made the classroom disagreement easier to discuss without turning a friend into an enemy. They found list in a short reading exercise near the end of the page. May drew a tiny sign beside main so it would be easier to remember. Theo chose note as a word to explain kindly to a new learner. A small study card showed popular, which May copied beside today's notes. Theo asked how to use rabbit in a sentence about their day.

第一個想法聽起來很簡單，這是好事。但芽衣還想確認是否尊重每個人的選擇。哲也建議只修改其中一部分，不必推翻整個計畫。他們在紙上畫兩個方框：我們能做的事，以及必須先詢問的事。有了這份清單，討論教室裡的意見不同便容易多了，也不會把朋友當成敵人。 他們在頁面末尾的短文練習裡找到「list」（清單）。 芽衣在「main」（主要的）旁畫上小記號，幫助記憶。 哲也選了「note」（筆記；便條），準備耐心向初學者說明。 一張小小練習卡上寫著「popular」（受歡迎的），芽衣把它抄在今日筆記旁。 哲也問起，如何用「rabbit」（兔子）寫一句與今天有關的話。

Paperboat agreed, and their first shared work began. Before calling the task finished, May went back over the facts. One detail had changed, and another still needed an answer. Theo wrote both down rather than hiding the uncertain part. They could be pleased with progress without pretending that every worry had disappeared. By comparing the new result with the first note, they could see what had actually improved. The respect between classmates still mattered, but now the group knew one more way to protect it. In the English practice book, real appeared on the next line. May read start aloud, then checked the meaning with Theo. Another card carried there and a simple drawing by a classmate. The word until gave the group a new example to discuss. Theo put west on the board without turning it into a test.

紙船同意了，兩人的第一件合作作品就此開始。 在宣布完成前，芽衣重新檢查事實。有一個細節改變了，另一個仍有待解答。哲也把兩者都寫下，沒有隱藏不確定之處。大家可以為進展高興，卻不必假裝所有擔憂都消失。比對新結果與第一張筆記後，他們看出真正改善的部分。同學之間的尊重依然重要，現在小組又多學會一種守護它的方法。 英文練習本的下一行出現「real」（真實的）。 芽衣朗讀「start」（開始），再和哲也確認意思。 另一張圖卡上有「there」（那裡），旁邊是同學的簡筆畫。 「until」（直到）這個字讓小組多了一個可以討論的例子。 哲也把「west」（西方；西門）寫在板上，卻沒有把它變成考試。

Near the end of the day, May stayed beside Theo for one more minute. They remembered how strange the morning had felt. A problem that once seemed to fill the whole room had become several smaller questions. Each question could be handled in turn. They did not know what the following day would bring, and that was all right. What they had learned was not a magic answer but a habit: notice a change, ask with care, and let people choose for themselves. May closed the song and drawing and left with a lighter heart.

一天快結束時，芽衣在哲也身邊多待了一分鐘。他們想起早上的奇怪感受。原本像占滿整間房間的難題，現在分成幾個較小的問題，可以逐一處理。他們不知道明天會帶來什麼，但這沒有關係。今天學到的不是神奇答案，而是一種習慣：留意變化、仔細發問，也讓別人自己選擇。芽衣闔上歌曲和圖畫，帶著較輕鬆的心情離開。
