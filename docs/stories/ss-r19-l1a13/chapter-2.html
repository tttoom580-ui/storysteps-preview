Sol fell asleep in math class, and Mina heard a quiet fan inside his chest. Before anyone called the day strange, Mina had noticed how quiet the school hall could become when people were worried. The smallest sounds seemed clear, and the low sound of the hallway clock made the place feel both familiar and new. Rather than act as if every answer was obvious, Mina opened the class notebook and began with what had really happened. The word “cousin” appeared in an example someone had written earlier, and Sol asked if the example fit their situation. “Let us keep ‘lip’ in our word notes,” Mina said. “We can check its meaning again if we need it.” In the corner of the class notebook, Mina found the word “sunny” beside an old drawing and stopped to ask what it meant.

A friend joked about charging him, but Sol said that jokes about his body hurt. This was the point where a simple wish met the world as it really was. The answer took longer than either had expected. They agreed not to build the rest of their plan on a fact they had never checked.
