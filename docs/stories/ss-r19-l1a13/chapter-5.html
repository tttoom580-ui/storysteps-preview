Sol remembered the day he helped a lost child find her classroom. Some days begin with a plan; others begin with a question no one had expected. This one gave Mina both. At the school hall, people were coming and going, each carrying a different idea about what was important. The class notebook made it possible to hold on to a few clear details while the rest of the world kept moving. The word “frog” appeared in an example someone had written earlier, and Sol asked if the example fit their situation. “Let us keep ‘Ms.’ in our word notes,” Mina said. “We can check its meaning again if we need it.” In the corner of the class notebook, Mina found the word “snow” beside an old drawing and stopped to ask what it meant.
