At the city repair shop, engineer Ivo explained that Sol belonged to a school trial program. The school hall had its own familiar rhythm. Mina knew the sound of footsteps there, yet something about this moment called for a closer look. It was tempting to make a quick story out of a small surprise. Instead, Mina looked for one thing that could be checked and one person who deserved to be heard. There was room in the class notebook for questions as well as answers, and today the questions were beginning to matter. In the corner of the class notebook, Mina found the word “bridge” beside an old drawing and stopped to ask what it meant. “pull,” Sol read from the practice page, then tried to connect the word to something they could actually see. A bottle of water stood beside the notebook, ready for a long day.

在市區維修店，工程師艾沃說索爾屬於學校試驗計畫。學校走廊有自己熟悉的節奏。米娜熟悉那裡的腳步聲，但此刻某件事值得更仔細地看。一個小驚喜很容易讓人立刻編出解釋。米娜卻先尋找一件可核對的事，以及一位需要被傾聽的人。班級筆記本裡不只可以寫答案，也能記下問題；今天，那些問題開始變得重要。在班級筆記本的角落，米娜看見一幅舊畫旁寫著「bridge」，於是停下來詢問意思。「pull。」索爾讀出練習頁上的字，接著試著把它和眼前真正看得到的東西連起來。筆記本旁放著一瓶水，為漫長的一天做準備。

The first reaction in the school hall was a mixture of surprise and hope. Mina felt both, and did not pretend otherwise. Sol pointed out that a helpful choice for one person might still make trouble for someone else. Together they tried to find a step small enough to test, but useful enough to show whether their idea worked. “Let us keep ‘born’ in our word notes,” Mina said. “We can check its meaning again if we need it.” In the corner of the class notebook, Mina found the word “happen” beside an old drawing and stopped to ask what it meant. “table,” Sol read from the practice page, then tried to connect the word to something they could actually see.

The printed rules said the program would end after seven days, but did not explain Sol's wishes. The trouble was real, even if the danger had not yet become clear to everyone in the school hall. Mina took a step back to let someone with more experience speak. Help did not always mean being the first person to act. That gave Sol time to notice a detail that had been lost in the noise. The word “bird” appeared in an example someone had written earlier, and Sol asked if the example fit their situation. “Let us keep ‘far’ in our word notes,” Mina said. “We can check its meaning again if we need it.” In the corner of the class notebook, Mina found the word “station” beside an old drawing and stopped to ask what it meant.

印出的規章只說七天後計畫結束，卻沒提到索爾的想法。麻煩是真實的，即使學校走廊裡並非每個人都看清了風險。米娜退一步，讓更有經驗的人說話。幫忙不一定代表第一個採取行動。這讓索爾有時間注意到先前被吵鬧掩蓋的細節。「bird」出現在前人留下的例句裡，索爾問這個例子是否適合現在的情況。「把『far』留在詞彙筆記裡吧，」米娜說，「有需要時再確認意思。」在班級筆記本的角落，米娜看見一幅舊畫旁寫著「station」，於是停下來詢問意思。

Ms Lee joined the conversation after hearing both sides. The questions were simple, but the answers were not. Who would be affected? What could be checked today? What should wait until someone with the right skill was present? Mina copied the answers into the class notebook. A plan became easier to follow when it was written for people besides its maker. The number on Sol’s wrist was small, yet it seemed louder than the school bell. Mina wished that numbers could explain feelings as well as time. Mina circled “beach” on a reading sheet, not to win a test but to remember the meaning of the whole sentence. 李老師聽過雙方意見後加入討論。問題很簡單，答案卻未必簡單。誰會受影響？今天能確認什麼？哪些事要等具備專業能力的人在場？米娜把答案抄進班級筆記本。當計畫是寫給所有人看，而不只是制定者自己時，也就更容易執行。索爾手腕上的數字很小，卻彷彿比校鐘還響。米娜希望數字也能像表示時間那樣表達感受。米娜在閱讀單上圈出「beach」，不是為了考高分，而是為了理解整句話。「every」出現在前人留下的例句裡，索爾問這個例子是否適合現在的情況。「把『side』留在詞彙筆記裡吧，」米娜說，「有需要時再確認意思。」

Sol quietly asked whether anyone had ever asked a machine what it wanted. The new detail made the room seem a little different. Things that had looked unrelated began to form a line Mina could follow. Sol warned that one clear clue did not mean they knew everything. Mina agreed and wrote the date beside the note. There was work still to do, but now they understood why that work mattered. A little note used “cow” as its key word; Mina kept the note because the rest of the line was still useful. Mina circled “shoe” on a reading sheet, not to win a test but to remember the meaning of the whole sentence.

索爾輕聲問，是否有人曾問過機器想要什麼。新細節讓房間看起來有些不同。原本互不相關的事物，開始連成米娜能循著走的一條線。索爾提醒，一條清楚線索不代表知道全部。米娜同意了，在筆記旁寫下日期。仍有很多事要做，但大家現在知道為什麼這些事重要。一張小筆記以「cow」為關鍵字；米娜留下它，因為剩下的句子仍然有用。米娜在閱讀單上圈出「shoe」，不是為了考高分，而是為了理解整句話。

When Mina returned to the school hall, the place was almost the same. The difference was in what Mina now understood. Sol had helped without taking away the choice, and that made the help easier to accept. For now, they could rest. Tomorrow would ask its own question. “clean,” Sol read from the practice page, then tried to connect the word to something they could actually see. 當米娜回到學校走廊，地方看起來幾乎沒變。真正不同的是米娜的理解。索爾提供幫助，卻沒有奪走選擇權，這份幫助也就更容易被接受。現在他們可以休息。明天自有明天的問題。「clean。」索爾讀出練習頁上的字，接著試著把它和眼前真正看得到的東西連起來。一張小筆記以「road」為關鍵字；米娜留下它，因為剩下的句子仍然有用。
