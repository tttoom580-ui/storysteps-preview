失蹤的手環

The Missing Bracelets

1997，青潭泳池的第六條水道 · 第 12 / 25 章 · 3000–4000 · 英中對照 · 校訂中

用互動版查詞、朗讀與收藏

The following Monday, twenty identification bracelets disappeared from the pool's front desk. A clerk suggested that a burglar had entered through the window. Xu Mengzhe's security agent made a comment about careless public workers and warned that a thief could occupy the building whenever it was empty. Ruoqing found no definite sign of forced entry. The plastic bin that usually held the bracelets had been moved, and one shelf was wet. She asked the staff not to pursue accusations until the stock list had been checked. Her mother remembered that a school group had borrowed equipment for a ceremony on Sunday. The note might explain part of the missing supply.

接下來的星期一，泳池櫃檯有二十條識別手環不見了。職員猜測有竊賊從窗戶進來。徐孟哲的保全代理人批評公共員工粗心，還警告空館時小偷隨時可以占據建築。若青沒有找到明確破窗痕跡。平常放手環的塑膠箱被移動過，一層架子也濕了。她請員工核對庫存清單前不要追著誰指控。母親想起星期日有學校團體借器材辦典禮；那張借用紙可能解釋其中部分去向。

In the storeroom Ruoqing found ten bracelets in a box of durable rescue floats. Another six lay beside a torn banner about swimming fame from a much earlier era. The remaining four had been thrown away because their clasps were broken. No theft had occurred. Bohan confessed that he had carried the supplies into storage but forgotten to sign the form. He had feared the loss would end his training. Ruoqing asked him to commit to a corrected procedure, including a witness for transfers. It was embarrassing, but not tragic. The greater hardship belonged to workers who were accused publicly before anyone examined the evidence.

若青在儲藏室的耐用救生浮具箱裡找到十條手環，另外六條在一幅早年宣傳泳壇名氣的破旗幟旁，最後四條則因扣環壞掉而被丟棄。根本沒有偷竊。柏翰承認自己把用品移到倉庫，卻忘了簽表。他害怕這次失誤會讓他失去實習資格。若青請他承諾遵守修正後的流程，移交物品必須有人見證。這很丟臉，卻不是悲劇。真正承受困難的是那些還沒看過證據就被公開指控的工作者。

That afternoon an exhibition about local tribal history opened in the hall. Its organizers had asked permission to display photographs of river use across different generations. The display showed that recreation and access had changed over time, often because people had lost land or working hours. A visitor asked whether inflation had made swimming lessons too expensive. Ruoqing showed the current fee list and promised to compare it with household income data, without pretending one number explained everything. She also told the organizers how the missing bracelets had been found. The lesson was not about heroic detective work. It was about resisting the urge to turn uncertainty into blame.

那天下午，活動中心開了一場地方原住民族歷史展。主辦者先徵得同意，展出不同世代使用河流的照片。展覽呈現休閒與通行權如何隨年代改變，往往牽涉土地和工作時間的流失。一位訪客問，通貨膨脹是否讓游泳課變得太貴。若青拿出現行收費表，承諾與家庭收入資料比較，但不會假裝一個數字就能解釋全部。她也告訴主辦者手環失而復得的經過。這一課不是英雄偵探故事，而是學會抵抗把不確定當成罪名的衝動。

Before closing, Jingyi collected the day's forms and locked the cabinet. She had been a faithful employee for twenty years, yet the security agent had described her team as untrustworthy within minutes. Ruoqing wrote him a polite letter requesting a correction. If he refused, she would still preserve the record rather than organize an angry scene. The workers needed respect, and the pool needed a way to admit errors without destroying trust. Bohan returned his key for the night and thanked her for letting him repair his mistake. Ruoqing said that repair was not a favor she gave him. It was what a responsible workplace was supposed to allow.

關門前，靜宜收好當天表格，鎖上櫃子。她忠心工作二十年，保全代理人卻在幾分鐘內說她的團隊不值得信任。若青寫了一封禮貌信件，要求對方更正說法。如果被拒絕，她仍會保留紀錄，而不是發起憤怒場面。員工需要尊重，泳池也需要容許人承認錯誤而不毀掉信任的辦法。柏翰交回當晚鑰匙，感謝若青給他彌補錯誤的機會。若青說，彌補不是她施予的恩惠，而是一個負責任的職場應該具備的制度。

When Ruoqing returned home, Mother was repairing the cover of an old ledger. Its pages held records of lost towels, broken doors, and borrowed equipment going back twenty years. Ruoqing had once thought these pages were evidence of failure. Now she saw a different pattern. Problems had emerged over and over, but many had been corrected by employees who stayed late without recognition. The destruction of one old shelf had been recorded in a single line; beneath it, her mother had noted the name of the worker who paid for a replacement with her own wages. Ruoqing asked whether the worker had ever been repaid, and how often other employees had paid for supplies from their own pockets. Mother could not remember. That answer bothered both of them. They decided to review the oldest unpaid expenses as part of the new employment plan. No one could restore every lost hour, but they could stop acting as though personal sacrifices were free. The missing bracelets had been found; the missing respect for staff would take longer to repair.

若青回家時，母親正在修補一本舊帳簿的封面。書頁裡記著過去二十年遺失的毛巾、損壞的門和借出的設備。若青以前以為這些頁面證明經營失敗，如今卻看見另一種模式：問題反覆浮現，但往往由沒有受到表揚的員工加班修正。一座舊架被破壞的事件只寫了一行，下面卻有母親記下某位員工用自己的薪水買替用品的名字。若青問，那人後來有拿回錢嗎？還有多少員工曾自掏腰包買用品？母親記不得了。這個答案讓兩人都難受。她們決定在新聘僱計畫裡檢查最舊的未報銷支出。沒有人能把所有失去的時間還回去，但至少可以不再把私人犧牲當成免費資源。手環找到了；員工失去的尊重還需要更久才能修好。
