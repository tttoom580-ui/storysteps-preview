紅色排水蓋

The Red Drain Cover

1997，青潭泳池的第六條水道 · 第 4 / 25 章 · 3000–4000 · 英中對照 · 校訂中

On Tuesday, mechanic Luo Jinlong arrived to inspect the drain pumps. Ruoqing accompanied him through a narrow service passage, carrying a diagram copied from the old manual. Jinlong loosened a rusted screw and found a damaged seal. A second mechanic wanted to try an experimental glue, but Zonghe warned that the material had not been tested under pressure. 'The essential thing is to understand why the seal failed,' he said. Ruoqing drafted a maintenance note with simple drawings. The small repair would not be a magnificent public attraction, but it could prevent a child from being trapped by suction. She asked Jinlong to demonstrate the emergency switch before anyone entered the pool.

星期二，機械技師羅金龍來檢查排水幫浦。若青帶著從舊手冊抄下的圖解，陪他穿過狹窄的維修通道。金龍鬆開生鏽的螺絲，發現密封圈損壞。另一位技師想試用實驗性膠材，宗和卻提醒，該材料尚未通過壓力測試。「最重要的是弄清楚密封圈為什麼失效。」他說。若青畫了簡單的圖，起草維修紀錄。這種小修理不會成為宏偉的觀光景點，卻可能避免孩子被吸力困住。她要求金龍在有人下水前示範緊急開關。

During the demonstration a group of visitors arrived for a recreation workshop. Among them was a retired diplomat who had worked overseas and now helped neighborhood associations exchange safety ideas. He offered formal greetings, then asked whether the new sign could be understood by a foreign visitor. Xiaotang showed a red symbol, a large arrow, and the words STOP—NO ENTRY. A child asked if the germ painted on a health poster was the danger under the drain. Jingyi explained the difference between dirty water and mechanical force. They were separate risks, and confusing them might cause hesitation in an emergency. The diplomat offered to translate the basic warning into two additional languages.

示範期間，一群參加休閒工作坊的訪客抵達，其中有位退休外交官，曾在海外工作，如今協助地方團體交換安全做法。他正式致意後，問新標示是否也讓外國訪客看得懂。小棠展示紅色符號、大箭頭和「停止——禁止進入」的字樣。一名孩子指著衛生海報的病菌圖案，問排水口下面是不是也有病菌。靜宜解釋，髒水和機械吸力是不同風險；混淆它們，可能在緊急時造成遲疑。外交官願意協助把基本警告翻成另外兩種語言。

Outside the fence, the horizon cleared after several days of rain. Yizhen said the empty water looked magnificent and wanted to begin racing practice immediately. Ruoqing refused. The new gasket might strengthen the drain, but it could not prove the whole system was sound. An elderly neighbor recalled fleeing the changing room during a flood years earlier. Ruoqing recorded the memory, then checked it against the service manual rather than treating it as a complete technical account. A weather satellite image shown on the evening television suggested another storm. The pool would remain closed until the second pressure test and an evacuation drill were finished.

圍欄外，連日陰雨後的地平線終於放晴。宜臻說空蕩蕩的水面壯麗得很，希望馬上開始競速練習。若青拒絕了。新的墊圈可能加強排水口，卻無法證明整個系統都安全。一位老鄰居記得多年前水災時，自己從更衣室逃出去的情景。若青記下回憶，再對照維修手冊，而不把它當作完整的技術說明。晚間電視上的氣象衛星影像顯示可能又有風暴。泳池必須等第二次壓力測試和疏散演練完成，才會開放。

Xu Mengzhe called that night with a generous offer. His club would pay for a new pump if he could reserve the sixth lane every weekday afternoon. Ruoqing thanked him, then requested a written plan explaining what would happen to public swimmers during those hours. He said he would think about it. Mother listened from the kitchen. 'When I was young, I believed every outstanding athlete deserved the best training space,' she said. 'I still do. I just no longer believe that everyone else deserves none.' Ruoqing put the phone down. The damaged seal had been replaced, but the question of who belonged in the pool was still open. No mechanic could answer that for them.

那晚，徐孟哲來電提出慷慨的條件：如果每個平日下午都能保留第六水道給他的俱樂部，他願意出錢買新幫浦。若青道謝後，要求他書面說明那些時段的公共泳者該怎麼辦。他說會考慮。母親在廚房裡聽著。「我年輕時相信每位傑出運動員都該有最好的訓練空間，」她說，「現在還是相信。但我不再認為其他人因此什麼都不配得到。」若青放下電話。壞掉的密封圈已經換好，但誰有權使用泳池，仍沒有答案。這不是技師能替他們決定的事。

After the visitors left, Ruoqing asked Jinlong to accompany her to the storage room to check spare parts. He showed her the difference between a cracked rubber ring and a new one, letting her feel the change with her fingers. She had spent years knowing the exact timing of a racing turn, yet she had never understood the small parts beneath the water. Jinlong did not treat her ignorance as a failure. He asked whether she would teach him how swimmers recognized an unsafe current near the wall. They spent half an hour trading knowledge. Ruoqing described the sensation of water pulling against a hand, and Jinlong explained why a valve might create it. Neither became an expert in the other's profession. Instead, they learned which questions to ask and when to call for help. Later, Ruoqing wrote both names beside the repair notes. The record would show who had explained the problem, who had tested it, and what remained uncertain, rather than assigning every success to the manager.

訪客離開後，若青請金龍陪她去儲藏室檢查備用零件。他讓她用手指觸摸裂開的橡膠圈與新零件的差別。若青多年來知道競賽轉身的精確時機，卻從未了解水面底下的小零件。金龍沒有把她不懂當成失敗，反而問她能不能教自己，泳者如何察覺牆邊不安全的水流。他們花了半小時交換知識。若青描述水拉扯手掌的感覺，金龍解釋哪種閥門可能造成它。兩人都沒有變成對方領域的專家，卻學會該問什麼、何時求助。稍後，若青把兩人的名字都寫在維修筆記旁。紀錄會呈現誰解釋、誰測試、還有什麼不確定，而不是把所有成果都算在經理身上。
