學校的水族箱

The School Aquarium

1997，青潭泳池的第六條水道 · 第 7 / 25 章 · 3000–4000 · 英中對照 · 校訂中

用互動版查詞、朗讀與收藏

A school science teacher, Wu Huifen, offered to bring her aquarium class to the recreation hall, which remained open while the pool was repaired. The children had been studying basic chemistry, and one student asked whether pool water and aquarium water should be treated in the same way. Huifen explained that each environment had a different balance. Ruoqing admired her fluent teaching. Instead of giving an academic lecture, Huifen poured water into three clear basins and asked the children to observe the color of simple test strips. The activity had a prime purpose: learning to ask relevant questions rather than trusting the loudest adult in the room.

學校自然科教師吳慧芬提議把水族箱班帶到仍開放的活動中心，泳池則繼續維修。孩子們正在學習基礎化學，一名學生問游泳池和水族箱是否應該用相同方法處理水。慧芬解釋，每種環境都有不同平衡。若青欣賞她流暢的教學。慧芬沒有發表艱深的學術演講，而是把水倒入三個透明水盆，請孩子們觀察簡易試紙的顏色。活動最重要的目的，是讓大家學會問有關的問題，而不是相信房間裡聲音最大的大人。

The town accountant arrived carrying a folder of repair costs and a warning about expansion. New equipment was expensive, and the council had not decided who would make the contribution. Xu argued that private clubs would pay only if they received the best lanes. Jingyi said that gave wealthy athletes an unfair advantage before a race even began. The accountant asked for a more careful calculation. 'I have admiration for champions,' she said, 'but invoices are not medals.' Ruoqing proposed separate budgets for essential safety work and optional expansion. The first had to be funded before the second. Even Xu admitted that a functioning drain mattered more than a beautiful new scoreboard.

鎮上的會計帶著修繕費用資料和擴建警告前來。新設備很貴，議會還沒決定誰出資。徐孟哲主張私人俱樂部只有取得最佳水道才願意付錢。靜宜認為，這會讓有錢的運動員在比賽開始前就得到不公平優勢。會計要求更仔細地計算。「我很欣賞冠軍，」她說，「但發票不是獎牌。」若青提議把必要安全工程與可選擴建分成兩筆預算。前者必須先得到經費，後者才能討論。就連徐孟哲也承認，正常運作的排水口比漂亮的新計分板重要。

During the lesson, Yizhen showed a younger swimmer how to keep a steady rhythm while breathing. She wanted to motivate the children, not defeat anyone. A boy became annoyed because he could not copy her movements quickly. Ruoqing told him it was fortunate that practice allowed mistakes. 'You are not competing today,' she said. 'You are learning what your body can do.' Xiaotang moved a lamp into the shade so the picture cards would remain readable without glare. Huifen thanked the group for striving to include people who learned differently. No one had expected a science lesson to improve swim instruction, but it had revealed how much the teachers needed to listen.

上課時，宜臻示範如何在呼吸時保持穩定節奏。她想激勵孩子，不是打敗任何人。一名男孩因為無法立刻模仿動作而惱火。若青告訴他，練習最幸運的地方就是容許犯錯。「你今天不是來比賽，」她說，「你是在學自己的身體能做到什麼。」小棠把燈移到陰影處，讓圖像字卡不會因反光而難以閱讀。慧芬感謝大家努力照顧不同學習方式的人。沒人料到科學課能改善游泳教學，但它讓老師看見自己還需要多聽學生說話。

Late that night, Ruoqing woke when a phone rang beside her bed. The laboratory had finished a second water test. The main pool was safe to fill after the pipe repair, but the small teaching basin still showed irregular readings. They could reopen one area, not both. Ruoqing knew that a partial opening would disappoint families with young children. She asked Jingyi whether the training staff could handle a smaller schedule without being overworked. Jingyi wanted written shifts and a clear closing time. Ruoqing agreed. Being open every day meant nothing if the workers who kept the water safe could never rest.

深夜，床邊電話響起，若青被吵醒。實驗室完成第二次水質測試：主泳池修好管線後可以注水，但教學小水槽的讀數仍不規則。因此只能先開放一區，不能兩區都開。若青知道，部分開放會讓有小孩的家庭失望。她問靜宜，工作人員能否在不過勞的情況下執行縮小後的課表。靜宜要求書面班表和清楚的閉館時間。若青同意。如果守護水質的人永遠無法休息，天天開門就毫無意義。

On Saturday, Huifen invited parents to watch the children explain their experiments. One characteristic of the class was that no child had to speak faster than others. Students could use drawings, written notes, or a short demonstration. A boy who had struggled with reading showed how a test strip changed color, and his mother looked surprised by how confidently he explained it. Ruoqing asked the adults to stop comparing children and instead ask what the results actually showed. They had all come to learn, not to choose a winner. After the exhibit, the boy approached Yizhen and asked whether swimming lessons would make him brave. She answered that courage was not a permanent quality someone could acquire in one afternoon. It came from practice, support, and knowing when to seek help. The reply seemed to waken an idea in him: perhaps he could learn to float without first being fearless. Ruoqing heard him ask his mother to register when the shallow basin reopened. She hoped they would still be patient if it took longer than expected.

星期六，慧芬邀請家長觀看孩子解說實驗。這堂課的一個特色是，沒有孩子被要求比別人講得快。學生可以用畫圖、文字或簡短示範表達。一名閱讀較吃力的男孩展示試紙如何變色，母親驚訝他竟解釋得如此有自信。若青請大人停止比較孩子，改問結果究竟顯示什麼。大家是來學習，不是選冠軍。展覽後，男孩問宜臻，學游泳會不會讓自己變勇敢。宜臻回答，勇氣不是一個下午就能獲得的永久特質；它來自練習、支持和知道何時求助。這個回答彷彿喚醒男孩一個想法：也許不必先變得無所畏懼，也能開始學漂浮。若青聽見他請母親等淺水槽開放後報名。她希望如果進度比預期慢，他們仍能保持耐心。
