紫色水痕

The Purple Stripe

1997，青潭泳池的第六條水道 · 第 6 / 25 章 · 3000–4000 · 英中對照 · 校訂中

用互動版查詞、朗讀與收藏

On a cold morning in October, the pool's test basin showed a purple stripe along its edge. Xu Mengzhe said it was probably dye from a nearby laundry workshop. Jingyi refused to accept that explanation without evidence. She isolated a sample and asked a water-quality consultant to examine it. Ruoqing inspected the adjoining storehouse, where old linen towels and cleaning goods were piled against a pipe. A timid assistant admitted that a delivery driver had poured a colored liquid into a drain. He trembled while speaking, afraid of losing his occupation. Ruoqing promised to record what he knew without publishing his name. A worker's fear was not proof of guilt.

十月的一個寒冷早晨，泳池測試水槽的邊緣出現一道紫色水痕。徐孟哲說那大概是附近洗衣工場流出的染料。靜宜拒絕在沒有證據的情況下接受這個說法。她隔離一份水樣，請水質顧問檢查。若青查看相鄰儲藏室，發現舊亞麻毛巾和清潔用品堆在水管旁。一名害羞的助手承認，有位送貨司機曾把有顏色的液體倒進排水口。他說話時發抖，害怕失去工作。若青答應記下他知道的事，不公開姓名。工人的恐懼並不能證明他的罪行。

The consultant set a microscope on a table and explained what it could and could not reveal. The purple trace might be harmless coloring, but another chemical could still be present. He warned against confusing a conservative estimate with a guarantee. Xu wanted to reopen at once, arguing that consumers expected a certain level of luxury from a paid pool. Ruoqing replied that swimmers were not merely consumers buying a pleasant afternoon. They also trusted staff to tell them when something was unknown. A clear orientation meeting would be held before any reopening. The consultant would supply the results in writing, and no one would be allowed to alter his findings.

顧問把顯微鏡放在桌上，說明它能看見和不能判斷的事。紫色痕跡可能只是無害色素，但仍可能混入其他化學物質。他提醒，不要把保守估計誤當保證。徐孟哲主張立刻重新開放，說付費泳池的消費者期待一定程度的豪華享受。若青回答，泳者不只是購買愉快午後的消費者，也信任工作人員在未知時坦白告知。重新開放前會舉行清楚的說明會。顧問必須以書面提供結果，任何人都不准改動檢驗資料。

At noon a radio stereo played a song from the 1970s while volunteers washed the floors. Bohan, who had a habitual way of humming through every difficult task, almost stepped across a wet warning line. Jingyi caught his sleeve. He apologized, then became angry when another helper laughed. 'You think I am ignorant because I am new,' he snapped. Ruoqing asked them both to stop. One person's grace under pressure did not excuse another person's careless behavior. They enclosed the testing area with rope and placed a large sign on each side. Bohan agreed to teach the next volunteer where the unsafe floor began.

中午，志工清洗地板時，一台立體聲收音機播放著七○年代歌曲。柏翰有個習慣，做困難的工作時總會哼歌；他差點跨過濕地警戒線。靜宜抓住他的袖子。他道歉後，卻因另一名助手笑他而生氣。「你以為我是新人就什麼都不懂。」他怒道。若青請兩人停止爭吵。在壓力下保持風度，不代表可以忽略別人的粗心行為。大家用繩子圍住測試區，兩側都放上大字標示。柏翰答應教下一位志工從哪裡開始是危險地面。

That evening the laboratory confirmed that the purple color came from a leaking pipe belonging to a private laundry supplier. It had not reached the main swimming water, but the damaged wall required repair. Xu said the city should invest in a modern filtration unit and raise admission prices. Mother disagreed with the idea that only wealthy families deserved a clean pool. Ruoqing asked for two estimates: one for immediate safe repairs and one for future improvements. She refused to turn a technical problem into a quarrel about personal character. As the meeting ended, she noticed an old invoice showing that the same pipe had been repaired three times in six years. That was no longer an isolated accident.

當晚，實驗室證實紫色來自私人洗衣供應商的漏水管，尚未進入主要泳池水體，但受損牆面必須修理。徐孟哲認為市府應投資現代過濾設備，並提高門票。母親不認同只有富裕家庭才配用乾淨泳池的想法。若青要求兩份估價：一份是立即安全修繕，一份是未來改善。她拒絕把技術問題變成人格爭吵。會議結束時，她注意到一張舊發票，顯示同一根管子六年內修了三次。這已不是單一偶發事件。

The following morning, the laundry workshop's owner visited Ruoqing. He had heard the town's rage and feared that everyone would treat his business as poisonous. She showed him the measured results without exaggerating them. The owner explained that he had replaced a drain after a previous storm, but the new pipe had never been tested together with the pool's system. Ruoqing invited him to attend the next inspection, while making clear that the town would pay for its own testing and seek repayment only for damage that could be demonstrated. He agreed to share the workshop's repair records. When he left, the timid assistant returned and asked whether he could correct one detail in his earlier statement. Ruoqing listened, then asked him to read the correction before signing. It mattered that the story could change when new evidence arrived. A careful investigation was not a contest between a good shop and a bad shop. It was a way to keep water safe while treating the people involved fairly. Jingyi closed the file and checked that the purple sample remained sealed.

隔天早晨，洗衣工場老闆來找若青。他聽見鎮上的憤怒，害怕大家把他的生意當成有毒的東西。若青向他展示測量結果，沒有誇大。老闆解釋，上次風暴後他換過排水管，但新管從未和泳池系統一起測試。若青邀他參加下次檢查，並說明鎮上先支付自己的檢測費，只有能證明的損害才會要求償還。老闆同意分享維修紀錄。離開後，那名害羞助手又回來，問能否修正先前證詞的一項細節。若青聽完，請他在簽名前讀過更正內容。新證據出現時，故事可以改變，這很重要。謹慎調查不是好店與壞店的競賽，而是讓水保持安全、同時公平對待相關人的方法。靜宜收起檔案，再次確認紫色水樣仍密封保存。
