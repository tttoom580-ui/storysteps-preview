Names on the Bulletin Board

1997，青潭泳池的第六條水道 · 第 8 / 25 章 · 3000–4000 · 英中對照 · 校訂中

用互動版查詞、朗讀與收藏

The opening-week bulletin caused trouble before the first swimmers entered the water. A printed schedule listed men's competitive practice as the dominant use of the deep end and placed women's lessons at inconvenient hours. Ruoqing had not approved it. The clerk said he had copied the arrangement from an old club software file, stored on a floppy disk. Xu Mengzhe called the schedule a practical estimate of demand. Ruoqing asked who had been counted and who had been ignored. Gender was not a reason to assume that one athlete's time mattered more than another's. She removed the notice and replaced it with the actual hours approved at the public meeting.

開放週的公告在第一位泳者下水前就引發麻煩。印好的課表把男子競賽訓練列為深水區的主要用途，女子課程則排在不方便的時段。若青根本沒有批准這份安排。職員說他從一片磁片中的舊俱樂部軟體檔複製了時間表。徐孟哲稱這是對需求的務實估算。若青問，到底統計了誰，又忽略了誰。性別不能成為某位運動員的時間比別人重要的理由。她撤掉公告，換上公開會議真正核准的時段。

A local composer had reserved the hall for a concert celebrating a bride and bridegroom from two fishing families. Their wedding party needed the entrance for three hours, and the pool office had forgotten to record the booking. When the groom arrived, his enthusiasm changed to confusion. He thought the pool staff were trying to dominate the entire building. Ruoqing apologized and found an alternative entrance, though it was less convenient. The celebration had no connection to the timetable dispute, but the mistake demonstrated how easily one careless notice could invade another person's plans. She asked the clerk to create a systematic booking book with dates and signatures.

一位地方作曲家預訂活動中心，要為兩個漁家的一對新人舉辦音樂會。婚禮隊伍需要使用入口三小時，泳池辦公室卻忘記登記。新郎抵達時，原本的熱情變成困惑。他以為泳池員工想獨占整棟建築。若青道歉並安排另一個入口，雖然不太方便。婚禮與水道排程爭議沒有關係，但這次錯誤顯示，一張草率公告多容易侵入別人的計畫。她請職員建立有日期與簽名的系統化預約簿。

During the cleanup, Xiaotang discovered a folder of abstract paintings left by an art club. One depicted a broad gulf between two figures, with a thin bridge in the middle. Yizhen joked that it looked like the distance between rich and poor swimmers. Ruoqing said a picture could be interpreted in many ways, and that a pool schedule was not a painting. It had to state clearly what people were allowed to do. An older visitor brought a learned essay about fertile public spaces, arguing that a recreation center could support many kinds of community life. Ruoqing liked the idea but asked how its author would measure success. Pleasant language alone did not constitute a plan.

整理場地時，小棠找到美術社留下的一疊抽象畫。其中一張畫著兩個人之間寬闊的鴻溝，中間只有一座窄橋。宜臻開玩笑說，那像富裕泳者和普通泳者的距離。若青說畫作可以有許多解讀，但泳池課表不是畫，必須明確說出誰可以做什麼。一位年長訪客帶來一篇學識豐富的文章，談論有活力的公共空間能支持多種社區生活。若青喜歡這個想法，但想知道作者打算如何衡量成果。好聽的文字本身不能構成計畫。

At the evening committee meeting, Xu accused Ruoqing of jealousy because his club had more professional athletes. She replied that this was not a contest of personal feelings. The council had asked her to provide a schedule that ordinary civilians could understand and use. A small crowd applauded, but Ruoqing did not mistake applause for agreement on every detail. She invited Xu to propose his preferred training hours in writing and promised that his athletes would receive the same careful consideration as other users. He seemed surprised that she had not tried to defeat him in public. The meeting ended with a temporary arrangement and a date for a complete review.

晚上的委員會上，徐孟哲指控若青嫉妒他的俱樂部擁有較多職業運動員。她回答，這不是私人感情的較量。市府要求她提出一般市民看得懂、用得上的時間表。一小群人鼓掌，但若青沒有把掌聲當成大家同意每個細節。她邀請徐孟哲書面提出偏好的訓練時段，並承諾他的選手和其他使用者一樣會獲得仔細考量。他似乎意外她沒有當眾擊敗他。會議以臨時安排和完整複審日期結束。

At home Ruoqing studied the two versions of the booking book. The old computer file had been prepared years earlier for an elite program, and the clerk had treated it as a ready-made answer. She could see how easy the mistake was to make. People often acquire a habit of trusting documents that look professional. The wrong schedule had caused genuine harm, however, and someone had to correct the consequences. She invited the clerk and Xiaotang to design a clearer form that required a date, the name of the person making a booking, and a second check before publication. The next morning they rehearsed the process with several imaginary events. Every spoken cue had a matching visual signal, and the instructor could adjust its intensity so that visitors nearby were warned without being frightened by unnecessary noise. Xu Mengzhe arrived during the rehearsal. He watched in silence, then offered a suggestion about avoiding conflicts between pool practices and community ceremonies. Ruoqing accepted the suggestion after it passed the same test as every other one.

回家後，若青研究預約簿的兩個版本。舊電腦檔數年前是為菁英課程製作的，職員卻把它當成現成答案。她看得出錯誤多容易發生：人常養成信任外表專業文件的習慣。然而錯課表確實造成傷害，必須有人修正後果。她邀職員與小棠設計更清楚的表格，要求填日期、預約者姓名，並在公布前由第二人核對。隔天早上，大家用數個假想活動演練流程。每個口頭提示都配上視覺訊號，教練也能調整聲音強度，讓附近訪客收到警告而不被不必要的噪音嚇到。徐孟哲在演練時抵達，沉默觀看後，提出避免泳池訓練和社區典禮撞期的建議。若青在這項建議通過與其他提案相同的測試後，接受了它。
