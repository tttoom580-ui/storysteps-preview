淺水區的一堂課

A Lesson in Shallow Water

1997，青潭泳池的第六條水道 · 第 3 / 25 章 · 3000–4000 · 英中對照 · 校訂中

用互動版查詞、朗讀與收藏

The first trial lesson took place on dry ground while repairs continued. Jingyi assigned each volunteer a different task: one would watch the gate, one would explain the emergency signal, and one would record the maximum number of people allowed near the shallow end. Ruoqing asked seventeen-year-old swimmer Xu Yizhen to demonstrate a slow walking pace rather than a racing start. Yizhen looked insulted. 'I came for serious training,' she said. Ruoqing answered that an intermediate athlete must first learn to defend a weaker swimmer without putting herself in danger. In that context, discipline was not punishment. It was the skill of knowing when to stop.

維修期間，第一次試辦課改在岸上進行。靜宜分配志工工作：一人看守入口，一人解釋緊急訊號，一人記錄淺水區周邊允許的最多人數。若青請十七歲的泳者許宜臻示範緩慢步行，而不是競賽起跳。宜臻覺得受到羞辱。「我是來認真訓練的。」她說。若青回答，中階運動員首先要學會在不危及自己的情況下保護較弱的泳者。在這個情境裡，紀律不是懲罰，而是知道何時該停下來的能力。

The pool's new lifeguard trainee, Chen Bohan, wanted a luxurious staff room and complained that volunteers were doing too much housework. Jingyi showed him the cleaning schedule. A safe facility could not proceed without somebody removing wet towels and checking slippery floors. Bohan apologized and took the next shift. Xiaotang added picture cards for visitors who could not hear the whistle. 'It looks simple,' Bohan said, 'but the difference is incredible.' Ruoqing agreed. Photography from the trial would help them show inspectors how the new signs worked, although no picture could replace a live rescue exercise.

新進救生員實習生陳柏翰想要一間豪華員工室，抱怨志工得做太多清潔家務。靜宜拿出值日表給他看。沒有人清理濕毛巾、檢查濕滑地板，設施就不能安全運作。柏翰道歉後接下下一班。小棠則增加圖像字卡，讓聽不清哨聲的訪客也能理解訊號。「看起來很簡單，」柏翰說，「效果卻令人難以置信。」若青同意。試辦課的攝影紀錄可以讓稽查員看到新標示如何運作，但照片不能取代真正的救援演練。

At lunch Yizhen counted the calorie values printed on snack packets and passed out raisins from a tin. Her coach wanted her to concentrate only on winning, but she kept looking at the older swimmers waiting outside the fence. One woman had arrived by ferry from the far shore to attend the open session. The journey had cost a large percentage of her weekly allowance. 'If classes are always cancelled, that money is gone,' she said. Ruoqing wrote down the fare and the missed work hours. Inspiration was useful, but reliable opening times were a higher priority than speeches about courage. Yizhen offered to help count arrivals at the next session.

午餐時，宜臻查看點心包裝上的卡路里標示，又從鐵盒拿葡萄乾分給大家。教練希望她只專注於贏比賽，但她一直看向圍欄外等候的年長泳者。其中一位婦人搭渡輪從對岸來參加開放課程，車船費占她每週零用金很大比例。「如果每次都取消課，那些錢就白花了。」婦人說。若青記下票價和請假損失的工時。鼓舞人心固然有用，但可靠的開放時間比勇氣演說更優先。宜臻自告奮勇下次協助統計到場人數。

Before leaving, Ruoqing asked each participant to say one thing the lesson had changed. Bohan said he now understood why cleaning belonged in rescue training. Xiaotang asked for a second visual alarm above the changing rooms. Yizhen said she had been afraid of losing speed, but she had noticed how speed could make other people feel invisible. Jingyi collected the notes without praising anyone too quickly. She knew that a thoughtful class could still fail when an actual crowd arrived. The next test would include a timed evacuation and a clearer account of who had the authority to close a lane. Ruoqing took the papers home, aware that she had taught less swimming than expected but perhaps more safety.

離開前，若青請每位參與者說出課程帶來的一項改變。柏翰說他終於懂得為什麼清潔也是救援訓練的一環。小棠希望更衣室上方多裝一個視覺警報。宜臻說自己一直害怕失去速度，卻發現速度有時會讓其他人彷彿不存在。靜宜收起筆記，沒有急著稱讚任何人。她知道，一堂用心的課也可能在真正的人潮來時失敗。下次測試將包含計時疏散，以及誰有權關閉水道的明確說明。若青把紙張帶回家，知道自己教的游泳動作比預期少，卻可能教了更多安全觀念。

On the following morning, Yizhen helped prepare a new exercise card. She wanted to describe each movement so clearly that a stranger could follow it without having to guess what the teacher meant. Xiaotang checked the drawings and suggested larger arrows. Bohan read the text aloud, stumbling over several instructions that seemed simple on paper. Jingyi asked them to make the signal for leaving the water more noticeable than the signal for starting an exercise. After several revisions, the group tried the cards with two volunteers who had never attended a swim lesson. The volunteers understood the first steps but could not locate the nearest exit. Nobody blamed them. Ruoqing moved the exit symbol, then tested the card again. She realized that teaching was not a speech given by a clever person to a silent audience. It was a conversation in which an unclear instruction became visible through someone else's confusion. By afternoon, Yizhen was asking better questions of the beginners than she had asked of her own coach the previous week.

隔天早晨，宜臻協助準備新的運動指示卡。她希望把每個動作寫得夠清楚，讓陌生人不必猜老師的意思也能跟上。小棠檢查圖畫，建議把箭頭畫大。柏翰朗讀文字，卻在幾項紙上看似簡單的指示卡住。靜宜要求把離水訊號做得比開始運動的訊號更醒目。數次修訂後，大家找來兩名從未上過游泳課的志工測試。志工懂了前幾個步驟，卻找不到最近的出口。沒有人責怪他們。若青移動出口符號，再測試一次。她明白，教學不是聰明人對沉默聽眾的演說，而是透過別人的困惑讓不清楚的指示現形的對話。到了下午，宜臻向初學者提出的問題，已比前一週問自己教練的問題更好。
