The Bandage Cabinet

1997，青潭泳池的第六條水道 · 第 10 / 25 章 · 3000–4000 · 英中對照 · 校訂中

The rescue-training day began with a surgeon from the county hospital demonstrating how to clean a minor wound and apply a bandage. Bohan wanted to bargain for a shorter session, but Jingyi forbade him from leaving early. 'An apology after an accident is not enough,' she said. 'You need a skill before someone gets hurt.' A pregnant visitor asked whether she could use the shallow exercise area when it reopened. The surgeon explained that each person's medical circumstances were different and that she should follow her own clinician's advice. Ruoqing wrote a clear rule: staff should not pretend to diagnose visitors, but they should know when to call qualified help.

救援訓練日由縣立醫院的外科醫師示範如何清洗小傷口、包紮繃帶。柏翰想討價還價，縮短上課時間，靜宜卻禁止他提早離開。「事故之後道歉並不夠，」她說，「有人受傷之前，你就要有技能。」一位懷孕的訪客問，淺水運動區重新開放後能否使用。醫師解釋每個人的醫療情況不同，應依自己的主治醫療人員建議行事。若青寫下清楚規則：員工不可假裝替訪客診斷，但必須知道何時尋求合格協助。

Outside, a road crew accidentally caused a small explosion while repairing an old pipe. No one at the pool was injured, but the sound sent frightened people toward the gates. Jingyi stopped the crowd and illustrated the correct exit route on a large board. Her actions were critical because the main walkway was blocked by the crew's equipment. The incident was a mere warning rather than a major disaster, yet it revealed how quickly panic could spread. Ruoqing decided to install additional exit signs and organize a second drill. She congratulated Xiaotang for using clear gestures, but Xiaotang reminded her that the staff needed a practiced procedure, not one person's quick thinking.

外面，道路工程隊修理舊管時意外引發一聲小爆響。泳池沒有人受傷，但聲音嚇得大家朝大門湧去。靜宜攔住人群，在大板上畫出正確出口路線。這很重要，因為主要步道被工程器材擋住。事件只是一次警告，還不是重大災難，卻顯示恐慌傳得多快。若青決定加裝出口標示並安排第二次演練。她稱讚小棠手勢清楚，小棠卻提醒，員工需要的是熟練程序，而不只靠某個人反應快。

The town's small history society held a talk nearby about an ancient tomb and early settlers in the valley. A student asked how civilizations made decisions when resources were scarce. Ruoqing, who was delivering the pool's safety lecture in the next room, heard the question and thought about the sixth lane. The town was not facing a revolution, but even a small schedule change could feel revolutionary to people who had never been invited to speak. Xu Mengzhe made a suspicious remark about the new accessibility plan costing too much. Ruoqing asked him to bring figures rather than suspicion. They would need a resolution supported by actual numbers before reopening.

鎮上的小型史學社在隔壁談論古墓和早期移民。一名學生問，不同文明在資源不足時如何做決定。若青正在隔壁講泳池安全課，聽見問題便想到第六水道。小鎮沒有發生革命，但對從未被邀請發言的人來說，即使一個小小的課表變動也可能像革命。徐孟哲語帶懷疑地說，新的無障礙計畫會花太多錢。若青請他帶來數字，而不是只有猜疑。重新開放前，他們需要一項有實際數據支持的決議。

A college volunteer offered to create a simple website so the pool's timetable could be read from a computer at the local library. In 1997, few households in Qingtan had internet access, and Ruoqing insisted that printed notices and telephone messages remain available. She refused to present technology as a magic solution. After the training, Jingyi opened the bandage cabinet and found that someone had used nearly all the supplies without recording it. Nothing proved theft, but the stock count was wrong. Ruoqing asked Bohan to check deliveries with her rather than accusing a colleague. The next morning they discovered that a school event had borrowed the supplies under an unsigned note. Another procedure needed repair.

一名大學生志工提議製作簡易網站，讓居民可以在圖書館的電腦查看泳池課表。一九九七年的青潭很少家庭能上網，因此若青堅持保留紙本公告和電話語音訊息。她拒絕把科技當成萬能解答。訓練後，靜宜打開繃帶櫃，發現大部分用品不見了，也沒有使用紀錄。沒有人能證明遭竊，但庫存數字確實不對。若青請柏翰一起核對交貨資料，不先指控同事。隔天早上，他們查出是學校活動借走物品，卻只留下沒有簽名的紙條。又有一項程序需要修正。

On the following day, Jingyi asked Bohan to illustrate the first-aid procedure without looking at his notes. He remembered the beginning but forgot when to call the hospital. Instead of scolding him, she returned the group to the first step. She asked participants to explain what they would do if a visitor lost consciousness while the pool remained closed. They practiced with a training dummy, checking the route for emergency workers before touching any equipment. Bohan eventually performed the sequence correctly. Jingyi congratulated him, then made clear that this was only a drill, not a license to work unsupervised. Ruoqing noticed that the stock list for bandages had also improved. A simple sign-out book now recorded each item, its purpose, and the person who borrowed it. No one needed to forbid school events from borrowing supplies; they needed to know what was available when an emergency came. Even a prosperous facility could fail if a cabinet that looked full was secretly empty.

隔天，靜宜請柏翰不看筆記示範急救程序。他記得開頭，卻忘了何時該叫救護車。靜宜沒有責罵，而是帶大家回到第一步，問如果泳池仍封閉時有訪客失去意識，應如何處置。大家用假人演練，先確認救護人員的路線，再接觸設備。柏翰最後正確完成順序。靜宜恭喜他，也說明這只是演練，不代表可以在無監督下工作。若青發現繃帶庫存也改善了。簡單的借用簿記下每件物品、用途及借用者。沒有必要禁止學校活動借用品，真正需要的是在緊急時知道庫存還剩什麼。即使設備充裕、經營繁榮的場館，如果櫃子看似裝滿、實際卻空了，也可能出事。
