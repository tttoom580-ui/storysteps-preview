The Wall Beneath the Water

1997，青潭泳池的第六條水道 · 第 2 / 25 章 · 3000–4000 · 英中對照 · 校訂中

用互動版查詞、朗讀與收藏

Ruoqing invited county engineer Lin Zonghe to inspect the pool before any contract meeting. He spread an old landscape map on a table and marked the extent of the original building. Under the modern tiles stood part of a stone fort constructed in an earlier era; a nearby museum had a fossil display describing the layers beneath the hill. The connection was interesting, but Ruoqing asked whether it affected the drains. Zonghe smiled at the practical question. He pointed to a delicate joint where two kinds of pipe met. Their combination had survived many winters, yet the newest repair might have made the joint less flexible.

若青邀請縣府工程師林宗和在任何合約會議前檢查泳池。他在桌上攤開舊地形圖，標出原建築的範圍。現代磁磚底下曾有一部分古老石堡的遺跡；附近博物館的化石展也介紹了山坡底下的地層。這種關聯很有趣，但若青問的是它是否影響排水。宗和對這個務實的問題笑了笑，指出兩種管材相接的一個脆弱接縫。這個組合撐過許多個冬天，但最新一次修理可能讓接縫失去彈性。

The maintenance room held a poster from an atomic science exhibition. It explained that an atom was far too small to see with the naked eye, while a leak could be measured by ordinary instruments. 'We cannot gaze into concrete and guarantee it is sound,' Zonghe said. They listened to the low murmur of water running behind the wall. Ruoqing asked for a pressure test rather than a dramatic guess. Jingyi worried about the psychological effect of another delayed opening. 'People have heard guarantees for years,' she said. 'Every promise made without a test becomes another reason for them not to trust us.' Ruoqing agreed to revise the public announcement that evening.

維修室裡留著一張原子科學展的海報，說明原子小到肉眼看不見，但漏水卻能用普通儀器測量。「我們不能只是盯著混凝土看，就保證它安全。」宗和說。他們聽著牆後水流低沉的聲音。若青要求進行壓力測試，而不是憑想像猜測。靜宜擔心再次延後開放造成心理影響。「大家已聽過太多保證，」她說，「每個沒經過測試的承諾，都讓人更難相信我們。」若青答應當晚修訂對外公告。

A messenger from the old rice mill delivered a letter about the site's history. The mill's owner described a voyage that his grandparents had taken to reach Qingtan, along with families who later worked in immigration offices and railway workshops. He wanted the pool to retain a small public history wall. Xu Mengzhe dismissed the request as unproductive. Ruoqing thought differently. A public pool was not merely a place for elite athletes; families had intimate memories of learning to float here, and those memories belonged to the community. She promised no museum that the budget could not support, but she put the old photographs on the agenda.

舊米廠派來一名送信人，帶來一封關於土地歷史的信。米廠主人寫到祖父母當年來到青潭的旅程，以及後來在移民事務處與鐵路工場工作的家庭。他希望泳池保留一面公共歷史牆。徐孟哲認為這項要求沒有生產效益。若青卻不這麼想。公共泳池不只是菁英運動員訓練的地方；許多家庭在這裡第一次學會漂浮，留下親密的記憶。她沒有承諾建造經費負擔不起的博物館，但把老照片列入議程。

That afternoon Zonghe removed one service panel and found moisture behind the new plaster. He did not call it a disaster. Instead, he photographed the problem, sealed the opening, and explained that an additional test would decide whether the pool could reopen in stages. Ruoqing watched the water line without speaking. She knew the quickest solution would be to let the private club pay for the repair in exchange for the sixth lane. It was tempting, especially when her mother mentioned the electricity bill waiting at home. Yet a productive answer needed more than money; it needed a clear statement of who would be able to swim afterward. She asked Zonghe to retain the original test sheets, even if the result disappointed everyone.

那天下午，宗和拆下維修板，發現新粉刷層後方有水氣。他沒有把它說成災難，而是拍下問題、封住開口，並解釋下一次測試才能決定泳池是否可以分階段開放。若青沉默地看著水線。她知道最快的辦法，是讓私人俱樂部負擔修理費，換取第六水道。尤其母親提到家裡還有電費帳單時，這方法更有誘惑力。但真正有用的答案不能只有金錢，還必須說清楚修好後誰可以游泳。她請宗和保留原始測試表，無論結果多麼令人失望。

The next day Ruoqing took the old photographs to the library, where a volunteer helped her compare them with building records. A narrow path appeared in several images, but it disappeared from later plans after an extension was built. Nobody knew whether the original passage was still open. Ruoqing considered asking an employee to enter and check, then remembered the cracked pipe. She called Zonghe instead. He arrived with a flashlight, a measuring tape, and a permit for a safe inspection from above. They discovered that the passage had been sealed deliberately during a previous renovation. The seal was not proof that anything improper had happened; earlier workers might have had sound reasons. But the reason should have been recorded. Ruoqing asked the library volunteer to preserve copies of each plan, not to decide which era had been better. The woman said her own grandchildren had learned to swim at the pool and that she wanted them to have a place with a future, not merely a beautiful past. Ruoqing carried that distinction home with her.

隔天，若青把舊照片帶到圖書館，志工協助她與建築紀錄比對。幾張照片出現一條窄路，但後來擴建後的圖面不再有它。沒人知道原通道是否還能通行。若青一度想派員工進去查看，隨即想起裂開的管線，改為打電話給宗和。他帶著手電筒、捲尺及從上方安全檢查的許可到場。大家發現通道在前次改建時被刻意封住。這不代表有人做錯，當年的工人可能有充分理由，只是理由應該留下紀錄。若青請圖書館志工保存每版圖面，而不是裁定哪個年代比較好。志工說，她的孫子也在這座泳池學游泳；她希望他們擁有一個有未來的地方，而不只是漂亮的過去。若青把這項區別放在心裡帶回家。
