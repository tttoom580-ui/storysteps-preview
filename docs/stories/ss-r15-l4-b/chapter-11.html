長距離試游

The Long Swim

1997，青潭泳池的第六條水道 · 第 11 / 25 章 · 3000–4000 · 英中對照 · 校訂中

用互動版查詞、朗讀與收藏

The main pool reopened for a carefully limited trial in November. Its first event was not a championship but a supervised distance swim. Yizhen wanted to complete a ten-kilometer marathon over several sessions, proving she was ready for the regional team. Her father called the plan an eventual ticket to fame; Ruoqing called it a test of endurance and safe pacing. A coach from the private club arrived with a solar timing device and a printed code of race conduct. He insisted his athletes should have the dominant lanes. Jingyi answered that the pool would not expand its opening hours until the lifeguard schedule was secure. A swimmer's ambition could not erase another person's right to safety.

十一月，主泳池在嚴格限制下開始試辦。第一項活動不是錦標賽，而是有救生員監督的長距離游泳。宜臻想分次完成十公里馬拉松式長泳，證明自己準備好進入區域代表隊。父親說那終將成為她成名的門票；若青則說，這是耐力與安全配速的測試。私人俱樂部的教練帶著太陽能計時裝置和印好的競賽守則到場，堅持他的選手應占主要水道。靜宜回答，在救生員班表穩定前，泳池不會延長開放時間。泳者的企圖心不能抹去其他人享有安全的權利。

A dusty box of records turned up beneath the office counter. It contained a telegraph message about a storm decades earlier and photographs of families gathering at the old riverside bathing place. One picture showed a large dog; a handwritten note identified its breed. Bohan joked that the dog looked more graceful than the swimmers. The joke made Yizhen laugh, but she soon noticed that the old pictures had been used in an advertisement for the new private resort. No one had asked the families' permission. Ruoqing took copies of the photographs and their dates, not to start a scandal but to understand how they had begun to circulate.

辦公室櫃檯下翻出一箱蒙塵紀錄，裡面有幾十年前颱風期間的電報，以及居民在老河邊浴場聚集的照片。一張照片裡有隻大狗，手寫註記標出牠的品種。柏翰開玩笑說那隻狗的姿態比泳者還優雅。宜臻被逗笑，但很快注意到那些舊照片被用在新私人度假村的廣告上，沒人詢問照片中家庭的同意。若青複印照片和日期，不是為了製造醜聞，而是想查明它們怎麼開始流傳。

Xu Mengzhe came to negotiate about the photographs and the lane schedule. He argued that capitalism rewarded those who could invest, and that the private club had kept local swimming alive during the shutdown. Ruoqing acknowledged that contribution. She also showed an indication that former pool staff had provided the photographs for a temporary exhibition, not for permanent advertising. Xu called her a rebel against progress. She said she was asking for permission to be respected, not declaring an invasion of his business. Their discussion was difficult, but a possible agreement emerged: the images would be removed until the families were contacted, and training hours would remain shared.

徐孟哲來談照片和水道課表。他主張資本主義獎勵願意投資的人，並說私人俱樂部在泳池停館期間維持了地方游泳訓練。若青承認這份貢獻，同時提出線索：前員工提供照片是給臨時展覽，不是永久商業廣告。徐孟哲說她是反抗進步的叛逆者。她表示自己只是要求尊重同意，並沒有要侵犯他的生意。討論很不容易，但仍出現可能的協議：聯絡家屬前先撤下照片，訓練時段則繼續共享。

When Yizhen entered the water, Ruoqing counted her strokes from the deck. She watched for fatigue rather than speed. Halfway through the session, Yizhen stopped and said her shoulder felt strange. She feared that stopping would be seen as weakness. Ruoqing told her about the injury that had ended her own career. 'Ignoring pain had a consequence,' she said. 'It did not make me brave.' Yizhen climbed out. The race clock continued ticking, but nobody asked her to return. Jingyi brought a towel, and Bohan tried to tickle away the tension with a bad joke. Outside, a passing truck carried the wreck of an old delivery bicycle. Ruoqing thought how easily people learned to replace equipment, and how slowly they learned to protect bodies.

宜臻下水時，若青站在池邊數她的划水次數，觀察的是疲勞而非速度。游到一半，宜臻停下來，說肩膀感覺不對勁。她擔心停下會被當成軟弱。若青說起終結自己職涯的肩傷。「忽略疼痛有後果，」她說，「那沒有讓我更勇敢。」宜臻爬上岸。計時鐘仍在走，卻沒有人叫她回去。靜宜拿來毛巾，柏翰用冷笑話試圖逗大家放鬆。外頭一輛貨車載著損毀的舊腳踏車經過。若青心想，人們學會換設備很快，學會保護身體卻慢得多。

After the distance swim, Yizhen asked Ruoqing whether stopping early had damaged her chances of joining the regional team. Ruoqing refused to guess. She offered to help Yizhen prepare an honest training record, including the point where discomfort had begun. A coach could then evaluate her progress with proper information. Yizhen's father worried that the private club would use the incident as persuasion to move her into an expensive program. Ruoqing said that the family should compare the costs, coaching methods, and safety arrangements before making a decision. Xu Mengzhe happened to overhear and offered a written explanation of his club's policies. She thanked him, then asked for the same information about its financial limits. The conversation stayed calm. Yizhen was still angry at her shoulder, but she no longer treated a pause as personal failure. The next morning she joined the beginners' class as a helper, counting strokes and talking about how a body gave warnings. She discovered that she could encourage another swimmer without pretending she had always been fearless herself.

長距離試游後，宜臻問若青，提早停止是否傷害了她入選區域隊的機會。若青拒絕猜測，提議幫她準備誠實的訓練紀錄，包括不舒服從哪一刻開始。教練才能依適當資訊評估進度。宜臻的父親擔心私人俱樂部會藉這件事遊說她轉進昂貴課程。若青說，家人應先比較費用、教學方式與安全安排，再決定。徐孟哲剛好聽見，願意書面說明俱樂部政策。若青道謝後，也要求說明財務方面的限制。對話保持平和。宜臻仍對肩膀生氣，卻不再把暫停視為個人失敗。隔天早上，她到初學者課當助手，數划水次數、說明身體如何發出警告。她發現，不必假裝自己向來無所畏懼，也能鼓勵別人。
