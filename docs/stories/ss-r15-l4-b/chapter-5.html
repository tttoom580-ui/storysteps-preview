可以移動的斜坡

A Portable Ramp

1997，青潭泳池的第六條水道 · 第 5 / 25 章 · 3000–4000 · 英中對照 · 校訂中

Ruoqing invited the neighborhood association to test a portable ramp for swimmers who could not manage the steep steps. She adjusted its position twice while Xiaotang measured the angle with a simple device. When the ramp seemed favorable, an older visitor still objected: the handrail stopped too soon. Her objection mattered. The ramp could not be declared safe merely because the demonstration looked imaginative. They marked three distinct problems and asked the manufacturer to enlarge the final drawing. Xu Mengzhe accused Ruoqing of wasting an allowance that should have gone to training equipment. She answered that avoiding injuries was part of training, not an optional extra.

若青邀請鄰里協會測試一座可移動斜坡，讓無法走陡階的泳者使用。她調整了兩次位置，小棠用簡單裝置測量角度。當坡度看似合宜，一名年長訪客仍提出反對：扶手太早就結束了。這個異議很重要。不能因為示範看起來有創意，就宣布斜坡安全。大家標出三項不同的問題，請製造商放大最後的設計圖。徐孟哲指責若青浪費本來該拿去買訓練器材的補助。她回答，避免受傷本來就是訓練的一部分，不是可有可無的附加項。

The community hall next door hosted a children's orchestra rehearsal, and its music floated across the empty pool. A visiting canoe instructor stopped by after class, carrying a sample paddle. He described how the shape of a boat and the impact of a wave could change a person's balance. His story offered a useful comparison for the ramp. Yizhen, who worshiped a famous swimmer as her idol, wanted to test the slope at racing speed. Jingyi gently refused. 'There is an idiom about learning to walk before running,' she said. 'Today we are learning how to move safely.' Yizhen sighed, but she agreed to record each test rather than compete.

隔壁活動中心正在進行兒童管弦樂團排練，音樂飄過空泳池。一位剛下課的獨木舟教練帶著示範船槳走來，說明船形和浪的衝擊會如何改變平衡。這個例子有助於理解斜坡。宜臻崇拜一位知名泳者，把他當成偶像，想用競速的速度測試坡道。靜宜溫和地拒絕。「有句成語說，先學走再學跑，」她說，「今天我們要學的是安全移動。」宜臻嘆氣，但同意記錄每次測試，不再把它當比賽。

A protest formed at the front gate when a rumor spread that the public pool would abandon ordinary members. Ruoqing came out carrying the revised plan. A few people tried to seize the microphone, and one woman shouted that the neighborhood would lose its prosperity if the pool closed. Ruoqing understood the anxiety but did not pretend money could solve every problem. She separated immediate repairs from the long-term membership question. Her proposal allowed the private club to rent certain hours only after the public timetable was protected. A parent asked whether the children would still learn to swim. Ruoqing answered with dates, costs, and names, rather than another hopeful promise.

有人傳出公共泳池要放棄一般會員，門口便聚集了一場抗議。若青帶著修訂計畫走出來。幾個人想搶走麥克風，一位婦人高喊，如果泳池關閉，社區的繁榮就會消失。若青理解大家的焦慮，但沒有假裝錢能解決所有問題。她將眼前修繕與長期會員問題分開處理：只有先保障公共時段，私人俱樂部才能租用部分時段。一名家長問孩子是否還能學游泳。若青用日期、費用與負責人回答，而不是再給空泛的希望。

Mother had spent the day making tea for guests without being paid. Ruoqing found her washing cups after sunset and asked her to stop. Mother laughed at first, as if family labor were simply part of the job. Ruoqing offered to hire a paid helper for busy evenings or reduce the events they held. 'I came home to work,' she said. 'Not to turn your retirement into my hidden budget.' Mother looked at the portable ramp beside the fence. It had taken several careful adjustments to make one person's path easier. 'Maybe families need adjustments too,' she said. Ruoqing wrote that thought in the corner of her notebook and went home before dark for the first time that week.

母親一整天都在無薪替客人泡茶。日落後，若青發現她還在洗杯子，便請她停下。母親起初笑了，彷彿家人幫忙本來就是工作的一部分。若青提議繁忙夜晚聘請有薪助手，或減少活動。「我回來是要工作，」她說，「不是把妳的退休生活當成看不見的預算。」母親望向圍欄旁那座移動斜坡。為了讓一個人的路更容易走，大家做了好幾次細心調整。「也許家庭也需要調整。」她說。若青把這句話記在筆記本角落，這一週第一次在天黑前回家。

On Saturday the neighborhood children returned with a school reading sheet. One task asked them to count the syllable in each word on a safety poster. They discovered that a long word was not always more difficult than a short one; the hard part was knowing what to do when the bell rang. Ruoqing let them draw a new warning board, but she asked them to test it from the far end of the hall. The children were excited when the red symbol could be seen from every doorway. A parent pointed out that the board was still too high for a small child. They lowered it. Ruoqing invited two older visitors to repeat the test, and both found the new position easier to see. At the end of the afternoon, the children left carrying their drawings rather than prizes. The ramp still needed another safety check, but the class had shown them that an improvement could come from anyone who noticed a problem and helped test a better answer.

星期六，社區孩子帶著學校的閱讀練習單回來。其中一道題目要他們數安全海報上單字的音節。孩子發現，長單字不一定比短單字難，真正困難的是警鈴響起時知道該做什麼。若青讓他們畫新的警示板，但要求從大廳最遠端測試。紅色符號在每個門口都看得到時，孩子很興奮。一位家長指出，看板對小孩來說還是太高，大家便把它降低。若青再請兩位年長訪客測試，她們也覺得新位置更容易看清。下午結束，孩子帶著畫作離開，沒有拿獎品。斜坡還需要另一次安全檢查，但這堂課讓大家看見：只要有人注意到問題並協助測試更好的答案，改進就可能發生。
