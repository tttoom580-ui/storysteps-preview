第六條水道

The Sixth Lane

1997，青潭泳池的第六條水道 · 第 1 / 25 章 · 3000–4000 · 英中對照 · 校訂中

用互動版查詞、朗讀與收藏

On a fierce evening in September 1997, Shen Ruoqing returned to Qingtan Pool carrying one suitcase and a folder of coaching notes. Her mother lived in a small cottage behind the swimming complex, with a blue cushion on the kitchen chair. The city had asked Ruoqing to give a presentation about reopening the pool, but no one had promised her a job. She had once been a promising athlete. A shoulder injury ended her competition years before she could become a champion. Now thirty-six, she possessed experience but little money. Her mother was pessimistic: the pool had lost members, and a private club wanted its valuable sixth lane.

一九九七年九月的一個風雨猛烈的傍晚，沈若青帶著一只行李箱和游泳教學筆記回到青潭泳池。母親住在泳池後方的小屋，廚房椅子上擺著藍色坐墊。市府請若青提出重新開放泳池的簡報，卻沒有人承諾聘用她。她曾是備受期待的運動員，但肩傷在她成為冠軍之前就終結了競賽生涯。如今三十六歲的她有經驗，卻沒有多少錢。母親很悲觀：泳池流失了會員，私人俱樂部還想要占用珍貴的第六條水道。

At the entrance, veteran lifeguard He Jingyi had assembled a diverse group: an older swimmer, two parents, an anxious teenager, and Du Xiaotang, a young instructor who could not hear every announcement clearly. 'Diversity is not the problem,' Jingyi said. 'A single set of rules for everyone is.' A businessman named Xu Mengzhe arrived with a glossy visual chart. He promised a tremendous increase in income if the sixth lane became an exclusive training area. He tried to flatter Ruoqing by calling her a genius. She did not smile. The chart contained no reference to the people who used the lane for slow rehabilitation.

入口處，資深救生員何靜宜集合了一群不同需求的人：一位長者、兩名家長、一名焦慮的少年，還有無法清楚聽見每次廣播的年輕教練杜小棠。「多樣性不是問題，」靜宜說，「把同一套規則套在每個人身上才是。」商人徐孟哲帶著精美的視覺圖表前來，承諾只要把第六道變成專屬訓練區，收入就能大幅增加。他奉承若青是天才。她沒有笑。那張圖表沒有引用任何使用該水道進行緩慢復健者的資料。

During a light workout, a child dropped a paper parachute from the viewing stand as part of a school science project. It floated into the shallow end, and two children gave a nervous giggle. Jingyi removed it before anyone slipped. The simple incident revealed a larger issue: the viewing stand was open while the pool was supposedly closed for inspection. Ruoqing wrote 'possible safety violation' in her notebook. Xu said this was an unfair defense against his proposal. Ruoqing replied that protecting his reputation was not the same thing as protecting swimmers. A witness could confirm the open gate, but only an inspection could establish how it had happened.

一次輕度運動訓練時，一名孩子為了學校的科學作業，從看臺丟下一個紙降落傘。它飄到淺水區，兩個孩子緊張地輕笑。靜宜在有人滑倒之前把它拿走。這件小事卻暴露更大的問題：泳池理應封閉檢查，看臺的門卻還開著。若青在筆記本寫下「可能違規」。徐孟哲認為她故意找藉口反對提案。若青回答，保護他的名聲和保護泳客是兩回事。目擊者可以確認門開著，但只有檢查能查明原因。

The next morning, Ruoqing walked past a spiritual gathering in the park, where neighbors were offering wishes for the pool's future. She respected their hopes but wanted evidence as well. She asked Xiaotang to convey a simple warning on large cards: no one should enter the water until the gates and drains were examined. That afternoon she made her first choice. She would not accept a contract based on projected revenue. She would prepare a public safety plan, including the people the private club had forgotten. Her mother watched from the office doorway. 'You know how to swim fast,' she said. 'Do you know how to persuade people who are tired of waiting?' Ruoqing admitted she did not yet know.

隔天早晨，若青經過公園裡的祈福聚會，鄰居們正為泳池的未來許願。她尊重他們的期盼，但同樣想要證據。她請小棠用大型字卡傳達簡單警告：在閘門與排水口完成檢查前，任何人都不能下水。當天下午，她做了第一個決定。她不會接受只依預估收入制定的合約，而要提出一份包含被私人俱樂部忽略者的公共安全計畫。母親站在辦公室門口看著她。「妳知道怎麼游得快，」她說，「可是妳知道怎麼說服已經等累的人嗎？」若青承認，她還不知道。

Before going to bed, Ruoqing returned to the office and studied the old timetable. Lane Six had been listed as a rehabilitation lane only on Wednesday mornings, hours when most workers could not attend. She imagined the older woman she had seen at the gate, holding a towel and waiting for permission to enter. Her own shoulder tightened at the memory of the injury that ended her racing career. It had taken her years to accept that a slower body was still a capable body. When she started to write a new schedule, she stopped herself. She had promised to listen before deciding. On a fresh page she wrote three questions: Who uses the lane now? Who wants to use it but cannot? What must change so that more people can use it safely? She placed the page beside the financial proposal and left the rest blank. In the kitchen, her mother had made tea and set out two cups. They talked for a while without trying to solve the future. It was the first quiet evening they had shared since Ruoqing came home, and she wanted to remember it.

睡前，若青回到辦公室研究舊課表。第六水道只在星期三早上被列為復健水道，而那正是大部分上班族不能來的時間。她想起門口那位拿著毛巾、等待獲准入場的老婦人。想到終結競賽生涯的傷，她的肩膀又緊繃起來。她花了多年才接受：速度慢下來的身體仍然有能力。若青剛要寫新課表便停下，她曾承諾先聽再決定。她在新紙上寫下三個問題：現在誰使用這條水道？誰想使用卻不能？要怎樣改變才能讓更多人安全使用？她把紙放在財務提案旁，其他部分留白。廚房裡母親泡好茶，擺出兩個杯子。母女聊了一會兒，沒有急著解決未來。這是若青回家後第一次和母親共度安靜夜晚，她想記住這段時光。
