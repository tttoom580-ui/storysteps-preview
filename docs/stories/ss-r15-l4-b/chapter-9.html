The Recorded Promise

1997，青潭泳池的第六條水道 · 第 9 / 25 章 · 3000–4000 · 英中對照 · 校訂中

用互動版查詞、朗讀與收藏

A woman arrived at the pool office holding a receipt and demanding a refund. She had paid for a ten-session course that had been cancelled during repairs. The clerk had told her to wait, but she had already missed work twice. Her economic circumstances were difficult, and Ruoqing could see the strain in her frown. The woman played a small cassette recording through an earphone. It contained a message promising that every cancelled class would be replaced free of charge. The accent of the speaker sounded like a former employee, but the recording did not identify him. Ruoqing asked for the original tape rather than acting on a secondhand copy.

一位婦人拿著收據來到泳池辦公室，要求退費。她已付十堂課的費用，課程卻因維修取消。職員叫她繼續等，但她已請假兩次。她的經濟處境艱難，若青從她皺起的眉頭看得出壓力。婦人用耳機播放一段小卡帶錄音，裡面承諾每堂取消的課都會免費補上。說話者的口音很像前員工，錄音卻沒有署名。若青要求查看原始磁帶，而不是只憑轉錄版本行事。

The old tape was kept in a cardboard box with an attachment showing the dates of a special promotion. It had been recorded before the shutdown, under circumstances quite different from the present. A clumsy edit had joined two sentences that were spoken several minutes apart. Ruoqing did not accuse the customer of trying to mislead her. Someone else might have copied the tape carelessly. Jingyi showed the woman the written terms and explained which sessions had actually been paid for. The refund policy still needed correction, because people should not lose money when a public service closed for safety. Ruoqing promised a clear answer by Friday.

原始磁帶放在紙箱裡，旁邊附了一張記載特別促銷日期的資料。它是在停館前錄的，當時的情況和現在很不一樣。一次笨拙的剪接把相隔數分鐘的兩句話接在一起。若青沒有指控顧客故意誤導她，也許是別人粗心複製了磁帶。靜宜給婦人看書面條款，說明哪些課程確實已付款。退費規定仍需要修正，因為公共服務為安全而停辦時，居民不該平白損失金錢。若青答應星期五前給出清楚答覆。

Later Yizhen leaned against the fence, watching a small boy peep through a gap at the empty sixth lane. 'I used to think this was my homeland in a way,' she said. 'Then the pool closed, and I felt that I belonged nowhere.' Ruoqing asked her not to instruct the boy to climb inside. Yizhen laughed and gave a brief wink, but she moved away from the gate. The conversation was interrupted by a delivery truck. Someone had ordered a new timing clock without approval. Ruoqing returned it unopened. She would not allow one dramatic purchase to hide the modest work that still needed doing: repairs, fair schedules, and honest communication.

稍後，宜臻靠著圍欄，看一個小男孩從縫隙偷看空蕩的第六水道。「我以前在某種意義上把這裡當成家鄉，」她說，「後來泳池關了，我就覺得自己沒有地方可去。」若青請她別教男孩爬進去。宜臻笑著眨了下眼，卻也離開了閘門。談話被送貨卡車打斷。有人未經批准訂了一座新的計時鐘。若青原封不動地退貨。她不會讓一件引人注目的採購掩蓋仍需要做的基本工作：修理、公平排程和誠實溝通。

On Friday the woman received two choices: a full refund for sessions not delivered or a replacement course on a date that suited her work. She selected the refund and thanked the staff for finally explaining the policy. Xu Mengzhe arrived just as she left and said the conflict proved that public pools were inefficient. Ruoqing replied that an inefficient system was a reason to improve it, not to hand the only affordable swimming space to a single club. She added the corrected rule to a notice written in plain language. Her mother's handwriting appeared below it, offering to help people read the forms. Ruoqing quietly replaced that offer with a paid office shift.

星期五，婦人得到兩個選擇：未上課程全額退款，或在符合她工作時間的日期補課。她選擇退費，並感謝員工終於把規則講清楚。徐孟哲在她離開時抵達，說這種爭議證明公共泳池效率低落。若青回答，制度沒效率就應該改進，不是把唯一負擔得起的泳池交給單一俱樂部。她把修正規則用白話寫進公告。公告下方出現母親的字跡，說願意幫大家讀表格。若青悄悄把那句話改成一個有薪的辦公室班次。

That evening Ruoqing sat with the cashier to review the wording of each refund notice. The old forms were full of legal language that made people afraid to ask questions. She wanted the new content to explain, in ordinary terms, what would happen if a lesson stopped because of repairs, a storm, or a teacher's illness. The cashier said that a revolutionary change was unnecessary; most visitors simply wanted someone to answer the telephone. Ruoqing agreed. They ate crunchy rice crackers while comparing the notices, and she laughed at how much time they had spent arguing about a missing line. An interruption came when a student from the science club called to ask whether the laboratory's nuclear physics poster could be used in a school project. Ruoqing gave permission for a copy but made clear that no claim about pool safety should be taken from the unrelated poster. When the cashier thanked her for working late, Ruoqing answered with modesty that the cashier had done most of the careful reading. She would pay for the extra hours.

那晚，若青和收銀員一起檢查每張退費公告的文字。舊表格充滿法律術語，讓人害怕發問。她希望新的內容能用白話解釋：如果課程因維修、風暴或教師生病而停辦，會發生什麼事。收銀員說，不必來一場革命性的改變；多數訪客只是希望有人接電話。若青同意。兩人一邊吃酥脆米餅，一邊比對公告，她笑自己竟為了一條漏掉的文字爭論這麼久。這時有通電話打斷工作：科學社學生問，能不能複製實驗室的核物理海報做作業。若青准許複印，但提醒不能從不相關的海報推論泳池是否安全。收銀員謝她加班，若青謙虛地說，多數細心閱讀的工作其實是收銀員完成的。她會支付這段額外工時。
