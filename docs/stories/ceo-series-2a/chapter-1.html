The Number at the Window

總裁先生，請先排隊 · 第 1 / 25 章 · 1000–2000 · 英中對照 · 校訂中

Mira Lin had designed bus stops for six years, but today she was only visitor number forty-two. A tall glass office stood above the metro station. People moved through its doors as if time belonged to the building. Mira carried a small drawing of the Lantern Market and a letter from its sellers. A company called Northstar wanted to replace the market with a private shopping hall. She had come to say no.

林米拉設計公車站已有六年，但今天她只是四十二號訪客。高大的玻璃辦公樓聳立在捷運站上方。人們穿過大門，彷彿時間都屬於這棟樓。米拉帶著燈市的小幅設計圖，以及攤商寫的信。北星公司打算把市場改成私人商場，她今天是來說不的。

Mira chose a direct approach: ask who had approved the sale. From the balcony, she could see the market's bright roofs. Her plan had a simple base: keep the public path open. Her belief in public space came from her mother's old stall. A better building meant little if the old sellers had nowhere to go.

米拉決定直接切入，詢問究竟是誰批准了出售案。從陽台上，她看得見市場明亮的屋頂。她的計畫有一個簡單基礎：保留公共通道。她對公共空間的信念，來自母親以前的攤位。如果老攤商無處可去，再好的建築也沒有意義。

At the reception desk, an assistant told Mira that the company president could not see walk-in visitors. Mira asked for the written rule and waited. After twenty minutes a young man in an ordinary gray coat picked up the letter she had dropped. He did not introduce himself. He only asked why her drawing left so much space between the shops. She said that a person using a wheelchair needed room, not a special favor.

在接待櫃檯，助理告訴米拉，總裁不接待臨時訪客。米拉請對方出示書面規定，然後安靜等待。二十分鐘後，一個穿普通灰外套的年輕男人撿起她掉落的信。他沒有自我介紹，只問她為何在店面之間留下那麼寬的空間。她說，使用輪椅的人需要的是通道，而非特別恩惠。

While she waited for her number, Mira studied the people crossing the lobby. A cleaner moved a heavy cart around a child in a wheelchair. A delivery worker held the door for a woman carrying flowers. None of these people appeared in the shining model on the wall. Mira made a quick sketch, adding an arrow to show how one narrow turn could stop a cart. When Evan came back from a phone call, she put the sketch beside the company's drawing. He said the company had studied the street for months. She asked whether anyone had followed a delivery cart through it. He was quiet. Then he called the project team and asked them to check that route before making another promise. Mira did not call this a victory. She asked for the date of the check and the name of the person responsible. The answer went into her notebook before she left.

等候號碼時，米拉觀察穿過大廳的人。清潔員推著重車，繞過一名坐輪椅的孩子；送貨員替抱花的女士扶著門。牆上光鮮亮麗的模型裡，沒有這些人。米拉迅速畫了草圖，加上一支箭頭，標示狹窄轉角如何讓推車動彈不得。伊凡講完電話回來時，她把草圖放在公司圖紙旁。他說公司已研究那條街好幾個月。她問，有沒有人真的推著貨車走過那裡。他沉默了，然後打電話請專案團隊在做出更多承諾前，先檢查那條路線。米拉沒有稱之為勝利，她要求檢查日期與負責人姓名，離開前把答覆寫進筆記本。

The man called her drawing a challenge to his company's plan. Mira did not deny that her own studio needed new clients. She wanted to develop the street without driving families away. They agreed to discuss the public path before discussing any price. Her field was practical design, not the sale of land.

那男人說，她的圖紙是對公司計畫的挑戰。米拉不否認自己的工作室需要新客戶。她想發展這條街，卻不願把家庭趕走。兩人同意先討論公共通道，再談任何價格。她的專業領域是實用設計，不是土地買賣。

When the assistant called the man Mr. Shen, Mira understood. Evan Shen, thirty-four, had become president after saving Northstar from a failed project. The newspapers called him cold, rich, and impossible to change. He offered Mira a chair, but she remained standing until he promised her letter would be entered into the record. That small promise was the first thing she accepted from him.

助理稱男人為沈先生時，米拉才明白他的身分。三十四歲的沈伊凡曾挽救北星一項失敗的專案，之後成了總裁。報紙說他冷漠、有錢，而且從不改變主意。他請米拉坐下，但她直到他答應把攤商的信列入正式紀錄，才坐下來。那是她接受他的第一個小承諾。

For instance, she showed where an older shopper would rest. A male guard quietly helped an elderly visitor find the lift. The market had food from every part of the nation. A bus operator had already warned about the narrow entrance. Evan called parking a secondary question, but Mira disagreed.

例如，她指出年長顧客可以在哪裡休息。一位男性保全悄悄帶年長訪客去找電梯。這座市場販售來自全國各地的食物。一名公車營運人員早就警告入口太窄。伊凡認為停車只是次要問題，米拉卻不認同。

Before leaving, Mira asked for the next public meeting date. Evan wrote it himself and gave her a plain card with no private number. He did not ask her to dinner or offer to buy her studio. He said, almost with surprise, that she might be right about the entrance. Mira folded the date into her drawing. Her mother had taught her that a respectful answer was worth more than a beautiful promise.

離開前，米拉詢問下次公開會議日期。伊凡親自寫下日期，給她一張只有公務聯絡資訊的素色名片。他沒有邀她吃飯，也沒有提出收購工作室。他近乎驚訝地說，她對入口的看法或許是對的。米拉把日期夾進圖紙。母親教過她，有禮的回答，比漂亮的承諾更有價值。

A broken seesaw beside the market showed years of ignored repairs. She measured the speed of bikes near the school gate. The metro brought workers to the market before sunrise. A weekly flower fair was the neighborhood's favorite tradition. It was windy outside, yet Mira finally felt able to breathe.

市場旁壞掉的翹翹板顯示，修繕需求已遭忽視多年。她測量學校門口附近自行車的速度。捷運在日出前把上班族送到市場。每週的花市是社區最喜愛的傳統。外面風很大，米拉卻終於覺得能呼吸。
