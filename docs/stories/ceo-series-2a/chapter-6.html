小小聲音的街道

A Street of Small Voices

總裁先生，請先排隊 · 第 6 / 25 章 · 1000–2000 · 英中對照 · 校訂中

用互動版查詞、朗讀與收藏

On Saturday, the market held a free meeting for anyone who lived or worked nearby. Mira asked the people to choose their own speaker. Evan sat at the back, without a camera crew or a company banner. At first no one wanted to talk. Then a woman who worked the night shift explained why the last bus mattered more to her than a bright new entrance. A second person said that early food deliveries kept the small restaurants alive.

週六，市場舉辦一場免費開放給居民與工作者的會議。米拉請大家自行選出發言人。伊凡坐在後排，沒有攝影團隊，也沒帶公司布條。一開始沒有人願意說話。後來一位值夜班的女士解釋，對她而言末班公車比漂亮的新入口更重要。另一個人說，清晨的食材配送讓小餐館得以生存。

Mira said the market affair was public business, not a family secret. The school offered its badminton court for the meeting when rain began. A farmer brought cabbage to show how early deliveries worked. A clever child suggested drawing the bus route with colored chalk. The last bus problem was a daily crisis for late workers.

米拉說市場這件事是公共事務，不是家族祕密。下雨時，學校借出羽球場供大家開會。農夫帶來高麗菜，說明清晨配送如何進行。一名聰明的孩子建議用彩色粉筆畫公車路線。對晚班工作者來說，末班公車問題是每天的危機。

Victor sent an assistant to offer free gift cards if the sellers signed a letter supporting the new hall. The sellers returned the cards. Evan learned about the offer only after the meeting and asked to see the message. He could not erase what his company had done. He could stop the offer, publish a correction, and let the sellers make their own choice. Mira told him a useful apology needed a change in procedure, not only a softer voice.

維克多派助理送免費禮券，只要攤商簽署支持新商場的信即可。攤商退回禮券。伊凡直到會後才知道這件事，要求查看訊息。他無法抹去公司的作為，卻能停止禮券活動、公開更正，讓攤商自行選擇。米拉告訴他，有用的道歉需要改變程序，而不只是語氣變溫柔。

The meeting notes were written in two languages because several sellers had come from other places. A young cook offered to check the words used for food deliveries. A retired teacher helped make the letters larger for older readers. Mira found that a simple sentence could be difficult when it carried a hidden rule. She changed one question from "Do you support the new building?" to "Which parts of this plan would help or hurt your work?" The answers became longer, but also more useful. Evan asked for a copy of the new form, and Mira gave it to him. When he suggested a small reward for anyone who completed it, she explained why the reward might pressure people. He withdrew the idea and left the answers alone. That evening the team received fewer forms than expected, but more of them included clear reasons and possible solutions.

會議筆記寫成兩種語言，因為幾位攤商來自不同地方。年輕廚師主動檢查食材配送用語，退休老師協助把字體加大，方便年長讀者。米拉發現，簡單句子若藏著規則，也可能變得難懂。她把「你支持新大樓嗎？」改成「計畫哪些部分會幫助或傷害你的工作？」回答變長了，卻也更有用。伊凡要了一份新表單，米拉給了他。他提議給填表者小禮物，她解釋這可能形成壓力。他撤回想法，不再干預答案。當晚收到的表單比預期少，卻有更多清楚的理由與解決建議。

The market's culture included both old recipes and new families. A delivery truck's engine was loud beside the school gate. The chance to design a safer lane began to excite the volunteers. A firewoman described how wide an emergency lane needed to be. People stepped back and forth to test the crossing width.

市場文化包含老食譜，也包含新住戶。送貨卡車的引擎在校門旁發出很大的聲音。設計更安全車道的機會開始讓志工們興奮。一位女性消防員說明緊急車道需要多寬。人們來回走動，測試路口的寬度。

After everyone left, Mira found Evan sitting under the sports hall roof with a wet paper map. He asked why she kept the children's drawing beside the professional plans. She answered that children knew where they were afraid to cross, and adults did not always ask. Evan folded the drawing with care. He could buy a new wall, she thought, but he was only beginning to learn how to hear a street.

大家離去後，米拉發現伊凡坐在體育館屋簷下，手裡拿著被雨打濕的紙地圖。他問，為什麼她把孩子的畫和專業圖紙放在一起。她說孩子知道自己害怕在哪裡過馬路，只是大人不一定會問。伊凡小心摺好那張畫。米拉心想，他買得起新牆，卻才剛開始學會聆聽街道。

The city must govern public roads, not leave every rule to one company. A safe street was a human need, not a luxury gift. Each individual seller could choose whether to sign a letter. Mira kept a journal of promises made at every meeting. An elderly neighbor offered chairs for people waiting to speak.

城市必須管理公共道路，不能把規則全交給公司。安全的街道是人的基本需求，不是奢華禮物。每一位攤商都能自行決定是否簽信。米拉在日誌裡記下每場會議做出的承諾。年長的鄰居借出椅子，給排隊發言的人坐。

Mira went home with thirty-seven comments and one question she could not answer: if the market stayed, who would pay for repairs next year? Her mother placed soup on the table and told her not to confuse helping with carrying everything alone. Mira wrote a new task for Monday: find a clear way to share costs. The task was less exciting than winning an argument, but it was the work that could keep doors open.

米拉帶著三十七份意見回家，仍有一個答不出的問題：如果市場留下，明年的維修費誰付？母親把湯放到桌上，提醒她別把幫忙和獨自扛下全部責任混為一談。米拉記下週一的新任務：找出透明的成本分攤方式。這件事不如辯贏一場爭論令人興奮，卻是讓大門能持續打開的工作。

Visitors could participate without agreeing with Mira or Evan. A nurse brought a pillow for a tired older man. One seller said the narrow lane felt like a prison at night. Fresh fish came from the nearby shore before dawn. A child's wet socks dried near the meeting room door.

訪客不必認同米拉或伊凡，也能參與。護理師拿了枕頭給疲憊的長者。一名攤商說，夜間狹窄的巷道讓人像被關在監獄裡。新鮮魚貨在黎明前從附近海岸送來。孩子的濕襪子在會議室門口晾乾。
