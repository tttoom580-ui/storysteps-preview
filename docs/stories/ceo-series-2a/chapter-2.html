A Different Angle

總裁先生，請先排隊 · 第 2 / 25 章 · 1000–2000 · 英中對照 · 校訂中

用互動版查詞、朗讀與收藏

The next morning Evan invited Mira to an open project review, not a private meal. He gave her a seat with the other guests and said that every speaker would have five minutes. Victor, Evan's cousin and the project manager, smiled as if he already owned the street. On the wall, a picture showed a lovely square. Mira noticed there were no market sellers in it. The missing people mattered more than the glass towers.

隔天早上，伊凡邀米拉參加公開專案審查，而不是私人餐敘。他安排她和其他訪客一起入座，宣布每人有五分鐘發言。專案經理、伊凡的堂兄維克多笑得彷彿整條街已是他的。牆上的圖像展示漂亮的廣場，米拉卻發現畫面沒有半個市場攤商。消失的人比玻璃大樓更值得注意。

Mira turned the plan to a new angle so all could see the steps. Evan said he could appreciate a clear objection even when it hurt. They should avoid promising a street that had not been measured. The barber from the corner shop stood up to speak first. Victor showed the board a picture without any sellers.

米拉換個角度轉動圖紙，讓大家看見階梯。伊凡說，即使聽了難受，他也能欣賞清楚的反對意見。他們應避免承諾一條根本未測量的街道。街角理髮師第一個站起來發言。維克多向董事會展示了一張沒有攤商的圖片。

Mira handed out a copy of the street map she had made with the sellers. Each shop had been marked only with its owner's permission. Victor called the map emotional and unprofessional. Evan stopped him: the company's rule required each claim to be supported. When the meeting ended, Mira found a small error in Victor's cost estimate. Two rows used the same number, although one street was twice as long as the other.

米拉把和攤商共同製作的街道圖分給大家，每間店都經過店主同意才標記。維克多說這張圖情緒化、不專業。伊凡阻止他，指出公司規則要求每一項主張都要有依據。會議結束時，米拉在維克多的成本估算中發現小錯誤。兩列明明是長度不同的街道，卻用了相同數字。

At her studio that night, Mira studied the two cost tables again. A number in the second table had been copied from an earlier version, before the city added a new public path. That explained how the error might have happened, but it did not explain why no one had checked it. She sent one email to the project office and another to the city clerk. Both contained the same question and a clear picture of the difference. June, her assistant, asked whether she was afraid of making an enemy of the richest company in town. Mira told her that the person who owned a mistake did not have to become her enemy. The problem was whether the mistake could be found and fixed. She saved the emails and waited for written replies instead of posting an angry message online. By morning, both offices had agreed to review the numbers.

那晚，米拉在工作室再次研究兩份成本表。第二張表的一個數字是從早期版本複製過來的，當時市府尚未加入新的公共通道。這解釋了錯誤可能怎麼發生，卻無法解釋為何沒有人檢查。她分別寄信給專案辦公室與市府書記，兩封信提出同樣的問題，並附上清楚的差異圖。助理君問她，是否害怕得罪城裡最有錢的公司。米拉告訴她，犯錯的人不必變成敵人，重點是錯誤能否被發現和修正。她保存郵件，等待書面答覆，沒有在網路上發怒。隔天早上，兩個單位都同意重新審查數字。

A painted deer marked the old children's crossing on her map. A white dove rested on the window while the guests argued. The latest edition of the plan removed the public drinking fountain. Mira asked whether a real repair estimate could exist before the vote. A huge screen hid the narrow path behind the new shops.

她的地圖上畫著一隻鹿，標示舊兒童穿越道。訪客爭論時，一隻白鴿停在窗邊。最新版計畫刪除了公共飲水台。米拉問，在投票前是否可能提出真正的修繕估價。巨大的螢幕遮住了新店面後方狹窄的通道。

After the meeting Evan caught up with Mira in the public hall. He did not ask her to keep the error secret. Instead, he wanted permission to share her note with an outside checker. She agreed, but asked for the checker’s name in writing. He looked at her carefully, then signed the request. For a man who was used to quick orders, such a small written step seemed new.

會後，伊凡在公共大廳追上米拉。他沒有要求她隱瞞錯誤，而是問她是否同意把筆記交給外部審查員。米拉同意，但要求書面提供審查員姓名。他仔細看著她，然後簽署了申請。對一個習慣迅速下令的人來說，這樣的小小書面程序似乎很新鮮。

An old lantern still hung outside the market's first tea shop. The report had ignored a minority of older sellers on short leases. The sellers formed an organization with an elected speaker. It was painful for Mira to hear her mother's shop called useless. One particular mistake appeared on every page of Victor's estimate.

市場第一家茶店外仍掛著一盞舊燈籠。報告忽略了少數租約較短的年長攤商。攤商成立了一個組織，並選出發言人。聽見母親的老店被說成毫無用處，米拉很難受。維克多的估算每一頁都出現同一個特定錯誤。

That evening the sellers held their own small meeting under the market roof. Mira explained the error without promising that it would stop the project. A public question, she told them, needed a public answer. A shop owner handed her a cup of tea that tasted too sharp. They laughed together. Mira was tired, but for the first time she knew the fight could be about facts rather than pride.

傍晚，攤商在市場屋頂下開了自己的小會。米拉解釋那個錯誤，卻沒有保證它能阻止開發案。她告訴大家，公開的問題需要公開回答。店主遞給她一杯味道過重的茶，大家一起笑了。米拉很累，卻第一次知道這場爭取可以談事實，而不是只爭一口氣。

The president promised to publish the corrected figures the following week. The tea was sour, so the owner added a little honey. Mira could spot the same missing cost in three separate tables. A city planning textbook explained why public paths needed clear widths. The truth was smaller than a scandal but strong enough to check.

總裁承諾下週公開修正後的數據。茶有點酸，店主便加了一點蜂蜜。米拉在三張不同表格裡都看見同一筆漏列成本。一本都市規劃教科書解釋了公共通道為何需要足夠寬度。真相不如醜聞驚人，卻有足夠依據可供查證。
