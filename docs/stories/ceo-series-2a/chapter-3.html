An Alarm at Noon

總裁先生，請先排隊 · 第 3 / 25 章 · 1000–2000 · 英中對照 · 校訂中

用互動版查詞、朗讀與收藏

At noon the following day, an alarm rang in the market's storage wing. People hurried toward the front door, but a stack of boxes blocked the exit. Mira had come to inspect the paths, so she moved the nearest box with a seller and told others to use the open side door. No one was hurt. Evan arrived without reporters and asked the safety officer what had actually happened before making a public statement.

隔天正午，市場倉儲區的警報響起。人們急忙走向正門，卻發現出口被箱子堵住。米拉原本在檢查動線，她和攤商移開最近的箱子，請其他人改走開放的側門。沒有人受傷。伊凡抵達時沒有帶記者，他先問安全主管實際發生了什麼，才準備對外說明。

The alarm came from a kitchen sensor, not a fire. Mira stayed calm and counted everyone outside. She used her cell phone to call the safety office. The old wing had a classic wooden entrance that needed repair. A cowboy hat on a coat hook belonged to the flower seller.

警報來自廚房感測器，並非真的起火。米拉保持冷靜，在戶外清點人數。她用手機聯絡安全辦公室。舊區有一座典雅的木製入口，需要修繕。衣帽鉤上的牛仔帽是花販的。

Victor told the press that careless market sellers had caused a dangerous event. The safety officer had not said that. Mira asked Evan to correct the claim, even if doing so embarrassed Northstar. Evan ordered no one to speak for the officer and requested the signed report. He then offered to pay for a proper check. Mira accepted the inspection, not his attempt to own the story.

維克多告訴媒體，是粗心的攤商造成危險事件。但安全主管根本沒這麼說。米拉要求伊凡更正，即使北星會因此難堪。伊凡指示任何人都不得替安全主管發言，並要求取得簽署過的報告。接著，他提議出資進行正式檢查。米拉接受檢查，卻不接受由他替大家決定事件的說法。

The safety officer later showed Mira the log from the sensor test. The page had spaces for the date, the worker's name, and the result, but the result line had been left without a signature. He said the device had sounded during an ordinary kitchen test, and no fire had been found. The open door helped everyone leave safely; the blocked door still needed attention. Mira asked the shop owners to help make a simple rule: nothing could stay within the marked exit area, even for five minutes. A baker said that deliveries would be harder. She agreed and added a separate stopping place to the plan. Evan offered extra staff for the first week, but she asked the group to decide whether they wanted that help. The sellers accepted two helpers and a public review at the end of the week.

後來，安全主管讓米拉看感測器測試紀錄。頁面上有日期、員工姓名和結果欄，結果欄卻沒有簽名。他說裝置是在一般廚房測試時響起，並未發現火災。開著的門幫大家安全離開，但被堵住的門仍需要處理。米拉請店主一起訂簡單規則：標示的逃生範圍裡不能堆東西，連五分鐘也不行。麵包師傅說這會讓配送更難。她同意，於是計畫裡另加一處卸貨停靠點。伊凡提議第一週增派人手，她請市場團體自行決定是否接受。攤商最後同意兩名幫手，並約定一週後公開檢討。

Mira gave reporters a direct answer and refused to guess. A broken dryer in the laundry stall was not connected to the alarm. The guard knew his duty was to keep both exits clear. The storage room was nearly empty after everyone left safely. Victor's first report gave a false reason for the event.

米拉直接回答記者的問題，拒絕猜測。洗衣攤壞掉的烘乾機與警報並無關係。保全知道自己的職責是確保兩個出口暢通。所有人安全離開後，倉庫幾乎空了。維克多最初的報告給出了錯誤原因。

Mira spent the afternoon drawing a new path around the stalls. The shop owners helped her, but she made each person check their own doorway. Evan watched from a distance. At dusk he offered her a ride home. She said she wanted the train; her next stop was her mother's apartment. He asked which train line she used, then stopped himself. She smiled at his effort to be polite rather than in control.

米拉整個下午都在畫攤位間的新通道。店主協助她，但她讓每個人檢查自己的門口。伊凡在遠處看著。黃昏時，他提議送她回家。她說自己想搭火車，下一站是母親的公寓。他問她搭哪條線，隨即停住。米拉對他努力表現禮貌而非控制的樣子微笑了。

The barber offered free water while waiting for his next haircut. A cook moved a tray of lamb away from the emergency path. Mira placed each heavy object behind a marked safety line. The press wanted a dramatic story, but she gave them the report. She did not want to punish a worker before hearing the facts.

理髮師等下一位客人剪髮時，主動提供免費飲水。廚師把一盤羊肉移離緊急通道。米拉把每件重物放到標示過的安全線後方。媒體想要戲劇化的故事，她卻交出正式報告。她不想在查清事實前懲罰任何工作者。

On her way home, Mira received a copy of the signed safety note. The kitchen sensor had been tested badly after a recent change, and two emergency paths required work. The facts did not prove a secret plan or a great crime. They did prove that the market needed repairs before the city decided its future. She wrote the date in her notebook. A true small finding could be the start of a much bigger choice.

回家途中，米拉收到已簽署的安全紀錄。廚房感測器最近改裝後測試不周，兩條逃生路線也需要改善。這些事實不能證明有什麼祕密陰謀或重大犯罪，卻能證明城市在決定市場的未來前必須先修繕。她在筆記本寫下日期。一個真實的小發現，可能是更大選擇的起點。

A model boat with a red sail hung above the seafood shop. A photographer took one clear shot of the blocked passage. The cleaners said they might strike if exits remained unsafe. The safety officer arranged a tour of both emergency routes. The sellers wanted safe trade, not a dangerous shortcut.

海產店上方吊著一艘有紅色帆的小船模型。攝影師清楚拍下一張通道遭堵塞的照片。清潔人員說，如果出口持續不安全，他們可能罷工。安全主管安排大家巡查兩條緊急路線。攤商想要安全營業，而不是冒險抄捷徑。
