診所門口

The Clinic Door

總裁先生，請先排隊 · 第 4 / 25 章 · 1000–2000 · 英中對照 · 校訂中

用互動版查詞、朗讀與收藏

The next week, Mira visited the small clinic beside the market. Its patients used the same path as delivery trucks, and the path was narrow at the corner. The clinic director had asked the company for changes before, but no one had replied. Mira listened to the patients and marked their routes on her map. Evan came alone this time, carrying the earlier letter and a new form for public comments.

隔週，米拉拜訪市場旁的小診所。病人和送貨卡車使用同一條路，轉角尤其狹窄。診所主任早就要求公司改善，卻始終沒有得到回應。米拉聽取病人的意見，在地圖上畫出路線。這次伊凡獨自前來，手裡拿著早先的信和新的公開意見表。

The nurse carried a blood test box through the busy crossing. The clinic was a branch of the public health service. Mira wanted to combine the clinic route with the market walkway. The director showed where patients waited when the rain came. People with serious disease needed a clear and quiet entrance.

護理師拿著驗血盒穿過忙碌的路口。這家診所是公共醫療服務的一間分所。米拉想把診所動線和市場步道結合起來。主任指出下雨時病人候診的位置。患有重病的人需要通暢而安靜的入口。

Victor suggested moving the clinic to a far side street. His plan looked tidy, but he had not asked the patients. Evan asked Mira for a full cost comparison. She said she could provide one, but only if her studio was paid for the work and allowed to publish its method. He agreed to a short contract with a clear end date. Her signature made her a contractor, not his employee or his guest.

維克多提議把診所移到較遠的側街。他的計畫看來整齊，卻沒有詢問病人的意見。伊凡請米拉提出完整成本比較。她說可以做，但她的工作室必須獲得合理報酬，且能公開方法。他同意簽一份期限清楚的短約。她簽名後成為獨立承包人，而不是他的員工或賓客。

Mira's final cost sheet showed three possible routes beside the clinic. The first kept the old road and added lights. The second made the corner wider by moving a storage wall. The third sent delivery trucks behind the market. She listed the price, time, and problem for each choice. The director chose the second as the best starting point, but the delivery group worried about losing work space. Mira did not cross out their concern. She drew a smaller storage area and asked an engineer to check whether it was possible. When Evan saw the revised sheet, he said it looked less beautiful than Victor's bright model. Mira agreed. She told him that a useful plan was sometimes full of notes and crossed lines. He asked to keep that untidy sheet for the next public meeting. She allowed it, provided the date and her studio's name stayed visible.

米拉最後的成本表在診所旁列出三種可能路線。第一種保留舊路、增設照明；第二種移動倉庫牆，把轉角加寬；第三種讓送貨卡車改走市場後方。她逐一列出價格、時間和問題。主任認為第二種最適合先試，但配送團體擔心失去工作空間。米拉沒有刪掉他們的憂慮，而是畫出較小的倉儲區，請工程師確認可不可行。伊凡看見修訂表時說，它不如維克多的漂亮模型好看。米拉同意，並告訴他，有用的計畫有時就是寫滿註記與刪改。他想保留這張凌亂的表，留待下次公開會議使用。她同意，條件是日期與工作室名稱必須清楚保留。

The entire clinic staff signed a request for a safer path. A side gate gave patients another way to escape during an emergency. Evan offered a generous fee, but Mira asked for fair terms first. A worker shared a burger with the exhausted survey team. Mira asked Evan to imagine crossing that road with a sick child.

診所全體員工簽署了改善通道的請求。側門讓病人在緊急時有另一條逃生路線。伊凡提出優厚報酬，米拉卻先要求公平條款。一名工人和疲憊的調查團隊分享漢堡。米拉請伊凡想像抱著生病的孩子過那條馬路。

On the walk back, Evan asked if she always worked this hard. Mira answered that effort was not a special gift she gave to rich people. She worked hard because a design could keep someone safe or place them in danger. Evan looked at the crossing again and said he understood. Mira did not know whether that was true. She decided to judge him by the next document, not by the warm tone of his voice.

走回去時，伊凡問她是否總是這麼努力。米拉回答，努力不是她特別送給有錢人的禮物。她努力，是因為設計可能保護人，也可能讓人陷入危險。伊凡再次望向路口，說他懂了。米拉不確定他是否真的懂，決定以他下一份文件來判斷，而不是只聽他溫柔的聲音。

The new walking surface needed safe material that stayed firm in rain. The clinic was part of a wider network of public services. A fruit seller offered ripe papaya to patients waiting outside. The local population included many older people without cars. Mira gave each route a safety score based on simple tests.

新步道需要雨天仍能穩固的安全材料。診所屬於更廣大的公共服務網絡。水果攤商拿熟木瓜給在外面等候的病人。當地居民中有許多沒有車的年長者。米拉依據簡單測試，替每條路線評安全分數。

That night she compared the two plans at her own desk. Moving the clinic looked cheaper on Victor's sheet, but that sheet did not include the cost of new medical rooms. Keeping the clinic beside the market was not free either; the road had to change. Mira marked both numbers in red. If she wanted people to trust her, she could not hide the cost of her favorite choice.

那晚，米拉在自己的桌前比較兩種方案。維克多的表格顯示搬遷較便宜，卻沒有列入興建醫療空間的成本。診所留在市場旁也不是零成本，因為道路必須改造。米拉把兩組數字都標成紅色。如果她希望大家相信她，就不能隱瞞自己偏愛方案的成本。

Her sense of duty told her to show the bad numbers too. The team's first target was a safe entrance by winter. The old gate was too narrow; therefore, it needed a new route. The wooden bench was made of old wood from the market. A clear path was worth more than a bright wall.

她的責任感提醒她，難看的數字也要公開。團隊的第一個目標，是冬季前完成安全入口。舊門太窄，因此需要一條新的通道。那張木椅由市場的舊木材製成。一條暢通的路比漂亮的牆面更有價值。
