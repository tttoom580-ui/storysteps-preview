A Seat for Every Age

總裁先生，請先排隊 · 第 8 / 25 章 · 1000–2000 · 英中對照 · 校訂中

用互動版查詞、朗讀與收藏

Mira placed three empty chairs beside the public display: one for someone who walked slowly, one for a parent carrying a child, and one for anyone who needed rest. She asked visitors to try the path at their own pace. Some students laughed at first, but their faces changed when an older woman explained how difficult it was to stand in a moving bus. Evan stayed beside the entrance, taking notes rather than giving orders.

米拉在公開展示旁放了三張空椅：一張給走得慢的人，一張給抱孩子的家長，一張給任何需要休息的人。她請訪客用自己的速度試走路線。有些學生起初發笑，但一位年長女士說明在行駛中的公車上站立有多難後，他們的表情改變了。伊凡站在入口旁，專心記筆記，而非下指令。

Every adult visitor could ask for a chair without explaining why. The clinic offered first aid during the walking test. An ancient stone marker stood beside the market's first entrance. June walked backward to check whether the signs were easy to see. The tea shop had served this street for nearly a century.

每位成年訪客都可以要求椅子，不必解釋原因。診所在步行測試期間提供急救協助。一塊古老石碑立在市場最早的入口旁。君倒著走，檢查指示牌是否容易看清。那間茶店服務這條街將近一個世紀。

The test showed that Victor's smooth-looking steps were difficult for people pulling carts. Mira recorded the time each person needed and asked whether they wanted their comments included. A woman refused and Mira thanked her anyway. Evan watched that refusal closely. Later he admitted that his staff often treated silence as agreement. Mira said silence could mean fear, tiredness, or simply a wish to go home. It was never a blank signature.

測試顯示，維克多設計的漂亮階梯讓拉推車的人很難通過。米拉記下每人需要的時間，並詢問是否願意收錄意見。有位女士拒絕，米拉仍向她道謝。伊凡仔細看著那次拒絕。後來他承認，自己的員工常把沉默當作同意。米拉說，沉默可能是害怕、疲累，或只是想回家，絕不是一張空白的簽名。

The older visitors taught Mira something that no single drawing could show. One woman could walk quickly but needed to sit after ten minutes. Another moved slowly but never wanted help unless she asked. A father pushed a child in a cart while holding the hand of another child. Mira wrote three different stories beside the same path. She explained to Evan that an average walking time hid the difference between people. The company could publish one useful number, but it also needed notes about where the number came from. He asked the architect to add resting places without turning them into private café seats. The architect said the change would cost more. Mira asked for a price. By that afternoon the team had a number they could discuss instead of a vague argument about whether older people mattered.

年長訪客教了米拉一件單靠圖紙看不出的事。一位女士走得快，卻每十分鐘就需要坐下；另一位走得慢，但除非主動開口，否則不想接受幫忙。一位父親推著孩子的車，同時牽著另一個孩子的手。米拉在同一路線旁寫下三種不同故事。她向伊凡解釋，平均步行時間會藏起人與人的差異。公司可以公布有用的數字，但也需要標註數字從何而來。他請建築師增設休息點，且不能把它們變成私人咖啡館的座位。建築師說會增加費用。米拉要求估價。當天下午，大家終於有一個可以討論的數字，而不是模糊爭論年長者是否重要。

Mira changed the design after watching the older woman rest twice. An elder from the neighborhood described the old public benches. A child with a fever waited at the clinic instead of joining the test. The grain seller explained why heavy sacks needed a flat path. No visitor had to share a private identity number to answer questions.

米拉看見長者休息兩次後，改了設計。社區長輩描述了從前的公共長椅。一名發燒的孩子留在診所等候，沒有參加測試。穀物商解釋了沉重米袋為何需要平坦通道。任何訪客都不必提供私人身分號碼才能回答問題。

At lunch Evan bought meals for the entire team from several market stalls. Mira thanked him but paid for her own. He asked whether she thought every kindness hid a plan. She answered that kindness could be real, and so could the need for boundaries. The words made him quiet, not angry. Later he washed the used cups without being asked. It was a small task, but he stayed until the last table was clean.

午餐時，伊凡向幾個攤位訂了大家的餐點。米拉向他道謝，卻自己付錢。他問，她是否覺得每一份善意都藏著算計。她回答，善意可以是真實的，界線也同樣需要。他沉默了，沒有生氣。後來他主動洗杯子，留下直到最後一張桌子也擦乾淨。那只是小事，卻讓人記住。

The printed instruction asked people to stop if the path felt unsafe. A mother held her sleeping child on her lap near the clinic. The city would need money to maintain the benches each year. The fruit shop gave a ripe peach to the oldest volunteer. Mira would not ask anyone to pose for a photo without consent.

印好的說明請大家在覺得不安全時立刻停止。一位母親在診所旁把睡著的孩子抱在腿上。市府每年都需要經費維護長椅。水果店送一顆熟桃子給年紀最大的志工。未經同意，米拉不會要求任何人擺姿勢拍照。

That night Mira sent the results to the city office and copied the market group. She included the good news and the difficult numbers. The test had not proved that the whole plan was safe, only that several routes needed change. She felt tired but pleased. Evan sent a brief message: Thank you for making the people visible. Mira read it twice, then replied with the time of the next meeting. She saved the personal feeling for later.

那晚，米拉把結果寄給市府，並副本通知市場團體。她把好消息和難看的數字一起放進去。測試沒有證明整份計畫安全，只證明幾條路線需要調整。她很疲倦，卻感到欣慰。伊凡傳來簡短訊息：「謝謝妳讓人被看見。」米拉看了兩次，回覆下次會議時間。至於心裡其他感受，她留待以後再說。

A stranger asked where the public drinking fountain had gone. Children could swim at the community pool, but not in the canal. A small tear in the map was repaired with clear tape. A typical morning brought shoppers, carts, bikes, and buses together. The used chairs were cleaned and checked before the public test.

一名陌生人詢問公共飲水台去了哪裡。孩子可以去社區泳池游泳，不能在運河裡游。地圖上的小裂口用透明膠帶補好了。一般早晨會有購物者、推車、自行車和公車同時出現。舊椅子在公開測試前都經過清潔與檢查。
