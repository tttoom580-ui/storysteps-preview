合約有兩頁

The Contract Has Two Pages

總裁先生，請先排隊 · 第 5 / 25 章 · 1000–2000 · 英中對照 · 校訂中

用互動版查詞、朗讀與收藏

Mira arrived at the contract meeting with her own lawyer's notes. She was not surprised by the money. She was surprised by a line on page two: Northstar would own every draft, including work Mira had made years before. She pushed the page back across the table. Evan looked at the clause and frowned. He said the company used the same form for every supplier. Mira said a common form could still be unfair.

米拉帶著律師的筆記出席合約會議。金額並未讓她意外；第二頁的一行字才令她震驚：北星將擁有所有草稿，包括她多年前完成的作品。她把那一頁推回桌子另一端。伊凡看著條款皺眉，說公司對所有供應商都用同一份表格。米拉回答，常見的表格也可能不公平。

The new clause would place a heavy burden on her small studio. Her first design had been made during her last year of college. The fee could double, but she would not sell her old drawings. Mira listed each travel expense instead of hiding costs in the total. A clear public path was the main feature of her design.

新條款會讓她的小工作室承擔沉重負擔。她第一份設計作品做於大學最後一年。酬金可以加倍，她也不會出售舊作品。米拉逐一列出交通費用，而不是把成本藏進總數。明確的公共通道是她設計的主要特色。

Evan asked the legal team to rewrite the clause and invited Mira to leave if she wanted. No one blocked the door. She walked to the lift, then returned because she still believed the market deserved a sound plan. This time Evan put the revised page on the table without a speech. He had removed the old-work clause and added a right to show the project's data in a public meeting after private details were removed.

伊凡請法務團隊重寫條款，並告訴米拉若想離開也可以。沒有人擋門。她走到電梯，又回來，因為她仍認為市場值得一份周全的計畫。這次伊凡沒有長篇大論，只把修訂頁放到桌上。他刪掉舊作品條款，並加入在移除私人資訊後可於公開會議展示專案數據的權利。

The new contract also stated how Mira would be paid if the city stopped the project. She had asked for that line because a small studio could not spend months working for nothing. Evan thought the request was reasonable, but Victor called it a sign that she was not loyal. Mira answered that loyalty belonged to people and honest promises, not to unpaid work. The lawyer wrote a payment schedule tied to completed reports. No one would own the results until they paid for them, and public safety facts could still be shared. After the meeting, June asked whether Mira now trusted the handsome president. Mira laughed and said she trusted the signed page more than his face. Yet she admitted that he had listened when she spoke. That was something a good contract could protect, but not create. She left with her copy and the freedom to accept her next job.

新合約也寫明，如果市府停止專案，米拉應如何取得已完成工作的報酬。她要求加入這一條，因為小工作室不能白做幾個月。伊凡認為合理，維克多卻說這證明她不忠誠。米拉回答，忠誠應給人與誠實的承諾，而不是給無薪工作。律師按完成報告的進度訂付款時程。對方未付款前不能取得成果所有權，而公共安全事實仍可分享。會後，君問米拉是不是已相信那位英俊總裁。米拉笑說，她比起他的臉更相信簽好的紙。但她也承認，自己說話時他真的有聽。好合約可以保護這件事，卻不能創造它。她帶著副本與接受下一份工作的自由離開。

A fellow designer checked the drawings for hidden ownership claims. The new stairs had to function for families carrying heavy bags. An older lady from the market asked whether rents would rise. Evan admitted that no contract was perfect on its first draft. The studio kept control of the production of its own drawings.

另一位設計師同行幫忙檢查圖紙有無隱藏的所有權要求。新樓梯必須讓提著重物的家庭也能正常使用。市場的一位老太太問租金是否會上漲。伊凡承認沒有合約在第一稿就完美無缺。工作室保留自己設計圖的製作控制權。

When the papers were ready, Mira signed and asked for a copy before she left. Evan offered a small celebration lunch. She declined because she had promised to visit the sellers. He surprised her by asking whether he could join that visit as a listener. She said he could come only if the sellers agreed. He accepted the condition without arguing, and the corner of her mouth moved toward a smile.

文件完成後，米拉簽名，離開前要求拿一份副本。伊凡提議一起吃午餐慶祝。她婉拒，因為已答應去看攤商。他出乎她意料地問，能不能以旁聽者身分同行。她說必須先取得攤商同意。他沒有爭辯，接受條件，米拉嘴角微微上揚。

Mira seldom mixed her business duties with private invitations. The sellers wanted a voice in the selection of temporary stalls. Children played soccer beside the unfinished public square. The market's spirit depended on people, not expensive signs. Mira had to swallow her anger before explaining the clause again.

米拉很少把工作職責與私人邀約混在一起。攤商希望參與臨時攤位的選擇。孩子們在尚未完工的公共廣場旁踢足球。市場的精神取決於人，而不是昂貴招牌。米拉必須先壓下怒氣，才能再次解釋條款。

Outside, a sudden shower washed the dust from the sidewalk. Evan opened an umbrella but did not move toward Mira. Instead he held it out, letting her choose. She took the umbrella, thanked him, and walked in the direction of the station. Their hands touched for a moment at the handle. That brief contact was strange because it was offered without pressure. The real promise between them was still written on two pages.

外面突然下起雨，沖去了人行道上的灰塵。伊凡撐開傘，卻沒有直接走向米拉，而是把傘遞過來，讓她選擇。她接過傘、道了謝，朝車站走去。兩人的手在傘柄上短暫碰到。那觸碰特別，是因為沒有任何壓迫。此時，他們真正的承諾仍寫在兩頁紙上。

Evan gave a dry sweater to a volunteer caught in the rain. A cleaner came to sweep water away from the shop doors. A safety guide explained that a terrorist threat required official police advice. The lawyer offered Mira a tissue when rain reached her face. She would upload the signed public version after checking private details.

伊凡把乾毛衣交給一名淋雨的志工。清潔工過來把店門前的積水掃開。安全指南說明，若遇恐怖分子威脅，必須依警方正式指示行動。雨水流到米拉臉上時，律師遞給她一張面紙。她會在確認私人資訊已移除後，上傳可公開的簽署版本。
