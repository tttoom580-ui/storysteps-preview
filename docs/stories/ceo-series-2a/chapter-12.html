空白的一行

The Blank Line

總裁先生，請先排隊 · 第 12 / 25 章 · 1000–2000 · 英中對照 · 校訂中

用互動版查詞、朗讀與收藏

At the next city review, the proposed budget contained a blank line beside the cost of moving the clinic. Mira asked the city clerk to leave the page on the screen while she explained the missing figure. Victor said a future buyer could decide it later. The clinic director disagreed. Patients could not wait for an unknown owner to decide where they would receive care. Evan asked Northstar's finance team to calculate the cost before the session ended.

下次市府審查時，預算表的診所搬遷成本旁竟是一行空白。米拉請市府書記把那頁留在螢幕上，讓她解釋缺少的數字。維克多說未來買家可以之後決定。診所主任不同意，因為病人不能等未知的業主決定他們到哪裡就醫。伊凡要求北星財務團隊在會議結束前算出費用。

Mira refused to blame the clerk for a line the company had left empty. The blank space stood between two printed cost totals. Northstar needed capital for repairs, not just a bright sales plan. A nurse traveled from the countryside each morning to work at the clinic. A regular customer asked whether the pharmacy would remain open.

公司漏填的那一行，米拉拒絕怪罪書記。空白欄位位於兩筆印好的費用總額之間。北星需要維修資金，而不只是漂亮的銷售計畫。一名護理師每天早上從鄉下通勤到診所。一名熟客問藥局是否會繼續營業。

The review was paused for thirty minutes. In the hall Evan asked Mira how she could still be polite after so many missing numbers. She said anger was useful for noticing harm but poor at adding figures. He laughed, then grew serious. His father had taught him that the first person to speak loudly would win. Mira answered that sometimes the quiet person holding the correct paper was the one who changed the room. Evan looked at her as though she had opened a window.

會議暫停三十分鐘。大廳裡，伊凡問米拉，面對這麼多漏掉的數字，她怎麼還能有禮貌。她說憤怒能幫人發現傷害，卻不擅長做加法。他笑了，隨後正色說，父親教他，先大聲說話的人就會贏。米拉回答，有時真正改變房間的是安靜拿著正確文件的人。伊凡望著她，彷彿她打開了一扇窗。

Back at the studio, June helped Mira prepare two versions of the cost sheet. One had all the figures for the city, while the second used short explanations and pictures for visitors who did not work with budgets. June pointed out that some people might feel ashamed if they could not understand the first version. Mira agreed and asked her to test the second one with three sellers. The sellers found two unclear words and one picture that suggested the clinic would move even though no decision had been made. June corrected them. When Mira thanked her, June said she had wanted to draw pretty signs when she first joined the studio. Now she was learning that a useful sign could stop a wrong idea before it spread. Mira let her put her own name on the finished page. A good team needed more than one person whose work could be seen.

回到工作室後，君協助米拉準備兩種成本表。一種包含所有正式數字，供市府審查；另一種用簡短解釋與圖片，讓不熟悉預算的訪客閱讀。君指出，有些人若看不懂第一份版本，可能會覺得羞愧。米拉同意，請她找三名攤商測試第二版。攤商找到兩個不清楚的詞，以及一張讓人誤以為診所已決定搬遷的圖片。君把它們改好。米拉道謝時，君說自己剛進工作室只想畫漂亮招牌，如今才知道有用的標示能在錯誤想法傳開前阻止它。米拉讓君在完成頁面上署名。好的團隊需要不只一個人的作品被看見。

The hidden cost created danger for patients who might lose local care. A dentist in the next building shared the same narrow entrance. The new plan changed the direction of delivery trucks at the corner. The food vendor sent warm fries to people waiting outside. Mira could hardly hear Victor when the crowd began talking.

隱藏成本會讓可能失去在地醫療的病人陷入危險。隔壁大樓的牙醫使用同一個狹窄入口。新計畫改變轉角送貨卡車的行駛方向。小吃攤送熱薯條給在外面等候的人。群眾開始交談時，米拉幾乎聽不清維克多的聲音。

When the finance team returned, the number was far larger than Victor had suggested. The city requested a revised plan. Mira wrote to every person who had submitted a comment and explained the change in ordinary language. Some sellers wanted her to declare victory, but she refused. A missing number had been found; the project still required a fair decision. She went home before sunset for the first time that week.

財務團隊回來時，數字遠高於維克多原本暗示的金額。市府要求修訂計畫。米拉寫信給每一位提出意見的人，用一般人聽得懂的語言解釋變化。有些攤商想讓她宣布勝利，她拒絕了。漏掉的數字找到了，但專案仍需公平決策。那是她一整週第一次在日落前回家。

The clerk used blue ink to mark the corrected budget page. An engineer came to measure the width of the clinic gate. Mira's mother had left a bowl of pudding in the fridge for her. The committee agreed to regard the clinic as an essential public service. Evan showed respect by letting the clinic director finish speaking.

書記用藍墨水標示修正後的預算頁。工程師前來測量診所大門寬度。米拉的母親在冰箱裡留了一碗布丁給她。委員會同意把診所視為必要公共服務。伊凡讓診所主任講完話，以此展現尊重。

That evening Evan called only to ask whether Mira had reached home safely. She told him she had, and they spoke for a few minutes about the flowers on her mother's windowsill. Evan did not bring up his board or the figures. Mira leaned against the kitchen door, hearing the softness in his voice. She was beginning to see the man who remained when the president put down his papers. Still, she wanted the freedom to decide what that man meant to her.

那晚，伊凡打電話來，只問米拉是否平安到家。她說到了，兩人聊了幾分鐘母親窗邊的花。他沒有提董事會或數據。米拉倚著廚房門，聽見他聲音裡的溫柔。她開始看見那個把總裁文件放下後仍存在的男人。但她仍想保有自己決定這人對她有何意義的自由。

A child said she felt scared on the old crossing at night. The city asked for a fair settlement before any shop had to move. An elder tested the floor in soft slippers to check for slips. A sudden change in the numbers stopped the vote. Mira poured tea from her mother's old teapot after dinner.

一個孩子說，夜裡走舊路口會害怕。市府要求所有店面搬遷前先談妥公平安排。一位長者穿軟拖鞋測試地板是否容易滑倒。數字突然改變，使表決暫停。晚餐後，米拉用母親的舊茶壺倒茶。
