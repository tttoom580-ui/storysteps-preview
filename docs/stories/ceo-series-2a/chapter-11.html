沒有鏡頭的咖啡

Coffee Without Cameras

總裁先生，請先排隊 · 第 11 / 25 章 · 1000–2000 · 英中對照 · 校訂中

用互動版查詞、朗讀與收藏

The first review ended without a final vote. The city asked for new numbers, and Evan agreed that the sellers could stay until the next meeting. Mira met him at a small café after work, as promised. No one carried a camera. For the first ten minutes they spoke about simple things: old music, their families, and how a city smelled after rain. Evan admitted he had once tried to solve every argument by offering more money. Mira said money could help, but it could not answer every question.

第一輪審查沒有進行最終表決。市府要求補充數據，伊凡同意攤商在下次會議前可以留下。米拉下班後如約在小咖啡館與他見面，沒有人帶攝影機。前十分鐘，他們聊的都是簡單的事：老音樂、家人，以及雨後城市的氣味。伊凡承認，他過去總試著用更多錢解決爭執。米拉說金錢可以幫忙，但無法回答所有問題。

Evan was a beginner at having coffee without a business purpose. Mira asked the café to charge her for her own drink. He did not chase her for an answer about their next meeting. She wanted to emphasize that her contract would stay independent. Her goal was a fair market, not a powerful boyfriend.

伊凡對於不帶工作目的地喝咖啡，還是個新手。米拉請咖啡館把自己的飲料單獨計價。他沒有追著她要下一次見面的答覆。她想強調，自己的合約仍要保持獨立。她的目標是公平的市場，不是找一個有權勢的男友。

A soft sound came from Evan's phone. It was a message from Victor: the board wanted to end Mira's contract before her full report arrived. Evan showed her the message at once. He said he would argue against it, but he could not promise how the board would vote. Mira thanked him for the warning. She also reminded him that he was not her only path to a fair hearing. She could submit the same report through the city office.

伊凡的手機輕響，是維克多的訊息：董事會想在米拉的完整報告送達前終止合約。伊凡立刻把訊息給她看。他說會反對，但無法保證董事會怎麼投票。米拉感謝他的提醒，也告訴他，她通往公平聽證的路不只有他一條；她可以透過市府提交同一份報告。

The café owner brought the bill to the table while Evan was still speaking about his father. He reached for it without looking, and Mira gently placed her own money beside his. He stopped and asked whether splitting the bill was important to her. She said it was, at least tonight. She did not want him to wonder if a cup of coffee bought an answer. They paid separately, then stayed a little longer. Evan described a childhood in which every meal had been a discussion about business, and every quiet choice had needed approval. Mira told him about her mother buying a small flower stall with saved coins. Her mother had often been afraid, but she had owned the choice. Evan listened to that story with a seriousness that made Mira shy. For the first time, he did not talk about how he could help her mother. He asked what her mother had enjoyed about the work.

伊凡還在談父親時，咖啡館老闆把帳單送來。他看也不看便伸手想拿，米拉輕輕把自己的錢放在旁邊。他停下來，問分開付帳對她是否重要。她說至少今晚很重要。她不希望他以為一杯咖啡就能買到某種答覆。兩人各自付錢，又坐了一會兒。伊凡描述自己的童年：每餐都在談生意，每個安靜的選擇都要得到批准。米拉則說起母親如何用積蓄買下一座小花攤。母親常害怕，卻擁有自己的選擇。伊凡認真聽著，使米拉有點害羞。這是他第一次不說能怎麼幫她母親，而是問她母親喜歡工作的哪個部分。

A gold sign on the hotel door did not make a bad rule fair. The waiter brought a plate with one grape and apple slices. Evan began to understand the importance of a simple public record. The café's old iron table stood firmly on the stone floor. A traffic jam outside made the buses run late.

飯店門上的金色招牌無法讓壞規則變公平。服務生送來一只盤子，裡面有一顆葡萄與蘋果片。伊凡開始理解簡單公開紀錄的重要性。咖啡館的舊鐵桌穩穩立在石地板上。外面的塞車讓公車誤點。

When the café closed, Mira told Evan something she had not told the market group. She was afraid her small studio would fail if she spent too much time on one case. Evan offered to introduce clients, then stopped himself. He asked whether that would put unfair pressure on her. Mira said it might. They found another answer: she would look for work through the ordinary public process, and he would not decide who hired her.

咖啡館打烊時，米拉告訴伊凡一件沒向市場團體說的事：她害怕自己把太多時間花在單一案件上，小工作室可能倒閉。伊凡想介紹客戶，又停住了，問那是否會給她不公平的壓力。米拉說可能會。他們找到另一個答案：她會經正常公開流程尋找工作，而他不決定誰雇用她。

Mira kept both hands around her warm mug while she spoke. Their conversation touched on religion, but neither tried to change the other. A local film student asked permission to shoot the café window. Evan said simply, "I want to know you beyond this project." Mira preferred a quiet talk over a large social event.

米拉說話時，雙手一直捧著溫熱的馬克杯。兩人聊到宗教，卻都沒有試著改變對方。一名電影系學生先徵求同意，才拍攝咖啡館窗戶。伊凡簡單地說：「我想在專案之外認識妳。」比起大型社交活動，米拉更喜歡安靜談話。

Outside, Evan offered his hand. Mira looked at it, then placed her own hand in his for a moment. It was neither a contract nor a promise about forever. He smiled as if the small contact had changed his evening. On the train, Mira thought about how she had once imagined a powerful man would always want the last word. Evan had listened instead. That did not solve the project, but it made her curious about him.

屋外，伊凡伸出手。米拉看了看，短暫把自己的手放進他掌心。那既不是合約，也不是永遠的承諾。他微笑，彷彿這小小接觸改變了整個夜晚。車上，米拉想到自己曾以為掌權的人總要說最後一句話，但伊凡今晚選擇聆聽。這沒有解決專案，卻讓她更想認識他。

The success of their evening did not mean the public case was finished. She refused to let kindness become a trap for her professional choices. A true promise needed actions that still mattered the next day. Trust grew when Evan let her choose how close to stand. They left by the usual side door, without a waiting driver.

這個夜晚的順利不代表公共案件已結束。她拒絕讓善意變成限制專業選擇的陷阱。真正的承諾需要隔天仍有意義的行動。伊凡讓她決定彼此站多近，信任就在其中成長。他們從平常的側門離開，沒有司機等候。
