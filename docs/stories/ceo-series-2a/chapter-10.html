A Suit and a Rumor

總裁先生，請先排隊 · 第 10 / 25 章 · 1000–2000 · 英中對照 · 校訂中

用互動版查詞、朗讀與收藏

Evan held a formal dinner for investors at a hotel near the river. Mira arrived in the same dark jacket she wore to public meetings. She had agreed to attend as the owner of an independent design studio, and her invitation said so. When a reporter asked whether she was Evan's secret girlfriend, she smiled and said her work had its own name. Evan, hearing the question, repeated that answer instead of turning the rumor into a joke.

伊凡在河邊飯店舉辦投資人晚宴。米拉穿著參加公開會議時一樣的深色外套到場。她以獨立設計工作室負責人的身分赴宴，邀請函也這樣寫。記者問她是否為伊凡的祕密女友，她微笑說，自己的工作有自己的名字。伊凡聽見後也重複了這個回答，沒有把傳言拿來開玩笑。

The project needed active discussion, not pictures of rich guests. Mira's greatest concern was still the market's public entrance. The bus cycle changed after midnight, leaving longer waits. She showed how the distance from the last bus stop hurt night workers. A red flag on the model marked a dangerous crossing.

專案需要積極討論，而不是富有賓客的照片。米拉最在意的仍是市場的公共入口。午夜後公車班次循環改變，等候時間變長。她指出末班公車站的距離如何影響夜班工作者。模型上的紅旗標示危險路口。

Victor announced that all sellers would soon move to temporary shops beside the river. Mira had never seen that decision in the public record. Evan stopped the dinner speech and asked who had signed the move order. No one could produce it. The room became silent. Mira felt the old wish to fight rising inside her, but she took a breath and asked for the meeting minutes. Evan promised a written answer the next morning.

維克多宣布所有攤商即將搬到河邊臨時店面。米拉從未在公開紀錄見過這項決定。伊凡打斷晚宴致詞，問是誰簽署搬遷命令。沒有人拿得出來。房間沉默了。米拉感覺熟悉的戰意又升起，但她深呼吸，要求查閱會議紀錄。伊凡答應隔天早晨書面回覆。

The hotel had set a long table for visitors from different parts of the city. Mira used the time before dinner to ask a bus worker about the planned late route. He showed her a schedule with two missing stops, including the one closest to the clinic. She photographed it after getting permission and sent it to her team. An investor watched her work and asked why a designer cared so much about buses. Mira said a beautiful market was useless if people could not reach it. Later, as music filled the dining room, she saw Evan look toward her from across the table. She liked him enough to want to smile back. She also liked her work enough to finish the question first. She raised one finger to say "one minute," and he smiled and waited. It was a quiet kind of attention she had not expected at such a rich dinner.

飯店為來自城市不同地方的訪客擺了一張長桌。晚宴開始前，米拉趁機詢問公車人員擬議的夜間路線。對方拿出時刻表，上面少了兩站，其中一站離診所最近。她取得同意後拍照，傳給團隊。一名投資人看著她工作，問設計師為何如此在乎公車。米拉說，若人們到不了市場，再漂亮也沒用。後來餐廳響起音樂，她看見伊凡隔著桌子望向自己。她喜歡他，想對他微笑；也同樣熱愛工作，想先問完問題。她舉起一根手指示意「等一分鐘」，他便笑著等待。這種安靜的關注，是她沒料到會在如此奢華的晚宴上遇見的。

The proposed new shops were on flat ground but far from buses. Mira stepped forward when Victor called the move final. The company could gain money but lose the street's trust. No signed document could indicate that a move had been approved. City law required public notice before such a large change.

擬議的新店面位於平地，卻離公車很遠。維克多宣稱搬遷已定案時，米拉向前站出來。公司或許賺得到錢，卻會失去街道的信任。沒有任何簽署文件顯示搬遷已獲批准。市府法律要求重大變更前必須公開通知。

After the dinner, Evan walked Mira to the hotel lobby. He said he was sorry for the rumor and for Victor's statement. She told him she did not need protection from gossip as much as she needed his company to follow its rules. Evan asked if she would meet him for coffee on a day with no project meeting. Mira looked at him, surprised by the careful question. She did not answer at once. She wanted to know whether she liked him outside a fight.

晚宴後，伊凡陪米拉走到飯店大廳。他為傳言和維克多的發言道歉。她說，比起保護她免受八卦困擾，她更需要公司遵守規則。伊凡問，能否在沒有專案會議的一天請她喝咖啡。米拉看著他，對這個謹慎的問題感到意外。她沒有立刻回答，因為她想知道，離開爭論後自己是否仍喜歡他。

She wrote his question in her notebook beside the meeting date. A move could not occur before the city published its decision. Mira said riches were no excuse for treating people as pieces on a board. The form asked for age but not sex, which was unnecessary for the survey. The room fell into silence when no one could show a signed order.

她把他的問題寫在筆記本上，放在會議日期旁。市府公布決定前不能進行搬遷。米拉說，財富不是把人當棋子擺弄的藉口。表單詢問年齡，卻不問此次調查不需要的性別資訊。沒有人拿得出簽署命令時，房間陷入沉默。

She walked to the riverside platform and watched the lights reflect on the water. Evan stood a respectful distance away until her taxi arrived. He did not touch her arm or turn the moment into a grand speech. She finally said, "One coffee, after the first public review. No cameras." He smiled and nodded. Mira went home knowing that a promise about coffee was easier than a promise about a city, but both deserved to be kept.

她走到河邊平台，看燈光映在水面上。伊凡站在合宜的距離外，直到計程車抵達。他沒有碰她的手臂，也沒把這一刻變成盛大告白。她終於說：「第一次公開審查後，喝一杯咖啡。沒有攝影機。」他笑著點頭。米拉回家時明白，關於咖啡的承諾比關於城市的承諾容易，但兩者都應該遵守。

A single false announcement could frighten hundreds of sellers. Evan loosened his formal suit jacket as the guests left. The team had to track every change in the relocation plan. A guest spoke about a similar market in the northern valley. Mira paid her own taxi fare from a worn leather wallet.

一則不實公告就能讓數百名攤商恐慌。賓客離去時，伊凡鬆開正式西裝外套。團隊必須追蹤搬遷計畫的每一次改動。一名賓客談起北方山谷裡的類似市場。米拉從磨舊的皮夾掏錢，自行支付車資。
