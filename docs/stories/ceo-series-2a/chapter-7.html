會改變的地圖

The Map That Could Change

總裁先生，請先排隊 · 第 7 / 25 章 · 1000–2000 · 英中對照 · 校訂中

用互動版查詞、朗讀與收藏

The city announced the first open review for the Lantern Market. Mira had seven days to prepare the map and publish the method behind her figures. Evan's office wanted a bright model of the new shopping hall, but he ordered the team to show the old routes too. Victor complained that this would make the company look weak. Evan replied that a strong plan could survive questions. Mira heard the exchange from the doorway and wrote down both statements.

市府宣布燈市的第一場公開審查。米拉有七天準備地圖，並公開數據背後的方法。伊凡辦公室想展示光鮮的新商場模型，但他要求團隊也呈現舊動線。維克多抱怨這會讓公司顯得軟弱。伊凡回答，好的計畫應該經得起提問。米拉站在門口聽見這段對話，把兩句話都記下來。

The public review would advance only after every document was posted. Each human being deserved to understand the plan before voting. Mira marked the public hearing on a large calendar. The team studied the local climate before choosing street trees. The map would contain the clinic route and the night bus stop.

所有文件公布後，公開審查才會繼續推進。每個人都有權在表決前理解計畫。米拉把公開聽證日期標在大日曆上。團隊在挑選行道樹前研究當地氣候。地圖將包含診所動線與夜間公車站。

At the market, vendors checked each line and corrected two wrong addresses. Mira did not hide the mistakes; she dated the new version and kept the older one. Her young assistant, June, asked why this took so long. Mira explained that a wrong picture could make a person invisible. A corrected map, by contrast, gave that person a chance to speak. June offered to create a larger print copy for those who could not read the screen.

市場裡，攤販逐條核對資料，改正兩個錯誤地址。米拉沒有隱藏錯誤；她替新版標上日期，保留舊版。年輕助理君問為何如此費時。米拉解釋，錯誤圖像可能讓一個人被忽略；修正後的地圖反而能給他說話的機會。君主動提出替無法看清螢幕的人做一份大字印本。

Mira's studio had other bills to pay while she worked on the map. She called a school that needed a new bus sign and arranged the work for the following week. June would lead the first drawing, and Mira would review it after the market meeting. The arrangement mattered to both women: June needed real practice, and Mira needed her studio to survive without one rich client. Evan found out only when he saw a second project pinned on the wall. He asked about it with interest, not anger. Mira told him that her working hours belonged to her contracts, not to his schedule. He nodded and asked whether the market meeting could begin thirty minutes later. She checked with the sellers before agreeing. It was the first time he had asked a group for time instead of telling them when to arrive.

米拉工作市場地圖的同時，工作室還有別的帳單得付。她致電一所需要新公車站牌的學校，安排下週施工。君將主導第一份圖，米拉在市場會議後審查。這個安排對兩個女人都重要：君需要實務經驗，米拉則需要工作室不靠單一富有客戶也能生存。伊凡直到看見牆上釘著第二個專案才知道。他是感興趣地詢問，不是生氣。米拉說，她的工作時間由合約決定，不屬於他的行程。他點頭，問市場會議能否晚三十分鐘開始。她先和攤商確認才同意。這是他第一次向一群人詢問時間，而不是告訴大家幾點到。

An unused square looked like a desert at noon without any shade. The quiet cleaner gave a humble but useful warning about wet floors. No single road was ideal for every person in the neighborhood. Mira asked June to introduce the new map to the shop owners. A student liked to jog through the market before breakfast.

一處沒有遮蔭的空廣場，正午看起來像沙漠。沉默的清潔員謙虛地提醒大家地板濕滑，卻非常實用。沒有任何一條路適合社區裡的所有人。米拉請君向店主介紹新地圖。一名學生喜歡早餐前慢跑穿過市場。

Evan visited the studio in the evening, bringing the review notice and two paper cups of tea. He stood at the open door until Mira invited him in. She showed him a line on the map that his own architects had forgotten. He admitted he had once thought every problem was a choice between two prices. Now he saw a third question: who would live with the result? Mira said she would not answer that question for the people affected.

傍晚，伊凡拿著審查公告和兩杯熱茶來工作室。他站在開著的門口，直到米拉邀他進來。她指出地圖上他公司的建築師遺漏的一條線。他承認自己以前總把問題看成兩種價格之間的選擇。現在他看見第三個問題：誰要承受結果？米拉說，她不會替受影響的人回答。

The cleaner used a mop where rainwater crossed the hall. The review period lasted one full week before public comment closed. Their principle was simple: every affected voice deserved a place. The team posted its progress online every Friday afternoon. The old service road was rocky after years without repairs.

雨水流過大廳時，清潔員用拖把把水擦乾。審查期長達整整一週，之後才停止收取公眾意見。他們的原則很簡單：每個受影響的聲音都該被聽見。團隊每週五下午在網路上公開進度。舊服務道路多年未修，表面滿是石塊。

After midnight Mira walked home beneath the street lights. The city had not changed in one week, but people now knew the date, the drawings, and the questions. She thought of Evan waiting politely at her office door. It was easier to trust someone who had learned to hear no. She still knew trust was a long road, and she was only on its first safe step.

午夜過後，米拉走在街燈下回家。城市不可能在一週內改變，但大家現在知道日期、圖紙和問題。她想起伊凡禮貌地等在工作室門外。對一個已學會接受「不」的人，信任似乎容易了一些。可她也明白，信任是一條長路，她才剛踏上第一個安全的階梯。

The first step was to check every street at walking speed. The figures were open; thus, the public could question them. The school nurse found a lost toothbrush near the waiting area. A painted turtle marked the slow walking route for young children. Mira said a corrected map was a small victory, not the end.

第一步是以步行速度檢查每條街。數據已公開，因此大眾可以提出質疑。學校護理師在等候區附近撿到一支遺失的牙刷。一隻畫上的烏龜標示孩子適用的慢行路線。米拉說修正地圖是小小勝利，不是終點。
