照片裡的出口

The Exit in the Picture

總裁先生，請先排隊 · 第 9 / 25 章 · 1000–2000 · 英中對照 · 校訂中

用互動版查詞、朗讀與收藏

A newspaper published a picture of the market after closing time. In the picture a row of food carts blocked a gate, and the headline blamed the sellers for careless work. Mira recognized the gate immediately: the photograph had been taken during a special delivery test, with the gate closed by the construction team. The paper had not shown the sign outside the picture. She asked the editor for a correction and attached the dated safety record.

一家報紙刊出市場打烊後的照片，照片裡一排餐車堵住大門，標題指責攤商粗心。米拉立刻認出那道門：照片拍攝於特別配送測試期間，大門由施工團隊暫時關閉。報紙沒有拍進畫面外的告示牌。她向編輯要求更正，附上註明日期的安全紀錄。

The evening market was still alive with people after the shops closed. Mira read the correction request aloud before the sellers signed. A sudden burst of rain sent visitors beneath the covered path. Evan judged Victor by his actions, not by his family name or character claims. A cook made delicious soup for the tired night workers.

即使店鋪打烊，晚間市場仍充滿人聲。攤商簽署前，米拉大聲讀出更正請求。一陣突如其來的大雨讓訪客躲進有頂棚的通道。伊凡以維克多的行動判斷他，而不是聽家世或品格宣稱。一名廚師替疲憊的夜班工人煮了好喝的湯。

Victor refused to say who had sent the photograph. Evan could have ordered his cousin to apologize in public, but an apology without proof would not correct the record. Instead, he asked a neutral staff member to compare the gate log with the picture time. Mira agreed only after the market group could send an observer. They found a clear match. The gate had been closed by contractors for nineteen minutes, and the sellers had been told to move their carts there.

維克多不肯說出照片來源。伊凡原可命堂兄公開道歉，但沒有證據的道歉無法更正紀錄。於是，他請中立員工比對大門日誌和照片時間。米拉同意，但要求市場團體能派觀察員。結果清楚吻合：大門由承包商關閉十九分鐘，攤商還被要求把餐車推到那裡。

The editor called Mira after checking the gate records. She said the first headline had been too strong and offered to print a correction the next day. Mira accepted but asked her to include the safety officer's words, not only her own complaint. The editor agreed. A seller wanted to celebrate by sending an angry letter naming Victor as the source of the photograph. Mira stopped him. They had a photo and a gate log, but they did not yet know who had sent the image. She refused to replace one false claim with another. Evan later told her he had asked his staff to review the source of every public statement in the project. Mira thanked him, then asked whether the rule would remain after this particular story disappeared from the news. He said it would be written into the office guide. She asked for the page number.

編輯核對大門紀錄後打電話給米拉，承認最初標題太強烈，提議隔天刊登更正。米拉接受，但要求放入安全主管的說法，不只是她的抱怨。編輯同意。一名攤商想寄怒信，指名維克多就是照片來源，當作慶祝。米拉阻止他。他們手上有照片和大門紀錄，卻還不知道誰傳出影像。她不願用另一項錯誤指控取代原本的錯誤說法。伊凡後來告訴她，已要求員工審查專案對外發言的來源。米拉道謝，又問當這則新聞不再受關注時，規則是否仍會存在。他說會寫進辦公室指南。她問了頁碼。

The exit was closed for a test, not by the sellers' choice. Mira let the sellers express their anger without making false charges. The cook said he could forgive a mistake after it was honestly corrected. The image alone did not show who had ordered the gate closed. The team hoped to improve the night delivery rules before winter.

出口是為了測試而關閉，並非攤商自行決定。米拉讓攤商表達憤怒，但不提出不實指控。廚師說，錯誤若誠實改正，他願意原諒。單靠影像看不出誰下令關閉大門。團隊希望冬季前改善夜間配送規則。

Evan met Mira outside her studio, not inside the boardroom. He looked tired. His company would have to tell the public that its own workers had directed the carts. Mira said the truth was not a gift she could give or refuse; it belonged to the people who had been blamed. Evan looked down for a long time, then answered that she was right. For once she saw uncertainty on his face, and it made him more human rather than less strong.

伊凡在米拉工作室外見她，不是在董事會議室。他看起來很累。公司必須對外承認，是自己的員工指示餐車位置。米拉說，真相不是她能送出或收回的禮物，它屬於被責怪的人。伊凡低頭沉默了很久，最後說她是對的。她第一次在他臉上看見不確定，那沒有削弱他，反而讓他更像一個人。

A loose lid on a soup pot showed why the carts had been parked. Hot liquid had to be kept away from the walking lane. The photo showed the carts' position but not the contractor's order. The review result placed responsibility on the contractor's planning team. A toy shark hung from the seafood seller's old sign.

湯鍋鬆動的蓋子說明了餐車當時為何停靠。熱液體必須遠離人行通道。照片顯示餐車的位置，卻未顯示承包商的指示。審查結果認定責任在承包商的規劃團隊。海鮮攤的舊招牌上掛著一隻玩具鯊魚。

The newspaper printed its correction the next morning. It was small, beneath a larger story about the city festival. Yet Mira cut it out for the market notice board. People deserved to know when a public claim changed. Evan called to ask whether she would come to the company dinner that week. Mira said she would consider the invitation only if it had nothing to do with the market contract. Evan said he understood, and the line went quiet in a surprisingly comfortable way.

隔天早晨，報紙刊出更正。更正很小，排在城市節慶的大新聞下面。米拉仍剪下它，貼上市場公告板。人們有權知道公開說法何時改變。伊凡來電邀她參加當週公司晚宴。米拉說，只有在邀請與市場合約無關時，她才會考慮。伊凡回答理解，電話兩端出現了一段意外舒服的沉默。

A silly rumor said Mira had bought the newspaper herself. The fair solution was a correction, a review, and a new safety rule. The small red gate on Mira's map became a symbol of public trust. The newspaper changed the title of its online story after checking facts. A worker replaced a damaged water tube beside the gate.

一則荒唐傳言說米拉自己買下了報紙。公平的解法是更正、調查和新的安全規則。米拉地圖上的小紅門成了公共信任的象徵。報紙核實事實後更改網路報導標題。工人更換了大門旁受損的水管。
