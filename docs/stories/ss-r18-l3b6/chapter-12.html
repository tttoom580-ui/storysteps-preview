For a moment Leon seemed ready to walk away. Aria closed the duel contract and asked what had gone wrong. Yet by the time the conversation ended, they had changed their next step. It was a change neither could have achieved by giving another order. In the library, Leon placed two examination sheets side by side. A tiny change in the scoring ink mattered more than all the rumors about which student was stronger. The plan mentioned a need to edit; neither of them accepted that instruction without a reason. At first the change seemed elderly; a second observation made the team less certain.
