The digital log recorded an uneventful return voyage. The paper log found beneath the galley table included seventy-nine additional days. Its handwriting was irregular, and several pages had water damage. Lu Sha had the ink and paper examined before reading the most alarming entry: "Do not enter the mirror chamber without a second witness." Arsen recognized the handwriting but not the order. An older engineer found evidence that the ship's clock had been disconnected from its navigation computer. That could explain the missing days, but not why the paper pages described meals the crew remembered eating. Lu Sha photographed the log in front of two independent witnesses. Someone had written a small note beside the final entry: "If I return younger, ask me whom I left behind." The signature had been cut away, leaving only a single letter: E.
