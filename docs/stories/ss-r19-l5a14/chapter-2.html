Lu Sha ordered the airlock sealed before anyone could greet the returning crew. Captain Arsen watched through the glass while two quarantine nurses measured the atmosphere inside the Oriole. He did not object. "I remember this station," he said, pointing to a faded red square painted beside the door. "A child used to signal ships from there." Lu Sha's hand stopped over her tablet. Thirty years earlier she had stood in that place, sending a private sequence of flashes to ships passing her father's workshop. The records did not include it. She asked Arsen to repeat the pattern, and he did so perfectly. His knowledge was not proof that he was the original captain; someone might have taught him. She wrote both possibilities in her report. At the airlock, Arsen pressed his palm to the glass. "Have you still got the red lamp?" he asked. Lu Sha had kept it locked in a drawer for twenty-nine years.

陸莎下令封鎖氣閘，在檢疫完成前誰也不能接觸返航船員。船長阿森隔著玻璃看護士檢測艙內空氣，並未反對。他指向門旁褪色的紅方格：「我記得這裡，曾有個孩子在這裡對船打燈號。」陸莎的手停在平板上。三十年前，她確曾在父親的工坊外用自創的閃光順序向太空船招呼，公開紀錄沒有留下。她要阿森重複，他一個節拍也沒錯。但這不能直接證明他是真船長，也可能有人教過他。她在報告列出兩種解釋。阿森把掌心貼在玻璃上問：「妳還留著那盞紅燈嗎？」陸莎已將它鎖在抽屜二十九年。
