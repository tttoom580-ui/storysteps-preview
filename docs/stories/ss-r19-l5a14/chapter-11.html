Behind the eighth station was the mirror array: a sequence of recovery beds, chemical tanks, and sensory screens. A manual warned that cellular repair would require long periods of suspended activity. The biological youth of the crew was real, but the procedure depended on replacing damaged cells and suppressing many months of memory formation. Lu Sha interviewed the ship's medic, who remembered agreeing to a short test, never a thirty-year voyage. The consent sheet recorded thirty days and carried no warning about erased decades. Its date had been overwritten by a system update. The crew had not simply traveled through time. Their bodies had been repeatedly restored while their memories were edited. Lu Sha photographed the equipment, then turned the screen away from the crew until they could choose whether they wanted to see it.
