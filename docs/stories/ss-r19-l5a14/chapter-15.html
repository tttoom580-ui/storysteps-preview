Mara was offered power of attorney for her father's treatment because he appeared younger and was considered confused. She refused to sign. "He is not a child," she told the medical board. "He needs information and time." The board arranged a separate evaluation of Arsen's capacity to consent. He understood the risks and chose to postpone memory recovery until the station could prove it was safe. Mara visited again. They spoke not about vanished years but about a song he had taught her. He sang the first verse incorrectly. She corrected him, and they laughed for the first time. The reunion did not restore their old family. It gave them the right to build a different relationship. When Mara left, Arsen asked if she would return next week. "If you want me to," she said. He did.
