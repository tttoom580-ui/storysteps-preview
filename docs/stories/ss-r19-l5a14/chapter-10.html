Its control panel displayed a different departure date from the seven passenger stations. Arsen looked at the drawing and began shaking. He knew the crooked green tree, but not the child who had made it. Lu Sha asked whether anyone recalled another passenger. Nobody did. A voice from the ship's internal system answered instead: "The operator remained. The seven returned." The message had no identifying code. Outside, station officials demanded an arrest for possible unlawful experimentation. Lu Sha insisted on identifying who had actually operated the device before naming a criminal. At the end of the day, the eighth chair sent a signal into the anomaly—and someone answered.
