While cleaning an emergency audio file, a technician isolated a voice that did not belong to any of the seven crew. It was a woman asking the ship not to open the return channel yet. The voice appeared before the oldest surviving entry, where a file name had been replaced by blank characters. Arsen listened without moving. "Eleni," he whispered, and then asked why he had said it. The original boarding list had seven names; the maintenance plan mentioned eight sleeping stations. Lu Sha treated the discrepancy as a question, not a ghost story. She searched passenger manifests, engineering licenses, and medical transport records. An eighth seat could have belonged to a passenger, a test dummy, or an operator who joined later. As they debated, the ship transmitted an automatic response to the woman's voice: CHANNEL HELD. OPERATOR PRESENT.
