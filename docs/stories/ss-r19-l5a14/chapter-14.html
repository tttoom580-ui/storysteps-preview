Eleni's farewell record appeared in a damaged section of the physical log. She had accepted command of the habitat's failing gate, but only after learning that the mirror array could carry seven people home and not eight. The ship's navigator had tried to stay instead. Eleni refused because she understood the gate's controls and he did not. She left no demand to be remembered as a hero. She asked that her medical history remain private and that no authority use her choice to justify similar sacrifices. Lu Sha read only the operational part of the record aloud. Arsen wept at the name but could not remember saying goodbye. His grief was real, even if his recollection was incomplete. On the station's public channel, Lu Sha announced that a living operator might need aid—not rescue without consent.
