Paper Lies

1998，海禾戲院不打烊 · 第 9 / 25 章 · 3000–4000 · 英中對照 · 校訂中

用互動版查詞、朗讀與收藏

A leaflet appeared at the market claiming that Yulan had promised to close two streets for festival traffic. She frowned at the claim; she had made no such promise. Its printing used a familiar local accent in the words, as though the message came from an old neighbor. She inspected the paper attachment on the noticeboard and recognized Xu's assistant's clumsy drawing of the theater door. Under other circumstances she might have laughed, but the content was designed to frighten shopkeepers. Mei was selling crunchy fried noodles when a customer asked whether her daughter intended to ruin business. "Ask her directly," Mei said. "I will not sell you a story alongside your lunch."

市場出現一張傳單，聲稱雨蘭答應在節慶時封閉兩條街。雨蘭皺起眉；她從未做過那種承諾。傳單用字刻意模仿當地口音，像是老鄰居寫的。她查看公告板上的附紙，認出許健助理畫戲院門口的笨拙筆法。換個情況，她也許會笑，但這些內容顯然是要嚇唬商家。廖梅正賣酥脆炒麵時，有客人問她女兒是否打算破壞生意。「你直接問她，」廖梅說，「我不會連午餐一起賣給你一個故事。」

Yulan used an old earphone to review a recorded interview about the town's economic changes. One speaker recalled leaving his homeland during a difficult decade, while another described returning to open a shop. Their experiences contradicted the leaflet's simple promise that a parking lot would solve everything. Wen advised caution. "Do not mislead people in the opposite direction," he said. Yulan instructed the volunteers to copy the town's actual road plan and place it beside the false claim. A brief interruption came when the photocopier stopped. Instead of guessing, they waited for the shopkeeper to repair it. Accurate information was worth an extra hour.

雨蘭戴著舊耳機，重聽一段關於小鎮經濟變化的訪談。一位受訪者回憶艱難年代離開故鄉，另一位則說回鄉開店的經過。他們的經歷顯然比傳單上「停車場能解決一切」的承諾複雜。沈文提醒她要小心。「別反過來誤導大家，」他說。雨蘭指示志工影印鎮上真正的道路計畫，與錯誤宣傳並排展示。影印機突然故障，工作暫時中斷。他們沒有亂試，而是等店主修好。為了正確資訊，多花一個鐘頭是值得的。

A retired engineer came to the courtyard and leaned over the maps. He had worked at a nuclear research facility many years earlier and knew how easily frightening technical words could be used in arguments. He told Yulan that the real task was to separate evidence from fear, whatever the subject. A child tried to peep through the theater's boarded window, and Qin gently guided her away. The ticket office had received requests for a refund from people who mistakenly believed the cinema was already open. Yulan returned every payment, keeping the receipts. "Modesty means knowing what we cannot provide yet," she told the volunteers. "We have nothing to sell inside that room."

一位退休工程師來到庭院，俯身看地圖。他多年前曾在核能研究機構工作，知道可怕的技術詞彙多容易被拿來爭辯。他告訴雨蘭，不管議題是什麼，真正重要的是區分證據與恐懼。一個孩子想從封起來的戲院窗戶偷看，秦姨溫和地帶她離開。售票間收到幾筆退費要求，原來有人誤以為戲院已經重新營業。雨蘭把款項全數退回，並保存收據。「謙遜就是知道自己目前做不到什麼，」她對志工說，「我們還沒有任何能在室內賣給觀眾的東西。」

That evening, a local artist called the idea of a community-owned theater revolutionary. Yulan winked at Qin, who knew she disliked large labels. She corrected the artist politely: the land belonged to the town, and they were seeking a lease, not claiming ownership. The distinction mattered because an inspiring slogan could become another false promise. Wen helped her write a short correction for the market noticeboard. It listed what they had proposed and what they had not proposed. No insult appeared in the text. When Xu's assistant passed the board, he stopped long enough to read it. Yulan could not tell whether he felt embarrassed, and decided not to invent an answer.

傍晚，一位在地藝術家稱「社區共同經營戲院」是革命性的構想。雨蘭對秦姨眨眨眼，秦姨知道她不喜歡這種大帽子。她禮貌地更正：土地屬於鎮上，她們爭取的是租約，不是宣稱所有權。這個區別很重要，因為鼓舞人心的口號也可能變成另一個錯誤承諾。沈文幫她寫了張短短的更正公告，列出提出與未提出的事項，沒有任何侮辱字眼。許健的助理經過公告板時，停下來讀了一會兒。雨蘭不知道他是否尷尬，也決定不替他猜答案。

The false leaflet had done damage, even after the correction. Two shopkeepers withdrew their signatures, explaining that they could not risk losing access to their stores. Yulan accepted their decision and updated the count. She began to understand how trust was lost: not in a single dramatic moment, but in small doubts that people carried home. Her mother washed the evening dishes and said that returning money quickly had been more convincing than a long speech. Yulan wrote that beside the day's accounts. She still wanted people to choose the cinema. More importantly, she wanted their choice to be based on facts they could check for themselves.

錯誤傳單即使澄清後仍造成損害。兩位店主撤回簽名，說自己承擔不起無法進出店面的風險。雨蘭接受並更新數字。她漸漸明白信任如何流失：並非只在戲劇性的一刻，而是人們帶回家的小小疑慮。母親洗著晚餐碗盤，說迅速退錢比長篇演說更有說服力。雨蘭把這句話寫在當日帳旁。她仍希望大家選擇戲院；更重要的是，她希望選擇建立在每個人都能親自查證的事實上。

Mei asked why Yulan spent so much effort answering a leaflet that could simply be thrown away. "Because people read it," Yulan replied. "And some people believed it." She had learned in advertising that a repeated sentence could sound true even without evidence. Now she used the same knowledge to correct rather than decorate a claim. She made the notice short enough to read while waiting for noodles and clear enough to check at the clerk's office. She hoped that would be enough.

廖梅問，雨蘭為什麼花這麼多力氣回應一張可以直接丟掉的傳單。「因為有人讀過，」雨蘭說，「而且有人相信。」她在廣告業學過，重複的句子即使沒有證據也可能聽起來像真的。如今她用相同知識來更正，而不是美化說法。她把公告寫得短到等麵時就能讀完，也清楚到可以去鎮公所查證。她希望這樣已經足夠。
