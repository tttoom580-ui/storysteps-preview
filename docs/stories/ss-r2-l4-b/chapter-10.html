屋頂不是承諾

A Roof, Not a Promise

1998，海禾戲院不打烊 · 第 10 / 25 章 · 3000–4000 · 英中對照 · 校訂中

用互動版查詞、朗讀與收藏

Xu Jian requested a meeting and began with an apology for his assistant's misleading leaflet. He did not ask Yulan to forget it. "I can discipline my own office," he said, "but I cannot guarantee what every supporter says." She accepted the apology as a beginning and asked him to provide the correct construction plan in writing. Their conversation became a bargain over information, not ownership. Outside, the old medical clinic was holding a first-aid class, and a student appeared with a bandage around his wrist. The town needed more than beautiful memories: safe streets, reliable services, and ways for ordinary people to solve problems together.

許健要求會談，先為助理誤導人的傳單道歉。他沒有要求雨蘭忘記這件事。「我能約束自己的辦公室，」他說，「卻無法保證每個支持者說什麼。」雨蘭把道歉當成開始，請他書面提供正確的施工方案。兩人的談話變成資訊交換，而非所有權交易。戶外，舊診所正開急救課，一位學生手腕上纏著繃帶。小鎮需要的不只有美好回憶，也需要安全的街道、可靠的服務，以及一般人共同解決問題的方法。

An elderly historian came to review the exhibition files. He had spent decades studying how civilization had changed along the coast. He brought an old newsreel of a factory explosion and explained why the town had once forbidden large gatherings inside unsafe buildings. "People forget what rules were written for," he said. Yulan asked him to illustrate the history on a timeline. It showed the theater being used as a temporary shelter after a storm, then closing a decade later when funds disappeared. The film also recorded settlers building the first market near a family tomb, a place now separated from the main road by shops. Nothing about the town's past was simple enough for a single poster.

一位年長歷史學者前來檢查展覽資料。他多年研究海岸邊的文明變遷，帶來一卷舊新聞片，記錄早年的工廠爆炸。他說明當年鎮上為何禁止人在不安全建築裡大量聚集。「人們會忘記規定原本是為了什麼而寫，」他說。雨蘭請他把歷史畫成時間線，呈現戲院曾在風災後用作臨時避難所，十多年後又因經費消失而關閉。影片也記錄早期移居者在家族墓地附近建立市場，如今墓地已被商店與主要道路隔開。小鎮的歷史從來無法用一張海報說完。

The engineer finally sent a written estimate. The roof repair was critical and expensive, but it could be completed without replacing the whole building. A qualified crew would need to install stronger supports and inspect the exit. The committee's resolution allowed a limited grant application if residents supplied matching funds. Yulan read the document three times. "It is a mere chance," she told Qin, "not permission to open." Mei congratulated her on reaching this point, but reminded her that a prosperous year was still only a hope. The women drew up a fundraising target and made it public. No one would be asked to sign a blank agreement, and no repair would begin before a certified contractor accepted the work.

工程師終於送來書面估價。屋頂修繕既重要又昂貴，但不必拆掉整棟建築也能完成。合格工班需要安裝更強的支撐件，並檢查出口。委員會的決議允許居民在自籌相對經費後，申請有限額的補助。雨蘭讀了三遍文件。「這只是一次機會，」她對秦姨說，「不是開門許可。」廖梅恭喜她走到這一步，卻提醒繁榮的一年仍只是希望。她們公布募款目標，聲明不會讓任何人簽空白同意書，也不會在合格承包商接案前動工。

A pregnant visitor brought a video interview with her father, a retired surgeon who had treated injured workers after the factory accident. She had always been suspicious of public displays that turned suffering into spectacle. Yulan promised the recording would remain private unless the family approved a particular excerpt. "There are histories that teach without being shown," she said. The visitor thanked her and offered to help with the public archive later. At the school computer room, students created a simple website describing the project. It worked over a slow telephone connection and contained only public facts. The three hundred required signatures would still have to be collected on paper from verified local residents; a number on a screen could not replace them.

一位懷孕的訪客帶來父親的訪談錄影。她父親是退休外科醫師，曾在工廠事故後治療受傷工人。她一向懷疑把苦難變成展覽的做法。雨蘭承諾，除非家屬核准特定片段，否則錄影保持私人狀態。「有些歷史即使不公開，也能教我們事情。」她說。訪客道謝，表示日後願協助公共檔案整理。學校電腦教室裡，學生架了簡單的網站，透過緩慢電話連線顯示計畫的公開事實。規定的三百份簽名仍須由確認身分的在地居民紙本簽署；螢幕上的數字不能取代它們。

By the end of the week, the repair fund had enough confirmed promises for the first roof contract, although not enough for the entire project. One hundred and ninety-eight residents had signed the hearing petition. Yulan placed the figures beside the competing parking plan so visitors could compare them. She expected Xu to challenge the numbers, but he simply asked for a copy. The town had not reached a revolution or a miracle. It had reached a practical point: a professional inspection, an open budget, and a limited repair that could begin lawfully. Qin stood on the pavement watching the work crew measure the entrance. "Perhaps," she said, "we have finally stopped asking the building to save itself."

週末時，修繕基金已得到足以支付第一筆屋頂工程的確定認捐，但仍不足以完成整體計畫。一百九十八位居民簽署了公聽會連署。雨蘭把數字放在停車場方案旁，供訪客比較。她以為許健會挑戰數字，他卻只是要了一份副本。小鎮沒有迎來革命或奇蹟，只走到一個實際的關口：專業檢查、公開預算，以及可以合法開始的有限修繕。秦姨站在人行道上，看著工班丈量入口。「也許，」她說，「我們終於不再要求這棟建築自己拯救自己了。」

The clerk explained that the town's rules forbid opening any public hall before the final occupancy inspection, even if the roof already looked strong. Yulan copied the sentence into the volunteers' handbook. Then she posted the current fundraising total beside the signature count. Her mother's stall was closing when Qin came by with a bag of clean rags for the workers. They were ordinary supplies for an ordinary repair. To Yulan, that made the day more hopeful than any announcement of immediate victory.

職員解釋，鎮上的規則禁止任何公共集會廳在最終使用檢查前開放，即使屋頂看起來已很堅固。雨蘭把這句話抄進志工手冊，再把最新募款總額貼在簽名數旁。廖梅快收攤時，秦姨帶來一袋乾淨抹布給工人。這只是普通修繕的普通用品，卻比任何立即勝利的宣告更讓雨蘭有希望。
