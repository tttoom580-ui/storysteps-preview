銀手鐲

The Silver Bracelet

1998，海禾戲院不打烊 · 第 12 / 25 章 · 3000–4000 · 英中對照 · 校訂中

The problem began with a silver bracelet. Qin discovered it inside a bin of old costumes, along with a torn note accusing a former cashier of being a burglar. An outside agent working for a film collector offered to buy the entire box and promised to say nothing about the accusation. Yulan refused to comment before she knew the facts. "Buying a thing does not mean buying somebody's reputation," she said. The collector's letter asked them to commit to a sale immediately, but the theater had no definite record of ownership. Wen recommended a simple procedure: photograph the objects, record where they had been found, and contact the people named in the note.

事情從一只銀手鐲開始。秦姨在舊戲服箱裡發現手鐲，旁邊還有一張破紙條，指控從前某位出納是小偷。一位替電影收藏家工作的代理人想買下整箱物件，並承諾不提指控。雨蘭在查清事實前拒絕評論。「買下一件東西，不等於買下一個人的名譽。」她說。收藏家的信要求立即承諾出售，但戲院根本沒有明確的所有權紀錄。沈文建議採用簡單程序：拍照、記錄發現地點，並聯絡紙條提到的人。

A retired seamstress arrived with surprising information. She had helped make the costumes in an earlier era and recognized the bracelet as a stage prop. It was durable enough to survive rough handling, but it was not silver. The supposed burglar had been her younger brother, who had carried props home for repair. He had later gained local fame as a stage builder, yet the rumor had followed him through years of hardship. Yulan asked whether the family wanted the note displayed as an example of unjust suspicion. The woman shook her head. "I came to correct a story," she said. "I did not come to make my brother's pain a public attraction."

一位退休裁縫帶來驚人的消息。她曾在較早的年代製作戲服，認出手鐲只是舞台道具。它很耐用，能經得起粗手粗腳搬動，卻並非銀製。被當成小偷的人其實是她弟弟，他曾把道具帶回家修理，後來成為當地有名的舞台搭建師，但謠言始終跟隨著他，讓他歷經困苦。雨蘭問家屬是否願意公開紙條，作為錯誤猜疑的例子。婦人搖頭。「我來更正故事，」她說，「不是要把弟弟的痛苦變成公開景點。」

The discovery forced the archive team to reconsider what it meant to preserve a record. A tragic rumor was still part of the past, but repeating it without care could cause fresh destruction. Wen proposed that the note be sealed in a private envelope with a correction attached. The family agreed. At the same meeting, a community elder asked the cinema to pursue permission before using footage of a tribal ceremony filmed decades earlier. The original participants had not all agreed to public distribution. Qin remembered the filming but did not know who still lived nearby. Yulan began a careful search, accepting that some material might remain unseen forever. Ownership was not the same as permission.

這次發現迫使檔案團隊重新思考保存紀錄的意義。悲傷的謠言也是過去的一部分，但不小心重述，可能造成新的破壞。沈文建議把紙條與更正說明一起封入私人信封，家屬同意。同場會議上，一位社區長者要求戲院在使用數十年前拍的部落儀式影像前，先取得同意。當年參與者並未全部同意公開散布。秦姨記得拍攝經過，卻不知道如今還有誰住在附近。雨蘭開始謹慎尋訪，也接受有些素材可能永遠不能公開。擁有物品，不等於擁有公開的許可。

A storm approached that evening. Water began to occupy the shallow drain beside the temporary entrance, and workers moved their tools inside the protected storage room. The contractor worried that another wet week would delay the roof. Yulan did not ask him to continue in unsafe conditions. Instead, she helped prepare waterproof covers, then checked the clear route around the courtyard. Qin stood beside her like a faithful guard, though she looked tired. "When I was young," Qin said, "I thought keeping a theater meant keeping every show running." Yulan answered that protecting people sometimes meant letting a show stop. The older woman held the flashlight while they finished the last check.

傍晚暴風雨逼近。臨時入口旁的淺排水溝開始積水，工人把工具搬進受到保護的儲藏室。承包商擔心連續下雨會拖延屋頂工程。雨蘭沒有要求他在不安全情況下繼續施工，而是幫忙準備防水布，再檢查庭院的安全動線。秦姨像忠誠守衛一樣站在旁邊，雖然顯得疲累。「年輕時，」秦姨說，「我以為守住戲院就是讓每場演出不停。」雨蘭回答，保護人有時正是要讓演出停下來。老人家替她舉著手電筒，完成最後一次檢查。

The rain brought an unexpected gift: time to ask questions. During two closed days, Yulan wrote a policy for donated films and objects. Every item would have an owner, a contact method, a condition report, and an agreed level of access. No one would be asked to surrender personal photographs merely because the theater needed material. Wen checked the language for promises the team could not keep. When the sky cleared, Yulan posted the rules beside the theater door. They were less exciting than an opening announcement, but she believed they would help the cinema remain trustworthy long after the current volunteers had moved away.

雨帶來意外的禮物：提問的時間。兩天停工期間，雨蘭寫下捐贈影片與物件的規則。每項物品都必須標出所有人、聯絡方式、保存狀況，以及同意的使用範圍。沒有人會因為戲院缺素材，就被要求交出私人照片。沈文檢查文字，避免團隊做出辦不到的承諾。天晴後，雨蘭把規則貼在戲院門旁。它們不像開幕公告那麼刺激，她卻相信，等今日的志工都搬走，這些規則仍能讓戲院值得信任。

As the storm clouds began to lift, new questions emerged from the accounts. The price of lumber had increased since the original quotation, and Wen explained that inflation affected even a small restoration project. Yulan updated the public estimate and left the old one attached for comparison. She refused to make the new figure look smaller by removing necessary work. The changes were disappointing, but the board would know what it was being asked to approve. That knowledge was worth preserving beside the films.

風暴雲層開始散去時，帳目冒出新問題。木材價格自原始報價後上漲，沈文解釋通貨膨脹連小型修復計畫都會影響。雨蘭更新公開估價，並把舊版本附在旁邊方便比較。她拒絕藉刪掉必要工程，讓新數字看起來更小。變動令人失望，但董事會將清楚知道自己要核准什麼。這樣的知識同樣值得和影片一起保存。
