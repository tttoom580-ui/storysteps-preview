學校裡的問題

Questions at School

1998，海禾戲院不打烊 · 第 7 / 25 章 · 3000–4000 · 英中對照 · 校訂中

用互動版查詞、朗讀與收藏

The school invited Yulan to speak at an academic fair about the theater's past. She was nervous because she was neither a historian nor an architect. Wen, who was an accountant, suggested she bring original documents and explain only what they showed. She felt admiration for his patience, even when he began to annoy her by checking every date twice. The children had built a small aquarium near the school entrance and filled a basin with seawater to study fish. Their teacher explained that curiosity was a characteristic worth protecting. Yulan wondered whether the cinema could become another room for such questions, where children studied old films without being told what conclusions they must reach.

學校邀請雨蘭到學術成果展介紹戲院的歷史。她很緊張，因為自己既不是歷史學家，也不是建築師。做會計的沈文建議她帶原始文件，只說文件確實能證明的事情。她佩服他的耐心，雖然他連每個日期都要檢查兩遍的習慣有時令她煩躁。孩子們在校門口設了小水族箱，還用盆子盛海水研究魚。老師說，好奇心是值得保護的特質。雨蘭想到，也許戲院能成為另一個讓孩子提問的空間，在那裡研究老電影，而不必被要求接受指定的結論。

A science club compared the chemistry of rust with the weathered beams of the cinema. Their report was not an inspection, but it gave useful background. The teacher made an important contribution by introducing a qualified engineer from the next town. He could visit in three weeks. Xu Jian's team had already announced that its parking plan would defeat any attempt to keep the old building. Yulan refused to turn the disagreement into a contest of loud speeches. "If our plan fails," she told the students, "it should fail because the numbers or the structure cannot support it, not because we were afraid to test them." The answer surprised them, and it surprised her as well.

科學社比較鐵鏽的化學變化與戲院風化的梁柱。報告不能取代正式檢查，卻提供了有用背景。老師又帶來重要貢獻：介紹鄰鎮一位合格工程師，他三週後可以來查看。許健的團隊早已宣稱，停車場計畫會擊敗任何保存老建築的努力。雨蘭不願把分歧變成比誰喊得大聲。「如果計畫失敗，」她對學生說，「應該是因為數字或結構撐不起來，而不是因為我們不敢檢驗。」這句話讓孩子驚訝，也讓她自己意外。

The school principal proposed an expansion of the film program if the theater ever reopened. Students who were fluent in different languages could help translate short interviews with permission. Wen called that a fortunate idea, but reminded them that volunteers needed clear duties. Yulan began looking for relevant lessons: local history, film editing, and the rhythm of spoken stories. A music teacher offered to motivate hesitant students by letting them choose songs from their grandparents' collections. Qin warned that the prime purpose of the project must remain public service, not a hunt for famous names. They agreed that nobody would be pushed to share a private family recording.

校長提議，如果戲院重新開幕，應擴展電影課程。能流利使用不同語言的學生可以在得到許可後，協助翻譯訪談短片。沈文認為這很幸運，但提醒志工必須有清楚職責。雨蘭開始尋找相關課題：地方歷史、影片剪輯與口述故事的節奏。音樂老師願意讓學生挑選祖父母珍藏的歌曲，藉此鼓勵猶豫的孩子。秦姨提醒大家，計畫首要目的必須是公共服務，而不是追逐名人。他們同意，不強迫任何人分享私人家庭錄音。

On the way home, Yulan stopped in the shade of a banyan tree. Xu's assistant was hanging a colorful poster that promised new parking spaces and safer streets. The poster was persuasive because the need was real. She would have to strive for something better than nostalgia. She wrote down three questions for the coming engineer: what repairs were essential, what could wait, and what should not be attempted at all. A passing bicycle bell seemed to waken her from a long dream. People did not need to love the same past to build a useful future together. They only needed a process in which their different choices could be heard.

回家路上，雨蘭停在榕樹的樹蔭下。許健的助理正張貼彩色海報，承諾新停車位與更安全的街道。海報很有說服力，因為需求確實存在。她必須努力提出比懷舊更好的事物。她記下準備問工程師的三個問題：哪些修繕不可少、哪些可以延後、哪些根本不應嘗試。一聲腳踏車鈴彷彿把她從長夢中喚醒。人們不必愛同一個過去，也能一起創造有用的未來。他們只需要一個能讓不同選擇都被聽見的過程。

At supper, Mei showed her daughter a note from the market association. It offered tables and electricity for a second outdoor gathering, but no free labor. Yulan accepted that condition. She also proposed that the next event show both the theater plan and the parking proposal, each with the same space for questions. Mei laughed that the developer might decline such an invitation. "Then people will still see who was willing to answer," Yulan said. She had once imagined winning as making everyone agree with her. The school fair had changed that. A fair discussion might leave people divided, but it could prevent them from building their decisions on fear and rumors.

晚餐時，廖梅拿出市場協會的一張便條。協會願意提供桌子與電力辦第二場戶外活動，但不提供免費勞動。雨蘭接受這個條件。她還提議下一場同時展示戲院計畫與停車場方案，給雙方相同的提問空間。廖梅笑說，開發商可能不願意。「那大家至少會看見誰願意回答，」雨蘭說。她以前以為勝利就是讓所有人同意自己。學校成果展改變了她。公平討論也許仍讓人意見分歧，卻能避免大家根據恐懼和謠言做決定。

The principal asked Yulan whether the school should formally endorse the theater plan. She preferred a more careful answer. "Let the students study both proposals," she said. "Then they can explain which benefits matter to them." The principal accepted the suggestion and offered the classroom after lessons for a small public workshop. Yulan left with a stack of questions instead of a letter of praise. She felt oddly encouraged. A school that taught children how to question its guests was already helping the project.

校長問雨蘭，學校是否該正式支持戲院計畫。她希望回答更謹慎。「讓學生研究兩個方案，」她說，「再解釋哪些好處對他們重要。」校長接受，願意放學後提供教室辦小型公共工作坊。雨蘭帶走的不是讚美信，而是一疊問題。她反而感到鼓舞。願意教孩子質疑受邀來賓的學校，本身就已在幫助計畫。
