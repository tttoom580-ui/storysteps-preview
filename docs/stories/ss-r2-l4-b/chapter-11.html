牆後的水箱

The Hidden Tank

1998，海禾戲院不打烊 · 第 11 / 25 章 · 3000–4000 · 英中對照 · 校訂中

When the roof crew began, the theater filled with the dusty noise of careful construction. Qin asked Yulan to circulate a notice describing the work and its likely consequence for nearby shops: the side lane would be closed for three mornings. A shopkeeper complained that the schedule allowed the contractor too much control. Wen suggested negotiating delivery hours rather than treating the matter as a struggle between a dominant company and powerless neighbors. The discussion was difficult, but the eventual agreement let workers bring materials through the lane before market opening. Yulan made sure every affected shop received the same code number on its copy of the schedule. The people who lived beside a project deserved to know how it would change their days.

屋頂工程開始後，戲院裡滿是謹慎施工的灰塵與聲響。秦姨請雨蘭發放公告，說明施工對附近店家的影響：側巷將連續三個上午封閉。一位店主抱怨工班掌控太多。沈文建議協商送貨時段，而不是把這件事當成強勢公司與無力鄰居的對抗。討論並不容易，但最後達成協議，讓工人在市場開門前由側巷運料。雨蘭確保每家受影響的店都拿到有相同編碼的時間表。住在工程旁的人，有權知道它會怎麼改變自己的日常。

One afternoon, an old newspaper began to circulate with a headline about capitalism and the loss of local theaters. A teenager said the project should rebel against every business owner. Mei replied that her noodle stall was a business too. She had survived bad seasons by serving people faithfully, not by becoming a symbol in somebody else's argument. Wen warned that ideological battles might distract from the eventual decision: could the cinema operate safely, and would enough residents use it? Yulan chose to expand the public ledger to include the contractor's certified payments. The names of individual donors remained private when requested, but the totals were clear. A fair process required more than choosing a side.

某天下午，一份舊報紙在鎮上流傳，標題談資本主義與地方戲院消失。一名少年說這個計畫應該反抗所有企業主。廖梅回答，她的麵攤也是生意。她靠著忠實服務客人度過景氣不好的季節，不是靠當別人辯論裡的象徵。沈文提醒，意識形態爭執可能讓大家偏離最後真正要決定的事：戲院能否安全經營、是否有足夠居民使用？雨蘭決定擴充公開帳簿，加入承包商核准的付款紀錄。要求保密的捐款者姓名不公開，但總額清清楚楚。公平程序不只是選邊站而已。

A school marathon brought new visitors past the building. Qin watched runners pass with graceful steps and remembered the silent films she had once shown about local sports. The repair crew had installed a small solar lamp for the temporary entrance, so people could read the warnings after sunset. A student asked whether a telegraph line had once run through the cinema. Yulan showed him a photograph indicating the old railway office next door, where telegrams had been received. The documents did not prove the theater had used the line itself. She explained the difference between an indication and proof, and the boy wrote it in his notebook. He said history was harder than inventing a good story.

學校馬拉松路線經過戲院，帶來不少新訪客。秦姨看著跑者優雅的步伐，想起以前放過的地方運動默片。工班已在臨時入口裝上小型太陽能燈，讓大家入夜後仍能讀警告。學生問戲院以前是否有電報線。雨蘭拿出照片，指出隔壁舊鐵道辦公室曾收發電報。但文件並不能證明戲院本身用過那條線。她解釋線索與證明的差別，男孩把它寫進筆記，說歷史比編好故事還難。

During the fourth day, workers found a wreck of an old water tank above the back wall. It had been hidden behind a board and was heavier than anyone expected. The contractor stopped work and ordered a new assessment. Yulan feared the cost would rise, but she refused to ask them to remove it without permission. The tank was lifted out safely the following afternoon. A child watching from the legal viewing area asked why everyone looked tired. Qin tickled his arm and told him that adults also became frightened when they did not know what was inside a wall. Yulan wrote the extra cost into the ledger that evening, before anybody could accuse the team of hiding it.

第四天，工人發現後牆上方有一個損壞的舊水箱。它被木板藏住，重量遠超預期。承包商停工，要求重新評估。雨蘭擔心成本增加，卻不願讓工人未經確認就拆除。隔天下午，水箱安全吊離。一名站在合法觀察區的孩子問，為何大家看起來那麼累。秦姨搔搔他的手臂，說成年人不知道牆裡藏了什麼時，也會害怕。當晚，雨蘭立刻把額外成本寫入帳簿，不讓人有理由認為團隊在隱瞞。

The week's progress was smaller than their first schedule predicted. The roof looked better from the street, but its supports were still unfinished. Yulan spent Friday evening arranging the receipts and copies of inspection notes. Wen suggested a public walk around the courtyard on Sunday, where the contractor could explain what remained closed. She agreed, provided that everyone stayed behind the marked line. Her father had once taught her that a movie depended on what the camera did not show as much as what it did. Running a theater, she was discovering, was different. What mattered most was often the difficult work people could not see, and the courage to make that work visible.

這一週進度比原定的少。從街上看屋頂已經改善，內部支撐卻尚未完成。星期五晚上，雨蘭整理收據與檢查紀錄副本。沈文建議星期日在庭院辦公開導覽，請承包商說明哪些區域仍封閉。她同意，但要求所有人都待在標示線後。父親曾教過她，電影仰賴鏡頭沒拍到的東西，和拍到的同樣重要。如今她發現經營戲院不同：真正重要的常是觀眾看不見的艱難工作，以及願意讓這些工作被看見的勇氣。

A local bird club asked to show a short film about how sea birds breed on the nearby cliffs. Their request reminded Yulan that even a beautiful nature film could disturb animals if people treated it as an invitation to visit nests. The teacher discussed preventing a human invasion of the nesting area. His calm persuasion won more support than dramatic warnings. Yulan added the film to the future program list, marked "permission and safety review required," and smiled at how familiar that phrase had become.

在地賞鳥社想放一部短片，介紹海鳥如何在附近海崖繁殖。這使雨蘭想到，即使美麗的自然片，也可能讓人誤以為可以隨便靠近鳥巢。老師談到如何避免人類侵入築巢區。平靜的勸說比戲劇化警告更有說服力。雨蘭把影片加入未來節目清單，標上「需審查授權與安全」。她笑著發現這句話已經多麼熟悉。
