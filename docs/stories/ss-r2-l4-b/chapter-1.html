還能打開的門

The Door Still Opens

1998，海禾戲院不打烊 · 第 1 / 25 章 · 3000–4000 · 英中對照

用互動版查詞、朗讀與收藏

The first thing Chen Yulan noticed was that the old theater still smelled of oranges and rain. She had returned to the coastal town of Haihe in April 1998, carrying one suitcase and a cardboard box of drawings. Outside the cinema, a narrow street led toward her grandmother's empty cottage. Inside, torn red seats had lost their cushion stuffing, and a notice on the door announced that the building might be replaced by a parking lot. Yulan wanted to convey more than anger when she met the committee. Her defense would have to explain why a small cinema mattered to people with very different lives. The audience's diversity had once filled this room with laughter, arguments, and songs.

陳雨蘭第一個注意到的，是老戲院仍有橘子與雨水的氣味。1998年四月，她帶著一只行李箱和一箱畫稿，回到海邊小鎮海禾。戲院外的窄街通往祖母留下的空屋。戲院裡，破損的紅椅子露出椅墊填充物，門上的公告則說，這棟建築可能被停車場取代。雨蘭明白，向委員會表達的不能只有憤怒。她必須說清楚，小戲院為何對不同生活背景的人都重要。當年多元的觀眾曾讓這裡充滿笑聲、爭論與歌聲。

A fierce wind pushed open a side door. Aunt Qin, the former ticket seller, appeared with a bucket beneath one arm. "Don't flatter me with a promise to save everything," she said. "First find out why the roof leaks." As a girl, Yulan had thought Qin a genius for remembering every customer's favorite seat. Today a young boy giggled at a poster showing an actor descending by parachute. He was not too young to notice the broken ceiling. Qin looked pessimistic, yet she handed Yulan the basement key. "You may look," she said, "but you must not pretend that looking is the same as repairing." Yulan accepted the warning instead of arguing.

一陣猛烈的風推開側門。從前的售票員秦姨抱著水桶出現。「別用保證能救下一切的話來奉承我，」她說，「先查清楚屋頂為何漏水。」小時候，雨蘭總覺得秦姨是天才，因為她記得每位客人最愛的座位。一個小男孩對著海報咯咯笑，海報上的演員正乘降落傘下降。他年紀雖小，也看得出天花板壞了。秦姨神情悲觀，卻把地下室鑰匙交給雨蘭。「妳可以查看，」她說，「但別把查看當成修好了。」雨蘭沒有爭辯，只記下這句警告。

The town did not possess enough money to repair every old building. That fact was written plainly in the committee's presentation, which Yulan had collected from the public library. Yet the same pages named the theater as a possible community center. She marked the sentence as a reference for the hearing. On the projection-room wall hung photographs of weddings, memorial films, and harvest festivals. The place had carried a spiritual meaning for some neighbors, although others simply wanted inexpensive entertainment. Preserving it would demand a tremendous amount of work. Yulan had no wish to make a sacred monument that nobody could afford to enter. She wanted a working room with ordinary people inside it.

鎮上沒有足夠的錢修好每一棟老建築，這是雨蘭從公共圖書館取得的委員會簡報中明白寫出的事實。但同一份資料也將戲院列為可能的社區中心。她把這句話標成公聽會要用的參考依據。放映室牆上掛著婚禮、追思影片與豐收節的照片。對某些鄰居而言，此地帶著精神寄託；對另一些人來說，它只是便宜的娛樂場所。保留下來需要投入極大的心力。雨蘭不想把戲院變成沒人負擔得起的神聖紀念碑，她想要的是一個真正運作、能容納普通人的空間。

On the second floor, she found a paper report stating that the emergency exit had been locked. If true, it was a serious violation of the town's safety conditions. But the report included no photograph or signature. Yulan took a visual record with her old film camera and wrote down the time. She asked Qin to witness the inspection rather than merely trust her memory. Then they walked through the narrow exit together. It was dusty, but the handle opened. After climbing the stairs twice, both women laughed that the inspection had become an unexpected workout. The missing signature mattered more than their discovery: an open door today did not prove it had been open when the inspector visited.

在二樓，雨蘭找到一份紙本報告，上面寫著緊急出口曾被上鎖。若屬實，便是嚴重違反鎮上安全條件的行為。但報告沒有照片，也沒有簽名。雨蘭用舊底片相機留下影像紀錄，並記下時間。她請秦姨共同見證檢查，而不是只相信自己的記憶。接著，兩人穿過狹窄的出口。那裡滿是灰塵，門把卻轉得開。她們上下樓梯兩趟，都笑說這次檢查成了意外的運動。不過，比起門能打開，報告缺少簽名更重要：今天門是開的，並不能證明稽查當天也是如此。

At dusk, Yulan carried her notes to the noodle stall where her mother, Liao Mei, was cutting green onions. Mei did not celebrate her daughter's return. "The theater was your father's dream," she said. "I want to know what yours is." Yulan answered that she had spent nine years drawing advertisements in Taipei and had forgotten how to choose her own work. She would not ask Mei to mortgage the stall, and she would not ask Qin to work without pay. Tomorrow she would visit the town clerk to learn the rules: three hundred local signatures, a qualified safety inspection, and a workable first-year budget before any new lease could be considered. The public hearing was in ninety days. For the first time since coming home, she knew what to do in the morning.

黃昏時，雨蘭帶著筆記走到母親廖梅的麵攤。廖梅正在切蔥，並沒有因女兒回來而慶祝。「戲院是妳父親的夢，」她說，「我想知道妳自己的夢是什麼。」雨蘭說，她在台北畫了九年的廣告，幾乎忘了如何選擇自己的工作。她不會要求母親抵押麵攤，也不會讓秦姨免費工作。明天，她要向鎮公所職員問清楚規定：三百名在地居民的簽名、合格的安全檢查，以及可行的第一年預算，三項齊全才能考慮新租約。公聽會在九十天後舉行。回家以來，她第一次知道隔天早上該做什麼。

Later, Yulan found the old cinema's opening-day photograph in a wooden drawer. Her father stood at the far edge, carrying a ladder rather than smiling for the camera. She had spent years imagining him as the person who held the place together. In the photograph, though, the room was full of workers, neighbors, and children. She put the image beside her new notebook. Perhaps what she missed was not his authority, but the ordinary way everyone had once been allowed to help.

後來，雨蘭在木抽屜裡找到戲院開幕當日的照片。父親站在最邊緣，扛著梯子，沒有對鏡頭微笑。多年來她一直想像是父親獨自撐起這個地方，但照片裡還有工人、鄰居和孩子。她把照片放在新筆記本旁。也許她懷念的不是他的權威，而是當年每個人都能伸手幫忙的平凡方式。
