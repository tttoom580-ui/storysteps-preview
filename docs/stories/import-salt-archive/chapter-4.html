沒有死亡證明的房客

The Tenant Without a Death Certificate

灰潮檔案 · 第 4 / 25 章 · 5000–6000 · 英中對照

用互動版查詞、朗讀與收藏

They hid overnight in a house painted blue, where a seagull had built its nest above a broken rainpipe. The landlady, Mrs. Iven, accepted payment in medicine rather than coins. Her rooms smelled of bleach, but the kitchen was warm and the garden unexpectedly lush. She offered them wholesome soup and placed a thermometer beside Jory, whose fever had risen after the harbor escape. Lena asked whether the woman knew anything about Island Nine. Mrs. Iven paused beside her dresser. "I used to supervise transports from that island," she said. "My son worked there as a superintendent. He died before he could tell me why he wanted to resign." She did not mourn him with speeches; she polished the same drawer handle again and again until the brass gleamed. Lena recognized grief disguised as work. She had spent twelve years arranging other people's records because she could not arrange her own sorrow.

三人在一幢漆成藍色的房子裡過夜。破損的雨管上方，一隻海鷗築了巢。房東伊文太太收藥品，不收硬幣。屋內有漂白水的氣味，廚房卻很溫暖，花園也意外地繁茂。她端來營養均衡的熱湯，又在喬里旁邊放了溫度計；港口逃亡之後，他的高燒愈來愈嚴重。莉娜問伊文太太是否知道第九島。女人停在梳妝櫃旁。「我以前監督那座島的運輸，」她說，「兒子在那裡當總管理員。他死前一直想辭職，卻來不及告訴我原因。」她不靠言語哀悼，只是一再擦拭同一個抽屜把手，直到黃銅閃閃發亮。莉娜認出那種以工作掩飾的悲痛。十二年來，她不停整理別人的檔案，因為無法理清自己的傷心。

At midnight, Lena overheard Mrs. Iven arguing with someone in the courtyard. "The children are not evidence to be burned," she said. A man's voice answered that public disorder would cause water systems to overflow and hospitals to fail. The exchange made Lena suspect that the landlady had reported their arrival. She confronted her in the morning, but Mrs. Iven did not deny the conversation. "My son left me a comprehensive list of every transfer," she explained. "A council officer wants me to surrender it, because once the list becomes public the entire hierarchy will collapse." Lena demanded to see it. "You must first decide what you're willing to protect," the woman replied. The words angered Lena, who had risked her life for facts. Yet she remembered the children on the ship. Telling their names to an excited crowd might expose them to new danger.

午夜時，莉娜偷聽到伊文太太在庭院和人爭執。「孩子不是能燒毀的證據。」她說。男人回應，公開動亂會使供水系統溢流，醫院也將癱瘓。這段對話讓莉娜懷疑房東通報了他們的行蹤。隔天早上她當面質問，伊文太太卻沒有否認。「兒子留下一份完整的轉送名單，」她解釋，「議會官員要我交出去，因為名單一旦公開，整個權力階層就會崩潰。」莉娜要求查看。「妳得先決定願意保護什麼。」房東回答。這句話讓冒著性命危險追查真相的莉娜很生氣。但她想起船上的孩子：若把他們的姓名直接告訴激動的人群，可能讓他們再次陷入危險。

Mrs. Iven produced a packet of letters and a recording of her son's final conversation. Behind his tired voice, a faint symphony played on a broken radio. The recording was credible because it contained details that no stranger could have invented: the scar above his eyebrow, the smell of the pier, his childhood fear of thunder. He had described an effort to nurture the children in secret after the experiments were abandoned. The council had ordered every remaining patient classified as dead, while island nurses kept them alive using supplies smuggled out of official stores. It was a tangled story of cruelty and dedication, not a simple battle between monsters and heroes. Lena felt a painful familiarity in the son's hesitation. He had delayed exposing the system because the people he wanted to rescue would be the first to suffer from publicity.

伊文太太拿出一包信件，以及兒子最後一次談話的錄音。疲憊的人聲背後，破舊收音機正播放一段微弱的交響樂。錄音可信，因為裡面提及陌生人無從捏造的細節：他眉上的傷疤、碼頭的氣味，以及童年時對雷聲的恐懼。他說，實驗被放棄之後，仍有人在暗中照顧孩子。議會命令把所有剩餘病患列為死亡，島上的護理師卻靠著從官方倉庫偷運的物資，努力讓他們活下去。這是一則糾纏著殘酷與奉獻的故事，不是怪物與英雄簡單分明的戰爭。莉娜從那兒子的遲疑裡感到熟悉的痛楚。他拖延揭露體系的原因，是擔心最先遭殃的，正是自己想拯救的人。

The council officer returned before noon and demanded the letters. Lena stepped out to face him. Rather than refute his concerns about public turmoil, she offered a narrow bargain: the originals would go to an independent medical team, while the council could receive copies with patient names concealed until their safety was assured. The officer called the plan unrealistic. Mrs. Iven surprised them both by placing the letters in Lena's hands. "My son wanted someone to make a decision that I could not," she said. Outside, a young girl watered seedlings beneath the gull's nest. Life kept growing within sight of the city's cruelty. Lena finally understood that the truth did not belong to her grief alone. She would carry the letters to the island, but she would let the survivors decide which parts of their stories could be told. The children had trusted adults before and suffered for it. Any promise of protection would need witnesses, a right to refuse, and rules that could not be quietly altered after the meeting ended.

議會官員在中午前返回，要求交出信件。莉娜走出去面對他。她沒有否認公開真相可能引發動亂的疑慮，而是提出一項有限的交換條件：正本交給獨立醫療團隊，議會可取得複本，但在確保病患安全前必須遮去姓名。官員說這計畫不切實際。伊文太太卻出乎兩人意料，把信件放進莉娜手中。「我兒子希望有人做出我一直不敢做的決定。」她說。屋外，一名小女孩正在海鷗巢下替幼苗澆水。即使在城市的殘酷陰影裡，生命仍不斷生長。莉娜終於明白，真相並不只屬於她的悲傷。她會把信帶到島上，但要讓倖存者自己決定，哪些故事能被公開。那些孩子曾信任過大人，卻因此受苦。任何保護承諾都必須有見證者、拒絕的權利，以及會後不能私下更改的規則。
