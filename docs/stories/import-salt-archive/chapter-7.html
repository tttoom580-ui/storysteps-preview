山崩之前

Before the Mountain Fell

灰潮檔案 · 第 7 / 25 章 · 5000–6000 · 英中對照 · 校訂中

The search for an independent doctor took them to a hill district built on five hundred acres of unstable ground. At the intersection, a woodpecker hammered an old tree above a boxing club. Its owner, a cheerful gay man named Sol, had once been an emigrant from the northern border. He trained children for free and kept a small library beside the ring. "I was a bachelor when I came here," he told Lena, "and I thought living alone meant being free of responsibility. The children cured me of that idea." Sol had heard that the council intended to announce a travel prohibition between the hills and the coast. He was preparing provisional medical passes in case the order arrived. Lena admired his energy but worried that the passes were illegal. "A law that prevents injured children from seeing a doctor has already forsaken its purpose," Sol said. "The question is what we do when the paperwork catches up with us."

為替喬里妹妹尋找獨立醫師，他們來到一處建在五百英畝不穩定地面的山區。交叉路口旁，一隻啄木鳥敲著拳擊俱樂部上方的老樹。俱樂部老闆索爾是位開朗的同志男性，過去曾從北方邊境移居此地。他免費訓練孩子，拳台旁還擺著一座小圖書館。「剛到這裡時我是單身漢，」他告訴莉娜，「我以為獨居就不用負責任。孩子們治好了我這個想法。」索爾聽說議會打算宣布山區與海岸間的旅行禁令，便預先準備臨時醫療通行證，以防命令真的發布。莉娜佩服他的幹勁，卻擔心通行證不合法。「一條阻止受傷孩子看醫生的法律，早就背離了自己的目的，」索爾說，「問題是等公文真的來了，我們要怎麼辦。」

A farmer interrupted them with news of a landslide above the reservoir. The earth had begun to move after three unusually dry weeks. At first Lena thought the water shortage had caused it, but Sera pointed out a pattern: the council had diverted the old irrigation stream and blocked the drainage channels. Without nourishment, the hillside plants had died, leaving the soil bare. Its fertility was gone, and nothing remained to bind the slope. "Do not equate a natural disaster with an unavoidable disaster," Sera said. They climbed toward the damaged terraces, where residents worked to move families away from the cliffs. A young inspector tried to issue disciplinary notices to workers who used unauthorized equipment. Lena asked him whether he would rather preserve his authority or the people beneath the slope. His confidence began to falter when another crack opened across the road. He put away his notebook and helped carry a child.

一名農夫帶著水庫上方山崩的消息打斷談話。連續三週異常乾燥之後，山體開始滑動。莉娜起初以為是缺水造成，瑟拉卻指出一個規律：議會改道了舊灌溉溪流，還封住排水溝。失去水分滋養，山坡植物逐漸死亡，裸露的土壤失去了肥力，已沒有東西能固定山體。「別把天災等同於不可避免的災難。」瑟拉說。他們攀向受損梯田，居民正努力把家庭移離斷崖。一名年輕督察試圖對使用未授權設備的工人開出紀律處分。莉娜問他，究竟要保存自己的權威，還是救出斜坡下的人。第二道裂縫突然橫過道路，他的自信開始動搖。他收起筆記本，幫忙抱走一個孩子。

At an emergency meeting, the villagers rejected an orthodox engineering proposal to seal every channel with concrete. Sol argued that the immediate solution would trap future rainwater above the homes. The alternative was to clear the older streams, guided by maps kept by Sera's grandmother. Some officials claimed the maps were too old to trust. Sera asked everyone to inspect them beside the present terrain. The evidence was clear enough that the assembly reached a unanimous decision to open the channels. It was a modest victory against the vanity of experts who feared admitting they were wrong. Within an hour, workers had dug a trench that carried the first surge of water away from the houses. They were still in danger, but their action had bought them time. Lena felt a little of the gloom lift. Ordinary people had solved a problem the council had insisted only its engineers could manage. Sol refused to forsake the families below the slope simply because an old map was difficult to read. He asked Sera to explain its marks beside the current drainage plan.

緊急會議上，村民否決了把所有水道用水泥封死的正統工程方案。索爾指出，眼前的辦法反而會在未來下雨時，把水困在屋舍上方。替代方案是依瑟拉祖母留下的地圖，疏通更古老的溪流。有官員聲稱地圖年代太久，不可採信。瑟拉要大家把圖拿到實地逐項核對。證據清楚到足以讓議會一致決議疏通水道。這是對那些害怕承認錯誤的專家虛榮心，一次不大的勝利。一小時內，工人挖出的新溝便把第一股水導離屋舍。危險尚未解除，但這次行動爭取到時間。莉娜感覺陰鬱稍微散去。平凡居民解決了議會一再宣稱只有自家工程師能處理的問題。索爾不肯只因舊地圖難讀，就放棄山坡下的家庭。他請瑟拉把舊圖放在現行排水圖旁，說明上面的記號。

A second tremor shook the road at dusk. This time a retaining wall collapsed beside the clinic, burying the medicine shed. Sol's students formed a chain and passed rocks away from the entrance. Lena joined them until her injured leg failed and Sera ordered her to rest. Then she saw Jory emerging from the clinic with a small box of emergency medicine. He had arranged transport for his sister through Sol's network. The treatment would begin the following morning, provided the mountain road remained open. Jory took Lena's hand and apologized for nearly accepting Havel's bargain. "I wanted to be the sort of friend who never hesitates," he said. "You were afraid," Lena replied. "That doesn't erase what you've done." Below them, workers raised a temporary barrier against the moving earth. It was provisional, fragile, and the most hopeful thing Lena had seen since receiving her mother's photograph.

黃昏時第二次震動搖撼道路。這回診所旁的擋土牆倒塌，掩埋了藥品棚。索爾的學生排成一列，將石頭一塊塊傳出入口。莉娜加入他們，直到舊傷腿撐不住，瑟拉命她休息。這時她看見喬里抱著一小盒急救藥品走出診所。他已透過索爾的網絡替妹妹安排運送。只要山路還能通行，隔天早上就能開始治療。喬里握住莉娜的手，為自己差點接受哈維爾的交易道歉。「我想當一個永遠不會猶豫的朋友，」他說。「你只是害怕，」莉娜回答，「那不會抹去你之前做過的好事。」下方，工人正在移動的土石前豎起臨時屏障。它不穩、脆弱，卻是莉娜收到母親照片以來最有希望的一樣東西。
