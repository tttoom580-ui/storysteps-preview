海上的公投

The Vote at Sea

灰潮檔案 · 第 5 / 25 章 · 5000–6000 · 英中對照

用互動版查詞、朗讀與收藏

At dawn, the group boarded a fishing vessel from the offshore settlement. Its captain, a former academy teacher named Vale, had been accused of stealing fertilizer from government warehouses. He explained that the goods had actually been intended for a damaged fishery whose owner was too poor to qualify for aid. "An accusation travels faster than a repair," he said, steering around floating debris. The deck creaked, and Jory slipped as the boat turned. Sera caught his harness before he fell into the water. The sudden strain reopened an old fracture in his wrist. She secured it with a strip of cloth while Lena checked the cargo. Alongside medical supplies were a pair of headphones, a packet of letters, and a sealed box labeled INTAKE. Inside she found records of the children who had been taken to Island Nine, including a file for her mother.

天亮時，三人搭上一艘來自外海聚落的漁船。船長瓦爾曾是學院教師，後來被控偷走政府倉庫的肥料。他解釋，物資原本就是要送給一處受損的漁場，只因漁場主人太窮，無法符合援助資格。「指控傳得永遠比修理快。」他一邊繞開漂流物一邊說。甲板嘎吱作響，船一轉彎，喬里就滑了一跤。瑟拉抓住他的安全吊帶，才沒讓他掉進海裡。猛然拉扯讓他手腕的舊骨折再度裂傷。瑟拉用布條固定，莉娜則去清點貨物。醫療用品旁放著耳機、一包信件，還有標著「收容登記」的密封盒。裡面是被送往第九島的孩子紀錄，其中竟也有母親的檔案。

The island looked serene from a distance, surrounded by pale water and black rocks. At the dock, however, prospective patients and frightened tenants waited beside a crowded clinic. Many had never seen a doctor outside the government system. A nurse welcomed them to a banquet of rice, preserved fruit, and soup, prepared in honor of the first visitors from the mainland in years. It was a modest feast, yet the children rejoiced as though a royal ship had arrived. Lena could not enjoy it. She was reading the intake records, trying to refine her understanding of the transfers. Her mother's name appeared under a maiden surname Lena had never known. The file described a senior physician who had volunteered to oversee care after protesting the original experiment. "She isn't listed as a patient," Lena told Sera. "She's listed as the person responsible for keeping them alive."

遠看第九島十分寧靜，淡色海水與黑岩圍繞著它。然而碼頭上，候診者與神情緊張的房客擠在診所旁等候。許多人從未在政府體制外看過醫生。一名護理師招待他們吃米飯、醃水果與湯，說是多年來第一批大陸訪客抵達而特地舉辦的宴席。那不過是簡陋的一餐，孩子們卻像迎接王室船艦般欣喜。莉娜吃不下，正埋首收容紀錄，試圖更精確理解轉送過程。母親的姓名出現在一個莉娜從未聽過的娘家姓氏之下。檔案記載，一名資深醫師在抗議原始實驗之後，自願留下負責照護。「她不是病患，」莉娜告訴瑟拉，「她是負責讓他們活下來的人。」

The clinic director invited Lena into a small chapel, where residents were debating whether the mainland council should ever be allowed to govern them again. Some wished to hold a referendum. Others said a public vote would invite soldiers before the island was ready. An elderly tenant delivered a stern sermon about loyalty, while a young nurse objected that obedience had nearly killed them. The disagreement was not merely political; each speaker had lost someone, and each feared losing more. Lena listened until a woman with silver hair rose from the back row. She moved with a cane and carried herself as if every step had to be chosen. Lena recognized her mother's hands before she recognized her face. "I hoped you would never have to come," the woman said. Lena wanted to run to her, but an old resentment stopped her feet. "You let me think you were dead."

診所主任邀請莉娜進一間小教堂。居民正辯論，是否還該讓大陸議會統治他們。有人想辦公投，也有人認為現在投票只會招來士兵，因為島上尚未準備好。一位老房客發表嚴厲的忠誠布道，年輕護理師則反駁，正是服從險些害死大家。分歧不只關乎政治：每個發言者都失去過重要的人，也都害怕再次失去。莉娜聽到後排有位銀髮女子站起。她拄著拐杖，每踏一步都像經過慎重選擇。莉娜先認出了母親的手，才認出那張臉。「我一直希望妳不必來這裡。」女人說。莉娜想奔過去，積壓已久的怨恨卻讓雙腳動彈不得。「妳讓我以為妳已經死了。」

Her mother did not ask for forgiveness. She explained that the council had threatened to return the children to the experimental wards if she exposed their location. Years earlier she had helped design the treatment, believing it could prevent suffering; when she learned how patients were selected, she tried to shut it down. The guilt had never left her. "I thought hiding here was the only way to protect them," she said. "Then I convinced myself it was also protecting you." Lena looked at the people preparing ballot papers in the next room. The decision could not belong to her mother alone. They agreed to shuffle the discussion into smaller groups, so every patient could speak without being overwhelmed by powerful adults. At sunset, the residents voted to contact independent physicians before seeking a wider referendum. Lena did not embrace her mother yet. She sat beside her and listened, because reconciliation required more than the miracle of finding someone alive.

母親沒有要求原諒。她解釋，議會曾威脅：若她洩露孩子們的所在，就把他們送回實驗病房。多年前她參與設計療程，原以為能減輕痛苦；發現病患如何被挑選後，才試圖終止計畫。那份罪惡感從未消失。「我以為躲在這裡是保護他們唯一的方法，」她說，「後來我還說服自己，這也能保護妳。」莉娜望向隔壁房間，居民正準備選票。這個決定不能只由母親做主。大家同意把大型辯論拆成小組，讓每名病患都能不受強勢大人壓迫地發言。日落時，島民投票決定先聯絡獨立醫師，再尋求更大範圍的公投。莉娜尚未擁抱母親。她只坐在母親身旁聽著，因為和解需要的，遠不只是發現某個人仍活著的奇蹟。
