水壩上的假日

The Day the Dam Lied

灰潮檔案 · 第 10 / 25 章 · 5000–6000 · 英中對照 · 校訂完成

用互動版查詞、朗讀與收藏

The survey teams reached the reservoir before sunrise. The dam rose in cold splendor above the valley, its smooth walls concealing decades of repairs. Lena's group entered through a service door that required a screwdriver to release the rusted latch. A nervous young jockey, formerly employed to train horses for patrol units, had volunteered as their guide. He spoke with a slight stutter whenever frightened, but he knew how to navigate the narrow paths along the water. "The guards use the horses here because engines make too much noise," he explained. Jory carried the sample containers; Sera watched the waterline for changes. Lena noticed a synthetic fragrance near the drainage pipes, a smell she recognized from the island clinic. She had expected mud and algae, not something manufactured to mask another substance.

調查隊在日出前抵達水庫。水壩以冰冷壯麗的姿態矗立山谷之上，平滑牆面藏著數十年的維修痕跡。莉娜一行從維修門進入，用螺絲起子撬開生鏽門閂。嚮導是一名緊張的年輕騎師，過去替巡防隊訓練馬匹。他害怕時說話會微微口吃，卻很熟悉水邊狹窄路徑。「守衛在這裡騎馬，因為引擎聲太吵。」他解釋。喬里提著採樣容器，瑟拉則留意水位變化。莉娜聞到排水管附近有一股合成香料的味道，正是第九島診所曾有的氣味。她原以為只會聞到泥與藻，沒想到竟有某種人造氣味在掩蓋另一種物質。

Inside the control room, a chart listed the amounts of radioactive tracer used during tests of underground water movement. The quantities were low enough for safe laboratory work but should never have entered drinking supplies. Havel had approved a concession allowing a private contractor to handle the material, then subsidized its disposal from public funds. When workers complained that the storage tanks were leaking, the company filed their reports under default maintenance failures. Lena read the sequence aloud, hearing how ordinary words had hidden a dangerous decision. "They knew the tracer was escaping," she said. "And they kept supplying it because stopping would expose the contract." The guide asked whether anyone had been prosecuted. Jory found a blank legal file; it contained only a receipt for one serving of food given to a worker who had reported the leak. The smallness of the compensation was almost unbearable.

控制室裡一張圖表列著地下水流測試使用的放射性追蹤物質數量。用量低到可在實驗室安全操作，卻不該流進飲用水。哈維爾批准特許契約，讓私人公司處理物質，又用公帑補貼廢棄物處置。工人抱怨儲存槽漏水時，公司把報告歸類為「例行維護預設故障」。莉娜朗讀文件，聽見普通字眼如何掩蓋危險決策。「他們知道追蹤物質外洩，」她說，「但停下來就會曝光合約，所以繼續供應。」嚮導問是否有人被起訴。喬里找到一份空白的法律檔案，裡頭只有工人舉報漏水後獲得的一份餐食收據。那份補償的微小，幾乎令人難以承受。

An alarm erupted as the water level began to fall. The reservoir was being deliberately depleted through a lower channel. A cynical guard stepped into the doorway and told Lena that her evidence would be irrelevant once the valley flooded. "The public needs water, not literal explanations of every engineering decision," he said. Lena retorted that people needed both. Behind the guard appeared Havel, carrying an old family photograph. "Your stepfather kept this," he told her. Lena froze. Her mother had remarried during the years Lena believed her dead, and the man in the picture was the contractor who signed the water concession. Havel had known all along. His calm expression made the revelation worse. He had intruded into her family history deliberately, keeping the contractor near enough to influence every decision about water and medicine. He offered to let the team leave if Lena surrendered the sample records and refrained from exposing her family's part in the system. The bargain had been prepared with cruel care.

水位忽然下降，警報猛然響起。有人正故意透過下方水道抽空水庫。一名嘲諷神情的守衛走進門口，說只要山谷遭淹，莉娜的證據便毫無用處。「人民需要水，不需要逐字解釋每個工程決策。」他說。莉娜回敬，人民兩者都需要。哈維爾出現在守衛身後，手裡拿著一張舊全家福。「妳繼父留著這個。」他說。莉娜僵住。母親在她以為人已死去的歲月裡再婚，而照片中的男人，竟是簽下水務特許契約的承包商。哈維爾從頭就知道。那張平靜的臉讓事實更加殘酷。她意識到，哈維爾不是偶然闖入她的家庭歷史，而是故意讓承包商留在身邊，好左右每一項關於水與藥物的決策。他表示，只要莉娜交出樣本紀錄，不揭發自家人在體系裡的角色，就讓整隊人離開。這是一份經過精心設計的殘忍交易。

Lena looked at Jory, then at the falling gauge. There was no time to negotiate. She told Sera to open the emergency overflow and ordered the guide to warn families downstream. The action was spontaneous but not careless; they had rehearsed the procedure with the hill engineers. Water roared through an older channel, sparing the villages directly below the dam. Yet the diversion destroyed the bridge by which the team had arrived. Their morale fell as they realized they were trapped with Havel's guards. Lena could hear distant families shouting in relief, unaware of the danger inside the control house. She had won a narrow victory and paid for it immediately. Havel took her sample case, locked the door, and left them there. "You can keep the truth," he said. "Let's see what it does for you without a way out." For the first time, Lena wondered whether telling the truth might cost not only her own safety but the stability of the communities she had promised to protect. The thought frightened her more than the locked door.

莉娜看著喬里，又看著持續下降的水位指示器。已經沒有談判時間。她命瑟拉打開緊急溢流道，要嚮導去警告下游家庭。行動雖出於當下決斷，卻非毫無準備；他們曾與山區工程師演練流程。水流轟然衝進舊河道，避開水壩正下方的村莊。然而改道也沖毀了他們來時的橋。大家發現自己被哈維爾的守衛困在這裡，士氣頓時低落。遠處傳來逃過洪水的家庭歡呼，沒人知道控制室裡的危險。莉娜取得了一次狹小勝利，卻立刻付出代價。哈維爾帶走樣本箱，鎖上門，將他們留在原地。「真相留給妳，」他說，「我倒要看看，沒有出路的時候，真相能幫妳什麼。」莉娜第一次懷疑，說出真相的代價可能不只是自己的安危，還包括她答應保護的社群能否維持安定。這想法比鎖上的門更令她害怕。
