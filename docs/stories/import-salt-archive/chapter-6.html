被收回的春天

The Spring They Tried to Recall

灰潮檔案 · 第 6 / 25 章 · 5000–6000 · 英中對照 · 校訂中

The island's children greeted morning with a jolly song, but the clinic director did not smile. Parliament had issued a new order that would close independent schools and transfer their pupils to mainland institutions. The order did not allege that the island had committed a crime; it simply called the settlement an inefficient use of resources. A teacher with a splendid mustache showed Lena the extracurricular program he had built: music lessons, basic medicine, and lessons in how to repair fishing nets. Near the school, a sculptor was teaching children to carve faces in discarded driftwood. "They want to beautify the relocation centers," the teacher said bitterly. "They call this place ugly because it has no marble." On the outskirts of the clinic, two girls were learning to mow the grass with a hand tool. They stopped when Lena approached and asked whether the officials would take their garden too. She had no answer she could give without lying.

島上的孩子以歡快歌聲迎接早晨，診所主任卻笑不出來。議會發布新命令，準備關閉獨立學校，將學生送往大陸機構。命令並未指控島民犯罪，只把聚落稱為「沒有效率的資源使用」。一名蓄著漂亮小鬍子的教師帶莉娜參觀自己開辦的課外活動：音樂、基礎醫療，以及修補漁網。學校旁，一位雕塑家正教孩子在漂流木上刻人臉。「他們想美化遷置中心，」教師苦澀地說，「因為這裡沒有大理石，就說這裡醜。」診所外圍，兩名女孩正拿手工具學習割草。她們見莉娜走近，停下來問官員會不會連花園都收走。莉娜無法在不說謊的情況下回答。

Her mother took her to the water purification room, a humid oasis beneath the rocky hill. The filtration machinery had begun to slump into the soft foundation, and repairs required simultaneous work on three heavy valves. A technician extracted a sample from the final tank and weighed an ounce of dark sediment. "The council claims we are contaminating the mainland," he said. "But this arrived in sealed barrels bearing its own seal." He handed Lena a photograph of the shipments. The caption identified the barrels as standard medical waste, though their labels had been removed. Lena recognized a supplier associated with Havel. The evidence was troubling but not sufficient. She asked the technician to preserve a second sample and prepare an independent analysis. "It sounds complicated," her mother said. "It is," Lena replied. "That doesn't mean we should choose a simpler lie."

母親帶莉娜前往淨水室，那是岩丘下潮濕得如綠洲般的空間。過濾設備正因地基鬆軟而下陷，維修必須同時操作三個沉重閥門。技師從最後一座水槽抽取樣本，秤出一盎司的黑色沉積物。「議會說是我們污染了大陸，」他說，「但這些東西是裝在蓋有議會印章的密封桶裡運來的。」他遞給莉娜一張貨運照片。照片說明寫著「標準醫療廢棄物」，但桶上的標籤已被移除。莉娜認出供應商與哈維爾有關。證據令人不安，卻還不夠充分。她要求技師保留第二份樣本，交由獨立單位分析。「聽起來很複雜。」母親說。「是啊，」莉娜回答，「但這不代表我們應該選擇比較簡單的謊言。」

By afternoon, the island assembly had gathered to debate its response. The clinic director advised surrender, claiming that ruthless authorities would punish resistance. The teacher argued for immediate evacuation, while the boat captain wanted to block the harbor. Lena listened as each proposal became a contest of fear. Finally Sera stood. "My grandmother used to nag me about one thing," she said. "Never let someone who owns the water decide whether you deserve to drink." Her words made the room quiet. Lena suggested a less dramatic plan: keep the school open, send the samples to an independent laboratory, and ask every family to record its own position before parliament could speak on its behalf. The plan had the simplicity of an ordinary meeting, but it required people who had been denied a voice to trust that their testimony mattered. No one could promise that such courage would be rewarded.

下午，島民議會集會商討對策。診所主任主張投降，說無情的當局一定會懲罰抵抗者。教師要求立刻撤離，船長則想封鎖港口。莉娜聽著每項提議逐漸變成恐懼的較量。最後瑟拉站了起來。「祖母以前總念我一件事，」她說，「不要讓掌握水源的人，決定妳是否配喝水。」房間安靜下來。莉娜提出一個不那麼戲劇性的計畫：學校保持開放，樣本送交獨立實驗室，每戶人家都先記錄自己的立場，不讓議會代替他們發言。這計畫像平常開會一樣簡單，卻要求長期被剝奪聲音的人，相信自己的證詞有價值。沒有人能保證這份勇氣一定得到回報。

As the meeting approached its climax, a messenger arrived with a packet from Havel. Inside was a photograph of Jory's younger sister in a mainland hospital. The message said she would be denied treatment unless Lena returned with the original archive key. Jory read it twice and sat down, his face drained of color. The council had found the one person whose suffering could reach him faster than any argument. Lena wanted to offer reassurance, but there was nothing hospitable in the threat and nothing she could honestly guarantee. She asked Jory to tell them what he needed. "A doctor for my sister," he whispered. "Not another speech about justice." The room, which had been preparing for resistance, confronted a more difficult task: to protect one vulnerable person without sacrificing all the others. Lena felt the boundaries of her certainty begin to crack. She understood that winning a public argument would mean very little if a single child died while the adults congratulated themselves on their principles.

會議接近高潮時，信使帶來哈維爾的封包。裡面是喬里妹妹躺在大陸醫院的照片。訊息寫著，若莉娜不帶檔案館原始鑰匙返回，女孩就無法接受治療。喬里看了兩遍，坐倒在椅子上，臉色慘白。議會終於找到那個能比任何論證更快擊中他的人。莉娜想安慰，卻明白威脅裡沒有半點善意，而自己也無法保證什麼。她問喬里需要什麼。「我妹妹需要醫生，」他低聲說，「不是另一篇正義演講。」原本準備抵抗的眾人，現在得面對更難的任務：如何保護一個脆弱的人，同時不犧牲所有其他人。莉娜感到自己的確信開始出現裂縫。她明白，若大人只顧著為自己的原則拍手稱讚，卻讓一個孩子因此死去，那麼辯論勝利也毫無意義。
