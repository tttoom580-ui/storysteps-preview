珊瑚下的診間

The Surgery Beneath the Coral

灰潮檔案 · 第 12 / 25 章 · 5000–6000 · 英中對照 · 校訂中

用互動版查詞、朗讀與收藏

A small brook led them inland to a hidden coastal hospital built beside a coral shelf. The entrance was almost invisible from the road; a rescue helicopter sometimes hovered above the rocks to bring in patients. The hospital's lease had expired years earlier, but its staff remained because no other clinic served the fishing families. A legislator named Kira had tried to secure their right to stay. She showed Lena the paperwork, its expiration notices repeated so often that the stack was nearly a book. "The council can deprive us of a building with a signature," she said. "It cannot make the sick disappear by pretending there's no clinic." Inside, a surgeon was preparing an emergency operation on a child whose infected wound had begun to strangle the circulation in his arm. The child's mother stood beside the door, exhausted but determined. Lena stepped away from her questions and helped carry the surgical supplies.

一條小溪引他們往內陸走，來到珊瑚礁旁隱藏的海岸醫院。入口幾乎看不見，救援直升機有時會在礁岩上方盤旋送來病患。醫院租約多年前已到期，員工仍留下來，因為附近漁家再也沒有其他診所可去。一名叫綺拉的議員一直想替他們取得續留權利。她給莉娜看文件，一張張到期通知重複得幾乎成書。「議會一個簽名就能剝奪我們的建築，」她說，「卻不能靠假裝診所不存在，就讓病人消失。」裡面，一名外科醫師正準備替孩子動緊急手術。感染傷口嚴重壓迫手臂血液循環。孩子母親精疲力盡，仍堅持守在門旁。莉娜暫時放下問題，幫忙搬運外科用品。

The surgeon, an elderly man with failing eyesight, asked Lena to read the patient's chart aloud. His insistence on having every dose repeated sounded excessive until she realized the supply labels had been changed. The drug was stronger than the chart indicated. One wrong injection could have caused permanent harm. "People blame old eyes," he said, "but the real problem is a system that rewards haste and punishes questions." He rotated a lamp to illuminate the wound, then began working with steady hands. After the procedure, the child slept while the mother ate a hearty bowl of broth. The surgeon told Lena he had once been appointed to Island Nine. He had resigned when the council ordered treatment without consent. "Your mother tried to stop it," he said. "But she also helped build the administrative system that made it possible. Both facts matter."

那位視力衰退的老外科醫師請莉娜朗讀病歷。他堅持每個劑量都重複確認，起初聽來過分，直到莉娜發現藥品標籤遭到更換。實際藥力比病歷顯示得強，一次錯誤注射就可能造成永久傷害。「人們怪老眼睛，」醫師說，「但真正的問題是制度獎勵匆忙，懲罰提問。」他轉動燈具照亮傷口，穩穩開始動手術。術後孩子睡著了，母親吃下一大碗濃湯。醫師告訴莉娜，他曾被派往第九島，當議會命令在未經同意下治療病患時便辭職。「妳母親曾努力阻止，」他說，「但她也幫忙建立了讓那一切變得可能的行政系統。兩件事都是真的。」

Kira took Lena to the clinic roof, where the sea opened in a majestic blue arc. She explained that before the floods, a monarch had granted the harbor its first public medical charter. The charter was imperfect, but it recognized that care belonged to residents and their offspring rather than to the ruler's favorites. Later administrations had reduced the promise to a narrow list of eligible names. Kira wanted to elevate the older principle without pretending the past had been just. Lena considered the momentum of the evidence they had gathered. Every institution contained traces of good intentions and of the ways those intentions were narrowed. "We could ask for a new charter," she said. "One that cannot be revoked when an official dislikes a question." Kira nodded but warned that constitutional promises required people capable of enforcing them. Papers alone could not protect a patient.

綺拉帶莉娜上醫院屋頂，海面展開壯闊的藍色弧線。她解釋，在洪災以前，一位君主曾賦予港口最早的公共醫療憲章。憲章並不完美，卻承認照護屬於居民及其後代，而非統治者寵愛的人。後來的政府把這份承諾縮成只有合格姓名才能享有的短短名單。綺拉希望提升舊原則，同時不把歷史美化成公平的過去。莉娜思索一路累積的證據。每個機構都留著善意的痕跡，也留著善意如何被一步步限縮的痕跡。「我們可以要求新憲章，」她說，「不能因官員不喜歡有人發問，就隨意撤銷。」綺拉點頭，但提醒憲章承諾需要人來執行。紙張本身保護不了病患。

A boat arrived late that afternoon, carrying one of Sera's cousins and a fuel canister of precious petrol. He had brought evidence that the council was assembling a private security force near the southern wharf. Their leaders intended to seize the hospital's medicine and declare it a public emergency stock. Kira could have hidden the supplies, but then the inland villages might lose access. Instead she arranged a protected distribution: volunteer witnesses would record each shipment, and local doctors would verify every recipient's need. Lena offered to help. She had begun to believe that a community could survive even while its government failed, provided people did not let fear turn scarce goods into weapons. At dusk, the hospital windows glowed above the coral. The child who had nearly lost his arm woke and asked for his mother. No official decree had been needed to tell the nurses to answer his call. Lena watched them embrace, reminded why every argument about records and laws began with a living person.

當天下午，一艘船很晚才抵達，載來瑟拉的表親和一罐珍貴汽油。他帶來證據，顯示議會正於南方碼頭附近組建私人武裝。領導者打算沒收醫院藥品，宣稱它們屬於公共緊急儲備。綺拉可以把藥藏起來，但那可能讓內陸村莊拿不到藥。她改為安排受保護的分配方式：志工見證人記錄每批貨物，當地醫師確認每名領取者的需求。莉娜也願意協助。她漸漸相信，政府即使失靈，社群仍有生存可能，前提是人們不能讓恐懼把稀缺物資變成武器。黃昏時醫院燈光映在珊瑚礁上。差點失去手臂的孩子醒來，喊著要找母親。護理師回應他的呼喚，不需要任何官方命令。莉娜看著他們相擁，再次想起所有關於檔案與法律的爭辯，起點都是活生生的人。
