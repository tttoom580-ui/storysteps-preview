不該存在的姓名

The Name That Should Not Exist

灰潮檔案 · 第 1 / 25 章 · 5000–6000 · 英中對照

用互動版查詞、朗讀與收藏

The flood siren sounded at six, although the sea had been calm for seventeen days. Lena was standing in a bank queue when the teller pushed a red envelope under the glass. There was no money inside, only a photograph of her mother, who had supposedly drowned twelve years earlier. On its reverse, a single sentence had been written in the old government's shorthand: Do not abbreviate the names of the living. Lena knew that hand. Her mother used to write that way on grocery lists. A uniformed guard approached, and the teller immediately pretended to count coins. The envelope smelled of salt and smoke. Beneath the photograph was a warrant for Lena's arrest, dated tomorrow. Whoever had sent it knew something about the future, or had enough power to decide what the future would say.

洪水警報在六點響起，儘管海面已經平靜了十七天。莉娜正排在銀行隊伍裡，出納員忽然從玻璃窗下推來一只紅色信封。裡面沒有錢，只有一張她母親的照片——母親據說十二年前就溺死了。照片背後，以舊政府的速記方式寫著一句話：「活人的姓名不可縮寫。」莉娜認得那筆跡。母親以前寫購物清單時就這樣寫。一名制服警衛走近，出納員立刻假裝清點硬幣。信封帶著鹽與煙的氣味。照片下方竟是一張逮捕莉娜的拘票，日期寫著明天。寄件者不是知道未來，就是有權決定未來將如何被記錄。

Lena left the bank by the delivery door and took the covered walkway to the eastern junction. Her left leg still had a limp from the evacuation years, but fear made her quick. She passed a shop where cooks would broil river fish over recycled charcoal. A woman rocked a feverish child against her bosom, murmuring a lullaby Lena remembered from childhood. The sight was almost enough to stop her. Above the market, green plants flourished in boxes made from broken windows, stubborn evidence that life could persist in borrowed soil. An anchor from a wrecked cargo ship hung over the entrance to the public archive. It had been there since the city's founding, a symbol of safety that now looked like a threat. Lena had once worked inside that archive. She knew which doors were locked and which locks had been installed merely to impress visitors.

莉娜從銀行的送貨門離開，沿著有頂棚的走道朝東邊交叉口前進。多年前撤離時，她的左腿留下跛行的毛病，但恐懼讓她走得飛快。她經過一間小店，廚師正用回收的木炭炙烤河魚。一名女子將發燒的孩子抱在胸前，低聲哼著莉娜童年聽過的搖籃曲。那畫面幾乎讓她停下腳步。市場上方，綠色植物在破窗框改造的花箱裡繁茂生長，頑強地證明生命能在借來的土壤中延續。一只打撈自貨船的船錨懸在公共檔案館入口上方。它自建城之初便掛在那裡，原本象徵安全，如今看來卻像威脅。莉娜曾在那座檔案館工作，知道哪些門真上了鎖，哪些鎖只是用來嚇唬訪客。

At the archive's rear entrance, an orderly from the medical ward waited beside a stack of linen bags. His name was Jory, though the dockside gangster who employed him called him Needle. He had been Lena's oldest friend before survival forced everyone to take several jobs. "Your mother paid me to keep this safe," he said, producing a strip of cloth cut from a faded brassiere. A tiny metal key had been sewn into its lining. Lena felt her eyelid twitch. She was ten when her mother disappeared; that garment had belonged to the woman who taught her how to swim. "Why didn't you tell me?" she whispered. "Because she made me promise not to," Jory said. "And because the man who wanted it offered me a bribe worth five years of food." He did not look proud of refusing the money. He looked exhausted by the consequences.

在檔案館後門，一名醫療站的勤務員靠著一堆布袋等候。他叫喬里，雖然雇用他的碼頭幫派分子都叫他「針頭」。在人人不得不兼幾份工作求生之前，喬里是莉娜最老的朋友。「妳母親付錢要我保管這個。」他說著，拿出一條從褪色胸罩剪下的布料。內襯縫著一把小金屬鑰匙。莉娜的眼皮抽動了一下。母親失蹤時她才十歲；那件衣物曾屬於教她游泳的女人。「你為什麼不告訴我？」她低聲問。「因為她要我發誓不能說，」喬里回答，「也因為想得到它的那個人，曾想拿足夠吃五年的糧食來收買我。」他並未因拒絕金錢而感到驕傲，只顯得被代價折磨得筋疲力盡。

They slipped into the restricted records room. Lena laid the key beside a comparative chart of the flood years and opened a cabinet of maps describing virgin ground, land never touched by salt water. Inside lay maps, birth certificates, and photographs that the government had declared not applicable to current ration claims. "These records don't prove your mother survived," Jory warned. "They prove someone wanted to erase the possibility." Lena found twenty-seven names crossed out in blue ink. She could narrate the official account of every death from memory; she had typed most of those accounts herself. Yet the addresses on the maps showed entire houses above the flood line. The sea had never reached them. "Our water is finite," the government liked to say, whenever people questioned the missing. The phrase now seemed designed to make citizens fear asking who had received the city's supplies.

兩人溜進限制閱覽的檔案室。莉娜把鑰匙放在一張比較歷年洪災的圖表旁，打開標著「未受侵蝕土地」的檔櫃——那是對從未被海水浸過土地的分類。櫃內放著地圖、出生證明，以及政府認定「不適用於現行配給申請」的照片。「這些文件不能證明妳母親還活著，」喬里提醒，「只能證明有人想抹去她可能生還的證據。」莉娜看到二十七個被藍墨水劃掉的姓名。每一個人的官方死因，她都能背誦；大部分紀錄還是她親手打的。然而地圖上的地址顯示，整排房屋都位於洪水線之上。海水根本沒淹到那裡。「我們的水資源有限。」每當民眾追問失蹤者，政府總愛這麼說。如今她覺得，那句話只是為了嚇阻人們追問物資究竟發給了誰。

A door slammed upstairs. Jory reached for the emergency ladder, but Lena held him back. The archive had a second system, an old manual register kept in the basement. They would need the original names, not the copies on the council's servers. Before she could explain, a voice came through the ventilation shaft: "Lena Vale. I know you're there." Director Havel stood somewhere above them, patient as a hunter. "Leave the photograph and we'll consider your crime a misunderstanding." Lena could hear boots moving into position. She understood the bargain. If she surrendered the proof, her mother would remain dead on paper, and every person whose name had been stolen would remain invisible. She slid the cabinet drawer shut, pocketed the register key, and spoke loudly enough for Havel to hear. "Then you'll have to come down and explain whose lives you're protecting." A light went out. Jory caught her hand. Together they descended into the dark.

樓上有扇門砰然關上。喬里伸手去抓緊急逃生梯，莉娜卻拉住他。檔案館還有第二套系統：地下室保存著一本舊式手寫登記簿。他們需要真正的姓名，而不是議會伺服器裡的複本。她還來不及解釋，通風管就傳來聲音：「莉娜・維爾，我知道妳在那裡。」哈維爾局長站在樓上某處，像獵人般耐心。「把照片留下，我們可以把妳的罪當作一場誤會。」莉娜聽見靴子挪動，正逐步包圍他們。她明白這筆交易的意思。如果交出證據，母親在紙上就永遠是死人，所有被奪走姓名的人也將繼續隱形。她關上抽屜，把登記簿鑰匙放進口袋，刻意提高音量，讓哈維爾聽見：「那你得親自下來，解釋你們到底在保護誰的命。」燈熄了。喬里握住她的手。兩人一起走下黑暗中的階梯。
