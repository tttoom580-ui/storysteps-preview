二十七個孩子

Twenty-Seven Children

灰潮檔案 · 第 3 / 25 章 · 5000–6000 · 英中對照

用互動版查詞、朗讀與收藏

The map led Lena, Jory, and Sera to the abandoned harbor, where the council's advisory board had once promised to rebuild the coast. Now weeds grew through the concrete, and the air smelled of grease and salt. A huge passenger liner lay stranded between two warehouses, its lower decks sealed after an outbreak of plague. According to the council, everyone aboard had died. Yet a faint light shone behind one window. A boy stood there holding a cucumber, as calmly as if he were waiting for lunch. Lena waved, and the boy vanished. "Someone is using that ship," she said. Jory inspected the cables. Their insulation was new. The ship was still drawing electricity from a city that claimed to have no power to spare. Nearby, a feeble old generator struggled against the wind, and Lena saw the first sign that the government's carefully managed shortages were not shortages at all.

地圖引領莉娜、喬里和瑟拉抵達廢棄港口。議會顧問團過去曾承諾重建這片海岸。如今野草從水泥縫中冒出，空氣滿是油脂和鹽的氣味。一艘龐大的客輪擱淺在兩座倉庫之間，下層甲板在瘟疫爆發後被封死。依官方說法，船上無人生還。可是，一扇窗戶後方竟透出微光。一名男孩站在那裡，手中拿著黃瓜，神情平靜得像在等午餐。莉娜向他揮手，他隨即消失。「有人正在使用那艘船。」她說。喬里檢查電纜，發現絕緣層很新。船仍從宣稱電力不足的城市取得能源。附近一台虛弱的舊發電機迎風掙扎；莉娜第一次清楚看見，政府精心管理的「短缺」，可能根本不是短缺。

A guard appeared on the upper deck. Jory lit a firecracker and rolled it beneath an empty fuel drum. The noise sent the guard running toward the wrong side of the ship. "That trick won't survive a second performance," Sera muttered, but they gained time to climb aboard. Inside, Lena found a recreational room with painted stars, child-sized chairs, and a cabinet full of medical supplies. A label on the cabinet showed an approved vaccine for the fever that had devastated the port. The boxes were not empty. Hundreds of doses sat in cold storage while families on the mainland had buried children who were told the vaccine did not exist. Lena felt anger rise so sharply that she could scarcely breathe. Sera gripped her arm. "Don't let anger make you savage," she said. "We came for evidence. If we destroy it, they'll keep telling their version."

上層甲板出現一名守衛。喬里點燃鞭炮，滾到一個空油桶底下。爆響引得守衛往船的另一端跑去。「這招用第二次就不靈了。」瑟拉咕噥，但這爭取到他們登船的時間。船內有一間休閒活動室，畫著星星的牆面、孩童尺寸的椅子，以及滿櫃醫療用品。櫃子標籤上寫著一種已獲核准的疫苗，能對抗曾重創港口的熱病。盒子竟然不是空的。數百劑疫苗藏在冷藏庫裡，大陸上的家庭卻被告知疫苗根本不存在，只能埋葬自己的孩子。莉娜的怒火猛烈上湧，幾乎喘不過氣。瑟拉緊握住她的手臂。「別讓憤怒把妳變成野獸，」她說，「我們來找證據。要是把它毀了，他們就能繼續編故事。」

In the captain's office they discovered a ledger written partly in port slang. Jory translated the abbreviations. The orphanage named in the entries was not a charity; it was a testing center where children from the flooded districts received experimental treatment. The council called its program a conquest of disease, but the records showed that the injections had sometimes worsened the illness. A shipment of textile uniforms had concealed crates of medicine, while a second shipment had carried children to a remote island. The operation was not the work of one greedy official. It was a conspiracy involving traders, physicians, and council members. Lena found her mother's initials beside a column of objections. Far from helping the program, she had repeatedly refused to approve it. The last entry read: Transferred to Island Nine after attempting to warn families. For the first time in twelve years, Lena had a plausible destination rather than a grave.

他們在船長辦公室發現一本帳冊，部分內容以港口俚語記錄。喬里翻譯那些縮寫。帳冊提到的孤兒院並非慈善機構，而是一座測試中心，讓來自淹水區的孩子接受實驗性治療。議會把計畫稱為「征服疾病」，但紀錄顯示，注射藥劑有時反而讓病情惡化。某批紡織制服貨物掩藏了藥箱，另一批則把孩子送往偏遠島嶼。這不是單一貪官的作為，而是商人、醫生和議員共同參與的陰謀。莉娜在一欄反對意見旁發現母親的姓名縮寫。母親非但沒協助計畫，還多次拒絕批准。最後一筆寫著：「因試圖警告家屬，轉送第九島。」十二年來，莉娜終於找到一個可能的去處，而非一座墳墓。

Footsteps thundered outside. They escaped through a service hatch beside the ship's kitchen, where spoiled fuel began to fume against a hot pipe. Jory smeared grease on a stubborn hinge and forced it open. On the shore, beneath a shrub that had survived the salt, Sera discovered twenty-seven small stones arranged in a circle. Each bore a child's first name. Someone had made this memorial to symbolize lives the official record denied. Lena counted the stones twice. The number matched the names in the archive. The children were not merely missing adults on paper; they had been taken, hidden, and assigned new identities. "We can lessen their fear only by telling them what we know," Sera said. "And what we don't." Lena looked toward the distant island, where a dark shape interrupted the sunrise. She had begun the journey wanting proof about one woman. Now the responsibility was larger than her hope, and might surpass anything she could bear alone.

外頭腳步聲如雷。他們從廚房旁的維修孔逃出，變質燃料正因接觸熱管而冒出刺鼻煙氣。喬里在卡住的鉸鏈上抹上潤滑脂，終於把它推開。岸邊，一株在鹽霧中倖存的灌木下，瑟拉發現二十七顆小石頭排成一圈，每顆都刻著一個孩子的名字。有人立下這座紀念物，象徵官方不承認的生命。莉娜把石頭數了兩次。數目和檔案中的姓名完全相符。那些孩子不只是紙上失蹤的人，而是遭人帶走、藏匿，並被賦予新身分。「能減少他們恐懼的方法，是說清我們知道什麼，」瑟拉說，「以及不知道什麼。」莉娜看向遠方的島嶼，黑色的輪廓截斷了日出的光。她原本只想證明一個女人的下落。如今她肩上的責任，比個人的希望更巨大，甚至可能超過獨自能承受的範圍。
