水聲的對稱

The Symmetry of Water

灰潮檔案 · 第 11 / 25 章 · 5000–6000 · 英中對照 · 校訂中

用互動版查詞、朗讀與收藏

Lena measured the temperature of the reservoir wall with her palm. The metal was cold, perhaps twelve degrees Celsius, while the emergency outlet vibrated with an unfamiliar growl. They had been locked inside the control room for nearly an hour. Jory found a maintenance diagram showing a service tunnel below the machinery, but its entrance lay behind a mound of obsolete tools. As they moved the equipment, friction against the floor made the old wheels shriek. Sera insisted on checking the supports before anyone entered. "The building's symmetry isn't decorative," she said. "If we remove one brace, the weight will shift onto the others." Their guide, who had trained horses rather than engineers, made a surprisingly useful suggestion. Instead of lifting the heavy cabinet, they could slide it across a metal sheet. Within minutes they exposed a hatch. Beyond it came the sound of running water and the faint melody of a pianist playing in a distant room.

莉娜用掌心測量水庫牆面的溫度。金屬冰冷，大概只有攝氏十二度，緊急出口則發出陌生的低吼。他們已被鎖在控制室將近一小時。喬里從維修圖上找到機械下方的服務隧道，可是入口被一堆廢棄工具擋住。他們搬動設備時，輪子與地面摩擦，發出刺耳聲響。瑟拉堅持先檢查支撐結構。「建築的對稱不是裝飾，」她說，「移掉一根支柱，重量就會轉移到其他地方。」本來訓練馬匹而非修工程的嚮導，提出意外實用的建議：別抬沉重鐵櫃，改用金屬板讓它滑過去。幾分鐘後，檢修孔露出。裡面傳來流水聲，還有遠處鋼琴師隱約彈奏的旋律。

The tunnel led to a disused inspection chamber near the strait where the river met the sea. A painted peacock stood on the door, a foolish decoration left by a wealthy engineer. Inside, a woman sat beside a portable keyboard. She was the pianist who had played during the reservoir's opening ceremony, but now she worked as a water inspector. "Music helped me remember how the systems were arranged," she explained. "Their respective pumps produce different notes when operating normally." She offered to probe the hidden pipes with a listening device. Lena handed her a copy of the laboratory report. The inspector compared the sounds and identified a second outlet concealed below the official one. It carried polluted water into a canal that fed the lower districts. The council had suppressed complaints about the odor for years. Lena felt urgency harden into anger. They had not merely failed to maintain a water system; they had made its victims pay for the failure.

隧道通向一間棄用的檢查室，附近正是河流入海的海峽口。門上畫著孔雀，是某位富有工程師留下的滑稽裝飾。室內一名女子坐在攜帶式鍵盤前。她曾在水庫啟用典禮演奏鋼琴，如今成了驗水員。「音樂讓我記得設備怎麼排列，」她解釋，「每個幫浦正常運作時，都有各自不同的聲音。」她願意用聽音器探查隱藏管路。莉娜交給她實驗室報告。驗水員比對聲響，找出官方排水口下方另有第二條出口，將污染水送進供應下城區的水道。議會多年來一直壓下居民對異味的投訴。莉娜感到急迫之情化成怒火。他們不只是沒維護供水系統，還讓受害者為錯誤付出代價。

The inspector warned that the repairs could not be simplified into shutting a valve. Pressure would rise elsewhere, and shortsighted action might burst the pipes beneath a hospital. Lena requested a plan that addressed the whole system. They worked with a small team from the university, each person responsible for a distinct section. A young apprentice suggested using colored water as a harmless token of the contamination path. "The superficial stain might be enough to show people where the leak travels," he said. The inspector corrected him gently: dye would reveal movement, not prove toxicity. They agreed to publish the test with that limitation clearly stated. The careful distinction felt slow, almost frustrating, but it was the difference between evidence and propaganda. Lena understood that if they hoped to unify the districts, their claims had to survive the same scrutiny they demanded of the council.

驗水員提醒，維修不是關上一個閥門就能簡化的事。別處壓力會升高，短視的行動可能弄破醫院下方的管線。莉娜要求考慮整套系統的方案。她們和大學小隊合作，每人負責不同區段。一名學徒建議用染色水作為污染路徑的無害標記。「表面的顏色也許就能讓大家看出漏水去了哪裡。」他說。驗水員溫和地糾正：染料能顯示流向，不能證明毒性。他們同意公開測試，同時明確說明限制。這份謹慎緩慢得令人焦躁，卻正是證據與宣傳的差別。莉娜明白，想團結各區，自己的主張就必須經得起她對議會提出的同樣檢驗。

When the tunnel finally opened to the shore, dawn was breaking over the water. The pianist stood beside Lena and asked whether she had thought about what would happen after Havel fell. Lena admitted she had not, at least not clearly. "A man can be removed in one day," the inspector said. "A broken habit takes years to repair." The words stayed with Lena as they climbed toward the road. It was ironic that the tunnel built to isolate workers now led people out of captivity. They would not allow fear to induce another silence, nor would they ignore the families who inhabit the lower streets. She had spent the first part of her journey identifying enemies. Now she saw the harder task: designing a city where ordinary people could question an official account without losing food, medical care, or a place to live. The sound of the sea followed them uphill. In that simple rhythm she heard something like a warning, and something like the beginning of a different future.

隧道終於通向海岸時，晨光正越過水面。鋼琴師站在莉娜身旁，問她想過哈維爾倒台後會怎樣嗎？莉娜承認自己想得不夠清楚。「一個人可以一天內下台，」驗水員說，「壞習慣卻要好幾年才能修正。」這句話一路伴著莉娜爬上公路。諷刺的是，原本用來隔離工人的隧道，如今卻帶領人們走出囚禁。他們不會再讓恐懼誘發沉默，也不會忽略居住在下城街道的家庭。旅程前半段，她忙著辨認敵人；現在她看見更艱難的任務：建立一座能讓普通人質疑官方說法，卻不因此失去糧食、醫療與居所的城市。海聲跟著她們往山上走。那簡單的節奏既像警告，也像另一種未來的開端。
