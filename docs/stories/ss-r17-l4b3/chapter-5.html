At the manor's mirrored ballroom, the first sign of trouble was not a scream or an alarm. It was a small disagreement about something everyone believed they understood. Five players described a masked person crossing the ballroom, though their directions of travel did not agree. Xu Fei listened to the surrounding voices before making a judgment. Rumors traveled faster than careful questions, especially when the public wanted a simple villain. The team needed a record that would remain useful even if their favorite explanation proved false. Xu Fei asked whether the team could abandon under the safety rules; the answer was not yet clear. The old manual told its readers to adjust, and Wen Xi wanted to know who had written that rule. The evidence list included allowance, although nobody had explained why that detail mattered. The working file contained a separate entry for anxiety, and Xu Fei asked who had verified it.

在莊園鏡廳，麻煩不是從尖叫或警報開始，而是從一件大家自認了解的小事產生分歧。五位玩家都說看見蒙面人穿越鏡廳，卻對行進方向說法不一。許霏先聽周遭人的說法，沒有急著下判斷。群眾想找到簡單的壞人時，謠言總比仔細提問傳得快。即使最喜歡的推測最後被推翻，小隊也需要留下仍然有用的紀錄。許霏詢問小隊能否依安全規則「拋下」，答案仍不明朗。舊手冊要求讀者「調整」，文希想知道這條規則由誰制定。證物清單上有「津貼」，卻沒有人解釋這個細節為何重要。工作檔案為「焦慮」設了獨立項目，許霏詢問是誰查證過。

桌上最舊的文件看起來不如最新螢幕重要，但那只是表面。文希證明旋轉鏡面能從不同位置產生同一反射人影。文希念出手寫備註，許霏逐項核對事件順序。此時兩人的不同專長，比誰的職位更高還重要。找到矛盾後，他們把兩個版本都保存下來。毀掉一份不方便的資料，只會讓下一次調查更加困難。文希在舊筆記裡找到關於「獨木舟」的記載，但紙上沒有可信的日期。見證人在訪談中提到「裝置」，小隊照實記錄，卻沒有直接認同。有爭議的通知使用「明顯不同的」一詞，作者卻沒有在頁面簽名。書面指示要求工作人員在期限前「放大」，卻沒有人記下結果。

By afternoon, the discovery had reached the people who could lose the most. Manager Lin argued the inconsistent testimony proved that at least one player was lying. The report called the arrangement favorable, a judgment the group refused to accept without proof. 到了下午，這項發現已傳到最可能蒙受損失的人耳中。林經理說證詞不一致證明至少一人說謊。林經理提出快速和解，條件卻是所有人不准再追問。許霏看著房內不安的臉孔。此時說些英雄話、要求大家冒一樣的風險很容易；他們卻讓每個人自己說明願意做什麼，以及拒絕承擔什麼。報告說這項安排「有利的」，小隊不願在沒有證據前接受此判斷。見證人在訪談中提到「慣用語」，小隊照實記錄，卻沒有直接認同。證物清單上有「偶像」，卻沒有人解釋這個細節為何重要。檢查員把情況形容為「有想像力的」，許霏卻想要數據，而不是形容詞。

Every witness had seen the evening from a different angle. Xu Fei did not expect their memories to fit perfectly, and she refused to treat an honest difference as a confession. Wen Xi marked where the stories overlapped and where the rooms themselves offered independent evidence. Their questions grew less theatrical and more useful. The missing chair was still important, but the safety of the missing worker mattered far more.

每名見證人都從不同角度經歷那個晚上。許霏沒期待記憶完美吻合，也不把誠實的差異當成自白。文希標出說法重疊處，以及房間本身能提供獨立證據的地方。他們的問題不再那麼戲劇化，卻更有用。空椅子依然重要，但失蹤工作者的安全遠比它重要。

A delay forced Xu Fei to choose between keeping the schedule and checking the safety condition again. The group checked again. They had lost time, but a false success would have cost them much more. Wen Xi marked the revised sequence where everyone could see it. Wen Xi found a reference to impact in the old notes, but the page gave no reliable date. The streets outside the manor's mirrored ballroom were quieter, but quiet was not proof of safety. The shadow belonged to a printed silhouette, not an unseen ninth person. Xu Fei checked that everyone knew how to ask for help before the group separated. They had gained one answer and inherited two new responsibilities. Tomorrow's work would require patience from people who had every reason to be angry. For the first time that day, the team had a direction worth following. Wen Xi found a reference to prosperity in the old notes, but the page gave no reliable date. A witness mentioned protest during the interview, so the team recorded the statement without endorsing it.
