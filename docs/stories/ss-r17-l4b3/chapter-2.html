The Rule Written Backward

At the manor's entrance corridor, the first sign of trouble was not a scream or an alarm. It was a small disagreement about something everyone believed they understood. A rule card claimed nobody could leave until the murderer confessed, though the reverse side listed a right to withdraw. Xu Fei listened to the surrounding voices before making a judgment. Rumors traveled faster than careful questions, especially when the public wanted a simple villain. The team needed a record that would remain useful even if their favorite explanation proved false. A witness mentioned ATOM during the interview, so the team recorded the statement without endorsing it. The disputed notice used the word ATOMIC, but its author had not signed the page. The working file contained a separate entry for combination, and Xu Fei asked who had verified it. The report called the arrangement delicate, a judgment the group refused to accept without proof.

在莊園入口走廊，麻煩不是從尖叫或警報開始，而是從一件大家自認了解的小事產生分歧。規則卡聲稱兇手坦白前無人能離開，背面卻列著退出權利。許霏先聽周遭人的說法，沒有急著下判斷。群眾想找到簡單的壞人時，謠言總比仔細提問傳得快。即使最喜歡的推測最後被推翻，小隊也需要留下仍然有用的紀錄。見證人在訪談中提到「原子」，小隊照實記錄，卻沒有直接認同。有爭議的通知使用「原子能的」一詞，作者卻沒有在頁面簽名。工作檔案為「組合」設了獨立項目，許霏詢問是誰查證過。報告說這項安排「微弱的／精細的」，小隊不願在沒有證據前接受此判斷。

The oldest document on the table looked less important than the newest screen. That appearance was misleading. Wen Xi compared both sides with the signed booking contract and found the reverse side legally controlling. Wen Xi read the handwritten notes aloud while Xu Fei checked the sequence of events. Their different skills mattered more than either person's rank. When they found a contradiction, they preserved both versions. Destroying an inconvenient record would only make the next investigation harder. A witness mentioned extent during the interview, so the team recorded the statement without endorsing it. The evidence list included fort, although nobody had explained why that detail mattered. The working file contained a separate entry for fossil, and Xu Fei asked who had verified it. Wen Xi found a reference to gaze in the old notes, but the page gave no reliable date.

桌上最舊的文件看起來不如最新螢幕重要，但那只是表面。文希比對正反面與預約合約，發現背面才是正式條款。文希念出手寫備註，許霏逐項核對事件順序。此時兩人的不同專長，比誰的職位更高還重要。找到矛盾後，他們把兩個版本都保存下來。毀掉一份不方便的資料，只會讓下一次調查更加困難。見證人在訪談中提到「程度」，小隊照實記錄，卻沒有直接認同。證物清單上有「堡壘」，卻沒有人解釋這個細節為何重要。工作檔案為「化石」設了獨立項目，許霏詢問是誰查證過。文希在舊筆記裡找到關於「凝視」的記載，但紙上沒有可信的日期。

By afternoon, the discovery had reached the people who could lose the most. Manager Lin tried to hide the reverse side under a decorative frame. Manager Lin offered a quick settlement, but it required the group to stop asking questions. Xu Fei considered the frightened faces around the room. It would be easy to sound heroic and demand that everyone take the same risk. Instead, each person was allowed to describe what they were prepared to do and what they would refuse. The old manual told its readers to guarantee, and Wen Xi wanted to know who had written that rule. The evidence list included immigration, although nobody had explained why that detail mattered. An inspector described the situation as intimate; Xu Fei wanted measurements instead of adjectives. Wen Xi found a reference to landscape in the old notes, but the page gave no reliable date.

到了下午，這項發現已傳到最可能蒙受損失的人耳中。林經理企圖把背面藏進裝飾畫框。林經理提出快速和解，條件卻是所有人不准再追問。許霏看著房內不安的臉孔。此時說些英雄話、要求大家冒一樣的風險很容易；他們卻讓每個人自己說明願意做什麼，以及拒絕承擔什麼。舊手冊要求讀者「保證」，文希想知道這條規則由誰制定。證物清單上有「入出境／移民」，卻沒有人解釋這個細節為何重要。檢查員把情況形容為「私密的」，許霏卻想要數據，而不是形容詞。文希在舊筆記裡找到關於「風景布景」的記載，但紙上沒有可信的日期。

Every witness had seen the evening from a different angle. Xu Fei did not expect their memories to fit perfectly, and she refused to treat an honest difference as a confession. Wen Xi marked where the stories overlapped and where the rooms themselves offered independent evidence. Their questions grew less theatrical and more useful. The missing chair was still important, but the safety of the missing worker mattered far more.

每名見證人都從不同角度經歷那個晚上。許霏沒期待記憶完美吻合，也不把誠實的差異當成自白。文希標出說法重疊處，以及房間本身能提供獨立證據的地方。他們的問題不再那麼戲劇化，卻更有用。空椅子依然重要，但失蹤工作者的安全遠比它重要。

The plan looked simple on paper and complicated the moment people began moving. Xu Fei read the withdrawal procedure aloud and asked for the doors to remain available. A delay forced Xu Fei to choose between keeping the schedule and checking the safety condition again. The group checked again. They had lost time, but a false success would have cost them much more. Wen Xi marked the revised sequence where everyone could see it. A witness mentioned messenger during the interview, so the team recorded the statement without endorsing it. The evidence list included mill, although nobody had explained why that detail mattered. The working file contained a separate entry for murmur, and Xu Fei asked who had verified it. The report called the arrangement productive, a judgment the group refused to accept without proof.

計畫寫在紙上很簡單，人一動起來就變得複雜。許霏當眾讀出退出程序，要求大門保持可用。一次延誤讓許霏必須在守住時間表與重新檢查安全條件之間選擇。小隊決定再查一次。雖然損失時間，虛假的成功卻可能造成更大代價。文希把修改後的順序標在所有人都看得見的位置。見證人在訪談中提到「送件員」，小隊照實記錄，卻沒有直接認同。證物清單上有「磨坊」，卻沒有人解釋這個細節為何重要。工作檔案為「低語」設了獨立項目，許霏詢問是誰查證過。報告說這項安排「有成果的」，小隊不願在沒有證據前接受此判斷。

The streets outside the manor's entrance corridor were quieter, but quiet was not proof of safety. The lock clicked, but an alarm still required an independent manual release. Xu Fei checked that everyone knew how to ask for help before the group separated. They had gained one answer and inherited two new responsibilities. Tomorrow's work would require patience from people who had every reason to be angry. For the first time that day, the team had a direction worth following. Someone had marked the warning psychological; Wen Xi checked whether that description was accurate. One witness promised to retain if conditions improved, but Xu Fei would not treat hope as evidence. A written instruction told the staff to revise before the deadline, but nobody had logged the result. Wen Xi found a reference to voyage in the old notes, but the page gave no reliable date.

莊園入口走廊外的街道變安靜了，但安靜不代表安全。門鎖響了一聲，警報系統卻仍需要獨立人工解鎖。許霏先確認大家知道如何求助，才讓小隊分開。他們得到一個答案，也多了兩份責任。明天的工作需要那些本來就有理由憤怒的人保持耐心。至少在這一天結束時，小隊終於有了一個值得追查的方向。有人把警告標成「心理上的」，文希則查證這種描述是否準確。一名見證人答應條件改善就會「保留」，許霏卻不能把希望當成證據。書面指示要求工作人員在期限前「修改」，卻沒有人記下結果。文希在舊筆記裡找到關於「航程」的記載，但紙上沒有可信的日期。
