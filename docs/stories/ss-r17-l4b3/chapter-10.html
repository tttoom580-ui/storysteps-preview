The Vanished Actress's Contract

The day began inside the theater administration office. Xu Fei had come expecting an ordinary task, but the room already contained the sort of silence that follows bad news. The host's contract described a rehearsal assignment in a technical room that was not on the game map. Nobody could afford to confuse a confident explanation with a reliable observation. They had made that mistake before, and the cost was still visible in the people waiting outside. Xu Fei opened the notebook and decided to write down what could actually be tested. A witness mentioned apology during the interview, so the team recorded the statement without endorsing it. The evidence list included bandage, although nobody had explained why that detail mattered. The working file contained a separate entry for bargain, and Xu Fei asked who had verified it. Wen Xi found a reference to civilization in the old notes, but the page gave no reliable date.

這一天從劇場行政辦公室開始。許霏原以為只是處理例行工作，房間卻靜得像剛傳出壞消息。主持人合約寫著她要到遊戲地圖沒有的技術室彩排。他們不能把說得肯定的解釋誤當成可靠觀察。大家以前犯過這種錯，代價仍寫在門外那些等待者的表情上。許霏打開筆記本，決定只記下真正能夠驗證的事情。見證人在訪談中提到「道歉」，小隊照實記錄，卻沒有直接認同。證物清單上有「繃帶」，卻沒有人解釋這個細節為何重要。工作檔案為「交易」設了獨立項目，許霏詢問是誰查證過。文希在舊筆記裡找到關於「文明」的記載，但紙上沒有可信的日期。

A new message reached Wen Xi while the group was still checking its first result. Wen Xi found the rehearsal had been scheduled by a different manager than the advertised host role. Xu Fei refused to celebrate too early. They had learned that two records could repeat the same lie when both came from a single office. Before treating the discovery as independent evidence, the group searched for a witness who had nothing to gain by agreeing with them. The old manual told its readers to congratulate, and Wen Xi wanted to know who had written that rule. The disputed notice used the word critical, but its author had not signed the page. The working file contained a separate entry for explosion, and Xu Fei asked who had verified it. Xu Fei asked whether the team could forbid under the safety rules; the answer was not yet clear.

小隊還在核對第一項結果時，文希就收到新的消息。文希發現這場彩排由另一名經理安排，不是宣傳主持人的那位。許霏不肯太早慶祝。他們已學到，兩份紀錄如果來自同一辦公室，也可能只是重複同一個謊言。要把發現視為獨立證據前，大家先去尋找一個不會因附和他們而得利的見證人。舊手冊要求讀者「恭賀」，文希想知道這條規則由誰制定。有爭議的通知使用「關鍵的／批判的」一詞，作者卻沒有在頁面簽名。工作檔案為「爆炸」設了獨立項目，許霏詢問是誰查證過。許霏詢問小隊能否依安全規則「禁止」，答案仍不明朗。

The easiest choice would have made Xu Fei popular for a single evening. Manager Lin insisted the host had agreed to disappear for dramatic effect. Wen Xi reminded the group that their decision would still matter tomorrow, after the applause and anger faded. Nobody could offer a guarantee that every person would approve. They could, however, make the reasons public and leave a safe way for a witness to disagree. The old manual told its readers to illustrate, and Wen Xi wanted to know who had written that rule. One witness promised to install if conditions improved, but Xu Fei would not treat hope as evidence. An inspector described the situation as mere; Xu Fei wanted measurements instead of adjectives. The report called the arrangement pregnant, a judgment the group refused to accept without proof.

最省事的選擇，或許能讓許霏當晚受到歡呼。林經理堅稱主持人已同意為劇情效果消失。文希提醒大家，掌聲與怒氣消失後，決定明天仍然會造成影響。沒有人能保證所有人都滿意，但至少可以公開理由，並留下見證人安全表達反對的管道。舊手冊要求讀者「圖解說明」，文希想知道這條規則由誰制定。一名見證人答應條件改善就會「安裝」，許霏卻不能把希望當成證據。檢查員把情況形容為「僅僅的」，許霏卻想要數據，而不是形容詞。報告說這項安排「懷孕的」，小隊不願在沒有證據前接受此判斷。

In an ordinary mystery game, players agreed to misunderstand one another for an evening. Outside the script, that agreement did not give the host permission to place real people in danger. Xu Fei enjoyed clues and surprises, which was why the distinction mattered to her. Wen Xi kept the building's safety information beside the fictional case file. The two folders could inform one another, but they could never be allowed to become the same document.

普通推理遊戲裡，玩家同意在一個晚上互相猜疑；劇本外的這份同意，卻不代表主持人可以讓真人置身危險。許霏喜歡線索與反轉，因此更重視這個差別。文希把建築安全資料放在虛構案件旁邊。兩份檔案可以互相提供線索，卻不能混為一談。

They agreed to test a limited part of the plan before touching the main system. Xu Fei checked the signed contract and found no consent to be locked in any room. Xu Fei assigned roles according to practical skill rather than confidence. Wen Xi recorded each step, including the moments when someone had to stop. If the trial failed, the notes would explain why. If it worked, the notes would help the next group repeat it without depending on a hero who happened to be present. Someone had marked the warning prosperous; Wen Xi checked whether that description was accurate. The evidence list included resolution, although nobody had explained why that detail mattered. The working file contained a separate entry for revolution, and Xu Fei asked who had verified it. Wen Xi found a reference to settlers in the old notes, but the page gave no reliable date.

他們同意先做小範圍測試，不直接碰主要系統。許霏檢查簽署合約，發現從未同意被鎖在任何房間。許霏依照實際專長分派工作，不看誰說話最有自信。文希記錄每個步驟，連必須停止的時刻也不漏掉。如果測試失敗，筆記能說明原因；如果成功，下一批人也能按紀錄重做，不必依賴恰好在場的英雄。有人把警告標成「繁榮的」，文希則查證這種描述是否準確。證物清單上有「決議」，卻沒有人解釋這個細節為何重要。工作檔案為「革命」設了獨立項目，許霏詢問是誰查證過。文希在舊筆記裡找到關於「遷居者」的記載，但紙上沒有可信的日期。

The result was smaller than the promise they had hoped to make, but it was real. The technical room's ventilation report showed a fault that could not wait for the game to end. Wen Xi closed the notebook only after marking the unresolved questions. Xu Fei was tired of victories that depended on hiding their cost. This one could be examined in daylight. And the detail they had not solved was already pointing toward the next part of the investigation. A witness mentioned surgeon during the interview, so the team recorded the statement without endorsing it. The disputed notice used the word suspicious, but its author had not signed the page. The working file contained a separate entry for tomb, and Xu Fei asked who had verified it. Wen Xi found a reference to website in the old notes, but the page gave no reliable date.

結果比原先希望宣布的成果小，卻是真的。技術室通風報告顯示故障，不能等遊戲結束才處理。文希直到標出所有未解問題，才闔上筆記本。許霏早已厭倦靠隱瞞代價換來的勝利；這次的結果可以在日光下接受查證。那個尚未解開的細節，也已指向調查的下一步。見證人在訪談中提到「外科醫師」，小隊照實記錄，卻沒有直接認同。有爭議的通知使用「可疑的」一詞，作者卻沒有在頁面簽名。工作檔案為「墓穴」設了獨立項目，許霏詢問是誰查證過。文希在舊筆記裡找到關於「網站」的記載，但紙上沒有可信的日期。
