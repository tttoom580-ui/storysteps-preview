A Victim Made of Wax

劇本殺之夜，兇手拒絕登場 · 第 3 / 25 章 · 3000–4000 · 英中對照 · 校訂中

Rain, dust, and distant voices made the manor candlelit drawing room feel smaller than it was. Xu Fei arrived with a plan, then saw a detail that the plan had not accounted for. The supposed victim lay beneath a blanket, but the body was a wax model with a serial number under its wrist. The difference between guessing and checking suddenly seemed enormous. A person who sounded brave could still be mistaken; a person who admitted uncertainty could still save the group. Xu Fei chose the second kind of courage. One witness promised to assign if conditions improved, but Xu Fei would not treat hope as evidence. The working file contained a separate entry for calorie, and Xu Fei asked who had verified it. Xu Fei asked whether the team could concentrate under the safety rules; the answer was not yet clear. A witness mentioned context during the interview, so the team recorded the statement without endorsing it.

雨聲、灰塵與遠處的人聲，讓莊園燭光客廳顯得格外狹窄。許霏帶著計畫抵達，卻看見計畫沒有考慮到的細節。所謂受害者躺在毛毯下，身體其實是蠟像，手腕下刻著序號。猜測和查證的差別突然變得很大。聽起來勇敢的人也可能犯錯，敢承認不確定的人反而可能救下整個團隊。許霏決定選擇後者。一名見證人答應條件改善就會「指派」，許霏卻不能把希望當成證據。工作檔案為「卡路里」設了獨立項目，許霏詢問是誰查證過。許霏詢問小隊能否依安全規則「專注」，答案仍不明朗。見證人在訪談中提到「脈絡」，小隊照實記錄，卻沒有直接認同。

Wen Xi brought the next piece of information on a sheet that had been folded several times. Wen Xi discovered the serial number belonged to a theater prop rented from another venue. The team compared its date with what they had witnessed for themselves. One line was persuasive; another contradicted the first. Xu Fei asked for the original source instead of selecting whichever version made their work easier. Even a genuine clue could be dangerous when removed from its context. One witness promised to defend if conditions improved, but Xu Fei would not treat hope as evidence. The working file contained a separate entry for ferry, and Xu Fei asked who had verified it. Wen Xi found a reference to housework in the old notes, but the page gave no reliable date. Someone had marked the warning incredible; Wen Xi checked whether that description was accurate.

文希把下一份資料帶來，紙張已被折過好幾次。文希查出序號屬於從另一家場館租來的舞台道具。小隊把上面的日期與親眼所見逐項比較。一行文字很有說服力，下一行卻與它矛盾。許霏沒有挑比較方便的版本，而是要求查看原始來源。即使是真的線索，一旦脫離脈絡也可能造成危險。一名見證人答應條件改善就會「支持／辯護」，許霏卻不能把希望當成證據。工作檔案為「渡輪」設了獨立項目，許霏詢問是誰查證過。文希在舊筆記裡找到關於「家務」的記載，但紙上沒有可信的日期。有人把警告標成「驚人的」，文希則查證這種描述是否準確。

An argument broke out before anyone could announce a solution. Manager Lin warned players against examining props because it would spoil the mystery. Xu Fei did not win it with a long speech. Instead, the team drew two columns: what the record established and what remained uncertain. Manager Lin challenged their interpretation, which was uncomfortable but useful. A plan that collapsed under one honest question was not ready to protect anyone outside the room. The evidence list included inspiration, although nobody had explained why that detail mattered. The working file contained a separate entry for insult, and Xu Fei asked who had verified it. The report called the arrangement intermediate, a judgment the group refused to accept without proof. A witness mentioned lifeguard during the interview, so the team recorded the statement without endorsing it.

尚未有人宣布解法，爭論便已爆發。林經理警告玩家不能仔細檢查道具，否則會破壞懸疑。許霏沒有用長篇演說壓倒對方，而是在紙上分成兩欄：紀錄已證實的事，以及仍然不確定的事。林經理質疑他們的解讀，雖然令人不快，卻也有用。禁不起一次誠實提問的計畫，還不能拿去保護門外的人。證物清單上有「靈感」，卻沒有人解釋這個細節為何重要。工作檔案為「侮辱」設了獨立項目，許霏詢問是誰查證過。報告說這項安排「中間版本」，小隊不願在沒有證據前接受此判斷。見證人在訪談中提到「救生員」，小隊照實記錄，卻沒有直接認同。

The most attractive solution was often the one that made the loudest accusation. Xu Fei had learned that a neat accusation could also hide the simplest possibility: the story's premise was wrong. Wen Xi asked each player to repeat one fact they had checked personally. Nobody was forced to perform fear for an audience. The silence that followed gave their investigation a kind of honesty the scripted thunder could never create.

最吸引人的解答，往往是指控最響亮的那一個。許霏知道整齊漂亮的指控，也可能掩蓋最簡單的可能：故事前提本來就錯了。文希請每個玩家各說一項親自查核的事實。沒有人必須為觀眾表演恐懼。隨後的沉默，比劇本安排的雷聲更加誠實。

Nothing in the trial worked exactly as predicted. Xu Fei recorded that no human death had yet been verified and let each player inspect the model. Xu Fei insisted on keeping the unsuccessful measurements instead of replacing them with cleaner numbers. That choice annoyed Manager Lin, who wanted a quick announcement. But the failure established a boundary they had only guessed at before. A useful boundary was better than a promise built on wishful thinking. The disputed notice used the word luxurious, but its author had not signed the page. An inspector described the situation as maximum; Xu Fei wanted measurements instead of adjectives. Wen Xi found a reference to pace in the old notes, but the page gave no reliable date. A witness mentioned percentage during the interview, so the team recorded the statement without endorsing it.

這次測試沒有一項完全照預測發展。許霏記錄目前沒有證據顯示真人死亡，並讓每位玩家檢查蠟像。許霏堅持留下不成功的測量值，不用好看的數字替換。林經理急著發公告，因此相當不滿；然而失敗讓他們找到了先前只能猜測的限制。清楚的界線，總比建立在一廂情願上的承諾可靠。有爭議的通知使用「豪華的」一詞，作者卻沒有在頁面簽名。檢查員把情況形容為「最大容量」，許霏卻想要數據，而不是形容詞。文希在舊筆記裡找到關於「步調」的記載，但紙上沒有可信的日期。見證人在訪談中提到「百分比」，小隊照實記錄，卻沒有直接認同。

When evening arrived, the immediate danger had changed shape rather than vanished. Inside the blanket lay an unsigned accusation naming the ninth guest as murderer. Xu Fei gathered the others and repeated only the facts they could support. Some people would need more time. Others had already decided to leave. Neither choice made anyone less worthy of protection. The next step belonged to those who would live with its consequences, not merely to the person speaking loudest. The evidence list included photography, although nobody had explained why that detail mattered. The working file contained a separate entry for priority, and Xu Fei asked who had verified it. Xu Fei asked whether the team could proceed under the safety rules; the answer was not yet clear. A witness mentioned raisin during the interview, so the team recorded the statement without endorsing it.

傍晚到來時，眼前的危險只是改變形態，並未完全消失。毛毯裡卻藏著沒有簽名的指控，說第九名客人是兇手。許霏召集同伴，只重述有證據支持的事。有人還需要時間，也有人已決定離開；這兩種選擇都不會使任何人失去受保護的資格。下一步應由承擔後果的人決定，而不是交給聲音最大的人。證物清單上有「攝影」，卻沒有人解釋這個細節為何重要。工作檔案為「優先事項」設了獨立項目，許霏詢問是誰查證過。許霏詢問小隊能否依安全規則「繼續進行」，答案仍不明朗。見證人在訪談中提到「葡萄乾」，小隊照實記錄，卻沒有直接認同。
