A Door No One Crossed

劇本殺之夜，兇手拒絕登場 · 第 6 / 25 章 · 3000–4000 · 英中對照 · 校訂中

Rain, dust, and distant voices made the manor's secured kitchen passage feel smaller than it was. Xu Fei arrived with a plan, then saw a detail that the plan had not accounted for. A door sensor recorded no human passage during the interval when the murderer supposedly escaped. The difference between guessing and checking suddenly seemed enormous. A person who sounded brave could still be mistaken; a person who admitted uncertainty could still save the group. Xu Fei chose the second kind of courage. Someone had marked the warning conservative; Wen Xi checked whether that description was accurate. The evidence list included consultant, although nobody had explained why that detail mattered. The working file contained a separate entry for consumer, and Xu Fei asked who had verified it. Wen Xi found a reference to dye in the old notes, but the page gave no reliable date.

雨聲、灰塵與遠處的人聲，讓莊園安全管制廚房通道顯得格外狹窄。許霏帶著計畫抵達，卻看見計畫沒有考慮到的細節。所謂兇手逃離的時段，門禁感測器沒有記錄任何真人通過。猜測和查證的差別突然變得很大。聽起來勇敢的人也可能犯錯，敢承認不確定的人反而可能救下整個團隊。許霏決定選擇後者。有人把警告標成「保守的」，文希則查證這種描述是否準確。證物清單上有「顧問」，卻沒有人解釋這個細節為何重要。工作檔案為「消費者」設了獨立項目，許霏詢問是誰查證過。文希在舊筆記裡找到關於「染料」的記載，但紙上沒有可信的日期。

Wen Xi brought the next piece of information on a sheet that had been folded several times. Wen Xi checked the sensor's batteries and found no interruption or missing samples. The team compared its date with what they had witnessed for themselves. One line was persuasive; another contradicted the first. Xu Fei asked for the original source instead of selecting whichever version made their work easier. Even a genuine clue could be dangerous when removed from its context. The old manual told its readers to enclose, and Wen Xi wanted to know who had written that rule. The evidence list included goods, although nobody had explained why that detail mattered. The working file contained a separate entry for grace, and Xu Fei asked who had verified it. The report called the arrangement habitual, a judgment the group refused to accept without proof.

文希把下一份資料帶來，紙張已被折過好幾次。文希檢查感測器電池，沒有發現中斷或缺樣。小隊把上面的日期與親眼所見逐項比較。一行文字很有說服力，下一行卻與它矛盾。許霏沒有挑比較方便的版本，而是要求查看原始來源。即使是真的線索，一旦脫離脈絡也可能造成危險。舊手冊要求讀者「封入」，文希想知道這條規則由誰制定。證物清單上有「貨品」，卻沒有人解釋這個細節為何重要。工作檔案為「從容」設了獨立項目，許霏詢問是誰查證過。報告說這項安排「習慣性的」，小隊不願在沒有證據前接受此判斷。

An argument broke out before anyone could announce a solution. Manager Lin suggested the killer had supernatural powers, although the game rules promised a realistic solution. Xu Fei did not win it with a long speech. Instead, the team drew two columns: what the record established and what remained uncertain. Manager Lin challenged their interpretation, which was uncomfortable but useful. A plan that collapsed under one honest question was not ready to protect anyone outside the room. Someone had marked the warning ignorant; Wen Xi checked whether that description was accurate. One witness promised to invest if conditions improved, but Xu Fei would not treat hope as evidence. A written instruction told the staff to isolate before the deadline, but nobody had logged the result. Wen Xi found a reference to linen in the old notes, but the page gave no reliable date.

尚未有人宣布解法，爭論便已爆發。林經理暗示兇手可能有超能力，儘管遊戲規則承諾真實推理。許霏沒有用長篇演說壓倒對方，而是在紙上分成兩欄：紀錄已證實的事，以及仍然不確定的事。林經理質疑他們的解讀，雖然令人不快，卻也有用。禁不起一次誠實提問的計畫，還不能拿去保護門外的人。有人把警告標成「不了解的」，文希則查證這種描述是否準確。一名見證人答應條件改善就會「投資」，許霏卻不能把希望當成證據。書面指示要求工作人員在期限前「隔離」，卻沒有人記下結果。文希在舊筆記裡找到關於「亞麻布」的記載，但紙上沒有可信的日期。

The most attractive solution was often the one that made the loudest accusation. Xu Fei had learned that a neat accusation could also hide the simplest possibility: the story's premise was wrong. Wen Xi asked each player to repeat one fact they had checked personally. Nobody was forced to perform fear for an audience. The silence that followed gave their investigation a kind of honesty the scripted thunder could never create.

最吸引人的解答，往往是指控最響亮的那一個。許霏知道整齊漂亮的指控，也可能掩蓋最簡單的可能：故事前提本來就錯了。文希請每個玩家各說一項親自查核的事實。沒有人必須為觀眾表演恐懼。隨後的沉默，比劇本安排的雷聲更加誠實。

Nothing in the trial worked exactly as predicted. Xu Fei preserved the original sensor log and ruled out that doorway as an escape route. Xu Fei insisted on keeping the unsuccessful measurements instead of replacing them with cleaner numbers. That choice annoyed Manager Lin, who wanted a quick announcement. But the failure established a boundary they had only guessed at before. A useful boundary was better than a promise built on wishful thinking. A witness mentioned luxury during the interview, so the team recorded the statement without endorsing it. The evidence list included microscope, although nobody had explained why that detail mattered. The working file contained a separate entry for occupation, and Xu Fei asked who had verified it. Wen Xi found a reference to orientation in the old notes, but the page gave no reliable date.

這次測試沒有一項完全照預測發展。許霏封存原始感測紀錄，排除那道門作為逃跑路徑。許霏堅持留下不成功的測量值，不用好看的數字替換。林經理急著發公告，因此相當不滿；然而失敗讓他們找到了先前只能猜測的限制。清楚的界線，總比建立在一廂情願上的承諾可靠。見證人在訪談中提到「奢華」，小隊照實記錄，卻沒有直接認同。證物清單上有「顯微鏡」，卻沒有人解釋這個細節為何重要。工作檔案為「職業」設了獨立項目，許霏詢問是誰查證過。文希在舊筆記裡找到關於「職前說明」的記載，但紙上沒有可信的日期。

When evening arrived, the immediate danger had changed shape rather than vanished. A second, smaller sensor had been removed from the service corridor. Xu Fei gathered the others and repeated only the facts they could support. Some people would need more time. Others had already decided to leave. Neither choice made anyone less worthy of protection. The next step belonged to those who would live with its consequences, not merely to the person speaking loudest. A witness mentioned rage during the interview, so the team recorded the statement without endorsing it. The evidence list included stereo, although nobody had explained why that detail mattered. An inspector described the situation as timid; Xu Fei wanted measurements instead of adjectives. Xu Fei asked whether the team could tremble under the safety rules; the answer was not yet clear.

傍晚到來時，眼前的危險只是改變形態，並未完全消失。服務走廊的另一個小型感測器竟遭拆除。許霏召集同伴，只重述有證據支持的事。有人還需要時間，也有人已決定離開；這兩種選擇都不會使任何人失去受保護的資格。下一步應由承擔後果的人決定，而不是交給聲音最大的人。見證人在訪談中提到「盛怒」，小隊照實記錄，卻沒有直接認同。證物清單上有「立體聲」，卻沒有人解釋這個細節為何重要。檢查員把情況形容為「膽小的」，許霏卻想要數據，而不是形容詞。許霏詢問小隊能否依安全規則「顫抖」，答案仍不明朗。
