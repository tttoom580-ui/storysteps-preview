At the dining room evidence table, the first sign of trouble was not a scream or an alarm. It was a small disagreement about something everyone believed they understood. The eight players each supplied an independently checkable account of the moment the fake murder occurred. Xu Fei listened to the surrounding voices before making a judgment. Rumors traveled faster than careful questions, especially when the public wanted a simple villain. The team needed a record that would remain useful even if their favorite explanation proved false. The evidence list included breed, although nobody had explained why that detail mattered. The working file contained a separate entry for capital, and Xu Fei asked who had verified it. Xu Fei asked whether the team could circulate under the safety rules; the answer was not yet clear. A witness mentioned code during the interview, so the team recorded the statement without endorsing it.

在餐廳證物桌，麻煩不是從尖叫或警報開始，而是從一件大家自認了解的小事產生分歧。八名玩家都提供可獨立查核的假謀殺發生時刻行蹤。許霏先聽周遭人的說法，沒有急著下判斷。群眾想找到簡單的壞人時，謠言總比仔細提問傳得快。即使最喜歡的推測最後被推翻，小隊也需要留下仍然有用的紀錄。證物清單上有「滋生」，卻沒有人解釋這個細節為何重要。工作檔案為「資本／資本主義」設了獨立項目，許霏詢問是誰查證過。許霏詢問小隊能否依安全規則「流傳／傳閱」，答案仍不明朗。見證人在訪談中提到「程式碼」，小隊照實記錄，卻沒有直接認同。

The oldest document on the table looked less important than the newest screen. That appearance was misleading. Wen Xi lined up receipts, camera times and wristwatch notes without forcing them into one perfect story. Wen Xi read the handwritten notes aloud while Xu Fei checked the sequence of events. Their different skills mattered more than either person's rank. When they found a contradiction, they preserved both versions. Destroying an inconvenient record would only make the next investigation harder. The evidence list included consequence, although nobody had explained why that detail mattered. An inspector described the situation as dominant; Xu Fei wanted measurements instead of adjectives. The report called the arrangement dusty, a judgment the group refused to accept without proof. Someone had marked the warning eventual; Wen Xi checked whether that description was accurate.

桌上最舊的文件看起來不如最新螢幕重要，但那只是表面。文希排列收據、攝影時間與手錶筆記，沒有硬拼成完美故事。文希念出手寫備註，許霏逐項核對事件順序。此時兩人的不同專長，比誰的職位更高還重要。找到矛盾後，他們把兩個版本都保存下來。毀掉一份不方便的資料，只會讓下一次調查更加困難。證物清單上有「後果」，卻沒有人解釋這個細節為何重要。檢查員把情況形容為「占主導的」，許霏卻想要數據，而不是形容詞。報告說這項安排「積灰的」，小隊不願在沒有證據前接受此判斷。有人把警告標成「最終的」，文希則查證這種描述是否準確。

By afternoon, the discovery had reached the people who could lose the most. Manager Lin argued that a mystery required one person to be lying. Manager Lin offered a quick settlement, but it required the group to stop asking questions. Xu Fei considered the frightened faces around the room. It would be easy to sound heroic and demand that everyone take the same risk. Instead, each person was allowed to describe what they were prepared to do and what they would refuse. One witness promised to expand if conditions improved, but Xu Fei would not treat hope as evidence. An inspector described the situation as graceful; Xu Fei wanted measurements instead of adjectives. Wen Xi found a reference to indication in the old notes, but the page gave no reliable date. A witness mentioned invasion during the interview, so the team recorded the statement without endorsing it.

到了下午，這項發現已傳到最可能蒙受損失的人耳中。林經理說既然是推理遊戲，就必定有人撒謊。林經理提出快速和解，條件卻是所有人不准再追問。許霏看著房內不安的臉孔。此時說些英雄話、要求大家冒一樣的風險很容易；他們卻讓每個人自己說明願意做什麼，以及拒絕承擔什麼。一名見證人答應條件改善就會「擴展」，許霏卻不能把希望當成證據。檢查員把情況形容為「優美的」，許霏卻想要數據，而不是形容詞。文希在舊筆記裡找到關於「跡象」的記載，但紙上沒有可信的日期。見證人在訪談中提到「入侵」，小隊照實記錄，卻沒有直接認同。

Every witness had seen the evening from a different angle. Xu Fei did not expect their memories to fit perfectly, and she refused to treat an honest difference as a confession. Wen Xi marked where the stories overlapped and where the rooms themselves offered independent evidence. Their questions grew less theatrical and more useful. The missing chair was still important, but the safety of the missing worker mattered far more.

每名見證人都從不同角度經歷那個晚上。許霏沒期待記憶完美吻合，也不把誠實的差異當成自白。文希標出說法重疊處，以及房間本身能提供獨立證據的地方。他們的問題不再那麼戲劇化，卻更有用。空椅子依然重要，但失蹤工作者的安全遠比它重要。

The plan looked simple on paper and complicated the moment people began moving. Xu Fei explained that a script's promise did not establish the existence of a real killer. A delay forced Xu Fei to choose between keeping the schedule and checking the safety condition again. The group checked again. They had lost time, but a false success would have cost them much more. Wen Xi marked the revised sequence where everyone could see it. The evidence list included marathon, although nobody had explained why that detail mattered. A written instruction told the staff to negotiate before the deadline, but nobody had logged the result. Wen Xi found a reference to persuasion in the old notes, but the page gave no reliable date. A witness mentioned rebel during the interview, so the team recorded the statement without endorsing it.

計畫寫在紙上很簡單，人一動起來就變得複雜。許霏說劇本承諾有兇手，不代表現實真有兇手。一次延誤讓許霏必須在守住時間表與重新檢查安全條件之間選擇。小隊決定再查一次。雖然損失時間，虛假的成功卻可能造成更大代價。文希把修改後的順序標在所有人都看得見的位置。證物清單上有「馬拉松」，卻沒有人解釋這個細節為何重要。書面指示要求工作人員在期限前「協商」，卻沒有人記下結果。文希在舊筆記裡找到關於「勸說」的記載，但紙上沒有可信的日期。見證人在訪談中提到「反叛者」，小隊照實記錄，卻沒有直接認同。

The streets outside the dining room evidence table were quieter, but quiet was not proof of safety. Their eight accounts left a blank space exactly where the ninth character should have stood. Xu Fei checked that everyone knew how to ask for help before the group separated. They had gained one answer and inherited two new responsibilities. Tomorrow's work would require patience from people who had every reason to be angry. For the first time that day, the team had a direction worth following. The disputed notice used the word solar, but its author had not signed the page. The working file contained a separate entry for telegraph, and Xu Fei asked who had verified it. Xu Fei asked whether the team could tickle under the safety rules; the answer was not yet clear. A witness mentioned wreck during the interview, so the team recorded the statement without endorsing it.

餐廳證物桌外的街道變安靜了，但安靜不代表安全。八份證詞留下的空白，正是第九角色應在的位置。許霏先確認大家知道如何求助，才讓小隊分開。他們得到一個答案，也多了兩份責任。明天的工作需要那些本來就有理由憤怒的人保持耐心。至少在這一天結束時，小隊終於有了一個值得追查的方向。有爭議的通知使用「太陽能」一詞，作者卻沒有在頁面簽名。工作檔案為「電報」設了獨立項目，許霏詢問是誰查證過。許霏詢問小隊能否依安全規則「輕碰」，答案仍不明朗。見證人在訪談中提到「毀壞」，小隊照實記錄，卻沒有直接認同。
