A grandfather clock jumped backward seven minutes before the scripted scream was heard. Nobody could afford to confuse a confident explanation with a reliable observation. They had made that mistake before, and the cost was still visible in the people waiting outside. Xu Fei opened the notebook and decided to write down what could actually be tested. A written instruction told the staff to accompany before the deadline, but nobody had logged the result. Wen Xi found a reference to attraction in the old notes, but the page gave no reliable date. A witness mentioned diagram during the interview, so the team recorded the statement without endorsing it. The evidence list included diplomat, although nobody had explained why that detail mattered.

這一天從樓上肖像畫廊開始。許霏原以為只是處理例行工作，房間卻靜得像剛傳出壞消息。劇情尖叫前，一座老爺鐘往後跳了七分鐘。他們不能把說得肯定的解釋誤當成可靠觀察。大家以前犯過這種錯，代價仍寫在門外那些等待者的表情上。許霏打開筆記本，決定只記下真正能夠驗證的事情。書面指示要求工作人員在期限前「陪同」，卻沒有人記下結果。文希在舊筆記裡找到關於「賣點」的記載，但紙上沒有可信的日期。見證人在訪談中提到「圖表」，小隊照實記錄，卻沒有直接認同。證物清單上有「外交官」，卻沒有人解釋這個細節為何重要。

A new message reached Wen Xi while the group was still checking its first result. Wen Xi checked the clock mechanism and found its hands controlled by the room's lighting schedule. Xu Fei refused to celebrate too early. They had learned that two records could repeat the same lie when both came from a single office. Before treating the discovery as independent evidence, the group searched for a witness who had nothing to gain by agreeing with them. The working file contained a separate entry for draft, and Xu Fei asked who had verified it. The report called the arrangement essential, a judgment the group refused to accept without proof. Someone had marked the warning experimental; Wen Xi checked whether that description was accurate. One witness promised to flee if conditions improved, but Xu Fei would not treat hope as evidence.

小隊還在核對第一項結果時，文希就收到新的消息。文希檢查機械裝置，發現指針受燈光排程控制。許霏不肯太早慶祝。他們已學到，兩份紀錄如果來自同一辦公室，也可能只是重複同一個謊言。要把發現視為獨立證據前，大家先去尋找一個不會因附和他們而得利的見證人。工作檔案為「草案」設了獨立項目，許霏詢問是誰查證過。報告說這項安排「必要的」，小隊不願在沒有證據前接受此判斷。有人把警告標成「實驗性的」，文希則查證這種描述是否準確。一名見證人答應條件改善就會「逃離」，許霏卻不能把希望當成證據。

Wen Xi reminded the group that their decision would still matter tomorrow, after the applause and anger faded. Nobody could offer a guarantee that every person would approve. They could, however, make the reasons public and leave a safe way for a witness to disagree. The working file contained a separate entry for germs, and Xu Fei asked who had verified it. Wen Xi found a reference to greeting in the old notes, but the page gave no reliable date. A witness mentioned hesitation during the interview, so the team recorded the statement without endorsing it. 最省事的選擇，或許能讓許霏當晚受到歡呼。林經理堅稱時間是真的，藉此指控站在畫廊旁的玩家。文希提醒大家，掌聲與怒氣消失後，決定明天仍然會造成影響。沒有人能保證所有人都滿意，但至少可以公開理由，並留下見證人安全表達反對的管道。工作檔案為「病菌」設了獨立項目，許霏詢問是誰查證過。文希在舊筆記裡找到關於「問候」的記載，但紙上沒有可信的日期。見證人在訪談中提到「猶豫」，小隊照實記錄，卻沒有直接認同。證物清單上有「地平線」，卻沒有人解釋這個細節為何重要。

In an ordinary mystery game, players agreed to misunderstand one another for an evening. Outside the script, that agreement did not give the host permission to place real people in danger. Xu Fei enjoyed clues and surprises, which was why the distinction mattered to her. Wen Xi kept the building's safety information beside the fictional case file. The two folders could inform one another, but they could never be allowed to become the same document.

普通推理遊戲裡，玩家同意在一個晚上互相猜疑；劇本外的這份同意，卻不代表主持人可以讓真人置身危險。許霏喜歡線索與反轉，因此更重視這個差別。文希把建築安全資料放在虛構案件旁邊。兩份檔案可以互相提供線索，卻不能混為一談。

They agreed to test a limited part of the plan before touching the main system. Xu Fei wrote down the wall clock, wristwatch and camera times separately. Xu Fei assigned roles according to practical skill rather than confidence. Wen Xi recorded each step, including the moments when someone had to stop. If the trial failed, the notes would explain why. If it worked, the notes would help the next group repeat it without depending on a hero who happened to be present. A written instruction told the staff to loosen before the deadline, but nobody had logged the result. The report called the arrangement magnificent, a judgment the group refused to accept without proof. A witness mentioned manual during the interview, so the team recorded the statement without endorsing it. The evidence list included mechanic, although nobody had explained why that detail mattered.

他們同意先做小範圍測試，不直接碰主要系統。許霏分開記錄掛鐘、手錶與攝影機時間。許霏依照實際專長分派工作，不看誰說話最有自信。文希記錄每個步驟，連必須停止的時刻也不漏掉。如果測試失敗，筆記能說明原因；如果成功，下一批人也能按紀錄重做，不必依賴恰好在場的英雄。書面指示要求工作人員在期限前「鬆開」，卻沒有人記下結果。報告說這項安排「壯麗的」，小隊不願在沒有證據前接受此判斷。見證人在訪談中提到「操作手冊」，小隊照實記錄，卻沒有直接認同。證物清單上有「機械技師」，卻沒有人解釋這個細節為何重要。

The result was smaller than the promise they had hoped to make, but it was real. The clocks disagreed, but the power controller had kept an independent log. Wen Xi closed the notebook only after marking the unresolved questions. Xu Fei was tired of victories that depended on hiding their cost. This one could be examined in daylight. And the detail they had not solved was already pointing toward the next part of the investigation. An inspector described the situation as outstanding; Xu Fei wanted measurements instead of adjectives. Wen Xi found a reference to recreation in the old notes, but the page gave no reliable date. 結果比原先希望宣布的成果小，卻是真的。幾座鐘互相矛盾，電源控制器卻保留了獨立紀錄。文希直到標出所有未解問題，才闔上筆記本。許霏早已厭倦靠隱瞞代價換來的勝利；這次的結果可以在日光下接受查證。那個尚未解開的細節，也已指向調查的下一步。檢查員把情況形容為「傑出的」，許霏卻想要數據，而不是形容詞。文希在舊筆記裡找到關於「休閒活動」的記載，但紙上沒有可信的日期。見證人在訪談中提到「衛星」，小隊照實記錄，卻沒有直接認同。一名見證人答應條件改善就會「加強」，許霏卻不能把希望當成證據。
