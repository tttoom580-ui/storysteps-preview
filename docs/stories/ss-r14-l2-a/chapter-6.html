帶鎖箱子的訪客

A Guest With a Locked Box

把風還給山｜讓風箏教我修仙 · 第 6 / 25 章 · 1000–2000 · 英中對照 · 校訂中

用互動版查詞、朗讀與收藏

The spring trial began with an unexpected affair: a council officer arrived carrying a locked box. Children put away their badminton rackets and gathered beside the cabbage beds. The officer, Vale, was clever and spoke politely, but he called the broken wind ribbon a crisis that only his company could solve. Lan did not argue with his fine clothes or his smooth voice. She asked why he had brought a box to a public test. Vale said he represented a culture of progress and would show a new engine for moving air. Qiu reminded him that the morning had been reserved for comparing measurements, not selling machines. Vale smiled without opening the box.

春季測試一開始就出了意外：議會官員維爾帶著上鎖的箱子來了。孩子們收起羽毛球拍，聚集在高麗菜苗床旁。維爾很聰明，說話也有禮，卻把壞掉的風向絲帶說成只有他的公司才能解決的危機。蘭不與他的華服或口才爭論，只問為何公開測試要帶鎖箱。他說自己代表進步文化，將展示一臺能推動空氣的新引擎。秋守園人提醒，今天早晨是比對測量，不是賣機器。維爾微笑，卻沒有打開箱子。

The news caused excitement among visitors, including a retired fireman who had worked on the southern shore. He said a strong machine could help in an emergency, but machines could also push smoke the wrong way. “Before we rush forth,” he said, “someone must tell us where the air goes.” Lan drew two lines on the ground, one for her paper wing and one for the old ribbon. Tao described how the garden was supposed to govern its trials: anyone could watch, every result had to be written down, and no human being should stand under a flying frame. The fireman offered to watch the safety line.

消息讓訪客興奮起來，其中有位曾在南岸工作的退休消防員。他說強力機器能在緊急時幫忙，也可能把濃煙吹向錯誤方向。「在我們往前衝之前，」他說，「得先知道空氣會往哪裡走。」蘭在地上畫兩條線，一條給紙翼，一條給舊絲帶。陶解釋藥圃的試驗管理原則：任何人都能觀看、每次結果都要記錄、不能有人站在飛行框架下方。消防員主動幫忙看守安全線。

An individual grower from the neighboring hill opened her journal and asked if she could participate. She was their nearest neighbor and had noticed weak leaves too. Lan made room for her beside the board. Vale complained that a public discussion took too long. He took a soft pillow from his box and placed his engine on it as if the device were a treasure. Then he said the device should remain in his control even after the garden paid for it. That sounded less like a purchase than a prison for the garden’s choices. Lan asked him to put the condition in writing before anyone signed anything.

鄰山一位個別種植者打開日誌，問能否參與。她是最近的鄰居，也發現葉片衰弱。蘭在木板旁替她留位置。維爾抱怨公開討論太費時間，從箱裡拿出柔軟的枕墊，把引擎像寶物般放上去，接著說即使藥圃付錢，機器的控制權仍應留在他的公司。這聽起來不像購買，更像把藥圃的選擇關進牢籠。蘭請他在任何人簽字前先把條件寫下來。

After the test, the fireman showed Lan an old photograph from the shore. The picture showed workers carrying sandbags during a storm, their socks soaked through, but smiling because the people behind them were safe. He said the best repair was sometimes one that kept workers from needing to be brave at all. Lan looked at the little wings drying on the bench. The newest one had turned correctly in four places, but had caught briefly against a branch in the fifth. She recorded both facts. Vale's engine had not yet been tested in the garden. People could admire it, but admiration was not a result.

測試後消防員拿出一張南岸舊照片。畫面裡工人在暴風雨中搬沙包，襪子全濕，仍因身後的人平安而微笑。他說最好的修理，有時是讓工人不必冒險逞英雄。蘭望向長椅上晾乾的小紙翼。最新一片在四個地方轉得正確，卻在第五處短暫勾到樹枝。她把兩件事都記錄下來。維爾的引擎尚未在藥圃測試。人們可以欣賞它，卻不能把欣賞當成結果。

Qiu called a meeting after sunset. He would not accept the engine contract until the public terms were clear, and he would not give Lan a free pass merely because he liked her work. Everyone had two days to test the wings, the ribbon, and a small borrowed fan in the same conditions. Lan was tired, but the decision felt fair. She walked past the sleeping children and their shoes under the door. Somewhere beyond the garden, people still believed the mountain spirit alone decided which plants lived. She hoped the next two days would replace some of that fear with questions they could answer.

日落後秋守園人召開會議。他不會在公開條件清楚前接受引擎契約，也不會只因喜歡蘭的工作就給她特權。大家有兩天時間，在相同條件下測試紙翼、舊絲帶與借來的小風扇。蘭累了，卻覺得決定公平。她經過睡著的孩子與門下的鞋子。藥圃外仍有人相信只有山靈能決定植物生死。她希望接下來兩天，能用可回答的問題替代一些恐懼。

After the meeting, the fireman asked Lan how she would know when to end a public test. She showed him her notes: stop if a frame cracks, if someone enters the flight space, or if the wind rises beyond the measured range. He suggested adding one more reason, when the workers were too tired to make careful choices. Lan wrote it down. She had thought of danger as something outside the team: storms, broken roofs, or bad machines. Now she understood that exhaustion could be just as real. She sent the apprentices home early and stayed only long enough to lock the tools away.

會後消防員問蘭，怎樣知道公開測試該結束。她拿出筆記：框架裂開、有人進入飛行區、風勢超出測量範圍時就停止。他建議再加一項：工人累到無法謹慎判斷時。蘭記下。她以前把危險想成來自外面，例如風暴、破屋頂或壞機器，如今懂得疲勞也是真實風險。她提早讓見習生回家，自己只留下鎖好工具便離開。
