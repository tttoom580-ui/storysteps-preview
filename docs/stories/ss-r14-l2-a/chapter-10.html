第一次公開讀數

The First Public Reading

把風還給山｜讓風箏教我修仙 · 第 10 / 25 章 · 1000–2000 · 英中對照 · 校訂中

The garden was active from sunrise, but Lan's biggest concern was the difference between one good day and a full growing cycle. She placed a flag on the far side of the field and measured the distance between the flag and each paper wing. The ground was not flat, so Tao helped her set every frame at the same height. Workers walked forward carefully, counting their steps. They hoped to gain a clear picture of the air without moving the young plants. The marks on Lan's board indicated that one sheltered corner stayed still while the open rows changed direction several times. That difference was what they needed to understand.

日出後藥圃十分忙碌，但蘭最擔心的是：一天的好結果不等於整個生長週期都成功。她在田地另一端插旗，量出旗幟與每片紙翼的距離。地面不平，陶幫她把每個架子調到同樣高度。工人小心往前走，數著步數，希望不碰幼苗便能得到清楚的氣流圖。蘭板上的記號顯示，有個遮蔽角落一直無風，開闊苗床卻幾度改變風向。這正是他們要理解的差別。

Qiu reminded everyone that the local law required written notice before a public trial took place. Lan opened her notebook and read the safety rules. A sudden gust might occur, so visitors had to remain beyond the line. Vale offered riches if the council chose his company's fan, but he still had not shown its test results. One parent asked why the student list recorded sex at all. Lan said the team needed names for responsibility, not private details unrelated to the work, and she removed that field from the form. The crowd fell into silence for a moment. Then people began discussing the measurements rather than the students' personal lives.

秋守園人提醒大家，當地法律要求公開試驗前必須書面公告。蘭打開筆記本讀安全規則：可能突然起強風，訪客必須待在線外。維爾提出若議會選他的風扇就有豐厚利益，卻仍未展示測試結果。一名家長問為何學生名單必須記錄性別。蘭說團隊需要姓名來分配責任，不需要與工作無關的私人資料，便把那欄刪去。人群安靜片刻，隨後開始談測量，而非學生的私生活。

A single visitor in a dark suit asked to walk beside the workers. Lan offered a safe track that followed the outer edge of the valley. The visitor agreed, then lost his wallet near a bench. Nobody treated the missing wallet as proof of theft. Tao asked the owner where he had last opened it, and the group searched the path together. They found it under a folded map, exactly where the visitor had stopped to write a note. The small event took ten minutes, but Lan was glad they had not turned an accident into blame. A public trial was also a chance to show how a community behaved under pressure.

一位穿深色西裝的訪客想跟工人走近測試區。蘭安排一條沿山谷外緣的安全步道。訪客同意後卻在長椅附近丟了皮夾。沒有人把遺失皮夾當成有人偷竊的證據。陶問物主最後在哪裡打開過皮夾，大家一起沿路尋找，最後在他停下寫字時放的折疊地圖下找到。這件小事花了十分鐘，蘭卻很慶幸沒有人把意外變成責備。公開試驗也是社群如何面對壓力的一次示範。

The readings were not as strong as the visitors had hoped. The paper wings turned clearly in eight places, gave weak signals in three, and failed in one. Vale said a machine could have made every wing move, but Tao asked whether that would measure the natural wind or simply the force of a machine. Sera thanked him for the question. Her young plants needed fresh air, not an impressive show. Lan posted the twelve results in order, including a sketch of the failed location. Some visitors looked disappointed. Others studied the drawing and offered to return after the next rain to see whether the pattern changed.

讀數沒有訪客期待的那麼亮眼。紙翼在八處清楚轉動，三處訊號微弱，一處失敗。維爾說機器能讓每片翼都動，陶便問那測到的是自然風，還是機器自身推動的力。莎拉感謝這個問題：幼苗需要新鮮空氣，不需要漂亮表演。蘭依順序公布十二項結果，包括失敗地點草圖。有些訪客失望，另一些則仔細研究，表示下次雨後願意再來觀察模式是否改變。

After the meeting, Lan and Tao carried their boards to the quiet work shed. They did not call the public test a final victory. They had gained permission to continue, and the council had delayed the fan purchase until an independent review. That was enough for one day. Lan looked back at the seedlings. A few leaves stood taller than last week, but she knew she could not yet credit her wings for that change. She wrote a question beneath the result: “What else could explain this?” Tao saw it and laughed softly. “That,” he said, “is the sort of question that keeps a good tool honest.”

會後蘭和陶把木板搬回安靜工棚。他們沒有把公開測試說成最終勝利。議會准許繼續試驗，並將購買風扇延後到獨立審查後，這樣一天就夠了。蘭回望幼苗，幾片葉子比上週直挺，但她知道還不能把變化全歸功於紙翼。她在結果下寫：「還有什麼可能造成這個現象？」陶看到後輕笑。「這種問題，」他說，「才會讓好工具保持誠實。」

The last line of the public report did not indicate that everything was safe. It indicated where the workers still needed better information. Qiu read the line twice and asked Lan to make it larger before the next meeting. A visitor said a report should end on a happy note. Lan replied that the good news was the chance to improve without hiding facts. Later she and Tao walked along the marked path and counted the small wings moving above the beds. They were tired, but the garden looked different to them now: less like a mystery to defeat and more like a place to understand.

公開報告最後一行沒有指出一切安全，而是指出工人還需要哪些資訊。秋守園人讀了兩遍，請蘭下次開會前把字放大。有訪客說報告應以快樂句子收尾。蘭回答，好消息是大家能不隱藏事實地持續改進。稍後她與陶沿標記小路走，數著苗床上方轉動的小紙翼。兩人很累，但眼裡的藥圃已不同：不再是要打敗的謎，而是需要理解的地方。
