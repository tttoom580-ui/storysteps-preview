不花靈石的地圖

A Map That Costs Nothing

把風還給山｜讓風箏教我修仙 · 第 5 / 25 章 · 1000–2000 · 英中對照 · 校訂中

用互動版查詞、朗讀與收藏

Lan carried a burden heavier than her tool box: the director wanted a result before the yearly college visitors arrived. Two firms offered to sell the garden double-sized magic fans, but the expense would use most of its planting money. Tao said each fan had a special feature, yet the sellers could not explain how it would function beside the crooked walls. Lan asked a fellow apprentice to test a smaller paper wing first. A visiting lady from the teaching college watched with interest. “A tool is only good if it solves the real problem,” she said. Lan wrote the sentence on her board, under the cost of a single bamboo strip.

蘭肩上的負擔比工具箱還重：主任希望每年學院訪客抵達前就看到成果。兩家公司提議賣給藥圃雙倍大的魔法風扇，但費用將耗掉大部分播種經費。陶說每臺風扇都有特殊功能，銷售員卻說不清它在歪牆邊如何運作。蘭請同伴先測試較小的紙翼。一位來自教學學院的女士興味盎然地看著。「能解決真正問題的工具才是好工具，」她說。蘭把這句話寫在木板上，旁邊記著一根竹條的成本。

A perfect map would be lovely, but production had to begin with what they could check. They seldom chose a single plan without a selection test. This afternoon they made three wings, each from the same paper but with a different string. At the playing field, children stopped their soccer game to watch. The children had more spirit than patience, and one boy swallowed his words when Sera asked him to wait behind the line. Lan lent her old sweater to a younger helper who felt cold. Then she counted each rise and fall of the wings. The smallest one turned most clearly when the air moved past the north wall.

完美地圖固然好，但製作必須從能查證的事情開始。他們很少未經挑選測試就決定唯一方案。下午團隊用同樣的紙和不同繩子做了三片翼。運動場上的孩子停下足球比賽圍觀，精神十足卻缺乏耐性。一名男孩被莎拉要求等在線後，話到嘴邊又吞回去。蘭把自己的舊毛衣借給覺得冷的年輕幫手，接著記錄每片翼的升降次數。最小的那片在北牆風道附近轉得最清楚。

Before leaving, everyone helped sweep the grass and collect torn paper. A stranger had spread a frightening story that a terrorist was hiding among the workers, though no one had given any evidence. The gate keeper asked people not to repeat the rumor. He handed Lan a tissue when ink from her pen stained her fingers. Tao showed her how to upload a drawing of the readings to the college notice page so distant growers could see the test. Lan checked that no faces or private names appeared in the picture. She wanted help from others, not a crowd watching someone else’s embarrassment.

離開前大家一起掃乾淨草地，收集撕破的紙。有陌生人散播恐怖分子藏在工人中的嚇人傳聞，卻沒有人提出證據。守門人請大家別再傳謠言。蘭的手指沾了墨，他遞給她一張面紙。陶示範如何把讀數草圖上傳到學院公告頁，讓遠方種植者也看得見測試。蘭確認照片沒有拍到任何人的臉或私人姓名。她想得到其他人的幫助，而不是引來一群人看別人的難堪。

That evening the director returned with a paper about the expensive fans. He said the garden would lose its place in the spring festival if the machines were not ordered tomorrow. Lan asked whether the festival rules truly required new equipment. The director did not answer at once. Tao opened the public notice and found only a demand for reliable observations. Lan asked for one more day to show their result. She did not promise the wings could solve every problem. She promised only to show how they worked, what they cost, and where they failed. The director agreed to a trial at sunrise, but he warned that the council would be watching.

傍晚主任帶著昂貴風扇的文件回來。他說若明天不訂機器，藥圃就會失去春祭名額。蘭問祭典規則是否真的要求新設備，主任一時沒有回答。陶翻開公開公告，看到的要求只有「可靠觀察」。蘭請求多一天展示結果。她沒有保證紙翼能解決所有問題，只承諾說明它如何工作、費用多少、在哪裡失效。主任同意日出進行試驗，也提醒議會會在場觀看。

Late that night Lan returned to the empty field to pack the wings away. She found the children had left small stones beside the test line, one for each successful flight. There were only seven, far fewer than the total attempts, but she liked the honest count. She moved the stones into a bowl and set a note beside them: “Success does not mean nothing failed.” She needed sleep, yet she stood for a moment in the quiet grass and thought about the workers waiting for healthier plants. Tomorrow was not about defeating the director. It was about giving the garden one useful choice that did not require a fortune.

深夜蘭回到空地收好紙翼，發現孩子在測試線旁留下小石子，每成功飛行一次就放一顆。只有七顆，遠少於嘗試次數，但蘭喜歡這誠實的計數。她把石子移進碗裡，旁邊寫著：「成功不代表沒有失敗。」她需要睡覺，卻仍在寂靜草地站了片刻，想著等待植物恢復健康的工人。明天的重點不是擊敗主任，而是讓藥圃多一個不必花大錢的有用選擇。

Before dawn Tao checked the notice page once more. Three distant growers had written questions beneath the drawing. One asked about the weight of the paper wing, another about wet weather, and the third about how a child could stand safely nearby. Lan answered only what they had actually tested and marked the other questions for later work. She felt the garden becoming connected to people she had never met. That connection carried responsibility as well as hope. At sunrise she walked to the field with her notebook, ready to show both the good results and the places where the wings had failed.

天亮前陶又查看公告頁。有三名遠方種植者在圖下留言，一人問紙翼重量，一人問雨天情況，第三人問孩子如何安全站在附近。蘭只回答實際測過的部分，其餘標成待辦。她感覺藥圃正和從未見面的人連結，這份聯繫帶來希望，也帶來責任。日出時，她帶著筆記本走向田地，準備展示好結果與紙翼失敗之處。
