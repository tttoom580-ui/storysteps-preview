日曆上的四個記號

Four Marks on the Calendar

把風還給山｜讓風箏教我修仙 · 第 7 / 25 章 · 1000–2000 · 英中對照 · 校訂中

用互動版查詞、朗讀與收藏

At dawn Lan asked whether the test could advance without adding more tools. Tao said each being in the garden experienced the wind differently, including birds and people. On their calendar he drew four marks for the times when fog usually arrived. The mountain climate had changed during the last few years, so an old date might not be enough. Lan put her paper wings inside a covered box to contain them until the morning air became steady. A traveler from the desert watched her work and said the valley's cold mist was harder for him to understand than hot sand. Lan listened instead of laughing at him.

黎明蘭問是否能不增加工具就推進測試。陶說園裡每個生命感受到的風都不同，包括鳥與人。他在日曆上畫四個記號，標出霧常到來的時間。近幾年山地氣候已有變化，舊日期未必足夠。蘭先把紙翼放進加蓋木箱，等晨風穩定。一位來自沙漠的旅人看她工作，說山谷冷霧比熱沙更令他難懂。蘭沒有笑他，而是認真聽完。

Their visitor was humble and asked for a clear explanation of the experiment. Lan described the ideal test: two wings made in the same way, set at the same height, with just one thing changed between them. She offered to introduce him to Sera, who knew the plants better than anyone. As they walked toward her beds, two apprentices jogged past carrying a mop and a bucket. They had spilled water beside a worktable. The traveler stepped aside so they could clean it before anyone fell. “The safest work often looks ordinary,” he said. Lan agreed, and wrote that observation beside the test rules.

旅人態度謙遜，請蘭清楚解釋實驗。蘭說理想測試應該用兩片做法相同、高度相同的紙翼，每次只改變一個條件。她要把旅人介紹給最懂植物的莎拉。走向苗床時，兩名見習生提著拖把和水桶小跑而過，因工作桌旁灑了水，正急著擦乾。旅人讓出路，讓他們先清理，以免有人滑倒。「最安全的工作常看起來很平凡，」他說。蘭同意，把這句話寫進試驗規則。

The afternoon test lasted through one long period of light rain. Their principle was simple: record failure as carefully as success. On a rocky path, Tao took the first step while Lan held the string; when the wing dropped, neither tried to pull it upward by force. They marked the place, moved back, and repeated the trial from the safe ground. Thus their progress was slow but clear. On the board they drew a turtle beside the careful tests, not as an insult but as a reminder that speed was not the same as good work. A child added a tiny smile to the turtle's face.

下午測試持續了一整段細雨時間。原則很簡單：對失敗的記錄要與成功同樣慎重。走在多石的小徑上，陶先踏出一步，蘭抓住繩子。紙翼落下時，兩人都沒有硬拉，而是標記位置、退回安全地，再重複試驗。因此進度雖慢，卻很清楚。大家在木板上畫一隻烏龜，代表小心測試，不是嘲笑速度慢。孩子替烏龜加上一個小小的笑臉。

After supper the apprentices washed their hands and put their toothbrushes away. Qiu asked Lan to tell him the most important result of the day. She could have said the kite had worked in six places, but instead she pointed to the seventh, where the string had pulled against a roof corner. She had not found a safe way to place it there. Qiu nodded. An honest test could not claim victory in a place it had not examined. He asked the team to keep that corner closed to visitors until a worker with proper equipment could inspect it. The decision disappointed some children but protected them.

晚餐後見習生洗手並收好牙刷。秋守園人要蘭說出今日最重要的結果。她本可報告風箏在六個地點成功，卻指向第七個地點：繩子被屋角拉住，她還沒找到安全的架設方法。秋守園人點頭。誠實的測試不能在未檢查的地方宣稱勝利。他要求團隊暫時禁止訪客靠近，直到合格工人攜設備檢查屋角。這令部分孩子失望，卻保護了他們。

Before sleep, Lan placed four pebbles beside the dates on the calendar. Each pebble stood for a question the team had answered, and every empty square stood for work that remained. She realized that a repairer's job was not to promise comfort to everyone. It was to make careful choices that others could test. Outside, the night wind touched the closed windows and moved the tree leaves. For once she did not rush to record it. Some observations could wait for the morning, when the instruments and the people holding them would be ready.

睡前蘭把四顆小石頭放在日曆日期旁，每顆代表團隊已回答的一個問題，每個空格則代表尚待完成的工作。她明白修理人的職責不是向所有人保證安心，而是做出能被他人查驗的謹慎選擇。屋外夜風吹過關好的窗，也吹動樹葉。這次她沒有急著記錄。某些觀察可以等到早晨，等工具與持工具的人都準備好了再做。

The visiting traveler asked whether the workers would jog back to the garden if another bell sounded at night. Lan answered no. They had written a plan for emergencies, and running through an unlit path could create another problem. Two trained keepers would first check the signal from safe places and call for help if needed. The traveler said he liked a plan that did not depend on heroes arriving at the perfect moment. Lan smiled. She had begun this project because she wanted to be useful, not because she wished to become a hero. The difference mattered more each day.

來訪旅人問夜裡若再次響鈴，工人是否要小跑回藥圃。蘭說不。團隊已寫緊急計畫，黑暗中奔跑可能製造更多問題。先由兩位受訓守園人從安全位置確認訊號，必要時再求援。旅人說他喜歡不必仰賴英雄剛好及時出現的計畫。蘭笑了。她開始這項工作是因為想做有用的人，不是想當英雄。這差別一天比一天重要。
