A Line in the Wind

把風還給山｜讓風箏教我修仙 · 第 2 / 25 章 · 1000–2000 · 英中對照 · 校訂中

The next morning Tao measured the angle of the roof shadows while Lan tied new strings. She could appreciate his patience, though his notes were so small she had to lean close to read them. Their first task was to avoid the narrow path above the old barber shop. A loose sign there had nearly struck a visitor the night before. The barber let them stand in his yard instead. He used to cut the hair of wind keepers, and he remembered that their hats always blew away at one corner of the road. Lan placed a paper arrow there. Its point turned sharply toward the north, where the main ribbon had never moved.

隔天早晨，陶測量屋頂陰影的角度，蘭則重新綁繩。她很欣賞他的耐心，雖然他的筆記小到必須靠近才能看清。第一項工作是避開舊理髮店上方的窄路，前晚有塊鬆動招牌差點打到訪客。理髮師讓他們改站在自家院子。他從前替測風人剪髮，記得那些人的帽子總在路口某處被吹走。蘭在那裡放了紙箭頭，箭頭突然轉向北方，而主要絲帶從沒動過。

The barber brought out a wooden board marked with arrows and a date. A deer had once entered the yard during a storm, and a dove had sheltered under the eaves. The board came from an older edition of the garden rules, which said birds and animals could show where wind passed through trees. Lan did not claim that animals always knew the answer. They were living creatures, not machines. Still, the marks on the board matched the new arrow. The old records existed for a reason: people had watched the same corner for many years. Lan copied the dates without taking the board away from its owner.

理髮師拿出一塊木板，上面標著箭頭與日期。從前暴風雨時有鹿走進院子，鴿子躲在屋簷下。木板來自較舊版本的藥圃規則，寫著鳥獸的行為有時能顯示風吹過樹林的路徑。蘭沒有聲稱動物一定知道答案；牠們是活物，不是機器。然而板上的記號與新箭頭吻合。舊紀錄之所以存在，是因為許多人多年來觀察同一個角落。她抄下日期，沒有把木板帶走。

At the noon market a huge lantern hung above a booth run by a minority group from the northern farms. Their lantern was designed to turn gently when a draft passed through it. A young girl pointed out a dark spot on one side where the cloth had been repaired. She said it turned poorly after rain. Lan tried the light paper frame and watched it wobble. A beautiful object could still give a poor reading. The girl asked whether Lan was building a magic lantern. “No,” Lan replied. “I am building a way for everyone to ask the same question and compare what they see.”

正午市集有一盞巨大燈籠掛在北方農場少數族群的攤位上。這盞燈籠設計成微風吹過便緩緩旋轉。一名女孩指出燈布一側有修補後留下的暗點，下雨後轉動就不順。蘭試著推動輕紙燈架，看見它搖晃。漂亮的物品仍可能給出錯誤讀數。女孩問蘭是否在製作魔法燈。蘭說：「不，我要做一個讓大家能問同樣問題，再比較所見結果的方法。」

The market organization had a president, Madam Yu, who listened with careful interest. She said the northern families had found the cold wind painful for their oldest workers. Their particular problem was not a lack of wind but sudden changes near the high wall. Yu offered to let the team place a small kite in the public garden for three evenings. In return, they had to share every result, including failures. Tao agreed immediately. Lan asked for a written copy so nobody would forget the limits of the plan. Yu smiled and signed it. This was the first time the project had gained a partner beyond Cloudtip Garden.

市集組織的會長余女士認真聽完。她說北方家庭的年長工人常被刺骨寒風折磨，特定問題不是無風，而是高牆旁風向突然改變。余女士同意讓小隊連續三晚在公共花園放小風箏，交換條件是所有結果都要公開，包括失敗。陶立刻同意，蘭則要求寫下約定，以免有人忘記計畫的界線。余女士笑著簽名。這是計畫第一次得到雲岬藥圃以外的合作夥伴。

Before they left, the barber offered a cup of sour berry tea and his old textbook of simple repairs. He had written the word truth on the inside cover and crossed out several pages of guesses. Lan liked that honesty. A book was useful because readers could challenge it, not because it looked old. The three of them followed the paper arrow back up the hill. At the top, the wind moved through a narrow opening in the trees and curled around the seed house. Their next experiment would need two kites of the same size. Only then could they tell whether the changing readings came from the air or from their own tools.

離開前，理髮師端來酸莓茶，還借給蘭一本簡易修理教科書。扉頁上寫著「真相」，其中幾頁不可靠的猜測被劃掉。蘭欣賞這種誠實。書有用，是因為讀者能質疑它，而不是因為它很古老。三人循著紙箭頭上山，發現風穿過樹間狹口後，在種子屋旁打轉。下次實驗需要兩只同樣大小的風箏，才能判斷讀數差異來自空氣，還是他們自己的工具。

The barber's daughter offered to show them another corner where people often lost their hats. She did not ask for money, only that they stop calling her a little child who could know nothing. Lan agreed and followed her at a careful distance. The paper arrow moved there as well, but the girl noticed that it changed direction only when the market carts passed. Tao wrote down the detail. The carts blocked the road for a moment and pushed air toward the wall. The team had almost taken a short human event for a steady natural wind. Lan thanked the girl for seeing what they had missed.

理髮師的女兒帶他們去另一個人們常被吹掉帽子的角落。她不收錢，只希望大家別再說小孩子什麼都不懂。蘭同意，與她保持安全距離走去。紙箭頭也在那裡轉動，但女孩發現只有市集車經過時方向才改變。陶記下細節：車暫時擋住道路，把空氣推向牆。團隊差點把短暫的人為事件當成持續自然風。蘭感謝她看見大家忽略的事情。
