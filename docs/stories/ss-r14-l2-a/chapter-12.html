紀錄上的空白

An Empty Space in the Record

把風還給山｜讓風箏教我修仙 · 第 12 / 25 章 · 1000–2000 · 英中對照 · 校訂中

用互動版查詞、朗讀與收藏

A visiting customer tried to blame Lan when he saw a blank square in the public chart. He had come from the capital to learn how the countryside garden handled wind. Lan explained that the square meant no one had safely measured that corner yet. It did not mean the air was calm. The customer said the empty space looked like a danger they were hiding. Nearby, a dentist who volunteered with the local clinic asked him to listen before making a judgment. She pointed to the direction of the roof and explained why an unsafe climb would prove nothing. Lan was grateful for the support, but she still wanted to fill the gap safely.

一位從首都來的顧客看見公開圖表上的空白格，便責怪蘭。他是想了解鄉村藥圃如何處理風向而來。蘭解釋空白代表那個角落尚未安全測量，不表示沒有風。顧客說空格像是在隱藏危險。附近一位自願協助診所的牙醫請他先聽完再判斷。她指向屋頂方向，說明冒險攀爬不能證明任何事。蘭感謝她支持，仍希望用安全方法補上缺口。

At the noon stand children bought fries and pudding, but Lan hardly tasted her own lunch. The ink on her old chart had run in the rain. Tao proposed a simple measurement system using dry stones and string marks instead of paper alone. Sera said the team should regard the workers' time as a real cost, even when nobody asked for payment. Lan agreed and wrote a second chart that showed hours of work beside each result. The customer returned and read it slowly. “I was scared that you were hiding something,” he admitted. Lan answered that fear was easier to manage when people could ask direct questions.

正午攤位的孩子買薯條和布丁，蘭卻幾乎沒嘗自己的午餐。舊圖上的墨被雨水暈開了。陶提議除了紙張，還要用乾石頭和繩結做簡單的測量系統。莎拉說工人的時間也是成本，即使沒有人要求報酬也該重視。蘭同意，重新製表，在每項結果旁加上工時。顧客回來慢慢閱讀。「我原本怕你們隱瞞事情，」他承認。蘭回答，能直接提問，恐懼就比較容易處理。

A sudden rain drove the visitors indoors. They left muddy slippers near the door, and Sera poured tea from a heavy teapot. The dentist asked whether the council would settle the disagreement by choosing the cheapest plan. Lan shook her head. Cost mattered, but so did safety, repair time, and whether ordinary workers could understand the tools. Tao spread the chart across the table and invited people to suggest missing questions. They added one about injuries and one about storms. A plan that saved money today but hurt a worker next month was not truly cheap. By dusk the list of questions had grown longer than the list of answers.

突如其來的雨把訪客趕進室內。他們把沾泥拖鞋留在門口，莎拉用沉重茶壺倒茶。牙醫問議會是否會只憑最便宜的價格解決爭議。蘭搖頭。成本重要，安全、維修時間與普通工人能否理解工具也重要。陶在桌上攤開圖表，邀大家提出遺漏的問題。眾人加上傷害與風暴兩項。今天省錢、下月卻讓工人受傷的方案，不算真正便宜。到傍晚，問題清單比答案還長。

When the rain stopped, the dentist walked with Lan to the closed corner of the roof. They stayed on the safe path and looked up. A loose beam stood between the edge and the measuring point. The dentist said a trained builder should examine it, not a student with a kite. Lan wrote down the need for an expert visit and the likely delay. She was disappointed but relieved. For a week she had imagined filling that square herself. Now she understood that accepting a limit could be part of doing careful work. She drew a clear border around the empty place rather than coloring it in.

雨停後牙醫陪蘭沿安全路走到封閉屋角。他們沒有攀爬，只抬頭看。一根鬆動梁木擋在屋緣與測量點之間。牙醫說應讓受訓的建築工人檢查，不該讓學生帶風箏上去。蘭記下需要專家到場及可能延誤。她既失望又安心。一週來她總想親自填滿那格，現在明白接受限制也是謹慎工作的一部分。她替空格畫上清楚邊界，而非擅自塗滿。

Qiu later reviewed the board and thanked the visitors who had questioned it. The garden had not lost face by showing an empty space. It had gained a way to decide what to do next. Vale was present for the review. He looked at the blank square and suggested placing his engine there instead of sending anyone to the roof. Tao pointed out that a machine on the ground still could not tell them whether the beam overhead was safe. Vale had no answer. The council agreed to fund a proper inspection before any equipment was installed in that corner.

秋守園人後來檢查圖表，感謝提出質疑的訪客。藥圃展示空白沒有丟臉，反而知道下一步該做什麼。維爾也在場，提議把他的引擎放在那裡，免得有人上屋頂。陶指出地面的機器仍無法證明頭上的梁木安全。維爾答不出來。議會同意先出資做正式檢查，再決定那處是否安裝設備。

The customer from the capital returned before leaving town. He said he had come to respect the workers after seeing how they answered difficult questions. Lan thanked him, but asked whether he would help by telling others not to mistake an empty space for a secret. He promised to share the chart exactly as it stood, with the unfinished corner shown clearly. Tao gave him a copy and wrote the date beside it. Lan did not expect every visitor to become a supporter. She only hoped people would leave knowing the difference between a missing answer and a dishonest one. This visitor seemed to understand.

從首都來的顧客離城前又回來。他說看見工人如何回答難題，開始尊重他們。蘭道謝，也請他幫忙告訴別人，別把空白誤認成祕密。他答應原樣分享圖表，清楚保留尚未完成的角落。陶給他副本，寫上日期。蘭不期待每位訪客都成為支持者，只希望大家能分辨尚無答案與不誠實。這名訪客似乎懂了。
