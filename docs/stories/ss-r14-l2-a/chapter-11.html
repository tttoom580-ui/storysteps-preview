好風的價錢

The Price of a Good Breeze

把風還給山｜讓風箏教我修仙 · 第 11 / 25 章 · 1000–2000 · 英中對照 · 校訂中

Lan was still a beginner in the eyes of the council, but she knew someone would have to charge the garden for the new engine. Vale tried to chase that question away by showing a polished case and calling it a gift. Lan wanted to emphasize their real goal: keeping the seedlings alive without putting the garden into debt. He opened the case. Inside lay a gold plate shaped like a grape leaf. It was beautiful, but beauty did not explain the importance of the machine. Tao noticed that the handle was made of iron, much heavier than the wooden frame Vale had described. He quietly wrote down the difference.

在議會眼中蘭仍是初學者，但她知道新引擎一定有人要向藥圃收費。維爾展示拋光盒子，稱機器為禮物，試圖迴避價錢問題。蘭想強調真正目標：讓幼苗存活，又不讓藥圃負債。他打開盒子，裡面有片葡萄葉形狀的金牌，十分漂亮，卻無法說明機器的重要性。陶注意到把手是鐵製的，比維爾描述的木架重得多，便悄悄記下差異。

In the kitchen a jar of berry jam stood beside a chipped mug. Sera explained that the old growers held different views about religion and mountain spirits, but they all agreed that good food needed care. Vale said their discussions could not shoot down his machine's value. Lan answered that she was not trying to destroy anything; she simply wanted a fair social test. The council secretary asked whether their own wing had been a success. Lan pointed to the written results: eight clear readings, three weak ones, and one failure. Those numbers were true even if she wished they were better. The secretary nodded and asked Vale for numbers of his own.

廚房裡一罐莓果醬放在有缺口的杯子旁。莎拉說老園丁對宗教和山靈有不同看法，卻都同意好食物需要細心照料。維爾說這些討論不能推翻他機器的價值。蘭答她不是要摧毀任何東西，只想進行公平的公共測試。議會書記問她的紙翼是否成功。蘭指著紀錄：八次清楚、三次微弱、一次失敗。即使她希望更好，那些數字仍是真實的。書記點頭，要求維爾也交出自己的數字。

The garden's usual rule was to trust a result only after another person could repeat it. Vale showed a single shining picture instead of a record. Lan noticed a narrow trap in his offer: the engine was free for the first month, but the garden would pay for every repair after that. When she asked about the cost, he changed the subject to how important the festival would be for young students. A mother in the audience held up the contract and read the repair section aloud. The hall grew quiet. Nobody accused Vale of a crime. They merely asked him to explain a condition he had wanted them to ignore.

藥圃的一般規則是，必須讓別人重複得到結果，才能信任它。維爾只展示一張閃亮圖片，沒有測試紀錄。蘭發現提案藏著陷阱：引擎首月免費，此後每次維修都要由藥圃付錢。她問價格，他卻改談春祭對學生有多重要。一位母親舉起契約，大聲讀出維修條款。大廳安靜了。沒有人指控維爾犯罪，只請他解釋原本希望大家忽略的條件。

After the hearing Qiu invited the younger workers to write questions for both teams. A boy asked whether the paper wings could last through heavy rain. Lan answered that the current paper would fail and showed how she planned to replace it with thin cloth. A girl asked if the big engine could be repaired without special parts. Vale admitted that only his company kept those parts. The questions were simple, but their answers showed how different the two plans were. Tao suggested placing both offers on one public board, with costs, limits, and expected work written in plain language. Everyone would be able to read it before voting.

聽證後秋守園人請年輕工人替雙方寫問題。男孩問紙翼能否撐過大雨，蘭回答目前的紙會失效，並展示改用薄布的方法。女孩問大引擎能否不靠特殊零件修理，維爾承認零件只由他的公司保存。問題很簡單，答案卻顯示兩個方案的差別。陶建議把雙方提案放在同一塊公開板上，以淺白語言寫出成本、限制和所需工作，讓大家在投票前讀懂。

In the late light Lan found Vale standing alone beside the broken ribbon. He asked whether she enjoyed making him look foolish. She said no; she had been afraid he would persuade people to sign without reading. For a moment he seemed tired rather than proud. He told her his company had promised him a reward if the garden ordered the engines. Lan understood the pressure but did not change her decision. She invited him to join the open test under the same rules as everyone else. He did not answer, but he did not walk away immediately either.

夕光下蘭看見維爾獨自站在壞絲帶旁。他問她是否喜歡讓自己難堪。她說不喜歡，只是害怕他說服大家不看條款就簽字。他看起來忽然疲倦，不再那麼自信。他說公司答應，若藥圃訂購引擎便給他獎勵。蘭理解壓力，卻不改變決定。她邀他在相同規則下參加公開測試。他沒有回答，但也沒有立刻走開。

On the way home the mother who had read the contract stopped Lan by the gate. She was worried that speaking at the hearing might anger people who wanted the engines. Lan told her that every visitor had the right to ask about a public purchase, and the garden would record questions without publishing home addresses. The mother looked relieved. She had asked because her daughter worked in the seed house and would use whatever tool the council chose. Lan understood. The numbers on her board were connected to real people with jobs, families, and choices of their own. She promised to keep that in mind.

回家路上，讀出契約條款的母親在門口叫住蘭。她擔心聽證時發言會惹怒想買引擎的人。蘭說每位訪客都有權問公共採購問題，藥圃會記錄提問，但不公開住址。母親放鬆了。她提問是因女兒在種子屋工作，議會選的工具將由女兒使用。蘭明白，板上的數字連著有工作、家庭與自身選擇的人。她答應記住。
