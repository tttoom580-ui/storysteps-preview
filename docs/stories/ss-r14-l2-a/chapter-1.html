沒有法術的風箏

The Kite Without a Spell

把風還給山｜讓風箏教我修仙 · 第 1 / 25 章 · 1000–2000 · 英中對照 · 校訂中

用互動版查詞、朗讀與收藏

Lan climbed the narrow approach to Cloudtip Garden with a wooden box under her arm. She stopped on the balcony of the seed house, where a blue ribbon should have moved above a stone base. Today it hung straight down. The growers said the mountain spirit had gone away, but Lan had a different belief: a ribbon could fail even when the wind was strong. She was twenty and had no magic. She repaired ordinary tools for the garden, and she had noticed a loose nail in the ribbon frame. Below her, the young plants bent in one direction while the ribbon pointed nowhere. Someone had trusted a broken sign.

蘭抱著木箱沿狹窄的山路走向雲岬藥圃。她停在種子屋的陽臺，石製底座上方原本應該飄動的藍絲帶，今天卻筆直垂下。種植者說山靈離開了，蘭卻相信：即使風很強，絲帶也可能因故失靈。二十歲的她沒有法術，只負責修理藥圃的普通工具，早就注意到絲帶架上一枚鬆動的釘子。下方的幼苗朝同一方向傾斜，絲帶卻沒有指向任何地方。有人把壞掉的標記當成了事實。

Keeper Qiu asked Lan to bring him a better sign before the spring planting. It was a difficult challenge, and the apprentice gardeners did not hide their doubts. One said she should deny the problem and let the senior mages decide. Lan answered that they could develop a simple test without using any magic. She invited Tao, a quiet wind student, to discuss the difference between wind and a picture of wind. He placed two loose feathers on the table. One rolled toward the door while the other stayed still because a cup blocked it. “That is our first lesson,” he said. “A measurement depends on where we place the tool.”

秋守園人請蘭在春播前做出更可靠的風向標記。這是艱難的挑戰，見習園丁也不掩飾懷疑。有人說她應該否認問題，把決定交給高階術士。蘭回答，不用魔法也能設計簡單測試。她邀請安靜的測風學徒陶討論真正的風與風的圖像有何不同。陶把兩根羽毛放上桌，一根滾向門口，另一根因杯子擋住而靜止。「這是第一課，」他說，「測量結果取決於工具放在哪裡。」

The lower field held a test plot for the whole nation, not only the students from Cloudtip. For instance, seeds from a dry western town had been planted beside seeds from the rainy coast. A male visitor named Fen had come to record the work, but he expected to see beautiful spells. Lan showed him the damaged frame instead. “Please record what is really here,” she said. Fen watched as she tied a strip of paper to a light piece of bamboo. Her first kite rose only as high as her shoulder. It fell at once, yet it turned in the same direction as the leaves. That small agreement made the others look again.

下方田地是供全國參考的試驗圃，不只屬於雲岬的學生。例如，乾燥西城的種子就種在多雨海岸的種子旁。名叫芬的男性訪客來記錄工作，原先期待看見漂亮的法術。蘭卻先讓他看損壞的架子。「請記下真正發生的事，」她說。芬看著她把紙條綁上輕竹片。第一只風箏只飛到她肩膀高便落地，卻跟葉子朝相同方向轉動。這個微小的一致，讓其他人重新觀察。

The old wind-wheel operator came down from the upper path and examined the kite. He said a secondary measure could be useful, but only if the students understood its limits. A seesaw in the children’s yard helped Lan demonstrate his point. Even when one end moved quickly, the other end moved according to the same balance, not according to anyone’s wishes. She marked the wind speed on a board with four simple lines. Tao then moved the kite to three places and found three different results. The garden could no longer accept a single ribbon as proof for the whole slope. Qiu gave Lan permission to keep testing until dusk.

舊風輪的操作員沿上方小徑下來檢查風箏。他說第二套測量方法很有用，但學生必須懂得它的限制。孩子庭院裡的翹翹板幫蘭示範了這一點：一端即使快速移動，另一端仍受平衡關係影響，不會照任何人的願望行動。她用四道簡單的線在木板上記錄風速。陶把風箏移到三個地點，得到三種不同結果。藥圃再也不能把一條絲帶當成整個山坡的證明。秋守園人准許她試到黃昏。

The day grew windy, and the bamboo kite pulled hard on its string. A family arriving from the underground market line called their freight tunnel a subway. They had come for the spring tradition of planting the first seed. Lan asked them to wait while she checked the landing area. If the kite struck a child, the test would have harmed the people it meant to help. She brought it down slowly, then made a new rule: no flying above visitors, and no announcement about a safe wind until two places had been checked. The ribbon on the stone frame was still broken. For the first time, however, the garden knew why it could not be trusted.

風漸漸大起來，竹風箏用力拉扯線繩。從地下市集貨運隧道來的一家人把那條線稱為地下鐵，他們是來參加春季播下第一粒種子的傳統。蘭請他們稍候，好讓她檢查落地區。如果風箏撞到孩子，測試就傷害了原本想幫助的人。她慢慢收下風箏，訂出新規則：不能在人群上方放飛；沒有比對兩個地點，就不能宣布風向安全。石架上的絲帶仍壞著，但藥圃第一次知道它為何不可信。

At the gate that night, Lan found a small note left by a child who had watched the test. It said, “I thought the ribbon was never wrong because grown-ups looked at it.” She folded the note carefully and showed it to Tao the next morning. He said the most difficult part of repairing a tool might be changing people's habits after the repair. Lan did not know how to do that yet. She decided that every future test would include a chance for visitors to ask what the signs meant. The little kite could point toward the wind, but questions could point toward mistakes.

那晚蘭在大門發現一張看過測試的孩子留下的小紙條：「我原以為絲帶永遠不會錯，因為大人總是看它。」蘭仔細摺好，隔天早晨拿給陶看。陶說修理工具最難的部分，也許是修好後還要改變人的習慣。蘭尚不知道怎麼辦，便決定未來每次測試都讓訪客有機會問標記的意思。小風箏能指出風向，問題則能指出錯誤。
