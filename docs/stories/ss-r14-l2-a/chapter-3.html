第一次錯誤警報

The First False Warning

把風還給山｜讓風箏教我修仙 · 第 3 / 25 章 · 1000–2000 · 英中對照 · 校訂中

用互動版查詞、朗讀與收藏

A sharp alarm woke the workers before sunrise. The kite by the seed house had pulled free, yet the air felt calm in the lower yard. Tao opened the little paper cell that held the warning bell and found a classic mistake: someone had tied the cord through the wrong hole. Lan remembered a cowboy in an old picture book, chasing his own hat while his horse stood still. The picture was funny, but the lesson was serious. A moving object did not always mean a strong wind. She asked everyone to leave the new alarm silent until it had passed a direct test. Nobody wanted another frightening cry in the dark.

日出前一聲尖銳警報叫醒工人。種子屋旁的風箏脫了繩，下院的空氣卻很平靜。陶打開裝著警鈴的紙製小格，發現一個典型錯誤：有人把線穿進錯誤的孔。蘭想起舊圖畫書裡的牛仔追著帽子跑，馬卻靜靜站著。畫面好笑，道理卻很重要。物品移動不一定代表風很強。她請大家在警報通過直接測試前先讓它保持安靜。沒有人希望黑夜再出現嚇人的叫聲。

Outside the kitchen, the dryer was running to save wet seed bags. Head grower Sera said it was her duty to keep the garden warm enough for the first young plants. The machine sent a narrow stream of warm air through an open window. When the kite crossed that stream, its ribbon rose quickly. Lan turned the dryer off and the warning stopped. The room was empty except for Sera’s tools and a basket of clean cloth. Tao wrote the time of both readings. They had found a source of false wind inside the garden itself, not a curse from beyond the mountain.

廚房外的烘乾機正在搶救受潮的種子袋。首席園丁莎拉說，讓第一批幼苗保持足夠溫暖是她的職責。機器的熱風吹過開著的窗，風箏穿過那股氣流時，絲帶突然高揚。蘭關掉烘乾機，警報便停止。房裡除了莎拉的工具和一籃乾淨布料，沒有其他人。陶記下兩次讀數的時間。他們找到藥圃內部的假風來源，而不是山外的詛咒。

A visitor with a fresh haircut stood by the lamb pen and said the whole report was false. He tried to grab the kite as if it were an object that could answer for itself. Lan asked him not to press the bamboo frame, because one cracked part would change the next result. He said the council ought to punish someone for the alarm. Sera answered that finding a fault and choosing a punishment were two different matters. The visitor looked away. Later he offered a small sail from his market stall, which Lan could use for tests. She accepted the cloth but did not accept his claim that the fault had been solved forever.

一位剛剪過頭髮的訪客站在羊圈旁，說整份報告都是假的。他伸手想抓風箏，彷彿物品本身能回答所有問題。蘭請他別壓竹架，因為一處裂縫就可能改變下次讀數。他說議會應該懲罰造成警報的人。莎拉回答，找出錯誤與決定懲罰是兩回事。訪客別過頭。稍後，他拿來市集攤位的一小塊帆布，供蘭做測試。蘭收下布，卻不接受「問題從此永遠解決」的說法。

At midday the team took a shot at testing three places in order. A bell would strike once for a change near the kitchen and twice for a change in the open field. During a tour of the lower sheds, Lan showed the workers the difference between the two signals. She also asked merchants who traded cloth and paper to tell her when a strong draft damaged their goods. This was not a competition for the cleverest student. It was an attempt to make the whole garden easier to understand. Tao spoke slowly to a child who had heard the morning bell and wanted to know whether the plants were safe.

中午團隊嘗試依順序測試三個地點。廚房附近風向改變，鈴響一下；空地風向改變，鈴響兩下。參觀下方棚屋時，蘭向工人示範兩種訊號的差別，也請買賣布匹和紙張的商人回報強風損壞貨物的時刻。這不是爭當最聰明學生的比賽，而是讓整座藥圃更容易被理解的努力。陶慢慢向一名聽到清晨鐘聲的孩子解釋幼苗是否安全。

By evening Lan had learned that a warning must name its source. “Wind near the window” was not the same as “wind across the mountain,” and a good tool should help people tell the difference. Qiu let her hang the repaired alarm beside the old ribbon, but only with a clear notice saying it was still under trial. She felt proud of the small progress and worried about the days ahead. If a simple dryer could fool the device, what would happen when many people lit fires during the spring festival? She decided to ask Sera to join the next test, even though they often disagreed.

傍晚蘭明白，警告必須交代來源。「窗邊的風」不等於「整座山的風」，好工具要幫助人們分辨。秋守園人讓她把修好的警鈴掛在舊絲帶旁，但必須明白標示仍在試驗。她為這小步進展感到驕傲，也擔心未來。如果簡單的烘乾機就能騙過裝置，春祭時許多人一起生火，又會怎樣？她決定邀請常與自己意見不合的莎拉參加下次測試。

The sail maker returned after the market closed. He wanted to trade two pieces of light cloth for a copy of the safety drawing, so his own workers could hang things more carefully. Lan gave him the public drawing without taking his cloth as payment. The notes belonged to the garden, she said, and anyone could learn from them. The maker still offered the cloth for the next trial, but asked that his name be recorded as the person who supplied it. She agreed. A good record could give people credit without turning common knowledge into private property.

市集關門後，製帆人回來，希望用兩塊輕布交換安全圖副本，好讓工人掛東西時更小心。蘭沒有收布作為報酬，直接把公開圖給他。她說筆記屬於藥圃，任何人都可學習。製帆人仍願提供下次測試用布，只要求在物資來源上寫下自己的名字。蘭同意。好紀錄能給人應得的肯定，不必把共同知識變成私人財產。
