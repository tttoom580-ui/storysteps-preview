葉下的斷枝

The Branch Under the Leaves

把風還給山｜讓風箏教我修仙 · 第 4 / 25 章 · 1000–2000 · 英中對照 · 校訂中

用互動版查詞、朗讀與收藏

The morning after the false alarm, a worker cut his finger on a broken branch. A drop of blood fell on the dry earth, and Lan stopped the test at once. The garden director wanted to combine two damaged frames into one strong kite, but Lan said the wood should first be checked for sharp edges. Sera brought clean cloth and helped the worker sit. Nobody had a serious injury, yet the event showed how easily a hurried repair could cause harm. Lan drew a small red line beside the damaged tools on her board. She would not use an unsafe frame merely to prove that her idea worked.

假警報隔天，一位工人被斷枝割傷手指，一滴血落在乾地上，蘭立刻停止測試。園區主任想把兩個壞框架組成一只結實的風箏，蘭卻說木材得先檢查是否有尖角。莎拉帶來乾淨的布，扶工人坐下。雖然傷勢不重，這件事仍顯示匆忙修理很容易傷人。蘭在木板上的危險工具旁畫了紅線。她不願只為證明自己的想法有效，就使用不安全的框架。

A fever had spread among plants near the wall, though Sera explained that plant disease was not the same as human illness. They inspected the entire row and found that the leaves were staying wet long after sunrise. If the plants could not escape the still, warm air, they might weaken even without bad insects. A generous older gardener offered a spare shelter, but Lan declined to move everything at once. She wanted one small group to remain where it was so they could compare results. Sera finally agreed. The two women had different habits, yet both wanted the garden to survive the spring.

牆邊植物出現蔓延的病狀，莎拉解釋植物的病與人的病並不相同。他們檢查整排幼苗，發現日出後很久葉片仍是濕的。若植物無法擺脫溫暖而停滯的空氣，即使沒有害蟲也可能衰弱。一位慷慨的老園丁借出空棚，但蘭沒有立刻把所有植株搬走。她希望留一小組在原處，才有辦法比較結果。莎拉終於同意。兩人習慣不同，卻都希望藥圃平安度過春天。

At lunch the workers ate hamburgers from a passing cart. Lan could imagine a simple network of small wind markers along the beds, each made from cheap material. She drew a circle for every place the leaves dried at a different rate. Tao asked whether a map could show air that nobody could see. “Not perfectly,” she said. “But it may show where our guesses fail.” A girl selling papaya slices watched them and pointed to the shade cast by the north wall. She had been carrying fruit there every afternoon and knew the cold patch better than any visitor. Lan marked her observation with the same care as the keeper’s.

午餐時，工人吃流動攤車的漢堡。蘭想像能在苗床旁架設簡單的風向標記網路，每個標記都用便宜材料做成。她在圖上把葉片乾燥速度不同的地方畫上圓圈。陶問地圖能不能顯示誰都看不見的空氣。「不能完全顯示，」她說，「但至少能告訴我們哪裡猜錯了。」賣木瓜片的女孩看見他們，指出北牆的陰影。她每天下午都在那裡送水果，比訪客更熟悉那塊冷地。蘭以同樣慎重的態度記下她的觀察。

The growing population of visitors gave the garden more eyes, but also more noise. Qiu asked Lan to give a simple score to each marker: one for a clear reading, zero for a broken part. She thought that made sense only if the team wrote down the reason for each score. Her real target was not a perfect number on a board; it was knowing which beds needed help. Therefore she walked through the paths again, testing the wood joints and listening to the dry leaves. A worker said her little maps might be worth more than one costly charm. Lan smiled, but she wanted proof before praise.

來訪的人口增加，讓藥圃多了觀察者，也多了噪音。秋守園人請蘭為每個標記打簡單的分數：讀數清楚得一分，零件損壞得零分。蘭認為只有記下每個分數的理由才有意義。她真正的目標不是板上的完美數字，而是知道哪些苗床需要幫忙。因此她再次沿小徑行走，檢查木製接點，聆聽乾葉聲。工人說她的小地圖可能比昂貴的護符更有價值。蘭笑了，卻仍想先看證據。

That night the injured worker returned with his finger wrapped and asked to read the map. He found a place Lan had missed: a low shelf where the roots of a climbing plant blocked a vent. Removing all the roots would damage the vine, so Sera suggested moving the shelf instead. They tried it with a single tray and left the rest unchanged. The next morning would tell them something. Lan wrote the names of everyone who had helped beside the test, not merely the names of those who could use magic. If they saved the garden, she wanted the record to show how.

那晚受傷的工人包好手指回來，請求看看地圖。他指出蘭忽略的地方：一處低架上的攀藤根部擋住了通風口。把根全部拔掉會傷害藤蔓，莎拉因此建議移動架子。他們先試移一盤，讓其餘部分保持原樣。明早就能得到新的資訊。蘭在試驗旁寫下每位幫忙者的名字，不只寫會用法術的人。如果藥圃得救，她希望紀錄能說清楚是怎麼做到的。

Sera asked Lan to remain for one more hour and watch the small group of plants they had moved. Their leaves were beginning to dry, but that alone did not mean the roots were healthier. The two women measured the soil and agreed to compare it again after three days. Lan was relieved that Sera did not demand an instant answer. They sat beside the bed and talked about why they had disagreed before. Sera feared losing her plants; Lan feared people would trust an unsafe sign. Neither fear was silly. Understanding both made the next experiment easier to plan.

莎拉請蘭再留一小時，看已移動的那組植物。葉子開始乾了，但不代表根就更健康。兩人量好土壤，約三天後再比較。蘭很安心，因為莎拉沒有要求立刻給答案。她們坐在苗床旁，談以前為何爭執。莎拉害怕失去植物，蘭害怕人們相信不安全的標記。兩種恐懼都不愚蠢。理解彼此後，下一次實驗更容易安排。
