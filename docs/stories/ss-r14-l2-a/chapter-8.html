長者與老穀粒

The Elder and the Old Grain

把風還給山｜讓風箏教我修仙 · 第 8 / 25 章 · 1000–2000 · 英中對照 · 校訂中

用互動版查詞、朗讀與收藏

An adult farmer came to ask for aid because his northern seedlings had stopped growing. He carried an ancient basket that his family had repaired for a century. Lan turned it backward and saw that wind could pass through the bottom without lifting the seeds. Her design for a new seed tray had the same simple opening. An elder woman explained that the mountain had once used baskets like this before magic fans became common. Lan did not copy the basket without asking. She wrote down the family's name and asked whether they would allow a small test. The farmer nodded, but wanted to see the result before making any change to his field.

一位成年農夫來求助，因北方的幼苗停止生長。他帶著家族修補了一個世紀的古老籃子。蘭把籃子向後轉，發現空氣能從底部穿過，卻不會把種子吹起。她的新種子盤設計也有同樣簡單的開口。一位長者說，魔法風扇普及前山上就用這種籃子。蘭沒有擅自照抄，而是記下家族姓名，詢問能否做小規模測試。農夫點頭，卻希望先看結果再改動田地。

The youngest worker had a fever and stayed home, so Sera took his place in the greenhouse. A sack of grain had arrived with no clear identity label, and nobody knew which farm had sent it. The instruction tied to the sack said to maintain it in a dry room. Tao laid the bag across his lap to inspect the knot, while Lan checked the other shipments. A woman selling peach cakes recognized the string used by the northern farmers. She offered to ask them for the original record. Lan thanked her and refused to plant the grain until they knew its history. Carelessness could destroy months of careful growing.

最年輕的工人發燒留在家，莎拉因此代班進溫室。一袋穀物沒有清楚的身分標籤，沒人知道由哪座農場寄來。袋上的指示寫著必須保持乾燥。陶把袋子放在膝上檢查繩結，蘭則查其他貨物。賣桃子糕的婦人認出北方農民常用的繩子，願意替他們詢問原始紀錄。蘭道謝，在確認來源前堅持不播種。粗心可能毀掉數月的辛勤栽培。

By afternoon a stranger offered to swim across the small lake and bring back a missing basket. He struck a heroic pose, but the water was cold and no one had checked the depth. Lan asked him not to risk it. He looked hurt and said that a typical mountain student should welcome brave acts. “Bravery can mean stopping,” she answered. She used an old hook tied to a pole instead, and Tao helped her pull the basket to the bank. A small tear in the basket showed why it had floated away. The stranger helped carry it back and apologized for treating danger like a game.

下午一名陌生人提議游過小湖，取回遺失的籃子。他擺出英雄姿勢，湖水卻很冷，也沒人查過水深。蘭請他別冒險。他受傷似地說，典型山門學徒應歡迎勇敢行為。「勇敢有時是停下來，」她回答。她改用綁在長竿上的舊鉤子，陶幫忙把籃子拉回岸邊。籃上的小裂口說明它為何漂走。陌生人幫忙搬回，並為把危險當遊戲而道歉。

The farmer returned carrying two trays he had used for years. Their holes were different sizes, yet both had kept his seeds dry through a storm. Lan asked Sera to compare the trays with the new design. The largest hole let in more air but also allowed small seeds to fall through. The smallest kept every seed safe but held too much moisture. Together they chose a middle size for one test row. They did not call it a finished invention. Sera placed a red mark beside the row so nobody would mistake it for the regular crop. The elder farmer watched and smiled at their care.

農夫帶回兩個使用多年的種子盤，孔洞大小不同，卻都曾讓他的種子在暴風雨中保持乾燥。蘭請莎拉把它們與新設計比較。大孔讓更多空氣進入，也讓小種子掉落；小孔能保住所有種子，卻留住太多水分。大家選擇中等孔徑，先試一排。沒有人稱它為完成的發明。莎拉在試驗排旁畫上紅記號，避免被誤認為一般作物。老農夫看著他們的細心，露出微笑。

That night Lan thought about how much the garden depended on knowledge carried by ordinary families. No one had called the basket a grand treasure, and no official had been invited to study it. Yet a small repair made long ago had helped people grow food during hard years. She wrote the family's account in her notebook, keeping their words separate from her own guesses. The next day they would compare the drying time of the three trays. If the new idea failed, the old basket would still have taught them something useful. Lan could live with that kind of result.

那晚蘭想到，藥圃多麼依賴普通家庭保存的知識。沒有人把籃子稱作寶物，也沒有官員受邀研究它，卻是很久以前的一次小修補，幫助人們度過艱難年歲。她把這家的說法寫進筆記，並與自己的推測分開。隔天要比較三種種子盤的乾燥時間。即使新點子失敗，舊籃子仍教了他們有用的事。蘭可以接受這樣的結果。

The elder woman returned with a second basket that had been used by her parents. Its wooden edge was rough, and she asked the team not to sand it before taking pictures. The marks along that edge showed where earlier repairs had been made. Lan understood the request. If they made every old tool look new, they might erase useful evidence of how people learned. She invited the woman to explain the repairs in her own words, then wrote those words beside the photographs. The new seed tray was only one experiment. The history of the old baskets would remain useful even if the experiment failed.

長者帶來父母用過的第二只籃子，木邊粗糙，她請團隊拍照前不要磨平。邊上的記號顯示過去在哪裡修補過。蘭懂得原因：若把每件舊工具都弄得像新的一樣，就可能抹去人們如何學習的證據。她邀長者親自說明修補史，再把原話寫在照片旁。新種子盤只是一次試驗，舊籃子的歷史即使試驗失敗也仍有用。
