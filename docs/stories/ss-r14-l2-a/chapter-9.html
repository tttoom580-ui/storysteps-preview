關不緊的蓋子

The Lid That Would Not Stay Shut

把風還給山｜讓風箏教我修仙 · 第 9 / 25 章 · 1000–2000 · 英中對照 · 校訂中

Most of the seedlings were still alive, but a glass jar suddenly burst beside the east wall. Sera called everyone to the exit and asked Lan to read the warning aloud. The main character in an old training story had ignored pressure inside a closed jar, and Lan remembered the ending well. No one was hurt, though the smell of hot herbs was delicious and strangely comforting. The lid had been too tight for warm air to escape. Tao brought water to cool the table, then stopped when Lan asked whether the liquid might make the hot glass break further. They waited until a worker with gloves could remove the pieces.

多數幼苗仍活著，但東牆旁一只玻璃罐突然爆裂。莎拉叫大家走向出口，請蘭大聲念出警告。舊訓練故事的主角曾忽略密閉罐中的壓力，蘭清楚記得那個結局。沒人受傷，熱草藥的香氣甚至令人感到奇異的安慰。罐蓋太緊，熱空氣無法逸出。陶拿水來要冷卻桌面，蘭卻問液體是否可能使熱玻璃裂得更嚴重。大家等戴手套的工人把碎片清掉。

Vale arrived just then and said the broken jar proved the garden needed his machine. Lan tried to express her concern without accusing him of anything. She showed him an image of the jar before it broke, taken by a student who had been counting seeds. “This was a storage problem,” she said. “It does not prove your fan can improve the wind.” Vale looked angry, but Sera asked him to forgive the interruption and wait until the room was safe. In a quiet corner, Tao helped Lan repair the paper wing that had fallen during the alarm. Their work went on without any wonderful spell.

維爾正好抵達，宣稱玻璃罐破裂證明藥圃需要他的機器。蘭努力表達擔憂，卻沒有無證據地指控他。她拿出學生數種子時拍的罐子破裂前影像。「這是儲存問題，」她說，「不能證明你的風扇會改善風。」維爾生氣，莎拉仍請他原諒安全處理的中斷，等房間恢復安全再談。安靜的角落裡，陶幫蘭修理警報時落下的紙翼。沒有神奇法術，工作仍繼續。

They changed the position of the storage table and opened a high window. The result was easier to see than anyone expected: light paper strips moved away from the warm shelves, and the jars cooled more slowly. Outside, a seller showed children a painted shark on a toy cart. One boy made a silly joke about sharks flying into the greenhouse. Lan smiled, then asked him to help place a warning symbol where visitors could see it. She wanted a solution that did not depend on remembering one frightening accident. If the danger could be noticed early, fewer people would need to be brave when something went wrong.

他們改變儲物桌的位置，打開高窗。結果比預期更清楚：輕紙條從溫暖架子旁飄開，罐子也更慢地冷卻。屋外小販在玩具車上畫了一隻鯊魚，孩子開玩笑說鯊魚會飛進溫室。蘭笑了，接著請他幫忙在訪客容易看見的地方貼警告符號。她想要的解法不能依賴大家記住一次嚇人事故。如果能提早注意危險，出了事就不用有那麼多人被迫勇敢。

On the notice board Lan wrote a title in large letters: “Where Warm Air Goes.” She drew a tube, a shelf, and two arrows, making the path of the air easy to follow. Her first drawing had been too full of tiny details, so Tao helped her remove the parts that did not matter. Workers tested the picture by explaining it to one another. Sera listened and corrected one arrow that pointed in the wrong direction. By evening the notice had become a shared tool. Nobody needed to agree with Lan's every word to improve it. They only needed to show which part had been misunderstood.

蘭在公告板上用大字寫下標題：「暖空氣往哪裡走」。她畫出管子、架子和兩個箭頭，讓氣流路線容易理解。第一版細節太多，陶幫她去掉無關部分。工人互相解釋圖示來測試，莎拉聽後修正了一個指錯方向的箭頭。傍晚公告成了共用工具。沒有人非得同意蘭說的每一句話才有辦法改進，只須指出哪裡被誤解。

Qiu later asked if Lan was disappointed that the day had produced no stronger kite. She admitted she was tired, but said the garden had learned to stop and look after people before returning to an experiment. He did not give her a prize. Instead he asked her to teach the new warning sign to tomorrow's visitors. Lan accepted. It was not the sort of task that appeared in the stories of great magic masters, yet she thought it mattered. A garden that could explain its mistakes might become safer for the next generation of growers.

秋守園人後來問蘭，今天沒有做出更強的風箏是否令她失望。她承認疲倦，卻說藥圃學會先停下照顧人，再回到實驗。他沒有給她獎賞，而是請她明天教訪客讀懂新警示。蘭答應了。這不是偉大法師故事裡常見的任務，她卻覺得重要。一座能說清楚自己錯誤的藥圃，或許會成為下一代種植者更安全的地方。

Late in the afternoon a student suggested sealing every jar with thicker lids so none could fall off. Lan asked the class what might happen to warm air inside. Several remembered the broken glass and answered at once. Sera then showed them a safe container designed for the herbs they actually stored. It had a clear label, a proper opening, and a place for the date. They practiced checking it without heating anything. The lesson ended with the class making warning signs for the store room. Lan kept one sign drawn by a young girl because its words were both kind and direct.

下午晚些時候，一名學生提議給每個罐子換更厚的蓋子，免得掉落。蘭問班上暖空氣被封在裡面會怎樣，幾人想起破玻璃，立刻回答。莎拉展示適合實際儲存藥草的安全容器，上面有清楚標籤、正確開口和日期位置。大家不加熱任何東西，只練習檢查。課程最後為儲物室製作警示。蘭留下年輕女孩畫的一張，因為文字既親切又直接。
