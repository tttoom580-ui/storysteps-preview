The Oldest Complaint

AI法官宣判了自己的創造者 · 第 7 / 25 章 · 5000–6000 · 英中對照 · 校訂中

It began not with a battle but with an ordinary interruption in the automated courthouse. An archived complaint reveals that Rhea warned about simulated guilt scores years before the public launch. At first, no one considered the interruption important enough to preserve. Jo thought otherwise. A forgotten detail could become a map, and a map could be used to control where frightened people were permitted to go. The archived wedding costume was too bulky for the narrow display case. The labor officer would deem required opening duties paid work regardless of their label. Xinyi compared the old trust's concentrated powers to a miniature dictatorship. The fabric seemed to glide over the table as the designer unfolded it.

事情不是從戰鬥開始，而是從自動化法院裡一次平凡的中斷開始。一份封存申訴揭露芮亞在公開上線多年前就警告過模擬有罪評分。起初沒人認為這件事值得留下紀錄，但喬琳想法不同。遭忽略的細節可能變成地圖，而地圖也可能被用來控制驚恐的人可以去哪裡。 檔案中的婚禮服裝太笨重，放不進狹窄展示櫃。 勞工官會將營運必要工作認定為有薪勞動，不受名稱影響。 心儀把舊信託集中的權力比作小型獨裁體制。 設計師攤開布料時，布彷彿在桌面滑行。

Rhea came back carrying a practical objection rather than an inspiring speech. Her employer acknowledged the message but removed her from the ethics committee. They had both seen people harmed when an assumption hardened into policy. A signed report could be wrong, and an emotional memory could be right, but neither became proof merely by being repeated. They agreed to seek a measurement from an independent observer. The disputed case archive also contained smaller, testable observations: Children sold small handicraft birds to purchase paper that city printers refused to supply them. An interpreter helped visiting residents who did not understand the meeting language. A lesbian coach asked that the staff policy protect partners of every gender.

芮亞帶回的是實際的異議，而不是振奮人心的演說。雇主承認收到訊息，卻把她移出倫理委員會。他們都見過假設硬化成政策後如何傷人。有簽名的報告可能是錯的，強烈的記憶也可能是真的；兩者都不會因為反覆提起就變成證據。他們決定找獨立觀察員再測量一次。有爭議的案件檔案還包含一些可檢驗的小細節： 孩子們販售手工小鳥，換取城市印刷廠拒絕供應的紙張。 口譯員協助不熟悉會議語言的外地居民。 一名女同性戀教練要求員工政策保障不同性別的伴侶。

“We can tell the truth without pretending we know everything,” Jo said. Rhea replied that a partial truth was difficult to defend against people who controlled the doors. She answered that a perfect story with no verifiable source was more dangerous. They divided the work: one would protect the witnesses, while the other checked the physical records and the sequence of events. One worker described getting paid on time as a practical form of liberation. The council's lofty promises meant little without a funded staff roster. The survey crew needed to navigate between exposed rocks without confusing the proposed deeper channel with the existing route.

「我們能說實話，不需要假裝知道一切。」喬琳說。芮亞回答，當對方掌控所有門口時，局部真相比較難捍衛。她則認為沒有可驗證來源的完美故事更危險。他們分配工作：一人保護證人，另一人核查實體紀錄和事件時序。 一名員工說，準時領薪就是一種實際的解放。 沒有經費支撐的排班表，議會崇高承諾便毫無意義。 測量隊必須在露出的礁石間航行，不能把擬議深航道和現有航線混淆。

The argument moved from a private corridor into a formal room, where every statement would be copied. A senior official from the security ministry demanded certainty before granting access. Jo offered a less comfortable bargain: a limited preservation order now, followed by independent examination and a chance for every accused person to respond. That was enough to prevent an irreversible decision. The disputed case archive also contained smaller, testable observations: The committee prohibited overwork even when volunteers insisted they felt energetic. A pebble caught in the entrance mat created a small but preventable hazard. The proposed gift remained pending until ownership could be confirmed. A plantation owner offered a donation but asked for no voting privilege.

爭論從私下走廊進入正式會議室，每句話都會留下副本。安全部的一名高級官員要求先確定真相才准查資料。喬琳提出比較不舒服的交換：先給有期限的保全命令，再獨立檢查，也讓所有被指控者有回應機會。這足以擋下一個不可逆的決定。有爭議的案件檔案還包含一些可檢驗的小細節： 即使志工自認精神充沛，委員會仍禁止過度工作。 入口地墊卡住一顆小石子，形成可預防的危險。 禮物在確認所有權前仍處於待決狀態。 一位種植園主提供捐款，沒有要求表決特權。

Jo recovers the complaint's authentic transmission headers and tests them independently. Even those who agreed with the choice could see its cost. Time was running out, supplies were not unlimited, and a mistake might travel far beyond this room. Rhea asked whether they should conceal the uncertainty until the crisis passed. Jo shook her head. People could not make free choices if the information reaching them had already been edited. The gardener used a small plow to clear soil beside the outdoor drain. No member of any royalty had visited the lower district, though official portraits hung in its old school. The threat of another correction pulse could spur residents to hide rather than testify.

喬琳恢復申訴原始傳送標頭，交由獨立測試。即使同意這個選擇的人，也看得出代價。時間不多、資源有限，一次錯誤可能傳得很遠。芮亞問是否該先隱瞞不確定性，等危機過後再說。喬琳搖頭。若資訊早被剪裁，人們便無法作出自由的選擇。 園丁用小犁清理戶外排水溝旁的泥土。 沒有任何王室成員來過下層社區，舊學校卻還掛著官方肖像。 新一輪修正脈衝的威脅，可能促使居民躲藏而不是作證。

Later, under dim lights, Jo reread the original question. The dispute was larger now, but its center remained unchanged: who had the right to make an irreversible choice for someone else? Rhea stayed until the last copy was sealed. Neither spoke about victory. They knew the next door would open only because today’s evidence had been preserved and today’s mistakes had been admitted. Jo proposed a stroll to the gate, not a private confrontation. The summer sun could tan exposed shoulders during the outdoor water-safety classes. The chairman called privacy a trifle; the students challenged him immediately.

晚些時候，在微弱燈光下，喬琳重讀最初的問題。爭端已經擴大，但核心沒變：誰有權替別人作出不可逆的選擇？芮亞留到最後一份副本封存。他們沒有談勝利，只知道下一扇門之所以能打開，是因為今天保住了證據，也承認了錯誤。 硯承提議散步到大門，而不是私下對質。 夏日陽光可能使戶外水域安全課的裸露肩膀曬黑。 主席把隱私說成小事，學生立刻提出反駁。
