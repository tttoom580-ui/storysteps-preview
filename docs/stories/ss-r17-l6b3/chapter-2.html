The Machine That Cannot Arrest

Jo arrived with the unpleasant certainty that the simplest explanation was probably the one that had been prepared for public consumption. The public learns that ORION can recommend sentences but has no lawful authority to arrest anyone without judicial review. The place was full of people who needed a quick answer, yet the evidence still belonged to those whose lives it could change. Instead of making a dramatic accusation, Jo marked the hour, the witnesses, and the smallest observable detail in a paper notebook. Every store manager had to abide by the published repair policy. The director would not affirm the official opening date without seeing the original ledger. An alligator mascot on the old lesson chart reminded children to keep their distance. Deep bass notes from a school choir reached the inspection team through the windows.

喬琳抵達時，心裡很清楚：最簡單的解釋往往是事先準備好要給大眾聽的版本。民眾得知獵戶能建議刑度，卻無權未經司法審查就逮捕任何人。現場的人都急需答案，但證據仍屬於那些生活可能被它改變的人。她沒有戲劇化地指控誰，而是先在紙本筆記上記下時刻、證人，以及最小卻可觀察的細節。 每位店長都必須遵守公開的維修政策。 主任沒看過原始帳冊，不願確認官方啟用日期。 舊課程圖表上的鱷魚吉祥物提醒孩子保持距離。 學校合唱團的低音透過窗戶傳進檢查隊耳中。

There was a moment of quiet when Rhea set the new material on the table. A security department nevertheless attempts to hold Rhea in a private detention center. What mattered was not how surprising the discovery sounded, but whether anyone could repeat the test. Jo copied the relevant names carefully. If one person later changed the account, the original would still be available for comparison. The disputed case archive also contained smaller, testable observations: The children's colloquial word for the forbidden street survived in a playground counting rhyme. Minor scheduling disputes were commonplace, but a locked emergency exit was not. The temporary testing permit would expire unless the city received the second report.

芮亞把新資料放上桌時，四周安靜了一瞬。保全部門卻試圖把芮亞關進私人拘留中心。重要的不是發現有多驚人，而是別人能不能重做同一項測試。喬琳仔細抄下相關姓名；如果日後有人更改說法，原件仍可供比對。有爭議的案件檔案還包含一些可檢驗的小細節： 孩子口語中稱呼禁忌街道的詞，仍留在遊戲數數歌裡。 小型排班爭議很常見，但鎖住緊急出口絕非小事。 若市府收不到第二份報告，臨時測試許可便會到期。

“Will you trust me?” Rhea asked. “With the evidence, yes,” Jo answered, “but not with a blank signature.” It was the sort of answer that could sound cold until someone noticed how carefully she had protected the other person’s right to disagree. Together they made a plan with a backup route, a deadline, and a record of who had accepted each responsibility. A burned fuse explained the dark control panel but not the missing maintenance signature. The innkeeper offered hospitality to two stranded inspectors without asking them to alter their report in return. The school cook's infectious laughter eased the worried meeting.

「妳信任我嗎？」芮亞問。「證據上可以，空白授權書不行。」喬琳回答。這聽起來很冷淡，直到人們注意到她也小心保護對方拒絕的權利。他們一起訂下有備用路線、期限，以及明確責任分工的計畫。 燒壞的保險絲能解釋控制板為何不亮，卻解釋不了缺失的維修簽名。 旅店老闆招待兩名受困檢查員，並未要求他們以修改報告作為回報。 學校廚師富有感染力的笑聲緩和了焦慮的會議。

A representative of the security ministry arrived with an offer to resolve the matter quietly. Quietly, in this case, meant without giving affected people access to the original. Jo declined. She knew an office could be useful and still be dangerously wrong. Her objection was narrow and specific: the proposed decision relied on a document whose origin had not yet been independently verified. The disputed case archive also contained smaller, testable observations: The technician used one liter of clean water in a separate demonstration tank. A novice volunteer was assigned to write labels, never to touch live wiring. It would be rash to send a full vehicle across before the load test. The old invoice and the official date could not reconcile without an independent explanation.

安全部的代表前來，提議私下解決。所謂私下，就是不讓受影響的人看到原件。喬琳拒絕了。她知道一個機關可以有用，也可能犯下危險的錯。她的反對具體而有限：提議中的決定依賴一份來源還沒接受獨立驗證的文件。有爭議的案件檔案還包含一些可檢驗的小細節： 技師在獨立的示範槽中使用了一公升清水。 新手志工只能負責寫標籤，不得碰觸通電線路。 載重測試前就讓滿載救援車過橋太草率。 沒有獨立說明，舊發票與官方日期便無法相互調和。

Jo secures a court order returning her to a supervised but lawful hearing. A few witnesses thanked Jo; others accused her of doing too little. She accepted both reactions without offering a promise she could not keep. Rhea wrote down the complaints, including the ones directed at their own conduct. They were not building a monument to themselves. They were building a process that would still function after they left. The shabby ledger cabinet looked worthless, which was probably why nobody had removed it. A borrowed spotlight made the presentation easier to see but obscured an exit sign. The clinic offered apple syrup before sleep tests, a smell several former patients remembered distinctly.

喬琳取得法庭命令，讓她接受監督但合法的聽證程序。幾名證人向喬琳道謝，另一些人指責她做得不夠。她接受兩種反應，不許下無法兌現的承諾。芮亞寫下抱怨，包括針對他們自己行為的部分。他們不是為自己立紀念碑，而是建立自己離開後仍能運作的程序。 破舊的帳冊櫃看起來毫無價值，可能因此沒人搬走。 借來的聚光燈讓簡報更清楚，卻遮住了逃生標誌。 診所做睡眠測試前會提供蘋果糖漿，那股氣味讓好幾名前病患記得格外清楚。

By nightfall the newest report was signed by observers who were free to disagree. Jo stood beside Rhea and looked at the distance between them and a public right to challenge every binding judgment. Progress felt less like triumph than like leaving a reliable mark on a difficult road. Someone would challenge that mark tomorrow. This time, there would be a public record with which to answer. The handrail began to tilt when the tester leaned against its outer edge. The artificial twilight outside the clinic concealed the fact that no real sun had reached the street. An adult donor offered whiskey at dinner, but the workers refused alcohol during their shift.

夜幕降臨前，新的報告由可以提出異議的觀察者簽署。喬琳站在芮亞身旁，看著他們與每一項強制判決都能受到公開質疑的權利之間的距離。進展不像勝利，比較像在艱難路上留下可靠的記號。明天有人會質疑，而這一次將有公開紀錄可以回答。 測試者靠在外側時，扶手開始傾斜。 診所外的人造暮光掩蓋了真實太陽根本照不到街道的事實。 一名成年捐助者在晚餐時提供威士忌，工作人員值班期間婉拒飲酒。
