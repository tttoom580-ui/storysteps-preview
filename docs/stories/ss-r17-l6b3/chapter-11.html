Jo arrived with the unpleasant certainty that the simplest explanation was probably the one that had been prepared for public consumption. Rhea's private notebook contains instructions to stop automated sentencing when the underlying data cannot be verified. The place was full of people who needed a quick answer, yet the evidence still belonged to those whose lives it could change. Instead of making a dramatic accusation, Jo marked the hour, the witnesses, and the smallest observable detail in a paper notebook. The family archive had won international acclaim before the payment questions emerged. The shared-account policy contained an ambiguity that allowed several technicians to use one password without recording their names. The report ignored bodily limits that had been stated in the original medical note. A cold draught crossed the hearing room when someone opened the ventilation hatch.

喬琳抵達時，心裡很清楚：最簡單的解釋往往是事先準備好要給大眾聽的版本。芮亞私人筆記要求，若基礎資料無法驗證，必須停止自動量刑。現場的人都急需答案，但證據仍屬於那些生活可能被它改變的人。她沒有戲劇化地指控誰，而是先在紙本筆記上記下時刻、證人，以及最小卻可觀察的細節。 付款疑雲浮現前，家族檔案曾享有國際讚譽。 共用帳號政策有一處模糊之處，容許多名技師共用密碼而不記姓名。 報告忽略原始醫療紀錄中已明列的身體限制。 有人打開通風口時，一股冷風穿過聽證室。

There was a moment of quiet when Rhea set the new material on the table. A handwritten change appears beside the rule, signed with initials belonging to a deputy director. What mattered was not how surprising the discovery sounded, but whether anyone could repeat the test. Jo copied the relevant names carefully. If one person later changed the account, the original would still be available for comparison. The disputed case archive also contained smaller, testable observations: The old pool thermometer displayed Fahrenheit while the revised chart used Celsius. The archive's fireproof cabinet held a copy of the disputed training record. A geographical map showed how many districts depended on the public pool.

芮亞把新資料放上桌時，四周安靜了一瞬。規則旁有一項手寫修改，縮寫簽名屬於一位副主任。重要的不是發現有多驚人，而是別人能不能重做同一項測試。喬琳仔細抄下相關姓名；如果日後有人更改說法，原件仍可供比對。有爭議的案件檔案還包含一些可檢驗的小細節： 舊泳池溫度計顯示華氏，新圖表則使用攝氏。 檔案室防火櫃保存著爭議訓練紀錄的複本。 一張地理圖顯示多少行政區依賴公共泳池。

“Will you trust me?” Rhea asked. “With the evidence, yes,” Jo answered, “but not with a blank signature.” It was the sort of answer that could sound cold until someone noticed how carefully she had protected the other person’s right to disagree. Together they made a plan with a backup route, a deadline, and a record of who had accepted each responsibility. An outside hacker had not yet been ruled out in the access investigation. Even a layman could see that the contract granted indefinite data rights. Transparent voting rules could liberate employees from private pressure.

「妳信任我嗎？」芮亞問。「證據上可以，空白授權書不行。」喬琳回答。這聽起來很冷淡，直到人們注意到她也小心保護對方拒絕的權利。他們一起訂下有備用路線、期限，以及明確責任分工的計畫。 存取調查仍未排除外部駭客的可能。 即使外行人也看得出契約授予無限期資料使用權。 透明表決規則能使員工不再受私人壓力束縛。

A representative of the security ministry arrived with an offer to resolve the matter quietly. Quietly, in this case, meant without giving affected people access to the original. Jo declined. She knew an office could be useful and still be dangerously wrong. Her objection was narrow and specific: the proposed decision relied on a document whose origin had not yet been independently verified. The disputed case archive also contained smaller, testable observations: A mermaid mural from the lost railway appeared on two photographs taken decades apart. The seized archive included miscellaneous receipts that proved surprisingly important to the timeline. The committee recorded every occurrence of unauthorized contract changes. A staff outing was postponed so employees could attend the trust hearing.

安全部的代表前來，提議私下解決。所謂私下，就是不讓受影響的人看到原件。喬琳拒絕了。她知道一個機關可以有用，也可能犯下危險的錯。她的反對具體而有限：提議中的決定依賴一份來源還沒接受獨立驗證的文件。有爭議的案件檔案還包含一些可檢驗的小細節： 失蹤鐵路上的美人魚壁畫，出現在相隔幾十年拍攝的兩張照片裡。 查扣檔案裡有各種雜項收據，對重建時間線竟出乎意料重要。 委員會記錄每次未授權修改合約的事件。 為讓員工參與信託聽證，部門出遊延期。

Jo submits handwriting samples for analysis without announcing the author in advance. A few witnesses thanked Jo; others accused her of doing too little. She accepted both reactions without offering a promise she could not keep. Rhea wrote down the complaints, including the ones directed at their own conduct. They were not building a monument to themselves. They were building a process that would still function after they left. The staff promised to persevere through the difficult budget review. She paused to ponder whether she wanted the past discussed at every future meeting. The committee's questions continued to revolve around the missing payments.

喬琳送交筆跡樣本分析，不先公布作者身分。幾名證人向喬琳道謝，另一些人指責她做得不夠。她接受兩種反應，不許下無法兌現的承諾。芮亞寫下抱怨，包括針對他們自己行為的部分。他們不是為自己立紀念碑，而是建立自己離開後仍能運作的程序。 員工承諾堅持完成艱難的預算審查。 她停下思考，是否願意往後每場會議都再談過去。 委員會的問題始終圍繞失蹤款項。

By nightfall the newest report was signed by observers who were free to disagree. Jo stood beside Rhea and looked at the distance between them and a public right to challenge every binding judgment. Progress felt less like triumph than like leaving a reliable mark on a difficult road. Someone would challenge that mark tomorrow. This time, there would be a public record with which to answer. A metal kitchen utensil bearing the erased district's address was admitted as a physical exhibit. Jo refused to name one former coach the sole villain of a system that others enabled. Xinyi brought plain yogurt to the late meeting as a quick snack.

夜幕降臨前，新的報告由可以提出異議的觀察者簽署。喬琳站在芮亞身旁，看著他們與每一項強制判決都能受到公開質疑的權利之間的距離。進展不像勝利，比較像在艱難路上留下可靠的記號。明天有人會質疑，而這一次將有公開紀錄可以回答。 一件刻著被抹除行政區地址的金屬廚具，被列為實體證物。 喬琳拒絕把單一前教練當成整套制度唯一的壞人。 心儀帶原味優格到深夜會議當點心。
