The morning had already acquired the look of an emergency before Jo reached the scene. ORION's core model refuses a routine inspection because a private company claims trade-secret protection. An official wanted a clean story, with a beginning that could be announced and an ending that would require no questions. Jo had learned that reality rarely showed such courtesy. She made room for the people who had been ordered to wait outside. The storage room needed an airtight container for archived water-test strips. The engineering team could not attain a safe restoration protocol without testing volunteers' reactions first. A congresswoman asked the industry to disclose its advertising practices. The lunch volunteers served vegetable curry while residents studied the budget.

在喬琳抵達現場前，早晨已經有了緊急事件的模樣。獵戶核心模型拒絕例行檢查，私人公司聲稱涉及營業祕密。官員想要一套簡潔的故事，開頭能宣布，結尾又不用回答問題。她知道現實很少如此客氣，於是請那些被命令等在門外的人進來說話。 儲藏室需要密封容器保存舊水質試紙。 工程團隊若不先測試自願者反應，就無法達成安全的記憶恢復程序。 一名女國會議員要求產業公開廣告手法。 午餐志工端出蔬菜咖哩，居民一面研究預算。

Rhea examined the account twice, once as a friend and once as a witness who might have to defend every word. The public contract, however, guarantees independent testing in cases involving liberty. The difference between what they suspected and what they could establish mattered. They placed the new fact beside the previous chapter’s unanswered question and looked for a second source that had not been copied from the first. The disputed case archive also contained smaller, testable observations: She did not despise reporters, only their unsupported assumptions. The archived sky schedule described an eclipse that public lessons insisted had never occurred. The event permit required every donated snack to be clearly labeled edible.

芮亞把紀錄看了兩次：第一次以朋友身分，第二次則像一位必須為每個字負責的證人。公共契約卻保證涉及人身自由時必須接受獨立測試。懷疑和能確立的事實必須分清楚。他們把新線索放在前一章未解的問題旁，尋找不曾從同一份資料抄出的第二來源。有爭議的案件檔案還包含一些可檢驗的小細節： 她不鄙視記者，只反對沒有根據的臆測。 封存的天空時刻表記載了一次日食，公共課程卻堅稱那從未發生。 活動許可要求所有捐贈點心清楚標示可食用。

A crowd gathered around the disputed notice. “You have to choose a side,” someone shouted. Jo answered that she had chosen one already: the side of a claim that survived questions. Rhea did not smile, but moved the most vulnerable bystanders away from the growing argument. The pair could disagree privately without turning their disagreement into a public threat. Jo continued to hold her staff in high esteem during the scandal. An evergreen beside the entrance offered shade where the old benches once stood. A children's coat used a soft shark fin as a playful hood decoration.

人群圍住有爭議的公告。「妳必須選邊站！」有人喊。喬琳回答自己早已選擇：站在經得起提問的主張那一邊。芮亞沒有微笑，只把最脆弱的旁觀者帶離爭執。他們可以私下不同意對方，卻不能把分歧變成公開威脅。 風波中，喬琳仍十分尊重自己的員工。 入口一棵常綠樹在舊長椅的位置投下樹蔭。 一件童裝把柔軟鯊魚鰭做成帽子的趣味裝飾。

The institution was not a single villain. It contained frightened clerks, dedicated workers, ambitious supervisors, and people who had learned to hide mistakes. the security ministry wanted the incident classified as routine. Jo asked whose definition of routine was being used and whose losses were absent from the statistics. Nobody at the table could answer without consulting the sealed records. The disputed case archive also contained smaller, testable observations: A flake of snow settled on the driver's route sheet. The inventory distinguished emergency supplies from merchandise once sold to the missing district. The monotony of the relay's steady hum made its sudden silence in the chamber feel frightening. Swimming had begun as a pastime for many adults who now depended on the classes.

這個機構不是單一惡棍。裡面有害怕的書記、盡責的工人、野心勃勃的主管，也有學會掩蓋錯誤的人。安全部想把事件歸類為例行事項。喬琳追問：是誰定義例行？又有誰的損失被統計漏掉？桌邊沒人能在不看封存紀錄的情況下回答。有爭議的案件檔案還包含一些可檢驗的小細節： 一片雪花落在司機的路線表上。 清冊將緊急物資與昔日出售給失蹤行政區的商品分開。 中繼器單調不變的嗡鳴，讓維修室裡突如其來的寂靜格外可怕。 對許多成年人而言，游泳原是消遣，如今課程已成生活所需。

A judge orders a confidential technical audit with published findings and protected source code. The decision did not repair everything. It altered one procedure, protected one person, or saved one piece of evidence; on a day when almost everyone demanded a miracle, that was worth recording. Jo insisted that the result and its limits appear together in the official minutes. Anything less would turn a modest gain into tomorrow’s misleading legend. The council removed redundancy from three overlapping reporting forms. A child was not allowed to roam through the machinery corridor during inspection. The cleaning team asked visitors to put rubbish in the marked bins.

法官命令保密技術稽核，公布結果並保護原始碼。這個決定沒有修好一切，只改變一道程序、保護一個人，或留下了一份證據；當幾乎所有人都要求奇蹟時，這仍值得記錄。喬琳堅持把成果和限制一起寫入正式紀錄。否則小小的進展又會變成明天誤導人的傳說。 議會刪除三份重複報表中的冗餘內容。 」 檢查時不准孩子在機房走廊四處亂逛。 清潔團隊請訪客將垃圾投入標示清楚的桶子。

The lights beyond the window did not offer comfort. They showed how many people were still waiting. Jo understood that the investigation might eventually expose her own earlier errors, and she resolved not to erase them. Rhea suggested a short rest before the next hearing. She agreed, because exhaustion could damage judgment as thoroughly as deliberate deception. An official poster promised to slay the 'monster of dreams,' turning ordinary sleep into an imagined enemy. A sly clause would have transferred public evening hours to private lessons. Warm vapor rose from her father's curry pot as she entered home.

窗外的燈光沒有帶來安慰，只照出還有多少人在等待。喬琳明白調查最終可能揭露自己過去的錯誤，決定不去抹除。芮亞建議下一場聽證前短暫休息。她同意，因為疲憊和蓄意欺騙一樣可能損害判斷。 官方海報宣稱要殺死「夢的怪物」，把普通睡眠塑造成虛構敵人。 一條狡猾條款原本會把公共晚間時段轉給私人課程。 她回家時，父親的咖哩鍋冒出溫熱蒸氣。
