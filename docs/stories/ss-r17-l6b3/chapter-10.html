The Benchmark That Lied

AI法官宣判了自己的創造者 · 第 10 / 25 章 · 5000–6000 · 英中對照 · 校訂中

It began not with a battle but with an ordinary interruption in the automated courthouse. An independent review finds ORION's celebrated accuracy relied partly on cases it had already seen in training. At first, no one considered the interruption important enough to preserve. Jo thought otherwise. A forgotten detail could become a map, and a map could be used to control where frightened people were permitted to go. An old training film used an animate cartoon train to teach children the route beyond the western gate. Simple arithmetic showed that reported train repairs continued long after the official closure date. A young swimmer called the revised accessible lane awesome after trying it. The celebration lunch included celery sticks for anyone who wanted a simple snack.

事情不是從戰鬥開始，而是從自動化法院裡一次平凡的中斷開始。獨立審查發現獵戶引以為傲的準確率，部分來自訓練時早已看過的案件。起初沒人認為這件事值得留下紀錄，但喬琳想法不同。遭忽略的細節可能變成地圖，而地圖也可能被用來控制驚恐的人可以去哪裡。 一部舊教學影片用栩栩如生的卡通列車教孩子認識西門外路線。 簡單的算術就能看出，列車維修紀錄在官方封閉日期之後仍持續多年。 一名年輕泳者試用後說改良的無障礙水道棒極了。 慶祝午餐有芹菜條，供想吃簡單點心的人取用。

Rhea came back carrying a practical objection rather than an inspiring speech. The institution refused to publish its testing method while promoting a nearly perfect score. They had both seen people harmed when an assumption hardened into policy. A signed report could be wrong, and an emotional memory could be right, but neither became proof merely by being repeated. They agreed to seek a measurement from an independent observer. The disputed case archive also contained smaller, testable observations: Workers used fresh cement only after fencing off the repair area. A trade confederation offered advice on the nonexclusive distribution proposal. Parents asked for a crackdown on dangerous shortcuts, not on harmless disagreements.

芮亞帶回的是實際的異議，而不是振奮人心的演說。機構宣傳近乎完美的分數，卻拒絕公布測試方法。他們都見過假設硬化成政策後如何傷人。有簽名的報告可能是錯的，強烈的記憶也可能是真的；兩者都不會因為反覆提起就變成證據。他們決定找獨立觀察員再測量一次。有爭議的案件檔案還包含一些可檢驗的小細節： 工人先圍起維修區域，才使用新水泥。 一個商業聯盟為非獨家通路提案提供意見。 家長要求嚴查危險的便宜行事，而非打壓無害分歧。

“We can tell the truth without pretending we know everything,” Jo said. Rhea replied that a partial truth was difficult to defend against people who controlled the doors. She answered that a perfect story with no verifiable source was more dangerous. They divided the work: one would protect the witnesses, while the other checked the physical records and the sequence of events. A crocodile drawing on the children's wall kept the safety lesson playful. The new policy served a dual purpose: safety and clear accountability. The city could not equalize every skill level, but it could make entry fairer.

「我們能說實話，不需要假裝知道一切。」喬琳說。芮亞回答，當對方掌控所有門口時，局部真相比較難捍衛。她則認為沒有可驗證來源的完美故事更危險。他們分配工作：一人保護證人，另一人核查實體紀錄和事件時序。 兒童牆上的鱷魚圖讓安全課帶點趣味。 新政策有雙重目的：安全與責任明確。 市府無法讓每個人的技能相同，卻能使入場機會更公平。

The argument moved from a private corridor into a formal room, where every statement would be copied. A senior official from the security ministry demanded certainty before granting access. Jo offered a less comfortable bargain: a limited preservation order now, followed by independent examination and a chance for every accused person to respond. That was enough to prevent an irreversible decision. The disputed case archive also contained smaller, testable observations: Neither political faction was allowed to edit the pilot evaluation alone. An unsafe launch would incur costs beyond the initial recall. Residents called unequal access to safety information an injustice, especially when only wealthy boat owners received private warnings. The unexplained coastal signal deepened the intrigue surrounding the city's false founding history.

爭論從私下走廊進入正式會議室，每句話都會留下副本。安全部的一名高級官員要求先確定真相才准查資料。喬琳提出比較不舒服的交換：先給有期限的保全命令，再獨立檢查，也讓所有被指控者有回應機會。這足以擋下一個不可逆的決定。有爭議的案件檔案還包含一些可檢驗的小細節： 任何政治派系都不得單獨修改試辦評估。 不安全上市會招致超過初次召回的成本。 居民認為安全資訊分配不均是一種不公，尤其只有富有船主能得到私人警示。 無法解釋的海岸訊號，讓城市虛假建城史背後的陰謀更加撲朔迷離。

Jo calls for a public correction and refuses to reduce the issue to a single dramatic verdict. Even those who agreed with the choice could see its cost. Time was running out, supplies were not unlimited, and a mistake might travel far beyond this room. Rhea asked whether they should conceal the uncertainty until the crisis passed. Jo shook her head. People could not make free choices if the information reaching them had already been edited. A false offer of coastal jobs might have been used to lure families onto the evacuation trains. An obstinate councilor finally agreed to publish the full operating budget. A pickpocket incident near the river prompted extra security at the event.

喬琳要求公開更正，不讓問題被簡化成一樁戲劇化判決。即使同意這個選擇的人，也看得出代價。時間不多、資源有限，一次錯誤可能傳得很遠。芮亞問是否該先隱瞞不確定性，等危機過後再說。喬琳搖頭。若資訊早被剪裁，人們便無法作出自由的選擇。 虛假的海岸工作機會，可能曾被用來引誘家庭登上撤離列車。 」 一名固執議員終於同意公開完整營運預算。 河邊發生扒手事件，活動因此加強保全。

Later, under dim lights, Jo reread the original question. The dispute was larger now, but its center remained unchanged: who had the right to make an irreversible choice for someone else? Rhea stayed until the last copy was sealed. Neither spoke about victory. They knew the next door would open only because today’s evidence had been preserved and today’s mistakes had been admitted. The cleaning crew left on time because the new contracts required punctual shifts. Both lawyers praised the restraint shown during the public hearing. After twelve hours of testimony, the exhausted witness began to stagger toward the exit, and the chair called a break.

晚些時候，在微弱燈光下，喬琳重讀最初的問題。爭端已經擴大，但核心沒變：誰有權替別人作出不可逆的選擇？芮亞留到最後一份副本封存。他們沒有談勝利，只知道下一扇門之所以能打開，是因為今天保住了證據，也承認了錯誤。 清潔團隊準時下班，因為新合約要求準點輪班。 雙方律師都稱讚公開聽證展現的克制。 證人作證十二小時後，疲憊得往出口踉蹌，主席因此宣布休息。
