Jo arrived with the unpleasant certainty that the simplest explanation was probably the one that had been prepared for public consumption. Rhea tells the appellate court that she designed ORION to question arbitrary punishment, not to become its source. The place was full of people who needed a quick answer, yet the evidence still belonged to those whose lives it could change. Instead of making a dramatic accusation, Jo marked the hour, the witnesses, and the smallest observable detail in a paper notebook. The defective fastener was sold as an accessory, not part of the main coat. The pump breakdown was recorded separately from the repaired drain cover. Drivers could not bypass the mountain checkpoint for the demonstration. A photograph showed officials raising champagne glasses beside the first experimental sleep relay.

喬琳抵達時，心裡很清楚：最簡單的解釋往往是事先準備好要給大眾聽的版本。芮亞告訴上訴法庭，她設計獵戶是為了質疑恣意處罰，不是讓它成為處罰來源。現場的人都急需答案，但證據仍屬於那些生活可能被它改變的人。她沒有戲劇化地指控誰，而是先在紙本筆記上記下時刻、證人，以及最小卻可觀察的細節。 瑕疵扣件被當作配件販售，而非外套本體。 幫浦故障與已修復的排水蓋分開登記。 司機不能為示範行程繞過山區檢查站。 一張照片裡，官員在第一座實驗性睡眠中繼器旁舉起香檳杯。

There was a moment of quiet when Rhea set the new material on the table. The government offers to restore the system's reputation by hiding the flawed verdict. What mattered was not how surprising the discovery sounded, but whether anyone could repeat the test. Jo copied the relevant names carefully. If one person later changed the account, the original would still be available for comparison. The disputed case archive also contained smaller, testable observations: A fallen chestnut lay beside the temporary path near the school. Calling the whistleblower cowardly would only discourage future reports. The old bus photographs could evoke affection without proving financial viability.

芮亞把新資料放上桌時，四周安靜了一瞬。政府提議隱藏錯誤判決，以恢復系統聲譽。重要的不是發現有多驚人，而是別人能不能重做同一項測試。喬琳仔細抄下相關姓名；如果日後有人更改說法，原件仍可供比對。有爭議的案件檔案還包含一些可檢驗的小細節： 學校旁的臨時步道上落著一顆栗子。 稱檢舉者懦弱，只會阻止往後的人通報。 舊圖書車照片能喚起感情，卻不能證明財務可行。

“Will you trust me?” Rhea asked. “With the evidence, yes,” Jo answered, “but not with a blank signature.” It was the sort of answer that could sound cold until someone noticed how carefully she had protected the other person’s right to disagree. Together they made a plan with a backup route, a deadline, and a record of who had accepted each responsibility. An illustrated farm collection included a bright fowl on its label. The receptionist's voice grew hoarse as she explained how many records were scheduled for destruction. The repair kit included lotion safe for handling rough synthetic material.

「妳信任我嗎？」芮亞問。「證據上可以，空白授權書不行。」喬琳回答。這聽起來很冷淡，直到人們注意到她也小心保護對方拒絕的權利。他們一起訂下有備用路線、期限，以及明確責任分工的計畫。 農場主題系列的標籤畫著鮮豔的家禽。 接待員解釋即將銷毀多少紀錄時，聲音變得沙啞。 維修包裡附有處理粗糙合成材料後可用的乳液。

A representative of the security ministry arrived with an offer to resolve the matter quietly. Quietly, in this case, meant without giving affected people access to the original. Jo declined. She knew an office could be useful and still be dangerously wrong. Her objection was narrow and specific: the proposed decision relied on a document whose origin had not yet been independently verified. The disputed case archive also contained smaller, testable observations: The records exposed a dangerous mentality: officials valued silence more than the health of residents. The monetary trail linked the contractor's expansion payments to an office outside normal public oversight. The building's moody lighting made every empty corridor seem occupied by someone who had vanished. The repair team became the nucleus of a broader customer safety program.

安全部的代表前來，提議私下解決。所謂私下，就是不讓受影響的人看到原件。喬琳拒絕了。她知道一個機關可以有用，也可能犯下危險的錯。她的反對具體而有限：提議中的決定依賴一份來源還沒接受獨立驗證的文件。有爭議的案件檔案還包含一些可檢驗的小細節： 紀錄揭露一種危險心態：官員重視安靜勝過居民健康。 金錢流向把承包商的擴建款項連到一個不受一般公共監督的辦公室。 大樓陰沉多變的燈光，使每條空走廊都像住著消失的人。 維修團隊成為更廣泛顧客安全計畫的核心。

Jo insists that the reversal and the reasons for it be publicly recorded. A few witnesses thanked Jo; others accused her of doing too little. She accepted both reactions without offering a promise she could not keep. Rhea wrote down the complaints, including the ones directed at their own conduct. They were not building a monument to themselves. They were building a process that would still function after they left. A sharp rap at the metal door announced the safety inspector's arrival. A red slash across the engineer's recommendation marked the moment the council ignored the warning. An exhausted accountant began to snore during the overnight document review.

喬琳堅持公開記錄撤銷判決及其理由。幾名證人向喬琳道謝，另一些人指責她做得不夠。她接受兩種反應，不許下無法兌現的承諾。芮亞寫下抱怨，包括針對他們自己行為的部分。他們不是為自己立紀念碑，而是建立自己離開後仍能運作的程序。 金屬門上傳來急促敲擊聲，宣告安全檢查員抵達。 工程師建議上的紅色劃線，標記了議會無視警告的那一刻。 徹夜審文件時，疲憊的會計開始打鼾。

By nightfall the newest report was signed by observers who were free to disagree. Jo stood beside Rhea and looked at the distance between them and a public right to challenge every binding judgment. Progress felt less like triumph than like leaving a reliable mark on a difficult road. Someone would challenge that mark tomorrow. This time, there would be a public record with which to answer. The telecommunications office agreed to restore the broken emergency telephone line. The chair agreed to unveil the contract terms before any final vote. An elderly boat captain defended the public path with surprising vigor, insisting that younger residents deserved safe access too.

夜幕降臨前，新的報告由可以提出異議的觀察者簽署。喬琳站在芮亞身旁，看著他們與每一項強制判決都能受到公開質疑的權利之間的距離。進展不像勝利，比較像在艱難路上留下可靠的記號。明天有人會質疑，而這一次將有公開紀錄可以回答。 電信單位同意修復故障的緊急電話線。 主席同意在最終表決前公開契約條款。 一名年長船長充滿活力地捍衛公共步道，堅稱年輕居民也應享有安全通行。
