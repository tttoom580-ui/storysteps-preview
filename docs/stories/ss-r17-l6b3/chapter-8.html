The Human Override

AI法官宣判了自己的創造者 · 第 8 / 25 章 · 5000–6000 · 英中對照 · 校訂中

Jo arrived with the unpleasant certainty that the simplest explanation was probably the one that had been prepared for public consumption. A court clerk discovers that the human override was disabled during a software update labeled routine maintenance. The place was full of people who needed a quick answer, yet the evidence still belonged to those whose lives it could change. Instead of making a dramatic accusation, Jo marked the hour, the witnesses, and the smallest observable detail in a paper notebook. A clinic poster reminded families that an antibiotic treated bacteria, not every cough. The group did not applaud until the inspector had finished explaining the limitations. An astronomer from the local college donated a star-map print to the children's room. The group promised to computerize the warehouse register with individual logins.

喬琳抵達時，心裡很清楚：最簡單的解釋往往是事先準備好要給大眾聽的版本。法庭書記發現人類覆核功能在一次標成例行維護的軟體更新中遭停用。現場的人都急需答案，但證據仍屬於那些生活可能被它改變的人。她沒有戲劇化地指控誰，而是先在紙本筆記上記下時刻、證人，以及最小卻可觀察的細節。 診所海報提醒家庭，抗生素用來治療細菌感染，不是所有咳嗽。 團隊等檢查員說完測試限制，才鼓掌。 當地大學天文學家捐了一幅星圖給兒童休息室。 集團承諾把倉庫登記電腦化，並設個別登入帳號。

There was a moment of quiet when Rhea set the new material on the table. The update passed through a government administrator's account rather than Rhea's laboratory. What mattered was not how surprising the discovery sounded, but whether anyone could repeat the test. Jo copied the relevant names carefully. If one person later changed the account, the original would still be available for comparison. The disputed case archive also contained smaller, testable observations: A power fault could disable the automatic doors, so a manual release was necessary. The technician told everyone to evacuate the loading area during inspection. The medical panel asked why expenditure on sleep control had tripled while ordinary clinics lost funding.

芮亞把新資料放上桌時，四周安靜了一瞬。更新透過政府管理員帳戶，而不是芮亞實驗室。重要的不是發現有多驚人，而是別人能不能重做同一項測試。喬琳仔細抄下相關姓名；如果日後有人更改說法，原件仍可供比對。有爭議的案件檔案還包含一些可檢驗的小細節： 電力故障可能使自動門失效，因此必須設置手動開啟機制。 技師要求檢查期間所有人撤離裝卸區。 醫療小組詢問，為何睡眠控制支出增加三倍，一般診所預算卻遭削減。

“Will you trust me?” Rhea asked. “With the evidence, yes,” Jo answered, “but not with a blank signature.” It was the sort of answer that could sound cold until someone noticed how carefully she had protected the other person’s right to disagree. Together they made a plan with a backup route, a deadline, and a record of who had accepted each responsibility. An irritable official complained about the extra testing time. The lower district demanded that missing patients' kin be notified before any memory study began. A quiet meditation class used the hall after the swimmers had departed.

「妳信任我嗎？」芮亞問。「證據上可以，空白授權書不行。」喬琳回答。這聽起來很冷淡，直到人們注意到她也小心保護對方拒絕的權利。他們一起訂下有備用路線、期限，以及明確責任分工的計畫。 一名暴躁官員抱怨額外測試耗時。 下層社區要求，任何記憶研究開始前，都必須通知失蹤病患的親屬。 泳客離開後，安靜的冥想課使用大廳。

A representative of the security ministry arrived with an offer to resolve the matter quietly. Quietly, in this case, meant without giving affected people access to the original. Jo declined. She knew an office could be useful and still be dangerously wrong. Her objection was narrow and specific: the proposed decision relied on a document whose origin had not yet been independently verified. The disputed case archive also contained smaller, testable observations: A loose nickel coin was found near the warehouse loading scale. The investigator used the floor map to orient new witnesses. An oriental carpet from the old lounge was moved away from the wet entrance. A child treated for pneumonia had been wrongly classified as suffering from forbidden-dream sickness.

安全部的代表前來，提議私下解決。所謂私下，就是不讓受影響的人看到原件。喬琳拒絕了。她知道一個機關可以有用，也可能犯下危險的錯。她的反對具體而有限：提議中的決定依賴一份來源還沒接受獨立驗證的文件。有爭議的案件檔案還包含一些可檢驗的小細節： 倉庫裝卸秤旁發現一枚鬆落的五分鎳幣。 調查員用樓層圖協助新證人辨明方向。 舊休息室的東方風格地毯被移離潮濕入口。 一名肺炎孩童被錯誤分類為罹患禁忌夢境疾病。

Jo seeks the original authorization instead of blaming the nearest programmer. A few witnesses thanked Jo; others accused her of doing too little. She accepted both reactions without offering a promise she could not keep. Rhea wrote down the complaints, including the ones directed at their own conduct. They were not building a monument to themselves. They were building a process that would still function after they left. The rented lift was still a prototype under limited supervised evaluation. The school's psychiatry adviser urged staff not to treat every anxious swimmer alike. The temporary handrail looked robust but still required a load test.

喬琳追查原始授權，不把責任推給最近的程式員。幾名證人向喬琳道謝，另一些人指責她做得不夠。她接受兩種反應，不許下無法兌現的承諾。芮亞寫下抱怨，包括針對他們自己行為的部分。他們不是為自己立紀念碑，而是建立自己離開後仍能運作的程序。 租來的升降設備仍是只准在有限監督下評估的原型。 學校精神醫學顧問提醒員工，不要把所有焦慮泳者視為相同。 臨時扶手看起來堅固，仍須做負載測試。

By nightfall the newest report was signed by observers who were free to disagree. Jo stood beside Rhea and looked at the distance between them and a public right to challenge every binding judgment. Progress felt less like triumph than like leaving a reliable mark on a difficult road. Someone would challenge that mark tomorrow. This time, there would be a public record with which to answer. A sovereign emblem on the display wall was unrelated to warehouse ownership. A spokesperson read the committee's findings without hiding the unanswered questions. Behind the children's wardrobe, staff found an unused cable channel leading to the relay maintenance shaft.

夜幕降臨前，新的報告由可以提出異議的觀察者簽署。喬琳站在芮亞身旁，看著他們與每一項強制判決都能受到公開質疑的權利之間的距離。進展不像勝利，比較像在艱難路上留下可靠的記號。明天有人會質疑，而這一次將有公開紀錄可以回答。 展示牆上的主權徽章與倉庫所有權無關。 發言人宣讀委員會結論，沒有隱藏尚未解答的問題。 兒童衣櫃後面，員工發現一條未使用的電纜通道，通往中繼器維修井。
