AI法官宣判了自己的創造者 · 第 3 / 25 章 · 5000–6000 · 英中對照 · 校訂中

The morning had already acquired the look of an emergency before Jo reached the scene. Jo tours the original machine-learning laboratory and finds that one training room was omitted from the safety audit. An official wanted a clean story, with a beginning that could be announced and an ending that would require no questions. Jo had learned that reality rarely showed such courtesy. She made room for the people who had been ordered to wait outside. The cashier's handwritten signature on the toy receipt survived two rounds of official records cleaning. A routine checkup form showed that the children were healthy before the forced relocation. Police claimed the midnight searches would curb public panic, but the raids only frightened residents. The railway alarm could deafen passengers standing too close to the old tunnel speakers.

在喬琳抵達現場前，早晨已經有了緊急事件的模樣。喬琳巡查原始機器學習實驗室，發現一間訓練室被安全稽核漏列。官員想要一套簡潔的故事，開頭能宣布，結尾又不用回答問題。她知道現實很少如此客氣，於是請那些被命令等在門外的人進來說話。 玩具收據上收銀員的親筆簽名，熬過了兩輪官方檔案清理。 例行健康檢查表顯示，孩子們在強制搬遷前身體健康。 警方宣稱午夜搜查能遏止恐慌，突襲卻只讓居民更加害怕。 鐵路警報足以震聾站在舊隧道喇叭旁的乘客。

Rhea examined the account twice, once as a friend and once as a witness who might have to defend every word. Its data servers hold simulated cases labeled as real criminal histories. The difference between what they suspected and what they could establish mattered. They placed the new fact beside the previous chapter’s unanswered question and looked for a second source that had not been copied from the first. The disputed case archive also contained smaller, testable observations: The supervisor called the stolen design a disgrace to his department. The crew documented the safe disposal of contaminated fuel instead of pouring it into the tidewater behind the station. A technician recorded her dissent from the first inspection report.

芮亞把紀錄看了兩次：第一次以朋友身分，第二次則像一位必須為每個字負責的證人。伺服器把模擬案件標成真實刑事紀錄。懷疑和能確立的事實必須分清楚。他們把新線索放在前一章未解的問題旁，尋找不曾從同一份資料抄出的第二來源。有爭議的案件檔案還包含一些可檢驗的小細節： 主管稱設計遭竊是部門的恥辱。 船員記錄受污染燃料的安全處置，沒有把它倒進站後海水裡。 一位技師對首份檢驗報告提出並記錄異議。

A crowd gathered around the disputed notice. “You have to choose a side,” someone shouted. Jo answered that she had chosen one already: the side of a claim that survived questions. Rhea did not smile, but moved the most vulnerable bystanders away from the growing argument. The pair could disagree privately without turning their disagreement into a public threat. One family planned to emigrate, yet wanted library access until departure. The false relocation policy did nothing to enrich the children whose school materials were confiscated. A lengthy transport invoice listed train journeys that were absent from the official timetable.

人群圍住有爭議的公告。「妳必須選邊站！」有人喊。喬琳回答自己早已選擇：站在經得起提問的主張那一邊。芮亞沒有微笑，只把最脆弱的旁觀者帶離爭執。他們可以私下不同意對方，卻不能把分歧變成公開威脅。 一家人打算移民，仍希望離開前能繼續借書。 錯誤的搬遷政策沒有改善孩子生活，反而沒收了他們的學習用品。 一張冗長運輸發票列出官方時刻表上不存在的列車旅程。

The institution was not a single villain. It contained frightened clerks, dedicated workers, ambitious supervisors, and people who had learned to hide mistakes. the security ministry wanted the incident classified as routine. Jo asked whose definition of routine was being used and whose losses were absent from the statistics. Nobody at the table could answer without consulting the sealed records. The disputed case archive also contained smaller, testable observations: An older swimmer described the pool as a lifelong place of friendship. The clerk addressed the elderly witness as madam and waited for her complete answer. A melancholy song drifted from the school hall while the hearing continued. The lawyer checked whether a private interview was permissible under the order.

這個機構不是單一惡棍。裡面有害怕的書記、盡責的工人、野心勃勃的主管，也有學會掩蓋錯誤的人。安全部想把事件歸類為例行事項。喬琳追問：是誰定義例行？又有誰的損失被統計漏掉？桌邊沒人能在不看封存紀錄的情況下回答。有爭議的案件檔案還包含一些可檢驗的小細節： 一名高齡泳者說，這座泳池承載了她終生的友誼。 書記尊稱那位年長證人為女士，並等她說完。 聽證進行時，憂傷的歌聲從學校禮堂飄來。 律師查核依命令是否允許私下訪談。

She seals the servers and gives affected researchers a chance to describe how the labels were applied. The decision did not repair everything. It altered one procedure, protected one person, or saved one piece of evidence; on a day when almost everyone demanded a miracle, that was worth recording. Jo insisted that the result and its limits appear together in the official minutes. Anything less would turn a modest gain into tomorrow’s misleading legend. The public speakers continued to preach obedience while officers confiscated the stalls' paper records. The counterfeit stitching was reminiscent of her earliest student experiments. Old rivalry between two clubs made joint planning harder than it needed to be.

她封存伺服器，讓受影響研究者說明標籤如何產生。這個決定沒有修好一切，只改變一道程序、保護一個人，或留下了一份證據；當幾乎所有人都要求奇蹟時，這仍值得記錄。喬琳堅持把成果和限制一起寫入正式紀錄。否則小小的進展又會變成明天誤導人的傳說。 警員沒收攤商紙本紀錄時，公共喇叭仍不斷宣講服從的重要。 仿品縫法令人想起她學生時代最早的試作。 兩個俱樂部多年的競爭讓共同規劃更加困難。

The lights beyond the window did not offer comfort. They showed how many people were still waiting. Jo understood that the investigation might eventually expose her own earlier errors, and she resolved not to erase them. Rhea suggested a short rest before the next hearing. She agreed, because exhaustion could damage judgment as thoroughly as deliberate deception. The suspicious transport account deserved scrutiny from an independent auditor, not an instant accusation. The waiting room was full of suspense before the safety officer spoke. The chain's trademark appeared on advertisements before the public lease was approved.

窗外的燈光沒有帶來安慰，只照出還有多少人在等待。喬琳明白調查最終可能揭露自己過去的錯誤，決定不去抹除。芮亞建議下一場聽證前短暫休息。她同意，因為疲憊和蓄意欺騙一樣可能損害判斷。 可疑的運輸帳戶需要獨立稽核員仔細審查，而不是立刻定罪。 安全官發言前，等候室充滿懸念。 租約尚未核准，連鎖企業商標卻已出現在廣告上。
