倒數五天：語音留言

Five Days: A Voice Note

校慶前七天，主唱消失了 · 第 8 / 25 章 · 1000–2000 · 英中對照 · 校訂中

The morning began quietly in the music building, although Rina knew that quiet did not mean safe. It was a difficult week, and the group had already learned that a confident guess could be worse than an honest question. Rina reviewed the rehearsal book and invited Leo and the four band members to look at the same page. Nobody had to agree before hearing the whole problem. Xia sends a short note confirming she is safe but needs time away from the stage. The band honors the message and does not demand her location. Their task suddenly seemed larger than it had a moment before. Rina made one important choice: the next step would be written down where everyone could check it.

在音樂大樓，這個早晨起初很安靜，但莉娜知道安靜不代表安全。現在已是困難的一週，大家明白，自信的猜測有時比誠實提問更危險。莉娜翻閱排練紀錄本，邀請里歐與另外四位團員一起查看相同頁面。所有人都可以先聽完整個問題，再決定是否同意。當天的第一份紀錄寫道：夏傳來簡短留言，確認平安但需要暫時離開舞臺。樂團尊重留言，不追問她的位置。那一刻，眼前任務突然顯得比剛才更加龐大。莉娜做出重要決定：下一步要公開記下，讓每個人都能核對。

They did not treat the description average as evidence by itself. The group discussed the barbecue openly rather than allow a rumor to decide. The childhood became one more detail to check before the group moved on. Rina made a record of the chopsticks, so everyone could compare it later.

他們沒有只憑「平均的」這種描述就當成證據。 團隊公開討論烤肉，不讓傳聞替大家做決定。 在團隊繼續之前，童年成了需要查核的另一項細節。 莉娜記下了筷子，讓所有人日後能夠比較。

Before acting, Leo asked which facts came from direct observation. The team drew two columns: what they knew and what they had only heard. Rina moved a doubtful claim into the second column because its source was missing. Nobody called the correction a defeat. They wanted the next person to understand the evidence, not to inherit an untested guess.

行動之前，里歐問大家哪些事實來自直接觀察。團隊畫出兩欄：一欄是確知的事，一欄是聽來的話。莉娜把找不到來源的說法移到第二欄。沒有人把這項更正當成失敗；大家希望後來的人能理解證據，而不是繼承未經驗證的猜測。

Leo asked how the command might change their plans. There was still a chance to disappear, if the next test worked. The group discussed the fate openly rather than allow a rumor to decide. At first the answer looked forward, but the team checked again.

里歐詢問指令可能如何改變大家的計畫。 如果下一次測試成功，仍然有機會消失。 團隊公開討論命運，不讓傳聞替大家做決定。 答案起初看來向前，但團隊再次核對。

They made a small test before changing the larger plan. Rina explained each step aloud while Leo watched for a mistake. A second person kept the old record so that the result could be compared later. At first the difference looked too small to matter. Then the four band members noticed that one part of the plan would affect people who were not in the room. The group paused and changed the order of its work. They did not pretend a single success was enough. A good test was something another person could repeat, with clear limits and a way to stop safely.

他們先進行小型測試，再考慮改動大計畫。莉娜逐步大聲說明操作，里歐則注意可能的錯誤。另一個人保留舊紀錄，方便日後比較。起初差異小得像是不重要，後來另外四位團員注意到其中一步會影響當時不在場的人。團隊停下來調整工作順序，沒有假裝一次成功就足夠。真正好的測試，是別人也能重複操作、限制清楚，而且知道如何安全停止的測試。

Another detail changed the mood of the group. Rina answered Xia with one message: "We know you are safe; you do not owe us a performance." The others had a chance to ask questions before the decision became final. A shared choice was not always easy, but it was easier to defend when everyone knew its cost.

另一個細節改變了團隊氣氛。莉娜只回夏一則訊息：「我們知道你平安，你不欠我們一場演出。」大家在決定正式生效前仍有提問的機會。共同選擇不一定容易，但當所有人都知道代價時，就更能為它負責。

The practice room held old posters from bands whose members had long graduated. Rina read the dates on them while the other students took a break. Every group had changed its shape at least once. She wondered why her band had believed a single concert must look exactly as it had in the first plan. The thought made the next rehearsal easier.

練習室裡留著許多早已畢業樂團的舊海報。其他學生休息時，莉娜讀著上面的日期。每支樂團都曾至少改變一次形狀。她不明白自己的團為何相信一場演出必須完全符合最初計畫。這個想法讓下一次排練變得輕鬆。

The latest report called the result gentle, and Rina asked why. Leo asked how the load might change their plans. They examined the meaning and waited for a clearer answer. The group discussed the means openly rather than allow a rumor to decide.

最新報告稱結果溫和的，莉娜追問原因。 里歐詢問負荷可能如何改變大家的計畫。 他們檢查意思，等待更清楚的答案。 團隊公開討論手段、方式，不讓傳聞替大家做決定。

Later, Rina and Leo spoke away from the others. Rina admitted to being afraid of making a decision that could not be repaired. Leo said that fear was useful when it encouraged care, but dangerous when it stopped people from listening. Neither offered an easy promise. They returned to the group and explained what had changed, inviting questions from every person affected. The time they spent together began to feel more personal, though neither owed the other a promise. They could still disagree the next morning, and they could still work on the same problem. That seemed more valuable than winning an argument in front of an audience.

稍後，莉娜與里歐離開人群談話。莉娜坦承自己害怕做出無法挽回的決定。里歐說，害怕若能提醒人謹慎就有用；若讓人不肯聆聽就會危險。兩人沒有輕易保證一切順利，而是回到團隊說明改動，邀請所有受影響的人提問。他們相處的時間逐漸有了更私人的意義，但任何一方都不欠對方承諾。隔天他們仍可能意見不同，也仍能為同一問題努力。這似乎比在眾人面前贏得爭論更有價值。

The reality became one more detail to check before the group moved on. Rina made a record of the servant, so everyone could compare it later. Leo asked how the shelf might change their plans. They examined the source and waited for a clearer answer.

在團隊繼續之前，現實成了需要查核的另一項細節。 莉娜記下了僕人，讓所有人日後能夠比較。 里歐詢問書架可能如何改變大家的計畫。 他們檢查來源，等待更清楚的答案。

By evening the team had completed one useful piece of work, not the whole journey. Rina checked the rehearsal book one last time and wrote both the result and the remaining doubt. Leo read the entry and asked whether a newcomer could understand it without meeting the people who wrote it. The answer was not quite yes, so they added the missing detail. Their shared goal remained to give an honest, safe performance without forcing the missing singer to return. Tomorrow would bring a different test, and the group could not know its answer in advance. For tonight, however, everyone knew exactly what had been done, what had not been done, and who had agreed to the next step.

傍晚時，團隊完成了一項有用的工作，卻還沒走完整段旅程。莉娜最後檢查排練紀錄本，同時寫下結果與尚未解決的疑問。里歐讀完後問：如果新來的人沒見過作者，能看懂嗎？答案還不完全是肯定的，於是大家補上缺少的細節。他們共同的目標仍是在不強迫失蹤主唱回來的前提下完成安全又誠實的演出。明天會有不同的考驗，誰也不能提前知道答案。不過今晚每個人都清楚：哪些事已完成、哪些尚未完成，以及下一步由誰共同同意。

They agreed not to stick until the facts were clearer. The stone became one more detail to check before the group moved on. The latest report called the result whatever, and Rina asked why. When they read whom, the team paused to make the meaning clear.

大家同意等事實更清楚後才棍子。 在團隊繼續之前，石頭成了需要查核的另一項細節。 最新報告稱結果無論什麼，莉娜追問原因。 讀到「誰」時，團隊停下來確認意思。
