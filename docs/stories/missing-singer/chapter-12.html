Four Days: Rewrite the Bridge

The morning began quietly in the music building, although Rina knew that quiet did not mean safe. It was the middle of the work, and the group had already learned that a confident guess could be worse than an honest question. Rina reviewed the rehearsal book and invited Leo and the four band members to look at the same page. Nobody had to agree before hearing the whole problem. The band rewrites the middle section so it can be sung by four voices. Rina adds a pause that lets the crowd breathe and listen. Their task suddenly seemed larger than it had a moment before. Rina made one important choice: the next step would be written down where everyone could check it.

在音樂大樓，這個早晨起初很安靜，但莉娜知道安靜不代表安全。現在已是工作的中段，大家明白，自信的猜測有時比誠實提問更危險。莉娜翻閱排練紀錄本，邀請里歐與另外四位團員一起查看相同頁面。所有人都可以先聽完整個問題，再決定是否同意。當天的第一份紀錄寫道：樂團改寫歌曲橋段，讓四人都能合唱。莉娜加入短暫停頓，讓觀眾能喘息並仔細聆聽。那一刻，眼前任務突然顯得比剛才更加龐大。莉娜做出重要決定：下一步要公開記下，讓每個人都能核對。

Leo asked how the ankle might change their plans. They examined the bark and waited for a clearer answer. They agreed not to click until the facts were clearer. At first the answer looked cultural, but the team checked again.

里歐詢問腳踝可能如何改變大家的計畫。 他們檢查吠叫聲，等待更清楚的答案。 大家同意等事實更清楚後才點擊。 答案起初看來文化的，但團隊再次核對。

Before acting, Leo asked which facts came from direct observation. The team drew two columns: what they knew and what they had only heard. Rina moved a doubtful claim into the second column because its source was missing. Nobody called the correction a defeat. They wanted the next person to understand the evidence, not to inherit an untested guess.

行動之前，里歐問大家哪些事實來自直接觀察。團隊畫出兩欄：一欄是確知的事，一欄是聽來的話。莉娜把找不到來源的說法移到第二欄。沒有人把這項更正當成失敗；大家希望後來的人能理解證據，而不是繼承未經驗證的猜測。

Rina made a record of the delay, so everyone could compare it later. Leo asked how the description might change their plans. They examined the evil and waited for a clearer answer. Someone described the result as exact, which the team needed to verify.

莉娜記下了延誤，讓所有人日後能夠比較。 里歐詢問描述可能如何改變大家的計畫。 他們檢查邪惡的，等待更清楚的答案。 有人形容結果確切的，團隊仍需要確認。

They made a small test before changing the larger plan. Rina explained each step aloud while Leo watched for a mistake. A second person kept the old record so that the result could be compared later. At first the difference looked too small to matter. Then the four band members noticed that one part of the plan would affect people who were not in the room. The group paused and changed the order of its work. They did not pretend a single success was enough. A good test was something another person could repeat, with clear limits and a way to stop safely.

他們先進行小型測試，再考慮改動大計畫。莉娜逐步大聲說明操作，里歐則注意可能的錯誤。另一個人保留舊紀錄，方便日後比較。起初差異小得像是不重要，後來另外四位團員注意到其中一步會影響當時不在場的人。團隊停下來調整工作順序，沒有假裝一次成功就足夠。真正好的測試，是別人也能重複操作、限制清楚，而且知道如何安全停止的測試。

Another detail changed the mood of the group. Rina let the pause after the chorus last three full beats, and the song finally sounded honest to her. The others had a chance to ask questions before the decision became final. A shared choice was not always easy, but it was easier to defend when everyone knew its cost.

另一個細節改變了團隊氣氛。莉娜讓副歌後的停頓維持完整三拍，終於覺得歌曲聽起來真誠。大家在決定正式生效前仍有提問的機會。共同選擇不一定容易，但當所有人都知道代價時，就更能為它負責。

Leo tested the guitar cable twice, then rolled the extra length away from the main walkway. Their songs were about courage, but real courage also meant refusing an unsafe stage. Rina looked at the empty front microphone and imagined the audience hearing a new kind of opening. She still missed Xia's voice. Missing it did not mean she had to copy it.

里歐把吉他線測試兩遍，將多餘的線收離主要走道。歌曲談勇氣，但真正的勇氣也包括拒絕危險舞臺。莉娜望著前方空麥克風，想像觀眾聽到另一種開場。她仍懷念夏的聲音，但懷念不表示一定要模仿。

The guava became one more detail to check before the group moved on. Rina made a record of the novel, so everyone could compare it later. Leo said the situation felt protective, though he could still be wrong. They examined the reply and waited for a clearer answer.

在團隊繼續之前，芭樂成了需要查核的另一項細節。 莉娜記下了小說，讓所有人日後能夠比較。 里歐說情況感覺保護性的，但也承認自己可能判斷錯誤。 他們檢查回覆，等待更清楚的答案。

Later, Rina and Leo spoke away from the others. Rina admitted to being afraid of making a decision that could not be repaired. Leo said that fear was useful when it encouraged care, but dangerous when it stopped people from listening. Neither offered an easy promise. They returned to the group and explained what had changed, inviting questions from every person affected. The time they spent together began to feel more personal, though neither owed the other a promise. They could still disagree the next morning, and they could still work on the same problem. That seemed more valuable than winning an argument in front of an audience.

稍後，莉娜與里歐離開人群談話。莉娜坦承自己害怕做出無法挽回的決定。里歐說，害怕若能提醒人謹慎就有用；若讓人不肯聆聽就會危險。兩人沒有輕易保證一切順利，而是回到團隊說明改動，邀請所有受影響的人提問。他們相處的時間逐漸有了更私人的意義，但任何一方都不欠對方承諾。隔天他們仍可能意見不同，也仍能為同一問題努力。這似乎比在眾人面前贏得爭論更有價值。

The group discussed the roof openly rather than allow a rumor to decide. The team decided to select only after checking the risks. Rina made a record of the spoon, so everyone could compare it later. Leo asked how the swing might change their plans.

團隊公開討論屋頂，不讓傳聞替大家做決定。 團隊決定先確認風險，再嘗試選出。 莉娜記下了湯匙，讓所有人日後能夠比較。 里歐詢問擺動可能如何改變大家的計畫。

By evening the team had completed one useful piece of work, not the whole journey. Rina checked the rehearsal book one last time and wrote both the result and the remaining doubt. Leo read the entry and asked whether a newcomer could understand it without meeting the people who wrote it. The answer was not quite yes, so they added the missing detail. Their shared goal remained to give an honest, safe performance without forcing the missing singer to return. Tomorrow would bring a different test, and the group could not know its answer in advance. For tonight, however, everyone knew exactly what had been done, what had not been done, and who had agreed to the next step.

傍晚時，團隊完成了一項有用的工作，卻還沒走完整段旅程。莉娜最後檢查排練紀錄本，同時寫下結果與尚未解決的疑問。里歐讀完後問：如果新來的人沒見過作者，能看懂嗎？答案還不完全是肯定的，於是大家補上缺少的細節。他們共同的目標仍是在不強迫失蹤主唱回來的前提下完成安全又誠實的演出。明天會有不同的考驗，誰也不能提前知道答案。不過今晚每個人都清楚：哪些事已完成、哪些尚未完成，以及下一步由誰共同同意。

They examined the travel and waited for a clearer answer. They checked whether Upon changed the order of the instructions. At first the answer looked upset, but the team checked again. Rina made a record of the youth, so everyone could compare it later.

他們檢查旅行，等待更清楚的答案。 他們核對「一……就」是否改變指示的順序。 答案起初看來難過的，但團隊再次核對。 莉娜記下了青年，讓所有人日後能夠比較。
