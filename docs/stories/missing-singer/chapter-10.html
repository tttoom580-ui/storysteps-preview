Five Days: A Song for Every Seat

The morning began quietly in the music building, although Rina knew that quiet did not mean safe. It was a difficult week, and the group had already learned that a confident guess could be worse than an honest question. Rina reviewed the rehearsal book and invited Leo and the four band members to look at the same page. Nobody had to agree before hearing the whole problem. Rina adds a quieter accessible performance option for students who cannot stand in a loud crowd. The band tests it with volunteer listeners. Their task suddenly seemed larger than it had a moment before. Rina made one important choice: the next step would be written down where everyone could check it.

在音樂大樓，這個早晨起初很安靜，但莉娜知道安靜不代表安全。現在已是困難的一週，大家明白，自信的猜測有時比誠實提問更危險。莉娜翻閱排練紀錄本，邀請里歐與另外四位團員一起查看相同頁面。所有人都可以先聽完整個問題，再決定是否同意。當天的第一份紀錄寫道：莉娜加入較安靜的無障礙演出方案，讓不適合站在吵雜人群的學生也能參與，並找志願聽眾測試。那一刻，眼前任務突然顯得比剛才更加龐大。莉娜做出重要決定：下一步要公開記下，讓每個人都能核對。

At first the answer looked available, but the team checked again. Rina made a record of the biscuit, so everyone could compare it later. Leo asked how the congratulations might change their plans. They examined the courage and waited for a clearer answer.

答案起初看來可使用的，但團隊再次核對。 莉娜記下了餅乾，讓所有人日後能夠比較。 里歐詢問祝賀可能如何改變大家的計畫。 他們檢查勇氣，等待更清楚的答案。

Before acting, Leo asked which facts came from direct observation. The team drew two columns: what they knew and what they had only heard. Rina moved a doubtful claim into the second column because its source was missing. Nobody called the correction a defeat. They wanted the next person to understand the evidence, not to inherit an untested guess.

行動之前，里歐問大家哪些事實來自直接觀察。團隊畫出兩欄：一欄是確知的事，一欄是聽來的話。莉娜把找不到來源的說法移到第二欄。沒有人把這項更正當成失敗；大家希望後來的人能理解證據，而不是繼承未經驗證的猜測。

The group discussed the fox openly rather than allow a rumor to decide. The team decided to gather only after checking the risks. Rina made a record of the glue, so everyone could compare it later. Leo asked how the goose might change their plans.

團隊公開討論狐狸，不讓傳聞替大家做決定。 團隊決定先確認風險，再嘗試聚集。 莉娜記下了膠水，讓所有人日後能夠比較。 里歐詢問鵝可能如何改變大家的計畫。

They made a small test before changing the larger plan. Rina explained each step aloud while Leo watched for a mistake. A second person kept the old record so that the result could be compared later. At first the difference looked too small to matter. Then the four band members noticed that one part of the plan would affect people who were not in the room. The group paused and changed the order of its work. They did not pretend a single success was enough. A good test was something another person could repeat, with clear limits and a way to stop safely.

他們先進行小型測試，再考慮改動大計畫。莉娜逐步大聲說明操作，里歐則注意可能的錯誤。另一個人保留舊紀錄，方便日後比較。起初差異小得像是不重要，後來另外四位團員注意到其中一步會影響當時不在場的人。團隊停下來調整工作順序，沒有假裝一次成功就足夠。真正好的測試，是別人也能重複操作、限制清楚，而且知道如何安全停止的測試。

Another detail changed the mood of the group. One listener who disliked loud rooms said the new version made her feel included for the first time. The others had a chance to ask questions before the decision became final. A shared choice was not always easy, but it was easier to defend when everyone knew its cost.

另一個細節改變了團隊氣氛。一位不喜歡吵雜空間的聽眾說，新版讓她第一次覺得自己也被包含在內。大家在決定正式生效前仍有提問的機會。共同選擇不一定容易，但當所有人都知道代價時，就更能為它負責。

On the score sheet, Rina left a blank line below Xia's name. It was not a demand that Xia return. It was a reminder that a person could help create a song and still choose not to perform it. Leo looked at the line and nodded. They turned back to the next bar of music, where their own choices were waiting.

莉娜在樂譜上夏的名字下留了一行空白。那不是要求夏回來，而是提醒大家：一個人可以參與創作，也可以選擇不演出。里歐看著空白點頭。兩人回到下一小節，繼續處理屬於自己的選擇。

They did not treat the description gradual as evidence by itself. They agreed not to ignore until the facts were clearer. The length became one more detail to check before the group moved on. Rina made a record of the meter, so everyone could compare it later.

他們沒有只憑「逐漸的」這種描述就當成證據。 大家同意等事實更清楚後才忽略。 在團隊繼續之前，長度成了需要查核的另一項細節。 莉娜記下了計量表，讓所有人日後能夠比較。

Later, Rina and Leo spoke away from the others. Rina admitted to being afraid of making a decision that could not be repaired. Leo said that fear was useful when it encouraged care, but dangerous when it stopped people from listening. Neither offered an easy promise. They returned to the group and explained what had changed, inviting questions from every person affected. The time they spent together began to feel more personal, though neither owed the other a promise. They could still disagree the next morning, and they could still work on the same problem. That seemed more valuable than winning an argument in front of an audience.

稍後，莉娜與里歐離開人群談話。莉娜坦承自己害怕做出無法挽回的決定。里歐說，害怕若能提醒人謹慎就有用；若讓人不肯聆聽就會危險。兩人沒有輕易保證一切順利，而是回到團隊說明改動，邀請所有受影響的人提問。他們相處的時間逐漸有了更私人的意義，但任何一方都不欠對方承諾。隔天他們仍可能意見不同，也仍能為同一問題努力。這似乎比在眾人面前贏得爭論更有價值。

Leo said the situation felt musical, though he could still be wrong. Leo noted per in the margin and asked for an example. Someone described the result as proper, which the team needed to verify. The snail became one more detail to check before the group moved on.

里歐說情況感覺音樂的，但也承認自己可能判斷錯誤。 里歐在頁邊記下「每」，並要求舉例。 有人形容結果適當的，團隊仍需要確認。 在團隊繼續之前，蝸牛成了需要查核的另一項細節。

By evening the team had completed one useful piece of work, not the whole journey. Rina checked the rehearsal book one last time and wrote both the result and the remaining doubt. Leo read the entry and asked whether a newcomer could understand it without meeting the people who wrote it. The answer was not quite yes, so they added the missing detail. Their shared goal remained to give an honest, safe performance without forcing the missing singer to return. Tomorrow would bring a different test, and the group could not know its answer in advance. For tonight, however, everyone knew exactly what had been done, what had not been done, and who had agreed to the next step.

傍晚時，團隊完成了一項有用的工作，卻還沒走完整段旅程。莉娜最後檢查排練紀錄本，同時寫下結果與尚未解決的疑問。里歐讀完後問：如果新來的人沒見過作者，能看懂嗎？答案還不完全是肯定的，於是大家補上缺少的細節。他們共同的目標仍是在不強迫失蹤主唱回來的前提下完成安全又誠實的演出。明天會有不同的考驗，誰也不能提前知道答案。不過今晚每個人都清楚：哪些事已完成、哪些尚未完成，以及下一步由誰共同同意。

Rina made a record of the style, so everyone could compare it later. Leo asked how the unit might change their plans. They examined the vacation and waited for a clearer answer. The group discussed the yam openly rather than allow a rumor to decide.

莉娜記下了風格，讓所有人日後能夠比較。 里歐詢問儲物單位可能如何改變大家的計畫。 他們檢查假期，等待更清楚的答案。 團隊公開討論地瓜，不讓傳聞替大家做決定。
