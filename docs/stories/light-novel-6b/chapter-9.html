A Vacation for the Dark Knight

被勇者隊伍開除後，我替魔王城訂了週休二日 · 第 9 / 25 章 · 5000–6000 · 英中對照 · 校訂中

In the knight barracks above the river, the day started with the kind of crisis that usually inspired a heroic speech and a very large repair bill. The castle's best knight had never taken leave because nobody knew the route he patrolled. Aya preferred lists to speeches. She asked for the time of the first warning, the names of those actually present, and the place where the original instructions were kept. Her small goblin colleague Pipi called this her most frightening magic. Aya did not object. If accurate paperwork frightened powerful people, perhaps the kingdom needed a little more of it.

在河上方的騎士營房，這天以一場通常會催生英雄演說和龐大修理帳單的危機開場。最強騎士從未休假，因為沒有人知道他的巡邏路線。綾比起演說更愛清單。她索取首次警告的時間、真正到場的人名，以及原始命令的保存地點。哥布林同事皮皮稱這是她最可怕的魔法。綾沒有反對。如果準確文書會嚇到掌權者，或許王國正需要多一點。

A model with acne asked for camera lighting that respected her skin. The clinic would administer the follow-up program without sharing medical records. The recall brochure explained refunds in plain language. Aya rejected a campaign that portrayed disability as captivity.

一位有痘痘的學徒要求尊重膚況的攝影燈光。 診所將管理追蹤計畫，但不分享醫療紀錄。 召回手冊以簡明文字說明退款方法。 綾拒絕把身心障礙描繪成囚禁的宣傳。

A practical test interrupted the argument: Aya discovered that maps existed but were locked inside the commander's private wardrobe. Aya insisted that every measurement be recorded before anyone proposed a remedy. She had learned from injured adventurers that a heroic shortcut could move a danger rather than remove it. The castle engineers repeated the test, and a worker chosen by the staff watched the result. When the two records agreed, Aya permitted herself a brief smile. "One step," Leon observed. "One step that belongs to everyone," she replied.

一項實際測試打斷了爭論：綾發現地圖其實存在，卻鎖在指揮官的私人衣櫃裡。綾堅持先記錄每項測量，再討論補救方法。她從受傷的冒險者身上學到，英雄式捷徑可能只是轉移危險，而非消除危險。城堡工程師重做測試，一名由員工推派的工人旁觀。兩份紀錄一致時，綾才短暫一笑。雷昂說：「一步。」她回答：「是大家共同的一步。」

The director tried to cram the recalled coats into a storage cabinet. The technician showed how to detach the defective strap safely. The apprentice's discomfort revealed a problem beyond ordinary fitting pressure. A forthcoming report would describe the replacement material in detail.

總監試圖把召回斗篷硬塞進儲藏櫃。 技師示範如何安全拆下瑕疵固定帶。 學徒的不舒服揭露了超出普通試衣壓迫的問題。 即將發布的報告會詳細說明替換材料。

The easiest solution would have been to let Leon issue a royal command. Aya proposed something less dramatic: She required a second person to learn every emergency route before the knight could leave. A command might produce immediate silence, but it could not produce lasting agreement. She asked the affected people to speak without their supervisors in the room. Leon remained close enough to answer questions and far enough away to let them choose. For a ruler who could frighten armies with a glance, standing quietly by a door was an unexpectedly demanding kind of courage.

最容易的解法是讓雷昂直接下王命。綾提出比較不戲劇化的做法：她要求每條緊急巡邏線都至少有第二人學會，騎士才能放假。命令也許能立刻換來安靜，卻不能產生長久的共識。她請受影響的人在主管不在場時發言。雷昂待在能回答問題、又不妨礙他們選擇的位置。對一個眼神就能震懾軍隊的統治者而言，安靜站在門邊竟是相當需要勇氣的事。

A bright inspection lamp helped illuminate the hidden seam. A pale jade button was tested as an alternative decoration. The apprentices hoped the corrected garments would support a joyous performance. The recall instructions were tested for different levels of reading literacy.

明亮檢查燈照亮了隱藏縫線。 一顆淡玉色鈕扣被測試作為替代裝飾。 學徒希望修正後的服裝能讓演出更愉快。 召回說明依不同閱讀識字程度進行測試。

The test gave them something better than applause: The knight took his mother to the seaside, leaving a thank-you card with a terrible drawing. Aya asked the team to mark not only successes but also delays and near misses. A report that hid mistakes could never protect the next shift. Leon signed the corrected report only after the workers had read it. In the corridor, Cinder the young dragon asked whether this meant adults would stop arguing. Aya said no. "Then why do it?" the dragon asked. "Because next time," she said, "they will argue with facts."

這次試驗帶來比掌聲更有價值的結果：騎士終於帶母親去海邊，還留下畫得很糟的謝卡。綾要求團隊不只標記成功，也記錄延誤與差點發生的事故。隱藏錯誤的報告保護不了下一班人。雷昂等工人讀過更正報告才簽名。走廊裡，幼龍小燼問這是否代表大人不會再爭吵。綾說不會。「那為何要做？」小燼問。她答：「因為下次他們至少會拿事實爭論。」

A narrator described the changes during the accessible product demonstration. A participant reported irritation near one nostril from a synthetic scent. The craft collection's travel photographs had been taken along a quiet peninsula. The dye consultant warned that a potent solvent required safer handling.

旁白員在無障礙產品示範時說明改動。 一位參與者說合成香味刺激了單側鼻孔附近。 工藝系列旅行照片拍攝於寧靜的半島。 染劑顧問提醒，強效溶劑需要更安全的處理方式。

Before the lights went out, they learned one more thing: The adviser tried to charge the vacation to the castle's disaster fund. Aya pinned a copy of the new information beside the first rule of the castle: no binding spell without informed agreement. Someone had tried to turn obedience into a substitute for trust. She intended to do the opposite, even if it meant losing another respectable position. Leon remained in the doorway until the workers finished reading. For once, he did not look like a ruler waiting to be obeyed. He looked like a person learning to listen.

熄燈前，他們又知道一件事：顧問企圖把休假費列入城堡災難基金。綾把新資訊副本貼在城堡第一條規則旁：未經知情同意，不得締結誓約魔法。有人想用服從取代信任，她偏要反其道而行，即使因此再失去一份體面的職位也在所不惜。雷昂留在門口，直到工人讀完。那一刻他不像等待服從的統治者，更像正在學習傾聽的人。

The revised coat marked a revival of Aya's original design principles. The team rejected selective reporting of only favorable test results. One model's stepchild helped test the children's adjustable jacket. A small trout appeared on the sustainable fabric's printed pattern.

改版斗篷象徵綾原始設計理念的復興。 團隊拒絕只挑有利測試結果報告。 一位學徒的繼子女協助測試兒童可調斗篷。 永續布料的印花上有一條小鱒魚。
