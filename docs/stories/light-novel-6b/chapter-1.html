被勇者開除的那天

The Heroic Dismissal

被勇者隊伍開除後，我替魔王城訂了週休二日 · 第 1 / 25 章 · 5000–6000 · 英中對照 · 校訂中

Aya had spent twelve years keeping the Hero's Guild alive with rope inventories, ration cards, and maps that nobody read until something caught fire. At thirty-four, she expected many things from her captain, but not dismissal for refusing a dangerous assignment. Three exhausted porters had just returned from a monster tunnel. The captain wanted them to go straight back for a ceremonial banner. Aya put the rescue sheet on his desk. 'A banner is not a person,' she said. 'And these people need sleep before anyone sends them underground again.'

綾用繩索清冊、口糧卡和地圖維持勇者公會運作了十二年。那些地圖平時沒人看，總要等出事才派上用場。三十四歲的她對隊長早有各種心理準備，卻沒想到自己會因拒絕危險任務遭到解僱。三名筋疲力盡的搬運工剛從魔物坑道回來，隊長卻要他們立刻返回取出典禮旗幟。綾把救援表攤在他桌上。她說：「旗幟不是人。他們需要先睡覺，才能再進地底。」

The clasp on Aya's pay-record case snapped as she left the guild. The porters asked permission to convene beneath the shelter roof. A bear cub peeked from the sack of a traveling animal keeper. The infirmary had recorded dental injuries among workers without protective masks.

綾離開公會時，薪資紀錄箱的扣環突然斷了。 搬運工請求在避難棚屋頂下集合開會。 一隻小熊寶寶從旅途動物照護者的袋子裡探頭。 醫務室記錄到沒有防護面罩的工人出現牙齒受傷。

The captain called her insubordinate. Aya asked him to look at the brass duty clock in the hallway. Its public record showed thirty-six consecutive hours assigned to the porters, though the official papers described the work as voluntary. The clock was not a perfect witness; somebody might have made a recording error. Yet it was better than an angry speech. She copied the entries, asked two workers to verify the transcription, and sealed the copies in separate envelopes. The captain watched her prepare evidence and looked more offended by the envelopes than by her refusal.

隊長說她不服從命令。綾請他看看走廊的黃銅工時計：公開紀錄顯示搬運工連續值勤三十六小時，官方文件卻把工作寫成自願。工時計不是完美證人，也可能登錄錯誤，但總比憤怒的演說可靠。她抄下條目，請兩名工人核對，再把副本分別封進信封。隊長看著她保存證據，比起她拒絕命令，似乎更受不了那些信封。

The guild master behaved like a dictator when he threatened the tired porters. A midnight directive had ordered sick workers toward the monster tunnel. The guild called its overcrowded dormitory a temporary dwelling. The messenger horse began to gallop when the black carriage appeared.

公會長威脅疲憊的搬運工時，行徑就像獨裁者。 一份午夜指令曾要求生病工人前往魔物坑道。 公會把過度擁擠的宿舍稱作臨時住所。 黑色馬車出現時，信使的馬開始奔馳。

A junior adventurer whispered that Aya ought to apologize. 'For refusing to endanger you?' she asked. The porters were listening by the door, too tired even to look embarrassed. She gave them the location of an authorized shelter and told them they could make their own choice about reporting the shift. Then she laid her guild key on the captain's desk. He announced that she was fired. Aya was surprised by how light the key felt once it was no longer hers. She did not feel brave; she felt suddenly, inconveniently unemployed.

年輕冒險者低聲勸她道歉。她問：「為了拒絕讓你們陷入危險而道歉？」搬運工就在門旁聽著，疲累到連尷尬都沒力氣。她告訴他們合法避難所的位置，也說明是否申報這段工時由他們自行決定。接著她把公會鑰匙放上桌。隊長宣布開除她。鑰匙不再屬於自己的那一瞬間，綾竟驚訝於它原來如此輕。她並不覺得英勇，只覺得自己突然而且很不方便地失業了。

Missing rest periods could heighten the risk of a tunnel accident. A janitor had seen the captain remove the overtime sheet from the wall. Aya wrote her motto on the notebook cover: Rest makes rescue possible. A pilgrim outside offered the porters warm bread and a bed.

缺乏休息可能加劇坑道事故的風險。 一名清潔員看見隊長從牆上取走加班表。 綾把座右銘寫在筆記本封面：休息才能讓救援持續。 一名門外的朝聖者提供搬運工熱麵包與床鋪。

Outside, rain polished the cobblestones. A black carriage stopped beside her, its horses moving with the careful dignity of creatures who had been taught to fear paperwork. The driver handed over a vacancy notice. The Demon Castle required a temporary inspector of working hours; applicants needed practical experience, independent judgment, and no fear of difficult supervisors. Aya read the last qualification twice. A demon crest glowed on the wax seal, but the notice also listed salary, housing, and paid rest days. This was either a trap or the most promising job advertisement she had ever seen.

外頭下著雨，石板路被沖得發亮。一輛黑色馬車停在她身邊，馬匹步伐端正得像受過害怕公文的訓練。車夫交給她一張職缺公告：魔王城徵求臨時工時稽核官，應徵者須有實務經驗、獨立判斷，且不怕難搞的主管。綾把最後一項看了兩遍。蠟封上發出魔族徽章的光，但公告也列明薪水、住所與有薪休假。這要不是陷阱，就是她看過最有希望的徵才廣告。

A retired clerk remembered a precedent for refusing dangerous overtime. The captain hoped to reap praise while his workers paid the price. Their rescue cart remained stationary because a wheel was broken. Aya told the captain that loyalty was not a synonym for exhaustion.

一位退休文書官記得有拒絕危險加班的先例。 隊長希望獲得稱讚，代價卻由工人承擔。 救援推車因輪子壞了而保持靜止。 綾告訴隊長，忠誠不是精疲力竭的同義詞。

Aya climbed into the carriage only after copying the terms. She had no reason to trust the Demon King, whom every human story described as a merciless enemy. Yet she trusted a written schedule more than a heroic promise, and she was willing to test whether even a castle of monsters could keep its word. The driver offered hot tea and a blanket. 'Does the Demon King really allow weekends?' Aya asked. The driver considered this. 'Madam,' he answered gravely, 'I believe nobody has ever been brave enough to ask.' Aya smiled for the first time that day.

綾抄下條件才登上馬車。人類故事都把魔王形容成冷酷敵人，她沒有理由立刻相信他。但她相信白紙黑字的排班勝過英雄的口頭承諾，也願意試試魔物的城堡能不能守信。車夫遞來熱茶與毛毯。綾問：「魔王真的准許週休嗎？」車夫想了想，鄭重回答：「女士，我想從來沒有人勇敢到敢問這件事。」綾這天第一次笑了。

The shelter garden needed to transplant young herbs before winter. A trustee of the guild fund refused to show Aya the wage account. Aya demanded unconditional access to her own duty entries. She refused to utter a false confession just to keep her job.

避難所菜園需要在冬季前移植幼小的香草。 一位公會基金受託人拒絕讓綾查看薪資帳戶。 綾要求無條件查閱自己的值勤紀錄。 她拒絕為了保住工作而說出虛假的認罪詞。
