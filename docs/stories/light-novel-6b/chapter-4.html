The Accountant's Secret Door

The trouble began at the castle treasury behind the library, where a perfectly respectable official announcement had made ordinary life almost impossible. The treasury claimed it could not afford paid breaks or replacement guards. Aya examined the announcement twice. It had a seal, a solemn title, and absolutely no explanation of what would happen to the people affected. Even a novice adventurer would have demanded clearer directions before entering a dungeon. Aya had once been dismissed for insisting on that principle. Being unemployed had not cured her of it. She unfolded her notebook and invited the nearest worker to speak first.

麻煩發生在圖書館後方的城堡財務室，一份看似體面的官方公告竟讓普通人的生活幾乎無法繼續。財務室聲稱無法負擔有薪休息及替班守衛。綾把公告看了兩遍。它有印章、嚴肅的標題，卻完全沒有解釋受影響的人會遭遇什麼。連冒險新手進地城前都會要求更清楚的指引。綾曾因堅持這個原則而被開除；失業並沒有治好她這個習慣。她打開筆記本，請最近的一名工人先發言。

The sponsor's addiction to publicity worried the event manager. Aya balanced an elegant aesthetic with practical accessibility. The planner could give only an approximate count of attendees. A museum donor funded archaeology lessons for the apprentices' families.

贊助商沉迷曝光的態度讓活動經理擔心。 綾在優雅美學與實用無障礙之間取得平衡。 規劃者只能提供來賓人數的大約估計。 博物館捐款人資助學徒家庭的考古課程。

The first useful clue came from someone powerful people had almost forgotten to question: Pipi found duplicate columns labeled as emergency gifts, though the money came from regular tax payments. Aya thanked the witness without asking for a promise of loyalty. A witness should be able to change an honest answer without fearing punishment. She checked how the record had traveled, who had touched it, and whether a copy existed outside the castle. Pipi wanted to celebrate immediately. Aya bought him a sweet bun instead. Celebrations, she said, could wait until the evidence survived scrutiny.

第一條有用線索來自掌權者幾乎忘了詢問的人：皮皮發現帳冊有兩欄重複的「緊急捐款」，款項實際來自常規稅收。綾道謝，卻沒有要求證人承諾效忠。證人應能在不怕受罰的情況下更正誠實的答案。她查清紀錄如何傳遞、誰碰過它、城堡外是否有副本。皮皮想立刻慶祝，綾只替他買了甜麵包。她說，證據通過審查後再慶祝也不遲。

Every volunteer wore a badge with their approved access zone. A controlled blaze on the projection screen simulated stage lighting. An elderly tailor called her devoted apprentice a disciple of fine craft. The fitting demonstration could enlighten buyers about hidden barriers.

每位志工佩戴標示授權區域的識別證。 投影幕上的模擬火光呈現舞台燈效。 老布術師稱自己勤奮的學徒是工藝門徒。 試衣示範可以使贊助人了解看不見的障礙。

There was a moment when Aya nearly reached for the familiar comfort of doing everything herself. She stopped, looked at the people who would live with the result, and said: Aya froze the disputed budget line without accusing a particular accountant. A worker asked what would happen if the plan failed. Aya answered that they would record the failure, repair what they could, and explain what remained unsafe. Leon said this sounded terribly unlike a heroic legend. "Good," she replied. "The workers need a tomorrow, not a legend."

有一刻，綾差點又想照老習慣一個人把事情做完。她停下，看看那些將承擔結果的人，然後說：綾暫停有疑義的預算項目，沒有直接指控任何會計。一名工人問計畫失敗怎麼辦。她回答：記錄失敗、修正能修的地方，並公開還有什麼不安全。雷昂說這聽起來實在不像英雄傳說。她回：「很好。工人需要的是明天，不是傳說。」

Aya asked the host not to make a fuss about her arrival. The seating plan carried an implicit assumption that everyone could climb stairs. Momo agreed to preside over the independent design-credit session. Corporate prestige could not excuse rude treatment of apprentices.

綾請主持人別為她的到場大驚小怪。 座位圖暗含每個人都能爬樓梯的假設。 莫莫同意主持獨立的設計署名公會會議。 公會聲望不能成為粗魯對待學徒的理由。

By evening, the practical result was visible: Leon permitted an independent review despite objections from his oldest advisers. The change was not a miracle; it had required people, money, and the willingness to admit uncertainty. Aya entered the figures in the brass clock's public register. She let the people who had done the work take credit before the officials spoke. Leon watched the room grow less afraid of him. "Is this what a victory feels like?" he asked. "Sometimes," Aya answered, "it feels like a proper dinner."

到了傍晚，實際成果已經看得見：雷昂不顧老臣反對，准許獨立審核。這項改變不是奇蹟，而是需要人手、金錢以及承認未知的勇氣。綾把數字登錄在黃銅工時計的公開紀錄。她讓真正做事的人先領功，再輪到官員發言。雷昂看著眾人漸漸不再害怕他，問：「勝利就是這種感覺嗎？」綾說：「有時候，勝利只是能好好吃頓晚餐。」

One sponsor proposed to privatize the community training program. The warm stage lights seemed to radiate through the silk. The volunteer rotation gave everyone time for dinner. A backstage shaver used to remove lint had a damaged cord.

一名贊助商提議把社區訓練計畫私有化。 溫暖的舞台燈光彷彿透過絲綢向外散發。 志工輪班安排讓每個人都有時間吃晚餐。 後台去毛球的小型電動刮除器電線受損。

Then a new detail appeared at the bottom of the record: A note under the ledger named the Adventurers' Guild as the recipient. Pipi reached for a celebratory stamp, but Aya caught his wrist. "We have not solved it," she said. "We have learned what to ask next." Leon walked her as far as the public stairs. He did not ask where she lived or insist on accompanying her farther. In the world Aya had left, people had called boundaries unfriendly. Here, in a castle ruled by a Demon King, they were beginning to feel surprisingly safe.

接著，紀錄下方出現新的細節：帳冊下方的便條寫著受款方是冒險者公會。皮皮想拿慶祝印章，綾抓住他的手腕說：「我們還沒解決問題，只是知道接下來該問什麼。」雷昂送她走到公共樓梯，沒有問她住哪裡，也沒有堅持繼續陪走。在綾離開的世界裡，人們常說界線很不友善。但在這座由魔王統治的城堡裡，清楚的界線反而令人安心。

A stray cat slept beneath the service corridor's heater. A thin veil covered a historical headpiece in the display. A vineyard owner sponsored reusable garments for agricultural workers. The invitation included a clear warranty for every purchased garment.

一隻流浪貓睡在服務走廊的暖氣下。 展櫃裡一件歷史頭飾覆著薄紗。 一位葡萄園主人贊助農業工人的可重複使用服裝。 邀請資料清楚說明每件售出服裝的保固。
