魔王城第一次公開表決

The First Public Vote

被勇者隊伍開除後，我替魔王城訂了週休二日 · 第 10 / 25 章 · 5000–6000 · 英中對照 · 校訂中

At the Grand Commons beneath the clock tower, everyone appeared to know what the rules were, but nobody agreed on where those rules had come from. The council demanded that Leon cancel the experiment before the summer market opened. Aya stood beside the public brass clock and refused to guess. A name-bound spell could be cast only with informed consent; any working spell used the caster's energy; and the clock recorded hours unless two independent witnesses approved a correction. Those limits had been stated at the beginning of her appointment. She would not let a frightened official quietly invent a fourth rule.

在鐘塔下方的公民大廳，每個人似乎都知道規則，卻沒有人說得清規則從何而來。議會要求雷昂在夏日市集開幕前取消試辦。綾站在公共黃銅鐘旁，拒絕靠猜測。具名誓約魔法必須取得知情同意；施作工作魔法會消耗施術者體力；工時計記錄若要更正，必須有兩名獨立見證人批准。這些限制在她上任時已說明。她不會讓恐慌的官員偷偷發明第四條。

A short film would animate the repair process for customers. Simple arithmetic exposed the missing return figures in the summary. A visitor called the accessible collection awesome after trying it. The riverside café served celery soup during the long meeting.

短片會以動畫呈現來訪者維修流程。 簡單算術揭露了摘要漏列的退貨數據。 一位參觀者試穿後稱無障礙工藝系列非常棒。 河邊咖啡館在漫長公會會議中供應芹菜湯。

The detail that changed the room was unexpectedly small: Independent records showed fewer injuries and the same response time under the new schedule. Aya asked Pipi to copy the original record before anyone moved it. She then requested a second source with a different keeper. The impatient officials wanted to know whether she had found a villain. She answered that she had found a question worth investigating. In the Demon Castle, that distinction was beginning to sound revolutionary. Leon listened without interrupting, which was itself enough to disturb three elderly advisers.

改變現場氣氛的細節出奇地小：獨立紀錄顯示新排班讓受傷減少、應變時間維持不變。綾請皮皮在任何人移動原件前先抄錄。她接著要求另一份由不同人保管的資料。急躁的官員想知道她是否找到壞人。她回答，自己只是找到值得調查的問題。在魔王城，這種區分已經有點像革命。雷昂沒有打斷她，光是這件事就讓三名老顧問坐立難安。

The council checked whether the exhibition ramp's cement base was secure. A trade confederation offered advice on the nonexclusive distribution proposal. A sudden crackdown on returns would have punished honest customers. A playful crocodile costume appeared in the exhibition's children's section.

委員會檢查展示坡道的水泥底座是否牢固。 一個商業聯盟為非獨家通路提案提供意見。 突然嚴格限制退貨，反而會懲罰誠實來訪者。 展覽兒童區出現一套俏皮鱷魚裝。

"Are you always this difficult?" Leon asked, his voice carrying more curiosity than anger. "Only when someone tries to save time by spending other people's lives," Aya replied. She then made her decision: Aya presented the data and asked workers to answer questions without her prompting. She wrote the choice in clear language so nobody could later claim they had misunderstood. Pipi checked the copies, and Momo the dwarf engineer checked the practical details. Aya never confused being decisive with being unquestionable. Even the Demon King was learning why that mattered.

雷昂問：「妳一直都這麼難搞嗎？」他的語氣好奇多於生氣。綾回答：「只有別人想用他人的生命換時間時。」她接著做出決定：綾提出數據，讓工人不受她引導地回答問題。她用清楚的文字記下選擇，讓任何人日後都不能聲稱誤會。皮皮檢查副本，矮人工程師莫莫檢查實務細節。綾從不把果決和不容質疑混為一談。連魔王也慢慢明白其中差別。

The new policy served a dual purpose: safety and clear accountability. The store tried to equalize repair access across all customer groups. A faction of trustees opposed extending the trial. An unsafe launch would incur costs beyond the initial recall.

新政策有雙重目的：安全與責任明確。 門市試圖讓各類來訪者都享有公平維修管道。 一派受託人反對延長試辦。 不安全上市會招致超過初次召回的成本。

For one uncertain hour, the new arrangement seemed almost ordinary: The council approved thirty additional days by a narrow but recorded vote. That was exactly what Aya wanted. A reform that depended on one exceptional person would die when that person left. She trained a replacement, left instructions in plain language, and made sure someone other than Leon held a copy. He seemed mildly insulted by the lack of royal ceremony. She told him that predictable service was a greater honor than a hundred banners. His laughter startled the guards outside.

有一個不太確定的小時，新安排看起來幾乎平凡無奇：議會以些微但公開記錄的票差通過延長三十天。這正是綾想要的。若改革只依賴一位特別的人，那人離開後改革便會死去。她訓練替班者、用白話寫指引，並確保雷昂以外的人也持有副本。雷昂似乎有點介意缺少王室儀式。她告訴他，可靠的服務比一百面旗幟更值得尊敬。他的笑聲嚇到了門外守衛。

Aya called hidden repair exclusions an injustice to customers. The finance dispute contained enough intrigue to attract newspapers. An unrealistically low price could lure customers into unfair terms. Aya stayed obstinate about retaining her craft rights.

綾稱隱藏維修排除條款對來訪者不公平。 財務爭議充滿權謀色彩，足以吸引報紙。 不切實際的低價可能誘使來訪者接受不公平條款。 在保留工藝權上，綾始終固執堅持。

The apparent ending did not last. A messenger announced that the human heroes were approaching the border. Aya hated dramatic timing almost as much as she hated unpaid overtime. Still, she admitted that a story needed the next question to remain honest. She marked the evidence with the date, gave the workers a copy, and closed her notebook. Leon asked whether she was frightened. "Yes," she answered, "but not enough to leave other people alone with the problem." He nodded as if that mattered more than any declaration of loyalty.

眼前的結尾沒有維持太久。信使宣布人類勇者已經接近邊境。綾討厭戲劇化的時機，幾乎和討厭無薪加班一樣。但她承認，一個誠實的故事得保留下一個問題。她在證據上註記日期、交給工人副本，再闔上筆記本。雷昂問她害不害怕。她回答：「怕，但還不至於把問題丟給別人承擔。」他點頭，彷彿這比任何效忠宣誓都重要。

A pickpocket incident near the river prompted extra security at the event. Leon arrived punctual for their first walk outside work. Both lawyers praised the restraint shown during the public hearing. Exhaustion made Leon stagger slightly after the late review.

河邊發生扒手事件，活動因此加強保全。 第一次工作以外的散步，雷昂準時到達。 雙方公證員都稱讚公開聽證展現的克制。 深夜檢討後，疲勞讓雷昂微微踉蹌。
