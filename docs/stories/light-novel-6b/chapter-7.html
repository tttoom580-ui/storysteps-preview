吃掉星期日的契約

The Contract That Ate Sundays

Nobody at the Hall of Binding Seals had expected a woman with a notebook to interrupt the day's established routine. An ancient contract classified every Sunday as a state of emergency. Aya was thirty-four, old enough to recognize an order designed to make someone else pay for its convenience. The handwritten schedule looked ordinary until she asked who was allowed to refuse. Several people stared at her as though she had proposed replacing the castle with a bakery. She wrote down their objections instead of pretending not to hear them. A good plan, she believed, had to survive an honest question.

在誓約封印大廳，沒有人料到一名拿著筆記本的女子會打斷行之有年的日常。古老契約把每個星期日都列為緊急狀態。三十四歲的綾早已看得出，某些命令的便利是建立在別人承擔代價之上。手寫排班表乍看普通，直到她問誰有權拒絕。幾個人瞪著她，彷彿她提議把城堡改成麵包店。她沒有假裝聽不見反對，而是把意見記下。她相信，好的計畫必須經得起誠實的提問。

The archived wedding costume was too bulky for the narrow display case. Aya refused to deem a family dinner a corporate obligation. Momo compared the old trust's concentrated powers to a miniature dictatorship. The fabric seemed to glide over the table as the designer unfolded it.

檔案中的婚禮服裝太笨重，放不進狹窄展示櫃。 綾拒絕把家宴視為公會義務。 莫莫把舊信託集中的權力比作小型獨裁體制。 工藝師攤開布料時，布彷彿在桌面滑行。

Behind the arguments, one stubborn fact remained: Momo showed that the exemption had been added after the original seal was witnessed. Aya separated what the documents proved from what she merely suspected. A wrong date could be a mistake. A wrong date repeated on three independently stamped pages demanded a different explanation. She drew two columns in her notebook and refused to combine them for the sake of a satisfying accusation. Leon asked whether that caution made her work slower. "Yes," she said, "and much harder to overturn."

爭論背後仍有一項無法忽視的事實：莫莫指出，豁免條款是在原封印見證後才加上的。綾把文件能證明的事與自己的推測分開。單一錯誤日期可能只是失誤；若三頁各自蓋章的文件都重複相同錯誤，就需要不同的解釋。她在筆記本上畫出兩欄，不願為了好聽的指控混在一起。雷昂問謹慎是否會拖慢工作。她說：「會，但也更難被推翻。」

A handicraft display honored the family's forgotten tailors. An interpreter helped the visiting tailor understand the trust meeting. A lesbian designer asked that her partner be welcomed without intrusive questions. Aya described financial independence as one form of liberation.

手工藝展示向被家族遺忘的布術師致敬。 口譯員幫助來訪布術師理解信託公會會議。 一位女同志工藝師希望伴侶受到歡迎，不必回答冒犯的問題。 綾把財務獨立視為一種解放。

Aya knew the decision would anger someone. Nevertheless, she chose a route that could be explained in public: Aya refused to destroy the contract until its older copy could be secured. Leon did not offer to make her opponents disappear. He asked which resources she needed, and she gave him a list with prices, deadlines, and the people responsible. Their growing trust was made of these unromantic conversations. Aya appreciated his help without mistaking it for ownership. When he smiled at the list, she wondered whether his smile was becoming a problem of its own.

綾知道這個決定一定會惹惱某些人，但她仍選擇能在公開場合解釋的做法：綾拒絕立刻銷毀合約，堅持先保全更早的副本。雷昂沒有提議讓反對者消失，只問她需要哪些資源。她交出列有價格、期限與負責人的清單。他們的信任逐漸由這些毫不浪漫的談話構成。綾感謝他的協助，卻不認為那代表誰擁有誰。當雷昂對清單微笑時，她忽然覺得，他的笑容可能成為另一種麻煩。

A lofty portrait of the guild founder dominated the dining room. Momo helped Aya navigate the complicated trust documents. Pipi warned that overwork would hurt the apprentices before the gala. Aya kept a smooth pebble from the coast in her pocket for calm.

創辦人高掛的肖像占據餐廳最醒目的位置。 莫莫幫綾看懂複雜的信託文件。 皮皮提醒晚宴前過勞會傷害學徒。 綾口袋放著一顆海邊光滑石子，讓自己冷靜。

Not everyone welcomed the outcome, but it was real: A neutral notary confirmed a mismatch between the archive and the active spell. Aya let dissent appear in the minutes. She had seen too many organizations call silence harmony, then wonder why their walls collapsed. Leon offered her tea, not an order, and she accepted after finishing the record. The two sat with a respectful distance between them. Their conversations had become easy, which made the unspoken questions harder. Aya did not rush to answer them.

並非所有人都歡迎結果，但成果確實存在：中立公證員證實典藏本與現行魔法版本不一致。綾准許會議紀錄保留不同意見。她見過太多組織把沉默當和諧，最後還驚訝牆壁為何倒塌。雷昂遞給她的是茶，不是命令，她完成紀錄後才接過。兩人保持尊重的距離坐著。談話變得輕鬆，沒說出口的問題反而更難回答。綾沒有急著給答案。

The proposed gift remained pending until ownership could be confirmed. An archive photo showed workers at an old cotton plantation. A historical photograph showed farmers using a plow before the cotton harvest. The contract preserved the original artist's right to a design royalty.

禮物在確認所有權前仍處於待決狀態。 一張檔案照片拍到舊棉花種植園的工人。 歷史照片顯示棉花收成前，農民用犁耕地。 合約保留原創者收取工藝權利金的權利。

The day's remaining question arrived without any heroic music: The missing witness was said to live beyond the eastern mountains. Aya read the notice aloud so the room could hear the precise words. A threat written on paper was still only a claim until evidence supported it. She secured the document, arranged the next meeting, and went home instead of working through the night. Her former captain would have called that lazy. The Demon King quietly moved the meeting to after breakfast. When Aya noticed, she did not thank him for being considerate; she thanked him for following the schedule.

這天最後的問題沒有任何英雄配樂就出現了：傳聞失蹤的見證人住在東邊群山之外。綾把公告逐字念出，讓房間裡的人聽清楚。寫在紙上的威脅仍只是主張，必須有證據才成立。她保全文件、安排下次會議，然後回家，而非通宵工作。舊隊長恐怕會說她懶惰。魔王默默把會議移到早餐後。綾發現時，沒有因他的體貼道謝，而是感謝他遵守排班。

The uncomfortable dinner would spur Aya to seek independent legal advice. Leon proposed a stroll to the gate, not a private confrontation. A tan leather sample lay beside the unused brooch box. Aya refused to treat workers' design credits as a trifle.

這場不舒服的晚宴促使綾尋求獨立法律意見。 雷昂提議散步到大門，而不是私下對質。 未使用的胸針盒旁放著一塊棕褐色皮革樣本。 綾拒絕把員工設計署名當成小事。
