A Dragon with a Fever

被勇者隊伍開除後，我替魔王城訂了週休二日 · 第 6 / 25 章 · 5000–6000 · 英中對照 · 校訂中

At the volcanic nursery and infirmary, everyone appeared to know what the rules were, but nobody agreed on where those rules had come from. A young dragon named Cinder had been made to light festival torches while ill. Aya stood beside the public brass clock and refused to guess. A name-bound spell could be cast only with informed consent; any working spell used the caster's energy; and the clock recorded hours unless two independent witnesses approved a correction. Those limits had been stated at the beginning of her appointment. She would not let a frightened official quietly invent a fourth rule.

在火山旁的幼龍育兒所與醫務室，每個人似乎都知道規則，卻沒有人說得清規則從何而來。名叫小燼的幼龍生病時仍被要求點燃祭典火炬。綾站在公共黃銅鐘旁，拒絕靠猜測。具名誓約魔法必須取得知情同意；施作工作魔法會消耗施術者體力；工時計記錄若要更正，必須有兩名獨立見證人批准。這些限制在她上任時已說明。她不會讓恐慌的官員偷偷發明第四條。

Aya wanted an airtight privacy policy rather than vague assurances. A publicity campaign could not attain trust through false engagement claims. A congresswoman asked the industry to disclose its advertising practices. Aya's father packed homemade curry for her late dinner.

綾要的是無漏洞的隱私政策，而非模糊保證。 宣傳活動不能靠虛構訂婚來取得信任。 一名女國公會會議員要求產業公開廣告手法。 綾父親替她準備自製咖哩當晚餐。

The detail that changed the room was unexpectedly small: The nursery's medicine had been billed to a different department, hiding the true cost of care. Aya asked Pipi to copy the original record before anyone moved it. She then requested a second source with a different keeper. The impatient officials wanted to know whether she had found a villain. She answered that she had found a question worth investigating. In the Demon Castle, that distinction was beginning to sound revolutionary. Leon listened without interrupting, which was itself enough to disturb three elderly advisers.

改變現場氣氛的細節出奇地小：育兒所的藥品費被列在其他部門，掩蓋實際照護成本。綾請皮皮在任何人移動原件前先抄錄。她接著要求另一份由不同人保管的資料。急躁的官員想知道她是否找到壞人。她回答，自己只是找到值得調查的問題。在魔王城，這種區分已經有點像革命。雷昂沒有打斷她，光是這件事就讓三名老顧問坐立難安。

She did not despise reporters, only their unsupported assumptions. The rumor threatened to eclipse the apprentices' actual achievements. Pipi checked that the event's decorative flowers were not presented as edible. Aya continued to hold her staff in high esteem during the scandal.

她不鄙視記者，只反對沒有根據的臆測。 謠言恐怕會掩蓋學徒真正的成就。 皮皮確認活動裝飾花不會被誤認為可以食用。 風波中，綾仍十分尊重自己的員工。

"Are you always this difficult?" Leon asked, his voice carrying more curiosity than anger. "Only when someone tries to save time by spending other people's lives," Aya replied. She then made her decision: Aya stopped the torch assignment and asked Cinder's guardian to approve treatment. She wrote the choice in clear language so nobody could later claim they had misunderstood. Pipi checked the copies, and Momo the dwarf engineer checked the practical details. Aya never confused being decisive with being unquestionable. Even the Demon King was learning why that mattered.

雷昂問：「妳一直都這麼難搞嗎？」他的語氣好奇多於生氣。綾回答：「只有別人想用他人的生命換時間時。」她接著做出決定：綾停止點火工作，請小燼的監護人同意治療。她用清楚的文字記下選擇，讓任何人日後都不能聲稱誤會。皮皮檢查副本，矮人工程師莫莫檢查實務細節。綾從不把果決和不容質疑混為一談。連魔王也慢慢明白其中差別。

An evergreen tree shaded the bench where Aya read the correction. A children's coat used a soft shark fin as a playful hood decoration. A flake of old paint fell from the guild workshop's window frame. The newspaper photograph had turned ordinary merchandise into a romantic symbol.

一棵常綠樹為綾讀澄清稿的長椅遮蔭。 一件童裝把柔軟鯊魚鰭做成帽子的趣味裝飾。 公會工坊窗框掉下一小片舊漆。 報紙照片把普通商品變成了戀愛象徵。

For one uncertain hour, the new arrangement seemed almost ordinary: Leon carried the sleeping dragon himself, ruining his fearsome public image. That was exactly what Aya wanted. A reform that depended on one exceptional person would die when that person left. She trained a replacement, left instructions in plain language, and made sure someone other than Leon held a copy. He seemed mildly insulted by the lack of royal ceremony. She told him that predictable service was a greater honor than a hundred banners. His laughter startled the guards outside.

有一個不太確定的小時，新安排看起來幾乎平凡無奇：雷昂親自抱著熟睡的小龍，威嚴形象瞬間崩壞。這正是綾想要的。若改革只依賴一位特別的人，那人離開後改革便會死去。她訓練替班者、用白話寫指引，並確保雷昂以外的人也持有副本。雷昂似乎有點介意缺少王室儀式。她告訴他，可靠的服務比一百面旗幟更值得尊敬。他的笑聲嚇到了門外守衛。

Aya missed the comforting monotony of normal pattern cutting. Sketching birds remained her favorite private pastime. The contract forbade using the scandal as an excuse for sudden redundancy. Paparazzi were not permitted to roam through the private workshop.

綾懷念平常裁版時令人安心的單調節奏。 畫鳥一直是她最喜歡的私人消遣。 合約禁止拿醜聞當藉口突然裁員。 狗仔不得在私人公會工坊裡四處遊走。

The apparent ending did not last. Someone left an unsigned order to restart the torch duty. Aya hated dramatic timing almost as much as she hated unpaid overtime. Still, she admitted that a story needed the next question to remain honest. She marked the evidence with the date, gave the workers a copy, and closed her notebook. Leon asked whether she was frightened. "Yes," she answered, "but not enough to leave other people alone with the problem." He nodded as if that mattered more than any declaration of loyalty.

眼前的結尾沒有維持太久。有人留下沒有簽名的命令，要求恢復點火勤務。綾討厭戲劇化的時機，幾乎和討厭無薪加班一樣。但她承認，一個誠實的故事得保留下一個問題。她在證據上註記日期、交給工人副本，再闔上筆記本。雷昂問她害不害怕。她回答：「怕，但還不至於把問題丟給別人承擔。」他點頭，彷彿這比任何效忠宣誓都重要。

Aya called the fabricated engagement story rubbish, but kept her response factual. A fantasy costume showed a heroine who chose not to slay the defeated dragon. The sly caption implied promises neither person had made. Warm vapor rose from her father's curry pot as she entered home.

綾說假訂婚故事是胡扯，但回應仍只談事實。 一套奇幻服裝呈現選擇不殺敗北巨龍的女英雄。 狡猾的標題暗示兩人從未做過的承諾。 她回家時，父親的咖哩鍋冒出溫熱蒸氣。
