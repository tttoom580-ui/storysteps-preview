不要偷偷跟魔王交易

No Secret Deals

被勇者隊伍開除後，我替魔王城訂了週休二日 · 第 22 / 25 章 · 5000–6000 · 英中對照 · 校訂中

At the city courthouse, everyone appeared to know what the rules were, but nobody agreed on where those rules had come from. The adviser promised to repay part of the stolen wages if the king ended public hearings. Aya stood beside the public brass clock and refused to guess. A name-bound spell could be cast only with informed consent; any working spell used the caster's energy; and the clock recorded hours unless two independent witnesses approved a correction. Those limits had been stated at the beginning of her appointment. She would not let a frightened official quietly invent a fourth rule.

在城市法院，每個人似乎都知道規則，卻沒有人說得清規則從何而來。顧問表示若魔王停止公開聽證，就願意返還部分欠薪。綾站在公共黃銅鐘旁，拒絕靠猜測。具名誓約魔法必須取得知情同意；施作工作魔法會消耗施術者體力；工時計記錄若要更正，必須有兩名獨立見證人批准。這些限制在她上任時已說明。她不會讓恐慌的官員偷偷發明第四條。

An abundance of flowers made the unwanted farewell party feel overwhelming. Leon noticed Aya's distress before asking the guests to continue without them. She expressed her fury without insulting the event staff. A children's costume designer packed a playful gorilla mask for the trip.

過量鮮花讓不想要的告別派對顯得令人難以招架。 雷昂察覺綾的不安，才請賓客繼續用餐。 她表達憤怒，卻沒有羞辱活動員工。 童裝工藝師替旅行收進一張俏皮大猩猩面具。

The detail that changed the room was unexpectedly small: The witnesses found that the proposed amount omitted three villages from the compensation list. Aya asked Pipi to copy the original record before anyone moved it. She then requested a second source with a different keeper. The impatient officials wanted to know whether she had found a villain. She answered that she had found a question worth investigating. In the Demon Castle, that distinction was beginning to sound revolutionary. Leon listened without interrupting, which was itself enough to disturb three elderly advisers.

改變現場氣氛的細節出奇地小：證人發現提議金額漏列三座村莊的補償。綾請皮皮在任何人移動原件前先抄錄。她接著要求另一份由不同人保管的資料。急躁的官員想知道她是否找到壞人。她回答，自己只是找到值得調查的問題。在魔王城，這種區分已經有點像革命。雷昂沒有打斷她，光是這件事就讓三名老顧問坐立難安。

The extravagant farewell could hamper the quiet conversation Aya wanted. A street musician played harmonica outside the café. The studio handbook did not presume that every couple was heterosexual. The sudden music began to irritate Aya after a tiring day.

隆重告別可能妨礙綾期待的安靜談話。 咖啡館外一位街頭藝人吹奏口琴。 公會工坊手冊不預設所有伴侶都是異性戀。 疲憊一天後，突來的音樂開始使綾煩躁。

"Are you always this difficult?" Leon asked, his voice carrying more curiosity than anger. "Only when someone tries to save time by spending other people's lives," Aya replied. She then made her decision: Leon rejected the bargain and left settlement choices to the affected workers. She wrote the choice in clear language so nobody could later claim they had misunderstood. Pipi checked the copies, and Momo the dwarf engineer checked the practical details. Aya never confused being decisive with being unquestionable. Even the Demon King was learning why that mattered.

雷昂問：「妳一直都這麼難搞嗎？」他的語氣好奇多於生氣。綾回答：「只有別人想用他人的生命換時間時。」她接著做出決定：雷昂拒絕交易，讓受影響工人自行決定和解方式。她用清楚的文字記下選擇，讓任何人日後都不能聲稱誤會。皮皮檢查副本，矮人工程師莫莫檢查實務細節。綾從不把果決和不容質疑混為一談。連魔王也慢慢明白其中差別。

Aya chose to mute the group chat while packing. The trip would originate at the institute rather than Leon headquarters. A small pony appeared on the cover of a children's sketchbook. Pipi's predecessor left clear notes about emergency handovers.

收行李時，綾選擇把群組聊天靜音。 這趟行程以學院為起點，而非王室商會總部。 兒童速寫本封面畫著一匹小馬。 皮皮的前任留下清楚的緊急交接筆記。

For one uncertain hour, the new arrangement seemed almost ordinary: The judge appointed an independent manager for the recovered funds. That was exactly what Aya wanted. A reform that depended on one exceptional person would die when that person left. She trained a replacement, left instructions in plain language, and made sure someone other than Leon held a copy. He seemed mildly insulted by the lack of royal ceremony. She told him that predictable service was a greater honor than a hundred banners. His laughter startled the guards outside.

有一個不太確定的小時，新安排看起來幾乎平凡無奇：法官指派獨立管理人管理追回款項。這正是綾想要的。若改革只依賴一位特別的人，那人離開後改革便會死去。她訓練替班者、用白話寫指引，並確保雷昂以外的人也持有副本。雷昂似乎有點介意缺少王室儀式。她告訴他，可靠的服務比一百面旗幟更值得尊敬。他的笑聲嚇到了門外守衛。

Aya declined a surprise preview of the farewell video. Her freedom to leave without guilt felt priceless. They could salvage the evening by moving to an ordinary café. A sneaky photographer waited near the restaurant's service door.

綾拒絕突如其來的告別影片預映。 她能不帶愧疚地離開，這份自由無價。 他們可以轉到普通咖啡館，挽救這個晚上。 一位鬼祟攝影師等在餐廳服務門旁。

The apparent ending did not last. Aya heard that her own dismissal had been arranged to conceal the false volunteer spell. Aya hated dramatic timing almost as much as she hated unpaid overtime. Still, she admitted that a story needed the next question to remain honest. She marked the evidence with the date, gave the workers a copy, and closed her notebook. Leon asked whether she was frightened. "Yes," she answered, "but not enough to leave other people alone with the problem." He nodded as if that mattered more than any declaration of loyalty.

眼前的結尾沒有維持太久。綾得知當初解僱她，是為掩蓋虛假的志工魔法。綾討厭戲劇化的時機，幾乎和討厭無薪加班一樣。但她承認，一個誠實的故事得保留下一個問題。她在證據上註記日期、交給工人副本，再闔上筆記本。雷昂問她害不害怕。她回答：「怕，但還不至於把問題丟給別人承擔。」他點頭，彷彿這比任何效忠宣誓都重要。

Aya kept her own surname on every research document. A sudden torrent of rain began as they left the café. A veterinarian in the volunteer group offered to care for the center's stray cat. The restaurant's violinist stopped playing when the private dinner ended.

綾在每份研究文件上保留自己的姓氏。 離開咖啡館時突然下起傾盆大雨。 志工群裡一位獸醫願照顧中心的流浪貓。 私人晚宴結束時，餐廳小提琴家停下演奏。
