The Witness Who Would Not Bow

In the reopened hero-guild warehouse, the day started with the kind of crisis that usually inspired a heroic speech and a very large repair bill. The former guild adviser denied that any unpaid labor had supported his private business. Aya preferred lists to speeches. She asked for the time of the first warning, the names of those actually present, and the place where the original instructions were kept. Her small goblin colleague Pipi called this her most frightening magic. Aya did not object. If accurate paperwork frightened powerful people, perhaps the kingdom needed a little more of it.

在重新開啟的勇者公會倉庫，這天以一場通常會催生英雄演說和龐大修理帳單的危機開場。前公會顧問否認自己的私人事業曾依靠任何無薪勞動。綾比起演說更愛清單。她索取首次警告的時間、真正到場的人名，以及原始命令的保存地點。哥布林同事皮皮稱這是她最可怕的魔法。綾沒有反對。如果準確文書會嚇到掌權者，或許王國正需要多一點。

The new charter made every board member accountable for documented decisions. Momo used an analogy about shared keys to explain weak governance. Employee representatives gathered in the auditorium before the vote. A cardinal rule required disclosure of any related-party payment.

新章程要求每位董事對有紀錄的決定負責。 莫莫用共用鑰匙的比喻說明治理薄弱。 員工代表在表決前聚集禮堂。 一項最重要的規則要求揭露所有關係人付款。

A practical test interrupted the argument: Workers compared sealed parcels with supply vouchers and identified deliberate double billing. Aya insisted that every measurement be recorded before anyone proposed a remedy. She had learned from injured adventurers that a heroic shortcut could move a danger rather than remove it. The castle engineers repeated the test, and a worker chosen by the staff watched the result. When the two records agreed, Aya permitted herself a brief smile. "One step," Leon observed. "One step that belongs to everyone," she replied.

一項實際測試打斷了爭論：工人比對封存包裹與補給券，辨識出蓄意重複請款。綾堅持先記錄每項測量，再討論補救方法。她從受傷的冒險者身上學到，英雄式捷徑可能只是轉移危險，而非消除危險。城堡工程師重做測試，一名由員工推派的工人旁觀。兩份紀錄一致時，綾才短暫一笑。雷昂說：「一步。」她回答：「是大家共同的一步。」

Leon became more communicative with workers during the reform. A retired union comrade helped explain the old pension agreement. A long meeting gave one director a painful leg cramp. A safety report explained when an injured worker might need doctors to hospitalize them for treatment.

改革期間，雷昂與員工的溝通變得更開放。 一位退休工會同志協助解釋舊退休協議。 漫長公會會議讓一名董事小腿抽筋。 安全報告說明受傷工人何時需要住院治療。

The easiest solution would have been to let Leon issue a royal command. Aya proposed something less dramatic: Aya gave the witnesses control over whether their names would be public. A command might produce immediate silence, but it could not produce lasting agreement. She asked the affected people to speak without their supervisors in the room. Leon remained close enough to answer questions and far enough away to let them choose. For a ruler who could frighten armies with a glance, standing quietly by a door was an unexpectedly demanding kind of courage.

最容易的解法是讓雷昂直接下王命。綾提出比較不戲劇化的做法：綾讓證人自行決定姓名是否公開。命令也許能立刻換來安靜，卻不能產生長久的共識。她請受影響的人在主管不在場時發言。雷昂待在能回答問題、又不妨礙他們選擇的位置。對一個眼神就能震懾軍隊的統治者而言，安靜站在門邊竟是相當需要勇氣的事。

Aya felt an itch beneath the label of a new fabric sample. The reform aimed at company longevity, not one president's reputation. Clear evidence could propel the castle council toward meaningful oversight. The final statement used plain prose rather than complicated legal jargon.

綾覺得新布樣標籤下方皮膚發癢。 改革追求的是公會長久存續，不是單一總裁聲望。 清楚證據能推動城堡議會建立實質監督。 最終聲明使用平易散文式文字，而非艱深法律術語。

The test gave them something better than applause: A neutral judge ordered the records protected and froze the suspect's assets. Aya asked the team to mark not only successes but also delays and near misses. A report that hid mistakes could never protect the next shift. Leon signed the corrected report only after the workers had read it. In the corridor, Cinder the young dragon asked whether this meant adults would stop arguing. Aya said no. "Then why do it?" the dragon asked. "Because next time," she said, "they will argue with facts."

這次試驗帶來比掌聲更有價值的結果：中立法官命令保全紀錄並凍結嫌疑人的資產。綾要求團隊不只標記成功，也記錄延誤與差點發生的事故。隱藏錯誤的報告保護不了下一班人。雷昂等工人讀過更正報告才簽名。走廊裡，幼龍小燼問這是否代表大人不會再爭吵。綾說不會。「那為何要做？」小燼問。她答：「因為下次他們至少會拿事實爭論。」

A magazine's psychic prediction about their romance amused Pipi. An independent referee moderated the employee debate on governance. The relentless media attention could not change the vote's documented result. A relic of the founder's old office was displayed outside the guild meeting hall.

雜誌通靈者對戀情的預測讓皮皮覺得好笑。 獨立仲裁主持人主持員工治理辯論。 媒體不間斷的關注改變不了有紀錄的表決結果。 創辦人舊辦公室的遺物展示在公會會議廳外。

Before the lights went out, they learned one more thing: The adviser offered Leon a bargain that would close the case quietly. Aya pinned a copy of the new information beside the first rule of the castle: no binding spell without informed agreement. Someone had tried to turn obedience into a substitute for trust. She intended to do the opposite, even if it meant losing another respectable position. Leon remained in the doorway until the workers finished reading. For once, he did not look like a ruler waiting to be obeyed. He looked like a person learning to listen.

熄燈前，他們又知道一件事：顧問向雷昂提出私下結案的交易。綾把新資訊副本貼在城堡第一條規則旁：未經知情同意，不得締結誓約魔法。有人想用服從取代信任，她偏要反其道而行，即使因此再失去一份體面的職位也在所不惜。雷昂留在門口，直到工人讀完。那一刻他不像等待服從的統治者，更像正在學習傾聽的人。

A technician had to scrape old paint from the renovated entry ramp. Aya had to scroll through several pages of the castle council statement. The apprentice laboratory required a comfortable sneaker rather than formal heels. The directors tended to underestimate the workers' ability to review financial policy.

技師必須刮除翻新入口坡道上的舊漆。 綾得滑動好幾頁才能讀完城堡議會聲明。 學徒實驗室要求穿舒適運動鞋，而不是正式高跟鞋。 董事低估了員工審查財務政策的能力。
