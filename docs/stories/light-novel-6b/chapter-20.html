鐘聲停下的那一夜

The Night the Bells Stopped

被勇者隊伍開除後，我替魔王城訂了週休二日 · 第 20 / 25 章 · 5000–6000 · 英中對照 · 校訂中

The trouble began at the city during a dangerous thunderstorm, where a perfectly respectable official announcement had made ordinary life almost impossible. Lightning damaged the public clock while floodwater threatened the southern gate. Aya examined the announcement twice. It had a seal, a solemn title, and absolutely no explanation of what would happen to the people affected. Even a novice adventurer would have demanded clearer directions before entering a dungeon. Aya had once been dismissed for insisting on that principle. Being unemployed had not cured her of it. She unfolded her notebook and invited the nearest worker to speak first.

麻煩發生在危險雷雨中的城市，一份看似體面的官方公告竟讓普通人的生活幾乎無法繼續。雷擊損壞公共工時計，洪水也威脅南門。綾把公告看了兩遍。它有印章、嚴肅的標題，卻完全沒有解釋受影響的人會遭遇什麼。連冒險新手進地城前都會要求更清楚的指引。綾曾因堅持這個原則而被開除；失業並沒有治好她這個習慣。她打開筆記本，請最近的一名工人先發言。

The exhibition ramp used a lightweight aluminum frame. The committee praised the breadth of the visitor consultation. A visiting delegate from a commonwealth nation attended the design panel. Apprentices from outside the city stayed in a supervised dormitory.

展覽坡道使用輕量鋁製框架。 委員會肯定來訪者諮詢涵蓋的廣度。 一位來自大英國協成員國的代表出席設計座談。 外地學徒住在有管理的宿舍。

The first useful clue came from someone powerful people had almost forgotten to question: The backup ledger and independent bell tower still recorded the guards' duty hours. Aya thanked the witness without asking for a promise of loyalty. A witness should be able to change an honest answer without fearing punishment. She checked how the record had traveled, who had touched it, and whether a copy existed outside the castle. Pipi wanted to celebrate immediately. Aya bought him a sweet bun instead. Celebrations, she said, could wait until the evidence survived scrutiny.

第一條有用線索來自掌權者幾乎忘了詢問的人：備援帳冊與獨立鐘塔仍保存守衛工時。綾道謝，卻沒有要求證人承諾效忠。證人應能在不怕受罰的情況下更正誠實的答案。她查清紀錄如何傳遞、誰碰過它、城堡外是否有副本。皮皮想立刻慶祝，綾只替他買了甜麵包。她說，證據通過審查後再慶祝也不遲。

A fashion editorial featured the repair process rather than celebrity romance. The policy prohibited exclusion from repairs based on body size. An apprentice compared the guild agreement rumor to a familiar fable about greed. A fragrant pot of tea stood beside the design consultation desk.

祭典專題報導維修流程，而非名人戀情。 政策禁止因身形而排除維修服務。 一名學徒把公會協議傳聞比作常見的貪婪寓言。 設計諮詢桌旁放著一壺香氣四溢的茶。

There was a moment when Aya nearly reached for the familiar comfort of doing everything herself. She stopped, looked at the people who would live with the result, and said: Aya directed a limited evacuation and refused to restart the damaged spell until inspection. A worker asked what would happen if the plan failed. Aya answered that they would record the failure, repair what they could, and explain what remained unsafe. Leon said this sounded terribly unlike a heroic legend. "Good," she replied. "The workers need a tomorrow, not a legend."

有一刻，綾差點又想照老習慣一個人把事情做完。她停下，看看那些將承擔結果的人，然後說：綾指揮有限度撤離，拒絕在檢查前重啟受損魔法。一名工人問計畫失敗怎麼辦。她回答：記錄失敗、修正能修的地方，並公開還有什麼不安全。雷昂說這聽起來實在不像英雄傳說。她回：「很好。工人需要的是明天，不是傳說。」

The inclusion guide stated that homosexual customers deserved equal, respectful service. The team refused to industrialize an unfinished technique without safety tests. Green ivy framed the exhibition's courtyard entrance. A small bird chose to perch above the open entrance.

共融指引寫明同性戀來訪者應享有平等且受尊重的服務。 安全測試前，團隊拒絕把未完成技術工業化量產。 綠色常春藤圍繞展覽中庭入口。 一隻小鳥選擇停棲在敞開入口上方。

By evening, the practical result was visible: Leon worked alongside the volunteers but went to the infirmary when his magic reserves fell dangerously low. The change was not a miracle; it had required people, money, and the willingness to admit uncertainty. Aya entered the figures in the brass clock's public register. She let the people who had done the work take credit before the officials spoke. Leon watched the room grow less afraid of him. "Is this what a victory feels like?" he asked. "Sometimes," Aya answered, "it feels like a proper dinner."

到了傍晚，實際成果已經看得見：雷昂與志工並肩工作，魔力降到危險程度時便前往醫務室。這項改變不是奇蹟，而是需要人手、金錢以及承認未知的勇氣。綾把數字登錄在黃銅工時計的公開紀錄。她讓真正做事的人先領功，再輪到官員發言。雷昂看著眾人漸漸不再害怕他，問：「勝利就是這種感覺嗎？」綾說：「有時候，勝利只是能好好吃頓晚餐。」

The material report listed petroleum-based fibers separately from recycled ones. The dye supplier documented the purity of the test chemicals. An emergency drill considered how a strong quake would affect the exhibition. Workers' representatives warned that ignored safety demands could provoke revolt.

材料報告把石油基纖維與回收纖維分開列示。 染劑供應商記錄測試化學品的純度。 緊急演練考慮地震震動時會發生什麼。 工人代表提醒，忽視安全訴求可能引發反抗。

Then a new detail appeared at the bottom of the record: The city survived, and the council finally saw why rest was part of readiness. Pipi reached for a celebratory stamp, but Aya caught his wrist. "We have not solved it," she said. "We have learned what to ask next." Leon walked her as far as the public stairs. He did not ask where she lived or insist on accompanying her farther. In the world Aya had left, people had called boundaries unfriendly. Here, in a castle ruled by a Demon King, they were beginning to feel surprisingly safe.

接著，紀錄下方出現新的細節：城市撐過風暴，議會終於明白休息正是應變能力的一部分。皮皮想拿慶祝印章，綾抓住他的手腕說：「我們還沒解決問題，只是知道接下來該問什麼。」雷昂送她走到公共樓梯，沒有問她住哪裡，也沒有堅持繼續陪走。在綾離開的世界裡，人們常說界線很不友善。但在這座由魔王統治的城堡裡，清楚的界線反而令人安心。

The travel line included a rugged jacket for difficult weather. Visitors could skim the report or read the complete technical appendix. The exhibition celebrated the vitality of designers of different ages. Aya began to yearn for the research work waiting overseas.

旅行工藝系列有一件適合惡劣天氣的耐用斗篷。 來賓可以快速瀏覽報告，也可閱讀完整技術附錄。 展覽讚頌不同年齡工藝師的生命力。 綾開始渴望前往海外展開研究。
