那道魔法的最後一個加班日

The Last Workday of the Spell

被勇者隊伍開除後，我替魔王城訂了週休二日 · 第 24 / 25 章 · 5000–6000 · 英中對照 · 校訂中

The trouble began at the Hall of Binding Seals after public notice, where a perfectly respectable official announcement had made ordinary life almost impossible. Some advisers feared that ending the volunteer spell would leave border shelters understaffed. Aya examined the announcement twice. It had a seal, a solemn title, and absolutely no explanation of what would happen to the people affected. Even a novice adventurer would have demanded clearer directions before entering a dungeon. Aya had once been dismissed for insisting on that principle. Being unemployed had not cured her of it. She unfolded her notebook and invited the nearest worker to speak first.

麻煩發生在公開公告後的誓約封印大廳，一份看似體面的官方公告竟讓普通人的生活幾乎無法繼續。部分顧問害怕終止志工魔法會讓邊境避難所人力不足。綾把公告看了兩遍。它有印章、嚴肅的標題，卻完全沒有解釋受影響的人會遭遇什麼。連冒險新手進地城前都會要求更清楚的指引。綾曾因堅持這個原則而被開除；失業並沒有治好她這個習慣。她打開筆記本，請最近的一名工人先發言。

The institute's design workshop overlooked a distant coastline. Investigators worked to compile the complete shipping evidence. The signed sketchbook would complement the verified delivery records. Aya tried to console a worker whose reputation had been damaged.

學院設計工作坊遠望一條海岸線。 調查員努力彙整完整出貨證據。 有簽名的草圖簿可補足經查證的交貨紀錄。 綾試著安慰名譽受損的工人。

The first useful clue came from someone powerful people had almost forgotten to question: The new council produced a funded staffing plan and an independently tested emergency roster. Aya thanked the witness without asking for a promise of loyalty. A witness should be able to change an honest answer without fearing punishment. She checked how the record had traveled, who had touched it, and whether a copy existed outside the castle. Pipi wanted to celebrate immediately. Aya bought him a sweet bun instead. Celebrations, she said, could wait until the evidence survived scrutiny.

第一條有用線索來自掌權者幾乎忘了詢問的人：新委員會提出有經費來源的人力計畫與獨立測試的緊急班表。綾道謝，卻沒有要求證人承諾效忠。證人應能在不怕受罰的情況下更正誠實的答案。她查清紀錄如何傳遞、誰碰過它、城堡外是否有副本。皮皮想立刻慶祝，綾只替他買了甜麵包。她說，證據通過審查後再慶祝也不遲。

The tribunal could not detain an executive without the proper legal process. Momo used careful diplomacy when explaining difficult findings to the families. The audit traced a downward adjustment in the reported repair costs. The castle council adopted drastic changes only after independent review.

沒有合法程序，審理機關不能拘留高層。 莫莫向家屬說明困難結果時展現謹慎的外交手腕。 稽核追查報表中維修成本遭向下調整的情況。 城堡議會經獨立審查後才採取劇烈改革。

There was a moment when Aya nearly reached for the familiar comfort of doing everything herself. She stopped, looked at the people who would live with the result, and said: Aya let the workers vote before any new binding spell was permitted. A worker asked what would happen if the plan failed. Aya answered that they would record the failure, repair what they could, and explain what remained unsafe. Leon said this sounded terribly unlike a heroic legend. "Good," she replied. "The workers need a tomorrow, not a legend."

有一刻，綾差點又想照老習慣一個人把事情做完。她停下，看看那些將承擔結果的人，然後說：綾讓工人在准許任何新誓約魔法前先表決。一名工人問計畫失敗怎麼辦。她回答：記錄失敗、修正能修的地方，並公開還有什麼不安全。雷昂說這聽起來實在不像英雄傳說。她回：「很好。工人需要的是明天，不是傳說。」

A glacier photograph inspired the research team's winter fabric palette. Aya refused to accept a company announcement as unquestionable gospel. A false online account tried to hijack the public conversation about compensation. Leon accepted criticism rather than calling every opponent a hypocrite.

冰河照片啟發研究團隊的冬季布料色彩。 綾拒絕把公會公告當成不容質疑的金科玉律。 假網路帳號試圖劫持關於補償的公開討論。 雷昂接受批評，沒有把每個反對者都稱為偽君子。

By evening, the practical result was visible: The false spell was retired, with its history preserved so nobody could deny the harm. The change was not a miracle; it had required people, money, and the willingness to admit uncertainty. Aya entered the figures in the brass clock's public register. She let the people who had done the work take credit before the officials spoke. Leon watched the room grow less afraid of him. "Is this what a victory feels like?" he asked. "Sometimes," Aya answered, "it feels like a proper dinner."

到了傍晚，實際成果已經看得見：虛假魔法正式退役，歷史紀錄保留下來，讓傷害不能遭否認。這項改變不是奇蹟，而是需要人手、金錢以及承認未知的勇氣。綾把數字登錄在黃銅工時計的公開紀錄。她讓真正做事的人先領功，再輪到官員發言。雷昂看著眾人漸漸不再害怕他，問：「勝利就是這種感覺嗎？」綾說：「有時候，勝利只是能好好吃頓晚餐。」

A familiar advertising jingle played quietly in the hearing lobby. Aya felt lonesome for a moment after the long remote hearing. A map showed the institute's longitude alongside its local time zone. The notary warned against calling someone monstrous before individual guilt was proven.

聽證大廳輕輕播放熟悉的廣告短曲。 漫長遠端聽證後，綾短暫感到孤單。 地圖標示學院經度和當地時區。 公證員提醒，個人罪責證明前不應稱人為怪物。

Then a new detail appeared at the bottom of the record: Aya received an offer to direct the independent labor academy, far from the castle. Pipi reached for a celebratory stamp, but Aya caught his wrist. "We have not solved it," she said. "We have learned what to ask next." Leon walked her as far as the public stairs. He did not ask where she lived or insist on accompanying her farther. In the world Aya had left, people had called boundaries unfriendly. Here, in a castle ruled by a Demon King, they were beginning to feel surprisingly safe.

接著，紀錄下方出現新的細節：綾收到遠離魔王城的獨立勞動學院院長邀請。皮皮想拿慶祝印章，綾抓住他的手腕說：「我們還沒解決問題，只是知道接下來該問什麼。」雷昂送她走到公共樓梯，沒有問她住哪裡，也沒有堅持繼續陪走。在綾離開的世界裡，人們常說界線很不友善。但在這座由魔王統治的城堡裡，清楚的界線反而令人安心。

The historical archive documented oppression of junior factory workers. A reef appeared in the coastal photograph beside the research table. Workers showed solidarity by supporting colleagues seeking corrected records. The governance report warned that secrecy could become an institutional vice.

歷史檔案記錄年輕城堡工坊員工受到的壓迫。 研究桌旁的海岸照片中可見珊瑚礁。 工人團結支持同事要求更正紀錄。 治理報告提醒，保密可能變成制度性的惡習。
