真正的第一個星期日

Our First Real Sunday

被勇者隊伍開除後，我替魔王城訂了週休二日 · 第 25 / 25 章 · 5000–6000 · 英中對照 · 校訂中

For the first time in living memory, the bells of the Demon Castle rang on a Sunday without summoning anyone to work. They marked a holiday. Aya stood in the riverside park with a paper bag of sweet buns and watched guards in ordinary clothes discover how uncomfortable leisure could be. Cinder, the young dragon, complained that there was nobody left to order him around. 'Try choosing a game,' Aya suggested. 'That sounds suspiciously like responsibility,' he said. She handed him a bun and let him complain in peace.

在眾人記憶中，魔王城的鐘聲第一次於星期日響起，卻不是召喚誰上工，而是宣告休假。綾提著一袋甜麵包站在河畔公園，看守衛們穿著便服，發現休閒原來也會讓人不知所措。幼龍小燼抱怨再也沒有人命令他做事。綾建議他自己選遊戲。小燼說：「這聽起來可疑地像責任。」她遞給他一個麵包，任由他繼續抱怨。

The academy made its dormitory safe for apprentices with asthma. The two kingdoms signed a bilateral agreement on safety training. Aya greeted her human counterpart across a table without a throne. Older diesel carts would be replaced by quieter electric wagons.

學院讓宿舍環境適合有氣喘的學徒居住。 兩個王國簽署了安全訓練的雙邊協議。 綾隔著沒有王座的桌子與人類對口官員打招呼。 較舊的柴油車將由更安靜的電動貨車逐步取代。

The signed charter had been posted at every gate: a public record of duty hours, paid leave, and the right to decline unsafe assignments. Two independent witnesses were required before anyone altered the clock. The old volunteer spell was no longer active, but its history remained in the archive. Pipi insisted that hiding an embarrassing mistake was how kingdoms repeated it. Momo had already trained replacement inspectors. Aya was proudest of that detail. The new rules did not depend on her standing in every doorway with a notebook.

簽署後的勞動憲章已張貼在每一座城門：公開工時紀錄、有薪休假，以及拒絕不安全工作的權利。任何人要更動工時計，都必須有兩名獨立見證人。舊志工魔法已不再生效，但其歷史保留在檔案庫。皮皮堅持，隱藏丟臉的錯誤只會讓王國重蹈覆轍。莫莫也已訓練好接任稽核員。綾最為這件事驕傲，因為新規則不必靠她拿著筆記本守在每扇門口才能運作。

The guild replaced disposable festival cups with washable ones. The charter prohibited officers from trying to divert training funds. Leon began to doze in the afternoon sun, free of royal summons. Aya laughed when Pipi called Leon her fiancé before either had proposed marriage.

公會以可清洗杯子取代祭典的一次性杯子。 憲章禁止官員企圖挪用訓練經費。 雷昂不再被王命召喚，開始在午後陽光下打瞌睡。 皮皮在兩人還沒談婚事前就稱雷昂是綾的未婚夫，讓她笑了出來。

An offer from the independent labor academy waited in Aya's coat pocket. She had accepted the director's position. It meant living in another city, designing courses for both human and demon apprentices, and visiting the castle as a guest rather than an employee. Leon knew the decision. He had neither asked her to stay nor offered her a grander title to make leaving difficult. He simply wanted to know whether the academy had a safe route home. Aya showed him the timetable and reminded him that she could read a map without royal supervision.

綾的外套口袋裡放著獨立勞動學院的聘書。她已接受院長職位，將住到另一座城市，替人類與魔族學徒設計課程；日後回城堡，是以客人而非員工身分。雷昂知道她的決定，既沒要求她留下，也沒用更高頭銜為難她。他只是問學院是否有安全的回家路線。綾拿出時刻表，提醒他自己不必靠王室監督，也看得懂地圖。

Goodwill helped the treaty, but clear records made it trustworthy. The transport office tested a hybrid system of enchanted lamps and ordinary batteries. A young lad offered to repair the park benches because he enjoyed carpentry. The new travel cloak had a lining soft enough for Cinder's scales.

善意促成協議，清楚的紀錄才讓它值得信任。 運輸所測試結合附魔燈與普通電池的混合系統。 一位年輕小伙子因喜歡木工，主動修理公園長椅。 新旅行斗篷有足夠柔軟的內襯，不會刮傷小燼的鱗片。

They found a quiet bench beyond the festival stalls. Leon removed the ceremonial gloves that made every gesture look like an official decree. 'I would like to visit you,' he said, 'not as your employer, and not as your king. Would you consider dating me?' Aya looked at the man who had learned to ask instead of command. She told him she would keep her new job, manage her own time, and remain free to change her mind. 'Those are not obstacles,' Leon said. 'They are the conditions under which I hope you'll say yes.' So she did.

兩人在祭典攤位外找到安靜長椅。雷昂脫下讓一舉一動都像王命的禮儀手套。他說：「我想去看妳，不以雇主或國王的身分。妳願意考慮跟我交往嗎？」綾望著這個學會詢問而非下令的男人。她說自己會保留新工作、自己安排時間，也仍有改變心意的自由。雷昂回答：「那不是障礙，而是我希望妳願意的前提。」於是她說了願意。

Aya kept the first manuscript of the labor charter in her archive. The false volunteer spell had provoked outrage in three villages. It seemed a paradox that the Demon King grew stronger by letting workers refuse. A neutral fund would rehabilitate workers unfairly dismissed from the guild.

綾把勞動憲章的第一份手稿保存在檔案庫。 虛假志工魔法曾引發三座村莊的公憤。 魔王讓工人學會拒絕，反而變得更強，看似矛盾。 獨立基金將協助被公會不公平解僱的工人恢復職涯。

The next train would leave at dawn. Nothing about their future was guaranteed, and that uncertainty was strangely comforting. They had chosen a first date, not a binding spell. Beside them, Cinder discovered that a holiday gave him enough time to finish two buns and invent a terrible song about the king falling in love. Leon groaned; Aya laughed until she nearly dropped her tea. When the Sunday bells sounded again, nobody ran to a duty post. The kingdom had not become perfect. It had learned to give its people room for tomorrow.

下一班列車將在黎明出發。未來沒有任何保證，而這種不確定竟意外令人安心。他們選擇的是第一次約會，不是束縛彼此的魔法。身旁的小燼發現休假時間足夠他吃完兩個麵包，還能編一首魔王戀愛的難聽歌曲。雷昂哀號，綾笑到差點打翻茶。星期日鐘聲再次響起時，沒有人奔往值勤崗位。王國並未因此完美，但它學會替人民的明天留出空間。

A renowned dragon historian agreed to teach labor history at the academy. The holiday helped revive an old custom of singing beside the river. Aya found a small note underneath the calendar: You deserve rest, too. The unification of wage records would prevent invoices from vanishing. Leon refused a feast at his private villa and joined everyone in the park.

一位知名龍族歷史學者同意到學院教授勞動史。 休假日使河畔歌唱的古老習俗得以復興。 綾在月曆底下發現小紙條：妳也值得休息。 薪資紀錄的統整將防止請款單再次消失。 雷昂拒絕在私人別墅辦宴會，選擇到公園與眾人同樂。
