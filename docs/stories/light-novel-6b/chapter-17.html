魔王的私人時間，不列入公務

The King's Private Hours

被勇者隊伍開除後，我替魔王城訂了週休二日 · 第 17 / 25 章 · 5000–6000 · 英中對照 · 校訂中

In the rooftop observatory after closing time, the day started with the kind of crisis that usually inspired a heroic speech and a very large repair bill. Advisers expected Leon to be available every hour and treated his silence as an emergency. Aya preferred lists to speeches. She asked for the time of the first warning, the names of those actually present, and the place where the original instructions were kept. Her small goblin colleague Pipi called this her most frightening magic. Aya did not object. If accurate paperwork frightened powerful people, perhaps the kingdom needed a little more of it.

在下班後的屋頂觀景台，這天以一場通常會催生英雄演說和龐大修理帳單的危機開場。顧問們期待雷昂全天候待命，甚至把他沉默當作緊急事件。綾比起演說更愛清單。她索取首次警告的時間、真正到場的人名，以及原始命令的保存地點。哥布林同事皮皮稱這是她最可怕的魔法。綾沒有反對。如果準確文書會嚇到掌權者，或許王國正需要多一點。

The stolen credit was not an abstraction to the cloth mage's family. The elderly designer asked for a chair that would not restrict her airway. The restored archive would amplify voices previously excluded from its history. A small capsule collection honored the original sleeve technique.

對布術師家人而言，署名遭竊不是抽象概念。 年長工藝師要求一張不妨礙呼吸道的椅子。 修復後的檔案將放大過去被排除的聲音。 一個小型膠囊工藝系列向原始袖型技術致敬。

A practical test interrupted the argument: Aya compared the king's magical exhaustion with the ordinary workers' lost rest. Aya insisted that every measurement be recorded before anyone proposed a remedy. She had learned from injured adventurers that a heroic shortcut could move a danger rather than remove it. The castle engineers repeated the test, and a worker chosen by the staff watched the result. When the two records agreed, Aya permitted herself a brief smile. "One step," Leon observed. "One step that belongs to everyone," she replied.

一項實際測試打斷了爭論：綾比較魔王的魔力耗損與一般工人失去的休息時間。綾堅持先記錄每項測量，再討論補救方法。她從受傷的冒險者身上學到，英雄式捷徑可能只是轉移危險，而非消除危險。城堡工程師重做測試，一名由員工推派的工人旁觀。兩份紀錄一致時，綾才短暫一笑。雷昂說：「一步。」她回答：「是大家共同的一步。」

The archive's nighttime curfew protected fragile materials during renovation. The cloth mage's defiance had survived in her carefully preserved letter. The trust reviewed whether past dividend payments had ignored rightful creators. The designer explained the geometry behind the folding sleeve.

整修期間，檔案室夜間宵禁保護脆弱材料。 老布術師的反抗精神保留在精心保存的信裡。 信託審查過去股利分配是否忽略合法創作者。 工藝師解釋摺袖背後的幾何原理。

The easiest solution would have been to let Leon issue a royal command. Aya proposed something less dramatic: She asked Leon what he wanted for himself instead of telling him how to live. A command might produce immediate silence, but it could not produce lasting agreement. She asked the affected people to speak without their supervisors in the room. Leon remained close enough to answer questions and far enough away to let them choose. For a ruler who could frighten armies with a glance, standing quietly by a door was an unexpectedly demanding kind of courage.

最容易的解法是讓雷昂直接下王命。綾提出比較不戲劇化的做法：她問雷昂自己想要什麼，而非替他決定人生。命令也許能立刻換來安靜，卻不能產生長久的共識。她請受影響的人在主管不在場時發言。雷昂待在能回答問題、又不妨礙他們選擇的位置。對一個眼神就能震懾軍隊的統治者而言，安靜站在門邊竟是相當需要勇氣的事。

Visitors received grapefruit tea after the long interview. Aya refused to let the scandal harden her into distrust of everyone. The museum offered the elder tailor an honorary advisory role. One historical costume depicted a heroine trying to imprison a corrupt ruler.

漫長訪談後，來賓喝到葡萄柚茶。 綾不願讓醜聞使自己變得對所有人都不信任。 博物館提供老布術師榮譽顧問職務。 一件歷史戲服描繪女英雄逃離監禁。

The test gave them something better than applause: He asked for one evening without royal duties and invited Aya to watch the meteor shower. Aya asked the team to mark not only successes but also delays and near misses. A report that hid mistakes could never protect the next shift. Leon signed the corrected report only after the workers had read it. In the corridor, Cinder the young dragon asked whether this meant adults would stop arguing. Aya said no. "Then why do it?" the dragon asked. "Because next time," she said, "they will argue with facts."

這次試驗帶來比掌聲更有價值的結果：他希望有一個不處理王務的晚上，並邀綾一起看流星雨。綾要求團隊不只標記成功，也記錄延誤與差點發生的事故。隱藏錯誤的報告保護不了下一班人。雷昂等工人讀過更正報告才簽名。走廊裡，幼龍小燼問這是否代表大人不會再爭吵。綾說不會。「那為何要做？」小燼問。她答：「因為下次他們至少會拿事實爭論。」

A notable difference appeared between the old pattern and later copies. A local playwright offered to document the cloth mages' history with consent. A short procession of apprentices carried the corrected credit cards. The archive stored a serial number for each original design sample.

舊版型與後來複製品之間出現顯著差異。 本地劇作家提議在取得同意後記錄布術師歷史。 一小隊學徒依序帶著更正後的署名卡走過。 檔案庫為每件原始設計樣品保存序號。

Before the lights went out, they learned one more thing: Aya accepted the invitation but said it was not a promise about tomorrow. Aya pinned a copy of the new information beside the first rule of the castle: no binding spell without informed agreement. Someone had tried to turn obedience into a substitute for trust. She intended to do the opposite, even if it meant losing another respectable position. Leon remained in the doorway until the workers finished reading. For once, he did not look like a ruler waiting to be obeyed. He looked like a person learning to listen.

熄燈前，他們又知道一件事：綾接受邀約，卻說那不代表對明天作出承諾。綾把新資訊副本貼在城堡第一條規則旁：未經知情同意，不得締結誓約魔法。有人想用服從取代信任，她偏要反其道而行，即使因此再失去一份體面的職位也在所不惜。雷昂留在門口，直到工人讀完。那一刻他不像等待服從的統治者，更像正在學習傾聽的人。

A sociology student interviewed workers about the meaning of authorship. Warm tea helped soothe the elderly designer after the emotional interview. Journalists began to swarm near the archive entrance before the announcement. The corrected names moved upward on the exhibition's credit board.

社會學學生訪談工人對創作者署名的看法。 情緒激動的訪談後，熱茶幫年長工藝師平靜。 公告前，記者開始湧向檔案庫入口。 更正後的姓名被移到展示署名板較上方的位置。
