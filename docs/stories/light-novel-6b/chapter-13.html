把加班變義工的魔法

The Volunteer Spell

被勇者隊伍開除後，我替魔王城訂了週休二日 · 第 13 / 25 章 · 5000–6000 · 英中對照 · 校訂中

In the abandoned temple where work spells were renewed, the day started with the kind of crisis that usually inspired a heroic speech and a very large repair bill. A volunteer spell had relabeled paid night patrols as charitable service. Aya preferred lists to speeches. She asked for the time of the first warning, the names of those actually present, and the place where the original instructions were kept. Her small goblin colleague Pipi called this her most frightening magic. Aya did not object. If accurate paperwork frightened powerful people, perhaps the kingdom needed a little more of it.

在替工作魔法續期的廢棄神殿，這天以一場通常會催生英雄演說和龐大修理帳單的危機開場。一項志工魔法把有薪夜巡重新標記為慈善服務。綾比起演說更愛清單。她索取首次警告的時間、真正到場的人名，以及原始命令的保存地點。哥布林同事皮皮稱這是她最可怕的魔法。綾沒有反對。如果準確文書會嚇到掌權者，或許王國正需要多一點。

The copied account was a major point of contention in the hearing. The fitting team kept cosmetics away from unfinished fabric samples. Pipi left a cracker packet beside Aya's late-night notes. An electrician inspected the emergency lights near the archive.

複製帳號是聽證中的重大爭議點。 試衣團隊讓化妝品遠離未完成布樣。 皮皮在綾的深夜筆記旁放了包餅乾。 電工檢查檔案室旁的緊急照明。

A practical test interrupted the argument: An electrician among the temple keepers found an unauthorized crystal powering the alteration. Aya insisted that every measurement be recorded before anyone proposed a remedy. She had learned from injured adventurers that a heroic shortcut could move a danger rather than remove it. The castle engineers repeated the test, and a worker chosen by the staff watched the result. When the two records agreed, Aya permitted herself a brief smile. "One step," Leon observed. "One step that belongs to everyone," she replied.

一項實際測試打斷了爭論：神殿守護人中的電工發現一顆未經授權的水晶，正維持這項竄改。綾堅持先記錄每項測量，再討論補救方法。她從受傷的冒險者身上學到，英雄式捷徑可能只是轉移危險，而非消除危險。城堡工程師重做測試，一名由員工推派的工人旁觀。兩份紀錄一致時，綾才短暫一笑。雷昂說：「一步。」她回答：「是大家共同的一步。」

A tiny eyelash had stuck to the adhesive on the prototype box. The campaign's glamour could not hide weak account security. Aya refused to humiliate apprentices while questioning their memories. An imminent board hearing made the evidence deadline urgent.

樣衣箱黏膠上沾著一根細小睫毛。 宣傳的華麗掩蓋不了帳號安全漏洞。 詢問學徒記憶時，綾拒絕羞辱他們。 董事聽證迫在眉睫，使證據期限十分緊急。

The easiest solution would have been to let Leon issue a royal command. Aya proposed something less dramatic: Aya ordered the crystal sealed for examination rather than smashing it in anger. A command might produce immediate silence, but it could not produce lasting agreement. She asked the affected people to speak without their supervisors in the room. Leon remained close enough to answer questions and far enough away to let them choose. For a ruler who could frighten armies with a glance, standing quietly by a door was an unexpectedly demanding kind of courage.

最容易的解法是讓雷昂直接下王命。綾提出比較不戲劇化的做法：綾命人封存水晶送驗，沒有氣得當場打碎。命令也許能立刻換來安靜，卻不能產生長久的共識。她請受影響的人在主管不在場時發言。雷昂待在能回答問題、又不妨礙他們選擇的位置。對一個眼神就能震懾軍隊的統治者而言，安靜站在門邊竟是相當需要勇氣的事。

The suspicion continued to linger after the box was sealed. An archival farm collection illustrated clothing designed for livestock workers. The technician used a lens to magnify the access badge's scratches. The guild workshop was no longer entirely reliant on one distributor.

箱子封存後，疑慮仍揮之不去。 農場舊工藝系列展示為畜牧工人設計的服裝。 技師用放大鏡放大門禁證的刮痕。 公會工坊不再完全依賴單一經銷商。

The test gave them something better than applause: Momo and a neutral witness recorded the spell's remaining charge. Aya asked the team to mark not only successes but also delays and near misses. A report that hid mistakes could never protect the next shift. Leon signed the corrected report only after the workers had read it. In the corridor, Cinder the young dragon asked whether this meant adults would stop arguing. Aya said no. "Then why do it?" the dragon asked. "Because next time," she said, "they will argue with facts."

這次試驗帶來比掌聲更有價值的結果：莫莫與中立見證人記下魔法剩餘的能量。綾要求團隊不只標記成功，也記錄延誤與差點發生的事故。隱藏錯誤的報告保護不了下一班人。雷昂等工人讀過更正報告才簽名。走廊裡，幼龍小燼問這是否代表大人不會再爭吵。綾說不會。「那為何要做？」小燼問。她答：「因為下次他們至少會拿事實爭論。」

The castle workshop's sanitation checklist was separate from its security review. The exhibition hall's scenic backdrop was visible in the suspicious delivery photograph. An archive shutter remained closed during the evidence inspection. The guild needed to stabilize its records before restarting production.

城堡工坊衛生檢查表與保全審查分開進行。 可疑送貨照片裡看得見展示大廳的風景布景。 證物檢查期間，檔案室的百葉窗保持關閉。 恢復生產前，公會得先穩定紀錄系統。

Before the lights went out, they learned one more thing: The crystal supplier was connected to a forgotten royal debt. Aya pinned a copy of the new information beside the first rule of the castle: no binding spell without informed agreement. Someone had tried to turn obedience into a substitute for trust. She intended to do the opposite, even if it meant losing another respectable position. Leon remained in the doorway until the workers finished reading. For once, he did not look like a ruler waiting to be obeyed. He looked like a person learning to listen.

熄燈前，他們又知道一件事：水晶供應商與一筆被遺忘的王室債務有關。綾把新資訊副本貼在城堡第一條規則旁：未經知情同意，不得締結誓約魔法。有人想用服從取代信任，她偏要反其道而行，即使因此再失去一份體面的職位也在所不惜。雷昂留在門口，直到工人讀完。那一刻他不像等待服從的統治者，更像正在學習傾聽的人。

The stolen prototype was stylish but still unfinished. The investigator requested a supplement to the initial witness statement. The login suspension applied to all teams, not only Aya's. The guild workshop's sewing tempo slowed while evidence was preserved.

遭移走的原型樣衣祭典，卻尚未完成。 調查員要求補充第一份證人陳述。 登入權暫停措施適用所有團隊，不只綾的。 證據保全期間，公會工坊縫紉節奏慢了下來。
