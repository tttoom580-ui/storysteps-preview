週休試辦第一天

Weekend Trial, Day One

被勇者隊伍開除後，我替魔王城訂了週休二日 · 第 5 / 25 章 · 5000–6000 · 英中對照 · 校訂中

In the north-gate guardhouse, the day started with the kind of crisis that usually inspired a heroic speech and a very large repair bill. A scheduled day off left only two trained guards at a gate used by merchants and travelers. Aya preferred lists to speeches. She asked for the time of the first warning, the names of those actually present, and the place where the original instructions were kept. Her small goblin colleague Pipi called this her most frightening magic. Aya did not object. If accurate paperwork frightened powerful people, perhaps the kingdom needed a little more of it.

在北門守衛室，這天以一場通常會催生英雄演說和龐大修理帳單的危機開場。排定休假後，商旅常走的城門只剩兩名受訓守衛。綾比起演說更愛清單。她索取首次警告的時間、真正到場的人名，以及原始命令的保存地點。哥布林同事皮皮稱這是她最可怕的魔法。綾沒有反對。如果準確文書會嚇到掌權者，或許王國正需要多一點。

The defective fastener was sold as an accessory, not part of the main coat. The accountant requested a full breakdown of repair expenses. Lin's proposal would bypass the independent review process. No champagne was served at the tense finance meeting.

瑕疵扣件被當作配件販售，而非斗篷本體。 會計要求完整分項列出維修支出。 林朔的提議會繞過獨立審查程序。 緊張的財務公會會議沒有供應香檳。

A practical test interrupted the argument: The old schedule counted statues as guards even though they could not answer an alarm. Aya insisted that every measurement be recorded before anyone proposed a remedy. She had learned from injured adventurers that a heroic shortcut could move a danger rather than remove it. The castle engineers repeated the test, and a worker chosen by the staff watched the result. When the two records agreed, Aya permitted herself a brief smile. "One step," Leon observed. "One step that belongs to everyone," she replied.

一項實際測試打斷了爭論：舊排班表竟把無法回應警報的石像算成守衛。綾堅持先記錄每項測量，再討論補救方法。她從受傷的冒險者身上學到，英雄式捷徑可能只是轉移危險，而非消除危險。城堡工程師重做測試，一名由員工推派的工人旁觀。兩份紀錄一致時，綾才短暫一笑。雷昂說：「一步。」她回答：「是大家共同的一步。」

A chestnut-colored sample revealed uneven dye along the collar. Calling the whistleblower cowardly would only discourage future reports. The original sketch could evoke memories of Aya's first workshop. An illustrated farm collection included a bright fowl on its label.

一件栗色樣衣顯示領口染色不均。 稱檢舉者懦弱，只會阻止往後的人通報。 原始草圖能喚起綾第一間公會工坊的回憶。 農場主題工藝系列的標籤畫著鮮豔的家禽。

The easiest solution would have been to let Leon issue a royal command. Aya proposed something less dramatic: Aya introduced paid standby shifts and trained replacements before reducing a single post. A command might produce immediate silence, but it could not produce lasting agreement. She asked the affected people to speak without their supervisors in the room. Leon remained close enough to answer questions and far enough away to let them choose. For a ruler who could frighten armies with a glance, standing quietly by a door was an unexpectedly demanding kind of courage.

最容易的解法是讓雷昂直接下王命。綾提出比較不戲劇化的做法：綾先安排有薪待命和替班訓練，再調整任何一個哨點。命令也許能立刻換來安靜，卻不能產生長久的共識。她請受影響的人在主管不在場時發言。雷昂待在能回答問題、又不妨礙他們選擇的位置。對一個眼神就能震懾軍隊的統治者而言，安靜站在門邊竟是相當需要勇氣的事。

The trade assistant was hoarse after explaining the recall all morning. The repair kit included lotion safe for handling rough synthetic material. Aya challenged the mentality that higher prices always meant better quality. The audit compared monetary losses with the cost of honest repairs.

交易助理解釋召回一整個早上，嗓音已沙啞。 維修包裡附有處理粗糙合成材料後可用的乳液。 綾挑戰價格愈高品質必然愈好的心態。 稽核比較金錢損失與誠實維修的成本。

The test gave them something better than applause: The gate remained safe through the trial, and the guards went home on time. Aya asked the team to mark not only successes but also delays and near misses. A report that hid mistakes could never protect the next shift. Leon signed the corrected report only after the workers had read it. In the corridor, Cinder the young dragon asked whether this meant adults would stop arguing. Aya said no. "Then why do it?" the dragon asked. "Because next time," she said, "they will argue with facts."

這次試驗帶來比掌聲更有價值的結果：城門平安通過試辦，守衛也準時回家。綾要求團隊不只標記成功，也記錄延誤與差點發生的事故。隱藏錯誤的報告保護不了下一班人。雷昂等工人讀過更正報告才簽名。走廊裡，幼龍小燼問這是否代表大人不會再爭吵。綾說不會。「那為何要做？」小燼問。她答：「因為下次他們至少會拿事實爭論。」

Leon looked moody until he saw the detailed return figures. The repair team became the nucleus of a broader customer safety program. A short rap performance introduced the apprentices at the staff gathering. One executive wanted to slash repair costs despite the recorded defects.

雷昂看起來心情陰鬱，直到看見詳細退貨數字。 維修團隊成為更廣泛來訪者安全計畫的核心。 員工聚會用一段簡短饒舌表演介紹學徒。 一位高層即使知道瑕疵，仍想大砍維修成本。

Before the lights went out, they learned one more thing: The fifth day's report contained a forged overtime signature. Aya pinned a copy of the new information beside the first rule of the castle: no binding spell without informed agreement. Someone had tried to turn obedience into a substitute for trust. She intended to do the opposite, even if it meant losing another respectable position. Leon remained in the doorway until the workers finished reading. For once, he did not look like a ruler waiting to be obeyed. He looked like a person learning to listen.

熄燈前，他們又知道一件事：第五天的報表上出現偽造的加班簽名。綾把新資訊副本貼在城堡第一條規則旁：未經知情同意，不得締結誓約魔法。有人想用服從取代信任，她偏要反其道而行，即使因此再失去一份體面的職位也在所不惜。雷昂留在門口，直到工人讀完。那一刻他不像等待服從的統治者，更像正在學習傾聽的人。

An exhausted accountant began to snore during the overnight document review. The telecommunications contract covered secure calls from trial stores. The team would not unveil the revised coat before independent testing. Pipi defended the workers' reporting rights with unexpected vigor.

徹夜審文件時，疲憊的會計開始打鼾。 電信合約涵蓋試辦門市的安全通訊。 獨立測試前，團隊不會揭曉改版斗篷。 皮皮以出人意料的活力捍衛員工通報權。
