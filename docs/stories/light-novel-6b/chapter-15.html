麵包店屋頂的黑煙

Smoke over the Bakery

被勇者隊伍開除後，我替魔王城訂了週休二日 · 第 15 / 25 章 · 5000–6000 · 英中對照 · 校訂中

Nobody at the riverside bakery district had expected a woman with a notebook to interrupt the day's established routine. A warehouse fire threatened the bakery and the dormitory where apprentices slept. Aya was thirty-four, old enough to recognize an order designed to make someone else pay for its convenience. The handwritten schedule looked ordinary until she asked who was allowed to refuse. Several people stared at her as though she had proposed replacing the castle with a bakery. She wrote down their objections instead of pretending not to hear them. A good plan, she believed, had to survive an honest question.

在河畔麵包街區，沒有人料到一名拿著筆記本的女子會打斷行之有年的日常。倉庫火災威脅麵包店與學徒宿舍。三十四歲的綾早已看得出，某些命令的便利是建立在別人承擔代價之上。手寫排班表乍看普通，直到她問誰有權拒絕。幾個人瞪著她，彷彿她提議把城堡改成麵包店。她沒有假裝聽不見反對，而是把意見記下。她相信，好的計畫必須經得起誠實的提問。

Lin left an untouched cigar beside his private negotiation folder. Leon rejected closure of the audit as part of a private bargain. The castle council's descent into personal bargaining alarmed several trustees. The legal team would not dispose of evidence before the review ended.

林朔把一支未點的雪茄放在私下談判資料旁。 雷昂拒絕把結束稽核當成私下交易條件。 城堡議會滑向私人交易的局面令幾位受託人警覺。 審查結束前，法務團隊不會處置證物。

Behind the arguments, one stubborn fact remained: The public clock showed emergency crews responding promptly, but one evacuation signal arrived late. Aya separated what the documents proved from what she merely suspected. A wrong date could be a mistake. A wrong date repeated on three independently stamped pages demanded a different explanation. She drew two columns in her notebook and refused to combine them for the sake of a satisfying accusation. Leon asked whether that caution made her work slower. "Yes," she said, "and much harder to overturn."

爭論背後仍有一項無法忽視的事實：公共時鐘顯示應變隊伍及時出勤，卻有一道撤離訊號延遲。綾把文件能證明的事與自己的推測分開。單一錯誤日期可能只是失誤；若三頁各自蓋章的文件都重複相同錯誤，就需要不同的解釋。她在筆記本上畫出兩欄，不願為了好聽的指控混在一起。雷昂問謹慎是否會拖慢工作。她說：「會，但也更難被推翻。」

Aya planned to diversify her distribution partners after the trial. The apprentices continued to excel at precise hand stitching. Leon admitted his fascination with Aya's refusal to surrender control. A business rival was not automatically a personal foe.

綾打算試辦後分散通路合作夥伴。 學徒持續在精細手縫技術上表現出色。 雷昂承認自己對綾不放棄控制權的堅持著迷。 商業對手不會自動變成人生仇敵。

Aya knew the decision would anger someone. Nevertheless, she chose a route that could be explained in public: Aya insisted on using only trained teams and opened a temporary shelter. Leon did not offer to make her opponents disappear. He asked which resources she needed, and she gave him a list with prices, deadlines, and the people responsible. Their growing trust was made of these unromantic conversations. Aya appreciated his help without mistaking it for ownership. When he smiled at the list, she wondered whether his smile was becoming a problem of its own.

綾知道這個決定一定會惹惱某些人，但她仍選擇能在公開場合解釋的做法：綾堅持只派受訓隊伍，並設置臨時避難所。雷昂沒有提議讓反對者消失，只問她需要哪些資源。她交出列有價格、期限與負責人的清單。他們的信任逐漸由這些毫不浪漫的談話構成。綾感謝他的協助，卻不認為那代表誰擁有誰。當雷昂對清單微笑時，她忽然覺得，他的笑容可能成為另一種麻煩。

City lights began to glitter along the bridge where they walked. A global sales map divided the trial markets by hemisphere. Aya had made innumerable small choices to protect her design archive. The apprentices' mastery of seams impressed the independent inspectors.

他們散步的橋邊，城市燈光開始閃爍。 全球交易圖依半球劃分試辦市場。 綾做過無數細小決定保護咒紋檔案。 學徒對縫線的熟練掌握令獨立檢查員佩服。

Not everyone welcomed the outcome, but it was real: The fire was contained without sending sick or exhausted workers into danger. Aya let dissent appear in the minutes. She had seen too many organizations call silence harmony, then wonder why their walls collapsed. Leon offered her tea, not an order, and she accepted after finishing the record. The two sat with a respectful distance between them. Their conversations had become easy, which made the unspoken questions harder. Aya did not rush to answer them.

並非所有人都歡迎結果，但成果確實存在：火勢獲控制，沒有把生病或過勞工人送進險境。綾准許會議紀錄保留不同意見。她見過太多組織把沉默當和諧，最後還驚訝牆壁為何倒塌。雷昂遞給她的是茶，不是命令，她完成紀錄後才接過。兩人保持尊重的距離坐著。談話變得輕鬆，沒說出口的問題反而更難回答。綾沒有急著給答案。

Lin Shuo was notorious for aggressive negotiation tactics. Leon rejected the buyout demand outright. The craft collection's public premiere remained scheduled after the audit. A promise of easy power could seduce even a careful executive.

林朔以強硬談判手段惡名昭彰。 雷昂斷然拒絕公會協議要求。 工藝系列的公開首演仍安排在稽核之後。 輕易獲得權力的承諾可能誘惑謹慎的高層。

The day's remaining question arrived without any heroic music: An inventory sheet revealed that lantern oil had been moved the previous night. Aya read the notice aloud so the room could hear the precise words. A threat written on paper was still only a claim until evidence supported it. She secured the document, arranged the next meeting, and went home instead of working through the night. Her former captain would have called that lazy. The Demon King quietly moved the meeting to after breakfast. When Aya noticed, she did not thank him for being considerate; she thanked him for following the schedule.

這天最後的問題沒有任何英雄配樂就出現了：庫存單顯示燈油在前一夜遭到移動。綾把公告逐字念出，讓房間裡的人聽清楚。寫在紙上的威脅仍只是主張，必須有證據才成立。她保全文件、安排下次會議，然後回家，而非通宵工作。舊隊長恐怕會說她懶惰。魔王默默把會議移到早餐後。綾發現時，沒有因他的體貼道謝，而是感謝他遵守排班。

The charity collection refused leather sourced from unverified animal slaughter. The employee fund helped workers facing food insecurity, not literal starvation claims. A retired statesman attended the public ethics lecture as a guest. The vote required tedious review of each trust provision.

慈善工藝系列拒絕使用來源未經查核的動物屠宰皮革。 員工基金協助面臨糧食困難的人，不誇大成普遍飢餓死亡。 一名退休政治家以來賓身分出席公開倫理講座。 表決前必須繁瑣地逐條審閱信託規定。
