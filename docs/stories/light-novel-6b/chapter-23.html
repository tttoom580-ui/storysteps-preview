An Apology Is Not a Contract

Nobody at the guild's crowded assembly hall had expected a woman with a notebook to interrupt the day's established routine. The hero captain asked Aya to forgive him in front of the people he had once disappointed. Aya was thirty-four, old enough to recognize an order designed to make someone else pay for its convenience. The handwritten schedule looked ordinary until she asked who was allowed to refuse. Several people stared at her as though she had proposed replacing the castle with a bakery. She wrote down their objections instead of pretending not to hear them. A good plan, she believed, had to survive an honest question.

在公會擁擠的大會堂，沒有人料到一名拿著筆記本的女子會打斷行之有年的日常。勇者隊長在曾被他辜負的眾人面前請求綾原諒。三十四歲的綾早已看得出，某些命令的便利是建立在別人承擔代價之上。手寫排班表乍看普通，直到她問誰有權拒絕。幾個人瞪著她，彷彿她提議把城堡改成麵包店。她沒有假裝聽不見反對，而是把意見記下。她相信，好的計畫必須經得起誠實的提問。

The timeline ruled out a supposed collision between the two delivery trucks. A comet pattern appeared on the institute's welcome card. The new audit log was designed to deter unauthorized file access. A professor with eccentric ties admired Aya's practical jacket design.

時間線排除兩輛送貨車據稱發生碰撞的可能。 學院歡迎卡上有彗星圖案。 新稽核紀錄設計用來阻止未授權存取檔案。 一位領帶奇特的教授欣賞綾實用斗篷設計。

Behind the arguments, one stubborn fact remained: The original dismissal record proved that the adviser had altered the captain's decision. Aya separated what the documents proved from what she merely suspected. A wrong date could be a mistake. A wrong date repeated on three independently stamped pages demanded a different explanation. She drew two columns in her notebook and refused to combine them for the sake of a satisfying accusation. Leon asked whether that caution made her work slower. "Yes," she said, "and much harder to overturn."

爭論背後仍有一項無法忽視的事實：原始解僱紀錄證實顧問曾竄改隊長的決定。綾把文件能證明的事與自己的推測分開。單一錯誤日期可能只是失誤；若三頁各自蓋章的文件都重複相同錯誤，就需要不同的解釋。她在筆記本上畫出兩欄，不願為了好聽的指控混在一起。雷昂問謹慎是否會拖慢工作。她說：「會，但也更難被推翻。」

An institute employee offered to escort Aya to the laboratory. Every examinee in the design assessment received equal preparation time. The corrected credit appeared beside the original garment. The institute provided a navigation map for visitors with limited mobility.

學院員工提議陪同綾到實驗室。 設計測驗每位應試者都獲得相同準備時間。 更正署名放在原始服裝旁。 學院提供行動不便來賓使用的導覽地圖。

Aya knew the decision would anger someone. Nevertheless, she chose a route that could be explained in public: Aya accepted the factual correction but did not return to the team or promise friendship. Leon did not offer to make her opponents disappear. He asked which resources she needed, and she gave him a list with prices, deadlines, and the people responsible. Their growing trust was made of these unromantic conversations. Aya appreciated his help without mistaking it for ownership. When he smiled at the list, she wondered whether his smile was becoming a problem of its own.

綾知道這個決定一定會惹惱某些人，但她仍選擇能在公開場合解釋的做法：綾接受事實更正，卻沒有回隊，也沒有承諾恢復友情。雷昂沒有提議讓反對者消失，只問她需要哪些資源。她交出列有價格、期限與負責人的清單。他們的信任逐漸由這些毫不浪漫的談話構成。綾感謝他的協助，卻不認為那代表誰擁有誰。當雷昂對清單微笑時，她忽然覺得，他的笑容可能成為另一種麻煩。

Aya's career outlook widened during the overseas research. A paperback guide to textile history rested on her desk. The investigation did not paralyze the guild workshop's normal operations. A lecture examined why a patriot might still criticize national industries.

海外研究期間，綾的職涯前景變得更寬廣。 她桌上放著一本紡織史平裝指南。 調查沒有讓公會工坊日常運作癱瘓。 一場講座探討愛國者為何仍能批評本國產業。

Not everyone welcomed the outcome, but it was real: The workers voted to establish a joint labor council for both kingdoms. Aya let dissent appear in the minutes. She had seen too many organizations call silence harmony, then wonder why their walls collapsed. Leon offered her tea, not an order, and she accepted after finishing the record. The two sat with a respectful distance between them. Their conversations had become easy, which made the unspoken questions harder. Aya did not rush to answer them.

並非所有人都歡迎結果，但成果確實存在：工人投票建立兩國共同勞資委員會。綾准許會議紀錄保留不同意見。她見過太多組織把沉默當和諧，最後還驚訝牆壁為何倒塌。雷昂遞給她的是茶，不是命令，她完成紀錄後才接過。兩人保持尊重的距離坐著。談話變得輕鬆，沒說出口的問題反而更難回答。綾沒有急著給答案。

Aya took a quick peek at her phone before the interview. The wrongly accused employee sounded sorrowful when describing the ordeal. The sleeve pattern formed a careful spiral around the elbow. Leon refused to blame a subordinate without evidence.

訪談前，綾快速偷看了一眼手機。 被錯誤指控的員工描述遭遇時，聲音十分悲傷。 袖型在手肘周圍形成精確的螺旋。 沒有證據前，雷昂拒絕責怪下屬。

The day's remaining question arrived without any heroic music: Leon asked Aya whether she wanted to travel with him once her contract ended. Aya read the notice aloud so the room could hear the precise words. A threat written on paper was still only a claim until evidence supported it. She secured the document, arranged the next meeting, and went home instead of working through the night. Her former captain would have called that lazy. The Demon King quietly moved the meeting to after breakfast. When Aya noticed, she did not thank him for being considerate; she thanked him for following the schedule.

這天最後的問題沒有任何英雄配樂就出現了：雷昂問綾在合約結束後，是否願意和他一起旅行。綾把公告逐字念出，讓房間裡的人聽清楚。寫在紙上的威脅仍只是主張，必須有證據才成立。她保全文件、安排下次會議，然後回家，而非通宵工作。舊隊長恐怕會說她懶惰。魔王默默把會議移到早餐後。綾發現時，沒有因他的體貼道謝，而是感謝他遵守排班。

The institute used secure software to transmit design records. Lights began to twinkle beyond Aya's apartment window. A strip of velvet lined the repaired historical sleeve. Her phone began to vibrate just as the examiner called.

學院使用安全軟體傳送設計紀錄。 綾公寓窗外的燈開始閃爍。 修復後的歷史袖口內襯著一條絲絨。 審查員打來時，她的手機開始震動。
