The Trial of the Royal Ledger

被勇者隊伍開除後，我替魔王城訂了週休二日 · 第 16 / 25 章 · 5000–6000 · 英中對照 · 校訂中

The trouble began at the independent audit chamber, where a perfectly respectable official announcement had made ordinary life almost impossible. The finance minister called Aya's safety rules a wasteful imitation of human politics. Aya examined the announcement twice. It had a seal, a solemn title, and absolutely no explanation of what would happen to the people affected. Even a novice adventurer would have demanded clearer directions before entering a dungeon. Aya had once been dismissed for insisting on that principle. Being unemployed had not cured her of it. She unfolded her notebook and invited the nearest worker to speak first.

麻煩發生在獨立稽核庭，一份看似體面的官方公告竟讓普通人的生活幾乎無法繼續。財務大臣批評綾的安全規則只是浪費的模仿人類政治。綾把公告看了兩遍。它有印章、嚴肅的標題，卻完全沒有解釋受影響的人會遭遇什麼。連冒險新手進地城前都會要求更清楚的指引。綾曾因堅持這個原則而被開除；失業並沒有治好她這個習慣。她打開筆記本，請最近的一名工人先發言。

Public rumors could accumulate faster than the corrections that followed. Aya refused to accustom her employees to unjust scrutiny. Leon's affectionate message contained no demand for a reply. A fashion editorial used astronomy as inspiration for its night collection.

公開謠言累積的速度可能比後續更正快。 綾不願讓員工習慣不公平的審視。 雷昂充滿關愛的訊息沒有要求她回覆。 一篇祭典專題以天文學啟發夜間工藝系列。

The first useful clue came from someone powerful people had almost forgotten to question: Wage records and duty clocks revealed that the emergency donations concealed regular operational costs. Aya thanked the witness without asking for a promise of loyalty. A witness should be able to change an honest answer without fearing punishment. She checked how the record had traveled, who had touched it, and whether a copy existed outside the castle. Pipi wanted to celebrate immediately. Aya bought him a sweet bun instead. Celebrations, she said, could wait until the evidence survived scrutiny.

第一條有用線索來自掌權者幾乎忘了詢問的人：薪資紀錄與工時計揭露「緊急捐款」掩蓋了正常營運成本。綾道謝，卻沒有要求證人承諾效忠。證人應能在不怕受罰的情況下更正誠實的答案。她查清紀錄如何傳遞、誰碰過它、城堡外是否有副本。皮皮想立刻慶祝，綾只替他買了甜麵包。她說，證據通過審查後再慶祝也不遲。

Aya reduced her caffeine intake after several sleepless nights. An online account attempted to clone her brand's official profile. A young design contestant asked how to handle online harassment. The newspaper correction was useful but not a definitive answer to every rumor.

連續失眠後，綾減少咖啡因攝取。 一個網路帳號試圖複製她的工坊章程官方頁面。 一位年輕設計競賽參賽者詢問如何處理網路騷擾。 報紙更正有幫助，卻不是所有謠言的最終答案。

There was a moment when Aya nearly reached for the familiar comfort of doing everything herself. She stopped, looked at the people who would live with the result, and said: Aya admitted her first pilot had miscounted reserve guards and submitted the correction herself. A worker asked what would happen if the plan failed. Aya answered that they would record the failure, repair what they could, and explain what remained unsafe. Leon said this sounded terribly unlike a heroic legend. "Good," she replied. "The workers need a tomorrow, not a legend."

有一刻，綾差點又想照老習慣一個人把事情做完。她停下，看看那些將承擔結果的人，然後說：綾承認首次試辦誤算替班守衛，主動提交更正。一名工人問計畫失敗怎麼辦。她回答：記錄失敗、修正能修的地方，並公開還有什麼不安全。雷昂說這聽起來實在不像英雄傳說。她回：「很好。工人需要的是明天，不是傳說。」

Her father asked which detergent would protect the repaired coat. The scandal tested Aya's emotional endurance more than her business skills. Pipi tried to gauge whether the staff needed a break. A charity goalkeeper wore a custom jacket designed for easy shoulder movement.

父親問哪種清潔劑能保護修好的斗篷。 醜聞考驗綾情緒上的耐力，更甚於商業技能。 皮皮試著評估員工是否需要休息。 慈善球隊守門員穿著方便肩部活動的訂製斗篷。

By evening, the practical result was visible: The panel suspended one funding channel and authorized wages to be paid from a protected account. The change was not a miracle; it had required people, money, and the willingness to admit uncertainty. Aya entered the figures in the brass clock's public register. She let the people who had done the work take credit before the officials spoke. Leon watched the room grow less afraid of him. "Is this what a victory feels like?" he asked. "Sometimes," Aya answered, "it feels like a proper dinner."

到了傍晚，實際成果已經看得見：調查小組暫停一條資金管道，並准許從受保護帳戶支付薪資。這項改變不是奇蹟，而是需要人手、金錢以及承認未知的勇氣。綾把數字登錄在黃銅工時計的公開紀錄。她讓真正做事的人先領功，再輪到官員發言。雷昂看著眾人漸漸不再害怕他，問：「勝利就是這種感覺嗎？」綾說：「有時候，勝利只是能好好吃頓晚餐。」

The firm decided to modernize its privacy complaint system. Aya sliced a radish for the soup she made with her father. The realization that she could rest without guilt surprised Aya. A student panel compared socialism and capitalism in the festival industry.

公會決定將隱私申訴系統現代化。 綾切了白蘿蔔，和父親一起煮湯。 發現自己可以不愧疚地休息，令綾驚訝。 學生座談比較祭典產業中的社會主義與資本主義。

Then a new detail appeared at the bottom of the record: A guard warned that the remaining money might be transferred across the border. Pipi reached for a celebratory stamp, but Aya caught his wrist. "We have not solved it," she said. "We have learned what to ask next." Leon walked her as far as the public stairs. He did not ask where she lived or insist on accompanying her farther. In the world Aya had left, people had called boundaries unfriendly. Here, in a castle ruled by a Demon King, they were beginning to feel surprisingly safe.

接著，紀錄下方出現新的細節：守衛警告剩餘款項可能被轉往邊境外。皮皮想拿慶祝印章，綾抓住他的手腕說：「我們還沒解決問題，只是知道接下來該問什麼。」雷昂送她走到公共樓梯，沒有問她住哪裡，也沒有堅持繼續陪走。在綾離開的世界裡，人們常說界線很不友善。但在這座由魔王統治的城堡裡，清楚的界線反而令人安心。

A subjective opinion about romance could not replace documented business facts. Leon tried to sympathize without pretending he fully understood her fear. The clinic's emergency ward offered confidential support to stressed staff. Aya felt weary after answering another round of hostile messages.

對戀情的主觀評論不能取代有記錄的商業事實。 雷昂試著同理，卻不假裝完全理解她的恐懼。 診所急診病房為受壓員工提供保密支持。 又回覆一輪惡意訊息後，綾感到疲倦。
