湖底的運輸路線

The Route under the Lake

Nobody at the moonlit ferry harbor had expected a woman with a notebook to interrupt the day's established routine. The hidden money had traveled on relief boats without appearing in the public manifest. Aya was thirty-four, old enough to recognize an order designed to make someone else pay for its convenience. The handwritten schedule looked ordinary until she asked who was allowed to refuse. Several people stared at her as though she had proposed replacing the castle with a bakery. She wrote down their objections instead of pretending not to hear them. A good plan, she believed, had to survive an honest question.

在月光下的渡船港，沒有人料到一名拿著筆記本的女子會打斷行之有年的日常。失蹤款項搭乘救濟船運送，卻沒有出現在公開貨單上。三十四歲的綾早已看得出，某些命令的便利是建立在別人承擔代價之上。手寫排班表乍看普通，直到她問誰有權拒絕。幾個人瞪著她，彷彿她提議把城堡改成麵包店。她沒有假裝聽不見反對，而是把意見記下。她相信，好的計畫必須經得起誠實的提問。

The café stood on a quiet boulevard near the river. Aya ordered vegetable broth after the exhausting press review. She refused advertisements promising to change a customer's natural complexion. The audit found that the problem was not merely cosmetic.

咖啡店位在河邊安靜的林蔭大道。 疲憊的媒體檢討後，綾點了蔬菜高湯。 她拒絕宣稱能改變來訪者自然膚色的廣告。 稽核發現問題不只是外觀上的。

Behind the arguments, one stubborn fact remained: A retired ferryman recognized the number on the love letter as a cargo route identifier. Aya separated what the documents proved from what she merely suspected. A wrong date could be a mistake. A wrong date repeated on three independently stamped pages demanded a different explanation. She drew two columns in her notebook and refused to combine them for the sake of a satisfying accusation. Leon asked whether that caution made her work slower. "Yes," she said, "and much harder to overturn."

爭論背後仍有一項無法忽視的事實：退休船夫認出情書上的數字其實是貨運路線代碼。綾把文件能證明的事與自己的推測分開。單一錯誤日期可能只是失誤；若三頁各自蓋章的文件都重複相同錯誤，就需要不同的解釋。她在筆記本上畫出兩欄，不願為了好聽的指控混在一起。雷昂問謹慎是否會拖慢工作。她說：「會，但也更難被推翻。」

A lunar crater pattern decorated the research invitation folder. The assistant took dictation while Aya corrected the public statement. The tabloid's romance story became a diversion from the cost report. Aya planned to enroll in a short research course during the visit.

研究邀請資料夾上有月球隕石坑圖樣。 綾修正聲明時，助理逐字記錄她口述的內容。 八卦戀情故事成了轉移成本報告注意力的話題。 綾打算在訪問期間報名短期研究課程。

Aya knew the decision would anger someone. Nevertheless, she chose a route that could be explained in public: Aya preserved both the original letter and the ship's stamped log. Leon did not offer to make her opponents disappear. He asked which resources she needed, and she gave him a list with prices, deadlines, and the people responsible. Their growing trust was made of these unromantic conversations. Aya appreciated his help without mistaking it for ownership. When he smiled at the list, she wondered whether his smile was becoming a problem of its own.

綾知道這個決定一定會惹惱某些人，但她仍選擇能在公開場合解釋的做法：綾同時保全原始信件與蓋章船舶紀錄。雷昂沒有提議讓反對者消失，只問她需要哪些資源。她交出列有價格、期限與負責人的清單。他們的信任逐漸由這些毫不浪漫的談話構成。綾感謝他的協助，卻不認為那代表誰擁有誰。當雷昂對清單微笑時，她忽然覺得，他的笑容可能成為另一種麻煩。

The independent auditor faced a formidable amount of financial data. A gleam of sunlight crossed the sample fabric on the café table. Aya challenged the idea that disability was simply a personal handicap. The conflict committee would intervene if a contract favored either lover unfairly.

獨立稽核員面對數量龐大的財務資料。 一道陽光在咖啡桌上的布樣閃過。 綾質疑把身心障礙只當作個人不利條件的想法。 若合約不公平地偏袒任一戀人，利益衝突委員會將介入。

Not everyone welcomed the outcome, but it was real: The crew agreed to testify only after receiving protection against retaliation. Aya let dissent appear in the minutes. She had seen too many organizations call silence harmony, then wonder why their walls collapsed. Leon offered her tea, not an order, and she accepted after finishing the record. The two sat with a respectful distance between them. Their conversations had become easy, which made the unspoken questions harder. Aya did not rush to answer them.

並非所有人都歡迎結果，但成果確實存在：船員在獲得免遭報復的保護後才同意作證。綾准許會議紀錄保留不同意見。她見過太多組織把沉默當和諧，最後還驚訝牆壁為何倒塌。雷昂遞給她的是茶，不是命令，她完成紀錄後才接過。兩人保持尊重的距離坐著。談話變得輕鬆，沒說出口的問題反而更難回答。綾沒有急著給答案。

A lunar motif appeared in the institute's design brief. Leon ate oatmeal before the early shareholder meeting. From the outset, Aya had insisted on retaining her designs. Pipi warned the publicity team not to overdo romantic headlines.

學院設計簡報中出現月亮主題圖案。 清晨議員會前，雷昂吃了燕麥粥。 從一開始，綾就堅持保有工藝權。 皮皮提醒公關團隊不要過度渲染戀情標題。

The day's remaining question arrived without any heroic music: The final destination was an abandoned hero-guild warehouse. Aya read the notice aloud so the room could hear the precise words. A threat written on paper was still only a claim until evidence supported it. She secured the document, arranged the next meeting, and went home instead of working through the night. Her former captain would have called that lazy. The Demon King quietly moved the meeting to after breakfast. When Aya noticed, she did not thank him for being considerate; she thanked him for following the schedule.

這天最後的問題沒有任何英雄配樂就出現了：最終目的地竟是已廢棄的勇者公會倉庫。綾把公告逐字念出，讓房間裡的人聽清楚。寫在紙上的威脅仍只是主張，必須有證據才成立。她保全文件、安排下次會議，然後回家，而非通宵工作。舊隊長恐怕會說她懶惰。魔王默默把會議移到早餐後。綾發現時，沒有因他的體貼道謝，而是感謝他遵守排班。

The travel invitation helped sharpen Aya's future research goals. The visiting professor sounded sociable during their first video call. Leon rejected arguments based solely on his company's superiority. No single victor could claim the trial's success as personal property.

海外邀請幫助綾更清楚聚焦未來研究目標。 來訪教授第一次視訊時顯得很善於交際。 雷昂拒絕只憑公會優越地位提出的論點。 沒有單一勝利者能把試辦成功據為己有。
