龍族郵筒不能拿來求婚

The Dragon Postbox

被勇者隊伍開除後，我替魔王城訂了週休二日 · 第 18 / 25 章 · 5000–6000 · 英中對照 · 校訂中

At the dragon village beyond the western ridge, everyone appeared to know what the rules were, but nobody agreed on where those rules had come from. A messenger system misdelivered family letters because children and elders shared a legal address. Aya stood beside the public brass clock and refused to guess. A name-bound spell could be cast only with informed consent; any working spell used the caster's energy; and the clock recorded hours unless two independent witnesses approved a correction. Those limits had been stated at the beginning of her appointment. She would not let a frightened official quietly invent a fourth rule.

在西方山脊外的龍族村落，每個人似乎都知道規則，卻沒有人說得清規則從何而來。送信系統因孩童與長者共用法定地址而誤投家書。綾站在公共黃銅鐘旁，拒絕靠猜測。具名誓約魔法必須取得知情同意；施作工作魔法會消耗施術者體力；工時計記錄若要更正，必須有兩名獨立見證人批准。這些限制在她上任時已說明。她不會讓恐慌的官員偷偷發明第四條。

A space-themed children's jacket showed an astronaut waving from the moon. The event team feared a breakup of the partnership after the storm. A city bureaucrat insisted that the safety inspection be documented. The finance chief used a calculator to estimate cancellation costs.

太空主題童裝上畫著太空人在月球揮手。 風暴後活動團隊擔心合作關係破裂。 一名市府官員堅持安全檢查要留下書面紀錄。 財務指揮官用計算機估算取消活動的成本。

The detail that changed the room was unexpectedly small: Cinder showed the group a different marking used by dragon families for guardianship. Aya asked Pipi to copy the original record before anyone moved it. She then requested a second source with a different keeper. The impatient officials wanted to know whether she had found a villain. She answered that she had found a question worth investigating. In the Demon Castle, that distinction was beginning to sound revolutionary. Leon listened without interrupting, which was itself enough to disturb three elderly advisers.

改變現場氣氛的細節出奇地小：小燼展示龍族家庭用來區分監護關係的另一種標記。綾請皮皮在任何人移動原件前先抄錄。她接著要求另一份由不同人保管的資料。急躁的官員想知道她是否找到壞人。她回答，自己只是找到值得調查的問題。在魔王城，這種區分已經有點像革命。雷昂沒有打斷她，光是這件事就讓三名老顧問坐立難安。

The apprentices looked carefree only after the escape routes were approved. Aya refused to risk a catastrophe for a beautiful photograph. The revised evacuation notice had to be concise and readable. Rainwater entered the glass-roofed courtyard before the show.

逃生路線核准後，學徒才顯得無憂無慮。 綾拒絕為了美麗照片冒災難風險。 修訂後的疏散公告必須簡潔易讀。 走秀前，雨水流進玻璃屋頂中庭。

"Are you always this difficult?" Leon asked, his voice carrying more curiosity than anger. "Only when someone tries to save time by spending other people's lives," Aya replied. She then made her decision: Aya asked families to design their own registration format instead of imposing castle labels. She wrote the choice in clear language so nobody could later claim they had misunderstood. Pipi checked the copies, and Momo the dwarf engineer checked the practical details. Aya never confused being decisive with being unquestionable. Even the Demon King was learning why that mattered.

雷昂問：「妳一直都這麼難搞嗎？」他的語氣好奇多於生氣。綾回答：「只有別人想用他人的生命換時間時。」她接著做出決定：綾請各家庭自行設計登記格式，不強加城堡標籤。她用清楚的文字記下選擇，讓任何人日後都不能聲稱誤會。皮皮檢查副本，矮人工程師莫莫檢查實務細節。綾從不把果決和不容質疑混為一談。連魔王也慢慢明白其中差別。

The new lighting could dazzle viewers without compromising safe movement. The inspection found that the ramp's incline was too steep. A technician stored the damaged panel in a locked equipment locker. A medieval costume inspired one pattern in the small exhibition.

新燈光能使觀眾驚豔，又不犧牲安全動線。 檢查發現坡道的傾斜度太陡。 技師把受損板材放進上鎖的設備櫃。 一件中世紀戲服啟發了小型展覽的一款版型。

For one uncertain hour, the new arrangement seemed almost ordinary: Leon discovered a stack of misdelivered love letters addressed to him and turned scarlet. That was exactly what Aya wanted. A reform that depended on one exceptional person would die when that person left. She trained a replacement, left instructions in plain language, and made sure someone other than Leon held a copy. He seemed mildly insulted by the lack of royal ceremony. She told him that predictable service was a greater honor than a hundred banners. His laughter startled the guards outside.

有一個不太確定的小時，新安排看起來幾乎平凡無奇：雷昂發現一疊寄給自己卻寄錯的情書，整張臉紅透。這正是綾想要的。若改革只依賴一位特別的人，那人離開後改革便會死去。她訓練替班者、用白話寫指引，並確保雷昂以外的人也持有副本。雷昂似乎有點介意缺少王室儀式。她告訴他，可靠的服務比一百面旗幟更值得尊敬。他的笑聲嚇到了門外守衛。

The show included garments designed for different experiences of motherhood. The venue's first-aid plan identified the nearest pharmacy. Aya would publicize the changed venue only after safety approval. A model looked radiant in the repaired blue coat.

走秀包含為不同母職生活經驗設計的服裝。 場地急救計畫標出最近的藥局。 安全核准後，綾才會公布更換場地。 學徒穿著修好的藍色斗篷，神采奕奕。

The apparent ending did not last. One letter contained a shipping code for the missing relief money. Aya hated dramatic timing almost as much as she hated unpaid overtime. Still, she admitted that a story needed the next question to remain honest. She marked the evidence with the date, gave the workers a copy, and closed her notebook. Leon asked whether she was frightened. "Yes," she answered, "but not enough to leave other people alone with the problem." He nodded as if that mattered more than any declaration of loyalty.

眼前的結尾沒有維持太久。其中一封信藏著失蹤救濟金的運送代碼。綾討厭戲劇化的時機，幾乎和討厭無薪加班一樣。但她承認，一個誠實的故事得保留下一個問題。她在證據上註記日期、交給工人副本，再闔上筆記本。雷昂問她害不害怕。她回答：「怕，但還不至於把問題丟給別人承擔。」他點頭，彷彿這比任何效忠宣誓都重要。

The crisis exposed the team's excessive reliance on a single event planner. A charity exhibit rejected photographs that stereotyped residents of a slum. Aya preferred a safe conversation to a dangerous spectacle. A volunteer's dog began to wag its tail when the guests departed.

危機暴露團隊過度依賴單一活動規劃者。 慈善展拒絕使用刻板描繪貧民區居民的照片。 綾寧願要安全的對話，也不要危險的壯觀場面。 來賓離去時，一名志工的狗開始搖尾巴。
