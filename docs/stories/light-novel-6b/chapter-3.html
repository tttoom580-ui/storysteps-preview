A Lunch Break That Changed History

被勇者隊伍開除後，我替魔王城訂了週休二日 · 第 3 / 25 章 · 5000–6000 · 英中對照 · 校訂中

Nobody at the underground kitchen below the Demon Castle had expected a woman with a notebook to interrupt the day's established routine. The cooks had been ordered to prepare six meals per day without stopping the ovens. Aya was thirty-four, old enough to recognize an order designed to make someone else pay for its convenience. The handwritten schedule looked ordinary until she asked who was allowed to refuse. Several people stared at her as though she had proposed replacing the castle with a bakery. She wrote down their objections instead of pretending not to hear them. A good plan, she believed, had to survive an honest question.

在魔王城地下的廚房，沒有人料到一名拿著筆記本的女子會打斷行之有年的日常。廚師們被要求每天準備六餐，卻不能停下烤爐。三十四歲的綾早已看得出，某些命令的便利是建立在別人承擔代價之上。手寫排班表乍看普通，直到她問誰有權拒絕。幾個人瞪著她，彷彿她提議把城堡改成麵包店。她沒有假裝聽不見反對，而是把意見記下。她相信，好的計畫必須經得起誠實的提問。

The cashier preserved a receipt instead of obeying the supervisor's demand. The castle workshop offered a voluntary checkup after workers reported skin irritation. Aya wanted to curb unauthorized access to the pattern archive. The castle workshop alarm was loud enough to deafen anyone standing nearby.

收銀員保存了收據，沒有照指揮官要求銷毀。 工人反映皮膚刺激後，城堡工坊提供自願健康檢查。 綾希望遏止未授權存取版型檔案。 城堡工坊警報大得足以震聾站在附近的人。

Behind the arguments, one stubborn fact remained: The glowing emergency roster was copied from a decades-old war order rather than current staffing rules. Aya separated what the documents proved from what she merely suspected. A wrong date could be a mistake. A wrong date repeated on three independently stamped pages demanded a different explanation. She drew two columns in her notebook and refused to combine them for the sake of a satisfying accusation. Leon asked whether that caution made her work slower. "Yes," she said, "and much harder to overturn."

爭論背後仍有一項無法忽視的事實：發光的緊急輪班表竟抄自數十年前的戰時命令，而非現行人力規則。綾把文件能證明的事與自己的推測分開。單一錯誤日期可能只是失誤；若三頁各自蓋章的文件都重複相同錯誤，就需要不同的解釋。她在筆記本上畫出兩欄，不願為了好聽的指控混在一起。雷昂問謹慎是否會拖慢工作。她說：「會，但也更難被推翻。」

The supervisor called the stolen design a disgrace to his department. The warehouse's disposal register did not mention the missing fabric. A technician recorded her dissent from the first inspection report. A former seamstress planned to emigrate after her final contract ended.

指揮官稱設計遭竊是城堡部門的恥辱。 倉庫廢棄物處理簿沒有提到失蹤布料。 一位技師對首份檢驗報告提出並記錄異議。 一名資深布術師計畫合約期滿後移居海外。

Aya knew the decision would anger someone. Nevertheless, she chose a route that could be explained in public: Aya offered three rotating lunch teams and let each cook choose a workable position. Leon did not offer to make her opponents disappear. He asked which resources she needed, and she gave him a list with prices, deadlines, and the people responsible. Their growing trust was made of these unromantic conversations. Aya appreciated his help without mistaking it for ownership. When he smiled at the list, she wondered whether his smile was becoming a problem of its own.

綾知道這個決定一定會惹惱某些人，但她仍選擇能在公開場合解釋的做法：綾提出三組輪流用餐方案，並讓每位廚師選擇適合的位置。雷昂沒有提議讓反對者消失，只問她需要哪些資源。她交出列有價格、期限與負責人的清單。他們的信任逐漸由這些毫不浪漫的談話構成。綾感謝他的協助，卻不認為那代表誰擁有誰。當雷昂對清單微笑時，她忽然覺得，他的笑容可能成為另一種麻煩。

A fair apprenticeship could enrich the lives of junior designers. The investigator warned that a lengthy inquiry might delay the launch. Aya viewed textile design as a lifelong commitment to practical beauty. “Madam, please keep the receipt,” the cashier said quietly.

公平的學徒制度能豐富年輕工藝師的人生。 調查員提醒，冗長調查可能延誤上市。 綾把紡織設計視為畢生追求實用之美的承諾。 收銀員輕聲說：「女士，請保留這張收據。」

Not everyone welcomed the outcome, but it was real: The first uninterrupted meal reduced broken dishes and sent laughter down the corridors. Aya let dissent appear in the minutes. She had seen too many organizations call silence harmony, then wonder why their walls collapsed. Leon offered her tea, not an order, and she accepted after finishing the record. The two sat with a respectful distance between them. Their conversations had become easy, which made the unspoken questions harder. Aya did not rush to answer them.

並非所有人都歡迎結果，但成果確實存在：第一頓不被打斷的午餐減少了破盤，也讓笑聲傳遍走廊。綾准許會議紀錄保留不同意見。她見過太多組織把沉默當和諧，最後還驚訝牆壁為何倒塌。雷昂遞給她的是茶，不是命令，她完成紀錄後才接過。兩人保持尊重的距離坐著。談話變得輕鬆，沒說出口的問題反而更難回答。綾沒有急著給答案。

A melancholy silence filled the room when the stolen sketches appeared. The notary checked whether a private interview was permissible under the order. Aya refused to preach integrity while ignoring her own archive security. The counterfeit stitching was reminiscent of her earliest student experiments.

被竊草圖出現時，房間籠罩著憂鬱的沉默。 公證員查核依命令是否允許私下訪談。 綾拒絕一邊宣講誠信，一邊忽略自身檔案安全。 仿品縫法令人想起她學生時代最早的試作。

The day's remaining question arrived without any heroic music: A visiting hero accused her of weakening the fortress. Aya read the notice aloud so the room could hear the precise words. A threat written on paper was still only a claim until evidence supported it. She secured the document, arranged the next meeting, and went home instead of working through the night. Her former captain would have called that lazy. The Demon King quietly moved the meeting to after breakfast. When Aya noticed, she did not thank him for being considerate; she thanked him for following the schedule.

這天最後的問題沒有任何英雄配樂就出現了：一名來訪勇者指責她削弱了城堡防衛。綾把公告逐字念出，讓房間裡的人聽清楚。寫在紙上的威脅仍只是主張，必須有證據才成立。她保全文件、安排下次會議，然後回家，而非通宵工作。舊隊長恐怕會說她懶惰。魔王默默把會議移到早餐後。綾發現時，沒有因他的體貼道謝，而是感謝他遵守排班。

The rivalry between distributors made the evidence politically sensitive. Every payment would now face independent scrutiny. The staff waited in suspense for the court's preservation decision. Aya's registered trademark remained outside the proposed acquisition.

經銷商之間的競爭讓證據變得敏感。 今後每筆款項都要接受獨立審查。 員工懷著懸念等待法院的保全裁定。 綾已註冊的商標仍不列入公會協議範圍。
