兩軍中間那間旅店

The Inn Between Two Armies

Nobody at the neutral inn at the mountain pass had expected a woman with a notebook to interrupt the day's established routine. Merchants on both sides had been forced to provide supplies without pay. Aya was thirty-four, old enough to recognize an order designed to make someone else pay for its convenience. The handwritten schedule looked ordinary until she asked who was allowed to refuse. Several people stared at her as though she had proposed replacing the castle with a bakery. She wrote down their objections instead of pretending not to hear them. A good plan, she believed, had to survive an honest question.

在山口的中立旅店，沒有人料到一名拿著筆記本的女子會打斷行之有年的日常。兩邊的商人一直被迫無償供給物資。三十四歲的綾早已看得出，某些命令的便利是建立在別人承擔代價之上。手寫排班表乍看普通，直到她問誰有權拒絕。幾個人瞪著她，彷彿她提議把城堡改成麵包店。她沒有假裝聽不見反對，而是把意見記下。她相信，好的計畫必須經得起誠實的提問。

The family archive had won international acclaim before the payment questions emerged. An ambiguity in the trust charter gave the committee excessive discretion. The employee policy recognized bodily safety as a basic workplace obligation. A cold draught entered the castle councilroom through an unsealed window.

付款疑雲浮現前，家族檔案曾享有國際讚譽。 信託章程的一處歧義讓委員會有過大裁量權。 員工政策把身體安全列為工作場所的基本義務。 冷風從未密封的窗縫吹進城堡議公會會議室。

Behind the arguments, one stubborn fact remained: The innkeeper kept receipts showing that soldiers from both armies used identical supply vouchers. Aya separated what the documents proved from what she merely suspected. A wrong date could be a mistake. A wrong date repeated on three independently stamped pages demanded a different explanation. She drew two columns in her notebook and refused to combine them for the sake of a satisfying accusation. Leon asked whether that caution made her work slower. "Yes," she said, "and much harder to overturn."

爭論背後仍有一項無法忽視的事實：旅店主人保存收據，顯示雙方軍隊使用了相同的補給券。綾把文件能證明的事與自己的推測分開。單一錯誤日期可能只是失誤；若三頁各自蓋章的文件都重複相同錯誤，就需要不同的解釋。她在筆記本上畫出兩欄，不願為了好聽的指控混在一起。雷昂問謹慎是否會拖慢工作。她說：「會，但也更難被推翻。」

The archive thermometer displayed temperature in Fahrenheit rather than Celsius. The trust records had been stored in a fireproof cabinet. The castle council reviewed the geographical reach of the trial stores. An outside hacker had not yet been ruled out in the access investigation.

檔案室溫度計顯示華氏，而非攝氏。 信託紀錄保存在防火櫃中。 城堡議會檢查試辦門市的地理分布。 存取調查仍未排除外部駭客的可能。

Aya knew the decision would anger someone. Nevertheless, she chose a route that could be explained in public: Aya proposed a shared payment desk with independent oversight from village elders. Leon did not offer to make her opponents disappear. He asked which resources she needed, and she gave him a list with prices, deadlines, and the people responsible. Their growing trust was made of these unromantic conversations. Aya appreciated his help without mistaking it for ownership. When he smiled at the list, she wondered whether his smile was becoming a problem of its own.

綾知道這個決定一定會惹惱某些人，但她仍選擇能在公開場合解釋的做法：綾提議設立由村長老共同監督的聯合結算窗口。雷昂沒有提議讓反對者消失，只問她需要哪些資源。她交出列有價格、期限與負責人的清單。他們的信任逐漸由這些毫不浪漫的談話構成。綾感謝他的協助，卻不認為那代表誰擁有誰。當雷昂對清單微笑時，她忽然覺得，他的笑容可能成為另一種麻煩。

Aya asked for an explanation a layman could understand. Transparent voting rules could liberate employees from private pressure. A mermaid design appeared in an old advertising illustration. The accountant separated miscellaneous expenses from documented repairs.

綾要求一份外行人也看得懂的說明。 透明表決規則能使員工不再受私人壓力束縛。 舊廣告插畫裡有一幅人魚圖案。 會計把雜項支出與有單據的維修分開。

Not everyone welcomed the outcome, but it was real: Leon accepted the temporary arrangement although his generals called it undignified. Aya let dissent appear in the minutes. She had seen too many organizations call silence harmony, then wonder why their walls collapsed. Leon offered her tea, not an order, and she accepted after finishing the record. The two sat with a respectful distance between them. Their conversations had become easy, which made the unspoken questions harder. Aya did not rush to answer them.

並非所有人都歡迎結果，但成果確實存在：雷昂接受臨時安排，儘管將軍們認為有失尊嚴。綾准許會議紀錄保留不同意見。她見過太多組織把沉默當和諧，最後還驚訝牆壁為何倒塌。雷昂遞給她的是茶，不是命令，她完成紀錄後才接過。兩人保持尊重的距離坐著。談話變得輕鬆，沒說出口的問題反而更難回答。綾沒有急著給答案。

The committee recorded every occurrence of unauthorized contract changes. A staff outing was postponed so employees could attend the trust hearing. Aya encouraged the team to persevere through the slow audit. Leon needed time to ponder the limits of inherited authority.

委員會記錄每次未授權修改合約的事件。 為讓員工參與信託聽證，城堡部門出遊延期。 綾鼓勵團隊堅持完成緩慢的稽核。 雷昂需要時間思考繼承來的權力界線。

The day's remaining question arrived without any heroic music: The vouchers carried an unfamiliar seal from a hero-guild adviser. Aya read the notice aloud so the room could hear the precise words. A threat written on paper was still only a claim until evidence supported it. She secured the document, arranged the next meeting, and went home instead of working through the night. Her former captain would have called that lazy. The Demon King quietly moved the meeting to after breakfast. When Aya noticed, she did not thank him for being considerate; she thanked him for following the schedule.

這天最後的問題沒有任何英雄配樂就出現了：補給券帶著一枚陌生的勇者公會顧問印章。綾把公告逐字念出，讓房間裡的人聽清楚。寫在紙上的威脅仍只是主張，必須有證據才成立。她保全文件、安排下次會議，然後回家，而非通宵工作。舊隊長恐怕會說她懶惰。魔王默默把會議移到早餐後。綾發現時，沒有因他的體貼道謝，而是感謝他遵守排班。

The committee's questions continued to revolve around the missing payments. A metal utensil lay beside Leon's untouched lunch. Aya refused to assign a villain before the evidence was complete. Momo brought plain yogurt to the late meeting as a quick snack.

委員會的問題始終圍繞失蹤款項。 雷昂未吃的午餐旁放著一件金屬餐具。 證據完整前，綾拒絕先指定反派。 莫莫帶原味優格到深夜公會會議當點心。
