哥布林也要勞資會議

The Goblins Organize

被勇者隊伍開除後，我替魔王城訂了週休二日 · 第 8 / 25 章 · 5000–6000 · 英中對照 · 校訂中

The trouble began at the crowded goblin print shop, where a perfectly respectable official announcement had made ordinary life almost impossible. The workers feared that openly reporting missed pay would cost them their homes. Aya examined the announcement twice. It had a seal, a solemn title, and absolutely no explanation of what would happen to the people affected. Even a novice adventurer would have demanded clearer directions before entering a dungeon. Aya had once been dismissed for insisting on that principle. Being unemployed had not cured her of it. She unfolded her notebook and invited the nearest worker to speak first.

麻煩發生在擁擠的哥布林印刷工坊，一份看似體面的官方公告竟讓普通人的生活幾乎無法繼續。工人害怕公開申報欠薪就會失去住所。綾把公告看了兩遍。它有印章、嚴肅的標題，卻完全沒有解釋受影響的人會遭遇什麼。連冒險新手進地城前都會要求更清楚的指引。綾曾因堅持這個原則而被開除；失業並沒有治好她這個習慣。她打開筆記本，請最近的一名工人先發言。

The warehouse nurse stored an antibiotic prescription in a locked cabinet. Staff chose to applaud the worker who had preserved the shipping record. An astronomer lent the exhibition hall a star chart for its ceiling projection. The group promised to computerize the warehouse register with individual logins.

倉庫護理師把抗生素處方保存在上鎖櫃中。 員工為保存出貨紀錄的同事鼓掌。 一位天文學家借給展示大廳星圖作天花板投影。 集團承諾把倉庫登記電腦化，並設個別登入帳號。

The first useful clue came from someone powerful people had almost forgotten to question: Pipi recovered archived wage slips proving that the same work had once been paid fairly. Aya thanked the witness without asking for a promise of loyalty. A witness should be able to change an honest answer without fearing punishment. She checked how the record had traveled, who had touched it, and whether a copy existed outside the castle. Pipi wanted to celebrate immediately. Aya bought him a sweet bun instead. Celebrations, she said, could wait until the evidence survived scrutiny.

第一條有用線索來自掌權者幾乎忘了詢問的人：皮皮找回舊薪資單，證明同樣的工作過去曾合理給薪。綾道謝，卻沒有要求證人承諾效忠。證人應能在不怕受罰的情況下更正誠實的答案。她查清紀錄如何傳遞、誰碰過它、城堡外是否有副本。皮皮想立刻慶祝，綾只替他買了甜麵包。她說，證據通過審查後再慶祝也不遲。

The damaged gate could disable the automatic alarm without warning. The technician told everyone to evacuate the loading area during inspection. Aya requested a detailed record of warehouse security expenditure. Lin Shuo grew irritable when the court asked for original delivery logs.

損壞的閘門可能在無預警下使自動警報失效。 技師要求檢查期間所有人撤離裝卸區。 綾要求完整記錄倉庫保全支出。 法院要求原始交貨紀錄時，林朔變得易怒。

There was a moment when Aya nearly reached for the familiar comfort of doing everything herself. She stopped, looked at the people who would live with the result, and said: Aya helped them elect their own representatives and promised not to speak over them. A worker asked what would happen if the plan failed. Aya answered that they would record the failure, repair what they could, and explain what remained unsafe. Leon said this sounded terribly unlike a heroic legend. "Good," she replied. "The workers need a tomorrow, not a legend."

有一刻，綾差點又想照老習慣一個人把事情做完。她停下，看看那些將承擔結果的人，然後說：綾協助他們自行選出代表，並承諾不代替他們發言。一名工人問計畫失敗怎麼辦。她回答：記錄失敗、修正能修的地方，並公開還有什麼不安全。雷昂說這聽起來實在不像英雄傳說。她回：「很好。工人需要的是明天，不是傳說。」

The investigation asked whether the intermediary was Lin Shuo's kin. Pipi recommended a short meditation break after the stressful inspection. A loose nickel coin was found near the warehouse loading scale. The investigator used the floor map to orient new witnesses.

調查要查明中間商是否為林朔親屬。 緊張檢查後，皮皮建議短暫靜坐。 倉庫裝卸秤旁發現一枚鬆落的五分鎳幣。 調查員用樓層圖協助新證人辨明方向。

By evening, the practical result was visible: The goblins delivered their petition with a spectacularly noisy printing press. The change was not a miracle; it had required people, money, and the willingness to admit uncertainty. Aya entered the figures in the brass clock's public register. She let the people who had done the work take credit before the officials spoke. Leon watched the room grow less afraid of him. "Is this what a victory feels like?" he asked. "Sometimes," Aya answered, "it feels like a proper dinner."

到了傍晚，實際成果已經看得見：哥布林們用吵得驚人的印刷機送出請願書。這項改變不是奇蹟，而是需要人手、金錢以及承認未知的勇氣。綾把數字登錄在黃銅工時計的公開紀錄。她讓真正做事的人先領功，再輪到官員發言。雷昂看著眾人漸漸不再害怕他，問：「勝利就是這種感覺嗎？」綾說：「有時候，勝利只是能好好吃頓晚餐。」

A historical label used the outdated word oriental, which the curator replaced. A former driver recovering from pneumonia gave testimony by video. The missing prototype was more valuable than the damaged fabric. The employee clinic offered voluntary psychiatry referrals after severe workplace stress.

歷史標籤用了過時的 oriental 一詞，館員將它更正。 一位從肺炎恢復中的前司機透過視訊作證。 失蹤的原型樣衣比受損布料更有價值。 員工診所為遭遇嚴重職場壓力者提供自願精神科轉介。

Then a new detail appeared at the bottom of the record: A rival adviser called their meeting an illegal rebellion. Pipi reached for a celebratory stamp, but Aya caught his wrist. "We have not solved it," she said. "We have learned what to ask next." Leon walked her as far as the public stairs. He did not ask where she lived or insist on accompanying her farther. In the world Aya had left, people had called boundaries unfriendly. Here, in a castle ruled by a Demon King, they were beginning to feel surprisingly safe.

接著，紀錄下方出現新的細節：一名對立顧問稱這場會議是非法叛亂。皮皮想拿慶祝印章，綾抓住他的手腕說：「我們還沒解決問題，只是知道接下來該問什麼。」雷昂送她走到公共樓梯，沒有問她住哪裡，也沒有堅持繼續陪走。在綾離開的世界裡，人們常說界線很不友善。但在這座由魔王統治的城堡裡，清楚的界線反而令人安心。

The revised access system needed robust records that could survive an outage. A sovereign emblem on the display wall was unrelated to warehouse ownership. The guild spokesperson confirmed the shipment delay without naming suspects. The missing cloth was intended for a modular travel wardrobe.

修訂後門禁系統需要即使停電也可靠的紀錄。 展示牆上的主權徽章與倉庫所有權無關。 公會發言人確認出貨延遲，卻沒有公布嫌疑人姓名。 失蹤布料原本要做成可組合的旅行衣櫃工藝系列。
