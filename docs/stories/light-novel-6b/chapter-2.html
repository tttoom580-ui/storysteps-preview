Interview with the Demon King

At the Demon Castle's absurdly large reception hall, everyone appeared to know what the rules were, but nobody agreed on where those rules had come from. The castle staff believed a human clerk would impose human laws on their customs. Aya stood beside the public brass clock and refused to guess. A name-bound spell could be cast only with informed consent; any working spell used the caster's energy; and the clock recorded hours unless two independent witnesses approved a correction. Those limits had been stated at the beginning of her appointment. She would not let a frightened official quietly invent a fourth rule.

在魔王城大得離譜的接待廳，每個人似乎都知道規則，卻沒有人說得清規則從何而來。城堡職員擔心人類文書官會強迫他們遵守人類習俗。綾站在公共黃銅鐘旁，拒絕靠猜測。具名誓約魔法必須取得知情同意；施作工作魔法會消耗施術者體力；工時計記錄若要更正，必須有兩名獨立見證人批准。這些限制在她上任時已說明。她不會讓恐慌的官員偷偷發明第四條。

Every store manager had to abide by the published repair policy. The employee asked Leon to affirm the policy in writing. A playful alligator appeared on an optional children's raincoat print. A low bass melody played softly in the waiting room.

每位店長都必須遵守公開的維修政策。 店員請雷昂以書面確認政策。 兒童雨衣可選的圖樣上有隻俏皮鱷魚。 等候室輕輕播放著低沉的低音旋律。

The detail that changed the room was unexpectedly small: King Leon presented a public duty clock and three clear magical limits written on its frame. Aya asked Pipi to copy the original record before anyone moved it. She then requested a second source with a different keeper. The impatient officials wanted to know whether she had found a villain. She answered that she had found a question worth investigating. In the Demon Castle, that distinction was beginning to sound revolutionary. Leon listened without interrupting, which was itself enough to disturb three elderly advisers.

改變現場氣氛的細節出奇地小：雷昂魔王展示公共工時計，以及刻在鐘框上的三條魔法限制。綾請皮皮在任何人移動原件前先抄錄。她接著要求另一份由不同人保管的資料。急躁的官員想知道她是否找到壞人。她回答，自己只是找到值得調查的問題。在魔王城，這種區分已經有點像革命。雷昂沒有打斷她，光是這件事就讓三名老顧問坐立難安。

Pipi rewrote the return instructions in colloquial language customers understood. Hidden service charges had become commonplace in competing city markets. The temporary showroom permit would expire at the end of the quarter. A damaged fuse briefly disabled the training room lights.

皮皮把退貨說明改寫成來訪者聽得懂的口語。 隱藏服務收費在其他城市市集裡已經十分常見。 臨時展示大廳的許可將於季度末到期。 損壞的保險絲讓訓練室燈光短暫熄滅。

"Are you always this difficult?" Leon asked, his voice carrying more curiosity than anger. "Only when someone tries to save time by spending other people's lives," Aya replied. She then made her decision: Aya agreed to test one department for ten days but refused any contract that controlled her private life. She wrote the choice in clear language so nobody could later claim they had misunderstood. Pipi checked the copies, and Momo the dwarf engineer checked the practical details. Aya never confused being decisive with being unquestionable. Even the Demon King was learning why that mattered.

雷昂問：「妳一直都這麼難搞嗎？」他的語氣好奇多於生氣。綾回答：「只有別人想用他人的生命換時間時。」她接著做出決定：綾同意用十天試辦一個部門，但拒絕任何控制私生活的合約。她用清楚的文字記下選擇，讓任何人日後都不能聲稱誤會。皮皮檢查副本，矮人工程師莫莫檢查實務細節。綾從不把果決和不容質疑混為一談。連魔王也慢慢明白其中差別。

Leon rejected hospitality gifts offered by a contractor under review. The consultant reminded staff that an infectious illness required safe leave arrangements. The dye workshop used a measured liter of water for each test. A novice assistant discovered the missing price information.

雷昂拒絕接受正在受查的承包商所送的招待禮品。 顧問提醒員工，傳染性疾病需要安全的請假安排。 染色工作坊每次測試使用精確量好的一公升水。 一名新手助理發現價格資訊遺漏。

For one uncertain hour, the new arrangement seemed almost ordinary: Leon signed her conditions while a goblin accidentally showered them with registration papers. That was exactly what Aya wanted. A reform that depended on one exceptional person would die when that person left. She trained a replacement, left instructions in plain language, and made sure someone other than Leon held a copy. He seemed mildly insulted by the lack of royal ceremony. She told him that predictable service was a greater honor than a hundred banners. His laughter startled the guards outside.

有一個不太確定的小時，新安排看起來幾乎平凡無奇：雷昂簽下她提出的條件，一名哥布林卻不慎把登記紙灑滿兩人。這正是綾想要的。若改革只依賴一位特別的人，那人離開後改革便會死去。她訓練替班者、用白話寫指引，並確保雷昂以外的人也持有副本。雷昂似乎有點介意缺少王室儀式。她告訴他，可靠的服務比一百面旗幟更值得尊敬。他的笑聲嚇到了門外守衛。

Aya warned that a rash decision could end the trial unfairly. The finance team had to reconcile sales records with customer returns. An old, shabby coat became the best example of affordable repairs. A narrow spotlight illuminated the costume's adjustable fasteners.

綾提醒，草率決定可能讓試辦不公平地中止。 財務團隊必須核對交易紀錄與來訪者退貨資料。 一件破舊斗篷反而成為平價維修的最佳示例。 一束聚光燈照亮了服裝的可調扣件。

The apparent ending did not last. The kitchen chief announced that the castle had no record of lunch breaks. Aya hated dramatic timing almost as much as she hated unpaid overtime. Still, she admitted that a story needed the next question to remain honest. She marked the evidence with the date, gave the workers a copy, and closed her notebook. Leon asked whether she was frightened. "Yes," she answered, "but not enough to leave other people alone with the problem." He nodded as if that mattered more than any declaration of loyalty.

眼前的結尾沒有維持太久。廚房總管宣布魔王城從來沒有午休紀錄。綾討厭戲劇化的時機，幾乎和討厭無薪加班一樣。但她承認，一個誠實的故事得保留下一個問題。她在證據上註記日期、交給工人副本，再闔上筆記本。雷昂問她害不害怕。她回答：「怕，但還不至於把問題丟給別人承擔。」他點頭，彷彿這比任何效忠宣誓都重要。

The café served warm tea with a little ginger syrup. The display stand began to tilt until a technician stabilized it. At twilight, Aya reviewed the agreement beside the river window. Leon declined whiskey at lunch to keep the negotiations clear.

咖啡廳供應加少量薑糖漿的熱茶。 展示架開始傾斜，直到技師把它固定。 暮色中，綾在河景窗邊重讀協議。 雷昂午餐時婉拒威士忌，以保持談判清醒。
