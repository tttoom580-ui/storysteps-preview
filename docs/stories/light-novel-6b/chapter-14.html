Festival of Honest Promises

被勇者隊伍開除後，我替魔王城訂了週休二日 · 第 14 / 25 章 · 5000–6000 · 英中對照 · 校訂中

At the midsummer lantern festival, everyone appeared to know what the rules were, but nobody agreed on where those rules had come from. The city planned to use the festival to announce a compulsory loyalty oath. Aya stood beside the public brass clock and refused to guess. A name-bound spell could be cast only with informed consent; any working spell used the caster's energy; and the clock recorded hours unless two independent witnesses approved a correction. Those limits had been stated at the beginning of her appointment. She would not let a frightened official quietly invent a fourth rule.

在仲夏燈籠祭，每個人似乎都知道規則，卻沒有人說得清規則從何而來。城市打算利用祭典宣布強制忠誠誓約。綾站在公共黃銅鐘旁，拒絕靠猜測。具名誓約魔法必須取得知情同意；施作工作魔法會消耗施術者體力；工時計記錄若要更正，必須有兩名獨立見證人批准。這些限制在她上任時已說明。她不會讓恐慌的官員偷偷發明第四條。

An adaptation of the historic sleeve respected its original inventor. Aya told the team to brace for uncomfortable findings in the archive. A charitable fund could support restitution without controlling its decisions. The family had treated undocumented design purchases as customary practice.

一款歷史袖型的改編設計尊重原創者。 綾提醒團隊要為檔案裡令人不安的發現作準備。 慈善基金能資助補救，卻不控制裁決。 家族一直把未記錄的設計採購當成慣例。

The detail that changed the room was unexpectedly small: Aya found that the oath's glowing text hid a clause waiving future unpaid wages. Aya asked Pipi to copy the original record before anyone moved it. She then requested a second source with a different keeper. The impatient officials wanted to know whether she had found a villain. She answered that she had found a question worth investigating. In the Demon Castle, that distinction was beginning to sound revolutionary. Leon listened without interrupting, which was itself enough to disturb three elderly advisers.

改變現場氣氛的細節出奇地小：綾發現誓約的發光文字藏著放棄追討欠薪的條款。綾請皮皮在任何人移動原件前先抄錄。她接著要求另一份由不同人保管的資料。急躁的官員想知道她是否找到壞人。她回答，自己只是找到值得調查的問題。在魔王城，這種區分已經有點像革命。雷昂沒有打斷她，光是這件事就讓三名老顧問坐立難安。

Madame Leon offered an endowment for the independent restitution office. A curator warned against treating unusual designs as a freak attraction. The delivery truck reached the archive by the northern freeway. A longtime inhabitant of the district recognized the old tailor's shop.

裴夫人為獨立補救辦公室提供永久基金。 館員提醒不要把不尋常設計當成怪異噱頭。 運送卡車經由北方高速公路抵達檔案庫。 一位住在當地很久的居民認出了舊布術師店。

"Are you always this difficult?" Leon asked, his voice carrying more curiosity than anger. "Only when someone tries to save time by spending other people's lives," Aya replied. She then made her decision: She organized a public reading with interpreters instead of staging a dramatic protest. She wrote the choice in clear language so nobody could later claim they had misunderstood. Pipi checked the copies, and Momo the dwarf engineer checked the practical details. Aya never confused being decisive with being unquestionable. Even the Demon King was learning why that mattered.

雷昂問：「妳一直都這麼難搞嗎？」他的語氣好奇多於生氣。綾回答：「只有別人想用他人的生命換時間時。」她接著做出決定：她安排有口譯員參與的公開朗讀，沒有以戲劇化抗議取代查證。她用清楚的文字記下選擇，讓任何人日後都不能聲稱誤會。皮皮檢查副本，矮人工程師莫莫檢查實務細節。綾從不把果決和不容質疑混為一談。連魔王也慢慢明白其中差別。

Leon hoped the corrected credits would lighten the families' burden. An old advertisement praised the majesty of the royal-inspired coat. The false credit continued to mar the archive's reputation. The copied design tried to mimic the elder tailor's distinctive seam.

雷昂希望更正署名能減輕家屬負擔。 舊廣告稱讚仿王室斗篷的莊嚴氣派。 錯誤署名持續損害檔案庫聲譽。 複製設計試圖模仿老布術師獨特縫法。

For one uncertain hour, the new arrangement seemed almost ordinary: Leon dismantled the false oath and confessed he had once signed without reading the whole page. That was exactly what Aya wanted. A reform that depended on one exceptional person would die when that person left. She trained a replacement, left instructions in plain language, and made sure someone other than Leon held a copy. He seemed mildly insulted by the lack of royal ceremony. She told him that predictable service was a greater honor than a hundred banners. His laughter startled the guards outside.

有一個不太確定的小時，新安排看起來幾乎平凡無奇：雷昂拆除虛假誓約，承認自己過去也曾沒讀完就簽名。這正是綾想要的。若改革只依賴一位特別的人，那人離開後改革便會死去。她訓練替班者、用白話寫指引，並確保雷昂以外的人也持有副本。雷昂似乎有點介意缺少王室儀式。她告訴他，可靠的服務比一百面旗幟更值得尊敬。他的笑聲嚇到了門外守衛。

The archive's modernization required better ownership records. A mournful silence followed the reading of the unpaid tailor's letter. A small puff of dust rose when the archive box opened. The repaired garment included a reflective strip for night travel.

檔案現代化需要更好的所有權紀錄。 念完未獲付款布術師的信後，現場一片哀傷沉默。 檔案箱打開時，揚起一小團灰塵。 修好的服裝有夜間旅行使用的反光條。

The apparent ending did not last. The crowd cheered for consent, while Aya refused a romantic speech on a public stage. Aya hated dramatic timing almost as much as she hated unpaid overtime. Still, she admitted that a story needed the next question to remain honest. She marked the evidence with the date, gave the workers a copy, and closed her notebook. Leon asked whether she was frightened. "Yes," she answered, "but not enough to leave other people alone with the problem." He nodded as if that mattered more than any declaration of loyalty.

眼前的結尾沒有維持太久。群眾為自願同意喝采，綾則拒絕在公開舞台上被迫聽浪漫告白。綾討厭戲劇化的時機，幾乎和討厭無薪加班一樣。但她承認，一個誠實的故事得保留下一個問題。她在證據上註記日期、交給工人副本，再闔上筆記本。雷昂問她害不害怕。她回答：「怕，但還不至於把問題丟給別人承擔。」他點頭，彷彿這比任何效忠宣誓都重要。

Pipi offered tea to refresh the visiting families. A historical fabric pattern included a colorful reptile along the border. Aya needed solitude after listening to the families' painful accounts. The archive scandal exposed flaws in the family's succession practices.

皮皮提供茶水，讓來訪家屬提振精神。 歷史布料圖樣的邊緣畫著彩色爬蟲。 聽完家屬痛苦經歷後，綾需要獨處。 檔案醜聞暴露家族繼承制度的缺陷。
