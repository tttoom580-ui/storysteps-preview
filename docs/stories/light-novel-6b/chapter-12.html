An Unwelcome Reunion

被勇者隊伍開除後，我替魔王城訂了週休二日 · 第 12 / 25 章 · 5000–6000 · 英中對照 · 校訂中

The trouble began at the inn's open meeting room, where a perfectly respectable official announcement had made ordinary life almost impossible. The hero captain insisted that Aya should return because she had been the only reliable planner. Aya examined the announcement twice. It had a seal, a solemn title, and absolutely no explanation of what would happen to the people affected. Even a novice adventurer would have demanded clearer directions before entering a dungeon. Aya had once been dismissed for insisting on that principle. Being unemployed had not cured her of it. She unfolded her notebook and invited the nearest worker to speak first.

麻煩發生在旅店的公開會議室，一份看似體面的官方公告竟讓普通人的生活幾乎無法繼續。勇者隊長堅持綾應回隊，因為只有她能可靠規劃後勤。綾把公告看了兩遍。它有印章、嚴肅的標題，卻完全沒有解釋受影響的人會遭遇什麼。連冒險新手進地城前都會要求更清楚的指引。綾曾因堅持這個原則而被開除；失業並沒有治好她這個習慣。她打開筆記本，請最近的一名工人先發言。

A community center attendant helped visitors find the accessible entrance. The cooking class showed how batter could stain a cotton apron. A local census helped estimate demand for accessible clothing. A textile chemist explained how different dyes affected sensitive skin.

社區中心服務員協助來賓找到無障礙入口。 烹飪課示範麵糊如何弄髒棉圍裙。 地方人口普查有助估算無障礙服裝需求。 紡織化學師說明不同染劑如何影響敏感皮膚。

The first useful clue came from someone powerful people had almost forgotten to question: Aya learned that the captain had never been told the full reason for her dismissal. Aya thanked the witness without asking for a promise of loyalty. A witness should be able to change an honest answer without fearing punishment. She checked how the record had traveled, who had touched it, and whether a copy existed outside the castle. Pipi wanted to celebrate immediately. Aya bought him a sweet bun instead. Celebrations, she said, could wait until the evidence survived scrutiny.

第一條有用線索來自掌權者幾乎忘了詢問的人：綾得知隊長從未被告知她遭開除的完整原因。綾道謝，卻沒有要求證人承諾效忠。證人應能在不怕受罰的情況下更正誠實的答案。她查清紀錄如何傳遞、誰碰過它、城堡外是否有副本。皮皮想立刻慶祝，綾只替他買了甜麵包。她說，證據通過審查後再慶祝也不遲。

Birds began to chirp outside when the power returned. The center would dispense emergency supplies only through trained staff. A volunteer gave a tired groan while carrying the last folding chair. An apprentice compared hidden production costs to the unseen part of an iceberg.

電力恢復時，窗外鳥兒開始啁啾。 中心只由受訓人員發放緊急用品。 搬最後一張折疊椅時，志工疲倦地呻吟。 一位學徒把隱藏成本比作冰山看不見的部分。

There was a moment when Aya nearly reached for the familiar comfort of doing everything herself. She stopped, looked at the people who would live with the result, and said: She refused the return offer and requested copies of the original dismissal notice. A worker asked what would happen if the plan failed. Aya answered that they would record the failure, repair what they could, and explain what remained unsafe. Leon said this sounded terribly unlike a heroic legend. "Good," she replied. "The workers need a tomorrow, not a legend."

有一刻，綾差點又想照老習慣一個人把事情做完。她停下，看看那些將承擔結果的人，然後說：她拒絕回隊，要求取得原始解僱通知副本。一名工人問計畫失敗怎麼辦。她回答：記錄失敗、修正能修的地方，並公開還有什麼不安全。雷昂說這聽起來實在不像英雄傳說。她回：「很好。工人需要的是明天，不是傳說。」

The manager's calm directions proved invaluable during the outage. Aya preferred a simple supper to a lavish corporate banquet. The apprentices designed a logo that belonged to their training cooperative. A portable loudspeaker guided visitors to the safe exit.

停電時，管理員冷靜的指示非常寶貴。 綾寧願吃簡單宵夜，也不要奢華公會晚宴。 學徒設計了一個屬於培訓合作社的標誌。 手提擴音器引導來賓走向安全出口。

By evening, the practical result was visible: Leon stayed outside the discussion until Aya invited him to help compare the records. The change was not a miracle; it had required people, money, and the willingness to admit uncertainty. Aya entered the figures in the brass clock's public register. She let the people who had done the work take credit before the officials spoke. Leon watched the room grow less afraid of him. "Is this what a victory feels like?" he asked. "Sometimes," Aya answered, "it feels like a proper dinner."

到了傍晚，實際成果已經看得見：雷昂一直待在會議外，直到綾邀請他協助核對紀錄。這項改變不是奇蹟，而是需要人手、金錢以及承認未知的勇氣。綾把數字登錄在黃銅工時計的公開紀錄。她讓真正做事的人先領功，再輪到官員發言。雷昂看著眾人漸漸不再害怕他，問：「勝利就是這種感覺嗎？」綾說：「有時候，勝利只是能好好吃頓晚餐。」

A martial arts teacher showed how flexible sleeves helped movement. The center reviewed its plan for a possible infectious outbreak. The street stall served oyster noodles beside the community center. Families began to populate the hall as the demonstration started.

武術老師示範有彈性的袖子如何幫助動作。 中心檢查可能發生傳染病爆發時的應變計畫。 社區中心旁的攤位供應蚵仔麵。 示範開始時，家庭陸續走進大廳。

Then a new detail appeared at the bottom of the record: The two notices bore different dates and signatures. Pipi reached for a celebratory stamp, but Aya caught his wrist. "We have not solved it," she said. "We have learned what to ask next." Leon walked her as far as the public stairs. He did not ask where she lived or insist on accompanying her farther. In the world Aya had left, people had called boundaries unfriendly. Here, in a castle ruled by a Demon King, they were beginning to feel surprisingly safe.

接著，紀錄下方出現新的細節：兩份通知的日期與簽名竟然不同。皮皮想拿慶祝印章，綾抓住他的手腕說：「我們還沒解決問題，只是知道接下來該問什麼。」雷昂送她走到公共樓梯，沒有問她住哪裡，也沒有堅持繼續陪走。在綾離開的世界裡，人們常說界線很不友善。但在這座由魔王統治的城堡裡，清楚的界線反而令人安心。

The sudden power failure was a setback, not the end of the lesson. Aya remained skeptical of publicity claims that lacked actual training funds. Visitors could subscribe to updates without buying the craft collection. The charity promised not to withhold apprentice wages during an audit.

突然停電是挫折，卻不是課程終點。 沒有真正培訓經費的宣傳說法讓綾保持懷疑。 來賓不必購買工藝系列，也能訂閱更新資訊。 慈善機構承諾稽核期間不扣留學徒薪水。
