The Lease Is a Trap

地下城的怪物開始向我繳房租 · 第 10 / 25 章 · 2000–3000 · 英中對照 · 校訂中

用互動版查詞、朗讀與收藏

Old leases reveal the Dungeon Board intends to evict every nonhuman tenant and seize the property. Jin felt the meaning of this new event before the full crowd understood it. In Hollowkeep, each unusual sign could become a rumor by evening, and rumors were hard to pull back once they had spread. Mara stood beside the nearest witness and asked for the sequence of actions, not a dramatic conclusion. Some details contradicted what they had learned the day before. That was disappointing and useful at the same time. Jin opened a fresh page, wrote the date at the top, and decided the truth would need more than one voice.

舊租約揭露地下城管理會打算驅逐所有非人類房客並沒收產權。 群眾尚未完全理解時，金已感到這件新事的分量。在空堡，每個異常徵兆到了晚上都可能變成謠言，而謠言一旦傳開就很難收回。瑪拉站到最近的證人身邊，詢問行動的先後順序，而非戲劇性的結論。有些細節與昨天得到的資訊矛盾，既令人失望也很有用。金翻開新的一頁，在上方寫下日期，決定真相需要不止一個人的聲音。

A messenger reached Hollowkeep carrying two conflicting reports. Jin read them slowly rather than asking everyone to trust the louder witness. Mara placed the final door beside the papers and drew a line under the part they could verify. It was tempting to guess what had happened, especially when people were afraid. Yet one wrong guess could send a friend into danger. The group wrote down the times, the names, and the things nobody had seen clearly. Someone objected to wasting time. Jin replied that a moment spent checking facts was less expensive than a mistake that could not be repaired.

一名信使帶著兩份互相矛盾的報告抵達空堡。金慢慢讀完，沒有要大家相信聲音較大的證人。瑪拉將最後一扇門放在紙張旁，在可以確認的地方畫線。尤其人們害怕時，猜測發生了什麼很容易；但一次猜錯就可能把朋友送入危險。團隊記下時間、姓名和沒有人看清的事。有個人抗議浪費時間。金回答，花一點時間查證，總比犯下無法補救的錯誤便宜。

The first test gave the team a little hope and one new problem. Jin insisted they record both results. Mara watched the boundary of the working area and told observers to keep their distance. The second test was slower, but it showed exactly where the old method failed. A careless account would have called the effort a complete success. They knew better. Each person signed the record only for the part personally checked, leaving space for another witness to disagree. The honest gaps in the notes were not weaknesses; they were signs that the writers understood the limits of what they knew.

第一次測試帶來一點希望，也帶來一個新問題。金堅持同時記下兩項結果。瑪拉守著工作區邊界，請旁觀者保持距離。第二次測試更慢，卻準確指出舊方法的失敗位置。草率的紀錄會把行動說成完全成功，但他們知道不能如此。每個人只為親自確認的部分簽名，也留空間讓其他證人提出異議。筆記裡誠實的空白不是弱點，而是執筆者理解自身所知有限的證明。

During a pause in the investigation, Mara unfolded an old language ledger. Five marked entries on the first line read "banker", "bay", "beam", "champion", "creative". The next line held "decrease", "echo", "fearful", "historic", "holder". None was a magic password or an answer by itself; each was a fragment of a larger history. Jin asked who had written the marks, why they had been kept, and whether they belonged to the same account. It would have been easy to declare the puzzle solved simply because the words were now visible. Instead, they copied the terms, recorded the source, and returned to the unresolved problem outside the room.

調查暫停時，瑪拉攤開一本舊語詞紀錄。第一行的五個註記寫著「banker」、「bay」、「beam」、「champion」、「creative」；下一行則有「decrease」、「echo」、「fearful」、「historic」、「holder」。它們都不是魔法密碼，也無法單獨解答問題；每個詞只是更大段歷史的片段。金詢問誰寫下這些字、為何保存，以及它們是否屬於同一份記錄。只因文字可見就宣稱解開謎題很容易，但他們只抄下詞條、標明來源，便回到室外仍未解決的問題。

'The last room must remain closed until every tenant agrees on a safe inspection.' Mara said, reading from the page they had written together. The sentence sounded simple until it was tested by actual need. A stranger asked for an exception, claiming the danger was too urgent for ordinary rules. Jin listened and checked whether anyone else would pay for that exception. The answer was not comfortable. It required time, an explanation, and a promise to review the decision when new evidence appeared. Jin could feel the crowd waiting for a dramatic command. Instead, the group made a smaller choice they could explain to the people who would live with it.

「最後一個房間必須保持關閉，直到所有房客同意安全檢查。」瑪拉讀出兩人一起寫下的句子。這話聽來簡單，直到真正的需求開始考驗它。一名陌生人以危險太急迫為由要求例外。金傾聽後先確認是否有其他人得替這個例外付代價。答案並不舒服：他們需要時間、說明，以及在新證據出現時重審的承諾。金感覺人群等著一道戲劇性命令，團隊卻做出較小、能向承擔後果的人解釋的選擇。

During a pause in the investigation, Mara unfolded an old language ledger. Five marked entries on the first line read "leak", "mob", "normal", "palace", "penny". The next line held "postpone", "receiver", "scholarship", "steal", "zipper". None was a magic password or an answer by itself; each was a fragment of a larger history. Jin asked who had written the marks, why they had been kept, and whether they belonged to the same account. It would have been easy to declare the puzzle solved simply because the words were now visible. Instead, they copied the terms, recorded the source, and returned to the unresolved problem outside the room.

調查暫停時，瑪拉攤開一本舊語詞紀錄。第一行的五個註記寫著「leak」、「mob」、「normal」、「palace」、「penny」；下一行則有「postpone」、「receiver」、「scholarship」、「steal」、「zipper」。它們都不是魔法密碼，也無法單獨解答問題；每個詞只是更大段歷史的片段。金詢問誰寫下這些字、為何保存，以及它們是否屬於同一份記錄。只因文字可見就宣稱解開謎題很容易，但他們只抄下詞條、標明來源，便回到室外仍未解決的問題。

After the meeting, Mara walked with Jin toward a quieter road. There was still a great deal to repair, and the group had not agreed on every detail. Jin admitted being afraid of failing the people who had offered their trust. Mara answered that the aim was not to become impossible to question. It was to make a plan that could be corrected before anybody's pride became everybody's danger. They checked the next appointment and promised to bring another pair of eyes. Friendship did not remove the risk. It made admitting mistakes possible, and that changed how they understood courage.

會議後，瑪拉陪金走向較安靜的道路。仍有許多事情待修，團隊也不是每項細節都同意。金坦承害怕辜負那些信任自己的人。瑪拉說，目標不是變成不容質疑的人，而是讓計畫能在某人的自尊危害大家之前被改正。他們確認下次約定，答應多找一雙眼睛來檢查。友情不會消除風險，但它讓認錯變得可能，也改變了他們對勇氣的理解。

Before leaving, Jin wrote one line at the bottom of the page: the day's answer could still be corrected. Mara checked that people affected by the decision could read it and reply. A messenger brought news that the next meeting would concern 'A Kitchen for Everyone'. Neither knew precisely what this new matter would demand. They did know what should not be traded away to save a few hours: honest records, a safe path, and the right of someone weaker to disagree. Jin carried the unfinished notes toward Hollowkeep, listening to the footsteps behind them and thinking of the question that morning would bring.

離開前，金在紙頁底下寫道：今天的答案仍然可以訂正。瑪拉確認受決定影響的人能讀到並回應。信使傳來消息，下次會議將處理〈共享廚房〉。兩人都不確定新事件要求什麼，卻知道哪些東西不能只為節省幾小時就被交換掉：誠實紀錄、安全道路，以及弱者提出異議的權利。金帶著未完成的筆記走向空堡，聽著背後腳步聲，想著明早會出現的問題。
