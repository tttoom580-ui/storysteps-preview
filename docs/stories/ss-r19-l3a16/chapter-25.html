The Door Stays Closed

地下城的怪物開始向我繳房租 · 第 25 / 25 章 · 2000–3000 · 英中對照 · 校訂中

Jin gives the keys to elected tenants and celebrates the first year of honest rent and open accounts. Jin felt the meaning of this new event before the full crowd understood it. In Hollowkeep, each unusual sign could become a rumor by evening, and rumors were hard to pull back once they had spread. Mara stood beside the nearest witness and asked for the sequence of actions, not a dramatic conclusion. Some details contradicted what they had learned the day before. That was disappointing and useful at the same time. Jin opened a fresh page, wrote the date at the top, and decided the truth would need more than one voice.

金把鑰匙交給民選房客代表，慶祝第一年的誠實租金與公開帳目。 群眾尚未完全理解時，金已感到這件新事的分量。在空堡，每個異常徵兆到了晚上都可能變成謠言，而謠言一旦傳開就很難收回。瑪拉站到最近的證人身邊，詢問行動的先後順序，而非戲劇性的結論。有些細節與昨天得到的資訊矛盾，既令人失望也很有用。金翻開新的一頁，在上方寫下日期，決定真相需要不止一個人的聲音。

At the end of the day, Jin sat beside the final door and tried to count what had genuinely changed. There was no sudden victory, but several people who had been afraid now knew whom to ask for help. Mara brought a second copy of the records and placed it out of reach of anyone who might remove the first. The plan had become less exciting and more dependable. That did not disappoint Jin. The question was never how to appear fearless. It was how to leave a safer place behind, even if the next person in charge had a different name.

一天結束時，金坐在最後一扇門旁，試著清點真正改變了什麼。沒有突然的勝利，但原本害怕的幾個人如今知道該向誰求助。瑪拉帶來第二份紀錄，放到即使第一份被拿走也不會一起消失的地方。計畫變得不那麼刺激，卻更可靠。金並不失望。問題從來不是如何顯得無所畏懼，而是即使下個負責人換了名字，也能留下更安全的地方。

While others argued over a fast solution, Mara asked who had the right to make the final decision. Jin could have answered with a name or a title. Instead, they made a list of everyone whose home, work, or safety would change. The list was longer than expected. Some people could come to a meeting; others needed a visit or a written invitation. There was no way to make a difficult choice painless, but a choice made without listening would be worse. The team planned another discussion and made sure the invitation did not require wealth or rank.

其他人爭論最快解法時，瑪拉問誰有權作最後決定。金本可回答一個姓名或頭銜，卻改列出家園、工作或安全會受到影響的所有人。清單比預期長。有些人能來開會，另一些需要拜訪或書面邀請。艱難選擇不可能毫無痛苦，但不傾聽就做決定更糟。團隊安排下一次討論，確保受邀不需要財富或階級。

During a pause in the investigation, Mara unfolded an old language ledger. Five marked entries on the first line read "aboard", "beetle", "bet", "broadcast", "crab". The next line held "destroy", "editor", "enjoyable", "frank", "interrupt". None was a magic password or an answer by itself; each was a fragment of a larger history. Jin asked who had written the marks, why they had been kept, and whether they belonged to the same account. It would have been easy to declare the puzzle solved simply because the words were now visible. Instead, they copied the terms, recorded the source, and returned to the unresolved problem outside the room.

調查暫停時，瑪拉攤開一本舊語詞紀錄。第一行的五個註記寫著「aboard」、「beetle」、「bet」、「broadcast」、「crab」；下一行則有「destroy」、「editor」、「enjoyable」、「frank」、「interrupt」。它們都不是魔法密碼，也無法單獨解答問題；每個詞只是更大段歷史的片段。金詢問誰寫下這些字、為何保存，以及它們是否屬於同一份記錄。只因文字可見就宣稱解開謎題很容易，但他們只抄下詞條、標明來源，便回到室外仍未解決的問題。

There was a practical problem behind the dramatic story. A route could look short on paper and still be impossible for a tired person to use. Jin walked it at a normal pace while Mara counted the steps and listed obstacles. At the narrowest point they stopped because the safest answer was not the fastest one. Someone suggested using the final door as proof that everything was under control. Jin refused the easy claim. An impressive object could not replace a careful test, especially when strangers might have to trust it without knowing the story behind it.

戲劇性的故事背後有個實際問題。路線在紙上看起來很短，對疲憊的人卻可能走不了。金以正常速度走一遍，瑪拉數著步數並列出障礙。他們在最窄處停下，因為最安全的答案未必最快。有人提議用最後一扇門證明一切都在掌握中，金拒絕了這種輕率說法。再令人印象深刻的物件，也不能取代仔細測試，尤其當陌生人必須在不知道背景時相信它。

During a pause in the investigation, Mara unfolded an old language ledger. Five marked entries on the first line read "misery", "motel", "pine", "politics", "regional". The next line held "republic", "romantic", "salary", "studio", "stuff", "victim". None was a magic password or an answer by itself; each was a fragment of a larger history. Jin asked who had written the marks, why they had been kept, and whether they belonged to the same account. It would have been easy to declare the puzzle solved simply because the words were now visible. Instead, they copied the terms, recorded the source, and returned to the unresolved problem outside the room.

調查暫停時，瑪拉攤開一本舊語詞紀錄。第一行的五個註記寫著「misery」、「motel」、「pine」、「politics」、「regional」；下一行則有「republic」、「romantic」、「salary」、「studio」、「stuff」、「victim」。它們都不是魔法密碼，也無法單獨解答問題；每個詞只是更大段歷史的片段。金詢問誰寫下這些字、為何保存，以及它們是否屬於同一份記錄。只因文字可見就宣稱解開謎題很容易，但他們只抄下詞條、標明來源，便回到室外仍未解決的問題。

After the meeting, Mara walked with Jin toward a quieter road. There was still a great deal to repair, and the group had not agreed on every detail. Jin admitted being afraid of failing the people who had offered their trust. Mara answered that the aim was not to become impossible to question. It was to make a plan that could be corrected before anybody's pride became everybody's danger. They checked the next appointment and promised to bring another pair of eyes. Friendship did not remove the risk. It made admitting mistakes possible, and that changed how they understood courage.

會議後，瑪拉陪金走向較安靜的道路。仍有許多事情待修，團隊也不是每項細節都同意。金坦承害怕辜負那些信任自己的人。瑪拉說，目標不是變成不容質疑的人，而是讓計畫能在某人的自尊危害大家之前被改正。他們確認下次約定，答應多找一雙眼睛來檢查。友情不會消除風險，但它讓認錯變得可能，也改變了他們對勇氣的理解。

There was no perfect speech at the end. People in Hollowkeep could question their leaders, change their minds, and share the results without waiting for a chosen hero. Jin remembered the fear of the first day and saw how many ordinary people had helped carry the work. Mara stood nearby, no longer needing to fill every silence. Tomorrow would bring repairs, meals, new arguments, and new mistakes. That was not a weakness of their victory; it was proof that life had continued. They walked home without asking the world to stop changing, ready to make another choice together.

結局沒有完美演說。空堡的人民終於能質疑領袖、改變想法，並分享成果，不必等待命定英雄。金想起第一天的恐懼，也看見有多少普通人共同完成這些工作。瑪拉站在一旁，不再需要填滿每段沉默。明天還會有修理、飯菜、新爭執與新錯誤。這不是勝利的弱點，而是生活仍在繼續的證明。他們一起走回家，不要求世界停止改變，只準備再一起做新的選擇。
