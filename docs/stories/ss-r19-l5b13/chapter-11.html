At a closed hearing, specialists debated whether Lina's recording could count as evidence from a person who might never exist. A philosopher spoke of rights; an engineer spoke of branching probabilities; a parent spoke of a child who had already been made famous without consent. Qi asked the court to examine the mechanism without deciding that every possible future person could be summoned to testify. The court limited the signal's use to investigative guidance. No personal prediction could be published. Han sat beside his lawyer, relieved and frightened at once. The decision protected Lina, but it also removed the easiest defense argument that somebody else would surely confess in the future.
