二十年後傳來的聲音

A Voice from Twenty Years Ahead

The first witness in the Riverbridge case had not been born. Lawyer Qi Yan read the notice three times before accepting that the court had truly sent it. In the space reserved for a witness's birth date, someone had entered a year twenty years in the future. Below it stood a single sentence: TESTIMONY RECEIVED AND VERIFIED. Qi had no certainty about what “verified” meant. The metropolitan court had strict rules for evidence, and its public forum was usually crowded with arguments about even the smallest technical mistake. Yet today, not a single clerk had objected. They were all watching a sealed silver case on the evidence table.

河橋案的第一位證人，甚至尚未出生。律師祁言把通知讀了三次，才接受這真的是法院寄來的文件。證人出生日期欄填著二十年後的某一年，下面只有一句話：「證詞已接收並完成驗證。」祁言不知道「驗證」究竟意味著什麼。都會法院對證據有嚴格規定，就連最小的技術錯誤，也常在公開討論區引起爭辯。但今天沒有任何書記官提出異議。所有人都望向證物桌上的銀色封箱。

The courtroom stood beside an old chapel, now preserved as part of the city's heritage. On the chapel roof, a cluster of cameras monitored the corridor leading to the court's evidence wing. They had recorded the silver case arriving at 6:12 that morning. The courier was a maintenance robot, carrying no sender's name. A generator in the basement had supplied power during a short outage; the security system showed no interruption. Qi had checked the records twice. He disliked mysteries dressed as facts. “A future witness,” he murmured, “is an extraordinary claim, not proof.” His assistant, Lin, was almost envious of his calm expression.

法院位於一座古老禮拜堂旁；那棟建築如今被保存為城市文化遺產。禮拜堂屋頂裝著一組攝影機，監看通往法院證物區的走廊。影像拍到銀色箱子在當天早上六點十二分抵達。送件者是一台沒有託運人資料的維修機器人。地下室的發電機在短暫停電時提供電力，保全系統並未中斷。祁言反覆查過紀錄。他不喜歡把謎團打扮成事實。「來自未來的證人，」他低聲說，「是非比尋常的主張，不是證明。」助理小林幾乎羨慕起他平靜的表情；

A time-evidence researcher named Cen Yu arrived with a document that Qi had never seen before. She explained that the device inside the case could receive a brief signal from one possible future, but could not send a person through time. “It may show an event that never happens,” she said. “Observation can change the conditions that produced it.” Qi asked whether opening the case would count as an authorized transaction between two times. Cen shook her head. No such legal category existed. The rules did contain a provision allowing courts to preserve unusual records while their reliability was tested. Viewing the record, however, was optional. Entering it into evidence was an entirely different decision.

研究時間證據的岑予帶著一份祁言從未見過的文件來到法院。她解釋，箱內設備可以接收某一種可能未來傳來的短暫訊號，卻無法把人送往不同的時間。「它可能呈現一件最後根本沒有發生的事，」她說，「觀察本身就可能改變產生訊號的條件。」祁言問，打開箱子是否算兩個時間之間獲得授權的交易。岑予搖頭，法律根本沒有這個分類。不過規則中確有一項條款，允許法院在可靠性尚待檢驗時保全特殊紀錄。是否觀看紀錄可以選擇；是否把它採為證據，則是另一個完全不同的決定。

The supervisor of the evidence unit entered carrying a pot of flowers. Their leaves had begun to wither beneath the court's dry lights. She set the pot near the window, where the chapel's garden provided a small habitat for insects and birds. Then she looked at Cen. “The government has offered compensation to the families harmed by the bridge collapse,” she said. “If this recording can identify who ignored the warnings, it could change the case.” Qi felt an impulse to open the case immediately. He had represented several families, and their losses were immense. But a possible future was not a substitute for a witness who had actually seen the disaster.

證物組主管捧著一盆花走進來。葉片在法院乾燥的燈光下已開始枯萎。她把花放到窗邊；禮拜堂的庭園原本就為昆蟲與鳥類提供一小片棲地。接著，她看向岑予。「政府已向橋梁坍塌事件的受害家庭提出賠償，」她說，「如果這段錄影能指出誰忽略了警告，案件可能因此改變。」祁言有一股衝動，想立刻打開箱子。他代理過幾戶受害家庭，深知他們失去的東西有多巨大。然而，一個可能的未來，不能直接取代真正親眼見過災難的證人。

Cen requested a controlled viewing. The silver case stood behind a protective screen. Qi read the procedure aloud while Lin checked every step. When the screen lit up, he saw a girl of perhaps seventeen, sitting in a room filled with afternoon sun. She had dark hair and an old watch around her wrist. She did not stumble over her words. “I witnessed the accident at Riverbridge,” she said. “I remember the railing breaking before the warning bell rang.” Qi almost interrupted. The girl's face was familiar, although he could not say why. Then she continued: “The people asking you to play this record are not asking the right question.”

岑予申請受控觀看。銀箱放在防護屏後。祁言朗讀操作程序，小林逐步核對。螢幕亮起時，畫面裡坐著一名大約十七歲的女孩，房內灑滿午後陽光。她有一頭深色頭髮，手腕上戴著舊錶。她說話很穩，沒有結巴。「我親眼見過河橋事故，」她說，「我記得警報鐘響起以前，欄杆就已經斷裂。」祁言幾乎忍不住打斷她。女孩的臉有些熟悉，他卻說不出原因。接著她又說：「要求你們播放這段紀錄的人，問錯了問題。」

The girl leaned closer to the camera. “You want to know who caused the collapse. But if you disclose everything on this recording, one person who could stop the next disaster will never be born.” The image froze. Qi waited for the rest of the statement, but only silence came. Cen checked the device and confirmed that the transmission had ended. The room seemed much smaller than before. Outside, lawyers were preparing questions about liability and responsibility. Inside, Qi faced an impossible choice: use an uncertain warning to help people already suffering, or protect a child whose existence was still only a possibility. The insight disturbed him: the recording could not decide for him.

女孩向鏡頭靠近。「你們想知道是誰造成橋梁坍塌。但如果公開這段錄影裡的全部內容，一個原本可能阻止下一次災難的人，就永遠不會出生。」影像停住。祁言等著後續，卻只剩沉默。岑予檢查設備，確認傳輸已結束。房間忽然顯得比剛才狹小。外頭，律師們正準備詢問賠償責任與過失。室內，祁言面對一個幾乎無法回答的抉擇：利用未經確認的警告幫助正在受苦的人，還是保護一個目前只存在於可能性中的孩子。這個領悟使他不安：錄影無法替他作決定。

He asked Cen to copy the raw signal and seal the original without changing a frame. Then he requested that the court restrict public access until an independent review could establish what the device had actually measured. The supervisor frowned. “The families deserve answers.” “They do,” Qi replied, “and they deserve answers that survive examination.” He took out his notebook and wrote three tasks: verify the bridge's maintenance records, identify the source of the old watch, and determine who had placed the case in the courthouse. None required accepting the future as settled history. Cen read the list and nodded. “That's a beginning,” she said. “Not a verdict.”

他請岑予複製原始訊號，將原件封存，不能改動任何一格影像。接著，他請求法院暫時限制公開閱覽，直到獨立審查能確認設備究竟測量到了什麼。主管皺起眉頭。「那些家庭有權知道答案。」「他們當然有，」祁言回答，「而且他們有權得到經得起檢驗的答案。」他拿出筆記本，寫下三項工作：核對橋梁維修紀錄、找出那只舊錶的來源，以及確認是誰把箱子送進法院。這些工作都不需要先認定未來已成為既定歷史。岑予看了看清單，點頭說：「這是一個開始。還不是判決。」

As they left the room, a clerk hurried toward them holding a second envelope. It had appeared in the filing system during the viewing, although the network had been isolated. Inside lay one photograph: a hospital nursery, an empty bed, and the same old watch hanging from its rail. A date had been written on the back in careful handwriting—three years from now. Qi understood nothing about the mechanism, but the message's purpose was clear. Someone wanted the future witness to have a particular mother, a particular birth, and perhaps a particular fate. He closed the envelope without showing the photograph to the waiting press. The first hearing would begin in twenty minutes.

他們離開房間時，一名書記官拿著第二個信封匆匆跑來。信封在剛才觀看影像的期間出現在收件系統裡，儘管網路已經隔離。裡面只有一張照片：醫院嬰兒室、一張空床，以及掛在床欄上的同一只舊錶。背面以端正的字跡寫著日期——三年後。祁言仍不明白這套機制怎麼運作，但訊息的目的很清楚。有人想讓未來證人擁有某位特定的母親、某個特定的出生時刻，甚至某種特定命運。他把信封闔上，沒有向守候的媒體展示照片。距離第一次庭訊，只剩二十分鐘。

Why did Qi Yan refuse to treat the future recording as conclusive evidence?
