In the other, the trial proceeded without changes and more people were injured. The images did not identify which future would occur. A television producer called the child a prophet and demanded a live feed. Qi filed for a publication restriction, arguing that a public broadcast could influence the futures being examined and expose private lives. The judge limited access to neutral experts. Outside the courthouse, Han admitted he had delayed a maintenance review under budget pressure. Qi recorded the admission and warned him it might affect the defense. The case was becoming more difficult—and more honest.
