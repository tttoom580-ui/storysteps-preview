The Fair Share

The morning began quietly in the small island, although Lin knew that quiet did not mean safe. It was a difficult week, and the group had already learned that a confident guess could be worse than an honest question. Lin reviewed the rescue map and invited Hao and Mina, Sora, and Tao to look at the same page. Nobody had to agree before hearing the whole problem. Sora argues that the strongest hikers need more food; Mina asks how injured people can contribute. They agree on measured rations and shared tasks. Their task suddenly seemed larger than it had a moment before. Lin made one important choice: the next step would be written down where everyone could check it.

在這座小島，這個早晨起初很安靜，但琳知道安靜不代表安全。現在已是困難的一週，大家明白，自信的猜測有時比誠實提問更危險。琳翻閱救援地圖，邀請皓與米娜、空與陶一起查看相同頁面。所有人都可以先聽完整個問題，再決定是否同意。當天的第一份紀錄寫道：空認為走得遠的人需要更多食物；米娜則問受傷的人如何參與。五人同意定量分配並安排適合各人的任務。那一刻，眼前任務突然顯得比剛才更加龐大。琳做出重要決定：下一步要公開記下，讓每個人都能核對。

Lin made a record of the affair, so everyone could compare it later. Hao asked how the badminton might change their plans. They examined the cabbage and waited for a clearer answer. Someone described the result as clever, which the team needed to verify.

琳記下了事件，讓所有人日後能夠比較。 皓詢問羽球可能如何改變大家的計畫。 他們檢查高麗菜，等待更清楚的答案。 有人形容結果聰明的，團隊仍需要確認。

Before acting, Hao asked which facts came from direct observation. The team drew two columns: what they knew and what they had only heard. Lin moved a doubtful claim into the second column because its source was missing. Nobody called the correction a defeat. They wanted the next person to understand the evidence, not to inherit an untested guess.

行動之前，皓問大家哪些事實來自直接觀察。團隊畫出兩欄：一欄是確知的事，一欄是聽來的話。琳把找不到來源的說法移到第二欄。沒有人把這項更正當成失敗；大家希望後來的人能理解證據，而不是繼承未經驗證的猜測。

The crisis became one more detail to check before the group moved on. Lin made a record of the culture, so everyone could compare it later. Hao asked how the engine might change their plans. There was still a chance to excitement, if the next test worked.

在團隊繼續之前，危機成了需要查核的另一項細節。 琳記下了文化，讓所有人日後能夠比較。 皓詢問引擎可能如何改變大家的計畫。 如果下一次測試成功，仍然有機會興奮。

They made a small test before changing the larger plan. Lin explained each step aloud while Hao watched for a mistake. A second person kept the old record so that the result could be compared later. At first the difference looked too small to matter. Then Mina, Sora, and Tao noticed that one part of the plan would affect people who were not in the room. The group paused and changed the order of its work. They did not pretend a single success was enough. A good test was something another person could repeat, with clear limits and a way to stop safely.

他們先進行小型測試，再考慮改動大計畫。琳逐步大聲說明操作，皓則注意可能的錯誤。另一個人保留舊紀錄，方便日後比較。起初差異小得像是不重要，後來米娜、空與陶注意到其中一步會影響當時不在場的人。團隊停下來調整工作順序，沒有假裝一次成功就足夠。真正好的測試，是別人也能重複操作、限制清楚，而且知道如何安全停止的測試。

Another detail changed the mood of the group. Mina volunteered to record portions, showing that an injured person could still protect the whole group. The others had a chance to ask questions before the decision became final. A shared choice was not always easy, but it was easier to defend when everyone knew its cost.

另一個細節改變了團隊氣氛。米娜自願記錄份量，證明受傷的人仍能保護整個團隊。大家在決定正式生效前仍有提問的機會。共同選擇不一定容易，但當所有人都知道代價時，就更能為它負責。

The sea made a deep sound against the far rocks. Lin watched the foam instead of trying to guess how high the next wave would rise. A thin line of shells marked where earlier water had reached. Hao measured the distance from that line to their supplies. The number did not promise safety, but it helped them choose where to sleep.

海浪撞上遠方岩石，發出低沉聲響。琳盯著浪花，沒有隨意猜測下一波海水會升多高。一條細細的貝殼線標示先前潮水抵達的位置。皓量了那條線到物資堆的距離。數字不能保證安全，卻幫助大家決定睡覺的位置。

The group discussed the fireman openly rather than allow a rumor to decide. The note used forth to describe a step, but the timing still needed checking. Lin offered to govern, but wanted the others to agree first. Hao said the situation felt human, though he could still be wrong.

團隊公開討論消防員，不讓傳聞替大家做決定。 筆記使用「向前」描述某個步驟，但時間仍要確認。 琳願意治理，但希望先取得其他人的同意。 皓說情況感覺人的，但也承認自己可能判斷錯誤。

Later, Lin and Hao spoke away from the others. Lin admitted to being afraid of making a decision that could not be repaired. Hao said that fear was useful when it encouraged care, but dangerous when it stopped people from listening. Neither offered an easy promise. They returned to the group and explained what had changed, inviting questions from every person affected. Their friendship did not solve the problem, but it made disagreement less frightening. They could still disagree the next morning, and they could still work on the same problem. That seemed more valuable than winning an argument in front of an audience.

稍後，琳與皓離開人群談話。琳坦承自己害怕做出無法挽回的決定。皓說，害怕若能提醒人謹慎就有用；若讓人不肯聆聽就會危險。兩人沒有輕易保證一切順利，而是回到團隊說明改動，邀請所有受影響的人提問。友誼本身無法解決問題，卻讓意見不同不再那麼可怕。隔天他們仍可能意見不同，也仍能為同一問題努力。這似乎比在眾人面前贏得爭論更有價值。

They did not treat the description individual as evidence by itself. The group discussed the journal openly rather than allow a rumor to decide. The neighbor became one more detail to check before the group moved on. Lin offered to participate, but wanted the others to agree first.

他們沒有只憑「個別的」這種描述就當成證據。 團隊公開討論日誌，不讓傳聞替大家做決定。 在團隊繼續之前，鄰居成了需要查核的另一項細節。 琳願意參與，但希望先取得其他人的同意。

By evening the team had completed one useful piece of work, not the whole journey. Lin checked the rescue map one last time and wrote both the result and the remaining doubt. Hao read the entry and asked whether a newcomer could understand it without meeting the people who wrote it. The answer was not quite yes, so they added the missing detail. Their shared goal remained to reach a safe rescue point without risking anyone's life. Tomorrow would bring a different test, and the group could not know its answer in advance. For tonight, however, everyone knew exactly what had been done, what had not been done, and who had agreed to the next step.

傍晚時，團隊完成了一項有用的工作，卻還沒走完整段旅程。琳最後檢查救援地圖，同時寫下結果與尚未解決的疑問。皓讀完後問：如果新來的人沒見過作者，能看懂嗎？答案還不完全是肯定的，於是大家補上缺少的細節。他們共同的目標仍是在不讓任何人冒險的前提下抵達安全的救援點。明天會有不同的考驗，誰也不能提前知道答案。不過今晚每個人都清楚：哪些事已完成、哪些尚未完成，以及下一步由誰共同同意。

Hao asked how the pillow might change their plans. They examined the prison and waited for a clearer answer. The group discussed the shore openly rather than allow a rumor to decide. The socks became one more detail to check before the group moved on.

皓詢問靠墊可能如何改變大家的計畫。 他們檢查監獄，等待更清楚的答案。 團隊公開討論海岸，不讓傳聞替大家做決定。 在團隊繼續之前，襪子成了需要查核的另一項細節。
