米娜不能奔跑

Mina Cannot Run

無人島的第五道求救訊號 · 第 10 / 25 章 · 1000–2000 · 英中對照 · 校訂中

The morning began quietly in the small island, although Lin knew that quiet did not mean safe. It was a difficult week, and the group had already learned that a confident guess could be worse than an honest question. Lin reviewed the rescue map and invited Hao and Mina, Sora, and Tao to look at the same page. Nobody had to agree before hearing the whole problem. Mina twists her ankle while carrying the water bag. Lin stops the long march, makes a rest plan, and divides the load again. Their task suddenly seemed larger than it had a moment before. Lin made one important choice: the next step would be written down where everyone could check it.

在這座小島，這個早晨起初很安靜，但琳知道安靜不代表安全。現在已是困難的一週，大家明白，自信的猜測有時比誠實提問更危險。琳翻閱救援地圖，邀請皓與米娜、空與陶一起查看相同頁面。所有人都可以先聽完整個問題，再決定是否同意。當天的第一份紀錄寫道：米娜背水袋時扭傷腳踝。琳停止長途探路，安排休息，重新分配負重。那一刻，眼前任務突然顯得比剛才更加龐大。琳做出重要決定：下一步要公開記下，讓每個人都能核對。

At first the answer looked active, but the team checked again. Lin made a record of the concern, so everyone could compare it later. Hao asked how the cycle might change their plans. They examined the distance and waited for a clearer answer.

答案起初看來積極的，但團隊再次核對。 琳記下了關心，讓所有人日後能夠比較。 皓詢問週期可能如何改變大家的計畫。 他們檢查距離，等待更清楚的答案。

Before acting, Hao asked which facts came from direct observation. The team drew two columns: what they knew and what they had only heard. Lin moved a doubtful claim into the second column because its source was missing. Nobody called the correction a defeat. They wanted the next person to understand the evidence, not to inherit an untested guess.

行動之前，皓問大家哪些事實來自直接觀察。團隊畫出兩欄：一欄是確知的事，一欄是聽來的話。琳把找不到來源的說法移到第二欄。沒有人把這項更正當成失敗；大家希望後來的人能理解證據，而不是繼承未經驗證的猜測。

The group discussed the flag openly rather than allow a rumor to decide. At first the answer looked flat, but the team checked again. Lin asked what forward meant in this part of the instruction. Hao asked whether they could gain without putting anyone in danger.

團隊公開討論旗子，不讓傳聞替大家做決定。 答案起初看來平坦的，但團隊再次核對。 琳詢問這段指示裡的「向前」究竟是什麼意思。 皓問大家能否在不讓任何人陷入危險的情況下得到。

They made a small test before changing the larger plan. Lin explained each step aloud while Hao watched for a mistake. A second person kept the old record so that the result could be compared later. At first the difference looked too small to matter. Then Mina, Sora, and Tao noticed that one part of the plan would affect people who were not in the room. The group paused and changed the order of its work. They did not pretend a single success was enough. A good test was something another person could repeat, with clear limits and a way to stop safely.

他們先進行小型測試，再考慮改動大計畫。琳逐步大聲說明操作，皓則注意可能的錯誤。另一個人保留舊紀錄，方便日後比較。起初差異小得像是不重要，後來米娜、空與陶注意到其中一步會影響當時不在場的人。團隊停下來調整工作順序，沒有假裝一次成功就足夠。真正好的測試，是別人也能重複操作、限制清楚，而且知道如何安全停止的測試。

Another detail changed the mood of the group. Tao took over the longer walk, while Mina taught the others to read the water measurements. The others had a chance to ask questions before the decision became final. A shared choice was not always easy, but it was easier to defend when everyone knew its cost.

另一個細節改變了團隊氣氛。陶接手較遠的路線，米娜則教其他人讀懂水量紀錄。大家在決定正式生效前仍有提問的機會。共同選擇不一定容易，但當所有人都知道代價時，就更能為它負責。

From the high slope, the open sea looked impossibly wide. Hao used stones to hold the bright cloth in place, while Mina checked whether its shape remained clear from farther away. They practiced without imagining a rescue ship that was not there. A signal mattered most when tired hands could make it again the following day.

從高坡望去，開闊海面寬得難以想像。皓用石塊固定亮色布，米娜從較遠的位置確認形狀是否仍清楚。大家練習時沒有幻想海上已經出現救援船。真正有用的訊號，是疲倦的手隔天仍能再次完成的訊號。

There was still a chance to indicate, if the next test worked. The group discussed the law openly rather than allow a rumor to decide. The notebook became one more detail to check before the group moved on. Lin offered to occur, but wanted the others to agree first.

如果下一次測試成功，仍然有機會顯示。 團隊公開討論法律，不讓傳聞替大家做決定。 在團隊繼續之前，筆記本成了需要查核的另一項細節。 琳願意發生，但希望先取得其他人的同意。

Later, Lin and Hao spoke away from the others. Lin admitted to being afraid of making a decision that could not be repaired. Hao said that fear was useful when it encouraged care, but dangerous when it stopped people from listening. Neither offered an easy promise. They returned to the group and explained what had changed, inviting questions from every person affected. Their friendship did not solve the problem, but it made disagreement less frightening. They could still disagree the next morning, and they could still work on the same problem. That seemed more valuable than winning an argument in front of an audience.

稍後，琳與皓離開人群談話。琳坦承自己害怕做出無法挽回的決定。皓說，害怕若能提醒人謹慎就有用；若讓人不肯聆聽就會危險。兩人沒有輕易保證一切順利，而是回到團隊說明改動，邀請所有受影響的人提問。友誼本身無法解決問題，卻讓意見不同不再那麼可怕。隔天他們仍可能意見不同，也仍能為同一問題努力。這似乎比在眾人面前贏得爭論更有價值。

Hao asked how the riches might change their plans. They examined the sex and waited for a clearer answer. The group discussed the silence openly rather than allow a rumor to decide. At first the answer looked single, but the team checked again.

皓詢問財富可能如何改變大家的計畫。 他們檢查性別，等待更清楚的答案。 團隊公開討論沉默，不讓傳聞替大家做決定。 答案起初看來單一的，但團隊再次核對。

By evening the team had completed one useful piece of work, not the whole journey. Lin checked the rescue map one last time and wrote both the result and the remaining doubt. Hao read the entry and asked whether a newcomer could understand it without meeting the people who wrote it. The answer was not quite yes, so they added the missing detail. Their shared goal remained to reach a safe rescue point without risking anyone's life. Tomorrow would bring a different test, and the group could not know its answer in advance. For tonight, however, everyone knew exactly what had been done, what had not been done, and who had agreed to the next step.

傍晚時，團隊完成了一項有用的工作，卻還沒走完整段旅程。琳最後檢查救援地圖，同時寫下結果與尚未解決的疑問。皓讀完後問：如果新來的人沒見過作者，能看懂嗎？答案還不完全是肯定的，於是大家補上缺少的細節。他們共同的目標仍是在不讓任何人冒險的前提下抵達安全的救援點。明天會有不同的考驗，誰也不能提前知道答案。不過今晚每個人都清楚：哪些事已完成、哪些尚未完成，以及下一步由誰共同同意。

Lin made a record of the suit, so everyone could compare it later. Hao asked how the track might change their plans. They examined the valley and waited for a clearer answer. The group discussed the wallet openly rather than allow a rumor to decide.

琳記下了西裝，讓所有人日後能夠比較。 皓詢問步道可能如何改變大家的計畫。 他們檢查谷地，等待更清楚的答案。 團隊公開討論錢包，不讓傳聞替大家做決定。
