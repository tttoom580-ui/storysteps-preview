雨不等於飲用水

Rain Is Not Yet Water

The morning began quietly in the small island, although Lin knew that quiet did not mean safe. It was the early days, and the group had already learned that a confident guess could be worse than an honest question. Lin reviewed the rescue map and invited Hao and Mina, Sora, and Tao to look at the same page. Nobody had to agree before hearing the whole problem. Rain fills the plastic sheets, but the team must choose clean containers and protect them from sand. A muddy inland pool remains unsafe. Their task suddenly seemed larger than it had a moment before. Lin made one important choice: the next step would be written down where everyone could check it.

在這座小島，這個早晨起初很安靜，但琳知道安靜不代表安全。現在已是最初幾天，大家明白，自信的猜測有時比誠實提問更危險。琳翻閱救援地圖，邀請皓與米娜、空與陶一起查看相同頁面。所有人都可以先聽完整個問題，再決定是否同意。當天的第一份紀錄寫道：雨水落上塑膠布，但大家仍得選擇乾淨容器並避免混入沙粒；島內混濁的水池仍不適合飲用。那一刻，眼前任務突然顯得比剛才更加龐大。琳做出重要決定：下一步要公開記下，讓每個人都能核對。

The group discussed the blood openly rather than allow a rumor to decide. The branch became one more detail to check before the group moved on. Lin offered to combine, but wanted the others to agree first. Hao asked how the director might change their plans.

團隊公開討論血，不讓傳聞替大家做決定。 在團隊繼續之前，樹枝成了需要查核的另一項細節。 琳願意結合，但希望先取得其他人的同意。 皓詢問主管可能如何改變大家的計畫。

Before acting, Hao asked which facts came from direct observation. The team drew two columns: what they knew and what they had only heard. Lin moved a doubtful claim into the second column because its source was missing. Nobody called the correction a defeat. They wanted the next person to understand the evidence, not to inherit an untested guess.

行動之前，皓問大家哪些事實來自直接觀察。團隊畫出兩欄：一欄是確知的事，一欄是聽來的話。琳把找不到來源的說法移到第二欄。沒有人把這項更正當成失敗；大家希望後來的人能理解證據，而不是繼承未經驗證的猜測。

They examined the disease and waited for a clearer answer. Someone described the result as entire, which the team needed to verify. The team decided to escape only after checking the risks. The latest report called the result generous, and Lin asked why.

他們檢查疾病，等待更清楚的答案。 有人形容結果整條、全部，團隊仍需要確認。 團隊決定先確認風險，再嘗試逃離、避開。 最新報告稱結果慷慨的，琳追問原因。

They made a small test before changing the larger plan. Lin explained each step aloud while Hao watched for a mistake. A second person kept the old record so that the result could be compared later. At first the difference looked too small to matter. Then Mina, Sora, and Tao noticed that one part of the plan would affect people who were not in the room. The group paused and changed the order of its work. They did not pretend a single success was enough. A good test was something another person could repeat, with clear limits and a way to stop safely.

他們先進行小型測試，再考慮改動大計畫。琳逐步大聲說明操作，皓則注意可能的錯誤。另一個人保留舊紀錄，方便日後比較。起初差異小得像是不重要，後來米娜、空與陶注意到其中一步會影響當時不在場的人。團隊停下來調整工作順序，沒有假裝一次成功就足夠。真正好的測試，是別人也能重複操作、限制清楚，而且知道如何安全停止的測試。

Another detail changed the mood of the group. Tao moved the rain sheet away from the cooking area to keep ash and dirt out of the water. The others had a chance to ask questions before the decision became final. A shared choice was not always easy, but it was easier to defend when everyone knew its cost.

另一個細節改變了團隊氣氛。陶把接雨水的布移離炊事區，避免灰塵落進水裡。大家在決定正式生效前仍有提問的機會。共同選擇不一定容易，但當所有人都知道代價時，就更能為它負責。

The shelter did not look like a home, but each person knew where to put wet shoes and where to keep dry cloth. Lin asked them not to move the medical bag without telling the others. During the evening check, five voices answered from five different corners. That small habit turned fear into a task they could finish together.

避難棚不像家，但每個人都知道濕鞋要放哪裡、乾布要收在哪裡。琳請大家不要未告知就移動醫藥袋。傍晚清點時，五個角落傳來五個人的應答。這個小習慣把恐懼變成了可以共同完成的工作。

Hao asked how the hamburgers might change their plans. There was still a chance to imagine, if the next test worked. The group discussed the material openly rather than allow a rumor to decide. The network became one more detail to check before the group moved on.

皓詢問漢堡可能如何改變大家的計畫。 如果下一次測試成功，仍然有機會想像。 團隊公開討論材料，不讓傳聞替大家做決定。 在團隊繼續之前，分散的標記網路成了需要查核的另一項細節。

Later, Lin and Hao spoke away from the others. Lin admitted to being afraid of making a decision that could not be repaired. Hao said that fear was useful when it encouraged care, but dangerous when it stopped people from listening. Neither offered an easy promise. They returned to the group and explained what had changed, inviting questions from every person affected. Their friendship did not solve the problem, but it made disagreement less frightening. They could still disagree the next morning, and they could still work on the same problem. That seemed more valuable than winning an argument in front of an audience.

稍後，琳與皓離開人群談話。琳坦承自己害怕做出無法挽回的決定。皓說，害怕若能提醒人謹慎就有用；若讓人不肯聆聽就會危險。兩人沒有輕易保證一切順利，而是回到團隊說明改動，邀請所有受影響的人提問。友誼本身無法解決問題，卻讓意見不同不再那麼可怕。隔天他們仍可能意見不同，也仍能為同一問題努力。這似乎比在眾人面前贏得爭論更有價值。

Lin made a record of the papaya, so everyone could compare it later. Hao asked how the population might change their plans. They examined the score and waited for a clearer answer. The group discussed the sense openly rather than allow a rumor to decide.

琳記下了木瓜，讓所有人日後能夠比較。 皓詢問人口可能如何改變大家的計畫。 他們檢查分數，等待更清楚的答案。 團隊公開討論有道理，不讓傳聞替大家做決定。

By evening the team had completed one useful piece of work, not the whole journey. Lin checked the rescue map one last time and wrote both the result and the remaining doubt. Hao read the entry and asked whether a newcomer could understand it without meeting the people who wrote it. The answer was not quite yes, so they added the missing detail. Their shared goal remained to reach a safe rescue point without risking anyone's life. Tomorrow would bring a different test, and the group could not know its answer in advance. For tonight, however, everyone knew exactly what had been done, what had not been done, and who had agreed to the next step.

傍晚時，團隊完成了一項有用的工作，卻還沒走完整段旅程。琳最後檢查救援地圖，同時寫下結果與尚未解決的疑問。皓讀完後問：如果新來的人沒見過作者，能看懂嗎？答案還不完全是肯定的，於是大家補上缺少的細節。他們共同的目標仍是在不讓任何人冒險的前提下抵達安全的救援點。明天會有不同的考驗，誰也不能提前知道答案。不過今晚每個人都清楚：哪些事已完成、哪些尚未完成，以及下一步由誰共同同意。

The target became one more detail to check before the group moved on. Lin asked what Therefore meant in this part of the instruction. Hao asked how the wood might change their plans. They did not treat the description worth as evidence by itself.

在團隊繼續之前，目標成了需要查核的另一項細節。 琳詢問這段指示裡的「因此」究竟是什麼意思。 皓詢問木材可能如何改變大家的計畫。 他們沒有只憑「值得」這種描述就當成證據。
