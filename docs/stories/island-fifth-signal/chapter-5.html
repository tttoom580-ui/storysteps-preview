Before acting, Hao asked which facts came from direct observation. The team drew two columns: what they knew and what they had only heard. They wanted the next person to understand the evidence, not to inherit an untested guess.

行動之前，皓問大家哪些事實來自直接觀察。團隊畫出兩欄：一欄是確知的事，一欄是聽來的話。琳把找不到來源的說法移到第二欄。沒有人把這項更正當成失敗；大家希望後來的人能理解證據，而不是繼承未經驗證的猜測。

They made a small test before changing the larger plan. Lin explained each step aloud while Hao watched for a mistake. A second person kept the old record so that the result could be compared later. 他們先進行小型測試，再考慮改動大計畫。琳逐步大聲說明操作，皓則注意可能的錯誤。另一個人保留舊紀錄，方便日後比較。起初差異小得像是不重要，後來米娜、空與陶注意到其中一步會影響當時不在場的人。團隊停下來調整工作順序，沒有假裝一次成功就足夠。真正好的測試，是別人也能重複操作、限制清楚，而且知道如何安全停止的測試。

Later, Lin and Hao spoke away from the others. Lin admitted to being afraid of making a decision that could not be repaired. Hao said that fear was useful when it encouraged care, but dangerous when it stopped people from listening. Neither offered an easy promise. They returned to the group and explained what had changed, inviting questions from every person affected. Their friendship did not solve the problem, but it made disagreement less frightening. They could still disagree the next morning, and they could still work on the same problem. That seemed more valuable than winning an argument in front of an audience.
