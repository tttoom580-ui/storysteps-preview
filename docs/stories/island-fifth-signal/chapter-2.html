The morning began quietly in the small island, although Lin knew that quiet did not mean safe. It was the early days, and the group had already learned that a confident guess could be worse than an honest question. Lin reviewed the rescue map and invited Hao and Mina, Sora, and Tao to look at the same page. Nobody had to agree before hearing the whole problem. The students spread their bags on dry sand and count water, food, medicine, rope, and working batteries. They agree not to hide private supplies. Their task suddenly seemed larger than it had a moment before. Lin made one important choice: the next step would be written down where everyone could check it.

在這座小島，這個早晨起初很安靜，但琳知道安靜不代表安全。現在已是最初幾天，大家明白，自信的猜測有時比誠實提問更危險。琳翻閱救援地圖，邀請皓與米娜、空與陶一起查看相同頁面。所有人都可以先聽完整個問題，再決定是否同意。當天的第一份紀錄寫道：學生把背包攤在乾沙上，清點水、食物、藥品、繩索與還能使用的電池，並約定不隱瞞個人攜帶的物資。那一刻，眼前任務突然顯得比剛才更加龐大。琳做出重要決定：下一步要公開記下，讓每個人都能核對。

Hao asked how the angle might change their plans. There was still a chance to appreciate, if the next test worked. They agreed not to avoid until the facts were clearer. The barber became one more detail to check before the group moved on.

皓詢問角度可能如何改變大家的計畫。 如果下一次測試成功，仍然有機會感謝、欣賞。 大家同意等事實更清楚後才避免。 在團隊繼續之前，理髮師成了需要查核的另一項細節。

Before acting, Hao asked which facts came from direct observation. The team drew two columns: what they knew and what they had only heard. Lin moved a doubtful claim into the second column because its source was missing. Nobody called the correction a defeat. They wanted the next person to understand the evidence, not to inherit an untested guess.

行動之前，皓問大家哪些事實來自直接觀察。團隊畫出兩欄：一欄是確知的事，一欄是聽來的話。琳把找不到來源的說法移到第二欄。沒有人把這項更正當成失敗；大家希望後來的人能理解證據，而不是繼承未經驗證的猜測。

Lin made a record of the board, so everyone could compare it later. Hao asked how the deer might change their plans. They examined the dove and waited for a clearer answer. The group discussed the edition openly rather than allow a rumor to decide.

琳記下了木板，讓所有人日後能夠比較。 皓詢問鹿可能如何改變大家的計畫。 他們檢查鴿子，等待更清楚的答案。 團隊公開討論版本，不讓傳聞替大家做決定。

They made a small test before changing the larger plan. Lin explained each step aloud while Hao watched for a mistake. A second person kept the old record so that the result could be compared later. At first the difference looked too small to matter. Then Mina, Sora, and Tao noticed that one part of the plan would affect people who were not in the room. The group paused and changed the order of its work. They did not pretend a single success was enough. A good test was something another person could repeat, with clear limits and a way to stop safely.

他們先進行小型測試，再考慮改動大計畫。琳逐步大聲說明操作，皓則注意可能的錯誤。另一個人保留舊紀錄，方便日後比較。起初差異小得像是不重要，後來米娜、空與陶注意到其中一步會影響當時不在場的人。團隊停下來調整工作順序，沒有假裝一次成功就足夠。真正好的測試，是別人也能重複操作、限制清楚，而且知道如何安全停止的測試。

Another detail changed the mood of the group. Sora admitted that she had carried an extra bottle, and put it on the public supply sheet. The others had a chance to ask questions before the decision became final. A shared choice was not always easy, but it was easier to defend when everyone knew its cost.

另一個細節改變了團隊氣氛。空承認自己另帶了一瓶水，並把它登記到公共物資表上。大家在決定正式生效前仍有提問的機會。共同選擇不一定容易，但當所有人都知道代價時，就更能為它負責。

Every container had a mark showing how much water it held. Mina checked the marks at the same hour each day, even when she felt too tired to move far. The others learned not to laugh at a small measure. On an island, a little water could decide whether somebody had enough strength to walk the next morning.

每個容器都有標線，表示裡面裝了多少水。即使米娜累得走不遠，仍在每天同一時間核對標線。其他人學會不嘲笑小小的測量。在島上，一點水就可能決定某人隔天早上是否還有力氣行走。

The team decided to existed only after checking the risks. The latest report called the result huge, and Lin asked why. Hao asked how the lantern might change their plans. They examined the minority and waited for a clearer answer.

團隊決定先確認風險，再嘗試存在。 最新報告稱結果巨大的，琳追問原因。 皓詢問提燈可能如何改變大家的計畫。 他們檢查少數族群，等待更清楚的答案。

Later, Lin and Hao spoke away from the others. Lin admitted to being afraid of making a decision that could not be repaired. Hao said that fear was useful when it encouraged care, but dangerous when it stopped people from listening. Neither offered an easy promise. They returned to the group and explained what had changed, inviting questions from every person affected. Their friendship did not solve the problem, but it made disagreement less frightening. They could still disagree the next morning, and they could still work on the same problem. That seemed more valuable than winning an argument in front of an audience.

稍後，琳與皓離開人群談話。琳坦承自己害怕做出無法挽回的決定。皓說，害怕若能提醒人謹慎就有用；若讓人不肯聆聽就會危險。兩人沒有輕易保證一切順利，而是回到團隊說明改動，邀請所有受影響的人提問。友誼本身無法解決問題，卻讓意見不同不再那麼可怕。隔天他們仍可能意見不同，也仍能為同一問題努力。這似乎比在眾人面前贏得爭論更有價值。

The group discussed the organization openly rather than allow a rumor to decide. At first the answer looked painful, but the team checked again. The latest report called the result particular, and Lin asked why. Hao asked how the president might change their plans.

團隊公開討論組織，不讓傳聞替大家做決定。 答案起初看來痛苦的，但團隊再次核對。 最新報告稱結果特定的，琳追問原因。 皓詢問會長可能如何改變大家的計畫。

By evening the team had completed one useful piece of work, not the whole journey. Lin checked the rescue map one last time and wrote both the result and the remaining doubt. Hao read the entry and asked whether a newcomer could understand it without meeting the people who wrote it. The answer was not quite yes, so they added the missing detail. Their shared goal remained to reach a safe rescue point without risking anyone's life. Tomorrow would bring a different test, and the group could not know its answer in advance. For tonight, however, everyone knew exactly what had been done, what had not been done, and who had agreed to the next step.

傍晚時，團隊完成了一項有用的工作，卻還沒走完整段旅程。琳最後檢查救援地圖，同時寫下結果與尚未解決的疑問。皓讀完後問：如果新來的人沒見過作者，能看懂嗎？答案還不完全是肯定的，於是大家補上缺少的細節。他們共同的目標仍是在不讓任何人冒險的前提下抵達安全的救援點。明天會有不同的考驗，誰也不能提前知道答案。不過今晚每個人都清楚：哪些事已完成、哪些尚未完成，以及下一步由誰共同同意。

They did not treat the description sour as evidence by itself. The group discussed the spot openly rather than allow a rumor to decide. The textbook became one more detail to check before the group moved on. Lin made a record of the truth, so everyone could compare it later.

他們沒有只憑「酸澀的」這種描述就當成證據。 團隊公開討論位置、斑點，不讓傳聞替大家做決定。 在團隊繼續之前，教科書成了需要查核的另一項細節。 琳記下了真相，讓所有人日後能夠比較。
