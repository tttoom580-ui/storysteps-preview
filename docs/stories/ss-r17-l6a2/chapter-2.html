水底的規則

A Rule Written Under Water

無限副本的最後一名管理員 · 第 2 / 25 章 · 5000–6000 · 英中對照 · 校訂中

用互動版查詞、朗讀與收藏

It began not with a battle but with an ordinary interruption in the linked trial worlds. In the flooded timetable hall, Yara discovers that exits accept witnesses rather than combat victories. At first, no one considered the interruption important enough to preserve. Yara thought otherwise. A forgotten detail could become a map, and a map could be used to control where frightened people were permitted to go. An aboriginal community upstream requested access to the substation's emergency warning system. Reports of earlier power failures abound in the neighborhood's handwritten complaint register. Yara asked the technicians to align the two timestamps before comparing the footage. The substation's altitude made it safe from river water but difficult for older residents to reach.

事情不是從戰鬥開始，而是從相連的試煉世界裡一次平凡的中斷開始。在淹水的時刻表大廳，雅拉發現出口認可的是證人，而不是戰鬥勝利。起初沒人認為這件事值得留下紀錄，但雅拉想法不同。遭忽略的細節可能變成地圖，而地圖也可能被用來控制驚恐的人可以去哪裡。 上游原住民族社群要求能接收到變電站的緊急警報。 社區手寫申訴簿裡充滿過去停電的通報。 雅拉請技師先對齊兩組時間戳，再比對影像。 變電站的海拔讓它不易淹水，卻使年長居民難以前往。

Nilo came back carrying a practical objection rather than an inspiring speech. An abandoned ticket proves the game recorded a child's testimony after the child supposedly died. They had both seen people harmed when an assumption hardened into policy. A signed report could be wrong, and an emotional memory could be right, but neither became proof merely by being repeated. They agreed to seek a measurement from an independent observer. Around the trial chamber, incidental testimony complicated the picture: The guide's annoyance was visible. “The capital still draws bridges where only wreckage remains,” she said, blunt as a hammer. A student described the dark pedestrian crossing where a cyclist had nearly struck him.

尼洛帶回的是實際的異議，而不是振奮人心的演說。一張遺棄車票證明，遊戲在一名孩子據稱死亡後仍記錄了他的證詞。他們都見過假設硬化成政策後如何傷人。有簽名的報告可能是錯的，強烈的記憶也可能是真的；兩者都不會因為反覆提起就變成證據。他們決定找獨立觀察員再測量一次。試煉室周圍還有一些看似偶然的證詞，使情況更加複雜： 嚮導的惱怒溢於言表。 「京城還在畫橋，這裡卻只剩殘骸，」她直言不諱。 學生描述漆黑的行人穿越道，說自己差點被自行車撞到。

“We can tell the truth without pretending we know everything,” Yara said. Nilo replied that a partial truth was difficult to defend against people who controlled the doors. She answered that a perfect story with no verifiable source was more dangerous. They divided the work: one would protect the witnesses, while the other checked the physical records and the sequence of events. No technician was allowed to discard the damaged seal before independent photographs were taken. The inspector recorded an unexpected electrical discharge near the damaged control panel. Their joint endeavor would be assessed through measurements, not a flattering press release.

「我們能說實話，不需要假裝知道一切。」雅拉說。尼洛回答，當對方掌控所有門口時，局部真相比較難捍衛。她則認為沒有可驗證來源的完美故事更危險。他們分配工作：一人保護證人，另一人核查實體紀錄和事件時序。 完成獨立拍照前，技師不得丟棄受損封條。 檢查員記錄損壞控制板附近一次意外放電。 雙方共同努力將以測量結果評估，而非以討好的新聞稿。

The argument moved from a private corridor into a formal room, where every statement would be copied. A senior official from the network overseer demanded certainty before granting access. Yara offered a less comfortable bargain: a limited preservation order now, followed by independent examination and a chance for every accused person to respond. That was enough to prevent an irreversible decision. Around the trial chamber, incidental testimony complicated the picture: The first hurdle was getting both companies to accept a technician chosen by workers. “Yet they announced he was discharged for desertion.” The princess maintained a careful posture while examining the seal; her anger could not improve the evidence. The team needed precision because a three-minute error could distort the entire timeline. A rigorous inspection revealed a damaged timestamp seal but an undamaged outer casing.

爭論從私下走廊進入正式會議室，每句話都會留下副本。網路監督者的一名高級官員要求先確定真相才准查資料。雅拉提出比較不舒服的交換：先給有期限的保全命令，再獨立檢查，也讓所有被指控者有回應機會。這足以擋下一個不可逆的決定。試煉室周圍還有一些看似偶然的證詞，使情況更加複雜： 第一道障礙是讓兩家公司接受工人推選的技師。 」雅拉檢查封印時努力保持鎮定，因為怒氣不能改善證據。 團隊必須精確，因為三分鐘誤差可能扭曲整段時間線。 嚴格檢查發現時間戳封條損壞，外殼卻完好。

Yara rescues the child's recorded voice and gains the first of three signatures required to disable a level. Even those who agreed with the choice could see its cost. Time was running out, supplies were not unlimited, and a mistake might travel far beyond this room. Nilo asked whether they should conceal the uncertainty until the crisis passed. Yara shook her head. People could not make free choices if the information reaching them had already been edited. At a wind-battered terrace, the party found an elderly widow repairing her roof. A workers' uprising was not the issue; they were requesting the safety rights already promised. A volunteer usher led witnesses through the side entrance to protect their privacy.

雅拉救回孩子留下的錄音，取得關閉關卡所需三個簽名中的第一個。即使同意這個選擇的人，也看得出代價。時間不多、資源有限，一次錯誤可能傳得很遠。尼洛問是否該先隱瞞不確定性，等危機過後再說。雅拉搖頭。若資訊早被剪裁，人們便無法作出自由的選擇。 在飽受狂風吹襲的梯田邊，隊伍找到一名修屋頂的年長寡婦。 這不是工人起義；他們只是要求已被承諾的安全權利。 志工引導員帶證人從側門進入，以保護隱私。

Later, under dim lights, Yara reread the original question. The dispute was larger now, but its center remained unchanged: who had the right to make an irreversible choice for someone else? Nilo stayed until the last copy was sealed. Neither spoke about victory. They knew the next door would open only because today’s evidence had been preserved and today’s mistakes had been admitted. The versatile backup unit could power either a clinic refrigerator or an emergency radio. The workers retained a safety veto over repairs that failed the written threshold. A widow asked the board to remember patients who depended on home oxygen machines.

晚些時候，在微弱燈光下，雅拉重讀最初的問題。爭端已經擴大，但核心沒變：誰有權替別人作出不可逆的選擇？尼洛留到最後一份副本封存。他們沒有談勝利，只知道下一扇門之所以能打開，是因為今天保住了證據，也承認了錯誤。 多功能備用裝置可供應診所冰箱或緊急無線電。 工人保有對不符合書面門檻之維修工作的安全否決權。 一名寡婦請董事會記住依賴家用氧氣機的病人。
