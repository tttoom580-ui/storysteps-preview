避難所的表決

The Shelter Vote

無限副本的最後一名管理員 · 第 10 / 25 章 · 5000–6000 · 英中對照 · 校訂中

用互動版查詞、朗讀與收藏

The morning had already acquired the look of an emergency before Yara reached the scene. Survivors discover the central shelter has room for everyone but only enough medicine for a month. An official wanted a clean story, with a beginning that could be announced and an ending that would require no questions. Yara had learned that reality rarely showed such courtesy. She made room for the people who had been ordered to wait outside. The supplier sought a concession that would grant it control over the canal for a decade. A cynical investor said patients were useful only when their stories moved the market. The old procurement rule treated silence as consent by default, which the new board rejected. An emergency fund should not deplete the clinic budget merely to improve quarterly profit.

在雅拉抵達現場前，早晨已經有了緊急事件的模樣。倖存者發現中央避難所容得下所有人，藥品卻只夠用一個月。官員想要一套簡潔的故事，開頭能宣布，結尾又不用回答問題。她知道現實很少如此客氣，於是請那些被命令等在門外的人進來說話。 供應商尋求特許權，想控制運河十年。 一位憤世嫉俗的投資人說，病人故事只有能推動市場時才有用。 舊採購規則預設沉默即同意，新董事會予以否決。 緊急基金不該只為改善季度利潤就耗盡診所預算。

Nilo examined the account twice, once as a friend and once as a witness who might have to defend every word. The overseer offers preferential access in exchange for silence about the erased registry. The difference between what they suspected and what they could establish mattered. They placed the new fact beside the previous chapter’s unanswered question and looked for a second source that had not been copied from the first. Around the trial chamber, incidental testimony complicated the picture: Soldiers handed out sweet buns, and the fragrance of crushed flowers covered the smell of oil along the river. Reporters were not allowed to intrude into confidential witness interviews. A former jockey now driving delivery vans recognized the unusual generator model.

尼洛把紀錄看了兩次：第一次以朋友身分，第二次則像一位必須為每個字負責的證人。監督者提出優先入內的條件，交換他們對遭刪登錄保持沉默。懷疑和能確立的事實必須分清楚。他們把新線索放在前一章未解的問題旁，尋找不曾從同一份資料抄出的第二來源。試煉室周圍還有一些看似偶然的證詞，使情況更加複雜： 士兵發放甜麵包，壓碎花朵的香氣蓋住河邊油味。 記者不得闖入保密的證人訪談。 一名曾當賽馬騎師的廂車司機認出了不尋常的發電機型號。

A crowd gathered around the disputed notice. “You have to choose a side,” someone shouted. Yara answered that she had chosen one already: the side of a claim that survived questions. Nilo did not smile, but moved the most vulnerable bystanders away from the growing argument. The pair could disagree privately without turning their disagreement into a public threat. The lawyer insisted on a literal reading of the signed annex rather than the chairman's memories. Worker morale improved after the company published the failed-test results without punishment. Investigators could prosecute fraud only after assembling reliable evidence of intent.

人群圍住有爭議的公告。「妳必須選邊站！」有人喊。雅拉回答自己早已選擇：站在經得起提問的主張那一邊。尼洛沒有微笑，只把最脆弱的旁觀者帶離爭執。他們可以私下不同意對方，卻不能把分歧變成公開威脅。 律師堅持按簽署附約的字面意思解讀，而非採信董事長的記憶。 公司公布失敗測試結果且未任意處罰後，工人士氣改善。 調查員只有蒐集可靠意圖證據後，才能起訴詐欺。

The institution was not a single villain. It contained frightened clerks, dedicated workers, ambitious supervisors, and people who had learned to hide mistakes. The network overseer wanted the incident classified as routine. Yara asked whose definition of routine was being used and whose losses were absent from the statistics. Nobody at the table could answer without consulting the sealed records. Around the trial chamber, incidental testimony complicated the picture: The maintenance inspector checked for radioactive materials because an old sensor carried a misleading label. The chancellor's lawyer made a sharp retort about national morale, claiming that questioning the trial generator would deplete public confidence. A young courier came forward with a screwdriver and a box of broken clock parts. Outside, an elderly vendor offered each departing witness a serving of warm soup.

這個機構不是單一惡棍。裡面有害怕的書記、盡責的工人、野心勃勃的主管，也有學會掩蓋錯誤的人。網路監督者想把事件歸類為例行事項。雅拉追問：是誰定義例行？又有誰的損失被統計漏掉？桌邊沒人能在不看封存紀錄的情況下回答。試煉室周圍還有一些看似偶然的證詞，使情況更加複雜： 維修檢查員因舊感測器標示誤導而檢查有無放射性物質。 宰相律師尖銳反駁，談起國民士氣，聲稱質疑試煉發電機會耗盡公眾信心。 一名年輕信差帶著螺絲起子和一箱壞時鐘零件上前。 庭外，一名年長攤販給離開的每位證人一份熱湯。

The group votes for open ration records and refuses to trade testimony for comfort. The decision did not repair everything. It altered one procedure, protected one person, or saved one piece of evidence; on a day when almost everyone demanded a miracle, that was worth recording. Yara insisted that the result and its limits appear together in the official minutes. Anything less would turn a modest gain into tomorrow’s misleading legend. The tower's splendor seemed irrelevant beside the dark apartments across the river. A spontaneous murmur passed through the audience. A witness explained that her stepfather had worked on the first version of the old substation.

眾人投票採取公開配給紀錄，拒絕拿證詞交換安逸。這個決定沒有修好一切，只改變一道程序、保護一個人，或留下了一份證據；當幾乎所有人都要求奇蹟時，這仍值得記錄。雅拉堅持把成果和限制一起寫入正式紀錄。否則小小的進展又會變成明天誤導人的傳說。 相較河對岸漆黑的公寓，大樓的華麗顯得毫無意義。 旁聽席傳出自發的低語。 一名證人解釋繼父曾參與舊變電站的初版工程。

The lights beyond the window did not offer comfort. They showed how many people were still waiting. Yara understood that the investigation might eventually expose her own earlier errors, and she resolved not to erase them. Nilo suggested a short rest before the next hearing. She agreed, because exhaustion could damage judgment as thoroughly as deliberate deception. The nervous clerk began to stutter before the judge invited her to speak slowly. The city agreed to subsidize emergency batteries for low-income patients during the trial. A synthetic seal material had been used in the forged authorization package.

窗外的燈光沒有帶來安慰，只照出還有多少人在等待。雅拉明白調查最終可能揭露自己過去的錯誤，決定不去抹除。尼洛建議下一場聽證前短暫休息。她同意，因為疲憊和蓄意欺騙一樣可能損害判斷。 緊張的書記開始口吃，法官請她慢慢說。 市府同意在試辦期間補助低收入病人的緊急電池。 偽造授權封套使用了合成封條材料。
