A Market of Borrowed Faces

無限副本的最後一名管理員 · 第 6 / 25 章 · 5000–6000 · 英中對照 · 校訂中

Yara arrived with the unpleasant certainty that the simplest explanation was probably the one that had been prepared for public consumption. The third level sells identities to players desperate to escape the pursuit of automated hunters. The place was full of people who needed a quick answer, yet the evidence still belonged to those whose lives it could change. Instead of making a dramatic accusation, Yara marked the hour, the witnesses, and the smallest observable detail in a paper notebook. “We allege deliberate treason,” he declared, though no test had occurred yet. The contractor had promised to beautify the riverside warehouse before its safety inspection. Its stone entrance was decorated with carved birds and a caption praising impartial justice. The contract dispute reached its climax when the two shipping weights appeared together.

雅拉抵達時，心裡很清楚：最簡單的解釋往往是事先準備好要給大眾聽的版本。第三關把身分賣給急著躲避自動獵人的玩家。現場的人都急需答案，但證據仍屬於那些生活可能被它改變的人。她沒有戲劇化地指控誰，而是先在紙本筆記上記下時刻、證人，以及最小卻可觀察的細節。 「我們指稱這是蓄意叛國，」他說，儘管測試根本還未進行。 承包商曾承諾在安檢前美化河岸倉庫。 石門飾有鳥類雕刻，下方的題字讚美公正司法。 當兩張出貨重量表一起出現時，契約爭議達到高潮。

There was a moment of quiet when Nilo set the new material on the table. Yara finds her own missing name printed on a contract signed years before the first advertised trial. What mattered was not how surprising the discovery sounded, but whether anyone could repeat the test. Yara copied the relevant names carefully. If one person later changed the account, the original would still be available for comparison. Around the trial chamber, incidental testimony complicated the picture: Yara asked the judge to extract the precise accusation from the speech and place it in the record. An extracurricular engineering club helped sort community reports without handling confidential files. Their teacher was hospitable, offering water and a shady bench to witnesses who had traveled far.

尼洛把新資料放上桌時，四周安靜了一瞬。雅拉發現自己失去的姓名印在一份契約上，簽署日期竟早於首場公開試煉好幾年。重要的不是發現有多驚人，而是別人能不能重做同一項測試。雅拉仔細抄下相關姓名；如果日後有人更改說法，原件仍可供比對。試煉室周圍還有一些看似偶然的證詞，使情況更加複雜： 雅拉要求法官從演說中抽取具體指控，寫入紀錄。 一個課外工程社團協助分類社區通報，但不碰機密檔案。 他們的老師十分好客，為遠道而來的證人準備飲水與陰涼長椅。

“Will you trust me?” Nilo asked. “With the evidence, yes,” Yara answered, “but not with a blank signature.” It was the sort of answer that could sound cold until someone noticed how carefully she had protected the other person’s right to disagree. Together they made a plan with a backup route, a deadline, and a record of who had accepted each responsibility. The retired mechanic stayed jolly even when the executives questioned the generator's strange name. Workers asked permission to mow the overgrown grass around the emergency exit. A supervisor with a white mustache signed the original warehouse inventory.

「妳信任我嗎？」尼洛問。「證據上可以，空白授權書不行。」雅拉回答。這聽起來很冷淡，直到人們注意到她也小心保護對方拒絕的權利。他們一起訂下有備用路線、期限，以及明確責任分工的計畫。 即使主管質疑發電機奇怪的名稱，退休技師仍很愉快。 工人請求修剪緊急出口旁長得過高的草。 一名留白鬍子的主管簽署了倉庫原始清冊。

A representative of the network overseer arrived with an offer to resolve the matter quietly. Quietly, in this case, meant without giving affected people access to the original. Yara declined. She knew an office could be useful and still be dangerously wrong. Her objection was narrow and specific: the proposed decision relied on a document whose origin had not yet been independently verified. Around the trial chamber, incidental testimony complicated the picture: A grandmother told him to stop nagging and gave him an ounce of sweet tea. The old cafeteria became an oasis for night workers exhausted by the investigation. One ounce of damaged insulation was sealed for laboratory comparison with the other samples. The missing battery modules might have been stored on the outskirts of the city.

網路監督者的代表前來，提議私下解決。所謂私下，就是不讓受影響的人看到原件。雅拉拒絕了。她知道一個機關可以有用，也可能犯下危險的錯。她的反對具體而有限：提議中的決定依賴一份來源還沒接受獨立驗證的文件。試煉室周圍還有一些看似偶然的證詞，使情況更加複雜： 一位祖母要他別再嘮叨，遞給他一小杯甜茶。 舊餐廳成為調查期間疲憊夜班工人的休息綠洲。 一盎司受損絕緣材料被封存，供實驗室與其他樣本比對。 失蹤的電池模組可能存放在城市郊區。

She buys no identity and instead copies the seller's ledger where everyone can see it. A few witnesses thanked Yara; others accused her of doing too little. She accepted both reactions without offering a promise she could not keep. Nilo wrote down the complaints, including the ones directed at their own conduct. They were not building a monument to themselves. They were building a process that would still function after they left. A visiting parliament observer requested that the public hearing remain open to workers. The nobleman's methods were ruthless but often lawful on paper: he delayed funding, demanded impossible permits, and threatened public employees with dismissal. A local sculptor offered a warehouse sketch that showed the original side entrance.

她沒有購買身分，而是當眾抄下商人的帳本。幾名證人向雅拉道謝，另一些人指責她做得不夠。她接受兩種反應，不許下無法兌現的承諾。尼洛寫下抱怨，包括針對他們自己行為的部分。他們不是為自己立紀念碑，而是建立自己離開後仍能運作的程序。 一名國會觀察員要求公開聽證繼續向工人開放。 這名貴族的方法冷酷，但在紙面上常常合法：延後經費、索取不可能取得的許可，並以解雇威脅公務員。 一名在地雕塑家提供倉庫素描，顯示原有側門。

By nightfall the newest report was signed by observers who were free to disagree. Yara stood beside Nilo and looked at the distance between them and a lawful exit that preserves the survivors and their memories. Progress felt less like triumph than like leaving a reliable mark on a difficult road. Someone would challenge that mark tomorrow. This time, there would be a public record with which to answer. The repair team preferred simplicity in emergency instructions over impressive technical language. Two simultaneous transfers made it difficult to trace the original emergency payment. The company's shares entered a slump after the investigation became public.

夜幕降臨前，新的報告由可以提出異議的觀察者簽署。雅拉站在尼洛身旁，看著他們與保護倖存者和記憶的合法出口之間的距離。進展不像勝利，比較像在艱難路上留下可靠的記號。明天有人會質疑，而這一次將有公開紀錄可以回答。 維修團隊偏好簡單的緊急指示，而非炫目的技術詞。 兩筆同時進行的轉帳使原始緊急款項難以追蹤。 調查公開後，公司的股價陷入低迷。
