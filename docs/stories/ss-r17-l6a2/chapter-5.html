第一道真正關閉的門

The First Closed Door

無限副本的最後一名管理員 · 第 5 / 25 章 · 5000–6000 · 英中對照 · 校訂中

用互動版查詞、朗讀與收藏

It began not with a battle but with an ordinary interruption in the linked trial worlds. The team reaches a control room that can end the orchard level but not release its prisoners alone. At first, no one considered the interruption important enough to preserve. Yara thought otherwise. A forgotten detail could become a map, and a map could be used to control where frightened people were permitted to go. The company academy offered a practical course on emergency power rather than executive slogans. A false accusation against the warehouse team was withdrawn after the access records were checked. A banquet was taking place across the courtyard, where donors praised the war effort. A relief grant originally intended for fertilizer purchases had been redirected without explanation.

事情不是從戰鬥開始，而是從相連的試煉世界裡一次平凡的中斷開始。隊伍抵達控制室，能結束果園關卡，卻無法獨自放出受困者。起初沒人認為這件事值得留下紀錄，但雅拉想法不同。遭忽略的細節可能變成地圖，而地圖也可能被用來控制驚恐的人可以去哪裡。 公司學院開設緊急供電實務課，而非宣傳主管口號。 查核門禁紀錄後，對倉庫團隊的不實指控遭撤回。 庭院對面正在舉行宴會，捐助者讚美戰爭。 原訂購買肥料的救助補助遭不明轉用。

Nilo came back carrying a practical objection rather than an inspiring speech. A second signature belongs to the game's oldest janitor, a man administrators have treated as scenery. They had both seen people harmed when an assumption hardened into policy. A signed report could be wrong, and an emotional memory could be right, but neither became proof merely by being repeated. They agreed to seek a measurement from an independent observer. Around the trial chamber, incidental testimony complicated the picture: The riverside fishery requested a dedicated backup line to protect its refrigerated catch. The inspection revealed a small fracture in the connector supporting the temporary generator. Technicians learned to harness surplus power without bypassing the required safety checks.

尼洛帶回的是實際的異議，而不是振奮人心的演說。第二個簽名屬於遊戲裡最老的清潔員，管理層一直把他當成背景裝飾。他們都見過假設硬化成政策後如何傷人。有簽名的報告可能是錯的，強烈的記憶也可能是真的；兩者都不會因為反覆提起就變成證據。他們決定找獨立觀察員再測量一次。試煉室周圍還有一些看似偶然的證詞，使情況更加複雜： 河岸漁業要求專用備電線路，以保護冷藏漁獲。 檢查發現支撐臨時發電機的接頭有一道小裂縫。 技師學習利用剩餘電力，不繞過必要安全檢查。

“We can tell the truth without pretending we know everything,” Yara said. Nilo replied that a partial truth was difficult to defend against people who controlled the doors. She answered that a perfect story with no verifiable source was more dangerous. They divided the work: one would protect the witnesses, while the other checked the physical records and the sequence of events. Each operator wore headphones to hear control-room instructions over the storm warnings. The clinic reviewed its oxygen intake records to calculate emergency battery demand. In the courtyard, a troupe rehearsed a ceremonial dance called the Maiden's Promise, though its lyrics had been rewritten to glorify the war.

「我們能說實話，不需要假裝知道一切。」雅拉說。尼洛回答，當對方掌控所有門口時，局部真相比較難捍衛。她則認為沒有可驗證來源的完美故事更危險。他們分配工作：一人保護證人，另一人核查實體紀錄和事件時序。 每位操作員戴耳機，以在風暴警報聲中聽清控制室指示。 診所檢視氧氣攝入紀錄，計算緊急電池需求。 庭院裡，一個劇團排練名為〈少女的承諾〉的儀式舞蹈，但歌詞早被改成頌揚戰爭。

The argument moved from a private corridor into a formal room, where every statement would be copied. A senior official from the network overseer demanded certainty before granting access. Yara offered a less comfortable bargain: a limited preservation order now, followed by independent examination and a chance for every accused person to respond. That was enough to prevent an irreversible decision. Around the trial chamber, incidental testimony complicated the picture: An offshore contractor had received the suspicious payment three weeks before the blackout. Now she allowed them to enter a sealed lecture hall, but only as prospective witnesses, not celebrated heroes. A resident asked for a referendum on the long-term public ownership of the canal land. Yara proposed to refine the test schedule instead of rushing a ceremonial reopening.

爭論從私下走廊進入正式會議室，每句話都會留下副本。網路監督者的一名高級官員要求先確定真相才准查資料。雅拉提出比較不舒服的交換：先給有期限的保全命令，再獨立檢查，也讓所有被指控者有回應機會。這足以擋下一個不可逆的決定。試煉室周圍還有一些看似偶然的證詞，使情況更加複雜： 一家離岸承包商在停電前三週收到可疑款項。 如今她允許他們進入封閉講堂，但只能以潛在證人身分入內，不能當成受歡迎的英雄。 一名居民要求針對運河土地的長期公共所有權舉辦公投。 雅拉建議改進測試時程，而非趕著辦象徵性重啟。

With his permission, Yara closes the level and opens a real route to the next shelter. Even those who agreed with the choice could see its cost. Time was running out, supplies were not unlimited, and a mistake might travel far beyond this room. Nilo asked whether they should conceal the uncertainty until the crisis passed. Yara shook her head. People could not make free choices if the information reaching them had already been edited. Several students quietly began to rejoice; others feared that the academy would be closed for allowing dissent. Some shareholders began to resent the safety veto, calling it an obstacle to profit. The operating room remained serene after its generator switched smoothly to backup power.

取得他的同意後，雅拉關閉關卡，開出通往下一座避難所的真實道路。即使同意這個選擇的人，也看得出代價。時間不多、資源有限，一次錯誤可能傳得很遠。尼洛問是否該先隱瞞不確定性，等危機過後再說。雅拉搖頭。若資訊早被剪裁，人們便無法作出自由的選擇。 幾名學生悄悄歡欣，其他人則害怕學院因容許異議而被關閉。 部分股東開始怨恨安全否決權，稱它阻礙獲利。 手術室的發電機順利切換備電後，室內依舊平靜。

Later, under dim lights, Yara reread the original question. The dispute was larger now, but its center remained unchanged: who had the right to make an irreversible choice for someone else? Nilo stayed until the last copy was sealed. Neither spoke about victory. They knew the next door would open only because today’s evidence had been preserved and today’s mistakes had been admitted. A neighborhood pastor gave a short sermon about caring for people before property. The board refused to shuffle expenses between accounts to make the trial look cheaper. A tenant demanded proof that her rent would not rise because of the emergency repair.

晚些時候，在微弱燈光下，雅拉重讀最初的問題。爭端已經擴大，但核心沒變：誰有權替別人作出不可逆的選擇？尼洛留到最後一份副本封存。他們沒有談勝利，只知道下一扇門之所以能打開，是因為今天保住了證據，也承認了錯誤。 社區牧師簡短講道，提醒人們應先照顧人，再談財產。 董事會拒絕在帳戶間調換支出，營造試辦更便宜的假象。 一名房客要求保證不會因緊急維修而漲租。
