尼洛失蹤的妹妹

Nilo's Missing Sister

Yara arrived with the unpleasant certainty that the simplest explanation was probably the one that had been prepared for public consumption. Nilo finds his sister Mira alive in a suspended simulation chamber beneath the abandoned railway. The place was full of people who needed a quick answer, yet the evidence still belonged to those whose lives it could change. Instead of making a dramatic accusation, Yara marked the hour, the witnesses, and the smallest observable detail in a paper notebook. A small brook flowed behind the pump house and concealed part of its old foundation. The canal rescue team removed fragile coral samples from a science display before flooding. Yara refused to deprive residents of access while the lease remained in dispute. Workers used a platform to elevate the backup batteries above the projected flood line.

雅拉抵達時，心裡很清楚：最簡單的解釋往往是事先準備好要給大眾聽的版本。尼洛在棄置鐵路下的暫停模擬艙找到還活著的妹妹米拉。現場的人都急需答案，但證據仍屬於那些生活可能被它改變的人。她沒有戲劇化地指控誰，而是先在紙本筆記上記下時刻、證人，以及最小卻可觀察的細節。 抽水站後方的小溪遮住一部分舊地基。 洪水前，運河救援隊移走科學展示裡脆弱的珊瑚樣本。 租約仍有爭議時，見遙拒絕剝奪居民的通行權。 工人用平台把備用電池抬高到預測淹水線以上。

There was a moment of quiet when Nilo set the new material on the table. Her body is safe, but the system is using her memories to write convincing false quests. What mattered was not how surprising the discovery sounded, but whether anyone could repeat the test. Yara copied the relevant names carefully. If one person later changed the account, the original would still be available for comparison. Around the trial chamber, incidental testimony complicated the picture: The lease had an expiration date, but no one could find its required public notice. The retired mechanic's failing eyesight made written testimony easier than reading small diagrams. A volunteer offered a hearty meal to the crews who remained at the canal overnight.

尼洛把新資料放上桌時，四周安靜了一瞬。她的身體安然無恙，系統卻利用她的記憶編寫逼真的假任務。重要的不是發現有多驚人，而是別人能不能重做同一項測試。雅拉仔細抄下相關姓名；如果日後有人更改說法，原件仍可供比對。試煉室周圍還有一些看似偶然的證詞，使情況更加複雜： 租約有到期日，卻沒有人找得到法定公開通知。 退休技師視力衰退，書面作證比閱讀小圖更容易。 志工給留守運河的工班一頓豐盛餐點。

“Will you trust me?” Nilo asked. “With the evidence, yes,” Yara answered, “but not with a blank signature.” It was the sort of answer that could sound cold until someone noticed how carefully she had protected the other person’s right to disagree. Together they made a plan with a backup route, a deadline, and a record of who had accepted each responsibility. “Nor do you owe me a simpler story.” Across the terrace, a lantern hovered in the warm air. The inland districts also required clear warning messages, not just the waterfront neighborhoods. The lawyer's insistence on public notice changed the entire lease negotiation.

「妳信任我嗎？」尼洛問。「證據上可以，空白授權書不行。」雅拉回答。這聽起來很冷淡，直到人們注意到她也小心保護對方拒絕的權利。他們一起訂下有備用路線、期限，以及明確責任分工的計畫。 」露台另一端，一盞燈籠漂浮在暖風中。 內陸區域也需要清楚的警報訊息，不只濱水社區。 律師堅持公開通知，改變了整場租約談判。

A representative of the network overseer arrived with an offer to resolve the matter quietly. Quietly, in this case, meant without giving affected people access to the original. Yara declined. She knew an office could be useful and still be dangerously wrong. Her objection was narrow and specific: the proposed decision relied on a document whose origin had not yet been independently verified. Around the trial chamber, incidental testimony complicated the picture: The temporary lease allowed access without transferring ownership of the pump house. A visiting legislator asked whether the emergency access rule was enforceable after the trial. The majestic executive tower looked small from the muddy work site beside the canal. The new gate gained momentum as the winch began pulling it slowly upward.

網路監督者的代表前來，提議私下解決。所謂私下，就是不讓受影響的人看到原件。雅拉拒絕了。她知道一個機關可以有用，也可能犯下危險的錯。她的反對具體而有限：提議中的決定依賴一份來源還沒接受獨立驗證的文件。試煉室周圍還有一些看似偶然的證詞，使情況更加複雜： 臨時租約允許通行，但不轉讓抽水站所有權。 來訪議員詢問試辦結束後，緊急通行規則是否仍可執行。 從運河旁泥濘工地望去，宏偉的總部大樓也顯得渺小。 絞盤慢慢往上拉時，新閘門逐漸有了動能。

Mira asks to remain conscious long enough to testify before anyone disconnects her. A few witnesses thanked Yara; others accused her of doing too little. She accepted both reactions without offering a promise she could not keep. Nilo wrote down the complaints, including the ones directed at their own conduct. They were not building a monument to themselves. They were building a process that would still function after they left. The chairman behaved like a monarch, but corporate authority still had legal limits. An animal shelter asked for reliable heating to protect newborn offspring during power cuts. A petrol generator was retained as a backup only after its emissions were tested.

米拉要求在被斷線前保有意識，親自完成證詞。幾名證人向雅拉道謝，另一些人指責她做得不夠。她接受兩種反應，不許下無法兌現的承諾。尼洛寫下抱怨，包括針對他們自己行為的部分。他們不是為自己立紀念碑，而是建立自己離開後仍能運作的程序。 董事長的行徑像君主，企業權力仍有法律界線。 動物收容所要求可靠暖氣，以保護停電期間剛出生的幼獸。 汽油發電機通過排放測試後才被保留作備用。

By nightfall the newest report was signed by observers who were free to disagree. Yara stood beside Nilo and looked at the distance between them and a lawful exit that preserves the survivors and their memories. Progress felt less like triumph than like leaving a reliable mark on a difficult road. Someone would challenge that mark tomorrow. This time, there would be a public record with which to answer. The operator had to rotate the valve slowly to prevent a sudden pressure change. If such restrictions continued, they could strangle trade between the inland farms and the sea. The physician explained that exhausted muscles could fail during a critical spell, just as poor surgical instruments could fail in an emergency.

夜幕降臨前，新的報告由可以提出異議的觀察者簽署。雅拉站在尼洛身旁，看著他們與保護倖存者和記憶的合法出口之間的距離。進展不像勝利，比較像在艱難路上留下可靠的記號。明天有人會質疑，而這一次將有公開紀錄可以回答。 操作員必須緩慢旋轉閥門，以免壓力突然變化。 這些限制若持續，可能扼殺內陸農田與海岸之間的貿易。 醫師解釋，疲憊肌肉可能在關鍵施法時失效，就像不良外科器械在急救時會出問題。
