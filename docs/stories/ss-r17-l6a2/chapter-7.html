沒有鑰匙的管理員

An Administrator Without a Key

無限副本的最後一名管理員 · 第 7 / 25 章 · 5000–6000 · 英中對照 · 校訂中

The morning had already acquired the look of an emergency before Yara reached the scene. A guard recognizes Yara's badge yet insists her administrator key was revoked for refusing an order. An official wanted a clean story, with a beginning that could be announced and an ending that would require no questions. Yara had learned that reality rarely showed such courtesy. She made room for the people who had been ordered to wait outside. The trust owned one acre of land beside the canal, but ownership did not erase public access. A wealthy bachelor on the board objected to being excluded from the safety committee. Employees organized a charity boxing exhibition to support families hurt by the outage. The disciplinary rules required an independent hearing before any worker could be fired.

在雅拉抵達現場前，早晨已經有了緊急事件的模樣。守衛認得雅拉的徽章，卻堅稱她因拒絕命令而被撤銷管理員鑰匙。官員想要一套簡潔的故事，開頭能宣布，結尾又不用回答問題。她知道現實很少如此客氣，於是請那些被命令等在門外的人進來說話。 信託持有運河旁一英畝土地，但所有權不會抹去公共通行權。 董事會上一位富有的單身漢反對自己被排除在安全委員會外。 員工舉辦慈善拳擊表演，支援停電受影響的家庭。 紀律規則要求解雇任何工人前先有獨立聽證。

Nilo examined the account twice, once as a friend and once as a witness who might have to defend every word. An old audit records that she opposed deleting children from the survivor counts. The difference between what they suspected and what they could establish mattered. They placed the new fact beside the previous chapter’s unanswered question and looked for a second source that had not been copied from the first. Around the trial chamber, incidental testimony complicated the picture: An emigrant engineer explained why the old contract translation was unclear to new workers. The emperor tried to equate dissent with treason. His expression did not falter, but the room seemed to grow colder.

尼洛把紀錄看了兩次：第一次以朋友身分，第二次則像一位必須為每個字負責的證人。一份舊稽核記載她曾反對從倖存者統計中刪去孩子。懷疑和能確立的事實必須分清楚。他們把新線索放在前一章未解的問題旁，尋找不曾從同一份資料抄出的第二來源。試煉室周圍還有一些看似偶然的證詞，使情況更加複雜： 一名移居海外的工程師解釋舊合約翻譯為何讓新工人難懂。 皇帝試圖把異議等同叛國。 他的表情沒有動搖，房內卻彷彿更冷了。

A crowd gathered around the disputed notice. “You have to choose a side,” someone shouted. Yara answered that she had chosen one already: the side of a claim that survived questions. Nilo did not smile, but moved the most vulnerable bystanders away from the growing argument. The pair could disagree privately without turning their disagreement into a public threat. The clinic requested backup power for its fertility treatment laboratory. Yara would not forsake her obligations merely to keep the board's favor. A gay employee asked whether the revised benefits would cover his husband equally.

人群圍住有爭議的公告。「妳必須選邊站！」有人喊。雅拉回答自己早已選擇：站在經得起提問的主張那一邊。尼洛沒有微笑，只把最脆弱的旁觀者帶離爭執。他們可以私下不同意對方，卻不能把分歧變成公開威脅。 診所要求為生育治療實驗室提供備用電力。 見遙不願為保住董事會好感而背棄自己的義務。 一名男同性戀員工詢問新福利是否同等保障他的丈夫。

The institution was not a single villain. It contained frightened clerks, dedicated workers, ambitious supervisors, and people who had learned to hide mistakes. The network overseer wanted the incident classified as routine. Yara asked whose definition of routine was being used and whose losses were absent from the statistics. Nobody at the table could answer without consulting the sealed records. Around the trial chamber, incidental testimony complicated the picture: The workplace gloom lifted slightly after the tribunal protected the workers' safety veto. At the intersection of two estate paths, the gloom of approaching rain made the palace garden seem smaller. The route had been damaged by a landslide years earlier and never fully repaired. The children needed warm food to nourish their bodies before another winter arrived.

這個機構不是單一惡棍。裡面有害怕的書記、盡責的工人、野心勃勃的主管，也有學會掩蓋錯誤的人。網路監督者想把事件歸類為例行事項。雅拉追問：是誰定義例行？又有誰的損失被統計漏掉？桌邊沒人能在不看封存紀錄的情況下回答。試煉室周圍還有一些看似偶然的證詞，使情況更加複雜： 法庭保護工人安全否決權後，工作場所的陰鬱稍稍散去。 在莊園兩條小徑交叉處，將至的雨天陰鬱讓宮廷花園顯得更小。 該路線多年前遭山崩破壞，從未完全修復。 孩子們需要溫暖食物滋養身體，才能迎接下一個冬天。

Yara accepts that she may have been part of the institution and asks for a hearing on her own decisions. The decision did not repair everything. It altered one procedure, protected one person, or saved one piece of evidence; on a day when almost everyone demanded a miracle, that was worth recording. Yara insisted that the result and its limits appear together in the official minutes. Anything less would turn a modest gain into tomorrow’s misleading legend. An orthodox interpretation of the old lease favored the trust, but the law required public notice. The prohibition on unsafe work could not be canceled through a private annex. A provisional court order preserved public access while the disputed lease was reviewed.

雅拉承認自己或許曾在制度中任職，要求對自己的決定也進行聽證。這個決定沒有修好一切，只改變一道程序、保護一個人，或留下了一份證據；當幾乎所有人都要求奇蹟時，這仍值得記錄。雅拉堅持把成果和限制一起寫入正式紀錄。否則小小的進展又會變成明天誤導人的傳說。 按照傳統解讀，舊租約有利信託，但法律仍要求公開告知。 禁止危險作業的規定不能透過私人附約取消。 暫時法院命令在爭議租約受審期間保住公共通行權。

The lights beyond the window did not offer comfort. They showed how many people were still waiting. Yara understood that the investigation might eventually expose her own earlier errors, and she resolved not to erase them. Nilo suggested a short rest before the next hearing. She agreed, because exhaustion could damage judgment as thoroughly as deliberate deception. The safety committee reached a unanimous decision to postpone the untested repair. He knew something about the cost of the family's vanity. A woodpecker struck a roadside pole while the technicians inspected the power lines.

窗外的燈光沒有帶來安慰，只照出還有多少人在等待。雅拉明白調查最終可能揭露自己過去的錯誤，決定不去抹除。尼洛建議下一場聽證前短暫休息。她同意，因為疲憊和蓄意欺騙一樣可能損害判斷。 安全委員會一致決定延後尚未測試的維修。 他深知家族虛榮的代價。 技師檢查電線時，一隻啄木鳥啄著路邊電線桿。
