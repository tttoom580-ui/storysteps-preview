The morning had already acquired the look of an emergency before Yara reached the scene. The trial opens in a flooded station where Yara wakes wearing an administrator badge with her name scratched away. An official wanted a clean story, with a beginning that could be announced and an ending that would require no questions. Yara had learned that reality rarely showed such courtesy. She made room for the people who had been ordered to wait outside. The legal clerk refused to abbreviate the witness's full name on a disputed record. At the river market, war refugees lined up for bread beneath an old anchor displayed outside the sailors' guild. The preservation rule was applicable even to the chairman who proposed the emergency vote. The public archive stored a copy of every switching order issued that week.

在雅拉抵達現場前，早晨已經有了緊急事件的模樣。試煉在淹水的車站展開，雅拉醒來時胸前別著管理員徽章，自己的名字卻遭刮除。官員想要一套簡潔的故事，開頭能宣布，結尾又不用回答問題。她知道現實很少如此客氣，於是請那些被命令等在門外的人進來說話。 法務書記拒絕在有爭議的紀錄上縮寫證人全名。 河岸市場裡，戰爭難民在水手公會門外的舊船錨下排隊領麵包。 證據保全規則即使對提出緊急表決的董事長也適用。 公共檔案室保存了那週每份切換命令的副本。

Nilo examined the account twice, once as a friend and once as a witness who might have to defend every word. Her companion Nilo finds that ordinary players have numbered wrists while hers displays an empty square. The difference between what they suspected and what they could establish mattered. They placed the new fact beside the previous chapter’s unanswered question and looked for a second source that had not been copied from the first. Around the trial chamber, incidental testimony complicated the picture: An elderly resident pressed the emergency card against her bosom as she waited. A worker reported that her bra had been taken with her uniform during the inspection. A contractor had offered a bribe for access to the missing hour of footage.

尼洛把紀錄看了兩次：第一次以朋友身分，第二次則像一位必須為每個字負責的證人。同伴尼洛發現普通玩家的手腕都有編號，只有她顯示一個空白方格。懷疑和能確立的事實必須分清楚。他們把新線索放在前一章未解的問題旁，尋找不曾從同一份資料抄出的第二來源。試煉室周圍還有一些看似偶然的證詞，使情況更加複雜： 一位年長居民等待時，把緊急卡片緊貼胸前。 一名女工反映，檢查時她的胸罩與制服一同被拿走。 一名承包商曾為取得失蹤那一小時的影像提出賄賂。

A crowd gathered around the disputed notice. “You have to choose a side,” someone shouted. Yara answered that she had chosen one already: the side of a claim that survived questions. Nilo did not smile, but moved the most vulnerable bystanders away from the growing argument. The pair could disagree privately without turning their disagreement into a public threat. Volunteers used a small grill to broil vegetables for families at the center. The minister offered a comparative report instead of the original. His eyelid twitched whenever soldiers passed.

人群圍住有爭議的公告。「妳必須選邊站！」有人喊。雅拉回答自己早已選擇：站在經得起提問的主張那一邊。尼洛沒有微笑，只把最脆弱的旁觀者帶離爭執。他們可以私下不同意對方，卻不能把分歧變成公開威脅。 志工用小烤架炙烤蔬菜，供服務中心的家庭食用。 議事廳下方有保存軍令的檔案庫，然而眼前封存的文件竟標著明天的日期。 每當士兵經過，他的眼皮便會抽動。

The institution was not a single villain. It contained frightened clerks, dedicated workers, ambitious supervisors, and people who had learned to hide mistakes. The network overseer wanted the incident classified as routine. Yara asked whose definition of routine was being used and whose losses were absent from the statistics. Nobody at the table could answer without consulting the sealed records. Around the trial chamber, incidental testimony complicated the picture: Their reserve power was finite, so the clinic had to prioritize essential equipment. The safety fund would let small neighborhood repair workshops flourish after the blackout. A hired gangster threatened the courier who had delivered the original maintenance sheets. The freight van vanished at the junction between the riverside road and the tunnel.

這個機構不是單一惡棍。裡面有害怕的書記、盡責的工人、野心勃勃的主管，也有學會掩蓋錯誤的人。網路監督者想把事件歸類為例行事項。雅拉追問：是誰定義例行？又有誰的損失被統計漏掉？桌邊沒人能在不看封存紀錄的情況下回答。試煉室周圍還有一些看似偶然的證詞，使情況更加複雜： 備用電力有限，因此診所必須優先供應必要設備。 安全基金將讓社區小修理工坊在停電後蓬勃發展。 一名受雇黑幫分子威脅遞送原始維修表的快遞員。 貨車在河岸道路與隧道交會的路口消失。

She refuses the obvious exit when its fee demands a stranger's memory, choosing to search the station's maintenance office instead. The decision did not repair everything. It altered one procedure, protected one person, or saved one piece of evidence; on a day when almost everyone demanded a miracle, that was worth recording. Yara insisted that the result and its limits appear together in the official minutes. Anything less would turn a modest gain into tomorrow’s misleading legend. A porter with a limp identified the sealed box he had carried upstairs. He said he could narrate the entire pursuit if she promised the guards would not drag him away. An orderly guided exhausted families to separate waiting rooms in the emergency clinic.

出口要求交出陌生人的記憶作為費用，她拒絕通過，改去尋找車站維修室。這個決定沒有修好一切，只改變一道程序、保護一個人，或留下了一份證據；當幾乎所有人都要求奇蹟時，這仍值得記錄。雅拉堅持把成果和限制一起寫入正式紀錄。否則小小的進展又會變成明天誤導人的傳說。 一名跛行的搬運員辨認出自己搬上樓的封盒。 他表示，只要公主保證守衛不把他拖走，便願意敘述整場追捕經過。 勤務員引導疲憊家庭前往緊急診所不同的候診室。

The lights beyond the window did not offer comfort. They showed how many people were still waiting. Yara understood that the investigation might eventually expose her own earlier errors, and she resolved not to erase them. Nilo suggested a short rest before the next hearing. She agreed, because exhaustion could damage judgment as thoroughly as deliberate deception. A bank teller confirmed that the suspicious transfer had occurred before the outage. The alleged contract was printed on virgin paper, unlike the stained original file. A warrant authorized capture, not execution; the distinction was applicable even to an enemy of the throne.

窗外的燈光沒有帶來安慰，只照出還有多少人在等待。雅拉明白調查最終可能揭露自己過去的錯誤，決定不去抹除。尼洛建議下一場聽證前短暫休息。她同意，因為疲憊和蓄意欺騙一樣可能損害判斷。 銀行出納確認可疑轉帳發生於停電之前。 所謂合約印在全新紙上，與有污漬的原檔不同。 逮捕令授權的是拘捕，不是處決；即使對王座的敵人，這項區別仍然適用。
