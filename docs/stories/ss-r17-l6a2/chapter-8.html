永遠星期二的學校

The School That Repeats Tuesday

無限副本的最後一名管理員 · 第 8 / 25 章 · 5000–6000 · 英中對照 · 校訂中

It began not with a battle but with an ordinary interruption in the linked trial worlds. A deserted school repeats the same Tuesday, while its pupils remember a disaster that teachers deny. At first, no one considered the interruption important enough to preserve. Yara thought otherwise. A forgotten detail could become a map, and a map could be used to control where frightened people were permitted to go. An accumulation of sediment had changed the waterways, and the official map was no longer accurate. A vendor offered each inspector a sealed beverage, but they declined gifts during the audit. The blond driver in the delivery photograph confirmed that he had used the wrong route. Only the independent laboratory could certify the chemical condition of the stored batteries.

事情不是從戰鬥開始，而是從相連的試煉世界裡一次平凡的中斷開始。一所廢棄學校不斷重演星期二，學生記得一場老師否認的災難。起初沒人認為這件事值得留下紀錄，但雅拉想法不同。遭忽略的細節可能變成地圖，而地圖也可能被用來控制驚恐的人可以去哪裡。 累積的泥沙改變了水道，官方地圖已不準確。 供應商給每位檢查員密封飲料，但眾人在稽核期間拒絕收禮。 送貨照片裡的金髮司機證實自己走錯了路。 只有獨立實驗室能證明儲存電池的化學狀況。

Nilo came back carrying a practical objection rather than an inspiring speech. The clock resets whenever a pupil tries to identify the missing head teacher. They had both seen people harmed when an assumption hardened into policy. A signed report could be wrong, and an emotional memory could be right, but neither became proof merely by being repeated. They agreed to seek a measurement from an independent observer. Around the trial chamber, incidental testimony complicated the picture: The warehouse manager demanded official clearance before the team opened the locked crates. A copy of those figures survived in the harbor office, where a careful official had refused to deduct unexplained losses. At noon, a provincial official presented a report declaring that all missing equipment had been destined for hospital repairs.

尼洛帶回的是實際的異議，而不是振奮人心的演說。每當學生試圖指認失蹤的校長，時鐘就會重置。他們都見過假設硬化成政策後如何傷人。有簽名的報告可能是錯的，強烈的記憶也可能是真的；兩者都不會因為反覆提起就變成證據。他們決定找獨立觀察員再測量一次。試煉室周圍還有一些看似偶然的證詞，使情況更加複雜： 倉庫經理要求團隊開啟鎖箱前先取得正式許可。 港務處保留了一份數字副本，一名謹慎的官員拒絕扣除來源不明的損失。 中午，一名省府官員提交報告，宣稱所有失蹤設備都注定要送去修理醫院。

“We can tell the truth without pretending we know everything,” Yara said. Nilo replied that a partial truth was difficult to defend against people who controlled the doors. She answered that a perfect story with no verifiable source was more dangerous. They divided the work: one would protect the witnesses, while the other checked the physical records and the sequence of events. The inspection team could exert pressure through lawful requests, not threats of punishment. A heroic rescue story could not substitute for the full record of warehouse risks. A retired army lieutenant volunteered to organize transport without taking control of the inquiry.

「我們能說實話，不需要假裝知道一切。」雅拉說。尼洛回答，當對方掌控所有門口時，局部真相比較難捍衛。她則認為沒有可驗證來源的完美故事更危險。他們分配工作：一人保護證人，另一人核查實體紀錄和事件時序。 檢查團隊可以依法提出要求施壓，而非恐嚇處罰。 英雄式救援故事不能取代倉庫風險的完整紀錄。 一名退役陸軍中尉志願安排運輸，沒有接管調查。

The argument moved from a private corridor into a formal room, where every statement would be copied. A senior official from the network overseer demanded certainty before granting access. Yara offered a less comfortable bargain: a limited preservation order now, followed by independent examination and a chance for every accused person to respond. That was enough to prevent an irreversible decision. Around the trial chamber, incidental testimony complicated the picture: Literate workers could read the manual, but the team also prepared diagrams for everyone else. The canal's lotus flowers were untouched, though the adjacent storage fence was broken. That error had made their petitions appear marginal, even when hundreds signed them. Two security routes overlap near the gate, creating a blind spot in the records.

爭論從私下走廊進入正式會議室，每句話都會留下副本。網路監督者的一名高級官員要求先確定真相才准查資料。雅拉提出比較不舒服的交換：先給有期限的保全命令，再獨立檢查，也讓所有被指控者有回應機會。這足以擋下一個不可逆的決定。試煉室周圍還有一些看似偶然的證詞，使情況更加複雜： 識字的工人能閱讀手冊，團隊也替其他人準備圖解。 運河裡的蓮花完好，旁邊儲藏圍籬卻破損。 這個錯誤使他們的請願看來無足輕重，即使有數百人簽名也一樣。 兩條保全路線在大門附近重疊，造成紀錄盲點。

Yara lets the pupils write independent accounts before changing the clock, preserving what happened. Even those who agreed with the choice could see its cost. Time was running out, supplies were not unlimited, and a mistake might travel far beyond this room. Nilo asked whether they should conceal the uncertainty until the crisis passed. Yara shook her head. People could not make free choices if the information reaching them had already been edited. A provincial transport official reviewed the company's hazardous-cargo permit. The new technician's qualification was checked by an independent professional board. Volunteers brought refreshments to the residents waiting outside the warehouse.

雅拉先讓學生分別寫下證詞，再動手改鐘，以保存真相。即使同意這個選擇的人，也看得出代價。時間不多、資源有限，一次錯誤可能傳得很遠。尼洛問是否該先隱瞞不確定性，等危機過後再說。雅拉搖頭。若資訊早被剪裁，人們便無法作出自由的選擇。 一名省級交通官員檢視公司的危險貨物許可。 新技師的資格由獨立專業委員會核對。 志工為倉庫外等待的居民送來茶點。

Later, under dim lights, Yara reread the original question. The dispute was larger now, but its center remained unchanged: who had the right to make an irreversible choice for someone else? Nilo stayed until the last copy was sealed. Neither spoke about victory. They knew the next door would open only because today’s evidence had been preserved and today’s mistakes had been admitted. The team tried to retrieve a broken seal without disturbing the surrounding evidence. The warehouse entrance bordered a swamp that became unsafe after heavy rain. “My vocation is to keep people safe,” he said.

晚些時候，在微弱燈光下，雅拉重讀最初的問題。爭端已經擴大，但核心沒變：誰有權替別人作出不可逆的選擇？尼洛留到最後一份副本封存。他們沒有談勝利，只知道下一扇門之所以能打開，是因為今天保住了證據，也承認了錯誤。 團隊試圖取回破損封條，同時不干擾周圍證物。 倉庫入口鄰近沼澤，豪雨後變得危險。 「我的志業是讓人安全，」他說，「不是保住大臣的名聲。
