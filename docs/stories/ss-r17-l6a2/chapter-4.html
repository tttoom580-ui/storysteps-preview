重生的代價

The Price of Respawning

無限副本的最後一名管理員 · 第 4 / 25 章 · 5000–6000 · 英中對照 · 校訂中

The morning had already acquired the look of an emergency before Yara reached the scene. A player who dies returns to the starting square but forgets one person who mattered to him. An official wanted a clean story, with a beginning that could be announced and an ending that would require no questions. Yara had learned that reality rarely showed such courtesy. She made room for the people who had been ordered to wait outside. The cleaning team used bleach only after investigators recorded the stains near the door. The union requested a comprehensive list of every visitor with access to the storage area. A credible witness described what she heard but refused to guess who issued the command. Yara praised the workers' dedication to preserving damaged equipment instead of concealing it.

在雅拉抵達現場前，早晨已經有了緊急事件的模樣。一名玩家死亡後回到起點，卻忘記了一位重要的人。官員想要一套簡潔的故事，開頭能宣布，結尾又不用回答問題。她知道現實很少如此客氣，於是請那些被命令等在門外的人進來說話。 調查員記錄門旁污漬後，清潔隊才使用漂白水。 工會要求完整列出所有能進入儲藏區的訪客。 可信的證人描述聽到的話，拒絕猜測誰下命令。 雅拉讚賞工人保存損壞設備而非藏匿的投入。

Nilo examined the account twice, once as a friend and once as a witness who might have to defend every word. Yara discovers that the loss is transferred into a hidden repository rather than destroyed. The difference between what they suspected and what they could establish mattered. They placed the new fact beside the previous chapter’s unanswered question and looked for a second source that had not been copied from the first. Around the trial chamber, incidental testimony complicated the picture: The guest room held an old dresser and a thermometer with a cracked glass stem. The guard's familiarity with the corridor helped reconstruct the route taken that night. The office hierarchy did not give the chairman permission to disregard witness protection rules.

尼洛把紀錄看了兩次：第一次以朋友身分，第二次則像一位必須為每個字負責的證人。雅拉發現那些記憶不是消失，而是被送進隱藏的資料庫。懷疑和能確立的事實必須分清楚。他們把新線索放在前一章未解的問題旁，尋找不曾從同一份資料抄出的第二來源。試煉室周圍還有一些看似偶然的證詞，使情況更加複雜： 客房裡有一座舊五斗櫃，以及玻璃管裂開的溫度計。 警衛對走廊的熟悉協助重建當晚路徑。 辦公室階級制度不允許董事長無視證人保護規則。

A crowd gathered around the disputed notice. “You have to choose a side,” someone shouted. Yara answered that she had chosen one already: the side of a claim that survived questions. Nilo did not smile, but moved the most vulnerable bystanders away from the growing argument. The pair could disagree privately without turning their disagreement into a public threat. The landlady of the nearby boarding house remembered an unusual delivery before dawn. Beyond it, the marsh looked lush and peaceful, concealing collapsed drainage channels. He had spent years trying to nurture survivors who were taught to mourn in silence.

人群圍住有爭議的公告。「妳必須選邊站！」有人喊。雅拉回答自己早已選擇：站在經得起提問的主張那一邊。尼洛沒有微笑，只把最脆弱的旁觀者帶離爭執。他們可以私下不同意對方，卻不能把分歧變成公開威脅。 附近寄宿屋的女房東記得黎明前一件異常送貨。 更遠處的沼澤看似草木茂盛而平靜，實際卻藏著崩毀的排水道。 他多年來努力照顧那些被要求默默哀悼的倖存者。

The institution was not a single villain. It contained frightened clerks, dedicated workers, ambitious supervisors, and people who had learned to hide mistakes. The network overseer wanted the incident classified as routine. Yara asked whose definition of routine was being used and whose losses were absent from the statistics. Nobody at the table could answer without consulting the sealed records. Around the trial chamber, incidental testimony complicated the picture: He had spent years trying to nurture survivors who were taught to mourn in silence. Rain pressed against the windows, and the river threatened to overflow its banks. A cleaner had begun to overhear the order before the supervisor closed the door. The company's new report could refute a rumor only by presenting verifiable records.

這個機構不是單一惡棍。裡面有害怕的書記、盡責的工人、野心勃勃的主管，也有學會掩蓋錯誤的人。網路監督者想把事件歸類為例行事項。雅拉追問：是誰定義例行？又有誰的損失被統計漏掉？桌邊沒人能在不看封存紀錄的情況下回答。試煉室周圍還有一些看似偶然的證詞，使情況更加複雜： 他多年來努力照顧那些被要求默默哀悼的倖存者。 雨水敲打窗戶，河水幾乎溢出河岸。 主管關門前，清潔員已無意間聽到那項命令。 公司新報告只能用可驗證紀錄反駁謠言。

She tells the frightened survivors the truth and promises no restoration without their consent. The decision did not repair everything. It altered one procedure, protected one person, or saved one piece of evidence; on a day when almost everyone demanded a miracle, that was worth recording. Yara insisted that the result and its limits appear together in the official minutes. Anything less would turn a modest gain into tomorrow’s misleading legend. A seagull circled the river dock while technicians inspected the abandoned van. The site superintendent admitted that he had approved a broken gate's temporary use. A recorded symphony played in the lobby, strangely calm beside the anxious witnesses.

她向驚恐的倖存者說明真相，承諾未經同意不會替任何人恢復記憶。這個決定沒有修好一切，只改變一道程序、保護一個人，或留下了一份證據；當幾乎所有人都要求奇蹟時，這仍值得記錄。雅拉堅持把成果和限制一起寫入正式紀錄。否則小小的進展又會變成明天誤導人的傳說。 技師檢查廢置廂車時，一隻海鷗繞著河港盤旋。 現場總監承認自己曾批准暫時使用損壞閘門。 大廳播放錄製的交響樂，與焦慮證人形成奇特反差。

The lights beyond the window did not offer comfort. They showed how many people were still waiting. Yara understood that the investigation might eventually expose her own earlier errors, and she resolved not to erase them. Nilo suggested a short rest before the next hearing. She agreed, because exhaustion could damage judgment as thoroughly as deliberate deception. A thermometer showed that the overheated cabinet had not cooled to safe levels. Yara said transparent testimony mattered more than turning the hearing into public turmoil. Volunteers prepared wholesome meals for interviewees who had missed the last bus.

窗外的燈光沒有帶來安慰，只照出還有多少人在等待。雅拉明白調查最終可能揭露自己過去的錯誤，決定不去抹除。尼洛建議下一場聽證前短暫休息。她同意，因為疲憊和蓄意欺騙一樣可能損害判斷。 溫度計顯示過熱櫃體尚未降至安全溫度。 見遙說透明證詞比把聽證變成公眾騷動更重要。 志工為錯過末班車的受訪者準備營養均衡的餐點。
