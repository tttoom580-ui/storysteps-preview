穿越未完成世界的列車

The Train Through Unfinished Worlds

無限副本的最後一名管理員 · 第 11 / 25 章 · 5000–6000 · 英中對照 · 校訂中

It began not with a battle but with an ordinary interruption in the linked trial worlds. An unmanned train links levels that appear in the game menu but were never officially opened. At first, no one considered the interruption important enough to preserve. Yara thought otherwise. A forgotten detail could become a map, and a map could be used to control where frightened people were permitted to go. The repair room felt hotter than thirty Celsius when the cooling fan stopped. Excessive friction inside the relay produced the unusual vibration recorded during the test. A stray dog began to growl when inspectors approached the abandoned loading dock. The lawyer said a forged instruction could induce workers to override a safe setting.

事情不是從戰鬥開始，而是從相連的試煉世界裡一次平凡的中斷開始。一列無人駕駛列車連接著遊戲選單中存在、卻從未正式開放的關卡。起初沒人認為這件事值得留下紀錄，但雅拉想法不同。遭忽略的細節可能變成地圖，而地圖也可能被用來控制驚恐的人可以去哪裡。 散熱風扇停轉時，維修室溫度超過攝氏三十度。 繼電器內過度摩擦造成測試中記錄的異常震動。 檢查員靠近廢置裝貨區時，一隻流浪狗開始低吼。 律師說偽造指令可能誘使工人覆寫安全設定。

Nilo came back carrying a practical objection rather than an inspiring speech. Its cargo manifests list vanished people as maintenance equipment. They had both seen people harmed when an assumption hardened into policy. A signed report could be wrong, and an emotional memory could be right, but neither became proof merely by being repeated. They agreed to seek a measurement from an independent observer. Around the trial chamber, incidental testimony complicated the picture: Many families still inhabit older buildings without reliable backup electricity. It was ironic that the trust's security account had been active long after its owner died. A retired mechanic lived on a grassy mound near the station.

尼洛帶回的是實際的異議，而不是振奮人心的演說。貨運清單竟將失蹤的人列為維修設備。他們都見過假設硬化成政策後如何傷人。有簽名的報告可能是錯的，強烈的記憶也可能是真的；兩者都不會因為反覆提起就變成證據。他們決定找獨立觀察員再測量一次。試煉室周圍還有一些看似偶然的證詞，使情況更加複雜： 許多家庭仍居住在沒有可靠備電的老建築裡。 諷刺的是，信託保全帳戶在原持有人死後很久仍活躍。 一名退休技師住在測站附近的草丘上。

“We can tell the truth without pretending we know everything,” Yara said. Nilo replied that a partial truth was difficult to defend against people who controlled the doors. She answered that a perfect story with no verifiable source was more dangerous. They divided the work: one would protect the witnesses, while the other checked the physical records and the sequence of events. A peacock from a nearby garden wandered close to the temporary generator fence. A pianist played for patients while the clinic's backup system was tested. Investigators used a thermal probe to identify the relay's overheating point.

「我們能說實話，不需要假裝知道一切。」雅拉說。尼洛回答，當對方掌控所有門口時，局部真相比較難捍衛。她則認為沒有可驗證來源的完美故事更危險。他們分配工作：一人保護證人，另一人核查實體紀錄和事件時序。 附近花園的一隻孔雀走近臨時發電機圍欄。 診所測試備電系統時，一位鋼琴家為病人演奏。 調查員使用熱探針找出繼電器過熱點。

The argument moved from a private corridor into a formal room, where every statement would be copied. A senior official from the network overseer demanded certainty before granting access. Yara offered a less comfortable bargain: a limited preservation order now, followed by independent examination and a chance for every accused person to respond. That was enough to prevent an irreversible decision. Around the trial chamber, incidental testimony complicated the picture: The respective duties of the city and company had been confused in the old emergency plan. The urgency of the investigation did not justify shortsighted risks. The nurse asked the engineers to simplify the battery instructions for home patients. A vessel crossing the strait was delayed by the same storm approaching the city.

爭論從私下走廊進入正式會議室，每句話都會留下副本。網路監督者的一名高級官員要求先確定真相才准查資料。雅拉提出比較不舒服的交換：先給有期限的保全命令，再獨立檢查，也讓所有被指控者有回應機會。這足以擋下一個不可逆的決定。試煉室周圍還有一些看似偶然的證詞，使情況更加複雜： 舊緊急計畫混淆了市府與公司的各自責任。 調查再緊急，也不能合理化目光短淺的風險。 護士請工程師簡化居家病人的電池使用說明。 一艘穿越海峽的船因同場暴風雨而延誤。

Yara sends copies to every carriage and asks passengers to verify their own names. Even those who agreed with the choice could see its cost. Time was running out, supplies were not unlimited, and a mistake might travel far beyond this room. Nilo asked whether they should conceal the uncertainty until the crisis passed. Yara shook her head. People could not make free choices if the information reaching them had already been edited. The superficial inspection had missed heat damage deep inside the relay cabinet. No director could suppress a failed test merely to preserve the company's image. The diagram's symmetry made one missing sensor unusually easy to notice.

雅拉把副本送到每節車廂，請乘客自行核對姓名。即使同意這個選擇的人，也看得出代價。時間不多、資源有限，一次錯誤可能傳得很遠。尼洛問是否該先隱瞞不確定性，等危機過後再說。雅拉搖頭。若資訊早被剪裁，人們便無法作出自由的選擇。 表面的檢查漏掉了繼電器櫃內部深處的熱損傷。 董事不得只為保護公司形象而壓下失敗測試。 圖面對稱的配置讓一個缺失的感測器格外明顯。

Later, under dim lights, Yara reread the original question. The dispute was larger now, but its center remained unchanged: who had the right to make an irreversible choice for someone else? Nilo stayed until the last copy was sealed. Neither spoke about victory. They knew the next door would open only because today’s evidence had been preserved and today’s mistakes had been admitted. An expired access token had been used to send the dormant account's last message. The public committee tried to unify the three separate emergency notification systems. The nurse emphasized the urgency of restoring the clinic's refrigerated medical supply.

晚些時候，在微弱燈光下，雅拉重讀最初的問題。爭端已經擴大，但核心沒變：誰有權替別人作出不可逆的選擇？尼洛留到最後一份副本封存。他們沒有談勝利，只知道下一扇門之所以能打開，是因為今天保住了證據，也承認了錯誤。 最後一封休眠帳戶訊息使用了一枚過期存取憑證。 公共委員會試圖整合三套分離的緊急通知系統。 護士強調恢復診所冷藏醫療用品供應的急迫性。
