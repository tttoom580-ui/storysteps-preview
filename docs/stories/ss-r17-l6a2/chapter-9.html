One Signature Too Many

無限副本的最後一名管理員 · 第 9 / 25 章 · 5000–6000 · 英中對照 · 校訂中

Yara arrived with the unpleasant certainty that the simplest explanation was probably the one that had been prepared for public consumption. The rescue petition carries four names although the closure rule allows exactly three witnesses. The place was full of people who needed a quick answer, yet the evidence still belonged to those whose lives it could change. Instead of making a dramatic accusation, Yara marked the hour, the witnesses, and the smallest observable detail in a paper notebook. The public statement might alienate frightened investors, but hiding the altered seal would be worse. A black blot on the purchase order concealed the first digit of a serial number. Residents shared chili soup while waiting for the procurement hearing to begin. The envoy explained that continuity of village access was guaranteed in the oldest agreement.

雅拉抵達時，心裡很清楚：最簡單的解釋往往是事先準備好要給大眾聽的版本。救援請願書有四個姓名，但關卡關閉規則只允許三位證人。現場的人都急需答案，但證據仍屬於那些生活可能被它改變的人。她沒有戲劇化地指控誰，而是先在紙本筆記上記下時刻、證人，以及最小卻可觀察的細節。 公開聲明可能疏遠害怕的投資人，但隱瞞改動封條更糟。 採購單上的黑墨漬蓋住序號第一個數字。 居民等候採購聽證開始時，分享辣椒湯。 使者解釋，最早的協議保障村民持續使用這條路；後來的修正案逐漸刪除渡口相關文字，累積的省略使權利在沒有任何正式廢止令的情況下消失。

There was a moment of quiet when Nilo set the new material on the table. The extra name belongs to a player who never entered the school and whose biometric record has been forged. What mattered was not how surprising the discovery sounded, but whether anyone could repeat the test. Yara copied the relevant names carefully. If one person later changed the account, the original would still be available for comparison. Around the trial chamber, incidental testimony complicated the picture: The cumulative delays had increased costs far beyond the original repair estimate. They showed a decisive increase in heat extraction on the night the orphanage burned. No worker was dispensable merely because an executive feared an embarrassing question.

尼洛把新資料放上桌時，四周安靜了一瞬。多出的姓名屬於從未進入學校的玩家，生物辨識資料也遭偽造。重要的不是發現有多驚人，而是別人能不能重做同一項測試。雅拉仔細抄下相關姓名；如果日後有人更改說法，原件仍可供比對。試煉室周圍還有一些看似偶然的證詞，使情況更加複雜： 累積延誤讓成本遠超原始維修估算。 作者最後寫下一個簡短問題：「為什麼測試開始後，撤離卻被取消？ 不能因主管害怕尷尬提問，就把任何工人當成可有可無。

“Will you trust me?” Nilo asked. “With the evidence, yes,” Yara answered, “but not with a blank signature.” It was the sort of answer that could sound cold until someone noticed how carefully she had protected the other person’s right to disagree. Together they made a plan with a backup route, a deadline, and a record of who had accepted each responsibility. A visitor wore jasmine perfume that matched the scent noted by the night guard. The river lodge provided temporary rooms for families displaced during repairs. A maple tree near the entrance helped the courier identify the delivery location.

「妳信任我嗎？」尼洛問。「證據上可以，空白授權書不行。」雅拉回答。這聽起來很冷淡，直到人們注意到她也小心保護對方拒絕的權利。他們一起訂下有備用路線、期限，以及明確責任分工的計畫。 某位訪客使用茉莉香水，與夜班警衛記下的氣味一致。 河邊旅舍為維修期間搬離的家庭提供臨時房間。 入口旁的楓樹協助快遞員辨認送貨地點。

A representative of the network overseer arrived with an offer to resolve the matter quietly. Quietly, in this case, meant without giving affected people access to the original. Yara declined. She knew an office could be useful and still be dangerously wrong. Her objection was narrow and specific: the proposed decision relied on a document whose origin had not yet been independently verified. Around the trial chamber, incidental testimony complicated the picture: The next witness was Yara, an envoy from the eastern federation whom the network officially called a rebel mistress of spies. In the next room, people debated how to mobilize villagers for a public meeting without provoking soldiers. Yara paused to muse over how often public trust had been treated as private property. The whistleblower's persistence finally persuaded investigators to examine the old account.

網路監督者的代表前來，提議私下解決。所謂私下，就是不讓受影響的人看到原件。雅拉拒絕了。她知道一個機關可以有用，也可能犯下危險的錯。她的反對具體而有限：提議中的決定依賴一份來源還沒接受獨立驗證的文件。試煉室周圍還有一些看似偶然的證詞，使情況更加複雜： 下一名證人是東方聯邦使者伊絲拉；試煉網路官方稱她是指揮間諜的叛軍女頭目。 隔壁房裡，人們討論如何動員村民出席公開集會，又不激怒士兵。 見遙停下來沉思，公共信任究竟有多少次被當成私人財產。 吹哨者的堅持終於讓調查員檢查舊帳戶。

Nilo proposes an independent comparison instead of accusing the nearest frightened teacher. A few witnesses thanked Yara; others accused her of doing too little. She accepted both reactions without offering a promise she could not keep. Nilo wrote down the complaints, including the ones directed at their own conduct. They were not building a monument to themselves. They were building a process that would still function after they left. A socialist council member supported public oversight without claiming every private firm was guilty. Employees had a chance to socialize after the hearing without being questioned by reporters. The access log covered a span of seven hours, with one unexplained gap near midnight.

尼洛主張獨立比對，不願直接指控最近那位驚慌的老師。幾名證人向雅拉道謝，另一些人指責她做得不夠。她接受兩種反應，不許下無法兌現的承諾。尼洛寫下抱怨，包括針對他們自己行為的部分。他們不是為自己立紀念碑，而是建立自己離開後仍能運作的程序。 一名社會主義議員支持公共監督，沒有宣稱所有私人企業都有罪。 聽證後，員工終於能不受記者盤問地社交。 門禁紀錄涵蓋七小時，其中午夜附近有一段無法解釋的空白。

By nightfall the newest report was signed by observers who were free to disagree. Yara stood beside Nilo and looked at the distance between them and a lawful exit that preserves the survivors and their memories. Progress felt less like triumph than like leaving a reliable mark on a difficult road. Someone would challenge that mark tomorrow. This time, there would be a public record with which to answer. The author had been ordered to trek into the mountains and test the trial generator before civilians were evacuated. Their disagreement was vigorous but not violent. Every emergency sensor needed a waterproof housing before the coming storm.

夜幕降臨前，新的報告由可以提出異議的觀察者簽署。雅拉站在尼洛身旁，看著他們與保護倖存者和記憶的合法出口之間的距離。進展不像勝利，比較像在艱難路上留下可靠的記號。明天有人會質疑，而這一次將有公開紀錄可以回答。 作者奉命徒步進入山區，在平民撤離前測試試煉發電機。 他們爭論得激烈，卻沒有暴力。 每個緊急感測器都必須在風暴來前裝上防水外殼。
