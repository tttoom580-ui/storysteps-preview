回到現實只一夜

One Night Back in the World

Jianxia woke in her own dormitory beneath a bulky winter blanket, certain that the station had been a nightmare until she found the ticket inside her pillowcase. News reports showed no sign of a missing platform. Her professor deemed her sudden absence unacceptable, and her roommates demanded explanations she couldn't give. Outside, a parade celebrated the liberation of an old neighborhood from demolition plans. Its banners proclaimed that no dictatorship could last forever. To Jianxia, the declaration sounded less like history and more like advice. She went to the station entrance at noon, but where Platform 13 ought to have been, there was only a florist selling pebbles painted like cartoon animals.

見夏在自己宿舍的厚重冬被下醒來，原以為車站只是一場噩夢，直到她在枕套裡摸到黑票。新聞完全沒有失蹤月台的消息。教授認為她突然缺席無法接受，室友則追問她答不出的問題。街上正舉辦遊行，慶祝老社區免於拆除，旗幟高喊獨裁統治不可能永久持續。見夏聽來不像歷史，更像忠告。中午她回到車站入口，本該是第十三站台的地方，只有一家賣卡通動物彩繪小石頭的花店。

Shen Man met her at a café and displayed a notebook filled with medical observations. Zhou Yan arrived late carrying a handicraft key chain shaped like a train. He explained that an interpreter had given it to him years ago, after he woke from a hospital stay without memories. He had searched for the interpreter ever since. “Why didn't you tell us?” Jianxia demanded. “Because I wasn't sure you were the same girl,” he answered. His admission left an ache she couldn't name. He showed her a photograph of a distant plantation, its rows of trees stretching beneath a lofty hill. A child in the picture looked like Jianxia; another appeared to be Zhou Yan. Neither remembered the trip.

沈漫在咖啡館見她，帶著記滿醫學觀察的筆記本。周硯遲到，手裡拿著火車造型的手工鑰匙圈。他說多年前住院失憶後，一名口譯員給他的，自己一直在找對方。「怎麼不早說？」見夏問。「因為我不確定妳是不是照片裡的女孩。」這句坦白讓她心頭發酸。他拿出一張遙遠種植園照片，整齊樹列延伸到高聳山坡。照片裡一個孩子像見夏，另一個像周硯，但兩人都不記得去過。

He Chuan joined them with a strange file about a company that had once tested navigation systems for disaster evacuations. According to the document, employees had been made to overwork for months before the project was canceled. One anonymous employee described the board's decision as a trifle compared with the lives already lost. A former manager had written that company royalty should be awarded to whoever discovered how to separate memory from fear. “That is a dreadful business model,” Shen Man said. Jianxia wondered whether their ordeal was a test that had never been shut down, or a machine still following instructions after its operators vanished. The idea spurred her to investigate instead of hiding. The archived maps might help them navigate an unfamiliar route without trusting the station’s signs. The possibility was enough to spur another argument about whether to return.

賀川帶來奇怪檔案，一家公司曾測試災難疏散導航系統。文件顯示員工連續數月超時工作，專案才遭取消。一位匿名員工說，董事會決策相較於已失去的生命不過是小事。前經理則主張，誰能把記憶與恐懼分離，誰就該得到技術權利金。「真是可怕的商業模式，」沈漫說。見夏懷疑試驗根本沒有關閉，或者操作員消失後機器仍在照命令運作。這想法促使她調查，而不是躲藏。 封存地圖也許能幫他們導航陌生路線，不必相信車站指標。 光是這種可能就足以促使大家再度爭論該不該返回。

They took an evening stroll along the river, trying to behave like normal university students. A passing cyclist waved to a young couple and called them by name. Shen Man quietly mentioned that her cousin was a lesbian woman who had once been rejected by her family, and that the station's promise of unconditional belonging had frightened her more than its threats. “Real acceptance cannot be purchased by losing your memory,” she said. He Chuan nodded. He had spent years pretending not to care about his own family's judgment. The group fell silent, unexpectedly close. Jianxia saw that even outside the station, all of them carried wounds the system might exploit.

四人傍晚沿河散步，努力像正常大學生。一名騎士向路過的情侶招手，直接叫出他們名字。沈漫提起一位女同志表姊，曾因性向被家人排斥；車站那種「無條件歸屬」的承諾，反而比威脅更令她恐懼。「真正的接納不能靠失去記憶換來，」她說。賀川點頭，他多年來也假裝不在乎家人評價。大家安靜下來，卻因此意外靠近。見夏明白，即使離開車站，每個人身上仍有系統可利用的傷口。

Near sunset, Zhou Yan suggested returning to the florist. Beneath its steps he found a rusty farm tool, a miniature plow, mounted under glass beside a picture of the old plantation. “Someone has arranged these clues,” he said. Jianxia turned the miniature over and discovered the number thirteen scratched underneath. In the store mirror, a train glided behind them although the street remained empty. The florist, a woman with a deep tan, looked at the reflection without surprise. “Your return is pending,” she said. “The second ticket arrives after dark.” Before Jianxia could ask who employed her, the woman closed the shutters. In their place stood a wall of bricks that looked a century old.

接近日落，周硯提議回花店。階梯下玻璃盒展示著生鏽的小犁，旁邊是那座種植園的照片。「有人在安排線索，」他說。見夏翻過小犁，底下刻著十三。店內鏡子映出一列車從身後滑過，街上卻空無一物。膚色黝黑的花店老闆娘望著倒影，完全不驚訝。「你們的歸程尚待處理，」她說，「第二張車票天黑後會到。」見夏還沒來得及問她為誰工作，女人便關起百葉窗，原地只剩一面看似百年老牆。

At 11:59 p.m., every phone in the group displayed the same notification: YOUR FIRST RETURN WAS A COURTESY. Beneath it a red button said CONTINUE. Jianxia refused to press it. The screen changed anyway, showing the old janitor's badge and a photograph of her brother standing beside a hospital bed. A second black ticket slid from her phone case. Somewhere far away, rails began to sing. She called Zhou Yan. He answered on the first ring and said, “I'm already at the station.” She looked out her window. A figure was walking beneath the streetlight, notebook under one arm, waiting. She hurried downstairs, not because she believed the platform was safe, but because leaving him there alone felt worse.

晚上十一點五十九分，四人的手機同時顯示：「第一次歸還屬於禮遇。」下面是紅色「繼續」按鈕。見夏沒有按，畫面卻自行換成老清潔工胸牌和哥哥站在病床旁的照片。第二張黑票從手機殼滑出。遠方鐵軌開始發出歌聲。她打給周硯，他第一聲就接：「我已經到車站了。」她望出窗，街燈下有人夾著筆記本等待。她急忙下樓，不是因為相信月台安全，而是覺得把他一個人留在那裡更難忍受。
