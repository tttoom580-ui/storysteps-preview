第十三次潮汐

The Thirteenth Tide

第十三站台 · 第 19 / 25 章 · 5000–6000 · 英中對照 · 校訂中

The flooded platform lay beneath a moon so large it seemed to have swallowed the sky. Along the upper boulevard, deserted market stalls floated in shallow water. A vendor's sign offered hot broth and oatmeal to passengers awaiting the lunar tide. Jianxia tested the water with a metal rod and discovered that the surface rose in time with an invisible clock. Beneath it, a crater opened and closed like a mouth. “This is a diversion,” Zhou Yan said, studying a distant gleam under the tracks. “The real entrance is somewhere the tide is hiding.” Their first challenge was to identify what counted as a doorway in a place where everything moved.

淹水月台上方，月亮大得像吞下天空。上層林蔭大道的市集攤位漂在淺水裡，招牌賣熱湯與燕麥粥給等待月潮的乘客。見夏用鐵棒測水，發現水面隨看不見的時鐘起落。水下坑洞像嘴一開一合。「這是轉移注意的手法，」周硯望向軌道下的微光，「真正入口藏在潮水掩護的地方。」首先得認出一座萬物移動的地方裡，什麼才算門。

A cheerful attendant asked them to enroll in a training course for tidal survival. The brochure promised that even travelers with a physical handicap could complete the route if they obeyed every command. Shen Man questioned why any course was needed when the platform was already flooded. The attendant replied that the program had been established at the outset of the station to prepare survivors for extreme emergencies. He called the system formidable but ultimately beneficial. Jianxia saw the familiar trick: praise the machine until people blamed themselves for any failure. “Your promises sound impressive,” she said, “but where are the people who finished the course?” The attendant looked toward the deep water and said nothing. He cautioned them not to overdo the training, as exhaustion would be counted against them.

熱情服務員邀他們報名潮汐生存課程，宣傳冊承諾即使有身體障礙，照命令做也能通關。沈漫質問月台都淹了為何還要上課。對方說課程自車站創立之初便存在，為極端緊急事件訓練倖存者，稱系統雖強大卻最終有益。見夏認出熟悉伎倆：先讚揚機器，失敗時人就怪自己。「承諾很動聽，」她說，「完成課程的人在哪裡？」服務員望向深水，沒有回答。 他提醒大家別把訓練做得過火，因為疲憊還會被當成扣分依據。

He Chuan found a sheet of dictation exercises floating inside a sealed bottle. The sentences instructed students to repeat the words I AM THE VICTOR until they believed themselves superior to others. Jianxia frowned. “That's not survival. That's a lesson in superiority.” Nearby, a cosmetic display showed faces with perfect complexions smiling above water that had swallowed homes. The advertisement made suffering look like a personal failure to maintain beauty. Shen Man smashed the display with a loose board, uncovering a metal hatch. A whistle sounded from below. The old janitor rose from the water, still carrying his mop, his eyes exhausted. “You have reached the place that was meant to be forgotten,” he said. The smiling models appeared sociable, but not one acknowledged the people drowning behind them.

賀川在密封瓶裡找到漂浮的聽寫練習紙，句子要學生重複「我是勝利者」，直到相信自己高人一等。「這不是求生，」見夏皺眉，「是優越感教育。」附近美妝廣告讓膚色完美的人笑在吞沒房屋的水上，彷彿受苦只是沒維持美貌的錯。沈漫用木板砸碎展示架，露出金屬艙口。下方響起哨聲，老清潔工從水裡升上來，仍拿著拖把，眼神疲倦。「你們來到本該被遺忘的地方了。」 模特兒看似很善於交際，卻沒有一人理會身後溺水的人。

The janitor admitted that he had once served as a trustee of the laboratory and had failed to intervene when tests became cruel. He spent years trying to repair the damage by guiding passengers through hidden passages. “I cannot undo what I did,” he said. “But I can prevent the station from claiming another child.” Jianxia wanted to condemn him, yet the truth demanded more than a single label. He showed them a map of tunnels sharpened into a pattern like the ribs of a fish. At the center was a closed door marked CORE, accessible only during the thirteenth tide. The next rise of water was only minutes away.

清潔工承認自己曾任實驗室受託管理人，試驗變殘酷時沒出手阻止。多年來他靠引導乘客走暗道試著補救。「我無法抹去自己做過的事，」他說，「但能阻止車站再吞下一個孩子。」見夏想責備他，卻知道真相不能只靠一個標籤。老人展示魚肋狀隧道地圖，中央有寫「核心」的封門，只有第十三次潮汐能進入。下一次漲水只剩幾分鐘。

They tied themselves together with a rope and followed the janitor down the flooded tunnel. The water tugged at their legs, trying to separate them. He Chuan nearly lost his footing, and Jianxia caught him. He whispered thanks without asking for forgiveness. Through the water she saw faces pressed against the glass walls, not angry but waiting. The old man explained that the station had preserved people as records after their bodies vanished from ordinary time. “If we break the wrong machine,” Zhou Yan said, “we could erase the only thing keeping them here.” Jianxia answered that they would have to find a way to open the prison without destroying the prisoners.

他們用繩子把彼此連住，跟清潔工走進淹水隧道，水流拉扯雙腿，企圖沖散。賀川差點滑倒，見夏伸手拉住他，他低聲道謝，沒有索求原諒。玻璃牆後有人臉貼著觀看，不憤怒，只像在等待。老人說車站在人們身體離開正常時間後，把他們保存成紀錄。「要是破壞錯機器，」周硯說，「可能抹掉維持他們存在的唯一東西。」見夏回答，他們得找到打開牢籠卻不毀掉囚犯的方法。

The tide reached its peak. A broad section of wall slid upward, revealing a staircase descending into darkness. The janitor could go no farther; his badge began to crack in the rising water. “Tell the others I tried,” he whispered. Jianxia looked back at him. “I'll tell them what you did,” she said, “both the wrong and the effort to repair it.” He bowed his head, accepting the fairness of that promise. The group descended as the tunnel closed behind them. From below came the quiet chime of a train arriving at its final destination. A sign glowed through the darkness: TERMINUS. ENTRY COST: ONE MEMORY YOU CANNOT BEAR TO LOSE.

潮水升到最高，牆壁大片上升，露出向下的黑暗樓梯。清潔工胸牌在水中碎裂，無法繼續。「告訴大家我努力過，」他低語。見夏回頭：「我會把你做過的都說出來，包括錯事和補救。」老人垂首，接受這份公平。眾人向下走，隧道在身後關閉。下面傳來列車抵達終點的輕響。黑暗裡站牌亮起：「終點站。入場代價：一段你最不能失去的記憶。」
