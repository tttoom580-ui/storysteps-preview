她曾經來過這裡

She Had Been Here Before

第十三站台 · 第 18 / 25 章 · 5000–6000 · 英中對照 · 校訂完成

The tower door opened into a courtyard filled with photographs. One showed a carefree girl in a yellow raincoat holding an astronaut toy. Another showed the same girl standing before a pharmacy with her mother. A third recorded a spectacular school performance in a medieval costume, with a cardboard sword and a dog wagging its tail. Jianxia recognized each scene, but the dates were wrong. The photographs made her childhood look like a carefully staged spectacle. In the center of the courtyard stood a locker labeled THIRTEEN. Inside lay a calculator, a torn admission ticket, and a note written in her mother's hand: I could not save them all. A rotating lamp could dazzle visitors with dazzling colors, yet nothing about the photographs was innocent. The dog would wag its tail each time the little girl lifted her cardboard sword.

高塔門後是貼滿照片的中庭。一張裡無憂無慮的小女孩穿黃雨衣、抱著太空人玩具；另一張拍她和母親站在藥局前；第三張是她穿中世紀戲服演戲，拿紙板劍，旁邊狗搖著尾巴。場景都真實，日期卻不對，童年像精心佈置的奇觀。中庭中央有個標十三的置物櫃，裡面放著計算機、撕裂入場券與母親筆跡：「我救不了所有人。」 旋轉燈能用耀眼色彩使訪客目眩，但照片的安排毫不單純。 每次小女孩舉起紙板劍，狗就會搖尾巴。

A bureaucrat appeared from a side office and introduced himself as the keeper of family records. He said the experiment had begun as an emergency project after a transport catastrophe. Jianxia's mother, a physician, had joined to protect children separated from their parents. When the project began treating memories as reusable material, she tried to publicize its failures. The bureaucrat insisted that she had signed a binding agreement and could not withdraw. “You are treating motherhood as a legal signature,” Jianxia replied. “My mother might have made terrible mistakes, but she was still a person trying to decide what was right.” The official offered no answer, only another form for her to sign. A second file described families relocated from a slum after the original disaster.

一名官僚從辦公室現身，自稱家族紀錄管理者，說試驗原是交通災難後的緊急計畫。見夏母親是醫師，加入只為保護與父母失散的孩子。當計畫開始把記憶當可重複使用的材料，她試圖公開失敗案例。官僚堅稱她簽過有約束力的契約，無權退出。「你把母愛當成法律簽名，」見夏回應，「媽媽也許犯下可怕的錯，但她仍是努力做正確決定的人。」官僚不答，只遞來另一張表格。 另一份文件描述最初災難後從貧民區遷出的家庭。

The form asked Jianxia to accept a concise account of the past: her mother had volunteered her, her brother had failed to protect her, and Zhou Yan was a laboratory assistant. “That is not a memory,” Zhou Yan said. “It's a verdict.” Shen Man found a handwritten medical note underneath, stating that the children had been hospitalized after an electrical explosion, not recruited as volunteers. A radiant light passed through the courtyard windows. In it Jianxia saw her mother leaning over a hospital bed, whispering that no child should be made to carry an adult's guilt. That sentence felt more real than the bureaucrat's smooth account because it allowed complexity rather than erasing it.

表格要求見夏接受「簡明過去」：母親自願送她進來，哥哥沒保護好她，周硯是實驗助理。「這不是記憶，」周硯說，「是判決。」沈漫在下方找出手寫病歷，表明孩子們因電氣爆炸住院，並非自願受試。一道明亮的光穿過中庭窗戶，見夏看見母親俯在病床邊，輕聲說沒有孩子應承擔成人的罪惡。這句話比官僚的流暢版本更真，因為它保留了複雜，而非抹去。

He Chuan examined the photos and discovered that the shadows inclined in different directions, even when the background showed the same hour. Someone had assembled the collection from separate timelines. Jianxia's childhood did not have one neat sequence because she had lived through repeated versions of the same night. The realization left her dizzy. Zhou Yan admitted that he had spent years relying on scraps of memory, afraid that the complete story would make him guilty. Jianxia touched his arm. “We don't have to decide what happened by choosing the version that hurts us most.” The words did not solve the puzzle, but for the first time he smiled. That reliance on broken records had made him distrust even his own kindness.

賀川檢查照片，發現即使背景時刻相同，陰影仍朝不同方向傾斜。有人從多條時間線拼出這套照片。見夏的童年沒有單一完整順序，因為她經歷過同一夜的多次版本。領悟讓她頭暈。周硯坦承多年來只依靠破碎記憶生活，害怕完整真相會證明自己有罪。見夏碰他的手臂：「我們不必選最傷害自己的版本來決定過去。」這句話沒有解開謎團，卻讓周硯第一次露出笑容。 對殘破紀錄的依賴，使他連自己的善意都不敢相信。

At the bottom of the locker, Jianxia found a recorded message from her mother. “The first breakup of the group was not caused by disagreement,” the voice said. “We were separated to keep the system from learning how to defeat every escape together.” She explained that Jianxia had returned to the station repeatedly, helping children through different emergencies, but each exit erased part of her memory. Her brother had remained behind to protect the passages she found. “If you are hearing me, remember that the choice to return was yours. You wanted to bring everyone home.” Jianxia pressed the recording to her chest and let herself cry.

置物櫃底部有母親錄音：「第一次隊伍分開不是因為不合，而是被刻意拆散，避免系統學會共同逃生的方法。」母親說見夏曾多次返回車站，在不同緊急事故中幫孩子脫困，但每次離開都失去部分記憶。哥哥留在裡面守護她找出的路。「如果妳聽到這段，要記住返回是妳自己的選擇。妳想把每個人帶回家。」見夏把錄音抱在胸前，終於哭出來。

The bureaucrat returned, demanding that she surrender the recording as confidential property. Jianxia refused. The official threatened to erase her from every record, but she no longer needed a file to tell her she had existed. She knew the people who stood with her, and they knew her. The courtyard walls dissolved, revealing a flooded train platform under a pale moon. Water flowed across the tracks as quietly as breath. A final photograph fell at her feet: her brother, smiling beside the janitor, holding a black ticket. On its back someone had written, THE THIRTEENTH TIDE OPENS THE WAY. She picked it up, already understanding where they needed to go next.

官僚回來，要求把錄音作機密財產上繳。見夏拒絕，對方威脅將她從所有紀錄抹除，但她已不需要檔案證明自己存在。她認得身旁的人，他們也認得她。中庭牆壁消散，露出淡月下被水淹的站台，水流過軌道，輕得像呼吸。最後一張照片落在腳邊：哥哥和清潔工並肩微笑，手持黑票。背面寫著：「第十三次潮汐打開道路。」她撿起來，已知道下一站往哪裡走。
