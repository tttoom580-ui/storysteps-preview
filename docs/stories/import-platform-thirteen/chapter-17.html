The Informant

第十三站台 · 第 17 / 25 章 · 5000–6000 · 英中對照 · 校訂完成

They emerged from the shaft into a city square where a nighttime curfew was in force. A public procession moved silently between concrete buildings while cameras watched from every corner. He Chuan's phone began to vibrate. On its screen appeared a capsule-shaped icon and a message promising a generous dividend if he submitted the team's location. Jianxia saw him hide the phone and felt cold. The system had named an informant; was it him? A large billboard showed an honorary playwright receiving an award for a drama about obedience. Beneath the picture, a slogan declared that defiance was a dangerous abstraction. Jianxia wondered how many citizens had accepted a word as proof of guilt.

他們從維修井走進深夜宵禁中的城市廣場。沉默遊行經過水泥樓群，攝影機從每個角落監視。賀川手機震動，螢幕顯示膠囊圖示，訊息承諾只要上傳隊伍位置便能獲得豐厚分紅。見夏看見他藏起手機，心裡一冷。系統說隊裡有告密者，是他嗎？巨大看板上，一位名譽劇作家因歌頌服從的戲獲獎，下面口號寫著「反抗是不可靠的抽象概念」。見夏想，究竟多少人把這句話當成定罪證據？

Shen Man noticed an airway vent in a corner and heard a child crying inside it. She asked the others to help, but a guard warned that rescue attempts would result in imprisonment. He Chuan offered to speak to the guard, using the smooth voice that had once won him interviews. Jianxia blocked his path. “Show me your phone,” she demanded. He hesitated, and her fear hardened into certainty. Finally he handed it over. The screen displayed photographs of the group, their routes, and even the handwritten messages in the shaft. “You've been selling us,” she said. The betrayal hurt because he had been the one who told them to question official stories. A half-eaten grapefruit sat beside the guard’s desk, the most ordinary object in a deeply abnormal place. The guard repeated that the penalty was imprisonment, but would not say who had written the law.

沈漫聽到角落通風道裡有孩子哭，想請大家救援，警衛卻警告擅自救人將被監禁。賀川自告奮勇去和警衛交涉，聲音像過去採訪時一樣流暢。見夏擋住：「把手機給我。」他猶豫，她的恐懼便凝成確信。最後他交出手機，上面有隊伍照片、路線，甚至維修井手寫訊息。「你把我們賣了，」她說。背叛讓她更痛，因為賀川一直是提醒大家質疑官方敘事的人。 警衛桌旁放著吃了一半的葡萄柚，是這個異常地方最普通的東西。 警衛重複說處罰是監禁，卻不肯告訴他們誰制定了法律。

He Chuan did not deny the evidence. Instead he showed her the final page of his messages. “Every time I sent a route, the system moved its guards away from a group of children trapped elsewhere,” he said. “I thought I could bargain for their safety.” Shen Man looked furious. “You had no right to make that choice alone.” He Chuan agreed. His intention did not erase the harm. Jianxia knew he had committed a notable betrayal, but she also saw the frightened man who believed one wrong act might prevent something worse. She asked him to explain all the messages, without leaving out anything that made him look bad.

賀川沒否認，只翻出訊息末頁。「我每送出一條路線，系統就把警衛移離另一群被困孩子，」他說，「我以為能換他們平安。」沈漫很憤怒：「你沒權自己決定。」賀川承認了。動機不能抹去傷害。見夏知道這是重大的背叛，也看見一個以為錯事能阻止更大災難的恐懼者。她要他把所有訊息說清楚，連會讓自己難堪的也不能省略。

They discovered that the station used He Chuan's transmissions to amplify a false story about the group. Each serial report described them as dangerous travelers who would destroy the city. A sociologist's archived note explained that fear spread faster when people were shown repeated warnings without context. “We're being turned into monsters by a sequence of ordinary announcements,” Jianxia said. In the distance, a swarm of mechanical birds circled over a tower. One broke away and flew toward them, tapping the familiar rhythm on a fence. The bird had followed Jianxia since the first trial. Its arrival made her wonder whether the guards were truly as powerful as they appeared. The archived field was labeled sociology, though its data had been used to frighten rather than understand people.

他們發現車站用賀川傳送的資料放大假敘事，每一份連續報告都稱他們是會毀城的危險旅客。社會學者封存筆記說，缺乏脈絡而反覆播放警告，恐懼傳得特別快。「它用一連串平常廣播把我們變成怪物，」見夏說。遠處一群機械鳥盤旋塔頂，其中一隻飛來，敲出熟悉節奏。牠從第一輪就跟著見夏。鳥的出現讓她懷疑警衛是否真如表面那麼強大。 封存欄位寫著社會學，但資料被拿來恐嚇人，而不是理解人。

The bird led them to a wall covered in geometry diagrams. At its center was a map of air vents that ran upward through the city. A handwritten note said that the vents could carry words as well as air. He Chuan offered to transmit the truth through the network he had misused. Jianxia hesitated, then let him try under Shen Man's supervision. His voice carried through the vents, listing the names of missing children and the false reports used to hide them. The guards moved toward the sound, giving the group time to open the vent cover and rescue the child they had heard crying. A small hand reached out of the darkness and gripped Jianxia's.

機械鳥引他們到一面畫滿幾何圖的牆，中央是向上延伸的通風道地圖，手寫註記說風道能傳話，也能送風。賀川提議利用自己濫用的網路廣播真相。見夏猶豫後，在沈漫監督下讓他試。賀川聲音從通風道傳出，念出失蹤孩子名字和掩蓋他們的假報告。警衛向聲音集中，大家趁機打開風口，救出剛才哭泣的孩子。一隻小手從黑暗伸出，緊緊握住見夏。

The rescued child was calm once Shen Man spoke to him, but he would not release Jianxia's hand. He wore a badge marked TEST GROUP THIRTEEN. “Did someone hurt you?” she asked. He shook his head and pointed toward the tower. “They told us our families had abandoned us.” He Chuan bowed his head. Jianxia did not forgive him then. Forgiveness, she knew, was not a reward he could demand for one brave act. Yet he had helped undo part of the harm he caused. As they climbed toward the tower, he walked at the rear without complaint. At the top waited a door covered with photographs from Jianxia's childhood. Shen Man tried to soothe the rescued child by humming a tune he recognized.

被救的孩子在沈漫安撫後很平靜，卻一直抓著見夏的手。他戴著「第十三測試組」的徽章。「有人傷害你嗎？」見夏問。孩子搖頭，指向高塔：「他們說家人把我們丟下了。」賀川垂首。見夏當下沒有原諒他，因為原諒不是做一件勇敢的事就能索取的獎賞，但他至少開始修復自己造成的傷害。眾人往高塔爬，賀川安靜走在最後。頂端有扇門，貼滿見夏童年的照片。 沈漫輕哼孩子熟悉的旋律，試著安撫他。
