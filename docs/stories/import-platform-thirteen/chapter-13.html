車廂審判

The Carriage Trial

第十三站台 · 第 13 / 25 章 · 5000–6000 · 英中對照 · 校訂中

用互動版查詞、朗讀與收藏

The next train arrived with its shutters closed. Inside, wooden benches formed a courtroom, and the passengers were told to decide which traveler would be left behind. A clock increased its tempo whenever someone raised a hand. Above the judge's chair hung cosmetics advertisements and a portrait of a stylish woman smiling beside a jar of crackers. He Chuan called the scene a terrible attempt at glamour. Jianxia noticed an electrician repairing cables under the bench, apparently oblivious to the trial. When she approached him, he whispered that every vote altered the station's power supply. The judge wanted more than a victim; it wanted the passengers to choose which memories would continue to exist.

下一班車的遮板全數關閉，車內木椅排成法庭，乘客被迫投票決定誰留下。有人舉手，時鐘的節奏就變快。法官椅上方掛著化妝品廣告，一名時髦女人微笑站在餅乾罐旁。賀川說這是糟糕的華麗舞台。見夏注意到有電工在座椅下修線，似乎不理會審判。她靠近時，電工悄聲說每張票都會改變車站電源。法官要的不是單一受害者，而是乘客選擇哪些記憶可以繼續存在。

A woman accused He Chuan of spreading false stories. He accepted her contention, acknowledging the harm he had once caused, but refused to claim he deserved death. Another passenger said the medical student was reliant on procedures and would freeze without instructions. Shen Man answered that procedures were meant to protect people, not replace judgment. The judge threatened to humiliate everyone who spoke out of turn. Jianxia saw fear linger in their faces. She also noticed an eyelash caught on the judge's desk, too real for the otherwise flawless wooden figure. “Someone made this trial from a person's memories,” she said. “We must find out whose.”

一名女人指控賀川散播假消息。賀川承認她的爭論有根據，也承認曾造成傷害，卻不認為自己該死。另一位乘客說沈漫太依賴程序，沒有指示就動不了。沈漫回答程序是為了保護人，不是取代判斷。法官威脅羞辱插話者。見夏看著恐懼停留在每張臉上，又注意到法官桌上一根睫毛，真實得和無瑕木偶不相稱。「這場審判是用真人記憶做的，」她說，「我們得查出是誰。」

The walls showed scenic countryside photographs that changed to images of factories, then to livestock crowded in filthy stalls. A sign promised improved sanitation, but the scene only became more oppressive. Shen Man found a hidden compartment with medical supplements, a reminder that someone had once tried to keep the passengers alive. Zhou Yan took apart a clock and found a suspension mechanism that could stop the voting display temporarily. “If we stabilize the timer,” he said, “they cannot rush us into a decision.” Jianxia helped him hold the springs while He Chuan asked witnesses to describe what they had actually seen, rather than what the judge told them to believe.

車壁風景照片從田園變成工廠，再變成擠滿牲畜的骯髒圈舍。標示承諾改善衛生，畫面卻越來越壓迫。沈漫在暗格找到營養補充品，提醒眾人曾有人努力維持乘客生命。周硯拆開時鐘，發現可暫停投票顯示的懸吊機構。「如果穩住計時器，」他說，「它就不能逼我們倉促決定。」見夏幫他按住彈簧，賀川則逐一詢問證人真正看見什麼，而不是法官要求相信什麼。

Evidence accumulated slowly. One passenger had heard an argument before the train departed. Another had seen an engineer leave through the final carriage. A third remembered a stranger using a magnifying glass to inspect the ticket numbers. The judge tried to magnify the smallest contradictions into proof of guilt. Jianxia refused to let those differences decide a person's fate. “Different memories of one event do not automatically make anyone a liar,” she told the carriage. Outside the windows, another train paced them, its passengers identical but motionless. The voting numbers kept climbing despite nobody touching the buttons. “The system is voting for us,” Zhou Yan said.

證據慢慢累積：一位乘客聽過出發前的爭吵，另一位看見工程師從末節車廂離開，第三人記得陌生人拿放大鏡查看票號。法官試圖把最小的矛盾放大成有罪證明。見夏拒絕讓記憶差異決定命運。「同一件事記法不同，不自動代表有人撒謊。」窗外另一列車與他們並行，乘客長得一樣卻全都靜止。即使沒人按鍵，投票數仍持續上升。「系統在替我們投票，」周硯說。

An alarm warned of imminent failure. He Chuan opened the emergency shutter and found the hidden cable feeding false votes into the machine. The electrician finally looked up. “I was told not to interfere,” he said. Jianxia asked whether he wanted to spend the rest of his life obeying. Slowly he reached for a tool and cut the false connection. The numbers stopped at eleven against one. The single dissenting vote was Jianxia's, registered earlier when she refused to condemn anyone. The judge declared that the trial had no majority because the majority had never been real. His wooden face cracked, revealing a frightened child beneath it.

警報預告即將故障，賀川打開緊急遮板，找到把假票數送進機器的電纜。電工終於抬頭：「他們說不准插手。」見夏問他是否願意一輩子只是服從。他慢慢拿起工具，切斷假訊號。票數停在十一比一，唯一的反對票是見夏先前拒絕定罪時留下的。法官宣布審判沒有有效多數，因為多數根本不是真的。木臉碎裂，露出底下受驚的小孩。

The child stared at the floor, waiting to be punished for losing. Jianxia crouched beside him instead. “You don't have to judge anyone,” she said. He looked at her, astonished that the sentence could be true. The courtroom benches folded into ordinary train seats, and the windows showed a platform drenched in red rain. Zhou Yan held Jianxia's hand as the doors opened. “If the system can force a vote,” he said, “it can probably force a memory too.” Jianxia thought of the woman whose eyelash had been left on the judge's desk. Somewhere, somebody had once been this frightened child. She wanted to find them before the machine erased their last trace.

孩子垂頭等著因失敗受罰，見夏卻蹲下來說：「你不必審判任何人。」他抬頭，驚訝這句話也能是真的。木椅折成普通列車座，窗外露出被紅雨浸透的月台。周硯牽著見夏的手走向車門。「如果系統能逼人投票，」他說，「多半也能逼人記得某件事。」見夏想起法官桌上的睫毛，某個地方曾有人真的是這個受驚孩子。她想在機器抹去最後痕跡前找到對方。
