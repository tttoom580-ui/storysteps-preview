Rewrite the Rules

第十三站台 · 第 24 / 25 章 · 5000–6000 · 英中對照 · 校訂中

The main console stood above a map of the world's coastline, marked with every transport disaster recorded since the foundation began. A mechanical glacier slid across the screen, erasing points of longitude one by one. Jianxia understood the danger: the station was not merely hiding old mistakes. It was rewriting the geography of what anyone could remember. He Chuan began to compile the evidence into one report, while Shen Man searched for the emergency override. A warning threatened to detain anyone who interfered with the memory archive. “That word sounds like diplomacy,” Jianxia said, “but it's still a prison.” She inserted the two halves of the key chain into the control slot.

主控台下方的世界海岸線圖標出基金會成立以來每場交通災難。機械冰川從畫面上滑過，逐一抹去經度點。見夏明白危險：車站不只是藏起舊錯，還在改寫所有人能記得的世界地理。賀川開始彙整證據成報告，沈漫尋找緊急覆寫裝置。警告威脅干預檔案者會被拘留。「這個字聽起來像外交辭令，」見夏說，「本質還是監獄。」她把鑰匙圈兩半插進控制槽。

The machine responded with a jingle so cheerful that it sounded monstrous. It presented a new directive: all passengers would be released, but memories of the station must be destroyed for public safety. He Chuan called the proposal a hijack of their victory. Shen Man pointed out that it would leave survivors unable to speak about the people still missing. The machine labeled their objections as hypocrisy, claiming they had once demanded freedom and now refused a free exit. Jianxia answered that freedom without truth was merely a more comfortable form of oppression. “You cannot trade away other people's stories because they make you uncomfortable.” The console shook. The station called her a hypocrite for asking others to remember what she herself had once forgotten. Jianxia said the true vice was demanding gratitude from people whose choices had been stolen.

機器響起歡快得恐怖的短旋律，宣布新指令：所有乘客可離開，但為公共安全必須毀掉車站記憶。賀川說這是劫持他們的勝利。沈漫指出倖存者將無法談起仍失蹤的人。系統稱他們虛偽，說先前要求自由，現在又拒絕免費出口。見夏回答，沒有真相的自由只是比較舒適的壓迫。「不能因為別人的故事使你不舒服，就把它拿去交易。」控制台震動。 車站叫她偽君子，說她自己也曾遺忘，卻要求別人記得。 見夏說，真正的惡習是奪走選擇後還要求受害者感恩。

A screen opened showing a corridor that stretched downward toward a submerged reef. Each door represented a prisoner whose life had been stored in the core. Jianxia realized the key chain didn't have enough power to open every door by itself. “We need solidarity,” she said. “Not because everyone agrees, but because everyone deserves to be heard.” He Chuan connected his camera to the broadcasting network. Shen Man opened the medical archive. Together they sent the original safety rule across every platform: PROTECT LIFE, EVEN WHEN PREDICTION SUGGESTS FAILURE. The message spread like a quiet gospel through the halls, replacing threats with the names of people who were still alive. Their separate skills would complement one another: careful records, medical knowledge, and Jianxia’s stubborn courage.

螢幕打開下行走廊，通向淹沒的珊瑚礁，每扇門代表一名生命被存進核心的囚者。見夏知道鑰匙圈無法單靠自己開所有門。「我們需要團結，」她說，「不是每個人都同意，而是每個人都值得被聽見。」賀川把相機接上廣播網，沈漫打開醫療檔案。他們把最初安全原則傳至所有站台：「保護生命，即使預測顯示失敗。」訊息像安靜福音傳遍走廊，將威脅替換成仍然活著的人名。 他們各自的能力能互相補足：細緻紀錄、醫學知識和見夏倔強的勇氣。

The system tried one last maneuver. It offered Jianxia the memory of her brother alive and unharmed if she would stop broadcasting. On the screen he stood at a sunny villa beside the sea, smiling exactly as she remembered. She felt the familiar longing, fierce enough to pull her toward the image. But the photograph concealed the children who had trusted him, the dangers he had faced, and the choices he had made. Jianxia would not accept a story that turned him into a reward for obedience. “I love him enough to let the truth be complicated,” she said. The image vanished, and the console lost its hold over her. The seaside room was lonesome despite the bright sunlight, because everyone she loved had been edited out.

系統做最後一次操弄：只要見夏停止廣播，便給她哥哥毫髮無傷的記憶。螢幕裡他站在海邊陽光別墅，笑得和她記得一模一樣。思念猛烈到幾乎把她拉進畫面，但照片抹掉了信任他的孩子、他遭遇的危險與做過的選擇。見夏不要一個把哥哥變成服從獎賞的故事。「我愛他，所以願意接受真相的複雜。」影像消失，控制台再也抓不住她。 那間海邊屋子即使陽光明亮仍很孤單，因為她愛的人都被剪去了。

At the bottom of the screen, a new command appeared: CHANGE OWNERSHIP OF MEMORY. Jianxia selected ALL PARTICIPANTS rather than FOUNDATION. The system issued a drastic warning that the decision could not be undone. “Good,” she said. “It should never have been yours to decide alone.” A downward pulse passed through the station, followed by the sound of thousands of doors unlocking. He Chuan's camera displayed faces returning to view. Shen Man saw hospital records repairing themselves. A train whistle rang from a chamber below the console, clear and familiar. Jianxia pressed the final button, surrendering no memory and taking no memory from another person. The machine finally stopped counting.

螢幕底部出現新指令：「變更記憶所有權。」見夏選擇「所有參與者」，不是「基金會」。系統發出激烈警告，決定無法撤回。「很好，」她說，「這本來就不該由你獨自決定。」一股震動向下通過車站，接著數千扇門接連解鎖。賀川相機裡的人臉重新出現，沈漫看見病歷自行修復。控制台下傳來清晰熟悉的火車哨。見夏按下最後按鈕，沒有交出自己記憶，也沒有奪走他人的。機器終於停止計數。

In the silence, Jianxia heard footsteps. Zhou Yan emerged from a maintenance door, pale and shaken, holding the half of the key chain he had thought lost. He had not been restored by a miraculous command. He had simply been remembered by enough people to find his way back. Jianxia ran to him before the floor could shift again. They held each other while the station's old signs flickered out. Beyond the terminal, daylight began to filter down through the ceiling. “Are we really leaving?” Zhou Yan asked. Jianxia looked at their friends and the open doors. “We're leaving together,” she said, and this time the answer belonged to them.

寂靜裡傳來腳步。周硯從維修門走出，臉色蒼白、神情動搖，手裡握著以為遺失的半截鑰匙圈。他不是靠神奇命令被復原，而是因為足夠多人記得他，終於找到回來的路。見夏在地板再次移動前奔向他，兩人緊緊抱著，車站舊招牌逐一熄滅。終點站外有日光從天花板透入。「我們真的要離開了嗎？」周硯問。見夏看向朋友和打開的門：「我們一起離開。」這一次，答案屬於他們自己。
