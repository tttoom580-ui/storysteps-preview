交換秘密

A Trade in Secrets

第十三站台 · 第 10 / 25 章 · 5000–6000 · 英中對照 · 校訂中

The red corridor ended in a marketplace built from memories. A pickpocket in a green coat offered to steal the worst thing a person remembered, charging only a smaller secret in return. A vendor sold celery in a cement bowl. Another kept a glass crocodile alive with a hidden motor, claiming it could calculate a customer's fate through arithmetic. A large sign called the market a confederation of equal traders, but Jianxia noticed that those with fewer secrets received fewer choices. “That's not equality,” Shen Man said. “It's injustice dressed up as bargaining.” A bell rang, and every stallholder stopped talking at precisely the same moment. Their silence was more frightening than the sales pitches.

紅色走廊盡頭是一座記憶市集。綠衣扒手願替人偷走最痛苦的記憶，只收一個較小的秘密。攤販把芹菜放在水泥碗裡賣，另一人展示電動玻璃鱷魚，宣稱能用算術算命。大招牌說這裡是平等商人的聯盟，可見夏發現秘密少的人選項也少。「這不是平等，」沈漫說，「是不公披上交易外衣。」鐘聲響起，所有攤販在同一秒住口。那份寂靜比叫賣聲還可怕。

The market judge announced a crackdown on travelers hiding information. Anyone who failed to disclose a secret would incur a penalty: the loss of a cherished memory. He Chuan attempted a joke, but the judge's face remained severe. “They are using restraint as a weapon,” Zhou Yan observed. “We can't buy our way out, and refusal is punishable.” Jianxia studied a dual-sided scale on the judge's desk. One side measured the weight of spoken confessions, the other the weight of remembered pain. The scales could never equalize, because the market valued suffering differently depending on who owned it. “The measure itself is broken,” Jianxia said. A sharp voice behind her asked whether she intended to challenge the judge.

市集法官宣布取締隱瞞資訊的旅客，拒絕坦白者將付出失去珍貴記憶的代價。賀川試圖開玩笑，法官表情仍冷硬。「它拿自制當武器，」周硯說，「我們付不起代價，拒絕又受罰。」見夏研究法官桌上的雙面天平，一側秤說出的秘密，一側秤回憶的痛楚；兩邊永遠無法平衡，因為市集依秘密主人不同替痛苦標價。「衡量方法根本壞了，」見夏說。背後尖銳聲音問她是否打算挑戰法官。

A woman stepped forward wearing an awesome golden mask. She introduced herself as the keeper of the city's oldest faction. Her followers claimed that truth should be preserved at any cost; a rival faction believed merciful lies were necessary for survival. The argument intrigued Jianxia, though the woman's polished speech was clearly meant to lure them into taking sides. “Neither group is asking who controls the scales,” she replied. “And neither group is asking permission from the people whose memories you trade.” The woman called her obstinate. He Chuan whispered that obstinacy was becoming Jianxia's most reliable survival skill. The judge ordered them to speak one secret each.

一名戴著令人驚嘆的金色面具的女人走出來，自稱城中最古老派系的守護者。她的追隨者主張真相不計代價也該保存；敵對派系則認為仁慈謊言是生存必要。見夏被辯論吸引，卻看得出優美說詞是在引誘他們站隊。「兩邊都沒問誰控制天平，」她回答，「也沒問被交易記憶的人願不願意。」女人說她頑固。賀川低聲道，頑固快成見夏最可靠的求生技能了。法官命令每人說出一個秘密。

Shen Man spoke first. She had once withheld a diagnosis from a friend because she feared causing panic. He Chuan admitted that he had published a story based on an incomplete source, harming an innocent family. Zhou Yan stared at his hands. “I remember watching a child fall from a platform,” he said. “I tried to help. I believe that child was you.” Jianxia felt her legs stagger under her. All at once she remembered a tunnel, a red emergency lamp, and a boy shouting that he would not leave her. “I thought my brother saved me,” she whispered. “Maybe both of you were there.” The scale on the judge's desk trembled, unable to register a shared memory.

沈漫先說，她曾因害怕朋友恐慌而隱瞞診斷。賀川承認自己根據不完整消息報導，傷害了無辜家庭。周硯望著雙手。「我記得看見一個孩子從月台掉下去，」他說，「我試著救她。我想那孩子是妳。」見夏膝蓋一軟。隧道、紅色緊急燈、喊著絕不留下她的男孩忽然在腦中浮現。「我一直以為是哥哥救我，」她低聲說，「也許你們都在。」法官桌上的天平震動，無法判定一段共享記憶。

A secret belonged to more than one person. Jianxia seized on that flaw. “If pain can be shared,” she told the judge, “you cannot decide its price from a single confession.” The market's animated figures began to protest. One cracked open to reveal a smaller puppet that looked exactly like the original. He Chuan photographed the scene while Shen Man studied the unstable scale. Jianxia took Zhou Yan's hand and repeated the sentence from her childhood card: I will never leave him here again. The judge demanded proof, but the scales shifted by themselves. The market was built on isolated memories; two people remembering together made its arithmetic impossible. Lights failed from stall to stall.

秘密原來能屬於不只一人。見夏立刻抓住漏洞。「如果痛苦可以共享，」她告訴法官，「你就不能只憑一個人的自白來訂價。」市集裡的活動人偶開始抗議，其中一個裂開，露出更小卻相同的木偶。賀川拍照，沈漫檢查失衡天平。見夏牽住周硯的手，重複童年卡片上的話：「我不會再把他留在這裡。」法官要她證明，天平卻自行傾斜。市集建立在孤立的記憶上，兩人共同記住同一件事，便讓它的算術失效。燈光一攤接著一攤熄滅。

As the market collapsed into darkness, the masked woman stepped aside and pointed to an exit. “You have found a route that was not priced,” she said. “But be punctual: the train is arriving.” Jianxia refused to run until everyone was accounted for. They reached the platform just as the final carriage opened. Behind the window, the glass crocodile stared at them with a human eye. The woman in the golden mask raised one finger to her lips, warning them to remain silent. Jianxia turned to Zhou Yan. He looked relieved, yet grief shadowed his expression. She understood that his confession had not been complete. The boy who had tried to save her might still be trapped somewhere ahead.

市集陷入黑暗，金面具女人讓開出口。「你們找到一條沒有標價的路，」她說，「但要準時，列車快到了。」見夏堅持先確認大家都在，才一起跑。最後車廂開門時，他們剛好抵達月台。窗後的玻璃鱷魚竟以人類眼睛凝望他們。金面具女人豎起手指要他們保持安靜。見夏看向周硯，他似乎鬆了口氣，神色仍蒙著悲傷。她明白他的告白還沒結束——那個試圖救她的男孩，或許至今還被困在前方。
