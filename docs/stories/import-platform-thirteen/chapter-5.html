消失的檢票員

The Vanishing Ticket Inspector

第十三站台 · 第 5 / 25 章 · 5000–6000 · 英中對照

用互動版查詞、朗讀與收藏

The corridor ended in a waiting hall decorated for a celebration that had never happened. A bottle of champagne stood on an untouched table beside a bowl of chestnut shells. Someone had hung cheap party hats from every chair. A faded newspaper described the sudden breakdown of a telecommunications network during a foundation gala. The only photograph showed a ticket inspector standing among guests, his face scratched away. Jianxia searched for the janitor but found nobody. “He wants us to notice the missing face,” Zhou Yan said. “Or he's trying to make us forget the people beside it.” Shen Man picked up a metal accessory from the floor: an inspector's whistle with an unfamiliar number scratched into the back.

走廊盡頭是間為未曾舉行的慶典裝飾的候車大廳。未開封的香檳放在桌上，旁邊是裝著栗子殼的碗，廉價派對帽掛滿椅背。褪色報紙記錄基金會晚宴當晚電信網路突然癱瘓，唯一照片裡的檢票員被刮掉了臉。見夏找不到清潔工。「他想讓我們注意那張缺臉，」周硯說，「或者想讓我們忽略旁邊的人。」沈漫從地上撿起金屬配件：檢票哨，背面刻著陌生編號。

He Chuan found a service bypass behind the vending machine. It led to a cramped control room where a screen displayed the nucleus of the station's transport system. A red message flashed: WARNING: INSPECTOR UNAVAILABLE. Jianxia felt cowardly for wanting to hide behind someone who understood the machine. But Zhou Yan refused to approach the controls until they examined the room. One monitor showed rows of people asleep, another showed live footage of their empty platform. “Those are not cameras,” Shen Man said. “They're memories.” She touched a screen and heard a familiar snore: her father's, from a childhood afternoon she had not thought about in years.

賀川在販賣機後找到勤務旁道，通向狹窄控制室。螢幕顯示車站運輸系統的核心，紅色訊息閃爍：「警告：檢票員無法使用。」見夏因為想躲在懂機器的人後面而覺得自己怯懦，但周硯堅持先查看整間房。某個監視器裡躺著成排睡眠中的人，另一台則播放空無一人的月台。「這不是攝影機，」沈漫說，「是記憶。」她碰了一下螢幕，竟聽到父親多年以前午睡的鼾聲。

On the floor lay a tube of hand lotion and a wounded bird. The bird was not a fowl from any species Jianxia recognized: its beak was silver, and thin wires moved beneath its feathers. It tapped a rhythm like rap against the tile. He Chuan jokingly called it the station's mascot, but a slash of red light crossed the wall as soon as he tried to photograph it. The machine issued a monetary penalty against unauthorized observation. “Their mentality is simple,” the reporter said. “Everything that looks personal is treated as company property.” Jianxia took the bird into her hands. Its heart beat weakly, and the room filled with an ache she couldn't explain. The bird recovered a little vigor when she wrapped it in her scarf.

地板上有條護手乳液和一隻受傷的鳥。牠不是見夏認得的任何家禽：喙是銀色，羽毛下有細線移動。牠用嘴在磁磚上敲著饒舌般的節奏。賀川開玩笑說這是車站吉祥物，剛舉相機，紅光便劃過牆面，機器判定未授權觀察須繳罰金。「它的思維模式很單純，」賀川說，「所有私人的東西都當成公司資產。」見夏捧起鳥，感覺牠心跳微弱，房內忽然湧起難以解釋的酸楚。 見夏用圍巾裹住牠後，鳥兒恢復了些許活力。

The control system offered to unveil the inspector's location if they accepted one condition: surrender one memory of a person they loved. Zhou Yan said no immediately. “We've learned that these bargains do not end where the wording ends.” He sounded unusually hoarse. Jianxia remembered the angry expression he had worn in the pharmacy, and suspected the question had evoked something painful. She suggested finding the inspector without the machine's permission. Shen Man found another hallway behind the control cabinet, and He Chuan located the lock with his flashlight. Inside was a row of uniforms without bodies. The nameplates contained dates rather than names. The final uniform carried a note: THE NEXT INSPECTOR HAS ALREADY ARRIVED.

控制系統說只要交出一段關於所愛之人的記憶，就會揭露檢票員位置。周硯立刻拒絕：「我們已經知道，這些交易的代價不止寫出來那些。」他的嗓音異常沙啞。見夏想起他在藥局的憤怒神情，猜測問題勾起了痛處。她提議不經許可找人。沈漫發現控制櫃後另一條走廊，賀川用手電照出門鎖。裡面掛著整排沒有身體的制服，名牌寫的不是姓名，而是日期。最後一件制服留著紙條：「下一任檢票員已經抵達。」

An announcement reported that the inspector had been transferred, not killed. The station promised that no guest would be harmed during the transfer. Jianxia distrusted the careful language. “We can bypass the trade,” she said, “by letting the bird identify his signal.” The creature tapped three times, then pointed its beak toward a brass handle. Behind it they found a hidden receiver, which played a recording of the janitor saying, “Do not confuse the inspector with the person wearing the uniform.” Before Jianxia could replay it, the receiver dissolved into gray dust. Zhou Yan looked positively moody. He had been calm through impossible windows and disappearing walls, but the mention of the inspector had left him afraid.

廣播報告檢票員已被調職，而非死亡，並保證調動過程不傷害乘客。見夏不信那種措辭。「我們可以繞過交易，」她說，「讓鳥辨識他的訊號。」機械鳥敲了三下，鳥喙指向銅把手。後面藏著接收器，傳來清潔工的錄音：「不要把檢票員和穿制服的人混為一談。」見夏還沒能重播，接收器便化成灰塵。周硯的情緒明顯陰沉。面對不可思議的窗戶和消失的牆他都很冷靜，唯獨檢票員讓他害怕。

They returned to the waiting hall. The champagne had vanished, the chestnut shells now formed a neat circle, and the old newspaper had changed its headline to ANNIVERSARY OF THE FIRST RESCUE. At the center of the room stood the janitor, watching them. “A rescue from what?” Jianxia demanded. He did not answer. Instead he pointed to the bird resting in her hands. Its damaged wing opened, revealing an emblem matching the missing inspector's number. “You were never supposed to care for it,” the janitor said. “The first test is almost over, and compassion has changed the result.” The next train approached with a deep metallic cry. The bird suddenly spoke in her brother's voice: “At the sixth door, don't believe the light.”

他們回到候車廳，香檳不見了，栗子殼排列成整齊的圓圈；舊報紙標題竟變成「第一次救援週年」。清潔工站在大廳中央看著大家。「從什麼地方救出來？」見夏追問，他不回答，只指向她手裡的鳥。受傷的翅膀展開，露出與失蹤檢票員相同的編號。「妳本來不該照顧牠，」清潔工說，「第一場測試快結束了，妳的憐憫改變結果。」下一班列車發出金屬長鳴，機械鳥忽然用哥哥的聲音說：「第六扇門前，不要相信光。」
