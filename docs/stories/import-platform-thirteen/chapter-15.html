The Message from the Dead

第十三站台 · 第 15 / 25 章 · 5000–6000 · 英中對照 · 校訂中

Beyond the school stood a silent theater where a premiere had been scheduled for a film about the research foundation. The seats were empty, but the floor glittered with broken glass. A stale cigar lay beneath the director's chair. He Chuan searched the projection booth and found a tape labeled FINAL MESSAGE, recorded by a notorious test participant years earlier. The tape began with a tedious list of safety procedures, then switched to a tired voice describing children trapped in an underground ward. “We did not enter this place willingly,” the speaker said. “We were brought here because someone believed memories could be separated from living people.” Jianxia sat down, desperate for the next sentence.

學校外是座無聲劇院，原本要首映關於研究基金會的電影。座位全空，地板閃著碎玻璃，一支發霉的雪茄掉在導演椅下。賀川在放映室找到標示「最後留言」的錄音帶，錄於多年前，說話者是聲名狼藉的實驗參與者。開頭是冗長安全程序，接著疲憊聲音敘述被困在地下病房的孩子。「我們不是自願進來，」對方說，「有人相信記憶能與活人分離，才把我們帶來。」見夏坐下，焦急等下一句。

The voice described how the staff had tried to diversify the trials, creating stations for fear, longing, loyalty, and grief. Some researchers had excelled at keeping children alive but showed little interest in what survival cost them. Others had treated suffering as a scientific fascination. A senior statesman visited the project, spoke publicly about compassion, then demanded results at any price. “Our true foe,” the recording said, “was not one cruel man but a system that rewarded people for ignoring the people in front of them.” Jianxia thought of the hotel, the school, and the market. Their horrors had different shapes, but the principle behind them was the same. The researchers could excel in technical work while failing completely at kindness.

聲音說工作人員把試驗多樣化，建立恐懼、思念、忠誠與悲傷的車站。有些研究員擅長維持孩子生命，卻不在乎生存付出什麼；另一些則把痛苦當科學迷戀。一名資深政治家參觀時公開談憐憫，私下卻要求不計代價交成果。「我們真正的敵人，」錄音說，「不是一個殘暴的人，而是會獎賞人忽略眼前生命的制度。」見夏想起旅館、學校與市集，恐怖的外形不同，底下原理卻一致。 那些研究員可以在技術工作上表現優異，卻在善待人這件事上徹底失敗。

The tape continued with the account of a desperate descent into the core laboratory. Food had run low, and some children suffered from starvation. The staff insisted that outright escape was impossible because the building existed across more than one point in time. When riots began, guards used threats of slaughter to control frightened families. The speaker had refused to cooperate and was left in darkness with innumerable recordings of people calling for help. “I mastered the system's language,” he said, “but never gained mastery over my own fear.” The confession sounded painfully honest. Jianxia could hear the strain in every breath. One map divided the simulated world into a northern and a southern hemisphere, both under the same sealed roof.

錄音繼續描述通往核心實驗室的絕望下行。食物不足，有些孩子遭受飢餓。員工堅稱完全逃離不可能，因為建築同時存在於不同時間點。暴動開始後，警衛以屠殺威脅控制家屬。說話者拒絕合作，被留在黑暗中，周圍是無數求救錄音。「我學會了系統的語言，」他說，「卻從沒掌握自己的恐懼。」這句坦白真實得令人心痛，見夏能聽出每一次呼吸裡的疲憊。 有張地圖把模擬世界分成南北兩個半球，實際卻同在一座封閉屋頂下。

The speaker warned listeners not to let the system seduce them with the promise of total closure. “There is no clean ending for a wound made by many hands,” he said. The proper task was to preserve the names of those harmed and find a way to dispose of the machine without destroying its prisoners. Jianxia heard a faint sound behind the voice: someone was tapping a little tune on a metal rail. She knew the rhythm. Her brother had used it to call her home when they played in the neighborhood. Suddenly the narrator spoke the childhood nickname only her family knew. Jianxia covered her mouth to keep from shouting.

說話者警告，不要讓系統以徹底了結一切的承諾誘惑人。「那種由無數雙手造成的傷口，不可能有乾淨結局。」真正該做的是保留受害者姓名，處理掉機器時又不能毀掉被困者。見夏聽見錄音背後有人在金屬欄杆敲小調，那是哥哥小時候叫她回家的節奏。接著，錄音者竟說出只有家人知道的乳名。見夏摀住嘴，忍住大叫。

“I could not save everyone,” her brother's voice said. “But I learned how to leave a path through the machine. Each rule is a warning disguised as an order. Read what it forbids, then ask what it is afraid you might do.” He paused. “Jianxia, if you are hearing this, don't come looking for the version of me that still smiles in your memories. Look for the people who survived because you helped them.” A crash interrupted the tape. The recording ended on a single whispered phrase: CORE STATION. Zhou Yan lowered his head. “He taught me the same thing,” he admitted. “I just couldn't remember until now.”

「我救不了所有人，」哥哥的聲音說，「但我學會在機器裡留下路。每條規則都是偽裝成命令的警告。讀它禁止什麼，再問它害怕你做什麼。」他停頓：「見夏，如果妳聽到這段，別追逐記憶裡還會微笑的我。去找那些因妳伸出援手而活下來的人。」一聲碰撞打斷錄音，最後只剩低語：「核心站。」周硯垂下頭：「他也這樣教我，直到現在我才想起。」

Jianxia looked around the empty theater, imagining the people who had once come to watch a triumphant account of progress. Perhaps the film had never been shown because truth would have ruined the celebration. She put the recording in her pocket. “We're not going to perform their ending,” she said. He Chuan nodded and turned off the projector, leaving the screen blank. The darkness no longer felt like surrender. Outside, train brakes screamed in the tunnel. A light appeared where the screen had been, forming an exit with the words CORE ACCESS: RESTRICTED. Zhou Yan looked at Jianxia, and she knew the next part would demand more than cleverness. It would demand the courage to keep remembering.

見夏看著空蕩劇院，想像曾有人來看宣揚進步的成功電影。也許首映從未發生，因為真相會毀了慶典。她把錄音帶收入口袋。「我們不會演出他們寫好的結局，」她說。賀川點頭，關掉投影，銀幕變白。黑暗這次不再像投降。外頭隧道響起剎車尖聲，銀幕原位出現光門，寫著：「核心通道：限制進入。」周硯看向見夏，她知道接下來需要的不只是聰明，還需要不斷記住的勇氣。
