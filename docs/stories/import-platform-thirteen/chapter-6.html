The First Exit

第十三站台 · 第 6 / 25 章 · 5000–6000 · 英中對照 · 校訂中

At the sixth door, white light poured across the tracks so brightly that everyone raised a hand to shield their eyes. Above the threshold, an airtight glass case displayed a silver fin from a mechanical fish. A notice promised that anyone who could attain the final platform would regain one lost memory. “That is precisely the sort of promise we should despise,” Jianxia said. Shen Man inspected the tracks and found a thin layer of vapor moving against the wind. Beyond the brightness lay a platform filled with merchandise: toys, coats, medicines, even warm bowls of curry. Each item was priced in minutes of a passenger's life. He Chuan reached for his wallet, then realized that it contained photographs instead of money.

第六扇門前，刺眼白光灑滿軌道，大家舉手遮眼。門檻上方的密封玻璃盒展示著機械魚的銀色魚鰭。公告保證抵達最後月台者能取回一段遺失記憶。「這正是我們該鄙視的那種承諾，」見夏說。沈漫檢查軌道，發現薄薄的水蒸氣逆風移動。光後是一個滿是商品的月台：玩具、外套、藥物，甚至熱騰騰的咖哩，每件商品都以乘客生命分鐘數計價。賀川伸手拿錢包，才發現裡面只剩照片。

A recording introduced a smiling congresswoman who had financed the station's earliest trials. She spoke about the esteem due to people who volunteered for scientific progress. Behind her stood an evergreen tree in an underground garden, a symbol of survival. Yet the tree shed metallic flakes, not leaves. Jianxia watched one flake drift onto the ground, where it unfolded into a tiny map. It showed two exits: one marked LIGHT and the other labeled RUBBISH. “A system that values us only as resources would never call its safe door rubbish,” she reasoned. Zhou Yan admitted that the observation matched something he half remembered. “Then the darker door is worth trying,” he said, though his hand shook.

錄音介紹一名微笑的女國會議員，她曾資助車站最早期試驗，談論自願參加科學進步者應得到的尊敬。她背後的地下花園長著一棵常青樹，象徵生存，但樹落下的是金屬薄片。見夏看著一片落地，竟展開成小地圖，上面有兩個出口：「光」與「垃圾」。「只把人當資源的系統，不會把真正安全的門叫垃圾，」她推理。周硯承認這讓他想起某件模糊的舊事。「那就試試暗門，」他說，手卻在發抖。

The light doorway began playing scenes of pleasant memories. Shen Man saw her mother cooking. He Chuan saw the newsroom before its closure. Jianxia saw her brother teaching her to swim, laughing as she tried to slay an imaginary sea monster. Every image tempted her to step closer. “This is a trap designed around longing,” she said. “It can keep us here through monotony rather than violence. Every day it shows us the same perfect day.” From behind the darkness came scraping and the smell of damp concrete. She remembered her brother's warning and turned away from the light. It cost her something to reject even a counterfeit image of his smile. Swimming had been their favorite pastime, long before any train had begun to haunt her nights.

光門開始播放美好回憶：沈漫看見母親做飯，賀川看見尚未關閉的報社，見夏則看見哥哥教她游泳，笑她假裝斬殺海怪。每幅畫面都誘使她靠近。「這是用思念設計的陷阱，」她說，「它不必動粗，只要讓我們在日復一日的單調裡沉溺，重播完美一天。」黑暗後傳來刮擦聲和潮濕水泥味。她記起哥哥的警告，轉身遠離亮光。即使明知笑容是假的，拒絕哥哥的影像仍令她心痛。 在列車纏上她的夜晚之前，游泳一直是兄妹最愛的消遣。

At the dark exit they found a storage room where broken objects had been piled almost to the ceiling. A sign said SPARE PARTS, but someone had crossed out the word spare and written PEOPLE. The mechanical bird hopped onto a crate and began tapping instructions. Its movements were sly, almost mischievous, but the pattern was precise. Zhou Yan translated: “Anything not required by the system may leave.” Shen Man pointed to the repeated safety rule on the wall. It contained three identical clauses, a strange redundancy for a machine obsessed with efficiency. Jianxia removed the clauses one by one from a magnetic board. Beneath them was a narrow release lever, polished by countless hands. The bird was free to roam among the shelves, though it always returned to Jianxia.

暗門後是堆滿損壞物品的倉庫，箱子幾乎頂到天花板。標示原寫「備用零件」，有人把「備用」劃掉，改成「人」。機械鳥跳上箱子，狡黠地敲打指令，節奏卻很精準。周硯翻譯：「系統不需要的，都可以離開。」沈漫指出牆上安全規則有三段一模一樣的重複文字，對講效率的機器來說非常多餘。見夏逐一拆下磁性板上的重複條款，下面露出狹窄的釋放拉桿，表面被無數雙手磨亮。 小鳥能在層架間自由漫遊，卻總回到見夏身邊。

Before she pulled it, the loudspeaker warned that a government official would inspect their conduct. “A congresswoman?” He Chuan muttered. “Or a ghost dressed for a speech?” The bird spread its wings as if impatient. Jianxia tried the lever, and the station announced: REQUEST DENIED. She tried again while saying the name written on the blank cup: the elderly woman had recovered it only an hour earlier. This time the system stalled. “It can't classify someone who has reclaimed her name,” Zhou Yan realized. The woman took the lever in both hands. The control room shook, and the artificial daylight vanished. In the dark, a real emergency lamp blinked on, revealing a service staircase to the street.

拉桿前廣播警告，有政府官員將檢查他們的行為。賀川嘀咕：「女議員？還是穿著講稿服裝的鬼？」機械鳥不耐地展翅。見夏拉動拉桿，系統拒絕。她再次嘗試，同時說出老婦人一小時前才找回的名字，機器突然停頓。「它無法歸類重新取回名字的人，」周硯明白了。老婦人雙手握住拉桿，控制室震動，人造白晝消失。真正的緊急燈在暗處亮起，照出通往街上的維修樓梯。

They climbed together, prepared for a final assault, but the last door opened quietly beside an all-night noodle shop. Rain fell onto ordinary asphalt. A man walking his dog asked whether they needed help. Jianxia almost cried at the normality of it. She had survived the first trial without sacrificing a companion, and she wanted to believe the ordeal was over. On a nearby television, a commentator discussed a solar eclipse scheduled for next month. Shen Man bought food because it was edible and not priced in years. Zhou Yan did not smile. “We have not escaped,” he said. “We've only learned how the station defines an exit.” In Jianxia's pocket, the black ticket was still warm.

他們一起往上爬，以為會迎來最後攻擊，頂端門卻安靜地開在深夜麵店旁。雨落在普通柏油路上，遛狗男子還問他們需不需要幫忙。見夏差點因平凡而哭。她沒有犧牲同伴，通過第一輪試煉，真希望一切已結束。附近電視正在討論下個月的日食。沈漫買了食物，因為它可以吃，而且不是拿壽命標價。周硯沒有笑。「我們沒有逃出去，」他說，「只是學會了車站對出口的定義。」見夏口袋裡，黑色車票仍帶著溫度。
