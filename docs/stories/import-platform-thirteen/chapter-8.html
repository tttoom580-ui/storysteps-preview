第二張車票

The Second Ticket

第十三站台 · 第 8 / 25 章 · 5000–6000 · 英中對照 · 校訂完成

The second ticket had a tiny nickel-colored mark in one corner. Shen Man studied it with a magnifying glass and found a date corresponding to the night of Jianxia's childhood accident. “Your kin were involved before any of us,” she said. Jianxia disliked hearing her family described as evidence. At the station, a spokesperson on a digital billboard announced a new government program to computerize transportation records, promising perfect safety. Behind the billboard, someone had scratched a different message into the wall: DO NOT EVACUATE UNTIL YOU KNOW WHO COUNTED YOU. A robust security guard marched past them and failed to notice the writing. When Jianxia called out, his face flickered like a damaged screen.

第二張票角落多了個鎳色小記號。沈漫用放大鏡看出日期，竟對應見夏童年出事那晚。「妳的親屬在我們之前就捲進來了，」她說。見夏不喜歡家人被當成證據。車站數位看板上一位發言人宣布電腦化運輸紀錄的新政府計畫，承諾絕對安全。看板後的牆卻刻著：「弄清是誰在計算人數之前，不要撤離。」強壯的警衛走過卻看不見文字。見夏叫他時，他的臉像壞掉的螢幕閃動。

Shen Man produced an antibiotic bottle she had taken from the pharmacy. Its label now listed a patient with pneumonia and directed doctors to refer any continuing nightmares to psychiatry. “They are rewriting diagnoses to make witnesses seem unreliable,” she said, irritable from a night without sleep. Zhou Yan asked whether she could disable the billboard's connection. She couldn't, but she knew someone who could. An astronomer from the university had studied old observatory computers, including a prototype designed to orient rescue teams without satellites. They reached him by phone. He warned that the system's original expenditure records had disappeared after the fire. “Someone paid a great deal to hide the project's real purpose,” he said.

沈漫拿出從藥局帶出的抗生素藥瓶，標籤竟寫著肺炎病人的資料，並要求醫師把持續惡夢轉介精神醫學科。「他們正在改寫診斷，好把目擊者說成不可靠，」一夜未睡的她煩躁地說。周硯問能否切斷看板連線。她辦不到，但認識一名研究舊天文台電腦的天文學家，他曾研究無衛星環境的救援導航原型機。電話接通後，對方表示大火之後專案支出紀錄全被抹掉。「有人花了大錢掩蓋真正目的。」

The ticket directed them to a museum with an exhibition on trade between East and West. Its old catalog used the word oriental on a display label, wording the curator had replaced with the more specific name of a culture. Jianxia noticed a sealed wardrobe beside the exhibit, too modern to belong among the antiques. He Chuan opened it and found four gray uniforms and a fifth garment bearing the station logo. Beneath the clothes lay a diagram of an imaginary sovereign city where every road led to one terminal. A voice asked visitors to choose their rank. “No,” Jianxia said. “We won't become citizens of a place that won't tell us its laws.” The wardrobe doors slammed shut.

車票指引他們前往一間東西方貿易博物館。舊型錄曾在標牌使用「東方的」這種籠統舊稱，館方已改寫成更精確的文化名稱。見夏發現展櫃旁有一座密封衣櫃，現代得不合時宜。賀川打開，裡面有四套灰制服和一件印有車站標誌的衣服。下面是虛構主權城市的地圖，每條路都通往同一座終點站。聲音要求訪客選擇階級。「不，」見夏說，「我們不當一座不肯公布法律的城市的公民。」衣櫃門猛然關上。

The museum lights dimmed, leaving only a red emergency strip on the floor. From another room came a recording of applause, as though an unseen audience approved their choice. He Chuan called it encouragement; Zhou Yan called it behavioral conditioning. Jianxia sat down for a minute of meditation, concentrating on the difference between real memories and fabricated comfort. She recalled the warmth of her brother's hand when he had helped her across a busy road. That memory contained fear as well as love. It could not be one of the station's polished advertisements. Then she heard a voice that belonged to no one in the room: “The sovereign has approved your second trial.” No unseen crowd had any right to applaud decisions made under a threat, she decided.

博物館燈光變暗，只剩地板紅色逃生燈。隔壁傳來預錄掌聲，彷彿看不見的觀眾贊同他們選擇。賀川說是鼓勵，周硯認為是行為制約。見夏坐下短暫冥想，試著分辨真實記憶與人造安慰。她想起哥哥曾牽她過繁忙道路，那份記憶有愛，也有恐懼，不可能是車站精心剪輯的廣告。接著，一個不屬於在場任何人的聲音說：「主權者已核准第二場試煉。」 她心想，看不見的觀眾根本無權為受脅迫做出的決定鼓掌。

Beyond the museum was a glass passage lined with photographs of survivors. Some pictures were decades old, others showed people who had entered the station only yesterday. Every face bore a faint silver dot. A new notice told visitors to choose between loyalty to blood relatives and duty to strangers. “They're trying to force a false choice,” Shen Man said. Her voice had regained its strength. Jianxia remembered the first trial, when saving the mechanical bird had opened a door. She refused to accept that kindness had to be selective. Together they marked every photograph with a pencil line, proving that the dot appeared on all of them. The control panel flashed ERROR: NO VALID DIVISION.

博物館後的玻璃通道排滿倖存者照片，有幾十年前的，也有昨天才進站的人，每張臉都帶著淡銀點。新公告要訪客選擇對血親忠誠，或對陌生人負責。「它在逼我們接受假的二選一，」沈漫說，聲音恢復堅定。見夏想起第一輪救機械鳥時打開的門，拒絕相信善意必須有所篩選。他們在所有照片旁做記號，證明每人都有銀點。控制面板閃出：「錯誤：無法有效分類。」

The glass beneath them became clear. Far below lay a second platform, filled with mirrors instead of passengers. A sign warned that only guests who could recognize themselves should enter. Jianxia wondered whether that meant their faces, their memories, or something harder to name. Zhou Yan held out the train-shaped key chain. He had split it into two pieces, keeping one and giving her the other. “In case the system makes us strangers again,” he said. She closed her fingers around the gift. The black ticket dissolved into a line of numbers on the glass. The passage opened, and their reflections went down the stairs before they did.

腳下玻璃變得透明，深處的第二個月台擺滿鏡子而非乘客。標語警告只有認得自己的人才能進。見夏不知道這指外貌、記憶，還是更難說明的東西。周硯遞來火車鑰匙圈，他已拆成兩半，一半自己留著。「萬一系統又把我們變成陌生人，」他說。見夏握緊禮物，黑票在玻璃上化成一排數字。通道打開，而他們的倒影竟先一步走下樓梯。
