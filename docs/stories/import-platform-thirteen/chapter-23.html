One Person Stays Behind

The rescued passengers moved toward the exits, but Jianxia turned back. Her friends knew she would. He Chuan offered to escort her to the core, while Shen Man insisted on carrying medical supplies. Jianxia refused to endanger them without warning. “You both have a way out,” she said. “Use it if you need to.” Then she climbed a spiral staircase toward the machine, guided by the faint vibration of the key chain. Outside a window, a comet crossed a black sky over an impossible city. Its light made the railing twinkle for an instant. Jianxia could not tell whether the sky was a memory or a view into another time, but she kept climbing. Their navigation would depend on remembered signals rather than the signs overhead.

被救乘客往出口移動，見夏卻折返，朋友們早知道她會這樣。賀川說要護送她回核心，沈漫堅持帶藥品同行。見夏不肯毫無告知就讓他們冒險：「你們有路能離開，需要時就走。」她循著鑰匙圈微弱震動，登上往機器的螺旋樓梯。窗外彗星掠過不可能城市的黑天，讓欄杆短暫閃爍。她不知道那是記憶還是另一時間的景象，只管繼續往上。 他們的導航必須靠記住的訊號，而不是頭頂標示。

A checkpoint stood halfway up. Its eccentric guard wore a velvet garment over a railway uniform and demanded that each examinee declare a purpose. Jianxia said she was searching for a person whose name had been deleted. The guard laughed sorrowfully. “The absence of a record is proof that nobody belongs to that name.” “It proves your records are incomplete,” she answered. He demanded a written outlook on the future, as if uncertainty were a defect. Jianxia pulled a worn paperback from her bag: her brother's childhood adventure book, carried for comfort since the day she learned he was missing. On its first page, he had written: The world is bigger than whatever a map can show. One poster called the guard a patriot for defending the city, though he defended only the machine.

樓梯半途有個檢查站，古怪警衛在鐵道制服外披著天鵝絨衣物，要求每名考生說明目的。見夏說要找名字遭刪除的人。警衛悲哀地笑：「沒紀錄就是無此人。」她回答：「只能證明你們紀錄不完整。」他要她寫未來展望，彷彿不確定是缺陷。見夏從背包拿出哥哥的舊平裝冒險書。知道哥哥失蹤後，她一直帶在身邊求安慰。扉頁寫著：「世界比任何地圖所能顯示的都大。」 某張海報把警衛稱作保衛城市的愛國者，其實他只是在保衛機器。

The guard tried to deter her by projecting a collision of trains inside the stairwell. Bodies flew across the screen, and a warning claimed that rescuing one person would cost thousands of lives. Jianxia recognized the manipulation: show the worst possible outcome and present it as certainty. She refused to let the image paralyze her. “You cannot know what everyone will do,” she said. “You only know what your old models predict.” A hidden speaker began to transmit the same warning in several languages. He Chuan broke its power line. The silence allowed Jianxia to hear footsteps above them, faint but unmistakably human.

警衛投出列車相撞影像試圖阻止她，人體飛過螢幕，警告稱救一人會害數千人死亡。見夏認出操弄方式：拿最壞可能說成必然。她不許影像使自己癱瘓。「你不知道每個人會怎麼做，」她說，「只知道舊模型預測。」隱藏喇叭開始多語傳送警告，賀川切斷電源。安靜下來後，見夏聽見上方腳步，微弱卻確實是人的。

At the top was a room full of people whose names had become subordinate entries beneath machine-generated labels. Jianxia recognized faces from the school and the hotel. Some looked ashamed of having survived, as though they were less worthy than those who had died. She thought of her brother and understood how sorrow could become another prison. A child peeked from behind a cabinet and handed her a folded map. “The boy you're looking for taught me how to hide,” the child said. “He never asked me to pay.” The map showed a maintenance route shaped like a spiral, ending beneath the core's main control panel. The faces were sorrowful, yet their willingness to help made the room less lonely.

頂端房間裡的人名都變成機器標籤下的次要紀錄。見夏認出學校和旅館裡的臉。有些人為存活羞愧，彷彿自己不如死者值得被愛。她想到哥哥，懂得悲傷也會變成監獄。孩子從櫃後偷看，遞給她折疊地圖：「妳要找的男孩教我怎麼躲，從沒要我付錢。」地圖標出螺旋形維修路，終點在核心主控台下方。 那些面孔很哀傷，願意相助的心意卻讓房間不再那麼孤獨。

They followed the map into a shaft barely wide enough for one person. Shen Man went first, carrying the small emergency lamp. Jianxia came after her, feeling every breath scrape against the metal walls. Beneath the controls they found a chamber filled with faded paper tickets. One was marked with Zhou Yan's handwriting, another with Jianxia's childhood nickname. Their names had survived on objects the system classified as worthless. He Chuan photographed each ticket while Shen Man read the faded dates. The pattern revealed that Zhou Yan had left signs across multiple timelines. He had not simply erased himself; he had distributed his identity into memories others still held.

他們沿圖鑽進僅能容一人通過的維修井，沈漫持緊急燈在前，見夏在後，呼吸似乎都磨著金屬壁。主控台下是裝滿褪色紙票的房間，一張寫周硯筆跡，一張有見夏童年乳名。名字竟保留在系統判定沒價值的物品上。賀川逐張拍攝，沈漫讀日期，拼出周硯在多條時間線留下記號的規律。他不只是抹去自己，而是把身分分散進其他人仍保有的記憶。

Jianxia held the two halves of the key chain together. Their edges fit perfectly, and a light appeared underneath the metal. She remembered every version of Zhou Yan: the stranger at the train door, the boy in the photograph, the frightened man on the laboratory floor, the friend who trusted her to challenge his sacrifice. Each memory was incomplete, but together they formed a person no file could contain. “I know who you are,” she said. The floor began to vibrate; the control panel opened like a book. Inside was a final rule, written in her brother's handwriting: TO FREE THE FORGOTTEN, CHANGE WHO IS ALLOWED TO REMEMBER.

見夏把鑰匙圈兩半合上，邊緣完美相接，金屬底下亮起光。她想起每一種周硯：車門口的陌生人、照片裡的男孩、實驗室地板上受驚的人、相信她會反抗他犧牲的朋友。每段記憶都不完整，合在一起卻是一個檔案容不下的人。「我知道你是誰。」地板震動，控制台像書本打開。裡面是哥哥筆跡的最後規則：「要讓被遺忘的人自由，就改變誰有權記得。」
