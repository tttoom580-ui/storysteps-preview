終點站開門

Opening the Terminus

第十三站台 · 第 20 / 25 章 · 5000–6000 · 英中對照 · 校訂中

用互動版查詞、朗讀與收藏

The terminus stood under a vaulted ceiling made of aluminum panels, its vast breadth disappearing into mist. Ivy climbed the walls of what had once been a dormitory for researchers. A bird perched on a station clock and watched the travelers cross a rugged stone floor. The air smelled faintly of petroleum and fragrant flowers, an unsettling combination. Above the gate, a warning described the station as a commonwealth of shared memories. Its language celebrated unity while requiring exclusion: anyone unwilling to surrender a precious memory would be barred. Jianxia knew that a community built on forced forgetting could not be the home its sign promised. Yet the gate remained closed.

終點站高拱天花板由鋁板組成，廣闊空間沒入霧中。常春藤爬上舊研究員宿舍的牆，鳥停在站鐘上，看旅客穿過崎嶇石地。空氣混著石油與芬芳花香，很不協調。閘門警告稱車站是共享記憶的共同體，口號讚美團結，實際卻要求排除：不肯交出珍貴記憶的人不能進。見夏知道建立在強迫遺忘上的社群，不可能是招牌承諾的家，但大門仍緊閉。

A short fable appeared on the wall: a traveler traded the memory of his mother for passage, then forgot why he had wanted to return home. A second traveler traded his own name and emerged unable to recognize anyone who greeted him. Shen Man said the tales were not warnings against selfishness but demonstrations of what the system extracted. He Chuan found an editorial printed beneath the stories, praising the project for preparing humanity to industrialize consciousness. “They're selling the destruction of a person as innovation,” he said. A tremor shook the platform, and the walls began to quake. Above them, a red light signaled that the first memory payment was due.

牆上出現短寓言：旅人用母親記憶換取通行，出去後卻忘了為何想回家；另一人交出名字，出站後認不出任何向他打招呼的人。沈漫說這些不是勸人無私的寓言，而是展示系統擷取了什麼。賀川在故事下找到社論，稱頌專案推動意識工業化。「它把摧毀一個人賣成創新，」他說。地板震動、牆面搖晃，頭頂紅燈表示第一筆記憶付款到期。

Each traveler received a glass token. The screen asked Jianxia for the memory she most yearned to protect. She thought immediately of her brother laughing beside the sea. Zhou Yan looked at her and seemed to understand the choice forming in her mind. He offered to surrender his own memory instead. Jianxia refused. “One person shouldn't always be the payment for everybody else.” Nearby, a visitor protested that the rules disadvantaged him because he was homosexual and had been denied the chance to name the man he loved in earlier records. Jianxia supported his demand. Nobody should have to reduce a loved person to a category before being allowed to grieve them.

每個人拿到玻璃代幣。螢幕要見夏交出最渴望守住的記憶，她立刻想到哥哥在海邊笑的模樣。周硯理解她正在考慮什麼，主動說可以交自己的記憶。見夏拒絕：「不該總是同一個人替大家付代價。」附近另一位旅客抗議，他是男同志，過去紀錄不准他寫出所愛男子名字，規則因此對他不利。見夏支持他的要求：任何人都不該先把愛人縮成分類，才獲准悲傷。

The screen repeated its demand for purity of payment, insisting every memory be free from conflicting feelings. Jianxia laughed in disbelief. “No real memory is pure like that. Even happiness contains fear of losing it.” Shen Man added that grief could have vitality as well as sorrow because it proved love had existed. The machine rejected the explanation. It preferred clean categories. He Chuan skimmed a stack of discarded applications and noticed that the gate had opened once for a group who shared a single memory rather than surrendering it. The applicants had refused to revolt violently; instead they had stood together and told the same story from different perspectives. He Chuan did not need long to skim the paperwork; the same exploitative clause appeared on every page.

螢幕要求付款記憶必須純粹，沒有矛盾感情。見夏難以置信地笑：「真實記憶哪有那麼純？連幸福都藏著失去的恐懼。」沈漫補充，悲傷也可能帶著生命力，因為它證明愛存在過。機器拒絕，它只喜歡乾淨分類。賀川快速翻閱廢棄申請書，發現閘門曾對一群共享記憶的人打開：他們沒有以暴力反抗，只站在一起，從不同角度講述同一個故事。 賀川不必花太久快速瀏覽文件，因為每頁都重複同樣剝削條款。

They stood before the gate and began with the night at the first platform. Jianxia spoke of fear, Zhou Yan of a promise, Shen Man of care, and He Chuan of his own mistake and attempt to repair it. Their memories disagreed in details but complemented one another in meaning. The gate's screen tried to sort their accounts into separate boxes. Each time it did, another voice filled what had been left out. The glass tokens warmed. A sign flashed ERROR: MEMORY CANNOT BE DIVIDED WITHOUT LOSS. Jianxia whispered that this was the first sensible statement the station had made. With a low metallic groan, the gates finally opened.

他們站在閘門前，開始講述第一個月台的夜晚。見夏談恐懼，周硯談承諾，沈漫談照顧，賀川談錯誤和修補。細節不一致，意義卻互補。螢幕想把敘述分進不同格子，每次都有人補上被遺漏的一段。玻璃代幣變暖，訊息顯示：「錯誤：記憶無法無損分割。」見夏低聲說這是車站第一句有道理的話。閘門低鳴，終於打開。

Beyond the gate stretched a corridor lined with people asleep inside transparent rooms. They were breathing. Some looked young, some very old, and each had a name written beside the glass. A woman woke when Jianxia touched her door, then smiled as though she had been waiting for years. “Do you know where we are?” Jianxia asked. “At the place where they keep those who refuse to forget,” the woman answered. She pointed toward an immense chamber glowing beneath the platform. “The core is there. But before you enter, understand this: the system does not merely store memories. It chooses which people deserve to have a future.” Jianxia tightened her grip on the notebook and walked toward the light.

閘門後透明房間裡躺著許多人，都還在呼吸，有年輕有年老，玻璃旁寫著名字。見夏碰某扇門，一名女人醒來，微笑得像已等待多年。「我們在哪裡？」見夏問。「在收容不肯遺忘之人的地方。」女人指向月台下發光巨室：「核心就在那裡。但進去前要懂一件事：系統不只是保存記憶，它會決定誰配擁有未來。」見夏握緊筆記本，走向光亮。
