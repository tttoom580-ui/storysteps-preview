被複製的周硯

Two Men Named Zhou Yan

第十三站台 · 第 12 / 25 章 · 5000–6000 · 英中對照 · 校訂中

用互動版查詞、朗讀與收藏

Floor Minus One opened into a white laboratory with lavish curtains and a bright logo of a smiling sun. The loudspeaker announced that a census of all passengers would begin in five minutes. An attendant in spotless clothes offered them hot tea, but Shen Man refused to accept anything she could not identify. Through a window they saw a huge iceberg drifting in a dark sea, although the room stood beneath a city. A chemical odor suggested an outbreak of something harmful. The attendant insisted they were perfectly safe and invited them to subscribe to a new wellness plan. Jianxia looked past him. At the far end of the laboratory stood Zhou Yan, identical to the man beside her.

負一樓通向白色實驗室，掛著奢華窗簾，牆上有微笑太陽標誌。廣播宣布五分鐘後清點乘客。穿著潔淨的服務員端來熱茶，沈漫不肯碰不知成分的東西。窗外巨大冰山在黑海漂流，儘管他們理應位於城市地下。化學氣味暗示某種有害物質爆發。服務員堅稱絕對安全，邀他們訂閱新的健康方案。見夏越過他往後看，實驗室盡頭竟站著另一個周硯，和身旁的男人一模一樣。

“You have copied my face,” the newcomer said to Zhou Yan. His tone was calm, but anger moved beneath it. The first Zhou Yan answered, “I could say the same.” Shen Man stood between them and asked questions from the medical checkup: childhood injuries, allergies, blood type. Both men answered correctly. A chemist entered carrying a vial and offered to dispense a harmless test solution. “We don't need another experiment,” Jianxia said. He called the test invaluable, claiming it could settle the matter permanently. He Chuan photographed both faces and discovered that the camera showed only one of the men. The screen displayed an empty chair where the second stood.

「你複製了我的臉。」新來者對周硯說，語氣平靜，底下卻壓著怒。原本的周硯回答：「我也能這麼說。」沈漫站在中間，用健康檢查題目提問：童年傷、過敏、血型。兩人答得一樣。化學師端著試管進來，表示可以發放無害測試劑。「我們不需要另一場實驗，」見夏說。對方稱試驗價值無可取代，能永久解決問題。賀川拍下兩人，卻發現相機畫面裡只有一個周硯；另一個位置顯示空椅。

The second Zhou Yan pointed toward a training hall where martial arts students were practicing in silence. “That one took my place when I refused to cooperate,” he claimed. The first Zhou Yan did not argue. Instead he asked Jianxia to remember the key chain. “Which half did I give you?” She opened her palm. Both men held out matching pieces. The situation made her groan. A small artificial bird landed on the table and began to chirp, but its voice broke into static midway through the song. Shen Man noticed tiny differences in its reaction to each man. “One of you has information the other lacks,” she said. “That does not necessarily make the other a copy.”

第二個周硯指向武術學員在無聲練習的訓練廳。「我拒絕合作後，是他取代我，」他說。原本的周硯不爭辯，只問見夏還記不記得鑰匙圈：「我給妳哪一半？」見夏攤開掌心，兩個男人都伸出匹配的一半。她忍不住低吟。人造小鳥落在桌上啁啾，唱到一半卻變成雜音。沈漫注意到鳥對兩人的反應略有不同。「你們一個知道另一個不知道的事，」她說，「但這不等於不知道的人就是複製品。」

The laboratory's census screen began to populate with names. Every passenger appeared twice, one entry marked ORIGINAL and one marked REVISION. The old woman's name did not appear at all. Jianxia realized that the system was counting records, not people. A lavish meal arrived as a distraction: oyster stew, sweet batter cakes, and sparkling water. He Chuan nearly laughed at the absurd hospitality. “It has manufactured a problem so we'll accept its cure,” he said. The second Zhou Yan showed a photograph of the day Jianxia fell at the platform. In his version, the boy holding her wore a hospital wristband. The first Zhou Yan's version showed the same boy without it.

實驗室清點螢幕開始填入名字，每位乘客都出現兩筆，分別標示「原版」和「修訂版」。老婦人名字完全沒有。見夏發現系統算的是檔案，不是人。此時奢華餐點送到：牡蠣燉湯、麵糊甜餅、氣泡水，想分散注意。賀川幾乎被荒謬的款待逗笑。「它先製造問題，好讓我們接受治療，」他說。第二個周硯展示見夏墜落月台當天照片，他那份裡男孩戴著醫院腕帶；原本周硯的版本裡，男孩沒有。

Shen Man became skeptical of both pictures. “A photograph can be revised. We should ask what neither of you remembers.” The two men fell silent. Then Jianxia asked them why they had come back for her. The first answered that he had promised never to leave a child alone. The second said he had made that promise after failing once before. There was a setback in Jianxia's memory: a sudden white space, followed by a child's crying. She realized both answers might be true at different points in time. “You aren't rivals,” she told the men. “You're two incomplete versions of the same choice.” The room's ceiling began to split. Neither man had meant to withhold the truth; each held only a different fragment of it.

沈漫對兩張照片都抱持懷疑：「照片可以修改。我們該問你們都不記得的事。」兩個男人沉默。見夏問他們為何回來救她。第一個說自己承諾過不讓孩子獨自留下；第二個說他是在曾經失敗一次後才發誓。見夏記憶裡出現斷層：大片空白後是一個孩子的哭聲。她理解兩種回答可能各自屬於不同時間。「你們不是敵人，」她說，「你們是同一個選擇的不完整版本。」房頂開始裂開。 兩個男人都不是刻意隱瞞真相，而是各自只握有不同碎片。

The attendant shouted for them to separate the two men, but Jianxia refused. She took both their hands. A strange current passed between them, and a single memory rose in her mind: a boy trying to pull her from the tracks while his future self held the emergency door. Both had been there because the station could bend time. The second Zhou Yan faded, leaving a notebook page covered with warnings. Its final line read: DO NOT TRUST THE NEXT JUDGMENT. The first Zhou Yan sank to his knees and wept without sound. Jianxia held him as the room dissolved, knowing now that remembering the truth could hurt more than forgetting it.

服務員尖聲命令把兩個男人分開，見夏拒絕了。她牽起兩人的手，一股奇異電流穿過，記憶忽然浮現：小男孩想把她從軌道拉起，未來的他則撐著緊急門。兩人確實都在，因為車站能彎折時間。第二個周硯逐漸消失，留下一頁寫滿警告的筆記，最後一句是：「不要相信下一場審判。」第一個周硯跪倒，無聲落淚。房間溶解時，見夏抱著他，終於懂得記住真相有時比遺忘更痛。
