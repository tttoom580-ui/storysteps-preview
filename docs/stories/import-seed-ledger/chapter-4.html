溫暖之塔

The Tower of Warmth

種子帳冊 · 第 4 / 25 章 · 2000–3000 · 英中對照

The subway opened into an alley between abandoned apartment blocks. At the far end rose the northern tower, its upper windows glowing despite the city's shortages. A thin cable ran from the building to a row of vans parked below. Pip pointed to a label on one vehicle: MEDICAL RELIEF. Inside were boxes of cherry-flavored sweets instead of medicine. 'They sell donations and send the empty cartons back,' he said. Iris felt dizzy with anger. An elderly woman sat on a step, trying to knit a blanket while a child slept beside her. The woman told them that a crash at the tower had killed several workers the previous winter. The authorities called it an accident, but no one had been allowed to investigate. Mara asked for the woman's name and promised to preserve her testimony. Iris knew that bravery could be quiet: sometimes it meant continuing to speak when every door had closed.

地鐵出口通往兩棟廢棄公寓間的小巷。盡頭聳立著北方高塔，儘管城市物資短缺，塔頂的窗戶仍燈火通明。一條纜線從高塔延伸到下方停放的一排廂型車。皮普指著一輛車上的標籤：「醫療救助」。車內裝的不是藥物，而是一箱箱櫻桃口味糖果。「他們把捐贈物賣掉，再把空箱送回去。」他說。艾莉絲憤怒得頭暈。一位老婦坐在台階上編織毛毯，旁邊有個孩子熟睡。她說去年冬天塔內的撞擊事故奪走了幾名工人的生命。當局稱是意外，卻不許任何人調查。瑪拉記下老婦的名字，承諾保存證詞。艾莉絲明白，勇敢也可以很安靜：有時它只是每一扇門都關上時，仍選擇開口。

That afternoon the team entered the building through a maintenance shaft. Theo carried a metal saucer to catch dripping water and test its quality. The passage was narrow, its walls coated in solid ice. They found a heater connected to a private greenhouse above the public hospital. The warmth flowing through the pipes belonged to patients, but wealthy officials were using it to grow flowers. Pip took a short nap while Iris and Mara examined the controls. On the wall was a signed order authorizing the heat transfer. It bore the name of the city's security chief, Commander Rusk. 'He didn't merely steal warmth,' Theo said. 'He left people to starve and freeze.' Mara studied the signature, then explained that the law's definition of murder might apply when an official knowingly sent people into deadly conditions. Iris photographed every page before the security cameras could turn toward them.

當天下午，四人由維修井進入建築。西奧帶著一個金屬碟子，接取滴下的水來檢測水質。通道狹窄，牆上覆滿堅冰。他們發現一台暖氣裝置，把公立醫院的熱能轉送到上層私人溫室。這些熱本來屬於病人，富裕官員卻拿它種花。皮普短暫打盹時，艾莉絲與瑪拉檢查控制系統。牆上有份簽核命令，批准熱能轉移，上面是治安首長魯斯克的名字。「他不只偷走溫暖，」西奧說，「還讓人挨餓、凍死。」瑪拉看著簽名，解釋法律對謀殺的定義，也可能涵蓋明知會致人死亡卻仍下令的官員。艾莉絲在監視鏡頭轉向他們前，拍下每一頁文件。

Before climbing to the roof, they passed a private dining room where portraits of the city's founders covered the walls. Each painting showed smiling families in clean clothes, sitting beside tables loaded with fruit. Iris wondered how many had known that patients were freezing below. A worker admitted the portraits were printed every year, each showing the same faces while the people outside grew older. The images were not history, she realized. They were a carefully maintained advertisement for a life that few citizens could enter.

上屋頂前，他們經過私人餐廳，牆上掛滿城市創辦家族的肖像。畫裡每戶都穿著乾淨衣服，坐在堆滿水果的桌旁微笑。艾莉絲不禁想，有多少人知道樓下病人正在挨凍。工人說肖像每年都重新印製，臉始終一樣，外頭居民卻不斷老去。她明白那些不是歷史，而是精心維持的廣告，展示少數人才能進入的生活。

On the roof they discovered the greenhouse under a clear dome. Workers grew fruit for a private banquet while children outside waited for soup. A young security guard stepped into their path. His dark uniform looked almost fashionable, even sexy in the advertisements pasted around the city, but his face was frightened. He admitted his sister was in the hospital below. 'I can let you pass,' he whispered, 'if you promise not to hurt her.' Iris asked him to turn off the camera for sixty seconds. He did. Pip inserted his chip into the control box, and Theo rerouted heat toward the hospital. Alarms screamed at once. From the greenhouse balcony, Iris saw hundreds of small lights appear in apartment windows, one by one. The hospital had become warm again. She felt thankful, but knew the alarm had told Rusk exactly where they were.

屋頂上有座透明圓頂溫室。工人在裡面種水果，準備私人宴會；外頭孩子們卻還在等湯。年輕守衛擋住去路。他的深色制服在城內廣告中看起來時髦，甚至被宣傳成性感的象徵，但本人滿臉恐懼。他承認妹妹正在樓下醫院。「只要你們答應不傷害她，我就放行。」他低聲說。艾莉絲請他關閉攝影機六十秒，他照做了。皮普把晶片插入控制盒，西奧將熱能改送醫院。警報立刻尖叫。從溫室陽台望去，艾莉絲看見公寓裡數百盞小燈一盞接一盞亮起。醫院恢復了溫暖。她心懷感謝，但也知道警報已向魯斯克暴露他們的位置。

They fled down an emergency stairwell while boots thundered above. At the ground floor they came upon a sealed service room where a young nurse was trying to free patients trapped behind a fallen shelf. Iris helped lift it, and the nurse gave them a key to the building's basement exit. Outside, the air smelled of wet metal; for the first time that year, a few drops of rain touched Iris's face. The nurse said the weather bureau had predicted dry skies for another month. She looked toward the tower, where a column of steam was rising from a broken pipe. 'Someone is controlling more than water,' she whispered. In the alley, Orin's mechanical fairy appeared from the darkness with an urgent message: Rusk had ordered the arrest of everyone connected to the old seed project. One name flashed on its tiny screen. THEO CHEN. Iris grabbed her brother's hand and ran.

他們沿緊急樓梯逃跑，頭頂傳來士兵的腳步聲。一樓有間封閉的服務室，年輕護理師正想救出被倒下架子困住的病人。艾莉絲幫忙搬開架子，護理師則給他們一把地下室出口的鑰匙。外頭有濕金屬的氣味。那一年的第一次，幾滴雨落在艾莉絲臉上。護理師說氣象局預報未來一個月仍是乾燥天氣。她望向高塔，一道蒸汽正從破管升起。「有人控制的東西，不只是水。」她低聲說。巷裡，奧林的機械精靈自黑暗中現身，帶來緊急訊息：魯斯克下令逮捕所有參與舊種子計畫的人。螢幕閃出一個名字：「西奧・陳」。艾莉絲抓住弟弟的手，拔腿就跑。
