製造天氣的工廠

The Weather Factory

種子帳冊 · 第 6 / 25 章 · 2000–3000 · 英中對照 · 校訂中

用互動版查詞、朗讀與收藏

At the weather station, Juno discovered a control room built into the inner side of a mountain. The equipment was older than the city but still active. Iris had assumed the drought was simply the result of damaged climate systems. Now a screen showed artificial clouds drifting over wealthy districts while the outer farms remained dry. 'Anyhow, there's our answer,' Theo said. A tired politician appeared on a security recording, discussing how water could become a valuable resource if shortages continued. His partner called himself the creator of the new weather program and praised its excellence. The recording made Iris's stomach turn. They had turned a necessity into a business. Juno examined a row of old batteries and found wax seals on their covers, each carrying the same symbol as the hidden seed bank. Whoever designed these machines had expected someone to connect the two systems one day.

到達氣象站後，朱諾發現山內側藏著一間控制室。設備比城市還古老，卻仍在運作。艾莉絲本以為乾旱只是氣候系統受損所致，螢幕現在卻顯示人造雲飄過富人街區，外圍農場依舊乾燥。「不管怎樣，答案就在這裡。」西奧說。一段監視錄影中，一名疲憊的政治人物正在談論缺水如何讓水資源變得值錢。夥伴自稱新氣象計畫的創造者，誇耀其卓越表現。艾莉絲胃裡翻攪。他們把生存必需品變成了生意。朱諾檢查一排舊電池，發現外殼上有蠟封，全都帶著與祕密種子庫相同的圖案。設計這些機器的人，似乎早就預料終有一天會有人把兩套系統連接起來。

Pip bumped into a shelf and uncovered a notebook behind a loose panel. It contained a summary of the original project. The station had been built to restore a tropical climate in selected valleys, but a defense company had later added controls for a missile system. In a conflict, the machinery could multiply clouds over one region while drying out another. 'The same technique can grow forests or destroy them,' Mara said. Worse, the station's cooling tanks were full of dangerous bacteria. An accident could send contaminated mist toward thousands of homes. Juno wanted to disconnect the entire machine immediately. Theo suspected that doing so might release the tanks. Iris asked them to stop arguing and study the plans. They had all seen what happened when leaders acted first and asked questions later. Here, even a good intention could kill someone.

皮普撞到架子，從鬆脫面板後找到一本筆記。裡面有原始計畫摘要。氣象站原本用來恢復選定山谷的熱帶氣候，後來一家軍工公司加入了飛彈系統的控制功能。戰時，機器可以讓某一地區的雲量倍增，同時讓另一地區乾涸。「同樣的技術能培育森林，也能毀滅森林。」瑪拉說。更糟的是，冷卻槽充滿危險細菌；一旦意外洩漏，受污染水霧就會飄向數千戶人家。朱諾想立刻關掉整套機器。西奧懷疑這麼做反而會釋放冷卻槽。艾莉絲請兩人停止爭辯，先研究圖紙。他們都見識過領袖先行動、後提問的後果。在這裡，連善意都可能害死人。

Near the storage tanks stood a display from the station's original opening ceremony. Children in bright coats had planted trees beside the new machinery. A plaque promised to keep rainfall available to all communities. Iris read it twice. She could not understand how an invention made for public safety had become an instrument of control. Mara explained that rules were only useful when people could check how they were enforced. Otherwise a beautiful promise might survive on a wall long after its meaning had vanished.

儲水槽旁展示著氣象站原始啟用典禮的照片。穿鮮豔外套的孩子在新機器旁種樹，牌匾承諾讓所有社區共享雨水。艾莉絲讀了兩遍，不明白原本為公眾安全而生的發明，如何變成控制工具。瑪拉說，只有人民能檢查規則如何執行時，規則才有用；否則美麗承諾可能留在牆上很久，意義卻早已消失。

They found a narrow maintenance tunnel leading to the mountain's heart. Heat pressed against the walls, and the air smelled of stale bread. A sensor flashed when it detected a change in pressure. Juno explained that only a slow release would protect the cooling tanks. Mara held a mirror to see around the next corner, where guards were standing beside a sealed door. Iris had spent her teenage years learning which adults could be trusted; now she had to decide whether to trust machines designed by people who had lied for years. The fairy projected a message in her mother's handwriting: THE WEATHER FACTORY CAN BE RESET, BUT IT CANNOT BE OWNED. Iris felt a strange relief. Her mother had left instructions, not commands. Each generation would have to choose what to do with the power it inherited.

他們找到通往山心的狹窄維修隧道。熱氣緊貼牆壁，空氣像放久的麵包。壓力改變時，感測器閃起燈。朱諾解釋，只有緩慢洩壓才能保護冷卻槽。瑪拉用鏡子查看轉角，幾名守衛正站在封閉門旁。艾莉絲整個少女時期都在學習分辨可靠的大人；現在她得決定能否信任一群長年說謊者造出的機器。小精靈投射出母親筆跡：「氣象工廠可以重設，但不能被任何人占有。」艾莉絲感到一絲安慰。母親留下的是說明，不是命令。每一代人都必須決定如何使用繼承的力量。

With Pip watching the corridor, Juno and Theo rewired the pressure controls. Iris placed the recording of the politician's conversation into the public broadcast channel. Instead of sending a speech, she sent the full files so people could examine the evidence for themselves. Alarm bells rang, and a guard struck the door. 'We suspect an intruder,' he shouted to the others. Juno completed the reset just as the guards forced their way in. A thin cloud rose from the tanks but settled harmlessly into a collection basin. On the screens, weather maps changed. Moisture began moving toward the dry neighborhoods. Iris raised her hands and offered to surrender if the workers were allowed to finish. The guard hesitated when he saw pictures of his own neighborhood turning green. Behind him, the first true storm in eleven years gathered over the city. Nobody moved. Then a drop of rain struck the station's window.

皮普守著走廊，朱諾與西奧重新接線，調整壓力控制。艾莉絲把政治人物的談話錄音送入公共廣播頻道。她沒有發表演說，而是公開完整檔案，讓大家自行檢視證據。警鈴響起，守衛猛敲門。「我們懷疑有人入侵！」他向同伴喊。朱諾剛完成重設，守衛就破門而入。薄霧從冷卻槽上升，卻無害地落入收集池。螢幕上的氣象圖開始改變，水氣往乾燥街區移動。艾莉絲舉起雙手，表示只要讓工人完成工作，她願意投降。守衛看見自己住的街區在圖上轉綠，猶豫了。十一年來第一場真正的暴雨正在城市上空聚集。沒有人動。接著，一滴雨敲上氣象站窗戶。
