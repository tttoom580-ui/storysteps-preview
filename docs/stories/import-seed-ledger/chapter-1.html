邊境的馬戲團

The Circus at the Edge

種子帳冊 · 第 1 / 25 章 · 2000–3000 · 英中對照

用互動版查詞、朗讀與收藏

On the morning the last rain tank ran dry, Iris Chen found a red berry growing through the roof of her family's hut. It was impossible. Nothing had grown outside the city's glass walls for eleven years. Even the old heater beside her bed had stopped working, and her little brother Theo wore two coats to breakfast. Beyond the fence, a broken circus tent stood among rusted buses, its painted stars fading into gray. Iris put the berry in her pocket and went there because the tent's lights had flashed three times during the night. Inside she met an elderly magician named Orin, who had once performed tricks for children before the long drought. A tiny mechanical fairy hovered over his shoulder. Its wings made no noise; its eyes shone green. Orin showed her a paper deed to a forgotten underground seed bank. 'Your mother hid this,' he said. 'She wanted someone brave enough to find it.'

最後一座雨水槽乾涸的早晨，艾莉絲・陳在自家小屋屋頂發現一顆紅色莓果。這根本不可能。十一年來，城市玻璃牆外沒有任何植物生長。連床邊的舊暖氣都壞了，弟弟西奧吃早餐時得穿兩件外套。圍牆外，一座殘破的馬戲團帳篷立在生鏽的巴士間，帳篷上的彩繪星星已褪成灰色。艾莉絲把莓果收入口袋，走向帳篷，因為她昨夜看見那裡的燈閃了三次。裡面有位叫奧林的老魔術師，過去曾為孩子表演。一隻機械小精靈盤旋在他肩上，翅膀無聲，雙眼泛綠光。奧林拿出一張地下種子庫的舊地契。「這是妳母親藏的，」他說，「她希望有人勇敢到願意去找它。」

Orin lived without fancy furniture or guards, but he protected a remarkable collection of tools. He played an old jazz record while Iris examined the map. A jet had crashed near the northern ridge years ago, he explained, and its fuel had poisoned the surface soil. The government called the accident a natural disaster. Its political leaders then promised to deliver food if citizens surrendered control of their wells. 'They didn't just rob us of water,' Orin said. 'They robbed us of the truth.' The fairy projected a moving picture onto the wall: a mountain, a dry river, and a buried door marked with a blue circle. Iris recognized the mountain from her childhood drawings. Orin offered her a pint of clean water for the journey. She tried to refuse, but he insisted. 'A generous deed is still worth doing when nobody is watching,' he said. His hands shook when he gave her the bottle.

奧林沒有華麗家具，也沒有守衛，卻珍藏著一批了不起的工具。他播放老爵士唱片，讓艾莉絲研究地圖。他說，多年前一架噴射機墜毀在北方山脊附近，燃料污染了地表土壤。政府卻稱那是天然災害。政治領袖隨後承諾供應糧食，條件是人民交出水井的控制權。「他們不只搶走我們的水，」奧林說，「還搶走了真相。」機械精靈將動態影像投射到牆上：一座山、一條乾河，以及標著藍圈的地下門。艾莉絲認出那座山，童年時她曾畫過。奧林給她一品脫乾淨的水作為旅途補給。她想拒絕，他卻堅持。「善行即使無人看見，也值得去做。」他把瓶子交給她時，雙手正在顫抖。

A harsh signal suddenly rang from the fairy. On the map a warning icon began to spin. City patrols had detected the transmission and were moving toward the tent. Iris grabbed a rusty screwdriver and tried to loosen a screw on the back door, but it would not move. 'Don't quit now,' Orin urged. He pointed toward a tame-looking dog sleeping beneath a table. The animal was actually a rescue machine wearing artificial fur. When Orin whispered a command, it pushed the door open with its metal shoulder. Iris had learned various escape routes while delivering medicine, yet she had never imagined she would need one to save a seed bank. The streets outside were empty. A patrol drone flashed across the sky, searching for heat. She ducked behind a bus, counting the seconds between its passes, and wondered whether the berry in her pocket was truly alive.

機械精靈突然發出刺耳訊號。地圖上的警告符號開始旋轉。城市巡邏隊偵測到傳輸訊號，正朝帳篷靠近。艾莉絲拿起生鏽的螺絲起子，想鬆開後門的螺絲，門卻紋風不動。「現在別放棄，」奧林催促。他指向桌下那隻看似溫馴的狗。其實牠是覆著人工毛皮的救援機器。奧林低聲下令，牠便用金屬肩膀推開門。艾莉絲送藥時學過許多逃生路線，卻從未想過有一天會為了拯救種子庫而逃亡。外頭街道空無一人。一架巡邏無人機在天際閃過，搜尋熱源。她躲在巴士後，計算無人機兩次飛過間隔的秒數，並想著口袋裡的莓果是否真的有生命。

She could have returned home, hidden the map, and pretended to know nothing. That would have kept Theo safe for another day. Instead she remembered her mother saying that a miracle was not magic; it was a possibility someone refused to abandon. Iris crossed the circus yard and climbed into a drainage tunnel. The fairy followed, carrying a weak blue light. Behind them came the sound of boots and an officer's voice ordering Orin to surrender. 'Who has the girl?' another officer shouted. The old magician began a ridiculous performance, loudly claiming that he had turned her into a mouse. For one precious moment the soldiers laughed. Iris crawled onward. At the far end of the tunnel she found a drawing carved into the stone: the same blue circle as on the map. Beneath it were three words her mother had written years before: WHEN WATER RETURNS. Iris touched the marks. Somewhere beyond the walls, a secret garden was waiting.

她原本可以回家、藏起地圖，假裝一切都不知道。至少西奧還能再安全一天。但她記得母親說過，奇蹟不是魔法，而是有人拒絕放棄的一種可能。艾莉絲穿過馬戲團院子，爬進排水隧道。機械精靈跟在身後，發出微弱藍光。背後傳來靴聲，以及軍官命令奧林投降的喊聲。「那女孩在哪裡？」另一名軍官叫道。老魔術師開始荒唐的表演，大聲宣稱自己把她變成了老鼠。士兵們因此笑了一瞬。艾莉絲繼續往前爬。隧道末端，她發現石壁上刻著與地圖相同的藍圈，下面還有母親多年前留下的三個字：「當水回來。」艾莉絲摸著刻痕。圍牆之外的某個地方，有座祕密花園正在等待。
