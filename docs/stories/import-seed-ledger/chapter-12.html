峽谷船長

The Canyon Captain

種子帳冊 · 第 12 / 25 章 · 2000–3000 · 英中對照 · 校訂中

用互動版查詞、朗讀與收藏

The new river poured through a deep canyon beyond the mountain. To follow it, the travelers joined a small ferry run by Captain Bex, a woman with silver hair and a sharp voice. She had carried clients across the valley long before the drought, and she refused to abandon her boat when the water vanished. Now that it flowed again, she was eager to travel. Elderly passengers waited along the shore, hopeful that they might reach their families downstream. Bex warned everyone to put aside heavy bags; the narrow craft could carry only essential supplies. Theo edited the route on a tablet while Juno checked the electricity system powering the ferry. Iris studied a monthly chart of river levels from the captain's cabin. It showed that a sudden flash of rain had been predicted years before, then removed from later reports. Even the weather bureau's records had been shaped to protect a lie.

新生河流穿過山外深邃峽谷。為了沿河前進，旅人搭上一艘由銀髮、聲音尖銳的貝克斯船長駕駛的小渡船。乾旱之前，她就長年載客穿越山谷，河乾後也不肯丟掉船。現在河重新流了，她急著出航。年長乘客在岸邊等候，盼望能見到下游家人。貝克斯提醒大家把沉重行李放一旁，窄船只能載必需品。西奧在平板上修改航線，朱諾檢查渡船的電力系統。艾莉絲在船長艙裡看到每月水位圖，發現數年前曾預測過一場突如其來的暴雨，後來的報告卻刪掉了它。連氣象局的資料都被修改來保護謊言。

The ferry moved between towering walls of stone. Bubbles rose from underwater vents, and a faint light flashed beneath the surface. Pip asked whether the river contained dangerous creatures. Bex laughed and said the only creatures she feared wore uniforms. At midday they discovered a narrow hatch built into the canyon wall. It opened onto a warehouse stacked with crates of spice, old navigation equipment, and cases marked PRIVATE PROPERTY. Mara read an invoice explaining that a wealthy company had claimed ownership of the riverbed. The document listed Bex as a former employee, although she denied ever working for them. 'They cannot own the river just by writing their name on it,' the captain said. Iris agreed, but she knew that powerful people had often used paper to make theft look legal.

渡船在高聳石壁間前進。水下通風口冒出氣泡，水面下閃著微光。皮普問河裡是否有危險生物，貝克斯笑說她只怕穿制服的生物。中午他們在峽谷壁上發現一個窄艙口，裡面倉庫堆滿香料、舊導航設備，以及標著「私人財產」的箱子。瑪拉讀到一張發票，顯示某富有公司聲稱擁有河床。文件將貝克斯列為前員工，她卻否認曾替對方工作。「不可能寫上名字就把河變成他們的。」船長說。艾莉絲同意，但她知道有權勢的人常用文件把偷竊包裝成合法。

Captain Bex insisted that every passenger learn where the life jackets were stored. She told the children that being brave meant respecting a river's strength rather than pretending it could not hurt them. Pip helped demonstrate how to fasten the straps, earning a laugh when the jacket swallowed his narrow shoulders. The lesson delayed their departure, but Iris welcomed the pause. Their previous escapes had often depended on luck; she wanted the people traveling with them to depend on preparation instead.

貝克斯船長堅持每個乘客都得知道救生衣放在哪裡。她告訴孩子，勇敢是尊重河的力量，而不是假裝河不會傷人。皮普示範扣帶，救生衣幾乎把他窄肩包住，惹得大家笑起來。這堂課延遲了開船，艾莉絲卻很樂意。之前的逃亡常靠運氣，她希望同行者以後能靠準備，而不只是幸運。

As they examined the crates, a small drone appeared above the hatch and projected a demand for payment. It claimed to represent the corporation's interests and ordered the ferry to turn back. Bex refused. 'The situation is simple,' she said. 'This river passes through my community, and my people need water.' The drone descended, its blades cutting the air near Iris's face. Theo swung a wooden sword left by a child on deck, knocking the machine into the river. Everyone stared at him. 'I wasn't trying to be dramatic,' he said. 'I just needed something long enough.' The captain grinned, then guided the ferry toward the next bend. Mara collected the fallen drone's information chip, hoping it would reveal which officials had authorized the company's claim. The river, meanwhile, carried them steadily toward a city whose walls were still dry.

他們檢查箱子時，一架小無人機出現在艙口上方，投射收費要求。它宣稱代表公司的利益，下令渡船掉頭。貝克斯拒絕。「情況很簡單，」她說，「這條河流經我的社區，我的人民需要水。」無人機下降，葉片切過艾莉絲臉旁的空氣。西奧抓起甲板上孩子留下的木劍，將機器打落河中。眾人瞪著他。「我不是故意演得很誇張，」他說，「只是需要夠長的東西。」船長咧嘴一笑，把船駛向下個轉彎。瑪拉收起落水無人機的資訊晶片，希望查出哪些官員批准公司的河床所有權。此時河水穩穩載著他們，前往牆壁仍乾燥的城市。

That evening a frightened child began to weep because she could not find her father among the passengers. Iris sat beside her and promised to help. They searched every cabin, then found the man in the engine room, repairing a broken pump. The child embraced him so tightly that he could hardly stand. Watching them, Iris wondered whether her own mother was alive somewhere beyond the canyon. She kept the thought private. Before sleep, Bex invited her onto the upper deck and pointed toward distant lights on the horizon. 'The next city is controlled by the company,' she said. 'It will not welcome us.' Iris looked at the river beneath them and the stars overhead. She was afraid, but fear had become familiar now. More important was what they might learn if they continued. The captain handed her a compass, and for the first time Iris felt capable of finding the way without a map.

傍晚一個害怕的孩子哭了起來，因為在人群裡找不到爸爸。艾莉絲坐在她旁邊承諾幫忙。他們搜尋各艙，終於在引擎室找到正在修幫浦的男子。孩子抱得太緊，父親差點站不穩。艾莉絲看著他們，想母親是否也還活在峽谷彼端。她把想法藏在心裡。睡前貝克斯邀她上甲板，指向地平線遠處的燈火。「下一座城由公司控制，」她說，「他們不會歡迎我們。」艾莉絲望著船下的河與頭頂星星。她害怕，但已逐漸習慣恐懼。更重要的是，若繼續前進，他們會學到什麼。船長把指南針交給她，艾莉絲第一次覺得，即使沒有地圖也能找到路。
