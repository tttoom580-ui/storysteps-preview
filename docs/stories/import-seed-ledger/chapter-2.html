沒有車票的乘客

A Passenger Without a Ticket

種子帳冊 · 第 2 / 25 章 · 2000–3000 · 英中對照

用互動版查詞、朗讀與收藏

Iris found Theo waiting at the old underground bowling hall, where their father once repaired machines for the district. He had followed her because the city police were asking about her in every market. A huge metal beast stood in the hall's center; it was a machine that sorted supplies, though children had always feared its flashing eyes. The machine had been silent for a decade. Theo opened his emergency kit and attached a small battery to its control panel. A recorded voice spoke: 'Passenger inspection begins in thirty seconds.' Iris nearly jumped out of her skin. The machine wasn't a beast at all but a transport controller connected to an abandoned railway. It might carry them north. However, a new permit was required to enter the rail tunnel, and neither sibling had one. Through the broken ceiling they could already hear patrol vehicles moving toward the district.

艾莉絲在舊地下保齡球館找到西奧。父親生前曾在這個區域修理機械。西奧之所以跟來，是因為警察已在每座市場打聽她的下落。保齡球館中央矗立著一隻巨大的金屬野獸；那其實是分配物資的機器，卻一直因為閃爍的眼睛嚇壞孩子。它已沉默了十年。西奧打開急救工具包，把小電池接上控制板。錄音響起：「三十秒後開始乘客檢查。」艾莉絲嚇得差點跳起來。它根本不是怪獸，而是一套連接廢棄鐵道的運輸控制器，或許能帶他們往北。不過進隧道需要新許可證，姊弟倆都沒有。破裂天花板外，巡邏車靠近的聲音已經傳來。

A woman in a worn necktie stepped from behind the ticket desk. Professor Mara Vale had once taught geography at the public school, before its final semester ended and the school became a food warehouse. She seemed expressive and warm, but her eyes checked every exit before she spoke. 'Your mother was my colleague,' she said. 'She warned that the water budget was false.' Mara showed them figures from the city council. For years the leaders had claimed there was not enough water to support the outer districts. Yet their private gardens remained green. 'They call it safety,' Mara said, 'but it is simply greedy management.' Theo asked why she had stayed silent. The professor looked away. 'I regret it every day.' Iris wanted to trust her. She also knew that a person who regretted one betrayal might commit another to avoid getting caught.

一名繫著磨舊領帶的女人從售票桌後走出。瑪拉・維爾教授從前在公立學校教地理，直到學校最後一學期結束，校舍改成糧倉。她神情生動而親切，說話前卻先確認每個出口。「妳母親是我的同事，」她說，「她警告過水務預算有假。」瑪拉拿出市議會的數據。多年來，領袖們聲稱水量不足以供給外圍街區，但他們自家的花園依舊青綠。「他們稱之為安全，」瑪拉說，「其實只是貪婪的管理。」西奧問她為什麼不早說。教授移開視線。「我每天都在後悔。」艾莉絲想相信她，但也知道，一個後悔曾經背叛的人，可能為了不再被抓而再次背叛。

While they waited for the station contact, the professor opened a locked drawer and showed them student notebooks saved from the final school year. The essays described rivers, forests, and cities the children had never visited. One student had written, 'A map is a promise that places continue beyond what we can see.' Iris copied the sentence. For a girl raised behind barriers, the thought was both frightening and wonderful. Theo asked to carry the notebooks so their owners might someday read them again.

等待車站聯絡時，教授打開上鎖抽屜，拿出學校最後一年保存的學生筆記。文章描述孩子們從未去過的河流、森林與城市。一位學生寫道：「地圖是一個承諾，承諾我們看不見的地方也持續存在。」艾莉絲抄下這句話。對在圍牆內長大的女孩來說，這想法既可怕又美好。西奧想把筆記帶走，希望有天能讓主人重新讀到。

Mara handed Iris a map marked with the river's old path. 'We can frighten the guards with a false evacuation alarm,' she suggested. 'But doing so would put innocent workers at risk.' Theo tugged at the lace on his boot, thinking. The city had recently held a public poll about water rights, but no voter from the outer districts had been allowed into the counting room. 'What if we show people what's happening rather than trick anyone?' he asked. The professor smiled sadly. 'A reasonable idea, if we survive long enough to try.' She pulled out a portable radio and called a friend at the rail station. The answer was brief: one train remained, and it would leave at midnight. Iris studied the platform through a dusty window. The tracks were visible beneath the sand, heading directly toward the northern hills.

瑪拉遞給艾莉絲一張標有古河道的地圖。「我們可以用假的疏散警報嚇走守衛，」她建議，「但這會讓無辜工人陷入危險。」西奧拉著靴子的鞋帶思考。市政府最近才舉行水權民調，外圍街區卻沒有任何選民獲准進入計票室。「如果我們把真相給大家看，而不是耍手段呢？」他問。教授苦笑。「這主意合理，只要我們活得到那時候。」她拿出攜帶式無線電，聯絡車站的朋友。回覆很簡短：還有一班列車，午夜出發。艾莉絲透過滿是灰塵的窗子看向月台。鐵軌仍清晰可見，從沙下伸向北方山丘。

Near midnight they slipped onto the last carriage, bringing only food, water, and the map. In the corner sat a man whose breath smelled of chemicals. He appeared drunk, yet he watched the doors with remarkable attention. When the train started moving, he produced a brass key and said, 'Your mother told me to give you this if you ever came.' Iris froze. Mara recognized him as a former city officer who had disappeared during the last election. The man was taking an enormous risk. He explained that the seed bank was more than a place to store plants; it was evidence of what the government had done to the land. Then he laid a sealed letter in Iris's hands. Across the envelope, in her mother's unmistakable writing, were five words: DO NOT TRUST THE PROFESSOR. Iris looked up. Mara was smiling, but her fingers had already closed around the carriage's emergency lock.

午夜將近，他們帶著食物、水和地圖，悄悄登上最後一節車廂。角落坐著一名男子，呼吸帶著化學藥品氣味，樣子像喝醉了，卻極專注地觀察車門。火車起動時，他拿出一把黃銅鑰匙。「妳母親交代我，妳若來了就把它給妳。」艾莉絲愣住。瑪拉認出他是上次選舉期間失蹤的前市府官員。男子冒了極大風險。他說種子庫不只是儲存植物的地方，更保存著政府對土地做過什麼的證據。接著他把封好的信交到艾莉絲手裡。信封上有母親熟悉的字跡：「不要相信那位教授。」艾莉絲抬頭。瑪拉還在笑，手指卻已經握住車廂的緊急門鎖。
