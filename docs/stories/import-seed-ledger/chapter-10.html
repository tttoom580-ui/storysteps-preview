漲潮的新娘

The Bride of the Rising Tide

種子帳冊 · 第 10 / 25 章 · 2000–3000 · 英中對照 · 校訂中

用互動版查詞、朗讀與收藏

The flooded bridge led to a settlement beneath the mountain where families had created a small indoor sea. The water rose and fell with a controlled tide, powering lights along the shore. As the travelers arrived, a bride in a plain silk dress was walking toward a community hall. Her hair held a tiny pearl, and she carried a white handkerchief instead of flowers. Her neighbors called her Angel because she cared for abandoned children. Iris asked why anyone would hold a wedding in such a dangerous place. The bride smiled. 'A moral choice isn't always a safe choice. We need reasons to remain human.' Her words impressed Iris more than the fashionable decorations surrounding the hall. Outside, a flame burned in a glass lantern to guide travelers through the tunnels. For one hour, the settlement had decided to celebrate life instead of speaking only of fear.

被淹的橋通往山下聚落，居民在裡面造了一片小型室內海。水會隨受控潮汐漲落，供應岸邊照明。旅人抵達時，一名穿素白絲裙的新娘正走向社區禮堂。她髮間有顆小珍珠，手裡拿的是白手帕而非花束。鄰居稱她為「天使」，因為她照顧被遺棄的孩子。艾莉絲問為何要在如此危險的地方辦婚禮。新娘笑說：「道德選擇不一定安全。我們需要一些繼續做人類的理由。」這比禮堂周圍的時尚裝飾更令艾莉絲印象深刻。外頭有玻璃燈罩內的火焰，指引旅人通過隧道。這一小時，聚落決定慶祝生命，而不是只談恐懼。

At the wedding meal they ate slices of melon warmed beside an iron stove. The groom, a bold engineer named Arun, explained the structure of the underground sea. The lake's pumps could send water to the city, but a security barrier prevented it. 'We have tried to determine who controls the gate,' he said. 'Every answer leads to Rusk.' Mara asked whether the guests were optimistic about change. Arun replied that optimism without plans was only a pleasant dream. He had drawn a map showing three ways to reach the control chamber, yet each involved danger. Pip stared at the lines until he noticed a fourth route through a maintenance channel. 'Somehow there must be a way for small people to pass where machines cannot,' he said. Arun laughed and gave him a friendly punch on the shoulder. The plan began to feel possible.

婚宴上，他們吃了在鐵爐旁烤暖的哈密瓜片。新郎阿倫是勇敢的工程師，他解釋地下海的結構。湖水幫浦能把水送往城市，卻被一道治安屏障阻擋。「我們一直想查明水閘控制者是誰，」他說，「每條線索最後都指向魯斯克。」瑪拉問賓客是否對改變保持樂觀。阿倫回答，沒有計畫的樂觀只是美夢。他畫出三條通往控制室的路線，每條都危險。皮普盯著線條，發現第四條可通過的維修通道。「總有辦法讓小個子通過機器進不去的地方。」他說。阿倫大笑，友善地輕捶他肩膀。計畫開始顯得可行。

The wedding guests had built a little library in a room above the hall. It held accounts of marriages, births, disagreements, and repairs completed during the drought. Angel explained that celebrations helped people remember one another as more than names on a ration list. Iris found the idea quietly radical. If a settlement could keep records of joy as carefully as it kept records of danger, perhaps its children would grow up expecting life to contain more than the next emergency.

婚禮賓客在禮堂上方設了一間小圖書室，收藏乾旱時期的婚姻、出生、爭執與修繕紀錄。天使說，慶祝活動讓人們記得彼此不只是配給名單上的姓名。艾莉絲覺得這想法安靜卻激進。如果聚落能像記錄危險一樣仔細記錄喜悅，孩子長大後或許會期待生活不只有下一場緊急事件。

After the ceremony Iris found the bride alone by the water. Angel said she had once worked as a cleaner in Rusk's office. She had heard him promise Iris's mother that the water project would remain public. Then the drought began, and the promise disappeared from every official document. 'I left when he asked me to burn the original record,' Angel said. She had hidden one copy in the settlement's old chapel. Iris felt relief that someone had preserved the truth, but she also feared what the record might reveal about her mother. Angel placed the document in her hands. It contained signatures from engineers, citizens, and government officers. On the final page was a note added after the project ended: RUSK DID NOT GIVE THE ORDER. Iris looked up in surprise. If he had not ordered the shutdown, who had?

儀式後，艾莉絲在水邊找到獨處的新娘。天使說她曾在魯斯克辦公室當清潔員，親耳聽見他向艾莉絲的母親承諾水務計畫會維持公有。乾旱開始後，這承諾卻從官方文件消失。「他叫我燒掉原始紀錄，我就離開了。」她說。她把一份副本藏在聚落舊教堂。艾莉絲為有人保存真相而鬆口氣，卻也怕文件揭露母親的事。天使把文件交給她，上面有工程師、居民與政府人員簽名。最後一頁另有計畫結束後補上的一句：「下令關閉的不是魯斯克。」艾莉絲驚訝抬頭。如果不是他下令，到底是誰？

That night the wedding guests gathered to discuss the record. Some argued that Rusk was guilty even if another person gave the original order; he had enforced the system for years. Others wanted to question him before making a final judgment. Iris listened to both sides. She had imagined one villain responsible for everything, because a simple enemy was easier to fight than a damaged structure built from many choices. Theo reminded her that protecting the lake mattered more than identifying a single face. They prepared to enter the maintenance channel at dawn. Before leaving, Iris returned the handkerchief to Angel, who had lent it to wrap the old document. 'Keep it,' the bride said. 'There will be more tears on the road.' Then she added, 'And there may also be joy.' Iris folded the cloth into her pocket beside the little red berry, now dry but still whole.

當晚婚禮賓客聚在一起討論紀錄。有人主張，即使原始命令不是魯斯克下的，他多年來執行制度仍有罪；有人則希望先聽他說明再下結論。艾莉絲聽著雙方意見。她以前想像所有事情都由一個壞人造成，因為單一敵人比無數選擇築成的受損制度容易對抗。西奧提醒她，保護湖水比找出一張臉更重要。他們決定黎明進維修道。出發前艾莉絲把包文件的手帕還給天使。「留著吧，」新娘說，「路上還會有更多眼淚。」她又補充：「也可能有歡喜。」艾莉絲把布折好，放進口袋，挨著那顆已乾燥但仍完整的小紅莓果。
