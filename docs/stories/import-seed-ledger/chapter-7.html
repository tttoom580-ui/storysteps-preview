名字之罐

The Jar of Names

種子帳冊 · 第 7 / 25 章 · 2000–3000 · 英中對照 · 校訂中

The guards did not arrest them. Instead they escorted the group to a shelter built from brick, where people had gathered during the sudden storm. The rain was welcome, but the station's old pipes had begun to leak. Someone shouted that a gas tank might explode. An emergency team moved everyone into a basement filled with broken cupboards and discarded furniture. There Iris found a glass jar holding slips of paper. Each listed a worker's name and unpaid wages. A faded note explained that the workers had invented a system for keeping records when the city stopped paying them. 'Sometime before the drought, our parents trusted the council,' a woman said. 'Now we need proof of every promise.' Her words reminded Iris that saving water was not enough. The people who carried it through the city needed food, safety, and respect as well.

守衛沒有逮捕他們，而是護送一行人到磚造避難所，居民正因突如其來的暴雨聚集。雨雖受歡迎，氣象站的舊管卻開始漏水。有人大喊瓦斯槽可能爆炸，緊急小組把大家疏散到地下室，裡面擺滿破櫥櫃與廢家具。艾莉絲在那裡找到一個玻璃罐，裝著寫有工人姓名與欠薪金額的紙條。褪色紙條說明：市府不再發薪後，工人自行發明了這套記錄方式。「乾旱以前某個時候，我們的父母還相信議會，」一名女人說，「現在每個承諾都得留下證據。」艾莉絲因此明白，救回水還不夠。負責把水送遍城市的人，也需要食物、安全與尊重。

A young woman named Rose was repairing a table beside the stairs. In a vase she had placed a white lily, grown from a seed saved by her grandmother. The flower's delicate flavor was not important, Rose joked, but its smell made the awful room feel alive. She recognized Mara and gave an awkward wave. Years earlier they had argued over the council's water plan. 'You can appeal to the public now,' Rose said, 'but first help us keep the pipes steady.' Iris and Theo joined the repair line, carrying tools and removing junk. When the pressure rose, Theo placed a metal plate against a crack, while Iris tightened the screws. The workers kept their voices low and their movements careful. An hour later the threat of an explosion had passed, and people cheered as if they had won a terrific prize.

叫蘿絲的年輕女子在樓梯旁修桌子，花瓶裡插著一朵祖母留下種子種出的白百合。她開玩笑說花沒有什麼滋味，但香氣讓這間糟糕的地下室像活了一樣。她認出瑪拉，尷尬地揮手。多年前兩人曾為議會水計畫爭論。「妳現在可以向公眾呼籲了，」蘿絲說，「但先幫我們穩住水管。」艾莉絲和西奧加入維修隊，搬工具、清垃圾。壓力上升時，西奧用金屬板抵住裂縫，艾莉絲旋緊螺絲。工人低聲而謹慎地動作。一小時後，爆炸危機解除，人們歡呼得像贏了大獎。

In a corner of the shelter, a nurse washed her hands after treating a worker whose flesh had been burned by leaking steam. The wound was painful but not life-threatening. Iris offered to bring fresh water, and the nurse showed her how to carry supplies without disturbing the patients. The small task steadied her breathing. So much of their journey had been about hidden systems and distant leaders, yet here she could help a particular person simply by listening and following clear instructions.

避難所角落，一名護士替遭蒸汽燙傷皮肉的工人治療後正在洗手。傷口疼痛，但沒有生命危險。艾莉絲主動送清水，護士教她如何搬物資而不打擾病人。這件小事讓她呼吸平穩了些。旅程總圍繞隱藏系統與遙遠領袖，現在她卻能只靠傾聽和遵照明確指示，幫助眼前一個具體的人。

Afterward, Rose invited them upstairs for soup. A cupboard held three jars of vegetables, a few dried herbs, and exactly one dime from the currency used before the collapse. 'My father kept it to remind us that a price isn't the same as value,' she explained. The soup had a surprising flavor, rich with greens from a shared garden. Iris asked whether the workers could grow more food if the weather changed permanently. Rose nodded, then warned that the government's grain stores were almost empty. The sudden rain could not undo a decade of bad decisions. Iris set the record jar beside her mother's map. Together they offered a different picture of the city: one showing both what had been stolen and what ordinary people had protected. Outside, thunder moved toward the northern hills, where the seed bank lay hidden.

之後蘿絲請他們上樓喝湯。櫥櫃裡有三罐蔬菜、一些乾燥香草，還有一枚崩壞前貨幣的十美分硬幣。「我爸爸留下它，是要提醒我們價格不等於價值。」她說。湯裡有共享菜園的新鮮青菜，味道出奇豐富。艾莉絲問天氣若永久改變，工人能否種更多食物。蘿絲點頭，卻警告政府的穀倉幾乎已空。突來的雨無法抹去十年的錯誤決策。艾莉絲把名字罐放在母親地圖旁。兩者合在一起，呈現了另一幅城市景象：既看見被奪走的事物，也看見平民努力守住的一切。外頭雷聲往北方山丘移動，種子庫就藏在那裡。

That night Pip asked whether the next generation would live differently. Juno answered that it would depend on who held the records. If the same families kept deciding everything in secret, even the finest machines would eventually serve them alone. Iris looked around at the people sharing blankets, repairing heaters, and dividing meals without orders. The jar of wages was a simple invention, but it had survived because nobody could erase all its copies at once. She promised Rose that whatever lay inside the seed bank, its contents would not become another private treasure. Mara listened silently, then offered to sign the promise as a witness. Rose smiled and placed a small paper flower in Iris's hand. 'Don't turn into a hero who forgets the people sweeping the floors,' she said. Iris folded the flower into her map. In the morning they would travel north, carrying a list of names as carefully as their water.

當晚皮普問下一代是否能活得不一樣。朱諾回答，要看誰保管紀錄。如果一切仍由同一批家族秘密決定，再好的機器終究也只會替他們服務。艾莉絲環顧四周：人們不需命令，就會分享毯子、修暖氣、分食物。欠薪名字罐是簡單的發明，卻因有許多副本而無法被一筆抹去。她向蘿絲承諾，不管種子庫裡有什麼，都不會再變成少數人的私人寶藏。瑪拉靜靜聽著，隨後表示願意簽名作證。蘿絲微笑著把一朵紙花放進艾莉絲手中。「別變成那種忘了掃地工人的英雄。」她說。艾莉絲把紙花夾進地圖。明早他們會往北走，像珍惜飲水一樣珍惜那份名單。
