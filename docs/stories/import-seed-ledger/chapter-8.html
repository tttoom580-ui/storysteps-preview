The Herd at Black Station

種子帳冊 · 第 8 / 25 章 · 2000–3000 · 英中對照 · 校訂中

用互動版查詞、朗讀與收藏

The northern railway ended at Black Station, where broken carriages lay under thick dust. A herd of robotic buffalo moved between the tracks, gathering abandoned metal for repair. Iris had never seen so many machines working without a human controller. Nearby a volunteer cooker stood beside a fire, offering bowls of rice with crispy vegetables. The station's citizens were building shelters for travelers who had fled the city after the broadcast. Pip wanted to grab a bowl immediately, but a guard asked to inspect their baggage. Juno gave him her tools and explained their request for safe passage. 'We owe these people honesty,' she told Iris. The guard checked the bags, then returned everything with a smile. Lately, he said, too many armed groups had pretended to be rescue teams. Trust had to be built one careful act at a time.

北方鐵路在黑站終止，破損車廂覆滿厚厚灰塵。成群機械水牛在鐵軌間移動，收集廢金屬做修理。艾莉絲從未見過那麼多機器不靠人類控制器就能工作。旁邊的志工炊事員站在火堆旁，分發配有酥脆蔬菜的米飯。車站居民正替逃離廣播騷動的旅人搭建住所。皮普想立刻拿一碗，守衛卻先要求檢查行李。朱諾交出工具，說明他們請求安全通行的原因。「我們欠這些人誠實。」她對艾莉絲說。守衛檢查完，微笑著把東西還回來。他說最近太多武裝團體假冒救援隊；信任必須由一件件謹慎的行動建立。

Inside the station hall, notices described an old process for choosing community leaders. Every citizen could speak, but those who controlled the radio often drowned out quieter voices. Iris met a talkative girl named Nell who maintained a public message web built from repaired computers. Nell explained how wealthy districts had purchased priority access while poorer groups received delays. A young officer questioned the system, saying it violated his permission to speak freely. Nell answered that permission was not the same as power. She asked Iris to upload the weather recordings, then promised to publish them without editing the evidence. While she worked, a boy yelled from the door that scouts had found fresh litter near the eastern tracks: empty food packets, a broken strap, and a single bullet. Someone with weapons was approaching the station.

車站大廳張貼著舊式社區領袖選拔程序的公告。人人都能發言，但掌握無線電的人往往蓋過較小的聲音。艾莉絲遇見健談的女孩妮爾，她維護著一套以舊電腦修復而成的公共訊息網。妮爾解釋，富裕街區買下優先通道，貧窮團體的訊息卻遭延遲。一名年輕軍官質疑系統侵害自己的言論許可權。妮爾回答，有權說話不代表有能力被聽見。她請艾莉絲上傳氣象錄音，保證會不經竄改地公開證據。工作時，一個男孩從門口大叫：斥候在東方鐵軌發現新的垃圾，包含食物包裝、斷掉的背帶與一顆子彈。有帶武器的人正靠近車站。

Nell's message web also carried notices from a health clinic trying to provide accurate sexual health information after years of censorship. The old council had blocked basic lessons and then blamed young people for confusion. Iris asked Nell to preserve the notices alongside the weather files. Access to reliable information was another public resource, she realized. A community would never become free if its people could discuss water openly but were forbidden to ask honest questions about their own bodies.

妮爾的訊息網也刊登一間診所的公告，診所想在多年審查後提供正確的性健康資訊。舊議會封鎖基本教育，卻責怪年輕人無知。艾莉絲請妮爾把公告與氣象檔案一起保存。可靠資訊也是公共資源，她明白了。如果一個社區能公開討論水，卻不許人誠實詢問自己的身體，那就不可能真正自由。

Juno turned the buffalo into a line of moving barriers and sent families into the underground passage. Iris remained with Nell to protect the records. The patrol arrived at sunset, led by a uniformed officer who accused them of stealing government property. 'These files concern public water,' Iris replied. 'They belong to everyone.' The officer advanced, but one of the buffalo blocked his vehicle and refused to move. Its control lights flashed orange, then green. Theo had reprogrammed it to stop any vehicle carrying loaded weapons near the shelters. The officer drew his gun. Nell stepped from the shadows and told him the whole station was watching through its repaired cameras. He looked around and slowly lowered the weapon. The station had survived without firing a shot, but Iris knew they had gained more enemies by proving that frightened people could protect themselves.

朱諾讓機械水牛排成移動屏障，安排家庭進入地下通道。艾莉絲留在妮爾身旁保護紀錄。日落時巡邏隊抵達，帶隊軍官指控他們竊取政府財產。「這些檔案涉及公共用水，」艾莉絲說，「屬於所有人。」軍官前進，一隻水牛擋住車子，拒絕移動。控制燈由橘轉綠。西奧重新編程，讓它阻止裝有武器的車輛靠近避難所。軍官拔槍。妮爾從陰影中走出，告訴他整座車站正透過修復的攝影機觀看。他環視四周，終於慢慢放下槍。車站沒有開一槍就存活下來，但艾莉絲知道，他們證明恐懼中的人也能自保，因此也招惹了更多敵人。

In the evening, the citizens held a meeting beneath the station clock. They invited Iris to describe her journey and asked whether opening the seed bank would solve their problems. She refused to make a promise she could not keep. Rain would help, but the soil remained damaged, and hungry people could not wait months for harvests. Nell offered to keep the message web running even if the government cut the city's main connections. The volunteers shared rice, repaired shoes, and prepared carts for the road north. Before sleeping, Iris visited the buffalo shelter. The machines rested like enormous animals, their metal sides warm from work. She thanked Theo for his clever programming. He laughed and said the buffalo had simply followed a rule any decent society should understand: nobody carrying a gun deserved a shorter line to safety. Iris watched the distant hills and wondered what other rules they would have to rewrite.

晚上居民在車站鐘下開會，邀艾莉絲講述旅程，問她打開種子庫是否就能解決問題。她拒絕給無法兌現的承諾。雨會有幫助，但土地仍受污染，飢餓的人也不能等上好幾個月才收成。妮爾願意繼續維護訊息網，即使政府切斷城市主線也是如此。志工分享米飯、修鞋，為北行準備推車。睡前艾莉絲探訪機械水牛棚。機器像巨大動物般休息，金屬身軀還因工作而溫熱。她感謝西奧聰明的程式設計。西奧笑說，水牛只是遵守任何正當社會都該懂的規則：帶槍的人，不該在逃生隊伍裡享有優先。艾莉絲望著遠方山丘，思索還有多少規則需要重寫。
