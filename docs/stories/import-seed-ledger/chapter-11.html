湖底溝渠

The Ditch Below the Lake

種子帳冊 · 第 11 / 25 章 · 2000–3000 · 英中對照 · 校訂中

用互動版查詞、朗讀與收藏

The maintenance channel was a narrow ditch cut through wet rock. Water fell from the ceiling as Iris crawled ahead, careful not to knock loose the old timber supports. Theo followed with a plastic bag protecting their papers. Roughly fifty meters inside, they discovered an empty freezer that once stored medical supplies. A violet light shone above it, marking an emergency station. Iris opened its cabinet and found a portable oxygen mask, two bottles of gasoline for the maintenance engine, and a handwritten operation schedule. The entries were accurate down to the minute, but the signatures had been crossed out. Mara said an intelligent observer might learn as much from what had been removed as from what remained. Iris photographed the pages while sweat ran down her neck, then tied the cabinet shut with a loose cable to prevent it from falling into the water.

維修通道是一條切入濕岩的窄溝。水從天花板落下，艾莉絲小心往前爬，以免碰鬆舊木支架。西奧拿著裝文件的塑膠袋跟在後面。約五十公尺後，他們發現一座曾儲存醫療用品的空冷凍櫃。上方亮著紫光，標示這是緊急站。艾莉絲打開櫃子，裡面有攜帶式氧氣面罩、兩瓶供維修引擎使用的汽油，以及手寫作業時刻表。紀錄精確到分鐘，簽名卻全被畫掉。瑪拉說，聰明的觀察者或許能從被刪除的資訊學到和保留資訊一樣多。艾莉絲拍下每頁，汗沿頸流下，再用鬆動纜線綁好櫃子，以免它掉進水裡。

At the next turn they reached a chamber full of repair equipment. A circular opening descended into the lake, and an old diving suit hung beside it. Theo developed a theory: the water gates could be opened from a manual station underwater. He wanted to dive, but Mara warned him that his injured shoulder could weaken in the cold. Juno examined the suit and said the seals could be repaired. They would need someone with strong muscle control and steady breathing. A former rescue worker named Daro appeared from a side passage, carrying a heavy wrench. He had heard their voices and offered to help. 'My family lives in the lower county,' he said. 'I would bless the day clean water reached them again.' He was not asking for payment, only the chance to bring the lake back to its proper course.

下一個轉彎處是一間堆滿修理設備的房間，圓形開口直通湖水，旁邊掛著舊潛水裝。西奧提出理論：水閘可從水下手動站開啟。他想下潛，瑪拉卻警告他受傷的肩膀可能在寒冷中無力。朱諾檢查潛水裝，表示密封處可以修復，但需要肌肉控制良好、呼吸穩定的人。前救難員達羅從側通道現身，手裡拿著沉重扳手。他聽到眾人聲音後前來幫忙。「我家住在下游郡，」他說，「若清水能再次流到那裡，我會感謝這一天。」他不求報酬，只希望讓湖水恢復該走的路。

The station's damaged radio could still receive reports from independent media, although broadcasts were often interrupted. One reporter described patients recovering after heat returned to the hospital. Another interviewed farmers who had seen real rain for the first time since childhood. Iris played the clips for Daro while Juno worked on the diving suit. He listened without speaking. The voices seemed to remind him that the people waiting downstream were not numbers on a pressure gauge but neighbors with lives of their own.

站內受損的收音機仍能接收獨立媒體報導，雖然常遭中斷。一名記者描述醫院恢復供暖後逐漸康復的病人，另一人訪問童年後第一次看見真雨的農夫。朱諾修潛水裝時，艾莉絲把錄音放給達羅聽。他靜靜聽著。這些聲音似乎提醒他，下游等待的不是壓力表上的數字，而是有各自生活的鄰居。

While Juno repaired the suit, Iris searched a nearby control room for the missing authorization code. A screen offered a choice between automatic and manual performance modes. The automatic setting would quickly drain the lake; the manual one would permit a controlled flow through three smaller gates. Theo argued that speed was useless if it destroyed the settlement above. Daro agreed to descend and perform the operation himself. Iris held his helmet as he climbed into the suit. His smile made him look younger than the lines on his face. 'When this works,' he said, 'tell my daughter that I didn't forget her birthday.' Then he stepped into the dark water. His lamp vanished beneath the surface, leaving Iris watching a thin cable and listening to his breathing over the radio.

朱諾修潛水裝時，艾莉絲在附近控制室搜尋缺失的授權碼。螢幕提供自動與手動兩種操作模式。自動模式會快速排乾湖水；手動模式可經三道小水閘控制流量。西奧說，如果速度會毀掉上方聚落，就毫無意義。達羅同意親自下潛操作。艾莉絲扶著頭盔，讓他穿上潛水裝。他笑起來，臉上的皺紋似乎少了許多。「成功之後，」他說，「告訴我女兒我沒忘記她生日。」接著他走進黑水，頭燈沉入水下，艾莉絲只能盯著細纜線，透過無線電聽他的呼吸。

For several minutes the water remained still. Suddenly the radio hissed, and Daro called that the first gate had moved. A strong current pulled at his suit. Juno shouted for him to hold the side rail. Iris watched the pressure numbers climb, then slowly settle. The second gate opened; the third resisted. Daro used all his strength to turn the wheel, but an old metal part broke loose and struck his arm. His voice changed with pain. Theo begged him to return, yet Daro refused to leave the work half finished. At last the final gate shifted. Water rushed through the tunnels toward the farms. When they lifted Daro from the lake, he was weak but alive. Iris wrapped him in blankets. She could no longer believe the problem would be solved by a single heroic moment. Every victory depended on many people, each choosing not to abandon the others.

幾分鐘內湖水毫無動靜。無線電突然沙沙作響，達羅說第一道閘門已移動，強勁水流拉扯他的潛水裝。朱諾叫他抓住側邊欄杆。艾莉絲看著壓力數字上升，才緩緩穩定。第二道閘門打開，第三道卻卡住。達羅用盡力氣轉輪盤，舊金屬零件卻鬆脫打中他的手臂。他因痛苦而變聲。西奧求他回來，他卻不肯半途而廢。最後一道閘門終於轉動，湖水衝向農場的隧道。他們把達羅拉出湖時，他虛弱卻還活著。艾莉絲用毯子包住他。她不再相信一次英雄壯舉就能解決一切。每場勝利都靠許多人共同做出不放棄彼此的選擇。
