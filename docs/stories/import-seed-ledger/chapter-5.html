假消息的車庫

The Garage of False Messages

種子帳冊 · 第 5 / 25 章 · 2000–3000 · 英中對照

用互動版查詞、朗讀與收藏

The fairy led them to a garage beneath an abandoned food store. A rough carpet covered the entrance, hiding a trapdoor that opened onto a room of old engines. The smell of bacon floated from a small stove where the mechanic Juno was cooking breakfast. She looked up when Theo began to cough. 'There is still dust in your lungs,' she said, examining his face. His jaw tightened as he tried not to complain. Juno had once been engaged to a city engineer who disappeared after refusing to change government records. Since then she had taken responsibility for repairing machines without asking who could afford to pay. She offered Theo water and a handful of peanuts, then listened to the story of the tower. When Iris showed her the signed order, Juno made a quiet sound of sorrow. 'They used my motor design,' she said. 'I never agreed to this.'

機械精靈帶他們來到廢棄食品店下方的車庫。入口鋪著粗糙地毯，遮住通往舊引擎室的活板門。小爐子上正煎著培根，香氣飄滿室內。技師朱諾抬起頭，看見西奧咳嗽，便檢查他的臉。「你肺裡還有灰塵。」她說。西奧咬緊下顎，努力不抱怨。朱諾曾與一位市府工程師訂婚，對方因拒絕更改政府紀錄而失蹤。此後她承擔起修理機器的責任，不再問人們是否付得起錢。她給西奧水與一把花生，聽完高塔的故事。艾莉絲拿出簽名命令時，朱諾難過地低嘆。「他們用了我的馬達設計，」她說，「我從沒答應這種事。」

Juno switched on a microphone and played a recorded announcement. The voice sounded exactly like Iris's mother. It instructed outer districts to surrender their water tanks for a future restoration project. 'Fake,' Iris said immediately. Her mother had never used the word restoration that way. Juno enlarged the audio pattern and found where two recordings had been joined. Someone had taken genuine speeches and created a new message. Mara asked Juno to pronounce a difficult technical term, then compared it with the voice on the recording. The rhythm was completely different. Even Theo could hear the cut. The government had used stolen recordings to persuade citizens to give up their wells. A considerable amount of public money had gone into that campaign. 'Words can be weapons,' Mara said. 'But they can also reveal where a lie has been stitched together.'

朱諾打開麥克風，播放一則錄音公告。聲音和艾莉絲的母親完全一樣，內容卻要求外圍區居民為了未來的復原計畫交出水槽。「假的。」艾莉絲立刻說。母親不會那樣使用「復原」一詞。朱諾放大音訊波形，找出兩段錄音接合的位置。有人截取真正的演說，拼成新訊息。瑪拉請朱諾唸出一個困難的技術詞，再和錄音比較，節奏完全不同，連西奧也聽得出剪接處。政府利用偷來的聲音說服人民交出水井，還投入相當龐大的公共預算。「語言能成為武器，」瑪拉說，「也能揭露謊言縫合的地方。」

Juno also showed them a dusty repair ledger. The same motor model had been sold to three hospitals, two prisons, and the city's richest residential tower. Each buyer had paid a different price. The records were not proof of a crime by themselves, yet they pointed toward the network controlling heat and water. Iris photographed them carefully. Whenever the journey felt overwhelming, she reminded herself that answers often emerged from many ordinary details, not one dramatic discovery.

朱諾還展示一本滿是灰塵的維修帳冊。同一型號馬達被賣給三家醫院、兩座監獄與城中最富有的住宅高塔，每位買家價格都不同。紀錄本身不足以證明犯罪，卻指向控制水與熱的網絡。艾莉絲仔細拍照。旅程令她不知所措時，她提醒自己，答案往往來自許多平凡細節，而不是一次戲劇性的發現。

The garage door rattled. Someone had found them. Juno switched off the light and handed Iris a flashlight. Beyond a narrow passage stood a covered vehicle, its wheels removed and its engine cold. 'There's a spare motor, and three sacks of parts,' Juno whispered. 'If we install it, the vehicle can move through the maintenance tunnels.' Theo reached for a tool, but a violent blow shook the outer door. Steam burst from a loose pipe. Juno shouted instructions while Iris held the flashlight and Pip sorted bolts. They worked quickly, hands shaking in the heat. Juno's younger assistant arrived with a basket of ripe fruit rescued from the tower greenhouse. She had been carrying it to sick children, but turned back when she heard the alarm. There would be enough for everyone if they lived to share it.

車庫門突然作響，有人找到他們了。朱諾關燈，交給艾莉絲一支手電筒。窄通道外有輛蓋住的車，車輪已拆，引擎冰冷。「有備用馬達，還有三袋零件，」朱諾低聲說，「裝好後這輛車就能走維修隧道。」西奧伸手拿工具，外門卻遭猛烈撞擊，鬆脫的管子噴出蒸汽。朱諾高喊指令，艾莉絲舉燈，皮普分類螺栓。他們在熱氣中顫抖著加快工作。朱諾的年輕助手帶著一籃從高塔溫室救出的熟水果進來。她本要送水果給生病的孩子，聽見警報才折返。只要大家能活下來，就能分到足夠的食物。

At last the vehicle started. Juno drove through a wall of dust while the patrol smashed into the empty garage behind them. They emerged onto an old road beneath the northern bridge. On the radio, Commander Rusk announced that Iris and her companions were dangerous criminals who had attacked a hospital. The lie made Iris furious, but she forced herself to listen. In the background of Rusk's broadcast she heard a short mechanical tone identical to the one on her mother's false recording. Pip traced it to a transmitter near the old weather station. 'That's where the voices come from,' he said. Juno steered north. Iris opened the map on her knees and marked a new destination. The seed bank could wait one more night. If they did not break the city's false-message system, every truthful warning they sent would be buried beneath a louder lie.

車子終於發動。朱諾駕車衝過塵牆，巡邏隊在後方撞進空車庫。他們來到北橋下的舊公路。無線電裡，魯斯克指揮官宣布艾莉絲一行是攻擊醫院的危險罪犯。這謊言讓艾莉絲暴怒，但她強迫自己繼續聽。魯斯克廣播的背景裡，有一聲短促的機械音，與母親假錄音裡的聲音完全相同。皮普追查到訊號來自舊氣象站附近的發射器。「那些聲音就是從那裡來的。」他說。朱諾往北開。艾莉絲在膝上展開地圖，標上一個新目的地。種子庫還能再等一夜。如果不先摧毀假訊息系統，他們發出的每個真實警告，都會被更響亮的謊言淹沒。
