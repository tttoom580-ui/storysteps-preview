失去的一週

The Missing Week

種子帳冊 · 第 9 / 25 章 · 2000–3000 · 英中對照 · 校訂中

On the next day the travelers reached a research cabin beside a dried lake. Its door was locked, but Nell opened it remotely through the station's repaired web. Inside, dusty screens displayed a database of the city's weather records. Every file from one particular week had been erased. Theo guessed that the missing entries covered the moment the old river disappeared. Iris felt a sudden fright when a loud horn sounded outside. A wounded courier stumbled into the room, blood beginning to bleed through his bandage. He had escaped Commander Rusk's men with an important message: the city's water reserve was not empty. It had been moved into a hidden underground lake. The courier pointed toward a stone staircase behind the cabin, then collapsed. Juno was skillful at first aid, but they had to move quickly if they wanted to keep him alive.

隔天一行人抵達乾湖旁的研究小屋。門雖上鎖，妮爾仍透過車站修復的網路遠端打開。室內滿是灰塵的螢幕顯示城市氣象資料庫，有整整一週的檔案被刪掉。西奧猜那就是古河流消失的時刻。外頭突然響起號角，艾莉絲嚇了一跳。一名受傷信差跌進房裡，血從繃帶滲出。他從魯斯克部下手裡逃脫，帶來重要訊息：城市儲水量並未耗盡，而是被轉移到隱藏的地下湖。信差指向小屋後方的石梯，隨後倒下。朱諾很擅長急救，但若要保住他的命，他們必須立刻行動。

They carried him to a table and cleaned the wound. His condition was serious but not hopeless. Mara searched for medicine while Iris explored the back room. An old sink had cracked in half, and water continued to splash from a pipe beneath it. The sound made Iris remember the strange whistle that had led her to Orin. She tuned a radio to the same frequency. A woman's voice spoke from the speaker, so faint that Iris almost missed it. The voice belonged to her mother. It was a weekly report, recorded years earlier, describing a project to restore the mountain river. Then came laughter in the background, followed by a warning that the project had been taken over by security forces. Her mother mentioned a secret weapon designed to redirect storms. The recording ended before she could name the person in charge.

他們把信差抬到桌上清理傷口。情況嚴重，卻還有希望。瑪拉找藥時，艾莉絲探索後屋。舊水槽裂成兩半，下方水管不斷濺水，聲音使她想起奧林帳篷裡的怪哨聲。她把收音機調到同一頻率，喇叭傳出細微的女人聲音，她差點沒聽見。那是母親的聲音。這是一段多年前錄製的每週報告，描述恢復山河的計畫。背景傳來笑聲，接著母親警告計畫已被治安部隊接管。她提到一種能改變風暴方向的祕密武器，錄音卻在她說出主導者姓名之前結束。

The cabin's public bulletin praised democracy, yet the records showed that only selected residents had been invited to meetings. Iris thought about the lover mentioned in one missing employee's letter, a person who had searched for answers for years. An officer had dismissed her questions as something that would bore the council. Theo shook his head. 'They called it democracy because people were allowed to watch, not because they could decide.' Iris kept the letter with the others; a personal story could expose what an official table concealed.

小屋的公開告示歌頌民主，但紀錄顯示只有特定居民受邀開會。艾莉絲想到一封失蹤員工的信提到的戀人；那人多年來努力找答案，官員卻說她的問題只會讓議會厭煩。西奧搖頭：「他們稱之為民主，只因人們能旁觀，卻不能決定。」艾莉絲把那封信收好；個人的故事有時能揭露官方表格遮掩的事。

Iris was angry that the records had been hidden for so long. Theo told her that anger could not replace evidence. Together they compared the surviving files with printed schedules on the cabin wall. The last day's record showed a sudden increase in underground pressure, followed shortly by the disappearance of three workers. One was Pip's father. Another was Iris's mother. The third name was hidden under a smear of ink. Outside, the sky darkened. A mechanical roar rose from the mountains, and the cabin windows shook. Iris looked at the courier, who had begun to regain consciousness. He whispered that the stairs led to an old observation chamber with a view of the underground lake. 'Don't wander past the blue lights,' he warned. 'The guards have flooded the lower floor.'

艾莉絲氣這些紀錄隱藏了如此多年。西奧提醒她，憤怒不能代替證據。姊弟比較剩餘檔案與小屋牆上印好的時刻表。最後一天的紀錄顯示地下壓力突然上升，接著不久便有三名工人失蹤。其中一人是皮普的父親，另一人是艾莉絲的母親，第三個名字被墨跡遮住。外頭天空暗下，山裡傳來機械轟鳴，窗戶跟著顫動。信差漸漸清醒，低聲說石梯通往舊觀察室，能看見地下湖。「不要走過藍燈，」他警告，「守衛把下層灌滿水了。」

As night fell, Iris descended the stairs with Theo and Pip, leaving Mara and Juno to care for the courier. Their lamps swept across wet stone and exposed pipes. Beneath them they heard water moving in a vast chamber. They reached a narrow bridge where the blue warning lights began. On the opposite side stood a rusted cabinet containing an emergency radio. Theo climbed carefully along the rail and pulled it open. Inside was a printed photograph of a man and woman standing beside a machine. Iris recognized her mother, but the man was Commander Rusk, much younger than in the current broadcasts. They looked like colleagues, not enemies. The photograph was dated during the missing week. Across the bottom her mother had written: ASK HIM WHAT HE PROMISED. Iris could hardly breathe. The mystery had suddenly become personal in a way no database could explain.

夜幕降臨時，艾莉絲與西奧、皮普走下石梯，留瑪拉和朱諾照顧信差。燈光掃過濕石與外露管線，下面巨大空間裡傳來水流聲。他們走到窄橋，藍色警示燈便從此開始。對岸生鏽的櫃子裡有緊急無線電。西奧小心沿欄杆爬過去，打開櫃門，裡面放著一張印好的照片：一男一女站在機器旁。艾莉絲認出母親，男子卻是年輕許多的魯斯克指揮官。兩人看起來像同事，而非敵人。照片日期正是失去的那週。照片下母親寫道：「問他答應過什麼。」艾莉絲幾乎喘不過氣。這謎團突然變得私密，沒有任何資料庫能解釋。
