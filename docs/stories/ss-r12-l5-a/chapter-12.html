玻璃後的證人

A Witness Behind the Glass

潮汐之城：第十三道閘門 · 第 12 / 25 章 · 4000–5000 · 英中對照 · 校訂中

Rui received a message from a former gate technician who wished to remain an observer rather than become the face of the investigation. The woman had witnessed workers bolt the inland door shut after a private meeting. She was frightened because another employee had been taken hostage during a wage dispute years earlier, and she feared a similar threat. Elara refused to confront her without consent. Instead, the team arranged a safe meeting in a public library room, where the witness could leave at any time. An attorney attended to explain what information could be shared without identifying family members. The witness said she had been ordered to change a maintenance script, but the wording was so abnormal that she kept a copy. She did not know who wrote the order. That uncertainty, Elara assured her, was important to preserve.

芮收到一名離職閘門技師的訊息。她希望只當觀察者，不想成為調查的公開面孔。她曾目睹工人在一場私人會議後把內陸水門鎖死，卻因數年前另一名員工在薪資糾紛中被扣為人質，害怕自己也受威脅。艾拉拒絕在沒有同意時逼她對質。團隊改在公共圖書館安排安全會面，證人隨時可以離開，律師則解釋哪些資訊可以分享而不暴露家人。證人說自己曾被要求改寫維護程序，但命令措辭異常，她因此留下副本。她不知道命令是誰寫的。艾拉向她保證，這種不確定性必須保留下來。

The witness had worked with a missionary school to teach engineering apprentices, but she disliked the common norm that older technicians must obey superiors without question. She once refused to sign a certificate whose date was incompatible with her work log. Afterward, a supervisor called her a traitor and hinted that she might lose her job. Another colleague mumbled that the order came from the top, although he had never seen its author. None of these memories established a crime, and the attorney warned against turning them into a story of kidnap or conspiracy without supporting evidence. Elara asked the witness to mark which sentences she recalled clearly and which she could only infer. The distinction protected both the people accused and the person speaking.

證人曾和一間傳教學校合作，教工程學徒，卻不喜歡老技師必須無條件服從上司的慣例。她有一次拒絕簽署日期與工作日誌不相容的證明，主管便罵她是叛徒，暗示要讓她失業。另一名同事含糊說命令來自高層，實際卻未見過作者。這些記憶都不能單獨證明犯罪，律師提醒，不可在沒有旁證時就寫成綁架或陰謀。艾拉請證人標出哪些話記得清楚，哪些只是推測。這個區別既保護被指控的人，也保護發言者。

The witness wanted to acquaint the reviewers with the social world of the old pump house, not just its paperwork. Workers of different backgrounds had once shared stock from a common tool room, yet discrimination determined who was promoted. A trainee whose family had survived continental displacement described the sensation of being treated as permanently foreign. Another mentioned stories of slavery from earlier generations and explained why threats of losing wages could silence people even without physical force. Elara asked the reviewers to keep those experiences in the record without using them to accuse particular workers of crimes. They were compatible with several explanations of why the disputed instruction went unchallenged. The witness thanked her. She had feared that telling the whole story would invite another spectacle. Instead, the archive would preserve a difficult context while the technical evidence received its own careful examination.

證人希望審查者不只認識泵房文件，也了解當時的社會環境。不同背景工人曾共用工具室庫存，升遷卻受歧視影響。一名家族經歷跨大陸流離的學徒，描述自己一直被當成外來者的感受。另一人提及前幾代遭奴役的故事，說明即使沒有肢體威脅，失去薪資的恐懼也能使人沉默。艾拉請審查者保留這些經歷，卻不要據此指控特定工人犯罪。這些背景可能符合多種解釋，說明為何爭議命令長期未受質疑。證人感謝她。她原怕講出全貌只會變成另一場表演，如今檔案將保留艱難脈絡，技術證據也能接受獨立審視。

Nessa asked whether the woman wanted viewers of the public hearing to see her face. She refused and offered to submit a written statement through the library instead. Her choice did not diminish the account's usefulness; the council could still compare her copy of the script with the original work orders. Rui prepared a reading room where the records could be checked by an independent lawyer and two trained observers. Elara explained that the aim was not to force witnesses to become symbols of courage. No one should have to sacrifice employment or privacy simply to make a viable engineering question receive attention. The witness agreed to have her statement examined, provided the city did not reveal her address. By evening, the team had a new piece of documentary evidence and a firm condition attached to it.

妮莎問她是否願意讓公開聽證的觀眾看到自己的臉。她拒絕，改由圖書館提交書面證詞。這個選擇不會降低證詞的價值；議會仍能把她的維護程序副本和原工作指令相比。芮準備一間閱覽室，讓獨立律師和兩名受訓觀察員查核紀錄。艾拉說，目的不是逼證人成為勇氣象徵；沒有人應因一個值得檢驗的工程問題而被迫犧牲工作或隱私。證人同意接受文件查核，條件是城市不能公布住址。傍晚，團隊多了一份新書證，也有一項必須遵守的保護條件。

The copied order revealed a curious instruction: “When the outer tide approaches its maximum, lock the inner release before closing the harbor door.” It was the reverse of the sequence Ivo remembered. Elara did not assume a malicious motive. The procedure might have been changed for a legitimate reason, or copied incorrectly during an earlier revision. She invited Kestrel to explain its technical basis and gave him the same document without the witness's identifying details. He studied it twice and shook his head. “If we do this under a heavy tide,” he said, “pressure could remain trapped beneath the lower streets.” His answer confirmed a possible danger, not the source of the command. At the bottom of the paper, a small number linked it to the council's contract office. Rui began searching for the matching register.

程序副本裡有一句奇怪的指示：「外潮接近最高點時，應先鎖住內陸洩洪口，再關閉港口門。」順序與伊沃記得的正好相反。艾拉沒有立刻推定惡意；可能確有正當原因，也可能早先修訂時抄錯。她把去除證人身分資料的文件交給凱斯特，請他說明技術依據。他看了兩遍，搖頭說：「如果高水位時這樣做，壓力可能困在低地街道下。」這只證實可能的危險，尚未找出命令來源。紙張底端有個小編號，連到議會合約辦公室。芮開始搜尋對應登記簿。
