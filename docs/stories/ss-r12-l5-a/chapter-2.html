比河道更老的屋子

The House Built Before the River

潮汐之城：第十三道閘門 · 第 2 / 25 章 · 4000–5000 · 英中對照 · 校訂完成

Rui opened a narrow drawer of plans drawn before Saltglass acquired its stone embankment. The drawings revealed an absurd mistake in the official exhibition: the museum had labeled an indigenous fishing settlement as an empty marsh. That error made the city's architecture appear to begin with the governors who displaced the settlement. A donor had paid for the exhibition and preferred heroic stories of conquest. Rui highlighted the oldest footpaths and found that several crossed the present canal at places where the water still moved strangely. The adverse effects of closing those paths were absent from the engineers' report. Elara asked to invite local families to correct the map, not merely to decorate it with names. “History does not become less scientific when the people who lived it are allowed to speak,” Rui said.

芮打開一個狹長抽屜，裡面是鹽鏡城興建石堤以前的圖紙。圖紙揭穿博物館展示裡荒謬的錯誤：展覽把原住漁民聚落標成無人沼澤，使城市建築史看起來從驅逐聚落的總督才開始。一名捐助者支付了展覽費用，偏好開拓英雄的故事。芮標出最古老的小徑，發現其中幾條穿越現今水道，位置恰好有異常水流。封閉那些路徑可能帶來的不利影響，工程報告完全沒提。艾拉提議請當地家族真正修正地圖，而不只是添上名字。芮說：「讓曾經生活在這裡的人開口，歷史不會因此比較不科學。」

At the fish market, journalist Nessa insisted that good journalism could not rest on a picturesque story alone. She had published a series of reports on canal safety, but a wealthy editor wanted a single villain. Her quest was to learn who had altered the drawings and why, not to render judgment before seeing records. An outsider from the north offered to identify the guilty official in exchange for money. Nessa refused. He grew angry and threatened prosecution against anyone who repeated his name. During the argument, a cook ripped open a crate of salmon to rescue fish trapped beneath a broken lid. The ordinary interruption reminded Elara that market families had work to finish while officials fought over ideology. Even a variation of a few inches in tide height could ruin their catch.

魚市場裡，記者妮莎堅持好的新聞工作不能只靠動人的故事。她已發表一系列水道安全報導，但有錢的編輯只想找一個壞人。她要追查誰改了圖、為何改圖，而不是先下判斷。一名北方外來者提出，只要給錢就能指出有罪官員；妮莎拒絕。他惱怒地威脅，任何重述他姓名的人都會遭起訴。爭吵間，一名廚師撕開鮭魚木箱，救出壓在破蓋底下的魚。這平凡的插曲讓艾拉想起，官員爭辯意識形態時，市場家庭仍有工作要做。潮位即使只差幾吋，也足以毀掉漁獲。

The discussion at the fish market continued after the formal interviews. Sela said that an excessive appetite for quick construction had made officials overlook slow changes in the shore. The old footpaths persisted through generations because fishermen repaired them every winter; they had not appeared by accident. Nessa accidentally ripped a corner of her notebook while turning a page, and Sela laughed at the way a tiny tear could frighten a journalist more than a wave. Then she grew serious. She wanted her grandchildren to inherit a working harbor, not a romantic picture of one. Elara promised to include seasonal maintenance in the cost comparison. The promise was neither expensive nor spectacular, but it changed what counted as a complete design. A city needed to account for the people who would repair a structure long after the opening ceremony ended.

魚市場的討論在正式訪談後還沒結束。瑟拉說，官員過度急於施工，才忽略岸線緩慢變化。舊小徑能持續數代，是因漁民每年冬天都修理，不是偶然出現。妮莎翻頁時不小心撕破筆記本一角，瑟拉笑說小小裂口竟比浪還讓記者緊張，隨後嚴肅起來。她希望孫輩繼承能使用的港口，而不是浪漫的港口圖片。艾拉答應把季節維護列進成本比較。這項承諾不昂貴也不戲劇化，卻改變了完整設計的定義。城市必須計算在剪綵多年後仍要修理結構的人們。

An elderly boatwoman named Sela welcomed them under a canvas awning. Years of rowing had left her shoulders stiff, and her granddaughter paused to massage one arm before Sela unfolded a family chart. The chart showed a seasonal passage between two islands, wide enough for fishing boats but not for freight barges. Elara compared it with the ministry's river model and saw why the model exaggerated the useful size of the new gate. The ministry had treated the old channel as if it were a straight pipe. In reality, reeds, shallow bends, and small gardens slowed the water. Sela warned that removing those obstacles too rapidly could send a wave directly toward houses. Elara promised to measure them before offering conclusions, while Nessa asked whether Sela wished to be named in the story.

年長船娘瑟拉在帆布棚下接待他們。多年划船讓她肩膀僵硬，孫女先替她按摩一隻手臂，她才攤開家傳水道圖。圖上有兩島之間的季節航道，漁船能過，大型貨駁卻過不了。艾拉拿它對照部會模型，終於明白為何模型誇大新閘門的運輸能力：當局把古河道當成直管，忽略蘆葦、淺彎與小菜園會減緩水流。瑟拉提醒，若太快移除那些阻礙，浪可能直接衝向房屋。艾拉承諾先量測才下結論；妮莎則問瑟拉是否願意在報導裡具名。

By sunset, the historical error had turned into a practical decision. Rui reserved the archive table for a public map-reading session, and Nessa agreed to present competing interpretations instead of one dramatic headline. The donor could still defend the exhibition, but the erased families would have an opportunity to answer. Sela marked a crossing beneath an old bakery and said it had once carried the flood toward open fields. If the council had sealed that crossing, she reasoned, the extra water must now travel elsewhere. Elara drew two possible routes in different inks. One led to the industrial docks, the other to a crowded neighborhood below the hill. Neither line could yet be trusted. The only honest next step was to enter the old drainage house before construction workers filled it with cement.

日落前，歷史上的錯誤變成實際決策。芮保留檔案室桌子供公開閱圖，妮莎答應報導不同解釋，而不是只下聳動標題。捐助者仍能替展覽辯護，被抹除的家庭也有回答的機會。瑟拉標出舊麵包店底下的一條通道，說洪水以前會沿那裡退往田地。若議會封住通道，多餘的水如今必定改走別處。艾拉用不同顏色畫出兩條可能路線，一條通向工業碼頭，一條通往山腳擁擠的住宅區。兩條線都還不能確信。下一步只能趁工人灌水泥前，進入舊排水屋查看。
