玻璃上的三道刻痕

Three Marks on the Glass

潮汐之城：第十三道閘門 · 第 1 / 25 章 · 4000–5000 · 英中對照 · 校訂中

On the first morning of the spring tide, Elara Venn received a batch of water-level sheets at the Saltglass pumping house. She was thirty-one, old enough to distrust dramatic announcements and young enough to believe that accurate drawings could still save a city. A loose circuit made the alarm lamp blink while her mentor, retired gate keeper Tomas, showed her three marks cut into the observation glass. The new council projection promised a decent margin of safety, yet the actual water stood above the highest mark. A nonprofit sailors' clinic had sent the sheets because the lower streets had flooded twice without a storm. Tomas pointed toward the river. “Water does not obey speeches,” he said. “And a measurement is only useful when someone is prepared to hear it.” Elara copied the figures instead of accepting the printed forecast.

春潮第一天早上，三十一歲的水工繪圖師艾拉在鹽鏡城抽水房收到一批潮位紀錄。她早已不再輕信激昂宣言，卻仍相信精確圖紙可以救一座城市。鬆動的電路使警報燈忽明忽暗，退休閘門守護員、也是她導師的托瑪斯，指給她看觀測玻璃上的三道刻痕。議會的新預測宣稱有充足安全餘裕，實際水位卻高過最高刻痕。非營利的水手診所寄來紀錄，因為低地街道明明沒有風暴，卻已淹水兩次。托瑪斯望向河流說：「水不服從演說。量測只有在有人肯聽時才有用。」艾拉抄下數字，而不接受印好的預測。

Outside, Hadi the boatbuilder was tightening the saddle on a delivery bicycle when a sudden surge struck the stone rim of the quay. The resulting strain shook the wooden bridge. Children cried, and a shopkeeper warned that the rising water would terrify customers into leaving. Across the channel, the council's banners promised to triple the shipping capacity by completing a thirteenth gate. Hadi had repaired boats through four bad seasons and knew the banner concealed a more difficult question: where would the displaced water go? He offered Elara a ride to the old inspection stairs. They watched a muddy ribbon move against the expected current, as though a hidden passage were pulling water inland. Neither called it proof. Their first task was to learn whether the movement occurred at every tide or only when the new machines operated.

外頭，造船匠哈迪正綁緊送貨腳踏車的坐墊，一股突來的潮浪撞上碼頭石緣，壓力震得木橋晃動。孩子哭了，店主擔心漲水會嚇走客人。對岸的議會布條保證，第十三道閘門完工後，貨運量能增為三倍。修過四季破船的哈迪知道，布條掩蓋了更難回答的問題：被擠走的水究竟流向哪裡？他載艾拉前往舊巡檢階梯。他們看到一條混濁的水帶逆著預期水流移動，彷彿有暗道把水吸往內陸。兩人都沒有急著稱它為證據；首要任務是查明這現象每次潮來都會發生，還是只在新機具運轉時才出現。

Before the archive visit, Elara added a small experiment to her notebook. She placed three cups at different heights on the inspection stairs and marked how quickly water reached each one. The cups did not measure the whole river, but they showed how a small height difference could change the first street to flood. Hadi helped carry the cups, then asked why the city had never posted a similar explanation at the market. Elara said engineers often wrote for other engineers, forgetting that the people living beside their machines needed to read the warnings too. She drew a simple chart for the clinic noticeboard. By evening, the first copy was already stained with fingerprints from curious residents. Nobody mistook the chart for a guarantee, and that was exactly why it might be useful.

前往檔案室前，艾拉在筆記本增加一個小實驗。她把三個杯子放在巡檢階梯不同高度，標出水多久才碰到杯緣。杯子不能量測整條河，卻能顯示微小高差如何改變哪條街先淹水。哈迪幫忙搬杯，問城市為何從不在市場張貼類似說明。艾拉說工程師常只寫給工程師看，忘了住在機具旁的人也得讀懂警報。她畫了簡單圖表貼在診所告示板；傍晚第一份影本已滿是好奇居民的指印。沒有人把圖表誤認成保證，這正是它可能有用的原因。

The present gate regime allowed engineers to close a channel after a single official inquiry, but it required three independent readings before anyone could implement a permanent change. Elara discovered that the most recent inspection report contained only two. The missing page described the placement of a balancing reservoir; someone had removed it after the archive was copied. Tomas suggested using an older slate gauge to reinforce the result, rather than accusing the ministry of theft. His advice annoyed Elara because it would take another day, and another day meant another tide. Yet a hurried accusation could dissolve the fragile cooperation between dock workers and surveyors. She asked Rui, a junior clerk at the council archive, for permission to examine the original register. Rui replied that the ledger was available, but only if every person entering the room signed a public log.

現行閘門制度允許工程師在一次正式調查後暫時封閉水道，但永久變更必須有三份獨立讀數。艾拉發現最新巡檢報告只有兩份，缺頁原本記載平衡水池的位置；有人在檔案複製後取走了那頁。托瑪斯主張先用舊式石板水尺加強證據，不要立刻指控部會偷竊。艾拉不喜歡這個建議，因為又要花上一天，而每天都有潮水。然而草率指控也可能瓦解碼頭工人與測量員之間脆弱的合作。她向議會檔案室的年輕書記芮申請查閱原簿。芮回覆，簿冊可以看，但所有進入檔案室的人都必須在公開登記表簽名。

“An idiot could see that the numbers are wrong,” Hadi muttered. Elara answered that an idiot could see a difference, but a careful person had to explain it. Their first breakthrough was modest: the old and new gauges agreed within a finger's width, regardless of which crew read them. They recorded the error, photographed the three marks, and sent copies to the dock assembly and the council at the same time. That evening the city's bell announced a ceremony for the thirteenth gate. Elara did not want to stop a useful project simply because she disliked the mayor. She wanted a design that named the streets at risk and a way for residents to challenge its assumptions. When the bell sounded again, water appeared on the dry side of the inspection wall. Something underneath the city was already changing direction.

「傻子都看得出數字不對。」哈迪咕噥。艾拉說，傻子看得出差異，細心的人卻得解釋差異。第一項突破其實很小：無論由哪組人讀取，新舊水尺的結果都只差不到一指寬。他們記下誤差，拍攝三道刻痕，同時把副本送交碼頭集會與議會。傍晚，城鐘宣布將為第十三道閘門舉行典禮。艾拉不想只因討厭市長就阻止有用的工程；她要的是說清哪些街道有風險的設計，以及居民能質疑其假設的途徑。鐘再次響起時，巡檢牆原本乾燥的一側竟冒出水來。城市地下某處，水流已經改變方向。
