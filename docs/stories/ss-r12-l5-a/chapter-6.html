朝內陸開的門

The Door That Opens Inland

潮汐之城：第十三道閘門 · 第 6 / 25 章 · 4000–5000 · 英中對照 · 校訂中

The archive revealed that the thirteenth gate had once been called the Inland Relief Door. Its purpose was not to shut the sea out completely but to let water escape into an empty basin before the tide reached the homes. Elara was startled by the abrupt change in the current construction plan. A private entity had acquired the basin and drawn a warehouse over it. An activist from the lower district carried evidence in a battered briefcase, but the council dismissed him as hostile to progress. His name was Beren. He had lived on the frontier of the old marsh before his family was forced into exile from their rented land. Elara offered him a place at the map table without asking him to accept her conclusions. An injured pedestrian interrupted their discussion, reminding everyone that the changed road had already become dangerous.

檔案揭露，第十三道閘門舊名為「內陸洩洪門」，目的不是完全阻擋海水，而是在潮水抵達住宅前，讓水流進空置的平衡池。艾拉震驚於現行施工圖突如其來的改動：一家私人法人取得平衡池土地，打算在上面蓋倉庫。低地運動者貝倫提著破舊公事包帶來證據，議會卻說他敵視進步。他家過去住在舊沼澤邊緣，後來被迫離開租地。艾拉請他到地圖桌旁，不要求他先同意自己的結論。此時一名受傷行人打斷討論，提醒所有人：改道的道路已經造成危險。

At a crowded seminar, an official claimed that a new outlet in the harbor would repay the city for every expense. A worker with a bandaged thigh disagreed. He had supervised an excavation and knew the outlet sat higher than the lower streets during storms. Engineers could not simply instruct water to run uphill. The official praised masculine courage and suggested the workers were too frightened to continue. Hadi called that argument naive. Courage could not replace a pressure measurement, and the city's obligation was to protect workers who reported faults. The debate became sharper when a doctor said a patient needed an injection at the clinic that afternoon, yet the hospital cart was trapped behind a fence. Elara asked the chair to record the immediate access failure separately from the land dispute.

一場擁擠的研討會上，官員聲稱港口新排水口將讓城市收回所有成本。一名大腿包著繃帶的工人反對。他曾監督挖掘，知道暴雨時排水口水位比低地街道更高；工程師不能命令水往上流。官員稱讚男子氣概，暗示工人不敢繼續施工。哈迪說這很天真：勇氣代替不了壓力量測，城市有義務保護通報缺失的工人。一名醫師又指出，病患下午需要注射藥物，醫療車卻被圍欄擋住。艾拉要求主席把眼前的通行問題與土地爭議分開記錄。

Before the hearing ended, Beren showed the committee how the former reed fields stored surplus water during unusually wet winters. A series of shallow ponds had once filled in sequence, allowing the village to endure a high tide without sacrificing its houses. The ponds were gone, but their stone edges remained beneath the soil. Elara asked permission to dig a narrow inspection trench, promising to restore the ground afterward. The landowner agreed only if a neutral observer documented every step. Beren accepted the condition. He had spent years being described as a troublemaker and wanted the evidence to speak without depending entirely on his reputation. The test recovered an old channel lining, and the committee agreed that the area merited a wider survey. Nothing was yet decided about ownership, but its importance was no longer invisible.

聽證結束前，貝倫向委員會說明，過去蘆葦地在異常潮濕的冬季如何容納多餘水量。一連串淺池依序蓄水，讓村落不必犧牲住宅也能撐過高潮。池子已不見，石緣仍埋在土裡。艾拉申請開一條窄探溝，承諾事後復原。地主只在中立觀察員逐步記錄的條件下同意，貝倫接受。他多年來被稱作麻煩人物，希望證據不必完全仰賴自己名聲。測試挖到舊水道內襯，委員會同意擴大勘查。土地所有權仍未決定，但這片地方的重要性不再隱形。

Beren took Elara to the hillside where his family used to grow reeds. He did not expect her to admire the place: broken posts marked the plots, and the soil was too salty for vegetables. Still, a narrow depression ran toward the abandoned reservoir. He asked her to contemplate what would happen if that depression were filled. Elara placed her folding level at three points and found a gradual slope toward the basin. Construction would disrupt that slope, turning the old relief passage into a wall. She photographed the measurements with Beren's consent and marked the limits of the evidence. The findings could show a likely drainage route, but they did not establish whether the developer knew the danger. That question belonged to a different inquiry, with different documents and witnesses.

貝倫帶艾拉上山坡，指給她看家族從前種蘆葦的地方。地上只剩破木柱，土壤鹽分太高，種不了蔬菜；一道淺溝仍通往荒廢的水池。他要艾拉想想，如果填平那條溝會怎樣。艾拉在三處放上折疊水準儀，發現地勢確實緩緩朝水池下降。工程會破壞坡度，把舊洩洪道變成牆。她在貝倫同意後拍下量測，也清楚標示證據限制。結果能顯示可能的排水路線，卻不能證明開發商早就知道危險；那是需要另找文件與證人的問題。

Back in the archive, Rui located the purchase agreement for the basin. It contained an unusual condition: the buyer could use the land only if an alternative public relief route remained available. Elara called it an important clue, not an automatic victory. The company might have believed the harbor outlet served that purpose. Kestrel agreed to compare both routes under the same simulated storm. For the first time he and Elara worked side by side, although each kept an independent copy of the numbers. At the end of the day, the model showed the harbor outlet becoming submerged before the lower district reached its danger mark. The inland route remained possible, provided the basin was not built over. Council members would now have to decide whether a warehouse was worth the cost of losing a way for the water to leave.

回到檔案室，芮找到水池土地的購買契約，其中有一項罕見條件：買方只有在保留另一條公共洩洪路線時才能使用土地。艾拉稱它為重要線索，不是必勝武器；公司可能相信港口排水口符合條件。凱斯特答應在同一場模擬暴雨下比較兩條路線。兩人第一次並肩工作，卻各自保留數據副本。結果顯示，低地到達危險水位前，港口排水口就已被淹沒；只要不在水池上建屋，內陸路線仍可行。議員如今得決定，倉庫是否值得讓城市失去退水之路。
