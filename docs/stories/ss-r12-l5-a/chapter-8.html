雨水的專利

The Patent of Rain

潮汐之城：第十三道閘門 · 第 8 / 25 章 · 4000–5000 · 英中對照 · 校訂完成

The city had granted a patent to the maker of the new gate sensors. Under its terms, only approved operators could read the full record, and the premium for access cost more than a dock worker earned in a month. Elara approached the manufacturer with caution. She did not object to paying for an invention, but a device that directed public water could not hide its error measurements from the people at risk. An undergraduate assistant at the engineering school helped her inspect a spare sensor. The interior contained an ordinary pressure tube and a clever mechanism that generated a written trace. Its sole unusual feature was a locked recording case. The assistant said he could explain how the case worked, but refused to break the seal without permission. Elara admired his ethical stance.

城市把新閘門感測器的專利授給一家製造商。條款規定只有核准操作員能讀取完整紀錄，而取得資料的額外費用比碼頭工人一個月薪資還高。艾拉小心地接觸廠商。她不反對發明人收費，但指揮公共水流的設備不能把誤差藏起來，不讓受影響的人知道。工程學校的一名大學生協助檢查備用感測器。內部有普通的壓力管和巧妙的描記裝置，唯一特殊之處是上鎖的記錄盒。學生說他能說明原理，卻拒絕在沒有授權時破壞封條。艾拉欣賞他的倫理立場。

The manufacturer's delegation arrived at the school in an expensive carriage, bringing a thick catalogue and a dreadful warning about stolen technology. Their speaker asked Elara to dedicate the meeting to cooperation rather than accusation. She agreed and began by asking for clarity about what the patent actually protected. Was it the sensor design, the written trace, or the measurements produced by a public installation? The delegates disagreed among themselves. One treated data as a commodity to be sold; another admitted that public officials might already own the readings they purchased with tax money. Rui recorded the distinction in the minutes. A technical argument had opened a civic question: how could residents verify decisions when the relevant numbers passed through a private lock?

製造商代表團乘著昂貴馬車抵達學校，帶來厚厚型錄，也嚴厲警告技術遭竊的危險。發言人要求艾拉把會議用於合作，而非指控。她同意，先請他們清楚說明專利究竟保護什麼：是感測器設計、描記紙帶，還是公共設備產生的讀數？代表自己都意見不同。一人把資料當成商品出售，另一人承認用稅金購買的讀數或許已屬公眾。芮把差異寫進紀錄。技術辯論於是變成市政問題：當相關數字被私人鎖住，居民怎麼驗證政府的決定？

To help residents follow the test, Rui asked a teacher with fluency in several local languages to explain the mechanism without specialized jargon. The teacher used a wooden boat and a bowl of water to show how operators would steer pressure away from the houses. An elderly resident immediately challenged the demonstration: a bowl had no mud, reeds, or narrow stone passages. Elara thanked her and added those features to the next version. What made the class useful was not a perfect first model but the opportunity to correct it. The manufacturer observed the session and offered a clearer instruction sheet without changing the protected internal design. By the end, several people who had arrived suspicious of engineers asked to visit the real gauge. Rui organized supervised visits so their curiosity could become informed participation rather than dangerous wandering through the works.

為協助居民理解測試，芮請一名精通幾種地方語言的老師不用專業術語解釋機構。老師用木船和水碗示範操作員如何引導壓力離開住宅。年長居民立刻指出，水碗裡沒有泥、蘆葦和狹窄石道。艾拉感謝她，並把這些元素加入下版模型。課堂的價值不在第一個模型完美，而在人人能修正它。廠商旁聽後提供更易懂的說明單，仍保留受保護的內部設計。結束時，幾名本來懷疑工程師的人要求參觀真水尺。芮安排監督下的參訪，使好奇變成有知識的參與，而不是危險地在工地亂走。

On the way to the testing venue, the team passed a fortune teller who blamed the unusual tides on a seaside superstition. Hadi smiled but would not insult her. “People want a story when nobody offers an explanation,” he told Elara. They had spent weeks on the chore of collecting measurements, and their exploration had produced no magical answer. At the school, the assistant demonstrated a transparent test case that made the mechanism visible while leaving the patented parts undamaged. The result matched an independent glass gauge. The city could now compare two readings without opening the commercial recorder. Kestrel suggested that the manufacturer shed the secrecy around public measurements while keeping the machine design private. That proposal offered a practical path between complete exclusion and uncompensated copying.

前往測試場地的路上，大家經過一名占卜師，她把異常潮汐歸咎於海邊迷信。哈迪笑了，卻不願羞辱她。他對艾拉說：「沒人解釋時，人就想找個故事。」他們花了幾週辛苦收集量測，探索至今沒有魔法答案。到了學校，助理展示透明測試盒，讓機構運作可見，又不破壞專利零件。結果與獨立玻璃水尺吻合。城市如今能在不打開商用記錄器的情況下，比較兩份讀數。凱斯特建議製造商不再對公共量測保密，機械設計則仍可私有。這給完全排除公眾與無償複製之間一條實際道路。

The council accepted a trial arrangement: technicians would publish pressure traces each evening and explain them in ordinary language. The manufacturer retained its rights to the machine, while residents gained the right to inspect the numbers used to justify closures. Nessa praised the arrangement but asked who would enforce it during an emergency. Elara wrote that unanswered question at the top of her notebook. As the meeting ended, the assistant brought out a second set of traces from the previous month. Several lines had been cut off precisely when the pumps reached maximum pressure. It could be a recording fault, or it could be a decision to hide an inconvenient result. The team chose to request maintenance logs before making accusations. Their next inquiry would begin with the missing minutes.

議會接受試辦安排：技師每天傍晚公布壓力曲線，並以一般語言解說。製造商保留機械權利，居民則獲得查核用於決定封路數字的權利。妮莎稱讚安排，卻問緊急時誰負責執行。艾拉把尚未回答的問題寫在筆記本首頁。散會時，助理拿出上個月的第二組曲線，有幾條線恰在水泵達到最大壓力時中斷。可能只是記錄器故障，也可能有人故意藏起不方便的結果。團隊決定先調閱維護日誌再指控。他們的新調查將從消失的幾分鐘開始。
