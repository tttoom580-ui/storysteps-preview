低潮聽證

The Hearing at Low Water

潮汐之城：第十三道閘門 · 第 5 / 25 章 · 4000–5000 · 英中對照 · 校訂中

用互動版查詞、朗讀與收藏

A crowd filled the public hall before the low-water hearing began. Attendance was higher than expected: residents from the flats, workers from the shipyards, and people who had never attended a council meeting stood along the walls. The contemporary design of the thirteenth gate was controversial, but Elara refused to contend that every member of the construction corporation had behaved dishonestly. She produced statistical charts comparing tide readings, clinic travel times, and the pressure test beneath the bridge. Her overall conclusion was limited: the current plan left important questions unanswered. A councillor asked whether she meant to defy the council. “I mean to give you a chance to ask the right questions before the concrete dries,” she replied. Nessa wrote the exchange down without turning it into a slogan.

低潮聽證還沒開始，公廳便擠滿了人。出席人數遠超預期：低地居民、船廠工人，以及從未參加議會的人都站在牆邊。第十三道閘門的當代設計確實有爭議，但艾拉不肯聲稱營建公司的每個人都不誠實。她提出統計圖，比較潮位讀數、診所行車時間和橋下壓力測試。她的整體結論很有限：現有計畫留下重大疑問。一名議員問她是不是要反抗議會。「我只是讓你們有機會在水泥凝固前問對問題。」她回答。妮莎記下對話，沒有把它變成口號。

Hadi arrived wearing his best outfit, though tar still marked one sleeve. His offering to the hearing was not money but a piece of timber cut from the causeway, showing how quickly it had weakened. A wealthy resident dismissed it as a ritual performance. Hadi answered that carrying the timber had saved the committee the trouble of walking through knee-deep mud. The irony raised a few uneasy smiles. Rui brought a list of questions gathered from people who could not leave work. She asked that the committee segment the discussion into safety, transport, and housing, so one popular speaker would not consume the entire day. The chair endorsed the change. For the first time, people from different neighborhoods could hear their concerns addressed in a clear order.

哈迪穿著最體面的衣服趕來，袖子上仍有柏油痕。他送給聽證會的不是金錢，而是一段從堤道切下的木料，顯示它已如何迅速變弱。一名有錢居民嘲笑這只是儀式表演。哈迪說，搬來木料省得委員們踩進及膝泥水。諷刺讓幾個人不安地笑了。芮帶來無法離開工作的居民所寫的問題，要求把討論分成安全、運輸和住宅幾個部分，避免一名受歡迎的發言人占去整天。主席支持這項改動。不同街區的人第一次能清楚聽到自身關切被依序討論。

Rui organized a short training session before the hall closed. She showed residents how to read the tide chart, mark an uncertain number, and submit a correction without insulting the person who made the first drawing. The exercise was meant to facilitate future participation, not to turn every neighbor into an engineer. One dock worker found a label he could not understand, and Kestrel offered to rewrite it in plain language. A merchant found that the chart measured a road from the wrong starting point. Elara checked the claim and corrected the distance in the public copy. Neither visitor had discovered a conspiracy, but both had improved the record. Rui placed a small sign by the door: “You do not need a title to ask what a line means.” People stopped to read it on their way out.

公廳關門前，芮安排一場短訓練，教居民如何看潮位圖、標示不確定數字，以及不用羞辱原繪圖者就能提出更正。練習是為了促進日後參與，不是要把每個鄰居都變成工程師。一名碼頭工人看不懂標籤，凱斯特願意改成白話。一名商人發現圖上道路的量測起點不對，艾拉核對後修正公開副本。兩人都沒發現陰謀，卻改善了紀錄。芮在門旁貼一張小牌：「不必有頭銜，也能問一條線代表什麼。」人們離開時紛紛停下來閱讀。

Kestrel gave a different perspective. He admitted the maps were incomplete, but argued that stopping all construction during a dangerous season would create other risks. The essence of his argument was sound: without a repaired barrier, a high tide could reach the grain stores. Elara replied that a staged transition would allow urgent repairs to continue while independent teams checked the thirteenth gate. She did not ask the committee to treat a bruise, an unusual current, and a missing map as proof of one secret plot. Each was a separate observation. The committee voted to open the original design archive and to permit an external test of the pumps. A corporation representative called the result a defeat, but the vote settled neither ownership nor blame. It only made the next set of questions possible.

凱斯特提出另一個觀點。他承認地圖不完整，卻說在危險季節全面停工也會造成風險。他論點的核心確有道理：若不修堤，高水位可能漫進糧倉。艾拉回應，可用分階段轉換讓急迫修繕繼續，同時讓獨立團隊檢查第十三道閘門。她沒有要求委員會把一塊瘀青、一股異常水流和一張遺失地圖當成同一陰謀的證據；那是三種不同觀察。委員會表決開放原始設計檔案，並准許外部測試水泵。公司代表稱此為失敗，但投票既沒決定所有權，也沒認定誰有罪，只讓下一輪問題能被提出。

Outside the hall, the afternoon tide exposed a wide strip of dark mud. A resident named Omari pointed toward a long-buried stone and explained that his family had once held a small water ceremony there each year. He did not want a museum built around it or an apology printed on a banner. He wanted to know whether the stone could still be reached when the project ended. Elara promised not to make that decision for him. She would draw several options and ask families which costs they were willing to bear. The hearing had produced no simple triumph, yet it had changed who could enter the conversation. Rui slipped the archive permission into a waterproof envelope. On its back, someone had written a warning in red ink: “The thirteenth gate was never meant to close.”

公廳外，午後退潮露出大片黑泥。一名叫奧馬里的居民指向被埋的石塊，說家族過去每年都會在那裡舉行簡單的水祭。他不想要圍著石頭蓋博物館，也不要布條上的道歉；他只想知道工程完成後還能不能走到石邊。艾拉答應不替他決定，而會畫出幾個方案，問家人願意承擔哪些代價。聽證沒有簡單的勝利，卻改變了誰有資格參與討論。芮把檔案許可放進防水信封。背面有人用紅墨水寫了一句警告：「第十三道閘門從來不是用來關閉的。」
