堤道上的藥箱

Medicine on the Causeway

潮汐之城：第十三道閘門 · 第 4 / 25 章 · 4000–5000 · 英中對照 · 校訂中

用互動版查詞、朗讀與收藏

The freight causeway was the only dry road to the lower district's clinic. At dawn, a wagon carrying medication stopped behind a line of carts whose wheels had sunk into mud. The driver had followed a new civic guideline directing heavy vehicles away from the construction site, but no one had checked whether the alternate driveway could support their weight. A nurse asked Elara to help. Together they moved the lighter crates by hand while a young mechanic examined the wagon's windshield and cleared mud from the lamps. He wore an eloquent expression of anger but said nothing until the medicine was safe. “Your gate will not matter,” he finally told Elara, “if the city cannot get a doctor to a sick child.” The remark stayed with her longer than the minister's speeches.

貨運堤道是通往低地診所唯一乾燥的路。天亮時，一輛載著藥物的車卡在泥中，前方貨車排成長龍。司機遵守新的市政指引，繞開工地，卻沒有人查過替代車道是否能承受重量。護士請艾拉幫忙。眾人用手搬動較輕的箱子，一名年輕技工檢查擋風玻璃、刮掉車燈上的泥。他怒火滿面，直到藥物安全送抵才開口：「如果城市沒辦法把醫師送到生病孩子身邊，你的閘門再好也沒用。」這句話比部長的演說更長久留在艾拉心中。

At the clinic, a patient who needed regular treatment began to vomit from pain and exhaustion. The doctor explained that the delay would prolong his suffering, though it was unlikely to cause permanent harm. Elara saw a bruise on the nurse's wrist from lifting a heavy box, and the sight made her despair of plans that measured buildings but ignored bodies. In the waiting area, a child rolled a tin toy boat across the table, trying to pass the time without surrendering to boredom. Elara asked the doctor which bridge closure would cause the greatest difficulty. He pointed to a small road omitted from the fashionable city maps. It was barely noticeable from the council tower, but for residents of the flats it was the difference between an ordinary journey and an emergency.

診所裡，一名需要固定治療的病患因疼痛與疲憊而嘔吐。醫師解釋，延誤會延長痛苦，雖不太可能造成永久傷害。艾拉看到護士手腕因搬重箱而留下瘀青，對那些只量建築、不顧人體的計畫感到絕望。候診區一個孩子把錫製玩具船推過桌面，努力消磨無聊的時間。艾拉問醫師，封閉哪座橋最麻煩。醫師指向時髦城市地圖漏掉的小路。從議會塔望去，它幾乎看不見；對低地居民而言，卻是普通路程與緊急事故的分界。

The nurse at the clinic had a trait Elara admired: she never raised her voice merely to make herself heard. When the road was blocked again, she listed the people who needed a timely journey, the supplies that could wait, and the volunteers available to carry boxes. Her calm planning prevented another argument from becoming a delay. Elara asked permission to place those categories on the public transport map. The nurse agreed but removed patients' names, explaining that the city needed to know the type of need, not private diagnoses. Together they drew a route where carts, wheelchairs, and pedestrians could pass without pushing one another into the mud. A practical improvement to a small street revealed a principle that the larger engineering project had neglected: a route was safe only when the people who needed it could use it.

診所護士有一種艾拉很欣賞的特質：她從不只是為了被聽見就提高嗓音。道路再度堵塞時，她列出需要及時通行的人、可以等待的物資、能搬箱子的志工。冷靜規劃阻止爭吵造成新的延誤。艾拉徵得同意，把這些類別標到公開運輸地圖。護士同意，卻刪掉病患姓名，說城市需要知道需求類型，不需要私人診斷。她們畫出一條讓推車、輪椅和行人不必彼此推擠進泥裡的路。小街道的實際改善揭示大型工程忽略的原則：只有需要的人真能使用，道路才算安全。

The council's housing committee had proposed an innovative scheme: move the poorest families uphill until the gate works were finished. Critics called it excessive because the city lacked enough rooms and offered no guarantee of return. A councillor pleaded for patience, claiming the move would stimulate business by clearing the wharves. The market representative answered that morality could not be counted only in shipping fees. Elara presented a narrower proposal: repair the causeway in sections, preserve the clinic road, and postpone any permanent closure until the river model had been tested. The discussion intensified when a merchant accused her of trying to stop trade entirely. She said that keeping one emergency route open was not the same as opposing every new bridge.

議會住宅委員會提出創新方案：閘門工程完工前，先把最窮的家庭搬到山上。批評者認為措施過度，因為城市沒有足夠房間，也不保證居民能回家。一名議員懇求大家耐心等待，聲稱清空碼頭能刺激商業。市場代表反駁，道德不能只用貨運收入衡量。艾拉提出較小的方案：分段修復堤道、保留診所道路，並在河流模型受測前延後永久封閉。商人指控她想全面阻止貿易，爭論隨即加劇。她說，保留一條緊急通道不等於反對所有新橋。

At the end of the meeting, no dramatic order was issued. The council allowed a temporary passage for medical carts and asked two engineers to document its condition each morning. Elara volunteered for the first shift, not because she thought the arrangement perfect, but because a working path would buy time for a safer decision. On her way home she passed a group of children drawing a river in chalk. One drew a huge wall; another added a door so the fish could return. She wondered which drawing the adult city was closer to building. The next morning the road remained open, and the first medicine wagon arrived on schedule. It was a modest improvement, but it proved the people affected by an engineering choice could help improve it.

會議最後沒有戲劇性的命令。議會允許醫療車暫時通行，請兩名工程師每天早上記錄道路狀況。艾拉自願值第一班，不是認為安排完美，而是能用可通行的路爭取安全決策的時間。返家途中，她看見孩子用粉筆畫河。一人畫高牆，另一人在牆上畫門，讓魚能游回去。她想知道成年人正要蓋的是哪一幅。隔天道路仍開著，第一輛送藥車準時抵達。改善很小，卻證明受工程影響的人也能幫忙改善工程。
