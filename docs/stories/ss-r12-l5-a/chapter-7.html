運河同盟

The Canal League

潮汐之城：第十三道閘門 · 第 7 / 25 章 · 4000–5000 · 英中對照 · 校訂中

The Canal League occupied a sturdy building beside the old warehouses. Its members included ferrymen, cooks, repair workers, and small traders who rarely agreed about anything except the need for dependable infrastructure. The president laid out an array of repair receipts on a long table. A recent controversy concerned a corrupt official who had charged boat owners for inspections that never took place. Some members demanded his removal before any new gate work continued; others feared that an immediate shutdown would disconnect the markets from their suppliers. Elara listened to both sides. Expertise did not belong only to people with university titles, she reminded the room. But good intentions were no substitute for records, and anger alone could not prove which payments had been stolen. The league agreed to keep its own register open to independent inspection.

運河同盟設在舊倉庫旁一棟堅固的房子。成員有渡船員、廚師、修理工和小商販，平時難得意見相同，卻都需要可靠的公共設施。主席在長桌排開一整列維修收據。近期爭議來自一名腐敗官員，他向船主收取從未進行的檢查費。有人要求先撤職才續建閘門，也有人擔心立即停工會讓市場斷貨。艾拉聽完雙方。她提醒大家，專業知識不只屬於大學畢業的人；但好意不能取代紀錄，憤怒也不能證明哪些款項被偷。最後，同盟同意開放自己的登記簿接受獨立查核。

During lunch, the league's kitchen served river cuisine to visiting crews. A cook joked that a pirate would be more honest than a clerk who charged two fees for one passage. The joke aroused laughter, then embarrassment when a young messenger entered with an envelope held in the custody of the port office. The recipient was a retired engineer who had once inspected the inland basin. His testimony could help the committee derive a more accurate history of the channel. Yet the letter was private, and the league refused to break its seal. Rui found the engineer's public address and sent a formal invitation instead. The distinction mattered to Elara. A public question did not create a right to seize every document that might answer it, however indispensable the answer felt.

午餐時，同盟廚房為來訪船員端出河鮮料理。一名廚師笑說，海盜恐怕都比同一航程收兩次費的書記誠實。笑聲剛起，一名信差便帶著港務所保管的信封進來，大家忽然尷尬。收件者是曾勘察內陸水池的退休工程師，他的證詞或許能幫委員會推導出更正確的河道歷史。然而信是私人的，同盟拒絕拆封。芮找到工程師的公開地址，改寄正式邀請。艾拉認為這個區別很重要：公眾的問題不代表可以任意拿走任何可能回答問題的私人文件，即使答案再不可或缺也一樣。

The nurse's report described how dirty floodwater could infect a scratch on a worker's hand. The explanation was ordinary medicine, but it changed the league's cleaning plan. Rather than send volunteers into the narrow tunnel with bare arms, the crews agreed to drain the corridor, supply protective equipment, and let trained workers inspect it first. Hadi told the younger members that bravery did not mean ignoring instructions. An older cook offered extra food to compensate for the lost work hours, and traders contributed money for replacement gloves. The decision cost the league more than a simple closure, yet it helped everyone remain part of the process. Elara marked the additional expense beside the technical reading. She wanted future officials to know that safe operation required people, time, and supplies, not merely a sound gate.

護士報告指出，骯髒洪水可能使工人手上的小傷口感染。這是普通醫學知識，卻改變了同盟的清潔計畫。工班不再讓志工赤手走入狹窄隧道，而是先排水、供應防護裝備，再由受訓工人巡檢。哈迪告訴年輕成員，勇敢不是不聽指示。老廚師多做食物補貼工時損失，商販捐錢買新手套。這決定比簡單封路更花錢，卻讓人人都能參與。艾拉把額外費用寫在技術讀數旁，希望未來官員知道安全運作需要人力、時間與物資，不只需要一座堅固的閘門。

The afternoon brought a more practical threat. An infection had spread among workers cleaning a blocked corridor beneath the warehouses. The nurse requested clean water, gloves, and a temporary change of route. The league's leaders worried that such a closure would destroy the day's tournament of rowing crews, a popular event that supported local shops. Hadi argued that a race could wait until people were safe. Instead of canceling the entire festival, the crews moved the course to a different reach of the river and staffed a public water point. The compromise was imperfect but worthwhile. It gave people a reason to keep cooperating while the lower channel was inspected. Elara recorded the exact delay and cost, so the committee would not pretend that safety decisions had no consequences.

下午出現更直接的威脅。清理倉庫下堵塞走廊的工人傳出感染，護士要求乾淨水源、手套與暫時改道。同盟領袖擔心封路會破壞當天的划船錦標賽，讓依賴活動生意的店家受損。哈迪認為比賽可以等到人安全再辦。船隊沒有取消整場節慶，而是把賽道移到河的另一段，並設置公共飲水點。折衷方案不完美，卻值得做，讓人們能繼續合作等待巡檢。艾拉記下確切延誤與成本，不讓委員會假裝安全決定沒有代價。

At dusk, the retired engineer arrived in person. He carried the record of a test performed twenty years earlier, when the basin had safely absorbed a dangerous tide. The test could not guarantee today's result because buildings and shorelines had changed. Nevertheless, it showed how earlier engineers had treated the inland relief route as part of the city's defense, not as vacant land to be sold. The league asked whether it could claim ownership of the entire canal system. Elara said no: different communities depended on the same moving water, and no single league could speak for them all. They agreed to appoint delegates from the upper and lower districts for the next test. A city-wide channel required a city-wide conversation, not a new private monopoly dressed as public justice.

黃昏時，退休工程師親自來了。他帶著二十年前的一次試驗紀錄：當時平衡池確實吸收過危險潮水。但建築與岸線已改變，舊試驗不能保證今天也成功。儘管如此，紀錄顯示早年的工程師把內陸洩洪道視為城市防護的一部分，而非可出售的空地。同盟問自己能否主張整條水道的所有權。艾拉說不能：不同社群依賴同一股流動的水，任何單一同盟都不能替所有人發言。大家同意請上下城區各派代表參加下一次測試。全城共用的河道需要全城對話，而不是披著公義外衣的新私人壟斷。
