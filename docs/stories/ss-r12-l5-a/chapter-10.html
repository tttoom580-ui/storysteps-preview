圓頂下的工坊

The Workshop Beneath the Dome

潮汐之城：第十三道閘門 · 第 10 / 25 章 · 4000–5000 · 英中對照 · 校訂中

用互動版查詞、朗讀與收藏

The city's research dome stood above a small wetland preserved for teaching. Inside it, an entrepreneur named Soren had developed a bronze water wheel that could measure the pressure of a rising tide. He had mortgaged his workshop to pay for the design and reacted with arrogant certainty when Elara questioned its accuracy. The instrument was not ridiculous; it worked well under ordinary conditions. Yet a fault in one vertical bearing caused an error whenever water carried sand. A biologist studying wildlife nearby explained that even a tiny molecule of clay could cling to the wheel's teeth. Soren accused her of betraying the city's industry. Elara replied that finding a flaw before installation was a way to sustain a useful invention, not destroy it. She proposed testing the wheel with muddy water from the lower river.

城市的研究圓頂建在一片供教學使用的小濕地旁。創業家索倫在裡面研發青銅水輪，可量測潮水上漲時的壓力。他抵押工坊支付研發費，當艾拉質疑精度時，便傲慢而篤定地辯護。儀器並不荒謬，普通條件下確實運作良好；但一個垂直軸承有瑕疵，水裡帶沙時就會產生誤差。研究附近野生動物的生物學家解釋，即使極小的黏土分子也可能黏在齒輪上。索倫指責她背叛本地工業。艾拉說，安裝前找出缺陷是維持有用發明的方法，不是摧毀它。她建議用低地混濁河水測試。

The dome hosted a public discussion that afternoon. An anonymous pamphlet had alleged that the research team intended to exploit poor families for dangerous experiments. The charge harmed the scientists' credibility, although it contained no verifiable facts. One doctor stood up to explain a separate controversy about abortion services and the right of patients to privacy. She said that medical records, like engineering data, required careful rules: people needed public safeguards without exposing private lives. A butcher from the market challenged her to give a simple answer to every question. She refused false simplicity and offered to testify about the clinic's actual procedures instead. Elara admired the exchange. Difficult subjects did not become clearer when speakers shouted louder. They became clearer when each claim was tied to the evidence needed to test it.

下午圓頂舉辦公開討論。匿名傳單指控研究團隊想利用貧困家庭進行危險試驗，卻沒有可驗證事實，傷害了科學家的公信力。一名醫師起身說明另一場關於終止妊娠服務與病患隱私的爭議。她指出，醫療紀錄和工程數據一樣需要謹慎規則：人們需要公共保障，但私人生活不能被任意曝光。市場屠夫要她對每個問題都給出簡單答案。她拒絕虛假的簡化，提議改就診所實際程序正式作證。艾拉欣賞這段交流。困難議題不會因為喊得大聲就變清楚，而會在主張與可檢驗證據相連時變清楚。

After the demonstration, Soren walked Elara through his factory records. The old workshop had been designed for clear water, and the muddy river conditions had not appeared in its original specification. He could have concealed that weakness to protect a lucrative sale, but instead offered the city permission to test a revised model publicly. Elara asked why he had changed his mind. Soren pointed toward the wetland outside the dome, where apprentices were counting birds. “A machine that works only when the world behaves as I wish is not an excellent machine,” he said. The answer was not a confession of criminal wrongdoing; it was a choice to improve the device. Rui attached the new specification to the public directory, with the date and the conditions of the test written clearly beside it.

展示後，索倫帶艾拉查看工廠紀錄。舊工坊以清水為設計條件，原規格沒考慮混濁河水。他本可以藏起弱點保住大筆訂單，如今卻同意城市公開測試改良型。艾拉問他為何改變主意。索倫指向圓頂外濕地，學徒們正在數鳥。「只有世界照我希望運作時才有用的機器，算不上好機器。」他說。這不是承認刑事不法，而是選擇改進產品。芮把新規格放入公共目錄，清楚記下日期與測試條件。

Soren eventually agreed to join the city's testing initiative. The team would integrate his bronze wheel with the old glass gauge and compare both against a calibrated weight. A young researcher who specialized in genetics volunteered to help with the wetland's plant surveys; she knew little about gate mechanics but could describe how repeated flooding affected the roots. The work crossed several sectors of knowledge, and the meeting became less hostile once everyone admitted the limits of their specialty. During the test, the bronze wheel slowed when sand entered its teeth. Its trace became smooth again after cleaning. Soren stared at the chart for a long time, then nodded. He would redesign the bearing at his own cost if the city agreed to publish the conditions under which the revised instrument could be trusted.

索倫最後同意加入城市的測試計畫。團隊將青銅水輪與舊玻璃水尺整合，並以校準砝碼比較兩者。一名專攻遺傳學的年輕研究者自願協助調查濕地植物；她不懂太多閘門機械，卻能解釋反覆淹水如何影響根部。這項工作跨越幾個知識領域，人人承認自身專長的限制後，會議氣氛也不再那麼敵對。測試時，砂進入齒輪便使水輪變慢，清潔後曲線又恢復平順。索倫看著圖很久，終於點頭。他願自費重設軸承，條件是城市公布新版儀器在何種環境下值得信任。

Before closing the laboratory, Elara checked the tide against three separate instruments. The sand effect explained part of the suspicious sensor record but not the missing minutes or the reversed drawing of the inland gate. That distinction disappointed people seeking one perfect explanation. Yet it prevented the team from accusing innocent technicians and kept a capable inventor willing to cooperate. As dusk filled the dome, small birds settled into the reeds around the test pool. Elara watched them without naming their future on a council chart. The wetland would have to be included in the next design, not treated as empty land between machines. When Rui brought the original archive folio from its locked cabinet, the final page showed an unfamiliar symbol beside the thirteenth gate: an arrow pointing both inward and outward.

收工前，艾拉用三種獨立儀器核對潮位。砂粒效應解釋了可疑感測紀錄的一部分，卻無法解釋消失的幾分鐘，也無法解釋內陸閘門圖紙為何被反向繪製。想要一個完美答案的人感到失望，但這個區別避免無辜技師被指控，也讓有能力的發明者願意合作。黃昏灑入圓頂，小鳥停在試驗池周圍的蘆葦上。艾拉不想把牠們的未來簡化成議會圖表裡的空地。下一版設計必須納入濕地。芮從鎖櫃取來原始圖卷，最後一頁在第十三道閘門旁畫著陌生符號：同時朝內與朝外的雙向箭頭。
