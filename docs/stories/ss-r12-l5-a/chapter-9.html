鎖鏈墓地

The Graveyard of Chains

潮汐之城：第十三道閘門 · 第 9 / 25 章 · 4000–5000 · 英中對照 · 校訂中

用互動版查詞、朗讀與收藏

Beyond the north cemetery lay a forgotten mooring ground where boats had once waited for permission to enter the city. Its exterior was a field of rusted chains, broken posts, and grass. Elara brought an ally from the ferry workers' union, because the inspection required moving a heavy gate arm without damaging old graves. A government guard mistook their equipment for tools of assault and demanded a compulsory search. Rui showed their permit and the list of people present. The guard examined the manifest, then allowed the expedition to proceed. He warned them that an ambitious councillor had made a bid to purchase the shore as an exclusive lounge for visiting merchants. Hadi whispered that the place smelled too bad to attract anyone; a rotting net did indeed stink beneath the rail.

北方墓園外有片被遺忘的泊船場，昔日船隻要在那裡等許可才能進城。外頭只剩生鏽鎖鏈、斷木柱與野草。艾拉帶來渡船工會的盟友，因為巡檢需要移動沉重閘臂，還不能傷到舊墓。守衛誤把工具當成襲擊器具，要求強制搜查。芮出示許可與在場名冊，守衛查核清單後放行，並警告有議員競標買下海岸，打算改建成接待商人的專屬休息室。哈迪小聲說那地方臭得不可能吸引客人；欄杆下腐爛的漁網果真散發惡臭。

The old keeper asked visitors not to squat on the low memorial wall, which still carried the names of people who had worked on the first embankment. Several families had never received full citizenship under the old kingdom, yet their labor supported the entire city. Elara insisted that the names be copied into the survey record, with the families' approval. The keeper showed her a mint-green marker once used to label safe flood levels. Its color had faded, but its position matched the earliest chart in Rui's archive. Hadi examined the rear of a chain drum and found an opening that could release the inner basin door. The mechanism had been immobilized by a newer clamp. Elara chose not to remove it before a structural inspection, although the discovery made her eager to test it.

老看守請訪客不要蹲在低矮紀念牆上，牆上還刻著第一代築堤工人的名字。有些家庭在舊王國時代始終未取得完整公民身分，勞力卻支撐了整座城市。艾拉在取得家屬同意後，堅持把姓名抄入調查紀錄。看守指給她看一個薄荷綠標記，曾用來標示安全洪水位。顏色已褪，位置卻與芮找到的最早圖表相符。哈迪檢查鎖鏈鼓輪後方，找到能放開內陸水池門的開口，但機構被較新的夾具固定住。雖然迫不及待想測試，艾拉仍決定先做結構巡檢再拆除。

At the edge of the cemetery, the keeper invited the visitors to embrace a modest rule: every repair must leave the names on the memorial readable. A trader selling dried tuna agreed, though he worried that fencing the area would block his path to the market. Rui drew a temporary crossing that passed behind the memorial without taking up the only dock space. When the keeper reviewed it, he pointed out an old drainage groove that the sketch had missed. Elara added the groove to the survey and marked where it entered the basin. A place the city had treated as a forgotten burial ground was teaching the engineers about working water. The keeper asked for no praise. He wanted a path that honored the dead without making life harder for the people who still used the shore every day.

墓園邊，老看守邀請訪客接受一條樸實規則：每次修理後，紀念牆上的名字仍要看得清楚。一名賣乾鮪魚的商人同意，卻擔心圍欄會擋住通往市場的路。芮畫出繞過紀念牆的臨時通道，不占唯一泊位。看守檢視時指出草圖漏了舊排水溝。艾拉把溝加進測量圖，標出它流入水池的位置。城市當成被遺忘墓地的地方，正在教工程師理解仍在運作的水。看守不求稱讚，只希望一條既尊重逝者、又不讓每天使用海岸的人更難生活的道路。

At midday, a messenger tried to snatch Rui's field notebook from a table. Hadi caught the strap but let go when the frightened boy stumbled toward the water. No one was hurt. The boy said he had been hired to distract the crew; he did not know the employer's name. Nessa wanted a dramatic story about enemies of the investigation, but Elara asked the guard to record only what he had witnessed. The city's premier appeared that afternoon and declared the cemetery an unsuitable place for technical work. He spoke of grand projects stretching toward the galaxy, as though distant ambitions could erase a broken chain in front of him. Elara responded that the city could build new ports, but first it must understand the costs of closing its oldest relief path.

正午一名信差企圖從桌上搶走芮的野外筆記本。哈迪抓住背帶，但少年踉蹌朝水邊跌去時，他立刻放手，沒有人受傷。少年說有人雇他轉移調查隊注意力，卻不知道雇主姓名。妮莎想寫調查遭敵人阻撓的新聞，艾拉要求守衛只記錄親眼所見。行政首長午後現身，宣稱墓園不適合技術工作。他談起遠達星河的宏偉工程，好像遙遠的野心能抹掉眼前斷裂的鎖鏈。艾拉回答，城市當然可以蓋新港，但必須先理解封閉最古老洩洪路線的代價。

When the crew left, the keeper pressed a small photograph into Elara's hand. It showed the same chain drum thirty years earlier, its door open and water flowing away from the lower quarter. The keeper had preserved the image in remembrance of his sister, who had worked at the site. He asked that the picture be used only with the family's name attached. Elara agreed. The photograph alone could not prove the drum was safe today, but it answered a historical question the official map had avoided: the inner basin had once been part of the city's daily water management. On the return road, Hadi asked whether they should tell the council immediately. Elara said yes, and prepared two identical sealed copies, one for the council and one for the neighborhood assembly.

離開時，老看守把一張小照片交給艾拉。照片拍於三十年前，同一座鎖鏈鼓輪的門開著，水正從低地流走。他為紀念在此工作的姊姊而保留照片，要求使用時必須附上家族姓名。艾拉答應。這張照片不能證明鼓輪今天仍安全，卻回答了官方地圖一直避開的歷史問題：內陸水池以前確實是城市日常治水的一環。回程路上，哈迪問要不要立刻告訴議會。艾拉說要，並準備兩份相同的封存副本，一份給議會，一份給街區集會。
