第一次無水試運轉

The First Dry Run

潮汐之城：第十三道閘門 · 第 11 / 25 章 · 4000–5000 · 英中對照 · 校訂中

用互動版查詞、朗讀與收藏

The council finally authorized a minimal test of the inland gate, with no water released and no changes to its permanent bolts. Kestrel's team would deploy two mechanical jacks, while Elara's observers watched for unexpected movement. A veteran operator named Ivo showed them the old mechanism's main component: a wheel connected to the pressure doors by a chain buried in stone. The institution that trained him no longer existed, yet he could still describe the correct sequence from memory. His recommendation was to accelerate the wheel only after measuring the strain on each link. “If you compliment me too much,” he told Hadi, “you will make me forget what I came here to check.” Everyone laughed, then returned to the grim task of finding out whether the door could move without threatening the bridge.

議會終於准許內陸閘門做最低限度的無水測試，不能放水，也不能改動永久螺栓。凱斯特的隊伍將部署兩座機械千斤頂，艾拉的觀察員監看異常位移。老操作員伊沃受過已不存在的機構訓練，仍記得正確順序。他指出主要零件：一座輪盤經由埋在石裡的鏈條連到壓力門。他建議量完每節鏈條的拉力再加速轉輪。「別稱讚我太多，」他對哈迪說，「我會忘了自己是來檢查什麼的。」大家笑了，又回到沉重的工作：確認閘門移動時不會威脅橋梁。

By noon, fatigue had begun to affect the workers. Elara ordered a break rather than treating their tiredness as a trivial matter. A specialist in metal fatigue brought a specimen of the oldest chain and placed it beneath a magnifying lamp. Its surface quivered when the jack took the load, but the measured movement stayed inside the allowed range. Rui recorded the time and names of everyone present, including people who disagreed with the test. Kestrel remained neutral in his public statement, saying the result supported limited movement but did not establish safe use during a storm. Elara appreciated the restraint. It gave residents a result they could actually understand instead of another promise that everything was either completely safe or hopelessly broken.

到中午，疲勞開始影響工人。艾拉下令休息，不把疲倦當成小事。金屬疲勞專家拿來最老鏈條的樣本，放在放大燈下。千斤頂承重時，表面微微顫動，但量得的位移仍在容許範圍。芮記下時間與全體在場者姓名，包括反對測試的人。凱斯特在公開聲明裡保持中立：結果支持有限移動，卻不能證明暴風中使用安全。艾拉欣賞他的克制。居民終於得到能理解的結果，而不是又一次宣稱一切完全安全或徹底無望的保證。

Ivo told the younger workers that the realm of engineering contained far more uncertainty than the polished diagrams in textbooks suggested. His own specialty was mechanical gates, yet he always asked a river observer to check his figures before he made a final decision. He had seen a strong lock fail because the water beneath it behaved differently from the water above. During the lunch break he invited two apprentices to repeat the dry-run calculation and identify what would happen if one chain stretched more than the others. They reached different answers at first, then compared the assumptions hidden in their sketches. The exercise did not make them experts overnight. It taught them to name the question that a test answered and the larger questions it could not. Elara added the lesson to the training manual.

伊沃告訴年輕工人，工程領域充滿的不確定性遠比教科書裡光滑圖表多。他自己的專長是機械閘門，但做最後決定前總請河流觀察員核對數字。他見過堅固鎖具失效，只因鎖具上下水流反應不同。午休時，他請兩名學徒重算無水試驗，想像一條鏈比其他鏈拉得更長會如何。兩人起初答案不同，後來比較草圖裡隱藏的假設。這練習無法使他們一夜成為專家，卻教會他們說清一項測試回答了什麼、不能回答什麼。艾拉把這堂課加進訓練手冊。

A petition arrived from the grain merchants, who feared the gate test would delay their shipping season. Their stake in the outcome was real: grain spoiled quickly in the heat, and farmers depended on prompt payment. Ivo proposed moving the trial to the earliest morning hour, when the river was quiet. The change gave the merchants a usable passage after sunrise and the engineers a reliable period of observation. It also required careful coordination with the clinic and ferry workers. Elara reviewed the schedule with representatives of each group instead of announcing it from a closed office. The trial was beginning to thrive as a shared effort, though the old rivalries remained. She had learned that cooperation did not mean pretending everyone wanted the same thing.

糧商送來請願書，擔心閘門試驗耽擱運貨季。他們的利害關係確實存在：穀物遇熱容易變質，農民還等著付款。伊沃建議清晨河面平靜時測試。這樣日出後商人能通行，工程師也能取得可靠觀測時段，但仍需與診所和渡船工協調。艾拉逐一和代表核對時程，而不是在密室宣布。試驗逐漸發展成共同工作，老舊對立卻仍存在。她明白合作不代表假裝人人追求同一件事。

After the jack released its load, the wheel returned to the original mark without visible damage. The result was a small achievement, not a license to open the door. Engineers would still have to undergo a wet test in a separate pool before using the mechanism in the river. Ivo wiped his hands, gave Elara the recorded values, and asked her to keep the raw sheets even if a future mayor disliked them. She placed one copy in the council archive and another in the clinic safe, with both custodians signing for receipt. That night the bell rang for the shipping festival. Elara listened from her window and realized she had not slept well in days. Tomorrow she would rest for an hour, then ask why the city's newest design had left the old balancing door out of its drawings.

千斤頂卸重後，輪盤回到原記號，沒有可見損傷。這是小小成果，不是打開水門的許可。工程師仍須先在獨立水池做濕測，才能在河中使用。伊沃擦乾手，交給艾拉原始讀數，要她保留，即使未來市長不喜歡也一樣。她把一份存入議會檔案，另一份放進診所保險櫃，兩位保管人都簽收。當晚航運節敲鐘。艾拉在窗前聽著，才意識到自己好幾天沒睡好。明天她會先休息一小時，再追問為何城市最新設計圖遺漏了舊平衡門。
