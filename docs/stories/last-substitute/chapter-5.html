On one side were the players who had earned a starting place; on the other were the substitutes waiting to be called. The division felt sharp, but the game itself needed both groups. Ren touched the small notebook in a pocket and listened to the sounds of shoes against the floor. Mei did not ask Ren to pretend to be taller. She asked what the team was missing when the ball came fast and low. That question sounded different from every test Ren had tried before. Although the group was tired, they finished the final check. Some students laugh that the last substitute did not even need a number. At first, the discovery seemed to point in only one direction. Then Mei asked a quiet question that made Ren look twice. A useful observation should not depend on everybody liking the same answer. Ren wrote down what was certain and left a blank space for what they did not know. It felt a little strange to leave a page unfinished. Still, that blank space was honest. It meant they could learn something new tomorrow without having to pretend yesterday's guess had always been right. A drawing of a frog hung on the classroom notice board. Ren remembered a drawing of height seen earlier that week. A notice near the entrance mentioned hen. Ren said the word least in a careful answer. The long line moved slowly, and nobody was pushed.

Ren checked the original registration instead of arguing with the students. Some people wanted to act immediately. Ren understood their worry, yet the choice would affect more than one person. Mei helped turn scattered suggestions into a plan with a beginning and an end. They wrote what each person could do, and what no one should be pushed to do. That last part mattered. 'A fair answer cannot depend on someone being too afraid to refuse,' Ren said. When they finally moved, it was with a plan that could be checked instead of a guess that only sounded confident. 阿任不爭辯，而是核對原始報名資料。 有些人希望立刻行動。阿任理解焦急，但這個選擇會影響不只一人。小美把零散建議整理成有開頭和結尾的計畫。他們寫下每人能做什麼，也寫下哪些事不能強迫別人做。後面這一點尤其重要。「公平的答案不能靠別人不敢拒絕。」阿任說。最後他們按可以檢查的計畫行動，而不是跟著聽起來很有信心的猜測。 翻開的書有一則談到分鐘的小故事。陳老師核對事實後簽了字。阿任想起本週稍早看到的脖子圖畫。課堂閱讀卡用指向造了一個例句。「把紙放在筆記本旁邊。」阿任說。

The manager corrected the list, and Ren kept his own number on his shirt. When the players put the balls back into their basket, Ren stayed long enough to check the court markings once more. A point could disappear quickly, but a lesson would last if someone remembered it. Mei had seen the effort and knew which part still needed work. The team was not one person who leaped into the air; it was a line of people who had to move together. Ren wrote a short note about position, timing, and the moment to call for help. The words looked simple. Turning them into action would take many more days. A notice near the entrance mentioned race. The safe path was marked clearly for every visitor. After the busy part ended, Ren and Mei found a quiet minute. There were still things they wanted to say, yet neither felt a need to win the conversation. Ren asked what had been most difficult. Mei answered honestly and then asked the same question back. They were surprised to hear two different answers. That did not make either one wrong. The talk helped them see that working together did not mean thinking exactly alike. It meant taking those differences seriously enough to make a plan both could understand.

忙碌告一段落後，阿任和小美有了片刻安靜。他們還有話想說，卻不必在對話中爭贏。阿任問最困難的是哪部分。小美誠實回答，也反問同一個問題。兩人的答案竟然不同，卻不代表誰錯了。這場談話讓他們理解，合作不必想法完全一致，而是認真看待差異，做出雙方都能理解的計畫。

That evening, Ren tried to describe the day in just two sentences. The first sentence was about the problem. The second was about what people did together. Both mattered, and neither would have made sense alone. Ren left space beneath them for another note tomorrow. Mei sent a short reminder to rest. Ren smiled at it. A person could care deeply about a goal and still eat dinner, talk with family, and sleep. Whatever happened next, those ordinary things were part of the answer too.

那天晚上，阿任試著用兩句話描述今天。第一句是問題，第二句是大家一起做的事。兩句都重要，單獨看卻不完整。阿任在下面留空，準備明天再記。小美傳來簡短提醒，要記得休息。阿任笑了。人可以非常在意目標，同時仍要吃晚餐、陪家人說話、好好睡覺。不論接下來如何，這些平常的事也是答案的一部分。
