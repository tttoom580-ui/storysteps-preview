Before leaving, Ren gave the day one last careful look. It had contained a surprise, a question, a decision, and a result. Those parts did not always arrive in the order people wanted. Mei asked whether they were ready for tomorrow. 'Ready to listen,' Ren answered, 'even if tomorrow changes the plan.' The reply was small, but it was true. They put away the things they had used, thanked the people nearby, and made sure nobody was left expecting a promise that had never been agreed to.

離開前，阿任最後仔細回顧這一天：有驚喜、問題、決定，也有結果。事情不一定照人希望的順序發生。小美問明天準備好了嗎？「準備好傾聽了，」阿任回答，「即使明天又要改計畫。」這句話很簡單，卻是真的。他們收拾用品、謝謝附近的人，也確認沒有讓誰期待一個從未答應的承諾。

When Ren turned the last page of the notes, one line stood out: people deserve the chance to explain their own choices. It was easy to forget that in a busy day, especially when everyone wanted a quick ending. Mei had reminded Ren more than once to keep an open mind. The lesson was not that people should wait forever. It was that acting with care usually made the next action safer and fairer. Ren closed the book and made a quiet plan to remember that tomorrow.
