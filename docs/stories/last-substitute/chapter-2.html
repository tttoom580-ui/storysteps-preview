During morning drills Ren tried to jump above the net and landed badly. The bright lines of the volleyball court reminded Ren of a page divided into clear parts. On one side were the players who had earned a starting place; on the other were the substitutes waiting to be called. The division felt sharp, but the game itself needed both groups. Ren touched the small notebook in a pocket and listened to the sounds of shoes against the floor. Mei did not ask Ren to pretend to be taller. She asked what the team was missing when the ball came fast and low. That question sounded different from every test Ren had tried before. "We can add when the others are ready," Ren suggested. Mei also wanted to hear another point of view. "Please bring the notebook tomorrow," Mei said. The plan looked simple, but one part needed checking. Ren remembered a drawing of cousin seen earlier that week.

晨練時阿任拼命想跳過網頂，落地卻不穩。 球場上的明亮界線，讓阿任想到一張分成幾個部分的紙。這一邊是已取得先發資格的球員，另一邊是等待被叫上場的替補。兩邊看似分明，真正比賽卻都需要。阿任摸摸口袋裡的小筆記本，聽球鞋摩擦地面的聲音。小美沒有要求阿任假裝自己長得更高，只問球快速又低地飛過來時，全隊還欠缺什麼。這個問題和阿任以前碰到的考驗都不一樣。 「等其他人準備好，我們就能增加、加入。」阿任提議。小美也想聽聽其他人的看法。「明天請把筆記本帶來。」小美說。計畫看來簡單，但有一部分需要查證。阿任想起本週稍早看到的表親、表哥圖畫。

Coach Lin explained that a team needed quick feet and safe passing as much as tall arms. For several seconds, nobody spoke. Ren was thinking about the new fact and the way it touched earlier choices. Mei drew a short line under the important part of the notes. They agreed that a record was useful only if it was clear enough to correct. If a number, name, or time was uncertain, they marked it as uncertain. That extra care took a little longer, but it protected people who were not in the room to explain themselves. At last, Ren could see a practical question worth asking next. A small poster on the wall included the word family. "What does famous mean in this note?" Ren asked. The open book contained a short story about fill. The class reading card used guy in an example sentence. A helpful classmate offered to carry the notes.

林教練說球隊需要敏捷腳步和穩定接球，不只有高大的手臂。 好幾秒沒有人開口。阿任思考新事實與先前選擇的關係。小美在筆記重要處畫了短線。他們同意紀錄要清楚，才方便日後更正。數字、姓名或時間若不確定，就標明不確定。這樣雖然多花時間，卻能保護不在場、無法替自己說話的人。最後阿任終於想到下一個值得問的實際問題。 牆上的小海報寫著家人這個字。「這張筆記裡的「有名的」是什麼意思？」阿任問。翻開的書有一則談到裝滿、填入的小故事。課堂閱讀卡用男子造了一個例句。一位熱心同學主動幫忙搬筆記。

Ren asked to work on receiving served with Mei after practice. Making a choice was harder than making a wish. Ren could imagine a quick solution, but a real plan needed to fit the time, the place, and the people taking part. Mei listened while Ren explained the steps aloud. They stopped at one unclear part, changed it, and started again. 'If somebody says no, we need another way,' Ren said. Mei agreed that helping was not the same as deciding for others. Together they wrote a short plan anyone involved could question, improve, or leave if it was not right for them. Ren remembered a drawing of lip seen earlier that week. The class reading card used power in an example sentence. They talked about the river near the entrance and how to keep it safe. A small poster on the wall included the word science. A drawing of a sheep hung on the classroom notice board.

阿任主動提出放學後和小美練接發球。 做選擇比許願難。阿任可以想像快速解法，但真正的計畫必須配合時間、地點與參與者。小美聽阿任逐步說明，在不清楚的地方停下修改，再重新開始。「如果有人說不，我們要有其他辦法。」阿任說。小美同意，幫忙不等於替別人做決定。他們一起寫下簡明計畫，讓相關的人都能提問、改善或選擇退出。 阿任想起本週稍早看到的嘴唇圖畫。課堂閱讀卡用權力造了一個例句。他們討論入口附近的河流，以及如何保持安全。牆上的小海報寫著科學這個字。教室公告欄上掛著一張綿羊的圖畫。

The first five balls fly away, but his sixth pass reached the target. When the players put the balls back into their basket, Ren stayed long enough to check the court markings once more. A point could disappear quickly, but a lesson would last if someone remembered it. Mei had seen the effort and knew which part still needed work. The team was not one person who leaped into the air; it was a line of people who had to move together. Ren wrote a short note about position, timing, and the moment to call for help. The words looked simple. Turning them into action would take many more days. Ren remembered a drawing of south seen earlier that week. "What does sunny mean in this note?" Ren asked. "They may need more time," Mei answered. Someone had asked Mei a question about tool. A notice near the entrance mentioned video.

前五球都飛偏，第六球終於落到指定位置。 隊員把球收回籃子後，阿任還留下來重新看了一次場上標線。一分很快就會過去，但只要有人記得，學到的事就能保留。小美看見努力，也知道哪裡還需練習。球隊不是一個跳得很高的人，而是一群必須一起移動的人。阿任簡短記下站位、時間與需要喊支援的時機。文字很簡單，把它們變成動作還得花很多天。 阿任想起本週稍早看到的南方圖畫。「這張筆記裡的「晴朗的」是什麼意思？」阿任問。「他們可能需要更多時間。」小美回答。有人曾問小美關於工具的問題。入口附近的告示提到影片。

Later, Ren looked back at the four parts of the day: what happened, what they learned, what they decided, and what followed. The list was simple enough to fit on a little page, but it held much more than a score or a rumor. Mei read the page and pointed to a place where the order could be clearer. Together they fixed it. They also left a line for the next person to write a question. That made the record useful even when the people who started it were not there.

稍後，阿任回想今天的四部分：發生了什麼、學到了什麼、做了什麼決定，以及結果如何。清單雖然只有小小一頁，卻比一個分數或流言包含更多內容。小美讀完後指出一處順序不清楚，兩人一起修改。他們還留下一行，讓之後接手的人能寫下問題。這樣，即使最初參與的人不在場，紀錄也依然有用。

As the lights outside grew softer, Ren remembered the feeling at the start of the chapter's trouble. It had seemed larger before anyone could name it. Now it had a shape, a few facts, and a next step. That did not make it harmless, but it made it possible to face. Mei had helped without taking the whole choice away. Ren hoped to remember that balance. It was a kind of strength that did not make a loud sound, and it might be needed again very soon.

窗外的光漸漸柔和，阿任想起麻煩剛開始時的感受。那時在還沒說清楚前，事情看起來特別巨大。如今它有了形狀、幾項事實和下一步。問題不一定變得無害，卻能面對了。小美提供幫助，卻沒有奪走阿任的選擇權。阿任想記住這種平衡。這份力量不需要大聲，也許很快又會派上用場。
