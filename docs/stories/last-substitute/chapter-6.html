下雨的體育館

A Rainy Gym

Rain forces the team to share a smaller gym with the basketball club. Near the net, every player seemed to have a different way of waiting. Some stood on their toes. Others rolled their shoulders or spoke in quiet voices. Ren watched the ball move through the air and tried to read its path before anybody touched it. A tall person could reach higher; a shorter person might turn sooner. The coach had not promised that one talent would replace all the others. When Mei looked over, Ren raised a hand to show that the note had been made. Nothing was won yet, but the first useful observation belonged to Ren. "We can arrive when the others are ready," Ren suggested. Before leaving, Ren checked the open list again. Ren remembered a drawing of boat seen earlier that week. Someone had asked Mei a question about box. Somebody had packed coffee for a short break later.

雨天讓球隊必須和籃球社共用較小的體育館。 球網旁，每位球員等待的方式都不同。有人踮起腳尖，有人轉動肩膀，也有人低聲交談。阿任盯著球在空中的路線，試著在別人碰到球前看出落點。高個子伸得比較高，矮個子或許轉得更快。教練從未說一項長處能取代所有本領。小美望過來時，阿任舉手表示自己已記好。比賽還沒贏，但第一個有用的觀察已屬於阿任。 「等其他人準備好，我們就能抵達。」阿任提議。離開之前，阿任又核對公開清單。阿任想起本週稍早看到的船圖畫。有人曾問小美關於箱子的問題。有人為稍後的休息準備了咖啡。

A ball rolls across the wet entrance, and the coach stopped the drill immediately. The information was small enough to overlook. Ren nearly moved on, but Mei wanted to know what it meant for the people involved. They talked through two possibilities, then checked which parts could actually be supported. In the court, other things were happening at the same time; it would have been easy to mix them together. 'Let's keep these separate,' Ren said. One careful question led to another, and the situation became clearer without anyone needing to raise a voice or make a dramatic accusation. The open book contained a short story about drive. Everyone agreed except one friend who needed more time. Finally, the last note was put into its proper place. A free chair was left for the next person. They talked about the house near the entrance and how to keep it safe.

有球滾過濕滑入口，教練立刻停止練習。 這項資訊很小，幾乎會被忽略。阿任正想往下走，小美卻想知道它對相關的人代表什麼。他們討論兩種可能，再確認哪些有證據支持。球場同時還發生其他事，很容易混淆。「先把它們分開。」阿任說。仔細的一問接著一問，不必大聲說話或戲劇性指控，情況也漸漸清楚。 翻開的書有一則談到開車的小故事。除了還需要時間的一位朋友，大家都同意。最後，最後一張紙也放回正確位置。留下一張空椅子給下一個人。他們討論入口附近的房屋，以及如何保持安全。

Ren offered to make a clear equipment path before training began again. The next step sounded small when Ren said it aloud. That was a good sign. A clear action was easier to try than a large promise that no one understood. Mei repeated the plan to make sure both had heard it in the same way. When one part sounded unfair, they changed the wording. The plan left space for a person to ask a question or take a break. Ren noticed a quiet sense of relief. The problem remained real, but it no longer needed to be faced through shouting, rushing, or guessing about another person's feelings. A drawing of a lion hung on the classroom notice board. "Can you see the small mark on this page?" Ren asked. A small poster on the wall included the word shape. The open book contained a short story about sign. "Calling someone stupid will not solve our problem," Ren said.

阿任提議先規劃器材安全通道再重新練習。 阿任把下一步說出口時，聽起來只是一件小事。這反而是好現象。明確行動比大家聽不懂的大承諾更容易嘗試。小美重複計畫，確認兩人理解相同。有一部分聽來不公平，他們便改了說法。計畫也讓人能提問和休息。阿任感到些許輕鬆。問題依然存在，卻不必靠吵鬧、急躁或猜測別人的心思來面對。 教室公告欄上掛著一張獅子的圖畫。「你看得見這頁的小記號嗎？」阿任問。牆上的小海報寫著形狀這個字。翻開的書有一則談到標誌的小故事。「說別人笨也不會解決問題。」阿任說。

His careful signed keep the floor open and let both clubs finish practice. A whistle sounded somewhere beyond the court, and the last echo faded against the high roof. Ren could still feel the steps taken during the day's work. Not every step had led to a clean pass. Some had only shown where another player needed room. Mei talked about the next practice, but did not make a promise that either of them would win. Ren liked that answer. The real promise was smaller: they would watch the ball, call clearly, and give each other a fair chance to do the job they had trained for. "That would be a terrible way to treat a friend," Mei said. The open book contained a short story about top. Ren remembered a drawing of uncle seen earlier that week. The class reading card used wave in an example sentence. A small poster on the wall included the word weekend.

他細心製作的標示讓通道暢通，兩個社團都能完成練習。 球場外響起一聲哨音，回聲慢慢消失在高高的屋頂下。阿任仍感覺得到今天踏出的腳步，不是每一步都接成好球，有些只顯示另一位隊友需要空間。小美談起下一次練習，沒有保證一定贏球。阿任喜歡這種回答。真正的承諾比較小：看準球、清楚喊聲、讓每個人公平地做自己練過的工作。 「那樣對待朋友太糟糕了。」小美說。翻開的書有一則談到頂端的小故事。阿任想起本週稍早看到的舅舅圖畫。課堂閱讀卡用揮手造了一個例句。牆上的小海報寫著週末這個字。

In the afternoon light, the court looked ordinary again. That surprised Ren a little. A room, a street, or a simple object could stay the same while the people who used it learned something new. Mei checked that nothing needed to be carried away without permission. Ren checked the time and remembered an ordinary promise waiting outside the day's work. They had made progress, but other parts of life still deserved care. Neither of them wanted to turn an important project into a reason to forget everyone else.

午後光線下，球場又顯得很平凡，這讓阿任有些驚訝。房間、街道或物品可能沒有改變，使用它們的人卻已學到新事。小美確認沒有物品被擅自帶走。阿任看了時間，想起工作以外還有平常的約定。事情有了進展，生活其他部分仍值得照顧。他們都不想因為一項重要計畫，就忘記身邊其他人。

The small objects from the day were back in their places. The important words were written down, and the people involved had heard the result. Ren could not know every part of the future, but there was comfort in knowing what to do first: ask, check, and listen. Mei said goodbye with a tired smile. Ren returned the smile. Somewhere beyond the familiar court, another ordinary day was waiting. It would bring its own surprises, but they did not have to meet them without friends or without a plan.

今天的小物件都回到了原位。重要的話已寫好，相關的人也知道結果。阿任不可能知道未來每件事，卻因知道第一步該怎麼做而安心：發問、查證、傾聽。小美疲憊地笑著道別，阿任也回以微笑。在熟悉的球場之外，又有平凡的一天等待著，會帶來新的驚喜，但他們不必獨自、毫無計畫地面對。
