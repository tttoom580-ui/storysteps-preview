空著的替補席

The Empty Seat

最後一名替補 · 第 1 / 25 章 · 1000以下 · 英中對照 · 校訂中

Ren, the shortest player on the school volleyball team, arrived before everyone and saw his name at the end of the list. The gym smelled faintly of polished wood and clean rubber. Ren had heard people speak of talent as though it were a number that could be written beside a name. Looking at the court, that idea no longer felt complete. Somebody had to serve, somebody had to pass, and somebody had to cover the space left behind. Mei pointed toward the part of the floor that most players forgot when they watched the net. Ren studied it with care. The next chance might last only a few seconds, and those few seconds would belong to the person who was ready. The small clock read 9 a.m. when Ren checked it. A bell rang once in the hall, marking the next part of the day. Ren remembered the breakfast that had helped the morning begin. A friend promised to bring a small cake for the break. Ren thought of the soft couch at home and wished for a rest.

全隊最矮的阿任提早到球場，發現自己的名字排在名單最後。 體育館飄著淡淡的木地板和乾淨橡膠氣味。阿任以前聽人把天分說得像能寫在名字旁的一個數字。如今看著球場，這個說法顯得不完整。有人發球、有人接球，也必須有人守住留下的空檔。小美指向大家盯著球網時最容易忘記的那塊地。阿任認真觀察。下一次機會可能只有幾秒，而那幾秒屬於準備好的人。 阿任看鐘時，時刻是上午九點。大廳響起一聲鈴，提醒大家進入下一個時段。阿任想起幫自己開始一天的早餐。一位朋友答應休息時帶來小蛋糕。阿任想起家裡柔軟的沙發，真想休息一下。

A tall new player got the last starting place, while Ren was asked to carry the spare balls. The information was small enough to overlook. Ren nearly moved on, but Mei wanted to know what it meant for the people involved. They talked through two possibilities, then checked which parts could actually be supported. In the court, other things were happening at the same time; it would have been easy to mix them together. 'Let's keep these separate,' Ren said. One careful question led to another, and the situation became clearer without anyone needing to raise a voice or make a dramatic accusation. A clean cover lay folded beside the shared equipment. Ren arrived early and waited beside the entrance. A short exercise helped everyone warm up and focus. A foreign visitor asked for a simple map. Fresh fruit waited in a bag for the afternoon break.

高個子新球員獲得最後一個先發位置，阿任只能幫忙搬備用球。 這項資訊很小，幾乎會被忽略。阿任正想往下走，小美卻想知道它對相關的人代表什麼。他們討論兩種可能，再確認哪些有證據支持。球場同時還發生其他事，很容易混淆。「先把它們分開。」阿任說。仔細的一問接著一問，不必大聲說話或戲劇性指控，情況也漸漸清楚。 共用器材旁整齊摺著一塊乾淨的罩布。阿任提早抵達，在入口旁等候。短短的運動讓大家暖身，也比較能集中精神。一位外國訪客詢問簡單地圖。袋子裡放著下午休息時要吃的新鮮水果。

Captain Mei asked Ren to watch where the ball landed rather than measure his height. The next step sounded small when Ren said it aloud. That was a good sign. A clear action was easier to try than a large promise that no one understood. Mei repeated the plan to make sure both had heard it in the same way. When one part sounded unfair, they changed the wording. The plan left space for a person to ask a question or take a break. Ren noticed a quiet sense of relief. The problem remained real, but it no longer needed to be faced through shouting, rushing, or guessing about another person's feelings. "He has a part in this too," Ren told Mei. The heavy bag was moved slowly to avoid an accident. Ren thought about a hobby that had taught patience. A cartoon monkey smiled from a child’s paper sticker. A teammate had packed salad in a lunch box.

隊長小美請阿任觀察球落點，而不是只盯著身高。 阿任把下一步說出口時，聽起來只是一件小事。這反而是好現象。明確行動比大家聽不懂的大承諾更容易嘗試。小美重複計畫，確認兩人理解相同。有一部分聽來不公平，他們便改了說法。計畫也讓人能提問和休息。阿任感到些許輕鬆。問題依然存在，卻不必靠吵鬧、急躁或猜測別人的心思來面對。 「他也參與了這件事。」阿任告訴小美。沉重的袋子被緩緩搬開，避免意外。阿任想起一項曾教會自己耐心的興趣。孩子的紙貼紙上有一隻微笑的卡通猴子。一名隊友把沙拉裝進午餐盒。

Ren drew the first court map in a small notebook and kept coming to practice. The list of players remained in its place, but Ren no longer looked at the names in quite the same way. A starting place was important, yet it did not tell the whole story of a match. Mei reminded the group to drink water and take care of tired legs. Ren carried the notebook instead of hiding it, and another teammate asked to see the drawing inside. The picture showed empty places between players rather than a hero at the center. That was the beginning of a different kind of strength. "Shall we ask before we decide?" Ren suggested. The sun came through the high window and warmed the floor. "Could you teach me that step?" Ren asked Mei. A child’s drawing showed a white tooth and a smiling face. Ren used a pen to write the result in the shared notebook.

阿任在小本子畫下第一張球場圖，決定繼續練習。 球員名單仍留在原處，阿任看待名字的方式卻有點不同了。先發位置很重要，卻不能代表一整場比賽。小美提醒大家喝水、照顧疲累的雙腿。阿任不再藏起筆記本，一位隊友還主動要求看裡面的畫。畫上沒有站在中央的英雄，而是隊友之間的空位。那是另一種力量的開始。 「我們先問清楚再決定，好嗎？」阿任提議。陽光穿過高窗，溫暖了地板。「你能教我那個步驟嗎？」阿任問小美。孩子的畫上有一顆白牙和一張笑臉。阿任用筆把結果寫進共用筆記本。

In the afternoon light, the court looked ordinary again. That surprised Ren a little. A room, a street, or a simple object could stay the same while the people who used it learned something new. Mei checked that nothing needed to be carried away without permission. Ren checked the time and remembered an ordinary promise waiting outside the day's work. They had made progress, but other parts of life still deserved care. Neither of them wanted to turn an important project into a reason to forget everyone else.

午後光線下，球場又顯得很平凡，這讓阿任有些驚訝。房間、街道或物品可能沒有改變，使用它們的人卻已學到新事。小美確認沒有物品被擅自帶走。阿任看了時間，想起工作以外還有平常的約定。事情有了進展，生活其他部分仍值得照顧。他們都不想因為一項重要計畫，就忘記身邊其他人。

The small objects from the day were back in their places. The important words were written down, and the people involved had heard the result. Ren could not know every part of the future, but there was comfort in knowing what to do first: ask, check, and listen. Mei said goodbye with a tired smile. Ren returned the smile. Somewhere beyond the familiar court, another ordinary day was waiting. It would bring its own surprises, but they did not have to meet them without friends or without a plan.

今天的小物件都回到了原位。重要的話已寫好，相關的人也知道結果。阿任不可能知道未來每件事，卻因知道第一步該怎麼做而安心：發問、查證、傾聽。小美疲憊地笑著道別，阿任也回以微笑。在熟悉的球場之外，又有平凡的一天等待著，會帶來新的驚喜，但他們不必獨自、毫無計畫地面對。
