One Place Is Gone

全班只有我看不見那隻幽靈 · 第 10 / 25 章 · 1000以下 · 英中對照 · 校訂中

The first marked place was now a new garden, and the old tree was gone. At first, Ryo thought the problem could be solved before the next bell. A closer look showed that someone else's wishes mattered too. Mika listened without interrupting, while Mr. Sato watched the group from nearby. They did not have to agree on everything immediately. There was time to separate a real observation from a story someone had only imagined. Ryo took out the welcome book and asked what should be checked first.

第一個標記地點已變成新花園，老樹不見了。 起初，亮平以為下次鐘響前就能解決問題。但仔細看過後才發現，別人的願望也很重要。美佳沒有打斷，靜靜傾聽，佐藤老師則在附近看著大家。眾人不必立刻同意每件事，還有時間分清真實觀察與想像中的說法。亮平拿出歡迎簿，問大家應先確認什麼。

For a minute, the group spoke all at once. Ryo raised a hand and suggested taking turns. Mika repeated the question in simpler words, so that even a quiet student could follow the talk. Nobody needed to win an argument. They needed to learn which part of the problem could be handled now and which part needed more help. The mystery of the blue notes remained difficult, but it no longer felt impossible. A small study card showed apple, which Ryo copied beside today's notes. Mika asked how to use between in a sentence about their day. In the English practice book, blind appeared on the next line. Ryo read brother aloud, then checked the meaning with Mika. Another card carried center and a simple drawing by a classmate.

有一分鐘，大家幾乎同時開口。亮平舉起手，提議輪流說話。美佳用更簡單的詞重述問題，讓安靜的學生也能跟上。沒有人需要爭贏；重要的是分清哪些事現在能做、哪些事需要更多協助。藍色留言的謎團仍然困難，卻不再讓人覺得無法處理。 一張小小練習卡上寫著「apple」（蘋果），亮平把它抄在今日筆記旁。 美佳問起，如何用「between」（在……之間）寫一句與今天有關的話。 英文練習本的下一行出現「blind」（失明的；看不見的）。 亮平朗讀「brother」（弟弟），再和美佳確認意思。 另一張圖卡上有「center」（中央），旁邊是同學的簡筆畫。

Ryo suggested asking the groundskeeper what had changed. Once everyone understood the choice, the next step was smaller than it had seemed. Ryo spoke first and waited for an answer. Mika watched the other person's face instead of trying to hurry the talk. There was room to say no, to ask for an explanation, or to think for another minute. The group used the welcome book as a guide, not as an order that could never be changed. The word half gave the group a new example to discuss. Mika put hit on the board without turning it into a test. They found ice in a short reading exercise near the end of the page. Ryo drew a tiny sign beside kick so it would be easier to remember. Mika chose love as a word to explain kindly to a new learner.

亮平建議詢問園丁這裡曾有哪些變化。 當所有人都了解選擇後，下一步其實沒有想像中困難。亮平先開口，然後等待回答。美佳注意對方的表情，沒有催促。大家可以說不、要求解釋，或多想一分鐘。小組把歡迎簿當作指引，而不是絕不能改變的命令。 「half」（一半）這個字讓小組多了一個可以討論的例子。 美佳把「hit」（擊中）寫在板上，卻沒有把它變成考試。 他們在頁面末尾的短文練習裡找到「ice」（冰）。 亮平在「kick」（踢）旁畫上小記號，幫助記憶。 美佳選了「love」（喜愛），準備耐心向初學者說明。

The first idea sounded simple, and that was useful. Still, Ryo wanted to know whether it respected everyone's choice. Mika suggested changing only one part rather than throwing the whole plan away. They drew two small boxes on a page: what we can do and what we must ask about. The list made the mystery of the blue notes easier to discuss without turning a friend into an enemy. A small study card showed net, which Ryo copied beside today's notes. Mika asked how to use online in a sentence about their day. Mika drew a small picture of pencil in the margin of the class story page. Ryo read right aloud, then checked the meaning with Mika. Another card carried simple and a simple drawing by a classmate.

第一個想法聽起來很簡單，這是好事。但亮平還想確認是否尊重每個人的選擇。美佳建議只修改其中一部分，不必推翻整個計畫。他們在紙上畫兩個方框：我們能做的事，以及必須先詢問的事。有了這份清單，討論藍色留言的謎團便容易多了，也不會把朋友當成敵人。 一張小小練習卡上寫著「net」（細線網），亮平把它抄在今日筆記旁。 美佳問起，如何用「online」（線上的）寫一句與今天有關的話。 美佳在班級故事頁的空白處畫了「pencil」（鉛筆）的圖。 亮平朗讀「right」（正確的），再和美佳確認意思。 另一張圖卡上有「simple」（簡單的），旁邊是同學的簡筆畫。

They learned that the tree's wooden sign had been saved in storage. Before calling the task finished, Ryo went back over the facts. One detail had changed, and another still needed an answer. Mika wrote both down rather than hiding the uncertain part. They could be pleased with progress without pretending that every worry had disappeared. By comparing the new result with the first note, they could see what had actually improved. The trust between classmates still mattered, but now the group knew one more way to protect it. The word spend gave the group a new example to discuss. Mika put still on the board without turning it into a test. They found teacher in a short reading exercise near the end of the page. Ryo drew a tiny sign beside voice so it would be easier to remember. Mika chose way as a word to explain kindly to a new learner.

大家得知老樹的木牌被收在倉庫裡。 在宣布完成前，亮平重新檢查事實。有一個細節改變了，另一個仍有待解答。美佳把兩者都寫下，沒有隱藏不確定之處。大家可以為進展高興，卻不必假裝所有擔憂都消失。比對新結果與第一張筆記後，他們看出真正改善的部分。同學間的信任依然重要，現在小組又多學會一種守護它的方法。 「spend」（花費（時間））這個字讓小組多了一個可以討論的例子。 美佳把「still」（仍然）寫在板上，卻沒有把它變成考試。 他們在頁面末尾的短文練習裡找到「teacher」（老師）。 亮平在「voice」（聲音）旁畫上小記號，幫助記憶。 美佳選了「way」（方法），準備耐心向初學者說明。

The hallway grew quiet, and Ryo finally had time to think. A good friend had not supplied every answer. Instead, Mika had left space for another idea and had stayed when the first idea failed. That was harder to notice than a loud victory, but it was real. Ryo made a promise to remember the person as well as the task. The trust between classmates was important; so were the people who helped shape it. With that thought, the next chapter of their ordinary and extraordinary days felt possible.

走廊逐漸安靜，亮平終於有時間思考。好朋友不是替自己提供每個答案；美佳反而留下思考空間，也在第一個想法失敗時陪在身旁。這比熱鬧的勝利難以察覺，卻是真實的。亮平答應自己，不只記得任務，也要記得參與的人。同學間的信任重要，幫忙塑造它的人也同樣重要。帶著這個念頭，他們平凡又不平凡的日子可以繼續寫下去。
