A Letter from Yesterday

Ryo found an old letter tucked behind the repaired shelf. Nobody had planned for this moment. The room was full of ordinary sounds, yet Ryo felt that something important had changed. Mika waited beside the door and let the first surprise settle. It was tempting to find an easy answer before anyone knew the whole story. Instead, they looked carefully at what was truly in front of them. Ryo opened the welcome book and made a small note about the day.

亮平在修好的書架後找到一封舊信。 沒有人預料到這一刻。教室充滿平常的聲音，亮平卻感覺有件重要的事改變了。美佳站在門旁，讓最初的驚訝慢慢沉澱。大家很容易在弄清事情之前就急著下結論，但他們決定先看清眼前真正發生的事。亮平翻開歡迎簿，記下今天的小小發現。

They began by asking who might be affected by the next step. Mika thought about the people who had not spoken yet, and Ryo thought about the rule they had already promised to follow. One person offered a quick answer, but the group asked for a reason. A good question was not an attack. It was a way to make the answer safer and kinder. Mr. Sato reminded them that everyone could ask for a pause. Ryo drew a tiny sign beside baseball so it would be easier to remember. Mika chose beef as a word to explain kindly to a new learner. A small study card showed behind, which Ryo copied beside today's notes. Mika asked how to use break in a sentence about their day. In the English practice book, different appeared on the next line.

大家先問，下一步會影響到誰。美佳想到還沒發言的人，亮平則想到自己早已承諾遵守的規則。有人很快提出答案，其他人卻請他說明理由。好問題不是攻擊，而是讓答案更安全、更體貼的方法。佐藤老師提醒大家，每個人都可以要求暫停。 亮平在「baseball」（棒球）旁畫上小記號，幫助記憶。 美佳選了「beef」（牛肉），準備耐心向初學者說明。 一張小小練習卡上寫著「behind」（在後面），亮平把它抄在今日筆記旁。 美佳問起，如何用「break」（斷掉；破裂）寫一句與今天有關的話。 英文練習本的下一行出現「different」（死亡）。

He asked Mr. Sato whether it belonged in the school records. Ryo chose the part of the work that could be done carefully. Mika handled a different task and promised to report back before making any large change. A short question from Mr. Sato caught an error in their first version. Instead of hiding it, they made a neat correction on the page. That correction did not erase their effort. It showed that the group was learning how to be responsible for its own work. Ryo read dress aloud, then checked the meaning with Mika. Another card carried easy and a simple drawing by a classmate. The word fly gave the group a new example to discuss. Mika put ghost on the board without turning it into a test. They found girl in a short reading exercise near the end of the page.

他詢問佐藤老師，這封信是否應交給學校保管。 亮平選擇自己能謹慎完成的工作，美佳負責另一項任務，並答應在重大更改前先回報。佐藤老師的一個簡短問題，讓大家發現第一版中的錯誤。他們沒有掩飾，而是在頁面上整齊修正。這項修正沒有抹去先前的努力，反而證明小組正在學習對自己的工作負責。 亮平朗讀「dress」（裙子；洋裝），再和美佳確認意思。 另一張圖卡上有「easy」（容易的），旁邊是同學的簡筆畫。 「fly」（飛）這個字讓小組多了一個可以討論的例子。 美佳把「ghost」（幽靈）寫在板上，卻沒有把它變成考試。 他們在頁面末尾的短文練習裡找到「girl」（女孩）。

Ryo was afraid that saying the wrong thing would make the day worse. Mika answered that staying silent could also leave a misunderstanding in place. They practiced one honest sentence and checked that it did not blame anyone unfairly. This took longer than making a joke, but the result was clearer. Near the table, Mr. Sato nodded and let the young people try their own words before offering advice. Ryo drew a tiny sign beside give so it would be easier to remember. Mika chose group as a word to explain kindly to a new learner. A small study card showed heart, which Ryo copied beside today's notes. Mika asked how to use nice in a sentence about their day. In the English practice book, officer appeared on the next line.

亮平害怕說錯話會讓事情變糟。美佳回答，保持沉默有時也會讓誤會繼續存在。他們練習說出一句誠實的話，再確認沒有不公平地責怪任何人。這比開玩笑花時間，結果卻更清楚。桌旁的佐藤老師點頭，先讓年輕人用自己的話嘗試，再提供建議。 亮平在「give」（給予）旁畫上小記號，幫助記憶。 美佳選了「group」（團體），準備耐心向初學者說明。 一張小小練習卡上寫著「heart」（心），亮平把它抄在今日筆記旁。 美佳問起，如何用「nice」（好的）寫一句與今天有關的話。 英文練習本的下一行出現「officer」（管理員）。

The letter mentioned a missing classroom time capsule. Mr. Sato asked the group to explain its choice in everyday words. Ryo tried once, paused, and then gave a shorter answer. Mika added an important detail that had been left out. The explanation became clear enough for another person to check. That mattered more than making their decision sound grand. The trust between classmates could grow only if other people understood how it worked. Ryo read outside aloud, then checked the meaning with Mika. Another card carried park and a simple drawing by a classmate. The word taste gave the group a new example to discuss. Mika put walk on the board without turning it into a test. They found why in a short reading exercise near the end of the page.

信中提到一個不見的班級時光膠囊。 佐藤老師請小組用平常的話解釋選擇。亮平試了一次，停頓後改用更短的回答。美佳補充了一個漏掉的重要細節。解釋變得足夠清楚，讓別人也能核對。這比把決定說得偉大更重要。只有其他人理解做法，同學間的信任才有機會繼續成長。 亮平朗讀「outside」（外面），再和美佳確認意思。 另一張圖卡上有「park」（公園），旁邊是同學的簡筆畫。 「taste」（品嚐）這個字讓小組多了一個可以討論的例子。 美佳把「walk」（步行）寫在板上，卻沒有把它變成考試。 他們在頁面末尾的短文練習裡找到「why」（為什麼）。

Near the end of the day, Ryo stayed beside Mika for one more minute. They remembered how strange the morning had felt. A problem that once seemed to fill the whole room had become several smaller questions. Each question could be handled in turn. They did not know what the following day would bring, and that was all right. What they had learned was not a magic answer but a habit: notice a change, ask with care, and let people choose for themselves. Ryo closed the welcome book and left with a lighter heart.

一天快結束時，亮平在美佳身邊多待了一分鐘。他們想起早上的奇怪感受。原本像占滿整間房間的難題，現在分成幾個較小的問題，可以逐一處理。他們不知道明天會帶來什麼，但這沒有關係。今天學到的不是神奇答案，而是一種習慣：留意變化、仔細發問，也讓別人自己選擇。亮平闔上歡迎簿，帶著較輕鬆的心情離開。
