冰涼的角落

The Cold Corner

全班只有我看不見那隻幽靈 · 第 7 / 25 章 · 1000以下 · 英中對照 · 校訂中

Books kept falling from the classroom's cold back corner. The light through the window looked ordinary, but the news did not. Ryo stopped for a moment, feeling both curious and unsure. Mika did not laugh at the question, even when it sounded strange. They knew from earlier mistakes that moving too fast could make a simple problem harder. The classroom offered enough space for a calm talk. On the first page of the welcome book, Ryo wrote what was known and left a blank space for what remained uncertain.

教室後方冰涼的角落，書本不斷掉下來。 窗外的光線和平常一樣，消息卻一點也不平常。亮平停了一會兒，既好奇又不確定。就算問題聽來奇怪，美佳也沒有取笑。他們從先前的錯誤學到，行動太快反而可能讓小問題變複雜。教室有足夠空間讓大家平靜討論。亮平在歡迎簿第一頁寫下已知的事，也留下空白給尚未確定的部分。

They began by asking who might be affected by the next step. Mika thought about the people who had not spoken yet, and Ryo thought about the rule they had already promised to follow. One person offered a quick answer, but the group asked for a reason. A good question was not an attack. It was a way to make the answer safer and kinder. Mr. Sato reminded them that everyone could ask for a pause. They found as in a short reading exercise near the end of the page. Ryo drew a tiny sign beside bedroom so it would be easier to remember. Mika chose believe as a word to explain kindly to a new learner. Mika drew a small picture of clock in the margin of the class story page. Mika asked how to use close in a sentence about their day.

大家先問，下一步會影響到誰。美佳想到還沒發言的人，亮平則想到自己早已承諾遵守的規則。有人很快提出答案，其他人卻請他說明理由。好問題不是攻擊，而是讓答案更安全、更體貼的方法。佐藤老師提醒大家，每個人都可以要求暫停。 他們在頁面末尾的短文練習裡找到「as」（當……的時候）。 亮平在「bedroom」（臥室）旁畫上小記號，幫助記憶。 美佳選了「believe」（相信），準備耐心向初學者說明。 美佳在班級故事頁的空白處畫了「clock」（時鐘）的圖。 美佳問起，如何用「close」（關閉）寫一句與今天有關的話。

Mika checked the loose shelf instead of blaming Luma. Ryo chose the part of the work that could be done carefully. Mika handled a different task and promised to report back before making any large change. A short question from Mr. Sato caught an error in their first version. Instead of hiding it, they made a neat correction on the page. That correction did not erase their effort. It showed that the group was learning how to be responsible for its own work. In the English practice book, day appeared on the next line. Ryo read dream aloud, then checked the meaning with Mika. Another card carried error and a simple drawing by a classmate. The word hear gave the group a new example to discuss. Mika put just on the board without turning it into a test.

美佳檢查鬆動的書架，而不是怪罪露瑪。 亮平選擇自己能謹慎完成的工作，美佳負責另一項任務，並答應在重大更改前先回報。佐藤老師的一個簡短問題，讓大家發現第一版中的錯誤。他們沒有掩飾，而是在頁面上整齊修正。這項修正沒有抹去先前的努力，反而證明小組正在學習對自己的工作負責。 英文練習本的下一行出現「day」（一天）。 亮平朗讀「dream」（夢），再和美佳確認意思。 另一張圖卡上有「error」（錯誤），旁邊是同學的簡筆畫。 「hear」（聽見）這個字讓小組多了一個可以討論的例子。 美佳把「just」（只是、剛好）寫在板上，卻沒有把它變成考試。

Ryo was afraid that saying the wrong thing would make the day worse. Mika answered that staying silent could also leave a misunderstanding in place. They practiced one honest sentence and checked that it did not blame anyone unfairly. This took longer than making a joke, but the result was clearer. Near the table, Mr. Sato nodded and let the young people try their own words before offering advice. They found laugh in a short reading exercise near the end of the page. Ryo drew a tiny sign beside nature so it would be easier to remember. Mika chose noise as a word to explain kindly to a new learner. A small study card showed proud, which Ryo copied beside today's notes. Mika asked how to use rainbow in a sentence about their day.

亮平害怕說錯話會讓事情變糟。美佳回答，保持沉默有時也會讓誤會繼續存在。他們練習說出一句誠實的話，再確認沒有不公平地責怪任何人。這比開玩笑花時間，結果卻更清楚。桌旁的佐藤老師點頭，先讓年輕人用自己的話嘗試，再提供建議。 他們在頁面末尾的短文練習裡找到「laugh」（笑）。 亮平在「nature」（自然）旁畫上小記號，幫助記憶。 美佳選了「noise」（噪音），準備耐心向初學者說明。 一張小小練習卡上寫著「proud」（驕傲的），亮平把它抄在今日筆記旁。 美佳問起，如何用「rainbow」（彩虹）寫一句與今天有關的話。

The caretaker fixed the shelf, and Luma thanked them in blue ink. Mr. Sato asked the group to explain its choice in everyday words. Ryo tried once, paused, and then gave a shorter answer. Mika added an important detail that had been left out. The explanation became clear enough for another person to check. That mattered more than making their decision sound grand. The trust between classmates could grow only if other people understood how it worked. In the English practice book, rich appeared on the next line. Ryo read sky aloud, then checked the meaning with Mika. Another card carried time and a simple drawing by a classmate. The word whether gave the group a new example to discuss. Mika put yard on the board without turning it into a test.

工友修好書架後，露瑪用藍色字跡向大家道謝。 佐藤老師請小組用平常的話解釋選擇。亮平試了一次，停頓後改用更短的回答。美佳補充了一個漏掉的重要細節。解釋變得足夠清楚，讓別人也能核對。這比把決定說得偉大更重要。只有其他人理解做法，同學間的信任才有機會繼續成長。 英文練習本的下一行出現「rich」（富有的）。 亮平朗讀「sky」（天空），再和美佳確認意思。 另一張圖卡上有「time」（時間），旁邊是同學的簡筆畫。 「whether」（是否）這個字讓小組多了一個可以討論的例子。 美佳把「yard」（院子）寫在板上，卻沒有把它變成考試。

Outside, the air was cooler than it had been at lunch. Ryo looked back once and saw the classroom in the evening light. It seemed like an ordinary place, but there was more to it now: a remembered question, a mistake corrected, and a choice made together. Mika waved from a few steps away. They still had their separate dreams and small worries, yet they also shared one clear memory. Whatever happened next, they could return to that memory without needing to pretend the day had been perfect.

外面的空氣比午餐時涼了些。亮平回頭望了一眼晚光中的教室。它看似平凡，現在卻多了值得記住的問題、改正的錯誤，以及共同做出的選擇。美佳在幾步外揮手。他們仍有各自的夢想和小煩惱，也有一段共同的清晰回憶。無論接下來發生什麼，都不必假裝今天完美，就能想起它的價值。
