One photograph showed Luma as a smiling young art teacher many years ago. Nobody had planned for this moment. The room was full of ordinary sounds, yet Ryo felt that something important had changed. Mika waited beside the door and let the first surprise settle. It was tempting to find an easy answer before anyone knew the whole story. Instead, they looked carefully at what was truly in front of them. Ryo opened the welcome book and made a small note about the day.

一張老照片裡，露瑪是位笑容燦爛的年輕美術老師。 沒有人預料到這一刻。教室充滿平常的聲音，亮平卻感覺有件重要的事改變了。美佳站在門旁，讓最初的驚訝慢慢沉澱。大家很容易在弄清事情之前就急著下結論，但他們決定先看清眼前真正發生的事。亮平翻開歡迎簿，記下今天的小小發現。

Ryo was afraid that saying the wrong thing would make the day worse. Mika answered that staying silent could also leave a misunderstanding in place. They practiced one honest sentence and checked that it did not blame anyone unfairly. This took longer than making a joke, but the result was clearer. Near the table, Mr. Sato nodded and let the young people try their own words before offering advice. In the English practice book, answer appeared on the next line. Ryo read bicycle aloud, then checked the meaning with Mika. Another card carried body and a simple drawing by a classmate. The word bow gave the group a new example to discuss. Mika put jeans on the board without turning it into a test.

亮平害怕說錯話會讓事情變糟。美佳回答，保持沉默有時也會讓誤會繼續存在。他們練習說出一句誠實的話，再確認沒有不公平地責怪任何人。這比開玩笑花時間，結果卻更清楚。桌旁的佐藤老師點頭，先讓年輕人用自己的話嘗試，再提供建議。 英文練習本的下一行出現「answer」（答案；回答）。 亮平朗讀「bicycle」（自行車），再和美佳確認意思。 另一張圖卡上有「body」（身體），旁邊是同學的簡筆畫。 「bow」（鞠躬）這個字讓小組多了一個可以討論的例子。 美佳把「jeans」（牛仔褲）寫在板上，卻沒有把它變成考試。

Ryo asked whether they should tell the whole class about her past. There was a quiet kind of courage in trying again. Ryo noticed that the last attempt had taught them something, even without a perfect result. Mika offered practical help and asked which part would be most useful. They worked together for a short time and then stopped to check whether they were still following the rule. Mr. Sato praised the careful check more than the speed of the work. They found language in a short reading exercise near the end of the page. Ryo drew a tiny sign beside mine so it would be easier to remember. Mika chose of as a word to explain kindly to a new learner. A small study card showed own, which Ryo copied beside today's notes. Mika asked how to use pick in a sentence about their day.

亮平問起，是否該把露瑪的往事告訴全班。 願意再試一次，是一種安靜的勇氣。亮平發現，就算上次結果不完美，大家仍學到了一些事。美佳提供實際協助，先問哪一部分最有用。他們一起工作一小段時間，再停下來確認是否仍遵守規則。佐藤老師稱讚的不是速度，而是這份細心。 他們在頁面末尾的短文練習裡找到「language」（語言）。 亮平在「mine」（我的）旁畫上小記號，幫助記憶。 美佳選了「of」（的、屬於），準備耐心向初學者說明。 一張小小練習卡上寫著「own」（自己的），亮平把它抄在今日筆記旁。 美佳問起，如何用「pick」（挑選）寫一句與今天有關的話。

They began by asking who might be affected by the next step. Mika thought about the people who had not spoken yet, and Ryo thought about the rule they had already promised to follow. One person offered a quick answer, but the group asked for a reason. A good question was not an attack. It was a way to make the answer safer and kinder. Mr. Sato reminded them that everyone could ask for a pause. In the English practice book, planet appeared on the next line. Ryo read salt aloud, then checked the meaning with Mika. Another card carried say and a simple drawing by a classmate. The word second gave the group a new example to discuss. Mika put so on the board without turning it into a test.

大家先問，下一步會影響到誰。美佳想到還沒發言的人，亮平則想到自己早已承諾遵守的規則。有人很快提出答案，其他人卻請他說明理由。好問題不是攻擊，而是讓答案更安全、更體貼的方法。佐藤老師提醒大家，每個人都可以要求暫停。 英文練習本的下一行出現「planet」（星球）。 亮平朗讀「salt」（鹽），再和美佳確認意思。 另一張圖卡上有「say」（說），旁邊是同學的簡筆畫。 「second」（第二個）這個字讓小組多了一個可以討論的例子。 美佳把「so」（所以）寫在板上，卻沒有把它變成考試。

Luma wrote that her story was welcome but her students' private names were not. The answer made someone smile, but Ryo still asked one final question: was everyone comfortable with what came next? Mika listened to the replies, including the quiet ones. They changed a small detail when one person needed something different. Nothing about the change made the story less meaningful. It helped the trust between classmates belong to more than one person, which was why the work had begun. They found some in a short reading exercise near the end of the page. Ryo drew a tiny sign beside soon so it would be easier to remember. Mika chose spring as a word to explain kindly to a new learner. A small study card showed wind, which Ryo copied beside today's notes. Mika asked how to use wish in a sentence about their day.

露瑪寫道，自己的故事可以分享，但昔日學生的私人名字不該公開。 答案讓某個人笑了，但亮平仍問最後一個問題：大家都能接受下一步嗎？美佳聽取回答，包括輕聲說出的意見。有人有不同需求，他們就修改一個小細節。這種修改沒有讓故事失去意義，反而讓同學間的信任不只屬於一個人；這也正是大家當初開始的原因。 他們在頁面末尾的短文練習裡找到「some」（一些）。 亮平在「soon」（很快）旁畫上小記號，幫助記憶。 美佳選了「spring」（春天），準備耐心向初學者說明。 一張小小練習卡上寫著「wind」（風），亮平把它抄在今日筆記旁。 美佳問起，如何用「wish」（希望）寫一句與今天有關的話。

Near the end of the day, Ryo stayed beside Mika for one more minute. They remembered how strange the morning had felt. A problem that once seemed to fill the whole room had become several smaller questions. Each question could be handled in turn. They did not know what the following day would bring, and that was all right. What they had learned was not a magic answer but a habit: notice a change, ask with care, and let people choose for themselves. Ryo closed the welcome book and left with a lighter heart.

一天快結束時，亮平在美佳身邊多待了一分鐘。他們想起早上的奇怪感受。原本像占滿整間房間的難題，現在分成幾個較小的問題，可以逐一處理。他們不知道明天會帶來什麼，但這沒有關係。今天學到的不是神奇答案，而是一種習慣：留意變化、仔細發問，也讓別人自己選擇。亮平闔上歡迎簿，帶著較輕鬆的心情離開。
