空著的椅子

The Empty Chair

全班只有我看不見那隻幽靈 · 第 1 / 25 章 · 1000以下 · 英中對照 · 校訂中

Every classmate waved to someone above the empty chair, but Ryo saw only sunlight. The day began with a small question and soon became more interesting than anyone expected. Ryo could hear other students talking, but did not hurry to join their guesses. Mika pointed out a detail that had been easy to miss. They stood together in the classroom, where the same familiar objects suddenly seemed to tell a different story. Ryo kept the welcome book close, not as a perfect answer, but as a place to begin.

每位同學都向空椅子上方揮手，亮平卻只看見陽光。 這一天從一個小疑問開始，後來卻比大家預想的更有趣。亮平聽到其他同學交談，沒有急著加入猜測。美佳指出了一個容易忽略的細節。他們站在教室裡，熟悉的東西忽然像在說另一段故事。亮平把歡迎簿帶在身邊，那不是完美答案，而是一個開始。

For a minute, the group spoke all at once. Ryo raised a hand and suggested taking turns. Mika repeated the question in simpler words, so that even a quiet student could follow the talk. Nobody needed to win an argument. They needed to learn which part of the problem could be handled now and which part needed more help. The mystery of the blue notes remained difficult, but it no longer felt impossible. Mika asked how to use a.m. in a sentence about their day. In the English practice book, bell appeared on the next line. Ryo read breakfast aloud, then checked the meaning with Mika. Mika drew a small picture of cake in the margin of the class story page. The word couch gave the group a new example to discuss.

有一分鐘，大家幾乎同時開口。亮平舉起手，提議輪流說話。美佳用更簡單的詞重述問題，讓安靜的學生也能跟上。沒有人需要爭贏；重要的是分清哪些事現在能做、哪些事需要更多協助。藍色留言的謎團仍然困難，卻不再讓人覺得無法處理。 美佳問起，如何用「a.m.」（上午（a.m.））寫一句與今天有關的話。 英文練習本的下一行出現「bell」（鐘聲；鈴鐺）。 亮平朗讀「breakfast」（早餐），再和美佳確認意思。 美佳在班級故事頁的空白處畫了「cake」（蛋糕）的圖。 「couch」（沙發）這個字讓小組多了一個可以討論的例子。

Mika asked Ryo to draw what he actually saw instead of pretending. Once everyone understood the choice, the next step was smaller than it had seemed. Ryo spoke first and waited for an answer. Mika watched the other person's face instead of trying to hurry the talk. There was room to say no, to ask for an explanation, or to think for another minute. The group used the welcome book as a guide, not as an order that could never be changed. Mika put cover on the board without turning it into a test. They found early in a short reading exercise near the end of the page. Ryo drew a tiny sign beside exercise so it would be easier to remember. Mika chose foreign as a word to explain kindly to a new learner. Mika drew a small picture of fruit in the margin of the class story page.

美佳請亮平畫出真正看見的景象，不必假裝。 當所有人都了解選擇後，下一步其實沒有想像中困難。亮平先開口，然後等待回答。美佳注意對方的表情，沒有催促。大家可以說不、要求解釋，或多想一分鐘。小組把歡迎簿當作指引，而不是絕不能改變的命令。 美佳把「cover」（外罩；覆蓋物）寫在板上，卻沒有把它變成考試。 他們在頁面末尾的短文練習裡找到「early」（提早）。 亮平在「exercise」（運動）旁畫上小記號，幫助記憶。 美佳選了「foreign」（外文的），準備耐心向初學者說明。 美佳在班級故事頁的空白處畫了「fruit」（水果）的圖。

The first idea sounded simple, and that was useful. Still, Ryo wanted to know whether it respected everyone's choice. Mika suggested changing only one part rather than throwing the whole plan away. They drew two small boxes on a page: what we can do and what we must ask about. The list made the mystery of the blue notes easier to discuss without turning a friend into an enemy. Mika asked how to use he in a sentence about their day. In the English practice book, heavy appeared on the next line. Ryo read hobby aloud, then checked the meaning with Mika. Another card carried monkey and a simple drawing by a classmate. The word salad gave the group a new example to discuss.

第一個想法聽起來很簡單，這是好事。但亮平還想確認是否尊重每個人的選擇。美佳建議只修改其中一部分，不必推翻整個計畫。他們在紙上畫兩個方框：我們能做的事，以及必須先詢問的事。有了這份清單，討論藍色留言的謎團便容易多了，也不會把朋友當成敵人。 美佳問起，如何用「he」（他；他的）寫一句與今天有關的話。 英文練習本的下一行出現「heavy」（沉重的）。 亮平朗讀「hobby」（興趣），再和美佳確認意思。 另一張圖卡上有「monkey」（猴子），旁邊是同學的簡筆畫。 「salad」（沙拉）這個字讓小組多了一個可以討論的例子。

His homework came back with a blue note saying, Hello, Ryo. Before calling the task finished, Ryo went back over the facts. One detail had changed, and another still needed an answer. Mika wrote both down rather than hiding the uncertain part. They could be pleased with progress without pretending that every worry had disappeared. By comparing the new result with the first note, they could see what had actually improved. The trust between classmates still mattered, but now the group knew one more way to protect it. Mika put shall on the board without turning it into a test. They found sun in a short reading exercise near the end of the page. Ryo drew a tiny sign beside teach so it would be easier to remember. Mika drew a small picture of tooth in the margin of the class story page. A small study card showed write, which Ryo copied beside today's notes.

他的作業上出現一行藍字：「你好，亮平。」 在宣布完成前，亮平重新檢查事實。有一個細節改變了，另一個仍有待解答。美佳把兩者都寫下，沒有隱藏不確定之處。大家可以為進展高興，卻不必假裝所有擔憂都消失。比對新結果與第一張筆記後，他們看出真正改善的部分。同學間的信任依然重要，現在小組又多學會一種守護它的方法。 美佳把「shall」（要不要／應否）寫在板上，卻沒有把它變成考試。 他們在頁面末尾的短文練習裡找到「sun」（太陽）。 亮平在「teach」（教）旁畫上小記號，幫助記憶。 美佳在班級故事頁的空白處畫了「tooth」（牙齒）的圖。 一張小小練習卡上寫著「write」（寫），亮平把它抄在今日筆記旁。

When the last bell sounded, Ryo saw that Mika was waiting by the familiar door. The two had not said everything perfectly. They had even disagreed about a detail, yet the disagreement had made their plan clearer. The moment did not need a large speech. A small smile and one honest thank-you were enough. At home, Ryo would remember the most surprising part of the day and write it on the next page. Tomorrow the mystery of the blue notes might look different again. This time they would know how to begin.

最後一聲鈴響時，亮平看見美佳在熟悉的門旁等待。兩人不是每句話都說得完美，甚至為某個細節意見不同，但那場分歧讓計畫更清楚。這一刻不需要長篇大論，一個微笑和真誠的道謝就夠了。回家後，亮平會把今天最驚訝的事寫在下一頁。明天藍色留言的謎團也許又有不同面貌，但這次他們知道如何開始。
