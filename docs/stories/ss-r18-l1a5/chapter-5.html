幽靈畫得真糟

The Ghost's Bad Drawing

Luma tried to draw a cat on Ryo's paper, but it looked like a potato. The day began with a small question and soon became more interesting than anyone expected. Ryo could hear other students talking, but did not hurry to join their guesses. Mika pointed out a detail that had been easy to miss. They stood together in the classroom, where the same familiar objects suddenly seemed to tell a different story. Ryo kept the welcome book close, not as a perfect answer, but as a place to begin.

露瑪想在亮平紙上畫貓，畫出來卻像馬鈴薯。 這一天從一個小疑問開始，後來卻比大家預想的更有趣。亮平聽到其他同學交談，沒有急著加入猜測。美佳指出了一個容易忽略的細節。他們站在教室裡，熟悉的東西忽然像在說另一段故事。亮平把歡迎簿帶在身邊，那不是完美答案，而是一個開始。

The first idea sounded simple, and that was useful. Still, Ryo wanted to know whether it respected everyone's choice. Mika suggested changing only one part rather than throwing the whole plan away. They drew two small boxes on a page: what we can do and what we must ask about. The list made the mystery of the blue notes easier to discuss without turning a friend into an enemy. The word although gave the group a new example to discuss. Mika put bean on the board without turning it into a test. They found deal in a short reading exercise near the end of the page. Ryo drew a tiny sign beside dig so it would be easier to remember. Mika chose during as a word to explain kindly to a new learner.

第一個想法聽起來很簡單，這是好事。但亮平還想確認是否尊重每個人的選擇。美佳建議只修改其中一部分，不必推翻整個計畫。他們在紙上畫兩個方框：我們能做的事，以及必須先詢問的事。有了這份清單，討論藍色留言的謎團便容易多了，也不會把朋友當成敵人。 「although」（雖然）這個字讓小組多了一個可以討論的例子。 美佳把「bean」（豆子）寫在板上，卻沒有把它變成考試。 他們在頁面末尾的短文練習裡找到「deal」（處理；作決定）。 亮平在「dig」（挖）旁畫上小記號，幫助記憶。 美佳選了「during」（在……期間），準備耐心向初學者說明。

Ryo offered a drawing lesson through short notes. The plan became real only when someone took the first small action. Ryo checked the time, Mika checked the shared materials, and Mr. Sato confirmed which steps needed adult approval. The group left a clear record so that a person arriving later could understand the decision. They also agreed on what to do if something went wrong. It was not exciting in the way stories often promised, but it let everyone move forward with confidence. A small study card showed frog, which Ryo copied beside today's notes. Mika asked how to use height in a sentence about their day. In the English practice book, hen appeared on the next line. Ryo read least aloud, then checked the meaning with Mika. Another card carried long and a simple drawing by a classmate.

亮平用小紙條教她畫畫。 有人做出第一個小小行動時，計畫才真正開始。亮平確認時間，美佳檢查共用材料，佐藤老師確認哪些步驟需要大人同意。小組留下清楚紀錄，讓稍後到的人也能理解決定。他們還約好若事情出錯該怎麼辦。這不像故事裡常見的刺激冒險，卻讓每個人都更有信心往前走。 一張小小練習卡上寫著「frog」（青蛙），亮平把它抄在今日筆記旁。 美佳問起，如何用「height」（高度）寫一句與今天有關的話。 英文練習本的下一行出現「hen」（母雞）。 亮平朗讀「least」（至少），再和美佳確認意思。 另一張圖卡上有「long」（長的），旁邊是同學的簡筆畫。

For a minute, the group spoke all at once. Ryo raised a hand and suggested taking turns. Mika repeated the question in simpler words, so that even a quiet student could follow the talk. Nobody needed to win an argument. They needed to learn which part of the problem could be handled now and which part needed more help. The mystery of the blue notes remained difficult, but it no longer felt impossible. The word minute gave the group a new example to discuss. Mika put Ms. on the board without turning it into a test. They found neck in a short reading exercise near the end of the page. Ryo drew a tiny sign beside point so it would be easier to remember. Mika chose put as a word to explain kindly to a new learner.

有一分鐘，大家幾乎同時開口。亮平舉起手，提議輪流說話。美佳用更簡單的詞重述問題，讓安靜的學生也能跟上。沒有人需要爭贏；重要的是分清哪些事現在能做、哪些事需要更多協助。藍色留言的謎團仍然困難，卻不再讓人覺得無法處理。 「minute」（分鐘）這個字讓小組多了一個可以討論的例子。 美佳把「Ms.」（女士）寫在板上，卻沒有把它變成考試。 他們在頁面末尾的短文練習裡找到「neck」（脖子）。 亮平在「point」（指向；重點）旁畫上小記號，幫助記憶。 美佳選了「put」（放置），準備耐心向初學者說明。

They laughed together before Ryo had seen her face. They gathered the papers and put the shared things back in their places. Ryo could feel that the room had changed even though the desks had not moved. Mika pointed to the page where the group had written its first worry. The words were still there, but they no longer told the whole story. Now there was also an action, a result, and a reason to look forward to the next meeting. A small study card showed race, which Ryo copied beside today's notes. Mika asked how to use safe in a sentence about their day. In the English practice book, snow appeared on the next line. Ryo read sorry aloud, then checked the meaning with Mika. Another card carried win and a simple drawing by a classmate.

亮平還沒看見露瑪的臉，就已經和她一起笑了。 他們整理紙張，把共用物品放回原處。雖然桌子沒有移動，亮平卻覺得房間已經不同。美佳指出小組寫下第一個擔憂的那一頁。那些字還在，卻已無法概括整件事。現在多了行動、結果，以及期待下次見面的理由。 一張小小練習卡上寫著「race」（賽跑），亮平把它抄在今日筆記旁。 美佳問起，如何用「safe」（安全的）寫一句與今天有關的話。 英文練習本的下一行出現「snow」（雪）。 亮平朗讀「sorry」（抱歉），再和美佳確認意思。 另一張圖卡上有「win」（贏得），旁邊是同學的簡筆畫。

When the last bell sounded, Ryo saw that Mika was waiting by the familiar door. The two had not said everything perfectly. They had even disagreed about a detail, yet the disagreement had made their plan clearer. The moment did not need a large speech. A small smile and one honest thank-you were enough. At home, Ryo would remember the most surprising part of the day and write it on the next page. Tomorrow the mystery of the blue notes might look different again. This time they would know how to begin.

最後一聲鈴響時，亮平看見美佳在熟悉的門旁等待。兩人不是每句話都說得完美，甚至為某個細節意見不同，但那場分歧讓計畫更清楚。這一刻不需要長篇大論，一個微笑和真誠的道謝就夠了。回家後，亮平會把今天最驚訝的事寫在下一頁。明天藍色留言的謎團也許又有不同面貌，但這次他們知道如何開始。
