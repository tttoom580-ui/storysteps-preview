Mika found a welcome book where every student except Ryo had signed a greeting. The light through the window looked ordinary, but the news did not. Ryo stopped for a moment, feeling both curious and unsure. Mika did not laugh at the question, even when it sounded strange. They knew from earlier mistakes that moving too fast could make a simple problem harder. The classroom offered enough space for a calm talk. On the first page of the welcome book, Ryo wrote what was known and left a blank space for what remained uncertain.

美佳找到班級歡迎簿，除了亮平，每個人都寫過問候。 窗外的光線和平常一樣，消息卻一點也不平常。亮平停了一會兒，既好奇又不確定。就算問題聽來奇怪，美佳也沒有取笑。他們從先前的錯誤學到，行動太快反而可能讓小問題變複雜。教室有足夠空間讓大家平靜討論。亮平在歡迎簿第一頁寫下已知的事，也留下空白給尚未確定的部分。

Ryo was afraid that saying the wrong thing would make the day worse. Mika answered that staying silent could also leave a misunderstanding in place. They practiced one honest sentence and checked that it did not blame anyone unfairly. This took longer than making a joke, but the result was clearer. Near the table, Mr. Sato nodded and let the young people try their own words before offering advice. Ryo read about aloud, then checked the meaning with Mika. Another card carried beach and a simple drawing by a classmate. The word bird gave the group a new example to discuss. Mika put born on the board without turning it into a test. They found bridge in a short reading exercise near the end of the page.

亮平害怕說錯話會讓事情變糟。美佳回答，保持沉默有時也會讓誤會繼續存在。他們練習說出一句誠實的話，再確認沒有不公平地責怪任何人。這比開玩笑花時間，結果卻更清楚。桌旁的佐藤老師點頭，先讓年輕人用自己的話嘗試，再提供建議。 亮平朗讀「about」（關於），再和美佳確認意思。 另一張圖卡上有「beach」（海灘），旁邊是同學的簡筆畫。 「bird」（鳥）這個字讓小組多了一個可以討論的例子。 美佳把「born」（出生的）寫在板上，卻沒有把它變成考試。 他們在頁面末尾的短文練習裡找到「bridge」（橋）。

Ryo remembered being absent on the day the book went around. There was a quiet kind of courage in trying again. Ryo noticed that the last attempt had taught them something, even without a perfect result. Mika offered practical help and asked which part would be most useful. They worked together for a short time and then stopped to check whether they were still following the rule. Mr. Sato praised the careful check more than the speed of the work. Ryo drew a tiny sign beside clean so it would be easier to remember. Mika drew a small picture of cow in the margin of the class story page. A small study card showed every, which Ryo copied beside today's notes. Mika asked how to use far in a sentence about their day. In the English practice book, follow appeared on the next line.

亮平想起歡迎簿傳閱當天自己請假。 願意再試一次，是一種安靜的勇氣。亮平發現，就算上次結果不完美，大家仍學到了一些事。美佳提供實際協助，先問哪一部分最有用。他們一起工作一小段時間，再停下來確認是否仍遵守規則。佐藤老師稱讚的不是速度，而是這份細心。 亮平在「clean」（乾淨的）旁畫上小記號，幫助記憶。 美佳在班級故事頁的空白處畫了「cow」（牛）的圖。 一張小小練習卡上寫著「every」（每個），亮平把它抄在今日筆記旁。 美佳問起，如何用「far」（遠的）寫一句與今天有關的話。 英文練習本的下一行出現「follow」（跟隨）。

They began by asking who might be affected by the next step. Mika thought about the people who had not spoken yet, and Ryo thought about the rule they had already promised to follow. One person offered a quick answer, but the group asked for a reason. A good question was not an attack. It was a way to make the answer safer and kinder. Mr. Sato reminded them that everyone could ask for a pause. Ryo read happen aloud, then checked the meaning with Mika. Another card carried into and a simple drawing by a classmate. The word line gave the group a new example to discuss. Mika put pull on the board without turning it into a test. They found road in a short reading exercise near the end of the page.

大家先問，下一步會影響到誰。美佳想到還沒發言的人，亮平則想到自己早已承諾遵守的規則。有人很快提出答案，其他人卻請他說明理由。好問題不是攻擊，而是讓答案更安全、更體貼的方法。佐藤老師提醒大家，每個人都可以要求暫停。 亮平朗讀「happen」（發生），再和美佳確認意思。 另一張圖卡上有「into」（進入），旁邊是同學的簡筆畫。 「line」（線條、路線）這個字讓小組多了一個可以討論的例子。 美佳把「pull」（拉動）寫在板上，卻沒有把它變成考試。 他們在頁面末尾的短文練習裡找到「road」（道路）。

Luma wrote that she only showed herself after a student chose to say hello. The answer made someone smile, but Ryo still asked one final question: was everyone comfortable with what came next? Mika listened to the replies, including the quiet ones. They changed a small detail when one person needed something different. Nothing about the change made the story less meaningful. It helped the trust between classmates belong to more than one person, which was why the work had begun. Mika drew a small picture of shoe in the margin of the class story page. Mika chose side as a word to explain kindly to a new learner. A small study card showed station, which Ryo copied beside today's notes. Mika drew a small picture of table in the margin of the class story page. In the English practice book, water appeared on the next line.

露瑪寫道，只有學生自願說聲你好，她才會現身。 答案讓某個人笑了，但亮平仍問最後一個問題：大家都能接受下一步嗎？美佳聽取回答，包括輕聲說出的意見。有人有不同需求，他們就修改一個小細節。這種修改沒有讓故事失去意義，反而讓同學間的信任不只屬於一個人；這也正是大家當初開始的原因。 美佳在班級故事頁的空白處畫了「shoe」（鞋子）的圖。 美佳選了「side」（側邊），準備耐心向初學者說明。 一張小小練習卡上寫著「station」（車站），亮平把它抄在今日筆記旁。 美佳在班級故事頁的空白處畫了「table」（桌子）的圖。 英文練習本的下一行出現「water」（水）。

Outside, the air was cooler than it had been at lunch. Ryo looked back once and saw the classroom in the evening light. It seemed like an ordinary place, but there was more to it now: a remembered question, a mistake corrected, and a choice made together. Mika waved from a few steps away. They still had their separate dreams and small worries, yet they also shared one clear memory. Whatever happened next, they could return to that memory without needing to pretend the day had been perfect.

外面的空氣比午餐時涼了些。亮平回頭望了一眼晚光中的教室。它看似平凡，現在卻多了值得記住的問題、改正的錯誤，以及共同做出的選擇。美佳在幾步外揮手。他們仍有各自的夢想和小煩惱，也有一段共同的清晰回憶。無論接下來發生什麼，都不必假裝今天完美，就能想起它的價值。
