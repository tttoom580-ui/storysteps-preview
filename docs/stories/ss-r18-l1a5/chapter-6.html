A Rule on the Wall

全班只有我看不見那隻幽靈 · 第 6 / 25 章 · 1000以下 · 英中對照 · 校訂中

A new classmate asked if everyone had to greet the ghost to belong. At first, Ryo thought the problem could be solved before the next bell. A closer look showed that someone else's wishes mattered too. Mika listened without interrupting, while Mr. Sato watched the group from nearby. They did not have to agree on everything immediately. There was time to separate a real observation from a story someone had only imagined. Ryo took out the welcome book and asked what should be checked first.

轉來的新同學問，不向幽靈打招呼是否就不能融入班級。 起初，亮平以為下次鐘響前就能解決問題。但仔細看過後才發現，別人的願望也很重要。美佳沒有打斷，靜靜傾聽，佐藤老師則在附近看著大家。眾人不必立刻同意每件事，還有時間分清真實觀察與想像中的說法。亮平拿出歡迎簿，問大家應先確認什麼。

The first idea sounded simple, and that was useful. Still, Ryo wanted to know whether it respected everyone's choice. Mika suggested changing only one part rather than throwing the whole plan away. They drew two small boxes on a page: what we can do and what we must ask about. The list made the mystery of the blue notes easier to discuss without turning a friend into an enemy. Mika put arrive on the board without turning it into a test. They found before in a short reading exercise near the end of the page. Ryo drew a tiny sign beside boat so it would be easier to remember. Mika drew a small picture of box in the margin of the class story page. A small study card showed coffee, which Ryo copied beside today's notes.

第一個想法聽起來很簡單，這是好事。但亮平還想確認是否尊重每個人的選擇。美佳建議只修改其中一部分，不必推翻整個計畫。他們在紙上畫兩個方框：我們能做的事，以及必須先詢問的事。有了這份清單，討論藍色留言的謎團便容易多了，也不會把朋友當成敵人。 美佳把「arrive」（抵達）寫在板上，卻沒有把它變成考試。 他們在頁面末尾的短文練習裡找到「before」（在……之前）。 亮平在「boat」（船）旁畫上小記號，幫助記憶。 美佳在班級故事頁的空白處畫了「box」（箱子）的圖。 一張小小練習卡上寫著「coffee」（咖啡），亮平把它抄在今日筆記旁。

Ryo proposed a rule: seeing Luma must always be a choice. The plan became real only when someone took the first small action. Ryo checked the time, Mika checked the shared materials, and Mr. Sato confirmed which steps needed adult approval. The group left a clear record so that a person arriving later could understand the decision. They also agreed on what to do if something went wrong. It was not exciting in the way stories often promised, but it let everyone move forward with confidence. Mika asked how to use drive in a sentence about their day. In the English practice book, except appeared on the next line. Ryo read finally aloud, then checked the meaning with Mika. Another card carried free and a simple drawing by a classmate. The word house gave the group a new example to discuss.

亮平提議一條規則：看見露瑪必須永遠出於自願。 有人做出第一個小小行動時，計畫才真正開始。亮平確認時間，美佳檢查共用材料，佐藤老師確認哪些步驟需要大人同意。小組留下清楚紀錄，讓稍後到的人也能理解決定。他們還約好若事情出錯該怎麼辦。這不像故事裡常見的刺激冒險，卻讓每個人都更有信心往前走。 美佳問起，如何用「drive」（開車）寫一句與今天有關的話。 英文練習本的下一行出現「except」（除了）。 亮平朗讀「finally」（終於），再和美佳確認意思。 另一張圖卡上有「free」（免費的），旁邊是同學的簡筆畫。 「house」（房屋）這個字讓小組多了一個可以討論的例子。

For a minute, the group spoke all at once. Ryo raised a hand and suggested taking turns. Mika repeated the question in simpler words, so that even a quiet student could follow the talk. Nobody needed to win an argument. They needed to learn which part of the problem could be handled now and which part needed more help. The mystery of the blue notes remained difficult, but it no longer felt impossible. Mika put lion on the board without turning it into a test. They found see in a short reading exercise near the end of the page. Ryo drew a tiny sign beside shape so it would be easier to remember. Mika chose sign as a word to explain kindly to a new learner. A small study card showed stupid, which Ryo copied beside today's notes.

有一分鐘，大家幾乎同時開口。亮平舉起手，提議輪流說話。美佳用更簡單的詞重述問題，讓安靜的學生也能跟上。沒有人需要爭贏；重要的是分清哪些事現在能做、哪些事需要更多協助。藍色留言的謎團仍然困難，卻不再讓人覺得無法處理。 美佳把「lion」（獅子）寫在板上，卻沒有把它變成考試。 他們在頁面末尾的短文練習裡找到「see」（看見）。 亮平在「shape」（形狀）旁畫上小記號，幫助記憶。 美佳選了「sign」（標誌），準備耐心向初學者說明。 一張小小練習卡上寫著「stupid」（笨的），亮平把它抄在今日筆記旁。

The class approved, and Luma drew a tiny blue heart beside the rule. They gathered the papers and put the shared things back in their places. Ryo could feel that the room had changed even though the desks had not moved. Mika pointed to the page where the group had written its first worry. The words were still there, but they no longer told the whole story. Now there was also an action, a result, and a reason to look forward to the next meeting. Mika asked how to use terrible in a sentence about their day. In the English practice book, top appeared on the next line. Ryo read uncle aloud, then checked the meaning with Mika. Another card carried wave and a simple drawing by a classmate. The word weekend gave the group a new example to discuss.

全班同意了，露瑪在規則旁畫上小藍心。 他們整理紙張，把共用物品放回原處。雖然桌子沒有移動，亮平卻覺得房間已經不同。美佳指出小組寫下第一個擔憂的那一頁。那些字還在，卻已無法概括整件事。現在多了行動、結果，以及期待下次見面的理由。 美佳問起，如何用「terrible」（嚴重的；糟糕的）寫一句與今天有關的話。 英文練習本的下一行出現「top」（頂端）。 亮平朗讀「uncle」（舅舅），再和美佳確認意思。 另一張圖卡上有「wave」（揮手），旁邊是同學的簡筆畫。 「weekend」（週末）這個字讓小組多了一個可以討論的例子。

The hallway grew quiet, and Ryo finally had time to think. A good friend had not supplied every answer. Instead, Mika had left space for another idea and had stayed when the first idea failed. That was harder to notice than a loud victory, but it was real. Ryo made a promise to remember the person as well as the task. The trust between classmates was important; so were the people who helped shape it. With that thought, the next chapter of their ordinary and extraordinary days felt possible.

走廊逐漸安靜，亮平終於有時間思考。好朋友不是替自己提供每個答案；美佳反而留下思考空間，也在第一個想法失敗時陪在身旁。這比熱鬧的勝利難以察覺，卻是真實的。亮平答應自己，不只記得任務，也要記得參與的人。同學間的信任重要，幫忙塑造它的人也同樣重要。帶著這個念頭，他們平凡又不平凡的日子可以繼續寫下去。
