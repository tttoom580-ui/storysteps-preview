假新娘的規則

Rules for a False Bride

被龍族選中的假未婚妻 · 第 2 / 25 章 · 2000–3000 · 英中對照 · 校訂中

On the second morning, Cael took Mira to the royal training hall instead of presenting her to the court. A young dragon in human form was losing a game of bowling and complaining to an older beast keeper. Beside the lanes stood an old professor who taught geography at the city college. He had spent a decade studying mountain streams and could tell which caves carried water from the sound of the stone. Mira nearly asked him about Ashvale, but Cael placed a printed contract in her hands. “First we discuss what the engagement does,” he said, “and what it does not permit.”

第二天清晨，凱爾沒有帶米菈去見朝臣，而是領她走進王室訓練廳。一位化為人形的年輕龍族正輸掉保齡球比賽，向年長的馴獸員抱怨。球道旁站著一位在城市學院教授地理的老教授。他研究山間水流已有十年，光聽石壁聲音就能判斷哪個洞穴有水經過。米菈差點問起灰谷，凱爾卻先把一份印好的契約交到她手裡。「先談這場訂婚會帶來什麼，」他說，「以及它不允許做什麼。」

The document gave Mira a separate bedroom, a travel permit, and a personal budget for research. Neither person could demand private meetings, affection, or a public performance without consent. The prince's expressive face turned serious when he read the final page aloud. “You may end this agreement with one written request. I will not frighten you into staying.” Mira had expected a greedy ruler in a jeweled necktie. Instead she saw someone who understood the risk of being trapped by a title. A visiting minister, visibly drunk after an early celebration, laughed at the conditions. Cael asked a guard to escort him out rather than argue.

文件給米菈獨立房間、旅行許可與個人的研究預算。任何一方都不能在未經同意下要求私下見面、親密接觸或公開表演。凱爾念到最後一頁時，表情變得嚴肅。「妳只需要提交書面要求，就能終止協議。我不會恐嚇妳留下。」米菈原以為會遇到一個繫著寶石領帶、貪婪的統治者，眼前的人卻很懂得身分如何困住別人。一位提早喝醉的訪問大臣嘲笑這些條款，凱爾沒有爭辯，只請衛兵帶他出去。

They passed a notice board filled with reports from the palace academy. This semester, students were collecting memories from travelers and each passenger who had crossed the eastern border. A voter registration project was also underway in the nearby human districts, where each citizen could choose a local delegate. Mira knew that laws written on paper did not always protect poor families. Her father had once carried a survey kit through the valley and found broken pipes that officials called imaginary. “Who gets to speak when the council decides about water?” she asked. Cael answered, “Not enough people. That is the first thing I intend to change.”

兩人經過一塊貼滿王宮學院報告的布告板。這學期，學生正在蒐集穿越東部邊界的旅客與乘客記憶。附近的人類行政區也在辦理選民登記，每位公民都能選出地方代表。米菈知道紙上的法律不一定保護窮困家庭。她父親曾背著測量工具組走遍山谷，找到破裂水管，卻被官員說成憑空想像。「議會決定水務時，誰有權發言？」她問。凱爾回答：「有權發言的人還不夠多。這就是我想改變的第一件事。」

An apprentice offered Mira a ribbon edged with lace, part of the court's bridal clothing. She took it politely but asked to wear her own boots. Cael did not object. The professor arrived with a map and pointed to a visible line of pale stone east of the city. “The old river went there,” he explained. “Someone moved the border gate after the mine opened.” A guard said it was too dangerous to visit, but Mira felt regret for every hour spent in a warm room while her village carried water by hand. She asked for a permit to travel with a qualified escort, not for a favor that could be withdrawn without explanation.

一位學徒遞給米菈一條鑲蕾絲的緞帶，是王庭新娘服飾的一部分。她禮貌收下，卻要求繼續穿自己的靴子。凱爾沒有反對。教授帶著地圖過來，指向城市東方一條可見的淡色岩層。「舊河道原本經過那裡，」他解釋，「礦場開工後有人移動了邊界閘門。」衛兵說前往那裡太危險；可是村民正在用手提水，米菈每待在溫暖室內一小時就更懊悔。她要求有合格護衛同行的通行證，不要那種隨時可被收回的人情。

At noon, Cael showed her a small model of the capital. A tower represented the dragon council, another the human assembly. Mira noticed that the royal spring appeared on neither side; it stood between them like a secret that belonged to both. The professor explained that a century-old law required consent from both communities before changing the channels. “The law was forgotten after a war,” he added. “Not destroyed.” Mira looked up. A forgotten law might be safer than a stolen key, provided anyone could prove it still applied. Cael watched her face with an expression she could not read. For a moment, she wanted to ask what he knew about the copper ring.

中午，凱爾讓她看王都的小模型。一座塔代表龍族議會，另一座代表人類議會。米菈發現王室泉源不在任何一邊，而是站在兩者中間，像一個屬於雙方的秘密。教授說，百年前的法律規定，改動水道前必須徵得兩個社群同意。「戰後這條法律被人遺忘，」他補充，「但沒有被廢除。」米菈抬頭。若有人能證明舊法依然有效，它可能比偷來的鑰匙可靠。凱爾看著她的表情，眼神難以捉摸。有一刻，她很想問他究竟知道多少銅戒的事。

That evening, Mira found a message from Tessa hidden in the lining of her bag. The village wells had fallen another hand's width. A child had asked whether the dragon prince would save them; Tessa had answered only that Mira was working on a plan. The news struck harder than any palace insult. Mira wrote her first request in her own handwriting: access to the eastern water records. She was not a princess, and she could not solve a decade of neglect in one afternoon. But she was a mapmaker, and maps began with a point that could be checked. She placed the request beneath Cael's door, then finally let herself sleep.

當晚，米菈在旅行袋的襯裡找到泰莎的訊息。村裡的水井又下降了一掌深。有孩子問龍王子會不會救大家，泰莎只回答米菈正在想辦法。這消息比任何宮廷侮辱都更難受。米菈用自己的筆跡寫下第一份申請：查閱東部水道紀錄。她不是公主，不可能一個下午就解決十年的忽視；但她是製圖師，而每張地圖都從一個可查證的點開始。她把申請放到凱爾門下，終於讓自己睡去。

A dragon noble named Aurine announced that she might challenge Cael's claim to the throne. She demanded the same access to the water records that the prince possessed. Mira thought the demand reasonable, even though Aurine's allies hoped to use it against Cael.

龍族貴族奧琳宣布可能挑戰凱爾的王位繼承權，要求取得與王子同等的水務資料。米菈認為要求合理，即使奧琳的支持者想拿資料攻擊凱爾。
