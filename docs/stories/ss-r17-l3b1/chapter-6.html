The Wax Beneath the Seal

被龍族選中的假未婚妻 · 第 6 / 25 章 · 2000–3000 · 英中對照 · 校訂中

On the sixth morning, Mira spread the two engagement contracts across a sunlit table. At first she could not see why they differed. Chancellor Varr's new copy used the same paper, the same purple wax, and the same royal mark. Then she found a single sentence hidden in the middle: the eastern spring could be transferred to a private mining company after the wedding. “Anyhow, it is not signed,” Cael said. Mira shook her head. “Do not assume that makes it harmless. Someone expected us to miss it.” She had never studied royal politics, but she knew how maps were forged: a tiny change could move a whole river.

第六天早晨，米菈把兩份訂婚契約攤在陽光下。起初她看不出差別。瓦爾宰相的新版本用了相同的紙、紫色封蠟與王室印記。後來她發現藏在中間的一句話：婚禮之後，東泉可以轉交私人礦業公司。「不管怎樣，它還沒簽署，」凱爾說。米菈搖頭。「別假定這樣就沒有危險。有人就是希望我們漏看。」她沒學過宮廷政治，卻懂偽造地圖：微小的修改就能移動整條河。

The royal printer, an elderly woman named Iona, taught them a technique for reading impressions beneath a seal. When she warmed the wax carefully, a second mark appeared, made by a metal tool from the chancellor's office. Iona refused to guess who had used it. “An accurate summary is a necessity,” she said, “but a story about a suspect is not proof.” Mira wrote down the sequence of each copy and where it had been stored. She put aside a stale biscuit, then noticed that the royal crest had been pressed on after the private company's stamp. Whoever had created the forgery had wanted it to look official without asking the prince.

王室印刷師伊奧娜是一位年長婦人，她教兩人看封蠟下壓痕的技法。她小心加熱封蠟，第二個印記逐漸顯現，那是宰相辦公室使用的金屬工具留下的。伊奧娜拒絕猜測誰操作了它。「精確摘要很必要，」她說，「但談論嫌疑人不等於證據。」米菈記下每份文件的流轉與保管地點，她放下已不新鮮的餅乾，還發現王室紋章是在私人公司印記之後才壓上去。造假者想讓文件看起來正式，卻根本沒徵求王子同意。

They visited the palace greenhouse to examine the water tests. Tropical plants grew under lamps, although the courtyard outside was cold. The garden's creator, a teenage apprentice, explained that harmless bacteria appeared in the clean pool while a different substance had been found near the mine. Its amount seemed to multiply whenever the pumps ran at night. He felt a bump as his trolley rolled against a shelf, apologized, and handed Mira a chart. “The numbers hurt my stomach,” he admitted. “Our manager says all the ponds are safe.” Mira did not promise a cure. She asked for the original samples and an independent laboratory, so no single person could rewrite the result.

他們前往王宮溫室查看水質測試。院外很冷，熱帶植物卻在燈下茂盛生長。建造這座花園的年輕學徒說，乾淨水池裡有無害細菌，礦場附近卻測出另一種物質。每當夜間水泵運轉，濃度似乎就成倍增加。他推車撞到架子，連忙道歉，把圖表交給米菈。「這些數字讓我胃痛，」他坦白，「主管卻說所有池塘都安全。」米菈沒有保證能治好一切，只要求保存原始樣本並送獨立實驗室，免得任何一人能改寫結果。

In the inner courtyard, a politician from the northern districts praised Varr's excellence in public speeches. She offered a plan to send more water tanks to Ashvale. Mira appreciated the immediate help, but tanks were a temporary resource, not a lasting river. “We need both,” she replied. The politician seemed surprised that a supposed bride understood the difference. Cael said nothing until they were alone. “You made her listen,” he observed. Mira smiled without joy. “I did not make her do anything. I asked a question she could answer.” Their conversations were becoming easier, though the false name between them still felt like a stone in her pocket.

內院裡，一位北區政治人物稱讚瓦爾的公開演說十分出色。她提議派更多水車去灰谷。米菈感謝即時援助，卻提醒水箱是短期資源，不能取代長久的河流。「兩種都需要，」她回答。那位政治人物似乎意外，準王妃竟懂其中差別。獨處時，凱爾才說：「妳讓她聽進去了。」米菈笑得沒有喜色。「我沒有強迫她，只問了一個她能回答的問題。」兩人的談話越來越自然，可是他們之間的假名字仍像一顆藏在口袋裡的石頭。

That afternoon, a historian brought them a model missile from an old war museum. It had once been used in an exhibition about how the mountain tunnels were built and damaged. “The weapons are no longer here,” he said, “but some of the blocked passages were left behind.” Mira recognized one tunnel on her father's map. It ran beneath the private mine and might connect to the spring. Cael ordered no secret raid. He requested a legal inspection with civil engineers and human witnesses. Excellence in leadership, he said, was not the same as speed. Mira admired his restraint, even as she feared the village had little time to spare.

那天下午，歷史學者帶來戰爭博物館的一枚模型飛彈，原本用在介紹山地隧道如何興建、又如何受損的展覽。「武器已經不在這裡，」他說，「部分封堵的通道卻還留著。」米菈認出父親地圖上的一條隧道，正好從私人礦場下方穿過，可能通往泉源。凱爾沒有命令秘密突襲，而是要求由土木工程師和人類證人共同進行合法檢查。他說，卓越領導不等於一味求快。米菈欣賞他的克制，也害怕家鄉已沒有太多時間。

When night fell, Cael sent her a copy of the independent testing request. A note below it read, “The law protects a guest even if the court dislikes her.” Mira traced that sentence with one finger. She thought about telling him everything: her name, her village, the ring. Then she imagined Ashvale losing access to the spring the moment the lie was discovered. For now she chose to preserve the evidence and prepare a truthful statement for the right moment. She did not know that Cael had already written her real name in a sealed private letter, together with instructions that nobody was to punish her without a fair hearing.

入夜後，凱爾送來獨立檢測申請的副本，下面附著一句話：「即使王庭不喜歡一位客人，法律仍保護她。」米菈用指尖慢慢沿著那行字滑過。她想把一切告訴他：自己的名字、村子、銅戒；又想到謊言一旦拆穿，灰谷可能立刻失去接近泉源的機會。她暫時選擇先保存證據，準備在適當時刻說明真相。她不知道，凱爾早已在密封的私人信件寫下她的真名，並明令任何人不得在公平聽證之前處罰她。

At a private council dinner, Varr praised whichever candidate could deliver the most water before the vote. Aurine rejected the contest. She said a throne won by withholding water would not be worth keeping, and Mira noticed how carefully Cael listened.

私人議會晚宴上，瓦爾讚美能在表決前供應最多水的候選人。奧琳拒絕這種競爭，說靠扣住水源贏得的王位不值得保有。米菈注意到凱爾聽得很仔細。
