工人的申訴

The Workers' Appeal

被龍族選中的假未婚妻 · 第 7 / 25 章 · 2000–3000 · 英中對照 · 校訂中

A cold wind followed Mira and Cael into the lower district on the seventh day. They had come to hear an appeal from workers whose wages had been delayed. The hearing room was made of old brick, with a crooked cupboard and a small jar holding a dried lily on the table. A clerk asked each person to speak in turn. A young mechanic described an awful emergency at the water station: a pressure pipe had almost been allowed to explode, but the supervisor called the warning junk. Mira realized that the same supervisor had signed one of Varr's altered reports. She asked the clerk to preserve the original complaint.

第七天，寒風跟著米菈與凱爾走進下城區。他們是來聽一群工人申訴拖欠工資。聽證室由舊磚砌成，桌邊有歪斜的櫥櫃，桌上放著一只盛了乾燥百合的玻璃罐。書記員請大家輪流發言。年輕技工描述水務站一次可怕的緊急事件：壓力管差點爆裂，主管卻把警告當成垃圾。米菈發現，那位主管正是瓦爾修改報告的簽署人之一。她請書記員保存原始申訴。

During the break, a cook offered the workers soup with a warm flavor of herbs. She said the canteen had not received a dime for extra meals, though its staff had worked late for weeks. Mira felt awkward receiving a bowl while others waited, so she joined the queue and paid the listed price. A man jokingly called her the royal bride who could invent fair rules in a day. “I cannot,” she answered. “But I can remind the council that your wage is real.” His laugh made the room less tense. Cael stood in line behind her and bought his own soup, which caused more surprise than either expected.

休息時，廚師端出帶著香草溫暖風味的湯。她說，食堂員工連續加班好幾週，額外餐費卻一毛也沒收到。米菈不好意思在別人排隊時先收下湯，於是走進隊伍，照標價付款。有個男人開玩笑叫她一天就能發明公平規則的王室新娘。「我做不到，」她說，「但我能提醒議會，你們的工資是真實存在的。」他的笑讓室內放鬆不少。凱爾也排在她後面買湯，反而比預期引起更多驚訝。

After lunch, the mechanic showed them a vase made for his daughter. A narrow crack ran down its side. “Sometime last winter, the pressure rose too quickly,” he said. “We ran out before the glass broke. My hand touched the hot metal.” He rolled up his sleeve to show a healed mark on his flesh. Mira asked if he wanted medical help and whether his account could be used publicly. He agreed to both, on the condition that his child's name be removed. A steady record required respect for the people who made it possible. Cael signed the medical referral without asking the worker to thank him.

午餐後，技工拿出一只做給女兒的花瓶，瓶側有一道狹長裂痕。「去年冬天某個時候，壓力突然升太快，」他說，「我們在玻璃破裂前逃出來，我的手碰到燙金屬。」他捲起袖子，讓他們看皮膚上已癒合的痕跡。米菈問他是否需要醫療協助，也問證詞能否公開。他答應，條件是隱去女兒姓名。穩固的紀錄，必須尊重提供紀錄的人。凱爾簽了醫療轉介單，沒有要求工人感謝。

Varr's representative arrived with a pile of documents and an argument that the complaints were too old. The clerk corrected him: there was no deadline for reporting an ongoing safety risk. Mira offered the original pressure chart, which showed the warning had been filed again last month. The representative tried to call the chart inaccurate, but an independent engineer confirmed the readings. “Terrific,” the cook whispered, not because the danger was good but because the workers had finally been heard. Mira learned that brave people often sounded ordinary. They explained what happened, brought their evidence, and stayed in the room even when powerful people wished they would disappear.

瓦爾的代表拿著一疊文件趕來，辯稱工人的申訴太舊。書記員糾正他：持續存在的安全風險沒有這種通報期限。米菈提出原始壓力圖，證明上個月又有人再次警告。代表想說圖表不準，獨立工程師卻確認讀數沒有錯。「太好了，」廚師低聲說，不是危險值得高興，而是工人終於有人願意聽。米菈發現勇敢的人說話往往很平凡：解釋經過、帶來證據，即使權勢者希望他們消失，仍留在房間裡。

On the walk back, Cael asked Mira whether the public hearing had changed her opinion of him. She considered the question. “I used to think princes were born knowing how to use power. Today you listened more than you spoke.” He accepted her answer with a small smile. “I was taught to give orders. Listening has taken practice.” The setting sun caught the copper ring on Mira's hand. She wanted to ask why he kept noticing it, but a messenger interrupted them: the inspection team had reached the mine gate and been denied entry. Varr claimed that private property mattered more than a village's emergency. The next argument would have to be fought with law, not force.

回程時，凱爾問米菈，公開聽證是否改變她對他的看法。她想了想。「我以前以為王子天生就懂怎麼使用權力。今天你聽得比說得多。」他微微一笑。「我從小被教導下命令。學會傾聽，需要練習。」夕陽照著米菈手上的銅戒。她想問他為什麼總盯著戒指，一名信使卻打斷他們：檢查隊到達礦場門口，遭到拒絕入內。瓦爾聲稱私人財產權高於村子的緊急需求。下一場爭論必須用法律而非武力解決。

That evening the appeal clerk sent an official notice requiring the mining company to explain the refusal. The document was small and plain, without gold or royal decorations. Mira held it as carefully as she held her grandmother's ring. She knew a single hearing would not change every worker's life. Yet a record that could be checked by strangers was harder to bury than a private promise. Cael asked if she wanted to celebrate. She said she wanted hot tea and a quiet hour to finish her map. He brought the tea, left it by her door, and did not come in. She found herself smiling at that simple courtesy long after the cup was empty.

那夜，申訴書記員送來正式通知，要求礦業公司說明拒檢理由。文件小小一張，沒有黃金或王室裝飾，米菈卻像捧著祖母的銅戒一樣珍惜。她知道單次聽證無法改變每位工人的生活，但可供陌生人查核的紀錄，比私人承諾更難被掩埋。凱爾問她要不要慶祝。她說只想喝熱茶，安靜一小時畫完地圖。他把茶放在門外，沒有進房。直到茶杯空了很久，米菈仍因這份簡單的尊重而微笑。

A report claimed that Aurine had threatened the valley. Mira examined the full conversation and found that the printed quotation left out her promise of independent inspections. The correction did not make Aurine a friend, but it kept a false accusation from shaping the succession debate.

報告宣稱奧琳威脅山谷。米菈檢查完整對話，發現印出的引言漏掉她承諾接受獨立檢查的部分。更正並未讓奧琳成為朋友，卻阻止不實指控左右王位辯論。
