A Contract About Consent

被龍族選中的假未婚妻 · 第 8 / 25 章 · 2000–3000 · 英中對照 · 校訂中

The eighth morning brought a palace review of all guest permissions. A talkative officer tried to grab Mira's baggage and carry it to a different room, saying Varr had requested the move. Mira refused and asked for written permission. The officer looked offended, but Cael supported the established process: a guest could not be relocated by a private request. Wealthy families often treated staff and visitors as if they owed obedience in return for shelter. Mira knew that pressure well. “Lately,” she said, “everyone keeps telling me what will be best for me. I would rather be asked what I want.” Cael promised the room would remain hers.

第八天早晨，王宮開始重新檢查訪客的各項許可。一位健談的官員伸手要拿米菈的行李，說瓦爾要求她換房。米菈拒絕，要求書面許可。官員露出不悅，凱爾卻支持既定程序：私人請求不能強迫客人搬遷。有錢家族常把員工和訪客當成欠他們服從的人，只因提供了住處。米菈太懂這種壓力了。「最近，」她說，「人人都告訴我怎樣對我最好。我比較希望他們先問我想要什麼。」凱爾保證房間仍由她使用。

In the records office, Mira examined a web of connected complaints. One case involved a wealthy councilor using sexual harassment threats to silence an employee. Another concerned a citizen who had been denied access to a public meeting. Mira was careful not to use these stories as decoration for her own struggle. The complainants had given permission for limited review, not for gossip. She requested a process that would hide their names while allowing inspectors to compare dates and official responses. “A person's safety is not a price for a good headline,” she told the clerk. The clerk wrote the sentence in the new policy notes.

在紀錄室裡，米菈查看彼此相連的多起申訴。一件涉及富裕議員用性騷擾相關威脅壓制員工，另一件關於公民被阻止參加公開會議。米菈很小心，不拿別人的經歷裝飾自己的困境。當事人同意有限查閱，不是同意成為流言話題。她要求建立能隱去姓名、又容許檢查員比對日期與官方回應的流程。「一個人的安全，不該成為好新聞的代價，」她對書記員說。書記員把這句話寫進新政策筆記。

At noon, they walked through a public square where a mechanical buffalo carried supplies for the animal clinic. A child dropped a bag of litter near the fountain, and a guard began to yell. Mira asked the child to pick it up, then helped her find a bin. Nearby, a street cooker served crispy bread to a long queue. Above them, the palace controller operated a weather screen, which warned of strong winds over the eastern ridge. Cael pointed out that the same forecast had been missing from the mining company's safety report. They requested a copy of the weather archive rather than assuming the company had hidden it.

中午，他們經過公共廣場，一頭機械水牛正替動物診所搬運物資。有個孩子把垃圾袋落在噴泉旁，衛兵立刻大聲斥責。米菈請孩子自己撿起，並陪她找到垃圾桶。附近的街邊爐灶正烤出酥脆麵包，排起長隊。廣場上方，王宮控制員操作天候螢幕，顯示東部山脊即將吹起強風。凱爾指出，礦業公司的安全報告裡沒有相同預報。他們要求調取天候檔案，而不是先認定公司故意隱瞞。

Back at the palace, a historian showed them a small bullet lodged in the stone from a century-old battle. It had been left there as a warning against fighting over water. Mira studied the shadow it cast on the floor. “The object is tiny,” she said, “but the story around it can be used by anyone.” Cael agreed that fear made a convenient political tool. The council needed to move beyond stories about heroes and enemies toward facts about gates and pipes. He asked whether she wished to speak at the next public hearing. Mira said yes, provided her actual evidence was shared beforehand and she could refuse questions unrelated to the river.

回宮後，歷史學者讓他們看一顆嵌在石頭裡的小子彈，那是百年前的戰爭遺物，作為爭水之禍的警示留下。米菈注視子彈在地上投下的影子。「東西這麼小，」她說，「圍繞它的故事卻能被任何人利用。」凱爾同意，恐懼是方便的政治工具。議會需要從英雄與敵人的故事，轉向閘門和水管的事實。他問米菈願不願意在下一場公聽會發言。她說願意，條件是事前公開實際證據，並能拒答與河川無關的問題。

That evening, Mira found an unsigned note in her luggage: “You owe the palace your silence.” Her first reaction was anger; the second was caution. She photographed the note, preserved the envelope, and asked the guard captain to record how it had entered her room. She refused Varr's suggestion that the matter should stay private to protect the engagement's image. Cael offered to replace the guard team but asked whether she preferred that change. She did not. She wanted the present officers questioned as witnesses, not blamed without evidence. They spent an hour documenting the door seals and the servants' schedule, leaving the investigation open.

當晚，米菈在行李中發現沒有署名的紙條：「妳欠王宮的，就是保持沉默。」她先是憤怒，隨即冷靜下來。她拍下紙條、保存信封，請衛隊長記錄東西如何進房。瓦爾建議為維護訂婚形象而私下處理，她拒絕了。凱爾提議更換守衛，卻先問她願不願意。她不願意把現任守衛當成未經證明的犯人，而是要求把他們列為證人。兩人花一小時記錄門封與侍從班表，讓調查持續進行。

Before sleep, Mira considered how strange her own situation had become. She had entered as a false guest and was now defending the rights of real guests. It would be easy to claim that a good cause excused every lie. She refused that comfort. Her borrowed name still demanded an answer. She wrote a letter to the absent Celestine, telling her what had happened without asking for forgiveness in advance. Then she placed it in the outgoing mail. If she wanted the council to stop hiding the truth, she would eventually have to stand in the same light. The thought frightened her, but it also felt like the first honest promise of the engagement.

睡前，米菈想著自己的處境多麼奇異。她以假客人身分進宮，如今卻在保護真實客人的權利。說「目的良善就能原諒一切謊言」很容易，她卻不肯用那種藉口安慰自己。借來的名字終究要有交代。她寫信給不在場的賽蕾絲汀，說明經過，沒有預先要求原諒，再把信放入寄送箱。若她希望議會停止隱瞞事實，自己終究也得走進同樣的光裡。這念頭令她害怕，卻也像是假訂婚開始以來第一個真正誠實的承諾。

The council scheduled a succession hearing on the same day as the village water meeting. Cael asked to move one session rather than prevent residents from attending both. Varr called him weak. Mira saw that protecting public participation could look like weakness to people who preferred control.

議會把王位聽證排在村莊水務會議同一天。凱爾要求調整其中一場時間，免得居民無法兩邊參與。瓦爾說他軟弱。米菈看見，保護公眾參與在愛好控制的人眼中可能像弱點。
