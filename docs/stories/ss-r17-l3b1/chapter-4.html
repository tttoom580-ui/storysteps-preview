雨中的高塔

The Tower in the Rain

被龍族選中的假未婚妻 · 第 4 / 25 章 · 2000–3000 · 英中對照 · 校訂中

The fourth day began in a narrow alley behind the eastern market. Mira followed an old cable line from the ruined bridge, hoping to investigate where it ended. A delivery van passed too close and splashed dirty water over her boots. She stepped back, suddenly dizzy, and heard a crash as she fell against a stack of wooden boxes. A shopkeeper rushed out with cherry tea and a saucer, insisting she rest. “Bravery does not mean walking into every danger alone,” the woman said. Mira accepted the warmth of the cup and admitted she had not slept well. In the palace she could hide her fear, but here, among ordinary people, she felt it more honestly.

第四天從東市場後方狹窄的小巷開始。米菈沿著舊橋留下的電纜線追查，希望調查它通往何處。一輛送貨廂型車貼得太近，髒水濺滿她的靴子。她往後一退，突然頭暈，撞上成堆木箱。店主端出櫻桃茶和杯碟，堅持讓她休息。「勇敢不代表每種危險都要自己去闖，」婦人說。米菈接過帶著暖意的茶杯，承認自己沒睡好。在王宮她可以掩飾恐懼，在普通人之間反而能更誠實地感受它。

A child in the shop was learning to knit a scarf for her brother. On the wall hung a hand-drawn picture of the water tower, with a red label reading DO NOT ENTER. The shopkeeper said a worker had died there years ago. Some people called it an accident; others whispered murder. No one had been allowed to see the official report. Mira wrote down the difference between rumor and fact. “The definition of an accident matters,” she told the child, who asked why adults argued about words. “It decides who must repair the problem, and who can pretend nothing happened.”

店裡一名孩子正學著編織圍巾，要送給弟弟。牆上掛著手繪水塔圖，上面貼著紅色標籤：禁止進入。店主說多年以前有工人在那裡喪命。有人稱意外，也有人私下說是謀殺，但沒人獲准看正式報告。米菈記下流言和事實的差別。「意外的定義很重要，」她告訴那個問大人為何老是爭字眼的孩子，「因為它決定誰應修復問題，誰又能假裝什麼都沒發生。」

Cael met her beside the tower with two independent engineers. He had dressed in simple black clothes, though a passing student called his coat unexpectedly sexy. Mira laughed for the first time that day, and Cael looked pleased rather than proud. The engineers examined the solid foundation from a safe distance. They found a missing access plate, not a broken pipe. One said the tower could still feed the outer canal if the old control system worked. Another advised against climbing until they understood the weight on the upper steps. Mira agreed. A person who wanted to save others had no right to starve herself of sleep or ignore practical limits.

凱爾帶著兩位獨立工程師在水塔旁與她碰面。他穿著簡單的黑衣，路過的學生卻說那件外套意外地性感。米菈當天第一次笑了，凱爾看起來高興，卻不自滿。工程師在安全距離外檢查堅固的地基，發現問題不是破管，而是缺少檢修蓋。其中一人說，只要舊控制系統仍能運作，水塔就可以把水送往外渠。另一人提醒，上樓前必須先確認高處階梯承重。米菈同意。想救別人的人，也不能讓自己餓著、缺眠，或忽略實際限制。

While waiting, Mira took a short nap in a supervised office. She dreamed of East Pass, where she had once helped a stranger with a cut hand. He had thanked her and insisted that he could walk. When she woke, she saw Cael speaking to the engineers through the window, his posture familiar in a way she could not explain. The copper ring warmed against her skin. The shopkeeper brought bread and asked whether the prince cared about human troubles. “I am thankful that he is listening,” Mira replied, “but listening is only the beginning.” She wanted to judge actions over time, not a handsome face or a title.

等候期間，米菈在有專人照看的辦公室短暫睡了一覺。她夢見東隘口：曾有個手受傷的陌生人接受她的幫忙，向她道謝後仍堅持自己能走。醒來時，她隔窗看見凱爾與工程師交談，姿態竟有種說不上來的熟悉。銅戒貼著皮膚微微發熱。店主送來麵包，問王子是否真的在乎人類的苦難。「我很感謝他願意聽，」米菈回答，「但傾聽只是開始。」她想用時間判斷行動，而不是憑一張俊美臉孔或頭銜。

That afternoon they opened an exterior service panel. The original cable still connected the tower to a locked lower room, but it had been cut and hidden behind new stonework. The engineers took photographs and sealed the panel again. “No repairs without a complete plan,” one said. At the gate, Varr's aide demanded the images. Cael refused politely and requested a written order with a legal reason. The aide could offer neither. For the first time, Mira saw a royal title used as a shield for evidence rather than as a weapon against a weaker person. She did not mistake that for a solution, but it gave the investigation time to breathe.

下午，他們打開外側維修板，發現原本的電纜仍通向上鎖的下層房間，只是遭人切斷，再用新石牆遮住。工程師拍照後重新封好面板。「沒有完整計畫，不能先修，」其中一人說。閘門前，瓦爾的助手要求交出照片。凱爾禮貌拒絕，請他提供附法律理由的書面命令。對方拿不出來。米菈第一次看見王族頭銜被用來保護證據，而不是欺壓弱者。她並不把這當成解決方案，但調查終於得到喘息空間。

On their way home, Mira stopped before the empty tower and tried to imagine water moving through it again. The first four days had given her a permit, a contract, a vote, and a broken cable. None was a romantic gift. All were steps toward the village. Cael stood beside her without asking to hold her hand. The rain slowed. “Tomorrow,” he said, “the council will present you as my future wife.” Mira's stomach tightened. “Then tomorrow I must remember who I am.” Cael glanced at the copper ring and answered, “I have not forgotten.” The meaning of those words remained with her long after they returned to the palace.

回程時，米菈停在空蕩水塔前，試著想像水重新流過其中的景象。前四天，她得到通行證、契約、投票紀錄與斷裂電纜。沒有一樣是浪漫禮物，但每一樣都把她帶近家鄉。凱爾站在旁邊，沒有要求握她的手。雨勢漸小。「明天，」他說，「議會會正式介紹妳是我未來的妻子。」米菈的胃縮緊了。「那麼明天我得記住自己究竟是誰。」凱爾瞥向銅戒，回答：「我一直沒有忘記。」那句話的意思，直到回宮很久仍留在她心裡。

The succession committee tried to make a temporary water agreement into a test of loyalty to Cael. Mira asked why villages that needed water should have to choose a royal heir first. Nobody answered her directly, and the silence became another fact worth remembering.

繼承委員會試圖把臨時用水協議變成對凱爾的忠誠測試。米菈問，缺水的村莊為什麼必須先選王位繼承人。沒有人直接回答，這份沉默成了她記下的另一個事實。
