珍珠節的約定

The Pearl Festival

被龍族選中的假未婚妻 · 第 10 / 25 章 · 2000–3000 · 英中對照 · 校訂中

The tenth day was the Pearl Festival, when dragons celebrated the sea tide that fed the mountain springs. A fashionable designer brought Mira silk clothes, a pearl necklace, and a white handkerchief edged with gold. “A bride must impress the court,” she declared. Mira liked the soft fabric but refused the heavy necklace. She wanted to move freely during the ceremony. Cael appeared in a dark coat, looking bold beneath the blue festival lights. He offered his arm without touching hers first. Mira accepted it because she chose to, not because the royal audience expected it. That difference mattered more than the pearls.

第十天是珍珠節，龍族慶祝海潮滋養山間泉水的日子。時髦設計師送來絲綢衣物、珍珠項鍊，以及鑲金邊的白手帕。「新娘得讓王庭留下深刻印象，」她宣布。米菈喜歡柔軟布料，卻拒絕沉重項鍊。她希望典禮中能自由行動。凱爾穿著深色外套現身，在藍色節慶燈下顯得勇敢而沉著。他伸出手臂，沒有先碰她。米菈自願挽上，並非因為王室觀眾期待。這項差別比珍珠更重要。

In the square, an old woman dressed as an angel gave children slices of melon. A street performer showed how a dancer could punch the air without striking anyone. Beneath a decorated stove, a ceremonial flame burned steadily. Mira studied the structure of the festival stage and noted its emergency paths, a habit she could no longer turn off. Cael teased her gently, asking whether every celebration needed a map. “Every crowded place needs an exit,” she answered. He laughed and asked the festival manager to confirm the signs were clear. Mira could only stare at him, surprised that he could turn a small joke into useful action.

廣場上，一位扮成天使的老婦人分給孩子一片片甜瓜。街頭表演者示範舞者如何對空揮拳、卻不打到任何人。裝飾爐座下，儀式火焰穩定燃燒。米菈仔細查看舞台結構與緊急通道，已改不掉這種習慣。凱爾輕聲笑她，是不是每場慶典都要畫地圖。「只要人多就需要出口，」她答。他笑著請節慶管理員確認標示清楚。米菈盯著他，驚訝他能把一個小玩笑轉成真正有用的行動。

During the public dance, Chancellor Varr approached with a new offer. He would send water to Ashvale immediately if Mira signed a statement praising the private mine. “It is only one paragraph,” he said. Mira thought of the dry wells and wanted relief so badly that her hands shook. But the statement would declare the untested water safe. That was a moral line she could not cross. She refused and asked the council to send emergency water under its existing public duty instead. Varr's smile disappeared. Cael remained beside her but let the refusal be hers. His silence felt like support rather than abandonment.

公開舞會中，瓦爾帶著新提案靠近。他願意立刻送水去灰谷，條件是米菈簽署一份讚揚私人礦場的聲明。「只是一段話，」他說。米菈想到乾掉的井，渴望援助到雙手發抖，但文件等於宣告未經檢測的水安全。這條道德界線她不能跨。她拒絕，要求議會依既有公共責任提供緊急用水。瓦爾的笑容消失。凱爾留在旁邊，卻讓拒絕由她自己說出。他的沉默是支持，不是丟下她。

Later, in a quiet garden, Cael said he had once believed that a prince could solve every problem by being optimistic and working harder. “Somehow I kept missing the people who were never invited into the room,” he confessed. Mira asked why he had chosen her for the engagement. He answered that the court had chosen Celestine, but he had accepted only a thirty-day arrangement because permanent marriage required a separate decision by both people. Mira watched a leaf float in the fountain. “You speak as if you know I might not stay,” she whispered. “I know nobody can promise a future on another person's behalf,” he replied.

後來兩人在寧靜花園裡，凱爾說自己以前相信王子只要樂觀、更加努力，就能解決所有問題。「不知怎地，我總是忽略那些根本沒被邀進房間的人，」他承認。米菈問他為何選她訂婚。他說王庭選的是賽蕾絲汀，他只接受三十天安排，因為永久婚姻必須經過雙方另一次決定。米菈看著樹葉在噴泉上飄。「你說得好像知道我可能不會留下，」她低聲道。「我知道沒有人能替另一個人承諾未來，」他回答。

At the edge of the garden stood a mural of an ancient dragon beside a human woman. According to the legend, she had carried water through fire while he protected the road, and together they had built the first spring gate. Mira studied the picture carefully. The woman held a measuring rod, not a wedding ring. The artist had painted their hands apart. “They were partners,” Mira said. “Perhaps,” Cael replied. “History usually remembers the crown more clearly than the work.” She began to determine where the original account might be kept. The legend might be decoration, but it could also contain a clue to the law governing the spring.

花園邊有幅壁畫：古龍站在一位人類女子旁邊。傳說她穿過火焰運水，他守護道路，兩人共同築起第一道泉閘。米菈仔細看，女子手上拿的是測量桿，不是婚戒，畫家還把兩人的手畫得分開。「他們是夥伴，」米菈說。「也許，」凱爾回答，「歷史通常記得王冠，比記得實際工作更清楚。」她開始查明原始紀錄可能存放何處。傳說也許只是裝飾，卻可能包含泉源法律的線索。

Before the festival ended, Mira received a message from home: the first public water tanks had reached Ashvale. The villagers were relieved, but they still needed a permanent channel. She stepped away from the music to send thanks to the transport team. When she returned, Cael was waiting near the garden gate with two cups of tea. He did not ask for a dance or a kiss. He asked what she wanted next. “A copy of the spring's first agreement,” she answered. “And one evening when I do not have to borrow somebody's name.” Cael looked at her for a long moment. “I would like that evening too,” he said.

節慶結束前，米菈收到家鄉消息：第一批公共水車已抵達灰谷。村民稍獲紓解，但仍需要永久水道。她走離音樂，先向運輸團隊道謝。回來時，凱爾拿著兩杯茶在花園門口等她。他沒有要求跳舞或親吻，只問她接下來想要什麼。「第一份泉源協議的副本，」她回答，「還有一個不用借別人名字的晚上。」凱爾看著她許久。「我也想要那個晚上，」他說。

During the festival, Varr tried to turn a public celebration into a demonstration of support for one heir. Mira and the volunteers kept the water information booth open to everyone. A royal banner could not repair the village's broken channels, however bright its colors were.

節慶時瓦爾試圖把公開慶典變成對某位繼承人的支持大會。米菈與志工讓水務資訊攤位向所有人開放。王旗再鮮明，也修不好村莊斷裂的水道。
