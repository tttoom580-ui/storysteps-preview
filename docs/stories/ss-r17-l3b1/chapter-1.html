The Borrowed Ring

被龍族選中的假未婚妻 · 第 1 / 25 章 · 2000–3000 · 英中對照 · 校訂中

Mira had a berry stain on her sleeve when the invitation arrived. In Ashvale, a traveling circus had set up beside the old heater station, and a magician in a blue coat was making a silver coin spin above the children. The show was meant to raise money for the village wells. Mira was twenty-four, a mapmaker who knew that even a miracle would not fill a dry river by tomorrow. She had already decided to quit waiting for the capital. When the messenger's jet crossed the valley, a sealed letter fell at her feet, addressed to Lady Celestine, the dragon prince's promised bride.

請柬送來時，米菈的袖口還沾著莓果汁。灰谷村裡，巡迴馬戲團在舊暖氣站旁搭起帳篷，一位穿藍外套的魔術師正讓銀幣在孩子頭頂旋轉。演出是為村子的水井募款。二十四歲的製圖師米菈明白，就算有奇蹟，明天也不會讓乾涸的河流重新注滿水。她早就決定不再坐等王都出手。當信使的噴射飛行器越過山谷，一封封蠟的信恰巧落在她腳邊，收件人竟是龍族王子的未婚妻賽蕾絲汀小姐。

The real Celestine had fled across the border, leaving a fancy traveling case and a note in the village hut. She had refused a thirty-day political engagement arranged by her family. Mira found the note while sheltering from an ash storm. It was not permission to steal a name. Yet the letter promised the bride access to the royal spring, whose waters might save Ashvale. Mira studied the old land deed of her grandmother's farm. A nearby mine had broken a natural channel, and various officials kept claiming that the damage was harmless. Someone had tried to rob the valley of its last chance to recover.

真正的賽蕾絲汀已越境離開，只在村裡的小屋留下華麗的旅行箱與一張字條。她拒絕家族安排的三十天政治訂婚。米菈是在躲避灰燼風暴時發現那張紙的。這當然不代表她有權冒用別人的名字。然而，請柬承諾未婚妻可以接近王室泉源，也許能救活灰谷。米菈翻看祖母農田的舊地契：附近礦場破壞了天然水道，各級官員卻一再宣稱無害。有人正試圖奪走山谷最後的復原機會。

Her friend Tessa said the plan sounded like a fairy tale with a terrible ending. Mira agreed. She borrowed a pale cloak, packed one pint of clean water, and repaired the loose screw on her father's compass. Then she drew a map of the road to the dragon capital. Her grandmother, who played old jazz records while making bread, pressed a copper ring into her hand. It was plain, with a tiny mark like a pair of wings. “This belonged to the stranger you helped at East Pass,” she said. “Take it back if you find him.” Mira remembered a wounded traveler who had refused to tell her his name.

好友泰莎說，這個計畫聽起來像會以悲劇收場的童話故事。米菈同意。她借來淡色斗篷、裝好一品脫乾淨的水，又修緊父親羅盤上鬆掉的螺絲，接著畫好前往龍族王都的路線圖。祖母一邊用老爵士唱片伴著揉麵，一邊塞給她一枚銅戒。戒指樸素，只有一對小翅膀般的記號。「這是你在東隘口幫過的陌生人留下的，」祖母說，「遇到他就還回去。」米菈想起那位受傷旅人，當時他一直不肯說出名字。

The palace was built around a living mountain. Steam rose from the stone, and a red signal flashed above the entrance whenever a dragon landed. Guards stood in two lines. Beyond them waited Prince Cael, tall and quiet, with an expression that seemed difficult to tame. He bowed to the name on her invitation, but his eyes stopped on the copper ring. For an instant, Mira thought he might call her an impostor. Instead he said, “Thirty days, Lady Celestine. You will have your own room, your own keys, and the right to leave whenever you choose.” The formal words sounded unexpectedly like a warning against his own court.

王宮環繞著一座活火山建成。石地冒出蒸氣，每當有龍降落，入口上方就會閃起紅色訊號燈。衛兵分站兩列。王子凱爾在他們後方等候，身形高大而安靜，神情似乎難以馴服。他向請柬上的名字行禮，視線卻停在銅戒上。那一瞬間，米菈以為他會當眾揭穿她。凱爾只是說：「三十天，賽蕾絲汀小姐。妳有自己的房間、鑰匙，隨時可以選擇離開。」這番正式說詞，竟像是在提醒她提防他的王庭。

At dinner, the prince asked whether the eastern rivers were still dry. Mira froze. No one had told a royal stranger where she came from. He spoke softly, so the ministers could not hear. “Some promises matter more than the names written on them.” Then he turned to welcome his guests, leaving her with the sound of a question he had not asked. Across the table, Chancellor Varr smiled as if he owned the night. Mira realized that her strongest weapon was not a clever disguise. It was the map hidden under her sleeve, marked with villages the royal reports had forgotten. She would survive by telling the truth carefully, not by pretending she had nothing to fear.

晚餐時，王子突然問東部河川是否仍然乾涸。米菈僵住了。沒有人告訴這位王族陌生人她從哪裡來。他壓低聲音，讓大臣聽不見：「有些承諾，比寫在上面的名字更重要。」接著他轉身招呼賓客，留下那個沒真正問出口的問題。對面的瓦爾宰相微笑得彷彿今晚都屬於他。米菈明白，自己最有力的武器不是高明的偽裝，而是袖子裡的地圖：上面標著王室報告早已遺忘的村落。她必須審慎說出真相，而不是假裝自己不害怕。

Late that night, a small paper dragon slipped beneath her door. It carried a note in Cael's handwriting: “The spring opens to a guest, not to a bride. Read the contract before you sign.” Mira sat beside the cold window, looking at the words until the moon moved. If the prince knew her secret, why had he not exposed her? She could not answer, but she drew one fresh line on her map: a path from the palace to the eastern spring. Outside, bells rang once for the first night of their supposed engagement. Twenty-nine nights remained, and a whole valley was depending on a woman whom everyone here called by the wrong name.

深夜，一隻紙做的小龍從門縫滑進房間，帶著凱爾親筆寫的字條：「泉源向客人開放，不是向新娘開放。簽名前先讀合約。」米菈坐在冰冷窗邊，反覆看著那些字，直到月光移了位置。如果王子知道她的秘密，為何不揭穿？她暫時沒有答案，只在地圖上添了一條新線：由王宮通往東泉。窗外的鐘為這段假訂婚的第一夜敲了一聲。還剩二十九晚，而整個山谷都在依靠一個被所有人叫錯名字的女人。

That same night, Chancellor Varr told the succession council that Cael's choice of a bride would decide who could inherit the dragon throne. Mira heard the claim through a half-open door. She knew she had entered a political contest, not simply an engagement ceremony.

同一晚，瓦爾宰相告訴王位繼承議會，凱爾挑選的未婚妻將決定誰有資格繼承龍族王位。米菈從半開的門聽見這說法，明白自己闖進的不只是訂婚儀式，而是一場政治競逐。
