The Engagement Broadcast

被龍族選中的假未婚妻 · 第 5 / 25 章 · 2000–3000 · 英中對照 · 校訂中

用互動版查詞、朗讀與收藏

On the fifth day, the palace prepared an engagement broadcast for every district. Servants rolled out a red carpet; cooks brought bacon, ripe fruit, and bowls of peanut snacks for the guests. Mira wore a simple dress rather than the heavy bridal gown Varr had ordered. Before the ceremony, she visited the garage where royal vehicles were kept. A tired worker there had a cough from engine smoke and said the old motor had needed repair for months. The worker began to complain about being ignored, then stopped when she saw Mira's badge. Mira asked her to continue. Being a future princess should not mean people had to hide their troubles.

第五天，王宮準備向各區直播訂婚儀式。侍從鋪開紅地毯，廚師送來培根、熟水果和一碗碗花生招待賓客。米菈穿著簡單洋裝，沒有換上瓦爾指定的厚重新娘禮服。典禮前，她先去王室車庫。那裡一名疲憊工人因引擎廢氣而咳嗽，說舊馬達早該修理，卻幾個月沒人理會。工人開始抱怨，看見米菈的徽章又停口。米菈請她繼續說。成為準王妃不該代表所有人都得掩藏困難。

A considerable crowd waited outside, and the microphone made every footstep sound larger. A young announcer struggled to pronounce the dragon names. Behind the stage, a technician held a flashlight over the control box, where steam had entered the wiring. He warned that the sound might fail. Varr's staff wanted to proceed anyway. Mira asked the technician to speak directly to the safety manager instead of being silenced. “This engagement may be fake,” she thought, “but our responsibility to the crowd is not.” The broadcast began ten minutes late, after the unsafe cable was isolated and a backup speaker was tested.

外面聚集了相當多人，麥克風把每個腳步聲放得很大。一名年輕主持人念龍族名字時頻頻卡住。舞台後方，技師拿手電筒照著控制箱，發現蒸氣滲入電線。他提醒聲音可能中斷，但瓦爾的幕僚仍想照常開始。米菈請技師直接向安全主管報告，不要被噤聲。「這場訂婚可能是假的，」她心想，「我們對群眾的責任卻是真的。」不安全的電線隔離、備用喇叭測試完畢後，直播晚了十分鐘才開始。

When Cael took the stage, he did not call Mira his property or promise that she would obey. He said they were entering a temporary engagement to explore a partnership between their communities. Mira felt a sharp ache of sorrow for the name she was using. She had not meant to hurt Celestine, who had fled an unfair arrangement. She would need to find her and make matters right. In the front row, Varr's jaw tightened. He expected a decorative bride, not a woman who insisted on reading every condition. Mira reached into her pocket and touched the small sack of ash from home. It kept her purpose clear.

凱爾上台時，沒有說米菈屬於自己，也沒承諾她會服從。他宣布這段暫時訂婚是為了探索兩個社群的合作。米菈想到自己借用的名字，心裡湧起尖銳的悲傷。她不想傷害那位逃離不公平安排的賽蕾絲汀，日後一定得找到她、好好補償。前排的瓦爾咬緊下顎。他預期的是一位裝飾用的新娘，沒想到她堅持逐條閱讀條件。米菈伸手摸了摸口袋裡裝著家鄉灰燼的小袋子，提醒自己此行的目的。

During the formal reading, an official announced that a royal engagement automatically allowed the crown to control the eastern spring. Mira knew that was not written in the deed or in the old voting record. She interrupted and asked the official to identify the law. A violent silence followed. Cael looked toward her, then calmly repeated the question. When the official could not produce a legal text, the claim was removed from the broadcast summary. It was a tiny victory, but the audience heard it. Mira's knees shook beneath her dress. Courage, she discovered, sometimes meant making your voice steady while the entire room wished you would be quiet.

正式宣讀時，一位官員聲稱王室訂婚會自動讓王冠取得東泉的控制權。米菈知道地契與舊投票紀錄都沒這樣寫。她打斷他，要求指出法條。現場陷入令人窒息的寂靜。凱爾看向她，接著平靜地重複問題。官員拿不出法律文字，這項聲明便從直播摘要中移除。勝利很小，觀眾卻都聽見了。米菈的膝蓋在裙下發抖。她發現，有時勇敢就是在全場希望你閉嘴時，仍努力讓聲音保持穩定。

Afterward, the palace offered an expensive dinner in a room full of roses. Mira asked instead to see the technician who had prevented the electrical fault. She thanked him and requested that the unsafe equipment be listed publicly. Cael accompanied her, but let her lead the conversation. “You handled that better than I did,” he said as they returned through the hall. Mira shook her head. “You had the power to stop it sooner.” He accepted the criticism. The honest reply surprised her more than praise would have. At the far end of the corridor, Varr watched them from a dark doorway, holding a fresh engagement contract neither of them had signed.

儀式結束後，王宮安排一桌擺滿玫瑰的昂貴晚餐。米菈反而要求先去見那位避免電線故障的技師，向他道謝，並要求把危險設備列入公開紀錄。凱爾陪她同行，卻由她主導談話。「妳處理得比我好，」回廊上他說。米菈搖頭。「你有權力更早阻止它。」他接受批評。這種誠實的回應，比讚美更令她意外。長廊另一端，瓦爾站在暗門口看著他們，手裡拿著一份兩人都沒簽過的新訂婚契約。

Back in her room, Mira discovered that the broadcast had reached Ashvale. Tessa sent a picture of villagers gathered around one borrowed screen. Beneath it she wrote, “For the first time, someone asked them to explain the spring.” Mira wanted to answer that she was frightened, that the whole palace might turn on her at dawn. Instead she sent the village a copy of the corrected statement and a list of dates for the coming water inspection. Then she wrote a private note to Cael: “Tomorrow, we discuss who changed the agreement.” The paper dragon returned with a single answer: “Yes. Together, if you still choose to.”

回房後，米菈發現直播已傳到灰谷。泰莎傳來一張照片，村民圍著借來的螢幕觀看。下方寫著：「第一次有人要求他們解釋泉水的事。」米菈想回覆自己多麼害怕，整座王宮可能明天就與她為敵；最後她寄出修正後的聲明副本，以及即將進行水務檢查的日期。接著她寫給凱爾一張私人字條：「明天，我們談談是誰改了協議。」紙龍送回簡短的回答：「好。如果妳仍願意，我們一起談。」

Aurine sent a warning that the ministers planned to count the false engagement as a promise of votes. Cael placed a signed statement on record: no guest, bride, or partner could cast a community's vote. Mira began to understand why he had insisted upon separate rights in their contract.

奧琳送來警告：大臣打算把假訂婚當成選票承諾。凱爾留下簽名聲明，表明客人、未婚妻或伴侶都不能替社群投票。米菈漸漸明白他為何堅持在契約裡分清權利。
