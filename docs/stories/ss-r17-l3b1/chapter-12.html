第五道水閘

The Fifth Water Gate

被龍族選中的假未婚妻 · 第 12 / 25 章 · 2000–3000 · 英中對照 · 校訂中

On day twelve, Cael and Mira met at the archive with Captain Irena, an elderly engineer who had once led the spring operation. She was still capable of reading the old gauges without electricity. Irena spread a map of a canyon across the desk and pointed to five water gates. Four had public records; the fifth had vanished from every monthly report. “That is the situation,” she said. “If someone chose to edit one gate out of the record, nobody can safely move the others.” Mira asked permission to copy the map. The captain agreed, provided the original property stamps remained visible.

第十二天，凱爾與米菈在檔案館見到艾蓮娜隊長。她是年長工程師，曾帶領泉源系統作業，即使沒有電力仍能讀懂舊儀表。艾蓮娜把峽谷地圖攤在桌上，指向五道水閘。四道有公開紀錄，第五道卻從每月報告中消失。「情況就是這樣，」她說，「如果一座閘門被從紀錄中刪掉，其他閘門就不能安全操作。」米菈要求複製地圖。隊長同意，條件是保留原始財產印記。

The group traveled to a narrow test station where a rusty hatch stood beneath a low roof. A bubble rose, then another followed from the channel outside. Irena explained that the missing gate might still control the direction of the flow. A flash of blue light crossed the water when an old sensor woke, and a clerk hurried to collect its information. “The display is hopeful,” said Irena, “but do not open anything until the reading is checked.” Mira remembered how a single untested claim had nearly entered the engagement broadcast. She appreciated that the captain was cautious even when good news seemed close enough to touch.

小隊前往狹窄的測試站，低屋頂下有一道鏽蝕的艙蓋。站外渠道冒出一個泡泡，接著又冒出另一個。艾蓮娜說失蹤的水閘可能仍掌控流向。舊感測器啟動時，藍色閃光掠過水面，書記員急忙收集數據。「畫面讓人有希望，」隊長說，「但讀數核實前不要開啟任何設備。」米菈想起差點混入訂婚直播的未經測試聲明。好消息明明近在眼前，隊長仍保持謹慎，令她十分欣賞。

At lunch, they ate simple bread with a little spice in a roadside shelter. Mira asked Cael about East Pass. He said he had traveled there without an escort to learn how people beyond the capital lived. A storm had injured his hand, and she had found him beside the road. “I did not tell you who I was because I feared you would help the prince instead of the person,” he admitted. Mira set down her cup. “You should have trusted me to decide.” He accepted the correction. “You were right then. You are right now.” Her anger softened, though it did not disappear. A kind memory did not erase an old choice.

午餐時，他們在路邊遮雨棚吃簡單麵包，加了少量香料。米菈問凱爾東隘口的事。他說自己曾不帶護衛前往，只為了解王都以外的人如何生活。暴風傷了他的手，她在路邊找到他。「我沒說自己是誰，因為怕妳幫的是王子，而不是那個人，」他承認。米菈放下杯子。「你應該相信我能自己判斷。」他接受指正。「妳當時說得對，現在也對。」她的氣略微消了，卻沒有完全消失。善意的回憶不會抹去過去的選擇。

Back at the station, Irena was chosen to represent the engineers before the inspectors. She asked that a small sword displayed above the hatch be removed. It was a ceremonial symbol, not a useful tool, and its sharp edge blocked safe access. When the old cover was lifted, trapped air escaped in a long sigh. The team discovered that a private pipe had been connected to the fifth gate. The connection could be measured without opening the water channel. Mira drew every joint, while Cael held the lamp and another worker checked the distances. They collected three independent records so no client of the mine could dismiss the finding as one person's mistake.

回到測試站，艾蓮娜代表工程師向檢查員說明。她要求移走艙蓋上方的展示長劍。那只是儀式象徵，不是工具，尖銳刀緣還妨礙安全通行。舊蓋一掀，困住的空氣長長嘆出。小隊發現第五閘被接上一條私人管線，不開啟水道也能測量這項連接。米菈畫下每個接頭，凱爾持燈，另一名工人核對距離。他們保留三份獨立紀錄，免得礦場的客戶將發現推成單人失誤。

Mira noticed that the oldest map listed two keepers for the fifth gate: a human delegate and a dragon delegate. The law did not say husband and wife. It required two people who could each refuse. She felt her eyes fill with tears and stepped aside to weep quietly so nobody would mistake her feelings for an instruction to act. The mountain spring had not been locked by romance or fate. It had been locked by an agreement that required shared responsibility. She told Irena what she had found, and the captain began searching for the original register. Cael stood quietly near the doorway, letting Mira explain the discovery in her own words.

米菈注意到最舊地圖為第五水閘列出兩位保管人：一位人類代表、一位龍族代表。法律沒有寫丈夫與妻子，只要求兩個都有權拒絕的人。她眼眶湧上淚水，先走到一旁悄悄哭泣，不希望別人把她的感動誤作行動指令。山泉不是被浪漫或命運鎖住，而是被一份要求共同承擔責任的協議保護。她把發現告訴艾蓮娜，隊長開始尋找原始登記冊。凱爾靜靜站在門旁，讓米菈用自己的話說明。

That night, Mira wrote to Tessa about the fifth gate. She did not promise water before the safety checks were complete. The letter described the new evidence, the risks, and the next meeting. Then she faced Cael across a quiet table. “You said you recognized me at East Pass,” she began. “Do you also know the name I was born with?” He took a slow breath. “Yes, Mira. I knew when I saw your ring on the first day.” She closed her eyes. The question she had feared most had finally been spoken. There was nowhere left to hide, and unexpectedly that brought a small measure of peace.

當晚，米菈寫信告訴泰莎第五水閘的發現。安全檢查完成前，她沒有承諾供水。信裡列出新證據、風險與下一場會議。接著她在安靜桌邊面對凱爾。「你說認出我是在東隘口，」她開口，「你也知道我的本名嗎？」他緩緩吸氣。「知道，米菈。第一天看到妳的戒指時就知道。」她閉上眼睛。最害怕的問題終於說出口。再也無處躲藏，而這意外帶來一絲平靜。

The fifth gate records proved that neither royal candidate possessed the entire water system. The succession council had treated a shared public duty as a prize. Aurine asked that the gate's history be read aloud before the council considered another crown.

第五水閘的紀錄證明，任何一位王位候選人都不擁有整套水系。繼承議會卻把公共責任當成獎品。奧琳要求議會在討論王冠前，先公開宣讀水閘歷史。
