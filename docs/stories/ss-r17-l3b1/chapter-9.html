隧道裡的聲音

The Voice in the Tunnel

被龍族選中的假未婚妻 · 第 9 / 25 章 · 2000–3000 · 英中對照 · 校訂中

用互動版查詞、朗讀與收藏

On the ninth day, the inspectors finally entered the old tunnel. A small cabin at the entrance held maps, a horn for emergencies, and a damaged database terminal. The air smelled of wet stone. Mira was accompanied by two engineers and a skillful guide who knew the passages from childhood. Cael joined them as a witness, not as commander of the team. From somewhere deep inside came a low roar. Mira felt a moment of fright, then heard water splash against a wall. The guide said the faint sound grew louder every week when the pumps ran. No one wanted to wander beyond the marked route without checking the ceiling.

第九天，檢查員終於進入舊隧道。入口小屋放著地圖、緊急用號角和一台損壞的資料庫終端。空氣裡有濕石氣味。米菈身邊有兩位工程師，以及從小熟悉通道的熟練嚮導。凱爾以證人而非隊伍指揮者身分同行。深處傳來低沉吼聲，米菈一陣驚嚇，接著聽出是水拍打石牆的聲音。嚮導說，每週只要水泵運轉，微弱的聲音就會變大。沒有確認天花板前，誰都不願離開標示路線亂走。

The first chamber contained an old voting notice from the years when the city had experimented with democracy. Someone had left it pinned above a broken chair. Mira copied the dates into her notebook. Below the notice, a weekly maintenance sheet listed a missing control key. An engineer tested the nearby floor and warned it might sink under too much weight. Everyone stepped back. Cael handed Mira a lamp but did not move ahead of the guide. The inspection was less dramatic than stories about dragon battles, yet much more useful: a crack measured correctly might save dozens of people. Mira began to feel that her map was becoming a team effort.

第一個洞室裡，掛著城市嘗試民主制度年代的舊投票公告，有人把它釘在壞椅子上。米菈把日期抄進筆記。公告下方，每週維修表列著一把失蹤的控制鑰匙。工程師測試附近地板，警告承重過大可能下陷。所有人立刻退開。凱爾把燈交給米菈，卻沒有搶先走到嚮導前方。這趟檢查不像龍族戰鬥故事那樣戲劇化，卻更有用：正確測量一道裂縫，也許就能救下幾十人。米菈開始感到，自己的地圖正成為團隊成果。

Farther inside, Mira scraped her finger on a rusty edge. The cut began to bleed, leaving a small mark on the cloth she wrapped around her finger, and the medic told her to stop working until it was cleaned. Mira almost argued; then she remembered the injured mechanic and agreed. Cael sat beside her while she rested. “I know I can bore you by talking about law,” he said. Mira answered with laughter. “You have not bored me once. You simply make terrible jokes.” He smiled, and for a moment neither seemed to be a prince or a false bride. She wondered whether they could have become friends without the court between them.

再往裡走，米菈的手指被鏽邊刮傷，一點血滲過她纏上的布。醫護人員要求先清潔傷口再繼續。米菈差點爭辯，隨即想起那位受傷技工，答應休息。凱爾坐在旁邊。「我知道一直談法律會讓妳無聊，」他說。米菈笑了。「你一次也沒讓我無聊，只是笑話真的很差。」他也笑了。那一刻，兩人似乎都不是王子或假新娘。她不禁想，若沒有王庭夾在中間，他們是否也能成為朋友。

The guide found a narrow door with a fresh lock. Beyond it the water roared louder. The mining company's record said the room was empty, but the engineers detected heat and recent movement on the other side. They did not break in. They used a viewing pipe to confirm the presence of machinery, then called for an authorized entry team. Mira photographed a cable leading from the locked room toward the dry riverbed. She knew that no single picture proved deliberate harm. Still, the equipment was real, and its position did not match the public map. Cael asked everyone to keep their notes separate until the reports could be compared.

嚮導找到一扇裝了新鎖的窄門，門後水聲更大。礦業公司紀錄說房間是空的，工程師卻測出另一側有熱源及近期活動跡象。他們沒有直接破門，而是用觀測管確認裡頭存在機械，再請有權限的進入小隊接手。米菈拍下由鎖房通往乾河床的電纜。她知道單張照片無法證明有人蓄意傷害，但設備確實存在，位置也不符合公開地圖。凱爾請每人分別保存筆記，等報告完成再互相比對。

On the way out, the guide whistled a simple tune. It echoed down the tunnel and returned like another voice. Mira remembered the stories of people missing in these hills, stories used to frighten children away from the old mine. She asked whether any names had been recorded. The guide said some workers had moved away and others had been lost during floods. No reliable list existed. A name was a more useful weapon against neglect than any blade. Mira wrote a reminder to request the old employment records. “People are not numbers on a map,” Cael said. She agreed, but added, “A missing name on a record is still a clue that someone was not counted.”

離開時，嚮導吹起簡單曲調，聲音沿隧道回盪，像有另一個人回應。米菈想起山裡失蹤者的故事，過去常被用來嚇阻孩子靠近礦場。她問是否留下姓名紀錄。嚮導說有人搬走，也有人在洪水中失蹤，從來沒有可靠名單。對抗忽視，一個名字比刀劍更有力。米菈寫下要調取舊雇用資料的提醒。「人不是地圖上的數字，」凱爾說。她同意，又補充：「但紀錄上少了一個名字，仍是某人不被計入的線索。」

At the palace, Varr criticized the inspection for wasting time. He claimed the princess's supposed lover had influenced her judgment. Mira stood before the council and corrected him. “We are engaged for thirty days. That does not make the prince my lover, and it gives him no right to direct my report.” Her voice stayed calm while her heart raced. She then presented the photographs, measurements, and written requests, each labeled with a date. Shortly afterward, an independent engineer confirmed the cable had been installed after the last public survey. A quiet room was suddenly full of questions Varr could not dismiss with a joke.

回宮後，瓦爾批評檢查浪費時間，還聲稱「公主的情人」影響了她的判斷。米菈站在議會前糾正他。「我們訂婚三十天，不代表王子是我的情人，也不表示他有權指揮我的報告。」她心跳加速，聲音卻保持平靜。接著她提出照片、測量數據與書面申請，每項都標明日期。不久後，獨立工程師確認電纜是在最後一次公開測量後才安裝。原本安靜的房間忽然充滿問題，瓦爾再也無法用玩笑帶過。

A royal archivist discovered that the same seal had been used on two competing claims to the throne. Aurine and Cael agreed to let the archive examine it before another announcement. Their supporters disliked the delay, but forged certainty would be worse.

王室檔案員發現，兩份互相競爭的王位主張竟使用同一枚印章。奧琳與凱爾同意讓檔案館先檢驗再公告。支持者不喜歡延誤，但偽造的確定性只會更糟。
