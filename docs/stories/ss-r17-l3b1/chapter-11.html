暴風裡的龍

The Dragon in the Storm

被龍族選中的假未婚妻 · 第 11 / 25 章 · 2000–3000 · 英中對照 · 校訂中

Day eleven began with an accurate report from the tunnel engineers. The hidden motor was moving water toward a private storage basin, and a loose joint could break under pressure. The county safety office ordered a temporary stop to the operation. Chancellor Varr protested that his company needed time to respond. Mira agreed he deserved a hearing, but not permission to keep an unsafe machine running. She carried the report through cold rain, her boots filling with mud beside a ditch. Cael met her at the gate and offered a dry cloak. “Bless the engineers for writing everything down,” she said. He nodded, knowing records were their strongest protection.

第十一天，隧道工程師送來精確報告。隱藏馬達正把水引向私人蓄水池，鬆動接頭可能因水壓破裂。郡安全辦公室下令暫停操作。瓦爾抗議公司需要時間回應。米菈承認他有受聽證的權利，卻不能因此繼續運轉危險機械。她在冷雨中提著報告，靴子踩進溝渠邊的泥巴。凱爾在閘門迎接，遞給她乾斗篷。「還好工程師把一切都記下來，」她說。他點頭，明白紀錄是最好的保護。

At the eastern ridge, the team found a wooden bridge built from old timber. A plastic warning sign had fallen into the water. They could not drive heavy equipment across, so a small crew carried instruments by hand. Mira helped move a medium case of sensors, a gas canister for the generator, and a freezer box for water samples. An engineer explained his theory: if the stored basin overflowed, it could weaken the natural wall above Ashvale. The estimate was roughly three days of heavy rain away from a serious failure. Mira felt each muscle tighten. Her village did not have three comfortable days. It needed a safe plan immediately.

東部山脊上，團隊找到一座以舊木材搭建的小橋。一面塑膠警示牌倒在水裡。重設備無法開過去，只能由小隊手提儀器。米菈幫忙搬一箱中型感測器、一罐發電機用的燃氣，以及保存水樣的冷凍箱。工程師提出理論：蓄水池若溢出，可能削弱灰谷上方的天然岩壁。粗估再有三天豪雨，便有嚴重失效風險。米菈全身肌肉繃緊。家鄉已沒有三天可以舒適等待，現在就需要安全計畫。

A sudden flash of lightning struck the ridge, and the bridge began to shake. The guide shouted for everyone to move toward the marked stones. Mira slipped; the sample box fell toward the stream. Before anyone could dive after it, Cael stepped into the open and changed. Red wings spread from his shoulders, and his human shape grew into a dragon larger than the bridge. He caught the falling box with one claw and held the broken rail steady while the crew crossed. Mira had seen dragons from a distance, but never this close. His scales shone violet beneath the storm, and steam rose where the rain touched them.

一道閃電突然打上山脊，木橋開始搖晃。嚮導大喊要大家往標記石塊移動。米菈滑了一跤，樣本箱朝溪流掉去。還沒有人能跳下去撿，凱爾就踏向空地變身了。紅色雙翼從肩頭展開，人形長成比橋還大的巨龍。他用爪子接住掉落的箱子，撐住斷裂欄杆，讓隊員逐一通過。米菈遠遠見過龍，從未這麼近。他的鱗片在暴風中泛紫，雨落在上面冒起蒸氣。

Once everyone reached safe ground, Cael lowered his head and asked whether anyone was hurt. His voice was deeper but recognizably his. The engineers checked their equipment before declaring the crossing complete. “That was an intelligent use of strength,” Mira said, still shaking. “You supported the bridge instead of trying to fly everyone away.” The dragon blinked, almost embarrassed. “I cannot carry a whole team safely in this wind. And a dragon is not a substitute for a bridge inspection.” One engineer laughed. Mira felt an unexpected warmth. The prince who could become a powerful creature had still respected the team's judgment.

所有人抵達安全處後，凱爾低下龍首，詢問是否有人受傷。他的聲音更低沉，卻仍是她熟悉的聲音。工程師檢查好設備才宣布渡橋完成。「那是聰明地運用力量，」米菈仍發抖地說，「你支撐橋，而不是逞強把所有人載走。」巨龍眨眨眼，像有點不好意思。「這種風裡，我不能安全載整隊人。龍也不能取代橋梁檢查。」一位工程師笑出聲。米菈心底升起意外的暖意。能變成強大生物的王子，仍尊重團隊專業。

They reached a sheltered station, where the guide agreed to perform a safety count. Mira's pulse was fast, and sweat cooled beneath her collar despite the cold. The engineers inspected the water samples; the chemical reading was high enough to demand independent testing but not enough to explain every illness in the valley. “We should not pretend one theory answers all questions,” Mira said. Cael, returned to human form, agreed. They sent the data to the county office and requested an emergency inspection of the private basin. Their plan had three parts: protect the ridge, restore public access, and preserve the evidence. None required a royal wedding.

他們抵達遮蔽站後，嚮導進行安全點名。米菈脈搏跳得很快，衣領下的汗在寒冷中變涼。工程師檢查水樣：化學讀數足以要求獨立檢測，但還不足以解釋山谷的所有疾病。「不能假裝一個理論回答所有問題，」米菈說。恢復人形的凱爾同意。他們將資料送到郡辦公室，並要求緊急檢查私人蓄水池。計畫分成三部分：保護山脊、恢復公共通行、保存證據。沒有任何一項需要王室婚禮。

That night, Mira thanked Cael for helping the crew, then asked him a harder question. “When we met at East Pass, was it you?” He paused, looking at the copper ring. “Yes,” he said, “but I owe you a longer answer than a tired night can hold.” Mira wanted that answer immediately. Yet she saw how pale he was after the transformation, and she was exhausted too. They agreed to talk the next day with no attendants present, in a room either could leave. For once she did not feel that waiting meant surrendering control. It meant giving a difficult truth enough space to be told properly.

那晚，米菈先謝謝凱爾救了隊員，再問更難的一題。「我們在東隘口見過的人，是你嗎？」他停住，望向銅戒。「是，」他說，「但我欠妳的答案，不是一個疲累夜晚說得完的。」米菈很想立刻聽完，卻看到他變身後臉色蒼白，自己也筋疲力盡。兩人約好明天在沒有侍從、任何一方都能自由離開的房間談。這次，她不覺得等待等於放棄控制。那是替困難的真相留出足夠空間。

After the storm, Aurine offered dragon messengers to carry weather warnings to remote farms. Cael accepted the help without making her pledge loyalty. Mira noticed that two people seeking the same throne could still choose to protect the same children.

暴風雨後，奧琳提供龍族信使，為偏遠農場傳送天氣警報。凱爾接受幫助，沒有要求她宣誓效忠。米菈發現爭奪同一王位的兩人仍能選擇保護同一群孩子。
