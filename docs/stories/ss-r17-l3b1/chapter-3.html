兩個名字的地圖

A Map with Two Names

被龍族選中的假未婚妻 · 第 3 / 25 章 · 2000–3000 · 英中對照 · 校訂中

用互動版查詞、朗讀與收藏

Mira's request was approved before breakfast, but the archive clerk examined her carefully. “Your background is not on the public list,” he said. She had prepared an answer about private study and kept it short. The clerk gave her a bundle of yellow papers, a bamboo ruler, and a damaged map with one missing corner. Cael waited outside, pretending to chat with a guard while she read. The map showed two lines running downtown from the mountains, one for the dragon district and one for the poorer human quarter. Between them was a narrow channel, crossed out in fresh black ink. Even a simple chip in the paper could not hide that change.

米菈的申請早餐前就獲准了，但檔案員仔細打量她。「公開名單上沒有妳的背景資料，」他說。米菈事先想好自己只是私人研究，簡短回答。檔案員交給她一捆泛黃文件、一把竹尺，以及一張缺了角的地圖。凱爾在外面假裝與衛兵閒聊，等她閱讀。地圖畫著兩條由山間流入市中心的水道，一條供龍族區，一條供較窮的人類區。兩者之間有道狹窄支流，被新鮮黑墨劃掉。即使紙上有缺口，也遮不住這項改動。

The civil records were stranger still. Twenty years earlier, an elected committee had held a democratic poll on the use of mountain water. The written result supported shared access, but the later construction papers said the opposite. “That was when poverty began to deepen in the east,” an elderly archivist whispered. She once worked for a stone dealer who sold to the mines. He had instructed her to dine with important people and never discuss those contracts. She had kept copies because the figures troubled her. Mira offered her a seat and a warm doughnut from the library café, then asked whether she would permit an independent check of her account.

民事紀錄更加奇怪。二十年前，一個民選委員會曾就山泉用途舉行民主投票。書面結果支持共享水源，後來的工程文件卻寫著相反的決定。「東部的貧困就是從那時開始加深的，」一位年長的檔案員輕聲說。她過去替供應礦場石材的商人工作，老闆命令她只與重要人物用餐，絕不能談合約。她因數字可疑而私下保存副本。米菈請她坐下，遞上圖書館咖啡店的溫甜甜圈，詢問她是否願意讓獨立人員查核證詞。

On the roof, Mira watched a pigeon leap from one railing to the next. From here the palace looked mighty, but the east district lay beneath a layer of gray dust. Cael joined her without a guard. “I saw you read the old vote,” he said. “It embarrasses my council.” Mira studied him. “How will they react if I show them the true numbers?” He did not promise an easy victory. “Some will protect their power. Others will listen if the substance of your evidence is clear.” For the sake of the valley, she needed more than courage. She needed copies that a hostile witness could not dismiss.

米菈在屋頂看著鴿子從一段欄杆躍到另一段。從這裡望去，王宮氣勢雄偉，東區卻籠罩灰塵。凱爾沒有帶護衛就走近。「我看見妳讀了舊投票結果，」他說，「那讓我的議會難堪。」米菈看著他。「如果我公布真實數字，他們會如何反應？」他沒有承諾輕鬆獲勝。「有人會保護自己的權力；也有人會聽，只要妳證據的實質內容清楚。」為了山谷，她需要的不只是勇氣，還要有連敵對證人都無法輕易否認的副本。

They spent the afternoon comparing the original survey with a newer map. Every measurement was correct until the final page, where the water channel suddenly moved behind the mine. “An impossible turn,” Mira said. She unfolded her father's notes and laid them beside the archive copy. A single bridge that had once served both districts was erased from the recent drawing. Cael did not reach across the table to take her papers. He waited until she pushed them toward him. “May I?” he asked, and only then touched the page. Such a small question startled Mira more than a royal speech. In Ashvale, officials had always taken her work without asking.

兩人花了整個下午對照原始測量圖與新地圖。每一筆測量都正確，直到最後一頁，水道竟突然繞到礦場後方。「不可能這樣轉彎，」米菈說。她展開父親的筆記，放在檔案副本旁。那座曾同時服務兩區的橋，在新圖上被抹去了。凱爾沒有伸手搶她的文件，而是等她推過去。「我可以看嗎？」他問，得到同意才碰紙。這麼小的問題，卻比王室演說更令米菈吃驚。在灰谷，官員總是不問就拿走她的成果。

At sunset they followed the line of the vanished bridge through the public gardens. There, beneath flowers, Mira found the foundation stones still standing. The river had not changed its mind. Someone had changed its story. Cael admitted that the former water minister had served Chancellor Varr, who still controlled the mining permits. A single accusation would be too weak. They would need the archive clerk, the old poll, physical measurements, and the village's own record. “You could leave the palace now,” he said quietly. “The safe path is still open.” Mira answered that she would stay until she had enough evidence to help the valley, but not one day longer for a lie.

日落時，兩人沿著消失的橋位走過公共花園。米菈在花草下找到仍然存在的地基石。河流沒有自己改變方向，是有人改寫了它的故事。凱爾承認前任水務大臣曾替瓦爾宰相做事，而瓦爾仍掌管採礦許可。單一指控太薄弱，他們需要檔案員、舊投票、現地測量，以及村民自己的紀錄。「妳現在就能離宮，」他低聲說，「安全的路還開著。」米菈回答，她會留下直到找到足以幫助山谷的證據，但絕不會為謊言多待一天。

That night, the archivist slipped an unsigned note into Mira's room. It said the bridge records had been altered two months before the engagement was announced. The dates could be related, or they could be coincidence. Mira wrote both possibilities on separate pages rather than choosing the more dramatic one. She began to understand that a map was not merely a picture of land. It was a record of who could travel, who received water, and who was expected to remain unseen. She locked the copies in her own case, placed the copper ring beside them, and wondered whether Prince Cael had invited a false bride because he needed an honest mapmaker.

那夜，檔案員悄悄把一張沒有署名的紙條送入房間。橋梁資料的修改時間，竟是訂婚宣布前兩個月。兩件事可能有關，也可能只是巧合。米菈沒有選擇更戲劇性的解釋，而是把兩種可能寫在不同頁面。她開始明白，地圖不只是土地圖像，更記錄了誰能通行、誰能獲得水，以及誰被期待一直隱身。她把副本鎖進自己的箱子，將銅戒放在旁邊，心裡忽然冒出一個念頭：凱爾邀請假新娘，難道正是因為他需要一位誠實的製圖師？

Varr presented a beautiful chart showing the royal springs as property of the crown. Aurine questioned its missing dates, and Cael refused to certify it. For the first time, Mira saw two rivals agree that the court had to produce better evidence.

瓦爾拿出一幅華麗圖表，把王室泉水全列成王冠財產。奧琳質疑圖表缺少日期，凱爾拒絕為它背書。米菈第一次看見兩名競爭者同意：王庭必須拿出更可靠的證據。
