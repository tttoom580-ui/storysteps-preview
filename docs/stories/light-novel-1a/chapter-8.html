公園後面的幽靈

The Ghost Behind the Park

轉學生的尾巴，藏不住了！ · 第 8 / 25 章 · 1000以下 · 英中對照 · 校訂中

用互動版查詞、朗讀與收藏

Tuesday's biggest news was a ghost in the park behind the school. Toma had heard it from a boy at baseball practice. Someone in a white dress had been seen flying past the trees. Yuna thought it was a bad story to spread. Riko agreed. But Kai looked worried, because in his home some sky animals could look like ghosts when the light was low. He asked if they could walk through the park after school and find out what people had seen. “Only while it is still light,” Riko said. They made a small group and told Ms. Aki where they were going. No one would go alone.

星期二最大的新聞是學校後方公園鬧鬼。斗真從棒球練習的男孩那裡聽說，有人看見穿白裙子的影子飛過樹梢。優奈覺得不該亂傳，理子也同意。但海有些擔心，因為他的家鄉確實有天空動物在昏暗光線下看起來像幽靈。他問大家能否放學後去公園看看目擊者究竟看到了什麼。「只能趁天還亮。」理子說。他們組成小隊，也先告訴秋老師要去哪裡。任何人都不單獨行動。

Behind the baseball field they found a girl holding a broken kite. The white dress in the story was really a piece of cloth tied to a long string. When the wind came, the cloth went up and down between two trees. The girl looked ready to cry. Her kite had broken, and the other children had run away because they were afraid. Kai gave her a nice smile. “We are not afraid of your kite,” he said. “But we need to ask an adult before we climb after it.” The girl nodded. The park officer arrived and showed them a safe place to wait while he brought the kite down.

他們在棒球場後面找到一個抱著破風箏的女孩。傳說中的白裙子，其實是綁在長線上的一片布。風吹來時，布就在兩棵樹間上下飄動。女孩快哭了。她的風箏壞掉，其他孩子因為害怕都跑開。海對她微笑：「我們不怕你的風箏，但要先請大人幫忙，不能自己爬上去拿。」女孩點頭。公園管理員趕來，請大家在安全位置等候，再把風箏取下。

Toma wanted to laugh at himself for believing the ghost story, but Yuna asked him not to laugh at the girl. “She did not make up the story,” Yuna said. “She only wanted her kite back.” They sat outside the park on a bench while the officer checked the string. The girl told them that she liked to make different kites with her father. This one had a little heart painted on it. Kai said he could make paper wings, though he was not good at flying things in the human world. Riko gave him a quick look. He stopped before saying anything more about dragons.

斗真想笑自己竟相信鬧鬼傳言，優奈卻提醒他不要笑那個女孩。「故事不是她編的，」優奈說，「她只是想拿回風箏。」大家坐在公園外的長椅上，等管理員檢查線。女孩說她喜歡和爸爸做各式各樣的風箏，這一隻還畫了小愛心。海說他會做紙翅膀，只是不擅長讓人類世界的東西飛起來。理子快速看了他一眼，他便及時停住，沒有再提龍的事。

After the kite was safe, Kai asked the girl whether she wanted to fly it again. “Not today,” she said. “I want to take it home.” The choice surprised him. In his world, a broken thing was often fixed at once, and everyone wanted to see it fly. The girl cared more about taking the kite back to her father. They walked with her to the park gate, where her father waited. He gave them each a small bag of popcorn. Toma tasted his and said it was better than beef from the school lunch. Everyone laughed, even Yuna, who usually tried to look serious.

風箏安全拿下後，海問女孩要不要再放一次。「今天不要了，」她說，「我想帶回家。」這個回答讓海意外。龍的世界裡，東西壞了常會立刻修好，大家也都想看它再次飛起。可是女孩更想把風箏帶回去給爸爸。他們陪她走到公園門口，女孩的父親在那裡等。他送每人一小袋爆米花。斗真嚐了一口，說這比學校午餐的牛肉還好吃。大家都笑了，連平常故作嚴肅的優奈也不例外。

The girl asked whether she could try the kite again another day. Her father said yes, but first they needed to check the string so it would not break. “It is not easy to fix every small thing,” he told them, “but we can give it time.” He offered them a taste of the sweet popcorn from his bag. Kai said he liked the taste and was glad they had waited instead of climbing the tree.

女孩問改天能不能再放風箏。父親說可以，但首先得檢查線，免得再次斷掉。「不是每一件小事都容易修好，」他說，「但我們可以花點時間。」他請大家嚐嚐袋裡的甜爆米花。海說自己喜歡那味道，也很高興他們選擇等待，而沒有爬樹。

On their walk back to school, Kai thanked the group. “You came even when the story sounded silly,” he said. “And you asked why before you made a choice.” Yuna looked at him. “Is that something special where you come from?” she asked. Kai hesitated. Then he said, “Yes. At my old school, being first was important. I am learning that a group can be better than one fast person.” Yuna nodded slowly. “That is why I like group work.” Riko was glad they were speaking honestly. Yet she also saw how hard Kai was trying to choose his words. His tail moved once beneath his coat, then became still.

回學校路上，海謝謝大家。「就算傳言聽起來很傻，你們還是來了，」他說，「而且不是先下結論，而是先問為什麼。」優奈看向他：「在你原來的地方，這很特別嗎？」海遲疑了一下，說：「是。以前的學校很重視誰第一名。我正在學習一群人能比一個跑得快的人做得更好。」優奈緩緩點頭：「所以我喜歡小組合作。」理子很高興他們能坦誠交談，但也看見海選字遣詞有多辛苦。他的尾巴在外套下動了一下，又安靜了。

That night the ghost story was already changing online. Someone wrote that a white bird had landed on a car. Another person said a girl had disappeared. Riko asked Yuna whether they should post the truth. Yuna said the girl's father should decide if the picture could be shared. They did not need the picture to say the story was only a kite. The next morning Ms. Aki put a short note on the school board: “The park is safe. Please ask before repeating a rumor.” Kai read it twice. A good answer, he thought, could be small and still help many people.

那晚，鬧鬼故事在網路上已變了樣。有人說白鳥落在車上，有人甚至說女孩消失。理子問優奈要不要公開真相。優奈說照片能否分享，應由女孩的父親決定。他們不需要公開女孩的照片，也能說明那只是一隻風箏。隔天秋老師在公告板貼了簡短訊息：「公園安全。轉述傳言前，請先查證。」海讀了兩遍。他想，一個好的答案可以很簡單，卻仍然能幫到很多人。
