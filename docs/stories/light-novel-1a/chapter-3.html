河邊的地圖

A Map by the River

轉學生的尾巴，藏不住了！ · 第 3 / 25 章 · 1000以下 · 英中對照 · 校訂中

On Wednesday their science class went outside to study water. The teacher led every group along a road to a little bridge. Below it ran a clean river, and beyond the trees was a beach. Kai had never been to a human beach. He thought the sand must be made from old clouds. Riko wanted to laugh, but she saw he was honestly trying to understand. Toma carried a table of notes on a board, and Yuna, the class leader, held the map. A bird called from the far side of the bridge. Kai looked up as if the sound were a question. “We are here to study the river,” Yuna said. “Please follow the line on the road.”

星期三，科學課全班到戶外研究水。老師帶著各組沿路走到一座小橋。橋下是乾淨的河流，樹林另一頭則是海灘。海從沒去過人類的海邊，以為沙子是老雲朵變成的。理子本想笑，卻發現他很認真地努力理解。斗真抱著貼有紀錄表的板子，班長優奈拿著地圖。橋遠處有鳥鳴，海抬起頭，像有人在向他提問。「我們是來研究河流的。」優奈提醒，「請沿著路上的線走。」

Yuna showed them where the river turned into the sea. “About two hundred years ago, people built this bridge,” she explained. “My grandfather was born near that old station.” Kai leaned forward to see the water. His shoe touched a loose stone, and he slipped toward the low edge. Riko caught his sleeve. “Stay on this side!” she called. Kai stopped at once. He could have pulled himself up with his hidden tail, but doing so in front of the class would bring more questions. He took her hand instead. “I am sorry,” he said. “I was thinking about the clouds, not my feet.”

優奈指給大家看河水轉入海的地方。「大約兩百年前，人們在這裡建橋。」她解釋，「我祖父出生在舊車站附近。」海向前傾身看水，鞋子碰到一顆鬆石，差點滑向矮矮的橋邊。理子抓住他的袖子。「待在這一側！」她喊。海立刻停下。他本來能用藏起來的尾巴拉住身體，可在全班面前那樣做只會引出更多問題。於是他握住理子的手。「對不起，」他說，「我只顧著想雲，忘了看腳下。」

After that, the group watched a cow drinking beyond the fence and a bird cleaning its feathers. Toma asked what could happen if the river rose after heavy rain. Yuna pointed to the road and the higher ground. “That is our way out,” she said. Kai studied each sign. At home he could fly over a problem, but here he could not. The new rule mattered to everyone, not just him. He helped Riko carry their water samples to the table. They marked each place on a map without going into the river. Riko noticed that Kai looked more careful than he had in the morning.

之後，小組看見圍籬另一側的牛在喝水，也看見鳥兒清理羽毛。斗真問大雨後河水上漲會怎樣。優奈指向道路與地勢較高的地方。「那是我們離開的路。」她說。海認真看每個標誌。在家鄉，他能飛過難題；但在這裡不能。安全規則是為了所有人，不只是他。海幫理子把採集的水樣搬到桌上。他們把位置標在地圖上，沒有踏進河裡。理子發現，海比早晨小心多了。

At the beach, the class was allowed to stand only behind a rope. No one was to cross it. A little boy from another school dropped his paper bird, and the wind carried it across the sand. The boy wanted to run after it. Kai called to him gently, “Please stop. We can ask your teacher.” He did not jump over the rope, although he wanted to help. The teacher used a long stick from a safe path to pull the paper back. The boy smiled, and his mother thanked them. Kai looked at Riko. “So helping is not always doing the fastest thing.” “That is right,” she said. “Sometimes waiting is the real help.”

到了海邊，老師只允許大家站在繩線後面，不能跨過去。另一所學校的小男孩掉了紙鳥，風把它吹到沙上，他想衝過去撿。海溫柔地叫住他：「先等一下，可以請老師幫忙。」雖然海也想幫忙，他並沒有跳過繩子。老師沿著安全步道，用長棍把紙鳥勾回。男孩笑了，母親也向他們道謝。海看著理子：「所以幫忙不一定要用最快的方法。」理子點頭：「有時候，願意等待才是真正的幫忙。」

On the way back, Yuna checked their notes. She had seen Kai almost fall, but she did not make fun of him. “Next time, tell me if you do not know a place,” she said. “I can show you.” Kai bowed and said thank you. Riko was surprised by how much kindness could fit in a short talk. She thought about her own first day at school and how afraid she had been of looking foolish. When they reached the station, Kai asked to look at the big map once more. The line from the mountain to the sea made sense now. Even a dragon needed someone to show him the way.

回程時，優奈檢查小組筆記。她看見海差點滑倒，卻沒有嘲笑。「下次不認識路就告訴我，」她說，「我可以帶你。」海鞠躬道謝。理子很驚訝，短短幾句話竟能裝下這麼多善意。她想起自己開學第一天，也很怕看起來笨拙。走到車站後，海又看了一次大地圖。山到海的路線終於在他眼裡有了意義。原來連龍都需要有人帶路。

That evening Riko wrote about the day in her own notebook. She recorded that the water was clear near the bridge, that the old station stood on high ground, and that her class had worked well together. She did not write Kai's secret name beside the drawing of the tail. He was not a strange thing for a science report. He was her classmate. On his way home, Kai opened his special book. The first space showed a small light, but it had not become a full mark. The book seemed to be waiting for one more moment when he would listen before acting.

傍晚，理子在自己的筆記本裡寫下今天的見聞：橋邊的水很清，舊車站地勢較高，小組合作得不錯。她沒有在尾巴圖旁寫上海的祕密名字。他不是科學報告裡的奇怪物品，而是她的同學。回家路上，海打開那本特別的書。第一個欄位浮出小小亮光，卻還沒形成完整的記號。那本書似乎還在等，等他下一次願意先傾聽再行動的時刻。
