第二個藍色記號

The Second Blue Mark

轉學生的尾巴，藏不住了！ · 第 10 / 25 章 · 1000以下 · 英中對照 · 校訂中

Wednesday's test was not written on paper. The story room needed a net of thin string above the center table to hold light paper stars. Toma wanted to finish it fast. He hit the ball from the sports room against the wall while waiting, and the ball knocked down a box of pencils. “Sorry!” he called. Yuna looked at the mess. “We will need a simple plan,” she said. “Half the group can sort the cards, and half can check the net with a teacher.” Kai stood between them, unsure of his place. In the sky world, the strongest dragon always took the first job. Here, people asked what each person could do.

星期三的考驗不在考卷上。故事屋需要在中央桌上方架一張細線網，掛上輕紙星星。斗真想趕快完成，等候時拿體育室的球往牆上打，結果撞翻了一盒鉛筆。「抱歉！」他喊。優奈看著混亂現場：「我們需要一個簡單計畫。一半的人整理卡片，另一半請老師一起檢查細線網。」海站在大家中間，不知道自己該做什麼。天空世界裡，最強的龍總是第一個領工作；這裡的人卻會先問每個人能做什麼。

Riko’s little brother came to the room with an apple and a ball before the groups began. He wanted to kick the ball toward the net to see if it was strong. Riko stopped him. “The net is for paper, not for a game,” she explained. Her brother looked disappointed, but he put the ball away and ate his apple. Kai watched them. In his world, a younger dragon might have tried the same thing without asking. He was learning that saying no could protect good work.

分組開始前，理子的弟弟帶著蘋果與球來到教室。他想把球踢向細線網，看看網子結不結實。理子阻止他：「這張網是掛紙的，不是拿來玩球。」弟弟雖然失望，還是收起球，吃起蘋果。海看著兩人。在他的世界裡，年幼的龍可能早已不問就試了。他逐漸明白，說不有時是在保護大家完成的成果。

Riko gave Kai two choices. He could help the group hang the cards, or he could spend time online finding a safe way to tie the strings. “I love drawing small plans,” Kai said, “but I do not know much about your tools.” Toma offered to show him. Kai followed the steps slowly. He drew a line between each hook and marked the right place for the knots. Yuna checked his work. “You are good at this,” she said. Kai smiled. It was the first time a teacher or friend in this world had praised him for something other than being strange or fast.

理子給海兩個選擇：幫忙掛卡片，或上網查找安全綁線的方法。「我很喜歡畫小計畫，」海說，「可是對你們的工具不熟。」斗真主動教他。海慢慢照步驟做，在每個掛鉤之間畫線，標出正確的打結位置。優奈檢查後說：「你很擅長這件事。」海笑了。這是來到這個世界後，第一次有人因為他做得好的事而稱讚他，而不是因為他奇怪或跑得快。

During lunch their teacher asked whether anyone could explain the plan in a clear voice. Kai raised his hand. He spoke about the hooks, the thin strings, and the way a low net would stay away from everyone's head. Then a boy at the back asked whether a blind visitor could enjoy the story room. The class became quiet. Riko had not thought about that. Yuna asked the teacher for advice. The group decided to read every card aloud when visitors wished, and to keep the way to the door clear. “We cannot know everything at first,” Yuna said, “but we can still change the plan.”

午餐時，老師問有誰能清楚說明配置。海舉起手，解釋掛鉤、細線，以及把網架低時該如何避免碰到人的頭。後排一個男孩接著問：「看不見的訪客也能享受故事屋嗎？」全班安靜下來。理子之前沒想到這一點。優奈向老師請教後，大家決定只要訪客願意，就把每張卡片讀出來，也保持通往門口的走道暢通。「我們起初不可能什麼都知道，」優奈說，「但計畫仍然可以改進。」

After school the whole group spent one hour on the room. They had no ice or magic to make the work easy. They had their hands, some tape, a pencil, and each other's eyes. A boy who was good at drawing checked the wall pictures. Toma counted the cards. Yuna watched the door, and Riko helped Kai tie the last knot. When he finished, the net stayed level. No star fell. He did not shout or run, yet his heart felt full. The teacher gave them a cheerful word of praise. It was a little room, but the group had made it work together.

放學後，全組花了一小時整理故事屋。他們沒有冰系魔法，也沒有任何能讓工作立刻變簡單的法術，只有雙手、膠帶、鉛筆，以及彼此的眼睛。擅長繪畫的男孩檢查牆上的圖，斗真清點卡片，優奈守著門邊，理子則幫海打好最後的結。網平穩地留在原處，一顆星也沒有掉。海沒有大喊，也沒有奔跑，心裡卻滿滿的。老師開心稱讚大家。房間雖小，卻因大家合作而完成。

That evening Kai opened the life book and saw a second blue mark appear in the space named Work Together. Under it were the words: “He made a place for other people and let them help him too.” He read the sentence aloud. “I thought teamwork meant I had to help everyone,” he told Riko. “I did not know it also meant letting others help me.” Riko nodded. “Sometimes I forget that part too.” She asked whether he was ready to tell Yuna his secret. Kai looked at the second mark. “Maybe soon,” he said. “I want to ask her when we can speak without other people listening.”

傍晚，海打開生活之書，看見第二個藍色記號出現在「合作」欄位。下方寫著：「他讓別人有參與的位置，也允許他人幫助自己。」他把句子念出來。「我原以為合作就是我得幫所有人，」他告訴理子，「沒想到也包含接受別人的幫忙。」理子點頭：「有時我也會忘記這部分。」她問海是否準備向優奈說明祕密。海看著第二個記號：「也許快了。我想找個沒有旁人的時候先問她。」

On Thursday morning Kai and Riko met Yuna by the school gate. He said, “Can we talk in the music room after class? It is about my old school. You may say no.” Yuna looked from him to Riko. She had already noticed that they were keeping something quiet. “I can come,” she answered. “But do not make me promise anything before I know what it is.” Kai blinked, then gave the biggest smile Riko had ever seen on him. “That is fair,” he said. The blue bracelet warmed on his wrist. For one small second, the tip of his tail moved under his coat. Yuna did not seem to notice, but the next door was about to open.

星期四早上，海和理子在校門口遇見優奈。「放學後能來音樂教室談談嗎？是關於我以前的學校。妳也可以拒絕。」海說。優奈看看他，又看看理子。她早已注意到兩人藏著什麼。「我可以去，」她回答，「但在知道內容前，我不會先答應保密。」海眨了眨眼，接著露出理子見過他最大的笑容。「這很公平。」他說。藍手環在腕上暖了起來，尾巴尖在外套下輕輕動了一瞬。優奈似乎沒注意到，可下一扇門已經快要打開了。
