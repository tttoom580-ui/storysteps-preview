學園祭卡片

The Festival Card

轉學生的尾巴，藏不住了！ · 第 9 / 25 章 · 1000以下 · 英中對照 · 校訂中

The school festival was getting close, and every class had a different plan. Riko's class wanted to open a small story room where visitors could hear tales from students' families. Yuna handed out a card to each group. “Please write one true story and ask the person whether we may tell it,” she said. Kai's grandmother had told him many stories about the sky lake, but he was not sure he could share them. Toma wanted to prepare dishes with rice and banana for visitors. He hoped every dish would be easy to share. The kitchen team had its own rules, and no one could cook without a teacher. Riko said they should visit the room and see how much space was ready.

學園祭越來越近，每個班都在規劃不同活動。理子的班級想開一間小小故事屋，讓訪客聽見學生家人的故事。優奈發給每組一張卡片：「請寫一個真實故事，還要先問當事人是否願意分享。」海的祖母說過許多天空湖的故事，但他不確定能否說給人類聽。斗真想替訪客準備米飯與香蕉小點心，希望每道料理都方便分享。廚房組有自己的規定，沒有老師不能烹調。理子提議大家先去看看教室空間準備得怎樣。

In the empty room they found a large paper moon and a picture of a street at night. Yuna showed where guests could sit. “We need quiet, not a big shout,” she explained. Kai asked if the group could tell a story about a creature that felt different. Toma said that sounded interesting, but he did not want a sad ending. “A story can have joy without everyone getting the same thing,” Riko said. She spoke from the heart. Kai gave a small smile. He was not ready to tell his own story yet, but perhaps he could create a tale that carried a little of what he knew.

空教室裡有一個大紙月亮，還有一幅夜晚街道的圖。優奈指著讓客人坐的位置：「我們需要安靜，不需要大喊。」海問大家能否講一個與別人不同的生物的故事。斗真覺得有趣，但不希望結局太悲傷。「一個故事可以很快樂，即使大家最後得到的東西不同。」理子真心說道。海微微一笑。他還沒準備好講自己的事，但也許能創作一則帶著自己經驗的故事。

Later they visited the music room to test a small reading show. Toma was quite ready to speak, but his voice became too loud in the little room. Yuna asked him to try again. He took her tip and lowered his voice. Kai read a story about a bird that wanted to live near a lake even though its family lived on a mountain. It was not a perfect story, yet it made Riko think about him. When the bird's family said, “O.K., take your time,” the room became very quiet. Even Toma forgot to eat his snack. Everyone knew the bird still had a hard choice to make.

之後，他們到音樂教室測試朗讀活動。斗真已經準備好，卻在小房間裡喊得太大聲。優奈請他重新來一次，他接受建議，放低了聲音。海讀了一個故事：一隻家人住在山上的鳥，想搬到湖邊生活。故事不算完美，卻讓理子想起海。當故事裡的家人說：「好，慢慢決定吧。」整間教室都安靜了。連斗真都忘了吃點心。大家知道那隻鳥仍要做出困難的選擇。

After practice Kai went to the back of the school, where his life book was hidden in a locked bag. He was thinking about the word festival. At home, a festival meant dragons flying in circles above the clouds. Here, people made food, drew pictures, and asked visitors to listen. He was not sure which was better. Riko came outside and found him sitting under a tree. “You do not have to choose one world today,” she said. Kai looked down at the card in his hand. “Are you sure?” he asked. “Yes,” she said. “But you can choose what you will tell us tomorrow.”

練習後，海走到校舍後方。他的生活之書鎖在包裡。他想著「學園祭」這個詞。在家鄉，祭典是龍群在雲端繞圈飛舞；這裡的人卻準備食物、畫圖，並邀請訪客來聆聽。他不知道哪一種比較好。理子在樹下找到他。「今天不用在兩個世界之間做選擇。」她說。海低頭看著手上的卡片：「真的嗎？」「真的，」她答，「但你可以決定明天要告訴我們什麼。」

At dinner Riko's grandmother came to visit. She heard about the story room and offered to tell how she had lived near the sea as a child. She asked that her name not be printed on the wall. “I want the children to remember the story, not my face,” she explained. Riko said she would treat that wish with care. Her grandmother smiled and told her that a good listener sometimes worked harder than a good speaker. Riko thought of Kai and the bird by the lake. She hoped his new friends would be ready to listen when he finally decided to speak.

晚餐時，理子的祖母來作客。聽到故事屋的計畫，她主動說可以分享兒時住在海邊的經歷，但希望不要把自己的名字印在牆上。「我希望孩子記得故事，不是我的臉。」她解釋。理子答應尊重這個心願。祖母笑著說，好聽眾有時比好講者還辛苦。理子想起海與湖邊那隻鳥。她希望等海終於想開口時，他的新朋友已準備好傾聽。

The next day Yuna placed the cards in a neat line on the table. Some were full, some were empty, and one had a drawing with no words. She did not call anyone lazy. “You can still change your mind,” she told the class. Kai gave her the bird story and asked whether she could keep his name off the display. “Of course,” she answered. “The work belongs to you, so the choice is yours.” Kai looked surprised. His old school had often treated every good story as something for the whole group to use. This one simple answer made him want to work with Yuna even more.

隔天，優奈把卡片整齊排在桌上。有的寫滿字，有的還空著，有一張只有圖畫。她沒有責怪任何人懶惰。「你們還能改變主意。」她告訴全班。海把小鳥的故事交給她，請她先不要把作者名字放在展示板上。「當然可以，」優奈回答，「作品是你的，選擇也應該是你的。」海有些驚訝。以前的學校常把好故事當成全體都可以使用的東西。這個簡單的答案，讓他更想和優奈合作。
