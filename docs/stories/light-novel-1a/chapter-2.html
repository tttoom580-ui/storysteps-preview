天空龍的三條規則

The Three Rules of a Sky Dragon

轉學生的尾巴，藏不住了！ · 第 2 / 25 章 · 1000以下 · 英中對照 · 校訂中

用互動版查詞、朗讀與收藏

On Tuesday Kai brought a strange book to school. Riko's cousin Hana was staying with her family for the week, and she had helped pack an extra lunch. Riko placed it beside Kai's bag. “My cousin made too much rice,” she explained. Kai thanked her, then opened his book under the desk. The pages looked empty to Riko. Kai touched a blue stone on its cover, and words rose like little lights. “Please bring no one else yet,” he said. “The book has power, but it is not a toy.” Riko wanted to take a video. She put her phone away instead. A secret should not become someone's online show without asking.

星期二，海帶來一本奇怪的書。理子的表妹花奈這星期住在她家，幫忙多準備了一份午餐。理子把飯盒放在海的書包旁。「我表妹飯煮太多了。」她解釋。海道謝後，在課桌下翻開那本書。理子看見的頁面一片空白；海碰了碰封面的藍石，文字竟像小燈一樣浮出來。「先別帶別人來。」他說，「這本書有力量，可不是玩具。」理子很想錄影，最後還是收起手機。沒問過對方，就不該把祕密變成網路表演。

The first rule was about the blue bracelet on Kai's wrist. It could hide his horns and most of his tail, but strong feelings could make the tail appear. The second rule said that a sky dragon must not use fire in a human town. A small warm light in the hand was allowed only when an adult said the place was safe. The third rule was about time. Kai had come for a school exchange, and he would meet his teacher at the wind mirror on the night of the school festival. He could decide whether to return home. Another visit would need a new family agreement. “They are not trying to keep me here,” Kai said. “They want me to choose carefully.”

第一條規則和海手腕上的藍色手環有關。它能藏起角和大部分尾巴，但情緒太強時，尾巴仍會現形。第二條是天空龍不能在人類城鎮使用火焰；只有成年人確認安全，才可以使用掌心那種小小的暖光。第三條和時間有關。海來這裡參加交換學習，必須在學園祭當晚到風鏡前見老師。他可以決定是否回家，但下次來訪需要重新取得家人同意。「他們不是想困住我。」海說，「他們希望我認真做選擇。」

Kai showed Riko five blank spaces in the book. “Listen, Work Together, Respect, Stay Safe, and Choose,” he read. “I need to fill them with true stories, not marks from a test.” Riko smiled. “Then we can start with science.” Their class had been asked to study how water moved from the mountains to the river. They made a little map with blue paper. Kai said his home had a river in the sky, but the water there moved from one cloud to another. “That sounds famous in your world,” Riko joked. “Not famous,” Kai said. “Just normal.” She liked how the same word could mean something different to them.

海指給理子看書裡五個空白欄位：「傾聽、合作、尊重、安全，以及選擇。」他念道，「我要填進真實經歷，不是考卷分數。」理子笑了。「那就先從科學課開始吧。」班上正在研究山上的水如何流進河裡，他們用藍紙畫了小地圖。海說他的家鄉有一條天空河，水會從一朵雲流向另一朵雲。「那在你們世界一定很有名吧。」理子開玩笑。「沒那麼有名，」海說，「那很普通。」理子喜歡同一件事對兩人竟有如此不同的意義。

At lunch a guy from the class named Toma came over. He was helpful when someone needed a tool, but he asked too many questions. “Why do you always look south from the window?” he asked Kai. “My family lives that way,” Kai answered. It was true enough. Toma smiled and spoke about his uncle's sheep farm beyond the city. Then he noticed a small blue line under Kai's sleeve. Kai pulled the sleeve down so quickly that he bit his lip. Riko added a new question about the weather before Toma could look again. “It is sunny today,” she said. “Will it rain tomorrow?” Toma happily began a very long answer.

午餐時，同班男孩斗真走了過來。他很熱心，誰需要工具都願意幫忙，但問題也特別多。「你為什麼老是看著南邊的窗外？」他問海。「我家在那個方向。」海答道。這至少是真的。斗真笑著說起城外叔叔養羊的農場，接著發現海袖子下有一道藍光。海急忙拉下袖子，還咬到了嘴唇。理子趁斗真再次低頭前插進一個天氣問題：「今天很晴朗，明天會下雨嗎？」斗真立刻開心地講起長篇預報。

After school Riko and Kai sat beneath a tree and reviewed the rules. The book did not ask him to be the strongest dragon in his family. It asked him to write what people did when something was hard. Riko found that idea helpful. “You can add a story even if you make a mistake,” she said. “But I need to know when your tail may show. We must keep you and other students safe.” Kai nodded. He explained that fear made the bracelet feel hot. Laughing could also make it warm. Riko wondered whether the most dangerous thing at school might be Toma's jokes. They both laughed, and Kai's tail knocked over an empty cup.

放學後，理子與海坐在樹下重新確認規則。這本書不是要海成為家族最強的龍，而是要他記下人類遇到困難時如何行動。理子覺得這個想法很有幫助。「就算犯錯也能寫成故事，」她說，「但我要知道你什麼時候可能露出尾巴。我們得保護你，也保護其他同學。」海點頭，說害怕會讓手環發熱，大笑也會。理子懷疑校園裡最危險的東西可能是斗真的笑話。兩人同時笑出聲，海的尾巴也打翻了空紙杯。

When they separated, Kai gave Riko one small paper star. He said it did not have magic. It was only a thank-you gift made by his sister. Riko put it inside her pencil case. Her cousin Hana asked about it over dinner, and Riko answered that a classmate had given it to her. That was true. She felt proud that she could keep a promise without making up a new lie. The book's first blank page remained empty. Kai had learned a few human words and found a friend, but he still needed to show what listening meant when a real choice stood in front of him.

分開時，海送理子一顆小紙星星。他說那沒有魔法，只是妹妹做來送人的謝禮。理子把它放進鉛筆盒。晚餐時花奈問起紙星星，理子回答是同學送的，這是真話。她有點高興：原來不必再編一個謊言，也能守住約定。書裡第一個空白欄位仍然空著。海學了幾個人類詞彙，也交到朋友；但他還需要在真正面臨選擇時，明白「傾聽」是什麼意思。
