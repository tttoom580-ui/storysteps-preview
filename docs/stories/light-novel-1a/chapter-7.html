犯錯的時鐘

The Clock That Made an Error

轉學生的尾巴，藏不住了！ · 第 7 / 25 章 · 1000以下 · 英中對照 · 校訂中

用互動版查詞、朗讀與收藏

On Sunday night Kai heard a strange noise in his bedroom. The little clock above his desk said eight, but the one in the hall said seven. He looked at both and felt afraid. At home he had always believed that a sky clock could never make an error. If this one was wrong, would his wind mirror open at the wrong time too? He called Riko. She was in her yard looking at the stars. “Just check the clock with your aunt,” she said. “Maybe one of them stopped.” Kai did not want to sound like a foolish dragon, yet he knew she was right. He asked his aunt, who found that the desk clock had an old battery.

星期日晚上，海在臥室聽見奇怪的聲音。書桌上的小鐘指著八點，走廊的鐘卻是七點。他看著兩個時鐘，忽然害怕起來。在家鄉，他一直相信天空鐘絕不會出錯。如果這裡的鐘錯了，風鏡會不會也在錯誤的時間開啟？他打給理子。理子正站在院子裡看星星。「先和你阿姨一起確認時鐘吧，」她說，「也許其中一個停了。」海不想顯得笨拙，卻知道她說得對。他去請阿姨檢查，才發現書桌鐘的電池已經老了。

Later Kai fell asleep and had a dream about his home in the sky. The mountains shone under a rainbow, and nature seemed to sing. His teacher, Sumi, stood beside the wind mirror. She said, “A clock tells time, but it cannot tell you whether a choice is kind.” When Kai woke, he could still hear her voice. He wrote the sentence down before breakfast. He had thought being rich in magic would make life easy. Now he was learning that a small human question could be harder than a huge dragon spell. He was not proud of his fear, but he no longer needed to hide it from himself.

之後海睡著了，夢見天空中的家。山巒在彩虹下發亮，整個自然世界像在唱歌。老師澄站在風鏡旁說：「時鐘能告訴你時間，卻不能告訴你一個選擇是否善良。」海醒來時，彷彿仍聽得到老師的聲音。他早餐前就把那句話寫下。他原以為魔力強大，生活就會簡單，現在才發現人類的小問題有時比巨大的龍族法術更難。他不以自己的恐懼為榮，卻也不再需要對自己隱瞞。

As they left the science room, Riko asked Kai what he believed about mistakes. He said he used to believe a wrong answer could close every door at once. “Then let me give you a new idea,” Riko said. “A door can close for the day and open again tomorrow.” Kai began to laugh at his own fear. He was still scared of the coming festival, but now the fear had a little less power over him.

離開科學教室時，理子問海怎麼看待錯誤。海說，以前他總相信，一次答錯就可能讓所有門同時關上。「那我給你一個新想法，」理子說，「門今天關上，明天仍可能再開。」海開始笑自己的恐懼。他仍害怕即將到來的學園祭，但恐懼對他的控制已減弱了一點。

On Monday Riko showed Kai how the school kept time. A teacher checked the main clock every morning. The bell did not run from a dream or a wish; it worked through simple parts that people could test. Kai asked whether a mistake could happen even when everyone was careful. “Of course,” Riko said. “That is why we check again.” They went to the science room, where a small rainbow made by a glass cup moved across the table. Kai watched the colors and laughed. “Your world has so many ways to make light without magic.” Riko laughed too. “And sometimes a rainbow is just a rainbow. That is nice enough.”

星期一，理子帶海認識學校如何校時。每天早上都有老師檢查主鐘。鐘聲不是靠夢或願望運作，而是靠能夠測試的簡單零件。海問即使大家都很小心，是否仍會出錯。「當然，」理子說，「所以才要再檢查。」他們來到科學教室，玻璃杯折出的細小彩虹正移過桌面。海看著色彩笑了。「你們的世界有好多不靠魔法也能發光的方法。」理子也笑：「有時彩虹就只是彩虹，這樣已經很美好了。」

At lunch Toma asked Kai a question about the future. “Do you want to stay in this class?” Kai looked at Riko, then back at Toma. He had not decided. His home was far above the clouds, and the people there loved him. But this class had become important in just a few days. “I do not know yet,” he said. Toma looked surprised by the plain answer. “Then tell us when you know.” Kai felt warm inside. It was such a simple thing. He had expected a demand, or a loud joke, or someone who wanted an answer at once. Instead, Toma gave him time.

午餐時，斗真問海一個關於未來的問題：「你想留在這個班嗎？」海看看理子，再看向斗真。他還沒有決定。家鄉遠在雲上，那裡的人都愛他，可這個班級才幾天就變得重要。「我還不知道。」他說。斗真對這麼直接的回答有些驚訝。「那等你知道再告訴我們吧。」海心裡暖暖的，事情竟可以這麼簡單。他原以為會有人強求、開大玩笑，或逼他當場回答，斗真卻給了他時間。

That afternoon a student asked Riko why she kept looking at Kai's wrist. Riko had to choose between a lie and a kind refusal. “I was checking whether he needed help,” she said. “But that is his business.” The student shrugged and went away. Riko felt proud that she had not made up a story. Kai heard about it later and smiled. “I want to tell people the truth one day,” he said. “I just do not know who should hear it first.” Riko suggested Yuna, who had helped him by the river. Kai said he needed more time. She did not ask again.

下午有同學問理子為什麼老在看海的手腕。理子必須在說謊與有禮貌地拒答之間做選擇。「我只是在確認他需不需要幫忙，」她說，「但那是他的私事。」同學聳肩走了。理子為自己沒有再編故事感到高興。海聽說後笑了。「有一天我想告訴大家真相，」他說，「只是還不知道該先告訴誰。」理子提議先找在河邊幫過他的優奈。海說他需要再想想，她便沒有追問。

Before sleep, Kai placed the new battery beside his life book. He checked the clock, closed the window, and looked out at the quiet sky. The first blue mark was still there, and four spaces remained empty. He had made an error about a clock, but he had not lost the day. He could correct it. He imagined his teacher reading his notes and smiling. Then he wrote a small question under the next space: “Can I work with people who know my secret and still feel like myself?” He did not have an answer yet. For once, that did not make him afraid.

睡前，海把新電池放在生活之書旁，確認時鐘，關上窗戶，望著安靜的天空。第一個藍色記號仍在，另外四欄還空著。他曾看錯時鐘，卻沒有失去整天；錯誤可以改正。他想像老師讀到筆記時微笑的樣子。接著他在下一欄寫下一個小問題：「如果有人知道我的祕密，我還能自在地和他合作嗎？」他暫時沒有答案。這一次，他卻不再害怕。
