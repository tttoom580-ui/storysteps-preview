七號課桌下的尾巴

The Tail Under Desk Seven

轉學生的尾巴，藏不住了！ · 第 1 / 25 章 · 1000以下 · 英中對照 · 校訂中

At seven a.m., Riko Tanaka was already early for school. She had eaten breakfast on the couch because her little brother was doing exercise in the kitchen. He had left a heavy book on her lunch bag, and a slice of cake had fallen out of its box. Riko took the cake back, added some fruit, and ran into the morning sun. It was a new school term at Minato Middle School. The bell would ring soon, and her biggest worry was a science test. She did not know that a far bigger surprise was sitting under desk seven, with blue eyes, silver hair, and a tail that did not belong to a human boy.

上午七點，田中理子已經提早準備上學。弟弟在廚房做運動，她只好坐在沙發上吃早餐。他把一本厚重的書壓在她的午餐袋上，害一小片蛋糕掉出盒子。理子把蛋糕放回去，添上水果，就迎著晨光出門。這是港南國中的新學期。鐘聲快響了，她最大的煩惱原本只是科學考試。她還不知道，七號課桌下坐著更驚人的祕密：一個有藍眼睛、銀色頭髮，以及不屬於人類男孩的尾巴的學生。

The new boy was Kai. Their teacher said he came from a foreign school and asked Riko to teach him where everything was. Kai held a notebook with both hands. When he turned, something blue moved under his chair. Riko first thought it was the cover of a long bag. Then she saw the point at the end. It moved again. Kai saw her eyes move, and his face went white. “That is a school hobby,” he said quickly. “A hobby?” Riko asked. “You know, a monkey costume!” He tried to smile. One sharp tooth showed between his lips. Riko had seen many strange costumes, but none had ever moved on its own.

新同學叫海。他的導師說他來自外地學校，請理子帶他熟悉校園。海雙手抱著筆記本，轉身時椅子下有個藍色東西動了一下。理子起初以為那是長袋子的外罩，隨後卻看見末端有個尖尖的尾梢。它又動了。海發現她的視線，臉色立刻變白。「那是學校興趣活動。」他急忙說。「興趣？」理子反問。「就是……猴子裝！」他硬擠出笑容，嘴唇間還露出一顆尖牙。理子見過各種奇怪服裝，卻沒看過會自己動的那種。

At lunch, Riko found Kai alone by the window. He was staring at a salad as if it might ask him a question. “You do not have to eat that,” she said. “I will show you the lunch line.” Kai looked up. “Shall we go together?” he asked in a voice so serious that she almost laughed. They walked past a wall with pictures for a writing class. One picture showed a dragon beside a mountain. Kai stopped in front of it. Riko noticed the same blue mark on his notebook and on the dragon's wing. He reached for the paper, then pulled back his hand. Something about the drawing had made him sad.

午餐時，理子在窗邊找到獨坐的海。他盯著一碗沙拉，像怕它開口問問題。「不喜歡就不用吃那個。」理子說，「我帶你去看午餐隊伍。」海抬頭，用認真到近乎好笑的語氣問：「我們一起去，好嗎？」他們經過寫作課的作品牆，其中有張畫著山與龍的圖。海在畫前停住。理子發現那隻龍的翅膀與海筆記本上的記號相同。他伸手想摸紙，最後又收回。那幅畫似乎讓他難過了。

After class Riko asked him to write one true sentence in the notebook. “You may say no,” she added. Kai studied her face. At last he wrote, “I am not from this town.” Riko waited. He turned the page and drew a small mountain in the sky. “My home is above the clouds,” he whispered. The blue tail came out from under his coat. Riko did not run or shout. She placed her own notebook over the gap by the door so no one looking in could see. “If you need a place to talk, the old music room is empty after school,” she said. “But you must tell me what is going on.”

放學後，理子請他在筆記本上寫下一句真話。「你也可以拒絕。」她補充。海看著她，最後寫下：「我不是這座鎮的人。」理子等著。他翻頁畫出天空裡的一座小山。「我的家在雲上。」他小聲說。藍色尾巴從外套下露出來。理子沒有尖叫，也沒有逃走，而是把自己的筆記本放在門邊的縫隙上，擋住走廊的視線。「如果你需要說話的地方，放學後舊音樂教室是空的。」她說，「但你得告訴我到底怎麼回事。」

Kai followed her there. The room smelled of wood and old music books. He pointed at the bell above the door and explained that its sound made him feel safe. His grandmother had a bell just like it. “I am a young sky dragon,” he finally said. “I came here to learn how people live. I must learn without making anyone afraid.” Riko took a slow breath. She did not know whether to believe him, but she could see the tail. He was shaking hard enough to make the chair move. “First,” she said, “sit down. Second, we do not tell the whole school today. Third, no more monkey stories.”

海跟她走進舊音樂教室，裡面有木頭與舊樂譜的氣味。他指向門上的鈴鐺，說那聲音讓他安心，因為祖母也有一個一模一樣的。「我是一隻年幼的天空龍。」他終於承認，「我來學人類怎麼生活，而且不能嚇到任何人。」理子慢慢吸氣。她還不知道自己能不能相信，但尾巴確實就在眼前。海抖得連椅子都在動。「第一，先坐下。」她說，「第二，今天不告訴全校。第三，不准再講猴子裝的故事。」

Riko walked home with more questions than she could count. A dragon in her class sounded like the start of a book, not a normal Monday. Yet Kai had asked for help instead of trying to frighten her. She remembered how carefully he had held his notebook and how he had tried to hide his fear. At dinner, her brother asked why she was so quiet. “I met someone new,” Riko answered. She wanted to write everything down, but she had promised not to tell his secret. She opened a fresh page and gave it a safer title: “Things I Need to Understand.” Under it she wrote a single question: “Can a dragon become my friend?”

回家路上，理子滿腦子都是數不清的疑問。班上出現一隻龍，聽起來像小說的第一章，怎麼看都不像普通星期一。但海沒有嚇人，而是向她求助。她想起他小心抱著筆記本，以及努力藏住恐懼的樣子。晚餐時弟弟問她怎麼那麼安靜。「今天認識了一個新同學。」理子回答。她很想把事情全寫下來，卻已答應保密。於是她翻開新的一頁，寫下一個比較安全的標題：「我還需要弄懂的事。」下方只有一句：「龍也能成為我的朋友嗎？」
