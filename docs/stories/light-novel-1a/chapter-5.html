不必獲勝的賽跑

A Race No One Had to Win

轉學生的尾巴，藏不住了！ · 第 5 / 25 章 · 1000以下 · 英中對照 · 校訂中

用互動版查詞、朗讀與收藏

Friday was the day of the class race. Although the sky was gray, the track was dry. Ms. Aki, the sports teacher, had asked each group to choose a safe height for the small jump and to keep water nearby. Toma wanted to win. Kai wanted to understand why anyone liked running in a circle. A frog sat by the grass, and a hen from the school farm made a loud sound. “Maybe the animals want to race too,” Riko said. Kai laughed so hard that the end of his tail slipped out. He put his hands on his neck and stopped laughing. No one else had seen it yet.

星期五是班級賽跑日。雖然天空灰濛濛，跑道卻很乾。體育老師秋老師請每組自行選擇安全的跨越高度，也記得準備飲水。斗真想贏，海卻不懂人類為什麼喜歡繞圈跑。草邊坐著一隻青蛙，校園小農場的母雞也大聲叫了起來。「也許動物也想參賽。」理子說。海笑得尾巴尖都露出來，急忙伸手摸脖子，努力停住。幸好還沒有人看見。

During the first race, Toma fell near the turn. It was not a terrible fall, but his shoe came off. Kai was in the lead. If he kept going, he could win easily. He heard Toma call for help, and he stopped. A student shouted that a winner should never turn back. Kai did not move. He pointed to the teacher, who came at once. “At least ask if he is safe,” Kai said. Toma was more angry about losing than about his knee. “Go on!” he told Kai. But the race was already over for their group. Kai took a long minute to breathe and wondered if he had done something wrong.

第一場賽跑時，斗真在轉彎處跌倒，並不嚴重，但鞋子掉了。海原本領先，只要繼續跑就能輕鬆獲勝。聽見斗真求助，他停住腳步。有人大喊，領先的人不該回頭。海沒有動，而是指向老師，老師立刻趕來。「至少先確認他安全吧。」海說。斗真與其說膝蓋痛，不如說更生氣自己輸了。「快跑啦！」他叫海。但這一組的比賽已結束。海花了好長一分鐘才調整呼吸，懷疑自己是否做錯。

Ms. Aki checked Toma's knee and gave him a cold cloth. “You did not win the race,” she told Kai, “but you made a safe choice.” Toma looked away. Later he came back carrying a small bag of beans from the farm. “I am sorry I shouted,” he said. “I thought the point of running was only to be first.” Kai smiled. “In my home, the fastest dragon always wins a race. I thought I had to do that here too.” They made a deal: next week Toma would teach Kai how to run, and Kai would teach him how to stay calm after a mistake.

秋老師檢查斗真的膝蓋，拿冷布給他。「你沒有贏得比賽，」她對海說，「但你做了安全的選擇。」斗真別開視線。稍後，他帶著校園農場的一小袋豆子回來。「對不起，我剛剛大吼。」他說，「我以為跑步唯一的目的就是拿第一。」海笑了。「在我的家鄉，最快的龍總會贏。我以為這裡也一樣。」兩人約好，下週斗真教海跑步，海則教斗真犯錯後如何冷靜。

After school they helped the farm club dig a shallow line in the soil. Snow never came to this town, but the students still learned how cold weather could hurt plants. Kai was told not to put his hand into the ground until the teacher checked the tool. He followed the rule. The farm leader showed them where to put each bean. Riko said the work would be done in ten minutes if everyone helped. Kai felt happy doing a little job that did not need magic. He could not fly over the garden or move the soil with fire, but his hands were good enough.

放學後，他們幫農場社團在土裡挖一條淺溝。這座城鎮從不下雪，但學生仍會學習寒冷天氣如何傷害植物。老師檢查工具前，海被要求先別把手伸進土裡，他照做了。農場小組長示範每顆豆子該放哪裡。理子說大家一起做，十分鐘就能完成。海很高興自己能做一件不靠魔法的小事。他不必飛越田地，也不必用火移動土壤，光靠雙手就夠了。

That evening Kai opened his life book in the old music room. The first space, Listen, became a bright blue mark. A sentence appeared beneath it: “He heard a friend in trouble and chose to stop.” Riko read it twice. The words did not say he had won anything. They did not call him strong or weak. They simply told the true story. Kai looked very quiet. “I think my teacher wanted this,” he said. “She did not want me to be the best dragon. She wanted me to see other people.” Riko held out her hand. Kai touched it with two fingers, a small promise between friends.

那晚，海在舊音樂教室翻開生活之書。第一個「傾聽」欄位變成明亮的藍色記號，下方出現一句話：「他聽見朋友需要幫忙，因此選擇停下。」理子讀了兩遍。文字沒有說他贏了什麼，也沒有稱讚他多強或多弱，只記下真實的故事。海安靜了很久。「我想老師要的就是這個，」他說，「不是要我當最厲害的龍，而是要我看見別人。」理子伸出手。海用兩根手指輕碰她，像朋友之間小小的約定。

When Riko got home, she told her brother the race had been a little strange. He asked who won. “That is not the most interesting part,” she answered. She told him about a boy who stopped to help another boy, but did not mention the blue tail. Her brother listened with his mouth open. “Then I hope I have a friend like that in my class,” he said. Riko looked out at the evening sky. She had been worried that her new friend might be too different to understand. Now she worried about something else: whether she could be as brave as Kai when the next hard choice came.

回家後，理子告訴弟弟今天的賽跑有點奇怪。弟弟問誰贏了。「那不是最有意思的部分。」她說。她講了一個男孩停下來幫助另一個男孩的故事，卻沒有提藍色尾巴。弟弟張著嘴聽完。「那我也希望班上有這樣的朋友。」他說。理子望向傍晚天空。她原本擔心新朋友與自己太不同，無法互相理解。現在她開始擔心另一件事：下次遇到困難的選擇時，自己能不能像海一樣勇敢。
