風找到他的那一天

The Day the Wind Found Him

轉學生的尾巴，藏不住了！ · 第 4 / 25 章 · 1000以下 · 英中對照 · 校訂中

Thursday morning was windy enough to blow a hat across the school yard. A tall boy ran after it, and a butterfly rose from a tomato plant near the wall. Riko was helping the school service club set up a square table when Kai came running from the gate. His blue bracelet was shaking. He had heard a worker at the factory across town speak about dragons on the radio. “He said they are dangerous,” Kai whispered. “Maybe I should leave before anyone knows.” Riko turned off the radio at their table. “That was one man's story,” she said. “It is not a fact about you.”

星期四早上，風大得能把帽子吹過操場。一個高個子男孩追著帽子跑，牆邊番茄株上還飛起一隻蝴蝶。理子正幫學校服務社擺放方桌，海卻從校門急忙跑來，藍色手環一直發抖。他聽見城另一頭工廠的員工在廣播裡談到龍。「那個人說龍很危險。」海低聲說，「也許大家知道之前，我就該離開。」理子關掉桌上的收音機。「那只是一個人的故事，」她說，「不是關於你的事實。」

The club was making a cheap wind cover for the school notice board. It would keep rain off the paper and protect the children's drawings. The teacher asked them to use clean cloth, not plastic that could fly into the road. Kai offered to help. While he held one side, a large cloud hid the sun, and a hard gust made the cloth shake. His tail appeared beneath his uniform. A younger student saw it move and dropped a box. “What was that?” she asked. Riko stepped beside Kai, but did not push the child away. “He has a costume piece for the school play,” she said. Kai opened his mouth, then closed it.

社團正在替學校公告板做便宜的防風罩，好讓紙張不被雨打濕，也保護孩子的畫。老師請他們用乾淨布料，不要用會飛進道路的塑膠。海主動幫忙。當他抓著布的一端，大片雲遮住太陽，一陣強風把布吹得猛烈晃動，他的尾巴從制服下露出來。低年級學生看見它動，嚇得掉了箱子。「那是什麼？」她問。理子站到海身旁，沒有推開小孩。「那是他準備學校話劇的服裝配件。」她說。海張口想說什麼，最後沒有出聲。

The child asked to touch the tail. “Please do not,” Kai said quickly. It was the first clear answer he had given all day. “It is part of my clothes, and I do not want anyone to pull it.” The girl nodded. She was not angry. She only wanted to know. Riko felt sorry that she had made up a story for him without asking first. “I should not have spoken over you,” she said when the child left. Kai looked at her in surprise. “You wanted to help.” “Yes, but help without asking can still feel wrong.” He thought about that while they tied the last piece of cloth.

小女孩問能不能摸尾巴。「請不要。」海立刻回答。這是他今天第一次清楚表達自己的意思。「那是我衣服的一部分，我不想讓人拉它。」女孩點點頭，沒有生氣，只是好奇。理子卻覺得抱歉，因為自己沒先問過他，就替他編了理由。小女孩走後，她說：「我不該搶著幫你回答。」「你明明是想幫忙。」海很驚訝。「沒錯，可是沒先問就幫忙，也可能讓人不舒服。」他一邊想，一邊和她綁好最後一段布。

At lunch the class talked about the wind. A teacher named A young student asked, “Sir, can we take the tools away?” Mr. Sato answered, “Yes, but do not drop them. Without care, even a light board can hurt someone.” He showed how the new cover worked and thanked the students. The girl who had seen the tail thanked Kai too. “You kept me from standing under the board,” she said. He smiled a little. The town outside was full of workers, shops, and noisy streets. He could not learn all of it in one day. Yet he could learn that a person might ask a question without trying to be cruel. That thought made his bracelet stop shaking.

午餐時，全班都在談那場大風。一名學生問：「老師，我們可以收走工具嗎？」佐藤老師回答：「可以，但別讓工具掉落。不小心的話，連輕薄的板子也會傷人。」他示範防風罩的效果，謝謝參與的學生。看到尾巴的小女孩也向海道謝：「你沒有讓我站在板子下面。」海微微笑了。校外的城鎮有工人、商店與吵雜街道，他不可能一天就學完人類世界的一切。但他至少懂得，有人提問不一定是惡意。這個念頭讓手環不再顫動。

On her way home, Riko bought a tomato and some bread for dinner. She saw a small butterfly settle on the shop window. She thought about what she had said to the girl. A lie could be easy, but it could also cover another person's chance to speak for himself. Her mother asked why she was making such a serious face. Riko said she had learned an important lesson about being a friend. Her mother smiled. “Then that was a healthy school day.” Riko was not sure it had been healthy for her heart, which had nearly jumped out of her chest at the sight of the blue tail.

回家路上，理子買了番茄與麵包當晚餐，看見一隻小蝴蝶停在店窗上。她想著自己對小女孩說的話。謊言很容易出口，卻可能把別人替自己說話的機會蓋住。母親問她怎麼一臉嚴肅。理子說今天學到了當朋友的重要一課。媽媽笑著說：「那就是健康又充實的一天。」理子卻不確定自己的心臟是否健康，因為看到藍色尾巴時，她差點嚇得心都跳出來了。

That night Kai read a note from his teacher through the wind mirror in his room. The note said that the human school was not a place to test how well he could hide. It was a place to learn how to speak and listen. “Do not lose yourself just to pass,” it said. Kai looked at the old picture of his family beside his bed. They were all tall dragons with bright wings. He was the smallest and most afraid. But the girl at school had said that he could refuse to be touched, and she had listened. He slept with the bracelet on his wrist and a little more hope in his chest.

那晚，海透過房間裡的風鏡讀到老師的訊息。老師寫道，人類學校不是用來考驗他能把自己藏得多好，而是讓他學會說話與傾聽。「不要為了過關，連自己都丟掉。」海看向床邊的家族合照。家人個個是翅膀明亮的高大龍族，只有他最小，也最害怕。但學校那個女孩告訴他，他可以拒絕被觸碰，而她也真的聽了。海戴著手環睡下，胸口多了一點希望。
