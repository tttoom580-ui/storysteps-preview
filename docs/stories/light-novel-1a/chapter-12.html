山丘上的風鏡

The Wind Mirror on the Hill

轉學生的尾巴，藏不住了！ · 第 12 / 25 章 · 1000以下 · 英中對照 · 校訂中

Yuna wanted to understand the wind mirror before the festival night. She asked Kai whether they could see it together, but she did not ask him to use any power. “The mirror belongs to my family,” Kai said. “I can show you the place, but it is not mine to give away.” They rode bicycles up the hill in the early spring air, with Riko walking beside them because her bike was at the shop. The wind moved through the trees. At the top stood a quiet stone gate. Behind it was a round piece of glass that looked like water. Riko felt her heart beat faster. She had expected something bright and huge, not a little mirror beneath a tree.

優奈想在學園祭之夜前弄懂風鏡，便問海能不能一起去看看，但沒有要求他展示力量。「風鏡屬於我的家族，」海說，「我可以帶妳們到那裡，但不能把它隨便送人。」三人沿山坡而上。優奈與海騎腳踏車，理子的車正在修理，只好步行。早春的風穿過樹木。山頂有道安靜的石門，後方是一面像水一樣的圓鏡。理子的心跳快起來。她以為那裡會有巨大明亮的奇觀，沒想到只是一棵樹下的小鏡子。

Kai set his bag on the ground and bowed to the mirror. It did not open. “It will show the way to my planet only on the final night,” he said. Yuna stared at its surface. “So this is the second place you call home.” “Not exactly,” Kai answered. “My home is beyond it. This is only the door.” Riko wanted to pick up a small white stone near the gate, but Kai asked her to leave it. “That one is mine,” he said with a quick smile. “My sister gave it to me.” Riko put her hands in her pockets. She liked that he could now say what belonged to him without fear.

海把包放在地上，向鏡子行禮。鏡面沒有開啟。「只有最後那天晚上，它才會顯示通往我們星球的路。」他說。優奈凝望鏡面：「這就是你說的第二個家嗎？」「也不完全是，」海回答，「家在門的另一邊，這裡只是入口。」理子想撿石門旁一顆白色小石子，海卻請她留下。「那是我的。」他笑著說，「妹妹送我的。」理子把手放回口袋。她喜歡海現在能自在說出什麼東西屬於自己。

Yuna asked how a dragon chose where to live. Kai said some dragons stayed in the sky all their lives, while some traveled to other worlds. Their language had many words for wind but only one for a long goodbye. “I wish our world had another word for it,” he said. Riko could hear that his voice was sad. She did not try to fix the feeling. Instead, she said, “You can miss your family and still like our school.” Kai looked up. He had thought he must choose one feeling and throw the other away. “Can both be true?” he asked. “Of course,” Yuna said. “People feel two things at the same time all the time.”

優奈問龍通常怎麼決定住在哪裡。海說有些龍一輩子待在天空，有些會到其他世界旅行。他們的語言裡有許多描述風的詞，表示漫長道別的詞卻只有一個。「真希望我們的世界還有別的說法。」他說。理子聽得出他的難過，沒有急著消除那種心情，只說：「你可以想家，也可以喜歡我們的學校。」海抬起頭。他原以為必須選擇一種感受，丟掉另一種。「兩個都可以是真的嗎？」「當然，」優奈說，「人本來就可能同時有兩種心情。」

Yuna wore blue jeans for the hill walk, and she carried a scarf in case the wind grew cold. Kai asked what answer he should give his aunt when she asked about his future. “I cannot choose for you,” Yuna said. “It is your own life.” He felt his body relax a little. The wind touched his face, but he no longer felt that it was pushing him in one direction. For the first time, he could imagine thinking about a choice without needing to make it at once.

優奈穿著藍色牛仔褲登山，還帶了圍巾，以防風轉冷。海問阿姨問起未來時，自己該給什麼答案。「我不能替你選，」優奈說，「那是你自己的人生。」海感覺身體放鬆了一些。風拂過臉頰，他不再覺得風一定要把自己推往某個方向。第一次，他能想像自己慢慢思考選擇，而不必立刻決定。

At lunch on the hill, they shared rice and a little salt from Riko's bag. Yuna said her family would ask many questions if she came home too late. Kai promised they would return soon. He showed them a drawing of his sister, who was learning to make a bow from soft grass. She was younger than him and loved to run in the wind. “I want her to know that human children are not all the same,” he said. “Some are loud, some are quiet, and some ask before they touch.” Yuna smiled. “Then you may tell her about us, if you wish.” Kai looked at her with clear thanks.

山丘上午餐時，大家分著理子帶來的飯與一點鹽。優奈說如果太晚回家，家人會問很多問題，海答應很快下山。他給她們看妹妹的畫像。妹妹正在學用柔軟的草做蝴蝶結，比海年幼，也喜歡迎風奔跑。「我想讓她知道，人類小孩不全都一樣，」海說，「有人吵，有人安靜，也有人會先問過再碰。」優奈笑了：「你願意的話，就告訴她我們的事吧。」海感激地望著她。

On the way down, Riko said she thought it was possible for someone to have more than one home. Yuna agreed, but reminded them that a new visit needed a family agreement. Kai knew that rule already. What was new was hearing his friends take it seriously without making it a trap. They returned to town before the second school bell of the afternoon. Kai held his small white stone once more, then put it in his bag. He wished he could show his sister everything he had learned. Soon he might get the chance, but he would not make that choice because of fear alone.

下山時，理子說她覺得一個人可以擁有不只一個家。優奈同意，卻也提醒下次來訪仍需要家人同意。海早就知道這條規則，但讓他感到新鮮的是，朋友們很認真看待它，卻沒有把規則當成威脅。他們在下午第二次鐘響前回到城裡。海又握了握白色小石頭，放回包中。他真希望把學到的一切分享給妹妹。也許很快有機會，但他不想只因恐懼就做出選擇。

Before bed Kai opened his life book and wrote about the gate. The third space still waited, and he did not try to make it glow with a spell. He wrote a page of questions instead. Could a person respect a rule and still ask if it was fair? Could someone love two places? Could a good friend let him leave? The answers were not written on the mirror or in his teacher's notes. For now, Kai closed the book gently. He was learning that a question was not a weak thing. Sometimes it was the first part of a brave choice.

睡前，海翻開生活之書，寫下山門的事。第三個欄位仍空著，他沒有用法術強迫它發亮，而是寫滿問題：一個人能尊重規則，同時問它是否公平嗎？能同時愛兩個地方嗎？好朋友能允許他離開嗎？答案沒有寫在鏡子或老師的筆記上。海輕輕闔上書。他漸漸明白，提問不是軟弱，有時反而是勇敢選擇的第一步。
