小船與獅子雲

A Boat and a Lion Cloud

轉學生的尾巴，藏不住了！ · 第 6 / 25 章 · 1000以下 · 英中對照 · 校訂中

On Saturday Riko visited her uncle's house by the lake. Kai came with her, carrying a box of snacks and a paper map. They had agreed to meet before noon, but Kai arrived at nine with an apology. “I was afraid the boat would leave without me,” he said. Riko's uncle laughed. “We will not drive to the dock until everyone is ready.” He had a small coffee shop near the water and knew how to welcome guests. The house stood at the top of a little hill. From there Kai could see a white cloud in the shape of a lion. He stared at it so long that Riko had to call his name twice.

星期六，理子去探望住在湖邊的舅舅，海也跟著來，抱著點心盒與紙地圖。他們原本約中午前碰面，海卻九點就帶著歉意出現。「我怕船不等我就開走了。」他說。舅舅笑道：「大家準備好之前，我才不會開車去碼頭。」舅舅在湖旁經營小咖啡店，很懂得招待客人。房子位在小山丘頂上。從那裡望出去，海看見一朵像獅子的白雲，盯得出神，直到理子喊了兩次他的名字。

The boat was small, and every person had to wear a life jacket. Kai liked the blue color but did not like the tight shape. “It looks stupid on me,” he muttered. Riko's uncle heard him. “It may look funny, but it is here to keep you safe.” Kai thought of the river bridge and put it on. Riko checked the sign near the dock. It said that swimming was not allowed except in the marked place farther down the lake. A terrible accident had happened there years before. The sign made Kai serious. Being able to fly at home did not give him the right to ignore a rule here.

小船不大，每個人都得穿救生衣。海喜歡藍色，卻討厭束緊身體的形狀。「穿起來好蠢。」他嘟囔。舅舅聽見了：「可能有點好笑，但它是為了保護你。」海想起河邊的橋，還是乖乖穿上。理子讀了碼頭旁的標誌：只有湖的下游指定區域可以游泳。多年前這裡發生過嚴重事故。這行字讓海神色嚴肅。即使在家鄉會飛，也不代表他能在這裡忽略規則。

When they finally sat in the boat, Riko pointed at the waves. Kai saw that each wave made a different line. He asked whether the water had a secret sign for the sky. Riko's uncle said the wind made those lines. Then he showed them how to watch the top of the trees to see where the wind was coming from. They did not try to touch the water. Kai opened his notebook and drew the lake, the shore, and the lion cloud. “A good map is not only pretty,” Riko said. “It helps us see where we really are.” Kai gave a small nod and added the safe path back to the dock.

終於上船後，理子指著波浪。海發現每一道浪都畫出不同的線，問水是不是在對天空發祕密訊號。舅舅解釋，那些線是風吹出來的，還教他們看樹梢來判斷風向。兩人沒有伸手去碰水。海打開筆記本，畫下湖、岸邊和獅子雲。「好的地圖不只是漂亮，」理子說，「還要能告訴我們自己真正在哪裡。」海點點頭，補畫了一條回碼頭的安全路線。

At the shop, a little girl asked Kai whether he could draw a dragon. He froze. Riko watched him carefully. He could see the shape of a dragon clearly in his mind, but he might not want to show what he knew. “You can say no,” Riko reminded him. Kai took a breath. “I can draw a cloud lion instead,” he told the girl. She agreed happily. He made a picture and gave it to her for free. Later he said he was happy that the girl had not asked a second time. “I thought humans always wanted what they asked for,” he said. “Sometimes,” Riko answered, “we just want to share a little time.”

回到店裡，一個小女孩問海能不能畫一條龍。他僵住了。理子仔細看著他。海腦中一定很清楚龍的形狀，卻未必願意畫出來。「你可以拒絕。」理子提醒。海深吸一口氣，對女孩說：「我可以畫一隻雲獅子。」小女孩開心答應。他畫好後免費送給她。稍後海說，他很高興女孩沒有再要求一次。「我以為人類一定要得到自己開口要的東西。」他說。「不一定，」理子回答，「有時我們只是想一起度過一點時間。」

Before dinner, Riko's uncle showed them an old wooden shape above the door. It was a bird with its wings open. He had made it with his father when he was young. “Not everything from the past needs to become a secret,” he said. “Some old things are better when people can ask about them.” Kai looked at the bird and thought of his family. He would see them again after the festival if he chose to go home. For the first time, that choice felt real instead of far away. At bedtime he wrote one line: “Today I learned that a guest may bring a gift, but a host must also make room for a choice.”

晚餐前，舅舅帶他們看門上方的木雕，那是一隻張開翅膀的鳥，是他年輕時和父親一起做的。「不是所有過去的東西都得成為祕密，」舅舅說，「有些舊物正適合讓大家提問。」海看著木鳥，想起家人。學園祭後，如果他選擇回去，就會再見到他們。第一次，那個決定不再是遙遠的問題。睡前他寫下一句：「今天我學到，客人可以帶禮物，但主人也要留出讓人選擇的空間。」

On Sunday morning Riko and Kai sat on the hill behind the coffee shop. They watched the weekend boats arrive at the dock and move across the quiet lake. Kai said he had once felt that visiting the human world was a test he must pass quickly. Now he was beginning to see it as a place where he could ask questions without being laughed at. Riko said she wanted to visit the lake again, even without a special reason. Her uncle waved from below. Kai waved back, with both hands held high so the blue bracelet was easy to see and the hidden tail stayed safely inside his coat.

星期日早上，理子和海坐在咖啡店後的小山坡上，看著週末船隻一艘艘抵達碼頭，再穿過平靜湖面。海說，他原本以為來到人類世界只是必須盡快通過的測驗；現在他開始明白，這裡也能讓他自在提問，而不會被嘲笑。理子說，即使沒有特別理由，她也想再來湖邊。舅舅從下方揮手，海也高高舉起雙手回應。這樣藍手環看得見，藏著的尾巴也能安全待在外套裡。
