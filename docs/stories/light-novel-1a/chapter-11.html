音樂教室裡的藍色祕密

The Blue Secret in the Music Room

轉學生的尾巴，藏不住了！ · 第 11 / 25 章 · 1000以下 · 英中對照 · 校訂中

用互動版查詞、朗讀與收藏

After class Yuna came to the music room alone. The weather had turned cold, and she wore dark pants under her uniform. A small blue fan stood on the piano, but no one had turned it on. Kai looked at Riko, then at Yuna. “I need to tell you something about my past,” he began. “You can leave if you do not want to hear it.” Yuna sat under the window and folded her hands. “I came because you asked,” she said. Kai took off the blue bracelet. Two short horns appeared above his hair, and his tail moved slowly behind the chair. Yuna did not scream. She stared at him for a long time and said, “Oh.”

放學後，優奈獨自來到音樂教室。天氣轉冷，她在制服裙下穿了深色長褲。鋼琴上有一台小藍電扇，卻沒有人開。海看看理子，又看向優奈。「我想告訴妳一些關於過去的事。」他開口，「如果不想聽，妳可以離開。」優奈在窗下坐好，雙手交握。「我是因為你邀請才來的。」她說。海取下藍手環，銀髮上方立刻出現兩支小角，尾巴也在椅子後緩緩移動。優奈沒有尖叫，盯著他許久，只說了一聲：「喔。」

“You are a dragon,” Yuna said at last. “Yes,” Kai answered. “And I am also the same boy you worked with yesterday.” Yuna nodded slowly. “I know that.” Riko let out the breath she had been holding. Kai explained the three rules and showed them the life book, but he kept one hand on its cover. Yuna asked whether a dragon could be hurt by human tools. “Probably,” Kai said. “I have a body, not a stone coat.” She asked whether he needed the school nurse to know. Kai thought carefully. “Maybe later, if I need help and can speak to her safely.” Yuna wrote the question down instead of making the choice for him.

「你是龍。」優奈終於說。「是，」海回答，「也是昨天和妳一起工作的同一個男孩。」優奈緩緩點頭：「我知道。」理子終於吐出一直憋著的氣。海解釋三條規則，還展示生活之書，卻仍把一隻手留在封面上。優奈問龍會不會被人類工具弄傷。「大概會吧，」海說，「我有身體，又不是石頭做的。」她問需不需要讓保健室護士知道。海想了想：「也許以後需要幫忙、又能安全說明時吧。」優奈把問題記下，沒有替他做決定。

Yuna took a small dictionary from her bag. She had wanted to find one perfect sentence about secrets, but no word seemed big enough. “When someone tells me something important, I do not tell other people without asking,” she said. “I can agree to that. But if there is a real danger, I need to call a teacher.” Kai nodded. “That is fair.” Outside, a car went past the school gate, and the bell rang for cleaning time. No magic came from the sound. Yet Kai felt something change. He had shown his true face to another human, and she had answered with a clear rule instead of fear.

優奈從包裡拿出一本小字典。她本來想找一句最完整的話來描述祕密，卻覺得沒有任何字足夠。「有人告訴我重要的事，我不會不問就轉告別人。」她說，「這點我可以答應。但如果真的有危險，我必須打電話找老師。」海點頭：「很公平。」窗外有車經過校門，打掃鐘響起。那聲音沒有魔法，海心裡卻有了變化。他向另一個人類露出真面目，而對方用清楚的原則回應，而不是恐懼。

The next day their class visited a small temple beside an old tree. It had stood there for more than a thousand years, and the teacher asked them to think about what people remembered from the past. Kai touched the bark of the tree but did not step over the rope around its roots. Yuna stayed beside him. “Your old school must be very different,” she said. Kai smiled. “It is. Our classrooms are in the sky, and our teacher can fly.” Yuna looked up. “Then you must have a good view.” Riko laughed at how calm she sounded. Kai laughed too, a little less afraid each time.

隔天，全班參觀老樹旁的一座小寺廟。它在那裡已經存在超過一千年，老師請大家想想過去的人留下了什麼記憶。海碰了碰樹皮，卻沒有跨過保護樹根的繩線。優奈站在一旁。「你的舊學校一定很不一樣。」她說。海笑了：「是啊，教室在天空，老師還會飛。」優奈抬頭：「那景色一定很好。」理子聽見她如此平靜，不禁笑了。海也笑出聲，而且每笑一次，就少害怕一點。

When they returned to school, Yuna helped Kai plan the story room again. She said she could cook a simple meal for the group at the next practice if a teacher helped. She did not ask to put a dragon picture on the wall or make his secret part of the show. Instead, she asked if his bird story needed another ending. He said not yet. “I want the bird to choose only after it has seen both places,” he explained. Yuna agreed. A student council secretary came to collect the class plan, and Yuna handed over the page without Kai's private notes. Riko watched with relief. Trust was not a grand speech. Sometimes it was a person choosing which paper not to show.

回校後，優奈又幫海規劃故事屋。她說下次練習如果有老師協助，自己可以替小組煮一頓簡單的餐點。她沒有要求把龍的畫貼上牆，也沒想拿他的祕密當表演內容，只問他的小鳥故事是否要改寫結局。海說還不用。「我希望那隻鳥看過兩個地方後，再自己選。」他解釋。優奈同意了。學生會祕書來收班級計畫，優奈交出正式頁面，沒有附上海的私人筆記。理子鬆了口氣。信任不是一段華麗演講，有時只是某個人知道哪張紙不該給別人看。

That evening Kai put his bracelet on again before going home. He did not feel that he was returning to a false face. He was choosing when to show parts of himself. His aunt had once told him that humans would never understand the sky. Today two human girls had listened and asked good questions. He could not prove that every human would be kind. He did not need to. He only needed to remember what these two had done. He wrote it in his book, with no stamp yet: “A true friend does not own my story.”

那晚回家前，海重新戴上手環。他不覺得自己又戴回假面具，而是在決定何時讓別人看見自己的一部分。阿姨以前說，人類永遠不會理解天空。今天，兩個人類女孩卻認真聽，也問了好問題。他不能證明所有人都善良，更不必如此。他只需要記住眼前這兩人做過的事。他在書裡寫下，雖然還沒有新印記：「真正的朋友不會把我的故事當成自己的東西。」
