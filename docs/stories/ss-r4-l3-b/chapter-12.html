不署名的提議

An Offer Without a Name

被改掉的那句話 · 第 12 / 25 章 · 2000–3000 · 英中對照 · 校訂中

Celeste met Mara in the closed rehearsal hall. She admitted approving a softer ending and said she had been asked to remove any mention of the company behind the river gate. She still refused to identify who had written the black squares.

席莉絲在關閉的排練廳見瑪拉。她承認核准了比較溫和的結局，也說有人要求刪去水閘背後公司的名字，但仍拒絕透露是誰寫下黑方塊。

Celeste's hands stayed on the closed script while she spoke. She said she had once saved the theatre from a fire and had spent years keeping young actors employed. Mara believed those facts could be true. They did not give Celeste a permanent right to decide what every witness was allowed to say.

席莉絲說話時，雙手一直放在闔上的劇本上。她說自己曾從火災中保住劇院，多年來讓年輕演員有工作。瑪拉相信這些事可能是真的，但它們不等於席莉絲永遠有權決定每位證人能說什麼。

Mara asked for the written request and the date. Celeste replied that most instructions had been spoken during meetings. Mara said she would not call an oral memory a signed order. She would report what Celeste had admitted and mark the rest uncertain.

瑪拉要求書面要求與日期。席莉絲說大部分指示都在會議口頭提出。瑪拉表示，不能把口述回憶當作簽署命令。她會記下席莉絲承認的部分，其餘註明尚未確定。

They moved a heavy barrel aside to open a cupboard of emergency supplies. A bubble rose in a water bottle; the young captain of the volunteer group stayed capable and calm. From the radio came news of a rescue in a distant canyon. A client at a legal advice desk asked whether a song was property and whether an editor could alter it without permission. The assistant explained that details matter and offered general information, not a final ruling. A sudden flash lit the room when workers restored electricity. In a nearby science display, visitors watched eggs hatch and read a monthly report about community spending. Mara felt hopeful that their situation could be discussed in daylight. A poster showed a sword from a historic play, while a food table offered rice with mild spice. She was careful to represent only her own experience, not the wishes of every artist. When the elderly singer began to weep, Mara sat beside her rather than asking for another statement. The clerk said a client could ask the office to edit a factual mistake, but any edit to an official record had to leave a trace. Mara underlined that rule in her notes.

大家把沉重木桶移開，打開緊急物資櫃。水瓶裡冒起氣泡，年輕的志工隊長仍冷靜能幹。收音機傳來遠處峽谷救援的消息。法律諮詢桌旁一名當事人問歌曲是否屬於財產，以及編輯能否未經許可改動。助理說細節很重要，提供一般資訊而非最終判決。維修人員恢復供電時，一道亮光閃過房間。旁邊科學展示讓參觀者看蛋孵化，並閱讀社區支出的每月報告。瑪拉覺得事件終於有希望在光明處討論。海報畫著歷史劇的長劍；食物桌提供加了溫和香料的米飯。她小心只代表自己的經歷，不宣稱代表所有藝人。年長歌手開始落淚時，瑪拉坐在她身旁，而沒有再要求證詞。 職員說，當事人可以要求辦公室編輯修正事實錯誤，但官方紀錄的每次修改都必須留下軌跡。瑪拉在筆記中畫線標出這項規定。

After the comparison reading, Mara asked the actors which ending had asked a genuine question and which had supplied a comfortable answer. No one needed much time. Nia said the comfortable ending had felt like a curtain drawn before the story was over. Mara wrote that description down.

比較讀劇後，瑪拉問演員哪個結局提出真正的問題、哪個只提供舒服的答案。大家幾乎不需思考。妮雅說，舒服版本像在故事還沒結束前就拉上幕簾。瑪拉把這種形容記了下來。

Nia proposed a public reading of both versions so the cast could hear what had changed. Mara agreed only after removing all private material from the comparison. The point was to discuss editorial choices, not expose survivors as evidence.

妮雅提議公開讀兩版劇本，讓團員聽見差異。瑪拉同意，但先從比較文本移除私人資料。目的在討論編輯選擇，而不是把倖存者當證據展示。

For the comparison, June gave the versions letters instead of calling one honest and the other dishonest. Mara initially disliked the neutral labels. Then she heard an actor defend a line he had not realized came from the altered ending. Without an accusation hanging over him, he could explain why it seemed easier to perform. Nia answered by reading the preceding question. The easy answer no longer fitted. Mara kept both comments. A useful comparison required people to hear the change before being told what judgment to make.

比對時，君用字母稱呼兩個版本，沒有先說哪版誠實、哪版不誠實。瑪拉起初不喜歡這種中性標示，後來聽見一位演員維護某句台詞，卻不知道它出自改過的結尾。沒有指責壓在頭上，他能說明為何覺得比較好演。妮雅接著念前面的問題，那個輕鬆的答案立刻顯得不合。瑪拉把兩人的話都留下。有用的比對，要讓人先聽見改變，再做判斷。

When the actors compared the endings, the first version asked who could make a safety decision; the second thanked an unnamed company for saving the town. The difference was not a matter of style. It changed who had power in the story.

演員比較兩個結局，原版問「誰有權做安全決定」，新版卻感謝一間未具名公司拯救小鎮。這不是單純風格不同，而是改變了故事裡誰掌握權力。
