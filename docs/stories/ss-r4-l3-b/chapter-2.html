Two Marks in the Margin

被改掉的那句話 · 第 2 / 25 章 · 2000–3000 · 英中對照

用互動版查詞、朗讀與收藏

On Monday, stage manager June laid every script edition across three tables. The blue dots appeared only beside personal details from interviews; the black squares appeared beside the old river gate and the company that had repaired it. The pattern made Mara change her first theory.

星期一，舞台監督君把所有版本攤在三張桌上。藍點只出現在訪談的個人細節旁；黑方塊則出現在舊水閘及維修公司相關段落旁。這個規律迫使瑪拉修正最初的推測。

On the oldest draft, the pencil marks were close to sentences about names and houses. On the new printout, whole passages about the gate company were gone. Mara drew two separate arrows rather than one. She still remembered how certain she had felt when she first called the changes a single act of theft.

最舊的稿上，鉛筆記號靠近姓名與住家細節；新印本則整段刪掉水閘公司的內容。瑪拉畫出兩支不同箭頭，而不是一支。她還記得自己起初多麼肯定這只是同一場偷改事件。

Theo checked the printer clock against the rehearsal calendar. The black-square pages were made late on Saturday, when the theatre was closed to actors. The blue marks were already present on Friday afternoon. One person might still have made both kinds, but they were not one editing event.

提歐對照印表機時間和排練日程。黑方塊頁面在週六深夜印出，當時演員不在劇院；藍點則週五下午就已出現。兩種記號仍可能出自同一個人，卻不是同一次修改。

June opened the theatre's archive room. Posters from a decade of festivals covered one wall: a painted beast from a children's show, a bowling fundraiser that once paid for stage lights, and a faded photograph of an expressive professor conducting a student choir. The current budget was smaller. June showed Mara a repair kit with only two tools and a permit for the festival stage; both had been signed by the same office that produced the black-square pages. Mara drew the geography of the venue on a sheet of paper. The sponsor controlled one entrance, not the whole square. A tired passenger from the late ferry had complained that a man in a necktie tried to cut the line at that gate. He had been drunk, and his shouting had begun to frighten the volunteer checking tickets. The clerk had refused to let him through. It would be greedy, Mara thought, to call that disorder proof that the sponsor had altered the script. She felt regret about her earlier accusation. A strip of lace caught on a folder, and June gently freed it before turning the page. The file revealed a visible date: Friday, not Saturday. Their retired professor, now a voter on the independent arts board, had watched rehearsals during the previous semester. He advised them not to risk confusing the blue-pencil marks about personal privacy with the later black squares about the river gate. Two edits might demand two different explanations.

君打開劇院的檔案室。一整面牆貼著十年來的藝術節海報：兒童劇裡畫出的野獸、曾為舞台燈光募款的保齡球活動，以及一位表情生動的教授指揮學生合唱團的褪色照片。今年的預算縮水了。君拿給瑪拉看只有兩件工具的修理組，以及藝術節舞台的許可證；兩份文件都由列印黑方塊版本的辦公室簽署。瑪拉在紙上畫出場地的地理配置。贊助商控制的只是一處入口，不是整個廣場。一名搭末班渡輪來的疲憊乘客曾投訴，一個打領帶的男人想在那道入口插隊。他喝醉了，大吼大叫，開始嚇到負責驗票的志工；職員不准他進去。瑪拉心想，如果只憑這場混亂就斷定贊助商竄改劇本，未免太貪心、太武斷。她後悔先前急著指控。文件夾勾住了一條蕾絲飾邊，君輕輕把它解開才翻頁。檔案顯示清楚可見的日期：星期五，而不是星期六。那位退休教授現在是獨立藝術委員會的投票成員，上一學期還曾旁聽排練。他提醒她們，別冒險把涉及個人隱私的藍鉛筆記號與後來涉及水閘的黑方塊混為一談。兩種修改，也許需要兩套不同的解釋。

June fetched a clean envelope and asked everyone to sign across its edge after the three versions were placed inside. Mara said the sealed originals were not a prize to be displayed. They were a way to make sure tomorrow's argument began with the same pages as today's.

君找來乾淨信封，三種版本放入後請大家在封口上簽名。瑪拉說，封存的原稿不是拿來炫耀的獎品，而是讓明天的討論能從與今天相同的紙張開始。

Mara asked Celeste for the office access log. Celeste offered a new script instead, saying the upcoming festival could not wait. Mara refused to swap a question for a convenient answer. She requested a copy of the schedule in writing.

瑪拉向席莉絲索取辦公室出入紀錄。席莉絲卻遞給她另一本新劇本，說藝術節不能等。瑪拉拒絕用方便的答案取代問題，正式書面要求排程紀錄。

Before sealing the envelope, June counted the sheets aloud. The shortest edition lacked no numbered page; the speeches had been deleted inside otherwise complete pages. Mara had imagined someone pulling paper from a pile. Now she crossed that picture out of her notes. Theo held a lamp above the table while June checked the last number. Their explanation would have to change with the evidence. The actor waiting for a verdict looked disappointed, but he helped June carry the sealed envelope to the office cupboard instead of demanding a name.

封好信封前，君把頁數逐張念出。最短的版本並沒有缺頁；台詞是在完整編號的頁面內被刪去。瑪拉本來想像有人從紙堆抽走幾張，現在把這個猜想劃掉。提歐舉燈照桌面，君核對最後的頁碼。說法必須隨證據改變。等著結論的演員有點失望，卻還是幫君把封好的信封搬到辦公室櫃裡，沒有逼她說出嫌疑人。

June warned that someone could lose work if the sponsor withdrew. Mara said the company should prepare a second plan, not pretend the women in the story had never spoken. Their first small success was simple: they locked away untouched examples of all three drafts.

君提醒她，若贊助商撤資可能有人失業。瑪拉說，劇團應準備備案，而不是假裝故事裡的女性不曾發聲。他們取得的第一個小進展很簡單：封存三種未經再加工的版本。
