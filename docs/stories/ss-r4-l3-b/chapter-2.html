Two Marks in the Margin

被改掉的那句話 · 第 2 / 25 章 · 2000–3000 · 英中對照

用互動版查詞、朗讀與收藏

On Monday, stage manager June laid every script edition across three tables. The blue dots appeared only beside personal details from interviews; the black squares appeared beside the old river gate and the company that had repaired it. The pattern made Mara change her first theory. Nia asked to try a different way of standing. Instead of facing the audience like a speaker giving a lesson, she turned toward the empty chair at the side of the stage. It made the conversation feel unfinished. Mara realized that an unfinished question could be honest and dramatic at once. She kept the chair in the plan but left Nia free to change her movement again.

星期一，舞台監督君把所有版本攤在三張桌上。藍點只出現在訪談的個人細節旁；黑方塊則出現在舊水閘及維修公司相關段落旁。這個規律迫使瑪拉修正最初的推測。妮雅要求嘗試不同站位。她不再像講課者那樣面向觀眾，而是轉向舞台側邊的空椅子。這讓對話顯得尚未結束。瑪拉發現，沒有答案的問題也可以既誠實又有戲劇張力。她把椅子保留在設計裡，仍讓妮雅有權再改動作。

On the oldest draft, the pencil marks were close to sentences about names and houses. On the new printout, whole passages about the gate company were gone. Mara drew two separate arrows rather than one. She still remembered how certain she had felt when she first called the changes a single act of theft.

最舊的稿上，鉛筆記號靠近姓名與住家細節；新印本則整段刪掉水閘公司的內容。瑪拉畫出兩支不同箭頭，而不是一支。她還記得自己起初多麼肯定這只是同一場偷改事件。

Theo checked the printer clock against the rehearsal calendar. The black-square pages were made late on Saturday, when the theatre was closed to actors. The blue marks were already present on Friday afternoon. One person might still have made both kinds, but they were not one editing event. A small clock stood on the office shelf, several minutes slower than the one above the stage. That difference reminded Mara to compare records rather than join them carelessly. Two files could describe the same evening and still use different times. The team wrote down the clocks they had used. It was uninteresting work, but it was better than building a mystery on a mistake they could have corrected. Theo checked a measurement while Mara checked a sentence. Their jobs did not look alike, but both required them to admit when a first attempt was wrong. That common habit made it easier for them to work together without deciding every question for the other person.

提歐對照印表機時間和排練日程。黑方塊頁面在週六深夜印出，當時演員不在劇院；藍點則週五下午就已出現。兩種記號仍可能出自同一個人，卻不是同一次修改。辦公室架上的小鐘，比舞台上的鐘慢了幾分鐘。這提醒瑪拉應該比對紀錄，而不是隨便拼在一起。兩份檔案可能描述同一晚，使用的時間卻不同。團隊記下各自參照的鐘。這工作乏味，卻比用原本可以修正的錯誤製造謎團更好。提歐檢查測量值時，瑪拉檢查句子。兩人的工作看起來不同，卻都必須承認第一次嘗試可能有錯。這種共同習慣，讓他們能合作而不替對方決定每個問題。

Between rehearsals, the cast visited community events and collected details for their stage exercises. The office wall carried posters from a decade of festivals. A painted beast advertised a children’s show, and an old bowling team had paid for one year’s lights. This year’s budget was tight. A drunk visitor had damaged a table, so an expressive volunteer wrote a notice asking people not to frighten staff. Mara studied the geography of the event map and found that only one stage was run directly by Vale. The greedy choice would be to promise every artist a place, the clerk explained, when they had only one repair kit. A strip of lace hung from a costume; a man with a bright necktie tried to push ahead of a tired passenger. The clerk would not permit it. A retired professor waiting nearby said he had taught music for a semester and could still recognize an honest melody. “Do not risk your evidence by changing a file,” he advised. Mara felt regret that she had not kept more copies, but the original date remained visible beside the recording. The professor was also a voter on the independent arts board.

排練間隙，演員走訪社區活動，把觀察到的細節帶回舞台練習。牆上掛著十年來的音樂節海報。畫著野獸的海報宣傳兒童節目，另一年的舞台燈光曾由保齡球隊贊助。今年預算吃緊。一名醉酒訪客弄壞桌子後，一位善於表達的志工寫下通知，請大家不要驚嚇職員。瑪拉看著活動地圖，發現只有其中一座舞台由韋爾直接負責。職員說，只有一組修理工具卻向每位藝人保證演出，是貪心而不負責任的選擇。一條蕾絲掛在服裝旁；打亮眼領帶的男人想插到疲累的乘客前面，職員不允許。旁邊一位退休教授說，他曾教過一學期音樂，仍聽得出真誠的旋律。「別為了證明自己而改動檔案，」他提醒。瑪拉後悔沒有留更多備份，但原始日期仍清楚顯示在錄音旁。那位教授也是獨立藝術委員會的投票成員。

June fetched a clean envelope and asked everyone to sign across its edge after the three versions were placed inside. Mara said the sealed originals were not a prize to be displayed. They were a way to make sure tomorrow's argument began with the same pages as today's.

君找來乾淨信封，三種版本放入後請大家在封口上簽名。瑪拉說，封存的原稿不是拿來炫耀的獎品，而是讓明天的討論能從與今天相同的紙張開始。

Mara asked Celeste for the office access log. Celeste offered a new script instead, saying the upcoming festival could not wait. Mara refused to swap a question for a convenient answer. She requested a copy of the schedule in writing. The table was covered with ordinary objects that suddenly mattered: tape, pencil marks, a folder, and a few folded receipts. None was exciting by itself. Mara arranged them in order and saw that the sequence could tell a story the objects could not tell alone. She decided that any public explanation should show the same order, so other people could test it rather than simply believe her.

瑪拉向席莉絲索取辦公室出入紀錄。席莉絲卻遞給她另一本新劇本，說藝術節不能等。瑪拉拒絕用方便的答案取代問題，正式書面要求排程紀錄。桌上堆著忽然變得重要的平凡物件：膠帶、鉛筆痕、資料夾和幾張摺過的收據。單看都不精彩。瑪拉依時間排列後，才看出物件本身說不出的故事。她決定公開說明也要依同樣順序，讓別人能檢驗，而不是只能相信她。

June warned that someone could lose work if the sponsor withdrew. Mara said the company should prepare a second plan, not pretend the women in the story had never spoken. Their first small success was simple: they locked away untouched examples of all three drafts. For a moment Mara imagined the audience sitting in front of the stage. She did not picture a crowd that always agreed with her. She imagined tired workers, younger students, and residents who had heard different stories about the river. Each would listen with a different experience. She could not control their conclusions. What she could control was whether the play gave them an honest chance to make one. The director's chair sat empty for a while. Nobody hurried to take its place. Mara used the pause to ask which questions required a director's approval and which could be decided by the whole team. Dividing those responsibilities made the next conversation less frightening.

君提醒她，若贊助商撤資可能有人失業。瑪拉說，劇團應準備備案，而不是假裝故事裡的女性不曾發聲。他們取得的第一個小進展很簡單：封存三種未經再加工的版本。一瞬間，瑪拉想像觀眾坐在舞台前。她沒有幻想所有人都會同意她，而是想像疲倦的工作者、年輕學生與聽過不同河岸故事的居民。每個人都會帶著不同經驗來聽。她無法控制他們的結論，能控制的是劇本有沒有誠實地給出判斷機會。導演椅暫時空著，沒有人急著坐上去。瑪拉利用這段時間詢問：哪些問題需要導演核准，哪些可以由全隊決定？釐清責任後，下一次談話就比較不令人畏懼。
