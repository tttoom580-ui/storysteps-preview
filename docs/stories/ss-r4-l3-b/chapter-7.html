A Light Left On

被改掉的那句話 · 第 7 / 25 章 · 2000–3000 · 英中對照 · 校訂中

用互動版查詞、朗讀與收藏

The theatre's night rehearsal ran late because a storm shook the roof. Theo checked each lamp before the actors walked across the set. Mara watched him replace a loose part without pretending that safety was less important than artistic beauty.

因為暴風雨搖晃屋頂，劇團夜間排練拖得很晚。提歐在演員走過布景前逐一檢查燈具。瑪拉看著他更換鬆動零件，沒有假裝藝術美感比安全更重要。

Theo had learned lighting in a small theatre where a performer once fell because a bright beam hid the edge of a step. He told the story without making himself the hero. Mara listened and understood why he checked so many little things before letting anyone walk onto the set.

提歐曾在一間小劇院學燈光，有演員因強光遮住台階邊緣而跌倒。他說起那件事，卻沒有把自己說成英雄。瑪拉聽後明白，為何他讓人踏上舞台前總要檢查那麼多小事。

While the others waited, Theo showed Mara a dated light plan. Its earlier version placed a bright square over the mother's speech; the new version lit only the sponsor's sign. Theo had kept both copies because changes to stage positions required his approval.

大家等待時，提歐拿出附日期的燈光圖。舊版把光打在母親說話的位置，新版卻只照亮贊助商標誌。他保存兩版，因為舞台位置變更需要經他同意。

The café stood beside an old brick shop. Behind the counter, cups filled a cupboard, and a glass jar held a single dime from a visitor overseas. A lily stood in a small vase. The cook had invented a new flavor of orange cake, but the first attempt was awful; the baker called the result junk and promised to try again sometime. Outside, an emergency siren cut through traffic. An awkward silence followed. Vale said a public appeal would explode into a scandal and hurt everyone. Mara reminded him that a private offer could not settle another person’s rights. She kept her face steady although her hands were cold. An assistant called the offer terrific, then looked at the floor when Mara asked whether it was fair. The wage for the children’s extra rehearsal was not even included. “I will not make a quiet answer for them,” Mara said. The café owner said they could invent a clear credit system instead of demanding a pound of flesh from the staff. Mara asked him to remind the manager of the unpaid rehearsal hours. The offer would mean little unless it also respected other people’s work.

咖啡館旁是一家老磚屋商店。櫃檯後的碗櫥擺滿杯子，玻璃罐裡放著一枚外國客人留下的十美分硬幣。一朵百合插在小花瓶裡。廚師發明了新口味橘子蛋糕，第一次做得糟透了；麵包師說那是垃圾，改天再試。窗外的緊急警笛劃過車流，室內陷入尷尬的安靜。韋爾說公開申訴會爆成醜聞，傷害所有人。瑪拉提醒他，私人提議不能決定別人的權利。她雖手心冰冷，表情依然穩定。助理稱讚條件好極了，瑪拉問公平與否時，他卻垂下頭。孩子們額外排練的工資甚至沒被列入。「我不會替他們做出沉默的回答，」瑪拉說。 咖啡館老闆說，他們可以發明清楚署名制度，而不是向員工索取殘酷的代價。瑪拉請他提醒經理還有未付的排練工時。除非提議也尊重其他人的工作，否則就沒有多少意義。

When he laid the two light plans side by side, Mara noticed that the change was not just technical. The mother's face would become less visible while the sponsor's name would become brighter. She felt uneasy about how easily a lighting decision could change the story without touching a single word.

提歐把兩張燈光圖並排時，瑪拉發現改動不只是技術問題。母親的臉會更難被看見，贊助商的名字卻更加明亮。她不安地想到，不必修改任何文字，燈光就能改變故事。

Mara asked why he had not shown her sooner. Theo answered that he had not recognized the significance until he saw the two printed marks. He did not ask her to trust him simply because they had started talking more often.

瑪拉問他為何沒有早點拿出來。提歐說，在看到兩種改稿記號前，他沒發現這項差異的重要。他並未因為兩人最近常聊天，就要求她無條件相信自己。

Under the umbrella, Mara asked whether Theo wanted her to mention his discovery at the next rehearsal. He said she should show the plans, including his own earlier approval of the revised layout. He had signed before thinking about the dramatic effect. Mara slowed her steps. It was difficult to offer evidence that included one's own mistake. She promised to explain what his signature meant and when he had changed his view. The umbrella tilted as they stopped at the crossing, and both laughed at the cold water running down their sleeves.

共撐傘時，瑪拉問提歐要不要在下次排練提到他的發現。他說應該展示燈光圖，包括自己先前批准新版配置的簽名。他當時沒想到戲劇效果就簽了。瑪拉放慢腳步。提出也包含自身錯誤的證據並不容易。她答應說清簽名的意義，以及他何時改變看法。兩人在路口停下時，傘歪了，冰水流進袖口，他們一起笑出聲。

On the way home they shared an umbrella and argued gently about whether art could change a town's memory. Theo said a lamp only showed what stood in front of it. Mara said choosing where to place the lamp was already a kind of answer.

回家路上，他們共撐一把傘，溫和地辯論藝術能否改變一座城鎮的記憶。提歐說，燈只能照出面前的東西。瑪拉說，決定把燈放在哪裡，本身就是一種回答。
