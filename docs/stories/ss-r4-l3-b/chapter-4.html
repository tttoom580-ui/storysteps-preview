A Consent Form with No Date

被改掉的那句話 · 第 4 / 25 章 · 2000–3000 · 英中對照

用互動版查詞、朗讀與收藏

At the history centre, Mara found an old permission form attached to an interview. The signature was genuine, but the page did not say that the story could appear in a commercial festival. The director of the centre explained why an old permission could not answer every new question. She checked the time and wrote it in the margin instead of trusting her memory. Later, two people might remember the same conversation in different ways. A date could not prove what someone meant, but it could prevent simple confusion about what happened first. She also noted who had actually been in the room. Names on a schedule were not always witnesses to an event.

在歷史中心，瑪拉找到附在訪談後的舊授權書。簽名是真的，但表格沒有寫明故事可以用於商業藝術節。中心主任解釋，舊有授權不一定能回答新的使用問題。她看了時間，寫在頁邊，而不是只靠記憶。事後兩人可能對同一段對話有不同回想。日期不能證明某人的用意，卻能避免事件先後的基本混淆。她也記下誰當時真的在場；排班表上的名字不一定代表那人親眼見證。

The librarian placed a small paper weight over the permission form so the corners would not rise in the draft. Mara stared at the blank space where the permitted uses should have appeared. She had been proud of finding the original interview; now she understood that finding a story was different from receiving it as a gift.

館員用小紙鎮壓住授權書，以免紙角被風吹起。瑪拉看著本該註明用途的空白處。她原本為找到原始訪談而自豪，如今明白找到故事與獲得分享的允許是兩回事。

Mara and Theo listened to the interview with a librarian. The speaker, Ada Lin, described waiting through the night while the river gate remained closed. She also described a conversation with her daughter, a detail that belonged to the family and not to a public argument. The actors tried the scene twice, first quickly and then with room for each question. The second reading lasted longer, yet nobody complained that it was slow. Words changed when the person saying them had time to consider their meaning. Mara crossed out a line that existed only to tell the audience what to think. She preferred to show a choice and let people notice its cost. One of the actors asked for the page to be read once more. Mara listened to the silence between the sentences. A missing line was not always an error, but a missing reason for cutting that line could be a serious problem.

瑪拉與提歐在館員陪同下聽錄音。受訪者林艾達談到自己整夜等待、水閘始終未開，也談到與女兒的私密對話。後者屬於家人，不該直接拿來當公共辯論的材料。演員把那場戲念了兩遍，第一次很快，第二次為每個問題留出空間。第二遍花的時間比較長，卻沒有人抱怨拖沓。當說話者有時間想清楚意義，台詞也會改變。瑪拉劃掉一句只為告訴觀眾該怎麼想的話；她寧願呈現選擇，讓觀眾看見代價。一名演員要求再讀一次。瑪拉傾聽句與句之間的沉默。少一句話不一定是錯誤，但若沒有人能說明刪掉它的理由，就可能是嚴重問題。

Mara carried a small notebook whenever the company visited public spaces for research. They followed a narrow alley to the storage room. Mara felt that asking for the key took more bravery than singing in front of a crowd. A cherry drink had spilled across the floor, and the sudden crash of a box made her dizzy. “Our definition of an emergency is different from yours,” Theo joked, then grew serious. They needed to investigate the damaged cable before connecting any power. A volunteer sat nearby to knit a scarf while another checked each label. A rehearsal for a radio mystery included the word murder, but it was only a scene in a play. After a short nap the stage manager brought tea on a saucer and complained that a singer’s costume looked too sexy for the family event. Mara argued for a respectful discussion instead of shame. A solid wooden tower held the lights; behind it stood a van with blankets for workers who might starve through another late shift. Mara was thankful when the manager offered warm food and a safe place to rest. That small warmth mattered more than a perfect rehearsal.

劇團外出走訪公共場所取材時，瑪拉總帶著小筆記本。他們沿著窄巷走向儲藏室。瑪拉覺得，開口借鑰匙比站在觀眾面前唱歌更需要勇氣。櫻桃飲料灑滿地，箱子突然砰地落下，讓她頭暈。「我們對緊急事件的定義可能不同，」提歐開玩笑，隨即認真起來。他們得先調查壞掉的線，再接上電源。一名志工坐在旁邊織圍巾，另一人檢查每張標籤。廣播懸疑劇的排練提到了謀殺，但那只是戲中的一幕。短暫小睡後，舞台經理把茶放在茶碟上端來，又抱怨歌手的服裝對家庭活動太性感。瑪拉主張應尊重討論，而非羞辱別人。堅固的木架塔支撐著燈具；後面停了一輛貨車，裡頭有毯子，供可能因加班太久而餓壞的工作人員休息。經理提供熱食和安全休息處時，瑪拉心存感激。這點溫暖比完美排練更重要。

On the bus home, Mara replayed the public part of Ada's account in her mind. The story had a terrible waiting period but no certain villain. She wondered whether she had made her first draft too neat because she feared the audience would not accept a difficult, unfinished question.

回家的公車上，瑪拉反覆想著艾達訪談中可公開的部分。故事裡有可怕的等待，卻沒有確定的反派。她懷疑自己初稿之所以太整齊，是因為怕觀眾無法接受難懂、尚未解答的問題。

Mara crossed the family conversation out of her own working copy. She left the fact about the gate marked for verification, not for performance. Theo noticed her shaking hand, but he waited until she was ready to speak. Nia asked to try a different way of standing. Instead of facing the audience like a speaker giving a lesson, she turned toward the empty chair at the side of the stage. It made the conversation feel unfinished. Mara realized that an unfinished question could be honest and dramatic at once. She kept the chair in the plan but left Nia free to change her movement again.

瑪拉從工作稿刪去那段家人對話，把水閘資訊標示為「待查證」，而非「可演出」。提歐看見她的手在抖，仍等她願意時才開口。妮雅要求嘗試不同站位。她不再像講課者那樣面向觀眾，而是轉向舞台側邊的空椅子。這讓對話顯得尚未結束。瑪拉發現，沒有答案的問題也可以既誠實又有戲劇張力。她把椅子保留在設計裡，仍讓妮雅有權再改動作。

The missing date on the form became their second important clue. The anonymous blue note had asked for consent, while the printed black deletion removed a public question about the gate. Mara wrote those two differences at the top of her notebook. A small clock stood on the office shelf, several minutes slower than the one above the stage. That difference reminded Mara to compare records rather than join them carelessly. Two files could describe the same evening and still use different times. The team wrote down the clocks they had used. It was uninteresting work, but it was better than building a mystery on a mistake they could have corrected. Theo checked a measurement while Mara checked a sentence. Their jobs did not look alike, but both required them to admit when a first attempt was wrong. That common habit made it easier for them to work together without deciding every question for the other person.

表格缺少使用日期範圍，是他們的第二項重要線索。匿名藍色字條要求取得同意，列印的黑色刪改卻移除了水閘的公共問題。瑪拉把這兩種差異寫在筆記本首頁。辦公室架上的小鐘，比舞台上的鐘慢了幾分鐘。這提醒瑪拉應該比對紀錄，而不是隨便拼在一起。兩份檔案可能描述同一晚，使用的時間卻不同。團隊記下各自參照的鐘。這工作乏味，卻比用原本可以修正的錯誤製造謎團更好。提歐檢查測量值時，瑪拉檢查句子。兩人的工作看起來不同，卻都必須承認第一次嘗試可能有錯。這種共同習慣，讓他們能合作而不替對方決定每個問題。
