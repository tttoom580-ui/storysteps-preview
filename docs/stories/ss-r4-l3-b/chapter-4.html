A Consent Form with No Date

被改掉的那句話 · 第 4 / 25 章 · 2000–3000 · 英中對照

用互動版查詞、朗讀與收藏

At the history centre, Mara found an old permission form attached to an interview. The signature was genuine, but the page did not say that the story could appear in a commercial festival. The director of the centre explained why an old permission could not answer every new question.

在歷史中心，瑪拉找到附在訪談後的舊授權書。簽名是真的，但表格沒有寫明故事可以用於商業藝術節。中心主任解釋，舊有授權不一定能回答新的使用問題。

The librarian placed a small paper weight over the permission form so the corners would not rise in the draft. Mara stared at the blank space where the permitted uses should have appeared. She had been proud of finding the original interview; now she understood that finding a story was different from receiving it as a gift.

館員用小紙鎮壓住授權書，以免紙角被風吹起。瑪拉看著本該註明用途的空白處。她原本為找到原始訪談而自豪，如今明白找到故事與獲得分享的允許是兩回事。

Mara and Theo listened to the interview with a librarian. The speaker, Ada Lin, described waiting through the night while the river gate remained closed. She also described a conversation with her daughter, a detail that belonged to the family and not to a public argument.

瑪拉與提歐在館員陪同下聽錄音。受訪者林艾達談到自己整夜等待、水閘始終未開，也談到與女兒的私密對話。後者屬於家人，不該直接拿來當公共辯論的材料。

They followed a narrow alley to the storage room. Mara felt that asking for the key took more bravery than singing in front of a crowd. A cherry drink had spilled across the floor, and the sudden crash of a box made her dizzy. “Our definition of an emergency is different from yours,” Theo joked, then grew serious. They needed to investigate the damaged cable before connecting any power. A volunteer sat nearby to knit a scarf while another checked each label. A rehearsal for a radio mystery included the word murder, but it was only a scene in a play. After a short nap the stage manager brought tea on a saucer and complained that a singer’s costume looked too sexy for the family event. Mara argued for a respectful discussion instead of shame. A solid wooden tower held the lights; behind it stood a van with blankets for workers who might starve through another late shift. Mara was thankful when the manager offered warm food and a safe place to rest. That small warmth mattered more than a perfect rehearsal.

他們沿著窄巷走向儲藏室。瑪拉覺得，開口借鑰匙比站在觀眾面前唱歌更需要勇氣。櫻桃飲料灑滿地，箱子突然砰地落下，讓她頭暈。「我們對緊急事件的定義可能不同，」提歐開玩笑，隨即認真起來。他們得先調查壞掉的線，再接上電源。一名志工坐在旁邊織圍巾，另一人檢查每張標籤。廣播懸疑劇的排練提到了謀殺，但那只是戲中的一幕。短暫小睡後，舞台經理把茶放在茶碟上端來，又抱怨歌手的服裝對家庭活動太性感。瑪拉主張應尊重討論，而非羞辱別人。堅固的木架塔支撐著燈具；後面停了一輛貨車，裡頭有毯子，供可能因加班太久而餓壞的工作人員休息。經理提供熱食和安全休息處時，瑪拉心存感激。這點溫暖比完美排練更重要。

On the bus home, Mara replayed the public part of Ada's account in her mind. The story had a terrible waiting period but no certain villain. She wondered whether she had made her first draft too neat because she feared the audience would not accept a difficult, unfinished question.

回家的公車上，瑪拉反覆想著艾達訪談中可公開的部分。故事裡有可怕的等待，卻沒有確定的反派。她懷疑自己初稿之所以太整齊，是因為怕觀眾無法接受難懂、尚未解答的問題。

Mara crossed the family conversation out of her own working copy. She left the fact about the gate marked for verification, not for performance. Theo noticed her shaking hand, but he waited until she was ready to speak.

瑪拉從工作稿刪去那段家人對話，把水閘資訊標示為「待查證」，而非「可演出」。提歐看見她的手在抖，仍等她願意時才開口。

The librarian showed Mara two boxes on the archive catalogue. One described where an interview could be consulted; the other described whether it could be reproduced. Mara had treated them as the same box. She copied the headings into her notebook and put her play's proposed uses beneath them. A live performance, an online extract, and a printed program each raised a different question. By the time they left, her original request had become three shorter requests. She could finally see what Ada would actually be choosing.

館員指給瑪拉看檔案目錄上的兩欄：一欄說明訪談可以在哪裡查閱，另一欄說明能否重製。瑪拉先前把它們當成同一回事。她抄下標題，再列出戲劇打算如何使用內容。現場演出、網路節錄、紙本節目單，各有不同問題。離開時，原本的一個大請求變成三個較小的請求。她終於看清楚艾達實際需要選擇什麼。

The missing date on the form became their second important clue. The anonymous blue note had asked for consent, while the printed black deletion removed a public question about the gate. Mara wrote those two differences at the top of her notebook.

表格缺少使用日期範圍，是他們的第二項重要線索。匿名藍色字條要求取得同意，列印的黑色刪改卻移除了水閘的公共問題。瑪拉把這兩種差異寫在筆記本首頁。
