午夜印表機紀錄

The Printer's Midnight Record

被改掉的那句話 · 第 11 / 25 章 · 2000–3000 · 英中對照 · 校訂中

用互動版查詞、朗讀與收藏

Theo and June obtained the theatre's printer-use report through its office manager. It showed a large print job at 11:42 on Saturday night, sent from the director's computer. The report did not show who had sat at that computer.

提歐和君透過辦公室管理員取得印表機使用紀錄。紀錄顯示週六深夜十一點四十二分，有大量檔案從導演電腦送印，但沒有顯示當時坐在電腦前的是誰。

A cleaner who worked the late shift told June that the printer had been running after the main doors were locked. The cleaner could not say who had sent the job, and Mara thanked her for not guessing. That limited account still supported the time recorded by the machine.

值夜班的清潔員告訴君，主要入口上鎖後印表機仍曾運轉。她不知道是誰下的列印指令，瑪拉感謝她沒有亂猜。這份有限證言仍能佐證機器所記的時間。

Mara compared file names with the draft copies. The black-square version carried the same number of pages as the late job. The match mattered, but a page count could not identify the writer or prove that the sponsor had ordered the changes.

瑪拉對照檔名與紙本。黑方塊版本的頁數與那次深夜列印相同。這有價值，卻無法只靠頁數辨認改稿者，更不能證明贊助商下令。

An accurate comparison required a loose cable to be fixed and all equipment to run at the same level. Behind the studio lay a county yard with timber stacked near a ditch. A worker filled a generator with gasoline while another tested a freezer. Students waiting for a music class discussed whether an intelligent machine could replace a human performer. Their teacher said a theory was useful only when checked. On a board, the specialist drew roughly matching lines in violet and blue. She explained the operation in plain language, choosing the correct medium for each recording rather than trusting a picture alone. Mara’s shoulder muscle tightened as she watched. The room grew warm; a drop of sweat ran down her face. Outside, swimmers prepared to dive into a pool, and a cleaner carried plastic boxes past the door. None of the ordinary noise could weaken the fact that the first three musical phrases matched. The teacher said she could bless every young singer with good wishes, but only practice would help them perform well. Mara agreed, preferring a fair rehearsal to an easy promise.

精確比較必須先修好鬆動的音源線，並讓所有設備以相同音量運作。錄音室後方是郡有院子，木材堆在溝渠旁。工人替發電機加汽油，另一人測試冷凍庫。等音樂課的學生討論智慧機器能否取代真人表演者；老師說，理論必須受檢驗才有用。專家在白板畫出大致相符的紫色與藍色線條，並用淺白的話解釋操作，為每個錄音選擇正確媒介，而不是只看圖。瑪拉看著看著，肩膀肌肉緊繃。房間暖起來，汗珠沿她臉頰滑下。外頭泳客準備跳水，清潔員提著塑膠箱經過。平凡噪音無法削弱前三段樂句相符的事實。 老師說，她可以祝福每個小歌手，但只有練習能幫助他們好好表演。瑪拉同意，寧可安排公平排練，也不要隨口承諾。

At the staff meeting Mara heard the fear behind the angry questions. One worker had a parent to support; another needed stable hours to pay rent. Mara asked them to help make the smaller budget instead of treating their worries as obstacles. A fair process would have to count those costs too.

員工會議裡，瑪拉聽見憤怒問題背後的恐懼。有人需要扶養父母，有人需要穩定工時付房租。她邀請大家一起編列較小的預算，而非把擔憂當阻礙。公平程序也必須計入這些代價。

Celeste called a staff meeting. She said the theatre's rent was overdue and the sponsor might withdraw its support. Several workers became angry at Mara. Mara acknowledged their worry and asked what money they could raise without changing witness testimony.

席莉絲召開員工會議，表示劇院租金逾期、贊助商可能撤資。幾位員工對瑪拉生氣。她承認大家的擔憂，並問是否能在不改寫證詞的情況下另籌經費。

The report had a column marked cancelled jobs. Theo asked the office manager to explain it before they drew any conclusion. A cancelled command might leave a record without producing paper. The manager separated those rows from completed jobs, and the Saturday entry remained in the completed column. Mara wrote down the distinction beside the page count. June exhaled. One tempting objection had been answered properly, and another remained: access to a computer was still wider than ownership of it. They would have to ask about that separately.

報表有一欄標著取消的工作。提歐請經理先說明，才下結論。取消的指令也可能留下紀錄，卻沒有印出紙張。經理把這些列與完成的工作分開，星期六那筆仍在完成欄。瑪拉在頁數旁記下差異。君鬆了口氣。一個可能的疑點得到正式解答，另一個仍在：能使用電腦的人，不只電腦的主人。她們得另外追問。

Theo offered to work with borrowed lighting equipment if necessary. Mara did not accept the offer as a promise of endless sacrifice. She asked him to set a limit and to protect his paid work elsewhere.

提歐提出必要時可借設備演出。瑪拉沒有把這當成他應無限付出的承諾，而是請他設定界線，保護其他有薪工作。
