The First Honest Reading

被改掉的那句話 · 第 5 / 25 章 · 2000–3000 · 英中對照

Mara met Ada, now sixty-three, at the history centre with a staff witness present. She apologized for writing the family conversation into the first draft. Ada accepted the apology but declined to let that scene be performed. She did agree to speak about the night at the river gate.

瑪拉在歷史中心、由職員在場見證下，見到了六十三歲的艾達。她為把家人的對話寫進初稿道歉。艾達接受道歉，但拒絕讓那場戲上演；她願意談那晚水閘前發生的事。

Ada did not rush to answer the questions. She folded the form once, opened it again, and asked Mara to read the choices aloud. Mara did so without giving advice about which boxes to mark. When Ada finally selected the stage option, her hand was steady. That choice mattered more than a generous speech from the writer.

艾達沒有急著回答。她把表格摺起又攤開，請瑪拉把各選項念出來。瑪拉照做，沒有暗示該勾哪一格。艾達最後選擇可在舞台使用時，手十分平穩。這個選擇比作家熱情的演說更重要。

Rather than asking for one broad signature, Mara separated the new form into choices: public name or no name, exact words or a summary, live stage or printed booklet. Ada chose to use her first name, keep the gate account, and remove her daughter's words.

瑪拉沒有要求一個概括的簽名，而是把新表格分成不同選項：公開全名或匿名、原話或摘要、現場演出或印刷手冊。艾達選擇只用名字、保留水閘描述，移除女兒的話。

The breakfast table held bacon and ripe fruit. Someone spread a carpet to stop the chairs from moving. A father began to complain about his considerable travel cost, then stopped to cough and laugh at himself. He agreed to engage with the listening exercise after Mara explained its purpose. A fake festival ticket lay beside a flashlight in the garage, where a small motor kept the fan running. June held her jaw when the old microphone failed, but Theo quietly fixed the cable. A child with a peanut allergy sat near the exit so the snack could be moved away. Mara asked each singer to pronounce the last word clearly and take responsibility for the sound. Outside, a sack of flour slipped from a delivery bike. The fall was harmless, yet it reminded her of the sorrow she had carried for days. Steam rose from tea in the corner, and the group chose a calm discussion instead of the violent argument some visitors had expected.

早餐桌上有培根和成熟水果。有人鋪了地毯，避免椅子滑動。一名父親抱怨自己花了相當多的交通費，隨即咳了兩聲，笑自己太激動。瑪拉解釋活動目的後，他願意參與試聽。車庫裡，手電筒旁放著一張假音樂節票券，小馬達讓風扇持續轉動。舊麥克風故障時，君緊張得托住下巴，提歐則默默修好連接線。一名對花生過敏的孩子坐在出口附近，方便移開零食。瑪拉請每位歌手清楚發出最後一個字的音，也對自己的聲音負責。外頭，一袋麵粉從送貨腳踏車滑落。沒有人受傷，卻讓她想起幾天來的悲傷。角落茶杯冒出蒸氣，大家選擇冷靜討論，而不是某些訪客預期的激烈爭吵。

After the reading, Mara returned to the seat where Ada had sat. She imagined how strange it must be to hear an actress speak a memory from long ago. The theatre could give that memory a different shape, but it could not become its owner. Mara underlined that thought on a new page.

讀劇後，瑪拉回到艾達坐過的位置。她想像一個人聽見演員重述自己多年以前的記憶會多麼奇妙。劇院可以賦予記憶不同形狀，卻不能因此成為它的主人。瑪拉在新頁上劃線記下這個想法。

At rehearsal Nia read the revised scene. The mother's questions were still present, but now they came from a person who had chosen to share them. Celeste called the scene less dramatic. The cast disagreed; a quiet answer could be more powerful than a shouted one.

讀劇時，妮雅念出修訂後的場景。母親的提問仍在，卻是由願意分享的人授權而來。席莉絲說情緒不夠戲劇化，演員們卻不同意：平靜的回答可能比喊叫更有力。

Ada asked to hear the revised speech once without anyone watching her face. Nia turned her chair toward the wall and read. Mara wanted to ask whether the scene was moving, but kept that question to herself. When the reading ended, Ada corrected the position of the doorway. The water had come from the left, she said; that was why the neighbor could not reach the steps. Nia moved her chair. A small physical change made the mother's decision clearer than the grand speech Mara had originally written.

艾達想聽一次修改後的台詞，但不希望大家盯著她的表情。妮雅把椅子轉向牆壁，開始朗讀。瑪拉很想問是否感人，卻忍住了。讀完後，艾達更正門口的位置：水是從左邊過來，所以鄰居無法走到台階。妮雅移動椅子。這個微小的身體位置變化，比瑪拉原先寫的宏大演說，更清楚地呈現母親的決定。

After rehearsal, Mara thanked Nia for refusing the earlier version. Nia said, 'You listened, so I came back.' Their first five days ended without a solution to the anonymous edits, but they had repaired a wrong decision of their own.

排練後，瑪拉感謝妮雅拒演早期版本。妮雅說：「因為妳願意聽，我才回來。」前五天仍沒找出匿名改稿者，卻先修正了自己造成的不當決定。
