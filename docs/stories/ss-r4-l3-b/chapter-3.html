拒絕開口的演員

The Actor Who Would Not Read

被改掉的那句話 · 第 3 / 25 章 · 2000–3000 · 英中對照

用互動版查詞、朗讀與收藏

Nia stopped a rehearsal when she saw the name of a real flood survivor printed in the script. She said an actor should not be asked to tell a private story without checking its owner. Mara realized that her own original draft might have caused harm even before anyone altered it.

妮雅看到劇本印出一位真實水災倖存者的姓名，便停止排練。她說，演員不應在未徵詢本人的情況下說出別人的私事。瑪拉驚覺，即使無人竄改，自己的原稿也可能造成傷害。

Nia's refusal came from an uncomfortable memory of her own. Years earlier, a director had used her family story to make an audition more moving. People praised the scene, but nobody had asked if she wanted it shared. She did not explain every detail to Mara. She only said she knew the feeling.

妮雅的拒絕來自一段不舒服的親身經驗。多年前有導演把她的家庭故事用在試鏡，讓場面更感人。觀眾稱讚，卻沒人問她願不願意公開。妮雅沒有向瑪拉解釋所有細節，只說自己懂那種感覺。

The interview had been recorded for a community history class, not clearly for a public stage. Mara placed the scene in a separate folder and told the company it would remain unused until permission could be confirmed. She did not demand that Nia continue reading.

那段訪談原本是社區歷史課程的紀錄，並未明確授權公開演出。瑪拉把該場戲另存，告訴大家在確認許可前不會使用，也沒有要求妮雅繼續念台詞。

The background of the recording was noisy. A bamboo wind chime knocked against the window, and a bundle of chairs stood by the wall. Two students had begun to chat while a volunteer fixed a chip in the old table. A civil argument followed when a ticket dealer wanted to charge for a free workshop. The disagreement did not deepen, because the teacher insisted that a democratic group must hear every voice. Afterward they went to dine at a cheap restaurant, sharing one doughnut on the way downtown. One child made a leap from a low step and shouted that the chorus sounded mighty. A pigeon landed near the door. In a short poll the children voted for a line about poverty, not for the sake of sadness but to name a real part of their neighborhood. Mara had watched them react with care. Those words gave the play substance before any ticket had been sold.

錄音的背景很吵。竹製風鈴撞著窗，椅子成捆靠牆放著。兩個學生開始聊天，志工則修理舊桌上的缺口。一位票券商想對免費工作坊收費，引發了一場有禮的爭論。老師堅持民主式的團體應聽每個人的聲音，因此爭執沒有加深。之後他們去平價餐館吃飯，途中分吃一個甜甜圈。一名孩子從低台階跳下，喊著副歌聽起來很有力量。一隻鴿子落在門邊。孩子們在簡短投票中選擇加入關於貧困的台詞，不是為了悲傷而悲傷，而是想說出社區的真實樣貌。瑪拉看著他們認真回應。在任何一張票賣出前，那些話已讓戲劇有了實質內容。

Mara removed the marked sheets from the rehearsal pile herself. She asked Nia what kind of part she would still like to play. Nia said she wanted a mother who made decisions rather than one who existed only to suffer. Mara wrote that answer down as a creative direction, not as permission to take more personal stories.

瑪拉親手把標記頁從排練稿裡抽出，問妮雅仍想演什麼樣的角色。妮雅說，她希望母親能做決定，而不是只為受苦而存在。瑪拉把它記為創作方向，並不因此認為自己又獲准拿取更多私事。

Theo suggested that they contact the survivor through the history centre rather than by searching for a private address. Mara agreed. The centre could deliver a request without giving away someone's information. This careful choice cost them a day but gained the cast's trust.

提歐建議透過歷史中心聯繫倖存者，而不是尋找私人住址。瑪拉同意了。中心可以代為傳達請求，不必透露個資。這個謹慎選擇讓他們多花一天，卻贏得劇團信任。

Mara began drafting a letter to the history centre. Her first sentence said that the theatre urgently needed an answer. She read it to Nia, who asked whether urgency belonged to the survivor or to the production schedule. Mara deleted the sentence. The second letter described the proposed scene, explained that refusing would not cost the woman anything, and offered to send the pages before a meeting. Nia stayed until Mara finished. She did not approve the letter for the survivor; she simply said it sounded like a request now.

瑪拉開始寫給歷史中心的信。第一句說劇院急需回覆。她念給妮雅聽，妮雅問，著急的是當事人，還是製作時程？瑪拉刪掉那句。第二封信描述預定場景，說明拒絕不會使對方付出代價，並提出可以在見面前先寄劇本。妮雅留下等她寫完。她沒有代當事人批准，只說這次聽起來像真正的請求了。

That evening, Nia returned to the empty stage. She said she still wanted to act, but not to borrow courage from a person who had never agreed to share it. Mara promised to rewrite the role only after hearing the person behind it.

當晚，妮雅回到空舞台。她說自己仍想演戲，但不願借用一個未曾同意分享故事者的勇氣。瑪拉答應先聽真實當事人的想法，再改寫角色。
