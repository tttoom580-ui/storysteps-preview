拒絕開口的演員

The Actor Who Would Not Read

被改掉的那句話 · 第 3 / 25 章 · 2000–3000 · 英中對照

Nia stopped a rehearsal when she saw the name of a real flood survivor printed in the script. She said an actor should not be asked to tell a private story without checking its owner. Mara realized that her own original draft might have caused harm even before anyone altered it. Mara was learning to separate a question about responsibility from a wish for revenge. A careful account might show that several people had made poor choices for different reasons. If the play pretended that one person caused every loss, it would be easy to follow and hard to trust. She wanted an audience to leave with questions they could use outside the building. Someone passed Mara a warm cup of tea after the meeting. She accepted it and stood quietly by the window. For a few minutes she let herself be tired without deciding that the work was hopeless. Tomorrow's questions could wait until the team had rested.

妮雅看到劇本印出一位真實水災倖存者的姓名，便停止排練。她說，演員不應在未徵詢本人的情況下說出別人的私事。瑪拉驚覺，即使無人竄改，自己的原稿也可能造成傷害。瑪拉慢慢學會把責任問題和報復慾望分開。謹慎的記述可能指出幾個人出於不同理由做了不好的決定。如果劇本假裝所有損失都來自同一個人，雖然容易理解，卻很難令人信任。她希望觀眾離開劇院後，仍能帶走有用的問題。會議後有人遞給瑪拉一杯熱茶。她接過，安靜站在窗邊。幾分鐘裡，她容許自己疲倦，而不因此認定工作無望。明天的問題可以等團隊休息後再處理。

Nia's refusal came from an uncomfortable memory of her own. Years earlier, a director had used her family story to make an audition more moving. People praised the scene, but nobody had asked if she wanted it shared. She did not explain every detail to Mara. She only said she knew the feeling.

妮雅的拒絕來自一段不舒服的親身經驗。多年前有導演把她的家庭故事用在試鏡，讓場面更感人。觀眾稱讚，卻沒人問她願不願意公開。妮雅沒有向瑪拉解釋所有細節，只說自己懂那種感覺。

The interview had been recorded for a community history class, not clearly for a public stage. Mara placed the scene in a separate folder and told the company it would remain unused until permission could be confirmed. She did not demand that Nia continue reading. The question of credit remained on Mara's mind. A name in a program could honor genuine work, but it could also hide the labor of someone who had not been asked. She proposed a simple record of contributions that people could check before publication. June liked the idea because it treated the people behind the curtain as collaborators instead of invisible helpers.

那段訪談原本是社區歷史課程的紀錄，並未明確授權公開演出。瑪拉把該場戲另存，告訴大家在確認許可前不會使用，也沒有要求妮雅繼續念台詞。瑪拉心裡一直掛念署名問題。節目單上的名字可以尊重真正的工作，也可能掩蓋未被徵詢的付出。她提議公開前先讓相關人士核對貢獻紀錄。君喜歡這項提議，因為它把幕後人員視為合作者，而不是看不見的幫手。

The company needed more than a quiet rehearsal room; the city itself offered ideas for movement, costume, and sound. The background of the recording was noisy. A bamboo wind chime knocked against the window, and a bundle of chairs stood by the wall. Two students had begun to chat while a volunteer fixed a chip in the old table. A civil argument followed when a ticket dealer wanted to charge for a free workshop. The disagreement did not deepen, because the teacher insisted that a democratic group must hear every voice. Afterward they went to dine at a cheap restaurant, sharing one doughnut on the way downtown. One child made a leap from a low step and shouted that the chorus sounded mighty. A pigeon landed near the door. In a short poll the children voted for a line about poverty, not for the sake of sadness but to name a real part of their neighborhood. Mara had watched them react with care. Those words gave the play substance before any ticket had been sold.

劇團不能只待在安靜的排練室；整座城市都提供了動作、服裝與聲音的靈感。錄音的背景很吵。竹製風鈴撞著窗，椅子成捆靠牆放著。兩個學生開始聊天，志工則修理舊桌上的缺口。一位票券商想對免費工作坊收費，引發了一場有禮的爭論。老師堅持民主式的團體應聽每個人的聲音，因此爭執沒有加深。之後他們去平價餐館吃飯，途中分吃一個甜甜圈。一名孩子從低台階跳下，喊著副歌聽起來很有力量。一隻鴿子落在門邊。孩子們在簡短投票中選擇加入關於貧困的台詞，不是為了悲傷而悲傷，而是想說出社區的真實樣貌。瑪拉看著他們認真回應。在任何一張票賣出前，那些話已讓戲劇有了實質內容。

Mara removed the marked sheets from the rehearsal pile herself. She asked Nia what kind of part she would still like to play. Nia said she wanted a mother who made decisions rather than one who existed only to suffer. Mara wrote that answer down as a creative direction, not as permission to take more personal stories.

瑪拉親手把標記頁從排練稿裡抽出，問妮雅仍想演什麼樣的角色。妮雅說，她希望母親能做決定，而不是只為受苦而存在。瑪拉把它記為創作方向，並不因此認為自己又獲准拿取更多私事。

Theo suggested that they contact the survivor through the history centre rather than by searching for a private address. Mara agreed. The centre could deliver a request without giving away someone's information. This careful choice cost them a day but gained the cast's trust. The room was not as simple as it looked. Each chair had a person who needed to trust the people onstage, and every actor brought a different reason for taking part. Mara watched those reasons appear in small movements: a page turned slowly, a hand stayed raised, or someone waited before answering. She was learning that listening could be visible, even during silence. They looked again at the paper on the table. The important part was not how many people agreed immediately but whether anyone could point to what had actually happened. Mara wrote one more question under the date and kept the answer space open.

提歐建議透過歷史中心聯繫倖存者，而不是尋找私人住址。瑪拉同意了。中心可以代為傳達請求，不必透露個資。這個謹慎選擇讓他們多花一天，卻贏得劇團信任。排練室沒有表面上那麼簡單。每張椅子上都有人需要信任舞台上的工作者，每位演員也有不同的參與理由。瑪拉從細小動作中看見那些理由：有人緩緩翻頁、有人持續舉手，也有人在回答前先等待。她開始明白，傾聽即使沒有聲音，也能讓人看見。他們再看桌上的紙。重要的不是有多少人立刻同意，而是能否指出實際發生了什麼事。瑪拉在日期下多寫一個問題，留下尚未填寫的答案欄。

That evening, Nia returned to the empty stage. She said she still wanted to act, but not to borrow courage from a person who had never agreed to share it. Mara promised to rewrite the role only after hearing the person behind it. Theo studied the marks made by the stage lights on the wooden floor. The smallest change in position could move a shadow across a speaker's face. He liked machines because their actions could often be tested. People were harder. A person might change a story out of care, shame, fear, or ambition, and the same result could hide very different reasons. He told Mara not to skip that difference.

當晚，妮雅回到空舞台。她說自己仍想演戲，但不願借用一個未曾同意分享故事者的勇氣。瑪拉答應先聽真實當事人的想法，再改寫角色。提歐研究舞台燈在木地板上形成的痕跡。位置只要有小小變動，陰影就可能移到說話者臉上。他喜歡機器，因為許多動作可以測試；人卻更難理解。同一項改動，可能來自關心、羞愧、恐懼或野心，結果相同，理由卻很不同。他提醒瑪拉不要忽略這點。
