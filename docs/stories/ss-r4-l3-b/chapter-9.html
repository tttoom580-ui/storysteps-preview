贊助商的邀請

The Sponsor's Invitation

被改掉的那句話 · 第 9 / 25 章 · 2000–3000 · 英中對照 · 校訂中

Vale Development invited the company to a private reception. Its owner, Mr. Vale, praised the theatre's talent and offered to pay for new seats. Then he asked Mara whether the river-gate scene could become a general story about bad weather instead of human decisions.

韋爾開發公司邀請劇團參加私人招待會。負責人韋爾先生讚美他們的才能，提議出資換新座椅，接著問瑪拉能否把水閘一幕改成單純描述壞天氣，而不談人的決定。

Vale spoke in a room with soft chairs and beautiful pictures of the town. Mara found herself remembering the theatre's cracked seats and unpaid bills. She understood why his offer was tempting. Still, he had asked for silence before anyone had discussed the evidence, and that detail would stay in her notes.

韋爾在一間有柔軟椅子、掛著小鎮美景的房間說話。瑪拉不禁想起劇院龜裂的座椅和未付帳單，明白為何這項提議誘人。然而他在討論證據之前就要求沉默，這個細節會留下紀錄。

Mara asked whether his company had worked on the river gate. Vale answered yes, years earlier, but said the past was too complicated for a play. She replied that complexity was a reason to check the facts, not to erase the question.

瑪拉問他的公司是否曾參與水閘工程。韋爾承認多年前有參與，卻說往事太複雜，不適合搬上舞台。她回答，事情複雜更應查證，而不是抹去問題。

A strong wind made the café door roar. Near the window stood a toy cabin with a missing door, part of a school model. A horn sounded outside, and a child’s laughter briefly broke the tension. A music teacher called one rehearsal bore after another a test of patience. Mei had once felt faint after working too long, but the fright had taught her to take breaks. She was skillful with records and could explain how a file might sink beneath hundreds of later entries. A visitor made a splash by dropping a cup into the sink. Two students discussed whether a lover in a play would wander away, while a prop maker carried a harmless cardboard weapon across the room. “A database is not a witness by itself,” Mei said. “It is a tool. People still must explain the changes.” Mara appreciated the tune of that careful answer. A volunteer cut a finger on a sheet of paper and began to bleed slightly, so Mei brought a bandage. Shortly afterward she returned to the records. “Democracy needs patient records as well as public debate,” she told the group. June scheduled a weekly meeting so the cast could discuss revisions before opening night.

強風讓咖啡館的門發出低吼。窗邊有一間少了門的模型小屋，屬於學校作品。外頭響起喇叭聲，孩子的笑聲短暫打破緊張。音樂老師說，一次次無聊的排練是在考驗耐心。梅以前工作太久，曾經快昏倒，那次驚嚇使她學會休息。她很擅長整理紀錄，也能解釋檔案怎麼會沉在數百筆新資料之下。一位客人把杯子掉進水槽，水花四濺。兩名學生討論戲中的戀人會不會走開，道具師則拿著無害的紙板武器經過。「資料庫本身不是證人，」梅說，「它只是工具，仍需要人來解釋變更。」瑪拉欣賞這個謹慎回答的語調。 一位志工被紙邊割到手指，流了一點血，梅便拿來繃帶。不久之後她回到紀錄前，告訴大家：「民主除了公開辯論，也需要耐心保存的紀錄。」君排定每週會議，讓演員能在首演前討論修改。

Outside the reception, Mara suggested that Theo send his account of the meeting to himself before discussing it with her. That way they would have two memories rather than one story made in agreement. Theo smiled at the suggestion, and they compared notes only afterward.

走出招待會，瑪拉建議提歐先把自己記得的內容寄給自己，再跟她討論。這樣他們會有兩份獨立記憶，而不是一起協商出來的故事。提歐笑著接受，兩人之後才交換筆記。

Theo noticed a copy of the black-square draft on Vale's desk. He did not touch it. Outside, he told Mara exactly what he had seen and what he had not seen. They agreed to record the meeting immediately while their memories were clear.

提歐在韋爾桌上看見一份帶黑方塊的改稿，但沒有碰它。離開後，他向瑪拉清楚說明自己看見與沒看見的事。兩人同意趁記憶清晰立即記下會面內容。

At the reception door a photographer asked Mara and Theo to stand beneath Vale's logo. Mara agreed to a company photograph only after asking how it would be captioned. The photographer showed her a prepared line calling the play a celebration of the development project. She declined that caption and suggested a plain record of the visit. Vale's assistant said they could settle it later. Mara stepped away from the marked spot. She had begun to understand that a description beneath a picture could change a story as effectively as a deleted speech.

酒會門口的攝影師請瑪拉和提歐站到韋爾公司的標誌下。瑪拉先問照片將配什麼說明，才考慮合照。攝影師拿出預備文字，把戲稱為開發計畫的慶祝演出。她拒絕這句，建議只記錄參訪。韋爾的助手說可以之後再談，瑪拉便離開地上的拍攝記號。她開始明白，照片下的說明也能像刪掉台詞一樣，改變故事的意思。

Vale's assistant sent a friendly message that night: the sponsor wanted a positive ending. Mara realized the dispute was not simply about a missing line. Someone was trying to decide which kinds of truth the audience would be allowed to hear.

當晚韋爾的助理送來親切訊息：贊助商希望有正面的結局。瑪拉發現爭議不只是少了幾句台詞；有人想替觀眾決定哪些真相能被聽見。
