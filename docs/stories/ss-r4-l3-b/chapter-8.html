A Witness Who Changed Her Mind

A former volunteer named Rina arrived to see the rehearsal. She had once described the flood for Mara, but she now wanted her words removed. Rina explained that her children did not know the story and might hear it from strangers at the festival.

昔日志工莉娜來看排練。她先前受訪談過水災，如今希望刪去自己的話。她說孩子們還不知道那段經歷，不願他們在藝術節從陌生人口中聽見。

Rina had brought a list of the passages she wanted withdrawn. Mara read it silently and did not question the woman about her children. Behind them, a rehearsal bell rang. Mara asked June to pause the next scene. A schedule was not a reason to ignore a person standing in front of them.

莉娜帶來一份希望撤回的段落清單。瑪拉默讀，沒有追問她的孩子。身後排練鈴響起，瑪拉請君暫停下一幕。排程不能成為無視眼前當事人的理由。

Mara told Rina she could withdraw without explaining further. She asked the stage manager to mark the affected scene as closed. An audience could learn about the river gate without being given every detail of one family.

瑪拉告訴莉娜，想撤回就可以，不必進一步解釋。她請舞台監督把相關場景標示為暫停使用。觀眾可以認識水閘事件，卻不需要知道每個家庭的私事。

The market displayed bags and other baggage for travellers. A painted buffalo watched over the entrance; a toy bullet sat safely behind glass in a history stall. One citizen demonstrated a remote controller, while a cooker offered crispy rice cakes. Mara had lately learned to grab every useful detail, not every rumor. A sign warned against litter. She still did not know whether the office owed her an apology, but she knew it owed her a clear process. Permission to view the old web page came from its owner. A volunteer described the steps she had taken to post the original request for songs. The page also linked to a workshop on sexual harassment prevention; Mara carefully kept that unrelated topic out of her public claim. A talkative merchant shouted across the street at a wealthy tourist, while another seller remained in the shadow of a tent. The noise made June want to yell, yet the evidence remained quiet on the screen. “We owe the volunteer a proper thank-you,” June said. Mara agreed and added her name to the message.

市集擺出旅客的手提包和行李。入口上方畫著水牛；歷史攤位的玩具子彈安全地陳列在玻璃櫃裡。一位市民展示遙控器，炊具攤販提供香脆米餅。瑪拉最近學會抓住每個有用細節，而不是每則傳聞。牌子提醒大家勿亂丟垃圾。她還不知道辦公室是否欠她道歉，但知道辦公室有義務提供清楚程序。舊網頁的主人同意她們查閱。志工說明當初張貼徵歌公告的過程。頁面也連到防治性騷擾的工作坊；瑪拉小心不把無關主題混進公開主張。一名健談商人對富有的遊客隔街大喊，另一名攤販則留在帳篷陰影裡。吵鬧使君想大叫，螢幕上的證據卻安靜得很。 「我們欠那位志工一聲正式的謝謝，」君說。瑪拉同意，也在訊息上署名。

The revised scene described the flooded street from a distance. It no longer contained Rina's living room or family details. Nia tested the lines and found that the distance did not make the fear unreal. Sometimes the audience could understand a danger without being invited into a stranger's private house.

修改後的場景從遠處描述淹水的街道，不再包含莉娜的客廳與家人細節。妮雅試讀後發現，距離不會讓恐懼變得不真實。有時觀眾不必闖進陌生人的私人住家，也能理解危險。

Rina looked at the blue-dot note on the page and said it had been written before she called. Someone in the theatre had worried about permission even before the witness made the request. This did not prove innocence, but it changed the meaning of the clue.

莉娜看見頁上的藍點字條，指出那是在她來電之前寫的。劇團內有人比證人更早擔心授權問題。這無法證明那個人毫無過錯，卻改變了線索的意義。

June checked the cupboard for extra copies of Rina's scene. There were two in a costume folder and one clipped behind a music sheet. Mara helped collect them before changing the rehearsal schedule. She had thought withdrawing words meant crossing out a paragraph in her own file. The scattered copies showed how quickly a story could travel beyond its speaker. June made a list of the copies returned and asked the actors to bring back any others. Rina would receive a clear account of what had been removed.

君查看櫃子裡是否還有莉娜那場戲的副本，結果服裝夾裡有兩份，樂譜後面又夾了一份。瑪拉先幫忙收回，再改排練表。她本以為撤回內容只要在自己的檔案劃掉一段。散在各處的副本讓她看見，故事離開說話者後傳得多快。君列出收回的份數，請演員交回其他副本。莉娜將會收到清楚的撤除紀錄。

That evening Celeste complained about the shrinking script. Mara answered with a shorter scene built from documents already approved for public use. Nia read it aloud. It contained fewer sentences, but every sentence could stand in the light.

當晚席莉絲抱怨劇本越來越短。瑪拉交出依據已核准公開資料寫成的較短場景。妮雅念出來，句子雖少，每一句卻都經得起檢視。
