被人拿走的台詞

The Lines Someone Took Away

被改掉的那句話 · 第 1 / 25 章 · 2000–3000 · 英中對照

用互動版查詞、朗讀與收藏

Mara Chen, twenty-six, returned to Harbor Lantern Theatre carrying the eighth draft of her play, When the River Rose. At rehearsal, the actress Nia raised an empty sheet. Every line spoken by the mother character had vanished from the new copies. Mara had not approved the changes.

二十六歲的陳瑪拉帶著《河水漲起時》第八版劇本回到港燈劇院。排練時，女演員妮雅舉起一張幾乎空白的紙：母親角色的所有台詞都從新印本裡消失了。瑪拉從未同意這些修改。

Mara had written the mother as a woman who stood in a doorway during a flood and refused to leave a neighbor behind. The missing speech was not a clever ending. It was the moment when she asked why the official warning had come so late. Without it, the character became a face in the crowd.

瑪拉筆下的母親在洪水時站在門口，拒絕丟下鄰居。被刪的台詞不是華麗結局，而是她問出官方警告為何來得太晚的時刻。少了那句話，這個角色只剩人群裡的一張臉。

Theo Peng, the twenty-nine-year-old lighting designer, found two signs on a discarded page: a small blue pencil dot and a black square printed beside a deleted sentence. Mara photographed both before anyone moved the papers. She decided to investigate instead of accusing the nearest person.

二十九歲的燈光設計師彭提歐在廢紙上發現兩種記號：藍色鉛筆小點，以及刪除句子旁列印的黑色方塊。瑪拉先拍照留存，沒有立刻指責身邊的人。

Outside the theatre, the festival square was crowded with various performers. A jazz trio practiced beside a wooden hut that served as a ticket booth. Nearby, a small circus troupe unloaded ropes while a child in a fairy costume chased a magician wearing a fancy purple hat. The child cheered when a coin vanished. "A miracle!" she cried. Mara wished the missing lines could return as easily. She held a cup of berry tea and tried to tame the shake in her hands. The volunteer had told her the thermos held a pint; now it was nearly empty, like the patience she had brought to rehearsal. A heater clicked beneath the booth. Theo bent to secure its loose screw, then pointed to the new festival program. Beneath Mara's name was a statement that the revised script had her approval. It was not merely an advertising error. It made her name look like a deed authorizing the transfer of her own words. A jet crossed above the roof as the band began another chorus. The announcer asked the crowd to stay for a political speech, and music threatened to spin into an argument. Mara would not rob the other artists of their opening night, but she would not quit asking who had changed the program. She photographed the false statement before the stage manager gave the signal to begin. "We can correct it without accusing anyone yet," Theo said. For the first time that evening, Mara knew exactly what evidence to keep.

劇院外的節慶廣場擠滿了各式表演者。一支爵士三重奏在兼作售票亭的小木屋旁練習。附近的小馬戲團正卸下繩索，一個穿仙子服的孩子追著戴華麗紫帽的魔術師跑。硬幣消失時，孩子歡呼：「奇蹟！」瑪拉真希望被刪的台詞也能這麼容易回來。她捧著莓果茶，試著壓住雙手的顫抖。志工說保溫壺原有一品脫的茶，如今幾乎見底，像她帶來排練的耐心。售票亭下的暖爐喀噠一響；提歐彎腰鎖緊一顆鬆動的螺絲，隨即指向新印的節目單。瑪拉的名字下竟寫著「修訂版已獲作者同意」。那不只是宣傳上的錯誤，而像一紙契據，冒用她的姓名把作品轉讓出去。噴射機掠過屋頂，樂團又唱起副歌。主持人請觀眾留下聽一場政治演說，現場的音樂眼看就要變成爭論。瑪拉不願奪走其他藝術家的首演夜，卻也不打算放棄追問誰改了節目單。舞台監督發出開演訊號前，她先拍下那句不實文字。「現在有證據了，不必急著指控任何人。」提歐說。那晚第一次，瑪拉知道該保存什麼。

Nia placed the empty page beside the complete one. She told Mara that an actress could suggest changes, but she needed to know which words she was being asked to defend. Mara promised not to demand loyalty before offering an explanation. The promise left both of them more exposed and more willing to continue.

妮雅把空白頁放在完整稿旁，說演員可以提出改動，但必須知道自己被要求捍衛哪些文字。瑪拉答應不會在說明之前就要求忠誠。這項承諾讓兩人都更無法躲避問題，卻也更願意繼續。

Director Celeste said the new version would be easier for the sponsor to support. Mara answered that easy did not mean honest. She asked the cast to read her original scene once, without making any public claim about who had edited it.

導演席莉絲說，新版本比較容易獲得贊助商支持。瑪拉回答，容易不代表誠實。她請演員先按原稿讀過一次，在弄清改稿者前不公開指控。

June asked the actors to put their copies face down. Someone might already have made a note beside the missing speech, and Mara did not want a new mark mistaken for an old one. Nia offered her rehearsal copy, with a tea stain across the corner, as the first comparison. Mara recognized the stain from Friday. The familiar accident helped her breathe. There were ordinary ways to answer this problem. They would begin with the words that had been there, and then find out how the empty space had arrived.

君請演員把各自的劇本朝下放好。有人也許已在消失的台詞旁做了註記，瑪拉不希望新記號被誤認為舊線索。妮雅拿出角落有茶漬的排練本，讓大家先比對。瑪拉認出那是星期五留下的茶漬。這個熟悉的小意外讓她喘了口氣。眼前問題有平實的查證方法。她們先確認原來的文字，再追查空白如何出現。

Nia spoke the mother's final question, and the room went quiet. For the first time, Mara heard how much that missing voice mattered. On the back of the deleted page somebody had written, ASK HER FIRST. The handwriting was careful, not threatening.

妮雅念出母親角色最後一句問話，室內頓時安靜。瑪拉第一次真正聽出那段失去的聲音有多重要。被刪頁背面有人寫著「先問她本人」，字跡工整，並沒有威脅意味。
