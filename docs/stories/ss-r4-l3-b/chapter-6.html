The Paper Drawer

June took Mara to the locked office drawer where spare paper was stored. Some sheets carried a faint mark from the printer's worn wheel. The black-square draft had exactly that mark. The finding connected the official office printer to the changes, not yet a particular person.

君帶瑪拉去看放備用紙的上鎖抽屜。某些紙張有印表機磨損滾輪留下的淺痕。帶黑方塊的改稿正好也有相同痕跡。這證明稿件來自辦公室印表機，還不能證明是誰操作。

The office drawer smelled of paper and machine oil. June showed which staff members had keys and which visitors were allowed to borrow them. Mara found no reason to turn the list into a list of suspects yet. The important first question was who could have printed the pages, not who looked guilty.

辦公室抽屜有紙張和機油的味道。君說明哪些員工有鑰匙、哪些訪客可借用。瑪拉還不打算把這份名單直接變成嫌疑人名單。眼前重要的是誰有能力列印那些頁面，而非誰看起來有罪。

The office had a repair request from the previous week. Theo photographed the page number rather than taking the repair book. He reminded Mara that even useful evidence could become doubtful if they lost track of where it came from.

辦公室留有上週的設備維修單。提歐只拍下頁碼，沒有擅自帶走維修簿。他提醒瑪拉，若不記清來源，再有用的證據也可能失去可信度。

The community centre was holding a tropical science fair that afternoon. A teenage creator demonstrated how bacteria multiply on stale food; a wax model showed the inner parts of the stomach. Beside it, students displayed a toy missile made of paper and a poster explaining why real weapons were not a solution to political problems. An older politician admired their work and called education a necessity. Mara, who had promised to help, used a simple recording technique to make a summary of each talk. She did not assume that excellence belonged only to people with expensive equipment. When a child gave a sudden bump to her microphone, she caught it before it fell. A teacher asked whether the suspect in a school mystery play was really guilty. “Anyhow,” Mara answered, “we need evidence before a verdict.” The fair reminded her that a resource is useful only when people can reach it.

當天下午，社區中心舉辦熱帶主題科學展。一位青少年創作者示範細菌如何在不新鮮的食物上繁殖；蠟製模型展示胃的內部構造。旁邊的學生展示紙做的玩具飛彈，海報說明真正的武器無法解決政治問題。一名年長的政治人物稱讚作品，說教育是一種必需。瑪拉答應協助，便用簡單錄音技巧替每場說明做摘要。她不認為只有昂貴設備才能創造優秀成果。孩子忽然碰撞她的麥克風時，她趕緊扶住。老師問起校園推理劇中的嫌疑人是否真有罪。「無論如何，」瑪拉回答，「我們得先有證據才能下結論。」這場展覽提醒她，資源唯有人人可取得才有價值。

Theo offered to compare the worn wheel with older theater programs. Mara said yes, but only if he did not change or clean the printer first. He laughed at her caution, then agreed. A machine might remember a pattern that the people around it preferred to forget.

提歐提議把磨損滾輪痕跡與舊節目單比較。瑪拉答應，條件是他先不要清洗或調整印表機。提歐笑她謹慎，最後仍同意。有時機器留下的規律，比旁邊的人更不容易忘記。

Celeste appeared before they left. She said she could explain everything after opening night. Mara replied that the cast needed safe, accurate material before opening night. She gave Celeste a written list of questions, including who had authorized printing.

席莉絲在他們離開前出現，說自己可以等首演後再解釋。瑪拉回答，演員在首演前就需要安全、正確的文本。她交出書面問題，包括誰授權列印。

At the bottom of the repair book, June found a sample sheet left by the technician. It showed the same faint stripe in three different shades of ink. Theo explained that changing the cartridge would not remove a mark caused by the wheel. He placed a fresh sheet beside the sample without printing another copy. Mara could compare the stripe while leaving the machine untouched. She asked June to include the sample's date in their request to the office manager. Now their question concerned a documented fault rather than a suggestive stain.

維修簿底下夾著技師留下的測試紙，同一道淡紋以三種墨色出現。提歐解釋，更換墨匣不能消除滾輪造成的痕跡。他沒有再印一張，而是把白紙放在測試紙旁。瑪拉可以比對條紋，又不必動機器。她請君把測試日期加進給辦公室經理的請求。現在她們追查的是有紀錄的故障，而不只是一道令人猜疑的污痕。

In the corridor June noticed blue graphite on her own sleeve and pulled her arm away. Mara saw the movement but did not make an accusation. A mark could point toward a writer; it could not tell her the writer's purpose.

在走廊上，君發現自己袖口沾到藍色石墨，立刻縮手。瑪拉注意到了，卻沒有指控。痕跡可能指向書寫者，卻無法自行說明寫字的用意。
