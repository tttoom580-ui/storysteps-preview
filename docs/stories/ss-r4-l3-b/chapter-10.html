藍色鉛筆的主人

The Blue Pencil's Owner

被改掉的那句話 · 第 10 / 25 章 · 2000–3000 · 英中對照 · 校訂中

June asked to speak with Mara and Nia before rehearsal. She placed a short blue pencil on the table and admitted that the blue notes were hers. She had seen private family details in the script and marked them, but had been afraid to sign her name.

排練前，君請瑪拉和妮雅談話。她把一枝短藍鉛筆放在桌上，承認藍色註記是自己寫的。她看見劇本含有家庭隱私而做記號，卻一直不敢署名。

June's blue pencil was almost too short to hold. She said she had used it for years because her grandmother gave it to her when she began stage work. Mara had expected the confession to sound dangerous. Instead it came with the tired embarrassment of someone who had waited too long to speak openly.

君的藍鉛筆短得快握不住了。她說那是祖母在她開始舞台工作時送的，用了很多年。瑪拉原本以為坦承會帶著危險氣息，結果只看見一個太晚公開說話的人，疲倦又難為情。

June had not removed the pages; she had sent her warnings to Celeste. The next day a fully printed script appeared, carrying unrelated black cuts. June had kept her marked copy because she worried that the two actions would be confused.

君沒有抽掉頁面，而是把提醒交給席莉絲。隔天出現了一本完整印好的新稿，裡面還有與隱私無關的黑色刪改。君保存自己的註記本，因為擔心兩種行動被混為一談。

In the garden a bride in a fashionable silk dress posed for photographs. Her father held a melon and joked that it was a bold wedding gift. A cook lit a small flame under a portable stove. A guest offered a handkerchief to a child who had dropped a drink. The scene looked optimistic, but Celeste could not enjoy it. She had tried to impress her manager by acting quickly and now feared she had made a moral mistake. Mara was determined not to punch down at a junior worker for obeying a confusing order. A woman nearby said an angel had blessed the couple; somehow the words gave Celeste brief relief. A teacher explained the structure of a marriage song while students stared at the tide beyond the park. Mara wanted the truth more than a pleasing story. Celeste would not determine who was guilty, but she could explain what she entered. She tried not to stare at Mara as if asking her to make the decision for everyone. Nia added a small pearl pin to the mother character's coat and asked whether the detail was appropriate.

花園裡，一名新娘穿著時髦絲質禮服拍照。父親拿著哈密瓜，開玩笑說這是大膽的結婚禮物。廚師在便攜爐下點起小火，客人把手帕遞給打翻飲料的孩子。場面充滿樂觀氣氛，席莉絲卻無心欣賞。她曾想用迅速辦事給主管留下好印象，如今擔心犯了道德上的錯。瑪拉決定不對層級較低、遵守混亂指示的員工揮拳責難。附近有人說天使保佑這對新人；不知怎地，這句話讓席莉絲稍感寬慰。老師解說婚禮歌曲的結構，學生望著公園外的潮水發呆。瑪拉要的是事實，不是好聽的故事。 席莉絲不會決定誰有罪，卻能說明自己輸入過什麼。她努力不直盯瑪拉，彷彿要求對方替所有人作決定。妮雅在母親角色外套別上小小的珍珠胸針，並詢問這個細節是否合宜。

Mara placed the blue notes beside the black pages. She told June that useful criticism deserved a signature, because other people should be able to ask what a note meant. June nodded. The two women did not call the earlier secrecy harmless, but they found a way to correct it.

瑪拉把藍點註記放在黑色印本旁，告訴君有用的批評值得署名，因為其他人應有權詢問註記的意思。君點頭。兩人沒有把過去的匿名當成無害，卻找到了修正方式。

Mara thanked June for raising the consent problem but asked her to sign future editorial notes. Secret warnings made useful questions harder to discuss. June agreed. Nia said she would return to the mother's role if the new permissions remained clear.

瑪拉感謝君指出授權問題，也請她今後在編輯意見上署名。匿名提醒讓重要議題更難討論。君同意了；妮雅則表示，只要授權清楚，她願意繼續演母親。

Nia asked June to sign a new note while they were all present. June hesitated, then wrote her name beside a specific request to remove a private address. Mara added a reply explaining where the change would appear. This exchange filled only a corner of the page, yet it looked completely different from the mysterious warning. June could disagree with the writer without disappearing behind a symbol. Mara could accept a correction without imagining that her entire play had been stolen. They left the signed note on the working copy for rehearsal.

妮雅請君當著大家簽一張新註記。君猶豫片刻，便在要求刪去私人地址的具體建議旁署名。瑪拉回覆，說明改動將出現在哪裡。這次交換只佔頁角，卻和神祕警告完全不同。君可以不同意作家，而不必藏在符號後面；瑪拉也可以接受更正，而不覺得整齣戲都遭竊。他們把簽名註記留在排練的工作稿上。

After several rehearsals, Mara could now explain the blue marks. The black edits remained unexplained. She wrote two headings on the rehearsal board: PROTECTION and SILENCE, leaving a space beneath each for evidence.

經過數次排練，瑪拉已能解釋藍點來源，黑色刪改卻仍無答案。她在排練板上寫下兩個標題：「保護」與「噤聲」，各自留出證據的位置。
