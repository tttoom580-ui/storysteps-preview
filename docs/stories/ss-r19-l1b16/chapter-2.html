Aria had been thinking about simple things in the indoor swimming pool when a detail refused to fit the old picture. For a moment, everyone around seemed busy with something else. Aria tried not to rush, because a wrong guess could make the problem harder for someone nearby. The training notebook was close at hand, ready for the facts that would be easy to forget once people began talking. For a while, Aria and Kit stood beside the training notebook and talked in low voices. Neither wanted a loud argument to take the place of useful work. They tried to say exactly what they knew.
