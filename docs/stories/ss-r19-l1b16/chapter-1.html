勁敵之間的規則

The Rule Between Rivals

決賽那天，對手把幸運星交給了我 · 第 1 / 25 章 · 1000以下 · 英中對照 · 校訂中

At the north end of the indoor pool, twenty-year-old Aria stood beside lane six and watched the red light on the race camera. The other swimmers were warming up. A friendly woman from the sports office handed out juice and asked whether anyone had noticed a bug in the timing screen. Aria did not look at the screen. She was watching Ren in the next lane. In a month they would meet in the national college final, and they had made a strange rule: no talking to each other until the race was over. She thought silence would help her focus. Now it felt like a wall.

在室內泳池北端，二十歲的艾莉亞站在第六泳道旁，看著競賽相機上的紅燈。其他選手正在熱身。體育辦公室一名友善女士分送果汁，問是否有人注意到計時螢幕出現小故障。艾莉亞沒有看螢幕，她望著隔壁泳道的任。一個月後，他們將在全國大學決賽交手。兩人訂了一條奇怪規則：比賽結束前不和對方說話。她原以為沉默能幫助專心，如今卻覺得它像一道牆。

Coach Hana called Aria to the middle of the pool deck. “Your last turn was too wide,” she said. “You lose time because you copy Ren's way of moving. You do not have the same body or the same strength.” Aria wanted to argue. Ren was famous, and she had trained hard to reach his time. But she knew the coach was telling the truth. One fact was clear: her own turn was faster when she stayed close to the wall. Coach Hana asked her to try it three times, not twenty. The first try felt odd. The second was better. On the third, Aria smiled. A little note used “lie” as its key word; Aria kept the note because the rest of the line was still useful.

花教練把艾莉亞叫到泳池邊中央。「你剛才轉身太大了，」她說，「你一直模仿任，所以浪費時間。你們的身體與優勢不同。」艾莉亞想反駁。任很有名，她也一直努力追上他的成績。但她知道教練說的是實話。有一件事很清楚：靠近池壁時，她自己的轉身更快。花教練請她試三次，不是二十次。第一次很不習慣，第二次好多了，第三次艾莉亞笑了。一張小筆記以「lie」為關鍵字；艾莉亞留下它，因為剩下的句子仍然有用。

After practice, Aria found a little paper star beside her training notebook. There was no name on it. The note inside said, Remember why you swim.* She looked toward Ren, but he was already leaving. Aria did not want to guess that he had left it. A young swimmer walked past with a red star sticker on her bag. “We make stars at our club,” the child said. “My pet dog eats paper, so I keep mine here.” Aria laughed at the story. The little star seemed less like magic and more like something a friend might make. Aria circled “notice” on a reading sheet, not to win a test but to remember the meaning of the whole sentence.

練習後，艾莉亞在訓練筆記本旁找到一顆小紙星星。上面沒有名字。她打開字條，讀到：「記得你為什麼游泳。」她望向任，但他已經離開。艾莉亞不想隨便認定是他留下的。一名年幼泳者走過，袋子上貼著紅星。「我們社團會摺星星，」孩子說，「我的寵物狗會吃紙，所以我把星星貼在這裡。」艾莉亞被逗笑了。小星星看起來不再像魔法，更像朋友可能做的小禮物。艾莉亞在閱讀單上圈出「notice」，不是為了考高分，而是為了理解整句話。

The coach asked Aria whether she wanted to join the community pool picnic on Saturday. Aria nearly said no. She wanted to use every free hour for extra training. Then she remembered how tired she had felt at the end of the last race. “I can come,” she answered, “but I need to rest first.” The coach nodded. Aria knew that reaching a good future would not depend on being in the water all day. She put the star between the pages of her notebook, where she could see it without carrying it into the pool. She still wanted to win, very much. The word “this” appeared in an example someone had written earlier, and Kit asked if the example fit their situation.

教練問艾莉亞星期六願不願意參加社區泳池野餐。艾莉亞差點說不，她想用每個空檔加練。但她想起上次比賽結束時有多累。「我可以參加，」她回答，「但得先休息。」教練點頭。艾莉亞知道想迎向美好未來，不代表整天都得泡在水裡。她把紙星星夾在筆記本裡，這樣能看見它，卻不用帶進泳池。她還是很想贏，非常想。「this」出現在前人留下的例句裡，琪問這個例子是否適合現在的情況。

Near the showers, two fans were making a video about the swimmers. One said that Ren had no real rival. Aria stopped for a moment and felt the old anger rise. She wanted to show them the times in her book, but she did not. A time could say how fast she had swum, not what kind of person she was. She noticed a small line on the wall: Use the pool safely. Respect the other swimmers.* It was there for everyone, not just champions. Aria turned away from the camera and went to dry her hair. She would answer in the water, with her own turn.

淋浴間附近，兩名粉絲正拍泳者影片。其中一人說任根本沒有真正的對手。艾莉亞停了一下，舊怒火又冒出來。她想拿筆記本裡的成績給他們看，最後卻沒有。成績能說明她游多快，卻無法定義她是什麼樣的人。她注意到牆上寫著一行小字：「安全使用泳池，尊重其他泳者。」這是寫給所有人的，不只是冠軍。艾莉亞轉身離開相機，去把頭髮擦乾。她會在水中，用自己的轉身回答。

That evening, Aria sat by the window and opened the star again. She thought about the rule she and Ren had made. No talking before the final was simple, but it made every small act look like a secret. She could not know who had folded the star or why. She did know she wanted to swim for the feeling of moving freely, not only to stand above someone else on a list. She wrote that thought under the note. Then she closed her training book. The paper star stayed inside, and in the morning she would return to the pool to try her own turn again.

當晚，艾莉亞坐在窗邊，再次打開紙星星。她想起自己和任訂的規則。決賽前不說話很簡單，卻讓每個小動作都像藏著祕密。她不知道誰摺了星星，也不知道為什麼。但她知道自己想游泳，是為了自由前進的感覺，而不只是排名在別人上面。她把這個念頭寫在字條下面，然後闔上訓練本。紙星星留在裡頭。隔天早上，她會再回泳池練習屬於自己的轉身。
