The Cost of a Prediction

雙時代之鐘 · 第 14 / 25 章 · 3000–4000 · 英中對照 · 校訂完成

用互動版查詞、朗讀與收藏

To gain access to a sealed registry in 1945, Yicheng visited a former acquaintance of his father who served on a local council. The man was proud of his reputation and worried about public criticism. He understood the damaged economy but had little tolerance for aggressive young people demanding immediate answers. Yicheng did not argue. Instead, he showed him copies of letters proving that immigrants to the city had contributed to rebuilding schools and markets. The councilor examined the postage stamps, asked careful questions, and gradually became keen to help.

為了進入1945年被封存的登記室，以誠拜訪父親一位在地方議會服務的舊識。 那人很看重名聲，害怕公眾批評；他懂戰後經濟的困境，卻不太能忍受激進年輕人要求立即回答。以誠沒有爭辯，只拿出信件副本，證明移居這座城市的人也參與重建學校和市場。議員細看郵票，問了許多問題，漸漸願意幫忙。

The registry held an exhibit of earlier city plans, along with a competitive contest for designing new streets. On one display, someone had written a formula predicting which neighborhoods would prosper. The prediction relied on figures collected before the war, yet ignored the families who had arrived afterward. Zhaoyue recognized the danger at once. “If numbers cannot see a person,” she said, “they may end up making rules that exclude that person.” Moreover, the clock's own map had made similar errors. It turned people into bright points and empty circles. Accurate information required more than a clever formula.

登記室陳列著早年的都市規畫，也正舉辦新街道設計競賽。 一塊展板上寫著預測哪些地區會繁榮的公式，但資料全取自戰前，忽略後來才抵達的家庭。昭月立刻看出危險：「若數字看不見一個人，用數字訂出的規則就可能把那人排除。」而且銅鐘地圖也有相似問題，只把人化成亮點與空圈。精確的資料需要的，絕不只是聰明的公式。

They found a ledger referring to Huang's business partners. The names linked the 1895 theatre to a 1945 company that sold old documents privately. A clerk offered to refer them to the director, but warned of a heavy penalty for copying the pages. Yicheng hesitated. Then the councilor quietly placed his official pass on the table and invited them to make a supervised copy. “The purpose of rules should be to reward honesty,” he said. “Sometimes the rule itself needs to be examined.” Zhaoyue thanked him, surprised that someone with authority could listen rather than command.

他們找到一本帳簿，列出黃某的生意夥伴。 名單把1895年的戲院連到一家1945年私下販售舊文件的公司。書記員願意轉介主管，卻警告擅自抄錄會受重罰。以誠正猶豫，議員把公務證件放上桌，請他們在監督下複印。「規則的目的應該是獎勵誠實，」他說，「有時規則本身也該受檢驗。」昭月向他道謝，意外發現掌權者也能選擇傾聽而非命令。

The councilor told Zhaoyue about his family's decision to immigrate to the city many years earlier. They had arrived poor and unfamiliar with local habits, and neighbors had sometimes made them feel ashamed of their accent. An older shopkeeper had helped them find work without demanding praise. “My family prospered because someone gave us room to begin,” he said. “I would rather be remembered for doing the same.” The story made Zhaoyue hasten to apologize for suspecting every official of bad motives. He shook his head. “Don't stop questioning authority,” he replied. “Just remember that the person answering is still a person.”

議員告訴昭月，家人在多年前決定移居這座城時又窮又不熟地方習慣，鄰居常使他們為口音羞愧。有位老店主不求讚美，幫他們找到工作。「我們家有機會繁榮，是因為有人讓我們開始，」他說，「我也希望那樣被記得。」昭月急忙為自己懷疑所有官員的動機而道歉。他搖頭：「別停止質疑權威，只要記得回答問題的人也是人。」

At a small coffee stall, Yicheng and Zhaoyue examined the copied ledger under a weak lamp. The pages listed debts, deliveries, and one payment to a man identified only by the letter X. Zhaoyue suggested that X might be Xu, but Yicheng refused to write the full name until they had more evidence. A shop owner overheard them and offered a receipt with the same mark. It proved a shipment had occurred, not who had ordered it. They thanked him anyway. Every honest fragment narrowed the space in which a false story could hide, even if it did not reveal the whole answer at once.

在小咖啡攤，他們借昏暗燈光檢查帳本副本。頁上列有債務、貨運，還有一筆付給代號X的人。昭月猜可能是許某，但以誠堅持證據不足不能直接寫全名。攤主聽見談話，拿出有同樣記號的收據。收據只能證明貨運存在，不能證明誰下令。他們仍道謝。每一塊誠實碎片都在縮小謊言藏身的空間，即使不能立即給出完整答案。

Zhaoyue looked back at the locked registry. She was no longer certain that one honest official could fix an entire system, but she knew that patient cooperation could open a door that fear had kept closed.

昭月回頭望向上鎖的登記室。她不再相信單憑一位誠實官員就能修好整套制度，但明白耐心合作能打開恐懼長久關閉的門。

Outside, the councilor asked whether the clock could predict another disaster. Yicheng thought of the dates in his newspapers and of everything he knew would happen before Zhaoyue grew old. He could make a convincing forecast and earn a powerful ally. But a false sense of certainty might also tempt the councilor to punish innocent people. Yicheng chose to tell him only what their evidence could support. They hurried back to the theatre carrying the copied ledger. Behind them, the clerk locked the room. Neither noticed the stranger who had been watching from across the street, smiling as though their honesty had just made his task easier.

離開時，議員問銅鐘能否預測下一場災難。 以誠想起報紙上的日期，以及昭月變老之前將發生的種種。他可以提出令人信服的預測，換取強大的盟友，但過分確定也可能使議員傷及無辜。以誠最後只說證據能支持的部分。他們抱著抄本趕回戲院，書記員在身後鎖門。兩人都沒發現街對面的陌生人正在微笑，彷彿他們的誠實反而讓對方更容易下手。
