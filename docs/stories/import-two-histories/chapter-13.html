三個聲音的傳說

A Legend in Three Voices

The third path did not open onto a street. It led to a foundation stone beneath the theatre, where three names had been carved long before Zhaoyue was born. A local legend said the stone could fulfill a wish if three generations sang together. Shen Zhong dismissed the magical explanation but admitted that a choir had once been appointed to test the clock. Their voices produced a steady chorus, and the copper responded. The experiment succeeded only because the singers were separated by fifty-year intervals. The discovery had become a memorable triumph for the clockmaker and a miserable burden for the families involved.

第三條路沒有通往街道，而是通往戲院地基的一塊石頭。 石上刻著三個名字，年代遠早於昭月出生。地方傳說說，只要三代人一起唱歌，石頭便能實現願望。沈仲不相信魔法，但承認當年曾指派一個合唱團測試銅鐘。他們以穩定的合唱聲讓金屬共鳴，而實驗之所以成功，是因為歌者分別來自相距五十年的年代。那是鐘匠難忘的勝利，也成了幾個家庭痛苦的負擔。

Yicheng proposed a conference with everyone who still understood the device. Shen insisted it must be secret, though he allowed a leisurely meal beforehand. Wenbo brought rice and vegetables; Mei supplied hot water and a small container of oxygen for an ill traveler who had recently appeared from another year. Zhaoyue noticed a blossom caught in the traveler's hair. It was a flower she had seen outside her grandmother's home in 1895. The stranger looked scarcely older than she was and wore a striped sleeve. His smile froze when he saw the clock. “I know that sound,” he said. “It is what people hear before they forget me.”

以誠提議召開會議，請還懂得裝置的人一起商量。 沈仲堅持保密，但讓大家先從容吃頓飯。文博帶來米飯和蔬菜，阿梅供應熱水，還為一名最近從另一個年代出現的病人準備了小型氧氣筒。昭月注意到旅人髮上沾著一朵花，正是1895年祖母家門外的花。他看起來年紀和她差不多，袖子有條紋。看到銅鐘時，他笑容凝住：「我知道那個聲音，每次人們忘記我之前，都會聽見它。」

The stranger introduced himself as Liang. He had carried the clockmaker's first proposal to a wealthy sponsor, explaining how records might be preserved across generations. The sponsor saw only economic advantage and demanded control of the discovery. When Liang refused, a curse was spread about him: he was called a liar and blamed for the deaths of people he had tried to save. He sketched the old workshop for Yicheng, marking a hidden chamber below the stage. “Every universal principle begins with one person's choice,” he said. “The clock was built to protect testimony. Someone has tried to make it protect power.”

旅人自稱阿良。 他曾替鐘匠把最初的計畫書帶給富有的贊助人，說明跨世代保存紀錄的方法。贊助人只看見經濟利益，要求獨佔發現。阿良拒絕，便被人散播詛咒般的謠言：他被罵騙子，還被怪罪那些他努力想救卻沒救到的人。他替以誠畫出舊工作坊，標明舞臺底下的密室。「每個普遍原則，」他說，「都從一個人的選擇開始。這座鐘原本保護證言，有人卻想讓它保護權勢。」

Yicheng had never enjoyed school lessons on economics. Graphs of rising and falling prices seemed far away from the people his father repaired radios for. But Liang's account of the wealthy sponsor made the subject suddenly personal. A prediction about future trade could enrich one family while leaving another without food. The clock had given a few men the temptation to treat every possible event as a market opportunity. Yicheng asked whether the clockmaker had anticipated this misuse. Shen admitted that he had warned about it, but not loudly enough. A warning written in a private notebook was easy for powerful people to ignore.

以誠從前不愛上經濟學課。價格上下波動的圖表，離父親替之修收音機的家庭太遠。但阿良說起富有贊助人，這學問突然與人有關了。未來貿易的預測能讓一家變富，也能讓另一家斷糧。銅鐘讓少數人有機會把每種可能事件當成市場商機。以誠問鐘匠是否預料到濫用。沈仲承認他曾警告，但聲音不夠大；寫在私人筆記裡的警告，很容易被有權勢的人忽略。

They turned the conference into a workshop. Each person described one record that mattered and one reason it might be wrong. Wenbo spoke of an address copied from memory. Mei spoke of a birth date that a mother had changed to protect her child. Zhaoyue confessed that she sometimes wrote more neatly when she agreed with a witness, as if the shape of her letters could express approval. The small admissions encouraged trust rather than destroying it. By the end of the meeting, they had drafted rules for checking evidence without claiming that any one person could see the whole truth.

他們把會議改成工作坊，每個人說一份重要的紀錄，以及它可能出錯的理由。文博提到憑記憶抄下的地址，阿梅說某位母親為保護孩子改過生日，昭月承認自己若同意證人，就會不自覺把字寫得更漂亮，彷彿筆畫也在表示贊成。這些小小坦白沒有摧毀信任，反而讓人更相信彼此。會議結束時，眾人擬好了核證原則，卻沒有宣稱誰能看見完整真相。

The group asked Yicheng to summarize everything they knew. As he spoke, a disturbing pattern emerged. The clock did not create missing people; it opened an opportunity for powerful men to destroy evidence of them. Their most important task was therefore not to defeat time itself, but to keep multiple honest accounts alive. Shen approved a plan to place three copies of the testimony in three different years. The first would be hidden in 1895. The second would be entrusted to someone in 1945. The third would be carried along the unknown path, if Liang could survive one more crossing.

眾人請以誠摘要整理發現。 他越說，模式越清楚：銅鐘本身沒有製造失蹤者，而是讓有權勢的人得到毀滅證據的機會。最重要的任務不是打敗時間，而是保住多份誠實記述。沈仲同意在三個年份分別留下證詞：第一份藏於1895年，第二份託付給1945年的人，第三份若阿良撐得過下一次穿越，就送往未知通道。
