承認錯誤的工作坊

The Workshop of Honest Errors

雙時代之鐘 · 第 23 / 25 章 · 3000–4000 · 英中對照 · 校訂完成

Yicheng and Zhaoyue carried the damaged mechanism to a small laboratory in the 1945 school. The room was muddy after the storm, and the shelves held a mixture of artificial materials and ordinary tools. A young technician calculated the pressure needed to keep the second bell from striking. He suggested using a spring from a damaged clock. Another volunteer tested the circulation of air through the copper box, afraid that heat might cause the repair to fail. Their work felt less like a heroic contest than a careful lesson in how many things could go wrong.

以誠與昭月把受損機關搬進1945年學校的小實驗室。 風雨過後房間泥濘，架上混放人工材料與普通工具。年輕技師計算需要多少壓力才能阻止第二聲鐘落下，提議使用壞鐘的彈簧。另一名志工檢查銅箱裡的空氣流通，怕高溫使修理失敗。這不像英雄競賽，倒像一堂謹慎的課：世界上有太多事情可能出錯。

They asked a woman who ran a nursery to help label hundreds of letters. She had little technical training but recognized family names and village customs. Her modest knowledge proved essential. A schoolboy who was her peer in curiosity, though not age, found the stem of a flower pressed between two pages. He remembered seeing the same flower beside a monument near the theatre. They went to inspect the stone and discovered a hidden box. Inside lay the original instructions for repairing the clock, written before Huang's family altered them. The box also contained a note apologizing for an incident that had harmed an innocent messenger.

他們請經營托兒所的婦人協助標示數百封信。 她不懂太多技術，卻認得家族名稱與村落習俗。這些樸實知識非常重要。一名好奇心與她不相上下的學生，在兩頁紙間發現壓扁的花莖。他記得戲院附近一座紀念碑旁也長著那種花。兩人前往查看，找到一只暗盒，裡面是黃家篡改之前的原始修鐘說明，還有一張為無辜信使受傷而道歉的便條。

The clockmaker's instructions were surprisingly philosophical. They explained how to calculate the balance of the bell but warned that a machine could not predict human motives. A false witness could be generous in one moment and selfish in the next. A truthful one could still misunderstand an event. The essential issue was to keep testimony open to examination rather than force every voice into agreement. Yicheng read the pages aloud and felt as if the clockmaker were speaking directly to him. The document's errors were visible, with scratched lines and corrected numbers. Unlike Xu, its author had left the mistakes where others could see them.

鐘匠的說明出乎意料地充滿哲思。 他解釋如何計算鐘擺平衡，也提醒機器無法預測人的動機。說謊者某一刻可以慷慨，下一刻又可能自私；誠實的人也可能誤解事件。重要的是讓證言一直可以被檢驗，而不是強迫每個聲音一致。以誠把文件念出來，彷彿鐘匠親自在對他說話。頁上保留著刮掉的字與改過的數字；與許某不同，作者把自己的錯誤留在了別人看得見的地方。

The technician had learned generosity from a teacher who lent tools to students who could not afford their own. He refused to keep his knowledge secret, even when a visitor offered him money for exclusive instructions. “A repair is better when another person can check it,” he said. Yicheng realized that this principle connected every part of the investigation. The clock had become dangerous not merely because it was powerful, but because a small group had decided that nobody else deserved to understand its operation. They drew diagrams clear enough for an apprentice to follow and kept copies in different places.

年輕技師的慷慨，是向曾借工具給貧困學生的老師學來的。就算有人出錢買獨家說明，他也不願把知識藏起來。「能讓另一個人檢查的修理才更可靠，」他說。以誠明白這原則貫穿整場調查。銅鐘之所以危險，不只因它有力量，更因少數人認為別人不配理解操作方式。他們畫出學徒也看得懂的圖，並在不同地方保存副本。

Zhaoyue tested the mechanism one final time while the schoolyard filled with children. Through the window she watched a girl teach a younger boy how to fold paper into a bird. The first attempt collapsed, but the girl unfolded the paper and showed where the crease should have been. The boy tried again, more carefully. Zhaoyue thought of the clockmaker leaving corrections in his instructions. Mistakes did not have to disappear in order for work to improve. Sometimes the visible record of a failure was what allowed another person to succeed. She returned to the workbench with that thought and checked the spring again.

昭月最後測試機關時，學校院子裡都是孩子。窗外一名女孩教弟弟把紙摺成鳥，第一次失敗，女孩攤開紙，指出摺痕應在哪裡。男孩再試一次，更仔細了。昭月想起鐘匠留下修改痕跡的說明。工作要變好，不一定要把錯誤藏起來；有時正是看得見的失敗，讓下一個人成功。她帶著這想法回到工作桌，又檢查一次彈簧。

A radar operator passing through the school offered to scoop water from the flooded workbench while they repaired the device. Despite having no connection to the mystery, he worked until dawn. By sunrise, the spring held steady. One volunteer predicted the repair would last a hundred years; Yicheng refused to make such a promise. Instead he tested the bell, documented the limits, and asked everyone to check his measurements. It was time to depart from the laboratory and return to the theatre. When Zhaoyue lifted the repaired mechanism, she knew that success would be measured not by a perfect clock, but by whether the witnesses could speak freely after it fell silent.

路過學校的一名雷達操作員幫忙舀掉工作桌旁的積水。 他與謎團毫無關係，卻一直做到天亮。日出時，彈簧已穩定。有志工預言修理成果能撐一百年，以誠拒絕保證。他只測試鐘、記錄限制，請大家核對測量。該離開實驗室回戲院了。昭月抬起修好的機關時明白，成功不在有一座完美的鐘，而在鐘聲停後，見證者是否還能自由說話。
