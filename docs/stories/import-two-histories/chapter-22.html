拱門下的刀

The Knife at the Arch

The music had barely started when a stranger crept beneath the theatre's stone arch. He wore several layers of clothing and carried a hook used for moving stage scenery. His plan was elementary: cut the rope connected to the copper gear and let the mechanism collapse. Xu had promised him money for the act. The man knew he was at a disadvantage against a crowd, but he had been told that the audience was helpless. He reached the clock, pulled a knife, and prepared to stab the rope. Wenbo spotted him from the wings and shouted a warning across the stage.

音樂才剛開始，一名陌生人就從戲院石拱門下悄悄爬進來。 他穿了好幾層衣服，帶著搬舞臺布景用的鉤子。計畫很簡單：割斷連著銅齒輪的繩索，讓機關塌下。許某答應付他錢。他知道自己面對人群居於劣勢，卻被騙說觀眾無力反抗。男子到了鐘旁，拔刀準備刺斷繩子，文博從側臺看見，立刻大喊警告。

A furious struggle followed. The saboteur pulled the rope, and a small spark flew from the clock. A respectable schoolteacher tried to secure the audience's exit while the musicians kept their instruments from falling. Zhaoyue grabbed the loose gear and burned her palm. Yicheng, hearing the crash across the time passage, dropped his music sheet and rushed toward the corresponding lever in 1945. Their cooperation had always required trust, but now it demanded immediate action without explanation. He braced the mechanism while she fastened the rope. The first note faltered, then returned.

隨後爆發激烈混亂。 破壞者拉扯繩索，銅鐘迸出一點火花。一位備受敬重的老師忙著確保觀眾能退出，樂師則穩住差點掉落的樂器。昭月抓住鬆脫齒輪，手掌被燙傷。以誠透過時間通道聽見撞擊聲，丟下樂譜，衝向1945年對應的拉桿。兩人合作一向需要信任，現在更得不經解釋立刻行動。他撐住機關，她重新綁繩。第一個音弱下去，隨後再次響起。

The stranger was caught. To everyone's surprise, he began to cry. He explained that he had spent a night in isolation after refusing an order, and that his family had no food. Xu had exploited both hunger and hatred. A woman from the audience offered the man a quilt to cover his shaking shoulders. Wenbo wanted a public admission of guilt. Zhaoyue agreed, but refused to humiliate him before the crowd. They took away his knife and recorded what he had done. She insisted that understanding his reasons did not mean excusing the danger he had caused. Both truths could exist together.

陌生人被制伏了。 出乎意料，他竟哭了起來。他說自己因拒絕命令而被單獨關了一夜，家裡又斷糧。許某利用了他的飢餓與仇恨。一名女觀眾把被子披在他顫抖的肩上。文博要求他公開認錯，昭月同意記錄罪行，卻拒絕在群眾面前羞辱他。他們收走刀，寫下行為經過。昭月強調，理解一個人的理由，不表示原諒他造成的危險。這兩件事可以同時成立。

As the intruder crossed the arch, Zhaoyue was speaking with an old woman about the theatre's capacity. They worried there would not be enough space for everyone wishing to attend. The woman suggested holding additional readings outside the walls, so those who could not enter would still hear the names. It was such a simple answer that Zhaoyue laughed. They began to converse about the first performance the woman had seen there, when she was a child. For a moment the room seemed peaceful, and neither noticed the shadow creep across the floor until Wenbo called out.

闖入者穿過拱門時，昭月正和老婦人討論戲院容納人數，擔心想來的人太多。婦人建議在牆外多安排幾場朗讀，讓進不來的人也聽見姓名。辦法簡單得讓昭月笑了。兩人聊起婦人小時候看過的第一次表演，室內一度很平靜，直到文博大喊，才發現有影子正慢慢爬過地板。

Yicheng had worked overnight repairing the radio connection between the two stages. His hands were sore, and he wanted to sleep, but a small difference in the clock's rhythm kept troubling him. He wrote down each beat and compared the pauses. Just before dawn he found an unfamiliar gap where the second bell should have remained silent. Someone had altered the timing from the other side. He tried to send a warning, but the passage opened only for sound at the correct frequency. He waited beside the machine, hoping that when the moment arrived Zhaoyue would understand the signal even if he had no time to explain it.

以誠熬夜修兩座舞臺之間的無線電連線，雙手痠痛，想睡卻被鐘聲節奏裡的細微差異困擾。他記下每拍並比較停頓。天亮前，他在第二聲本應沉默的地方發現異常空隙。有人從另一端改過時間。他想傳警告，通道卻只讓正確頻率的聲音通過。他守在機器旁，希望時刻一到，即使來不及解釋，昭月也能懂那個訊號。

The musicians shared a brief look, then continued holding the note. Their hands were shaking, but their decision was firm. They knew the crowd needed an audible sign that the performance had not been defeated.

樂師短暫交換眼神，仍繼續維持音符。手雖顫抖，決定卻很堅定。他們知道觀眾需要聽見演出未被擊敗的證明。

Afterward the clock opened multiple narrow windows across the wall. Within them stood families watching in both years. Some were angry, some exhausted, some still uncertain whether they should trust the performers. The audience was not a single body with a single opinion. At one window Zhaoyue saw Yicheng hold up a page bearing her name. At another she saw the man Xu had tried to erase fifty years earlier, alive and speaking. The second bell began to turn by itself. If it rang now, every open window might become another door through which memories would disappear.

事後，銅鐘在牆上打開好幾個狹長窗口。 裡頭站著兩個年代的家庭。有的生氣，有的疲憊，有的還不知道該不該相信演出者。觀眾並不是只有一種意見的整體。昭月在一個窗口看見以誠舉起寫著她名字的紙，另一個窗口則出現了許某五十年前試圖抹去的人，他仍活著、仍能說話。第二聲鐘的機關自行轉動。若現在響起，每扇窗口都可能變成讓記憶消失的門。
