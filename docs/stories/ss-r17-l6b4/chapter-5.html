沒有影子的世界

The World That Cast No Shadow

當所有平行世界決定關閉地球 · 第 5 / 25 章 · 5000–6000 · 英中對照 · 校訂中

The morning had already acquired the look of an emergency before Mira reached the scene. On a planet without shadows, the visitors find a monitoring station older than any assembly record. An official wanted a clean story, with a beginning that could be announced and an ending that would require no questions. Mira had learned that reality rarely showed such courtesy. She made room for the people who had been ordered to wait outside. The defective fastener was sold as an accessory, not part of the main coat. The archived report described a breakdown in the relay safeguards during the first citywide memory trial. The crew refused to bypass the safety switch simply to make the demonstration look successful before the hearing. A photograph showed officials raising champagne glasses beside the first experimental sleep relay.

在米拉抵達現場前，早晨已經有了緊急事件的模樣。在一顆沒有影子的行星上，訪客發現比議會紀錄更古老的監測站。官員想要一套簡潔的故事，開頭能宣布，結尾又不用回答問題。她知道現實很少如此客氣，於是請那些被命令等在門外的人進來說話。 瑕疵扣件被當作配件販售，而非斗篷本體。 封存報告描述第一次全城記憶測試時，中繼器的安全機制曾故障。 工作隊拒絕只為了讓聽證前示範看似成功就繞過安全開關。 一張照片裡，官員在第一座實驗性睡眠中繼器旁舉起香檳杯。

Dax examined the account twice, once as a friend and once as a witness who might have to defend every word. Its instruments show that energy disappears only when the central archive performs a scheduled collection. The difference between what they suspected and what they could establish mattered. They placed the new fact beside the previous chapter’s unanswered question and looked for a second source that had not been copied from the first. Delegates contributed observations from their separate worlds to the joint record: A chestnut-colored sample revealed uneven dye along the collar. Calling the whistleblower cowardly would only discourage future reports. The old bus photographs could evoke affection without proving financial viability.

達克斯把紀錄看了兩次：第一次以朋友身分，第二次則像一位必須為每個字負責的證人。儀器顯示，只有中央檔案庫定期收集時，能量才會消失。懷疑和能確立的事實必須分清楚。他們把新線索放在前一章未解的問題旁，尋找不曾從同一份資料抄出的第二來源。代表也把各自世界的觀察加入共同紀錄： 一件栗色樣衣顯示領口染色不均。 稱檢舉者懦弱，只會阻止往後的人通報。 舊圖書車照片能喚起感情，卻不能證明財務可行。

A crowd gathered around the disputed notice. “You have to choose a side,” someone shouted. Mira answered that she had chosen one already: the side of a claim that survived questions. Dax did not smile, but moved the most vulnerable bystanders away from the growing argument. The pair could disagree privately without turning their disagreement into a public threat. An illustrated farm collection included a bright fowl on its label. The receptionist's voice grew hoarse as she explained how many records were scheduled for destruction. The repair kit included lotion safe for handling rough synthetic material.

人群圍住有爭議的公告。「妳必須選邊站！」有人喊。米拉回答自己早已選擇：站在經得起提問的主張那一邊。達克斯沒有微笑，只把最脆弱的旁觀者帶離爭執。他們可以私下不同意對方，卻不能把分歧變成公開威脅。 農場主題工藝系列的標籤畫著鮮豔的家禽。 接待員解釋即將銷毀多少紀錄時，聲音變得沙啞。 維修包裡附有處理粗糙合成材料後可用的乳液。

The institution was not a single villain. It contained frightened clerks, dedicated workers, ambitious supervisors, and people who had learned to hide mistakes. the central archive wanted the incident classified as routine. Mira asked whose definition of routine was being used and whose losses were absent from the statistics. Nobody at the table could answer without consulting the sealed records. Delegates contributed observations from their separate worlds to the joint record: The records exposed a dangerous mentality: officials valued silence more than the health of residents. The monetary trail linked the contractor's expansion payments to an office outside normal public oversight. The building's moody lighting made every empty corridor seem occupied by someone who had vanished. The repair team became the nucleus of a broader customer safety program.

這個機構不是單一惡棍。裡面有害怕的書記、盡責的工人、野心勃勃的主管，也有學會掩蓋錯誤的人。中央檔案庫想把事件歸類為例行事項。米拉追問：是誰定義例行？又有誰的損失被統計漏掉？桌邊沒人能在不看封存紀錄的情況下回答。代表也把各自世界的觀察加入共同紀錄： 紀錄揭露一種危險心態：官員重視安靜勝過居民健康。 金錢流向把承包商的擴建款項連到一個不受一般公共監督的辦公室。 大樓陰沉多變的燈光，使每條空走廊都像住著消失的人。 維修團隊成為更廣泛來訪者安全計畫的核心。

Mira asks the station keepers to preserve original readings rather than publishing speculation. The decision did not repair everything. It altered one procedure, protected one person, or saved one piece of evidence; on a day when almost everyone demanded a miracle, that was worth recording. Mira insisted that the result and its limits appear together in the official minutes. Anything less would turn a modest gain into tomorrow’s misleading legend. A sharp rap at the metal door announced the safety inspector's arrival. A red slash across the engineer's recommendation marked the moment the council ignored the warning. An exhausted accountant began to snore during the overnight document review.

米拉請站務員保存原始讀數，不急著公開推測。這個決定沒有修好一切，只改變一道程序、保護一個人，或留下了一份證據；當幾乎所有人都要求奇蹟時，這仍值得記錄。米拉堅持把成果和限制一起寫入正式紀錄。否則小小的進展又會變成明天誤導人的傳說。 金屬門上傳來急促敲擊聲，宣告安全檢查員抵達。 工程師建議上的紅色劃線，標記了議會無視警告的那一刻。 徹夜審文件時，疲憊的會計開始打鼾。

The lights beyond the window did not offer comfort. They showed how many people were still waiting. Mira understood that the investigation might eventually expose her own earlier errors, and she resolved not to erase them. Dax suggested a short rest before the next hearing. She agreed, because exhaustion could damage judgment as thoroughly as deliberate deception. The telecommunications contract covered secure calls from trial stores. The chair agreed to unveil the contract terms before any final vote. An elderly boat captain defended the public path with surprising vigor, insisting that younger residents deserved safe access too.

窗外的燈光沒有帶來安慰，只照出還有多少人在等待。米拉明白調查最終可能揭露自己過去的錯誤，決定不去抹除。達克斯建議下一場聽證前短暫休息。她同意，因為疲憊和蓄意欺騙一樣可能損害判斷。 電信合約涵蓋試辦門市的安全通訊。 主席同意在最終表決前公開契約條款。 一名年長船長充滿活力地捍衛公共步道，堅稱年輕居民也應享有安全通行。
