The Delegate from the Drowned World

當所有平行世界決定關閉地球 · 第 4 / 25 章 · 5000–6000 · 英中對照 · 校訂中

用互動版查詞、朗讀與收藏

Mira arrived with the unpleasant certainty that the simplest explanation was probably the one that had been prepared for public consumption. Dax, a delegate whose world is covered by deep oceans, offers Mira evidence that the leakage began long before Earth's first collider. The place was full of people who needed a quick answer, yet the evidence still belonged to those whose lives it could change. Instead of making a dramatic accusation, Mira marked the hour, the witnesses, and the smallest observable detail in a paper notebook. A nurse posted a guide about digital addiction beside the station's volunteer schedule. The clean aesthetic of the new galleries concealed the violence of their missing captions. The planner could give only an approximate count of attendees. The curator called his unauthorized work urban archaeology because ordinary history departments refused the materials.

米拉抵達時，心裡很清楚：最簡單的解釋往往是事先準備好要給大眾聽的版本。來自深海覆蓋世界的代表達克斯向米拉提供證據，顯示能量洩漏早於地球第一座對撞機。現場的人都急需答案，但證據仍屬於那些生活可能被它改變的人。她沒有戲劇化地指控誰，而是先在紙本筆記上記下時刻、證人，以及最小卻可觀察的細節。 護理師把數位成癮的衛教指南貼在救援站志工班表旁。 新展廳整潔的美學風格，掩蓋了遭刪除說明所代表的暴力。 規劃者只能提供來賓人數的大約估計。 館長稱自己的未授權工作是都市考古學，因為正規歷史部門拒收這些資料。

There was a moment of quiet when Dax set the new material on the table. His data is incomplete because his world's libraries were lost beneath rising seas. What mattered was not how surprising the discovery sounded, but whether anyone could repeat the test. Mira copied the relevant names carefully. If one person later changed the account, the original would still be available for comparison. Delegates contributed observations from their separate worlds to the joint record: Every volunteer wore a badge with their approved access zone. A controlled blaze on the projection screen simulated stage lighting. An elderly tailor called her devoted apprentice a disciple of fine craft.

達克斯把新資料放上桌時，四周安靜了一瞬。他的資料不完整，因為世界圖書館已沉入上升海水中。重要的不是發現有多驚人，而是別人能不能重做同一項測試。米拉仔細抄下相關姓名；如果日後有人更改說法，原件仍可供比對。代表也把各自世界的觀察加入共同紀錄： 每位志工佩戴標示授權區域的識別證。 投影幕上的模擬火光呈現舞台燈效。 老布術師稱自己勤奮的學徒是工藝門徒。

“Will you trust me?” Dax asked. “With the evidence, yes,” Mira answered, “but not with a blank signature.” It was the sort of answer that could sound cold until someone noticed how carefully she had protected the other person’s right to disagree. Together they made a plan with a backup route, a deadline, and a record of who had accepted each responsibility. The fitting demonstration could enlighten buyers about hidden barriers. The operator accused the curator of making a fuss, while she calmly requested an inventory before anything was moved. The map carried an implicit assumption that everyone could climb stairs.

「妳信任我嗎？」達克斯問。「證據上可以，空白授權書不行。」米拉回答。這聽起來很冷淡，直到人們注意到她也小心保護對方拒絕的權利。他們一起訂下有備用路線、期限，以及明確責任分工的計畫。 試衣示範可以使贊助人了解看不見的障礙。 營運商說館長大驚小怪，她卻冷靜要求搬走任何物品前先列清單。 地圖隱含一個假設：人人都能爬樓梯。

A representative of the central archive arrived with an offer to resolve the matter quietly. Quietly, in this case, meant without giving affected people access to the original. Mira declined. She knew an office could be useful and still be dangerously wrong. Her objection was narrow and specific: the proposed decision relied on a document whose origin had not yet been independently verified. Delegates contributed observations from their separate worlds to the joint record: A government historian would preside over the reopening ceremony, though he had never inspected the archive. The museum's prestige depended on public trust, not simply on royal-looking seals and elegant lighting. The council planned to privatize the old station land before anyone could establish its rightful owners. The warm stage lights seemed to radiate through the silk.

中央檔案庫的代表前來，提議私下解決。所謂私下，就是不讓受影響的人看到原件。米拉拒絕了。她知道一個機關可以有用，也可能犯下危險的錯。她的反對具體而有限：提議中的決定依賴一份來源還沒接受獨立驗證的文件。代表也把各自世界的觀察加入共同紀錄： 一名從未檢查檔案的政府史官，卻將主持重新開幕典禮。 博物館的聲望來自公眾信任，不是華麗印章與燈光。 議會計畫在確定土地合法所有人之前，把舊車站用地私有化。 溫暖的舞台燈光彷彿透過絲綢向外散發。

Mira agrees to investigate together without promising to support his faction's vote. A few witnesses thanked Mira; others accused her of doing too little. She accepted both reactions without offering a promise she could not keep. Dax wrote down the complaints, including the ones directed at their own conduct. They were not building a monument to themselves. They were building a process that would still function after they left. The volunteer rotation gave everyone time for dinner. A backstage shaver used to remove lint had a damaged cord. A stray note beneath the glass included the route number of the missing passenger train.

米拉同意共同調查，但不保證支持他的派系投票。幾名證人向米拉道謝，另一些人指責她做得不夠。她接受兩種反應，不許下無法兌現的承諾。達克斯寫下抱怨，包括針對他們自己行為的部分。他們不是為自己立紀念碑，而是建立自己離開後仍能運作的程序。 志工輪班安排讓每個人都有時間吃晚餐。 後台去毛球的小型電動刮除器電線受損。 玻璃展示櫃下夾著一張零散紙條，上面寫著失蹤客車的路線編號。

By nightfall the newest report was signed by observers who were free to disagree. Mira stood beside Dax and looked at the distance between them and a fair decision that does not silence the people at risk. Progress felt less like triumph than like leaving a reliable mark on a difficult road. Someone would challenge that mark tomorrow. This time, there would be a public record with which to answer. Official captions placed a veil of respectable language over the fact that whole families had vanished. A photograph of a vineyard outside the wall contradicted the government's claim that no crops survived there. The contractor's warranty still covered the sealed valve, proving the tunnel had been serviced recently.

夜幕降臨前，新的報告由可以提出異議的觀察者簽署。米拉站在達克斯身旁，看著他們與不讓受威脅人民失聲的公平決議之間的距離。進展不像勝利，比較像在艱難路上留下可靠的記號。明天有人會質疑，而這一次將有公開紀錄可以回答。 官方說明以體面措辭掩蓋了整個家庭失蹤的事實。 城牆外葡萄園的照片，反駁了政府宣稱外面沒有作物存活的說法。 承包商的保固仍涵蓋封閉閥門，證明隧道不久前有人維修。
