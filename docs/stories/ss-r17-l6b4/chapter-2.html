不同天空的議會

The Assembly of Different Skies

當所有平行世界決定關閉地球 · 第 2 / 25 章 · 5000–6000 · 英中對照 · 校訂中

The morning had already acquired the look of an emergency before Mira reached the scene. The first assembly gathers delegates from ocean worlds, desert worlds, and cities built inside moving trees. An official wanted a clean story, with a beginning that could be announced and an ending that would require no questions. Mira had learned that reality rarely showed such courtesy. She made room for the people who had been ordered to wait outside. Every store manager had to abide by the published repair policy. The director would not affirm the official opening date without seeing the original ledger. A playful alligator appeared on an optional children's raincoat print. A low bass melody played softly in the waiting room.

在米拉抵達現場前，早晨已經有了緊急事件的模樣。第一場議會聚集來自海洋、沙漠與移動樹城的代表。官員想要一套簡潔的故事，開頭能宣布，結尾又不用回答問題。她知道現實很少如此客氣，於是請那些被命令等在門外的人進來說話。 每位店長都必須遵守公開的維修政策。 主任沒看過原始帳冊，不願確認官方啟用日期。 兒童雨衣可選的圖樣上有隻俏皮鱷魚。 等候室輕輕播放著低沉的低音旋律。

Dax examined the account twice, once as a friend and once as a witness who might have to defend every word. A proposed closure requires a two-thirds majority, but the rules say nothing about Earth's right to appeal. The difference between what they suspected and what they could establish mattered. They placed the new fact beside the previous chapter’s unanswered question and looked for a second source that had not been copied from the first. Delegates contributed observations from their separate worlds to the joint record: The children's colloquial word for the forbidden street survived in a playground counting rhyme. Hidden service charges had become commonplace in competing city markets. The temporary showroom permit would expire at the end of the quarter.

達克斯把紀錄看了兩次：第一次以朋友身分，第二次則像一位必須為每個字負責的證人。關閉提案需要三分之二多數，規則卻沒有寫地球的上訴權。懷疑和能確立的事實必須分清楚。他們把新線索放在前一章未解的問題旁，尋找不曾從同一份資料抄出的第二來源。代表也把各自世界的觀察加入共同紀錄： 孩子口語中稱呼禁忌街道的詞，仍留在遊戲數數歌裡。 隱藏服務收費在其他城市市集裡已經十分常見。 臨時展示大廳的許可將於季度末到期。

A crowd gathered around the disputed notice. “You have to choose a side,” someone shouted. Mira answered that she had chosen one already: the side of a claim that survived questions. Dax did not smile, but moved the most vulnerable bystanders away from the growing argument. The pair could disagree privately without turning their disagreement into a public threat. A damaged fuse briefly disabled the training room lights. The innkeeper offered hospitality to two stranded inspectors without asking them to alter their report in return. The school cook's infectious laughter eased the worried meeting.

人群圍住有爭議的公告。「妳必須選邊站！」有人喊。米拉回答自己早已選擇：站在經得起提問的主張那一邊。達克斯沒有微笑，只把最脆弱的旁觀者帶離爭執。他們可以私下不同意對方，卻不能把分歧變成公開威脅。 損壞的保險絲讓訓練室燈光短暫熄滅。 旅店老闆招待兩名受困檢查員，並未要求他們以修改報告作為回報。 學校廚師富有感染力的笑聲緩和了焦慮的會議。

The institution was not a single villain. It contained frightened clerks, dedicated workers, ambitious supervisors, and people who had learned to hide mistakes. the central archive wanted the incident classified as routine. Mira asked whose definition of routine was being used and whose losses were absent from the statistics. Nobody at the table could answer without consulting the sealed records. Delegates contributed observations from their separate worlds to the joint record: The dye workshop used a measured liter of water for each test. A novice technician had left the storage sensor disconnected, accidentally preserving the original poster. It would be rash to send a full vehicle across before the load test. The old invoice and the official date could not reconcile without an independent explanation.

這個機構不是單一惡棍。裡面有害怕的書記、盡責的工人、野心勃勃的主管，也有學會掩蓋錯誤的人。中央檔案庫想把事件歸類為例行事項。米拉追問：是誰定義例行？又有誰的損失被統計漏掉？桌邊沒人能在不看封存紀錄的情況下回答。代表也把各自世界的觀察加入共同紀錄： 染色工作坊每次測試使用精確量好的一公升水。 一名新手技師曾讓儲藏室感測器保持斷線，意外保存了原始海報。 載重測試前就讓滿載救援車過橋太草率。 沒有獨立說明，舊發票與官方日期便無法相互調和。

Mira challenges the rules before discussing the accusation against her universe. The decision did not repair everything. It altered one procedure, protected one person, or saved one piece of evidence; on a day when almost everyone demanded a miracle, that was worth recording. Mira insisted that the result and its limits appear together in the official minutes. Anything less would turn a modest gain into tomorrow’s misleading legend. The shabby ledger cabinet looked worthless, which was probably why nobody had removed it. A ceiling spotlight illuminated the faded train route when Mira opened the basement door. The clinic offered apple syrup before sleep tests, a smell several former patients remembered distinctly.

米拉在討論對她宇宙的指控前，先質疑程序規則。這個決定沒有修好一切，只改變一道程序、保護一個人，或留下了一份證據；當幾乎所有人都要求奇蹟時，這仍值得記錄。米拉堅持把成果和限制一起寫入正式紀錄。否則小小的進展又會變成明天誤導人的傳說。 破舊的帳冊櫃看起來毫無價值，可能因此沒人搬走。 米拉打開地下室門時，一盞聚光燈照亮了褪色的列車路線。 診所做睡眠測試前會提供蘋果糖漿，那股氣味讓好幾名前病患記得格外清楚。

The lights beyond the window did not offer comfort. They showed how many people were still waiting. Mira understood that the investigation might eventually expose her own earlier errors, and she resolved not to erase them. Dax suggested a short rest before the next hearing. She agreed, because exhaustion could damage judgment as thoroughly as deliberate deception. The display stand began to tilt until a technician stabilized it. The artificial twilight outside the clinic concealed the fact that no real sun had reached the street. An old label marked whiskey lay in the storeroom beside the railway invoices, dated before the city's founding.

窗外的燈光沒有帶來安慰，只照出還有多少人在等待。米拉明白調查最終可能揭露自己過去的錯誤，決定不去抹除。達克斯建議下一場聽證前短暫休息。她同意，因為疲憊和蓄意欺騙一樣可能損害判斷。 展示架開始傾斜，直到技師把它固定。 診所外的人造暮光掩蓋了真實太陽根本照不到街道的事實。 一張印著威士忌的舊標籤躺在儲藏室鐵路發票旁，日期早於官方建城日。
