The City Made of Questions

Mira arrived with the unpleasant certainty that the simplest explanation was probably the one that had been prepared for public consumption. Delegates visit a world where buildings rearrange themselves around public debates rather than private property. The place was full of people who needed a quick answer, yet the evidence still belonged to those whose lives it could change. Instead of making a dramatic accusation, Mira marked the hour, the witnesses, and the smallest observable detail in a paper notebook. An old training film used an animate cartoon train to teach children the route beyond the western gate. Simple arithmetic showed that reported train repairs continued long after the official closure date. A visitor called the accessible collection awesome after trying it. The riverside café served celery soup during the long meeting.

米拉抵達時，心裡很清楚：最簡單的解釋往往是事先準備好要給大眾聽的版本。代表走訪一個建築會隨公開辯論重新排列、而非依私人產權配置的世界。現場的人都急需答案，但證據仍屬於那些生活可能被它改變的人。她沒有戲劇化地指控誰，而是先在紙本筆記上記下時刻、證人，以及最小卻可觀察的細節。 一部舊教學影片用栩栩如生的卡通列車教孩子認識西門外路線。 簡單的算術就能看出，列車維修紀錄在官方封閉日期之後仍持續多年。 一位參觀者試穿後稱無障礙工藝系列非常棒。 河邊咖啡館在漫長公會會議中供應芹菜湯。

There was a moment of quiet when Dax set the new material on the table. Its council refuses to vote for Earth's erasure without speaking to Earth's ordinary inhabitants. What mattered was not how surprising the discovery sounded, but whether anyone could repeat the test. Mira copied the relevant names carefully. If one person later changed the account, the original would still be available for comparison. Delegates contributed observations from their separate worlds to the joint record: The council checked whether the exhibition ramp's cement base was secure. A trade confederation offered advice on the nonexclusive distribution proposal. A sudden crackdown on returns would have punished honest customers.

達克斯把新資料放上桌時，四周安靜了一瞬。該世界議會拒絕在未和地球一般居民對話前支持抹除地球。重要的不是發現有多驚人，而是別人能不能重做同一項測試。米拉仔細抄下相關姓名；如果日後有人更改說法，原件仍可供比對。代表也把各自世界的觀察加入共同紀錄： 委員會檢查展示坡道的水泥底座是否牢固。 一個商業聯盟為非獨家通路提案提供意見。 突然嚴格限制退貨，反而會懲罰誠實來訪者。

“Will you trust me?” Dax asked. “With the evidence, yes,” Mira answered, “but not with a blank signature.” It was the sort of answer that could sound cold until someone noticed how carefully she had protected the other person’s right to disagree. Together they made a plan with a backup route, a deadline, and a record of who had accepted each responsibility. A playful crocodile costume appeared in the exhibition's children's section. The new policy served a dual purpose: safety and clear accountability. The city could not equalize every skill level, but it could make entry fairer.

「妳信任我嗎？」達克斯問。「證據上可以，空白授權書不行。」米拉回答。這聽起來很冷淡，直到人們注意到她也小心保護對方拒絕的權利。他們一起訂下有備用路線、期限，以及明確責任分工的計畫。 展覽兒童區出現一套俏皮鱷魚裝。 新政策有雙重目的：安全與責任明確。 市府無法讓每個人的技能相同，卻能使入場機會更公平。

A representative of the central archive arrived with an offer to resolve the matter quietly. Quietly, in this case, meant without giving affected people access to the original. Mira declined. She knew an office could be useful and still be dangerously wrong. Her objection was narrow and specific: the proposed decision relied on a document whose origin had not yet been independently verified. Delegates contributed observations from their separate worlds to the joint record: A council faction argued that opening the gate would endanger the city's fragile social order. An unsafe launch would incur costs beyond the initial recall. Residents called unequal access to safety information an injustice, especially when only wealthy boat owners received private warnings. The unexplained coastal signal deepened the intrigue surrounding the city's false founding history.

中央檔案庫的代表前來，提議私下解決。所謂私下，就是不讓受影響的人看到原件。米拉拒絕了。她知道一個機關可以有用，也可能犯下危險的錯。她的反對具體而有限：提議中的決定依賴一份來源還沒接受獨立驗證的文件。代表也把各自世界的觀察加入共同紀錄： 一個議會派系主張開城門會危及城市脆弱的社會秩序。 不安全上市會招致超過初次召回的成本。 居民認為安全資訊分配不均是一種不公，尤其只有富有船主能得到私人警示。 無法解釋的海岸訊號，讓城市虛假建城史背後的陰謀更加撲朔迷離。

Mira opens a public communication channel instead of presenting herself as humanity's only voice. A few witnesses thanked Mira; others accused her of doing too little. She accepted both reactions without offering a promise she could not keep. Dax wrote down the complaints, including the ones directed at their own conduct. They were not building a monument to themselves. They were building a process that would still function after they left. A false offer of coastal jobs might have been used to lure families onto the evacuation trains. An obstinate councilor finally agreed to publish the full operating budget. A pickpocket incident near the river prompted extra security at the event.

米拉開啟公共通信頻道，不把自己當成人類唯一代言者。幾名證人向米拉道謝，另一些人指責她做得不夠。她接受兩種反應，不許下無法兌現的承諾。達克斯寫下抱怨，包括針對他們自己行為的部分。他們不是為自己立紀念碑，而是建立自己離開後仍能運作的程序。 虛假的海岸工作機會，可能曾被用來引誘家庭登上撤離列車。 」 一名固執議員終於同意公開完整營運預算。 河邊發生扒手事件，活動因此加強保全。

By nightfall the newest report was signed by observers who were free to disagree. Mira stood beside Dax and looked at the distance between them and a fair decision that does not silence the people at risk. Progress felt less like triumph than like leaving a reliable mark on a difficult road. Someone would challenge that mark tomorrow. This time, there would be a public record with which to answer. The coastal dispatcher sounded punctual and irritated, hardly like a ghost from a dead civilization. Both lawyers praised the restraint shown during the public hearing. After twelve hours of testimony, the exhausted witness began to stagger toward the exit, and the chair called a break.

夜幕降臨前，新的報告由可以提出異議的觀察者簽署。米拉站在達克斯身旁，看著他們與不讓受威脅人民失聲的公平決議之間的距離。進展不像勝利，比較像在艱難路上留下可靠的記號。明天有人會質疑，而這一次將有公開紀錄可以回答。 海岸調度員說話準時又不耐煩，完全不像死去文明的幽靈。 雙方公證員都稱讚公開聽證展現的克制。 證人作證十二小時後，疲憊得往出口踉蹌，主席因此宣布休息。
