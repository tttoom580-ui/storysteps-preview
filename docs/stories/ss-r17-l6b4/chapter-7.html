失敗世界檔案

The Archive of Failed Worlds

當所有平行世界決定關閉地球 · 第 7 / 25 章 · 5000–6000 · 英中對照 · 校訂中

用互動版查詞、朗讀與收藏

Mira arrived with the unpleasant certainty that the simplest explanation was probably the one that had been prepared for public consumption. The central archive stores records of hundreds of worlds labeled unstable and permanently closed. The place was full of people who needed a quick answer, yet the evidence still belonged to those whose lives it could change. Instead of making a dramatic accusation, Mira marked the hour, the witnesses, and the smallest observable detail in a paper notebook. The archived wedding costume was too bulky for the narrow display case. Officials could deem a resident nonexistent by deleting an address, even while collecting that person's taxes. The elder called the city's memory policy a quiet dictatorship built from forms rather than soldiers. The fabric seemed to glide over the table as the designer unfolded it.

米拉抵達時，心裡很清楚：最簡單的解釋往往是事先準備好要給大眾聽的版本。中央檔案庫保存數百個被標為不穩定並永久關閉的世界紀錄。現場的人都急需答案，但證據仍屬於那些生活可能被它改變的人。她沒有戲劇化地指控誰，而是先在紙本筆記上記下時刻、證人，以及最小卻可觀察的細節。 檔案中的婚禮服裝太笨重，放不進狹窄展示櫃。 官員可以刪除地址就認定居民不存在，卻同時向那人收稅。 長者稱城市的記憶政策是一種靠表格而非士兵建立的無聲獨裁。 工藝師攤開布料時，布彷彿在桌面滑行。

There was a moment of quiet when Dax set the new material on the table. Dax finds that several worlds were still inhabited when their closure orders were signed. What mattered was not how surprising the discovery sounded, but whether anyone could repeat the test. Mira copied the relevant names carefully. If one person later changed the account, the original would still be available for comparison. Delegates contributed observations from their separate worlds to the joint record: Children sold small handicraft birds to purchase paper that city printers refused to supply them. An interpreter helped an overseas vessel captain explain the chart discrepancy without relying on uncertain gestures. A lesbian designer asked that her partner be welcomed without intrusive questions.

達克斯把新資料放上桌時，四周安靜了一瞬。達克斯發現有些世界在關閉令簽署時仍有人居住。重要的不是發現有多驚人，而是別人能不能重做同一項測試。米拉仔細抄下相關姓名；如果日後有人更改說法，原件仍可供比對。代表也把各自世界的觀察加入共同紀錄： 孩子們販售手工小鳥，換取城市印刷廠拒絕供應的紙張。 一名口譯員協助外國船長解釋海圖差異，不必依靠容易誤解的手勢。 一位女同志工藝師希望伴侶受到歡迎，不必回答冒犯的問題。

“Will you trust me?” Dax asked. “With the evidence, yes,” Mira answered, “but not with a blank signature.” It was the sort of answer that could sound cold until someone noticed how carefully she had protected the other person’s right to disagree. Together they made a plan with a backup route, a deadline, and a record of who had accepted each responsibility. For residents, liberation meant an address and protection from raids, not a speech from aboveground heroes. The council's lofty promises meant little without a funded staff roster. The survey crew needed to navigate between exposed rocks without confusing the proposed deeper channel with the existing route.

「妳信任我嗎？」達克斯問。「證據上可以，空白授權書不行。」米拉回答。這聽起來很冷淡，直到人們注意到她也小心保護對方拒絕的權利。他們一起訂下有備用路線、期限，以及明確責任分工的計畫。 對居民而言，解放是擁有地址與免於突襲的保障，不是地面英雄的演說。 沒有經費支撐的排班表，議會崇高承諾便毫無意義。 測量隊必須在露出的礁石間航行，不能把擬議深航道和現有航線混淆。

A representative of the central archive arrived with an offer to resolve the matter quietly. Quietly, in this case, meant without giving affected people access to the original. Mira declined. She knew an office could be useful and still be dangerously wrong. Her objection was narrow and specific: the proposed decision relied on a document whose origin had not yet been independently verified. Delegates contributed observations from their separate worlds to the joint record: The committee prohibited overwork even when volunteers insisted they felt energetic. The elder placed a smooth pebble beside every confirmed name on the handwritten community register. The proposed gift remained pending until ownership could be confirmed. An abandoned plantation above the tunnels supplied seeds to gardens the city denied existed.

中央檔案庫的代表前來，提議私下解決。所謂私下，就是不讓受影響的人看到原件。米拉拒絕了。她知道一個機關可以有用，也可能犯下危險的錯。她的反對具體而有限：提議中的決定依賴一份來源還沒接受獨立驗證的文件。代表也把各自世界的觀察加入共同紀錄： 即使志工自認精神充沛，委員會仍禁止過度工作。 長者在手寫社區名冊每個核實姓名旁放一顆光滑小石頭。 禮物在確認所有權前仍處於待決狀態。 隧道上方一座廢棄種植園提供種子，供給城市否認存在的菜園。

Mira demands testimony from any survivors instead of treating the archive's labels as proof. A few witnesses thanked Mira; others accused her of doing too little. She accepted both reactions without offering a promise she could not keep. Dax wrote down the complaints, including the ones directed at their own conduct. They were not building a monument to themselves. They were building a process that would still function after they left. A historical photograph showed farmers using a plow before the cotton harvest. No member of any royalty had visited the lower district, though official portraits hung in its old school. The threat of another correction pulse could spur residents to hide rather than testify.

米拉要求倖存者作證，不把檔案標籤當成證據。幾名證人向米拉道謝，另一些人指責她做得不夠。她接受兩種反應，不許下無法兌現的承諾。達克斯寫下抱怨，包括針對他們自己行為的部分。他們不是為自己立紀念碑，而是建立自己離開後仍能運作的程序。 歷史照片顯示棉花收成前，農民用犁耕地。 沒有任何王室成員來過下層社區，舊學校卻還掛著官方肖像。 新一輪修正脈衝的威脅，可能促使居民躲藏而不是作證。

By nightfall the newest report was signed by observers who were free to disagree. Mira stood beside Dax and looked at the distance between them and a fair decision that does not silence the people at risk. Progress felt less like triumph than like leaving a reliable mark on a difficult road. Someone would challenge that mark tomorrow. This time, there would be a public record with which to answer. Dax proposed a stroll to the gate, not a private confrontation. The diver's sun tan contrasted with the pale marks where his safety goggles had covered his face. The chairman called privacy a trifle; the students challenged him immediately.

夜幕降臨前，新的報告由可以提出異議的觀察者簽署。米拉站在達克斯身旁，看著他們與不讓受威脅人民失聲的公平決議之間的距離。進展不像勝利，比較像在艱難路上留下可靠的記號。明天有人會質疑，而這一次將有公開紀錄可以回答。 達克斯提議散步到大門，而不是私下對質。 潛水員曬黑的皮膚與護目鏡遮住的蒼白部分形成對比。 主席把隱私說成小事，學生立刻提出反駁。
