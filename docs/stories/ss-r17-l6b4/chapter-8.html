A Survivor Without a Universe

當所有平行世界決定關閉地球 · 第 8 / 25 章 · 5000–6000 · 英中對照 · 校訂中

用互動版查詞、朗讀與收藏

The morning had already acquired the look of an emergency before Mira reached the scene. A displaced scientist appears from a supposedly erased world and describes the forced evacuation of her family. An official wanted a clean story, with a beginning that could be announced and an ending that would require no questions. Mira had learned that reality rarely showed such courtesy. She made room for the people who had been ordered to wait outside. The warehouse nurse stored an antibiotic prescription in a locked cabinet. Staff chose to applaud the worker who had preserved the shipping record. An astronomer lent the exhibition hall a star chart for its ceiling projection. The group promised to computerize the warehouse register with individual logins.

在米拉抵達現場前，早晨已經有了緊急事件的模樣。一名來自據稱已遭抹除世界的流亡科學家現身，描述家人被強制撤離。官員想要一套簡潔的故事，開頭能宣布，結尾又不用回答問題。她知道現實很少如此客氣，於是請那些被命令等在門外的人進來說話。 倉庫護理師把抗生素處方保存在上鎖櫃中。 員工為保存出貨紀錄的同事鼓掌。 一位天文學家借給展示大廳星圖作天花板投影。 集團承諾把倉庫登記電腦化，並設個別登入帳號。

Dax examined the account twice, once as a friend and once as a witness who might have to defend every word. Her memories differ from the official closure report, but she carries physical instruments that can be tested. The difference between what they suspected and what they could establish mattered. They placed the new fact beside the previous chapter’s unanswered question and looked for a second source that had not been copied from the first. Delegates contributed observations from their separate worlds to the joint record: The damaged gate could disable the automatic alarm without warning. The technician told everyone to evacuate the loading area during inspection. The medical panel asked why expenditure on sleep control had tripled while ordinary clinics lost funding.

達克斯把紀錄看了兩次：第一次以朋友身分，第二次則像一位必須為每個字負責的證人。她的記憶與官方關閉報告不同，但帶著可檢測的實體儀器。懷疑和能確立的事實必須分清楚。他們把新線索放在前一章未解的問題旁，尋找不曾從同一份資料抄出的第二來源。代表也把各自世界的觀察加入共同紀錄： 損壞的閘門可能在無預警下使自動警報失效。 技師要求檢查期間所有人撤離裝卸區。 醫療小組詢問，為何睡眠控制支出增加三倍，一般診所預算卻遭削減。

A crowd gathered around the disputed notice. “You have to choose a side,” someone shouted. Mira answered that she had chosen one already: the side of a claim that survived questions. Dax did not smile, but moved the most vulnerable bystanders away from the growing argument. The pair could disagree privately without turning their disagreement into a public threat. An irritable official complained about the extra testing time. The lower district demanded that missing patients' kin be notified before any memory study began. A teacher offered a quiet meditation room for anxious children, while medical staff kept the treatment area clear.

人群圍住有爭議的公告。「妳必須選邊站！」有人喊。米拉回答自己早已選擇：站在經得起提問的主張那一邊。達克斯沒有微笑，只把最脆弱的旁觀者帶離爭執。他們可以私下不同意對方，卻不能把分歧變成公開威脅。 一名暴躁官員抱怨額外測試耗時。 下層社區要求，任何記憶研究開始前，都必須通知失蹤病患的親屬。 老師替焦慮的孩子安排安靜的冥想室，同時讓醫護治療區保持暢通。

The institution was not a single villain. It contained frightened clerks, dedicated workers, ambitious supervisors, and people who had learned to hide mistakes. the central archive wanted the incident classified as routine. Mira asked whose definition of routine was being used and whose losses were absent from the statistics. Nobody at the table could answer without consulting the sealed records. Delegates contributed observations from their separate worlds to the joint record: A loose nickel coin was found near the warehouse loading scale. The investigator used the floor map to orient new witnesses. An oriental carpet from the old lounge was moved away from the wet entrance. A child treated for pneumonia had been wrongly classified as suffering from forbidden-dream sickness.

這個機構不是單一惡棍。裡面有害怕的書記、盡責的工人、野心勃勃的主管，也有學會掩蓋錯誤的人。中央檔案庫想把事件歸類為例行事項。米拉追問：是誰定義例行？又有誰的損失被統計漏掉？桌邊沒人能在不看封存紀錄的情況下回答。代表也把各自世界的觀察加入共同紀錄： 倉庫裝卸秤旁發現一枚鬆落的五分鎳幣。 調查員用樓層圖協助新證人辨明方向。 舊休息室的東方風格地毯被移離潮濕入口。 一名肺炎孩童被錯誤分類為罹患禁忌夢境疾病。

Mira arranges independent analysis and protects the witness's identity. The decision did not repair everything. It altered one procedure, protected one person, or saved one piece of evidence; on a day when almost everyone demanded a miracle, that was worth recording. Mira insisted that the result and its limits appear together in the official minutes. Anything less would turn a modest gain into tomorrow’s misleading legend. The neurologist refused to activate the untested prototype until an ethics committee approved the trial. The psychiatry department confirmed that fear and loss of sleep could explain some patients' symptoms. The temporary handrail looked robust but still required a load test.

米拉安排獨立分析，保護證人身分。這個決定沒有修好一切，只改變一道程序、保護一個人，或留下了一份證據；當幾乎所有人都要求奇蹟時，這仍值得記錄。米拉堅持把成果和限制一起寫入正式紀錄。否則小小的進展又會變成明天誤導人的傳說。 在倫理委員會批准試驗前，神經科醫師拒絕啟動未測試的原型機。 精神醫學部確認，恐懼與睡眠不足能解釋部分病患症狀。 臨時扶手看起來堅固，仍須做負載測試。

The lights beyond the window did not offer comfort. They showed how many people were still waiting. Mira understood that the investigation might eventually expose her own earlier errors, and she resolved not to erase them. Dax suggested a short rest before the next hearing. She agreed, because exhaustion could damage judgment as thoroughly as deliberate deception. A sovereign emblem on the display wall was unrelated to warehouse ownership. The guild spokesperson confirmed the shipment delay without naming suspects. Behind the children's wardrobe, staff found an unused cable channel leading to the relay maintenance shaft.

窗外的燈光沒有帶來安慰，只照出還有多少人在等待。米拉明白調查最終可能揭露自己過去的錯誤，決定不去抹除。達克斯建議下一場聽證前短暫休息。她同意，因為疲憊和蓄意欺騙一樣可能損害判斷。 展示牆上的主權徽章與倉庫所有權無關。 公會發言人確認出貨延遲，卻沒有公布嫌疑人姓名。 兒童衣櫃後面，員工發現一條未使用的電纜通道，通往中繼器維修井。
