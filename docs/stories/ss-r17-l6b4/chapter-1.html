The Invitation to End Everything

當所有平行世界決定關閉地球 · 第 1 / 25 章 · 5000–6000 · 英中對照 · 校訂中

用互動版查詞、朗讀與收藏

Mira arrived with the unpleasant certainty that the simplest explanation was probably the one that had been prepared for public consumption. Mira Chen receives an invitation from twenty-seven parallel worlds to a meeting that will decide whether her Earth should continue to exist. The place was full of people who needed a quick answer, yet the evidence still belonged to those whose lives it could change. Instead of making a dramatic accusation, Mira marked the hour, the witnesses, and the smallest observable detail in a paper notebook. Mira checked the broken clasp on the changing-room gate before allowing any inspection. The porters asked permission to convene beneath the shelter roof. A painted otter cub smiled from a faded children's swimming poster. A dental outreach team had used the lobby for a free clinic.

米拉抵達時，心裡很清楚：最簡單的解釋往往是事先準備好要給大眾聽的版本。陳米拉收到來自二十七個平行世界的邀請，會議將決定她的地球能否繼續存在。現場的人都急需答案，但證據仍屬於那些生活可能被它改變的人。她沒有戲劇化地指控誰，而是先在紙本筆記上記下時刻、證人，以及最小卻可觀察的細節。 米拉先檢查更衣室門上損壞的門扣，才允許進行查驗。 搬運工請求在避難棚屋頂下集合開會。 褪色的兒童游泳海報上畫著一隻微笑的水獺幼獸。 牙科巡迴團隊曾借用大廳舉辦免費門診。

There was a moment of quiet when Dax set the new material on the table. The invitation includes a map of stars absent from her own sky and a deadline of thirty days. What mattered was not how surprising the discovery sounded, but whether anyone could repeat the test. Mira copied the relevant names carefully. If one person later changed the account, the original would still be available for comparison. Delegates contributed observations from their separate worlds to the joint record: The guild master behaved like a dictator when he threatened the tired porters. A midnight directive had ordered sick workers toward the monster tunnel. Her childhood dwelling was listed as demolished, although the old utility bill still showed monthly charges.

達克斯把新資料放上桌時，四周安靜了一瞬。邀請函附上她天空裡不存在的星圖，以及三十天期限。重要的不是發現有多驚人，而是別人能不能重做同一項測試。米拉仔細抄下相關姓名；如果日後有人更改說法，原件仍可供比對。代表也把各自世界的觀察加入共同紀錄： 公會長威脅疲憊的搬運工時，行徑就像獨裁者。 一份午夜指令曾要求生病工人前往魔物坑道。 她童年的住所被列為已拆除，舊水電帳單卻仍顯示每月收費。

“Will you trust me?” Dax asked. “With the evidence, yes,” Mira answered, “but not with a blank signature.” It was the sort of answer that could sound cold until someone noticed how carefully she had protected the other person’s right to disagree. Together they made a plan with a backup route, a deadline, and a record of who had accepted each responsibility. The messenger horse began to gallop when the black carriage appeared. Missing rest periods could heighten the risk of a tunnel accident. A janitor had seen the captain remove the overtime sheet from the wall.

「妳信任我嗎？」達克斯問。「證據上可以，空白授權書不行。」米拉回答。這聽起來很冷淡，直到人們注意到她也小心保護對方拒絕的權利。他們一起訂下有備用路線、期限，以及明確責任分工的計畫。 黑色馬車出現時，信使的馬開始奔馳。 缺乏休息可能加劇坑道事故的風險。 一名清潔員看見隊長從牆上取走加班表。

A representative of the central archive arrived with an offer to resolve the matter quietly. Quietly, in this case, meant without giving affected people access to the original. Mira declined. She knew an office could be useful and still be dangerously wrong. Her objection was narrow and specific: the proposed decision relied on a document whose origin had not yet been independently verified. Delegates contributed observations from their separate worlds to the joint record: Her mother's motto, written in blue ink, was simple: a warning is useful only if the listener understands it. A pilgrim outside offered the porters warm bread and a bed. No legal precedent allowed the World Assembly to erase an engineer's private maintenance notes without notice. The agency promised citizens they would reap peace if they surrendered their dreams.

中央檔案庫的代表前來，提議私下解決。所謂私下，就是不讓受影響的人看到原件。米拉拒絕了。她知道一個機關可以有用，也可能犯下危險的錯。她的反對具體而有限：提議中的決定依賴一份來源還沒接受獨立驗證的文件。代表也把各自世界的觀察加入共同紀錄： 母親用藍墨水寫的座右銘很簡單：警告只有讓人聽懂才有用。 一名門外的朝聖者提供搬運工熱麵包與床鋪。 沒有法律先例允許世界議會在不通知的情況下，抹去工程師的私人維修筆記。 當局承諾人民，只要交出夢境，就能收穫和平。

Mira demands a seat for Earth rather than allowing distant delegates to vote on humanity without its knowledge. A few witnesses thanked Mira; others accused her of doing too little. She accepted both reactions without offering a promise she could not keep. Dax wrote down the complaints, including the ones directed at their own conduct. They were not building a monument to themselves. They were building a process that would still function after they left. The mechanical clock remained stationary during the city's three-second power interruption, preserving a crucial discrepancy. Mira told the captain that loyalty was not a synonym for exhaustion. Her mother had once tried to transplant a blue orchard tree into a public courtyard.

米拉要求地球取得席次，不允許遠方代表在人類不知情時投票。幾名證人向米拉道謝，另一些人指責她做得不夠。她接受兩種反應，不許下無法兌現的承諾。達克斯寫下抱怨，包括針對他們自己行為的部分。他們不是為自己立紀念碑，而是建立自己離開後仍能運作的程序。 城市停電三秒時，機械鐘保持靜止，留下了一項關鍵的時間差異。 米拉告訴隊長，忠誠不是精疲力竭的同義詞。 她母親曾試圖把一棵藍色果園的樹移植到公共庭院。

By nightfall the newest report was signed by observers who were free to disagree. Mira stood beside Dax and looked at the distance between them and a fair decision that does not silence the people at risk. Progress felt less like triumph than like leaving a reliable mark on a difficult road. Someone would challenge that mark tomorrow. This time, there would be a public record with which to answer. A city trustee had authorized the transfer of children's records to an unlisted storage facility. The World Assembly demanded unconditional obedience, but its own equipment still required independent safety checks. She refused to utter a false confession just to keep her job.

夜幕降臨前，新的報告由可以提出異議的觀察者簽署。米拉站在達克斯身旁，看著他們與不讓受威脅人民失聲的公平決議之間的距離。進展不像勝利，比較像在艱難路上留下可靠的記號。明天有人會質疑，而這一次將有公開紀錄可以回答。 一名市府受託管理人曾批准把兒童紀錄轉往未列名的儲存設施。 世界議會要求無條件服從，自己的設備卻仍需要獨立安全檢查。 她拒絕為了保住工作而說出虛假的認罪詞。
