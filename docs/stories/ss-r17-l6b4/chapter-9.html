被發明的不穩定

The Invention of Instability

當所有平行世界決定關閉地球 · 第 9 / 25 章 · 5000–6000 · 英中對照 · 校訂中

用互動版查詞、朗讀與收藏

It began not with a battle but with an ordinary interruption in the Assembly of Worlds. Researchers discover that the archive's instability score includes political disagreement as if it were a physical hazard. At first, no one considered the interruption important enough to preserve. Mira thought otherwise. A forgotten detail could become a map, and a map could be used to control where frightened people were permitted to go. A teenager with acne said the clinic treated his ordinary discomfort as proof that dreams were dangerous. Only licensed staff could administer the sleep medication, yet automated records named absent employees. The recall brochure explained refunds in plain language. The children had been told that confinement protected them, but many described the clinic as captivity.

事情不是從戰鬥開始，而是從諸世界議會裡一次平凡的中斷開始。研究者發現，檔案庫把政治分歧也算進不穩定分數，彷彿那是物理危險。起初沒人認為這件事值得留下紀錄，但米拉想法不同。遭忽略的細節可能變成地圖，而地圖也可能被用來控制驚恐的人可以去哪裡。 一名長青春痘的少年說，診所把他的普通不適當成夢境有害的證據。 只有持照人員能施用安眠藥，系統紀錄卻列出當天缺席的員工。 召回手冊以簡明文字說明退款方法。 孩子們被告知隔離是保護，許多人卻把診所形容為囚禁。

Dax came back carrying a practical objection rather than an inspiring speech. A thriving world can therefore be labeled dangerous merely for refusing central control. They had both seen people harmed when an assumption hardened into policy. A signed report could be wrong, and an emotional memory could be right, but neither became proof merely by being repeated. They agreed to seek a measurement from an independent observer. Delegates contributed observations from their separate worlds to the joint record: Officials tried to cram decades of different histories into one approved textbook chapter. The technician showed how to detach the defective strap safely. One swimmer described mild discomfort after the new bench was installed.

達克斯帶回的是實際的異議，而不是振奮人心的演說。因此繁盛世界只要拒絕中央控制，也可能被標成危險。他們都見過假設硬化成政策後如何傷人。有簽名的報告可能是錯的，強烈的記憶也可能是真的；兩者都不會因為反覆提起就變成證據。他們決定找獨立觀察員再測量一次。代表也把各自世界的觀察加入共同紀錄： 官員試圖把幾十年的不同歷史硬塞進一章核准教科書。 技師示範如何安全拆下瑕疵固定帶。 一名泳者描述新長椅安裝後造成輕微不適。

“We can tell the truth without pretending we know everything,” Mira said. Dax replied that a partial truth was difficult to defend against people who controlled the doors. She answered that a perfect story with no verifiable source was more dangerous. They divided the work: one would protect the witnesses, while the other checked the physical records and the sequence of events. The forthcoming parent meeting would examine school transfer records rather than children's private dreams. A bright inspection lamp helped illuminate the hidden seam. The school kept a small jade token donated by a parent from the missing eastern district.

「我們能說實話，不需要假裝知道一切。」米拉說。達克斯回答，當對方掌控所有門口時，局部真相比較難捍衛。她則認為沒有可驗證來源的完美故事更危險。他們分配工作：一人保護證人，另一人核查實體紀錄和事件時序。 即將舉行的家長會將檢查學校轉學紀錄，而不是孩子的私人夢境。 明亮檢查燈照亮了隱藏縫線。 學校保存著一枚小玉牌，是失蹤東區一名家長捐贈的。

The argument moved from a private corridor into a formal room, where every statement would be copied. A senior official from the central archive demanded certainty before granting access. Mira offered a less comfortable bargain: a limited preservation order now, followed by independent examination and a chance for every accused person to respond. That was enough to prevent an irreversible decision. Delegates contributed observations from their separate worlds to the joint record: The children's joyous train drawings contrasted painfully with the city's official story of terror. The recall instructions were tested for different levels of reading literacy. The narrator of one memoir had deliberately hidden her own name. A participant reported irritation near one nostril from a synthetic scent.

爭論從私下走廊進入正式會議室，每句話都會留下副本。中央檔案庫的一名高級官員要求先確定真相才准查資料。米拉提出比較不舒服的交換：先給有期限的保全命令，再獨立檢查，也讓所有被指控者有回應機會。這足以擋下一個不可逆的決定。代表也把各自世界的觀察加入共同紀錄： 孩子們快樂的列車畫作，與城市官方的恐怖敘述形成令人心痛的對比。 召回說明依不同閱讀識字程度進行測試。 一部回憶錄的敘述者刻意隱去自己的名字。 一位參與者說合成香味刺激了單側鼻孔附近。

Mira demands separate scientific and political evaluations before any final vote. Even those who agreed with the choice could see its cost. Time was running out, supplies were not unlimited, and a mistake might travel far beyond this room. Dax asked whether they should conceal the uncertainty until the crisis passed. Mira shook her head. People could not make free choices if the information reaching them had already been edited. The craft collection's travel photographs had been taken along a quiet peninsula. The potent combination of censorship and repeated correction had distorted what many residents thought possible. The island's educational revival began with a humble promise: public warnings would be tested by the people reading them.

米拉要求最終投票前，把科學與政治評估分開。即使同意這個選擇的人，也看得出代價。時間不多、資源有限，一次錯誤可能傳得很遠。達克斯問是否該先隱瞞不確定性，等危機過後再說。米拉搖頭。若資訊早被剪裁，人們便無法作出自由的選擇。 工藝系列旅行照片拍攝於寧靜的半島。 審查與反覆修正的強大組合，扭曲了許多居民對可能性的認知。 島上教育復興始於一個樸素承諾：公共警示要由真正閱讀的人測試。

Later, under dim lights, Mira reread the original question. The dispute was larger now, but its center remained unchanged: who had the right to make an irreversible choice for someone else? Dax stayed until the last copy was sealed. Neither spoke about victory. They knew the next door would open only because today’s evidence had been preserved and today’s mistakes had been admitted. The selective deletion of destination schools made ordinary transfers look like spontaneous disappearances. One parent searched for a stepchild whose name appeared on the same vanished train list. A small trout appeared on the sustainable fabric's printed pattern.

晚些時候，在微弱燈光下，米拉重讀最初的問題。爭端已經擴大，但核心沒變：誰有權替別人作出不可逆的選擇？達克斯留到最後一份副本封存。他們沒有談勝利，只知道下一扇門之所以能打開，是因為今天保住了證據，也承認了錯誤。 選擇性刪除目的地學校，讓普通轉學看起來像突然失蹤。 一名家長尋找自己的繼子女，名字出現在同一份消失列車名單上。 永續布料的印花上有一條小鱒魚。
