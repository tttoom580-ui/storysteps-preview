第一次反地球表決

The First Vote Against Earth

當所有平行世界決定關閉地球 · 第 6 / 25 章 · 5000–6000 · 英中對照 · 校訂中

用互動版查詞、朗讀與收藏

It began not with a battle but with an ordinary interruption in the Assembly of Worlds. An early procedural vote denies Earth access to the archive because its observers lack permanent credentials. At first, no one considered the interruption important enough to preserve. Mira thought otherwise. A forgotten detail could become a map, and a map could be used to control where frightened people were permitted to go. The storage room needed an airtight container for archived water-test strips. The engineering team could not attain a safe restoration protocol without testing volunteers' reactions first. A congresswoman asked the industry to disclose its advertising practices. One sealed ration crate still contained curry packets labeled for the train station's night workers.

事情不是從戰鬥開始，而是從諸世界議會裡一次平凡的中斷開始。第一次程序表決以觀察員沒有永久憑證為由，拒絕地球查閱檔案。起初沒人認為這件事值得留下紀錄，但米拉想法不同。遭忽略的細節可能變成地圖，而地圖也可能被用來控制驚恐的人可以去哪裡。 儲藏室需要密封容器保存舊水質試紙。 工程團隊若不先測試自願者反應，就無法達成安全的記憶恢復程序。 一名女國公會會議員要求產業公開廣告手法。 一箱密封口糧裡仍有咖哩包，標示是給車站夜班工人的。

Dax came back carrying a practical objection rather than an inspiring speech. Several delegates admit the credentials can only be issued by the very archive under investigation. They had both seen people harmed when an assumption hardened into policy. A signed report could be wrong, and an emotional memory could be right, but neither became proof merely by being repeated. They agreed to seek a measurement from an independent observer. Delegates contributed observations from their separate worlds to the joint record: She did not despise reporters, only their unsupported assumptions. The archived sky schedule described an eclipse that public lessons insisted had never occurred. The hillside guide identified edible sea greens but warned volunteers not to gather plants near polluted drainage channels.

達克斯帶回的是實際的異議，而不是振奮人心的演說。數名代表承認，憑證只能由正受調查的檔案庫發給。他們都見過假設硬化成政策後如何傷人。有簽名的報告可能是錯的，強烈的記憶也可能是真的；兩者都不會因為反覆提起就變成證據。他們決定找獨立觀察員再測量一次。代表也把各自世界的觀察加入共同紀錄： 她不鄙視記者，只反對沒有根據的臆測。 封存的天空時刻表記載了一次日食，公共課程卻堅稱那從未發生。 山坡嚮導辨識可食用海藻，卻提醒志工不要在受污染排水口附近採集。

“We can tell the truth without pretending we know everything,” Mira said. Dax replied that a partial truth was difficult to defend against people who controlled the doors. She answered that a perfect story with no verifiable source was more dangerous. They divided the work: one would protect the witnesses, while the other checked the physical records and the sequence of events. Mira continued to hold her staff in high esteem during the scandal. A painted evergreen tree marked the maintenance tunnel that led away from the live network. A children's coat used a soft shark fin as a playful hood decoration.

「我們能說實話，不需要假裝知道一切。」米拉說。達克斯回答，當對方掌控所有門口時，局部真相比較難捍衛。她則認為沒有可驗證來源的完美故事更危險。他們分配工作：一人保護證人，另一人核查實體紀錄和事件時序。 風波中，米拉仍十分尊重自己的員工。 一棵彩繪常綠樹標記著通往現行網路之外的維修隧道。 一件童裝把柔軟鯊魚鰭做成帽子的趣味裝飾。

The argument moved from a private corridor into a formal room, where every statement would be copied. A senior official from the central archive demanded certainty before granting access. Mira offered a less comfortable bargain: a limited preservation order now, followed by independent examination and a chance for every accused person to respond. That was enough to prevent an irreversible decision. Delegates contributed observations from their separate worlds to the joint record: A flake of snow settled on the driver's route sheet. The inventory distinguished emergency supplies from merchandise once sold to the missing district. The monotony of the relay's steady hum made its sudden silence in the chamber feel frightening. Keeping paper travel diaries had once been a common pastime before the World Assembly discouraged writing.

爭論從私下走廊進入正式會議室，每句話都會留下副本。中央檔案庫的一名高級官員要求先確定真相才准查資料。米拉提出比較不舒服的交換：先給有期限的保全命令，再獨立檢查，也讓所有被指控者有回應機會。這足以擋下一個不可逆的決定。代表也把各自世界的觀察加入共同紀錄： 一片雪花落在司機的路線表上。 清冊將緊急物資與昔日出售給失蹤行政區的商品分開。 中繼器單調不變的嗡鳴，讓維修室裡突如其來的寂靜格外可怕。 在世界議會不鼓勵書寫以前，紙本旅行日記曾是常見消遣。

Mira appeals the circular rule and gains temporary access from a minority coalition. Even those who agreed with the choice could see its cost. Time was running out, supplies were not unlimited, and a mistake might travel far beyond this room. Dax asked whether they should conceal the uncertainty until the crisis passed. Mira shook her head. People could not make free choices if the information reaching them had already been edited. The council removed redundancy from three overlapping reporting forms. Paparazzi were not permitted to roam through the private workshop. The cleaning team asked visitors to put rubbish in the marked bins.

米拉上訴這個循環規則，獲少數聯盟支持取得暫時權限。即使同意這個選擇的人，也看得出代價。時間不多、資源有限，一次錯誤可能傳得很遠。達克斯問是否該先隱瞞不確定性，等危機過後再說。米拉搖頭。若資訊早被剪裁，人們便無法作出自由的選擇。 議會刪除三份重複報表中的冗餘內容。 狗仔不得在私人公會工坊裡四處遊走。 清潔團隊請訪客將垃圾投入標示清楚的桶子。

Later, under dim lights, Mira reread the original question. The dispute was larger now, but its center remained unchanged: who had the right to make an irreversible choice for someone else? Dax stayed until the last copy was sealed. Neither spoke about victory. They knew the next door would open only because today’s evidence had been preserved and today’s mistakes had been admitted. An official poster promised to slay the 'monster of dreams,' turning ordinary sleep into an imagined enemy. The sly caption implied promises neither person had made. Warm vapor rose from her father's curry pot as she entered home.

晚些時候，在微弱燈光下，米拉重讀最初的問題。爭端已經擴大，但核心沒變：誰有權替別人作出不可逆的選擇？達克斯留到最後一份副本封存。他們沒有談勝利，只知道下一扇門之所以能打開，是因為今天保住了證據，也承認了錯誤。 官方海報宣稱要殺死「夢的怪物」，把普通睡眠塑造成虛構敵人。 狡猾的標題暗示兩人從未做過的承諾。 她回家時，父親的咖哩鍋冒出溫熱蒸氣。
