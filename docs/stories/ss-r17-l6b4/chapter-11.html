地球的回答

The Earth's Answer

當所有平行世界決定關閉地球 · 第 11 / 25 章 · 5000–6000 · 英中對照 · 校訂中

The morning had already acquired the look of an emergency before Mira reached the scene. People on Earth respond with fear, anger, scientific questions, and many incompatible hopes for the future. An official wanted a clean story, with a beginning that could be announced and an ending that would require no questions. Mira had learned that reality rarely showed such courtesy. She made room for the people who had been ordered to wait outside. The family archive had won international acclaim before the payment questions emerged. The shared-account policy contained an ambiguity that allowed several technicians to use one password without recording their names. The medical panel distinguished bodily harm from ordinary discomfort reported during sleep. A cold draught crossed the hearing room when someone opened the ventilation hatch.

在米拉抵達現場前，早晨已經有了緊急事件的模樣。地球居民回以恐懼、憤怒、科學問題和彼此不同的未來希望。官員想要一套簡潔的故事，開頭能宣布，結尾又不用回答問題。她知道現實很少如此客氣，於是請那些被命令等在門外的人進來說話。 付款疑雲浮現前，家族檔案曾享有國際讚譽。 共用帳號政策有一處模糊之處，容許多名技師共用密碼而不記姓名。 醫療小組區分身體傷害與睡眠時的一般不適。 有人打開通風口時，一股冷風穿過聽證室。

Dax examined the account twice, once as a friend and once as a witness who might have to defend every word. A single representative cannot honestly compress those voices into one command. The difference between what they suspected and what they could establish mattered. They placed the new fact beside the previous chapter’s unanswered question and looked for a second source that had not been copied from the first. Delegates contributed observations from their separate worlds to the joint record: The archived machine log measured heat in Fahrenheit, unlike the city's present metric settings. The original train invoices survived inside a fireproof document case beneath the market counter. The castle council reviewed the geographical reach of the trial stores.

達克斯把紀錄看了兩次：第一次以朋友身分，第二次則像一位必須為每個字負責的證人。一名代表無法誠實地把多種聲音壓成一道命令。懷疑和能確立的事實必須分清楚。他們把新線索放在前一章未解的問題旁，尋找不曾從同一份資料抄出的第二來源。代表也把各自世界的觀察加入共同紀錄： 封存機械紀錄用華氏溫度量測熱能，與現行公制設定不同。 原始列車發票存放在市場櫃檯下的防火文件箱，因此得以倖存。 城堡議會檢查試辦門市的地理分布。

A crowd gathered around the disputed notice. “You have to choose a side,” someone shouted. Mira answered that she had chosen one already: the side of a claim that survived questions. Dax did not smile, but moved the most vulnerable bystanders away from the growing argument. The pair could disagree privately without turning their disagreement into a public threat. An outside hacker had not yet been ruled out in the access investigation. Even a layman could see that the contract granted indefinite data rights. Transparent voting rules could liberate employees from private pressure.

人群圍住有爭議的公告。「妳必須選邊站！」有人喊。米拉回答自己早已選擇：站在經得起提問的主張那一邊。達克斯沒有微笑，只把最脆弱的旁觀者帶離爭執。他們可以私下不同意對方，卻不能把分歧變成公開威脅。 存取調查仍未排除外部駭客的可能。 即使外行人也看得出契約授予無限期資料使用權。 透明表決規則能使員工不再受私人壓力束縛。

The institution was not a single villain. It contained frightened clerks, dedicated workers, ambitious supervisors, and people who had learned to hide mistakes. the central archive wanted the incident classified as routine. Mira asked whose definition of routine was being used and whose losses were absent from the statistics. Nobody at the table could answer without consulting the sealed records. Delegates contributed observations from their separate worlds to the joint record: A mermaid mural from the lost railway appeared on two photographs taken decades apart. The seized archive included miscellaneous receipts that proved surprisingly important to the timeline. The committee recorded every occurrence of unauthorized contract changes. A staff outing was postponed so employees could attend the trust hearing.

這個機構不是單一惡棍。裡面有害怕的書記、盡責的工人、野心勃勃的主管，也有學會掩蓋錯誤的人。中央檔案庫想把事件歸類為例行事項。米拉追問：是誰定義例行？又有誰的損失被統計漏掉？桌邊沒人能在不看封存紀錄的情況下回答。代表也把各自世界的觀察加入共同紀錄： 失蹤鐵路上的美人魚壁畫，出現在相隔幾十年拍攝的兩張照片裡。 查扣檔案裡有各種雜項收據，對重建時間線竟出乎意料重要。 委員會記錄每次未授權修改合約的事件。 為讓員工參與信託聽證，城堡部門出遊延期。

Mira creates a transparent citizens' forum and reports its disagreements as well as its agreements. The decision did not repair everything. It altered one procedure, protected one person, or saved one piece of evidence; on a day when almost everyone demanded a miracle, that was worth recording. Mira insisted that the result and its limits appear together in the official minutes. Anything less would turn a modest gain into tomorrow’s misleading legend. The staff promised to persevere through the difficult budget review. The judge asked the court to ponder how a government could collect taxes from an officially nonexistent district. The committee's questions continued to revolve around the missing payments.

米拉設立透明公民論壇，既報告共識也報告歧見。這個決定沒有修好一切，只改變一道程序、保護一個人，或留下了一份證據；當幾乎所有人都要求奇蹟時，這仍值得記錄。米拉堅持把成果和限制一起寫入正式紀錄。否則小小的進展又會變成明天誤導人的傳說。 員工承諾堅持完成艱難的預算審查。 法官請法庭思考：政府怎能向官方不存在的行政區收稅？ 委員會的問題始終圍繞失蹤款項。

The lights beyond the window did not offer comfort. They showed how many people were still waiting. Mira understood that the investigation might eventually expose her own earlier errors, and she resolved not to erase them. Dax suggested a short rest before the next hearing. She agreed, because exhaustion could damage judgment as thoroughly as deliberate deception. A metal kitchen utensil bearing the erased district's address was admitted as a physical exhibit. Mira refused to turn the contractor into a cartoon villain without evidence. A market yogurt container carried a printed delivery address from the supposedly destroyed station district.

窗外的燈光沒有帶來安慰，只照出還有多少人在等待。米拉明白調查最終可能揭露自己過去的錯誤，決定不去抹除。達克斯建議下一場聽證前短暫休息。她同意，因為疲憊和蓄意欺騙一樣可能損害判斷。 一件刻著被抹除行政區地址的金屬廚具，被列為實體證物。 沒有證據前，米拉拒絕把承包商塑造成漫畫式惡棍。 一個市場優格容器印著據稱已毀車站區的送貨地址。
