A Crack in the Boundary

當所有平行世界決定關閉地球 · 第 3 / 25 章 · 5000–6000 · 英中對照 · 校訂中

用互動版查詞、朗讀與收藏

It began not with a battle but with an ordinary interruption in the Assembly of Worlds. Scientists from three worlds demonstrate that energy is leaking through a crack between neighboring realities. At first, no one considered the interruption important enough to preserve. Mira thought otherwise. A forgotten detail could become a map, and a map could be used to control where frightened people were permitted to go. The cashier's handwritten signature on the toy receipt survived two rounds of official records cleaning. A routine checkup form showed that the children were healthy before the forced relocation. Police claimed the midnight searches would curb public panic, but the raids only frightened residents. The railway alarm could deafen passengers standing too close to the old tunnel speakers.

事情不是從戰鬥開始，而是從諸世界議會裡一次平凡的中斷開始。三個世界的科學家示範，能量正從相鄰現實間的裂縫流失。起初沒人認為這件事值得留下紀錄，但米拉想法不同。遭忽略的細節可能變成地圖，而地圖也可能被用來控制驚恐的人可以去哪裡。 玩具收據上收銀員的親筆簽名，熬過了兩輪官方檔案清理。 例行健康檢查表顯示，孩子們在強制搬遷前身體健康。 警方宣稱午夜搜查能遏止恐慌，突襲卻只讓居民更加害怕。 鐵路警報足以震聾站在舊隧道喇叭旁的乘客。

Dax came back carrying a practical objection rather than an inspiring speech. The council blames Earth's experiments but cannot produce the original measurements. They had both seen people harmed when an assumption hardened into policy. A signed report could be wrong, and an emotional memory could be right, but neither became proof merely by being repeated. They agreed to seek a measurement from an independent observer. Delegates contributed observations from their separate worlds to the joint record: The supervisor called the stolen design a disgrace to his department. The crew documented the safe disposal of contaminated fuel instead of pouring it into the tidewater behind the station. A technician recorded her dissent from the first inspection report.

達克斯帶回的是實際的異議，而不是振奮人心的演說。議會責怪地球的實驗，卻拿不出原始測量。他們都見過假設硬化成政策後如何傷人。有簽名的報告可能是錯的，強烈的記憶也可能是真的；兩者都不會因為反覆提起就變成證據。他們決定找獨立觀察員再測量一次。代表也把各自世界的觀察加入共同紀錄： 指揮官稱設計遭竊是城堡部門的恥辱。 船員記錄受污染燃料的安全處置，沒有把它倒進站後海水裡。 一位技師對首份檢驗報告提出並記錄異議。

“We can tell the truth without pretending we know everything,” Mira said. Dax replied that a partial truth was difficult to defend against people who controlled the doors. She answered that a perfect story with no verifiable source was more dangerous. They divided the work: one would protect the witnesses, while the other checked the physical records and the sequence of events. A fisher who had planned to emigrate returned for his mother's treatment and now depended on the night ferry. The false relocation policy did nothing to enrich the children whose school materials were confiscated. A lengthy transport invoice listed train journeys that were absent from the official timetable.

「我們能說實話，不需要假裝知道一切。」米拉說。達克斯回答，當對方掌控所有門口時，局部真相比較難捍衛。她則認為沒有可驗證來源的完美故事更危險。他們分配工作：一人保護證人，另一人核查實體紀錄和事件時序。 一名原本打算移民的漁民因母親治療返鄉，如今仰賴夜間渡輪。 錯誤的搬遷政策沒有改善孩子生活，反而沒收了他們的學習用品。 一張冗長運輸發票列出官方時刻表上不存在的列車旅程。

The argument moved from a private corridor into a formal room, where every statement would be copied. A senior official from the central archive demanded certainty before granting access. Mira offered a less comfortable bargain: a limited preservation order now, followed by independent examination and a chance for every accused person to respond. That was enough to prevent an irreversible decision. Delegates contributed observations from their separate worlds to the joint record: For a lifelong resident, the changed light was more than a technical issue; it altered familiar night routes. The vendor said, 'Madam, you can copy this page, but the original stays with me.' A melancholy railway tune returned to the vendor whenever rain struck the market roof. The lawyer checked whether a private interview was permissible under the order.

爭論從私下走廊進入正式會議室，每句話都會留下副本。中央檔案庫的一名高級官員要求先確定真相才准查資料。米拉提出比較不舒服的交換：先給有期限的保全命令，再獨立檢查，也讓所有被指控者有回應機會。這足以擋下一個不可逆的決定。代表也把各自世界的觀察加入共同紀錄： 對一名終生住在島上的居民而言，變動的燈光不只是技術問題，也改變熟悉的夜間路線。 攤販說：「女士，妳可以抄這一頁，但正本要留在我手裡。」 每當雨打在市場屋頂，攤販便想起一段憂傷的鐵路旋律。 律師查核依命令是否允許私下訪談。

Mira requests independent observations on both sides of the crack. Even those who agreed with the choice could see its cost. Time was running out, supplies were not unlimited, and a mistake might travel far beyond this room. Dax asked whether they should conceal the uncertainty until the crisis passed. Mira shook her head. People could not make free choices if the information reaching them had already been edited. The public speakers continued to preach obedience while officers confiscated the stalls' paper records. The counterfeit stitching was reminiscent of her earliest student experiments. The rivalry between distributors made the evidence politically sensitive.

米拉要求裂縫兩側都進行獨立觀測。即使同意這個選擇的人，也看得出代價。時間不多、資源有限，一次錯誤可能傳得很遠。達克斯問是否該先隱瞞不確定性，等危機過後再說。米拉搖頭。若資訊早被剪裁，人們便無法作出自由的選擇。 警員沒收攤商紙本紀錄時，公共喇叭仍不斷宣講服從的重要。 仿品縫法令人想起她學生時代最早的試作。 經銷商之間的競爭讓證據變得敏感。

Later, under dim lights, Mira reread the original question. The dispute was larger now, but its center remained unchanged: who had the right to make an irreversible choice for someone else? Dax stayed until the last copy was sealed. Neither spoke about victory. They knew the next door would open only because today’s evidence had been preserved and today’s mistakes had been admitted. The suspicious transport account deserved scrutiny from an independent auditor, not an instant accusation. The staff waited in suspense for the court's preservation decision. The old railway trademark on a receipt matched the symbol painted beneath the clinic's animals.

晚些時候，在微弱燈光下，米拉重讀最初的問題。爭端已經擴大，但核心沒變：誰有權替別人作出不可逆的選擇？達克斯留到最後一份副本封存。他們沒有談勝利，只知道下一扇門之所以能打開，是因為今天保住了證據，也承認了錯誤。 可疑的運輸帳戶需要獨立稽核員仔細審查，而不是立刻定罪。 員工懷著懸念等待法院的保全裁定。 收據上的舊鐵路商標，與診所動物壁畫底下的符號吻合。
