另一個地球

The Other Earth

當所有平行世界決定關閉地球 · 第 12 / 25 章 · 5000–6000 · 英中對照 · 校訂中

用互動版查詞、朗讀與收藏

It began not with a battle but with an ordinary interruption in the Assembly of Worlds. An alternate Earth sends a delegate whose society survived a similar crisis by accepting strict central oversight. At first, no one considered the interruption important enough to preserve. Mira thought otherwise. A forgotten detail could become a map, and a map could be used to control where frightened people were permitted to go. A laboratory attendant identified the storage shelves where the children's original treatment registers had been kept. The cooking class showed how batter could stain a cotton apron. The old census counted hundreds of children absent from the city's approved founding population. A retired chemist explained how old ink could be dated without damaging the children's records.

事情不是從戰鬥開始，而是從諸世界議會裡一次平凡的中斷開始。另一個地球派代表出席，其社會曾藉嚴格中央監督度過類似危機。起初沒人認為這件事值得留下紀錄，但米拉想法不同。遭忽略的細節可能變成地圖，而地圖也可能被用來控制驚恐的人可以去哪裡。 一名實驗室服務員指出，兒童原始治療名冊存放在哪些架上。 烹飪課示範麵糊如何弄髒棉圍裙。 舊人口普查列出數百名不在官方建城人口名單上的兒童。 退休化學家解釋如何不損傷兒童紀錄，就能測定舊墨水年代。

Dax came back carrying a practical objection rather than an inspiring speech. She warns Mira that unregulated experiments can harm neighboring worlds. They had both seen people harmed when an assumption hardened into policy. A signed report could be wrong, and an emotional memory could be right, but neither became proof merely by being repeated. They agreed to seek a measurement from an independent observer. Delegates contributed observations from their separate worlds to the joint record: Birds began to chirp outside when the power returned. Only licensed nurses could dispense medication during the early voluntary memory trials. A volunteer gave a tired groan while carrying the last folding chair.

達克斯帶回的是實際的異議，而不是振奮人心的演說。她警告米拉，無監管實驗可能傷害鄰近世界。他們都見過假設硬化成政策後如何傷人。有簽名的報告可能是錯的，強烈的記憶也可能是真的；兩者都不會因為反覆提起就變成證據。他們決定找獨立觀察員再測量一次。代表也把各自世界的觀察加入共同紀錄： 電力恢復時，窗外鳥兒開始啁啾。 早期自願記憶試驗期間，只有持照護理師能配藥。 搬最後一張折疊椅時，志工疲倦地呻吟。

“We can tell the truth without pretending we know everything,” Mira said. Dax replied that a partial truth was difficult to defend against people who controlled the doors. She answered that a perfect story with no verifiable source was more dangerous. They divided the work: one would protect the witnesses, while the other checked the physical records and the sequence of events. An apprentice compared hidden production costs to the unseen part of an iceberg. The fire service gave invaluable advice on the safe collection point. A lavish renovation sketch looked impressive but lacked a credible operating budget.

「我們能說實話，不需要假裝知道一切。」米拉說。達克斯回答，當對方掌控所有門口時，局部真相比較難捍衛。她則認為沒有可驗證來源的完美故事更危險。他們分配工作：一人保護證人，另一人核查實體紀錄和事件時序。 一位學徒把隱藏成本比作冰山看不見的部分。 消防單位對安全領書點提供極有價值的建議。 豪華整修草圖令人驚艷，卻缺乏可信的營運預算。

The argument moved from a private corridor into a formal room, where every statement would be copied. A senior official from the central archive demanded certainty before granting access. Mira offered a less comfortable bargain: a limited preservation order now, followed by independent examination and a chance for every accused person to respond. That was enough to prevent an irreversible decision. Delegates contributed observations from their separate worlds to the joint record: The apprentices designed a logo that belonged to their training cooperative. A loudspeaker ordered visitors to leave, but the judge's preservation warrant kept the examination team inside. New martial security regulations threatened to transfer the medical archive to an armed enforcement office. No confirmed outbreak preceded the children's transfers, despite what the founding textbooks claimed.

爭論從私下走廊進入正式會議室，每句話都會留下副本。中央檔案庫的一名高級官員要求先確定真相才准查資料。米拉提出比較不舒服的交換：先給有期限的保全命令，再獨立檢查，也讓所有被指控者有回應機會。這足以擋下一個不可逆的決定。代表也把各自世界的觀察加入共同紀錄： 學徒設計了一個屬於培訓合作社的標誌。 擴音器命令訪客離開，但法官的保全令使檢查小組得以留下。 新的軍事化安全規章威脅要把醫療檔案移交武裝執法辦公室。 儘管建城教科書如此宣稱，兒童轉移之前沒有任何確診疫情爆發。

Mira accepts the warning while rejecting the idea that safety requires erasure. Even those who agreed with the choice could see its cost. Time was running out, supplies were not unlimited, and a mistake might travel far beyond this room. Dax asked whether they should conceal the uncertainty until the crisis passed. Mira shook her head. People could not make free choices if the information reaching them had already been edited. A local café served oyster soup at the fundraising dinner for adult volunteers. The early engineers never intended the technology to populate every home with compulsory monitors. The sudden power failure was a setback, not the end of the lesson.

米拉接受警告，卻拒絕把安全等同於抹除。即使同意這個選擇的人，也看得出代價。時間不多、資源有限，一次錯誤可能傳得很遠。達克斯問是否該先隱瞞不確定性，等危機過後再說。米拉搖頭。若資訊早被剪裁，人們便無法作出自由的選擇。 當地咖啡館在成年志工募款晚餐供應牡蠣湯。 早期工程師從未打算讓強制監測環遍布每戶家庭。 突然停電是挫折，卻不是課程終點。

Later, under dim lights, Mira reread the original question. The dispute was larger now, but its center remained unchanged: who had the right to make an irreversible choice for someone else? Dax stayed until the last copy was sealed. Neither spoke about victory. They knew the next door would open only because today’s evidence had been preserved and today’s mistakes had been admitted. Some residents remained skeptical of the proposed licensed detour. Visitors could subscribe to updates without buying the craft collection. The charity promised not to withhold apprentice wages during an audit.

晚些時候，在微弱燈光下，米拉重讀最初的問題。爭端已經擴大，但核心沒變：誰有權替別人作出不可逆的選擇？達克斯留到最後一份副本封存。他們沒有談勝利，只知道下一扇門之所以能打開，是因為今天保住了證據，也承認了錯誤。 部分居民仍懷疑提議中的合格繞道路線。 來賓不必購買工藝系列，也能訂閱更新資訊。 慈善機構承諾稽核期間不扣留學徒薪水。
