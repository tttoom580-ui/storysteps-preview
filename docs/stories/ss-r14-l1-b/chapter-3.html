學習如何觀察

Learning to Watch

霧杉谷的第一張天氣地圖 · 第 3 / 25 章 · 1000以下 · 英中對照 · 校訂中

用互動版查詞、朗讀與收藏

On Monday, Lumi went to the library to begin a short class. All the students needed to define three simple words: wind, rain, and cloud. Biko drew a lamp beside the words so that everyone would remember to read the board in good light. Only a few children had ever taken weather notes before. Their teacher put an orange eraser on the table and said, “A mistake is not a disaster. We can erase a wrong mark, but we must not hide it.” The class split the valley into four small areas, each with a safe place to stand and a simple way to name the sky. A basketball rested beside the door, and its owner agreed to leave it outside the work area. 'We will keep the table clear,' he said.

星期一，露米去圖書館參加短課。所有學生都要先定義三個簡單詞：風、雨和雲。比可在詞旁畫了一盞燈，提醒大家要在光線充足時看板。之前只有少數孩子做過天氣紀錄。老師把一塊橘色橡皮擦放在桌上說：「寫錯並不可怕。我們可以擦掉錯誤記號，但不能隱瞞。」全班把山谷分成四個小區域，每區都選擇安全的站立點和描述天空的方法。 門旁放著一顆籃球，球的主人答應把它移到工作區外。「我們會保持桌面乾淨，」他說。

The new school building had a modern computer, but their notes would begin on paper. At a quarter past three p.m., they would send one email with the day's numbers to the teacher. If the traffic near the school was heavy, no student would cross the road alone to deliver notes. A parent could mail the page later, or the class could read it the next day. They planted nothing yet, although one boy wanted to bring a plant to mark the wind. “A living thing is not a tool we can leave in a bad place,” Lumi said. They chose painted wooden signs instead.

新校舍有現代電腦，但紀錄仍先寫在紙上。他們約定每天下午三點十五分寄一封電子郵件，把當天數字交給老師。如果學校附近交通繁忙，任何學生都不能獨自過馬路送筆記；家長可以晚點郵寄紙張，或讓全班隔天再讀。有個男孩想搬一盆植物當風向記號，大家卻先不種任何東西。「活的東西不是可以隨便留在危險地方的工具，」露米說。於是他們改選塗色木牌。

Biko learned that a flag points in the direction the wind blows toward, but a weather report often names the direction the wind comes from. He made two arrows on separate cards. The first showed where the flag went; the second showed where the wind began. The children practiced with a fan inside the room. A girl gave the wrong answer and laughed. No one made fun of her. They used the eraser, turned the fan, and tried again. By the end, the class had learned less about predicting tomorrow than about asking careful questions today. Lumi thought that was a good trade.

比可學到旗子指向風正在吹去的方向，但天氣報告常用風吹來的方向命名。他做了兩張箭頭卡，一張表示旗子飄向哪裡，另一張表示風從哪裡來。孩子們在室內用電扇練習。一位女孩答錯後笑了，沒有人取笑她。他們用橡皮擦修改、調整電扇，再試一次。下課時，他們對預測明天知道得不多，卻更懂得今天如何仔細提問。露米覺得這樣的收穫很值得。

At noon the following day, Lumi visited the south area with Oren, a deer who walked slowly to read each sign. They watched an old orange leaf move across the path. Oren noted the time before he noted the direction. He said that his family often traveled at night when the road was quiet, and asked whether night observations would be possible. Lumi wanted everyone included, but she remembered their rule. No child would collect data outside after dark. Adults could make a report from a safe window, or the box could stay empty until morning. Oren agreed, and drew a moon beside that empty box.

隔天中午，露米和走路時總慢慢讀標示的鹿奧倫，一起到南邊區域。他們看著一片老橘葉飄過小路。奧倫先記時間，再記方向。他說家人常在夜晚路上安靜時出門，問是否能做夜間觀察。露米想讓每個人參與，卻記得安全規則。孩子不能天黑後到戶外採集數據。大人可以從安全窗邊回報，或讓方格空到早晨。奧倫同意，在空格旁畫了一輪月亮。

The class made a simple promise on a large sheet. Each report would say who had looked, when, and where. If a report came late, it would keep its real time, not the time the teacher received it. The students added this rule after two pieces of paper arrived together from different days. They had nearly placed both on the same line. “Good records are like good stories,” the teacher said. “They need the right order.” Lumi copied the promise into her notebook. She knew the first real map might look plain, but the valley could learn from plain marks if they were placed honestly.

全班在大紙上寫下約定：每份回報都要註明觀察者、時間與地點。如果資料晚到，仍保留實際觀察時間，而不是老師收到的時間。這條規則來自兩張不同日期的紙同時送到的事件，他們差點把兩張都放進同一欄。「好紀錄和好故事一樣，」老師說，「順序必須正確。」露米把約定抄進筆記本。她知道第一張真正的地圖可能很樸素，但只要標記誠實，山谷就能從中學到東西。

Before the class ended, the children placed their first four marks on the wall map. One showed a calm morning, two showed light wind, and one had no reading because its volunteer was ill. Biko wanted to fill that hole with the number next to it. Lumi shook her head. “The blank belongs there.” The teacher smiled and left the blank in place. It was a small but important choice. The map now showed not only what the children knew, but also where they needed help. When the school bell rang, Lumi left with a lighter bag and a clearer plan for the week ahead.

下課前，孩子們在牆上地圖貼出第一批四個記號：一處早晨無風、兩處微風，另有一處因志工生病而沒有紀錄。比可想拿隔壁的數字填空。露米搖頭。「那個空白就該留著。」老師微笑著保留空格。這雖是小決定，卻很重要。地圖不只顯示孩子們知道什麼，也顯示哪裡還需要幫助。放學鐘響時，露米帶著較輕的書包和更清楚的下週計畫離開。
