熊的照片

The Bear's Photograph

霧杉谷的第一張天氣地圖 · 第 9 / 25 章 · 1000以下 · 英中對照 · 校訂中

A bear named Haku visited Moss Vale with a photograph of a low cloud over the western farm. He was a farmer from another valley, and he hoped the picture would help the map team. “You are brave to show your work to people you do not know,” said Lumi. Haku looked sad. He had lost his old weather book on the way and felt lonely without it. A foreigner traveling with him offered to help look for the book, but Haku shook his head. “Nothing in that book can be copied if we do not have the dates,” he said. “I will keep looking, and I will tell you what I can remember.” He did not want to lose the memory of the days he had spent watching the fields.

名叫哈庫的熊帶著一張照片來到霧杉谷，照片拍到西邊農場上方的一朵低雲。他是外地山谷的農民，希望照片能幫上地圖團隊。「你願意把作品給不認識的人看，很勇敢，」露米說。哈庫神情難過，因為他在路上弄丟舊天氣簿，沒了它就感到孤單。同行的外地旅客提議幫忙找，但哈庫搖頭。「沒有日期，簿子裡的紀錄就無法準確重建，」他說，「我會繼續尋找，也會告訴你們我還記得什麼。」 他不想失去那些每天觀察田野的珍貴記憶。

Haku stayed at the guest house beside the warm kitchen. He ate bread and drank milk, but chose no meat that day. At supper, a singer from the town café came to welcome him. A small bell gave a clear ring whenever the door opened. Lumi put the photograph on a table and asked whose camera had taken it. “Mine,” Haku answered, “but my friend pressed the button.” Biko wrote both names down. He had learned that knowing who made a picture mattered. The singer looked at the cloud and thought it meant a storm. Haku answered gently that the picture alone could not tell them that.

哈庫住在溫暖廚房旁的旅館。他吃麵包、喝牛奶，那天沒有選肉食。晚餐時，鎮上咖啡館的歌手來歡迎他。門一開，小鈴鐺就清脆地響。露米把照片放在桌上，問是誰的相機拍的。「是我的，」哈庫回答，「但按快門的是朋友。」比可把兩個名字都寫下。他已經學到照片來源很重要。歌手看著雲，覺得那代表暴風雨。哈庫溫和地回答，單靠一張照片無法得知。

In the morning, Haku walked with the team to the public wall near the market. He pointed to a space where visitors could leave questions. A little child asked where the toilet was, and Lumi stopped to show her the sign before returning to the map. Haku liked that. “A helpful person does not have to finish one task before seeing another person's need,” he said. He took a new photo of the weather board, making sure the date was easy to read. Then he promised to send it to the other valley as an example of a group learning to work together. He did not call the work perfect.

早上，哈庫跟團隊走到市場旁的公共牆。他指著一塊空位，說可以讓訪客留下問題。一個小孩問廁所在哪裡，露米先帶她看指示牌，才回來繼續談地圖。哈庫很喜歡這樣。「會幫忙的人，不必等做完一件事才注意到別人的需要，」他說。他替看板拍了新照片，讓日期清楚可見，並答應寄給另一個山谷，當作大家學習合作的例子。他沒有把成果說成完美。

That afternoon, someone found Haku's lost book under a seat at the guest house. The cover was wet, but the pages could still be read. Haku thanked the cleaner, then compared three entries with his photograph. Only one date matched. Lumi saw how much trouble a lost book could cause even when the owner remembered the weather well. Haku let the children copy the one matching entry, with his name and the place written beside it. They did not copy the other two. It felt strange to leave useful-looking numbers behind, but the team had agreed not to mix uncertain dates into the main record.

下午，有人在旅館座位下找到哈庫遺失的簿子。封面濕了，頁面仍可閱讀。哈庫向清潔人員道謝，接著把三筆紀錄和照片比較。只有一個日期吻合。露米發現，即使主人很熟悉天氣，遺失簿子仍可能帶來不少麻煩。哈庫讓孩子們抄下唯一吻合的資料，並在旁邊註明姓名與地點；其他兩筆沒有抄進去。留下那些看似有用的數字有點奇怪，但團隊早已約定不把日期不確定的資料混進主要紀錄。

Before leaving, Haku told the children that a weather map must belong to no single person. “If only one person understands it, what happens when that person goes away?” he asked. Lumi had never considered that. She asked Biko to show Miri how to read the measurements, and Miri showed Oren how to check dates. Soon three people could explain the same page. Haku watched them practice and laughed. “Well done,” he said. He looked less lonely now. He had come with one photograph, but he was leaving with new friends and a promise to exchange careful reports, not guesses.

離開前，哈庫告訴孩子，天氣地圖不該只屬於一個人。「如果只有一個人看得懂，等他離開了怎麼辦？」他問。露米從沒想過這一點。她請比可教米莉讀數，米莉再教奧倫核對日期。很快三個人都能解釋同一頁。哈庫看著他們練習，笑著說：「做得很好。」他看起來沒那麼孤單了。他帶來一張照片，離開時卻多了新朋友，也有了交換可靠觀察、而非猜測的約定。

The following week, a report arrived from Haku's valley. It described a cool morning with no rain, while Moss Vale had enjoyed a warm one. Biko wanted to put both results in the same box. Lumi suggested two boxes with clear place names instead. The visitors and the village lived under one wide sky, but their hills and fields were different. That difference was part of the story. Haku's photograph stayed beside the first report, and the children wrote a thank-you note. The small exchange showed them how a local map might connect neighbors without pretending that all weather was the same.

下一週，哈庫山谷的回報送到了：那邊早晨涼爽、沒有下雨，霧杉谷則暖和。比可想把兩個結果放進同一格，露米建議分成兩格並寫清地名。雖然大家住在同一片廣闊天空下，丘陵和田野卻各不相同。這種差異也是故事的一部分。哈庫的照片留在第一份回報旁，孩子們還寫了感謝卡。這次小交流讓他們明白，地方地圖可以連結鄰居，卻不必假裝每個地方的天氣都一樣。
