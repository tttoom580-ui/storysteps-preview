人人看得懂的地圖

A Map for Everyone

霧杉谷的第一張天氣地圖 · 第 11 / 25 章 · 1000以下 · 英中對照 · 校訂中

用互動版查詞、朗讀與收藏

A white basket stood outside the village library for new weather cards. On Tuesday, Lumi came to check the cards and found a note from the hospital. The nurse wrote, “Dear map team, some visitors cannot tell your red and green colors apart. Could you add shapes?” Biko did not hate criticism, but he felt his hard work had been missed. Miri made a gentle joke about giving every cloud a funny hat, and the group laughed. Then they took the nurse's point seriously. A picture had to be useful to people who saw colors differently. They decided to test a new set of marks before sending out another page.

村圖書館外放著一個白色籃子，收新的天氣卡。星期二，露米來檢查，找到一張醫院送來的紙條。護理師寫著：「親愛的地圖小隊，有些訪客分不清你們的紅色和綠色。可以加上圖形嗎？」比可並不討厭批評，卻覺得自己的努力沒被看到。米莉開玩笑說要給每朵雲戴上滑稽帽子，大家笑了。接著，他們認真思考護理師的意見。圖畫必須讓色彩辨識不同的人也能使用。他們決定先測試新符號，再發布下一頁。

At the hospital, the nurse showed them two thin strips of paper. One had a red circle and a green circle; the other had a circle and a square with the same colors. Several visitors found the second easier. “If you often use only color,” she said, “some readers may miss a useful message.” Lumi asked whether the team could send a sample to the waiting room. The nurse said yes, if the sample clearly stated that it was a school exercise and not a medical or safety alert. That condition mattered. A visitor might trust a paper on a hospital wall more than the children meant them to. The nurse wore a belt that held a small work pouch, but she placed the test cards on the table for everyone to see.

在醫院，護理師拿出兩條細紙。一條只有紅圈和綠圈，另一條則是同樣顏色的圓形與方形。幾位訪客覺得第二條比較好懂。「如果常常只用顏色，」她說，「有些讀者就可能漏看有用訊息。」露米問能否把範例放進候診室。護理師答應，但必須清楚寫明這是學校練習，不是醫療或安全警報。這個條件很重要，因為醫院牆上的紙張可能比孩子原先預期更容易讓人信任。 護理師腰上繫著能放工作小物的腰帶，仍把測試卡放在桌上讓大家看清楚。

A young player from the school ball team joined the test. He carried a kite with a broken tail and asked whether the weather map could tell him when to fly it. Biko said, “It can show wind we have already seen, but it cannot promise the wind tomorrow.” The player nodded. He helped draw a wide arrow with two little lines to show the direction clearly. Miri added shapes for open and covered places. The library worker checked that the pictures would still be clear when copied in black and white. The team now had a map that could be understood without relying on color alone.

學校球隊的一位年輕球員也來測試。他帶著尾巴壞掉的風箏，問天氣地圖能否告訴他何時適合放風箏。比可說：「它能顯示我們已經觀察到的風，卻不能保證明天的風。」球員點頭，幫忙畫一個寬箭頭，旁邊加兩條小線，讓方向更清楚。米莉替開放區與遮蔽區加上不同圖形。圖書館員確認黑白影印後圖案仍清楚。現在的地圖不只靠顏色，也能讓人理解。

Later, the nurse asked how the children were looking after their own health. They had been standing over the board for hours, and Biko's back was tired. Lumi had to admit they needed breaks. They set a rule: after each hour of work, everyone could sit, stretch, and drink water. Those who did not want to take part could leave without giving a reason. The map team was not a contest to see who could suffer the most. Good work needed rested minds and hands. The nurse smiled at that idea and promised to check their next draft if she had time.

稍後護理師問，孩子們有沒有照顧自己的健康。他們在看板前站了好幾個小時，比可的背已經累了。露米承認需要休息。團隊訂下規則：每工作一小時，大家都能坐下、伸展、喝水；不想參與的人也可以不說理由就離開。地圖小隊不是比誰最能忍耐的比賽。好的工作需要休息過的頭腦與雙手。護理師喜歡這個想法，答應有空就幫忙看下一版。

That afternoon, Lumi wrote a short message to those who had tested the new design. She did not send a picture of anyone without asking first. A few visitors had agreed to share comments but not their faces. Biko put the comments in one file and the drawings in another. He was learning that useful information could be shared without sharing everything about a person. Miri pinned the new circles and squares on the board, then watched as an older neighbor read them correctly. The change seemed small: two shapes instead of two colors. For the neighbor, it made the difference between guessing and knowing.

那天下午，露米寫短訊感謝測試者。她沒有在未經同意時傳送任何人的照片。有幾位訪客願意提供意見，卻不想公開面孔。比可把意見和圖畫放在不同資料夾。他漸漸明白，分享有用資訊不代表要公開一個人的所有事情。米莉把新圓圈和方形貼上看板，看到一位年長鄰居順利讀懂。這改變看似很小：兩種圖形取代單靠兩種顏色。但對那位鄰居來說，這就是從猜測到真正理解的差別。

Before the next rain, the library put up a second map inside the front window. People could see it even when the library was closed. The worker added a line saying where to find town safety notices. Lumi checked those words twice. She wanted readers to understand that her map was a record of observations, not an order to travel or stay home. At the weekly meeting, the team read both maps and found one date missing on the window copy. They fixed it at once and wrote the correction in their log. The board had become more helpful, and so had the people who cared for it.

下次下雨前，圖書館在前窗內貼出第二張地圖，即使閉館，路人也看得見。館員加上一行字，說明哪裡可找到鎮公所的安全公告。露米把文字核對了兩次。她希望讀者知道，這是觀察紀錄，不是命令人出門或待在家的指示。每週會議時，團隊讀了兩張地圖，發現窗內那張漏寫日期。他們立刻修正，並在紀錄簿登記。看板更有用了，照顧它的人也更細心了。
