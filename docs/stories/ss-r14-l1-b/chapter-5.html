黃色田野

The Yellow Field

霧杉谷的第一張天氣地圖 · 第 5 / 25 章 · 1000以下 · 英中對照 · 校訂中

用互動版查詞、朗讀與收藏

A farm west of the valley had a long yellow field. Its owner, a teenager named Fenn, helped his mother feed the goats before school. He showed Lumi and Biko a slow wheel that turned above the barn. It had worked for many years, but needed a little oil. “We use it to see the wind,” he explained. “We do not sell its readings.” Behind the barn, a bank of earth rose toward the hill. A strange string was tied to a fence post. Lumi asked where it had come from. Fenn said it marked a place where trucks should not go after heavy rain.

山谷西邊有座農場，裡面是一大片黃色田地。農場主人的兒子芬恩是位青少年，每天上學前都先幫媽媽餵山羊。他帶露米和比可看穀倉上方緩慢旋轉的輪子。輪子用了很多年，現在需要一點油。「我們用它看風，」他說，「不會把讀數拿去賣。」穀倉後有道土堤往小山延伸，一條奇怪的繩子綁在圍籬柱上。露米問它從哪裡來。芬恩說，繩子用來提醒卡車在大雨後不要進入那片地。

Fenn's mother had received a letter about a movie planned for the farm school. The movie would teach children how plants need rain and sunlight. Lumi liked the topic, but she wanted to know what farmers already understood. Fenn pointed straight across the field. “When the wind comes through that gap, we move the light boxes inside,” he said. “We can see it before the wheel moves.” Biko wrote down both the wheel reading and Fenn's observation, keeping them separate. “A tool and a person may notice different things,” he said. The family nodded. Neither would replace the other. Lumi took a moment to think before she wrote down his explanation.

芬恩的母親收到一封信，說農校打算放映電影，教孩子植物為什麼需要雨水和日光。露米喜歡這個主題，卻更想知道農民已經明白哪些事。芬恩筆直指向田野另一端。「風從那個缺口吹來時，我們會把輕箱子搬進去，」他說，「有時輪子還沒轉，我們就感覺到了。」比可把輪子的讀數與芬恩的觀察分開記下。「工具和人可能看到不同的東西，」他說。農場一家都點頭，兩者不能互相取代。 露米先想了一會兒，才把他的解釋記下。

They stayed for a small supper when the moon rose. Lumi counted many drops of water on a cold cup and asked whether that meant rain would come. Fenn shook his head. “It may only mean the air here is wet.” Grandma Ena had warned them about making big claims from one sign. Lumi drew a line through the guess, then wrote a new question beside it. Fenn's youngest sister laughed because a goat stepped on his toe. He jumped, but did not shout at the animal. The children helped him close the gate and checked that the string marking the muddy field was still in place.

月亮升起時，他們留下來吃了簡單晚餐。露米看見冰涼杯子上有許多水滴，問這是否代表快下雨了。芬恩搖頭。「也可能只是這裡的空氣潮濕。」艾娜奶奶曾提醒，不要憑一個跡象就下大結論。露米畫線刪去猜測，在旁邊寫下新問題。芬恩的小妹妹笑了，因為山羊踩到了哥哥的腳趾。芬恩跳了一下，卻沒有對山羊大喊。孩子們幫忙關好門，並確認標記泥地的繩子還在。

On their way out, Fenn showed them a short path under the trees. It looked easy, but the family never used it when wet. The soil could slide, and the trees hid the deepest holes. Lumi asked if they might place a flag there. “No,” said Fenn, and he explained why. She accepted the answer. A good volunteer was someone who could say no, too. They chose a safe post beside the farm gate instead. It was a little farther from the yellow field, but anyone could read it without opening the gate. Their map now had a field station that respected both weather and farm work.

離開前，芬恩帶他們看樹下的一條短路。看起來很容易走，但家人在地面潮濕時從不使用。泥土可能滑動，樹木也遮住較深的坑。露米問能否在那裡放小旗。「不行，」芬恩說，並解釋原因。她接受這個回答。好志工也應有說不的權利。大家改選農場門旁安全的柱子。那裡離黃田稍遠，但任何人都能在不開門的情況下讀旗。地圖如今有了尊重天氣與農事的田野觀察站。

The next morning, the children compared the north meadow and the yellow field. Although the two places were near each other, the flags had not always pointed the same way. Biko wanted to find a rule at once. Lumi suggested watching for five more days. She remembered how the picnic wind had upset everything in a moment, yet a good answer would take time. Fenn agreed to record the wheel once each morning, after feeding the goats. He would not climb the barn; an adult could check the old wheel when needed. The team had gained not only a new place on the map, but a steady partner.

隔天早上，孩子們比較北邊草地與黃色田野的記錄。兩地雖然相近，小旗卻不總朝同個方向。比可想立刻找出規律。露米建議再看五天。她記得野餐時的一陣風讓一切瞬間亂了套，但要得到好答案需要時間。芬恩答應每天早上餵完山羊後記錄輪子方向，不會自行爬上穀倉；必要時由大人檢查舊輪子。團隊不只在地圖上增加一個地點，也多了一位可靠的夥伴。

At their fifth-day meeting, Grandma Ena checked the notes. Every station had a named observer, a time, and a safe position. Several boxes were still empty, and the children could explain why. Ena called that a successful beginning. Lumi expected praise for the pretty colors, but the old tortoise praised their questions instead. “You have learned where not to stand,” she said. “You have also learned when not to guess.” Lumi placed the yellow field card beside the meadow card. Two places in one valley could tell two different stories. The first page of the weather map was ready, and the children were eager to begin the next one.

第五天的會議上，艾娜奶奶檢查筆記。每個觀察點都有記錄者姓名、時間和安全位置。仍有幾個空格，但孩子們都能說明原因。艾娜稱這是成功的開始。露米原本以為奶奶會稱讚漂亮的顏色，沒想到她稱讚的是孩子們的問題。「你們學會哪裡不能站，」她說，「也學會何時不該亂猜。」露米把黃色田野卡放在草地卡旁。相同山谷裡的兩個地方，也可能說出不同故事。天氣地圖的第一頁準備好了，孩子們迫不及待想開始下一頁。
