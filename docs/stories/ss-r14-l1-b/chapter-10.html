安靜的傍晚測試

A Quiet Evening Test

霧杉谷的第一張天氣地圖 · 第 10 / 25 章 · 1000以下 · 英中對照 · 校訂中

用互動版查詞、朗讀與收藏

One evening, the weather board was hard to read because the street had become dark. Biko found a button beside an old lamp, but he did not press it. The lamp belonged to the shop, and the switch might have been broken. Lumi asked the owner to have it checked. While they waited, a student brought a menu from the café, where the evening meal was potato soup and sweet bread. A rose lay beside the menu. “Usually the market is still bright at this time,” said the student. Lumi made a note. The team would need to test the board at times when people actually used it, not just at noon.

一個傍晚，街上天色暗了，天氣板也變得難讀。比可在舊燈旁找到按鈕，卻沒有按下去。燈是商店的，開關可能故障。露米請店主找人檢查。等待時，一位學生帶來咖啡館菜單，晚餐有馬鈴薯湯與甜麵包，菜單旁放著一朵玫瑰。「平常這個時間市場還很明亮，」學生說。露米記下來。團隊必須在大家實際使用看板的時間測試，而不能只選中午。

The electrician arrived the next day and fixed the lamp. He also showed the children which part was safe to touch and which part was only for trained workers. At sunset, a small group came to watch the test. They stood to the left of the board where a clear path had been marked. Oren read the largest numbers without trouble. Miri could read the colors, but the date was too low. She moved the date card up. The electrician turned off the lamp for a moment, and the children noticed that the white paper was easier to see than the yellow paper. They changed the paper.

隔天，電工來修好燈，也告訴孩子哪些部分可以安全碰觸，哪些只能由受訓人員處理。日落時，一小群人前來測試。他們站在看板左側畫好標示的空地。奧倫能順利讀出最大的數字；米莉看得懂顏色，但日期放得太低，於是把日期卡往上移。電工暫時關燈，孩子發現白紙比黃紙容易看清。他們便換了紙張。

A sudden sound came from the hill. Several children began to worry that a storm was coming. Lumi told them to stay together and look around. A cart had turned too quickly near a gate, and an empty pan had fallen onto the road. The noise was loud, but the sky had not changed. The driver picked up the pan and apologized. “An interesting sound is not always a weather sign,” Biko said. He wrote “cart” in the notes so that no one would later mistake the noise for thunder. The children smiled at the mistake. They had learned to ask what caused a sound before using it as a clue. A few dry leaves began to fall from the tree beside the gate.

山坡突然傳來聲響。幾個孩子開始擔心暴風雨要來了。露米請大家留在一起，先看看四周。原來一輛推車在門口轉得太快，空鍋子掉到路上。聲音很大，天空卻沒有變化。車夫撿起鍋子並道歉。「有趣的聲音不一定是天氣訊號，」比可說。他在筆記寫下「推車」，避免日後有人把它誤認為雷聲。孩子們對這次誤會笑了，也學到使用聲音當線索前，得先弄清楚來源。 門旁樹上的幾片乾葉也開始落下。

The café owner asked whether the team could make a short notice about cold evenings. She worried that old people might stay outside too long while reading the board. Lumi did not want the map to become a long list of orders. She added a bench near the wall and a reminder that anyone could read a paper copy indoors at the café. The owner agreed to keep the copy dry and update it when she received a new page. A visitor who came only for supper tried the idea first. He stayed warm, read the weather notes, and said the indoor copy was a simple help.

咖啡館老闆問，團隊能否提醒大家晚間可能變冷。她擔心老人為了看板，在戶外站太久。露米不想讓地圖變成長串命令。她在牆旁安排長椅，並加上提醒：任何人都能進咖啡館看室內紙本。老闆答應保持紙本乾燥，收到新頁就更新。一位只來吃晚餐的旅客第一個試用。他在溫暖室內讀完天氣紀錄，說這是簡單而實用的幫助。

That weekend, the team made a second evening test with the lamp working and the new date card in place. They asked ten people to read one line and explain it in their own words. Eight understood it at once; two thought the wind arrow meant the opposite direction. Biko wanted to call the test a success, but Lumi pointed out that the two mistakes mattered. They added a small drawing showing the flag blowing away from the wind. On the next test, all ten readers understood. Lumi wrote both sets of results on the page. Good news was better when the first trouble had not been hidden.

週末，團隊在燈修好、日期卡移好後再次進行晚間測試。他們請十個人讀一行紀錄，並用自己的話解釋。八人立刻理解，另外兩人把風向箭頭看反了。比可想宣布測試成功，露米卻指出兩個錯誤也很重要。他們加畫一張小圖，說明旗子如何順風飄動。下一輪十人都看懂了。露米把兩次結果一起寫在紙上。沒有隱藏最初問題的好消息，才更有價值。

As they packed the board for the night, Miri smiled at the new lamp. “We may have trouble again,” she said, “but now we know how to test the answer.” Lumi looked down the road toward the east station, where the first evening flag was still moving. The town sounded different at night: softer voices, fewer wheels, and leaves against stone. There would always be things they did not understand at once. The map did not need to be perfect to be useful. It needed people willing to look, listen, ask, and change what did not work. On that point, the whole team agreed.

收拾看板準備過夜時，米莉對著新燈微笑。「以後可能還會遇到麻煩，」她說，「但現在我們知道如何驗證答案。」露米望向東站，傍晚的小旗仍輕輕飄動。鎮上的夜晚有不同聲音：更輕的談話、更少的車輪聲，以及樹葉摩擦石頭的沙沙聲。總有些事不會立刻懂。地圖不必完美才能有用；它需要願意觀察、傾聽、提問並改正錯誤的人。這一點，全隊都同意。
