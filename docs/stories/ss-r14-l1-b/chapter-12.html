小鎮開放日

The Town's Open Day

霧杉谷的第一張天氣地圖 · 第 12 / 25 章 · 1000以下 · 英中對照 · 校訂中

用互動版查詞、朗讀與收藏

On Saturday afternoon, the town held an open day at the supermarket hall. A band played soft music in one corner, and a young actor wore large glasses while reading the welcome signs. Lumi had been asked to play a short weather game with visitors. She was tired from the week, but wanted to celebrate the team's work. The last package of cards arrived just before two o'clock, carried by the shop clerk. Inside were simple tickets for the game. A ticket was free; it only helped the team count how many people tried the map. Lumi placed the cards by the door and waited for the first visitor. The clerk checked the wall clock again at three o’clock and noted when the first set of visitors finished.

星期六下午，小鎮在超市大廳舉辦開放日。樂團在角落演奏輕柔音樂，一位戴著大眼鏡的年輕演員朗讀歡迎標示。露米受邀帶訪客玩簡單的天氣遊戲。她這週已經很累，卻還是想慶祝小隊的成果。下午兩點前，店員送來最後一包卡片，裡面是遊戲使用的票券。票券免費，只用來計算多少人試讀地圖。露米把卡片放在門旁，等著第一位訪客。 三點整，店員再次查看牆上的鐘，記下第一批訪客完成活動的時間。

The game began with a question: “I see a circle on the map. What more must I read?” Visitors could choose the place, the date, or the name of the person who drew the circle. There was no single good answer, because all three mattered. Some children wanted a prize for giving the fastest reply. Lumi offered a paper stamp instead of sweets, so nobody needed to buy or eat anything to join. A reporter from local media took notes, but she asked before taking a photograph. The team was glad to explain its work without turning the event into a show about one clever child.

遊戲從一個問題開始：「我在地圖看到一個圓圈，還應該讀什麼？」訪客可以選擇地點、日期，或畫圈者的名字。沒有唯一的好答案，因為三者都很重要。有些孩子想給最快回答的人獎品。露米改用紙印章，讓大家不必購買或吃任何東西也能參加。地方媒體記者做了筆記，但拍照前先徵求同意。團隊很高興能解釋工作，也不想讓活動變成只讚美某個聰明孩子的表演。

Near the hall, a poor family had set up a table to share free used clothes and books. Lumi noticed that visitors were walking past without seeing it. She asked the hall manager whether the weather board could be moved to leave a wider path toward the table. The manager agreed after checking the space. Biko helped carry the light frame; the adults moved the heavier part. The change brought more people to both tables. Lumi liked that people could come to learn about weather and leave with something useful for daily life. The open day was not only about their own project. A wilted flower sat on the table; Lumi asked that it be put in water before it could die.

大廳附近，一個經濟拮据的家庭擺了桌子，免費分享二手衣物和書籍。露米注意到許多訪客沒看到就走過。她問管理員能否移動天氣板，讓通往那張桌子的路更寬。管理員檢查空間後答應。比可幫忙搬輕框架，較重部分由大人移動。調整後，兩個攤位都迎來更多人。露米很喜歡大家能來學天氣，也能帶回對生活有用的東西。開放日不只關乎她們的計畫。 桌上有朵花已經垂下來；露米請人趕快把花插進水裡，免得枯死。

Later, the actor invited Lumi to speak into the room microphone. She held the key card in her hand and said, “I am part of a team. My friends did more than I can tell today.” Then she gave the microphone to Miri, Biko, and Oren in turn. Each told one lesson. Miri spoke about shapes, Biko about dates, and Oren about safe places. The band stopped playing for a minute so everyone could listen. Lumi did not feel she had lost her moment. Giving other people room made the message stronger, and the open day more honest.

稍後，演員邀請露米用大廳麥克風說話。她拿著提示卡說：「我是團隊的一員。朋友做的事，比我今天能說的還多。」接著她把麥克風依序交給米莉、比可和奧倫。每人分享一件學到的事：米莉談圖形、比可談日期、奧倫談安全位置。樂團暫停一分鐘，好讓大家聽清楚。露米沒有覺得自己的時刻被搶走。讓別人有發言空間，反而讓訊息更有力，也讓開放日更真實。

At the end of the afternoon, the team counted the tickets. More people had played than they expected, but the result was not just a number. Several visitors had written questions on the back. One asked how to report from a second-floor window. Another asked whether old cards would be kept. Lumi read every question before going home. The supermarket manager offered the hall for one more meeting next month, if the town needed it. The children thanked her, then took down their posters. They left the room clean, with the band packing its instruments in the corner and the last visitors waving goodbye.

下午結束時，團隊清點票券。參加者比預期多，但收穫不只是一個數字。好幾位訪客在票背寫了問題：有人問如何從二樓窗邊回報，也有人問舊卡會不會保留。露米回家前一一讀完。超市管理員說，如果小鎮需要，下個月還可借大廳再開一次會。孩子們向她道謝，接著拆下海報。他們把場地整理乾淨，樂團在角落收樂器，最後的訪客也揮手道別。

When Lumi reached home, she looked at her own face in a window and laughed at how tired she was. Her mother asked whether she still wanted to lead the next meeting. “I want to help,” Lumi said, “but I do not need to lead every time.” She asked Biko to plan the next gathering, and he agreed after checking his school work. Lumi wrote that choice in the team book. Learning to share a microphone was easy for one afternoon. Learning to share a job might take longer. She was ready to try, because the map was no longer only her dream.

露米回家時，看見窗玻璃映著自己的臉，笑自己累成這樣。媽媽問下次會議是否還想由她主持。「我還想幫忙，」露米說，「但不必每次都是我主持。」她請比可規劃下次聚會，比可先確認課業，再答應。露米把這項安排寫進團隊簿。分享麥克風，一個下午就能做到；分享長期工作，可能要學更久。她願意試試，因為這張地圖早已不只是她一個人的夢想。
