許多幫忙的手

Many Helping Hands

霧杉谷的第一張天氣地圖 · 第 7 / 25 章 · 1000以下 · 英中對照 · 校訂中

用互動版查詞、朗讀與收藏

A rabbit named Miri came from the west with a large basket of paper flowers. She had helped her mother, a housewife who ran a small craft class, collect clean paper by asking neighbors. The flowers were popular at village parties, and Miri hoped to use them to mark the weather board. Lumi liked the idea but showed her the main list. “The flower colors must mean something real,” she said. Miri suggested one flower for every hour of observation. Biko answered that the page would soon be too full. They chose one flower for each safe station instead, with a clear note beside it. During the course, Miri gave each helper a small piece of chocolate after they had washed their hands. No one had to eat it to join.

名叫米莉的兔子從西邊來，帶著一大籃紙花。她和主持手工課的家庭主婦媽媽一起向鄰居募集乾淨舊紙。紙花很受村裡派對歡迎，米莉想拿它們標記天氣板。露米喜歡這個點子，卻先把主要清單給她看。「花的顏色必須有真實意思，」她說。米莉提議每觀察一小時放一朵，比可說那樣頁面很快就會太滿。大家改成每個安全站點放一朵，旁邊再加清楚說明。 課程中，米莉在大家洗完手後，各給一小塊巧克力。不吃巧克力也完全可以參加。

The main problem was not making the board pretty. Volunteers had begun to bring notes at different times, and one report arrived without a place name. “Let us start with a simple lesson,” Miri said. She wrote “where, when, who” on three big cards. The children practiced by pretending to report from different rooms. There were mistakes, but no one was hurt by them. At the end of the class, the group knew how to write a complete note. Biko said he would let them try the real board only after they had practiced twice. The new helpers agreed and stayed until the little lesson was finished.

主要問題不在於把板子做漂亮。志工開始在不同時間帶來筆記，有一張甚至沒寫地點。米莉說：「先上一堂簡單的課吧。」她把「在哪裡、什麼時候、由誰」寫在三張大卡上。孩子們假裝從不同房間回報，反覆練習。他們會出錯，但沒有人因此受傷。課程結束時，大家都知道怎樣寫完整紀錄。比可說要先練習兩次，才能碰真正的看板。新幫手們都同意，一直待到小課結束。

A house near the western hill had a covered porch where adults could watch the sky. Its owner wanted to help but could not leave the house during the day. Lumi brought him a paper card and explained that he could record what he saw from home. He wrote his first note at five and placed it in a box by his door. Miri collected it with an adult before dark. The children promised that the collection would stop whenever the weather became unsafe. They wanted real observations, not brave stories about walking through storms. The old neighbor said the rule made him feel respected.

西邊山坡有間房子，帶屋簷的門廊適合大人觀察天空。屋主願意幫忙，但白天不能離家。露米送來紀錄卡，告訴他可以從家中回報。屋主下午五點寫下第一張卡，放進門旁的盒子。米莉在天黑前由大人陪同收取。孩子們承諾，只要天氣變得不安全，收件工作就會停止。他們想要真實觀察，而不是誰冒著暴風雨走路的英勇故事。老鄰居說，這條規則讓他感到受尊重。

At the next meeting, the team compared the west porch with the north meadow. The porch was protected by a roof, so its flag moved less than the flag in the open field. Lumi asked whether that made the porch a bad station. Grandma Ena said it depended on the question. “It tells us what people feel near that house,” she said. “It does not tell us the wind in the whole valley.” They decided to mark covered and open places differently. That small choice would help readers understand why the same afternoon could feel calm at one door and windy in a field.

下次會議上，團隊比較西邊門廊與北邊草地。門廊有屋頂遮蔽，所以小旗晃動得比空曠草地少。露米問這是否代表門廊不是好站點。艾娜奶奶說要看問題是什麼。「它告訴我們屋子旁的人感受到的風，」她說，「不是整個山谷的風。」大家決定用不同記號區分遮蔽與開放的地方。這個小選擇能幫讀者了解，為什麼同一個下午，某家門口平靜，田裡卻很有風。

Miri wanted everyone to see the board, so she took the flowers to the market and asked people to read a sample. An old bear could not see the tiny writing. A young bird thought a blue flower meant rain, although the legend said it meant a covered station. Miri came back with both comments and no hurt feelings. She made larger letters and moved the legend above the board. Lumi praised her willingness to listen. “We are not making a board only for ourselves,” she said. “If people read it in a different way, we need to know.” The flowers remained, but their job became clearer.

米莉希望人人看得懂看板，便帶紙花去市場，請路人試讀範例。一隻老熊看不清小字；一隻年輕小鳥以為藍花代表下雨，雖然圖例寫的是「有遮蔽的站點」。米莉把兩個意見都帶回來，沒有生氣。她把字放大，將圖例移到看板上方。露米稱讚她願意傾聽。「這不是只做給我們自己看的，」她說，「別人的理解不同，我們就需要知道。」紙花保留了，但用途變得更清楚。

By the end of the seventh day, the team had a working list with names, places, and safe times. Miri drew a flower beside every station that had reported for three days. Not all flowers were filled in, and that was fine. She did not want a full garden of paper flowers to hide empty records. Lumi placed the new list beside the first week's map. More people had joined, but the questions were harder now. They needed to know not just who wanted to help, but which jobs each person could do safely and well. That was the next part of building trust.

到了第七天，團隊已有一張實用清單，記著姓名、地點與安全時間。連續三天有回報的站點，米莉就在旁邊畫一朵花。不是每朵花都有填色，這沒有關係。她不想讓滿滿紙花遮住缺失的紀錄。露米把新清單放在第一週地圖旁。幫手變多了，問題卻更難了：除了知道誰想幫忙，還得知道每個人能安全、穩定地做什麼工作。這是建立信任的下一步。
