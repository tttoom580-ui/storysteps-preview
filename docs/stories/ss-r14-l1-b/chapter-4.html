東邊的小路

The East Path

霧杉谷的第一張天氣地圖 · 第 4 / 25 章 · 1000以下 · 英中對照 · 校訂中

用互動版查詞、朗讀與收藏

At the east edge of the valley, two paths met beside a public bath house. One path was wide and green with grass; the other was a medium-size stone lane. A fallen branch blocked part of the lane. Mrs. Tern, a cheerful old bird, held a bowl of warm water for her cute puppy and asked the children to look before they crossed the street. “You must not pass this corner without an adult,” she said. Both Lumi and Biko nodded. They had come to choose an observation point, not to move the branch themselves. A knock on the bath house door brought the caretaker out to help.

山谷東邊兩條小路在公共浴室旁交會。一條寬闊，長著綠草；另一條是中等寬度的石巷。一根倒下的樹枝擋住部分巷道。活潑的老鳥特恩太太拿著一碗溫水給可愛的小狗，請孩子們過街前先仔細看。「沒有大人陪同，你們不能從這個轉角通過，」她說。露米和比可都點頭。他們是來選觀察點，不是自行搬開樹枝。有人敲了浴室門，管理員便出來幫忙。

The caretaker showed them a quiet place behind a low wall. A taxicab could stop nearby, but its wheels would not touch the little patch of grass. “A big surprise can come from a small mistake,” he warned, pointing to water on the steps. Lumi wrote “slippery after rain” beside the place on her map. Then the caretaker called the town workers about the fallen branch. He did not let the children cross under it. While they waited, Biko measured how far the flags could be seen from the safe side. Their choice was simple: good weather notes were not worth taking if the reader had to stand in danger.

管理員帶他們看矮牆後一處安靜的位置。計程車可以在附近停靠，但輪胎不會壓到那片草地。「小錯誤也可能帶來大意外，」他指著階梯上的水說。露米在地圖那個位置旁寫下「雨後易滑」。隨後管理員聯絡鎮上工人處理倒下的樹枝，沒有讓孩子從樹枝下穿過。等待時，比可量了從安全側能看清旗子的距離。他們的選擇很簡單：如果讀數時必須站在危險處，再好的天氣資料也不值得。

That afternoon, the workers removed the branch. Oren asked whether the path was safe now. Mrs. Tern answered, “It is safer, but please keep looking.” She explained that a road changes with light, rain, and use. The children recorded that the work had been done, yet kept a note about the wet steps. Lumi liked how the adults treated safety as a continuing job rather than one happy moment. She made a small drawing of the two paths, using different marks for walking and watching. The map would never tell anyone to go somewhere just because a straight line made the journey seem short.

那天下午，工人移開了樹枝。奧倫問現在小路是否安全。特恩太太回答：「更安全了，但仍要繼續觀察。」她解釋道路會因日照、雨水和使用而改變。孩子們記下清除工作已完成，卻保留濕階梯的提醒。露米喜歡大人把安全當成持續的工作，而不是一次性的好消息。她畫出兩條路，分別標示通行與觀察用途。地圖絕不會只因直線看起來比較短，就叫任何人走那條路。

On the way home, Biko found an old sign showing a shortcut through a private garden. “Perhaps we can use it,” he said. Lumi shook her head. They had no permission, and the sign was too old to trust. They spoke to the garden owner instead. The owner did not want people walking across her vegetables, but she was happy to report the weather from her window. Lumi thanked her for that choice. A useful map did not have to open every gate. Sometimes it needed to respect a closed one. They returned with a new volunteer and no new path, which Lumi considered a good result.

回家路上，比可發現一塊舊牌，指著穿過私人菜園的捷徑。「也許可以用，」他說。露米搖頭。他們沒有得到許可，舊牌也不一定可靠。於是兩人向菜園主人詢問。主人不希望別人踩過蔬菜，卻願意從窗邊回報天氣。露米感謝她的選擇。好地圖不一定要打開每一道門，有時還要尊重緊閉的門。他們帶回一位新志工，卻沒增加新路線；露米認為這是很好的結果。

Back at the meeting room, the children put green circles on places where adults had checked the ground, and orange circles where a question remained. They did not call a place dangerous simply because it had an orange mark. The mark meant “ask first.” This made their map more useful for families with young children, slow walkers, and people carrying heavy bags. The caretaker returned later with a note about the lane. His note was longer than theirs, so they read it together and chose the most important facts. A map could be simple without losing the truth, as long as its makers knew what they had left out.

回到會議室，孩子們在大人檢查過的地面位置貼綠圈，在仍有疑問的位置貼橘圈。他們沒有把橘圈直接說成危險，橘圈的意思是「先詢問」。如此一來，地圖對帶幼兒的家庭、走得慢的人及提重物的人更有用。管理員稍晚帶來石巷的說明，內容比孩子的筆記長很多。大家一起讀完，選出最重要的事實。地圖可以簡單，但不能失真；製作者必須知道自己省略了什麼。

Before bedtime, Lumi went through her notes once more. She realized that choosing a weather station was also choosing a place for people to return to again and again. That place had to work on ordinary days, not only during a single visit. The east station would use a flag that adults could maintain from the public side of the wall. Mrs. Tern promised to call if its line broke. Biko drew a small bowl beside the flag in his notebook, remembering the puppy's water. Lumi laughed, but she did not erase it. Little pictures could help the team remember real people behind every safe place.

睡前，露米再次翻看筆記。她發現選天氣觀察點，也是在選一個大家會反覆造訪的地方。那個地方不只一次探訪時要安全，平常日子也得合適。東站將使用一面旗子，由大人站在牆的公共側維護。特恩太太答應，若旗繩斷了就打電話。比可在筆記的旗子旁畫了一個小碗，想起小狗喝的水。露米笑了，但沒有擦掉。小圖畫能提醒團隊，每個安全地點背後都有真實的人。
