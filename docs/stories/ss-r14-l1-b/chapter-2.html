池塘旁的討論

A Meeting by the Pond

霧杉谷的第一張天氣地圖 · 第 2 / 25 章 · 1000以下 · 英中對照 · 校訂中

用互動版查詞、朗讀與收藏

The second day began with a meeting in the room above the village shop. Anybody could come, so even the residents of the small apartment building beside the pond were invited. The church bell rang just before lunch, and several neighbors came in late. Lumi put her drawing beside a sofa and asked everyone to look at it. A reporter from the valley newspaper held a pen above her page. “Is the map ready?” she asked. “Not yet,” Lumi said. “We have a clear question, but we need more observations.” The reporter nodded. She liked that answer more than a quick promise.

第二天，大家在村裡商店樓上的房間開會。任何人都可以參加，池塘旁小公寓樓的住戶也收到邀請。午餐前教堂的鐘剛響，幾位鄰居才匆匆趕到。露米把圖放在沙發旁，請大家看。山谷報紙的記者拿著筆問：「地圖做好了嗎？」「還沒，」露米回答，「我們有清楚的問題，但需要更多觀察。」記者點點頭。她更喜歡這樣的回答，而不是倉促的保證。

A visitor who had traveled across the country raised a hand. “I once saw a weather board with three colors,” she said. “Could you make one here?” Biko guessed that three colors might show wind, rain, and sun, but he did not want to forget the time of each reading. A serious goose who managed the pond asked whether the children would climb its bank. “No,” Lumi answered. “We can observe from the third stone on the public path.” She picked up a small piece of chalk and marked that spot on a drawing. Everyone could see what she meant without going near the water.

一位走過全國許多地方的旅客舉手。「我曾看過用三種顏色做的天氣板，」她說，「你們也能做嗎？」比可猜想三種顏色可以代表風、雨和晴天，但他不想忘記每次記錄的時間。管理池塘的嚴肅鵝先生問，孩子們會不會爬上池岸。「不會，」露米說，「我們可以站在公共步道的第三塊石頭旁觀察。」她拿一小塊粉筆，在畫上標記那個位置。大家不用靠近水邊也看得懂。

Some neighbors wondered who would pay for paper and colored pins. Lumi had not thought about payment. Grandma Ena suggested using the back of clean old pages first. The shop owner offered a board, and the reporter promised to lend a telephone when needed. “We do not need a new machine to begin,” Ena said. The children wrote down every offer and every limit. They also wrote that no one should have to pay to read the final map. A map about shared paths and weather should be easy for all neighbors to see, including people who never bought anything in the shop.

有些鄰居想知道紙張和彩色圖釘要由誰付錢。露米原本沒想過費用。艾娜奶奶建議先使用乾淨舊紙的背面。店主願意提供木板，記者則答應必要時借電話。艾娜說：「我們不需要新機器才能開始。」孩子們記下每份幫助與限制，也寫下最後的地圖應免費供人閱讀。關於公共道路和天氣的資訊，應讓所有鄰居都能看見，包括從不在店裡買東西的人。

When the meeting ended, a little duck asked, “Can I guess tomorrow's weather on the board?” Lumi thought about it. “You can draw a cloud in our question book,” she said, “but the main board must show what happened, not what we wish.” The duck looked unhappy for a moment, so Biko showed her how to compare two drawings taken at different times. She found it more interesting than her first idea. The reporter watched the exchange carefully. In her notebook she wrote that the children were learning how to say no kindly, and how to offer something useful in place of a promise they could not keep.

會議結束時，一隻小鴨問：「我可以在板上猜明天的天氣嗎？」露米想了想。「你可以在問題本畫雲，」她說，「但主要看板必須記錄發生過的事，不能只畫我們希望的事。」小鴨一時有些失望，比可便教她比較不同時間的兩張畫。她發現這比原本的點子更有趣。記者細心看著他們，在筆記本記下：孩子們正在學習如何友善地拒絕，也學會用有用的替代方法，取代做不到的承諾。

After lunch, Lumi and Biko walked to the pond with Ena. They stayed on the public path and counted stones until they reached the third one. The apartment windows reflected the sky, but Lumi saw that the water looked darker under a tree. “Which is a good weather sign?” she asked. Ena answered, “Neither, until we decide what we are measuring.” They chose a small flag on a dry post to show wind direction. The adults would fix the post, and the children would only read it from the path. A clean place to stand was now part of the plan, not an afterthought.

午餐後，露米和比可跟艾娜走到池塘邊。他們一直走在公共步道上，數到第三塊石頭才停下。公寓窗戶映著天空，露米卻注意到樹下的池水顏色較深。「哪一個是好的天氣訊號？」她問。艾娜說：「先決定要測量什麼，才能回答。」他們選擇在乾燥的木柱上裝一面小旗，顯示風向。大人負責固定木柱，孩子只在步道上閱讀旗子。安全的站立位置從現在起就是計畫的一部分。

That night, Lumi looked at the list of volunteers. Some could visit in the morning, others only after work. A few could not leave their homes at all. She drew boxes for telephone reports so that those neighbors could share what they saw from a window. The third line of her list was for uncertain facts; it was longer than the others. At first she wanted to erase it. Then she remembered the reporter's face when she had admitted that the map was not ready. A little truth had brought more helpers than a big claim. She left the line where it was.

當晚，露米查看志工名單。有些人能早上來，有些只能下班後來，也有幾位根本無法離家。她畫出電話回報欄位，讓那些鄰居從窗邊看到的景象也能參與。名單第三行專門記錄不確定的資訊，而且比其他欄都長。她原本想擦掉，接著想起自己承認地圖尚未完成時，記者的表情。一點真話帶來的幫手，比誇大的宣稱更多。她保留了那一行。
