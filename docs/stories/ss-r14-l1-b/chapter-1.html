野餐時吹來的風

The Wind at the Picnic

霧杉谷的第一張天氣地圖 · 第 1 / 25 章 · 1000以下 · 英中對照 · 校訂中

The north meadow of Moss Vale was full of picnic blankets. Lumi, a young hedgehog, carried red cups and a bottle of berry juice for her family. Her little brother brought his pet beetle in a safe box with air holes. A friendly woman at the next table used a camera to take a picture of a bright paper star above the food. Lumi noticed a tiny bug on the picnic cloth and moved it onto a leaf. Then a hard wind arrived. The star flew away, the cups fell, and everyone ran toward the trees. In the middle of the field, Lumi stood still long enough to see which way the grass bent.

霧杉谷北邊的草地鋪滿野餐墊。年輕刺蝟露米替家人拿著紅杯子和一瓶莓果汁。弟弟把養的小甲蟲放進有透氣孔的安全盒子裡帶來。隔壁桌一位友善的女士用相機拍攝食物上方明亮的紙星星。露米注意到野餐布上有隻小蟲，就把牠移到葉子上。接著一陣強風吹來。紙星星飛走，杯子翻倒，大家都往樹旁跑。露米在草地中央多站了一下，記住草往哪個方向彎。

Later, at home, her father said, “It was just a strange wind. We cannot know the weather.” Lumi did not want to lie and say she knew everything. “That is not quite a fact,” she answered. “We can look at clouds and write down what we see. Maybe that can help us in the future.” Her friend Biko, a mole who loved measuring things, reached for a pencil. “We could make a map,” he said. Lumi drew one red star where the picnic had taken place. They agreed that a map would not tell tomorrow's weather like magic. It would only show what people had really seen today.

回家後，爸爸說：「那只是一陣奇怪的風。我們不可能知道天氣。」露米不想說謊，假裝自己什麼都懂。「這不完全是事實，」她回答，「我們可以觀察雲，再把看到的寫下來。也許將來能幫上忙。」喜歡測量的鼴鼠朋友比可伸手拿筆。「我們可以做張地圖，」他說。露米在野餐地點畫了一顆紅星。他們同意，地圖不能像魔法一樣說出明天的天氣；它只能呈現今天人們真正看見的現象。

The other children wanted to draw colorful pictures at once, but Lumi asked them to wait. They had only one observation. A good map would need more than that. She put the fallen cup on her desk and watched a drop of water slide across it. “Even a small thing can show movement,” she said. Biko opened a notebook and drew a simple arrow beside the star. He asked whether the arrow should point toward the north or toward the place where the wind came from. Neither child knew. Instead of choosing by guess, they wrote a question on the first page. Their project began with a question, not an answer.

其他孩子想立刻畫出五顏六色的圖，露米卻請大家先等一等。他們只有一次觀察，好地圖不能只靠這點資訊。她把倒過的杯子放在桌上，看著一滴水從杯壁滑下。「小小的東西也能顯示移動，」她說。比可翻開筆記本，在星星旁畫了個簡單箭頭。他問箭頭應該指向北方，還是指向風吹來的地方。兩人都不知道，於是沒有亂猜，只把問題寫在第一頁。計畫從一個問題開始，而不是從一個答案開始。

That evening, Lumi visited Grandma Ena, an old tortoise who kept records of the valley. Ena listened without laughing. “When I was young, we watched the wind before taking hay up the hill,” she said. “But an old memory is not a measurement.” She gave the children three blank pages and told them to write the time and place beside every mark. “If you do not know, write that you do not know. It is more useful than a beautiful mistake.” Lumi felt both excited and a little afraid. She wanted to help the valley, yet she did not want a pretty map to make anyone careless.

傍晚，露米拜訪替山谷保存紀錄的老烏龜艾娜奶奶。艾娜沒有嘲笑她，而是仔細聽完。「我年輕時，搬乾草上山前會先看風向，」她說，「但舊記憶不等於測量。」她給孩子三張空白紙，囑咐每個記號旁都寫下時間與地點。「不知道就寫不知道，這比漂亮的錯誤更有用。」露米既興奮又有些害怕。她想幫助山谷，卻不希望一張好看的圖讓任何人掉以輕心。

The next morning, Biko brought a wooden board that he could use to hold the paper flat. Lumi checked the meadow from the safe path. No one went into the tall grass, because the wet ground might hide holes. They found the paper star against a fence and returned it to the woman with the camera. She offered to help photograph the same place each day. Lumi smiled. One picture could show a moment; many pictures might show a change. Together they decided to ask adults for help before setting up any instruments. The plan was growing, but the rules were clear: stay safe, write the truth, and never call a guess a warning.

隔天早上，比可帶來一塊能壓平紙張的木板。露米從安全的小徑查看草地。沒人走進高草叢，因為濕地可能藏著洞。他們在圍籬旁找到紙星星，還給拿相機的女士。她願意每天幫忙拍攝同一處景色。露米笑了。一張照片能留下片刻，許多照片或許能顯示改變。他們決定架設任何工具前，先徵求大人幫忙。計畫正慢慢成形，規則也很清楚：注意安全、如實記錄，絕不把猜測當成警報。

At supper, Lumi told her family about the three rules. Her little brother asked whether they could use the map to stop the wind. “No,” Lumi said. “We cannot stop it. We can learn when to put things away and where to stand.” Her father looked at the wet picnic blanket hanging by the door. He could see the use of that. He offered an old box for pencils and paper. Before going to bed, Lumi drew a small empty square beside the red star. This square would be for tomorrow's observation. For the first time, she felt that an empty space on a map could be honest and hopeful at the same time.

晚餐時，露米把三條規則說給家人聽。弟弟問他們能不能用地圖把風停下來。「不能，」露米說，「我們不能阻止風，但可以學會什麼時候收好東西、該站在哪裡。」爸爸看了看門邊晾著的濕野餐墊，明白這張圖的用處。他送給孩子一個舊盒子，裝鉛筆和紙。睡前，露米在紅星旁畫了一個空白小方格，留給明天的觀察。她第一次覺得，地圖上的空白也可以同時代表誠實與希望。
