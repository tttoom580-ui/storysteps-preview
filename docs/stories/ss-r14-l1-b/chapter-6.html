泥濘的轉角

The Muddy Corner

霧杉谷的第一張天氣地圖 · 第 6 / 25 章 · 1000以下 · 英中對照 · 校訂中

After a night of rain, the path behind the village shop was full of mud. Lumi held a small envelope of report cards under her arm while Biko carried their desk board. The ground beside the stream looked deep and soft. “We do not go down there,” Lumi said. A fish moved in the clear water, and Biko made a funny face when it splashed him. However, the joke did not change the danger. They could find the stream's water level from the high stone seat above it. “May we watch from there?” Biko asked the shop owner. She checked the path first and welcomed them to the safe area.

一夜下雨後，村裡商店後方的小路滿是泥巴。露米把裝著回報卡的信封夾在手臂下，比可則帶著木製寫字板。溪邊的地面看起來又深又軟。「我們不能下去，」露米說。一條魚在清澈的水中游動，濺起的水讓比可做了個滑稽表情。然而玩笑不能改變危險。他們可以從上方的高石座觀察溪水高度。「我們能站那裡嗎？」比可問店主。店主先檢查道路，才歡迎他們到安全區域。

The shop owner said that she had watched the stream rise each spring since she was young. Her family kept a small book about floods, but the old pages did not name exact heights. Lumi asked if she might read the history. The owner showed the pages at the dry desk inside the shop. Biko drew the shape of the stone seat and marked where the water had reached today. He did not pretend his drawing was a perfect measurement. “We need an adult to fix a proper scale,” he said. The owner smiled. “That is convenient. My sister works with wooden signs and may help.”

店主說，從小到大，每到春天她都會看著溪水上升。家裡留有一本洪水紀錄簿，但舊頁面沒有記下精確高度。露米問能不能閱讀這段歷史。店主把簿子拿到店內乾燥的書桌上。比可畫出石座的形狀，標記今天的水到達哪裡。他沒有假裝自己的畫是精確測量。「我們需要大人幫忙裝正式刻度，」他說。店主笑了。「剛好很方便，我妹妹會做木牌，也許能幫忙。」

By afternoon, news of the muddy path had traveled across the valley. Some neighbors wanted a red danger sign at once. Others said the stream was always like this and there was no reason to worry. Lumi spoke at a small meeting. “Today we saw mud on the lower path and water near the high stone. That is what we know. We have not measured a flood.” Grandma Ena helped everyone choose careful words for the notice. The message would ask people to use the upper path while town workers checked the lower one. It would not announce a disaster that had not happened. She asked the town worker to speak slowly when he explained the condition of the lower path.

到了下午，泥濘小路的消息傳遍山谷。有些鄰居想立刻掛紅色危險牌，另一些人則說溪水向來如此，不必擔心。露米在小會議上發言。「今天我們看見低處小路有泥，溪水也靠近高石座。這是已知的事，但我們還沒測到洪水。」艾娜奶奶幫大家替告示選擇謹慎的文字。訊息會請大家暫走上方小路，等鎮上工人檢查低處道路，而不會宣告尚未發生的災難。 她請鎮上工人說慢一點，才能清楚解釋下方道路的狀況。

The following day, the carpenter came with a clean wooden ruler. She attached it where the water could be read from the upper path. Adults tested the fixing and put a small cover above the numbers. Biko was surprised that the ruler could be simple and still useful. Lumi drew the new station on her map and left room for uncertain readings. When the rain returned, she stayed inside the shop and watched from the window. The water rose a little, then stopped. She wrote both parts down. A change that stops is still important; the line on the page should not keep going up after the water has stopped.

隔天，木匠帶來一把乾淨的木尺，把它裝在站於上方小路就能讀數的地方。大人測試固定是否穩妥，並在數字上方加小遮蓋。比可很驚訝，原來工具很簡單也可以很有用。露米在地圖畫上新站點，留下不確定讀數的空間。雨再次落下時，她待在店裡從窗邊看。水位上升一點，接著停住了。她把前後兩個現象都記下。停止變化同樣重要；水已停升，紙上的線就不能繼續往上畫。

Lumi invited Oren to check whether someone who could not climb the shop steps would be able to read the ruler. He looked from the public path and said the numbers were too small. The carpenter returned and made a large copy of the reading on a board near the front door. This cost time but not much money. It meant older neighbors and younger children could learn from the same observation. Biko suggested that the shop owner read the number aloud each morning. She agreed, as long as she was in the shop. The weather map was beginning to be a service, not only a school project.

露米請奧倫檢查，不能爬商店階梯的人是否看得清木尺。奧倫從公共小路看，說數字太小。木匠又回來，在前門旁的板上放大抄寫讀數。這需要時間，卻花不了太多錢。如此一來，年長鄰居和小孩都能從同樣的觀察學習。比可建議店主每天早晨朗讀數字。她答應了，只要當時人在店裡就行。天氣地圖正逐漸成為一種公共服務，而不只是學校作業。

That weekend, the town workers reopened the lower path after cleaning it and checking the bank. Lumi kept the old mud note in a file, with the date when the path was closed and the date it was opened. The worker thanked her for not throwing it away. “A safe path today does not erase what happened yesterday,” he said. She put a new card beside the stream drawing and asked the children to read both. Biko pointed out that the river had been higher after the second rain, although that rain felt shorter. The team did not know why yet. They added another question rather than making up an answer.

週末，鎮上工人清理並檢查溪岸後，重新開放下方小路。露米把舊泥巴紀錄留在檔案裡，註明封閉與開放的日期。工人感謝她沒有丟掉。「今天安全了，不代表昨天的事從未發生，」他說。她在溪流圖旁貼上新卡，請孩子兩張一起讀。比可發現第二場雨感覺比較短，溪水卻更高。他們還不知道為什麼，於是新增一個問題，而不是編出答案。
