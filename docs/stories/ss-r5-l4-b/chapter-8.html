婚禮訂單

The Wedding Order

1996，晨光照相館的第二張底片 · 第 8 / 25 章 · 3000–4000 · 英中對照 · 校訂中

The most unusual order that month came from a couple arranging a small wedding by the harbor. The groom, Jianhao, worked as a civilian mechanic, while the bride taught music at the school. They wanted photographs without a formal stage, but their relatives had entirely different ideas. An aunt insisted that a wedding album should dominate the family living room. A younger cousin wanted abstract images like those in fashion magazines. Meicen estimated the price of each option and placed the proposals on a bulletin board. "These are choices," she said, "not a test of who loves the family more."

那個月最特別的委託，是一對準備在港邊舉行小婚禮的新人。新郎建豪是民間機械技師，新娘則在學校教音樂。他們希望照片自然，不要搭正式舞台，但親友意見完全不同：姑媽堅持婚紗照應該是家裡客廳最顯眼的東西，年輕表妹卻想要時尚雜誌式的抽象影像。美岑逐項估價，把方案貼上公告板。「這些是選擇，」她說，「不是考驗誰比較愛這個家。」

The bride's brother was a composer who planned to perform an original piano piece. He gave Meicen a musical cue for the moment when the couple would enter. The music's intensity would rise slowly, and he wanted the photographer to avoid blocking the guests' view. Meicen wrote a systematic sequence of camera positions. She asked whether the family would acquire the negatives or merely receive prints. Jianhao said they wanted the originals, because they might move away after the wedding. That request became part of the contract, not a promise spoken between hurried preparations.

新娘的哥哥是作曲者，打算演奏原創鋼琴曲。他告訴美岑新人入場的音樂提示，旋律強度會逐漸提高，也希望攝影師別擋住賓客。美岑列出有系統的機位順序，再問新人是要取得底片正本，還是只收照片。建豪說他們希望擁有正本，因為婚後可能搬家。這項要求被寫進契約，而非在忙亂準備中留下口頭承諾。

The reception room stood near a fertile patch of family vegetable land, and a narrow gulf separated the village from the fishing docks. Meicen imagined that the landscape could connect the two families without making them look identical. Yet she refused to assign jobs by gender: the groom could arrange flowers if he wished, and the bride could carry chairs. A learned colleague from her museum years had once warned her that a photograph should not invade a person's choices. She discussed the plan directly with the couple rather than letting louder relatives speak for them.

婚宴場地在一片肥沃的家族菜地旁，村落與漁碼頭之間隔著一條狹灣。美岑想讓風景連起兩家的故事，卻不必把所有人拍得一樣。她也拒絕按性別分配工作：新郎想插花可以，新娘想搬椅子也行。從前一位博學的博物館同事曾提醒她，照片不應侵入他人的選擇。她直接跟新人商量，不讓聲音較大的親戚替他們作主。

A family quarrel erupted when Jianhao's uncle accused the bride's aunt of jealousy over the seating arrangement. Meicen did not attempt to become a judge. Instead she asked the couple to identify the moments that mattered to them. The bride said she wanted a photograph with her grandmother before the ceremony. The groom wanted the fishing boat his father had repaired to appear in one picture. Those two requests would constitute the center of the album. Everyone else could propose additions, but no one could replace the couple's priorities without their agreement.

建豪的叔叔指責新娘姑媽因座位安排而嫉妒，親戚突然吵了起來。美岑沒有當裁判，而是請新人說出最在意的時刻。新娘希望儀式前與祖母合影，新郎則希望父親修過的漁船能入鏡。這兩項需求構成相簿的核心；其他人可以提議增加照片，卻不能不經新人同意就替換重點。

At the studio, Mingxuan tried a basic editing program on the computer. The software could remove a dust spot, but it also made faces look strangely smooth when he pushed the controls too far. Meicen asked him to keep the original files alongside every altered copy. Their enthusiasm for the tool grew more cautious when they saw how easily changes could become invisible. Shulin listened to the discussion from his chair. "A camera already changes the world by choosing a frame," he said. "The computer simply makes the responsibility easier to forget."

回店後，明軒在電腦上試用簡單修圖軟體。程式能去掉灰塵斑點，但滑桿拉得太多，臉就變得不自然地光滑。美岑要求每個修改版本都要與原始檔案並存。看見修改多麼容易被掩蓋，大家對新工具的熱情也多了警覺。樹林坐在椅子上聽著，說：「相機選定畫框時，就已經改變了世界；電腦只是讓人更容易忘記那份責任。」

On the wedding day the light was gentle and the schedule mostly held. The bride cried when she embraced her grandmother; Jianhao laughed when his father pointed toward the repaired boat. Meicen missed one formal group picture because a cousin moved the chairs early, but she told the family before the guests left and arranged another. She did not hide the mistake behind elegant prints. Two days later the couple approved the album sequence and paid the balance. The order proved that the studio could plan a demanding event without surrendering its standards to the loudest guest.

婚禮當日光線柔和，行程大致順利。新娘擁抱祖母時落淚，建豪看見父親指著修好的船便笑了。表妹太早搬椅子，害美岑漏拍一張正式團體照；她趁賓客還沒離去坦白說明並安排補拍，沒有用漂亮成品掩蓋錯誤。兩天後新人核准相簿順序，付清尾款。這筆訂單證明，照相館能完成要求繁多的工作，而不必把專業標準讓給聲音最大的人。

Before handing over the album, Meicen offered to estimate the cost of printing an extra copy for the grandmother. The couple accepted the written price and selected a small format that she could hold comfortably. Their aunt asked whether the studio could make every guest look younger. Meicen explained which ordinary light corrections were possible and which changes required explicit approval. The answer disappointed the aunt, but the bride thanked her for setting the same boundaries for everyone.

交付相簿前，美岑提出替祖母加印一冊，並先估算費用。新人接受書面報價，選了祖母容易拿取的小尺寸。姑媽問能否把每位客人都修得年輕一點。美岑說明普通光線調整與須明確同意的外貌改動有何不同。姑媽有些失望，新娘卻感謝她對所有人採同一標準。
