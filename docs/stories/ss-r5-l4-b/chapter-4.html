The Manual in the Drawer

A week later, the town cultural office asked the studio to accompany an elderly diplomat visiting Spring Harbor. He had once helped organize a sister-city attraction, and the office wanted a portrait near the bay. Meicen drafted a simple schedule before accepting the task. A clear diagram showed the pier, two sheltered stops, and the road back to the studio. The diplomat's secretary appreciated the plan but said the man's knees were weak. Meicen replied that a magnificent sunset was not essential to a good portrait. A comfortable chair and sufficient time mattered more than the horizon.

一週後，鎮文化課請照相館陪同一位年長外交官走訪春港。他過去協助規劃姊妹市景點，公所想替他在海灣旁拍肖像。美岑接案前先擬好行程，畫圖標明碼頭、兩個避風停留點，以及回店道路。外交官祕書很欣賞規畫，卻提醒老人膝蓋不好。美岑說，壯麗落日不是拍好肖像的必要條件；舒適座椅和充裕時間比遠方地平線重要。

The studio's light meter failed during a practice session. Shulin opened a drawer to find the machine's manual, which was covered with notes in his handwriting. A mechanic from the neighboring bicycle shop offered to inspect the loose connection. He warned them to unplug the equipment first. Meicen noticed a moment of hesitation on her father's face. He disliked asking younger people for help, especially after teaching them for years. Yet he stepped aside and watched. The mechanic had to loosen a bent cover, wiped away a spot where moisture might help a germ grow, tightened a screw, adjusted a wire, and gave everyone a greeting so cheerful that the room felt less tense.

練習時，照相館的測光表突然失靈。樹林打開抽屜尋找機器說明書，上頭滿是自己的筆記。隔壁腳踏車行的技師願意來檢查鬆脫接點，先提醒眾人拔掉電源。美岑看見父親臉上短暫猶豫。他當老師傅多年，不喜歡求助年輕人；最後仍讓出位置。技師鎖緊螺絲、調整電線，並以歡快問候沖淡屋內緊張。

The secretary arrived carrying an experimental camera attachment from a university lab. It could send rough images through a cable to a computer, although it worked poorly under weak light. He called it the future of photography. Meicen was interested but asked for a paper record of the trial rather than an excited announcement. On the wall hung an old satellite image of the coast, a reminder that technology could reveal details without explaining their meaning. She wanted to strengthen the business through tools that suited actual customers, not through machines chosen to impress visitors.

祕書帶來大學實驗室的一個試驗型相機附件，能用線纜把粗糙影像送進電腦，但光線不足時效果不佳。他稱之為攝影的未來。美岑感興趣，卻要求留下測試紀錄，不急著宣傳。牆上掛著一幅舊衛星海岸影像，提醒她科技能看見更多細節，不代表就能解釋意義。她希望用真正適合顧客的工具強化生意，而不是用昂貴機器裝門面。

On the morning of the visit, heavy rain forced the party into a covered arcade. The diplomat laughed that he had traveled across oceans only to flee from a shower. The secretary worried that the photographs would lack the town's famous recreation grounds. Meicen moved a chair beside a shopfront and asked the guest about the people he had met here. His expression changed when he described an old fisherman who taught him to mend nets. Her best photograph captured that memory in his face. No impressive monument appeared behind him, only an ordinary street beginning to dry.

來訪當日上午，大雨逼得一行人躲進騎樓。外交官笑說自己跨海而來，沒想到只是為了躲一場雨。祕書擔心照片拍不到小鎮知名的休憩景點。美岑把椅子移到店門邊，問客人當年在此遇見什麼人。他想起曾教自己補網的老漁民，神情頓時不同。美岑最好的照片便捕捉了這段記憶。背後沒有宏偉紀念碑，只有一條剛開始變乾的普通街道。

Back at the studio, the diplomat requested greetings cards for several friends, but he insisted on approving the message and recipient list himself. Meicen produced a draft card, then marked the number of prints and the day they could be collected. Yuzhen, who had worried about unfamiliar guests, brought tea and listened as the visitor spoke. "I thought I was here as a decoration for the town," he admitted. "You have made me remember that my life included other people." The secretary called the resulting photograph outstanding, and this time Meicen accepted the praise without pretending she had done it alone.

回到店裡，外交官想替朋友製作問候卡，但堅持自己確認文字和收件名單。美岑先做草稿，註明張數與取件日。原本擔心陌生客人的玉珍端茶過來，聽訪客談起往事。「我以為自己只是鎮上的裝飾品，」他坦言，「妳讓我記得生命裡還有許多人。」祕書稱照片非常出色，這一次美岑接受稱讚，也沒假裝一切全是自己的功勞。

When the invoice was paid, Mingxuan noticed that preparation had taken longer than expected. The weather delay had consumed an hour; the repair had taken another. The studio's profit was real but modest. Meicen entered the details in a new cost sheet and proposed keeping a standard equipment inspection before every outside job. Shulin nodded, embarrassed about the meter but relieved they had not hidden the problem. After closing, Meicen copied the visiting secretary’s observations into the studio ledger. She added the weather delay, the guest’s request for extra rest, and the unexpected repair. If a similar job came next month, another worker could read this record instead of guessing. The report made no one look like a hero, but it made next month’s work easier. A business did not become modern by denying old weaknesses. It became more reliable each time someone wrote down a mistake and changed the next day's routine.

收到款項後，明軒發現準備工作耗時比預期長：天候耽誤一小時，修理又用掉一小時。生意有賺，但利潤不多。美岑把細節記入成本表，提議每次外拍前例行檢查設備。樹林對測光表故障仍有些不好意思，但慶幸家人沒掩蓋問題。打烊後，美岑把祕書的觀察寫進工作紀錄，補上天候延誤、訪客休息需求及臨時維修。下個月若接到類似工作，其他人就能照紀錄辦理，不用猜測。這份報告沒讓誰變成英雄，卻可能讓下次工作更順利。生意不是否認舊弱點就能變得現代；而是每次有人記下錯誤，隔天便真正改變做法，才會愈來愈可靠。
