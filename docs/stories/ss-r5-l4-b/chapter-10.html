The First Balance Sheet

1996，晨光照相館的第二張底片 · 第 10 / 25 章 · 3000–4000 · 英中對照 · 校訂中

On the thirtieth morning, Meicen posted the trial results on the studio wall. Ordinary printing had continued to fall, but restoration, school photographs, and the wedding order had brought enough income to cover the month's direct costs. It was not yet a prosperous business. She made that distinction at the start of her presentation. Her father's face tightened when Mingxuan asked whether selling the building remained an option. Meicen gave an apology for any moment she had sounded certain before the numbers existed. "This is evidence for one more trial," she said, "not a command for anyone to stay."

第三十天早晨，美岑把試辦結果貼在牆上。普通沖印收入仍下滑，但修復、學校拍攝與婚禮訂單帶來的錢，足以支付當月直接成本。照相館還算不上繁榮，她在簡報一開始就說清楚。明軒問賣屋是否仍是選項，樹林臉色一緊。美岑向家人道歉，承認自己有時在數字出來前講得太有把握。「這只能支持再試一次，」她說，「不能命令任何人留下。」

Their cousin, a surgeon at the town hospital, arrived with a bandage around his hand after a minor accident. He brought photographs for a public health display about preventing kitchen burns. He said the display should illustrate safe actions without showing identifiable patients. Meicen paused to congratulate him on choosing education over frightening pictures and offered a careful bargain: a reduced fee if the hospital supplied approved captions and handled consent. He agreed. The commission was useful, but its critical value lay in how clearly it separated patient privacy from public instruction.

在鎮立醫院當外科醫師的表哥來訪，手上因輕微意外纏著繃帶。他帶來一批要做廚房燙傷預防展示的照片，希望透過安全示範教育大眾，而不是露出可辨識的病人。美岑稱讚他的選擇，提出交易條件：院方若提供核可說明文字並處理同意程序，照相館可降低費用。表哥接受。這筆案子的價值不只在收入，更在清楚區分病人隱私與公共教育。

After lunch, an explosion of thunder shook the windows. Shulin said he must forbid anyone from entering the darkroom until he checked a loose electrical switch. Mingxuan agreed that mere speed was not worth a fire. He called an electrician and asked for a written inspection. The family had once treated such safety checks as unnecessary expenses. Now a fixed maintenance allowance appeared in the new budget. Meicen looked at the ledger and thought of the old photographs documenting changes in civilization: tools improved, but people still had to choose whether to use them responsibly.

午餐後，炸雷震動窗戶。樹林禁止任何人進暗房，直到自己檢查過鬆動的電源開關。明軒也認為，單純追求速度不值得冒火災風險。他請電工來，並索取書面檢查。家人以前常把這類安全工作當作不必要開銷，如今新預算終於列出固定維護費。美岑看著帳簿，想到那些記錄文明變遷的老照片：工具會進步，但人仍要選擇是否負責任地使用。

A pregnant teacher came to collect a family album in the afternoon. She asked whether the studio would ever install a computer system for checking appointments. Meicen said they were testing a basic program, but paper records would remain the official reference until the process was reliable. The teacher had heard that many businesses were beginning to advertise on a website. Mingxuan proposed a simple page with opening hours and prices, which a former classmate could help prepare. Meicen added a resolution: no client photographs would appear online without specific written permission, even if the page received few visitors.

下午，一位懷孕的教師來領全家福相簿，詢問照相館會不會裝電腦預約系統。美岑說正在試基本程式，但流程尚未可靠前，紙本紀錄仍是正式依據。教師聽說不少商家開始在網站刊登廣告。明軒提議請舊同學幫忙做簡單網頁，列出營業時間與價格。美岑在決議中加上一條：即使網頁沒多少人看，也不能未經個別書面同意，就放上顧客照片。

The family discussed the results until evening. Yuzhen wanted a room for album classes; Shulin wanted to continue selected restoration projects without a night shift; Mingxuan wanted a clear schedule for recovering any money he had invested. Meicen was suspicious of a quick agreement that ignored those differences. She wrote the options on separate cards and let each person mark a preferred path. No one voted to close the studio immediately, but no one promised to keep the building forever. They agreed to a second, sixty-day trial, with a strict limit on borrowing and paid hours.

家人談到傍晚。玉珍希望有一間教整理相簿的教室；樹林想接少量修復案，不再輪夜班；明軒希望投進去的錢有明確回收時程。美岑懷疑太快的一致意見會抹去差別，於是分卡列出選項，讓每人標記偏好。沒有人選擇立刻關店，也沒有人承諾永遠保住建築。大家同意再試六十天，嚴格限制借款與有薪工時。

Before closing, an elderly visitor arrived with a photograph of a settler family standing beside a weathered tomb. He asked whether the studio could turn it into a poster for a local history event. Meicen explained that the image first needed to be examined for ownership and permission. He was disappointed but left his telephone number. Later, Shulin turned off the window lights and said, "Perhaps a small shop survives because it keeps making careful decisions." Meicen placed the new balance sheet in a folder. Their first month had ended, and their hardest questions had only become clearer.

打烊前，一位老人帶來早年移居者家族站在風化墓碑旁的照片，想印成地方史活動海報。美岑解釋，必須先釐清影像權利與授權。老人雖失望，仍留下電話。稍後樹林關掉櫥窗燈，說：「或許小店能活下來，就是因為一直願意仔細做決定。」美岑把新損益表放進資料夾。第一個月結束了，最難的問題反而變得更清楚。

At the end of the meeting, Yuzhen said that a revolution did not need to begin with a famous speech. Sometimes it began when people stopped pretending that one exhausted mother could do three jobs without pay. She wrote her preferred teaching hours in the new schedule, leaving two afternoons each week free. Mingxuan checked the figures and signed beside her entry. Meicen realized that the second trial would succeed only if the people working in it could still recognize their own lives outside the shop.

會議結束時，玉珍說，革命不一定要從有名的演說開始。有時它只是大家不再假裝一位疲憊母親可以不支薪做三份工作。她把自己願意授課的時數寫進新班表，每週保留兩個下午休息。明軒核對數字，在旁邊簽名。美岑明白，第二輪試辦若要算成功，店裡工作的人也必須保有店外的生活。
