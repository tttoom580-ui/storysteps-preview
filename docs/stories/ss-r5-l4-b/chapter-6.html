銀行寄來的信

The Envelope from the Bank

1996，晨光照相館的第二張底片 · 第 6 / 25 章 · 3000–4000 · 英中對照 · 校訂中

On the tenth day of the trial, the bank sent a sealed envelope addressed to Zhou Shulin. A young consultant arrived soon afterward, carrying a folder about small-business loans. She explained that many consumer habits had changed: people wanted cheap prints quickly, while fewer were willing to wait for hand-colored portraits. Meicen agreed with the facts but questioned the proposed investment. The offer would require the family to guarantee a large machine with their house. Her father called the plan conservative because the bank was well known. She replied that a familiar name did not make every contract safe.

試辦第十天，銀行寄來一封給周樹林的密封信。不久，一位年輕顧問帶著小店貸款資料來訪。她說明消費習慣已改變：大家要便宜又快的沖印，願意等手工上色肖像的人愈來愈少。美岑同意觀察，卻質疑投資方案，因為銀行要求以家裡房屋替大型機器作擔保。父親認為知名銀行的提議算是保守穩健；美岑卻說，名字熟悉不代表每張契約都安全。

Yuzhen had been dyeing a sample of linen for a handmade album cover when the visitors arrived. She asked them not to touch the cloth before it dried. The consultant admired its grace and said such goods might be sold as luxury gifts. Mingxuan immediately began calculating profit, but Meicen reminded him that each cover required several hours. A business cannot simply attach a beautiful price to unpaid labor. They laid the items on a separate table so the wet fabric would not stain the customer negatives stored nearby.

顧問來時，玉珍正在替手工相簿封面用的亞麻布染色，請大家等布乾了再碰。顧問稱讚成品優雅，認為可以賣成高級禮品。明軒立刻試算利潤，美岑卻提醒，每個封面需要好幾小時的手工。生意不能替未計薪的勞動貼上漂亮價標。大家把布品放在獨立桌上，避免濕布染到旁邊存放的顧客底片。

The bank papers included a chart showing the habitual purchases of nearby households. The data had been gathered from one busy shopping street, not from the harbor neighborhood itself. "It would be ignorant to treat these customers as identical," Meicen said. She proposed a small orientation session for older clients, teaching them how to compare restoration options before ordering anything. The consultant argued that complicated explanations might isolate nervous customers. Meicen answered that a clear explanation could do the opposite. It could let someone say no without embarrassment, then return when ready.

銀行文件附上一張附近家庭慣常消費的圖表，但數據取自熱鬧商店街，並非港邊住宅區。「把兩邊顧客當成完全一樣，是不懂情況。」美岑說。她提議舉辦小型說明會，讓年長顧客先學會比較修復選項，再決定要不要下單。顧問擔心解說太複雜，反而讓緊張的人感到孤立；美岑認為清楚的說明能讓人自在拒絕，想好後再回來。

At dusk, Shulin tried to repair a small microscope that belonged to his old friend, a biology teacher. He said he missed the days when every occupation seemed to have its own patient tools. On the shelf, a stereo played a familiar song, but even the music did not soften his frustration. His hands began to tremble while he turned a screw. Meicen took the instrument gently and told him to rest. He was furious for a moment, not at her but at the fact that ordinary work now took such effort. They sat together without pretending to solve aging in a single evening.

黃昏，樹林替老友——一位生物教師——修理小顯微鏡。他懷念過去各行各業似乎都有耐心使用的工具。架上的音響播放熟悉歌曲，卻沒能舒緩他的挫折。轉螺絲時，他的手開始發抖。美岑輕輕接過儀器，請他休息。他一時怒不可遏，不是對女兒，而是惱怒平常的工作如今如此費力。父女安靜坐著，沒有假裝一晚就能解決老化問題。

Mingxuan came downstairs with a different proposal. Rather than investing in an expensive automatic printer, he wanted to enclose the current shop's rear room and rent its front counter to a parcel agency. Meicen pointed out that parcel traffic could expose private portraits on the shelves. "We can move the shelves," he answered. "We can," she said, "but let us count that labor too." Yuzhen, usually timid in money discussions, said clearly that she would not work in a windowless storeroom. The family stopped talking and listened as she described what a safe workplace meant to her.

明軒下樓提出另一個方案：不買昂貴自動沖印機，而是把後室隔起來，前櫃租給包裹代收業者。美岑指出，包裹人流可能看到架上的私人肖像。「把架子搬走就好。」明軒說。「可以，但搬動的工也要計算。」美岑答。玉珍平常談錢很膽怯，這次卻清楚表示自己不願在無窗儲藏室裡工作。家人終於停下來，聽她描述安全工作環境的意義。

Before the consultant left, Meicen gave her a page of questions rather than a signature. What would happen if sales dropped again? Could payments be delayed without losing the house? Who would own the machine after the final installment? The consultant promised written answers. A small rage still burned in Shulin's voice when he said he did not want to be treated as a man who understood nothing. Meicen answered, "Then no one should sign until everyone understands." It was not victory, but the bank envelope went back into the folder unopened to any new obligation.

顧問離開前，美岑遞給她的不是簽名，而是一頁問題：若銷售再次下滑會怎樣？能否延期付款而不失去房子？最後一期繳清後機器歸誰？顧問承諾書面回答。樹林仍帶著怒氣，說自己不想被當成什麼都不懂的人。美岑答：「那就等每個人都看懂再簽。」這不是勝利，但銀行信封被放回資料夾，沒有形成新的債務。

The next day, Yuzhen asked whether she should dye another piece of fabric for the album experiment. Meicen told her to choose the hours herself and write them down. They agreed to compare three samples and one plain cover, then ask ten customers which they preferred at different prices. The experiment would not require an expensive machine or a new loan. It required honesty about time, materials, and whether a family member truly wished to keep doing the work.

隔天，玉珍問是否還要染一塊布來試製相簿。美岑請她自己選擇工作時數並記下。大家決定比較三種染布樣本與一種素面封套，再讓十位顧客看不同價格時的偏好。這場試驗不需要昂貴機器或新貸款，只需要如實面對時間、材料與家人是否真的願意繼續做。
