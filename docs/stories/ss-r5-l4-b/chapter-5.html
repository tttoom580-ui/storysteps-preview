木店裡的小螢幕

A Small Screen in a Wooden Shop

1996，晨光照相館的第二張底片 · 第 5 / 25 章 · 3000–4000 · 英中對照

用互動版查詞、朗讀與收藏

A local school organized an evening fundraiser by the river, and Meicen carried a portable scanner there as part of her second trial service. It was an awkward device, borrowed from a former colleague, and the computer beside it needed ten minutes to start. She had no allowance for new equipment, so the team would have to adjust the room rather than buy more machines. A canoe club offered photographs of its races, while parents brought faded family portraits. Their requests were distinct: athletes wanted clear action, but grandparents wanted the faces in old pictures treated with care.

當地學校在河邊舉行募款夜，美岑帶著借來的攜帶式掃描器，測試第二項服務。這部機器笨重又不靈活，旁邊的電腦開機要十分鐘。她沒有添購設備的預算，只能調整場地來配合機器。獨木舟社拿來比賽照片，家長則帶來泛黃全家福。兩種需求很不同：選手想要清楚的動作影像，長輩卻希望舊照片的人臉被溫柔對待。

Yuzhen watched the first enlargement appear on the screen and felt a wave of anxiety. She was afraid that a strange machine would abandon the careful methods her husband had spent years teaching. Meicen explained that a device could save time without replacing judgment. A colorful poster beside them quoted an idiom about turning a new page. Some children saw the singer on it as an idol; others were drawn to its imaginative lettering. Meicen made sure no child entered a name in the computer without a parent nearby. The scanner was a service counter, not a box for collecting private details.

玉珍看著第一張放大影像出現在螢幕上，神情焦慮。她害怕陌生機器會讓丈夫多年傳授的細膩方法被丟棄。美岑解釋，設備能省時間，但不能代替判斷。旁邊色彩鮮明的海報引用「翻開新的一頁」的成語；有些孩子把海報歌手當偶像，另一些被富有想像力的字體吸引。美岑堅持，沒有家長陪同，孩子不能在電腦輸入姓名。掃描器是服務櫃檯，不是蒐集私人資料的盒子。

The school orchestra began to play, and the noise made instructions difficult to hear. A father raised an objection when he saw a picture of his daughter briefly displayed on the monitor. Meicen immediately turned the screen away from the crowd and apologized. She had tested the machine with permission to scan, but not permission to exhibit. The distinction made an impact on the volunteers. One suggested putting a curtain around the computer. Another offered a cardboard screen. They built a small private corner before accepting another picture, even though the line grew longer.

學校管弦樂團開始演奏，現場吵得聽不清指示。一位父親發現女兒照片短暫顯示在螢幕上，提出異議。美岑立即把螢幕轉離人群並道歉。她雖取得掃描同意，卻沒有展示許可。這個分別讓志工受到提醒：有人建議拉布簾，有人找來紙板擋板。儘管隊伍變長，大家仍先搭出保護隱私的小角落，才繼續收件。

Toward the end of the evening, the sponsor announced that a successful project could bring prosperity to the district. Meicen did not seize the microphone to make a grand speech. She explained the practical results to her brother: eleven scans, six paid prints, three customers requesting later appointments, and one mistake corrected on the spot. Mingxuan admitted that the figures were more favorable than he had predicted. "But will people return after the novelty is gone?" he asked. She had no answer. Their next step was to invite those customers back under ordinary shop conditions.

晚會快結束時，贊助者宣稱成功企畫能帶動地方繁榮。美岑沒有搶過麥克風高談闊論，而是向哥哥報告實際成果：十一件掃描、六份付費印相、三位客人預約日後到店，以及一件當場修正的錯誤。明軒承認數字比想像中有利。「等新鮮感過去，客人還會回來嗎？」他問。美岑答不出來。下一步只能在平常營業條件下，請那些顧客再來一次。

On the way home, they passed a group holding a protest against higher school activity fees. The gathering reminded Meicen that even reasonable prices could exclude families. She suggested two service levels: a basic reproduction at a posted price, and a longer restoration with an estimate approved beforehand. Yuzhen wanted one free community workshop each month. Mingxuan worried about giving away labor, but they agreed that the workshop could be limited to an hour and paid for from a separate education fund. A promise became useful only when its cost and boundary were visible.

回家途中，他們經過一群抗議學校活動費漲價的家長。美岑想到，即使價格合理，也可能讓一些家庭無法使用服務。她建議分成兩種：公開定價的基本翻拍，以及事先核價的長時間修復。玉珍則想每月開一次免費社區課。明軒怕白做工，最後大家同意把課程限制在一小時，費用另由教育基金支應。承諾只有在成本與界線清楚時，才真正有用。

Back at the studio, Meicen placed the borrowed scanner beside the old enlarger. Neither machine was a symbol of victory. One used lenses and chemicals; the other needed a computer and patient instructions. The family would test both for another three weeks. Meicen also wrote a borrowing agreement with her former colleague, including transport costs, insurance, and a return date. If the pilot failed, the machine would go back on time. If it succeeded, they could compare the monthly rental with the price of buying used equipment. Her father was surprised that a small experiment required so many decisions, but he signed the agreement after reading it twice. Before bed, Shulin wrote a new syllable on a label so his wife could pronounce the unfamiliar word "scanner" when customers asked. Yuzhen laughed and told him that she could learn it without help. Their conversation sounded like an ordinary family quarrel, but for the first time in months, it was about tomorrow's work rather than yesterday's losses.

回到店裡，美岑把借來的掃描器放在舊放大機旁。兩部機器都不是勝利的象徵：一部需要鏡片與藥水，一部需要電腦和耐心的操作。家人決定繼續測試三週。美岑也與借設備給她的舊同事寫下借用協議，列出運費、保險與歸還日期。試辦若失敗，機器要準時退還；若成功，則比較每月租金和二手設備的購買價。父親驚訝一場小試驗竟有這麼多決定，最後仍讀了兩遍才簽名。睡前，樹林在標籤上寫出「scanner」的音節，想教妻子回答顧客。玉珍笑說自己也學得會，不用他幫忙。他們像平常夫妻般拌嘴，但好幾個月來，這是第一次在討論明天的工作，而不是昨天的虧損。
