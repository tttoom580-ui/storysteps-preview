消失的肖像

The Missing Portrait

1996，晨光照相館的第二張底片 · 第 12 / 25 章 · 3000–4000 · 英中對照 · 校訂中

用互動版查詞、朗讀與收藏

Three days after the harbor archive opened its small exhibit, a portrait disappeared from Morning Light Studio's storage cabinet. Yuzhen had placed it in a blue bin after cleaning the room. By morning the bin was empty. Mingxuan feared a burglar had entered through the rear window, but there was no broken lock or damaged door. Meicen asked everyone to stop moving boxes until they could establish a definite sequence. A careless accusation would be another kind of destruction. They wrote down who had occupied each room and when, then searched the shelves without opening unrelated customer envelopes.

港口檔案小展開幕三天後，晨光照相館儲藏櫃裡的一張肖像不見了。玉珍清理房間時把它放進藍色收納箱，隔天箱子卻空了。明軒懷疑竊賊從後窗闖入，但門鎖與窗框毫無破壞痕跡。美岑請所有人先別搬箱子，以便建立確切時間順序。隨便指控別人，同樣可能造成傷害。他們記下各人在什麼時間進出哪個房間，再搜查架子，沒有打開無關顧客的信封。

The missing picture belonged to Mrs. Tan, a widow who wore a silver bracelet in every family photograph. The image showed her husband as a young volunteer teacher in the mountain villages. A film agent had once offered money for the original, hoping to use it in a television documentary. Mrs. Tan refused because the family was still divided about how to portray him. Meicen called her immediately. The widow made no angry comment; instead, she asked the studio to continue looking and promised to bring the original receipt in the afternoon.

失蹤照片屬於譚太太。這位寡婦在每張全家福裡都戴著銀手環，照片拍的是丈夫年輕時在山村做志工教師的模樣。曾有影視經紀人想花錢買原件，用於電視紀錄片，但譚太太拒絕，因為家人對如何描述丈夫仍有分歧。美岑立刻打電話給她。譚太太沒有生氣責備，只請大家繼續找，並答應下午帶原始收據來。

While checking the record book, Meicen found an order slip folded into the wrong page. A student assistant had moved the portrait into a durable cardboard sleeve because he feared the old bin would collapse. He had put the sleeve behind a poster without updating the location code. The student returned and confessed the mistake before Meicen could ask. Shulin wanted him dismissed, but Yuzhen said his wish to protect the picture had been faithful, even though his procedure was wrong. Meicen asked him to help redesign the storage log, then reviewed the result with both parents.

核對登記簿時，美岑找到夾錯頁的訂單紙。一名學生助理怕舊收納箱會垮，便把肖像移進更耐用的紙板護套，放到海報後方，卻沒有更新存放代碼。他回來後主動承認。樹林原本想辭退他，玉珍說，他保護照片的心意是真誠的，只是程序錯了。美岑讓他一起重設儲藏紀錄，再請父母共同檢查結果。

Mrs. Tan arrived in the afternoon and listened to the explanation. She said the experience reminded her of an era when families kept everything in a single locked drawer, and the oldest person carried the only key. "That drawer created hardship for everyone after my husband died," she said. "He had notebooks no one could locate." She wanted the portrait returned and the receipt corrected, but she did not pursue punishment. The student apologized directly. They photographed the label and placed the portrait in a numbered box where two people could find it without relying on memory.

譚太太下午到店，聽完說明。她想起過去家家戶戶把重要東西鎖在同一抽屜，只有最年長的人掌握鑰匙。「丈夫過世後，那個抽屜讓大家吃了很多苦，」她說，「他留下的筆記誰也找不到。」她只要求拿回肖像並更正收據，不打算追究懲罰。學生親自道歉。眾人拍下新標籤，把肖像放進雙人都能依編號找到的位置。

That evening, a documentary producer telephoned and asked whether the story of the missing portrait might bring the studio unexpected fame. Meicen refused the suggestion. The family had already faced enough trouble with privacy; it did not need to turn a client's fear into publicity. The producer mentioned a rising inflation rate and said small shops needed attention to survive. Meicen answered that attention was not the same as trust. A tragic mistake could produce a headline, but the only proper use of this incident was to improve how future photographs were handled.

傍晚，一位紀錄片製作人來電，問失蹤肖像事件是否能讓照相館意外成名。美岑拒絕了。家人早已因隱私問題受過教訓，不需要把客戶的恐懼拿來宣傳。製作人提到物價上漲，說小店需要關注度才能生存。美岑回答，受到注意不等於得到信任。悲劇性的失誤或許能製造新聞，但這次事件唯一合適的用途，是改進日後保存照片的方法。

After closing, the student showed Meicen a small project about tribal weaving patterns that he had photographed during a school trip. He had asked permission before taking any pictures, but had never learned how to record that permission. She suggested adding a simple consent sheet to the lessons he was planning for his classmates. He agreed to commit one afternoon a week to learning archive care instead of working alone in storage. The new system emerged from an error, not from someone's brilliance. Meicen added one more rule to the ledger: every transfer of an original must leave a trace that another person can verify.

打烊後，學生讓美岑看他校外旅行拍攝的原住民族織紋習作。他當時曾徵求拍攝同意，卻沒學過如何記錄那份許可。美岑建議他下次教同學時加入簡單同意表。他願意每週投入一個下午學習檔案照護，不再獨自進儲藏室工作。新制度來自一次錯誤，而非誰突然變天才。美岑在帳本增加一條：每次移動正本都必須留下可由他人查證的紀錄。

Before the studio reopened the cabinet, Meicen asked the student to help occupy the doorway so nobody would move boxes during the review. She explained that an accurate story could emerge only when the order of events remained clear. Then she crossed his name from the temporary access list and replaced it with a two-person sign-out rule. He understood that the change was not a punishment for telling the truth. It was a safeguard for the next person who might make an ordinary mistake.

重新開櫃前，美岑請學生站在門口守著，避免查核期間有人移動箱子。她解釋，只有先釐清事件順序，才能得到可靠的說明。接著她從臨時單人進出名單劃掉他的名字，改成雙人簽出規則。學生明白，這不是懲罰他說實話，而是保障下一個可能犯普通錯誤的人。
