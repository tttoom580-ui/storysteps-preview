裂玻璃的委託

The Case of the Broken Glass

1996，晨光照相館的第二張底片 · 第 2 / 25 章 · 3000–4000 · 英中對照

用互動版查詞、朗讀與收藏

The first customer of the trial brought a wooden box to Morning Light Studio. Inside lay a delicate glass negative, wrapped in a newspaper from 1954. Its image showed an old stone fort above the harbor, with a distant mill beyond the hills. The customer, Mrs. Luo, had found it after clearing her late aunt's desk. Her gaze remained fixed on a dark shape near the water. "My mother arrived here after a long voyage," she explained. "The family says this is the first picture taken of her, but no one can identify the figure."

試辦開始後的第一位顧客帶來一只木盒。裡面是一張脆弱的玻璃底片，包在一九五四年的報紙裡。影像中，港口上方有一座舊石堡，山邊還有一間遙遠的磨坊。羅太太清理亡姨的書桌時找到它，目光一直停在水邊的暗影上。「母親歷經長途航程才來到這裡，」她說，「家人說這是她的第一張照片，可是沒人認得那個身影。」

Meicen first considered the extent of the damage: three scratches, a missing corner, and a silver patch that reflected light. Her father warned against opening the box beneath the strongest lamp. "A silver atom is small, but this is not an atomic experiment," he said. "You cannot bring back what the surface no longer holds." He had repaired many photographs without inventing missing faces. Meicen agreed to make only a clear copy, plus a transparent explanation of every change. She would guarantee careful handling, not a miraculous reconstruction.

美岑先評估損壞程度：三道刮痕、一個缺角，還有一片會反光的銀色區域。父親提醒她不要在強燈下打開木盒。「銀是由原子組成的，但這不是原子實驗，」他說，「表面沒有留下的東西，不能憑空變回來。」他修過許多照片，從不捏造缺失的人臉。美岑同意只做清晰翻拍，並透明說明每一步修改。她能保證小心處理，卻不能保證神奇復原。

A local history teacher arrived that afternoon as a messenger from the school. He needed pictures for an exhibition on immigration, old trades, and how the harbor landscape had changed. The teacher offered a combination of payment and access to a school enlarger, but Meicen refused to promise Mrs. Luo's picture without permission. The image was an intimate family record before it was a historical object. She wrote a separate consent form and asked Mrs. Luo to retain the original in her own possession. The school could display only the approved copy and its factual description.

下午，一位地方史教師代表學校登門，希望為移民、舊行業與港灣地景變遷展覽蒐集照片。他提出現金報酬，也願借學校放大機，作為合作組合，但美岑不肯擅自承諾羅太太的相片。這首先是親密的家庭紀錄，其次才是史料。她另寫同意書，請羅太太保管正本。學校最多只能展示核可影本及確定的說明文字。

At supper, Mingxuan complained that his sister was making a productive afternoon disappear into paperwork. He pointed out that a chain shop would copy the glass in minutes. Meicen answered that the chain probably had no holder for such a fragile plate. Her mother looked at the receipt and said in a murmur, "What if the customer cannot afford all this work?" They agreed to revise the price after measuring the actual labor. The family could not survive by giving everything away. Nor could it build a new trade by charging people for mysteries that no camera could solve.

晚餐時，明軒抱怨妹妹把原本能有產值的下午全耗在文書上。他說連鎖店幾分鐘就能翻拍。美岑回答，對方的設備未必能固定這樣脆弱的玻璃片。母親看著收據低聲問：「萬一顧客負擔不起呢？」眾人同意先算實際工時，再修訂價格。不能每次都免費，否則一家人無法生活；也不能為相機無法解開的謎題向顧客收錢。

The following morning, Meicen visited the town museum. A faded map identified the fort, while an old photograph of a fossil collection showed the same hillside before a road was built. In the corner of another picture she recognized the mill chimney, which placed Mrs. Luo's image between two known construction dates. She took notes rather than announcing a discovery. The museum director discussed the psychological cost of a false identification: a family might live for years with a mistake if it came from an apparently trustworthy expert.

次日上午，美岑前往鎮立博物館。泛黃地圖標出古堡的位置，一張化石收藏展覽照也拍下道路興建前的同一座山。另一張照片角落出現磨坊煙囪，能把羅家的照片年代縮小至兩次工程之間。她只記筆記，沒有急著宣布發現。館長提醒她，錯誤辨識會造成心理代價：若權威人士講錯話，一個家庭可能抱著錯誤的身分故事過上多年。

When Mrs. Luo returned, the studio offered two prints. The first reproduced the scratches honestly; the second improved contrast but marked every repaired area on a separate sheet. Mrs. Luo did not demand a name for the shadow. Instead, she recognized a scarf hanging from the dark figure's arm, the scarf her mother wore in every later family portrait. She sat quietly for a long time. "The picture remains uncertain," she said, "but now I can ask a better question." Meicen felt the room change. They had sold more than a sheet of paper without pretending to sell certainty.

羅太太回來時，照相館交出兩張影本。一張忠實保留刮痕；另一張增加對比，並另附修復位置說明。她沒有要求替暗影取名，反而認出身影手臂上的圍巾，與母親後來每張家庭照中的圍巾相似。她靜坐了好一會兒。「照片還有不確定的地方，」她說，「但現在我知道該問什麼。」美岑感到屋內氣氛變了。他們賣出的不只是紙張，也沒有假裝能販賣確定的答案。

In her trial notebook, Meicen named the new service "documented restoration." She recorded each stage, price, and client decision. Mingxuan studied the figures and admitted that the margin might be better than ordinary prints, provided there were enough customers. Shulin worried about keeping glass safely, so they built a padded shelf instead of piling boxes on the floor. Yuzhen suggested an appointment system that allowed older visitors time to tell their stories. The business plan had not crossed an ocean, but for the family it felt like the beginning of an unfamiliar voyage.

美岑把新服務命名為「附紀錄的影像修復」，在試辦筆記裡逐項記下流程、價錢與顧客決定。明軒看過數字後承認，若有足夠客源，利潤可能高於一般沖印。樹林擔心玻璃底片的存放安全，於是大家先做了有襯墊的架子，而不是把盒子堆在地上。玉珍建議採預約制，留時間聽長輩說故事。生意計畫還沒跨過海洋，對一家人而言，卻已像踏上一場陌生航程。
