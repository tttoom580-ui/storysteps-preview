渡輪上的相機

A Camera on the Ferry

1996，晨光照相館的第二張底片 · 第 3 / 25 章 · 3000–4000 · 英中對照

用互動版查詞、朗讀與收藏

By Wednesday the school principal had offered Morning Light Studio a small assignment: photograph a safety exercise on the harbor ferry. Meicen would receive a fixed payment if she supplied twelve clear images and a written account of the event. Mingxuan called the sum incredible for half a day's work; he had forgotten transportation, film, processing, and housework waiting at home. Yuzhen packed rice and raisin bread. She insisted that lunch mattered as much as any camera, because a tired photographer could fail to concentrate exactly when a useful moment appeared.

星期三，校長給晨光照相館一份小委託：拍攝港口渡輪上的安全演習。若交出十二張清楚照片與事件文字紀錄，就可獲得固定費用。明軒覺得半天賺這麼多很驚人，卻忘了車費、底片、沖洗，以及家裡還得做的家事。玉珍準備米飯和一盒葡萄乾，堅持午餐跟相機一樣重要：攝影師疲倦時，往往會在關鍵瞬間失去專注力。

A lifeguard named A-Qin met Meicen by the ferry gate. He explained that this was an intermediate exercise, not a dangerous full rescue. The maximum number of participants was thirty, and only two could enter the practice boat at one time. Meicen asked who would defend the students if her photographs accidentally showed them breaking rules. A-Qin said the pictures should illustrate procedures, not shame individuals. They agreed that her priority was to capture the order of the exercise and the visible safety equipment. She could not interrupt the drill for a more dramatic angle.

救生員阿欽在渡輪門口迎接美岑，說明這只是中級演習，不是完整的高風險救援。參與者最多三十人，練習艇一次只准兩人進入。美岑問，若照片拍到學生操作失誤，誰來維護他們的權益。阿欽說，影像應用來說明流程，而非羞辱個人。他們同意拍攝重點是演習順序與安全設備，不能為了戲劇性角度打斷演練。

The boat moved at a slow pace along the pier. A teacher described the context: the harbor had suffered two minor accidents, yet many people still treated life jackets as decoration. On the opposite deck, a visitor in luxurious clothes complained about the delay and tried to insult the trainees. Meicen did not point her camera at him. Instead she photographed how the captain checked each buckle. She also measured what percentage of the students could close a jacket without assistance. Seven needed help, an observation far more useful than a picture of an angry stranger.

渡輪沿碼頭緩慢前行。教師說明背景：這座港口曾發生兩次小事故，但許多人仍把救生衣當裝飾。對面甲板上，一位衣著華麗的乘客抱怨延誤，還出言羞辱學員。美岑沒有對準他拍照，而是記錄船長如何檢查每個扣環；她也計算多少比例的學生能自行穿好救生衣。結果七人需要協助。這項觀察比一張憤怒乘客的照片更有用。

When the ferry docked, a wave struck the side and made a student stumble. The lifeguard moved before Meicen raised her camera. No one was hurt, but the student began to cry from embarrassment. Meicen lowered her lens. She could proceed with the assignment without publishing a frightening close-up. Later, A-Qin thanked her. "People think photography is about catching everything," he said. "Sometimes good judgment means choosing what not to keep." Meicen realized her new business might be built upon decisions like that, rather than upon faster machines.

渡輪靠岸時，側浪打來，一名學生踉蹌了一下。阿欽在美岑舉起相機前便上前扶住。沒有人受傷，學生卻因難堪而哭泣。美岑放下鏡頭。她仍可完成委託，不必刊登一張令人恐懼的特寫。之後阿欽向她道謝：「很多人以為攝影就是什麼都抓下來，其實好判斷有時在於知道什麼不該留下。」她開始理解，新生意或許取決於這類選擇，而不是更快的機器。

Back at the studio, Shulin examined the contact sheet. One photograph showed the captain's hand fastening a bright orange strap; another showed the orderly queue before boarding. The images offered a better source of inspiration for a school poster than a staged heroic pose. Mingxuan helped calculate the calorie cost of the packed lunch, joking that he would soon charge for every grain of rice. Meicen replied that a serious business should record material costs without making its workers feel guilty for eating. Their argument ended when Yuzhen placed the invoice between them.

回店後，樹林檢查接觸印樣。一張拍到船長扣緊鮮橘色束帶的手，另一張記錄登船前整齊的隊伍。這些照片比安排好的英雄姿勢，更適合作為學校海報的靈感。明軒開玩笑替便當計算熱量，說以後連每粒米都要收費。美岑回答，認真經營應記錄材料支出，卻不該讓工作的人因吃飯而感到罪惡。玉珍把發票放在兩人中間，爭論便結束了。

The principal came that evening and approved ten photographs. For the remaining two, she asked Meicen to choose images with clearer faces; Meicen explained why the original request required an action record, not close portraits of pupils. The principal agreed after reading the written brief again. They signed the receipt and discussed the possibility of a regular school photography contract. Mingxuan was impressed, but Meicen insisted that one payment was not proof of a thriving business. She wanted evidence from several kinds of clients before promising her parents a future.

傍晚，校長來看照片，核准了十張。剩下兩張，她希望能換成學生臉部較清楚的照片。美岑重讀委託書，說明原本要求的是動作流程紀錄，不是學生特寫。校長同意後簽了收據，並提到可能有定期拍攝的合作。明軒很受鼓舞，美岑卻提醒，一筆收入還不能證明新事業能活下去。她需要不同類型顧客的證據，才有資格向父母承諾未來。

That night, Meicen pinned the ferry sequence above the darkroom sink. It showed a story that began with preparation, moved through uncertainty, and ended with an ordinary safe arrival. "There is no hero in these photographs," her father observed. "Perhaps that is why they work," she answered. He handed her his old notebook from his first year as a photographer. Between pages of lighting measurements she found a sentence: Never ask a picture to prove more than it can show. She copied it into the new appointment ledger, leaving room below for the next lesson.

當晚，美岑把渡輪照片順序貼在暗房洗槽上方。故事由準備開始，經過不確定的片刻，最後平安靠岸。「這些照片裡沒有英雄。」父親說。「也許這正是它們有用的原因。」美岑回答。樹林把自己第一年拍照的筆記交給她。燈光數據之間寫著一句話：「別要求照片證明它無法證明的事。」她把這句話抄進新預約簿，並在下方留下空白，等著下一堂課。
