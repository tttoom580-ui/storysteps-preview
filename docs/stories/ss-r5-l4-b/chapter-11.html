A Code on the Back

1996，晨光照相館的第二張底片 · 第 11 / 25 章 · 3000–4000 · 英中對照 · 校訂中

The second trial began with a dusty carton from the harbor archive. Inside were photographs and an old telegraph codebook, collected when the port's first radio station replaced the coastal signal post. A historian wanted the pictures to circulate in a small exhibition about communication. He showed Meicen an image of a ship wreck after a storm, and another of sailors enjoying a marathon race along the waterfront. The material could expand the studio's work, but some negatives belonged to families who had never agreed to public display. Meicen proposed a catalog first, before anyone ordered posters.

第二輪試辦從港口檔案室送來的一只滿是灰塵的紙箱開始。裡頭是老照片與電報密碼本，都是海港第一座無線電站取代岸邊訊號塔時留下的資料。歷史研究者希望用照片舉辦小型通訊展。他指給美岑看風暴後的船隻殘骸，還有水手沿海濱參加長跑比賽的照片。這些材料可能讓照相館擴展服務，但有些底片屬於從未同意公開的家族。美岑提議先建目錄，再談海報。

The historian believed that photographs of old occupations could teach children how capitalism had changed the harbor. He also hoped to show how one dominant shipping firm had controlled wages. Mingxuan warned that the exhibit might anger people who still worked for that company. The historian argued that hiding the past had a consequence too. Meicen asked him to separate documented facts from opinions and to identify which photographs were safe to use. Persuasion alone would not replace consent, and neither would a worthy educational goal.

研究者認為舊行業照片能教孩子理解資本主義如何改變港口，也想揭露某家占主導地位的船運公司曾如何控制工資。明軒擔心展示會惹怒仍在那家公司上班的人。研究者說，掩蓋過去也會有後果。美岑請他分清可證實的事實與意見，並標出可安全使用的照片。再有說服力的理由，也不能代替當事人同意；教育目標再好也一樣。

A retired sailor recognized one photograph showing his sister at a protest. He said she had been called a rebel when she demanded safer boats, but the picture could not show why she had spoken out. The family's eventual reconciliation with the company was a story known only to a few relatives. He granted permission for a small caption and a private school talk, not a commercial calendar. Meicen negotiated those terms in writing. The sailor smiled at her careful questions and said, "You do not need to make my sister graceful. She was brave while she was angry."

一位退休水手認出照片中參與抗議的妹妹。她當年要求船隻安全，卻被叫作叛逆者；單憑照片看不出她為何站出來。家族後來與公司和解的過程，只有少數親友知道。他同意小型說明牌和私下校園演講，不同意做商業月曆。美岑把條件寫清楚。水手對她細心提問露出笑容：「不用把我妹妹修成優雅模樣。她生氣時也很勇敢。」

Meanwhile, the studio's old solar calculator stopped working. Shulin teased Mingxuan about being helpless without electricity, then watched him repair the contacts. The tiny machine was not important by itself, but it offered an indication of the family's new habits: someone noticed a problem, another person checked it, and nobody had to pretend to know everything. A visiting child asked whether the calculator could tickle a number into changing. Yuzhen laughed, handed him paper, and showed him how to add prices by hand. The boy left proudly carrying his own neat list.

同時，店裡的太陽能計算機故障了。樹林取笑明軒離了電就無法工作，接著看著他修理接點。小機器本身不重要，卻反映出家人漸漸養成的新習慣：有人發現問題，有人接手檢查，誰都不必假裝無所不知。來訪孩子問，能不能搔一下計算機就讓數字改變。玉珍笑著遞紙，教他手算價錢。男孩得意地帶著整齊清單離開。

By the end of the week, the historian had approval for nineteen photographs but not for twelve others. He was disappointed; he had hoped for a larger opening. Meicen suggested that missing permissions could become part of the exhibit's lesson. A label could explain why a historical picture might be withheld even when it mattered. The historian thought about this and agreed. They planned a short talk on how archives make choices, instead of presenting the archive as a complete picture of the past. The studio would be paid for catalog work whether or not every image appeared in public.

到了週末，研究者取得十九張照片的公開許可，另外十二張沒有。他原本希望展覽規模更大，因此有些失望。美岑提議把缺少授權本身變成教育內容：標牌可以解釋，為什麼重要史料也可能不能公開。研究者想了想，答應安排一場講座，討論檔案如何做選擇，而不是把館藏當作完整歷史。無論每張照片是否展出，照相館的目錄整理工作都會照約付款。

That evening, Mingxuan received a message from a property agent who wanted to inspect the shop. He told Meicen that he had merely asked about prices, not accepted any offer. She thanked him for saying so before the visit. They agreed that a sale remained possible, but a visitor would not be allowed to examine customer records. Mingxuan wrote the rule on the appointment sheet himself. The second trial had begun with old codes and broken signals; it ended its first week with one new code of conduct that belonged to the family rather than to a machine.

傍晚，明軒收到房仲留言，希望到店看屋。他告訴美岑，自己只是詢價，沒有接受任何出價。美岑感謝他在對方到訪前先說明。兩人同意出售仍是可能的選項，但看屋者不能檢查顧客資料。明軒親自在預約單上寫下這條規則。第二輪試辦從舊密碼與壞訊號展開，第一週結束時，家人也有了自己的新行為準則。

The historian later brought a picture of a rare coastal dog breed kept by the former signal station. Its owner feared that publishing the image might invite an invasion of privacy at the family home. Meicen offered to negotiate a caption that named the station without giving a residential address. The owner accepted after reading the proof. The family was paid for the additional research, not for pressuring anyone into surrendering personal details. Even the smallest photograph needed a clear boundary between public history and private life.

研究者後來帶來一張海岸訊號站飼養稀有犬種的照片。飼主擔心公開後有人侵入家中隱私。美岑提出協商一段只寫訊號站、不透露住家的說明。飼主看過校樣後同意。照相館收取額外查證工作的費用，而不是靠施壓取得私人資料。再小的一張照片，也要清楚畫出公共歷史與私人生活的界線。
