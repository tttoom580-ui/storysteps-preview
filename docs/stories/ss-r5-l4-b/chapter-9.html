寄錯的信封

The Wrong Envelope

1996，晨光照相館的第二張底片 · 第 9 / 25 章 · 3000–4000 · 英中對照 · 校訂完成

On a humid Tuesday, a customer returned with a photograph that belonged to someone else. Her voice had a sharp accent from another part of the island, and her frown showed she expected a fight. She had ordered a picture of her grandmother in their homeland village, but the envelope held a stranger's wedding portrait. Meicen asked her not to leave the picture on the open counter. A clumsy assistant had placed the wrong attachment with the receipt while the telephone rang. The circumstance was ordinary; the privacy error was not. The customer requested a refund and a full explanation.

潮濕的星期二，一名顧客帶著不屬於自己的照片回店。她有臺灣另一地區的明顯口音，皺著眉，看起來已準備爭執。她原本訂的是祖母故鄉村落的照片，信封裡卻是陌生人的婚照。一位不夠熟練的助理在電話響起時，把錯誤附件與收據放在一起。這種情況很平常，侵犯隱私卻不能因此忽略。顧客要求退款與完整說明。

Meicen apologized without trying to mislead the visitor about the cause. She offered to recover the misplaced portrait, check the remaining orders, and notify its owner if necessary. Mingxuan wanted to explain that the assistant was new and had been interrupted, but Meicen said an interruption was a risk to plan for, not an excuse. The customer listened through a small earphone connected to a portable radio while Meicen wrote the incident report. When the visitor removed the earphone, she asked one question: "How can I be sure my grandmother's picture has not gone to another family?"

美岑道歉，沒有用話術誤導客人。她願意收回錯寄婚照、檢查其餘訂單，必要時通知照片主人。明軒想解釋助理還是新人，而且中途被打斷；美岑卻說，工作中斷是應預先管理的風險，不是藉口。客人戴著連接小收音機的耳機，聽美岑寫事件紀錄。她摘下耳機後只問一件事：「我怎麼知道祖母的照片沒有送到別人家？」

They checked every order processed that morning. The grandmother's picture had remained in its sleeve, because the wrong wedding portrait had been selected from a nearby tray before the final packing step. Meicen invited the customer to peep at the labeled storage boxes but not at other clients' images. She demonstrated the new two-person check that would be used from then on. The visitor asked for the correct photograph and accepted a partial refund for the delay. She did not smile, but her voice grew less cold as Meicen explained the sequence without hiding any step.

他們核對當日上午的全部訂單。祖母照片仍在護套裡，因為包裝前，有人從旁邊托盤誤拿了婚照。美岑請客人查看貼標籤的存放盒，但不讓她看到其他顧客影像；接著示範今後採用的雙人核對。客人領回正確照片，也接受因延誤而退還部分費用。她沒有笑，但聽見美岑毫不隱瞞流程，語氣漸漸緩和。

After she left, Mingxuan studied the economic cost of the incident. The lost income was small, but the time spent checking every envelope was not. The assistant offered to pay for the mistake from his wages. Meicen refused; he had followed the studio's old packing procedure, which placed two orders too close together. "We need responsibility, not a convenient person to blame," she said. Her own modesty did not stop her from admitting that she had approved that layout. Shulin leaned against the counter and agreed to separate completed orders from unfinished work.

顧客離去後，明軒計算這次失誤的經濟成本。退掉的錢不多，重查每封信的工時卻不少。助理提出從薪水扣款，美岑拒絕，因為他遵循的是照相館原有的包裝流程，而流程本就把兩筆訂單放得太近。「我們需要負責，不需要找個方便責怪的人。」她說。她也坦承自己批准過那個桌面配置。樹林靠在櫃檯旁，同意以後將完成品和未完成品分區放置。

That afternoon, a local science teacher came for prints of a nuclear energy exhibit. He listened to the story and said that small failures could reveal weaknesses in a larger system. Meicen laughed that a photograph envelope was hardly a nuclear reactor. Yet the comparison helped her think about warning signs, handoffs, and independent checks. The teacher recommended a revolutionary idea for a tiny shop: make a public service promise in plain language, then measure whether the staff actually kept it. Mingxuan raised an eyebrow and gave a playful wink. "Now even the scientists want to run our counter."

下午，一位理科教師來取核能展覽照片，聽過事件後說，小失誤有時能揭露大系統的弱點。美岑笑說，照片信封終究不是核子反應爐，但這個比喻幫她重新思考警示、交接與獨立覆核。教師提出對小店而言頗有革命性的想法：用簡單語言公開服務承諾，再實際計量員工是否做得到。明軒挑眉眨眼：「現在連科學家也想管我們櫃檯了。」

The customer telephoned again on Thursday. She had received the correct picture and said the family appreciated the repair, although her grandmother would have preferred the old scratches to remain visible. Meicen offered a second copy that preserved them. Before hanging up, the woman said, "My grandmother does not want to look younger. She wants to look like herself." Meicen wrote the sentence beneath the incident report. A studio that could correct a mistake honestly might deserve another chance, but trust would have to be earned through many ordinary envelopes after this one.

星期四，那位顧客又打來。她已收到正確照片，家人感謝修復，但祖母其實更希望保留舊刮痕。美岑提出再製作一張保留痕跡的版本。掛電話前，女人說：「我阿嬤不想看起來更年輕，只想看起來像自己。」美岑把這句話寫在事件報告下方。願意誠實改錯的照相館或許值得第二次機會，但信任仍得靠接下來無數普通信封慢慢累積。

During the review, Shulin found a half-finished order and asked the assistant to read its content aloud without mentioning the customer name. He told him, "First, instruct the next worker to check the label", not the photograph itself. Someone entered carrying crunchy rice crackers and nearly put the bag beside a row of envelopes. Meicen asked everyone to lean away from the counter while eating, then closed the packing tray. Small habits were easy to ignore when the shop was calm; those were exactly the habits that mattered when the next telephone rang.

覆核時，樹林找到一張未完成的訂單，要助理讀出訂單內容，但別念出顧客姓名。他指示助理核對標籤，而不是偷看相片。有人拿著酥脆米餅進來，差點把袋子放到信封旁。美岑請大家吃東西時別靠著包裝櫃，並先關上托盤。店裡平靜時，這些小習慣很容易忽略；下次電話響起時，它們恰恰最重要。
