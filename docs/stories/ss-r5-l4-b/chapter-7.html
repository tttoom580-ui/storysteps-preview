The Numbers Beneath the Photograph

1996，晨光照相館的第二張底片 · 第 7 / 25 章 · 3000–4000 · 英中對照 · 校訂中

用互動版查詞、朗讀與收藏

The following Saturday, an accountant named Mrs. He came to inspect the trial ledger. Meicen expected criticism, but the visitor's first response was admiration for the neatly labeled receipts. Then she discovered that several hours of family labor had not been recorded at all. "Good intentions cannot defeat missing numbers," Mrs. He said. "If your mother works for free, this shop appears healthier than it is." Mingxuan felt annoyed, yet he listened. The accountant assigned an ordinary wage to each task, including cleaning the basin, mixing developing chemistry, answering calls, and meeting clients.

下一個星期六，何會計師來檢查試辦帳簿。美岑原本預期挨批，沒想到對方先稱讚收據標示整齊；接著就發現好幾個小時的家庭勞動完全沒入帳。「好意抵不過漏掉的數字，」何會計師說，「如果母親免費工作，店面看起來就會比實際健康。」明軒有點不高興，還是聽下去。會計師替每項工作估算一般工資，包括清洗水盆、調配沖印藥水、接電話與接待客人。

An academic researcher from the marine college happened to visit that morning. She needed photographs of a new aquarium exhibit, where pupils would learn why different fish required different water conditions. Meicen worried that shooting through glass would create reflections. The researcher explained a characteristic of the tanks: one side was deliberately shaded to reduce stress on the animals. She offered to let Meicen photograph during the quiet hour before opening. It was a fortunate chance to test a new service, but the studio would still need a written price and permission to use the images.

當日上午，一位海洋學院研究者剛好來訪，想委託拍攝新水族展。學生將在那裡學習不同魚類需要不同水質。美岑擔心隔著玻璃拍攝會反光。研究者解釋，魚缸有個特點：一側故意遮陰，降低魚類壓力。她讓美岑在開館前安靜時段拍攝。這是難得的測試機會，但照相館仍要列明價錢及影像使用權。

At the aquarium, Meicen met a bilingual guide who spoke fluent English and Taiwanese. His calm rhythm helped a group of visiting students understand the rules. A boy repeatedly tapped the glass, so the guide gently explained why noise could disturb the fish. Meicen waited until the room became quiet before raising her camera. The strongest image was not of the largest animal. It showed a tiny fish emerging from shade while a child watched without touching. The guide said it captured the exhibit's prime lesson: observation works best when the observer knows how to wait.

在水族館，美岑遇見一位英語與臺語都很流利的導覽員。他平穩的說話節奏讓來訪學生理解規則。一個男孩不停敲玻璃，導覽員溫和說明噪音會干擾魚。美岑等大家安靜才舉起相機。最好的畫面不是最大的魚，而是一尾小魚從陰影游出，一名孩子靜靜觀看而沒有觸碰。導覽員說，這拍出了展覽最重要的課：懂得等待，觀察才有效。

The college asked for a possible expansion of the project to include the harbor's old fishing records. Mrs. He, who had accompanied Meicen, warned against accepting too much work before they knew their capacity. She proposed a contribution margin for each service, separating direct costs from rent and household bills. Meicen asked the researcher for a schedule instead of promising an immediate answer. The visitor respected that decision and left with a list of relevant sample pictures. On the way home, Meicen felt that boundaries could motivate clients to trust a small shop, not drive them away.

學院詢問能否擴大合作，把港口舊漁業紀錄也納入。陪同前來的何會計師提醒，還沒算清產能，不要接太多案子。她建議逐項計算邊際貢獻，把直接成本與房租、家計分開。美岑先向研究者要時程，沒立刻答應。對方尊重這個決定，帶著相關樣張清單離開。回家途中，美岑愈發相信，清楚界線能促進顧客信任，不一定把客人趕走。

That evening, the family compared their old books with the trial figures. The shop had not reached profit, but the losses were smaller than Mingxuan expected when wages were counted honestly. Shulin tried to claim they should simply work harder. Mrs. He asked whether he expected his wife to strive without sleep at sixty. He was silent. Yuzhen said that the shop should be something people could work in without losing their health. "Then we must change what success means," Meicen said. "A busy counter is not enough if everyone behind it is exhausted."

當晚，一家人把舊帳本和試辦數字並排。店還沒賺錢，但在如實計算工資後，虧損比明軒原先預期小。樹林說大家只要更努力就好。何會計師問，難道他希望六十歲的妻子不睡覺也一直拚？他沉默了。玉珍說，這家店應該是人能健康工作下去的地方。「那就要改變成功的定義，」美岑說，「櫃檯再忙，若裡頭人人精疲力盡，也不算成功。」

Early the next morning, Meicen woke to find her father already at the counter, but he was not developing film. He had prepared a clean sheet listing each family member's hours. He admitted that her questions had finally wakened him to the cost of treating relatives as an endless supply of help. Together they wrote a weekly limit for night work and agreed to review it after the next school assignment. Meicen placed the aquarium photograph above the list. The little fish hovered in the shade, a reminder that careful movement could still be progress.

隔天一早，美岑醒來時父親已在櫃檯，但他沒有沖片，而是準備好一張乾淨表格，列出每個家人的工時。他承認，女兒的提問終於喚醒自己：把親人當成用不完的人力，原來也是一種成本。大家一起訂下每週夜間工作的上限，約好下次學校委託後再檢討。美岑把水族館照片貼在表格上方。小魚停在陰影裡，提醒她細緻而緩慢的移動，仍然是進步。

The accountant returned once more to explain the wage column. "These numbers may annoy you at first," she told Shulin, "but they protect both your business and your family." He said he was beginning to waken to the difference between a gift and an obligation. If someone volunteered an evening, they should know they were volunteering. If the work was needed every week, it belonged in the budget. Meicen added those two rules to the family agreement.

會計師又回來解釋工資欄。「這些數字剛開始可能惹您不高興，」她對樹林說，「卻能同時保護生意與家人。」樹林說，自己漸漸明白贈與與義務的差別。有人自願幫忙一晚，就該知道那是自願；若每週都需要那份工作，就必須列入預算。美岑把兩條規則寫進家庭協議。
