Algebra of a Missing Hour

In a quiet classroom, Hera spread three series of figures across the floor: the hill dial, the lighthouse clock, and the harbor water gauge. The apprentice had called the mystery seventeen minutes, but the underlying error was not identical in every record. Some figures had been rounded, and some were written after an instrument had stopped. Veyra insisted that the reconstruction distinguish direct observations from interpolated intervals. They used simple algebra to calculate a range in which the gate bell must have been advanced. One number might satisfy a story; a defensible interval satisfied the evidence. The apprentice did not ask for a heroic speech. That practical question changed the discussion. The students used algebra to test whether missing entries followed a rule. The teacher explained that present and absent were antonym terms in the logbook. The committee agreed to conserve fragile cards instead of replacing them. Witnesses could request a discreet interview outside the crowded hall. The nurse checked the wound dressing before allowing the child to travel.

安靜教室裡，赫拉把三組資料攤在地上：山站日影盤、燈塔鐘與港口水位計。學徒稱它為十七分鐘之謎，但各份資料的原始誤差並不完全一樣。有些數字經四捨五入，有些則在儀器停擺後才寫下。薇拉堅持重建時區分直接觀測與插值時段。他們用簡單代數推算水閘鐘可能被撥快的區間。單一數字可以滿足故事，能自我辯護的範圍才能滿足證據。這個務實問題改變了討論。 學生用代數測試消失條目是否符合某種規則。 老師解釋觀測紀錄簿裡的「在場」與「缺席」是反義詞。 委員會同意保存脆弱卡片，而不是直接換掉。 證人可以要求在人群之外進行審慎低調的訪談。 護士檢查傷口敷料後才允許孩子出發。

Jory placed the mechanical inspection beside the calculations. A scratched gear could support an abrupt change, but wear might also affect its exact size. When they included that uncertainty, the reconstructed adjustment centered on seventeen minutes without proving the operator's identity. Silas proposed printing a clean figure anyway so the public would not be confused. Anwen objected. Veyra chose to publish the central estimate together with the range and the reasons for it. She knew such caution might be mocked as hesitation, but confidence without limits had helped produce the danger. Those protections mattered. Dusk fell over the school as the comparison work continued. An old encyclopedia included a map with Children's Bend marked clearly. The lost signatures continued to haunt families who could not claim wages. Official indifference had made small clerical errors costly for years. The council asked for a public inventory of stored crew cards.

喬里把機械檢查結果放在計算旁。刮傷齒輪可支持突然調動的判斷，但磨損也影響精確幅度。把這項不確定納入後，重建結果仍以十七分鐘為中心，卻不能指認操作者。賽拉斯建議印出單一乾淨數字，免得民眾困惑。安溫反對，薇拉選擇連同範圍與理由公布。她知道謹慎可能被譏笑為猶豫，但毫無界線的自信曾經促成危險。 比對工作持續進行時，暮色降臨學校。 一本舊百科全書裡的地圖清楚標示孩子灣。 消失的簽名持續困擾無法領工資的家庭。 官方的冷漠讓小小文書錯誤多年來代價高昂。 議會要求公布庫存船員戶籍卡清單。

A clerk brought an anonymous note accusing the lighthouse keeper of selling data. The note named no transaction, date, or witness. Veyra refused to place the accusation beside verified measurements as if both carried equal weight. Instead the team looked for a documented payment. They found ordinary wages, a repair allowance, and no unexplained transfer within the period being examined. That could not prove the keeper innocent of every conceivable act, but it gave the court no basis for treating the rumor as a finding. The anonymous note was preserved separately as an unverified allegation. The sealed copies travelled by different routes. A mother sang a lullaby to calm children waiting for new documents. An outgoing student offered to explain the method at the council meeting. The volunteers began to outnumber the clerks assigned to the project. Veyra studied the outward appearance of the seal before checking its contents. She asked guards to restrain themselves from questioning children without counsel.

書記帶來匿名紙條，指控燈塔守護人販賣資料。紙條沒有交易、日期或證人。薇拉拒絕把它和已查證測量並列成同等證據，團隊改找可追溯付款，結果只有正常薪資與維修津貼，查驗期間沒有不明轉帳。這不能證明守護人絕不可能犯任何事，卻不足以讓法院把流言當事實。匿名紙條另存為未核實指控。 母親唱搖籃曲，安撫等候新文件的孩子。 一名外向的學生自願在議會解釋方法。 參與計畫的志工人數開始超過被派來的書記。 薇拉先看印章外觀，再查驗內文。 她要求衛兵克制，不要在無人陪同時質問孩子。

By evening the team produced a timeline with every confirmed reading and every blank openly displayed. The most important surprise was a short interval during which the central clock and the private contractor's ledger had matching altered values, while the distant stations retained their old times. Anwen circled the interval without claiming its cause. Veyra felt the novel's final courtroom approaching, but she also saw how different the evidence had become. The next question was no longer whether someone had edited a number; it was who had authorized the edit and why. No convenient apology would be allowed to replace either obligation. A sportswoman donated notebooks but did not request access to private names. The teacher said sportsmanship meant respecting a result even after losing. A suitcase of old attendance books arrived under a signed receipt. The students made a tentative estimate and clearly labeled it uncertain. The identity restoration review was already underway before midnight.

傍晚團隊完成時間線，列明每筆已確認讀數與所有空白。最大驚訝是某個短時段裡，中央鐘與私人承包商帳目同時出現相同的改後數值，遠方站點卻保留舊時間。安溫圈出時段，沒有臆測原因。薇拉感到原著的最後審判愈來愈近，也看見證據已與小說完全不同。下一個問題不再是有沒有人改數字，而是誰授權、為何而改。 一名女運動員捐贈筆記本，卻不要求查閱私人姓名。 老師說運動家精神是即使落敗也尊重結果。 一只裝著舊點名簿的手提箱在簽收後送達。 學生提出暫定估計，清楚標為尚有不確定性。 身分恢復複查在午夜前已經展開。
