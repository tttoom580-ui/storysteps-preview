The First Ten Corrections

Ten disputed entries were placed side by side in the council hall. Some contained ordinary transcription mistakes, some involved mechanical drift, and three required further investigation because their signed originals had been replaced. Veyra refused to label the entire almanac fraudulent. Such a claim would be easier to shout and harder to prove. Each correction listed its source, uncertainty, effective date, and the route by which an affected captain could challenge it. Jory and Hera signed their own sections. Veyra signed only the record of the inquiry, not the certification of her disputed former work. Outside, the city continued its ordinary life. Veyra could not give certainty simply because uncertainty frightened them. The company offered a concession on wages but refused to return all documents. A cynical official claimed that ordinary residents could not understand records. The software's default setting could hide seasonal addresses from the summary. Winter shortages threatened to deplete the town's emergency stores. The fragrance of bread rose from the shelter kitchen before the hearing.

議會大廳並列十個有爭議的條目。有些只是普通抄寫錯誤，有些出自鐘的機械漂移，另有三項因簽名原件被替換而須深入調查。薇拉拒絕把整本星曆都喊成造假，那樣容易高呼，卻難證明。每項更正列出來源、誤差、施行日期及船長申訴途徑。喬里與赫拉分別簽自己負責的部分；薇拉只簽調查紀錄，不再核定有爭議的舊工作。 窗外的城市依舊過著日常生活。 公司在工資上提出讓步，卻拒絕歸還所有文件。 一名憤世嫉俗的官員聲稱一般居民看不懂紀錄。 軟體的預設設定會在摘要中隱藏季節住址。 冬季短缺可能耗盡鎮上的緊急儲備。 聽證前，收容所廚房飄出麵包香氣。

Captain Nolin appeared carrying his crew's accounts. Two delayed departures had cost wages and spoiled a portion of the cargo, but the revised window had also allowed the next ship to pass safely. He demanded that the council recognize both facts. Veyra welcomed the demand and asked finance officers to separate ordinary market losses from damages caused by official negligence. Compensation would require evidence, not a sentimental speech. The minister accused her of courting public approval. Nolin replied that he had never liked her, but he trusted a timetable he could challenge. She was right. Veyra would not intrude on private family meetings without consent. A former jockey recognized a horse used on the eastern route. The clerk's literal reading of the code ignored the explanation beside it. The restoration improved morale among volunteers who had worked for weeks. The magistrate could prosecute fraud only after individual responsibility was established.

諾林船長帶著船員帳目到場。兩次延航使工人少拿薪水，也壞了部分貨物，但修訂時段確實讓下一艘船安全通過。他要求議會承認兩件事。薇拉支持，請財務官區分一般市場損失與官方疏忽造成的損害。補償需要證據，不是感人演說。大臣指責她討好群眾，諾林說自己從不喜歡她，但至少信得過一份能提出異議的時間表。 未經同意，薇拉不會闖入家庭的私人會議。 一名退役賽馬騎師認出東路使用的一匹馬。 書記只按字面解讀代碼，忽略旁邊的說明。 身分恢復讓工作數週的志工士氣提升。 法官必須先確立個人責任，才能追訴詐欺。

Anwen read aloud the three entries still lacking signed originals. One concerned the northern gate, another the western lighthouse, and the last a private vessel that had entered the royal dockyard without inspection. A journalist asked whether those three incidents proved a conspiracy. Anwen answered that the shared pattern deserved investigation but did not establish a single author. Her careful reply earned fewer cheers than a dramatic accusation would have. Veyra considered that a sign of progress: the apprentice was learning to value precision over applause. Both questions belonged at the hearing. A shipment marked radioactive was quarantined until a safety inspector checked it. The minister tried to retort, but the numbered log contradicted his statement. A screwdriver lay beside the opened logbook cabinet. The kitchen kept one serving for each worker returning from inspection. The observatory quay's splendor could not conceal the poverty of its workers.

安溫朗讀三個仍缺簽名原件的條目，分別涉及北方水閘、西方燈塔與一艘未經檢查就進入皇家船塢的私人船。記者問，三件事是否足以證明陰謀。安溫回答，共同模式值得調查，卻不能證明同一個人所為。她的謹慎回答沒有戲劇化指控那麼多掌聲，薇拉反而認為是進步：學徒正在學會重視精確，多於喝采。 一批標記具放射性的貨物被隔離，等待安全稽查員檢查。 部長想反駁，編號紀錄卻與他的陳述矛盾。 一把螺絲起子放在打開的登記櫃旁。 廚房為每名檢查歸來的工人留了一份餐食。 港口的壯麗掩蓋不了工人的貧困。

At dusk the council published the revised ten entries and extended the independent inquiry. The minister responded with a petition demanding Veyra's removal for her earlier negligent signature. She did not tear up the accusation. In the novel, a similar paper had been the first step toward an execution. This time she placed it beside her own account and requested a separate hearing. Correcting a table did not undo the old harm, but the old harm did not justify allowing new errors to continue. Both propositions could be true. The announcement described both the rescue and its limits. A spontaneous cheer rose when the first cards were handed back. Elsi's stepfather signed as a witness after confirming his own identity. The nervous clerk began to stutter while reading the deletion log. The council voted to subsidize transport for families without wages. A synthetic rope replaced the damaged line at the survey boat landing.

黃昏議會發布十項修訂並延長獨立調查。大臣隨即請願，以薇拉過去疏忽簽名為由要求免職。她沒有撕毀指控。小說裡類似文件正是走向處決的第一步。這次她把它放在自己陳述旁，申請另案聽證。修正表格無法抹去舊傷害，但舊傷害也不能成為繼續放任新錯誤的理由。兩件事可以同時成立。 公告同時交代救援成果與侷限。 第一批卡片發還時，人群自發歡呼。 艾爾西的繼父確認自身身分後，以證人身分簽名。 緊張的書記朗讀刪除紀錄時開始結巴。 議會表決補助沒有工資家庭的交通費。 碼頭通道受損纜繩換成了合成纖維繩。
