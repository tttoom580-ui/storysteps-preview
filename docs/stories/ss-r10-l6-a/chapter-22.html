大臣的承認

The Minister's Admission

Under oath, Navigation Minister Halden admitted that he had pressured staff to reduce delays for expensive shipments. He called the instruction an administrative reform, not an order to falsify measurements. The court accepted the admission as evidence of pressure but did not treat it as proof that he had personally touched the clock. His former secretary produced a memorandum ordering selected gate records to follow the palace time. The minister said the secretary had misunderstood. The judge asked for the original dispatch chain rather than choosing whichever version sounded more persuasive. Veyra knew how tempting a clean story could be. A solemn anthem was played outside, but the hearing remained open to criticism. Visitors had to ascend two staircases to reach the public gallery. An independent audit compared the island accounts with treasury receipts. Veyra rejected the term barbarian for people whose customs differed from hers. The court identified a defect in the old address approval procedure.

航務大臣哈爾登宣誓後承認，曾施壓員工縮短昂貴貨運的等待時間。他稱此舉是行政改革，不是命人偽造測量。法院把承認視為施壓證據，卻未認定他親手碰鐘。前祕書出示備忘錄，要求部分水閘紀錄依王宮時間處理。大臣聲稱祕書誤解，法官要求出示完整發文鏈，而不是選聽起來較有說服力的版本。薇拉知道一個乾淨整齊的故事多麼誘人。 外頭奏起莊嚴頌歌，聽證卻仍可接受批評。 訪客必須登上兩段樓梯才能抵達旁聽席。 獨立稽核把島上帳目與國庫收據比較。 薇拉拒絕用野蠻人稱呼習俗不同的人。 法院確認舊地址核准程序存在缺陷。

Silas traced the memorandum through three offices. The first received it as a draft, the second stamped it for circulation, and the third changed one phrase from 'compare against' to 'use in place of.' The alteration made a technical cross-check into an instruction to replace independent readings. A clerk acknowledged inserting the phrase after a private meeting with the minister's chief aide. He had kept no recording of the meeting. The prosecution would need to test his recollection against visitors' logs, handwriting, and the sequence of the original paper copies. His first drafts had once been neat enough to conceal confusion. A clerk could grumble about paperwork without refusing lawful duties. Heavy hail delayed several witnesses traveling from the mountains. The clinic provided healthful meals to children waiting for testimony reviews. A tall hedge separated the court garden from the public road. The pharmacist kept strict records of heroin used under hospital control.

賽拉斯追查備忘錄經過三個辦公室。第一處當草稿收件，第二處蓋章傳閱，第三處把一句「與之比較」改成「以之取代」，使技術核對變成替換獨立讀數的命令。一名書記承認，在與大臣首席助理私下開會後插入詞句，卻沒有會議錄音。控方仍需以訪客登記、筆跡與紙本先後順序檢驗他的記憶。他把廢棄計算放入信封，而不是燒掉。 書記可以抱怨文書工作，但不能拒絕合法職責。 大冰雹延誤了幾名山地證人的行程。 診所為等候證述審查的孩子提供健康餐食。 一道高樹籬將法院花園與公共道路分開。 藥師嚴格記錄醫院管制使用的海洛因。

Anwen disliked the clerk's trembling voice and asked whether fear could make his statement unreliable. Hera explained that fear mattered, but it did not automatically make a witness either honest or dishonest. The judge permitted questions about employment threats and possible benefits. The clerk acknowledged that he hoped to avoid dismissal, yet signed a statement identifying the amendment in his own hand. When the original page was displayed, the added words sat awkwardly between two lines. The minister's counsel requested further testing, and the court granted it. Veyra separated them aloud. Veyra said intimacy between friends never excused hidden financial favors. The mainland court received documents from island and mountain offices. Witnesses could mingle in the public hall but not discuss sealed evidence. The pharmacist also confirmed which medicines the depot had requested. A polar wind rattled the high windows during the morning session.

安溫看著書記顫抖，擔心恐懼會使證詞不可靠。赫拉解釋，恐懼重要，卻不能自動使證人變誠實或不誠實。法官准許追問失業威脅與可能利益。書記承認希望保住工作，但仍簽下陳述，指出自己親筆加上的詞。原件展示時，新增字句擠在兩行中間。大臣律師要求進一步鑑定，法院准許。 薇拉說朋友間的親密不能為暗中金錢利益開脫。 本土法院收到島上與山地辦公室的文件。 證人可在公共大廳交流，但不得討論封存證據。 藥師也確認轉運站曾申請哪些藥物。 極地般的寒風在早晨庭期吹得高窗作響。

By evening the evidence linked the pressure for faster shipping to the altered instruction, but the minister continued to deny authorizing a change to the clock itself. Veyra resisted the temptation to call the case closed. She wanted the facts strong enough to survive an appeal, even if that meant allowing a person she distrusted to challenge them. The next witness would be Jory, whose physical examination might answer the question that the documents still left open. Outside, rain began tapping against the bronze courthouse bell. Now their names were returned to the page. Families could reside temporarily in Northbank during the appeals process. The new records paper was resistant to ordinary water damage. Elsi's stepmother testified about the child's original travel arrangements. The accountant explained that even a trillion coins would not replace consent. The witness sat upright and answered only questions he understood.

傍晚證據已把加速航運的壓力與修改後的指令連在一起，大臣仍否認授權改鐘。薇拉忍住宣布結案的衝動。她希望事實足以通過上訴檢驗，即使必須讓自己不信任的人質疑。下一位證人是喬里，他的物理檢查或許能回答文件尚未解開的問題。法院外，雨滴敲打青銅鐘。如今他們的名字重新回到頁面。 申訴期間，家庭可以暫住北岸。 新紀錄紙能抵抗一般水損。 艾爾西的繼母就孩子原先的旅行安排作證。 會計解釋，就算有一兆枚錢幣也不能取代同意。 證人坐直身體，只回答自己聽懂的問題。
