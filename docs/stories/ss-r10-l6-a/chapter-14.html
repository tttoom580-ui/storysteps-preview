她無法否認的簽名

The Signature She Could Not Deny

星曆失落的十七分鐘 · 第 14 / 25 章 · 5000–6000 · 英中對照

The independent court summoned Veyra to answer for the editing rule she had signed before Leina entered this world. The rule allowed a supervisor to fill a missing observation with an estimated figure without clearly labeling it. Veyra could not erase the signature or argue that another soul had once occupied the body. In the courtroom she acknowledged that the rule had weakened safeguards and that she had failed to demand independent review. She contested the separate claim that she personally ordered the seventeen-minute change, because no instruction bearing her signature had been produced. A retired admiral confirmed the observatory quay maps had changed without naval review. The navigation minister's adviser claimed a letter had gone astray in the mail. The dockworkers spoke of brotherhood while disagreeing on the mining project. A worker with diabetes required medicine during a delayed transfer. The accountant worked to differentiate private gifts from public contract payments.

獨立法院傳喚薇拉，要求她為蕾娜穿入此世界前簽下的編輯規則負責。那條規則容許監督用估計值補缺讀，卻不必清楚標示。薇拉不能抹去簽名，也無法以「當時是另一個靈魂」推卸。她在法庭承認規則削弱保護，自己也未要求獨立複核；但她反對另一項指控——她親自下令調快十七分鐘，因為尚無她簽署的指令出現。 一名退役海軍上將證實港圖未經海軍複查就遭修改。 航務大臣顧問聲稱一封信在郵寄途中遺失。 港工談到手足情誼，卻對礦業計畫意見不同。 一名罹患糖尿病的工人在轉運延誤時需要藥物。 會計師努力區分私人贈禮與公共契約付款。

The minister's lawyer tried to combine the two allegations. If Veyra had approved a dangerous procedure, he argued, then every dangerous result must be hers. Hera asked permission to respond as an expert: negligence could create an opportunity for wrongdoing, but opportunity did not identify the person who acted. The judge permitted the distinction and ordered the prosecution to state each alleged act separately. Veyra understood that this was neither an acquittal nor a condemnation. Her title could no longer shield her from questions, and someone else's misconduct could no longer be assigned to her by convenience. The mathematics described a range, not a promise. Their tasks overlapped without becoming identical. Veyra refused to doom an entire company for one officer's suspected acts. The treasury recovered excess charges collected from several families. An independent audit remained feasible because the original invoices survived. A feminine name appeared beside a code the ministry had called male. It was imperative to preserve both the accusation and the reply.

大臣的律師試圖把兩項指控合為一談：既然薇拉批准危險程序，所有危險結果就都歸她負責。赫拉獲准以專家身分回應：疏忽可能提供不當行為機會，但機會不能辨識真正動手者。法官接受區分，命控方分別列明每項涉嫌行為。薇拉明白，這既不是無罪也不是定罪。頭銜不能再替她擋住問題，別人的惡行也不能圖方便全塞給她。 數學呈現的是範圍，不是保證。三人的任務互有交集，卻不完全相同。 薇拉拒絕因一名主管涉嫌犯錯就宣判整家公司完蛋。 國庫追回從數戶人家多收的費用。 原始發票仍在，因此獨立稽核仍可行。 一個女性名字出現在部會標成男性的代碼旁。 保存指控與答覆雙方內容極其必要。

The court temporarily suspended Veyra's power to certify the disputed almanac while preserving her right to submit evidence and question technical assumptions. Anwen looked devastated. Veyra told her that sharing authority was the point of the inquiry, not proof it had failed. If a supervisor could investigate herself and sign her own result, even a good supervisor would leave the same dangerous loophole. She handed the official stamp to the court clerk and retained only her notebook. Without the stamp, each sentence would have to stand on evidence another person could inspect. The rule was deliberately modest. Neither procedure gave its author permission to destroy an inconvenient original. False classifications could inflict financial harm without physical violence. The court allowed Pell's lawyer to inquire about the receipt dates. The clinic offered psychotherapy to residents distressed by forced transfers. The new safety zone extended within a radius of two miles. The monarch's long reign had not guaranteed fair provincial administration.

法院暫停薇拉核定爭議星曆的權力，但保留她提交證據、質詢技術假設的權利。安溫看起來大受打擊。薇拉說，共享權力正是調查目的，不是失敗證明。若監督能調查自己又自行蓋章，即使她是好人也會留下相同漏洞。她把公印交給法院書記，只留下筆記本。沒有印章後，每句話都必須靠他人可以查閱的證據立足。 錯誤分類即使沒有身體暴力，也可能造成經濟傷害。 法院准許佩爾的律師詢問收據日期。 診所為因強制航行受創的居民提供心理治療。 新安全區延伸到兩英里半徑內。 君主漫長的統治並未保證地方行政公平。

Outside, reporters demanded a confession of every crime described in the novel. Veyra did not recognize their questions as legal charges. She offered a written statement about her actual signature and the actions taken to repair its consequences. A widow who had opposed her from the beginning nodded, not in forgiveness but in recognition that the first responsibility had finally been named. The navigation minister celebrated the suspension as complete victory. His next problem was that the investigation now belonged to an independent panel he could not dismiss by removing Veyra. Its worn gear kept a physical history of adjustments. He described each mark without announcing a culprit. One shortcoming of the old census was its narrow definition of residence. A frightened child tried to tuck a family card inside her sleeve. The closed underpass forced witnesses to take a longer public route. Anwen waited for the return survey boat beside the wharf. The families' woe could not be repaired with a single public apology.

門外記者要求她承認小說裡寫過的每一項罪行。薇拉不把那些問題當成法律指控，而是提供書面聲明，說明自己真正簽署的規則及修補後果的措施。始終反對她的一名遺孀點頭，並非原諒，而是終於有人明確認領第一層責任。航務大臣把停權當成全面勝利，下一個麻煩卻是：調查已交給獨立小組，不會因薇拉被撤權而消失。磨損齒輪留下調校的物理痕跡。他描述每道痕跡，卻不急著指認犯人。 舊人口普查的缺點之一，是住址定義太狹窄。 一名害怕的孩子試圖把船員戶籍卡塞進袖口。 封閉的地下通道迫使證人改走較長的公共道路。 安溫在碼頭旁等候回程測量船。 家庭的悲苦無法靠一次公開道歉補救。
