公開的星曆

An Almanac in Public

The council permitted a public comparison at the old harbor basin. Anwen and Hera read their instruments separately while Jory checked the gate clock. Merchants watched from one side of the quay, families from the other. The printed almanac marked an opening window that placed the strongest current too close to the channel's narrow turn. The revised window was later and less convenient, but the observers could explain how they had reached it. Veyra reminded the crowd that the demonstration tested a calculation, not the character of everyone who had worked on the original book. They arranged the evidence in chronological order and left visible gaps where a document was missing. Anwen objected when a clerk tried to fill one gap with an assumption. She was right. Even a plausible explanation would become dangerous once it was copied into an official register as if somebody had observed it directly. The naval academy had trained the inspector in document preservation. Veyra refused to turn an accusation into a judgment without a hearing. The navigation minister hosted a banquet while dock workers waited for wages. Farms upstream depended on fertilizer delivered through the observatory quay. The fishery had lost access to the shoreline mentioned in the lease.

議會准許在舊港池進行公開比對。安溫與赫拉分別讀取儀器，喬里檢查水閘鐘。商人站在碼頭一側，家屬站在另一側。印刷星曆的開閘時段，讓最強水流太接近航道狹窄轉角。修訂時段較晚、也較不方便，但觀測者說得出計算方式。薇拉提醒眾人，這場示範測的是數據，不是把每一個曾參與舊書工作的人都判為壞人。 他們按時間排列證據，缺少文件的地方明確留白。書記想用推測補上一處空缺，安溫立即反對。她是對的：即使解釋聽起來合理，一旦被抄進官方紀錄、看似有人親眼觀察，就可能帶來危險。 海軍學院曾訓練這名稽查員保存文件。 薇拉拒絕在聽證前把指控變成判決。 航務大臣舉辦宴會，港口工人卻還在等工資。 上游農場依賴港口運來的肥料。 漁業社群失去了租約提到的海岸使用權。

To test the procedure, the safety officer ordered an empty workboat through the gate during the revised interval. Its captain controlled the passage and could turn back at any time. The boat cleared the bend without scraping stone. Then the officer asked the team to calculate what would have happened at the earlier printed interval. They gave a risk range rather than staging a dangerous second voyage. The merchant representative protested that a model was not proof. Veyra agreed. It was precisely why the team combined the model with observed current and last winter's physical damage. The person whose livelihood depended on the gate had a different question from the person who calibrated it. Both questions belonged at the hearing. Veyra asked the dockworkers to specify lost shifts and the engineers to specify mechanical limits. A policy that answered only one group would fail once the weather changed. A worker's arm fracture appeared in the company's safety log. The loading harness was worn and should have been replaced months earlier. The court typist used headphones to review recorded statements. The camp recorded each worker's food intake but ignored unpaid overtime. A maiden voyage had been advertised for a vessel not yet inspected.

為了測試程序，安全官安排一艘空工作船在修訂時段通過水閘。船長全程掌控航行，隨時可以折返。船隻順利通過彎道，沒有刮到石壁。安全官接著請團隊計算原印刷時段可能的結果；他們提出風險範圍，沒有為了表演再冒險開第二趟。商人代表抗議模型不能算證明。薇拉同意，因此團隊才必須結合模型、實測水流及去年冬天的物理損害。 靠水閘維生的人，與校準水閘的人，提出的是不同問題；兩種問題都應出現在聽證會上。薇拉請碼頭工人列出失去的班次，請工程師標明機械極限。只回答其中一方的政策，天氣一變就會失敗。 一名工人手臂骨折的紀錄出現在公司安全簿裡。 裝卸吊帶磨損，幾個月前就該更換。 法院打字員戴耳機複查錄音證述。 營地記下每名工人的食物攝取，卻忽略未付加班費。 一艘尚未檢驗的船被宣傳即將首航。

The navigation minister offered a compromise: he would approve the safer schedule if Veyra withdrew the request to inspect the printing office. For a moment she felt the old instinct to exchange silence for a quick victory. But her next decision would determine whether the same manipulation could return. She refused the condition and asked the council to separate immediate safety rules from responsibility for missing documents. Even Nolin, whose crews had lost two profitable shifts, said the calculation should remain public so that captains could prepare their own routes. The announcement described both the rescue and its limits. One successful operation did not prove that every station was safe; one broken device did not prove that the entire warning network was worthless. Citizens received the raw readings, a corrected chart, and a date for the next independent inspection. An offshore platform was included in the land company's future plans. Prospective employees demanded to read the full contract before signing. The council proposed a referendum on public access to the coast. The accountant wanted to refine the chronology before presenting conclusions. Some families began to rejoice when the first wage records were recovered.

航務大臣提出交換：若薇拉撤回查閱印刷所的要求，他就同意較安全的時刻表。她一瞬間感到熟悉的衝動，想用沉默換取快速勝利。但下一個決定會左右同樣的操弄是否重演。她拒絕條件，要求議會將即時安全規則與文件遺失責任分開處理。連損失兩個賺錢班次的諾林都說，計算應公開，才能讓船長自行規劃航線。 公告同時交代救援成果與侷限。一次成功行動不能證明每座站都安全；一件設備損壞也不表示整套預警系統毫無價值。居民拿到了原始讀數、修正圖表，以及下次獨立檢查的日期。 土地公司的未來計畫還包含一座近海平台。 有意應徵的員工要求簽名前閱讀完整合約。 議會提議對海岸公共使用權舉行公民投票。 會計師想在提出結論前完善時間線。 第一批薪資紀錄尋回時，一些家庭開始歡欣。

After hours of debate, the council granted a ten-day independent review and temporary publication of the revised window. The observatory could advise but would not certify its own disputed data. That limit hurt Veyra's pride more than she expected, because the title had once been her entire protection. At dusk Anwen helped her remove the old gold stamp from the desk. They had prevented another unsafe departure, but the story's greater danger was still hidden in the paperwork that made the false time look ordinary. Outside, the city continued its ordinary life. Shopkeepers argued about deliveries, apprentices hurried across the square, and families wanted definite answers. Veyra could not give certainty simply because uncertainty frightened them. She could, however, explain what had been checked, what remained unknown, and who would make the next decision. Others could not hide resentment toward officials who had ignored them. The observatory quay looked serene while the court struggled with contradictory files. A traveling minister delivered a sermon about duty, then avoided questions. Workers refused to shuffle names between lists to satisfy quotas. A tenant showed Veyra a receipt proving that the land was occupied.

經過數小時辯論，議會核准十日獨立複查，並暫時公告修訂時段。天文台可以提供建議，卻不能自行核定有爭議的資料。這項限制比薇拉預想得更刺痛自尊，因為過去頭銜是她唯一的護身符。黃昏時安溫幫她收起桌上舊金印。他們阻止了另一次危險出航，但真正更大的危機，仍藏在那些讓錯誤時間顯得理所當然的文書裡。 窗外的城市依舊過著日常生活。店主爭論送貨，學徒匆匆穿過廣場，家庭盼望明確答案。薇拉不能因為人們害怕不確定，就隨口承諾肯定的結果；但她可以說清楚哪些事已查證、哪些仍未知、下一步由誰決定。 其他人無法掩飾對長期忽視他們的官員的怨懟。 法院苦於矛盾文件時，港口表面上十分寧靜。 一名巡迴牧師講完責任的布道，便迴避提問。 工人拒絕為了滿足配額而在名單間任意挪動姓名。 一名租戶向薇拉出示收據，證明土地有人使用。
