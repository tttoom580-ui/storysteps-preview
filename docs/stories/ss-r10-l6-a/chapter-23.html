鐘輪上的一道齒痕

The Tooth of the Clock

Jory placed a replica gear beside the original before the court. He showed how ordinary wear rounded the edges gradually, while a deliberate reset left a sharp mark under the locking tooth. The original bore both patterns. Its recent scratch aligned with the period in which the printed almanac lost seventeen minutes. This examination proved a physical adjustment but did not reveal who wielded the tool. Jory said so twice, to the irritation of spectators hoping for a confession. The judge asked the clerk to record his limitation as carefully as his conclusion. The sealed copies travelled by different routes. The supply office worked to avert a new shortage during property reviews. A caretaker stayed with children who did not wish to travel immediately. The physician found a dental cavity during a routine health check. The clerk learned to condense public statistics without hiding uncertainty. News of the disputed land transfers caused dismay among returning families.

喬里在法庭把仿製齒輪放在原件旁，示範正常磨損如何逐漸磨圓邊緣，而刻意重設會在鎖齒下留下尖銳痕跡。原齒輪兩種痕跡都有，較新的刮痕吻合星曆失去十七分鐘的時段。鑑定證明有人實際調動，卻不能告訴法庭誰握工具。喬里說了兩次，使期待犯人當場招認的旁聽者不耐。法官要求書記把侷限和結論記得同樣清楚。 物資辦公室努力避免產權複查期間再次發生短缺。 一名照顧者陪伴不想立刻出行的孩子。 醫師在例行健康檢查中發現一處蛀牙。 書記學會濃縮公開統計，同時不隱藏不確定性。 爭議土地轉移的消息使返鄉家庭沮喪。

Hera then produced the western lighthouse's sealed shadow and bell readings. The station had never received the ministry's correction sheet, so its clock remained independent of the altered central time. When the figures were aligned with the gate mechanism, they showed that the royal bell had been advanced by a human adjustment near the disputed interval. The calculation did not rely on the novel, on Veyra's memory, or on any witness's guess about hearing a distant bell. The defense mathematician checked the method and agreed with its range while disputing one minor rounding choice. No convenient apology would be allowed to replace either obligation. The office opened a separate desk for cases involving emigration records. A flaw in the old appeal form had prevented residents from choosing a name. The committee required inclusive language in notices for temporary housing. No official was permitted to intimidate a family during a property review. The court would decide who was liable for specific unlawful payments.

赫拉接著提出西方燈塔封存的日影與鐘聲讀數。該站從未收到部會更正紙，鐘因此獨立於遭改動的中央時間。與水閘機械數值對齊後，結果顯示王家鐘在爭議時段附近遭人為撥快。計算不依賴小說、薇拉記憶或某名證人猜測遠方鐘聲。辯方數學家驗算後同意範圍，只對一項小幅四捨五入提出異議。 辦公室另設櫃檯處理涉及移居國外紀錄的案件。 舊申訴表格的缺陷曾阻止居民自選姓名。 委員會要求臨時住房公告使用包容性語言。 產權複查時，任何官員都不得恐嚇家庭。 法院將裁定誰須為具體非法付款負責。

The final link came from workshop entry records. A contractor technician had entered the clock chamber carrying an adjustment key after receiving the altered ministry instruction. His signed work note described a 'temporary alignment' without specifying the old time. The technician admitted moving the mechanism but said he believed he was following a lawful repair order. The court separated his physical act from the minister's intention and the clerk's amended words. Each participant would be judged for what the evidence showed that person had known and done. The apprentice did not ask for a heroic speech. That practical question changed the discussion. The community held a small lottery for garden plots, separate from housing rights. A nearsighted clerk requested a larger copy of the original receipt. Elsi laughed when she noticed a pimple on her brother's nose. The reunion brought a revelation about another child still missing. The examiner kept each shred of damaged paper inside a labeled envelope.

最後一環來自工坊進出紀錄。承包商技師在收到修改後的部會指令後，帶調整鑰匙進入鐘房。他簽署的工作紙寫「暫時對時」，沒有記原時間。技師承認動過機械，卻稱自己以為是在執行合法維修令。法院把他的實際行為、大臣意圖與書記增添字句分開。每名參與者都必須按證據顯示的知情程度與行為受評判。這個務實問題改變了討論。 社區為菜圃舉行小型抽籤，與住房權無關。 一名近視書記要求放大原始收據。 艾爾西看見哥哥鼻上的青春痘，不禁笑了。 團聚帶來一項關於另一名失聯孩子的新發現。 鑑查員把每片紙屑放在標示清楚的信封裡。

Veyra left the chamber with no sensation of triumph. The missing seventeen minutes had finally acquired a physical explanation, but the discovery brought choices rather than an easy ending. The technician might be negligent without sharing the minister's purpose; the minister might be responsible without touching a single gear. Her own editing rule had made all their actions harder to detect. She asked the court to include a written account of that vulnerability in its final reasons. The judge agreed, then closed the evidentiary hearing. Those protections mattered. The panel made a solemn promise to publish the limits of its findings. A crowded waiting room could suffocate open discussion without private appointments. Claims began to surge after the remedy plan was posted. A flag moved upward as the town reopened the restored survey boat station. The workforce included residents who had never appeared in the official census.

薇拉走出法庭沒有勝利狂喜。消失的十七分鐘終於有了物理解釋，發現帶來的卻是抉擇，而非輕鬆結尾。技師可能疏忽，卻未必分享大臣目的；大臣即使不碰齒輪也可能須負責。她自己的編輯規則讓所有行動更難被察覺。她請法院在最終理由書記下這項制度漏洞，法官同意後宣布證據調查結束。 審判團鄭重承諾公布裁定的限制。 擁擠候診室若沒有私人約談，會壓抑公開討論。 補救計畫張貼後，申訴數量開始激增。 城鎮重新開放修復的測量船站時，旗幟向上升起。 勞動人口包含從未出現在官方普查裡的居民。
