有自己時間的山丘

The Hill That Kept Its Own Time

星曆失落的十七分鐘 · 第 2 / 25 章 · 5000–6000 · 英中對照

The hill station stood above the haze that covered the capital's harbor. Veyra climbed its narrow steps with Anwen and a guard who disliked the journey. The local observer, Hera, had kept a simple shadow dial beside the royal clock for twenty years. She had measured the same dawn on the disputed date, and her book differed from the printed almanac. A blank strip in the capital copy lay exactly where the two figures should have been reconciled. Veyra asked to photograph nothing and to copy everything openly in front of the station staff. The council's written record included both the majority conclusion and the objections. A dissenting voice might prove mistaken, but removing it would make tomorrow's investigation harder. Veyra knew how tempting a clean story could be. In the novel, every official had possessed the right answer; in life, answers needed revision dates. The aboriginal families requested recognition of their own land records. Wild grasses abound on the slopes even in a harsh season. The surveyor tried to align his grid with an ancient footpath. At this altitude, the river sounded distant and strangely thin. Silas's annoyance showed when the official refused to explain his method.

山丘觀測站位於首都港灣薄霧之上。薇拉與安溫沿窄梯上行，跟著一名對此行不耐的衛兵。當地觀測者赫拉在王家鐘旁維護簡單的日影盤已有二十年。爭議那天，她測過同一個黎明，記錄卻與印刷星曆不同。首都副本有一條空白，正好是兩組數字應該核對的地方。薇拉沒有要求偷偷拍下任何東西，而是請站員在場公開抄錄全部內容。 議會的書面紀錄同時保留多數結論與反對意見。不同聲音可能是錯的，但刪除它只會讓明天的調查更困難。薇拉知道一個乾淨整齊的故事多麼誘人。小說裡每位官員都掌握標準答案；真實生活中的答案卻需要標明修訂日期。 原住民族家庭要求承認他們自己的土地紀錄。 即使季節嚴酷，坡地上仍長滿野草。 測量員試圖讓方格對齊一條古老步道。 在這樣的海拔，河聲聽來遙遠而微弱。 官員拒絕解釋方法時，賽拉斯露出了不悅。

Hera challenged Veyra before handing over the book. During the previous winter, Veyra herself had approved a rule that allowed blank observations to be replaced with an editor's estimate. The change had been called efficient. Now it could conceal an alteration. Veyra acknowledged her signature and asked Hera to put the accusation in writing. Neither woman trusted the other immediately, but they agreed that a disputed historical entry could not be repaired by adding another silent correction. Anwen wrote a visible note beside the missing strip and invited Hera to sign her own account. Silas copied the dates onto a separate sheet and signed every correction. His first drafts had once been neat enough to conceal confusion. Now he understood that a crossed-out number with an explanation was often more useful than a spotless page. He placed the discarded calculations in an envelope instead of burning them. Hera gave a blunt answer: families were not numbers to be sold. The mountain crossing remained open until the weather warning took effect. No witness was asked to discard a crew card without a receipt. The inspector demanded records of each worker's medical discharge. Their endeavor began with listening rather than announcing a conclusion.

赫拉交出紀錄前先質問薇拉。上一個冬天，正是薇拉批准一項規則，容許編輯用估計值取代空白觀測，當時號稱提升效率，如今卻可能藏住竄改。薇拉承認自己的簽名，請赫拉把指控寫下。兩人沒有立刻互相信任，但都同意不能再用不留痕跡的修正來修補有爭議的歷史資料。安溫在空白旁留下明顯註記，請赫拉親自簽述經過。 賽拉斯把日期另抄在一張紙上，並在每處更正簽名。他過去的草稿整齊得足以掩蓋混亂，如今才懂得，有說明的刪改數字往往比毫無污點的頁面更有用。他把廢棄計算放入信封，而不是燒掉。 赫拉直截了當地回答：家庭不是可以出售的數字。 山間碼頭通道在天氣警報生效之前仍保持開放。 沒有人被要求在沒有收據時丟棄船員戶籍卡。 稽查員要求每名工人的醫療出院紀錄。 他們的努力從傾聽開始，而非先宣布結論。

A courier from the navigation ministry appeared with orders to seize the hill book for national security. Hera produced the station's charter: a sealed original could move only with a receipt signed by the custodian and the independent court. The courier had neither. Veyra refused to improvise an exception for her own convenience, even though she desperately wanted the book. They made two authenticated extracts and left the original in its stone cabinet. On the descent, Anwen said the minister would describe this as rebellion. Veyra answered that a careful refusal had a name: procedure. The accusation sounded convincing because it mixed one true detail with several unsupported conclusions. Veyra separated them aloud. She acknowledged what she had signed, denied what she had not ordered, and submitted the remaining questions to the independent panel. It was an uncomfortable method of self-defense, but discomfort was not evidence of guilt. A collapsed retaining wall was another hurdle for the survey team. The guard's threatening posture made the younger witnesses retreat. The measurements required precision because one marker changed the boundary. Hera accepted rigorous questions but rejected a rehearsed official answer. Their families cultivated beans on a narrow terrace above the stream.

航務部信差帶來以國安為名沒收山站紀錄的命令。赫拉拿出站區章程：封存原件若要移動，必須由保管人與獨立法院簽收，而信差沒有任何一方的手續。薇拉雖迫切想取得書本，仍拒絕為自己方便而破例。他們做了兩份驗證摘錄，原件繼續留在石櫃。下山時安溫說，大臣一定會稱此舉為叛亂。薇拉回答，謹慎拒絕有另一個名字，叫作程序。 指控聽起來可信，因為它混入一件真事，卻附帶好幾個沒有證據的結論。薇拉當眾把它們分開：承認自己簽過的文件，否認自己未下的命令，其餘疑問交給獨立小組。這種自我辯護方式很不舒服，但不舒服不等於有罪。 倒塌的擋土牆是測量隊的另一項障礙。 衛兵威嚇的姿態讓年輕證人後退。 測量必須精確，因為一個標記就會改變海岸邊界。 赫拉接受嚴格提問，卻拒絕事先排練的官方答案。 他們的家庭在溪上方狹窄的梯田種豆。

That night the two women compared the hill dial with the western lighthouse log. Neither station depended on the capital's toll bell. The mismatch narrowed to a window in which a correction sheet had entered the printing office without the second observer's initials. They still could not tell whether an error or deliberate interference had produced it. Veyra sent a public notice that listed the missing evidence without assigning blame. The shipping companies protested the delay, and she promised a hearing about costs, not a false promise that any loss would vanish. For years, elegant charts had concealed the work of people who climbed wet stairs, adjusted lenses, and waited for a cloud to pass. Now their names were returned to the page. The instruments were not infallible, and neither were the people using them. That was precisely why the procedure required more than one pair of eyes. The navigation minister feared an uprising more than he feared an inaccurate census. A station town teacher volunteered to usher visitors into the meeting hall. Anwen was versatile enough to read maps and repair a boat engine. The minister threatened to veto the proposed independent review. A widow described how camp fees had swallowed her husband's wages.

當夜兩人比對山丘日影盤與西方燈塔紀錄。兩座站都不依賴首都報時鐘。差異縮小到印刷所收到一張沒有第二名觀測者簽押的更正紙的時段。他們仍無法判定是疏失還是刻意干預。薇拉發出公告，列出缺少的證據卻不先指責任何人。航運公司抗議延誤，她答應召開成本聽證，而非虛假保證損失會自行消失。 多年來，精美圖表掩蓋了真正做事的人：他們爬上潮濕階梯、調整透鏡、等候雲層散開。如今他們的名字重新回到頁面。儀器並非永不犯錯，操作者也一樣；這正是程序需要不只一雙眼睛的原因。 航務大臣害怕起義，勝過害怕錯誤的人口統計。 一名村教師自願引導訪客進入會堂。 安溫既能看地圖又會修船引擎，十分多才多藝。 部長威脅否決獨立複查的提案。 一名寡婦描述營地費用如何吞掉丈夫的工資。
