消失的十七分鐘

The Seventeen-Minute Gap

星曆失落的十七分鐘 · 第 1 / 25 章 · 5000–6000 · 英中對照

Leina woke to the sound of a bell she had read about in a novel. She was no longer a data verifier in her own city but Veyra, the disgraced superintendent of the Royal Observatory. A finished almanac waited beside a seal and an order to certify tomorrow's opening of the northern tide gate. In the book, that opening wrecked three ships, and Veyra was executed as the wicked woman responsible. The page looked official, but the roof observatory and the harbor gauge named different times. Exactly seventeen minutes had vanished between them. She reminded the team that a prediction was not the same as an observation. A prediction could suggest when to look, but the actual measurement had to be written down with its uncertainty and the name of the observer. If the instruments disagreed, the disagreement belonged in the record instead of disappearing from it. At the harbor desk, a junior clerk tried to abbreviate a grandmother's surname in the passenger record. Veyra asked him to keep her full name so the family could search for it in the archive. Anwen pointed out that the survey boat's anchor had held through three violent winters, but no one had checked the boat's clock against the observatory. Veyra also asked whether the new sailing order was applicable to children without papers. The grandmother pressed her rejected ticket against her bosom and waited for an answer.

蕾娜在鐘聲中醒來，那鐘聲她曾在小說裡讀過。她不再是原本城市的資料校驗師，而成了惡名昭彰的王立天文台監督薇拉。完成的星曆、印章與明天北方水閘開放的核准令擺在桌上。原著裡，這次開閘毀掉三艘船，薇拉被當成罪魁禍首處死。文件看似正式，屋頂天文台與港口水位計卻記著不同時間；兩份資料之間，整整十七分鐘消失了。 她提醒團隊，預測並不等於觀測。預測可以提示該在何時查看，但實際測量仍須記下不確定範圍與觀測者姓名。如果儀器讀數不一致，分歧就應留在紀錄裡，而不是被悄悄抹去。 港口櫃檯的年輕書記想縮寫一位祖母的姓氏。薇拉請他保留全名，好讓家屬日後能在檔案庫裡查到。安溫指出，測量船的船錨雖然撐過三個猛烈的冬天，卻從沒有人核對船鐘與天文台時鐘是否一致。薇拉又問，新航行命令是否適用於沒有證件的孩子。祖母把遭退回的船票緊按在胸前，等待答覆。

Back at the observatory, Veyra asked her young apprentice Anwen to bring the unbound observation sheets, not another polished summary. Anwen arrived with red eyes, expecting the usual accusation of incompetence. Instead Veyra asked who had watched the clock and who had recorded the water. The apprentice named two observers who had never met. A minister's messenger arrived to demand the seal immediately. Veyra declined, wrote down the reason, and asked the harbor office for a temporary suspension pending a narrow safety inspection. Refusing a document was not the same as proving its author dishonest. A temporary victory did not settle responsibility. The ministry could still object, and a corrected timetable could create new costs for dockworkers who relied on predictable shifts. Veyra asked the council to publish those costs rather than conceal them. An honest solution had to protect lives without pretending that cancelled voyages harmed nobody. An orderly brought in a travel bag abandoned near the quay. Inside were a bra, children's socks, and a ticket with no passenger name. A frightened child rubbed an eyelid while trying to remember who had left it there. Silas, the harbor supervisor, refused a bribe from a stranger who wanted the bag removed from the register. Jory, a local clockmaker, proposed a comparative count of tickets and crew cards before anyone was blamed. The kitchen would broil its remaining fish so families could eat while the inquiry continued.

回到天文台後，薇拉請年輕學徒安溫拿出尚未裝訂的原始觀測紙，而不是另一份修飾過的摘要。安溫雙眼通紅，以為又要被斥責無能。薇拉卻問，是誰看鐘、誰記水位？安溫說出兩名互不相識的觀測者。大臣的信差趕來催她立刻蓋印，薇拉拒絕並寫明理由，還請港務處在有限度安全檢查前暫停開閘。拒簽文件並不代表她已證明撰寫者說謊。 暫時的勝利不等於責任已獲釐清。部會仍可提出異議，修正航行時間也可能增加依靠固定輪班的碼頭工人負擔。薇拉要求議會公開這些成本，不要遮掩。誠實的解方必須保護人命，也不能假裝取消航次不會傷害任何人。 一名勤務員帶來在碼頭附近發現的旅行袋。袋裡有一件胸罩、幾雙童襪和一張沒有乘客姓名的船票。一個受驚的孩子揉著眼皮，努力回想是誰把袋子留下。港口主管賽拉斯拒絕陌生人提出的賄賂；對方想讓他把旅行袋從紀錄中刪除。當地鐘匠喬里建議先比較船票和船員卡的數量，再決定是否指責任何人。廚房則準備炙烤剩下的魚，讓等候調查的家屬先吃上飯。

Anwen explained the kingdom's rule: an almanac entry became binding only after two independent observation series were signed, while a credible immediate danger could justify a temporary warning. Veyra realized the novelist had treated these rules as decorative background, though here they could save lives. She ordered the original pages placed in separate sleeves and invited Jory, the clockmaker, to examine the bell mechanism. He warned that a clock's hands could be moved without changing the sky. The minister's messenger replied that trade would suffer. Veyra requested that both the safety risk and the economic cost be recorded. No one demanded that Veyra forget the original ending. They only demanded that she stop treating memory as testimony. What she knew from a story could direct her attention toward a suspicious hour, but it could not replace a signed observation. Her new life would be measured by the records she made available to other people. At the junction of two old roads, relatives of the missing ship crews crowded around a volunteer desk. Their food supplies were finite, but Veyra hoped the coastal villages could flourish once safe crossings resumed. A local gangster offered to silence the survey-boat witnesses for a fee. Veyra ordered his words recorded; she would not call an accusation proof without further investigation. Jory walked with a limp, yet carried the original receipts to the desk himself so the families could compare them.

安溫說明王國規定：星曆條目必須由兩組獨立觀測簽認才具效力，但可信的即時危險足以發布暫時警告。薇拉發現小說把這些條文寫成背景裝飾，這個世界卻能用它們救命。她命人分別封存原件，再請剛才提出比對建議的鐘匠喬里檢查報時鐘機構。喬里提醒她，鐘針能被移動，天空不會跟著變。信差說貿易將受到損害；薇拉要求安全風險和經濟損失都列入紀錄。 沒有人要求薇拉忘記原著結局；大家只要求她別把記憶當證詞。她從故事得知的資訊，可以讓她注意某個可疑時刻，卻無法取代簽名觀測紀錄。她的新人生將由她願意公開、讓其他人查證的紀錄來衡量。 在兩條舊路的交叉口，失蹤船員的家屬擠在志工服務桌前。大家的食物有限，但薇拉仍希望沿海村莊能在安全通航後恢復繁榮。一名地方幫派分子提議收錢封住測量船證人的嘴。薇拉命人把他的話記下；在進一步調查前，指控不等於證據。喬里走路雖然跛行，仍親自把原始收據送到服務桌，讓家屬能逐一比對。

At sunset a notice appeared on the observatory door, accusing Veyra of creating panic to hide her own earlier mistakes. She recognized the first sentence from the novel's accusation scene. What she remembered next was not evidence: a courtroom full of applause and the villainess dying before she could speak. This time she signed a different paper. It requested independent copies from the western lighthouse and granted the dockworkers a chance to challenge the temporary closure. Someone outside tore down the notice before the ink dried. Inside, the disputed seventeen minutes remained clearly marked in red. Veyra listened while the others spoke first. Her title had once ended disagreements by making them disappear. She would not allow it to work that way again. The youngest apprentice corrected a numerical error, and the oldest sailor admitted that his memory of a bell could be mistaken. Both statements improved the final account. Anwen offered to narrate the sequence of recorded crossings to an independent clerk. An orderly guided the families to a waiting room, while the ticket teller at the observatory quay identified several passengers from the handwritten register. Veyra placed the seal beside a sheet of virgin paper—unused, with no earlier entries to conceal. She would approve only a narrow warrant for the disputed logbook, never a search of private homes. The list of passengers remained available for relatives to check before the final notice was issued.

日落時，一張告示貼上天文台大門，指責薇拉製造恐慌以掩蓋自己先前的錯誤。她認出那正是小說指控場景的第一句話。接下來記得的情節並不是證據：滿庭喝采，惡女還沒開口就被處死。這一次她簽下另一份文件，要求西方燈塔提供獨立副本，也讓碼頭工人得以申訴暫時封閉。門外有人在墨跡未乾時撕掉告示；屋內，那十七分鐘的爭議仍被清楚用紅筆標記。 薇拉先聽其他人說話。過去她的頭銜常以壓下異議的方式結束爭論，她不願再如此。最年輕的學徒改正一道計算錯誤，最年長的船員也承認自己可能記錯鐘聲。兩個說法都改善了最終紀錄。 安溫願意向獨立書記敘述紀錄中的各次船隻通行。一名勤務員引導家屬前往候客室；天文台碼頭的售票員則根據手寫名冊認出幾位乘客。薇拉把印章放在一張全新、尚未留下任何紀錄的白紙旁。她只會核准範圍有限的調閱令，准許查驗有爭議的航海紀錄，不會允許搜查民宅。正式公告發布前，家屬仍可查閱名單，核對乘客姓名。
