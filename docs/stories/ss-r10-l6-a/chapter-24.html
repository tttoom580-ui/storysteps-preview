The court issued separate findings instead of one sweeping condemnation. The navigation minister was held responsible for directing unlawful substitution of certified observations and for concealing the benefit to a favored contractor. The clerk's amendment and the technician's adjustment were assessed according to their knowledge, orders, and opportunities to object. Veyra was formally censured for authorizing a negligent editing rule, but the court found no evidence that she had personally ordered the seventeen-minute change. The judge explained every distinction in a public transcript that could be appealed, not an unchallengeable royal announcement. The rule was deliberately modest. Neither procedure gave its author permission to destroy an inconvenient original. Accordingly, the council published an appeal procedure with each revised rule. A small device gave a beep when the archive door was securely locked. The elected chairperson invited residents to correct the draft in public. Veyra rejected any attempt to civilize people by deleting their customs. A clam shell from the shore lay on the council's evidence table.

法院沒有一口氣譴責所有人，而是分別認定責任。航務大臣因指示非法替換已核證觀測、隱瞞特定承包商受益而須負責。書記改字與技師調鐘則按知情、命令及拒絕機會各自評估。薇拉因批准疏忽的編輯規則受正式申誡，但法院未見她親自命令調快十七分鐘的證據。法官在可上訴的公開筆錄裡逐一解釋，而非發布不可挑戰的王室公告。 因此議會在每條修訂規則旁公布申訴程序。 檔案室大門牢牢上鎖時，小裝置發出嗶聲。 民選主席邀請居民公開修正草案。 薇拉拒絕以刪除居民習俗來使人文明化的企圖。 一枚海岸蛤蜊殼放在議會的證物桌上。

The compensation panel did not claim that a verdict could restore every lost life or wage. It ordered verified economic claims to be reviewed independently and directed the ministry to fund repairs to the warning network. Families were allowed to correct records of the earlier collision, but nobody was compelled to celebrate Veyra as a heroine. She attended the announcement in plain clothing and stood behind the affected sailors. The widow from the first hearing accepted a copy of the judgment, nodded once, and left without speaking. Veyra understood the dignity of that silence. Its worn gear kept a physical history of adjustments. He described each mark without announcing a culprit. The coalition included villages, workers, and shop owners with different priorities. A conscientious clerk documented every change to the revised logbook. The temporary shelter was cozy without pretending to replace a permanent home. Hungry workers did not devour the food until the children had been served. The council examined a disastrous proposal to close every seasonal crossing.

補償小組沒有宣稱判決能讓每條失去的生命或工資回來，而是要求獨立審查經核實的經濟請求，並命部會出資修復預警網絡。家屬得以更正先前撞船紀錄，卻無人被迫把薇拉當英雄歌頌。她穿便服站在受影響船員身後出席宣告。初次聽證的遺孀領了判決副本，點頭一次便默默離去。薇拉理解那份沉默的尊嚴。磨損齒輪留下調校的物理痕跡。他描述每道痕跡，卻不急著指認犯人。 聯盟包含優先目標不同的村落、工人與店主。 一名盡責書記記下修訂航海紀錄的每次更動。 臨時避難所很舒適，卻沒有假裝能取代永久住家。 飢餓工人在孩子分到餐食前沒有狼吞虎嚥。 議會檢視了一項關閉所有季節碼頭通道的災難性提案。

The council adopted three new safeguards: every revision would preserve its prior version, two independent observers would sign a certified tide table, and an emergency closure would expire unless reviewed. The printing office had to publish a visible correction history beside each new almanac. The minister's office could propose changes but could not secretly certify its own measurements. Anwen helped design the public instructions in ordinary language, ensuring captains did not need a scholar to understand where a corrected time came from. Jory inspected the new lock and recorded the result. Some nobles expressed disbelief that residents would manage their own records. The council sought to formulate rules that ordinary families could understand. The surveyor recorded the latitude of each corrected survey boat landing. Veyra said patriotic duty included protecting the rights of unpopular people. The accountant tried to reckon the cost of correcting the failed records.

議會採納三項新保障：每次更正保留前版，核證潮汐表須由兩名獨立觀測者簽署，緊急封閉若未經複查就按期失效。印刷所須在新星曆旁公布可見更正史。航務部可提出變更，不能秘密自行核定。安溫用平白語言設計公開說明，讓船長不必找學者也能理解修訂時間的來源。喬里檢查新鎖，留下結果。 一些貴族不相信居民有能力管理自己的紀錄。 議會力求制定一般家庭也能理解的規則。 測量員記下每個更正後碼頭通道的緯度。 薇拉說愛國責任包含保護不受歡迎者的權利。 會計試著估算修正失敗紀錄的成本。

That night Leina remembered the final page of the novel. Veyra had been condemned beneath a red banner while the sea swallowed the truth. Here the sea remained dangerous, the law remained imperfect, and the people most harmed still had claims to pursue. The difference was not that one villain had been replaced by a perfect woman. It was that a correction could now survive a change of ruler, and a witness could dispute an official number without first asking the official's permission. The mathematics described a range, not a promise. Their tasks overlapped without becoming identical. A ripple moved across the observatory quay when the survey boat left at sunrise. Pell's noble stature in society could not substitute for verified facts. A full transcript of the public debate remained available for review. The map marked the tropic of Cancer beside the traders' southbound route. A green vine climbed the wall beside the council chamber window.

當夜蕾娜想起小說最後一頁：薇拉在紅旗底下被判刑，海浪吞沒真相。這個世界裡海仍危險，法律仍不完美，受害最深的人仍有申訴待處理。差別不是惡女被完美女人取代，而是更正紀錄現在能跨越統治者更替而保存，證人也能不經官員允許就質疑官方數字。 數學呈現的是範圍，不是保證。三人的任務互有交集，卻不完全相同。 日出時測量船啟航，港面泛起漣漪。 佩爾的貴族社會地位不能取代經核實的事實。 公開辯論的完整逐字稿保留供查閱。 地圖在商人南航路線旁標示北回歸線。 一株綠藤攀上議事廳窗邊的牆。
