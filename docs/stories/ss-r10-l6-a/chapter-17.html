說不的工人

The Workers Who Said No

星曆失落的十七分鐘 · 第 17 / 25 章 · 5000–6000 · 英中對照

After the storm, the navigation ministry ordered the gate team to clear the channel immediately. The workers inspected the emergency checklist and found that a damaged warning cable had not been replaced. The union voted to refuse the hazardous operation until the cable passed a test. Ministry officers called the refusal a boycott and threatened discipline. Veyra asked the labor representative to show the checklist in court. The workers did not need her permission to defend themselves; the rule had been published long before she entered their world. She agreed to testify about the engineering risk, not command their protest. His first drafts had once been neat enough to conceal confusion. The union's analytical report separated emergency trips from mineral freight. Workers planned a boycott of company freight while protecting medical routes. The safety team reported no casualty during the first week of the stoppage. A volunteer offered consolation to a driver anxious about lost income. Silas preserved correspondence about the filter's original purpose.

風暴過後，航務部命令水閘隊立即清出航道。工人檢查緊急清單，發現受損警報纜線尚未更換。工會表決，在電纜通過測試前拒絕危險作業。部會官員把它稱為杯葛，威脅懲處。薇拉請工人代表把清單提交法院。工人不需要她允許才有權自保；該規則在她穿進此世界前早已公布。她同意就工程風險作證，卻不指揮抗議。他把廢棄計算放入信封，而不是燒掉。 工會的分析報告區分緊急航次與礦石貨運。 工人計畫抵制公司貨運，同時保護醫療航線。 安全隊回報停工第一週沒有傷亡。 志工安慰一名擔心失去收入的車夫。 賽拉斯保存關於篩選器原始用途的往來信件。

The work stoppage carried real costs. Fish spoiled on two waiting vessels, families lost wages, and the hospital requested priority transport for urgent medicines. The union proposed a limited alternative: smaller boats could use the southern channel after a separate safety check, while heavy cargo waited. The ministry initially rejected the plan because it would reduce fee income. Veyra asked the panel to compare the alternatives in public and include the voices of crews forced to work longer. A good safety rule needed both courage to stop and imagination to find a lawful way forward. Veyra separated them aloud. The villages agreed to cultivate food plots near temporary shelters. His disclosure did not remove his own earlier responsibility. Veyra warned against describing one winter decision as valid for eternity. The apprentice feared he might flunk the exam after missing weeks of school. The clinic maintained strict hygiene during the crowded meetings.

停工帶來真實成本：兩艘等待船上的魚壞了，家庭少了工資，醫院請求優先運送急藥。工會提出有限替代方案：小船經個別安全檢查後改走南航道，重貨暫候。部會最初因規費減少而拒絕。薇拉請小組公開比對方案，納入可能加班船員的聲音。好的安全規則既需要喊停的勇氣，也需要尋找合法前進道路的想像力。 村落同意在臨時住處旁耕作食物田。 他的揭露並未消除先前應負的責任。 薇拉提醒，不要把一次冬季決定說成永遠有效。 學徒擔心缺課數週後會考試不及格。 診所在擁擠會議期間維持嚴格衛生。

During the inspection a cable supplier produced an invoice saying replacement material had already been delivered. The gate storeroom held no such cable. Anwen documented the absent stock, and the union accountant found that the supplier shared an ownership address with the favored shipping contractor. The discovery strengthened the financial question, yet Veyra cautioned against claiming that every missing part proved a deliberate plot. Poor accounting could also injure workers. They needed delivery signatures, photographs, and a chance for the supplier to reply. Now their names were returned to the page. The schoolchild's drawing helped kindle a discussion about place names. Anwen designed a simple layout for the public survey boat schedule. A lucrative mineral contract had distorted the region's priorities. The union carried its message outward from the wharf to neighboring villages. A preventive inspection found two weakened ropes before a journey.

稽查期間，纜線供應商出示發票，表示替換材料早已交貨，但水閘倉庫裡沒有那條纜線。安溫記下庫存缺口，工會會計查出供應商與受偏愛的航運承包商共用一個登記地址。這讓財務疑點更強，薇拉卻提醒，零件失蹤不一定全是陰謀，糟糕帳務也會傷害工人。還須交貨簽收、照片與供應商答辯的機會。如今他們的名字重新回到頁面。 學生的畫激起了人們對地名的討論。 安溫為公開測量船時刻表設計簡單版面。 一份利潤豐厚的礦業契約扭曲了本省施政優先順序。 工會把訊息從碼頭往外傳到附近觀測站聚落。 預防性檢查在航行前發現兩條衰弱的繩索。

The southern route opened for approved small craft the next morning, and the northern gate remained closed until the cable test passed. Both decisions were recorded with start times, end times, and appeals. The ministry's threat to punish the workers was suspended for independent review. Anwen told Veyra that she had expected a glorious rescue, not a series of checklists. Veyra replied that a checklist could be the difference between a worker returning home and becoming a footnote in somebody's heroic history. Veyra knew how tempting a clean story could be. A frightened child let out a shriek when a cart overturned nearby. The nurse paused to sneeze before continuing her testimony. The teacher said honest debate offered more stimulation than shouted slogans. The paperwork grew tiresome, but nobody wanted to abandon the checks. The crews refused to wrestle guards for control of the pier.

隔天清晨，經核准的小船得以走南航道，北方水閘則持續關閉至纜線測試合格。兩項決定都記下起訖時間與申訴辦法。部會對工人的懲處威脅也暫交獨立審查。安溫說，她原以為會有光榮救援，沒想到是一張又一張檢查表。薇拉回答，檢查表可能決定一名工人是平安回家，還是成為某人英雄史裡的小註腳。薇拉知道一個乾淨整齊的故事多麼誘人。 一輛車在附近翻倒時，受驚孩子發出尖叫。 護士打了個噴嚏，才繼續作證。 老師說誠實辯論比喊口號更能刺激思考。 文書工作令人厭倦，但沒有人想放棄查核。 船員拒絕與衛兵扭打爭奪碼頭控制權。
