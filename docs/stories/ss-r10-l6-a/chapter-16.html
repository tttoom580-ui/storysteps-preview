東站燈熄滅時

When the Eastern Lamp Went Dark

星曆失落的十七分鐘 · 第 16 / 25 章 · 5000–6000 · 英中對照

The first severe storm struck before dawn, while the provisional advisory still carried legal force. Rain crossed the observatory windows in slanting sheets. At the eastern station, a generator failed and the recording mechanism stopped at a crucial hour. The station keeper sent a messenger with a handwritten report marked incomplete. Veyra admired the honesty more than she feared the gap. A broken instrument was dangerous, but a false claim of perfect operation could send sailors into the wrong current. She asked whether the western lighthouse and the hill station could offer independent readings. A temporary victory did not settle responsibility. A schoolteacher wrote the word welcome in careful calligraphy on the shelter sign. The children gathered discarded cardboard for a classroom model of the river. Each carton of filters received a numbered receipt before unloading. The town kitchens offered to cater meals for volunteers working overnight. A doctor checked a patient's cholesterol record while planning long-term treatment.

第一場強烈風暴在黎明前襲來，暫行建議仍具法律效力。雨斜斜拍打天文台窗戶。東站發電機故障，記錄機械在重要時段停擺。守站人派信差送來手寫報告，明白標示資料不完整。薇拉欣賞這份誠實，勝過恐懼空白。儀器壞掉很危險，但假裝它完美運作，可能使船員駛進錯誤水流。她詢問西方燈塔與山站能否提供獨立讀數。 暫時的勝利不等於責任已獲釐清。薇拉要求議會公開這些成本，不要遮掩。 一名教師以工整書法在避難所招牌寫下歡迎。 孩子收集廢紙板，製作河川的教室模型。 每箱濾水器卸貨前都取得編號收據。 鎮上廚房願為徹夜工作的志工供餐。 醫師規畫長期治療時查看一名病人的膽固醇紀錄。

Anwen coordinated the station calls without ordering anyone to climb an exposed tower during lightning. Hera used a sheltered dial enclosure and the lighthouse keeper reported the water from a protected platform. Their estimates agreed within a range broad enough to require continued closure of the northern gate. The minister protested that an uncertain figure could not justify an order. The safety officer answered that the statute required a credible immediate hazard, not an impossible degree of certainty. He signed the closure himself and fixed an expiration time, leaving the independent panel free to review his judgment. No one demanded that Veyra forget the original ending. They only demanded that she stop treating memory as testimony. The disturbance at the dock ended when the inventory was read aloud. Water ran downward through the old drain toward the station town well. The foreman looked indignant when asked to withdraw his accusation. Anwen began to marvel at how much residents knew about forgotten pipes. The supply officer agreed to mediate a dispute over delivery priorities.

安溫協調各站通訊，沒有命令任何人在閃電下爬上裸露塔頂。赫拉使用有遮蔽的日影裝置，燈塔守護人在受保護平台回報水位。估算值在一定範圍內相符，足以繼續關閉北方水閘。大臣抗議不確定讀數不能支持命令。安全官回答，法條要求可信的即時危險，不要求不可能的絕對確定。他親自簽署封閉並寫上失效時間，交由獨立小組複查。 碼頭騷動在庫存清單被朗讀後平息。 水順著舊排水道往下流向村井。 工頭被要求撤回指控時顯得憤憤不平。 安溫開始驚嘆居民對被遺忘水管竟如此熟悉。 物資官同意調解配送優先順序的爭議。

A young courier arrived soaked, carrying the eastern keeper's manual notebook. Its pages contained rough intervals rather than clean numbers. Jory compared the written times with his own clock and marked the uncertainty introduced by travel. Veyra remembered the royal novel describing such notebooks as the untidy work of lesser servants. Here those rough lines were the only reason the council understood what the failed machine had missed. She had the book copied and returned immediately to its owner, who would need it when the equipment came back online. Veyra listened while the others spoke first. Her title had once ended disagreements by making them disappear. She would not allow it to work that way again. Both statements improved the final account. Veyra said reliable records were useful, not miraculous solutions. A volunteer used a paddle to guide the small boat through reeds. The nurse remained persistent when officials questioned the larger water estimate. A station porter carried the medical crates into dry storage. The temporary plant could purify enough water for the nearest settlement.

一名年輕信差全身溼透，送來東站守護人的手寫筆記。頁面記著粗略區間，不是漂亮數字。喬里用自己的鐘比對，標出運送途中增加的不確定。薇拉記得原著曾把這類筆記貶為低階僕役的凌亂作品；在這裡，正是那些粗線條讓議會理解機器漏掉了什麼。她命人複製後立刻把原冊還給主人，因為設備復工時仍要用到。 薇拉先聽其他人說話。兩個說法都改善了最終紀錄。 薇拉說可靠紀錄很有用，卻不是奇蹟般的解方。 一名志工用槳引導小船穿過蘆葦。 官員質疑更高用水估計時，護士仍堅持說明。 車站搬運工把醫療箱送進乾燥倉庫。 臨時設備能淨化足夠最近聚落使用的水。

By evening the storm weakened, but the eastern generator could not be certified without a repair and a separate check. The provisional advisory was updated openly rather than silently copied forward. Veyra watched Anwen explain the change to impatient captains. She spoke with authority without pretending to know more than the measurements showed. The apprentice had once waited for Veyra to supply every answer. Now she was capable of saying that a safe answer required another day. Veyra found that more reassuring than obedience. The survey boat crews decided to rehearse emergency unloading before the storm. Anwen asked a runner to relay the new quantities to the northern station town. The school donated stationery so families could submit written corrections. No one needed a superstitious explanation for the broken filter seals. A bag of walnut shells had been used to protect fragile glass bottles.

傍晚風暴漸弱，但東站發電機未修妥、未受獨立複查前不能重新核定。暫行建議公開更新，而不是偷偷沿用。薇拉看安溫向不耐煩的船長解釋改動。學徒說話有權威，卻不假裝知道超出測量的事。她以前等薇拉給出每個答案，現在懂得有時安全答案需要再等一天。薇拉覺得這比服從更令人安心。 她提醒團隊，預測並不等於觀測。 測量船船員決定在風暴前演練緊急卸貨。 安溫請跑腿的人把新數量轉告北方觀測站聚落。 學校捐贈文具，讓家庭可以提出書面更正。 破損的濾器封條不需要迷信的解釋。 一袋胡桃殼被用來保護易碎玻璃瓶。
