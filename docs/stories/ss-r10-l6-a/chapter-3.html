黎明前的水閘

The Gate Before Dawn

星曆失落的十七分鐘 · 第 3 / 25 章 · 5000–6000 · 英中對照

Before dawn the northern channel began to draw water faster than the published table predicted. Captain Nolin had been instructed to move three supply boats through the gate at the royal bell. Anwen brought a fresh gauge reading showing a dangerous current, not merely an alarming story from a book. Veyra showed it to the harbor safety officer, who possessed authority to delay the opening for one watch. The officer hesitated because the cargo belonged to the minister's favored contractor. Veyra asked him to sign his decision in front of the captains. The sealed copies travelled by different routes. It seemed inefficient until they learned how easily a single courier could be delayed. Independent evidence was not merely about having two documents on one desk; it required two custodians who could explain where their documents had been and who had handled them. An advisory board had approved the home without visiting its sickroom. The director spoke of conquest when he meant control of another valley. Veyra suspected a conspiracy but refused to label a rumor as evidence. A sliced cucumber lay untouched beside the sick child's breakfast. The youngest apprentice answered in a feeble voice after the fever.

黎明前，北方航道的水流比印刷表預測得更快。諾林船長接到命令，必須在王家鐘響時把三艘補給船送過水閘。安溫拿來最新水位讀數，顯示實際存在的危險流速，而非小說裡一句嚇人的預言。薇拉把讀數交給有權延後一個值更開閘的港務安全官。安全官猶豫，因為貨物屬於大臣偏愛的承包商。薇拉要求他當著船長的面簽下決定。 封存副本沿不同路線送出，起初看似沒效率，直到他們發現一名信差多容易被拖延。獨立證據不只是同桌放著兩張紙，而是有兩名保管者能說清楚文件去過哪裡、由誰接觸。 一個諮詢委員會沒看過病房，就批准了這所機構。 院長談到征服，實際上是想控制另一座山谷。 薇拉懷疑有人共謀，卻拒絕把謠言當成證據。 一根切片黃瓜留在病童早餐旁，無人動過。 最年幼的學徒發燒後以虛弱聲音回答。

The water struck the stone pier hard enough to scatter spray over the inspectors. Nolin demanded to know how long his crew would remain unpaid if the departure failed. Veyra could not answer from a telescope. She asked the labor representative to join the calculation of lost shifts and emergency stores, then told Nolin he could appeal the delay. The safety officer issued a temporary order based on the measured current. No one called it a verdict on the disputed almanac. When the royal bell rang, the gate remained shut and the anchored boats were still intact. The final decision could not return the lost wages or erase every frightening night, but it could assign responsibility and establish a remedy. Veyra insisted on separate compensation for economic losses and a separate finding about the altered clock. No convenient apology would be allowed to replace either obligation. A firecracker had frightened the horses hours before the orchard blaze. The cook began to fume when officials blamed her uninspected stove. Fresh grease on a cart wheel suggested a delivery after curfew. The physician hoped clean air would lessen the children's coughing. A coastal liner was listed as a destination, although no ship had arrived.

水浪猛擊石碼頭，噴濺到稽查員身上。諾林問，若不能出航，船員多久領不到工資。薇拉無法靠望遠鏡回答，便請工人代表一起核算失去的班次與緊急物資，並告訴諾林可以申訴延航。安全官依實測水流發布暫時命令，沒有人把它說成對星曆的最終判決。王家鐘聲響起時，水閘仍關著，停泊的船隻安然無恙。 最終決定無法讓失去的工資立刻回來，也不能抹去每個恐懼的夜晚，卻可以分配責任並建立補救途徑。薇拉堅持把經濟損失補償與鐘被調動的調查結果分開處理；任何方便的道歉都不能取代這兩種義務。 果園起火前數小時，一枚鞭炮曾嚇到馬匹。 官員責怪尚未檢查的爐子時，廚師氣得冒火。 車輪上的新油脂暗示宵禁後仍有貨物運送。 醫師希望乾淨空氣能減輕孩子們的咳嗽。 目的地記載了一艘沿海定期船，卻沒有船靠港。

An hour later a floating measuring post struck the gate wall and split. Jory examined the fracture and told the crowd that the post would not have survived a vessel's hull scraping across it. The captain looked at the pale boards of his own ship and fell silent. Anwen recorded the water height before anyone could move the broken pieces. The minister's agent tried to confiscate her notebook, but the safety officer required a written warrant. For the first time, dockworkers who disliked Veyra defended her apprentice's right to preserve the figures. The apprentice did not ask for a heroic speech. She asked where to place the next piece of evidence and who could inspect it without seeking permission from the accused official. That practical question changed the discussion. They agreed on separate copies, an open list of corrections, and a recorded opportunity for witnesses to challenge the account. The orphanage received donations but lacked basic medicines. Officials used the word plague before doctors had identified any disease. The courtyard was meant for recreational games, not military drills. The director called the fleeing children savage, an accusation Veyra rejected. A broken shrub beside the gate showed where a cart had turned.

一小時後，一根浮動測量柱撞上水閘牆壁裂開。喬里查看裂口，告訴群眾，如果換成船殼刮過去也可能受損。船長望著自己船身淺色木板，沉默下來。安溫趁殘件尚未被搬動前記下水位。大臣代理人想沒收她的筆記，安全官卻要求書面令狀。第一次，向來不喜歡薇拉的碼頭工人也站出來支持學徒保存數字的權利。 學徒沒有要求英雄式的演說，而是問下一件證物該放在哪裡，以及誰能在不經被控官員同意下查閱它。這個務實問題改變了討論。他們同意製作獨立副本、公開更正清單，並記錄證人提出異議的機會。 孤兒院收到捐款，卻缺乏基本藥物。 醫師尚未確認疾病，官員便使用瘟疫一詞。 院子原本供休閒遊戲使用，不是軍事操練場。 院長把逃跑的孩子稱作野蠻人，薇拉拒絕接受這種指控。 大門旁折斷的灌木顯示馬車曾在那裡轉彎。

The postponed boats delivered their supplies on the following watch after a second gauge agreed with the first that conditions had improved. Veyra insisted that the safer departure be logged beside the cost of waiting. She did not announce that she had saved three crews with supernatural knowledge. She had acted upon measurements other people could reproduce. By evening the council received a petition from merchants asking for a review, and another from families asking why last winter's collision had not received the same caution. That older question could no longer be ignored. The warning board outside the observatory carried a plain sentence: no schedule overruled a visible hazard. People could appeal an unnecessary closure, and an independent officer had to answer within a fixed period. Those protections mattered. Safety used as an excuse for unlimited power could eventually become another form of danger. The children used slang words to describe guards without revealing names. An accurate investigation had to surpass the director's convenient version. Elsi said the blue thread could symbolize a river crossing. The ribbon was made of rough textile woven by the older apprentices. A delayed vaccine delivery had left the infirmary dangerously unprepared.

下一值更時，第二座水位計也證實條件改善，延後的補給船終於運出貨物。薇拉堅持將較安全的出航時間與等待成本一併記錄。她沒有宣稱自己憑超自然預知拯救三組船員，而是依他人可以重測的資料行事。傍晚議會收到兩份請願：商人要求複查，家屬則問，為何去年冬天的撞船事故未得到同樣的謹慎？那個舊問題再也無法忽略。 天文台外的警示板寫著一句白話：任何時間表都不能凌駕眼前危險。不必要的封閉可以申訴，獨立官員必須在期限內回覆。這些保障很重要；若把安全當成無限權力的藉口，最後也會成為另一種危險。 孩子用俚語形容衛兵，卻不透露姓名。 準確的調查必須超越院長那套方便的說法。 艾爾西說藍線可能象徵一處渡河點。 緞帶由年長學徒織成的粗糙紡織品製成。 疫苗配送延誤，讓醫務室陷入危險的準備不足。
