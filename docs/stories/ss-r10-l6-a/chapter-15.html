The independent panel met without Veyra in the chair. Hera presented the time reconstruction, Jory the mechanical report, and the labor representative the evidence of unsafe work orders. They agreed to issue a provisional tide advisory, valid for twelve days and subject to renewed measurement. No single observer could extend it by personal order. Veyra spoke only when invited, identifying one unresolved question about the private contractor's access to the central clock. The panel recorded both the majority view and a dissenting note from the laboratory director, who believed the risk estimate was too cautious. The announcement described both the rescue and its limits. The navigation minister's affiliate received several payments for unused transport equipment. A young clerk said she would aspire to become an independent examiner. The eastern road stood on the brink of closure after heavy snow. A ceramic seal from the archive matched the material found on the false paper. A crowd began to chant the names of ship crews restored that day.

獨立小組開會時，薇拉不再坐主持席。赫拉提出時間重建，喬里提出機械報告，工人代表出示不安全作業命令。他們同意發布十二日有效、須重新測量的暫行潮汐建議，任何觀測者都無法憑個人命令無限延長。薇拉受邀時才發言，指出承包商如何接近中央鐘仍有疑問。小組同時記錄多數結論與實驗室主任的異議；他認為風險估計太保守。 公告同時交代救援成果與侷限。 航務大臣的關係企業因未使用的運輸設備收到數筆款項。 一名年輕書記說她立志成為獨立鑑查員。 大雪後，東路瀕臨封閉。 檔案庫的陶製封印與假文件上發現的材料相符。 群眾開始齊聲呼喊當天恢復的家庭姓名。

The minister tried to suppress the advisory by claiming a national emergency, but his written authority covered troop movements rather than public observations. The court clerk read the limitation aloud. Veyra resisted the urge to celebrate loudly; a defective legal argument was only one obstacle removed. Shipping companies still needed a workable plan, and inland towns still needed food. The panel appointed an operations group with captains, engineers, and workers to produce alternative departure slots. Those most affected by the rule now had a formal role in determining how it would function. Outside, the city continued its ordinary life. Veyra could not give certainty simply because uncertainty frightened them. The children's home kept a picture of a chimpanzee in its reading room. Each family received a coupon for food while its card was being prepared. Veyra warned that noisy celebration must not become a distraction from appeals. The examiner compared the seal impression with three original examples. An instinctive fear of uniforms kept some witnesses away from the hall.

大臣想用國家緊急狀態壓下建議，然而書面權力只涵蓋軍隊調動，不涵蓋公開觀測，法院書記當眾念出限制。薇拉忍住大聲慶祝；一項法律藉口失效，不過是少了一個障礙。航運公司仍需要可行方案，內陸城鎮仍需要食物。小組指派由船長、工程師與工人組成的作業團隊，提出替代出航時段。受規則影響最深的人，終於有正式角色參與制定。 窗外的城市依舊過著日常生活。 兒童之家閱覽室掛著一張黑猩猩圖片。 卡片製作期間，每個家庭拿到一張食物兌換券。 薇拉提醒，熱鬧慶祝不能分散對申訴的注意。 鑑查員把印章印痕與三份原件比較。 對制服本能的恐懼讓一些證人不敢進會堂。

That evening an observer approached Anwen with a concern. The provisional tables carried correct calculations, but one rain gauge at the eastern station had been repaired by an employee of the suspect contractor. Anwen reported the conflict of interest instead of concealing it for the sake of a clean announcement. The panel placed that gauge under renewed independent test and published the limitation. Veyra praised her apprentice privately. She did not take the credit; the decision belonged to the person who had found the difficulty and refused to hide it. She was right. ' Without shelter, the youngest travelers could perish during a freezing night. The council would ratify the temporary agreement only after public reading. It would be reckless to send more wagons over an uninspected bridge. The students learned to reproduce the calculations without copying private names. A sergeant confirmed that the new wagons had passed the eastern checkpoint.

當晚，一名觀測者告訴安溫一項疑慮：暫行表格計算正確，但東站一個雨量計曾由涉案承包商員工維修。安溫沒有為了讓公告漂亮而藏起利益衝突，而是立即通報。小組決定讓水位設備重新接受獨立檢測，並公開侷限。薇拉私下稱讚學徒，沒有攬功；真正做決定的，是發現困難又拒絕隱藏的人。 沒有住處，最年幼的旅人可能在寒夜喪命。 議會只會在公開宣讀後批准暫時協議。 讓更多馬車通過未檢查的橋，是魯莽之舉。 學生學會重現計算過程，而不複製私人姓名。 一名中士確認新馬車經過東方檢查站。

When the advisory went up on the harbor wall, some merchants cheered and others complained. No one received a promise that trade would become effortless. What the city obtained was a timetable that named its authors, its evidence, and the conditions under which it would change. For Veyra, this marked a different kind of success from the triumphant acquittal she once imagined. The system was learning to correct itself even when the woman who initiated the inquiry was not allowed to command it. Both questions belonged at the hearing. A sparrow landed on the open courtroom windowsill during the break. The town offered a subscription fund for independent legal assistance. Thereafter, every disputed deletion would require a recorded explanation. A vocational school agreed to offer voluntary classes to former mine apprentices. Anwen practiced yoga beside the survey boat shed to ease her aching back.

暫行建議貼上港口牆壁時，一些商人歡呼，另一些抱怨。沒有人得到貿易將毫不費力的保證。城市真正獲得的，是一份標出撰寫者、證據與變更條件的時刻表。對薇拉而言，這不同於她曾幻想的盛大無罪宣判：即使發起調查的女人不能指揮它，制度也開始學會自行修正。 休庭時，一隻麻雀停在法庭敞開的窗臺上。 城鎮設立定期捐款基金，支援獨立法律協助。 此後，每筆有爭議的刪除都必須留下解釋紀錄。 一所職業學校同意提供前港口倉場學徒自願參加的課程。 安溫在測量船棚旁做瑜伽，舒緩背痛。
