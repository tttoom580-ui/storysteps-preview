The Squall at North Gate

星曆失落的十七分鐘 · 第 20 / 25 章 · 5000–6000 · 英中對照

The squall arrived as the evening crews prepared to depart under the provisional schedule. Anwen saw the water rise faster than the forecast range and ordered the lookout to notify the safety officer. He issued a temporary closure and directed smaller craft toward the sheltered southern landing. Veyra was not in command. She watched the process she had helped design work without her personal signature. One boat's signal lamp failed, but the neighboring watch passed the warning by hand and the captain turned back before reaching the narrow bend. Both questions belonged at the hearing. The rescue plan followed the law in accordance with published emergency rules. Adolescence should not remove a young person's right to be heard. A caterpillar had taken shelter inside an abandoned classroom box. Anwen turned the wheel clockwise to tighten the survey boat chain carefully. The surveyor used a compass to check the marked rescue route.

傍晚船隊準備依暫行時刻出航時，突然颳起強烈驟風。安溫看見水位上升快於預測範圍，請瞭望員通知安全官。安全官宣布暫時封閘，引導小船前往有遮蔽的南方泊地。薇拉不是指揮官，只看著自己曾協助設計的流程在不需她親自蓋章下運作。一艘船信號燈失靈，鄰近值更員以人工方式轉遞警告，船長在狹彎前及時折返。 救援計畫依照公布的緊急規定執行。 青春期不應讓年輕人失去被傾聽的權利。 一隻毛毛蟲躲進廢棄教室的盒子裡。 安溫順時針轉動輪盤，小心拉緊測量船纜鏈。 測量員使用羅盤核對標出的救援路線。

The storm injured no one at the gate, but it damaged a measuring platform and left two fishing crews unable to reach the market. Veyra urged the committee to report those losses alongside the successful warning. A council member called such frankness politically foolish. She replied that the same system which exaggerated success would eventually conceal failure. When the weather cleared, inspectors found a corroded bracket on the platform, and they marked the instrument unfit for certification until repaired. The provisional procedure had worked once; it had not become flawless forever. The announcement described both the rescue and its limits. Veyra asked the officer not to contradict residents without checking their evidence. A dubious rumor claimed the navigation minister had ordered the snow itself. The new map could enhance the speed of medical deliveries. No powerful family was exempt from the shelter's safety rules. A horse tried to graze beside a snow-covered fence.

風暴沒有造成水閘傷亡，但破壞了一處測量平台，兩組漁船也錯過市場。薇拉敦促委員會把這些損失與預警成功一同公告。有議員說坦承在政治上愚蠢。她答，今天誇大成功的制度，明天就可能隱藏失敗。天氣轉晴後，稽查員發現平台支架腐蝕，將儀器標為修好之前不得核定。暫行程序成功一次，不代表此後永遠完美。 公告同時交代救援成果與侷限。 薇拉請官員不要未查證就反駁居民證據。 一則可疑謠言竟稱航務大臣下令讓雪降下。 新地圖可以提升醫療配送速度。 沒有權貴家庭能豁免避難所安全規則。 一匹馬試圖在積雪柵欄旁吃草。

A woman whose brother had died in last winter's collision came to the harbor wall and read the corrected notice. She thanked no official; she simply asked why the same precautions had not existed earlier. Veyra told her about the negligent editing policy and the separate investigation into deliberate time changes. The woman listened and said that explanations did not return the dead. Veyra agreed. She did not ask for forgiveness. She offered access to the compensation inquiry and promised that its outcome would be recorded even if it criticized her. Outside, the city continued its ordinary life. Veyra could not give certainty simply because uncertainty frightened them. Anwen had a hunch the survey boat chain needed another inspection. The nurse valued practical intellect as much as formal titles. The navigation minister invoked nationalism, but the station townrs demanded safe roads. A broken ornament lay near the temporary school entrance. The rescue crews offered a salute to volunteers at dawn.

一名兄長在去年撞船事故中喪生的女子來到港牆，讀了修訂公告。她沒有感謝官員，只問為何以前沒有相同防護。薇拉告訴她疏忽的補值規則，以及對刻意調時的另案調查。女子聽完說，解釋不能讓亡者回來。薇拉承認，不求原諒，只提供補償調查查閱權，承諾即使結果批評自己也會如實記錄。 窗外的城市依舊過著日常生活。 安溫直覺測量船纜鏈還需要檢查。 護士重視實際才智，不亞於正式頭銜。 航務大臣訴諸民族主義，站區居民卻要求安全道路。 一件破損飾品躺在臨時學校入口旁。 黎明時救援人員向志工致敬。

That night the council confirmed that the emergency route had met its stated conditions and set a date for retesting the failed platform. The minister's supporters tried to call the operation a staged spectacle. Nolin, who had turned back his vessel, replied that the current had been real and the lost income was real too. The committee entered both facts into the same report. For the first time since Leina arrived, Veyra understood that changing a story's ending might mean building a system that continued after the central character stepped aside. She was right. A sloppy delivery label had sent medicine to the wrong depot. One solitary traveler waited until she chose which shelter to enter. Bread was the staple food in several mountain shelters. The committee could summon more drivers if the weather improved. The sleeping children heard a volunteer tiptoe past their beds.

當晚議會確認緊急航路符合公告條件，並排定故障平台重測日期。大臣支持者稱整場行動是安排好的表演。親自折返的諾林說，水流是真的，失去的收入也是真的。委員會把兩者放進同一份報告。蕾娜來到此世界後第一次真正明白，改寫故事結局可能不是讓主角永遠站在中心，而是建造一套她退下後仍能運作的制度。 潦草的配送標籤把藥品送往錯誤轉運站。 一名獨行旅客等到自己選好避難所才入內。 麵包是幾處山地避難所的主食。 天氣改善後，委員會可以傳喚更多車夫協助。 熟睡的孩子聽見志工踮腳走過床邊。
