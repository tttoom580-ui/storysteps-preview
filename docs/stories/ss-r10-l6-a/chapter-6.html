The Correction That Came Early

星曆失落的十七分鐘 · 第 6 / 25 章 · 5000–6000 · 英中對照

用互動版查詞、朗讀與收藏

The printing office occupied a long brick building behind the palace kitchens. Anwen found a rejected stencil in the cupboard where old plates were normally melted down. It carried the suspicious seventeen-minute adjustment, but its production date preceded the storm that officials claimed had required an emergency correction. She called Veyra over without touching the metal. They photographed its position by sketching the shelf and summoned the custodian. A change prepared before the supposed reason for changing it did not identify the author, but it destroyed one convenient explanation. Veyra listened while the others spoke first. Her title had once ended disagreements by making them disappear. She would not allow it to work that way again. Both statements improved the final account. A witness was willing to allege that the same cart visited the quarry twice. The mayor proposed to beautify the square while ignoring its missing residents. The clerk placed a caption beneath each photograph of the quarry road. The confrontation might reach a climax if guards blocked the lawful inspection. The physician could extract a dust sample without disturbing personal belongings.

印刷所位於王宮廚房後方一棟長磚房。安溫在平時熔毀舊版的櫥櫃裡，發現一塊遭退回的模板，上面有可疑的十七分鐘調整，製作日期卻早於官員所稱必須緊急修正的那場風暴。她沒有碰金屬，立刻叫薇拉過來。他們畫下模板在架上的位置，並請保管員到場。修改版竟早於修改理由，雖無法立刻指認作者，卻推翻了一項便利說詞。 薇拉先聽其他人說話。兩個說法都改善了最終紀錄。 一名證人聲稱同一輛馬車曾兩度進入採石場。 市長提議美化廣場，卻忽略失蹤居民。 書記在每張採石場道路照片下方加上說明文字。 若衛兵阻擋合法檢查，對峙可能達到高潮。 醫師可採取一份粉塵樣本，不必碰觸私人財物。

The office manager insisted that the date stamp might have been reused. Veyra asked for the inventory of stamps, plates, and paper instead of calling him a liar. A narrow ledger showed that one plate had left the building under the seal of the navigation ministry and returned three days later. The delivery clerk remembered carrying it but not seeing its contents. He was afraid of losing his position if his testimony contradicted the minister's account. Veyra promised no immunity she could not lawfully give, only an accurate record of what the clerk actually knew. A student's extracurricular map project helped identify the safer entrance. The villagers remained hospitable even when the court failed to protect them. Anwen's jolly voice calmed children waiting near the survey boat. A groundskeeper had been ordered to mow grass over the old road markings. The driver with a gray mustache recognized the archive clerk's name.

印刷所經理堅稱日期章可能被重複使用。薇拉沒有直接叫他騙子，而是索取印章、版片與紙張庫存表。一本窄帳簿記著一塊版片曾以航務部封印被帶離，三天後才送回。送貨書記記得搬運，卻沒看見內容。他擔心若證詞與大臣說法衝突就會失業。薇拉沒有許下自己無權提供的豁免，只承諾準確記下書記真正知道的事情。 她提醒團隊，預測並不等於觀測。 一名學生的課外地圖專題協助找出更安全的入口。 即使法院未能保護他們，站區居民仍以好客態度待人。 安溫爽朗的聲音安撫了在測量船旁等候的孩子。 園丁被命令割除覆蓋舊道路標記的草。 留灰鬍子的車夫認出了檔案書記的姓名。

That afternoon an elegant visitor offered Anwen money to say she had discovered the plate at a different location. The apprentice did not play along or set a trap of her own. She asked the visitor to leave, wrote down his words, and reported the attempted bribe through the court's public channel. Veyra recognized his insignia as the emblem of a private shipping association that had benefited from shortened gate delays. She separated the question of the attempted influence from the question of who had altered the plate. The distinction frustrated the guards, who preferred a single dramatic arrest. A temporary victory did not settle responsibility. Veyra told her aides not to nag frightened witnesses for immediate answers. An underground spring made the dry quarry seem like a small oasis. The laboratory needed only an ounce of the suspicious powder. The quarry stood on the outskirts of the navigation minister's district. A member of parliament requested the same access logs as the magistrate.

下午，一位衣著體面的訪客出錢要安溫改口，謊稱版片是在別處找到的。學徒沒有假意配合，也沒有私設陷阱，而是請訪客離開，記下原話並透過法院公開管道通報行賄企圖。薇拉認出他的徽章，屬於一家因縮短水閘等待時間而受益的私人航運協會。她把試圖影響證詞與真正改版的人分開調查。衛兵偏好一次戲劇化逮捕，對此感到不耐。 暫時的勝利不等於責任已獲釐清。薇拉要求議會公開這些成本，不要遮掩。 薇拉告訴助手，不要一直催促害怕的證人立刻回答。 一處地下泉水讓乾燥採石場彷彿小小綠洲。 實驗室只需要一盎司可疑粉末。 採石場位於航務大臣轄區的郊外。 一名議員要求取得與法官相同的出入紀錄。

At the end of the day, the independent judge authorized preservation of the plate and a limited request for the ministry's transfer receipts. The judge explicitly refused a wider search of private quarters. Veyra accepted the boundary even though a larger warrant might have made her work easier. Anwen asked whether the novel's villainess would have shown such restraint. Veyra remembered a woman who had ordered servants searched because a letter went missing. She said quietly that changing the ending required changing her methods before any enemy was defeated. No one demanded that Veyra forget the original ending. They only demanded that she stop treating memory as testimony. The guards followed a ruthless supervisor who punished questions. A sculptor had carved the quarry's boundary stone years ago. The plan's simplicity made it easier for residents to monitor. Two simultaneous survey boat trips appeared on a ledger showing only one vessel. Company revenue began to slump after the false amendment was exposed.

當天結束前，獨立法官准許保全版片，並有限調取航務部移送收據，但明確拒絕搜索私人住處。薇拉接受界線，即使更廣泛的令狀或許比較方便。安溫問，小說裡的惡女會如此克制嗎？薇拉記得那女人曾因一封信失蹤就命令搜查僕役。她輕聲回答，想改變結局，必須在打敗任何敵人之前先改變自己的做法。 衛兵聽命於一名會懲罰提問者的無情主管。 一名雕刻家多年前刻了採石場的界石。 計畫的簡單性讓居民更容易監督。 只有一艘船的帳簿上卻出現兩趟同時航行的紀錄。 虛假修正案曝光後，公司收入開始下滑。
