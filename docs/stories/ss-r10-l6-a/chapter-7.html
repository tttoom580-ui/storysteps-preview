A Seal Turned Backward

星曆失落的十七分鐘 · 第 7 / 25 章 · 5000–6000 · 英中對照

用互動版查詞、朗讀與收藏

Jory found the gate's oldest sealing diagrams in a workshop drawer. The diagrams were stained and incomplete, but the direction of each lock was clear. On the day of last winter's collision, an inspector had described a seal fixed on the opposite side of its hinge. That could happen during maintenance, yet no maintenance order carried the required second signature. Veyra and Jory walked to the gate with two union observers. They touched nothing until the custodian recorded every mark, because their own fingerprints would otherwise confuse the examination. Now their names were returned to the page. The quarry covered several acre plots of disputed farmland. A bachelor worker had no family contact on his registration card. The guards had arranged boxing matches to settle disputes over rations. The manager threatened disciplinary punishment for anyone who requested a lawyer. An emigrant planned to settle abroad once his identity papers were restored.

喬里在工坊抽屜裡找到水閘最舊的封印圖。圖紙有污漬且不完整，但每把鎖的方向清楚。去年冬天撞船那天，稽查員記錄封印固定在鉸鏈相反側。維修時可能如此，但沒有任何維修命令具備必要的第二簽名。薇拉、喬里和兩名工會觀察員走到水閘，在保管人記下每道痕跡前誰也不碰設備，避免自己的指紋干擾檢驗。如今他們的名字重新回到頁面。 採石場涵蓋好幾英畝有爭議的農地。 一名單身漢工人的登記卡上沒有緊急聯絡親屬。 衛兵曾安排拳擊比賽來解決配給爭執。 經理威脅，要對要求律師的人施以紀律處分。 一名移居國外的人打算身分文件恢復後在海外定居。

The minister's engineer claimed that wear in the mechanism explained the change. Jory agreed that metal could deform, then demonstrated a feature the engineer had overlooked: a small groove beneath the lock remained straight. Someone had removed and refitted the seal rather than forcing it open. The discovery supported interference but not yet a person's identity. Veyra asked the engineer to write his alternative explanation in full. He was surprised to find she intended to include it in the public report. Good evidence became stronger when honest objections could be tested. Veyra knew how tempting a clean story could be. Veyra refused to equate missing documents with criminal conduct. The youngest worker began to falter after a long day in the dust. A physician warned that polluted water could harm future fertility. Talen would not forsake workers who still wished to remain employed. A gay worker asked for equal treatment of his chosen family contact.

大臣的工程師宣稱機械磨損足以解釋封印變動。喬里承認金屬可能變形，接著指出對方忽略的一道鎖下細槽：凹槽仍筆直，顯示有人拆下封印再裝回，而不是硬撬。這證實設備曾受干預，卻仍不能指認個人。薇拉要求工程師完整寫下其他解釋，對方訝異她竟要把它一起收入公開報告。誠實反對意見若能接受檢驗，反而使證據更堅固。薇拉知道一個乾淨整齊的故事多麼誘人。 薇拉拒絕把文件遺失等同犯罪行為。 最年輕的工人在粉塵裡工作一整天後開始站不穩。 醫師警告污染的水可能傷害日後生育能力。 塔倫不會拋棄仍想繼續工作的工人。 一名同志工人要求平等承認他選擇的家人聯絡人。

The union observers brought a separate difficulty. Dockhands had often been ordered to work beside the gate while its warning bell failed, because halting the channel would cost the ministry fees. One worker showed an unsigned complaint from two months earlier. It named no villain, only a dangerous practice. Veyra asked the union to decide whether to submit the complaint and whether any worker needed confidential protection. She had previously treated workers as instruments for carrying out an order. Now she had to let them decide how much risk they wished to undertake. His first drafts had once been neat enough to conceal confusion. An atmosphere of gloom filled the room until the documents were returned. A cart waited at the intersection of the eastern road and quarry track. An old landslide had made the northern escape trail dangerous. The doctor arranged meals to nourish workers recovering from illness. The manager invoked an orthodox reading of a rule that had been revised.

工會觀察員提出另一個難題：工人常在警鈴故障時被命令靠近水閘作業，因為停航會讓部會損失規費。一名工人拿出兩個月前未具名的投訴，沒有指控某個惡人，只描述危險作法。薇拉請工會自行決定是否呈交，以及證人是否需要保密保障。她過去把工人當成執行命令的工具，如今必須讓他們決定願意承擔多大風險。他把廢棄計算放入信封，而不是燒掉。 文件歸還前，房間裡始終瀰漫陰鬱氣氛。 一輛馬車停在東路與採石場小徑的交叉口。 一場舊山崩讓北方撤離步道變得危險。 醫師安排餐食來滋養病後復原的工人。 經理援引一條已修訂規則的正統解釋。

In the workshop ledger, Anwen found an entry for a replacement part that had never been installed. The invoice listed a private contractor connected to the same shipping association as the attempted bribe. There was now a possible route from money to machinery, but too many gaps remained for an accusation. Jory marked the parts that could still be tested and locked the workshop under joint custody. The gate reopened only under the revised safety limits, and no official was allowed to call the unresolved inquiry finished. Veyra separated them aloud. The labor code contained a prohibition against retaining identity documents. The inspector approved provisional shelter until safe travel could be arranged. The workers reached a unanimous decision to elect Talen as spokesperson. Veyra understood that personal vanity was an obstacle to accepting criticism. A woodpecker knocked against a dead pine beyond the outer fence.

安溫在工坊帳本裡找到一筆替換零件紀錄，那零件其實從未安裝；發票供應商與試圖行賄的航運協會有關。金錢與機械之間終於出現可能路徑，但要指控誰仍有太多缺口。喬里標出可檢測零件，讓工會與法院共同封存工坊。水閘只能在修訂安全限制下重新運作，沒有官員有權把未結案調查說成已完成。 勞動法規禁止扣留身分文件。 稽查員批准臨時住處，直到能安排安全交通。 工人一致決定選塔倫為發言人。 薇拉明白個人虛榮會阻礙接受批評。 一隻啄木鳥在外圍柵欄後敲擊枯松。
