三座鐘與一片雲

Three Clocks and a Cloud

The royal laboratory offered to recalibrate every station clock against a single magnificent instrument in the palace. Hera objected that such a scheme would make the same possible error spread through the entire network. Veyra sided with her despite the insult to royal prestige. They designed a comparison using the hill shadow dial, Jory's mechanical clock, and a coastal station's independent bell. The first night brought dense cloud and no visible stars. Anwen refused to fill the blank with a value borrowed from the central table, even when the laboratory supervisor complained about the delay. Those protections mattered. An accumulation of unmatched receipts made the official count less believable. The witnesses were offered a warm beverage before the hearing began. A blond courier carried the eastern map to the court office. Only a qualified officer could certify the safety of that route. The judge required written clearance before any further transport.

王家實驗室提出以宮內一座華麗儀器統一校準所有站鐘。赫拉反對，因為如此可能把同一個錯誤散播到整個網絡。薇拉不顧損及王室威信，支持她。團隊改用山站日影盤、喬里的機械鐘及海岸站獨立鐘鈴比較。第一晚雲層濃厚，看不見星星。安溫拒絕用中央表格的數值填補空白，即使實驗室監督抱怨進度落後。 未配對的收據逐漸累積，讓官方統計更不可信。 聽證前，證人得到一杯溫熱飲料。 一名金髮信差把東路地圖送到法院辦公室。 只有合格官員能證明該路線安全。 法官要求後續運輸必須先取得書面許可。

At midday the following day, a narrow interval of sunlight allowed Hera to measure the shadow on her calibrated stone. The other teams kept their clocks untouched until the comparison was complete. Jory calculated a drift range rather than a single perfect correction. His clock was old and reliable within limits, not immortal. Veyra asked each group to explain whether the measurement was direct, calculated, or copied. For the first time, palace officials heard a royal supervisor accept an answer that included the words, 'We do not know exactly.' The sealed copies travelled by different routes. The company could not deduct food charges without a valid agreement. No child was destined to work in the dockyards simply because of birthplace. Veyra refused to exert private pressure on the independent engineer. The rescuers rejected heroic publicity until every worker was accounted for. A lieutenant described the guard deployment but could not explain the altered map.

隔天中午，一小段陽光讓赫拉在校準石盤上量到日影。其他小組直到比對完成都不碰自己的鐘。喬里計算出一個漂移範圍，而非假裝得到完美修正。他的鐘古老且在一定限制內可靠，卻不是永恆不壞。薇拉要各組說明資料是直接測得、推算或抄錄。宮廷官員第一次聽見王家監督接受含有「我們無法完全確定」的答案。 公司不能在沒有有效協議時扣除餐費。 沒有孩子注定只因出生地就得在港口倉場工作。 薇拉拒絕私下對獨立工程師施壓。 救援者在每位工人獲清點前拒絕英雄式宣傳。 一名中尉描述衛兵部署，卻無法解釋改過的地圖。

The results complicated Veyra's favorite theory. Two clocks showed ordinary wear that accounted for several minutes of error, while a third showed a separate, abrupt adjustment. She had hoped for one neat cause. The data described at least two. Anwen feared the public would interpret the admission as failure. Veyra answered that an investigation built around a single villain could become another misleading story. They wrote the mechanical drift and the unexplained adjustment on different lines, then asked an independent specialist to review both. No convenient apology would be allowed to replace either obligation. The court ensured that a literate witness could read statements aloud to others. Lotus flowers grew along the shallow water beside Hollow Ford. The company called the route's danger marginal, but the engineer disagreed. Two official boundaries seemed to overlap on the disputed survey. A provincial inspector was assigned to compare the road and its drawing.

結果使薇拉最喜歡的理論變得複雜。兩座鐘的普通磨損可解釋幾分鐘差距，第三座鐘卻有另一次突兀調整。她曾希望只有一個整齊原因，資料卻顯示至少兩個。安溫擔心公眾會把坦承視為失敗。薇拉說，若調查只想找到一個惡人，也可能變成另一個誤導故事。他們把機械漂移與不明調整分列兩行，交由獨立專家檢視。 法院安排識字的證人朗讀陳述給其他人聽。 空谷渡旁的淺水處長著蓮花。 公司稱路線風險微不足道，工程師卻不同意。 爭議測量圖上有兩條官方海岸邊界似乎重疊。 一名地方稽查員受派比較道路與圖面。

The laboratory's director tried to claim the experiment as a triumph of central authority. Hera politely corrected him in front of the council: the result was valuable because the clocks had remained independent. Veyra added that the test did not yet settle the disputed seventeen minutes. It merely ruled out several harmless explanations. The director left without offering congratulations. On the steps, Anwen laughed for the first time in days. She said that an honest uncertainty sounded less frightening once everyone could see how it was measured. The apprentice did not ask for a heroic speech. That practical question changed the discussion. The driver's qualification had expired before the last transport. A volunteer brought refreshments to those waiting for their documents. The court ordered officers to retrieve the original route approval. The old trail sank into a swamp beyond the eastern bridge. A worker said his vocation was carpentry, not forced mining.

實驗室主任想把試驗稱為中央權力的勝利。赫拉在議會面前禮貌更正：結果之所以有價值，正因為鐘保持獨立。薇拉補充，試驗尚未解決十七分鐘爭議，只是排除了幾項無害解釋。主任沒有道賀就走了。階梯上，安溫數日來首次笑出聲。她說，當人人都能看見不確定是如何測量時，它聽起來反而沒有那麼可怕。這個務實問題改變了討論。 車夫的資格在上次運輸前已過期。 志工送茶點給等候領回文件的人。 法院命令官員取回原始路線核准書。 舊步道在東橋外陷入沼澤。 一名工人說自己的志業是木工，不是被迫採礦。
