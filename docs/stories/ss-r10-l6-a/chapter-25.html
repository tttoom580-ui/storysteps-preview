天空不會替人簽名

The Sky Does Not Sign Itself

星曆失落的十七分鐘 · 第 25 / 25 章 · 5000–6000 · 英中對照

The first corrected almanac reached the harbor at the start of spring. Its cover was plain, and its pages carried more explanations than the splendid royal edition. Named observers signed the relevant measurements, uncertain intervals appeared openly, and every revised time referred to an earlier version. Veyra stood beside Anwen as sailors turned the pages. Some complained that the book was harder to read. Anwen showed them the brief guide printed inside, where the main warning and the route to an appeal were set out in simple language. No promise claimed the sea would obey the paper. She was right. Amid the cheering, some families quietly prepared their remaining appeals. There was anticipation before the panel read its final judgment. A young apprentice offered to repair the sign beside the survey boat junction. The prosecution did not allege that anyone had tried to assassinate the monarch. The community's backbone was the network of people keeping independent records.

春初，第一本修訂星曆送到港口。封面素樸，頁裡的說明卻比華麗王家舊版多。負責觀測者在相應數據旁簽名，不確定區間明列，每處修訂都能追溯前版。薇拉與安溫站在翻書船員旁。一些人抱怨新書難懂，安溫便指給他們看內附簡明指南，主要警告與申訴途徑都以白話列出。沒有人承諾海洋會服從紙張。 在歡呼聲中，有些家庭仍安靜準備尚未解決的申訴。 審判團宣讀最終判決前，大家充滿期待。 一名年輕學徒自願修復碼頭通道交叉處的路牌。 檢方並未指稱有人企圖刺殺君主。 社群的支柱，是持續保存獨立紀錄的人際網絡。

Jory arrived with a small brass clock that had been repaired for the apprentice workshop. He offered it to the observatory, but only if the students were permitted to inspect its gears and record every adjustment. Veyra accepted the condition. In the old administration, a royal instrument had been treated as too sacred for ordinary hands to question. Now the apprentices learned its limits by taking it apart and putting it together again. Anwen taught the first lesson, and Veyra watched from the back row without claiming that the idea had been hers. Both questions belonged at the hearing. The clerks could compute corrected totals without exposing private family details. A medical examiner confirmed that a recovered corpse belonged to a missing worker. The injured carpenter no longer needed a crutch after months of treatment. The court reviewed every detention order issued during the forced transfers. People could dwell in seasonal houses without losing legal recognition.

喬里帶著一座修給學徒工坊的小黃銅鐘抵達。他願意捐給天文台，條件是學生可檢視齒輪、記錄每次調整。薇拉同意。舊制度把王家儀器看得神聖，普通人連質疑都不行；如今學徒藉由拆解、組裝學習它的界限。安溫主持第一課，薇拉坐在後排看，沒有把點子算在自己頭上。 書記可以計算更正總數，而不公開私人家庭細節。 法醫確認一具尋獲遺體屬於失蹤工人。 受傷木匠經過數月治療後不再需要拐杖。 法院複查強制轉移期間核發的每道拘留令。 人們可以居住在季節性家屋，仍保有法律承認。

The compensation office continued its work, and the dismissed minister began an appeal. Veyra did not announce that every danger was over. She had declined an invitation to reclaim sole command of the observatory, choosing instead a research role subject to independent review. The court's censure remained in her record, as did her testimony and her efforts to repair the system. When a young sailor asked if she believed the new calculations, she replied that he should learn how to check them. A trustworthy book was one that welcomed the next reader's question. The announcement described both the rescue and its limits. A frantic messenger arrived with the last unanswered property appeal. The town hostel offered rooms to witnesses traveling for the appeal. A knowledgeable elder checked the spelling of traditional mountain names. The new road rules forbade travel over a bridge with lethal damage. A lizard basked on the warm stone outside the restored records office.

補償處仍在辦案，被撤職的大臣也開始上訴。薇拉沒有宣布危險全都結束。她拒絕重新獨掌天文台，選擇接受獨立審查的研究職務。法院申誡仍留在她的人事紀錄裡，證詞和修補制度的努力也一樣。年輕水手問她是否相信新計算，她說他應該學會自己查核。值得信任的書，是歡迎下一位讀者提問的書。 公告同時交代救援成果與侷限。 一名焦急信差帶來最後一件尚未答覆的產權申訴。 鎮上旅舍為前來申訴的證人提供房間。 一名知識豐富的長老檢查山地傳統地名的拼字。 新道路規則禁止通行有致命損傷的橋梁。 一隻蜥蜴在修復的檔案室外暖石上曬太陽。

At sunset the harbor bells sounded across the water, each from a station that kept its own log. Leina no longer waited for the cruel final sentence she remembered from the novel. She had not become free by making the world agree with her; she had helped make disagreement possible without destroying a life. The missing seventeen minutes had been found, explained, and recorded, yet the larger discovery lay elsewhere. The sky had never signed the almanac. People had, and people could be required to show their work. Veyra closed the final page and went to meet the new observers. Outside, the city continued its ordinary life. Veyra could not give certainty simply because uncertainty frightened them. The remainder of unresolved cases would remain eligible for further review. The scale of the deception could stun anyone who saw the ledgers. Successive reviews would test whether the new registry stayed fair. Recovered funds returned to the public treasury under court supervision. A faint vibration came from the survey boat engine as the evening boat departed. The teacher showed children how one changed vowel could alter a place name.

日落時，各站鐘聲越過海面，每個站都保存自己的紀錄。蕾娜不再等待記憶中小說殘酷的結尾。她的自由不是讓全世界同意她，而是幫助異議得以存在、不必毀掉一個人。消失的十七分鐘終於被找回、解釋、記錄，但真正發現的事更大：天空從未在星曆上簽名；是人簽的，人也能被要求交出計算過程。薇拉合上最後一頁，去迎接新一批觀測者。 窗外的城市依舊過著日常生活。 其餘未解案件仍可繼續申請複查。 看見帳冊的人都可能被欺騙規模震驚。 接連的複查將檢驗新航海紀錄是否持續公平。 追回款項在法院監督下回到公共國庫。 傍晚測量船啟航時，引擎傳來輕微震動。 老師示範一個母音改變如何讓地名不同。
