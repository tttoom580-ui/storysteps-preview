檔案室裡的手

A Hand in the Archive

星曆失落的十七分鐘 · 第 18 / 25 章 · 5000–6000 · 英中對照

用互動版查詞、朗讀與收藏

Near midnight a guard heard movement in the observatory archive. He found an intruder trying to open the cabinet containing the old central-clock readings. The intruder fled when challenged, leaving a damaged lock but taking no original book. Veyra arrived after the room had been secured. She ordered nobody to invent a face from a glimpse in poor light. Instead they documented the lock, interviewed the guard separately, and asked the court to preserve the corridor entry records. The attempted intrusion showed that someone wanted the evidence moved, not yet who had sent them. No convenient apology would be allowed to replace either obligation. The witness asked for awhile to collect himself before questioning. Iven's account might broaden the inquiry beyond the records office. A storm warning covered the cape near the safe survey boat passage. Veyra refused to treat a frightened witness as a captive. A school exercise asked children to identify each consonant in a place name.

午夜前後，一名衛兵聽見天文台檔案室有動靜，發現闖入者正試圖打開存放中央鐘舊讀數的櫃子。對方被喝止後逃走，留下損壞的鎖，但沒有帶走正本。薇拉在房間封鎖後才到場，命令眾人別憑昏暗光線中的一瞥編造面孔。他們記下鎖痕，分別詢問衛兵，並請法院保全走廊出入紀錄。入侵顯示有人想移動證據，還不能證明是誰派來。 證人要求稍待片刻，好在受詢前整理情緒。 艾文的陳述可能把調查擴大到檔案室以外。 風暴警報涵蓋安全測量船航線附近的岬角。 薇拉拒絕把害怕的證人當成囚犯。 一項學校練習要孩子辨認地名中的每個子音。

The archive officer feared that the only proof had nearly vanished. Anwen reminded him that the hill station, western lighthouse, and court already held independently sealed copies. Their existence did not make the intrusion harmless, but it prevented the loss of one cabinet from ending the inquiry. Jory inspected the damaged hinge and described what sort of tool might have touched it. He did not try to match it to an employee's toolbox without a proper warrant. Veyra supported that restraint despite pressure from the crowd gathering outside the gate. The apprentice did not ask for a heroic speech. That practical question changed the discussion. The navigation minister could not dictate what Iven was permitted to remember. A dwarf pine grew beside the hospital annex's side door. The examiner read an excerpt from the notebook without exposing unrelated names. The unknown intruder left before guards could record a face. The deputy called the inquiry mischievous but did not answer the key question.

檔案官害怕唯一證據幾乎消失。安溫提醒，山站、西方燈塔與法院早有各自封存的副本。這不代表入侵無害，卻讓一個櫃子的損失無法結束調查。喬里檢查受損鉸鏈，描述可能留下痕跡的工具類型，但沒有在缺少令狀時隨意搜查員工工具箱。即使門外群眾催促，薇拉仍支持他的克制。這個務實問題改變了討論。 航務大臣不能命令艾文只記得哪些事。 醫院附樓側門旁長著一棵矮松。 鑑查員朗讀筆記摘錄，沒有公開無關姓名。 不明入侵者在衛兵記下容貌前就離開。 副手稱調查是胡鬧，卻未回答鑰匙問題。

A second complication appeared when Silas checked the messenger registry. Someone had requested the archive cabinet's location under the name of a junior court officer who had been ill that day. The forged request used the correct office stamp but lacked the officer's characteristic signature. This supported an impersonation, not necessarily a conspiracy directed by the minister. Veyra asked the registrar to identify every person who could access the stamp. The list included palace servants, clerks, and several contractors; its breadth warned against choosing the most convenient suspect. Those protections mattered. The court's operative rule required security arrangements to be documented discreetly. Iven's ordeal had not made every statement he offered automatically correct. The company tried to label anyone helping him an outlaw. Anwen felt a sharp pinch when she tightened a loose safety strap. A written summons must precede questioning of Pell's deputy.

賽拉斯檢查信差登記簿時，發現另一問題。有人冒用一名當天生病的初級法院官員姓名，詢問檔案櫃位置。假請求用了正確公章，卻沒有官員慣用簽名。這支持冒名行為，仍未必證明大臣指使。薇拉請登記官列出所有可接觸公章的人，名單包含宮廷僕役、書記與多名承包商；範圍太廣，提醒眾人不能選最方便的嫌疑人。 法院現行生效的規定要求低調記錄安全安排。 艾文遭受的磨難不代表他的每項陳述都自動正確。 公司試圖把幫助他的人都稱為亡命之徒。 安溫拉緊鬆掉的安全帶時感到一陣刺痛。 書面傳票必須先於對佩爾副手的訊問。

At dawn, Veyra met the archive staff and apologized for a policy she had enforced months earlier: keys had been held by one supervisor, leaving ordinary custodians unable to monitor access. She proposed shared custody, two signatures for removal, and a public record of each inspection. The staff approved the change only after insisting that visitors' private information be protected. Veyra accepted their amendment. The system would not become trustworthy merely because a new heroine stood at its center; it needed rules strong enough to restrain her as well. The sealed copies travelled by different routes. The restoration of public trust would require more than a single arrest. The records statute required a dated signature for exceptional access keys. An approaching tornado was only a rumor, but the forecast warned of violent wind. A wholesale accusation against all clerks would hide individual responsibility. The technician used a lens to zoom in on the duplicate key's serial mark.

黎明時，薇拉向檔案職員道歉。數月前她執行的政策把鑰匙集中在單一監督手裡，使一般保管員無法監督進出。她提出共同保管、取出文件雙簽名與公開檢視記錄。職員同意，但要求保護訪客私密資料。薇拉接受修正。制度不會因站上新的女主角就變可信，它還需要足以約束女主角本人的規則。 重建公眾信任需要的不只一次逮捕。 檔案法規要求例外存取鑰匙必須有日期與簽名。 龍捲風將至還只是傳聞，預報確實警告強風。 對所有書記的全面指控會掩蓋個別責任。 技術員用鏡頭放大複製鑰匙的序號標記。
