提早開閘的價格

The Price of Early Water

星曆失落的十七分鐘 · 第 11 / 25 章 · 5000–6000 · 英中對照

Silas returned from the treasury with a ledger of channel fees. Veyra expected proof of simple theft, but the figures were more complicated. The state had collected ordinary fees on delayed ships, while a private contractor received bonuses whenever a favored vessel passed ahead of the published opening time. A payment did not establish that anyone forged an astronomical record. Yet the dates matched two of the unexplained edits. She asked the finance office to certify the ledger and disclose the contract's amendment history instead of letting angry sailors seize the contractor's warehouse. No one demanded that Veyra forget the original ending. They only demanded that she stop treating memory as testimony. The room stayed below ten degrees Celsius to protect fragile documents. There was friction between the navigation minister's agents and the court custodians. A guard began to growl threats until the magistrate entered. The order could induce fear without granting any lawful power. Several families inhabit the houses marked vacant in the logbook.

賽拉斯從國庫帶回航道收費帳簿。薇拉原以為會看到簡單的偷竊證據，數字卻更複雜：國家照常收取延航船的費用，特定承包商則在受偏愛船舶提早通行時領到獎金。款項本身不能證明天文紀錄被偽造，但日期恰好吻合兩處不明修改。她請財務處核證帳本，公開契約修訂歷史，而不是放任憤怒船員衝進承包商倉庫。 室內維持在攝氏十度以下，以保護脆弱文件。 航務大臣代理人與法院保管員之間出現摩擦。 一名衛兵低聲咆哮威脅，直到法官進來。 這道命令可能引起恐懼，卻未授予合法權力。 好幾戶人家仍住在航海紀錄標為空置的房屋裡。

The contractor's accountant offered a plausible explanation: early departures reduced congestion, so the bonuses rewarded efficiency. Nolin replied that hidden priority made every other captain's timetable unreliable. Veyra refused to decide the legal question in a shouting match. She asked the accountant how bonus eligibility was calculated, who supplied the time stamp, and whether the time could be corrected after payment. His answer led to a private memorandum signed by the navigation minister's secretary. It described an instruction to accept a palace clock reading even when the coastal gauge disagreed. Veyra listened while the others spoke first. Her title had once ended disagreements by making them disappear. She would not allow it to work that way again. Both statements improved the final account. It was ironic that a records office was accused of preserving records. A small mound of rejected paper lay beside the binding table. A peacock wandered through the navigation minister's garden outside the archive. A visiting pianist volunteered to carry meals to the tired clerks. The examiner would probe the paper fibers before naming their origin.

承包商會計提出看似合理的解釋：提早出航減少壅塞，獎金是為了效率。諾林回應，秘密優先權會使其他船長的時刻表失去可靠性。薇拉拒絕在吵架中裁決法律問題，而是問會計獎金如何計算、誰提供時間戳、付款後能否修改。他的回答引出一份由航務大臣祕書簽署的私人備忘錄，其中指示即使海岸水位計不符，也要採用王宮鐘的時間。 薇拉先聽其他人說話。兩個說法都改善了最終紀錄。 檔案室竟因保存檔案受到指控，實在諷刺。 裝訂桌旁堆著一小堆被退回的紙張。 一隻孔雀在檔案室外的航務大臣花園徘徊。 一名來訪鋼琴家自願替疲倦書記送餐。 鑑查員會先探查紙張纖維，再判斷來源。

Hera examined the memorandum without announcing that it was genuine. The signature shape was familiar but a copy could deceive. The paper's watermark matched the ministry's supply order, and a dispatch book contained a corresponding outgoing number. Three clues now supported the document's origin, but its meaning remained disputed. The minister could have demanded a standardized time without asking anyone to falsify observations. Veyra drew two columns, one for what the memorandum said and the other for what different witnesses believed it meant. She allowed both columns to remain incomplete. Residents and officials kept their respective copies in separate rooms. It was shortsighted to trade permanent evidence for temporary convenience. Silas tried to simplify the inventory without omitting any page numbers. The coastal strait remained open for lawful survey boat traffic that evening. A superficial comparison would miss the mismatched thread holes.

赫拉查看備忘錄，沒有立刻宣布真偽。簽名筆形熟悉，影本卻可能誤導；紙張浮水印符合部會供應批次，發文簿也有相對應的發出編號。三項線索支持文件出處，但意義仍有爭議。大臣可能只是要求統一時間，未必命人造假。薇拉畫兩欄，一欄記文件原文，另一欄記各證人對它的理解，容許兩欄保持未完成狀態。 她提醒團隊，預測並不等於觀測。 居民與官員把各自的副本放在不同房間。 為了短暫方便而犧牲永久證據，是目光短淺的作法。 賽拉斯試圖簡化清單，同時保留所有頁碼。 當晚沿海海峽仍開放合法測量船通行。 表面的比較會錯過不相符的線孔。

At the end of the hearing, workers asked whether they would recover lost wages. Veyra told them that the bonus inquiry might identify a responsible payer, but it could not calculate individual compensation without payroll evidence. The union volunteered copies rather than surrendering its original books to the ministry. Silas arranged independent custodians and a date for questions. The minister's supporters called this delay weakness. Veyra remembered how quickly the fictional court had moved from an accusation to a sentence. She preferred a slower record that could survive an appeal. A temporary victory did not settle responsibility. Veyra refused to suppress records merely because they embarrassed the region. The false pages disturbed the symmetry of the original binding. Each custodian received a token proving the exact package entrusted to them. The communities agreed to unify their evidence without merging private identities. The urgency of the deadline could not excuse an unlawful seizure.

聽證會結束時，工人問能否追回失去的工資。薇拉說，獎金調查可能找出支付責任者，但沒有薪資紀錄就無法計算個別補償。工會自願提供副本，不肯把正本交給部會。賽拉斯安排獨立保管與提問日期。大臣支持者罵拖延是軟弱，薇拉想起小說法院從指控走到判刑有多快。她寧願記錄慢一些，卻能經得起上訴。 暫時的勝利不等於責任已獲釐清。薇拉要求議會公開這些成本，不要遮掩。 薇拉拒絕只因紀錄讓本省難堪就加以壓制。 假頁面破壞了原始裝訂的對稱性。 每名保管員都領到一枚憑證，證明受託保管的確切包裹。 社群同意整合證據，但不合併私人身分資料。 期限再緊迫，也不能成為非法扣押的藉口。
