She invited the pamphlet writer to the public observation room and showed him the empty column. The missing reading had been labeled missing, not fabricated. 第二個多雲夜晚使西方燈塔比較計畫受阻。大臣的小冊子把看不到星空說成薇拉調查依賴迷信的證明。她請作者到公開觀測室，看那個空白欄；缺讀就是缺讀，沒有人捏造。鐘在雲下照樣運轉，但準確度取決於先前校準與已知漂移。團隊準備用隔天的日影測量檢驗累積誤差。 數學呈現的是範圍，不是保證。三人的任務互有交集，卻不完全相同。 地圖更動可能讓家庭與自己的歷史疏離。 複製地圖上的一團墨漬遮住了舊地名。 一碗辣豆燉菜讓受寒的船員暖和起來。 消失的地名切斷新舊地址紀錄的連續性。 測量船票的累計總數揭露多次未記錄的渡河。

The lighthouse keeper explained that navigators rarely trusted one signal alone. They read the beacon, estimated the current, listened for the fog bell, and checked the ship's own instruments. The almanac had once been treated as a command instead of one source among several. Veyra asked whether the lighthouse log could be copied and sealed before ministry couriers arrived. The keeper agreed only after the station committee approved. That small delay mattered: it meant the observers were not changing masters, merely transferring obedience from the minister to Veyra. The rule was deliberately modest. Neither procedure gave its author permission to destroy an inconvenient original. Anwen made a decisive turn before the boat reached the reeds. The ministry considered the old landing dispensable, but villagers did not. A woman sold jasmine flowers near the historic school gate. The inspector asked to lodge the wet photographs in a dry evidence room. A fallen maple branch had struck the boat's steering cable.

燈塔守護人解釋，航海者很少只信一個訊號。他們看燈、估水流、聽霧鐘，再核對船上儀器。星曆過去被當成命令，而不是多個資料來源之一。薇拉想在部會信差到達前複製燈塔紀錄，守護人要站區委員會批准才同意。這點延遲很重要：觀測者不是單純換主人，不是把服從大臣改成服從薇拉。 安溫在船抵達蘆葦前果斷轉彎。 部會認為舊碼頭可有可無，站區居民卻不這樣想。 一名婦人在舊校門旁販賣茉莉花。 稽查員要求把濕照片存放在乾燥證物室。 一根倒下的楓樹枝撞上船的操舵纜線。

At the next clear interval, Anwen measured the shadow while Hera checked the instrument's orientation. Their result fell within the calculated range for two clocks but outside the interval printed in the royal book. The discrepancy pointed toward an editorial intervention rather than cloudy weather. Still, a witness might have copied a wrong number without knowing. Veyra ordered the team to identify the last verified original and reconstruct each hand that touched the next revision. She refused to call the vanished minutes supernatural simply because she had entered this world through a book. Its worn gear kept a physical history of adjustments. He described each mark without announcing a culprit. A former tavern mistress knew which ferrymen worked during winter. Veyra refused to mobilize armed guards without a specific danger. The historian began to muse on how names survived in songs. The families' persistence had preserved the crossing's forgotten name. A socialist organizer asked how survey boat ownership could serve ordinary workers.

下一段晴朗時刻，安溫量日影，赫拉核對儀器方向。結果落在兩座鐘預估範圍內，卻超出王家星曆印出的區間。差距更像編輯介入，而非雲層造成。但仍可能有人不知情抄錯數字。薇拉要求確認最後一份已核定原件，再重建之後每次傳遞與修改。她拒絕僅因自己穿書，就把消失的分鐘稱為超自然現象。磨損齒輪留下調校的物理痕跡。他描述每道痕跡，卻不急著指認犯人。 一名曾經營酒館的女老闆知道冬季有哪些測量船夫值班。 薇拉拒絕在沒有明確危險時動員武裝衛兵。 史家開始沉思地名如何透過歌曲流傳。 家庭的堅持保存了那處被遺忘的碼頭通道名稱。 一名社會主義組織者詢問測量船所有權如何服務一般工人。

A message arrived from the palace: unless she publicly apologized for undermining confidence in the royal almanac, her title and salary would be suspended. Veyra replied that confidence required accurate information and visible corrections, not ceremonial praise. She offered to explain the current limits on a public platform, including her own former authorization of unsafe editing shortcuts. Anwen wondered whether the confession would hand the minister a weapon. Veyra answered that the weapon already existed; hiding it would only make the next person easier to hurt. The meeting gave residents time to socialize before formal questions began. The old timber bridge could span the creek but not carry heavy machinery. The inspection team completed its trek over muddy ground before sunset. Talen offered a vigorous defense of the workers' right to leave. The historian stored his notes in a waterproof case.

王宮來訊：若薇拉不為動搖王家星曆信任公開道歉，頭銜與薪俸將停用。她回覆，真正的信任依靠準確資訊與可見更正，不靠典禮上的讚美。她願意公開說明現行侷限，包括自己曾批准危險的編輯捷徑。安溫擔心這會把武器交到大臣手中。薇拉答，武器原本就存在；若藏起來，只會讓下一個人更容易受傷。 正式提問前，集會讓居民有時間交流。 舊木橋可橫跨小溪，卻無法承載重型機具。 檢查隊在日落前走完泥地長途跋涉。 塔倫有力地捍衛工人離開的權利。 史家把筆記放進防水盒。
