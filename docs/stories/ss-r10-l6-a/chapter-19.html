The independent finance auditor finally matched the gate bonus ledger with the contractor's departure slips. Several private vessels had crossed during the disputed interval while ordinary ships were ordered to wait. The same seventeen-minute adjustment appeared in the contractor's office timebook. Veyra asked the auditor to establish whether the figures had been copied from the royal clock or supplied to it. The answer mattered: shared numbers could reflect obedience to an official notice, or they could reveal a coordinated attempt to alter that notice. A beneficiary was not automatically an author. Its worn gear kept a physical history of adjustments. He described each mark without announcing a culprit. The town agreed to fortify the survey boat shelter against the forecast wind. The navigation minister held a glamorous reception while displaced families waited outside. Investigators could infer a possible link but still needed direct proof. The witness's careful intonation helped the interpreter preserve his meaning. An unsigned order was invariably questioned by the court officers.

獨立財務稽核員終於把水閘獎金帳本與承包商出航單對上。數艘私人船在爭議時段通過，普通船卻被命令等待。承包商辦公室的時間簿也有相同十七分鐘調整。薇拉請稽核員查明數字是從王家鐘抄來，還是反過來提供給王家鐘。答案很重要：共同數字可能是服從公告，也可能表示有人協調改寫公告。受益者不必然就是作者。磨損齒輪留下調校的物理痕跡。他描述每道痕跡，卻不急著指認犯人。 城鎮同意加固測量船避難所，以抵禦預報的強風。 流離家庭在外等候時，航務大臣卻舉辦華麗招待會。 調查員可以推論可能有關聯，但仍需要直接證據。 證人謹慎的語調協助翻譯保留原意。 沒有簽名的命令總會受到法院官員質疑。

A former dispatcher agreed to testify after the court promised protection against retaliation, not immunity for his own actions. He said the shipping association had complained repeatedly that strict gate windows delayed expensive cargo. A ministry official then instructed clerks to accept a revised palace time during selected private departures. The dispatcher had carried the message but claimed he never touched the clock. His account fit the financial pattern, although the court still needed to verify the message and distinguish his knowledge from hearsay. Anwen wrote every limitation beside the testimony. The payment ledgers had been transferred to an isle beyond the observatory quay. Anwen found a quiet corner to meditate before the difficult crossing. A migrant worker explained why he still wanted a lawful eastern contract. The cold posed a mortal danger to travelers without shelter. A nude figure on the old fountain had been covered during repairs.

一名前調度員同意作證，前提是法院保障他不受報復，但不免除他自己的責任。他說航運協會多次抱怨嚴格水閘時段耽誤昂貴貨物，之後部會官員指示書記在部分私人航次採用修訂的王宮時間。調度員送過訊息，卻聲稱從未碰鐘。他的說法符合財務模式，法院仍須核對訊息，區分親身所知與傳聞。安溫把每項侷限寫在證詞旁。 付款帳冊被送往港口外的一座小島。 安溫在艱難渡河前找了個安靜角落冥想。 一名移工解釋，自己為何仍想取得合法東方合約。 寒冷對沒有住所的旅人構成致命危險。 舊噴泉上的裸體雕像在修繕時被遮蓋。

Veyra had once imagined that catching the minister would resolve every injury. The accounts showed a network of decisions made by officials, merchants, and supervisors, some deliberately dishonest and others merely careless. A conviction of one man could not automatically repair unpaid wages or unreliable warning cables. She asked the panel to preserve separate tracks for fraud, technical negligence, and compensation. Jory agreed. The minister's guilt, if proved, would not excuse errors at the observatory, and the observatory's failures would not excuse someone who knowingly altered a certified record. The mathematics described a range, not a promise. Their tasks overlapped without becoming identical. The magistrate required each official to swear an oath about document custody. An unusual odor came from the barrels loaded beside the waiting hall. The passengers called the food-card threats outrageous and degrading. The physician explained that ozone did not replace ordinary ventilation. A sealed packet held the original photograph of the disputed stamp.

薇拉曾想像抓到大臣就能解決所有傷害。帳目卻顯示許多官員、商人、監督共同作出決定，有人刻意不誠實，也有人只是粗心。判一人有罪不會自動補上工資或修好警報纜線。她請小組將詐欺、技術疏失與補償分案保留。喬里同意。即使大臣有罪，也不會使天文台錯誤消失；天文台失職同樣不能替明知故犯的改表者脫罪。 數學呈現的是範圍，不是保證。三人的任務互有交集，卻不完全相同。 法官要求每位官員就文件保管宣誓。 候船廳旁裝載的桶子散發奇怪氣味。 乘客稱取消糧卡的威脅荒唐且有辱人格。 醫師解釋臭氧不能取代一般通風。 一個密封包裹裝著爭議印章的原始照片。

That evening the minister summoned Veyra to a private dinner and offered to close the investigation in exchange for a generous scientific grant. She refused to meet without witnesses and requested that his proposal be submitted in writing. No written offer followed. The next morning, the court received the departure slips, a verified company ledger, and the dispatcher's protected testimony. The evidence now pointed toward a motive and an instruction chain, but the final link—the physical act of moving the clock—still awaited examination. The rule was deliberately modest. Neither procedure gave its author permission to destroy an inconvenient original. The interpreter's proficiency prevented several harmful misunderstandings. Veyra refused to scorn travelers who still chose paid work far from home. The drivers denied trying to smuggle anyone across the river. A guard walked with a long stride along the sheltered pier. The kitchen served warm milk scented with vanilla to younger passengers.

當晚大臣邀薇拉私下晚餐，表示若結束調查就提供優渥科研補助。她拒絕在沒有見證人的情況下會面，請他把提案寫下。後來沒有任何書面提案。隔天法院收到出航單、驗證過的公司帳本與調度員受保護的證詞。證據開始指向動機及命令鏈，但最後一環——實際撥動鐘的行為——仍待查驗。 翻譯員熟練的語言能力避免了幾項有害誤解。 薇拉不會鄙視仍選擇離鄉工作的人。 車夫否認試圖偷運任何人渡河。 一名衛兵在有遮蔽的碼頭上大步行走。 廚房給年幼乘客供應帶香草味的溫牛奶。
