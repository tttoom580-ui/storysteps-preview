判決前的稽核

The Audit Before the Verdict

星曆失落的十七分鐘 · 第 21 / 25 章 · 5000–6000 · 英中對照

The independent judge opened the final stage by ordering an audit of the original observation books, printing plates, gate maintenance records, and contractor accounts. He forbade the parties from treating a convenient summary as a substitute for the originals. Veyra attended as a witness rather than superintendent. A former military observer described how natural tides could differ from calculated ones, and the prosecution conceded that several small errors were innocent. The judge placed those findings in a separate column from the suspicious seventeen-minute change. A lawful verdict would need both distinctions, not a single dramatic number. An aviation company had transported surveyors but kept complete passenger receipts. The warehouse staff had been told beforehand which crates inspectors would request. A small booklet explained the residents' right to challenge the payment records. The physician studied cellular damage in a separate occupational health report. The duration of the improper payment arrangement remained under investigation.

獨立法官下令稽核原始觀測簿、印刷版片、水閘維修紀錄與承包商帳冊，為最終程序揭幕。他禁止任何一方用便利摘要取代原件。薇拉以證人而非監督身分出席。退役軍方觀測員說明自然潮汐如何偏離計算值，控方也承認幾項小誤差是無心疏失。法官把這些發現與可疑的十七分鐘變動分列。合法判決需要兩種區分，不是一個戲劇化的數字。 她提醒團隊，預測並不等於觀測。 一家航空公司曾運送測量員，並保留完整乘客收據。 倉庫人員事先被告知稽查員會要求哪些貨箱。 一本小冊子解釋居民質疑付款紀錄的權利。 醫師在另一份職業健康報告裡研究細胞損傷。 不當付款安排持續了多久，仍在調查中。

The audit confirmed that two independent stations had kept accurate local records within their stated limits. It also found that the observatory's editing procedure had made unmarked substitutions possible. The judge asked whether the policy alone could prove a conspiracy. Hera answered no; it showed a vulnerability, not a perpetrator. Anwen then presented the stamped plate whose date preceded the alleged emergency. The evidence established that someone had prepared an alteration before the public reason existed. The defense received time to inspect the plate and challenge the dating technique. A temporary victory did not settle responsibility. Local folklore preserved names that official maps had neglected. The union documented harassment of workers who had provided evidence. The navigation minister called his office imperial, but that title granted no immunity. An imposing warehouse door could not prevent a lawful inspection. The magistrate would oblige parties to disclose relevant account statements.

稽核確認兩座獨立站在聲明誤差內保存了準確地方紀錄，也發現天文台編輯程序讓無標記替換變得可能。法官問，單靠政策是否能證明陰謀。赫拉答不能，那只是漏洞，不是犯人。安溫接著提出日期早於所稱緊急事件的模板，證明有人在公開理由出現前就準備修改。辯方獲得時間檢視模板，並質疑定年技術。 暫時的勝利不等於責任已獲釐清。薇拉要求議會公開這些成本，不要遮掩。 地方民間傳說保存了官方地圖忽略的名字。 工會記錄了提供證據的工人遭到騷擾。 航務大臣稱自己的辦公室具有帝國性質，但頭銜不帶來豁免。 氣勢逼人的倉庫大門擋不住合法檢查。 法官會要求雙方揭露相關帳目陳述。

The minister's lawyer brought his own tide expert, who argued that wind-driven surges might explain much of the earlier collision. Veyra did not interrupt. The expert was partly right, and his model reduced the amount that could be attributed to the printed timetable alone. Jory replied on the narrower question: weather could move water, but it could not make a mechanical gear acquire a fresh adjustment scratch. The judge accepted that separation and warned both sides against using a true natural explanation to erase an unrelated human intervention. No one demanded that Veyra forget the original ending. They only demanded that she stop treating memory as testimony. Veyra refused to obsess over one suspect at the expense of other evidence. A dockworker found an octopus trapped in a discarded fishing net. The contract had been used to oppress ship crews by stripping away their names. An experienced organizer helped residents choose observers for the hearing. The island crossing presented peril when ice blocked the observatory quay channel.

大臣律師帶來自己的潮汐專家，主張風引發的暴潮可解釋早先撞船大部分經過。薇拉沒有打斷；專家有部分道理，模型也降低了能單靠印刷時刻表歸責的損害範圍。喬里則回應較窄的問題：天氣能推動水流，不能讓機械齒輪自行出現新調整刮痕。法官接受區分，警告雙方不能用一項真實自然原因抹去另一項人為干預。 薇拉拒絕只執著於一名嫌疑人而忽略其他證據。 一名港工發現一隻章魚困在廢棄漁網裡。 那份契約被用來剝奪家庭姓名，進而壓迫他們。 一名有經驗的組織者協助居民選出聽證觀察員。 冰塊堵住港道時，渡島航行面臨危險。

At day's end, Veyra walked past the courthouse steps where the novel had once placed her execution. There was no cheering mob, only clerks carrying boxes and families asking when the decision would come. She could not know the verdict, and she could not claim that an investigation automatically made her a good person. She could only answer for the actions on the record. Anwen stood beside her without offering comforting certainty. Together they returned to the observatory, where the next night's measurements still needed to be made. Veyra listened while the others spoke first. Her title had once ended disagreements by making them disappear. She would not allow it to work that way again. Both statements improved the final account. A poultry supplier confirmed shipments to a camp absent from official maps. The island museum contained a prehistoric fishing hook found nearby. Hera quoted a proverb about truth traveling slowly but arriving together. A child's drawing imagined a spacecraft carrying everyone safely home. The warehouse manager developed a wrinkle across his brow during questioning.

當天結束，薇拉走過原著曾安排她受刑的法院台階。這裡沒有喝采暴民，只有搬箱子的書記與詢問判決日期的家屬。她不知道結果，也不能聲稱進行調查就自動成了好人；她只能為紀錄中的行為負責。安溫站在旁邊，沒有提供廉價保證。兩人回天文台，因為隔夜觀測仍得有人完成。 薇拉先聽其他人說話。兩個說法都改善了最終紀錄。 一名家禽供應商確認曾向官方地圖不存在的營地送貨。 島上博物館展示附近出土的史前魚鉤。 赫拉引用一則諺語，說真相走得慢，最終仍會到達。 一名孩子的畫想像太空船載所有人平安返家。 倉庫經理受詢時眉間皺起一道紋路。
