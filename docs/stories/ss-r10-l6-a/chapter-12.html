The People Between the Bells

星曆失落的十七分鐘 · 第 12 / 25 章 · 5000–6000 · 英中對照

The coastal hearing began in a school hall because the courthouse could not hold all the families. Some wanted the channel reopened without restriction; others feared another winter collision. A fisherwoman named Mae explained that her income depended on an early return before the market opened. A hospital porter said the same early current had once delayed a boat carrying medicine. Veyra asked each speaker to distinguish an observed event from an interpretation. She promised that witnesses could review their recorded statements and request corrections before publication, an option that had rarely existed under her previous administration. Veyra separated them aloud. A narrow brook ran behind the former navigation school. The classroom held a glass tank with a piece of coral from the sea. No official could deprive a child of food for refusing an interview. The nurse asked guards to elevate a feverish child's injured leg. Several travel permits had reached expiration before the depot received them.

海岸聽證會借用學校大廳，因為法院容不下所有家庭。有些人希望毫無限制地恢復航道，有些人害怕冬季再撞船。漁婦梅說，她的收入靠清晨市場開門前返港；醫院搬運員則說，同一股早潮曾使載藥船延誤。薇拉請每名發言者區分親眼經歷與自己的解釋，並承諾證人可在公布前查閱、更正筆錄。她以前掌權時幾乎不提供這種權利。 舊航海學校後方流過一條狹窄小溪。 教室裡有座玻璃缸，放著一塊海裡的珊瑚。 官員不得因孩子拒絕訪談而剝奪食物。 護士請衛兵抬高一名發燒孩子受傷的腿。 幾張通行許可送到轉運站前就已到期。

A merchant tried to turn the meeting into a vote on whether Veyra was wicked. Anwen interrupted with the chair's permission and read the specific questions being examined: the accuracy of the published window, the lawful basis for changing it, and the losses caused by any false instructions. The crowd settled when the agenda became concrete. The merchant still spoke, but now he had to present shipping documents rather than insults. Mae challenged a numerical estimate in the council's model and gave the team her actual catch receipts. That disagreement improved the report. Now their names were returned to the page. Poor eyesight made the tiny identification codes difficult for Talen to read. The kitchen supplied a hearty meal to children who had traveled all night. A rescue bird seemed to hover above the old school roof. The inland depot received shipments that never appeared on coastal lists. The nurse's insistence on written care plans protected the patients.

一名商人試圖把會議變成「薇拉是否邪惡」的投票。安溫取得主持人同意後，念出正在調查的具體問題：印刷時段是否準確、修改它的法律依據、錯誤指令造成哪些損失。議程具體後，群眾逐漸安靜。商人仍可發言，卻必須提交航運文件，不能只辱罵。梅指出議會模型一項估算有誤，拿出實際漁獲收據。這個分歧讓報告更好。如今他們的名字重新回到頁面。 塔倫視力不好，很難讀出微小的身分代碼。 廚房為整夜趕路的孩子提供一頓豐盛餐食。 一隻救援用鳥似乎盤旋在舊校舍屋頂上。 內陸轉運站收到的貨物，從未出現在沿海清單上。 護士堅持書面照護計畫，保護了病人。

The most difficult testimony came from the widow of a sailor lost in the earlier collision. She did not want her husband's name transformed into a symbol for either political side. Veyra offered to omit his private history and retain only the facts relevant to the gate opening. The woman agreed on the condition that the ministry's earlier refusal to answer her letters remain visible. Silas found three unanswered letters and one envelope marked delivered. The evidence established neglect of correspondence, although it still could not explain the clock's missing minutes. Veyra knew how tempting a clean story could be. A hidden lease linked the depot operator to the dockyardral company. A legislator requested an independent review of the children's transfers. The majestic mountains looked close despite the long journey home. The inquiry gained momentum when the unopened letters were discovered. The monarch had signed the original rule protecting family contact.

最困難的證詞來自去年撞船亡者的遺孀。她不希望丈夫姓名成為任一政治陣營的象徵。薇拉提議刪去私人過往，只保留與開閘相關的事實。遺孀同意，條件是部會以前拒絕回信的紀錄必須保留。賽拉斯找到三封未回覆信件和一只標示送達的信封。證據足以證明函件處理失職，仍無法直接解釋鐘上消失的分鐘。薇拉知道一個乾淨整齊的故事多麼誘人。 一份隱藏租約把轉運站經營者與礦業公司連在一起。 一名議員要求獨立複查孩子們的轉移。 雄偉山脈看似近在眼前，回家的路卻還很長。 未拆封的信件曝光後，調查獲得了動力。 君主曾簽署保護家人聯絡權的原始規定。

The hearing ended without consensus on the ideal timetable. Veyra did not call that a failure. The panel accepted a revised safety notice, a transparent compensation study, and a procedure for correcting witness transcripts. Anwen carried the transcript box to the court herself. Outside, the navigation minister announced that Veyra was exploiting public grief. She answered no insult; instead she posted the hearing schedule and invited the minister to submit his own documents under the same rules. The next inquiry would examine not emotion but the arithmetic behind the missing interval. His first drafts had once been neat enough to conceal confusion. A mother waited for news of her offspring at the station gate. The depot had bought petrol for vehicles absent from its public accounts. The physician told the nurse to rotate beds away from the damp wall. Veyra refused to let secrecy strangle the children's opportunity to speak. A surgical team from the district hospital offered urgent treatment.

聽證結束時，眾人仍無法同意哪張時刻表最好。薇拉不認為那就是失敗。審查團接受修訂安全告示、公開補償研究，以及更正證詞筆錄的程序。安溫親自把筆錄箱送去法院。門外，大臣聲稱薇拉利用公眾悲痛。她不回罵，只張貼聽證日程，請大臣依同樣規則提交文件。下一階段要查的不是情緒，而是消失時段背後的計算。他把廢棄計算放入信封，而不是燒掉。 一位母親在站門口等待孩子的消息。 轉運站買了汽油給未列在公開帳冊上的車輛。 醫師要護士輪流把床移離潮濕牆面。 薇拉不願讓保密扼殺孩子們發聲的機會。 區醫院的外科團隊提出提供緊急治療。
