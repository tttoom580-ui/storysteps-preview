The Warehouse Ledger

南河雙時錄 · 第 21 / 25 章 · 4000–5000 · 英中對照 · 校訂完成

用互動版查詞、朗讀與收藏

A document recovered from a riverside warehouse seemed to connect the ancient siege with modern property claims. The scroll recorded an abundant grain supply sent to Nanhe by villagers who wanted fair treatment rather than a reward. An scanned the fragile pages and placed them in a setting with controlled light. The handwriting was crude but clear enough to reveal a declaration: every family contributing grain would receive equivalent access to irrigation after the war. It was an early statement of equity, though the writer used words unlike those in modern law.

一份從河岸倉庫找回的文書，似乎將古圍城與現代土地爭議連在一起。卷軸記著鄰村送給南河的大批穀糧，村民要求的是公平待遇，不是獎賞。安掃描脆弱紙頁，放入恆定光線的保存環境。字跡粗糙，卻足以看出一項宣告：戰後，所有捐糧家庭都應取得相應灌溉權。這是早期公平分配的聲明，雖然用語與現代法律不同。

A cognitive historian helped An interpret the record. He said that people remember agreements through ceremonies, songs, and repeated practice as much as through writing. This gave An a new notion of what evidence might look like. She asked elders from the river community for commentary and learned that a ceremonial bread made from coarse dough was shared each spring to commemorate an old promise. The custom was supposedly older than the city's modern charter. Some scholars had dismissed it as folklore, but the warehouse ledger gave it a possible historical basis.

研究認知的史學家幫安解讀。他說，人們會透過儀式、歌謠與重複實踐記住協議，不一定只靠文字。安於是對證據有了新想法。她請河民長者評論，得知每年春天會分食粗麵糰做的紀念餅，記住古老約定。習俗據說比現代市章更早。有學者把它當民俗，但倉庫帳本可能提供歷史基礎。

During a public discussion, a political student asked whether the ancient agreement could support a modern presidential campaign about land rights. An resisted the temptation to turn scholarship into a campaign slogan. The record mattered for its historical content, not because any present candidate might gain from it. Another visitor argued that its ethics should prohibit the sale of all river property. An replied that an ethic of shared responsibility did not automatically answer every modern legal question. The evidence had to be examined alongside present needs, environmental reports, and living people's rights.

公開座談中，一名政治系學生問，古契約能否支持某場關於土地權的總統選戰。安不想把學術變成競選口號。史料的重要性在其歷史內容，而不是現任候選人能否得利。另一名觀眾說，這種倫理應禁止出售所有河岸土地。安回答，共同責任的倫理不會自動回答每個現代法律問題；仍得把證據與今日需求、環境報告和現存人權一併考量。

A child on a school tour pointed at a drawing of a river mammal beside the grain list. “Why is an animal on a contract?” he asked. An explained that the creature marked the season when the river was high enough for boats. Its image linked an economic promise to the natural rhythm of the valley. The child nodded and asked whether modern people would someday forget such signs. An did not know. She had seen how easily documents could shatter when paper, stone, or public interest was neglected. Preserving the ledger was one answer; teaching people to ask questions was another.

一名校外教學的孩子指著糧單旁畫的河中哺乳動物問：「契約上為什麼有動物？」安解釋，那是表示適合舟行的高水季節，讓經濟承諾和河谷的自然節律相連。孩子點頭，又問現代人將來會不會忘記這些記號。安不知道。她見過紙張、石頭或大眾關注一旦被忽略，紀錄便多容易破碎。保存帳本是一種回答，教人發問則是另一種。

The education team asked an artist to depict the grain agreement in a mural. He drew workers passing sacks from hand to hand, with no ruler in the center of the picture. A school group watched him paint, and one child wondered why nobody wore a crown. “Because the agreement was not a gift from a king,” An replied. “It was a promise among neighbors.” She then showed the children where the document described penalties for breaking that promise. Several were surprised that kindness and firm rules could belong together. An explained that promises were easier to trust when everyone knew how disagreements would be settled. The children proposed adding a blank space to the mural where visitors could write one public good they wanted to protect. The artist left the space unpainted, and the wall became a conversation rather than a monument.

教育團隊請畫家把分糧協議畫成壁畫。他畫出工人一袋袋傳糧，畫面中央沒有君王。校外教學的孩子問，為什麼沒人戴王冠。「因為這不是國王的恩賜，」安說，「這是鄰居之間的承諾。」她也指出文件如何規定違約責任。有些孩子驚訝，善意和嚴格規則竟能並存。安解釋，人人知道爭議如何解決，承諾才更可信。孩子們建議在壁畫留白，讓訪客寫下想保護的公共事物。畫家於是留下空格，讓牆面成為對話，而非紀念碑。

The newly translated declaration became the center of the next exhibit. Beside it An posted a simple comparison of ancient grain measures and modern currency, carefully stating where an equivalent could only be approximate. Visitors found the record less glamorous than the bronze seals, yet they spent longer reading it. It described neighbors deciding how to share water, which was still a question the city faced. At closing time, An stood before the case and imagined Mei receiving the grain. Perhaps the most lasting legacy of the siege was not its battle, but its promise.

新譯的宣告成為下一場展覽主角。安在旁邊張貼古糧量與現代貨幣的比較，並清楚標示只能近似換算的部分。觀眾起初覺得帳本不如青銅印華麗，最後卻讀得更久。它談的是鄰人如何分水，而城市今日仍面對相同問題。閉館時，安站在展櫃前想像梅收糧的情景。或許圍城最持久的遺產不是戰鬥，而是承諾。
