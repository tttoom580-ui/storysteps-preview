永不關上的水門

The Gate That Stayed Open

南河雙時錄 · 第 25 / 25 章 · 4000–5000 · 英中對照 · 校訂完成

The final stage of restoration began as the rainy season approached. Forecasters warned of a slow-moving storm whose coverage extended from the northern mountains to the river plain. Engineers checked every pipeline and structural joint along the embankment, while volunteers prepared supplies for shelters. An felt awe standing beside the gate Mei had once saved. Its stone surface was obscure beneath layers of repair, yet the mechanism still worked. The team's main criterion was simple: the city must remain safe even if the new museum exhibition had to close. Beautiful displays could wait; living people could not.

雨季將近，修復進入最後階段。預報說一場移動緩慢的暴雨將籠罩北山到河原。工程師檢查堤上每條管線與結構接縫，志工準備避難物資。安站在梅曾救過的水門旁，心中滿是敬畏。石面在層層修補下難辨原貌，機關卻仍能動。團隊最重要的判準很簡單：即使新展覽必須關閉，也要先確保城市安全。漂亮展示可以等，活人不行。

At a public meeting, a resident asked whether the city faced a new siege, this time by rain rather than soldiers. An understood the comparison, but reminded everyone that a storm was not an enemy with intentions. A chronic problem of neglected drains had increased flood danger, and the team needed a commitment to long-term maintenance. An showed how the old and new channels would merge below the southern bridge. She explained the correlation between rising water and pressure on the wall, noting that correlation alone was not proof of every possible cause. The engineers invited independent reviewers to check their calculations.

公民會議上，有人問城市是否再次被圍城，只是這回圍城的是雨，不是士兵。安懂這比喻，仍提醒風暴不是有意圖的敵人。排水道長期失修才會增加危險，城市需要維護的長期承諾。她展示新舊水道如何在南橋下匯合，解釋水位上升與牆面受壓的相關性，也提醒相關不代表能證明每種可能原因。工程師邀外部審查計算。

When the rain arrived, volunteers opened the shelter. A landlord carried blankets down the stairs; a young father hurried in holding a baby whose diaper had soaked through. Outside, the sky was gloomy, and distant thunder rolled like stones inside a volcano. A medical worker explained the symptoms of cold exposure to parents waiting in line. Nobody presumed the city would be entirely safe simply because the gate had been tested. Teams walked the neighborhoods, checking on people unable to travel. An helped tackle the sudden shortage of drinking water by organizing deliveries from schools and shops.

暴雨抵達時，志工打開避難所。一位房東抱著毯子下樓，一名年輕父親抱著尿布濕透的嬰兒匆匆進門。屋外天色陰沉，遠雷像火山裡滾動的石塊。醫護向排隊家長說明失溫症狀。沒人因水門通過測試就認定萬無一失。各隊走訪社區，查看不便出門的人。安則聯絡學校與店家運水，處理飲用水突然短缺的問題。

At the courthouse, a jury in an unrelated land dispute had recently examined the old documents and confirmed the public right to reach the river. The ruling meant volunteers could use the southern path without asking a developer's permission. An thought about how many decisions had led to this moment: the historical inquiry, the repaired archive, the public hearings, the difficult compromises. The city had once treated profit versus safety as if it were a battle with only one winner. Now it understood that responsible development must work within limits set by shared need and public evidence.

不久前，法院陪審團在一件土地糾紛裡查看古檔，確認公眾有權通行河岸。這項裁決意味著志工能使用南路，無須向開發商求准。安想起通往此刻的無數決定：歷史調查、修好的檔案、公開聽證，以及艱難的妥協。城市曾把利益與安全當成只能一方取勝的對決，如今逐漸明白，負責任的發展必須受到共同需求與公開證據的限制。

After the storm, An returned to the restored gate with a group of students. She showed them two inscriptions: the one honoring Mei and another listing workers whose names had been missing from the first official history. A student asked which inscription mattered more. An answered that neither could stand alone. The famous name drew people to the story; the others explained how the work was actually done. She did not presume that every mystery had been solved. Some documents remained unreadable, and the original purpose of certain stones was still uncertain. Yet the students left with notebooks full of questions rather than a single approved lesson. An considered that a better ending for a museum—and perhaps for any history—than a polished sentence that pretended there was nothing left to learn.

風暴過後，安帶學生回到修好的水門，指給他們看兩塊銘文：一塊紀念梅，另一塊列出初版官史遺漏的工人姓名。學生問哪塊更重要。安說，兩塊都不能單獨成立。知名者引人走進故事，其他名字則說明事情究竟如何完成。她不認為所有謎團都已解開，有的文件還讀不出來，有些石塊原用途仍未知。但學生離開時，筆記滿是問題，而不是單一標準答案。安覺得，這比一句假裝再無可學的漂亮結論更適合博物館，也更適合歷史。

An remembered the storm's last morning, before she had brought students to the gate. The river had risen, but the Third Gate diverted the worst of the flood toward the open plain. No neighborhood was entirely untouched; several homes needed repairs, and families mourned what the water took. Yet there was no disaster on the scale once feared. At the shelter a small choir began to sing, and the sound reached An through an open window. She imagined another morning, centuries earlier, when Mei had stood above the same river. The two women would never meet, yet their work belonged to one story: protecting memory so that memory could protect life.

安想起暴雨最後一個清晨，那時她尚未帶學生前來。河水雖已暴漲，第三道水門仍把最凶猛的洪流導往空曠平原。沒有哪個社區完全不受影響；幾間屋子待修，人們也為被水帶走的東西哀傷。但最可怕的災難沒有發生。避難所裡，一小群人組成的合唱團開始唱歌，歌聲穿過開窗傳到安耳邊。她想像數百年前另一個清晨，梅站在同一條河上。兩人永不會見面，所做的事卻屬於同一個故事：保存記憶，好讓記憶繼續守護生命。
