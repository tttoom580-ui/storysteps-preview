手扶梯旁的會議

The Escalator Meeting

南河雙時錄 · 第 24 / 25 章 · 4000–5000 · 英中對照 · 校訂完成

用互動版查詞、朗讀與收藏

On the day the restoration contract was signed, An saw a familiar figure near the museum escalator: the developer's chief adviser, speaking with a man who had bullied workers at earlier meetings. They were trying to substitute a private security company for the public team assigned to protect the ruins. An remained courteous and asked for a written explanation. The adviser claimed that the change was necessary to improve the project's capability. Yet the new company had no profile of archaeological work, and its previous clients included a suburban warehouse investigated for illegal cocaine trafficking. An refused to approve the substitution without review.

修復合約簽署日，安在博物館手扶梯旁看見熟悉身影：開發商首席顧問正和一名先前在會議上霸凌工人的男子交談。他們打算用私人保全取代原訂守護遺跡的公共團隊。安維持禮貌，要求書面說明。顧問說換人是為了提升工程能力，但新公司毫無考古經歷，過往客戶還包括一間遭調查涉及非法古柯鹼運輸的郊區倉庫。安拒絕在審查前批准。

The adviser argued that security was a separate matter from heritage. An replied that interference with excavation records could alter the core evidence on which every design rested. During a break she studied a photographic profile of the worksite and spotted a new fence where none had been authorized. A local ranch owner had reported seeing trucks deliver soil during the night. The contractor said the soil was meant to sow grass on an empty slope. An demanded signed receipts lest undocumented earthworks destroy evidence at the protected site before anyone noticed. Even harmless work needed a traceable record near the ancient drainage opening.

顧問說保全與文化資產是兩回事。安回答，干擾發掘紀錄會改變所有設計所依據的核心證據。休息時，她查看工地攝影側寫，發現一處未核准新圍籬。附近牧場主人曾看見卡車夜裡送土。承包商說土是拿來在空坡播草。安要求具名簽收單據，免得未經記錄的土方工程破壞受保護遺址的證據，直到太晚才被發現。古排水口附近即使是無害的施工，也必須留下可追查的紀錄。

At the hearing, residents spoke about the site's autonomy from private interests. Some demanded that every contractor be removed at once. An disagreed; responsible companies deserved fair treatment and compensation if contracts were changed without cause. She condemned intimidation but refused to condemn workers who had done their jobs honestly. A young technician presented photographs showing that the mysterious soil was being dumped on an old drainage opening. It was a serious danger, because the blocked channel could divert future floods toward homes. The meeting, once focused on paperwork, became an urgent discussion of safety.

聽證會上，居民要求遺址管理保持不受私人利益控制。有些人主張立即撤掉所有承包商。安不同意，盡責公司應受公平對待；無故變約時也應得到補償。她譴責恐嚇，但不譴責誠實工作的工人。年輕技師展示照片，證明神祕土方正倒在一處古排水口。這是重大危險，因堵塞會在下次洪水時把水導向民宅。原本只談文書的會議，忽然成了急迫的安全討論。

At the second hearing, the supposed security expert tried to bully a young engineer into withdrawing her statement. An intervened and invited the engineer to explain the inherent weakness in the proposed wall. She used a glass pitcher filled with water to demonstrate how pressure followed a hidden crack. The audience watched as a thin line of colored water escaped through the model. The expert called the demonstration childish, but the independent inspectors confirmed its relevance. An then asked the council to protect whistleblowers from retaliation, regardless of their employer. The project would compensate anyone whose lawful contract had been unfairly canceled, while still investigating misconduct. The engineer had spoken because she believed a public structure should not endanger the people living beside it. She deserved evidence-based criticism if her model was wrong, An said, not threats for daring to show it. The council adopted a reporting process that let technicians raise concerns without first seeking permission from the very supervisors whose decisions might be at issue. After the meeting, the engineer stood alone by the river and watched the rain gather in small pools. She was still frightened about her job, and she did not pretend otherwise. An joined her, offered a quiet thank-you, and promised to include her findings in the permanent public archive. The engineer finally smiled. She knew one careful report could not change every institution, but it had already changed one decision that might have harmed the neighborhood.

第二次聽證時，那位自稱保全專家的男子試圖霸凌年輕工程師，逼她收回證詞。安出面，請工程師解釋提議牆體本身的弱點。她用裝水的玻璃水壺示範壓力如何沿隱藏裂縫移動。觀眾看見一絲染色水從模型滲出。專家罵示範幼稚，獨立檢查員卻確認相關性。安接著要求議會保護吹哨者，不分受雇於誰。工程也會補償那些合法合約遭不公平取消的人，同時繼續調查不當行為。工程師發言，是因她相信公共構造不該危及鄰居。若模型有錯，應接受有證據的批評，而非因敢說話遭威脅。議會最後建立通報制度，讓技術員無須先獲可能涉事的主管批准，便能提出疑慮。會後，工程師獨自站在河邊，看雨水積成小水窪。她仍怕丟工作，並不假裝自己不怕。安走來道謝，答應把她的發現收入永久公開檔案。工程師終於微笑。她知道一份報告改不了所有制度，卻已改變一項可能傷害社區的決定。

The city ordered the soil removed and suspended the security change. An watched the trucks leave as rain began to fall. She had spent months writing a thesis about public memory, but this day reminded her that history was not confined to academic arguments. An old channel could still save houses, and an undisclosed truckload of soil could still threaten them. She thought of Mei confronting the governor centuries earlier. The costumes and machines had changed. The central question had not: who bore the risk when powerful people made decisions behind closed doors?

市府命人移除土方，暫停更換保全。安看著卡車在雨中離開。她花數月寫公共記憶的論文，這天卻提醒她歷史不只存在學術辯論。古渠道仍能救房屋，一車未公開的土仍能威脅房屋。她想起數百年前面對太守的梅。服裝和機器變了，核心問題沒有：掌權者在密室做決定時，究竟由誰承擔風險？
