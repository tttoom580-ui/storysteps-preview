Sora had been thinking about simple things in the present-day school when a detail refused to fit the old picture. For a moment, everyone around seemed busy with something else. Sora tried not to rush, because a wrong guess could make the problem harder for someone nearby. The paper route map was close at hand, ready for the facts that would be easy to forget once people began talking.
