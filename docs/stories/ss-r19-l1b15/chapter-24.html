Sora had been thinking about simple things in the present-day school when a detail refused to fit the old picture. For a moment, everyone around seemed busy with something else. Sora tried not to rush, because a wrong guess could make the problem harder for someone nearby. The paper route map was close at hand, ready for the facts that would be easy to forget once people began talking. Sora saw two towns at once: one he remembered and one that still had to grow into it. The same street could carry more than one story. A little note used “pin” as its key word; Sora kept the note because the rest of the line was still useful.
