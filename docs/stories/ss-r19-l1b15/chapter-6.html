At a library, Sora copied a street map onto paper with permission from the librarian. Before anyone called the day strange, Sora had noticed how quiet the old town square could become when people were worried. The smallest sounds seemed clear, and the sound of a street clock made the place feel both familiar and new. Rather than act as if every answer was obvious, Sora opened the paper route map and began with what had really happened. Sora saw two towns at once: one he remembered and one that still had to grow into it. The same street could carry more than one story. In the corner of the paper route map, Sora found the word “arm” beside an old drawing and stopped to ask what it meant. “find,” Mika read from the practice page, then tried to connect the word to something they could actually see. A little note used “mud” as its key word; Sora kept the note because the rest of the line was still useful. Sora circled “welcome” on a reading sheet, not to win a test but to remember the meaning of the whole sentence.

空在圖書館徵得管理員同意後，抄下一張紙本街道圖。在人們說這一天奇怪之前，空就注意到，當大家擔心時，舊鎮廣場會變得多麼安靜。細微的聲音變得清楚，街鐘的聲音也讓這地方既熟悉又陌生。空沒有裝作一切都很明白，而是打開紙本道路地圖，從真正發生的事情開始。空同時看見兩個小鎮：一個在自己的記憶裡，一個還沒長成後來的模樣。同一條街可以承載不只一段故事。在紙本道路地圖的角落，空看見一幅舊畫旁寫著「arm」，於是停下來詢問意思。「find。」美佳讀出練習頁上的字，接著試著把它和眼前真正看得到的東西連起來。一張小筆記以「mud」為關鍵字；空留下它，因為剩下的句子仍然有用。空在閱讀單上圈出「welcome」，不是為了考高分，而是為了理解整句話。

Nobody could say yet how the story would end. Sora found this uncomfortable, especially with the road back to 2026 still waiting for an answer. Mika suggested they take turns speaking, because repeating the same fear would not create a new solution. A little order returned to the conversation, and that made the next difficult step possible. “Let us keep ‘envelope’ in our word notes,” Sora said. “We can check its meaning again if we need it.” In the corner of the paper route map, Sora found the word “may” beside an old drawing and stopped to ask what it meant. “surprised,” Mika read from the practice page, then tried to connect the word to something they could actually see.

沒有人知道事情會如何結束。空覺得不安，尤其回到二○二六年的路還等著答案。美佳建議輪流發言，因為一再重複恐懼並不會帶來新方法。談話恢復了一些秩序，也讓接下來的難題變得有機會處理。「把『envelope』留在詞彙筆記裡吧，」空說，「有需要時再確認意思。」在紙本道路地圖的角落，空看見一幅舊畫旁寫著「may」，於是停下來詢問意思。「surprised。」美佳讀出練習頁上的字，接著試著把它和眼前真正看得到的東西連起來。

The class learned that some familiar parks and roads did not yet exist. This was the point where a simple wish met the world as it really was. Sora wanted to help, but wanting did not make every road safe or every rule fair. Mika asked what the other people involved might lose if the wrong choice were made. The answer took longer than either had expected. They agreed not to build the rest of their plan on a fact they had never checked. The word “do” appeared in an example someone had written earlier, and Mika asked if the example fit their situation. “Let us keep ‘however’ in our word notes,” Sora said. “We can check its meaning again if we need it.” In the corner of the paper route map, Sora found the word “speak” beside an old drawing and stopped to ask what it meant.

同學發現熟悉的公園與道路當時還不存在。這正是簡單願望遇上真實世界的時刻。空想幫忙，但只靠想法不會讓所有路變安全，也不會讓所有規則公平。美佳問，如果選錯了，牽涉其中的人可能失去什麼。兩人花了比預期更長的時間才回答。他們同意，不把整個計畫建立在尚未核實的事實上。「do」出現在前人留下的例句裡，美佳問這個例子是否適合現在的情況。「把『however』留在詞彙筆記裡吧，」空說，「有需要時再確認意思。」在紙本道路地圖的角落，空看見一幅舊畫旁寫著「speak」，於是停下來詢問意思。

The group returned to the paper route map and looked at the first line again. It now seemed to ask a different question. Sora asked Mika to explain the idea in plain words, as if telling it to someone who had just arrived. When the idea sounded confusing, they changed the plan rather than blaming the listener. Sora circled “desk” on a reading sheet, not to win a test but to remember the meaning of the whole sentence. The word “history” appeared in an example someone had written earlier, and Mika asked if the example fit their situation. “Let us keep ‘shop’ in our word notes,” Sora said. “We can check its meaning again if we need it.”

大家重新看著紙本道路地圖的第一行，發現它彷彿提出了另一個問題。空請美佳用簡單的話解釋，就像對剛來的人說明。說明若讓人困惑，他們就修改計畫，而不是責怪聽的人。空在閱讀單上圈出「desk」，不是為了考高分，而是為了理解整句話。「history」出現在前人留下的例句裡，美佳問這個例子是否適合現在的情況。「把『shop』留在詞彙筆記裡吧，」空說，「有需要時再確認意思。」

A red line on the map showed where the old bridge might return at sunset. The answer brought relief, but it also carried a new responsibility. Sora did not look away from that part. Mika asked who should hear the news next, and whether it belonged to them to share it. They chose the careful answer over the exciting one, though excitement was still in the air. A little note used “deep” as its key word; Sora kept the note because the rest of the line was still useful. Sora circled “funny” on a reading sheet, not to win a test but to remember the meaning of the whole sentence. The word “seat” appeared in an example someone had written earlier, and Mika asked if the example fit their situation.

地圖上的一條紅線標出舊橋日落時可能再現的位置。答案帶來安心，也帶來新的責任。空沒有迴避這一部分。美佳問下一個該讓誰知道消息，以及他們是否有權公開。他們選擇審慎的回答，而非最刺激的那個，雖然空氣中仍充滿興奮。一張小筆記以「deep」為關鍵字；空留下它，因為剩下的句子仍然有用。空在閱讀單上圈出「funny」，不是為了考高分，而是為了理解整句話。「seat」出現在前人留下的例句裡，美佳問這個例子是否適合現在的情況。

The clock moved on. Sora felt tired, yet there was a good kind of energy left after honest work. Nobody had to say that the day was perfect. They only had to remember what had worked and what still needed care. Sora closed the paper route map gently, already curious about the next choice. “convenient,” Mika read from the practice page, then tried to connect the word to something they could actually see. A little note used “fish” as its key word; Sora kept the note because the rest of the line was still useful. Sora circled “rise” on a reading sheet, not to win a test but to remember the meaning of the whole sentence.

時鐘繼續走。空雖然疲倦，卻保有完成誠實工作後的好心情。沒有人必須宣稱這天完美；只要記得哪些方法有效、哪些事仍需照顧。空輕輕闔上紙本道路地圖，開始好奇下一個選擇。「convenient。」美佳讀出練習頁上的字，接著試著把它和眼前真正看得到的東西連起來。一張小筆記以「fish」為關鍵字；空留下它，因為剩下的句子仍然有用。空在閱讀單上圈出「rise」，不是為了考高分，而是為了理解整句話。
