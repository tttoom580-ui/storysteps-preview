Back in 2026, the school still stood and the library shared part of its building. The present-day school had its own familiar rhythm. Sora knew the sound of footsteps there, yet something about this moment called for a closer look. It was tempting to make a quick story out of a small surprise. Instead, Sora looked for one thing that could be checked and one person who deserved to be heard. There was room in the paper route map for questions as well as answers, and today the questions were beginning to matter. A little note used “computer” as its key word; Sora kept the note because the rest of the line was still useful. Sora wanted everyone to reach home safely at the end of the day. The word “tie” appeared in an example someone had written earlier, and Mika asked if the example fit their situation.

The first reaction in the present-day school was a mixture of surprise and hope. Sora felt both, and did not pretend otherwise. The same street could carry more than one story. “bed,” Mika read from the practice page, then tried to connect the word to something they could actually see. The class could not tell whether their visit had changed history or completed something that always happened. Help did not always mean being the first person to act. That gave Mika time to notice a detail that had been lost in the noise. Sora brought the old class photo to the school history room. The new detail made the room seem a little different. Things that had looked unrelated began to form a line Sora could follow. Mika warned that one clear clue did not mean they knew everything. Sora agreed and wrote the date beside the note. There was work still to do, but now they understood why that work mattered. When Sora returned to the present-day school, the place was almost the same. The difference was in what Sora now understood. Mika had helped without taking away the choice, and that made the help easier to accept. For now, they could rest. Tomorrow would ask its own question.
