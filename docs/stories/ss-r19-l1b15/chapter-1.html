The Bus That Turned Left

The bus had a red star above the camera by its front door, and Sora had noticed it only because the school trip was running late. His class had planned a picnic at the history park north of town. At the middle of the trip, their driver turned onto a road that was not on any of their maps. The other students were busy trading juice and snacks, but Sora saw an old bridge ahead. A friendly woman stood beside it with a pet dog on a short lead. The woman waved at the bus as if she had been waiting all morning. Then the radio went quiet.

巴士前門旁的相機上方貼著一顆紅星。空之所以注意到，是因為校外教學已經遲到了。全班原本要去小鎮北邊的歷史公園野餐。行程走到一半，司機卻轉上地圖沒有的路。其他同學正忙著交換果汁和點心，空卻看見前方有座舊橋。橋旁站著一位友善女士，手裡牽著寵物狗。那人向巴士揮手，彷彿等了整個早上。接著收音機安靜了。

Ms Day stepped toward the driver. “Can we reach the park from this road?” she asked. The driver slowed. “I don't know. This bridge was not here a minute ago.” A boy laughed and said it must be a new road, but the teacher did not accept a guess as fact. She asked everyone to remain seated. The camera still worked, and she used it to take a picture of the bridge from inside the bus. Sora looked out of the window again. The stone sides were old, and the sign by the road carried a name he had seen in his grandfather's album. A little note used “lie” as its key word; Sora kept the note because the rest of the line was still useful.

戴老師走向司機。「這條路能到公園嗎？」她問。司機放慢速度。「我不知道。一分鐘前這座橋還不在這裡。」一名男孩笑著說一定是新路，老師卻不願把猜測當成事實。她請大家繼續坐好。相機還能使用，老師從車裡拍下一張橋的照片。空再次望向窗外。石造橋邊很古老，路牌上的名字他曾在祖父的相簿裡見過。一張小筆記以「lie」為關鍵字；空留下它，因為剩下的句子仍然有用。

On the other side of the bridge, the buildings changed. A small shop sold simple toys, and a bus stop showed a route with no place for the new train station. People held old telephones instead of the phones the students used. Sora saw a paper poster with the words SUMMER FAIR 2006. “That cannot be right,” he whispered. A girl beside him tried to call her mother, but no signal came. Ms Day began a list of every student's name and asked the driver to park in a safe place. “We will not leave anybody alone,” she said. Sora circled “notice” on a reading sheet, not to win a test but to remember the meaning of the whole sentence.

橋的另一邊，建築變了模樣。小店賣著簡單玩具，公車站路線圖上沒有新的火車站。人們拿著舊電話，而不是學生用的手機。空看見一張寫著「二○○六夏季園遊會」的紙海報。「不可能吧，」他低聲說。身旁女孩試著打給母親，卻沒有訊號。戴老師開始寫下每位學生的名字，請司機在安全位置停車。「我們不會讓任何人獨自行動，」她說。空在閱讀單上圈出「notice」，不是為了考高分，而是為了理解整句話。

The driver opened the old box beside his seat to find a paper road map. Under the map lay a small card with a red mark in the shape of a star. On it someone had written, When the street clock reaches sunset, return by the bridge.* Sora showed the card to Ms Day. They could not know whether it was true, but it was the only clear instruction they had. “This is not a game,” she told the class. “We need to keep time, stay together, and ask before we use anything that belongs to this town.” Sora put the card carefully back in its place.

司機打開座位旁的舊盒子，想找紙本道路圖。地圖下有張小卡片，畫著星星形狀的紅記號。上面寫著：「街鐘到達日落時刻，沿橋返回。」空把卡片拿給戴老師看。他們無法確定是否可信，但這是目前唯一清楚的指示。「這不是遊戲，」老師告訴全班，「我們得注意時間、不離隊，也要先詢問才能使用屬於這座小鎮的東西。」空小心把卡片放回原處。

A reporter on the street was asking people about the town's future. The reporter noticed the bright school bus and came near, but Ms Day politely said the class could not answer questions. Sora looked toward the old school building beyond the shops. It was much smaller than the school he knew. In the window, a young student was holding a sheet of paper with a worried face. Sora could not read the words, only the large red mark above them. He wondered whether their school had once been in trouble. He also wondered if they would ever see the school of 2026 again. “bug,” Mika read from the practice page, then tried to connect the word to something they could actually see.

街上一名記者正訪問居民對小鎮未來的看法。記者注意到鮮亮的校車走近，戴老師禮貌地說學生不便接受訪問。空望向商店後方的舊校舍。那比他熟悉的學校小得多。窗邊一名年輕學生手拿紙張，神情擔憂。空看不清上面的字，只看見一個巨大的紅色標記。他開始懷疑母校是否曾經遇到危機，也擔心自己能否再看到二○二六年的學校。「bug。」美佳讀出練習頁上的字，接著試著把它和眼前真正看得到的東西連起來。

When the old street clock rang, the bus radio came back to life. A man with a slow voice announced the date: July 18, 2006. Nobody laughed this time. Ms Day asked students to take one deep breath and stay with her. The sun was already moving west. Sora held the map and understood that they had to find the way home before it went down. The bridge behind the bus had vanished. At the same moment, the worried girl at the school window raised her paper. This time Sora could read the words: OUR SCHOOL MAY CLOSE.*

舊街鐘響起時，巴士收音機又有聲音了。男子慢慢播報日期：「二○○六年七月十八日。」這次沒有同學笑出來。戴老師請大家深呼吸並留在身邊。太陽已往西移。空拿著地圖，明白必須在日落前找到回家路。巴士後方的橋已經消失。就在這時，校舍窗邊那名憂心的女孩舉高紙張。這次空看清上面的字：「我們的學校可能關閉。」
