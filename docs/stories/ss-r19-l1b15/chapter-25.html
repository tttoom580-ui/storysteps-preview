On the next normal school trip, Sora looked out the bus window at familiar streets. Some days begin with a plan; others begin with a question no one had expected. This one gave Sora both. At the present-day school, people were coming and going, each carrying a different idea about what was important. The paper route map made it possible to hold on to a few clear details while the rest of the world kept moving. A little note used “afraid” as its key word; Sora kept the note because the rest of the line was still useful. Sora circled “interest” on a reading sheet, not to win a test but to remember the meaning of the whole sentence. The word “thank” appeared in an example someone had written earlier, and Mika asked if the example fit their situation.

下一次普通校外教學，空望著窗外熟悉的街道。有些日子從計畫開始，有些日子卻從意料之外的問題開始。這一天，空兩者都遇上了。在現在的學校，人們來來往往，每個人都覺得不同的事最重要。紙本道路地圖讓空在世界不停變動時，仍能抓住幾個清楚的細節。一張小筆記以「afraid」為關鍵字；空留下它，因為剩下的句子仍然有用。空在閱讀單上圈出「interest」，不是為了考高分，而是為了理解整句話。「thank」出現在前人留下的例句裡，美佳問這個例子是否適合現在的情況。

Mika listened without offering an easy answer. That was unusual enough for Sora to notice. They named the part they understood, then left space for the part that still felt unclear. A good plan did not have to hide its weak points. Someone nearby had a different idea, and the disagreement made the question sharper instead of ending the conversation. Sora saw two towns at once: one he remembered and one that still had to grow into it. The same street could carry more than one story. “able,” Mika read from the practice page, then tried to connect the word to something they could actually see. A little note used “goodbye” as its key word; Sora kept the note because the rest of the line was still useful. Sora circled “sometimes” on a reading sheet, not to win a test but to remember the meaning of the whole sentence.

美佳仔細聆聽，沒有立刻給出簡單答案。空注意到這份不同尋常的耐心。他們先說出已了解的部分，再為仍不清楚的地方留空間。好的計畫不需要掩藏弱點。附近有人提出不同想法，這場分歧沒有讓對話結束，反而讓問題更清楚。空同時看見兩個小鎮：一個在自己的記憶裡，一個還沒長成後來的模樣。同一條街可以承載不只一段故事。「able。」美佳讀出練習頁上的字，接著試著把它和眼前真正看得到的東西連起來。一張小筆記以「goodbye」為關鍵字；空留下它，因為剩下的句子仍然有用。空在閱讀單上圈出「sometimes」，不是為了考高分，而是為了理解整句話。

He no longer needed to visit 2006 to know that a place could be saved by many small acts. Sora felt a quick wish to fix everything at once. Then the faces nearby made the choice more complicated. It was not only about the road back to 2026. Other people had time, hopes, and worries of their own. Mika drew a line across the paper route map: facts on one side, guesses on the other. That simple mark changed how they spoke. In the corner of the paper route map, Sora found the word “doll” beside an old drawing and stopped to ask what it meant. “skirt,” Mika read from the practice page, then tried to connect the word to something they could actually see. A little note used “word” as its key word; Sora kept the note because the rest of the line was still useful.

他不必再去二○○六年，也知道許多小行動能守護一個地方。空一瞬間很想把所有事一次解決，但附近每張臉都讓選擇變得複雜。這不只是回到二○二六年的路的問題。其他人也有自己的時間、希望與憂慮。美佳在紙本道路地圖上畫一條線，一側寫事實，另一側寫猜測。這個簡單記號改變了他們說話的方式。在紙本道路地圖的角落，空看見一幅舊畫旁寫著「doll」，於是停下來詢問意思。「skirt。」美佳讀出練習頁上的字，接著試著把它和眼前真正看得到的東西連起來。一張小筆記以「word」為關鍵字；空留下它，因為剩下的句子仍然有用。

A fresh page in the paper route map gave the group a way to start again without pretending the past had not happened. Sora wrote down what could happen next, while Mika named the one thing they still needed to ask. They were not making a perfect picture. They were making a map that another person could read and challenge. “Let us keep ‘comfortable’ in our word notes,” Sora said. “We can check its meaning again if we need it.” In the corner of the paper route map, Sora found the word “ship” beside an old drawing and stopped to ask what it meant. “week,” Mika read from the practice page, then tried to connect the word to something they could actually see.

紙本道路地圖的新頁面讓大家重新開始，也不必否認過去發生的事。空寫下接下來可能發生的事，美佳則說出仍要詢問的一件事。他們畫的不是完美圖像，而是一張別人也看得懂、也能提出疑問的地圖。「把『comfortable』留在詞彙筆記裡吧，」空說，「有需要時再確認意思。」在紙本道路地圖的角落，空看見一幅舊畫旁寫著「ship」，於是停下來詢問意思。「week。」美佳讀出練習頁上的字，接著試著把它和眼前真正看得到的東西連起來。

When the driver asked where to go, the class answered together: forward. Once the surprise passed, Sora wanted to know what the new fact would mean tomorrow. Mika turned back to the paper route map and crossed out a guess that no longer fitted what they knew. Neither called the mistake foolish. Changing your mind after new evidence was part of learning. 司機問下一站去哪裡，全班一起回答：「向前。」驚喜過後，空開始想知道這個新事實明天會帶來什麼改變。美佳重新翻開紙本道路地圖，劃掉一個已不符合事實的猜測。兩人都沒有說那是愚蠢的錯。依新證據改變想法，本來就是學習的一部分。「cat」出現在前人留下的例句裡，美佳問這個例子是否適合現在的情況。「把『pack』留在詞彙筆記裡吧，」空說，「有需要時再確認意思。」在紙本道路地圖的角落，空看見一幅舊畫旁寫著「twice」，於是停下來詢問意思。

There was no great speech at the end of the day. Sora and Mika shared a look that said they had both noticed the change. Sora watched the road ahead without wishing for another turn into the past. Yesterday had given him a story, but today gave him a place to care for. The bus kept going, and every classmate reached the next stop together. Sora circled “allow” on a reading sheet, not to win a test but to remember the meaning of the whole sentence. The word “live” appeared in an example someone had written earlier, and Mika asked if the example fit their situation. “Let us keep ‘tidy’ in our word notes,” Sora said. “We can check its meaning again if we need it.”

這一天結束時沒有華麗演說。空與美佳交換眼神，知道彼此都看見了改變。空望著前方道路，不再盼望又轉進過去。昨天送他一段故事，今天則給他一個值得守護的地方。巴士繼續前進，全班一起抵達下一站。空在閱讀單上圈出「allow」，不是為了考高分，而是為了理解整句話。「live」出現在前人留下的例句裡，美佳問這個例子是否適合現在的情況。「把『tidy』留在詞彙筆記裡吧，」空說，「有需要時再確認意思。」
