At the clock square, Sora thanked Hana for helping strangers find their way. Some days begin with a plan; others begin with a question no one had expected. This one gave Sora both. At the old town square, people were coming and going, each carrying a different idea about what was important. The paper route map made it possible to hold on to a few clear details while the rest of the world kept moving. Hana was a friend, not a person who had to agree with every idea. “Let us keep ‘pair’ in our word notes,” Sora said. “We can check its meaning again if we need it.”

在鐘樓廣場，空感謝小花幫陌生人找路。有些日子從計畫開始，有些日子卻從意料之外的問題開始。這一天，空兩者都遇上了。在舊鎮廣場，人們來來往往，每個人都覺得不同的事最重要。紙本道路地圖讓空在世界不停變動時，仍能抓住幾個清楚的細節。小花是朋友，不是必須同意每個主意的人。「把『pair』留在詞彙筆記裡吧，」空說，「有需要時再確認意思。」

Hana listened without offering an easy answer. That was unusual enough for Sora to notice. They named the part they understood, then left space for the part that still felt unclear. A good plan did not have to hide its weak points. Someone nearby had a different idea, and the disagreement made the question sharper instead of ending the conversation. Sora circled “excited” on a reading sheet, not to win a test but to remember the meaning of the whole sentence. The word “mother” appeared in an example someone had written earlier, and Hana asked if the example fit their situation. “Let us keep ‘wise’ in our word notes,” Sora said. “We can check its meaning again if we need it.”

小花仔細聆聽，沒有立刻給出簡單答案。空注意到這份不同尋常的耐心。他們先說出已了解的部分，再為仍不清楚的地方留空間。好的計畫不需要掩藏弱點。附近有人提出不同想法，這場分歧沒有讓對話結束，反而讓問題更清楚。空在閱讀單上圈出「excited」，不是為了考高分，而是為了理解整句話。「mother」出現在前人留下的例句裡，小花問這個例子是否適合現在的情況。「把『wise』留在詞彙筆記裡吧，」空說，「有需要時再確認意思。」

Hana asked whether he believed their little school would have a future. Sora felt a quick wish to fix everything at once. Then the faces nearby made the choice more complicated. It was not only about the road back to 2026. Other people had time, hopes, and worries of their own. Hana drew a line across the paper route map: facts on one side, guesses on the other. That simple mark changed how they spoke. A little note used “drum” as its key word; Sora kept the note because the rest of the line was still useful. Sora circled “most” on a reading sheet, not to win a test but to remember the meaning of the whole sentence. The word “throw” appeared in an example someone had written earlier, and Hana asked if the example fit their situation.

小花問他是否相信她的小學校會有未來。空一瞬間很想把所有事一次解決，但附近每張臉都讓選擇變得複雜。這不只是回到二○二六年的路的問題。其他人也有自己的時間、希望與憂慮。小花在紙本道路地圖上畫一條線，一側寫事實，另一側寫猜測。這個簡單記號改變了他們說話的方式。一張小筆記以「drum」為關鍵字；空留下它，因為剩下的句子仍然有用。空在閱讀單上圈出「most」，不是為了考高分，而是為了理解整句話。「throw」出現在前人留下的例句裡，小花問這個例子是否適合現在的情況。

A fresh page in the paper route map gave the group a way to start again without pretending the past had not happened. Hana turned back to the paper route map and crossed out a guess that no longer fitted what they knew. Neither called the mistake foolish. Changing your mind after new evidence was part of learning. 空說相信，卻沒有解釋口袋裡的照片。驚喜過後，空開始想知道這個新事實明天會帶來什麼改變。小花重新翻開紙本道路地圖，劃掉一個已不符合事實的猜測。兩人都沒有說那是愚蠢的錯。依新證據改變想法，本來就是學習的一部分。在紙本道路地圖的角落，空看見一幅舊畫旁寫著「build」，於是停下來詢問意思。「knowledge。」小花讀出練習頁上的字，接著試著把它和眼前真正看得到的東西連起來。一張小筆記以「seed」為關鍵字；空留下它，因為剩下的句子仍然有用。

Later, when the noise of the day grew quiet, Sora remembered one face more clearly than any sign or number. The work had not been easy, and the result did not belong to one person. Still, something had moved forward. Before putting away the paper route map, Sora wrote one question for the next day. It was not the end of the story yet. Hana talked about tomorrow as if it were wide open. Sora knew the future could still need someone to care for it. “Let us keep ‘best’ in our word notes,” Sora said. “We can check its meaning again if we need it.” In the corner of the paper route map, Sora found the word “hand” beside an old drawing and stopped to ask what it meant. “remember,” Hana read from the practice page, then tried to connect the word to something they could actually see.
