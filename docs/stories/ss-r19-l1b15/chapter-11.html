The back of the future photo carried the name of a town librarian, not a teacher. The old town square had its own familiar rhythm. Sora knew the sound of footsteps there, yet something about this moment called for a closer look. It was tempting to make a quick story out of a small surprise. Instead, Sora looked for one thing that could be checked and one person who deserved to be heard. There was room in the paper route map for questions as well as answers, and today the questions were beginning to matter. The word “hate” appeared in an example someone had written earlier, and Hana asked if the example fit their situation. “Let us keep ‘often’ in our word notes,” Sora said. “We can check its meaning again if we need it.” In the corner of the paper route map, Sora found the word “white” beside an old drawing and stopped to ask what it meant.
