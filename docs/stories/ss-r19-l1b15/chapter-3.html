The class walked with Ms Day to an open shop and saw prices from twenty years before. The old town square had its own familiar rhythm. Sora knew the sound of footsteps there, yet something about this moment called for a closer look. It was tempting to make a quick story out of a small surprise. Instead, Sora looked for one thing that could be checked and one person who deserved to be heard. There was room in the paper route map for questions as well as answers, and today the questions were beginning to matter. In the corner of the paper route map, Sora found the word “begin” beside an old drawing and stopped to ask what it meant. “leave,” Mika read from the practice page, then tried to connect the word to something they could actually see. A little note used “p.m.” as its key word; Sora kept the note because the rest of the line was still useful.

The first reaction in the old town square was a mixture of surprise and hope. Sora felt both, and did not pretend otherwise. Sora took a step back to let someone with more experience speak. Help did not always mean being the first person to act. That gave Mika time to notice a detail that had been lost in the noise.
