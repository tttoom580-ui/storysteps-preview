The Seat Beside the Door

我的同桌是偶像練習生 · 第 6 / 25 章 · 1000以下 · 英中對照 · 校訂中

用互動版查詞、朗讀與收藏

Ren woke with a deep ache in his arm after dance practice. He sat at his desk and tried to do the day's reading, but the words moved before his tired eyes. Mira did not treat the pain as funny. “You may need to speak to the school health office,” she said. “It is convenient because it is near our class.” Ren looked surprised. He was used to adults telling him to work harder, not to ask for help. He went to the office and explained what hurt. The nurse gave him simple advice about rest and told him when to seek further medical care if the problem continued.

余任舞蹈排練後醒來，手臂深處仍隱隱作痛。他坐到書桌前試著讀當天教材，字句卻在疲倦的眼前晃動。米拉沒把痛當笑話。「你可以去學校健康中心問問，」她說，「就在我們教室附近，很方便。」余任有些意外。他習慣大人叫他更努力，卻很少有人叫他求助。他去了健康中心說明疼痛位置。護理師給他簡單的休息建議，也說明若症狀持續，何時需要進一步就醫。

At noon Mira sat in the school shop beside a display of old books. One book told the history of sound in the city. She opened it to a picture of people recording fish in a river. Ren sat opposite her and said he had never thought that the sound of a fish could be news. “Sometimes the most useful thing is not loud,” she replied. “However, we have to find out whether a sound belongs in our story.” She showed him the film's simple plan. The first page had a drawing of home. The next had a question: what makes a place feel safe?

中午米拉坐在校內商店的舊書展示旁。一本書講述城市聲音的歷史，裡面有河邊錄製魚類動靜的人們。余任坐在對面，說自己從沒想過魚發出的聲音也會成為新聞。「最有用的不一定最大聲，」她回答，「不過還是要想清楚哪種聲音適合我們的故事。」她給他看電影計畫，第一頁畫著家，下一頁寫著問題：什麼會讓一個地方感到安全？

An envelope arrived at the college office for Ren that afternoon. It held a new work plan from his company. His night lessons would end earlier on two weekdays. He could also keep a seat by the door in class so that he could step out for water without crossing everyone else's work. The plan was not perfect; a big show was still near. But it let him rise in the morning without feeling that every minute belonged to a manager. He showed the envelope to the class adviser, who welcomed the plan and suggested checking it again in a week.

下午，一封給余任的信封送到學校辦公室，裡面是公司更新的工作安排。兩個平日的晚間課會提早結束。他也可以坐在教室門邊，想喝水時不必穿越其他人的作品。計畫並不完美，大型演出仍然將近，但它讓他早上起床時不再覺得每分鐘都屬於經紀人。他把信拿給班級導師，導師歡迎這項調整，還建議一週後再次檢查是否有效。

That evening rain covered the path near the bus stop with mud. Ren and Mira walked carefully around it. He asked whether she ever feared that her film would fail. “All the time,” she said. “I may not win any prize. But I want to finish it in a way I can stand behind.” Ren listened. His own work often came with numbers: viewers, votes, and the next song. He had almost forgotten how to make something because he liked it. “I want to sing one song that sounds like me,” he admitted. “Then begin with eight honest seconds,” she said. He smiled at the idea.

傍晚，雨把公車站旁的小路弄得滿是泥。兩人小心繞過。余任問米拉是否害怕影片失敗。「一直都怕。」她說，「我可能拿不到獎，但我想用自己能坦然面對的方式完成。」余任聽著。他的工作總與數字有關：觀看、投票、下一首歌。他幾乎忘了能因為喜歡而創作。「我想唱一首真正像我自己的歌。」他承認。「那就從八秒真誠的歌聲開始。」她說。他喜歡這個想法，微微一笑。

Before the next class, the adviser came to the doorway to welcome Ren back after his health visit. She did not ask him to share private medical details. She simply said that he could speak to the school if he needed a change in his schedule. Mira watched him accept that offer without making a joke. It seemed to her that the college had a different sound when people were willing to listen. The bell for class rang, and the students began opening their books. Ren sat down in his new seat near the door. He could see the teacher clearly, and for once he did not feel the need to hide his tired face.

下堂課前，導師到門口歡迎余任健康諮詢後回來。她沒有追問私人醫療細節，只說如果需要調整課表，可以向學校反映。米拉看著余任沒有開玩笑，而是認真接受。她覺得大家願意傾聽時，學院似乎有不同的聲音。上課鐘響，學生打開課本。余任坐進靠門的新位置，能清楚看見老師。這次他不必躲起疲倦的神情。

At home Ren looked at the message from his manager again. The plan allowed one free evening. He could use it to rest, see a friend, or do nothing at all. He had not known that an empty hour could feel like a gift. He wrote Mira, “Thank you for telling me it was okay to ask for help.” She answered, “The nurse and adviser did the work. You were brave enough to speak.” Ren placed the phone down and opened his school book. He read three pages before sleep, then closed it without shame. Tomorrow he would need both practice and rest.

回家後，余任又看了一次經紀人的訊息。新安排給他一個自由的傍晚，可以休息、見朋友，也可以什麼都不做。他從沒想過空出一小時像收到禮物。他傳訊給米拉：「謝謝妳告訴我可以求助。」她回：「是護理師和導師幫你，你只是勇敢說出口。」余任放下手機，打開課本。睡前讀了三頁，然後心安理得地闔上。明天他仍需要排練，也需要休息。
