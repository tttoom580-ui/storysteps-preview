畫面上的白風箏

The White Kite on the Screen

我的同桌是偶像練習生 · 第 11 / 25 章 · 1000以下 · 英中對照 · 校訂中

用互動版查詞、朗讀與收藏

Mira's next film scene showed a white kite caught between two trees. The little cat watched it from below, but could not pull it free. Hana suggested drawing a long belt around the cat, like a hero. Mira laughed. “It is a joke, but perhaps the cat needs help from a friend, not a hero belt.” They checked each picture for color and motion. In one frame the kite looked too thin, almost like a line. Mira drew it again. Ren watched them work in the library and realized that making a small film required as much care as preparing a song for a large stage.

米拉的新片段畫著一只白風箏卡在兩棵樹間。小貓從下面望著，卻沒辦法把它取下。花奈開玩笑，建議替小貓畫一條像英雄那樣的長腰帶。米拉笑了。「很好笑，但小貓也許需要朋友幫忙，不是英雄腰帶。」她們逐張檢查顏色和動作。有一格的風箏太薄，幾乎成了一條線，米拉便重畫。余任在圖書館看她們工作，才發現小電影也需要和大舞台歌曲一樣用心。

That morning Ren had a health appointment because his tired days had become too frequent. He told Mira before leaving class, but did not ask her to go with him. The school nurse suggested keeping a regular sleep record and speaking with his doctor about any continuing problems. Ren sent the record to his manager, who agreed to check whether rehearsals were running too late again. Ren did not hate his work. He loved singing. But he was learning that loving a thing did not mean giving it every hour. He came back to class looking calmer, holding a basket of fruit from the shop.

那天早上，余任因為最近常感疲倦，去做健康諮詢。他出教室前告訴米拉，但沒有要求她陪同。校護建議他規律記錄睡眠，如果問題持續就與醫師討論。余任把紀錄交給經紀人，經紀人同意再檢查排練是否又拖得太晚。余任並不討厭工作，他很愛唱歌；只是逐漸懂得，愛一件事不表示要把每小時都交出去。他回教室時平靜些了，手裡提著水果店的小籃子。

During afternoon club time a new player joined the film team. Her name was Nia, and she was studying piano. She offered to make a simple sound for the kite scene without charging anyone. Mira was happy but asked her to write down what she made, so the team could credit her. “Dear Mira,” Nia wrote in a playful note, “I will send three sounds, and you can choose one.” Mira thanked her. Ren smiled at the note. In the past he had often received pictures and gifts from people who wanted his attention. This was different: Nia wanted the film to be useful to everyone.

下午社團來了一位新夥伴妮雅，她學鋼琴，主動提議替風箏場景做簡單聲效，不收費。米拉很高興，仍請她記下創作內容，好在片尾署名。「親愛的米拉，」妮雅在玩笑紙條上寫，「我會寄三種聲音給妳選。」米拉道謝。余任看了紙條笑了。過去常有人送他照片或禮物，只想得到注意。這次不同：妮雅想讓電影對整個團隊都有用。

A student asked if Ren would share a picture of himself with the film club online. Ren answered, “Not today. You can share our work after we agree on what to show.” The student looked hurt for a moment. Mira sat with him and explained that a person could say no and still like the club. He understood and helped put away those white paper kites from the art table. They did not need a fight to settle it. Ren later said to Mira, “I often forget that no can be a kind answer.” “It can,” she replied, “if you give people a clear one.”

有同學問余任能否和電影社拍照上網。余任說：「今天不行。等大家同意哪些作品能公開，再分享吧。」那位同學一時有點受傷。米拉坐下解釋，一個人說不，仍可能喜歡這個社團。對方明白了，並幫忙收拾美術桌上的白色紙風箏。這件事不需要爭吵也能解決。余任後來告訴米拉：「我常忘了拒絕也能很友善。」「可以的，」她說，「只要說得清楚。」

On Friday, Ren called his mother to say the health visit had gone well. She asked if he had needed to go to the hospital. “No,” he answered, “the school nurse and my doctor helped me plan what to watch for.” He did not give Mira his private records, because she did not need them to be his friend. Instead he told her that he would be taking an evening off. She said that was good news and invited him to look at the kite drawings on Monday. Ren put the phone down and made dinner with his family. A normal evening had become something he was proud to protect.

星期五余任打電話告訴媽媽健康諮詢很順利。媽媽問是否需要去醫院。「不需要，」他回答，「學校護理師與醫師幫我安排該注意什麼。」他沒有把私人紀錄給米拉看，因為當朋友不需要那些資料，只告訴她自己今晚要休息。米拉說這是好消息，邀他星期一再來看風箏畫稿。余任放下手機，和家人一起做晚餐。普通夜晚成了他想守護的事。

At the end of the day the teacher looked at the new film scene. She asked why the kite was important. Mira pointed to the cat waiting under the tree. “Because the cat will learn that being helped does not make it weak,” she said. Ren sat beside her and watched the final drawing. For a moment he thought of himself, sleeping with his head on a jacket in the middle of class. Many people had tried to carry him higher. Very few had asked whether he wanted to rest. He did not say all of this aloud. Instead he told Mira that the kite scene was his favorite so far.

一天結束前，老師看了新場景，問風箏為何重要。米拉指向樹下等待的小貓。「因為牠會知道，被幫助不代表自己軟弱。」她說。余任坐在旁邊看最後一格畫面。他一時想起自己在教室把頭枕在外套上睡覺的樣子。許多人都想把他推往更高處，卻很少有人問他想不想休息。他沒把這些全說出來，只告訴米拉，風箏是目前最喜歡的一幕。
