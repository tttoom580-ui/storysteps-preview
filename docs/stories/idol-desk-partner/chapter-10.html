One Button, Two Answers

我的同桌是偶像練習生 · 第 10 / 25 章 · 1000以下 · 英中對照 · 校訂中

用互動版查詞、朗讀與收藏

Every evening Ren usually watched his practice video before sleep. Now he left his phone face down because the comments made him worry about Mira. He had a sweet message from a fan, but a cruel one sat right below it. The difference hurt. In the dark practice room he pressed a button to start the song, then stopped after one line. His teacher asked whether he was sick. “No,” Ren said, “but I have trouble with something outside this room.” The teacher let him sit quietly for a moment. “You have to care about the person singing,” she said, “not only the song.”

余任通常每天晚上睡前看排練影片，現在卻把手機倒扣，因為留言讓他擔心米拉。粉絲寄來甜蜜的訊息，下面卻緊接著難聽的話，差異令人難受。黑暗的練習室裡，他按下播放按鈕，唱了一句就停。老師問他是不是病了。「不是，」他說，「只是房間外有件事讓我困擾。」老師讓他靜坐片刻。「你得關心唱歌的人，」她說，「不只是歌曲。」

The next day Mira found him sitting near the left side of the canteen. He had ordered potato soup but was staring at the menu again. “Did you forget what you chose?” she asked. Ren smiled a little. “I forgot I was allowed to choose.” She sat across from him and told him about a new scene in her film. A rose had fallen from a high window, and the cat ran outside to bring it back. “Why a rose?” he asked. “Because even a small thing can have a long way home,” she said. They talked about the film until his soup became cool.

隔天米拉發現余任坐在餐廳左側。他點了馬鈴薯湯，卻又盯著菜單。「你忘了點什麼嗎？」她問。余任淡淡地笑。「我忘了自己可以選。」她坐到對面，講起動畫的新場景：玫瑰從高窗落下，小貓跑出去把它帶回來。「為什麼是玫瑰？」「因為即使小小的東西，也可能有很長的回家路。」米拉說。他們聊電影聊到湯都涼了。

After lunch Ms. Park offered Ren two possible public answers. The first was a long post about his private life. The second was a short note: the college student in the picture was working on an approved school film, and the public should respect her privacy. “You do not have to name Mira,” she said. Ren asked whether Mira could read the second note before it went out. She did. She suggested changing one line so it would not sound as if he owned her work. Ms. Park accepted the change. That evening the company shared the short note, with no photo and no new details.

午餐後朴小姐給余任兩種公開說法。第一種是長篇描述他的私人生活。第二種是短聲明：照片裡的學院學生正在參與經核准的校園電影，請大眾尊重她的隱私。「不用公布米拉的名字。」她說。余任請米拉先看第二種。米拉建議改掉一句，免得聽起來像她的作品屬於余任。朴小姐接受修改。當晚公司發布了短聲明，沒有照片，也沒有其他私人細節。

Some people online were still curious. Others said the short answer was enough. Ren could not control them, and this was the hard part of being seen. Mira told him that the film room was still open if he wanted to work, but he could also stay home. He chose to come for twenty minutes. They listened to the eight-second song and found that it was a little too loud under the cat's first steps. Ren lowered the sound himself. “I want people to hear the story,” he said. “I do not want my voice to stand in front of it.” Mira looked at him with new warmth.

網路上有些人仍很好奇，也有人覺得簡短說明已足夠。余任無法控制他們，這是被看見最難的一部分。米拉說，他想工作時電影教室仍然開著，但也能回家休息。他選擇來二十分鐘。兩人聽八秒配樂，發現放在小貓最初的腳步下太大聲。余任親手調小音量。「我希望大家聽見故事，」他說，「不是讓我的聲音擋在故事前面。」米拉看著他，心裡生出新的暖意。

In art class the students were asked to watch a short film and explain one interesting moment. Mira chose the scene where a leaf began to fall from a tree. The camera stayed still, but the leaf crossed the picture and showed the wind. Ren said the scene felt calm because the sound was small. The teacher asked what would happen if the music became loud. “We might stop seeing the leaf,” Mira answered. Ren wrote that down. When he looked at her later, she was busy drawing again. He liked that she could notice a leaf on a screen even on a day when many people were talking about her online.

美術課上，老師請學生看短片，說出一個有趣的瞬間。米拉選了樹葉開始掉落的場景。鏡頭沒有移動，但葉子穿過畫面，讓人看見風。余任說，因聲音很輕，場景格外安靜。老師問如果音樂變大會怎樣。「我們可能就不看葉子了。」米拉回答。余任把話記下。之後他望向米拉，她又忙著畫畫。他喜歡她的這點：即使網路上好多人議論她，她仍能注意到螢幕上的一片葉子。

On their way out, the sky turned pink around the school gate. A student from the dance club waved at Ren. He waved back instead of pulling his cap over his eyes. It was a small change, but Mira noticed. “Will you have time to finish your work tonight?” she asked. “I will,” he said. “I also plan to sleep.” They walked to the bus stop together, careful not to stand in the road. Ren did not reach for her hand, although he wanted to. He had learned that a smile could be a full answer for one evening. He would rather move slowly than make her feel trapped.

出門時學校大門周圍的天空染成粉紅色。舞蹈社的同學向余任揮手，他也回應，沒有把帽子拉下來遮臉。只是小改變，米拉卻看見了。「今晚來得及做完工作嗎？」她問。「可以，」他說，「也打算睡覺。」他們一起走到公車站，小心沒有站上車道。余任想牽她的手，卻沒有伸手。他學會了，一個微笑也能是完整的晚間回答。他寧願慢慢走，也不想讓她感到被困住。
