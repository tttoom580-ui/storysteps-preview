第三張桌子的約定

The Third Desk Agreement

我的同桌是偶像練習生 · 第 2 / 25 章 · 1000以下 · 英中對照 · 校訂中

On Tuesday Ren arrived late again. Mira put a pen on his desk beside a clear copy of the class notes. “You missed the third question,” she said. “You can ask me about it, but you must do your own answer.” Ren looked at the page as if it were a piece of gold. “Will you tell anybody?” he asked quietly. “Not without a good reason,” Mira replied. “But I will not hide a problem that could hurt someone.” He thought about this for a long time. The teacher asked the class to work in pairs, and neither student moved from the third desk.

星期二余任又遲到了。米拉把一枝筆放在他桌上，旁邊是清楚的課堂筆記。「你漏了第三題。」她說，「可以問我，但答案得自己寫。」余任看著那張紙，像看到黃金。「你會告訴任何人嗎？」他輕聲問。「沒有正當理由，我不會。」米拉回答，「但如果有事會傷害別人，我不會替你隱瞞。」他想了好久。老師請大家兩人一組，第三張桌子的兩人都沒有移動。

At lunch they went to the small café near the old church. Ren lived in an apartment above a bakery on the next street. His company practiced at night because its room was full during the day. He had moved to this country with his family years ago, but his fans believed he lived far away. “People guess many things from one photograph,” he said. “They forget there is a real person here.” Mira understood. Her own family had once believed that making cartoons was only a game. She had learned that clear work was more useful than angry words.

午餐時兩人去了舊教堂附近的小咖啡館。余任住在隔壁街麵包店樓上的公寓。因為公司白天的練習室都有人用，他只能夜間排練。他多年前和家人搬來這個國家，粉絲卻以為他住得很遠。「大家看一張照片就亂猜，」他說，「然後忘記照片裡是真正的人。」米拉懂。她的家人以前也以為畫動畫只是玩遊戲。她逐漸學會，清楚的作品比生氣的話更有用。

Mira opened her notebook. “Here is my offer. You may study from my notes, but no copying answers. In return, help me record a short song for my own animation. Your company must say it is allowed before I use it.” Ren stared at her. “You do not want a ticket to a show?” “No.” “A photo with me?” “No.” “A little money?” “I can pay for sound work if that is required,” she answered. He laughed for the first time. On the café sofa, a man was speaking into a telephone, so they moved to a quiet table by a window.

米拉打開筆記本。「我的條件是這樣：你可以看我的筆記學習，但不能抄答案。交換的是你幫我的動畫錄一小段歌，使用前必須先獲得公司許可。」余任盯著她。「妳不想要演出門票？」「不要。」「跟我合照？」「不要。」「一點報酬？」「如果錄音工作需要付費，我可以照規定付。」她回答。他第一次笑了。咖啡館沙發上有個男人正講電話，兩人於是移到窗邊安靜的小桌。

They wrote four rules on the page. One: Mira would keep Ren's private information to herself unless safety made it necessary to speak to a responsible adult. Two: Ren would not ask Mira to finish his schoolwork. Three: neither would post the other person's pictures. Four: either person could stop the exchange. Ren wanted to pay her for the notes; she refused because everyone could get the same notes from the school site. “The song is real work,” she added. “We should ask the company about that separately.” He nodded. For once a serious talk made him feel lighter, not smaller.

他們在紙上寫下四條規則。第一，除非安全需要向負責任的大人說明，米拉不透露余任的私人資訊。第二，余任不請米拉代寫作業。第三，兩人都不擅自發布彼此的照片。第四，任何一方都能終止交換。余任想付錢買筆記，她拒絕，因為學校網站本來就有相同資料。「但唱歌是正式工作，」她補充，「這得另外向公司確認。」他點頭。這回嚴肅談話竟讓他輕鬆，而不是感到被責備。

After the café, Mira showed Ren the school pond. A duck rested near the water. “I come here when my mind is full,” she said. “You look like you have no place to rest.” Ren sat on the bank and watched the light move. A reporter from a local music page walked past, but she did not know his face without stage lights. Ren stayed still until she left. “I thought you would want to use my name,” he said. Mira closed her notebook. “I want to use my own name on my film,” she answered. “I need help, but I do not want a borrowed future.”

離開咖啡館後，米拉帶余任去看校園池塘。一隻鴨子在水邊休息。「腦袋太亂時，我就來這裡。」她說，「你看起來沒有地方可以好好休息。」余任坐在岸邊，看水光移動。一位地方音樂網站的記者走過，沒認出沒有舞台燈光的他。他等對方離開才開口：「我以為妳會想利用我的名氣。」米拉闔上筆記本。「我想把自己的名字寫在自己的電影上，」她回答，「我需要幫忙，但不想借別人的未來。」

That night Ren showed the agreement to his manager, Ms. Park. She read each line carefully and drew a circle around the song request. “A new recording needs permission and a written plan,” she said. “That can be done. But your school hours cannot disappear because you work late.” Ren almost said he could manage everything. Then he remembered falling asleep in class. Ms. Park promised to ask about earlier practice times. Ren sent Mira a short message: “The exchange can begin, but we will wait for written approval for the music.” Mira wrote back, “Good. And please eat lunch tomorrow.”

那晚余任把協議拿給經紀人朴小姐看。她仔細讀每一條，圈起錄歌曲的要求。「新錄音需要許可和書面計畫，」她說，「這可以辦。但你不能因為工作晚，就讓上課時間消失。」余任差點說自己都能應付，隨即想起白天在課堂上睡著的事。朴小姐答應詢問能否提早排練。他傳訊給米拉：「交換可以開始，但音樂要等正式核准。」米拉回覆：「很好。還有，明天記得吃午飯。」
