電影裡的黃線

The Yellow String in the Film

我的同桌是偶像練習生 · 第 5 / 25 章 · 1000以下 · 英中對照 · 校訂中

Mira's film had a yellow string hanging by the little cat's window. It belonged to a toy moon. In the next scene a child would pull the string, and the moon would rise above a paper city. Ren asked why she had made this strange thing. “When I was a teenager, my father took me to a farm,” she said. “I saw an old wind toy there. It moved very slow, but it looked like a real night sky.” She had saved the memory for years. Now she wanted it to show that even a small hand could change the way a room felt.

米拉的動畫裡，小貓窗旁垂著一條黃色細線，連著玩具月亮。下一幕，孩子會拉動細線，讓月亮在紙做的城市上方升起。余任問她為何做這麼奇怪的東西。「我還是青少年時，爸爸帶我去農場，」她說，「那裡有一個舊風力玩具。動得很慢，卻很像真正的夜空。」她把記憶收藏多年，如今想用它表現：小小一隻手，也能改變房間的氣氛。

The college was holding a short movie lesson about how to make sound from everyday objects. A teacher showed them a bowl, a door, and a bicycle wheel that needed oil. Ren listened as a group of students made rain sounds with paper. Mira held up the yellow string from her model. “If I pull it straight through this little hole, there is a soft sound,” she explained. “That could be the sound of hope.” Ren tried it and laughed. The tiny noise was almost lost in the room. But when everyone became still, they could hear it clearly. It was not magic, only good attention.

學校正在上一堂短片聲音課，教大家用日常物品製作聲效。老師展示碗、門，以及需要加油的腳踏車輪。余任聽同學們用紙做出雨聲。米拉舉起模型上的黃線。「如果把它筆直拉過這個小洞，就有輕輕的聲音，」她解釋，「可以當希望的聲音。」余任試拉，笑了。聲音在教室裡幾乎聽不到，但大家安靜下來後就聽得很清楚。這不是魔法，只是認真聽見細節。

During lunch Ren received a letter from his company. It was about a shop that wanted to sell a toy with his picture on it. The shop also wanted to use part of the school song, although the music had not yet been released. He frowned. “That song belongs to our film project,” he said. “It is not for a toy shop.” Mira read the letter only after he invited her to. “Ask Ms. Park where the rights begin and end,” she advised. “Do not try to settle this through a private message.” They wrote down the topic and took it to the proper office after class.

午餐時余任收到公司的一封信。有家商店想賣印著他照片的玩具，還想用一段尚未公開的校園歌曲。他皺起眉。「那首歌是我們電影計畫的一部分，不是給玩具店用的。」他說。等他邀請後，米拉才看信。「問朴小姐著作使用的範圍，」她建議，「不要靠私人訊息處理。」兩人記下議題，下課後交給合適的單位。

On the way back they passed a bank and a small food shop. The shop owner was trying to feed a tired yellow cat. Mira stopped to help set down a clean dish, but did not touch the cat without asking. Ren watched the animal eat. “You think about small things,” he said. “They are not small to the person who needs them,” Mira answered. As they walked, Ren stepped on the edge of a stone and hurt his toe a little. Mira stopped and waited, refusing to pull him along. He laughed at his own slow step. For once he was not afraid of being behind.

回校路上，他們經過銀行和小食店。店主正試著餵一隻疲倦的黃貓。米拉停下幫忙放乾淨的食盤，卻沒在未經同意下摸貓。余任看牠吃飯。「妳很在乎小事情。」他說。「對需要的人來說，那就不是小事。」米拉回答。走著走著，余任踩到石頭邊緣，腳趾有點疼。米拉停下等他，沒有拉著他趕路。他笑自己的腳步慢。這一次，他不害怕落在後面。

Back in the drawing room, many students were working on their own films. Mira had never noticed how different their stories were until Ren came to look. One film was about a grandmother who loved music. Another followed a small dog through a city. A third showed the same corner in morning and evening light. Ren said he had spent so much time making songs for an audience that he had forgotten how many kinds of art people could make. Mira put the yellow moon beside her computer and gave him a small task: count the seconds between the cat's first step and the bell. He did it twice and wrote the result clearly.

回到繪圖教室，許多學生都在製作自己的電影。直到余任來看，米拉才發現大家的故事多麼不同：有片子講愛音樂的祖母，有片子跟著小狗穿城而過，還有一部比較同一街角早晚的光線。余任說他太久只做給觀眾看的歌曲，差點忘記藝術能有這麼多種類。米拉把黃色月亮放到電腦旁，給他一項小工作：數小貓第一步和鈴聲之間隔了幾秒。他數兩遍，把結果清楚寫下。

That evening Ms. Park called. The toy shop's request was outside the agreed use, and it would not go ahead. The school's little film could keep its own eight seconds of music. Ren told Mira the news while she drew the last frame of the moon rising. “Where do you want the sound to begin?” he asked. She pointed to the moment the string moved. “Here. Not when the moon is already high.” He nodded. They were learning to notice what came before the lovely moment, not just the lovely moment itself. It was a lesson he wished he had learned before becoming famous.

當晚朴小姐來電。玩具店的要求不在原先核准的範圍，計畫不會進行。校園小電影能保有自己的八秒配樂。余任在米拉畫完月亮升起的最後一格時告訴她。「妳想從哪裡開始放聲音？」他問。米拉指向細線剛移動的瞬間。「這裡，不是月亮已經升高時。」他點頭。他們開始學會注意美好瞬間之前的過程，而不只看結果。余任真希望自己出名之前就懂得這件事。
