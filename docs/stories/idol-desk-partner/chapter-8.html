不該上網的檔案

The File That Did Not Belong Online

我的同桌是偶像練習生 · 第 8 / 25 章 · 1000以下 · 英中對照 · 校訂中

用互動版查詞、朗讀與收藏

At noon the film club met in a small room above the college museum. The room was full of books, posters, and old cameras. A student complained that learning about file names was boring. The teacher held up a folder and said, “Every film has pictures and sounds made by different people. A mistake in one file can hurt all of them.” Each student needed a clear name for every piece of work. Mira called her sound file CAT_WINDOW_APPROVED, and she wrote the date beside it. Ren leaned over to listen. “That is less beautiful than a song name,” he said. “But much more useful,” Mira replied.

中午電影社在學院博物館樓上的小房間聚會。房裡擺滿書、海報與舊相機。有學生抱怨學檔案名稱很無聊。老師舉起資料夾說：「一部片的畫面與聲音來自不同的人，一個檔案的錯誤就可能傷到大家。」每個學生都得為作品清楚命名。米拉把聲音檔叫作 CAT_WINDOW_APPROVED，旁邊記上日期。余任彎過身聽。「這比歌名難聽。」他說。「卻好用得多。」米拉回答。

A new assistant from the studio came to the club with a small card. He asked if he could use Ren's recording in a short online video about the school. “It is only eight seconds,” he said. “I can pay one dollar for it if that helps.” Mira did not laugh at him. “The size of a song does not decide who owns it,” she explained. “Our permission is for my film, not for something else.” Ren asked him to speak with Ms. Park before using anything. The assistant looked disappointed but put the card away. A small rule had stopped a big problem.

錄音室的新助理拿著小卡片來社團，問能否把余任的錄音用在學校的網路短片。「才八秒，」他說，「如果有幫助，我可以付一元。」米拉沒有嘲笑他。「歌曲短不短，不會改變權利歸屬，」她解釋，「我們的許可是給我的電影，不是其他用途。」余任請他先跟朴小姐談。助理雖失望，仍收起卡片。一條看似小的規則避免了大麻煩。

Later the club watched student work. Hana had made a film about a queen who could not find her crown, and another student had filmed a train rolling through an empty town. Mira liked them both. But she noticed an old photo in one student's draft: people standing outside Ren's studio, with faces clear enough to know. “Was everyone asked?” she said. The student looked again. “I did not think about that.” They agreed to remove the picture until the people in it had given permission. The teacher called it a successful lesson because no one had needed to be shamed to learn.

之後社團看同學作品。花奈拍了找不到王冠的女王，另一人拍了列車穿過空城。米拉都很喜歡，但在其中一部片裡看見一張舊照片：人們站在余任的錄音室外，臉清楚得能辨認。「有問過每個人嗎？」她問。那位同學重新看了一眼。「我沒想到。」大家同意先移除照片，等獲得當事人同意再說。老師稱這是成功的學習，因為不需要羞辱任何人也能改進。

At lunch Ren tried to eat while reading the script for his next performance. He set his fork down because he was not tasting the food at all. “Listen,” Mira told him, pointing to the quiet place outside the window. “You do not have to do two things at once.” Ren closed the book and looked at the clouds. “When I make a mistake, people tell me to work twice as hard,” he said. “What if I work twice as hard and make more mistakes?” Mira took a slow breath. “Then stop and ask what the mistake is teaching you. More work is not always the answer.”

午餐時余任一邊吃東西一邊讀下場演出的腳本。他放下叉子，發現自己根本沒吃出味道。「聽我說，」米拉指向窗外安靜的角落，「你不用同時做兩件事。」余任闔上書，看著雲。「我一犯錯，大家就叫我加倍努力，」他說，「如果加倍努力反而犯更多錯呢？」米拉慢慢吸氣。「那就停下來想想錯誤在教什麼。更多工作不一定是答案。」

As the group packed up, a large roll of paper began moving toward the open stair. It could have been dangerous if someone had stepped on it. Hana caught the roll and set it flat on the table. Mira thanked her and asked the teacher whether they should keep long paper near the door at all. The teacher said no and moved the storage place. Ren watched this and thought about the online video request. A problem could begin with something small: a loose paper roll, a copied sound, or a picture posted without asking. None was a reason to stop making art, but all were reasons to pay attention. He helped put the last folder away.

大家收東西時，一大捲紙朝敞開的樓梯口滾去。若有人踩到會很危險。花奈接住紙捲，把它平放桌上。米拉道謝，也問老師長紙捲是否適合放門邊。老師說不適合，便換了收納位置。余任看著，想起有人想擅自用錄音的事。問題常從小事開始：會滾的紙、被複製的聲音、沒問就發布的照片。這些都不是停止創作的理由，卻都是認真注意的理由。他幫忙收好最後一份資料夾。

Before leaving, Mira put a large label on the approved sound folder: NOT FOR OTHER USE. The teacher said it was a good idea to make the file rules easy to see. Ren looked at the folder and thought of the million people watching his videos. Some days he felt as if his voice belonged to everyone else. The room was full of students who wanted to learn, not take. “Tell me if I forget this lesson,” he said. Mira held up the label. “I will. But you can remind me too.” Neither of them knew that someone outside the room had already taken a different picture.

離開前，米拉在核准的聲音資料夾貼上大標籤：「不得作其他用途。」老師說，把檔案規則標清楚是好主意。余任看著資料夾，想起觀看他影片的百萬人。有些日子，他覺得自己的聲音彷彿屬於所有人。這房間卻滿是想學習而非占有的學生。「如果我忘了這堂課，提醒我。」他說。米拉舉起標籤。「會的，但你也可以提醒我。」兩人都不知道，房間外早有人拍下另一張照片。
