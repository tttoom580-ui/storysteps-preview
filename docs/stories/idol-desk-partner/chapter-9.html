那是誰的照片？

Whose Picture Was It?

我的同桌是偶像練習生 · 第 9 / 25 章 · 1000以下 · 英中對照 · 校訂中

用互動版查詞、朗讀與收藏

The photo appeared online on Saturday morning. It showed Ren carrying Mira's drawing box outside the studio. A bright ring of light from a street sign made them look as if they stood on a stage. Under the photograph, people asked whose friend she was. Some said Mira was a singer. Others said she was trying to become famous. None of those words was true. Mira sat on her bed with a warm cup of milk and felt lonely for the first time since joining the film club. She had done nothing wrong, but strangers were making a new life for her in comments.

星期六早上，一張照片出現在網路上：余任在錄音室外幫米拉搬畫箱，街牌的圓形燈光讓兩人像站在舞台上。照片下方有人問她是誰的朋友，有人說她是歌手，也有人說她想靠余任出名。這些都不是真的。米拉坐在床上，捧著一杯溫牛奶，第一次在加入電影社後感到孤單。她明明沒做錯事，陌生人卻在留言裡替她寫另一種人生。

Ren called. “I am sorry. If you want me to say something, I will.” Mira was glad he had asked rather than acted first. “Please do not tell the world my name,” she said. “We should learn where the picture came from before we say anything else.” Ren agreed. He knew how a photograph could lose its first meaning when people shared it again and again. Ms. Park asked the company to check the original post without guessing who had taken it. They were not trying to find a person to hate. They wanted to stop the use of a private moment.

余任打電話來。「對不起。如果妳想要我說些什麼，我可以。」米拉很慶幸他先問而非擅自行動。「請不要對全世界說我的名字，」她說，「先弄清照片從哪裡來，再決定要說什麼。」他答應。余任知道照片被轉傳一次又一次，原意可能完全消失。朴小姐請公司查原始貼文，不先猜是誰拍的。他們不是要找一個人來恨，而是希望私人片刻不再被利用。

At school on Monday, a boy asked Mira if she liked Ren only because he was popular. She took a breath. “I like working on my film,” she answered. “That is a fact you can check. Please do not ask about my private life.” The boy looked embarrassed. Hana walked over and sat beside her. “You do not have to bear this alone,” she said. “I can come with you if you want to speak to the student office.” Mira was thankful. She did not need a brave speech to a whole crowd. She needed one kind person in a difficult hour.

星期一在學校，一個男生問米拉是不是因為余任受歡迎才喜歡他。她深吸一口氣。「我喜歡做我的電影，」她回答，「這是可以查證的事實。請不要問我的私生活。」男生有些尷尬。花奈走來坐在她旁邊。「妳不用一個人承受，」她說，「如果想去學生事務處，我能陪妳。」米拉很感激。她不需要在眾人面前慷慨陳詞，只需要困難時刻的一個好朋友。

That afternoon the school adviser spoke to the class about pictures. She gave examples from a farmer who had posted a photo of a field and a foreigner who did not understand the local signs. “A photo may show a real place,” she said, “but it cannot tell every story behind it. Keep the difference between what you see and what you guess.” The students wrote the idea down. Ren listened carefully from the back seat. He had spent months keeping his two lives separate, but now he saw that a secret could also affect someone who had never asked to enter his public world.

下午導師在班上談照片。她舉例：一位農夫發布田地照片，以及一名看不懂當地標誌的外國人。「照片可能拍到真實的地方，」她說，「但拍不到全部背景。記得分清眼見與猜測。」學生們把重點記下。余任從後排認真聽著。他幾個月來努力分開兩種生活，如今才明白，祕密也可能影響從未要求走進他公眾世界的人。

After the class talk, Mira went to wash her hands near the toilet at the end of the hall. She looked at herself in the mirror and wondered why one picture had made her feel so small. Hana waited outside and asked if she was well enough to return to the film room. “I am not happy, but I can work,” Mira said. “That is enough for this afternoon.” Hana offered to sit with her while she drew, without asking for all the details. Mira accepted. She had thought she needed a perfect answer to every comment. Instead she needed a quiet table, her own work, and a friend who would stay.

班級談話後，米拉到走廊盡頭廁所旁洗手。她看著鏡中的自己，不明白為何一張照片會讓她感到如此渺小。花奈等在外面，問她狀態還好嗎，能不能回電影教室。「我不開心，但可以工作，」米拉說，「今天下午這樣就夠了。」花奈提出陪她畫畫，不追問所有細節。米拉答應了。她原以為每則留言都要有完美回答；其實更需要安靜桌面、自己的工作，以及願意陪伴的朋友。

The company found that the picture came from a public street camera used by a music page. Its writer had not asked Mira before sharing an image of her face. Ms. Park asked for the image to be removed and for the page to correct its words. There was nothing to gain from making up a new story about the writer. The page took the picture down that night. Mira still felt sad, and Ren did not try to make her stop feeling that way. They sat by the wall of the drawing room and ate sandwiches with meat and cheese. For a little while, no one had to be a star.

公司查到照片來自音樂網站使用的公開街景攝影畫面。撰稿人刊登米拉清楚的臉，卻沒有先徵得同意。朴小姐要求下架照片並更正文字。編造撰稿人的新故事對誰都沒好處。網站當晚移除了照片。米拉仍難過，余任沒有要求她立刻開心。他們坐在畫室牆邊，吃著夾肉與起司的三明治。短短一會兒，誰也不必當明星。
