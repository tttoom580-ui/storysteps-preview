沒有名字的門票

A Ticket Without a Name

我的同桌是偶像練習生 · 第 12 / 25 章 · 1000以下 · 英中對照 · 校訂中

用互動版查詞、朗讀與收藏

On Tuesday afternoon, a package came from the studio. Inside were two tickets to a band rehearsal, a small note, and a card with the correct entry time. Ren had invited Mira and her teacher to watch the group practice, not to celebrate him, but to hear how a song changed on a real stage. Mira put on her glasses and read the note twice. “The last train leaves early,” she said. “We must be home before that.” Ren agreed. He looked tired, yet his eyes grew bright when he talked about music. The teacher offered to come with them, so no one had to find a way through the busy building alone.

星期二下午，錄音室寄來一個包裹，裡面有兩張樂團排練門票、一張短箋和寫著入場時間的卡片。余任邀米拉和老師去看練習，不是為了慶祝自己，而是想讓她們聽真正的舞台如何改變歌曲。米拉戴上眼鏡，把紙條讀了兩遍。「最後一班火車很早開，」她說，「我們得趕上。」余任答應。他看起來疲倦，談起音樂時眼睛卻亮了。老師也會同行，大家不用獨自穿過繁忙的大樓。

At five o’clock the hall was full of workers. An actor from a small college play was trying to carry a large chair around a corner. Ren stopped to help him without making a show of it. “Thank you,” the actor said. “I thought famous people were too busy for this.” “I am a student before I am anything else today,” Ren replied. Mira liked the answer, though she noticed he said it very quietly. She had seen him stand under stage lights online, but there was something more interesting about the young man who stopped to move a chair out of someone's way.

五點時大廳滿是工作人員。一名小型校園戲劇的演員正努力把大椅子搬過轉角。余任停下幫忙，沒有刻意讓人看見。「謝謝，」演員說，「我以為有名的人忙得沒空做這些。」「今天我先是學生，才是別的身分。」余任回答。米拉喜歡這句話，雖然他講得很輕。她在網路上見過他站在舞台燈下的樣子，但眼前願意替別人挪椅子的青年，反而讓她更感興趣。

The band played three songs. Ren stood in the middle for one of them, but the others had their own parts. His voice sounded different beside the drums and piano. At first he tried to make every note perfect; then the band leader asked him to listen to the full sound. “It is not only you,” she said. “We have to play together.” He closed his eyes for a moment. When the music began again, he stopped fighting the other voices. Mira heard a kind of joy that had not been in his old videos. She wanted her film to feel that open too.

樂團演奏了三首歌。余任其中一首站在中央，但其他人也各自有重要部分。他的聲音在鼓和鋼琴旁變得不同。起初他想每個音都完美，樂團隊長卻請他聽整體聲音。「不只你一個人，」她說，「我們得一起演奏。」他閉上眼一會兒。音樂再起時，他不再和其他聲部競爭。米拉聽見他舊影片裡沒有的快樂，也希望自己的電影能有那份開闊。

During the break a media worker asked Mira if she was Ren's girlfriend. Mira did not feel ready to answer a question about feelings she was still learning. “I am an art student working on my own film,” she said. “Please ask about the music, or the show.” The worker looked at the ticket and moved on. Ren came over when he saw her face. “Was that hard?” he asked. “A little,” she admitted. “But not as hard as pretending I know an answer when I do not.” They went to a quiet corner and drank water. No one spoke for a while, and the silence felt comfortable.

休息時一位媒體工作者問米拉是不是余任的女朋友。米拉還在理解自己的感覺，並不想被迫回答。「我是藝術學院學生，正在完成自己的電影，」她說，「請問音樂或演出的事。」工作者看了看門票便離開。余任見她神情不對，走來問：「很難回答嗎？」「有點，」她承認，「但不會比假裝知道答案更難。」他們走到安靜角落喝水，暫時都沒說話。沉默反而很舒服。

The band room had one old door that needed a key, so a worker stood beside it to guide guests through the correct entrance. At six o’clock the lights above the back stairs were poor, and the worker asked everyone to use the brighter hall. Mira was glad the college teacher had come with them. It was easy to lose the way in a busy building, especially when people were carrying instruments. Ren helped point visitors toward the front door without sharing any private workroom details. Later Mira wrote the route in her notebook. She could keep a memory of a lovely evening without putting other people's faces on a public page.

樂團房有一扇舊門需要鑰匙，工作人員便在旁邊引導訪客走正確入口。六點時後樓梯的燈光不足，工作人員請大家改走較明亮的走廊。米拉慶幸老師同行。繁忙大樓裡人們又搬著樂器，確實容易迷路。余任幫忙指向正門，沒有透露私人工作室的細節。後來米拉在本子上記下路線。她可以留下美好晚上的回憶，卻不必把別人的臉放上公開網頁。

On the way home they passed a supermarket where flowers stood in buckets by the door. Mira pointed at one dry plant. “If nobody gives that flower water, it may die,” she said. “A story is like that too. It needs the right care.” Ren nodded, thinking of his own songs and his sleep. He was beginning to see why an empty evening had mattered so much. At the station Mira thanked him for the tickets. Ren said, “I hope you will come to a real concert one day.” Mira smiled. “I might. But not if I have to stop making my film.” “I would not ask you to,” he replied.

回家路上，他們經過超市，門口一桶桶放著鮮花。米拉指著其中一盆乾枯的植物。「如果沒人澆水，它可能會死，」她說，「故事也一樣，需要合適的照顧。」余任點頭，想著自己的歌與睡眠。他漸漸懂得，為何一個空下來的傍晚那麼珍貴。車站裡米拉謝謝他給門票。余任說：「希望有一天妳會來看正式演唱會。」米拉笑了。「可能會，但不能因此停下我的電影。」「我不會那樣要求妳。」他說。
