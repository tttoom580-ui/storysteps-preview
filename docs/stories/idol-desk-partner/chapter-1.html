午睡的同桌

The Boy Who Slept at Noon

我的同桌是偶像練習生 · 第 1 / 25 章 · 1000以下 · 英中對照 · 校訂中

用互動版查詞、朗讀與收藏

Mira Chen was twenty years old and in her second year at North Bay Arts College. On Monday, a friendly woman from the school office led a new student into her class. His name was Ren Yu, and he was twenty-one. He sat in the middle row, beside Mira, put his head on a folded red jacket, and went to sleep before the teacher finished speaking. Mira took notice of a small bug near his desk and moved it gently to a plant. Ren did not move at all. The other students whispered that the new boy looked like a star, but Mira wanted only to finish her film project.

二十歲的陳米拉就讀北灣藝術學院二年級。星期一，一位友善的女職員領著新生進教室。他叫余任，二十一歲。他坐在中間那排、米拉旁邊，把頭枕在摺好的紅外套上，老師還沒講完話就睡著了。米拉注意到桌旁有隻小蟲，輕輕把牠移到盆栽上。余任完全沒動。其他同學低聲說新同學像明星，米拉卻只想完成自己的動畫作品。

The teacher asked everyone to share one fact about a future goal. Mira said she wanted to make short films that told true stories. When it was Ren's turn, he opened one eye and said, “I hope to reach tomorrow with enough sleep.” The class laughed. Mira did not. She could see that he was truly tired, not trying to be funny. At lunch she offered him a box of juice. “You should use your lunch break to eat,” she said. He thanked her but looked at the clock. “I have another place to be,” he answered. Then he left without touching the drink.

老師請每個人分享一項未來目標。米拉說，她希望製作能說真實故事的短片。輪到余任時，他睜開一隻眼說：「我希望明天還能睡飽地醒來。」全班笑了，米拉卻沒有。她看得出他是真的累，不是在故意逗笑。午餐時，她遞給他一盒果汁。「午休應該吃點東西。」她說。他道了謝，卻看著時鐘。「我還有別的地方得去。」說完便離開，飲料也沒碰。

After class Mira walked north toward the media building. Her animation was about a small pet cat waiting at a window for someone to return. She was making it for the college film show, not for a job or a prize. By a picnic table, she saw Ren talking to a woman in a black coat. The woman held a camera. Ren pulled his hat low and asked her not to take pictures on school ground. Mira could not hear their other words, but she saw the woman nod. She decided not to guess about it. A person had a right to a quiet afternoon.

下課後，米拉往北走向媒體大樓。她的動畫講的是一隻小寵物貓，坐在窗邊等某人回家。她是為學校影展而做，不是為了找工作或拿獎。經過野餐桌時，她看見余任正在和一位黑外套女士談話。對方拿著相機。余任把帽子壓低，請她別在校園拍照。其他話米拉沒聽清，但她看見女士點頭。她決定不要亂猜；一個人有權享受安靜的下午。

That evening Mira stayed to draw. From the open window came music and a strong voice from a small studio across the street. She could see nothing behind its glass, but the song made her stop her pencil. It was the kind of sound that seemed to say, “Please do not give up on this day.” The voice belonged to a singer known online as RAY. His videos had more than a million followers. Mira had never cared about the number. She cared that the voice seemed warm, even when the music was sad. She took a short break and listened until the song ended.

那天傍晚，米拉留在教室畫圖。窗外傳來對街小錄音室的音樂與有力歌聲。她看不清玻璃後的人影，卻不由得停下畫筆。那聲音像在說：「請別放棄今天。」唱歌的人是網路上叫 RAY 的歌手，影片有超過一百萬追蹤者。米拉從不在乎數字，她只在乎那聲音即使唱悲傷的歌，仍帶著溫度。她暫停畫圖，聽到歌曲結束。

When she reached the door, a delivery worker dropped a paper bag. Mira helped collect the loose pages. On one sheet was a simple picture of a red star and a rehearsal time. A young man came running from the studio and thanked her. He looked up, and she saw the tired eyes of her new classmate. The worker said, “RAY, your next camera test starts soon.” Ren froze. Mira held the paper out to him. “This belongs to you,” she said. He took it, but his hands were shaking. She understood one fact at last: the sleeping boy and the night singer were the same person.

米拉走到門口時，一位送貨員不小心掉落紙袋。她幫忙撿起散開的紙張。其中一張畫著簡單的紅星和排練時間。一個年輕人從錄音室跑出來道謝；抬頭時，米拉認出新同桌那雙疲憊的眼睛。送貨員說：「RAY，你下個鏡頭測試快開始了。」余任僵住。米拉把紙遞給他。「這是你的。」她說。他接過紙，雙手卻在發抖。米拉終於確定一件事：白天睡覺的男孩，就是夜裡唱歌的人。

For several seconds no one spoke. Mira could have used her phone to tell the whole school, and people might have called her lucky. Instead she put her phone away. “I will not tell anyone tonight,” she said. “But this is not a promise to tell a lie for you. We need to talk about what happens next.” Ren looked more surprised by her clear words than by the discovery itself. Behind him, the studio door opened and someone called his name. Mira stepped aside. She had a film to finish, and now her quiet class had become much more interesting than she wanted.

好幾秒裡沒有人說話。米拉大可以用手機告訴全校，大家或許還會說她很幸運。但她收起手機。「今晚我不會告訴任何人，」她說，「不過這不代表我答應替你說謊。我們得談談接下來怎麼辦。」余任似乎比起祕密被發現，更驚訝她說得這麼清楚。他身後的錄音室門打開，有人喊他的名字。米拉退到一旁。她還有動畫要完成，而原本平靜的班級突然變得太有戲劇性了。
