東翼的安靜房間

A Quiet Room in the East Wing

我的同桌是偶像練習生 · 第 4 / 25 章 · 1000以下 · 英中對照 · 校訂中

用互動版查詞、朗讀與收藏

On Friday afternoon Mira came to the studio's east wing with her drawing bag. Mrs. Park met her at the door. There was a green line across the floor, and a sign asked visitors not to cross it without help. “Both of you must follow our safety rules,” she said. Ren stood beside a medium-sized bowl of water, waiting for his voice lesson to begin. His hair was still wet from a quick bath after dance practice. “You look tired,” Mira told him. “I am,” he replied. “But today I made a choice. I said no to an extra late practice so I could finish my school work.”

星期五下午，米拉帶著畫袋來到錄音室東翼。朴小姐在門口迎接，地板上畫著綠線，告示請訪客不要在無人陪同下越線。「你們兩個都得遵守安全規定。」她說。余任站在一碗中等大小的水旁，等聲樂課開始。他的頭髮還濕著，是舞蹈練習後快速洗澡的結果。「你看起來很累。」米拉說。「我確實累，」他回答，「但今天我做了選擇。我拒絕額外的深夜排練，好完成作業。」

They passed a small room where a cute dog slept under a table. The dog belonged to a sound worker who had permission to bring it on certain days. Mira wanted to pet it, but she asked first and waited until the worker said yes. Ren watched this with a smile. “You ask about everything,” he said. “It makes life easier,” Mira replied. “I do not like a surprise that another person did not choose.” She looked at the red light above the recording door. It meant someone was working. Ren raised a hand to knock, then saw that it was on and lowered his head politely.

他們經過一間小房，一隻可愛的狗睡在桌子下。牠是錄音工作者的狗，只有特定日子獲准入內。米拉想摸牠，先徵求許可，等對方說好才伸手。余任笑著看她。「妳什麼都問。」他說。「日子會簡單一點，」米拉答，「我不喜歡別人沒同意的驚喜。」她看向錄音室門上的紅燈，那代表有人正在工作。余任舉手想敲門，看見燈亮著便禮貌地放下手。

The practice room was quiet and bright. An engineer put a small microphone in front of Ren and asked Mira to show the part of the film she wanted to use. The cat in the film was looking out of a high window. Rain passed across the glass; then the door opened, but nobody came in. “It should feel like hope, not fear,” Mira explained. Ren tried one high note and stopped. “Too strong?” he asked. “A little,” Mira answered. He tried again, softer this time. The engineer nodded. They were working, not making a show for a camera.

練習室安靜明亮。錄音師在余任前方放好小麥克風，請米拉播放想配樂的片段。動畫裡小貓望著高窗。雨水滑過玻璃，門打開了，卻沒有人走進來。「我希望是期待，不是恐懼。」米拉說明。余任試唱一個高音，便停下。「太強了？」「有一點。」米拉回答。他再唱一次，這次柔和多了。錄音師點頭。他們是在工作，不是在替鏡頭表演。

During a break Ren told Mira that some students at the college had asked why he did not join their singing group. “They think I do not want friends,” he said. Mira sat on the other side of the table. “You can tell them you have another job. You do not have to tell every detail.” Ren looked at the empty chair. “What if they dislike me when they learn the truth?” “Some may,” Mira said. “But a real friend will care whether you are well, not whether you are famous.” He did not answer at once. Her words felt more useful than a hundred kind comments online.

休息時余任告訴米拉，學校有人問他為何不加入歌唱組。「他們以為我不想交朋友。」他說。米拉坐到桌子另一側。「你可以說自己有別的工作，不用交代所有細節。」余任望著空椅子。「如果他們知道真相後不喜歡我呢？」「有些人可能會，」米拉說，「但真正的朋友在乎你過得好不好，不是你有沒有名氣。」他沒有立刻回答。她的話比網路上一百句好聽的留言還有用。

Before they reached the studio exit, a cleaning team asked the visitors to wait. A box was blocking the hall, and one worker needed help to move it. Ren offered to carry the box, but the worker said it was too heavy for a student. Mira stood beside the wall and read the safety notice. She could see how the box might block people who needed the wide path. When the worker finished, everyone could pass the door easily. “I used to believe that a good day meant nothing stopped me,” Ren said. “Now I think a good day can include stopping at the right time.” Mira smiled. “You are learning more than music.”

走到錄音室出口前，清潔人員請訪客稍等。走廊裡有個箱子擋住通道，工作人員正要移開。余任想幫忙搬，但對方說箱子太重，不適合學生拿。米拉站在牆旁讀安全告示，看得出箱子會擋住需要寬敞走道的人。工人完成後，大家都能順利通過門口。「我以前以為好日子就是什麼都不阻止我，」余任說，「現在覺得好日子也可以包括適時停下。」米拉笑了。「你學到的不只音樂。」

At sunset the engineer played the recording back. It was only eight seconds, but Mira felt that her little cat had finally found its voice. Mrs. Park promised to send a written note about how the music could be used. Outside, a taxi waited for staff who worked far from the studio. Ren offered Mira the ride, but she said she had a safe bus home and wanted to keep her own plan. He accepted that without argument. Before she left, she said, “Next week, please try to stay awake for our art lesson.” He gave a small, tired laugh. “I will look at the clock,” he promised.

日落時，錄音師播放成果。只有八秒，米拉卻覺得小貓終於有了自己的聲音。朴小姐答應寄來正式的使用說明。外頭有一輛計程車，等著載路途遠的員工。余任邀米拉搭車，她說自己有安全的公車路線，想照原計畫回家。他沒有爭辯。離開前她說：「下週美術課請盡量保持清醒。」他疲憊地笑了。「我會記得看時鐘。」他答應。
