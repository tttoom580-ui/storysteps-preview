重要事情的清單

A List of Things That Matter

我的同桌是偶像練習生 · 第 7 / 25 章 · 1000以下 · 英中對照 · 校訂中

用互動版查詞、朗讀與收藏

The next class began with a small gift. Mira found a piece of chocolate on her desk and a note from Ren: “For the person who reminds me to eat.” She laughed and put it beside her lunch. Their teacher asked everyone to make a list of the main things they needed before the film show. Mira wrote, “Finish drawings, collect sound, test the film, get permission.” Ren wrote, “Go to class, learn the song, rest for one hour, be a real friend.” The paper looked almost too simple. But there was one new word for him that mattered most: friend.

下一堂課從一個小禮物開始。米拉在桌上發現一塊巧克力，旁邊有余任的紙條：「送給提醒我吃飯的人。」她笑著把它放在午餐旁。老師請大家列出影展前要做的主要事情。米拉寫：「完成畫面、收集聲音、測試電影、取得許可。」余任寫：「上課、學歌、休息一小時、當真正的朋友。」紙上看起來簡單得過分，但有一個詞對他特別重要：朋友。

By the west gate, the film club was setting up a large board of student projects. A classmate named Hana wanted to use a flower garden as her main scene. Another student was drawing a rabbit who traveled across the city. Mira made a note to watch their films. Ren wondered whether he should join the club too. “It may be popular when people know you sing,” Mira said, “but that is not a reason to join.” He watched students carry papers and chairs by hand. “I want to help,” he answered, “even if no one knows my stage name.”

西門旁，電影社正在架設大展板。叫花奈的同學想以花園為主要場景，另一名學生則畫著穿越城市的兔子。米拉記下要看他們的片。余任猶豫是否也加入社團。「大家知道你會唱歌後，可能會很歡迎你，」米拉說，「但那不該是加入的理由。」他看見學生們親手搬紙和椅子。「我想幫忙，」他回答，「即使沒人知道我的藝名。」

At the club table a woman was helping to collect forms. She described herself as a housewife who had come back to college after many years. Her name was Mrs. Li, and she was taking the course because she loved stories. “I do not care if your film is small,” she told the students. “Start with something you can do well.” Ren asked whether volunteers could help for only one hour a week. “Of course,” she said. “We let people choose what they can give. The work is there until the show, and it will not go away if you miss one day.”

社團桌旁，一位女士正幫忙收表格。她說自己多年來是家庭主婦，現在重新回到學院上課，叫李太太，因為喜愛故事才選這門課。「電影小不小我不在乎，」她對學生說，「先做好你能做的。」余任問志工能否一週只幫忙一小時。「當然，」她說，「每個人選自己能給的時間。直到影展前都有工作，不會因為你一天沒來就消失。」

Ren signed up to carry chairs and set out water on the evening of the show. He did not offer to sing or bring his fans. That made Mira respect him more than a promise of a big concert would have. Later, sitting near a window, they listened to the small recording again. It was soft, but the notes were in the right place. Ren asked if Mira wanted more music. She shook her head. “Not until I finish the next scene. Too many ideas can cover the one I need.” He made a note beside his own list: “Listen before you add.”

余任報名影展當晚搬椅子與擺水。他沒有提議唱歌或帶粉絲過來。這反而比承諾一場大演唱會更讓米拉敬重。之後他們坐在窗旁，又聽一次短錄音。音量很輕，但旋律剛好在對的位置。余任問是否需要更多音樂。她搖頭。「等我畫完下一幕再說。太多點子會蓋住真正需要的那一個。」他在清單旁記下：「先聽，再加。」

The club's first job was small: put a note at the main door so visitors would know where to leave their bags. Ren copied the words neatly, and Mira drew one simple picture beside them. When the teacher checked the sign, she suggested moving it lower so children could read it. Ren moved it at once. “I thought bigger was always better,” he said. “It is better only if people can use it,” Mira answered. They worked until the last chair was in place. Then they sat for five minutes without doing anything useful. Ren had once believed that an hour without work was a failure. Now he began to see it as part of life.

社團第一項工作很小：在正門貼告示，讓訪客知道把包包放哪裡。余任工整抄好字，米拉在旁邊畫簡圖。老師檢查時，建議把告示貼低，孩子才看得清。余任立刻調整。「我以前以為越大越好。」他說。「只有大家用得到才比較好。」米拉回答。他們把最後一張椅子放好才停下，接著坐五分鐘，什麼有用的事也沒做。余任以前覺得不工作的時間是失敗，現在開始把它視為生活的一部分。

That night Ren walked home by the market. At a corner he passed a shop selling rabbit toys and bright flowers. On its screen a video of RAY was playing. The shop worker sang along, unaware that the man in a plain coat beside her was the same singer. Ren felt the old pull to stop and smile for the public. But he had no camera with him and no work to do there. He bought bread, thanked the worker, and walked away. For the first time in months, he enjoyed being one person rather than two names. He could begin again tomorrow.

夜裡余任走過市場回家。轉角一家店賣兔子玩具和鮮花，螢幕播放著 RAY 的影片。店員跟著唱，沒發現身旁穿素色外套的男人就是歌手本人。余任感到熟悉的衝動，想停下來對公眾微笑，但他沒有帶鏡頭，也沒有工作要做。他買了麵包、向店員道謝，就走了。幾個月來第一次，他享受自己只是同一個人，而不是兩個名字。明天還能重新開始。
