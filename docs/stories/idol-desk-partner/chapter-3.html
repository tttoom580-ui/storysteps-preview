八點十五分的郵件

An Email at Eight Fifteen

我的同桌是偶像練習生 · 第 3 / 25 章 · 1000以下 · 英中對照 · 校訂中

用互動版查詞、朗讀與收藏

Wednesday began with a basketball rolling into the drawing area. Ren caught it before it hit Mira's work. “Thank you,” she said. “Our class will begin a new sound project today.” The teacher explained that each student needed to define what a picture should make people feel. Mira wanted her short film to sound like a home where someone was welcome. She had a few ideas, but no music. Ren used an eraser to draw a small smile beside the word HOME. “A simple sound may be better than a big song,” he said. For a boy with a million followers, it was a surprisingly small answer.

星期三，一顆籃球滾進繪畫區。余任在它撞上米拉作品之前接住。「謝謝。」她說，「今天要開始新的聲音作業。」老師說明，每位學生要定義畫面希望帶給觀眾的感覺。米拉想讓短片聽起來像有人等你回家的地方。她有幾個點子，卻沒有音樂。余任拿橡皮擦，在「家」字旁畫了個小笑臉。「簡單的聲音可能比大歌更合適。」他說。對有百萬粉絲的人來說，這答案意外地樸素。

At a quarter past eight that night, Mira received an email from the studio. It said she could not use any music until the college and company approved a small plan. She was glad to have it in writing. The mail included a simple form: purpose, recording time, public use, and names of the makers. She sat under an orange lamp beside a plant and filled the spaces. The recording would be at six p.m. on a school day, not late at night. It would last less than one hour. She promised that Ren could leave if the work made him too tired.

那晚八點十五分，米拉收到錄音室的電子郵件。信中說，學校和公司核准小型計畫之前，她不能使用任何音樂。有書面說明，她反而安心。郵件附著簡單表格：用途、錄音時間、公開方式和製作者姓名。米拉坐在橘色桌燈與盆栽旁，填好空格。錄音會安排在上課日傍晚六點，而非深夜；時間少於一小時。她承諾，如果余任太累，可以中途離開。

The next morning a traffic problem delayed the bus. Ren arrived after the teacher and looked worried. Mira did not say she could fix his life. She pointed to the modern class site, where all students could read the missed lesson. “The notes are there,” she said. “You will have to ask questions when you are awake.” He sat under the wall clock, rubbed his eyes, and tried. At first he could read only one page. After a short break, he understood the main point: sound had to match a picture, not fight it. “I can do a little today,” he told himself.

隔天早上，交通問題讓公車誤點。余任晚於老師進教室，神情焦慮。米拉沒有說自己能替他解決人生，只指向現代化課程網站，所有學生都能在上面讀到漏掉的內容。「筆記在那裡，」她說，「但醒著時要自己問問題。」他坐在壁鐘下揉揉眼睛，努力讀書。一開始只能讀一頁，短暫休息後才懂得重點：聲音要配合畫面，不是和畫面打架。「今天先做到一點點。」他對自己說。

After lunch Mira showed him a rough film scene. A little cat waited at the window; outside, people walked home with bags and flowers. Ren listened without speaking. “Do you want the music to tell us the cat is sad?” he asked. “Not only sad,” Mira said. “The cat is waiting, but it can enjoy the night too.” Ren hummed three soft notes. Mira knew the sound would change before the final film, but she wrote it down. They spent fifteen minutes talking about one scene. That was the first time Ren worked with her without looking at his watch every minute.

午餐後，米拉給他看動畫草稿：小貓守在窗邊，窗外的人提著袋子和花束走回家。余任聽完才問：「妳想讓音樂說小貓很難過嗎？」「不只難過，」米拉說，「牠在等待，也能享受夜晚。」余任哼出三個輕柔的音。米拉知道正式版本還會改，但先把旋律記下。他們花十五分鐘討論一個場景。那是余任第一次與她合作時，沒有每分鐘都看手錶。

The school film show was in six weeks. The deadline was on a large board in the hall, and Mira had seen other students hurry past it all week. She felt the same worry, yet she did not want to rush the part that made her story honest. Ren offered to sing several different sounds, so she could choose. She asked him to wait for the approved form first. “Even a small film has rules,” she reminded him. He smiled and helped carry the drawing papers to class. They were learning how to work together before they even made a real song.

學校影展還有六週。大廳的大板子寫著截止日期，這星期好多學生都匆匆經過。米拉也焦慮，卻不想匆忙完成最需要真心的部分。余任提議唱幾種不同的聲音供她挑選，她請他先等表單核准。「小電影也有規則。」她提醒。他微笑著幫忙把畫紙搬回教室。他們還沒錄出正式歌曲，卻已開始學怎麼合作。

That evening Ren's manager called with news. The company agreed to the small school recording, provided his name and song rights were written correctly. The college teacher agreed too. Ren read both messages twice before replying. He no longer had to hide the work from the adults responsible for him, although he still wanted his classmates to know him simply as Ren. Mira drew a line under the date in her notebook. In the dark window she saw her own reflection and smiled. The boy at the next desk had helped her see the film in a new way. That was enough for tonight.

傍晚經紀人帶來消息。公司同意小型校園錄音，只要姓名與歌曲權利記載清楚。學校老師也同意。余任把兩則訊息讀了兩遍才回覆。他不必再對負責的成年人隱瞞這份工作，儘管仍希望同學只把他當作余任。米拉在筆記本日期下畫了一條線。黑色窗面映著她的笑容。隔壁桌的男孩讓她用新眼光看自己的短片。今晚這樣就夠了。
