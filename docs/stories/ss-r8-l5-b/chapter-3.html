河港的見證人

Witness at the River Port

星債未償：我們替夜空記住名字 · 第 3 / 25 章 · 4000–5000 · 英中對照

The river port looked peaceful until Xu saw a boy being pushed away from a public desk. The port deputy called the boy's questions abuse of official time and treated his request for consultation with open contempt. A traveling correspondent had already written about the dispute, but his report gave only the deputy's explanation. Yin stopped beside a creek that flowed into the harbor and asked the boy whether he wished to speak. The child said his mother had been a witness to the old road vow; her name was missing from the port register. Xu's first response was deliberate anger. He wanted the clerk to read the book aloud. Yin instead proposed a diplomatic meeting in the open, with the boy's aunt present. No one needed to make a child stand alone against a powerful office. They agreed to examine the register before deciding whether the missing line was an error or an intentional removal.

河港原本平靜，直到徐岸看見一名男孩被推離公共服務桌。港口副官指責男孩濫用公務時間提問，對他的諮詢要求顯露輕蔑。一名旅行通訊員已寫過這場爭議，報導卻只採用副官的說法。殷洛在流入港口的小溪旁停下，問男孩願不願意說明。男孩說母親曾見證舊道路誓約，姓名卻從港口名冊上消失了。徐岸刻意壓著怒氣，仍想逼書吏當眾讀出紀錄。殷洛提議在公開場所開會，並請男孩的阿姨到場。沒有理由讓孩子單獨面對強勢機關。他們決定先查名冊，再判斷缺漏究竟是失誤還是有人故意刪除。

The register had once supported an entire river ecosystem. Every boat allowed through the locks paid for fish ladders and the marsh where rare birds nested. Without that shared agreement, an extinct species of local reed fish was blamed for each new shortage, although the fish had disappeared fifty years earlier. Xu felt in his gut that someone was using old fears to avoid present responsibilities. The deputy promised to notify the governor when the next inspection phase began. But a woman cleaning nets on the pier showed Yin an older copy: the witness's name appeared beside a transfer of the north road. The odds of two clerks accidentally cutting away the same line were small. The woman had tried to sneak the book out herself and had nearly lost her job. Yin thanked her but refused to take it without the cooperative's permission. Public truth, he said, could not be built on another person's forced silence.

這本名冊曾支撐整條河流的生態系。每艘通過水閘的船都要繳費，供應魚梯與稀有鳥類築巢的沼地。共同協議失效後，新的短缺竟被歸咎於早已絕種的地方蘆魚——那種魚五十年前就消失了。徐岸直覺有人在利用舊恐懼逃避眼前責任。副官承諾下一階段檢查開始時通知總督。然而碼頭上修補漁網的婦人拿出更早的副本，失蹤見證人的名字就在北路移轉條款旁。兩名書吏都碰巧刪去同一行的機率很小。婦人曾試著偷偷把書帶出，差點失去工作。殷洛向她道謝，卻不願在未經合作社許可下拿走原件。他說，公共真相不能建立在迫使另一個人沉默之上。

The cooperative's successor, a fisherman named Tao, offered a practical arrangement. The travelers could inspect the book beside three local witnesses; in exchange, they would help prepare a sustainable plan for keeping the pier open during winter storms. Xu knew metalwork but not tidal currents, so he agreed to repair lamps rather than promise a miracle. Yin proposed that they swap authenticated copies with the river school, where fire and flood were less likely to destroy both collections at once. A dusty trophy stood on the teacher's shelf. It honored Master Rui for designing a transparent witness system thirty years earlier. On its base, Xu found a faded inscription naming a fourth partner—someone the official biographies had never mentioned. The name was Mei Qiu, a woman who had worked in the observatory's glass workshop. Xu copied it carefully. For the first time he wondered whether his teacher had disappeared because he was protecting another person's work rather than his own.

合作社的繼任管理者陶漁夫提出務實安排：兩人可在三名地方見證人面前查看名冊，交換條件是協助制定冬季風暴時維持碼頭運作的永續方案。徐岸懂金工卻不懂潮流，因此只答應修燈，不誇口能創造奇蹟。殷洛建議與河畔學校交換已驗證的副本，避免火災或洪水同時毀掉全部資料。教師書架上擺著布滿灰塵的獎盃，紀念芮師傅三十年前設計公開見證制度。底座上的褪色銘文還寫著第四位合作者——一名官方傳記從未提過的人：觀星臺玻璃工坊的梅秋。徐岸仔細抄下姓名，第一次懷疑師父失蹤是為了保護別人的成果，而不是自己的。

That night, the boy and his aunt joined the public reading. The deputy arrived with guards, but the gathering did not seek a fight. The correspondent returned and recorded both versions of the story. When the witness names were compared, one empty space in the official register aligned with a cut in the silver film from the ridge. The evidence was still incomplete: a missing name could not identify the person who had removed it. Nevertheless, the port agreed to freeze the disputed transfer until an independent inspection. Xu walked back to the guesthouse with the fisherman carrying a newly repaired lamp. Yin stayed behind to obtain written consent from each witness. Later he found Xu waiting beside a window that reflected the first bright stars. Xu said he had begun the journey expecting to rescue one man; now he could not leave the boy's mother nameless. Yin answered that this was how promises worked. Once made in good faith, they changed the road ahead.

當晚男孩與阿姨參加公開讀檔。副官帶著衛兵來了，但聚會沒有尋求衝突。通訊員也回來記錄雙方說法。對照見證人名單時，官方名冊的空格正好對上山脊銀片的切口。證據仍不完整，缺名不能直接指出是誰刪除的；但港務處同意在獨立調查前暫停爭議移轉。徐岸和帶著修好燈具的漁夫走回旅店，殷洛則留下取得各見證人的書面同意。稍後他發現徐岸站在窗邊，玻璃映著初亮的星星。徐岸說自己出發時只想救一個人，如今卻不能讓男孩的母親繼續無名。殷洛回答，承諾就是這樣；真心說出後，前方道路也會隨之改變。
