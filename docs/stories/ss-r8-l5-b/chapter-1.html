沒有名字的星座

The Constellation with No Name

星債未償：我們替夜空記住名字 · 第 1 / 25 章 · 4000–5000 · 英中對照

Xu An had spent sixteen years repairing instruments that measured the night sky, yet he had never expected to doubt the certainty of a familiar star. At dawn, he stood outside the old chapel of Lantern Square, where a cluster of silver lamps should have outlined the constellation called the Weaver's Promise. Three lamps were dark. A public notice offered compensation for damaged equipment but refused to name what had happened. The keeper who posted it would not disclose the maintenance journal. Xu noticed an envious young apprentice watching his travel tools; the boy admitted that apprentices were forbidden to approach the locked dome. Xu was thirty-five, old enough to recognize fear disguised as obedience. He carried a brass astrolabe that had belonged to his missing teacher, Master Rui. Its engraved needle pointed not toward the missing star lines but toward the chapel cellar. He decided not to open the door by force. First, he wanted to know who had ordered it shut.

徐岸修理測量夜空的器具已經十六年，卻從沒想過自己會懷疑一顆熟悉的星。他在黎明站在燈廣場的舊禮拜堂外；一群銀燈原本應勾勒出名為「織者之約」的星座，如今有三盞熄滅。公告承諾賠償受損器材，卻不肯說明原因，張貼公告的人也拒絕公開維修日誌。徐岸發現一名年輕學徒羨慕地望著他的旅行工具。少年坦承，學徒不准靠近封鎖的圓頂。三十五歲的徐岸看得出，那是披著服從外衣的恐懼。他帶著失蹤師父芮師傅留下的黃銅星盤。盤上的刻針沒有指向熄滅的星線，而是指向禮拜堂地下室。他決定不強行開門，先查明是誰下令封鎖。

At noon the square became a public forum. A small generator powered a painted sky, and officials described the darkness as an ordinary failure of projection. The crowd knew the difference. The lamps represented promises spoken before witnesses, an old method of mapping which roads and water sources belonged to everyone. For generations, this network had protected the mountain habitat from private seizure and kept its heritage visible even to travelers. Now the empty spaces were immense. Yin Luo, a former observatory clerk aged thirty-three, stepped forward with the calm of a man who expected to be interrupted. He said the physical stars were unharmed; only the public lines joining them had vanished. Someone had removed records from the lenses. An official asked whether he had proof. Yin held up a receipt carrying a number that should have existed only in the observatory's private collection. Xu recognized Master Rui's handwriting beneath it, cut off halfway through a sentence.

中午，廣場成了公開論壇。一臺小型發電機供應繪製星圖的裝置，官員宣稱黑暗只是普通的投影故障，但群眾知道差別。那些燈代表在見證人面前說出的承諾；古老的製圖法記錄哪些道路與水源屬於所有人。這套網絡世代保護山地棲地免於私人侵占，也讓共同傳承對旅人清楚可見。如今大片星線空白。三十三歲的前觀星臺書吏殷洛站出來，平靜得像是早就料到會被打斷。他說真正的星星沒壞，消失的是連接星辰的公共線條；有人取走了鏡片內的紀錄。官員問他有何證據。殷洛舉起一張收據，上面的編號本來只應出現在觀星臺私人收藏中。徐岸認出收據下方芮師傅的字跡，句子寫到一半就被截斷。

Xu felt an impulse to accuse the keeper immediately, but Yin gave him a more useful insight. In the metropolitan archive a promise could be copied into a lens only when its speaker and a witness both agreed. A second copy was optional; erasing the first did not erase anyone's mind, but it could make a public duty almost impossible to prove. There was also a forgotten provision: every transfer had to preserve the speaker's name and the witness's mark. Yin had once resigned rather than sign a transfer that lacked both. He was not interested in becoming an investigator, he told Xu, until Xu showed him the broken handwriting. Then Yin's expression changed. Master Rui had taught him to read damaged star maps. The two men compared their evidence on the edge of the fountain. Xu wanted his teacher found. Yin wanted to know whether his own signature had been used after he left. They agreed to travel together only as far as the northern observatory.

徐岸一時衝動，想立刻指控守臺人，但殷洛提出更有用的洞察。在都城檔案庫中，只有說出誓約的人與見證人都同意，承諾才可複製進鏡片。第二份備本可以自由選擇；抹去第一份不會清除人的記憶，卻可能讓公共義務幾乎無法證明。還有一條被遺忘的規定：每次移轉都必須保留發誓者的姓名與見證人的記號。殷洛曾拒絕替缺少兩者的轉移簽名，因此辭職。他告訴徐岸，自己本不想當調查者，直到看見那截斷的筆跡才改變神色。芮師傅教過他讀取損壞的星圖。兩人在噴泉旁比對證據。徐岸想找到師父；殷洛想知道自己離職後的簽名是否遭人冒用。他們約定只同行到北方觀星臺。

Near the market stairs, a girl began to stumble while carrying a basket of dried seeds. Xu caught her before she fell. Her supervisor, a merchant in a blue coat, demanded payment for a lost seed packet and called the loss a private transaction. Yet the girl's basket belonged to the free planting guild, and the label named the very constellation that had gone dark. Without its old record, the merchant claimed no one could prove the guild's right to the hillside. A line of herbs had already begun to wither behind his fence. Yin wrote down the girl's account with her permission, then gave her the choice of keeping her name private. Xu examined the paper packet and discovered a tiny silver thread identical to the one tied around Master Rui's astrolabe. The mystery was no longer a missing lamp alone. It had already changed who could plant, travel, and speak. Before sunset they boarded a cart north, with one question neither man was ready to ask: how much of an old promise could two strangers safely carry?

市場階梯旁，一名女孩抱著乾燥種子的籃子踉蹌，徐岸趕忙扶住她。穿藍外衣的商人自稱主管，要求她賠償丟失的一包種子，說那是私人交易。然而籃子屬於免費栽種公會，標籤上正是熄滅星座的名字。舊紀錄不見後，商人聲稱沒人能證明公會有權使用山坡；他柵欄後的一排藥草已開始枯萎。殷洛先徵求女孩同意才寫下證詞，也讓她選擇是否隱藏姓名。徐岸查看紙包，發現一道微小銀線，和芮師傅星盤上綁著的線一模一樣。謎題已不只是少了幾盞燈，而是影響了誰能種植、行走與說話。日落前，兩人坐上北行貨車，心裡都藏著一個尚不敢問的問題：兩個陌生人究竟能安全承擔多少舊承諾？
