風暴中的庇護所

Shelter in the Storm

星債未償：我們替夜空記住名字 · 第 11 / 25 章 · 4000–5000 · 英中對照 · 校訂中

The route to the high sanctuary was apt to close after sunset, so Xu and Yin left White Rock before dawn. They passed a ruined casino whose owner had once sold lucky star charms to travelers. The decorations seemed designed to characterize the observatory as a place of wealth rather than public service. At a roadside clinical station, a nurse warned that the coming storm could cut off access to the mountain refuge. She showed them a printed notice claiming copyright over old star charts. Xu was fascinated by the claim because some drawings had belonged to families long before the company existed. He gave a gasp when he recognized the name of a deceased glassmaker on the notice. Her heir, a grandson named Fen, had been asking for the originals for years. A howl of wind forced everyone indoors. Even as the shutters shook, Xu saw that the older stamp on the nurse's chart remained intact. Someone had preserved it despite the later claim of ownership.

通往高地聖所的道路入夜後容易封閉，徐岸與殷洛天未亮便離開白石。他們經過破敗賭場，主人以前向旅人賣幸運星符。裝飾似乎想把觀星臺塑造成聚財之地，而非公共服務設施。路邊診所的護理師警告風暴將至，山上庇護所可能斷路。她拿出一張印刷公告，聲稱古星圖受到著作權保護。徐岸很感興趣，因為部分圖紙早在公司成立前就屬於居民家庭。看見公告上已故玻璃匠的名字時，他倒抽一口氣。她的繼承人、孫子芬，多年來一直要求歸還原稿。一聲狂風吹得眾人退進屋裡；即使窗板震動，徐岸仍看見護理師圖紙上的舊印章完好保留。有人在後來的權利主張下依然保存了它。

The nurse recommended that the group take the lower trail because the upper bridge had a fracture from last winter. At the refuge a former ranger lifted his sleeve to show a scar caused by falling stone. He warned of a sheer drop beside the watch path. A spectator who had arrived to watch a ceremonial hunt offered to guide them, but the ranger rejected his plan: he carried a rifle meant for sport and had no training in mountain rescue. A public complaint about theft of medicines had also brought armed suspicion into the camp. Yin insisted that no traveler should be searched without a clear rule and an independent witness. Xu spent an hour helping a mechanic upgrade a damaged wheelchair so that an elderly visitor could move between sleeping rooms. The workshop smelled of wet wood and warm metal. When the storm struck, the repaired chair and the cleared lower passage proved more valuable than the attractive but dangerous shortcut.

護理師建議走較低的山路，因為上橋去年冬天已斷裂。庇護所裡，前巡山員捲起袖子，露出落石留下的傷疤，並警告守望小徑旁有陡直深崖。一名原本來看儀式狩獵的旁觀者願意帶路，但巡山員否決了他的計畫：他帶的步槍是運動用途，沒有山難救援訓練。有人申訴藥品遭竊，也讓營地出現武裝猜疑。殷洛堅持搜查旅人必須有明確規則與獨立見證。徐岸花一小時幫技師改良受損輪椅，讓長者能在寢室間移動。工坊充滿濕木與溫金屬氣味。風暴到來時，修好的輪椅與清出的低路，比那條漂亮卻危險的捷徑更有價值。

Fen arrived after dark carrying a leather tube of drawings. He was angry that his grandmother's work had been displayed without attribution, yet he did not demand that the sanctuary remove every copy. He wanted visitors to know who had made them and why. One diagram showed a second lock attached to a public lens; another showed the same device being disconnected. The dates suggested a failed early attempt to stop the transfer scheme, years before Master Rui vanished. Xu asked why Fen had never taken the drawings to court. Fen said he had tried, but the complaint had been dismissed because he could not identify the original owner of the paper. His grandmother had made the design with several colleagues, and he did not want to claim sole authorship on her behalf. Yin offered to contact the surviving workshop families. Fen agreed on the condition that the group not turn his grandmother into a convenient symbol for a cause she had never chosen.

芬在天黑後帶著裝圖紙的皮筒抵達。他氣憤祖母的作品被展出卻沒署名，但不要求聖所撤下所有副本；他希望旅人知道製作者是誰、為何製作。一張圖顯示第二道鎖裝在公共鏡上，另一張畫的是同裝置遭拆除。日期暗示早在芮師傅失蹤多年以前，就有人嘗試阻止這套移轉計畫卻失敗。徐岸問芬為何沒把圖紙交法院。芬說試過，但因無法證明紙張最初所有者，申訴被駁回。祖母與多名同事共同設計，他也不願替祖母獨占作者身分。殷洛提議聯絡其他工坊家族，芬同意，條件是別把祖母變成她自己從未選擇的運動象徵。

Near midnight the sanctuary's old bell sounded twice. A frightened child had gone missing while searching for a dog, and the rangers organized a quiet search along the safe passage. Xu joined them with a repaired lamp while Yin stayed to record which routes had been checked. The child was found asleep behind a storage screen, unharmed but cold. When Xu returned, Yin had placed a blanket over his chair. Neither spoke of courage; everyone had simply done the work they could. Fen then showed them one final page: a list of preserved witness marks that had been moved from the high observatory shortly before the first line vanished. At its bottom was a location, not a person's name: the Glass Arena. The storm would keep them at the refuge another day. For once Xu did not resent a delay. He had learned that reaching the right place meant little if people were injured on the way.

午夜將近，聖所舊鐘連響兩次。一名孩子為了找狗走失，巡山員沿安全通道組織安靜搜尋。徐岸提著修好的燈加入，殷洛留守記錄已搜尋路線。孩子被發現睡在儲物屏風後，平安無傷，只是受寒。徐岸回來時，殷洛已把毯子放在他的椅上。兩人沒談勇敢，因為每個人只是做自己能做的事。芬最後拿出一頁清單：最初星線消失前不久，有批保存的見證印記被移離高觀星臺。頁底寫的不是人名，而是一個地點：玻璃競技場。風暴會使他們多留一天。徐岸這次不再厭惡拖延。他已明白，如果有人在路上受傷，抵達正確地點也沒有意義。
