空圓頂的顏色

The Color of an Empty Dome

The empty dome felt alien in daylight, an open eye without a pupil. Its owner had left before the meeting, and the courtyard was in chaos because nobody knew who could authorize repairs. Yin asked the librarian to prepare a short guide for public comprehension, explaining which records entitled residents to enter the building. The guide allowed flexibility for elderly visitors and anyone who could not climb the stairs. By noon a gathering of students, workers, and shopkeepers filled the courtyard. Their goal was to minimize damage while preserving evidence. A local storyteller repeated a myth that the missing star-lines had been stolen by a jealous spirit. Xu listened politely and then showed him the mechanical grooves. Superstition might express fear, but it could not replace observation. The storyteller agreed and offered to turn the real story into a play that would help children understand why witnesses mattered. Together they hung a ragged sheet across the entrance to keep rain from the books.

白天的空圓頂顯得陌生，像沒有瞳孔的眼睛。屋主在會議前離開，院子陷入混亂，沒人知道誰能批准修繕。殷洛請圖書員準備簡短導覽，讓公眾理解哪些紀錄賦予居民入館權利，也為長者與無法爬樓梯的人保留彈性。中午學生、工人與店主聚集院中，目標是在保存證據的同時減少損壞。地方說書人講起神話，說嫉妒的精靈偷走星線。徐岸禮貌聽完，拿出機械槽痕給他看。傳說可以表達恐懼，不能替代觀察。說書人同意，還提議把真實事件編成讓孩子懂得見證重要性的戲。他們一起用破布遮住入口，防止雨水打濕書籍。

A woman who ran a small hair salon volunteered a room for temporary storage. There was a shortage of dry shelves, and the guild's closed kiln could no longer provide new glass cases. Yin arranged the damaged lenses along a spectrum of colors from pale blue to deep violet. Each color corresponded to the number of witnesses recorded in the metal frame, not to the moral worth of a promise. The darkest lens had no recorded witness at all. A silver vein in its frame suggested that someone had altered it after manufacture. The owner had tried to stall the inspection by claiming that every lens was a private decoration. Xu proposed a strategic test: compare a lens from the public archive with one of the suspicious pieces while both remained in sealed boxes. The librarian agreed, but she wanted the board to suspend any transfer until results were known. A theoretical model from Master Rui's notebook predicted a faint silver line when a true witness groove was present. They would test that prediction openly rather than treat it as unquestionable truth.

一名經營美髮沙龍的女子願意借出房間作臨時倉庫。乾燥書架不足，關閉的公會熔爐也無法再製作玻璃盒。殷洛依淡藍到深紫的色譜排列受損鏡片，每種顏色對應框內記錄的見證人數，而非承諾的道德價值。最深色的鏡片完全沒有見證紀錄。框上一條銀色脈紋顯示，有人在製成後動過手腳。屋主企圖拖延檢查，聲稱每片鏡子都是私人裝飾。徐岸提出策略性測試：讓公共檔案鏡片與可疑鏡片各自留在密封盒內，並排比較。圖書員同意，但要求董事會在結果出爐前暫停任何移轉。芮師傅筆記裡的理論模型預測，真正的見證槽會顯示微弱銀線。兩人決定公開檢驗預測，不把它奉為不可質疑的真理。

During the evening performance, children placed paper stars beneath the sheet in tribute to relatives whose names had disappeared from public lists. A traveler carrying an expired visa asked whether he could add his sister's name, even though she had never lived in the city. The librarian said the memorial belonged to everyone who had been affected, with no test of citizenship whatsoever. The visitor bowed, surprised that a question of permission could receive such a generous answer. Xu discovered a pale line inside the suspicious lens when it was placed near his astrolabe, but it appeared only for a moment. There was no need to break the glass; a carefully measured distance was enough. Yin checked the distance twice and invited the student to repeat it independently. The demonstration confirmed that the witness channel existed, but it did not reveal whose name should have appeared there. They now had a method, not a verdict.

晚間演出時，孩子把紙星星放在布幕下，紀念在公共名單中失去名字的親人。一名簽證已過期的旅人問可否加上姊姊的名字，儘管她從未住在城裡。圖書員說紀念活動屬於所有受影響者，不會用國籍作任何門檻。旅人鞠躬，驚訝許可問題竟能得到如此寬厚的回答。徐岸把可疑鏡片靠近星盤時，看見內部一瞬閃過淡銀線。無須打破玻璃，只要精準調整距離。殷洛重複量測兩次，請學生獨立重做。示範確認見證通道確實存在，卻無法揭露原本應有誰的姓名。他們找到的是方法，不是裁決。

Later, the owner sent a letter offering to donate the dome if the citizens promised never to discuss its history. The offer divided the crowd. A free building could become a school; silence might also protect the very system that had deprived people of their records. Xu remembered how quick he had been to demand a single dramatic answer. He said he would support the library whether or not it obtained the building, but he could not sign a condition that erased the past. Yin stood beside him without speaking for him. The gathering voted to seek a temporary preservation order and negotiate ownership separately. The next morning they left copies of the test results with three independent institutions. Outside the city, the first unbroken constellation rose over the road. Xu watched it until Yin asked whether he was cold. Xu said no, although he welcomed the offer of Yin's extra scarf.

稍晚屋主來信，願意捐出圓頂，條件是居民承諾永不談論它的歷史。提議讓眾人分裂。免費建築可能變成學校，但沉默也會保護奪走人們紀錄的制度。徐岸想起自己曾急著尋求戲劇化答案。他表示不論圖書館能否得到建築，自己都會支持，但不能簽署抹去過去的條件。殷洛站在旁邊，沒有替他發言。集會投票決定先申請臨時保存命令，所有權另行談判。翌晨，他們將測試副本分交三個獨立機構。城外道路上升起第一個完整星座，徐岸看了許久。殷洛問他冷不冷，他說不冷，卻仍接受對方遞來的圍巾。
