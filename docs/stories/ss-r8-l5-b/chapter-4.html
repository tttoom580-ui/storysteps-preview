測量不會道歉

A Measurement Cannot Apologize

星債未償：我們替夜空記住名字 · 第 4 / 25 章 · 4000–5000 · 英中對照

用互動版查詞、朗讀與收藏

They entered Selene Province under a cold gray sky, prepared to anticipate resistance at the regional observatory. Its bureaucracy required three forms before a visitor could touch a single lens. Xu noticed a coincidence: one application number was the same as the cut film recovered near the river. A commuter waiting for the morning ferry challenged his claim, saying old forms often shared similar numbers. Yin did not dismiss the objection. He requested a written denial or approval and asked to measure the diameter of the sealed instrument instead of opening it. Its distinctive outer ring matched the design in Master Rui's manual. A retired member of the observatory faculty offered a classroom for inspection. The office first objected on fiscal grounds: even a small repair could cost a fraction of its yearly budget. Xu offered his own tools and asked that the observers remain independent. He wanted a test the public could understand without asking anyone to believe in his reputation.

兩人走進塞勒涅省時，灰冷天空像在預告區域觀星臺的阻力。官僚程序要求訪客填三份表，才能碰一片鏡。徐岸發現巧合：一份申請編號與河邊取回的切割薄片相同。等早班渡輪的通勤者質疑他，說舊表格經常出現相似編號。殷洛沒有忽視異議，而是要求書面准駁，並申請測量封存器具的直徑，不先打開。其獨特外圈與芮師傅手冊中的設計一致。退休觀星學院教師借出教室供檢查，官署卻先以財務理由反對：即使小修也可能花掉年度預算一部分。徐岸願意自備工具，要求觀察員維持獨立。他想要的是人人能理解的測試，而不是要求大眾相信他的名聲。

The inspection carried a hazard no one had mentioned: the old lens had been stored beside medicine meant to regulate a rare hormone disorder among high-altitude workers. A practitioner from the regional clinic insisted that the two collections be separated before any heat was applied. She was right. Several lenses had been coated with salts used in marine laboratories; burning them in a closed room could damage lungs. She had studied mortality after a previous workshop accident and refused to repeat the mistake. Yin drew parallel lines on the classroom floor to mark clean and contaminated areas. A young technician made a plea for speed, explaining that families below were waiting for the water road to reopen. Xu tried to reassure him that caution was not indifference. Delaying the test for half a day might prevent another closure for half a year. The clinic worker agreed to supervise the ventilation, and the faculty member arranged witnesses from both the town and the observatory.

檢查有一個沒人提過的危險：舊鏡片和用於調節高海拔工人罕見荷爾蒙疾病的藥品放在一起。區域診所的醫護人員堅持加熱前先分開兩種收藏，她果然是對的。幾片鏡子表面覆有海洋實驗室使用的鹽類，若在密室燃燒可能傷肺。她研究過上次工坊事故的死亡率，不願重演。殷洛在地板畫出平行線，標示清潔區與污染區。年輕技師懇求加快速度，因為山下家庭正等著水路重開。徐岸安慰他，謹慎不是漠不關心；延遲半天，也許能避免再封路半年。醫護人員同意監督通風，退休教師安排城鎮與觀星臺雙方見證。

Once the room was safe, Xu compared the outer ring with an untouched diagram. There were three grooves where the manual showed four. Someone had not simply forgotten a name; the machine had been altered to accept transfers without a witness mark. Yin placed his resignation letter beside the instrument. It contained the same warning, issued before he left. The retired teacher remembered the board treating his complaint as an inconvenience. Xu could see why Yin had kept the letter for so long. A man accused of bitterness may save every scrap of proof that he once spoke clearly. The teacher agreed to file the results, but she would not certify who made the changes. Yin thanked her for drawing that boundary. They could uncover the mechanism now, yet intent and responsibility remained separate questions. The difference would soon matter more than either man knew.

確認安全後，徐岸把外圈與未改動的圖紙比較。手冊顯示四道槽，實物卻只剩三道。這不只是忘記姓名；機器已被修改，能在沒有見證印記下接受移轉。殷洛把自己的辭職信放在器具旁，信中早在離職前就提出同樣警告。退休教師記得董事會當年只把投訴當成麻煩。徐岸終於理解殷洛為何留信多年：被指責心懷怨懟的人，會保留每一張證明自己曾清楚說話的紙。教師同意存檔結果，卻不肯證明誰動手修改。殷洛感謝她守住這條界線。他們現在能揭開機械原理，至於動機與責任仍是兩個問題，而這差別很快會變得更加重要。

At supper they sat across from each other beneath a single lantern. Xu admitted that he used to believe every skilled repair needed one decisive hand. Yin told him that the greatest repair he had ever seen was performed by five people who disagreed about nearly everything, except that no one should be hidden from the record. The words made Xu unexpectedly quiet. He could picture Master Rui beside Mei Qiu, holding a lamp while someone else did the difficult glasswork. Before sleeping, he sent a notice to the river school: the groove was altered, but the maker was still unknown. The answer came through a courier before dawn. A former workshop worker wished to speak, provided her brother could remain unnamed. Yin read the conditions aloud and asked Xu whether he accepted them. Xu said yes. It was a small answer, but it marked the first time he placed someone else's safety ahead of his urgent search.

晚餐時兩人隔著一盞燈對坐。徐岸承認自己以前以為，精湛修理一定仰賴一雙果斷的手。殷洛說他見過最好的修復由五個幾乎事事意見相左的人完成，他們只同意一件事：任何人都不該在紀錄中被抹去。徐岸忽然安靜，想像芮師傅站在梅秋身旁持燈，而真正困難的玻璃工序由別人完成。睡前，他通知河畔學校：溝槽確實遭修改，但改造者仍不明。黎明前信使帶來回覆。一位前工坊員工願意開口，條件是讓她弟弟匿名。殷洛念出條件，問徐岸是否接受。徐岸答應了。這只是短短一聲「好」，卻是他第一次把別人的安全放在急迫尋師之上。
