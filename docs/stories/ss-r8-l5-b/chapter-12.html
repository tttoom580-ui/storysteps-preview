玻璃競技場

The Glass Arena

星債未償：我們替夜空記住名字 · 第 12 / 25 章 · 4000–5000 · 英中對照 · 校訂中

The Glass Arena was once a training ground where artists tested lenses before a public audience. The city council had later placed a ban on unauthorized demonstrations, supposedly after a celebrity performer lost control of a suspended crystal. Old records said the accident had depressed public attendance, but the surviving witnesses remembered a different problem: the demonstration had been staged to make a false transfer seem possible. A merchant had been charged with fraud, although the court never established his intent. He filed a lawsuit and displayed his portfolio of legitimate work to argue that one mistake did not define his career. Xu read the documents with care. His teacher had testified at the hearing, but the transcript recorded only the conclusion. The original questions were missing. Even the plural word "witnesses" had been changed to the singular "witness" in a key sentence. Yin said a record could hide bias through the order in which details were presented, without containing an outright lie. They entered the arena through its unused east passage.

玻璃競技場原是工匠在觀眾面前測試鏡片的練習場所。後來議會禁止未經授權的展示，據說因一位明星表演者失控，讓懸吊水晶墜落。舊檔說事故使觀眾低落，但倖存見證人記得另一個問題：那次展示被安排來讓錯誤移轉看似可行。商人曾被控詐欺，法院卻未確立其意圖。他提起訴訟，展示合法作品集，主張一次錯誤不能定義整個職涯。徐岸仔細讀文件。師父曾出庭作證，但逐字稿只留下結論，原始問答消失。關鍵句甚至把複數的「見證人們」改成單數的「見證人」。殷洛說，紀錄就算沒有明確謊言，也能藉由資訊排列藏入偏見。兩人從閒置的東通道進入競技場。

The arena now housed a medical charity that served families unable to pay for proper nutrition. Prescriptions hung in labeled folders on a rack near the entrance. A child tried to poke a cracked lens with a stick, and Xu gently moved it out of reach. The charity director explained that the room had been given to them after a random auction of abandoned property. Some residential landlords wanted the charity removed because people waiting for appointments crowded the street. Yin asked to see the lease and discovered that the transfer could be reversed only through a public session. A worker angrily tried to shove their paperwork aside, thinking the travelers intended to evict the clinic. Xu apologized for arriving without enough explanation. They were not there to claim the building. Their ultimate goal was to find the missing witness marks while ensuring the clinic could continue treating patients. Once the worker understood, she offered to show them the storage room.

競技場如今成了醫療慈善團體的場地，服務負擔不起良好營養的家庭。入口的架子上掛著貼標籤的處方資料夾。孩子拿木棒想戳裂鏡，徐岸輕輕移開。慈善負責人說，這房間是廢產隨機拍賣後分給他們的。部分住宅房東因等診的人擠在街上，希望趕走診所。殷洛查看租約，發現移轉只能在公開會議中撤銷。一位員工憤怒地想推開他們的文件，以為旅人要迫遷診所。徐岸為未事先說清來意道歉。他們不是來爭建築，最終目標是找失蹤見證印記，同時確保診所繼續治病。員工明白後，自願帶他們看倉庫。

Behind a curtain were seven wooden cases, each containing a lens that had been declared damaged and discarded. Xu examined the first under a small lamp. The witness groove was intact, but a new metal collar hid it from the machine. Yin compared the collar's shape with the White Rock report. It matched the illegal attachment precisely. The charity director refused to let them remove the cases until an independent inspector could inventory them. Xu agreed, although he had hoped for a quick answer. A former performer arrived and described how a false demonstration had convinced the crowd that promises could be traded like tickets. He had protested at the time and lost work for years afterward. The director offered him a chair and asked if he wanted his testimony made public. He chose to speak under his own name, saying that silence had not protected him. His decision belonged to him alone. The cases were sealed pending review.

布簾後放著七只木箱，每只裝著一片被宣告損壞而丟棄的鏡。徐岸用小燈檢查第一片，見證槽其實完好，只是被新金屬環遮住，讓機器看不見。殷洛與白石報告比較，形狀完全符合非法附件。慈善負責人堅持獨立檢查者清點前不得移走木箱。徐岸雖想快點得到答案，仍同意。一位前表演者來了，述說假展示如何使群眾相信承諾能像票券般轉賣。他當年反對，失去多年工作。負責人請他坐下，問是否願公開證詞。他選擇用本名說話，表示沉默並沒有保護自己。這個決定只屬於他。木箱隨即封存待查。

The public hearing began the next morning. The council agreed to suspend the transfer of the seven lenses and fund a neutral inspection, but it would not declare every former owner guilty. Several residents protested that the decision was too slow. The clinic director asked them to consider how quickly a mistaken accusation could close the only treatment center in the district. Xu listened and understood that justice required not just speed, but a place where people could keep living while questions were resolved. After the meeting, Yin found a narrow slip of paper inside the old transcript binding. It listed three measurements and the phrase "gravity vault." The term sounded impossible in a glass arena, but Master Rui had once designed a chamber beneath the mountain reservoir that used a weighted sphere to keep delicate lenses level. Xu remembered seeing a drawing of that device as an apprentice. The next stage of the journey would take them below the waterline.

次日上午公開聽證開始。議會同意暫停七片鏡子的移轉並出資中立檢查，卻不會宣告所有舊主人有罪。有居民抗議進展太慢。診所負責人請他們想想，錯誤指控多容易讓區內唯一治療中心關門。徐岸明白，公正不只需要速度，也要讓人們在等待答案時仍有地方生活。會後殷洛在舊逐字稿裝訂處找到窄紙條，寫著三個測量值與「重力室」。玻璃競技場怎會有這東西？但芮師傅曾在山下水庫設計使用配重球保持鏡片水平的空間。徐岸想起當學徒時看過圖。下一階段的旅程將帶他們進入水位以下。
