公會的第二道鎖

The Guild's Second Lock

The glassworkers' guild met in a municipal hall whose agenda was written on a rotating board. Yin warned Xu to beware of easy explanations. The owners had contracts with a federal alliance of cities, and not all of them knew about the hidden attachment. One member tried to cling to the claim that the device was an art project, a new genre of moving pictures. Another compared the dispute to the final inning of a long game: everyone wanted the score settled without admitting who had changed the rules. Xu disliked the metaphor, but he understood the fear beneath it. The guild was choosing a nominee for its next chair, and a scandal could close workshops that supported hundreds of families. When a small organism crawled from a damaged lens, the meeting grew silent. The glass had been stored improperly. Mei Qiu had warned them that a fire could overtake the workshop before the sealing resin cooled, releasing harmless-looking dust that irritated the lungs. Xu asked to halt the machinery until a safety test was completed.

玻璃工匠公會在市政廳集會，議程寫在可旋轉告示板上。殷洛提醒徐岸提防過於簡單的解釋。工坊主人與城邦聯盟簽有合約，並非每個人都知道隱藏裝置。一位會員堅持那只是藝術企畫，屬於新型動態影像類型。另一人把爭議比作漫長比賽的最後一局：人人想要分數結清，卻不願承認誰改了規則。徐岸不喜歡這種比喻，但理解其下的恐懼。公會正提名新主席，醜聞可能讓供養數百家庭的工坊關門。一隻微小生物從受損鏡片爬出時，會場安靜下來。玻璃保存不當，梅秋早已警告，火勢可能在封膠冷卻前迅速蔓延整個工坊，釋出看似無害、實則刺激肺部的粉塵。徐岸要求停機，先完成安全測試。

In the courtyard, retired workers had gathered to protest a rule they called a quiet rebellion against the original charter. One elder lifted his shirt to show a healed rib injury from a furnace collapse. He had watched a rigid safety committee ignore warnings until someone was hurt. A young woman held a painted shield bearing the names of former apprentices; behind her stood the skeleton of a disused glass kiln. When an elderly worker began to sob, Xu wondered whether his teacher had once promised these people something he could not deliver. A student of theology asked whether recorded vows were sacred objects. Yin replied that the lens was a tool, and no tool should become an excuse to disregard consent. Someone had discovered toxic waste near the guild's storeroom, mixed with spilled vinegar from a cleaning station. The details felt absurd together, yet each had to be checked separately. Grief, belief, and chemical danger were not interchangeable forms of evidence.

院子裡，退休工人聚集抗議，他們把新規稱為對原憲章的一場無聲叛亂。一位長者掀起衣服露出熔爐倒塌留下的肋骨舊傷；當年僵化的安全委員會無視警告，直到有人受傷。一名年輕女子舉著畫有舊學徒姓名的盾牌，身後是廢棄玻璃窯的骨架。老工人哭出聲時，徐岸懷疑師父是否曾向他們許下無法實現的承諾。神學學生問被記錄的誓約是否是聖物。殷洛回答，鏡片只是工具，任何工具都不該成為忽視同意的理由。有人在倉庫旁發現有毒廢棄物，和清潔站打翻的醋混在一起。細節看似荒謬，卻應分別查核。哀傷、信仰與化學危險不是可以互換的證據。

The acting chair invited Xu and Yin into the workshop on three conditions: the workers would control the furnace, the evidence would be copied in front of both sides, and no one would release a worker's medical record without consent. Xu accepted. Inside, they found the second lock drawn into a repair diagram as if it were an ordinary hinge. Mei's replacement design appeared on the next page, crossed out with red ink. A margin note said the removed witness groove had been expensive to maintain. That note offered a motive for cutting corners, not proof of a conspiracy. Yin recorded the date and asked for the minutes of the decision. The chair admitted that a private buyer had offered a bonus if the guild could move promises between lenses without waiting for witnesses. He could not produce the buyer's name. Xu felt exhausted. The more they learned, the less useful it seemed to identify a single villain; the damage came from many decisions made with a little convenience in mind.

代理主席邀請兩人進工坊，但開出三條件：熔爐由工人操作、證據在雙方面前複製、未經同意不公開任何工人的病歷。徐岸接受。室內的修理圖把第二道鎖畫成普通鉸鏈，下一頁梅秋的替代設計卻被紅筆劃掉。頁邊註記說保留見證槽太花錢。這能說明偷工減料的動機，卻不能證明陰謀。殷洛記下日期並索取會議紀錄。主席承認，一名私人買家承諾支付獎金，條件是公會能在見證人不在場時，把承諾從一片鏡轉到另一片。他拿不出買家姓名。徐岸感到疲倦。知道越多，越難把傷害歸咎於單一惡人；那些破壞是眾人為了些微便利做出的許多決定累積而成。

Before leaving, they met a boy who had inherited his mother's polishing tools. He wanted to continue the craft but feared that every product of the guild would now be treated as dishonest. Xu asked him to demonstrate how to cut a clean witness groove. The boy made three attempts before his hand steadied. Yin praised his patience, not his family name. After sunset the acting chair allowed the guild's public books to be copied by an independent archivist, and the furnace stayed closed for inspection. It was an uncertain gain, but the workers could go home without breathing more dust that night. On the road out, Xu admitted he had imagined finding Master Rui behind one locked door and bringing him home. Yin said a rescue could still be real without being simple. He offered Xu half his bread, and Xu accepted without arguing about whose supplies had paid for it. Both men recognized how far they had traveled from Lantern Square.

離開前，他們遇見一名繼承母親磨光工具的少年。少年想繼續手藝，卻怕公會今後所有產品都會被當成欺詐。徐岸請他示範如何刻出完整的見證槽。少年試了三次才穩住手。殷洛稱讚的是他的耐心，不是家世。日落後，代理主席同意由獨立檔案員複製公開帳簿，熔爐也維持關閉受檢。進展尚不確定，但當晚工人至少能不再吸入粉塵、平安回家。離城路上，徐岸坦承自己曾想像只要打開一扇門，就能找到芮師傅並帶回家。殷洛說，救援即使不簡單，仍是真正的救援。他分給徐岸半塊麵包，徐岸沒有爭論是誰付了食物錢。兩人都明白，他們已走得遠離出發時的燈廣場。
