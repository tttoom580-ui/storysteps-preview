邊境營地

The Camp beyond the Border

星債未償：我們替夜空記住名字 · 第 9 / 25 章 · 4000–5000 · 英中對照 · 校訂中

A stubborn ass pulled the travelers' cart up the steep road while a bodyguard hired by the observatory followed at a distance. The mountain boundary was marked by stones that had once comprised part of a shared irrigation map. Now guards checked travelers differently according to their clothing, a practice that began to discriminate against the poorest migrants. Only residents with old permits were eligible for water rations, although the founding agreement had said otherwise. Xu watched rainwater become fluid threads across the rocks and thought of the people excluded by a line on paper. Yin asked the camp's clerk to incorporate testimony from new arrivals into the daily register. An investigator from the border court arrived that afternoon. She was passionate about accurate records but refused to treat rumors as facts. She asked everyone to describe what they had seen, and what they merely suspected. Xu began to understand how easily a frightened community could mistake a border for a moral judgment, especially when vulnerable people had few ways to challenge it.

一頭頑固的驢拖著兩人的車爬上陡坡，觀星臺雇用的保鏢則遠遠跟在後方。山界的石頭原本是共用灌溉地圖的一部分，如今守衛依旅人衣著差別查驗，開始歧視最窮的移民。只有持舊證的居民才有資格分配飲水，雖然建城協議並非如此規定。徐岸看著雨水沿石頭流成絲線，想到被紙上界線排除的人。殷洛請營地書吏把新抵達者的證詞納入每日名冊。下午邊境法院調查官到場。她熱衷精確紀錄，卻拒絕把傳言當事實；她請每個人分清親眼所見與純粹推測。徐岸逐漸理解，受驚的社區多容易把邊境界線誤當道德判決，尤其當脆弱的人缺乏申訴管道。

In the camp workshop, a woman repaired tents with a square patch of dark fabric. Her house had been destroyed during a storm, and the observatory offered her a pyramid-shaped residence that was too far from the common well. She still wore one broken sandal and carried a souvenir from her childhood village: a little copper bell tied to a string. A volunteer therapist listened to her account without promising to undo every loss. "We can repair a roof," the volunteer said, "but we cannot decide which memories are worth keeping for you." Xu copied the woman's description only after obtaining her permission. Yin examined the bell and found a valid maker's mark from the same workshop where Master Rui had once worked. The version of the seal matched the earliest witness records, before the second lock was installed. It was a hopeful clue, but Yin reminded Xu that similar objects might have been traded across the entire mountain range. They needed a document connecting this bell to a particular maker.

營地工坊裡，一名女子用深色布片修補帳篷。她的房子在風暴中毀壞，觀星臺提供的金字塔形住處卻離公共水井太遠。她仍穿著一隻破涼鞋，身上帶著童年村落的紀念品：一顆綁繩的小銅鈴。志工心理師聽她述說，卻沒有承諾能撤銷所有失去。「我們能修屋頂，」志工說，「卻不能替妳決定哪些記憶值得留下。」徐岸徵得同意才抄下她的描述。殷洛查看銅鈴，發現來自芮師傅昔日工坊的有效製作者印記，版本與第二道鎖裝設前的最早見證紀錄相同。這是有希望的線索，但殷洛提醒徐岸，類似物件可能早已傳遍整片山脈；仍需文件把銅鈴與特定製作者連結。

The bodyguard finally approached them at the communal kitchen. He had been ordered to report their movements, but he also carried a letter from his sister, a clerk in the high observatory. The letter said she had been asked to erase the ration entries for several displaced families. She refused and was suspended. The guard did not ask for forgiveness; he asked whether the investigator would receive the letter through a protected channel. She agreed and explained the risks. No one could guarantee that his employer would not discover his decision. He signed his statement anyway, using his real name. Xu felt the weight of that choice. The investigator then asked him to stop approaching the observatory's workers without warning. Even well-meant questioning could put them in danger. Xu promised to contact witnesses through representatives chosen by the camp, not through private pressure. Yin watched him make the promise and saw how much effort it cost him.

那名保鏢終於在公共廚房靠近他們。他奉命報告兩人的動向，卻也帶來在高觀星臺任職的姊姊寫的信。信中說有人要求她抹去幾戶流離家庭的配水紀錄，她拒絕後被停職。保鏢沒有要求原諒，只問調查官能否透過保護管道接收信件。調查官同意，也說明風險；沒人能保證雇主不會知道他的決定。他仍用真名簽下證詞。徐岸感到這選擇的重量。調查官接著要求徐岸別再不預告就接近觀星臺員工，因為善意提問也可能讓人置身危險。徐岸承諾以營地選出的代表聯繫證人，不再私下施壓。殷洛看著他許下承諾，知道這對徐岸有多不容易。

That evening the camp prepared a meal for families leaving the next morning. Xu repaired three lamps, Yin helped children label their belongings, and the investigator read the protected complaint aloud without identifying its author. The displaced households asked that the hearing be held before any new ration rule took effect. Their request was practical, not symbolic. Without water, even the most beautiful star map meant little. In a quiet corner, Xu asked Yin whether he missed the observatory he had abandoned. Yin admitted that he missed its work, not its obedience. He had loved the precision of a perfect measurement and the silence of a room full of stars. Yet he could not return while his name was used to justify removing other people's choices. Xu said he did not want Yin to return alone. Yin glanced at him, and for once let the silence hold a different possibility. They would decide what that meant after the next safe crossing.

當晚營地替隔天出發的家庭準備餐食。徐岸修了三盞燈，殷洛幫孩子標示物品，調查官則在保護申訴者身分的前提下朗讀內容。流離家庭要求新配水規則生效前先開聽證，這是實際需求，不只是象徵：沒有水，再美的星圖也無用。安靜角落裡，徐岸問殷洛是否懷念離開的觀星臺。殷洛承認懷念的是工作，而非服從。他喜愛精準測量，也愛滿室星光的安靜；然而只要有人仍用他的名字為奪走他人選擇辯護，他就不能回去。徐岸說不願讓他獨自回去。殷洛看了他一眼，第一次讓沉默包含另一種可能。至於那是什麼，他們決定等下一次安全渡越後再談。
