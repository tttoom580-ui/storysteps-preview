白石實驗

The Test at White Rock

The White Rock Institute required visitors to report any allergy before entering its laboratory. Shelves held the bulk of the region's older witness lenses, each tagged with a number that allowed researchers to cite its source. The director welcomed collaboration but warned that no one's fear would compel her to issue a premature conclusion. A counselor from the border camp had come with a descriptive account of families forced to travel farther for food and water. A physician added that poor digestion among children had worsened during the drought. At the meeting, an impatient official began to exclaim that the entire crisis could be solved by replacing one defective lens. Xu pointed to a grill beside the test room, where the heat of past repairs had left different marks on different metals. The evidence was already more complicated. The number of separate reports could overwhelm an impatient committee, so the director grouped them by testable question. What they needed was an experiment that isolated each suspected cause without exposing witnesses to unnecessary danger.

白石研究所要求訪客進實驗室前先申報任何過敏。架上存放本區大部分舊見證鏡，每片都貼有編號，讓研究者能引用來源。所長歡迎合作，卻警告不會因任何人的恐懼而被迫過早下結論。邊境營地的諮商員帶來家庭為取得食物與水而走更遠路的描述性報告。醫師補充，乾旱時孩子消化不良更加嚴重。會議上，一名急躁官員大聲嚷著，只要換掉一片故障鏡便能解決全部危機。徐岸指向測試室旁的金屬柵架，舊修繕的熱痕在不同金屬留下不同印記，證據顯然更複雜。大量獨立報告可能使急躁的委員會不知所措，因此所長依可測試的問題分類。他們需要的是能分離各個疑因、又不讓見證人暴露於額外危險的實驗。

The institute created three sealed boxes. One contained an untouched public lens, another held a copy made with both witness marks, and the third held the suspicious piece from the empty dome. The preliminary results were almost invisible. A silver line appeared in the first two boxes and faded in the third. The director refused to call the difference proof of fraud, since temperature, age, or dust might also affect the result. She proposed a radical but rational precaution: repeat the test before a mixed panel of citizens and researchers, changing only one variable each time. Yin recorded the measurement protocol, while Xu calibrated the device using an instrument borrowed from a railway terminal. A novelist visiting the institute said the procedure lacked the excitement of a thriller. Xu replied that an exciting accusation could ruin a life, while a careful measurement might help save several. The novelist stayed to assist with plain-language explanations.

研究所準備三個密封盒：第一個裝未修改的公共鏡，第二個裝有雙方見證印記的副本，第三個則是空圓頂的可疑鏡片。初步結果幾乎看不見，第一、二盒顯出銀線，第三盒的線卻迅速淡去。所長拒絕把差異直接視為詐欺證據，因為溫度、年代或灰塵也可能影響結果。她提出激進卻理性的預防方式：由居民與研究者組成混合小組重做實驗，每次只改動一個變因。殷洛記錄量測程序，徐岸用從鐵路終點站借來的儀器校正設備。來訪小說家說這種過程一點也不像驚悚小說刺激。徐岸回答，刺激的指控可能毀掉一個人的人生，仔細量測卻可能救好幾個人。小說家留下來協助寫淺白說明。

The second round confirmed that the third lens had been deliberately altered to function without a witness mark, although it still could not identify the person responsible. A record of the machine's power output showed that it had been activated during the week Master Rui disappeared. Yin's former signature appeared on the activation log. He went pale. Years ago he had signed a different order allowing a harmless inspection; the shape of the signature had been copied onto a new form. The director said she could verify a mismatch in dates, not the identity of the person who made the copy. Yin asked for a private moment. Outside, a field of white stones stretched toward the wilderness, and the sky above them looked enormous. Xu followed at a respectful distance. He did not offer the easy comfort of saying everything would be all right. He said only that he had seen Yin refuse a false transfer, and would say so under oath if needed.

第二輪實驗確認第三片鏡子確實遭人故意修改，得以不靠見證印記運作，但仍無法指認改造者。機器的能量輸出紀錄顯示，芮師傅失蹤那週曾啟動過它。殷洛昔日的簽名出現在啟動日誌上，他臉色發白。多年前他簽的是允許無害檢查的另一份命令，簽名形狀卻被複製到新表。所長說她能驗證日期不符，不能驗證是誰複製。殷洛請求獨處片刻。室外白石原野延伸到荒地，天空大得驚人。徐岸保持距離跟隨，沒有輕率安慰「一切都會沒事」，只說自己親眼看過殷洛拒簽虛假移轉，必要時願意宣誓作證。

At sunset the institute offered them two reports: a technical record available to the public and a protected attachment containing identifying details of the workers involved. The distinction mattered. Those workers had not agreed to become characters in a public accusation. Yin reviewed the wording, correcting every sentence that treated an inference as a fact. Xu drew a small diagram showing the difference between a lens that records a vow and a machine that moves the recorded image. The director approved it for display at the next town meeting. Their first controlled test had answered one question and created several more. As they walked back to the guesthouse, Xu asked Yin to teach him how to read the old activation codes. Yin said yes, then admitted that the idea of doing so made him afraid. Xu did not ask him to stop being afraid. He asked whether they could study the first page together after breakfast. Yin smiled and agreed.

日落時研究所交給兩人兩份報告：一份供公眾閱讀的技術紀錄，以及包含涉案工人身分細節的受保護附件。區分很重要，因為那些工人並未同意成為公開指控的角色。殷洛逐句修正報告中把推論寫成事實的語句。徐岸畫出簡圖，區別記錄誓約的鏡片與移動影像紀錄的機器。所長同意在下一次市民會議展示。第一次受控測試回答了一個問題，也帶來更多疑問。回旅店途中，徐岸請殷洛教他讀取舊啟動代碼。殷洛答應，隨即坦承光是想到就害怕。徐岸沒有要求他別怕，只問能不能早餐後一起讀第一頁。殷洛微笑同意。
