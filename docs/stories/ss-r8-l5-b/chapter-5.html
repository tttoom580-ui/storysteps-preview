一座敞開城門的代價

The Price of an Open Gate

The former workshop worker met them at an orchard beyond the provincial wall. She had helped draft the constitutional charter of the witness guild, although she was neither an official nor a trained lawyer. In her village, a farmer called himself a democrat because he believed every household deserved a vote on shared roads. A recent episode at the gate had proved why that mattered. A wealthy company wanted exclusive rights to a spring, and its advertisements used exaggeration to describe the danger of public ownership. A fleet of private carts stood ready to replace community carriers. The woman, Mei Qiu, told Xu that greed was too simple an explanation for every participant. Some guards feared losing their wages. Others had been promised medicine for sick relatives. She had hidden rather than sign a paper that concealed those incentives. Her eyes filled when Xu spoke Master Rui's name. "He stayed," she said, "because he knew the cost of the second lock."

前工坊員工在省城牆外的果園與他們碰面。她曾協助草擬見證公會的憲章，卻不是官員，也沒受過法律訓練。她村裡有名農夫自稱民主信徒，因為他相信每戶都應對共用道路有投票權。最近城門的一件事證明了其重要性：富商想獨占泉水，廣告誇大公有制的危險，一整隊私人貨車準備取代社區運輸。婦人梅秋告訴徐岸，不能把所有參與者都簡化成貪婪。有些衛兵怕失去薪水，另一些則被承諾提供親人的藥物。她因拒簽隱瞞這些誘因的文件而躲藏。聽見芮師傅的名字，她眼裡泛起淚光。「他留下來，」她說，「因為他知道第二道鎖的代價。」

Mei led them to a massive stone gate used by peasant farmers and traveling healers. A basket of dried herb leaves hung above the hinge as a sign that the spring belonged to all. The door began to rattle when a hired team arrived with orders to close it. A recession had struck the border villages; the company offered to recruit unemployed guards by paying them to control passage. Some families had already opened retail stalls outside the wall and feared losing customers. Xu argued with the guards until Yin placed a hand on his arm. They needed to understand the contract before trying to smash a lock that could crush someone. Mei described the company's tactic: offer substantial emergency repairs, then quietly treat temporary control as permanent ownership. The true threshold was a sentence in the lease. A local assembly had issued a verdict allowing repairs for one winter, not private control of every future season.

梅秋領他們到一座巨大的石門，農民與行腳醫者都靠它通行。門鉸上掛著一籃乾燥藥草，象徵泉水人人共享。受僱隊伍帶著關門命令抵達，門扇開始震響。邊村正逢經濟衰退，公司以付薪控制通道為條件招募失業衛兵。部分家庭在牆外經營零售小攤，也怕失去客人。徐岸與衛兵爭辯，直到殷洛把手放在他臂上。他們必須先讀懂合約，而不是砸開可能壓傷人的鎖。梅秋說明公司策略：以提供大規模緊急維修為誘因，再悄悄把暫時管制當成永久所有權。真正的關鍵是租約中的一句話。地方議會的裁決只允許一個冬季的修繕，沒有把每個未來季節都交給私人控制。

Mei produced a notebook with the original vote count and a list of repairs still owed. The figures alone did not prove every accusation, but they could be compared with the town's records. A public meeting formed beside the gate. Xu repaired the broken hinge while Yin read the lease to residents, stopping whenever someone wanted a difficult term explained. Several guards moved their carts aside. The company representative demanded that the meeting end, saying its documents were confidential. Mei replied that a contract affecting a public road could not be enforced through secret changes to an older promise. The crowd did not attack him. They asked for another vote and a safe passage schedule until the dispute was resolved. By evening the gate remained open under shared supervision. It was their first visible victory, and it had not required the astrolabe to do anything beyond confirming the existence of the silver thread. The consequences of the missing vows were widespread across villages far beyond the city.

梅秋取出記有原始票數和未完成修繕清單的筆記本。數字本身不能證明所有指控，卻可與城鎮檔案互相核對。城門旁形成公開會議。徐岸修好受損門鉸，殷洛向居民朗讀租約，遇到有人不懂艱深詞語就停下解釋。幾名衛兵把貨車移開。公司代表以文件機密為由要求終止會議。梅秋回答，影響公共道路的合約不能靠秘密修改舊承諾來執行。群眾沒有攻擊他，只要求重新投票，並在爭議解決前建立安全通行時間表。到傍晚，城門在共同監督下保持開放。這是他們第一次看得見的勝利，而星盤除了確認舊銀線存在，沒有施展任何奇蹟。誓約紀錄消失造成的影響廣泛蔓延到遠方村莊。

After the meeting, Mei told Xu the truth she had withheld. Master Rui had discovered a second lock inside the high observatory, one capable of accepting a vow copy without the witness who had created it. He intended to remove the illegal attachment, but the workshop's owners had threatened to destroy its records if he proceeded alone. Mei had escaped carrying the design for a safer replacement; Rui had stayed to protect the original witnesses. She did not know whether he was alive. She knew only that someone continued to issue orders in his handwriting. Xu felt the news like a stone dropped through his chest, yet he did not accuse her of abandoning him. She had chosen to save what she could. Yin offered to copy the drawings while she watched. As they worked in the orchard's fading light, the fifth dark line in the sky returned for a moment, then vanished. The observatory was listening.

會後，梅秋說出先前隱瞞的真相。芮師傅曾在高處觀星臺內發現第二道鎖，能在原見證人缺席時接收誓約副本。他想拆除非法裝置，但工坊主人威脅，若他獨自行動就銷毀紀錄。梅秋帶著安全替代設計逃走；芮留下來保護原始見證人。她不知道他是否還活著，只知道有人持續用他的筆跡發命令。消息像石塊落入徐岸胸口，他卻沒有責怪梅秋拋下師父。她只能選擇救出自己救得了的東西。殷洛請她看著，自己則複製圖紙。果園暮色裡，他們工作時夜空第五條暗線短暫復亮，又迅速熄滅。觀星臺正在注意他們。
