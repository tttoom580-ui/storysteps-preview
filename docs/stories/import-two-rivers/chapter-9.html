不該開的門

A Door That Should Not Open

雙河，同心 · 第 9 / 25 章 · 1000–2000 · 英中對照 · 校訂完成

用互動版查詞、朗讀與收藏

The stranger ran when Qi stepped outside. Qi could have chased him, but Yu pointed to the woman resting upstairs. “We cannot leave everyone alone.” Qi stayed. Instead, Yu called Elder Song, who closed the outer gate and asked guards to check the road. An hour later, they found the stranger's silver key near a stone exit. A bright symbol on it looked like two fish facing each other. “That character is alive in our oldest stories,” Song said. “It expresses the idea that two different forces can share one home.” Qi studied the image, then looked toward the silent river.

韓祈走到門外時，陌生人立刻跑了。韓祈本可以追上去，沈雨卻指向樓上休息的女子。「我們不能把大家獨自留下。」韓祈留下。沈雨通知宋長老，長老關上外門，請守衛檢查道路。一小時後，他們在石造出口旁找到陌生人的銀鑰匙。鑰匙上有個亮色符號，像兩尾相對的魚。宋長老說：「那個字活在最古老的故事中，它表達兩種不同力量能共享一個家的意思。」韓祈端詳圖案，再望向寂靜的河。

At dinner, Aunt Lin sent delicious rice and soup to the tower. Qi tried to eat, but the mark on his wrist began to burn. A thin tube behind the wall carried a blue liquid that had not moved all day. Now it rose slowly toward the ceiling. Yu found a lid on the floor beside the pipe and closed the nearest open jar so its contents would not spill. He listened to the sound of the water. It was not a natural stream. The liquid moved in short bursts, each one matching a distant bell. “Someone is testing the channel,” he said aloud.

晚餐時，林姨送來好吃的飯和湯。韓祈想吃東西，手腕印記卻開始發燙。牆後一條細管裡裝著藍色液體，一整天都沒有流動，此刻竟慢慢升向天花板。沈雨看到管子旁地上有個蓋子，便先把附近開著的罐子蓋好，以免內容物灑出。他細聽水聲。這不是天然河流。液體一陣一陣地向前，每一下都和遠處鐘聲相合。「有人正在測試水道。」他大聲說。

Elder Song led them to a safe viewing room with a window above the channel. “We can observe without opening anything,” he said. Yu used a sheet of clear paper to record each burst. Qi watched the position of a wooden float that moved with the water. After nine bursts, the float stopped. “The result is the same every time,” Qi said. “This is a repeated order, not a river finding its way.” Yu felt silly for having thought the bell was a ghost. Qi shook his head. “You had a question. You looked for a solution. That is not foolish.”

宋長老帶他們到水道上方、有窗戶的安全觀察室。「我們不用開任何機關就能看。」他說。沈雨用透明紙記下每一陣水流。韓祈觀察隨水漂動的木浮標位置。九陣之後，浮標停下。「每次結果都相同，」韓祈說，「這是重複下達的命令，不是河自己找路。」沈雨覺得自己之前以為鐘聲是鬼，實在有點傻。韓祈搖頭：「你提出疑問，也努力找解答。那不叫傻。」

They compared the key's symbol with the old wall text. A shark had been drawn beside one of the water gates, and a smaller fish beside the other. The pictures were not warnings about animals; they gave each gate a title. The larger gate was called the Hungry River, the smaller one the Quiet River. “One takes water quickly,” Yu said, “and one returns it slowly.” Song agreed that this was likely the meaning. Yet the model in the tower showed the larger gate open all year. If the main gate took too much, the smaller villages would receive almost nothing.

他們把鑰匙上的符號與舊牆文字比較。一道閘門旁畫著鯊魚，另一道旁畫著小魚。圖案不是在警告動物，而是各個閘門的名稱。大閘叫「飢河」，小閘叫「靜河」。「一條快速取水，另一條慢慢送回。」沈雨說。宋長老同意這很可能是原意。但塔裡模型顯示，大閘一整年都開著。若大閘取水太多，小村落幾乎得不到水。

Before sleep, Qi spoke of the strict house where he had grown up. He had been praised for strength and punished for showing fear. “I thought that if I made no mistakes, my father would forgive me for being different from the others,” he said. Yu asked what made him different. Qi held out the dark mark. “Nobody else heard the eight bells. They called me a broken instrument.” Yu felt anger rise, but kept his voice quiet. “I cannot change what they said. But I can tell you that you are not broken to me.” Qi looked away until he could speak.

睡前，韓祈說起自己成長的嚴格家庭。他因力量強大受到稱讚，卻因表露恐懼遭責罰。「我以為只要不犯錯，父親就會原諒我和其他人不同。」他說。沈雨問哪裡不同。韓祈伸出帶黑印的手腕。「沒有人聽見那八聲鐘。他們說我是壞掉的器具。」沈雨感到憤怒，卻放輕聲音。「我無法改變他們說過的話，但對我來說，你不是壞掉的人。」韓祈別開臉，等能說話時才轉回來。

Earlier that evening, Elder Song asked a worker to review the safety rules. Yu suggested that they improve the warning system by placing a second alarm in the public road. The worker wrote down the idea but reminded him that an engineer would need to approve it. Yu was pleased. He did not need every idea to be accepted immediately; he wanted the people nearby to have a better chance when danger came.

那天傍晚稍早，宋長老請工人重新檢查安全規則。沈雨建議改善警報系統，在公共道路加設第二個警鈴。工人把構想記下，也提醒必須經工程師批准。沈雨很高興。他不需要每個點子立刻被採用，只希望附近居民遇到危險時多一分機會。

At midnight the blue liquid rose again, faster this time. A glass tube burst inside the closed channel. The guards shut the control wheel, and Elder Song ordered the tower cleared. While helping the others leave, Yu noticed something small fall from a pipe: a paper square with a fresh drawing of the silver key. Someone had placed it there within the last day. On the back was written, “Return the name they stole, and the river will remember its way.” Yu held the paper to the light. In one corner stood the clear outline of the academy's official seal.

午夜，藍色液體又升起，這回更快。一根玻璃管在封閉水道裡破裂。守衛關上控制輪，宋長老命人撤出塔。沈雨幫大家離開時，看見管子裡掉出一張小紙片，上面剛畫好銀鑰匙。有人在過去一天內把它放進去。背面寫著：「把被偷走的名字還回來，河便會記得自己的路。」沈雨把紙對向燈光。角落清楚印著學院的正式印章。
