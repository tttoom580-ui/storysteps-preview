生病的果園

The Sick Orchard

雙河，同心 · 第 4 / 25 章 · 1000–2000 · 英中對照

用互動版查詞、朗讀與收藏

The paper bird led the boys to a grove of spirit trees beyond the city. Its largest branch had turned black, and dry leaves covered the entire path. A farmer named Aunt Lin showed them a wounded deer, with a little blood on its leg. “The animals are getting sick,” she said. “People say it is a disease from the river.” Qi knelt beside the deer but did not touch the wound. Yu examined the soil and found pale dust around the roots. He remembered the blue light from the stone yard. “We should ask someone who understands this material,” he said, “before we imagine a monster.”

紙鳥引兩人來到城外的靈樹果園。最大的樹枝已經變黑，乾葉鋪滿整條小徑。一名叫林姨的農婦帶他們看一頭受傷的鹿，鹿腿上有一點血。「動物都在生病，」她說，「大家說是河裡傳來的疾病。」韓祈蹲在鹿旁，沒有碰傷口。沈雨檢查泥土，發現樹根附近有淡色粉塵。他想起石材場的藍光。「在想像怪物之前，」他說，「我們應該先請懂這種材料的人看看。」

The academy's medical director, Teacher Mei, came after receiving their message. She tested the dust and smelled it carefully. “This is ground stone from a mountain mine,” she said. “It may block the roots from drinking water. It is not a disease that can pass from one animal to another.” Aunt Lin looked relieved, then angry. “If the mine is doing this, how will my trees survive?” Mei promised to report what they had found. Yu noticed that the shape of the dust trail was like a network, with each thin line leading toward the same old water pipe.

學院醫療部門主任梅老師接到消息後趕來。她檢驗粉塵，並小心辨認氣味。「這是山礦磨碎的石粉，」她說，「可能堵住樹根吸水，不是動物之間會傳染的疾病。」林姨先鬆一口氣，又生起氣來。「如果是礦場造成的，我的樹怎麼活？」梅老師承諾回報發現。沈雨注意到粉塵痕跡像一張網，細線最後都通往同一條舊水管。

Qi helped carry water from a clean spring. A generous shop owner sent bags of fruit for the workers: papaya, apples, and oranges. Yu bought a hamburger from a cart for Qi, who had missed breakfast. Qi looked at the strange round meal as if it were a test of courage. “You really grew up on the mountain,” Yu said with a laugh. “I have eaten rice every morning for sixteen years,” Qi replied. After one bite he smiled before he remembered not to. That small smile made Yu happier than his own first successful stone test.

韓祈幫忙從乾淨的泉水處提水。一位慷慨的店主送來水果給幫忙的人，有木瓜、蘋果和橘子。沈雨從攤車買了漢堡給錯過早餐的韓祈。韓祈看著那個奇怪的圓麵包，彷彿正在面對勇氣測驗。「你真的是在山上長大的，」沈雨笑了。韓祈回答：「我十六年來早上都吃飯。」他咬了一口，還沒想起自己不該笑便露出了微笑。那一瞬間，沈雨甚至比第一次成功移動石頭還高興。

The farm village had a small population, and almost every family depended on the grove. One man asked why the boys cared about trees when someone had stolen a valuable plate. Yu answered, “Because these things may be part of the same problem.” They combined the map, the dust trail, and the river levels on a single page. The paths pointed to a wood bridge behind the mine. Mei gave them a simple rule: never enter a closed mine, never drink unknown water, and tell an adult if anyone became ill. Their target was information, not a dangerous fight.

農村人口不多，幾乎每一家都靠果園生活。一個男人問他們，既然有珍貴河盤遭竊，為何還要關心樹木。沈雨回答：「因為這可能是同一件事。」他們把地圖、粉塵痕跡和河水水位結合到同一頁。線條都指向礦場後的木橋。梅老師訂下簡單規則：不能進封閉礦坑，不能喝不明水源，有人生病要立刻告訴大人。他們的目標是取得資訊，不是冒險打鬥。

At sunset, Qi discovered a row of small marks on the bridge. They were not damage from rain; each was cut into the wood at a regular distance. Yu could sense water moving deep below. “This old bridge may be worth more than it looks,” he said. Mei pointed out that the mine could not control the whole river by itself. There must be a second gate or an underground channel. Therefore, they agreed to measure the flow over several days. A farmer offered his house as a safe base for the work. Yu wrote the plan while Qi kept watch for the returning boat.

日落時，韓祈發現橋上有一排小刻痕。那不是雨水造成的破損，而是有人每隔固定距離刻在木頭上。沈雨感覺到下方深處有水在流。「這座老橋也許比看起來更有價值。」他說。梅老師指出，單靠礦場不可能控制整條河，一定還有第二座閘門或地下水道。因此他們約好連續幾天測量水流。農夫提供自己的屋子作為安全據點。沈雨寫下計畫，韓祈則留意那艘船是否回來。

Night brought the boat. It passed under the bridge without a lantern. Two people pushed a long box from the deck onto a cart. The boys stayed behind the farmer's wall and did not try to escape into the dark to follow them. But when the cart rolled away, it left a broken board on the road. On that board someone had kept score with eight straight marks and one deeper line. Yu's stomach tightened. Nine marks. Eight bells for Qi and nine for him. Someone had left them a message that matched the strange sounds under the city.

夜裡，船來了。它沒有點燈，從橋下通過。兩個人把長箱從甲板推上推車。兩人躲在農夫家的牆後，沒有衝進黑暗追趕。但推車離開時，有一片碎木板掉在路上。木板上有人用八道直線和一道更深的刻痕記分。沈雨的胃一緊：九道記號，正好是韓祈聽到的八聲，加上自己聽到的九聲。有人留下了與城市地底怪聲相符的訊息。
