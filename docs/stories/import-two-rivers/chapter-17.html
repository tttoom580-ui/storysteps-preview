承諾的代價

The Price of a Promise

雙河，同心 · 第 17 / 25 章 · 1000–2000 · 英中對照 · 校訂完成

用互動版查詞、朗讀與收藏

The shelter bell rang three times, not nine. Elder Song studied the river and said the condition of the old gate was growing worse. “Three bells mean the pressure has reached the outer wall,” he said. Yu asked how deep the channel ran. Song drew the depth on a piece of paper. Qi wanted to hurry into the tower, but Song stopped him. “An accident there could drown the people below.” The chief of the emergency team agreed. They would first clear the road, then send trained workers to inspect the wheel. Yu was willing to help, but not to break the safety plan.

避難所的鐘響了三下，不是九下。宋長老看著河面，說舊閘門情況愈來愈差。「三聲表示壓力已到外牆。」他說。沈雨問水道多深，宋長老在紙上畫出深度。韓祈想立刻衝進塔裡，卻被宋長老攔下。「在那裡出意外，可能淹死下方居民。」緊急隊總指揮也同意。他們要先清路，再派受過訓練的工人檢查輪盤。沈雨願意幫忙，但不願破壞安全計畫。

A nearby army unit came to support the emergency team with trucks and lights. Soldiers offered warm corn soup to the families waiting at the shelter. One man carried a bottle of beer, but the chief asked him to put it away until the work was done. Everyone needed clear minds. Yu watched the careful mix of people: soldiers, doctors, water workers, students, and elders. Each had a role. The sight gave him a positive feeling, even while thunder sounded far away. “We are not alone,” he told Qi. “For once, that does not bother me,” Qi replied.

附近的軍隊派卡車和燈具支援緊急隊。士兵送熱玉米湯給避難所等候的家庭。一名男子拿著啤酒瓶，總指揮請他先收起來，等工作結束再說。大家需要清醒的頭腦。沈雨看著分工仔細的人們：士兵、醫生、水務人員、學生和長者。每個人都有任務。即使遠處已傳來雷聲，這一幕仍讓他感到積極的希望。「我們不是孤單的。」他對韓祈說。「這回我也不介意了。」韓祈回答。

During a quiet moment, Qi received a message from his father. The family expected him to return home after the crisis and accept a marriage arrangement with another powerful house. Yu saw Qi's face change. “You do not have to explain,” he said. Qi folded the message. “I want to. My father believes marriage is a trade between families. He says love makes people weak.” Yu kept his voice low. “What do you believe?” Qi looked at their two marks. “I believe a promise matters only when the people making it are free to choose.” Yu felt warmth rise in his chest.

短暫休息時，韓祈收到父親的訊息。家族要求他危機過後回家，接受與另一個有權勢家族的婚姻安排。沈雨看見他的臉色變了。「你不用解釋。」他說。韓祈摺起訊息。「我想說。我父親認為婚姻是家族間的交易，還說愛會讓人軟弱。」沈雨放輕聲音：「你自己怎麼想？」韓祈看向兩人相對的印記。「我相信承諾只有在雙方能自由選擇時才有意義。」沈雨胸口湧上一陣暖意。

That evening, the academy held a public dialogue about the gates. Several speakers feared that opening the second river would destroy the city. Others argued that leaving it closed would destroy the farms. A woman who had lost a home in the first flood began to cry. Yu did not judge her fear. Instead, he asked what information would help everyone make a decision. Elder Song showed the old models, and Teacher Mei explained the health reports. People who had shouted at one another now listened to actual numbers. It was not peace yet, but it was a beginning.

傍晚，學院舉辦水閘公開對話。幾位發言者擔心開小河會毀了城市，其他人則認為不開會毀了農田。一名在第一次大水中失去家的婦人哭了。沈雨沒有批判她的恐懼，而是問需要什麼資料才足以讓大家決定。宋長老拿出舊模型，梅老師說明健康報告。原本互相喊叫的人，開始聽真正的數字。這還不是和平，卻是開始。

A group of children from the lowland villages offered a short prayer for their homes. Afterward they clapped for the rescue workers. Yu found that emotion could be honest without turning into anger. Qi stood with him near a window. A loose wire shook against the wall in the wind. Its sound was like the beginning of a bell, but then it stopped. “The marks on our hands are not a command,” Yu said. “They only mean we may be able to help.” Qi nodded. “If the people agree, and if the engineers say it is safe, we will do our part.”

低地村落的孩子們為家園做了簡短祈禱，之後替救援工作者鼓掌。沈雨發現情感可以真誠，卻不必變成憤怒。韓祈與他站在窗邊，一條鬆掉的電線在風裡敲著牆，聲音像鐘聲開頭，隨後又停了。「我們手上的印記不是命令，」沈雨說，「只代表也許能幫忙。」韓祈點頭：「如果大家同意，工程人員也說安全，我們就盡一份力。」

A worker from the lower village spoke about the lack of clean water in his home. “Some neighbors pray every evening,” he said. “It helps them keep hope, but we still need safe pipes.” Yu thanked him for making the difference clear. He felt that people could believe different things and still work toward the same safety plan. Qi listened and wondered how many such voices his father had never heard.

一名來自低地村落的工人談起家鄉缺乏乾淨水源。「有些鄰居每晚祈禱，」他說，「能讓人保持希望，但我們仍需要安全的水管。」沈雨謝謝他把差別說清楚。他覺得即使信念不同，大家仍能為同一項安全計畫合作。韓祈聽著，不知父親曾忽略過多少這樣的聲音。

Just before midnight, a worker came from the northern gate with troubling news. The main channel had begun to leak into the old service tunnel. The depth was increasing quickly. Someone had cut a wire connected to the alarm, so the town below might not receive a warning. The chief sent a team to restore it, and Qi offered to guide them along the outer path. Yu stayed at the shelter to help send messages. They had decided not to work alone, but for one night their duties would be different. As Qi left, Yu caught his sleeve. “Come back,” he said. “I will,” Qi answered.

將近午夜，一名工人從北閘趕來，帶著令人不安的消息。主河道開始漏水進舊維修隧道，水深快速增加。有人剪斷接警報器的電線，下方小鎮可能收不到警告。總指揮派人修復，韓祈自告奮勇沿外側道路替他們帶路。沈雨留在避難所協助傳訊。他們決定不獨自行動，但今晚任務不同。韓祈離開前，沈雨抓住他的袖子。「回來。」他說。「我會。」韓祈回答。
