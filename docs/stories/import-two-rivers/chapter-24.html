山巔之上

At the Mountain Peak

雙河，同心 · 第 24 / 25 章 · 1000–2000 · 英中對照 · 校訂完成

用互動版查詞、朗讀與收藏

Although the flood had slowed, Luo still held the private control tower on the mountain peak. An international trade company had placed a commercial guard team there, and nobody knew how many people remained inside. The chief inspector planned a careful examination of the tower after the weather improved. Qi and Yu stayed at the safe control station. “We are not going to attack a building because we are angry,” Qi said. Yu agreed. On the screen, the private line showed a slow increase in pressure, but the public gate remained stable. The threat was not over, and they had to remain alert.

洪水雖然減緩，羅仍占著山巔私人控制塔。一家國際貿易公司在那裡安排商業警衛隊，沒人知道裡面還剩多少人。總督察打算天氣轉好後仔細檢查塔。韓祈和沈雨留在安全控制站。「我們不會因為生氣就攻打一棟樓。」韓祈說。沈雨同意。螢幕顯示私人線路壓力緩緩增加，但公共閘仍穩定。危險未解除，大家必須保持警覺。

The inspector arranged a schedule for a trained team to approach the tower from the upper road. The guards had been given a gun, which made the plan more dangerous. Nobody wanted violence. A negotiator spoke through a loudspeaker, asking the guards to lay down their weapons and leave by the open door. Her tone was calm, not threatening. From behind a curtain, one guard shouted that he had been tricked: the company had promised him an easy job, not a fight against his own town. The negotiator told him there was still a safe way out.

督察安排時程，讓受訓隊伍從上路靠近塔。警衛手上有槍，行動因此更危險，沒有人希望發生暴力。談判人員透過擴音器，請警衛放下武器，從開門走出來。她語氣平和，沒有威脅。布幕後一名警衛喊著自己被騙了：公司答應是輕鬆工作，不是要對抗自己的城鎮。談判人員告訴他，仍有安全離開的方式。

While waiting, Yu and Qi ate sandwiches brought by volunteers. A woman at the station wore a panda badge on her coat and explained that she had joined the rescue team after a school swimming accident years earlier. “People need practice before a crisis,” she said. “Not just brave words.” Yu watched a boy hop over a puddle outside the shelter and thought of how different this day was from his first test at the academy. Then he looked down at his palm. The white mark had grown dimmer, almost like a normal line of skin. Qi's black mark had faded too.

等待時，沈雨與韓祈吃志工送來的三明治。站裡一名女子外套別著熊貓徽章，她說多年前學校曾發生游泳意外，所以她加入救援隊。「危機來臨前就得練習，」她說，「不是只會說勇敢的話。」沈雨看見避難所外一名男孩跳過積水，想到這一天和入學第一場測驗有多不同。他低頭看掌心，白印漸漸變淡，幾乎像普通皮膚紋路。韓祈的黑印也淡了。

At noon the team entered the tower without a fight. The guards had agreed to surrender, and the police took charge of the weapons. Luo was found in the upper room, beside a machine that allowed one user to direct water without public approval. “I built it for the city,” he insisted. Qi answered from the public station over the radio, “You built it so nobody could stop you.” The inspector told Luo that his reasons would be heard in court, but the machine would remain shut. She ordered the last private wire removed and the control records secured.

中午，隊伍沒有打鬥便進入塔。警衛同意投降，警方接收武器。羅在上層房間被找到，旁邊有台機器能讓單一使用者未經公共同意就改變水流。「我為城市造它！」他堅持。韓祈從公共站透過無線電回答：「你造它，是為了沒有人阻止你。」督察告訴羅，法院會聽他的理由，但機器必須繼續關閉。她命人拆除最後的私人電線，封存控制紀錄。

When the news reached the shelter, people began to cheer. Yu felt relief, but also exhaustion. He and Qi walked to a high path where the city and farms could be seen together. Qi bent down and picked up a small stone shaped like half a circle. “Do you think the marks will vanish?” he asked. Yu held out his hand. “Maybe. We have learned what they were meant to teach.” Qi placed the stone in his palm. “Then I will need a new reason to stay near you.” Yu smiled. “You could simply want to.” Qi did not let go of his hand.

消息傳到避難所，人們開始歡呼。沈雨鬆一口氣，也感到筋疲力盡。他和韓祈走上高路，能同時看見城市與農田。韓祈彎腰撿起一顆半圓形小石。「你覺得印記會消失嗎？」他問。沈雨伸手：「也許。我們已經學到它們要教的事。」韓祈把石放進他掌心。「那我需要另一個理由待在你身邊。」沈雨笑了：「你可以只是想待著。」韓祈沒有放開他的手。

At the safe station, a guard bent to pick up a fallen bag. Inside were cash, a wet swimsuit, and a paper badge similar to those worn by the company's workers. It belonged to a young man who had come to the tower for a summer job. He told the inspector that the company had used fear of terrorism to justify armed guards, although no such threat had been proved. “I believed them till I saw the flood,” he said. The inspector recorded his statement and returned the harmless personal items.

安全站裡，一名警衛彎腰撿起掉落的袋子。裡面有現金、濕泳衣，以及與公司工人佩戴的相似紙徽章。袋主是來塔裡打暑期工的年輕人。他告訴督察，公司利用人們對恐怖主義的恐懼，替武裝警衛找理由，但那種威脅根本沒有得到證實。「直到我看到洪水才知道他們說的有問題。」他說。督察記下證詞，歸還沒有危險性的私人物品。

For the first time since the bells began, the mountain fell truly quiet. No hidden signal ran beneath the roads. The main river and Quiet River continued to flow in their planned paths, watched by the public engineers. Yu imagined another examination waiting at the academy, and laughed at how small it now seemed. “We still have lessons to finish,” he said. “And you still have to learn how to play badminton.” Qi gave him a look of wounded pride. “I would rather face another river.” They stood together until the clouds broke and sunlight touched the peak.

自從鐘聲開始，山第一次真正安靜了。道路下不再有隱藏訊號。主河與靜河依照規畫流動，由公共工程師監測。沈雨想到學院還有考試在等，忍不住笑了，因為那些考試現在顯得好小。「我們還有課要上，」他說，「而且你得學會打羽球。」韓祈露出自尊受傷的表情。「我寧可再面對一條河。」兩人並肩站到雲散，陽光照上山巔。
