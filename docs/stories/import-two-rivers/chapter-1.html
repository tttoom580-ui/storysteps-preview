鐵道上方的鐘

The Bell Above the Tracks

雙河，同心 · 第 1 / 25 章 · 1000–2000 · 英中對照

用互動版查詞、朗讀與收藏

The bell above the underground station rang nine times, although there was no bell in the station. Shen Yu stopped on the final step of the stairs. Hundreds of people moved around him, but nobody else looked up. A cold line of water ran across the dry floor and vanished beneath the wall. Yu had come to Cloud Gate Academy to develop his power, yet he could barely lift a cup with spirit breath. He followed the wet mark instead of the crowd. At the far end of the platform, a boy in a white coat was trying to hold open a closing door for an old man.

地下車站上方傳來九聲鐘響，但車站根本沒有鐘。沈雨停在樓梯最後一階。數百人從身邊走過，卻沒有人抬頭。一道冰涼的水痕劃過乾燥的地板，消失在牆下。沈雨來雲門學院，是想修煉靈力，但他連用靈息舉起一只杯子都很吃力。他沒有跟著人群走，而是循著水痕前進。月臺盡頭，一名白衣少年正替一位老人撐住即將關上的車門。

The door operator shouted a warning. The white-coated boy let the door close, then led the old man toward a safe seat. His movements were fast, but his face was calm. “You heard it too,” Yu said before he could stop himself. The boy turned. “Heard what?” “Nine bells.” A pause. “I heard eight,” the boy answered. His name was Han Qi, the best male student of the mountain's secondary school. His speed had made him famous, but people said he never smiled. Qi looked at the floor. “That water should not be here. Where does it lead?”

車門操作員大聲警告。白衣少年鬆開車門，扶老人坐到安全的位置。他動作極快，神情卻很平靜。「你也聽到了。」沈雨話一出口便後悔。少年轉過頭。「聽見什麼？」「九聲鐘。」「我聽見八聲。」他停了一下才回答。少年名叫韓祈，是山中附屬中學最出色的男學生。他以速度聞名，卻被人說從不笑。韓祈低頭看地板。「這裡不該有水。它流去哪裡了？」

They climbed to the windy street together. On a balcony above the entrance hung a stone sign: CLOUD GATE ACADEMY, NORTH BASE. New students stood in a field below, waiting for the entrance challenge. A teacher explained the tradition: each student must move a small stone across a line using only a steady breath. “My belief is that every person can learn,” she said. “A good teacher must give each student a better way to try.” Yu was moved by her words. He had failed the test twice in his village, and his family had sold their cart to pay his train fare.

兩人一起走上風大的街道。入口上方的陽臺掛著一塊石牌：「雲門學院北基地」。新生站在下方的廣場，等待入門挑戰。老師說明傳統：每位學生必須只靠平穩的靈息，把一顆小石頭移過界線。「我相信人人都能學習，」她說，「好老師應該幫每個學生找到更好的方法。」沈雨被這番話打動。他在村裡已經兩次測試失敗，家人還賣掉推車才付得起他的車票。

Qi watched Yu take his turn. The stone shook but did not move. A student behind them laughed. “For instance, some people have no talent at all.” Yu wanted to deny his failure, but he kept his hand still. Qi placed a second stone beside the first. “Try listening to the ground,” he said. The teacher was surprised; that method was not part of the test. Yu heard a low sound like water traveling through a pipe. He pushed gently. One stone slid forward, then the other. He did not win the challenge, yet for the first time he knew what his breath could do.

韓祈看著沈雨輪到測試。石頭晃了晃，卻沒有移動。身後一名學生笑道：「比如說，有些人根本沒有天分。」沈雨想否認自己的失敗，但仍讓手保持穩定。韓祈把第二顆石頭放在旁邊。「試著聽地面，」他說。老師很驚訝，因為測驗並沒有這種方法。沈雨聽見低沉的聲音，像水流過管子。他輕輕推動，第一顆石頭滑向前，接著是第二顆。他沒有贏得挑戰，卻第一次知道自己的靈息能做什麼。

At lunch they sat on a seesaw in the empty children's yard beside the field. Qi spoke of the nation beyond the mountains and the many schools that belonged to its great organization. “Not all of them teach the same path,” he said. “Some students build fire; some heal. You seem to hear moving water.” Yu asked why the bell had sounded differently to them. They discussed the question until the sun touched the roof. “A broken bell?” Yu suggested. “There is no bell at the station,” Qi reminded him. Then he showed Yu a black mark on his wrist shaped like half a circle.

午餐時，他們坐在廣場旁空蕩蕩的兒童遊戲區翹翹板上。韓祈說起山外的國度，以及屬於大型組織的許多學院。「不是每所學院都教同一條修行路，」他說，「有人修火，有人療傷。你似乎能聽見流動的水。」沈雨問兩人為何聽到不同的鐘聲。他們討論到夕陽碰上屋頂。「鐘壞了？」沈雨猜測。「車站沒有鐘。」韓祈提醒他，並露出手腕上一道半圓形的黑印。

When Yu remembered the challenge later, he realized how much his approach to learning had already changed. In his village he had hidden every failure. Here Qi had seen him struggle and had stayed beside him. That did not remove the hard work ahead, but it made the work feel possible. He hoped he might someday offer someone else the same kindness.

沈雨後來想起入門試煉，才發現自己的學習方法已經改變許多。在村裡，他總想藏住每次失敗。來到這裡，韓祈看見他吃力的樣子，仍站在他身旁。這沒有減少未來的辛苦，卻讓努力看起來可能有結果。他希望自己有一天也能給別人同樣的善意。

“I have had this mark since I was a child,” Qi said. “When the bell rings, it hurts.” Yu raised his own hand. On his palm lay a white mark, also half a circle. They stared at each other. Far under the street, something heavy shifted, and the seesaw moved although neither boy had touched the ground. Across the yard, a narrow crack opened in the dry earth. The teacher came running and ordered everyone away. Yu heard a tenth bell, very deep and very slow. Qi seized his sleeve. “Do not tell them about our marks,” he whispered. “Not until we know who is ringing that bell.”

「這道印記從我小時候就在了，」韓祈說，「鐘聲一響就會痛。」沈雨舉起自己的手，掌心也有一道白色半圓印記。兩人互相凝視。街道深處有沉重的東西移動，翹翹板在兩人都沒踩地時搖了起來。廣場另一頭，乾燥的土地裂出細縫。老師奔來，叫所有人退開。沈雨聽見第十聲鐘，既深又慢。韓祈抓住他的袖子。「先不要告訴他們我們的印記，」他低聲說，「至少在查清誰敲鐘之前。」
