名字的代價

The Price of a Name

雙河，同心 · 第 5 / 25 章 · 1000–2000 · 英中對照

用互動版查詞、朗讀與收藏

Cloud Gate College held its yearly selection ceremony, and Qi was expected to win a place among the highest students. Yu had received an invitation only because Teacher Mei had asked for a special test. The school hall was crowded. A young lady in a green sweater explained that the challenge had two parts: moving stones and working with a fellow student. Yu liked the second part, but Qi looked worried. “They may use my scores to judge you,” he said. “That is a burden I do not want to place on you.” Yu shook his head. “Then let us make our own score.”

雲門高等學院舉行年度選拔，大家都認為韓祈會進入最優秀的學生名單。沈雨之所以收到邀請，是因為梅老師替他申請了特別測驗。大廳擠滿了人。一位穿綠毛衣的年輕女士說明挑戰分兩部分：移石與和同伴合作。沈雨喜歡第二部分，韓祈卻很擔心。「他們可能拿我的分數來評價你，」他說，「我不想讓你背負這個負擔。」沈雨搖頭：「那我們就做自己的分數。」

The first test measured how long they could keep two stones in the air. Qi's stones rose high, while Yu's hardly moved. A crowd laughed. “A perfect pair,” someone said in an unkind voice. The second test was different: one student had to guide the other across a floor that moved like water. Qi used speed, but fell. Yu closed his eyes and sensed the movement before it reached his feet. “Left,” he said. “Now wait. Step forward.” Together they crossed the floor, earning a double score for listening and trust. The teachers watched in silence.

第一項測驗是讓兩顆石頭停在空中。韓祈的石頭升得很高，沈雨的卻幾乎不動。人群笑了。「真是完美的一對。」有人語帶嘲諷。第二項完全不同：一個學生要帶另一人穿過像水面般移動的地板。韓祈依靠速度卻跌倒了。沈雨閉眼，在地板移動前便感覺到變化。「左邊，現在等等，往前一步。」兩人一起走過，因善於傾聽與信任而得到雙倍分數。老師們安靜地看著。

After the ceremony, a student showed everyone a strange video on his phone. It seemed to show Qi entering the western cell and carrying a weapon. The title accused him of being a terrorist who wanted to harm the mountain. Yu's face grew hot. “That word is too serious to use without proof,” he said. “Who made this video?” Nobody answered. The image had been cut and placed over a scene from a school production. A student confessed that he had uploaded it after receiving a small payment. “I thought it was a trick for a play,” he said, shaking.

典禮後，有學生拿手機給大家看一段奇怪的影片。畫面像韓祈走入西邊石牢，還拿著武器。標題指控他是想傷害山城的恐怖分子。沈雨臉色發燙。「沒有證據，不該用這麼嚴重的字眼。」他說，「是誰製作的影片？」沒有人回答。那個影像被剪下，貼在另一段校園演出的畫面上。一名學生承認自己收了小筆錢後上傳。「我以為只是戲劇惡作劇。」他發抖著說。

The school director ordered the video removed and asked the student to write a public apology. Yet the damage was already done. Some people refused to sit near Qi. Yu had to swallow his anger when one boy called him Qi's foolish servant. At dinner, he used a tissue to clean tea from his notebook. Qi sat across from him and said, “You do not have to keep standing beside me.” Yu answered, “If I leave whenever things become hard, what does our partnership mean?” Qi looked down at his hands. “It means more than I know how to say.”

學院主任要求刪除影片，也要那位學生公開道歉，但傷害已經造成。有些人不肯坐在韓祈附近。一名男生罵沈雨是韓祈愚蠢的跟班，沈雨只得忍住怒火。晚飯時，他拿面紙擦掉筆記本上的茶。韓祈坐在對面說：「你不必一直站在我旁邊。」沈雨回答：「如果事情一難我就走，我們的搭檔關係算什麼？」韓祈低頭看著雙手。「那對我的意義，比我能說出來的還多。」

Teacher Mei explained the expense of keeping old water gates open. The gates' main feature was a wheel that needed two people to turn together. “Their function was to share water across the mountain,” she said. “Now only one channel remains active.” The spirit stone system could produce light for the city, but it also needed water. Yu seldom thought about the cost of magic before coming here. Qi stared at the model. “If a second gate opens, the river might return to the farms,” he said. Mei replied, “Or flood a town. Knowledge must come before action.”

梅老師向他們解釋維持舊水閘的費用。水閘最大的特點，是一個必須兩人一起轉動的輪盤。「它原本用來讓山區各地共享水源，」她說，「現在只有一條水道仍在運作。」靈石系統能替城市供光，但也需要水。沈雨來到這裡以前，很少想過法術的代價。韓祈盯著模型。「如果打開第二道閘門，水也許就會回到農田。」梅老師回答：「也可能淹掉村鎮。必須先懂，才能動手。」

By nightfall, the damaged video had spread to several groups. Yu asked the school to keep a copy before removing it, so investigators could learn who had changed the picture. The student who uploaded it agreed to help. It was a small sign that a mistake did not have to become a person's entire life.

夜晚來臨時，那段造假的影片已傳到好幾個群組。沈雨請學院在刪除前留下副本，讓調查者能查出誰動了畫面。上傳的學生同意協助。這是個微小的徵兆：一個錯誤不必成為一個人一生的全部。

On the way home, they passed a school soccer game. The players' feet swept dust into the air, and the moving line on the field looked like a wave. Yu suddenly stopped. “The bell is not calling for one person,” he said. “It is calling for two.” Qi looked at him, and the black mark on his wrist began to glow. The white mark in Yu's palm answered with a pale light. Together they heard nine notes, followed by a voice so quiet it might have been part of the wind: “Bring back the other half before the new moon.”

回宿舍的路上，他們經過一場校園足球賽。球員的腳掃起塵土，球場上的移動線條像波浪。沈雨忽然停下。「鐘不是在呼喚一個人，」他說，「它在呼喚兩個人。」韓祈看向他，手腕上的黑印開始發光，沈雨掌心的白印也亮起微光。兩人一起聽見九個音，隨後是一道細得幾乎與風融在一起的聲音：「新月之前，把另一半帶回來。」
