河港的火

Fire at the River Port

雙河，同心 · 第 6 / 25 章 · 1000–2000 · 英中對照 · 校訂完成

用互動版查詞、朗讀與收藏

The river port held a small night market where workers sold soup, cabbage cakes, and sweet tea. Yu and Qi went there to ask about the boat from the mine. A man invited them to play badminton beside his shop, and Qi surprised everyone by missing the first three shots. “Your feet are fast, but your hands do not know the game,” Yu laughed. For a short time they forgot the strange affair. Then a fireman ran past the court shouting that an engine had caught fire on a cargo boat. Red light spread across the shore, and the port became a place of crisis.

河港有個小夜市，工人賣湯、高麗菜餅和甜茶。沈雨與韓祈來打聽礦場的船。一名店主請他們在店旁打羽球，韓祈竟接連漏接三球。「你的腳很快，手卻不懂這個遊戲。」沈雨笑道。兩人短暫忘了怪事。接著一名消防員衝過球場，喊著貨船引擎失火。紅光沿河岸擴散，港口頓時陷入危機。

Qi stepped forth to help the fireman carry water hoses, while Yu helped a neighbor lead children away from the smoke. Each individual had a different task. Some workers opened the road; others carried old people to a clean hall. A woman had left a pillow and her son's small socks on a bench. Yu gathered them and found the family together near a tea stand. He was not strong enough to fight the fire with spirit power, but he could count the people who had reached safety. That simple work mattered more than any school score he had received.

韓祈向前幫消防員搬水管，沈雨協助鄰居帶孩子遠離濃煙。每個人都有不同任務：有人清出道路，有人扶老人到乾淨的大廳。一位婦人把枕頭和兒子的小襪子留在長椅上。沈雨收好東西，在茶攤附近找到一家人。他的靈力還弱得不足以滅火，卻能清點安全到達的人數。這份平凡的工作，比他在學院得到的任何分數都重要。

When the flames were under control, Officer Tian from the harbor office questioned the captain. The boat was carrying broken machines, not fruit or grain. Its owner claimed that the engine had failed by accident. Qi noticed a copper box hidden under a seat, but he did not touch it. “We should let the officials examine it,” he told Yu. The captain shouted that the boys had no right to participate in port business. Teacher Mei, who had come to offer aid, replied that the government had asked all witnesses to speak. A clever lie could not govern a real investigation.

火勢控制住後，港務處的田警官詢問船長。船上載的是壞掉的機器，不是水果或穀物。船主聲稱引擎只是意外故障。韓祈發現座位下藏著一個銅盒，卻沒有碰。「讓官員檢查。」他對沈雨說。船長大喊學生無權參與港口事務。趕來協助的梅老師指出，政府請所有見證者提供資訊。再聰明的謊話也不能主導真正的調查。

Yu kept a journal of what he had seen. He wrote about the burning engine, the place where Qi had noticed the box, and the calm actions of the fire team. “You write as if each person is a piece of a larger picture,” Qi said. “My village has no money for spirit machines,” Yu replied. “People there survive by remembering who needs help.” His neighbor had once lost a house in a flood, and no one had come in time. That memory was why he wanted to learn water arts. Qi listened without speaking and offered Yu a clean cloth for his smoke-covered face.

沈雨把看到的事寫進日誌：燃燒的引擎、韓祈發現盒子的位置，以及消防隊沉著的行動。「你寫得像每個人都是大圖的一部分。」韓祈說。沈雨回答：「我的村子沒有錢買靈力機器。人們靠記得誰需要幫忙才活得下去。」他的鄰居曾在洪水中失去房子，救援來得太晚。這段記憶讓他想學水系術法。韓祈靜靜聽著，遞給他一塊乾淨的布擦去臉上的煙。

The harbor held many kinds of people, and Yu had always liked its culture of shared work. Tonight, however, fear had made strangers angry with one another. A merchant accused the academy, while a student blamed the town prison for letting thieves go free. Qi said that neither accusation was supported by evidence. “We should be excited when we find truth, not when we find someone easy to hate.” Yu raised an eyebrow. “That sounded like something a teacher would say.” “Do not tell anyone,” Qi answered. It was the nearest he had come to making a joke.

河港聚集各種人，沈雨一直喜歡這裡互相幫忙的文化。但今晚，恐懼讓陌生人彼此生氣。商人怪罪學院，學生指責城中監獄放任小偷。韓祈說，兩種指控都沒有證據。「找到真相時我們該感到興奮，不是找到一個容易討厭的人時。」沈雨挑眉：「這聽起來像老師會講的話。」「別告訴別人。」韓祈回答。這是他最接近說笑的一次。

As the smoke lifted, Yu watched Qi bring a frightened child a cup of water. The boy had spent years training to appear stronger than any human, yet he was gentle with someone who could offer him nothing in return. Yu wondered whether Qi knew that this kindness was as important as his sword practice. He made a quiet note to tell him someday.

煙霧漸散時，沈雨看著韓祈替一名受驚孩子端水。韓祈多年來努力鍛鍊，想顯得比任何普通人都強，卻能溫柔對待無法回報他的人。沈雨不知韓祈是否明白，這份善意和劍術練習同樣重要。他默默記下，想找一天告訴他。

Near midnight, Teacher Mei returned with news. The copper box held no stolen plate. Inside were old machine parts and a note carrying the seal of the Mountain Society. It ordered the captain to move the parts from the north tower before the next full moon. “That is only eighteen days away,” Yu said. “Why such a hurry?” Mei could not answer. She showed them a second fact: one part had a carved space shaped like half a circle. Qi placed his marked wrist beside the drawing and felt a sudden cold. The old tower was calling them again, but this time with a deadline.

接近午夜，梅老師帶來消息。銅盒裡沒有失竊的河盤，只有舊機件和一張蓋著山盟印章的字條。字條命令船長在下一次滿月前，把機件從北塔移走。「還有十八天。」沈雨說，「為什麼這麼急？」梅老師無法回答。她又出示第二項事實：某個機件刻著半圓形的凹槽。韓祈把有印記的手腕放在圖旁，忽然感到一陣寒冷。北塔又在召喚他們，這回還有期限。
