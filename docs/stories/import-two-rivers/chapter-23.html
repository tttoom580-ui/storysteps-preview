The Contract Under the Tower

雙河，同心 · 第 23 / 25 章 · 1000–2000 · 英中對照 · 校訂完成

用互動版查詞、朗讀與收藏

The repaired wheel turned, and the Quiet River opened another small distance. Pressure began to fall. But the chief inspector still needed to know why Luo had started the private channel one night early. She sent a search team to the room beneath his hall, where the hidden wire had led. Within an hour the team found a metal album of old plans, a complex contract, and letters signed by the Mountain Society. The documents showed that a private company had paid Luo to direct extra water to spirit towers. An emergency would have allowed the company to take control of the gates permanently.

修好的輪盤轉動，靜河又開了一小段，壓力開始下降。但總督察仍要查明羅會長為何提前一晚啟動私人水道。她派調查隊到他大廳下方、暗線通往的房間。不到一小時，隊伍找到裝著舊圖的金屬相簿、一份複雜契約，以及山盟簽署的信件。文件顯示一家私人公司付錢給羅，讓額外水流進靈塔。若發生緊急狀況，公司便可能永久掌控水閘。

The inspector showed only the public facts to the waiting crowd. She did not call Luo an enemy, but said his actions were being examined under the law. “Our focus is survival first,” she told everyone. “The court can decide what responsibility follows.” Teacher Mei provided the medical reports about the stream and mine. A university scientist explained how stone powder could make water unsafe without being a secret poison. This clear explanation mattered. People were frightened enough without stories that made them scare each other. The inspector asked them to stay at the shelters until the engineers declared the roads safe.

督察只向等待的人群公開可公布的事實。她沒有直接稱羅是敵人，只表示他的行為正在依法調查。「我們先以生存為重，」她說，「之後由法院決定責任。」梅老師提供小溪和礦場的醫療報告。大學科學家說明石粉如何讓水不安全，並非什麼祕密毒藥。這份清楚的解釋很重要，人們已夠害怕，不需要再用故事互相恐嚇。督察請大家留在避難所，直到工程師宣布道路安全。

At the control station, Yu noticed a broad change in the water sound. The flow was becoming smooth and even. He told Qi, who checked the air pressure against the engineer's model. The numbers matched. They had not defeated the river by force; they had followed a plan based on old knowledge and new tests. Then the chief announced that the oldest wall had stopped moving. She said the gate could remain at its current position. Qi looked at Yu, exhausted. “We did it,” he whispered. Yu shook his head gently. “All of us did.”

控制站裡，沈雨聽見水聲大幅改變，流動變得平順均勻。他告訴韓祈，韓祈把氣壓和工程模型比對，數值吻合。他們不是用武力打敗河，而是遵守結合古老知識與新測試的計畫。接著總指揮宣布，最老的石牆已停止移動，閘門可以維持目前位置。韓祈疲憊地看向沈雨。「我們成功了。」他低聲說。沈雨輕輕搖頭：「是大家一起。」

A worker brought a paper from Luo's private room. Under a false contract was the missing third name from the tower wall: Luo himself. He had once been trained as a guardian, but failed the test that required two people to share control. Rather than accept that limit, he built a system he could rule alone. Yu read the paper with sadness. “He had a chance to work with others,” he said. Qi nodded. “And chose control instead.” The discovery answered the mystery of the erased name without giving Luo an excuse for the harm he had caused.

一名工人帶來羅私人房間的文件。假契約下方，寫著北塔石牆被擦掉的第三個名字：羅本人。他過去受過守護者訓練，卻在要求兩人共同控制的測驗中失敗。羅沒有接受限制，而是建造一個只有自己能統治的系統。沈雨難過地讀著。「他本來有機會和別人合作。」他說。韓祈點頭：「他卻選了獨占。」這項發現解釋被刪去的名字，卻不能成為羅傷害他人的藉口。

As dawn neared, the emergency team began a careful search of the railroad and playground below the hill. Some paths were still under water, so the chief preferred to keep them closed. Children at the shelter asked when they could go home. “Within two days, if the checks pass,” Aunt Wei answered. An old man asked whether the town could pardon the workers who had stayed silent from fear. Mei said those people deserved a fair hearing, and that helping investigators now could have value. No one was promised freedom from responsibility, but nobody was treated as a monster either.

接近黎明，緊急隊開始仔細搜尋山下鐵路與遊戲場。有些路仍泡在水裡，總指揮寧可先封閉。避難所孩子問何時能回家。「檢查順利的話，兩天內。」魏姨回答。一位老人問能否原諒那些因害怕而沉默的工人。梅老師說，他們應有公平聽證的機會，如今協助調查也有價值。沒有人被保證可以免責，也沒有人被當成怪物。

When Luo heard that the hidden contract had been found, he refused to speak for a long time. An older teacher said shame might explain his silence, but it could not erase his decisions. “People have to answer for what they do,” she said. Yu agreed, while hoping the court would also listen to workers who had been afraid to refuse orders. The future of the river required both fairness and open records. The council would also found a public water center where residents could read those records themselves.

羅聽說隱藏契約被找到後，很長一段時間拒絕說話。一位年長教師說，羞恥也許能解釋他的沉默，但不能抹去他的選擇。「人必須對自己的行為負責。」她說。沈雨同意，也盼法院願意聽那些害怕違抗命令的工人。河流的未來需要公平，也需要公開的紀錄。議會還將成立公開水務中心，讓居民能自己查閱紀錄。

Yu returned to the high window of the control station. Below him the two rivers moved side by side for the first time in twenty years. The sight made him remember the final line beneath the dragon: chosen, not born. He had come to Cloud Gate wanting proof that he was worth something. Now he knew his worth did not depend on a test, a mark, or a family name. Qi came to stand next to him. There was no need for a speech. They listened to the water for a long time, until its sound became as natural as breathing.

沈雨回到控制站高窗旁。下方兩條河二十年來第一次並肩流動。他想起龍圖下最後那句：不是天生，是選擇。他曾帶著證明自身價值的渴望來到雲門，如今知道自己的價值不取決於考試、印記或姓氏。韓祈來到他身旁，不需要講話。兩人聽水很久，直到那聲音變得像呼吸一樣自然。
