牆上的龍

The Dragon on the Wall

雙河，同心 · 第 14 / 25 章 · 1000–2000 · 英中對照 · 校訂完成

用互動版查詞、朗讀與收藏

The bell drew Yu and Qi back to the academy, where a new activity was being held in the old arts hall. Students had built a model dragon from paper, wood, and silver paint. A brief notice claimed that the dragon protected the city's spirit power. Yu wondered why such an ancient story had become the school's main lesson. He examined a print of the design and found a narrow edge missing from the page. “Someone changed this picture,” he told Qi. “The dragon should have two tails, not one.” Qi remembered the stone beside the small stream.

鐘聲把沈雨與韓祈引回學院。古老藝術廳正舉行一場新活動。學生用紙、木頭和銀漆製作龍的模型。簡短公告宣稱這條龍保護城市靈力。沈雨疑惑古老故事為何成了學校的主要教材。他檢查圖樣印刷品，發現頁面邊緣少了一條。「有人改過這張圖，」他告訴韓祈，「龍應該有兩條尾巴，不是一條。」韓祈想起小溪旁的石雕。

A young student greeted them near the stairs. She was deaf and used written notes to explain that she had helped paint the dragon. “The director told us to remove the second tail,” her note said. “He said it looked childish.” Yu showed her the photograph of the old carving. Her face changed. She led them upstairs to a storage room where old drawings lay under cloth. There, hidden beneath a heavy frame, was a diamond-shaped metal plate. A line cut across it like a river dividing at a fork. “I saw this when I cleaned the room,” she wrote, “but nobody believed me.”

一名年輕學生在樓梯旁向他們打招呼。她有聽力障礙，用寫字說明自己曾幫忙替龍上色。紙條寫著：「主任叫我們拿掉第二條尾巴。他說那樣太幼稚。」沈雨給她看舊石雕照片，她的神情變了。她帶兩人上樓，到布下面放著舊圖的儲藏室。沉重畫框後藏著一塊菱形金屬牌，一道線把它切成像分流的河。「我打掃時看過，」她寫道，「可是沒人相信。」

Qi read the small marks on the plate. The words described an existence older than the academy: the two river gates were once joined by a stone flight of stairs and a shared control wheel. The dragon was a picture of that system, not a living beast. Yu felt his fear ease. “We can prove this,” he said. “We have the wall, the map, and this plate.” He did not touch the object until the student had called the keeper. Their actions were now careful and regular, because the evidence mattered more than anyone's excitement.

韓祈讀出金屬牌上的細字。它描述學院出現以前就存在的事：兩道河閘過去靠一段石階和共用控制輪相連。龍只是整套水道的圖像，不是真正的獸。沈雨心中的恐懼稍稍緩解。「我們能證明了，」他說，「有石牆、地圖，還有這塊牌。」在學生找來管理員前，他沒有碰證物。他們的動作如今仔細而有條理，因為證據比任何人的興奮更重要。

Teacher Mei arrived with a notebook of medical records. Workers from the northern mine had complained of sore throats and stomach trouble, and one man's liver tests had changed after months of contact with dust. “Do not try to cure them with magic alone,” Mei warned. “They need real medical care and clean water.” The academy kitchen sent hot tofu with garlic for dinner. Qi ate quietly while Yu arranged the papers. The meal reminded them that saving a river was not an idea on a wall. It meant ordinary people being able to eat, work, and sleep safely.

梅老師帶著醫療紀錄來了。北方礦場工人抱怨喉嚨痛和胃不舒服，其中一人長期接觸粉塵後，肝功能檢查值出現變化。「別想只靠法術治他們，」梅老師警告，「他們需要真正的醫療和乾淨的水。」學院廚房送來蒜香豆腐當晚餐。韓祈靜靜吃著，沈雨整理紙張。這餐提醒他們：拯救河流不是牆上的觀念，而是讓普通人能安心吃飯、工作和睡覺。

A shadow crossed the upstairs window. Qi went to check the public corridor and saw Luo's assistant leaving the building. The assistant stopped when he noticed Qi. “Your family promised to provide the river with guardians,” he whispered. “You should be grateful the president made room for you.” Qi felt the old shame rise. “If you want me to believe a promise,” he replied, “show me the words.” The assistant's face grew hard. “You will regret asking.” He left before Qi could answer. Yu came to the doorway, and Qi told him everything.

一道影子掠過樓上窗戶。韓祈到公共走廊查看，看見羅會長的助理正離開。助理發現韓祈，停了下來。「你們家答應為河道提供守護者，」他低聲說，「會長肯給你位置，你該感激。」韓祈感到熟悉的羞恥湧上來。「想要我相信約定，」他回答，「就給我看原文。」助理臉色一沉。「你會後悔問的。」他走了，沒給韓祈回話機會。沈雨走到門口，韓祈把事情全部告訴他。

Mei told them that the highway above the old river had been built much later than the stone gates. Its foundations could make repairs difficult if the builders had forgotten the channels below. Yu added the road to the map and asked the engineer to check it. The dragon drawing had taught him that something hidden under a familiar place could still affect daily life many years later.

梅老師說，舊河上方的公路比石閘晚建了許多年。若建造者忘記地下水道，公路地基會讓維修變得困難。沈雨把道路加到地圖，請工程師檢查。龍圖讓他明白，熟悉地方下方隱藏的事物，很多年後仍可能影響日常生活。

Late that night, the deaf student returned with another note. She had found the full drawing on the back of an old stage curtain. Below the dragon were three words in a language she could not read. Yu compared them with the script on the northern tower. The translation made his hands shake: NOT BORN. CHOSEN. He looked at Qi. They had assumed their marks were family gifts, perhaps even signs of a fixed fate. But the oldest message said something else. The river was not waiting for their blood. It was waiting for two people willing to choose each other.

深夜，那名聽障學生帶著另一張紙回來。她在舊舞臺布幕背面找到完整圖案，龍下有三個她不認識的古字。沈雨拿它們和北塔文字比對。翻譯讓他的手發抖：「不是天生。是選擇。」他看向韓祈。兩人一直以為印記是家族的贈禮，甚至是固定命運的證明。但最古老的訊息說的卻是另一回事。河流等的不是他們的血，而是兩個願意選擇彼此的人。
