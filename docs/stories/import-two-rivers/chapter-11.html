祭典門後的金色

Gold Behind the Festival Gate

雙河，同心 · 第 11 / 25 章 · 1000–2000 · 英中對照 · 校訂完成

用互動版查詞、朗讀與收藏

Cloud Gate's spring festival was usually a social event, with grape drinks, sweet jam, and lantern games. This year President Luo stood on the main stage beside a gold gate, praising the academy's success in protecting the capital. Yu and Qi joined the crowd with Teacher Mei. “Our goal is to find who ordered the river plates moved,” Yu said. “Not to win a prize.” Qi understood. He had made a promise to trust Yu's judgment, but a part of him still wanted to chase the first person who looked guilty. He kept both hands at his sides while the chief guard opened the ceremony.

雲門春祭通常是社交活動，有葡萄飲料、甜果醬和燈籠遊戲。今年羅會長站在金色大門旁的主舞臺上，稱讚學院保護首都的成就。沈雨和韓祈跟梅老師走入人群。「我們的目標是找出誰下令移走河盤，」沈雨說，「不是贏獎品。」韓祈明白。他已答應相信沈雨的判斷，但心裡仍想追趕第一個看起來可疑的人。他讓雙手留在身旁，看著總守衛宣布祭典開始。

The first game was simple: beginners had to shoot a small wooden ball into a ring. Qi took the bow, but Yu asked him to wait. A boy had placed an iron trap behind the target, and a younger girl was about to step on it. Qi moved swiftly and caught her before she fell. The crowd began to clap. “Who put that there?” someone demanded. The boy who owned the trap admitted that an adult had paid him to make the game more exciting. He had not understood the danger. “Then tell us everything,” Mei said. “This is important.”

第一個遊戲很簡單：新手要把小木球射進圓環。韓祈拿起弓，沈雨卻請他稍等。有個男孩把鐵製陷阱放在靶後，一名小女孩正要踩上去。韓祈迅速拉住她。人群開始鼓掌。「誰放的？」有人追問。放陷阱的男孩承認，大人給他錢，要讓遊戲更刺激。他不知道會有危險。「那就把全部經過說清楚，」梅老師說，「這很重要。」

Near the gate, Yu noticed the same official from the record hall speaking with Luo. Their dialogue was too quiet to hear, but the official passed a small mug covered with cloth to the president. Yu did not try to steal it. Instead, he looked at the table where cups were washed. Every cup except one had the same mark. The unusual cup held a bit of gold dust. “Gold does not belong in tea,” he whispered. Mei examined the dust and asked an assistant to keep a sample. “We will test it. Simply seeing a color is not enough.”

大門旁，沈雨看見檔案廳的官員正在和羅會長交談。他們的對話太小聲，但官員把一只蓋著布的馬克杯交給會長。沈雨沒有想偷。他轉而看向洗杯子的桌子。除了那一只，每個杯子都有同樣的記號。不同的杯子裡殘留一些金色粉末。「茶裡不該有金粉。」他低聲說。梅老師檢查粉末，請助手保存樣本。「我們會驗。光看到顏色還不夠。」

A religious group from the lowland villages sang by the open gate. Their song told of water shared freely, without charge. Yu had heard it at home. Qi listened as if the words were new. “They emphasize trust,” he said. “Not strength.” Yu nodded. He watched an old man explain the meaning to a child: success did not belong only to the strongest person. It belonged to the community that helped people survive. The song reminded Yu why his village had sold their cart for him. It was not to make him famous, but to help him become useful.

來自低地村落的宗教團體在大門旁唱歌。他們唱的是不收費、人人共享的水。沈雨在家鄉聽過這首歌。韓祈聽得像第一次知道歌詞。「他們強調信任，」他說，「不是力量。」沈雨點頭。一位老人向孩子解釋，成就不只屬於最強的人，也屬於讓大家能活下來的整個社群。這首歌讓沈雨想起村人為何替他湊車費：不是讓他出名，而是盼他有能力幫忙。

Behind the festival gate, Yu found a folded page that had fallen from a worker's bag. It listed dates when the main river had been closed for repairs, but another list showed the gate was open on those same days. Both documents carried Luo's seal. “One of these cannot be true,” Yu said. Qi wanted to rush to the stage and accuse Luo at once. “Wait,” Yu insisted. “We need to learn which one is correct.” Qi breathed slowly until his shoulders relaxed. Together they took the page to Teacher Mei, who recognized the paper as coming from the council's private store.

祭典門後，沈雨撿到一頁從工人袋裡落出的摺紙。上面記著主河道維修而關閉的日期，但另一份名單卻顯示同一天閘門開著。兩份文件都蓋了羅會長的印。「不可能兩份都是真的。」沈雨說。韓祈想立刻衝上舞臺指控羅會長。「等等，」沈雨堅持，「要先弄清楚哪份正確。」韓祈緩緩呼吸，直到肩膀放鬆。他們把紙交給梅老師。她認出這是議會私人庫房使用的紙。

The old singer returned to tell Yu that religion had never been a reason to control the river. “Our usual work is feeding neighbors and helping after storms,” she said. “The importance of the song is simple: everybody needs water.” Yu thanked her. His village had taught the same lesson without using grand words. He wished Luo could hear the people rather than only the machines he had built.

那位年長歌者回來告訴沈雨，宗教從不該成為控制河流的理由。「我們平常做的是送食物給鄰居，暴風雨後互相幫忙，」她說，「這首歌的重要性很簡單：每個人都需要水。」沈雨向她道謝。他的村子不用華麗語句，也教過同樣的道理。他希望羅會長能聽聽人們，而不是只聽自己造的機器。

That night the festival ended with fireworks over the city. Qi and Yu stayed away from the crowd to watch the river instead. The great gold gate opened for a moment, and water rushed through the old stones below. Nine low sounds rose from the ground. Yu placed his white palm against Qi's marked wrist. For the first time, the two halves formed a full circle, and a clear picture appeared in Yu's mind: a locked room beneath Luo's hall. Qi saw the same room. “Now we know where to search,” Qi said. Yu nodded. “And now he may know we can see it.”

當晚祭典以城市上空的煙火結束。沈雨與韓祈避開人群，望著河面。巨大的金門短暫開啟，水從下方舊石間湧過。地底響起九聲低音。沈雨用白色掌印碰上韓祈有印記的手腕。兩個半圓首次合成完整圓環，沈雨腦中浮現清楚影像：羅會長大廳下方的一間上鎖房間。韓祈也看見了。「現在知道去哪裡找了。」韓祈說。沈雨點頭：「他可能也知道我們看得見。」
