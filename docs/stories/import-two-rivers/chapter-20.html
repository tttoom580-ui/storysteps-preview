海岸的最後一個輪盤

The Last Wheel at the Coast

雙河，同心 · 第 20 / 25 章 · 1000–2000 · 英中對照 · 校訂完成

用互動版查詞、朗讀與收藏

Qi and the engineers reached the coast after a long drive through heavy rain. At the port, an old worker kept a wheel from the first spirit gate in a small store. Its metal joint was still strong, but the wheel itself was nearly covered with sand. “We can compare the size with our model,” an engineer said. “If it fits, we take it back before the road floods.” Qi helped carry the part to a platform beside the store. It weighed more than a hundred kilograms, so they used a lifting machine rather than trying to raise it by hand.

韓祈和工程師們在大雨裡長途行車，終於抵達海岸。港口一位老工人把第一代靈閘輪盤留在小倉庫裡。金屬接頭仍很堅固，但輪盤幾乎被沙埋住。「把尺寸和模型比較，」工程師說，「合用就趕在道路淹水前帶回去。」韓祈幫忙把零件搬到倉庫旁的平臺。它重達一百多公斤，大家用吊運機，沒有硬用人力抬起。

The worker's wife had baked bread for the crew. She was ill and could not join them, but she had left a note asking the workers to eat before starting the return trip. Qi read the note twice. He had once believed that impressive strength meant working without rest. Now he ate slowly and waited for the patient engineer to finish checking each bolt. “Your teacher influenced you,” the worker observed. Qi shook his head. “Two people did,” he said. “One taught me how water moves. The other taught me why people matter.”

老工人的妻子替大家烤了麵包。她生病無法來，卻留字條要工人們回程前先吃東西。韓祈讀了兩遍。他以前以為驚人的力量就是不停休息地做事。現在他慢慢吃著，等有耐心的工程師檢查每一顆螺栓。「你的老師影響你很深。」老工人說。韓祈搖頭：「有兩個人，」他說，「一個教我水如何流動，另一個教我人為什麼重要。」

On the road back, they passed a town festival where a dancer performed in front of a crowded square. Children played a sport with a light ball, and a couple sold hot food beside the street. The scene looked peaceful even under clouds. Qi thought of the families living below the main gate. They would be in danger if Luo opened it without warning. Luck alone would not save them. Qi contacted the chief inspector and explained where the wheel was. The inspector ordered an escort vehicle to meet them at the bridge outside the capital.

回程途中，車隊經過一座城鎮的慶典，舞者在擁擠廣場前表演，孩子們玩輕球運動，一對情侶在街邊賣熱食。即使雲層壓低，畫面仍很平和。韓祈想起住在主閘下方的家庭。如果羅會長不預警就開閘，他們會有危險。只靠運氣救不了他們。韓祈聯絡總督察，報告輪盤的位置。督察命護送車在首都外的橋邊會合。

Yu was waiting at the bridge with a clean map, a team of volunteers, and enough blankets for the workers. He had spent the day comparing the old channel plans with new flood levels. “The lower road may close in two hours,” he warned. Qi stepped down from the truck, wet and tired. They stood together for a moment without speaking. Then Yu took the wet coat from Qi's hands and gave him a dry one. “You came back,” he said. Qi looked directly at him. “I said I would.” No great spell passed between them, only the strength of a promise kept.

沈雨帶著清楚的地圖、一隊志工和足夠工人使用的毛毯在橋邊等候。他花了一天比對舊水道圖與新洪水高度。「下面的路可能兩小時後封閉。」他提醒。韓祈從卡車下來，渾身濕透，滿臉疲憊。兩人安靜地站了一會兒，沈雨才接過濕外套，給他換上乾衣。「你回來了。」他說。韓祈直視他：「我說過會回來。」沒有驚天法術，只有守住承諾的力量。

The engineers fitted the wheel into the test frame before moving it to the gate. It turned smoothly, and the joint held under pressure. Everyone was satisfied with the mechanical result, but the inspector still required a public warning and a second safety check. “A good part cannot make a bad plan safe,” she said. Yu agreed. Qi watched the old wheel stop, then placed his hand beside Yu's on the cold metal. For a moment both marks warmed. They felt the same slow water under the road, moving toward the closed passage like a question waiting to be answered.

工程師先把輪盤裝進測試架，再送往水閘。它轉得平順，接頭也承受住壓力。大家滿意機械測試結果，但督察仍要求公開警告和第二次安全檢查。「好零件不會讓壞計畫變安全。」她說。沈雨同意。韓祈看著舊輪停下，將手放在冰冷金屬上，正好靠近沈雨的手。兩人的印記短暫發暖。他們感覺同一股緩慢水流在路下前進，朝封閉通道移動，像等待回答的問題。

The chief checked the route on a map. The bridge was less than a mile from the gate, but heavy rain could make even that short distance dangerous. Qi asked the driver to slow down and follow the escort vehicle. He had learned that reaching a place safely mattered more than arriving first. In the back of the truck, the repaired wheel stayed firm against its wooden supports.

總指揮查看地圖上的路線。橋距離閘門不到一英里，但大雨能讓短路也變危險。韓祈請司機放慢，跟著護送車走。他已學會安全抵達比搶第一更重要。卡車後方，修好的輪盤穩穩固定在木製支架上。

Near sunset, the convoy reached the gate and delivered the wheel to the repair team. Before the workers could begin, the main river suddenly rose. A signal flashed from the private control tower on the opposite hill. Someone was using the hidden channel again. The chief ordered the public warning bells rung and the road closed. Qi looked toward the tower. He could see a figure at the high window, wearing the long dark coat of the Mountain Society. Yu recognized the shape of the man's face from the academy stage. It was Luo, and he had started the plan one night early.

接近日落，車隊抵達水閘，把輪盤交給修理隊。工人還沒動手，主河水位突然上升。對面山丘的私人控制塔閃出訊號。有人再次使用隱藏水道。總指揮命人敲響公開警鐘，封閉道路。韓祈望向控制塔，在高窗裡看見一個人影，穿著山盟的深色長袍。沈雨認出那張在學院舞臺見過的臉。是羅會長，他把計畫提前了一晚。
