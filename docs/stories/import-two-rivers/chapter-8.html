水下的名字

The Names Beneath the Water

雙河，同心 · 第 8 / 25 章 · 1000–2000 · 英中對照 · 校訂完成

用互動版查詞、朗讀與收藏

At the northern watchtower, Elder Song opened a room sealed for almost a century. Its walls showed an ancient design: two rivers joining under a circle. “Our people once called this the Twin Current,” he said. “It carried water and spirit energy together.” An older stone named the guardians of the rivers. Yu traced one mark without touching it. The name was Shen, followed by Han. “Is that our identity?” he asked. Song shook his head. “A family name is only part of who you are. The choices you make tell me more.”

在北方瞭望塔，宋長老打開一間封了將近一世紀的房間。牆上有古老圖案：兩條河在圓環下匯合。「我們以前叫它雙流，」他說，「水與靈氣一同流動。」另一塊更古老的石頭刻著守河者名字。沈雨隔著一點距離描看筆畫，一個姓沈，另一個姓韓。「這就是我們的身分嗎？」他問。宋長老搖頭：「姓氏只是你的一部分。你的選擇更能告訴我你是誰。」

Elder Song showed them a box of grain collected from farms that had used the river. The old seeds were still dry and hard. “Every year we tried to maintain a small garden,” he said. “But the water grows lower.” An elder farmer entered with a cup of peach tea and told them what had happened after the flood. Officials had issued instructions to close the smaller channel, saying it would protect the people below. Soon the valley grew too dry for rice. The younger generation moved to the city. Houses stayed empty, and the watchtower became a place for strangers to photograph.

宋長老拿出一盒以前河邊農場留下的穀粒。舊種子仍乾硬。「我們每年都努力維持一座小園，」他說，「但水愈來愈少。」一位年長農夫端著桃子茶進來，說起洪水後的事。官員下令關閉較小的水道，宣稱這樣能保護下游居民。不久，山谷乾到無法種稻。年輕一代搬去城裡，房屋空下來，瞭望塔成了陌生遊客拍照的地方。

Qi found a metal model behind a wooden screen. It showed two streams, one open and one closed. Small figures could be moved along the paths to pose different questions: what if rain increased, what if the main channel broke, and what if both gates opened? Yu moved one figure backward and watched the water level on the model change. “This is not a map of one day,” he said. “It is a lesson about making decisions.” Song smiled. “Exactly. A guardian must not only have power. A guardian must be willing to think about other people.”

韓祈在木屏風後找到一具金屬模型。上面有兩條水道，一條開、一條關。小模型人能沿著道路移動，模擬不同問題：雨量增加怎麼辦，主水道斷了怎麼辦，兩閘同時打開又如何？沈雨把一個模型人往後移，看著水位跟著改變。「這不是某一天的地圖，」他說，「這是在教人做決定。」宋長老笑了：「沒錯。守護者不只要有力量，也得願意為其他人思考。」

A noise came from upstairs. Qi led Yu toward the stairs and found a woman with a fever lying on a bench. She had walked from a mountain village to request medicine but lost her way. Mei had left a medical pack at the tower, and Song asked the boys to fetch it. Qi took the pack while Yu brought clean water. They checked the woman's condition and contacted a health worker in the next town. “A person who is used to fighting may forget how to wait,” Qi said later. “I nearly tried to carry her down the hill alone.” Yu answered, “You listened instead.”

樓上傳來聲音。韓祈帶沈雨上樓，看見一名發燒的女子躺在長椅上。她從山村走來求藥，途中迷路。梅老師曾在塔裡留下醫療包，宋長老請兩人去拿。韓祈取來包，沈雨端水。他們確認女子的情況，聯絡鄰鎮的醫護人員。「習慣戰鬥的人可能忘了等待，」韓祈後來說，「我差點想自己背她下山。」沈雨回答：「但你選擇先聽別人的。」

While they waited, Song told them why the old guardians had worked in pairs. One listened for pressure in the stone; the other sensed changes in the open air. The two gifts were different, like swimming and running. “Neither is better in every situation,” he said. “When one path fails, the other can guide you.” Qi turned his wrist so the black mark faced Yu's white palm. For the first time, he did not try to hide it. “These marks do not make us special,” he said. “Maybe they just show what we must learn to do together.”

等待時，宋長老告訴他們，古代守護者為何總是兩人一組。一人聽石中的壓力，另一人感應空氣的變化。兩種天賦不同，就像游泳與跑步。「沒有哪一種在所有情況下都更好，」他說，「當一條路失效，另一條就能帶你前進。」韓祈把手腕轉向沈雨的白色掌印。他第一次沒有試著藏起印記。「印記不會讓我們高人一等，」他說，「它也許只是提醒我們該一起學會的事。」

The woman upstairs was an adult, but pain had made her as helpless as a child. Qi placed the medical pack in her lap so she could show Song what she had already taken. The elder explained that fever was a typical sign of many illnesses and that only trained health workers should decide on treatment. Their aid was to keep her safe and comfortable while help traveled up the mountain.

樓上的女子雖是成年人，疼痛卻讓她像孩子一樣無助。韓祈把醫療包放在她腿上，讓她向宋長老指出自己已用過什麼。長老解釋，發燒是許多疾病的典型徵兆，只有受訓醫護人員能決定治療。他們能給的援助，是在醫護上山前確保她安全、舒服。

Before leaving the tower room, Yu noticed a tear in the old cloth covering the wall. Behind it lay a narrow line of writing: THE GATE IS NOT A THRONE. THE WATER BELONGS TO ALL. Beneath the words was an empty space where a third name had once been carved. The stone was newer there, and someone had worked hard to hide the mark. Yu copied the shapes into his notebook. Then he saw a shadow cross the entrance. A stranger in a dark coat stood outside, watching the boys with a silver key in his hand.

離開塔室前，沈雨發現蓋牆的舊布裂了一道口。後面藏著一排字：「閘門不是王座，水屬於所有人。」字下方原本刻著第三個名字，卻留下空白。那一塊石頭比較新，像有人費力遮掉刻痕。沈雨把形狀抄進筆記本。這時一道影子掠過入口。一名深衣陌生人站在外面，手裡拿著銀色鑰匙，正看著兩人。
