石頭山路

The Stone Road

雙河，同心 · 第 7 / 25 章 · 1000–2000 · 英中對照 · 校訂完成

用互動版查詞、朗讀與收藏

The academy calendar showed seventeen days before the full moon. Teacher Mei offered to introduce Yu and Qi to Elder Song, who maintained the old mountain paths. Their journey would take them through a rocky valley and a dry area the locals called the Little Desert. Qi wanted to advance quickly, but Mei warned him that progress did not always mean speed. “A living being has limits,” she said. “Your ideal is not to reach the tower first. It is to reach it safely with everyone.” Yu packed water, a map, a toothbrush, and three old books. Qi packed almost nothing.

學院行事曆顯示距離滿月還有十七天。梅老師提議帶沈雨與韓祈認識守護山中古道的宋長老。旅程會穿過岩石山谷和當地人叫「小沙漠」的乾地。韓祈想快速前進，梅老師卻提醒進步不等於速度。「每個生命都有極限，」她說，「你的理想不是第一個抵達塔，而是讓大家都安全抵達。」沈雨帶了水、地圖、牙刷和三本舊書，韓祈卻幾乎什麼都沒裝。

Elder Song greeted them at dawn wearing an old coat and a humble smile. “For this first period of training,” he said, “you will practice one principle: take one step only after the ground tells you it is ready.” The students began to jog on a wide path, then slowed near a narrow rock bridge. Yu placed his hand against the wall and listened. Deep below, water knocked against stone in a steady pattern. He counted eight beats, followed by a silence. Qi heard only the eight. “Thus the bell is not in the air,” Yu said. “It is in the stone.”

黎明時，宋長老穿著舊外套，以謙和的笑容迎接他們。「第一段訓練只有一個原則，」他說，「要等地面告訴你可以前進，才踏下一步。」學生們先沿著寬路慢跑，在狹窄的石橋前放慢。沈雨把手按上石壁傾聽。深處的水有節奏地拍打石頭：八下，接著安靜。韓祈只聽得到八下。「所以鐘不在空中，」沈雨說，「在石頭裡。」

They stopped at an abandoned guard house. A dusty mop stood in a corner, and a turtle slept beside an empty bowl. Elder Song laughed when Yu offered the animal water. “It has lived here longer than I have,” he said. “The climate was once wet, but after the gates closed, the valley grew dry.” The turtle woke and moved toward a crack in the floor. Yu followed it with his eyes. “It knows where water used to run.” Song nodded. “Old creatures remember what maps forget. But memory alone cannot tell us whether a path is still safe.”

三人停在一座廢棄守衛屋。一支沾滿灰塵的拖把靠在角落，一隻烏龜睡在空碗旁。沈雨拿水給牠，宋長老笑了。「牠住在這裡比我還久。」他說，「從前氣候潮濕，但水閘關閉後山谷就乾了。」烏龜醒來，爬向地板裂縫。沈雨的目光跟著牠。「牠知道水以前走哪裡。」宋長老點頭：「老生物記得地圖遺忘的事。但光靠記憶，無法判斷那條路現在是否安全。」

Inside the guard house was a board with marks showing the river levels for many years. It was meant to contain information, not magic. Yu copied the marks into his book, while Qi checked the dates against the calendar. The lower lines changed after the year of the flood, but the upper lines did not. “The water did not vanish,” Yu said. “It moved somewhere else.” Song studied him for a moment. “That is the right question.” He did not give them the answer. Instead, he asked them to make a plan for observing the next part of the valley.

守衛屋裡有塊木板，記著多年來的河水高度。那不是法器，只是用來保存資料。沈雨抄下記號，韓祈把日期和行事曆比對。大水那年之後，下方的線改變了，上方卻沒有。「水不是消失，」沈雨說，「它流去了別的地方。」宋長老看了他一會兒。「問對問題了。」他沒有直接回答，而是要兩人計畫如何觀察接下來的山谷。

The road became steep, and a cold wind pushed from the northern rocks. Qi offered to carry Yu's heavy pack, but Yu refused. “I need to learn my own strength.” Qi walked beside him anyway. For most of the afternoon they spoke of ordinary things: favorite meals, village games, and the mistakes they had made at school. Qi admitted that he had never learned to relax. “At home, every victory was treated as something I owed my family,” he said. “There was no time to be happy.” Yu answered, “Then we can learn that too.”

道路變陡，冷風吹過北方岩石。韓祈想替沈雨背沉重的行李，沈雨拒絕了。「我得知道自己的力量。」韓祈仍放慢腳步陪他。下午大半時間，他們談普通的小事：喜歡的食物、村子裡的遊戲、在學校犯過的錯。韓祈承認自己不會放鬆。「在家裡，每場勝利都被當成我欠家族的事，」他說，「沒時間高興。」沈雨回答：「那我們也能一起學。」

At the last rise of the road, Yu stopped to stretch his aching legs. He had expected cultivation to mean sitting still beside a quiet lake, not walking for hours with a heavy bag. Elder Song told him that listening to his tired body was also a lesson. The air grew colder as they crossed the final ridge, and Qi slowed his pace without being asked.

爬上道路最後一段坡時，沈雨停下伸展痠痛的腿。他原以為修仙是靜坐湖旁，不是背著重包走好幾小時。宋長老說，傾聽疲累的身體也是一課。跨過最後一道山脊時，空氣更冷了，韓祈不用人說就放慢腳步。

That evening, Yu saw something bright between two rocks. It was a metal plate carrying the same half-circle design as the copper machine part found at the river port. Elder Song looked troubled. “This plate should have been kept at the north tower,” he said. Qi turned it over. On the back was a date from twenty years earlier, and below it the names of two students. One name was Shen. The other was Han. Yu and Qi stood close in the dying light. Their families had crossed paths long before they were born. Neither boy knew what to say first.

傍晚，沈雨看見兩塊岩石間有東西發亮。那是一塊金屬牌，刻著與河港銅盒內那件機械零件相同的半圓圖案。宋長老面露憂色。「它本該存放在北塔。」他說。韓祈把牌翻過來。背面有二十年前的日期，下面寫著兩個學生的姓，一個是沈，一個是韓。暮色中，兩人並肩站著。他們的家族早在他們出生前就有過交集。誰也不知道該先說什麼。
