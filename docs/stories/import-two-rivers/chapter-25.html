雙河，同心

Two Rivers, One Heart

雙河，同心 · 第 25 / 25 章 · 1000–2000 · 英中對照 · 校訂完成

用互動版查詞、朗讀與收藏

Three months later, fresh green growth covered the valley. The old court had completed the first public hearing, and the full case against Luo would continue under the law. He was no longer allowed to control the gates. The principal of Cloud Gate Academy stood among farmers, workers, and students at a ceremony beside the Quiet River. She read a record of the repairs and explained the data collected since the flood. The two rivers now flowed steadily, and clean water had reached farms long absent from the old maps. The judgment about the people responsible would take time, but the safety work was already real.

三個月後，山谷又披上了新綠。舊法院已完成第一次公開聽證，對羅的完整案件仍將依法進行；他不再能控制水閘。雲門學院校長站在農夫、工人和學生之間，於靜河旁主持典禮。她朗讀修復紀錄，說明洪水後蒐集的資料。兩條河穩定流動，乾淨水已抵達多年不在舊地圖上的農田。責任人的判決還需要時間，但安全工程已是眼前的事實。

A small market filled the riverbank. Sellers offered pumpkin cakes, seafood soup, and soft rice buns. Musicians played a light pop melody while children tossed coins into a box to support the new water school. Yu had brought a book for the young students, showing how to read river levels and apply simple safety rules. A little girl asked whether the two guardians had used a magic bell to open the gate. “No,” Yu said with a laugh. “We used a repaired wheel, a good plan, and many people working together.” Qi added, “And we listened when the experts told us to stop.”

河岸擺起小市集。攤販賣南瓜糕、海鮮湯和柔軟米包。樂師演奏輕快的流行旋律，孩子們把硬幣投進盒裡，支持新成立的水學校。沈雨帶來一本給小學生看的書，教他們讀河水高度、運用簡單安全規則。一名女孩問兩位守護者是不是用魔鐘打開閘門。「沒有。」沈雨笑道，「我們用修好的輪盤、好計畫，還有許多人一起工作。」韓祈補充：「而且專家叫停時，我們就停。」

The academy had also changed. Students could now choose among different paths of training, and the Water Hall welcomed anyone willing to learn. Yu no longer felt foolish for taking more time than others. He had learned that freedom included the right to ask questions and the duty to hear the answers. Qi no longer believed that strength meant living without help. He had written to his father, saying he would not accept a marriage arranged for family power. The reply had been cold, but Qi had kept his own judgment. He had chosen a life he could respect.

學院也改變了。學生如今能在多條修行道路中選擇，水堂歡迎任何願意學的人。沈雨不再因自己比別人花更多時間而覺得愚蠢。他學到自由也包括提問的權利，以及聆聽答案的責任。韓祈不再相信強者必須不受幫助地生活。他寫信給父親，表明不接受為家族權勢安排的婚事。回信很冷淡，但韓祈維持自己的判斷。他選了自己能尊重的人生。

After the ceremony, Yu and Qi followed the old river path past the restored gate. The shallow water caught the light, and tiny waves washed the stones. A boy was learning to surf on a safe board at a public pool nearby; his friends cheered every time he fell and tried again. Qi stopped to watch. “You would be good at that,” Yu said. “Perhaps,” Qi replied. “But I would rather learn something with you.” Yu looked at him. He could now figure out the meaning behind Qi's quiet words. “Then stay,” he said. “Not because of a mark or a promise. Because you want to.”

典禮後，沈雨和韓祈沿著修好的閘門旁走舊河路。淺水映著光，細浪沖洗石頭。附近公共水池裡，一個男孩在安全的板上學衝浪，朋友每次都替跌倒又站起的他歡呼。韓祈停下觀看。「你應該很擅長。」沈雨說。「也許，」韓祈回答，「但我更想跟你一起學些東西。」沈雨看向他，如今已能明白韓祈安靜話語下的意思。「那就留下，」他說，「不是為印記或承諾，只因為你想。」

Qi took Yu's hand. The marks on their skin were almost gone, but the warmth remained. “I want to,” he said. “For the ordinary days, not just the dangerous ones.” Yu felt his face grow warm. He had prepared speeches for courts and councils, but no words seemed right now. Instead, he stepped closer. They walked along the river with their hands joined, while farmers called greetings from the fields. The water did not need them to stand guard every hour. It had a proper flow, public care, and room to move. That was enough for a new beginning.

韓祈握住沈雨的手。皮膚上的印記幾乎不見了，溫度卻還在。「我想，」他說，「我想要的不是只有危險時刻，也包括平凡日子。」沈雨臉熱起來。他曾替法院和議會準備演講，如今卻一句合適的話也找不到。於是他只向前靠近。兩人牽手沿河走，農夫從田裡向他們問好。河不需要他們每小時守著，它有正確的水路、公開管理和流動的空間。這足夠成為新開始。

At the last bridge, Yu opened his notebook one final time. He wrote the story of the hidden bell, the stolen plate, the stone dust, the council's decision, and the two rivers returning home. He did not write that they had saved the world. That would have been a foolish claim. He wrote instead that people had listened to one another before it was too late. Qi read the page and smiled. Far away, the restored bell rang once to mark the hour. It was a clear, ordinary sound. Neither boy was afraid. They kept walking, with a future both of them had chosen.

走到最後一座橋時，沈雨再度打開筆記本。他寫下隱藏的鐘聲、失竊河盤、石粉、議會決定，以及兩條河如何回家。他沒有寫自己和韓祈拯救了世界，那樣太誇大也太傻。他寫的是人們如何在太遲之前聽見彼此。韓祈讀完，露出微笑。遠處修復的鐘響了一聲，報出時間。那是清楚、平凡的聲音。兩個少年都不再害怕。他們繼續往前，走向兩人自己選擇的未來。
