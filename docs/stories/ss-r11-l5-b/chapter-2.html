The next morning the calf made a low call toward the red ridge, beyond which the older cloud beasts had last been seen. Ye believed the journey could be beneficial if they traveled slowly and let the animal choose its own pace. An offered to convert an abandoned handcart into a resting platform, but a corporate contractor claimed the cart as company property. Its painted number had almost vanished, and no one could find a receipt. Rather than argue, An built a lighter frame from the chapel's fallen branches. Their destination was not the city sanctuary advertised on every sign. It was a high meadow where the herd had gathered each spring. The calf stepped onto the platform only after Ye spread fresh moss across its floor. An noticed that permission mattered to the creature more than comfort did.

隔天早晨，幼獸朝紅色山脊低鳴；長大的雲獸最後一次出現，就在山脊另一側。葉青認為，只要慢慢走並讓幼獸自己決定步調，這段旅程可能有益。安知提議把廢棄手推車改成休息平臺，但企業承包商宣稱車子屬於公司。車上的漆字幾乎全褪了色，卻沒人找得到收據。安知沒有爭執，而是利用禮拜堂掉落的樹枝另做一個較輕的架子。他們的目的地不是告示牌上到處宣傳的城市保護所，而是每年春天獸群聚集的高山草甸。葉青在平臺上鋪好新鮮苔草後，幼獸才自行踏上去。安知發現，對牠而言，選擇權比舒適更重要。

At the village gate a healer warned them about an epidemic of coughing among the pack animals. She gave Ye an errand: carry clean cloth to the ridge shelter, where travelers had run out of supplies. Her young assistant called the calf extraordinary and begged to come along. Ye explained that excitement could frighten a sick creature as easily as cruelty. The route required them to haul the platform over loose stones, and every sharp turn made its wooden wheels complain. The contractor warned that if they damaged the public path, the village would face legal liability. An quietly recorded the cart's weight and the width of its wheels. He had learned from old mistakes that a good intention did not cancel the responsibility to protect everyone who used the same road.

村口一名醫者警告他們，馱獸之間正流行咳嗽疾病。她交給葉青一件差事：把乾淨布料送到物資耗盡的山脊小屋。年輕助手說幼獸非同尋常，央求同行。葉青解釋，過分興奮和殘忍一樣，都可能驚嚇病中的動物。他們必須拖著平臺越過鬆動石塊，每次急轉彎都讓木輪發出呻吟。承包商警告他們，若損壞公共道路，村莊就得負起法律責任。安知默默記下車子的重量與輪子寬度。他從過去的錯誤學到，出於善意也不能免除保護共同道路使用者的責任。

Near the summit a slope suddenly gave way, and a stream of red gravel began to plunge toward the path. An pushed the platform into a hollow while Ye held the calf steady. A reminder from his old apprenticeship came back to An: never trust one reading from an unfamiliar instrument. He questioned a road survey respondent who had seen small cracks there the previous week. Together they examined a cracked stone sensor and discovered that it had been placed on solid rock, far from the sliding soil. The rider's evidence was more useful than the impressive display at the village office. Ye offered the traveler a bowl of warm stew and listened without interrupting. The calf watched from beneath the cart, breathing in rhythm with the slow movement of their hands.

接近山頂時，一處坡面突然鬆脫，紅色碎石朝小路急墜而下。安知把平臺推進凹地，葉青則穩住幼獸。安知想起學徒時的一項提醒：不熟悉的儀器只讀一次，絕不能輕信。他詢問一名道路調查受訪者，對方上週曾看見那裡出現細縫。兩人檢查裂損的石製感測器，發現它被安裝在堅硬岩石上，遠離正在滑動的土壤。旅人的目擊資料，比村公所華麗的展示還有用。葉青請對方喝一碗熱燉湯，安靜聆聽，沒有插嘴。幼獸躲在車底，呼吸逐漸跟上兩人緩慢的手勢。

At twilight, smoke from a distant cooking fire formed a silver line above the ridge. An felt the temptation to take a shortcut along an exposed ledge, but Ye pointed to the dark clouds gathering above it. They lit a torch at the shelter and waited for the wind to settle. The keeper there showed them a drawing of the landscape before the energy towers arrived: a wide ribbon of flowers connected every resting meadow. The transformation had been gradual. First workers trim the grass to make room for poles; then heat from the cables dries the soil; finally the moss stops flowering. The keeper was vocal about his anger, yet he allowed the calf to sleep before asking questions. An realized that restoring a route would require more than moving a fence.

黃昏時，遠處炊煙在山脊上方拉成一條銀線。安知很想走曝露在外的窄崖捷徑，但葉青指著上方聚起的烏雲。他們在小屋點起火把，等待風勢平息。小屋管理人拿出能源高塔興建前的風景圖：一整條寬闊花帶串連各處休息草甸。這場轉變是逐步發生的。工人先修剪草地替電桿騰出空間，接著電纜熱氣使土壤乾燥，最後氣苔不再開花。管理人憤怒地直言不諱，卻仍讓幼獸先睡，再來提問。安知意識到，恢復遷徙路線絕不只是移走一道圍籬。

During the night the calf woke and pressed one foot into the wet soil at the shelter door. A blue spark escaped harmlessly, leaving a round print that gleamed for an instant. Ye showed An the mark and explained why damp ground must remain part of every resting place. An drew a new route, with a note beside each possible meadow stating when the last flowers had opened. He wanted to see the whole herd, but refused to promise they would find it at once. At first light the coughing animals in the shelter were better, and the healer's cloth had arrived in time. The two men set off again with more supplies, one old map, and a young creature that had begun to recognize their footsteps but still listened for its own kind.

夜裡幼獸醒來，把一隻腳按在小屋門前濕潤的土壤上。一道藍色電光無害地逸散，留下短暫發亮的圓形腳印。葉青指給安知看，解釋為何每個休息地都必須保留潮濕地面。安知重新繪製路線，在每片可能的草甸旁記下最後一次開花時間。他想親眼看到整個獸群，卻不肯保證立刻就能找到。天剛亮，小屋裡咳嗽的馱獸已有好轉，醫者託付的布料也及時送達。兩人帶著更多補給、一張舊地圖，以及一隻開始辨認他們腳步、卻仍傾聽同伴呼喚的幼獸再次出發。
