The mayor tried to attribute the first improvement in air quality to the tower company's new filters alone. An spoke on behalf of the volunteer gardeners and explained that the result came from several changes, including protected soil and lower traffic. He carried a compact measuring kit and showed that his own competence mattered less than the ability of many observers to coordinate reliable readings. The largest cloud beast approached the outer meadow just after dawn, accompanied by six smaller animals. It did not descend into a cage or wait for a signal. Ye stood beside An and watched the elder cross the open gate. For the first time in years, a herd entered the city without being led by a human. Several workers began to cheer, but Ye raised a hand and asked for quiet.

市長試圖把空氣品質初步改善完全歸功於高塔公司新裝的過濾器。安知代表志工園丁發言，說明成效來自多項變化，包括保護土壤與減少交通。他帶著小巧的測量工具組，展示自己個人的能力遠不如許多觀察者協調取得可靠讀值來得重要。黎明後不久，最大的雲獸帶著六隻較小動物來到外圍草甸。牠沒有降進籠子，也沒有等待訊號。葉青站在安知旁，看著長輩雲獸穿過敞開大門。多年來第一次，獸群不需人類引導就進入城市。幾名工人開始歡呼，葉青舉手請大家安靜。

A documentary team hoped to capture the herd's debut in the city and called it a moment of destiny. Ye told the director that a living animal's route was not anyone's dramatic property. The park belonged to the public domain, but access still required respect for protected wildlife. The team agreed to make a donation to the community meadow fund without demanding the right to approach the animals. An promised to execute the day's safety plan in the order the volunteers had rehearsed. First, people cleared the path; next, the water stations opened; finally, the observers withdrew to marked positions. The elder cloud beast paused at each stop, apparently checking where the ground remained damp. No one interfered with its decisions.

紀錄片團隊希望拍下獸群首次進城的亮相，稱之為命運時刻。葉青告訴導演，活生生動物的路線不是任何人的戲劇財產。公園屬於公共領域，但進出仍必須尊重受保護野生動物。團隊同意捐款給社區草甸基金，不要求接近動物的權利。安知承諾依照志工排練的順序執行當天安全計畫：先清空道路，再開放供水站，最後讓觀察者退到標示位置。長輩雲獸在每處停歇，似乎確認地面是否仍然潮濕。沒有人干預牠的決定。

One physician said the children watching from the clinic were immune to neither excitement nor disappointment. They had waited through months of repairs, and a failed migration could frighten them. A muscular old farmer volunteered to stand with the spectators, while a former opponent of the conservation project helped direct traffic. The station's output fell briefly when the herd released electrical charge into the wet meadow. An called it an expected fluctuation, then checked the emergency supply rather than scramble to restart every machine. The physician reported that the clinic still had power. Ye heard the calf make the three-note call it had been trying to learn since Reed Chapel. The elder answered. For a moment the small animal stood completely still, then ran across the grass toward its family.

一名醫師說，在診所觀看的孩子並非不會感到興奮或失望。他們等了好幾個月的修復工程，若遷徙失敗，可能讓他們害怕。一名肌肉結實的老農自願陪伴觀眾，一位過去反對保育計畫的人則協助指揮交通。獸群將電能釋入濕草甸時，電站輸出短暫下降。安知說這是預期中的波動，隨即檢查緊急供應，而不是慌忙重啟每一臺機械。醫師回報診所仍有電力。葉青聽見幼獸發出從蘆葦禮拜堂起就努力學習的三音呼喚。長輩回應了。小動物有一瞬間完全不動，然後越過草地奔向自己的家族。

The movement was spectacular without being arranged for any audience. The elder spread wide membranes that caught the rising air, and the calves followed with uneven leaps. A cleaner stood holding a wet sponge beside the walkway, waiting to wipe any fallen dirt from the smooth tile used by wheelchair visitors. A food vendor had placed hot soup away from the animals' path so no one would have to cross the meadow to buy it. Ye noticed these small preparations even while he watched the herd. The most successful parts of the day were almost invisible: nobody had to shout, nobody blocked an exit, and the animals could leave without being touched. An glanced at Ye, who was smiling openly for the first time since their journey began.

這場移動壯觀得令人屏息，卻不是為任何觀眾安排。長輩雲獸展開寬闊薄膜，接住上升氣流，幼獸們則跳得高低不齊地跟上。一名清潔工拿著濕海綿站在步道旁，準備擦掉供輪椅使用者通行的平滑地磚上落下的泥土。食物攤販把熱湯放在遠離動物路線的地方，讓買東西的人不必穿越草甸。葉青看著獸群時，也注意到這些小準備。當天最成功的部分幾乎看不見：沒人需要喊叫，沒人堵住出口，動物也能在不受碰觸的情況下離開。安知看向葉青，發現他自旅程開始以來第一次毫不掩飾地笑了。

The herd did not fly away immediately. It rested near the city garden until evening and then rose a short distance toward the northern hills. Ye resisted the urge to follow at once; the young calf was finally among its own kind, and it did not need a human shadow at every step. An walked with him to the observation tower, where they could see the animals without disturbing them. He said the day had not proved the whole route safe, only that one part could work under known conditions. Ye agreed and asked for tomorrow's weather forecast. A storm was forming beyond the mountains. The men returned to the office to check the next section of their map, knowing the hardest crossing was still ahead.

獸群沒有立刻飛走。牠們在城市花園附近休息至傍晚，然後短暫飛向北方山丘。葉青忍住立即跟隨的衝動；幼獸終於回到同伴之間，不需要人類在每一步都如影隨形。安知陪他走上觀察塔，在不打擾動物的距離觀看。他說，這天並未證明整條路線安全，只證明其中一段在已知條件下可行。葉青同意，並問明天的天氣預報。山另一側正形成風暴。兩人回到辦公室檢查地圖下一段，知道最困難的跨越仍在前面。
