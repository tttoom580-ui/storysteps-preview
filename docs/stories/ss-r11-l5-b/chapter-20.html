不肯等待的風暴

The Storm That Would Not Wait

雲獸不肯飛：兩個凡人的春天 · 第 20 / 25 章 · 4000–5000 · 英中對照 · 校訂中

The approaching storm created a complication nobody could solve by moving the migration date. The city's chief constraint was electricity: a sudden shutdown could be deadly for patients using life-support devices. An prepared an elaborate sequence for reducing tower heat without cutting the hospital supply. At the same time the mountain clouds threatened to erupt into violent electrical winds. Ye watched the herd gather along the grassland edge and picked up a fragment of broken fence that might injure a running calf. He knew he could not command the animals to wait for human preparations. Instead, he asked An to ensure there were several open paths, each leading to wet ground. Their work had become a race against weather, not a contest to control a living herd.

逼近的風暴帶來一項麻煩，無法只靠改變遷徙日期解決。城市最主要的限制是電力：突然停電可能讓依賴維生設備的病人遭遇致命危險。安知準備一套詳細的分階段程序，在不切斷醫院供電的情況下降低高塔熱量。同時，山間雲層隨時可能爆出猛烈的帶電強風。葉青看著獸群聚集在草原邊緣，撿起可能割傷奔跑幼獸的一段破圍欄。他知道無法命令動物等待人類準備。於是他請安知確保有幾條敞開路線，每條都通向濕地。他們的工作變成和天氣賽跑，而不是爭奪對活著獸群的控制權。

A graphic weather report showed how the storm might cross the valley within hours. Its interpretation was uncertain because the temperature was changing faster than usual. The emergency council authorized a limited intervention: engineers could switch off two empty observation towers while leaving hospitals connected to a separate source. The plan was not mainstream among the company's older managers, but the independent technicians said it could qualify for an emergency trial. An reviewed the wiring twice and explained the risks aloud. He refused to call it a perfect answer; if the wind shifted, they would have to stop the test. Ye stood with the field volunteers, making sure they knew which routes were safe for people and which had to remain open for animals.

圖像化天氣報告顯示，風暴可能在幾小時內穿過山谷。但因氣溫變化快於平常，解讀仍有不確定性。緊急議會核准有限度的介入：工程師可關閉兩座空置觀測高塔，同時讓醫院維持由另一電源供電。老一輩公司經理並不支持這種非主流方案，但獨立技師說它符合緊急試行條件。安知再次檢查配線，並大聲說明風險。他拒絕稱這是完美答案；若風向改變，就必須停止試驗。葉青站在田野志工旁，確認他們知道人員安全通道在哪裡，以及哪些路必須留給動物使用。

The first lightning strikes produced unusual radiation readings in one sensor, although the source had not been confirmed. A technician compared the ratio of measurements from two stations and found that the alarming display came from a damaged wire. The council's financial officer worried that lower power production would reduce revenue for the day. A visiting senator, unable to command the local team, offered to provide emergency funds if the need was documented. An felt the old urge to tempt everyone with a dramatic solution, but Ye asked him to describe only what the instruments could prove. The council accepted that answer and kept the emergency plan limited. Outside, the wind lifted dust from the road and bent the young trees toward the ground.

第一波閃電使一處感測器出現異常輻射讀數，雖然來源尚未證實。技師比較兩個站的測量比率，發現嚇人的畫面其實來自受損電線。議會財務官擔心發電量下降會減少當日收入。一名來訪參議員沒有權力指揮地方團隊，但願在需求有文件證明時提供緊急經費。安知再次想用戲劇性解法誘惑眾人，葉青卻請他只描述儀器能證明的事。議會接受這種回答，維持有限應急方案。屋外，風吹起道路塵土，將小樹壓得向地面彎曲。

As the storm began to unfold, the tower team faced unprecedented pressure. A damaged cable created a dangerous vacuum in one cooling chamber, drawing hot air toward the old animal pens. An ordered the chamber sealed and asked workers to leave by the marked exits. An apprentice called him a warrior for keeping his voice steady, but An said bravery meant remembering the people who might be hurt by a mistake. Ye checked the pens and found them empty, as the new policy required. He opened the outer gates anyway, ensuring no frightened animal would be trapped if the herd approached. By the time the wind reached its strongest point, the hospital lights remained on and the meadow paths were still clear. The plan had held for its first difficult hour.

風暴逐步展開，高塔團隊面臨前所未有的壓力。受損電纜讓一處冷卻室形成危險的真空，將熱空氣吸向舊動物欄舍。安知命令封閉冷卻室，請工人沿標示出口撤離。一名學徒稱讚他聲音穩定得像戰士，但安知說勇敢是記得誰可能因錯誤而受傷。葉青檢查欄舍，依新政策裡面都沒有動物。他仍打開外門，確保若獸群靠近，沒有受驚的動物會被困住。風勢達最強時，醫院仍亮著燈，草甸通道也維持暢通。計畫撐過了艱難的第一個小時。

Near midnight a cloud beast made a deep call from the north, and the herd began to move away from the towers. Ye stood under the shelter roof and watched the youngest animals follow older ones across the wet grass. Not all of them could fly in the strong wind, so several continued on foot until they reached the lower hillside. An joined him after the electrical team confirmed that the hospital was safe. Neither man called the storm a victory. They had been fortunate, and the next wave could be stronger. Yet the carefully prepared exits, the separate power source, and the maintained moss beds had each mattered. Ye rested his shoulder briefly against An's. Their task was not finished, but they were no longer carrying it alone.

接近午夜，北方傳來一隻雲獸低沉的呼喚，獸群開始遠離高塔。葉青站在遮棚下，看最小的動物跟隨長輩穿越濕草。強風中不是每隻都飛得起來，有些一路步行到較低的山坡。電力團隊確認醫院安全後，安知來到他身旁。兩人都沒有把風暴稱為勝利。他們只是幸運，下一波可能更強。然而事先備好的出口、獨立電源，以及持續維護的苔草床，確實都發揮了作用。葉青短暫把肩膀靠向安知。任務尚未結束，但再也不是兩人孤獨承擔。
