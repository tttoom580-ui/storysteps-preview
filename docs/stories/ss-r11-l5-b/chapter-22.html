呼吸之橋

The Bridge of Breath

The regional committee asked An to assess the corridor before the herd reached its final mountain crossing. A commission of engineers and animal keepers traveled with him, while Ye tried to conceive a way to protect the narrowest resting meadow from heavy traffic. The old bridge there had begun to decline, and a wooden coffin carried past years before had been the last large load it supported safely. Local families still remembered that funeral, not because of tragedy alone but because the bridge had brought the whole village together. The commission studied the timber carefully and proposed a lightweight route beside the original structure. No one wanted to risk another crossing before the supports had been tested. The work would be slow, and the herd might arrive first.

區域委員會請安知在獸群抵達最後山口前評估整條走廊。一組工程師與動物照護員組成的委員會同行，葉青則試圖想出保護最狹窄休息草甸、免受重車影響的方法。那裡的老橋已逐漸衰敗，數年前通過的一具木棺是它最後安全承受的大型載重。當地家庭還記得那場葬禮，不只因為悲傷，也因那座橋曾把全村聚在一起。委員們仔細研究木材，提議在舊橋旁另建輕量通道。支撐結構測試前，誰也不想冒險再過橋。工作將很慢，獸群可能先到。

A village artist offered to envision the crossing as a garden rather than an engineering obstacle. The local judicial officer reminded the committee that the bridge had protected status, so any change required public notice. Reaching an agreed design would be a milestone for the village, and An asked residents not to mock those who worried about the cost. The soil around the bridge was thin, with little nutrient matter and a high level of fine particle dust. Ye worked with farmers to plant roots that could hold the bank together. He did not promise that new flowers would bloom before the herd arrived. Instead, they placed removable mats of healthy moss along the temporary route, leaving the growing plants undisturbed. The bridge might remain unfinished, but a safe resting place could still exist nearby.

一名村莊藝術家提出把渡口想像成花園，而非工程障礙。當地司法官提醒委員會，老橋屬於受保護設施，所以任何修改都要公開通知。達成共同設計將是村子的一座里程碑，安知請居民不要嘲笑擔心成本的人。橋邊土壤很薄，養分不足，細微粉塵含量高。葉青與農民合作種植能穩定河岸的根系植物。他沒有承諾獸群到來前新花會開，而是在臨時路線鋪上可移動的健康氣苔墊，讓正在生長的植物不受打擾。橋也許無法立刻完工，附近仍可能先有安全休息地。

The oldest bridge keeper had retired on a small pension, but he came to the meeting because he knew where the supporting beams had once been repaired. The village hoped the new plan would improve productivity, allowing people to transport vegetables more easily without harming the meadow. Ye said the difference between a road and a home for another species was profound: the first existed for passage, while the second required time and care. An's younger sibling arrived from the wind school to help with the measurements. She had learned to work solo but asked to join a team because the crossing had too many unknowns for one person. An welcomed her without pretending that their past disagreements were settled. They shared tools first, then spoke during the evening meal.

最年長的守橋人靠微薄退休金生活，仍來參加會議，因為他知道當年支撐梁修補的位置。村莊希望新計畫提升生產效率，讓人們更容易運送蔬菜，卻不傷草甸。葉青說，道路與另一物種的家有深刻差別：前者為通行而存在，後者卻需要時間與照料。安知的妹妹從風學校前來協助測量。她已學會獨立工作，卻主動要求加入團隊，因為這道渡口的未知太多，不適合一個人處理。安知歡迎她，沒有假裝過去的分歧已解決。他們先共享工具，晚飯時才慢慢交談。

Some workers held a stereotype that cultivators could simply fly over any broken bridge. Ye explained that most people in the valley had no such ability, and cloud beasts themselves could not always rise in strong winds. The subsequent meeting approved a crossing designed for walkers, wheelchairs, and resting animals, with a separate route for heavy loads. When a gust threatened to knock over the measuring stand, An had to thrust one hand against its base. He laughed afterward, embarrassed by how ordinary the danger had been. The commission agreed on a treaty between the two neighboring villages: each would maintain its half of the shared path, and any closure would require advance warning. There would be no private gate at either end.

有些工人抱持刻板印象，以為修行者能直接飛越任何壞橋。葉青解釋，山谷裡多數人沒有那種能力，就連雲獸在強風中也未必飛得起來。隨後會議通過一條供步行者、輪椅與休息動物使用的通道，另設重型載運路線。一陣風差點吹倒測量架，安知只好伸手用力頂住底座。他事後笑了，為這樣平凡的危險有些不好意思。委員會同意兩個鄰村之間的協約：各自維護共享道路的一半，任何封閉都必須事先通知。道路兩端都不會設私人門鎖。

The temporary crossing opened just before sunset, when the first band of cloud appeared above the south ridge. The herd did not rush across. An watched from the bridge, counting the time each animal spent investigating the new ground. Ye stood on the far bank where fresh moss had been laid, making no call that might confuse the elders. One by one the cloud beasts moved over the stones and onto the meadow, while villagers waited beside the path in silence. The old bridge remained standing, and the new one could be removed after the migration. An's sister took a final measurement and smiled. They had not conquered the landscape; they had made one crossing possible without demanding that every traveler be the same.

臨時渡口在日落前開放，當時第一道雲帶已出現在南方山脊上。獸群沒有急著跨越。安知站在橋上，計時每隻動物檢查新地面的時間。葉青立於對岸新鋪氣苔處，不發出可能混淆長輩的叫聲。雲獸一隻接一隻走過石塊，進入草甸；村民在路旁安靜等候。老橋仍然矗立，新橋則可在遷徙結束後拆除。安知的妹妹做完最後一次測量，露出笑容。他們並未征服地景，只是讓一處渡口變得可行，而不要求所有旅者都一模一樣。
