草原不需要英雄

No Heroes on the Grass

雲獸不肯飛：兩個凡人的春天 · 第 6 / 25 章 · 4000–5000 · 英中對照 · 校訂中

In the highland settlement the community meeting had only one agenda: how to protect the remaining grazing ground before the dry season. A sign warned visitors to beware of sudden electrical storms, yet children still liked to cling to the fence and watch the blue sparks dance above the cloud beasts. The highlands belonged to a federal alliance of small provinces, each with a different rule about animal movement. Ye asked the local keeper which rule mattered most at night. She pointed to the open gates and said that nothing in the valley could be truly safe if the animals could not walk away. An thought about the cages he had helped build and quietly loosened the strap on the calf's platform. The young creature stepped into the grass by itself.

高地聚落的社區會議只有一項議程：乾季到來前，如何保護剩下的牧草地。告示警告訪客提防突發電暴，孩子們卻仍愛攀住圍欄，看藍色火花在雲獸頭頂跳動。高地屬於數個小省組成的聯邦聯盟，各省對動物移動有不同規定。葉青問當地照護員夜晚最重要的規則是什麼。她指著敞開的門，說只要動物不能自由走開，山谷裡便談不上真正安全。安知想起自己參與建造的籠舍，默默鬆開幼獸平臺上的皮帶。幼獸自己踏進了草地。

That evening a traveling storyteller performed a familiar genre of heroic ballads. One song compared a famous swordsman's victory to the final inning of a baseball game, when a single hit changes everything. Ye admired the music but disliked the metaphor. A migration could not be saved by one glorious action, he said, because grass must grow again after the crowd leaves. The municipal librarian agreed and showed him a collection of ordinary farmers' records. Their names rarely appeared in songs, but their work had kept the valley alive through repeated hard summers. An listened while the storyteller changed the final verse, replacing a lone warrior with a group of people carrying water together. The audience laughed at first, then began to sing along.

傍晚，一名巡迴說書人表演常見的英雄歌謠。其中一首把著名劍客的勝利比作棒球比賽最後一局，彷彿一記安打就能改變一切。葉青欣賞音樂，卻不喜歡這個比喻。他說遷徙無法靠一次光榮行動拯救，因為群眾散去後，草仍須重新長起來。市立圖書館員表示贊同，拿出一批平凡農人的紀錄。他們的名字很少寫進歌裡，但多年艱苦夏季中，正是這些勞動維繫山谷生命。安知聽著說書人修改最後一段歌詞，把孤獨戰士換成一群共同提水的人。觀眾起初笑了，後來也跟著唱。

At the next assembly the council considered three candidates for a meadow committee. One nominee wanted fences tall enough to keep every animal under supervision. Another proposed a complete ban on visitors. The third asked what kind of organism the flowering moss was and whether people understood its life cycle well enough to care for it. Before the voting ended, a herd of ordinary goats began to overtake the path outside, scattering the crowd with their bells. An moved the calf to a quiet corner and helped the keeper clear a passage. He noticed that the goats' small act of rebellion against the fence had not endangered anyone once the gate was opened. The council selected the third candidate and funded a study of moss instead of another barrier.

隔天的集會中，議會考慮三位草甸委員會候選人。一位被提名人想建造足夠高的圍欄，把所有動物都置於監督之下；另一位提議完全禁止訪客；第三位則詢問開花氣苔究竟是什麼生物，以及人們是否足夠了解其生命週期，能夠妥善照料。表決還沒結束，一群普通山羊便追過外面的道路，鈴鐺響個不停，擠散了人群。安知把幼獸移到安靜角落，協助照護員清出通道。他注意到，山羊對圍欄的小小反抗，在門打開後並沒有傷害任何人。議會最後選了第三人，並資助氣苔研究，而非再建一堵障礙。

After lunch the keeper showed them a harmless skeleton from an old natural history collection. Its ribs curved around a hollow chamber where cloud beasts stored air, and the bones were lighter than wood. An recognized a rigid metal shield fitted over the same part of the body in the city's transport cages. The shield had been meant to stop sparks from touching workers, but it also prevented the animal from expanding its chest when frightened. The keeper said the device had been rejected by several veterinarians, although copies were still sold across the provinces. An sketched a safer frame that could open when an animal pushed against it. Ye reminded him that a good design was only useful if people stopped forcing the beasts inside. An folded the sketch and kept it as a question rather than a solution.

午飯後，照護員讓他們看一具自然史收藏中的無害骨骼標本。雲獸的肋骨圍住儲存空氣的中空腔室，骨頭比木材還輕。安知認出城市運輸籠曾在相同身體部位裝上堅硬金屬護盾。護盾原本用來阻止火花碰到工人，卻也讓受驚動物無法擴張胸腔。照護員說，幾名獸醫早已反對這種裝置，但各省仍有人販售複製品。安知畫出一種當動物向外推時便能打開的安全框架。葉青提醒他，即使設計再好，只要人們仍強迫雲獸進籠，就沒有用處。安知把草圖折好，將它當成一個問題保留，而不是立刻稱作答案。

That night a small child began to sob when thunder shook the windows. Her grandmother comforted her with a story from the valley's theology: clouds were once people who had learned to share the sky. Ye respected the story, but he carefully explained that the animals were not human beings and should not be asked to carry human guilt. Outside, a pool of water had turned toxic after careless visitors poured cleaning liquid into the stream. The keeper used a little vinegar to remove residue from a wooden basin, then carried fresh water from an uncontaminated spring. An helped without making speeches about redemption. At dawn the child came to see the calf again. This time she did not reach through the fence. She sat on the ground and waited for the animal to approach her.

夜裡，雷聲震動窗戶，一名孩子開始啜泣。祖母用山谷神學傳說安慰她：雲朵曾是學會分享天空的人。葉青尊重故事，卻仔細解釋雲獸並非人類，不該被要求承擔人的罪疚。屋外，一池水因粗心訪客倒入清潔液而變得有毒。照護員用少許醋去除木盆上殘留的污漬，再從未受污染的泉水提來新水。安知一起幫忙，沒有發表任何自我救贖的演說。天亮時，那孩子再來看幼獸。這次她沒有把手伸進圍欄，而是坐在地上，等動物自己走近。
