風球的重量

The Weight of a Wind Sphere

A sudden storm brought an acute problem to the reservoir station: the cloud calf had begun to store more electricity than damp earth could safely release. The station administrator worried that the visitors would frighten residents already anxious about their drinking water. An admitted that his past involvement with city towers might create bias in any recommendation he made. A technician suggested they test the animal beside a large wind sphere used to measure air pressure. Ye insisted that the calf remain free to move, and the station agreed to build an open platform rather than a cage. The technician explained each measurement to local volunteers before touching the equipment. An felt a familiar desire to solve the problem quickly, but the animal's calm breathing reminded him that speed was not the only form of competence.

突如其來的風暴為水庫站帶來急迫問題：幼獸體內累積的電能已超過潮濕地面能安全釋放的程度。站長擔心訪客讓原本就焦慮飲水安全的居民更害怕。安知承認，自己曾參與城市高塔設計，可能使任何建議帶有偏見。一名技師提議在測量氣壓的大型風球旁測試動物。葉青堅持幼獸必須能自由移動，站方因此同意搭建開放平臺，而非籠子。技師在碰觸設備前，向當地志工解釋每項測量。安知感到熟悉的衝動，想迅速解決問題，但幼獸平穩的呼吸提醒他，速度不是能力的唯一形式。

The city broadcast a warning that the animal might be contagious, although the physician had found no disease. A former defendant in an unrelated environmental case approached Ye and offered to help explain how such rumors could spread. She had worked on a documentary about the dimensions of the mountain wind system. The crew showed a film in which engineers erect towers without checking how sound traveled between the cliffs. During a question period An had to flip the diagram upside down to show why the air moved differently at night. The audience laughed, then paid closer attention as he explained how gravity and temperature shaped the flow. The film was useful not because every conclusion was perfect, but because it gave villagers enough information to challenge the official account.

城市廣播警告動物可能帶有傳染性，雖然醫師並未發現疾病。一位曾在無關的環境案件中擔任被告的女子來找葉青，願意協助解釋這類謠言如何傳播。她曾參與一部關於山間風系統各種尺度的紀錄片。影片顯示工程師豎立高塔時，沒有查驗聲音如何在懸崖之間傳播。提問時，安知不得不把示意圖倒過來，才能說明夜間氣流為何不同。觀眾笑了，隨後更仔細聽他說明重力與溫度如何塑造流動。影片有用，不是因為每項結論都完美，而是讓村民擁有足夠資訊質疑官方說法。

A new piece of legislation required outside observers whenever energy equipment was tested near protected wildlife. The station invited a teacher and a farmer to oversee the experiment. The company asked the regional office to overturn that requirement for one afternoon, arguing that no time could be wasted. Ye rejected the premise that consultation necessarily meant delay. While the engineers prepared the reservoir walkway, local students collected olive branches to mark a safe area for viewers. The branches were visible but could be removed without damaging the soil. The calf investigated them and walked past without alarm. When the test began, An held a measuring rod while Ye watched the animal's feet. The first reading was too high, so they stopped rather than hiding the result.

一項新立法要求能源設備在受保護野生動物附近測試時，必須有外部觀察員。站方邀請教師與農民監督實驗。公司要求區公所為了這一下午推翻規定，理由是不能浪費時間。葉青拒絕接受『諮詢必然造成延誤』的前提。工程師準備水庫步道時，當地學生蒐集橄欖枝，標示觀眾安全區。枝條清楚可見，也能在不傷土壤的情況下移走。幼獸走近探看，然後安然走過。測試開始，安知拿著測量桿，葉青則注視幼獸的腳。第一次讀值過高，所以他們停下，而不是藏起結果。

The second test used a hollow copper sphere that allowed the calf to release a small charge into the moist soil without touching a fence. A visiting therapist asked why anyone had assumed fear would make the animal more obedient. She said therapy for frightened people began with safety rather than obedience. Ye answered that fear often made behavior less predictable, not more. The station's engineer calculated the toll that repeated electrical shocks had taken on older cage walls, then showed An a broken metal panel. He called the structure weird because it bent inward rather than outward. An recognized the pattern immediately. The original design had directed the force toward the animal instead of the ground. He could have blamed a distant supervisor, but he told Ye that he had helped approve that design years before. Ye listened and asked what An intended to change now.

第二次測試使用中空銅球，讓幼獸不碰圍欄，也能把少量電荷釋入濕土。一名來訪治療師問，為何有人會以為恐懼能使動物更服從。她說，受驚者的治療應從安全感開始，而不是服從。葉青回答，恐懼通常使行為更難預測，而不是更聽話。站方工程師計算了反覆電擊對老舊籠壁造成的損耗，接著向安知展示一塊破損金屬板。他說結構很古怪，因為它往內彎，而不是往外。安知立刻認出這個形狀。原始設計把力量導向動物，而不是土地。他大可把責任推給遙遠的主管，卻告訴葉青，多年前自己也曾核准這設計。葉青聆聽後問他，如今打算改變什麼。

An did not answer with a speech. He asked the engineer for permission to examine the remaining cages and compare their drawings with the newer open platform. Ye stayed behind to watch the calf, whose charge had fallen to a safer level. At sunset the two men walked beside the reservoir and spoke about the difference between admitting a mistake and repairing its effects. Ye said trust would grow only through repeated choices, not one dramatic confession. An agreed, though he feared what the coming days would demand of him. In the distance the sky darkened where the city towers stood. The herd was still traveling beyond them, and the calf had begun to recognize a deep call in the clouds. Their next task would bring them closer to the very machines An most wanted to avoid.

安知沒有用演說回答。他向工程師請求檢查剩下籠舍的許可，想把舊圖與新的開放平臺比較。葉青留下照顧幼獸，牠的電荷已降至較安全程度。日落時，兩人沿水庫散步，談論承認錯誤和修復影響之間的不同。葉青說，信任只能藉由反覆的選擇慢慢建立，不是一場戲劇性告白就能換來。安知同意，卻害怕接下來幾天自己必須面對的事。遠處，高塔矗立的城市上方天色變暗。獸群仍在高塔另一側移動，幼獸開始辨認雲層裡低沉的呼聲。他們下一項任務，將帶兩人靠近安知最想迴避的機械。
