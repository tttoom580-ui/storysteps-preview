沒有勝者的競技場

An Arena Without a Winner

雲獸不肯飛：兩個凡人的春天 · 第 12 / 25 章 · 4000–5000 · 英中對照 · 校訂中

The next city had built an arena for cloud-beast racing, although the animals had not entered it willingly for years. A proposed ban on the races had attracted a celebrity athlete who hoped to rebuild his reputation by supporting animal care. He wore a crystal pendant shaped like a flying beast and smiled for every camera. Ye worried that the event would depress the calf after its long journey, but the athlete's assistant invited him to speak without displaying any animal. The stadium manager had advertised an encounter with a real cloud beast. Ye demanded that the misleading promise be removed before he entered. An told him he would stand beside him but would not take over the speech. The decision belonged to the keeper who knew the animal best.

下一座城市建了一座雲獸競速競技場，雖然多年來動物都不願自發進場。一項禁賽提案吸引一名名人運動員參與，他希望藉支持動物照護挽回名聲。他戴著雲獸飛行造型的水晶墜飾，對每臺攝影機微笑。葉青擔心長途跋涉的幼獸會因活動而沮喪，但運動員助理邀他發言，不必展示任何動物。場館經理早已宣傳可與真正雲獸近距離接觸。葉青要求先撤掉這項誤導承諾，才願意入場。安知說自己會站在旁邊，卻不代替他演說。這項決定屬於最了解幼獸的照護員。

The manager denied any intent to mislead and claimed a lawsuit against the campaign would ruin the local economy. Ye avoided accusing him of fraud before checking the advertisements. Instead, he explained the calf's nutrition needs: live moss, clean water, and the freedom to graze on damp soil. When the audience heard that cloud beasts could not simply be fed grain from a bag, several parents asked about the animals they had seen in private collections. An held up two signs, one reading 'animal' and the other 'animals,' to show that the plural did not mean a single keeper could speak for an entire species. The athlete looked uncomfortable but remained on stage. He began to understand that public attention could create obligations he had never considered.

經理否認自己有誤導意圖，並聲稱若活動遭到訴訟，地方經濟將受到摧毀。葉青沒有在核對廣告前就指責他詐欺，而是解釋幼獸的營養需求：活氣苔、乾淨水，以及在潮濕土地上吃草的自由。觀眾聽到雲獸不能只靠袋裝穀物餵養後，幾位家長開始詢問私人收藏裡的動物。安知舉起兩張標示，一張寫單數的『動物』，另一張寫複數的『動物們』，說明即使談的是整個物種，也不是一位照護員就能代所有個體發言。運動員顯得不自在，卻仍留在臺上。他開始明白，公共關注會帶來自己從未想過的義務。

A curious boy tried to poke the calf through a gap in the temporary screen, and a steward gently guided his hand away. Near the seats An found a portfolio of architectural sketches beside a medical prescription someone had dropped. He placed both at the lost-property desk without examining the private pages. The manager ordered staff to move a tall rack of racing trophies so the audience could see the stage. During the rush a worker made a random shove that almost tipped it onto the walkway. An caught the rack with two others and asked the manager to slow the event down. The residential neighborhood outside the stadium had already complained about noise; making people hurry inside would not improve safety. The athlete canceled the planned demonstration race.

一名好奇男孩試圖從臨時屏風縫隙戳幼獸，服務人員輕輕引導他的手移開。安知在座位旁發現一夾建築草圖作品集，和一張有人掉落的醫療處方。他沒有翻看私人頁面，便把兩者交給失物招領處。經理命令員工移動高大的賽事獎盃架，好讓觀眾看見舞臺。匆忙中，一名工人隨意推了一把，架子差點倒向走道。安知和另外兩人扶住架子，請經理放慢活動節奏。場館外的住宅區早已抱怨噪音，讓裡面的人更匆忙並不會提升安全。運動員取消了原定示範賽。

During a public session the organizers considered whether the stadium could reverse its old purpose. A volunteer proposed turning the arena into a seasonal market for plants and repairs, with quiet open space for animals that chose to pass nearby. The manager resisted because the ultimate financial result remained uncertain. An suggested a limited pilot rather than a permanent promise. He drew a plan showing removable stalls and clear exits, and the athlete offered to fund the first year without demanding naming rights. Ye said the plan would succeed only if people stopped treating access to the cloud beasts as a reward for buying tickets. He invited the town's young gardeners to speak. Their questions shifted attention from spectacle to the unglamorous work of soil, water, and shade.

公開討論時，主辦方考慮場館能否扭轉昔日用途。一名志工提議把競技場改成季節性的植物與修繕市集，並保留安靜開放空間，讓自願經過的動物使用。經理反對，因為最終財務結果仍不確定。安知提議先有限試辦，而不是永久承諾。他畫出可拆攤位和清楚出口的計畫，運動員願意資助第一年，而且不要求冠名權。葉青說，只有大家不再把接近雲獸當成買票獎賞，方案才可能成功。他邀鎮上的年輕園丁發言。園丁們的問題使焦點從華麗演出轉向土壤、水與遮蔭等不耀眼的工作。

When the hearing ended the stadium doors stood open on both sides. The calf walked through the empty corridor and paused at a patch of afternoon sunlight. No audience was allowed to follow. Ye watched from a doorway while An put away the signs. The athlete approached and asked whether one day he might learn to care for an animal instead of competing with it. Ye told him that care began with hours no camera would record. The man nodded and offered to help prepare the first flower beds. By evening the stadium staff had removed the dangerous trophy rack and posted corrected announcements. Nothing had been settled forever; even a changed building could return to old habits. Yet for one afternoon the arena had been useful without declaring anyone a winner.

聽證結束時，競技場兩側的門都敞開著。幼獸穿過空蕩走廊，停在一片午後陽光裡。沒有觀眾獲准跟隨。葉青從門邊觀看，安知則收拾標示牌。運動員走近，問自己能否有一天學會照顧動物，而不是和牠競爭。葉青告訴他，照護從鏡頭不會記錄的漫長時光開始。男人點頭，願意幫忙準備第一批花床。傍晚前，場館人員已移除危險的獎盃架，張貼更正公告。沒有任何事一勞永逸；改變用途的建物也可能重蹈舊習。然而至少那個下午，競技場不必宣布勝利者，也能發揮作用。
