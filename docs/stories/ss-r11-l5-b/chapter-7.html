廢鐵果園

The Scrap Orchard

The seventh stop was a ruined orchard on land the provincial railway had recently marked for acquisition. Workers had been promised a bonus if they cleared the old structures before the cloud-beast migration began. A gardener welcomed Ye and An through her backyard and handed them a lump of chalk, a piece of calcium-rich stone, and a chunk of broken rail. She wanted the men to understand what was beneath their feet: the orchard stood on a former colonial railway yard, and its waste had slowly mixed with the soil. The moss could grow along the edges, but not in the center, where oil still stained the ground. Ye thanked her for allowing them to test a small patch rather than assuming the whole property could be used as a resting place.

第七站是一片荒廢果園，省鐵路局最近將其列為徵收用地。工人獲得承諾，只要在雲獸遷徙前清除舊建物，就能領取獎金。一名園丁從後院迎葉青與安知進來，交給他們一塊粉筆、一塊富含鈣的石頭，以及一大塊斷鐵軌。她希望兩人了解腳下的土地：果園原本是殖民時代的鐵道場站，廢料長年混入土壤。氣苔可以長在邊緣，卻無法在中央生長，那裡的土仍有油污。葉青感謝她准許在小塊土地試種，而不是理所當然地認定整片果園都適合讓雲獸休息。

A visiting science teacher said he could comprehend the public fears, but he disapproved of moving the railway without a full study. An agreed that the implication of changing a transit line went beyond the interests of one animal. The teacher pushed a narrow probe into the ground to see whether roots could penetrate the deeper layer. A local republican councilor accused the project of being risky and wasteful. An responded by offering a simple trial: they would plant flowers in removable beds along the existing fence, leaving the railway untouched. If the beds failed, the community could stop without losing a road. Ye watched the calf drink from a clean bowl while the adults negotiated. It was learning to stand near human voices without mistaking them for commands.

來訪的科學教師說他能理解居民的擔憂，但不贊成未經完整研究就遷移鐵道。安知同意，改變交通路線的影響不只牽涉一隻動物。教師把細長探針推進地面，檢查植物根部能否穿透較深的土層。一名共和派地方議員指責計畫風險過高、浪費資源。安知提出簡單試驗作為回應：先沿既有圍欄，在可移動的花床種植植物，不碰鐵道。如果花床失敗，社區可以停止，不必失去道路。成年人協商時，葉青看著幼獸從乾淨的碗喝水。牠正學會站在人聲附近，卻不把那些聲音誤認為命令。

One volunteer was a college sophomore named Lan, who had grown up beside the orchard. She cleared a spacious work area and showed the group how to lift heavy pots without hurting their spines. The team stacked boards for the flower beds, then separated useful wood from scrap metal that could be recycled. Lan asked whether it was fair for students to do work that paid contractors had been hired to complete. Ye said unpaid help should never replace promised wages. The gardener agreed to cover the materials but insisted that each worker receive food and travel costs. For An, the conversation brought back his early days as an apprentice, when he had accepted dangerous jobs because he thought gratitude required silence. He promised the group a written record of every expense.

志工中有一位名叫蘭的大學二年級學生，從小住在果園旁。她清出一塊寬敞工作區，教大家如何搬起重花盆而不傷到脊椎。團隊把木板堆好做花床，並將可用木材和可回收廢金屬分開。蘭問，學生做了原本承包商領薪應完成的工作，是否公平。葉青說，無償協助絕不應取代本來承諾的工資。園丁同意負擔材料，但堅持每位工作者都應獲得餐食與交通費。這段對話讓安知想起當學徒的日子；那時他接受危險工作，只因以為感恩就必須沉默。他答應為團隊留下每筆開支的書面紀錄。

At dusk a small surveillance drone appeared above the fence, recording everyone who entered the orchard. The railway agency described the device as symbolic protection against theft, but the gardener objected to being filmed in her own yard. Lan asked for the camera to face the transit line rather than private homes. The operator agreed after seeing a clear diagram of the field of view. An was surprised by how quickly a technical dispute became manageable when the people affected were allowed to speak. The group finished its first bed and placed a bowl of water at each end. The calf inspected both bowls, then chose a quiet corner where the flowers had not yet opened. Ye left it there and walked back toward the house for the evening meal.

黃昏時，一架小型監視無人機出現在圍欄上方，記錄每一位進出果園的人。鐵路機構稱這裝置是防盜的象徵性保障，但園丁反對自己家院子被拍攝。蘭要求攝影機朝向鐵道，而非私人住宅。操作員看過清楚的視角圖後同意調整。安知驚訝地發現，只要受影響的人能開口，技術爭議往往很快就能處理。團隊完成第一座花床，在兩端各放一碗水。幼獸仔細查看兩個碗，最後選了一處尚未開花的安靜角落。葉青讓牠待著，自己走回屋裡吃晚飯。

Before leaving the orchard, An drew a line connecting its two healthy beds with the meadow they had protected earlier. The distance was still too great for the calf to travel in one evening, but there were three possible stopping points between them. Ye did not call the drawing a completed corridor. He called it a question the next village would have to answer. Lan volunteered to visit that village with samples of healthy moss, while the gardener promised to monitor the new beds through the summer. No one expected every seed to survive. When the cloud calf stepped onto the old path again, it turned back once, as though it recognized the effort left behind. An found that he was no longer ashamed to be proud of work that might need changing tomorrow.

離開果園前，安知在地圖上畫出一道線，把兩處健康花床連到先前保住的草甸。距離仍太遠，幼獸無法在一個晚上走完，但中間有三個可能的停歇點。葉青沒有把圖畫稱為已完成的走廊，而說這是下一個村子必須回答的問題。蘭自願帶健康苔草樣本走訪下一村，園丁答應整個夏天監測新花床。沒人期待每顆種子都能存活。幼獸再次踏上舊路時，回頭望了一眼，彷彿認得留在身後的努力。安知發現，自己不再為那種明天可能需要修正的工作感到自豪而羞愧。
