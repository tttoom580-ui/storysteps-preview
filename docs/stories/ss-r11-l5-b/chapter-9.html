The Border Without a Fence

The border guard rode a patient gray ass instead of a horse, and his hired bodyguard walked beside him carrying a bright umbrella. The boundary was marked by stones, not wire, but the new rules were confusing. Travel documents comprised three separate forms, and an official could discriminate against applicants who lacked a fixed address. Ye argued that migrating animals should be eligible for passage whether they belonged to a wealthy collector or an ordinary village. An asked the guard to show them the written rule. The guard was courteous but uncertain. He had never handled an animal that was neither livestock nor someone's private property. Ye waited while the man spoke to his supervisor, resisting the urge to turn a difficult encounter into a personal argument.

邊境警衛騎著一頭溫馴灰驢，而不是馬；他雇用的保鑣拿著鮮亮的雨傘步行在旁。邊界由石塊而非鐵絲網標示，但新規定令人困惑。旅行文件由三份表格組成，官員可能歧視沒有固定地址的申請者。葉青主張，無論雲獸屬於富有收藏家或普通村落，都應有資格通行。安知請警衛拿出成文規定。警衛態度禮貌，卻不太確定；他從未處理過既非家畜也不屬於任何私人的動物。葉青等他聯絡主管，克制自己不把困難的接觸變成人身爭執。

The guard's supervisor agreed that the exemption was valid, but asked how the travelers would supply water along the dry border road. An described a system in which fluid collected from morning fog could be stored in clay jars. The town could incorporate the device into its public wells without changing ownership of the land. A local investigator arrived after a farmer accused the visitors of carrying stolen equipment. The accusation was mistaken, and the farmer apologized when An showed the receipts. The investigator was passionate about protecting local resources but admitted that strong feelings were not evidence. She walked with Ye to inspect a damaged patch of moss, then asked whether border villages could share the cost of repair rather than sending more police to watch it.

警衛的主管同意豁免有效，卻問旅客將如何沿乾燥的邊境道路補水。安知描述一種把晨霧凝結的液體收進陶罐的裝置。小鎮能將它納入公共水井，不必改變土地所有權。一名農民指控訪客攜帶偷來的設備後，當地調查員趕到。這項指控是誤會；安知拿出收據，農民便道歉。調查員熱心保護當地資源，卻也承認強烈情感不能當證據。她陪葉青查看受損苔地，接著問邊境村落能否共同分擔修復費用，而非派更多警察看守。

A child offered Ye a handmade patch for the calf's traveling blanket. The patch was shaped like a small pyramid, and the child said she had learned the pattern at school. Her family had recently lost its residence after the road widened, and she now lived in a crowded shelter near the border. One sandal had a broken strap, which An repaired while they talked. The child tried to give him a souvenir in return, but he said a conversation was enough. Her mother was a therapist who worked with displaced families. She explained how sudden noise could frighten children who had been moved too often, and Ye recognized a similar response in the calf. He did not claim that people and animals suffered in the same way; he simply listened more closely to both.

一名孩子送給葉青一塊手製布貼，想縫在幼獸的旅行毯上。布貼是小金字塔形，她說圖案是在學校學的。道路拓寬後，她家失去了住處，如今住在邊境附近擁擠的收容所。孩子的一隻涼鞋帶斷了，安知一邊聊天一邊替她修好。孩子想拿一份紀念品回贈，安知說這段談話就夠了。她的母親是協助被迫遷居家庭的治療師。她解釋，頻繁搬家使孩子容易被突如其來的聲響嚇到；葉青在幼獸身上也看見類似反應。他沒有聲稱人與動物以相同方式受苦，只是更仔細地傾聽兩者。

At the registration desk An noticed that an old version of the crossing rule still appeared on the public noticeboard. The newer version was less restrictive, but the clerk had not received an updated copy. She apologized and offered to undo a rejected application without charging another fee. Ye said the correction mattered particularly to vulnerable travelers who could not afford to return another day. The guard agreed to keep both versions visible until the regional office confirmed which one was current. Nobody accused the clerk of plotting against the migrants; she had been working from incomplete information. An remembered how quickly mistakes had grown under the secret procedures of his former employer. This small, public correction felt like a different way of working.

登記處裡，安知注意到公告欄仍貼著舊版通行規定。新版限制較少，但書記尚未收到更新本。她道歉，並願意撤銷先前的駁回決定，不再收第二次費用。葉青說，這項更正對無法負擔另跑一趟的弱勢旅客尤其重要。警衛同意，在區公所確認現行版本前，先把新舊規定並列。沒有人指責書記陰謀對付遷徙者；她只是依不完整資訊辦事。安知想起過去雇主的祕密程序如何讓小錯誤迅速擴大。這種公開而微小的修正，讓他感覺看見另一種工作方式。

The gate opened near sunset. Beyond it, clouds cast long shadows over a narrow strip of green land. The calf moved slowly, pausing to sniff each new plant before walking on. Ye followed at a distance that allowed the animal to turn back if it wished. An carried the repaired sandal and a note from the child's mother, who hoped the next village might have space for families displaced by the road. He agreed to deliver the note himself, although no one had asked him to become a messenger. At the campfire Ye told him that kindness did not need to be turned into a permanent obligation. An smiled and set the note beside his map. They had crossed a political border; the harder boundaries between fear, duty, and choice remained ahead.

日落前，大門終於打開。門外，雲影拖過狹長綠地。幼獸走得緩慢，每遇新植物就停下來聞一聞。葉青保持距離跟隨，好讓牠想回頭時可以回頭。安知帶著修好的涼鞋，以及孩子母親寫的一張便條；她希望下一村能收容因道路工程而失去家園的家庭。安知答應親自交付，雖然沒有人要求他當信使。營火旁，葉青告訴他，善意不必變成永久義務。安知笑著把便條放到地圖旁。他們穿過了政治邊界，恐懼、責任與選擇之間更難跨越的界線，仍在前方。
