大教堂的拍賣

The Cathedral Sale

The city's energy council proposed to abolish the old migration corridor and replace it with a covered road. An hoped the proposal could be amended, but Ye said the animals themselves had no vote in that process. At the public hall An tried to articulate why a protected meadow mattered to everyone, not just the cloud beasts. Across the street an auction was taking place inside a former cathedral, where wealthy buyers bid on land near the route. The city had promised to use the proceeds for public improvements. Ye asked what counted as an improvement if it made the next drought harder to survive. Several buyers paused when he explained that drying the moss would also loosen the soil around their properties. For the first time, the sale's future profits looked less certain.

城市能源議會提議廢除舊遷徙走廊，改建有頂道路。安知希望能修正提案，但葉青說，動物本身在這程序裡沒有投票權。公開會堂上，安知試著清楚表達保護草甸為何攸關所有人，而不只是雲獸。街對面，一座舊大教堂正舉行拍賣，富有買家競標路線附近的土地。市府承諾將收益用於公共建設。葉青問，若一項建設讓下一次乾旱更難生存，究竟算不算改善。當他解釋乾枯苔地也會讓買家土地周邊土壤鬆動時，幾位買家停下出價。拍賣原本看似確定的未來獲利，第一次顯得不那麼穩固。

A private security team was hired to combat protest, and a compassionate doctor asked the officers to leave an open route for people needing medical care. The company insisted its equipment met all standards of compliance. An replied that compliance could not excuse a design that continued to deceive people about its effects. He showed heat readings taken near the cages and asked the company to account for the emission of warm air into the valley. The company's engineer grew defensive, but an enthusiastic student offered to help repeat the measurements publicly. The city agreed to grant a supervised test before completing the land sale. Ye accepted because an open test could protect both the residents' safety and the calf's route. The auction was postponed until the measurements could be reviewed.

私人保全隊受雇對抗抗議活動，一名富有同情心的醫師請警員為需要醫療照護的人留出通道。公司堅稱設備完全符合規範。安知回答，符合規範並不能成為繼續欺瞞人們其實際影響的理由。他展示籠舍附近的熱量讀數，要求公司說明排放到山谷的熱氣。公司工程師變得防衛，但一名熱心學生願意協助公開重測。市府同意在土地出售前先進行受監督的測試。葉青接受，因為公開測試可以同時保護居民安全與幼獸路線。拍賣因此延期，等待測量結果審查。

A juvenile cloud beast appeared unexpectedly at the edge of the city park, following the same low call as Ye's calf. Its arrival changed the legislative debate more than any speech had. Several spectators rushed forward, but Ye made them stop on the porch of the park office. A hungry hawk circled above like a predator searching for an easy meal, though it did not attack the larger animal. An saw that the new arrival wore an old collar made from metal and cloth. He feared a grim prospect: the beast might have escaped a private collection. The animal's skull carried a narrow scar where the collar had rubbed. Ye kept a safe distance and waited until it approached the wet grass. Neither man tried to catch it. Their first duty was to let it rest.

一隻少年雲獸突然出現在城市公園邊緣，追隨著與幼獸相同的低沉呼聲。牠的出現對立法辯論造成的影響，比任何演說都大。幾名圍觀者衝上前，葉青請他們停在公園辦公室的門廊。一隻飢餓的鷹在上空盤旋，像掠食者尋找容易到手的獵物，但沒有攻擊較大的雲獸。安知看見新來的雲獸戴著舊的金屬布料項圈。他害怕一種不好的可能：這隻雲獸或許逃離私人收藏。牠頭骨附近有項圈磨出的狹長傷疤。葉青保持安全距離，等牠自己走近濕草。兩人都沒有試圖捕捉牠。首要任務是讓牠休息。

The park manager offered a small storage building to hold supplies, but Ye refused to turn it into an animal enclosure. An inspected the floor and found space for clean water, food, and medical equipment without blocking the exits. A costume vendor from the postponed auction brought a discarded wig to use as soft padding, then laughed when Ye explained that ordinary cloth would be easier to clean. The vendor donated the cloth instead. The newly arrived animal drank, then answered the calf's call with a note so low that An felt it through the ground. His old training told him to record the frequency, but he left the instrument alone. For one hour the two creatures moved together through the meadow, learning the distance between them without human direction.

公園經理提供一棟小倉庫存放補給，但葉青拒絕把它改成動物圍籠。安知檢查地板，找出擺放清水、食物及醫療器材，又不阻擋出口的空間。延期拍賣會的一名戲服商送來丟棄的假髮，想拿來當柔軟墊料；葉青解釋普通布料較容易清洗，對方笑了，改捐布料。新來的雲獸喝了水，接著回應幼獸，聲音低得安知能透過地面感覺到。過去的訓練催促他記錄頻率，他卻暫時放下儀器。一個小時裡，兩隻動物在草甸間一同移動，沒有人類指揮，自己摸索彼此距離。

When the city council met again, it agreed to reserve the park as a temporary resting ground while the larger corridor proposal underwent review. The decision disappointed investors who wanted the land sold quickly, but several local businesses offered to care for the meadow. Ye was not ready to call the park safe for every animal; its water had to be tested and its gates had to remain open. An volunteered to train workers in maintaining the instruments without depending on his presence. At sunset the young cloud beast stood beside the calf, and the two called toward the sky together. An heard an answer from far above the city. The larger herd had not forgotten this place, even though the place had nearly forgotten it.

城市議會再次開會後，同意在大型走廊提案審查期間，先將公園保留為臨時休息地。這決定讓想迅速賣地的投資者失望，但幾家在地商店願意照料草甸。葉青尚未準備稱公園對所有動物都安全；水仍需檢驗，大門也必須保持開放。安知自願訓練工人維護儀器，不讓日常工作依賴自己在場。夕陽時分，少年雲獸站在幼獸旁，兩者一起朝天空呼喚。安知聽見城市高空傳來回應。龐大的獸群沒有忘記此地，雖然此地幾乎忘了牠們。
