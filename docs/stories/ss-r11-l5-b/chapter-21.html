靜燈嘉年華

The Carnival of Quiet Lamps

The town held a small carnival after the storm, not to celebrate controlling the herd but to thank the people who had kept the paths open. Ye felt affection for the volunteers who had returned without being asked. A regional alliance of villages offered to help maintain the corridor, provided no single city could close it. The council agreed to authorize a shared maintenance group, whose first task was to restore the barren strip beside the damaged tower. Some residents wanted a grand monument to the animals, but Ye asked them to think about living grass instead. An suggested a garden with low lamps that did not imitate the herd's calls. For once the city could honor a journey without interrupting it, and the celebration left room for quiet.

風暴後，小鎮舉辦一場小小的嘉年華，不是慶祝掌控獸群，而是感謝讓通道保持開放的人。葉青對那些不待邀請便再次前來的志工感到親切。一個區域村落聯盟願意協助維護走廊，條件是沒有任何單一城市能將它關閉。議會同意授權成立共同維護小組，第一項任務是修復受損高塔旁貧瘠的土地。有些居民想為雲獸立巨大紀念碑，葉青卻請他們先想想活著的草。安知建議做一座有低矮燈光、不模仿獸群呼聲的花園。城市終於能在不打斷旅程的情況下致敬，慶典也為寧靜留下空間。

A group of gardeners held a symbolic burial for the old metal fences, then sent the pieces to a recycling project, where harmful paint would be removed before reuse. The town warned visitors that consumption of electricity might rise during the festival, so the lights would run only for two hours. A former convict who now worked as an electrician asked to donate his labor. The organizer agreed on the condition that he receive the same safety equipment and respect as every other worker. A child finished a piece of mustard bread and carried a clay jug of water to the new moss beds and asked whether the flowers would live forever. Ye said no living thing was eternal, and that was one reason care mattered. The child watered the soil gently, without trying to make the plants grow before their time.

園丁為舊金屬圍欄舉行一場象徵性的葬禮，再將材料送往回收計畫，仔細除去有害油漆後重新利用。小鎮提醒訪客，慶典期間電力消耗可能上升，所以燈只點兩小時。一名已服完刑、如今擔任電工的前受刑人想捐出勞力。主辦者同意，條件是他必須和其他工作者一樣獲得安全裝備與尊重。一名孩子吃完一片芥末麵包，提著陶製水壺走向新苔草床，問花會不會永遠活著。葉青說，沒有生命是永恆的，這也是照顧之所以重要的原因。孩子輕柔澆水，不急著逼植物提前長大。

The electrical team demonstrated the new safety mechanism by opening a model roof and directing a harmless charge into damp sand. The equipment was operational, but An insisted that the trial had tested only a limited range of weather conditions. An official wanted to proclaim the danger solved forever. Ye declined to stand behind such a statement. He had seen propaganda turn careful work into an excuse for careless expansion. Instead, a small maintenance squad presented the measurements and explained when the system should be shut down. Visitors were disappointed by the modest report, but several asked questions about keeping their own homes safe. An thanked them and said a reliable system should invite questions long after the festival ended.

電力團隊透過模型展示新安全機制：打開屋頂，將無害電荷導入濕沙。設備已能運作，但安知堅持，試驗只涵蓋有限的天候條件。一名官員想宣布危險已永久解決。葉青拒絕替這種說法背書。他見過宣傳如何把謹慎工作變成粗心擴張的藉口。維修小隊改為公布測量結果，說明什麼時候應關閉系統。訪客對這份樸實報告有點失望，卻有人開始詢問如何保障自家安全。安知道謝，並說可靠的系統應在慶典結束很久之後，仍歡迎人們提問。

The evening test measured the stability of the power system while the garden lamps were lit. A wireless transmission carried readings from three stations to the public square, where everyone could see the numbers. One display showed a vague warning because a cable had loosened. An shut that channel down until a technician repaired it, rather than tell the audience the warning meant nothing. Ye walked among the new flowers with the calf's old traveling blanket folded under his arm. The young animal had rejoined the herd and no longer needed the blanket, but the children who had sewn its patches wanted to keep it as a memory. Ye promised to bring it to the community workshop after it had been washed and repaired.

晚間測試在花園燈光亮起時測量電力系統的穩定性。無線傳輸把三個站的讀數送到廣場，人人都能看見數字。一個螢幕因電線鬆脫而出現模糊警示。安知先關閉那個通道，直到技師修好，沒有向觀眾宣稱警示毫無意義。葉青抱著幼獸的舊旅行毯，在新花朵間行走。幼獸已回到獸群，不再需要毯子，但曾縫製布貼的孩子想留下當紀念。葉青答應清洗修補後，將它送到社區工作坊。

After the last lamp went dark, Ye and An sat beside the meadow and listened to the distant calls from the hills. They could no longer distinguish their former calf from the other young animals. Ye admitted that he felt a little lonely; for weeks he had been responsible for every mouthful it ate and every step it took. An asked whether loneliness made him wish to bring it back. Ye shook his head. Wanting to be needed, he said, was not the same as being needed. An reached for his hand, then waited until Ye turned his palm upward. The city continued its ordinary work around them. Above the roofs the cloud beasts traveled freely toward the north, and the two men let the sound fade without following it.

最後一盞燈熄滅後，葉青與安知坐在草甸旁，聽遠山傳來的呼喚。他們已無法從其他幼獸中辨認原本照料的那一隻。葉青承認自己有點寂寞；過去幾週，他負責牠的每一口食物與每一步路。安知問，寂寞是否讓他想把牠帶回來。葉青搖頭。他說，想被需要，不等於真的被需要。安知伸出手，先等葉青翻過掌心，才握住。城市在兩人四周繼續平常的工作。屋頂上方，雲獸自由向北方移動；兩人讓聲音漸漸消失，沒有追去。
