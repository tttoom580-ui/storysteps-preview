人人都能走的路

A Path Everyone Can Use

雲獸不肯飛：兩個凡人的春天 · 第 15 / 25 章 · 4000–5000 · 英中對照 · 校訂中

A city engineer inspected the park and declared the main entrance accessible to most visitors. A wheelchair user pointed out that 'most' still excluded people who could not climb the final steps. An invited her to help design a better route, while Ye asked a local seed collector how to protect fragile plants during construction. A comedian performing nearby offered to make a fundraising show, but he agreed not to joke about the injured animals. The group prepared a natural soil compound that could stabilize the new walkway without sealing the ground beneath it. Ye liked the conception of a route that supported people and grazing creatures at the same time. He insisted that the design remain flexible, because neither a human body nor an animal's needs would stay unchanged forever.

城市工程師檢查公園後，宣稱主要入口對多數訪客而言都無障礙。一名輪椅使用者指出，『多數』仍排除了無法爬上最後幾階的人。安知邀她共同設計更好的路線，葉青則請教當地種子採集員，施工時如何保護脆弱植物。附近表演的喜劇演員提出募款演出，也同意不拿受傷動物開玩笑。團隊準備一種天然土壤混合物，可以穩定新步道，卻不封死底下土地。葉青喜歡這種讓人和吃草生物同時受益的構想。他堅持設計保留彈性，因為人的身體與動物需求都不會永遠不變。

The park had begun to decay during years of neglect, but neighbors wanted to foster new growth rather than restore every old feature. A retired gardener said they had inherited responsibility for the trees without receiving money to care for them. An proposed short work intervals so volunteers would not be exhausted by the hot weather. A patrol officer agreed to keep delivery trucks away from the narrow entrance while the route was repaired. Ye noticed that the officer's perception of the animals had changed after she saw two cloud beasts leave a field without damaging anything. She said she had expected the creatures to be stubborn and dangerous. Now she saw that the fences, not the animals, had often caused the loudest disturbances. Ye thanked her for saying so publicly.

公園多年疏於照料，已開始衰敗，但鄰居想培養新的生機，而不是把每個舊設施恢復原狀。退休園丁說大家繼承了照顧樹木的責任，卻沒有獲得維護經費。安知提議採用較短的工作間隔，避免志工在炎熱天氣裡累垮。一名巡邏警員同意施工時不讓送貨卡車進入狹窄入口。葉青注意到，警員見過兩隻雲獸離開草地而沒有破壞任何東西後，對牠們的看法改變了。她原本以為牠們固執又危險，現在卻看見往往是圍欄，而非動物，造成最響亮的騷動。葉青感謝她公開說出這項改變。

The community distributed a questionnaire about the path, asking residents what they needed rather than assuming everyone wanted the same features. An worried that the large number of suggestions would prevent work from resuming. He asked when the team could resume building. Ye helped him group the answers: access, safety, shade, and water. A builder donated a steel rod for the temporary supports, and the town sheriff arranged safe storage until the work began. The plan included a subtle curve to keep walkers away from the most fragile moss. Some volunteers called the final drawing a superb design, but An asked them to underline the parts still untested. He had once been praised for beautiful plans that failed living creatures. This time he wanted the drawing to remain honest about its limits.

社區發出關於步道的問卷，詢問居民真正需要什麼，而不是假定每個人都想要相同設施。安知擔心建議太多，工程無法重新開始。他問團隊何時能恢復施工。葉青協助把回答分為通行、安全、遮蔭與飲水四類。一名建築師捐出鋼棒作臨時支撐，鎮警長安排施工前安全存放。路線設計有一處細微轉彎，讓步行者避開最脆弱的氣苔。有些志工稱最終圖面十分出色，但安知請他們在尚未測試的部分畫上底線。他過去曾因漂亮的計畫受讚賞，卻讓活生生的動物承受失敗。這次他希望圖面誠實呈現限制。

Construction took three days. In a small suite above the park office, volunteers prepared meals and rested between shifts. The new path was not wide enough for large trucks, but it allowed wheelchairs, walkers, and animals to move through the park without crossing the same narrow gate. The seed collector said the flower beds would yield little during the first season, because healthy roots needed time. Ye nodded; growth should not be measured by how quickly it served visitors. On the final evening An tested the ramp himself, carrying a heavy toolbox while the wheelchair user followed at her own pace. She stopped halfway and asked for a resting place with shade. The team added one before declaring the work finished. No one treated the request as an inconvenience.

施工持續三天。公園辦公室樓上的小套房裡，志工準備餐食，也在輪班間休息。新步道不夠寬，無法讓大型卡車進入，但輪椅使用者、步行者與動物都能穿越公園，不必擠在同一個窄門。種子採集員說，第一季花床產量不會多，因為健康根系需要時間。葉青點頭；生長不該以多快服務遊客為衡量標準。最後一晚，安知抱著沉重工具箱親自測試坡道，輪椅使用者則按自己的速度跟在後面。她走到一半停下，請求增設一處有遮蔭的休息點。團隊先加上，才宣布完工。沒有人把這項要求當成麻煩。

That night both cloud beasts answered a sound from the northern sky. The older juvenile took several running steps, then spread the thin membranes along its sides. For a breath it lifted above the flowers before settling safely back down. Ye watched without reaching toward it. An stood beside him and said they had preserved a place where the creatures could decide when to move. Ye took his hand briefly, a quiet gesture offered without an audience or promise. They had saved more than one meadow now, but the long crossing to the city towers remained uncertain. Tomorrow An would have to face the people who still worked at the cages he had helped design. The two men did not pretend that a good path erased an old mistake. They only knew they would walk it together.

那夜，兩隻雲獸都回應了北方天空的叫聲。年紀較大的少年獸向前跑了幾步，展開身側薄膜。牠短暫離地，掠過花朵，然後安全落下。葉青只是觀看，沒有伸手。安知站在身邊，說他們保住了一處能讓動物自己決定何時移動的地方。葉青短暫握住他的手，那是一個沒有觀眾也沒有承諾的安靜動作。他們如今保住的已不只一片草甸，但通往城市高塔的漫長路段仍充滿不確定。明天安知就得面對那些仍在他參與設計的籠舍工作的人。兩人沒有假裝好道路能抹去舊錯，只知道他們會一起走下去。
