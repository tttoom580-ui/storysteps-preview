Two Scales of the Same Map

雲獸不肯飛：兩個凡人的春天 · 第 4 / 25 章 · 4000–5000 · 英中對照

An tried to anticipate trouble before the group entered Saltwind Province, but the local bureaucracy had made even a simple crossing permit difficult. It was a coincidence that the morning commuter train stopped beside their camp, giving Ye a chance to speak with workers who traveled through the valley every day. Several had reported a strange humming near the towers, only to receive letters of denial from the company. An marked each report on his map instead of treating all of them as proof of the same cause. He knew that witnesses might describe a single sound in different ways, especially when they were frightened or tired. Ye watched the calf breathe beside the rails and wondered how many other animals had learned to avoid places that humans called perfectly safe.

安知試著在隊伍進入鹽風省前預料麻煩，然而當地繁瑣的官僚制度連簡單通行證都難辦。巧的是，早晨通勤列車恰好停在他們營地旁，葉青因此能和每天穿越山谷的工人交談。幾人曾通報高塔附近有奇怪的嗡鳴，卻只收到公司否認問題的回函。安知把每項通報標在地圖上，沒有將所有說法當成同一原因的證明。他知道證人對單一聲音的描述可能不同，尤其在害怕或疲倦時。葉青看著鐵軌旁呼吸的幼獸，心想究竟還有多少動物學會避開人類稱為絕對安全的地方。

At the survey office An measured the diameter of a wind tunnel entrance and compared it with the distinctive circular footprints cloud beasts left in wet soil. A university faculty member had published figures claiming the new equipment saved money, but her fiscal report did not include the cost of damaged meadows. Only a fraction of the grasslands appeared on the official map. An laid the old route beside the new one, using identical scales so the change could be seen without an argument. A student pointed out that the old map had been drawn before the latest road was built. An thanked him and marked that limitation clearly. A map, he told the student, was useful because it admitted what it could not show.

在測量辦公室裡，安知量了風洞入口的直徑，並與雲獸留在濕土上的獨特圓形腳印比較。一名大學教師曾發表數據，聲稱新設備節省經費，但她的財政報告並未計入草甸受損成本。官方地圖上只有一小部分草地。安知把新舊路線地圖並排，採用相同比例尺，讓人不必爭論也能看出變化。一名學生指出舊圖繪製於最新道路興建之前。安知道謝，並清楚註明這項限制。他告訴學生，地圖之所以有用，正是因為它能承認自己無法顯示的事物。

The provincial clinic had recorded an unusual hazard: sparks from trapped cloud beasts were disturbing medical equipment during dry weather. One physician explained that stress hormone levels rose in animals kept away from soil. She also studied marine mammals, whose migration patterns changed when noise filled their usual routes. The comparison was imperfect, but it helped Ye explain why the problem involved more than the calf's mood. A local mortality report listed several dead grazing animals after the driest summer, although it did not identify the direct cause. The physician drew two parallel columns, one for verified observations and another for possible explanations. Ye copied the distinction carefully. If he claimed too much, people who needed reliable warnings might stop believing him.

省立診所記錄了一項異常危害：乾燥天氣裡，被困住的雲獸放出的火花會干擾醫療設備。一名醫師解釋，動物被迫遠離土地時，壓力荷爾蒙會升高。她也研究海洋哺乳類，牠們原本的遷徙路線充滿噪音時，也會改變方向。這項類比並不完美，卻幫助葉青說明問題不只關乎幼獸的心情。當地死亡率報告列出最乾燥的夏季後死去的幾隻牧養動物，但並未確認直接死因。醫師畫了兩欄，一欄放已證實的觀察，另一欄放可能的解釋。葉青仔細抄下這種區別。如果他把話說得太滿，真正需要可靠警告的人反而可能不再相信他。

At a public meeting a farmer made a plea for a path wide enough to move his animals before the next storm. An asked an experienced road practitioner to test whether a narrow crossing could support a temporary bridge. The engineer replied that heavy machines would damage the soil, then proposed a removable walkway that workers could carry in pieces. The proposal reassured the farmers because it did not demand that they abandon the road forever. Ye wanted to uncover the reasons the tower company had rejected earlier petitions, but An suggested solving the immediate crossing problem first. They were beginning to disagree without treating disagreement as betrayal. The calf slept between their chairs while the committee planned a small experiment for the following morning.

公開會議中，一名農夫懇求興建足夠寬的通路，好在下一場風暴前帶走牲口。安知請一位經驗豐富的道路專業人員測試，窄口能否承受臨時橋。工程師回答，重型機具會傷害土壤，隨後提議採用可拆卸、能分段搬運的步道。農民因此放心，因為這方案不要求他們永遠放棄道路。葉青想揭開高塔公司先前為何拒絕請願，安知卻建議先解決眼前的過河問題。他們開始能在意見相左時不把分歧視為背叛。委員會規劃隔天早上的小型試驗時，幼獸就在兩人的椅子之間睡著。

By sunset the new walkway was standing over the shallow channel, and a family carrying vegetables crossed it first. An waited for the child at the rear to reach solid ground before allowing Ye and the calf to follow. The bridge made no dramatic sound, and its success hardly attracted attention. Yet the farmers could move their stock, students could reach the clinic, and the cloud beast could rest where flowers still grew. Ye drew a small circle around the crossing on An's map. He said it should be marked as a place where several kinds of life had found room, not as a victory belonging to either of them. An looked at the circle for a long moment before packing his tools. He had spent years building machines that demanded obedience; this quiet bridge asked nothing from those who crossed it.

日落時，新步道已架在淺水道上，第一個走過的是一戶提著蔬菜的人家。安知等走在最後的孩子踏上穩固地面，才讓葉青與幼獸跟上。橋沒有發出驚天動地的聲響，成功也沒引來多少注意。然而農民能移動牲口，學生能抵達診所，雲獸也能在仍有花朵的地方歇息。葉青在安知的地圖上，替這處渡口畫了一個小圓圈。他說這應標示成多種生命都找到空間的地方，而不是他們任一人的勝利。安知凝視那個圓圈許久，才收起工具。他花了多年建造要求服從的機械；這座安靜小橋卻不向過橋者索求任何事。
