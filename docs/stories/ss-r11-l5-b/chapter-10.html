壞水之夜

The Night of Bad Water

雲獸不肯飛：兩個凡人的春天 · 第 10 / 25 章 · 4000–5000 · 英中對照 · 校訂中

At the outer meadow Ye discovered that the calf had developed an allergy to a new kind of pollen. He removed the flowers nearest its bed but refused to cut the whole field. An brought the bulk of their supplies indoors as dark clouds gathered. A village teacher asked them to cite their reasons for believing the water was unsafe. Ye welcomed the question, because the solution would require collaboration between her students, the healer, and the people who maintained the wells. The village head wanted to compel everyone to use bottled water at once, but the local counselor pointed out that poorer households could not afford it. They agreed to test the wells first and distribute clean water to those who needed it most.

在外圍草甸，葉青發現幼獸對一種新的花粉過敏。他移走睡處附近的花，卻拒絕砍掉整片花田。烏雲聚集時，安知把大部分補給搬進屋內。村裡的教師請他們引用依據，說明為何認為水不安全。葉青歡迎這個問題，因為解法需要學生、醫者與水井維護者合作。村長想強制每個人立刻改喝瓶裝水，但當地輔導員指出，較窮的家庭負擔不起。他們同意先檢測水井，並優先供應乾淨飲水給最需要的人。

The healer wrote a descriptive record of the calf's symptoms, distinguishing skin irritation from problems with digestion. A long drought had lowered the well water below its usual level. When the first sample turned cloudy, a child began to exclaim that the whole village was poisoned. Ye asked her to wait for the second test, then helped volunteers carry a clean pot to the community grill where water could be boiled. A visiting scientist from the Mountain Institute said preliminary results suggested soil runoff rather than a new disease. She warned that one sample could not reveal every problem in the water supply. An drew the wells on a map and marked them with dates, so residents could see what had actually been tested.

醫者寫下對幼獸症狀的詳細描述，將皮膚刺激與消化問題區分開來。長期乾旱讓井水降到平常水位以下。第一份水樣變混濁時，一名孩子驚叫整個村子都中毒了。葉青請她等第二次檢驗，並幫志工將乾淨鍋子搬到社區烤爐，準備煮沸飲水。山地研究所的一名來訪科學家表示，初步結果較像土壤沖刷，而非新疾病。她警告，只靠一份樣本無法揭露水源所有問題。安知在地圖上標出水井與測試日期，讓居民看得見實際檢驗過哪些地方。

By evening fear threatened to overwhelm the shelter. Some residents proposed a radical answer: burn the flowering moss so that no creature would come to drink nearby. The scientist said that was not rational, because the plants helped hold the soil during heavy rain. Instead, she recommended closing one contaminated spring and opening a temporary water station at the old train terminal. The plan depended on a variable nobody could control, the weather, so volunteers prepared for both dry conditions and sudden flooding. Ye stayed with the calf as it drank a small amount of tested water. An joined the night watch, watching the level rise in a measuring jar by the doorway. The men did not sleep much, but no one had to make a decision alone.

傍晚，恐懼幾乎淹沒收容所。有人提出激進辦法：燒掉開花氣苔，讓任何動物都不再來附近喝水。科學家表示那並不理性，因為植物有助於暴雨時固定土壤。她建議先封閉一處受污染泉眼，在舊火車終點站設臨時供水點。計畫取決於大家無法控制的一項變數：天氣。因此志工同時準備乾旱與突發洪水兩種情況。葉青陪幼獸喝下一小口檢驗合格的水。安知加入夜間值守，看著門邊量杯中的水位升高。兩人睡得很少，但沒有人需要獨自決定。

A traveling novelist had been staying at the terminal to gather material for a thriller set in the wilderness. He offered to turn the water scare into a dramatic story about a hidden monster. The teacher refused. She said the real event had enough tension without inventing a villain or frightening the children further. Instead, she invited the writer to help copy the test results into language that families could understand. He accepted the task and discovered that a straightforward explanation could hold readers' attention. When the rain finally came, the closed spring spilled muddy water across the path. Because the temporary station was already operating, residents walked there calmly with their jars. An felt relief, but also a more difficult kind of responsibility: their precautions would need to remain useful after they left.

一名巡迴小說家正住在終點站，蒐集荒野驚悚小說的素材。他提出要把水源恐慌寫成藏有怪獸的戲劇性故事。教師拒絕了。她說真實事件已有足夠張力，不必再捏造反派或進一步嚇壞孩子。她反而邀小說家協助把檢測結果改寫成家庭能理解的文字。小說家接受任務，並發現直接清楚的解釋也能吸引讀者。大雨終於落下，封閉泉眼溢出泥水，流過小路。由於臨時供水點早已啟用，居民得以提著水罐平靜地前往。安知感到鬆口氣，卻也感到一種更難承擔的責任：他們的預防措施必須在離開後仍然有用。

At dawn the calf was breathing normally and the pollen rash had faded. Ye did not celebrate as if their work were finished. He checked the drinking water again, thanked the volunteers by name, and helped return unused supplies to the community storehouse. The teacher asked whether the men would continue their journey that day. Ye looked at the sky, where a broad band of cloud had begun to move north. He believed the herd was still somewhere ahead, but he would not chase it while the calf remained weak. An surprised him by agreeing before the question had been fully asked. They stayed one more day, built a second water station, and trained local volunteers to test the wells. Their next departure would be slower, but the village would not be left with an unfinished rescue.

黎明時，幼獸呼吸已恢復正常，花粉引起的紅疹也褪了。葉青沒有把工作當成結束而慶祝。他再度檢查飲水，逐一向志工道謝，並協助將未用完的補給送回社區倉庫。教師問兩人是否當天繼續趕路。葉青望向天空，一道寬闊雲帶已開始向北移動。他相信獸群仍在前方某處，卻不願在幼獸虛弱時追趕。安知還沒聽完問題便意外地表示贊同。兩人多留一天，建立第二處供水站，並教當地志工檢測水井。他們的下一次出發會慢些，但不會留給村莊一場尚未完成的救援。
