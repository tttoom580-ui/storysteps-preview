雲獸不肯飛：兩個凡人的春天 · 第 17 / 25 章 · 4000–5000 · 英中對照 · 校訂中

The city tower office requested a full accounting of the old cage program before allowing An to examine the machines. Ye watched An read the letter and said he could see why people might adore a brilliant engineer while ignoring those harmed by his work. An did not ask Ye to defend him. Instead, he visited the attic of his former workshop, where the original drawings lay beneath a cracked wooden box. The plans were comparable to the ones still used by the company, although several details had changed. He called Ye to witness how he would conduct the review: list each design date, identify who approved changes, and keep copies in public hands. The task frightened him because the evidence might show he had known more than he remembered.

城市高塔辦公室要求先完整核算舊籠舍計畫，才准許安知檢查機械。葉青看著安知讀信，說他能理解為何人們會崇拜一位聰明工程師，卻忽略受到其作品傷害的人。安知沒有要求葉青替自己辯護，而是走進舊工作坊的閣樓，原始圖紙就放在裂開的木箱底下。那些圖與公司仍使用的版本大致可比，只是幾處細節改變了。他請葉青見證自己的查核方式：逐項列出設計日期、辨認核准修改的人，並保留公眾可取得的副本。這工作讓他恐懼，因為資料可能顯示自己當時知道的比現在記得的更多。

During the review An made a plain confession: he had accepted the company's claim that a locked gate protected workers, even after field keepers warned of its danger. He asked a student delegate to record the statement exactly rather than soften it. The documents showed how destructive the choice had been, though they also showed several engineers had raised objections. An felt devotion to his craft but no longer wanted that feeling to excuse anything. At the doorway Ye waited while he finished reading. Ye said an apology could open a conversation but could not demand trust in return. An answered that he understood. The two men had traveled together long enough to care for each other, yet neither was required to agree on every judgment. That distinction gave their closeness room to breathe.

查核過程中，安知坦白承認：即使現場照護員警告危險，他仍接受公司『上鎖的門能保護工人』的說法。他請學生代表照原話記錄，不要替他修飾。文件顯示這選擇造成多大的破壞，卻也記載幾名工程師當時曾提出反對。安知熱愛自己的技藝，卻不再想讓這份熱愛成為任何藉口。葉青站在門口等他讀完。葉青說，道歉可以開啟對話，卻不能以此要求信任作為回報。安知回答他明白。兩人相伴旅行已久，彼此關心，卻不必對每項判斷都意見一致。這個區別反而讓親近有了呼吸空間。

The company arranged to place a filter around the damaged machines and initiate repairs before the spring migration reached the towers. Its manager described the old system as legendary, but Ye asked about the likelihood that a new cage could still trap an animal in a storm. The manager insisted the work had no harmful motive. An said a person's intention did not determine whether a design was safe. A cold wind passed through the workshop and made the apprentices shiver. The calf approached the open window to sniff the damp air, then withdrew when a machine inside began to hum. Ye marked its reaction but refused to call it proof of a specific danger. The manager agreed to shut the machine down for a supervised test the next day.

公司安排在受損機械周圍加裝過濾器，並在春季遷徙抵達高塔前開始維修。經理稱舊系統具有傳奇地位，葉青卻問新籠舍在暴風中仍困住動物的可能性有多高。經理堅持工作毫無傷害動機。安知說，一個人的意圖不能決定設計是否安全。冷風吹過工作坊，讓學徒們打寒顫。幼獸靠近敞開窗戶嗅聞濕空氣，室內機械開始嗡鳴時，牠又退了回去。葉青記下反應，卻拒絕把它當成某個特定危險的證明。經理同意隔天在監督下關閉機械進行測試。

Outside the workshop a mural portrayed the towers as the supreme achievement of modern cultivation. Its polished surface hid the rough texture of older stone that had once guided migrating animals through the valley. An traced the old marks with one finger and felt a sharp torment at the thought that he had helped cover them. Ye stood beside him and asked whether he wanted to leave the city for a day. An considered the offer, then chose to stay for the scheduled repair. He did not need Ye to witness every difficult moment, but he was grateful that Ye had given him a choice. They walked back to the inn without talking much. The silence between them had changed: it no longer meant two strangers avoiding each other, but two adults allowing thought to take its time.

工作坊外，壁畫把高塔描繪成現代修行最至高的成就。光亮表面掩住了更古老石頭的粗糙紋理；那些石頭曾指引遷徙動物穿越山谷。安知用手指沿著舊痕跡滑過，想到自己曾協助遮蓋它們，心中一陣折磨。葉青站在旁邊，問他想不想離開城市一天。安知考慮後，決定留下參加預定維修。他不需要葉青見證每個艱難時刻，卻感激葉青給了他選擇。他們幾乎沒說話就走回旅店。兩人之間的沉默已改變：不再是陌生人互相躲避，而是兩個成年人給彼此思考的時間。

The supervised test exposed the fault plainly. When a technician pushed the model gate from inside, its support jammed against the frame. An removed the locking bar and showed how an open shelter could redirect energy safely into the ground. The manager approved a small trial but demanded an independent safety check before expanding it. Ye agreed; his care for the calf did not make every engineering judgment his to decide. Later that night An handed Ye a copy of his confession and asked him to keep it only if he wished. Ye took the paper, folded it once, and placed it beside the travel map. The men did not speak of forgiveness. They spoke of the open gates they still needed to build before the next thunderstorm.

受監督的測試清楚暴露故障。技師從內側推動模型門時，支架卡住門框。安知拆下鎖桿，示範開放式遮棚如何把能量安全導入地面。經理核准小規模試行，但要求擴大前先做獨立安全檢查。葉青同意；他照顧幼獸，並不代表所有工程判斷都該由他決定。當晚，安知交給葉青一份自己的坦白紀錄，請他若願意再收下。葉青接過紙，折了一次，放在旅行地圖旁。兩人沒有談寬恕，而是談下一場雷暴前，還有哪些敞開的大門必須建好。
