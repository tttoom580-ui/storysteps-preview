天空不屬於我們

The Sky Does Not Belong to Us

Three months later Ye and An walked alongside the restored canal that fed the first meadow at Reed Chapel. A new alternate route allowed farmers to move heavy carts without crossing the animal resting ground. At the town library a public exhibition compared different ideas of land ownership, including private estates, cooperatives, and communism; the historian carefully distinguished political theories from the practical agreements local people had made. Ye said the crucial question was not which label sounded noblest, but whether the flowers would remain alive when nobody famous was watching. The village council had continued measuring the water and repairing the paths. Children who once chased the cloud calf now learned to watch wild creatures from a distance. Their patience had begun to change the habits of an entire town.

三個月後，葉青與安知沿著修復的運河散步，水道正供應蘆葦禮拜堂的第一片草甸。新的替代路線讓農民能運送重車，不必穿越動物休息地。鎮圖書館的公開展覽比較各種土地所有權觀念，包括私有莊園、合作社與共產主義；歷史學者謹慎區分政治理論和當地居民實際達成的協議。葉青說，關鍵問題不是哪個標籤聽起來最高尚，而是當沒有名人觀看時，花是否仍能活著。村議會持續測量水源並維修道路。過去追著雲獸幼獸跑的孩子，如今學會保持距離觀察野生動物。他們的耐心已開始改變整個小鎮的習慣。

An had watched his work evolve from sealed cages into open resting places, and he no longer kept a tight grip on every design. Each village could adapt the structure to its own climate, provided it tested the safety conditions and kept public records. A student wrote a hypothesis that more connected moss beds would help migrating animals survive dry seasons. Ye encouraged her optimism but warned that a good idea still needed evidence. An admired the originality of a small garden built by children from recycled wood, especially because every path led back to open ground. The work was less glamorous than a great tower, but it drew people into responsibility rather than obedience. The two men helped only when asked, leaving room for local hands to do their own work.

安知看著自己的作品從封閉籠舍逐漸演變成開放休息地，也不再緊抓每項設計不放。各村可以依自己的氣候調整結構，前提是測試安全條件並保留公開紀錄。一名學生提出假設：連結更多苔草床，能幫助遷徙動物度過乾季。葉青鼓勵她保持樂觀，同時提醒好想法仍需證據。安知欣賞孩子們利用回收木材建造的小花園，認為設計很有原創性，尤其每條小路都能走回開放土地。這份工作不如宏偉高塔華麗，卻讓人願意承擔責任，而非被要求服從。兩人只有受到邀請才協助，讓當地居民保留自己的工作空間。

At the annual festival a poet read a poetic account of the migration, describing the elder cloud beast as a prophet of spring. Ye smiled but reminded listeners that the animal had never promised them good weather. A careful scientific station now tracked the pulse of the returning winds, while the council set a quota on visitor numbers during sensitive weeks. Some merchants objected that the limits reduced income; others had found new work caring for paths and selling locally grown plants. An said a fair compromise required continual review, not a permanent celebration of one policy. When the first silver cloud appeared beyond the mountains, the townspeople remained in the garden rather than rushing to greet it. They allowed the sky to tell its own story.

年度慶典上，一名詩人朗讀一段富有詩意的遷徙紀事，將長輩雲獸比作春天的先知。葉青微笑，卻提醒聽眾，動物從未承諾帶來好天氣。一座審慎運作的科學站如今追蹤回返風勢的脈動，議會也在敏感時段限制訪客名額。有些商人反對，說限制減少收入；另一些人則在維護步道與販售本地植物中找到新工作。安知表示，公平的折衷需要持續檢討，而不是永遠慶祝某項政策。第一朵銀雲出現在群山後方時，鎮民留在花園裡，沒有急著前去迎接。他們讓天空自己述說故事。

In the city a company shareholder asked why open shelters bore such a strong resemblance to the old cages if the principles had changed so much. An showed how the walls had been removed, the floor changed, and the exits widened. A cloud beast could now soar away at any time, and no human held a key to its movement. The shareholder asked the engineers to specify the costs and the safety tests clearly. A new stimulus program offered vouchers to neighborhoods that planted public moss beds and maintained accessible paths. Ye admired the striking contrast between a system built to own animals and one designed to share space with them. He warned the committee not to turn the voucher into another reward for capturing wildlife. The program was revised before it began.

城市裡，一名公司股東問，若原則改變如此多，為何開放遮棚看起來仍與舊籠舍如此相似。安知展示牆壁如何移除、地板如何改變、出口如何加寬。雲獸如今隨時都能高飛，沒有人類握有控制牠行動的鑰匙。股東請工程師具體列明成本與安全測試。一項新的刺激計畫向種植公共苔草床、維護無障礙通道的社區發放代金券。葉青欣賞兩套系統的鮮明對照：一套為佔有動物而建，另一套則為與動物共享空間而設。他警告委員會，不要把代金券變成另一種捕捉野生動物的獎勵。計畫因此在開始前獲得修正。

One clear evening Ye and An returned to the chapel where their journey had begun. The bell was still there, but nobody used it to summon a cloud beast. They sat on the steps while the last light crossed the grass and talked about where they would go next. Ye wanted to open a traveling care station for animals hurt by human roads. An wanted to teach young craftspeople how to build exits before they built walls. Neither plan required the other man to give up his work. They decided to travel together for the next season, sharing a small cart and choosing each new stop by agreement. Far above them a young cloud beast crossed the sky with its herd, no longer needing to be known by name. Ye watched it vanish into the evening. An took his hand, and they turned toward the open road.

某個晴朗傍晚，葉青與安知回到旅程起點的禮拜堂。鐘還在，卻沒有人再用它召喚雲獸。兩人坐在臺階上，看最後一道光掠過草地，談起下一步要去哪裡。葉青想設立巡迴照護站，幫助被人類道路傷害的動物。安知想教年輕工匠在建牆以前先設計出口。任何一項計畫都不要求另一人放棄自己的工作。他們決定下一季一起旅行，共用一輛小車，每到一站都共同決定。高空中，一隻年輕雲獸隨族群穿越天空，不再需要被人類記住名字。葉青看著牠消失於暮色。安知握住他的手，兩人轉身走向敞開的大路。
