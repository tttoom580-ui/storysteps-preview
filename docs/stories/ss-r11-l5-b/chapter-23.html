A Rehearsal for Rain

The northern city had tried to reduce carbon emissions by covering its streets with stone, leaving few places for rain to reach the soil. A chubby young cloud beast stepped into the public garden and stared at a dry fountain. The council expected every new project to conform to the old construction schedule, but the migrating herd had no interest in human deadlines. Ye asked the planning office to allow an open discussion about what the animals actually needed. An brought drawings from the villages they had visited, showing how roads could remain useful without cutting through every patch of living ground. The first meeting was uncomfortable because several officials believed the animals would leave soon and the problem would disappear. Ye pointed to the thirsty trees. They would remain after the last beast flew north.

北方城市曾想減少碳排放，卻把街道鋪滿石頭，讓雨水幾乎無法滲入土壤。一隻圓胖的幼年雲獸踏入公共花園，盯著乾掉的噴泉。議會希望每項新工程都符合舊施工時程，但遷徙獸群對人類期限毫無興趣。葉青請規劃處舉行公開討論，了解動物真正的需求。安知帶來沿途各村的圖紙，顯示道路不必切開每一片活土，也能保持實用。第一次會議很不自在，因為幾位官員認為動物很快離開，問題也會消失。葉青指著缺水的樹。最後一隻雲獸飛往北方後，樹仍會留下。

A university discussion group invited villagers and engineers to hold a public discourse on the ecological effects of paved land. One student wrote a simple equation comparing rainfall, drainage, and water held by soil. She made her assumptions explicit so residents could question the numbers. An said that no single equation could decide a moral issue, but careful measurements helped people understand the choices. The city gardener proposed planting narrow green strips along the road, where rain could seep into the earth. Several shopkeepers worried that the plan would reduce parking, and the gardener invited them to help choose the sites. Ye appreciated the change in tone: the debate was no longer about proving who loved nature most, but about designing a place people could continue living in.

大學討論社邀請村民與工程師，公開討論鋪面土地對生態的影響。一名學生寫出簡單方程式，比較降雨、排水以及土壤儲水量。她清楚列出假設，讓居民可以質疑數字。安知說，沒有任何單一方程式能決定道德問題，但謹慎測量有助於理解選擇。城市園丁提議沿路種植狹長綠帶，讓雨水能滲進土裡。幾位店主擔心減少停車位，園丁就邀他們一起選擇地點。葉青欣賞討論氣氛的改變：大家不再爭著證明誰最愛自然，而是開始設計人們能持續生活的場所。

The first construction team wanted to inject water deep into dry soil through narrow pipes. An said the method might work but workers would have to modify it near old tree roots. A bakery owner brought warm pastry to the workers and asked whether the dust would harm her customers. Instead of dismissing her concern, the team covered its tools and worked only in the quiet hours after the market closed. A wealthy patron offered to pay for a fountain decorated with cloud-beast statues, but Ye suggested that working rain channels would help more than an expensive symbol. The patron agreed to fund the drainage work after visiting the garden. By evening a small test bed had absorbed water without flooding the nearby shop entrance. The workers celebrated by washing their hands and going home to rest.

第一組施工隊想用細管把水注入乾燥土層。安知說方法可能可行，但工人必須在老樹根附近修改設計。麵包店老闆帶熱酥點給工人，問灰塵會不會傷害顧客。團隊沒有忽視她的顧慮，而是蓋好工具，僅在市場打烊後的安靜時段施工。一名富有贊助人願意支付一座裝飾雲獸雕像的噴泉，葉青卻建議，能運作的雨水渠道比昂貴象徵更有用。贊助人參觀花園後，同意資助排水工程。傍晚，小型試驗花床吸收了水，沒有淹到附近商店入口。工人洗了手，回家休息，便是當天的慶祝。

The weather office scheduled a rehearsal for the next storm, using a scenario in which intense rain arrived while the cloud herd crossed the city. Its new computer was sophisticated, but the villagers insisted that any plan must make sense without a screen. An described how temporary gates should be opened, how ambulances could pass, and where animals could find wet ground. The exercise revealed a problem: the southern market still had high smog levels because trucks waited there with engines running. Shopkeepers agreed to change delivery times, and the city offered electric carts for the busiest hours. Ye reminded the officials that the exercise was not a real storm. Successful rehearsal meant they had found weaknesses early, not that they had made danger disappear.

氣象局安排下一場風暴的預演，設定強降雨恰好遇上雲獸穿城的情境。新電腦非常精密，但村民堅持計畫即使沒有螢幕，也必須讓人理解。安知說明臨時門如何開啟、救護車如何通行，以及動物能在何處找到濕土。演練揭露一個問題：南側市場的煙霧污染仍嚴重，因為卡車停在那裡不熄引擎。店主同意調整送貨時間，市府也提供繁忙時段使用的電動推車。葉青提醒官員，演練並不是真正的風暴。成功的預演代表他們及早找到弱點，不代表危險已消失。

At the end of the week a delivery cart accidentally began to squash a young flower bed. Workers stopped it before the roots were harmed, then agreed to submit a report describing the error. An helped remove the tangle of a long hose that had become caught beneath the wheels. A shopkeeper complained that changes in transport might increase unemployment, while another began to whine about having to walk farther with heavy boxes. The council offered training and paid delivery assistance rather than telling either person to be quiet. Ye watched a chubby calf step over the repaired bed and settle beside a shallow pool. The first real rain began just after sunset. Water moved through the new channels into the soil, and the flowers lifted their heads without any human hand pulling them upward.

那週結束時，一輛送貨車意外開始壓到年輕花床。工人及時停車，根部尚未受傷，隨後提交報告描述錯誤。安知協助解開卡在輪子下的長水管糾結。一名店主抱怨交通改變可能增加失業，另一人則不停哀怨，說搬重箱得走更遠。議會提供訓練與有薪送貨協助，而不是叫任何一方閉嘴。葉青看一隻圓胖幼獸跨過修好的花床，安坐在淺水池旁。真正的第一場雨在日落後落下。水沿新渠道流進土裡，花朵抬起頭來，無須任何人用手將它們拉高。
