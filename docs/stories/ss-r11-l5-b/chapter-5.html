草甸的表決

The Meadow Vote

雲獸不肯飛：兩個凡人的春天 · 第 5 / 25 章 · 4000–5000 · 英中對照

The capital newspaper called the valley vote a constitutional dispute, although the issue was simply whether residents could protect a strip of grass. A visiting democrat argued that town councils should control their own land. The tower company dismissed the entire episode as an exaggeration by sentimental herders. Its fleet of trucks waited beside the market; the rattle of loose chains followed the drivers as they prepared to flatten the meadow before the next planting season. A shopkeeper accused the company of greed, but an elderly peasant reminded everyone that the power towers had also brought light to homes that once had none. Ye refused to divide the crowd into good people and villains. He asked what each family would lose if the grass disappeared, and what each family feared losing if the machines stopped.

首都報紙稱山谷表決是憲政爭議，事實上問題只是居民能否保護一小條草地。一名來訪的民主派人士主張，鎮議會應有權管理自己的土地。高塔公司將整件事斥為多愁善感的牧人誇大其詞。公司的卡車車隊停在市場旁，鬆動的鐵鏈喀啦作響，司機準備在下一季播種前壓平草甸。店主指責公司貪婪，年長農民卻提醒大家，電塔也為以前沒有燈光的家庭帶來照明。葉青拒絕將群眾分成好人與壞人。他詢問每戶人家，失去草地會損失什麼；若機械停止運轉，又怕失去什麼。

At the meal table an old woman added mountain herb to a pot of soup and described how the company offered an incentive for every acre surrendered. The payment looked generous until families calculated the massive cost of buying fodder after their own grass failed. An showed the figures to a mechanic who had lost his job during the last recession. Instead of arguing, the man asked whether the meadow project could recruit local workers. An said the removable bridge and flower beds would need careful maintenance. A woman who ran a retail stall offered to keep supplies and tools at fair prices. Ye saw the beginning of an answer: preservation could create work, provided the work served the animals and the people rather than another distant owner.

吃飯時，一名老婦在湯裡加入山間香草，並說公司每收下一英畝土地，就提供一筆獎勵金。那筆錢看起來可觀，直到家家戶戶算出草地枯死後購買飼料所需的龐大費用。安知把數據拿給一名在上次經濟衰退時失業的技師看。那人沒有爭論，而是問草甸計畫能否招募當地工人。安知表示，可拆橋和花圃都需要細心保養。一名經營零售攤位的女子願意以合理價格供應物料和工具。葉青看見答案逐漸成形：保育可以創造工作，前提是這些工作服務動物與居民，而非另一位遠方的主人。

Before the hearing, the contractor tried to smash the old bell so that the calf would stop calling toward the hills. An stood between the hammer and the chapel door. He did not strike back. He said the bell had substantial value to the whole village and could be moved only through an agreed process. Ye suggested a quieter tactic: place the calf where everyone could see it graze, but give it enough space to leave. The animal stepped over the threshold of the open shelter and lowered its head to fresh moss. No one needed to touch it. The sight made a stronger case than their speeches. Several residents who had expected a helpless creature were surprised to see an animal capable of making clear choices.

聽證前，承包商企圖砸碎舊鐘，讓幼獸停止朝山上呼喚。安知擋在鐵鎚與禮拜堂門之間，卻沒有反擊。他說鐘對全村具有實質價值，必須經過共同同意的程序才能移動。葉青提出一個更安靜的策略：讓每個人看見幼獸吃草，同時保留牠離開的空間。幼獸跨過敞開小屋的門檻，低頭吃新鮮苔草。沒有人需要碰牠。這景象比兩人的演說更有說服力。幾位原先以為牠毫無自保能力的居民，驚訝地發現牠能做出清楚的選擇。

The committee returned a verdict that nobody had expected. The village would preserve the first meadow for one year, conduct an independent energy study, and permit the company to keep supplying electricity under temporary limits. News of the decision became widespread before the meeting had even ended. Neither Ye nor An called it a final victory. Only one resting place had been saved, and the herd still needed an unbroken route through the mountains. An offered to remain and build more bridges, but Ye said he intended to follow the calf until it found its elders. An faced a choice that was no longer about machinery. He could stay where success was visible, or travel with a person who could not promise an outcome.

委員會作出一項誰都沒料到的裁決。村莊將保護第一片草甸一年，委託獨立能源研究，並允許公司在臨時限制下繼續供電。會議尚未結束，消息就已廣泛傳開。葉青與安知都沒有稱之為最後勝利。他們只保住一處休息地，獸群仍需要一條不間斷的山間路線。安知提出要留下建更多橋，葉青卻說自己打算跟隨幼獸，直到牠找到長輩。安知面對的不再是機械問題。他可以留在看得見成果的地方，也可以跟隨一個無法承諾結果的人繼續前進。

That night An walked alone to the little bridge, where the water ran between wooden boards and shadows moved across the flowers. He remembered the first tower he had designed, the applause when it opened, and the discomfort he had ignored when a cloud beast refused to enter its cage. Ye found him there and offered no easy forgiveness for mistakes he did not yet know. Instead, he asked what An planned to do with the skill he still possessed. An answered that he wanted to build paths with exits. In the morning they handed the village a set of clear maintenance instructions and chose two residents to supervise the new crossing. Then An placed his tools beside Ye's supplies. The calf walked ahead of them, toward the uncertain mountains, and An followed of his own free will.

那夜，安知獨自走到小橋上，流水從木板間穿過，花朵上有影子移動。他想起自己設計的第一座高塔、啟用時的掌聲，以及一隻雲獸拒絕進籠時被他忽略的不安。葉青在那裡找到他，沒有對尚不了解的錯誤輕易給予原諒，只問安知打算如何運用仍然擁有的技術。安知回答，他想建造讓人能夠離開的道路。隔天早晨，兩人把清楚的維護說明交給村莊，並選出兩名居民負責監督新通道。接著安知把工具放到葉青的行囊旁。幼獸走在前頭，朝未知的群山前進；安知出於自己的意願跟上。
