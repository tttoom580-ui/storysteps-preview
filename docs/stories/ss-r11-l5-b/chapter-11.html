賭場上方的避風所

The Refuge Above the Casino

雲獸不肯飛：兩個凡人的春天 · 第 11 / 25 章 · 4000–5000 · 英中對照 · 校訂中

The road climbed toward a mountain refuge, but a sudden fog made the hour an apt time to stop. The only building open nearby was a casino that catered to winter travelers. Its owner liked to characterize the cloud beasts as lucky symbols, and he offered money to display the calf in his entrance hall. Ye declined and asked whether the town had a clinical station where he could check the animal's lungs. The owner said the physician lived upstairs and would come after the evening crowd left. An found the answer useful but disliked the owner's smile. He placed the calf in a sheltered courtyard away from music and smoke, then drew a simple sign asking visitors to keep their distance. No one had yet tried to take the animal, but the offer reminded him how quickly fascination could become possession.

道路一路爬向山間避風所，突然起霧，這時停下來再適合不過。附近唯一仍營業的建物是一家接待冬季旅客的賭場。老闆喜歡把雲獸形容成幸運象徵，並出錢想在入口大廳展示幼獸。葉青拒絕，轉而詢問鎮上是否有臨床診所能檢查幼獸的肺。老闆說醫師住在樓上，待晚間人潮散去就會來。安知覺得這消息有用，卻不喜歡老闆的笑。他把幼獸安置在遠離音樂與煙味的遮蔽庭院，再畫出簡單標誌，請訪客保持距離。尚未有人企圖帶走動物，但這提議提醒他，迷戀能多快變成佔有。

The casino had printed postcards using drawings made by local children without asking permission. When a teacher challenged the copyright, the manager said that pictures of animals belonged to everyone. The children were fascinated by the cards but wanted their names on them. One boy gave a surprised gasp when he saw his drawing reversed on a window. The casino's young heir apologized and offered to print new cards with proper credit, then donate the earnings to the refuge. Ye accepted the apology only after the teacher confirmed the children's families had a say. Outside, the calf answered a distant howl from the high cliffs. An recognized the sound as a full-grown cloud beast, though he could not tell how far away it stood. Their destination finally felt close enough to hear.

賭場把當地孩子畫的圖印成明信片，卻未徵求同意。教師提出著作權問題時，經理回答動物圖片屬於所有人。孩子們雖然喜歡卡片，卻希望上面印著自己的名字。一名男孩看見自己的畫被反轉印在窗上時，驚訝地倒抽一口氣。賭場年輕繼承人道歉，提出重新印卡並正確署名，再把收益捐給避風所。直到教師確認孩子家長也能參與決定，葉青才接受這份道歉。庭院外，幼獸回應了高崖上傳來的遠方嚎鳴。安知認出那是成年雲獸的叫聲，卻無法確定距離。他們的目的地終於近得能聽見了。

The mountain refuge had survived a landslide, leaving a deep scar on its eastern wall, although the building remained intact. A physician examined the calf and said she would recommend rest rather than another day of climbing. The wind outside was sheer force, striking the shutters with enough strength to knock a person down. A curious spectator tried to enter the courtyard but stopped when Ye explained that visitors could watch from the doorway. The physician noticed an old theft report on the wall and asked whether the refuge still possessed its emergency supplies. The keeper answered that nearly everything had been returned except a weather instrument. An volunteered to help repair the empty stand, but he refused to assume the missing item had been stolen by the first stranger who passed through the town.

山間避風所曾在土石滑落中倖存，東牆留下一道深深的傷痕，主體卻仍完好。醫師檢查幼獸後表示，她會建議休息，而非再爬一天山。屋外的狂風力道十足，撞擊百葉窗的力量足以將人吹倒。一名好奇旁觀者想走進庭院，但葉青解釋訪客可從門口觀看，對方就停下了。醫師注意到牆上一份舊失竊報告，問避風所是否還有緊急物資。管理人說，幾乎都已找回，只剩一件氣象儀器。安知自願幫忙修理空著的支架，卻拒絕在查清前就假定失物是第一位過鎮陌生人偷走的。

At night a security guard carried a rifle while patrolling the mountain path. An asked him to point the weapon away from the refuge, where children were sleeping. The guard agreed and said his main duty was to warn of falling rocks, not hunt animals. A woman with a wheelchair arrived late, unable to climb the broken steps to the main room. An remembered the removable bridge they had built and offered to make a temporary ramp with the caretaker. The work turned into a small workshop: travelers took measurements, held boards, and checked the slope before anyone used it. Ye stayed beside the calf, whose breathing eased as the wind weakened. When the ramp was finished, the woman rolled inside without applause or help she had not requested. The caretaker promised to upgrade the permanent entrance before winter.

夜間，一名保全拿著步槍巡視山路。安知請他把槍口避開有孩子熟睡的避風所。保全同意，並說自己的主要責任是警告落石，而不是狩獵動物。一名使用輪椅的女子很晚抵達，無法爬上通往大廳的破損階梯。安知想起以前做的可拆橋，提議與管理人一起搭臨時坡道。工作變成一場小型工作坊：旅客幫忙測量、扶木板，在使用前確認坡度。葉青陪在幼獸旁，隨著風勢減弱，牠的呼吸逐漸放鬆。坡道完成後，女子自行推輪椅進屋，沒有掌聲，也不需要任何未曾要求的協助。管理人答應在冬季前升級永久入口。

The next morning the guard found the missing weather instrument under a collapsed shelf. The keeper crossed the theft notice off the wall and wrote a correction beneath it. Ye wondered how many people had been blamed because someone preferred an easy story to an uncertain one. The casino's heir came uphill with new postcards showing the children's names, and the refuge accepted the donation after checking the accounts. Before they left, An walked with Ye to a ridge overlooking the far valley. A long trail of clouds moved across the horizon. In its center a dark shape rose and fell, too large to be any bird. Ye said they should continue carefully, not race to reach it. An nodded. For the first time their patience felt like a form of hope rather than a delay.

隔天早晨，保全在倒塌架子底下找到失蹤的氣象儀器。管理人把牆上失竊公告劃掉，在下方寫上更正。葉青不禁想，究竟有多少人只因別人喜歡容易理解的故事勝過不確定，就受了責備。賭場繼承人帶著印有孩子姓名的新明信片上山，避風所核對帳目後收下捐款。出發前，安知陪葉青走到可俯瞰遠方山谷的山脊。一道長長的雲帶橫越地平線，其中有個深色身影起伏，巨大得不可能是鳥。葉青說應該小心前進，而不是競速追上。安知點頭。耐心第一次不像拖延，反而像一種希望。
