雲獸不肯飛：兩個凡人的春天 · 第 3 / 25 章 · 4000–5000 · 英中對照

The third village stood beside a narrow creek, where children practiced cloud-beast calls on hollow reeds. Ye asked them to stop until the calf was safely behind a screen. He did not accuse the children of abuse; they were repeating a custom that adults had never explained. The village head offered a consultation with the local herders, but the new road official watched them with open contempt. A traveling correspondent named Fen had arrived to write about the disputed migration. She wanted a picture of the animal in a cage, because her editor believed readers would pay more attention to a dramatic image. Ye refused the photograph. Fen lowered her camera and asked what kind of picture would show the problem truthfully. An suggested the dry moss beside the busy road.

第三個村落依傍狹窄的溪流，孩子們正用中空蘆葦練習模仿雲獸叫聲。葉青請他們先停下，等幼獸安全躲到屏風後。他沒有指責孩子虐待動物；他們只是在重複大人從未解釋的習俗。村長提出要與當地牧人開諮詢會，但新來的道路官員毫不掩飾輕蔑地看著他們。旅途記者芬前來報導這場遷徙爭議。她想拍一張幼獸關在籠裡的照片，因為編輯認為戲劇性的畫面更能吸引讀者。葉青拒絕拍照。芬放下相機，詢問什麼影像才足以真實呈現問題。安知建議她拍攝繁忙道路旁乾枯的苔地。

During the meeting An made a deliberate effort to listen to people who disagreed with him. The deputy road officer admitted that his department had promised new traffic lanes before the spring festival. A diplomatic answer would have blamed weather alone, but An showed a map explaining how road heat had narrowed the ecosystem on both sides. An older herder said cloud beasts were not extinct, although nobody had seen the largest group that year. The uncertainty frightened him more than the official's impatient remarks. Ye asked how long the village could protect the remaining flowers if the road stayed busy. Nobody knew. A young woman proposed moving the festival market instead of closing the road, a smaller change that neighbors might actually accept.

開會時，安知刻意努力傾聽不同意見。副道路官承認部門曾承諾在春季慶典前增加車道。若只求外交式的圓滑回答，他大可將責任全推給天氣，但安知拿出地圖，解釋道路熱氣如何使兩側生態系縮窄。年長牧人表示雲獸尚未滅絕，雖然今年沒人見過最大的族群。這種不確定，比官員的不耐更讓他害怕。葉青問，如果道路持續繁忙，村子能保護剩下的花多久。無人知道。一名年輕女子提議搬移節慶市集，而非封閉道路；這是鄰居們可能真正接受的較小改變。

An's gut told him to distrust the contractor, yet Ye reminded him that instincts could not replace measurements. They agreed to notify the families living downstream before any experiment began. The odds of saving every moss bed were poor, and the next phase would involve moving a timber platform near an old pier. Fen offered to publish the exact timetable so fishers could decide whether to work elsewhere that day. The contractor suggested they sneak the machinery past the river at night to avoid a crowd. An refused. He had once believed that speed made a project successful, and he knew how easily secrecy could turn a simple mistake into a lasting injury. The villagers approved a daylight trial with two independent observers.

安知直覺上不信任承包商，葉青卻提醒他，直覺不能取代測量。他們同意在任何實驗開始前通知下游住戶。要救回每一片苔地的機會不大，下一階段還需把木平臺移到舊碼頭旁。芬願意刊登精確時程，讓漁民自行決定當天是否換地方工作。承包商建議夜間偷偷把機具送過河，避開群眾。安知拒絕了。他以前認為速度就是專案成功的指標，深知保密如何讓一個普通錯誤變成長久傷害。村民同意白天試行，並安排兩名獨立觀察者。

The village chief was nearing retirement, and his chosen successor wanted the town to become a model of sustainable tourism. She proposed that the festival committee swap its loud brass instruments for hand-painted signs along the migration path. A shopkeeper objected that visitors would be disappointed without music. Ye answered that a festival could honor a living creature without requiring the creature to perform. To illustrate, he placed an old trophy beside a bowl of moss. The trophy had belonged to a former cloud-beast racing champion, a man who now regretted what his competitions had taught young riders. The shopkeeper stared at the damaged cup, then asked whether children could help grow the flowers instead. For the first time that afternoon, the meeting moved from accusation toward practical work.

村長即將退休，他選定的接班人想讓小鎮成為永續旅遊的典範。她提議慶典委員會將喧鬧的銅管樂器換成遷徙路旁的手繪告示。店主反對，認為沒有音樂，遊客會失望。葉青回答，節慶可以尊重活著的生物，卻不必要求牠們表演。為了說明，他把一座舊獎盃放到苔草碗旁。那座獎盃屬於一位曾在雲獸競速比賽奪冠的人，如今他後悔比賽灌輸年輕騎手的觀念。店主凝視受損的獎盃，接著問孩子們能不能改為幫忙種花。那天下午的會議第一次從指責轉向實際行動。

On the following morning the daylight trial proceeded without an accident. The platform crossed the water slowly, and the calf made a soft answering sound when a distant adult called from beyond the wooded hills. Fen captured the moment without photographing the animal's face. Later she showed Ye the picture: sunlight on a ripple, two sets of boot prints, and a thin line of flowers continuing along the bank. He approved it because the photograph told a story of a shared path, not a captured prize. An listened to the faraway call and marked its direction on his map. He could not yet tell whether the sound came from the main herd or a lone traveler. But the calf was no longer entirely silent, and the road ahead had become slightly less uncertain.

隔天早晨，白天試行平安完成。平臺緩慢渡水，當林木後方傳來遠處成年雲獸的呼聲時，幼獸發出柔和回應。芬拍下這一刻，卻沒有拍攝幼獸的臉。後來她把照片給葉青看：陽光映在水波上、兩組靴印，以及沿河岸延伸的一道細花帶。葉青同意刊登，因為照片訴說的是共享道路，而不是被捕捉的獎品。安知聽著遠方的叫聲，把方向標上地圖。他尚無法判定聲音來自主群還是落單的旅者；但幼獸已不再全然沉默，前方道路的不確定也減少了一點。
