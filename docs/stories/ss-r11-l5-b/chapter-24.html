The Last Descent

雲獸不肯飛：兩個凡人的春天 · 第 24 / 25 章 · 4000–5000 · 英中對照 · 校訂中

The two cities reached an accord on the final northern corridor just as the storm clouds thickened. Ye felt a sharp agony when he saw how frightened the youngest animals were by thunder. Rain began to blur the path markers, and several travelers tried to browse their maps while walking through the wind. An asked everyone to stop in a sheltered place before looking at directions. Across the valley a familiar three-note chord sounded from the elder cloud beast. Its call remained coherent even as echoes broke apart against the cliffs. Ye recognized the pause between the notes and understood that the herd was choosing a lower passage. Rather than lead the animals uphill, he moved the human volunteers away from the route they had selected.

兩座城市剛就最後一段北方走廊達成協議，風暴雲便迅速變厚。葉青看見幼獸被雷聲嚇得不輕，心中一陣痛楚。雨水開始模糊路標，幾名旅人試圖一邊在風中行走，一邊翻看地圖。安知請所有人先停進遮蔽處，再看方向。山谷對面，長輩雲獸傳來熟悉的三音和弦。即使回聲被懸崖撕碎，呼喚仍清楚連貫。葉青認出音與音之間的停頓，明白獸群選擇較低的通道。他沒有引領動物上山，而是讓人類志工避開牠們自行選定的路線。

A confrontation began when a tower guard ordered the lower gates closed, following a safety doctrine written before the open corridor had been built. An explained that executing the old order would trap the herd between the gates and the flooded road. The guard asked for written authority, and the mayor sent a clear emergency instruction. An oversaw its execution while Ye checked the meadow beyond. The volunteers had carried out extensive preparations, including clean water, shelter, and paths wide enough for frightened animals to turn around. Each task had been assigned to a person who understood it. The storm made the work difficult, but it did not remove the value of that preparation. By the time the gates opened, the first cloud beast had already begun to descend toward the lower meadow.

一名高塔警衛依照開放走廊興建前的安全教條，命令關閉低處大門，雙方因此發生對峙。安知解釋，執行舊命令會把獸群困在大門與淹水道路之間。警衛要求書面授權，市長便送來清楚的緊急指示。安知監督指示執行，葉青檢查另一側草甸。志工已完成廣泛準備，包括乾淨飲水、遮蔽物，以及足夠寬、讓受驚動物能轉身的路線。每項工作都交給理解任務的人。風暴增加難度，卻沒有讓那些準備失去價值。大門打開時，第一隻雲獸已開始朝較低的草甸下降。

The office's emergency index showed a list of people who could be contacted if the bridge failed. A nasty gust tore one page from the board, and a rumor spread that a riot had begun near the market. An checked the report before sending workers away from their posts. The truth was less dramatic: merchants had gathered to move their stalls out of the floodwater. One woman described the evacuation as a sensible scheme to keep food dry until morning. The mayor repeated the correct information over the public speakers, and the crowd returned to work. A child felt a branch slap his coat and began to cry, but a volunteer took him indoors. Ye heard the cries above the storm and kept the animal path clear while others helped the people.

辦公室的緊急索引列出橋梁失效時可聯絡的人。一陣惡劣強風把公告板上的一頁撕下，市場附近發生暴動的謠言也傳開。安知在派工人離開崗位前先核實消息。事實遠不如謠傳戲劇化：商人聚在一起，只是要把攤位移出積水。一名女子稱這是合理的安排，先讓食物保持乾燥到天亮。市長透過公共廣播重述正確資訊，人群便回去工作。一名孩子的外套被樹枝啪地打到，嚇得哭起來，志工帶他進屋。葉青在風暴聲中聽見哭聲，但繼續維持動物通道暢通，由其他人協助孩子。

The rain began to soften after midnight. People wanted to speculate about whether the herd would cross the last ridge before dawn, but Ye declined to predict what the animals would choose. At the shelter a married couple took turns carrying warm soup to the workers, and one spouse brought dry blankets for those whose homes had flooded. An checked the bridge supports again and found them sound. The mayor asked whether the open corridor had been worthy of the cost. An answered that it had served farmers, patients, visitors, and animals through a dangerous night; its usefulness would have to be measured over years, not one storm. Ye listened from the doorway, muddy and exhausted, with an expression An could not read. Then Ye stepped outside and lifted his face toward the clearing sky.

午夜後，雨勢開始緩和。人們很想猜測獸群是否會在黎明前翻越最後山脊，葉青卻不願預言動物會怎麼選。避風所裡，一對夫妻輪流送熱湯給工人，其中一位配偶還為住家淹水的人送來乾毯。安知再次檢查橋梁支撐，發現仍然穩固。市長問，開放走廊是否值得付出這些成本。安知回答，危險的一夜中它服務了農民、病人、訪客與動物，真正的效用應以多年衡量，而非一場風暴。葉青站在門口聆聽，滿身泥濘、疲憊不堪，神情讓安知看不透。接著葉青走出去，抬頭迎向逐漸放晴的天空。

At dawn the elder cloud beast stood on the last rise, silver fur glittering with rain. The young calf that Ye had carried from Reed Chapel waited beside it. Ye knew the animal by a small uneven mark near one hoof, though he did not call out. The elder gave three slow notes. The young animal answered and ran, lifting into the cold morning air. Others rose beside it until the valley seemed full of moving clouds. An put his arm around Ye, who leaned into him without taking his eyes from the sky. They watched until the herd crossed the northern ridge and disappeared. Nothing had guaranteed this ending. It had required soil, careful work, arguments, corrections, and the animal's own choice to fly. Ye let the last note fade before he spoke.

黎明時，長輩雲獸站在最後的山坡上，銀毛閃著雨光。葉青從蘆葦禮拜堂一路照顧來的幼獸就在旁邊等候。他認得牠一隻蹄旁小小的不對稱印記，卻沒有呼喚。長輩發出三個緩慢的音，幼獸回應後奔跑，升入清冷晨空。其他雲獸在旁邊陸續飛起，整個山谷彷彿滿是流動的雲。安知把手臂搭在葉青肩上，葉青靠向他，目光仍留在天空。兩人一直看到獸群越過北方山脊，消失不見。沒有任何事保證過這樣的結局。它需要土壤、細心工作、爭論、更正，也需要動物自己選擇飛翔。葉青等最後一個音消失後，才開口說話。
