風學校的舊課本

Lessons from the Wind School

The wind school had been founded in the home of an alcoholic industrialist who later gave the building to the city. Its trustees faced a dilemma about how to allocate money fairly among repairs, student support, and animal conservation. A banner over the entrance declared that the institution taught humane engineering, but An remembered the lessons he had received there. In a classroom he found a dusty canvas covering an old demonstration cage. The teacher warned that the previous project files were confidential. Ye asked whether that secrecy protected students or hid mistakes. The woman did not answer immediately. She opened the room, let them look at the cage from a safe distance, and promised to check which records could legally be shared. An was not yet ready to walk inside his former school without feeling ashamed.

風學校最初設在一名酗酒工業家的宅邸裡，後來這人把建物贈給城市。學校董事面臨兩難，不知該如何在維修、學生扶助與動物保育之間公平分配經費。入口上方的布條宣稱這是一所教授人道工程的學校，但安知記得自己當年在這裡學到的東西。在一間教室裡，他看見一塊積塵帆布蓋住舊展示籠。教師警告，過去的專案檔案屬於機密。葉青問，保密究竟是在保護學生，還是在掩蓋錯誤。女子沒有立刻回答。她打開房間，讓兩人從安全距離查看籠舍，並答應查清哪些紀錄依法可以公開。安知尚無法毫無羞愧地走進自己的母校。

An had once believed with absolute conviction that clever machines could solve almost any problem. The school's engineering curriculum encouraged students to optimize speed, cost, and power output, but rarely mentioned the wishes of living creatures. A small enterprise had offered the school equipment in exchange for testing the cages. In the courtyard, students played hockey with a soft ball while their instructor spoke to the visitors. She said an honorable school should teach the consequences of unsuccessful designs as openly as the successes. Yet a wealthy sponsor had tried to manipulate the exhibition by removing the damaged cage. Ye suggested keeping it, alongside an explanation written by students and reviewed by animal keepers. The instructor agreed to propose the change rather than promise what she could not authorize.

安知曾絕對相信，聰明的機械幾乎能解決任何問題。學校的工程課程鼓勵學生最佳化速度、成本和輸出功率，卻很少提及活生生的動物想要什麼。一家小型企業曾向學校提供設備，交換測試籠舍的機會。庭院裡，學生拿軟球打曲棍球，老師則與訪客交談。她說，值得尊敬的學校應和展示成功作品一樣，公開教授失敗設計的後果。然而一位富有贊助人試圖操弄展覽，要求移除受損籠舍。葉青建議保留它，旁邊加上由學生撰寫、動物照護員審查的說明。老師同意提出建議，而不是承諾自己無權決定的事。

The head teacher lived in an old mansion beside the workshop. She showed An a miniature model of the first cage he had built, complete with a spring-loaded gate. He could perceive the exact flaw before she pointed to it. One supporting pillar trapped the gate if an animal pushed from inside, turning an emergency exit into a locked wall. The teacher described the sequence of revisions that followed, but none had corrected the central mistake. An tried the model again and the sudden snap made him startle. Ye did not comfort him with empty praise. He asked An to explain the failure to three apprentices who were waiting in the doorway. An did so, drawing the force of an animal's movement rather than beginning with the design's promised efficiency.

校長住在工作坊旁的舊宅邸。她向安知展示他設計的第一座籠舍縮小模型，連彈簧門都做了出來。老師尚未指出缺陷，安知便看得出來：一根支柱使動物從內側推門時，門反而卡住，將緊急出口變成鎖死的牆。老師說明後來依序進行的修改，但沒有一次修正核心錯誤。安知再次操作模型，突然的彈響讓他嚇了一跳。葉青沒有用空洞的讚美安慰他，而是請他向門邊等候的三名學徒解釋故障。安知照做，先畫出動物移動時的力量，而不是從設計宣稱的效率談起。

In the evening the school held a ceremony for its founders. Some alumni spoke about their success with the reverence usually reserved for worship, while others quietly recalled classmates injured during careless experiments. An attended without taking the platform. He listened as a young student asked why the school's proud history had so little space for people who disagreed with it. The head teacher promised a public review of the course materials and invited Ye to comment on the animal-care sections. Ye accepted only a limited role; he would advise on welfare but would not permit the school to use the calf in an exhibition. An realized that setting a boundary could be a form of generosity. It gave the institution a real chance to learn rather than a convenient photograph.

當晚學校為創辦人舉行紀念儀式。有些校友談起成就時，語氣敬畏得彷彿在崇拜；另一些人則低聲想起因粗心實驗受傷的同學。安知出席，卻沒有上臺。他聽見一名年輕學生問，為何學校引以為傲的歷史，很少為不同意見者留位置。校長承諾公開檢討教材，並邀葉青評論動物照護章節。葉青只接受有限角色：他可以提供福利建議，卻不允許學校拿幼獸做展覽。安知明白，設定界線也可以是一種慷慨。這讓學校有真正學習的機會，而不只得到一張方便宣傳的照片。

After the ceremony An returned to the empty workshop and began drawing a gate that opened outward under gentle pressure. He tested the hinge on wood, then stopped when he noticed that the floor beneath the model was still hard metal. An animal could escape the cage and still fail to discharge its electrical charge safely. The solution, he realized, was not simply a better gate but a resting place that did not require the cage at all. Ye joined him and placed fresh moss on the worktable. They sketched a movable shade roof and wide paths leading directly to wet ground. Nothing in the design depended on an animal being forced to enter. An had come back to the school expecting judgment. He left with something more demanding: a task he was finally willing to finish responsibly.

儀式後，安知回到空蕩工作坊，開始畫一扇能在輕推時向外打開的門。他用木頭試作鉸鏈，隨後停下，發現模型下方仍是堅硬金屬地板。動物就算逃出籠舍，也可能無法安全釋放體內電荷。他明白，解法並非只是一扇更好的門，而是不需要籠舍的休息空間。葉青走進來，在工作桌上放下一把新鮮氣苔。兩人畫出可移動遮陽頂棚，以及直接通往濕地的寬闊道路。整份設計都不依靠強迫動物進入。安知回到學校原本期待的是審判，離開時得到的卻是更艱難的事：一項終於願意負責完成的工作。
