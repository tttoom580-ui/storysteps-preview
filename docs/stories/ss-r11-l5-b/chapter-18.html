舊機械的果園

The Orchard of Old Machines

雲獸不肯飛：兩個凡人的春天 · 第 18 / 25 章 · 4000–5000 · 英中對照 · 校訂中

The tower administration maintained an antique power station behind the city orchard. Its machines made a bizarre rhythm whenever the wind changed, and local gardeners believed officials were trying to conceal the reason. Ye listened to their complaints but asked them to describe what they had actually heard. An inspected the station's public plans and discovered that a belt had been installed backward during an earlier repair. The error could explain the strange noise without proving anyone had acted dishonestly. The gardeners were relieved to hear a possible cause, yet they wanted to see the machine tested rather than accept another official assurance. An promised to work with the station's mechanic in daylight. He no longer considered an unanswered question a threat to his professional pride.

高塔管理局在城市果園後方維護一座古老發電站。風向變化時，機械會發出怪異節奏，當地園丁相信官員正試圖隱瞞原因。葉青聽他們抱怨，卻請每個人描述實際聽到的聲音。安知檢查電站公開圖紙，發現某次舊維修時有一條皮帶被裝反。這個錯誤足以解釋怪聲，卻不能證明有人存心不良。園丁們得知可能原因後鬆了口氣，但仍希望親眼看測試，而非再接受官方保證。安知答應白天與電站技師一起工作。他不再把未解的問題當成威脅自己專業尊嚴的事。

A newspaper claimed the station was proof of widespread corruption, although its reporter had not visited the site. The article described the company's fabric roof as a dangerous new experiment; in reality the roof had been there for twenty years. An suggested a framework for testing every claim separately. The city inspector agreed but warned that the gross cost of replacing the plant at once would force hospitals to cut services. Ye said the animals could not be asked to absorb unlimited harm merely because humans feared expensive repairs. They needed a staged plan that protected both. The inspector offered to publish the costs, the environmental readings, and the limits of each alternative. For the first time, the debate began to include what the city could actually change.

一份報紙宣稱電站證明貪腐普遍存在，然而記者根本沒到過現場。文章把公司的布製屋頂描述成危險的新實驗，事實上那屋頂已有二十年歷史。安知提出將每項指控分開檢驗的框架。城市稽查員同意，卻警告一次更換整座電站的總成本將迫使醫院縮減服務。葉青說，不能只因人類害怕昂貴維修，就要求動物無限承擔傷害。他們需要兼顧兩者的分階段方案。稽查員願意公布成本、環境讀數與每個替代方案的限制。爭論第一次開始納入城市實際能改變的事情。

There was still hostility toward Ye at the market, where an old tower logo had become an icon of the city's prosperity. The mayor asked residents to nominate members for a temporary energy committee. A retired electrician from the orchard said he would serve only if farmers had equal representation. An supported that condition and offered to train anyone willing to understand the equipment. One merchant accused the group of wanting to destroy business; another said business would suffer more if the valley became unlivable. Ye did not answer with insults. He invited both merchants to the evening observation walk, where they could see the calf grazing freely beside the repaired station. Not everyone attended, but the invitation was not withdrawn.

市場上仍有人敵視葉青，因為舊高塔標誌早已成為城市繁榮的象徵。市長請居民提名臨時能源委員會成員。果園的一位退休電工說，只有農民擁有同等代表權，他才願意參加。安知支持這條件，也願意訓練任何願意了解設備的人。一名商人指責團隊想摧毀生意；另一名則說若山谷不再適合居住，生意會受到更大損失。葉青沒有以辱罵回應。他邀兩名商人一起參加晚間觀察步行，親眼看幼獸如何在修復電站旁自由吃草。不是每個人都出席，但邀請沒有因此撤回。

The next morning a crowd held a rally near the main gate, with one group demanding immediate closure and a rival group calling for unrestricted power production. The committee tried to find a plan that could prevail without crushing either side. A proposed subsidy would help small businesses buy low-heat equipment while engineers modified the older towers. An explained why shutting every machine off at once would leave patients in danger. Ye explained why leaving every machine running would destroy the moss corridor. The mayor asked both men to stay for a public demonstration rather than offer competing slogans. Each agreed to speak only on matters he understood. The crowd was disappointed by the lack of a simple answer, but many stayed to listen anyway.

隔天早晨，人群在正門附近集會，一方要求立即關閉，另一個對立團體要求毫無限制地供電。委員會努力尋找能通過、又不壓垮任何一方的方案。擬議補助將協助小商家購買低熱設備，工程師也會改造老高塔。安知解釋為何同時關閉全部機械會讓病人陷入危險。葉青說明為何全部維持運轉又會摧毀苔草走廊。市長請兩人留在公開示範場，而非喊彼此競爭的口號。兩人都同意只談自己理解的事情。人群因沒有簡單答案而失望，但仍有許多人留下傾聽。

For An the public test brought a strange thrill. He had once enjoyed applause when a successful machine opened; now he found greater satisfaction in watching strangers question the design calmly. A wind-school student said tuition had risen partly because of the failed cage program, and she did not want new repairs to undermine the school's scholarship fund. The mayor asked whether a community venture could maintain the new pathways more cheaply than a distant contractor. Gardeners, engineers, shopkeepers, and animal keepers offered their skills. By evening the committee had approved a six-month pilot, with measurements reviewed in public every thirty days. Ye took the calf to the orchard meadow, where the older juvenile was waiting. Above them a low call rolled across the city, so close that every leaf seemed to tremble.

對安知而言，公開測試帶來一種陌生的興奮。他過去喜歡成功機械啟用時的掌聲，如今卻在陌生人平靜質疑設計時獲得更大滿足。風學校一名學生說，學費上漲部分源自失敗籠舍計畫，她不希望新維修再破壞獎學金。市長問，社區合作事業是否能比遠方承包商更便宜地維護新通道。園丁、工程師、店主與動物照護員都提出自己的技能。傍晚前，委員會核准六個月試辦，並每三十天公開檢視測量值。葉青帶幼獸到果園草甸，少年獸已在等待。兩者頭頂，低沉呼喚掠過城市，近得彷彿每片葉子都在顫抖。
