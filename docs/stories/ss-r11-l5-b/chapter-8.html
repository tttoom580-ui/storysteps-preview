三個音之間的靜默

The Three-Note Silence

雲獸不肯飛：兩個凡人的春天 · 第 8 / 25 章 · 4000–5000 · 英中對照 · 校訂中

At Starfall Town a visiting musician demonstrated an alien instrument whose notes sounded almost like distant cloud-beast calls. The market fell into chaos when the calf answered and tried to run toward the stage. Ye caught the loose walking strap but did not drag the animal back. He waited for it to settle near the fountain, then explained to the crowd that sound was not simply entertainment for every species. The musician listened with unexpected comprehension. He said the festival permit did not entitle him to frighten an animal, even by accident. An examined the instrument and found that its hollow pipes carried one note much farther than the others. He asked whether the performer would be willing to change the piece, not cancel the concert. The musician agreed, provided the audience could hear why.

在星落鎮，一名來訪樂手展示了一種陌生樂器，音色幾乎像遠處雲獸的呼喚。幼獸回應並朝舞臺奔去時，市場頓時混亂。葉青抓住鬆開的牽行帶，卻沒有硬把動物拖回來。他等牠在噴泉旁安定下來，才向人群解釋：對每個物種而言，聲音並不只是娛樂。樂手出乎意料地理解了。他說，即使無意，節慶許可也沒有賦予他驚嚇動物的權利。安知檢查樂器，發現其中一根中空管能把聲音傳得比其他音遠。他問表演者願不願意改編曲子，而不是取消演奏。樂手同意，但希望觀眾能了解原因。

The evening gathering moved from the central square to a sheltered garden, where the group could adjust its instruments with greater flexibility. Ye wanted to minimize sudden sounds, while An proposed that musicians leave a long interval between the three lowest notes. An old myth claimed cloud beasts danced for people who played perfectly, but a shepherd showed them a ragged notebook describing something different: the animals answered only when they recognized an elder's call. At a small salon behind the garden, performers tried a piece built around pauses rather than imitation. Its quietest passages made the audience lean forward. No animal was brought onto the stage, and no one demanded a demonstration. Ye realized that an honest performance could make room for another creature's silence.

晚間聚會從中央廣場移到有遮蔭的花園，樂團能更有彈性地調整樂器。葉青希望將突如其來的聲響降到最低，安知則提議讓三個最低音之間保留較長停頓。一個古老神話說，人類若演奏得完美，雲獸便會為他們起舞；但牧羊人拿出一本破舊筆記，記載的是另一回事：雲獸只會在辨認出長輩呼喚時回應。花園後方的小型沙龍裡，表演者嘗試以停頓而非模仿為主的樂曲。最安靜的樂段反而讓觀眾向前傾身。沒有動物被帶上臺，也沒有人要求展示。葉青明白，誠實的演出可以為另一種生命的沉默留下空間。

The town faced a shortage of healthy moss, and the instruments alone could not solve that problem. An mapped the sound spectrum near the old theater, then helped the mayor choose a strategic place to stall traffic during the evening performance. The temporary restriction would protect a narrow strip of flowers without shutting the whole town. A baker offered to feed the volunteers, while shopkeepers worried about losing customers. The mayor agreed to publish a clear timetable and compensate vendors who had already paid to use the closed street. Ye supported the plan only after confirming that the calf could reach a quiet meadow without passing the crowd. He was learning that useful cooperation often involved people whose main concerns had little to do with animals.

小鎮缺少健康苔草，僅靠樂器無法解決問題。安知測量舊劇院附近的聲音頻譜，協助鎮長選擇一處適當位置，在晚間表演時策略性地暫緩車流。臨時管制能保護狹窄花帶，卻不必讓整座城停擺。麵包師願意供應志工餐食，店主則擔心流失顧客。鎮長答應公布清楚時間表，補償已付費使用封閉道路的攤販。葉青在確認幼獸能不經人群走到安靜草甸後，才支持方案。他正逐漸明白，真正有用的合作常涉及主要關心的事與動物毫不相干的人。

A council member asked whether they should suspend the festival entirely until the migration ended. An answered that the proposal was theoretical unless someone could explain where hundreds of performers and visitors would go. Instead, the musicians prepared a tribute to the valley's workers, using hand drums to imitate rainfall on leaves rather than a cloud beast's voice. Ye noticed a thin vein of silver moss spreading across the damp stone near the theater. The plant could not replace a meadow, but it suggested that the street was not beyond repair. He invited the council member to inspect it in daylight. The woman came, touched the stone gently, and asked what the town might plant next year. Their debate moved away from deciding who should be silenced.

一名議員問，是否應暫停整個慶典直到遷徙結束。安知回答，若無法說明數百名表演者和旅客該去何處，這提議便只是理論。樂手們改為準備一首向山谷工作者致敬的曲子，用手鼓模擬雨落葉片的聲音，而非雲獸叫聲。葉青注意到劇院旁潮濕石面上延伸出一道細細的銀色苔脈。它無法代替草甸，卻顯示街道並非完全無法修復。他邀議員白天來看。女子真的來了，輕輕摸過石頭，詢問明年鎮上可以種些什麼。辯論從此不再只圍繞著應讓誰閉嘴。

Before departure An learned that the next province required a travel visa for visitors bringing protected animals across the border. He could not think of a single reason why the calf should have to prove its value to enter a place its ancestors had crossed for centuries. The official at the desk said she had no authority whatsoever to ignore the form, but she helped him locate an exemption for animals traveling without commercial owners. Ye completed the health record honestly, including the days when the calf had been frightened and refused food. At night the three-note composition filled the garden. The calf listened from its meadow and did not answer, which Ye considered a success. The musicians had learned to perform without asking the animal to become part of their show.

出發前，安知得知下一省要求攜帶受保護動物的訪客申辦旅行簽證。他想不出一個理由，為何幼獸要證明自己的價值，才能進入祖先數百年來自由穿越的地方。櫃臺人員表示她完全無權略過表格，卻幫他找到一項適用於沒有商業主人的遷徙動物的豁免。葉青誠實填寫健康紀錄，包括幼獸因受驚而拒食的日子。夜裡，三音樂曲在花園響起。幼獸在草甸上聆聽，沒有回應；葉青認為那是成功。樂手終於學會表演，而不要求動物成為節目的一部分。
