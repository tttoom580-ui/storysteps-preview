誰決定巡演路線

A Vote About the Route

把星空借給每一個人 · 第 2 / 25 章 · 2000–3000 · 英中對照

用互動版查詞、朗讀與收藏

By the time the volunteers reached the adult college meeting room, folding chairs were already waiting beneath the evening sky. Rui An, twenty-eight, led the tour because she believed that learning about stars could belong to ordinary people, not only to those who could buy tickets. Jonas Qiao, thirty-one, had designed the light that made their small canvas dome seem immense. They had argued about timing before, but never about the value of a clear exit. Then they learned that the first proposed route passed only through rich neighborhoods. Rui asked the group not to hide the problem behind a smiling advertisement. She placed a blank sheet beside the entrance, ready for facts, questions, and corrections. A night's success would depend on what people could honestly decide together.

志工抵達成人學院的會議室時，折疊椅早已在暮色中排好。二十八歲的芮安帶領這場巡演，因為她相信星空知識應該屬於一般人，不該只有買得起門票的人才能接近。三十一歲的喬森設計了燈具，使小小的帆布帳篷看起來像無邊天空。他們曾為時程爭論，卻不會拿安全出口妥協。然而，大家得知：原定巡演路線只經過富裕的社區。芮安要求不要把問題藏在微笑的廣告後面。她在入口擺一張白紙，準備記下事實、問題和更正。一夜是否成功，取決於眾人能否誠實地共同決定。

The next set of four prompts was "beast", "bowling", "budget", and "decade". Jonas moved the lamp a little lower so that the cards could be read without forcing people to stand. One card about beast brought two different memories together. A volunteer discussed bowling through a concrete detail, not a guess. A neighbor talked about budget in a story about the town. A listener remembered decade from an ordinary day. Some visitors wanted to turn the words into a single cheerful story. Others found the same cards difficult.

下一組四個提示是beast（野獸）、bowling（保齡球）、budget（預算）、decade（十年）。喬森把燈調低一些，讓大家不必站起來也能讀清楚卡片。一張關於「野獸」的卡片連起兩段不同回憶。志工把「保齡球」和具體細節連結，而不是靠猜測。一名鄰居把「預算」放入小鎮故事。一位聽眾想起日常生活裡的「十年」。有些訪客想把這些詞寫成單一的快樂故事，其他人卻覺得同樣的卡片很難面對。

Four more words arrived from the visitors' notebooks: "drunk", "expressive", "frighten", and "geography". Rui listened for what the speakers wanted to keep, rather than for a line that would sound clever in an advertisement. An adult remembered a drunk driver and asked for a safe ride home. A speaker asked whether the word expressive described their memory fairly. Someone asked when it would be right to frighten. One card about geography brought two different memories together. An older neighbor explained one card through a small object that could be passed from hand to hand. A younger person offered a different scene, and the two were allowed to disagree.

訪客筆記裡又出現四個詞：drunk（喝醉的）、expressive（善於表達的）、frighten（使害怕）、geography（地理位置）。芮安在意的是講述者想保留什麼，而不是能否摘出適合廣告的漂亮台詞。一名成年人想起酒駕者，提醒大家安排安全返家方式。有人稱某段回憶是「善於表達的」，並說明原因。有人思考什麼時候該「使害怕」，又該何時停下。一張關於「地理位置」的卡片連起兩段不同回憶。一位年長鄰居用能在手中傳遞的小物件解釋其中一張卡，年輕人則提出不同的場景；兩人被允許保留分歧。

The small team found a point they could actually test: a ten-year-old map ignored villages beyond the hills. Rui asked Jonas to describe the limit of the evidence before they discussed a solution. He did not pretend one measurement answered every question. Instead, they invited a local volunteer to observe the same test. Only after that did Rui asked residents to vote on locations after examining travel costs. A few visitors were surprised to hear an organizer say that she did not know everything. Rui welcomed the doubt. If a child could challenge the explanation without being laughed at, then adults could challenge the budget and the safety plan too. They wrote the findings on the public board, even the parts that did not flatter the project.

小組找到一項真正可以檢驗的線索：一張十年前的地圖漏掉山丘另一側的村落。芮安先請喬森說明證據的限制，再討論解法。他沒有假裝一次測量就能回答所有問題，而是邀請當地志工見證相同測試。之後，芮安請居民先檢查交通成本再投票決定站點。有些訪客驚訝地聽到主辦者承認自己並非無所不知。芮安歡迎質疑。若孩子可以提出問題而不被嘲笑，大人也應能質疑預算與安全計畫。他們把結果寫上公開板，連那些不利於專案形象的部分也保留下來。

The last cards of this round were "greedy", "kit", "lace", and "necktie". Rui kept the circle open even when the words did not seem to belong to the same story. A speaker asked whether the word greedy described their memory fairly. A listener remembered kit from an ordinary day. One card about lace brought two different memories together. A volunteer discussed necktie through a concrete detail, not a guess. One speaker described a scene from work; another remembered a journey, while a third chose to leave the card unanswered. Rui crossed out no one's silence.

這一輪最後幾張卡寫著greedy（貪婪的）、kit（工具組）、lace（蕾絲）、necktie（領帶）。即使這些字似乎不屬於同一個故事，芮安仍讓故事圈保持開放。有人稱某段回憶是「貪婪的」，並說明原因。一位聽眾想起日常生活裡的「工具組」。一張關於「蕾絲」的卡片連起兩段不同回憶。志工把「領帶」和具體細節連結，而不是靠猜測。有人說了工作中的場景，有人想起一段旅程，第三個人則選擇不回答。芮安沒有抹去任何人的沉默。

Rui opened four prompts from the community word box. The cards read "passenger", "permit", "professor", and "regret". She asked why a single word could make two people remember different nights. A listener remembered passenger from an ordinary day. One card about permit brought two different memories together. A volunteer discussed professor through a concrete detail, not a guess. A neighbor talked about regret in a story about the town. The words did not explain the sky by themselves. They became useful only when a real person placed them next to something seen, heard, or tested.

芮安從社區詞語盒打開四張提示卡，寫著passenger（乘客）、permit（允許）、professor（教授）、regret（後悔）。她問大家，為什麼同一個字會讓兩個人想起不同的夜晚。一位聽眾想起日常生活裡的「乘客」。一張關於「允許」的卡片連起兩段不同回憶。志工把「教授」和具體細節連結，而不是靠猜測。一名鄰居把「後悔」放入小鎮故事。那些字本身無法解釋天空。只有真人把它們放在親眼看過、親耳聽見或實際查證的事情旁邊，它們才有用。

Success was still uncertain. The sharpest objection was that the sponsor offered money if its favored town stayed first. Rui did not dismiss the worry merely because it came from someone powerful. She separated its real cost from the demand hidden inside it. Then she kept the vote public and refused the private condition. Jonas helped with tools and timings, but he left the final public explanation to the person who had promised the audience a fair program. Later, when they folded the chairs together, he admitted that he had once mistaken agreement for affection. Rui told him she preferred a partner who could argue carefully and still listen. Neither offered a grand promise. The next stop would test them both.

事情是否成功仍不確定。最尖銳的反對聲是：贊助商表示只要把它指定的小鎮排第一就會出資。芮安沒有因為這話來自權勢者就直接否定，而是把真正的成本與藏在其中的要求分開。然後，她維持公開投票並拒絕私下條件。喬森協助工具與時程，卻把公開說明留給曾答應觀眾公平待遇的芮安。稍後兩人一起收椅子時，他承認自己過去曾把意見一致誤當成好感。芮安告訴他，她更喜歡能好好爭論、也願意聽的人。兩人都沒許下宏大的誓言；下一站還會考驗他們。

At the second story circle, the volunteers turned over four cards marked "risk", "semester", "visible", and "voter". Nobody was asked for a perfect definition or a personal secret. One card about risk brought two different memories together. A volunteer discussed semester through a concrete detail, not a guess. A speaker asked whether the word visible described their memory fairly. A listener remembered voter from an ordinary day. The talk moved from easy memories to questions about who was allowed to speak and who would carry the results home. Rui reminded the group that permission could change tomorrow.

在第二輪故事圈，志工翻出四張卡，分別寫著risk（風險）、semester（學期）、visible（看得見的）、voter（投票者）。沒有人被要求說出完美的定義或私人祕密。一張關於「風險」的卡片連起兩段不同回憶。志工把「學期」和具體細節連結，而不是靠猜測。有人稱某段回憶是「看得見的」，並說明原因。一位聽眾想起日常生活裡的「投票者」。對話從輕鬆回憶轉向誰有權發言，以及結果由誰帶回家。芮安提醒大家，同意可以在明天改變。
