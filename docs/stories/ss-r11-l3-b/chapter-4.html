The Red Cable

把星空借給每一個人 · 第 4 / 25 章 · 2000–3000 · 英中對照

用互動版查詞、朗讀與收藏

At the old hilltop lookout, Rui An arrived before sunset with a portable star dome, a box of paper story cards, and two open doors to keep clear. At twenty-eight, she had spent a year saving for this traveling exhibition. Jonas Qiao, the thirty-one-year-old engineer who built its turning lamp, checked each support instead of praising the view. Tonight's immediate difficulty was clear: the proposed entrance crossed a dark service alley. Rui wrote the problem where every helper could see it. She separated the facts they had checked from the claims they had merely heard. This was meant to be a free show, not a secret test of visitors.

在山頂的舊瞭望台，芮安於日落前帶著移動式星空帳篷、一盒紙製故事卡和必須保持暢通的兩個出入口抵達。二十八歲的她花了一年為這場巡迴展覽存錢。三十一歲、設計旋轉燈具的機械師喬森逐一檢查支架，而不是忙著讚美風景。今晚的困難很清楚：預定入口必須穿過一條漆黑的維修巷。芮安把問題寫在所有志工看得見的地方，將已查證的事實與只聽說的傳言分開。這應該是免費展覽，而不是觀眾不知情的測試。

The last cards of this round were "alley", "bravery", "cable", and "cherry". Rui kept the circle open even when the words did not seem to belong to the same story. A neighbor talked about alley in a story about the town. A listener remembered bravery from an ordinary day. One card about cable brought two different memories together. A volunteer discussed cherry through a concrete detail, not a guess. The talk moved from easy memories to questions about who was allowed to speak and who would carry the results home. Rui reminded the group that permission could change tomorrow.

這一輪最後幾張卡寫著alley（巷弄）、bravery（勇氣）、cable（電纜）、cherry（櫻桃）。即使這些字似乎不屬於同一個故事，芮安仍讓故事圈保持開放。一名鄰居把「巷弄」放入小鎮故事。一位聽眾想起日常生活裡的「勇氣」。一張關於「電纜」的卡片連起兩段不同回憶。志工把「櫻桃」和具體細節連結，而不是靠猜測。對話從輕鬆回憶轉向誰有權發言，以及結果由誰帶回家。芮安提醒大家，同意可以在明天改變。

Rui opened four prompts from the community word box. The cards read "crash", "definition", "dizzy", and "investigate". She asked why a single word could make two people remember different nights. A listener remembered crash from an ordinary day. One card about definition brought two different memories together. A visitor said dizzy did not mean the same thing to every family. A listener asked what it meant to investigate in an everyday choice. Some visitors wanted to turn the words into a single cheerful story. Others found the same cards difficult.

芮安從社區詞語盒打開四張提示卡，寫著crash（撞擊）、definition（定義）、dizzy（頭暈的）、investigate（調查）。她問大家，為什麼同一個字會讓兩個人想起不同的夜晚。一位聽眾想起日常生活裡的「撞擊」。一張關於「定義」的卡片連起兩段不同回憶。一名訪客說「頭暈的」對每個家庭不一定相同。一名聽眾問，在日常選擇裡該如何理解「調查」。有些訪客想把這些詞寫成單一的快樂故事，其他人卻覺得同樣的卡片很難面對。

They did not have to guess about everything. An important clue emerged: a red cable hidden beneath the platform had worn through its cover. Jonas repeated the observation with another volunteer watching, then wrote down both the result and the conditions. Rui was tempted to solve the matter herself, but a traveling show could become a private kingdom if every choice belonged to one person. So Jonas isolated the power while Rui moved the audience toward the open square. Their plan left room for someone to disagree. They checked the path, explained the change aloud, and kept a written record that the next town could examine.

並不是每件事都得靠猜測。當平台下方的一條紅色纜線外皮已經磨損，他們找到重要線索。喬森在另一名志工見證下重做觀察，將結果與條件一起記下。芮安原本想自己解決，卻想到如果所有選擇都由她一人決定，巡迴展也會變成私人王國。因此，喬森先切斷電源，芮安則把觀眾引導至開放廣場。他們的計畫容許別人反對。他們檢查路線、公開解釋變動，留下可供下一個小鎮查閱的文字紀錄。

At the second story circle, the volunteers turned over four cards marked "knit", "label", "murder", and "nap". Nobody was asked for a perfect definition or a personal secret. One card about knit brought two different memories together. A volunteer discussed label through a concrete detail, not a guess. A card mentioned murder in a fictional mystery, not in a claim about a real neighbor. A listener remembered nap from an ordinary day. An older neighbor explained one card through a small object that could be passed from hand to hand. A younger person offered a different scene, and the two were allowed to disagree.

在第二輪故事圈，志工翻出四張卡，分別寫著knit（編織）、label（標籤）、murder（謀殺）、nap（打盹、小睡）。沒有人被要求說出完美的定義或私人祕密。一張關於「編織」的卡片連起兩段不同回憶。志工把「標籤」和具體細節連結，而不是靠猜測。有張卡在虛構推理故事裡提到謀殺，並非指控真實的鄰居。一位聽眾想起日常生活裡的「打盹、小睡」。一位年長鄰居用能在手中傳遞的小物件解釋其中一張卡，年輕人則提出不同的場景；兩人被允許保留分歧。

The next set of four prompts was "saucer", "sexy", "solid", and "starve". Jonas moved the lamp a little lower so that the cards could be read without forcing people to stand. A volunteer discussed saucer through a concrete detail, not a guess. A speaker asked whether the word sexy described their memory fairly. Someone wondered whether the situation was truly solid. A volunteer used starve in an example about responsibility. One speaker described a scene from work; another remembered a journey, while a third chose to leave the card unanswered. Rui crossed out no one's silence.

下一組四個提示是saucer（茶碟）、sexy（性感的）、solid（堅固的）、starve（使缺乏）。喬森把燈調低一些，讓大家不必站起來也能讀清楚卡片。志工把「茶碟」和具體細節連結，而不是靠猜測。有人稱某段回憶是「性感的」，並說明原因。有人思考當時的情況是否真的「堅固的」。一名志工用「使缺乏」舉出責任的例子。有人說了工作中的場景，有人想起一段旅程，第三個人則選擇不回答。芮安沒有抹去任何人的沉默。

The difficult choice came after the practical work. Someone pointed out that the landowner wanted the opening to go ahead for photographs. Rui felt the familiar desire to keep everyone pleased; a traveling producer could lose a season of bookings after one angry message. Yet a pleasing answer was not always a fair one. She listened to the people affected and read the written agreement once more. Her response was clear: they postponed the show and published a simple safety notice. Jonas did not make the choice for her. He asked whether she wanted his help carrying it out, and accepted her answer. Outside the dome, stars appeared slowly as the lights went down. Rui liked working beside him, but she liked even more that their work could remain open to criticism.

實際工作完成後，困難的選擇才出現。有人堅持：地主希望為了拍照讓開幕照常進行。芮安又想起自己總希望讓每個人滿意；巡迴策展人可能因一則憤怒訊息失去整季預約。然而討人喜歡的答案不一定公平。她傾聽受影響者的聲音，再讀一次書面協議，最後決定：兩人延後展覽，並公開一份簡明安全公告。喬森沒有替她決定，只問她是否希望自己協助執行，也尊重她的回答。星空帳篷外，燈光變暗，星星緩緩出現。芮安喜歡和他工作，但她更珍惜這份工作仍能接受批評。

Four more words arrived from the visitors' notebooks: "thankful", "tower", "van", and "warmth". Rui listened for what the speakers wanted to keep, rather than for a line that would sound clever in an advertisement. A speaker asked whether the word thankful described their memory fairly. A listener remembered tower from an ordinary day. One card about van brought two different memories together. A volunteer discussed warmth through a concrete detail, not a guess. The words did not explain the sky by themselves. They became useful only when a real person placed them next to something seen, heard, or tested.

訪客筆記裡又出現四個詞：thankful（感謝的）、tower（塔）、van（貨車）、warmth（溫暖）。芮安在意的是講述者想保留什麼，而不是能否摘出適合廣告的漂亮台詞。有人稱某段回憶是「感謝的」，並說明原因。一位聽眾想起日常生活裡的「塔」。一張關於「貨車」的卡片連起兩段不同回憶。志工把「溫暖」和具體細節連結，而不是靠猜測。那些字本身無法解釋天空。只有真人把它們放在親眼看過、親耳聽見或實際查證的事情旁邊，它們才有用。
