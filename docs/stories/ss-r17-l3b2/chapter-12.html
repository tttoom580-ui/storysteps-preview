Not everyone agreed with the plan. A younger observer asked why anyone should trust Celia when the story had already gone wrong once. The question stung, but it was fair. They returned to the evidence instead of arguing about intentions. Celia compared the latest observation with her honest account book, while Ren asked who had recorded each detail.
