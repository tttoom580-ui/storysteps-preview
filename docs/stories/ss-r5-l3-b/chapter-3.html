A Map Made by Walking

兩把鑰匙，同一座舞台 · 第 3 / 25 章 · 2000–3000 · 英中對照

用互動版查詞、朗讀與收藏

Ruo took a small team downtown to ask residents what they actually wanted from the old station. She had prepared questions about oral history, but a shop owner talked about broken fans and expensive repairs instead. One older woman said she missed having a place to sit without being asked to buy something.

若晴帶小隊到市中心，詢問居民真正希望舊車站提供什麼。她準備的是口述歷史問題，店主卻談起壞掉的電風扇和昂貴維修。一位長者說，她想要一個不必消費也能坐下的地方。

Evan followed another route and returned with a diagram of repair needs. His map showed the same blocks where Ruo's team had found social isolation. They laid the two sheets together and noticed that a simple seating area beside a repair desk might help both groups. Nobody called it a merger yet.

亦辰走另一條路，帶回維修需求圖。他的地圖顯示的街區，正是若晴小隊發現居民缺少交流的地方。兩張圖疊在一起，他們發現修理桌旁的一處座位可能同時幫助兩種人。兩人還沒有把它稱作合併。

At the final shop on the route, an owner showed them a chair with a broken leg. Evan could repair it, but the owner said the real problem was having nowhere to sit during market hours. Ruo asked whether a public resting area might help. The owner nodded, then warned that someone would need to keep it clean. Their earlier drawings had shown tables, lights, and walls, not people doing daily work. Ruo added a line for cleaning hours to the plan. Evan noted that the repair lesson should include maintenance rather than only dramatic fixes. They did not solve the neighborhood's needs in one afternoon; they learned which questions had been missing from both proposals.

在路線最後一間店，老闆拿出一張斷腿椅。亦辰能修，但老闆說真正問題是市場營業時間沒有地方坐。若晴問公共休息區是否有幫助，老闆點頭，提醒還需要人維持清潔。他們原來的圖畫了桌子、燈和牆，卻沒畫日常工作的人。若晴在企畫加上清掃時數，亦辰決定維修課也要教保養，而不只示範戲劇性的修復。他們沒有在一個下午解決社區需求，卻找出了兩份企畫共同漏掉的問題。

Their downtown walk began at a bamboo bench. Connie carried a bundle of maps, and the team paused to chat with a ticket dealer beside a chipped table. Ruo insisted on a civil conversation when a visitor demanded payment for a free event. The argument did not deepen. A democratic club, she said, needed to hear why the man was worried before reacting. At lunch they went to dine in a narrow shop and split a doughnut. A pigeon hopped near the window; a child made a mighty leap over a puddle. A short poll revealed that many families wanted free seating more than a grand show. Ruo studied the background of the neighborhood: poverty shaped daily choices, but residents were not defined by poverty alone. For the sake of fairness, their proposal needed substance, not just a cheerful picture.

市中心踏查從竹製長椅旁開始。可妮帶著一捆地圖，團隊停下和破損桌旁的售票員聊天。一名訪客要求免費活動收費，若晴堅持文明討論。爭論沒有惡化；民主的社團應先理解擔心，再作反應。午餐時大家在窄小店裡吃飯、分食甜甜圈。鴿子在窗邊跳動，一個孩子有力地躍過水窪。簡短民調顯示，許多家庭更想要免費座位而非盛大演出。若晴研究社區背景：貧困影響日常選擇，但不能只用貧困定義居民。為了公平，企畫必須有實質內容，而非只是一張開朗照片。

A small chip had fallen from the table, so Evan put it in a labeled bag for repair. Ruo waited to see how residents would react before drawing a conclusion from their survey.

桌子掉下一小塊碎片，亦辰把它放進有標籤的袋子準備修補。若晴等待居民如何反應，再對調查下結論。

A boy at a free workshop asked if the stories would only celebrate successful people. Ruo said she wanted to include mistakes and hard choices, too. The boy pointed at an empty box labeled QUESTIONS. His grandmother could not read the small print, so he offered to help rewrite the sign.

免費工作坊裡，一個男孩問故事是否只會稱讚成功者。若晴說，她也想保留犯錯和艱難選擇。男孩指著標示「問題」的空箱子。他的祖母看不清小字，便主動提議一起重寫標牌。

That night Ruo changed a full page of her proposal. It was painful to remove an elegant introduction, but a plan built from her own imagination alone would not belong to the neighborhood. Evan read the revised page and returned it with one note: Ask who will clean the room afterward.

當晚若晴改掉企畫書整整一頁。刪去漂亮的引言很心痛，但只靠她想像的計畫不會真正屬於社區。亦辰讀完修訂稿，留下唯一一條註記：「先問活動結束後誰清掃。」

The map exercise ended at a bus stop where two elderly residents waited in the sun. One said the old station was beautiful but had never felt like a place for her. The other asked whether a quiet room would be available when the festival grew noisy. Ruo added both questions to her notes and gave them the public meeting date. Evan checked the sidewalk and found that the nearest crossing was missing a clear sign. He could not change the road by himself, so he wrote a request to the responsible office. Ruo was pleased that he did not promise a quick solution simply to sound helpful. On the ride home she read her earlier proposal and noticed how often it said 'we will give.' The residents had not asked her to give them an identity. They wanted to use a room and make choices in it. She changed the first sentence of her plan to begin with those choices.

地圖活動在公車站結束，兩位長者站在陽光下等車。一人說舊車站很美，卻從不覺得是自己能使用的地方；另一人問節慶變吵時有沒有安靜室。若晴記下兩個問題，告知公開會議日期。亦辰查看人行道，發現最近的路口少了清楚標誌。他不能自己改道路，便寫信給負責單位。若晴欣賞他沒有為了顯得有幫助而隨便承諾。回程車上，她重讀企畫書，發現自己太常寫「我們將提供」。居民沒有要求她賦予他們某種身分，只想使用房間並自己選擇。她把企畫第一句改成從居民的選擇出發。

The maps remained on the table after the students left. Ruo's was full of voices; Evan's was full of objects and pathways. Connie placed a plain sheet beside them for tasks neither map contained. It was nearly empty. They all knew it would not stay that way for long.

學生離開後，地圖仍攤在桌上。若晴的滿是聲音，亦辰的滿是物件和動線。可妮在旁邊放白紙，記兩張圖都漏掉的工作。紙幾乎空白，但大家知道不會一直如此。
