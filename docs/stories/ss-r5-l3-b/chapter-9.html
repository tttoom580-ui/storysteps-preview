少了一欄的數字

A Page of Missing Numbers

兩把鑰匙，同一座舞台 · 第 9 / 25 章 · 2000–3000 · 英中對照 · 校訂完成

Connie discovered that the original grant form asked for an estimated number of visitors with disabilities, but Simon's printed example had left that box blank. Some applicants had copied his form without noticing. Ruo and Evan checked the council's official file and found no change to the requirement.

可妮發現正式補助表格要求估算有障礙的訪客人數，但賽門提供的範例把那欄留白。有些申請者照抄而沒注意。若晴和亦辰核對議會的正式檔案，確定要求並未改變。

They did not accuse Simon of forgery. His sample was incomplete, which was enough reason not to use it. Ruo drafted a clear public correction. Evan made a simple spreadsheet that showed which numbers were estimates and which came from recorded visits. Both documents could be checked by anyone.

兩人沒有指控賽門偽造。他的範本確實不完整，這就足以不用。若晴寫清楚的公開更正，亦辰做簡單表格，分開標示估計值與實際紀錄。兩份文件都可讓任何人核對。

Connie made a rule for every number in the application: it needed a source, a date, and a label saying whether it was observed or estimated. Ruo tested the rule on her own visitor forecast and found three figures she could not explain. She deleted them. Evan had to reduce his repair count because several objects had been checked but not fixed. The totals looked less impressive afterward, yet they could withstand ordinary questions. Helen read the revised chart and asked whether the low estimate meant the organizers expected fewer older visitors. Ruo answered that the estimate described the trial size, not who was welcome. They changed the chart title to make that difference clear.

可妮訂下每個數字都要有來源、日期與「觀察或估計」標籤的規則。若晴用自己的訪客預測測試，發現三項數字說不出依據，便刪掉。亦辰也減少「完成修理」數量，因為幾件物品只是檢查，尚未修好。總數不再漂亮，卻經得起普通提問。海倫讀修訂表，問低估計是否代表主辦預期較少長者。若晴解釋，那是試辦規模，不代表歡迎哪些人；他們修改圖表標題以免誤會。

A weekly meeting brought unexpected laughter when a radio tune sounded like a bear's roar. In the museum cabin, a horn from an old vehicle sat beside a disabled weapon prop. Connie entered details into a database. A visitor asked if a clip about democracy could be played, but the owner had not granted permission. Ruo said a missing credit was not a small matter even if the speaker was a skillful performer. A child made the sound system sink into silence by pressing the wrong button. Nobody needed to faint with fright; Evan reset it shortly afterward. An older lover of music told them that his partner used to wander through the station every Saturday. Ruo heard the story without treating his grief as entertainment. The report recorded what happened, including the risk that an untested display could bore visitors or make them splash water near cables.

每週會議上，收音機曲調像熊的吼聲，引起意外笑聲。博物館小室裡，舊車號角放在無法使用的武器道具旁。可妮把細節輸入資料庫。一名訪客想播放民主紀錄短片，但沒有得到片主授權。若晴說，即使表演者技巧再好，漏掉署名也不是小事。孩子按錯按鈕，音響頓時沉寂。誰也不必嚇昏，亦辰不久就重設完成。一位熱愛音樂的長者說，過去伴侶每週六都會在車站漫步。若晴傾聽，沒有把他的悲傷當成娛樂。報告也記錄不良測試可能讓參觀者無聊，或在電線旁潑到水。

An old first-aid poster warned that even a small cut might bleed if a child touched sharp metal. Evan covered the edges of the historical exhibit and moved the poster beside the safety desk.

舊急救海報提醒孩子碰尖銳金屬時，小傷口也可能流血。亦辰包好歷史展的邊緣，並把海報移到安全桌旁。

At a weekly meeting, one volunteer asked if accessible design would be too costly for a small event. Helen answered that a visitor should not have to pay extra merely to enter the same room. The budget could be smaller, she said, but the basic welcome could not be optional.

每週會議上，一名志工問小活動負擔得起無障礙設計嗎。海倫回答，訪客不該為進入同一個空間而額外付費。預算可以縮小，但基本的歡迎不能變成選配。

Ruo placed the two corrected forms side by side. Evan noticed that she had credited his spreadsheet without putting his name under her club. They had learned to share a project without treating either member's work as a gift that needed no acknowledgment.

若晴把兩份修正表並排放好。亦辰發現她替他的表格署名，沒有把它掛在自己社團名下。他們逐漸學會共享企畫，而不是把別人的付出當成不需承認的禮物。

At the end of the form workshop, Ruo invited volunteers to explain one number in plain words. The youngest member chose the estimated attendance figure and immediately realized that an estimate might be mistaken for a promise. Connie added a note describing the method. Evan explained the difference between checking an object and completing a repair, then corrected his count. Ruo showed how a public history exhibit could be visited more than once by the same person. They made a sample table with fewer columns and clearer headings. Simon's colorful form still looked more exciting, but the council's official rules demanded evidence. Ruo no longer thought careful work was the opposite of imagination. A well-made record could free the team to tell a richer story, because readers would know which parts were facts and which were hopes.

表格工作坊結束時，若晴請志工用白話解釋一個數字。最年輕成員挑了預估人數，立刻發現估計容易被誤當承諾。可妮加上計算方法。亦辰說明檢查物品和完成維修不同，修正自己的數量。若晴示範同一訪客可能多次參觀歷史展。他們做一張欄位較少、標題更清楚的樣表。賽門的彩色版仍較精彩，但議會正式規則要求證據。若晴不再認為謹慎與想像相反。做好紀錄反而能讓團隊說出更豐富的故事，因為讀者分得清事實與期待。

Connie placed the corrected form where every volunteer could find it. The sample was not beautiful, but nobody had to guess which number was an estimate. Ruo wrote a date at the top. Evan wrote the version beneath it. Both names remained on the page.

可妮把修正表放在志工人人能找到的地方。範例不漂亮，卻不必猜哪些數字只是估計。若晴在上方寫日期，亦辰在下面註版本。兩人的名字都留在紙上。
