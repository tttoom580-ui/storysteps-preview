安全紀錄不是表演

A Safety Record, Not a Show

兩把鑰匙，同一座舞台 · 第 11 / 25 章 · 2000–3000 · 英中對照 · 校訂中

用互動版查詞、朗讀與收藏

Evan arranged a second technical test in the hall. Ruo brought residents to observe but made clear that they were not there to approve equipment they did not understand. A qualified electrician checked the main circuits. Volunteers recorded only the tasks they had been trained to perform.

亦辰安排第二次技術測試。若晴邀居民旁聽，也清楚說明他們不必替不懂的器材背書。合格電工檢查主迴路，志工只記錄自己受過訓練的工作。

When an old lamp failed, Evan turned off the supply before anyone touched it. Simon's crew wanted to continue filming a promotional video. Evan refused. Ruo supported him and asked Connie to write the interruption into the official test log. An unfinished film was safer than a hidden failure.

舊燈具故障時，亦辰先切斷電源，才讓人靠近。賽門的拍攝團隊想繼續錄宣傳影片，亦辰拒絕。若晴支持他，請可妮將中斷寫入正式測試紀錄。沒拍完的影片，總比隱瞞故障安全。

During the power test, the entire crew watched the technician work without crowding him. Ruo noticed how Evan translated each step into language a newcomer could follow. When the technician paused, Evan did not fill the silence with guesses. He asked what had been tested and what still needed inspection. A junior volunteer asked whether the video team could use a close shot of the damaged lamp. The technician said yes after power was disconnected, but no one should touch it for the camera. Ruo wrote those limits into the filming notice. Safety had to be part of the story they told, not an interruption hidden between exciting images.

電力測試時，全隊在不妨礙技師的距離觀看。若晴注意亦辰如何把每個步驟轉成新手聽得懂的話。技師停下時，亦辰沒有用猜測填滿沉默，而是詢問已測試和待檢查項目。年輕志工問攝影團隊能否拍壞燈特寫。技師說切斷電源後可以拍，但不能為鏡頭去碰它。若晴把界線寫進拍攝通知。安全必須成為他們要說的故事之一，而不是被刺激畫面遮住的中斷。

The electrician gave an accurate account of the operation. He inspected the loose cable, a small freezer, and a container once used for gasoline. The fire rules were strict because even intelligent volunteers could make mistakes under pressure. A member of the local media asked to perform the demonstration, but the electrician said only qualified workers could do that part. Evan estimated the distance roughly, then measured it properly. He noted which timber shelf needed replacement and which plastic case could remain. Ruo helped move chairs, feeling her arm muscle ache and sweat collect under her collar. A student's theory about a violet light was interesting but untested. The expert did not bless a guess as proof. The building's rural county records were available for review, but a ditch behind the hall would weaken any unsafe temporary foundation. Nobody would dive into the test without permission.

電工準確說明了這次作業。他檢查鬆動電纜、小冷凍庫和曾裝過汽油的容器。防火規定嚴格，是因為再聰明的志工也可能在壓力下出錯。在地媒體成員想親自操作示範，電工說這部分只能由合格人員進行。亦辰先粗估距離，再正式測量。他記下哪片木架需要換、哪個塑膠箱可留。若晴幫忙搬椅子，感到手臂肌肉痠痛、衣領下冒汗。一名學生提出紫光理論，雖有趣卻尚未驗證。專家不會把猜測當成證據。郡級鄉村紀錄可供查閱，但大廳後的排水溝會削弱任何不安全的臨時地基。沒有人能未經許可就衝進測試。

The teaching team chose a medium-sized display that residents could read while seated. Its height mattered more than whether the photograph would appear large in the newspaper.

教學團隊選用中等大小、坐著也能看清的展板。高度是否合宜，比報紙照片拍起來夠不夠大更重要。

At lunch they reviewed who could sign each section of the inspection form. Evan could report what he observed but could not replace the electrician's certificate. Ruo could describe accessibility feedback but would not claim to be a building engineer. Their written limits made the report stronger.

午餐時他們討論檢查表誰有權簽署。亦辰可以回報觀察，不能取代電工證明；若晴可以描述無障礙回饋，不能自稱建築工程師。明確寫出能力邊界，反而讓報告更可靠。

That evening Ruo told Evan she had been impressed by his refusal to continue filming. He answered that he was equally impressed when she corrected a mistake in public. They were learning each other's character through unglamorous choices, not through promises made under bright lights.

當晚若晴說，她很欣賞亦辰拒絕繼續拍攝。亦辰說，他同樣欣賞她願意公開更正錯誤。他們正從不華麗的選擇認識彼此，而不是從聚光燈下的承諾。

When the video crew left, one volunteer said the lost footage had ruined the day's promotion. Evan asked what could still be shown truthfully. They had photographs of the safe setup, the completed measurements, and the electrician's signed notes. Ruo suggested a short public explanation of why filming stopped. Connie wrote a first draft that did not blame the cameraman or invent an excuse. It said the power test had revealed an issue, work had paused, and qualified staff were reviewing it. The note was less glamorous than a dramatic video, but it let future volunteers understand the decision. A student asked whether they might retry the demonstration next week. Evan answered that the answer depended on the repair and its inspection, not the publicity calendar. Ruo felt proud of his patience.

攝影團隊離開後，志工說少了影片，當天宣傳就毀了。亦辰問還有哪些內容可以如實公開。他們有安全布置的照片、完成的測量和電工簽名紀錄。若晴建議寫短說明，交代拍攝為何停止。可妮草擬文字，沒有責怪攝影師，也沒編藉口，只寫電力測試發現問題、工作暫停、合格人員正在檢查。說明不如戲劇影片華麗，卻讓未來志工懂得決策理由。學生問下週能否重拍，亦辰回答取決於維修和檢查，而不是宣傳行事曆。若晴為他的耐心感到驕傲。

The test log ended with a clear line: power remained off until the qualified inspection. Ruo signed beneath it as a witness, not as a technical authority. Evan thanked her for understanding the difference. The distinction made their shared work safer.

測試紀錄最後清楚寫著：合格檢查完成前持續斷電。若晴在下方以見證人身分簽名，而不是裝作技術權威。亦辰感謝她理解差別。這種區分讓共同工作更安全。
