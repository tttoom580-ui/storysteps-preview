議會的第一個問題

The Council's First Question

兩把鑰匙，同一座舞台 · 第 12 / 25 章 · 2000–3000 · 英中對照 · 校訂中

用互動版查詞、朗讀與收藏

At the first public review, the council asked whether the clubs had already agreed to combine. Ruo said no. They had shared tests and drawings, but their constitutions required a member vote before a formal joint application. Evan presented the same answer, even though it left the judges uncertain.

第一次公開審查上，議會問兩社是否已同意合併。若晴說沒有。他們共享測試和圖面，但章程規定必須先由社員投票，才能正式聯合申請。亦辰也給出相同答案，儘管這讓評審還無法確定。

A councillor asked why the application included accounts of local families beside repair instructions. Ruo explained that a broken household object could carry a memory, while a story could lead people to ask for practical help. Evan demonstrated this with an old radio repaired beside a recorded recollection of its owner.

一名議員問，為何申請案同時收錄家庭故事與維修教學。若晴說，壞掉的家用品也可能承載記憶，故事則可能讓人願意尋求實際幫助。亦辰展示一台修好的舊收音機，旁邊搭配物主授權的回憶錄音。

Ruo had worried that the council would see one project as soft and the other as serious. The hearing surprised her. A councillor asked Evan about the memories attached to objects; another asked Ruo whether her displays had practical safety rules. Each found themselves answering a question usually meant for the other. Neither borrowed expertise they did not possess. They showed how the two fields could strengthen each other while keeping separate standards. Afterward Connie pointed out that the judges had not praised their romance or their personalities. They had asked about the work. Ruo was relieved. She wanted the plan to stand on its own when she and Evan were not in the room.

若晴原以為議會會把一個企畫看成溫情、另一個看成專業，聽證卻出乎意料。議員問亦辰物品承載的記憶，也問若晴展示的安全規則。兩人都回答了原以為會問另一人的問題，但沒有假裝懂不熟悉的專業。他們展示兩個領域如何互補，又保留各自標準。會後可妮指出，評審沒稱讚兩人的戀情或個性，而是認真問工作。若晴感到放心。她希望即使兩人不在場，企畫仍能站得住。

The visiting captain brought a photograph of a canyon and a boat, not a plan to sail one. He explained how a small bubble might signal an air problem in a model, and how a hatch should be kept clear. Ruo set the example aside when an elderly client asked for information about the station's property records. A student editor could edit the display but not rewrite those records. A sudden flash from the camera made one visitor weep from surprise; the team turned off the light. The school was capable of building a safe educational exhibition about electricity without pretending to run a rescue ship. The monthly report would represent the situation plainly. A hopeful teacher offered spice samples from a travel class, while a child held a foam sword from a history game. Neither object belonged near the electrical table.

到訪船長帶來峽谷與船隻的照片，並不是要大家真的出航。他說明模型裡的小氣泡如何提示空氣問題，以及艙蓋應維持暢通。當一位年長委託人詢問車站產權資料時，若晴把案例先擱到一旁。學生編輯可以編排展示，卻不能改寫紀錄。相機閃光突然亮起，使一名訪客驚得流淚，團隊立即關燈。學校有能力設計安全的電力教育展，不必假裝操作救援船。月報會如實呈現情況。樂觀的教師帶來旅行課的香料樣本，孩子則拿著歷史遊戲的泡棉劍；兩者都不該放在電氣桌旁。

After the review, Simon said he could deliver a higher attendance figure if they accepted his package. Connie asked what evidence supported that figure. Simon produced no survey, only a colorful slide. The clubs thanked him and declined to put a guess into an official application as a fact.

審查後賽門說，只要接受他的整套方案，參加人數可以大幅提高。可妮問有什麼依據。賽門只拿出彩色簡報，沒有調查資料。兩社道謝，但拒絕把猜測當成事實寫進正式申請。

Ruo was disappointed by the uncertain score. Evan reminded her that they had met the same test as everyone else. She replied that fairness did not erase frustration. They sat on the steps until they could laugh about how many forms had followed them into what was supposed to be a festival.

若晴對尚未確定的分數感到失望。亦辰提醒她，他們接受了和其他人相同的審查。她說公平也不會讓挫折消失。兩人坐在階梯上，直到終於能笑談：本來是辦節慶，結果竟被這麼多表格追著跑。

After their council appearance, the presidents received several letters. Some praised their shared plan; others worried that the clubs were becoming too dependent on one another. Ruo posted both kinds of comments for members to read. Evan suggested arranging a meeting where each club could explain which tools, materials, and decisions it would still control. They also invited Helen to correct the accessibility section, but did not ask her to speak for every person with a disability. The meeting ended with a revised responsibility chart and no dramatic applause. Ruo was tired, but she felt better than after meetings where everyone had praised a plan without asking how it would work. They had gained something modest and useful: fewer promises that could not be kept.

議會簡報後，兩位社長收到不少信。有些稱讚聯合案，有些擔心兩社過度依賴。若晴將兩種意見都貼出，供社員閱讀。亦辰提議開會，分別說明各社仍掌管哪些工具、材料與決策。他們也邀海倫修正無障礙段落，但不要求她代表所有障礙者發言。會議結束時有修訂責任圖，沒有戲劇性掌聲。若晴疲倦，卻比以往只聽讚美、不問執行的會議舒服。他們得到一個不華麗卻有用的成果：更少無法履行的承諾。

Ruo kept the council's questions beside her proposal. She had feared they would be asked only who deserved the hall. Instead the review asked what would happen after the first excited visitors went home. That question would follow them into every later plan.

若晴把議會問題留在企畫旁。她原以為評審只問誰值得得到活動廳，結果審查問的是第一批興奮訪客回家後會怎樣。這個問題將陪伴他們之後每一份計畫。
