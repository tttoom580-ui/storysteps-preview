夜市的測試

The Night Market Test

兩把鑰匙，同一座舞台 · 第 10 / 25 章 · 2000–3000 · 英中對照 · 校訂中

用互動版查詞、朗讀與收藏

The clubs tested a small display at the night market. A stall owner agreed to lend power, but asked them not to block his customers. Evan marked the cable route, and Ruo measured how far someone could read the text from a seated position. They kept notes, not a list of excuses.

兩社在夜市測試小型展示。攤商願意借電，條件是不擋住客人。亦辰標出電纜路徑，若晴測量坐著時能從多遠讀清文字。他們記下的是測試結果，不是一串藉口。

Simon offered a glossy display for free if his company logo appeared above the residents' names. Ruo declined. Evan suggested a smaller board on wheels that could be moved without tools. It was less impressive in a photograph, but older visitors could reach it easily.

賽門願意免費提供亮面展板，條件是公司標誌放在居民名字上方。若晴拒絕。亦辰提議做可移動的低矮展板，不需工具就能調整。照片裡不算氣派，長者卻更容易接近。

The walk after the night market took longer than either had planned. Evan told Ruo about his first job fixing appliances, when a customer paid for a repair that did not last. He had learned to explain uncertainty rather than promise perfection. Ruo told him about an exhibit she had designed that visitors admired but could not navigate. She had learned the same lesson in a different field. When they reached a crossing, he asked if he could see her again outside college work. She said yes, provided they kept the clubs' decisions in the clubs. He agreed without asking her to change that condition. Their conversation ended at a corner, leaving room for a beginning.

夜市後的散步比預計久。亦辰談起第一份家電維修工作：一位客人付了錢，修理效果卻沒有持續。他從此學會說明不確定性，而非保證完美。若晴談起曾設計一場人人稱讚、卻難以找到路線的展覽，她在不同工作裡學到同一課。到了路口，他問能否在社務之外再見面。她說可以，前提是社團決策仍由社員決定。他沒有要求改條件，就答應了。對話在轉角結束，也留出開始的空間。

A costume club brought a fashionable bride's coat and a bold hat to the night market. Someone pinned a pearl beside a silk ribbon and asked Ruo for a moral judgment about showing a family's wedding clothes. She determined that consent mattered more than an impressive display. A cook offered melon and fruit punch, then carefully moved the hot stove away from children. On the far table, a small flame showed how the old station lantern once worked. Evan stared at the structure, impressed by its careful design. He used a handkerchief to wipe dust, not a bare hand near the heated metal. Ruo felt relief when the public test ended. Somehow she remained optimistic despite a rising tide of questions. She told Evan that each question was an invitation to build a clearer answer.

服裝社把時髦的新娘外套和大膽帽飾帶到夜市。有人在絲帶旁別上珍珠，問若晴能否展示某家庭的婚禮服飾。她認為同意比華麗展示重要。廚師提供甜瓜和水果調製飲料，接著小心把熱爐移離孩子。遠端桌上小火焰示範車站舊燈的運作。亦辰盯著結構，對細緻設計印象深刻。他用手帕擦灰，不讓手直接接觸熱金屬。公開測試結束時，若晴鬆了一口氣。儘管問題如潮水而來，她不知怎地仍保持樂觀。她告訴亦辰，每個問題都是把答案建得更清楚的邀請。

A student dressed as an angel asked what would determine the costume contest. She wanted to impress the judges, but Ruo said nobody should stare at clothing as a reason to judge a person's worth.

裝扮成天使的學生問服裝比賽用什麼決定勝負。她想讓評審留下印象，若晴卻說不能只盯著衣服，就評斷人的價值。

During the test Ruo saw Evan patiently teach a boy to tighten a loose handle. When the boy succeeded, Evan stepped back and let him explain it to his grandmother. Ruo liked the way Evan gave away credit. She noticed him smile when the grandmother thanked the boy instead of him.

測試期間，若晴看見亦辰耐心教一個男孩鎖緊鬆動把手。男孩成功後，亦辰退後讓他向祖母解說。若晴喜歡他把功勞交出去的方式。祖母謝的是男孩而非亦辰時，他還笑了。

Walking home, they almost held hands. Ruo chose to say what she was thinking instead: she was interested in Evan, but did not want a relationship to settle the clubs' vote. He said he felt the same. Neither asked the other to decide tonight. The streetlights came on as they separated.

走回家時，兩人差點牽起手。若晴選擇說出心裡的話：她對亦辰有好感，卻不希望感情左右社團投票。亦辰說他也一樣。誰都沒要求今晚決定。兩人分開時，街燈正好亮起。

After the market closed, Ruo and Evan stayed to clean the boards they had borrowed. One sign had been scratched, and its owner asked for compensation. Evan checked the condition notes written when the boards were collected. The scratch was already recorded. He did not use the evidence to embarrass the owner. He offered to show the earlier photograph, and the owner apologized for forgetting. Ruo realized that a record could prevent a fight without creating a winner and loser. Evan placed the board carefully against the wall. Then he asked Ruo whether she would still want to have dinner with him if the joint proposal failed. She considered the question. 'I might be disappointed in the project,' she said, 'but I could still want to see you.' His smile made the long cleaning shift feel lighter.

市場關門後，若晴和亦辰留下清理借來的展板。其中一面有刮痕，物主要求賠償。亦辰檢查借板時的狀況紀錄，發現刮痕早已記下。他沒有拿證據羞辱物主，而是提出展示舊照片；物主為忘記道歉。若晴發現紀錄可以避免爭吵，而不必製造贏家和輸家。亦辰小心把板靠牆，接著問若聯合申請失敗，她還願不願意和他吃飯。她想了想說：「我可能會對企畫失望，但仍可能想見你。」他的微笑讓漫長清潔工作輕鬆了些。

The next morning, Ruo found a message from Evan asking whether she had arrived home safely. She answered yes and asked whether the borrowed boards had been returned. He laughed in his reply. Their conversation moved naturally between work and something warmer, without demanding a decision.

隔天早上，若晴收到亦辰問她是否平安到家的訊息。她回答是，又問借來的展板還了沒。他回訊笑她。兩人的對話自然穿梭在工作和更溫暖的情意之間，卻不逼彼此立刻決定。
