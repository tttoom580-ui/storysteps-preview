第一次緊急演練

The Emergency Drill

兩把鑰匙，同一座舞台 · 第 7 / 25 章 · 2000–3000 · 英中對照 · 校訂中

用互動版查詞、朗讀與收藏

The first evacuation drill went badly. Several volunteers rushed toward the same door, while a child could not find the marked resting area. Evan stopped the exercise and began again at a slower pace. Ruo interviewed the participants separately so they could describe the problem without copying one another's memories.

第一次疏散演練很不順利。幾名志工一起衝向同一扇門，一個孩子找不到標示的休息區。亦辰停下演練，改以較慢速度重來。若晴分開詢問參與者，避免大家不自覺地重複同一份說法。

Helen told them that an emergency plan should not require someone to be carried without consent. Evan changed the layout so the main program could be reached without stairs. Ruo moved the reading corner to a brighter place where a listener could come and go freely.

海倫說，緊急計畫不該假設可以不經同意就搬動別人。亦辰修改動線，讓主要活動不必爬樓梯即可到達。若晴把閱讀角移到更明亮的位置，讓聽眾自由進出。

After the revised drill, Helen stayed to review the maps. She pointed out that an emergency exit was only helpful if a visitor could identify it in a hurry. The map used three kinds of arrows, and two were too similar. Connie simplified the signs while Evan checked their placement. Ruo sat beside Helen and asked which words made sense without extra explanation. They changed 'assisted circulation' to 'step-free route.' Helen smiled and said the new phrase was not elegant, but people could use it. Ruo thought about the beautiful descriptions she had written in her proposal and felt willing to replace some of them. Good communication was sometimes plain.

修訂演練後，海倫留下來檢查地圖。她指出，緊急出口必須讓訪客在匆忙中辨認，才有意義。地圖用了三種箭頭，其中兩種太像。可妮簡化標示，亦辰檢查放置位置。若晴坐在海倫旁，詢問哪些詞不用解釋就能懂。他們把「輔助動線」改成「無階梯通道」。海倫笑說，新詞不漂亮，卻真的能用。若晴想到自己企畫裡華麗的描述，願意替換其中一些。好的溝通有時就是樸實。

The appeal for safer exits began beside a brick wall. It felt awful to admit the first drill had failed, and the conversation was awkward. In the emergency cupboard, Evan found a jar, a vase, and an old lily kept for decoration; none belonged with the medical supplies. A volunteer offered a dime for a new sign, but signs cost more than one coin. The teacher described why a small electrical fault could explode into a larger emergency without proper protection. At the food stand a cook discussed flavor, while the first-aid booth used a drawing of human flesh to explain where a bandage goes. Ruo suggested they invent a steadier system for checking the tools. Evan cleared junk from the exit and promised to remind helpers about the route sometime before opening. The revised plan was terrific, but Ruo also asked about the wages of trained workers.

要求改善出口的呼聲從磚牆旁開始。承認第一次演練失敗很難受，談話也尷尬。緊急用品櫃裡，亦辰發現裝飾用罐子、花瓶和一枝百合花，這些都不該混放醫療用品。一名志工捐出一角硬幣買標牌，但一枚硬幣遠遠不夠。教師說明小小電氣故障如何在防護不足時引發更大緊急事故。食物攤討論味道，急救攤用人體皮膚示意圖說明繃帶位置。若晴提議創造更穩定的工具檢查制度。亦辰清走出口雜物，答應開幕前找時間提醒幫手熟悉動線。新計畫很棒，若晴也追問受訓人員的工資。

The second drill needed a steady pace, not a race. Connie also explained the hourly wage offered to trained staff, rather than hiding pay in a promise of future gratitude.

第二次演練需要穩定步調，不是比快。可妮也說明受訓工作者的時薪，而非用日後感謝掩蓋報酬。

Simon complained that the changes reduced the space for profitable stalls. Connie pointed to the council's published scoring system. Safety had always counted for twenty-five points; no one had changed the rules halfway through. Evan thanked Connie for holding the printed notice where everyone could read it.

賽門抱怨這些改動減少了賺錢攤位空間。可妮指著市議會公開的評分標準：安全一直占二十五分，沒有人臨時改規則。亦辰感謝可妮把公告放在人人看得見的地方。

That evening the members agreed to place two trained volunteers at the entrances, keep a clear route to the toilets, and run another drill with Helen. The trial cost them an afternoon, but it gave them an improved plan and their first shared safety record.

當晚社員同意在入口安排兩位受訓志工、保持通往廁所的動線暢通，並邀海倫再參加一次演練。試驗花了一下午，卻換來更好的計畫和兩社第一份共同安全紀錄。

When the second drill began, Helen asked for the same written instructions as everyone else. She did not want someone standing over her telling her how brave she was to participate. Ruo understood and handed her the sheet without comment. A student took notes about the time needed to clear each path. Evan asked the group to repeat the slowest stage rather than focus on their fastest result. One route was still too narrow, and the team changed the table positions again. Nobody clapped when the measurement improved. They simply added the new figure to the plan. Ruo liked that ordinary seriousness. Later she saw Helen laughing with a young volunteer over a badly drawn arrow. They corrected it together, and the new sign became the one used on opening day. The solution had not been invented by a single expert.

第二次演練開始，海倫要求和所有人拿一樣的書面指示。她不希望有人站在旁邊稱讚她參與多勇敢。若晴明白，安靜把紙交給她。學生記下各路線清空需要的時間，亦辰請大家重做最慢的階段，不只強調最快成績。某路線仍太窄，團隊又移桌子。測量改善時沒人鼓掌，只把新數字寫進計畫。若晴喜歡這種平凡的認真。後來她看海倫和年輕志工笑著討論畫得很糟的箭頭，兩人一起改好，新標誌後來用在開幕日。解法不是由單一專家想出來的。

After the volunteers went home, the room felt larger. Evan marked the route with tape that could be removed without damaging the floor. Ruo tested the words on the sign once more. Neither was satisfied yet, but both could point to what had improved since morning.

志工回家後，房間顯得寬敞。亦辰用不傷地板、可移除的膠帶標路線。若晴再次測試標牌文字。兩人仍不完全滿意，卻都能指出比早上改善了哪些地方。
