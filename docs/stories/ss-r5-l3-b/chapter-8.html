Who Owns a Story?

兩把鑰匙，同一座舞台 · 第 8 / 25 章 · 2000–3000 · 英中對照 · 校訂中

用互動版查詞、朗讀與收藏

For the public history booth, Ruo had collected recordings from residents. One speaker asked her to remove a detail about a family member before the festival. She did so immediately. Evan wondered whether the removal damaged the historical record. Ruo explained that an archive could preserve evidence privately without putting it on a public wall.

若晴為公共歷史攤位收集居民錄音。一位受訪者在活動前要求刪掉家人細節，若晴立刻照辦。亦辰擔心這是否破壞歷史紀錄。若晴解釋，檔案可以在保護隱私的情況下保存證據，不代表什麼都該公開張貼。

A student volunteer wanted to publish every recording on the internet so more people would come. Ruo asked each speaker what kind of sharing they had approved. Several had agreed only to listening inside the station. Instead of pressuring them, she prepared shorter descriptions with their permission.

一名學生志工想把所有錄音上網，吸引更多人。若晴逐一確認受訪者同意的使用方式。好幾人只同意在車站現場播放。她沒有施壓，而是在徵得同意後改寫較短的介紹。

Ruo spent a quiet hour listening to an interview with no public permission attached. She did not use it. Instead she asked the speaker what information they would want a younger generation to hear, if any. The reply surprised her: the speaker wanted people to know the sound of the old station clock, but not why a certain journey had ended. Evan helped locate a recording of the clock itself. The display became simpler, but also kinder. When Ruo played the approved sound to the speaker, the woman laughed at how different it was from her memory. She was pleased to be allowed to disagree with the exhibit about her own past.

若晴花一個安靜小時聽一段沒有公開授權的訪談，最後沒有使用。她問受訪者有沒有想讓年輕世代聽到的資訊。答覆出乎意料：對方想讓人聽見老車站時鐘的聲音，卻不想解釋某次旅程為何結束。亦辰幫忙找出時鐘錄音。展示變簡單，也更溫柔。若晴把核准的聲音播給當事人聽，她笑說記憶裡完全不是這個樣子。她很高興能對自己的過去與展品有不同意見。

At the market gate, travelers left baggage under a painted buffalo. A museum stall displayed a harmless toy bullet; a citizen ran a model vehicle using a remote controller. A cooker sold crispy rice, and Connie warned the visitors not to grab hot pans. Ruo had lately noticed how quickly litter covered a public path. She asked who would clean it and whom the supplier owed for that work. The permission form for interviews was kept in a locked box, while a public web page explained the process for making a request. Nearby a workshop discussed sexual harassment prevention. A talkative visitor wanted to post a wealthy neighbor's name on the wall, but Ruo refused to reveal someone else's private story. The hall offered quiet space in the shadow of a roof. Nobody needed to yell to be heard.

市場門口，旅客把行李放在彩繪水牛下。博物館攤位展示無害玩具子彈，一位市民用遙控器操作模型車。炊具攤販賣香脆米飯，可妮提醒訪客別伸手抓熱鍋。若晴最近注意到垃圾如何迅速蓋滿公共通道，便問誰來清掃、供應商該支付誰的工資。訪談同意書放在上鎖箱中，公開網頁則說明提出申請的程序。附近工作坊討論性騷擾預防。一位話多的訪客想把有錢鄰居的名字貼上牆，若晴拒絕洩漏別人的私事。屋簷陰影下提供安靜空間，不必吼叫也能被聽見。

Ruo asked the supplier to explain what payment they still owe the cleaning team. She would not put anyone's private story on the web merely to attract a larger crowd.

若晴請供應商說明還欠清潔團隊哪些款項。她不會只為吸引更多觀眾就把別人的私事放上網。

Evan recognized a familiar voice among the interviews: his father had once repaired the station clock. He wanted to surprise the audience with the recording, but his father had asked for a private copy only. Evan put the file away. Ruo saw how difficult the rule was when it involved somebody he loved.

亦辰在訪談中聽到熟悉聲音：他父親以前修過車站時鐘。他想把錄音當成驚喜播給觀眾，父親卻只同意私下保存。亦辰把檔案收起。若晴看見規則涉及所愛之人時，有多不容易遵守。

The history club voted to add a permission check to every display. Evan's members offered to build simple controls so visitors could choose subtitles or printed copies. For once, a technical idea and a storytelling rule arrived at the same answer: give people a choice.

故事社投票決定每個展示都必須有授權查核。修理社提出製作簡單的選擇裝置，讓訪客自行選字幕或紙本。這回技術想法和敘事規則得到相同答案：把選擇權交給人。

The history club created three choices for every interview: private archive, listening inside the station, or broader public use. No box was selected in advance. Ruo explained that permission could be changed later and that the club would clearly record any removal. Evan helped make a simple interface, but he kept an ordinary paper form available as well. During testing, a visitor pressed the wrong option and worried that the recording had already been published. Evan showed her the confirmation screen, which required a second clear choice. Ruo asked the visitor whether the explanation was understandable. She said the system was much better when it did not expect her to know technical language. Ruo thanked Evan for the design. He answered that it had only become useful after the visitor told them what confused her. The lesson belonged to all three.

故事社替每段訪談提供三種選擇：私人保存、車站內聆聽、或更廣泛公開。沒有預先勾好任何方框。若晴說明同意之後可改變，社團也會清楚記錄撤回。亦辰設計簡單介面，仍保留普通紙本。測試時訪客按錯選項，擔心錄音已公開。亦辰展示必須二次確認的畫面，若晴問解說是否易懂。訪客說，不預設使用者會懂技術語言就好多了。若晴感謝亦辰，他回答，設計是訪客指出困惑後才真正變得有用。這堂課屬於三個人。

Ruo asked the club to keep the private and public records in different folders. Evan built separate access settings, but she insisted on a paper list of who could open each file. A good system required both sensible tools and people willing to use them responsibly.

若晴請社團把公開與私人資料分開保存。亦辰設定不同存取權限，她還堅持用紙本列出可開檔的人。好系統需要合宜工具，也需要願意負責使用的人。
