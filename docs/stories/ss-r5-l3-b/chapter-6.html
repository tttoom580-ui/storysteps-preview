廚房裡的傳言

A Rumor in the Kitchen

兩把鑰匙，同一座舞台 · 第 6 / 25 章 · 2000–3000 · 英中對照 · 校訂完成

用互動版查詞、朗讀與收藏

At a food-safety class, somebody said the Repair Club had broken a refrigerator on purpose to make its repair work look important. Evan wanted to confront the speaker. Ruo asked for the maintenance record first. The record showed that the refrigerator had failed before either club entered the building.

食品安全課上有人說，修理社故意弄壞冰箱，藉此凸顯維修重要。亦辰想直接找傳話者理論。若晴先索取維修紀錄，證明冰箱在兩社進場前就已故障。

The rumor had spread from a photograph with no date. Connie located the original picture and its caption. Ruo posted both the photograph and the correction, taking care not to reveal the person who had first asked a worried question. She wanted a clear record, not a public enemy.

傳言從一張沒有日期的照片傳開。可妮找到原始圖片及說明。若晴公布照片與更正，也小心不揭露最初提出疑問者的身分。她想建立清楚紀錄，不想製造全民公敵。

The original photograph turned out to be from a school assignment two years earlier. The student who had shared it had not intended to harm Evan; she had believed the rumor and wanted to warn others. Ruo offered to explain the correction at the next meeting without naming her. Evan was not ready to forgive the careless message, but he agreed to leave the student's name out of it. They separated the harm from the person, and the person from the evidence. The lesson mattered to the Repair Club as well. When a machine failed, blaming the last person who touched it was rarely a reliable method. They were discovering how much repair and public communication had in common.

原始照片其實來自兩年前的學校作業。分享照片的學生並非存心害亦辰；她相信傳言，想警告別人。若晴提議下次開會時說明更正，不公布她的名字。亦辰還無法原諒草率的訊息，但同意不曝光學生。他們把傷害與人、以及人與證據分開。這堂課對修理社也重要：機器故障時，責怪最後碰它的人通常不可靠。他們逐漸發現，維修和公共溝通竟有這麼多共同之處。

Anyhow, Ruo refused to assume a rumor was true. A food teacher explained that bacteria could multiply inside a poorly kept meal. The demonstration included a model missile from the history exhibit, but she asked that it be stored away from the kitchen. A bump in the refrigerator door was visible. Its creator, a former student, described the inner parts and the technique needed to repair them. A politician who visited the market called the machine a symbol of local excellence. Evan preferred a factual summary: clean equipment, a safe stomach, and clear resource limits were necessities, not speeches. A stale bun was discarded. A teenage volunteer from a tropical island asked if the same rule applied in warmer weather. Yes, the teacher answered, while sealing a sample with wax. The group did not suspect anyone without evidence.

無論如何，若晴拒絕把傳言當真。食品教師解釋，保存不當的餐點會讓細菌繁殖。歷史展示區有一枚飛彈模型，但她請人把它移離廚房。冰箱門的撞痕清楚可見。設計它的校友說明內部零件和維修技巧。到訪市場的政治人物稱冰箱是地方卓越技術的象徵。亦辰寧願寫事實摘要：乾淨設備、不讓人吃壞肚子、清楚的資源限制都是必要條件，不是演說。變味麵包被丟棄。來自熱帶島嶼的青少年志工問，天熱時規則是否一樣。教師一邊用蠟封住樣本，一邊回答是的。團隊沒有無憑無據懷疑任何人。

The teacher called clean storage a necessity, not a luxury. Ruo added that exact phrase to the food-safety instructions, where it could guide the next class.

教師說清潔儲藏是必要條件，不是奢侈。若晴把這句原話加進食品安全指示，讓下一堂課能依照辦理。

Evan showed Ruo how a food thermometer worked. She asked him to explain it again without technical words; visitors would need to understand it in one minute. He tried a simpler example and was pleased when she could repeat it. Teaching, they discovered, was itself a form of design.

亦辰示範食物溫度計的用法。若晴請他不用術語再解釋一次，因為訪客必須在一分鐘內理解。他改用簡單例子，見她能複述便十分高興。他們發現，教學本身也是設計的一部分。

At the end of the day they wrote a rule for the event: equipment reports would include dates and names, but personal health details would stay private. Both clubs approved the rule. Ruo felt that cooperation was becoming real, not because every discussion was pleasant, but because disagreements now produced useful procedures.

當天結束前，他們訂下一條活動規則：器材報告應註明日期和負責人，個人健康資訊則維持隱私。兩社都同意。若晴覺得合作開始成真，不是因為每場討論都愉快，而是分歧已能形成有用程序。

Connie offered to speak at the rumor meeting because she had kept the maintenance documents. Ruo asked whether she was comfortable answering questions in public. Connie said yes, provided the group discussed the records and not the motives of individual students. Evan agreed. During the meeting someone asked why the photo had been shared without a date. Connie replied that people sometimes act quickly when they are worried, but that did not make the information reliable. The room stayed quiet. Instead of asking who deserved punishment, several members volunteered to help label future photographs with time and source. Ruo watched Evan add the equipment inspection date to a shared calendar. She had expected a difficult public argument. What she saw instead was a practical repair to a communication problem. It did not remove the hurt of the rumor, but it gave everyone a way to avoid repeating the same mistake.

可妮提出願在謠言會議發言，因為維修文件是她保存的。若晴問她是否願意公開回答問題。可妮說可以，條件是只討論紀錄，不猜測個別學生動機。亦辰同意。會上有人問，為何照片沒日期就被分享。可妮說人擔心時有時會做得很快，但資訊不因此可靠。房間安靜下來。沒人再追問該懲罰誰，幾位社員反而自願替未來照片標上時間和來源。若晴看亦辰把設備檢查日期加進共同月曆。她原以為會有艱難的公開爭執，結果看見對溝通問題的實際修復。傷害沒有消失，卻有了避免重犯的辦法。

Ruo closed the corrected photograph file and returned the original to its owner. Evan updated the repair log without adding the student's name. The records did not contain a story of heroes and enemies. They contained something smaller and more useful: a mistake, its source, and the correction.

若晴關上修正後的照片檔，把原件還給物主。亦辰更新維修紀錄，沒有加上那名學生的名字。紀錄不講英雄與敵人，只留下更小也更有用的內容：錯誤、來源和更正。
