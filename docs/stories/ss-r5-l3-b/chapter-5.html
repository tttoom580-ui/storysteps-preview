免費桌子的成本

The Cost of a Free Table

兩把鑰匙，同一座舞台 · 第 5 / 25 章 · 2000–3000 · 英中對照

用互動版查詞、朗讀與收藏

The clubs held a breakfast meeting to study the numbers. Evan had counted tools and safety supplies; Ruo had counted chairs, printing, and interpreters. Neither budget included cleaning, storage, or replacements. When Connie added those costs, both plans exceeded the small grant. Silence followed the final number.

兩社舉行早餐會議討論數字。亦辰算了工具和安全用品，若晴算了椅子、印刷和翻譯人員。兩份預算都漏掉清掃、倉儲和替換成本。可妮加上那些費用後，兩案都超過補助，總額讓大家沉默。

Simon offered to cover the difference if the clubs let his staff handle all public messages. Ruo recognized the price of the offer: residents would have no direct way to question the organizers. She refused on behalf of her own club, but did not claim to speak for Evan's members.

賽門表示只要由他的團隊統一處理對外訊息，就補足差額。若晴看出代價：居民將無法直接向主辦者提問。她代表自己社團拒絕，卻沒有自稱也能替亦辰的社員做決定。

Before the budget meeting ended, Connie asked who would answer requests when a repair could not be completed. Nobody raised a hand. Ruo realized that a free service could still create a painful promise if visitors believed every object would be saved. Evan wrote a clear limit: volunteers would inspect safe items, explain possible repairs, and refer dangerous work to qualified professionals. The club could offer knowledge without claiming power it did not have. Ruo volunteered to help make the notice readable, including a version with larger letters. She did not ask Evan to give up his standards for a softer message. Instead, she learned how to translate technical honesty into a welcome that ordinary people could understand.

預算會議快結束時，可妮問若修理無法完成，誰負責回覆。沒有人舉手。若晴發現，即使免費服務，若訪客誤以為每樣物品都能修好，也會形成痛苦承諾。亦辰寫下清楚界線：志工只檢查安全物件、解釋可能的維修方式，危險工作交給合格專業人員。社團可以提供知識，卻不能假裝擁有不存在的能力。若晴自願把告示改得易讀，包含大字版。她沒有要求亦辰放棄標準，只努力把技術誠實轉成一般人能理解的歡迎。

The meeting took place over bacon and ripe fruit beside a carpet that kept the tables from sliding. A volunteer began to complain about the considerable cost of rental equipment, then stopped to cough. Evan invited her to engage with the figures instead of accepting a fake discount. In the garage, a flashlight revealed an old motor and a sack of spare parts. Ruo held her jaw when a microphone failed in the middle of a sentence. A student with a peanut allergy pointed to an unsafe snack table, so Connie moved the food away. Evan asked everybody to pronounce the safety warning clearly; responsibility belonged to the whole team. Steam rose from a kettle while the group discussed the sorrow of people losing old possessions. They rejected a violent advertisement that promised to 'fight' broken machines. The repair work, they agreed, should be patient and calm.

會議在早餐桌旁進行，桌上有培根和熟透的水果，地毯防止桌腳滑動。一名志工抱怨設備租金高得可觀，接著咳了幾聲。亦辰邀她一起核對數字，而不是接受假折扣。車庫裡，手電筒照出舊馬達和一袋零件。麥克風在一句話中途故障，若晴托著下巴思考。對花生過敏的學生指出點心桌有風險，可妮便移走食物。亦辰請大家清楚唸出安全警告，提醒責任屬於全隊。水壺冒出蒸氣，眾人談起舊物失去時的悲傷。他們拒絕一份鼓吹「打敗故障機器」的暴力宣傳文案，決定耐心平和地進行維修。

Evan asked a retired electrician to inspect the existing equipment. Most of it could be repaired for less than the rental fee in Simon's quote. Ruo offered to run a public workshop about what repairs cost and why skilled labor deserved fair pay. The two budgets finally began to share a language.

亦辰請退休電工檢查現有設備。多數器材的修理費，比賽門報價中的租金便宜。若晴提議開一堂公開課，說明修理的成本，以及為何專業勞動值得合理報酬。兩份預算終於開始有共同語言。

Before leaving, Ruo told Evan that she had once treated the repair club as a collection of useful hands. He said he had treated her stories as decoration. They apologized without turning the moment into an agreement about everything. The next day they would ask their members again.

離開前，若晴對亦辰坦白，她曾把修理社當成一群可支援的人手；亦辰也承認把她的故事視為裝飾。兩人道歉，卻沒有把這一刻當成凡事都同意。隔天他們會重新徵詢社員意見。

After the numbers were corrected, Ruo proposed a new kind of workshop. Visitors could bring an object and share a memory if they wished, while Evan's team explained whether it could be safely inspected. The workshop would not require a story to receive help, and nobody would be filmed without permission. Evan liked the idea, but worried that people might arrive with dangerous machines. They wrote a clear list of items the volunteers could accept. Connie estimated staffing costs, and Helen asked whether a visitor could participate from a chair. Each question changed the proposal slightly. By the end, the workshop no longer belonged to either president's first plan. It had grown through the limits and hopes of many people. Ruo noticed she was not unhappy about losing control of the idea. She was relieved that someone else could see what she had missed. Evan said he felt the same about the tools he had planned to show off.

數字修正後，若晴提出新工作坊：訪客可帶物品，也可自願分享回憶，亦辰團隊則說明能否安全檢查。求助不必先說故事，任何人也不會未經同意被拍攝。亦辰喜歡，但擔心有人帶來危險機器。他們列出志工能收的物品。可妮估算人力成本，海倫問坐著能不能參與。每個問題都讓企畫稍有變化。最後這已不屬於任何一位社長最初的版本，而是眾人期待與限制共同長出的工作坊。若晴發現失去主導權沒有讓她難受，反而因有人看出她漏掉的事情而放心。亦辰說，他原本想炫耀的工具也一樣。

The new notice was printed in large letters. Ruo read it aloud once to check that it sounded welcoming instead of defensive. Evan added his contact hours underneath. They were beginning to discover that a clear limit could be an invitation rather than a refusal.

新告示用大字印出。若晴朗讀一遍，確認聽起來是歡迎而非防衛。亦辰在下面加上可聯絡時段。他們逐漸發現，清楚界線可以是一種邀請，不只是拒絕。
