給風的日曆

A Calendar for the Wind

不會飛的信使，替群山點燈 · 第 7 / 25 章 · 1000–2000 · 英中對照 · 校訂完成

用互動版查詞、朗讀與收藏

The first full trial would begin after the market day. Lan opened a calendar and marked a period of three clear evenings when the climate was usually dry. Mrs. Sun said the weather could still change. Their advance plan therefore contained two choices: a light test on a clear evening or a walk through the stations when clouds were thick. Bo introduced the plan to the volunteers and asked everyone to name one thing that might go wrong. A runner said the northern path was too rocky for a quick jog at night. Lan crossed out the word QUICK from the walking rule. Thus, progress would matter only if people returned safely.

第一次完整測試預定在市集日之後。嵐打開日曆，圈出通常乾燥晴朗的三個傍晚。孫太太提醒，天氣仍可能變化。因此，提前計畫包含兩個選項：晴天做光訊號測試，厚雲時則由人沿站點步行巡查。博向志工介紹計畫，請每人說出一件可能出錯的事。一名跑腿者說北方小路太多石頭，夜裡不適合快速慢跑。嵐把步行規則中的「快速」兩字劃掉。因此，只有人們能安全回家，進展才有意義。

They met beside a rocky hill where an old gardener grew trees in dry soil. He called one part of his garden a little desert, though it lay beside a stream. The gardener was humble about his work, but he understood the land. He said that a good path should not harm the plants keeping the hill in place. He showed them a turtle moving slowly under a flat stone. “Even this small being knows where to shelter,” he said. Lan watched the turtle until it was safe from their feet. She had imagined an ideal straight line between posts. Now she drew a curve that followed the land and protected the garden.

他們在多石山丘旁集合，一名老園丁在乾土裡種樹。他把園中一角稱作小沙漠，雖然不遠處就有溪流。園丁很謙虛，卻十分熟悉土地。他說好路線不該傷害讓山坡穩固的植物，還指給他們看一隻躲在平石下慢慢爬行的烏龜。「連這樣的小生命都知道哪裡能避難，」他說。嵐看著烏龜安全離開他們腳邊。她原本想畫出站點間理想的直線，現在改畫一條順應地形、保護花園的曲線。

At the next post, a worker used a mop to clean the floor of the small watch room. The room had been empty for years, but it still held a wooden shelf and a box of old notes. One note set out a useful principle: no watcher should work alone during a dangerous test. Lan showed it to the others. The team had planned to place one person at each post to save time. They changed the plan to two people at a time, with shorter shifts. It took more volunteers, but it also meant a witness could confirm every signal. Lan wrote the change on the calendar and asked the workers to sign only for hours they could truly give.

到下一個站點時，一名工人正拿拖把清理小守望室。房間空了多年，仍留著木架和一箱舊筆記。其中一張紙寫著重要原則：危險測試時，守望者不可單獨工作。嵐把紙給大家看。原本為了省時，他們打算每站安排一人；現在改成每班兩人、輪值較短。雖然需要更多志工，卻也讓每一道訊號都有另一人核對。嵐把變更記進日曆，請工人只簽下自己真正有空的時段。

The school doctor visited the watch room before supper. She checked the drinking water and asked where people would wash their hands. A child nearby had left a toothbrush beside a cup, and she gently moved it away from the shared tools. It was a small step, but Lan understood why it mattered. A safe station was also a place where tired people ate, rested, and waited. Bo wanted to hang a large victory flag over the door. Lan suggested a simple sign with the station name instead. They could celebrate after a real test, not before one. The doctor approved the clean room but asked them to keep a written list of supplies.

晚餐前，學校醫生來查看守望室。她檢查飲用水，也問大家要在哪裡洗手。旁邊有個孩子把牙刷留在杯子旁，她溫柔地將牙刷移開，與公用工具分開放。這只是小小一步，嵐卻明白它的重要。安全的站點同時也是疲憊的人吃飯、休息和等候的地方。博想在門上掛大型勝利旗，嵐建議先掛一塊寫站名的簡單牌子。慶祝應該等測試成功，不是提前舉行。醫生認可乾淨的室內環境，也請他們保留用品清單。

On the first clear evening, Lan stood at the southern post with Bo while Mrs. Sun waited on the north ridge. One short flash meant READY. Mrs. Sun answered with the same mark. The test began. A message moved from hill to hill until the final station sent two long flashes back. Lan checked the time and found that the whole journey had taken less than five minutes. That was enough for a warning, but not enough to declare the line perfect. She asked the watchers to repeat the message without looking at one another's notes. All of them agreed on the meaning. For the first time, the valley had a working chain of light.

第一個晴朗傍晚，嵐和博守在南站，孫太太則在北方山脊等候。一次短閃代表「準備完成」，孫太太用相同訊號回覆。測試開始了。訊息從一座山送往下一座山，最後一站回傳兩次長閃。嵐查看時間，整趟不到五分鐘。這對警訊已足夠，卻不能說系統完美。她請守望者不看彼此的筆記，獨立重述訊息含意，結果大家一致。這是山谷第一次擁有完整運作的光之鏈。

That night, Lan carried the calendar home and placed it beside the boy's letter. She wrote the names of the watchers who had made the test possible. No one had flown. No one had used rare power. The work had required patient eyes, careful hands, and people willing to listen. She understood that a message could now travel faster than a person, but a person still had to decide whether to send it. That decision was the heart of the system. She turned out the lamp, leaving the signed calendar on the table so that anyone on the morning shift could find it.

當晚，嵐把日曆帶回家，放在男孩的信旁，記下參與測試的守望者姓名。沒有人飛行，也沒有誰使用稀有力量。這項工作需要有耐心的眼睛、小心的雙手，以及願意聽別人說話的人。她明白訊息現在能比人走得快，但仍須由人決定是否發出；那個決定才是制度的核心。她熄了燈，把簽過名的日曆留在桌上，讓早班的任何人都能找到。
