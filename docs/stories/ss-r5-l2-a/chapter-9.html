沒有署名的訊息

The Message with No Name

不會飛的信使，替群山點燈 · 第 9 / 25 章 · 1000–2000 · 英中對照 · 校訂中

用互動版查詞、朗讀與收藏

At sunrise, a strange signal burst from the north ridge. It used the HELP pattern, then flashed a symbol no one had agreed on. Lan asked Bo to read the record aloud. The final mark looked like the open mouth of a shark, a silly image children sometimes drew on their school bags. The funny shark looked alive in the children's drawings. But the position of the flash was wrong. It came from an unused ledge above the official post. Lan refused to treat it as a real warning until someone checked the source. She also refused to ignore it. Two trained adults went to a safe viewing point while the watchers kept the normal line open.

日出時，北方山脊突然爆出奇怪的訊號。先是「求救」的閃法，接著出現沒人約定過的符號。嵐請博把紀錄大聲讀出。最後一個記號像張嘴的鯊魚，是孩子常在書包上畫的滑稽圖案。在孩子的畫裡，那條有趣的鯊魚看起來栩栩如生。但光的位置不對，來自正式站點上方一處未使用的平台。嵐不肯在有人核對來源前，把它當作真實警報；她也沒有忽略它。兩名受訓成年人前往安全觀察點，其他守望者照常維持訊號線。

The viewing team found a metal tube near the ledge. Someone had placed a bright lid at its end to reflect sunlight. A bottle of clear liquid stood behind it. Lan examined the objects without touching them. The liquid might simply be water, but she would not guess. Bo drew an image of their exact position and noted the time. The team returned by the regular exit instead of climbing a short wall. They brought no object away from the scene. If somebody was trying to harm the project, the right solution was to keep a careful record, not to create more danger by chasing a shadow.

觀察小隊在平台附近找到一支金屬管，末端放著明亮的蓋子，用來反射陽光。後方還有一瓶透明液體。嵐只觀察，不去碰它們。液體可能只是水，但她不願猜測。博畫下物件的精確位置，記上時間。小隊循正常出口返回，沒有攀越矮牆，也沒有取走現場任何物品。若有人想破壞計畫，正確的解法是保留仔細紀錄，而非追逐影子、製造更多危險。

The school head teacher recognized the shark drawing. It belonged to a game invented by her students, not to the signal code. A child admitted that he had shown the game to a stranger at the market. He began to cry and asked the adults to forgive him. Lan explained that sharing a drawing was not a crime. The stranger was responsible for what the stranger did with it. She asked the child to express what he remembered about the meeting without adding guesses. He remembered a blue coat and a bag with a silver handle. That was useful, but it did not prove who had placed the tube on the ledge.

校長認出了鯊魚圖案，那是學生發明的遊戲，不是訊號代碼。一個孩子承認，曾在市集把遊戲教給陌生人。他哭了，請大人原諒。嵐解釋，分享圖案不是犯罪；陌生人如何使用它，責任在陌生人。她請孩子說出自己記得的見面細節，不要加上猜測。孩子記得藍外套和有銀色提把的袋子。這些細節有用，卻不能證明是誰把金屬管放上平台。

That afternoon, Lan called the volunteers together. She wrote the title FALSE SOURCE on a new page and asked them how to improve the rules. One watcher suggested that every post send its name before any urgent message. Another wanted a second person to read the signal before it was passed along. The team agreed to both changes. They also decided to make a simple list of stations whose light could be accepted. Any other flash would be watched but not repeated as a confirmed request. It was a small improvement, yet it protected the line from being turned into a game by people outside it.

下午，嵐召集志工，在新的一頁寫下標題「來源不明」，請大家想想如何改進規則。一名守望者提議，任何緊急訊息前先傳站名；另一人主張，轉送之前要有第二人核讀。隊伍同意兩項修改，也決定列出可以接受訊號的正式站點。其他閃光可以觀察，卻不能當成已確認的求救轉送。這項改進雖小，卻能防止外人把訊號線當成遊戲。

While the adults argued, the youngest student brought a tray of delicious buns. She told Lan that her brother had made the shark drawing because he loved sea animals. It was a cheerful character, not a warning. Lan smiled and thanked her for explaining it. She realized that a mark could have one meaning in a child's game and another meaning in a public service. People needed to know which world they were speaking in. She drew a clear border around the official code on the classroom wall, leaving the children's pictures nearby. The pictures were still welcome. They simply could not be used as signals.

大人討論時，最小的學生端來一盤美味的小麵包。她告訴嵐，弟弟畫鯊魚是因為喜歡海洋動物；那是快樂的角色，不是警告。嵐微笑道謝。她發現同一個記號，在孩子的遊戲與公共服務中可能代表完全不同的事。人們必須知道自己說的是哪一種語言。她在教室牆上替正式代碼畫出清楚邊框，仍讓孩子的畫留在旁邊。圖畫依然受歡迎，只是不能被當作訊號。

When the day ended, the real stations sent a test message using their new order. NAME, DATE, REQUEST, RECEIVED: every watcher read it correctly. Lan wrote the result beside the false message record and closed the notebook. She could not yet say who had made the strange flash or why. She could say that the line had stayed open and no one had been sent into danger. That night Bo asked whether she was afraid. “Yes,” she said. “But fear is a reason to check our work, not a reason to stop asking questions.” They locked the tools away and went home together.

當天收工前，真正的站點依新順序傳了一次測試訊息：「站名、日期、請求、收到。」每名守望者都正確讀出來。嵐把結果寫在假訊號紀錄旁，合上筆記本。她還不知道奇怪閃光是誰發的、為什麼發，但確定線路一直暢通，也沒有人被送入危險。當晚博問她會不會害怕。「會，」她說。「但害怕應該讓我們檢查工作，而不是停止提問。」他們收好工具，一同回家。

Why was the strange flash not repeated as a real warning?

  1. It came from an unknown source and used an unapproved symbol.
  2. It was sent during daylight.
  3. It came with a complete station number.
