岔路與人心

The Branch Road

不會飛的信使，替群山點燈 · 第 4 / 25 章 · 1000–2000 · 英中對照

用互動版查詞、朗讀與收藏

On the fourth day, Lan climbed a branch road above the village with Mrs. Sun. A man there had cut his hand and left a few drops of blood on his work glove. His neighbors worried about disease because the village doctor was far away. Lan could not treat him, but she could use the light line to ask the doctor whether the wound needed quick attention. The doctor sent back a clear answer through the first two posts: wash the cut, cover it with clean cloth, and come down for care that afternoon. The message did not replace a medical visit. It helped the man decide what to do before he could reach one.

第四天，嵐和孫太太沿村莊上方的岔路前進。那裡有個男人割傷手，工作手套沾了幾滴血。鄰居擔心感染疾病，因為村醫住得很遠。嵐不會治療，卻能用光訊號詢問醫生傷口是否需要緊急處理。醫生透過前兩個站點傳回清楚的答覆：洗淨傷口、用乾淨布包好，下午下山就醫。訊息不能代替看診，卻讓男人在見到醫生之前知道該怎麼做。

News of the small success spread through the entire valley. The director of a local farm offered them wood from a fallen tree to build another frame. A generous shop owner brought tools and a bag of hamburgers for the workers. Bo asked everyone to keep the tools separate from the food so that grease would not cover the clean material. Lan began to combine the gifts into a simple plan. They would place mirrors only where two stations could see each other without cutting down living trees. The farm director wanted to help, but she also asked that the new path avoid the young fruit trees on her land.

小小的成功傳遍整座山谷。當地農場主任願意提供倒下樹木的木材，再做一座鏡架。一名慷慨的店主帶來工具和一袋漢堡給工人。博請大家把工具和食物分開，以免油污弄髒乾淨材料。嵐把各方捐助整理成簡單計畫：只有在兩站不砍活樹就能彼此看見的地方，才設鏡子。農場主任想幫忙，也要求新路避開她土地上的幼果樹。

The farm served sweet papaya at lunch. Beside the kitchen was a wall covered with a network of old footpaths. Lan studied the marks and tried to imagine how a message could travel around a hill rather than across it. A path that seemed longer on paper might have fewer dangerous turns. Bo counted the people living in each area and wrote the population beside every station. They agreed that every community should know who could receive a message for it. The target was not to have the highest tower. It was to reach families who had been invisible to the old system.

農場午餐供應甜木瓜。廚房旁的牆畫滿古老步道的路網。嵐研究那些記號，想像訊息如何繞過山，而非硬要穿過山。一條地圖上較長的路，也許反而少了幾個危險轉彎。博統計各區人口，把數字記在每個站點旁。他們同意，每個社區都應知道誰會替自己收訊息。目標不是蓋最高的塔，而是讓舊制度看不見的家庭也能收到訊息。

That afternoon a sudden shower forced the team into a shed. Water ran down the roof, and a ladder slipped from its place. Lan told the younger workers not to escape through the back door, where the ground fell sharply. Instead, everyone moved to the dry front wall. One worker began to laugh from fear. Another offered him a seat and water. “You cannot score a good day only by counting finished frames,” Mrs. Sun said. “You must also count the people who go home safely.” Lan wrote those words in her notebook because she sensed they would matter more when the project became larger.

下午突然下雨，隊伍只好躲進棚屋。雨水順著屋頂流下，梯子從原位滑開。嵐叫年輕工人不要從後門逃出去，因為那裡地勢陡降。大家改靠到乾燥的前牆。一名工人嚇得笑了起來，另一人讓他坐下喝水。「不能只用完成幾個鏡架來替今天打分數，」孫太太說。「還得算算有多少人平安回家。」嵐把話寫進筆記本，因為她感覺，計畫變大後這句話會更重要。

When the rain stopped, Lan looked at the marks left on the wet road. No wheel tracks had entered the farm during the storm. The villagers had brought all the material by hand. Therefore, the new station would require a better way to move heavy boards. Bo said they could borrow a cart, but the track near the river was broken. The farm director offered a team of adults to repair the track after the doctor had visited the injured man. Lan thanked her and refused to hurry them. The project was worth doing only if it made the valley safer, not if it gave the valley one more reason to fear an accident.

雨停後，嵐查看濕路上的痕跡。暴雨期間沒有車輪進入農場的印子，材料全靠村民手搬。因此，新站需要更好的方式運送厚木板。博說可以借推車，但河旁的小路破損了。農場主任願意派成年人修路，等醫生看過受傷的男人就動工。嵐道謝，也拒絕催促。只有讓山谷更安全，這項計畫才值得做；如果只是多添一個人們害怕事故的理由，就失去意義了。

At dusk, the director walked with Lan to the edge of the orchard. The town lights shone like small stars below them. “My father told me that helpers must always be strong,” she said. “Today you helped a hurt man without carrying him, and you helped my workers by letting them stop.” Lan thought about the letter still resting in her bag. She had begun this work because one child needed to reach his sister. Now she saw that the whole valley needed a way to ask for help before danger became too great. The first route was still short, but the people along it were beginning to trust one another.

黃昏時，主任陪嵐走到果園邊緣。下方的城鎮燈光像小星星。「我父親總說，幫人的人一定要很強壯，」她說。「今天妳沒搬動傷者，卻幫到了他；妳讓工人停工，也幫到了他們。」嵐想起袋裡的信。這項工作最初只是為了讓一個孩子聯絡姊姊；現在她看見整座山谷都需要在危險變大之前求助的方法。第一條路線仍很短，但沿線的人已開始彼此信任。
