不是真的求救訊號

A Signal That Was Not True

不會飛的信使，替群山點燈 · 第 3 / 25 章 · 1000–2000 · 英中對照

用互動版查詞、朗讀與收藏

A sharp alarm sounded from the school garden just after lunch. Lan ran down the steps, but Bo called for everyone to stay calm. The new mirror had flashed three short lights. That meant HELP, and they had promised never to ignore it. Yet the garden was empty. A boy dressed like a cowboy in a school play stepped out from behind a tree. He admitted that he had pressed the mirror's handle to make the flash. “It was a classic trick,” he said, trying to smile. Lan was angry, but she asked him to put the handle down before they spoke. No one was hurt, and now the real work was to make sure it would not happen again.

午飯後不久，學校花園響起尖銳的警報。嵐跑下階梯，博卻先叫大家保持冷靜。新鏡子發出三次短閃，也就是「求救」，而他們曾答應絕不忽略這種訊號。可是花園空無一人。一個穿著校慶牛仔戲服的男孩從樹後走出來，承認自己按了鏡子的把手。「只是老套的惡作劇，」他勉強笑著說。嵐很生氣，但她請男孩先放下把手再談。沒人受傷，現在真正重要的是避免再發生。

The teacher wanted to punish the boy immediately. Lan suggested a different duty. He could help make a cover for the handle and explain to the younger children why the false alarm mattered. Bo drew a direct line from the garden to the nearest road. If a rescue team answered every false call, someone else with a real emergency might wait too long. The boy looked down at his new haircut and said he had not thought about that. Lan did not excuse what he had done. She told him that making a mistake and helping repair it were two different choices, and he still had the second one.

老師想立刻處罰男孩。嵐提出另一項責任：男孩可以幫忙替把手做保護罩，也要向年幼的孩子解釋假警報的嚴重性。博畫出從花園到最近道路的直線。若救援隊每次都得回應假求救，真正有緊急情況的人可能等太久。男孩低頭看著剛剪的新髮型，說自己從未想過。嵐沒有替他的行為開脫。她說犯錯和協助補救是不同的選擇，而他還有機會做後者。

At the workshop, Bo examined an old storage cell that might power a lamp after sunset. Beside it stood a dryer used to protect paper from rain. Lan asked him not to connect anything before an adult expert checked the wires. A worker brought an odd object wrapped in cloth: a brass tube from a retired river boat. The boat had once used a short whistle when it was ready to sail. Bo lifted the tube and said a sound signal could help when clouds blocked the light. Lan liked the thought, but it raised another problem. A sound might echo from the wrong hill and send a team in the wrong direction.

回到工坊，博檢查一個舊蓄電池，或許能在日落後為燈提供電力。旁邊有一部用來把紙張烘乾、防止受潮的乾燥機。嵐要他在成年人專家檢查電線之前，先不要接上任何東西。一名工人帶來用布包著的奇怪物件：退役河船上的黃銅管。那艘船以前準備啟航時會用短哨示意。博舉起銅管，說雲擋住光時，聲音訊號或許有用。嵐喜歡這想法，卻想到另一個問題：聲音可能從錯誤的山頭反射回來，把救援隊引向錯誤方向。

The evening market offered them another lesson. A woman selling lamb said she had heard three loud bangs the week before, followed by a single shot from a hunter's tool. The sounds had seemed to come from the river, but they were made in the hills. A newspaper writer had called the noise the start of a strike, and traders had closed their stalls in fear. Later everyone learned the report was false. Lan thought about the trouble caused by one guess. She wrote a new rule: a help signal must name the station where it started, and no second station may change its meaning. The children could now read the rule on a large board.

晚市又教會他們另一件事。一名賣羊肉的婦人說，上週聽見三聲巨響，接著又有獵人器具發出的一聲槍響。聲音像從河邊傳來，其實發生在山裡。一名報紙作者把噪音寫成罷工開始，商人們害怕得收起攤位。後來大家才知道報導不實。嵐想到一個猜測能造成多少麻煩。她新增規則：求救訊號必須說明最初發出的站點，任何轉送站都不能改變它的意思。孩子們現在可以在大木板上讀到這條規則。

The local trade school invited Lan to give a short tour of their signal project. A student asked why anyone should trust a line operated by people who had no flying power. Lan showed the protective cover the boy had made. She described the false alarm, the mistake, and the change in their rules. She did not hide the bad news in order to make the project look good. “We can improve a tool only when we admit what it cannot do,” she said. The students agreed to test the whistles later, but only in a quiet place where someone could check the source of every sound.

當地技藝學校邀嵐帶學生簡單參觀訊號計畫。有學生問，為什麼人們應相信一套由不會飛的人操作的路線。嵐拿出男孩做的保護罩，說明假警報、錯誤以及規則的修改。她沒有隱藏壞消息來美化計畫。「只有承認工具做不到什麼，才能改進它，」她說。學生同意日後測試哨聲，但必須選在安靜、能確認每道聲音來源的地方。

At closing time, the boy returned with a clean cover and a written apology. Lan checked that it fit over the handle without blocking the mirror itself. She then gave him a small task: when the next practice began, he would stand with the teacher and check the station name before any signal was sent. He looked relieved. Outside, a cold breeze moved through the trees, and the distant river carried a thin line of silver light. Lan knew the school had not yet received the letter. Nevertheless, she had learned that a safe message system needed more than a clever mirror. It needed people who could correct themselves.

收工時，男孩帶著乾淨的護罩和一封道歉信回來。嵐確認護罩能蓋住把手，卻不會擋住鏡面，然後交給男孩一份小任務：下次練習開始前，他要和老師一起核對站名，確認後才准發訊號。男孩鬆了一口氣。屋外冷風吹過樹林，遠處河流映出一道細銀光。嵐知道學校仍未收到那封信。但她已明白，安全的訊息制度需要的不只是一面聰明的鏡子，還需要願意修正自己的人。
