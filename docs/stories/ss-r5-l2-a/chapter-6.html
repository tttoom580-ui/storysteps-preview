The Day of Small Questions

不會飛的信使，替群山點燈 · 第 6 / 25 章 · 1000–2000 · 英中對照 · 校訂中

用互動版查詞、朗讀與收藏

On Monday, the school invited the signal team to its yearly sports day. Children played badminton beside a garden of cabbage, and their parents carried baskets of food. Lan had come to talk about a serious affair: the school was the last point on the northern route, yet no safe signal could reach it after dark. The head teacher was clever and knew that public excitement did not solve a crisis by itself. She offered a meeting room and invited every neighbor to hear the plan. Lan placed the boy's letter on the table. She explained why she had begun, but she did not open the envelope. It belonged to the boy and his sister, not to the meeting.

星期一，學校邀請訊號小隊參加一年一度的運動日。孩子們在種滿高麗菜的花園旁打羽毛球，家長則提著食物籃。嵐此行要談一件重要事情：學校是北方路線最後一個點，入夜後卻沒有安全訊號能抵達。校長很聰明，也明白群眾的興奮本身不能解決危機。她借出會議室，邀每位鄰居聽取計畫。嵐把男孩的信放在桌上，說明自己為何開始這件事，但沒有拆開信封。信屬於男孩和他姊姊，不屬於會議。

The school had an old engine in the shed, but Bo explained that turning it on to power a lamp might be unsafe without proper checks. A fireman from the lower village volunteered to inspect the shed. He also asked what would happen if the light failed during a storm. Lan answered that a human watcher would have to write down a failed signal and send a runner only when the road was safe. The children began to call the mirror line a wonderful invention. Their teacher reminded them that it was also a responsibility. A bright light could travel forth over the hills, but every person receiving it had to understand the same message.

學校棚屋裡有一部舊引擎，但博解釋，未經妥善檢查就啟動它替燈供電，可能不安全。下村的一名消防員自願查看棚屋，也問風暴中燈若熄滅怎麼辦。嵐回答，守望者必須記下失敗的訊號，只有道路安全時才派跑腿的人。孩子開始把鏡線稱作了不起的發明。老師提醒他們，那也代表責任。光可以向前跨過山丘，但接收它的每個人都得明白同一個意思。

During lunch, a visitor from the Sky Parcel Guild asked who would govern the new line. He warned that an individual could send a harmful message without permission. Lan agreed that the danger was real. She handed him her journal, open to the rules about station names and dates. She invited the guild to participate in the safety review, but not to control who could ask for help. The visitor studied the page. “Our culture is to trust trained flyers,” he said. “Your plan asks us to trust ordinary people.” Lan replied that ordinary people could also be trained, and trained people could also make mistakes. A clear record was useful in either case.

午餐時，天遞公會的訪客問，誰將管理新路線。他警告個人可能在未經允許下傳送有害訊息。嵐同意這種危險真實存在，便把日誌翻到站名和日期的規則，遞給他看。她邀公會參加安全審查，但不允許它決定誰能求助。訪客研究那一頁。「我們的文化是信任受訓的飛行者，」他說。「妳的計畫卻要我們信任普通人。」嵐回答，普通人也能接受訓練，而受訓者同樣可能犯錯。清楚的紀錄對雙方都有用。

A little girl brought Lan a pillow from the reading room so she could sit while her sore ankle rested. She wore blue socks with a red bird on each side. She asked why the north road was closed, and whether children were being kept there like people in a prison. Lan said the school was not a prison. Winter had made travel unsafe, but the pupils could speak with their families through trained adults, and people were working on a better route. The girl wanted to send a message to her father, a fisherman on the far shore. Lan took her request seriously and explained that the light could send only a short sign, not a full letter.

一個小女孩從閱覽室搬來枕頭，讓腳踝痠痛的嵐坐著休息。女孩穿著兩側繡紅鳥的藍襪子，問北路為何封閉，以及孩子是不是像監獄裡的人一樣被關著。嵐說學校不是監獄。冬季讓旅途不安全，但學生仍能透過受訓的大人聯絡家人，人們也正努力開闢更好的路。女孩想傳訊息給遠岸當漁夫的父親。嵐認真看待她的請求，說明光線只能傳簡短記號，不能傳一整封信。

In the afternoon, the school children showed the team their own ideas. One student wanted a larger mirror. Another suggested bells. The fireman asked them to list the risks beside the benefits. They worked in small groups, and every idea got a fair hearing. Bo demonstrated how the cover kept an accidental touch from sending a false alarm. Lan showed how two long flashes confirmed that a message had arrived. No one claimed the system was ready for the whole valley. Before leaving, the team tested one short route between the school garden and the farm. The reply arrived clearly. It was only one link, but it was the link the old route had lacked.

下午，學生向小隊展示自己的想法。有人想要更大的鏡子，有人建議使用鐘。消防員要求他們把每種方案的風險和好處並列。大家分組工作，每個想法都得到公平傾聽。博示範護罩如何防止誤觸引發假警報，嵐則展示兩次長閃代表訊息已收到。沒有人宣稱整座山谷的系統已經準備完成。離開前，小隊測試學校花園與農場之間的一段路線。回訊清楚抵達。這只是其中一段，卻正是舊路線缺少的那一段。

As the sun set, the head teacher gave Lan an empty page from the school journal. “Write the question you still cannot answer,” she said. Lan wrote: Who will be awake when a child needs help at night? The teacher promised to ask the adult staff about a watch plan, but she did not promise anyone's free time without asking first. Lan thanked her. Outside, the boy who had brought the letter waved from the gate. He had waited almost a week, yet he did not ask whether his sister had read it. He asked instead whether the light had reached the school. When Lan said yes, he smiled as if the mountains had become a little smaller.

日落時，校長從學校日誌撕下一張空白頁交給嵐。「寫下妳現在仍回答不了的問題，」她說。嵐寫道：夜裡有孩子求救時，誰會醒著守望？校長答應詢問成年教職員能否輪值，但沒有擅自承諾別人的休息時間。嵐向她道謝。屋外，最初帶來信的男孩在門口揮手。他已等近一週，卻沒問姊姊讀到信了沒有，而是問光有沒有到達學校。嵐說有，他笑得像群山稍微縮小了一點。
