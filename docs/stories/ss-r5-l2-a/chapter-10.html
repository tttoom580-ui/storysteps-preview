公會的紅旗

The Guild's Red Flag

不會飛的信使，替群山點燈 · 第 10 / 25 章 · 1000–2000 · 英中對照 · 校訂中

用互動版查詞、朗讀與收藏

On the day of the autumn fair, a red flag appeared beside the Sky Parcel Guild office. Its manager, Master Ke, said the village's light line might break an old transport law. He wore a neat black suit and carried a small wallet filled with travel cards. Lan felt concern but did not run away. She brought her notebook and asked which part of the law applied to a message that did not carry a person or goods. Ke gave her a document and pointed to one line. It described paid couriers, not short public warnings. Lan said she would ask the town clerk for a written opinion instead of fighting about the words in the street.

秋季市集那天，天遞公會辦公室旁升起一面紅旗。經理柯師傅宣稱，村裡的光訊號線可能違反舊交通法。他穿著整齊的黑色套裝，錢包裡裝滿交通卡。嵐雖然擔心，卻沒有逃避。她帶著筆記本，詢問哪條法律適用於不載人、不運貨的簡短訊息。柯師傅拿出文件，指著一行。那一行描述的是收費信使，不是公共警報。嵐說，自己會向鎮職員索取書面解釋，而非在街上爭論文字。

A crowd gathered. Some people wanted the guild to keep its riches; others thought the new line would make all flying messengers useless. Lan said neither idea was true. The valley still needed trained couriers to carry medicine and full letters. A light message could indicate where help was needed, not deliver the help itself. The manager asked what would occur if the sun disappeared behind clouds. Lan answered that they would mark the line closed until they had tested a safe night method. She pointed to the rule in her notebook. Her silence after that was deliberate. She wanted the crowd to read the rule rather than hear more promises.

人群聚集起來。有些人希望公會保住財富，有些人認為新路線會讓所有飛行信使失業。嵐說兩種想法都不正確。山谷仍需要受訓信使運送藥品和完整書信。光訊息只能指出哪裡需要幫助，不能自己把幫助送去。經理問，太陽被雲遮住會怎樣。嵐回答，在安全的夜間方法完成測試前，就將線路標記為關閉。她指向筆記裡的規則，之後故意保持沉默。她想讓人們閱讀規則，而不是繼續聽承諾。

The town clerk met Lan in a quiet room. She asked for the line's distance, the number of active posts, and its usual cycle of practice. She also asked who used it. Lan explained that the records included broad age groups and whether adults of each sex were able to take watch shifts, but no private health details. The clerk agreed that fairness and privacy could go together. A single child should be able to request help without having a personal story placed on a public board. She promised a formal answer in three days and told Lan to keep the normal rules until then.

鎮職員在安靜的房間與嵐見面，詢問路線距離、啟用站點數量與平常的練習週期，也問誰在使用。嵐說紀錄包含大致年齡層，以及不同性別的成年人能否輪值，但不記錄私人的健康細節。職員同意，公平和隱私可以兼顧。單一孩子應有權求助，而不必把個人故事貼在公告板上。她答應三天內給正式答覆，並請嵐在此之前維持現有規則。

Bo spent the afternoon checking the track to the north watch post. He moved a flat stone that had blocked a cart and placed a warning sign beside a steep drop. The work gained little attention at the fair, but it made the path safer. When Lan arrived, he showed her a diagram pointing forward through the valley. The arrow did not mean that people should run. It showed the safe direction to walk. They counted the distance again and agreed to test the route with a loaded cart before the next snowfall. A shining mirror was useless if its operators could not reach it safely.

博整個下午都在檢查通往北方守望站的小路。他移開一塊擋住推車的平石，並在陡坡旁豎起警示牌。這工作在熱鬧的市集中沒引起注意，卻讓路變得更安全。嵐到時，他拿出一張指向山谷前方的圖。箭頭不是叫人跑，而是指示安全的步行方向。他們重新量了距離，同意下雪前用載重推車測試路線。再明亮的鏡子，如果操作員無法安全到達，也毫無用處。

In the evening, Master Ke came to the post house without his red flag. He asked to see the false-message record from the northern ledge. Lan showed him the page but kept the child's name covered. Ke studied the drawing of the metal tube. “Someone may be testing your weak points,” he said. “That worries me too.” He did not admit that the guild was involved, and Lan did not accuse him. They agreed on one thing: a false call for rescue could endanger flyers as well as walkers. Ke offered to send one trained reviewer to the next practice. Lan accepted on the condition that the reviewer would follow the same public rules as everyone else.

傍晚，柯師傅沒有帶紅旗，獨自來到郵屋，要求看北方平台的假訊號紀錄。嵐給他看，卻遮住孩子的名字。柯師傅研究金屬管的圖。「有人可能在測試你們的弱點，」他說。「我也擔心。」他沒有承認公會涉入，嵐也沒有指控他。兩人至少同意一件事：假的救援請求不只危及步行者，也危及飛行信使。柯師傅願意派一名受訓審查員參加下次演練，嵐答應，條件是審查員必須遵守和其他人相同的公開規則。

The boy who owned the first letter visited that night. He asked whether the light line would still be there tomorrow. Lan told him about the review and the law, using words he could understand. She did not promise victory. Instead, she showed him the map with every safe station marked. He studied it, then said that grown people always seemed to fight about who owned the road. Lan looked at the valley outside. She understood why he was tired of waiting. “A road can have many builders,” she said, “but a call for help should not need a rich owner.” She would carry that thought into the coming meeting.

最初那封信的主人晚上來了。他問明天光線路還在不在。嵐用他聽得懂的話說明審查和法律，沒有保證會勝利，只把每個安全站點都標好的地圖給他看。男孩研究一會兒，說大人好像總在爭論誰擁有道路。嵐望向外面的山谷，理解他等得多麼厭倦。「一條路可以有許多建造者，」她說，「但求救不該一定要有富有的主人。」她會把這想法帶進接下來的會議。
