老石上的記號

The Marks on the Old Stone

不會飛的信使，替群山點燈 · 第 8 / 25 章 · 1000–2000 · 英中對照 · 校訂中

用互動版查詞、朗讀與收藏

An elder from the northern village invited Lan to visit an ancient stone gate. The gate had stood for more than a century, and small marks ran along its side. The elder said the marks were once used to tell travelers where to stop in bad weather. Lan asked for an instruction about reading them. He laughed and said there was no book, only stories that had been used for years. A used map hung beside the gate. When Bo tried to pose for a picture, the elder pointed him toward the safe ground. Bo wanted to walk backward along the road to compare the marks, but the elder stopped him. A person who could not see the next step could easily fall. They moved slowly and drew the stones one at a time.

北村一名長者邀嵐參觀古老石門。石門已立了超過一個世紀，側面刻著細小記號。長者說，以前旅人靠這些記號知道天氣不好時該停在哪裡。嵐問有沒有解讀它們的說明。長者笑說沒有書，只有傳了多年的故事。石門旁還掛著一張用舊的地圖。博想擺姿勢拍照時，長者指向安全的地面。博想倒退沿路比對記號，卻被長者制止。看不見下一步的人很容易跌倒。他們於是慢慢走，一塊石頭一塊石頭地畫下來。

The elder showed Lan a family symbol carved above each safe shelter. One looked like a peach, another like a grain of rice. At first she thought they marked food stores, but he explained that each sign gave the identity of a family that once maintained the shelter. The names mattered because travelers needed to know whom to thank and where to report damage. A stranger visiting the valley might not understand the signs, so Lan proposed adding a short written guide. The elder agreed as long as the original marks stayed visible. He did not want the new design to erase the people who had cared for the path before them.

長者指出每個安全避難處上方刻著的家族符號。一個像桃子，另一個像米粒。嵐起初以為那是在標示食物倉庫，長者卻說，每個符號都代表曾負責維護避難處的家族身分。名字很重要，旅人才能知道要謝誰、發現損壞要報告給誰。外來的陌生旅客可能看不懂，因此嵐提議加上簡短文字指引。長者同意，只要求保留原來的刻痕；他不想讓新設計抹去以前守護道路的人。

During the visit, the head teacher sent a short message that a pupil had a fever. The child's mother was trying to reach the school, but the northern bridge was wet. Lan and Bo used the signal line to ask whether she needed aid from the village clinic. The reply was yes. An adult nurse went to the child by the safe road. She did not tell the mother to swim across the stream or climb an unsafe wall. Lan watched the nurse pass the gate and felt relief. The light line had not cured the fever. It had helped the right person reach the right place without delay.

參訪時，校長傳來簡短訊息，說一名學生發燒。孩子的母親想趕到學校，但北橋濕滑。嵐和博透過訊號線詢問是否需要村診所協助，答覆是需要。一名成年護理師便循安全道路去看孩子，沒有叫母親游過溪流或攀危險的牆。嵐看著護理師通過石門，鬆了一口氣。光訊號線沒有治好發燒，卻讓對的人及時、安全地到達對的地方。

On the way back, Bo slipped and landed on his lap in the mud. A long tear appeared at one corner of his map. He felt foolish and wanted to throw it away, but Lan showed him how to press the two parts together until they could make a clean copy. The elder gave them a peach from his basket and smiled. “A torn page still teaches,” he said. Lan took the words seriously. She put the torn map in the records with a note about where Bo had fallen. Later workers could use that fact to choose a safer path. Their mistakes would not have to be repeated simply because no one wanted to remember them.

回程時，博滑倒在泥地裡，地圖一角也出現了一道長長的裂口。他覺得自己很笨，想把圖丟掉，嵐卻教他先把兩片紙壓好，之後可以重新抄繪。長者從籃子拿出一顆桃子送給他們，笑說：「破掉的紙也能教人。」嵐認真記住這句話，把破圖放進紀錄，並註明博跌倒的位置。以後的工人就能據此選更安全的路。他們不必因為不願記得錯誤，而讓別人重蹈覆轍。

Back at the workshop, Lan asked the students to make a new sign for the gate. It showed the three main shelter marks and the names of the people responsible for keeping them clean. She checked that the guide could be read by a typical traveler without special training. Bo added a small drawing of the steep path and a note saying not to use it in rain. The students asked whether the older symbols should be painted over to match the new signs. Lan said no. The old and new marks could stand together. A useful future did not require people to forget the past.

回到工坊，嵐請學生替石門製作新標誌，畫出三種主要避難記號，以及負責清潔維護的人名。她確認普通旅人不必特殊訓練也看得懂。博加上一幅陡路小圖，註明下雨不可通行。學生問要不要把舊符號蓋上新漆，讓它們配合新標誌。嵐說不用。新舊記號可以並存；要有有用的未來，不代表必須忘記過去。

That evening, the elder visited the post house to see the finished guide. He traced his finger along the picture of the peach and said his grandmother had once cared for that shelter. Lan asked whether the story could be added to the local history book. He gave permission, then corrected the date she had written. She thanked him. Before he left, he noticed the boy's letter lying in a wooden tray. Lan explained that the message line had now reached the school, but the road was still closed to ordinary travelers. The elder offered to carry the letter when the winter road was judged safe. Lan accepted, and they signed the plan together.

當晚，長者來郵屋看完成的指引，手指沿著桃子的圖案滑過，說祖母曾照顧那處避難屋。嵐問能不能把故事寫進地方史冊。他同意了，還更正她寫錯的年份。嵐向他道謝。離開前，長者看見木盤裡男孩的信。嵐說訊號已到學校，但山路仍不對一般旅人開放。長者主動提出，等冬路正式確認安全後替他送信。嵐接受，兩人一起簽下計畫。
