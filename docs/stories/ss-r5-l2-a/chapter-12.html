空白的一頁

The Blank Page

不會飛的信使，替群山點燈 · 第 12 / 25 章 · 1000–2000 · 英中對照 · 校訂中

用互動版查詞、朗讀與收藏

On the next cloudy day, a customer arrived from the countryside with a wet letter. He had come from a small settlement near the capital and wanted to know whether the signal line could carry a message to his sick father. Lan explained its limit with respect. It could send a short request to a clinic, but not the long letter in his hand. The customer looked scared and said the road had become dangerous after a sudden fall of rocks. Bo asked for the direction of the fall and took a measurement of the safe path still open. The customer drew the place in ink on a blank page of Lan's notebook.

接下來一個陰天，一名客人帶著濕信從鄉下趕來。他住在靠近首都的一個小聚落，想知道訊號線能否把信送給生病的父親。嵐尊重地說明限制：它能送出給診所的簡短請求，卻不能傳遞手中的長信。客人看起來很害怕，說最近突然落石，讓道路變危險。博詢問落石方向，量了仍可通行的安全道路距離。客人用墨水在嵐筆記本的空白頁畫下地點。

Mrs. Sun invited the traveler to drink tea while they checked the weather. Her teapot sat on a low table beside a plate of fries and warm pudding. The traveler was still wearing wet slippers because he had crossed a stream in a hurry. Lan gave him dry socks from the station supply box. He hardly touched the food at first. He was afraid people would blame him for choosing the wrong road. Lan said he had come for help and had done the useful thing by reporting the rockfall. She added his description to the public danger map without placing his name on it. The road mattered more than his embarrassment.

孫太太請旅人喝茶，大家一邊查看天氣。她的茶壺放在矮桌上，旁邊是一盤薯條和溫布丁。旅人急著過溪，還穿著濕拖鞋。嵐從站內用品箱拿出乾襪子給他。旅人起初幾乎不碰食物，害怕大家責怪自己選錯路。嵐說他是來求助的，並且做了有用的事：報告落石。她把描述加進公共危險地圖，卻不寫上他的姓名。比起他的難堪，道路情況更重要。

The town dentist was one of the evening watchers. She had learned to keep careful records when helping patients, and she agreed to check the spelling of the settlement name. She pointed out that two villages had almost the same name. If a warning reached the wrong place, its effect could be serious. Lan decided that every message should include a station number as well as a name. The dentist tested the new form with Bo, reading each number twice. They found one confusing mark and changed its shape. Small changes like this were not exciting, but they made the line easier to use when people were tired.

鎮上的牙醫也是晚班守望者。她替病人工作時習慣仔細記錄，願意幫忙核對聚落名字的拼法。她指出有兩個村莊名稱極為相似；警訊若傳錯地方，後果可能嚴重。嵐決定每則訊息除站名外還必須加站號。牙醫和博一起測試新格式，每個數字都讀兩遍。他們發現一個容易混淆的記號，便改了寫法。這類小改變不刺激，卻能讓疲累的人更容易正確使用訊號線。

A storm reached the valley before dark. The team had planned a light test but called it off because the sky was too thick with clouds. One young volunteer wanted to try anyway. Lan asked him to regard the closed line as a safety decision, not an insult to his courage. They took the mirrors inside and put their notes in dry bags. A sudden gust blew open the watch room door, and rain covered the desk. For a moment Lan feared that the day's work was lost. Then Bo found the map under the shelf, and Mrs. Sun saved the visitor's drawing. The original note had become wet, but they had made a copy.

天黑前，風暴抵達山谷。隊伍原訂做光線測試，但雲層太厚，只好取消。一名年輕志工還想試試。嵐請他把線路關閉看成安全決定，不是對勇氣的侮辱。大家把鏡子收進室內，把筆記裝進防水袋。突然一陣狂風吹開守望室的門，雨水打濕書桌。嵐一時害怕整天成果都毀了。幸好博在架子下找到地圖，孫太太也救下旅人的畫。原稿濕了，但他們保有副本。

At the clinic, the nurse received the short message about the sick father through a runner who had used the safe road. She sent back advice to contact a doctor in person. Lan felt sorry that the light line could do no more that evening, yet she refused to pretend otherwise. The traveler thanked her because somebody had taken his question seriously. He asked whether the team could store his letter until a courier was available. Lan agreed and wrote a receipt showing the date and the closed state of the line. She told him exactly when to return for news, and he went to rest in Mrs. Sun's guest room.

診所那邊，一名走安全道路的跑腿者把病父的簡短訊息交給護理師。護理師回覆，仍需親自聯絡醫生。嵐很遺憾光訊號線今晚幫不上更多忙，卻拒絕假裝有其他能力。旅人感謝她，因為終於有人認真看待問題。他問能不能暫存信件，等信使可出發再送。嵐同意，開了收據，記上日期與線路關閉狀態，還告訴他何時回來查詢。旅人便到孫太太的客房休息。

After midnight, Lan opened the notebook to the page where the traveler had first drawn the fallen rocks. The ink had spread, leaving much of the page blank. But the copy on the wall was clear. She thought about how easy it was to lose a small fact and how difficult it might be to recover it. She wrote a new instruction: any warning about a closed road must be copied at two places before it is sent on. Tomorrow the storm might end, and the mirrors might shine again. Tonight the most important work was to keep people informed that the line was not open. The empty sky was also a message.

午夜過後，嵐打開筆記本，翻到旅人最初畫落石的那頁。墨水暈開，大片紙面變成模糊的空白，但牆上的副本仍很清楚。她想，一個小事實多容易遺失，之後又可能多難找回。她新增規則：任何封路警報都要先在兩處備份，才可繼續轉送。明天風暴也許停了，鏡子也許又能閃亮。今晚最重要的工作，是讓人知道線路沒有開放。空白的天空本身也是一則訊息。
