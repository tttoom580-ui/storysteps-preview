第一批守望者

The First Watchers

不會飛的信使，替群山點燈 · 第 11 / 25 章 · 1000–2000 · 英中對照 · 校訂中

用互動版查詞、朗讀與收藏

The new training day began with twelve people, including a beginner from the Sky Parcel Guild. Her name was Ada, and she had come to check the line for Master Ke. Lan greeted her without asking her to support the project. Every trainee received a mug of tea and a page of rules. Lan used a small iron plate to demonstrate how a mirror should be turned and why no one must shoot a strong flash directly into another person's eyes. She wanted to emphasize the goal of the course: send one true message and know whether it had been received. A clever mirror was not enough. The people using it needed skill and care.

新訓練日有十二人參加，其中包括一名來自天遞公會的初學者，名叫艾達。她奉柯師傅之命檢查路線。嵐迎接她，卻不要求她支持計畫。每個學員都拿到一杯茶和一張規則紙。嵐用小鐵板示範如何轉鏡，也解釋為什麼不能把強光直接射入別人的眼睛。她想強調課程目標：送出一則真實訊息，並確定對方已收到。聰明的鏡子並不足夠，使用它的人需要技巧與細心。

Ada asked how much the watchers would charge a family. Lan said the HELP signal would be free, while other short messages might need a small shared fee to pay for supplies. She showed Ada the account book, which held no hidden line for gold or private gifts. “Why work so hard if there is no reward?” Ada asked. Lan pointed to the children learning outside. “Because a warning should reach them even when their parents have no money,” she said. Ada did not answer at once. She had grown up in a guild where success meant a high place on a list of paid deliveries. Lan's answer was simply different.

艾達問守望者要向家庭收多少錢。嵐說求救訊號免費，其他簡短訊息日後可能收少量共同費用，以支付用品。她拿出帳本，裡面沒有金子或私人禮物的暗藏項目。「沒有報酬，為什麼這麼努力？」艾達問。嵐指著外面學習的孩子說：「因為就算父母沒錢，警訊也應該送到他們那裡。」艾達一時沒答話。她在公會長大，成功通常代表有很多付費送件；嵐的答案只是不同。

At lunch, the hikers brought grape jam and fresh bread. A discussion about religion began when one volunteer said the mountains were protected by an old temple. Another volunteer believed the hills were safe only because people worked on the paths. Lan asked them to respect both views and return to the safety plan. Their social beliefs did not have to match for them to watch the same signal. Mrs. Sun reminded everyone of the importance of a clear record. “You may trust your partner,” she said, “but still check what your partner wrote.” Ada nodded. She understood that rule from flying work, where a mistaken number could send a courier to the wrong village.

午餐時，健行協會帶來葡萄果醬和新鮮麵包。一名志工說山受到古廟保護，另一人則認為山路安全是因為人們努力維護，於是談起宗教。嵐請大家尊重彼此觀點，回到安全計畫。他們不必擁有相同的社會信念，才能看守同一道訊號。孫太太提醒大家清楚紀錄的重要。「你可以信任搭檔，」她說，「但仍要檢查搭檔寫了什麼。」艾達點頭。飛行工作也有這項規則，數字錯誤就可能把信使送錯村莊。

During practice, a young watcher tried to chase a moving light across the yard. He nearly stepped into a trap made of loose rope and tools left by the repair team. Bo stopped him before he fell. Lan gathered everyone and explained that their usual habit of following a flash with their eyes could not become a habit of running after it with their feet. They moved the rope, marked the work area, and placed a sign on the ground. Ada helped. She said the guild had the same rule around landing places. Lan thanked her for bringing that knowledge. A reviewer did not need to be an enemy to notice a useful change.

練習時，一名年輕守望者想追著院子裡移動的光點跑，差點踏進修理隊留下的鬆繩和工具陷阱。博在他跌倒前阻止他。嵐召集眾人，解釋平常用眼睛追光的習慣，不能變成用腳追光。大家移開繩子，劃出工作區，在地上立標誌。艾達也幫了忙，說公會降落處有同樣規則。嵐感謝她提供經驗。審查者不必是敵人，也能指出有用的改變。

The last test was simple. Each pair received a short message and had to pass it to the next post without changing a word. One pair made a mistake and sent the wrong station name. The next pair noticed it and asked for a repeat. No one was blamed. Lan explained that the test had worked because the mistake was caught before it traveled farther. Ada wrote the result in her own book and asked for a copy of the rules. She said the guild might learn from them. Lan smiled but did not ask for praise. She cared more that a future emergency would not depend on someone's memory alone.

最後一項測試很簡單。每組拿到一則短訊息，必須一字不改地送往下一站。有一組傳錯站名，下一組發現後要求重傳。沒有人因此受責備。嵐說測試成功，是因為錯誤在繼續傳播前被攔住。艾達把結果寫進自己的本子，還索取一份規則副本。她說公會也許能從中學習。嵐笑了，卻沒有求表揚。她更在乎未來的緊急情況不必只靠某個人的記憶。

At sunset, Ada approached Lan beside the first mirror. She said Master Ke wanted to see a real test, not a lesson for children. Lan replied that the lesson had been real because the mistakes were real, even though the messages were for practice. She offered Ke a place at the next full trial. Ada agreed to carry the invitation. As they watched the sky turn orange, Lan remembered the first boy and his letter. She hoped the line would soon do more than prove an idea. It might give families an honest way to know whether someone they loved was safe.

日落時，艾達來到第一面鏡子旁，告訴嵐柯師傅想看真正的測試，而不是給孩子上的課。嵐回答，課雖然用練習訊息，錯誤卻是真的，所以同樣是正式訓練。她邀柯師傅參加下一次全線測試，艾達答應轉達。兩人看著天空轉橘，嵐想起最初的男孩與他的信。她希望這條線很快能做的不只是證明構想，還能讓家庭真正知道自己愛的人是否平安。
