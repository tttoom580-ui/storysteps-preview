看得見彼此的山丘

The Hill That Could See

不會飛的信使，替群山點燈 · 第 2 / 25 章 · 1000–2000 · 英中對照

Early the next morning, Lan climbed to a high spot with Bo and a wooden board. They needed to find the correct angle between their first mirror and the north ridge. The path passed a barber's shop, where an old man pointed toward a trail that did not appear on their map. “I appreciate that you want to help,” he said, “but avoid the loose stones after the bridge.” He had watched a deer fall there last spring. A dove rested on the roof while he spoke. Lan listened, then marked the dangerous place clearly. A map that showed only the shortest road would not be useful to a real traveler.

第二天一早，嵐和博帶著木板爬上高處。他們得找出第一面鏡子與北方山脊之間正確的角度。路經理髮店時，一位老人指向地圖上沒有的小徑。「我很感謝你們想幫忙，」他說，「但過橋後要避開鬆動的石頭。」去年春天，他曾看見一頭鹿在那裡跌倒。他說話時，一隻白鴿停在屋頂。嵐認真聽完，把危險地點清楚標在圖上。只畫最短道路的地圖，對真正的旅人並沒有用。

At the top of the hill stood a huge empty frame. Someone had removed its glass long ago. Under the frame was a box with a lantern and a thin book. The book was an old edition of the hill watch records. It named five places where signal posts had once existed. One particular page showed a dark gap between the third and fourth hills. The school lay behind that gap. Bo said they needed to find out why the old organization had left it empty. Lan copied the drawing. She did not yet know whether a broken signal route could be made whole, but at least its shape was beginning to appear.

山頂立著一個巨大的空框，玻璃早就被人拆走。框下有個盒子，裡面放著一盞提燈和一本薄冊。那是舊版的山丘守望紀錄，列出從前五個訊號站的位置。其中一頁特別畫出第三座與第四座山頭之間的黑色空隙，學校就在那個空隙之後。博說，得查出舊組織為什麼把那裡空下來。嵐抄下圖。她還不知道斷掉的訊號路線能不能接起來，但至少開始看見它原本的樣子。

The president of the local hikers' organization met them by the gate. Her name was Mrs. Sun. She explained that a minority of families on the west side had never joined the old watch system, not because they refused help, but because they could not afford its fees. That was a painful truth. Lan asked whether the president could help her speak with those families before any new station was built. Mrs. Sun agreed. She also warned that the Sky Parcel Guild owned the best mirrors in the valley. Its manager disliked free messages. “Then we must be careful,” Lan said. “But first we must listen.”

當地健行協會的會長在門口遇見他們。她叫孫太太。她解釋，西側少數家庭從未加入舊守望制度，不是因為他們拒絕幫助，而是負擔不起費用。這是令人難受的事實。嵐問會長能不能協助她先和那些家庭談談，再決定新站的位置。孫太太答應了，也提醒他們，天遞公會擁有山谷裡最好的鏡子，而經理不喜歡免費訊息。「那就得小心，」嵐說，「不過我們得先聽人說話。」

The west-side families welcomed them with hot tea and a bowl of sour fruit. A young teacher placed a textbook on the table and showed a lesson about light. She asked whether the old mirror could still work under a cloudy sky. Bo said it could not, at least not in the same way. Lan was glad he told the truth instead of promising too much. A child pointed to a bright spot on the distant rock face. It was sunlight reflected from a kitchen window. From there, Lan could see the empty frame on the first hill. The gap on the old drawing might be crossed by a new station placed beside the school garden.

西側居民用熱茶和一碗酸水果招待他們。一名年輕教師把教科書放在桌上，翻到光線的一課，問陰天時舊鏡子還能不能工作。博說，至少不能用同樣的方法運作。嵐很高興他說的是實話，而不是胡亂保證。一個孩子指向遠處岩壁上的亮點，那是廚房窗戶反射的陽光。從那裡，嵐看得見第一座山上的空框。也許在學校花園旁加建一站，就能跨過舊圖上的缺口。

In the afternoon they returned to the frame and tried a simple test. Bo held the board upright while Lan turned a piece of polished metal. Mrs. Sun watched from the village path and waved when she saw the light. The distance was not great, but it was much longer than the yard behind the post house. Lan used a piece of white cloth to make the flashes easier to see. Three short lights meant HELP. Two long lights meant RECEIVED. She practiced them slowly until everyone could tell the difference. The children wanted to play with the mirror, but she explained that a false call for help might send a person onto a dangerous winter road.

下午，他們回到空框旁做簡單測試。博扶直木板，嵐轉動磨亮的金屬片。孫太太站在村道上看，見到亮光便揮手。距離不算遠，卻遠超過郵屋後的院子。嵐拿白布讓閃光更清楚。三次短閃是求救，兩次長閃是收到。她緩慢練習，直到每個人都能分辨。孩子們也想玩鏡子，但她解釋，假的求救訊號可能害人走上危險的冬季山路。

Before dark, Mrs. Sun led them to the garden where the new post might stand. From that place they could see two hills and a narrow part of the northern road. The school itself was still hidden. Lan thought of the boy's letter in her bag. She had not solved the whole problem, and her fingers hurt from carrying the wooden board. Still, a line now existed on the map where there had been only an empty gap. Mrs. Sun offered a room for their notes and promised to ask the families what times they could help. Lan accepted the room but asked for no money until they had tested the full route.

天黑前，孫太太帶他們到可能設置新站的花園。從那裡能看見兩座山頭和一段北路，學校本身仍藏在山後。嵐想起背包裡男孩的信。問題還沒解決，搬木板也讓她手指發疼，但地圖上原本空白的地方如今有了一條線。孫太太願意借出房間存放筆記，也答應詢問居民能提供哪些時段的協助。嵐接受房間，卻說在整條路線測試完畢之前，不收任何費用。
