Xia discovered that a paragraph had been removed from her original charity investigation. It explained that volunteer Wei Min had asked for an audit rather than defended the director. Without it, readers believed Wei had hidden wrongdoing. Her editor claimed the cut was necessary for length. Xia checked the revision history and found it had been made after legal review, by an account assigned to senior management. She requested the reason in writing. The missing paragraph did not prove who sent the flowers, but it showed how editorial decisions could expose innocent people to danger. Xia contacted Wei, apologized for the omission, and offered to publish a correction even if the newsroom objected.
