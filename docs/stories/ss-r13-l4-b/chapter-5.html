The Festival Notice

1998，長堤種子行的第七號紙袋 · 第 5 / 25 章 · 3000–4000 · 英中對照 · 校訂中

The harvest festival committee offered Long Embankment Seed Shop a narrow table near the river. It sounded favorable until Yingrong read the conditions: only approved brands could appear on signs, and no merchant could display a complaint. The rule would force her to abandon the customer board. A committee member called it a small adjustment for the sake of prosperity. Mother was tempted; their monthly allowance for electricity had almost vanished into the greenhouse tests. Xiao-an placed a portable moisture device in its wooden case and asked whether they could demonstrate it without naming a company. Yingrong decided that a public test was not a protest unless people tried to silence it. She wrote an objection to the sign rule, using plain words so customers could judge it themselves.

豐收節委員會提供長堤種子行一張河邊小攤位，聽起來很有利，直到映蓉讀到條件：招牌上只能出現核准品牌，商家不得展示抱怨內容。這項規則迫使她放棄顧客看板。一名委員說，為了地方繁榮，做點小調整沒什麼。母親有些動心；她們每月用來付電費的預算幾乎都花在溫室檢驗了。小安把可攜式濕度裝置放進木盒，問可否不提公司名稱，只做操作示範。映蓉認為，公開測試本身不算抗議，除非有人試圖禁止它。她用淺白文字寫下對招牌規則的異議，讓顧客自己判斷。

At rehearsal, a student orchestra played beside a children's canoe exhibition. A young performer had written an imaginative song about a river carrying seeds to new fields. His favorite idol was a radio singer, but he knew this tune had to stand on its own. The music stopped when a member of the festival board tried to seize the students' noticeboard. He said the word defective might hurt local trade. Yingrong stepped between him and the display without touching him. The notice named no guilty seller; it stated the testing method and an uncertain result. She asked which rule allowed the board to remove it. He could not produce one in writing. The interruption drew a small crowd, some sympathetic, some angry, most simply curious. The impact of that interruption was larger than the committee member expected: people began asking to read the written rule.

彩排時，學生管弦樂團在孩子們的獨木舟展示旁演奏。一名年輕表演者寫了一首富有想像力的歌，唱河流如何把種子帶到新田。他崇拜的偶像是電臺歌手，但知道這首曲子必須有自己的樣子。當一名節慶委員試圖奪走學生公告板時，音樂停了。他說「不合格」一詞可能傷害地方生意。映蓉走到他與看板之間，沒有碰他。公告沒有指名任何有罪賣家，只寫了測試方法與仍未確定的結果。她請對方指出哪條書面規定允許委員會撤下它。他拿不出來。這次打斷吸引了一群人，有人同情，有人生氣，多數人只是好奇。 這次打斷造成的影響比委員預期更大：人們開始要求閱讀書面規則。

Mother watched from the edge of the crowd, her anxiety visible in the way she held her shopping bag. A seed shop had to remain distinct from a campaign office, she later told Yingrong. It needed customers, not endless arguments. Yingrong understood. She enlarged the board's letters so older farmers could read them, then removed a sentence that sounded more certain than the evidence allowed. The word she chose was sometimes a matter of a single syllable. A local proverb, an idiom about planting before the rain, helped her explain why a decision could not wait forever. But she also said that the same weather would affect different varieties differently. An honest claim could still guide action without pretending every farm was identical.

母親站在人群邊緣看著，從她緊抓購物袋的樣子就看得出焦慮。種子行必須和競選辦公室有所區別，她後來告訴映蓉。店需要顧客，不需要無止境的爭吵。映蓉理解。她放大看板字體，讓年長農民能讀，再刪掉一句比證據更肯定的話。有時她選用的措辭只差一個音節。當地一句關於雨前播種的慣用語，幫她解釋為何不能永遠等下去。但她也說，同樣天氣會對不同品種造成不同影響。誠實的說法仍能幫助行動，不必假裝每片田都一樣。

By afternoon, three customers signed up for a second trial. One had grown the same seed near the river and reported a much better result than Fengzhi's field. That account might have a favorable explanation: different soil, different dates, or a different packet despite similar printing. Yingrong did not seize on the good story as proof that nothing was wrong. She wrote the conditions beside the result and asked the farmer to keep his remaining seed in a dry jar. A visitor suggested making a larger exhibit next year, but Yingrong said they had to finish this year's work first. Mother quietly counted the orders from other customers. The money would not solve their debt, yet it covered the cost of the next set of trays.

下午，有三位顧客報名參加第二輪試種。一人曾在河邊播同一種子，結果比鳳枝的田好得多。這件事可能有合理解釋：土壤不同、日期不同，或雖印相似標籤卻根本不是同批貨。映蓉沒有急著把好消息當作一切沒問題的證明。她把種植條件和結果並列，請農民把剩下的種子存進乾燥的罐子。訪客建議明年辦更大的展示，但映蓉說必須先做完今年的工作。母親安靜地數著其他顧客的訂單。這筆錢不能解決債務，卻足夠支付下一批淺盤的成本。

After the festival, Mother and daughter sat behind the shop listening to the distant orchestra pack away its instruments. Mother admitted that the board had not destroyed their trade. Yingrong admitted that she had overlooked how much a public argument could frighten a shopkeeper already close to closing. They agreed on a new rule: customer records would be open to the people who supplied them, but personal addresses would stay covered. Every new sample would have a date, a source, and two signatures when possible. They also decided to reserve a little money for emergency refunds before ordering more goods. Xiao-an wrote the agreement on the back of the old fair notice. The page was ordinary, yet it was the first business plan the three had made together.

節慶結束後，母女坐在店後，聽遠處樂團收拾樂器。母親承認看板沒有毀掉生意；映蓉也承認自己忽略了公開爭論會讓快關門的店主多麼害怕。她們訂出新規則：顧客可以查看自己提供的紀錄，但私人地址必須遮住。每份新樣本要標明日期、來源，能做到時由兩人簽名。她們還決定先留下一點緊急退款金，再訂新貨。小安把協議寫在舊市集公告的背面。那是一張普通的紙，卻是三人第一次一起完成的經營計畫。
