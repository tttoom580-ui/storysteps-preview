春天的第七號紙袋

The Seventh Packet in Spring

1998，長堤種子行的第七號紙袋 · 第 25 / 25 章 · 3000–4000 · 英中對照 · 校訂中

The school held a spring planting championship beside the river, but the seed shop did not enter as a competitor. Yingrong served on a small panel evaluating whether the children's labels were clear and the plants safely handled. One boy brought a tortoise he had found near the field; the teachers asked him to return it to its familiar shelter rather than keep it as a prize. Another child nearly placed poisonous berries beside edible seeds, and the adults moved them apart before anyone tasted anything. A girl carrying a spade asked why caution mattered so much at a feast. Yingrong said that a fantastic afternoon should still end with everyone going home safely. She wrote the correction in the exhibition register so the class would remember it next year.

學校在河邊舉辦春季種植錦標賽，種子行沒有以參賽者身分加入。映蓉擔任小組評審，檢查孩子的標籤是否清楚、植物是否安全處理。一名男孩帶來田邊找到的烏龜，老師請他送回熟悉的棲所，不要留作獎品。另一名孩子差點把有毒莓果放在可食種子旁，大人在有人品嚐前先分開。一名拿著鏟子的女孩問，為什麼快樂宴會也需要這麼謹慎。映蓉說，美好的下午仍應讓每個人安全回家。她把修正記在展覽登記簿，讓班級明年也記得。

At the shop, Xiao-an checked the new electronic scale against the old balance weights. The electronics made counting faster, but Yingrong required a manual check each week. She had gone broke once during her years in the city, and she knew that a small business could fail even after making every reasonable effort. The shop's latest finance statement showed a modest surplus, enough for wages, rent, and a reserve for refunds, but not enough for expansion. Mother praised the result without pretending it was a fortune. She had registered for a gardening class as a student, something she had not allowed herself in decades. Her profession had given her a life, and now she wanted a life beyond that profession as well.

店裡，小安用舊天平砝碼校正新的電子秤。電子設備讓計數更快，映蓉仍要求每週人工核對。她過去在城市曾一度破產，知道小生意即使做了所有合理努力也可能失敗。最新財務表顯示小幅盈餘，足夠支付工資、房租並預留退款準備金，卻不足以擴張。母親稱讚這個結果，沒有假裝它是一筆財富。她報名了一門園藝課，這次是以學生身分參加，而不是多年來只能教別人。她的職業給了她一段人生，如今她還想擁有職業之外的人生。

Golden Field sent the last scheduled payment for the seventh lot's documented damage. Its public publication of corrected storage procedures had been reviewed by the district inspector. The company was not required to close, but it had to preserve records and obey new inspection terms. Fang Dingchuan visited to say he had transferred to a position where he could work on training rather than aggressive sales. He admitted he felt guilty for having dismissed the first complaints, even though he had not known the full problem. Yingrong accepted his apology without offering to erase the past. She said no one should impose forgiveness or blame as a condition of doing business. The record would remain available, including the mistakes they had all made and corrected. The public notice concerning the company's corrective actions described the labeling error, but did not declare any unproved criminal offense.

金田付清第七批貨文件化損失的最後一期賠款。公司公開出版的修訂保存程序已經區域檢查員審查。公司不必關閉，卻必須保存紀錄並遵守新檢查條件。方定川來說，他已調到訓練職務，不再主要負責強勢推銷。他承認當初輕視申訴令他感到內疚，雖然當時並不知道完整問題。映蓉接受他的道歉，卻不答應抹去過去。她說，任何人都不應把原諒或責怪強加成做生意的條件。紀錄會一直保留，包括大家曾犯下、後來修正的錯誤。 有關公司改善措施的公開公告描述了標籤錯誤，卻沒有宣稱任何未證實的刑事罪行。

In the afternoon, a farmer from a different ethnic community arrived with samples of a traditional grain. Yingrong asked how he wanted its name written and whether the shop had permission to test it. He appreciated being asked rather than having the variety treated as a curiosity. Together they planned a small trial, with the results belonging first to the growers who provided the seed. Outside, festival banners swayed above the river. Xiao-an glanced at the original seventh paper packet displayed behind glass. Its label was now accompanied by dates, the storage report, and a note explaining which questions remained unanswered. The packet had never contained a secret miracle. It had carried an error that people chose to face rather than hide.

下午，一名來自不同族群社區的農民帶著傳統穀物樣本來。映蓉問他希望如何書寫名稱，以及店是否得到測試許可。農民很高興她先問，而不是把品種當成稀奇物。兩人規劃一個小型試種，並約定結果先交給提供種子的農戶。門外，節慶旗幟在河上搖擺。小安看向玻璃櫃裡原本的第七號紙袋。標籤旁現在附了日期、保存報告，以及仍未回答的問題。紙袋從未藏著神奇祕密；它承載的錯誤，是人們選擇面對而非掩藏的事。

At sunset, Mother locked the counter and handed the keys to Yingrong. It was not a surrender; it was an agreed transfer of daily responsibility. The family had sold the unsafe warehouse after approved repairs and reduced the debt, while keeping only the small rented shop they could afford. Fengzhi's second planting had produced a useful harvest, though some rows still failed. No champion had saved the town. People had tested, argued, repaired, paid, and learned. Yingrong stepped outside and watched the wind move through the young fields. She understood that preserving seed meant more than keeping it in a packet; it meant preserving the right to know what was inside, to choose whether to plant, and to begin again after a mistake. She turned off the light and went home for dinner.

日落時，母親鎖上櫃檯，把鑰匙交給映蓉。這不是投降，而是雙方同意移交日常責任。一家人在核准修繕後賣掉不安全倉庫，減輕債務，只保留負擔得起的小租店。鳳枝第二次播種得到實用收成，雖然仍有幾行失敗。沒有冠軍英雄拯救小鎮。人們檢驗、爭論、修理、付款，也學習。映蓉走出店外，看風穿過年輕田地。她明白，保存種子不只是把它留在紙袋裡，還要保存知道袋裡有什麼、選擇要不要播、犯錯後能再開始的權利。她關上燈，回家吃晚飯。
