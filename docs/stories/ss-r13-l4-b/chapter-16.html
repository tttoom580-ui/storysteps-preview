臨時試驗室

The Temporary Chamber

1998，長堤種子行的第七號紙袋 · 第 16 / 25 章 · 3000–4000 · 英中對照 · 校訂中

The district school offered a small chamber beside its science classroom as temporary storage. The room was functional but messy, with an old overcoat hanging from a hook and boxes of broken teaching equipment beneath the bench. Yingrong and Xiao-an cleaned it without touching the materials assigned to other classes. They needed to eliminate dust and disorder before bringing in any samples. A teacher of physics checked the thermometer and explained how airflow changed the speed of drying. Yingrong recorded the restrictions on the door: only labeled samples, no open food, and no entry without the key holder. She felt desperate to reopen the store, but desperation did not make an unsafe room suitable. Mother inspected the place from the doorway and said it was small enough to manage if they counted every box.

區立學校提供科學教室旁的一間小房當臨時倉庫。房間可以使用，卻十分凌亂：舊大衣掛在掛鉤上，工作檯下堆著壞掉的教具。映蓉和小安清理時，不碰其他課堂指定的材料。她們必須先消除灰塵和混亂，才能搬入樣本。物理老師檢查溫度計，說明氣流如何改變乾燥速度。映蓉在門上記下限制：只收有標籤的樣本、不准開放食物、沒有鑰匙保管人在場不能進入。她急著讓店重新開張，但焦急不會使不安全的房間變得適合。母親從門口檢視，說只要每箱都算清楚，這空間就還能管理。

A neighbor's funeral brought the family back to the village temple that weekend. The dead man had once repaired the irrigation gates after a storm. People spoke about his creation of a simple water channel, not about titles or prizes. Yingrong heard his daughter confess that she had wanted him to stop working years earlier. 'He was useful to everyone,' the daughter said, 'but he forgot what he wanted for himself.' Mother stood quietly through the service. On the way home she admitted that the words troubled her. Yingrong did not turn the funeral into an argument about retirement. She walked beside Mother in the rain and let the silence remain. There were decisions a person had to reach herself, even when other people thought they knew the answer. His family remembered his humanity as clearly as his skill with irrigation tools.

那週末，鄰居的葬禮讓一家人回到村裡的廟。死者曾在暴風雨後修理灌溉水閘。人們談起他創造的簡易水道，而不是頭銜或獎牌。映蓉聽見他的女兒坦白，自己多年前就希望父親停止工作。「他對所有人都有用，」女兒說，「卻忘了自己想要什麼。」母親安靜地站完全程。回家路上，她承認那些話讓自己難以平靜。映蓉沒有把葬禮變成退休辯論，只在雨中陪母親走，任沉默存在。有些決定必須由當事人自己做，即使旁人自認知道答案。 家人記得他的仁愛，也記得他修理灌溉工具的技術。

At the temporary workroom, Pan found a plot of land behind the school suitable for the next trial. Its rainfall had been recorded for twenty years, although the figures were incomplete in certain months. A young volunteer wanted to sew a cloth cover for the seed trays; Yingrong thanked her and asked whether the fabric could be cleaned without damaging the labels. The teacher suggested a trial using three small covers rather than one large one. The women interacted with the students as equals in the decision, allowing them to ask questions and reject unsafe methods. Xiao-an was drowsy after a late shift and nearly dropped a tray. Yingrong sent him home. The work could wait; nobody gained reliable results from an exhausted helper.

在臨時工作室，潘維找到學校後方一小塊適合下輪試種的地。那裡有二十年降雨紀錄，雖然部分月份不完整。一名年輕志工想縫布罩保護淺盤；映蓉道謝，問布料能不能清潔而不傷標籤。老師建議先用三個小罩試驗，不要直接做一個大的。女工與學生平等討論，讓他們能提問，也能拒絕不安全的方法。小安因前晚工作太晚而昏昏欲睡，差點摔落一盤種子。映蓉請他回家。工作可以等；疲憊的助手無法帶來可靠結果。

Fang Dingchuan telephoned to say the company's insurer would consider compensation if the farmers submitted individual claims. He asked whether the seed shop would collect them. Yingrong said it could provide forms, but would not claim legal authority it did not possess. Each farmer needed to decide whether to join, and the procedure had to be lawful. She asked the insurer to explain any restriction on repayment in plain language. Meanwhile, the school principal offered a letter confirming that the test room was for temporary use only. Yingrong accepted it gratefully. Clear limits helped keep relationships fair. It would be easy to rely on a friendly promise until someone needed the room for an examination. Written dates made the arrangement less personal and more dependable.

方定川打電話說，公司的保險方願意在農民分別提出申請後考慮補償。他問種子行能不能代收文件。映蓉說可以提供表單，卻不能假稱擁有自己沒有的法律權限。每位農民都應自行決定是否參加，程序也必須合法。她請保險方用淺白文字說明賠付限制。另一方面，校長提供公文，確認試驗室只供暫時使用。映蓉感激地收下。清楚界線有助於公平相處。若只靠友善口頭承諾，等學校需要房間考試時就會出問題。書面日期讓安排不那麼依賴私人交情，也更可靠。

That night Mother opened her sewing box and mended the torn label pockets on a few old sacks. Yingrong sat beside her, cutting clean squares of paper. They talked about ordinary matters: the price of rice, a neighbor's new roof, and whether rain would last through Sunday. Mother said the shop's future had become the theme of every conversation in the family, and she was tired of being asked to defend a decision she had not yet made. Yingrong apologized. They agreed that the next family meeting would be about options, not demands. Outside the temporary chamber, the school clock struck nine. Its steady sound reminded Yingrong that a deadline could be useful without becoming a threat. Tomorrow they would test the covers and begin moving only the clean, dry stock.

那晚，母親打開針線盒，修補幾只舊袋上破掉的標籤袋。映蓉坐在旁邊裁乾淨紙片。兩人聊平常事：米價、鄰居的新屋頂，以及雨是否會下到週日。母親說，店的未來已成全家每段對話的主題，她厭倦一直被要求替尚未做出的決定辯護。映蓉道歉。她們約定，下次家庭會議要談選項，而非要求。臨時試驗室外，學校時鐘敲了九下。穩定的聲音提醒映蓉，期限可以有用，而不必變成威脅。明天她們會測試布罩，只搬入清潔乾燥的庫存。
