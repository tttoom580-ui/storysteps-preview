失去的一頁帳

The Missing Ledger Page

1998，長堤種子行的第七號紙袋 · 第 12 / 25 章 · 3000–4000 · 英中對照 · 校訂中

On Monday a cabinet in the back room stood open. One accounting page had been torn out, and a broken bracelet lay near a storage bin. Xiao-an shouted that a burglar had entered during the night. Yingrong asked him not to turn a possibility into a definite statement. The room's rear window had no damage, and Mother had opened the cabinet herself the previous evening. A visiting agent from the insurer asked whether the shop wanted to report a theft. Yingrong said they would report the missing page and the physical condition, not an imagined criminal. The difference mattered because accusation could destroy a neighbor's reputation. They photographed the cabinet, locked the remaining records, and wrote down who had handled them.

星期一，後房的櫥櫃敞開著。一頁帳被撕走，儲物箱旁躺著一條斷裂手環。小安喊有竊賊夜裡闖入。映蓉請他不要把可能性當成確定事實。後窗沒有損壞，母親前一晚也曾自己打開櫥櫃。保險公司的代理員問店裡要不要報竊案。映蓉說，她們會報告失去的一頁和現場狀況，不會報告想像出的罪犯。這個區別很重要，因為指控可能毀掉鄰居的名聲。她們拍下櫥櫃、鎖好剩餘紀錄，並寫下誰曾接觸這些資料。

Mother admitted she had used the missing page to wrap a leaking bottle of ink and had then put the bundle outside for disposal. Her face showed hardship, not deceit. She had been worried that Yingrong would criticize her careless action in front of customers. The page was found in the dry paper basket behind the shop, stained but still readable. Xiao-an made a small comment about how a tragic mystery had turned into household cleaning. Mother did not laugh at first. Then she said that faithfully keeping records did not mean pretending people never made mistakes. Yingrong agreed to replace the damaged page with a copy, retaining the original in a protective sleeve. They marked what had happened so a future reader would not mistake the repair for a secret change. They were looking for a faithful copy of the facts, not for a perfect story. Yingrong said the destruction of a record, even by accident, had to be noted.

母親承認，她曾拿失去的那頁包住一瓶漏墨水的罐子，接著把整包東西放到外面待丟。她的臉上是窘迫，不是欺騙。她擔心映蓉會在顧客面前責怪自己粗心。那頁紙在店後的乾紙回收籃找到，沾了墨但還讀得出來。小安評論，原本像悲劇的謎團竟成了家務清潔事件。母親起初沒笑，接著說，忠實保存紀錄不代表假裝人永不犯錯。映蓉同意用影本替換受損頁，原件則放保護套。她們標明事件經過，免得後人把修補誤認為祕密修改。 她們要的是忠實的事實影本，不是完美故事。映蓉說，紀錄即使意外遭到破壞，也必須註明。

Pan used the incident to teach the apprentices a durable procedure: when a record is altered, leave an explanation and the earlier version. A famous historical case of forged maps had once made people distrust an entire era of local surveys; he did not want a seed dispute to cause the same damage. He said the shop could commit to accuracy without claiming perfection. Yingrong wrote the rule on the inside of the cabinet door. The town had changed under inflation, with every supplier asking for higher transport fees. A small business could not afford the fame of a scandal, but neither could it afford the hidden cost of errors left uncorrected. The new procedure looked slow, yet it required only a pencil, a date, and an honest sentence.

潘維利用這件事教學徒一套耐久的程序：紀錄若有改動，就留下說明與早期版本。他提起一樁有名的歷史偽造地圖事件，曾使人不信任一整個年代的地方測量；他不希望種子爭議造成同樣傷害。他說，店可以承諾準確，卻不用宣稱完美。映蓉把規則寫在櫥櫃門內側。小鎮受通貨膨脹影響，供應商都要求提高運費。小店承受不起醜聞的名氣，也承受不起不更正錯誤的隱藏成本。新程序看起來很慢，實際只需要鉛筆、日期與一句誠實的話。

That afternoon a farmer described how his grandfather's tribal community had stored grain in clay jars high above the damp ground. Yingrong asked whether the knowledge could be included in a local storage booklet with proper credit. The farmer agreed to explain the technique but did not want photographs of his family's private home. Their conversation reminded Yingrong that old knowledge could emerge from unexpected places without becoming free property for a merchant to occupy. She offered payment for the farmer's time if the booklet went into production. He said he would think about it. Meanwhile, Pan continued to pursue the question of why the seventh lot had been exposed to moisture. A good lead could be valuable without belonging to whoever heard it first.

下午，一位農民談起祖父所屬部落群體如何把穀物裝進陶罐，架高以避開濕地。映蓉徵求同意，希望在地方儲存手冊裡註明來源，介紹這項技術。農民同意解釋方法，但不希望拍自家私人住所。這段談話提醒映蓉，老知識可以從意想不到的地方出現，卻不代表商人能免費占為己有。若手冊正式製作，她願支付農民時間費用。農民說會考慮。另一方面，潘維繼續追查第七批貨為何受潮。好線索很有價值，但不一定屬於最先聽見的人。

When the insurer's agent returned, he was relieved that there was no confirmed burglary. Yingrong still filed a short report about the open cabinet and asked how to improve locks. The incident had cost half a morning, but the shop had learned a practical lesson. Mother made an appointment to discuss her retirement savings. She had spent so long protecting the business that she had forgotten to protect her own future. Yingrong accompanied her without promising to take over every responsibility. Back at the counter, Xiao-an began copying the week's actual orders. He left a clean place for corrections. In the quiet shop, the repaired ledger seemed less like an accusation against the past and more like a tool the next generation could use.

保險代理員回來時，得知沒有確認發生竊案，鬆了口氣。映蓉仍提出關於敞開櫥櫃的簡短報告，詢問如何改善門鎖。事情花掉半個早晨，但店學到了實際教訓。母親預約了退休儲蓄諮詢。她長期保護生意，卻忘記保護自己的未來。映蓉陪她去，但沒有承諾接下所有責任。回到櫃檯，小安開始抄本週的實際訂單，並留下一塊乾淨地方供更正。安靜的店裡，修補過的帳簿不再像對過去的指控，而更像下一代能使用的工具。
