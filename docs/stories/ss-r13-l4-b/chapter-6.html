藍色染料與清水

Blue Dye and Clear Water

1998，長堤種子行的第七號紙袋 · 第 6 / 25 章 · 3000–4000 · 英中對照 · 校訂中

用互動版查詞、朗讀與收藏

The next week a visiting consultant offered to modernize the shop. He spoke confidently about consumer habits: people wanted bright pictures, perfect packaging, and the promise of a luxury harvest. His plan would replace the old linen sacks with colored plastic goods and stamp each seed with a blue dye. Mother liked the cheaper storage space, but Yingrong asked whether the dye had been tested for planting. The consultant called her conservative and said the world's successful merchants had learned to invest before asking every question. She answered that his price did not include lost crops or disposal of damaged packaging. Xiao-an enclosed a sample of the proposed plastic in a clean jar so they could compare it with the sacks. A cheaper package was useful only if it protected what was inside.

下一週，一位外來顧問提出替種子行現代化的計畫。他自信地談消費者習慣：人們要鮮明圖片、完美包裝，以及豪華收成的承諾。他計畫把舊亞麻袋換成彩色塑膠商品，並在每粒種子上染藍色染料。母親喜歡較省空間的儲存方式，映蓉卻問染料是否經過播種測試。顧問說她太保守，成功商人都學會先投資，不會每件事都追問。映蓉回答，他的價格沒有計入作物損失或受損包材的處理費。小安把擬用塑膠的樣本裝進乾淨罐子，以便和布袋比較。便宜包裝只有在能保護內容物時才真正有用。

The district inspector lent Pan a microscope. Under it, the dark seeds showed cracked surfaces that the naked eye had missed. The pale seeds did not show the same pattern. Yingrong did not call anyone ignorant for failing to notice. Her own occupation in the laboratory had taught her that instruments improved sight but could not explain every cause. She asked Xiao-an to isolate samples from each bag and make sketches, then measure the moisture in their containers. He trembled when he saw how differently two apparently identical bags had been stored. Pan said it was habitual to blame the last person who touched a product, but that habit rarely identified the whole chain. They needed purchase records as well as measurements.

區公所的檢查員借給潘維一臺顯微鏡。在鏡下，深色種子的表面出現肉眼看不出的裂縫；淺色種子則沒有同樣痕跡。映蓉沒有因別人沒發現就說他們無知。她過去在實驗室的工作教她明白，儀器能改善視線，卻無法解釋每個原因。她請小安把每袋樣本分開，畫下觀察圖，再測量容器裡的水分。兩袋看似相同的貨，保存方式竟然如此不同，讓小安手微微發抖。潘維說，人們習慣責怪最後接觸產品的人，但這種習慣很少能找到整條責任鏈。他們需要進貨紀錄，也需要量測值。

At lunchtime, the consultant returned with a portable stereo and played a cheerful advertisement. He claimed the music would give the shop an air of grace and attract wealthier customers. Yingrong turned down the volume because Fengzhi was trying to read the lot numbers. A timid boy delivering potatoes waited outside, afraid to interrupt the adults. Mother invited him in and asked whether his family had received the new packets. He said his father bought seed from a traveling merchant, with no receipt. His answer changed the orientation of their investigation. A packet might have passed through several hands before reaching the shop. Yingrong wrote down the source as 'reported, not verified' and offered the boy a dry towel. A careful investigation could still be kind.

午飯時，顧問帶著可攜式立體音響回來，播放愉快的廣告。他說音樂能讓店顯得優雅，吸引有錢顧客。映蓉調低音量，因為鳳枝正努力讀批號。一名送馬鈴薯的害羞男孩等在外面，不敢打斷大人。母親請他進來，問他家是否收到新紙袋。他說父親向流動商人買種子，沒有收據。他的回答改變了調查的方向：紙袋到店前可能經過好幾手。映蓉把來源記為「口述，未確認」，又遞給男孩乾毛巾。仔細調查不代表不能待人溫和。

That evening a supplier accused Yingrong of causing public rage by displaying uncertain figures. She could have answered with a sharper accusation, but she knew the shop's unpaid wages were a more immediate danger. Mother had worked for years without recording her own hours. Xiao-an received small cash gifts instead of a clear salary. Yingrong counted the sales and asked both of them to help write down the labor they actually did. The work included cleaning jars, advising customers, and carrying sacks when rain threatened. It did not belong outside the accounts merely because it happened at home. Mother resisted the idea of charging the shop for her own time. Yingrong said she could choose to contribute unpaid labor only after seeing its true cost.

那晚，一名供應商指責映蓉展示不確定的數字，惹得大眾憤怒。她本可以用更尖銳的話回擊，卻知道店裡未結清的工資才是眼前危險。母親多年來沒有記錄自己的工時；小安也只收到零星現金，沒有清楚薪資。映蓉計算銷售額，請兩人一起寫下實際做的工作，包括洗罐子、向顧客說明和大雨前搬麻袋。這些事不該因為發生在家裡，就從帳上消失。母親不願向自己的店計算勞力成本。映蓉說，只有先看見真正代價，她才能自由決定要不要提供無薪協助。

They placed the dyed plastic, the linen bag, and a plain paper envelope in a cabinet for a week-long storage trial. No one promised that any material would solve the damaged-seed mystery. The consultant left, irritated but still willing to send written specifications. That was progress of a modest kind. At closing time Yingrong found a small note from Mother beside the cash tin: 'If you want the shop to live, do not turn it into another laboratory that forgets its people.' She read it twice. The next morning she made two appointments, one with a distributor and one with a labor adviser. She was beginning to see that protecting a seed and protecting the worker who sold it were not separate businesses.

她們把染色塑膠、亞麻袋與素面紙封套放進櫥櫃，進行為期一週的保存試驗。沒有人承諾任何材料能解開種子受損之謎。顧問有些惱怒地離開，但仍答應寄來書面規格。這也是一種小小進展。關店時，映蓉在收款鐵盒旁找到母親留下的便條：「妳想讓店活下來，就別把它變成另一間忘記人的實驗室。」她讀了兩遍。隔天早上，她約了兩個人：一名批發商和一名勞動顧問。她開始明白，保護種子與保護賣種子的人，其實不是兩回事。
