過河的樣本

Across the River

1998，長堤種子行的第七號紙袋 · 第 3 / 25 章 · 3000–4000 · 英中對照 · 校訂中

用互動版查詞、朗讀與收藏

On Thursday the town ferry carried farmers, workers, and two boxes of carefully wrapped seed trays to the south bank. Yingrong assigned each box a number and a named handler; an assignment meant responsibility, not blame. Fengzhi had asked to join them because the district laboratory was across the river. At the ferry landing, a lifeguard checked the slippery steps while a vendor offered tea and raisin bread. Xiao-an examined a small card listing calories for field workers and wondered whether a seed shop could ever afford such luxurious printed labels. 'Food labels and seed labels serve different purposes,' Yingrong said. 'Our priority is to keep the lot number readable.' She made everyone proceed at a slow pace. A maximum load notice hung over the entrance, and the water was still high.

星期四，鎮上的渡輪載著農民、工人與兩箱仔細包好的種子淺盤前往南岸。映蓉替每箱指定編號與負責人；分派工作代表責任，不等於責怪。鳳枝要求同行，因為區域檢驗室就在河對岸。渡輪碼頭上，救生員檢查濕滑的階梯，攤販則賣茶和葡萄乾麵包。小安看著一張列有田間工作者熱量需求的小卡，心想種子行是否有一天也印得起那麼豪華的標籤。「食品標籤與種子標籤用途不同。」映蓉說。「我們優先要讓批號清楚。」她請所有人放慢腳步、有序前進。入口上方掛著最大載重公告，而河水仍然很高。

The laboratory technician asked them to concentrate on what could be measured. He checked a sample from each tray and recorded the percentage of visible roots, then explained the context of the result: three days in warm conditions could not predict an entire season under irregular rain. The figures were incredible to Fengzhi because they contradicted the neat promise on Golden Field's leaflet. Yingrong defended the farmer's right to ask for a refund but would not yet call the company dishonest. An intermediate result could change after another day, she reminded the group. The technician invited them to photograph the setup rather than relying on memory. Xiao-an used his father's simple camera, practicing photography by keeping the labels and the clock in every frame. This made the evidence less exciting and more useful.

檢驗員請大家專心看可測量的事情。他檢查每盤的樣本並記錄可見根部的百分比，再說明結果的背景：溫暖環境中的三天，不能預測雨量不穩的整季生長。這些數字讓鳳枝難以置信，因為它們與金田傳單上整齊的承諾相矛盾。映蓉捍衛農民要求退款的權利，但還不願稱公司不誠實。她提醒大家，中期結果可能在隔天改變。檢驗員請他們拍下設置情況，不要只靠記憶。小安使用父親那台簡單相機練習攝影，讓每張照片都同時包含標籤和時鐘。這使證據少了刺激，卻更有用。

On the return trip, Fengzhi said her brothers considered the failed planting an insult to the family's skill. They wanted a fight with the salesman, while she wanted enough seed to plant before the cool weather ended. At home she also had housework and an elderly aunt to feed, tasks that did not stop for a market dispute. Yingrong told her that anger was understandable but that planting decisions could not wait for a dramatic confession. Their first aim was to identify what was still safe to sow. The boat passed a painted holiday resort, its empty luxurious rooms looking toward fields worked by people who could not take holidays. Yingrong saw the imbalance without knowing how to fix it. She did know that a ledger could include the cost of wasted time, not only the price of a packet.

回程時，鳳枝說兄弟們把這次種植失敗當成對家族技術的侮辱。他們想找業務吵架，她只想趁涼季結束前拿到能播的種子。她回家還有家務和年邁姑媽要照顧，這些事不會因市場爭執停下。映蓉說，憤怒可以理解，但播種決策不能等一場戲劇性的認錯。第一個目標是找出現在還能安全播什麼。船經過一座彩繪度假村，空蕩蕩的豪華客房面朝一片片田，耕田的人卻無法休假。映蓉看見這種不平衡，卻不知道怎麼解決。她至少知道，帳簿可以計入浪費掉的時間，而不只是紙袋的售價。

That night, Mother listened to the preliminary report without interrupting. She said the shop's older stock could serve as a temporary alternative, but only after its own germination rate was checked. The family's past reputation was not a substitute for a current test. Xiao-an found inspiration in this plain rule and suggested a customer board with three columns: tested, under test, and not for sale. Mother worried that displaying uncertainty would drive everyone away. Yingrong replied that hiding it could leave farmers with no choice at all. Their disagreement was real. Mother had survived decades by making quick judgments, and Yingrong had spent years asking for repeatable measurements. Neither method was enough alone. They decided to put the board inside the doorway where customers could read it without being forced to take either woman's word.

當晚，母親不打斷地聽完初步報告。她說，店裡較舊的庫存可以作臨時替代，但也必須先檢查本身的發芽率。家族過去的名聲不能代替現在的檢驗。小安從這條樸素規則得到靈感，建議做一塊顧客看板，分成「已測試」「測試中」「暫不販售」三欄。母親擔心公開不確定性會趕走所有人。映蓉回答，隱瞞只會讓農民完全失去選擇。兩人確實有分歧。母親幾十年來靠快速判斷維持生意，映蓉則花了許多年追求可重複的測量。兩種方法單獨使用都不夠。她們決定把看板放在門內，讓顧客不必被迫只相信其中一人的話。

The next morning, Fengzhi brought another unopened packet from a neighbor and a small notebook containing planting dates. Its handwriting was uneven but precise. Xiao-an copied the lot codes while Yingrong compared them to the ferry receipts and the laboratory photographs. Both packets had traveled through the same wholesale depot, yet their printed dates were a week apart. Yingrong did not know whether that difference mattered. She wrote it as a question and set a time to call the depot. A young customer laughed at the board and called it a strange way to sell seeds. Fengzhi answered before Yingrong could: 'A shop that tells me what it does not know lets me decide what to risk.' Yingrong wrote down those words, not for an advertisement, but to remember why the work mattered.

隔天早上，鳳枝又從鄰居那裡帶來一包未開封的種子，以及一本記錄播種日期的小筆記。字跡不整齊，資料卻很精確。小安抄錄批號，映蓉則拿它和渡輪收據及檢驗照片比較。兩包貨都經過同一個批發倉，但印的日期相差一週。映蓉還不知道這個差異是否重要，便把它寫成問題，約好時間打電話詢問。年輕顧客笑看板是奇怪的賣種子方法。鳳枝先替映蓉回答：「一家肯告訴我它不知道什麼的店，才讓我自己決定願意冒什麼風險。」映蓉把這句話記下來，不是為了做廣告，而是提醒自己這份工作為何重要。
