A Recipe for the Trial

The school's autumn fair included a competition for local crops. A retired chef offered a recipe for ginger soup, demonstrating how the quality of each ingredient affected the result. Yingrong asked him to join a seed demonstration, not to judge flavor, but to explain why a fair comparison required precise conditions. He agreed and helped label samples by planting date and storage method. An athletic student carried the water bucket but was reminded that strength did not replace careful measurement. Xiao-an prepared a registration sheet so everyone who contributed a sample could later check the result. Pan called the work research in the ordinary sense: a question, a method, and a record that another person could repeat. The fair visitors seemed surprised that the dull part mattered most. The chef set his sharp blade aside before inviting children near the display.

學校秋季市集有地方作物競賽。一名退休廚師帶來薑湯食譜，示範每種食材的品質如何影響成品。映蓉請他加入種子示範，不是要他評味道，而是說明公平比較為何需要精確條件。他答應，幫忙按播種日期與保存方式標樣本。一名體格健壯的學生提水桶，卻被提醒力氣不能代替仔細量測。小安準備登記表，讓每位提供樣本的人日後都能查結果。潘維說，這是平凡意義上的研究：有問題、有方法，也有別人能重做的紀錄。市集來客似乎很意外，原來最乏味的部分反而最重要。 廚師在邀孩子靠近展示前，先把鋒利刀片收好。

Fang Dingchuan came to observe, accompanied by a young colleague who had recently transferred from the city office. He asked whether a new method could overthrow the company's existing packaging system. Yingrong answered that the aim was not to overthrow anyone. It was to find where labels had lost their connection to the goods inside. She consulted the technician about a continuous temperature record and learned that the school could lend a simple chart recorder for two weeks. The instrument was not elegant, but it left a line on paper that could be checked. Fang's companion said his company might adopt such a method if it did not cost too much. Yingrong welcomed the possibility. Better procedures could spread beyond her shop without making its dispute meaningless. He asked the company to transfer a copy of its revised procedure to the public records office.

方定川帶著一名剛從城市辦公室調來的年輕同事觀察。他問，新方法是否要推翻公司的現行包裝系統。映蓉回答，目標不是推翻任何人，而是找出標籤在哪裡失去與袋內商品的連結。她向技師諮詢連續溫度紀錄，得知學校能借一臺簡易紙帶記錄器兩週。儀器不優雅，但能在紙上留下一條可核對的線。方的同事說，只要成本不太高，公司也許會採用這方法。映蓉歡迎這種可能。更好的程序可以傳到店外，不會因此讓爭議失去意義。 他請公司把修訂程序的影本移交公共紀錄辦公室。

In the afternoon, Xiao-an tried to memorize the entire list of lot codes and mixed up two numbers. Yingrong stopped him and suggested using a written table instead of memory. He looked embarrassed, but she told him sincerity was more useful than a performance of perfect recall. They compared photographs, labels, and receipts, placing uncertain matches in a separate folder. The tension eased when a visitor asked a basic question none of the adults had considered: could a customer see how a result had been revised? Xiao-an opened the earlier chart and the current one side by side. The change was visible, along with the date and reason. The visitor nodded. The store's confidence was beginning to come from its willingness to show corrections rather than hide them.

下午，小安試圖把所有批號背下來，卻把兩個數字搞混。映蓉叫停，建議用書面表格取代記憶。他很尷尬，映蓉告訴他，真誠比表演完美記憶更有用。她們比較照片、標籤與收據，把不確定的配對放在另一個文件夾。來客問了一個大人沒想過的基本問題，緊張感才緩和：顧客能不能看見結果如何修訂？小安把早期表格和現在的表格並排打開，變更、日期和理由都看得見。來客點頭。店的信心逐漸來自願意展示修正，而不是把錯誤藏起來。

A local newspaper reporter interviewed Fengzhi and wanted to publish a dramatic story about the woman who had challenged a company. Fengzhi refused to be portrayed as a helpless victim. She had asked for a test because she needed to plan her fields, not because she wanted fame. Yingrong supported her. The reporter then asked for a quotation about the shop's future, and Yingrong said only that the old warehouse was still closed and the family was reviewing its finances. She offered written facts, not an inspiring ending that had not happened. In the schoolyard, the three trial rows showed different growth. They were useful precisely because they did not all tell the same story. A real result allowed the observer to be surprised.

地方報紙記者採訪鳳枝，想寫成一個女人挑戰大公司的戲劇故事。鳳枝拒絕被描繪成無助的受害者。她要求檢驗，是因為需要規劃田地，不是為了出名。映蓉支持她。記者接著問店的未來，映蓉只說老倉庫仍封閉，家人正在檢討財務。她提供書面事實，不提供尚未發生的勵志結局。校園裡，三組試種行長得不同。它們的用處正是沒有說出同一個故事。真正的結果容許觀察者感到意外。

After the fair, Mother asked Xiao-an whether he still wanted to become a professional seed tester. He said he preferred a job where he could learn and also have enough time for his family. Mother told him that was a sensible answer. Yingrong watched her speak and understood how much had changed. The woman who once believed a good merchant never closed the doors early now praised the right to stop working. There was no sudden cure for debt or fatigue, but the family's arguments were changing shape. Before bed, Yingrong checked the recorder's paper strip. Its marks were clear, with one brief gap where the electricity had failed. She circled the gap and left it in place. The imperfect record was still more honest than an unbroken line drawn from memory. It had become apparent that the shop could survive only if work was shared and recorded.

市集後，母親問小安是否仍想成為專業種子檢驗員。他說更希望找到能學習，也有足夠時間陪家人的工作。母親說這回答很合理。映蓉看著她，才明白改變有多大。那個曾認為好商人永不提早關門的女人，如今稱讚人有停止工作的權利。債務和疲勞沒有突然消失，但一家人的爭論方式變了。睡前映蓉檢查記錄器紙帶。上面線條清楚，只有停電造成的一小段空白。她把缺口圈起來，保留原樣。不完美的紀錄仍比憑記憶補畫的完整線條誠實。 現在顯然可見，只有把工作分擔並留下紀錄，店才可能繼續存在。
