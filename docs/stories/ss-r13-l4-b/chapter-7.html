The River Valley Ledger

The accountant arrived carrying two pencils and an old adding machine. She was a former academic researcher who now helped small businesses understand their costs. She asked to see the shop's purchases before hearing the story about the seventh packet. Mother was annoyed by the request; admiration for decades of work seemed to count for nothing. The accountant answered politely that experience was a contribution, but not a replacement for figures. They listed rent, transport, electricity, labor, and goods returned by customers. The results were uncomfortable. The prime selling season had produced enough cash to pay suppliers but almost nothing for Mother. Xiao-an looked at the figures and asked why people called a shop successful when its owner could not afford to rest.

會計師帶著兩支鉛筆和一臺老式加算機來了。她過去做學術研究，如今協助小商家弄清成本。她還沒聽第七號紙袋的故事，就先要求查看進貨資料。母親對這個要求不高興，彷彿幾十年的努力不值得尊敬。會計師禮貌地回答，經驗是貢獻，但不能取代數字。她們列出房租、運輸、電費、人工和顧客退貨。結果令人不安：旺季收入足以付供應商，卻幾乎沒有留給母親的薪水。小安看著數字問，為何一家讓店主無法休息的店，還會被稱為成功。

Pan brought an aquarium from the school so students could watch how water carried fine soil into a basin. The simple experiment showed a relevant characteristic of the damaged fields: their top layer had washed away near the river. That fact did not defeat the laboratory results, but it made their meaning more complicated. Yingrong asked the learners to describe what had happened in their own words. One boy, fortunate enough to speak fluent English, translated the findings for a visiting volunteer. Another used a sketch because words came slowly to him. Both explanations mattered. Pan reminded everyone that expansion of the trial required more than extra trays; they needed different fields and an agreed way to record failures. Pan also explained that basic soil chemistry could help them understand what rain had washed away.

潘維從學校帶來一個水族箱，讓學生看水如何把細土帶入盆中。這個簡單實驗顯示受損田地的一項相關特性：河邊的表土被沖走了。這個事實不能推翻實驗室結果，卻讓它的意義更複雜。映蓉請學員用自己的話描述現象。一名幸運學得流利英語的男孩，替來訪志工翻譯結果；另一人說話較慢，就用圖畫解釋。兩種說明都有價值。潘維提醒大家，擴大試驗不只是多放幾個盤子；還需要不同田地，以及共同同意的失敗記錄方式。 潘維也解釋，基本土壤化學可以幫助大家理解雨水沖走了什麼。

By late afternoon, the first outside trial began in Fengzhi's garden. They chose two shaded rows and two open rows with similar soil. Yingrong asked everyone to strive for the same planting depth, while allowing the farmers to work at a safe rhythm. The accountant recorded the cost of labor so no one could later claim that volunteers made the experiment free. A neighbor tried to motivate the group by promising a wonderful harvest. Fengzhi shook her head. 'Promise me honest records instead.' Her words wakened an old memory in Yingrong: at the city laboratory, a supervisor had praised results that matched his expectations and quietly shelved inconvenient ones. She had left that job because she wanted work she could explain without hiding half the page.

傍晚前，第一個戶外試驗在鳳枝家的菜園展開。她們選出兩行有樹蔭和兩行露天、土壤相近的田。映蓉請大家努力保持相同播種深度，但讓農民按安全的節奏工作。會計師記下勞動成本，免得日後有人說志工讓試驗免費。一名鄰居想鼓舞大家，保證會有好收成。鳳枝搖頭：「請保證把紀錄寫真就好。」她的話喚醒映蓉的舊記憶：在城市實驗室，上司誇獎符合期待的結果，卻悄悄擱置不方便的數據。她離開那份工作，是因為想做能完整說明、不必藏起半頁的事。

Before leaving, the accountant suggested a small expansion of sales: offer farmers several tested varieties rather than one heavily advertised choice. Mother objected that too many varieties meant more storage and more uncertainty. Yingrong proposed a pilot with only three small lots. The accountant calculated the possible income and the worst-case unsold inventory. It was not a heroic decision. Even the most relevant number could not predict rain, and every new item would tie up money the shop might need for rent. They agreed to revisit the calculation after the first outdoor observations. Xiao-an wanted a celebratory photograph but settled for a picture of the four labeled rows. A modest beginning, Yingrong thought, was better than a grand promise that could not survive its first question.

離開前，會計師建議小幅擴展銷售：提供數種經測試的品種，而不是只賣一種大量廣告的選擇。母親反對，因為品種太多代表更多儲存費用與不確定性。映蓉提出只用三小批貨試辦。會計師算出可能收入和最糟的滯銷庫存。這不是英雄式決定。再相關的數字也無法預測下雨，每樣新商品都會占用可能要付房租的錢。她們約定第一批田間觀察後再重算。小安想拍張慶祝照片，最後只拍了四行標記清楚的田。映蓉心想，保守而踏實的開始，勝過一場經不起第一個問題的豪語。

The accountant went home with a copy of the ledger and a written limit on what she could share. The shop was not a public office, and customer identities were not materials for a lecture. Mother looked exhausted but less angry than at noon. She asked Xiao-an whether he wanted to continue working there when the trial ended. He said he did, if the pay and study time were clear. Mother wrote a weekly schedule beside the counter. Yingrong realized that this small act changed the room more than the new packaging proposal had. The shop's next season would be decided not just by which seeds grew, but also by which people could afford to stay.

會計師帶著帳簿影本回家，另有一份書面限制，說明哪些資料可以分享。店不是公家機關，顧客身分也不是演講素材。母親看來疲憊，卻沒有中午那麼生氣。她問小安，試驗結束後還願不願意在店裡工作。小安說願意，只要薪水與讀書時間明確。母親就在櫃檯旁寫下一張週班表。映蓉發現，這個小動作比新的包裝提案更能改變店裡的氣氛。下一季的命運不只由哪些種子發芽決定，也由哪些人有能力繼續留下決定。
