The Misprinted Date

1998，長堤種子行的第七號紙袋 · 第 9 / 25 章 · 3000–4000 · 英中對照 · 校訂中

用互動版查詞、朗讀與收藏

A farmer with a strong regional accent arrived carrying three packets in a cloth bag. He had attached the original receipt to a board because the ink was already fading. Under the circumstances, he said, the shop should refund him at once. Yingrong examined the printing and saw that the product content listed on two packets was the same, but the lot dates differed. The farmer frowned, thinking she was seeking an excuse. She asked him to lean closer and peep at the narrow line below the price. A second stamp had been placed over an older one. She explained that a refund could begin immediately, while the cause still needed investigation. The farmer took the money but allowed her to keep one packet for testing. The man put down a crunchy rice biscuit before handling the packet, and Yingrong asked him to wipe his hands first.

一名帶著濃厚地方口音的農民提著三包種子進門。他把原始收據黏在木板上，因為墨水已經褪色。他說，在這種情況下，店就該立刻退款。映蓉檢查印字，發現兩包寫的內容相同，批號日期卻不同。農民皺眉，以為她在找藉口。她請他靠近，看清價格下面那一條細細的字；第二道印章蓋在較舊的一道上。她解釋，退款可以立即開始，原因仍需調查。農民拿走錢，但准許她留一包作檢驗。 農民放下酥脆的米餅才碰紙袋，映蓉請他先把手擦乾淨。

Xiao-an had been clumsy while copying earlier labels, but the mistake led him to check every old photograph again. Using an earphone, he listened to Yingrong's tape-recorded notes while matching pictures to receipts. In one photograph the stamp had been applied before the wholesale invoice was dated. This did not prove fraud, but it meant the shop's assumption about the packaging order might mislead the inquiry. Yingrong instructed Xiao-an to preserve the original negatives and avoid writing names on them. A rumor spread that someone had used nuclear radiation to change the seeds. Pan called the claim revolutionary nonsense without mocking the people who feared it. Ordinary storage damage was a far more likely explanation, and they could test that possibility.

小安之前抄標籤時有些笨手笨腳，但這個錯誤反而使他重新檢查所有舊照片。他戴著單邊耳機聽映蓉錄下的筆記，把影像與收據逐一對照。一張照片顯示，印章的使用時間比批發發票日期還早。這不能證明有人詐欺，卻表示店裡對包裝順序的假設可能誤導調查。映蓉指示小安保留原始底片，不要在上面寫人名。鎮上流傳有人用核能輻射改造種子的謠言。潘維說那是荒謬的革命性傳聞，卻不嘲笑害怕的人。普通保存不良遠比那種說法可能，而且他們可以測試。

Fang Dingchuan called from the company office. He said several cartons had been repacked after rain damaged a warehouse wall, but he did not know whether the seventh lot was among them. Yingrong wrote down the call and asked for a copy of the packing instructions. The salesman said his manager controlled those documents. 'Then please ask him,' she replied. Fang sounded tired. He had grown up in this homeland and disliked being treated as an enemy by neighbors. Yingrong told him the question was economic as well as personal: if the firm supplied defective goods, farmers could lose a planting season, and the shop could not survive unlimited refunds. He said he would look for the warehouse record, without promising what it would show.

方定川從公司辦公室打電話來。他說，有些箱子在倉庫牆壁被雨水損壞後重新包裝過，但不知道第七批是否在其中。映蓉記下通話內容，請他提供包裝指示影本。業務說，那些文件由經理掌管。「那就請你問他。」她回答。方定川聽來很疲憊。他在這片故鄉長大，不喜歡被鄰居當成敵人。映蓉說，這不只關乎個人，也關乎經濟：公司若供應有問題的貨，農民可能失去一季播種，店也承受不起無限退款。他答應去查倉庫紀錄，但不保證結果會是什麼。

Mother was proud of handling the refund without a public fight. She said modesty in business meant admitting what the shop could and could not control. Yingrong agreed, though she felt an interruption whenever the word 'reputation' appeared. Too often people had used reputation as an excuse to hide uncomfortable facts. She decided to publish a short notice listing the affected lot numbers and the amount reserved for refunds. The office clerk helped her check the printing so nobody would confuse a reported case with a confirmed one. Xiao-an drew a small symbol beside each category. When the board went up, several customers stopped to read it and left without buying anything. It was painful, but the absence of sales was not evidence that the notice had been wrong.

母親對這次沒有公開吵架就完成退款很滿意。她說，生意上的謙遜，是承認店能控制與不能控制的部分。映蓉同意，但每當「聲譽」這個詞出現，她總有一種被打斷的感覺。人們常拿聲譽作為隱瞞不方便事實的藉口。她決定公布簡短公告，列出受影響批號和預留退款金額。辦公室職員幫她核對印字，避免有人把通報案件和已確認案件混為一談。小安在每個類別旁畫了小符號。看板貼出後，幾名顧客停下閱讀，卻沒有買東西就走了。這很難受，但沒有成交不代表公告做錯。

Later that evening, Pan showed Yingrong an old report about the town's seed exchange. Its author had once called the project revolutionary because farmers could compare results across fields. In reality the work had consisted of ordinary notebooks and long conversations. Yingrong liked that contrast. She asked Xiao-an to prepare a copy of the damaged-label photograph for the next meeting, with identifying household details covered. The comparison needed to be clear enough that anyone could question it. Mother brought tea to the desk and gave a small wink when she saw the refund figures balanced against the remaining stock. It was not a celebration. It was a moment when the shop's accounts and its public words finally described the same event.

當晚，潘維拿給映蓉一份關於鎮上種子交換的舊報告。作者曾說那計畫具有革命性，因為農民能跨田地比較結果。實際工作卻只由平凡筆記與長時間交談構成。映蓉喜歡這種對比。她請小安準備受損標籤的照片影本，供下次會議使用，並遮掉可識別住戶的資料。比較內容必須清楚到任何人都能提出質疑。母親把茶送到桌邊，看見退款數字與庫存對得上，便輕輕眨眼。這不是慶祝，只是店裡的帳和對外說法終於描述同一件事的時刻。
