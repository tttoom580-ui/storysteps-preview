第七號紙袋

The Seventh Paper Packet

1998，長堤種子行的第七號紙袋 · 第 1 / 25 章 · 3000–4000 · 英中對照 · 校訂中

用互動版查詞、朗讀與收藏

In October 1998, Ji Yingrong stepped off the country bus with a suitcase and a metal case of seed-testing tools. Her mother Qiu-lan lived behind the Long Embankment Seed Shop, in a cottage whose front window looked toward the river. A cushion lay on Mother's favorite chair, although she had spent the morning lifting heavy sacks. Rain fell in a fierce curtain beyond the doorway. A customer called from under the eaves, asking whether the shop would close for good. Mother gave a pessimistic answer: after thirty years, the rent and debts had grown too large. Yingrong had returned from a city laboratory, but she possessed neither savings nor a clear plan. She had only her father's old ledger and a promise to listen before changing anything.

一九九八年十月，紀映蓉提著行李箱和裝有種子檢驗工具的金屬盒，走下鄉間公車。母親秋蘭住在長堤種子行後方的小屋，前窗正對著河流。母親常坐的椅子上有個軟墊，但她整個早上都在搬沉重的麻袋。門外正下著大雨。一名顧客躲在屋簷下，問店是不是要永遠關門。秋蘭說，店開了三十年，如今光是房租和欠款就快撐不下去了。映蓉從城市實驗室返鄉，卻沒有存款，也沒有完整計畫。她只有父親留下的舊帳簿，以及在改變任何事情之前先聽人說話的承諾。

The first visitor was farmer Yang Fengzhi, carrying a paper packet marked SEVEN. She had planted twelve rows from it; only three showed green shoots. A salesman from the larger Golden Field Company had called her careless, but Fengzhi wanted someone to witness the damage before he sold her another batch. Yingrong opened her case and spread the seeds on a white plate. Their diversity was obvious: some were round and dark, others long and pale. Mother said a clever merchant could flatter a customer into buying anything; Yingrong was not interested in being called a genius. She made a visual chart of the rows, then asked Fengzhi to describe the planting day. The chart might convey a fact that angry voices could not. They would test the seeds before assigning blame.

第一位來客是農民楊鳳枝，手裡拿著一個標著「七」的紙袋。她用裡面的種子播了十二行，只有三行長出綠芽。大型種苗公司「金田」的業務說她耕種粗心，但鳳枝希望有人先見證損失，不要又被推銷另一批貨。映蓉打開工具箱，把種子放到白盤上。種子的差異很明顯：有的又圓又黑，有的細長而蒼白。母親說，聰明的商人靠奉承也能讓顧客掏錢；映蓉並不想被稱作天才。她畫了一張可視化的行數圖表，請鳳枝說明播種那天的情況。圖表也許比爭吵更能傳達事實。她們要先測試種子，才決定責任歸屬。

At noon, apprentice Xiao-an arrived after his school workout, sweating through his shirt. He saw the packet and gave a nervous giggle. At a county fair the previous year, children had dropped paper parachute models to show how wind could carry light seeds. The seventh packet had been placed near that display. Xiao-an remembered because the exhibit's presentation included a photograph of Yingrong's father. He found a reference to it in an old fair catalogue. A spiritual song from the river temple drifted through the open window, but Mother closed the shutters against the rain. She did not believe the photograph proved anything. 'Memories make poor receipts,' she said. Yingrong agreed. She wrote the remembered exhibit down as a lead, not a conclusion.

中午，學徒小安做完學校的運動訓練來到店裡，襯衫都被汗浸濕了。他看見紙袋，緊張地輕笑一聲。前一年縣展上，孩子們曾讓紙製降落傘模型落下，示範風如何帶走輕種子。第七號紙袋當時就在展品旁。小安記得這件事，是因為展示簡報中有映蓉父親的照片。他從舊展覽目錄找到相關參考資料。河神廟傳來帶有宗教意味的歌聲，但母親關上窗板，擋住雨水。她不相信照片能證明什麼。「回憶不能當收據。」她說。映蓉同意，把這段展覽記憶列作線索，而不是結論。

Golden Field's representative, Fang Dingchuan, entered before sunset with a polished smile and a sales folder. His defense was simple: no one could guarantee a harvest against weather, and a failed field did not prove a defective packet. He claimed that accusing his company without laboratory evidence would be a violation of fair trade. Yingrong asked him to leave one sealed sample from the same lot for comparison. He hesitated, then placed a small envelope on the counter but would not sign it. 'You can keep it,' he said, 'but don't pretend you have authority to judge us.' Mother wanted the argument over. She knew the tremendous cost of a public dispute in a town where everyone bought on credit. Yingrong wrote his refusal to sign in the ledger without adding a single insult.

傍晚前，金田公司的代表方定川帶著光亮的笑容和推銷資料夾進門。他的辯護很簡單：沒人能保證天氣下的收成，田地歉收不代表紙袋有問題。他還說，沒有檢驗證據就指控公司，可能違反公平交易規範。映蓉請他留下同批貨的未拆封樣本作比較。他猶豫片刻，把一個小信封放在櫃檯上，卻不願簽名。「你可以留著，」他說，「但不要假裝你有權審判我們。」母親只希望爭執快點結束。她知道，在人人賒帳的小鎮公開起爭議，代價可能非常大。映蓉在帳簿上記下他拒絕簽名的事實，沒有加上一句辱罵。

That evening Yingrong placed three shallow trays under a lamp, one for Fengzhi's remaining seeds, one for the sealed envelope, and one for seed taken from an older stock jar. She counted twenty grains into each and labeled the water, temperature, and starting time. She also left an empty space for the result. Mother watched silently, then brought a second towel to protect the wooden table. 'I do not want a presentation that saves your pride but ruins my shop,' she said. Yingrong answered that a test could not promise success. It could only tell them what to try next. Mother nodded toward the seventh packet. 'Your father marked things that way when he did not know their names.' Yingrong looked again at the number. The mystery was smaller than a family legend and more urgent: someone was selling seeds that might never grow.

當晚，映蓉把三個淺盤放在燈下，一盤裝鳳枝剩下的種子，一盤裝封口信封裡的種子，一盤裝舊庫存罐裡的種子。她每盤數了二十粒，標好水量、溫度和開始時間，也留一格空白記結果。母親默默看著，接著拿來第二條毛巾保護木桌。「我不要一場保住妳面子、卻毀掉我的店的表演。」母親說。映蓉回答，試驗不能保證成功，只能告訴大家接下來應該試什麼。母親朝第七號紙袋點點頭。「妳爸以前不知道東西的名字時，就會這樣編號。」映蓉重新看著那個數字。謎團沒有家族傳說那麼宏大，卻更急迫：有人正在賣可能根本不會發芽的種子。
