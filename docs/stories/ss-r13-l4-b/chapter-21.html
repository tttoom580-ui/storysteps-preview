新店的第一天

The First Day in the New Shop

1998，長堤種子行的第七號紙袋 · 第 21 / 25 章 · 3000–4000 · 英中對照 · 校訂中

用互動版查詞、朗讀與收藏

The former post office reopened as Long Embankment Seed Shop in early spring 1999. Yingrong had chosen its simple sign from three candidates because it was readable from the bus stop. Mother arrived carrying a cane, not because she could not walk, but because her knee was tender after the wet winter. A local newspaper called the reopening a glorious return. Yingrong did not correct the compliment, though the room was modest and the stock carefully limited. Xiao-an, now a freshman in a training program, checked the new cabinets and the engineering report on the roof. No one would dismiss safety because the shelves looked attractive. The first customer bought a small packet of beans and asked how much water it might consume. Yingrong pointed to the field notes instead of promising a perfect season.

一九九九年初春，舊郵局以「長堤種子行」重新開門。映蓉從三個候選招牌中選了最簡單的一個，因為公車站就能看清楚。母親拄著手杖來，不是走不了路，而是潮濕冬季讓膝蓋有點疼。地方報紙稱這次開幕是光榮回歸。映蓉沒有糾正這份好意，雖然房間很小、庫存也有嚴格上限。如今在訓練班就讀一年級的小安檢查新櫥櫃和屋頂的工程報告。沒有人會只因架子漂亮，就輕忽安全。第一位顧客買了一小包豆子，問要用掉多少水。映蓉指給他看田間筆記，而不是保證整季完美。

The morning market was crowded with farmers discussing a dense patch of soil that had remained wet for weeks. A salesman from Golden Field argued that the shop's trial results would clash with the company's national figures. Yingrong said different tests could coexist if the conditions were stated. The salesman tried to make the debate a fight between independent merchants and rich capitalists. Fengzhi objected that labels, labor, and soil were practical questions, not slogans. A young worker asked for representation at the company's safety meeting, where warehouse staff had been missing from the list. Yingrong suggested a written request naming the problem but not exposing private addresses. The worker nodded. Their dispute was becoming clearer as people learned to ask for specific rights rather than vague admiration. A few fragile seedlings stood at the edge of the wet soil, and nobody wanted their survival used as an advertising trick.

早市擠滿農民，大家討論一塊連續數週潮濕、土層厚重的田。金田業務說，種子行的試驗結果會與公司全國數據衝突。映蓉說，只要列出條件，不同試驗可以並存。業務想把討論說成獨立商人和富有資本家的對抗。鳳枝反對，說標籤、勞動和土壤是實際問題，不是口號。一名年輕員工要求在公司安全會議上有代表權，因為倉庫工人從名單中消失了。映蓉建議提出書面請求，清楚指出問題，又不暴露私人地址。員工點頭。大家逐漸學會要求具體權利，而不是含糊的讚美，爭議也開始明確。 濕地邊有幾株脆弱幼苗，沒有人希望它們的存活被拿來做廣告手法。

At the agricultural school, students held a drill on cleaning equipment before planting. A blade from a damaged cutter was removed and locked away; nobody was allowed to use it merely because work was busy. The instructor spoke about infection and plant disease, distinguishing bacteria from a virus. One student tried to memorize every warning and asked if an obedient worker simply followed every order. The instructor answered that safety required judgment as well as care: a worker had to question an instruction that threatened another person's health. Xiao-an took notes on the lesson and brought them to the shop. Yingrong posted a clear list of tools suitable for customers to borrow, with a separate shelf for broken objects awaiting repair. The shelves looked plain, and she liked them that way. The instructor showed how to shave a rough wooden handle smooth without leaving an exposed sharp edge.

農業學校的學生在播種前練習清潔設備。壞切割機的刀片被拆下鎖好，沒有人可以只因工作忙就繼續使用。老師談感染和植物疾病，區分細菌與病毒。一名學生努力背下所有警告，問服從指示的工人是否只要照做就好。老師回答，安全需要判斷也需要謹慎；若命令威脅他人健康，工人就有責任提問。小安把課堂筆記帶回店。映蓉張貼可借給顧客的工具清單，另設一架放待修的破損物件。架子看起來很樸素，她很喜歡。 老師示範如何削平粗糙木柄，同時不留下外露尖角。

In the afternoon, a draft from the open door made loose papers drift across the counter. Mother caught one before it fell into water and asked Xiao-an to hold the door shut. She said the new shop could not afford another damaged record. Yingrong agreed and moved the archive to a cabinet with a stronger seal. A visiting translator asked whether the English labels were accurate; one phrase had been translated into a promise of plentiful harvests when it only meant suitable conditions. Yingrong corrected the translation on the spot and marked the date. The episode was small but revealing. The shop was not protected by one perfect language or one perfect person. Its strength came from allowing different readers to notice errors and have them corrected in public.

下午，開門的穿堂風讓鬆散文件沿櫃檯飄走。母親抓住一張，沒讓它落進水裡，請小安關好門。她說新店經不起再損失紀錄。映蓉同意，把檔案移到密封更好的櫥櫃。一名翻譯員問英文標籤是否準確；其中一句原本只說「適合的條件」，卻被翻成保證「豐沛收成」。映蓉當場修正翻譯，標上日期。事件很小，卻讓人看清一件事：店不靠一種完美語言或一個完美人物保護，而靠不同讀者能發現錯誤，並公開更正。

That evening Jianping visited with the bank's revised schedule. The sale of the old building had not yet completed, but the lender had approved smaller monthly payments during the transfer. Mother read the figures carefully and said she was ready to stop working six days a week. She wanted to teach one short class each month and spend the rest of her time as she chose. Yingrong did not call her decision a victory for the business. It was Mother's choice about her own life. She agreed to prepare a paid teaching agreement and find a second person to cover the counter. Outside, the first spring wind moved through the newly planted rows. The shop was open, but nobody had to become a permanent servant to keep its doors that way.

那晚建平帶著銀行修訂的付款期程來。舊建物尚未完成出售，但貸款方已同意在移轉期間降低月付額。母親仔細讀數字，說自己準備停止一週工作六天。她希望每月教一堂短課，其餘時間由自己安排。映蓉沒有把這個選擇稱作生意的勝利，那是母親對自己生活的決定。她同意準備有薪授課協議，並找第二個人輪流顧櫃檯。門外，第一陣春風穿過新播的田行。店開著，卻不需要任何人永遠當僕人，才能讓門繼續開著。
