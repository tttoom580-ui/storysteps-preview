老磨坊旁的試驗

A Test beside the Old Mill

1998，長堤種子行的第七號紙袋 · 第 2 / 25 章 · 3000–4000 · 英中對照 · 校訂中

By morning, the three trays had taken in equal amounts of water, but no root had appeared. Yingrong carried them to the former rice mill, where retired teacher Pan Wei kept an agricultural classroom. The mill had once served as a fort during a flood, its stone walls holding back floating timber. Now children used its upper room to learn about soil, weather, and the difference between an atom and a grain of sand. A faded atomic model hung beside a display of fossil leaves. Xiao-an gazed at the strange collection and asked why ordinary seeds deserved such grand company. Pan answered, 'Because a small thing can change a landscape. That does not mean it is magical.' He set a thermometer beside each tray before anyone could make a guess.

第二天早上，三個淺盤吸了同樣多的水，卻還沒有根冒出。映蓉把它們帶到舊碾米廠，退休教師潘維在那裡開了一間農業教室。磨坊過去在洪水時曾像堡壘一樣，石牆擋住漂來的木頭。如今孩子們在樓上學習土壤、天氣，以及原子和沙粒的差別。一個褪色的原子模型掛在化石葉片的展示旁。小安凝視那堆奇特收藏，問普通種子為何要和這些偉大的東西放在一起。潘維回答：「因為小東西也能改變地景，但那不代表它有魔法。」在任何人開始猜測之前，他先把溫度計放到各盤旁。

Pan had made a voyage across the river in his youth to study the region's farms. Families returning through immigration had brought different vegetables and growing habits, and the resulting combination changed the local fields. He retained notebooks of those interviews, with delicate drawings of seed heads and planting dates. Their intimate details were not meant for a public advertisement. They showed how farmers made decisions under pressure. Pan explained that a laboratory could measure the extent of a germination problem, but not promise a whole season's yield. He offered no guarantee that the seventh packet had a valuable secret. Yingrong liked his caution. She asked permission to revise the old trial chart so that seed source, moisture, and handling would have separate columns.

潘維年輕時曾渡河走訪本地農家。透過移民而返鄉或定居的家庭帶來不同的蔬菜與種植習慣，這樣的組合改變了當地田地。他保留了當年的訪談筆記，裡面有細緻的穗形圖和播種日期。那些親密的生活細節並不是為公開廣告而寫，卻記錄農民如何在壓力下做決定。潘維解釋，實驗室能測量發芽問題的程度，卻不能保證整季的收成。他不保證第七號紙袋藏著珍貴祕密。映蓉喜歡他的謹慎，徵求許可修改舊試驗表，將種子來源、濕度和處理方式分成不同欄位。

A messenger from the upstream village arrived on a bicycle with a list of orders. The previous night's rain had closed the road, and the man had traveled along the embankment. He murmured that the Golden Field representative was offering discounts to anyone who threw away the seventh packet. Yingrong refused to treat the message as proof; a rumor could do psychological damage to farmers who already feared losing their income. Instead, she asked the messenger to carry a notice inviting customers with the same lot number to bring unopened samples. Pan suggested a productive task for Xiao-an: copy each label exactly, including faded letters, and keep the originals in separate envelopes. Xiao-an wanted to hurry, but a missing mark could be the one that mattered.

上游村莊的傳信人騎著腳踏車來，帶來一張訂單。昨晚的雨封閉公路，他只好沿著堤岸走。他低聲說，金田的業務正在向丟掉第七號紙袋的人提供折扣。映蓉拒絕把這段傳聞當成證據；謠言可能對已經擔心收入的農民造成心理傷害。她請傳信人帶一張公告回去，邀請拿到同一批號的人帶未開封樣本來。潘維交給小安一件實際的工作：逐字抄下每張標籤，連褪色的字母也要保留，原件則分開裝袋。小安想加快速度，但少掉的那個記號可能正是關鍵。

When the schoolchildren entered, Pan asked them to observe without touching the trays. One girl noticed that the new company's label had a second line of printing beneath the price. Another traced the number with her finger in the air, not on the paper. 'The number could refer to a field, not a variety,' she suggested. The room grew quiet. Yingrong had assumed SEVEN named a type of seed; she now saw how little supported that idea. She asked Pan whether he still had the mill's original storage maps. The teacher showed them a cupboard of records arranged by year. None of the pages could identify the packet yet. The strongest clue was also the simplest: two different suppliers appeared to have used the same printed symbol.

孩子們走進教室時，潘維請他們只觀察，不要碰淺盤。一名女孩發現新公司的標籤在價格下還印有第二行字。另一人用手指在空中描著數字，而不是直接碰紙。「這個數字可能代表田區，不是品種。」她提出。房間安靜下來。映蓉原以為「七」代表種子種類，如今才明白這個想法缺少依據。她問潘維，是否還留著磨坊原來的倉儲地圖。老師帶她們看按年份整理的紀錄櫃。當下沒有任何一頁能確認紙袋身分。最強的線索同時也是最簡單的：兩家不同供應商似乎用了同一個印刷記號。

They agreed to wait two more days before reading the trays. Mother disliked waiting because the shop's unpaid bills arrived every morning whether seeds sprouted or not. Yingrong brought home a copy of Pan's chart and placed it beside the cash book, not on top of it. 'If the trial is wrong, we say it is wrong,' she told Mother. 'If the supplier is wrong, we show exactly how we know.' Mother pointed at the rent date circled in red. She wanted the shop to survive long enough for either answer to matter. They drew a short list of expenses they could delay and a second list they could not. Testing, like farming, was a matter of time; a business also needed a way to live through that time.

她們約定再等兩天才讀取淺盤結果。母親不喜歡等待，因為無論種子是否發芽，未付的帳單每天早晨都會送到。映蓉把潘維的表格影本帶回家，放在收支簿旁邊，而不是蓋在上面。「如果試驗錯了，我們就說它錯了。」她告訴母親。「如果供應商錯了，我們要清楚展示怎麼知道。」母親指著紅筆圈起的房租日期。她希望店能活到任何答案有意義的時候。她們列出可以延後的費用，也列出絕不能延後的項目。檢驗和耕種一樣都需要時間，而生意也得找到撐過那段時間的方法。
