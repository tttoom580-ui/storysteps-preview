新的種子目錄

The New Catalogue

1998，長堤種子行的第七號紙袋 · 第 19 / 25 章 · 3000–4000 · 英中對照 · 校訂中

用互動版查詞、朗讀與收藏

In January the shop printed a small catalogue of tested seeds. It listed planting dates, observed conditions, and the people responsible for each trial. A local community worker praised the courtesy of asking contributors for permission before printing their names. An intellectual from the district college helped interpret the results but refused to claim certainty where there was none. The founder of a neighboring nursery asked whether the catalogue implied that old varieties were always better. Yingrong answered no. Older stock could be damp or damaged, while a newer variety might perform very well in the right field. The list was meant to reduce confusion, not create a new legend. Its most vital feature was the date beside every result, showing when the claim had last been checked. The booklet would alert customers to the limits of each trial rather than demand unquestioning trust.

一月，種子行印出一份小小的已測試種子目錄，列出播種日期、觀察條件與負責試驗的人。地方社區工作者稱讚她們在印出貢獻者姓名前先徵得許可。一名區立學院的知識分子協助解釋結果，卻拒絕對不確定處做肯定宣稱。附近育苗所的創辦人問，目錄是否暗示老品種永遠更好。映蓉回答不是。舊庫存可能潮濕受損，新品種也可能在適當田地表現很好。這份清單是為了減少混亂，不是製造新傳說。它最重要的特色，是每個結果旁都標明上次核對日期。 小冊子會提醒顧客每次試驗的限制，而不是要求不加質疑地相信。

The town fair included a display of household tools collected over decades. A wooden spear used in a historical fishing exhibition stood beside a hand press for extracting oil. The school physicist explained the measurable difference between force and energy, and children asked why their grandparents had used such heavy objects. Pan connected the display to the seed catalogue: tools and methods changed, but every claim needed a context. A retired shopkeeper offered Yingrong a bottle of liquor to celebrate the reopening plan. She thanked him but declined; they would be carrying boxes and wanted clear heads. Instead, she invited him to help recycle clean paper from the old storehouse. He agreed and returned the next morning with a cart.

鎮上展覽展示了幾十年收集的家用工具。歷史漁獵展示用的木矛放在榨油手壓機旁。學校物理學家解釋力量和能量之間可以測量的差別，孩子問祖父母為何用這麼沉重的器具。潘維把展示和種子目錄連結起來：工具與方法會改變，但每項主張都需要背景。一名退休商人送映蓉一瓶酒，慶祝重新開店的計畫。她道謝但婉拒；她們要搬箱子，必須保持清醒。她改請他幫忙回收舊倉的乾淨紙張。老人答應，隔天帶著推車來。

A young couple returned from their honeymoon and asked about seed for a new garden. They wanted a few vegetables that could grow near their rented house, not a commercial field. Xiao-an helped them choose small packets and explained how to record what grew. Yingrong was pleased with his patience. Mother noticed how much of the daily work the apprentice could now handle without being told every step. She asked whether he would like formal training at the agricultural office. He said yes, provided he could return afterward to help the shop through its first spring. They agreed on study days and wages in writing. The business was becoming a place where skills could be learned without requiring anyone to surrender the rest of life.

一對年輕夫妻度完蜜月回來，想為新家庭的花園買種子。他們只想在租屋旁種幾種蔬菜，不是要耕商業田。小安協助挑選小包裝，說明如何記錄生長情況。映蓉很欣賞他的耐心。母親注意到，學徒如今不必事事受指示，也能處理許多日常工作。她問他想不想去農業辦公室接受正式訓練。他說願意，只要之後能回來幫店度過第一個春天。她們書面約定上課日和工資。這家店逐漸成為能學技術、又不必放棄其他生活的地方。

Fengzhi asked whether the catalogue would be translated for customers who preferred other languages. She had met growers whose families came from different regions, and some felt embarrassed asking about unfamiliar terms. Yingrong said she would publish a short glossary with the next print run if volunteers could help translate it accurately. She did not want a rushed version that might imply a guarantee no one had intended. A teacher offered to review the draft, and the group agreed on a modest payment. Mother counted the printing costs and questioned whether they could afford it. Yingrong answered that the first run could be smaller, with only the most useful terms. Accessibility was an expense, but it was also part of selling a product people could understand.

鳳枝問，目錄會不會翻成其他語言給不同讀者。她認識來自不同地區的農戶，有些人不好意思詢問陌生詞彙。映蓉說，若能找到志工準確翻譯，下次印刷便加入簡短詞表。她不希望倉促版本暗示原本沒有人承諾的保證。老師答應審閱草稿，大家也同意支付合理報酬。母親核算印刷費，問是否負擔得起。映蓉回答，可以先少量印製，只放最實用的詞。便利理解會增加成本，但也是銷售能讓人明白的商品的一部分。

The new store's front room was nearly ready. Yingrong walked through it at sunset, imagining the counter beneath the east window and the labelled drawers along the wall. There would be no grand opening until the roof passed inspection. Jianping called with the first property valuation, lower than they had hoped. Mother listened to the number and asked for time to think. She was no longer being forced to choose between a collapsing warehouse and a false promise of endless work. The family could compare a safe small store, a partial sale, and the possibility of stopping entirely. Yingrong had learned that a useful catalogue included bad news and limits as well as attractive pictures. Perhaps a good family plan needed the same honesty.

新店前房幾乎準備好了。映蓉在夕陽下走過房間，想像櫃檯放在東窗下，有標籤的抽屜沿牆排列。屋頂沒有通過檢查前，不會辦隆重開幕。建平打電話報告第一份產權估價，比大家希望的低。母親聽了數字，要求再想想。她如今不必被迫在快塌的倉庫和永無止境的工作假象之間二選一。家人可以比較安全的小店、部分出售或完全停業的可能。映蓉已學會，好目錄不只放漂亮圖片，也放壞消息和限制。或許好的家庭計畫同樣需要誠實。
