磁鐵旁的種子

The Seed under the Magnet

1998，長堤種子行的第七號紙袋 · 第 13 / 25 章 · 3000–4000 · 英中對照 · 校訂中

The laboratory's full report arrived in three envelopes. The calculation was less dramatic than the rumors: the damaged lot had a much lower germination rate after humid storage, while older, properly dried samples performed better. Pan asked Yingrong to clarify that this result concerned stored seed, not every field or every species. A farmer wanted to know whether the finding was permanent. The technician explained that certain damage could not be reversed, but other losses depended on conditions still being studied. Xiao-an put a cube of wood beside the trays so the camera could show their scale. A magnetic clip held the paper labels away from wet surfaces. The devices were ordinary, yet they helped give the measurements a clear context.

檢驗室的完整報告分成三個信封送來。計算結果沒有謠言那麼戲劇化：受損批次在潮濕保存後發芽率顯著降低，較舊、妥善乾燥的樣本表現較好。潘維請映蓉澄清，結果說的是保存過的種子，不是每片田地或每個物種。農民問這種損害是否永久。技師解釋，有些傷害無法恢復，其他損失則和仍在研究的條件有關。小安在淺盤旁放一個木立方體，讓照片顯出比例。磁性夾把紙標籤固定在不會沾水的位置。這些器材很平凡，卻使量測有清楚背景。

Golden Field's manager offered a defensive explanation: they had been under pressure to meet orders and believed the sacks would dry before shipment. Yingrong did not accept that belief as evidence, but she also resisted the pursuit of an easy villain. She asked for the actual temperature and moisture logs. A junior clerk said some entries lag behind the dates because workers filled them in after shifts. That was an obstacle to reliable conclusions, and the shop's own records had once been similarly weak. Yingrong's reputation as a former technician could not fix missing numbers. The manager agreed to supply the remaining pages, while refusing to discuss compensation until the insurer reviewed the report. The farmers found the delay infuriating, and Yingrong understood why.

金田經理提出防禦性的解釋：公司急著趕訂單，以為麻袋在出貨前會乾。映蓉不接受這種相信作為證據，卻也不願只追求一個方便責怪的壞人。她要求實際溫度與濕度紀錄。一名年輕職員說，有些紀錄落後於日期，因為工人下班後才補寫。這妨礙可靠結論，而種子行過去的紀錄也曾同樣薄弱。映蓉身為前技師的名聲無法補回缺失數字。經理答應交出剩餘頁面，卻拒絕在保險審閱報告前談賠償。農民對拖延非常生氣，映蓉理解原因。

Pan invited two growers to test the results independently. One had spent years studying how a gene could influence a plant's resistance, while the other trusted close observation of soil and weather. Their methods were different but not enemies. They worked in harmony when each agreed to state the limits of a claim. During a demonstration a tray tipped and the dry grains began to tumble across the floor. Xiao-an wanted to gather them back into the sample, but Yingrong stopped him: once mixed with unknown dirt, they could no longer count as the same test. They cleaned the floor and recorded the loss. Such small interruptions made research slower and more honest. The growers were tolerant of the mistake because it had been acknowledged immediately. Pan proposed that they extend the observation for one more week rather than guess from a single afternoon.

潘維邀請兩位農戶獨立驗證結果。一位多年研究基因如何影響植物抗性，另一位相信對土壤和天氣的仔細觀察。方法不同，不代表彼此敵對。當兩人都願意說明主張的限制時，就能和諧合作。一次示範中，淺盤翻倒，乾種子滾落滿地。小安想把它們撿回樣本，映蓉阻止了他：混進來路不明的灰塵後，它們不能再算同一次檢驗。大家清潔地板並記下損失。這類小中斷讓研究變慢，也更誠實。農戶願意包容失誤，因為它被立刻承認。 潘維提議延長觀察一週，而不是只憑一個下午猜測。

At home, Mother said she felt no contentment in knowing that a supplier was wrong if the shop still could not pay the rent. Yingrong answered with sympathy rather than another lecture. She had wanted proof to settle everything, but it could only settle some questions. A claim for loss would take time; the coming season needed seed now. They calculated how much tested inventory remained and reserved enough spare stock for customers who had already paid. Yingrong suggested a temporary limit on new sales until the trial varieties were ready. Mother accepted it with reluctance. The limit meant turning away money, but it reduced the chance of repeating the same mistake with a new batch. Their decision was cautious because the consequences were real.

回家後，母親說，即使證明供應商出錯，店仍付不起房租，她就不會感到滿足。映蓉沒有再說教，而是同情地聽她說。她原以為證據能解決一切，實際只能解決部分問題。損失求償需要時間，新一季卻已急著要種子。她們計算剩餘已測試庫存，為先付款的顧客保留足夠備貨。映蓉提議暫時限制新銷售，直到試種品種準備好。母親不情願地接受。限制意味著拒絕收入，卻減少用新批次重犯錯誤的風險。她們小心決定，因為後果真實存在。

The family ate dinner with the laboratory report resting unopened on a side table. Yingrong had already read it enough times. Xiao-an arrived briefly to return a borrowed measuring cup and said he would rather work at a store that could say 'I do not know' than at one that promised magic. Mother smiled at the thought, then told him to go home before the rain. Yingrong looked at the empty chair once used by her father and felt a quiet shift. She could honor his interest in varieties without turning his memory into an order to preserve every old practice. Tomorrow they would begin a new field trial with clear labels, separate tools, and written limits. For now, doing no further harm was a worthwhile result.

家人吃晚餐時，檢驗報告放在旁桌上，沒有人再打開。映蓉已經讀了太多遍。小安短暫來歸還借走的量杯，說自己寧願在會說「我不知道」的店工作，也不願進一家承諾魔法的店。母親聽了微笑，接著叫他趁雨未下先回家。映蓉看著父親曾坐的空椅，心中有了安靜的轉變。她可以尊敬父親對品種的熱愛，卻不必把他的記憶變成保存所有舊習慣的命令。明天她們會用清楚標籤、分開工具和書面限制開始新田間試驗。此刻，先不要造成更多傷害，就是值得的成果。
