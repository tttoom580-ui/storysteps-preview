暴雨期限

The Storm Deadline

1998，長堤種子行的第七號紙袋 · 第 18 / 25 章 · 3000–4000 · 英中對照 · 校訂中

The deadline for the building survey arrived with another storm. The inspector found that the wall's concrete base had weakened, and he ordered the upper floor closed until reinforcement could be assessed. Mother was ready to surrender the whole property, but Jianping suggested asking whether a safer adjacent building might be rented instead. The collapse of one plan did not require the collapse of every possibility. Yingrong's intuition favored the nearby former post office, but she checked its actual dimensions and exposure to water before agreeing. A partnership with the owner would need clear costs and an exit clause. She did not want the shop's future to depend on a handshake that only one side remembered. They drew up a list of conditions while rain battered the windows. The inspector's authority concerned public safety, not the family's private choice to sell the land.

建物測量期限與另一場暴雨同時到來。檢查員發現牆體混凝土地基已弱化，命令樓上封閉，直到完成加固評估。母親準備放棄整處產權，建平卻建議先問是否有相鄰、安全的建物可以租。單一計畫崩潰，不代表所有可能都要一起崩潰。映蓉直覺偏向附近的舊郵局，但同意前先確認實際尺寸與淹水風險。與屋主合作需要清楚費用和退出條款。她不希望店的未來依賴一方記得、另一方忘記的握手。她們在雨打窗戶時列出條件。 檢查員的權限涉及公共安全，不包含替家人決定是否出售土地。

A group of farmers offered to form a cooperative to support the shop. Yingrong welcomed their care but explained that a cooperative had legal duties as well as a friendly name. Its members would need representation, accounts, and agreed rights to leave. The farmers preferred a simpler arrangement: each could reserve tested seed in advance, pay a small deposit, and cancel under clear terms. This bond of trust did not require turning every customer into an owner. One farmer asked for an exception because his planting season began earlier. Yingrong agreed to consider it if the stock was available and the rule was published for others. Special treatment hidden in private accounts would soon become another source of harm.

一群農民提議組成合作社支持種子行。映蓉歡迎關心，卻解釋合作社不只是友善名字，也有法律責任。成員需要代表權、帳目與約定好的退出權。農民最後比較喜歡簡單方式：各自預訂經測試種子，先付少量訂金，並依清楚條款取消。這種信任連結不必把每個顧客都變成所有人。一名農民因播種季較早而要求例外。映蓉答應在有庫存、且條件向其他人公開的前提下考慮。藏在私人帳裡的特殊待遇，日後很快又會造成傷害。

The old post office had a cabinet that could be adapted for dry samples and a room large enough for one counter. The owner supplied photographs, but Yingrong insisted on a physical inspection. The floor was splendid compared with the damaged warehouse, yet the roof needed repair. A carpenter pointed out curled boards near the rear window, evident signs of water exposure. He estimated the cost and said the work would take three weeks. Mother looked discouraged; Christmas sales would be missed. Yingrong replied that a shop's safety could not be made to fit a festival schedule. They agreed to rent the front room only after the work passed inspection. The carpenter would furnish a written warranty rather than a verbal assurance.

舊郵局有一座可改成乾燥樣本櫃的櫥櫃，也有一間足夠擺櫃檯的房間。屋主提供照片，映蓉卻堅持實地檢查。和受損倉庫相比，地板非常漂亮，但屋頂需要修。木匠指著後窗附近捲曲的木板，說那是明顯受潮跡象。他估算費用，說施工要三週。母親有些沮喪：聖誕節生意恐怕會錯過。映蓉說，店的安全不能硬配合節慶時間。她們同意等修繕通過檢查才租前房。木匠會提供書面保固，而不是只有口頭保證。

During the storm, Pan helped at a temporary shelter in the school gym. He asked whether the shop could supply spare dry sacks for bedding, and Yingrong checked which ones were clean and not treated with dye. She also carried a small globe from the classroom to entertain children who were frightened by the rain. A boy pointed to the valley and asked whether all storms came from the same place. Pan gave an answer he could understand without pretending that weather was simple. The shelter manager thanked the seed shop for the practical help. Mother said it felt strange to contribute something without selling it. Yingrong answered that the gift did not erase the need to pay workers or rent. Both could be true at once. The children's questions could inspire a lesson, but Pan refused to portray a complicated storm as a simple enemy. He hoped the quiet activity would relieve some of their fear.

暴雨期間，潘維到學校體育館的臨時避難所幫忙。他問種子行能不能提供乾淨備用麻袋當墊料；映蓉檢查哪些袋子沒染過色、也夠乾淨。她還從教室帶來一個小地球儀，讓被雨聲嚇到的孩子分心。一名男孩指著河谷問，暴風雨是否都從同一個方向來。潘維用孩子聽得懂的話回答，卻不假裝天氣很簡單。避難所管理員感謝種子行的實際幫助。母親說，不賣東西卻提供物資，感覺很奇怪。映蓉回答，贈送物品不會消除應付員工工資與房租的義務，兩件事可以同時成立。 孩子的提問能啟發一堂課，但潘維拒絕把複雜暴雨描繪成單純敵人。他希望安靜的活動能稍微減輕孩子的恐懼。

That evening, the inspector's report arrived. It confirmed that reopening the old building was not sensible without expensive structural work. Yingrong felt relief mixed with grief. She had wanted evidence, and now the evidence had closed a door she loved. Jianping sat with Mother to discuss selling the old warehouse once repairs made the site safe for transfer. No one rushed her signature. They would decide after receiving a full valuation and legal advice. Yingrong put the report beside the seed trial records and closed the folder. The business might continue in a new room, but the family had to let the old space remain closed. Safety had become a principle they were willing to follow even when it cost them something precious.

那晚，檢查報告送來。報告確認，若不做昂貴的結構工程，重開老建物並不合理。映蓉感到鬆一口氣，也感到悲傷。她一直想要證據，如今證據關上了她喜愛的一扇門。建平陪母親討論，等土地具備安全移轉條件後出售舊倉。沒有人催她簽名。她們會等完整估價和法律意見到齊再決定。映蓉把報告放在種子試驗紀錄旁，闔上文件夾。生意可能在新房間延續，但一家人必須讓老空間保持關閉。安全已成為一項即使代價珍貴也願遵守的原則。
