A Board with Two Columns

1998，長堤種子行的第七號紙袋 · 第 8 / 25 章 · 3000–4000 · 英中對照 · 校訂中

用互動版查詞、朗讀與收藏

The town bulletin announced that the agricultural office would buy seed supplies for three districts. Golden Field had submitted a polished bid, and Mother assumed the large firm would dominate the decision. Yingrong could not match its advertising budget, but she felt enthusiasm for a smaller task: explaining how her store tested its inventory. A learned clerk showed her the office's simple computer software, which could estimate demand and print forms. He warned that the figures were systematic only if the numbers entered were reliable. 'Bad input makes a neat mistake,' he said. Yingrong wrote the phrase on her notebook. Her store did not need the most fashionable machine; it needed a process whose mistakes were visible before customers paid for them. Yingrong hoped to acquire reliable records, not an expensive reputation for modern equipment.

鎮上的公告欄貼出消息：農業辦公室要替三個區採購種子。金田交出一份漂亮的標案，母親認為大公司一定會主導結果。映蓉比不上對方的廣告預算，卻熱衷一件小事：說明自己如何檢驗庫存。一名學識豐富的職員向她展示辦公室的簡易電腦軟體，可以估算需求並列印表單。他提醒，只有輸入的數字可靠，統計才算有系統。「錯誤輸入只會做出整齊的錯誤。」他說。映蓉把這句話寫在筆記裡。店不需要最時髦的機器，需要的是顧客付款前就看得見錯誤的流程。 映蓉希望取得可靠紀錄，而不是靠昂貴設備買來現代化名聲。

At a public meeting, an abstract debate about competition became suddenly personal. One council member asked why a woman should lead the shop when her brother owned land near the warehouse. Fengzhi objected that gender had nothing to do with seed quality. A young bridegroom standing at the back said he had learned more about planting from his mother than from any salesman. The room laughed, then quieted as Yingrong raised a question about the tender itself. Were independent trials worth anything in the scoring, or did price constitute the whole test? A clerk answered that documents and delivery capacity would matter too. Yingrong requested the rules in writing, without asking for special treatment. Her goal was not to defeat Golden Field by influence, but to compete under rules everyone could read.

公開會議上，一場抽象的競爭討論突然變得很私人。一名議員問，既然映蓉的兄弟在倉庫附近有地，為何由女人領導種子行。鳳枝反對，說性別和種子品質沒有關係。站在後面的一名新郎說，他從母親身上學到的耕種知識比任何業務教的都多。大家笑了一下，接著映蓉提出標案本身的問題：獨立試驗在評分裡有任何價值嗎？還是只比價格？職員回答，文件和配送能力也重要。映蓉要求拿到書面規則，沒有要求特殊待遇。她不想靠關係打敗金田，而是要在人人可讀的規則下競爭。

When the crowd left, Xiao-an noticed an unfamiliar man near the noticeboard. The man had been listening for a cue from the meeting room before copying names from a list. Yingrong asked who had given permission to display the list. The clerk removed it immediately: it contained customer addresses, which were not needed for a public tender. The incident did not prove an attempt to invade anyone's privacy, but it showed how easily such a mistake could happen. Mother said the town treated personal business as common property. Yingrong replied that a good procedure could protect the information even when people were careless. They added a second column to the shop board: information that customers had agreed could be shared, and information that must remain private.

人群離開時，小安注意到一名陌生男子站在公告欄前。那人像在等會議室傳來某個暗號，接著便抄下一張名單上的名字。映蓉問，誰允許展示這張名單。職員立刻撤下它：上面有顧客地址，而公開標案根本不需要。這件事不能證明有人故意侵犯隱私，卻顯示錯誤多麼容易發生。母親說，小鎮總把私人事情當成人人共有。映蓉回答，即使有人粗心，好的流程仍能保護資訊。她們在店裡的看板加上第二欄：顧客同意公開的資料，以及必須保密的資料。

That night Yingrong read the tender documents and saw the gulf between her hopes and the office's demands. A large supplier could hold spare inventory and guarantee rapid transport; her shop still used one aging bicycle and a hired truck. The intensity of the coming harvest season worried her. She did not want jealousy to turn every rival into a villain. Fang Dingchuan had delivered a sample when asked, and the company employed workers who needed wages just as Xiao-an did. Yingrong estimated how many orders the store could fill without extending credit beyond its means. The answer was small. Mother expected disappointment, but Yingrong said a modest bid could be honest. There was no merit in winning an order that would bankrupt them. The most fertile field was not necessarily the safest place for every variety, so the small bid included several conditions.

那晚，映蓉讀完採購文件，看見自己的希望與公所要求之間有多大落差。大型供應商能保留備用庫存，並保證快速運送；她的店卻還靠一輛老腳踏車和臨時雇用貨車。即將到來的旺季強度令她擔心。她不想讓嫉妒把每個對手都變成壞人。方定川受請託時確實送過樣本，金田也雇用了像小安一樣需要工資的人。映蓉估算，在不讓賒帳超出能力的前提下，店能完成多少訂單。答案不多。母親預期會失望，但映蓉說，小而誠實的投標也有價值。贏到一份使自己破產的合約，沒有任何意義。 最肥沃的田地不一定適合所有品種，因此小額標案列入了多項條件。

Pan suggested a short field demonstration rather than another argument. A composer from the youth center offered to provide music, but Yingrong asked that the test remain quiet enough for people to hear instructions. He agreed, enjoying the unusual request. The event would be open to civilians, farmers, and company staff alike. It would not be a contest to decide who owned the truth. The clerk promised to post the schedule and allow written questions. Mother drew a line beneath the expenses and agreed to fund exactly two additional trays. 'The limit is real,' she said. Yingrong thanked her. They had begun to replace family assumptions with negotiated choices, one small number at a time.

潘維提議辦一場簡短的田間示範，不再只是爭論。青年中心的作曲者自願提供音樂，映蓉卻請他讓現場安靜到聽得清楚指示。他樂意接受這個不尋常要求。活動對居民、農民和公司員工都開放，不是要比賽決定誰擁有真理。職員答應張貼時間，也接受書面問題。母親在費用表下畫一條線，同意出錢再買剛好兩個淺盤。「這個上限是真的。」她說。映蓉向她道謝。她們逐漸用協商後的選擇，取代家人之間想當然耳的假設，一次只處理一個小數字。
